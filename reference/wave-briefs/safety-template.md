The Dyaco safety text is boilerplate shared with Spirit and Sole: expect one
Xterra card per rule with `applies_to` listing every book that prints it, and a
`see_also` to the Spirit twin. The electrical page differs between books on
volts / amps / circuit rating / "never use a GFCI outlet" — every differing
figure is its own card with the figure in the title. Note the WS200/WS300 are
folding walking treadmills with uprights that must be locked up: their
"operate only with uprights up" rule is safety.
