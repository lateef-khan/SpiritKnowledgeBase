# Section brief — `specs` (Xterra wave X2: bikes, ellipticals, climber)

Read `$S/briefs-x2/COMMON.md` first. Your section facet is `section: specs`.

Yours: specification tables and figures (dimensions, weight, user weight as a
spec figure, resistance levels and type — magnetic / air / friction, flywheel
weight, stride length, step height, pedal Q-factor, drive type, power source:
batteries or adapter and its rating, console battery type), FCC / IC pages
(extend the X1 card when the page is the same), the SB600 service manual's
parts-callout and wiring pages, the FB160 and SB120 service documents' wiring
notes, and the **AIR650 service facts from the AB900 service manual**: outlines,
electrical configuration, block diagram, wiring and PCB pages — Xterra cards for
`air650-2021` with the `cards/ab900-2018/specs/` twin in `see_also`, Bluetooth
items omitted.

Not yours: board replacement → assembly; LED fault meanings → errors; part
numbers, exploded views, parts lists, tool lists → the `parts` agent; menus →
console; outlet / grounding instructions → safety; user weight as a safety rule
→ safety (say in the body that the safety card exists when you card it as a
spec).

Figures differ across thirty books: one card per value with the value in the
title, `applies_to` every machine that prints that value. A book that prints no
specification table is an absence card (X1 found none printed one; these books
may differ — check every one).
