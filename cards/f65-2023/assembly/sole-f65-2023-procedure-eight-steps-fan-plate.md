Eight illustrated assembly steps for the split-console F65-2023 (hardware item numbers in brackets).

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill from the carton onto a smooth, leveled floor with the rear at least 3 ft from any wall. Remove the plastic wrap and Styrofoam from under the unit before removing the Velcro belt; tilting it sideways with the belt off can let it fold and bounce upwards.
2. Pass the right upright (4) through Frame Base Cover (R) (65), connect 6P Computer Cable Middle (141) to 6P Computer Cable Lower (142), insert the right and left uprights (4) and (5) into the frame base (2), and tighten eight Button Head Socket Bolts (130). Do not tighten all screws yet; do not pinch the cables.
3. Connect 6P middle (141) to 6P upper (53-34), Speed Adjustment Switch W/Cable (54) to Speed Cable Upper (53-37), and Incline Adjustment Switch W/Cable (55) to Incline Cable Upper (53-38); insert Console Assembly Bottom (53) into the uprights and tighten four Button Head Socket Bolts (139) with four Split Washers (113) using the L wrench (132). Then install the Fan Plate Assembly (61) on the console bottom with two Sheet Metal Screws (175). Do not tighten all screws yet.
4. Connect the four upper-to-lower pairs (52-09 to 53-16, 52-10 to 53-17, 52-11 to 53-18, 52-16 to 53-34), place Console Assembly Top (52) onto the bottom (53), and tighten four Phillips Head Screws (137). Do not pinch the cables.
5. Install the Handrail Support (26) between the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (159), then fit Console Mast Cover Outer (L) (148), Inner (L) (149), Outer (R) (151) and Inner (R) (150) into the uprights. Tighten all screws after all components are assembled.
6. Fasten the console mast covers (148-151, positioned in step 5) with six Sheet Metal Screws (175). Ruling: the extracted step-6 sentence names only the tool (131) and the six screws; the step-6 drawing (read on the render) shows the screw points on the mast covers, so this card assigns them there.
7. Place the Tablet Holder (177) on the console top (52) with four Sheet Metal Screws (175), install the Beverage Holder (158) on the Handrail Support (26), then pull the locking pin (157) out and insert the End Cap (156) into the hole.
8. Install Frame Base Cover left (64) and right (65) into the uprights and tighten four Phillips Head Screws (128) and two Phillips Head Screws (95) into the frame base (2).
