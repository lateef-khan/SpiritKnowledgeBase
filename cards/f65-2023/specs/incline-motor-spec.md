**Rating.** A **110 or 230 volt AC motor**.

**Wiring.** The manual says five wire connections: red, black, white, green, plus **one 3 pin cable for the
position sensor**.

- AC voltage on the **Red wire (UP)** increases the incline.
- AC voltage on the **Black wire (DOWN)** decreases the incline.
- The **White wire (COM)** is neutral.
- The **green/yellow wire is the grounding wire**.

**Wire count differs between manuals.** The 2023 F80, F85 and F89 manuals describe the same motor as having four
wires plus the 3 pin position sensor cable. This manual counts five.
