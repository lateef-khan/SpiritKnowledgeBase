**Rating.** A variable speed DC motor, controlled from 0 to 180 DC volts by the main controller. On a
120Vac electronic power system the range is 0 to 90 DC volts.

**Wiring.** Three wires: red, black and green.

- The **Red wire is inserted into M+**.
- The **White wire is inserted into M-**.
- The higher the voltage, the faster the motor turns.
- The **green wire is the grounding wire**.

**As printed, and it contradicts itself.** The manual names the three wires as red, black and green, then says the
**White** wire goes into M-. Section 9.3 of the same manual says **Red to M+ and Black to M-** when the motor is
refitted. Use the section 9.3 wording.
