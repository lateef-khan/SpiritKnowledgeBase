**About 70~75 LBS**, measured with a tension meter.

Adjust it with a **14mm open-end wrench** while the four drive motor locking bolts are fitted but not yet
tightened. The full removal and refit sequence is on the drive motor replacement card.

This is the **drive belt** between the motor and the front roller, not the running belt the user walks on.
