1. The **house breaker** has tripped. Reset it.
2. The **treadmill breaker** has tripped. Reset the treadmill breaker.
3. The treadmill controller has shut down and its LED is ON. Lubricate the belt and deck, then turn the ON/OFF
   switch off and turn the power on again.

A controller that shuts down repeatedly is protecting itself, which is the same condition behind the E2 over
current code.
