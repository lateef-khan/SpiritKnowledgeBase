**Reason.** The controller is broken.

1. Turn the AC switch off and turn the power on again.
2. Replace the controller and calibrate it.

**The matrix prints this condition twice**, once with the power cycle first and once with only "replace with new
controller and calibrate it". Try the power cycle before ordering a controller.
