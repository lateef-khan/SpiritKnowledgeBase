1. A connector has fallen off. Check the connector again.
2. The LCD light is broken. Replace the LCD or the console.
3. Power to the console is too low. Check the power to the console.
4. Replace the lower controller.

**The matrix prints this condition twice.** The second row, "LCD displays not bright, incomplete, or
imperfect", gives one reason, "LCD displays are broken", and one solve, "replace with new console". Work the
four steps above first; replacing the console is the end of the same path.
