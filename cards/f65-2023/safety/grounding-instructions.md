This product **must be grounded**. If the treadmill malfunctions or breaks down, grounding gives electric
current a path of least resistance and reduces the risk of electric shock. The treadmill has a cord with an
equipment-grounding plug, which must go into an outlet that is properly installed and grounded to all local codes
and ordinances.

**DANGER.** Improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric shock. Check
with a qualified electrician or serviceman if you are in doubt as to whether the product is properly grounded.

- Do not modify the plug if it will not fit the outlet. Have a proper outlet installed by a qualified electrician.
- The product is for use on a **nominal 220-volt circuit** (a 120VAC electronic power system needs 110VAC) and has
  a grounding plug.
- A temporary adapter may be used to connect the plug to a 2-pole receptacle **only until a properly grounded
  outlet can be installed**. The green rigid earplug extending from the adapter must be connected to a permanent
  ground such as a properly grounded outlet box cover, and the adapter must be held in place by a **metal screw**.

**The voltage sentence.** This page says nominal 220-volt with 110VAC in brackets for the low-voltage system,
which is the same pairing the safety instructions use.
