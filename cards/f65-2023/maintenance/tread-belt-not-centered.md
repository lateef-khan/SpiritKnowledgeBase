**Reason.** The tread belt tension is not even across the tread belt.

**Solve.** Adjust the treadmill belt, following the belt adjustment instructions.

The manual points at its belt adjustment section rather than printing the steps in the matrix. Centring the belt
is also part of refitting the rollers and the running deck.
