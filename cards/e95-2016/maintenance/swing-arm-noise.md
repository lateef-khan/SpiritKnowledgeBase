1. There are **0.05mm~0.07mm tolerances** in the parts so the user can assemble the machine easily, and **slight play is normal**. Noises may be caused by these tolerances after a certain time of use. Add grease or thick lubricant on the **25 x 296L console mast shaft** for the handle bar. If noises come from the **6005 bearing**, replace the bearing.
2. If the handlebar button does not function, tear off the sticker and use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **M5x20mm flat head socket screw** securing the handlebar button. Check that the handlebar button is connected with the lower cable properly.
3. Check that the upper and lower handle wires are connected properly and free from damage.

Note the bearing number: this manual says 6005 at the console mast shaft. The E25 and E55 manuals of the same year name a **6203** bearing at the swing arm bottom bracket instead.
