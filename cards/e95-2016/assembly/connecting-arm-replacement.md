1. Remove the swing arm assembly and swing arm following 11-2. If the pedal tube is to be removed, simply remove the swing arm with the rod end bearing attached.
2. Use a **12mm open end wrench** to remove the **hex socket screw 5/16" x 15mm** and **flat washer 5/16" x 20**. Pull out the pedal carriage bolts to remove the pedal bar assembly.
3. To disassemble the adjustable pedal, use a **14mm wrench** to release the **3/8" x 19 hex head bolt**, **3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washer** and **3/8" x 7T nyloc nut**.
4. To remove the pedal adjustment set parts, use a **#17 wrench** and remove the **hex head bolt M10 x 40mm**, **flat washer 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T** and **nyloc nut M10 x 8T**. Remove the pedal adjustment set "E" button and twist the knob until it comes off.
5. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **Phillips head screw M5x10mm** securing the pedal, and remove the pedal.
6. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
