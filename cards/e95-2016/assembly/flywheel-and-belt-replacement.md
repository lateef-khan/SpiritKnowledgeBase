1. Follow the procedures to take the idler wheel assembly apart.
2. **Adjust the resistance to 20 with the console** and release the steel cable.
3. Use a **#15 open end combination spanner wrench** to loosen the **nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 11T** on the flywheel and mainframe. Remove the flywheel and belt.
4. To reassemble, reverse the above procedures and **adjust the belt in the centre of the drive pulley**.

Note the nut size: this manual names an **11T** nut. The E25 manual of the same year names a 9T nut at the same point. Check the nut on the machine before ordering.
