| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1 Worn brushes or bearings on the motor | Replace with a new motor |
| 2 Front roller bearings are defective | Replace with a new front roller |
| 3 Drive belt is misadjusted, too tight or too loose | Adjust the motor position |

Noise from the **rear** of the machine is a different row with a different answer.
