Two entries in the manual cover this.

**The user's stride.** Someone who favours one foot moves the belt. If that walking pattern is natural for them, track the belt slightly **off-centre to the side opposite the belt movement**.

**Belt tension.** The row in the troubleshooting matrix reads "Tread belt tension not even across tread belt" and sends you to the treadmill belt adjustment.

**On this machine the belt is a slat belt, and centring is done at the front roller.** Section 10-6 note 1 is the procedure: check the gap between the rear roller gear and the belt rack, and release the front roller a little if the belt is too tight. Section 10-6 note 2 sets the roller axle to mainframe gap at 15 to 18 mm, the same on both sides.
