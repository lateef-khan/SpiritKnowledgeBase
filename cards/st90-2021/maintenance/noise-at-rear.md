| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1 Rear roller bearings are defective | Replace with a new rear roller |
| 2 Rear roller misaligned | Adjust the rear roller position |

Noise from **under the motor cover** at the front is a different row with a different answer.

The 2023 manual for this machine family adds a separate note about resonant noise from the aluminium pedals; this manual does not carry it.
