Two causes, both in the same row:

1. Insufficient lubricant on the tread belt. See belt lubrication.
2. Tread belt tension insufficient. See belt adjustment.

On this machine the belt is a slat belt; its tension is set at the **front roller**, and section 10-6 note 1 tells you how to read the gear and belt rack gap to judge it.
