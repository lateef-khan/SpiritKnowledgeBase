1. Follow the aluminium pedal procedure step 2 to remove **10 pieces of aluminium pedal**.
2. Unplug the incline motor wires, then use an open-end wrench to remove the **2 bolts and nuts** locking the incline motor.
3. Use an open-end wrench to remove the bolts locking the incline rack to the connecting set, then take off the connecting set and the incline rack.
4. Reverse the steps to install.

**Warning printed with every pedal step: ensure all the aluminium pedals are secure when installing them back.**

**Calibrate after fitting the new motor.** The incline motor carries four power wires (red UP, black DOWN, white COM, green ground) and a 3-pin position sensor, the VR.
