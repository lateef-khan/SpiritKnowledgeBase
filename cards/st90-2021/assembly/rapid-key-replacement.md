1. Unmount the rapid key **cap**, then use a screwdriver to remove the screw and take off the rapid key **outer cover**.
2. Pull out the rapid key **board** and unplug the wire.
3. Use a screwdriver to remove **2 screws** and take off the rapid key **inner cover**.
4. Reverse the steps to fit the new part.

**Right side is speed, left side is incline.**
