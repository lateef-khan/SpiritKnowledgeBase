1. The **upright cover** is held by plastic latches only. Press and hold both sides of the cover, then pull it off.
2. Remove the **8 screws** of the front and back cover and take the covers off. Remove the rear middle cover as well.
3. Remove the **8 screws** of the foot rail limit piece and take the foot rail off.
4. Reverse the steps to install.
