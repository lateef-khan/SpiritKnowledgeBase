1. Use an open-end wrench or a T-wrench to remove the **nuts and washers** of the pedal, then take the pedal off.
2. To reach the front roulette wheel, remove **10 pieces of aluminium pedal** to make space.
3. Use an L-Allen wrench and an open-end wrench to remove the bolts and nuts that lock the front roller, then take it off.
4. The aluminium pedal set can now come off.
5. Reverse the steps to install.

**Warning printed with every pedal step: ensure all the aluminium pedals are secure when installing them back.**

**Refitting is not finished when the bolts are tight.** The belt alignment and the roller gap both have to be set afterwards; that is a separate procedure.
