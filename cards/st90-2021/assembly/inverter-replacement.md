1. Follow the aluminium pedal procedure step 2 to remove **10 pieces of aluminium pedal**.
2. Use a screwdriver to remove the **4 screws** locking the inverter and unplug all the wires.
3. Use a screwdriver to remove the screws locking the adaptor and the filter, then take them off.
4. Reverse the steps to install.

**Warning printed with every pedal step: ensure all the aluminium pedals are secure when installing them back.**

**A new inverter needs a calibration afterwards.** Seven leads land on it: encode, AC power, incline power, incline VR, AC motor, brake and control.
