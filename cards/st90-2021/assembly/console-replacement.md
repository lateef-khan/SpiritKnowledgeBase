1. Use a screwdriver to remove the **5 screws (3.5 x 12 mm)** and take off the PAD holder.
2. Use an L-Allen wrench to remove **4 bolts, spring washers and flat washers**. Unplug all the wires, then remove the console.
3. Reverse the steps to fit the new console.

A new console needs a calibration afterwards.
