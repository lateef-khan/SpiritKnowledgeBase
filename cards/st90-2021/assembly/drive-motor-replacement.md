1. Follow the aluminium pedal procedure step 2 to remove **10 pieces of aluminium pedal**.
2. Unplug all the motor wires **from the inverter**.
3. Use an L-Allen wrench to remove the bolts locking the drive motor, then take the motor off.

**Warning printed with every pedal step: ensure all the aluminium pedals are secure when installing them back.**

The motor lands on the inverter's AC MOTOR terminal on three wires: U red, V white, W black. There is also an encoder cable from the motor to the inverter.
