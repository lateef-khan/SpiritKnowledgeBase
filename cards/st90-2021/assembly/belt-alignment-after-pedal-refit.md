Two adjustments, in this order.

**Note 2 first: the roller axle gap.** When you mount the front roller back, set the gap between the **axle of the roller and the mainframe to 15 to 18 mm**. Both sides must be the same.

**Note 1: the belt alignment.** Both sides of the belt have to line up. Use the rear roller gear and the belt rack gap to judge it.

1. Rotate the rear roller. Any part of the gear will do.
2. Look at the gap between the belt and the gear.
3. **If the gap based on the gear appears in the direction of the front roller, the belt is too tight.** Release the front roller a little.
4. When the belt rack sits centred in the gear, the belt tightness is right.

**Warning printed with every pedal step: ensure all the aluminium pedals are secure when installing them back.**
