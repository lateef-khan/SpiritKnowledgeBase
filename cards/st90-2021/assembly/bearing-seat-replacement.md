**The manual says not to do this unless you have to**, because the seat has to be realigned to both rollers afterwards.

**Removal**

1. Follow the aluminium pedal procedure to remove the pedal set.
2. Use **two open-end wrenches** to remove the bolts and nuts locking the bearing seat, then take it off.

**Alignment on refit.** Have a ruler of at least **50 cm** ready.

1. Refit the front and rear rollers. **Each roller must be screwed to the end of the mainframe** before you go on.
2. Mount the bearing seat but do **not** tighten the bolts.
3. Use the ruler to align the limit wheels with the roller, and adjust the bearing seat so the distance between the ruler and the roller is about **0.5 mm**. Do this for the front roller and again for the rear roller.
4. Secure all **three** bearing seat locking bolts.

**Warning printed with every pedal step: ensure all the aluminium pedals are secure when installing them back.**
