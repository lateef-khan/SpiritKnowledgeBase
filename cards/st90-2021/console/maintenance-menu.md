Open **Maintenance** from the engineering page.

**Default Language Setup** - 13 languages: English (default), Russian, Italian, Spanish, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, German, Japanese, Dutch, Portuguese, Norwegian, Korean, French.

**Update App (USB)** - touch **Search USB**. The update runs and the console returns to idle mode when it finishes. **Do not interrupt the power during the update**; the manual says the system may crash and not start again.

**Update OS** - the manual's entry reads "None for use." Do not use it.
