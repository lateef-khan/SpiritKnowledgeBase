From the **engineering page**, touch the word **Settings** ten times again.

| Function | What it does |
|---|---|
| CTP Testing | Screen touch test |
| Fast Sleep Testing | Puts the console into sleep mode straight away, without waiting out the 30 minute timer |

This is a second ten-touch gesture, one level deeper than the one that opens the engineering page.
