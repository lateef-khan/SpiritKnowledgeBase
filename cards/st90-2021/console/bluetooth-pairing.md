1. Open **Bluetooth** in the settings page and turn it on. The console scans for devices around the treadmill on its own.
2. Touch the name of the device you want. The system pairs to it.
3. If it pairs, touch back, then home, to return to the settings page.

If pairing fails the console simply does not link the device; the manual describes no retry or error message.
