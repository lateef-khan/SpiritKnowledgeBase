1. From the settings page, touch **Settings** ten times to reach the engineering page.
2. Open **Engineering Mode**.
3. Check the Max. Incline, Max. Speed and Min. Speed values first. The calibration runs to whatever is set there.
4. Touch **Calibration**. The procedure runs on its own.
5. When it finishes the console returns to idle mode.

The incline AD readings (Max. Incline, ADC, Min. Incline) fill in on this page while the calibration runs, so watch them to see the incline moving.

Calibrate after replacing the console, the inverter or the incline motor, and after an incline error.
