Display mode controls two things at once: whether the screen stays lit when the safety key is off, and whether the console sleeps.

| Setting | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **On** (default) | Take the safety key off and the screen stays on. The console never enters sleep mode. |
| **Off** | Take the safety key off and the screen goes off. With the key in and nothing happening on the idle page, the console sleeps after **30 minutes**. Press any key to wake it. |

The same setting appears twice, in the settings page and again in the engineering menu's Machine Information page; they are the same switch.

A fast sleep test button sits in the developer options page for a technician who does not want to wait out the timer.
