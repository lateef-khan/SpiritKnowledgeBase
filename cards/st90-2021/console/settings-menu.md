Touch the settings icon while the console is in **idle mode**. The settings page holds **11** sub-functions:

Unit, Wi-Fi, Software, Time, Date, Bluetooth, Media apps, Wired network, Display mode, Display brightness, Child lock.

Touching the word **Settings** ten times on this page opens the engineering page.
