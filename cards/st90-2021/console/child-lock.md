The default is **off**.

**Turning it on**: set Child Lock on in the settings page, or in the engineering menu's Machine Information page. It takes effect the next time you power the treadmill on or refit the safety key.

**What you see**: the idle page shows `Console Locked`.

**Unlocking**: press and hold the **Start** and **Stop** keys together for **3 seconds**.
