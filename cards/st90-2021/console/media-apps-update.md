Open **Media apps** in the settings page. If the console has found third party apps that need updating, the update button lights up. Touch the back button to return to settings.

**An update will not bring back an app the app maker has dropped.** Netflix, YouTube and Chrome all stopped working on this generation of console because their makers ended support for its Android version, and no console-side update fixes that. See `sole-media-apps-2020-touchscreen`.
