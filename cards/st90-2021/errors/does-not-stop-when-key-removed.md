| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The safety key device is broken | Replace it with a new safety key device |

**Take the machine out of use until this is fixed.** The safety key is the emergency stop.

The related row, where the treadmill will display and operate with no key fitted at all, points at the same part shorted.
