The manual splits this into two rows.

**TFT not bright, incomplete or imperfect**

1. TFT light is broken. Replace with a new TFT or console.
2. Power to console too low. Check AC power is 120V; check power to console; replace the lower controller.

**TFT displays not bright, incomplete, or imperfect**

1. TFT displays are broken. Replace with a new console.

Before replacing anything, check the brightness setting: the console has 11 brightness levels, 0 to 10, and level 0 looks like a failing backlight.
