The manual covers three conditions with one list: the FAST/SLOW switch not working at all, only FAST working, and only SLOW working.

1. The connector of the SPEED CABLE and the CONSOLE is not connected properly. Connect the cables again.
2. The connector of the SPEED CABLE and the SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH with cable is not connected properly. Connect the cables again.
3. The connector of the SPEED CABLE or the SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH with cable is damaged. Connect the cable again.
4. The button of the SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH is broken. Replace the buttons.
5. The connector of the SPEED CABLE or the switch cable is damaged. Replace with a new cable.
6. As above. Replace with a new cable.

**The speed rapid key is the right-hand handrail block.** The left-hand block is the incline. The speed key lands on a 3-pin SPEED KEY connector at both the console rack and the transfer board.
