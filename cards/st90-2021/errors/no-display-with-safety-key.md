Eight causes, in the manual's order:

1. The ON/OFF switch has not been switched on. Switch the AC switch on.
2. The safety key is in the wrong position. Fit it correctly.
3. Console connector not plugged in properly. Check the wire and connect it again.
4. Console cable is broken. Replace the console cable.
5. Fuse on controller is blown. Replace the fuse or the controller.
6. Varistor on controller is blown. Replace the varistor or the controller.
7. Safety device is broken (open). Replace the safety key device.
8. Other components are faulty. Replace the console.

The console cable in step 4 is the console rack bundle: the 9-pin RM6T6A control lead and the two keyboard leads.
