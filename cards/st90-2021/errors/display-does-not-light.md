Work through the manual's list in order:

1. The tether cord (safety key) is not in position.
2. The circuit breaker on the front grill has tripped. Push it in until it locks.
3. The plug is disconnected. Push it firmly into the wall outlet.
4. The household circuit breaker may have tripped.
5. Treadmill defect. Contact your dealer.

If the screen is lit but the **power switch** is not, that is a different row of the manual with its own list.
