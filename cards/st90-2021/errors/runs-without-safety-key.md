| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Safety device is broken (short) | Replace the safety key device or the console |

**This is a safety fault, not a convenience one.** The safety key is the machine's emergency stop; a shorted safety device means it cannot stop the belt.

A related row covers the case where the belt keeps running after the key is pulled during a workout.
