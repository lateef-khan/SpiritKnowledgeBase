| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Incorrect input power, varistor is blown broken on controller | Check the voltage of power is 120V. Replace controller. |

The manual for this machine gives **120V** as the figure to check. The 2023 manual for the same machine family prints this row twice with two different figures, 120V in one copy and 220V in the other; this manual prints only 120V.
