| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1 House breaker tripped | Reset it |
| 2 Treadmill breaker tripped | Reset the treadmill breaker |
| 3 Treadmill controller fuse is broken | Replace with a new fuse |
| 4 Treadmill controller shut down and TFT would be ON | Turn off the AC switch and turn the power on again |

Reason 4 is the one that looks like a fault but is not: the screen stays lit while the inverter has latched off, so the console looks healthy.

If it is a breaker that keeps going, the friction and breaker cards cover why.
