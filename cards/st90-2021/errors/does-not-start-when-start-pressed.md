| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1 Motor wire isn't connected into right position | Check and plug it in again |
| 2 Motor is broken | Replace the motor, or check the wire and connector for damage |
| 3 Treadmill controller shut down and TFT would be ON | Turn off the AC switch and turn the power on again |

Reason 3 is the common one on this machine: the screen is alive but the inverter has latched off, and a full power cycle at the AC switch clears it.

**The motor wires here are U, V and W at the inverter**, not the M+/M- pair of a DC treadmill.
