| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1 Another chest belt in use around the treadmill | Check for other chest belt use nearby |
| 2 Other magnetic field disturbance | Change the position or direction of the treadmill |
| 3 Receiver is broken | Replace with a new receiver |

The receiver on this machine is the 3-pin **RF board** in the console rack.
