| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Console is not calibrated | Calibrate the console |

That is the manual's whole answer for this row. A calibration is also needed after fitting a new console, inverter or incline motor.

If the incline window is showing the words `INCLINE ERR` rather than a wrong number, use that card instead.
