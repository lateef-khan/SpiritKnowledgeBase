The console cable bundle is four separate leads:

| Lead | Pins |
|---|---|
| Wired Network cable | 8 |
| RM6T6A control | 9 |
| KEYBOARD | 8 |
| KEYBOARD | 10 |

The 9-pin RM6T6A control lead is the console-to-inverter link. Replace or reseat this one first for a communication error.
