Seven leads land on the lower controller, which on this machine is the inverter:

- Encode cable (the motor encoder)
- AC power cable
- Incline power cable
- Incline VR cable
- AC motor cable
- Brake cable
- Control cable

The control cable is the 9-pin RM6T6A link up to the console. The encode cable is the speed feedback from the AC motor - this machine has an encoder on the motor rather than a magnet and reed sensor on the front roller.
