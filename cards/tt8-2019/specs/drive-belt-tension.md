**DC model: TT8 2019 ST928-YT035, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2019 (ST928A-YT037) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

Adjust the drive belt with a **14 mm open end wrench** and measure with a **tension measuring device**.

**Set the tension into the white LBS area, between 70 and 75 LBS.**

Then use a 14 mm T-shaped socket wrench to secure and tighten the **4 securing screws**.

The same 70-75 LBS figure and the same tools appear at the end of the motor replacement, the roller
replacement and the running deck replacement procedures — it is one specification, quoted three times.
