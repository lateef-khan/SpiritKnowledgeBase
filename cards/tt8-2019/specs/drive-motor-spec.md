**DC model: TT8 2019 ST928-YT035, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2019 (ST928A-YT037) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

- It is a variable speed **0-180 volt DC motor** on a 230 V system. **On a 120 Vac electronic power system
  it is a 0-90 volt DC motor.**
- Three wires: **red, black and green**.
- DC voltage on the **red (white) wire (M+)** turns the motor **clockwise**.
- DC voltage on the **black wire (M-)** turns the motor **counter-clockwise**.
- The higher the voltage, the faster the motor turns.
- The **green** wire is ground.

The main controller carries the DC power supply for the console, the incline driver and the **DC motor
driver**, and links the console to the motor.
