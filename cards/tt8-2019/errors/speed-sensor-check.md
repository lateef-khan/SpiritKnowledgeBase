**DC model: TT8 2019 ST928-YT035, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2019 (ST928A-YT037) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

1. Remove the motor cover hood.
2. The speed sensor is on the **left side of the frame, right next to the front roller pulley** (the pulley
   with a belt around it that also goes to the motor). It is small and black with a wire connected to it.
3. Make the sensor as close as possible to the pulley **without touching it**. There is a magnet on the
   face of the pulley — line the sensor up with the magnet. One screw holds the sensor; loosen it to adjust,
   and re-tighten when finished.

**Keep the gap between sensor and magnet under 3 mm.** A wider gap is the documented cause of a missing RPM
signal, which the console reports as E1.
