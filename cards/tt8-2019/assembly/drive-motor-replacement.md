**DC model: TT8 2019 ST928-YT035, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2019 (ST928A-YT037) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **5 tapping screws** securing the motor cover.
2. Remove the motor grounding wire (greenish yellow), the motor wire **(+) red** and the motor wire **(-)
   black**.
3. Use a **14 mm T-shaped socket wrench** to loosen the **4 screws**, and a **14 mm open end wrench** to
   loosen the **1 belt tension screw**. Remove the motor and fit the new one. Reassemble in reverse order and
   re-hook the belt, readjusting it to be parallel with the main frame after re-hooking. **Do not tighten the
   4 securing screws yet.**
4. Use the **14 mm open end wrench** to adjust belt tension with a tension measuring device. **Adjust to the
   white LBS area, between 70-75 LBS.** Use the 14 mm T-shaped socket wrench to secure and tighten the 4
   securing screws. Connect the grounding wires and the motor wires (**red M+, black M-**).
