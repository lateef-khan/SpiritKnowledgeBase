**DC model: TT8 2019 ST928-YT035, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2019 (ST928A-YT037) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

Remove the breaker connection wiring, replace the part, and reconnect the wiring.

That is the whole procedure as printed. The breaker itself is the one on the front grill that a technician
pushes in until it locks when the display will not light.
