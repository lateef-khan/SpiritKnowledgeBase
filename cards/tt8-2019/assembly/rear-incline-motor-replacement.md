**DC model: TT8 2019 ST928-YT035, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2019 (ST928A-YT037) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

**Zero the incline motor before taking it off the machine or fitting it to the frame. The zeroing distance is 210 mm.**

1. Flip the treadmill on its side, cut the wiring tie-wraps and disconnect the wires from the controller
   board, remove the motor mounting bolts and remove the motor. Use an **M8 L-shape Allen wrench** and an
   open end wrench.
2. **Make sure the incline motor distance is 210 mm.**
3. Connect the rear incline motor wiring to the controller:
   - **Red wire to "UP"**
   - **White wire to "COM"**
   - **Black wire to "DOWN"**

The rear incline motor is a separate part from the front one and takes a **different length setting**
(210 mm rear versus 235 mm front). Do not carry one figure over to
the other motor.
