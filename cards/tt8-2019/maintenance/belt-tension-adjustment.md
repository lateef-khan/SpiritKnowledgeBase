**DC model: TT8 2019 ST928-YT035, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2019 (ST928A-YT037) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

Adjustment is made **at the rear roller**. The adjustment bolts sit at the end of the step rails in the end
caps, reached **through a small hole in the end cap**.

1. Tighten the rear roller bolts **only enough to stop slippage at the front roller**.
2. Turn **both** tread-belt tension adjustment bolts in increments of **1/4 turn each**.
3. Check the tension by walking on the belt at low speed and confirming it does not slip.
4. Keep tensioning until the belt stops slipping.

If the belt feels tight enough and still slips, the problem may be a **loose motor drive belt under the
front cover**.

**DO NOT OVERTIGHTEN.** Over-tightening causes belt damage and premature bearing failure. Damage to the running belt resulting from improper tracking or tension adjustments is **not covered under the warranty**.
