**DC model: TT8 2019 ST928-YT035, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2019 (ST928A-YT037) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

The checklist reads: the treadmill only achieves approximately **10 kph** but shows a higher speed on the
display. This indicates the motor should be receiving power to operate, and the cause is **low AC voltage to
the treadmill**.

- **Do not use an extension cord.** If one is unavoidable it must be as short as possible and heavy duty,
  **16 AWG minimum**.
- Low household voltage — contact an electrician or your dealer.
- **A minimum of 220 volt AC current at 50 Hz is required** (110 Vac on a 120 Vac electronic power system).
