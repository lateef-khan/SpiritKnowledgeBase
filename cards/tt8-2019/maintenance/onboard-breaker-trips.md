**DC model: TT8 2019 ST928-YT035, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2019 (ST928A-YT037) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

The checklist row reads: **"Treadmill trips on board 15 amp circuit"** — high belt/deck friction.
The fix is belt and deck lubrication.

**The amp rating printed here differs between the TT8 variants.** This manual says 15 amp. Check the
rating stamped on the machine rather than assuming it matches another TT8.
