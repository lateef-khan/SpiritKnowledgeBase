**DC model: TT8 2019 ST928-YT035, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2019 (ST928A-YT037) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

**Reason**: incorrect input power, and the varistor on the controller has blown.

**Solve**: check the voltage of the power is **220 V** (110 V on a 120 Vac electronic power system),
then replace the controller.
