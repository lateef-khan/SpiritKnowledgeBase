**DC model: TT8 2019 ST928-YT035, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2019 (ST928A-YT037) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

1. Remove the safety key.
2. Press and hold **START** and **FAST (Speed ▲)** and replace the safety key. Keep holding START and FAST
   until the window displays **"CALIBRATION MODE"**.
3. Set the display to Metric or English (miles vs kilometres): first press ENTER and press the UP/DOWN (▲/▼) key to show which you want, then press ENTER.
4. **Grade return — On.** This lets the incline return to zero when STOP is pressed. EU standards require it
   to be off for sale in Europe.
5. Press **START** to begin calibration.

**The process is automatic and the belt starts up without warning, so do not stand on the belt.**
