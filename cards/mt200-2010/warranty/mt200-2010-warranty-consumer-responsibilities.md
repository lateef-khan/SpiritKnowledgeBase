Under the heading **NORMAL RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE CONSUMER**: this warranty applies to products in clinical use up to 5 hours per day, and the consumer is responsible for the items listed below:

1. The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card **within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty or register online at www.spiritmedicalsystems.com
2. Proper use of the treadmill in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual, **including maintenance**.
3. **Proper connection to a power supply of sufficient voltage, replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects in facility wiring.**
4. Expenses for making the treadmill accessible for servicing, including any item that was not part of the treadmill at the time it was shipped from the factory.
5. Damages to the treadmill finish during shipping, installation or following installation.

**There are five items and they are not the XT five.** Item 3 - the power supply, blown fuses and facility wiring - has no counterpart in any XT list, and it puts the electrical supply squarely on the clinic. There is no "proper installation ... local electric codes" item and no separate "routine maintenance" item; maintenance is folded into item 2 instead. Do not quote `xt-2015-warranty-consumer-responsibilities` or `xt-2023-warranty-consumer-responsibilities` for this machine.

Two items bite in practice. **Item 4** makes getting the treadmill to a place where it can be worked on the clinic's expense, not a warranty cost. **Item 5** puts finish damage during shipping and installation on the clinic, so inspect the machine before the carrier leaves.

**Item 1 is a hard condition, and exclusion 7 enforces it.** A product with no warranty registration on file at Spirit is excluded outright - see `mt200-2010-warranty-registration`.

This list is only half of what governs the warranty; the other half is the eight numbered exclusions below it, in `mt200-2010-warranty-exclusions`.
