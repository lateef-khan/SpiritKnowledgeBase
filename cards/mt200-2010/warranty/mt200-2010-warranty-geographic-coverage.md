One sentence, printed **once**, at the foot of the warranty section under the service steps:

> All warranties are made by Spirit Medical Systems Group. This warranty applies only in the **48 contiguous United States**. NOTE: This warranty **does not apply to Alaska or Hawaii**

Alaska and Hawaii are named as outside the covered area. The manual states no separate Alaska or Hawaii terms and no shipping surcharge for them.

**This manual has no overseas exclusion.** The XT, CT900 and CTSBS900 lists all carry an exclusion reading "Warranties outside of the United States may vary. Please contact your local dealer for details." The eight exclusions here do not include it, and no other sentence in the manual says anything about coverage outside the United States. So the manual limits the warranty to the 48 contiguous states and then says nothing further - it does not point the owner at a local dealer, and it does not say the terms merely vary.

Treat a machine outside the 48 contiguous states as something this manual does not cover and does not explain, rather than as covered on different terms. Do not answer it from `xt-2023-warranty-geographic-coverage`, whose second sentence this manual does not print.

The note is also the only place the warrantor is restated: **Spirit Medical Systems Group**, not Spirit Fitness. See `mt200-2010-warranty-start-date`.
