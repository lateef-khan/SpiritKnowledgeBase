Keep your bill of sale. Service under this warranty must be obtained by following these steps, **in order**:

1. Contact your selling authorized Spirit dealer. **OR**
2. Contact your local authorized Spirit service organization.
3. If there is a question as to where to obtain service, contact our service department at **(870) 935-1107**.
4. Spirit's obligation under this warranty is limited to repairing or replacing, at Spirit's option, the product through one of our authorized service centers. **All repairs must be preauthorized by Spirit.** If the product is shipped to a service center, freight charges to and from the service center will be the customer's responsibility. For replacement parts shipped while the product is under warranty, the customer will be responsible for shipping and handling charges. For **in-facility** service, the customer will be responsible for a trip charge. There will be an additional trip charge if the customer is located over **100 miles** from the nearest service center.
5. The owner is responsible for adequate packaging upon return to Spirit. Spirit is not responsible for damages in shipping. Make all freight damage claims with the appropriate freight carrier. **DO NOT SHIP ANY UNIT TO OUR FACTORY WITHOUT A RETURN AUTHORIZATION NUMBER. All units arriving without a return authorization number will be refused.**
6. For any further information, or to contact our service department by mail, send your correspondence to:

> Spirit Medical Systems Group
> P.O. Box 2037
> Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037

## What the clinic pays even inside the warranty period

Four charges, all in step 4, plus one refusal in exclusion 2:

| What the customer pays | Where |
|---|---|
| Freight **to and from** a service center, if the product is shipped to one | step 4 |
| **Shipping and handling** on replacement parts shipped while under warranty | step 4 |
| A **trip charge** for **in-facility** service | step 4 |
| An **additional trip charge** beyond **100 miles** from the nearest service center | step 4 |
| Expenses for making the treadmill accessible for servicing | responsibilities item 4 |

Exclusion 2 refuses **service call reimbursement to the consumer** outright. **No figure is printed for any of these charges**, and the manual does not say who sets the trip charge.

**Step 4 says "in-facility", not "in-home".** The XT manuals word the same sentence "For in-home service"; this is a clinical machine and the manual says so in this sentence too. The step order, the phone number and the P.O. Box are otherwise the XT wording, but the return address names **Spirit Medical Systems Group**, so a return shipped to Spirit Fitness is addressed to the wrong warrantor.

The paragraph above the steps says twelve months from the bill of sale, or eighteen months from factory shipping as read off the serial number, "establishes **the warranty period**" - the XT and 2026 manuals say "the **labor** warranty period" in the same sentence. That difference is unresolved in the source; see `mt200-2010-warranty-start-date`.

The same page repeats that the warranty applies only in the 48 contiguous United States: `mt200-2010-warranty-geographic-coverage`.
