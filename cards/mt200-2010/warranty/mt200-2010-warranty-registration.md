## Is registration required?

**Yes, and there is a deadline.** Responsibility 1:

> The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card **within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty or register online at www.spiritmedicalsystems.com

Exclusion 7 backs it with a flat exclusion:

> Products that do not have a warranty registration on file at Spirit. Spirit reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.

So an unregistered machine is named as something the warranty does not cover, and the 10-day window is the manual's own condition for validating it.

## How to register

Either route satisfies responsibility 1:

- Complete the **self-addressed product registration card** in full and return it to Spirit, **or**
- visit **www.spiritmedicalsystems.com** to register online.

The front of the manual adds: "Please complete and mail your registration card today."

**The serial number sticker is on the front of the treadmill**, and the Product Registration page prints an `SN ____` line to copy it into. That is the only location the manual gives.

## Do not answer this from a Spirit Fitness card

The registration site here is **spiritmedicalsystems.com**, not spiritfitness.com, and the record is held by Spirit Medical Systems Group. The 2022 MT200 manual sends the facility somewhere else again - `mt200-2022-warranty-registration` - and prints no deadline at all. The 2023 XT manuals impose no deadline either. Only the 2015-generation XT manuals and this one carry the 10-day card, and they name different addresses for it.

Where there is no receipt, the warranty clock falls back to eighteen months from the original factory shipping date: `mt200-2010-warranty-start-date`.
