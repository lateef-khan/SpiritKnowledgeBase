Printed above the table: **Effective July 15, 2013 - TREADMILL LIMITED WARRANTY**. There is one row and it is labelled Commercial. This manual prints **no residential row**.

| WARRANTY | Frame | Mech. Parts | Electronics | Wear Items \* | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial | Lifetime | 3 years | 3 years | 1 year | 1 year |

> \* Wear items are rubber hand grips, pedals, console overlay and drive belts

**The column names are not the ones the rest of the range uses.** There is no Motor column and no Deck column. Mechanical parts and electronics are split into two headings that both read 3 years, and the wear-items definition names four specific things - hand grips, pedals, console overlay, drive belts - and nothing else. A part that is none of those and is not the frame falls under Mech. Parts or Electronics at 3 years.

**This is not the later MT200 table.** The 2022 MT200 manual warrants the drive motor 10 years, parts and labor 3 years and wear items 6 months, and defines wear items with "drive belt" singular. The two generations share a model name and almost nothing else here: `mt200-2022-warranty-commercial-periods`.

It is not the Spirit CT900ENT table either, even though that one also opens with a lifetime frame. The CT900ENT gives Drive Motor 10 Years and Labor 3 Years; this manual gives 1 year of labor.

## The 2019 warranty sheet does not print this table

**Spirit's Cardio Warranty sheet, Revision 03.07.2019, warrants the MT200 as Frame Lifetime, Motor 10 Years, Parts 3 Years, Labor 3
Years** - the 2022 manual's table, three years before that manual - with no Mech. Parts,
Electronics or Wear Items column and **three years of labor where this manual gives one**. So
by March 2019 Spirit was already selling the MT200 on the later terms; a customer whose manual
is this one and whose sale is after that date holds two documents that disagree, and the sheet
itself says "See owner's manuals for complete warranty details". Quote both:
`spirit-commercial-warranty-sheet-2019-all-facilities-terms`.

Two things this table does not tell you on its own. When the clock starts is in the paragraph above it (`mt200-2010-warranty-start-date`), and the kind of use it assumes - clinical use up to 5 hours per day - is in the responsibilities paragraph below it (`mt200-2010-warranty-use-environment`).
