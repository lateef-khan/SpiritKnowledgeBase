**No.** Exclusion 5 puts outside the warranty:

> Products that have been; sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party.

Four routes out of the original buyer's hands are named and all four end the cover: a sale, a transfer, a barter, or a gift. The manual states no way to reinstate it, names no transfer fee, and describes no procedure for a second owner.

Two neighbouring exclusions are worth reading at the same time:

- **Exclusion 4** - products with original serial numbers that have been **removed or altered**. A machine passed on with its serial sticker gone is outside the cover on both counts, and the serial is also what the registration record is keyed to.
- **Exclusion 6** - **products that are used as store display models**. This one has no counterpart in any Spirit XT, CT900 or CTSBS900 list. A demonstration unit sold on from a showroom floor is excluded by exclusions 5 and 6 together.

**The later MT200 manual says it in plainer words.** The 2022 manual keeps the same third-party exclusion and adds a flat sentence in the opening paragraph - "Warranties are not transferable" - which this manual does not print: `mt200-2022-warranty-not-transferable`. The rule is the same; only this manual leaves it to the exclusion list to say so.

The full list of eight is in `mt200-2010-warranty-exclusions`.
