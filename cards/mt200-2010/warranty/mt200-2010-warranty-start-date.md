The periods in the warranty table are counted **from the date of retail sale as determined by a sales receipt**.

**In the absence of a sales receipt**, they are counted from **eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date**.

**The warrantor is Spirit Medical Systems Group, not Spirit Fitness.** The opening line reads "Spirit Medical Systems Group warrants this product", the closing note reads "All warranties are made by Spirit Medical Systems Group", the return address is Spirit Medical Systems Group, and the registration site is www.spiritmedicalsystems.com. Every "Spirit" in this warranty section is that entity. The manual never says how the two names relate, and nothing in it names Spirit Fitness, Inc. at all.

What Spirit owes: **new or remanufactured parts, at Spirit's option**, plus technical support to its independent dealers and servicing organisations. Where there is no dealer or service organisation, Spirit administers the warranty directly to a consumer.

**The service clock is stated separately and is worded differently from the XT manuals.** The SERVICE paragraph says twelve months from the bill of sale, or eighteen months from factory shipping read off the serial number, "establishes **the warranty period**" - not "the labor warranty period", which is what the XT and 2026 manuals say in the same sentence. This manual therefore appears to date the whole warranty from that sentence, and never reconciles it with the opening paragraph above. Both halves are real; see `mt200-2010-warranty-service-steps`.

The clock sentence itself is word for word the XT sentence. The rest of the paragraph is not, and the warrantor is a different company, so this fact is held separately from `xt-2023-warranty-start-date`.
