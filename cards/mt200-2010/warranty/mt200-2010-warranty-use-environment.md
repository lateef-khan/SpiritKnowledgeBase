One sentence carries the whole rule, and the manual states it once:

> This warranty applies to products in **clinical use up to 5 hours per day**.

The warranty table's single row is labelled **Commercial**, so the periods on it are the commercial ones and there is no residential row to fall back on.

**Read the limit as it is written.** It names a setting - clinical - and a daily ceiling of five hours. The manual does not define "clinical use", does not say what happens above five hours, and does not say whether a non-clinical commercial setting such as a dues-paying gym is inside or outside the cover.

**Three things the XT manuals say here are absent from this one.** There is no "non-dues paying facility" exclusion, no "ordinary household use" phrase anywhere in the warranty section, and no prisons and correctional facilities exclusion. Do not import any of them: `xt-2023-warranty-use-environment` and `spirit-2026t-warranty-use-environment` are different machines' facts, and the wording they turn on is not printed here.

The 2022 MT200 manual states no hours-per-day limit at all - see `mt200-2022-warranty-commercial-periods`. The five-hour ceiling belongs to this manual only.

The full responsibilities list this sentence introduces is in `mt200-2010-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`; the periods it governs are in `mt200-2010-warranty-coverage-periods`.
