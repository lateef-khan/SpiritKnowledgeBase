> Please record the Serial Number of this fitness product in the space provided below. You can find
> the serial number on a sticker that is located on the front of the treadmill.

**The front of the treadmill** - the manual names the place in words, and names nothing else. It
does not mention a barcode, does not print a sample number, and describes no other label on the
machine.

**This manual has no PRODUCT LABELS page.** No safety warning sticker and no Proposition 65 sticker
is described anywhere in it, and no California Proposition 65 wording is printed in it at all.

**The 2022 MT200 owner's manual states no location.** Its Product registration page gives only a
registration web address and never says where the number is, so there is no 2022 statement to
quote. Do not carry `the front of the treadmill` onto a 2022 machine from this card - point that
owner at the sticker on their own machine.

**Other Spirit treadmills put it elsewhere.** The 2023 XT and 2026 4.0T, 7.0T and 8.0T manuals put
the serial number sticker on the rear of the motor cover
(`xt-2023-safety-serial-number-sticker`), and the CTSBS900 on the side frame near the plug-in. Do
not answer an MT200 from those.
