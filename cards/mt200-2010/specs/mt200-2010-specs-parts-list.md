The manual heads the first column **"Dwg #"** - these are the callout numbers on
the Exploded View Drawing on page 31. The drawing itself is a picture with
numbers on it and no text, so this table is the only readable form of it.

**Three rows differ from the 2022 MT200 list.** 198 numbers appear on both and
carry the same part; the exceptions are:

| No. | On this 2010 manual | On the 2022 manual |
|---|---|---|
| 49 | not printed | Insulation bushing, 1 |
| 50 | Ground Isolation Cap, 1 | not printed |
| 60 | M8 × P1.25 × 45m/m Socket Head Cap Bolt, **10** | M8 × P1.25 × 45L Socket Head Cap Bolt, **4** |
| 253 | Linear Bearing, Step Sensor | Linear Bushing |
| 308 | not printed | M8 x 40L Socket Head Cap Bolt, 6 |

The 2022 book splits this manual's ten 45 mm socket head cap bolts into four
45 mm (60) and six 40 mm (308). Do not order ten 45 mm bolts for a machine built
to the later list. See `mt200-2022-specs-parts-list`.

**On the printed numbering.** 199 rows are printed. The whole numbers that
appear are 1-21, 24, 26, 27, 29-45, 50-68, 71-74, 76-78, 104, 108, 110, 112,
113, 117, 119, 125, 131, 132, 134, 136-140, 147, 148, 151-153, 178-193, 195-214,
216-218, 221-224, 226-228, 230, 231, 236, 240, 243-262, 264-271, 274-288,
290-300, 302-305, plus the three console sub-numbers 36-15, 36-16 and 36-17. Any
number outside that run is not printed in this manual at all - it is not a
missing page.

**Item 2 and item 180 are both "Incline Bracket", quantity 1.** The manual
prints them that way; check the drawing before ordering either.

**The parts list is the only place several assembly parts are named.** The
assembly instructions on pages 25-30 call out items 3, 4, 5, 18, 19, 21, 32, 33,
36, 58, 59, 73, 104, 108, 110, 112, 153, 181, 192, 207, 217 and 226 and nothing
else, so the quantities for every other row come from this table alone.

**No motor rating, roller size or belt size is printed for any of these parts.**
Item 279 is "Drive Motor" with no horsepower or wattage, item 16 is
"Drive Motor Belt, Poly-V" with no length or profile, and items 10, 11, 12 and
13 give the roller, deck and belt no dimensions. The Specifications page on
page 42 does not carry them either.
