**Certifications, exactly as printed.**

> TUV listed to ANSI/AAMI ES60601-1:2005+A2 (R2012) +A1, CAN/CSA-C22.2
> No. 60601-1:14, CE conformity to EN 60601-1 EMC, Compliance to EN 60601-1-2

Four entries, written as one running sentence rather than a list. This manual is
the only one of the two MT200 books that names **TUV** as the listing body and
the only one that uses the words "CE conformity".

**Classification.**

> Class I, Type B, ordinary equipment, continuous operation. This product is
> classed as ordinary equipment according to IEC/EN/UL60601-1 and is NOT
> protected against the ingress of water.

**Do not quote this list for the 2022 MT200.** The later book prints a longer,
differently worded list of seven standards, adds EN ISO 20957:2013, drops the
TUV and CE-conformity wording, and classifies the machine as
"Class I **measuring**, Type B" - a different classification. See
`mt200-2022-specs-certifications-and-classification`.

**Nor for the 2026 7.0T or 8.0T.** Those lists carry ISO 14971; 2019 and, on the
8.0T, EU MDR 2017/745 Class I - neither of which appears in any MT200 manual.

**No EU MDR entry appears here.** The manual is dated 2013 (revision I140406-A3,
Rev. 3, © 2013), before the regulation existed.

**Ingress protection.** Two other pages say the same thing in other words: the
Important Electrical Information page says the MT200 "is NOT protected against
the ingress of water or particulate matter", and adds that it "is not suitable
for use in an oxygen rich environment". The specification page gives no IP code.

**Disposal.** "Reference should be made to local regulations concerning the
disposal of this product at the end of useful life." No take-back scheme, no
WEEE marking and no dismantling instruction is given. The 2022 manual prints the
same sentence.
