**Dyaco International Inc., 12F, No.111, Songjiang Rd., Taipei 104, Taiwan
R.O.C.**

It is printed as four lines under the Specifications table, with no heading and
no factory symbol.

**This is not the address on the 2026 manuals.** The 2026 7.0T and 8.0T
specification pages give Dyaco International Inc. at **No. 1, Gong 1st Rd.,
Hemei Township, Changhua County 50843, Taiwan** - the same company at a
different address. Quote the address from the manual for the machine in hand;
see `spirit-2026t-specs-manufacturer`.

**Two other names appear in this book and neither is the manufacturer.** The
treadmill is sold and warranted by **Spirit Medical Systems Group**, P.O. Box
2037, Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037, whose service department is (870) 935-1107 and
whose registration site is www.spiritmedicalsystems.com. The warranty pages name
Spirit, not Dyaco.

**The 2022 MT200 manual prints a "Manufacturer" heading with nothing readable
under it** - the block did not survive the conversion of that PDF, so there is
no confirmed 2022 address in the knowledge base. Do not carry this 2010 address
forward without checking the printed page.

The manual is revision I140406-A3, Rev. 3, © 2013, and its warranty page is
effective July 15, 2013.
