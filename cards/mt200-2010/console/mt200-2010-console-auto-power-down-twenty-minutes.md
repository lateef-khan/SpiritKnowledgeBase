**The console automatically powers down after 20 minutes of inactivity. Press any key
to wake the console up again.**

**The timeout can be switched off.** The manual sends you to its Maintenance section
on p. 39, where the **Sleep** item turns sleep mode on or off; with sleep off the
display power is always on. See `70t-2026-console-maintenance-mode-menu`.

**Always turn off the main power switch when the machine is not in use.**

**This is 20 minutes, not 30.** The later MT200 manual, and the 2026 7.0T and 8.0T,
all print **30** minutes for the same behaviour - see
`spirit-2026t-console-auto-power-down`. Everything else about the timeout is worded
identically in both MT200 generations, so the figure is the only thing that tells
them apart. Check the manual, not the machine's looks.
