**130 cm (51.18") beyond the training area**, on every side the bench is entered from, and the live
area must include the room for an emergency dismount.

> The live area shall be not less than 130cm (51.18") greater than the training area in the
> directions from which the equipment is accessed. The live area must also include the area for
> emergency dismount.

It is the smallest perimeter of the six CSF machines — 50 cm less than the CSF-HRAC half power rack
asks for — but still more than twice the 600 mm a CSS single station uses.

The plan view on the same page dimensions the training area as **767 x 1202 mm** (30.2" x 47.3"), so
the live area is at least 3367 x 3802 mm if the bench is approached from all four sides.
