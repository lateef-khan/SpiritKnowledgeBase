The *Pad Holder* (2) is the tablet shelf on top of the console. **These numbers are the outline's
own and are not the parts-list item numbers** used in the replacement chapter (where the console
is 19 and the front shrouds 29 and 30). No skeleton drawing, dimensions or weights are printed.

The XBR95-2023 book prints the same nineteen-item outline and adds a skeleton:
`xbr95-2023-specs-outline-numbered-part-names`.
