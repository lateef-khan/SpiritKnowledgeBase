1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **4 pcs of Ø4 x 16 mm sheet-metal screws (105)**.
2. Use the **14 mm wrench, two of them**, to release **2 pcs of 3/8" x 4" hex head bolts (67)**, **2 pcs of 3/8" x 4-1/4" hex head bolts (142)**, **4 pcs of Ø3/8" x Ø19 x 1.5T flat washers (77)** and **2 pcs of 3/8" x 7T nyloc nuts (89)**.
3. Use a **12 mm and a 13 mm wrench** to release one **5/16" x 1-1/4" hex screw (70)** and one **5/16" x 18 mm x 1.5T flat washer (76)**, then take the **seat back assembly (5)** and the **mesh seat back (63)** off.
4. Loosen the cable from the mesh seat back (63).
5. Reverse the steps to refit.

**Four long bolts, not two.** The XBR55 and XBR95 books print only the two 3/8" x 4" bolts (67) in step 2; this book adds two 3/8" x 4-1/4" bolts (142) and four washers (77). Part 142 is "3/8"×4-1/4"_Hex Head Screw" in the 2008 XBR25 dealer parts list too. Two nyloc nuts for four bolts is what is printed.

This book has no gas-cylinder replacement section; the release-lever card is where its gas cylinder nut is held while the cable comes off.
