**This is the CE800 (2016) list, from the book titled XE890B: an M14 fish-eye nut, a cross bar at
550-600 lbs, and an M8x15 cap on the short bushing. The XE795 (2016) book pairs its M14 rod end with
500-550 lbs, and the XE195/XE295/XE395 books have an M12 rod end and a bushing-housing figure this
book does not print. Three lists, three cards.**

"Slipping problems can be verified by the following steps."

| Joint | Check |
|---|---|
| **M14 x P1.75 Fish-eye bearing** | The M14 nut must be firmly tightened with the Connecting Arm, and the fish-eye bearing **perpendicular to the bolt** |
| **Cross Bar** | The CAP Socket Head Cap Bolt M8x40 m/m on top of the cross bar must be tightened to **550~600Lbs** |
| **Short bushing** | The **bolt cap M8x15** on the short bushing has to be tightened |
| **Belt** | "Belt slipping is quite usual because the belt gets less tensioned as time lasts. Time duration and user weight are major factors. Follow procedure to adjust the belt tension" |

The torque is printed as "Lbs" with no unit of length. The belt adjustment is the J-bolt nut with a
13 mm wrench to 190 Hz - this book's own wording is on
`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-belt-slipping-hook-screw-cap-13-mm-190-hz`.
