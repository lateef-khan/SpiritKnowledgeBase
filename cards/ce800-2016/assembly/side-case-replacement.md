**This is the 2017 XE890B-AE10M book's procedure.** It removes the pedal arm and console mast first, where the
CE800 (2020) / CE800ENT books remove the swing arm and connecting arm, and it sizes the left cover's washer two ways.

1. Remove the **pedal arm** and the **console mast assembly**.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **7 sheet metal screws 4x19mm** from the left and right chain cover and
   the **tapping screws 5x16mm** that secure the right chain cover to the mainframe. Remove the right chain cover.
3. Remove the **4x16mm tapping screw (1 pc)** and the **Ø1/4" x 19 m/m flat washer** fixed on the main frame from the
   left chain cover, and remove **5x16 tapping screws (3 pcs)**. The left chain cover can then be taken out.
4. Reassemble in the reverse order. **The flat washer `5/16" x 23 x 1.5T` is to be placed on the left cover.**

**The washer is printed as two different parts.** Step 3 takes a `Ø1/4" x 19` flat washer off the left cover; step 4
puts a `5/16" x 23 x 1.5T` flat washer back on it. The CE800 (2020) and CE800ENT books print `5/16" x 23 x 1.5T`
in both places. Measure the washer you take off.
