**This is the 2017 XE890B-AE10M book's procedure. Its step 3 bolt is a `5/16" x 10mm` button head socket bolt with
a `5/16" x 35 x 1.0T` washer, turned with a 12 mm wrench; the CE800 (2020) / CE800ENT books use a `5/16" x 15mm`
hex head bolt with a `1.5T` washer, and the XE795 books use the same button head bolt but a 5 mm L Allen wrench.**

1. Remove the left and right chain cover. (The book says "refer to step 11.6"; this book has no chapter 11 - it means
   9-5 Side Case Replacement.)
2. Remove the **round disk cover** and use a **6 mm L Allen wrench** to release the **M8x40mm socket head cap bolt**
   fixed on the cross bar.
3. Use a **12 mm wrench** to remove the **5/16" x 10mm button head socket bolt** and **5/16" x 35 x 1.0T flat washer**,
   then take down the cross cranks.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **tapping screws** which secure the round disk and separate the
   cross bar and round disk from each other. (No count or size is printed; the CE800ENT book prints 8 x `5*16mm`
   with 8 x `1/4"*19` washers at the same step.)
5. When reassembling, place the cross bar into the **centre of the rotating mandrel**. Next place the **Woodruff key
   with the rounded part facing inwards**. Reassemble the remaining parts in the reverse order.

**A 12 mm wrench on a button head socket bolt is what the book prints.** A button head socket bolt takes an Allen
key; the XE795 books name a 5 mm L Allen wrench for this bolt. The step is quoted as printed.
