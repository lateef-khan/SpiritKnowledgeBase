**This is the 2017 XE890B-AE10M book's procedure. It differs from the CE800 (2020) / CE800ENT procedure in two
figures: the slide wheel goes back with a wave washer on each side of the wheel (two), where the later books say
"add 3 wave washers", and step 4's washer is printed `5/16" x 23 x 1.5T` here, where the later books print `15T`.**

1. First follow **procedure 2** (9-2, swing arm) to remove the handle bar. If you need the connecting bar only, just
   take apart the connection between the handle bar and the **fish-eye bearing**.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **pedal arm cover**, then release the **M6x15 m/m screws** and take
   off the **front covers**.
3. Use a hex-key wrench to remove the **5/16" x 3/4" hex head bolt** and **5/16" x 35 x 1.5T flat washer** of the pedal
   arm, to take down the pedal arm and connecting arm.
4. Use a **12 mm wrench** to release the **5/16" x 15 m/m hex head bolt** and **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** which
   secure the connecting arm. Then release the **carriage bolt** and take the connecting arm apart.
5. To remove the adjustable pedal set, use a Phillips head screwdriver and remove **four Phillips head screws
   M5x10mm**.
6. To take the slide wheel apart, use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **two Phillips head screws M5x15mm** and
   take the slide wheel cover off first.
7. Use circlip pliers to remove the **circlip Ø17** and remove the sliding wheels.
8. When you assemble the **Ø78 slide wheel, urethane**, build the stack in this order: **Ø17 x 0.5T wave washer, Ø78
   slide wheel (urethane), Ø17 x 0.5T wave washer, Ø17 C ring**.

**The wheel stack is the finding.** This book prints one wave washer on each side of the wheel and names the wheel
Ø78. The CE800 (2020) and CE800ENT books print "add 3 wave washers (Ø 17) to the slide wheel axle" for what
reads as the same axle. The two counts cannot both be right for one machine; count the washers you take off and
put back what came off.

The book prints no "reverse the steps" line for this section; step 8 is its last step.
