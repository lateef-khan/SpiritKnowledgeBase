**Five steps and four panels.** STEP ZERO has no hardware panel at all - it is one job
done with a wrench, and the two bolts it releases are already in the machine.

## Item 176 is printed twice in the step-one panel

Once as **2** flat washers and once as **4**, with the same part number and the same
size written two ways. **It is not a misprint of two different parts.** Job 1 of the
step uses two of them on the middle stabilizer bolts and job 3 uses four on the mast,
so the bag holds **six flat washers of item 176 in all**. Step three then supplies
eight more of the same item.

## Item 175 is a curved washer in the panel and a flat washer in the sentence

Step two job 1 says to tighten with "2 HEX HEAD BOLTS (136) and two **FLAT WASHERS**
(175)". The panel calls **175 a curved washer**, 3/8" x 30 x 2.0T, and gives the flat
washer as **180**, 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T - which job 2 uses on the connecting arm bolt.
**Trust the panel:** 175 is the curved washer that goes under the swing arm bolt, 180
the flat washer that goes under the connecting arm nut. The two are different
diameters and are not interchangeable.

## The four wave washers are named without a number

Step two job 1 says "together with 4 WAVE WASHERS" and never gives an item number. The
panel supplies **183, Ø25, quantity 4** - two per shaft. It is the only wave washer in
the book.

## What closes exactly

- **Step 1** closes on every row: 2 of item 137 with 2 of item 176; 4 of item 139 with
  4 of item 187; 4 of item 145 with 4 of item 186 and 4 more of item 176; 4 of item 150
  on the console.
- **Step 3** closes per side: 3 bolts, 4 flat washers, 2 curved washers and 3 nuts on
  each of two sides gives the panel's 6, 8, 4 and 6.
- **Step 4** closes across six jobs: 3 screws (152) a side on the connecting arm
  covers, 4 on the slide wheel covers and 4 on the rear stabilizer covers is
  **fourteen**; 4 sheet metal screws (153) a side on the handle bar covers is
  **eight**; 2 of item 154 on the console mast covers.
- **Step 2** closes as well - 2, 2, 2, 2, 2 and 4 against one bolt, washer and nut per
  side and two wave washers per shaft.

**Nothing is left over and nothing is short.** The XE195, XE295 and XE395 all leave
something in the bag or run out; every row of these four panels closes against its own
step text.

## Three of the four panels are the CE850's panels

Steps 1, 3 and 4 hold the same item numbers, sizes and quantities as the commercial
**CE850** - see
[that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ce850-assembly-hardware-kit.md) - down to item
152 being fourteen M5 x 15L screws and item 97's blank description cell. The CE850
prints step one's six flat washers of item 176 as one row of six; this book prints them
as two rows, of two and of four.

**Step two is the one that differs, and it differs in every fastener that matters:**

| | xe895-2018 | CE850 |
|---|---|---|
| 134 | Bolt, **5/16" X 1-1/4"** | Hex head bolt, **M10 x 1.5** |
| nut | **168**, Nyloc nut, 5/16" X 9T | **163**, Nyloc nut, M10 x 8T |
| 175 | **Curved washer**, 3/8"x30x2.0T | **Flat washer**, 3/8"x30x2.0T |
| 180 | Flat washer, 5/16"X20X1.5T, qty 2 | *not in the panel* |

Same job, same item number 134, a different bolt and a different nut. **Do not order a
step-two part across these two books.**

## No parts list to check any of this against

This manual prints no numbered parts list and no exploded view. The item numbers above
exist only in these four panels and the step text, and **they are this machine's own
numbering** - item 176 is a flat washer here and a button head socket bolt on the
XE195 and XE295, item 152 an M5 screw here and a split washer on the XE395. Do not
order across books.
