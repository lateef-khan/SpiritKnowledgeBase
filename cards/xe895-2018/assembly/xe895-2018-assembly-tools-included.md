The carton lists four tools, and nothing else:

- 13/14mm Wrench
- 12/14mm Wrench
- Phillips Screwdriver
- **8mm L Allen Wrench**

**Both wrenches are double ended**, so the carton covers 12, 13 and 14mm and gives you
two wrenches for the bolt-and-Nyloc-nut joints.

## This is four tools, but not the other XE's four tools

The XE195, XE295, XE395 2018, XE395ENT, XE795 and XG400 cartons list the same 13/14mm
wrench, 12/14mm wrench and Phillips screwdriver and then a **Combination M5 Allen
Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver**. This carton drops that tool and puts an **8mm L
Allen wrench** in instead. See
[the shared tools card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xe-assembly-tools-2021.md).

**That is the wrench the other builds ask for and do not supply.** The XE795 build
names an 8mm L wrench in its step three and the XG400 build an 8mm Allen wrench in its
step one, and neither carton holds one. This is the only residential XE carton in the
repository that does.

## The commercial CE850 carton lists the same four, and its build needs two more

The **CE850** 2016, 2020 and 2022 cartons list these four tools in this order, and its
build is otherwise this build - see
[the CE850 tools card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ce850-assembly-tools-included.md).
**The difference is what the steps ask for.** The CE850's step two calls for a **Wrench
(200)** and a **Wrench (201)** - an M12 L Allen wrench and a combination spanner -
neither of which is in its carton. **This book's step two does the same job with
wrenches 194 and 195, both of which are.**

## The step text calls the tools by item number, and the numbers are its own

| Tool | Item number in the steps |
|---|---|
| Allen wrench | **193** |
| Wrench | **194** |
| Wrench | **195** |
| Phillips head screw driver | **192** |

The steps never say which of 194 and 195 is the 13/14mm and which the 12/14mm; jobs
that need two wrenches at once name both. **None of these four numbers matches the tool
numbers in any other XE book** - the XE195, XE295 and XE395ENT call the 13/14mm wrench
155, the Phillips driver 157 and the 12/14mm wrench 158.

## Everything the build needs is in the box

Item 145's socket head cap bolts and the two bolts step zero releases take the 8mm
Allen key; everything else takes the two wrenches or the Phillips driver. **Nothing in
this build asks for a tool the carton does not hold**, which is unusual in this family.
