## Step zero - remove a cover and throw it away

Use the **ALLEN WRENCH (193)** to release the **2 BOLTS** on the mainframe that secure
the mast tube, and **remove and abandon the Side Back (28)**.

It has no hardware panel. Item 28 is named nowhere else in the book, so once it is off
there is nothing to put it back on with.

**The commercial CE850 opens the same way**, with the same wrench 193, the same two
bolts and the same Side Back 28 - see
[that build](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ce850-assembly-procedure.md). Those two are
the only Spirit elliptical builds that start at a step numbered zero, and the only ones
that tell you to throw a part away. **The CE850 card adds one thing this book does not
say: keep the two bolts**, because step one puts four of item 145 back in and only four
are bagged for six holes.

## Step one - middle stabilizer, console mast and console

1. Put the 2 **FLAT WASHERS (176)** on the 2 **HEX HEAD BOLTS (137)** and hand tighten
   them through the **TOP** of the **MIDDLE STABILIZER TUBE** into the **REAR RAIL
   ASSEMBLY (2)** with the **WRENCH (194)**.
2. Put 4 **CURVED WASHERS (187)** on the 4 **HEX HEAD BOLTS (139)** and hand tighten
   them through the **FRONT** of the middle stabilizer tube into the rear rail
   assembly with the same wrench.
3. Release the 2 socket head cap bolts with the **WRENCH (193)** and take the side back
   apart. Feed the **14P COMPUTER CABLE (68)** from the bottom of the mast tube through
   and out of the top, then secure with 4 **SOCKET HEAD CAP BOLTS (145)**, 4 **SPRING
   WASHERS (186)** and 4 **FLAT WASHERS (176)**.
4. Untie the **COMPUTER CABLE (68)** and connect the 2 **HANDPULSE W/CABLE ASSEMBLY
   (58)** and the upper handle wires **(61/62)** to the **CONSOLE ASSEMBLY (55)**. Put
   the console on top of the mast and secure it with 4 **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (150)**,
   driven with the **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREW DRIVER (192)**.

**Job 3 repeats step zero.** Both tell you to release two bolts with wrench 193 and
take the side back off. Do it once.

## Step two - lower swing arms and connecting arms

1. Put the **LEFT and RIGHT LOWER SWING ARMS (13, 14)** with **4 WAVE WASHERS** onto
   the left and right shafts of the mast tube, then tighten with 2 **HEX HEAD BOLTS
   (136)** and two washers **(175)** using the **WRENCH (194)**.
2. Untie the rod end bearing on the **LEFT CONNECTING ARM (8)**, push the **HEX HEAD
   BOLT (134)** through the rod end holes and the rod end bearing, then tighten with the
   **FLAT WASHER (180)** and **NYLOC NUT (168)** using **WRENCH (194)** and **WRENCH
   (195)**. Repeat for the **RIGHT CONNECTING ARM (9)** and the **RIGHT LOWER HANDLE
   BAR (14)**.

## Step three - upper swing arms, both of them wired

1. Insert the **LEFT UPPER SWING ARM (10)** onto the **LEFT LOWER SWING ARM (13)** and
   secure with 3 **HEX HEAD BOLTS (138)**, 4 **FLAT WASHERS (176)**, 2 **CURVED
   WASHERS (187)** and 3 **NYLOC NUTS (165)**, using wrenches 194 and 195. Repeat with
   the **RIGHT UPPER SWING ARM (11)** and the **RIGHT LOWER SWING ARM (14)**.
2. Connect the upper handle wires - **RESISTANCE (WHITE, 61)** and **STRIDE (RED, 62)**
   - to the 2 **HANDLE WIRES (LOWER), RESISTANCE/STRIDE (63)**, store the excess in the
   mast tube, and plug the **SWITCH WIRE CAPS** onto the mast tube to secure the wire.

## Step four - covers

1. **LEFT CONNECTING ARM COVER B (121)** on the left connecting arm with 1 **PHILLIPS
   HEAD SCREW (152)**, then **LEFT CONNECTING ARM COVER A (119)** with 2 screws (152)
   on the lower handle bar. Repeat with **RIGHT CONNECTING ARM COVER B (122)** and
   **RIGHT CONNECTING ARM COVER A (120)**.
2. Two **SLIDE WHEEL COVERS (99)** on the left and right pedal arms with 4 screws
   (152).
3. Two **REAR STABILIZER COVERS (A) (104)** and two **REAR STABILIZER COVER (B) (105)**
   on both sides of the rear tube of the rear rail assembly, with 4 screws (152).
4. **LEFT and RIGHT CONSOLE MAST COVERS (111, 112)** matched to the left and right side
   cases and secured with 2 **SHEET METAL SCREWS (154)**.
5. **FRONT HANDLE BAR COVER (100)** and **REAR HANDLE BAR COVER (101)** matched to each
   other on the left lower handle bar with 4 **SHEET METAL SCREWS (153)**; the same for
   the **RIGHT FRONT HANDLE BAR COVER (102)** and **REAR HANDLE BAR COVER (103)**.
   **Be careful not to pinch the wire.**
6. Plug both **OVAL END CAPS (89)** onto both ends of the **FRONT STABILIZER TUBE**.

## No step fits or adjusts a leveller

The carton list counts **6 Levelers** and the machine diagram labels them, and **none
of the five steps touches one**. The only levelling instruction in the whole book is
the general-maintenance sentence about **2 levelling pads under the rear stabiliser and
a 14mm wrench** - see
[that card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md). Every
other residential XE ends its build with a levelling job; this one does not.

## No step fits the foot pedals or the water bottle holder either

The carton list counts **2 Foot Pedals** and the machine diagram labels a **WATER
BOTTLE HOLDER**, and no job in this build installs either. The XE795 fits its pedals in
step six and its bottle cage in step five, and the XG400 fits both in step two.

## The red wire is called INCLINE once and STRIDE everywhere else

Step one job 4 names the pair "HANDLE WIRE (Upper), RESISTANCE/ **INCLINE**
(White/Red) (61/62)". Step three job 2 names the same red wire 62 **STRIDE**, and so
does every other page of the book - the console has a stride profile, a stride level
and a stride calibration, and the word *incline* appears **once in the whole manual**,
in that one sentence. **This machine has an adjustable stride and no incline.** Take
the step-three reading.

## This is the CE850 build with three real changes

Steps zero, three and four are word for word the **CE850** build, item number for item
number, and so is most of step one. Three things are genuinely different and must not
be carried across:

- **Step two's fasteners.** This book's rod-end joint takes a **5/16" x 1-1/4" hex head
  bolt (134)**, a **flat washer (180)** and a **5/16" x 9T nyloc nut (168)**, tightened
  with wrenches **194 and 195**. The CE850 takes an **M10 x 1.5 bolt (134)** and an
  **M10 x 8T nyloc nut (163)** with **no washer**, tightened with wrenches **200 and
  201** that are on no CE850 tools panel. Same item number 134, two different bolts.
- **Stride, not incline.** Where the CE850 wires an **Incline** cable, this book wires
  a **STRIDE** cable - see below.
- **The tube has a different name.** Step one bolts through the **MIDDLE STABILIZER
  TUBE**; the CE850 bolts through the **rear stabilizer tube (main frame)**. Same two
  jobs, same bolts, same wrench.

The CE850 2022 printing also adds a water bottle holder to step one. **No step in this
book fits one**, although the machine diagram shows one already fitted.

## The mast bolts are socket head cap bolts and the carton has the key for them

Step one job 3 drives 4 **SOCKET HEAD CAP BOLTS (145)** and step zero and job 3 both
use the **ALLEN WRENCH (193)**. Unlike the XE795 and XG400 builds, which name Allen
keys their cartons do not hold, **the XE895 carton ships an 8mm L Allen wrench** - see
[the tools card](xe895-2018-assembly-tools-included.md).
