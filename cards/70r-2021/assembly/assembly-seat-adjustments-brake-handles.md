**Two brake handles on the seat handlebar and one handle behind the seat.** The setting-up page and the assembly step disagree on which brake handle does what; both readings are below.

| Adjustment | Control | Repeatable by |
|---|---|---|
| **Fore / aft** | squeeze the **brake handle on the left side handle bar**, move the seat slightly until the seat lock clicks in place | numbered scale on the aluminum seat slide tube; the position is read where the front of the seat carriage lines up with the number |
| **Seat back angle** | squeeze the brake handle "located on the **left** side handle bar" and move the seat back | numbered scale just below the seat back cushion |
| **Swivel** | lift the **handle behind the seat** to disengage the latch, rotate, lower the handle when approaching the position to activate the latch | latches every **45 degrees** |

**The page says "left" for both brake handles.** Its own assembly step five, fitting the lever clamps (249) to the handle bar, says the levers are labelled Left and Right: **the left lever with the gray cable is for the fore/aft seat adjustment and the right lever with the black cable is for the seat back angle.** The step is the one that names the colours; take the right-hand, black-cable handle for the back angle. The seat back angle "can be adjusted for comfort, but also may be adjusted to change the hip angle for patients".

The same three sentences, brake handles included, are printed for the Spirit rehabilitation steppers (the 7.0S MED, 7.5S MED and MS300); that product line has its own card. The 2025 Spirit edition of the 7.0R replaced the brake handles with lift levers on the rotate seat assembly.
