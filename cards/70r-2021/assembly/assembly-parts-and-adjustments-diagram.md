Nine numbered items on a drawing headed "7.0R", under **Parts and adjustments**:

1. Electronic console
2. Manual mechanical brake
3. Pedal adjustment
4. A.C. Power input
5. Seat swivel adjustment
6. Hand pulse sensors
7. Seat back angle adjustment
8. Leveling glide
9. Seat angle adjustment

And under **Optional parts (not shown)**: **2.1 Neurological pedal set**.

The 2025 Spirit book's lettered diagram carries the first eight of these, A to H in the same order, and drops the ninth, "Seat angle adjustment" - on this edition the seat back angle and the seat angle are two adjustments. The optional neurological pedal set is named only here; the 2025 book lists adjustable crank arms instead. The 4.0R and 4.0U PT editions of the same period list an "adjustable crank set" as their option.
