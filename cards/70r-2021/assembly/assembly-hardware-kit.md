Three pages of captioned drawings headed **Hardware**, grouped by step; the captions give item number, size and count only.

## Step 1

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 65 | 3/8" x 2-1/4" | 4 |
| 77 | 3/8" x 3/4" | 8 |
| 71 | 3/8" x 2" | 4 |
| 84 | 3/8" x 1" | 4 |
| 175 | 3/8" x 2-3/4" | 2 |
| 205 | 8.5 mm x 26 mm | 2 |
| 208 | 5/16" x 1-1/4" | 1 |
| 89 | 3/8" | 6 |
| 213 | 5/16" | 1 |

**Eight washers (77), where the 2025 book pictures twelve** - the 2021 step fits four on the side bolts and four on the top bolts; the 2025 step fits eight on the top bolts. The gas-shock nut is item 213 here, 91 in the 2025 book.

## Step 2

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 216 | M6 x 6T | 2 |
| 221 | M6 x P1.0 x 40L | 2 |
| 83 | 5/16" x 3/4" | 4 |

The swivel seat release handle's hardware: two socket head cap bolts, two nuts, four curved washers. **The 2025 book's step two is six M8 x 30 bolts and six square nuts for an armrest** - a different seat mechanism.

## Step 3

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 220 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | 2 |
| 215 | 3/8" | 2 |
| 206 | 10 mm x 25 mm | 2 |

The seat-back nuts are item 215 here, 89 in the 2025 book.

## Step 4

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 68 | 5/16" x 5/8" | 8 |
| 82 | 5/16" | 2 |
| 76 | 5/16" x 3/4" | 6 |

Word for word the 2025 book's step-four panel. Item 83, the curved washer, is pictured in step 2 (four) and step 4 (two) - six in the kit.

## Step 5

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 99 | M5 x 12L | 8 |
| 222 | M6 x 25L | 4 |

The lever clamps (249) of step five arrive with their own Allen screws and are not pictured here. **No torque figure is printed anywhere.** The tools are on the next two pages - the tools card.
