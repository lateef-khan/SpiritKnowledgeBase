**The last two pages of the Dyaco MED 7.0R book are a symbol legend, and they are where the book
declares its medical-device class.** The 2025 Spirit printings of the 7.0R, 7.0U, 8.0U and 8.5R print
no such page.

| Symbol, as described | Meaning printed beside it |
|---|---|
| two figures lifting | Indicates that the package is heavy and two or more people are required to lift. |
| broken glass | Package contains fragile electronic equipment. Care should be used when handling. |
| dropped box | The product inside the packaging could be easily damaged if dropped or handled without care and attention. The contents are fragile! |
| crossed forklift | Do not use forklift truck here. |
| crossed spade truck | Do not use Spade truck here. |
| crossed razorback truck | Do not use Razorback truck here. |
| hand truck | Hand truck only. |
| recycling loop | Indicates that an object is capable of being recycled - not that the object has been recycled or will be accepted in all recycling collection systems. |
| arrows up | Top. This side up. |
| plug | **AC power 90-240 Volt, 15Amps, 50Hz** |
| CE 0123 | **Product certificate logo, CE MDD 93/42/EEC Class Im** |
| open book | read this entire manual carefully before operating your new 7.0R |
| stick figure | **Type B applied parts.** This is used for parts applied to the patient that require only ordinary electrical protection, e.g. headphones. |
| open book, second form | Follow Instructions for use |

The page is stamped `Rev. 1.2.1 2021/10/14 EN`.

- **Class Im under the Medical Device Directive 93/42/EEC**, with **0123** as the notified body
  number printed under the CE mark - the same class and body the specification page's
  CERTIFICATIONS block names (`MDD 93/42/EEC Class Im`, with `0123` beside the CE mark). The 2025
  Spirit 7.0U prints `MDD 93/42/EEC Class Im, NB: 0123` on its specification page and the 8.0U and
  8.5R `EU MDR 2017/745 Class I`; those are specification statements and are not on this card.
- **Type B applied parts** is the IEC 60601-1 category with the lowest patient-protection
  requirement; the manual's own gloss is `ordinary electrical protection, e.g. headphones`. The
  parts in patient contact are `hands, feet, and trunk`
  (`spirit-med-bike-safety-intended-patient-population-and-medical-purpose`).
- **The plug symbol says 50 Hz and 15 amps.** The safety page asks for a 90 to 240 volt, 50/60 Hz,
  15-amp outlet and the specification page rates the machine at 1.76-0.71 A; the legend's 15 amps
  is the circuit figure and its 50 Hz drops the 60
  (`spirit-med-bike-safety-outlet-90-to-240-volt-15-amp`).
- **Two or more people to lift** is the only handling instruction in the book; the assembly chapter
  gives no weight for the carton, and the specification page gives 180.1 lb (81.7 kg) for the bike.

**The rehabilitation steppers print the same legend** with 100-240 volts
(`spirit-med-stepper-safety-packaging-symbols-and-the-ce-mark`), and the MS300 a version with
template defects (`ms300-2021-safety-packaging-symbols-and-template-defects`). Those are climbers
with their own cards.
