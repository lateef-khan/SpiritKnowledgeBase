| Figure | Printed |
|---|---|
| **Length** | **57" (145cm)** |
| **Width** | **30" (77cm)** |
| **Height** | **51" (130cm)** |
| **Weight** | **180.1 Lbs. (81.7 Kgs)** |

The inch and centimetre figures agree with each other to the rounding the page uses.

**The 2025 Spirit book for the same machine prints 55.1 x 32.7 x 46.9 inches (140 x 83 x 119 cm)
and the identical weight** (`70r-2025-specs-dimensions-and-weight`). The two books were set from the
same template - the same power, fuse, resistance and certification text - and neither explains the
change; the 2021 book is the Dyaco "MED" edition, Rev. 1.2.1 of October 14, 2021, CE-marked under the
MDD. Quote the figures from the book that matches the machine's labelling, and say the other exists.

No shipping weight or carton size is printed. The 2021 book also prints a lower work load than the
2025 one (`70r-2021-specs-workload-5-to-750-watts`).
