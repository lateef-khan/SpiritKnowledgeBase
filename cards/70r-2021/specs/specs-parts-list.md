**This is the 2021 Dyaco MED edition's list (Rev. 1.2.1, 2021/10/14), not the 2025 Spirit list.** The two share most item numbers but differ in 52 rows, the 2025 list adds 17 rows this one does not have, and this one prints 23 rows the 2025 list drops. Order by the book in hand.

217 rows are printed and the numbers run 1 to 307 with 98 whole numbers absent. **No Dyaco or Spirit part number is printed.** The console is item 19 with seven sub-parts printed 19-01 to 19-07 (the 2025 list numbers the same seven 19-1 to 19-7).

Rows the 2025 list adds and this one lacks: 308, 314, 315, 316, 317, 318, 321, 322, 330, 331, 332, 333, 334, 335, 336, 337, 338 - the transportation wheel fixing plate, the second release lever and nylon handgrips, the seat front/aft adjustment lever, the `Steel Cable(340L)` and the `Seat Belt`. Rows in this list the 2025 one drops: 40, 41, 104, 109, 110, 117, 131, 167, 202, 209, 213, 215, 216, 221, 225, 230, 231, 239, 249, 295, 296, 298, 307 - among them 40 `Rotate Seat Adjusting Lever`, 41 `Adjusting Lever`, 117 `Pedal(R)`, 131 the adjusting-lever rotate axle, 249 a second release lever, and the 20x-23x fasteners.

**The renamings matter at a parts desk.** Item 55 is an `Induction Brake` here and a `Flywheel` in 2025; 236 is a `Power Adaptor` here and a `Switching Power Supply` there; 238 `AC Electronic Module` became `AC Input Module`; 250 `Brake Pad - Wool Felt` became `Brake Pad`; pedals are 116 `Pedal (L)` and 117 `Pedal (R)` here and one row `Pedal(L.R)` at 116 there. Quantities also move on the washers and bolts (68, 77, 83, 127, 166).

**The 2025 service manual carries this list invisibly.** The MR490-SB018-03 service manual's parts pages print the 2025 list, but the text layer under them is this 2021 list word for word - a PDF extraction of that book returns these rows, not the printed ones (`70r-2025-specs-parts-list`).
