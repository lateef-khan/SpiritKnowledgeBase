**Work load - 5 watts up to 750 watts**, and the features page agrees: "Work range up to 750 watts
(60 RPM) when level settings are at 15 watts per level." Fifty levels at the coarse 15 W scale is
750 W, so in this book the figure is the console's ceiling at 60 RPM.

**The 2025 Spirit book for the same machine raised the specification row to 1700 W and kept the
750 W features sentence** (`spirit-med-70-bike-specs-workload-5-to-1700-watts`). A 7.0R labelled as
the Dyaco MED edition is documented at 750 W; do not carry 1700 W back onto it, and do not carry 750 W
forward as the rating of a 2025 machine.

The brake and its fifty levels are `spirit-med-70-bike-specs-eddy-current-brake-and-fifty-levels`;
the 2021 book's dimensions also differ from the 2025 book's (`70r-2021-specs-dimensions-and-weight`).
