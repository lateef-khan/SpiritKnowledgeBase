Seven numbered items on a drawing headed "4.0 R-Recumbent bike", under **Parts and adjustments**:

1. Console
2. Pedal
3. Hand pulse sensors
4. Seat fore/aft adjustment
5. Seatback angle adjustment
6. Bottle holder
7. Leveling glide

And under **Optional parts (not shown)**: **2.1 Adjustable crank set**.

**This is the Dyaco "PT" edition of the 4.0R** (an undated user manual, February 2016 PDF, sold under the Dyaco cover). It names two adjustments and the levelling glide that the 2025 Spirit book's lettered diagram does not, and does not name the tablet rack, console upright, side rail, cup holders, crank arm or rear stabilizer that the Spirit diagram does. The optional adjustable crank set appears only here - neither the 2025 book nor the service manual mentions one for this machine, although the 7.0R and 8-series medical bikes list adjustable crank arms as an option.
