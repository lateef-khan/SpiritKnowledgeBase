**Five steps and no locking-pin step.** The XE295 book's connecting arm adds an adjustable-pedal locking pin (step
4 there); this arm has none.

1. First follow **procedure 2** (11-2) to remove the handle bar. If you need the connecting bar only, just take apart
   the connection between the handle bar and the **fish-eye bearing**.
2. Use **12 mm and 13 mm wrenches** to release the **5/16" x 1-1/4" hex head screw**, **5/16" x 20 x 1.5T flat
   washer** and **5/16" x 9T nut** which secure the rod end bearing.
3. Use a **12 mm wrench** to release the **5/16" x 15 m/m hex head screw** and **5/16" x 20 x 1.5T flat washer** which
   secure the connecting arm, then release the **carriage bolt under the pedal** to take the connecting arm apart.
4. Release **four M5x10mm Phillips head screws** which secure the pedal, to take the pedal from the connecting arm.
5. Reverse the above steps to refit the parts.

Step 2's nut is printed **9T** here; the swing arm section of the same book removes a **7T** nyloc nut from what
reads as the same rod-end joint. Quoted as printed.
