**The slide wheel on this machine comes off by pulling a bolted axle, and goes back with one wave washer.** The
XE295 / XE395 books hold the wheel with a `Ø17` circlip and a wave washer on each side; do not carry that stack
here.

1. Follow **procedure 3** (11-3) to take apart the connecting arm.
2. Disassemble the connecting arm first and use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **M5x10mm Phillips head
   screw** to take apart the **pedal arm cover**.
3. Use an **M8 hex wrench and a #14 wrench** to remove the **gap socket screw 3/8" x 2-1/4"**, **two flat washers
   3/8" x 19 x 1.5T** and **nyloc nut 3/8" x 11T** connecting the **inclinable rail assembly** and **rotating block**,
   to remove the inclinable rail assembly.
4. Use a **#12 hex wrench** to remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 15mm** and **flat washer 5/16" x 35 x 1.5T**, to
   remove the **bushing housing, pedal arm**.
5. To take the slide wheel apart, use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **two Phillips head screws M5x15mm** and
   take the slide wheel cover off first.
6. Use a **12 mm wrench** to release the **5/16" x 3/4" hex head screw x 1** with **5/16" x 20 x 1.5T flat washer x 1**
   and pull out the **slide wheel axle** to take the slide wheel apart.
7. To refit, **put a wave washer on the slide wheel**, slide in the wheel axle and tighten the **5/16" x 3/4" hex head
   screw x 1** with the **5/16" x 20 x 1.5T flat washer x 1**.
