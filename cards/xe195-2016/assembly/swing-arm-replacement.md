**Three steps, no wiring and no upper/lower split.** The XE295 and XE395 books add a wiring disconnect and a
three-bolt upper-to-lower joint; this book does not have them.

1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **sheet metal screws 3.5x12mm** securing the swing arm covers (front and
   back).
2. Use a **12 mm open end wrench** to remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 15mm** and **flat washer 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T**
   securing the swing arm assembly.
3. Use **#12 and #13 open end wrenches** to hold the swing arm assembly and remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x
   1-1/4"**, **flat washer 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T** and **nyloc nut 5/16" x 7T**. Remove the swing arm assembly.

The book prints no reassembly line for this section.
