**Plastic parts (p. 5):** Console Assembly, Handle Bar Cover (L) and (R), Console Mast Cover, Side
Case (L) and (R), Pedal Arm Cover (R), Round Disk Cover, Round Disk, Pedal (R) and (L), Rear
Stabilizer Cover (R) and (L), **Middle Stabilizer Cover**, Slide Wheel Cover.

**Steel parts (p. 6):** Swing Arm (L) and (R), Console Mast, Drive Pulley, Pedal Arm (L) and (R),
Cross Bar, Flywheel, Bushing Housing, Pedal Arm, Rear Rail Assembly (labelled twice, once per
rail), and **Pedal (L) three times** - on the left pedal, on the left pedal arm's end, and on the
right pedal - a labelling slip; the right one is Pedal (R) on the plastic-parts page.

The *Round Disk* is the crank disc behind the side case. The **Middle Stabilizer Cover** is the
one label the XE295's drawing does not have; the XE295 adds a Swing Arm Bushing, lower handle
bars and adjustable pedals instead (`xe295-2016-specs-outline-part-names`). The Outlines chapter
prints **no dimensions, no machine weight and no user weight limit**, and no part numbers.
