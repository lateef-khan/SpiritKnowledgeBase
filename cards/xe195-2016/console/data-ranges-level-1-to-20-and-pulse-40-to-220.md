| Reading | Display range | Work range | Notes as printed |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPEED | 0.0 to 99.9 | 0.0~99.9 | "the current speed in mile per hour" |
| LEVEL | 0 to 999 | **1 to 20** | "the level position from 0 to 20"; preset 1 to 20; "Press UP or DOWN to adjust incline, each increment and decrement is 1" |
| TIME | 0:00 to 99:99 | 0:00 to 99:59 | count up by default, count down once a time is set; count-down setup range **10:00 to 99:00**; Stop in Run Mode saves the value and the count continues on re-entry |
| LAPS | 0 to 99 | 0 to 99 | total working laps |
| DISTANCE | 00.0 to 99.9 | 00.0 to 99.9 | in miles |
| CALORIES | 00.0 to 999 | 00.0 to 999 | cumulative |
| PULSE | 0 to 999 | **40 to 220 BPM** | from the hand pulse or the receiver, which needs a chest belt; with no signal for **8 seconds** the value becomes 0 |

**"Adjust incline" is the spec's phrase for the resistance level**; the XE195 has no incline, and its
LCD's incline window is unused
(`spirit-xe-2016-console-lcd-layout-calories-time-distance-incline-level-and-a-ten-character-message-window`).

**One to twenty here; zero to twenty on the XE295, XE795 and XG400 of the same year**
(`spirit-xe-2016-console-data-ranges-level-0-to-20-and-pulse-40-to-220`). The same split runs
through the 2016 bike books - the XBR25 and XBU55 count 1 to 20
(`spirit-xbr25-xbu55-2016-console-data-ranges-level-1-to-20`).

**Twenty levels agrees with the owner's manuals** of the 2019 and 2021 XE195
(`spirit-xe-console-quick-start-with-twenty-resistance-levels`); the ranges, the 8-second pulse
drop-out and the count-down limits are printed only here.
