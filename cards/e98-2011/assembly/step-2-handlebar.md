1. (No numbered step 1 is printed.)
2. **Install two Washers (163)** onto the left and right sides of the Handle Bar axle. **Slide the Left (10) and Right (11) Handle Bars** on; the small stickers mark L and R.
3. **Install two Flat Washers (160) onto the two Hex Head Bolts (116)** in the threaded holes in the ends of each axle and tighten.
4. **Install the Handle Bar Covers (91 to 92) and (93 to 94)** over the Console Mast axle connections with the **eight Sheet Metal Screws (132)**.
