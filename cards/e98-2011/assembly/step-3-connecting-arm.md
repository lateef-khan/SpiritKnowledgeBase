**Untie the wire holding the Sleeve Spacer (19)** in place on the rod-end bushings at the ends of the **Connecting Arms (8 & 9)**. Align the rod-end holes with the holes in the brackets of the left and right **Handle Bars (10 & 11)**, rod ends inside the brackets. Secure both sides with a **Hex Head Bolt (114)**, **Flat Washer (154)** and **Nyloc Nut (143)** using **Wrenches (169 & 172)**.
