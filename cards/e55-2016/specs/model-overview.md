The manual's cover code is **SE585S-SE018**. Section 1 is titled "SE585S-SE018 Outlines" and the table of contents calls it the "SE585S Elliptical". The circuit diagram page is also headed SE585S-SE018.

This is the Sole E55 of model year 2016. The Spirit database SKU taken for it is **555016**.

The machine has a console with a **9" LCD display**, a main controller (DC power supply, incline driver and tension motor driver), a **tension motor** that changes brake resistance, and a **115 volt AC incline motor**.

The parts drawings name: console, console chin cover (rear), pedal arm covers L and R, handle bar cover, side case (L), console mast cover, round disk and round disk cover, pedals L and R, front stabilizer cover, incline cover, inclinable rail cover, rear bar cover, middle stabilizer cover, incline bottom cover and slide wheel cover; left and right handle bars (top), console holder assembly, console mast, main frame, lower handle bar (L), drive pulley, cross bar, bushing housing (pedal arm), idler bracket, adjustable pedal, rear rail assembly, flywheel, connecting arm (R), inclinable rail assembly, pedal arm (R) and incline motor.
