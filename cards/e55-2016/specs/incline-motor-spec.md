This is a **115 volt AC motor**. It has four wires: red, black, white and green, plus one 3-pin position sensor cable.

- AC voltage on the **red** wire (UP) makes the incline motor increase the incline.
- AC voltage on the **black** wire (DOWN) makes the incline motor decrease the incline.
- The **white** wire (COM) is neutral.
- The **green** wire is ground.

The position sensor wires are red = ground, white = position signal, black = 5vdc, with the signal running 0~5v depending on position.

This manual gives **no zeroing distance** for a replacement incline motor and no barrel rotation count. The E35, E95 and E98 manuals of the same year print 207 +/- 1mm between the two hole sites; do not apply that figure here without confirming it on the machine.
