To make assembly easier for consumers there is a minor difference in size, **0.05mm~0.07mm**. A little shake or wobble is therefore **normal**. After prolonged use small parts wear by friction and generate noise.

1. The solution is to add **thick lubricant into the top of the swing arm axle**.
2. If the noise comes from the bottom bracket, the **6203 bearings** need to be replaced.

Note the bearing number: this manual says 6203. The E35, E95 and E98 manuals of the same year name a **6005** bearing at the console mast shaft instead.
