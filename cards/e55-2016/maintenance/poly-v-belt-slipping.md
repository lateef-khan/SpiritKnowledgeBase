If the Poly-V belt is slipping, **adjust the cap on the hook-type screw with a #13 wrench**. The manual sends you to the disassembly procedures for the adjustment itself.

The manual does not repeat the tension figure here. The target is **190 Hz plus or minus 10 Hz on an acoustic gauge**; that number is printed only in the Poly-V belt replacement procedure and has its own card.

The manual sends you to disassembly procedure step 6 for the adjustment itself. Unlike the E25, E35, E95 and E98 manuals of the same year, this section does not add a fourth item telling you to replace a worn or damaged belt.
