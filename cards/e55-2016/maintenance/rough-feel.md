Check for loose screws if the machine motion feels unsmooth. Check whether there are foreign objects on or around the sliding rail; use an **alcohol wipe** to remove them and **re-lubricate with lubricant**.

Section 9-7 adds the mechanical reason: slide wheels moving parallel and freely on the rail is the key factor, which is affected by the pedal arm being parallel to the rail and perpendicular to the bearing housing.
