1. **Install the Incline Rail Assembly (2) into the U-channel bracket of the Main Frame (1).** From the sides install **two Hex Head Bolts (128)** with **two Flat Washers (171)** and **two Nylon Nuts (158)**. From the top install **four Hex Head Bolts (124)**, **four Split Washers (176)**, **four Flat Washers (166)**, and **four Star Washers (177)**. Tighten with **Wrenches (186 & 189)**.
2. **Connect the 3 wire harness (43)** to the female receiver from the Incline Rail Assembly (2). Connect the **Three pin position Sensor connector (44)** the same way.
3. **Slide the Console Mast Cover (95) onto the Console Mast (12).** Tie the twist tie to the **Computer Cable (45)** at the top opening of the Main Frame, pull it up through the mast until it exits the top, and install the mast into the receiving bracket. **Pull slightly on the cable at the top while installing** so it does not get pinched and shorted.
4. **Put one Split Washer (175) onto the Long Hex Head Bolt (129)** through the left side of the receiving bracket, and **two Curved Washers (179) onto the two Short Hex Head Bolts (127)** through the front. Using **Wrench (189)**, tighten the three bolts plus the pre-installed fourth firmly, as much as you possibly can.
5. **Plug the connectors into the back of the console: Computer Cable (45), two Hand pulse Cables (52, 53), Resistance switch wire (55) and Incline switch wire (56).** Secure the **Console (40)** with **four Phillips Head Screws (140)**.
