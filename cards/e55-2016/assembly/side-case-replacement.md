1. Remove the swing arm and inclinable rail assembly.
2. Separate the console mast cover by gently pulling back with your hands; separate the left and right cover.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **4x19 sheet metal screws** and **5x16 tapping screws** which secure the right side case, and take it apart.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **4x15 sheet metal screws**, **1/4" x 19 flat washers** and **5x16 tapping screws** which secure the left side case on the mainframe. Disconnect the ground wire and controller connecting wire from the AC power switch.
5. On reinstallation, connect the red jumpers and the grounding back on the AC electronic module.
6. Return the left side case, with one **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** on it, onto the main frame. Use a 4x15 self tapping screw with a 1/4" x 19 flat washer to hold it, not too tight at first, then tighten with 3 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws, making sure the side case matches the round disk. Tighten the 4x15 self tapping screws.
7. Match both side cases with each other and use **7 pcs of 4x19 self tapping screws** and **3 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws** to secure them.
8. Reverse the above to refit the pedal arm and connecting arm.

**Step 4 disagrees with itself in the source.** It says the screws "secure left Side Case on the Mainframe" and then ends "take right Side Case apart", after step 3 has already removed the right side case. Work out which case you are on from the screws in front of you.
