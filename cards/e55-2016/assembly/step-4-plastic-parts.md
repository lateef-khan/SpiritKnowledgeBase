Use screwdriver (187 or 188) for the plastic parts.

1. **Match the right and left Connecting Arm Covers (92, 93)** on the left Connecting Arm and fix with **two Phillips Head Screws (138)** and **one Sheet Metal Screw (145)** using the Short driver (187). Do the right arm the same way.
2. **Sliding Wheel Covers (94)**, each side with **four Phillips Head Screws (138)**.
3. **Stabilizer Covers (115 and 116)** on the middle stabilizer bar with **two Phillips Head Screws (138)**.
4. **Front Stabilizer Cover (110)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (138)**.
5. **Incline Cover brackets (21)**, bent-tab hole facing rearward, on the Incline Rail Assembly (2) with **four Phillips Head Screws (146)**; **Rear incline bar cover (113)** with **two Screws (138)**.
6. **Rear Stabilizer Cover (114)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (138)**.
7. **Plug in the power cord. Raise the incline 3-8 levels** with the Incline button on the console, then attach the **Incline Bottom Cover (111)** on both sides of the Incline Rail Assembly with **two Phillips Head Screws (138)**.
8. **Console covers (104 & 105)** with **three Phillips Head Screws (138)** and **two Sheet Metal Screws (145)**.
9. **Handle Bar Covers (106 & 107, 108 & 109)** with **four Sheet Metal Screws (145)**.
