1. Follow 11-2 and 11-3 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, left and right side cases, cross bar and the belt.
2. Use a **12 mm wrench** to release the **5/16" x 15 m/m hex head bolt** with the **5/16" x 35 x 1.5T flat washer**; the bushing housing is then released.
3. To reassemble, be sure the **25 wave washer** is returned onto the shaft, then return the bushing housing onto the shaft and tighten with the 5/16" x 15 m/m hex head bolt and 5/16" x 35 x 1.5T flat washer.
4. Follow 11-3 ~ 11-4 and 11-2 ~ 11-5 to return the pedal arm and connecting arm.

**The heading and the steps disagree.** The section is headed "Drive Pulley Replacement" and the table of contents calls 11.9 "Bushing Housing Replacement", but every step removes and refits the bushing housing. No drive pulley part is named.
