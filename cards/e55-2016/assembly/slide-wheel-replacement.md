1. To take the slide wheel apart, use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **two Phillips head screws M5x15mm** and take off the slide wheel cover first.
2. Use **circlip pliers** to remove **circlip 17** and remove the sliding wheels.
3. Reverse the above steps to refit the pedal arm, and return the **wavy washers 17** to the left and right slide wheels.
