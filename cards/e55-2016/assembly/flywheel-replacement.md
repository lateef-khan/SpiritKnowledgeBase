1. Follow 11-2, 11-3, 11-4, 11-5, 11-6 and 11-7 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, both side covers, cross bar, the belt and the idler wheel assembly.
2. **Adjust the resistance to 20 with the console** and release the steel cable.
3. Use a **#15 open end combination spanner wrench** to loosen the **nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 11T** on the flywheel and mainframe. Remove the flywheel and belt.
4. To reassemble, return the belt onto the grooves on the flywheel and return the flywheel onto the mainframe.
5. Tighten both **3/8"-UNF26 x 9T nuts**.
6. Return the steel cable onto the flywheel **at 45 degrees**, then return the idler wheel assembly.
7. Turn the drive pulley and check the belt is secured. Adjust both **3/8"-UNF26 x 4T nuts** to position the belt in the middle of the drive pulley, then tighten all other screws. Adjust the belt to a proper tension.
8. Follow 11-7, 11-6, 11-5, 11-4, 11-3 and 11-2 to return the other parts.

**Note the nut sizes.** Step 3 loosens an **11T** nut and step 5 tightens **9T** nuts. The E25 manual of the same year prints 9T in both places. Check the nut on the machine before ordering.
