**Five settings, 11 lb apart, one dumbbell:**

| LB | 11 | 22 | 33 | 44 | 55 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| KG | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |

"Checking the chart below, note the 5 weight increments from 11 LB to 55 LB or 5 KG to 25 KG." The steps are
metric-round - 5 kg each - which is why the pound figures are 11, 22, 33, 44, 55 rather than round pounds. The
carton holds one handle, one dumbbell tray and **eight weight plates**, each "2.5Kg" in the parts list. The weight
is chosen by rotating the middle of the handle grip with the handle fully seated in its tray; the unused plates
stay in the base.

As on the 25 lb book, the pack-list page's text layer carries the 12.5 lb pair's chart figures, unprinted and not
this dumbbell's. No total weight or dimension is printed; the base is "406*210*47" in the parts list
(`xterra-strength-specs-no-specification-table-in-the-dumbbell-and-stand-manuals`). Siblings: the 12.5 lb pair
(`adb125pr-2022-specs-five-weight-settings-2-5-to-12-5-lb-per-hand-with-sixteen-plates`) and the 25 lb dumbbell
(`adb25-2022-specs-five-weight-settings-5-to-25-lb-with-eight-plates`); the cradle mounts on the stand with the
longer M8 x 35 bolts (`dbstand-2022-specs-holds-only-xterra-adjustable-dumbbell-cradles-and-prints-no-load-rating`).
