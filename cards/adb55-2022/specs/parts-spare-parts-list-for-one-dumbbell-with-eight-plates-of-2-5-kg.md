The 55 LB Adjustable Dumbbell manual prints an **Exploded View** (PDF p. 9, printed 7) and a **55LB SPARE PARTS LIST** (PDF p. 10, printed 8) headed *S/N / DESCRIPTION / SPEC / QTY*. **The quantities are for one dumbbell**: one handle (20), one base (22), eight plates (21) of 2.5 kg. The list runs 1 to 22 with **no item 12** and the spiral sleeves split left and right (17L/17R), so it has 22 rows. The exploded view is rotated artwork whose callouts the text layer does not carry; they were read from a render and match the list. The *Spec* column is the book's - millimetres for the plastic and steel parts, thread sizes for the fasteners, the plate mass in kilograms.

**What the list tells a service call.** The plates are item 21, eight of 2.5 kg, so the dumbbell adjusts in five steps from 11 to 55 lb; the two clump weights are item 3, the selector dial item 14, the locks item 11 with square springs item 10 and ball plungers item 13, the base (tray) item 22, 406 x 210 x 47 mm. The 12.5 lb pair and the 25 lb dumbbell print their own lists with different numbering (`adb125pr-2022-specs-parts-spare-parts-list-for-the-pair-with-sixteen-plates`, `adb25-2022-specs-parts-spare-parts-list-for-one-dumbbell-with-eight-plates-of-1-1-kg`); the stand is `dbstand-2022-specs-parts-spare-parts-list-fourteen-items`.
