Three lines on the page: **Handle x 2**, **Dumbbell Tray x 2**, **Weight Plate x 16** - eight plates per dumbbell, the same count as the single 25 lb and 55 lb dumbbells. Nothing is bolted.

The text layer of this page also carries dial figures ("2.5 12.5", "1.13 5.57" and so on) that are artwork of the handle dial, not a table; the printed increments are on the weight-selection card and the specs card.
