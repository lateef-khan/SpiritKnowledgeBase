**Five settings per dumbbell, 2.5 lb apart:**

| LB | 2.5 | 5 | 7.5 | 10 | 12.5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| KG | 1.13 | 2.27 | 3.4 | 4.5 | 5.57 |

"Checking the chart below, note the 5 weight increments from 2.5 LB to 12.5 LB or 1.13 KG to 5.57 KG." This is a
**pair**: the carton holds two handles, two dumbbell trays and **sixteen weight plates** (eight per dumbbell), each
"0.55Kg" in the parts list. The weight is chosen "by rotating the middle of the handle grip" with the handle fully
seated in its tray; lifting the handle leaves the unused plates in the base (the instructions and the one
troubleshooting row are the assembly and errors sections').

The book prints no total weight and no dumbbell dimension; the only size figure is the base, "308*122*40" in the
parts list (`xterra-strength-specs-no-specification-table-in-the-dumbbell-and-stand-manuals`). The single-dumbbell
siblings step 5 lb (`adb25-2022-specs-five-weight-settings-5-to-25-lb-with-eight-plates`) and 11 lb
(`adb55-2022-specs-five-weight-settings-11-to-55-lb-with-eight-plates`), and their cradles mount on the same stand
(`dbstand-2022-specs-holds-only-xterra-adjustable-dumbbell-cradles-and-prints-no-load-rating`).
