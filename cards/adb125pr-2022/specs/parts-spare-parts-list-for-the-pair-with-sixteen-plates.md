The 12.5 LB Adjustable Dumbbell Pair manual prints an **Exploded View** (PDF p. 9, printed 7) and a **12.5LB SPARE PARTS LIST** (PDF p. 10, printed 8) headed *S/N / DESCRIPTION / SPEC / QTY*. **The quantities are for the pair**: two handles (18), two bases (20), sixteen plates (19) of 0.55 kg, four of every handle-end part. The list runs 1 to 20 with **no item 9** and the spiral sleeves and locating rings split left and right (6L/6R, 12L/12R), so it has 21 rows. The exploded view is rotated artwork whose callouts the text layer does not carry; they were read from a render and match the list. The *Spec* column is the book's - millimetres for the plastic and steel parts, thread sizes for the fasteners, the plate mass in kilograms.

**What the list tells a service call.** The plates are item 19 - sixteen of them, 0.55 kg each, so each dumbbell carries eight and adjusts in five steps from 2.5 to 12.5 lb; the selector dial is item 13 (*Handle inner cover*), the locks item 11 with their square springs item 10 and ball plungers item 8, the base (dumbbell tray) item 20, 308 x 122 x 40 mm. The single 25 lb and 55 lb dumbbells print their own lists with different numbering and eight plates each (`adb25-2022-specs-parts-spare-parts-list-for-one-dumbbell-with-eight-plates-of-1-1-kg`, `adb55-2022-specs-parts-spare-parts-list-for-one-dumbbell-with-eight-plates-of-2-5-kg`) - a number from this list does not carry across. The stand the three sit on has a fourteen-row list (`dbstand-2022-specs-parts-spare-parts-list-fourteen-items`).
