**The interface is divided into two sections, switched with the tab bar along the bottom of the
screen** - before, during or after a workout:

- **Training** - workout data and controls. Its main screen carries a **ranking list** and the most
  common workout choices: an immediate start, a timed workout, and popular workout patterns.
- **Media** - the entertainment options: `cu800ent-2024-console-media-tab-and-mirroring`.

**A workout is started either by the Start button on the screen or by the physical Start key.**

**Five icons sit in the status bar across the top:**

| Icon | What pressing it does |
|---|---|
| **WiFi** | Opens **WiFi settings** |
| **Gear** | Changes the **language** or the **units of measurement** |
| **GARMIN** logo | Pairs a Garmin device - `cu800ent-2024-console-garmin-pairing` |
| **Bluetooth** | Pairs a Bluetooth device - `cu800ent-2024-console-bluetooth-pairing` |
| **Heart Rate** | Pairs a heart rate measuring device |

**Language and units are behind the Gear icon on this console, not behind a Language tile.** The
2022 and 2023 ENT bikes have a **Language** tile on the Home Screen and print a count of **13**
languages; this manual prints no count and no tile:
`ct800ent-2022-console-language-selection`.

**The manual prints no procedure for the WiFi icon and none for the Heart Rate icon** - only that
pressing them opens those settings. Default language, WiFi and units can also be set from
maintenance mode: `cu800ent-2024-console-maintenance-mode-and-engineering-menu`.

**These are not the LED status icons of the 2023 XT range.** Those are four fixed indicators down the
left of a workout screen - USB charge, Bluetooth speaker, Bluetooth heart rate, Bluetooth on/off -
and nothing there is pressable: `xt-2023-console-status-icons`.

**The four 2024 ENT machines print this section word for word too**, so this card covers a treadmill
and an elliptical as well as bikes and its product line is no longer bike-only. Measured on native
PDF text, the whole TOUCHSCREEN OPERATION chapter of the CT800ENT-2024, CT850ENT-2024, CE800ENT-2024
and CR800ENT-2024 matches the CU800ENT-2024's **97.8% to 99.5%** word for word; every difference is a
page number or the machine's own name.

Locators: CT800ENT-2024 and CT850ENT-2024 p. 22, CE800ENT-2024 and CR800ENT-2024 p. 20, all under
**TOUCHSCREEN OPERATION - CONTINUED / Basic Operation & Home Page**. Same two tabs, same five icons in
the same order, same ranking list, and the same sentence that a workout starts either from the Start
button on the screen or from the physical Start key.

**The CE800ENT-2024 alone shortens one sentence.** Where the other four say All Programs opens "the
preset programs **and fitness tests**", the elliptical says only "the preset programs". It prints
fitness tests elsewhere in the book; the Programs sentence simply omits them.
