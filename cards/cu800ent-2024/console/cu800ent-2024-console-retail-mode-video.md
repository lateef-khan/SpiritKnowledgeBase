**In maintenance mode you can upload your own visual materials over USB.** Select **Retail Mode
Video** under the **Customization** tab and follow the on-screen guidelines to change the videos the
console plays when it enters **Retail Mode**.

Reach maintenance mode by pressing **Hello Guest ten times**:
`cu800ent-2024-console-maintenance-mode-and-engineering-menu`.

**That is everything the manual says.** It gives **no file format, no resolution, no size limit, no
folder name on the stick, and no length limit**, and it never says what puts the console into Retail
Mode in the first place or how to take it out again. Do not invent a specification for a customer.
