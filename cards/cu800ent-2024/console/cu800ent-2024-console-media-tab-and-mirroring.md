**Press the Media tab at the bottom of the screen** to reach the apps and the casting feature.
**A stable internet connection is required for all of it.**

- **To open an app, tap its icon** - news, video and other content.
- **Users do not have to log out when they have finished their workout: it happens automatically.**
  That is the opposite of the advice the Spirit Commercial support note gives for the 2022 ENT
  consoles, where signing out at the end of a workout is the user's job
  (`ct800ent-2022-console-internet-apps`).
- **Check for updates regularly** - third-party apps are managed from **Maintenance Mode / App
  Manager** (`cu800ent-2024-console-maintenance-mode-and-engineering-menu`).
- **Built-in apps are subject to change without notice**, so no list of apps on this console is a
  specification.

**Mirroring.** Customers may choose **Mirroring** to transfer their own content from a smartphone to
the console screen. **Press How It Works and follow the instructions shown on the screen.** **The
manual prints no steps, names no app, gives no minimum phone OS and says nothing about headphones** -
everything is deferred to the on-screen guide. Do not quote the 2022 procedure at an owner of this
machine; that one needs the **Spirit Mirroring** app on Android and iOS 8 or higher on an iPhone
(`ct800ent-2022-console-screen-mirroring-android`,
`ct800ent-2022-console-screen-mirroring-ios`).

**The Floating Panel keeps the workout controls over the content.**

- **Touch the panel's top edge and drag it** anywhere on the screen, so it does not sit over what you
  are watching.
- **Hide Panels** gives full screen - no stats on the top, no tabs on the bottom. **Show Panels**
  brings them back.
- The **arrow button on the right of the panel** hides and shows the text labels, shrinking the panel
  further.
- **All Media** goes back to the content-source selection.

**While in the Media section, use the physical keys to change level** - the on-screen level panels
belong to the workout views: `cu800ent-2024-console-workout-views`.
