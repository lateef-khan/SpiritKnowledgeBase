**Two ways to finish:**

- Press the **physical STOP button twice**, or
- Tap the **Pause icon in the bottom right corner** of the workout screen, then press **Finish**.

**The workout summary then pops up.** **Slide up and down on the right side of the screen to see all
the available charts.**

**That is everything this manual prints about stopping.** It gives **no pause length**, says nothing
about what a single press of Stop does on its own, prints **no hold-to-reset**, and does not say what
returns the console to the Home page from the summary.

**Do not fill those gaps from another machine.** The CU800ENT-2022 and CR800ENT-2023 need a **third**
press of Stop to reach the Home Screen (`ct800ent-2022-console-pause-stop`), and the LED bikes pause
for **5 minutes** and reset on a three-second hold
(`spirit-ce-console-stop-button-press-once-twice-hold`). Neither is stated for this console.
