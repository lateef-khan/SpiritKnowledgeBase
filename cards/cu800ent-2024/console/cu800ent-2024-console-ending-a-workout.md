**Two ways to finish:**

- Press the **physical STOP button twice**, or
- Tap the **Pause icon in the bottom right corner** of the workout screen, then press **Finish**.

**The workout summary then pops up.** **Slide up and down on the right side of the screen to see all
the available charts.**

**That is everything this manual prints about stopping.** It gives **no pause length**, says nothing
about what a single press of Stop does on its own, prints **no hold-to-reset**, and does not say what
returns the console to the Home page from the summary.

**Do not fill those gaps from another machine.** The CU800ENT-2022 and CR800ENT-2023 need a **third**
press of Stop to reach the Home Screen (`ct800ent-2022-console-pause-stop`), and the LED bikes pause
for **5 minutes** and reset on a three-second hold
(`spirit-ce-console-stop-button-press-once-twice-hold`). Neither is stated for this console.

**The four 2024 ENT machines print this section word for word too**, so this card covers a treadmill
and an elliptical as well as bikes and its product line is no longer bike-only. Measured on native
PDF text, the whole TOUCHSCREEN OPERATION chapter of the CT800ENT-2024, CT850ENT-2024, CE800ENT-2024
and CR800ENT-2024 matches the CU800ENT-2024's **97.8% to 99.5%** word for word; every difference is a
page number or the machine's own name.

Locators: CT800ENT-2024 and CT850ENT-2024 p. 26, CE800ENT-2024 and CR800ENT-2024 p. 24. Same two
routes out, same workout summary, same slide up and down for the charts, and the same silences: no
pause length, nothing about a single press of Stop on its own, no hold-to-reset, and nothing about
what returns the console to Home from the summary.

**On the two ENT treadmills a single press of Stop is described elsewhere in the book.** Their
FEATURES page (p. 17) prints the older PAUSE / STOP / RESET paragraph - one press decelerates the belt
and drops the incline to zero, the readings hold, and after **5 minutes** the display resets; a second
press ends the program on a summary and a third returns to idle
(`ct850-2020-pause-stop-reset` describes the same six behaviours for the LED consoles, and
`ct800ent-2022-console-pause-stop` the three the 2022 ENT manuals print). **The two books therefore
describe stopping in two places and do not reconcile them**; the elliptical and the bikes print only
this touchscreen version.
