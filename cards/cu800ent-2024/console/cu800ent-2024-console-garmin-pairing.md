**A Garmin device must be paired with the console before it can be used.**

1. Press the **Garmin logo** in the status bar.
2. Press **Connect Device** to run the pairing flow.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions: **switch the Garmin device into pairing mode**, **select it on
   the console screen**, and **type in the pin-code the Garmin device displays**.

**The pin comes from the watch, not from the console.** The console asks for it; the watch shows it.

**The manual names no Garmin model, no minimum firmware and no list of what the link carries**, and
it does not say what happens to the pairing at the end of a workout.

**This is a separate flow from ordinary Bluetooth pairing**, which has its own icon and no pin-code
step: `cu800ent-2024-console-bluetooth-pairing`. **No other Spirit bike manual mentions Garmin at
all.**
