1. **Click the Bluetooth icon** in the status bar to enter the pairing page.
2. The system **searches for available Bluetooth devices around the bike**.
3. **Press the device you want to connect with.**

That is the whole procedure the manual prints. **It gives no PIN, no timeout, no device name for the
console and no list of what the link will carry.**

**The machine's feature list calls the radio "Bluetooth (with FTMS)"**, and the manual describes
three separate uses for it: this pairing page, a **Heart Rate** icon in the same status bar for a
heart rate device, and the **Spirit+ app**, which pairs from the phone rather than from the console:
`xt-2023-console-spirit-plus-app-pairing`.

**A Garmin device does not pair here.** It has its own logo in the status bar and its own pin-code
flow: `cu800ent-2024-console-garmin-pairing`.

**This is not the older ENT pairing screen.** The CR900ENT and CU900ENT pair from a **BT icon on the
bottom left** and appear on the phone as **A2DP** (`ct900ent-bluetooth-pairing`); the CT800ENT-2022
and CT850ENT-2022 have Paired Devices and Available Devices lists with a toggle switch
(`spirit-ent-console-bluetooth-pairing`). None of those describes this console.
