## Two letters differ from the CR900 and CU900 2025 diagram

Those manuals use the same thirteen letters in the same order but label **A Console**
and **E Tablet Rack**. This machine labels **A Touchscreen** and **E USB Charger** -
a charging socket where the others have a shelf. Eleven of the thirteen are identical.
See [that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-bike-assembly-parts-diagram-letters.md).

## Names that appear nowhere else in the manual

**USB Charger**, **Pulse Grips** and **Console Fan** are used only here. No assembly
step mentions any of the three, and the carton list names none of them. The carton list
calls part K a **Drink Bottle Holder**; step 4 calls it the Beverage Holder; this page
calls it a **Cup Holder**. Three names for one part in one book.

## The diagram is not a carton list

Nothing on this page tells you what is packed loose. For that see
[the parts card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-cu800ent-assembly-parts-included.md).

**The 2022 printing of this machine prints a photograph with eight word labels
instead**, and no letters. See
[that card](../../cu800ent-2022/assembly/cu800ent-2022-assembly-parts-diagram-labels.md).
