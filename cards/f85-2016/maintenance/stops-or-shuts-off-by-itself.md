**This is a machine that stops part way through use. One that cuts out the instant start is pressed is a different card.**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| House breaker tripped | Reset it. |
| Treadmill breaker tripped | Reset the treadmill breaker. |
| The treadmill controller has shut down and its LED is on | Lubricate the belt and deck. Turn the ON/OFF switch off and turn the power on again. |

A controller that keeps shutting down is protecting itself from over current. Deal with the friction, not the symptom: lubricate the deck, and check the mechanical drive system is not locked or jammed.

If the **house** breaker trips but the treadmill's own breaker does not, the house breaker needs to be a high inrush type.
