**This is a belt that never moves. A belt that starts and then stops is a different card.**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The motor M+ or M- wire is not connected into the right position | Check and plug it in again. |
| The motor is broken | Replace the motor, or check the wire and connector for damage. |
| The treadmill controller has shut down and its LED is on | Turn the ON/OFF switch off and turn the power on again. |

**If the display shows E1 as well**, the diagnosis guide splits the two cases:

- The belt moves, then stops after a short time, and E1 appears: **run calibration.**
- You press start, the belt never moves, and E1 appears: **contact service.**
