1. Remove the motor hood, fold the treadmill, then remove the incline motor.
2. **The incline range must be adjusted to 225 mm minimum before installation.** The manual prints this twice, once as its own section 11 and once as step 2 here.
3. Use the **M6 and M8 L wrenches and a #14 wrench** to assemble the new incline motor.
4. Connect the incline motor wiring to the controller:
   - **Red** wire to **UP**
   - **White** wire to **COM**
   - **Black** wire to **DOWN**

Recalibrate the incline after fitting a new motor.
