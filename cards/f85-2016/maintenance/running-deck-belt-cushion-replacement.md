**Be sure to disassemble the cylinder before starting this procedure.**

1. Fold the treadmill. With the **M5 L Allen wrench and a 12 mm wrench**, release the screw securing the cylinder and take it apart. Then carry out the front and rear roller procedure.
2. Remove the **left and right foot rail caps**. With a Phillips head screwdriver remove the **6 screws** and the bracket from the back of the foot rail, then remove the foot rail in the direction of the arrows.
3. With the **M5 L Allen wrench**, remove the **8 screws** securing the running board. Remove the running board and replace the running board or the running belt. If cushions need replacing, remove the **6 cushions** and replace them. Reassemble in the reverse order.
4. Use a **14 mm open end wrench** and a **tension measuring device** to set the drive belt to the **white LBS area, between 70 and 75 LBS**. Use the 14 mm T-shaped socket wrench to secure and tighten the 4 securing screws.
5. Connect the grounding wires and the motor wires: **red M+, black M-**.
