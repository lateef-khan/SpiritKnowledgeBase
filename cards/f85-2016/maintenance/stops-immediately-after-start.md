**This is a machine that starts and cuts out at once. A belt that never moves, and a machine that shuts off part way through a workout, are different cards.**

The matrix prints this symptom **twice**, with a different answer each time. Both are reproduced here.

| Printed reason | Printed solve |
|---|---|
| Controller is broken | 1. Turn the AC switch off and turn the power on again. 2. Replace the controller and calibrate it. |
| Controller was broken | Replace with a new controller and calibrate it. |

Power cycling first is free, so start there. **Any new controller has to be calibrated afterwards.**
