1. With a Phillips head screwdriver, remove **5 tapping screws** and take off the motor cover.
2. Remove the **motor grounding wire (greenish yellow)**, the **motor wire (+) red** and the **motor wire (-) black**.
3. With a **14 mm T-shaped socket wrench**, loosen the **4 screws**. With the same wrench, loosen **one side tension screw** and the **rear drive belt tension screw**. Remove the motor and fit the new one.
   - Reassemble in reverse order. Re-hook the belt and readjust it to be **parallel with the main frame**. **Do not tighten the 4 securing screws yet.**
4. Use a **14 mm open end wrench** and a **tension measuring device** to set the drive belt to the **white LBS area, between 70 and 75 LBS**. Then use the 14 mm T-shaped socket wrench to secure and tighten the 4 securing screws.
5. Connect the grounding wires and the motor wires: **red M+, black M-**.
