The bed is a high efficiency, low friction bed, and it performs best kept clean.

- Wipe the **edge of the belt and the area between the belt edge and the frame** with a soft damp cloth or paper towel, reaching as far as practical under the belt edge. Do this **once a month** to extend belt and bed life.
- **Use water only.** No cleaners, no abrasives.
- A mild soap and water solution with a **nylon scrub brush** will clean the top of the textured belt. Let the belt dry before using it.

**Belt dust** happens during normal break in, until the belt stabilises. Wiping the excess off with a damp cloth keeps the buildup down.

**General cleaning.** Dirt, dust and pet hair block air inlets and gather on the running belt.

- Monthly: vacuum underneath the treadmill.
- Once a year: remove the black motor hood and vacuum out the dirt inside. **Unplug the power cord before this task.**
