**This is the drive belt between the motor and the front roller. The running belt tension at the rear roller is a different adjustment.**

The same step closes the motor replacement, the roller replacement and the running deck replacement, and it is printed identically in all three:

1. Use a **14 mm open end wrench** to adjust the belt tension using a **tension measuring device**.
2. Adjust to the **white LBS area, between 70 and 75 LBS**.
3. Use a **14 mm T-shaped socket wrench** to secure and tighten the **4 securing screws**.
4. Connect the grounding wires and the motor wires: **red to M+, black to M-**.

Before tensioning, the belt is re-hooked and **readjusted to be parallel with the main frame**, and the 4 securing screws are left loose until the tension is set.
