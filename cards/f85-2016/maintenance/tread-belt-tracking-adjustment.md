**Start with the floor.** The treadmill depends on the frame sitting on a reasonably level surface. If the frame is not level, the front and back rollers cannot run parallel and the belt will need constant adjustment.

Some drift toward one side while the belt runs empty is normal, and after a few minutes of use the belt should tend to centre itself. Adjust only if the belt keeps moving to one side **during use**.

1. Use the **10 mm Allen wrench** supplied to adjust the rear roller.
2. **Make tracking adjustments from the left side only.**
3. Set the belt speed to about **3 to 5 kph**.
4. Turn the bolt **clockwise to move the belt to the right**.
5. Turn only **1/4 turn**, then wait a few minutes for the belt to settle. Repeat in 1/4 turns until the belt stabilises in the centre of the deck.

A small adjustment makes a dramatic difference.

**If the belt will not stay centred**, the user may be favouring one foot. If that gait is natural, track the belt slightly off centre to the side opposite the belt movement.

Belt tracking is an owner responsibility common to all treadmills, and it settles down as the belt is used. **Damage to the running belt caused by improper tracking or tension adjustment is not covered under the warranty.**
