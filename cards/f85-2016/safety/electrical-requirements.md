**The outlet**

- Install the treadmill on a flat level surface with access to a **220 volt, 10 amp grounded outlet**, with only the treadmill plugged into that circuit. On a 120Vac electronic power system the requirement is **110 volt, 15 amp**.
- A minimum of **220 volt AC, 50 Hz** is required (110Vac on the 120Vac system). Low household voltage makes the machine reach only about 10 kph while showing a higher speed.

**Extension cords**

- Do not use one unless it is **16 AWG or better with only one outlet on the end**.
- Do not disable the grounded plug with an adapter, and do not modify the cord or outlet in any way.

**Never use a GFCI wall outlet with this treadmill.** As with any appliance with a large motor, the GFCI will trip often. Route the power cord away from every moving part, including the elevation mechanism and the transport wheels.

**If the house breaker trips but the treadmill's own breaker does not**, the house breaker is not rated for the inrush current a treadmill draws. Replace it with a **high inrush type**. This is not a warranty defect. Examples given: Grainger part **1D237**, or www.squared.com part **QO120HM**.
