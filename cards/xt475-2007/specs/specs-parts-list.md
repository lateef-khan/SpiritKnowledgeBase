These are the callout numbers on the **XT475 Exploded View** on PDF page 29, a drawing printed sideways on a portrait page and stamped **950821PM1345** in small type at its edge. The drawing is a picture with bare numbers on it, so this table is the only readable form of it. It was read from the native text layer of the 2008 dealer service manual; every part number on the pages the 300 dpi OCR sweep supplemented agrees with that layer, and pages 18 and 26 were also read from a render.

**Three columns, no quantity.** The list prints `No.`, `Description` and `Spirit Part #` only - it never says how many of an item the machine uses. A part number is a six-digit Spirit number. **`X` in the part-number column is printed on the page**; the book does not say what it means, and 9 rows carry it (9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 52~29, 159). 9 numbered rows carry no part number at all (1, 2, 3, 7A, 7B, 8, 13, 39~1, 142).

**166 rows are printed.** The whole item numbers that appear are 1-78, 81-120, 122-131, 133-135, 137-142, 144-148, 159-162. Any number outside that run - 79-80, 121, 132, 136, 143, 149-158 - is not printed for this machine at all, not a missing page. Sub-items are written with a tilde (`39~1`, `39~2`); paired parts carry a letter (`7A`, `7B`).

**Do not read this list against the XT375 list, even though the two share one drawing.** The XT375 exploded view carries the same stamp, 950821PM1345, and the same item numbering, but 29 of the shared item numbers carry a different part number, a different description or a blank on one list and a number on the other - the running deck, running belt, motor and console among them. Rows only on this list: none. Rows only on the XT375 list: 36~1. The row-by-row comparison is printed once, on `xt375-2007-specs-parts-list`.

**These numbers belong to the 2007 model year only.** The XT185, XT285, XT385, XT485 and XT685 of 2015 have no printed parts list anywhere (`xt-2015-specs-no-parts-list`), and the 2023 XT lists are numbered per manual with no Spirit part-number column - the XT185-2023 Drive Motor is item 027, the XT385/XT485-2023 Drive Motor is 34. Nothing establishes that any number here fits a later machine, or that a later item number fits this one.

**Numbers that differ from the XT375 for the same position.** Item 92 is `000125` here (the XT375 prints 008113), item 119 is `010113` (the XT375 prints 010013), item 51 Ground Wire is `005013` (the XT375 prints 000555) and item 52~3 is the six-digit `007058` (the XT375 prints 00758). Items 68, 69 and 70 - the left LR, right RL and right RR handgrip side caps - are 007011, 007012 and 007013 here and 007012, 007013 and 007011 on the XT375; one of the two lists has them in the wrong order and nothing on the page says which. Item 91 is printed `3/8" x 2"_Thumb Head Socket Screw` where the XT375 and XT175 call the same 022885 a Flat Head Socket Screw. Item 36 names the controller `ALT6330A`; the XT375 gives the same 022670 with no board name and adds a transformer row (36~1) this list does not have.

**Tools are items in the list.** Item 141 is the combination M5 Allen wrench and Phillips screwdriver (000072), item 142 an Allen wrench with no part number, and item 30 the lubricant (022271).
