**The manual prints no weight-stack rating.** This total is the sum of the plate quantities its
assembly hardware table lists, and it is not a figure the manufacturer states anywhere in the book -
say so when you quote it. Why that is, and how it was checked, is at
`spirit-strength-specs-no-weight-stack-rating-printed`.

**This assist stack shrank between printings.** The 2024 CSD-PUDA book lists a 330 lb assist stack -
`spirit-strength-specs-assist-stack-of-330-lb`. The 2025 revision prints a smaller table:

| No. | Description | Qty | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 117 | `15lb weight stack` | 12 | 180 lb |
| 075 | `10lb weight stack-C` | 1 | 10 lb |
| 074 | `10lb counter weight stack-A` | 2 | 20 lb |
| 073 | `10lb counter weight stack-B` | 1 | 10 lb |
| 076 | `10lb top weight stack` | 1 | 10 lb |
| | **Total** | **17** | **230 lb** |

Two things changed at once, both confirmed on a 600 dpi render of the 2025 book's PDF page 16: the
large plate is printed **15lb**, not the 2024 book's 20LB (its old row 117 read `20LB Weight Stack`
in the native, non-OCR text layer, so that figure is solid), and row 075's quantity dropped from 5 to
1. Together they take the stack from 330 lb to 230 lb - a 100 lb drop, not a rounding or naming
change.

**Treat this as what a machine shipping with the 2025-revision manual carries.** The tie-breaker
rule in this repository is the later printing, and here there is no reason to doubt it the way there
is for the CSD-ACBE weight figure - two independent quantities changed together in the same
direction, which looks like a deliberate stack redesign (fewer, smaller-count 10 lb plates and a
lighter large plate) rather than a copy-paste or OCR slip. If a customer's machine or parts order
predates 2025, the 330 lb card still describes it.

**This stack still assists rather than resists.** On a pull-up and dip assist machine the selected
weight counterbalances the user, so a higher pin setting makes the exercise easier, not harder.

The optional 5 lb increment weight rides on top of the stack on the machines that have one -
`spirit-strength-specs-five-pound-increment-weight`.
