The manual's whole exercise instruction for this machine is one picture of the
placard fitted to the machine. The placard is titled **PULL UP / DIP ASSIST**.

It is a two-panel placard, one panel per exercise. Each panel carries a front and
a back body figure with the muscles that exercise works picked out, and a **START**
and a **FINISH** drawing of a person on the machine.

Panel labels, as printed: **PULL UP**, **DIP ASSIST**.

**The page prints no placard part number.** Every other page of the book was
checked and the number is nowhere in it - several sibling manuals do print one,
so its absence here is the book's, not the extraction's.

**No words go with the drawing.** The manual names no muscle, gives no starting
position in prose, no range of motion, no rep or set count and no warm-up. See
`spirit-strength-programs-exercise-instruction-is-the-machine-placard` for what
that means across the range.
