1. **Worn brushes or bearings on the motor** - replace with new motor brushes (the troubleshooting matrix says replace with a new motor instead).
2. **Front roller bearings are defective** - replace with a new front roller.
3. **Drive belt is misadjusted, too tight or too loose** - adjust the motor position.
