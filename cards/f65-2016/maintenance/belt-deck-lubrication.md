First clean between the belt and deck to remove trapped debris. Use a clean non-fraying rag, t-shirt or light towel. Halfway between the end of the treadmill and the motor cover, push the garment under the belt until you can grasp it on both sides, then drag it the length of the belt 1-2 times and remove it.

**Do not lubricate with anything other than approved lubricant.** The treadmill comes with one tube of "Lube"; extra tubes and the Lube-n-Walk kit are available from an authorised dealer.

**How often**: the belt and deck come pre-lubricated. Lubricate **every 90 hours of use**, or whenever the deck is dry. Check for lubricant by reaching between the belt and deck **every other month**; if there is none, lubricate even though the "Lube" indicator is not lit. Otherwise lubricate when the console's lubrication reminder lights after 90 hours.

1. Turn the power switch off and unplug the power cord from the wall outlet.
2. Measure **18 inches** from the edge of the motor cover; kneel down and reach under the belt approximately **4-6 inches from one edge**. Squirt a line of lubricant about **1/8 inch wide by 15 inches long in an "S" pattern**, perpendicular to the motor cover.
3. Repeat on the opposite side.
4. Plug the cord back in and turn the power switch on.
5. Walk on the belt at a moderate speed for **five minutes** to distribute the silicone lube evenly.
6. Reset the "Lube" message if it is showing - see the lube message reset card.

If the lubricant dries out, friction between the belt and deck rises and puts undue stress on the drive motor, drive belt and electronic motor control board, which can destroy those expensive components. **Failure to lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty.**
