1. First adjust treadmill to folded position, then proceed with old incline motor replacement.
2. **The incline range must be adjusted to 225 mm minimum prior to installation.** Section 11 repeats this as the one rule for installing an incline motor.
3. User Combination M8 Allen Wrench and 14mm wrench to assemble new incline motor.
4. Connect the incline motor wiring to the controller:
   - **Red wire to "UP"**
   - **White wire to "COM"**
   - **Black wire to "DOWN"**

The E3 troubleshooting table ends with "Recalibrate the incline set", so recalibrate after the motor is fitted.
