| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Haven't switched the ON/OFF switch. | Switch the AC switch. |
| Safe key inserted in the wrong position. | Insert the safe key in the right position. |
| 6-PIN Main control wire not plugged in properly. | Check the wire and connect again. |
| 6-PIN Main control wire is broken. | Replace the 6-PIN Main control wire. |
| Fuse on the controller is blown. | Replace the fuse or controller. |
| Varistor on the controller is blown. | Replace the varistor or controller. |
| Safety device is broken (open circuit). | Replace the safety key device. |
| Other components are faulty. | Replace the console. |
