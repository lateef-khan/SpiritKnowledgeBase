| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Motor M+ or M- wire isn't connected into the right position. | Check and plug it in again. |
| Motor is broken. | Replace the motor, or check the wire and connector if it was broken. |
| Treadmill controller shut down. | Turn off the AC switch and turn the power on again. |
