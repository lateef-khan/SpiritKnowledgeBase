The matrix has two rows for this, one for the LCD light and one for the LCD displays.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| LCD light is broken. | Replace with a new LCD or console. |
| Power to console too low. | Check AC power is 110V or 230V. Check power to the console. Replace the lower controller. |
| LCD displays are broken. | Replace with a new console. |
