Besides the normal version, the ST155 treadmill has a **CE/GS version**.

Both versions are exactly the same in functions and outlines except:

- The power input is **110AC for the normal version** versus **230VAC for the CEGS version**.
- An additional **Filter Choke circuit** is added for the CEGS version, as shown in the circuit diagram.

The general information section adds that the 220V (or CEGS) lower controller area has a filter and choke.

The calibration procedure has a related European rule: Grade return is normally On, but EU standards require it to be **off** for sale in Europe.
