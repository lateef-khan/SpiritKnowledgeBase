The cover reads **ST155 Treadmill Service Manual**, and every section heading inside says ST155. This is the Sole F65 of model year 2016; the Spirit database SKU is **565816**.

Two things on the paperwork do not match:

- The table of contents item 1 reads "**ST125** Treadmill Outlines", while the section itself is headed "1. ST155 Treadmill Outlines".
- The source PDF is named "F65 2016 (ST155-YT018) Service Manual.pdf", so the full factory code is **ST155-YT018**. The cover page itself prints only ST155.

The machine is built from a console, a main controller (DC power supply for the console, incline driver and DC motor driver), a DC drive motor, an AC incline motor and a speed sensor. The safety key fits into the console; without it the console cannot be controlled and the treadmill will not run.
