**Section 3 summary**: "This is a DC motor with variable speed. Control the 0-90 (or 0-180) voltages from the main controller to increase or decrease speed of the running belt."

**General Information detail**: DC motor with variable speed range 0-90 or (0-180) volt. Requires three wire connection: red, black and green. The Red wire is inserted into M+. The White wire is inserted into M-. When voltage is higher and higher, the motor will be faster. The green wire is grounding wire.

Section 6.4 labels the driver board terminals: **M- is connected with the black wire of the motor and M+ with the red wire**. The motor replacement procedure repeats it as "red M+, black M-".

Note the wire list and the terminal assignment in the General Information block do not use the same colour name for the M- lead. Take the colours from the replacement procedure, which says motor wire (+) red and motor wire (-) black.
