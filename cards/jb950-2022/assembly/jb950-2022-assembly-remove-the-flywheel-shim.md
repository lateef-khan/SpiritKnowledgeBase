> **NOTE: Before pedaling bike, remove Flywheel Shim protecting proper magnet spacing.
> Locate Shim Hang Tag and pull on cord removing shim. Use shim when needing to transport
> bikes over long road distances.**

**Find the hang tag and pull the cord.** The shim comes out with it.

Three things follow from that sentence:

- **It must come out before the first pedal stroke**, not before the first workout. The
  shim sits in the magnet gap; the bike is not meant to be turned with it in.
- **Keep it.** It is not packaging. The manual says to put it back when transporting
  bikes over long road distances, which is the only shipping-protection instruction in
  the book.
- **The testing checklist does not mention it.** A reader who works from the checklist
  rather than from step five will miss it. See
  [that card](jb950-2022-assembly-testing-checklist.md).

## Nothing else in the manual explains the shim

The word appears exactly three times, all in this NOTE. There is no drawing of it, no
part number, and no instruction on how to refit it - only that you should. If it is lost,
the manual offers no replacement part.

**No other bike manual in this repository has a flywheel shim.** Do not look for one on
an air bike or an indoor cycle.
