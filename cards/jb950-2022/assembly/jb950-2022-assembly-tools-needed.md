Five tools, printed as a TOOLS NEEDED list:

- **#155. L M4 Allen Wrench** (1 pc)
- **#157. 17mm Wrench** (1 pc)
- **#158. 13/15 Wrench** (1 pc)
- **#159. L M2.5 Allen Wrench** (1 pc)
- **#167. 19mm Wrench** (1 pc)

> **Use of a large pedal wrench (not included) is highly recommended to secure the
> pedals.**

The pedal wrench sentence is printed twice - once in the tools panel and again inside
assembly step one. **It is the only tool you must supply**, and the manual otherwise says
all tools required are in the packaging.

## The manual calls 157 an Allen wrench in step one

The tools panel says **#157 is a 17mm Wrench**. Assembly step one says the long centre
bolt is "tightened with **17MM ALLEN WRENCH (157)**". Item 157 cannot be both; the panel
is the one printed as a specification, and the two Allen wrenches in this carton are the
**M4 (155)** and the **M2.5 (159)**. Reach for the 17mm open wrench.

## Which tool each step needs

- **Step one:** 19mm (167) for the stabilizers, 157 for the rear centre bolt, 15mm from
  the 13/15 (158) for the pedals.
- **Step three:** 2.5mm Allen (159) for the end cap bolt.
- **Steps four and five:** 4mm Allen (155) for the lever bolt and the console bolts.

**No torque wrench is supplied and no torque figure is printed anywhere in the assembly
chapter.** The maintenance chapter asks for the pedals to be re-torqued after the first
10 hours and every 100 hours thereafter, without stating a figure.
