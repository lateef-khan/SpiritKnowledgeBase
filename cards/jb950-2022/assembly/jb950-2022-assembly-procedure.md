Numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing item numbers. Quantities are on
[the hardware card](jb950-2022-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

**Always have assistance when lifting the main frame**, and **prop the frame off the
ground on the large foam packaging** to get at the stabilizer bolts. Both instructions
are printed three times between pre-assembly and step one.

## Step one - stabilizers and pedals

1. Assemble the **FRONT STABILIZER (2)** to the **MAIN FRAME (1)** with **three HEX
   BOLTS (165)** and **three WASHERS (166)**, using the **19mm wrench (167)**.
2. Assemble the **REAR STABILIZER (3)** to the main frame with **two short HEX BOLTS
   (165)**, **one LONG HEX BOLT (141)**, **one WASHER (125)** and **two WASHERS (166)**,
   using the 19mm wrench (167). **The long bolt (141) and washer (125) go in the centre
   hole**, tightened with wrench (157).
3. Assemble the **PEDALS (54)** using the **15mm wrench (158)**. A large pedal wrench,
   not included, is highly recommended.

## Step two - handlebar onto the slide rail

1. Run the **MAIN WIRE (90)** through the opening at the bottom of the **HANDLEBARS (4)**
   and out of the front.
2. Slide the **HANDLEBAR ASSEMBLY (4)** onto the aluminium slide rail while
   **simultaneously pulling the main wire out of the front** so it does not jam. The wire
   connector should protrude from the front of the handlebars when fully installed.

> **NOTE: Be careful not to pinch and damage the MAIN WIRE (90) when sliding the
> HANDLEBAR ASSEMBLY (4) on to main rail.**

No hardware table is printed for this step.

## Step three - end cap and handlebar anchor

1. Install the **HANDLEBAR END CAP (73)** onto the back end of the **ALUMINUM SLIDE RAIL
   (8)** and secure with the **SOCKET HEAD CAP BOLT (144)**, using the **2.5mm Allen
   wrench (159)**.
2. The **HANDLEBAR ANCHOR (51)** is pre-installed in the slide rail.
3. Using a screwdriver or Allen key, slide the anchor forward to the front end cap. Look
   through the handlebar hole and slide the handlebar forward to align with the screw
   hole in the anchor.

## Step four - adjusting axle and lever

1. Thread the **ADJUSTING AXLE (58)** through the hole into the **HANDLEBAR ANCHOR (51)**
   with **two FLAT WASHERS (121)**. Tighten fully. **Gently pull on the MAIN WIRE (90)**
   to leave space for the axle and prevent cable damage.
2. Insert a small Allen wrench into the lever from below to remove the plastic cap at the
   top, giving access for the bolt.
3. Install the **LEVER** at **12 o'clock** and attach it to the adjusting axle with the
   **BUTTON HEAD SOCKET BOLT (149)**, using the **4mm Allen wrench (155)**.
4. Refit the plastic cap to seal the lever.

## Step five - console

1. Connect **MAIN WIRE (A & B)** and **CONTROL WIRE (C & D)** to the **CONSOLE (33)**.
2. Attach the console to the aluminium slide rail with **two FLAT HEAD COUNTERSUNK BOLTS
   (148)**, using the 4mm Allen wrench (155).

> **NOTE: Before pedaling bike, remove Flywheel Shim** protecting proper magnet spacing.

That is a separate, easily missed step; see
[the shim card](jb950-2022-assembly-remove-the-flywheel-shim.md).

## The step count in pre-assembly is wrong

Pre-assembly says the hardware is separated into **four** steps. The manual prints
**five**.
