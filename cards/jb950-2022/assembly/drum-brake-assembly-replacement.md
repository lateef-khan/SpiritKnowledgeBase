1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **four 12 mm Phillips head screws (#134)** and **two 16 mm sheet-metal screws (#131)** from both chain covers (R and L).
2. Move the crank arm to the horizontal position to make the chain cover easier to take off.
3. Remove both chain covers (R and L).
4. Use an **M4 Allen wrench** to remove **two M5 bolts (#145)** and **flat washers (#120)**.
5. Use an **M5 Allen wrench** to remove the **socket head cap bolt** on the drum brake assembly.
6. Use diagonal pliers to cut the cable tie.
7. Use pliers to remove the **front-end rope clip of the steel cable**.
8. Use an **8 mm open-end wrench** to remove the steel cable.
9. Fit the new drum brake assembly and reverse the steps to refit. **If a cable tie was cut, fit a new one.**

**Two brakes on this bike.** The drum brake is the mechanical stop worked by the dynamic braking handle under the handlebars, cable-operated; the magnetic brake assembly (the resistance) is a different part with its own card. The drum brake job does not need the flywheel out.
