## Seat height - the pin turns, then pulls

Dismount. **Turn the seat height pop-pin counterclockwise and pull out** on the pin to
release it. Raise or lower the seat, then gently release the pin; nudge the seat until
the pin engages a preset hole, then **turn the pop-pin clockwise to secure. Tighten
firmly.**

**The turn is the difference from the air bikes**, whose pop-pin is only loosened and
pulled. A JB950 pin that will not pull out has probably not been turned first.

Set the height so the **knee joint is slightly flexed when the extended leg is at the
bottom of the pedal stroke**.

## Seat fore/aft

Loosen the seat fore-and-aft **tension handle** counterclockwise, move the seat, then
tighten clockwise. Firmly.

Position it so that **with the feet at 3 and 9 o'clock the forward knee is directly over
the pedal axle**, and **recheck the height afterwards** - moving the seat forward or back
has the same effect as raising or lowering it.

**The manual's own instruction here says "Move the handlebar forward or back"** under the
SEAT fore/aft heading. That is a copy-paste error from the handlebar section on the
facing page; the paragraph is about the seat.

## Handlebar

Position the handlebar **at the same height as your seat, or higher if you feel any
discomfort in your back**. Both height and fore/aft use tension handles: turn
counterclockwise to loosen, move, turn clockwise to tighten firmly.

## Pedal straps

Place the **balls of your feet securely in the toe cages**, with the widest part of the
shoe over the centre of the pedal, and concentrate on keeping the feet flat as you
pedal - it makes for a more powerful stroke. **The front of the shoe may not completely
fill the toe cage.** The straps should hold the foot snugly to the pedal.

## In a club, get fitted

> In a club setting, we recommend each user to initially be properly fitted on the bike
> by a certified indoor cycling instructor.
