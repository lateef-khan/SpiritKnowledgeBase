1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **four 12 mm Phillips head screws (#134)** and **two 16 mm sheet-metal screws (#131)** from both chain covers (R and L).
2. **Move the crank arm to the horizontal position** to make the chain cover easier to take off.
3. Remove both chain covers (R and L).
4. Remove the **drive belt (#43)**.
5. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to remove **four 12 mm Phillips head screws (#134)** from the **mounting bracket (#42)**.
6. Unplug the **connecting wire (#96)** from the **generator (#57)**.
7. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to remove **four flat washers (#118)** and **four 8 mm Phillips head screws (#130)** from the mounting bracket (#42).
8. Use diagonal pliers to cut the cable tie.
9. Remove the **generator bracket (#42)**.
10. Fit the new generator (#57) and reverse the steps to refit.

Steps 1 to 3 open every procedure in this book. The generator's output is the console's power: the troubleshooting matrix says to check its output, and replace the generator if it is below 30 V at 30 RPM.

The screw numbers (#134, #131, #130) are the book's call-outs; the sizes are the lengths printed with them.
