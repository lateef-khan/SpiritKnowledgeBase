1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **four 12 mm Phillips head screws (#134)** and **two 16 mm sheet-metal screws (#131)** from both chain covers (R and L).
2. Move the crank arm to the horizontal position to make the chain cover easier to take off.
3. Remove both chain covers (R and L).
4. Use a **7 mm open-end wrench** to remove **four nylon nuts (#163)**.
5. Use diagonal pliers to cut the cable tie.
6. Unplug the connecting wires from the **lower control board (#87)**.
7. Remove the lower control board (#87) and fit the new one. Reverse the steps to refit.

The board's five connectors (generator power in, the 9-pin system cable to the console, RPM sensor, the brake cable and the optional 9 V adapter) are defined on the wiring pages, which are a separate section; step 6 does not list them.

The troubleshooting matrix's "replace controller" for a dim display and "replace control board" for no resistance both mean this board.
