- **Recheck all the bolts** and make sure they are tightened to the proper torque
  specification (if indicated) and that no parts are missing.
- **Check the seat** is level and tight and does not rotate or tilt. Tighten and adjust
  as needed.
- **Test the seat and handlebar horizontal adjustment** for smooth movement front to
  rear.
- **Adjust the seat post and handlebar to your needs**, then ride and test the bike for
  proper operation.
- **Pedal at a moderate pace** and test for proper, smooth movement of the pedals.
- **When testing is complete, tip the bike forward using the arms**, roll it on a smooth
  surface to its final location and adjust the levelling feet so the bike is stable.

> **CAUTION: Do not dismount the bike until the pedals to come to a complete stop.**

The typo is the manual's.

## This is not the air bike checklist

The AB900 and AB950 print a seven-line list for a machine with movement arms - it tests
the seat post lock and the arms, and warns that the arms move whenever the pedals turn.
The Johnny G has no movement arms, so it drops both, and adds the **handlebar**
horizontal check that the air bikes have no need of. See
[that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-airbike-assembly-testing-checklist.md).

**The last line still says "tip the bike forward using the arms"** even though this bike
has none. The arms it means are the handlebars.

## Do not ride it until the flywheel shim is out

The checklist does not mention the shim. Its removal is a NOTE on the previous page; see
[that card](jb950-2022-assembly-remove-the-flywheel-shim.md).
