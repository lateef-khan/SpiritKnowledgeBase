1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **four 12 mm Phillips head screws (#134)** and **two 16 mm sheet-metal screws (#131)** from both chain covers (R and L).
2. Move the crank arm to the horizontal position to make the chain cover easier to take off.
3. Remove the chain covers (R and L).
4. Use an **M8 L Allen wrench** to remove the **M12 axle bolt (#105)** on the **crank arm (L) (#46)**.
5. Use a **crank wrench** to remove the crank arm (L) (#46).
6. Remove **the two drive belts (#43)** and fit new belts. Reverse the steps to refit.

**Two belts, one part number.** Step 6 says "the two drive Belts #43" - the generator is driven by a pair. No tension figure is printed for them; the 100 to 110 pound figure in this book is for the main (timing) belt to the flywheel, a different procedure.

The left crank arm has to come off, which is why this belt job needs the 8 mm Allen key and a crank wrench that the generator job does not.
