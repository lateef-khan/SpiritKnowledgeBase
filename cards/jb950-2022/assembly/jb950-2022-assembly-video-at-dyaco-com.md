> An assembly video is available to show the steps for assembly of the Johnny G Spirit
> bike. Follow the link below:
>
> **http://dyaco.com/JG_Assembly**

Printed once, on the pre-assembly page under the tools list.

**It is a Dyaco address, not a Spirit one.** Dyaco is the manufacturer; the rest of this
manual points readers at spiritfitness.com and at **www.johnnygspiritbike.com**, which is
the running footer on every page of this book and the only Spirit bike manual in this
repository with a site of its own.

**No other Spirit bike manual in this repository offers an assembly video.** The link is
printed as plain http and its state has not been checked here.
