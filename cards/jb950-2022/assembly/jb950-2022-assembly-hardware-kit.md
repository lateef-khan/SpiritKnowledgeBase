**Step two has no hardware table.** The handlebar assembly simply slides onto the rail.

## The five step-one bolts are not interchangeable

Three **M12 short bolts (165)** with washers (166) hold the front stabilizer; the rear
takes **two more of the same** plus **one M10 x 55L long bolt (141)** with its own washer
**(125)** in the **centre hole**. The long bolt is the only M10 in the kit and the only
one tightened with wrench (157) rather than the 19mm.

## Rows that do not agree with their own step

- **Item 67** is a *socket head cap bolt* in the step four table, but the step four text
  calls **(67) the LEVER** three times - "using LEVER (67) as a tool", "Install LEVER
  (67) in a 12 o'clock position". The bolt that attaches the lever is item **149**. Do
  not order 67 expecting a fastener.
- The step three table prints **"SOCKET HEAD CAP BIOLT"** and **"M3XPO.5X10L"**, and the
  step five table **"FLAT HEAT COUNTER-SUNK BOT"**. Read those as bolt, M3 x P0.5 x 10L,
  and flat head countersunk bolt.

## The pedals are in the hardware table

Item **54, Pedals set, R/L** is listed as step one hardware rather than as a separate
component. It is the only Spirit bike manual in this repository that does that.
