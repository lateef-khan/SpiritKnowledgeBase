**The bike includes both an ANT+ and a Bluetooth FTMS chip.** The manual explains FTMS as "the new
FITness Machine Service protocol to control fitness equipment, such as bike trainers, treadmills and
rowing machines."

**Named as compatible:**

- **Garmin**
- **MyZone**
- **Strava**

Download and connect **according to their individual app instructions** - the manual gives no
step-by-step procedure for any of them, and no pairing gesture on the console side.

**Leaderboards.** The bike also connects with leaderboards such as those from **MyZone** or
**Spivi**. For a Spivi-style leaderboard each bike needs its own number, set from the maintenance
menu: `jb950-2022-console-ant-id-setup`.

**Do not confuse the app link with the heart rate link.** Pairing a chest strap is a separate,
fully documented procedure done on the console itself, and it is the ANT+ side that carries it -
`jb950-2022-console-pair-heart-rate-strap`.

**No app name is printed anywhere else in the book**, and there is no Spirit or Johnny G app of its
own. The bike has its own website, `www.johnnygspiritbike.com`, printed as the footer of every
right-hand page.
