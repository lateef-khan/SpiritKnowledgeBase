**Purpose: to turn ON or OFF the HR Zone LED light when a heart rate strap is paired.** The manual's
reason: **a facility may choose to deactivate this light if it were a distraction or against their
class structure.**

1. Press the **Play Key** to change the setting.
2. Press the **Play Key** to change between **ON** and **OFF**.
3. Press **+** for the next screen, or reset the console to exit Maintenance Mode.

**The default setting is ON.**

**Turning it off does not stop heart rate tracking.** The manual is explicit: **"If a HR strap is
paired, the console maintains tracking of HR training performance regardless of the LED lights
activation."** Zone times still appear in the workout summary.

**The LED is on the right side of the console.** The Power Zone LED, on the left, is a different light
with its own colour spectrum and is not covered by this setting.

**It is not the Auto Pair HR setting** on the same page, which decides whether riders are asked to
pair a strap during Warm Up Mode and defaults to **OFF**. One setting is about a light, the other
about a prompt, and their defaults are opposite - do not read one for the other.

**A light that never changes colour may be a display fault rather than this setting.** The Display
Test cycles the HR Zone and Power Zone LEDs through their colours without needing a strap:
`jb950-2022-console-display-test`.
