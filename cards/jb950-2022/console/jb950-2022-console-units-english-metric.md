**Purpose: to change from ENGLISH (Imperial) to METRIC distance calculation.**

1. Step to the **UNITS** entry in Maintenance Mode.
2. **Press the Play Key to change the setting.**
3. Press **+** for the next screen, or reset the console to exit Maintenance Mode.

**The default setting is ENGLISH.**

**One caveat the manual prints under the same heading:** **`SPD AVG` and `SPD PEAK` in the workout
results will only display MPH.** Switching the console to metric changes the distance calculation but
**does not** change those two summary figures - they stay in miles per hour whatever the setting says.

**So a metric studio still reads two imperial numbers** at the end of every ride. That is by design in
this manual, not a fault, and it is worth telling an instructor before they report it.

**The Play Key toggles rather than opening a list.** There is no + or - adjustment inside this entry
and no confirmation - one press flips it.

**Nothing else in the manual switches units.** There is no rider-facing units control on the Home
screen, so this maintenance entry is the only route, and reaching it means pedalling above 30 RPM
while holding three keys.
