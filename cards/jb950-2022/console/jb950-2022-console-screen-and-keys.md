**Six labelled fields.** The console screen carries a single dot-matrix line with six labels printed
around it. Top row, left to right: **LEVEL, RPM, TIME, DISTANCE.** Bottom row: **WATTS, HEART RATE.**
At rest the line spells out **`JOHNNY G. METHOD`** across the whole width.

The Home screen displays those same six values: **Level, RPM, Time, Distance, Watts, Heart Rate.**

**Two colour LEDs, one at each lower corner:**

| LED | Side | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **HR LED** | lower **right** | the rider's heart rate zone, as a colour |
| **Power Zone LED** | lower **left** | the rider's FTP zone, as a colour |

**Five keys in three places** - the KEY TEST screen names all of them:

- **Forward Control Pad:** **+ Key**, **- Key**, **Play Key**.
- **Handlebar ends:** **right side + Key**, **left side - Key**.

So there are two ways to reach + and -, and the **Play Key exists only on the Forward Control Pad**.
Every menu gesture on this bike needs the Play Key, which is why none of them can be done from the
handlebar ends.

**A USB port sits underneath the console**, used only for software updates -
`jb950-2022-console-software-update-usb`.
