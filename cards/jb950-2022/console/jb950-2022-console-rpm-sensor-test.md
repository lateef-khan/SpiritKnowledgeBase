**RPM Sensor controls are a sensitive setting for the bike's functionality. Proper training is
required for this setting.** Purpose: to test the functionality of the RPM detection system.

1. Press the **Play Key** to enter the RPM Sensor Test.
2. **`REED ON RPM`** displays **ON** or **OFF**:
   - **When the REED switch is closed, it displays ON.**
   - **When the REED switch is open, it displays OFF.**
3. **`RPM`** displays the **PEDAL RPM**.
4. Press the **Play Key** to exit to the setting screen.
5. Press **+** for the next screen, or reset the console to exit Maintenance Mode.

**Turn the cranks slowly to use it.** With the magnet passing the reed switch once per revolution, a
working sensor toggles ON and OFF as you go round. A reading stuck on OFF with the cranks turning
points at the switch, its wiring or the magnet's position.

**This bike's console is powered by pedalling above 30 RPM**, so a completely dead RPM sensor is not
the same fault as a dead console - the console will still light up while the RPM field stays at zero.

**There is no calibration here.** This entry reports what the sensor is doing; nothing in it adjusts
a wheel size, a magnet gap or a pulse count.
