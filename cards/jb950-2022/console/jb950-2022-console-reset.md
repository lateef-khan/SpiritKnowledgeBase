**Press the Play Key and the + Key simultaneously for 3 seconds.** The console displays **`CONSOLE
RESET`** and restarts, flashing `JOHNNY G` and returning to Warm Up Mode.

**This is the bike's universal way out.** The manual gives it as the answer in five separate places:

| Where | Why you need it |
|---|---|
| Pairing a heart rate strap | the scan is not finding your device |
| A rider put a strap on **after** leaving Warm Up Mode | HR can **only** be paired during Warm Up Mode, so a reset is the only route back |
| Activating HR through the **USER** function when no strap was paired | the same - a reset is required |
| **Exiting Maintenance Mode** | this is the documented exit |
| **Motor Test Manual**, inside Brake Test | "To exit, Console Reset is required" |

**Use the Forward Control Pad.** The Play Key exists only there - the handlebar-end keys give you +
and - and nothing else.

**Keep pedalling while you do it.** The console is powered by the rider above 30 RPM; let the cranks
stop and there is nothing to press.

**It is not the same as the maintenance menu's EXIT entry**, which also resets the console but is
reached by stepping to EXIT and pressing Play.
