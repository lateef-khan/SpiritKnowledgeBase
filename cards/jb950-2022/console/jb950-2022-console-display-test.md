**Purpose: to ensure that each of the 23 LED panels is fully functional.**

1. Press the **Play Key** to enter the setting.
2. **All sections of each of the 23 LED panels illuminate.**
3. Press the **Play Key** to turn them off.
4. Press the **Play Key** again. All LED panels illuminate the **#1**, then automatically cycle from
   **#1 - 9 and A - Z**. The **LED colour lights for HR Zone and Power Zone cycle through colours.**
5. The display turns off and reverts to the setting screen.
6. Press **+** for the next screen, or reset the console to exit Maintenance Mode.

**Twenty-three panels.** That is the count to check against: the dot-matrix line that spells out
messages is built from 23 individual character panels, and a dead one shows as a permanent gap in
every message.

**It also tests the two colour LEDs** - the HR Zone LED on the lower right and the Power Zone LED on
the lower left - by running them through their colour spectrum. A rider reporting that the coloured
light never changes can be checked here without a heart rate strap or an FTP figure.

**The manual gives no pass or fail message for this test.** Unlike the Key Test it prints no
`TEST PASSED`; you watch the panels.
