**Purpose: to ensure that each of the buttons is signalling properly.**

1. Enter Maintenance Mode - **+, - and Play held together for 3 seconds** - and press Play. `KEY TEST`
   is the first screen.
2. Press the **Play Key** to enter the setting.
3. Press the Play Key again and follow the console's direction, **`PRESS ALL KEYS`**.
4. **`TEST PASSED`** displays if all keys are communicating properly. The system reverts to the
   setting screen.
5. Press **+** for the next screen, or reset the console to exit Maintenance Mode.

**There are five buttons to press:**

- **Forward Control Pad:** + Key, - Key, Play Key.
- **Handlebar Ends:** right side + Key, left side - Key.

**This is the authoritative list of the bike's controls.** A rider or technician counting three keys
has missed the two on the handlebar ends, which duplicate + and - so resistance can be changed
without letting go of the bars.

**The manual gives no failure message.** It prints `TEST PASSED` and nothing else, so a test that
does not pass simply never reaches it - which key is at fault is worked out by watching which press
the console did not register.
