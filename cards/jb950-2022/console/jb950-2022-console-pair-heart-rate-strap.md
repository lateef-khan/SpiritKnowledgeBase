**A strap can only be paired during Warm Up Mode.** If a rider puts a strap on after leaving Warm Up
Mode without pairing, **a console reset is needed** to get back.

1. While in Warm Up Mode, **press and hold the Play Key for 3 seconds.**
2. **`PAIR HR DEVICE`** displays. Press the Play Key to scan. The console may display **`SCAN HR
   DEVICES` up to 12 times.**
3. If sensors are detected, **the number of sensors displays** - for example `2 SENSORS FOUND`.
4. **Press the + Key** to step past a device that is not yours. Each is listed as
   **`MYZONE-12345678`** (the SN# on the MyZone module) or **`ANT+ 12345`** (the device's ID number).
5. **Press the Play Key** to connect the selected strap.
6. **`ENTER AGE`** displays with a default age of **35** flashing. Adjust with + or - and press Play.
7. **`MAX HR`** displays with the estimated max HR flashing. Press Play to accept.
8. `WARM UP MODE` flashes 3 times, then **`PRESS PLAY KEY, WHEN READY TO GO`**. The **HR LED on the
   right side of the console illuminates**.

**The MHR formula is `208 - (0.7 x age)`.** Adjust it with + or - **only if the actual figure is
known, or to adjust it down. Do not adjust it up without medical clearance.**

**The number beside each sensor is a distance, in decibel metres** - it may read as `65DBM`. Usually
the smaller number is your strap, **but not always**: a rider in front of you may be closer to your
console than you are. Know your MyZone SN# (printed on the back of the module) or your ANT+ number
before you start.

**If zero sensors are found:** `Re-scan` displays. **Dampen the HR contacts on the strap if they are
dry** and press Play to re-scan. To ride without heart rate, **press + until the screen shows `DO NOT
CONNECT`**, then Play - `WARM UP` flashes 3 times and the HR field on the lower display stays blank.
After 30 seconds Warm Up Mode recycles and the home screen returns.

**Auto pairing during Warm Up can be switched off entirely** for a facility that does not use heart
rate - `jb950-2022-console-auto-pair-hr`. With it off, this PAIR HR DEVICE route is the only way.
