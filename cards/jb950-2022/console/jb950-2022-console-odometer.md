**Purpose: to track the number of hours the bike has been in use.** Hours, not miles - this odometer
does not count distance at all.

1. Step to the entry in Maintenance Mode. The system **automatically displays `ODOMETER HRS`.**
2. **`HOLD PLAY TO RESET`** appears.
3. To reset the odometer, **press and hold the Play Key.**
4. The system reverts to the setting screen. Press **+** for the next screen, or reset the console to
   exit Maintenance Mode.

**The manual gives two reasons to read it**, and both are studio-management reasons rather than rider
ones:

- To keep track of bike usage **for routine maintenance considerations**.
- **When considering moving bikes around in a studio to even out usage** - the bikes at the front of a
  room wear faster than the ones at the back.

**The reset is one gesture and there is no confirmation step.** Holding Play from this screen zeroes
the counter; nothing asks you whether you meant it. Read the figure before you touch the key.

**This is not the air bikes' odometer**, which counts distance and cannot be zeroed from the menu at
all. Two different Spirit bike consoles, two different meanings for the same word.
