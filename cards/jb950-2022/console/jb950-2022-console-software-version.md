**Purpose: to display the current version of software contained in the console.** The manual says
**knowing the version is necessary for customer service when providing software updates.**

1. Press the **Play Key** to view the software version.
2. Press the **Play Key** to exit to the setting screen.
3. Press **+** for the next screen, or reset the console to exit Maintenance Mode.

**Read it before you call, and again after an update.** It is the only confirmation the manual offers
that an update actually landed - the update procedure's own `UPDATE DONE` message flashes past on the
console reset.

**It is a read-only screen.** Nothing here changes the version; updates come from a blank USB stick in
the port underneath the console - `jb950-2022-console-software-update-usb`.

**Getting to it means pedalling.** The console is powered above 30 RPM, so a technician reading the
version off a bike needs someone on the cranks while they hold +, - and Play for 3 seconds and scroll
fifteen entries.

**The manual prints no version number of its own**, so there is no "should be" figure to compare
against - only what customer service tells you.
