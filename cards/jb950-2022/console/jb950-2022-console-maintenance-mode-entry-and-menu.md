**Maintenance Mode contains sensitive settings for the bike's console.** The console must be powered
to reach it, and the console is powered by the rider: **pedalling above 30 RPM is all that is needed
to illuminate it.** Light to moderate pedalling is needed throughout, to enter the mode and to change
the settings.

**To enter:** simultaneously **press and hold the + Key, - Key and Play Key on the Forward Control
Pad for 3 seconds**. The console displays **`MAINTENANCE MODE`**.

**Then:**

1. Press the **Play Key**. **`KEY TEST`** displays.
2. Press the **+ Key** to scroll through the screens until the one you want displays.
3. Press the **Play Key** to enter the displayed setting.
   - Once inside, use **+ or -** to adjust.
   - Press **Play** to confirm and exit **some but not all** settings.
   - Press **+** to scroll to another screen.

**To leave:** reset the console - **Play Key and + Key together for 3 seconds**, which displays
`CONSOLE RESET`. Or scroll to the **EXIT** entry and press Play, which ends Maintenance Mode, resets
the console and flashes `JOHNNY G`, ready for a new ride.

**The sixteen entries, in the order the manual lists them:**

| # | Entry | # | Entry |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Key Test | 9 | ANT+ ID Setup |
| 2 | Display Test | 10 | Auto Detect HR |
| 3 | Odometer | 11 | HR Zone |
| 4 | Units - English/Metric | 12 | FTP Test 5 Min |
| 5 | Brake Test | 13 | Demo Mode |
| 6 | RPM Sensor | 14 | Calibration |
| 7 | Update Software | 15 | Software Version |
| 8 | LED Brightness | 16 | Exit |

**The tenth entry is named twice, differently.** The contents list on page 51 calls it **Auto Detect
HR**; its own page on 59 heads it **AUTO PAIR HR**. Same setting.

**Two entries are not on the list but are reached through Brake Test** - Motor Test Manual and Limit
Sensor. See `jb950-2022-console-brake-test`.

**This is the largest console menu of any Spirit bike in this repository**, at fifteen printed pages.
It is not the ENT touchscreen menu, which opens on ten presses of the Home icon, and not the air
bikes' three-entry engineering mode, which opens on Start and Enter.
