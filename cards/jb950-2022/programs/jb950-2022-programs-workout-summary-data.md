**To reach it:** press and hold the **Play Key for 3 seconds**; all current data pauses and **END
RIDE or USER** displays. Choose **END RIDE**, press **Play**, then toggle **NO** to **YES** with
**+** or **-** and press **Play**. Pressing Play while **NO** shows reverts to the existing program.

**The screen displays WORKOUT RESULTS, followed by ten figures:**

- **TIME** in minutes
- **DISTANCE** in miles or km
- **WATT AVG**
- **WATT PEAK**
- **SPEED AVG** in miles or km
- **SPEED PEAK** in miles or km
- **HR AVG**
- **HR PEAK**
- **RPM AVG**
- **RPM PEAK**

**SPEED AVG and SPEED PEAK are not really "in miles or km".** The Workout Results page says so,
but the owner's manual's UNITS setting page says "SPD AVG and SPD PEAK in workout results will only
display MPH", and the service manual's UNITS page says the same - "SPD AVG and SPD PEAK in Workout
Results will only display in ENGLISH" - and adds that the Miles for Lives distance goal is imperial
only as well. A console set to metric therefore still reports these two speeds in miles per hour;
see `jb950-2022-console-units-english-metric` and
`jb950-2022-programs-miles-for-lives-program-named-only-in-the-service-manual`.

**Pressing + or - scrolls through the summary screens. If you press nothing, they scroll themselves
every 5 seconds.** Pressing **Play** resets the display and reverts to Warm Up Mode.

**Program results come after those ten.** If the HR Zone, Power Zone or LAP program was enabled for
the workout, its results are shown after the summary data above:

- **POWER ZONE** - percent of total time in each zone: WHITE, BLUE, GREEN, YELLOW, RED **ZONE TIME,
  PCT**.
- **HR ZONE** - percent of time in each zone, the same five colours.
- **LAP** - time and distance for **LAP 1 to LAP 5**. **If no laps were recorded this screen will not
  display.** Only the **last 5 laps** are held in memory.

Press **Play** to display each set of results, then **+** repeatedly to scroll them. Pressing
**Play** returns to WORKOUT RESULTS.

**Warm Up Mode produces no summary at all.** It is the one mode that does not - see
`jb950-2022-programs-warm-up-mode`.
