**These are FTP zones, not heart rate zones.** The HR Zone program uses the same five colours on a
different LED. See `jb950-2022-programs-hr-zone-five-zones-and-led-colours`.

**The Power Zone Program takes a rider's Functional Threshold Power (FTP) in watts and tracks their
performance across 5 training zones, from less than 55% FTP to greater than 106% FTP.**

**The Power Zone LED is on the left side of the console.** Its colour changes with your percentage of
FTP:

| % FTP | LED colour |
|---|---|
| <55% | White |
| 56 - 75% | Blue |
| 76 - 90% | Green |
| 91 - 105% | Yellow |
| >106% | Red |

**To start it:**

1. If you are just beginning and have not paired a strap, pair HR first and pass out of Warm Up Mode
   into the HR Zone program.
2. Use the **USER function** to reach the Power Zone program - see
   `jb950-2022-programs-user-function-runs-three-programs-at-once`.
3. **POWER ZONE OFF or ON** displays. Press **+** to display **POWER ZONE ON**. The Power Zone LED
   illuminates on the lower left of the console. Press **Play**.
4. **ENTER FTP** displays and **150W flashes**. **The default FTP level is 150W.** Press **+** or
   **-** to adjust it if your actual FTP is known, **up to 500**.
5. Press **Play**. **RUN FTP TEST** displays with **NO** flashing. Toggle with **+** or **-**.
6. With **NO** selected, pressing **Play** starts the Power Zone Program. The home screen shows
   **Level, RPM, Time, Distance, Watts, Heart Rate**.
7. Adjust resistance with **+** or **-** on the Forward Control Pad or the handlebar ends.

**The program needs an FTP figure.** If you do not have one, the console can administer an FTP test -
see `jb950-2022-programs-ftp-test-of-five-or-twenty-minutes`.

**The workout summary shows the percentage of total time in each zone**, as **WHITE / BLUE / GREEN /
YELLOW / RED ZONE TIME, PCT**.

**This is not a Constant Watt program.** It does not hold a wattage for you - it colours a light
according to the watts you produce. Resistance stays under your hand throughout.
