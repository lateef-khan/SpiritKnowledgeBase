**To open the menu: while in Warm Up Mode, press and hold the Play Key for 3 seconds.** The first
option displayed is **PAIR HR DEVICE**.

The menu holds four entries:

- **Pair HR Device**
- **Power Zone with FTP Test**
- **Lap Program**
- **Exit**

**Everything defaults to OFF or DISABLED except the HR Zone program, which defaults to ON.** You have
to change a program's setting to ON or ENABLED before it will run. Each program's own page in the
manual describes what it does.

**Pairing a strap starts the HR Zone program automatically.** Once a heart rate strap is paired, the
HR Zone program begins and the **HR LED light in the lower right of the console illuminates**.

**Two more modes are not in this menu**, because they are not optional: **Warm Up Mode**, which is
where the console wakes up, and **Manual Mode**, which is what Warm Up becomes when you press Play.
Manual Mode and the HR Zone program are the same mode - see
`jb950-2022-programs-manual-mode-is-the-hr-zone-program`.

**There are no preset workout profiles.** No Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength or Interval profile,
and no custom segment program. Every mode on this bike is rider-controlled: you set the resistance
yourself with the **+ and - keys** on the Forward Control Pad or the handlebar shifters, in every
program - see `spirit-jb950-specs-twenty-motor-driven-resistance-levels`.

**This bike is unlike the other Spirit indoor cycles.** The XIC600, CIC850, CB900 and CIC800 have no
programs at all.
