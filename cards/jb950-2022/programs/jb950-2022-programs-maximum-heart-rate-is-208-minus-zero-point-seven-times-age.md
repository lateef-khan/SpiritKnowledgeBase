**The formula printed on this console is `208 - (0.7 x age) = MHR`.**

**It is not 220 minus your age.** Every other Spirit bike manual that prints a formula prints 220
minus age; this one does not, and the two disagree everywhere except at age 40. **The JB950 manual
prints no worked example**, but working both formulas out gives 194 against 200 at age 20, 180
against 180 at age 40, and 166 against 160 at age 60. **Do not substitute one formula for the
other, and do not quote a beats-per-minute figure from another Spirit bike's zone card onto this
machine.**

**Where you meet it.** During heart rate pairing - the full pairing procedure is
`jb950-2022-console-pair-heart-rate-strap`:

- **ENTER AGE** displays and **the default age 35 flashes**. Press **+** or **-** to adjust your age,
  then press **Play**.
- **MAX HR** displays and the estimated maximum flashes. **Press Play to accept.**

**You may override it, in one direction only.** The manual says to press **+** or **-** to adjust the
MHR "only if actual is known or wanting to adjust MHR down", and warns: **"Do not adjust up without
medical clearance."**

**Everything else on this console is derived from this number.** The HR Zone program's five zones are
percentages of it - see `jb950-2022-programs-hr-zone-five-zones-and-led-colours`.

**The manual's own training advice uses a different figure again.** Its Training Information page
says to work out "typically, at 60% - 80% of your maximum heart rate", which is general guidance and
does not match the zone bands the console tracks.
