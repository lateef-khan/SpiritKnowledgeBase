**The Manual Mode is also the HR Zone program.** The manual states it plainly: "Once HR is paired
during the Warm Up Mode, HR performance and zones will be tracked." There is no separate HR Zone
button - pairing a strap is what turns Manual Mode into the HR Zone program.

**The only difference between this mode and Warm Up Mode is that the rider gets a workout summary of
their effort.** Everything else is the same: the same home screen, the same rider-controlled
resistance on the **+** and **-** keys.

**To enter it:** at any time during Warm Up Mode, press the **Play Key**. The console displays
**Enjoy THE RIDE**, then the display screen. **Any Time and Distance data collected during Warm Up
Mode is erased.**

**To end it:**

1. When the ride is done, press the **Play Key**. Current data pauses.
2. The screen displays **END WORKOUT**, then **NO** flashes.
3. Press **+** or **-** to toggle between **NO** and **YES**.
4. Pressing **Play** while **NO** shows reverts to Manual Mode - time, distance, intensity and data
   continue from where they paused.
5. Pressing **Play** while **YES** shows ends the workout and displays **WORKOUT RESULTS**.

**This is not the Manual program on a Spirit upright or recumbent bike.** Those ask for time, age and
weight and start at level one. This one asks for nothing and starts wherever the resistance already
was.
