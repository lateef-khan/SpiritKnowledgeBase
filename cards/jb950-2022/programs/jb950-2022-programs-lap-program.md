**The Lap Program allows riders to perform repeated measures of Time and Distance efforts, and
compares your current lap with your previous lap.** You may perform as many laps as you like, **but
only the last 5 laps will display in the workout results.**

**To turn it on:**

1. If you are just beginning and have not paired a strap, pair HR first and pass out of Warm Up Mode
   into the HR Zone program.
2. Use the **USER function** to reach the LAP program. Press **+** to **ENABLE**. **ENABLED is
   required both for the program to run and for lap results to appear in the workout summary.**
3. Press and hold the **Play Key for 3 seconds** to activate it.
4. The home screen shows **LEVEL, RPM, TIME, DISTANCE, WATTS, HEART RATE**. Adjust resistance with
   **+** or **-**.
5. **Until Play is pressed to start a lap, the rider remains in Recovery Mode.**

**To start a lap:** press **Play**. **THIS.LAP** displays on the lower section of the console,
**replacing Watts and Heart Rate**. TIME resets to 0.00 and DISTANCE resets to 0.00. The lap is being
recorded.

**To end a lap:** press **Play** again. The display briefly goes away with **END LAP** shown on the
lower section, then shows TIME and DISTANCE paused with THIS.LAP. **After 5 seconds the full display
returns** with the total accumulated Time and Distance from the beginning of the program, and Watts
and Heart Rate come back. You are now in a recovery phase.

**The next lap:** pressing **Play** again repeats the cycle. THIS.LAP displays, TIME and DISTANCE
reset to 0.00, and **after 5 seconds LAST.LAP appears, flashing 3 times**, showing the total time and
distance from the previous lap. The display shows **LAST.LAP for 3 seconds, then back to THIS.LAP for
5 seconds**, repeating until Play is pressed.

**In the workout summary** the Lap results show **LAP 1 to LAP 5 Time and Distance**, each on its own
screen. **If no laps were recorded this screen will not display.**

**The manual contradicts itself on the LAST.LAP timing.** The "Next lap" paragraph first says
LAST.LAP appears after 5 seconds and flashes 3 times, then that the display shows LAST.LAP for 3
seconds; the closing paragraph says THIS.LAP data displays for 5 seconds, then LAST.LAP for 5
seconds. Both wordings are on the same printed page.
