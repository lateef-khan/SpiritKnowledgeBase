**The service manual names a program the owner's manual never mentions.** Under the UNITS
entry of Maintenance Mode it prints:

> For the MILES FOR LIVES program, distance goal and SPD AVG and SPD PEAK in Workout Results will
> only display in ENGLISH.

That is the only place the words *Miles for Lives* appear in either book. A loose search of the
owner's manual for `miles for lives` and for `distance goal` returns nothing; its own UNITS page
prints the shorter caveat "SPD AVG and SPD PEAK in workout results will only display MPH" and
names no program.

**What the sentence establishes.**

- The console holds a program called **MILES FOR LIVES** that has a **distance goal**.
- Its distance goal, and the **SPD AVG** and **SPD PEAK** figures in the Workout Results, display
  in **English (imperial) units only**, whatever the UNITS setting says.

**What it does not establish.** Neither book says how the program is started, what the goal
defaults to, or whether it appears in the Programs menu. The owner's manual's Programs menu lists
four entries - Pair HR Device, Power Zone with FTP Test, Lap Program and Exit - see
`jb950-2022-programs-program-menu-of-four-entries`. Do not describe Miles for Lives beyond the one
sentence above.

**The units caveat contradicts the Workout Results page.** That page says SPEED AVG and SPEED PEAK
display "in miles or km"; both the owner's UNITS page and this service-manual sentence say they
only ever display in miles per hour - see `jb950-2022-programs-workout-summary-data` and
`jb950-2022-console-units-english-metric`.
