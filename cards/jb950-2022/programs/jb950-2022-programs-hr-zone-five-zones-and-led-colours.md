**These are heart rate zones, not the Power Zone program's FTP zones.** Both use the same five
colours and both have an LED, but they sit on opposite sides of the console and mean different
things. See `jb950-2022-programs-power-zone-five-ftp-zones-and-led-colours`.

**The HR Zone program estimates your Maximum HR (MHR) from the age you enter, then tracks the time
you spend in five heart rate zones, from less than 59% MHR to greater than 90% MHR.**

**The HR LED is on the right side of the console.** Its colour changes with your percentage of
maximum heart rate:

| %MHR | LED colour |
|---|---|
| <59% | White |
| 60 - 69% | Blue |
| 70 - 79% | Green |
| 80 - 89% | Yellow |
| >90% | Red |

**The printed bands leave two gaps.** Nothing is stated for exactly 59-60% or for 89-90%. The manual
prints the table as shown above and the narrative as "less than 59% MHR to greater than 90% MHR".

**To start it:** pair a heart rate strap during Warm Up Mode - `jb950-2022-console-pair-heart-rate-strap` -
then press the **Play Key**. The
console shows **ENJOY THE RIDE** and the home screen: **Level, RPM, Time, Distance, Watts, Heart
Rate**. Any data from Warm Up Mode is erased. Adjust resistance with **+** or **-** on the Forward
Control Pad or the handlebar ends.

**The time spent in each zone appears in the workout summary**, as **WHITE / BLUE / GREEN / YELLOW /
RED ZONE TIME, PCT** - a time and a percentage of total time for each.

**A facility can switch the LED off without losing the tracking.** The zone times still reach the
workout summary with the light off; the Maintenance Mode setting that does it is
`jb950-2022-console-hr-zone-led`.

**This program is on by default.** Of the JB950's programs it is the only one that is - Power Zone
and Lap both default to OFF or DISABLED.
