**Warm Up Mode is the most basic function of the bike and the first thing that happens when the
console wakes from sleep.** Riders use it to prepare for a class, or just to pedal.

On waking, the console displays **JOHNNY G. METHOD**, then **THE RIDE OF TRUTH**, then **WARM UP**
flashing three times, then **PRESS PLAY KEY, WHEN READY TO GO**. The home screen then appears
showing **LEVEL, RPM, TIME, DISTANCE, WATTS, HEART RATE**.

- **Warm Up Mode continues until the Play Key is pressed.** You can stay in it as long as you like.
- **You can adjust the intensity while in it** - press **+** or **-** on either the Forward Control
  Pad or the handlebar ends.
- **Warm Up Mode will NOT display a summary screen.** Only the program modes do.

**Everything you do in Warm Up Mode is thrown away.** Any Time and Distance data collected during
Warm Up is **erased** the moment you press Play to enter Manual Mode or the HR Zone program.

**Warm Up Mode is the only place a heart rate strap can be paired.** Both the Programs menu and the
automatic pairing prompt are reachable only from here. If you put a strap on after leaving Warm Up
Mode, **a console reset is needed** - hold **+** and **Play** together for 3 seconds. See
`jb950-2022-console-pair-heart-rate-strap`.

**This is the console mode, not the five-minute warm-up advice.** The manual separately recommends a
five-minute warm-up and working out at 60% - 80% of maximum heart rate; that is training guidance,
not this mode. See
`spirit-bike-programs-work-out-at-sixty-to-eighty-percent-of-maximum-heart-rate`.
