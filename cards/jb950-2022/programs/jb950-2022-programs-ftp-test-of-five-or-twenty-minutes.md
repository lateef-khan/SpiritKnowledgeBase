**This is the JB950's only fitness test.** It is not the YMCA protocol test that Spirit upright and
recumbent bikes run, and it produces a watts figure rather than a VO2max score.

**Warm up first.** "A thorough warm-up is important for a successful and accurate FTP test. Use Warm
Up Mode for a minimum 5 minutes of steady pedaling and intervals prior to starting FTP test."

**To run it:**

1. Enter the **Power Zone** program. **ENTER FTP** displays with **150W** flashing; adjust with **+**
   or **-** if you already know your FTP. Press **Play**.
2. **RUN FTP TEST** displays with **NO** flashing. Press **+** or **-** to toggle to **YES**, press
   **Play**.
3. **FTP TEST** displays and **5 MIN** flashes. Press **+** or **-** to toggle between a **5-minute**
   and a **20-minute** test, then press **Play**.
4. **WARM UP** displays and **0.30 seconds counts down**. **GO** displays for 3 seconds. The test
   begins and the test time counts down.
5. Adjust resistance with **+** or **-** on the Forward Control Pad or the handlebar ends throughout.

**Which length to pick.** "A 20-minute test is a better measure of a user's FTP, however if choosing
5-minute test because of time constraints, attempt test again later for repeatability of the
results."

**How to pace it.** "Ideal pacing is a steady and consistent effort throughout the duration, where
the average power for the first half is also the same as the average power for the second half.
Listen to your body. Aim for a watt target that can be sustained."

**How it ends and what it scores.**

- If terminated early, the console displays **TEST ENDED EARLY** then reverts to **FTP TEST**.
- If completed, the console displays **TEST COMPLETED** with the FTP watts value.
- **FTP score after test = 95% of Average Watts during test.**
- Press **Play** to return to the Power Zone Program with the FTP score entered.

**This is a two-place fact and the two halves disagree.** This FTP TEST page prints one rule - **FTP
score = 95% of average watts** - while a Maintenance Mode setting elsewhere in the same manual makes
the **5-minute** coefficient a facility choice between **80%** and **85%**, defaulting to **80%**.
Neither page mentions the other. Read the 95% as the figure printed for the test in general, and take
the 80%/85% as what actually applies to a 5-minute test on a given machine - check that machine's
setting before quoting a number. See `jb950-2022-console-ftp-test-coefficient`.

**The manual also prints the training protocols the test is based on**, from *Training and Racing
with a Power Meter* (Allen and Coggan, 2nd Ed 2010):

- **20-minute protocol** - 20 min easy warm-up; 3 x 1-minute speed bursts with a minute rest between
  at 100 RPM; 5 min easy; 5 min all out; 10 min easy; 20-minute steady max effort (the test); 10 to
  15-minute cooldown.
- **5-minute protocol** - 20 min easy warm-up; 3 x 1-minute speed bursts with a minute rest between
  at 100 RPM; 5 min easy; 5 min all out (the test); 10 to 15-minute cool-down.
