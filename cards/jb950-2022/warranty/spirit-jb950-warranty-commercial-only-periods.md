| Warranty | Frame | Electronics | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial | **10 Years** | 2 Years | 2 Years | 1 Year |

**That is the whole table - one row, and it is the Commercial one.** The manual states **no residential terms**. A home owner of this bike has no figures to be quoted, and the contents page heads the section **COMMERCIAL WARRANTY** in as many words. Do not fill the gap from another Spirit bike's Residential row.

**The manual still administers the warranty "directly to a consumer"** where there is no dealer or service organisation, and heads its duty list NORMAL RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE **OWNER**. So the book addresses an individual owner throughout while warranting only a commercial row. It never reconciles the two.

**Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded**, as a footnote directly under the single row (`spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`). It is the only environment clause in the book: the seven-item exclusion list carries no dues-paying item, no residential-setting item and no hours-per-day cap.

**The warranty is dated November 19, 2021 and the book was revised on April 21, 2022.** The back cover reads *JB950 Owners Manual, (c) 2021 All Rights Reserved, Revision: 04.21.2022*. **The copyright line stayed at 2021 with the warranty date**, and only the revision stamp moved - so this is a 2021 book reprinted in 2022 whose warranty page was not re-dated, not a 2022 book carrying an old warranty. The CIC850 shows the same pattern and the same evidence (`spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-three-row-periods`). It is the opposite of the CU900, where the warranty page was replaced and the rest of the book was not.

## The service number is not the Spirit service number

**SERVICE step 3 sends the owner to (866) 463-2662**, not to the (870) 935-1107 that every other Spirit manual in this knowledge base prints in that step. The CUSTOMER SUPPORT paragraph on the previous page gives **1-800-258-8511** for replacement items, and the machine has its own website, **www.johnnygspiritbike.com**, printed as the page footer throughout. The back cover still carries the Spirit Fitness details - 800.258.4555, spiritservice@spiritfitness.com, 3000 Nestle Road, Jonesboro, AR 72401.

**Four numbers in one book, and the one that matters for a warranty claim is (866) 463-2662.** The full step order is `xt-2023-warranty-service-steps`.

## Registration works differently here too

The REGISTER YOUR PURCHASE page offers **no online route at all**: mail the warranty registration card to P.O. Box 2037, Jonesboro, AR 72402, **or call 800-258-8511**. No other Spirit bike manual offers a telephone registration, and none of the others omits the web address. There is **no ten-day deadline** stated anywhere, and the exclusion list carries **no** item excluding an unregistered product - only the reserved right to ask for proof of purchase (`spirit-ce-warranty-registration`).

## These are not the other Spirit bike figures

**The air bikes share this table's Parts 2 Years and Labor 1 Year but halve the frame** to 5 Years, and they print a Residential row with the same figures as their Commercial one (`spirit-air-bike-warranty-periods`). **The indoor cycles warrant Parts for 3 Years** on frames of 10, 15 or Lifetime (`spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-three-row-periods`, `spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-residential-only-periods`).

**The CR900, CU900 and CR900ENT print a one-row Commercial table too, and it is not this one** - Frame Lifetime, Brake 5 Years, Parts 3 Years, Labor 3 Years (`spirit-bike-900-warranty-commercial-periods`). A bike with one commercial row is not evidence about which figures that row holds.

**Damage done during assembly is excluded in as many words** on this manual's pre-assembly page: `spirit-bike-warranty-assembly-damage-excluded`. When the clock starts is `xt-2023-warranty-start-date`.
