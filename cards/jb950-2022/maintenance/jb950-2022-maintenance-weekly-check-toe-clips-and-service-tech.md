Weekly maintenance focuses on the **overall performance** of your Johnny G Spirit Bike. During
these inspections look for **vibration** and possible **loose assemblies**.

Have an **experienced rider ride each bike** to identify and help diagnose any vibration, noises
and any "unusual" feeling from the **drive belt**.

**Either faulty flywheel alignment or a loose belt can cause vibration.** Check for proper flywheel
alignment. **If the flywheel requires adjustment, refer maintenance to a properly trained service
tech.**

Inspect each bike for loose assemblies, parts, bolts and nuts, giving attention to:

- Tighten all frame base hardware.
- Tighten all pop-pin handles.
- Tighten seat hardware.
- **Tighten pedal toe clip / toe straps.**

## Two differences from the air bikes

The AB900 2018 and AB950 2024 print this page almost word for word, with two changes that matter:

1. Where this manual says *refer maintenance to a properly trained service tech*, they say
   **"Torque flywheel nuts as necessary"** — an owner task on those machines, not on this one.
2. They print **three** bullets. The **toe clip / toe straps** bullet is the JB950's own, and it is
   the only Spirit bike manual in the repository that asks for it weekly.

See `spirit-air-bike-maintenance-weekly-vibration-and-flywheel-torque`. Neither card may be quoted
for the other's machines.

## The JB950's monthly page is shorter too

Its MONTHLY MAINTENANCE / INSPECTION AND ADJUSTMENT (printed p. 68) names **pedals and nothing
else**. It prints **no rust and corrosion inspection and no wear-item list** — checked against a
300 dpi render of printed pp. 68 and 70, which recovered no additional text. The air bikes print
both — `spirit-air-bike-maintenance-monthly-rust-and-wear-inspection`.
