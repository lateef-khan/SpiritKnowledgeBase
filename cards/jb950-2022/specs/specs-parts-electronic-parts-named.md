**2.1 Upper Controllers (p. 22)** names four things on the handlebar and console: **FORWARD CONTROL PANEL**
(the + / - / PLAY pad in front of the console), **CONSOLE**, **HANDLEBAR SHIFTERS** (the + and - keys at
the handlebar ends) and **DYNAMIC BRAKING HANDLE** (the lever under the handlebar that applies the brake).
No fan, speaker or display is labelled - this console has none.

**2.2 Lower Controller and Driver (p. 23)** names four more: **SPEED RPM SENSOR**, **BRAKE ASSEMBLY**,
**GENERATOR** and **LOWER CONTROLLER**, all under the chain covers.

**These are the only Spirit bike pages that name a shifter, a braking handle or a brake assembly.** The
book's Electrical Configurations page describes the brake assembly as neodymium magnets for eddy-current
resistance, a gear motor to set the level and a closed-loop encoder for position - the level-control brake
whose wiring is on the specs cards for chapter 4. How this resistance system compares with the other
indoor cycles is on `spirit-cycle-specs-resistance-systems`.

**The same book's page 4 draws the drive system with fifteen numbered parts**, generator and lower control
board among them (`jb950-2022-specs-parts-drive-system-callouts`).

**This book prints no tool list**; the seven-tool list for the JB950 is in its owner's manual
(`jb950-2022-maintenance-tool-list-seven-tools`). No part number is printed on either chapter-2 page.
