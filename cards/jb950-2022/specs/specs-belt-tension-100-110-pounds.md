**"Adjust the belt tension between 100-110 pounds."** That is the whole of the instruction: a
force, not a frequency, and no gauge is named.

It is printed as step 12 of the belt replacement - after the front stabilizer assembly (#5) is
rotated down about 45 degrees, the two M12 nylon nuts (#169) and the M6 eye bolt (#129) are
taken off with a 19 mm open-end wrench, the flywheel assembly is lifted out and the belt swapped
- and printed again, word for word, as step 12 of the brake-assembly replacement, which reuses
the belt pages.

**Which belt is not said.** The drive-system drawing names two, a *Generator Drive Belt* (7) and
a *Timing Belt* (12) (`jb950-2022-specs-parts-drive-system-callouts`); the replacement step calls
its subject "the belt" (#43) and removes the flywheel to reach it, so it is the main drive belt
between the primary and secondary pulleys. **The owner's manual has no belt adjustment at all**
(`jb950-2022-maintenance-no-belt-or-brake-adjustment-procedure`).

The other Spirit indoor cycle in the range is set in hertz, 115-120 Hz on the CIC850
(`cic850-2022-specs-drive-belt-tension-115-120-hz`).
