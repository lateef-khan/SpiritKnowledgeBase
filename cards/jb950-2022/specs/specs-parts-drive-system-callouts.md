The Parts Replacement chapter opens with one drawing of the bike with its chain covers off, and a caption
row of fifteen numbered names. **The numbers are the drawing's own callouts**, printed in a caption under it;
they are not the item numbers the replacement steps use.

| No. | Part |
|---|---|
| 1 | Chain Cover |
| 2 | Front Stabilizer Assembly |
| 3 | Crank Arm |
| 4 | Aluminum Flywheel |
| 5 | Generator |
| 6 | Generator Mounting Bracket |
| 7 | Generator Drive Belt |
| 8 | Secondary Pulley |
| 9 | Primary Pulley |
| 10 | Drum Brake Assembly |
| 11 | RPM Sensor |
| 12 | Timing Belt |
| 13 | Idler Wheel Assembly |
| 14 | Lower Control Board |
| 15 | Brake Assembly |

**Two belts and two brakes.** The drawing separates a **Generator Drive Belt** (7) from a **Timing Belt**
(12), and a **Drum Brake Assembly** (10) - the emergency stop worked by the braking handle - from the
**Brake Assembly** (15), the magnetic level-control brake. The replacement steps that follow use a different
numbering - `#43` for the drive belt, `#57` for the generator, `#87` for the lower control board, `#86` for
the RPM sensor - which are callouts on an exploded view this book does not print, and the JB950 owner's
manual prints no parts list either (`spirit-cycle-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`). **No Spirit part
number is printed anywhere in the book.**
