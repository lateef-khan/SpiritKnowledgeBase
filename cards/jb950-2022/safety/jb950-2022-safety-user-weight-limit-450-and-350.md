**This manual states the rider weight limit twice and the two figures do not agree. Both sentences
are about the weight of the person riding the bike; neither is a shipping weight, a machine weight
or a flywheel weight.**

| Printed page | Section | Exactly as printed |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | IMPORTANT SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS, WARNING block | `Recommended User Weight Limit: 450 lb` |
| 15 | SAFEGUARDS | `The maximum weight for individuals riding the bike should not exceed 350lbs /159kg.` |

**Quote the lower figure, 350lbs, and say the manual also prints 450 lb.** The page 15 sentence is
the stricter of the two and is worded as an absolute maximum; the page 5 line calls itself
*Recommended*. Nothing in the book reconciles them, and there is no third statement anywhere - the
digits `350` and `450` appear nowhere else in the manual except a Taiwanese street address.

## Why this is a defect and not two different things

- **The page 15 wording is the AB900 air bike's wording, unchanged.** The AB900-2018 manual prints
  `The maximum weight for individuals riding the bike should not exceed 350 pounds (159
  kilograms).` in the same position of the same SAFEGUARDS list. The JB950 inherited that page from
  the air bike book.
- **The page 5 line is the JB950's own addition.** It is the single English bullet the JB950's
  safety chapter has that the AB900's does not
  (`spirit-air-bike-safety-user-precautions-list`), and 450 lb is the figure Spirit rates its
  commercial CR and CU bikes at (`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`).

So the likely reading is that the JB950 is rated 450 lb and the SAFEGUARDS page was copied from the
air bike without updating the number. **That is an inference, not something the manual states**, and
it is not safe to give a customer 450 lb on the strength of it.

**Both figures were read from the PDF's own text layer at printed pages 5 and 15, with no page
offset and no OCR involved.** The manual's table of contents lists SAFEGUARDS at page 16 and
WARNING LABELS at page 15; the pages themselves are the other way round, so a reader following the
contents page lands on the wrong one.
