| Warranty | Frame | Brake | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | Lifetime | Lifetime | **5 Years** | 1 Year |

**That is the whole table.** One row, and it is the Residential one: there is no Light Commercial row, no console column and no wear-items column.

**Parts is 5 Years, and it is the only Spirit XB bike manual that says so.** Every other printing of every other XB machine - and the XBR25's own 2023 manual - reads **10 Years** in this column (`spirit-xb-warranty-residential-periods`). Quoting ten years to the owner of this book promises five years the manual does not give.

**The manual prints "5Years" with no space**, exactly as it prints the neighbouring "1 Year" with one. The figure was confirmed on a 300 dpi render of the page, not only in the extracted text.

**The XBR25's own successor undoes the cut.** The XBR25 2023 manual, effective July 25, 2023, prints Parts **10 Years** in the same four-column table. So the parts period on an XBR25 depends entirely on which manual came with the machine, and this one is the shorter. Neither book mentions the other or says the terms changed.

**The other three columns are the range's usual figures.** Frame Lifetime, Brake Lifetime and Labor 1 Year are what every XB manual prints; only Parts moves.

## Two more things this manual does on its own

**It names the warrantor in full.** Its opening clause reads *Spirit Fitness, Inc. (Spirit Fitness) warrants all its **fitness bike** parts* - the only XB manual that both introduces the company that way and calls the machine a generic *fitness bike* in the warranting clause rather than a recumbent or upright bike. The heading above the table still reads *Semi-Recumbent Bike Warranty - Effective February 4, 2019*.

**It warrants a home and nothing else.** Its responsibilities list opens "ordinary household use", its exclusion 2 refuses service-call reimbursement outside ordinary household use, and its exclusion 7 bars "any environment other than a residential setting" - three statements that agree, which is not true of every manual in this range (`spirit-xb-warranty-use-environment`).

When the clock starts is in the paragraph above the table (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`), and the one-year labor figure is dated separately in the SERVICE paragraph (`xt-2023-warranty-labor-period`).
