Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. Hardware quantities are on
[the hardware card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xbr25-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Step one - rear stabilizer and seat back

1. Install the **Rear Stabilizer (7)** onto the Main Frame with 2 **Bolts (65)** and
   washers **(84)**. **The step says four washers and the hardware table holds two, and
   the step never mentions the 2 Cap Nuts (191) the table does hold** - see the hardware
   card. The XBR55 and XBR95 books, and the XBR25 2023 book, all say two washers and two
   cap nuts.
2. Install the **Seat Back (5)** onto the **Seat Carriage (4)** with 2 **Bolts (67)**
   through the **top** holes, secured with 2 **Flat Washers (77)** and **Nyloc Nuts
   (89)**; then 2 **Bolts (142)** through the **side** holes, secured with 2 more Flat
   Washers (77) and nuts (89).
3. Insert an **End Cap (32)** into each opening of the front stabilizer tube. A rubber
   mallet may be needed; **no mallet is in the carton**.

**This is the step that separates the XBR25 from the rest of the family.** Four bolts
through two sets of holes, and no instruction to leave the seat back free to pivot -
where the XBR55 and XBR95 use two bolts and tell you to leave front-to-back movement.

## Step two - console mast, console and handlebar

1. Slide the **Computer Cable (44)** through the bottom of the **Console Mast Cover
   (31)** and then the **Console Mast (2)**, cover the right way up.
2. Install the Console Mast (2) into the receiving tube of the **Main Frame (1)**,
   without pinching cable 44. Insert 2 **Bolts (68)** and 2 **Flat Washers (76)** on each
   side, and 2 Bolts (68) and 2 **Curved Washers (83)** on the front. Tighten all six
   firmly with the **Wrench (112)**.
3. Remove the white Styrofoam pad, factory-fitted to stop bolts dropping down the mast
   tube.
4. Insert the Computer Cable (44) and the **Hand Pulse Cable (45)** into their connectors
   behind the **Console Assembly (19)** and fix the console to the mounting plate with 4
   **Phillips Head Screws (99)**, using the **Phillips Head Screwdriver (114)**. Attach
   the **Handle Bar Assembly (3)** to the Console Mast (2) bracket with 2 Bolts (68), 2
   **Split Washers (82)** and 2 Flat Washers (76).

**Two cables, not three.** The XBR55 and XBR95 route a third, the Handlebar Resistance
Control Cable (148). The XBR25 has no handlebar resistance control.

## Step three - seat and seat handlebar

1. Install the **Seat (61)** on the Seat Carriage (4) with 4 **Phillips Head Screws
   (98)**.
2. Attach the **Seat Handle Bar (6)** to the Seat Carriage (4) with 4 **Bolts (71)**, 4
   **Flat Washers (77)** and 4 **Nyloc Nuts (89)**, using the wrenches provided
   (**112 & 132**).
3. Plug the **Hand Pulse Sensor Cables (27)** into the left side of the rear plastic
   cover.

The XBR55 and XBR95 name two rear holes and a third front hole here, for the resistance
cables this machine does not have.

## Step four - pedals and bottle holders

1. Attach the **Pedals (L, R) (116, 117)** to the **Crank Arms (51L, 51R)**, tightening
   with the **Wrench (132)**. The **left pedal is reverse-threaded** and goes on
   counterclockwise; there is an **"L" stamped in the end of the threaded post** of the
   left pedal and an "R" in the right. **No torque figure is printed** - see
   [the pedal card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-bike-assembly-pedal-tighten-as-much-as-possible.md).
2. Attach the **Drink Bottle Holders (L39, R38)** to the sides of the Seat Handle Bar (6)
   with 4 **Sheet Metal Screws (105)**, using the Phillips Head Screwdriver (114).

**There is no gas cylinder step.** The XBR55 and XBR95 fit one here with a bolt, washer
and nyloc nut; this machine has none, and its step-four hardware table holds the four
sheet metal screws and nothing else.

## The build ends here

No first power-on step, no levelling step, and no way to move the machine printed
anywhere in the book - see
[the absence card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xb-assembly-no-moving-or-transport-section.md).
The carton packs a Front Stabilizer Cover and a Rear Stabilizer Cover that no step fits.

## The 2023 XBR25 builds the same machine in a different order

Its step two runs the styrofoam pad and the console onto separate numbered items and its
step four drops the "L" and "R" stamp sentence into the same paragraph. Its hardware is
identical. See [that card](../../xbr25-2023/assembly/xbr25-2023-assembly-procedure.md).
