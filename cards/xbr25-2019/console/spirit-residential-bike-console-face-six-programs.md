**The drawing that opens the CONSOLE OPERATION chapter labels six things:**

| Call-out as printed | Where it sits |
|---|---|
| **Large Blue-LED Matrix Window** | Centre |
| **Message Center** | Below the matrix window |
| **Fan to keep you cool** | Right of the housing |
| **Convenient cargo compartment for keys, phone, or MP3 player** | Lower left |
| **Six innovative programs offer a variety of work-outs** | The program key row |
| **Easy-Touch Control Buttons** | Bottom |

**The key legend under the buttons reads MANUAL, CARDIO, HILL, FATBURN, INTERVAL, HR.** Six keys, and
the drawing's "six innovative programs" agrees with them.

**There is no Heart Rate % Profile call-out**, and no bar graph anywhere in this book. The seventh
call-out that every other blue-LED Spirit bike drawing carries is simply not on this one, and no
paragraph in the manual describes a percent-of-maximum graph. **Do not quote the amber-green-red
bands for this machine**: `spirit-xe-console-heart-rate-bar-graph-in-amber-green-and-red`.

**Nor is there a muscle figure.** The console panel carries no MUSCLE PROFILE silk-screen and the
manual prints no Muscle Activation Figure paragraph.

**The page is a flattened image.** `pdftotext` returns 35 words from it; the call-outs and the key
legend above were read by rendering PDF page 16 at 300 dpi.

**Four more things this console section never describes**, checked by loose-word search across the
whole book as well as by reading the pages: a **1/4 Mile Track** paragraph, a **Pulse Grip Feature**
paragraph, a **Speakers** paragraph and any mention of **Bluetooth, an app or a USB port**. The words
Bluetooth and USB appear nowhere in the manual, and "app" appears only inside "appliance". The bike
still reports **total Laps** on its Workout Summary and still scans **Laps completed** in its Message
Center, so the lap count exists without a track being drawn for it.

**Its 2023 successor is a different console again** - a workout-mode screen, Direct Resistance Level
Buttons and a Pulse Grip Feature paragraph, but still no USB and still no Bluetooth:
`spirit-residential-bike-console-face-without-usb-or-bluetooth`.

**Ten-program siblings are on their own card**: `spirit-residential-bike-console-face-ten-programs`.
