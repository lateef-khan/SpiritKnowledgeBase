> Never expose this product to rain or moisture. This product is not designed for use outdoors,
> near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment. The operating temperature
> specification is 5 to 48 degrees Celsius (40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit) and humidity is 95%
> non-condensing (no water drops forming on surfaces).

| Limit | Figure |
|---|---|
| Operating temperature | 5 to 48 degrees Celsius (40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit) |
| Humidity | 95%, non-condensing |

**Non-condensing** is the manual's own gloss - no water drops forming on surfaces.

**The two temperature scales do not convert exactly.** 5 degrees Celsius is 41 F and 48 degrees
Celsius is 118.4 F, so the printed `40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit` is a rounded pair rather than
the conversion. Both are printed; quote whichever scale the customer asked in, and do not
recalculate one from the other.

**This is a range, where the XT manuals print a single figure.** The 2015 and 2023 XT manuals say
only `The temperature specification is 40 degrees C, and humidity is 95%, non-condensing` with no
minimum (`xt-2023-safety-operating-environment-limits`). The 4.0T gives both ends.

**The 7.0T and 8.0T are much narrower**, at 10 to 36 degrees Celsius and 30 to 90% humidity, and
add air pressure, altitude and storage figures the 4.0T does not print:
`spirit-2026t-safety-operating-environment-limits`. Do not carry those onto a 4.0T, or this range
onto them.

The manual prints no storage or transport temperature for the 4.0T, and no altitude limit. Its
safety instructions page states the short form of the rule as item 8, `Do not use outdoors`.
