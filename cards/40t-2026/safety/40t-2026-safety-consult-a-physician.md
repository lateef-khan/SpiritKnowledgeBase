> 1. Before beginning exercise on this product, or any exercise program, consult a physician. This
>    is especially important for persons over the age of 35 or persons with preexisting health
>    conditions.

- **Everyone**, before beginning this or any exercise program.
- **Especially over the age of 35**, or with a pre-existing health condition. 35 is the only age
  figure this manual prints for an adult user.
- The manual asks for a consultation, not a written clearance, and does not say to repeat it.

**The 7.0T and 8.0T manuals ask for something different.** They require **medical clearance before
any rigorous exercise program** and name a history of heart disease or other high risk factors
rather than an age (`spirit-2026t-safety-medical-clearance-and-exertion`). No age 35 appears in
either of those manuals, and no clearance requirement appears in this one - do not swap the two.

The 2015 and 2023 XT manuals print the age 35 rule in the same words this manual does.
