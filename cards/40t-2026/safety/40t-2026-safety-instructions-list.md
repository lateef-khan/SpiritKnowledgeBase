**This is the 4.0T's own list. It is not the 7.0T and 8.0T list** - those two share a different
list of a different length, written for a commercial rehabilitation machine
(`spirit-2026t-safety-instructions-list`).

The page opens:

> To reduce risk of injury to persons: Read all instructions before using this appliance.
> WARNING - To reduce the risk of electric shock, disconnect your 4.0T from the electrical outlet
> prior to cleaning and/or service work.

1. Consult a physician before beginning, especially over the age of 35 or with pre-existing health
   conditions - `40t-2026-safety-consult-a-physician`.
2. Install on a flat level surface with access to a 120-volt, 20-amp grounded outlet -
   `spirit-2026t-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`.
3. No extension cord unless it is 1 mm2 or better with one outlet on the end; the treadmill the
   only appliance on the circuit; never disable the grounded plug -
   `40t-2026-safety-extension-cord-1-mm2-or-better`.
4. Keep children away; obvious pinch points and other caution areas -
   `spirit-2026t-safety-keep-children-away`.
5. Children should be supervised to ensure that they do not play with the appliance.
6. Keep hands away from all moving parts.
7. Never drop or insert any object into any openings.
8. Do not use outdoors.
9. To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position and then remove the plug from the outlet.
10. Do not operate under a blanket or pillow. Excessive heating can occur and cause fire, electric
    shock, or injury to persons.
11. Do not operate on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet - `40t-2026-safety-placement-and-floor`.
12. Do not use for any purpose other than the one it is intended for.
13. Do not operate where aerosol products are used or oxygen is administered -
    `spirit-2026t-safety-aerosol-and-oxygen`.
14. Never operate with a damaged cord or plug; call your dealer -
    `spirit-2026t-safety-power-cord-condition-and-routing`.
15. Keep the cord away from heated surfaces.
16. The hand pulse sensors are not medical devices -
    `spirit-2026t-safety-heart-rate-readings-are-not-medical`.
17. Heart rate monitoring may be inaccurate; over exercising may result in serious injury or death -
    `spirit-2026t-safety-over-exercise-can-cause-injury-or-death`.
18. Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable. Quality
    athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue.
19. Never leave the product unattended when plugged in. Unplug when not in use and before putting
    on or taking off parts.
20. Connect to a properly grounded outlet only - `spirit-product-must-be-grounded`.
21. Children aged 8 and above, and persons with reduced capabilities, may use it with supervision
    or instruction - `40t-2026-safety-children-aged-8-and-above`.
22. *(Unfinished boilerplate: "Suitable information about replacement parts that could affect the
    safe use of the treadmill." It names a subject and gives no information.)*
23. *(Unfinished boilerplate: 'The following statement: "Noise emission under load is higher than
    without load."')*
24. *(Unfinished boilerplate: "If applicable, the value of the A-weighted emission sound pressure
    level at the trainer's ear." No such value is printed anywhere in this manual.)*
25. Don't change any component by yourself -
    `spirit-2026t-safety-do-not-modify-or-fit-aftermarket-parts`.
26. Sound pressure level under 70dB, measured at 8 km/hr with no load -
    `40t-2026-safety-sound-pressure-level`.
27. Injuries to health may result from incorrect or excessive training.
28. Remove tether cord after use to prevent unauthorized treadmill operation -
    `spirit-2026t-safety-secure-the-tether-cord-after-use`.
29. Mount and stand on the step rails before starting; start at a low speed -
    `40t-2026-safety-getting-on-and-off-the-treadmill`.
30. **User Weight Limit: 440 lbs** - `spirit-2026t-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lbs`.

The boxed note beside the list:

> Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements
> of your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine.

**Three of the thirty items are unfinished.** Items 22, 23 and 24 are printed as instructions to
the manual's own author rather than to the reader. Item 23's quoted sentence is repeated properly
as part of item 26; items 22 and 24 supply nothing.

**This list has no clearance figures, no tether-cord description, and no commercial-use statement.**
The 7.0T and 8.0T lists carry all three.

**The 2025 printing of the 4.0T manual prints this page word for word**, so it holds for the 40t-2025 as well. The February 2025 book (revision 02.04.25) and its May 2025 reprint (revision 05.19.25) differ only in the version and revision line; both were compared with the 2026 book page by page and no rule or figure on the safety, electrical, grounding or operation pages changed.
