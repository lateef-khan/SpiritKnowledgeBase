Two safety instructions answer this, and the manual never gathers them.

> 2. To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons, install the
>    treadmill on a flat level surface with access to a 120-volt, 20-amp grounded outlet.

> 11. Do not operate treadmill on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and
>     treadmill may result.

- **A flat, level surface**, within reach of the required outlet
  (`spirit-2026t-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`).
- **Not on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet.** The reason the manual gives is damage to both
  the carpet and the treadmill, not instability.
- The manual does not forbid carpet generally - only the three kinds it names.

**This manual prints no clearance distances.** There is no figure for space at the sides, front or
rear anywhere in it. Do not fill the gap with the 7.0T's three and a half feet, the 8.0T's six
feet six inches, or the XT manuals' 20in and 6ft - those are other machines' figures
(`70t-2026-safety-clearance-around-the-treadmill`,
`80t-2026-safety-clearance-around-the-treadmill`, `xt-2023-safety-placement-and-clearance`). The
honest answer for a 4.0T is that its manual gives none.

The room itself has limits the same manual does state - 5 to 48 degrees Celsius, 95% humidity, and
never outdoors or near a pool: `40t-2026-safety-operating-environment-limits`.
