> 21. This appliance can be used by children aged from 8 years and above and persons with reduced
>     physical, sensory or mental capabilities or lack of experience and knowledge if they have
>     been given supervision or instruction concerning use of the appliance in a safe way and
>     understand the hazards involved. Children shall not play with the appliance. Cleaning and
>     user maintenance shall not be made by children without supervision.

Item 5 adds: `Children should be supervised to ensure that they do not play with the appliance.`

- **8 years is the minimum age**, and use above it is conditional: supervision or instruction in
  safe use, plus understanding of the hazards.
- **The same permission covers a person with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities**,
  or one lacking experience and knowledge, on the same conditions.
- **Playing with the machine is forbidden at any age.**
- **Cleaning and user maintenance may not be done by a child without supervision.**

**This is the opposite of what the older Spirit manuals say**, and the difference matters. The
2023 XT manuals state flatly that the equipment `is not intended for use by persons with reduced
physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge`, and the 2015 XT
manuals add `Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine`
(`xt-2015-safety-who-may-use-the-treadmill`). The 4.0T manual permits both groups under
supervision, from age 8. Answer each machine from its own manual.

**The same manual also says, three items earlier, to keep children away from the treadmill**
because of its pinch points (`spirit-2026t-safety-keep-children-away`). The manual never
reconciles the two; read item 4 as the rule when nobody is supervising and item 21 as the
condition under which a child may use it.

**The 7.0T and 8.0T manuals print no age at all.** They allow children only `at the discretion of
a Qualified Physical Therapist and or parental consent`
(`spirit-2026t-safety-intended-patient-population`), so the age-8 figure is a 4.0T statement.
