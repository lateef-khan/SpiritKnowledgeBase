- **Safety key** - fits on the console and activates all functions. With no safety key the console cannot
  be controlled.
- **Console** - the interface that controls all functions of the treadmill; key controls and an LED display.
- **Main controller** - the circuit board that carries the AC power supply for the console, the incline
  driver and the AC motor driver, and links the console to output the voltages for the "DC control
  Board". The general-information line lists power supply, AC motor, incline motor, AC control Board
  control circuit and incline control circuit.
- **AC motor** - controls speed increases and decreases. **No work voltage is printed** here; the owner's
  manual's 4.0HP AC is the only motor figure the machine has (`40t-2026-specs-drive-motor-rating`).
- **Incline motor** - a **120 volt AC** motor with four wires (red, black, white, green) plus one 3-pin
  position-sensor cable. AC on **red = UP**, AC on **black = DOWN**, **white = COM (neutral)**, **green =
  ground**.

The chapter is the CT850-2020 text reused, down to the "DC control Board" phrase on an inverter machine.
It says nothing about the parts this machine adds: the **rear incline motor** and the **rear incline
interface board** with its relay, which the driver-board pages and the circuit diagram show.

The ST8700A-ST026-01 revision of the same book (spirit-treadmill-40t-2026-service-manual-st8700a) prints this page unchanged; only the product photographs in chapter 2 were replaced by the black Spirit-branded machine.
