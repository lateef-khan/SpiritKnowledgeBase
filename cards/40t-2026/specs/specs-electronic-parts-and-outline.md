**Outlines (pp. 3-4)** are two product photographs with no call-outs.

**Upper controllers (p. 6)** - **Cooling FAN**, **Safety key**, **DISPLAY**.

**Lower controller and driver (p. 7)** - **INCLINE MOTOR**, **FILTER**, **AC MOTOR**, **REAR INCLINE
INTERFACE BOARD**, **INVERTER**. The filter and the rear incline interface board are the two call-outs no
CT book has; the rear incline motor itself is not called out here, only on the circuit diagram and in
the replacement procedure.

The chapter carries no dimension, machine weight, user weight or power rating; the owner's manual's
absence card lists what that book does and does not give.
