**This manual has no Specifications section and no Application Specification
section.** The table of contents runs Maintenance Schedule 46, Maintenance Mode
47, Exploded View 48, Parts List 49, Warranty 51. There is no specification page
anywhere in the book, and none of these figures is printed in it:

- assembled or folded dimensions, and floor footprint
- product weight or shipping weight
- running deck or running belt size
- speed range - no top speed, minimum speed or reverse speed figure
- incline or decline range
- a certifications list, or the manufacturer's name and address

**Do not answer any of these from the 2026 7.0T or 8.0T manual.** Those two books
do print a full specification table, and every figure in it belongs to a
different machine - the 8.0T alone weighs 252.5 kgs (556.7 lbs) and runs to
22 km/h. Get the 4.0T figures from Spirit Fitness or the product spec sheet, and
say the manual does not state them.

**What this manual does state.**

- **Motor: 4.0HP AC**, in the Electrical Safety bullets on page 6, next to the
  power requirement. See `40t-2026-specs-drive-motor-rating`.
- **Operating temperature 5 to 48 degrees Celsius (40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit)
  and humidity 95% non-condensing**, on the same page. It is the only
  environmental figure in the book, and it is **not** the 10 to 36 degrees C
  range the 7.0T and 8.0T give.
- **User Weight Limit: 440 lbs**, as item 30 of the Safety Instructions.
- A **full parts list and exploded view diagram** - see
  `40t-2026-specs-parts-list`. The 7.0T and 8.0T print the diagram with no list
  beside it.

**The service manual adds four things and still no specification table.** It sets the
**drive belt at 70 to 75 lbs on a belt tension gauge** (`spirit-ct850-drive-belt-tension`),
gives the **incline motor as a 120 volt AC motor** with no drive-motor voltage
(`40t-2026-specs-electrical-part-descriptions`), names the **KSP485 AC motor, RM6T6-1003
inverter and RFMB 20A breaker** on its circuit diagram (`40t-2026-specs-circuit-diagram`), and
prints the speed bounds only as factory settings - minimum 0.5 (0.3 to 0.7), maximum 12.0
(adjustable down to 10.0) - which are console settings, not a rated range. It prints no
dimension, weight, deck size or horsepower either.

**The 2025 owner's manual - both printings - is the same book on this point.** Its table of contents
runs Machine Care 41, Troubleshooting 44, Maintenance Schedule 45, Maintenance Mode 46, Exploded View
47, Parts List 48, Warranty 50; it prints the same 4.0HP AC motor bullet, the same 5 to 48 °C
operating range and the same 440 lb user weight limit, and nothing else. The ST8700A revision of the
service manual adds no figure the ST017 book lacks.
