**Motor: 4.0HP AC.**

That one bullet on the Electrical Safety page is the only motor figure in the
manual - there is no specification table at all, see
`40t-2026-specs-no-specification-table`. No wattage, no continuous-duty rating
and no motor part number is given; the parts list calls the part simply "AC
Motor", item 44.

**4.0 HP is this machine's figure and no other's.** The 2026 7.0T and 8.0T print
**3.0HP AC** in the same bullet position - a smaller number on the larger
machines - so never carry the figure between them. See
`spirit-2026t-specs-drive-motor-rating`.

The bullet directly below it gives the power requirement: 120 volts, 60 Hz,
dedicated 20-amp circuit, NEMA 5-20P plug and receptacle type. That is the
electrical-safety question rather than this one.
