The low maintenance (routine monthly cleaning), dual-sided hard wax deck is designed to withstand
up to **20,000 miles (32,000 kilometers) on each side**.

If the original side of the deck shows significant wear, it needs to be **flipped**. Contact your
service technician for assistance.

**Do not apply any type of lubricant or wax to the surface** —
`spirit-2026t-maintenance-deck-takes-no-lubricant`.

The schedule table on page 46 lists "Inspect for deck wear — Visual" **semi-annually**; that
inspection is what tells you the side is done. See `40t-2026-maintenance-schedule`.

## The text layer carries a second, smaller figure that is not printed

The PDF's text layer holds a second "GENERAL MAINTENANCE" block on this page that gives the deck life as
**"20,000 kilometers on each side"**. Renders of the 2025 (PDF p. 44) and 2026 (PDF p. 45) pages, made on
2026-09-11, show **only 20,000 miles (32,000 kilometers) printed**; the kilometre-only sentence is an invisible
OEM layer, not an overlay a customer can read. So the manual is not ambiguous on the page: **quote 20,000
miles**, which is also the figure the Spirit CT900 and CT900ENT owner's manuals print for the same deck
(`ct900-belt-deck-cleaning`, `ct900ent-belt-deck-cleaning`). The 2013 CT850 prints 20,000 miles too, with no
metric equivalent.

## The 2025 4.0T prints the same figure

The February and May 2025 printings of the 4.0T book - one document - carry this paragraph word for word:
**20,000 miles (32,000 kilometers) on each side**, flip when the original side shows significant wear, contact
your service technician, no lubricant or wax. The **7.0T 2025 and 2026 and the 8.0T 2026 print no deck-life
figure at all** on their pages; the sentence exists only in their invisible text layer.
