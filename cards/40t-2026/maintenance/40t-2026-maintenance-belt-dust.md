Belt dust occurs during **normal break-in**, or until the belt stabilizes. Wiping excess off with a
damp cloth will minimize buildup.

It is not a fault and needs no part.

The 7.0T and 8.0T manuals carry this paragraph only inside the duplicated "GENERAL MAINTENANCE"
block that overlays their belt-and-deck page; neither machine's own text mentions belt dust, so
neither is listed here.

## The four 2024 New Black Units treadmills print it on the visible page

The **CT800 2024**, **CT850 2024**, **CT800ENT 2024** and **CT850ENT 2024** print the same two
sentences under *Belt Dust*, in their own text rather than in an overlay. Word for word with the
4.0T, and no interval or quantity is given on any of them.

Black particles on the floor are the same observation seen from underneath, and older Spirit
manuals card it as a fault question instead: `ct900-black-particles-under-treadmill-normal`.
