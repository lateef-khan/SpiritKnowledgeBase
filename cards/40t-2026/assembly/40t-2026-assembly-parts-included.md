- 1 Main Frame
- 2 Console Mast
- 1 Main Panel
- 1 Console
- 1 Console Bracket Cover
- 4 Handrail Cover

That is the whole printed list. It names **no lubricant, no power cord and no
hardware kit line**, and it gives no item numbers - the item numbers appear only
in the assembly steps and the hardware tables.

The two handrails themselves - Left Handrail (74) and Right Handrail (75),
which step three bolts on - are not on this list either. Four Handrail Covers
are.

The 7.0T prints a different kind of panel - photographs of the fasteners bagged
per step - and the 8.0T prints no carton list at all.
