1. Plug the treadmill into an appropriate wall outlet.
2. Turn on the power switch, **located at the front of the treadmill below the
   motor hood**.
3. **Ensure the safety key is installed** - the treadmill will not power on
   without it.

When the power switch is turned on the console goes to the start up display,
also known as **Idle Mode**, and the Message Window scrolls the start up
message. You may now begin to use the machine.

That is all this manual prints. **It describes no self-test, no software
version, no odometer and no warm-up delay.** Do not fill those in from another
Spirit treadmill: the XT385-2023, XT485-2023 and XT685-2023 run a lamp-lighting
self-test that shows a version number and an odometer, and the CT900 takes
around ten seconds to come up. Neither sequence is printed for this machine.

The 7.0T and 8.0T describe their power-up differently again, as an input module
with its own circuit breaker.
