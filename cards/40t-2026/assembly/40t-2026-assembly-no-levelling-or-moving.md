This machine's SETTING UP YOUR TREADMILL page holds three things only: Power On,
C-Safe, and the safety tether cord. So the manual says nothing about:

- **Levelling.** No levelling foot, no levelling glide, no wrench size, no
  adjustment procedure. The words do not appear anywhere in the manual.
- **Folding or unfolding.** The word "fold" is not printed anywhere.
- **Moving or transporting the machine.** A Transportation Wheel appears in the
  parts list as item 20, and transport wheels are named once inside the
  electrical warning that tells you to route the power cord away from moving
  parts - and nowhere else. There is no procedure for rolling the machine.

Absence is the answer here, not a gap to fill from a sibling machine. **Do not
answer these questions from the 7.0T or 8.0T cards.** Those two document
levelling glides on the rear incline unit adjusted with a half-inch wrench; this
machine has no rear incline unit and the procedure is not printed for it.

The manual does say, in its belt-tracking section, that a treadmill's
performance depends on the frame running on a reasonably level surface and that
constant belt adjustment may be needed if the frame is not level. That is a
statement about the floor, not an instruction to adjust anything on the machine.
