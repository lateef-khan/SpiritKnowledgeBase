**This machine's roller procedure has six steps, not five: its rear end caps take four screws each side, and an
Adjustment Rail Pad comes off before the rear roller.** The CT850 books release two end-cap screws and have no
rail pad step.

1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **4 screws** for the rear roller end caps and remove the end caps.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **4 screws** for the **Adjustment Rail Pad** and remove it.
3. Use an **M8 L-shape Allen wrench** to two screws for the rear rollers and remove the roller.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the screws and remove the left and right motor base caps
   (**4 Phillips head screws**). Then remove the motor top cover (**6 tapping screws**). Use a **14mm wrench** to
   loosen the tension screw and the **M8 L-shape Allen wrench** to loosen the motor adjusting bolts and the
   **4 securing bolts**, and loosen the motor belt.
5. Use the M8 L-shape Allen wrench to release the front roller screw and remove the front roller. Replace with
   new front and rear rollers and resume all parts in reverse order.
6. Adjust the belt tension and run the belt in the centre.

Two defects in the printing, quoted as they stand:

- Step 3 is missing its verb - "Use M8 L-shape Allen wrench to two screws for rear rollers and remove the roller".
- Step 6 says "Refer to step 11.3 (5) for adjusting the belt tension". **There is no section 11 in this manual.**
  The belt-tension step is step 5 of Motor Replacement, section 9.3, which sets the motor belt to 70~75 lbs with
  a tension gauge; that procedure is on the shared drive-motor card.

The manual prints no torque figure and no part number for either roller.

The **ST8700A-ST026-01 revision** of this book (`spirit-treadmill-40t-2026-service-manual-st8700a`, cover "4.0T-740885 (ST8700A-ST017-01)") prints this procedure word for word - its chapter 9 measures 99.8% the ST017 chapter at word level, one space apart.
