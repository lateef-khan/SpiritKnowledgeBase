Chapter 9, "Disassembling and assembling of Parts", holds nine procedures. The body headings run 9.1 to 9.9
except the console page, which is headed **10.2**:

| Contents | Body heading | Procedure | Where the steps are |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9.1 | 9.1 | Lower Controller Replacement | shared with the CT850 2016 card |
| 9.2 | **10.2** | Console Replacement | this machine's own card |
| 9.3 | 9.3 | Motor Replacement | shared with the CT850 2016 card |
| 9.4 | 9.4 | AC Power Switch Replacement | shared CT850 card |
| 9.5 | 9.5 | Front/ Rear Roller Replacement | this machine's own card |
| 9.6 | 9.6 | Running Deck/ Belt & Cushion Replacement | shared CT850 card |
| 9.7 | 9.7 | Speed Sensor Replacement (including the wire) | shared with the CT850 2016 card |
| 9.8 | 9.8 | Incline Motor Replacement | this machine's own card, 257mm |
| 9.9 | 9.9 | Rear Incline Motor Replacement | this machine's own card, 210mm |

Six of the nine match a CT850 2016 or CT850 2020 procedure step for step and figure for figure, so those
facts live on the CT850 cards with this machine added. The three that differ - the console (eight Phillips screws, no
Allen bolts), the rollers (four-screw end caps and an Adjustment Rail Pad) and the incline motor (257mm, not
235mm) - have their own cards, and the rear incline motor has no counterpart in any CT850 book.

The manual's own cross-references: the deck procedure correctly sends you to 9.5 for the rollers; the roller
procedure sends you to "step 11.3 (5)" for belt tension, and **there is no section 11** - the tension step is
9.3 step 5.

Neither a torque value nor a tool list is printed inside this chapter. The "Prepare tools" page in the
troubleshooting chapter is a separate list.
