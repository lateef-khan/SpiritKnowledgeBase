**Zero the incline motor before you take it off the machine or fit it onto the frame. The zeroing distance is
257mm.** This is the front (elevation) motor; the rear incline motor on the same machine zeroes at a different
figure and has its own card.

1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the screws and remove the left and right motor base caps and the
   motor top cover. Turn the treadmill to stand on one side, remove the cables connected on the lower controller
   that run to the incline motor, then remove the incline motor with a **17mm open end wrench**.
2. Use the **17mm open end wrench** to install the new incline motor and resume all cable connections.

The steps are the same two steps the CT850 2020 and CT800 2020 books print, but **257mm is this machine's
figure and 235mm is theirs**. Do not carry either number across.

The manual gives the 257mm as a bare figure. It does not say between which two points the distance is
measured, and it does not say which way to turn the barrel to reach it.

The **ST8700A-ST026-01 revision** of this book (`spirit-treadmill-40t-2026-service-manual-st8700a`, cover "4.0T-740885 (ST8700A-ST017-01)") prints this procedure word for word - its chapter 9 measures 99.8% the ST017 chapter at word level, one space apart.
