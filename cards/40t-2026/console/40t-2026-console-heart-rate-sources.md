The machine has a built-in heart rate monitoring system with **two sources**:

- **grasping the hand pulse sensors**, or
- **wearing a heart rate chest belt transmitter**.

Either one starts heart rate detection. The **Message Window displays your heart
rate, or Pulse, in beats per minute**. This may take a few seconds.

**You must enter your correct age during program setup** for the heart rate
control program to be accurate.

The manual gives no accuracy figure and no pairing procedure for the grips.

**The 2025 printings of the 4.0T owner's manual - February (Revision 02.04.25) and May (Revision 05.19.25), one document twice - print this section word for word on the same page**, so this card covers the 40t-2025 as well. **What strap those 2025 printings name is not this book's Bluetooth or ANT+ but 5 kHz** (`spirit-2026t-console-chest-strap-5khz`).
