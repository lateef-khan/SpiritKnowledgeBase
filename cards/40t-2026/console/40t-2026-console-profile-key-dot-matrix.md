The dot matrix window shows graphic feedback and has **three basic displays** for
most programs.

- When you begin a program it shows a **speed profile**.
- The **Profile key is at the bottom right of the lower keypad**. Pressing it
  switches the display to an **incline grade**, and then to a **track**.
- **When the LEDs are blinking the graph scans through the three displays.**

The text readouts are changed by the Display key instead; see
`40t-2026-console-display-key-and-scan`.

**The 4.0T service manual prints this section word for word** in its chapter 4.
