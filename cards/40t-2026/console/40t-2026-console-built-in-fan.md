**The console includes a built-in fan to help keep you cool.**

That single sentence is all the manual prints about it. **It names no fan key, no
speed settings and no position on the console**, and the console overview drawing
on page 18 does not label a fan. Do not quote a fan key from another Spirit
treadmill for this machine.
