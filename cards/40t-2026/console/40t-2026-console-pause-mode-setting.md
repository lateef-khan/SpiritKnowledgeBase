**Pause mode on: the console allows 5 minutes of pause.**
**Pause mode off: the console pauses indefinitely.**

The setting lives under **Functions** in maintenance mode; see
`40t-2026-console-maintenance-mode-menu` for how to get in.

The five-minute figure is also printed on the Basic Information page as part of
what the Stop key does; see `40t-2026-console-stop-key-functions`.
