**The chest strap transmitter is not a standard part - it is a separate purchase.**

**Most transmitters that operate at Bluetooth or ANT+ will also work.**

**This figure is not shared with the other two 2026 machines.** The 7.0T and 8.0T
manuals print **5kHz** in the same sentence, which is a different radio entirely;
see `spirit-2026t-console-chest-strap-5khz`. Check the manual for the machine in
front of you before recommending a strap.
