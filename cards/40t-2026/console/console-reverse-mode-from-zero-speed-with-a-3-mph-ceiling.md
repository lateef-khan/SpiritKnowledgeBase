**"The reverse mode allows for targeted training for muscle groups used in backward locomotion, such
as the hamstrings, glutes, and calves."**

1. **While at the initial speed (0.3 mph), press the speed decrease key to reduce the speed to 0.0
   mph**, and make sure the belt has completely stopped moving.
2. **Two seconds later the message "REVERSE BELT SPEED" flashes three times** to warn you that reverse
   mode is about to start **at 0.3 mph**.

- **The maximum reverse speed is limited to 3.0 mph.**
- **The forward speed shortcut keys are nonfunctional during this mode.**

**"Please note the belt may not stop due to move inertia, which means the belt will not be locked
down by motor and stop. Therefore, after the belt stops moving, wait for about 3 seconds before
pressing the reverse button for safety."**

**There is a reverse button.** The owner's manual's text names it only in that last sentence, and its
console drawing does not label it; the ST8700A service manual's Display Windows drawing labels a block
of **REVERS Keys** beside the **Quick Keys** on the right of the keypad, where the 2026 owner's manual
labels Direct Keys and the 2025 printing Quick Keys (`40t-2026-console-screen-layout`). The 2026
book's own text gives no separate reverse-button gesture: the sequence is speed down to 0.0, wait,
and the belt reverses.

**The 2025 printings of this manual have no reverse mode.** Neither the February nor the May 2025 book
prints the page, so a 4.0T sold with a 2025 book may or may not run backwards; the service manual that
does print it is the **ST8700A** revision, not the ST8700 book the 2026 owner's manual was first
carded against. Ask for the revision on the console board before promising the feature.

The 7.0T has dedicated Forward and Reverse keys and shows a minus sign in its speed window
(`70t-2026-console-forward-reverse-keys`); the 4.0T's minimum forward speed of 0.3 mph is a factory
setting (`40t-2026-console-factory-settings-min-0-3-to-0-7-max-down-to-10-0`).
