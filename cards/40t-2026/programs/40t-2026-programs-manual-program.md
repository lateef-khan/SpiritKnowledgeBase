The Manual program works as the name implies, manually: **you** control the workload, not the computer.

1. Press the **Fitness** key, select the **Manual** program, then press **Confirm**. (Pressing the
   Manual key then Confirm also works.)
2. The Message Window asks for your **Age**. Set it with the Plus and Minus keys, then press Confirm.
3. Enter your **Weight** with the Plus and Minus keys, then press Confirm.
4. Enter the **Time**, then press Confirm.
5. Press **Start** to begin, or **Confirm** to go back and modify your settings.

During the Manual program you can scroll through the data in the Message Window with the **Display**
key. When the program ends, press Start to run it again or Stop to exit.
