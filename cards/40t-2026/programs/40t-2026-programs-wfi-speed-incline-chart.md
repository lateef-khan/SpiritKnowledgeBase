The 4.0T manual prints this **Program Speed and Incline Chart** directly beneath the WFI paragraph. It
runs in **one-minute stages for 18 minutes** and is *not* the Gerkin chart, even though the WFI
paragraph immediately above it says the WFI test "is the same as the Gerkin chart above". **The manual
therefore contradicts itself about what the WFI test runs; both are recorded as printed.** No other
2026 manual prints this chart.
