The treadmill has **four different programs** designed for a variety of workout goals: **5K Run**,
**Hill**, **Plateau** and **Interval**. They are factory preset work level profiles for achieving
different goals.

The initial built-in level of difficulty for each program is set to a relatively easy level. You may
adjust the level of difficulty (**Max speed**) for each program before beginning.

Beyond these four, the console also has a **Manual** program, a **Custom** program, a **METs**
program, three **heart rate** programs and nine **fitness tests** on the VO2 key.

The manual prints a profile picture for each preset program but **no numeric segment values**, so the
speed and grade of each individual segment are not recorded here.
