The Plateau program provides a **steady speed and varied grade** exercise, with **warm up and cool down
periods**.

The manual prints a profile picture only. It gives **no numeric speed or grade values** for the
individual segments, so none are recorded here.

The 7.0T manual prints a numeric segment chart for its own Plateau program, and that chart holds the
grade at 0 throughout - a different profile from the varied grade described here.
