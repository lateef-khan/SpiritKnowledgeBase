1. Select the desired program by pressing the **Fitness** key, then press **Confirm**.
2. The Message Window asks you to enter your **Age**. Adjust with the Plus and Minus keys, then press
   Confirm.
3. Enter your **Weight** with the Plus and Minus keys, then press Confirm.
4. Next is **Time** (**excluding the 5K program**). Adjust it and press Confirm.
5. Adjust the **Max Speed**. This is the peak exertion level you will experience during the program.
   Press Confirm.
6. Press **Start** to begin, or press **Stop** to go back one level or screen and modify your settings.

**Changing the workload mid-program.** Press Plus or Minus at any time to increase or decrease the
workload. This changes the workload settings of **the entire profile**, although the profile picture on
the screen will not change - so that you can see the entire profile at all times, undistorted. When you
make a change, the Message Window shows the current column and the program maximum levels of workload.

Press **Display** during the program to scroll through the data. When the program ends the Message
Window shows a **summary of your workout** for a short time, then the console returns to the start-up
display.
