A timed **1.5 mile (2.4 kilometer) run**. You control the speed manually.

## Male

| Rating | 20-29 Yrs. Old | 30-39 Yrs. Old | 40-49 Yrs. Old | 50-59 Yrs. Old | 60+ Yrs. Old |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Superior | <9:17 | <9:33 | <9:51 | <10:37 | <11:26 |
| Excellent | 9:18-10:09 | 9:34-10:46 | 9:52-11:15 | 10:28-12:08 | 11:27-13:23 |
| Good | 10:10-11:29 | 10:47-11:54 | 11:16-12:24 | 12:09-13:35 | 13:24-15:04 |
| Fair | 11:30-12:38 | 11:55-12:28 | 12:25-13:50 | 13:36-15:06 | 15:05-16:46 |
| Poor | 12:39-14:00 | 12:59-14:34 | 13:51-15:24 | 15:07-16:28 | 16:47-19:10 |
| Very Poor | >14:00 | >14:34 | >15:24 | >16:28 | >19:10 |
| Failure | >12:51 | >13:36 | >14:29 | >15:26 | >16:40 |

## Female

| Rating | 20-29 Yrs. Old | 30-39 Yrs. Old | 40-49 Yrs. Old | 50-59 Yrs. Old | 60+ Yrs. Old |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Superior | <10:28 | <11:00 | <11:33 | <12:53 | <14:05 |
| Excellent | 10:29-11:59 | 11:01-12:24 | 11:34-13:23 | 12:54-14:34 | 14:06-16:33 |
| Good | 11:59-13:34 | 12:25-14:08 | 13:24-14:53 | 14:35-16:35 | 16:34-18:27 |
| Fair | 13:25-14:50 | 14:09-15:43 | 14:54-16:31 | 16:36-18:18 | 18:28-20:16 |
| Poor | 14:51-16:46 | 15:44-17:38 | 16:32-18:37 | 18:19-20:44 | 20:17-22:52 |
| Very Poor | >16:46 | >17:38 | >18:37 | >20:44 | >20:52 |
| Failure | >15:26 | >15:57 | >16:58 | >17:55 | >18:44 |

*Preserved exactly as printed, including three things that do not read consistently: the "Failure" row
is faster than the "Very Poor" row in every column; the male 30-39 "Fair" range ends before it begins
(11:55-12:28); and the female 60+ "Very Poor" threshold (>20:52) is lower than the "Poor" range above it
(20:17-22:52). Several cells also differ by seconds from the same chart in the CT900 manual, which
prints male 30-39 Fair as 11:55-12:58, male 50-59 Poor as 15:07-16:58 and female 60+ Very Poor as
>22:52.*
