Press the **HR** key to select the HR program. There are three: **TA HR 65%**, **TA HR 80%** and
**HR interval**. Press **Confirm** to enter the one you want.

1. The Message Window asks for your **age**. Set it with the Plus and Minus keys, then press Confirm.
2. Enter your **weight**, then Confirm.
3. Enter the **Time**, then Confirm.
4. Adjust the **target HR**. This is the 65% / 80% heart rate level you will experience during the
   program. Press Confirm.
5. Press **Start** to begin, or Confirm to go back and modify your settings. At any time during data
   entry, **Stop** goes back one level or screen.

**During the program**, press Plus or Minus to change your target heart rate at any time. Press
**Display** to scroll through the data. When the program ends, press Start to run it again or Stop to
exit.

The manual describes no separate behaviour for the HR interval program beyond its name.

Entering your correct age matters here: the console works the target out from your age. How the console
reads your pulse - hand grips, chest strap - is covered in the console section, not here.
