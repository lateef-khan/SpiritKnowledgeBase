**These are the 4.0T's figures, whose first bracket is "<30" at 13:36. The 8.0T, CT900, CT900ENT and
CTSBS900 manuals print "<20" at 12:36 for the same row - see `ct900-air-force-prt-fitness-test`.**

A timed **1.5 mile (2.4 kilometer) run**. You control the speed manually. Maximum time allowed to pass
the test:

| Age | Male | Female |
|---|---|---|
| <30 | 13:36 | 16:22 |
| 30-30 | 14:00 | 16:57 |
| 40-49 | 14:52 | 18:14 |
| 50-59 | 16:22 | 19:43 |
| 60+ | 18:14 | 22:28 |

For more detailed information, visit: http://bit.ly/SF-AirForce

*Printed exactly as shown. The second bracket reads "30-30", which cannot be a range, and the table
skips the 30-39 bracket entirely. The 50-59 female time is 19:43 here and 19:42 in the 8.0T and CT900
manuals.*
