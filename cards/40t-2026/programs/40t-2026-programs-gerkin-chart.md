**This is the chart printed in the 4.0T manual, where stage 2.1 begins at 1:00. The 7.0T and 8.0T
manuals print a Gerkin chart whose stage 2.1 begins at 1:15 and whose speeds sit one quarter-stage
later throughout - see `spirit-2026t-programs-gerkin-chart`. The two charts are not interchangeable.**

The Gerkin protocol, also known as the fireman's protocol, is a sub-max Vo2 (volume of oxygen) test.
The test increases speed and elevation alternately until you reach **85% of your Max heart rate**. The
time it takes to reach 85% determines the test score (VO2max).
