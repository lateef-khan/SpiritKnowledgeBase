1. Press the **METs** key and then press **Confirm**.
2. The Message Window prompts you to enter your **age**. Use the Plus and Minus keys, then Confirm.
3. Enter your **weight**, then Confirm.
4. Enter your **workout time**, then Confirm.
5. Enter your **target METs**, then Confirm.
6. Press **Start** to begin the program.

For what a MET is and the MET value of everyday activities, see the MET table card. The 8.0T has a
comparable program called METs Target, which is set up on a touch screen instead.
