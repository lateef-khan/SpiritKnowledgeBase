When the **VO2** key is pressed the message window displays **GERKIN**. This is the first of **9
different tests** available:

**Gerkin, WFI (Wellness Fitness Initiative), Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, PEB (Physical Efficiency
Battery), Coast Guard, and CTT (Chester Treadmill Test).**

To select a different test press the VO2 key again and the next test is shown. Keep pressing until the
test you want is in the message window, then press **Confirm**.

1. **(Except CTT-performance)** Enter your **Age** with Plus and Minus, then Confirm.
2. Enter your **Weight**, then Confirm. **(Only for WFI)** you are then prompted for your **Height**.
3. Enter your **Gender**, then Confirm.
4. Press **Start** to begin the test.

*Two defects in the printed instructions: step 2's Height prompt tells you to "adjust the age" when it
means the height; and the introduction on the same page describes the VO2 program as "based on the YMCA
protocol... two to four stages, lasting 3 minutes each" with a Male/Female protocol choice, which does
not match any of the nine tests the same page lists - the Gerkin chart it prints uses 15-second stages.
Both are recorded as printed.*

At the end of the test a VO2 score is displayed. VO2 stands for Volume of Oxygen uptake, a measurement
of how much oxygen you need to perform a known amount of work.

**The 4.0T 2025 owner's manual (Revision 02.04.25, reprinted as Revision 05.19.25) prints this page word for word**; only the folio differs, and both printings are cited.
