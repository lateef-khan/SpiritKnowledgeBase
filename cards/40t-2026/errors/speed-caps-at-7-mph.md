**Problem:** Treadmill will only achieve approximately 7 mph (11 kmph) but shows higher
speed on display.

**Cause and fix, as printed:** This indicates motor should be receiving power to operate.
Do not use an extension cord. If an extension cord is required, it should be as short as
possible and heavy duty, 16-gauge minimum, low voltage. Contact an electrician or your
dealer.

**This manual prints no minimum supply voltage in this row.** The CT900ENT and XT manuals
add "a minimum of 110 volt AC current is required" to the same row
(`ct900ent-speed-display-mismatch-caps-at-7mph`); this one stops at the extension-cord
advice. The CT850 manuals print a different gauge again - 12 gauge for 2016, 16 gauge for
2020 - so the 16-gauge figure is this machine's, not a Spirit-wide one.

**The 4.0T 2025 owner's manual - both the February and the May 2025 printings - prints this row word for word**, so it holds for the previous model year too (added 2026-09-11).
