These are the callout numbers on the exploded view drawing. The drawing is a
picture with bare numbers on it and no text, so the table below is the only
readable form of it.

**128 rows are printed.** The whole item numbers that appear are 1-6, 8-14, 22-33, 35-43, 45-66, 71-98, 100-111, 113, 117-120, 122-126, 130, 133, 136, 141-145, 147-148, 153-157, 160-161, 163. Any number outside that run is **not printed in this manual at all** - 7, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 34, 44, 67, 68, 69, 70, 99, 112, 114, 115, 116, 121, 127, 128, 129, 131, 132, 134, 135, 137, 138, 139, 140, 146, 149, 150, 151, 152, 158, 159, 162 are absent, not a missing page.

## What changed from the 2023 CE800 list

The 2023 CE800 book prints 127 rows and this one prints 128, with the same
numbering. **Four rows changed and one was added:**

| Item | CE800-2023 | CE800-2024 |
|---|---|---|
| 88 | 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer, **qty 6** | same washer, **qty 4** |
| 125 | `17mm_ Combination Wrench` | `14/17m/m_Wrench` |
| 160 | **Water** Bottle Holder Clamp | **Drink** Bottle Holder Clamp |
| 161 | **Water** Bottle Holder | **Drink** Bottle Holder |
| 163 | **not printed** | 5/16" x 23 x 3T flat washer, qty 2 |

Nothing else moved. A part called a water bottle holder in one book and a drink
bottle holder in the next is the same part.

## What tells this list apart from the CE850-2024 list

The plain CE800 is generator-braked and has no adjustable stride, and the list
shows it:

- **Item 31 is `Generator/Brake Controller` and item 29 `Flywheel`.** There is no
  gear motor and no incline motor in this list at all.
- **The CE850-2024 list has a `Gear Motor` at item 56 and an `Incline Motor` at
  73**, plus stride and level handgrip labels at 52 and 53, and runs to 208 rows.
- The two lists share 108 item numbers and agree on **five** of them. Never read
  one against the other.

**The brake coil lives inside the console-assembly sub-numbering.** Items 30-14,
30-15 and 30-16 are the generator wire harness, the 1600 mm computer cable and
the 1000 mm wire brake coil harness; the list has no separate whole-brake item.

**Items 86 and 109 are printed identically**, both `3/8" x 19 x 1.5T_Flat Washer`
quantity 4, so this list cannot tell the two apart.

**No flywheel weight, no resistance rating and no dimension is printed against
any row.** See `spirit-ce-specs-no-specification-table`.
