| Reason | What to do |
|---|---|
| Another chest belt is in use near the climber. | Check whether anyone else near the climber is wearing a chest belt. |
| Other magnetic field disturbance. | Change the position or the direction of the climber. |
| The receiver is broken. | Replace with a new receiver. |

The console reads heart rate from a receiver only. The user must wear a chest belt. The working range is 40 to 220 BPM.

If there is **no** reading at all rather than a jumping one, see the card `cc81-no-pulse-displayed`.
