Check these four things:

- The adapter's electricity supply.
- Swap in another adapter.
- The power DC cable and the adapter are connected properly.
- The computer cables at the console are connected properly.

The machine runs from an adapter, not from a mains lead into the frame, so the adapter is the first thing to rule out.

**Note on the source.** The manual letters these a, b, c, d but prints them in the order b, c, d, a. The order above follows the printed order. The manual does not say the order matters.

If the console lights but the picture is faint or broken, see the card `cc81-lcd-dim-or-incomplete`.
