These are the names the manual uses. Use them when ordering parts or reading any other section of this manual.

**Covers and shrouds**

Console, Handlebar Cover (L) and (R), Console Mast Cover (L) and (R), Chain Cover (L) and (R), Slider Cover A, Slider Cover B, Right Slider Cover, Slide Wheel Cover, Rear Shroud, Pedal (L) and (R).

**Assemblies and frame parts**

Console Holder Assembly, Handgrip Connecting Assembly, Mast Handle bar Assembly, Handgrip Stabilizer Assembly, Strap Hold Down, Idler Wheel Assembly, Right Swing Assembly, Left Driving Assembly, Linked Assembly (R), Connecting Arm (L) and (R), Crank Arm, Drive Pulley, Rear Bar, Rail Tube, Main Frame.

The manual names the parts on labelled photographs only. It prints **no part numbers** anywhere, so a part is identified by name, not by number.
