| Problem | What the manual says to do |
|---|---|
| Pedal damage | Replace the pedal. |
| Vibration caused by the slide wheel | Replace the slide wheel. |

The manual gives no test and no adjustment for either one. Both answers are a replacement.

To get at either part, follow the card `cc81-connecting-arm-replacement`.
