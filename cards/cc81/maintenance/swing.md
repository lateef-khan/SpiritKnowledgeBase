Check two things:

1. Whether the screws in the swing areas are tight.
2. Whether the joints are worn.

That is everything the manual gives for this symptom. It names no torque figure and no specific joint.

Vibration coming from a slide wheel is a different answer — see the card `cc81-connecting-arm-pedal-or-vibration`.
