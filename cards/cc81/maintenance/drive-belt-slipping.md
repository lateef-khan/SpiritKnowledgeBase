**The manual answers this in two separate places and the two halves are different. Do both.**

**Section 9-5:** adjust the nut on the **J bolt** with a **13 mm wrench**.

**Section 9-7:**

1. Tighten the bolt and nut on the **crank arm**.
2. Adjust the drive belt tension with a **13 mm wrench**.

The target is **190 Hz, plus or minus 10** on a sonic belt tension meter — see the card `cc81-drive-belt-tension`.

A belt that comes off the pulley altogether is a different answer — see the card `cc81-drive-belt-dropping`.
