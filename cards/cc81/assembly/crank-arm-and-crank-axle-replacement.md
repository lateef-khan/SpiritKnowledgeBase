1. Follow the linked assembly replacement steps first to take the linked assembly off — see step 8 of the card `cc81-connecting-arm-replacement`.
2. Remove the screws that hold the **crank arm** on the **crank axle** with a **12 mm wrench**.
3. Loosen the screws with an **M6 open-end wrench**, then take the crank arm off.
4. Remove the screws that hold the **6005 bearing** on the crank axle with an **M4 open-end wrench**, then take the crank axle and the **drive pulley** off.
5. Refitting the crank arm: follow the steps in reverse.

**Two notes for refitting**

- **Note 1:** make sure the screws and the **flat of the crank axle** are lined up before tightening.
- **Note 2:** the **grooves of the crank arm and the crank axle must be lined up**. Then insert the **woodruff key** and tighten the bolt to approximately **550 pounds** with a torque wrench.

**The torque figure is printed as "550 pounds" with no unit of length.** It is copied exactly as printed. The manual gives no other torque figure to check it against.
