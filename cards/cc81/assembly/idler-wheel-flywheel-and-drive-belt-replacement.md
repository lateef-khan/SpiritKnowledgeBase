1. Follow the connecting arm replacement steps first to take the connecting arm off — see the card `cc81-connecting-arm-replacement`.
2. Loosen the nut that holds the **idler wheel assembly** on the main frame with a **13 mm wrench**.
3. Loosen the nut on the **J bolt** with a **13 mm wrench** and separate the **drive belt and drive pulley**.
4. Remove the screws on the idler wheel assembly with a **13 mm wrench** and take the idler wheel assembly off.
5. **Set the resistance to maximum, then cut the power off.**
6. Take the **steel cable and drive belt** off.
7. Loosen the nuts on the **flywheel** with a **15 mm wrench**, then take the flywheel off.
8. Refitting: follow the steps in reverse. Two things must be done first so the belt and flywheel run smoothly:
   - **A.** Adjust the **inner nut on the flywheel axle** with a **17 mm wrench** so the drive belt sits on the **centre of the drive pulley**. Then tighten the nuts.
   - **B.** Set the belt tension by adjusting the idler wheel assembly. The tension value must be **190 Hz, plus or minus 10**, measured with a sonic belt tension meter.

Step 5 matters. Resistance goes to maximum **before** the power is cut, so the steel cable can be taken off.
