1. Follow the connecting arm replacement steps first to take the connecting arm off — see the card `cc81-connecting-arm-replacement`.
2. Remove the screws on the **slide cover** with a screwdriver, then take the slide cover off.
3. Remove the screws that hold the **rail tube** on the main frame with **two 14 mm wrenches**, then take the rail tube off.
4. Refitting: follow the steps in reverse.
