1. Remove the screws on **handlebar cover (R) and (L)** with a screwdriver.
2. Remove the screws on the **handgrip connecting assembly** with **two 14 mm wrenches**, then take the handgrip connecting assembly off.
3. Remove the screws on the **mast handle bar assembly** with a **14 mm wrench**.
4. Loosen the **locking knob** by hand, then take the mast handle bar assembly off.
5. Refitting: follow the steps in reverse.

This is also step 1 of the rear rail and strap hold down job — see the card `cc81-rear-rail-and-strap-hold-down-replacement`.
