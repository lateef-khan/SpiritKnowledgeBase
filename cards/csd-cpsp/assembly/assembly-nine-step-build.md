**The 2025 update's step 8 has no guide rod fixed plate and one plain pulley plus the magnet pulley (210); the 2024 book has the plate, two plain pulleys and no 210.** The update alone prints the notes: the cable has to run through the incremental weight set; install the weight stacks, top weight stack and incremental weight in sequence; the upper pulley (210) is with magnets and the magnet side must face and interact with the sensor; after the weight stack is installed, lubricate the guide rods before attaching the shrouds. Ten 15 lb plates, four 10 lb plates and a 10 lb top plate is a 200 lb stack.

**Ten rows, not the eight-row panel the CSS stations print**: twelve fixed bushings (6) and twelve M6 x 16 countersunk screws (16) are added, in both printings. The 2025 update alone adds the note to connect the batteries and display connection from page 22 before installing the covers, and to install the front shroud (7), then the back shroud (8), then the upper shield (2).

**The two printings disagree on the tools.** The 2024 book: "All tools required to assemble the Strength Equipment are included within the packaging"; the 2025 update: "are NOT included within the packaging. Please prepare Allen wrench set, Phillips screw driver, and c ring pliers". Both say "Remove the tools first" in the unpacking list. No torque is printed anywhere in either.
