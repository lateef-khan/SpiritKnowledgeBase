**Two labelled photographs, fifteen callouts each.** The first (p. 3) shows the assembled bike from the console
side; the second (p. 4) shows the frame with the covers off.

| No. | Drawing 1 (p. 3) | No. | Drawing 2 (p. 4) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Console | 1 | Front Handlebar |
| 2 | Handle Pulse Sensor | 2 | Central Support Tube |
| 3 | Bottle Holder | 3 | Side Handlebar (R) |
| 4 | Aluminum Tracking | 4 | Plastic Belt Wheel |
| 5 | Crank (R) | 5 | Idler Wheel Assembly |
| 6 | Pedal (R) | 6 | Main Frame |
| 7 | End Cap | 7 | Front Stabilizer |
| 8 | Back Rest | 8 | Back Pad |
| 9 | Saddle | 9 | Side Handlebar (L) |
| 10 | Knob | 10 | Back Pad Support Tube |
| 11 | Stop Bar | 11 | Aluminum Tracking |
| 12 | Main Chain Cover (L) | 12 | Belt |
| 13 | Main Chain Cover (R) | 13 | Flywheel |
| 14 | End Cap Ø89 (L) | 14 | Motor |
| 15 | End Cap Ø89 (R) | 15 | Rear Stabilizer |

The *Aluminum Tracking* is the rail the sliding seat runs on; the *Stop Bar* is the seat-slide brake lever the
owner's manual calls the Braking Handlebar (E); the *Plastic Belt Wheel* is the large pulley on the crank, the *Belt*
runs from it to the *Flywheel*, and the *Motor* sets the magnetic resistance. The owner's manual letters the same
parts (A main frame, B rear stabilizer, C front stabilizer, D central support tube, E stop bar, F console, G saddle,
H back rest, I front handlebars, J1/J2 side handlebars, L pedals, M bottle holder, O back pad, P back pad support
tube, S adaptor). The parts list with item numbers and the exploded view are the parts section's; no dimension or
weight is printed on either drawing.
