**The SB600 service manual prints the parts list; the owner's manual prints only the drawing.** The service manual (V1.0, 30 pages) has a chapter headed *PARTS LIST*: an **Exploded view** on PDF p. 11 and a two-page **Parts List** on PDF pp. 12-13 headed *P/N / DESCRIPTION / Qty Per unit*. The owner's manual's **EXPLODED VIEW DIAGRAM** (PDF p. 40, printed 39) is the same drawing with the same codes and no list. The codes are letters for assemblies and numbered sub-parts: **A** is the main frame and A1-A94 everything bolted to it; B/C the rear and front stabilizers; D the central support tube; E the stop bar; F the console; G the saddle; H the back rest; J the side handlebars; L the pedals; M the bottle and holder; N the assembly hardware bag; O the back pad; P its support tube; S the adaptor.

**Gaps and oddities, as printed.** No **A90** and no **N9** are listed. The drawing carries callouts **I** and **I1** on the front handlebar and its end caps (the *Front Handlebar* of the Main Parts Indication page) that the list does not name, so that handlebar has no code to order by. The text layer reads A11 as *All*; the render shows A11. The *0260*, *017* and *CLIP 017* dimensions are the diameter sign as the text layer and OCR both read it - Ø260, Ø17. The list's own typos are kept: *SCREW M5\*l 0mm* (A7, i.e. M5x10), *NUTM8*, *HANDLE PLUSE SENSOR* (J4), *SPACEN RING* (J7), *WATHER BOTTLE* (M2), *BELT 900 J6* (A12).

**What the list tells a service call.** The belt is A12, **900 J6**; the motor A1 with its DC wire A46; the flywheel A31 with its two covers A32 and one-way bearing A37; the plastic belt wheel A8 (Ø260) on axle A9 (Ø17x150.3) with bearings A11 (6203 2RS); the sensor wires A3 (400 mm), A4 (1100 mm), A6 (700 mm), A34 (450 mm), A33 (six, unsized) and J1-1 (550 mm) and J2-1 in the side handlebars; the magnet A45 in its housing A42; the sliding track A56 with wheels A64, stoppers A62-A63, bearings A55 (6003) and wire traps A77-A78; the console F; the adaptor S. The replacement procedures in the same book (pp. 14-29) cite these codes.

The service manual's named-parts drawings and tool list are `sb600-2023-specs-parts-main-parts-indication-thirty-named-parts` and `sb600-2023-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-five-tools-and-grease`; which other Xterra books print a list is `xterra-specs-parts-which-bike-elliptical-and-climber-manuals-print-an-exploded-view-and-parts-list`.
