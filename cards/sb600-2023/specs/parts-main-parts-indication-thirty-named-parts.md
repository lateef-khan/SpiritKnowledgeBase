The SB600 service manual opens its *GENERAL INFORMATION* chapter with two **Main Parts Indication** drawings, each numbered 1-15.

**Assembled bike (PDF p. 3):** 1 Console, 2 Handle Pulse Sensor, 3 Bottle Holder, 4 Aluminum Tracking, 5 Crank (R), 6 Pedal (R), 7 End Cap, 8 Back Rest, 9 Saddle, 10 Knob, 11 Stop Bar, 12 Main Chain Cover (L), 13 Main Chain Cover (R), 14 End Cap Φ89 (L), 15 End Cap Φ89 (R).

**Bike with the covers off (PDF p. 4):** 1 Front Handlebar, 2 Central Support Tube, 3 Side Handlebar (R), 4 Plastic Belt Wheel, 5 Idler Wheel Assembly, 6 Main Frame, 7 Front Stabilizer, 8 Back Pad, 9 Side Handlebar (L), 10 Back Pad Support Tube, 11 Aluminum Tracking, 12 Belt, 13 Flywheel, 14 Motor, 15 Rear Stabilizer.

**These numbers are not part codes.** They belong to the two drawings only; the parts list four pages on uses letter codes (the stop bar is E, the aluminum tracking A20, the plastic belt wheel A8, the idler wheel assembly A5, the back pad support tube P) and the replacement chapters cite those (`sb600-2023-specs-parts-list-143-coded-items-from-the-service-manual`). *Aluminum Tracking* is the seat rail that the seat slides along; the *Stop Bar* is the bar the seat latches against; the *Plastic Belt Wheel* is the big pulley the belt runs on, driven by the crank and driving the flywheel through the idler. The Xterra treadmill service manuals open with the same two picture chapters (`xterra-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-outlines-and-electronic-parts-named`).
