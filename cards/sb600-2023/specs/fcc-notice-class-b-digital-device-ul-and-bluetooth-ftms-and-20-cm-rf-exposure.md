**This is the SB600 page, which prints "Class B" and lists UL; the SB240 page prints "Class C" and has no UL
line.** The certification list reads **"FCC part 15", "UL", "Bluetooth (with FTMS)"** - the same three the Spirit
2023-2025 books print (`xt-2023-safety-compliance-certifications`). Then the declaration: "This device complies with
part 15 of the FCC Rules. Operation is subject to the following two conditions: (1) this device must not cause
harmful interference, and (2) this device must not accept any interference received, including interference that may
cause undesired operation."

The NOTE: tested to "the limits for a **Class B digital device**, pursuant to Part 15 of the FCC Rules", designed for
"reasonable protection against harmful interference in a residential installation", with the four remedies -
reorient or relocate the receiving antenna; increase the separation between the equipment and receiver; connect the
equipment to an outlet on a different circuit from the receiver; consult the dealer or an experienced radio/TV
technician.

CAUTION: keep "a separation distance of at least **20 cm** ... between the antenna of this device and all persons",
and the transmitter "must not be co-located or operating in conjunction with any other antenna or transmitter".

Condition (2) is printed "must **not** accept", where the standard Part 15 sentence (and the Xterra WS treadmill
page, `xterra-ws-specs-fcc-warning-part-15-class-b-and-20-cm-rf-exposure`) reads "must accept"; the card keeps the
printed wording and reads it as a misprint. Unlike the WS page there is no Grantee note. The Bluetooth radio pairs
with the XTERRA+ app and FTMS apps; the console section holds that.
