The SB600 service manual's *TROUBLESHOOTING* chapter opens with a **Tools Required** page (PDF p. 5), five pictures with a name and, for the wrench sets, the sizes:

| Tool | Sizes printed |
|---|---|
| Phillips screwdriver | - |
| Multi-meter | - |
| Set of metric L-Allen wrenches | 5 mm, 6 mm, 8 mm |
| Set of metric open-end wrenches | 12 mm, 13 mm, 14 mm, 15 mm, 17 mm, 19 mm, 21 mm |
| Industrial lubricants - grease | - |

**This is a longer list than any Xterra treadmill service manual prints** - those name one tool, a multi-meter (`xterra-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`) - and it is the JKEXER format, with a picture per tool. The replacement procedures that follow (pp. 14-29) call for the sizes by name; the grease is for the sliding track and the flywheel axle. **Do not confuse it with the tools packed with the bike**: the owner's manual's *Parts Included* page shows the carton's own tools (a 13 mm wrench and 5 mm / 6 mm Allen keys, on the assembly cards), and the service list above is what a technician brings. The parts the tools go on are `sb600-2023-specs-parts-list-143-coded-items-from-the-service-manual` and `sb600-2023-specs-parts-main-parts-indication-thirty-named-parts`.
