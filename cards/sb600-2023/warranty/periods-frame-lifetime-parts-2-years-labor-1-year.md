| Warranty | Frame | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | Lifetime | 2 Years | 1 Year |

**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the first of the SB600 book's three warranty pages, headed *WARRANTY - RESIDENTIAL* and *Effective August 01, 2023- SB600 Recumbent Bike LIMITED WARRANTY*. One row, *Residential*; no commercial row is printed. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`). The SERVICE paragraph on the third page says *twelve (12) months ... establishes the **labor** warranty period*, which for once agrees with the table's labor figure.

**The SB240, printed the same day in the same layout, gets a one-column table - parts only** (`sb240-2023-warranty-periods-parts-1-year-only-no-frame-or-labor-column`). The older SB500 and SB4500 pages give the frame and brake for life with three years on parts (`xterra-warranty-periods-frame-and-brake-lifetime-parts-3-years-labor-1-year`); the SB120 and UB120 give a year on the frame and 90 days on parts (`xterra-bike-warranty-periods-frame-1-year-parts-90-days-labor-none`).

The book is the Online-Support layout: no registration card and no ten-day deadline, a QR code instead (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-qr-code-and-support-tab-no-deadline`); five consumer duties without a registration item (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-without-registration`); nine exclusions that admit a non-dues paying facility at five hours a day or less (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-with-a-non-dues-facility-allowance`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-use-environment-residential-or-non-dues-facility-5-hours-a-day`); service department **(870) 935-1107** in step 3 (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-service-steps-dealer-service-organization-then-the-service-department`, `xterra-warranty-support-contacts-which-phone-number-each-bike-elliptical-and-climber-book-prints`). The electrical page's ten-percent voltage sentence is `xterra-warranty-what-the-electrical-and-console-pages-say-is-not-covered`. The service manual for this bike prints no warranty page.

**The ERG650W water rower prints the same three figures** - one older-layout row, *Frame Lifetime / Parts 2 Years / Labor 1 Year*, under *Effective March 04, 2020 - ROWER LIMITED WARRANTY* (PDF p. 21, printed 18). The book is stamped *ERG650W_VER5_20210118* and its back cover *(c)2021*: the warranty page predates the book by ten months, so the stamp names the book and the 2020 date is the term. Everything else on its page is the older layout the SB600 does not have - registration card within ten days (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover`), five duties with the card (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-with-a-registration-card`), nine exclusions with store display and unregistered products (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-store-display-and-unregistered-products`), six steps at (870) 333-5500 (`xterra-warranty-support-contacts-which-phone-number-each-rower-and-strength-book-prints`), an ATTENTION box (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-use-environment-residential-only-any-other-use-voids-it`) and a SERVICE sentence that says *warranty period* without *labor*. Its tank page voids the warranty for chlorine or filling past level 6 (`xterra-ergw-warranty-tank-chlorine-bleach-or-filling-past-the-mark-voids-it`).
