*NO SPEED READING APPEARS ON THE SCREEN - Condition:* the screen displays normally, but the speed remains at
0 or is not shown while pedalling.

1. If the console is on but the speed is not displayed, **remove the main chain cover (L and R) (A48, A49)**
   and inspect the **computer cables (A33 / A36)** and **speed sensor (A6)** to ensure they are properly
   installed (refer to the figures).
2. If all components are installed correctly, the issue is likely with the **sensor (A6)** or the **magnet
   (A45)**. **Use a test magnet to check the functionality of the sensor (A6).** Replace either the sensor
   (A6) or the magnet (A45), depending on which component is faulty.

Part names from the book's list: A6 is `SENSOR WIRE 700mm`, A33 `SENSOR WIRE`, A36 `SENSOR WIRE`, A45
`MAGNET`, A48 / A49 `MAIN CHAIN COVER (L) / (R)`. The book gives no sensor-to-magnet gap; the air bike's
service book does (3 mm: `air650-2021-errors-no-speed-on-the-display-sensor-within-3-mm-of-its-magnet`).
Removing the chain covers is the *Replacing the Main Chain Covers* procedure in the same book.


The service manual closes its troubleshooting chapter with: *if the troubleshooting steps outlined above do not resolve the issue you are encountering, please contact the dealer for further assistance and resolution.* It prints no error codes; its four pages are symptom-based.
