*SCREEN REMAINS BLANK - Condition:* the console does not power on and shows no display.

1. Verify that the **console cable and computer cable** are securely connected, as shown in the figures
   (the page draws the connector behind the console and the junction in the upright).
2. If all connections are correct, **use a multimeter to measure the power output** and identify any issues.

The book gives no voltage figure to expect. The console is fed by the adaptor (parts list item S, ADAPTOR)
through a DC wire (A46, DC WIRE 600mm) into the frame, so the power output measured is the adaptor's; a
multimeter is the first tool on the book's Tools Required page.


The service manual closes its troubleshooting chapter with: *if the troubleshooting steps outlined above do not resolve the issue you are encountering, please contact the dealer for further assistance and resolution.* It prints no error codes; its four pages are symptom-based.
