*ABNORMAL BEHAVIOR OF THE BIKE WHEN PEDALING - Condition 2:* during pedalling, resistance suddenly drops to
zero, similar to a bicycle chain coming off.

This is usually caused by the **drive belt slipping off**. The book's sequence:

1. Reinstall the drive belt following the installation steps (*Replacing the Flywheel and Belt*).
2. After installation and adjustment, rotate the **drive belt (A12)** forward and backward to test its
   stability.
3. If the belt slips off, adjust the drive belt (A12) **one notch in the opposite direction of the slipping**
   and test again.
4. If the belt continues to fall off, the machine has probably been subjected to improper handling, such as
   dropping or impact, causing dimensional deviations: replace the **Small Iron Tube (A84)** and the
   **Idler Wheel Assembly (A16 / A17)**.
5. If the issue still persists, it indicates possible deformation of the **Main Frame (A)**, and the entire
   machine may need to be replaced - the book adds that this is rare, since every machine is tested before
   shipping.

A12 is `BELT 900 J6`, A16 `BEARING HOUSING`, A17 `BEARING 6000 2ZZ`, A84 `SMALL IRON TUBE`. *Condition 1* on
the same page - the belt slips but stays on - is cured by turning the idler wheel adjustment screw (A13) with
an open-end wrench until it stops slipping; that is a belt adjustment and a maintenance question.


The service manual closes its troubleshooting chapter with: *if the troubleshooting steps outlined above do not resolve the issue you are encountering, please contact the dealer for further assistance and resolution.* It prints no error codes; its four pages are symptom-based.
