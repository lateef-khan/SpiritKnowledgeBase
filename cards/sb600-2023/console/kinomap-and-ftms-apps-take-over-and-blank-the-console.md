"This console connects to 3rd party fitness apps on your phone or tablet via Bluetooth. **Once console is connected to your phone or tablet via Bluetooth, the console will not display. Your workout feedback will be displayed on your device.**" That is expected, not a fault.

**Kinomap, as printed:** 1. Open the Kinomap app and click the lower right corner to add devices. 2. Click equipment management, then "+" to add equipment. 3. Click the "Treadmill" graphic, then scroll the brand connection screen to find and select FTMS or iConsole. 4. Select Treadmill Machine, then the Bluetooth device. 5. Press Home to return to the Home screen. 6. To select a training video, select the type of walk and press the button to enter the training screen. 7. Start training; the pause button at the top of the screen stops. 8. From the pause screen continue or exit.

The steps say "Treadmill" and "type of walk" because the page was carried over from a treadmill book; on this bike choose the bike equivalent in the app. "This machine is compatible with other 3rd party fitness apps using FTMS protocol." The XTERRA+ app page (iOS 13.1 / Android 8.0) is on [the app card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-xterra-plus-app-ios-13-android-8.md); the compliance page lists "Bluetooth (with FTMS)". The CIC850 spinner's Kinomap page is the Spirit twin ([card](../../cic850-2022/console/cic850-2022-console-connecting-to-kinomap.md)).
