**Neither book prints one.** The 44-page owner's manual (Revision 08/01/23) has no engineering, calibration, service or hidden-key section; its only key holds are Reset for 3 seconds (standby and user selection) and Mode for 3 seconds (finish a user profile). The 30-page V1.0 service manual (2025) covers troubleshooting, an exploded view, a parts list and replacement guides; its console content is the blank-screen check (console and computer cables, then a multimeter on the power output) and the console replacement (four Phillips screws; press the clip in the middle of the 8-pin ribbon and 2-pin hand-grip connectors before pulling). The words engineer, calibrat, service mode, maintenance mode and diagnos do not occur in either text, and every service-manual page was read from a render as well as its text layer.

So there is no printed way to change units, silence the buzzer or test the display from the keypad. The SB4500's on-screen settings ([card](../../sb4500-2021/console/boot-beep-standby-and-settings-for-beep-units-brightness-8-and-sleep-5-minutes.md)) and the SB500 family's engineering mode ([card](../../sb500-2020/console/engineering-mode-start-stop-enter-5-seconds-with-lcd-test-type-set-and-key-tone.md)) do not transfer to this console.
