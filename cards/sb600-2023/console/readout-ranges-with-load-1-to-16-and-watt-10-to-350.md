| Item | Range as printed |
|---|---|
| Time | 0:00 - 99:00 |
| Speed | 0.0 - 99.9 |
| Distance | 0.00 - 99.95 |
| Calories | 0 - 990 |
| Pulse | 60 - 220 |
| RPM | 15 - 999 |
| Watt | 10 - 350 |
| Load | **1 - 16** |

The table sits under the mode list and gives no units; the exercise screens show miles/h. The load level is chosen with MODE before each mode starts and changed with the arrows during it. The service manual for this bike adds nothing about the console beyond a blank-screen check and how to unplug its 8-pin and 2-pin connectors ([absence card](no-engineering-or-calibration-mode-is-printed-in-either-book.md)).
