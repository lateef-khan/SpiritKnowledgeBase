**The SB600 service manual (V1.0, 30 pages) has no safety, precautions, electrical or grounding
chapter.** Its contents run: General Information (*Main Parts Indication*, *Tools Required*),
Troubleshooting Methods (screen blank, no speed, no heart rate, abnormal behavior when pedaling),
Parts List (exploded view, parts list) and a Parts Replacing Guide of fifteen procedures from the
console to the motor and idler wheel.

The only cautionary sentences in the book are inside procedures:

| Page | Sentence |
|---|---|
| 14, Replacing the Console | `For both the 8-pin ribbon cable and 2-pin hand grip connectors, always press the clip in the middle before disconnecting. Forcing the connector without releasing the clip may cause wire damage.` |
| 20, Replacing the Main Chain Covers | `Before removing the chain cover completely, first remove the DC power cable...` |

Nothing tells a technician to unplug the adapter before opening the machine, nothing names a
voltage, and there is no precautions page of the kind the JKEXER-style books usually open with. The
wave brief expected one; there is none.

How it was checked: both the native text and the OCR supplement of every page read; the text
searched, allowing spaces between letters (the native layer is letter-spaced), for `safety`,
`precaution`, `warning`, `caution`, `danger`, `unplug` and `disconnect`; the hits are the two
sentences above and the connector steps on pages 16, 19 and 22.

**The owner's manual is the whole safety text for this machine**: the 21-item list
(`xterra-sb-safety-instructions-list-21-items`), the Electrical Safety page
(`xterra-sb-safety-electrical-safety-page`), and a grounding cross-reference to a page that does not
exist (`xterra-sb-safety-grounding-instructions-referenced-but-not-printed`). The Xterra TRX5500 service
book has the same gap (`trx5500-2024-safety-service-manual-has-no-safety-chapter`).
