1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the 4 screws securing the console display.
2. To disconnect the display cable connector, **firmly press the clip in the middle of the connector. Once the clip pops up, pull the opposite end of the connector firmly to unplug it.**

**Note: for both the 8-pin ribbon cable and 2-pin hand grip connectors, always press the clip in the middle before disconnecting. Forcing the connector without releasing the clip may cause wire damage.**

Four screws here; the owner's manual mounts the console on two pre-installed screws (N12) and the service parts list gives N12 as four. No refit step is printed - reverse the two steps.
