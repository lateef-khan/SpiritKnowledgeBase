1. Use a 6 mm Allen wrench to remove the 4 screws securing the backrest. The backrest can then be removed.
2. **Turn the pop-pin knob counterclockwise to loosen it, then pull the knob outward and lift upward to remove the back pad.**
3. Use a 6 mm Allen wrench to remove the 4 screws securing the saddle. The saddle can then be removed.
4. Use a 6 mm Allen wrench to remove the 4 screws securing the back pad support tube. The support tube can then be removed.

Four screws on the backrest here; the owner's manual fits it with one screw (N7) and one washer (N14) at step 6 and ships four of each. The saddle's four screws are the N8 fitted from underneath at step 5.
