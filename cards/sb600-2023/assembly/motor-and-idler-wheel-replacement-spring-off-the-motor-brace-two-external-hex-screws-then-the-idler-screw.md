1. First, remove the spring from the motor brace.
2. Use an open-end wrench to remove the 2 external hex screws securing the motor, then remove the motor.
3. Use an open-end wrench to remove the external hex screw securing the idler wheel, then remove the idler wheel.

Parts: motor A1, brace A86, spring A87, idler assembly with bearing housing A16 and bearings A17, its adjustment screw A13 (M8 x 70) and bolt A14. The chain covers come off first. The idler screw (A13) is also the belt-tension adjuster in the troubleshooting chapter - set it after refitting so the belt no longer slips.
