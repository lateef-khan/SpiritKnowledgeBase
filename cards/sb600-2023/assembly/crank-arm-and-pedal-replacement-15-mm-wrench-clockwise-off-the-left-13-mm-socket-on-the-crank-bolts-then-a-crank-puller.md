1. Use a 15 mm open-end wrench to remove the pedals: **turn clockwise to remove the left pedal, and counterclockwise to remove the right pedal.**
2. Remove the crank cap first, then use a 13 mm socket wrench to turn counterclockwise and remove the M8 crank bolts on both sides.
3. Use a crank puller to remove the crank arms.

The pedal directions are the owner's-manual fitting directions reversed (right on clockwise, left on counterclockwise). The crank puller is not in the service manual's own tool list. No torque is printed for refitting.
