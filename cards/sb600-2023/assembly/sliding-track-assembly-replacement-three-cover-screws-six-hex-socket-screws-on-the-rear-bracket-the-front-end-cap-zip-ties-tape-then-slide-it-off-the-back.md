1. Remove the 3 screws securing the sliding track cover of the sliding track assembly, then take the covers off.
2. Use M3 and M5 hex wrenches to remove the 6 hex socket screws on the iron bracket at the rear end of the aluminum tracking.
3. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the front end cap of the aluminum tracking.
4. Cut off the zip ties securing the cables along the aluminum tracking.
5. Peel off the electrical tape on the connector, then unplug the connector.
6. **Slide the sliding track toward the rear end of the main frame until it completely disengages from the aluminum tracking**, then remove the entire sliding track assembly.

The "iron bracket" is A21 (2 pcs) and its screws A23, M5 x 16 (8 in the parts list, six named here); the covers are A52/A53. The cable along the track is the hand-pulse lead to the side handlebars, which is why it is taped and zip-tied.
