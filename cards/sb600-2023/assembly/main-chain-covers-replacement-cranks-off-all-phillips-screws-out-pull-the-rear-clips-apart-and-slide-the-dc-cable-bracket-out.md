1. Follow the section "Replacing the Crank Arm and Pedal" to remove the cranks.
2. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove all screws securing the chain cover.
3. **Firmly pull apart the two rear sections of the chain cover to the sides. The rear part is held in place by clips, not screws, and can be released by applying force.**
4. Before removing the chain cover completely, **first remove the DC power cable retaining bracket. This bracket is secured by a slot and can be gently pulled out along the slot direction - no screws need to be removed.**

The parts list has ten ST3.9 x 20 screws (A51) and six 3/16" x 5/8" screws (A50) around the covers (A48, A49). The troubleshooting chapter removes the same covers to reach the belt, the idler screw, the speed sensor and its magnet.
