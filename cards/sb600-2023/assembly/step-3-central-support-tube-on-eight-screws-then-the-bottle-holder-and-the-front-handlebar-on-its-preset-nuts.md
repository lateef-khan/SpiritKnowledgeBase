1. Connect the sensor wires (A33) and (A34) to the wires (A4) and (A36).
2. Secure the central support tube (D) to the main frame (A) using 8 screws (N6) using the 6 mm wrench.
3. Release the preset screw (N11) from the support tube (D) first, then secure the water bottle holder (M1) to the support tube (D) using the same screw and the 6 mm wrench.
4. Loosen the preset nuts (I2) and washers (I1) found on the front handlebar (I). Fit the front handlebar (I) to the support tube (D) using the 6 mm Allen wrench to fasten the previously preset washers (I1) and nuts (I2).

**Note: make sure that the wires are connected securely. Be careful not to pinch the wire while assembling.**

Screw N6 is M8 x 16 and N11 is M5 x 16 by the service manual's parts list. The service manual removes this tube the other way - two 13 mm nuts off the small handlebar, eight 6 mm Allen screws, two connectors (the linked card).
