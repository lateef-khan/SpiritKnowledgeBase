**Tools included**: 6 mm Allen wrench, 5 mm Allen wrench, 13/15 mm wrench.

**Parts included**: 1 main frame, front stabilizer, rear stabilizer, central support tube, stop bar, console, saddle, back rest, front handlebars, 2 side handlebars, 2 pedals, bottle holder, water bottle, back pad, back pad support tube, hardware bag, adapter.

**N Screw Bag**, as drawn on the second page (counted from the drawing) and as listed in the service manual's parts list:

| # | Description | Drawn | Service manual qty | Used at |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| N2 | Spring washer M8 | 6 | 8 | steps 1, 2, 4, 5 |
| N3 | Washer M8 | 6 | 8 | steps 1, 2, 4, 5 |
| N5 | Screw M8 x 90 | 2 | 2 | never named in a step |
| N7 | Screw M8 x 35 | 4 | 4 | step 6 (1) |
| N8 | Screw M8 x 20 | 8 | 8 | steps 4, 5 |
| N10 | Screw M6 x 16 | 1 | 1 | step 5, stop bar |
| N13 | Screw M8 x 40 | 4 | 4 | step 4 |
| N14 | Washer M8 | 4 | 4 | step 6 (1) |

**Not drawn, but called for**: step 1 uses "two Screws (N1)" for the rear stabilizer, step 2 "2 Screws (N4)" for the front, step 3 "8 Screws (N6)" for the central support tube and a preset screw (N11), step 7 two pre-installed screws (N12). The service manual lists them - N1 screw M8 x 102 (2), N4 nut M8 (2), N6 screw M8 x 16 (8), N11 screw M5 x 16 (2), N12 screw (4) - so they exist; the owner's-manual drawing simply omits them, and the two M8 x 90 screws (N5) it does draw are used nowhere. The likeliest reading is that N5 is the front stabilizer screw the step numbers N4, with N4 the nut that goes on it; the book does not say so.
