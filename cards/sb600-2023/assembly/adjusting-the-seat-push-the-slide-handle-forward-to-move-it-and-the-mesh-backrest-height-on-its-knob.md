**Seat**: push the seat slide adjustment handle forward, then slide the seat up and down until the desired position is found. When it is found, pull the adjustment handle back into the upward position.

**Backrest**: the height of the mesh backrest can be adjusted up and down. To find the proper height, loosen and tighten the knob.

The handle is the "braking handlebar" (stop bar) fitted at step 5; the knob is the pop-pin knob the service manual turns counterclockwise to take the back pad off. No fit guide is printed.
