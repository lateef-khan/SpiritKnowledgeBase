1. Secure the rear stabilizer (B) to the main frame (A) using two screws (N1), 2 spring washers (N2) and 2 washers (N3), using the wrench (the drawing shows the 13/15 mm wrench). **Note: if the machine is not sitting evenly, turn the adjustable end-cap to the desired level in order to balance the frame.**
2. Secure the front stabilizer (C) to the main frame (A) using 2 screws (N4), 2 spring washers (N2) and 2 washers (N3), using the 6 mm wrench.

The rear goes on first, with a wrench; the front takes the Allen key. Screw N1 is M8 x 102 by the service manual's parts list; "N4" is listed there as an M8 nut, so the front stabilizer's screw is probably the pair of M8 x 90 (N5) drawn in the bag. The adjustable end cap is on the rear stabilizer.
