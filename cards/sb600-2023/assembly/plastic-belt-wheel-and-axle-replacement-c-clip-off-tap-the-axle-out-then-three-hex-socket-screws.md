1. Use snap ring pliers to remove the C-clip from the axle of the plastic belt wheel.
2. After removing the C-clip, push the axle out. **If it cannot be pushed by hand, gently tap it with a hammer** to remove the plastic belt wheel along with the axle.
3. Use a hex wrench to remove the 3 hex socket screws securing the axle to the plastic belt wheel, allowing the axle and the plastic belt wheel to be separated.

Parts: plastic belt wheel A8 (260 mm), axle A9 (17 x 150.3), the three M8 x 12 screws A10 and clip A72 (17 mm). The cranks, covers and belt come off first.
