1. Use a 5 mm Allen wrench to remove the screw securing the stop bar. Then pull the stop bar outward to remove it.
2. Use a 6 mm Allen wrench to remove the two screws securing the side handlebars. After that, disconnect the cable connectors to fully remove the left and right side handlebars.

The stop bar is the "braking handlebar (E)" of the owner's manual, held by the one M6 x 16 stopping screw (N10); each side handlebar by two M8 x 20 (N8) with spring washers and washers, and its hand-pulse cable was pushed into the end cap under the bar at assembly.
