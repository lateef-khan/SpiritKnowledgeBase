1. Use a hex wrench to remove the headless hex socket screw on the bushing.
2. Use an open-end wrench to remove the external hex screw on the bushing.
3. Pull the axle outward until it is completely disengaged.
4. Once the axle is removed, take out the 2 bushings.
5. After the axle is removed, the 2 slider stoppers can also be taken out.
6. Use an open-end wrench to remove the hex socket screw beneath the aluminum tracking, then lift it upward to remove the aluminum tracking.

Parts: axle (A59), bushings (A60), slider stoppers (A62) and stoppers (A63), two each. The brake is what the seat slide handle engages; a seat that will not lock is a worn stopper or a loose set screw here.
