1. First, use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the 2 screws securing the wire trap.
2. Use a screwdriver to push the wire trap from the outlet hole on the side of the sliding track toward the inside. **The wire trap is clipped directly into the hole on the sliding track metal frame and is not fixed by screws, so simply pushing it inward will release it.**
3. Once both ends of the wire have been pushed out, the wire trap can be removed.

The parts list carries a wire trap (A77) and a longitudinal wire trap (A78); this page is the one at the side outlet.
