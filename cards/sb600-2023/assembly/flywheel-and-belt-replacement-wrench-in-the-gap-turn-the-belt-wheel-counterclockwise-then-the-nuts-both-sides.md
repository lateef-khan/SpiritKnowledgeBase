1. **Insert a wrench into the gap between the belt and the plastic belt wheel from underneath the plastic belt wheel, then rotate the plastic belt wheel counterclockwise to remove the belt.**
2. After removing the belt, use an open-end wrench to loosen the nuts on both sides of the flywheel. Pull the flywheel backward to remove both the flywheel and the belt.

The belt is A12, "BELT 900 J6" - a 6-rib poly-V. The chain covers and cranks have to be off first (the linked card). No refit tension figure is printed here; the troubleshooting chapter sets the belt by turning the idler wheel adjustment screw (A13) until it stops slipping, and a belt that jumps off goes back "one notch in the opposite direction of the slipping".
