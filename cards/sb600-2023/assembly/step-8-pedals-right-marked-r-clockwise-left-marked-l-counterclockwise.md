1. Thread the right pedal (L-R) into the right crank of the main frame (A). Secure it by turning it **clockwise** to tighten. *Note: the right pedal (L-R) is marked with an "R".*
2. Repeat for the left pedal (L-L), securing it by turning it **counter-clockwise** to tighten. *Note: the left pedal (L-L) is marked with an "L".*

No tool and no torque are printed here; the service manual takes them off with a 15 mm open-end wrench (the linked card). This is the last assembly step.
