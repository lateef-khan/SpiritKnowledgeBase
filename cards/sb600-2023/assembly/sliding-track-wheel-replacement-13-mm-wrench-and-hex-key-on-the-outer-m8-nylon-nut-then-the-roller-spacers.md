1. First, use a 13 mm open-end wrench and a hex wrench to remove the outer M8 nylon nut.
2. Then use a hex wrench to detach the roller assembly from the sliding track.
3. After that, use an open-end wrench and hex wrench to remove the roller spacers and fastening screws.

The parts list has four wheels (A64) with four bushes (A65), four M8 x 30 screws (A66) and four M8 nuts (A67). The track assembly has to be off the frame first (the linked card). No hex key size is printed for this page.
