1. Use a 13 mm open-end wrench to remove the two nuts securing the small handlebar.
2. After removing the nuts, the small handlebar can be detached.
3. Use a 6 mm Allen wrench to remove the eight screws securing the central support tube.
4. Disconnect the two cable connectors between the upright tube and the frame to fully remove the central support tube.

The "small handlebar" is the front handlebar (I) of the owner's manual, held by the preset nuts (I2); the eight screws are N6, M8 x 16. The console does not have to come off first.
