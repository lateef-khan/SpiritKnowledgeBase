1. Press and hold **Reset** for 3 seconds, choose the User and confirm the data; or press
   Reset only.
2. Select **FITNESS** with the Up/Down arrows and press **MODE**.
3. Press **Start**; adjust the load level with the Up/Down arrows.
4. "**After 12 minutes the display will automatically show the results of your workout.**"
5. Stop pauses; Reset returns to the main menu; holding Reset for 3 seconds enters standby.

The book says nothing more about what the result is or how it is scored. This is not the
FS5.8e's dumbbell "Fitness program"; see `not_to_be_confused_with`.
