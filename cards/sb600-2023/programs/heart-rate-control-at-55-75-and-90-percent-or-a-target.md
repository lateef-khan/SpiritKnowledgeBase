1. Press and hold **Reset** for 3 seconds, choose the User and confirm the data; or press
   Reset only.
2. Select **HRC** with the Up/Down arrows and press **MODE**.
3. Select from the heart rates **55%, 75%, 90% or Target** with the arrows. "The heart rate
   value will be different based on the selected age value."
4. For **Target**, use the arrows to set the desired value.
5. Set **Time, Distance, Calories and Target Pulse**.
6. Press **Start**. "The load level will adjust according to your current heart rate."
7. Stop pauses; Reset returns to the main menu; holding Reset for 3 seconds enters standby.

The book prints no formula and no adjustment interval; the "Heart Rate Exertion" page it
shares with the other books gives 220 minus age as the maximum. Hold the handgrips or wear a
Bluetooth chest strap (sold separately). The FS3.0 has the same three percentages plus a
target defaulting to 100; the SB150-family uses 55/65/75/85. See the links.
