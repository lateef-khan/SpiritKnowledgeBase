**p. 5, the shrouded machine:** Console Assembly, **Swing Arm Bushing**, Handle Bar Cover (L) and
(R), Console Mast Cover, Side Case (L) and (R), Round Disk, Round Disk Cover, Pedal (L) and (R),
Pedal Arm Cover (R), Slide Wheel Cover.

**p. 6, the frame:** Swing Arm (L) and (R), Console Mast, **Lower Handle Bar (L) and (R)**, Pedal Arm
(L) and (R), Drive Pulley, Cross Bar, Connecting Arm (L) and (R), Flywheel, Main Frame,
**Adjustable Pedal (L) and (R)**, Bushing Housing, Pedal Arm, Rear Rail Assembly.

The frame page matches the XE395's list apart from the XE395's Rail Base Assembly and Incline
Motor (`xe395-2016-specs-outline-part-names`); the shrouded page lacks the XE195's Middle
Stabilizer Cover (`xe195-2016-specs-outline-part-names`). No stabilizer covers are labelled at all
on this book's first drawing. The Outlines chapter prints **no dimensions, no machine weight and
no user weight limit**, and no part numbers.
