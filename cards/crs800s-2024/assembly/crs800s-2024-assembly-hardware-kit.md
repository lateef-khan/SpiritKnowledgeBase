**There are five tables for four steps.** Step two is printed across two pages and each
page carries its own table, both headed Hardware for Step 2. A reader who takes only
the first is six screws and six nuts short.

| 201 | Hex head bolt | 3/8" x 2" | 4 |

## Hardware for step 2, second table

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 245 | Flat head socket screw | M8 x 1.25 x 30L | 6 |
| 246 | Square nut | M8 x 1.25 x 6.5T | 6 |

**These are the only square nuts and the only flat head socket screws in the whole 2024
commercial redesign.** They fasten the handle sliders, three a side, and they run in a
channel rather than a plain hole.

## Hardware for step 4

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 155 | Phillips head screws | M5 x 12 | 4 |
| 213 | Button head bolt | 3/8" x 3/4" | 12 |
| 214 | Flat washer | 3/8" x 19 x 1.5 | 4 |
| 215 | Curved washer | ø10 x 21.3 x 7.8T | 8 |
| 216 | Split washer | ø10 x 2T | 12 |

**Twelve bolts hold the two swing arms on**, six a side, and the washers split four
flat and eight curved between them. It is the heaviest single joint in the book.

## Item 155 is printed at two lengths in the same manual

The step one table calls item 155 **M5 x 15** and the step four table calls the same
item **M5 x 12**. The parts list gives one item number. The manual does not say which
length goes where, and the two steps use it for different jobs - twelve into the pedal
plates in step one, four into the console in step four.

## Two counts that do not close

These are the manual's own numbers, reproduced as printed:

| Item | Table says | The step uses |
|---|---|---|
| 204, flat washer, step 1 | 8 | 6 - two on the handgrip bolts and four on the handlebar bolts |
| 206, nyloc nut, step 3 | 2 | 2, but item 2 of the step secures the seat cushion with Hex Head Bolts (No.145) and no nut at all |

Item 204 is the clear one: the step's own arithmetic asks for six and the bag holds
eight. Nothing in the book accounts for the other two.

## What the tables do not print

**No torque figure for any bolt.** No step of this build gives one, and the carton
holds no tool that could set one.

## The 2021 printing is the same chapter, word for word

`spirit-climber-crs800s-2021-owners-manual`, pp. 9-15, prints this unchanged - the same
steps in the same order and the same five hardware tables, item number for item number
and quantity for quantity. Compared from the two PDFs' own text, not from the
conversions. The only difference in the whole chapter is layout: the 2021 book puts
TOOLS INCLUDED and PARTS INCLUDED on the pre-assembly page and the 2024 book puts them
on a page of their own two pages earlier.
