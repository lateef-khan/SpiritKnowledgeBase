## The transport wheels are the only ones in this redesign that a step fits

Step two begins by installing the **Transportation Wheels (No.87)** with Button Head
Bolts (No.209) and Nyloc Nuts (No.161). Every other 2024 machine that lists transport
wheels on its carton panel - the CE850 and the CS800 - lists them and never fits them.

**The manual still never says how to use them to move the machine.** Fitting them is
the whole of what it tells you.

## No levellers on the panel, and no levelling step

The panel lists no levellers and no step of the build fits one, but the maintenance
chapter says there are **two levelling pads under the rear stabiliser** and to turn
them with a 14mm wrench. That sentence is the only levelling instruction in the book.
See
[the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

## The power cord is a DC cord

The setting-up page says the console powers up "when the DC Power cord is connected to
the equipment". This machine does not generate its own power the way the 2024
commercial bikes and the CE800 elliptical do.

## What the panel does not say

It gives no carton count, no shipping weight and no box dimensions, and it lists no
handlebars, handgrips, armrests or handle sliders even though steps one, two and four
fit all four.

## The 2021 printing is the same chapter, word for word

`spirit-climber-crs800s-2021-owners-manual`, pp. 9-15, prints this unchanged - the same
steps in the same order and the same five hardware tables, item number for item number
and quantity for quantity. Compared from the two PDFs' own text, not from the
conversions. The only difference in the whole chapter is layout: the 2021 book puts
TOOLS INCLUDED and PARTS INCLUDED on the pre-assembly page and the 2024 book puts them
on a page of their own two pages earlier.
