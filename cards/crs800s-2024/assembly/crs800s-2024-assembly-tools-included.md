Seven tools, printed as a TOOLS list above the carton parts list:

- 5mm L Allen Wrench
- 6mm L Allen Wrench
- 8mm L Allen Wrench
- 12/14mm Wrench
- 13/14mm Wrench
- Phillips Screwdriver
- Short Phillips Screwdriver

**This is the largest tool panel of the fourteen 2024 commercial machines.** No other
carton in that redesign holds more than six, and the stairclimber holds two.

## Why there are two screwdrivers

The panel lists a Phillips Screwdriver and a **Short Phillips Screwdriver** as separate
items. The build fits twelve Phillips head screws (No.155) into the pedal plates in
step one, six a side, and four more into the console in step four. The manual never
says which screwdriver is meant for which screw, and the assembly steps name no tool at
all - unlike the CS800, whose steps call their tools out by item number.

## The 12/14mm is the one that levels the machine

This manual prints the same maintenance sentence as the rest of the range - two
levelling pads under the rear stabiliser, adjusted with a **14mm wrench** - and the
12/14mm wrench in this carton is what opens to 14. See
[the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

## Nothing here is a torque wrench

No step of this build gives a torque figure, and the panel holds nothing that could
set one.

## The 2021 printing is the same chapter, word for word

`spirit-climber-crs800s-2021-owners-manual`, pp. 9-15, prints this unchanged - the same
steps in the same order and the same five hardware tables, item number for item number
and quantity for quantity. Compared from the two PDFs' own text, not from the
conversions. The only difference in the whole chapter is layout: the 2021 book puts
TOOLS INCLUDED and PARTS INCLUDED on the pre-assembly page and the 2024 book puts them
on a page of their own two pages earlier.
