Five adjustments, each on its own control, printed as the whole of the setting-up page:

| Adjustment | How |
|---|---|
| **Seat rotation** | Lift the **left** lever and hold while rotating the seat to the desired position. Release the lever. |
| **Seat recline** | Lift the **right** lever and hold while adjusting the seat to the desired position. Release the lever. |
| **Seat fore/aft** | Lift the lever and hold while adjusting the seat to the desired distance from the console. Release the lever. |
| **Armrest angle** | Push the button and hold while adjusting the armrests to the desired angle. |
| **Handhold reach** | Open the lever. Adjust the handhold to the desired distance from the seat. Close the lever securely. |

**Left is rotation and right is recline.** They are the only two the manual
distinguishes by side, and they are the two a rider is most likely to confuse: both
are lift-and-hold levers on the seat.

## The fore/aft lever is not given a side

Items 1 and 2 name a left and a right lever; item 3 says only "the lever". The page has
no drawing that labels a third one, so the manual never says where it is.

## The handhold lever is the only one you close

Rotation, recline and fore/aft say **release**; the handhold says **close the lever
securely**. It is a clamp, not a spring catch, and it runs on the handle sliders that
step two of the build fastens with three flat head socket screws and three square nuts
a side. See [the build](crs800s-2024-assembly-procedure.md).

## The armrest is a button, not a lever

It is the only one of the five worked with a button, and the manual gives no range and
no number of positions for any of the five.

## This page is where the first power-on lives

The same page says the console powers up automatically when the DC power cord is
connected, performs a self-test with all lights on, then shows the software version,
the total hours of use and the total ksteps before going to the start-up display. There
is no power switch and no safety key in the sequence.
