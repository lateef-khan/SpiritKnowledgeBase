Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. Hardware quantities are
on [the hardware card](crs800s-2024-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

**The chapter has four numbered steps and five pages.** Step two runs onto a second
page headed ASSEMBLY - STEP TWO - CONTINUED, which carries its own hardware table
also headed "Hardware for Step 2". Two tables, one step number; take both.

## Step one - the seat assembly, off the machine

1. Secure the Pedals to the pedal plates with **6 Phillips Head Screws (No.155) each**.
2. Use 2 Hex Head Bolts (No.203) with 2 Flat Washers (No.204) and 2 Nyloc Nuts (No.206)
   to secure the handgrip.
3. Use a Hex Head Bolt (No.202), 2 Flat Washers (No.205) and a Nyloc Nut (No.161) to
   secure **the locking gas piston on the seat bottom**.
4. Install the Seat Cover (No.45).
5. Use 4 Hex Head Bolts (No.201), 4 Flat Washers (No.204) and 4 Nyloc Nuts (No.206) to
   secure the Handlebar to the Seat Assembly.
6. Connect the Hand Pulse Cables; arrange them so they are **not crushed during seat
   rotation and adjustment**.

*This is the only build in the 2024 redesign whose first step is entirely off the main
frame. The gas piston in item 3 is what the seat recline lever releases later.*

## Step two - transport wheels and console mast

1. Install the **Transportation Wheels (No.87)** with Button Head Bolts (No.209) and
   Nyloc Nuts (No.161).
2. Place the Console Mast (No.2) through the Console Mast Cover (No.76) the right way
   round. Snake the computer cable through the bottom of the mast and out the top.
3. Insert the mast on the Main Frame. Use Hex Head Bolts (No.208) with Split Washers
   (No.211), Flat Washers (No.169) and Nyloc Nuts (No.206) **on the side**, and Hex
   Head Bolts (No.207) with Curved Washers (No.210) **at the front and back of the
   tube**.

## Step two continued - end caps, handle sliders and the bottle cage

1. Plug the End Caps (No.67) into the oval stabilizer tubes. **Do not pinch wires.**
2. Secure the Handle Sliders (No.7 & 8) using **3 Flat Head Socket Screws (No.245) and
   3 Square Nuts (No.246) on each side**.
3. Clamp the Water Bottle Cage (No.111) to either handlebar.

*The square nuts of item 2 are the only ones in the whole 2024 redesign. They slide in
a channel in the handle rail, which is why the handhold can be moved along it
afterwards.*

## Step three - seat back and seat cushion

1. Insert the **Seat Back (No.36)** in the seat carriage and secure with Hex Head Bolts
   (No.212), Flat Washers (No.172) and Nyloc Nuts (No.206).
2. Put the **Seat Cushion (No.35)** on the seat carriage and secure with Hex Head Bolts
   (No.145).

## Step four - console and swing arms

1. Connect the computer cables from the mast to the **Console (No.27)** and secure it
   with Phillips Head Screws (No.155).
2. Install the **Left and Right Swing Arms (No.4 & 3)** onto the Main Frame using Button
   Head Bolts (No.213), Flat Washers (No.214), Split Washers (No.216) and Curved Washers
   (No.215).

**The swing arms go on last here.** On the CE850 elliptical of the same redesign they
go on in step two, before the connecting arms; on the CS800 stepper there are no swing
arms at all.

## What the build does not do

No step fits a leveller, and no step tells you how to move the machine on the wheels
step two installs. There is no first-power-on step either: the setting-up page that
follows is five seat and armrest adjustments, and the console comes on when the DC
cord is connected. See
[the adjustments card](crs800s-2024-assembly-seat-and-armrest-adjustments.md).

## The 2021 printing is the same chapter, word for word

`spirit-climber-crs800s-2021-owners-manual`, pp. 9-15, prints this unchanged - the same
steps in the same order and the same five hardware tables, item number for item number
and quantity for quantity. Compared from the two PDFs' own text, not from the
conversions. The only difference in the whole chapter is layout: the 2021 book puts
TOOLS INCLUDED and PARTS INCLUDED on the pre-assembly page and the 2024 book puts them
on a page of their own two pages earlier.
