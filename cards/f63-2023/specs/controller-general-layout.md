**Main Controller**: the circuit board holds the DC power supply for the console, the incline driver and the DC motor driver. It links the console and outputs the appropriate voltages for the motor that control the treadmill functions. It also includes the control circuit and the speed sensor.

**The 220/120V (or CEGS) lower controller area has a Filter and Choke.**

**Console**: contains keys, an LCD display, a speaker, a fan, hand pulse grips and the safety key.

The wiring section also documents two patching boards, a **console patching board** and a **console outer cover patching board**, which sit between the display board and the rest of the console. Their connections are drawings only in the source, with no readable labels.
