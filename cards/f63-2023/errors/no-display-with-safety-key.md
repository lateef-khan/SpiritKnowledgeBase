| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The ON/OFF switch has not been switched on | Switch the AC switch on |
| The safety key is in the wrong position | Insert the safety key in the right position |
| The **12 PIN** computer connector is not plugged in properly | Check the wire and connect it again |
| The **12 PIN** computer cable is broken | Replace the 12 PIN computer cable |
| The fuse on the controller is blown | Replace the fuse or the controller |
| The varistor on the controller is blown | Replace the varistor or the controller |
| The safety device is broken (open) | Replace the safety key device |
| Other components are faulty | Replace the console |

The reverse fault, where the treadmill displays or operates with no safety key fitted, means the safety device is shorted. Replace the safety key device or the console.

**A dim, incomplete or imperfect TFT LCD** is a different fault: a connector that has fallen off, a broken TFT LCD light, or too little power to the console. Check the connector, replace the LCD or console, check the power to the console, and replace the lower controller. If the TFT LCD displays themselves are broken, replace the console.
