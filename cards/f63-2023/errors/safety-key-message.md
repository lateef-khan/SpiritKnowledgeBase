This is a message, not a numbered error code. **Do not confuse it with E0.**

The safety key fits on the console and activates all functions. **Without the safety key the console cannot be controlled, and the display shows "Please Replace the Safety Key".**

A missing or badly seated key is the first thing to check. If the key is fitted correctly and the message stays, work through the E0 card.
