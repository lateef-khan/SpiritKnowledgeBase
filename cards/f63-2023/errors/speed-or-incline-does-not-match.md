| Condition | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| The speed of the belt doesn't match the console display | The controller is not calibrated, or the parameters of the controller are incorrect | Execute the calibration procedure. Then replace the controller |
| The incline position doesn't match the console | The controller is not calibrated | Calibrate the console |
| INCLINE ERR, and the incline window displays "E3" | A connector has fallen off. The position sensor value of the incline motor is wrong | Check the connector of the cable. Calibrate the console |

**Keys that do not work** are a separate fault. If the FAST/SLOW speed switch or the UP/DOWN incline switch does not work, the manual blames a badly connected SPEED CABLE to console connector, a badly connected switch cable, a broken button, or a damaged cable. Reconnect the cables, check the switch wire, replace the cable, or replace the switch.
