**When you press START and the treadmill does not start**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The motor M+ or M- wire isn't connected in the right position | Check and plug it in again |
| The motor is broken | Replace the motor, or check the wire and connector if it was broken |
| The treadmill controller shut down, and its LED would be ON | Turn the ON/OFF switch off and turn the power on again |

**When the treadmill stops or shuts off by itself**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The house breaker tripped | Reset it |
| The treadmill breaker tripped | Reset the treadmill breaker |
| The treadmill controller shut down, and its LED would be ON | **Belt and deck lubrication.** Then turn the ON/OFF switch off and turn the power on again |

**When the treadmill stops immediately after START is pressed**: the controller is broken. Turn the AC switch off and on again; then replace the controller and calibrate it.

**When the safety key is removed and the treadmill does not stop**: replace the safety key device, then the controller, then the console.

**A popping sound after the power is turned on**: the input power is incorrect and the varistor on the controller has blown. Check the power is 220V, or 110V on a 120Vac electronic power system, and replace the controller.
