**This is E2, not E3 (incline) and not E7 (input power).**

**Definition**: when the controller detects that the operating current for the drive motor is above standard, the display lights up and shows E2. The controller is protecting itself and the drive motor from damage.

**Why it happens**: typically the running belt needs lubricating, or its **bottom fibre is worn seriously and the belt needs replacing**. A dried or worn running belt makes more friction against the running deck, and that high friction makes the controller supply more current to the drive motor to hold the speed.

**Troubleshooting, in the order the manual gives it**

1. Have the user **lubricate the bottom of the running belt** as the owner's manual instructs.
2. If that does not resolve it, the running belt is worn out and needs **replacing**. With a new belt the drive motor's operating current returns to normal.
3. If E2 still occurs after the belt is replaced, either the controller or the drive motor is defective. **Since the drive motor is a passive component it is less likely to be the cause, so replace the controller first.**
