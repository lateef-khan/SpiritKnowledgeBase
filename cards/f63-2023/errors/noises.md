**Noise under the motor cover**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Worn brushes or bearings on the motor | Replace with a new motor |
| Front roller bearings are defective | Replace with a new front roller |
| The drive belt is misadjusted, too tight or too loose | Adjust the motor position |

**Noise at the rear of the treadmill**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Rear roller bearings are defective | Replace with a new rear roller |
| Rear roller misaligned | Adjust the rear roller position |

**Other rows in the same table**

- **Tread belt does not run in centre**: tread belt tension is not even across the belt.
- **Tread belt hesitates while being stepped on**: insufficient lubricant on the belt, or insufficient tread belt tension.
- **Black particles collecting under the treadmill**: the drive belt is breaking in. Vacuum periodically.

**Note**: this manual has no general maintenance section, so the belt tensioning, tracking and lubrication instructions it points at ("See treadmill belt adjustment", "See treadmill belt lubrication") are not in it. They are in the owner's manual.
