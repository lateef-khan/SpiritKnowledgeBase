Nine illustrated assembly steps for the split-console F63-2023 (console top and bottom are separate assemblies joined in step 5; hardware item numbers in brackets).

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill from the carton onto a smooth, leveled floor with the rear at least 3 ft from any wall. Remove the plastic wrap and Styrofoam from under the unit before removing the Velcro belt; tilting it sideways with the belt off can let it fold and bounce upwards.
2. With help holding the uprights, connect Computer Cable Middle (117) to Computer Cable Lower (118), insert the right and left uprights (4) and (5) into the frame base (2), and tighten eight Hex Head Bolts (99) with eight Flat Washers (100) using the 13 mm wrench (90). Leave loose until step 6 is finished; do not pinch the cables.
3. Rest the console at a suitable height or have someone hold it. Connect Computer Cable Middle (117) to Computer Cable Upper (116), insert Console Assembly Bottom (22) into the uprights, and tighten four Button Head Socket Bolts (166) with four Split Washers (188) using the M6 L wrench (103). Leave loose until step 6 is finished; do not pinch the cables.
4. Install the Fan Plate Assembly (24) on the console assembly bottom (22) with two Sheet Metal Screws (168).
5. Connect the four upper-to-lower computer-cable pairs (21-09 to 22-16, 21-10 to 22-17, 21-11 to 22-18, 21-17 to 116), place Console Assembly Top (21) onto the bottom (22), and tighten four Phillips Head Screws (167). Do not pinch the cables.
6. Install the Handrail Support (9) between the left and right uprights (5) and (4) with four Button Head Socket Bolts (125), then tighten all screws left loose above.
7. Fit Console Mast Cover Outer (L) (119), Inner (L) (120), Outer (R) (121) and Inner (R) (122) into the uprights with six Sheet Metal Screws (168).
8. Insert the Tablet Holder (177) into the console assembly top (21) with four Sheet Metal Screws (168), then set the Beverage Holder (109) onto the Handrail Support (9). Ruling: the book prints step 8.2 as "Install Handrail Support (9) on Beverage Holder (109)", reversing the two numbers; the step-8 drawing shows the holder (109) sitting on the support bar (9), and the sibling F65 book phrases it that way round, so this card follows the drawing.
9. Install Frame Base Cover left (38) and right (39) into the uprights with four Phillips Head Screws (126), then pull the Locking Pin Assembly (157) out and insert the End Cap (115) into the hole while using the machine.
