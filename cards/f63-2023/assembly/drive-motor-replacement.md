1. Loosen the **5 motor cover front locking screws** with a screwdriver.
2. Unmount the drive motor **ground wire** and its **2 input wires (black and red)**.
3. Remove the **4 drive motor locking bolts** with a **14 mm wrench** and loosen the drive belt. Then reverse the move to refit the drive motor and its 4 locking bolts, **but do not secure them yet**.
4. Adjust the drive belt tension with a **14 mm open-end wrench**. Measure the belt tension with a tension meter. **The tension needs to be about 70~75 LBS.**
5. Plug the drive motor's 2 input wires into the controller, **red to M+ and black to M-**, and mount the ground wire.
