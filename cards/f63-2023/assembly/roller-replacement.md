1. Remove both **adjustment base cover screws** with a screwdriver and take them off.
2. Remove the **2 rear roller locking bolts** with an **M6 L-Allen wrench**, then take the rear roller off.
3. Remove the drive motor cover, then unmount the drive belt from the front roller.
4. Remove the **front roller locking bolt** with a **13 mm wrench**, then take the front roller off.
5. **Adjust the running belt to the centre when installing the front or rear roller back.**
