**The cylinder has to come off first.**

1. Fold the treadmill. Unmount the cylinder with an **L-Allen wrench and a 12 mm open-end wrench**.
2. Remove the foot rails with a screwdriver.
3. Remove the **8 running deck locking screws** and take the running deck off. Now the running deck, running belt and cushions can be replaced. Reverse the steps to install them back.
4. **Adjust the running belt tension and centre it.**

Replacing a worn running belt is the second step of the E2 over current fix.
