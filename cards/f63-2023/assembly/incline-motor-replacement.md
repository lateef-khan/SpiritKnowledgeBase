1. Take the motor cover off, then fold the treadmill.
2. Unplug the incline motor wires and unmount the incline motor from the treadmill.
3. **Adjust the spare incline motor to its lower position, 195 mm.**
4. Install the incline motor back with a **14 mm open-end wrench**.
5. Plug the incline motor wires into the controller.

The incline motor wire colours are on the incline motor spec card: red is UP, black is DOWN, white is COM.
