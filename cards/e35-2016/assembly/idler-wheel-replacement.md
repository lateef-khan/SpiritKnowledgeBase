1. Follow the procedures to take apart the cross bar.
2. Use a **13mm wrench** to loosen the **M8 x 9T nyloc nut on the J bolt** until the belt can be taken off, then use the 13mm wrench to release the **M8 x 20 carriage bolt**, **M8 x 7T nyloc nut** and **5/16" x 20 x 1.5T flat washer** to take the idler wheel assembly apart.
3. Follow the above procedures in reverse, and tighten the **M8 x 9T nyloc nut on the J bolt until the belt generates a sound wave of 190 Hz (+/- 10)**.
