1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **sheet metal screws 3.5x12mm** securing the swing arm covers, front and back.
2. Disconnect the wiring connecting the upper and lower swing arms.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **M5x15mm Phillips head screws** and **3.5x12 sheet metal screws** which secure the left and right connecting arm covers A.
4. Release the **M5x15mm Phillips head screws** and **3.5x12 sheet metal screws** which secure the left and right connecting arm covers B.
5. Use a **14mm wrench** to release the **3/8" x 3/4" hex head bolt** together with the **3/8" x 30 x 1.5T flat washer** which secure the swing arms.
6. Use **#12 and #13 open end wrenches** to hold the swing arm assembly and remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 1-1/4"**, **flat washer 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T** and **nyloc nut 5/16" x 7T**. Remove the swing arm assembly.
7. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
