1. Follow the procedure to disassemble the swing arm.
2. Separate the console mast cover by gently pulling back with your hands; separate the left and right cover.
3. Use a **14mm wrench** to remove the **hex head bolts 3/8" x 3/4"**, **curved washers 3/8" x 23 x 2T**, **external hex head bolts 3/8" x 2-1/4"** and **spring washer 3/8" x 2T** that secure the console mast.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **two 3x20 tapping screws** which secure the hand pulse assembly, pull it out and disconnect the cables.
5. Unplug the round cap under the handpulse tube and pull out the handpulse cables to take the handpulse sensor assembly apart.
6. Remove the console mast and console mast cover together, pull out the control wires and incline wires, and separate the covers from the console mast.
7. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
