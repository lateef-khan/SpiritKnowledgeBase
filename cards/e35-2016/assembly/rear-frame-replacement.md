1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **M5x15mm Phillips head screws** and disconnect the incline motor power connecting wire and incline motor connecting wire.
2. Use **two 14mm wrenches and a 12mm wrench** to release the **3/8" x 1-1/2" hex head bolts**, **3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washers**, **3/8 x 7T nyloc nuts**, **5/16 x 2-1/4 hex head bolts**, **5/16" x 1.5T split washers**, **5/16" x 20 x 1.5T flat washers** and **5/16 star washer** which secure the rail base assembly, and pull it apart.
3. Follow the above procedures to refit the parts, and **make sure the wires are connected with matching colour codes**.
