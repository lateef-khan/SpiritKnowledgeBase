**190 Hz, plus or minus 10 Hz.** The manual writes it as "tighten the M8x9T Nyloc Nut on the J Bolt until the Belt generate sound wave 190HZ(+/-10)".

The figure is printed once, inside the **idler wheel replacement** procedure, not the flywheel and belt procedure. The belt slipping entry in the troubleshooting section does not repeat it; it only tells you to adjust the cap on the hook-type screw with a #13 wrench.
