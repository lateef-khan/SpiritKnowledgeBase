1. Remove the right chain cover and the idler wheel plate, then reinstall the Poly-V belt. Rotate at low speed and watch whether the drive pulley or belt is offset or deformed. Check whether the belt pulley is offset, or whether the belt, drive pulley and magnetic flywheel are not aligned in a straight line. Then rotate at **high speed, 100-120 RPM**, and test with abrupt stops to check for abnormalities.
2. If the three parts are not aligned in a straight line, **adjust the magnetic flywheel** to the proper place. Alignment is always set before shipment from the factory.

The E25 manual of the same year says 100-200 RPM for the same test.

Slipping is a separate symptom with a separate fix.
