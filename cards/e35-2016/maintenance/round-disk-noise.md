Rubbing noises are the most common problem at this part. The cause is either the **round disk off centre** or the **swing of the round disk**.

1. If the round disk is off centre, take it apart and reassemble following the procedures for replacing the chain covers.
2. If the round disk swings with too much displacement, take apart the chain covers and the round disk, then use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **5x16mm tapping screws** and **1/4" x 19mm flat washers**. Put the round disk with the cross bar on a flat platform and check for deformation. Deformation can be corrected with force.

**The displacement due to the swing of the round disk is within 3 mm prior to shipping.**
