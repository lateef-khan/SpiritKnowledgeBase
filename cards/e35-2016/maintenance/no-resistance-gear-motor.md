If there is no resistance, check whether the console functions properly and whether the steel cable is damaged. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **5x19 tapping screws** to replace the steel cable.

That is the whole of section 9-8 in this manual. The table of contents calls it "9.8 Tension Motor Problem"; the body heads it "Gear Motor".

If the display also shows E2, work the E2 card instead.
