1. If the incline motor does not function, first check the proper connection of the cables, then check the incline controller, the transformer and the console for damage. Replace the damaged part.
2. **Make sure the incline motor is reset to zero when you install a new one.** Rotate the incline barrel **clockwise to the end**, then rotate the same barrel **counterclockwise one and a half circles**. Make sure the distance of the two hole sites is **207 +/- 1mm**.

The detailed nine-step electrical test, the LED and relay checks and the potentiometer voltages are in the E3 test procedure card.
