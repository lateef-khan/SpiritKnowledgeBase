## Every letter is one step out from its XBR55 and XBR95 sisters

The XBR55 2023 and XBR95 2023 diagrams run **A to N** and start with **A. Tablet
Holder**. This machine has no tablet holder, so its list starts at the LCD Screen and
**every remaining part sits one letter earlier**. K is the Rear Frame here and the Seat
Slide Rail there; M is the Transport Wheel here and the Rear Stabilizer there.

**Do not read a letter off the wrong book.** See
[the A-to-N card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xbr-2023-assembly-parts-diagram-letters.md).

## What the diagram does not point at

It does not point at the **levellers**, which the same book's setting-up page tells you to
turn with an M14 wrench - see
[the levelling card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xb-2023-assembly-levelling-m14-wrench.md).
It does not name the console mast cover, the crank arms, the front stabilizer or the main
frame, all of which the 2019 drawing labels.

## The 2019 XBR25 drawing is unlettered

It writes fourteen names on the picture instead, and points at a **CUP HOLDER**, a
**SEAT**, a **CONSOLE MAST COVER**, **CRANK ARMS**, a **FRONT STABILIZER** and a **MAIN
FRAME**, none of which is lettered here. See
[that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xbr-assembly-parts-diagram-callouts.md).
