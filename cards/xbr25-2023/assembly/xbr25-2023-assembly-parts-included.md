|---|---|
| 1 | Main Frame |
| 1 | Console Mast |
| 1 | Rear Stabilizer |

| 1 | Front Handle Bar |

| 2 | Cup Holders |
| 2 | Pedals |
| 1 | Hardware Kit |

## Three lines fewer than the 2019 panel, and no tablet holder

The XBR25 2019 panel lists **fifteen**. This one drops the **Console Mast Cover**, the
**Front Stabilizer Cover** and the **Rear Stabilizer Cover**, exactly as the XBR55 2023
and XBR95 2023 panels do - **but it adds nothing back.** Those two add a Console Tablet
Holder; this machine has none, and its build has no step that fits one.

**It is the only one of the four 2023 residential bikes without a tablet holder**, and
the only one whose step-2 hardware still uses four M5 screws rather than seven or nine.

**Dropping the Console Mast Cover is a mistake**, as it is on the XBR55 and XBR95 2023
panels: step two of this same manual slides the computer cable through the **Console Mast
Cover (31)** and tells you to get its orientation right. The two stabilizer covers were
never fitted by any step in this family.

See [the fifteen-line card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-cr800-assembly-parts-included.md)
and [the thirteen-line card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xbr-2023-assembly-parts-included.md).

## What the panel does not say

No carton count, no shipping weight, no box dimensions. It does not name the **End Caps
(32)** that step one taps into the front stabilizer tube, nor the **Drink Bottle Holders
(L39, R38)** that step four screws to the seat handle bar, nor the **Seat Carriage (4)**,
which arrives on the main frame.
