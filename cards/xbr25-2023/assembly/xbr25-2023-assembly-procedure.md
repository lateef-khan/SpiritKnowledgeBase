Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. Hardware quantities are on
[the hardware card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xbr25-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Step one - stabilizer and seat back

1. Attach the **Rear Stabilizer (7)** to the **Main Frame (1)** with 2 **Bolts (65)**, 2
   **Flat Washers (84)** and 2 **Cap Nuts (191)**. Tighten completely with the **Wrench
   (112)**.
2. Install the **Seat Back (5)** onto the **Seat Carriage (4)** with 2 **Bolts (67)**
   through the **top** holes, secured with 2 **Flat Washers (77)** and **Nyloc Nuts
   (89)**; then 2 **Bolts (142)** through the **side** holes, secured with 2 Flat Washers
   (77) and **Nylon Nuts (89)**.
3. Insert an **End Cap (32)** into each opening of the front stabilizer tube. A rubber
   mallet may be needed; **no mallet is in the carton**.

**Step one is corrected from the 2019 book.** The 2019 manual asks for "4 WASHERS (84)"
when the bag holds two, and never mentions the cap nuts. This one says two and two, which
is what the hardware holds.

## Step two - console mast, console and handlebar

1. Slide the **Computer Cable (44)** through the bottom of the **Console Mast Cover
   (31)** and then the **Console Mast (2)**, cover the right way up.
2. Install the Console Mast (2) into the receiving tube of the Main Frame (1), **without
   pinching cable 44**. Insert 2 **Hex Head Bolts (68)** and 2 **Flat Washers (76)** on
   each side, and 2 Hex Head Bolts (68) with 2 **Curved Washers (83)** on the front.
   Tighten all six firmly with the Wrench (112).
3. Remove the white Styrofoam pad fitted to stop bolts dropping down the mast tube.
4. Insert the Computer Cable (44) and the **Hand Pulse Cable (45)** into their connectors
   behind the **Console Assembly (19)** and fix the console to the mounting plate with 4
   **Phillips Head Screws (99)**, using the **Phillips Head Screwdriver (114)**.
5. Attach the **Handle Bar Assembly (3)** to the Console Mast (2) bracket with 2 Hex Head
   Bolts (68), 2 **Split Washers (82)** and 2 Flat Washers (76). Tighten with the Wrench
   (112).

**There is no console holder step.** The XBR55 2023 and XBR95 2023 fit a tablet holder
here with three more screws; this machine has none.

**Two cables, not three.** The XBR55 and XBR95 route a Handlebar Resistance Control Cable
(148) as well; the XBR25 has no handlebar resistance control.

## Step three - seat and seat handlebar

1. Install the **Seat (61)** on the Seat Carriage (4) with 4 **Phillips Head Screws
   (98)**.
2. Attach the **Seat Handle Bar (6)** to the Seat Carriage (4) with 4 **Hex Head Bolts
   (71)**, 4 **Flat Washers (77)** and 4 **Nyloc Nuts (89)**, "with the WRENCHES provided
   (112 & 130)".
3. Plug the **Hand Pulse Sensor Cables (27)** into the rear two holes on the left side of
   the rear plastic cover.

**There is no wrench 130 in this carton.** The tool panel on the same book's page 6 lists
**#112 (12/14mm), #132 (14/15mm) and #114 (Phillips)**. #130 is the XBR55's 13/14mm. The
2019 book gets it right and says 112 & 132; use the 14/15mm. See
[the tools card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-cr800-assembly-tools-included.md).

## Step four - pedals and bottle holders

1. Attach the **Pedals (L, R) (116, 117)** to the **Crank Arms (51L, 51R)**, tightening
   with the **Wrench (132)**. The **left pedal is reverse-threaded** and goes on
   counterclockwise; there is an "L" stamped in the end of the threaded post of the left
   pedal and an "R" in the right. **No torque figure is printed** - see
   [the pedal card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-bike-assembly-pedal-tighten-as-much-as-possible.md).
2. Attach the **Drink Bottle Holders (L39, R38)** to the sides of the Seat Handle Bar (6)
   with 4 **Sheet Metal Screws (105)**, using the Phillips Head Screwdriver (114).

**There is no gas cylinder step**, as there is none in the 2019 book. The XBR55 and XBR95
fit one here.

## Then set the machine up

This book carries a **SETTING UP YOUR BIKE** page after the build, which the 2019 book
does not: levelling with an M14 wrench, plugging in and powering on, and moving the bike
on its two transport wheels. See
[the levelling card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xb-2023-assembly-levelling-m14-wrench.md)
and [the transport card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xb-2023-assembly-transport-wheels.md).

## What changed from the 2019 XBR25 build

The hardware did not change at all. What changed is the wording: the step-1 washer and
cap-nut count is corrected, the console and the handlebar are split into two numbered
items instead of one run-on paragraph, and step three's wrench number is **broken** where
2019 had it right.
