**The console face, as labelled on the overview drawing:** **LCD Window**, **Adjustable Fan**,
**Program Buttons** and **Direct Resistance Level Buttons**. **Four call-outs, and that is all.**

**The workout screen, as labelled on the workout-mode drawing:**

| Where | Call-out |
|---|---|
| Top row | **Total Calories Burned**, **Current Heart Rate**, **Cumulative Total Mileage**, **Current Resistance Level**, **Current Revolutions per minute**, **Total Laps Completed** |
| Left edge | **Current Speed** |
| Right edge | **Current Resistance Level** |
| Left column | **Message Center** |
| Bottom | **Current Speed Level**, **Current Time (Elapsed or Countdown based on Program)**, **Resistance Program Profile** |

**This machine has no USB port and no Bluetooth.** Its three 2023 stablemates - the XBR55, XBR95 and
XBU55 - label **USB Charging** on the overview drawing and carry a **USB Charge Icon**, a **Bluetooth
Speaker** icon, a **Bluetooth Heart Rate Icon** and a **Bluetooth On/Off** icon down the left of the
workout screen, above the Message Center. **Every one of those is absent here**, and so is the
**Maximum Heart Rate Percentage** label on the right edge.

**The absence was proved twice.** The words **USB** and **Bluetooth** appear nowhere in the whole
manual, and "app" appears only inside "appliance"; and because both console pages are flattened
images - 15 and 47 native words - they were rendered at 300 dpi and read, which returned the four
call-outs above and no others. **Do not send an XBR25-2023 owner looking for a port.**

**So there is no Spirit+ app section either.** The manual has no USING THE SPIRIT+ APP page, unlike
the other three 2023 bikes: `xt-2023-console-spirit-plus-app-pairing`. Workout data does not leave
this machine.

**Everything else about the console is shared with the other three 2023 bikes**: the same twenty-column
dot matrix, the same Direct Resistance Buttons, the same Pulse Grip Feature, the same Calorie Display
and the same engineering menu.

**The three that do have the ports are on their own card**:
`xe795-2023-console-overview-and-workout-mode-call-outs`.
