1. Use an **8 mm hex key** to remove the **centre axle screw CP-M8 x P1.0 (34)**. Use a **crank puller** to extract the **left crank (33)**. Then use a Phillips screwdriver to remove **three umbrella head self-tapping screws 3.5 x 12L (79)** and **three umbrella head screws M5 x 12L (80)**, to detach the **left chain cover (48)**.
2. Use an **8 mm hex key** to remove the **centre axle screw CP-M8 x P1.0 (34)**. Use a crank puller to extract the **right crank (32)**. Then use a Phillips screwdriver to remove **three umbrella head screws M5 x 12L (80)**, to detach the **right chain cover (47)**.
3. Reverse the steps to reassemble.

The left cover takes six screws of two kinds; the right cover takes three.
