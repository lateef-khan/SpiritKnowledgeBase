1. Use a **5 mm hex key** to remove from the handle assembly:
   - **4 pieces of hexagon screws 5/16" x UNC18 x 3/4" (96)**
   - **4 pieces of spring washers Ø8 x 1.5T (5/16" x 1.5T) (97)**
   - **4 pieces of flat washers Ø5/16" x 16 x 1T (98)**
2. Loosen and remove the fixed bracket of the **quick release lever (38)**. Then use a **5 mm hex key** to remove the **hexagon screw 5/16" x UNC18 x 1" (92)** inside the handle to detach the **sliding handlebar mount (7)**.
3. Loosen and remove the fixed bracket of the **quick release lever (39)**. Remove **2 pieces of aluminium blocks (40)** and the **brake spring (41)**. Pull up the handle's **telescopic tube assembly (5)** along with the **hollow sleeve (42)**. Use a **4 mm hex key** to remove the **hexagon screw M5 x 10L** inside the socket and take out the hollow sleeve (42).
4. Reassemble in the reverse order.

**This machine's handlebar is not the SB1200's.** The SB1200 manual mounts the handlebar with six M8 x P1.25 x 15L hex screws and adds a screen rack and screen rotation assembly. This one uses four imperial hexagon screws and has no screen rack.
