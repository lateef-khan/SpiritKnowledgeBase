1. Remove the **right crank (32)**, **right chain cover (47)**, **left crank (33)**, **left chain cover (48)**, **belt (37)** and the idler wheel assembly. Rotate the Bluetooth wireless resistance knob to the **lightest** setting, then remove the **nylon nut M6 x 6T (83)** that secures the fixed magnetic control assembly. Pull down the **push rod axle (24)** and detach it from the **movable assembly A weldment (16)**. Push the magnetic control assembly **upward** to remove it.
2. Use a **6 mm hex key** to remove the **hex screw M8 x P1.25 x 30L (74)** from the Flywheel Fixed Plate, and a **13 mm open-end wrench** to remove the **nylon nut M8 x 7T (89)**. Then use a **4 mm hex key** to remove **three hex screws M6 x P1.0 x 10L (71)** from the Flywheel Fixed Plate.
3. Remove the **Flywheel Fixed Plate assembly (15)**.
4. Take out the flywheel assembly.
5. Use a **C-ring clip clamp** to remove the **C-ring Ø17 (77)**, so the **flywheel axle (28)** can be extracted. **Replace the 6003 bearings (62) at this step** if they need replacing.
6. Reverse the steps to reassemble.

The nylon nut in step 1 and the cap nut in section 6.3 are both called part 83.
