Hardware: **#80** M5 × 12mm Phillips Head Screw (4 pcs), **#99** 4 × 16mm Sheet Metal Screw (3 pcs).

1. Install the **Drink Bottle Holder (50)** and the **Flywheel Cover (49)** in front of the Main Frame (1) with four Phillips Head Screws (80), by using the **Short Phillips Head Screw Driver (103)** and the **M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (101)**.
2. Install the **Tablet Holder (60)** on the Handlebar (4) with three Sheet Metal Screws (99), by using the M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (101).

The tablet holder is item **60** on this machine; on the SB700 and SB900 from 2019 to 2021 it is item 138.

**The SB1200 2023 splits this work across two steps**: its step 3 is the console and tablet holder (with five #99 screws, not three), and its step 4 is the drink bottle holder and flywheel cover.
