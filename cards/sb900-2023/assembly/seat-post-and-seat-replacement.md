1. Use a **14 mm open-end wrench** to loosen the nut underneath the **seat cushion (36)** and remove the seat cushion.
2. Loosen and remove the fixed bracket of the **quick-release lever (38)**. That lets you detach the **dumbbell rack assembly (27)** and the **sliding seat mount (6)**.
3. Loosen and remove the fixed bracket of the **quick-release lever (39)**. Remove **2 pieces of aluminium blocks (40)** and the **brake spring (41)**. Pull up the seat post's **seat sliding tube assembly (8)** along with the **hollow sleeve (42)**. Use a **4 mm hex key** to remove the **hexagon screw M5 x 10L** inside the socket and take out the hollow sleeve (42).
4. Reassemble following the reverse steps.

**Quick-release lever 38 is used for two different jobs in this manual.** Here it holds the sliding seat mount; in section 6.7 it holds the sliding handlebar mount.
