| Item | Tool |
|---|---|
| #100 | 14/15mm Wrench |
| #101 | M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench |
| #102 | M6 L Allen Wrench |
| #103 | Short Phillips Head Screw Driver |

Four tools. The whole tool numbering changed for 2023: on the 2021 and earlier indoor cycles the wrench is #85 and the combination Allen/Phillips driver is #86.

**The SB1200 2023 ships only three of these four** - #100, #102 and #103. It has no #101 M5 Allen wrench, because its handlebar bolts are M8 socket head cap bolts driven with #102.

The console step also calls for an **M3 L Allen Wrench**, which is not listed in ASSEMBLY TOOLS and is given no item number anywhere in the manual.
