Hardware: **#96** 5/16" × 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt (4 pcs), **#97** 5/16" × 1.5T Split Washer (4 pcs), **#98** ø5/16" × 16 × 1.0T Flat Washer (4 pcs).

1. Install the Handlebar (4) and the Sliding Handlebar Mount (7) with four Button Head Socket Bolts (96), four Split Washers (97) and four Flat Washers (98). Tighten the bolts securely by using the **M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (101)**.

**The item numbers changed completely for 2023.** On the 2021 and earlier SB900 the handlebar bolt is #88 and the washers are #47/#146 or #48/#49. Here they are #96, #97 and #98, and #88 no longer exists in the pack.

**The SB1200 2023 uses #96 for a different bolt** - an M8 × P1.25 × 15L Socket Head Cap Bolt supplied 6 pcs, for the handlebar and screen holder - and has no #97 or #98 at all.
