Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #94 | ø10 × ø16.5 × 2T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| #93 | 3/8" × 3-3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #95 | ø10 × ø23 × 1.5T | Curved Washer | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #96 | 5/16" × 3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #97 | 5/16" × 1.5T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| #98 | ø5/16" × 16 × 1.0T | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 3**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #80 | M5 × 12mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| #99 | 4 × 16mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 3 pcs |

The checklist runs 1, 2, 3. The machine has seven numbered assembly steps - 1 stabilizer tubes, 2 sliding handlebar mount, 3 flywheel cover / tablet holder, 4 dumbbells holder, 5 left/right pedals, 6 Bluetooth wireless knob, 7 console - and steps 4 to 7 use no hardware out of the assembly pack.

**The whole pack was renumbered for 2023.** On the 2019-2021 indoor cycles `#93` is an M5 tapping screw and `#88` is the handlebar bolt; here `#93` is the 3/8" stabilizer bolt and there is no #88 in the pack. Never carry an item number across from an earlier SB900.

**The SB1200 2023 uses the same item numbers for a different pack:** its `#96` is an M8 × P1.25 × 15L socket head cap bolt supplied 6 pcs, not the 5/16" × 3/4" button head socket bolt supplied 4 pcs listed here.
