This step needs no hardware from the assembly pack.

1. Loosen the flywheel and remove the flywheel spacer from the flywheel: unfasten the velcro on the knob holder (1), connect the wireless resistance knob, then turn the wireless resistance knob (2) **counterclockwise to the minimum value** and remove the flywheel spacer (3) from the machine.

The circled numbers 1, 2 and 3 are positions on the drawing, not item numbers from the parts list.

**The flywheel spacer is a shipping part.** It comes out during assembly and is not refitted.

**The SB1200 2023 prints the equivalent step under a different name and with a different part.** Its step 7 is headed BRAKE TENSION KNOB and removes an **Aluminum Ring Mount**, not a flywheel spacer.

The manual prints "fiywheel" for "flywheel" once in this instruction.
