**The wireless transmitter**

1. Pull the wireless transmitter **downwards** to unmount it.
2. Push the **back cover** in the direction of the arrow to reach the batteries and replace them.
3. Reverse the steps to reinstall.

**The Bluetooth wireless (Smart Tension) knob**

Use a **2.5 mm hex key** to remove **three M3 x 6L hex screws** from the socket under the knob. Pull up the **top cover** to reach the battery. Reverse the steps to reassemble.

**The manual describes the knob battery job twice, and the two descriptions do not match.** Section 6.1 step 3 gives the three M3 x 6L hex screws and the top cover, as above. Section 5.2 on page 12 says instead: "Remove both sides' screws in order to change the batteries. To open turn counterclockwise, to close turn clockwise." Take the section 6.1 description as the working one; it names the screws and the tool.

The manual does not name the battery type for either part.
