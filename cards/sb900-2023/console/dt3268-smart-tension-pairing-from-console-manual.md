1. First **install and calibrate** the smart tension / auto gear on the bike.
2. Make sure the tension knob and the console are **awake**. In **DIST or KCAL** mode, hold the **PAGE** key on the console for **3 seconds** to get into the setting.
3. In pairing mode, **repeat twisting the smart tension for a few seconds, until the word "Level" appears**. That means the pairing with the smart tension / auto gear succeeded.
4. Hold **MODE and PAGE** for a few seconds to get back to the main page.

**This procedure and the service manual's differ on how pairing mode is entered.** The service manual has you press and hold MODE and PAGE together once inside setting mode, and says the LEVEL symbol lights up when the knob has paired. This console manual gives no such key press and uses the appearance of the word "Level" as the success signal. Both are printed for this machine.

Calibration is a separate job from pairing, and the knob has its own setup steps done at the knob.
