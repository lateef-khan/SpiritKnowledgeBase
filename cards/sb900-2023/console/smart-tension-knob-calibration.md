**Pair the knob before calibrating it.** Pairing and calibration are two separate procedures.

1. Confirm the console is connected to Smart Tension. On a successful connection the **LEVEL symbol flashes rapidly for 2 seconds**.
2. With the **TIME** display active, **long-press the PHONE key (the middle button)** to enter calibration mode. The screen shows the left-level indicator and **"LE"** in the centre.
3. In calibration mode, press and hold **MODE and PAGE** to start the calibration. The number **1** begins flashing below "LE".
4. Turn the Smart Tension knob to the **lightest** resistance level, then press **MODE** to confirm. Level 1 is now set and the number **100** begins flashing at the bottom of the screen.
5. Turn the Smart Tension knob to the **heaviest** resistance level, then briefly press **MODE** to confirm. The heaviest setting is stored, the screen leaves calibration mode and returns to normal operation.
6. Calibration is complete.

A **blue light** shows on the tension knob while it is being touched and working properly. The light goes off when it is not touched.
