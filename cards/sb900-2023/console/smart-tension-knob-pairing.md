**This is the Smart Tension knob pairing, not the speed sensor transmitter pairing.** The transmitter pairing is a separate procedure in section 5.3 and uses different keys.

1. Press any button to wake the console, then press **MODE** to switch the display to **DIST** or **KCAL**. Press and hold the **PAGE** key to enter setting mode.
2. **The Smart Tension knob must be awake before pairing**, so it does not fall asleep during the process. Wake it by touching it.
3. In configuration mode, press and hold **MODE and PAGE together** to enter pairing mode.
4. When the knob has paired, the **LEVEL symbol at the bottom left of the console display lights up**.

**The knob's sleep timer is printed two different ways.** This procedure says the knob "enters sleep mode 15 seconds after losing Bluetooth connection". The Other Instructions section on page 12 says it enters sleep mode after **20 seconds** without a Bluetooth connection, a touch or a rotation. Both are in the same section of the same manual.
