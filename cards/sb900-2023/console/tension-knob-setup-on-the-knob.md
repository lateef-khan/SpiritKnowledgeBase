**This is the procedure done at the knob, using its own side button and LEDs. It is
not the console-side calibration**, which is a separate procedure driven from the
console keys. The knob manual gives these steps to take after installing the knob,
so the resistance level reads correctly.

1. **Hold the side button until a green light is on.** Then turn the knob right half
   a circle and left half a circle. Keep doing this — **repeat 3 to 5 times** — until
   the green light turns off by itself. The manual shows the button held for
   **3 seconds** to start this.
2. Turn the tension knob to the **lowest** resistance level, then **short press** the
   side button. The **(R) LED turns red**. With the red light on, turn the knob to the
   **highest** resistance, until the knob cannot turn any more. **Short press** the
   button again to turn the red light off. That finishes setting the levels on the knob.
3. A **blue light** shows when the tension knob is touched and working properly. The
   blue light goes off when it is not touched.

The knob has a **left (L)** and a **right (R)** LED, and hex socket head cap screws
on both sides.
