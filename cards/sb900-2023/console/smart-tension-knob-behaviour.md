**Waking it up.** In power-saving (standby) mode with no active connection, press any button to wake the **console** first. The LEVEL symbol on the console starts flashing, which means it is ready to connect to the knob. To wake the **knob**, **gently touch it rather than turning it quickly**. The knob flashes a blue light to show it is awake. If no blue light flashes, touch a larger surface area of the knob. Once the blue light flashes and the console's LEVEL symbol stops flashing, the connection is made.

**The blue light.** The knob is activated by touch and flashes a blue light rapidly for **2 seconds** when it wakes. A blue light also shows while the knob is being touched and working properly, and goes off when it is not touched.

**Sleep.** If the knob has no Bluetooth device connected and is not touched or rotated, it enters sleep mode after **20 seconds**.

**The wrong level number.** Because of the knob's mechanical structure, slight discrepancies in the level reading can happen. If turning to the first resistance level shows a different value, **press the side button on the Smart Tension knob to reset it**. The level then displays correctly as the first level, and the software completes the correction automatically.

**The sleep timer is printed two different ways in this section.** The pairing procedure on page 11 says the knob "enters sleep mode 15 seconds after losing Bluetooth connection". This page says 20 seconds. Treat 15 seconds as the shorter, safer assumption when pairing.
