The knob manual gives two faults and their fixes:

| What you see | What the manual says to do |
|---|---|
| The tension knob is not being touched, but the blue light is always on | Reinstall the battery |
| The blue light reacts slowly when the knob is touched | Allow the tension knob to go to sleep after **15 seconds**, then touch it to wake it up |

The 15 seconds here agrees with the pairing procedure and disagrees with the Other
Instructions section, which says 20 seconds. That disagreement is recorded on the
knob behaviour card.

**In the source these two entries are printed with the labels reversed** — the line
marked "A." is the symptom and the line marked "Q." is the remedy. The table above
puts them the right way round.
