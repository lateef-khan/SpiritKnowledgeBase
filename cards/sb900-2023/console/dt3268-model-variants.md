The console manual covers a **series**, and states that it is for spinning bike console DT3268 series:

- **DT3268 I** — with power function, can measure **L&R power balance**.
- **DT3268T** — with resistance function, can measure the **resistance level**.

The extra readout only appears when the matching hardware is paired:

- **Power balance** appears only once a power meter pedal is paired, and is measured by that pedal.
- **Level** appears only once a Smart Tension knob or auto gear is paired, and is measured by the wireless smart tension.

**The manual does not say which variant is fitted to any particular bike.** A console that never shows a Level or a Power balance figure may be the other variant, not a fault.
