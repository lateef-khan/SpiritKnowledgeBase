| Value | What the manual says |
|---|---|
| TIME | Length of time in minutes and seconds. It counts up or down while you pedal. When pedalling stops the timer stops after 3 seconds. |
| CADENCE & SPEED | Cadence is how fast the cranks turn, in RPM. Approximate speed can show here too, in MPH or KPH. Cadence also has a bar graph. Average cadence or speed shows automatically 3 seconds after you stop pedalling. |
| DISTANCE | Approximate distance achieved on the bike, calculated as if the tyres had the same diameter as the flywheel. |
| CALORIES (KCAL) | Approximate calories burned. **Calculated by measuring the rider's RPM, Watts, age and weight.** |
| HEART RATE | Approximate heart rate from a Bluetooth HR monitor. HR featured models only. Average heart rate shows after 3 seconds if the console cannot detect a current heart rate. |
| POWER (WATT) | Approximate applied force during the workout. **Measured by RPM and resistance level.** |

**Two of these differ from the 2020 and 2021 indoor cycles, and the difference matters.**

- Those manuals say calories are calculated from the rider's **instantaneous heart rate**, age and weight — not from RPM and Watts. On this machine a rider with no heart rate monitor still gets a calorie figure by a different route.
- Those manuals say Watt is measured by RPM alone, and put the phrase "This is measured by RPM" under DISTANCE instead. This manual puts resistance level into the power calculation.
