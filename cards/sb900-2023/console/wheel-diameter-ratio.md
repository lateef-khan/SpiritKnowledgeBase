The wheel diameter ratio feeds the RPM and speed calculation. **Set it wrong and the rider feels that the RPM or speed is inaccurate.**

- **The wheel diameter ratio on this machine is 1:3.25.**
- **The factory default setting is "325".**

To check or correct it:

1. Press **MODE** to select **KCAL** mode.
2. Long-press the **PAGE** key to enter the setting, then press **MODE** to select the screen with the wheel diameter icon.
3. Press **PAGE** to enter the wheel diameter ratio setting.
4. Press **MODE** to input the wheel diameter setting as **"325"**.
5. Press the **PAGE** key for **3 seconds** to return to the main screen when the setting is finished.

No other Sole indoor cycle service manual carries this setting. Do not assume 325 applies to the SB700 or the earlier SB900.
