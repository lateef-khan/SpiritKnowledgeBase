Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | #123 | 3/8" x 2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 6 pcs |
| 3 | #86 | 3/8" x 2T | Split Washer | 6 pcs |
| 3 | #79 | 10 mm | Star Washer | 6 pcs |
| 3 | #100 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #91 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #86 | 3/8" x 2T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 6 | #92 | M5 x 10 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 8 pcs |
| 6 | #90 | 3.5 x 12 mm | Self Tapping Screw | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #100 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 8 | #92 | M5 x 10 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |

Tools in the box: Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (#103), M6 Allen Wrench (#104), #18 Lubricant. The checklist panel misnumbers the two wrenches as #104 and #105; every assembly step in this book and the whole S77 2013 scheme call them (103) and (104), and this card follows the steps. The step-3 hardware label misprints #100 as 5/16" x 1/2"; the checklist, step 7 and the S77 2013 book agree on 5/16" x 3/4", and this card follows 3/4". The step-6 hardware label misprints #99; step text and checklist agree on (90), and this card follows (90). Only four #100 bolts are supplied but they are cited in both step 3 (mast fronts) and step 7 (support brace); this card follows the supplied count of four.
