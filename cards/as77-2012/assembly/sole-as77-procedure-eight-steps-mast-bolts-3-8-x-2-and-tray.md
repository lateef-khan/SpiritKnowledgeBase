Eight illustrated assembly steps (hardware item numbers in brackets). This book has no Velcro-strap unpacking warning.

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill base frame from the carton onto firm, level ground.
2. Slide the console mast tube covers (99 onto 5, 98 onto 4) onto the mast tubes with the holes facing outward, and connect Lower Computer Cable (32) to Middle Computer Cable (33).
3. Bolt the left (5) and right (4) mast tubes with brackets (7) to the base frame: three Star Washers (79), three Split Washers (86) and three Button Head Socket Bolts (123) per side, plus two Button Head Socket Bolts (100) with Curved Washers (124) at the front of each side. Leave everything loose until after step 7. Do not pinch the cables in the tubing.
4. Connect Speed Switch Cable (37) to upper Speed Cable (34), Incline Switch Cable (38) to upper Incline Cable (35), and Computer Cable (33) to upper Computer Cable (31).
5. Fix the Console Assembly (36) onto the uprights with two Button Head Socket Bolts (91) and two Split Washers (86) per side; tighten completely with the M6 wrench.
6. Fix the left (111, 112) and right (113, 114) handgrip side caps with four Phillips Head Screws (92) and two Self Tapping Screws (90) per side.
7. Bolt the upright support brace (3) between the uprights with two Button Head Socket Bolts (100) per side, set the accessory tray (120) on the brace, then completely tighten the four mast-to-frame bolts on each side.
8. Slide the mast tube covers to the bottom of each tube and fix with two Phillips Head Screws (92) per side.

Tool-number ruling: the steps call the combination driver (103) and the M6 wrench (104); the checklist panel numbers them #104 and #105. This card follows the steps.
