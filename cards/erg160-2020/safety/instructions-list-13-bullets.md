**The ERG160 book has no numbered appliance list at all.** Its safety page is the Proposition 65
warning (`xterra-treadmill-safety-proposition-65-warning`) followed by a single run of thirteen
Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions bullets - the shortest safety page of the eleven rower books.

| # | Bullet |
|---|---|
| 1 | Keep children and pets away from the machine at all times. DO NOT leave children unattended in the same room with the machine. |
| 2 | Only one person at a time should use the machine. |
| 3 | If the user experiences dizziness, nausea, chest pain, or any other abnormal symptoms, STOP the workout at once. CONSULT A PHYSICIAN IMMEDIATELY. |
| 4 | This exercise equipment is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge. |
| 5 | Use this appliance only for its intended use as described in this manual. Do not use attachments not recommended by the manufacturer. |
| 6 | Do not operate equipment on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and equipment may result. |
| 7 | Before beginning this or any exercise program, consult a physician. This is especially important for persons over the age of 35 or persons with pre-existing health conditions. |
| 8 | Keep hands away from all moving parts. |
| 9 | Never drop or insert any object into any opening. |
| 10 | Do not use outdoors. |
| 11 | Do not attempt to use your equipment for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended. |
| 12 | Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable for use on your equipment. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue. |
| 13 | **Weight Limit: 250 lb** (`erg160-2020-safety-user-weight-limit-250-lbs`) |

The page closes with `Failure to follow all guidelines may compromise the effectiveness of the
exercise experience, expose yourself (and possibly others) to injury, and reduce the longevity of the
equipment.`, `SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!` and `CAUTION!! Please be careful when unpacking
the carton.`

**What is missing, compared with the other rower books**: no *blanket or pillow*, *air openings*,
*aerosol*, *heated surfaces*, *household use* or *damaged or dropped* item, no pulse-sensor bullet,
no loose-clothing bullet and no clearance rule (`xterra-rower-safety-no-clearance-figure-printed`).
There is no cord item because the console runs on two AA batteries
(`xterra-rower-safety-battery-console-no-power-adapter`). The warning sticker drawn on the next
page adds the rules the list lacks - inspect before use, heart-rate readings for reference only,
and the yellow mark on the belt (`xterra-erg-safety-warning-sticker-with-the-yellow-belt-mark`).
The 2023 ERG180, the same frame in the new layout, prints a full numbered list
(`xterra-rower-safety-instructions-list-free-space-and-tighten-bolts-with-11-fitness-bullets`).
