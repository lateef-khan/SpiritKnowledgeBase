**What the list tells a service call.** The console is item 91, **X-3508**; the flywheel item 89 (Ф150); the belt item 17, **220J*14**; the tension knob item 30 and adjust knob item 47; the sensor wire item 40 (400 mm), the sensor magnet item 69 and the sensor bracket item 18; the one-way bearing item 80, **HF1712**; the seat item 38 with three seat rollers (60) on 608ZZ bearings (67); the volute (recoil) spring item 39. The two tools packed with the rower are the last two rows - item 99, the **Allen key L5**, and item 100, the **multi-function wrench**, which the assembly pack checklist (PDF p. 11) sizes as *S5* and *S13-14-19*. The book prints *Foot pat* for item 20 and *Meshbelt* for item 37 as written.

**The service manual prints no list of its own.** The ERG160 service manual (V1.0, 2025) has no parts list, exploded view or tool list; its replacement procedures cite these numbers - Pedal L/R (12), Belt Pulley (22) and Drawstring Pulley (23), Seat Roller (60) - so this list is what decodes them (`xterra-specs-parts-which-rower-and-strength-manuals-print-an-exploded-view-and-parts-list`). The ERG180 is the same frame with a different numbering from item 11 on (`erg180-2023-specs-parts-list-items-1-to-95-from-the-service-manual`); do not read an ERG180 callout off this table.

**Damage in the extraction.** The native text layer of the list is clean; the OCR supplement for the same pages misreads `Ф` as `0` or `©` (`0142*01670.5`, `©10*06.1*40`) and *6000ZZ* as *60002ZZ*. The table above is the native layer.
