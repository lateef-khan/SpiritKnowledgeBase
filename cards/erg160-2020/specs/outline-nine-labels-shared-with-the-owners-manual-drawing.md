**One drawing, nine labels, printed in both books.** The service manual's contents page is the owner's manual's
"Before you begin" picture with the same callouts and the remark "Some figure may different from the machine, but
you can refer to the procedure to replace parts."

| Label | Where it points |
|---|---|
| Console | the display on its bracket at the top of the main frame |
| Handlebar | the bar hanging below the console |
| Front Stabilizer | the front foot tube |
| Pedals | the pedal pair on the frame |
| Seat | on the rail |
| Sliding Rail | the rail running back to the rear stabilizer |
| Rear Stabilizer | the rear foot tube |
| Warning Label | the orange safety sticker on the frame |
| Serial Number Sticker | the white sticker with the barcode and (870) 333-5500 |

The drawing carries no dimension, no item number and no exploded view; the service manual has no parts list of
its own (the owner's manual's 100-item list is the parts section's). The ERG180 service manual instead prints a
numbered eleven-callout drawing (`erg180-2023-specs-main-parts-indication-eleven-callouts-console-to-pedal-l`).
