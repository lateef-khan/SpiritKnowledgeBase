1. Remove the screws from the end caps of the rear stabilizer, then gently tap off the end caps using a rubber mallet.
2. Reassemble in the reverse order.

The end caps (33) are the rower's levelers. The stabilizer itself comes off the slide rail on the four M8 x 20 Allen bolts of assembly step 2, which this page does not mention.
