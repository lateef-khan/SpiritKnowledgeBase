1.1 Remove the **slide rail (2)** by detaching both side and bottom mounting points as illustrated - the two side bolts (46) and four bottom screws (92) of assembly step 4.
1.2 Remove the two screws from the **Stop Bracket R / L (11, 15)**.
2.1 Remove the **Limiter Pad** from the slide rail.
2.2 Remove the four screws securing the **Rear Stabilizer (4)** to the slide rail.
3.1 Remove the **Seat (38)** from the slide rail and detach the **Aluminum Plate (57)**.
Reassemble in the reverse order.

The aluminum plates (57, two of them) are the wear strips the seat rollers run on; the book does not say how they fasten. The ERG180's version of this page is shorter (linked card).
