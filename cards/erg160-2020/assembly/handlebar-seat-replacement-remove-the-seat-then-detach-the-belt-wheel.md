1. Remove the handlebar seat.
2. Detach the belt wheel.
3. Follow the above steps to reinstall.

The handlebar seat (21, TPR) is the cradle the handle parks in at the front of the frame - not the rower's seat and not the handlebar. The book names no fastener; the belt wheel (28) behind it is the POM wheel the mesh belt runs over. The Spirit CRW800H2O's cradle comes off on two M6 x 15 screws with a 4 mm key (linked), which this book does not say.
