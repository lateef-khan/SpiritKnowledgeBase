**Parts** (the checklist drawing): 1 Main Frame, 2 Slide Rail, 3 Front Stabilizer, 4 Rear Stabilizer, 12R/L Pedals (2), 38 Seat Assembly, 91 Console, plus the hardware bag and the manual. "Above parts are all needed to assemble this machine" - check the hardware packing before you start.

**Hardware bag, by step**

| Step | # | Item | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 34 | Limiter pad | 1 |
| 1 | 62 | Flat head screw M6 x 10 | 2 |
| 2 | 81 | Allen bolt M8 x 20 | 4 |
| 2 | 52 | Spring washer ID8.5 | 4 |
| 2 | 55 | Flat washer OD16 x ID8.5 x 1.5 | 4 |
| 3 | 45 | Allen bolt M8 x 45 | 2 |
| 3 | 52 | Spring washer ID8.5 | 2 |
| 3 | 55 | Flat washer OD16 x ID8.5 x 1.5 | 2 |
| 4 | 92 | Flat head screw M8 x 15 | 4 |
| 4 | 46 | Allen bolt M8 x 15 | 2 |
| 4 | 44 | Flat washer OD20 x ID8.5 x 1.5 | 2 |
| 4 | 11 / 15 | Stop bracket R / L | 1 each |
| 4 | 59 | Self-tapping screw ST4.2 x 16 | 2 |
| 5 | 50 | Hex head bolt M12 x 160 | 4 |
| 5 | 64 | Pedal bushing | 2 |
| Tool | 99 | Allen key S5 | 1 |
| Tool | 100 | Multi-function wrench S13-14-19 | 1 |

Two figures disagree inside the book. The hardware page prints washer **44 as OD16 x ID8.5 x 1.5**; the parts list (p. 8) and the step 4 page both print it **OD20 x ID8.5 x 1.5** - this card follows the parts list and the step page, two against one. The hardware page and the step 5 page print bushing **64 as Ø18 x Ø13 x 26**; the parts list prints **Ø16 x Ø13 x 26** - unresolved, and it does not change what you do (two bushings, one per pedal). The hardware page also calls 59 "ST4.0 x 16"; the parts list says ST4.2.

The sister ERG180 ships the same seven parts but a different bag (linked card): cross-head screws instead of flat head screws in step 1, no stop brackets, and tools numbered 84 and 63.
