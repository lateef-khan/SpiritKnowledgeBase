1. Remove the screws from the **Magnet Bracket (8)**.
2. Remove the tension cable from the **Tension Knob (30)**.
3. Remove the screw behind the tension knob, then remove the knob.
4. Reassemble in the reverse order.

**Note: "the minimum space between magnets and flywheel is generally set at about 2.5-3 mm."** That gap is what the bracket's position sets on refitting; too close and the magnets touch the rim. The ERG180 service book has no magnet-bracket procedure and no gap figure.
