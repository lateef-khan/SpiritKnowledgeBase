1. Loosen the screws on both sides of the **Belt Pulley assembly (22, 23)** with an open-end wrench.
2. Remove the belt and extract the belt pulley assembly.
3. After reinstalling the assembly, **hand-tighten it clockwise through 3 full rotations, then pull the Meshbelt (37) outward in the clockwise direction** - that pre-loads the volute spring so the handle strap retracts.
4. Reassemble in the reverse order.

Parts 22 (belt pulley) and 23 (drawstring pulley) share one axle; the volute spring (39) sits behind them. The ERG180 book prints a belt tension figure at this step instead of the three turns (linked card).
