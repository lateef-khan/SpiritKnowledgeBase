1. Disconnect the terminals from the console.
2. Rotate the **Adjust knob (47)** counterclockwise to remove it.
3. Reassemble the console in the reverse order.

The adjust knob is the tilt knob behind the console bracket; on this machine it is what holds the console on, not screws. The four cross-head screws (65) fitted at assembly hold the console to its own bracket. The ERG180's console comes off differently (linked card).
