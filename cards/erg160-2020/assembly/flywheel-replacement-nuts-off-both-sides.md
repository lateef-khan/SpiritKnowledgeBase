1. Remove the nuts from both sides of the flywheel, then take off the flywheel.
2. Reassemble in the reverse order.

That is the whole procedure. The parts list names the flywheel (89, Ø150) on its axle (61, Ø10 x 126) with two M10 x P1.0 hex head nuts (77) and flange nuts (78); the chain covers must be off first. The magnet-to-flywheel gap after refitting is on the magnet bracket card.
