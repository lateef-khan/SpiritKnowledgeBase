The page numbers its steps 4 to 6, continuing from the slide rail page:

4. Remove the four screws from the **seat Fixed Plate (6)**.
5. Remove the **Seat Roller (60)** using an open-end wrench and a hex key - a bolt with a nut, so one tool each side.
6. Follow the above steps to reinstall.

The parts list has three rollers (60, Ø40 x Ø33 x 94) on bushings (56) with six spacers (68); the four seat screws are M6 x 15 Allen bolts (53). Sliding the seat off the rail is the slide rail page's step 3.1.
