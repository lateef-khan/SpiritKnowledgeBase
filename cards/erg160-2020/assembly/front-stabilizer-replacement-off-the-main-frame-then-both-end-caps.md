1. Remove the **Front Stabilizer (3)** from the **Main Frame (1)**, then detach both end caps (31 L, 32 R) from the stabilizer.
2. Reassemble in the reverse order.

The stabilizer is held by the two M8 x 45 Allen bolts of assembly step 3; the book does not repeat the size. The ERG180's page adds the floor mat and the moving wheel (linked card).
