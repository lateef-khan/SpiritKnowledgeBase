1. Attach the Safety Key to enable the display (if not already on).
2. Press the **Start/Stop** key to begin belt movement. Adjust the speed with the speed ▲/▼ keys (console or handgrip), or press one of the **Speed Keys 1 – 12**.
3. To adjust the deck incline, press the incline ▲/▼ key (console or handgrip), or press one of the **Incline Keys 0 – 15**.
4. To stop, press the Start/Stop key or pull out the Safety Key — the book encourages the Start/Stop key.

**Incline.** It may be adjusted any time after belt movement; press and hold the incline ▲/▼ keys on the left side (console or handgrip), or select a key 0 through 15 on the left of the console. The display indicates the incline position as adjustments are made.

**What is on the console.** "1 – 12" and "0 – 15" are the end values, not a count: the pictured face has ten speed keys reading 12, 10, 8, 7, 6 and 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, and ten incline keys reading 15, 12, 10, 8, 6 and 4, 3, 2, 1, 0. The console badge reads "0-12 mph speed range" and "0-15% incline range".

**A contradiction with the service manual.** The shared TRX3500/TRX4500 service manual says the Up key's maximum incline position is 12 and calibrates to a maximum incline of 12; this owner's manual has a 15 key and calibrates to 15 ([calibration card](calibration-enter-and-speed-up-wheel-60-max-12-mph-incline-15.md)). The service manual never names the TRX4500 (GT90D-NT024) on those pages, so it may simply describe the TRX3500.
