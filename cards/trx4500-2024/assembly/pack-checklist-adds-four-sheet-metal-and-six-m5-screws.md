| Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| #80 | Ø8 x 1.5T Split Washer | 4 |
| #99 | 5/16" x 1/2" Hex Head Bolt | 8 |
| #100 | Ø5/16" x Ø18 x 1.5T Flat Washer | 8 |
| #125 | 5/16" x 3/4" Bolt, Button Head | 8 |
| #90 | 13L Wrench | 1 |
| #102 | Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver | 1 |
| #103 | M6 L Allen Wrench | 1 |
| #74 | 3.5 x 12 mm Sheet Metal Screw | 4 |
| #126 | M5 x 15 mm Phillips Head Screw | 6 |

The first seven rows are the TRX2500/TRX3500 kit; the two screw rows are what this machine adds, for
its handlebar covers (step 5) and frame base covers (step 6). The TRX5500 kit has the same two screw
rows but its #99 bolt is 3/4" long.

The PDF's text layer also carries the string "BoltHex Head Bolt (8pcs)" under #125, which is not
printed on this page (the printed label is "Bolt, Button Head").
