**Two drawings.** The main parts indication (p. 3) is a labelled photograph with thirteen numbers:

| No. | Name | No. | Name |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Console | 8 | Pedal |
| 2 | Mesh Belt Wheel | 9 | Seat |
| 3 | Pedal Strap | 10 | Slide Rail |
| 4 | Impeller | 11 | Stopper |
| 5 | Tank | 12 | End Cap |
| 6 | Front Stabilizer | 13 | Rear Stabilizer |
| 7 | Handlebar | | |

The console appearance drawing (p. 4, under "Electronic System") labels the **Tablet Holder**, the **LCD Window**
and the **Program Buttons** row - RESET, ENTER, START/STOP, UP, DOWN and RECOVERY - the same picture as the owner's
manual's console overview.

The owner's manual letters the machine A Tablet Rack, B LCD Window, C Handle, D Water Tank, E Front Stabilizer,
F Pedals, G Seat, H Slide Rail, I Rear Stabilizer, and calls the seat the *Saddle* and the rail the *Aluminum
Rail* in its assembly steps; the service manual's parts list uses letters for groups (A frame, B rear stabilizer,
C sliding track, D front stabilizer, E drum pump, F paddle, G saddle, H console, J hardware, K adaptor, L
packaging, M handlebar, N wires). No dimension or weight is on either drawing. The parts list and exploded view
are the parts section's; the console wiring the same book describes is
`erg800w-2023-specs-console-wiring-a-2-pin-dc-power-cable-and-a-4-pin-sensor-cable-through-the-console-tube`.
