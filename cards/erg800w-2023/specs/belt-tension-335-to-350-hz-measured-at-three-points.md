**335 to 350 Hz.** Twice in the service manual, after a belt or idler-bearing replacement: "Use a belt tension
meter to measure the tension at three points on the belt. Turn the Screw M6 x 30 mm (A42) clockwise to tighten or
counterclockwise to loosen. Adjust the tension to 335~350 Hz. After adjusting the belt tension, remember to fully
tighten the Nut M6 (A43)."

The screw (A42) presses the **Pressing Pipe Hanger** (A23) - the idler arm carrying two 6000ZZ bearings (A28) -
against the belt; the nut (A43) locks it. Before adjustment the procedure loosens that nut with a #10 open-end
wrench and the idler's M8 x 25 screw and nut (A25, A26) with two #13 wrenches to take the pressure off the belt.
The belt itself is part A57, **190J8**, running between the belt pulley (A52, 32 x 24 on A56) and the drive; the
strap guiding wheel assembly (A33 to A36) must go back with its short side underneath the belt.

The ERG180 book prints its figure as "200 to 230" with no unit
(`erg180-2023-specs-belt-tension-200-to-230-with-no-unit-printed`); the Sole SR500 and SR550 print "70~80BLS"
(`sr500-2016-drive-belt-tension`). Neither is this machine's figure. The replacement steps around the figure are
the assembly section's.
