**The owner's manual prints the drawing; the service manual prints the drawing and the list.** The ERG800W owner's manual's **EXPLODED VIEW DIAGRAM** (PDF p. 56, printed 55) carries lettered callouts - `A13*2`, `J3*20`, `G5*6` - where the `*n` is the quantity, and an *Only for Packaging* tag on the packaging tube (L1). The book prints no list under it: the *Parts List* heading on its Parts Included page (PDF p. 12, printed 11) is the carton checklist, lettered A to K on a different scheme. The ERG800W_R80 service manual (V1.0, 2025) prints the same drawing (PDF p. 5) and a three-page **Parts List** (PDF pp. 6-8) headed *PART / DESCRIPTION / QTY*, in two columns per page.

**How the codes are grouped.** **A** is the frame and drive - A1 to A79 with gaps (there is no A7, A8, A15, A21 or A30) and split rows A18-1, A59-1/A59-2 (the two tank bushings, NOK and TPO) and A75-1/A75-2; **B** the rear stabilizer, **C** the sliding track, **D** the front stabilizer, **E** the drum pump, **F** the paddle (the pedal base and its straps), **G** the saddle and its six wheels, **H** the console and its stand, **J** the assembly hardware, **K** the adaptor, **L** the packaging and stickers, **M** the handlebar, **N** the wires. The console procedure (PDF p. 9) cites a **Sensor Cable (H1)** that has no row in the list.

**What the list tells a service call.** The console is H; the belt A57, **190J8**, on belt pulley A56 (32x24) and belt wheel A52; the water tank A64 with its front and rear covers A58 and A67, cap A66, plug A65, three propellers A74 and two O rings A79 (Ф177.17x5.33T); the bearings A11 and A28 (6000ZZ), A35 (608ZZ), A44 (6201ZZ), A45 (6202ZZ), A61 (6005ZZ), G4 (608ZZ); the spring box A46; the sensor wire N5 and the DC line N2; the adaptor K. No tool is a row - the procedures name the wrench sizes instead (`xterra-rower-specs-parts-service-manuals-print-no-tool-list-the-steps-name-the-wrenches`).

The ERG550W list is a numbered one (`erg550w-2023-specs-parts-list-items-1-to-115-and-a-hardware-package`); a code from this list means nothing there.
