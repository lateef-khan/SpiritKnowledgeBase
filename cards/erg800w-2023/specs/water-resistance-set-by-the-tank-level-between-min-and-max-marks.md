**Fill between Min and Max, and that is the resistance.** "Follow the water tank level label to fill the water
between the Min and Max Level. This will protect the product, setup the standard load, and ensure that the data on
the console displays correctly." The label on the tank (service manual part L6, "water level sticker") is a bar
gauge with Max at the top and Min at the bottom and no numbers between; the book prints no litre figure and no
count of levels, and the console's data table has no level window.

There is no knob, lever or console setting for the load. The electrical-safety page does say "Understand that
changes in resistance do not occur immediately. Set your desired work level on the computer console and release the
adjustment key. The computer will obey the command gradually" - that is treadmill and bike boilerplate carried into
this book, and nothing on this machine answers to it.

The tank is filled and drained with the syphon drum pump through the rear cover, filling cover and plug, using
"clean and filtered water" or distilled water where the supply is poor, changed every half year; the maintenance
section holds those steps. Also on the same page: do not hit the tank, do not let it touch the floor, do not put
fingers in it (the safety section). The other Xterra water rowers print six numbered marks
(`xterra-ergw-specs-water-resistance-set-by-the-tank-level-on-a-six-mark-gauge`) or a knob
(`erg750w-2025-specs-resistance-knob-on-the-tank-with-six-levels-shown-on-the-console`).
