**Item 12 of the safety list reads `Connect this appliance to a properly grounded outlet only. See
Grounding Instructions.` - and the book prints none.**

The contents page runs *Product Label 4, Warning, Compliances, and Notices 5, Safety Instructions 6,
Electrical Safety 8, Water Tank Instructions 9* and then *Parts and Assembly*. There is no Grounding
Instructions heading, the Electrical Safety page (`erg800w-2023-safety-electrical-safety-page`) says
nothing about grounding, and the word *ground* occurs elsewhere only as the floor (`Don't let the
water tank touch the ground`). The treadmill books that share this list do print the page - a nominal
120-volt or 110-volt circuit with a three-prong plug
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-grounding-nominal-120-volt-circuit`, `xterra-trx-safety-grounding-nominal-110-volt-circuit`)
- and the cross-reference was carried over with the list, as it was on the SB240 and SB600
(`xterra-sb-safety-grounding-instructions-referenced-but-not-printed`).

How it was checked: the text of the owner's manual and the service manual, including the OCR
supplements, searched for `ground` (owner's manual hits: item 12 and the two floor sentences; service
manual: none) and `volt` (hits: the ten-percent-variation sentence only); every page heading listed
from the contents page.

**What a customer can be told**: the rower runs from an external adaptor with no printed rating
(`xterra-erg-safety-external-power-supply-and-power-jack`); the only outlet rules are item 12's
*properly grounded outlet* and the Electrical Safety page's advice to avoid AFCI/GFCI breakers if
possible and to give the unit its own circuit. The ERG220 and ERG400 print item 12 without the
cross-reference (`xterra-erg-safety-instructions-list-13-item-plug-in-list`).
