**The only rower book with a clearance figure.** A diagram on the second safety page shows the rower
inside a TRAINING AREA of **85 inches by 21 inches**, with a FREE AREA of **23 inches** on all four
sides, and says:

> The free area shall be not less than 23 inches greater than the training area in the directions
> from which the equipment is accessed. The free area must also include the area for emergency
> dismount. Where the rower is positioned adjacent to each other the free area may be shared.

| | Figure |
|---|---|
| Training area (the machine and its stroke) | 85 in long by 21 in wide |
| Free area beyond it | 23 in, on the sides the rower is reached from - the drawing gives it on all four |
| Total floor, from the drawing | about 131 in by 67 in |
| Two rowers side by side | the 23 in between them may be shared |

**Package, working and storage dimensions are in the text layer of this page and not printed on it**
(`Working Dimension: 216 cm (L) x 55 cm (W) x 120 cm (H)` and two more lines); the render shows the
diagram and the sentence only. 216 cm is 85 in, so the ghost line agrees with the drawing, but it is
not on the page and this card does not rely on it.

The ten other rower books print no figure at all - four say *ample free space*
(`xterra-rower-safety-no-clearance-figure-printed`). The Spirit water rowers say 2 ft all round
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-two-feet-of-clearance`), the Xterra treadmills 20 in and 6 ft
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-location-20-in-front-and-sides-6-ft-behind`) - different machines, different figures.
