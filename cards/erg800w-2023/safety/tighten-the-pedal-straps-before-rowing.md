**Two sentences, on two pages:**

> Tighten the pedal straps when you are in motion. (the pedal-adjustment figure)

> Sit on the saddle. Adjust the paddle to fit your feet. Fasten your feet on each pedal by straps well.
> Grab the handle by two hands and then follow below rowing tips. (How to Row)

The first is printed after the pedal-height instruction - `pull up on the two buttons on the pedal at
the same time. Move the pedal up or down to the desired position. Release the buttons to lock the
pedal in position. There are 7 positions` - so the order the book gives is: set the pedal height,
strap in, then row with the straps tight. *Paddle* in the second sentence is the pedal.

It is the only rower book in the range that tells the user to row strapped in; the other ten books
print how to loosen and tighten the hook-and-loop strap and nothing about using it. The Spirit water
rowers make it a boxed rule (`spirit-water-rower-safety-feet-in-straps-and-footplate-locked`) - a Spirit
card. The rowing stroke itself (catch, drive, finish, recovery) is a programs or console matter.
