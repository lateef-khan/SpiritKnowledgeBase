**The ERG800W is the only rower book with a tank warning page in its safety chapter.** Printed
page 9, under WATER TANK & STORAGE WARNING, beside a crossed-out drawing of the rower tipped onto its
tank side:

| # | Warning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Be careful not to hit the water tank during assembly and exercise. |
| 2 | Be careful not to let the water tank touch the floor. The tank might be damaged. |
| 3 | Do not insert fingers into the tank! |
| 4 | Keep unsupervised children away from the equipment. |
| 5 | Injuries to health may result from incorrect or excessive training. |
| 6 | Please use clean and filtered water to fill the water tank. |
| 7 | Where water quality is known to be poor, we recommend the use of distilled water. |
| 8 | Please change the water each half year to protect the product. |

Under it, RECOMMENDED WATER LEVEL: `Follow the water tank level label to fill the water between the
Min and Max Level. This will protect the product, setup the standard load, and ensure that the data
on the console displays correctly.` - the level itself, filling with the drum pump and the
half-yearly change are maintenance and assembly matters.

The same two tank rules are repeated where the book has you handle the frame: in assembly,
`WARNING! Don't take the water tank as a force point to stand up the main frame. WARNING! Don't let
the water tank touch the ground.`; in transport, `Don't tilt the rower left/right so that only one
wheel touches the floor during the transportation. The water tank will be damaged if the rower falls
left/right.` (`xterra-rower-safety-vertical-storage-hazards-seat-slides-down-and-head-near-the-rear-stabilizer`).

**Read the page, not the extraction.** The text layer of this page also carries a second copy of the
eight items and three sentences that are not printed anywhere on it - `WARNING! Heart rate monitoring
systems may be inaccurate. Over exercising may result in serious injury or death. If you feel faint
stop exercising immediately.` - left behind by the authoring file. The 300 dpi render shows the eight
items and the level paragraph only; this card follows the render.

The other four water rowers print their tank rules on the Fill and Empty page - tap water only,
no chlorine, purification tablets, not for drinking - which is a maintenance card; none of them prints
*no fingers in the tank* or *never let the tank touch the floor*. The Spirit water rowers print three
boxed tank warnings for their own machines (`spirit-water-rower-safety-tank-warnings`).
