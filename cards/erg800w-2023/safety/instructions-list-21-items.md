**The only rower book that prints the plug-in twenty-one-item list** - the same list, word for word, as
the new-layout treadmills and recumbent bikes (`xterra-treadmill-safety-instructions-list-21-items`,
`xterra-sb-safety-instructions-list-21-items`); only item 21 is the rower's own figure, and item 12
points to a page the book does not have.

> When using an electrical appliance, basic precautions should always be followed, including the
> following: Read all instructions before using this appliance.
> **! DANGER - To reduce the risk of electric shock:** Always unplug this appliance from the
> electrical outlet immediately after using and before cleaning.
> **! WARNING - To reduce the risk of burns, fire electric shock, or injury to persons:**

| # | Instruction |
|---|---|
| 1 | An appliance should never be left unattended when plugged in. Unplug from outlet when not in use, and before putting on or taking off parts. |
| 2 | Do not operate under blanket or pillow. Excessive heating can occur and cause fire, electric shock, or injury to persons. |
| 3 | This exercise equipment is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge. |

| 7 | Keep the cord away from heated surfaces. |

A boxed note follows: `Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and
power requirements of your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to
your machine.`

The cord, plug and outlet items are real on this rower: it runs from a plug-in adaptor
(`xterra-erg-safety-external-power-supply-and-power-jack`). The list has no rower-specific item -
nothing about the seat, the rail, the handle or the tank; those are on the next three pages: the free
area diagram (`erg800w-2023-safety-free-area-23-inches-around-an-85-by-21-inch-training-area`), the
Electrical Safety page (`erg800w-2023-safety-electrical-safety-page`) and the Water Tank and Storage
Warning (`erg800w-2023-safety-water-tank-and-storage-warning-8-items`). The Proposition 65 warning is
a sticker on the Product Labels page (`xterra-rower-safety-product-labels-page`), and the safety
warning sticker adds the under-13 rule the list lacks
(`xterra-rower-safety-warning-sticker-bilingual-children-under-13`).

The other ten rower books print shorter lists without the cord items - eight of them are battery
consoles (`xterra-rower-safety-instructions-list-free-space-and-tighten-bolts-with-11-fitness-bullets`,
`xterra-erg-safety-instructions-list-13-item-plug-in-list`). The Spirit CRW800 prints its own list
(`crw800-2024-safety-instructions-list`).
