**This is the 330 lb figure**, printed once, as item 21 of the new-layout SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list:

> 21. User Weight Limit: 330 lbs.

followed by the boxed note `Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions
and power requirements of your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage
to your machine.` The book has no specification table; the only other figures near it - package,
working and storage dimensions in centimetres at the top of the free-area page - are in the text
layer and not printed on the page (`erg800w-2023-safety-free-area-23-inches-around-an-85-by-21-inch-training-area`).

330 is unique to this book: the other water rowers print 300 (ERG550W, ERG600W:
`xterra-rower-safety-user-weight-limit-300-lbs`) or 350 (ERG650W, ERG750W:
`xterra-ergw-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lbs`). The whole list is on
`erg800w-2023-safety-instructions-list-21-items`.

| Figure | Printed on | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 250 lb | ERG160 | `Weight Limit: 250 lb`, the last Fitness Equipment bullet |
| 300 lb | ERG180, ERG550W | `User Weight Limit: 300 LB`, the last Fitness Equipment bullet |
| 300 lb | ERG600W | `User Weight Limit: 300 LBS`, the last Fitness Equipment bullet |
| 300 lb | ERG220 | `Weight Limit: 300 lbs`, the last Fitness Equipment bullet |
| 330 lb | ERG800W | `21. User Weight Limit: 330 lbs.`, item 21 of the new-layout list |
| 350 lb | ERG650W | `User Weight Limit: 350 LBS`, the last Fitness Equipment bullet |
| 350 lb | ERG750W | `16. User Weight Limit: 350 lb.`, item 16 of the new-layout list |
| none | ERG400, ERG500, ERG700 | no weight limit anywhere in the book |
