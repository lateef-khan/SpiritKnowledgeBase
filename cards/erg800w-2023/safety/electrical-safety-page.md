**The only rower book with an electrical page, and it is the treadmill's page word for word**
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-electrical-information-page`), as the new-layout recumbents also print it
(`xterra-sb-safety-electrical-safety-page`). Everything on it is a rule the customer can follow; nothing
on it names a voltage, an outlet or a plug for the rower.

| Paragraph | Rule | Card |
|---|---|---|
| WARNING! Route the power cord away from any moving part of the unit **including the elevation mechanism and transport wheels.** | keep the adaptor lead clear of moving parts - the rower has transport wheels on the front stabilizer and no elevation mechanism | - |
| NEVER remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power. If voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more, the performance of your unit may be affected. Such conditions are not covered under your warranty... | covers off only unplugged; a 10% supply variation is for the power company or an electrician, not warranty | `xterra-treadmill-safety-disconnect-power-before-cleaning-or-service` |
| NEVER expose this unit to rain or moisture... The temperature specification is 40 degrees C, and humidity is 95%, non-condensing | 40 degrees C maximum, 95% non-condensing, never outdoors or by a pool - on a machine with an open water tank | `xterra-treadmill-safety-temperature-40-c-humidity-95-percent` |
| Circuit breakers: Avoid AFCI/GFCI circuit breakers if possible... Brands we have tested are: Eaton (Cutler Hammer Series), Leviton (Smart lock pro) and Schneider Electric (Canadian home series). | avoid AFCI/GFCI if possible; three tested brands; the unit should be the only device on the circuit | `xterra-treadmill-safety-avoid-afci-gfci-breakers-tested-brands` |
| NEVER operate this unit without reading and completely understanding the results of any operational change you request from the computer. Understand that changes in resistance do not occur immediately... The computer will obey the command gradually. | resistance ramps; do not repeat a key press - on a water rower the resistance is the water level and a knob, not a computer command | - |
| NEVER use your unit during an electrical storm... Unplug the unit during an electrical storm as a precaution. | never in a storm; unplug it | `xterra-treadmill-safety-use-during-an-electrical-storm` |
| Use caution while participating in other activities **while walking on your unit**; such as watching television, reading, etc. | distractions can cost you your balance | `xterra-safety-distractions-while-exercising` |
| Do not use excessive pressure on console control keys. They are precision set to function properly with little finger pressure. | light touch on the keys | `xterra-treadmill-safety-console-key-pressure` |

**The copy errors, so a reader is not misled:** *elevation mechanism*, *walking on your unit* and the
breaker paragraph's `high inrush currents of the unit drive electronics and motor` are the treadmill's
- the rower has no motor; the tested-brand list was not re-tested on a rower. This card follows the
page as printed and flags the three lines rather than dropping them.

The page never names the adaptor or its rating; the only power facts in the book are *Adaptor* in the
parts list and one assembly drawing (`xterra-erg-safety-external-power-supply-and-power-jack`). Item 12
of the safety list points to a *Grounding Instructions* page that is not printed
(`erg800w-2023-safety-grounding-instructions-referenced-but-not-printed`). The Spirit CT900 prints the
same page (`ct900-electrical-safety`); that card covers Spirit machines only.
