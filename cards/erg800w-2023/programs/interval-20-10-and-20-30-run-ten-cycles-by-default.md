**This console's second fixed interval is 20/30 - 20 seconds of training and 30 seconds of rest -
not the 10/20 interval of the magnetic rower** (see `not_to_be_confused_with`).

Both programs set one value, the number of **CYCLES**: **default 10, each step 1, adjustable 01 ~ 99**.

1. In Standby Mode press UP/DOWN until the training mode turns into **INT 20/10 PROGRAM** (or
   **INT 20/30 PROGRAM**).
2. Press ENTER, then UP/DOWN to adjust the CYCLE to your desired training cycle.
3. Press ENTER, then START to start the training.
4. Each CYCLE includes **20-second-train and 10-second-rest** (INT 20/10) or **20-second-train and
   30-second-rest** (INT 20/30).
5. The CYCLE counts down during the training.

The cycle count is the only setting; the work and rest lengths are fixed. For adjustable work and
rest lengths use INTERVAL CUST - see `see_also`.

**Not the air bike's Tabata**: the Xterra AIR650 and the Spirit air bike run eight fixed 20/10 or
10/20 intervals with no cycle setting.
