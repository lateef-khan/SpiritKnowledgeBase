**Chest strap required, sold separately.** The console takes a 5.3 kHz or Bluetooth chest belt; a
target heart rate is set in beats, not from your age.

1. In Standby Mode press UP/DOWN until the training mode turns into **HR PROGRAM**.
2. Press ENTER, then UP/DOWN to adjust the HR to your desired training heart rate. **Default 130,
   each step 5, adjustable range 100 ~ 180.**
3. Press ENTER, then UP/DOWN to adjust your desired HR training interval (the flow diagram calls it
   **TARGET ZONE**). **Default 30, each step 5, adjustable range 20 ~ 50.**
4. Press ENTER, then START to start the training.
5. During the training the console shows:

| Your heart rate | Console shows |
|---|---|
| lower than the HR you set | **SPEED UP** |
| higher than the HR you set | **SLOW DOWN** |
| "higher than HR interval for 2 minutes" | **GOOD**, "and then stop training for 30 seconds" |

The last row is quoted as printed; the book does not say what "higher than HR interval" means
beyond the zone width you set in step 3, nor whether the 30-second stop is a pause or a rest. **The
page also misprints step 5's second line as "If HR is higher than the SPM you set" - HR is meant;
the SPM program is a separate program.**

The console adjusts nothing on a water rower - the resistance is the tank level - so the program
coaches with the words above rather than changing the load. The Heart Rate Exertion page in the
same book says the machine "will automatically adjust speed or incline" during heart rate programs;
that is treadmill boilerplate and does not describe this rower.
