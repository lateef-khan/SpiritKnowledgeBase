1. In Standby Mode press UP/DOWN until the training mode turns into **SPM PROGRAM**.
2. Press ENTER, then UP/DOWN to adjust the SPM to your desired training strokes in 1 minute.
   **Default 30, each step 1, adjustable range 10 ~ 99.**
3. Press ENTER, then START to start the training.
4. During the training the console coaches your rate against the target:

| Your SPM against the SPM you set | Console shows |
|---|---|
| lower | **FASTER** |
| higher | **SLOWER** |
| equal | **GREAT** |

The program sets no time, distance or cycle count - it runs until you stop it. The console's data
table gives the SPM display range as 0 ~ 99.

No other rower book in this range prints a stroke-rate program; the magnetic rowers show SPM as a
readout only.
