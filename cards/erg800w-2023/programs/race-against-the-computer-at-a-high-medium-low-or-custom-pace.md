1. In Standby Mode press UP/DOWN until the training mode turns into **RACE PROGRAM**.
2. Press ENTER, then UP/DOWN to adjust the computer's rowing pace:

| Pace | Computer's TIME/500M |
|---|---|
| HIGH | **02:15** |
| MEDIUM | **03:00** |
| LOW | **03:30** |
| CUSTOM | set by yourself - **default 3:00, each step 00:01, adjustable 01:00 ~ 09:55** |

3. Press ENTER, then UP/DOWN to adjust the distance of the race in METERS. **Default 500 METERS,
   each step 100 METERS, adjustable range 100 ~ 9900 Meters.**
4. Press ENTER, then START/STOP to start the training. **You control the Y ship. The computer
   controls the C ship.**
5. When the race ends the console shows **YOU WIN** if you finish the race first; otherwise it
   shows **YOU LOSE**.

The flow diagram reads *SELECT RACE PROGRAM - SELECT HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW / CUSTOM - (SELECT CUSTOM
TIME/500M TIME) - SELECT TARGET METERS - PRESS ENTER - PRESS START/STOP*.

**The ERG550W, ERG600W and ERG650W race differently** - fifteen fixed paces from 8:00 to 1:00 per
500 m and a PC WIN / USER WIN result; see `not_to_be_confused_with`.
