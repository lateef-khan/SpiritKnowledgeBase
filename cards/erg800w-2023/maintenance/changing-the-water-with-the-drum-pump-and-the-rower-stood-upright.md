**This is the ERG800W's drum pump. The ERG550W, ERG600W, ERG650W and ERG750W use a squeeze siphon and a funnel
with the rower lying flat** (`xterra-ergw-maintenance-changing-the-water-siphon-pump-out-then-funnel-in`).

The book files both procedures under Assembly (Figures 13 and 14), but the half-yearly water change the
maintenance table asks for (`erg800w-2023-maintenance-clean-filtered-water-changed-every-half-year-no-tablet-named`)
is the same two jobs done in the other order: drain, then fill.

## Draining (Figure 14)

1. **Stand the rower upright**, as for storage
   (`erg800w-2023-maintenance-storing-the-rower-standing-vertically-on-a-level-floor-with-a-thin-cloth`).
2. Put one water pipe (E2) into the tank so that it can draw the water - **make sure it touches the bottom of
   the tank.** Put the other pipe in the bucket.
3. **Hold tight and release the drum pump (E) at least 5 times.** The water then drains from the tank into the
   bucket by siphon.

The service manual drains the tank the same way before the tank comes off - open the Water Tank Cap Cover (A71),
unscrew the Water Tank Cap (A66), remove the Plug (A65), then use the Drum Pump (E) - which names the three
parts the owner's manual calls the rear cover, filling cover and tank plug.

## Filling (Figure 13)

1. Remove the water tank rear cover, the filling cover and the tank plug.
2. Check the **water flow direction** on the pump - the arrow mark on the pump body - and put the side the water
   comes **out** of inside the tank; put the other side under the water in the bucket.
3. Hold tight and release the drum pump (E) at least 5 times; the water is drawn from the bucket by siphon.
4. **Stop when the level reaches the recommended range on the water reference sticker on the tank** - between
   the Min and Max marks.
5. Put the tank plug, filling cover and rear cover back.

The pump itself is assembled once (Figure 11): the plastic retaining nuts (E1) are wound counter-clockwise to
the bottom, the two pipes (E2) pushed on and the nuts turned clockwise to lock them. What to fill with - clean
filtered water, distilled where the supply is poor - and the absence of any tablet in this book are on the
water card linked above.
