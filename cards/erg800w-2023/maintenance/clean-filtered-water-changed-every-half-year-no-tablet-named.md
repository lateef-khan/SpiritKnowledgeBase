**This is the ERG800W rule. It names no purification tablet and no chlorine warning; the ERG550W family says
"tap water only, never chlorine" and the ERG750W says "distilled or pure water only"** - see the two cards
linked as not-to-be-confused.

From the Water Tank & Storage Warning list on printed page 9 (items 1-5 are the tank safety warnings, carded
by the safety section):

6. **"Please use clean and filtered water to fill the water tank."**
7. **"Where water quality is known to be poor, we recommend the use of distilled water."**
8. **"Please change the water each half year to protect the product."**

And under "Recommended Water Level": **"Follow the water tank level label to fill the water between the Min and
Max Level. This will protect the product, setup the standard load, and ensure that the data on the console
displays correctly."** The label is a vertical bar of level marks on the side of the tank with `Max` at the top
and `Min` at the bottom (drawn on the same page); the book prints no litre or gallon figure for either mark,
and the parts list of the service manual calls it `L6 WATER LEVEL STICKER`.

The Regular Maintenance table on printed page 54 repeats the interval as a row - **Water in the Tank / Every
Half Year / "Draw out the water and fill clean water inside"** - so the half-year figure is printed twice and
agrees with itself (`erg800w-2023-maintenance-schedule-weekly-rails-and-frame-half-yearly-water-and-hundred-hour-belt-check`).
How to draw it out is `erg800w-2023-maintenance-changing-the-water-with-the-drum-pump-and-the-rower-stood-upright`.

## What this book does not say

**No tablet, no chlorine, no bleach, no "cloudy".** `tablet` in the owner's manual hits only the tablet
holder on the console, and `chlorine`, `bleach`, `purif`, `algae` and `cloudy` return nothing in the owner's
or the service manual, including their OCR supplements. Do not carry the ERG550W's or ERG750W's six-month
tablet (`xterra-ergw-maintenance-water-purification-tablet-every-six-months`) onto this machine; its book's
answer to water quality is a half-yearly change of filtered water.
