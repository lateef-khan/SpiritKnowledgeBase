**This is the ERG800W, which stands the whole machine on end. The other ERG rowers hinge the slide rail up and
leave the frame on the floor** (`xterra-erg-maintenance-storing-upright-raise-the-slide-rail-to-vertical-and-the-seat-slides-down`).

As printed on page 30:

> You can stand the rower vertically to store it like below. However, before storing it vertically, please
> check if the floor is suitable for you to stand the rower vertically. **If the floor is ragged or
> un-horizontal, you are not recommended to store it at this kind of floor.** It is recommended to put a thin
> cloth between the standing rower and the floor to prevent from scratching the floor.

The drawing shows the rower stood on its **front stabilizer**, tank and rail pointing up, the two transport
wheels and the stabilizer feet on the floor, with the cloth called out under the front foot ("Recommend to put
one thin cloth here"). Two crossed-out drawings beside it show what not to do: a rower stood on a sloping
floor, and one stood on an uneven one.

The same upright position is the one Figure 14 uses to drain the tank with the drum pump
(`erg800w-2023-maintenance-changing-the-water-with-the-drum-pump-and-the-rower-stood-upright`), and the
"Water Tank & Storage Warning" on page 9 adds that the tank must not touch the floor or be hit while the rower
is being handled (the safety section's card).

Moving it is the page before: lift from the rear stabilizer onto the front wheels and **never tilt it left or
right onto one wheel** - the tank is damaged if the rower falls sideways
(`xterra-rower-maintenance-moving-lift-the-rear-until-the-front-transport-wheels-touch-the-floor`). The book
prints no rule about emptying the tank before storage; its water is changed every half year regardless
(`erg800w-2023-maintenance-clean-filtered-water-changed-every-half-year-no-tablet-named`).
