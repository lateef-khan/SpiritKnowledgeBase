The only maintenance table in any Xterra rower book, printed on the Maintenance & Care page under the
post-workout and sanitizing bullets:

| Item | Frequency | Instructions |
|---|---|---|
| Saddle & Aluminum Rail | **Every Week** | Wipe down seat wheels and side rails with lint free cloth. |
| Main Frame | **Every Week** | Wipe down Frame with lint free cloth. |
| Water in the Tank | **Every Half Year** | Draw out the water and fill clean water inside. |
| Rowing Belt | **Every Hundred Hours Usage** | Check if any signs of wear. |

A lint-free cloth, dry, is the whole rail instruction - **no alcohol, no lubricant, no scraper** is named for the
seat wheels or the rail (`xterra-rower-maintenance-no-lubricant-chain-oil-or-rail-lubricant-named-in-any-book`).
The half-year water change is printed a second time on the tank warning page and the drum-pump procedure is
its own card (`erg800w-2023-maintenance-clean-filtered-water-changed-every-half-year-no-tablet-named`,
`erg800w-2023-maintenance-changing-the-water-with-the-drum-pump-and-the-rower-stood-upright`).

## When to stop rowing

The paragraph above the table:

> During the training or during maintenance, **stop using the rower immediately if any signs of excessive
> wearing are present on the belt, pulleys, pedal straps and straps.** These are components most susceptible
> to wear. Do not use until said parts are repaired or replaced.

The hundred-hour belt row is the scheduled version of that rule; the paragraph makes it apply at any time. No
tension figure, no wear limit and no replacement interval is printed - replacement is a service-manual job.

The post-workout wipe-down and the 75% isopropyl sanitizing bullets on the same page are the new-layout
boilerplate carded for the whole brand (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-post-workout-care`,
`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-sanitizing-with-75-percent-isopropyl-alcohol`).

**Same table, different water figure, other brand:** Spirit's water rowers print weekly rails and frame and a
hundred-hour belt check too, but their water row reads "12m to 2 years"
(`spirit-water-rower-maintenance-weekly-rails-and-hundred-hour-belt-check`). Do not quote either machine's
water interval for the other.
