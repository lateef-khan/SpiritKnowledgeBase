"**Standby Mode:** Console will be in Standby Mode when it is powered on or after RESET is pressed for 3 seconds." "**QUICK START:** In Standby Mode, press START/STOP or pull the handle to begin QUICK START."

So a stroke on the handle starts a row with no key press, the way the Spirit water rowers launch ([Spirit twin](../../shared/console/spirit-water-rower-console-quick-start-on-the-first-strokes.md)), and RESET held 3 seconds is the way back to Standby, where UP and DOWN choose the programs (programs cards). **The book prints no sleep timer, no auto-off and no beep at power-on** for this console; power comes from the **adaptor (K)** in the parts list, plugged in at assembly Figure 12, and the electrical-safety page is a safety card. There are no batteries on this rower. Keys: [key card](six-keys-up-down-start-stop-enter-reset-and-recovery-under-a-tablet-holder.md).
