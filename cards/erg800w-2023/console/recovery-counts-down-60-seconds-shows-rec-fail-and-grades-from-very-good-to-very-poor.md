"*Chest Strap required. Chest Strap sold separately.* 1. Under each program, press RECOVERY to detect the Heart Rate Recovery Function. 2. After pressing RECOVERY, you will see a **60 seconds count down**. 3. After the count down, it will show the result. 4. **If the console hasn't received a heart rate signal for 10 seconds, it will turn into REC: FAIL.** 5. If the console hasn't received a heart rate signal for **20 seconds, it will stop.**"

| Result | Fitness level |
|---|---|
| F1 | Very good |
| F2 | Good |
| F3 | Average |
| F4 | Sufficient |
| F5 | Poor |
| F6 | Very poor |

The table is printed as a picture and does not extract; it was read from the render. REC: FAIL is a missing strap signal, not a machine fault. Which belts work is on [the heart-rate card](heart-rate-from-a-5-3-khz-or-bluetooth-chest-belt-not-included.md); the key itself on [the key card](six-keys-up-down-start-stop-enter-reset-and-recovery-under-a-tablet-holder.md). The ERG600W grades outstanding to poor with a 00:60 count-down ([ERG600W card](../../erg600w-2021/console/recovery-counts-down-from-00-60-and-grades-from-outstanding-to-poor.md)) and the ERG500 excellent to poor ([ERG500 card](../../erg500-2018/console/pulse-recovery-scores-from-excellent-to-poor-with-the-chest-belt-only.md)); the SB600 bike shows ER instead of REC: FAIL ([SB600 card](../../sb600-2023/console/recovery-grade-and-the-er-reading.md)).
