"**Heart Rate Source: 5.3 KHZ Chest Belt / Bluetooth Chest Belt (Not included).** Wear the chest belt and then the chest belt will send the heart rate signal. If the console receives the heart rate signal, the heart rate will be shown on the console. Take the above figure as an example. If your heart rate is 120, the console will display 120. ** Chest Belt is sold separately. Please contact the service center to ask if they sell the chest belt suitable for this rower."

So either an analogue 5.3 kHz belt or a Bluetooth belt works; there are no hand-pulse grips. The PULSE range is 40 to 240 ([data card](data-ranges-meters-to-99999-watts-to-9999-and-pulse-40-to-240.md)). The book does not say what the window shows without a signal. Wearing the belt, its CR2032 battery and the interference list are on [the strap card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-chest-strap-not-included-with-a-cr2032-battery.md) and [the erratic card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-erratic-heart-rate-and-the-seven-interference-sources.md); the RECOVERY and TARGET HR functions need the belt ([recovery card](recovery-counts-down-60-seconds-shows-rec-fail-and-grades-from-very-good-to-very-poor.md)). The ERG750W names a Bluetooth monitor only ([ERG750W card](../../erg750w-2025/console/pulse-from-a-bluetooth-heart-rate-monitor-that-stops-scanning-after-90-seconds.md)); the older water rowers an analogue strap only ([ERG550W family card](../../shared/console/xterra-ergw-console-pulse-30-to-240-only-from-a-wireless-chest-strap-sold-separately.md)).
