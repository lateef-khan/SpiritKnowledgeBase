"**Connection to Exercise App.** Open the exercise App in your smart device. Press it to restart the console. **Find the Bluetooth name ERG800 of this console in the App. You can check the Bluetooth name of the console at the back of it.** Connect the console and your smart device. Note: When using KINOMAP fitness App link to find rower communication, **please select FTMS communication for my Rower.** (The images found on page 34 are an iOS mobile phone.)"

"Press it" refers to the RESET key, which the button page defines as "press it to restart the console" ([key card](six-keys-up-down-start-stop-enter-reset-and-recovery-under-a-tablet-holder.md)). The next page's Kinomap screenshots show Equipment management, a Rowing machine of brand FTMS, the found equipment "(FTMS) ERG800W", and Kinomap asking for the brand and model because a generic brand was chosen. The compliance page lists "Bluetooth (with FTMS)" (a specs card). The XTERRA+ app page with iOS 13.1 / Android 8.0 is on [the app card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-xterra-plus-app-ios-13-android-8.md); the ERG180, ERG550W and ERG750W print the step-by-step Kinomap page instead ([Kinomap card](../../shared/console/xterra-rower-console-kinomap-pairing-and-the-console-goes-blank-while-an-app-is-connected.md)). The book does not say whether this console goes blank while an app is connected.
