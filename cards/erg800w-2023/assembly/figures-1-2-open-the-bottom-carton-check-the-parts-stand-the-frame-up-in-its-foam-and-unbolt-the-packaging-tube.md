**Figure 1 - the carton.** Expand the bottom carton (Fig 1-1). Take the polyfoam out (Fig 1-2), collect the parts except the main frame (A) and confirm they match the parts list.

**Figure 2 - the packaging tube.** Stand up the main frame (A) **together with the polyfoam clamping it**. Unscrew the two screws and remove the **Tube for Packaging**; "in the following assembly, you won't need these two screws and packaging tube". Tool: 6 mm Allen key. The service manual lists the tube as L1 with two M8 x 30 screws (L3).

**WARNING! Don't take the water tank as a force point to stand up the main frame. WARNING! Don't let the water tank touch the ground.** The Water Tank warning page adds: do not hit the tank during assembly, and do not insert fingers into it.

The frame stays in its foam through Figure 3; Figure 4 takes the foam off.
