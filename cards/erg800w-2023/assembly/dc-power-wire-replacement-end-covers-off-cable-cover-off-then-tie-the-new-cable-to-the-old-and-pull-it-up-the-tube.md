1. With a Phillips screwdriver remove **two M4 x 12 Screws (J7)** from the **End Cover (A2)**, then **two M4 x 12 Screws (J7) and one M4 x 19 Screw (J8)** from the **End Cover (A3)**.
2. Press the **Console Tube (H2)** slightly down toward the handlebar. Two connectors are visible inside the tube: a **2-pin connector (DC power cable)** and a **4-pin connector (signal sensor cable)**.
3. Remove **two M4 x 12 Screws (A22)** to detach the **DC Power Cable Cover (A20)**.
4. The **DC Power Plug (N2)** is held to the cover by a hex nut: hold the rear of the plug and rotate the cover counterclockwise to separate them.
5. Remove the old DC power cable (N2), then refit the cover and hex nut.
6. **Tie the opposite ends of the new and old power cables together** with string or tape; from the upper tube opening, gently pull the 2-pin cable upward until the old cable is out.
7. Untie, then connect the new 2-pin plug (male) to the 2-pin socket (female) at the lower end of the console tube.
8. Reassemble in reverse.

The DC line is N2, 620 mm, in the parts list; the jack it ends in is where the owner's manual's adaptor (K) plugs in.
