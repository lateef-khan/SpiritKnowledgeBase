1. A 4 mm Allen wrench (S4) unscrews the **two stainless steel M6 x 12 screws (A16)** counterclockwise.
2. Place the new **Handlebar Hanger (A14)** onto the **Frame (A1)** and fasten the two screws clockwise.

The page heading says "Handlebar Seat"; the parts list calls the part the handlebar hanger, and the tank page hooks the belt on it. It is the cradle the handle parks in.
