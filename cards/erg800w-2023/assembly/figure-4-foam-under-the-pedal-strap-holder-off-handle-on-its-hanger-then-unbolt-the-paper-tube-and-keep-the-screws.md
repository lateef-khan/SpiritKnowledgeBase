1. Stand up the main frame (A) together with the polyfoam clamping it. **Put one polyfoam block below the pedal** (Fig 4-1), then remove the polyfoam clamping the main frame.
2. Remove the **strap holder** and put the handle bar on the **handle bar hanger** (Fig 4-2) - the holder is a tie that keeps the belt retracted in the carton (L5 in the service parts list).
3. Unscrew the pre-screwed **screws (J3) and washers (J5)** and take off the **paper tube** (Fig 4-3). **Keep the screws and washers for the next step.** Tool: 6 mm Allen key.

The paper tube stands in for the front stabilizer in the carton; its two M8 x 16 screws and two arc washers (J5) become the front stabilizer's in Figure 5.
