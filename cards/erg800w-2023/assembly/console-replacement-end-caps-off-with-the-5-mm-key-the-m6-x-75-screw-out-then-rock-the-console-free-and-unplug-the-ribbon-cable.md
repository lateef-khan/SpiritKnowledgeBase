1. Using a 5 mm Allen wrench (S5), insert it into both sides of the **End Cap (H5)** and lever outward to remove them.
2. From the left side of the **Steel Tube (H2)**, loosen the **M6 x 75 Screw (H7)** counterclockwise; the **M6 Nylon Nut (J9)** on the opposite side disengages from the hexagonal recess in the **Plastic Housing (H4)**.
3. Remove the M6 x 75 screw. The **Console Bracket (H3)** and the **Console (H)** can now be separated.
4. Hold the console with both hands and gently rock it forward and backward while applying slight upward force. The **Sensor Cable (H1)** becomes visible inside the steel tube; carefully pull it outward, then disconnect the **Ribbon Cable Connector** on the console side from the lower tube cable. **Caution: ensure the sensor cable does not drop into the steel tube.**
5. Install the new console and reassemble in reverse.

The M6 x 75 screw is also the pivot the owner's manual's Figure 10 tilts the console on.
