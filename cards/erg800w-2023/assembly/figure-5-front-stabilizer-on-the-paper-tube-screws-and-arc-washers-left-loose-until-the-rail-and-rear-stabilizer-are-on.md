Use the **screw (J3) and washer (J5)** from the previous step to assemble the **front stabilizer (D)** on the **main frame (A)**. Tool: 6 mm Allen key.

**"However, don't tighten the screws hard at this moment. Please tighten the screws hard after the aluminum rail (C) and the rear stabilizer (B) are assembled on the main frame (A). This is to make sure the foot pad on the front stabilizer touches the ground better."** Figure 8 is where they get tightened, after the rower is laid down.

Two screws and two arc washers (J5, Ø8 x Ø19 x 1.5 in the service parts list) - the only arc washers on the machine, so they belong here, not with the rail.
