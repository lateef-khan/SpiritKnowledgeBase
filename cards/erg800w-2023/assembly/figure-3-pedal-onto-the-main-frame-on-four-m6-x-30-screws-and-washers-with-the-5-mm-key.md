Use the **screws (J1, M6 x 30)** and **washers (J2, M6)** to fasten the **pedal (F)** on the **main frame (A)** - Figs 3-1 and 3-2 show the four screws going down through the pedal base into the frame. Tool: 5 mm Allen key.

The pedal (F) is one base carrying both adjustable foot plates; it goes on while the frame is still upright in its foam, and the next page shows the "Main Frame after Assembled with Pedal". The service manual's parts list calls it PADDLE and counts the screws: J1 x 4, J2 x 4.
