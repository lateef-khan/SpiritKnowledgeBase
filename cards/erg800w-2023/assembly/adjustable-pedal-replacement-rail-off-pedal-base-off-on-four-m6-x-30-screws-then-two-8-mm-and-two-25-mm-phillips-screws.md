1. Stand the unit upright with the rear end of the **Aluminum Rail (C)** facing up. Using a 6 mm Allen wrench (S6), remove **four M8 x 16 Screws (J3)** and **four M8 Washers (J4)**, then lift the rail off upward.
2. Using a 5 mm Allen wrench (S5), remove **four M6 x 30 Screws (J1)** and **four M6 Washers (J2)**; lift the **Pedal Base Assembly (F)** up at an angle to remove it.
3. Flip the pedal base over. With a Phillips screwdriver remove the screws holding the **Adjustable Pedal (F2)** - **two M4 x 8 Screws (F5)** and **two M4 x 25 Screws (F7)**. The **Plastic Mat (F3)** separates from the metal base, freeing the pedal.
4. Flip the base back. Place the removed plastic mat onto the new adjustable pedal, align the holes and confirm the position. Reassemble in reverse.

The rail must come off first because it covers the pedal base's screws. The strap itself is the next page (linked).
