1. A Phillips screwdriver removes the **M4 x 12 (J7)** and **M4 x 19 (J8)** screws securing the **Chain Covers (A4, A5)**.
2. If the tank holds water: open the **Water Tank Cap Cover (A71)**, unscrew the **Water Tank Cap (A66)**, remove the **Plug (A65)** and drain with the **Drum Pump (E)**.
3. Stand the unit upright at 90 degrees, rail up. Remove **two M4 x 12 Screws (A69)** to detach the **Rear Cover of Water Tank (A67)**.
4. A 6 mm Allen wrench (S6) removes the **four M8 x 16 Screws (J3)** and **four M8 Washers (J4)** holding the black steel plate (A63) to the frame - two sets at the top and two at the bottom of the tank. **Tip: loosen all four first, then remove the bottom two before the top two.**
5. When removing the **Water Tank (A64)**, do not lose the white plastic **Cross Axle Connector (A54)** at the bottom - it is covered in grease.
6. Cut the two cable ties on the wiring and disconnect the connectors.
7. Pull out about **70-80 cm of running belt (A51-B)**, tie a slip knot and hook it on the **Handlebar Hanger (A14)** to support the unit while the handlebar comes apart.
8. A 4 mm Allen wrench (S4) loosens the two M10 nuts, then pull out the metal clasp (M8). Locking pliers hold and turn the nut (M9) counterclockwise to free either side and release the running belt.
9. Thread a rope of about **2.0 m** through the running belt loop and tie it, in place of the metal ring.
10. Unhook the belt from the hanger, untie the slip knot, smooth the belt and let it retract slowly into the **Belt Reel Mechanism (A51)**; tape it to the reel at the end.
11. A 6 mm key removes two M6 x 40 screws at the bottom (with the two Ø26 plastic posts), then four sets of M8 x 16 screws (J3) and washers (J4) holding the black steel plates (A31) to the frame. **The entire drive assembly comes out as one unit.**
12. Install the new drive assembly by reversing the steps.

**Notes.** The drive assembly includes the Belt Reel Spring Box (A46, A51-B), Belt Pulley (A52), Belt (A57), Strap Guiding Wheel (A34), Drive Axle (A49), Pressing Wheel Assembly (A23-A29) and two Black Steel Plates (A32). **During reassembly rotate the mechanism only in the direction the running belt is pulled out**; the other way can damage the spring box. The guiding wheel and belt pages both start "first follow steps 1 to 11" of this page.
