"If you would like to adjust the pedal, **pull up on the two buttons on the pedal at the same time**. Move the pedal up or down to the desired position. Release the buttons to lock the pedal in position. **There are 7 positions you can choose.** It is the same to adjust the left pedal. **Tighten the pedal straps when you are in motion.**"

The How to Row page repeats it: "Adjust the paddle to fit your feet. Fasten your feet on each pedal by straps well." The strap itself has no adjustment instruction in this book.
