1. A Phillips screwdriver removes the **M4 x 12 (J7)** and **M4 x 19 (J8)** screws securing the decorative covers (A4, A5); remove the covers.
2. Beside the belt (A57), a #10 open-end wrench fully loosens the **idler nut M6 (A43)**; a 6 mm Allen wrench (S6) loosens the **M6 x 30 screw (A42)** counterclockwise to release the pressure on the **idler arm (A23)**.
3. Two #13 open-end wrenches remove the **M8 nuts (A26)** and **M8 x 25 screws (A25)** on both sides, and the idler assembly (A23, A26-A29) comes off.
4. A #17 open-end wrench removes the **M10 x 30 screw (A29)**; replace the **two bearings (A28, 6000ZZ)**.
5. Reassemble back to step 1; the 6 mm key tightens the M6 x 30 screw (A42) clockwise to press firmly against the idler arm.
6. **Measure the belt tension at three points with a belt tension meter. Tighten the M6 x 30 screw (A42) clockwise to increase, loosen to reduce. Set it between 335 and 350 Hz.**
7. Securely tighten the nut M6 (A43).
8. Complete the assembly in reverse.

Unlike the belt page, this one does not need the tank and drive assembly out - the covers alone give access to the idler.
