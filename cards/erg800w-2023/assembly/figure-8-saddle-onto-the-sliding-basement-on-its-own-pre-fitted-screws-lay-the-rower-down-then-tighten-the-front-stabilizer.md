1. Unscrew the **prescrewed screws (J3) and washers (J4)** from the **saddle (G)**. Use these screws and washers to assemble the saddle on the **sliding basement** on the aluminum rail (C). Tool: 6 mm Allen key. The service manual counts four screws under the saddle.
2. After the saddle is on, **lay down the rower**.
3. **"After you lay down the rower, please fasten the screws on the front stabilizer which you haven't fastened hard in Step 5."**

The frame build ends here; Figures 9 to 14 are the pedal and console adjustments, the pump, the adaptor and the first tank fill (linked cards).
