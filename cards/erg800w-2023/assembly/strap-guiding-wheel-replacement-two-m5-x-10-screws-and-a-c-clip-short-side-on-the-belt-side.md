To replace the guiding wheel assembly (A33-A36) alone, first follow steps 1 to 11 of the Water Tank and Core Drive Components page. Then:

1. With an electric Phillips screwdriver remove **two M5 x 10 Screws (A37)**; the running belt guiding wheel assembly comes off.
2. With C-clip pliers remove one of the **C Clips (A36)** to separate the guiding wheels (A33-A34) from the metal axle (A33).
3. Fit the new guiding wheels by reversing the steps.

**Orientation: the short side of the guiding wheel assembly must be fixed on the same side as the belt (A57)** - the belt page says the same, "the short side must be positioned underneath the belt". The assembly is axle A33, wheel A34, two 608ZZ bearings (A35) and two E6 C-clips (A36).
