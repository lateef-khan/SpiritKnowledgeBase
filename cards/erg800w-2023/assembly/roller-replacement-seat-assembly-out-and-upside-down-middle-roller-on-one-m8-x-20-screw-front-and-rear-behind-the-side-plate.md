Steps 1-3 are the saddle page's: stop blocks and double head screws out at the rear and front, the rear cover (C2) tapped off, the **Sliding Seat Assembly (G-G9)** slid out of the track. Then place it upside down.

**(A) Middle roller (G5):** a 5 mm Allen wrench (S5) holds the **M8 x 20 Screw (G9)**; a #13 open-end wrench turns the **M8 Hex Cap Nut (G8)** counterclockwise, off with its **Ø8 x Ø12 x 2 mm Washer (G7)**. Replace the wheel.

**(B) Front or rear roller (G5):** two #13 open-end wrenches loosen the two **M8 Hex Cap Nuts (G8)**; remove the two washers (G7) and the **Wheel Protection Plate (G2)**. A 6 mm Allen wrench (S6) removes the **M8 x 16 Screws (J3)** and washers (J4) holding the **Saddle Side Plate (G1)**, then the plate. **Before removal, note the direction of the two rollers and the position of the Ø8 x Ø12 x 2 mm washers (G6).** With the side plate off, remove the two washers (G6), the two rollers and one **Axle (G3)**.

Reassemble by reversing. The seat runs on six wheels (G5) on 608ZZ bearings (G4): one middle and two end rollers each side.
