**Tools included:** 6 mm Allen Wrench, 5 mm Allen Wrench, 13/15 mm Wrench. The drawing's TOOL box shows only the two Allen keys (5 m/m, 6 m/m); no step in the book calls for the 13/15 mm wrench.

**Parts included** (letters are the assembly-drawing labels): A Main Frame, B Rear Stabilizer, C Aluminum Rail, D Front Stabilizer, E Water Pump with (E2) Pipe, F Pedal, G Saddle, K Adaptor, Hardware Bag. The checklist prints both "Adaptor" and "Adapter" as separate lines; the drawing has one adaptor (K), the power supply.

**Hardware bag (J)**

| # | Item | Drawn |
|---|---|---|
| J1 | Screw M6 x 30 | 4 |
| J2 | Washer M6 | 4 |
| J3 | Screw M8 x 16 | 6 |
| J4 | Washer M8 | 6 |

J1/J2 fit the pedal (Figure 3); J3/J4 fit the rear stabilizer and the aluminum rail (Figures 6 and 7). The steps also use **screws J3 and washers J5 that arrive pre-screwed on the paper packaging tube** (Figures 4 and 5) and **J3/J4 pre-screwed on the saddle** (Figure 8). J5 is not on this page at all; the service manual's parts list names it: **arc washer Ø8 x Ø19 x 1.5T, 2 pcs**.
