"The console angle is adjustable as shown. After proper position find, fixed position."

The drawing shows three pivots - the console on its tube, the console tube on the frame, and the smart phone (tablet) stand above the console - each with an arc of travel. The book names no knob and no tool; the service manual's console procedure shows the pivot is the M6 x 75 screw (H7) through the steel tube, with a nylon nut in the plastic housing, so tightening that screw is what "fixes" the position.
