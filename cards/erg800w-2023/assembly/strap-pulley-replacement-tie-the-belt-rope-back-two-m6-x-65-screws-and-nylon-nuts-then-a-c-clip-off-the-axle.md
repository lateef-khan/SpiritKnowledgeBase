1. With a Phillips screwdriver remove two M4 x 12 Screws (J7) from the **End Cover (A2)**, then the **End Cover (A3)** by taking out two M4 x 12 Screws (J7) and one M4 x 19 Screw (J8).
2. **Secure the running belt rope to the Support Tube (H2) with a cable tie**, so the belt does not press on the idler pulley.
3. Use a 5 mm Allen wrench (S5) on the left and a 10 mm socket wrench on the right to loosen the **M6 x 65 Screw (A6)** and **M6 Nylon Nut (J9)** counterclockwise. Remove that screw; the idler pulley (A10) still cannot come out. Loosen the other set of M6 x 65 screw and nylon nut the same way; the idler pulley with parts A11-A13 can then be removed.
4. To replace the idler pulley, use C-clip pliers to remove one of the **C-clips (A13)**; the pulley separates from the **Axle Shaft (A12, Ø10 x Ø6.2 x 48.8 mm)**.
5. Install the new idler pulley (A10) with the **#6000Z bearing (A11) pressed in**, and reassemble in reverse.

Two long screws, two nuts, two C-clips: the parts list has five M6 x 65 screws (A6) on the machine, two of them here.
