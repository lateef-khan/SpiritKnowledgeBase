To replace the belt (A57) alone, first follow steps 1 to 11 of the Water Tank and Core Drive Components page. Then:

1. A #10 open-end wrench fully loosens the **Nut M6 (A43)**; a 6 mm Allen wrench (S6) loosens the **M6 x 30 Hex Socket Head Cap Screw (A42)** counterclockwise until the **Pressing Pipe Hanger (A23)** is no longer pressed tight.
2. Two #13 open-end wrenches loosen the **Nut M8 (A26)** and the **M8 x 25 Hex Head Cap Screw (A25)** to release the idler pulley assembly's pressure on the belt.
3. Slide the belt outward and flip it to the bottom side.
4. An electric Phillips screwdriver removes **two M5 x 10 Truss Head Screws (A37)** to detach the **Strap Guiding Wheel Assembly (A33-A36)**, then remove the belt.
5. Fit the new belt (A57) and reassemble back to step 1. **The guiding wheel assembly's short side must sit underneath the belt.**
6. Back at step 1, the 6 mm key tightens the M6 x 30 screw (A42) clockwise to press the pressing pipe hanger firmly.
7. **With a belt tension meter, measure the tension at three points on the belt. Turn the M6 x 30 screw (A42) clockwise to tighten or counterclockwise to loosen. Adjust the tension to 335-350 Hz.**
8. After adjusting the tension, fully tighten the Nut M6 (A43).

The belt is a 190J8 poly-V (A57). The tension figure is repeated, word for word, on the pressing pipe hanger bearing page.
