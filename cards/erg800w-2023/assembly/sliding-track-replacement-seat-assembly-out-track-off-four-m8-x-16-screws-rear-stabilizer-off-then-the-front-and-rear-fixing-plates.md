1. Grip the plastic stop pins (C5) on both sides at the rear of the **Sliding Track (C)**, turn counterclockwise to loosen one side, then pull out the other side's pin and the **threaded rod Ø5.25 x 85 mm (C4)**. Repeat at the front.
2. Gently tap off the rear cover (C2).
3. Slide the **Sliding Seat Assembly (G-G9)** toward the rear end and out.
4. Stand the frame upright, rear end of the track up. A 6 mm Allen wrench (S6) removes the **four M8 x 16 screws (J3)** and washers (J4); lift the track off.
5. Place the track upside down. The 6 mm key removes **two M8 x 16 screws (J3)** and washers (J4), and the **Rear Stabilizer (B)** comes off.
6. Inside the track, remove the front and rear fixing plates: at the front, a Phillips screwdriver removes four M4 x 10 screws (C7) and the internal iron frame (C6); at the rear, the 6 mm key removes two M6 x 10 screws (J6) and the internal iron plate (B1).
7. Install the new track by reversing the sequence.

**The page is headed "Replacing the Handlebar Seat"** - a copy of the previous page's heading; the contents page has no entry for it at all. The four-plus-two screw counts are the ones the owner's manual's Figures 6 and 7 leave unstated.
