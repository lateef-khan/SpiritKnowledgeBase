Use the **screw (J3, M8 x 16) and washer (J4, M8)** to fasten the **rear stabilizer (B)** on the **aluminum rail (C)**. Tool: 6 mm Allen key. **"Be aware of the direction of the rear stabilizer"** - the drawing has its foot tubes pointing away from the rail's open end.

The owner's manual gives no count. The bag holds six J3 and six J4; the service manual removes the rear stabilizer by taking out **two** M8 x 16 screws and washers, and the rail from the frame by taking out **four** - so two here, four in Figure 7.
