**Figure 11 - the pump.** Make sure the plastic retaining nut (E1) is on the water pump (E) and lock it **counterclockwise** to the bottommost position. Connect the two water pipes (E2) to the pump and turn the two plastic nuts (E1) **clockwise** to lock them. **The arrow mark on the pump shows the direction of water flow.**

**Figure 13 - filling.**
1. Remove the water tank rear cover, the filling cover and the tank plug.
2. Check the flow direction on the pump; put the outlet side inside the water tank and the other side beneath the water in your bucket.
3. **Hold tight and release the drum pump at least 5 times** - the syphon then draws water from the bucket.
4. **Stop when the level reaches the recommended range on the water reference sticker** on the tank.
5. Put the tank plug, filling cover and rear cover back.

**Figure 14 - drawing water out.** Stand the rower upright. Place one pipe so it reaches the bottom of the tank and the other in a bucket. Squeeze and release the pump at least 5 times and the syphon empties the tank.

**Water rules (the warning page):** fill between the **Min and Max** marks on the tank label - that protects the tank, sets the standard load and keeps the console data right; use **clean, filtered water, distilled where the supply is poor**; change the water every half year (the maintenance card holds the schedule).
