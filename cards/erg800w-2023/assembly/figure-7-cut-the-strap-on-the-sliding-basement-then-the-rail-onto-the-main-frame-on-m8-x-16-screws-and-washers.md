1. **Cut the strap holding the sliding basement on the aluminum track off.** "Notice that the sliding basement will slide after the strap is cut off. Please be careful to not pinch yourself." The sliding basement is the seat carriage, shipped tied to the rail.
2. Use the **screw (J3, M8 x 16) and washer (J4, M8)** to assemble the **aluminum rail (C)** on the main frame. Tool: 6 mm Allen key.

The rail goes on with the frame still standing on its front - four screws, by the service manual's count ("four M8 x 16 Screws (J3) along with four M8 Washers (J4)" hold the rail to the frame).
