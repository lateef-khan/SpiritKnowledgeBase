1. Stand the unit upright, rail end up; a 6 mm Allen wrench (S6) removes **four M8 x 16 Screws (J3)** and **four M8 Washers (J4)**, and the **Aluminum Rail (C)** lifts off.
2. A 5 mm Allen wrench (S5) removes **four M6 x 30 Screws (J1)** and **four M6 Washers (J2)**; tilt and lift the **Pedal Base Assembly (F)** off.
3. Flip the base over; a Phillips screwdriver removes the pedal screws on the side to be replaced - **two M4 x 8 (F5)** and **two M4 x 25 (F7)** - and the **Plastic Mat (F3)** and **Adjustable Pedal (F2)** come off the metal base.
4. Flip the base to the front; remove **two M5 x 10 Screws (F9)** to detach the rear **Fixing Plate (F8)**.
5. Replace the **Pedal Strap (F4)** and secure it properly.
6. After confirming proper function, reassemble in reverse.

**The book's contents page calls these two pages "Replacing the Chain Covers".** They are the strap procedure - the chain covers (A4, A5) come off with the J7/J8 screws at the start of the sensor, tank and bearing pages and have no page of their own.
