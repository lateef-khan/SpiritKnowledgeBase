1. Hold the **Sliding Track (C)** firmly at the rear. Twist the **Stop Block Ø14 x 22 mm (C5)** on the left and right counterclockwise to loosen one side, then pull out the stop block and the **Double Head Screw Ø5.25 x 85 mm (C4)** from the opposite side. Repeat for the two stop blocks and one double head screw at the front of the track.
2. Lightly tap off the **Rear Cover of Sliding Track (C2)**. **Note the "recessed direction under the saddle" before the next step.**
3. Slide the **Sliding Seat Assembly (G-G9)** toward the rear end of the track and off.
4. Place the seat assembly upside down; a 6 mm Allen wrench (S6) removes all **four M8 x 16 Screws (J3)** and **M8 Washers (J4)** to detach the **Saddle (G)**.
5. Fit the new saddle and reverse the steps. **Make sure the seat assembly goes back into the track in the direction noted in step 2.**

The stop blocks and double head screws are the seat's end stops - four blocks on two threaded rods, one pair at each end of the track.
