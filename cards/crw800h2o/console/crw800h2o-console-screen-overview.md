**A portrait LCD with a SPIRIT badge above it, five quick mode keys beside it, and a five-key
control keypad below.**

**The drawing names six areas:** INTERVAL DISPLAY PANEL; QUICK MODE BUTTONS (labelled twice, once for
each column); TIME, DISTANCE, CALORIES, WATTS, SPEED, RPM DISPLAY PANEL; HEART RATE DISPLAY PANEL;
CONTROL KEYPAD; and WATER LEVEL BUTTON.

**Five quick mode keys, each with its own icon:**

| Side | Keys, top to bottom |
|---|---|
| **Left** | **TARGET TIME**, **TARGET DISTANCE**, **TARGET STROKE** |
| **Right** | **TARGET CALORIES**, **TARGET HR KEY** |

*"The Quick Mode buttons will go into the associated mode immediately."*

**The control keypad is five keys around one large round ENTER:** **UP** (top left), **LEVEL**
(bottom left), **ENTER** (centre), **DOWN** (top right), **RESET** (bottom right).

- **UP and DOWN** - *"The arrow buttons allow the user to increase or decrease console values."*
- **ENTER** - *"used to confirm a value as making adjustments to a mode."*
- **RESET** - *"clear a value, to pause your workout, end your workout. Hold for 2 seconds for a
  Total Reset of all values (except Total Strokes)"*
  (`crw800h2o-console-total-reset-two-second-hold`).
- **LEVEL** - *"to communicate the water level in the tank to the console"*
  (`crw800h2o-console-water-level-button-six-steps`).

**There is no Bluetooth on this machine, and it is not a gap in the extraction.** A loose word search
of the whole manual returns **zero** hits for `bluetooth` and **zero** for `usb`; every page was
rendered at 300 dpi and compared against the extraction, and no page hides a wireless section behind
a picture. **The key the CRW900 gives to Bluetooth, this console gives to LEVEL**
(`crw900-2021-console-screen-overview`), and its interval panel carries no Bluetooth symbol. **Do not
promise a CRW800H2O owner the Spirit Fit App upload**; that is the CRW900's feature.

**The heart rate panel on this console has no AGE, 65%, 85% or Max banners** - the CRW900's does
(`spirit-water-rower-console-heart-rate-panel-three-dashes`).

**One call-out on the drawing names windows this console does not have.** The right-hand label reads
**"TIME, DISTANCE, CALORIES, WATTS, SPEED, RPM DISPLAY PANEL"**, but the panel it points at has
**SPM** and **WATTS** windows and **no SPEED window and no RPM window** anywhere on the screen. **It
is a bike call-out left on a rower drawing** - the CRW900 manual prints the same label with the same
error. The stroke-rate reading on this machine is **SPM**.
