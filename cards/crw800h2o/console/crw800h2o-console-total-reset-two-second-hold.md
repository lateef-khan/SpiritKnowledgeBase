**Two seconds on this machine.** *"To reset all saved settings, press and hold RESET button for
2 seconds."* The keypad call-out puts it the same way: *"Hold for 2 seconds for a Total Reset of all
values (except Total Strokes)."*

**Three seconds on the CRW900**, whose console is otherwise the same casting and the same five keys
(`crw900-2021-console-total-reset-three-second-hold`). **One second apart on two near-identical water
rowers - check which machine you are answering before you quote a hold time.** The same two-second
hold is also what enters engineering mode on both machines, but with **Reset and Enter together**
(`spirit-water-rower-console-engineering-mode-sound-and-odo`); a two-second hold of Reset alone is
the Total Reset.

**TOTAL STROKES survives, and the manual states the exception in the call-out and nowhere else.**
Everything else the console has stored is cleared.

**Two things a Total Reset does not touch:**

- **TOTAL STROKES**, as above.
- **The ODO and the engineering mode settings.** *"The console settings and ODO will not be reset
  using the RESET Mode, however, removing the batteries will return the console to original factory
  settings and the ODO value will be zero."*

**Reset has four jobs on this console**, all printed in one call-out: *"clear a value, to pause your
workout, end your workout. Hold for 2 seconds for a Total Reset."* **There is no separate Pause
Mode on this machine** (`crw800h2o-console-sleep-ready-and-stop-after-four-minutes`), so a short press
of Reset ends the workout and shows STOP.

**An unset target on this console does nothing, rather than reusing your last value.** *"If no value
has been entered, the console will not count down."* **The CRW900 does the opposite** - *"the console
will use the last setting as the target value on each mode."* Two sentences in the same position in
two near-identical books, saying opposite things.
