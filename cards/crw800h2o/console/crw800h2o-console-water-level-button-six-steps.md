**The key does not change the resistance. It tells the console what the resistance already
is.**

*"Once you fill the tank to your desired resistance between 1 and 6 you can use the LEVEL Button to
communicate the resistance level to the console. Use the arrow keys to adjust from L1 to L6, pressing
ENTER to confirm the desired level. The default Level is L1."*

**The procedure:**

1. **Fill the tank** to the level you want. The water is the resistance; the console has no brake to
   adjust.
2. **Press LEVEL** - the bottom-left key of the control keypad
   (`crw800h2o-console-screen-overview`).
3. **Use the up and down arrows** to move between **L1** and **L6**.
4. **Press ENTER** to confirm.

**Six levels, and the default is L1.** A console straight out of the box, or one whose batteries have
been out, reads **L1** whatever is in the tank
(`spirit-water-rower-console-four-aa-batteries`). **A customer who filled the tank to level 5 and
never pressed LEVEL will see L1 on the display and correct watt and calorie figures that were
computed from the wrong level.**

**This is the "settings" the Quick Start paragraph means.** *"The 7-segment display will show level
according to the settings"* - the settings are this key's value
(`spirit-water-rower-console-quick-start-on-the-first-strokes`).

**The CRW900 has no LEVEL key.** That key position is its BLUETOOTH button, and its console reads
resistance *"based on current water level"* without being told
(`crw900-2021-console-bluetooth-uploads-the-workout-summary-to-the-app`). **Do not tell a CRW900
owner to press LEVEL, and do not tell a CRW800H2O owner it has Bluetooth.**

**Six levels here, sixteen on the air rowers, ten on the other water rower.** The CRW800 has a fan
and flywheel braked by a gear motor, on a **1 - 16** level range driven by the Up and Down keys
(`crw800-2024-specs-resistance-system`, `crw800-2024-specs-level-range-1-to-16`); the XRW600 shares
that scale and **its manual never says what its brake is**
(`spirit-xrw600-specs-resistance-mechanism-never-named`). The CRW900 has a ten-position knob on the
tank (`crw900-2021-specs-ten-level-tank-adjuster`). **None of these scales converts into another**,
and no Spirit rower manual describes a magnetic brake.
