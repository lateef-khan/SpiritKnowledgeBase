**Three modes, and there is no Pause.**

| Mode | How it is entered | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **SLEEP** | **60 seconds** of inactivity | The display switches off. **Pulling the handlebar** or pressing any button launches Ready Mode |
| **READY** | Waking from Sleep | Tells the user the console is powered on and ready for any mode. **60 seconds** of inactivity returns it to Sleep |
| **STOP** | Pressing **RESET**, or **4 minutes** with no console input | The 7-segment display shows **STOP** and every total is held |

**Four minutes, not thirty seconds.** The near-identical CRW900 console enters Stop after **30
seconds** and passes through a **Pause Mode** on the way, buzzing for two seconds as it goes
(`crw900-2021-console-sleep-ready-pause-and-stop-modes`). **This console has neither.** A CRW800H2O
owner who stops rowing keeps a live display for four minutes; a CRW900 owner loses it in half a
minute. **Do not answer one from the other - the two consoles differ by a factor of eight here.**

**What Stop Mode shows:** TIME the total time, DISTANCE the total distance, CALORIES the total burned,
STROKES the strokes for the workout, TOTAL STROKES the accumulative count, and **TIME/500m and
TIME/500m AVERAGE alternating for one minute**. **If heart rate was input it shows the current heart
rate**; if no heart rate monitor was used, **no values are displayed**.

**Another CRW900 difference in the same paragraph.** The CRW900's Stop Mode replays the heart rate at
**85%, 65% and max, five seconds each**; this one shows only *"current heart rate"*. **The percentage
banners are not on this console's heart rate panel**
(`spirit-water-rower-console-heart-rate-panel-three-dashes`).

**RESET does the pausing work here.** Its own call-out on the keypad drawing says the key is used *"to
clear a value, to pause your workout, end your workout"*, and holding it for **2 seconds** is a Total
Reset (`crw800h2o-console-total-reset-two-second-hold`). **The manual describes no state in which the
figures are held and the workout is resumed** - a press of RESET goes to Stop.
