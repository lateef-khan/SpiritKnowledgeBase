1. Refer to above procedures to take out the seat assembly from slide rail.
   *(That is the **Seat** procedure on the previous page.)*
2. Reverse the seat assembly, use **screw driver** to release the roller, as shown in figure 1.
3. Reverse above steps to replace the bad roller, **maybe one or more**.

**This procedure names no screw size and no roller count.** It is the only one on the machine that
does not. Step 3 says only "maybe one or more" - inspect every roller while the seat is off, because
the seat has to come off again to reach the next one.

The **two lower rollers** below the rail are removed in the Seat procedure, not here.
