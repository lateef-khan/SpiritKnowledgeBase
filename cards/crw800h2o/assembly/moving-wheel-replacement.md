1. Use **Spanner and Allen wrench S5** to release **one `M8*45mm` screw** and then remove moving
   wheel, as shown in figure 1.
2. Replace a new one and then secure it with the bolt.

One screw per wheel. This is the last replacement procedure in the manual; the two pages after it are
the Q&A section.
