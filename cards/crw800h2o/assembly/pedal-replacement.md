1. **Upper pedal** can be replaced directly by **slip down from lower pedal** - no tools.
2. To replace **lower pedal**, use **screw driver and Allen wrench** to remove **two M5 cross
   screws**.
3. Reverse above steps to assemble new pedal.

**The pedal is two parts.** The upper pedal slides off the lower one with no fastener at all. Only
the lower pedal is bolted, with two M5 cross screws. Establish which half you need before ordering.

The manual gives **no length** for the M5 screws and does not say what size Allen wrench holds the
nut.
