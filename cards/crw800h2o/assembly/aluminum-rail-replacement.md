1. Use **screw driver** to remove **two pieces cover for Aluminum Plate** from the rail, as shown in
   figure 1.
2. Take out **Aluminum Plate** from rail. **You may need some tools to help as it have application of
   gum on the back of the plate.**
3. To resume the Aluminum Plate, reverse above steps.

**The plate is glued down.** Step 2 is the whole job - the two covers come off with a screwdriver,
then the plate has to be worked off the adhesive. The manual names no screw size, no adhesive and no
replacement adhesive.

**The heading and the steps disagree on the part name.** The contents page and the row heading call
it the **Aluminum Rail**; every step calls it the **Aluminum Plate**. They are the same part.
