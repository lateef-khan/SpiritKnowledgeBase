**This page is a flat image.** The extraction returns the heading and nothing else - four
words for a page that carries the entire hardware kit. The rows below were read from a
render at 300 dpi and confirmed at 600 dpi.

| Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 63 | M8*45 | 4 pcs |
| 65 | M6*15 | 6 pcs |
| 68 | M8*15 | 4 pcs |
| 74 | OD20*ID8.5*1.5 (washer) | 4 pcs |
| 77 | M8*27 | 2 pcs |
| 93 | S5 (5mm Allen wrench) | 1 pc |
| 94 | S13-17 (13-17mm wrench) | 1 pc |
| 99 | M8*57 | 4 pcs |

## The kit closes exactly against the four steps

| Item | In the kit | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| 63 | 4 | step 1 - "2 Screws (No. 63) ... on both sides" |
| 74 | 4 | step 1 - "2 Washers (No. 74) ... on both sides" |
| 99 | 4 | step 2 - "4 Long Bolts (No. 99)" |
| 77 | 2 | step 2 - "2 Small Bolts (No. 77)" |
| 65 | 6 | step 2 - "6 Screws (No. 65)" |
| 68 | 4 | step 4 - "4 Small Screws (No. 68)" |

**Nothing is left over and nothing is short.** Step one says two of each "on both sides",
which is four of each in total - the one place where the step text and the kit count read
differently at a glance.

## Item 67 is not in this kit

Step three removes **4 Screws (No. 67), M5*15**, from the back of the console and puts the
same four back through the support plate. They arrive fitted to the console. Their size is
printed on the step page, not here. **Counting this kit will not account for them.**

## Two of the eight rows are tools

Items **93** and **94** are the 5mm Allen wrench and the 13-17mm wrench, packed with the
fasteners. See [the tools card](crw800h2o-assembly-tools-included.md).

## No torque figure

No step of this build gives one.
