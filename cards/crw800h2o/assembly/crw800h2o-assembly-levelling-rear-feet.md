The whole instruction, printed under **Adjusting the Balance**:

> **Adjust the rear feet if the machine is out of balance.**

**No count, no tool, no direction of turn.** The panel is one sentence beside a drawing
that numbers the rear stabiliser and the slide rail, and the book says nothing further
about levelling anywhere.

## Do not carry the range's usual figures across to this machine

Most Spirit manuals answer this question with "there are 2 leveling pads on the bottom of
the rear stabilizer, use a 14mm wrench (or adjustable wrench)". Both **XRW600** printings
and the **CRW800 2021** and **2024** print it word for word. **This book does not**, and
its carton holds a 13-17mm wrench rather than a 13/14mm. See
[that card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

The other Spirit water rower, the **CRW900**, gives a third answer again: no levellers at
all, and a line telling you to choose a flat surface. See
[that card](../../crw900-2021/assembly/crw900-2021-assembly-choose-a-flat-surface.md).

## The same panel carries a warning that is not about levelling

Beside the sentence, in the same box:

> **CAUTION! Moving parts, such as the seat, can crush and cut your fingers. Keep hands
> clear of the Slide Rail during use.**

## The page is outside the numbered build

**Adjustment Guides** is p. 19, after the tank-filling page and before the storage page,
and it is not in the table of contents. Its other half is the pedal-strap adjustment.
