1. Use **Allen wrench S6 and spanner** to release **one `M10*55mm` bolt** and then remove **two lower
   rollers** below the rail, as shown in figure 1.
2. Take out the seat assembly then reverse it. Use **Allen wrench S4** to release **four `M6*15mm`
   screws** to take apart the seat from **seat carriage**, as shown in figure 2.
3. Reverse above steps to resume the seat. **Don't secure the lower rollers too tightly. Make sure
   the seat can be moved smoothly on the rail.**

The lower rollers are the part that gets over-tightened. Snug, not tight - the seat has to run free.
