**Lift up the rear stabilizer until the transportation wheels on the front stand touch the
ground.** With the wheels on the ground you can transport the rower to the desired location
with ease.

> **CAUTION: Move the Seat (No. 38) to the front of the Slide Rail (No. 3) first, or it
> will glide down when you raise the rower up.**

> **CAUTION: Move with caution when you raise the rower up, as your head may touch the Rear
> Stabilizer (No. 2).**

**The seat caution applies to any lift**, not only to storing the machine. It is printed
once, at the top of the page, and covers both the moving instruction and the upright-storage
one below it.

## The wheels are at the front and you lift the back

This is the opposite of the CRW800 and XRW600 air rowers, which fold their rails and are
**lifted by the rear to roll away on the rear**. On this machine the wheels are on the
**front stand**, so raising the rear puts the weight onto them. The other Spirit water
rower, the CRW900, prints no moving procedure at all. See the linked cards.

## This machine does not fold

Nothing on it folds, and the word *fold* appears nowhere in the English text of the book.

## The page is headed in Chinese and is not in the contents

The heading above "Storage" is **折叠步骤图** - Chinese for "folding step diagram" - left
untranslated in an English manual, and printed over a page about wheeling and standing a
machine that does not fold. The page itself is not listed in the table of contents; it sits
between the tank-filling page and the console chapter, after the numbered build has ended.

## Storing it upright is the other half of the same page

Below this instruction the page says the rower can be stored vertically, lifted by the rear
stabiliser, and that the tank must be emptied if it will stand more than a month. **That
half is held on
[the storage card](../maintenance/crw800h2o-maintenance-empty-the-tank-before-storing.md)**,
and this card does not repeat it.
