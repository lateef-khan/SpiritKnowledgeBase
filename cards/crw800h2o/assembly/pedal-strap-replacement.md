1. Use **screw driver** to remove **two M5 screws** and take the defective pedal strap out from the
   pedal, as shown in figure 1.
2. Put new pedal strap **from one side of plate to another side**, and then secure with **two
   screws**, as shown in figure 2.

The strap threads across the plate before the screws go back in. The manual gives the **M5** diameter
but **no length** for these screws.
