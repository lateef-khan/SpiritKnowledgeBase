1. Use **Allen wrench S5 and spanner** to release **one `M10*55mm` bolt** to take apart guide roller,
   as shown in figure 1.
2. To resume guide roller, put the **`M10*55mm` bolt** through into guide roller, **two spacers** and
   **one Nylon nut**, then use Allen wrench S5 and spanner to secure it, as shown in figure 2.
   **Make sure the strap in right location.**

Two spacers and one nylon nut go back with the bolt. The strap must be routed correctly before the
bolt is tightened.

**The seat procedure uses an S6 Allen wrench on a bolt of the same `M10*55mm` size.** This one is
printed **S5**. Both are quoted from the manual as printed; check the socket before you lean on it.
