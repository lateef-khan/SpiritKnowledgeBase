This is one procedure. The manual does not split the mesh belt wheel, the strap and the volute
spring - they come apart in one order and go back in one order.

1. Refer to above manual to remove the **Volute Spring Set** *(the Sensor Wire procedure, step 2)*.
   Use **screw driver** to release **four `ST5*12` screws** in red circle and then take out the
   **Axle for Volute Spring**, as shown in figure 1.
2. Use a tool to **fix the plastic impeller to make sure it can't be rotated**; use a **socket
   wrench** to release a **Hex screw** to take out the Mesh Belt Wheel, as shown in figure 2.
3. Take out the mesh belt wheel. If the strap is damaged, please release **one `M5*30mm` screw** to
   remove the strap, as shown in figure 3.
4. To assemble mesh belt wheel, fix the strap onto it and **put through as right direction**, as
   shown in figure 4.
5. Put **Axle for Volute Spring** through into mesh belt wheel and secure with **four screws**, as
   shown in figure 5.
6. Remove **four `ST4*19mm` screws** to take out the plate on the Volute Spring. If Volute Spring have
   breakage, please take it out and replace it with a new one. Then **press the hook rack into the
   groove of Axle for Volute Spring**. **You must be very careful to avoid Volute Spring jump out
   from the box.** As shown in figure 6.
7. Secure the plate back to the **Base of Volute Spring**; **pre-roll the Volute Spring box for 4
   circles in arrow direction** to make sure strap can be back mesh belt wheel, as shown in figure 7.
8. Put back other parts as above steps.

**Two different screws, easily swapped.** Step 1 takes out **four `ST5*12`** screws (axle for volute
spring). Step 6 takes out **four `ST4*19mm`** screws (plate on the volute spring). They are not
interchangeable.

**The pre-load in step 7 is four turns.** Pre-roll the volute spring box **4 circles in the arrow
direction** or the strap will not retract into the mesh belt wheel.

**Safety:** the volute spring is a wound clock spring. Step 6 warns in the manual's own words that it
can jump out of the box.

Step 2 needs the impeller held. The manual says "use a tool" and does not name one.
