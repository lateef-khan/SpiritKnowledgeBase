1. Use **Allen wrench S5** to release **four `M8*15mm` screw** to take apart the console assembly.
   Disconnect the **sensor wire** and **connection wire** from the tube, as shown in figure 1.
2. Take apart **two pieces decorative covers** from the main frame.
3. Use **spanner** to release **M6 nylon nut** from **both sides** of the belt wheel, then take out
   the defective belt wheel.
4. Replace a new one by reverse above steps. **Don't secure too tightly or miss washers or spacers,
   make sure the wheel can be rotated smoothly.**

There is a nylon nut on **each** side of the wheel. Step 4 is the whole trick: washers and spacers
must go back, and the nuts must not be pulled up tight or the wheel will not spin.

**This is the belt wheel, not the mesh belt wheel.** They are two different parts with two different
procedures - the mesh belt wheel sits on the volute spring axle at the tank end.
