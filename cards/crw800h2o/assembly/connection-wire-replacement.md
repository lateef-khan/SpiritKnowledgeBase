1. Use **Allen wrench S5** to release **four `M8*15mm` screws** to take apart the console support,
   take the **two pieces decorative covers** out from the main frame, as shown in figure 1.
2. Use **Allen wrench S5 and Spanner** to disassemble the console plate, remove the **adjustable
   knob** to take apart support tube from the decorative cover, as shown in figure 2.
3. **Cut the black fastening wire** and then pull connection wire out from tube, as shown in
   figure 3.
4. Replace the new wire by reverse the above steps. **The sensor plate maybe tight when assemble,
   please pay attention to two black ties in red circles** as shown in figure 3.

Step 3 destroys a tie. Have a replacement cable tie ready before you start - the wire will not stay
in the tube without it.
