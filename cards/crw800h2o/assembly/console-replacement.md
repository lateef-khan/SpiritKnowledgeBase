1. Use **Phillips head screw driver** to release **four `M5*12` Phillips head screws**, as shown in
   figure 1.
2. Disconnect the **connection wire & wire** from the console and take apart the console, as shown in
   figure 2.
3. To resume the console, connect the connection wire & wire with the console and secure the
   **Support plate** with **four `M5*12mm` screws** onto the back of the console.

The screws go through the **support plate** on the back of the console, not through the console face.

**The `Rev.` column on this page is blank.** Every procedure page in this manual prints a `Rev.`
heading with no number under it. The numbers down the right-hand edge of the page are **figure
numbers**, not revisions.
