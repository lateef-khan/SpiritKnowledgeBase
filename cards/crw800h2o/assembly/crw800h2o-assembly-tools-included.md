Two tools, packed in with the fasteners:

| Item | Tool | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 93 | **S5** - 5mm Allen wrench | 1 |
| 94 | **S13-17** - 13-17mm wrench | 1 |

> **NOTE: All tools required to assemble the rower are included within the packaging.**
> - CRW800H2O PRE-ASSEMBLY, p. 11

## The only page that lists them is a flat image

There is no TOOLS INCLUDED panel in this book. The two tools are rows 6 and 7 of the
hardware picture on p. 13, which **`pdftotext` returns as four words**. An extraction of
this manual that has not been rendered contains no tool list at all. See
[the hardware card](crw800h2o-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Only step two names them

Step two ends "**Use the Allen Wrench (No. 93) and Wrench (No. 94) to secure**". Steps one,
three and four name no tool, although step one drives four M8*45 screws and step three and
four drive M5 and M8 screws that need the same 5mm key.

## Nothing in the carton drives a Phillips head

The steps call items 63, 65, 67 and 68 "screws" throughout, and there is **no screwdriver
in the kit**. The manual never says what head they have and never says to supply your own
tool; the only driver in the box is the 5mm Allen wrench.

## The 13-17mm wrench is the widest in the rower family

The Spirit CRW800 and XRW600 cartons hold a 13/14mm; the CRW900 a plain 13mm. **This is
the only Spirit rower carton with a wrench that opens past 15mm.** No page of this book
says what needs 17.

## Nothing here is a torque wrench

No step of this build gives a torque figure.
