1. Use **Allen wrench S4** to release **two `M6*15mm` screws** from the back of the **Main Frame
   Plate** to take it out, as shown in figure 1.
2. Take out main frame plate out from the main frame. Release **four `M8*15mm` flat head screws** to
   take out the **Volute Spring Set**, as shown in figure 2.
3. Use **screw driver** to release **four `M3*6mm` cross head screws** to remove the sensor wires, as
   shown in figure 3.
4. Reverse above steps to assemble new sensor wires. **Take care not to damage the wires when you
   operate.**

Three different screws in three steps: **`M6*15mm`** (frame plate), **`M8*15mm` flat head** (volute
spring set), **`M3*6mm` cross head** (sensor). The `M3*6mm` ones are tiny and are the only M3 fastener
on the machine.

The volute spring set has to come out to reach the sensor. Read the volute spring procedure before
you take it apart - the spring can jump out of its box.
