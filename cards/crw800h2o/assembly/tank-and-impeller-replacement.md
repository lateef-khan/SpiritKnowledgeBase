1. Disassemble the **tank plate** after finishing all above guided steps and take it out from the
   main frame.
2. Take out the tank assembly and remove **all screws in the middle of upper and lower tank**, then
   you can replace the defective tank or impeller or related rubber parts.
3. Prepare a new **plastic impeller**, put **one stainless washer** through shaft, press
   **Mechanical Sealing Ring** into shaft, **make sure it touch stainless washer closely**, as shown
   in figure 1.
4. Prepare upper tank and turn it over, insert a **white graphite spacer** between middle hole of
   tank, and keep it fixed. **You need to add some special glue for plastic at edge of tank hole.**
   Place a **big rubber sealing ring** properly in upper tank. Insert impeller shaft to the hole of
   tank. **You may need a base to support the tank** to make sure the steps go on smoothly. As shown
   in figure 2.
5. Cover another tank and secure with **12 `M3*20` stainless screws** in the middle, as shown in
   figure 3.
6. Put **one Lower Bearing base with bearing** through shaft, **take care the direction**; assemble
   total tank assembly onto the main frame. Put tank plate up on the tank, use **S4 Allen wrench** to
   tight **6 `M6*15` screws** on upper tank and use **S5 Allen wrench** to tight **4 `M8*15` screws**,
   as shown in figure 3.
7. Go on above steps to assemble mesh belt wheel and handlebar.

**Twelve `M3*20` stainless screws close the tank halves** - all of them, or it leaks.

Three things in this procedure are not in the box with a new tank and are the usual reason a repair
fails: the **special glue for plastic** at the tank hole edge (step 4), the **white graphite spacer**,
and the **big rubber sealing ring**. Have them before you open the tank.

Step 6's **lower bearing base is directional**. The manual says "take care the direction" and does
not say which way round.

Step 1 says to do this "after finishing all above guided steps" - the tank is the deepest part of the
machine and everything above it comes off first.
