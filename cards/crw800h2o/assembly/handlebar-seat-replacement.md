1. Use **Allen wrench S4** to release **two `M6*15mm` screws** to take apart the defective handlebar
   seat, as shown in figure 1.
2. To resume the new handlebar seat, secure **two `M6*15mm` screws** with **Allen wrench S4**.

Two screws, one wrench. This is the shortest procedure in the manual.

**The handlebar seat is the cradle the handle rests in. It is not the rower's seat**, and it is not
the handlebar itself - the handlebar and its strap are replaced with the mesh belt wheel.
