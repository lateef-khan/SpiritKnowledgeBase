Item numbers in brackets are the assembly-drawing numbers, as the pre-assembly page
explains. Fastener counts are on
[the hardware card](crw800h2o-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

**This is not the CRW800 air rower and not the CRW900.** All three are Spirit rowers, all
three build differently, and this one shares no item number with either.

## Before you start

1. **Position the shipping carton so the heavy end is at the bottom.**
2. Cut the straps, lift the box over the unit, remove the foam inserts and check the parts
   list.
3. Locate the hardware package. Remove the tools first, then take the hardware for each
   step as you reach it.

> **CAUTION: Damage to the rower during assembly is not covered** by the limited warranty.
> Do not drop the rower or lean it on its side; stand it up in the normal upright position
> on a stable surface so it cannot tip over during assembly.

## Step one - seat onto the rails, and the rear stabiliser

1. **Turn the Seat (No. 38) over** and attach its **Rollers (No. 39)** to the **Slide Rails
   (No. 3L, 3R)**, making sure **the plate touches the rollers**. To get the rails between
   the rollers, **insert at an angle and rotate** as the diagram shows.
2. Secure the **Rear Stabilizer (No. 2)** to the slide rails using **2 Screws (No. 63) and
   2 Washers (No. 74) on both sides** - four of each in total.

## Step two - mainframe upright

> **This step is easier with a second person.** Be careful of the sliding **Seat (No. 38)**
> - it will move once the machine is upright.

Set the **Main Frame (No. 1)** upright and connect the **Slide Rails (No. 3)** using **4
Long Bolts (No. 99)**, **2 Small Bolts (No. 77)** and **6 Screws (No. 65)**. Use the
**Allen Wrench (No. 93)** and **Wrench (No. 94)** to secure.

## Step three - console

1. **Connect the Console Wires (No. 96)** together.
2. **Remove the 4 Screws (No. 67)** from the back of the **Console (No. 54)**, align the
   holes on the console with the holes on the **Support Plate (No. 11)**, and secure with
   the same screws.

*Those four screws come off the console, not out of the hardware kit. Keep them.*

## Step four - batteries, sensor wire and cover

1. Place the **4 AA batteries** into the **Console (No. 54)**.
2. Turn the **Main Frame (No. 1)** upright. Connect the **Sensor Wire (No. 56)** to the
   **Connection Wire (No. 96)**. **Be careful not to pinch the wires**, and feed any extra
   length to lie inside the main frame.
3. **Plug the Decorative Cover (No. 26)** into the main frame.
4. Secure the **Console (No. 54)** to the main frame using **4 Small Screws (No. 68)**.

## The build is not finished when step four is

**The tank is empty.** The machine has no resistance until it is filled, and the filling
procedure is printed on the next page under its own heading rather than as a fifth step.

The two pages after that - pedal-strap adjustment, balancing on the rear feet, and moving
and storing - are also outside the numbered build. See
[the levelling card](crw800h2o-assembly-levelling-rear-feet.md) and
[the storage card](crw800h2o-assembly-storage-and-moving.md).
