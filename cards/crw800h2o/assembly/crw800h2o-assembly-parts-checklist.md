> **Note: Above parts are all needed to assemble this machine. Before you start to
> assemble, please check the hardware packing to make sure they are included.**

## Four lines carry no part number

The tablet, the manual, the hardware pack and the batteries are listed by description
only. The first seven have numbers and match the numbers the steps and the outline drawing
use.

## The batteries agree with the step that fits them

Four AA here, and step four says "**Place the 4 AA batteries into the Console (No. 54)**".
The other Spirit water rower, the CRW900, contradicts itself on exactly this figure; this
one does not.

## The funnel and the siphon are for the tank, not the build

No assembly step touches either. The **Funnel (No. 52)** and **Pumping Siphon (No. 53)**
are used on the tank-filling page that follows the build, together with the single
purification tablet. **The machine will not work until the tank is filled**, and the manual
prints that under its own heading rather than as a sixth step.

## The panel lists no console mast, no pedals and no transport wheels

The **Support Plate (No. 11)** that step three bolts the console to, the pedals, the
**Decorative Cover (No. 26)** step four plugs in and the transport wheels the storage page
rolls the machine on are all on the machine or on the outline drawing, and none of them is
on this list. See [the outline card](../specs/outlines-part-names.md).

## What the panel does not say

No shipping weight, no box count and no box dimensions.
