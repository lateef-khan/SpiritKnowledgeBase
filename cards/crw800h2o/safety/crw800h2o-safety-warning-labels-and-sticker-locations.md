> The image below shows an example of Spirit Fitness warning labels and communication stickers
> placed on the equipment as part of the manufacturing process. It is critical that owners maintain
> the integrity and placement of these stickers. If you find any stickers missing or damaged contact
> your local dealer or distributor for a replacement. NOTE: STICKERS AND LABELS ARE NOT SHOWN TO
> SCALE.

**This page is a drawing of the machine with three callout lines, not a set of reproduced decals.**
Read from a 300 dpi render of printed page 8; the page returns 87 native words and 93 from a render,
and the callouts are inside the image.

| Callout | Where the line points |
|---|---|
| SERIAL NUMBER STICKER | the **front left stabilizer foot**, at the outer end |
| CALIFORNIA PROP 65 WARNING DECAL (For US model only) | the same front left foot, just inboard of the serial sticker |
| MADE IN CHINA STICKER | the same front left foot, inboard again toward the tank |

All three sit within a few inches of each other on the front left foot, on the tank end of the
machine. **The drawing does not reproduce the serial or Made in China stickers**, so their contents
are unknown from this book; only the Prop 65 decal's text is shown, and it reads
`WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm - www.P65Warnings.ca.gov`.

**The drawing disagrees with the manual's own sentence about the serial number.** Two pages earlier
the book says the serial number `is located on the center floor tube behind the crank`
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-serial-number-on-the-centre-floor-tube`). The drawing points at the
front left foot instead, and the manual never reconciles the two. Tell a customer to check both.

**The `MADE IN CHINA STICKER` is unique to this machine in the Spirit rower range.** The CRW900's
labels page instead reproduces a serial decal reading `MADE IN TAIWAN`
(`crw900-2021-safety-warning-labels-and-serial-decal`). The two water rowers share 83.3% of their
safety chapters' eight-word phrases and are built in different countries.

**There is no exercise warning sticker on this page.** The CRW900 shows a `KEEP CLEAR OF MOVING
PARTS` decal and this book shows none - an absence, not an oversight to fill from the sibling.

**No certification mark appears anywhere.** Unlike the CRW900's decal, which carries CE, TÜV and GS
marks, this manual reproduces no compliance mark and prints no standards list
(`crw800-2024-safety-no-compliance-page`).
