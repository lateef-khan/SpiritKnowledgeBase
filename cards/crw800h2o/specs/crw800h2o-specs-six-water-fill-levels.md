**Six levels, and the only thing that sets them is how much water is in the
tank.**

> The water level gauge is on the side of the tank. **The maximum fill level is
> 6.** Never fill it over this limit. Filling the tank over this limit will void
> the warranty.
>
> **The resistance depends on the water level in the tank. Water level 1 is the
> lowest resistance. Level 6 is the highest resistance.**

**The top of the scale and the overfill limit are the same mark.** There is no
headroom above level 6 - a customer who wants "a bit more than the maximum" is
being told to void their warranty. Say so plainly.

## There is no adjuster and no brake

This machine has **no resistance knob, no damper and no motorised brake**. To
change the resistance you add or siphon out water, which means it is not something
that can be changed mid-workout. The filling and emptying procedure, the funnel
(No. 52) and the siphon pump (No. 53) are `maintenance` -
`crw800h2o-maintenance-filling-and-emptying-the-tank`.

## The LEVEL key on the console does not change anything

It **tells the console** what you already poured in, on an L1 to L6 scale
defaulting to L1. A tank filled to 5 on a console left at L1 shows L1 and computes
its watts and calories from the wrong figure. That key is a `console` question -
see `crw800h2o-console-water-level-button-six-steps`.

## Six here, ten on the other Spirit water rower, sixteen on the air rowers

| Machine | Scale | How it is set |
|---|---|---|
| **CRW800H2O** | **1 - 6** | how much water you pour in; the top mark is the fill limit |
| **CRW900** | **1 - 10** | an Adjustment Knob moving water between two chambers, adjustable while rowing |
| **CRW800 and XRW600** | **1 - 16** | console Up/Down keys against a fan and a motorised brake |

**None of these scales converts into another.** A level 6 on this machine is not a
level 6 on a CRW900 and not a level 6 on a CRW800. See
`crw900-2021-specs-ten-level-tank-adjuster` and
`crw800-2024-specs-level-range-1-to-16`.

**No tank capacity in litres or gallons is printed in this manual.** The CRW900's
book gives 20 litres for its own tank; that figure is not this machine's and must
not be carried across. No watt figure, drag factor or level-to-watts table is
printed either.
