The outline drawing labels: **Console**, **Decorative Cover**, **Handlebar & Strap**, **Pedal**,
**Seat**, **Plastic Impeller**, **Moving Wheel**, **Tank**, **Pedal Strap**,
**Front Stabilizer**, **Volute Spring**, **Roller**, **Lower Roller**, **Sliding rail**,
**Rear Stabilizer**, **Adjust Knob**.

This drawing is the whole of the manual's specification content. The CRW800H2O service manual
has **no electronic-parts chapter, no electrical configuration, no unit block diagram, no
connector or wiring map and no circuit diagram** - it is a part-replacement manual with a
two-page Q&A. It prints no dimensions, no weight, no maximum user weight and no tank capacity
either.
