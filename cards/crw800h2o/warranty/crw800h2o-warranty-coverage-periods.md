**Two rows, three columns**, residential printed first:

| Warranty | Frame | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | Lifetime | 10 Years | 2 Years |
| Commercial *(Non dues paying facility.)* | Lifetime | 5 Years | 2 Years |

with the footnote *\*Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage.*

**The tank has no column of its own on this machine.** The water tank, its seals and its plug are covered as **Parts** - ten years at home, five in a facility - and the manual never names them separately. That is the opposite of the CRW900, whose table gives *Tank & Seals* a column of its own at three years commercially and five residentially: `crw900-2021-warranty-five-column-periods-tank-and-seals`. Two water rowers, two different answers to "is the tank covered".

**Two things the owner does to the tank void the warranty outright**, and neither is on this page - they are printed in the tank-filling instructions on p. 18: `crw800h2o-warranty-void-chlorine-and-overfilling`. A tank claim has to clear that first.

**There is no brake column and no wearable-parts column.** Everything that is not the frame is a part.

**Labor is two years in both rows**, and the SERVICE bullets still date the labor term from twelve months on the bill of sale or eighteen from factory shipping. The manual does not reconcile the two. Quote both.

**"Commercial" is a non-dues-paying facility, per the row label.** Unlike the air rowers, this manual's exclusion list carries **no** use-environment item at all, so the row label is the only place the environment is restricted: `spirit-water-rower-warranty-exclusions-eight-items`.
