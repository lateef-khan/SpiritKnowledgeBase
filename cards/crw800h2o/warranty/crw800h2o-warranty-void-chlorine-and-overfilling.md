**Two things void the warranty on this machine, and neither is in the warranty section.** Both are printed in the tank-filling instructions on p. 18.

**1. Anything but tap water and the supplied purification tablet.**

> Fill the tank only with tap water. Add 1 water-purification tablet. **Never use pool chlorine or chlorine bleach. This will damage the tank and void the warranty.**

**2. Filling past level 6.**

> The water level gauge is on the side of the tank. **The maximum fill level is 6. Never fill it over this limit. Filling the tank over this limit will void the warranty.**

**Level 6 is also the maximum resistance**, so a user chasing a harder stroke by adding water past the gauge is voiding the warranty to gain nothing the machine offers. Level 1 is the lowest resistance, level 6 the highest.

**"Voids the warranty" is stronger than anything the warranty section says.** The eight-item exclusion list on p. 36 excludes abuse, misuse and improper maintenance in general terms and names neither chlorine nor the fill level: `spirit-water-rower-warranty-exclusions-eight-items`. These two sentences are the only place the manual voids the whole warranty for a specific act, and a reader who only reads the warranty pages will never see them.

**This is a CRW800H2O rule, not a water rower rule.** The CRW900 water rower prints **no** warranty-voiding sentence anywhere - the phrase does not occur in that document, checked over the whole text and on a 300 dpi render of its tank-filling page. What the CRW900 prints instead is an instruction with no stated consequence: *DO NOT, UNDER ANY CIRCUMSTANCE, USE A WATER TREATMENT TABLET OTHER THAN THOSE SUPPLIED WITH YOUR UNIT.* Do not tell a CRW900 owner their warranty is void; the book does not say it.

**Routine tank care is a separate matter and does not void anything**: a purification tablet every 6 months or as needed, and replacing the water if it stays cloudy.

**The water is not drinkable.** The manual says water from the tank is not suitable for consumption and should be disposed of after pumping out.
