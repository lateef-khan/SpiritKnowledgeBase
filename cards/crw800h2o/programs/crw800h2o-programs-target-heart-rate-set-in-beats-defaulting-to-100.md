*"The Target Heart Rate program allows the maximum beats per minute (BPM) target to be customized to
maximize cardiovascular training."*

1. **Launch** - press the **Target Heart Rate** button.
2. **Setup** - **the user enters the desired target heart rate. The default is 100.** Adjust with the
   up and down arrows, then press **ENTER** to confirm.
3. **Start** - the display shows your heart rate and your progress.
   - **More than 10% below the target**, the **up arrow flashes** - the minimum goal has not been
     reached.
   - **More than 10% above the target**, the **down arrow flashes** - you are over the maximum goal.
   - **Within 10% of the target, the target number is not displayed.**
4. **End** - *"The target program will end when the user stops rowing."* Pressing **RESET** at any
   time also ends it, and the workout summary follows.

The heart rate display page states the band the same way: *"the target value range will vary by 10%
from the default data"*, with the up or down symbol flashing to say whether to work harder or ease
off, and the range icons lighting when you are in it.

**This machine never asks for your age and never mentions a maximum heart rate.** The target is the
number you type in. The manual prints no 220-minus-age formula and no training zone - see
`spirit-water-rower-programs-no-maximum-heart-rate-formula-or-training-zone-page`.

**The CRW900 water rower works the other way round.** It asks for your **age**, calculates the
maximum itself and trains you between **65% and 85%** of it, with 65% and 85% banners that flash. See
`crw900-2021-programs-target-heart-rate-set-by-age-between-65-and-85-percent`. Two water rowers, two
different setups; the 100 default belongs to this one only.

**A heart rate receiver and a chest strap (not included) are required.** Without a signal the console
shows three dashes "- - -".
