## To fill

1. **Remove the Fill Plug (No. 50)** from the **Upper Tank (No. 24)**.
2. Insert the **Funnel (No. 52)** into the tank, then use a cup or the **Siphon Pump (No. 53)** and
   a bucket to fill the tank. Use the **water level gauge on the side of the tank** to measure the
   desired level.
3. **Insert the Fill Plug (No. 50)** back into the Upper Tank (No. 24) and **wipe excess water off
   the frame**.

**Fill only with tap water**, and add **1 water-purification tablet** — the machine ships with
**1 packet**.

## To empty

1. Place a bucket next to the rower.
2. Use the **Siphon Pump (No. 53)** to pump the water out of the tank into the bucket.
3. Replace the fill plug and wipe the frame.

**The water is not drinkable.** The manual says water from the tank is not suitable for consumption
and should be disposed of after pumping out.

## The gauge, and the two limits that are warranty matters

The gauge on the side of the tank runs **1 to 6**. **Level 1 is the lowest resistance and level 6 the
highest**, so the fill level is how this machine is set — it has no resistance lever.

**Two acts void the warranty**, and both are printed on this page rather than in the warranty
chapter: filling **past level 6**, and using **pool chlorine or chlorine bleach** instead of the
purification tablet. See `crw800h2o-warranty-void-chlorine-and-overfilling` before quoting either.

## Not the CRW900 procedure

The CRW900 water rower fills a **different tank a different way** — a siphon squeezed from a bucket
with the resistance adjuster set to "10", a **20 litre** maximum read off a decal, and a different
tablet. Nothing here transfers to it:
`crw900-2021-maintenance-tank-filling-and-initial-water-treatment`. This book prints no litre
figure at all, and the CRW900 book prints no gauge levels 1 to 6.

**Draining is never complete on the CRW900** — about 40% stays in. This book says nothing of the
kind about the CRW800H2O tank, so do not carry that warning across:
`crw900-2021-maintenance-removing-and-changing-tank-water`.
