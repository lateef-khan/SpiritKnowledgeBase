Where the water comes from decides the answer. The manual gives three cases.

## A little water from the middle of the upper tank, when the rower is stood up

**Normal.** As printed: "If you find a little water spill out from the middle of upper tank when
you stand the rower up, this is a normal phenomenon but may check if you put too much water. You
can go on running this machine."

So: check the fill level, and keep using the machine. This is the only one of the three that is
not a fault.

## Water from the **bottom** of the tank

"Maybe the lower tank has little crack, please replace tank as above related Manual." — replace
the tank, per the tank replacement procedure in section 12 (Tank/Impeller).

## Water from **between** the upper and lower tank

"Sealing ring is defective or not properly assembled, change a new sealing ring to assemble tank."
— replace the sealing ring and reassemble the tank.

Note the first and third cases are both described as the middle of the tank. The distinguishing
detail is that the harmless one happens **only when the rower is stood up**, and is a little water;
a leak at the joint while the machine sits normally is the sealing ring.
