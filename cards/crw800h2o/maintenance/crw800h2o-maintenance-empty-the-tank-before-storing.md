**If the rower will not be used for more than a month, empty the tank before storing it.**

That single sentence is the only long-term storage instruction in the book. Pump the water out with
the **Siphon Pump (No. 53)** as for a normal drain and dispose of it —
`crw800h2o-maintenance-filling-and-emptying-the-tank`.

## Standing the rower up

When not in use the rower can be stored **vertically** to save space. Lift by the **Rear Stabilizer
(No. 2)** to raise it.

Two cautions are printed with it:

- **Move the Seat (No. 38) to the front of the Slide Rail (No. 3) first**, or it will glide down the
  rail as you raise the machine.
- **Move with caution as you raise it** — your head may touch the Rear Stabilizer (No. 2).

## Moving it

Lift the rear stabilizer until the **transportation wheels on the front stand** touch the ground.
With the wheels down, wheel the rower to where you want it.

## What the manual does not say

**Nothing about freezing.** Neither water rower manual mentions frost, cold, freezing or a storage
temperature anywhere — the words return zero hits across both texts including their OCR
supplements. There is no instruction to empty the tank before winter and no minimum temperature.
The only stated reason to empty it is the one-month figure above, so do not tell a customer their
book covers freezing.

**Nothing about cleaning the tank itself.** The manual asks for the seat rails and the frame to be
wiped weekly and for the water to be treated, but it never asks anyone to open, scrub or rinse the
tank; the tank comes apart only in the service manual, for an impeller or seal replacement
(`crw800h2o-tank-and-impeller-replacement`).
