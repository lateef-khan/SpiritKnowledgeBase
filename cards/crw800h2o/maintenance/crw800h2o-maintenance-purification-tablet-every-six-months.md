> **Add a water purification tablet every 6 months or as needed. If water remains cloudy, replace
> the water in the tank.**

That is the whole interval this manual gives. **Six months**, or sooner if the water needs it, and a
full water change only when a tablet has not cleared it.

The machine is supplied with **1 packet** of purification tablets and is filled with **tap water**
only.

## The maintenance table gives a different-looking figure for the same job

The GENERAL MAINTENANCE table on printed p. 32 gives **Tank and Water Treatment: 12m to 2 years**,
and points at the wrong page for the detail
(`spirit-water-rower-maintenance-weekly-rails-and-hundred-hour-belt-check`). The two are not the
same measurement: **6 months is the tablet interval; 12 months to 2 years is how long the tank goes
between water changes.** Quote both, in that order, and say which is which — a reader who finds only
the table will leave the water untreated for a year.

## The CRW900's interval is not this one

The CRW900 water rower expects **8 to 12 months near a bright, sunlit window and 2 years or more in a
darker location**, and adds a tablet only when the water is *already* slightly cloudy —
`crw900-2021-maintenance-long-term-water-treatment-interval`. It does not print a six-month figure,
and this book does not print a sunlight-dependent one.

## What must not go in the tank

**Never pool chlorine or chlorine bleach** — the manual says it damages the tank and voids the
warranty: `crw800h2o-warranty-void-chlorine-and-overfilling`.

If the water has already discoloured, the troubleshooting table adds two more causes — direct
sunlight, and poor local tap water, for which it recommends refilling with **distilled water**:
`spirit-water-rower-errors-tank-water-turns-cloudy`.
