The service manual's answer, word for word. The question is printed as `The console without display`.

> Check if batteries have enough power? If not, change new battery to try. If still not OK, change a
> new console.

**The owner's manuals of both water rowers answer the same question at greater length**, under the
issue `The console does not illuminate after battery installation`, with a single cause -
`Batteries installed incorrectly or need replacing`:

1. Check to see if the batteries are installed in the **correct position**.
2. Reinstall or replace as needed.
3. If the LCD screen fails to illuminate, **try rotating the batteries slightly in the computer**.
4. If this fails, contact your local service center.

**Rotating the cells in their holder is the step nobody guesses**, and it is free. Do it before
condemning anything: on these consoles a cell that sits slightly proud of its contact reads as a
dead console. Check the polarity first - the manuals put "installed in the correct position" ahead
of "replace".

**A second question in the service manual reads the same and answers it at greater length still.**
`The console without data` is answered with five checks, including the wiring and the magnet in the
mesh belt wheel, and its answer opens with the words `When there is no display`. It is on
`crw800h2o-console-shows-no-data`. Do those five checks before condemning the console; the
two-step answer above would have a technician buy a console over a displaced magnet.

Every source here agrees on the first step: **check the batteries**. Both are battery consoles and
both console chapters name **4 AA cells**, in a compartment on the back of the console, so there is
no mains supply to test. **The CRW900 manual contradicts itself on the count**: its assembly step
says "Install supplied **2** AA batteries [15]" while its console chapter and its parts list both
say **4**. Fit four.

The service manual covers the CRW800H2O only; the CRW900 contributes the owner's manual row.

**The CRW900 service manual repeats the owner's-manual row word for word** - batteries installed incorrectly or needing replacement, check the position, reinstall or replace, rotate the cells slightly, then the service centre - so for the CRW900 the two books agree and neither adds a measurement.
