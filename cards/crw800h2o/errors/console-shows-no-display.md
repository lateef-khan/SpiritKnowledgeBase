The manual's answer, word for word. The question is printed as `The console without display`.

> Check if batteries have enough power? If not, change new battery to try. If still not OK, change a
> new console.

**A second question in the same table asks something that reads the same and answers it at greater
length.** `The console without data` is answered with five checks, including the wiring and the
magnet in the mesh belt wheel, and its answer opens with the words `When there is no display`. It is
on `crw800h2o-console-shows-no-data`. Do the five checks before condemning the console; this
two-step answer would have a technician buy a console over a displaced magnet.

Both answers agree on the first step: **check the batteries**.
