Seven assembly steps (hardware item numbers in brackets). There is no support-brace step.

1. Remove the treadmill from the carton onto smooth, level ground. Read the Velcro-strap warning first: keep the treadmill flat in its using orientation while removing the strap; never stand it on its side.
2. Install six Speed Nuts (128) on the frame base; fit the Frame Base Caps (62 & 63) through the upright tubes (4 & 5); connect Lower Computer Cable (49) to Middle Computer Cable (50).
3. Fix the upright tubes (4 & 5) into the frame base (2) with eight 5/16 x 1/2 Button Head Socket Bolts (130).
4. Fix the frame base caps (61 & 62) on the frame base (2) with six 5 x 16 mm Tapping Screws (120).
5. Connect Speed Adjustment Switch Cable (54) to Speed Cable Upper (37), Incline Adjustment Switch Cable (55) to Incline Cable Upper (38), and Middle Computer Cable (50) to Upper Computer Cable (48).
6. Fix the Console Assembly (52) into the uprights with four 3/8 x 1-3/4 Button Head Socket Bolts (139) and four 10 mm Split Washers (113) using the M6 wrench (132).
7. Fix the left and right handgrip side caps over the upright tops and console support tubes with eight M5 x 10 mm Phillips Head Screws (140) and four 3.5 x 12 mm Screws (119).

Tighten all screws after all components are assembled.
