**One page, two photographs, three differences.** A black Spirit AB900 on the left, a white Xterra AIR650 on
the right, and:

| | AB900 | AIR650 |
|---|---|---|
| Blue Tooth | Yes | No |
| Iron Net Wind Cover | fitted - the shroud over the top of the fan, circled in red | none |
| Connecting Arm Cover | fitted - the cover at the foot of each connecting arm, circled in red | none |

**The service manual for the AB900 is the AU800 / AU800A book, and it marks the same two covers "(For
AU800)".** Its outline drawing labels *Iron Net Wind Cover (For AU800)* and *Connecting Arm Cover (R) (For
AU800)*, and its replacement steps open "(For AU800) Use Phillips Screw Driver to unscrew 2pcs of M6x10mm
Screws (90) to take off the Connecting Arm Cover (R.L) (143).(144)" and "(For AU800) ... to take off Iron Net
Wind Cover (142)". So the covers belong to the AU800 build and not to the AU800A build. **The sheet does not
say which of the two codes the AIR650 is, and neither does the manual**; it shows only that the white bike
has neither cover and no Bluetooth. The same manual stamps every Bluetooth page "Bluetooth function: Only
the model of Bluetooth device configuration" (`ab900-2018-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`).

**The AIR650 is an Xterra machine and this knowledge base holds no card for it.** Order the wind cover
(item 142) and the connecting arm covers (143, 144) for an AB900 only; an AIR650 was never fitted with them.
The sheet is a Dyaco page and prints no part numbers.
