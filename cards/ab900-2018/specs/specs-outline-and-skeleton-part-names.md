**Outline (p. 4):** Console Assembly, **Oval End Cap**, Handgrip Foam, Seat, **Iron Net Wind Cover
(For AU800)**, **Button Head Plug**, **Fan Net (L.R)**, **Adjustment Foot**, Pedal (R), **Seat Up/Down
Adjustment Knob**, Handgrip End Cap, **Outside Chain Cover**, **Connecting Arm Cover (R) (For AU800)**.

**Skeleton (p. 5):** Seat Handle Bar (R), Seat Handle Bar (L), **Console Support**, Fan Net, **Seat
Slider**, Sliding Seat Mount, Front Stabilizer, Rear Stabilizer, **Chainring**, **Rocker link**, Main
Frame, Crank Arm.

**"(For AU800)" marks parts fitted to one build of this book's two.** The service manual is the
AU800 / AU800A book, and the wind cover over the fan and the covers at the foot of each
connecting arm belong to the AU800 build - the Spirit AB900; the white Xterra AIR650 built from
the same book has neither (`ab900-2018-specs-parts-air650-discrepancy`). The *Rocker link* is the
pivoting arm that joins the pedal bar to the handlebar; the *Chainring* drives the fan.

**This is the only labelled drawing of the AB900 anywhere.** The owner's manual prints no parts
picture and no carton list (`ab900-2018-assembly-no-parts-list-or-labelled-diagram`); the drawing
here carries no numbers, and the replacement steps use item numbers from an exploded view the
book does not print. No dimensions or weights are given.
