The AB900 (AU800/AU800A) is chain driven — two chains, Chain Joint-100 (14) and Chain Joint-68
(15), run over the Zhonghua Drum Group — and it is the one Spirit bike whose service manual asks
for a lubricant. As printed:

1. **Use general chain oil to maintain the chain.**
2. **If there is dirt or water on the chain, wipe it clean first and then grease the chain.**

No brand, part number, quantity or interval is given. 'Oil' in item 1 and 'grease' in item 2 are
the manual's two words for the same job; it names no second product.

Chain slack is a separate adjustment with two figures — 7–12 mm on the long chain and 3–7 mm on
the short one (`ab900-2018-maintenance-chain-slack-7-to-12-mm-and-3-to-7-mm`). The owner's-manual
care regimen for the air bikes — the daily dry-off, the weekly vibration check, the monthly rust
inspection — never mentions the chain. The belt-driven Spirit bikes take no lubricant at all
(`spirit-bike-maintenance-no-lubricant-belt-or-battery-service`); do not read this card onto one.
