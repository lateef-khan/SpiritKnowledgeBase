Two chains, two figures. A chain is right when light pressure at the centre of its horizontal run
makes it sag (the manual's word is 'subsidence') by:

| Chain | Sag under light pressure at the centre | Loosen first | Adjust with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chain Joint-100 (14)** — the long chain to the fan | **7 ~ 12 mm** | 15 mm wrench on the 3/8" × 7T nut (76) on the Zhonghua Drum Group | 10 mm wrench on the M6 × 6T nyloc nut |
| **Chain Joint-68 (15)** — the short chain | **3 ~ 7 mm** | 17 mm and 15 mm wrenches on the 3/8" × 7T nut (76) on the Zhonghua Drum Group | 10 mm wrench, then tighten |

As printed under 'How to adjustment the chain when that slack':

1. Chain Joint-100 (14): use a 15 mm wrench to loosen the 3/8" × 7T nut (76) on the Zhonghua Drum
   Group, then a 10 mm wrench to adjust the M6 × 6T nyloc nut and the Chain Joint-100 tension. The
   tension is right when the chain, pressed lightly at the centre of its horizontal run, sags
   7 ~ 12 mm.
2. Chain Joint-68 (15): use 17 mm and 15 mm wrenches to loosen the 3/8" × 7T nut (76) on the
   Zhonghua Drum Group, then a 10 mm wrench to adjust the Chain Joint-68 tension and tighten. The
   tension is right at 3 ~ 7 mm of sag.

When the adjustment is finished the replacement steps say to tighten the 3/8" × 7T nut (76) on
both sides with the 15 mm wrench.

**Too tight, too loose.** The Note on the next page: if the chain is adjusted too tight it produces
a shock that can be felt — loosen it. If it is too loose the chain jumps up and down easily and
sounds louder — tighten it. The Noises Q&A (9-3) sends a chain-tension noise back to this page.

The drum-group and fan-assembly replacement steps (8-6 step 4, 8-7 step 5) set the same two figures
when the chains go back on; those are assembly cards. Oiling the chain is
`ab900-2018-maintenance-chain-lubrication`.
