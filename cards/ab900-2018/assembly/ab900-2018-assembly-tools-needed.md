Five tools, printed as a TOOLS NEEDED list:

- **#94. 10mm Wrench** (1 pc)
- **#95. 22mm Wrench** (1 pc)
- **#100. 14/15mm Wrench** (1 pc)
- **#93. Phillips Head Screw driver** (1 pc)
- **#126. M4 L Allen Wrench** (1 pc)

> **NOTE: All tools required to assemble the bike are included within the packaging,
> except for a Rubber Mallet, which is recommended when assembling the pedals onto the
> bike.**

**The rubber mallet is the only tool you must supply**, and it is recommended for the
pedals - which the assembly step otherwise says to fasten with wrench (100).

## The 22mm wrench exists for one fastener

Wrench **95** appears once, in step three, to fasten the left and right handlebars onto
the main frame over a star washer. The AB950, which has no star washer, **ships no 22mm
wrench at all** and adds a #8 L Allen wrench and a 13/15mm wrench instead. See
[that card](../../ab950-2024/assembly/ab950-2024-assembly-tools-needed.md).

## No torque wrench is supplied or asked for

The maintenance chapter lists a torque wrench among the tools an owner should keep, but
the assembly chapter neither supplies one nor prints a figure to set it to.
