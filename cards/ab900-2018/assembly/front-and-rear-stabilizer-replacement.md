1. Use the wrench (100) to fasten the **front stabilizer (2)** onto the **main frame (1)** with **2 pcs of 3/8" x 2-1/4" hex head bolts (84)** and **Ø3/8" x 2.0T flat washers (71)**.
2. Use the wrench (100) to fasten the **rear stabilizer (5)** onto the main frame (1) with **2 pcs of 3/8" x 53L carriage bolts (50)**, **Ø3/8" x 2.0T flat washers (71)** and **3/8" x 12.5T cap nuts (135)**.
3. The **adjustment foot** turns counterclockwise. Use a **14 mm wrench** to adjust the height of the four feet when the bike is not flat, until it is balanced.

The service manual prints this as a fitting procedure, not a removal; reverse it to take a stabilizer off. The owner's-manual assembly and levelling cards (linked) cover the same bolts for first assembly and the four levellers; this page adds the carriage-bolt and cap-nut names for the rear and the 14 mm wrench for the feet.
