1. Use a **14 mm wrench** to loosen both hex head bolts and remove the **seat (19)**.
2. Remove the **quick release lever (86)**, then take down the **sliding seat mount (7)**.
3. Use the **22 mm wrench (95)** to remove the **seat up/down adjustment knob (106)** and pull out the **seat slider (6)**.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.

The section heading says "Inner Slide"; the steps call the same part the seat slider (6). No bolt sizes are printed for the two seat bolts beyond the 14 mm wrench.

The owner's manual's seat-position card (the linked one) is how the user sets height and fore/aft with the pop-pin; this is the service removal.
