1. *(AU800 only)* Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **2 pcs of M6 x 10L Phillips head screws (90)** to take off the **iron net wind cover (142)**.
2. Remove the drum group and the **chain joint (100)**, then use a **10 mm wrench** to unscrew **2 pcs of M6 x 6T nyloc nuts (75)** and **2 pcs of nut stoppers**.
3. Use a **15 mm wrench** to unscrew **2 pcs of nuts** on the fan assembly.
4. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **1 pc of 5 x 19 mm tapping screw (139)**, **2 pcs of Ø8 x 5T nylon washers (138)**, **1 pc of 5 x 16 mm tapping screw (89)**, **1 pc of Ø5 x 1.5T split washer (88)** and **1 pc of Ø5 x 1.0T flat washer (87)** to take off the **fan assembly (20)** and the **fan nets (L, R)**.
5. To refit: before fitting the fan assembly, hang the **chain joint-100 (14)** on the fan assembly (20) inside the **fan nets (65L, 65R)**; install the two fan nets and the fan assembly on the **main frame (1)**; pass the chain joint-100 (14) through the fan net and onto the drum group; then use the 10 mm wrench to set the **M6 x 6T nyloc nut (75)**, the **Ø6 nut stopper (146)** and the chain joint-100 (14) tension. **Light finger pressure at the centre of the horizontal chain run should give 7 to 12 mm of sag.** When the tension is set, use the 15 mm wrench to tighten the **3/8" x 7T nut (76)** on both sides.
6. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to tighten the 5 x 19 mm tapping screw (139), the 2 pcs of Ø8 x 5T nylon washers (138), the 5 x 16 mm tapping screw (89), the Ø5 x 1.5T split washer (88) and the Ø5 x 1.0T flat washer (87) on the main frame, then install the two fan nets (65L, 65R).

**The long chain, joint-100 (14), is set to 7 to 12 mm of sag here; the short chain, joint-68 (15), is set to 3 to 7 mm in the drum-group procedure.** Two different figures for two chains.

"For AU800" marks step 1: the iron net wind cover is an AU800 part, as the discrepancy sheet against the AIR650 shows. Step 5's "hang the chain on the fan assembly inside the fan nets" is the order that matters - the chain cannot be fed through once the nets are on.
