1. *(AU800 only)* Use a Phillips screwdriver to unscrew **2 pcs of M6 x 10 mm screws (90)** to take off the **connecting arm covers (R, L) (143) (144)**.
2. Use a **10 mm wrench** and an **L Allen wrench** to release **2 pcs of M8 x 20L bolts (136)**, **2 pcs of Ø5/16" x 20 x 3.0T flat washers (81)** and **2 pcs of M6 x 6T nyloc nuts (75)** that secure the **handlebar assembly (3, 4)**, and remove the handlebar assembly (3, 4) and the **rocker link (8)**.
3. Use a **22 mm wrench** to remove the **seat handle bars (L, R)** from the **main frame (1)**.
4. Use a **21 mm socket wrench** and the **22 mm wrench** to remove **2 pcs of M14 x 10T nuts (51)** on the **pedal bar (48)** and remove the pedal bar from the seat handle bars (L, R).
5. Reverse the steps to refit.

**"For AU800" marks step 1 only.** The book covers two variants, AU800 and AU800A; the connecting arm covers (and the iron-net wind cover in the fan procedure) are AU800 parts. The AB900/AIR650 discrepancy sheet shows the AB900 with the connecting arm cover and the AIR650 without it.

**The M8 bolt is held by an M6 nyloc nut** as printed - "2pcs of M8 x 20L Bolt (136) ... and 2pcs of M6 x 6T Nyloc Nut (75)". That is what the page says; an M8 bolt does not take an M6 nut, and the page does not resolve it.

The wrench sizes 21 mm and 22 mm appear nowhere else in this book; the owner's-manual tool list for the machine is the linked assembly card.
