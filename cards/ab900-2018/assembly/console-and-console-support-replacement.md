1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to unscrew **4 pcs of M5 x 12 mm screws (58)** and the **cable (23)** to take the **console (34)** off.
2. Reverse the step to refit the console (34).
3. Use the Phillips screwdriver to unscrew **3 pcs of M6 x 10 mm screws (90)** to take the **console support (10)** off.

The AU800/AU800A service manual (the AB900) prints this as one section. The cable (23) is the RPM sensor cable that the troubleshooting chapter checks when the display shows no speed; the console has no other wiring - it runs on four AA batteries.

No refit step is printed for the console support.
