1. Remove the **outside chain cover (R) (37)** and the **outside chain cover (38)**, then use a **10 mm wrench** to unscrew **2 pcs of M6 x 6T nyloc nuts (75)**.
2. Use a **17 mm wrench** on the nut that secures the drum group, then a **15 mm wrench** to unscrew **2 pcs of 3/8" x 7T nuts (76)**.
3. Remove the drum group from the **two chains (14, 15)**.
4. To refit, pass the drum group through the two chains (14, 15) and seat it in the **main frame**. On both sides of the drum group fit the **3/8" x 25 x 3T flat washer (73)**, the **M6 x 57 mm idle wheel screw (74)** and the **3/8" x UNF26 x 7T nut (76)**, then put the two chains (14, 15) onto the drum group. Lock the **M6 x 6T nyloc nut (75)** and the M6 x 57 mm idle wheel screw (74) firmly on both sides. Use the 10 mm wrench to adjust the tension of **chain joint-68 (15)** and tighten: **light finger pressure at the centre of the horizontal chain run should give 3 to 7 mm of sag**. When the chain joint-68 (15) tension is set, use the 15 mm wrench to tighten the 3/8" x 7T nut (76) on both sides.

**Two chains, two sag figures.** The short chain, joint-68 (15), is set here to 3 to 7 mm; the long chain, joint-100 (14), is set in the fan-assembly procedure to 7 to 12 mm. The maintenance chapter repeats both figures with the note that a chain set too tight is felt as a shock and one too loose jumps and gets loud.

"Zhonghua Drum Group" is the manual's name for the hub between the two chains, printed that way in the heading and every step. The nut (76) is printed as "3/8" x 7T" in step 2 and "3/8" x UNF26 x 7T" in step 4.
