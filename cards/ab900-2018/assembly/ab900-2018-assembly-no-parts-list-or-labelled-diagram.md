**The AB900 manual has no PARTS INCLUDED list and no labelled parts drawing.**

Pre-assembly item 2 ends "verify that all parts are included in your shipment:" - with a
colon, and **nothing follows it**. The next NOTE then says "If you are missing any of the
parts listed above, inspect the packing material and the box", referring to a list that
was never printed. The 2024 AB950 manual, on the same machine family, prints both a
hardware picture list and a twelve-label drawing.

## What you can work from instead

The four assembly steps name every major part with its drawing number - main frame (1),
front stabilizer (2), left and right handlebar (3, 4), rear stabilizer (5), sliding seat
mount (7), rocker links (8), console base (10), crank arms (16L/16R), seat (19), cable
(23), console set (34), pedals (45L/45R), rod end bearings (64) and the two connecting
arm covers (143, 144). The hardware is on
[the hardware card](ab900-2018-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

**The AB950 labels are close but not identical**, and its machine has armrest handles and
pedal bars the AB900 has not; see
[that card](../../ab950-2024/assembly/ab950-2024-assembly-parts-diagram-labels.md). Do not use them as AB900 part
names.

## How this absence was established

A whole-document search of the extraction for `PARTS OF YOUR`, `PARTS INCLUDED` and the
AB950's label words returned nothing, and a 300 dpi render of every page of the assembly
chapter (PDF pp. 18-24) matched the native extraction within two words on each page - so
no page is a flattened image hiding a list. The three partly-imaged pages in this book
(PDF pp. 13-15) were recovered earlier and are Chinese and Japanese safety text and the
warning-label page, not assembly.
