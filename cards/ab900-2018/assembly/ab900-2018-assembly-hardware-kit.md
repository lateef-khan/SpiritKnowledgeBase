Screw **90** appears in two steps - three for the console base, two for the connecting
arm covers - so the kit holds five.

The **ROD END BEARINGS (64)** that step three fits are not in any hardware table; they
arrive on the rocker links.

## The star washer is what separates this kit from the AB950's

The AB950 kit has **no star washer and no 22mm wrench**. Where the AB900 seats a star
washer (148) on each handlebar before fastening it to the frame, the AB950 replaces that
row with **2 socket head cap bolts (159), M10 x 40L**, which fasten armrest handles the
AB900 does not have. Every other row above is identical in both kits.

**Do not build an AB900 from an AB950 parts list**, or the reverse - see
[that card](../../ab950-2024/assembly/ab950-2024-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## The AB900 prints no torque for any of it

The testing checklist says to tighten every bolt "to the proper torque specification (if
indicated)". None is indicated anywhere in this book.
