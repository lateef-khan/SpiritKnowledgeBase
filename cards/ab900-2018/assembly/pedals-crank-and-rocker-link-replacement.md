1. Use a **15 mm wrench** to take the **pedals (45L, 45R)** off.
2. Take the **crank arm end cap (96)** off and use a **plug wrench** to release the **M8 x 20L arbor screw (83)**.
3. Remove the crank arm and the **rocker link (8)**, the **Ø17 x 2T nylon washer (69)** and the **Ø17 x 1T flat washer (68)**.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.

**No pedal direction and no torque are printed here.** The owner's manual for this machine names the wrench and never mentions the left-hand thread (the linked card); the maintenance card carries the re-torque interval. The book's noise answer adds only that worn pedals (45) (46) are replaced, and a loose crank is tightened.

The nylon washer (69) sits with the rocker link on the crank; the order in step 3 is as printed - link, nylon washer, flat washer.
