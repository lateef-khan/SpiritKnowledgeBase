Section 9-1, `Console and Error Messages`, `Q The console without display`:

1. When there is no display, check as below:
2. Make sure the **console (34) and cables (23)** are connected properly.
3. If all are connected properly, take measurements of power outputs to find out the problem.
4. **Open the battery cover then check that the four batteries are fully charged or installed.**

**Four batteries.** The AB900 console is battery powered - there is no adaptor and no generator feeding it - so step 4 is the one that matters, and the book's matrix row for a dim display asks for **1.5 V per battery** (`ab900-2018-errors-lcd-does-not-shine-check-1-5-v-batteries`). Steps 1-3 are the same words the CU800 2020-book prints for its generator-fed console (`spirit-bike-errors-display-wont-come-on-check-computer-cable-then-meter`); step 4 is this book's own.

The book prints no error code of any kind and no `Error Messages` content under its chapter 7 heading beyond the circuit diagram, the engineering mode and the four-row lookup table. The note at the end of the same Q&A: *the console and materials have been used in the factory before the actual use of testing, and after a long time use of the failure rate is not high, so the maintenance probability is low.*
