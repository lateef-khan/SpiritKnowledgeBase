Section 9-3, `Noises`:

> The causes of noises are mostly loose screws. Sometimes it is because of parts being deformed or shifting causing rubbing or unsmooth moving.

1. **Left and right pedals (45) (46)**: mostly pedal wear. Worn pedals must be replaced.
2. **Left and right crank**: sometimes the crank gets loose. Tightening fixes it.
3. **The chain tension noise** - refer to the previous page to adjust.

**Cause 3 is the air bike's own.** The AB900 drives its fan by chain, and the page it points at (9-2, `Chain Maintenance and adjustment`) gives the two chain joints and their slack figures - 7 to 12 mm on the long chain, 3 to 7 mm on the short one - and warns that a chain adjusted too tight *will produce a shock felt* and one too loose *is easy to jump up and down and the chain will sound larger*. The adjustment itself is a maintenance procedure; the diagnosis here is only that a chain noise means the tension is off in one direction or the other.

Causes 1 and 2 are the same two the upright bikes print (`spirit-upright-errors-noises-pedals-crank-then-chain-covers-or-drive-pulley`); the AB900 has no chain cover or drive pulley entry.
