Section 9-1, printed under the heading `Q No heart rate is displayed:` - **the heading is wrong; the answer is about speed**, and the book prints no heart-rate Q&A at all (the AB900 has no handgrip sensors; its chest-strap rows are in the lookup table).

1. Console is displaying but without speed showing. **Checking the speed sensor cable and magnet to keep the gap-distance less than 3mm.**
2. If installation is OK, normally there is problem with **cables (23)**. Use another magnet to determine if there is a problem with cables (23). Either cables (23) requires replacement.

**The figure is 3 mm**, and it is the only sensor gap any Spirit bike Q&A prints. The XBR, XBU, CR800 and CU800 books give the same two steps without a gap (`spirit-bike-errors-no-speed-readout-hall-sensor-or-magnet-test-with-another-magnet`); the CU900 and CU1000ENT parts chapters set a reed switch 1 mm from its magnet on reassembly, a different machine and a different figure.

`Cables (23)` is the speed sensor with its cable - the circuit diagram of the same book labels it `RPM SENSOR (REED SWITCH)` and `SENSOR WIRE`.
