1. Press **STOP** once while running to pause: the belt decelerates slowly to a stop and the incline goes to zero percent. (The middle of this item – the held readings and the minutes-to-reset – is garbled in extraction, so only the readable ends are carded.)
2. Press **START** in Pause mode to resume; speed and incline return to their previous settings.
3. A **second** STOP press ends the program (this book mentions no workout summary); a **third** press resets the console (this book mentions no idle screen).
