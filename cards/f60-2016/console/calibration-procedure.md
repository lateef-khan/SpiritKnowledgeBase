1. In idle mode, pull off the safety key. **The display shows E0.**
2. Press and hold the **PROGRAM** key and replace the safety key. The unit enters engineering mode.
3. Press **Clear/Set** to change the set value, and use the **SPEED and INCLINE UP/DOWN** keys to make the selection. The settings appear in this order:
   1. **Metric/English** selection, for km/h or mph readings. Press Clear/Set after setting.
   2. **"0" or "1"**: 0 switches the audible beep off, 1 switches it on. Press Clear/Set after setting.
   3. **Wheel size**, set at **60**. Press Clear/Set after setting.
   4. **Minimum speed**, set at **10** for speed 1. Press Clear/Set after setting.
   5. **Maximum speed**, set at **160** for speed 16. Press Clear/Set after setting.
   6. **Incline level**, set at **10** for level 10 or **12** for level 12. Press Clear/Set after setting.
4. Press the **START** key to enter the automatic calibration.
5. The console returns to idle mode when the calibration is completed.
