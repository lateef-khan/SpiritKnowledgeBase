The Pulse (Heart Rate) readout shows current heart rate in beats per minute from **both stainless steel sensors on the side hand rails** (not the front cross bar); the value shows anytime the upper display receives a pulse signal. The owner's book prints no chest strap; the service manual documents a receiver that needs a chest belt.
