Incline may be adjusted anytime after belt movement with the **INCLINE 5/6** keys; keys **10 and 12** are also available to adjust the incline percent, and **the incline range is from 0 to 12 levels**. The display shows the incline position as it changes. The middle of the book's incline bullet is garbled in extraction (the handgrip/rapid-key wording between 'INCLINE 5/6' and '10 and 12' is unreadable), so only the printed figures are carded.
