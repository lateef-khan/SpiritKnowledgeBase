| Readout | Display range | Working range |
|---|---|---|
| SPEED | 0.0 to 99.9 Km/H | **0.5 Mi to 10 Mi (0.8 Km to 16.0 Km)**. Each press of FAST or SLOW changes it by 0.1 km/h (mph) |
| TIME | 0:00 to 99:99 | 0:00 to 99:59. Count down setup range 1:00 to 99:00 |
| DISTANCE | 00.00 to 99.99 | 1 Km (Mile) to 9 Km (Mile), each increment and decrement 1 Km (Mile) |
| CALORIES | 0000 to 9999 | 0000 to 9999 |
| PULSE | 0 to 999 | 60 to 220 |

**Incline** has no row in the manual's Function section. The button descriptions give it: each press of UP or DOWN changes the position by **0.5**, the **maximum incline position is 10** and the minimum is 0. The calibration screen offers an incline level of 10 or 12.

Time counts up or counts down. Pressing STOP in RUN Mode saves the time value, and re-entering RUN Mode continues counting from it.

Pulse comes from the hand grips or from a receiver, and the receiver needs a chest belt. **If the treadmill has no pulse signal for 8 seconds the display becomes "---".**
