**This is E6, not E5 (communication) and not E2 (over current).**

**Definition**: the lower controller is broken. Normally it is broken at the **IGBT or the MAIN IC**.

**If the console shows E6, just change the lower controller.** Only in extremely few cases is it something else, such as the program or the upper console.

| Possible cause | Things to check | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Lower controller or upper console possibly broken | Reboot the power of the treadmill. If it is the same situation, just change the lower controller | Change the lower controller |
