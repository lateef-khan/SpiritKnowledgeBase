**This is E4, not E5 (communication) and not E6 (controller fault).**

**Definition**: the motor or lower controller speed voltage is abnormal, or the motor input voltage lead is not inserted.

**Cause**: normally the motor power wires are not inserted. Less often the lower controller is bad in the speed output voltage components, or the motor itself is abnormal.

| Possible cause | Things to check | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Motor power wires aren't inserted | Check whether the motor power wires are inserted | Insert the motor power wires |
| Lower controller or motor possibly broken | If the motor power wires are already inserted, the lower controller or the motor is the likely fault | Change the lower controller or the motor |
