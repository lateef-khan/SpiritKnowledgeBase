| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The ON/OFF switch has not been switched on | Switch the AC switch on |
| The safety key is in the wrong position | Insert the safety key in the right position |
| The 5-PIN computer connector is not plugged in properly | Check the wire and connect it again |
| The 5-PIN computer cable is broken | Replace the 5-PIN computer cable |
| The fuse on the controller is blown | Replace the fuse or the controller |
| The varistor on the controller is blown | Replace the varistor or the controller |
| The safety model is broken (open circuit) | Replace the safety key model |
| Other components are faulty | Replace the console |

The reverse fault, where the treadmill displays or operates with no safety key fitted, means the safety device is shorted. Replace the safety key device or the console.

**A dim, incomplete or imperfect LCD** is a different fault: a broken LCD light, or too little power to the console. Replace the LCD or console, check the AC power is 110V or 220V, check the power to the console, and replace the lower controller.
