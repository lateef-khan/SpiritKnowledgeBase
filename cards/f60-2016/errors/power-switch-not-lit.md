| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Power cord isn't plugged into the outlet | Plug the power cord into the outlet |
| Power cord isn't plugged into the unit | Plug the power cord into the unit |
| The voltage of the outlet is too low | Check the voltage of the outlet |
| The plug or connector of the power cord is open | Replace the power cord |
| The connector of the power cord is broken | Replace the power cord |
| A connecting cable is disconnected | Check whether the wire is disconnected and connect it again |
| The breaker tripped | Press the small red button to return it to its original status |
| The breaker is broken | Replace the breaker |
| The ON/OFF switch is broken | Replace the AC switch |

A popping sound after the power is turned on means the input power is incorrect and the varistor on the controller has blown. Check the power is 110V or 220V, and replace the controller.
