| Condition | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| The speed of the belt doesn't match the console display | The console is not calibrated | Calibrate the console |
| The incline position doesn't match the console | The console is not calibrated | Calibrate the console |
| INCLINE ER, and the incline window displays "ER" | The position sensor value of the incline motor is wrong | Turn the AC switch off and turn the power on again. Then calibrate the monitor |

**Keys that do not work** are a separate fault. If the FAST/SLOW speed switch or the UP/DOWN incline switch does not work, or only one direction works, the manual blames a broken button, a damaged TXD/RXD or switch connector, or a badly connected console cable. Replace the buttons, replace the cable, or reconnect the cables and, failing that, replace the console.
