This is the check the manual gives for an E1 error.

1. Remove the motor cover hood.
2. The speed sensor is on the **left side of the frame, right next to the front roller pulley**. That pulley carries a belt that also goes to the motor. The sensor is small and black with a wire connected to it.
3. Make the sensor as close as possible to the pulley without touching it. There is a magnet on the face of the pulley; make sure the sensor is aligned with the magnet.
4. A screw holds the sensor in place. Loosen it to move the sensor, and re-tighten it when finished.

**Keep the gap between the sensor and the magnet under 3 mm.**

The part is a reed switch RPM speed sensor device.
