**WARNING: This product may contain a chemical known to the state of California to cause cancer, or birth defects or other reproductive harm.** It names no chemical and gives no information URL. It is printed once, under the table of contents on PDF page 2.

The other 24 books in this wave were searched for "state of California", "birth defects", "Proposition"/"Prop 65", letter-spaced variants and the OCR supplements: none of them prints any Proposition 65 warning. The 2026 Sole treadmill books print a different, longer warning naming Toluene and Acrylamide.
