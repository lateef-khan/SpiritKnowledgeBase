Five illustrated assembly steps (hardware item numbers in brackets). Step 1 (main base frame) is illustration only; the book prints no unpacking sentence and no Velcro-strap warning.

1. Main base frame (illustration only, no printed instruction).
2. Uprights and cables (works better with a second person): connect Lower Computer Cable (55) to Middle Computer Cable (54); insert the right and left uprights (4) and (5) into the frame base (2) and tighten eight Hex Head Bolts (99) with eight Flat Washers (100) using the 13 mm wrench (90). Do not pinch the cables: a short circuit from pinched cables is not covered under warranty.
3. Console (works better with a second person): connect Middle Computer Cable (54) to Upper Computer Cable (53), Upper Speed Cable (162) to Speed/Hand Pulse cable (28), and Upper Incline Cable (163) to Incline/Hand Pulse cable (29); fix the Console Assembly (21) into the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (125) and four Split Washers (80). Same pinch warning as step 2.
4. Console mast support: bolt the handrail support (9) between the uprights (5) and (4) with four Button Head Socket Bolts (125).
5. Final process: fix the left and right frame base covers (38) and (39) on the frame base with four Phillips Head Screws (128).
