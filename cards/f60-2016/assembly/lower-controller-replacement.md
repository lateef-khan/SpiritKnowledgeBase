1. With a Phillips head screwdriver, remove the **5 tapping screws** securing the motor cover, and remove the motor cover.
2. Disconnect all lower controller wiring. Remove the controller with a Phillips head screwdriver, fit the new lower controller and reconnect all the wiring.

A new controller has to be calibrated afterwards.
