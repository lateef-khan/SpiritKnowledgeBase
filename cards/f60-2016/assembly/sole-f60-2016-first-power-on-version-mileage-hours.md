First power-on in the F60 2016 book is a single sentence with no plug-in instruction:

When the power is turned on, the console will show the current software version, the total running mileage, hours. The treadmill will then enter idle mode, which is the starting point for operation.

Unlike every other A1 book, this manual prints no "plug into a wall outlet and turn on the power switch below the motor hood" sentence anywhere: a full-text search finds no outlet instruction outside the safety pages, so the missing first-power-on plug step is the book's omission, not an extraction gap.
