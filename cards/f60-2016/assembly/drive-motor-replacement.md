1. With a Phillips head screwdriver, remove the **5 tapping screws** securing the motor cover.
2. Remove the motor grounding wire (greenish yellow), the motor wire (+) red and the motor wire (-) black.
3. With a **14mm T-shaped socket wrench** loosen the **4 screws**, and with a **14mm open end wrench** loosen the **1 belt tension screw**. Remove the motor and fit the new one.
4. Reassemble in the reverse order, re-hook the belt and readjust it to be parallel with the main frame. **Do not tighten the 4 securing screws yet.**
5. With the **14mm open end wrench**, adjust the belt tension using a tension measuring device. **Adjust to the white LBS area, between 70 and 75 LBS.** Then use the 14mm T-shaped socket wrench to secure and tighten the 4 securing screws.
6. Connect the grounding wires and the motor wires: **red to M+, black to M-**.
