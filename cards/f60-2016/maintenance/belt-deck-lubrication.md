The treadmill needs little maintenance other than periodic lubricant. Lubricating under the tread belt gives better performance and a longer life.

**How to check**: lift one side of the tread belt and feel the top surface of the treadboard.

- **Slick to the touch** - no further lubrication is required.
- **Dry to the touch** - apply one packet of lubricant, or half of the bottle.

**How to apply**

1. Lift one side of the tread belt.
2. Pour **one half of the lubricant bottle** under the centre of the tread belt, on the top surface of the treadboard.
3. Walk on the treadmill at a slow speed for **3 to 5 minutes** to spread the lubricant evenly.

**Do not over lubricate the treadboard.** Wipe off any excess that comes out. **Use only half the bottle of lubricant per application.**

**Lubrication schedule**

| When | Amount |
|---|---|
| After the first **25 hours** of use (2 to 3 months) | one half bottle |
| Every **50 hours** of use (5 to 8 months) | one half bottle |
