The F60-2016 owner's book is the only one in the wave that wipes **every other month** instead of monthly, so it gets its own card rather than joining the monthly-wipe card.

With a **soft, damp cloth or paper towel**, wipe the edge of the belt and the area between the belt edge and the frame. Reach as far as practical directly under the belt edge. Do this **once every other month** to extend belt and deck life. **Use water only, no cleaners and no abrasives.** A mild soap and water solution with a nylon scrub brush cleans the top of the textured belt. **ALLOW THE BELT TO DRY BEFORE USING.**

Belt dust occurs during normal break-in, until the belt stabilises; wiping the excess off with a damp cloth keeps the buildup down.

The monthly vacuum under the treadmill and the every-other-month motor-hood vacuum for this machine are on the extended `f80-2026-vacuuming-and-general-cleaning` card. The maintenance chapter of this book prints the sibling-brand name ("FUEL Fitness approved lubricant", "contact FUEL Fitness"); the book itself is Sole's throughout (cover, safety pages, warranty), and this card follows that identity.
