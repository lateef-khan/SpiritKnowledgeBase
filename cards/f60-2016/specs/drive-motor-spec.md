A **variable speed DC motor**: 0 to 90 volt DC for 120V, or 0 to 180 volt DC for 220V. The main controller varies that voltage to raise or lower the speed of the running belt. The higher the voltage, the faster the motor turns.

**Three wires: red, black and green.**

- DC voltage on the **red (white)** wire (**M+**) turns the motor **clockwise**.
- DC voltage on the **black** wire (**M-**) turns the motor **counter-clockwise**.
- The **green** wire is ground.

The main controller carries the DC power supply for the console, the motor driver control circuit and the incline control circuit.
