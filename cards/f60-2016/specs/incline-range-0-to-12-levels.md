**The F60 incline range is 0 to 12 levels (percent):** the F60-2016 book says "The incline range is from 0 to 12 levels", and its calibration page sets "Incline level ... at 10 for level 10 or 12 for level 12". The stencil-era F60-2020 book prints the same span on its console page ("from 0 to 12") and in engineering mode ("incline (12)"). This is lower than the 15 the 2011-2015 generation allows, so it keeps its own card.
