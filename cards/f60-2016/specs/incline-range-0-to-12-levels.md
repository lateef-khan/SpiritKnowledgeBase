**The F60-2016 incline range is 0 to 12 levels (percent):** "The incline range is from 0 to 12 levels", and the calibration page sets "Incline level ... at 10 for level 10 or 12 for level 12". This is lower than the 15 the 2011-2015 generation allows, so it has its own card.
