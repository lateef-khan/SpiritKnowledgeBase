Besides the normal version this treadmill has a **CE/GS version**. Both versions are exactly the same in functions and outlines, except:

- the power input is **110AC for the normal version** versus **230VAC for the CEGS version**, and
- an additional **filter choke** circuit is added for the CEGS version.

The manual carries two circuit diagrams for this reason, one for 110V and one for 220V/CEGS. The cover of the manual reads "110~120V & 220~230V".
