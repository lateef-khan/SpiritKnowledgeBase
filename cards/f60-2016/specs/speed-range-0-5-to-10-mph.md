**The F60-2016 calibration allows 0.5 minimum and 10 maximum speed (mph):** "Minimum speed is set at 5 for speed 0.5" and "Maximum speed is set at 100 for speed 10". Its QUICK SPEED keys (1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10) top out at 10, agreeing with the calibration maximum. This is lower than the 12 mph calibration maximum the 2011-2015 generation prints, so it has its own card.
