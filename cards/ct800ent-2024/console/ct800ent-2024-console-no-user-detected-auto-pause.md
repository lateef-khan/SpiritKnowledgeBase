**The machine will pause automatically if no user is detected for 30 seconds.**

- The mechanism is active **when the treadmill is running at a speed higher than 0.8 km/hr
  (0.5 mile/hr)**.
- A **pop-up window asks whether to stop or resume the workout program**.
- **That window stays on the screen for 5 minutes.**
- **If nothing is done within those 5 minutes the treadmill ends the workout automatically.**

**This is a new feature of the 2024 ENT treadmills.** No earlier Spirit treadmill manual - LED or
touchscreen - describes a no-user detection of any kind, and neither of the two 2024 LED books
(CT800-2024, CT850-2024) prints this paragraph. Do not tell an owner of any other machine that their
treadmill should be doing this.

**The manual does not say how the user is detected.** It names no sensor, no mat, no handrail contact
and no infrared device, and it does not say what happens to speed and incline while the pop-up is
showing.

**This is not the ordinary pause.** Pressing Stop once decelerates the belt, drops the incline to
zero and holds the readings for 5 minutes (`ct850-2020-pause-stop-reset`), and finishing a workout
deliberately takes two presses of Stop or the on-screen Pause icon and Finish
(`cu800ent-2024-console-ending-a-workout`). **It is not sleep mode either**, which is a 30-minute
display timeout on an idle console (`ct900ent-sleep-mode-auto-shutoff`).

**The 30 seconds and the 5 minutes are different clocks.** Thirty seconds with nobody on the belt
raises the pop-up; five minutes without an answer ends the workout.
