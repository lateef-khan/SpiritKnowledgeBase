These are the callout numbers on the exploded view drawing. The drawing is a
picture with bare numbers on it and no text, so the table below is the only
readable form of it.

**147 rows are printed.** The item numbers that appear are 1-29, 31-70, 72, 75, 77-82, 85-94, 101-109, 111-117, 119-140, 142-148, 150-156, 158-164, 166. Any number outside that run is **not printed in this manual at all** - 30, 71, 73, 74, 76, 83, 84, 95, 96, 97, 98, 99, 100, 110, 118, 141, 149, 157, 165 are absent, not a missing page.

**This is the 2024 CT800ENT list. The 2022 CT800ENT list has 148 items and is a
different document** - see below before quoting a number.

## What changed from the 2022 CT800ENT list

**Item 118 is gone**, taking the list from 148 rows to 147. Nine further rows
changed:

| Item | CT800ENT-2022 | CT800ENT-2024 |
|---|---|---|
| 62 | **250 mm** connecting wire (white) | **500 mm** connecting wire (white) |
| 63 | **250 mm** connecting wire (black) | **500 mm** connecting wire (black) |
| 79 | 900 mm **CSAFE** connecting cable (upper) | 900 mm **Network** connecting cable (upper) |
| 80 | 2400 mm **CSAFE** connecting cable (lower) | 2400 mm **Network** connecting cable (lower) |
| 81 | 900 mm connecting wire (upper) | 900 mm **CSAFE** connecting wire (upper) |
| 82 | 2400 mm connecting wire (lower) | 2400 mm **CSAFE** connecting wire (lower) |
| 131 | M3 x **50 mm** Phillips head screw | M3 x **40 mm** Phillips head screw |
| 139 | 5 x **20 mm** tapping screw | 5 x **19 mm** tapping screw |
| 142 | M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screw, **qty 12** | same screw, **qty 14** |
| 143 | Ø5 x 1.5T split washer, **qty 10** | same washer, **qty 12** |
| 118 | 3/8" x UCN16 x 2" socket head cap bolt (33L), qty 4 | **not printed** |

**Items 79 to 82 are the trap.** The four cables kept their numbers and swapped
their names: what the 2022 book called a CSAFE cable at 79 and 80, the 2024 book
calls a Network cable, and what it called a plain connecting wire at 81 and 82 it
now calls a CSAFE connecting wire. Ordering "item 79, the CSAFE cable" against
the wrong book gets the wrong wire.

## What tells this list apart from the CT850ENT-2024 list

- **Only this list has item 42 Hall Sensor Rack, 43 Hall Sensor, 54 DC digital
  control, 69 1300 mm Sensor W/Cable, 91, 92, 147 and 148**, and its item 55 is
  `Drive Motor`.
- **Only the CT850ENT-2024 list has item 73 Inverter Plate, 74 Inverter, 76
  300 mm Ground Wire and 110**, and its item 55 is `AC Motor`.
- Item 108 is qty 12 here and 8 there; item 117 qty 4 here and 2 there; item 139
  qty 25 here and 23 there.

**Item 86 is a Power Adaptor**, the part the LED-console CT800-2024 does not
have; the entertainment console is mains-powered rather than run from the
treadmill's own supply. There is no `TV Adapter (5C2V)` at item 71 in this list -
that item belongs to the LED books.

**No motor rating is printed.** Item 55 `Drive Motor` and item 66 `Incline Motor`
carry no horsepower or wattage. See `spirit-ct800-specs-no-specification-table`.

**The exploded view drawing is a flat image dated `20240311`** at its foot, six
months before the manual; the number is legible only from a 300 dpi render.

source also carries an OCR supplement for each of these pages; the supplement is
garbled and **is not the source of any figure on this card**. Where the two
disagree, the native text layer is right.
