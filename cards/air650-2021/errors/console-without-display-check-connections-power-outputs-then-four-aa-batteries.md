**The AIR650 has no service manual of its own.** The manufacturer's service note for it reads *See Spirit AB900 Service Manual* and lists the only differences: the AIR650 has **no Bluetooth**, no iron-net wind cover and no connecting-arm covers. Everything below is the AB900 book's text, applied to the AIR650 by way of that note.


Section 9-1, *Console and Error Messages*, *Q: The console without display*:

1. When there is no display, check as below:
2. Make sure the **console (34) and cables (23)** are connected properly.
3. If all are connected properly, take measurements of power outputs to find out the problem.
4. **Open the battery cover, then check that the four batteries are fully charged or installed.**

**Four AA batteries, and nothing else feeds the console.** The AIR650 owner's manual confirms it: *the
console operates on 4 x AA batteries (not included); the battery compartment is on the back side of the
console*. There is no adaptor and no generator, so step 4 is the one that usually matters, and a dim rather
than dead display has its own row asking for 1.5 V per cell
(`air650-2021-errors-lcd-does-not-shine-backlight-or-batteries-at-1-5-v-each`). The owner's manual also
puts the console to sleep after 60 seconds of inactivity - a dark screen wakes when you pedal or press a key.
