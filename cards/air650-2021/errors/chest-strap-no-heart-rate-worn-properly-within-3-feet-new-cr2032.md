**The AIR650 has no service manual of its own.** The manufacturer's service note for it reads *See Spirit AB900 Service Manual* and lists the only differences: the AIR650 has **no Bluetooth**, no iron-net wind cover and no connecting-arm covers. Everything below is the AB900 book's text, applied to the AIR650 by way of that note.


The book's *Troubleshooting Quick Lookup Table*, third and fourth rows:

| Happening | Caused | Processing Step |
|---|---|---|
| Wireless heartbeat has no effect (the heartbeat value is not displayed on the monitor) | 1. The heart rate strap is not properly worn. 2. The distance is too far from the receiver. 3. The battery of the heart rate strap is low or has no voltage. | 1. Check that the strap is in proper contact with the skin and in the right direction. 2. Keep the chest strap **within 3 feet** of the console. 3. Replace with a new lithium battery, type **CR2032**. |
| Wireless heartbeat reception is too short (must be very close to the console) | The battery is low. | Replace with a new lithium battery, type CR2032. |

**3 feet and a CR2032.** The AIR650 owner's manual agrees on the cell - *the replacement battery is Panasonic
CR2032*, with an estimated transmitter life of 2500 hours - and adds that the strap is not included with the
bike. A reading that is present but wrong is the row above these
(`air650-2021-errors-heart-rate-value-incorrect-another-strap-interference-or-receiver`).

The Spirit card for these rows across the commercial range is `spirit-wireless-chest-belt-no-pulse`; the
Xterra treadmill matrix prints them as
`xterra-treadmill-errors-wireless-pulse-not-working-chest-belt-3-feet-cr2032`.
