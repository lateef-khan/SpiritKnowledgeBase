"The TARGET HEART RATE program allows you to be able to set the BPM range for maximum
cardiovascular training. This range will depend on your age and the program will calculate
the maximum heart rate value as the optimized target (**between 65% - 85% of maximum
BPM**)." The book adds that the range is based on average values and your own health and
weight may make it different; speak with a trusted medical advisor.

**Launch:** press the Target Heart Rate button. **Setup:** enter your **Age** with the
up/down arrows and press ENTER. **Start:** the fields show progress; "the arrow keys will
display to direct you to work harder or work less". **End:** the program ends as you stop
pedaling; STOP ends it at any time, and a workout summary is displayed.

The console needs a **wireless chest strap (not included)** - the BPM shows in the
three-digit field with a flashing heart, or "- - -" with no signal. When your heart rate is
in the target range the Target Heart Rate icon is active.
