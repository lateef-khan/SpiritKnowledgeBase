**Read from the AB900 service manual by way of the AIR650 service note**, which says *See Spirit AB900 Service Manual* and lists the differences: no Bluetooth, no iron-net wind cover, no connecting-arm cover (`ab900-2018-specs-parts-air650-discrepancy`). The AB900 book's chapter 1, *Outlines*, is the only labelled drawing of this frame anywhere - the AIR650 owner's manual prints no parts picture and no list (`xterra-specs-parts-which-bike-elliptical-and-climber-manuals-print-an-exploded-view-and-parts-list`).

**Outline (AB900 book PDF p. 4)** - the parts the AIR650 shares: Console Assembly, Oval End Cap, Handgrip Foam, Seat, Button Head Plug, Fan Net (L.R), Adjustment Foot, Pedal (R), Seat Up/Down Adjustment Knob, Handgrip End Cap, Outside Chain Cover. **Two callouts on the same drawing are marked *(For AU800)* and are not on the AIR650**: *Iron Net Wind Cover (For AU800)* - the shroud over the top of the fan - and *Connecting Arm Cover (R) (For AU800)* - the cover at the foot of each connecting arm. The service note's photographs circle exactly those two on the black AB900 and show the white AIR650 without them; do not order them for an AIR650.

**Skeleton (AB900 book PDF p. 5):** Seat Handle Bar (R), Seat Handle Bar (L), Console Support, Fan Net, Seat Slider, Sliding Seat Mount, Front Stabilizer, Rear Stabilizer, Chainring, Rocker link, Main Frame, Crank Arm. The *Rocker link* is the pivoting arm that joins the pedal bar to the handlebar; the *Chainring* drives the fan.

Neither drawing carries a number, a part number or a dimension (`ab900-2018-specs-outline-and-skeleton-part-names`). The electronic parts on the same frame are a console and an RPM sensor (`air650-2021-specs-parts-electronic-parts-console-and-rpm-sensor-no-tool-list`).
