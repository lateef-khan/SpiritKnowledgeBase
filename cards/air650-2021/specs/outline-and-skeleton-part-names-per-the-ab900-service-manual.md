**These names come from the Spirit AB900 service manual.** The manufacturer's one-page note for the AIR650 says
"See Spirit AB900 Service Manual" and lists the differences: **Blue Tooth: No**, and the **Iron Net Wind Cover** and
**Connecting Arm Cover** that are circled on the AB900 photograph and absent from the AIR650 photograph. The
service manual marks those two covers "(For AU800)", the black Spirit build, so on an AIR650 they do not exist and
must not be ordered.

**Outline (p. 4), minus the two AU800-only covers:** Console Assembly, Oval End Cap, Handgrip Foam, Seat, Button Head
Plug, Fan Net (L, R), Adjustment Foot, Pedal (R), Seat Up/Down Adjustment Knob, Handgrip End Cap, Outside Chain
Cover.

**Skeleton (p. 5):** Seat Handle Bar (R), Seat Handle Bar (L), Console Support, Fan Net, Seat Slider, Sliding Seat
Mount, Front Stabilizer, Rear Stabilizer, Chainring, Rocker link, Main Frame, Crank Arm.

The *Rocker link* is the pivoting arm joining the pedal bar to the handlebar; the *Chainring* drives the fan through
two chains. The AIR650 owner's manual uses its own numbers for the same parts - main frame (1), front stabilizer (2),
left and right handlebar (3, 4), rear stabilizer (5), sliding seat mount (7), rocker link (8), console base (10),
crank arms (16L, 16R), seat (19), computer cable (23), console set (34), pedals (45L, 45R), rod end bearing (64) -
and the service manual's replacement steps use that numbering too. Neither book prints a dimension or a weight.
The Spirit twin is `ab900-2018-specs-outline-and-skeleton-part-names`.
