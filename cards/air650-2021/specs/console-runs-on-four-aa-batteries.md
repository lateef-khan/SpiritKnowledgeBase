**"The console operates on 4*AA batteries (not included). The battery compartment is on the back side of the
console."** The assembly step says to "add in 4 AA batteries before installing" the console on its base, because
the compartment is on the back. There is no adapter, no mains lead and no power switch; the four cells are the whole
power supply.

The Spirit AB900 service manual, which the manufacturer's note says covers the AIR650, draws the same supply on its
block diagram ("AA * 4 PCS Batteries") and tells a technician facing a dim or incomplete LCD to "check whether the
battery is 1.5V/each" - a fresh alkaline AA. The Spirit twin is `spirit-bike-console-air-bike-four-aa-batteries`.

The chest-strap transmitter's own cell, a Panasonic CR2032 with an estimated 2500-hour life, is a separate item and
the strap is not included.
