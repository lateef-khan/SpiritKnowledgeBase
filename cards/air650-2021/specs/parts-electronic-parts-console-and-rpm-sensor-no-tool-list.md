**The AIR650 has no service manual of its own.** Its two-page service note says *See Spirit AB900 Service Manual* and lists the only differences: the AIR650 has **no Bluetooth**, and no iron-net wind cover or connecting-arm covers (`ab900-2018-specs-parts-air650-discrepancy`). Everything below is therefore read from the AB900 book by way of that note, with the Bluetooth build left out.

**Chapter 2, Electronic Parts (AB900 book PDF p. 7), has two callouts: CONSOLE and RPM SENSOR.** There is no Upper Controllers / Lower Controller split because there is nothing to put under the second heading - an air bike has no motor, no brake board and no controller. The console runs on **four AA batteries**; its inputs are the RPM sensor (a two-pin reed switch) and a wireless heart-rate receiver (`ab900-2018-specs-console-wiring-four-aa-batteries-and-2-pin-reed-switch-rpm-sensor`). The AB900 book's third input, a Bluetooth module on the *Bluetooth device configuration* build, is what the AIR650 does not have (`ab900-2018-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`).

**The book prints no tool list and no part number.** The AB900 book has no *Tools Required* page - the Xterra treadmill service manuals name a multi-meter and the SB600 book five tools (`xterra-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`, `sb600-2023-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-five-tools-and-grease`); the wrench sizes the AB900 replacement steps call for stay with those steps. The item numbers those steps cite (the wind cover is 142, the connecting-arm covers 143 and 144) key to an exploded view the AB900 book does not print, and the AIR650 owner's manual prints neither a drawing nor a list (`xterra-specs-parts-which-bike-elliptical-and-climber-manuals-print-an-exploded-view-and-parts-list`). What the AIR650 owner's manual does show the customer - the console face, the assembly pack - is on its console and assembly cards.
