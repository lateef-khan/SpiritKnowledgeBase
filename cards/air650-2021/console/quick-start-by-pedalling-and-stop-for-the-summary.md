The **quick start** option begins when you get on the bike and begin pedalling - no need to select a program. The console shows all data, including your heart rate as long as a signal is detected. **As the pedals stop moving, the mode ends.** Pressing STOP also ends the mode and shows the workout summary ([reset and summary card](reset-by-holding-stop-2-seconds-and-the-workout-summary.md)).

The seven program keys and what they set up are on the Programs cards.
