**Sleep Mode.** After **60 seconds of inactivity** the console goes into power-saving Sleep Mode and the display switches off. Rotate the pedals or press any button to launch Ready Mode.

**Ready Mode.** Tells you the console is powered on and ready to start a program; if no program is selected and the pedals are not touched, after 60 seconds it drops back into Sleep Mode.

**Pause Mode.** While any program is running, pressing **START** launches Pause Mode for **5 minutes**. The console **beeps every 30 seconds** and the display flashes as a reminder. With no input in 5 minutes the pause ends and the console reverts to Ready Mode, ending the program. Press START to continue the running mode; press STOP to quit to Ready Mode.

Start is the pause key - there is no separate pause button. The Spirit AB900 service manual adds that sleep counts 60 seconds "without pressing any key, RPM signal, heartbeat signal input", so a transmitting chest strap keeps the console awake, as pedalling does. The service facts on this card come from the **Spirit AB900 service manual**: the Xterra AIR650 service note says "See Spirit AB900 Service Manual" and lists only two differences, no Bluetooth and different wind and connecting-arm covers, so everything else in that book is taken to describe the AIR650 console.
