**Units, as the owner's manual prints it.** The factory sets distance to display in **miles**. In Ready Mode press and hold the **START and ENTER keys for two (2) seconds** until KM or M is flashing, press the up or down arrow to select metric (KM) or imperial (M), then press ENTER to confirm. Speed and distance both follow this one setting.

**The same hold is the engineering-mode door.** The Spirit AB900 service manual: long-press Start and Enter for 2 seconds, the buzzer rings for 1 second and DISTANCE flashes; the up and down keys then step through **distance -> sound -> odo -> distance**, and ENTER opens the flashing entry.

| Entry | What it does |
|---|---|
| DISTANCE | M or KM flashes (whichever is current); up/down switches it, ENTER confirms and returns to the menu |
| SOUND | ON or OFF flashes in the watt window; up/down switches the buzzer, ENTER confirms |
| ODO | The large window shows "odo", the time window the cumulative workout time and the distance window the cumulative distance. Past 1:59:59 the time switches to hours (H); past 99.99 the distance drops the decimal point |

With no action for 30 seconds the console returns to standby; the Stop key also leaves an entry. A keypad reset does not clear these settings or the odometer, **but removing the batteries restores the factory settings and zeroes the odometer** ([battery card](four-aa-batteries-not-included-and-what-removing-them-resets.md)). There is no calibration entry, no error log and no software version in this menu. The service facts on this card come from the **Spirit AB900 service manual**: the Xterra AIR650 service note says "See Spirit AB900 Service Manual" and lists only two differences, no Bluetooth and different wind and connecting-arm covers, so everything else in that book is taken to describe the AIR650 console. The Bluetooth-key behaviour on the same page is omitted because the AIR650 has none.
