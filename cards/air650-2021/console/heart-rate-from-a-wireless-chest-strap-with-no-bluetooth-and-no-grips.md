**Three things the book settles.** The heart-rate panel is headed "Chest Strap Not Included"; the reading comes "when console detects a signal from a **wireless monitor**"; and the book names no hand-pulse grips anywhere - the handlebars are moving arms, and the words "hand pulse" and "grip" do not appear.

**What kind of strap.** The Spirit AB900 service manual's block diagram draws the receiver as "WIRELESS Heart Rate (POLAR)" - the 5 kHz analogue type. The same diagram shows a "BLUETOOTH (For App)" module, but the AIR650 service note lists **"Blue Tooth: No"** as the first difference between the AB900 and the AIR650, so a Bluetooth-only strap will not pair with this console. The service facts on this card come from the **Spirit AB900 service manual**: the Xterra AIR650 service note says "See Spirit AB900 Service Manual" and lists only two differences, no Bluetooth and different wind and connecting-arm covers, so everything else in that book is taken to describe the AIR650 console.

The service manual's quick table for heart-rate trouble: a wrong value can be another strap nearby, electrical noise (turn the machine) or a damaged receiver; no value at all is a badly worn strap, being more than about 3 feet from the console, or a flat strap battery (CR2032). Those rows belong to the Errors cards. How to wear the strap is on [the strap card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-chest-strap-not-included-with-a-cr2032-battery.md), and the interference list on [the erratic card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-erratic-heart-rate-and-the-seven-interference-sources.md).
