**The console operates on 4 x AA batteries (not included).** The battery compartment is on the back side of the console; the assembly step says to add the four AA batteries before mounting the console. That is the whole power system - the book describes no mains lead, no power switch and no generator.

**Removing the batteries is a factory reset.** The Spirit AB900 service manual's engineering-mode page says the console does not clear its settings or the odometer when reset from the keypad, "but the original setting value will be restored after the battery is unplugged, and the Odo cleared to zero." Its block diagram draws the power as "AA * 4 PCS Batteries", and its troubleshooting table's check for a dim or incomplete LCD is whether each battery still reads 1.5 V. The service facts on this card come from the **Spirit AB900 service manual**: the Xterra AIR650 service note says "See Spirit AB900 Service Manual" and lists only two differences, no Bluetooth and different wind and connecting-arm covers, so everything else in that book is taken to describe the AIR650 console.

The keypad reset (hold STOP 2 seconds) keeps the units setting ([reset card](reset-by-holding-stop-2-seconds-and-the-workout-summary.md)). A blank display is usually Sleep Mode, not dead batteries ([sleep card](sleep-ready-and-pause-modes-with-60-second-and-5-minute-timers.md)).
