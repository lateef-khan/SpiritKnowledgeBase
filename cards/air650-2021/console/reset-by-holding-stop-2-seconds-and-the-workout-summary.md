**Reset.** To reset all saved settings (**except the metric or imperial units setting**), press and hold **STOP for 2 seconds**. The Spirit AB900 service manual describes the same "total RESET": in any mode hold Stop for 2 seconds; the LCD flashes once over 2 seconds and the buzzer rings for 2 seconds; the console returns to the standby screen with all settings back to default. The odometer survives this reset; only removing the batteries clears it ([battery card](four-aa-batteries-not-included-and-what-removing-them-resets.md)). The service facts on this card come from the **Spirit AB900 service manual**: the Xterra AIR650 service note says "See Spirit AB900 Service Manual" and lists only two differences, no Bluetooth and different wind and connecting-arm covers, so everything else in that book is taken to describe the AIR650 console.

**Workout summary.** Right after a workout completes the console shows a brief summary - total time, total distance and total watts. Pressing STOP during quick start also ends the mode and shows the summary ([quick start card](quick-start-by-pedalling-and-stop-for-the-summary.md)).
