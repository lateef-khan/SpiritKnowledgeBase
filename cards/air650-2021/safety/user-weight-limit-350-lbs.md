**This is the 350 lb figure.** The AIR650 Airbike Pro book prints it once, as the last item of its
eleven-item safety list:

> 11. Weight Limit: 350 lbs.

There is no specification table and the warranty page prints no capacity.

**The AIR350, the smaller air bike with the same eleven-item list, is 250 lbs**
(`xterra-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-250-lbs`); the two lists differ only in this line.

The AIR650's service manual is the Spirit AB900's, by the manufacturer's note, and the AB900's own
safeguards page also sets 350 lb (`spirit-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lb`) - a Spirit card, cited
here only as agreement, not as a source for this machine.
