**These figures come from the Spirit AB900 service manual by way of the manufacturer's AIR650 service
note** ("See Spirit AB900 Service Manual"; the only differences are Bluetooth, the wind cover and the
connecting-arm cover). The drive is the same two-chain drum group.

A chain is right when light pressure at the centre of its horizontal run makes it sag by:

| Chain | Sag | Loosen first | Adjust with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chain Joint-100 (14)** - the long chain to the fan | **7 to 12 mm** | 15 mm wrench on the 3/8" x 7T nut (76) on the Zhonghua Drum Group | 10 mm wrench on the M6 x 6T nyloc nut |
| **Chain Joint-68 (15)** - the short chain | **3 to 7 mm** | 17 mm and 15 mm wrenches on the 3/8" x 7T nut (76) | 10 mm wrench, then tighten |

As printed: for the Chain Joint-100, use a 15 mm wrench to loosen the 3/8" x 7T nut (76) on the drum group,
then a 10 mm wrench to adjust the M6 x 6T nyloc nut and the chain tension; the tension is right when the
chain, pressed lightly at the centre of its horizontal run, sags 7~12 mm. For the Chain Joint-68, use 17 mm
and 15 mm wrenches to loosen the same nut (76), then a 10 mm wrench to adjust the tension and tighten; right
at 3~7 mm of sag.

**Too tight, too loose** (the Note on the next page): a chain adjusted too tight produces a shock you can
feel - loosen it. Too loose, and the chain jumps up and down easily and sounds louder - tighten it. The
service manual's noise Q&A sends a chain-tension noise back to this adjustment
(`ab900-2018-errors-noises-pedals-crank-or-chain-tension` is the Spirit card).
