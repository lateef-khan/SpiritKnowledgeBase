**This figure comes from the Spirit AB900 service manual by way of the manufacturer's AIR650 service
note**, which says the AIR650 is serviced from that book and differs from the AB900 only in having no
Bluetooth and a different iron-net wind cover and connecting-arm cover. The chains are not on that list.

As printed under "How to maintenance the chain":

1. **Use general chain oil to maintain the chain.**
2. **If there is dirt or water on the chain, wipe it clean first and then grease the chain.**

No brand, part number, quantity or interval is given; "oil" and "grease" are the manual's two words for
the same job. The air bike has two chains - the long Chain Joint-100 to the fan and the short Chain
Joint-68 - and both are oiled the same way. Slack is a separate adjustment with its own figures:
`air650-2021-maintenance-chain-slack-7-to-12-mm-on-the-long-chain-and-3-to-7-mm-on-the-short-chain`.

The AIR650 owner's manual itself prints no maintenance, cleaning or lubrication page - its only care
lines are the Final Preparations (adjust the four levelers, tighten the pedals). Checked by loose-word
grep and page by page; nothing is imaged. The AIR350 has no service note and no chain instruction at all.

The Spirit card for the same page is `ab900-2018-maintenance-chain-lubrication`; the part differences
that make the note necessary are `ab900-2018-specs-parts-air650-discrepancy`.
