**The Xterra AIR650 has no service manual of its own.** Its two-page service note says "See Spirit AB900 Service Manual" and lists the only differences: no Bluetooth, no iron net wind cover, no connecting arm covers. The steps below are that book's, applied to the AIR650.

1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to unscrew **4 pcs of M5 x 12 mm screws (58)** and the **cable (23)** to take the **console (34)** off.
2. Reverse the step to refit the console (34).
3. Use the Phillips screwdriver to unscrew **3 pcs of M6 x 10 mm screws (90)** to take the **console support (10)** off.

The cable (23) is the RPM sensor cable; the console runs on four AA batteries and has no other wiring. No refit step is printed for the console support - it is the owner's-manual step 2 in reverse.
