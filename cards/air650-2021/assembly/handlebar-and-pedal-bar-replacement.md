From the Spirit AB900 service manual, which the AIR650 service note names as its own. **Its step 1 does not apply: it removes the connecting arm covers (R, L) (143, 144), marked "(For AU800)", and the AIR650 has none** - the discrepancy sheet shows the white bike without them.

1. Use a **10 mm wrench** and an **L Allen wrench** to release **2 pcs of M8 x 20L bolts (136)**, **2 pcs of 5/16" x 20 x 3.0T flat washers (81)** and **2 pcs of M6 x 6T nyloc nuts (75)** that secure the **handlebar assembly (3, 4)**, and remove the handlebar assembly (3, 4) and the **rocker link (8)**.
2. Use a **22 mm wrench** to remove the **seat handle bars (L, R)** from the **main frame (1)**.
3. Use a **21 mm socket wrench** and the 22 mm wrench to remove **2 pcs of M14 x 10T nuts (51)** on the **pedal bar (48)** and remove the pedal bar from the seat handle bars (L, R).
4. Reverse the steps to refit.

The M8 bolt held by an M6 nyloc nut is as printed in the Spirit book and in the AIR650 owner's manual; neither resolves it. The 21 mm socket is not in the owner's-manual tool kit.
