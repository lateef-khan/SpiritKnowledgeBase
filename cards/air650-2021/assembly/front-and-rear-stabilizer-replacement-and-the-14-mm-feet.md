From the Spirit AB900 service manual, which the AIR650 service note names as its own.

1. Use the wrench (100) to fasten the **front stabilizer (2)** onto the **main frame (1)** with **2 pcs of 3/8" x 2-1/4" hex head bolts (84)** and **3/8" x 2.0T flat washers (71)**.
2. Use the wrench (100) to fasten the **rear stabilizer (5)** onto the main frame (1) with **2 pcs of 3/8" x 53L carriage bolts (50)**, **3/8" x 2.0T flat washers (71)** and **3/8" x 12.5T cap nuts (135)**.
3. **The adjustment foot turns counterclockwise. Use a 14 mm wrench to adjust the height of the four feet when the bike is not flat, until it is balanced.**

The service page prints this as a fitting procedure, not a removal; reverse it to take a stabilizer off. The AIR650 owner's manual step 1 covers the same bolts for first assembly (printed as 3/8" x 2" carriage bolts there, 3/8" x 53L here); the 14 mm wrench for the feet is printed only on this page - the owner's manual says "adjust all four levelers" and names no tool.
