From the Spirit AB900 service manual, which the AIR650 service note names as its own. **Its step 1 does not apply: it removes the iron net wind cover (142) with two M6 x 10L screws (90), marked "(For AU800)", and the AIR650 has no wind cover** - the discrepancy sheet shows the white bike without one.

1. Remove the drum group and the **chain joint (100)**, then use a **10 mm wrench** to unscrew **2 pcs of M6 x 6T nyloc nuts (75)** and **2 pcs of nut stoppers**.
2. Use a **15 mm wrench** to unscrew **2 pcs of nuts** on the fan assembly.
3. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **1 pc of 5 x 19 mm tapping screw (139)**, **2 pcs of 8 x 5T nylon washers (138)**, **1 pc of 5 x 16 mm tapping screw (89)**, **1 pc of 5 x 1.5T split washer (88)** and **1 pc of 5 x 1.0T flat washer (87)** to take off the **fan assembly (20)** and the **fan nets (L, R)**.
4. To refit: before fitting the fan assembly, **hang the chain joint-100 (14) on the fan assembly (20) inside the fan nets (65L, 65R)**; install the two fan nets and the fan assembly on the **main frame (1)**; pass the chain joint-100 (14) through the fan net and onto the drum group; then use the 10 mm wrench to set the **M6 x 6T nyloc nut (75)**, the **6 mm nut stopper (146)** and the chain joint-100 (14) tension. **Light finger pressure at the centre of the horizontal chain run should give 7 to 12 mm of sag.** When set, use the 15 mm wrench to tighten the **3/8" x 7T nut (76)** on both sides.
5. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to tighten the 5 x 19 mm tapping screw (139), the two nylon washers (138), the 5 x 16 mm tapping screw (89), the split washer (88) and the flat washer (87) on the main frame, then install the two fan nets (65L, 65R).

**The long chain, joint-100 (14), is set to 7 to 12 mm here; the short chain, joint-68 (15), to 3 to 7 mm in the drum-group procedure.** The chain cannot be fed through once the nets are on, which is why step 4 hangs it first.
