From the Spirit AB900 service manual, which the AIR650 service note names as its own.

1. Use a **14 mm wrench** to loosen both hex head bolts and remove the **seat (19)**.
2. Remove the **quick release lever (86)**, then take down the **sliding seat mount (7)**.
3. Use the **22 mm wrench (95)** to remove the **seat up/down adjustment knob (106)** and pull out the **seat slider (6)**.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.

The heading says "Inner Slide"; the steps call the same part the seat slider (6). No bolt sizes are printed for the two seat bolts beyond the 14 mm wrench - the owner's manual fits the seat with the 14/15 mm wrench (100).
