From the Spirit AB900 service manual, which the AIR650 service note names as its own.

1. Use a **15 mm wrench** to take the **pedals (45L, 45R)** off.
2. Take the **crank arm end cap (96)** off and use a **plug wrench** to release the **M8 x 20L arbor screw (83)**.
3. Remove the crank arm and the **rocker link (8)**, the **17 x 2T nylon washer (69)** and the **17 x 1T flat washer (68)**.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.

The service page prints no pedal direction; the AIR650 owner's manual step 4 does - right pedal clockwise, left pedal counter-clockwise. No torque is printed in either. The order in step 3 is as printed: link, nylon washer, flat washer.
