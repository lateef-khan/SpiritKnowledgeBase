Hardware step 2: **#90 M6 x 10 mm Phillips head screws (3)**. Tool: the screwdriver (93).

1. Above the flywheel is a computer cable (23); feed the cable carefully through the console base (10). **Be careful to not pinch or break the wire as it controls the console input.**
2. Use the screwdriver (93) to fasten the console base (10) onto the main frame (1) with three Phillips head screws (90).

"You may find it helpful to gently pull the cable to one of the corners of the base so it does not get pinched in-between the screws."

Cable (23) is the RPM sensor cable - the console runs on batteries and has no other wiring, so a pinched cable here is the usual reason the display shows no speed.
