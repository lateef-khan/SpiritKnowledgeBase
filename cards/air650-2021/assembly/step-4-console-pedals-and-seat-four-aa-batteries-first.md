Hardware step 4: **#58 M5 x 12L Phillips head screws (4)**. Tools: the Phillips screwdriver (93) and the 14/15 mm wrench (100).

1. Carefully connect the computer cable (23) to the console set (34). **Be sure to add in 4 AA batteries before installing.**
2. Use the Phillips screwdriver (93) to fasten the console set (34) onto the console base (10) with four screws (58).
3. Use the wrench (100) to fasten the left pedal (45L) onto the left crank arm (16L) and the right pedal (45R) onto the right crank arm (16R). **Note: the right pedal should be threaded on clockwise and the left pedal on counter-clockwise.**
4. Use the wrench (100) to fasten the seat (19) onto the sliding seat mount (7).

**CHECK ALL BOLTS AND NUTS ARE TIGHTENED BEFORE USING THE MACHINE.**

The Spirit AB900 book prints the same step but never mentions the left-hand thread (the linked card); this book does. Neither prints a torque. The four levelers and the seat-fit guide follow on the Final Preparations page.
