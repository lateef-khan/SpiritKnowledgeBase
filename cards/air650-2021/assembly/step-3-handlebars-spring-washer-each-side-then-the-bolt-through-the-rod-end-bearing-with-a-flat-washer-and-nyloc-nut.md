Hardware step 3: **#136 bolt M8 x 20 (2), #81 flat washer 5/16" x 20 x 3T (2), #75 nyloc nut M6 x 6T (2), #148 spring washer M16 x 3T (2)**. Tools: the 22 mm wrench (95), the 10 mm wrench (94) and the M4 Allen wrench (126).

1. Using the wrench (95) fasten the left handlebar (3) and right handlebar (4) onto the main frame (1) using a spring washer (148) for each side.
2. Attach the right handlebar (4) by aligning the end of the right handlebar to fit over the rocker link (8), the higher post into the hole on the main frame around the center of the flywheel. Once aligned, slide a bolt (136) through the rod end bearing (64) and fasten with a flat washer (81) and a nyloc nut (75). Repeat for the left side.
3. Use the 10 mm wrench (94) and an Allen wrench (126) to secure all bolts and washers.

The 22 mm wrench exists for this one fastener. The Spirit book calls washer 148 a star washer; it is the same toothed washer. Bolt 136 is M8 and nut 75 is M6 as printed in both books; the page does not resolve the mismatch.
