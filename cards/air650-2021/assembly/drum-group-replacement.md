From the Spirit AB900 service manual, which the AIR650 service note names as its own.

1. Remove the **outside chain cover (R) (37)** and the **outside chain cover (38)**, then use a **10 mm wrench** to unscrew **2 pcs of M6 x 6T nyloc nuts (75)**.
2. Use a **17 mm wrench** on the nut that secures the drum group, then a **15 mm wrench** to unscrew **2 pcs of 3/8" x 7T nuts (76)**.
3. Remove the drum group from the **two chains (14, 15)**.
4. To refit, pass the drum group through the two chains (14, 15) and seat it in the **main frame**. On both sides fit the **3/8" x 25 x 3T flat washer (73)**, the **M6 x 57 mm idle wheel screw (74)** and the **3/8" x UNF26 x 7T nut (76)**, then put the two chains (14, 15) onto the drum group. Lock the **M6 x 6T nyloc nut (75)** and the idle wheel screw (74) firmly on both sides. Use the 10 mm wrench to adjust the tension of **chain joint-68 (15)** and tighten: **light finger pressure at the centre of the horizontal chain run should give 3 to 7 mm of sag**. When the tension is set, use the 15 mm wrench to tighten the 3/8" x 7T nut (76) on both sides.

**Two chains, two sag figures.** The short chain, joint-68 (15), is set here to 3 to 7 mm; the long chain, joint-100 (14), is set in the fan procedure to 7 to 12 mm. "Zhonghua Drum Group" is the book's name for the hub between the chains. Nut (76) is printed "3/8" x 7T" in step 2 and "3/8" x UNF26 x 7T" in step 4.
