From the Spirit AB900 service manual, which the AIR650 service note names as its own.

1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **3 pcs of M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screws (58)** to take off the **outside chain cover (R) (37)**.
2. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **2 pcs of M5 x 6 mm Phillips head screws (70)** and **1 pc of M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screw** to take off the **outside chain cover (38)**.

No refit step is printed. Both covers come off before the drum group, and the drum group comes out before the fan. The second cover has no side letter in the book.
