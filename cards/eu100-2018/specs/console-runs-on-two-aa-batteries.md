**"Computer requires two 'AA' batteries"** - AA, not the AAA cells the other Xterra battery consoles take. The book
prints no operating-temperature block for this console.

Two consequences the same page states: "(All the values will reset to ZERO automatically when change the
batteries)", and "If computer is illegible or partial segments appear, remove batteries and wait 15 seconds to
re-install. Removing the batteries will reset all values to zero." The computer turns on when a key is pressed or
pedalling starts and turns off after about four minutes without a signal.
