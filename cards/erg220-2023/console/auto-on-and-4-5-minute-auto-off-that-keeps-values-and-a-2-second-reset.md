"Whenever a user starts to do exercise, the display will light up and show the workout values automatically. **After 4.5 minutes of no exercise input, the display will shut off.** When a user begins to exercise again the workout values will continue from where they left off."

**Reset:** hold the MODE button for 2 seconds and all the function values reset to zero "**except odometer**". The book lists no odometer function and the face has no ODO legend - the sentence is carried over from a bike console - so on this rower the hold zeroes every value shown. Removing the batteries also zeroes everything ([battery card](../../shared/console/xterra-erg-console-two-aa-batteries-and-where-they-go.md)).

4.5 minutes is this book's figure alone: the ERG160 and ERG180 shut off after 2 minutes and show STOP after 4 seconds ([card](../../shared/console/xterra-erg-console-wakes-on-rowing-or-mode-shows-stop-after-4-seconds-and-shuts-off-after-2-minutes.md)); the ERG500 shuts off after 4 minutes and loses its values ([card](../../erg500-2018/console/shuts-off-after-4-minutes-without-a-signal-and-zeroes-every-value.md)). What MODE steps through is on [the readouts card](single-mode-key-two-row-lcd-with-spm-to-120-and-an-arrow-marker.md).
