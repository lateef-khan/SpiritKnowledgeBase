**The face:** a two-row LCD under the XTERRA logo and **one oval MODE button**. The top row is COUNT (with KM and M unit marks beside it); the bottom row carries TIME, DIST, CAL and SPM down its left edge and SCAN, a heart symbol and PULSE down its right, each with a triangular arrow, and a vertical STOP marker at the top left.

**Auto Scan:** after power-on, or when MODE is pressed, the display cycles "Time - Distance - Calories - SPM - Pulse", **6 seconds each** (the text omits COUNT from the list; the face has it). Press MODE to step through the values until the arrow points at the one you want - that ends Autoscan and the chosen value stays on.

| Function | As printed |
|---|---|
| Distance | 0 to 9999 meters |
| Time | 00:00 to 99:59 |
| Count | "Displays the step from 0 to 9999" - the stroke count |
| Calories | 0.0 to 9999 |
| SPM | current strokes per minute, **0 to 120** |
| Pulse | current heart rate in beats per minute - no range printed |

**Pulse has no stated source.** The book lists a PULSE value and its safety page says "the pulse sensors are not medical devices", but it prints no chest-strap page, no hand-grip note and no Bluetooth page, so how this console receives a heart rate is unconfirmed. The SPM ceiling of 120 is the lowest in the range - the ERG160 and ERG180 print 0 to 999 ([ERG160 card](../../erg160-2020/console/single-mode-key-with-scan-every-6-seconds-and-six-readouts.md)). Holding MODE, the auto-off timer and the batteries are on [the auto-off card](auto-on-and-4-5-minute-auto-off-that-keeps-values-and-a-2-second-reset.md) and [the battery card](../../shared/console/xterra-erg-console-two-aa-batteries-and-where-they-go.md).
