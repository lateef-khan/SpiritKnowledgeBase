**3-1 Packaging removal.** A piece of plywood is bolted to the **Main Frame (1)** to stabilize it in shipping. Remove its **2 bolts and 2 washers** (the render labels them M8 x 20 and M8 washers) with the Allen Wrench (97). The plywood, bolts and washers "are not needed in further assembly and can be recycled".

**3-2 Front stabilizer.** Attach the **Front Stabilizer (3)** to the Main Frame (1) and secure with **4 Screws (81, M8 x 20), 4 Spring Washers (52) and 4 Washers (55)** using the Allen Wrench (97, S6).

Four short bolts here, where the ERG160 and ERG180 use two long M8 x 45 bolts.
