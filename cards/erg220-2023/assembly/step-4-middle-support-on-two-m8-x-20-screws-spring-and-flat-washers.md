Attach the **Middle Support (7)** to the **Main Frame (1)**. Secure with **2 Screws (81, M8 x 20), 2 Spring Washers (52) and 2 Washers (55)** using the Allen Wrench (97, S6).

The parts drawing on p. 3 calls this tube the **Mid-Stabilizer**; it sits under the hinge and carries the rower's weight when the rail is folded up. The ERG160 and ERG180 have no middle support.
