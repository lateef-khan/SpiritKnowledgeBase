1. Attach the **Slide Rail (2)** to the **Main Frame (1)**. Insert the **Axis (46, Ø16 x 110)** through the hinge and secure it with **2 Screws (81, M8 x 20) and 2 Washers (44, OD20 x ID8.5 x 1.5)** using the Allen Wrench (97). The render shows the two Allen keys (97) both in use here - one each side.
2. Insert the **Ball Head Pin (65, Ø12)** into the lower slot on the main frame and slide rail. **Lift the centre of the frame to help line up the locking pin holes.** This locks the rower in the unfolded position; the pin comes out to fold the rower up.

The machine carries two stickers about this joint: a **Pivot Point** sticker ("LIFT CENTER OF FRAME TO HELP LINE UP LOCKING PIN HOLES") and a **Ball Head Locking Pin** sticker ("LOCKING PIN MUST BE FULLY ENGAGED DURING USE AND WHEN FOLDED UP").
