**Parts:** 1 Main Frame, 2 Slide Rail, 3 Front Stabilizer, 4 Rear Stabilizer, 7 Middle Support, 12 Pedals (2), 38 Seat Assembly, plus the hardware bag and the manual. The hardware page is headed **RE-6030** - an OEM code the rest of the book never explains.

**Hardware bag, by step**

| Step | # | Item | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 34 | Limit pad | 1 |
| 1 | 62 | Button head screw M6 x 10 | 2 |
| 1 | 11 / 15 | Stop bracket R / L | 1 each |
| 1 | 59 | Self-tapping screw ST4.0 x 16 | 2 |
| 2 | 81 | Allen bolt M8 x 20 | 4 |
| 2 | 52 | Spring washer ID8.5 | 4 |
| 2 | 55 | Flat washer OD16 x ID8.5 x 1.5 | 4 |
| 3 | 81 | Allen bolt M8 x 20 | 4 |
| 3 | 52 | Spring washer ID8.5 | 4 |
| 3 | 55 | Flat washer OD16 x ID8.5 x 1.5 | 4 |
| 4 | 81 | Allen bolt M8 x 20 | 2 |
| 4 | 52 | Spring washer ID8.5 | 2 |
| 4 | 55 | Flat washer OD16 x ID8.5 x 1.5 | 2 |
| 5 | 46 | Axis Ø16 x 110 | 1 |
| 5 | 81 | Allen bolt M8 x 20 | 2 |
| 5 | 44 | Flat washer OD20 x ID8.5 x 1.5 | 2 |
| 5 | 65 | Ball head pin Ø12 | 1 |
| 6 | 50 | Long screw (hex head bolt) M12 x 160 | 4 |
| 6 | 64 | Spacer sleeve Ø18 x Ø13 x 26 | 2 |
| Tool | 97 | Allen key S6 | 2 |
| Tool | 98 | Screwdriver S13-14-19 | 1 |

The book prints spacer 64 as Ø18 on the hardware page and Ø16 on the step 6 page (read from the render); the count and the fit are the same either way. Step 3-1 also removes two shipping bolts and washers (M8 x 20 and two M8 washers) that hold a plywood brace - they are not in this bag and are not reused.
