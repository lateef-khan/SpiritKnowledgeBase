1. Slide the **Seat Assembly (38)** onto the Seat Rail (2). **Make sure the seat faces the hinge** - this rail folds, and the seat must sit the right way round before the rail is joined to the main frame in step 5.
2. Use **two M6 x 10 Button Head Screws (62)** to secure the **Limit Pad (34)** to the rail.
3. Secure the **Stop Brackets (11, 15)** to the rail with **two ST4.0 x 16 screws (59)** "to prevent the seat from sliding off the rail".

The callout box also lists a tool "#99 ... 1pc" that the hardware page does not carry; the tools in the bag are the S6 Allen keys (97) and the screwdriver (98). The ERG160 fits the same brackets in its step 4; the ERG180 has none (linked card).
