1. To insert or remove batteries, the **Console (95)** can be removed from the **Main Frame (1)**.
2. **Pop the console out via the hinge** (drawn on the page) and be careful not to damage the connection wires. Unclip the wires and the console can be moved freely. **Insert the 2 AA batteries to activate the console.**

No screws hold this console. The wire shown is the sensor wire (40). The console chapter repeats the battery rule - 2 AA in the case on the back, and every value resets when the batteries come out - and is the console section's card.
