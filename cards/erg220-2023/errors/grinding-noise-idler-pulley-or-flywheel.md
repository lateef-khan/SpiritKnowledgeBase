**Two parts, both replaced - there is no crank bearing on this list**, which is where the recumbent-bike twin of the row starts (`xterra-errors-grinding-noise-crank-bearing-idler-pulley-or-flywheel`); a rower has no crank.

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Grinding noise | Idler pulley defective | Replace idler pulley |
| | Flywheel defective | Replace flywheel |

The row beneath it is a different kind of noise with a different kind of answer: *Squeaking or squealing noise - V-belt slipping - Adjust v-belt* is an adjustment, not a part, and is a maintenance question. The ERG500 air rower and the ERG550W and ERG600W water rowers print only that squealing row and no grinding row; the ERG160 prints neither.
