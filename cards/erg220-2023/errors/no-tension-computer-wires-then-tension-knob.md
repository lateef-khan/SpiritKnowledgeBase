**Two causes on this magnetic rower, one wiring and one adjustment; the air rower's version of the row has neither** (`erg500-2018-errors-no-tension-air-damper-needs-adjustment`).

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| No tension | Computer wires not connected properly | Ensure the computer wires are connected properly at the upright and the computer |
| | Tension needs adjustment | Turn tension adjustment knob accordingly |

The knob is the manual tension control on the frame; this console has no level buttons and sends no signal to a motor, so the wiring check is the same one the no-display and no-count rows make, and the knob is simply where the resistance is set. The book prints no part to replace for this fault.

The recumbent bikes and ellipticals with a gear motor print a one-cause version of this row (`xterra-errors-no-tension-computer-wires-not-connected-properly`); their "replace gear motor" answers sit under error codes this rower never shows.
