**The seat sways because there is a gap between the seat wheel adjustment plate and the aluminium track. The
fix is an adjustment, not a part.** 7.3.1, as printed:

> Sway of swivel seat is caused by the gap between seat wheel adjustment plate and aluminum track assembly.
> The solution is as follows: Use a cross screwdriver to remove the fixing screw (as shown in Figure 7.3.1).
> Then use the 11mm wrench and the right hand as the 13mm wrench. Tighten the 11mm wrench first and lock it in
> the front direction (as shown in Figure 7.3.2) and behind (as shown in Figure 7.3.3).

1. **Phillips screwdriver:** remove the fixing screw on the plate (figure 7.3.1).
2. **11 mm wrench on one nut, 13 mm on the other**, front then rear: tighten with the 11 mm first and lock it, as
   the two figures show. The turning senses are given only by the arrows in figures 7.3.2 and 7.3.3 - read them
   from the page.
3. Refit the fixing screw. No turn count, no torque and no gap figure is printed; the test is that the seat stops
   moving.

The plates are the two **Seat Wheel Adjustment Plates** each side of the carriage (parts 9L and 9R), riding on
seven PU wheels in the aluminium track; a dirty track wears the wheels
(`70r-2025-maintenance-aluminium-track-cleaned-regularly-to-avoid-pu-wheel-loss`).

**The same adjustment, other machines.** The CRS800S semi-recumbent stepper prints it with the same two wrench
sizes (`crs800s-2021-maintenance-swivel-seat-unstable-seat-wheel-adjustment-plate-11-mm-and-13-mm-wrenches`); the
CR900 recumbent takes the play out by loosening the wheels and lifting while retightening
(`spirit-cr900-maintenance-shaking-handlebars-seat-wheels-levellers`). A seat that will not slide or lock is a
different fault, the lever cable (`spirit-med-bike-maintenance-seat-will-not-slide-or-lock-thumb-nuts`).
