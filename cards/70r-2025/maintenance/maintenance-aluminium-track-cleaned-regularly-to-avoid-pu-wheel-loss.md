**One sentence, and the only rail instruction in the book.** 3.3 Check for Aluminum Track, as printed: "regular
cleaning of the surface of the aluminum track can avoid PU wheel loss" (figures 3.3.1 and 3.3.2, the seat
carriage rail).

Read *loss* as wear: the seat carriage runs on polyurethane wheels - parts-list item 166, **seven PU wheels** on
the machine - and grit on the rail eats them. Keep the rail surface clean and the wheels last.

**What is not printed:** no interval beyond "regular", no cloth, no cleaner and no lubricant. The rail is cleaned,
not greased. Say the book is silent rather than importing a product from another machine.

**The same sentence, other machines.** The MED 7.5S stepper's service manual prints it word for word
(`7-5s-med-maintenance-aluminium-sliding-rail-cleaned-regularly-to-avoid-pu-wheel-loss`). The CR900 recumbent's
book says more - a damp cloth, mild cleaners and rubbing alcohol on the tracks
(`spirit-cr900-maintenance-periodic-cleaning-and-seat-track-rails`) - and that is the CR900's instruction, not
this one's.

A seat that sways on this track is a gap at the seat wheel adjustment plate, closed with an 11 mm and a 13 mm
wrench (`70r-2025-maintenance-swivel-seat-sway-fixing-screw-11-mm-and-13-mm-wrenches`).
