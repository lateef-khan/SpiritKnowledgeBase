**This is the 7.0R figure, 220 to 240 N at the nuts of the eye bolt. The 7.0U of the same year sets its belt to
260 to 280 N** (`70u-2025-maintenance-belt-slip-eye-bolt-nuts-260-to-280-newtons`); the CU900 to 180-210 N and
the 4.0R to 180 ±10 Hz on an acoustic gauge. Read the figure from the book for the machine in front of you.

7.1 Slip/Shedding of Drive Belt, as printed: "If you find the belt slipping or falling off, please refer to Figure
7.1.1 and Figure 7.1.2, and **follow step 6.10.1.3 to tighten the nut to adjust the tension**."

Step 6.10.1.3, the install half of the eye-bolt replacement, gives the figure:

1. The idler is spring-loaded: an **eye bolt** through the frame, a **tension spring** on it, and nuts on the eye
   bolt. Removing the belt means loosening those nuts and lifting eye bolt and spring out together (6.10.1.2).
2. To tension, refit and **"use the nuts of the eye bolts to adjust the belt tension. The belt tension should be
   in 220N to 240N."**
3. The drive belt must sit in the middle of the groove of the drive pulley and of the induction brake's pulley
   (6.10.2.3, figures 6.10.5 and 6.10.6); the idler wheel assembly is locked counterclockwise on refitting
   (6.10.3.5).

The front shroud comes off first (6.8) and no gauge is named; the figure is a force. **The book prints no
new-belt figure, no stretch allowance and no interval** for checking the belt.

**The CR900 2018 recumbent sets the same 220-240 N**, on a J-bolt with a top nut and a bottom jam nut
(`spirit-cr900-maintenance-belt-slip-jam-nut-220-to-240-newtons`) - same figure, different hardware. The 8.0U and
8.5R print a belt-slip pointer with **no figure at all**
(`spirit-med-bike-maintenance-belt-slip-tighten-the-idler-nuts-no-tension-figure-printed`).

A belt that slips **and** a rough feeling underfoot are two sections: the second is 7.2, lock the pedal then check
the crank (`cu900ent-noise-and-feet-feeling`).
