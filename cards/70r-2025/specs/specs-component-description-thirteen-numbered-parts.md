The picture is the assembled recumbent from the front right, with a yellow **Release Lever,
Mechanical Brake (4)** on the mast below the console - the lever that stops the flywheel, which has
no freewheel - and yellow knobs on the **Adjustable Crank Arm (10)**. The preventive-maintenance
chapter adjusts the brake lever's steel cable when the felt wears; the replacement chapter takes the
two rear shrouds off to reach the controller and the switching power supply, and the two front
shrouds off to reach the induction brake.

**No part numbers, quantities or dimensions are on this page.** The upright 7.0U numbers fourteen
components under different names (`70u-2025-specs-component-description-fourteen-numbered-parts`).
