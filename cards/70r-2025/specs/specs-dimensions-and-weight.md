| Figure | Printed |
|---|---|
| **Length** | **55.1" (140 cm** - the closing bracket is missing on the page) |
| **Width** | **32.7" (83 cm)** |
| **Height** | **46.9" (119 cm)** |
| **Weight** | **180.1 Lbs. (81.7 Kgs)** |

All four convert correctly to the printed precision.

**The 2021 Dyaco MED book for the same machine prints different dimensions and the same weight** -
57 x 30 x 51 inches (145 x 77 x 130 cm), 180.1 lb
(`70r-2021-specs-dimensions-and-weight`). Nothing in either book says the frame changed; treat the
2025 figures as the current ones and say the older book disagrees.

**No shipping weight and no carton size** is printed anywhere in the book, and the machine does not
fold. The upright 7.0U is 43.3 x 22.4 x 54.3 inches and 117.5 lb
(`70u-2025-specs-dimensions-and-weight`); the 8.5R recumbent on the touchscreen platform is 171 cm
long and 108.9 kg (`85r-2025-specs-dimensions-and-weight`). The clearance the safety page asks for -
two feet at the sides, one foot front and rear - is a safety figure, not a dimension.
