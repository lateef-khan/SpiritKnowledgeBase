**This is the 2025 list - the one printed in the 2025 Spirit owner's manual and on the pages of the 2025 service manual (MR490-SB018-03).** It is not the list in the 2021 Dyaco MED edition of the 7.0R (`70r-2021-specs-parts-list`), which words 52 of the same rows differently, lacks 17 rows this list has, and ends at item 307.

The numbers are the balloon numbers on the exploded view, a flat drawing stamped `20250919`. **No Spirit part number is printed.** 210 rows are printed and the item numbers run 1 to 338; 135 whole numbers are absent, so a missing number is a gap in the numbering, not a missing page. Two positions are lettered, 9L / 9R (seat wheel adjustment plates). The console is item 19 with seven sub-parts 19-1 to 19-7 (top and bottom covers, display board, key board, interface board, HR receiver, UART adapter board).

## The service manual's text layer is not its printed page

The 7.0R service manual's parts pages carry **two different lists**: the one printed on the page, and an older one left in the PDF's text layer underneath it. `pdftotext` returns the older list - `Mast Handle bar Assembly`, `Induction Brake` at 55, `Pedal (L)` and `Pedal (R)` at 116 and 117, `Power Adaptor` at 236, `AC Electronic Module` at 238, `Cylinder` at 244 - and that older list is, row for row, the 2021 MED edition's list. **The printed page shows this list**, checked on renders of PDF pp. 41 and 43: `Nylon Nut` at 88-91 with item 91 quantity 14, `Pedal(L.R)` at 116, `Switching Power Supply` at 236, `AC Input Module` at 238, `Gas Cylinder` at 244. Read the service manual's parts pages from a render, never from its extraction.

## What the list names that the prose does not

- Item 55 `Flywheel` with item 56 `Magnet` and item 264 `1300mm_Speed Sensor W/Cable` - the 2021 edition calls the same item 55 an `Induction Brake`.
- The electronics: 236 `Switching Power Supply`, 237 `Brake Controller`, 238 `AC Input Module`, 43 `Power Cord (Optional)`, 271 and 301 ground wires, and cables 262-274 and 281 by length.
- The rotating seat: 38 `Rotate Seat Assembly`, 244 `Gas Cylinder`, 245 `Rotate Disk`, 247 `Release Lever(147mm×44mm×52mm)`, 250 `Brake Pad`, 251 `Steel Cable(358mm)`, 337 `Steel Cable(340L)`, 338 `Seat Belt`.
- Eight tools packed as items, drawn beside the machine on the diagram: 112 `12/14mm_Wrench`, 114 `Phillips Head Screw Driver`, 132 `14/15mm_Wrench`, 200 `5mm_L Allen Wrench`, 201 `Short Phillips Head Screw Driver`, 280 `10mm_Wrench`, 283 `8mm_L Allen Wrench`, 284 `13.14mm_Wrench`. No separate tool list is printed in either book.
