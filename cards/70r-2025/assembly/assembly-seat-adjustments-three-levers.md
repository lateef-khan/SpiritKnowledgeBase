**Three adjustments, three levers - right for the back angle, left for the swivel, and an unnamed lever for fore/aft.**

| Adjustment | Control | Repeatable by |
|---|---|---|
| **Seat back angle** | **lift the right lever** and hold while adjusting, release the lever | numbered scale just below the seat back cushion |
| **Fore / aft** | **lift the lever** and hold while adjusting the distance from the console, release, then move the seat slightly until the seat lock clicks in place | numbered scale on the aluminum seat slide tube; the position is read where the front of the seat carriage lines up with the number |
| **Swivel** | **lift the left lever** and hold while rotating, release | latches every **45 degrees** |

The seat back angle "can be adjusted for comfort, but also may be adjusted to change the hip angle for patients". The fore/aft paragraph does not say which lever; the service manual's cable procedures name a seat back angle cable, a seat rotation cable and no fore/aft cable at all, so which lever moves the carriage is not printed anywhere in the 2025 books.

**The 2021 Dyaco MED edition of this bike works the fore/aft and the back angle from brake handles on the seat handlebar** - the left one, gray cable, for fore/aft and the right, black cable, for the back angle - and its swivel from a handle behind the seat. Its rotate seat and armrest are a different mechanism; that is its own card. The 8.5R has the same three adjustments on a rear-right lever, a front handle and a rear handle.
