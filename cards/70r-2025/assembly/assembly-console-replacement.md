**6.1.1 Remove the electronic console.** As shown in figure 6.1.1, **remove the 4 screws from the bottom of the electronic console** and remove the cable connector. As shown in figure 6.1.2, unplug the connectors of the wires to remove the electronic console.

**6.1.2** Refit by following 6.1.1 in reverse order.

No screw size and no connector count are printed. The 7.0U on the same template has a handle bar cover to take off first; the 4.0R has a two-screw console chin cover. This book has neither. The console mast procedure begins "refer to step 6.1 to remove the console", so this is its first step.
