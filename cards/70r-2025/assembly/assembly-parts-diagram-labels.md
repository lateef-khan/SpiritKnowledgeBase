Eight letters on a line drawing headed PARTS OF YOUR RECUMBENT BIKE:

| Letter | Part |
|---|---|
| A | Electronic console |
| B | Manual mechanical brake |
| C | Pedal adjustment |
| D | A.C. Power input |
| E | Seat swivel adjustment |
| F | Hand pulse sensors |
| G | Seat back angle adjustment |
| H | Leveling glide |

**The drawing carries a ninth callout, "J", with no line in the key.** The letters run A to H in the key and the figure prints a J at the lower left, near the pedal, unexplained. There is no I.

Seven of the eight are the 2021 Dyaco MED edition's numbered list (which also names a "seat angle adjustment" this key drops); that is its own card. The parts-included page and the steps use assembly-drawing numbers, not these letters.
