**These pages are pictures in the PDF; the words below were read from the rendered pages, not from the extraction.**

**6.8.1 Front shroud (L).** As shown in figure 6.8.1, take off the **round disk**. As shown in figure 6.8.2, take off the **screw on the front of the front shroud (L)**. As shown in figure 6.8.3, take off the **7 screws** on the front shroud (L) to remove it. Refit by following 6.8.1.1 to 6.8.1.2 in reverse order.

**6.8.2 Front shroud (R).** Refer to 6.8.1 to remove the front shroud (L). As shown in figure 6.8.4, take off the round disk. As shown in figure 6.8.5, take off the **2 screws on the main frame** first. As shown in figure 6.8.6, take off the **2 screws on the front shroud (R)**. As shown in figure 6.8.7, **unplug 3 wires** in the front and inside the front shroud (R) to remove it. Refit by following 6.8.2.1 to 6.8.2.3 in reverse order.

No screw sizes are printed, and the procedure does not say to take the crank arms off - the round disks come off around them. The parts list carries **Round Disk (34), 2**, **Front Shroud (R) (30)** and, on its OCR page, "Front Shroud (L)".
