**6.7.1 Replace the steel cable for the seat rotation adjustment.** As shown in figure 6.7.1, cut the cable tie. As shown in figures 6.7.2 and 6.7.3, **rotate the seat to an angle that makes it easier to remove the cable end and the spring**. As shown in figure 6.7.4, adjust the **adjustment screw beneath the release handle** to the position that allows the cable to be removed, then remove and replace the cable.

**6.7.2** Refit by following 6.7.1 in reverse order.

No cable length or screw size is printed. This cable has a spring on its head; the seat back angle cable does not. The swivel latches every 45 degrees and is worked from the **left** lever - the setting-up card. The 7.0S and 7.5S steppers print the same page for their swivel seats; that product line has its own card.
