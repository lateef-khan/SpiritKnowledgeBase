**6.6.1 Replace the steel cable for the seat back angle adjustment.** As shown in figure 6.6.1, loosen the adjustment cable screw and remove the cable end. As shown in figure 6.6.2, adjust the **adjustment screw beneath the release handle** to the position that allows the cable to be removed, then remove and replace the cable.

**6.6.2** Refit by following 6.6.1 in reverse order.

That is the whole printed procedure - two figures, no screw size, no cable length. The parts list carries a **Steel Cable (1100L) (167)**, a **Steel Cable (358mm) (251)**, a **Steel Cable (84.5 x 76cm) (258)** and, on its OCR page, a "Steel Cable (S40L) (337)"; it does not say which is this one. The seat back angle is worked from the **right** lever on the seat handlebar; the rotation cable of the next procedure has a spring on its head and is worked from the **left** lever.

The 7.0S and 7.5S steppers' service manuals print this page word for word; that product line has its own card.
