**Six bolts hold the mast, and the manual brake lever's steel cover and plate come off it first.**

1. Refer to 6.1 to remove the console.
2. **Remove the steel cover and the bracket of the manual brake lever.** As shown in figure 6.3.1, take off the **2 screws on both sides of the steel cover** to remove the steel cover of the manual brake lever first. As shown in figure 6.3.2, take off the **2 screws** to remove the **plate of the manual brake lever** on the console mast.
3. **Remove the console mast.** As shown in figure 6.3.3, take off the **6 bolts** to remove the console mast.
4. Refit by following steps 1 to 3 in reverse order.

**Console mast cover (6.4).** Refer to 6.3 to remove the console mast, then, as shown in figure 6.4.1, take off the console mast cover to remove it; refit in reverse.

No bolt sizes are printed. The owner's manual bolts the same mast with **six 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts (68), four 5/16" flat washers (76) on the side bolts and two 5/16" curved washers (83) on the front bolts**, fits the brake lever assembly (246) with **two 6 mm Phillips screws (98)** and its covers (275) with **four 4 mm screws (187)** - "the top screws need to be tightened with the short screw driver" - which is what steps 2 and 3 undo. The 7.0U mast has a brake-cable hold-down bracket and three screws a side instead; its own card.
