**6.5.1 Remove the gas cylinder.** As shown in figure 6.5.1, remove the **four screws under the seat cushion**. As shown in figure 6.5.2, remove the **safety cover** by loosening the screws. As shown in figure 6.5.3, cut the cable ties. As shown in figure 6.5.4, loosen the adjustment cable and remove the cable head. As shown in figures 6.5.5 and 6.5.6, take off the screws and caps to remove the locking gas cylinder.

**6.5.2** Refit by following 6.5.1 in reverse order.

No screw sizes are printed. The parts list carries the part as **Cylinder (244)** ("Gas Cylinder" on the OCR parts page). The owner's manual fits its end to the seat back angle adjustment bracket with the **5/16" x 1-1/4" bolt (208), two 5/16" flat washers (205) and a 5/16" nut (91)** in step one, and the four cushion screws are the **M6 x 25 mm bolts (222)** of its step five.

**The Spirit rehabilitation steppers print this page word for word** (the 7.5S service manual's 6.12, safety cover and all). That is a different product line; the linked card holds it for the steppers.
