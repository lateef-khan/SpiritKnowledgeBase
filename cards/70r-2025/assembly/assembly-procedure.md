**Five steps, with the brake lever and its cable routed through the console mast cover before the mast is bolted down.** Item numbers in parentheses are assembly-drawing numbers; the panel of hardware is on the hardware card. The 2021 Dyaco MED edition of this bike builds differently in steps one, two and five; that is its own card.

## Step one - rear stabilizer, gas shock, handle bar and rotate seat

1. Install the **rear stabilizer (7)** onto the **main frame (1)** with the four 3/8" x 2-1/4" hex head bolts (65) and four 3/8" flat washers (84).
2. Attach the end of the **gas shock (244)** to the seat back angle adjustment bracket and secure with the 5/16" x 1-1/4" bolt (208), two 5/16" flat washers (205) and 5/16" nut (91).
3. Secure the **handle bar (6)** and the **rotate seat assembly (38)** by using four 3/8" x 2" hex head bolts (71) through the top holes in the seat handle bar and fix with four 3/8" x 7T nylon nuts (89) and eight 3/8" flat washers (77), side by, using the 12.14 mm wrench (112) and 14.15 mm wrench (132).
4. Install two 3/8" x 2-3/4" hex head bolts (175) through the side holes, the **motor cover (242)** and the **seat belts (338)** in the handle bars (6), and fix with two 3/8" x 7T nylon nuts (89) and four 3/8" flat washers (77) using the 12.14 mm wrench (112) and 14.15 mm wrench (132).
5. Install the handle bar (6) and rotate seat assembly (38) "in the onto two Handpulse Wire (L,R)" - printed as shown; the sentence is broken, and the two hand-pulse wires are what gets connected here.

## Step two - armrest

1. Install **six M8 x 30 mm bolts (321)** from the top side of the armrest and assemble the **six nuts (322)** by using the Phillips head screw driver (114).

## Step three - seat back

1. Slide the **seat back assembly (5)** into the seat back angle adjustment bracket and secure with the two 3/8" x 1-3/4" bolts (220), 3/8" washers (206) and 3/8" nuts (89).
2. Install the **seat back cover (128)** onto the seat back assembly (5) by snapping into the locking sockets on the seat back.

## Step four - console mast, brake lever, handle bar

1. Locate the **console mast cover (31)** and route the computer and hand pulse cables (266, 269, 272) and the **brake lever and cable (246)** through the cover. Temporarily place the cover down on the main body. **Do not snap the cover in place yet.**
2. Unravel the cables (266, 269, 272) and snake them through the **console mast (2)** until they exit the top opening of the mast. **Be sure the brake cable (133) is in the groove of the cover** when installing the mast during the next step.
3. Holding the console mast in one hand, and gently keeping tension on the cables at the top of the mast with the other, install the console mast (2) into the main frame receiving tube under the cover (31). Keeping tension on the cables will ensure the wires don't get caught between the mast and receiving tube. **Do not bolt the mast in place at this time.**
4. Install the **brake lever assembly (246)** on the mast with the two 6 mm Phillips screws (98). Install the **covers (275)** with the four 4 mm screws (187). **The top screws need to be tightened with the short screw driver.**
5. Slide the mast cover (31) up the mast and bolt the mast in place with **six 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts (68), four 5/16" flat washers (76) on the side bolts, and two 5/16" curved washers (83) on the front bolts**. Slide the cover down and snap in place on the main body.
6. Assemble the handle bar to the mast with two 5/16" x 5/8" bolts (68), two 5/16" split washers (82) and two 5/16" flat washers (76).

## Step five - covers, seat, console, pedals

1. Install the **front and rear stabilizer covers (32 and 37)** with the four 5 mm screws (99).
2. Install the **seat cushion (61)** with the four M6 x 25 mm bolts (222).
3. Plug in all the connectors in their mating sockets in the back of the console. Install the console onto the mounting plate and secure with the four 5 mm screws (99). **Make sure to store the excess wire into the console mast and check that no wire is caught between the back of the console and the mounting plate** before installing and tightening the screws. If the wires get pinched between the console and plate, damage could occur to the electronics.
4. Install the **left (116-L) and right (116-R) pedals** onto the crank arms. The left pedal has a reverse thread; there is an "L" stamped into the end of the threaded post of the left pedal and an "R" in the right. **Make sure to tighten the pedals as much as you possibly can.** It may be necessary to re-tighten the pedals if you feel a thumping during pedaling; a noise or feeling such as a thumping or clicking is usually caused by the pedals not being tight enough.

**Printed slips, quoted as they stand.** Step one item 3 reads "and eight 3/8" Flat washers (77) side by using" - "side by" is what is printed. Item 5 is the broken sentence above. Step four names the brake cable (133) in item 2 and the brake lever and cable (246) in items 1 and 4 - two numbers for the brake parts, and the parts list of the service manual has no item 133 at all (its brake lever is 247 and its plate 246). **No torque is printed for anything**; step one item 3 is the one place a wrench is named.

The build ends at the pedals; plugging in and switching on is the setting-up page's fact.
