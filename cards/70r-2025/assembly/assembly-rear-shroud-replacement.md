**6.9.1 Rear shroud (L).** As shown in figure 6.9.1, take off the **9 screws** on the rear shroud (L) to remove it. Refit by following 6.9.1.1 in reverse order.

**6.9.2 Rear shroud (R).** Refer to 6.9.1.1 to remove the rear shroud (L). As shown in figure 6.9.2, take off the **screw on the main frame** first. As shown in figure 6.9.3, take off the **2 screws on the rear shroud (R)** to remove it. Refit by following 6.9.2.1 to 6.9.2.2 in reverse order.

No screw sizes are printed. The controller and the switching power supply sit behind the **left** rear shroud, and their procedures begin here. The page that carries 6.9.1 is a picture in the PDF; the nine-screw count was read from the rendered page, where the nine screws are circled.
