One page of captioned drawings headed PARTS INCLUDED, grouped by step. The captions give item number, size and count and no part-type words; the types below are what the steps call them.

## Step 1

| Item | Size | Qty | The step calls it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 65 | 3/8" x 2-1/4" | 4 | hex head bolt, rear stabilizer |
| 77 | 3/8" x 3/4" | 12 | flat washer |
| 71 | 3/8" x 2" | 4 | hex head bolt, handle bar top holes |
| 84 | 3/8" x 1" | 4 | flat washer, rear stabilizer |
| 175 | 3/8" x 2-3/4" | 2 | hex head bolt, side holes |
| 205 | 8.5 mm x 26 mm | 2 | flat washer, gas shock |
| 208 | 5/16" x 1-1/4" | 1 | bolt, gas shock |
| 89 | 3/8" | 6 | nylon nut |
| 91 | 5/16" | 1 | nut, gas shock |

**Twelve washers (77) for six bolts** - eight on the four top bolts and four on the two side bolts, as the step counts them.

## Step 2

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 321 | M8 x 30 mm | 6 |
| 322 | M8 x 6.5T | 6 |

The armrest bolts and their nuts; the service manual's parts list calls 322 an **M8 x 1.25 x 6.5T square nut** and carries six M8 x 30 mm flat head socket screws on its OCR page beside it.
