1. First remove the pedal bar assembly, then use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew the pedal arm cover.
2. Use an **M8 hex wrench and a #14 wrench** to remove the **gap socket screw 3/8" x 2-1/4"**, two **flat washers 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T** and the **nylon nut 3/8" x 11T** connecting the inclinable rail assembly and the rotating block, to remove the inclinable rail assembly.
3. Use a **#12 hex wrench** to remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 15mm** and **flat washer 5/16" x 35 x 1.5T**, to remove the bushing housing and pedal arm.
4. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
