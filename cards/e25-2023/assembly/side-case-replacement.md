1. Remove the connecting arm and pedal arm first (sections 9.2 and 9.3).
2. Reverse the owner's manual assembly instructions and use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the console mast cover.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **9 pcs of 3.5x16 self-tapping screws** and **3 pcs of 5x16 x 3 tapping screws** to take the right side case apart.
4. To take the left side case apart, release the **3.5x16 self-tapping screws with 1/4" x 19 flat washers** on the main frame and **3 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws**. Then disconnect **two red jumpers and the white grounding** - mark the red jumpers first - to release the left side case.
5. On reassembly, connect the red jumpers and the grounding back on the **AC electronic module**.
6. Return the left side case, with one **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** on it, onto the main frame. Use a **3.5x16 self-tapping screw with a 1/4" x 19 flat washer** to hold it, not too tight for now, then tighten with **3 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws**, making sure the side case matches with the round disk. Tighten the **4x15 self-tapping screws**.
7. Match both side cases with each other and use **9 pcs of 3.5x16 self-tapping screws** and **3 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws** to secure them.
8. Reverse the above procedures to resume the pedal arm and connecting arm.
