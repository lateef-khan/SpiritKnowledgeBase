1. Follow 9.2, 9.3, 9.4, 9.5, 9.6 and 9.7 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, both side covers, cross bar, the belt and the idler wheel assembly.
2. Plug in the power, **adjust the resistance to level 20** and release the steel cable.
3. Use a **15 wrench** to loosen the **nylon nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 9T** on the flywheel and main frame. Remove the flywheel and belt.
4. To resume the flywheel, return the belt onto the grooves on the flywheel and return the flywheel onto the mainframe.
5. Tighten both **3/8"-UNF26 x 11T nuts**. Return the steel cable onto the flywheel **at 45 degrees** and then return the idler wheel assembly.
6. Turn the drive pulley and check if the belt is secured. Adjust both **3/8"-UNF26 x 4T nuts** to position the belt in the middle on the drive pulley, then tighten all other screws. Adjust the belt to a proper tension.
7. Follow 9.7, 9.6, 9.5, 9.4, 9.3 and 9.2 to return the other parts.

**The nut sizes in this procedure do not agree with each other.** Step 3 loosens a **9T** nylon nut, step 5 tightens **11T** nuts and step 6 adjusts **4T** nuts, all described as being on the same flywheel and main frame. The manual is printed that way. Check the nut on the machine before ordering.
