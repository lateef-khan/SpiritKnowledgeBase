1. Put the multi-meter on the **20VDC** setting and place the probes on the motor control wire on the drive board: **red probe in the brown wire, black probe in the black wire**.
2. Turn on the unit power. The display will light up.
3. Press **LEVEL UP**. The normal reading should be **+5~6.0 VDC**, and the motor should operate with resistance increasing.
4. Press **LEVEL DOWN**. The normal reading should be **-5~6.0 VDC**, and the motor should operate with resistance decreasing.
5. If there is no voltage, inspect the power socket and the holder **FUSE**. If it is broken, replace it.
6. Inspect the drive board **POWER LED**. If it is not lit, the drive board is bad and needs to be replaced.

The only tool the manual lists for the whole error section is a multi-meter.

This manual has no separate fuse replacement section. Step 5 is the only place it mentions the fuse.
