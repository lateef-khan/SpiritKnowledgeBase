Symptom row: **Erratic pulse display.**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1. Another chest belt in use around the elliptical. | 1. Check for other chest belt use around the elliptical. |
| 2. Other magnetic field disturbance. | 2. Change the position or direction of the elliptical. |
| 3. Receiver is broken. | 3. Replace with a new receiver. |
