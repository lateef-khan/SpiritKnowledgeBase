**Work voltage: DC 4.5~7.5V.** It controls resistance increases and decreases.

The drive board output during a level change is a different figure: **Level UP +5VDC, Level DOWN -5VDC**, with a normal meter reading of +5~6.0 VDC and -5~6.0 VDC at the motor control wire. Those numbers live in the E2 cards, not here.

The manual calls this part the gear motor throughout. The 2019 E25 manual calls the same part the tension motor.
