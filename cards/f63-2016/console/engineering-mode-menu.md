1. Press and hold the **Start**, **Stop** and **Enter** keys at the same time, until the display shows **"ENGINEERING MODE MENU PRESS ENTER"**. Depending on the software version it may say maintenance menu. Press **Enter**.
2. Scroll with the **Speed ▲ / ▼** keys. **Stop** goes back to the previous menu selection.

The menu selections:

- **A. Key Test** - test all the keys to make sure they work.
- **B. Display Test** - tests all the display functions.
- **C. Functions** - press Enter to reach the settings, then scroll with Speed ▲ / ▼:
  - **Display Mode** - turn it off to have the console power down automatically after **30 minutes** of inactivity.
  - **Pause Mode** - on allows **5 minutes** of pause; off makes the console pause indefinitely.
  - **Maintenance** - resets the lube message and the odometer readings.
  - **Units** - English or Metric display readings.
  - **Key Tone** - turns the key beep on or off.
- **D. Security** - locks the keypad against unauthorised use. With the child lock enabled the keypad does nothing until you press and hold **Start and Enter for 3 seconds**.
- **E. Exit**
