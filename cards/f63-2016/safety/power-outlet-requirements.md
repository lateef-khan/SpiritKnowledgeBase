- Install the treadmill on a flat level surface with access to a **230-volt, 10-amp (110-volt, 15-amp) grounded outlet**, with **only the treadmill plugged into that circuit**.
- **Do not use an extension cord** unless it is **16 AWG or better with only one outlet on the end**. Do not disable the grounded plug with an improper adapter, and do not modify the cord outlet in any way.
- **Never use a ground fault circuit interrupt (GFCI) wall outlet with this treadmill.** As with any appliance with a large motor, the GFCI trips often.
- Route the power cord away from every moving part, including the elevation mechanism and the transport wheels.
- Disconnect the treadmill from the outlet before cleaning or service work, to reduce the risk of electric shock.
