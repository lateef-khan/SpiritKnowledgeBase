**This product must be grounded.** If the treadmill malfunctions or breaks down, grounding gives electric current a path of least resistance and reduces the risk of shock. The machine ships with a cord carrying an equipment-grounding plug, which must go into an outlet installed and grounded to all local codes and ordinances.

**DANGER** - improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can cause electric shock. Check with a qualified electrician or serviceman if you are in any doubt.

Do not modify the plug if it does not fit the outlet; have a proper outlet installed by a qualified electrician. The manual says the product is for use on a **nominal 230-volt circuit**.

A temporary adapter may be used to connect the plug to a 2-pole receptacle only until a properly grounded outlet can be installed. The green rigid earplug extending from the adapter must be connected to a permanent ground, such as a properly grounded outlet box cover, and whenever the adapter is used it must be held in place by a metal screw.

**Note**: the safety instruction on the same page names a 230-volt, 10-amp (110-volt, 15-amp) outlet, so the "nominal 230-volt circuit" wording here covers only the 230V build.
