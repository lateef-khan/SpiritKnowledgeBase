**Take the cylinder off before you start.**

1. With the running board folded, use an **M5 Allen wrench and a 12 mm wrench** to remove the securing screws on the cylinder, and remove the cylinder.
2. Follow the front and rear roller procedure to remove the rollers.
3. With a Phillips head screwdriver loosen the **6 screws** and remove the top step cover in the direction of the indicating arrows. Then remove the **8 screws** on the front and back of the foot rail, and remove the front and back foot rails in the direction of the indicating arrows.
4. With a combination **M5 Allen wrench**, remove the **8 screws** securing the running board. Remove the running board and replace the running board or the running belt. **If the cushions need replacing, remove the 6 cushions and replace them.** Reassemble in the reverse order.
5. With a **14mm open end wrench**, adjust the belt tension using a tension measuring device. **Adjust to the white LBS area, between 70 and 75 LBS.** Use the 14mm T-shaped socket wrench to secure and tighten the 4 securing screws.
