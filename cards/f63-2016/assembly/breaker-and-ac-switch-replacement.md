**Breaker**: remove the breaker connection wiring, replace the part and reconnect the wiring.

**AC power switch**: disconnect the wiring to the AC power switch, replace the switch and reconnect the wiring.

A tripped breaker is not the same as a broken one. A tripped breaker is reset by pressing the small red button to return it to its original status.
