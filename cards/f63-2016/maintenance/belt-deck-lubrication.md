**How often**: the belt and deck come pre-lubricated. Lubricate **every 90 hours of use**, or whenever the deck is dry. Reach between the belt and the deck **every other month** to check that lubricant is present. If there is none, lubricate even though the console "Lube" indicator is not lit. Otherwise lubricate when the console reminder lights after 90 hours.

**What to use**: only the approved lubricant. The treadmill comes with one tube of "Lube"; extra tubes and a Lube-n-Walk kit can be ordered from the dealer. **Failure to lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty**, because dry lubricant raises the friction between belt and deck and stresses the drive motor, drive belt and motor control board to the point of catastrophic failure.

First clean between the belt and the deck: with a clean non-fraying rag, t-shirt or light towel, halfway between the end of the treadmill and the motor cover, push the garment under the belt until you can grasp it on both sides, then drag it the length of the belt 1 to 2 times and remove it.

1. Turn the power switch off and unplug the power cord from the wall outlet.
2. Measure **18 inches** from the edge of the motor cover, kneel down and reach under the belt approximately **4 to 6 inches** from one edge. Squirt a line of lubricant about **1/8 inch wide by 15 inches long in an "S" pattern**, perpendicular to the motor cover.
3. Repeat on the opposite side.
4. Plug the cord back in and turn the power switch on.
5. Walk on the belt at a moderate speed for **five minutes** to spread the silicone lube evenly.
6. If the "Lube" message is on the console, reset it: enter the Engineering Mode Menu, go to **Functions**, then **Maintenance**, and press Enter to reset the lube message. Press Stop to exit.
