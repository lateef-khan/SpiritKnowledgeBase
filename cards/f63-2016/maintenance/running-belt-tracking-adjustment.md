Performance depends on the frame standing on a reasonably level surface. If the frame is not level the front and back roller cannot run parallel, and the belt will need constant adjustment.

It is normal for a belt to drift towards one side while running with nobody on it, and after a few minutes of use it should tend to centre itself. Adjust only if the belt keeps moving to one side **during use**.

1. A **10 mm Allen wrench** is provided to adjust the rear roller. **Make tracking adjustments from the left side only.**
2. Set belt speed at approximately **3 to 5 kph**.
3. Turn the bolt **clockwise to move the belt to the right**. Turn it only **1/4 turn** and wait a few minutes for the belt to adjust itself.
4. Keep making 1/4 rotation turns until the belt stabilises in the centre of the running deck.

A small adjustment makes a dramatic difference. Expect to repeat this as the belt beds in; proper belt tracking is an owner responsibility common to all treadmills. Belt damage from improper tracking or tension adjustment **is not covered under the warranty**.
