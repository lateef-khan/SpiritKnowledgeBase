## Items 74 and 99 are printed as 5/6 inch

There is no such size. Every other bolt and washer of that family in this book is
**5/16"**, items 79, 91, 100, 167, 178 and 179 included, and the 5/16" flat washer
item 100 sits in the same joint family. **This card does not correct the manual** -
what is printed is `5/6"` - but order item 74 and item 99 by their item numbers, not
by that dimension. The same misprint reappears on the XE795 2023 panel for its item
106.

## Two panels supply four wave washers for two shafts

Step 2 supplies **4 of item 103** and its own job 1 fits **two**, one per console
mast shaft. Two are spare per the panel.

## Step 4 uses two screw sizes that look alike

Item **82** is M5 x 15mm and item **157** is M6 x 10mm. Only the two steel brackets
(156) take item 157; everything else plastic in step 4 takes item 82. They are not
interchangeable.

## Nothing here can be checked against a parts list

This manual prints no numbered parts list, so every item number above exists only in
these four panels and in the step sentences. See
[the exploded view card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ce-assembly-exploded-view-and-parts-list.md).
