| Item | Tool | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 124 | 13/14mm Wrench | 1 |
| 125 | Short Phillips Head Screw Driver | 1 |
| 126 | Phillips Head Screwdriver | 1 |
| 129 | 12/14mm Wrench | 1 |

Four tools and nothing else. **Both wrenches are double ended**, 13/14 and 12/14, so
between them the carton covers 12, 13 and 14mm and gives you two wrenches for the
bolt-and-nut joints in steps 1 and 3, which the manual tells you to tighten "using
the wrenches provided (124 & 129)".

## The short driver is needed and named

Step 4 job 1 says to tighten the connecting arm cover screws "using the Short
Phillips Head Screw Driver (125)". Every other screw in the build is driven with the
long one, item 126.

## The 13/14mm wrench is also the levelling wrench

Step 4 job 8 uses wrench **124** to loosen the lock nut under each floor leveller.
There is no separate levelling tool and no 14mm-only wrench in this carton. See
[the levelling card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xe-assembly-levelling-bottom-nut.md).

## This is not the 2021 panel

The 2021 XE cartons drop the short Phillips driver and add a **combination M5 Allen
wrench and Phillips screwdriver**. Same two wrench sizes, different third and fourth
tool. See
[the 2021 tools card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xe-assembly-tools-2021.md).
