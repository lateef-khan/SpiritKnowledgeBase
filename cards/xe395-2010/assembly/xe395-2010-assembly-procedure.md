## Step 1 - incline rail and console mast

1. Slide the **Incline Rail Assembly (2)** into the U channel of the **Main Frame
   (1)**, taking care not to damage the wires that leave each part.
2. Connect it **horizontally** with two **Hex Head Bolts (77)**, two **Flat Washers
   (96)** and two **Nyloc Nuts (89)**. Secure it **vertically** with four **Hex Head
   Bolts (167)**, four **split washers (178)**, four **Flat Washers (100)** and four
   **star washers (179)**. Tighten with wrenches 124 and 129.
3. Connect the **Incline Motor wires (37 & 38)** to the wiring harness and the black
   wire leaving the incline rail assembly, and push the excess inside the U channel.
4. Guide the twist tie on the **Computer Cables (36 & 39)** through the **Console Mast
   Cover (49)** and up the **Console Mast (12)** until it leaves the top. Loosely
   thread two **Hex Head Bolts (76)** with two **Curved Washers (104)** from the
   **front**, then one **Hex Head Bolt (75)** with a **Split Washer (128)** from the
   **side**. One bolt is already in the receiving bracket and engages the slot at the
   bottom of the mast. **Tighten the bolt from the left side last.**
5. Tighten the two front bolts first, then the side bolts, with wrench 124. Seat the
   **Console Mast Cover (49)** over the top of the plastic side covers (50 & 51).
6. Untie the twist tie and plug the two **Hand pulse cables (44)**, the **Computer
   cable (36 & 39)**, the **Resistance cable (146)** and the **Incline cable (146-1)**
   into the bottom of the **Console (32)**. Fit the console to the mounting plate with
   four **Phillips Head Screws (85)**.

## Step 2 - swing arms

1. Two **Wave Washers (103)** onto the left and right console mast shafts, then the
   left **(10)** and right **(11)** swing arms, secured with two **Hex Head Bolts
   (74)** and two **Flat Washers (99)**, tightened with wrench 129.
2. Slide a **grommet (145)** onto the left cable **(146-1)**, connect it to cable
   **(147)** from the left swing arm, snap the grommet into the hole in the side of
   the mast tube and push the excess cable through. Repeat on the right, joining
   cables 146 and 147.
3. Match the Front Handle Bar Cover (L) **(68)** to the Rear Handle Bar Cover (L)
   **(68-1)** on the left swing arm with three **Sheet Metal Screws (107)**. Repeat on
   the right with covers **69** and **69-1**.

## Step 3 - connecting arms

1. Untie the wire on the rod end bearing, connect the left swing arm **(10)** to the
   left connecting arm **(8)** with a **Hex Head Bolt (79)**, the **Rod End Sleeve
   (22)**, a **Flat Washer (100)** and a **Nyloc Nut (91)**. Tighten with wrenches 124
   and 129.
2. Repeat for the right connecting arm **(9)** and right swing arm **(11)**.

## Step 4 - plastic parts, and the machine gets plugged in

1. Connecting arm covers **(71, 72)** over the left connecting arm with two
   **Phillips Head Screws (82)** and a **Sheet Metal Screw (107)**, using the **short**
   driver (125). Repeat on the other side.
2. **Roller Covers (64)** onto each set of rollers, four **Phillips Head Screws (82)**.
3. The two middle stabilizer covers **(L-60, R-61)** - there is a sticker underneath
   identifying each - one **M5x15mm Phillips Head Screw (82)** each side.
4. **Front Stabilizer Cover (59)** with two **Phillips Head Screws (82)**.
5. A **steel bracket (156)** on the left and right of the rear stabilizer tube, single
   hole facing the rear, four **Phillips Head Screws (157)**.
6. The front rear-stabilizer cover with two screws **(82)**, then the back rear
   stabilizer cover **(73-3)** with four screws **(82)**.
7. **Plug the machine into a grounded outlet and switch it on at the power button on
   the bottom front of the unit. Raise the incline to level 8**, fit the **Incline
   Cover (73)** with two **Phillips Head Screws (82)**, then return the incline to 0.
8. Check the four floor levellers under the middle and rear of the machine. See
   [the levelling card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xe-assembly-levelling-bottom-nut.md).

## The incline has to be up before the last cover goes on

Job 7 is the only reason the build needs mains power, and it is not optional - the
incline cover cannot be fitted with the ramp down. The **power button is on the
bottom front of the unit**; the manual names no other switch. The later XE395ENT and
XE395 2023 builds do the same thing at the same point in step 4.

## What this build does not do

There is no leveller to fit, no transport wheel to fit and no foot pedal to fit -
they arrive on the machine. There is no water bottle holder step. The manual prints
four unheaded assembly drawings facing the four steps and **no parts list**, so an
item number used above cannot be checked against a table anywhere in the book.
