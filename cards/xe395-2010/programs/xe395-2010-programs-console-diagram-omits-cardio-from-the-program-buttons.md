The machine has **ten programs**: Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, **Cardio**, Strength, Interval, User 1,
User 2, HR 1 and HR 2. The manual's own heart rate bar graph page confirms the count - it says the
bar graph uses the age "you entered during the programming phase of any of the **10** programs".

**The console diagram on the facing page contradicts that.** It labels the program buttons
"(Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Strength, Interval, 2 User, 2HR)" - **nine buttons, with Cardio missing** -
even though the manual goes on to describe Cardio in full as one of the five presets. The omission is
in the caption, not in the machine.

The five **presets** are Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength and Interval. The manual says "the
Elliptical has five different programs that have been designed for a variety of workouts", meaning
those five.

**A third count appears in the same book.** Its heart rate page says the monitor may be used "during
manual mode or during any of the **nine** different programs" - boilerplate every Spirit residential
XE manual carries regardless of how many programs the machine actually has.
