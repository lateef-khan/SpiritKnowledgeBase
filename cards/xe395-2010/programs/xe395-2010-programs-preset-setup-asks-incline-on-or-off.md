The XE395 2010 adds two steps to the preset program setup that no other Spirit elliptical has. They
come after you set the **Max Resistance Level** and before you press Start.

1. **"Incline On or Off?"**
   - **On** - the machine automatically adjusts the incline **at random times throughout the program
     profile**.
   - **Off** - the incline does not function automatically. You can still adjust it manually on the
     **left swing arm** at any time during the program.
2. If you accepted Incline On, you are then asked for the **Max Incline Level** - the peak elevation
   level you will experience during the program. Adjust it on the console or the left swing arm, then
   press Enter.

Resistance is adjusted on the console or the **right** swing arm; incline on the console or the
**left** swing arm.

**In Manual mode the program starts with no incline at all**, and you raise it with the Up key on the
left swing arm whenever you want to - see `spirit-ce-programs-manual-mode`.

For what the incline then does in each program, see `xe395-2010-programs-incline-profiles`.
