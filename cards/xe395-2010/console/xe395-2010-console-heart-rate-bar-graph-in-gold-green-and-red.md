**The console LCD displays your current heart rate whenever a pulse is detected.** The **Bar Graph,
located to the left of the LCD screen**, shows your current heart rate **as a percentage of your
projected maximum**, worked out from the **age you entered during the programming phase of any of the
10 programs**.

| Percentage of maximum | Colours lit |
|---|---|
| **50-60%** | Gold |
| **65-80%** | Gold and Green |
| **85-90% or more** | Gold, Green and Red |

**The bands leave gaps.** Nothing is printed for 61-64% or for 81-84%, and nothing is printed below
50%. The manual states only the three bands above.

**Two things distinguish this console from its 2021 successors.** The bar graph sits **to the left**
of the LCD here and **to the right** on the XE295, XG400 and XE795, and this manual calls the first
colour **Gold** where those three call it **Amber**. See
`spirit-xe-console-heart-rate-bar-graph-in-amber-green-and-red`.

**The percentage is only as good as the age you entered.** If nobody has entered an age the console
uses the last one saved.
