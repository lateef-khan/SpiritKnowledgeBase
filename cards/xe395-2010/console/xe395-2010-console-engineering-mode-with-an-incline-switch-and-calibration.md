**To open it: press and hold Start, Stop and Enter together for about 5 seconds.** The Message
Center displays **`ALTXXXX Engineering mode`**. Press **Enter** to reach the menu. The manual notes
the menu "may be called Engineering mode, depending on version".

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **a. Key Test** | Tests all the keys |
| **b. Display Test** | Tests all the display functions |
| **c. Functions** | Press Enter for the settings below |
| **d. Security** | Locks the keypad. **Off by default; to turn it on press Level Up, then the Enter key** |
| **e. Factory Settings** | Access only allowed by manufacturing |
| **f. Exit** | |
| **g. Calibration** | See below - it is not entered from this menu |

**Inside Functions, in the printed order:**

1. **Sleep Mode** - turn on to have the console power down automatically **after 20 minutes** of
   inactivity
2. **Safety** - off. **To turn safety on, press and hold Start and Enter at the same time**
3. **Motor Test** - runs the resistance motor up and down in a continuous loop; the display shows the
   **level setting and position sensor reading**. Press Stop to end the test
4. **Beep** - turn the key beep on or off
5. **Units** - English or Metric display readings
6. **ODO reset** - resets the odometer
7. **Pause Mode** - turn on to allow **5 minutes** of pause, turn off to pause indefinitely
8. **Incline On or Off** - **switching to off eliminates the incline feature**

**Calibration is a separate keystroke, not a menu item.** If the console shows an error for the
incline/ramp, re-calibrating may clear it: **press and hold Start and the resistance level UP keys
for 5 seconds**. The Message Center shows **Calibration**; press Enter. **The process is automatic
and the system restarts when it finishes.**

**Two items exist only on this machine.** The **Incline On/Off** switch and the **Calibration**
keystroke both need the powered ramp no other residential XE elliptical of this family has.

**The 2007 XE100, XE200 and XE300 menu is the closest relative** and has neither:
`spirit-xe-console-maintenance-menu-with-a-motor-test-and-security`.
