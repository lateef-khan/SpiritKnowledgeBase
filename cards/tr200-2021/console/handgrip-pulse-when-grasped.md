Any time the treadmill is operating, grasping the handgrips activates the heart-rate value on the display. Firmly grasp the handgrips to ensure a proper reading.

That is the whole feature as the book prints it: no chest strap, no receiver, no reading range. The TR150 and TRX1000 books of the same year print no pulse feature at all.
