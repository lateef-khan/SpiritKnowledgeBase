Read from the console drawing (the page carries no text beyond its heading, and the OCR of the render caught only fragments):

- A blue backlit **LCD** with SPEED as a large figure and TIME, DIST, CAL and a heart-rate (pulse) field along the bottom; the model name TR200 under it.
- **Speed ▼**, quick keys **3 | 6 | 9**, **Speed ▲**; below them **STOP** (red), **PROGRAM** and **START** (green), and the red safety-key pad.
- Twelve printed **speed program profiles** either side of the LCD, P1-P6 left and P7-P12 right.
- Badges on the left wing: **16" x 50" walking deck**, **12 preset programs**, **0-10 MPH range of speed**, and a HOME WARRANTY block reading Lifetime frame, 1 year motor, 90 days parts & in-home labor. Badges on the right wing: **QUICK one touch keys**, **INCLINE 3 manual settings**, **1.5 CHP motor**, **FOLD-UP storage**.

The warranty block on the badge matches the warranty page of the same book (frame lifetime, motor 1 year, parts 90 days, labor 90 days); the warranty card, not this one, is the authority for it. The quick-start text says "SPEED buttons 3 through 9", but the console has only 3, 6 and 9 ([quick start card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-quick-start-with-speed-keys-3-6-and-9.md)).
