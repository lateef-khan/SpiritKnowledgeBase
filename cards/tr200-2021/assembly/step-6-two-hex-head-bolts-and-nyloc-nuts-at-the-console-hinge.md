With the **13/15 mm wrench (#103)** secure **2 hex head bolts (#86)** and **2 nyloc nuts (#68)**.

That single sentence is the whole step; the book does not name the part being fastened. The drawing
places the pair at the **top of each upright, where the console meets it** - one bolt (86) and one nut
(68) each side, with a wrench drawn on each side - so this is the console pivot, tightened after the
console was bolted on at step 5. Neither the #86 bolts nor the #68 nuts appear on the pack checklist.
