**On this machine the uprights are not inserted into the base - they are hinged to it and unfold.**

1. **Step 1.** Remove the unit from the box and put it on a flat and level floor. No hardware.
2. **Step 2.** Unfold the **uprights (L3, R4)** to the vertical upright position. No hardware. "This step
   may work better with a second person assisting."

Step 3 then secures the unfolded uprights with eight fasteners and two wrenches. The step 1 page prints
"Step 1." and "Step 2." labels a second time in small type beside the headings - layout leftovers, not
extra steps.
