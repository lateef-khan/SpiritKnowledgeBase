**Three fasteners the steps call for are not on this checklist:** the two button head bolts (#97) and
four nyloc nuts (#68) of steps 3 and 6, and the two hex head bolts (#86) of step 6. The manual does not
say where they are; the step 3 and step 6 drawings show them already sitting in the frame. The counts
that are listed do reconcile: #94 is used 2 + 4, #95 is 2 + 2, #79 is 2 + 2.
