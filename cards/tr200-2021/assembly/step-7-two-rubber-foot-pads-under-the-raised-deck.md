Lift the deck up until it locks in a fixed position. Using the combination wrench and screwdriver,
tighten **2 screws (#113, M5 x 20 mm Phillips head)** and **2 rubber foot pads (#114)** to the **main frame
(#2)**.

"NOTE: Please tighten all screws after assembly is complete." This is the last of the seven assembly
steps; the folding, transport and incline instructions follow on the next page.
