With the uprights unfolded (step 2), the step is in two parts and uses both wrenches. "This step may
work better with a second person assisting."

1. Insert the **uprights (L3, R4)** into the **frame base (2)** and, with the **combination wrench and
   screwdriver (#104)**, secure them with **2 flat head socket bolts (#96), 2 split washers (#98) and 2
   flat washers (#79)**. Also secure the uprights with **2 button head bolts (#94) and 2 curved washers
   (#95)**. **Leave the bolts loose and do not overtighten.**
2. Secure **2 button head bolts (#97) and 2 nyloc nuts (#68)** using the **13/15 mm wrench (#103)** and
   the combination wrench and screwdriver (#104). **Tighten all bolts, but do not overtighten.**

Hardware sidebar, as printed: 2 Bolt (#96), 2 Split Washer (#98), 2 Flat Washer (#79), 2 Button Head
Bolts (#94), 2 Curved Washer (#95), 2 Button Head Bolts (#97), 2 Nyloc Nuts (#68). The #97 bolts and
#68 nuts are not drawn on the pack checklist; the drawing shows them at the base of the upright with a
wrench on each side.

The sentence says "Leave the bolts loose" for the first group and "Tighten all bolts" for the second,
in that order; the book never says to go back and tighten the first group.
