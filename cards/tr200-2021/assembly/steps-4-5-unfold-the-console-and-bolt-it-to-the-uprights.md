**The console arrives folded onto the uprights**; it is unfolded, not lifted into place.

1. **Step 4.** Unfold the **console** to the furthest position. No hardware. "This step may work better
   with a second person assisting."
2. **Step 5.** Secure the **console (5)** onto the **uprights (L, R) (4, 3)** using the combination
   wrench and screwdriver (#104) and **4 button head bolts (#94), 2 curved washers (#95) and 2 flat
   washers (#79)**.

The book does not say which bolts take the curved washers and which the flat. Step 6 (two hex head
bolts and nyloc nuts at the top of the uprights) follows.
