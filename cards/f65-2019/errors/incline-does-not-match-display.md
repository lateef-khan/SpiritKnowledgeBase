**Reason**: the console is not calibrated.

**Solve**: calibrate the console.

**INCLINE ERR with "E3" in the INCLINE window** is listed separately in the same matrix.

**Reason**: the position sensor value of the incline motor is wrong.

**Solve**: turn off the AC switch and turn the power on again, then calibrate the monitor.
