The matrix groups three symptoms together: UP/DOWN of the incline adjustment switch cannot be used, only UP works, or only DOWN works. The solve column is numbered 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 in the original - there is no item 5.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The incline VR or incline power wires are not connected properly. | Connect the wires again. |
| The incline VR or incline power wire is damaged. | Replace the cable. |
| Maybe keys stuck. | Replace the buttons. |
| Inspect whether the incline motor is stuck. | Replace the incline. |
| Inspect whether the incline gears are cracked. | Replace the incline. |
