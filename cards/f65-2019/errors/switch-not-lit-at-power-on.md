| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Power cord isn't plugged into outlet. | Plug the power cord into the outlet. |
| Power cord isn't plugged into the unit. | Plug the power cord into the unit. |
| The voltage of the outlet is too low. | Check the voltage of the outlet. |
| Plug or connector of the power cord is open. | Replace the power cord. |
| Connector of the power cord is broken. | Replace the power cord. |
| Connecting cable disconnected. | Check if the wire is disconnected and connect it again. |
| Breaker tripped. | **Press the small red button** to return it to its original status. |
| Breaker is broken. | Replace the breaker. |
| ON/OFF switch is broken. | Replace the AC switch. |
