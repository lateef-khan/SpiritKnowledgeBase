**Take the cylinder off before starting this replacement.**

1. After the running board has been folded, use a **M5 Allen wrench and 12m/m wrench** to remove the securing screws on the cylinder and remove the cylinder.
2. Follow the front and rear roller replacement procedure.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to loosen 6 screws, remove the top step cover in the direction of the indicating arrows and remove 8 screws on the front and back of the foot rail then remove the front and back of foot rail in the direction of the indicating arrows.
4. User Combination M5 Allen Wrench to remove the 8 screws securing the running board. Remove the running board and replace the running board or running belt. If the cushions need replacing, remove the **6 cushions** and replace them. Reassemble in the reverse order.
5. Use a 14mm open end wrench to adjust belt tension with a tension measuring device. Adjust to the **white LBS area between 70-75LBS**. Use the 14mm T-shaped socket wrench to secure and tighten the 4 securing screws.
