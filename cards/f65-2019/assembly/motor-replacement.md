1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to loosen the **5 tapping screws** on the motor cover, then remove the cover.
2. Remove the **motor grounding wire (greenish yellow)**, the **motor wire (+) red** and the **motor wire (-) black**.
3. Use 14mm T-shaped socket wrench to loosen 4 screws, use 14mm open end wrench to loosen 1 belt tension screw, remove motor and replace with new. Reassemble in reverse order, re-hook the belt and readjust it to be parallel with the main frame. **Do not tighten the 4 securing screws yet.**
4. Use a 14mm open end wrench to adjust belt tension with a tension measuring device. Adjust to the **white LBS area between 70-75LBS**. Use the 14mm T-shaped socket wrench to secure and tighten the 4 securing screws. Connect the grounding wires and the motor wires (**red M+, black M-**).
