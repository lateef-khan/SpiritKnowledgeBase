1. Use phillips head screw driver to remove 5 button tapping screws from the motor top Cover, Remove motor cover.
2. Disconnect all lower controller wiring. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the controller, fit the new lower controller and reconnect all wiring.

The troubleshooting matrix pairs a controller change with a calibration: "Replace controller and calibrate it."
