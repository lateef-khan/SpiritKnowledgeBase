1. Press and hold **Start, Stop and Enter**, then insert the safety key at the same time. Keep holding until the message window displays **Engineering Mode Menu**. Press Enter to access it.
2. Press Speed ▲ (or Speed ▼ to go backwards) until **"Functions"** appears, then press Enter.
3. Press Speed ▲ until the **"Maintenance"** message appears, then press Enter to reset the lube message.
4. Press Stop to exit engineering mode and resume use.

The same Maintenance item resets the odometer reading.
