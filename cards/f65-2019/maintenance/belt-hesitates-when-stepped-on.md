1. **Insufficient lubricant on the tread belt** - lubricate the belt and deck.
2. **Tread belt tension insufficient** - tension the running belt at the rear roller.
3. The diagnosis guide adds that the **motor drive belt may be loose**. Its tension is set to the white LBS area between 70-75 LBS with a tension measuring device.
