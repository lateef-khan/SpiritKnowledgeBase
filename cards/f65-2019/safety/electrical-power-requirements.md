- Disconnect the treadmill from the electrical outlet before cleaning or service work, to reduce the risk of electric shock.
- Install the treadmill on a **flat level surface** with access to a **230-volt, 10-amp (110-volt, 15A) grounded outlet**, with **only the treadmill plugged into that circuit**.
- Do not use an extension cord unless it is **16 AWG or better with only one outlet on the end**. Do not disable the grounded plug with an adapter or modify the cord outlet in any way.

The diagnosis guide repeats the requirement from the other direction: if the treadmill only reaches about 10 kph while the display shows a higher speed, the AC voltage is low. A minimum of 220 volt AC current, 50 hz is required (of 110 volt AC current, 60 hz is required).
