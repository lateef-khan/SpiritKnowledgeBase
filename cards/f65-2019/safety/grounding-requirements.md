This product **must be grounded**. Grounding gives electric current a path of least resistance if the treadmill malfunctions, which reduces the risk of electric shock. The cord has an equipment-grounding plug and must go into an outlet installed and grounded to all local codes.

**DANGER** - improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric shock. Check with a qualified electrician or serviceman if you are in doubt about whether the product is properly grounded.

- Do not modify the plug if it does not fit; have a proper outlet installed by a qualified electrician.
- The product is for use on a **nominal 110-volt (or 220-volt) circuit**.
- A temporary adapter may be used to connect the plug to a 2-pole receptacle **only until a properly grounded outlet can be installed**. The green rigid ear lug extending from the adapter must be connected to a permanent ground such as a properly grounded outlet box cover, and the adapter must be held in place by a metal screw.
