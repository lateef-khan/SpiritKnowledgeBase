The house breaker has to be replaced with a **"high inrush current" type** breaker.

Some house breakers are not rated for the inrush current a treadmill draws at switch-on or during use. This is **not a warranty defect**; the manufacturer cannot control it. The part is sold by most electrical supply stores - the manual names **Grainger part # 1D237** and **www.squared.com part # QO120HM** as examples.
