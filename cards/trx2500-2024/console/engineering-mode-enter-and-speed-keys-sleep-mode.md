Before entering the Engineering Mode, turn the power off. Then hold down the **Enter** key plus the **Speed Up and Speed Down** keys, and power on while holding all three. The console enters Engineering mode; press Speed Up or Down to cycle through the menu.

1. **Sleep Mode** — lets the machine enter "Sleep Mode" and power down after **5 minutes** of non-use. By default it is **ON**. Toggle it ON and OFF with the Speed Up and/or Down keys, then press Enter to set the mode and exit.

That is the only entry the book prints. The TRX3500 and TRX4500 add Lube Reset and Child Lock and use the Program key instead of Enter ([their card](../../shared/console/xterra-trx-console-engineering-mode-program-and-speed-keys-sleep-lube-child-lock.md)). Calibration is a separate mode ([calibration card](calibration-program-key-wheel-60-max-10-mph-incline-10.md)), and the service manual prints no engineering mode at all.
