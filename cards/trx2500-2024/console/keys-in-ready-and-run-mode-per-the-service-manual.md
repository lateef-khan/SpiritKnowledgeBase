From the Dyaco GT90B-NT022 service manual:

**READY mode**
- SAFETY KEY: fit it to power on the console; pulling it away shuts the computer down.
- START/STOP: in standby the first press gives a **3-second countdown**, then the machine runs in the Manual program (a preset program starts at its default speed). The second press **pauses**; the third starts again; and so on in a cycle.
- ENTER: sets preset program values (count-down time, distance, calories) and confirms other settings.
- PROGRAM: in standby, enters the preset programs — any of P1-P24 and HRC.
- FAST, SLOW, UP, DOWN: non-functional unless a setting is being entered. Speed and incline rapid keys: non-functional.
- FAN: turns the fan ON/OFF.

**RUN mode**
- SAFETY KEY: pulling it away shuts the computer down.
- START/STOP: the same start/pause/start cycle.
- ENTER and PROGRAM: non-functional.
- FAST / SLOW: 0.1 kph (0.1 mph) per press; held continuously, speed runs to MAX or MIN quickly.
- UP / DOWN: one incline position per press; the page says the maximum incline position is **12** — the owner's manual and the console stop at 10.
- SPEED RAPID: 10 preset buttons, 1 to 10 kph (mph). INCLINE RAPID: 10 preset buttons — 0, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10.
- FAN: turns the fan ON/OFF.

The owner's manual's version is on [the quick-start card](quick-start-speed-keys-1-to-10-and-incline-keys-0-to-10.md) and [the pause card](../../shared/console/xterra-trx-console-pause-with-start-stop-and-reset-by-holding-enter.md).
