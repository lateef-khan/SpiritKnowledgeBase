1. Attach the Safety Key to enable the display (if not already on).
2. Press the **Start/Stop** key to begin belt movement. Adjust the speed with the speed ▲/▼ keys (console or handgrip), or press one of the **Speed Keys 1 – 10**.
3. To adjust the deck incline, press the incline ▲/▼ key (console or handgrip), or press one of the **Incline Keys 0 – 10**.
4. To stop, press the Start/Stop key or pull out the Safety Key — the book encourages the Start/Stop key.

**Incline.** It may be adjusted any time after the belt begins to move; press and hold the incline ▲/▼ keys on the left side (console or handgrip), or select an Incline Key 0 – 10 on the left of the console. The display indicates the incline position as adjustments are made.

**What is actually on the console.** The pictured face has ten speed keys (1 through 10) and **ten** incline keys: 10, 9, 8, 7, 6 and 5, 4, 3, 2, 0 — there is no 1 key. The service manual says the same ("10 preset buttons for rapid incline: 0, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10"). The service manual's Up-key line says "the maximum incline position is 12" and its Incline function says a work range of 0 to 15 with a preset of 0 to 12, all of which disagree with the 0-10 keys and the calibration maximum of 10 in both books; see [the service-manual card](keys-in-ready-and-run-mode-per-the-service-manual.md).
