**There are 24 groups of programs, P1 to P24.** Press the **PROGRAM** key to select P1 to P24,
then either press **START** to start directly or press **ENTER** to set the time.

- Time defaults to **30:00**, range **5:00 to 99:00**, changed in steps of **1:00**; after
  setting, press START.

**P1 to P14 change speed only; P15 to P24 change speed and incline.** The profile pictures on
page 15 (printed 13) carry a *Speed / Incline* key, and page 16 (printed 14) prints the
**Program Profile Detail Chart** with the speed of every program, and the incline of P15 to
P24, for each of the 30 minutes. The chart is carded in two parts (see `see_also`).

The Dyaco service manual corroborates: the PROGRAM button "in the standby mode ... can any
choose one of part (P1~P24 and HRC)", and a preset program "will be started at default speed".
