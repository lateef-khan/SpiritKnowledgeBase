**This is the TRX2500 page.** Its ranges differ from the TR260 and TRX1400 page (distance to
99.00 miles, calories to 990), which has its own card.

In idle mode you can press **Start** directly, or press **ENTER** to set the count-down
parameters (time, distance, calories). Press **SPEED up/down** to adjust and **ENTER** to
switch between time, distance and calories. **The system accepts only the last parameter
entered**: "if the last input is a distance, the previous parameter will be invalid and only
the distance setting will be accepted."

| Target | Default | Range | Minimum step |
|---|---|---|---|
| TIME | 30:00 | 5:00 to 99:00 | 1:00 |
| DISTANCE | 5.00 miles | 1.00 to 9.00 | 1.00 |
| CALORIE | 500 | 10 to 9990 | 10 |

The default **SPEED is 0.5** (printed "0.5 miles").

The Dyaco service manual for this console says the ENTER button sets "count-down time value,
count-down distance value, count-down distance calorie value" and that the START button in
standby starts "the Manual (normal) program" after a 3-second count-down.
