**This is the speed-and-incline half of the chart (P15 to P24); the fourteen speed-only
programs are on their own card.**

Columns **1 to 30** are the minutes of the 30-minute default. Each program has a **spd** row
(mph) and an **inc** row (incline level, 0 to 10 - the console's incline keys run 0 to 10).

Notable shapes as printed: **P16** holds 3 mph while the incline cycles 2 to 10 and back;
**P22** holds incline 9 and **P23** holds incline 10 for the whole 30 minutes; **P24** runs
incline 9 from minute 2 to minute 29 with speed steps of 2.5, 4.5, 7.5 and 1.5 mph; **P21**
steps the incline 0, 3, 6, 10, 3, 0 in blocks. The highest speed in the chart is **7.5 mph**
(P24, minutes 16 to 22).
