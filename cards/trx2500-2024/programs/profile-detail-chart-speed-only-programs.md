**This is the speed-only half of the chart (P-1 to P-14); the incline of these programs is not
printed, and the speed-and-incline programs P15 to P24 are on their own card.**

The chart is headed *time / mode* with columns **1 to 30**, one per minute of the 30-minute
default. Values are speeds in **mph** (the book is in miles; the Manual page gives "0.5 miles"
as the default speed). Read from a 300 dpi render of the page, which is a flat picture with no
text behind it.

Every speed-only program starts between 1 and 5.5 mph and ends at 1 to 1.5 mph; the highest
figure in this half is **6 mph** (P-5 and P-7). Changing the program time from the 30-minute
default is allowed (5:00 to 99:00) but the chart does not say how the 30 columns are then
stretched or cut.
