The book opens with a caution: "please consult your doctor or professional instructor before
use."

Press **PROGRAM** to select **H-1**, then either press **START** to start training or press
**ENTER** to enter the settings (time, heartbeat target), adjusting with **SPEED up/down** and
**INCLINE up/down**.

| Setting | Default | Range | Step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time | 30:00 | 5:00 to 99:00 | 1:00 |
| Heart rate target | **116** | 60 to 220 | 1 |

**After the program starts it must detect the heart rate to control the treadmill.** The book
does not say whether HRC adjusts the speed, the incline or both, and gives no age formula for
the 116 default.

The Dyaco service manual lists "P1~P24 and HRC" as the PROGRAM key's choices.
