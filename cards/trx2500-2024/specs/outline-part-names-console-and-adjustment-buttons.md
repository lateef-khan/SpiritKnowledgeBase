Two line drawings with boxed callouts and no numbers.

**Page 4, the whole machine**: **Console** (an inset of the console head with its two speaker grilles), **Speed
adjustment button** pointing at the right handlebar end, **Incline adjustment button** pointing at the left.

**Page 5, the front of the frame with the hood off**: **Driving Motor**, **Lower Controller** and **Incline
Motor**.

So the speed rocker is on the right grip and the incline rocker on the left. No dimension is printed and the book has
no specification table. The TRX3500 and TRX4500 book, which shares this chassis family, prints only photographs here
with no callouts at all.
