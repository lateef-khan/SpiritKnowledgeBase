**This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household use.** The consumer is responsible for:

- **Returning the warranty registration card within 10 days**, or registering later with proof of purchase.
- **Proper use of the treadmill in accordance with the instructions in this manual, including general maintenance** - **belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under the motor cover, and lubrication**. See all components under General Maintenance.
- **Proper installation in accordance with the instructions and with all local electric codes.**
- **Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage**, plus **replacement of blown fuses and repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring**.
- **Expenses for making the treadmill accessible for servicing**, including any item that was not part of the treadmill when it shipped from the factory.
- **Damages to the treadmill finish during shipping, installation or following installation.**
- **Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual.**
- **Providing pictures or video of any issues via email as requested by the service technician, and assisting with troubleshooting or diagnosing prior to a home visit.**
