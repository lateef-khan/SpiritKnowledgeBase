**Warranty will be void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment.** The examples printed in the manual are **a garage, outside, and a carport**.

The electrical page sets the physical limits behind that rule: **never expose the treadmill to rain or moisture**, it is **not designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment**, the **maximum operating temperature is 40 degrees C**, and **humidity is 95% non-condensing**.

The exclusions list adds that **product use in any environment other than a residential setting** is not covered either.
