**DAMAGE TO THE RUNNING BELT RESULTING FROM IMPROPER TRACKING / TENSION ADJUSTMENTS IS NOT COVERED UNDER THE SOLE WARRANTY.**

**This exclusion is not in the numbered exclusions list on page 27** - it is printed only in the maintenance section, which is why it is easy to miss.

The two ways to cause it are both described on the same pages:

- **Over-tightening the tension bolts** will cause **belt damage and premature bearing failure**.
- **Turning the tension bolts unevenly** makes the belt track to one side instead of running in the middle of the deck.

**Proper belt tracking is an owner responsibility common with all treadmills**, and the consumer responsibilities list names **belt tracking and belt tension** as maintenance the owner must perform correctly.
