**Effective February 1, 2026.** SOLE warrants all its treadmills' parts for the periods below.

| Component | Warranty period |
|---|---|
| Frame Weldments / Drive motor | Lifetime |
| Parts | 1 Year |
| Labor | 1 Year |
| Cosmetic Items | 90 Days |

**Cosmetic items include, but are not limited to: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.**

**SOLE's responsibilities** are to provide **new or remanufactured parts, at SOLE's option**, and **technical support to independent dealers and servicing organizations**. **In the absence of a dealer or service organization, these warranties will be administered by SOLE directly to a consumer.**

When the clock starts is on its own card, as are the consumer's responsibilities and the exclusions.
