**Products that have been sold, transferred, bartered or given to a third party are excluded. Warranties DO NOT transfer.**

That covers a private resale, a gift and a trade alike. The registration requirement points the same way: the warranty must be **on file at SOLE** for the original purchaser, and **SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists**.
