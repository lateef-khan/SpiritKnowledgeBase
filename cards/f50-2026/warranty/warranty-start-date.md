The warranty runs **from the date of retail sale, as determined by the sale receipt**.

**In the absence of a receipt**, it runs from **eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date**.

**The labour warranty is stated separately in the Service section**, with the same two-branch rule: "**Twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale, or eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number, establishes the labor warranty period should service be required.**"

**Keep your bill of sale.** If service is performed, it is in your best interest to obtain and keep all receipts.
