- **Record the serial number of this fitness product** in the space provided on page 1 of the manual.
- **The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to SOLE.**
- **You can also register online at www.soletreadmills.com under the support tab.**
- The introduction page also asks you to record the **name of the dealer, the telephone number of the dealer, and the purchase date** for easy contact in future.

**Registration is a warranty condition, not a courtesy.** The warranty asks for the card **within 10 days of the original purchase**, and lists "products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at SOLE" as an exclusion. **Products whose original serial numbers have been removed or altered are also excluded.**
