**This is preset program P9, one of twelve numbered profiles.** It is not the manual program, which has no profile.

Speed cycles 50 to 100 three times, and the incline reaches its 7 peak late, at segment 14. **Peak Speed% is 100 and peak incline is 7.**

| Cool-down 3 | 20 | 0 |

**Speed% has no stated reference and incline has no stated unit** - see the profile format card before quoting these as mph or percent. The **3 warm-up and 3 cool-down rows are this repo's reading of the printed column header**, which spans "Warm up" and "Cool down" over unnumbered columns.

Before the program runs, the console asks for **age, body weight, total workout time and a max speed ceiling**, then starts with a **3 minute warm-up** that a second press of Start will bypass.
