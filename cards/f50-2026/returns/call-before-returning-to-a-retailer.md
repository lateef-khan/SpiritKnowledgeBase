The same banner is printed **twice** in the manual, on the contents page and again as the last line of the warranty section:

> "**Before returning your Sole product to any retailer, or if you require any assistance with assembly or technical support, please call us first for assistance at 866-780-7653.** Thank you for your Sole purchase."

The introduction page gives the **same number for the service department**, and the warranty Service steps give it as **step 3, after the selling dealer and the online service request form**.

**The banner pages carry no printed page number in this extraction** - the first sits on the contents page ahead of page 1, the second on the last page of the warranty.
