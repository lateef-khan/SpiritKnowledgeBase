**WARNING.** To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock or injury, **install the treadmill on a flat level surface with access to a 120-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet, with only the treadmill plugged into that circuit.**

**DANGER.** To reduce the risk of electric shock, disconnect the treadmill from the electrical outlet before cleaning or service work.

Read all instructions before using this appliance. The plug must not be modified and the ground must not be defeated - see the grounding card.
