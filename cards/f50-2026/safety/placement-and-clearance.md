- **Do not operate the treadmill on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet.** Damage to both the carpet and the treadmill may result.
- **Do not block the rear of the treadmill. Provide a minimum of 3 1/2 feet of clearance between the rear of the treadmill and any fixed object.**
- Install the treadmill on a **flat, level surface** (stated with the outlet requirement).
- **Do not use outdoors.**
