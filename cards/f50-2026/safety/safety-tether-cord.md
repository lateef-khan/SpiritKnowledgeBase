A **safety tether cord is provided with this unit and should be used at all times**. It is for your safety should you fall or move too far back on the tread-belt. **Pulling the safety tether cord will stop tread-belt movement.**

**To use:**

1. **Place the safety key into position on the console control head.** The treadmill **will not start and operate without this**. Removing the safety key also secures the treadmill from unauthorized use.
2. **Fasten the plastic clip onto your clothing securely** to assure good holding power. The clip should be attached securely to make certain it does not come off. Be familiar with its function and limitations. **The treadmill will stop, depending on speed, with a one to two step coast any time the safety key is pulled off the console. Use the red Stop switch in normal operation.**
3. **If the console displays "SAFE. Please replace the key to Operate", put the safety key back to continue using the treadmill.**

**Remove the tether cord after use to prevent unauthorized treadmill operation.**
