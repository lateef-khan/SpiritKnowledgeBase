**The pulse sensors are not medical devices.** Various factors, including the user's movement, may affect the accuracy of heart rate readings. The pulse sensors are **intended only as exercise aids in determining heart rate trends in general**.

**Note a wording mismatch inside this manual.** Page 2 speaks of "the pulse sensors" on the machine, while the Pulse Feature on page 15 says you **must wear the wireless Bluetooth chest strap** to show a pulse and never mentions contact sensors. The manual never states whether this machine has hand-grip pulse sensors.
