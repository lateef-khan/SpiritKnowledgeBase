**Check that the treadmill is the only appliance on the circuit.** See "Important Electrical Information" at the front of the manual for more details.

Anything else drawing on the same circuit - fluorescent lights with electronic ballasts, a coffee maker, a space heater, a hair drier - can push the circuit over. If the breaker is an AFCI or GFCI type, see the nuisance-tripping card as well.
