**To disconnect: turn all controls to the off position, remove the tether cord, then remove the plug from the outlet** - in that order.

**DANGER.** Disconnect the treadmill from the electrical outlet before cleaning or service work. **Never remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power** (page 3).
