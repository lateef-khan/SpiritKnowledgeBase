**If voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more, the performance of the treadmill may be affected. Such conditions are not covered under the warranty.**

If you suspect the voltage is low, **contact your local power company or a licensed electrician for proper testing**.

Low household voltage also shows up as a top speed the machine cannot reach; see the 7 mph card.
