**NEVER expose this treadmill to rain or moisture.** The product is **NOT** designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.

- **Maximum operating temperature: 40 degrees C.**
- **Humidity: 95% non-condensing** (no water drops forming on surfaces).

The warranty adds a separate placement rule: it **is void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment** (garage, outside, carport). See the warranty card.
