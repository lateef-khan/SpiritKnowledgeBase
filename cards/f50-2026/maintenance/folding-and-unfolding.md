**To fold the treadmill.** Make certain the treadmill is **at minimum incline**. **Lift the treadmill's running deck until it is secured by the cylinder in the centre back of the base.**

**To unfold the treadmill.** **Using your foot, firmly push against the cylinder to release the folding mechanism.**

**Do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and locked position.** See the moving card for the rest of that rule.

The unpacking page warns that this folding mechanism can **spring open** if the machine is tilted on its side while the shipping strap is still on.
