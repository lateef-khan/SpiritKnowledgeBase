The treadmill uses a **very high-efficiency, low-friction deck**, and performance is maximized when the deck is kept as clean as possible.

- Use a **soft damp cloth or paper towel** to wipe the **edge of the belt and the area between the belt edge and the frame**. Also reach as far as practical **directly under the belt edge**.
- **Do this once a month** to extend belt and bed life.
- A **mild soap and water solution with a nylon scrub brush** will clean the top of the textured belt. **Allow to dry before using.**

Cleaning is not lubricating - the lubricant goes **under** the belt and has its own procedure and interval.
