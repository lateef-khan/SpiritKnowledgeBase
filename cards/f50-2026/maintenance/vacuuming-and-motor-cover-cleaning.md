**Dirt, dust and hair can block air inlets and accumulate on the running belt.**

- **Vacuum underneath the treadmill on a monthly basis** to prevent build-up of dirt that can get sucked into the inner workings under the motor cover.
- **Every other month, remove the motor cover and carefully vacuum out dirt and hair** that may accumulate.
- **UNPLUG THE POWER CORD BEFORE THIS TASK.**

The preventative maintenance chart words the same job as "**Vacuum Under Motor Cover & Check Wiring (Every Other Month)**" - the chart adds the wiring check, which this page does not mention.
