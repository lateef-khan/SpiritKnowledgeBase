**Do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and locked position.** **Be sure the power cord is secured to avoid possible damage. Use both handrails to maneuver the unit to the desired position.**

**The treadmill is equipped with transport wheels that are engaged when the treadmill is folded. After folding, simply roll the treadmill away.**

The power cord must also be **routed away from the transport wheels** and the elevation mechanism.
