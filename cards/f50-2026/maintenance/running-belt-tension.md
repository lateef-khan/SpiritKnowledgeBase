**Belt tension is not critical for most users.** It is very important for **joggers and runners**, to provide a smooth, steady running surface.

- **Adjustment must be made from the rear roller with the M6 L Allen wrench (98)** supplied in the parts package.
- **The adjustment bolts are located at the end of the step rails.** **Adjustment is through a small hole in the end cap.**
- **Tighten the rear roller only enough to prevent slippage at the front roller.**
- **Turn the tread-belt tension adjusting bolts 1/4 turn each**, then inspect for proper tension by **walking on the belt and making sure it is not slipping or hesitating with each step**.
- **Turn the bolts on both sides evenly**, or the belt could start tracking to one side instead of running in the middle of the deck.

**DO NOT OVERTIGHTEN.** Over-tightening will cause **belt damage and premature bearing failure**.

**If you tighten the belt a lot and it still slips**, the problem could be the **drive belt under the motor cover that connects the motor to the front roller**. A loose drive belt feels similar to a loose walking belt. **Tightening the motor belt should be done by a trained service person.**

**Damage to the running belt resulting from improper tracking or tension adjustments is not covered under the Sole warranty.**
