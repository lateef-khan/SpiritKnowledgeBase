The chart is a log sheet with a **Date** column against four tasks:

| Task | Interval printed in the chart |
|---|---|
| Vacuum Under Motor Cover & Check Wiring | Every Other Month |
| Clean & Inspect Deck for Lubrication | Every Other Month |
| Lubricate Deck | Every 90 hours or sooner if dry |
| Inspect Belt Tracking - Adjust if necessary | Monthly |

**A stray "6." is printed directly under the chart heading** in the extraction, with no text after it. The manual gives it no meaning.

**Where the chart and the prose differ.** The lubrication page also gives **every 90 days regardless of usage** as an interval, which the chart does not carry. The belt and deck cleaning page asks for cleaning **once a month**, while the chart's "Clean & Inspect Deck for Lubrication" is every other month - those are two different jobs in the manual, not one.
