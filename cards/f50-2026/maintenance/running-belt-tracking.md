The treadmill is designed so the tread-belt **remains reasonably centred while in use**. It is **normal for some belts to drift near one side**, depending on the user's gait and whether they favour one leg. **If the belt continues to move toward one side, adjustment is necessary.**

**To set tread-belt tracking:**

- **A 6 mm Allen wrench (98)** is provided. **Make tracking adjustments on the left side bolt.**
- **Set belt speed at 3 mph.**
- **A small adjustment can make a dramatic difference which may not be apparent right away.**
- **If the belt is too close to the left side, turn the bolt only a 1/4 turn to the right (clockwise)** and **wait a few minutes** for the belt to adjust itself. **Continue with 1/4 turns until the belt stabilizes in the centre of the running deck.**
- **If the belt is too close to the right side, turn the bolt counter-clockwise.**

The belt may require **periodic tracking adjustment** depending on use and walking or running characteristics. **Proper belt tracking is an owner responsibility common with all treadmills.**

**ATTENTION: DAMAGE TO THE RUNNING BELT RESULTING FROM IMPROPER TRACKING / TENSION ADJUSTMENTS IS NOT COVERED UNDER THE SOLE WARRANTY.**

**This page and page 26 give different targets.** This page says to adjust **until the belt stabilizes in the centre**. The service checklist on page 26 says that if the user naturally favours one foot, **"track the belt slightly off-center to the side opposite from the belt movement"**. The manual picks no winner between the two.
