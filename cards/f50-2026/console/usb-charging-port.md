**Charge your personal device during your workout using the fitness equipment's on-console type-c USB port.** **The fitness equipment power must be on.**

1. **Connect your type-c USB charging cable to the USB Power Port and to your device.**
2. **Check that your device icon indicates it is charging.**

**Notes:**

- **A USB charging cable is not included.** Make sure a compatible USB charging cable is used.
- **Your device's "charging" icon may or may not indicate that it is charging.** Depending on how much current your device requires, the icon may not come on but the device is still charging, **possibly at a lower charge rate**.
- **The port provides up to 5Vdc/1.0amp.**
- **You cannot save workout data to a USB stick through this port; it is for charging only.**
