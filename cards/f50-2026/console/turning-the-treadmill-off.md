1. **The display automatically turns off, by going into sleep, after 15 minutes of inactivity when in the Stop mode.** Almost all power for the treadmill is off except for some circuits needed to detect a key press, telling the console to wake up. **When the console is asleep the treadmill draws very little power, about as much as a television turned off.**
2. **Removing the Safety Key is similar to entering sleep mode. Turning off the main power switch in the front of the treadmill will completely remove any power.**

**The 15-minute auto-sleep is a setting, not a fixed rule** - see the sleep mode card for how to turn it off or put the console into retail demo mode.
