1. **Remove the Safety Key.**
2. **Press and hold the Start and Speed 3 buttons and at the same time replace the Safety Key, until the window displays "F1", then press the Display/Enter key.**
3. **Set the display to Metric or Mile** (Miles/E vs Kilometers/H) with the speed knob key, then press Display/Enter. The **Incline window then shows the Grade return setting**; press Display/Enter.
4. **Distance window shows roller diameter.** Confirm the value, then press Display/Enter.
5. **Time window shows motor diameter.** Confirm the value, then press Display/Enter.
6. **Speed window shows pulley diameter.** Confirm the value, then press Display/Enter.
7. **Speed window shows the maximum speed.** Press Display/Enter.
8. **Distance window shows minimum velocity.** Press Display/Enter.
9. **Incline window displays maximum elevation.** Press Display/Enter.
10. **Press the Start button to begin calibration. The process is automatic.**

**The values the routine expects at steps 4 to 9 are on their own card** - check each one against that card before pressing Display/Enter.

The incline error remedy also ends in a re-calibration.
