**Security** is item 4 of the Engineering Mode Menu. It **locks the keypad so no unauthorized use of the machine is allowed**.

**When the child lock is enabled, the console will not allow the keypad to operate unless you press and hold the Start and Display/Enter buttons for 3 seconds to unlock the console.**

The other way to stop unauthorized use is to **remove the safety key** - the safety instructions ask for that after every workout.
