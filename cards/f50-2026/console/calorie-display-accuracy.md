The Calorie window **displays the cumulative calories burned at any given time during your workout**.

**This is only a rough guide used for comparison of different exercise sessions, and cannot be used for medical purposes.**

The programming pages repeat the point in stronger terms: **no exercise machine can give an exact calorie count** because too many factors determine a particular person's burn. Even at the same body weight, age and height, one person's burn may be very different from another's. **The calorie display is to be used as a reference only, to monitor improvement from workout to workout.**

**Entering the correct body weight will affect the calorie count** - that is part of program setup.
