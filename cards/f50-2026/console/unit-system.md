The manual gives **two routes to the same setting on the same page**:

- **Calibration routine, step 3.** After "F1" appears and you press Display/Enter, **you can set the display to show Metric or Mile settings (Miles/E vs. Kilometers/H)**. Use the speed knob key to select, then press Display/Enter.
- **Engineering Mode Menu, Functions item V - Units.** "**Sets the display to readout in Mile E or Metric H display measurements.**"

The engineering menu intro also names this as the headline example of what the diagnostic software is for: "**change the console settings from mile to Metric**".

Which unit is in force also changes the **odometer readout at power on** - the Distance window shows **miles, or kilometres if the treadmill is set to metric**.
