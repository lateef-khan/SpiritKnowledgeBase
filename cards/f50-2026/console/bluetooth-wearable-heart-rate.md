**The exercise equipment's Bluetooth device can also be connected to Bluetooth wearables.** **The Bluetooth wearables can receive signals even when the App is already in use.**

**Wear the Bluetooth wearable first, then commence connection. While connected, the console will show the heart rate value via Bluetooth.**

The manual does not name a pairing menu for this, and does not list which wearables are supported.
