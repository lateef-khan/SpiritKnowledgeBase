The machine ships with a **Bluetooth transceiver** that lets it interact with **compatible iOS and Android smartphones or tablets via the SOLE+ App**.

1. **Download the free SOLE+ App from the Apple Store or Google Play** and follow the instructions in the app to sync with the machine.
2. **Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your device.**
3. **Tap the icon at the top right corner to search for Sole equipment.**
4. **After the equipment is detected, tap Connect.**
5. **When the app and equipment are synced, the Bluetooth icon on the equipment's console will light up.**

The app can **sync workout data with fitness cloud sites**: **Apple Health, Google Health Connect, Strava or MapMyFitness**, with more to come.

Music streaming is a **separate** Bluetooth connection with its own name; see the speaker card.
