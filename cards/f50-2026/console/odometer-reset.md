**Odometer Reset** is item IV under **Functions** in the Engineering Mode Menu: it **resets the odometer readings**.

Those readings are the ones shown **briefly at power on**: the **Time window shows how many hours the treadmill has been in use** and the **Distance window shows how many miles (or kilometres)** the treadmill has gone.

**The manual does not say whether resetting the odometer also resets the 90-hour lube reminder.** The lube message has its own separate reset.
