**Power the treadmill on by plugging it into an appropriate wall outlet, then turn on the power switch located at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood.** **Ensure the Safety Key is installed, as the treadmill will not power on without it.**

When the power is turned on:

1. **The Speed window displays the current version of the software.**
2. **The Time and Distance windows then display odometer readings for a short time.** The **Time window shows how many hours the treadmill has been in use** and the **Distance window shows how many miles** (or kilometres, if the treadmill is set to metric) **the treadmill has gone**.
3. **The treadmill then enters idle mode, which is the starting point for operation.**

The odometer readings can be cleared from the Engineering Mode Menu.
