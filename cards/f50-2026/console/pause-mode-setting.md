**Pause Mode** is item II under **Functions** in the Engineering Mode Menu:

- **On** - allows **5 minutes** of pause.
- **Off** - the console **pauses indefinitely**.

**This is the other half of a rule stated on page 14.** The Pause/Stop/Reset feature there says that after pausing, **"After 5 minutes the display will reset and return to the start up screen"** without mentioning that the 5 minutes is switchable. Neither page refers to the other; both are reproduced as printed.
