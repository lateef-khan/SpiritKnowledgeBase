- **Incline may be adjusted any time after belt movement.**
- **Use the Incline knob** to achieve the effect you want. You may also choose a **more rapid increase or decrease by selecting key 3, 6 or 9 on the left-hand side of the console (incline)**.
- **The Incline window display indicates incline position as adjustments are made.**

Incline changes, like speed changes, **do not occur immediately** - the computer obeys the command gradually.

Two other rules touch the incline: **pressing Stop once sends the incline to zero percent**, and **the treadmill must be at minimum incline before folding**. The **Grade return** setting in the calibration routine controls whether stop returns the incline to zero at all.
