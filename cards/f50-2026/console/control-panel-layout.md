The page is a labelled photograph of the console. The **callout labels, in the order they appear in the extraction**, are:

```
CONTROL KEYS
INCLINE
COOLING FANS
INCLINE
CHARGE PORTABLE DEVICES WITH USB PORT
SPEED
SPEAKERS
SPEED
```

**INCLINE appears twice on the left and SPEED twice on the right**, which matches the rest of the manual: there is an **incline knob and incline quick keys on the left-hand side of the console**, and a **speed knob and speed quick keys on the right**.

**The picture itself is lost in the text extraction**, so which arrow points at which part of the console cannot be recovered from this source. The label text above is reproduced in printed order and nothing has been mapped to a position.

Named elsewhere in the manual: the **Start key, red Stop key, Display/Enter key, Program keys, speed quick keys 3, 6 and 9, incline quick keys 3, 6 and 9, the safety key socket on the console control head**, a **Bluetooth icon** that lights when the app is synced, and the **Speed, Incline, Time, Distance, Calorie and Pulse windows**.
