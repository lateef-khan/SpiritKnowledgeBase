1. **Attach the Safety Key to wake the display up**, if it is not already on.
2. **Press the Start key to begin belt movement.** **Use the speed knob button to adjust to the desired speed.** You may also use the **speed keys 3, 6, 9** to adjust the speed.
3. **To slow down the tread-belt, use the speed knob** to adjust it to the desired speed. You may also **press the speed adjust keys, 3 through 9**.
4. **To stop the tread-belt, press the Stop key or pull away the Safety Key.**

**The manual names the speed quick keys two ways on the same page**: step 2 says "the speed keys 3, 6, 9" and step 3 says "the speed adjust keys, 3 through 9". Both spellings are printed as shown; the manual does not say which is right.

Speed changes are **not instant** - the computer obeys the command gradually.
