**Beep** is item VI under **Functions** in the Engineering Mode Menu: it **turns off the speaker so no beeping sound is heard**.

The menu introduction gives this as one of its two headline examples - the software lets you "**turn off the beeping of the speaker when a key is pressed**".

**This is the key beep, not the music speakers.** The console's audio speakers are a separate Bluetooth feature and are not affected by this setting as far as the manual says.
