The console ships in "Sleep mode" with **three choices**:

| Setting | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| ON | The unit goes to an energy-saving state after **15 minutes** of inactivity |
| OFF | The console stays lit while the power is on |
| RETAIL | The console runs the **demo video after 3 minutes** of inactivity |

**How to switch, by display type:**

- **On LED displays:** hold the **Stop and Display keys for 5 seconds** to switch between ON and OFF.
- **On LCD displays:** press and hold the **Stop, Enter and Display keys for 5 seconds** to switch between ON and OFF.
- **On TFT displays:** click **"Sleep Mode" under Settings** to switch among the three choices.

**The key combinations only toggle ON and OFF.** Only the TFT route reaches RETAIL. **This manual never states which display type this machine has**, so pick the row that matches the console in front of you.

The Engineering Mode Menu carries a **Sleep Mode** item as well ("turn on to have the console power down automatically after 15 minutes of inactivity"); it is the same setting reached a different way.
