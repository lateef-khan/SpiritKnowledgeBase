**The exercise equipment can also play music wirelessly via Bluetooth.**

1. **Turn on your mobile phone or tablet's Bluetooth function.**
2. **Search for the name "BT-Speaker" in your device's Bluetooth menu.**
3. **Tap to connect.**

**Now your device can transmit music to the exercise equipment.**

**"BT-Speaker" is a different Bluetooth entry from the one the SOLE+ App connects to.** The app searches for Sole equipment from inside the app; the speaker is paired from the phone's own Bluetooth menu.
