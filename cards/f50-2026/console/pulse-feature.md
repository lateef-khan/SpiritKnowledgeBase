**The Pulse (Heart Rate) window displays your current heart rate in beats per minute during the workout.** **You must wear the wireless Bluetooth chest strap to show your pulse.** **The pulse value will display any time the upper display is receiving a pulse signal.**

**The manual never says a chest strap is supplied**, and it never lists hand-grip pulse sensors on this machine - yet the safety page on page 2 warns about "the pulse sensors" and their accuracy. Those two statements sit in the manual unreconciled.

A Bluetooth wearable can also feed heart rate to the console.
