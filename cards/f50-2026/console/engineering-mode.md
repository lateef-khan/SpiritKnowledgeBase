The console has **built-in maintenance/diagnostic software**. It can change console settings from mile to metric and turn off the key beep, among other things.

**To enter:** **press and hold the Start, Stop and Display/Enter keys. Re-power or insert the Safety Key, and the Time window will display "F2".**

**Use the speed knob button to make a selection and press the Display/Enter key to access the menu:**

1. **Key Test** - test all the keys to make sure they are functioning.
2. **Display Test** - tests all the display functions.
3. **Functions** - press Display/Enter to access settings and use the speed rotation button key to select:
   - I. **Sleep Mode** - turn on to have the console power down automatically after 15 minutes of inactivity.
   - II. **Pause Mode** - turn on to allow 5 minutes of pause, turn off to have the console pause indefinitely.
   - III. **Lube Message Reset** - reset the lube message. (Printed in the manual as "Lube Messagr Reset".)
   - IV. **Odometer Reset** - reset odometer readings.
   - V. **Units** - sets the display to read out in Mile E or Metric H measurements.
   - VI. **Beep** - turns off the speaker so no beeping sound is heard.
4. **Security** - locks the keypad against unauthorized use.
5. **Exit**

**This is a different menu from calibration.** Calibration is entered with **Start + Speed 3** while replacing the safety key and shows **"F1"**; engineering mode is **Start + Stop + Display/Enter** and shows **"F2"**.
