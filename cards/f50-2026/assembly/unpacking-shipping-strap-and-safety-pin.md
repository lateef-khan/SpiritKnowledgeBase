**Serious injury could occur if this folding treadmill is not unpacked properly.**

**The shipping strap.** A **deck restraint SHIPPING strap** is installed around the treadmill base to stop the treadmill unfolding during shipping. **If this strap is not removed properly the treadmill could spring open unexpectedly and cause injury** to anyone standing near it.

- **Position the treadmill flat on the ground, in the orientation it would be in if you were using it.**
- **Do not tilt the treadmill up on its side while removing the shipping strap** - this could cause the folding mechanism to spring open.
- **To remove the strap, pull up on the strap and cut it with a pair of scissors.**

**The safety pin.** A **safety pin is locked on the treadmill base** and also prevents accidental unfolding during shipping. **This safety pin has to be removed before powering on or using the incline function, to avoid damage to the machine. When removing the safety pin, pull it away from the treadmill base completely.**

Assembly Step 6 removes a separate **Locking Pin Assembly (26)** from the frame base and plugs an End Cap (112) into the hole it leaves; the manual does not say whether that is the same pin.
