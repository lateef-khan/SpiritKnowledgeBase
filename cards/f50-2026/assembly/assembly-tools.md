| Part no. | Item |
|---|---|
| #20 | Safety Key |
| #112 | End Cap |
| #96 | 13mm Wrench |
| #98 | M6 L Allen Wrench |
| #97 | Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver |
| #116 | Lubricant |

Three of these are not really tools: the **Safety Key (20)** is fitted in Step 5, the **End Cap (112)** is fitted in Step 6, and the **Lubricant (116)** is the tube of deck lube. The manual lists them all under "Assembly Tools".

The **M6 L Allen wrench (98)** is the one kept for belt tension and belt tracking after assembly; the tracking section calls the same tool a **6 mm Allen wrench**.
