1. Remove the treadmill from the carton and position it aside on a smooth, levelled floor. **Connect Computer Cable (Lower) (111) to Computer Cable (Middle) (110)**, then organize the computer cable to the **Right Upright (5)**.
2. **Install the Left Upright (4) and Right Upright (5) onto the Frame Base (2)** with **Button Head Socket Bolt (93) 8pcs, Split Washer (64) 8pcs and Flat Washer (59) 8pcs**, using the **M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (97)**.
3. **Fix the Left Upright (4) and Right Upright (5)** with **Button Head Socket Bolt (83) 2pcs, Split Washer (64) 2pcs and Flat Washer (59) 2pcs**, using the Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (97). **Do not tighten the bolts completely until Step 6 is finished.**

**NOTE: Please DON'T Tighten All Screws.**

Steps 2 and 3 both defer final tightening; Step 4 is where the manual says "After above assembly, tighten the screws in place", while Step 2 says to wait until Step 6 is finished. The manual gives both instructions and does not reconcile them.
