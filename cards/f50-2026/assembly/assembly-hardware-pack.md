The pack is bagged **by assembly step**. There is **no hardware bag for Step 1 or Step 6** - those steps use none.

| Step | Part no. | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | #83 | 5/16" x 1/2" Button Head Socket Bolt | 2 pcs |
| 2 | #64 | O 8 x 1.5T Split Washer | 10 pcs |
| 2 | #59 | O 5/16" x O 18 x 1.5T Flat Washer | 10 pcs |
| 2 | #93 | 5/16" x UNC18 x 3" Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 pcs |
| 3 | #83 | 5/16" x 1/2" Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #64 | O 8 x 1.5T Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #59 | O 5/16" x O 18 x 1.5T Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| 4 | #94 | 5/16" x 1-1/2" Button Head Socket Bolt | 2 pcs |
| 5 | #87 | M5 x 10mm Phillips Head Screw | 6 pcs |
| 5 | #95 | 3.5 x 12mm Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #83 | 5/16" x 1/2" Button Head Socket Bolt | 2 pcs |
| 7 | #64 | O 8 x 1.5T Split Washer | 2 pcs |
| 7 | #59 | O 5/16" x O 18 x 1.5T Flat Washer | 2 pcs |

The diameter symbol printed in the manual is the Greek phi; it is written **O** above because the extraction cannot be relied on for that glyph.

**One fastener used in the instructions is not in this checklist.** Assembly Step 4 calls for **Hex Head Bolt (84) 2pcs** tightened with the 13mm wrench, but no bag lists part 84. The manual does not say where bolt 84 comes from.
