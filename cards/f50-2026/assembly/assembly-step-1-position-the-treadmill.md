**Remove the treadmill from the carton and position it aside on a smooth, levelled floor. The rear should be at least 3' from any wall.**

**You must remove the plastic wrap and Styrofoam from beneath the unit before removing the Velcro belt.** Tilting the unit sideways after removing the belt may cause the unit to **fold and bounce upwards. This can cause serious injury.**

The same sentence about removing the treadmill from the carton is printed again as the first line of Step 2; the two steps overlap in the manual.

Note the two different clearance figures in this manual: **3 feet from a wall here**, and **3 1/2 feet minimum behind the treadmill** in the safety instructions on page 2.
