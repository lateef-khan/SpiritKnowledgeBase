**Rotate the foot pad (8) upward until it is against the running deck. Fix the foot pad (8) on the running deck with Button Head Socket Bolt (83) 1pcs, Split Washer (64) 1pcs and Flat Washer (59) 1pcs**, using the **M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (97)**. **Install the foot pad on the left side the same way as the right side.**

One bolt, one split washer and one flat washer **per side**, which matches the Step 7 bag of 2 pcs of each.
