1. **Remove the Locking Pin Assembly (26) from the Frame Base (2), then plug End Cap (112) into the hole of the Frame Base (2).**
2. **Lift the running deck to fold it.**

Step 6 uses **no hardware bag**. Step 2 says the upright bolts are not to be fully tightened **until Step 6 is finished**.

The unpacking page on page 5 also describes a **safety pin locked on the treadmill base** that must be removed before powering on or using the incline. The manual does not state whether that pin and the Locking Pin Assembly (26) are the same part.
