1. **Place the lower edge of the Pad Top Cover Assembly (12) against the upper edge of the Console Assembly (11). Then slide it forward to the Hex Head Bolt (84) 2pcs** as shown in Figure 1.
2. **Install the Pad Top Cover Assembly (12) on the Console Assembly (11) with Button Head Socket Bolt (94) 2pcs**, using the **M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (97)**.
3. **Fix the Pad Top Cover Assembly (12) on the Console Assembly (11) with Hex Head Bolt (84) 2pcs**, using the **13mm Wrench (96)**.

**NOTE: After above assembly, tighten the screws in place.**

**Hex Head Bolt (84) is not listed in any hardware bag** on the assembly pack checklist, which lists only bolt 94 for Step 4. The manual does not say whether bolt 84 is pre-installed or supplied loose.
