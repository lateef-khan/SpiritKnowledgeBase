1. **Align square hole B (below view) of the Console Assembly (11) with the L-shaped hook plates A on the Left Upright (4) and Right Upright (5). Install in direction C.** After the Console Assembly (11) is **fully seated and flush** with the uprights, **push it further in direction D**. Fix the console assembly onto the uprights with **Button Head Socket Bolt (83) 4pcs, Split Washer (64) 4pcs and Flat Washer (59) 4pcs**, using the **M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (97)**.
2. **Connect Computer Cable (Middle) (110) to Computer Cable (Upper) (11~26)**, then organize the computer cable to the Right Upright (5).

**NOTE: Please DON'T Tighten All Screws.**

The letters A, B, C and D refer to callouts on a drawing that the text extraction did not preserve; only the letters survive.

The upper cable is printed as **"(11~26)"**, a range rather than a single part number. The manual gives no other identifier for it.
