**The manual treats "belt does not stay centred" and "belt hesitates when walked or run on" as one row with one answer:**

> "The user may be walking while favoring or putting more weight on either the left or right foot. If this walking pattern is natural, track the belt slightly off-center to the side opposite from the belt movement. See General Maintenance section on Tread-belt Tension. Adjust as necessary."

**Two things about this row do not line up with the rest of the manual.**

- **It sends you to Tread-belt Tension, but the advice it gives is a tracking adjustment.** Hesitation under the foot is what the **tension** section describes; drifting to one side is what the **tracking** section describes. Both sections exist and neither refers back to this row.
- **It contradicts the tracking section on the target.** Page 24 says to adjust in 1/4 turns **until the belt stabilizes in the centre of the running deck**. This row says to deliberately **track it slightly off-centre**, opposite the direction of drift, when the user's gait causes it. **No winner is picked here** - work through both sections.

The tension section also warns that a belt which still slips after tightening may actually have a **loose drive belt under the motor cover**, which is a job for a trained service person.
