1. **Circuit breaker on the front grill tripped. Push the circuit breaker in until it locks.**
2. **Plug is disconnected. Make sure the plug is firmly pushed into the AC household wall outlet.**
3. **Household circuit breaker may be tripped.**
4. **Treadmill defect. Contact your SOLE dealer.**

**This list does not mention the safety key**, but the Getting Started page states plainly that **the treadmill will not power on without the Safety Key installed**, and the console shows "SAFE. Please replace the key to Operate" when it is missing. **Neither list mentions the other's causes, so work through both.**

Also check that the machine has not simply **gone to sleep after 15 minutes of inactivity** - that is normal behaviour, and pressing a key wakes it.
