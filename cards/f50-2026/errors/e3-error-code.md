**This is E3, a single digit. It is not E03 (hardware current is too large) and not E31 (overtemperature).** **These codes look alike and mean different things. E3 is the incline adjustment error, E03 is the hardware overcurrent error, and E31 is overtemperature. E01 to E06 are two-digit codes and are not E1 to E6.**

**Meaning printed in the manual:** "Incline adjustment error".

1. **Check whether the Incline cable is properly connected.**
2. **Connect the Incline cable and power it on.**
3. **Connect the Incline wire and re-calibrate it.**

Step 3 ends in the **calibration routine**, whose incline step is "maximum elevation 17".

Also remember the **safety pin on the frame base must be removed before using the incline function**, or the machine can be damaged - that is an unpacking step, not an error code, but it produces an incline that will not move.
