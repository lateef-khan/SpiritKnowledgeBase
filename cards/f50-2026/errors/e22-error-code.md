**This is E22. It is not E02 (Hall mistake) and not E2.**

**Meaning printed in the manual:** "The communication between the upper computer and the lower controller is incorrect."

1. **Check whether the cable between the electronic watch and the controller is loose.**
2. **Check whether the controller is damaged.**
3. **Check whether the electronic watch is damaged.**
4. **After 2 minutes of power failure, power it on again.**

**"Electronic watch" is this table's name for the console head** - the same part the rest of the manual calls the console or the upper computer. The cable it means is the **computer cable** joined in three sections during assembly: Lower (111) to Middle (110) to Upper.

Remedy 4 asks for the machine to be **off for two full minutes** before restarting, not simply switched off and on.
