**This is E31, not E3 (incline adjustment error) and not E03 (hardware current is too large).** **These codes look alike and mean different things. E3 is the incline adjustment error, E03 is the hardware overcurrent error, and E31 is overtemperature. E01 to E06 are two-digit codes and are not E1 to E6.**

**Meaning printed in the manual:** "overtemperature".

1. **Wait until the temperature of the machine is normal before running.**

**This row is cut off by a page break in the source.** E31 is the last row on page 21, the text ends at "before running" with no full stop, and page 22 opens with the Calibration Procedure. **Whether the manual printed further remedies for E31 cannot be recovered from this extraction** - the single step above is everything that survives, and nothing has been supplied to fill the gap.

Related heat causes in this manual: the deck running dry raises friction and stresses the motor and control board, and the maximum operating temperature is stated as 40 degrees C.
