**Meaning printed in the manual:** "Reminder to check lubrication under walking deck."

1. **Check lubrication under the walking belt.**
2. **Power the treadmill three times to reset the lube message.**

**LUBE is a reminder, not a fault.** It lights after **90 hours of use**. The manual's own advice is to **check for lubricant by reaching between the belt and deck every other month** and to lubricate if there is none, **even when this message is not lit**.

**Three ways to clear it are printed in this manual**, and the manual never says whether they are the same action:

- Page 21: **power the treadmill three times**.
- Page 25, step 6 of the lubrication procedure: **power the treadmill three times**.
- Page 22, Engineering Mode Menu, Functions item III: **Lube Message Reset** (printed as "Lube Messagr Reset").

**The cross-reference in this row is wrong.** It says "Reference belt deck lubrication procedure on page 28". The Belt/Deck Lubrication section is on **page 25**; page 28 is the back page of the warranty.
