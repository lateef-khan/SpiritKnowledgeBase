**This is E03, not E3 (incline adjustment error) and not E31 (overtemperature).** **These codes look alike and mean different things. E3 is the incline adjustment error, E03 is the hardware overcurrent error, and E31 is overtemperature. E01 to E06 are two-digit codes and are not E1 to E6.**

**Meaning printed in the manual:** "The hardware current is too large."

1. **Exceeding the rated load leads to excessive current**; the system self-protects, shows the error code, and **re-power recovery** clears it.
2. **Check whether the connection between the power socket plug and the motor is loose.**
3. **Replace the controller.**

**E01 and E03 share remedy 1 word for word** but then diverge: E01 goes on to a stuck machine, lubrication, motor noise and motor replacement, while E03 goes to the power socket connection. **Neither list mentions the other's causes, so work through both** if the code is not clearly readable on the display.
