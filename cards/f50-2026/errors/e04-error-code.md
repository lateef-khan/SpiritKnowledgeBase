**This is E04, a two-digit code. It is not E4.**

**Meaning printed in the manual:** "Phase loss".

1. **Motor is not connected properly, or not connected to the controller.**
2. **Replace the motor.**
3. **Replace the controller.**

**This list is the same three actions as E02 (Hall mistake)**, which is printed without numbers. The two codes point at the same parts for different reasons.
