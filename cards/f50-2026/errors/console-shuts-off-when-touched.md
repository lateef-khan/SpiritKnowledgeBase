**Symptom:** "Computer shuts off when console is touched (on a cold day) while walking/running."

**The treadmill may not be grounded. Static electricity is "crashing" the computer. Refer to the Grounding Instructions on page 3.**

The grounding page is where the fix lives: the product **must be grounded**, the **equipment-grounding plug must go into a properly installed and grounded outlet**, and if a temporary 2-pole adapter is in use its **green rigid ear lug must be connected to a permanent ground and held by a metal screw**.

**The page 3 cross-reference in this row is correct** - unlike the LUBE row on page 21, which points at the wrong page.
