**Symptom:** "Treadmill will only achieve approximately 7 mph but shows higher speed on display."

**This indicates the motor should be receiving power to operate. Low AC voltage to treadmill.**

- **Do not use an extension cord. If an extension cord is required it should be as short as possible and heavy duty 16 gauge minimum.**
- **Low household voltage. Contact an electrician or your SOLE dealer.**
- **A minimum of 120 volt AC current is required.**

**The extension cord rule here contradicts page 2**, which says an extension cord must be **14AWG or better with only one outlet on the end**. 14AWG is heavier than 16 gauge, so the two pages set different minimums. Both are reproduced as printed and no winner is picked.

The electrical page adds that a **10% or greater voltage variation affects performance and is not covered by the warranty**. An undervoltage condition severe enough to be detected shows as **E05**.
