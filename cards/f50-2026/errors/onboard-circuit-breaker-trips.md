**Symptom:** "Treadmill trips on board 15 amp circuit breaker."

**Cause: high belt/deck friction. See the General Maintenance section on lubrication.**

**Where the breaker is and how to reset it:** the dark-display row of the same table says the **circuit breaker is on the front grill** and that you **push the circuit breaker in until it locks**.

**This is a different fault from the house breaker tripping.** If the household breaker trips while the machine's own breaker stays in, the cause given is a **shared circuit**, not friction. If the breaker is an AFCI or GFCI type, the electrical page has its own explanation.
