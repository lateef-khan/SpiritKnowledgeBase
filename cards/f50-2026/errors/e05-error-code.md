**This is E05, a two-digit code. It is not E5. E06 is the opposite fault, overvoltage.**

**Meaning printed in the manual:** "undervoltage".

1. **Check whether the input power voltage is normal.**
2. **Check whether the connector of the power socket is loose.**

Low supply voltage has its own consequences elsewhere in this manual: a **10% or greater voltage variation affects performance and is not covered by the warranty**, and low household voltage is the stated cause of a machine that will only reach about 7 mph.
