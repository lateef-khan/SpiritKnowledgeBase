**The console shows: "SAFE. Please replace the key to Operate".**

**Put the safety key back on the console to continue using the treadmill.**

This is not a fault. **The treadmill will not start and operate without the safety key**, and **removing the key also secures the treadmill from unauthorized use**. **Removing the safety key is similar to entering sleep mode**; the machine is only fully dead when the main power switch at the front is off.

Pulling the key while running is a stop, not an error: **the treadmill stops, depending on speed, with a one to two step coast**. **Use the red Stop switch in normal operation.**
