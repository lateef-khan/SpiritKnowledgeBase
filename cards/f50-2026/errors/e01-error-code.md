**This is E01, a two-digit code. It is not E1, and it is not E03 (hardware current too large), which has its own remedies.**

**Meaning printed in the manual:** "Over Current Protection. Treadmill over loaded, controller protection is activated."

1. **Exceeding the rated load leads to excessive current**; the system self-protects, shows the error code, and **re-power recovery** clears it.
2. **A certain part of the treadmill is stuck**, so the motor cannot turn, the load is too heavy and the current is too large, and the system reports an error. **Adjust the treadmill to restart the operation, or increase the lubricating oil.**
3. **Check whether there is a flowing sound or burning smell when the motor is running, and replace the motor.**
4. **Replace the controller.**

Remedy 2 points back at deck friction, which is what the lubrication interval exists to prevent.
