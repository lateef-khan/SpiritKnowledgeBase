**Symptom:** "Tread-belt stops quickly/suddenly when tether cord is pulled."

**Cause: high belt/deck friction. See the General Maintenance section on lubrication.**

**What normal looks like:** the safety instructions say the treadmill should stop, **depending on speed, with a one to two step coast** when the safety key is pulled. A belt that stops dead instead has too much drag between belt and deck.

**Why it matters beyond the stop.** The lubrication page warns that when the lubricant dries out, friction places **undue stress on the drive motor, drive belt and electronic motor control board, which could result in catastrophic failure of these expensive components**, and that **failure to lubricate at regular intervals may void the warranty**. The same friction is what trips the on-board 15 amp breaker.
