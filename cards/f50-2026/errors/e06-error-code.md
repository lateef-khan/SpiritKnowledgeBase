**This is E06, a two-digit code. It is not E6. E05 is the opposite fault, undervoltage.**

**Meaning printed in the manual:** "overvoltage".

1. **Check whether the input power voltage is normal.**

**That is the whole remedy list for E06** - one line, and the shortest entry in the table. E05 gets a second step about the power socket connector; E06 does not.
