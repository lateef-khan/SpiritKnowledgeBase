Most noise comes from **loose screws**. Sometimes a part has deformed or shifted and is rubbing or moving roughly.

1. **Noise from the left and right pedals** is mostly pedal wear, with the pedal no longer moving smoothly. Replace the worn-out pedal.
2. **Left and right crank.** A loose crank makes noise and rough movement. Tightening the crank fixes it.

That is the whole list, and it is the last page of the manual.

**The heading on this section is wrong.** Section 10.3 is titled "Troubleshooting for the Noise during pedaling", but the problem line under it reads "Problem: The drive belt is slipping when pedaling", copied from section 10.2. The steps under it are the noise steps.
