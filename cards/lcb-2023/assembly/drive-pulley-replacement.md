1. Follow section 9.7 to remove chain cover L.
2. Use **C ring tweezers** to remove the **Ø20 C ring** on the crank arbor.
3. Use **two 11 mm open end wrenches** to remove **four 1/4" x 3/4" hex head bolts** together with **four 1/4" x 8T nyloc nuts** on the crank arbor and drive pulley, and take the drive pulley apart.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.

Step 1 removes only chain cover L, but the drive pulley sits behind chain cover R in section 9.7's description. Section 9.8, which also needs the pulley turned, removes both covers.
