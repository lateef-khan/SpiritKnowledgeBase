1. Follow section 9.7 to remove chain covers L and R.
2. Use a **13 mm wrench** to release the **M8 x 7T nut (77)** and take off the **J-Bolt (85)**. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **3 pcs of M6 x 7T Phillips head screws** on the **idle arm (9)**, and the **belt (14)** can be taken off.
3. To release the idle arm (9), unscrew **3 pcs of M6 x 15 Phillips head screws (56)** with **3 pcs of Ø1/4" split washers (73)** and **3 pcs of 1/4" x 13 x 1T** flat washers.
4. To refit the belt (14) and idle arm (9), use a Phillips head screwdriver to secure the **generator flywheel (20)** with **3 pcs of M6 x 15 Phillips head screws** together with **3 pcs of Ø1/4" split washers (73)** and **3 pcs of 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers (69)**. Turn the **drive pulley (15)** to put the belt on the pulley, hook the J-Bolt on the mainframe, and use the 13 mm wrench to tighten the **M8 x 7T nut (77)** on the J-Bolt until the belt is at an acceptable tension. Use an **audio tension gauge** to confirm the frequency is within **180 to 205 Hz**. Last, turn the drive pulley (15) and check the drive belt (14) is centred and not falling out of the pulley.

**Two defects in the printed steps.**

- Step 2 calls the idle arm fasteners "M6x7T Phillips Head Screws"; step 3 calls the same fasteners "M6x15 Phillips Head Screws (56)". M6 x 7T is a nut description, not a screw length.
- Step 3 ends mid-phrase: "3pcs of 1/4"x13x1T." with the part name missing. Step 4 completes it as 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers (69).
