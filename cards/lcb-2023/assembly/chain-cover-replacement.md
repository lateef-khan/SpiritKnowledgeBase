1. Take the pedals and crank arms apart. Separate the console mast cover, then use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **nine Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet metal screws** and **three 5 x 16 mm tapping screws**, which secure the left shroud.
2. Use a **15 mm open end wrench** to take the DC power cord apart and take the left shroud off.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **3 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws** to remove chain cover R.
4. To refit, install the right side case on the main frame, aligning the centre of the wheel with the centre of the axle, and secure it with **3 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws**. Reconnect the DC power cord. Match the left side case to the right side case and secure with **9 pcs of 3.5 x 12 mm self tapping screws** and **3 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws**. Follow step 2.1 to refit the cranks and pedals.

**The screw sizes do not match between removal and refit.** Step 1 removes nine Ø3.5 x **16** mm sheet metal screws from the left shroud; step 4 refits nine 3.5 x **12** mm self tapping screws. Step 4 also refers to "step 2.1", a section number this manual does not have; the cranks and pedals are section 9.6.
