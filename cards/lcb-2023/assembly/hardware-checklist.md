Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #50 | 3/8" x 53L | Carriage Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #71 | 3/8" x 25mm x 2T | Flat Washer | 2 pcs |
| #135 | 3/8" x16 x 12.5T | Cap Nut | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #51 | 5/16" x 5/8" | Hex Head Bolt | 7 pcs |
| #72 | 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #99 | 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T | Curved Washer | 1 pc |

**Hardware step 3**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #74 | 5/16" x 5/8" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #72 | 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| #103 | 5/16" x 1.5T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 4**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #58 | M5 x 12mm | Phillips Head Screw | 6 pcs |
| #161 | 3.5 x 12L | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |
| #165 | ø5 x 0.6T | Star Washer | 4 pcs |

**#165 Star Washer is new for 2023 and is unique to this model in this family.** The four star washers go under the four #58 Phillips head screws that hold the console assembly onto the console mast. Neither the B94 2023 nor the LCB 2019 pack contains them.

**Item numbers are not shared between models.** On the R92 and LCR recumbents `#71` is a hex head bolt and `#99` is a Phillips head screw, while on these uprights `#71` is a flat washer and `#99` is a curved washer. Always use the checklist printed in your own machine's manual.
