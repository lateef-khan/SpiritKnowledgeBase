1. Follow sections 9.7 and 9.8 to remove chain covers L and R and the drive belt.
2. Unplug the electric wires connected to the **induction brake (20)**, then use an **11 mm wrench** to unscrew **4 pcs of 1/4" x 3/4" hex head screws (54)** with **4 pcs of 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers (69)** and **4 pcs of Ø1/4" split washers (76)**. The induction brake (20) can then be released.
3. Follow the steps in order to refit the induction brake.

Part 20 is called the **induction brake** here and the **generator flywheel** in section 9.8. They are the same part.
