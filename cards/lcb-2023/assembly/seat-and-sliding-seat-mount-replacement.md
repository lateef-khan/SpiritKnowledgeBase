1. Use a **14 mm wrench** to loosen both hex head bolts and remove the seat.
2. Use a **14 mm wrench** to remove the cap nut.
3. Turn to take the brake tension knob apart from the fix plate, then remove the sliding seat mount.
4. To refit, install the sliding seat mount on the seat slider, then put the fix plate on it. Install the brake tension knob from the bottom upward into the fix plate and tighten with a **3/8" x 7T nut**. Last, insert the seat into the sliding tube and tighten both hex head bolts with the 14 mm wrench.

The step calls the part a "brake tension knob" although this bike brakes electrically. It is the seat height clamp knob, carried over from the B94 wording.
