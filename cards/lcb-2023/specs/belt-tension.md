**180 to 205 Hz, read with an audio (sound wave) tension gauge.** This machine is set by frequency, not by Newtons.

Set it by tightening the **M8 x 7T nut (77)** on the **J-Bolt (85)** with a **13 mm wrench** until the gauge reads 180 to 205 Hz.

The manual prints the same figure in both places, section 9.8 step 4 and the belt slipping answer in section 10.2.

This matches the LCB 2016 and LCB 2019 manuals, which also give 180 to 205 Hz. **The LCR 2023 manual gives a different range for the same belt system, 180 ± 10 Hz.** Do not carry that figure to this machine.

The B94 and R92 bikes use 450 N instead. Do not carry that to this machine either.
