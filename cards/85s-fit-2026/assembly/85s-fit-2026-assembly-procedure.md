**The machine arrives in two cartons.** Steps one to eight build carton A; three lettered
steps build the seat out of **carton B**, which is also where the console is packed.

Numbers in parentheses are assembly-drawing item numbers.

## Steps one to three - arms and console mast

Word for word the 8.5S build: the **left and right arms** with **two split washers (195)
and one bolt (187)** each side plus the **washer (71), flat washer (184) and bolt (140)**,
the **cup (58)** underneath, and then the **console mast** onto the main frame with **four
bolts (140), four split washers (127) and four flat washers (125)**, keeping tension on
the computer cables throughout and making sure they exit the top opening and stay outside
the top of the mast. Tools: **6mm Allen wrench (167)**.

## Steps four and five - the covers, and a latch the 8.5S does not have

1. Attach the **front top cover (76)** onto the main cover.
2. **Insert the Back Cover (77) into the groove first**, then press it into the plastic on
   the main frame. **Make sure the Back Cover latch is combined with the plastic on the
   main frame firmly.**

The 8.5S says only "attach the rear top cover (77) onto the main cover". This machine's
back cover latches, and the step tells you how.

## Step six - the console holder

Locate the **Console Holder Assembly (Upper) (12)** onto the console mast with **four M5 x
12mm Phillips head screws (204)**, using the **Phillips screwdriver (170)**.

## Step seven - the console cables

Connect the **Computer Cable (103)** and the **Power Connecting Wire (104)** to the
**Console Assembly (86)**. **Remark: Console Assembly (86) is in carton B.**

The console is a **15.6"** unit - the item table's whole description of it.

## Step eight - the console onto the bracket

Place the **Console Assembly (86)** on the console bracket and secure **4 pcs M5 x 12mm
Phillips head screws (204)** with the Phillips screwdriver (170).

**There is no articulating arm on this machine.** The 8.5S mounts a 10" touch panel on an
articulating arm with five slotted set screws and a leather cover, over four steps. This
one puts a 15.6" console on a fixed holder in two.

## Carton B step A - seat back bracket, hinge cover and beverage holder

1. Locate the **Seat Back Fixed Bracket (508)** to the **Seat Back Bracket (509)** with
   **two 3/8" x 1-3/4" x 34L socket head cap bolts (587), two Ø3/8" x Ø25 x 2.0T flat
   washers (588) and two 3/8" x 7T nylon nuts (610)**, using the **8mm L Allen wrench
   (611)** and the **13/14mm wrench (612)**.
2. Fix the **Hinge Cover (551)** to the seat back fixed bracket at the proper angle, then
   combine it with the **Lower Plastic Cover (553)**. Locate the **Beverage Holder (550)**
   to the **Cup Holder Sheet Metal (510)** and install it firmly.

## Carton B step B - the seat back

1. Locate the **Seat Back (537)** to the seat back fixed bracket with **four M8 x P1.25 x
   20L button head socket bolts (609)**, using the **5mm L Allen wrench (613)**.
2. Locate the **Seat Back Cover (552)** to the **six buckles** of the seat back and plug it
   in firmly.

## Carton B step C - the seat onto the main frame

1. Install the seat to the main frame with **two M8 x 60mm flat head countersink bolts
   (606), two Ø8.5 x Ø26 x 2.0T flat washers (608) and two M8 x 7T nylon nuts (607)**,
   using the **5mm L Allen wrench (613)** and the **13/14mm wrench (612)**.
2. Fix **four M8 x 20mm flat head countersink bolts (605)** to the left and right sides.

## The seat hardware is the 8.5S's hardware under different names

Items 587, 588, 605, 606, 607, 608, 609 and 610 carry the same numbers and the same
descriptions in both books. What changed is the wording: the 8.5S calls 606 a bolt M8 x
P1.25 x 60L and this book calls it a **flat head countersink bolt M8 x 60mm**. Same part.

## What the build never does

No step fits a levelling glide, though the parts diagram labels **J. Leveling glides**,
and no step fits a transport wheel. There is no support arm pad step and no bottle-holder
snap-in step; the beverage holder is built into the seat in carton B step A instead. See
[the levelling card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-85s-assembly-levelling-glides-by-hand.md).
