**The drawing labels three things and no more:**

| Call-out | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Touchscreen** | The screen. The parts page calls it a **15.6" touch panel console** |
| **Tablet Friendly Reading Rack** | A shelf above the screen |
| **Start, Stop, Level controls** | The physical key row below the screen |

**The physical keys matter on this machine**, because the manual uses them in its own procedures: the
**Start** key begins a workout as an alternative to the on-screen button, the **"+" and "-" buttons
on the lower control panel** set the workload, and **two presses of the physical STOP button** end a
session (`85s-fit-2026-console-workout-views-and-finishing-with-two-stop-presses`).

**The drawing names no USB port, no power switch, no bottle holder and no heart rate grip.** The
parts page on printed p. 10 adds a **bottle holder** but names no port either - which is a gap,
because maintenance mode asks you to upload videos **via USB**
(`85s-fit-2026-console-maintenance-mode-behind-ten-presses-on-hello-guest`).

**15.6 inches, not 10.** The 8.5S of the previous year carries a **10" touch panel console** on an
articulating arm with vertical and horizontal adjustment; this one is a larger screen with a reading
rack and no articulation described. **The two are different consoles running different software**
(`85s-2025-console-screen-overview-prints-a-factory-mode-banner`).
