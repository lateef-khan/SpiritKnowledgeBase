**Press "Hello Guest" ten times on the Home page.** That is the whole entry gesture - no key
combination, no hold, no code.

Maintenance mode gives access to **the machine information, various settings and service
procedures**. The manual lists none of them individually.

**Inside it, you can upload your own visual materials via USB.** Select **Retail Mode Video** under
the **Customization** tab and follow the on-screen guidelines to change the videos the console plays
**when it enters Retail Mode**.

**The manual never says how Retail Mode is entered or left**, and never says what it does other than
play those videos.

**The parts drawing names no USB port on this machine.** Printed p. 10 labels eleven parts - a
**15.6" touch panel console**, seat adjustments, handles, footplates, wheels, levelling glides and a
bottle holder - and **no USB socket, no UART port and no power switch**. The 8.5S drawing does name
them (call-outs R and S). **So the manual asks for a USB stick and never says where to put it.**

**There is no three-key hold on this console.** Do not carry Start-Stop-Enter over from the 7.0S,
7.5S or MS300 (`spirit-rehab-stepper-console-maintenance-menu-with-a-five-window-sensor-test`), or
from the CS800 and CRS800S steppers; this is a touchscreen and the gesture is ten taps on a word.
