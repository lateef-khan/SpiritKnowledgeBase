**Choosing a program.** Press **All Programs** on the home page. **Tap a program** to see it
in **Preview Mode**, where you can slide left and right - or use the arrow icons on both sides - to
move between programs of the same category. **Tap the program card again** to reach the **Program
Setup** page.

**Program Setup.** Change a value by **dragging the scale**, using the **Plus and Minus** buttons, or
**tapping the number for a direct input**. Different programs offer different parameters. Press
**Start This Program** to begin, or **Back** to return to the selection.

**Workout Mode.** The console is in workout mode once the time is counting.

- **Control the level from the panels on the left, top and bottom of the screen.**
- **Choose the display view from the three on the right side: Stats, Charts and Garmin.**
- **Parameters displayed in the Stats view can be customised** - press on the number and select your
  preferred option.

**Ending a workout - two ways, and one of them is a physical key.**

- **Press the physical STOP button on the console twice**, or
- **tap the Pause icon in the bottom right corner of the workout screen, then press Finish.**

**When the workout is over the workout summary pops up. Slide up and down on the right side of the
screen to see all the available charts.**

**Do not answer this from the 8.5S.** That console's workout screen switches between **Status,
Diagram, Symmetry and Controls**, is paused with **STOP** and offers **Resume** or **Finish** on a
pause screen (`85s-2025-console-settings-and-the-four-workout-views`). This one has three views, none
of them called Symmetry, and no Close Up Mode.

**The manual gives no list of the parameters** the Stats view can be set to, and does not say how
long the summary stays up or whether it can be recalled.
