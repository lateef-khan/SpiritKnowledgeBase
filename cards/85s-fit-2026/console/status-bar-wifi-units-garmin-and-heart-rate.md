**Four icons in the status bar at the top of the screen, and that is the whole list:**

| Icon | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **WiFi** | WiFi settings |
| **Gear** | **Language or units of measurement** |
| **Garmin logo** | Pairs a **Garmin device** (`85s-fit-2026-console-garmin-pairing-with-a-pin-code`) |
| **Heart Rate** | Pairs **a heart rate measuring device** |

**The Home page holds an age/weight setting and the most common workout choices** - immediate start,
a timed workout, and popular workout patterns. **A workout starts either from the Start button on the
screen or from the physical Start key on the console.**

**Press All Programs on the home page** to open the full selection of preset programs and fitness
tests.

**Quick Start:** after the console powers up, press the **Start** button. The Time counts up from
zero, all workout data starts to display, and **the workload is adjusted by pressing on the screen or
with the "+" and "-" buttons on the lower control panel**. **No login is required** - the word Hello
Guest is what the home page shows.

**This console names no Bluetooth setting, no ANT+, no FTMS, no cloud account and no login method.**
The 8.5S of the previous year names all of them
(`85s-2025-console-wifi-ble-ftms-and-ant-plus-connectivity`), and its status bar carries a Log Out
and a Bluetooth Device icon this one has not
(`85s-2025-console-screen-overview-prints-a-factory-mode-banner`). **The two touchscreens are not the
same software; do not answer one from the other.**

**The manual names no strap brand for the Heart Rate icon**, and gives no pairing steps for it - only
Garmin gets a procedure.
