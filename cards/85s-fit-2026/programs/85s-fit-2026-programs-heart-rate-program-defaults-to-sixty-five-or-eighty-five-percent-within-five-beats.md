*"The default value is 65% or 85% of your projected rate maximum. You have the option of changing
your target heart rate. The machine will attempt to keep you within five beats of your target heart
rate."*

That is the whole of what this manual prints about the program - **four sentences, no numbered
procedure, and no statement of which of the two defaults is offered first.**

**Five beats, not three to five.** The Spirit XE ellipticals say three to five; the 8.5S-Fit says
five. Do not substitute one figure for the other - see
`spirit-xe-programs-heart-rate-programs-hold-you-within-three-to-five-beats`.

**65% and 85% match this machine's own training page**, which recommends 65% for burning fat and 85%
for the cardiovascular system - see
`85s-fit-2026-programs-target-zone-computed-as-117-and-153-but-printed-117-to-144`. That is unusual
in the Spirit range, where the program keys and the training page normally disagree.

**The 8.5S of the previous year defaults to 65% only**, expressed as `MHR = (220 - age) x 65%`, and
lets the target be entered as beats or as a percentage - see
`85s-2025-programs-heart-rate-target-defaults-to-sixty-five-percent-of-220-minus-age`.
