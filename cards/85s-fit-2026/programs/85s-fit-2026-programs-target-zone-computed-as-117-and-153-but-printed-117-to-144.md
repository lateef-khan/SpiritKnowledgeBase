**The three calculation lines and the summary line on this page do not agree. Read the calculation
lines.**

Determine your **Maximum Heart Rate** with the formula **220 minus your age**. Your heart rate
training zone is **50% to 90%** of maximum. **65% of MHR is recommended for burning fat while 85% is
recommended for strengthening the cardio vascular system. This 65% to 85% is the zone to stay in for
maximum benefit.**

**Example**, for someone who is 40 years old, exactly as printed:

- 220 - 40 = **180** (maximum heart rate)
- 180 x .65 = **117** beats per minute (65% of maximum)
- 180 x .85 = **153** beats per minute (85% of maximum)
- *"So for a 40 year old the training zone would be **117 to 144** beats per minute."*

**144 is not 85% of 180; 153 is.** 144 is 80% of 180 - the upper figure the Spirit XS895 uses, on a
page that everywhere else says 85%. The summary line is a misprint left over from a 60%/80% version
of the same page.

*Confirmed three ways before carding: the native PDF text of page 47, a 300 dpi render read
visually, and a `tesseract --psm 4` pass over that render all give **117 to 144**. It is what the
page prints, not an extraction artifact.*

The page also prints a **Target Heart Rate Training Chart** by age from 20 to 65, with the Weight
Loss line at **65%** and the Cardiovascular line at **85%**, and ends by saying the heart rate
monitor may be used *"during manual mode or during any of the nine different programs"* - on a
machine with **no manual mode** and **ten** described programs; see
`85s-fit-2026-programs-ten-programs-on-the-touchscreen`.

**The other four rehabilitation steppers print no version of this page at all** - see
`spirit-rehab-stepper-programs-no-maximum-heart-rate-formula-and-no-training-zone-page`.
