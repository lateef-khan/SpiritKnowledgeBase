**This drawing is labelled differently from the 2015 XT385/XT485/XT685 diagram**, which names the
LCD, the speakers and the fan in marketing phrases and calls the number keys Select Keys
(`xt-2015-console-layout-muscle-and-heart-rate`). Here the eleven call-outs, clockwise from the top
right:

| Label | What the drawing says it holds |
|---|---|
| **SPEAKER** | One at each top corner |
| **DATA DISPLAY** | Pace, Distance, Time [Elapsed/Countdown], Calories, & Pulse |
| **HEART RATE PROFILE** | The bar graph to the right of the LCD |
| **DOT MATRIX** | Speed, Program Profiles, Incline, Lap Track, Avg. Speed, & Avg. Heart Rate |
| **MESSAGE CENTER** | Avg. Speed, Avg. Pulse, Max Speed, Altitude [elevation gain], Segment Time |
| **COOLING FAN** | The grille under the keypad |
| **SAFETY KEY** | Its socket below the fan |
| **CONTROL KEYS** | The bottom row - Incline and Speed rockers, Start, Stop, Enter |
| **QUICK KEYS** | The row of digit keys **0 to 9** between the program buttons |
| **PROGRAM BUTTONS** | Manual, Strength, Fat Burn, Cardio, Hill, Interval, Calorie, Fusion, User 1&2, HR 1&2 |
| **MUSCLE PROFILE** | The anatomical figure at the top left of the LCD |

**Two things this drawing labels that the 2015 diagram does not**: an **Altitude [elevation gain]**
readout in the message centre, and the **Safety Key**. The program buttons include **Fusion**, the
cardio-and-dumbbell interval program the 2015 XT485 and XT685 books also carry.

**The text on the pages that follow describes each part**: the muscle figure and its colours
(`xt-2015-console-muscle-activation-figure`), the heart rate profile with this book's own top band
(`xt485-2013-console-heart-rate-bar-graph-with-an-85-to-95-percent-top-band`), the Quick Keys
(`xt685-2010-console-quick-speed-and-incline-keys`), the speakers and the audio jack on the right side
(`xt-2015-console-speakers-and-audio-jack`) and the handrail speed and incline controls with their
disable switch (`xt-2015-console-handrail-controls-and-disable-switch`). The readouts the text lists
are on `xt-2023-console-readouts`.

**The book is the Australian 230-volt edition** (Boyles Fitness Equipment Pty Ltd, SPT0033 ver. C
2013); the console it draws is the one its text describes, and the label text is invisible to
`pdftotext`.
