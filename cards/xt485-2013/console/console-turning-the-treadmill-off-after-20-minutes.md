**Twenty minutes, not the thirty the 2015 XT books and the 2010 XT685 book print** for the same three
steps (`xt-2015-console-turning-the-treadmill-off`). Same instruction, different figure.

1. **"Display will automatically turn off (go to sleep) after 20 minutes (no key operations).** The
   treadmill will draw very little current in sleep mode (about as much as your television when it is
   turned off)."
2. **Remove the tether cord.**
3. **Turn off the main switch on the front of the treadmill, below the motor cover.**

**The sleep time-out is a switch, not a setting.** The maintenance menu's Sleep Mode entry only turns
sleep off so the console "will stay on all the time unless power is turned off"; no other time can be
set (`xt485-2013-console-maintenance-menu-with-a-motor-test-and-a-grade-return-among-seven-functions`).
**No figure in watts** is given for the standby draw.

Waking the console again is the Start key or the tether key - the Quick Start page says installing
the tether key wakes it - and what it then shows is the power-on sequence
(`ct850-2020-power-on-sequence`).
