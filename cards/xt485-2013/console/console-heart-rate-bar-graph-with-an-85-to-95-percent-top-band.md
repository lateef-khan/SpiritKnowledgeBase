**The top band is printed "85-95% or more" here; the XT385/XT485/XT685-2015 and XT685-2010 books print
"85-90% or more"** for the same three colours (`xt-2015-console-heart-rate-percent-profile`). Same
graph, one figure apart, so this book is kept on its own card.

"The console LCD screen will display your current heart rate anytime a pulse is detected. The Bar
Graph, located to the right of the LCD screen, will show your current heart rate % in relation to your
projected maximum heart rate, which is determined by your age that you entered during the
programming phase of any of the **12 programs**."

| Percentage of maximum | Colours lit |
|---|---|
| **50-60%** | **Amber** |
| **65-80%** | **Amber and Green** |
| **85-95% or more** | **Amber, Green, and Red** |

**The scale as printed has gaps** - nothing below 50%, nothing between 60 and 65, nothing between 80
and 85 - exactly as the later books have them.

**Twelve programs agrees with this book's own count** - nine factory presets, two user programs and
one Manual - where the 2015 and 2010 books say "any of the 10 programs" on this page and twelve
elsewhere. The drawing labels the graph **HEART RATE PROFILE**
(`xt485-2013-console-layout-eleven-labels-with-a-data-display-a-message-center-and-a-safety-key`);
the three colours are also the muscle figure's, for a different reading
(`xt-2015-console-muscle-activation-figure`). Where the pulse comes from is on `xt-2023-console-pulse-grip`.
