**The 2013 XT485 book prints a full list; the 2015 XT books print none** (`xt-2015-specs-no-parts-list`). The columns are `Dwg #`, `Part description` and `Qty` - **no Spirit part number**, unlike the 2007 dealer lists (`xt475-2007-specs-parts-list`) which carry six-digit numbers. 174 rows run 1 to 153; 12 whole numbers are absent (7, 26, 64, 78, 91, 95, 119, 121, 123, 129, 143, 144). The drawing is stamped `010302PM0130`, the same stamp format as the 2007 dealer drawings.

**The console is item 52 with twenty-eight sub-parts, 52~1 to 52~34** (52~11 to 52~15 and 52~33 are not printed): covers, speaker covers, drink bottle holders, wind ducts, battery cover, fan grill anchors, front console covers, a `400m/m_Safety Switch Module W/ Cable`, the fan assembly, two speakers with cables, `52~24 Amplifier Controller`, `52~25 270m/m_W/Receiver, HR`, the amplifier cable and console ground wire, a `9" Water-resist Rubber`, the `LCD Transparent Piece` and the console screws. Two further sub-groups: 21~3 `Magnet` (two, on the front roller) and 39~2 to 39~5 the hand-pulse wire and covers; 42~1 and 42~2 are two ferrite cores on the power socket.

## What the list names that the prose does not

- Drive: 34 `Drive Motor`, 35 `Incline Motor`, 36 `Motor Controller`, 20 `Drive Belt`, 21 `Front Roller W/Pulley`, 22 `Rear Roller`, 23 `Running Deck`, 24 `Running Belt`, 150 `Choke`, 151 `Filter`, 41 `Breaker`, 42 `Power Socket`, 43 `On/Off Switch`.
- Cables by length: 37 `500m/m_Speed Cable (Upper)`, 38 `800m/m_Incline Cable (Upper)`, 40 `1200m/m_Sensor W/Cable`, 48 to 50 three computer cables (800, 1150, 1300 mm), 51 `1000m/m_Ground Wire`, 54 and 55 the speed and incline adjustment switches with cable, 145 `400m/m_Audio Cable`, 152 `600m/m_Connecting Cable Of Motor`.
- Packed with the machine: 30 `Lubricant`, 75 `Safety Key`, 138 `Chest Strap`, 146 `Drink Bottle`, and the tools 131 `Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver`, 132 `M6_Allen Wrench`, 148 `Phillips Head Screw Driver`.
- Item 117, a `5 × 16m/m_Tapping Screw`, is the largest quantity at 60; item 120 is the same screw again, thirteen of them.
