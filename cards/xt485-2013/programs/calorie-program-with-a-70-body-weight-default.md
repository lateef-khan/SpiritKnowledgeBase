**Same program as the 2015 XT485's, with two defaults printed differently**: body weight **70#**
(2015: 150) and top speed **3 mph or 3 kmph** (2015: 3 mph or 5 kmph). Every other figure - 35, 300,
1.0%, the 3:00 warm up and cool down - is the same
(`xt-2015-programs-calorie-program-setup-xt485-xt685`).

1. Press the **Calorie** key. The display prompts you through the programming.
2. The Message Center blinks your **Age (default is 35)** - + or -, Enter.
3. It blinks your **Body Weight (default is 70#)** - + or -, Enter.
4. *"Use the speed + or - keys to adjust Calorie burn" (default is 300)* - Enter.
5. It blinks the **preset top speed (3 mph or 3 kmph)** - Enter.
6. It blinks the **preset top incline (1.0%)** - speed + or -, Enter.
7. It displays the **Program Time**, worked out from the data you entered in steps 2-6. **Start**
   accepts the calculated time; **Enter** returns to steps 3-6 to change the data, which recalculates
   the time. *(The 2-6 / 3-6 mismatch is printed.)*

- When Start is pressed the program begins with a **3:00 warm up** - 1:00 @ 1 mph/kmph, 1:00 @ 2
  mph/kmph, 1:00 @ 3 mph/kmph - or press Start again to bypass it.
- **Both the Calorie and Time windows count down to 0. If the Time expires before the calories reach
  0, the Time window starts accumulating time until the Calories reach 0.**
- After the program ends there is a **3:00 cool down** - 1:00 @ 3, 1:00 @ 2, 1:00 @ 1 mph/kmph - or
  press Stop to end.

The Preset Programs page describes the **Calories** profile as a quick progression to the maximum
speed level held for two thirds of the workout, with elevation at the maximum for 90% of it - the
description held by `ct900ent-calorie-program`.
