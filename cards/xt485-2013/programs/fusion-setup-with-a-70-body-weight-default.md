**Same eight steps and the same interval figures as the 2015 XT485, with two defaults printed
differently**: body weight **70#** (2015: 150) and top speed **3 mph or 3 kmph** (2015: 3 mph or 5
kmph). The worked session and the five dumbbell exercises are word for word the 2015 and 2023
pages (`xt-2023-programs-fusion-workout-structure`, `xt-2023-programs-fusion-strength-exercises`).

1. Press the **Fusion** key.
2. **Age (default is 35)** - speed + or -, Enter.
3. **Body Weight (default is 70#)** - + or -, Enter. **The calorie count in this program will not be
   accurate, because the machine cannot calculate calories expended while strength training.**
4. **Preset top speed (3 mph or 3 kmph)** - Enter.
5. **Preset top incline (1.0%)** - Enter.
6. **Number of intervals (default is 10; you may select 10, 20 or 30)** - Enter.
7. **Interval time (default is 1:00)**, the duration of both the cardio and strength intervals. On
   average you complete 15-20 repetitions of the strength exercise in a 0:30 interval; the longer the
   interval, the less weight and speed required. Enter.
8. **Recovery time (default 0:30)** after each cardio-and-strength pair - Enter.

The manual stops at step 8 and prints no closing Start step. Its Preset Programs page describes the
Fusion profile as the Interval profile - high cardio and strength intensity with recovery, incline
alternating between 25% and 65% of maximum - and says the speed and incline graphs are similar to
the Interval program's.
