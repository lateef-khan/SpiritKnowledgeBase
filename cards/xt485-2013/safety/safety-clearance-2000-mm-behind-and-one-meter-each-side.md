> Do not block the rear of the treadmill. Provide a minimum of 2000mm clearance between the rear of
> the treadmill and any fixed object. There should be a treadmill width or one meter of the space of
> clearance at each side of your treadmill.

| Side | Minimum free space |
|---|---|
| Rear | **2000 mm** to any fixed object |
| Each side | **a treadmill width, or one metre** |
| Front | not stated |

- **The rear figure is the one the sentence is built around**: do not block the rear of the
  treadmill, and keep 2000 mm to any fixed object behind it.
- **The side rule gives two measures and does not say which governs** - `a treadmill width or one
  meter`. The manual prints no width figure of its own to settle it.
- **No front figure, no ceiling height and no imperial conversion** are printed.

**This is the only XT owner's manual before 2023 that prints a clearance figure.** The five 2015
XT manuals print none at all (`xt-2015-safety-placement-requirements`), and the 2023 manuals added
20 in at the front and sides and 6 ft behind (`xt-2023-safety-placement-and-clearance`) - 1.8 m,
less than this book's 2 m. Quote the figure from the book in the customer's hand.

**The commercial CT machines quote an ASTM figure of 1 m behind and 0.5 m each side instead**
(`ct900ent-clearance-requirements`), and the 2024 black units 6 ft behind
(`spirit-2024-safety-treadmill-location-requirements`). None of those is this book's number.
