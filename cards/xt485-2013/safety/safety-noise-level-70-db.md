> The noise level for the treadmill is 70db

**One figure, no conditions.** The sentence is the last bullet of the safety list, printed without a
full stop, and gives no belt speed, no load and no A-weighting - it says what the noise level *is*,
not what it is below or under what test.

**The 2026 machines print the same number with the conditions this book leaves out.** The 4.0T says
sound pressure is `< 70dB` measured at 8 km/h with no load, and that noise emission under load is
higher than without load (`40t-2026-safety-sound-pressure-level`); the 8.0T says no greater than
70 dB at 12 km/h (`80t-2026-safety-sound-pressure-level`). Those are other machines' statements
and other test conditions; for an xt485-2013 the answer is the bare `70db`.

**No other XT owner's manual prints a noise figure.** The 2015 and 2023 XT books have no such line;
it is one of the sentences the Australian edition adds (`xt485-2013-safety-instructions-list`).
