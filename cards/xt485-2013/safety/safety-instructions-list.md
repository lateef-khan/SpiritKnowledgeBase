**This is not the North American XT page. The 2013 book is an Australian edition - its dealer letter
is signed `BOYLES FITNESS Equipment Pty Ltd.`, its outlet is 230 volt, its cord gauge is metric and
its clearance is in millimetres - and its list is one merged list, not the two lists the 2015
manuals print.**

> **WARNING** - Read all instructions before using this appliance.
>
> **DANGER** - To reduce the risk of electric shock disconnect your treadmill from the electrical
> outlet prior to cleaning and/or service work.
>
> **WARNING** - To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons, install the
> treadmill on a flat level surface with access to a **230-volt, 10-amp grounded outlet** with only
> the treadmill plugged into the circuit.
>
> DO NOT USE AN EXTENSION CORD UNLESS IT IS A **1m㎡** OR BETTER, WITH ONLY ONE OUTLET ON THE END.
> The treadmill should be the only appliance in the circuit in which it is connected.
>
> DO NOT ATTEMPT TO DISABLE THE GROUNDED PLUG BY USING IMPROPER ADAPTERS, OR IN ANY WAY MODIFY THE
> CORD SET. A serious shock or fire hazard may result along with computer malfunctions. See
> Grounding Instructions.

Then twenty-two bullets:

- Do not operate treadmill on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and
  treadmill may result.
- Do not block the rear of the treadmill. Provide a minimum of **2000mm** clearance between the rear
  of the treadmill and any fixed object. There should be a treadmill width or one meter of the
  space of clearance at each side of your treadmill.
- Keep children away from the treadmill. There are obvious pinch points and other caution areas
  that can cause harm.
- Keep hands away from all moving parts.
- Never operate the treadmill if it has a damaged cord or plug. If the treadmill is not working
  properly, call your dealer.
- Keep the cord away from heated surfaces.
- Do not operate where aerosol spray products are being used or where oxygen is being
  administered. Sparks from the motor may ignite a highly gaseous environment.
- Never drop or insert any object into any openings.
- Do not use outdoors.
- To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position, **remove tether cord**, then remove the plug
  from the outlet.
- Do not attempt to use your treadmill for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended.
- The pulse sensors are not medical devices. Various factors, including the user's movement, may
  affect the accuracy of heart rate readings. The pulse sensors are intended only as exercise aids
  in determining heart rate trends in general.
- Use handrails provided; they are for your safety.
- Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable for use on your
  treadmill. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue.
- **Please verify and make sure safety key functions properly before using the treadmill. Always
  wear the safety key clip while in use.**
- **For energy savings, always unplug the power cord when treadmill is not in use.**
- This appliance is not intended for use by persons (including children) with reduced physical,
  sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge, unless they have been given
  supervision or instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible for their
  safety.
- Children should be supervised to ensure that they do not play with the appliance.
- **In case of emergency during workout, hold both handle bars and step away form the walking
  belt.** Lift both of your feet out of the walking belt and step on each side of the side rail.
- **To begin with your workout, step on each of the side rail, grasp the handle bars and set the
  speed at the minimum.**
- **The noise level for the treadmill is 70db**
- SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!

**What this list has that the 2015 XT list does not:** a 230-volt, 10-amp outlet
(`spirit-treadmill-safety-outlet-220-volt-10-amp`) and a 1 mm² cord
(`40t-2026-safety-extension-cord-1-mm2-or-better`) where the 2015 books say 110-volt, 15-amp and
14AWG; a clearance rule (`xt485-2013-safety-clearance-2000-mm-behind-and-one-meter-each-side`)
where the 2015 books print none; a disconnect sequence that takes the tether cord off first, which
the 2015 books omit and the CT800ENT-2022 alone shares; a safety-key check and a wear-the-clip
rule; an energy-saving unplug rule; an emergency step-off and a starting procedure; and a noise
line (`xt485-2013-safety-noise-level-70-db`). `step away form` is printed that way.

**What it lacks:** the DANGER unplug-after-use line, the blanket-or-pillow, unattended,
carry-by-cord, air-openings, close-supervision, `household use` and under-13 items of the 2015
appliance list, the Proposition 65 box, and any user weight figure
(`xt-2015-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`). Its reduced-capability sentence carries the
supervised exception the 2015 XT books do not print (`xt485-2013-safety-who-may-use-the-treadmill`).

**The electrical, grounding and operation pages that follow are the North American CT850 pages
with 230 volts written in** - the GFCI ban, the high-inrush breaker paragraph with the Grainger
and QO120HM examples, 40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit, a grounding page naming `a nominal 230-volt
circuit` (the `230` set in blue where the rest of the page is black) with the temporary-adapter
paragraph and a North American two-blade-and-pin plug drawing, and the tether-cord section
(`spirit-never-use-a-gfci-wall-outlet`, `spirit-house-breaker-needs-a-high-inrush-type`,
`ct900ent-operating-environment-voltage`, `spirit-product-must-be-grounded`, `xt-2015-safety-tether-cord`).

**The book prints no serial number panel and no product labels page** - the word `serial` does not
occur in it (`xt-2015-safety-product-labels-and-serial-number`). It is a manualslib download,
stamped `XT485 / SPT0033_ver. C (2013)` on the dealer letter, with a March 2014 PDF date.
