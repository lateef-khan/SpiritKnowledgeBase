Three bullets of the safety list answer this, and the manual never gathers them:

> Keep children away from the treadmill. There are obvious pinch points and other caution areas
> that can cause harm.

> This appliance is not intended for use by persons (including children) with reduced physical,
> sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge, unless they have been given
> supervision or instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible for their
> safety.

> Children should be supervised to ensure that they do not play with the appliance.

- **No age figure is printed anywhere in the book.** Children are kept away from the machine and
  supervised so that they do not play with it, without a number.
- **Reduced capability or lack of experience: not intended - unless supervised or instructed** by
  a person responsible for their safety. The exception is in the manual's own words.
- **No close-supervision-near-the-machine bullet**, no `invalids or disabled persons` wording and
  no `household use` line: this is the merged Australian list, not the appliance list
  (`xt485-2013-safety-instructions-list`).

**The 2015 XT manuals say something different on both points.** They print the reduced-capability
sentence flatly, with no supervised exception, and add `Keep children under the age of 13 away from
this machine` (`xt-2015-safety-who-may-use-the-treadmill`). So a supervised user of reduced
capability is admitted by this book and not by the 2015 book, and 13 is a 2015 figure this book
never prints - do not quote it for an xt485-2013.

**The CT800 and CT850 books of 2016 to 2020 print this card's exception together with the age-13
rule** (`spirit-ct800-safety-who-may-use-the-treadmill`); this book has the exception and not the
age.
