**This is the 2013 procedure: two routes, and the quantity is "most of the lubricant" or "the whole tube". The
2015 books replaced it with two 1/8" × 15" S-shaped lines** (`xt-2015-maintenance-deck-lubrication-procedure`)
and the 2023 books with half a bottle a side (`xt-2023-maintenance-deck-lubrication-procedure`). Neither of those
is this book.

## Route 1 - if you can reach under the belt about 6" on each side

1. **Unplug the electrical cord.**
2. At the middle of the deck, **lift up on the belt and reach under with the tube of lubricant. Squirt most of
   the lubricant on the deck surface.**
3. **Repeat the process on the opposite side.**
4. Plug the cord back in and **walk on the belt at a moderate speed for five minutes.**

## Route 2 - if you cannot reach under the belt

1. With the **6 mm Allen wrench** supplied, loosen the **two rear roller adjustment bolts** in the rear end caps
   enough to get your hand under the belt - **5 to 10 turns**. Loosen both bolts the **same** number of turns
   and **remember how many**, because they go back to exactly that point afterwards.
2. With the belt loose, **wipe the deck with a clean lint-free cloth** to remove any dirt.
3. Apply the **whole tube** onto the deck surface about **45 cm (18 inches) from the motor cover**: squeeze it
   out across the deck, parallel to the motor cover, in about a **one-foot line**, "like toothpaste on a
   toothbrush", in the middle of the deck at equal distance from both belt edges - about where your feet land.
   Walk on the treadmill first to note where that is; a runner's feet land somewhere else.
4. **Tighten the rear roller bolts the same number of turns** as you loosened them.
5. Run the treadmill at about **5 kmph (3 mph) with no one on it for a minute or two** and make sure the belt
   stays in the middle of the deck; if it tracks to one side, follow the tracking instructions
   (`xt-2023-maintenance-treadbelt-tracking`).
6. **Walk, not run, on the treadmill immediately for at least 5 minutes** so the lubricant spreads evenly.

If you bought a silicone-based Lube-N-Walk kit, follow the instructions that come with it
(`xt485-2013-maintenance-lube-n-walk-is-the-only-approved-kit-silicone-not-paraffin`).

**What is not printed:** no S-pattern, no line width, and no console reset sequence for the lube reminder - the
2015 books add all three. The interval is every **180 hours**, with a console indicator that lights at 180 hours
(`xt-2015-maintenance-lubrication-interval-180-hours`); the 2015 XT485 changed it to 90.
