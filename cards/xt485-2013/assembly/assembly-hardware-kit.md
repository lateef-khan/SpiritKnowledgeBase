Four panels of captioned drawings, one per step, and no single combined list:

| Step | Item | Part | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 130 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 5/16" x 15mm | 10 |
| 1 | 147 | Flat Washer | 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T | 2 |
| 1 | 128 | M5 Speed Nut Clip | *(no size)* | 4 |
| 2 | 113 | Split Washer | Ø10 x 2.0T | 4 |
| 2 | 133 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | 4 |
| 3 | 120 | Tapping Screw | 5 x 16mm | 4 |
| 4 | 122 | Sheet Metal Screw | 3.5 x 12m/m | 10 |

**Every count matches its step**: eight bolts plus two more with the two washers in step 1, four speed nuts, four console bolts and washers, four tapping screws, ten sheet metal screws.

**Against the 2015 XT385/XT485 kit, the same item numbers carry different counts and sizes.** The 2015 kit has six speed nuts (128) not four, eight of bolt 130 not ten, six tapping screws (120) not four, and prints bolt 133 as 3/8" x 1-1/2" where this panel prints **3/8" x 1-3/4"**. Items 147 (the two washers) and 122 (the ten sheet metal screws) do not appear in the 2015 tables at all, and the 2015 handrail-support bolts (150) and mast-cover screws (142) do not appear here. Read the numbers only against this book. No torque is printed.
