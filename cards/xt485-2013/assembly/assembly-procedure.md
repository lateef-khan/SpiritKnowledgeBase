**This is a four-step build, not the six-step XT385/XT485 2015 build.** It shares the 2015 machines' tool numbers and some part numbers (uprights 4 and 5, console 52, bolts 130 and 133, speed nuts 128, tapping screws 120), but its counts and its order are its own: ten upright bolts rather than eight, no handrail support, and the bottle holder built from two halves on ten sheet metal screws.

## Step 1 - upright tube assembly

1. Install the **four speed nuts (128)** onto the tabs of the frame.
2. Take the **Left and Right upright tubes (5 and 4)** and slip the **upright covers (67 and 61 left, 66 and 62 right)** onto the tubes (observe that the cover orientation is correct). Connect the **Middle Console Cable (50)** that exits from the bottom of the right upright tube with the **lower Console Cable (49)**. **Note: partially tighten all bolts in this step. This will allow for easier console assembly.**
3. Insert the Right and Left Uprights (4 and 5) into the **Frame Base (2)** and attach with **eight Button Head Socket Bolts (130)** using the Allen wrench/screwdriver (131). Attach **two more Button Head Socket Bolts (130) and two Flat Washers (147)** on the inside surface of the upright tubes. **Be careful not to pinch the wire** when installing the upright.

## Step 2 - console assembly

1. Connect the **Speed Adjustment Switch Cable (54)** to the **Speed Cable (Upper) (37)**. Connect the **Incline Adjustment Switch Cable (55)** to the **Incline Cable (Upper) (38)**. Connect the **Computer Cable (Middle) (50)** and **Computer Cable (Upper) (48)**.
2. Insert the **Console Assembly (52)** into the right and left Uprights (4 and 5) and secure with **four Button Head Socket Bolts (133)** and **four Split Washers (113)** using the **M6 Allen Wrench (132)**. Now go back and **completely tighten the ten bolts inserted in Step 1** that join the upright tubes to the frame base.

## Step 3 - base covers

1. Attach the **Frame Base Covers (Left 61, Right 62)** to the Frame Base (2) with **four Tapping Screws (120)** using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (148).

## Step 4 - plastic parts assembly

1. Attach the **lower half of the bottle holder (Right 66, Left 67)** to the right and left Uprights (4 and 5) and **Console Support (6)** with **ten Sheet Metal Screws (122)** using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (148).

**Items 61, 62, 66 and 67 are named twice with two meanings**: step 1 calls them the upright covers that slip onto the tubes, steps 3 and 4 the frame base covers and the bottle holder halves. They are the same four plastic parts - the covers slid on in step 1 are fixed down in steps 3 and 4. The console cable is numbered 50 as "Middle Console Cable" in step 1 and "Computer Cable (Middle)" in step 2. No torque is printed.

The XT685 2010 book is the nearest relative: four steps, speed nut clips, a cross brace instead of a console support, and its own item numbers. Folding, transport and powering on are on the linked cards.
