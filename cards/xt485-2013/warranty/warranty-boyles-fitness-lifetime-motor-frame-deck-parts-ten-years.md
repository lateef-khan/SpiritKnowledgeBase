**This book's warranty is given by Boyles Fitness, an Australian distributor, not by Spirit Fitness.** The page is headed *WARRANTY, SAFETY AND ASSEMBLY INFORMATION - SPT0033 - XT485* and opens: *"BOYLES FITNESS warrants their treadmill to be free from defects in material and workmanship under normal use and service conditions."* SPT0033 is Boyles' own product code for the machine; it appears nowhere in a Spirit book.

The periods are printed as three lines, not a table with columns:

| Period | Covers |
|---|---|
| Life Time | Motor and Life Time Frame |
| Life Time | Running Deck |
| 10 Years | Parts |

**No labor period is printed anywhere in the book.** The three lines are the whole coverage statement; the Spirit residential table for the same treadmill family prints Labor 1 Year (`xt-2023-warranty-labor-period`), and that figure is not this book's.

**Coverage runs from the date of purchase to the original retail purchaser only**, and Boyles' obligation is "limited to replacing or repairing, at BOYLES' option, the product or parts therein".

**This is not the Spirit XT warranty.** The 2015 and 2023 Spirit XT485 books warrant the frame, motor and deck for lifetime, parts 10 years and labor 1 year under a Spirit Fitness page with a US service address (`xt-2023-warranty-residential-coverage-periods`); the figures coincide on the motor, frame, deck and parts but the warrantor, the conditions and the claims route are different - see `xt485-2013-warranty-boyles-fitness-conditions-and-exclusions` and `xt485-2013-warranty-australian-consumer-law-six-months-and-claims`.
