**Two documents, and the greater period wins.** The book prints a second warranty page headed *WARRANTY INFORMATION - SPT0033*, written for the Australian Consumer Law that took effect on January 1, 2012. It says this statement "compliments the warranty found in the manual" and that **"if the warranty periods offered in the two documents are different then the greater of the two shall be honoured."** Its own period is short: *"The XT485 Treadmill are warranted to be free from defects in material and workmanship under normal use and service conditions for a period of **6 months** from the date of purchase."* The lifetime and ten-year figures on the page before it are therefore the ones that govern (`xt485-2013-warranty-boyles-fitness-lifetime-motor-frame-deck-parts-ten-years`).

The page then recites the statutory position: the benefits are in addition to the rights under the Competition and Consumer Act 2010, the goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded, and the buyer is entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure, to compensation for other reasonably foreseeable loss, and to repair or replacement if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality.

## Making a claim

1. Direct the enquiry to the Service number, **07 3272 7010** (8:00am - 4:00pm Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays; website www.bfe.net.au). Have the proof of purchase, the date of purchase, the retailer's name and the brand on the product; photos and scanned receipts may be requested.
2. A service representative assesses the claim. For a **minor fault** they may offer a replacement spare part, a replacement unit, a repair or another suitable remedy; for a **major fault** a replacement, a repair, or a return to the store for a full refund; if the product was damaged through abnormal use, no refund or repair.
3. **Replacements, spare parts and repairs by the Service Centre are organised and paid for by Boyles Fitness, and collection of the item, where required, is at their expense.** Do not return a product without authorisation - that is at the owner's expense.
4. On receiving a claim the Service Centre sends, by post or email, a "repair and refurbished goods or parts notice". A request for compensation needs documentary evidence of the loss and that it was a reasonably foreseeable result of a failure to comply with a consumer guarantee.

The warrantor is named at the foot of the page: **Boyles Fitness Equipment Pty Ltd, 130 Carrolls Road, Menangle NSW 2568.**

**This is not the Spirit Fitness service route.** The Spirit XT books send the owner to the selling dealer, then a service organization, then (870) 935-1107, with freight and trip charges on the owner (`xt-2023-warranty-service-steps`); none of that applies to a machine sold under this book, and the Spirit page's 48-contiguous-states limit (`xt-2023-warranty-geographic-coverage`) is not printed here.
