Five checks, in the order the manual lists them:

1. Tether cord not in position.
2. Circuit breaker on front grill tripped. Push circuit breaker in until it locks.
3. Plug is disconnected. Make sure plug is firmly pushed into **AC household wall outlet**.
4. **Household** circuit breaker may be tripped.
5. Treadmill defect. Contact your dealer.

**This is the one Spirit treadmill owner's manual that names no outlet voltage in this row.** The 2015 and 2023 XT manuals and the CT850 2018 print *115 VAC* (`ct900ent-display-does-not-light`), the CT850 2016 and 2020 owner's manuals *110 - 120 VAC*, the CT850 service manuals a plain *120 VAC* (`ct850-2016-display-does-not-light`). The 2013 XT485 says only *AC household wall outlet*, and calls the panel breaker the *household* breaker where the others say *breaker panel*.

**The reason is the market.** This is the 230 V export edition of the XT485 - its warranty page is written to the Australian *Competition and Consumer Act 2010*, and its 7 mph row demands *a minimum of 230 volt AC* (`xt485-2013-errors-only-reaches-12-kmph-7-mph-16-gauge-and-230-volt`). A 115 VAC figure would have been wrong for the outlets this book was printed for, so the row does not name one.

Steps 1, 2 and 5 are the same in every Spirit owner's manual that prints this row. The XT485 2015 service manual answers the same symptom with a measured procedure and both supply voltages (`spirit-xt-errors-display-does-not-light-220-vac-or-110-vac`).
