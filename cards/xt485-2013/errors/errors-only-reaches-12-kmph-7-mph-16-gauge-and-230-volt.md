The 2013 owner's manual, word for word:

> **Treadmill will only achieve approximately 12 kmph (7 mph) but shows higher speed on display**
> This indicates motor should be receiving power to operate. Low AC voltage to treadmill. Do not use an extension cord. If an extension cord is required it should be as short as possible and heavy duty **16 gauge minimum**. Low household voltage. Contact an electrician or your dealer. **A minimum of 230 volt AC current is required.**

**230 volts, and kilometres first.** This is the 230 V export edition of the XT485 - the same row in the 2015 and 2023 XT owner's manuals reads *7 mph* and *a minimum of 110 volt AC* (`ct900ent-speed-display-mismatch-caps-at-7mph`), and the XT485 2015 service manual's version reads *10 kph* on a *220 volt 50 Hz* supply (`spirit-xt-errors-only-reaches-10-kph-220-volt-50-hz`). Three different figures for what looks like one row; quote the one in the caller's own book.

**The cord figure did not change.** 16 gauge minimum is what the 2015 and 2023 XT manuals print too - only the outlet voltage moved with the market. The 2013 CT850 of the same year asks for 12 gauge and 120 volt AC at 60 Hz instead (`ct850-2013-errors-only-reaches-7-mph-12-gauge-and-120-volt-60-hz`).

**The cause is the same everywhere**: the motor is getting power but not enough of it, so the belt turns below the commanded speed while the display reads the command.
