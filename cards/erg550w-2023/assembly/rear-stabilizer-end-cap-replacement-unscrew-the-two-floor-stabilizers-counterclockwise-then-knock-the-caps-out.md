Take out the two **Adjustable Floor Stabilizers (19)** anticlockwise - they screw into the end caps - and knock out the two **Rear Stabilizer End Caps (18)**.

No screws hold these caps, unlike the front ones. The floor stabilizers are the levelers the owner's manual turns to balance the rower.
