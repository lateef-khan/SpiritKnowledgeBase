1. Take out the chain cover as on the chain-cover page.
2. Use two open-ended wrenches to take out the **two outer hexagon nuts** on the locking belt pulley, then take out the belt pulley (figure 1).
3. Use the S4 wrench to take out the **6 M6 x 15 screws** on the bearing seat and take out the bearing seat (figure 2).
4. Use locking pliers and an open-end wrench to take out the **flange nut**, and take out the drawstring pulley (figure 3).

The flange nut is M10 x P1.0 (58); the drawstring pulley (44) sits on the volute spring (49) that retracts the strap, so expect spring load when the nut comes off. The parts list's ten M6 x 15 round head screws (61) include the six here.
