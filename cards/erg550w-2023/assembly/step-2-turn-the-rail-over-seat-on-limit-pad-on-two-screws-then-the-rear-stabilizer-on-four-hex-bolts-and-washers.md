1. Turn over the **Slide Rail (2)** and the **Seat (17)** as shown - the rail is worked upside down.
2. Slide the seat onto the slide rail.
3. Secure the **Limit Pad (40)** with **2 Hex Socket Screws (65, M6 x 10)**. Then attach the **Rear Stabilizer (3)** to the slide rail with **4 Flat Washers (27) and 4 Hex Bolts (62, M8 x 10)**.
4. Tighten with the Allen Wrench with Phillips Screwdriver (96) and the Open Wrench (97).

The hardware page calls 40 a "rubber pad"; the parts list a "limit pad" - the same seat stop.
