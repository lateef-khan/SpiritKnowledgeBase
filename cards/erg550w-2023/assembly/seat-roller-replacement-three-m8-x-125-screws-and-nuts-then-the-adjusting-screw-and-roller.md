1. Take out the **3 M8 x 125 screws and nuts** with an Allen wrench and an open-end wrench (figure 1).
2. Then take out the adjusting screw, take out the seat roller and replace the roller.

Three rollers (39, POM) on three bushings (53); the "adjusting screws" (71, M6 x 35, two of them) set the side rollers against the rail. **The parts list counts only two M8 x 125 hex socket bolts (108)** against the three this page removes - the third long bolt is the M8 x 130 (42) on the same list.
