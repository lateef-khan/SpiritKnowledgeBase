1. Use a cross spanner to take out the **2 ST4 x 19 flat-head cross screws** (figure 1).
2. Knock out the front stabilizer end cap with a tool (figure 2).

The left and right caps (29, 30) carry the transport wheels; the parts list counts five ST4 x 19 self-tapping screws (85) on the machine.
