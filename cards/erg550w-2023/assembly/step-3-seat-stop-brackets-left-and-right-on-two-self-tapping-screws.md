1. Attach the **Stop Brackets L / R (111 / 112)** to the **Sliding Rail (2)** and tighten the caps with **2 Self-tapping screws (101)**.
2. Tighten with the Allen Wrench with Phillips Screwdriver (96).

The screw is ST4 x 8 on the hardware page and parts list, "ST4 x 10" on this page's callout. The parts list carries two of each bracket; the step fits one pair at the open end of the rail.
