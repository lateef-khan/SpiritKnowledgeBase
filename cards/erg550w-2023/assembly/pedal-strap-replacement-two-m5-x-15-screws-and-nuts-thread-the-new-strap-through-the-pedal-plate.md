1. Open the pedal strap, take out the **2 M5 x 15 screws and nuts** with a cross wrench and an open-ended wrench, then take out the strap (figure 1).
2. Assemble the new strap: **thread it from one side of the pedal iron plate to the other side** and fix it with the 2 M5 x 15 screws and nuts (figure 2).

The parts list's M5 x 15 hex socket round head screws (72, four on the machine) with M5 nuts (113). The Spirit CRW800H2O's strap goes the same way on two M5 screws (linked).
