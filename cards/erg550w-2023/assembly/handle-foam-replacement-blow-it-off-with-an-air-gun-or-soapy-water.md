"As shown in the figure, use the air gun to blow out the foam, or apply soapy water on the foam and pull it out" - rotating as you pull.

The handle bar sleeves (36, Ø33 x 3 x 265) are the foams; the end caps (35) come off first. The same trick is printed in the ERG160 magnetic rower's service book.
