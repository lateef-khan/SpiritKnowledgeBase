1. Take out the chain cover as on the chain-cover page.
2. Rotate the belt pulley to rotate the belt off the pulley (figure 1).
3. Use a cross spanner to take out the **6 ST5 x 15 screws** on the axis fixing piece, take out the axis fixing piece, and take out the belt (figure 2).

The belt is the PJ41" poly-V (34); the axis fixing piece is the plate over the impeller shaft's bearing seat (the parts list's twelve ST5.0 x 15 screws, 88, cover both plates). No tension figure is printed for this belt.
