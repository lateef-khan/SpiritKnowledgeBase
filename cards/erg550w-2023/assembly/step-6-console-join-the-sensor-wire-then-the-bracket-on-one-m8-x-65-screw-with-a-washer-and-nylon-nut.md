1. Connect the wire of the **Console (54)** to the **Sensor Wire Set (95)** from the **Main Frame (1)**.
2. Attach the **Console Bracket (8)**, which is pre-installed on the console, onto the main frame with **1 Hex Socket Round Head Screw (51, M8 x 65), 1 Flat Washer (74) and 1 Nylon Hex Nut (80)**.
3. Tighten with the Allen Wrench with Phillips Screwdriver (96) and the Open Wrench (97).

One long pivot screw holds the whole console bracket. The console's 2 AA batteries are fitted in the console chapter, not here; filling the tank (linked card) is what remains before rowing.
