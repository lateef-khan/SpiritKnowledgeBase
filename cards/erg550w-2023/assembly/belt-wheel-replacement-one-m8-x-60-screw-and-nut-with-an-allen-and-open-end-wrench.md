Take out **1 M8 x 60 screw and nut** with an Allen wrench and an open-end wrench (as shown in the figure), and the belt wheel comes off its axle.

One fastener - the parts list's hex socket round head screw M8 x 60 (94) through the POM belt wheel (38). The Spirit CRW800H2O reaches its belt wheel by taking the console assembly and decorative covers off first and freeing a nylon nut each side - a different mount.
