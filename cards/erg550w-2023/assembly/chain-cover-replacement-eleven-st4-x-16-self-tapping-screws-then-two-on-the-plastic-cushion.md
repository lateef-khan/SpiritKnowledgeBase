1. Use a cross spanner (Phillips screwdriver) to take out the **11 ST4 x 16 cross-head self-tapping screws** on the left and right chain covers (figure 1).
2. Use a cross wrench to take out the **2 ST4.2 x 16 self-tapping screws** on the **Plastic Cushion** (98, the folding cushion the frame rests on when stood up) (figure 2).
3. Take out the plastic cushion and the chain cover (figure 3).

The belt and pulley procedures both begin "take out the chain cover according to the above steps". The parts list counts nineteen ST4 x 16 screws (76) on the machine; eleven of them are here.
