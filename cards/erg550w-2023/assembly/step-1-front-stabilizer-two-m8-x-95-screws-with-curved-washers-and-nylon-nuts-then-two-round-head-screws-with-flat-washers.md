**NOTE: "We recommend having 2 people to assemble the product. Please be careful when assembling Step 1. The Main Frame is easy to topple."**

1. Attach the **Front Stabilizer (4)** onto the **Main Frame (1)** with **4 Curved Washers (99), 2 Hex Socket Round Head Screws (73, M8 x 95) and 2 Nylon Hex Nuts (80)** - the long screws pass right through the stabilizer tube, a curved washer under the head and under the nut.
2. Secure the stabilizer to the main frame with **2 Flat Washers (27) and 2 Hex Socket Round Head Screws (67, M8 x 15)**.
3. Tighten with the Allen Wrench with Phillips Screwdriver (96) and the Open Wrench (97).
