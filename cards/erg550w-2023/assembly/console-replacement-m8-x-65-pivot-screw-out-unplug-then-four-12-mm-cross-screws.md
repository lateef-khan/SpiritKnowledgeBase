1. Take out the **M8 x 65 screw** with a cross wrench and an open-end wrench (figure 1) - the pivot screw (51) and nylon nut that assembly step 6 fitted through the console bracket.
2. Disconnect the connecting wire of the console and take the console out (figure 1).
3. Take out the **four M5 x 12 screws** with a cross spanner (figure 2) to free the console from its bracket.

The book is bilingual and calls the console the "electronic watch". The Spirit CRW800H2O's console comes off on its four screws alone, without a pivot bolt - a different mount.
