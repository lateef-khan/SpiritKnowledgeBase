**Parts:** 1 Main Frame, 3 and 4 (see below), 54 Console, 17 Seat, 90 Funnel, 2 Slide Rail, 83 Pedal Plate L, 104 Pedal Plate R, 91 Siphon Pump, the user manual and the hardware parts.

**The parts table prints "Front Stabilizer" for both No. 3 and No. 4.** The parts list on p. 25 and every assembly step say **3 is the Rear Stabilizer** and **4 the Front Stabilizer**; this card follows them.
