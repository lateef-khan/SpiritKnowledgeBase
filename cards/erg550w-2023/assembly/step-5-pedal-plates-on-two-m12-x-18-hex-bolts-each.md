1. Screw the **Pedal Plate L (83)** into the **Main Frame (1)** with **2 Hex Bolts (114, M12 x 18)** using the Open Wrench (97). Tighten.
2. Repeat for the **Pedal Plate R (104)**.

Four short M12 bolts for the pair - unlike the magnetic ERG rowers, whose pedals hang on 160 mm bolts with spacer sleeves. The pedal straps arrive fitted to the plates.
