Use a cross spanner to remove the **2 ST4 x 10 screws on the Stop Brackets L/R** and the **8 ST4 x 16 screws on both sides of the aluminum strip** (figure 1); the strip then lifts off the rail.

Two aluminum plates (60, L925 x W27.5 x H42 x 2.0) run the length of the rail, one each side. The Spirit CRW800H2O's strip is glued down under two covers - a different fixing.
