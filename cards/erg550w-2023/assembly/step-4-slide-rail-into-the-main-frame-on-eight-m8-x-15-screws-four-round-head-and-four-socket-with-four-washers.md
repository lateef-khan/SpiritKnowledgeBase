**NOTE: Move the Seat (17) to the front of the Slide Rail (2) before assembly to prevent it from sliding down during assembly.**

Attach the **Slide Rail (2)** to the **Main Frame (1)** using **4 Flat Washers (74), 4 Hex Socket Round Head Screws (67, M8 x 15) and 4 Hex Socket Screws (66, M8 x 15)**. **Insert all 8 screws partially into the holes first**, then tighten with the Allen Wrench with Phillips Screwdriver (96).

Two screw types of the same size: the round-head ones (67) take the washers, the socket ones (66) go in bare.
