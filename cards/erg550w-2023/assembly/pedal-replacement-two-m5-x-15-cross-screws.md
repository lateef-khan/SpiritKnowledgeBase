Use a cross wrench to take out **2 M5 x 15 cross screws**, then take out the pedal.

One line and a photo. The parts list splits the pedal into an upper (63) and a lower (64) plastic half on the steel pedal plate (83 / 104); the book does not say which half these screws hold, where the Spirit CRW800H2O's book does (upper slips off, lower on two M5 screws).
