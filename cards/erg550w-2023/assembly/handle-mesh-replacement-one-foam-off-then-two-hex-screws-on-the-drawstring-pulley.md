1. Take out one side of the foam as on the handle-foam page, then take out the handle mesh (figure 1) - the strap end is under the foam.
2. Loosen the **2 hexagon screws on the drawstring pulley** with an open-ended wrench, then take out the belt pulley (figure 2).

The strap (37, "Braid") winds onto the drawstring pulley (44), so the pulley comes out with it. The full pulley strip-down, down to the flange nut, is the pulley page (linked).
