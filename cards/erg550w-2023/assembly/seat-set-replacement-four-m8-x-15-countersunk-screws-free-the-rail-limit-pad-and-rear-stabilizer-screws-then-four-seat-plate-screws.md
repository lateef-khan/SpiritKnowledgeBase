1. Use the S5 wrench to take out the **4 M8 x 15 countersunk screws** at the connection between the rail and the main frame, and pull out the slide rail (figure 1).
2. Use a cross spanner to take out the **2 M6 x 10 cross screws** that lock the limit pad on the slide rail, then an open spanner to take out the **4 M8 x 10 screws** on the rear stabilizer (figure 2).
3. Take out the seat cushion set, and take out the **4 M6 x 15 screws** on the seat plate (figure 3).

The owner's manual fits eight screws at the rail-to-frame joint (four round-head with washers, four socket); this page takes out four "countersunk" ones - read it as the four socket screws (66), the rail then hinging free. The Spirit CRW800H2O's seat comes off around one M10 x 55 roller bolt - a different rail.
