**One labelled picture, twelve labels**, on a page headed ERG550W (the book's cover says ERG550):

| Label | Where the arrow points |
|---|---|
| Console | the display on its post |
| Handlebar & Strap | the handle and the mesh strap |
| Chain cover | the side shroud over the drive |
| Tank | the water tank inside the shroud |
| Plastic Impeller | the paddle wheel in the tank |
| Front Stabilizer | the front foot tube |
| Pedal Strap | the strap on the pedal plate |
| Seat | on the rail |
| Roller | the seat roller under the seat |
| Slide Aluminum Plate | the aluminium strip on the rail the rollers run on |
| Rear Stabilizer | the rear foot tube |
| Adjust Knob | the round leveler foot under the rear stabilizer |

The *Adjust Knob* is **not a resistance control** - on the render the arrow reaches the foot under the rear
stabilizer, the owner's manual's Adjustable Floor Stabilizer (19); this machine's resistance is the water level
(`xterra-ergw-specs-water-resistance-set-by-the-tank-level-on-a-six-mark-gauge`). The *Slide Aluminum Plate* is the
owner's manual's Aluminum Plate (60), two strips 925 mm long, which is the closest the books come to a rail
dimension. The owner's manual's own drawing labels seven parts: Console, Handle Bar, Tank, Pedals, Seat, Sliding
Rail, Front and Rear Stabilizer.

No dimension or weight is on the drawing. The service manual is otherwise photo-step replacement procedures with
bilingual captions (the assembly section's) and a console Q&A (the errors section's).
