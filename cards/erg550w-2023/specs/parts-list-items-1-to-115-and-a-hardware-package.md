**What the list tells a service call.** The console is item 54, **ST-58088**; the belt item 34, **VEGA PJ41"**; the upper and lower tank items 24 and 25 (PC), the impeller item 26 and its shaft item 16, the fill plug item 50 and the rubber sealing ring item 52, the water tank bracket item 14 and its spacer item 23; the funnel item 90 and the siphon pump item 91, which the tank page cites by these numbers; the sensor wire item 95 and the two magnets item 69; the one-way bearing item 82, **HF2016**, and the other bearings 41 (608ZZ, eight of them), 56 (6904ZZ), 57 (16003ZZ), 79 (6000ZZ), 84 (6300ZZ), 89 (6002ZZ) and 93 (6004ZZ); the seat item 17 on three seat rollers (39). The two packed tools are item 96, the **Allen wrench with Phillips screwdriver** (S5), and item 97, the **open wrench** (13-19 mm).

**The service manual prints no list.** The November 2022 ERG550W service manual has an outline drawing with part names and photo-step procedures, no parts list, no exploded view and no tool list (xterra-specs-parts-which-rower-and-strength-manuals-print-an-exploded-view-and-parts-list, `xterra-rower-specs-parts-service-manual-outlines-and-main-parts-indication-names`). The other Xterra water rowers print no list in their owner's manuals; the ERG800W list is in its service manual and uses lettered codes (`erg800w-2023-specs-parts-list-lettered-codes-from-the-service-manual`).

**Damage in the extraction.** The native text is clean; the OCR supplement misreads `Ф` as `0` or `©`, *608ZZ* as *6082ZZ* and *6002ZZ* as *600222*. The table above is the native layer.
