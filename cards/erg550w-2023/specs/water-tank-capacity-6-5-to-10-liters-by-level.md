The book prints the volume behind each mark on the tank gauge:

| Level | Litres | US gallons | Quarts |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | 6.5 | 1.72 | 6.86 |
| L2 | 7.1 | 1.87 | 7.50 |
| L3 | 7.8 | 2.06 | 8.24 |
| L4 | 8.4 | 2.22 | 8.87 |
| L5 | 9 | 2.38 | 9.50 |
| L6 | 10 | 2.64 | 10.56 |

So a full tank is **10 litres**, and the difference between the lightest and heaviest setting is 3.5 litres. Mark 6
is also the fill limit - "Never fill it over this limit. Filling the tank over this limit will void the warranty"
(`xterra-ergw-specs-water-resistance-set-by-the-tank-level-on-a-six-mark-gauge`). The tank is filled with tap
water through the funnel and emptied with the siphon pump; the maintenance section holds that procedure and the
six-monthly purification tablet.

The ERG750W's tank is bigger and marked differently - 8.0 litres at Min, 12.7 at Max
(`erg750w-2025-specs-water-tank-capacity-8-to-12-7-liters-min-standard-max`). The ERG600W, ERG650W and ERG800W
books print no volume.
