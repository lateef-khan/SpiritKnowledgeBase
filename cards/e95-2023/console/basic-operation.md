**Power.** When the power cord is connected to the elliptical, the console powers up automatically.

**Quick Start.** After the console powers up, press **"Start Workout"** on the screen or the **START** key. Time counts from zero and the workload is adjusted manually with the Level Up/Down keys.

**The 20 resistance levels.** Levels 1-5 are very easy workloads with a progression set for de-conditioned users. Levels 6-10 are more challenging but the steps between levels stay small. Levels 11-15 start getting tough as the levels jump more dramatically. Levels 16-20 are extremely hard, good for short interval peaks and elite athletic training.

**The Stop key does two things.**

- Pressed **once** during a program it pauses the program for **5 minutes**. Press Start to resume.
- Pressed **twice** during a workout it ends the program and displays the Workout Summary: total time, average speed, average power, average HR and total laps.

**Age and weight.** Programs ask for them. Age matters in the heart rate programs so the work settings are right; weight makes the calorie reading closer.

**Calorie note, as the manual prints it.** Calorie readings on every piece of exercise equipment are not accurate and vary widely. They are a guide for comparing workout to workout, not a measurement.
