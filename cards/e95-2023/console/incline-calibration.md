Calibration lives on the **CALIBRATION** page of Engineer Mode.

1. Click **"start"** to calibrate the incline.
2. The incline motor moves **UP** until it stops moving for **3~6 seconds**.
3. It then moves **DOWN** until it stops moving for **3~6 seconds**.
4. Calibration is then finished. The result is displayed on the screen.

The troubleshooting matrix sends you here for the INCLINE ERR symptom, after turning the AC switch off and on again.
