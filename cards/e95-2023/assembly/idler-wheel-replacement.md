1. Follow 9.3, 9.4, 9.6 and 9.7 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, both left and right side cases, cross bar and the belt.
2. Use a **13 mm wrench** to loosen the **M8 x 9T nut on the M8x170mm J-bolt** together with the **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** until the J-bolt is released. With the same wrench release the **M8 x 20 carriage bolts** which secure the idler wheel assembly, together with the **5/16" x 20 x 1.5T flat washer** and **M8 x 7T nut**, to take the idler wheel assembly apart.
3. To resume, tighten the **M8x20 carriage bolt**, **5/16" x 20 x 1.5T flat washer** and **M8 x 7T nut** to secure the idler wheel assembly - **tighten these after the belt has been adjusted** - and return the J-bolt and the other parts.

Step 2 says carriage bolt**s** and step 3 says carriage bolt. The manual is printed that way; count the bolts on the machine.
