**Do not tighten anything fully in sub-step 2.** The book prints the note inside the sequence: tighten all screws after all components are assembled.

1. **Install 6pcs Adjustment Foots (90)** on the Front Stabilizer and Middle Stabilizer of the Main Frame (1) and the Rear Stabilizer of the Incline Rail Assembly (2).
2. **Install the Incline Rail Assembly (2) into the U-channel bracket of the Main Frame (1).** From the sides install **4pcs Hex Head Bolts (132)** with **2pcs Hex Head Bolts (130)**, **6pcs Split Washers (174)**, **6pcs Flat Washers (170)** and **4pcs Star Washers (195)**, as shown in figure 1, and tighten with **Wrench (183)**. (The book says "secure with the four bolts" and then names six; the washer counts match six.)
3. **Connect the 3 wire harness: Incline Motor Power Cord (48)** to the female receiver from the Incline Rail Assembly (2). Connect the **Three pin position Sensor connector / Incline Motor Connecting Wire (49)** the same way.
4. **Run the Computer Cable (50) through the Console Mast (12)**, install the mast into the receiving bracket, and fit **4pcs Socket Head Cap Bolts (138)** with **4pcs Split Washers (174)** and **4pcs Flat Washers (170)**. Tighten with the **L Allen Wrench (182)**, pulling slightly on the cable at the top so it does not get pinched and shorted.
5. The book numbers this step "5. 4." **Install the Bottle Holder (92) on the Console Mast (12) with 2pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)** using driver (181).
