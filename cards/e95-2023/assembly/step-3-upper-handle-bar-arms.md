1. **Insert the left Top Handle Bar (19) onto the left Lower Handle Bar** and secure with **3pcs Hex Head Bolts (133)**, **4pcs Flat Washers (170)**, **2pcs Curved Washers (173)** and **3pcs Nylon Nuts (159)** using **2 Wrenches (183 & 184)**. Secure the right Top Handle Bar (20) the same way. (The book fits four flat washers over three bolts per side without saying which bolt takes two.)
2. **Connect the Upper Resistance Handle Wire (61) with the Lower Resistance/Incline Handle Wire (63)**, store it in the frame tube, and **snap in the Switch Wire Cap (71)** on the console mast. Then connect the **Upper Incline Handle Wire (62)** with the Lower wire (63) the same way and snap in the second cap (71).
