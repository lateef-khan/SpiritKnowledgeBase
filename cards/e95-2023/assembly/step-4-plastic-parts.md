1. **Match the Connecting Arm Covers B (L) and (R)** on the Pedal Bar Assembly (L) (8) with **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)** and **2pcs Sheet Metal Screws (146)**; then match the **Connecting Arm Covers A (R) and (L), (118) and (119)**, on the lower Lower Handle Bar (L) (10) the same way using the Short driver (180). Repeat on the other side.
2. **Slide Wheel Covers (95)** above each side of the Slide Wheel Urethanes with **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)**.
3. **Front Stabilizer Cover (112)** with **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)**.
4. **Cover Holders (23)**, bent-tab hole facing rearward, with **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (148)**; **Inclinable Rail Cover (115)** with **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)**; **Rear Bar Cover (116)** on the Rear Stabilizer and the Cover Holders (23) with **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)**.
5. **Bottom Cover (113)** with **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)**.
6. **Handle Bar Covers (106 & 107, 108 & 109)** with **4pcs Sheet Metal Screws (146)**.
7. **Console Mast Covers (100 & 101)** with **4pcs Sheet Metal Screws (146)**.
8. **Tablet Holder (89)** on the back of the console with **4pcs Sheet Metal Screws (146)**.
9. **Plug both Oval End Caps (76)** into both ends of the stabilizer tube.
