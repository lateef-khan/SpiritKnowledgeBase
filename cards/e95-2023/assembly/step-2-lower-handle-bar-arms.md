1. **Align the plate of the Pedal Bar Assembly (L) (8) with the foot pad axle of the Left Pedal Arm (6)** and put it in. Secure the **Fixing Axle (25)** with **2pcs Flat Washers (168)** and **2pcs Hex Head Bolts (127)** using **Wrench (184)**. Do the same for the Pedal Bar Assembly (R) (9) on the Right Pedal Arm (7).
2. **Install the left and right Lower Handle Bars (10) (11)** onto the console mast shafts and secure with **Hex Head Bolts (131)** together with **Flat Washers (171)** using **Wrench (183)**.
3. **Untie the rod end bearing on the Pedal Bar Assembly (L) (8).** Insert **Hex Head Bolt (128)** through the rod end bearing bracket on the Lower Handle Bar (L) (10) and with wrenches **(183 & 184)** tighten together with **Flat Washer (166)** and **Nylon Nut (156)**. Fix the Lower Handle Bar (R) (11) and Pedal Bar Assembly (R) (9) the same way.
4. **Run the Handpulse W/Cable (57) from the Left Handpulse Assembly (15)** through the left round hole of the console mast and pull it out at the top. Secure to the console mast left side with **2pcs Hex Head Bolts (127)** and **2pcs Flat Washers (166)** using **Wrench (184)**. Do the same on the right with the **Handpulse W/Cable (58) from the Right Handpulse Assembly (16)**.
5. **Plug all of the connectors into the back of the console: Computer Cable (50), two Hand pulse Cables (57 & 58), Resistance switch wire (61) and Incline switch wire (62).** Secure the **Console (45)** with **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)** using driver (181).
