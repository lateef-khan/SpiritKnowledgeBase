1. Follow 9.3, 9.4, 9.6 and 9.7 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, both side cases and the cross bar.
2. Use a **13 mm wrench** to loosen the **M8 x 9T nut on the M8x170mm J-bolt** and on the idler wheel, and another **M8 x 7T nut**, and the belt can be released.
3. To resume, return the belt on the drive pulley and flywheel, then tighten the **M8 x 9T nut until the acoustic gauge reads 190 Hz (+/- 10 Hz)** when the belt is plucked with a finger, then tighten the **M8 x 7T nut**.
4. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
