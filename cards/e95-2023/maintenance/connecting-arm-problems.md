An elliptical is full of moving parts with joints, so **proper lubrication is important**. Lubricating with grease while maintaining or replacing parts eliminates potential problems.

There are many causes for un-smoothness:

1. **Slide wheels moving parallel and freely on the rail** is the key factor. That is affected by the pedal arm being parallel to the rail and perpendicular to the bearing housing.
2. Swing arms must connect freely to the connecting arms, the slide wheels must run true without slippage, and the **rod end (fisheye) bearing** must be undamaged and tight enough to eliminate noises. If necessary, use a **19mm wrench** to replace the rod end bearing.
3. If the incline motor does not function, first check the cables are properly connected, then check the **incline controller, the transformer and the console** for damage. Replace whichever part is damaged.

Point 3 is printed again, word for word, in section 10.6. It has its own card.
