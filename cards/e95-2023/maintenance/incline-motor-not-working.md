1. If the incline motor does not function, first check the proper connection of the cables, then check the **incline controller, the transformer and the console** for damage. Replace whichever part is damaged.
2. **Make sure the incline motor is reset to zero when you install a new one.** Rotate the incline barrel **clockwise to the end**, then rotate the same barrel **counterclockwise one and a half circles**. Make sure the distance of the two hole sites is **207 +/- 1mm**.

**This manual gives a different figure in the parts replacing guide.** Section 9.13 step 8 says the zeroing distance is **206mm (+/- 1mm)**. Section 10.6 says **207 +/- 1mm**. Both are printed. Measure before you fit the motor.

Step 1 is printed again, word for word, at the end of section 10.5.
