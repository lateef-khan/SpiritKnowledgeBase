**Work voltage: DC 4.5~7.5V.** It controls resistance increases and decreases.

Engineer Mode's SERVICE page has a **Motor Test** entry for fine-tuning and lifespan testing of this motor.

**This manual prints no meter procedure for the gear motor** - no drive board output figures and no probe placement. The keypad-console manuals carry those, and they were written for a different driver board.

The E98 of the same year has no gear motor at all. It uses an EMS brake at DC 0~21V.
