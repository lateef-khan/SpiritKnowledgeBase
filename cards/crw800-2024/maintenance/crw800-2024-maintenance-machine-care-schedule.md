**Seven items** — the shortest of the six schedules in the 2024 range — and although the header
prints five interval columns, **nothing is marked under Quarterly or Biannually except the console
overlay.**

## The two rows that exist on no other Spirit schedule

- **Seat & Seat Rails — clean weekly, inspect monthly.** No other Spirit machine care table has a
  seat-rail row.
- **Handle Pull Strap — inspect monthly.** This is the rower's wear item. The manual gives it an
  inspection interval and **no wear limit, no replacement criterion and no part number** on this
  page; it says only "Inspect".

**The manual prints no cleaning method for the seat rails** — no cloth, no alcohol, no lubricant,
and no instruction either way about lubricating them. Give the interval and say the method is not
printed rather than importing an elliptical rail procedure.

## What this table does not have

- **No Levelers row**, although the rower's Machine Care text carries the standard Spirit levelling
  check (`ct800-2016-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`).
- **No USB Port row and no Accessory Cups row.**
- **No row for the battery.** The rower is the one machine of the fourteen with a printed battery
  change (`crw800-2024-maintenance-battery-changing-instructions`), and that procedure has no
  interval here or anywhere else in the book.

## This is the CRW800, not the CRW800H2O

The Spirit **CRW800H2O** is a **water** rower and a different machine. It has a tank rather than a
strap-and-pedal drive, and nothing on this card was taken from its manual or should be read onto it.
