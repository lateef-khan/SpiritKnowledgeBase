**A battery change on this machine is two jobs: swap the cell, then re-code the handle to the
console. The manual prints them on facing pages and does not cross-reference them.**

## Changing the battery

The same three steps are printed twice, under two separate picture sequences:

1. **Remove the battery cover screw.**
2. **Remove the battery cover.**
3. **Replace the battery with a new one, put on the cover and secure.**

A screwdriver is shown in both sequences. **No tool size and no torque is given.**

## Which battery, and how many

**The manual never names a battery type on the battery-changing page**, and it does not label the
two sequences. What it prints is:

- The **first** picture sequence shows **two cylindrical cells** coming out of the cover.
- The **second** shows a **single button cell**.
- On the facing page, the coding note names the cell as **CR2032** — but that note is about the
  wireless handle control specifically, not about both covers.

**Do not tell a customer that both covers take a CR2032.** CR2032 is the only type the book names,
and it names it once, for the wireless handle. Ask which cover they have open, and read the cell in
front of them.

## Re-coding the wireless handle, which the machine needs after the change

From the facing page, printed word for word:

1. After replacing the battery (CR2032), the wireless handle control **needs to be coded again**.
2. When the console is turned on again, **press and hold the Up or Down handle button for
   3 seconds** to complete the coding between console and handlebar.
3. **Check the coding worked:** press **Start** on the console, then press **Up** or **Down** on the
   handle and see whether **LEVEL** on the LCD changes. If it does, coding succeeded; otherwise go
   back to step 1 and repeat.

**This is why the handle buttons appear dead after a battery change.** The console is not faulty and
the handle is not faulty; they are unpaired until the 3-second press is done.

## No interval and no other battery in the book

The machine care schedule has **no battery row and no interval** for this
(`crw800-2024-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`). The only other battery named anywhere in the
manual is the **CR2032 in the heart rate chest strap**, which the troubleshooting table tells you to
replace when the pulse reading drops out — a different battery in a different place.

**This is the CRW800, not the CRW800H2O water rower**, whose manual describes a different machine.
