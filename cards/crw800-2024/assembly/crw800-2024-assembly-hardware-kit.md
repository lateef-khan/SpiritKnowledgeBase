## Hardware for step 1

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 109 | Flat washer | 3/8"X19X1.5T | 4 |
| 120 | Bolt | 3/8"X3/4" | 4 |
| 121 | Spring washer | 10X2T | 4 |

## Hardware for step 2

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 89 | Bolt | M6XP1.0X18 | 2 |
| 129 | Bolt | M6XP1.0x10 | 4 |

**The two of item 89 are not in the hardware bag.** Step two takes them out of the
aluminium track, where they arrive fitted, and puts them back at the end of the step.
Counting the bag against this table will show two short. Keep them when they come out.

## Hardware for step 3

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 122 | Flat washer | 5/16"X18X1.5T | 4 |
| 123 | Bolt | M8XP1.25X12 | 5 |

**Five bolts and four washers.** The step says the same, so one of the five goes in bare.
The manual does not say which.

## Hardware for step 4

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 85 | Screw | M5X10 | 3 |
| 122 | **Bolt** | 5/16"X18X1.5T | 6 |
| 123 | Bolt | M8XP1.25X12 | 6 |
| 124 | Screw | M6X15 | 4 |
| 125 | Spring washer | 1/4" | 4 |

## Item 122 is a flat washer typed as a bolt

The step three table calls item 122 a **Flat washer** at 5/16"X18X1.5T. The step four
table calls the same item, at the same description, a **Bolt**. The step four text calls
for "6 BUTTON HEAD SOCKET BOLTS (123) and **6 FLAT WASHERS (122)**".

**It is a flat washer.** Two of the three printings say so and the description - a
thickness of 1.5T - is a washer's, not a bolt's. The step four table row is the outlier.

## Item 122 also disagrees with itself on 5/16 versus 3/8

It is described **5/16"X18X1.5T** in both tables, but the only 5/16 fastener anywhere in
the book is this washer; every bolt it sits under is metric - M8 x P1.25 in steps three
and four. The manual never explains the mix and never gives a metric equivalent.

## Item 124 is a Phillips screw the table types only as "Screw"

The step calls for **4 PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (124)**; the table says only SCREW, M6X15.
Item 85 is the same shape of thing at M5X10 and three off. Neither has a screwdriver in
the carton - see [the tools card](crw800-2024-assembly-tools-included.md).

## Where the counts close and where they do not

Steps one, three and four match their own text line for line. **Step two is the only one
that does not close against the bag**, and only because two of its five fasteners come off
the machine rather than out of the hardware kit.

## The 2021 and 2024 tables are identical row for row

All twelve rows across the four tables - part number, type, description and quantity -
match exactly between the two printings, whitespace aside. **Item 122 is typed as a bolt
in the step four table of both books**, so that is a fault the 2024 redesign inherited
rather than introduced, and the five-bolts-four-washers step three is in both as well.

## The XRW600 hardware is not this hardware

That rower fits its front stabiliser with three bolts and no spring washer, and shares
only item 123 with this table. See
[the XRW600 hardware card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xrw600-assembly-hardware-by-step.md).

## What the tables do not print

**No torque figure for any bolt.** No step of this build gives one.
