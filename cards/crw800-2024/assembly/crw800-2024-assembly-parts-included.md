Eight lines, with quantities as printed:

| Qty | Part |
|---|---|
| 1 | Main Frame |
| 1 | Aluminum Rail |
| 1 | Front Stabilizer |
| 1 | Rear Stabilizer |
| 1 | Console Mast |
| 1 | Console |
| 2 | Foot Pedals |
| 1 | Hardware Kit |

**This is the shortest carton panel of the fourteen 2024 commercial machines.** The next
shortest holds eleven lines.

## Four parts the build fits and the panel never lists

Step two fits a **Seat Stop Cover (No.65)**, step three a **Folding End Assembly (No.6)**,
and step four a **Connecting Cover (No.63)**, a **Seat (No.49)** and a **Seat Attaching
Board (No.16)**. None of the five is on this panel.

**The seat is the one that matters.** An owner counting the box against this list has no
line to tick it against, and step four is where it goes on.

## The panel lists no power cord

The console is battery- or generator-driven: its setting-up page says to wake it by
pressing any button and that it sleeps after inactivity, and there is no power switch and
no power input drawing in the book.

## The panel lists no levellers and no transport wheels

The machine diagram on the next page labels **Transport Wheels** as callout E, and the
maintenance chapter says there are **two levelling pads under the rear stabiliser**. This
panel counts neither, and no step of the build fits either. The transport wheels are what
the moving procedure rolls the machine away on - see
[the folding and moving card](crw800-2024-assembly-folding-and-moving.md).

## This is not the water rower's carton

The Spirit **CRW800H2O** is a different machine. Do not answer a question about one from
the other's parts list.

## What the panel does not say

It gives no carton count, no shipping weight and no box dimensions.
