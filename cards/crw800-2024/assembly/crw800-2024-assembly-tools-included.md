Three tools, and nothing else:

- 13/14mm Wrench
- Combination Wrench
- 4mm L Allen Wrench

**This is not the water rower.** The Spirit CRW800H2O in this repository is a different
machine with a different build; nothing on this card belongs to it.

## Step four needs a screwdriver this carton does not hold

Step four fits **3 Phillips head screws (No.85)** into the connecting cover and **4
Phillips head screws (No.124)** into the seat attaching board - seven driven screws - and
**there is no screwdriver in the carton**. The manual never says to supply your own.

**It tells you to use the combination wrench on them instead.** Step four's own words
are "Use again COMBINATION WRENCH (127) to install CONNECTING COVER (63) ... with 3
PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (85)" and, for the seat, "4 PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (124) together
with 4 SPRING WASHERS (125) by using COMBINATION WRENCH (127)". A combination wrench
does not drive a Phillips head. See [the build](crw800-2024-assembly-procedure.md).

## The steps name tools by item number and the panel gives none

The four steps call for the **WRENCH (128)**, the **ALLEN WRENCH (126)** and the
**COMBINATION WRENCH (127)**. This panel lists three tools and prints no item number
against any of them, so the manual never states which printed size is which number. The
Allen wrench is the only key in the carton, so item 126 is the 4mm; the other two are not
distinguished anywhere in the book.

## The 13/14mm is the one that levels the machine

The maintenance chapter prints the same sentence as the rest of the range - two levelling
pads under the rear stabiliser, adjusted with a **14mm wrench** - and the 13/14mm in this
carton opens to 14. See
[the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

## The 2021 printing lists the same three

The **CRW800 2021** manual prints the same three lines and the same four steps, and its
step four sends the same seven Phillips screws to the same combination wrench. Nothing
about the tools changed in the 2024 redesign.

## The XRW600 carton prints these same three lines and means something else by them

Its step four calls the third tool, in full, a **"Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips
Head Screw Driver"** - so that carton does hold something that drives a Phillips head,
and this one, on both printings, does not say so. Same panel, different books. See
[the XRW600 tools card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xrw600-assembly-tools-included.md).

## Nothing here is a torque wrench

No step of this build gives a torque figure.
