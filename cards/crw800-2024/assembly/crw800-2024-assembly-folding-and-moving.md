Two short panels, printed side by side.

## Folding/Unfolding Procedures

1. Turn the knob **counterclockwise** to release.
2. Fold up the aluminium rail assembly.

## Moving Procedures

1. Fold the aluminium rail assembly up.
2. **Lift the rear and roll away.**
3. Turn the knob **clockwise** to tighten.

**Lift the rear.** The transport wheels are at the front - callout E on the machine
diagram - so the machine pivots onto them when the rear end is raised.

## The heading says unfolding and the panel never describes it

The left panel is headed **Folding/Unfolding Procedures** and prints two items, both of
them folding. Nothing anywhere in the book says how to unfold the rail or how to lock it
down again for use. Reversing the two items is the obvious reading, and the manual does
not state it.

## The 2021 printing puts step three in the other column

The **CRW800 2021** manual prints the same five items, and puts **"Turn the knob
clockwise to tighten" in the Folding/Unfolding column** as its item 3, where it reads
naturally. The 2024 redesign moved that item across into the Moving column and left its
number behind. Same five instructions, two layouts, and the 2021 one is the one that
makes sense.

Its page also stands alone at p. 36, after the battery-changing instructions, rather than
sharing a SETTING UP page with the first power-on.

## The knob is tightened in the moving list, not the folding one

Item 3 - turn the knob clockwise to tighten - is printed in the **Moving** column, not the
Folding one. Read as printed, the folding procedure leaves the knob loose and only the
moving procedure retightens it. Whichever of the two you are doing, **tighten the knob
once the rail is up**; a released knob is what lets the rail move.

## This is the only moving procedure in the 2024 commercial range

None of the other thirteen manuals of Spirit's September and October 2024 redesign prints
one. Three of them list transport wheels on their carton panel - the CE850, the CS800 and
the CRS800S - and only the CRS800S even fits them during assembly; not one says how to
use them. **Do not carry this procedure across to another machine**: this rower is the
only one in the range that folds at all.

**The other Spirit rowers each print their own, and none matches this one.** The XRW600
folds on a release lever at the handlebar *and* folds its rear stabiliser on a knob; the
CRW800H2O does not fold at all and is stored standing on end; the CRW900 prints no moving
or folding procedure anywhere. See the linked cards.

## The rail is the only thing that folds

No other part of the machine is described as folding, and the book prints no folded
dimensions, no folded weight and no warning about the rail dropping.
