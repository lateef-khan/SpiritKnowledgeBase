Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. Hardware quantities are on
[the hardware card](crw800-2024-assembly-hardware-kit.md). **Every step opens with
"Gather HARDWARE FOR STEP n"** and then has exactly one instruction.

**This is not the water rower.** The Spirit CRW800H2O builds differently and shares no
item number with this machine.

## Step one - front stabilizer

Use the **Wrench (No.128)** to tighten **4 Hex Head Bolts (No.120)** together with 4
Spring Washers (No.121) and 4 Flat Washers (No.109) to secure the **Main Frame (No.1)**
and the **Front Stabilizer (No.2)** together.

## Step two - rear stabilizer onto the aluminium track

Use the **Allen Wrench (No.126)** to **remove 2 Button Head Socket Bolts (No.89)** from
the **Aluminum Track (No.51)**. Attach the **Seat Stop Cover (No.65)** to the **Rear
Stabilizer (No.3)** and install it at the rear end of the track. Then use the Allen
wrench to tighten **the 2 Button Head Socket Bolts (No.89)** and **4 Button Head Socket
Bolts (No.129)** onto the track.

**The two bolts of item 89 come off the machine, not out of the bag.** They arrive fitted
in the aluminium track, come out at the start of this step and go back in at the end of
it. They are still listed in the step's hardware table, so counting the bag against that
table will show two short. **Keep them.**

## Step three - folding end assembly

Attach the other end of the **Aluminum Track (No.51)** to the **Folding End Assembly
(No.6)** and use the **Combination Wrench (No.127)** to tighten **5 Button Head Socket
Bolts (No.123)** together with **4 Flat Washers (No.122)**.

*Five bolts and four washers. The step and its table agree on both figures, so one of the
five bolts goes in bare and the manual does not say which.*

## Step four - pedals, connecting cover and seat

1. Install the left and right **Pedals (No.4, 5)** on the Main Frame (No.1) with **6
   Button Head Socket Bolts (No.123) and 6 Flat Washers (No.122)**, using the Combination
   Wrench (No.127).
2. Install the **Connecting Cover (No.63)** on the Aluminum Track (No.51) with **3
   Phillips Head Screws (No.85)**.
3. Install the **Seat (No.49)** and the **Seat Attaching Board (No.16)** with **4 Phillips
   Head Screws (No.124)** together with **4 Spring Washers (No.125)**.

**The step tells you to drive all seven Phillips screws with the combination wrench**, and
the carton holds no screwdriver at all. See
[the tools card](crw800-2024-assembly-tools-included.md).

**Neither the seat nor the seat attaching board is on the carton panel.** See
[the parts card](crw800-2024-assembly-parts-included.md).

## The build ends here

No step fits a leveller, although the maintenance chapter says there are two under the
rear stabiliser, and no step fits the transport wheels the machine diagram labels. The
setting-up page that follows is the first power-on and the folding and moving procedures -
see [that card](crw800-2024-assembly-folding-and-moving.md).
