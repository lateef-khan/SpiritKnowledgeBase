These are the callout numbers on the exploded view drawing. The drawing is a
picture with bare numbers on it and no text, so the table below is the only
readable form of it.

**145 rows are printed.** The whole item numbers that appear are 1-40, 43-115, 120-132, 134-137, 139. Any number outside that run is **not printed in this manual at all** - 41, 42, 116, 117, 118, 119, 133, 138 are absent, not a missing page.

**The CRW800-2021 manual prints this same list**, with 98.3% of the part names
shared and exactly two rows different - see
`spirit-rower-specs-crw800-parts-list-2021-vs-2024` before quoting item 96 or
item 132 for a machine of either year.

**No other Spirit rower list resembles this one.** The two water rowers are
different machines with different mechanisms - the CRW900 numbers its parts with
five-digit part numbers on eight drawing sheets, and the CRW800H2O prints no list
at all. The XRW600 books print none either. See
`spirit-rower-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.

## Item 132 is printed twice, with two different parts

**Row `132  3 x 10m/m_Sheet Metal Screw  1` appears on the first parts page**,
between items 43-07 and 44 and out of numerical order, **and row
`132  3 x 10L_Sheet Metal Screw  3` appears again on the second page** in its
proper place. The two rows give the same screw a different length spelling and a
different quantity.

**Both are printed. The manual does not say which is right.** The table below
carries the first-page row, because that is where the number is first defined;
the second-page row is `3 x 10L`, quantity 3. Items 94 and 95 are also
`3 x 10L_Sheet Metal Screw`, at quantities 4 and 1. Ask Spirit Fitness before
quoting a quantity for item 132.

## What the list says about how the machine works

The manual's prose never names the resistance system. **The parts list does:**

- **Item 23 `Flywheel`, item 22 `Fan`, item 24 `Drive Belt`, item 25 `Flywheel
  Pulley`**, and items 76 and 77 a left and right `Galvanized iron net` - a fan
  guard.
- **Item 29 `Generator/Brake Controller` and item 35 `Gear Motor`**, with front
  and rear gear motor covers at 74 and 75.
- **Item 36 is a `Controller Assembly` with six sub-items**, 36-1 to 36-6,
  including a `Resistance Button W/Cable+Faceplate`, an `RF Module` and a
  `Battery` - a wireless resistance control on the handle.
- **Item 43 is the `Console Assembly` with seven sub-items**, 43-01 to 43-07,
  including a battery case and a battery cover.

See `crw800-2024-specs-resistance-system`.

## The exploded view carries a code the manual never uses

The exploded-view page is a flat image, but its native text layer carries the
string **`CW800B`** - not CRW800. It is a drawing code, not a model number and
not a part number. A stamp reading `1110908PM0300` sits at the foot of the same
drawing and is legible only from a 300 dpi render.

## Two item numbers use a letter suffix

Items **008L** and **008R** are the left and right attaching plates, printed with
the leading zeros the rest of the list does not use.

**No flywheel weight, machine weight, damper setting or resistance rating is
printed against any row.** See `spirit-crw800-specs-no-specification-table`.
