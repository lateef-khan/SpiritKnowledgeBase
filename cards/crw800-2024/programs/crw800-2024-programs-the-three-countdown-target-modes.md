All three are count-down modes. Use **Up/Down** to set the goal, then **Start/Stop** to confirm the
setting and start the workout. **When the goal reaches zero the workout completes and the buzzer
sounds with a long beep. If paddling continues, the count-down repeats.**

| Mode | Default | Increment | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Target Distance** | **100 M** | **50 M** up or down | not stated |
| **Target Time** | **1:00** | **1 minute** up or down | **99:00** |
| **Target Calorie** | **100** | **10** up or down | not stated |

*The Target Time paragraph opens "The default **distance** is 1:00" - the word distance where time
is meant. Recorded as printed.*

Under all three modes, **pressing Up or Down adjusts the resistance level**, and the centre of the
LCD scans every 5 seconds between the stroke-speed wave and the 500M/TIME split - see
`crw800-2024-programs-manual-mode-and-the-scanning-centre-display`.

The window for that mode - Distance, Time or Calorie - counts down from the setting and shows the
remaining amount.
