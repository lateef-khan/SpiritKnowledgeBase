**This is the LCD dot-matrix console fitted to the CRW800 and the XRW600, not the Spirit water
rower.** The CRW800H2O and the CRW900 have a different console with a different program list - see
`spirit-water-rower-programs-quick-start-and-five-target-programs`.

The screen shows a full display and the buzzer beeps for two seconds after turning on. Pressing
**Start** goes directly to Manual workout mode; pressing **MODE** steps through the workout
sequence:

**MANUAL - DISTANCE - TIME - CALORIES - 20/10 INTERVAL - 10/20 INTERVAL - CUSTOM INTERVAL - FAT BURN
- CARDIO - STRENGTH - GAME**

**Eleven modes.** The program name scrolls from left to right to tell the user what it is.

**All four books print the same eleven names in the same order.** The chapter heading differs: the
CRW800 books call it **PROGRAMMABLE FEATURES**, the XRW600 books **Operating Instruction**. *The 2021
CRW800 printing spells the seventh mode `CUSTOM NTERVAL` in the sequence line, with the I missing;
its own icon caption below spells it CUSTOM INTERVAL.*

**Three service manuals print the same list twice more.** The CRW800 2021 service manual, the
2016 CRW800 service manual (the CW800-YR001 book, the machine's own service book) and the XRW600 2019
service manual (DW400-YR002) each print the eleven names in this order under the **MODE Key** and
again under **Operating Instruction**, word for word, with the same scrolling-name sentence. The
2016 CRW800 has no owner's manual in this repository; its service manual is the evidence that it
carries this console and this program set. **All three service manuals print a marketing callout
that contradicts their own list**: the outline drawing of the console on each book's parts page is
captioned *"Ten innovative programs offer a variety of workouts"* beside *"Large LCD Matrix Window"*
and *"Built-in Heart Rate Receiver"* - ten, against the eleven modes the MODE button steps through.
Answer with the eleven-name list, not the caption.

**There is no heart rate program, no custom profile builder and no fitness test on any of them.**
The console reads a pulse and the manual carries a chest strap page, but no program targets a heart
rate - see `crw800-2024-programs-target-zone-of-108-to-153-beats`.

Three of the eleven names - Fat Burn, Cardio and Strength - are shared with the Spirit ellipticals,
bikes and steppers, but **on this machine they are fixed-profile timed workouts with no Max Level
step**, not the resistance profiles those manuals describe. Do not answer from
`spirit-ce-programs-fat-burn-profile` or `spirit-bike-programs-cardio-profile`.
