**This is an air rower with an LCD console, not the Spirit water rower.** The CRW800H2O is a
different machine and its cards do not describe this one.

The screen shows a full display and the buzzer beeps for two seconds after turning on. Pressing
**Start** goes directly to Manual workout mode; pressing **MODE** steps through the workout
sequence:

**MANUAL - DISTANCE - TIME - CALORIES - 20/10 INTERVAL - 10/20 INTERVAL - CUSTOM INTERVAL - FAT BURN
- CARDIO - STRENGTH - GAME**

**Eleven modes.** The program name scrolls from left to right to tell the user what it is.

**There is no heart rate program, no custom profile builder and no fitness test on this machine.**
The console reads a pulse and the manual carries a chest strap page, but no program targets a heart
rate - see `crw800-2024-programs-target-zone-of-108-to-153-beats`.

Three of the eleven names - Fat Burn, Cardio and Strength - are shared with the Spirit ellipticals,
bikes and steppers, but **on this machine they are fixed-profile timed workouts with no Max Level
step**, not the resistance profiles those manuals describe. Do not answer from
`spirit-ce-programs-fat-burn-profile` or `spirit-bike-programs-cardio-profile`.
