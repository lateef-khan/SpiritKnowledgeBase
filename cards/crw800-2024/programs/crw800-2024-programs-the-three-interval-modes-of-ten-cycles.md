The rower has **three interval modes, and every one of them runs ten Exercise/Rest cycles.**

- **20/10 INTERVAL** - 20 seconds Exercise / 10 seconds Rest. Press **Start/Stop** to begin.
- **10/20 INTERVAL** - 10 seconds Exercise / 20 seconds Rest. Press **Start/Stop** to begin.
- **CUSTOM INTERVAL** - user-defined Exercise time and Rest time. **The default is 10 seconds
  Exercise / 10 seconds Rest**, adjusted in **1-second** steps. The value at the **left** of the
  matrix window flashes for the exercise time; set it with Up/Down and press Start/Stop. The value
  at the **right** flashes for the rest time; set it the same way.

The centre of the LCD shows the **count-down of the current work segment with a wave**, or the
**rest count-down with a mark**. **Pressing Up or Down adjusts the resistance level.**

**There are 10 Exercise/Rest cycles for each workout time** on all three. When the workout completes
the buzzer sounds with a long beep; **if paddling continues, the count-down repeats.**

**The Spirit air bike's 20/10 and 10/20 modes are eight fixed Tabata intervals, not ten** - see
`spirit-bike-programs-air-bike-interval-twenty-ten-and-ten-twenty-are-eight-tabata-intervals`. The
count differs and the two must not be swapped.
