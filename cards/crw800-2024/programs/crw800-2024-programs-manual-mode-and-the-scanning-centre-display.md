Pressing **Start/Stop** begins the workout mode, or **pulling the paddle under idle mode enters
directly into Manual workout mode**.

The image at the centre of the LCD **scans every 5 seconds**, alternating between:

- the **stroke speed shown as a wave pattern**, and
- **500M / TIME** - your split.

Pressing **MODE** cancels the scanning and leaves the wave pattern only. **Pressing Mode again
switches the image to show 500M/TIME.**

The same scanning behaviour and the same Mode-button override apply in the Distance, Time and
Calories modes.

**Manual mode asks for nothing before you start** - no age, no weight, no time. Up and Down adjust
the resistance level while you row.
