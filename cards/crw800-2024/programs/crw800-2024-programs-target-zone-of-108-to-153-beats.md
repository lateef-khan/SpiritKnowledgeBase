**These are the 60% and 85% figures**, and the arithmetic on this page is correct.

Determine your **Maximum Heart Rate** with the formula **220 minus your age**. Your heart rate
training zone is **50% to 90%** of maximum. **60% of MHR is recommended for burning fat while 85% is
recommended for strengthening the cardiovascular system. This 60% to 85% is the zone to stay in for
maximum benefit.**

**Example**, for someone who is 40 years old:

- 220 - 40 = **180** (maximum heart rate)
- 180 x .6 = **108** beats per minute (60% of maximum)
- 180 x .85 = **153** beats per minute (85% of maximum)
- So for a 40 year old the training zone would be **108 to 153 beats per minute.**

**The 2021 and 2024 CRW800 books print this page identically, to the figure.** Neither machine has a
heart rate program to enter the age into. The page ends with the standard
Spirit sentence that you may use the heart rate monitor feature without using a Heart Rate program,
during manual mode or during any of the nine different programs - but this console offers **eleven**
modes and **none** of them is a heart rate program. See
`crw800-2024-programs-eleven-workout-modes-in-mode-button-order`.

The 2024 Spirit steppers print this same page and the same 108-to-153 example - see
`spirit-stepper-programs-target-zone-of-108-to-153-beats`. The 2024 ellipticals and bikes print 65%
and 80% and reach an impossible 225.

**The XRW600 rower is the one to be careful of.** Its page is otherwise word for word this one, but
it prints **80%** where these print 85%, and reaches **144** beats where these reach 153 - see
`spirit-rower-programs-target-zone-of-108-to-144-beats`. Two Spirit rowers, two different upper
figures.
