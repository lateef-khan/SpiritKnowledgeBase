**500M/TIME appears in four workout modes only: Manual, Distance, Time and Calorie.** In the other
seven modes the display function is not there at all.

**The console goes directly into Scan mode after start.** Taking Manual mode as the manual's own
example:

1. When the console starts, the **Matrix in the middle of the LCD shows the wave pattern**.
2. **After 5 seconds** it switches to **500M/TIME 00:00** across the centre display.
3. **After another 5 seconds** it switches back to the wave pattern.
4. The cycle repeats. **That is the Scan function.**

**The Mode button stops the cycling and picks one.** Pressing Mode shows the wave pattern; pressing
it again displays 500M/TIME; pressing it again returns to the Scan function. The manual describes it
as recyclable - the three states rotate.

**None of these four manuals says what 500M/TIME measures.** They give no definition, no units beyond
the `00:00` example, and do not say whether the figure is the split for the last 500 m, a projection,
or an average. Do not tell a customer it is a 500 m split; these documents do not.

**The water rowers do define it, and they are a different console.** The CRW900 and CRW800H2O print
*"Displays the time needed to row 500 meters according to current speed and average speed"* and give
the reading its own window with an AVERAGE banner beside it
(`spirit-water-rower-console-time-500m-and-average`). **That definition belongs to that console, not
to this one** - here 500M/TIME is a state of the centre matrix, not a separate window.

**Four machines print this section word for word:** CRW800-2021, CRW800-2024, XRW600-2019 and
XRW600-2021. The only difference is capitalisation - the XRW600 books shout `SCAN` and `MODE` where
the CRW800 books write `Scan` and `Mode`.

**This is not the Scan key of the other Spirit consoles.** On the LED ellipticals, bikes and steppers
a dedicated **Up/Down Scan** button steps a whole row of four readings and dwells four seconds on
each set (`spirit-ce-console-led-data-windows-scan`). Here there is no Scan key, only the Mode key,
and the dwell is **five** seconds.

**Three service manuals print this section word for word**, so this card now covers the
**CRW800-2016** (CW800-YR001) as well - the same four modes, the same five-second cycle and the same
Mode-key rotation. The 2016 and XRW600 service books draw the three states as a line, **SCAN
Function → Wave Pattern → 500M/TIME**; none of the three defines the reading either.
