**The drawing labels four things:**

| Call-out | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Large LED Matrix Window** | The centre of the display |
| **Built-in Heart Rate Receiver** | Left of the keypad |
| **Easy-Touch Control Buttons** | The key row below the display |
| **"Ten innovative programs offer a variety of workouts"** | A caption, not a part |

**The key row is five keys and reads, left to right: RESET, START/STOP, MODE, DOWN, UP.**

**Rendered at 300 dpi, both printings agree.** The 2024 drawing and the 2021 drawing carry the same
five keys in the same order, each with its own printed label under it: a circular-arrow glyph on
RESET, a play-and-square glyph on START/STOP, a list glyph on MODE, and a solid triangle on each of
DOWN and UP. **START/STOP is the only coloured key** - red on the 2021 drawing, mid-grey on the 2024
one, where the rest of the console art was redrawn in greyscale.

**The window labels around the matrix are STROKES/MIN, TIME, DISTANCE across the top and HEARTRATE,
CALORIES, WATT across the bottom.**

**"Ten innovative programs" contradicts the Mode list on the page before it**, which steps through
**eleven**: Manual, Distance, Time, Calories, 20/10 Interval, 10/20 Interval, Custom Interval, Fat
Burn, Cardio, Strength and Game. Nothing in the manual resolves it; both figures are reproduced as
printed.

**Neither book prints a Bluetooth symbol, an app name or a USB socket on this console.** A loose word
search of both returns no `bluetooth` and no `usb` at all. The CRW900 water rower does have a
Bluetooth key (`crw900-2021-console-bluetooth-uploads-the-workout-summary-to-the-app`); this console
does not.

**A built-in heart rate receiver is named on the drawing and nowhere else.** The manual gives it no
protocol, no pairing procedure and no strap model; its only other statement about heart rate is the
window's **40 - 220 bpm** range and the blinking dot that marks a live signal
(`crw800-2024-console-window-functions`). It does prescribe a chest strap transmitter, sold
separately, in its safety and heart rate chapters.

**Both printings draw the same console.** The 2021 drawing shows it lit with a worked example -
**36** strokes/min, **4:02**, **1317** distance, **135** heart rate, **64** calories, **460** watts,
level **4** - while the 2024 drawing shows every segment on, including a **Km / M** unit pair at the
right-hand end of the lower digit row that the 2021 example never lights. The 2021 drawing also
carries no page heading; the 2024 book gave the page one, **CONSOLE SCREEN - OVERVIEW**.

**No fan, no USB port, no water bottle holder, no power input and no reading rack is labelled** on
this console. The console section says it runs on **two C batteries**, while the troubleshooting table
on p. 32 names an **AC 100-240 V input with a DC12V output**; the manual contradicts itself and this
drawing settles nothing (`crw800-2024-console-two-c-batteries`).
