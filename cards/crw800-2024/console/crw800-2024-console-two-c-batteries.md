**The console operates on 2 x C batteries. They are not included.** **The battery compartment is on
the back side of the console.**

That is the entire section - two sentences. The manual gives **no expected battery life, no
alkaline-or-rechargeable guidance, no low-battery warning behaviour and no instruction for removing
the batteries during storage**, and it does not say what the console does as the batteries fail.

**But the same manual also describes a mains supply, and it does not reconcile the two.** Its
troubleshooting table, for "LCD display does not shine, incomplete or imperfect", says *"Console
power is too low. Check whether the **AC power input is AC100 ~ 240V** and the output is **DC12V**"*
and then *"Has the cable tensioner output DC12V, if not, please replace the new cable tensioner"*
(p. 32). Its machine-care page is headed **UNPLUG ROWER BEFORE PERFORMING ANY MAINTENANCE**. **The
console drawing labels no power input** (`crw800-2024-console-screen-overview`) and the assembly
chapter names no cord.

**Both statements are reproduced as printed.** A dead display on this machine is a battery question
*and* a supply question; check the C cells first, because that is the only power the console section
names, then the 12 V supply the troubleshooting table names.

**C cells, and only here.** The Spirit air bike console takes **four AA** batteries
(`spirit-bike-console-air-bike-four-aa-batteries`) and the XIC600 takes **two AAA** in each of two
compartments (`spirit-bike-console-xic600-two-aaa-batteries-each`). Do not carry a cell size across.

**Both CRW800 printings say it.** The 2021 book and the 2024 book print the same two sentences; the
2021 book spells it *"battery compartement"*. **That typo is the only textual change in the whole
console chapter between the two printings** - everything else, down to the eleven-mode order and the
270-second sleep, is word for word.

**The CRW800 (2020) service manual agrees that the console holds batteries, and adds a generator.**
Its battery replacement step is *"Use a screwdriver to remove a screw of battery cover on the back of
console then replace batteries"* - no cell size, no count. Its console-problem page says a dark
screen means *"check the generator power is the normal power supply"* and the generator controller's
output, and its lookup table's first two remedies for a dark LCD are **check the battery** and
**check the generator**. **The 12 V adapter text of the owner's manual is gone from this book**:
the word adapter appears nowhere in it. So on the 2021 machine the console runs on its batteries
with a generator in the drive, and the AC100-240V / DC12V line in the owner's manual is the older
adapter machine's text (`spirit-rower-console-powered-by-a-12v-dc-adapter-through-a-dc-jack`).

**The XRW600 is not covered here and takes nothing from this card.** Its manual has no BATTERY
INSTALLATION section at all and names no power source for its console
(`xrw600-console-no-power-source-is-named`). **Do not tell an XRW600 owner it takes two C cells** -
neither of its manuals says so.

**The CRW800H2O and CRW900 water rowers are different machines and are not covered here.** They take
**four AA** cells (`spirit-water-rower-console-four-aa-batteries`). Nothing on this card is claimed
for them.
