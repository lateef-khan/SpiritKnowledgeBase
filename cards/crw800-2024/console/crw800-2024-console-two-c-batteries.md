**The console operates on 2 x C batteries. They are not included.** **The battery compartment is on
the back side of the console.**

That is the entire section - two sentences. The manual gives **no expected battery life, no
alkaline-or-rechargeable guidance, no low-battery warning behaviour and no instruction for removing
the batteries during storage**, and it does not say what the console does as the batteries fail.

**But the same manual also describes a mains supply, and it does not reconcile the two.** Its
troubleshooting table, for "LCD display does not shine, incomplete or imperfect", says *"Console
power is too low. Check whether the **AC power input is AC100 ~ 240V** and the output is **DC12V**"*
and then *"Has the cable tensioner output DC12V, if not, please replace the new cable tensioner"*
(p. 32). Its machine-care page is headed **UNPLUG ROWER BEFORE PERFORMING ANY MAINTENANCE**. **The
console drawing labels no power input** (`crw800-2024-console-screen-overview`) and the assembly
chapter names no cord.

**Both statements are reproduced as printed.** A dead display on this machine is a battery question
*and* a supply question; check the C cells first, because that is the only power the console section
names, then the 12 V supply the troubleshooting table names.

**C cells, and only here.** The Spirit air bike console takes **four AA** batteries
(`spirit-bike-console-air-bike-four-aa-batteries`) and the XIC600 takes **two AAA** in each of two
compartments (`spirit-bike-console-xic600-two-aaa-batteries-each`). Do not carry a cell size across.

**The CRW800H2O water rower is a different machine and is not covered here.** Nothing on this card is
claimed for it.
