**The manual states nothing.** It names no standard, prints no FCC Part 15 declaration, no Class B
interference notice and no RF exposure separation distance.

**The other thirteen 2024 manuals all carry the page and the rower does not.** In those books
`WARNINGS, COMPLIANCE AND NOTICES` sits on printed page 4, between PRODUCT LABELS and SAFETY
INSTRUCTIONS, and lists FCC part 15, UL and Bluetooth (with FTMS)
(`xt-2023-safety-compliance-certifications`). The rower's printed page 4 is its SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS
page; there is no page between the labels and the safety list, and the chapter ends at the
electrical page on printed page 5.

The page is genuinely absent rather than unreadable: the manual's PDF pages 5, 6 and 7 extract 101,
307 and 164 native words respectively and are PRODUCT LABELS, SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS and ELECTRICAL
SAFETY in that order, with no image-only page among them. The whole 44-page book contains no
occurrence of `FCC`, `UL`, `Part 15` or `FTMS`.

**Do not answer a rower owner from the sibling manuals' compliance page.** Nothing in this book says
the machine is certified to those standards, and nothing says it is not.
