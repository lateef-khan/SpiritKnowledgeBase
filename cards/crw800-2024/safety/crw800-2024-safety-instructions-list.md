**This list opens straight at a WARNING.** Twelve of the other thirteen 2024 manuals print a DANGER
line above their list - `Always unplug this appliance from the electrical outlet immediately after
using and before cleaning` - and an opening `When using an electrical appliance, basic precautions
should always be followed`. **The rower prints neither.**

> Read all instructions before using this appliance.

> **WARNING - To reduce the risk of burns, fire electric shock, or injury to persons:**

| # | Instruction |
|---|---|
| 1 | Do not operate Rower on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and Rower may result. |
| 2 | Keep children away from the Rower. There are obvious pinch points and other caution areas that can cause harm. |
| 3 | Keep hands away from all moving parts. |
| 4 | Never operate the Rower is damaged. If the Rower is not working properly, call your dealer. |
| 5 | Do not operate where aerosol spray products are being used or where oxygen is being administered. Sparks from the motor may ignite a highly gaseous environment. |
| 6 | Never drop or insert any object into any openings. |
| 7 | Do not use outdoors. |
| 8 | Do not attempt to use your Rower for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended. |
| 9 | Use of a chest strap transmitter (sold separately) is an accurate method of heart rate analysis. Various factors, including the user's movement, may affect the accuracy of heart rate readings. |
| 10 | Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable for use on your Rower. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue. |
| 11 | This appliance is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge, unless they have been given supervision or instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible for their safety. |
| 12 | Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine. |
| 13 | User Weight Limit: 450 lb |

`Never operate the Rower is damaged` in item 4 is the manual's own wording; every sibling manual
prints `Never operate the [machine] if it has a damaged cord or plug`. The rower has no cord to
damage, and the sentence was cut rather than rewritten.

Item 5 warns of sparks from **the motor**. The rower has no motor and no mains supply; its console
runs on two C batteries (`crw800-2024-safety-console-runs-on-two-c-batteries`). The sentence is
carried over from the mains-powered manuals and the book does not reconcile it.

Item 9 says a chest strap **is** an accurate method. The eleven sibling manuals that print the
hand-pulse paragraph say the sensors `are not medical devices` and are `intended only as exercise
aids`; the rower prints no such disclaimer.

Beside the list is the boxed note printed on every one of the fourteen 2024 manuals:

> Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of
> your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine.

**This manual has no compliance page, no grounding page and no outlet figure** -
`crw800-2024-safety-no-compliance-page`.

**The CRW800H2O is a different machine.** It is a water rower with its own manual; nothing on this
page may be quoted for it, and nothing from its manual may be quoted here.
