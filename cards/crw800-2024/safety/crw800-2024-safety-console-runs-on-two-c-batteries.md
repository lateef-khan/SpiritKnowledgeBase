> The console operates on 2*C batteries (not included). The battery compartment is on the back side
> of the console.

- **Two C cells, and they are not in the box.** The manual says `not included`.
- **The compartment is on the back of the console.**
- **There is no mains plug, no outlet requirement, no voltage figure and no amperage figure.** That
  is an absence, not a figure of zero.

**The ELECTRICAL SAFETY page names no supply at all.** It is the shortest electrical page in the
2024 range - 164 native words - and it carries only the moisture and temperature limits and the
operation instructions. Alone among the fourteen manuals it prints no `NEVER remove any cover
without first disconnecting AC power` sentence and no ten-percent supply-voltage paragraph.

**There is no generator either.** The self-powered Spirit bikes and the CE800 elliptical are turned
by the user and produce their own AC power, which is why their manuals carry a shock warning with no
socket in sight (`spirit-bike-safety-no-mains-outlet-needed`,
`spirit-ce-safety-generator-produces-ac-power`). The rower prints neither statement: it is battery
powered, not self-generating. Do not answer a rower owner from the bike cards.

**Item 5 of this manual's safety list warns of `Sparks from the motor`**
(`crw800-2024-safety-instructions-list`). The rower has no motor. The sentence is carried over from
the mains-powered manuals of the same range and the book does not reconcile it.


## The 2021 CRW800 manual says the same thing

**The CRW800-2021 owner's manual prints the identical sentence** under BATTERY INSTALLATION:

> The console operates on 2*C batteries (not included). The battery compartement is on the back side
> of the console.

`compartement` is the manual's own spelling, in both printings. Its parts list names **no power
cord**, and its IMPORTANT ELECTRICAL INSTRUCTIONS section carries only the moisture and temperature
warning - no outlet, no voltage, no amperage and no ten-percent supply paragraph. The machine has
had no mains supply since 2021, not only since 2024.

**The two Spirit water rowers take four AA cells, not two C cells.** The CRW900-2021 and CRW800H2O
consoles run on `4 AA batteries` and their manuals print no electrical page at all
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-four-aa-batteries-and-no-electrical-page`). Never quote a C cell for a
water rower or an AA for a CRW800.

**The two XRW600 rowers do plug in.** Their manuals ask for a 110-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet and
list `1 Power Cord` among the parts (`spirit-xe-safety-outlet-110-volt-15-amp`). The XRW600 and the
CRW800 are both air rowers and are powered completely differently; read the figure off the book for
the machine in front of you.
