The keypad board is silkscreened **AB0054-K1-V1.0-20200313** and the page captions it
**AB0054-K1, front of the board**.

Four connectors sit in a row along the middle of the board. Left to right they are
**JK3, JK2, JK1, JK4**. JK1 is the wide one and its silkscreen reads **KEYBOARD**; JK3 and JK2
are three- and four-way plugs whose silkscreen both end in "FETY"/"ETY", i.e. SAFETY. The manual's
own white call-out boxes sit over the silkscreen beside JK3, JK2 and JK4, so those three labels
cannot be read in full from the page.

The CT800-2020 book prints the same page with the same four JK labels. The two ENT books
photograph the same **AB0054-K1** board and name the plugs instead of numbering them: **Safety
Key (3 PINS)**, **KEYBOARD+RF (10 PINS)**, a second **Safety Key** and **RF (3 PINS)** - so JK1 is
the 10-pin keyboard-plus-RF cable, JK3 and JK2 the two safety-key plugs, and JK4 the 3-pin RF
receiver.
