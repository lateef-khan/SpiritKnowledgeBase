These are the callout numbers on the exploded view drawing on page 50. The drawing
is a picture with bare numbers on it and no text, so this table is the only
readable form of it.

**The list heads itself `CT850 - ST8600 - YT058 - 01 Part List SPIRIT (LED, 120V`**
and is date-stamped **230809** at its foot. The closing bracket after `120V` is lost
in the conversion; nothing else follows it on the page.

**ST8600-YT058 is the drawing number this machine's own *service* manual prints on
its exploded view**, and that service manual carries no parts list at all - see
`ct850-2020-exploded-view-drawing`. So this owner's-manual table is the only printed
mapping from an ST8600-YT058 callout to a part name. The date stamp does not tell
Spirit's lists apart: the CT800-2020 owner's manual list carries the same **230809**
stamp and is headed `CT800 - ST8600 - YT057 - 01`, one digit away from this one. See
`spirit-commercial-service-manual-document-codes`.

**129 rows are printed.** The whole numbers that appear are 1-29, 31-41, 44-53,
55-68, 70-76, 94, 101-117, 119-140, 142-146, 150-156 and 158-163. Any number outside
that run is not printed in this manual at all - **30, 42, 43, 54, 69, 77-93, 95-100,
118, 141, 147-149 and 157 are absent, not a missing page.**

**Do not read this list against the CT850-2016 list.** Both hold 129 rows and 128 of
the numbers appear on both, which makes them look interchangeable. They are not.
Item **94** (16 concave washers) exists only here; the 2016 list has no 94 and prints
item **118** (`Ø4 × Ø10 × 1.0T_Flat Washer`, qty 1) instead. Nine more rows carry a
different part or a different quantity:

**Items 3 and 4 swap sides between the two lists.** This list calls item 3 the left
locking plate and item 4 the right; the 2016 list calls item 3 the right and item 4
the left. Confirm the hand against the machine before ordering either.

**Items 50 and 51 are printed identically** - both read
`400m/m_Handpulse Wire (Upper)`, quantity 1 each - so this list cannot tell the two
upper handpulse wires apart. The CT850ENT-2022 list distinguishes them as `(XHB-4)`
and `(XHB-3)`; nothing in this manual says which of the two is which here.

**Item 112 is printed `3/8" × UCN16 × 2"` while items 110 and 154 are printed
`3/8" × UNC16`.** UCN and UNC are the same thread callout spelled two ways in the one
list. Both spellings are left exactly as printed below; nothing here changes a
number, a size or a quantity.

**No motor rating, roller size, deck size or belt size is printed for any of these
parts.** Item 55 is "Drive Motor" and item 66 "Incline Motor", each with no
horsepower or wattage; items 15, 16, 17 and 18 give the front roller, rear roller,
running deck and running belt no dimensions. See
`spirit-ct850-specs-no-specification-table`.
