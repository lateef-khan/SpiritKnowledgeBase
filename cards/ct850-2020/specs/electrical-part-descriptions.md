- **Safety key** - fits on the console and activates all functions. With no safety key the
  console cannot be controlled.
- **Console** - the interface that controls all functions of the treadmill; key controls and an
  LED display.
- **Main controller** - carries the AC power supply for the console, the incline driver and the
  AC motor driver, and outputs the appropriate voltages for the **DC control Board**. The
  general-information line lists power supply, AC motor, incline motor, **AC control Board**
  control circuit and incline control circuit.
- **AC motor** - controls speed increases and decreases. This manual prints **no work voltage**
  for it.
- **Incline motor** - a **110 volt AC** motor with four wires (red, black, white, green) plus one
  3-pin position-sensor cable. AC on **red = UP**, AC on **black = DOWN**, **white = COM
  (neutral)**, **green = ground**.

The CT850ENT book prints the same section word for word, with one change: its console line
reads "Key controls and TFT Display". The same 110 volt incline motor and the same absence of an
AC-motor voltage apply to it.

The 2016 CT850 manual prints the same section with the incline motor as a **120 volt** AC motor
and the AC motor at AC 120V~. The two manuals disagree; use the one that matches the machine.
