| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| LED light is broken | Replace with new LED or console |
| Power to console too low | Check AC power is 120V. Check power to console. Replace lower controller. |

**This manual calls the display an LED; the CT850 2016 manual calls it an LCD**, and asks for
**110-120V** rather than 120V. See `spirit-lcd-dim-or-incomplete`.

Dead segments rather than a dim light are the next row down:
`ct850-2020-led-displays-dim-or-incomplete`.
