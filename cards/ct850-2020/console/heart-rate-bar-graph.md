The bar graph is a graphical representation of your heart rate **as a percentage of your estimated
maximum heart rate**. When you enter your age during programming, the console calculates your
maximum heart rate, then lights the graph to show the percentage of estimated maximum you are
currently achieving.

The manual does not print the formula it uses to estimate maximum heart rate.
