**The minimum speed here is 0.3 and the maximum is 15.0.** The CT850-2016 owner's manual prints the
same eight steps with a minimum of **0.5**: `ct850-2016-console-calibration-minimum-speed-0-5`. That
one figure is the only difference between the two routines. The CT800-2020 and CT900 routine sets a
maximum of **12.0** (`ct900-calibration-procedure`), and the CT800 2012 and 2016 routine adds a
ninth Grade return step and holds Start with **Fast +**
(`spirit-ct800-console-calibration-with-grade-return`).

1. **Remove the safety key.**
2. **Press and hold down the Start and Speed Up buttons and replace the safety button.** Continue to
   hold Start and Speed Up until the window displays **"Factory settings"**, then press **Enter**.
3. Set the display to show **Metric or Imperial** settings (Meters vs. Miles) with the **Up** or
   **Down** button, then press **Enter**.
4. Make sure the **wheel size diameter is 2.98**, then press **Enter**.
5. Adjust the **minimum speed** (if needed) to **0.3**, then press **Enter**.
6. Adjust the **maximum speed** (if needed) to **15.0**, then press **Enter**.
7. Adjust the **maximum elevation** (if needed) to **15**, then press **Enter**.
8. **Press Start to begin calibration.** The process is automatic; **the speed will start up without
   warning, so do not stand on the belt.**

Step 1 says safety key and step 2 says safety button; they are the same part.

This manual prints no adjustable range for any of these values and does not say what to do when
calibration fails.

**The CT850-2020 service manual contradicts steps 5 and 6.** Its factory settings table gives
ADJUST MIN SPEED a default of **0.5 mph** with a range of 0.3 to 0.5, and ADJUST MAX SPEED a default
of **12.0 mph** with a range of **10.0 to 12** - so the 15.0 this owner's manual tells you to set is
outside the range the service manual prints for the same machine
(`ct850-2020-factory-setting-ranges`). The service manual also describes the same gesture as an
incline calibration: `ct850-2020-incline-calibration`. Nothing in either document resolves the
disagreement; read both before you overwrite a value.
