- Press and release any button to wake the display up if it is not already on.
- Press and release the **Start** button to begin belt movement at **0.5 mph**, then adjust to the
  desired speed with the Up/Down Speed buttons. You may also use the **Direct Access Speed Buttons 1
  through 12** to set a specific speed directly.
- To slow the belt, press and hold the Speed Down button - on the console or on the hand rail - to
  the desired speed. The Direct Access Speed Buttons work here too.
- To stop the belt, press and release the red **Stop** button.

What Stop does on the second and third press is a separate behaviour.

**The CT800-2020 owner's manual prints these four bullets word for word on its p. 17**, including the
twelve Direct Access Speed Buttons, so this card covers that machine.

**The earlier CT800 console has five rapid speed keys numbered 2 through 6, not twelve numbered 1
through 12**: `spirit-ct800-console-quick-start-speed-keys-2-to-6`. The CT800ENT-2022 touchscreen has
none at all: `ct800ent-2022-console-quick-start`. Do not read one machine's key numbering onto
another.

**The CT850-2016 and CT850-2020 owner's manuals print fifteen direct access speed buttons, not
twelve**, in these same four bullets on their p. 17:
`spirit-ct850-console-quick-start-speed-keys-1-to-15`. The service manual drawing this card was
written from numbers the ladder 1 to 12. The two documents describe the same machine and disagree,
so the CT850 machines are held on that card and not on this one; count the ladder on the console in
front of you.
