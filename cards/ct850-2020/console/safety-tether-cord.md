A safety tether cord is supplied and should be used at all times. It is a simple magnetic design.
Pulling it stops tread-belt movement.

1. Place the magnet into position on the **round metal portion of the console control head**. The
   treadmill will not start or operate without it. Removing the magnet also secures the treadmill
   from unauthorized use.
2. Fasten the plastic clip onto your clothing securely to assure good holding power.

The manual's note, word for word:

> The magnet has strong enough power to minimize accidental, unexpected stopping. The clip should be
> attached securely to make certain it does not come off. Be familiar with its function and
> limitations. The treadmill will stop, depending on speed, with a one to two step coast anytime the
> magnet is pulled off the console. Use the Stop / Pause switch in normal operation.

This is printed in the Product Operation section as a console interaction, not in the Product Safety
Instructions section.
