The **Pulse** window displays your current heart rate in beats per minute during the workout.

- You must use **both** the left and right stainless steel sensors to pick up your pulse.
- Pulse values are displayed any time the computer is receiving a Grip Pulse signal.
- Grip Pulse may be used while in Heart Rate Control.
- The treadmill will also pick up **wireless heart rate transmitters that are Polar compatible,
  including coded transmissions**.

The manual names no Bluetooth chest strap for this machine and gives no pairing procedure.
