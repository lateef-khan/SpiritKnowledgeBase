**How to enter the factory settings:** press **Start** and **Speed UP** together while the console
is in safe key reset or power up reset. Press **Enter** to enter units setting.

The console then walks through these prompts. Press **Enter** after each one.

1. **UNITS: ENGLISH** - the default is English. Press any up/down arrow to change to Metric.
2. **ADJUST WHEEL SIZE** - default 2.98, adjustable 1.50 to 3.50.
3. **ADJUST MIN SPEED 0.5** - default 0.5 mph, adjustable 0.3 to 0.5 mph. This is the speed number
   shown in the speed window.
4. **ADJUST MAX SPEED 12.0** - default 12.0 mph, adjustable 10.0 to 12 mph.
5. **ADJUST MAX MAX ELEVATION 15** - default 15, adjustable 10 to 15. The message window shows
   `ADJUST MAX ELEVATION THEN PRESS START TO CALIBRATE`. **Press Start**, not Enter.
6. **INCLINE CALIBRATION** runs. The message window shows `CALIBRATION`, the incline window shows
   `A/D` and the speed window counts `C15` down to `C0`.
7. When it finishes, the console resets.

`ADJUST MAX MAX ELEVATION` is printed with MAX twice in the source; it is one setting.

The manual gives no separate speed calibration step - the maximum and minimum speed values above are
the whole of it - and it does not say what to do if the calibration fails.
