> A safety tether cord is provided with this unit. It is a simple magnetic design that should be
> used at all times. It is for your safety should you fall or move too far back on the tread-belt.
> Pulling this safety tether cord will stop tread-belt movement.

- **Use it every time.** The manual's words are `should be used at all times`.
- It protects against **falling or moving too far back on the tread-belt**.
- **The treadmill will not start or operate without the magnet in place.** Removing the magnet also
  secures the treadmill from unauthorized use.
- **Fasten the plastic clip to your clothing securely.** The manual warns that the magnet is strong
  enough to resist accidental stops, so a loose clip is what fails.
- **Expect a one to two step coast**, depending on speed, after the magnet is pulled off. It is not
  an instant stop.
- **Use the Stop / Pause switch in normal operation**, not the tether, and
  `Be familiar with its function and limitations`.

The console-side behaviour of the same magnet - powering the console up and down, and the reset it
performs - is described elsewhere in section 4 and belongs to the console section.

## All four CT850 owner's manuals print this section

**Word for word**, under the heading `SAFETY TETHER CORD`. Every instruction is the same: the
magnet is also the key, the treadmill will not start without it, removing it secures the machine
from unauthorized use, the clip must be fastened securely, and a **one to two step coast** is
normal.

The **CT850ENT-2022** manual changes two words, exactly as the CT800ENT manual does: `treadbelt`
where the 2016, 2018 and 2020 manuals write `tread-belt`, and `Stop/Pause button` where they write
`Stop / Pause switch`. Nothing in the rule changes.

**Where the section sits moves between books.** The 2016 and 2020 manuals print it in the console
chapter, the 2018 manual after the heart-rate programs, and the ENT-2022 manual in general
maintenance. It is the same section in all four.

**The CT850ENT-2022 manual adds a rule the other three do not print** - `Remove tether cord after
use to prevent unauthorized treadmill operation`, as a bullet of its safety instructions:
`spirit-2026t-safety-secure-the-tether-cord-after-use`. It also states in its starting-operation
instructions that the machine will not operate without the safety key inserted.

**The CT850 2016 service manual has no equivalent section.** It describes the safety key only as a
console control ("If no safety key, console can not be controlled", p. 11, printed 10) and mentions
the tether only in troubleshooting rows. Its safety chapter says nothing about a tether cord - but
the CT850 2016 **owner's** manual does print the full section, so the machine is not without the
fact.

The XT and CT800 machines print these same paragraphs in their own manuals
(`xt-2015-safety-tether-cord`), and the CT900 in its (`ct900-safety-tether-cord-and-key`). They are
other machines with their own cards.
