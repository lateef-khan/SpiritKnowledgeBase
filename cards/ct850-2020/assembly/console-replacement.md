1. Use an **M8 L Allen wrench** to remove the **8 bolts** of **Locking Plate (R)** and **(L)**.
2. Disconnect the console wirings and replace with the new console.

That is the whole procedure the manual prints. The figure marks the locking plates with an arrow
pointing in at each upright.

**The 2016 machine is not the same.** The CT850 2016 manual's section 10.2 removes **6 button head
socket bolts** from the **console support**, not 8 bolts from locking plates. See
`ct850-2016-console-replacement` before you work on a 2016 machine.

**Four books print these two steps word for word** - the CT850 2020 and CT800 2020 as section 11-2, the
CT800ENT and CT850ENT as section 9-2 - so this card covers all four machines. The ENT books print the same
eight locking-plate bolts for their ENT consoles; nothing in the procedure changes for the screen.
