**Zero the incline motor before you take it off the machine or fit it onto the frame. The zeroing
distance is 235mm.**

1. Use a **Phillips head screwdriver** to release the screws and remove the left and right motor base
   caps and the motor top cover. Turn the treadmill to stand on one side, remove the cables connected
   on the lower controller that run to the incline motor, then remove the incline motor with a
   **17mm open end wrench**.
2. Use the **17mm open end wrench** to install the new incline motor and resume all cable
   connections.

The manual gives the 235mm as a bare figure. It does not say between which two points the distance is
measured, and it does not say which way to turn the barrel to reach it.

**235mm is printed in the 2020 manual only.** The CT850 2016 manual prints the same caution but its
sentence is cut off before the figure, so it gives no zeroing distance at all - see
`ct850-2016-incline-motor-replacement`. Do not read 235mm back onto a 2016 machine without
confirming it.

**Four books print the same caution, the same 235mm and the same two steps** - the CT850 2020 and CT800 2020
as section 11-7, the CT800ENT and CT850ENT as section 9-7 - so this card covers all four machines. The 4.0T
2026 book prints the same two steps with **257mm**, and its rear incline motor zeroes at 210mm; both are on
that machine's own cards.
