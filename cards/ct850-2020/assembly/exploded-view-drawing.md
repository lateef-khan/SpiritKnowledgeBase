Chapter 10 is one full-page exploded view and nothing else. The title block reads:

- Drawing number **ST8600-YT058**
- Title **Explode View**
- Dated **20200713 10:00**
- Drawn under the **dyaco** mark

The drawing carries numbered call-outs. The highest number legible at 300 dpi is **163**, and a boxed
**Optional** inset in the top left shows item **70** on its own.

**There is no parts list.** The manual prints the drawing and stops - no table anywhere in the manual
maps a call-out number to a part number or a part name. To turn a call-out into an orderable part
number you need the separate parts list for drawing **ST8600-YT058**.

Two things worth knowing about this page:

- The drawing is titled **ST8600-YT058**, not CT850. The driver board section of the same manual is
  also headed YT058.
- The extracted text of this page reads the date as "220713 1000". The picture says **20200713
  10:00**.

The **CT850 2016 manual has no exploded view at all**, and neither manual prints a parts list.
