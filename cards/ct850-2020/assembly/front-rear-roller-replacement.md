1. Use a **Phillips head screwdriver** to release the **2 screws** for the rear roller end caps and
   remove the end caps.
2. Use an **M8 L-shape Allen wrench** to two screws for the rear rollers and remove the roller.
3. Use a screwdriver to remove the **Motor Base Cap (L)** and **(R)**, then remove the **Motor Top
   Cover**. Then use a **14mm open end wrench** on the tension screw and an **M8 L-shape Allen
   wrench** to release the motor adjusting bolts and the **4 motor locking bolts** under the
   treadmill. Then loose the drive belt.
4. Use the **M8 L-shape Allen wrench** to release the front roller screw and remove the front roller.
   Replace with new front and rear rollers and resume all parts in reverse order.
5. Adjust the belt tension and run the belt in the centre. **Refer to step 11.3 (5)** - that is
   step 5 of Motor Replacement, on `ct850-2020-motor-replacement`.

Step 2 is printed with its verb missing - "Use M8 L-shape Allen wrench to two screws for rear rollers
and remove the roller". It is quoted here as printed.

The CT850 2016 manual's step 3 also gives screw counts for the caps (4 Phillips head screws) and the
top cover (6 tapping screws), which this manual does not, and its cross-reference in step 5 points at
a section that does not exist. See `ct850-2016-front-rear-roller-replacement`.
