1. Use a screwdriver to remove the **Motor Base Cap (L)** and **(R)**, then remove the
   **Motor Top Cover**.
2. Disconnect all lower controller wirings.
3. Use a **Phillips head screwdriver** to remove and replace with the new lower controller, and
   reconnect all wirings.

The 2020 manual gives **no screw counts** for the base caps or the top cover, and no torque figure.

The CT850 2016 manual does print counts for its own machine - 4 Phillips head screws for the caps and
6 tapping screws for the top cover, on `ct850-2016-lower-controller-replacement`. They are printed
for the 2016 machine and this manual does not repeat them.
