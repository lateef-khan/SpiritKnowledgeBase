"**Unit is able to connect to Bluetooth heartrate transmitters (chest straps - sold separately), and to 3rd party apps via the FTMS protocol. Not compatible with 5khz chest straps (sold separately).**"

So an analogue (5 kHz, Polar-type) strap will not be read; a Bluetooth strap will, and its reading feeds the pulse window, the recovery test and the body fat measurement, each of which says "hands on the grips or the bluetooth chest strap (sold separately)". The compliance page lists FCC part 15 and "Bluetooth (with FTMS)". The XTERRA+ app pairing steps and minimum iOS 13.1 / Android 8.0 are on [the app card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-xterra-plus-app-ios-13-android-8.md); the About Your Machine page says data can be exported to Google Fit and Apple Fitness, iHealth and Garmin Connect. The book prints no chest-strap wearing page and no erratic-reading page.
