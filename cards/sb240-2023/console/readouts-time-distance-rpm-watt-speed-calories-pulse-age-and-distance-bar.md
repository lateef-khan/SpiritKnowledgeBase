| Readout | As printed |
|---|---|
| 1. TIME | Elapsed time, counting up from 0:00 to 99:59 in one-second steps; or set a count-down with UP and DOWN - **when it reaches 0:00 the computer beeps and resets itself to the original time** |
| 2. DISTANCE | Accumulated distance per workout up to 99.99 km or miles |
| 3. RPM | Pedal cadence |
| 4. WATT | The mechanical power the computer is receiving from your exercise |
| 5. SPEED | Workout speed in km or miles per hour |
| 6. CALORIES | An estimate of cumulative calories burned |
| 7. PULSE | Pulse rate in beats per minute |
| 8. AGE | Age-programmable from **10 to 99**; if none is set it defaults to **35** |
| 9. TARGET HEART RATE | The heart rate you should maintain, in BPM |
| 10. PULSE RECOVERY | The recovery grade ([recovery card](../../shared/console/xterra-console-recovery-grades-from-outstanding-to-poor.md)) |
| 11. DISTANCE BAR | Every bar indicates 100 meters or 0.1 mile |

The LCD face reads TIME/HEIGHT/FAT%, DISTANCE/WEIGHT, CAL/AGE/C-F, a SPEED dial, WATT, PROGRAM/LEVEL, PULSE, GENDER and an RPM dial. The page's note that the console takes Bluetooth chest straps and FTMS apps but not 5 kHz straps is on [the Bluetooth card](bluetooth-chest-straps-and-ftms-apps-but-not-5-khz-straps.md). The console-overview page carries a stray "116.5 mm" and two Chinese typesetting notes in its text layer that are **not printed on the page** - read the render, not the extraction, for that page.
