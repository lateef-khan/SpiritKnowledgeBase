Hardware step 1: **M10 x 75 carriage bolts (16) x4, 10 x 22 curved washers (17) x4, M10 domed nuts (18) x4**.

1. Attach the front stabilizer (2) to the main frame (1) and secure with 2 carriage bolts (16), 2 curved washers (17) and 2 domed nuts (18). Attach the rear stabilizer (3) to the main frame (1) and secure with 2 carriage bolts (16), 2 curved washers (17) and 2 domed nuts (18). **Screw the foot pad (14) into the bottom of the main frame (1).**

The foot pad is the single foot under the middle of the frame; the front stabilizer carries the transport wheels (the moving card).
