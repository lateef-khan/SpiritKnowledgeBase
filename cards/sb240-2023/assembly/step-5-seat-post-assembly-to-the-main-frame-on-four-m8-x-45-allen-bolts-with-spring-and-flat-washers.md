Hardware step 5: **M8 x 45 Allen bolts (23) x4, 8 spring washers (24) x4, 8 x 17 flat washers (25) x4**.

1. Attach the seat post assembly (11 & 12 & 13) to the main frame (1) and secure with 4 Allen bolts (23), 4 spring washers (24) and 4 flat washers (25).

The drawing stacks them bolt, spring washer, flat washer, two each side of the seat post foot.
