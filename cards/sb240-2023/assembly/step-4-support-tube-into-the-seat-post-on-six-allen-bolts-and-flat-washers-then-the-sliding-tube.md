Hardware step 4: **M8 x 16 Allen bolts (20) x6, 8 x 17 flat washers (25) x6**.

1. Insert the support tube (13) into the seat post (12) and secure with 6 Allen bolts (20) and 6 flat washers (25). Insert the sliding tube (11) into the seat post (12).

The sliding tube is not bolted here; the whole seat post assembly (11, 12, 13) bolts to the frame in step 5.
