Hardware step 7: **M8 x 45 carriage bolts (49) x2, 8 x 20 curved washers (19) x2, M8 domed nuts (39) x2**.

1. Attach the rear handlebar (74) to the seat post (12) and secure with 2 curved washers (19), 2 carriage bolts (49) and 2 domed nuts (39). Connect the lower pulse sensor wire (78) with the middle pulse sensor wire (47).

This is the last assembly step. The chapter prints no plug-in step; the adaptor (82) is in the parts list.
