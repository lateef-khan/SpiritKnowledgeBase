Hardware step 6: **M8 x 20 Allen bolts (26) x8, 8 x 17 flat washers (25) x8**.

1. Attach the seat (9) to the seat post (12) and secure with 4 flat washers (25) and 4 Allen bolts (26). Attach the back cushion (10) to the support tube (13) and secure with 4 flat washers (25) and 4 Allen bolts (26). **Secure the right pedal (15R) clockwise to the right crank. Secure the left pedal (15L) counterclockwise to the left crank.**

No tool and no torque are printed for the pedals.
