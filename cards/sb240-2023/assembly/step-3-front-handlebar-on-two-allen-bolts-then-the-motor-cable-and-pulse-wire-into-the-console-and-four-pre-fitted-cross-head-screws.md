Hardware step 3: **8 x 20 curved washers (19) x2, M8 x 16 Allen bolts (20) x2**; pre-assembled on the console, cross-head screws (44) x4.

1. Attach the front handlebar (7) to the front post (6) and secure with 2 curved washers (19) and 2 Allen bolts (20). Connect the upper motor cable (45) with the console (8). Connect the upper pulse sensor wire (46) with the console (8). Attach the console (8) to the front post (6) and secure it with the 4 cross-head screws (44) which are pre-assembled on the back of the console (8).
