| Residential | 1 Year |

**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the first of the SB240 book's three warranty pages, headed *WARRANTY - RESIDENTIAL* and *Effective August 01, 2023- SB240 Recumbent Bike LIMITED WARRANTY*. **The table has one column.** No Frame column and no Labor column are printed, so the page states no frame term and no labor term either way. It is the only one-column warranty table on any Xterra page in this knowledge base; the SB600 printed the same day in the same layout has three columns (`sb600-2023-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`).

**What the rest of the page does and does not add.** The opening paragraph warrants *all its home use Recumbent Bike parts for a period of time listed below*, so the one figure covers parts as a whole - a frame is a part, but no separate lifetime is promised for it. The SERVICE paragraph two pages on says *twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale or eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping ... establishes the labor warranty period*; that is the sentence every Xterra book prints whatever its table says (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`), and this card follows the table: the SB240 page promises one year on parts and prints no labor term. Tell a customer the labor sentence exists; do not promise twelve months of labor on the strength of it. The periods run from the date of retail sale on a sales receipt, or eighteen months from the factory shipping date without one.

The book is the Online-Support layout: no registration card and no ten-day deadline, a QR code instead (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-qr-code-and-support-tab-no-deadline`); five consumer duties without a registration item (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-without-registration`); nine exclusions that admit a non-dues paying facility at five hours a day or less (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-with-a-non-dues-facility-allowance`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-use-environment-residential-or-non-dues-facility-5-hours-a-day`); service department **(870) 935-1107** in step 3 (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-service-steps-dealer-service-organization-then-the-service-department`, `xterra-warranty-support-contacts-which-phone-number-each-bike-elliptical-and-climber-book-prints`).
