**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the first of the SB240 book's three warranty pages, headed *WARRANTY - RESIDENTIAL* and *Effective August 01, 2023- SB240 Recumbent Bike LIMITED WARRANTY*. **The table has one column.** No Frame column and no Labor column are printed, so the page states no frame term and no labor term either way. It was the only one-column warranty table on an Xterra page until the four strength books (below); the SB600 printed the same day in the same layout has three columns (`sb600-2023-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`).

**What the rest of the page does and does not add.** The opening paragraph warrants *all its home use Recumbent Bike parts for a period of time listed below*, so the one figure covers parts as a whole - a frame is a part, but no separate lifetime is promised for it. The SERVICE paragraph two pages on says *twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale or eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping ... establishes the labor warranty period*; that is the sentence every Xterra book prints whatever its table says (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`), and this card follows the table: the SB240 page promises one year on parts and prints no labor term. Tell a customer the labor sentence exists; do not promise twelve months of labor on the strength of it. The periods run from the date of retail sale on a sales receipt, or eighteen months from the factory shipping date without one.

The book is the Online-Support layout: no registration card and no ten-day deadline, a QR code instead (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-qr-code-and-support-tab-no-deadline`); five consumer duties without a registration item (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-without-registration`); nine exclusions that admit a non-dues paying facility at five hours a day or less (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-with-a-non-dues-facility-allowance`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-use-environment-residential-or-non-dues-facility-5-hours-a-day`); service department **(870) 935-1107** in step 3 (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-service-steps-dealer-service-organization-then-the-service-department`, `xterra-warranty-support-contacts-which-phone-number-each-bike-elliptical-and-climber-book-prints`). The electrical page's ten-percent voltage sentence is `xterra-warranty-what-the-electrical-and-console-pages-say-is-not-covered`.

**The four strength books print the same one-column table** - *Parts / 1 Year* under *Home Use Limited Warranty*, *Effective June 16, 2022 - Adjustable dumbbell LIMITED WARRANTY* (the stand: *Dumbbell Stand LIMITED WARRANTY*) - in the older layout, not the SB240's: registration within ten days (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover`), four consumer duties with no power-supply item (`xterra-strength-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-four-items-no-power-supply-item`), nine exclusions with store display and unregistered products (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-store-display-and-unregistered-products`), six service steps with a trip charge at (870) 336-4286 (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-service-steps-dealer-service-organization-then-the-service-department`, `xterra-warranty-support-contacts-which-phone-number-each-rower-and-strength-book-prints`), and an ATTENTION box on the contents page (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-use-environment-residential-only-any-other-use-voids-it`). No frame column and no labor column: an adjustable dumbbell has no frame the page warrants separately, and nothing on the page promises labor.
