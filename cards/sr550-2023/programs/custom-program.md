Custom is a program with a **fully customisable intensity pattern**.

1. On the program page press **"Add Pattern"** to start editing.
2. **Connect dots to map your workload across the 20 segments** of the program, from the easiest at the bottom to the hardest at the top.
3. Created patterns **can be edited later at any time**.

**Each segment's duration depends on the selected total time**, which you set right before starting.
