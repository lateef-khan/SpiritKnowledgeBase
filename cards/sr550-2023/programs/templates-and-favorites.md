**Templates** are preset programs with a preserved set of parameters.

- A program can be saved as a template **upon completion**.
- Or create one from scratch in the Templates tab: tap **"New Template"**, choose a base program, then save it with the properties you adjusted.
- **A maximum of 12 template programs can be stored.**
- To remove unwanted templates press **"Edit Templates"** in the Templates tab.

**Favorites.** A program can be labelled a favorite. **Up to 3 favorite programs appear right on the home screen.**

- To "like" a program, open its setup page and tap the **ribbon icon in the upper left corner**.
- All labelled programs are under the **Favorites** tab.
- To remove a label press **"Edit Favorites"**, or open the program setup page and un-tap the ribbon icon.
