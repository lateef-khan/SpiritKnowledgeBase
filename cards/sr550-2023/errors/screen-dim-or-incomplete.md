Condition as printed: "Screen not bright, incomplete, or imperfect."

Reasons:

1. **TFT light is broken.**
2. Power to console too low.

Solutions, in the manual's order:

1. Replace with a new TFT or console.
2. Check AC power is **110-120V**.
3. Check power to console.
4. Replace lower controller.
