Problem as printed: "The console doesn't display."

Solution: check the connection with the **750mm DC Power Cord (39)**, the **Gear Motor (35)** and the **500mm Computer Cable (Upper) (44)**.

The gear motor is in this list because it sits between the power input and the console on this machine.
