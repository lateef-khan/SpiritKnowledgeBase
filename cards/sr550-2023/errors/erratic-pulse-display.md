Condition as printed: "Erratic pulse display."

| Reason | Solution |
|---|---|
| 1. Another chest belt in use around the rower. | Check for other chest belt use around the rower. |
| 2. Other magnetic field disturbance. | Change the position or direction of the rower. |
| 3. Receiver is broken. | Replace with a new receiver. |
