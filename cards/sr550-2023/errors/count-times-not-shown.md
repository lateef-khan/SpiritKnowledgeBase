Problem as printed: "Count Times doesn't show in display."

Solution: check the connections with the **Optical Coupler Board (34)**, or the connection of all wires. If all the connections seem good, **try replacing the Optical Coupler Board (34)**.

Part (34) is the same board the console holder procedure tells you to separate the computer cables from.
