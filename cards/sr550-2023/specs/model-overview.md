The cover reads **SR550(2023)**, the factory code is **CW800C-YR005**, and the machine is described on the cover as an **ENT ROWER** - the ENT console is the Android touchscreen.

The Spirit database SKU is **555922**, named "SOLE SR550 2023". **Note that the SKU's last two digits are 22, not 23**, even though the name and the manual cover both say 2023.

Section 3 lists only two electrical assemblies:

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| Console | Interface that controls all functions of the rower. |
| Gear Motor | Increases or decreases the resistance level of the brake. |

The mechanical parts share their numbering with the earlier SR500 rower: Chain Cover (L)(71) and (R)(72), Nylon Net, Console (43), Console Holder Assembly (7), Handle (10), Controller Assembly (36), Ribbon Roll (30), Flywheel Pulley (25), Latch (26), Spring Latch (27), Fan (22), Flywheel (23), Gear Motor (35), Steel Cable (66), Aluminum Track (51), Seat (49), Rear Stabilizer (3), Folding End Assembly (6).
