The drive belt is a **6PJ-787L**, part (24).

Tensioning, as printed: tie up the **3/8" x UNF26 x 11T nut (82)** and adjust the **M6 x 6T nyloc nut (103)**. **Adjust the drive belt to the tension value of 70~80BLS** against the flywheel (23), then tie up the 3/8" x UNF26 x 11T nut (82) again.

The manual prints the unit as "BLS" with no gauge named; read it as pounds. It gives no frequency or acoustic gauge figure for this machine.
