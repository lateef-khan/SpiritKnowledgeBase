Working voltage: **DC 4.5~5.5V**. It controls the resistance increase and decrease.

**The manual's own test expects more than this.** The E2 voltage test procedure calls **+5~6.0 VDC** on Level Up and **-5~6.0 VDC** on Level Down a normal reading, which runs above the 5.5V top of the working range printed here. Both figures are printed, so read a value between 5.5 and 6.0 as inside the test's normal band, not as proof of a fault.
