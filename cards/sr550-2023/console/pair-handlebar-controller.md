Pair from inside engineer mode - reach it by pressing 10 times on the word "Settings" on the Settings page.

1. Press the **"Code"** button.
2. **Press and hold the Level up button on the controller** until the screen shows a number, which means the pairing is successful.

This is the first thing to try when the resistance buttons on the handle do nothing but the machine is otherwise healthy.
