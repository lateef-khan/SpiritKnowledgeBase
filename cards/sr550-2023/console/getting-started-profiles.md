Launched for the first time, the console prompts you to **enter as a guest or create a new profile**. A profile lets the machine remember your physical parameters and lets your workout records synchronise with the **SOLE+ mobile app**.

- Tap **+** to create a new profile.
- **A maximum of 9 profiles can be created.**
- **Each profile can be protected with a passcode.**

**Linking a SOLE+ account.**

1. Scan the QR code on the console screen to download the mobile app.
2. Install the app and complete registration.
3. Press **"Link"** at the bottom of the console screen.
4. Open the QR code scanning camera in the SOLE+ app and scan the QR code again to link your local profile with the SOLE+ account.

**A console profile cannot be linked to more than one SOLE+ account at the same time.** If you skip linking at setup you can do it later in Profile settings.
