The console powers up automatically when power is connected.

These models operate on **DC 12V / 3.3A**, supplied by the **power pack** that is connected to **115 VAC**. The **power socket is where the line cord plugs into the unit, on the front of the machine**.

The printed paragraph says "fitness bike" and "the front of the bike". This is a rower; the sentence was carried over from a bike manual.
