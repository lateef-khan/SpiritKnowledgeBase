The main screen of the **Training** section shows an overview of your activity and offers **5 training modes**:

- **Start**
- **Timer (Manual)**
- **Distance**
- **Calories**
- **Heart Rate**

**In Guest mode you can set your age and weight here**, which helps the machine calculate the workout summary more accurately.

Press **"All Programs"** on the home screen to reach the full program list.
