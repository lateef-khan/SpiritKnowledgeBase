Once a workout has started the console goes into its workout mode.

- Switch between **Stats** and **Charts** views of the Training section, or go to the Media section.
- In the Stats view, **tap on a number to choose which parameters are displayed**.
- **Turn the knob to adjust the resistance level.** This machine adjusts resistance with a knob, not with up and down keys on the console.
- Press **"Pause"** to pause. From there you can return to training, or end the workout and see the summary.
- **After 5 minutes of inactivity in Pause mode the workout ends automatically.**
