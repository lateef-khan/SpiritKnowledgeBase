Go to the **Settings** page, then **press 10 times on the word "Settings"** itself. That opens engineer mode.

The support note `sole-rw-engineering-mode-on-rowers` gives the way in from a cold start:

1. Turn the power switch on if the machine is not already on.
2. Log in to a personal account, or choose the Guest profile.
3. Tap the gear icon in the top left to open Settings.
4. Tap the word "Settings" rapidly until the Engineering Menu appears.

That note counts the taps as "several" rather than 10. Keep tapping until the menu opens.

The manual documents one job inside engineer mode - pairing the wireless handlebar controller - and shows no other menu items.

**The support note says engineering mode is "only on the SR500 model". Both service manuals contradict that.** These touchscreen steps match section 8.3 of the sr550-2023 manual. The sr500-2016 has an LCD console, and its manual opens engineering mode by holding UP and DOWN for 3 seconds in idle mode.
