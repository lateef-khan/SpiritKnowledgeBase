Tap the **right tab of the bottom panel** to reach the Media section. From there choose a **pre-installed third-party app**, or use the **Mirroring** function to cast content from a smartphone to the console screen. **Press the ? icon for detailed instructions.**

Once content is playing, use the **floating panel** to navigate and to work full screen mode:

- Touch the panel's **top edge and drag** the panel anywhere on the screen.
- Press **Hide Panels** for full screen with no stats on the top and no tabs on the bottom; press **Show Panels** to bring them back.
- Use the **arrow button on the right side of the panel** to hide and show the text labels, which shrinks the panel further.
- Press **"Apps"** to go back to the content source selection.
