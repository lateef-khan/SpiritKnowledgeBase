1. Remove the **Aluminum Track (51)** and the **Pedal (L)(R)**.
2. Use a **6mm wrench** to take apart the **M8 x P1.25 x 15L Socket Head Cap Bolt (98) x2pcs** and the **Folding End Assembly (6)**.

   **Do not take both bolts out at once.** The manual states it is not allowed to remove the 2 pcs of M8 x P1.25 x 15L Socket Head Cap Bolt (98) together: fit a new bolt into the original position first, then repeat the same steps for the other one.
3. Use a **10mm wrench** to take apart the **M6 x 6T Nyloc Nut (103)**.
4. Use a **22mm wrench** to take off the **22L x M16 x M22 x 37L Knob Nut (46)** and the **Seat Up/Down Adjustment Knob (47)**.

   **The Knob Nut (46) is fixed with thread-locker. Add 1~2cc of thread-locker when refitting it.**
5. Use a **13mm wrench** to remove the **M8 x P1.25 x 13T Cap Nut (100) x2pcs** and the **M8 x P1.25 x 20L Hex Head Bolt (99) x2pcs**, and take apart the **Tension Spring (48)** and the **Main Frame (1)**.
6. Use an **8mm wrench** to remove the **3/8" x UNC16 x 1" Socket Head Cap Bolt (101) x2pcs** and the **Ø3/8" x 20 x 3.0T Flat Washer (102) x2pcs**, and take apart the **Seat Stop Axle (15)** and the Tension Spring (48).
7. Reverse the above steps to resume the parts.

Part (47) is the **Seat Up/Down Adjustment Knob** here and the **Seat Folding Adjustment Knob** in the noise troubleshooting. It is one part.
