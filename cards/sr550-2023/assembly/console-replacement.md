1. Remove **two M5 x 10mm screws (85)** and take off the **Rotary Shaft Cover (169)**. Next, remove **four M5 x 10mm screws** from the back of the console and unplug the **11P upper computer cable (44)**. Then take off the console.
2. To remove the **Console Holder Assembly**, take out Chain Cover (L) and Chain Cover (R) first. Then separate the **11P Computer Cable (Upper) (44)** and the **8P Computer Cable (Lower) (45)**, apart from the **Board (34)**, and take out the **3/8" x UNC16 x 3-3/4" Socket Head Cap Bolt (83)**. The **Console Holder Assembly (7)** and **Console Assembly (43)** come away together.
3. Reverse the above steps. **Note that the 3/8" x UNC16 x 3-3/4" Socket Head Cap Bolt (83) and the 11P Computer Cable (Upper) can interfere with each other on the way through; the manual says this needs technique.**

The upper cable on this machine is an **11-pin**, and the lower an **8-pin**. Section 10.1 calls the same upper cable a "500mm Computer Cable (Upper) (44)".
