1. **Adjust the console resistance to the highest and remove the power cord.** Take out Chain Cover (L) (71) and Chain Cover (R) (72).
2. Pull the **Steel Cable (66)** out of the **Flywheel (23)**.
3. Use a driver to take out the **Ø5 x 16L Tapping Screw (111) x2pcs**. Take off the **Front Gear Motor Cover (74)** and **Rear Gear Motor Cover (75)**.
4. Use an **8mm wrench** to remove the **M5 x 5T Nyloc Nut** of the Steel Cable (66).
5. Remove the cables from the **Gear Motor (35)**. Use a driver to take out the **M5 x P0.8 x 15L Phillips Head Screw (78) x2pcs**.
6. Reverse the above steps to resume the parts.
