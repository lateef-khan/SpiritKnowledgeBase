The manual lists the main causes in this order:

1. **A screw not fixed during customer assembly.** Check them.
2. Check that all four **Adjustment Foot Pads (59)** are level with the ground and each nut is fixed.
3. Tie the **Seat Folding Adjustment Knob (47)**. If it makes noise, apply some lubrication at the position of the screw.
4. **Noise from the slide.** Clean the **Aluminum Track Pulley (53)**, **Pulley (54)** and **Aluminum Board (52)**. If that does not work, replace the Aluminum Track Pulley (53) and Pulley (54) directly.
5. **Noise from the Fan (22) or Flywheel (23).** Release the **Chain Cover (L) (71)** and **Chain Cover (R) (72)** and check.

Part (47) is called the **Seat Folding Adjustment Knob** here and the **Seat Up/Down Adjustment Knob** in the parts replacing guide. It is one part.
