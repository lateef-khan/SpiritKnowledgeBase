**The Ribbon Roll will not spring back.** Release the **Chain Cover (R) (72)** and check the **Latch (26)**. If the ribbon roll can spring back at that point, the break is on the other side - pull it out and check.

If the **Latch (26)** cannot spring back, release the **Attaching Plate (8R)** and check the connections of the **Spring Latch (27~2)** and the Latch (26). **If the Spring Latch (27~2) is broken, replace it directly.**

**The Ribbon Roll works roughly or gets stuck.** The ribbon roll may have got into a reverse position. Pull it out and check it, and check the Chain Cover (R) (72) as well.
