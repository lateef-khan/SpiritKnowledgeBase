- Install the machine on a flat level surface with access to a **120-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet**, with only this machine plugged into that circuit.
- Do not use an extension cord unless it is **16 AWG or better with only one outlet on the end**. Do not disable the grounded plug with an adapter or modify the cord outlet.
- **Never use a GFCI wall outlet with this machine.** As with any appliance with a large motor the GFCI will trip often. Route the power cord away from any moving part.
- Disconnect it from the outlet before cleaning or service work.
- The product must be grounded and is "for use on a nominal **120-volt** circuit". Improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric shock.
- The machine itself runs on **DC 12V / 3.3A**, supplied by a power pack connected to **115 VAC**.

**The safety pages call this machine a bike.** Sections 7.1, 7.2 and 7.3 say "bike" in every sentence and mention an elevation mechanism and transport wheels. This is a rower; the wording was carried over from a bike manual. The electrical figures still apply.
