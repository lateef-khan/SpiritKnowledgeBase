Go to the **Settings** page and **press 10 times on the word "Settings"** to enter engineering mode.

That is the whole of section 8.6. **The manual does not list what is inside the menu**, only how to open it. The LCR 2016, LCB 2016 and LCB 2019 manuals list a seven item menu reached by holding Start, Stop and Enter; that key combination does not apply to this console.
