**This is EEPROM ERR, not LWR not found and not LWR not match.** Those are the other two messages this bike has, and both are brake controller faults, not EEPROM faults.

Definition: all screens are off and outputs stop when the EEPROM is damaged or malfunctions. The display message shows **"EEPROM ERR"**.

Troubleshooting: **the EEPROM is abnormal, replace the Display Board directly.** The manual gives no other step.

The error table prints the meaning as "EEPROM failure".

A multi-meter is the only tool the manual asks for.
