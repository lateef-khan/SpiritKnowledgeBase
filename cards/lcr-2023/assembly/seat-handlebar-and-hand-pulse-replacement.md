1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **4 pcs of M6 x 15 mm Phillips head screws (98)** to take the **seat (61)** apart.
2. Use **two 14 mm wrenches** to unscrew, from the **seat carriage (4)**, **2 pcs of 3/8" x 2-3/4" hex head bolts (175)**, **4 pcs of 3/8" x 1-3/4" hex head bolts (71)**, **6 pcs of 3/8" x 7T nyloc nuts (89)** and **6 pcs of Ø3/8" x 19 mm x 1.5T flat washers (77)**, to take the **seat handlebar (6)** apart.
3. Unplug the **HGP wire grommet (126)** which secures the hand pulse with cable assembly (21, 27) and use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **4 pcs of Ø3 x 20 mm tapping screws (97)** to take the hand pulse with cable assembly apart.
