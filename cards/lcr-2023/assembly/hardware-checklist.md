Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #65 | 3/8" x 53L | Carriage Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #77 | 3/8" x 19mm x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #84 | 3/8" x 25mm x 2T | Flat Washer | 2 pcs |
| #176 | 3/8" x 3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 6 pcs |
| #191 | 3/8" x16 x 12.5T | Cap Nut | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 4**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #98 | M6 x 15mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |

| #192 | 3.5 x 12L | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |
| #196 | ø5 x 0.6T | Star Washer | 4 pcs |

**#196 Star Washer is new for 2023 and is unique to this model in this family.** The four star washers go under the four #99 Phillips head screws that hold the console onto its mounting plate. Neither the R92 2023 nor the LCR 2019 pack contains them, and the LCB 2023 uses a different number (#165) for its equivalent star washer.

**The step 1 line in the LCR 2023 checklist is badly typeset in the converted text**: the caption under the last two drawings reads "Cariage Bolt pes) CapNut@pe)". The same two items are printed cleanly in the step 1 hardware box on the instruction page as `#65 3/8" x 53L Carriage Bolt (2 pcs)` and `#191 3/8" x16 x 12.5T Cap Nut (2 pcs)`, and the values above are taken from there.

**Item numbers are not shared with the B94 or LCB uprights.** Here `#71` is a hex head bolt and `#99` is a Phillips head screw; on the uprights `#71` is a flat washer and `#99` is a curved washer. Always use the checklist printed in your own machine's manual.
