1. Use an open wrench to unscrew **6 pcs of 3/8" x 3/4" hex head bolts (176)** and **6 pcs of 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washers (77)** to take the **seat back frame (5)** apart.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew the seat back cover, **4 pcs of M5 x 15 mm Phillips head screws**.
3. Use a **combination M5 Allen wrench** to release the **M8 x 15 mm button head socket bolt** to take the **seatback cushion** apart.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.

The R92 2023 manual prints the same procedure without step 2; it has no seat back cover.
