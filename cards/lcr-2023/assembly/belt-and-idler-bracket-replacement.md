1. Take both left and right front shrouds apart.
2. Use a **13 mm wrench** to release the **M8 x 7T nut** and take off the **J-Bolt**.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **3 pcs of M6 x 7T Phillips head screws** on the **idle arm**, and the belt can be taken off.
4. To release the idle arm, unscrew **3 pcs of M6 x 15 Phillips head screws** with **3 pcs of Ø1/4" split washers** and **3 pcs of 1/4" x 13 x 1T** flat washers.
5. To refit the belt and idle arm, use a Phillips head screwdriver to secure the **generator flywheel** with **3 pcs of M6 x 15 Phillips head screws** together with **3 pcs of Ø1/4" split washers** and **3 pcs of 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers**. Turn the drive pulley to put the belt on the pulley, hook the J-Bolt on the mainframe, and use the 13 mm wrench to tighten the **M8 x 7T nut** on the J-Bolt until the belt is at an acceptable tension. Use an **audio tension gauge** to confirm the frequency is within **180 ± 10 Hz**. Last, turn the drive pulley and check the drive belt is centred and not falling out of the pulley.

**Two defects in the printed steps.**

- Step 3 calls the idle arm fasteners "M6x7T Phillips Head Screws"; step 4 calls the same fasteners "M6x15 Phillips Head Screws". M6 x 7T is a nut description, not a screw length.
- Step 4 ends mid-phrase: "3pcs of 1/4"x13x1T." with the part name missing. Step 5 completes it as 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers.

The tension figure here disagrees with section 10.2. See the belt tension card.
