1. Take both left and right front shrouds and the drive belt apart.
2. Unplug the electric wires connected to the **generator flywheel**, then use an **11 mm wrench** to unscrew **4 pcs of 1/4" x 3/4" hex head screws** with **4 pcs of 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers** and **4 pcs of Ø1/4" split washers**. The generator flywheel can then be released.
3. Follow the steps in order to refit the flywheel.

**The heading and the steps use two names for the same part.** The heading says induction brake; the steps say generator flywheel. Section 10.2 calls it "Generator/Brake (55)".
