1. Remove both left and right rear shrouds (35, 36) and the seat carriage (4), then use a **12 mm open end wrench** to remove **six 5/16" x 3/4" hex head bolts (94)**, **six 5/16" x 16 x 1.5T flat washers** and **six 5/16" x 1.5T split washers (82)** to take the **aluminium track (14)** apart.
2. Remove both left and right rear shrouds (35, 36) and both left and right front shrouds (29, 30). Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **four M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screws (99)** to take the **step cover** apart.

The heading calls the part a stabilizer cover; the steps call it the step cover.
