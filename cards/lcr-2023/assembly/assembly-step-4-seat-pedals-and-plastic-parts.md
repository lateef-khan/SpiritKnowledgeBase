Hardware: **#98** M6 x 15mm Phillips Head Screw (4 pcs), **#99** M5 x 12mm Phillips Head Screw (4 pcs), **#105** Ø4 x 16mm Sheet Metal Screw (4 pcs), **#192** 3.5 x 12L Sheet Metal Screw (4 pcs), **#196** ø5 x 0.6T Star Washer (4 pcs).

1. Install the Seat (61) on the Seat Carriage (4) with the four Phillips Head Screws (98), by using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (114).
2. Install the Computer Cable (44) and Handpulse Wire (45) into the connector in the back of the Console Assembly (19). Install the console onto the mounting plate with the 4 pcs of Phillips Head Screws (99) **and 4 pcs of Star Washers (196)**, by using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (114). **Be careful not to pinch the wires between the console and the mounting plate - this could short the wires and damage the electronics.**
3. Install the Drink Bottle Holders (L /39, R/38) onto **left of** the Seat Handle Bar (6) with the four Sheet Metal Screws (105).
4. Install the Pedals (L/116, R/117) in the Cranks by using the **14/15m/m Wrench (132)**. **The left Pedal (116 L) has a reverse thread** and screws into the crank in the opposite rotation from normal threads. There is an "L" stamped into the end of the threaded post of the left pedal and an "R" in the right. Make sure to tighten the pedals as much as you possibly can. It may be necessary to re-tighten the pedals if you feel a thumping during pedalling; a clicking or thumping sound during pedalling is usually caused by the pedals being too loose.
5. Install the Tablet Holder (190) to the back of the console with four Sheet Metal Screws (192), by using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (114).

**The hardware box on this page is mislabelled.** The step is printed as numbered step **4**, SEAT/PEDAL & PLASTIC PARTS, but the hardware box beside it is headed **HARDWARE STEP 3**. The assembly pack checklist calls the very same five items hardware step 4. This was checked against the page scan; it is a fault in the printed manual, not in the text conversion.

**The four star washers are the only hardware difference from the LCR 2019 step 4.** The R92 2023 has none.
