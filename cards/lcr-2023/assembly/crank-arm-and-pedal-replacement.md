1. Use a **13/15 mm wrench** to turn the **left pedal (116) clockwise** and the **right pedal (117) counterclockwise** to take the pedals off.
2. Turn the pedals in the reverse directions to refit them.
3. Take off the crank arm end cap (28) and release the **M10 x 1.25 nut (108)** with the wrench.
4. Use the plug that matches the cranks (51L, 51R) and turn with a hex wrench to release the cranks.
5. To refit the cranks (51L, 51R), use the hammer to seat the cranks tightly on the **drive pulley axle (8)**, turn the nut tight onto the axle, and plug in the crank arm end cap.
