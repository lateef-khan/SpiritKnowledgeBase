**180 ± 10 Hz, read with an audio (sound wave) tension gauge.** That is the figure in the assembly procedure, section 9.5 step 5, and it is the only usable figure this manual gives. This machine is set by frequency, not by Newtons.

Set it by tightening the **M8 x 7T nut** on the **J-Bolt** with a **13 mm wrench** until the gauge reads 180 ± 10 Hz.

**The manual states this figure two ways and they do not agree.**

| Where in the manual | What it says |
|---|---|
| Section 9.5 step 5, refitting the belt and idle arm | "Use audio tension gauge to make sure the frequency within the range of180±10HZ" |
| Section 10.2, the belt slipping answer | "Use 13m/m Wrench to turn M8x7T Nyloc Nut (88) clockwise until sound wave frequency falls between 450N" |

Section 10.2 mixes a frequency reading with a force unit, exactly as the R92 2016 and R92 2023 manuals do. 450 N is the figure for the B94 and R92 gear motor bikes, not for this belt system. Take **180 ± 10 Hz**.

**It also disagrees with the sister machines.** The LCR 2016, LCB 2016, LCB 2019 and LCB 2023 manuals all give **180 to 205 Hz** for the same belt system. This manual's 180 ± 10 Hz is 170 to 190 Hz, a narrower and lower band. Confirm with the technical department which range applies.
