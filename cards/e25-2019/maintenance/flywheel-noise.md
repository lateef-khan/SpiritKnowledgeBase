1. This problem is very rare. Most of the time it is caused by **incorrect cable adjustments or improper assembly causing offset**, which creates abnormal sounds. Follow the magnetic flywheel and cable disassembly procedures to make adjustments.
2. If there is abnormal sound from the magnetic flywheel, first check for foreign objects or friction and make adjustments. If adjustments cannot be made, replace the part.
