When the incline motor is not functioning, check all the wires for a secure connection, and check whether the incline controller and power adaptor are damaged or broken. Replace damaged or broken parts.

That is the whole of section 9.8 in this manual. The detailed nine-step electrical test, the LED and relay checks and the potentiometer voltages are in the E3 test procedure card.
