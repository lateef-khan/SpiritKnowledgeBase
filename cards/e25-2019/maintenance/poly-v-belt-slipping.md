If the Poly-V belt is slipping, **adjust the cap on the hook-type screw with a #13 wrench**. The manual sends you to the disassembly procedure for the adjustment itself.

If the belt is worn or damaged, replace it.

The manual does not repeat the tension figure here. The target is **190 Hz plus or minus 10 Hz** on an acoustic gauge; that number is printed only in the belt replacement procedure and has its own card.
