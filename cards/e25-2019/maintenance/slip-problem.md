Slipping is verified in these steps.

1. **M12 x P1.75 rod end bearing** - the M12 nut on top must be securely tightened with the pedal bar assembly, and the rod end bearing needs to be vertical.
2. **Bushing housing, pedal arm** - the CAP socket head cap bolt 3/8" x 2-1/4" on top and the nylon nut 3/8" x 11T must be tightened securely.
3. **Cross bar** - the CAP socket head cap bolt M8x40 m/m on top of the cross bar.
4. **Belt** - this appears quite often, because after a period of time most belts become loose from use. The solution depends on weight and adjustment method; use the belt disassembly procedure.

This manual gives no torque figure for steps 1 to 3. The E35, E55 and E98 manuals of the same year do give figures for the bushing housing and cross bar; do not carry those numbers onto this machine without confirming them.
