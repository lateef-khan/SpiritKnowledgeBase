Match the machine's serial number against the SN range column in the source table. Two pairs cover the sheet:

| Flywheel | Steel cable | SN ranges |
|---|---|---|
| K500042 | K020046 | 60 ranges, e.g. 5250182010041497~042000 |
| K500020 | K020005 | 8 ranges, e.g. 5250182011048469~048972 |
| K500090 | K020139 | 1 range: 5250182110085849~085932 |

The sheet carries one "Before 2021-08-19 is K500042+K020046" note, on a row already assigned K500020+K020005 - and rows with cut-off dates after 2021-08-19 still list both pairs. Match by SN range, not by date. The full range table is the source text.
