1. **Install the Incline Rail Assembly (2) into the U-channel bracket of the Main Frame (1).** From the sides install **two Hex Head Bolts (128)** with **two Flat Washers (171)** and **two Nylon Nuts (158)**. From the top install **four Hex Head Bolts (124)**, **four Split Washers (176)**, **four Flat Washers (166)**, and **four Star Washers (177)**. Tighten with **Wrenches (186 & 189)**.
2. **Connect the 3 wire harness: Incline Motor Power Cord (43)** to the female receiver from the Incline Rail Assembly (2). Connect the **Three pin position Sensor connector / Incline Motor Connecting Wire (44)** the same way.
3. **Run the Computer Cable (45) through the Console Mast (12)** with the twist tie, install the mast into the receiving bracket, and fit **four Socket Head Cap Bolts (129)** with **four Split Washers (175)** and **four Flat Washers (171)**. Tighten with the **L Allen Wrench (197)**, pulling slightly on the cable at the top so it does not get pinched and shorted.
4. **Install the Bottle Holder (105) on the Console Mast (12) with two Phillips Head Screws (140)** using driver (188). **The bottle holder must be assembled before the console.**
5. **Plug the connectors into the back of the console: Computer Cable (45), two Hand pulse Cables (52, 53), Resistance Switch Wire (55) and Incline Switch Wire (56).** Secure the **Console (40)** with **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (140)**.
