1. Follow 11-2, 11-3, 11-4, 11-5, 11-6 and 11-7 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, both side covers, cross bar, the belt and the idler wheel assembly.
2. Plug in the power, **adjust the resistance to level 20** and release the steel cable.
3. Use a **15 wrench** to loosen the **nylon nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 9T** on the flywheel and main frame. Remove the flywheel and belt.
4. To reassemble, return the belt onto the grooves on the flywheel and return the flywheel onto the mainframe.
5. Tighten both **3/8"-UNF26 x 11T nuts**. Return the steel cable onto the flywheel **at 45 degrees**, then return the idler wheel assembly.
6. Turn the drive pulley and check the belt is secured. Adjust both **3/8"-UNF26 x 4T nuts** to position the belt in the middle of the drive pulley, then tighten all other screws. Adjust the belt to a proper tension.
7. Follow 11-7, 11-6, 11-5, 11-4, 11-3 and 11-2 to return the other parts.

Two notes on the source. The section is numbered 11.7 in the body but is the eighth procedure, and the table of contents calls it 11.8. And the nut called out for loosening in step 3 is a **9T** nut while step 5 tightens **11T** nuts; the E55 manual prints the same two steps with the sizes the other way round. Check the nut on the machine before ordering.
