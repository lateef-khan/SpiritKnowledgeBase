Use screwdriver (187 or 188) for the plastic parts.

1. **Match the Connecting Arm Covers (L) and (R), (92) and (93)**, on the Connecting Arm (L) (8); each side with **two Phillips Head Screws (138)** and **one Sheet Metal Screw (145)** using the Short driver (187). Repeat on the other side.
2. **Slide Wheel Covers (94)** above each side of the Slide Wheel with **four Phillips Head Screws (138)**.
3. **Middle Stabilizer Covers (115 and 116)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (138)**.
4. **Front Stabilizer Cover (110)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (138)**.
5. **Cover Holders (21)**, bent-tab hole facing rearward, on the Incline Rail Assembly (2) with **four Phillips Head Screws (146)**; **Inclinable Rail Cover (113)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (138)**.
6. **Rear Bar Cover (114)** on the Rear Stabilizer and the Cover Holders (21) with **four Phillips Head Screws (138)**.
7. **Raise the incline by 3-8 levels by operating the console**, then secure the **Incline Bottom Cover (111)** on both sides of the Incline Rail Assembly with **two Phillips Head Screws (138)**.
8. **Console Mast Covers (95 & 95-1)** with **four Sheet Metal Screws (145)**.
9. **Handle Bar Covers (106 & 107, 108 & 109)** with **six Sheet Metal Screws (145)**.
10. **Tablet Holder (104)** on the back of the console with **four Sheet Metal Screws (145)**.
