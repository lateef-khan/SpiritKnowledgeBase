1. Follow 11-2 and 11-3 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, left and right side cases, cross bar and the belt.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **Phillips head screws M5x15mm** from the incline cover, then remove the parts.
3. Use **two 14mm wrenches** to remove the **hex head bolt 3/8" x 2-1/2"** together with the **flat washer 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T**, **nylon nut 3/8" x 7T** and two **nylon washers 3/8" x 35 x 5T** which secure the incline tube and incline motor.
4. Use **two 17mm open end wrenches** to remove the **hex head bolt 3/8 x 2-1/2**, **flat washer 3/8 x 19 x 1.5T** and **nylon nut M10 x 8T** from the outer rail tube and the incline adjustable assembly. Remove the inclined adjustable assembly.
5. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **tapping screw 5x16** from the incline motor grounding wire. Cut the wire and remove.
6. Use a **12mm wrench** to remove from the rail assembly the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 1** and **flat washer 5/16" x 35 x 2T**, pull out the locking tube assembly and remove the rail assembly.
7. If the aluminum rail must come off, remove the **Phillips head screws M5x15mm** from it.
8. **Zero the incline motor before taking it off the machine or fitting it to the frame. The zeroing distance is 207mm (+/- 1mm).**
9. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.

This manual gives the zeroing distance but not the barrel rotation that sets it. The E35 and E98 manuals of the same year describe rotating the incline barrel clockwise to the end then counterclockwise one and a half circles to reach the same 207 +/- 1mm.
