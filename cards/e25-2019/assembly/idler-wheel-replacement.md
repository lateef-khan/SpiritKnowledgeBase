1. Follow 11-2, 11-3, 11-4, 11-5 and 11-6 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, both side cases, cross bar and the belt.
2. Use a **13 mm wrench** to loosen the **M8 x 9T nut on the M8x170mm J-bolt** together with the **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** until the J-bolt is released. With the same wrench release the **M8 x 20 carriage bolt** securing the idler wheel assembly, together with the **5/16" x 20 x 1.5T flat washer** and **M8 x 7T nut**, and take the idler wheel assembly apart.
3. To reassemble, tighten the M8x20 carriage bolt, 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T flat washer and M8 x 7T nut to secure the idler wheel assembly. **Tighten these only after the belt has been adjusted.** Return the J-bolt and other parts.
