**Notes**

- The heart rate receiver plugs into the interface board at the HR receive point. It is a 5 kHz receiver: it takes the signal from a 5 kHz chest strap and passes it up to the display board. It calculates nothing, and it works separately from the hand pulse.
- The handpulse plates are glued to the handlebar, so replacing the console is easier than replacing them.
- The three lower computer cable entries are marked "I am pretty sure" in the source document. Confirm before ordering.
