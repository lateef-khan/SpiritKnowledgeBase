Some circuit breakers used in homes are not rated for the high inrush currents that can occur when a
treadmill is first turned on, or even during use.

If the treadmill trips the house circuit breaker even though it is the proper current rating, but **the circuit
breaker on the treadmill itself does not trip**, replace the home breaker with a **high inrush type**.

**This is not a warranty defect.** The manual states it is a condition the manufacturer has no ability to control.
The part is available through most electrical supply stores. The examples given are **Grainger part # 1D237**, or
online at www.squared.com **part # QO120HM**.
