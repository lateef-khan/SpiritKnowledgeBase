1. Hands are not on the hand pulse sensors, or only one hand is on a sensor. **Hold both hand pulse
   grips.**
2. The connector between the HANDPULSE W/WIRE and the console is not connected properly. Connect the cable again.
3. The wires were damaged when the HANDPULSE W/WIRE was connected to the console. Replace the cable.
4. The hand pulse board is broken. Replace the console or the hand pulse board.
