1. Remove the motor cover hood.
2. The speed sensor is on the **left side of the frame, right next to the front roller pulley**. That pulley has a
   belt around it that also goes to the motor. The sensor is small and black with a wire connected to it.
3. Get the sensor **as close as possible to the pulley without touching it**. A magnet sits on the face of the
   pulley; line the sensor up with the magnet. Loosen the screw that holds the sensor to move it, and re-tighten
   the screw when finished.

**Keep the gap between the sensor and the magnet under 3 mm.** A wider gap makes the console miss the speed
signal, which is what raises **E1** during calibration.
