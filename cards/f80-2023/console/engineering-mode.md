**Press 10 times on the word "Settings"** while you are on the Settings page. The word does not look
like a button, but it is.

Engineering mode lists **6 main functions**: Engineering Mode, Diagnostics, Machine Information, Maintenance,
Lube Setup and Key Test.

- **Engineering Mode** holds the calibration procedure.
- **Machine Information** holds GR Mode, Beep Mode, Display Mode and Child Lock.
- **Lube Setup** holds the lube reminder hours and its zeroing.
- **Key Test** walks through every key.

The manual gives no detail for the **Diagnostics** or **Maintenance** entries beyond their names.
