1. A **yellow dot on the gear icon** in IDLE MODE means a third-party media or mirroring application has
   an update waiting.
2. Go to **Settings > Media Apps**. Click the **">"** button to enter the update list.
3. Click the update button beside the app you want. Click **Back** to return to the Settings page.

**Do not interrupt the power supply during the update.** The manual warns the system may be damaged and unable to
function properly again.
