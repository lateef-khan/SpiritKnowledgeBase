In **Settings > Unit System**, one button toggles between **Metric and Imperial**. **The default setting
is Imperial.**

Four sub-items sit below it:

| Item | Units |
|---|---|
| Distance | mi / km |
| Speed | mph / km/h |
| Height | ft, in / cm |
| Weight | lb / kg |

The same choice can also be made during the calibration procedure in engineering mode.
