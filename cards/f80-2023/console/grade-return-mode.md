**GR Mode (Grade Return). Default: ON. Setting: ON/OFF.** It lives in Machine Information inside
engineering mode.

If the incline does not go back to zero while GR Mode is OFF, switch it to ON, go back to Setting, and the incline
should return to zero.

**With GR Mode ON:**

- The incline is at zero when the treadmill is powered on.
- The incline goes to zero when the user pauses during a workout.
- The incline goes to zero at the end of a workout, on the SUMMARY page.
- The incline is at zero on the HOME page.
- Unplugging the SAFETY KEY does **not** return the incline to zero. Plugging it back in does.

**With GR Mode OFF:**

- The incline is not at zero when the treadmill is powered on.
- Pausing during a workout leaves the incline at the current level, not zero.
- The incline is not at zero at the end of a workout on the SUMMARY page.
- Pressing START begins at zero or at the current set level.
- Unplugging and replugging the SAFETY KEY leaves the incline off the basic level.

**EU machines.** The F65 manual of the same year notes that EU standards require grade return to be **off** for
machines sold in Europe.
