1. Take off the **motor cover**, then fold the treadmill.
2. Unplug the incline motor wires and unmount the incline motor from the treadmill.
3. **Adjust the spare incline motor to lower (195mm).**
4. Install the incline motor back with a 14mm open-end wrench.
5. Plug the incline motor wires into the controller.

**Calibrate afterwards.** An incline motor that has not been zeroed to 195mm and calibrated raises
**E3**.

**The zeroing length differs between the 2023 treadmills.** The F65 manual and the F85 and F89 front motor
sections all print 225mm; the F80 manual prints 195mm. Use the figure from the manual for the machine in front of
you.
