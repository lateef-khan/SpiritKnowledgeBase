1. Remove both **adjustment base cover screws** with a screwdriver, then take the covers off.
2. Remove the **2 rear roller locking bolts** with an **M6 L-Allen wrench**, then take off the rear roller.
3. Remove the **drive motor cover**, then unmount the drive belt from the front roller.
4. Remove the **front roller locking bolt** with a **13mm wrench**, then take off the front roller.
5. **Adjust the running belt to the centre** when the front or rear roller goes back on. This step is necessary.
