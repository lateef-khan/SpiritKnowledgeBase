**"This product requires a 9V, 1000mA adaptor."** That one line, printed at the foot of the body-fat page, is the
only adapter rating in any of the thirty Xterra bike, elliptical and stepper books - the other fifteen
adapter-powered machines say "external power supply" and nothing more
(`xterra-specs-external-power-adapter-with-no-rating-printed`). Polarity and plug size are not printed.

The Power page says where it goes: "The power supply must be plugged into the power jack, located on the front of
the unit near the stabilizer tube", then into the wall outlet; unplug it when the machine is not in use, let a cold
machine warm to room temperature before plugging in, and do not use a damaged supply, plug or cord. The same note
adds that the computer enters power-save mode after 4 minutes without pedalling, keeps the user settings, and that
an abnormal display is cleared by unplugging and re-plugging the adaptor.
