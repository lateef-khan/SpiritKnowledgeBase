The book prints the model as **FS3.0**. Steps 1 to 3 are the pack of the 2019 and 2020 front-drive books (the linked card): step 1 - #43 button head bolt (4), #44 flat washer (8), #45 curved washer (1), #46 split washer (9), #53 M5 x 12 Phillips screw (4), #102 socket head cap bolt (9); step 2 - #150 (1), #100 (1), #46 (1), #55 nyloc nut (2), #56 (2); step 3 - #150 (1), #100 (1), #52 sheet metal screw (4), #55 (2), #56 (2), #46 (1).

**Step 4 differs:**

| # | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 29 | 1/2" x 70 mm Carriage Bolt | 2 |
| 30 | 1/2" Nyloc Nut | 2 |
| 44 | 5/16" Flat Washer | 2 |
| 53 | **M5 x 12 mm Phillips Head Screw** | **2** |
| 46 | 5/16" Split Washer | 2 |
| 52 | 5 x 12 mm Sheet Metal Screw | 2 |
| 98 | 5/16" x 1" Button Head Socket Bolt | 2 |

The two M5 screws (53) fasten the rear cover (125) on each pedal arm, a part the 2019 and 2020 books do not have.

**Tools**: #68 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (2 pcs), #69 19 mm Wrench, #103 "L Wrench" (the L Allen wrench of the other books).
