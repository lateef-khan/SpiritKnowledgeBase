1. Press **START** in the main menu to start a workout in manual mode, or press **MODE** to
   enter the mode list.
2. Press the up/down arrows to select **Manual** and press MODE to enter.
3. Use the arrows to preset **TIME, DISTANCE, CALORIES, PULSE**, pressing MODE to confirm each.
4. Press **START/STOP** to start; the arrows adjust the load level.
5. Press START/STOP to pause. Press **RESET** to return to the main menu.

Presets count down (time in 1-minute segments to 99:00, distance in 0.1 steps to 99.90,
calories in 10-calorie steps to 990) and the console beeps when the pulse target is reached.
