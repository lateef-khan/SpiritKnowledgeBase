Test body fat with the computer in STOP mode:

- **Step I** - make sure the personal data (age, gender, height, weight) has been input
  accurately.
- **Step II** - press the **BODY FAT** button and hold the handgrips.
- **Step III** - "- - - - - - - -" shows while testing for **8 seconds**; then the readouts
  alternate between **BODY FAT percentage and BMI**. You may instead see **E-1** (the grips
  did not get a consistent signal - try again) or **E-4** (the body fat exceeds the limits of
  the program); those codes are on their own error cards.

Press BODY FAT again to resume where you left off. "All the BMI & BODY FAT figures are
estimates for your health. These figures are not for medical purposes, or treatment."

**Scales as printed:**

| B.M.I. scale | LOW | LOW/MED | MEDIUM | MED/HIGH |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Range | < 20 | 20 - 24 | 24.1 - 26.5 | 26.5 |

| Body fat | LOW | LOW/MED | MEDIUM | MED/HIGH |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Symbol | - | + | triangle | * |
| Male | < 13% | 23% - 25.9% | 26% - 30% | > 30% |
| Female | < 23% | 23% - 35.9% | 36% - 40% | > 40% |

The male row is printed with a gap - nothing between 13% and 23% - and the MED/HIGH BMI
column prints "26.5" without a greater-than sign; they are reproduced as printed.
