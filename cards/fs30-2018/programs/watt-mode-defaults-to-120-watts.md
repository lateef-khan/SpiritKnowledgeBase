1. Use the up/down arrows to select the workout program; choose **WATT** and press MODE.
2. Press the arrows to preset the **WATT target (default: 120)**.
3. Use the arrows to preset the workout **TIME**.
4. Press **START/STOP** to start or stop; **RESET** returns to the main menu.

The book prints no watt range and does not describe how the load follows your cadence. The
SB2.5r-family watt program (40-250 W, default 100) and the SB250-family one (default 100)
are on their own cards.
