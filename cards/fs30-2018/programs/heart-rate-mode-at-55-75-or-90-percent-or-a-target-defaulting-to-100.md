1. Use the up/down arrows to select the workout program; choose **H.R.** and press MODE.
2. Press the arrows to select **55%, 75%, 90% or TAG (TARGET H.R.)** - the target defaults
   to **100**.
3. Use the arrows to preset the workout **TIME**.
4. Press **START/STOP** to start or stop; **RESET** returns to the main menu.

The book prints no formula for the percentages and does not say how or how often the load is
adjusted; its "Heart Rate" page gives 220 minus age as the maximum heart rate. Hold the
pulse grips for a reading. The SB600 offers the same three percentages plus a target; see
`see_also`.
