These are the callout numbers on the exploded view drawing. The drawing is a
picture with bare numbers on it and no text, so the table below is the only
readable form of it.

**208 rows are printed.** The whole item numbers that appear are 1-18, 20-38, 40-53, 55-59, 61-74, 77-81, 83-87, 89-91, 93-97, 99-126, 129-134, 136-167, 169-172, 174-179, 181-190, 192-196, 198-201, 203-208, 212-213. Any number outside that run is **not printed in this manual at all** - 19, 39, 54, 60, 75, 76, 82, 88, 92, 98, 127, 128, 135, 168, 173, 180, 191, 197, 202, 209, 210, 211 are absent, not a missing page.

## What changed from the 2022 CE850 list

Both books print **exactly 208 rows with the same numbering**, so a number looks
valid against either. **Five rows changed:**

| Item | CE850-2022 | CE850-2024 |
|---|---|---|
| 178 | Ø8.5 x Ø26 x 2.0T flat washer, **qty 10** | same washer, **qty 14** |
| 201 | `Open End Combination Spanner Wrench` | `14/17m/m_Wrench` |
| 208 | Ø5/16" x Ø23 x 3T flat washer, **qty 8** | same washer, **qty 4** |
| 212 | **Water** Bottle Holder Clamp | **Drink** Bottle Holder Clamp |
| 213 | **Water** Bottle Holder | **Drink** Bottle Holder |

Every other row is the same part at the same quantity.

## What tells this list apart from the CE800-2024 list

The CE850 is the powered-stride, powered-incline machine and it is not
generator-braked:

- **Item 56 is `Gear Motor`, item 73 `Incline Motor`, item 64 `Incline
  Controller` and item 65 `Adaptor`.** Item 59 is a `Power Cord`.
- Items 52 and 53 are the **handgrip resistance labels, one marked STRIDE and one
  marked LEVEL** - the two controls on the left swing arm.
- **The CE800-2024 list instead has a `Generator/Brake Controller` at item 31**
  and no motor of any kind.
- The two lists share 108 item numbers and agree on five. Never read one against
  the other.

**The whole console is item 55 with sixteen sub-items, 55-1 to 55-16.** The
sub-items are printed with a tilde in the manual (`55~1`); they are written with
a hyphen here so the numbers sort. Item 55-16 is printed `CASFE Board` - a
transposition of CSAFE that the book does not correct.

**Items 61 and 63 are printed with their names broken across a line.** Item 61
reads `450m/m_Handle Wire (Upper), Resis-` then `tance(White)` on the next line,
and item 63 reads `900m/m_Handle Wire (Lower), Resistance/In-` then `cline`.
Those are line-break hyphens in the printed table, and the table below rejoins
them; there is no part called "In-cline" and none called "Resis-tance".

**No flywheel weight, stride figure or motor rating is printed against any row.**
The 18-to-24-inch stride is stated in the console chapter, not here - see
`spirit-ce850-specs-stride-length-18-to-24-inches`.
