**No. The 2024 CE850 has no Constant Power program.** A loose search of the whole owner's manual for
"watt", "watts" and "constant power" returns nothing in the programs chapter, and the page where the
2024 CE800 prints the nine-step Constant Power procedure carries the second half of the HIIT
procedure instead.

**It is the only machine in the 2024 range whose LED console drops it.** The 2024 CE800, CR800 and
CU800 all print the Constant Power procedure, and the 2024 CE800ENT and CR800ENT carry it on the
touchscreen under the name **Watts**.

**The 2020 and 2022 CE850 manuals did not have it either**, so this is not a 2024 removal - the
CE850 has never carried it. The CE850's programs chapter otherwise tracks the CE800's almost exactly:
measured on native PDF text the two 2024 chapters agree to **93.9%**, and the Constant Power page
against the extra HIIT steps is the whole of the difference.

For the machines that do have it, see `spirit-ce-programs-constant-power-watt` and
`spirit-800ent-2024-programs-watts-is-faster-pedalling-or-a-higher-level`.
