The manual folds three conditions into one block of causes.

- UP/DOWN button of STRIDE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH can't be used.
- Stride button just can press UP, can't press DOWN.
- Stride button just can press DOWN, can't press UP.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The connector of STRIDE CABLE and CONSOLE not connected properly | Connect the wires again |
| The connector of STRIDE CABLE and STRIDE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/CABLE not connected properly | Connect the wires again |
| The connector of STRIDE CABLE or STRIDE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH CABLE got damage | Replace the cable |
| Button of STRIDE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH is broken | Replace buttons |
| The connector of STRIDE CABLE or STRIDE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH CABLE got damage | Replace the cable |
| The connector of STRIDE CABLE or STRIDE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH CABLE damaged | Replace the cable |

**The last three reasons are the same sentence printed three times**, and the manual gives no way to
tell them apart. The same defect is in the CT850 treadmill manuals' speed and incline versions of
this row, which is where this table was copied from
(`ct850-2016-speed-buttons-not-working`, `ct850-2016-incline-buttons-not-working`).

This row is about the **buttons**. If the stride moves but the number is wrong, or the window shows
dashes, see `ce850-2024-errors-stride-err-position-sensor-wrong`.

**No other 2024 New Black manual carries this row.** The CE800 2024 and CE800ENT 2024 ellipticals
have no adjustable stride and no such table entry.

**Three service manuals print this block word for word, triple-printed reason and all** - the CE850 2016 (XE898-SE011), the XE895 2016 (the same book under the residential name) and the CE850 (2020). The XE395 2016 and XE395ENT 2021 print the same six causes about their **incline** buttons and cable: `spirit-xe395-errors-incline-adjustment-buttons-not-working`. Sole's E95S 2016 prints the stride version (`e95s-2016-stride-buttons-not-working`).
