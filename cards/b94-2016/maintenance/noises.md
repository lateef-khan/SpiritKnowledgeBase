Most noise comes from **loose screws**. Sometimes a part has deformed or shifted and is rubbing or moving roughly.

1. **Noise from the left and right pedals** is normally pedal wear. Replace worn pedals (45)(46).
2. **Left and right crank.** A loose crank makes noise and rough movement. Tightening the crank fixes it.
3. **Chain covers.** Most cases are the **chain covers (36, 37)** deforming so the crank arms (16L, 16R) scrape them. The covers deform because safety rules require a minimum gap. Replace the chain covers. In some cases a deformed **drive pulley (15)** rubs the right chain cover (37); replace the drive pulley (15).
4. If there is still noise with the left and right shrouds off, find where the noise comes from and replace that part.

The manual's closing note calls this an elliptical manual. It is a bike manual. Dyaco technical service backs up whatever the manual does not cover.
