**450 Newton for a belt in service. 540 Newton for a brand new belt.**

The manual gives both halves in section 9-8:

- Step 8 says to tighten the **1/4" x 8T nyloc nut (130)** with an 11 mm open end wrench until a sound wave tension gauge reads **450 N**.
- The Remark on the next page says: **for a brand new drive belt (14) the tension must read 540 N**, because of the elasticity of the belt. The tension drops over time.

**Section 9-12, the belt slipping answer, gives only the 450 N figure.** If you fit a new belt to 450 N it will be slack once it settles.
