1. Take the **computer cable (29)** and the **handlebar (3)** apart.
2. Press the **chain cover L (36)** so a seam opens between it and the **console mast cover (38)**, then take the console mast cover (38) off the chain cover L (36). Do the same on the right side, then lift the console mast cover (38) upward and off.
3. Use a **12 mm wrench** to release **seven 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts (51)**, **six 5/16" x 18 mm x 1.5T flat washers (72)** and **one 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T curved washer (99)**.
4. To refit, guide the **computer cable (29)** through the **console mast (2)** and out of the console mounting plate, then put the console mast (2) onto the **mainframe (1)**. Use the 12 mm wrench to tighten the seven hex head bolts (51), six flat washers (72) and one curved washer (99), then refit the console mast cover (38) and the **chain covers (36, 37)**.

Step 3 in the manual says the handlebar can then be released. The part being released is the console mast (2).
