1. Remove the **chain cover L (36)**.
2. Remove the **steel cable (98)** from the **flywheel (20)**. **Release the aluminium plate gently. It deforms easily and then makes noise.**
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **two M5 x 12 m/m Phillips head screws (58)** on the **gear motor (21)** and take the gear motor (21) apart.
4. Release the steel cable (98) from the gear motor (21) and take it off.
5. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **M5 x 12 m/m Phillips head screw (58)** and take the **reed switch (23)** apart.
6. **Setting the cable range.** After fitting the steel cable (98), use the **console (34)** to turn the tension to maximum, then adjust the steel cable (98) so the exposed cable is tight. The aluminium plate sits at its lowest position at maximum tension. Tighten the adjusting screw with two **8 mm open end wrenches**.
