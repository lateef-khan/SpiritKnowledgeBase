1. Remove the **chain covers L and R (36)(37)**.
2. Use an **11 mm open end wrench** to release the **1/4" x 8T nyloc nut (130)** to the end, then use a **6 mm Allen wrench** to remove the **M10 x P1.25 x 15L button head socket bolt (127)** and take the **bearing housing (10)** apart.
3. Take the **steel cable (98)** off the **flywheel (20)**. **Release the aluminium plate gently. It deforms easily and then rubs and makes noise.**
4. Take the **drive belt (14)** off and cut the **DC power cord (31)** tie. Use a **12 mm open end wrench** to remove **four 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts (51)** with **four 5/16" x 18 mm x 1.5T flat washers (72)** and take the **front linkage tube (9)** apart.
5. Use a **15 mm open end wrench** to release **two 3/8"-UNF26 nuts (91)** and remove the flywheel (20) together with the drive belt (14).
6. To refit, secure the bearing housing (10) on the main frame (1) with the M10 x P1.25 x 15L button head socket bolt. Make sure the **nut stopper (129)** is secure on the main frame (1).
7. Return the drive belt (14) onto the flywheel (20) and back to the main frame (1). Line the flywheel (20) up with the **drive pulley (15)**. Turn the **3/8"-UNF26 x 4T nut (90)** by the drive pulley outward until it touches the main frame (1) and turn the other **3/8"-UNF26 nut (91)** inward. Use **15 mm and 17 mm** open end wrenches to tighten both. Do the same for the **3/8"-UNF26 x 3T nut (89)** and the 3/8"-UNF26 nut (91) on the other side.
8. Return the drive belt (14) to the drive pulley (15) and the flywheel (20). Turn the drive pulley (15) to centre the belt. If it is not centred, loosen the 3/8"-UNF26 nut (91) on the flywheel (20) and adjust nuts (89)(90). Use the **11 mm open end wrench** to tighten the **1/4" x 8T nyloc nut (130)** until the belt is tight against the bearing housing (10). Use a sound wave tension gauge to reach **450 N**. Turn the drive pulley (15) to check the belt is centred and does not come off. Return the steel cable (98) to the flywheel (20).

**A new drive belt is set to 540 N, not 450 N.** See the belt tension card.
