1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **four M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screws (58)**.
2. Disconnect the **computer cable (29)** and both **hand pulse sensor cables**, then take the **console (34)** off.
3. Reverse the steps to refit the console (34).
