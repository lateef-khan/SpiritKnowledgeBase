1. Take the pedals and the crank arm apart. Separate the console mast cover and the saddle cover from the left and right shrouds. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **seven 3.5 x 16 m/m sheet metal screws (63)** and **three 5 x 16 m/m tapping screws (61)** holding the left shroud, then take the left shroud off.
2. Use a **15 mm open end wrench** to remove the **DC power cord (31)** from the **AC on/off switch bracket (124)**.
3. Remove **three 5 x 16 m/m tapping screws (61)** to take the **chain cover R** apart.
4. To refit, put the **chain cover R (37)** back on the **main frame (1)**, use the 15 mm open end wrench to secure the DC power cord (31) on the AC on/off switch bracket (124), then refit both **chain cover L (36)** and chain cover R (37) with **seven 3.5 x 16 m/m sheet metal screws**. Refit the **console mast cover (38)** with **six 5 x 16 m/m tapping screws (61)** on the mainframe. Finally refit the **crank arms L and R (16)** with their crank arm end caps (96).
5. **Watch the on/off switch bracket (124) when you fit the left front chain cover (36).** The bracket attaches to both chain covers L and R (36) and (37). Its top and bottom go inside both covers, and its left and right sides go outside both covers.
