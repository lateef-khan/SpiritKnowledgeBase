1. Use a **14 mm wrench** to loosen both hex head bolts and remove the **seat (19)**.
2. Turn the **brake tension knob (86)** off the **fix plate (8)**, then remove the **sliding seat mount (7)**.
3. To refit, install the sliding seat mount (7) on the **seat slider (6)**, then put the fix plate (8) on it. Install the brake tension knob (86) from the bottom upward into the fix plate (8) and tighten with the **3/8" x 7T nut (78)**. Finally insert the seat (19) into the sliding tube and tighten both hex head bolts with the 14 mm wrench.
