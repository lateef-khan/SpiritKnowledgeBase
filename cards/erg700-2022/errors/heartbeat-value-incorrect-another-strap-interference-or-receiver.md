**A wrong number, not no number.** No reading at all is the next row (`erg700-2022-errors-wireless-heartbeat-no-effect-strap-worn-within-3-feet-cr2032`), and a reading that only holds close to the console is the one after (`erg700-2022-errors-wireless-heartbeat-reception-too-short-cr2032`).

| Cause | Solution |
|---|---|
| May be received another heart rate strap signal | Check that there is no other heart rate strap around the machine being used |
| There may be other electric field noise interference | Try changing the machine direction or position |
| The wireless heartbeat receiver is damaged | Replace the new wireless heartbeat receiver |

The only part on the list is the receiver, and it is last. The same book's chest-strap page gives the interference sources in more detail - microwaves, fluorescent lights, security systems, a pet fence, another transmitter within 3 feet, and turning the whole machine 90 degrees - and that is a console-page matter; this row is the technician's short form of it. The Xterra treadmill service manuals print the same three causes for their consoles (`xterra-treadmill-errors-erratic-pulse-display-chest-belt-interference-receiver`).

The Spirit CRW800 and XRW600 (`crw800-2024-errors-heartbeat-value-incorrect`) and the Sole SR500 (`sr500-2016-heart-rate-value-incorrect`) print this row word for word for their own rowers.
