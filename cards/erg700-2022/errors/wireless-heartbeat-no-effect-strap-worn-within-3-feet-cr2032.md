**No reading at all.** The condition is printed as *Wireless heartbeat has no effect (The heartbeat value is not displayed on the monitor)*.

| Cause | Solution |
|---|---|
| The Heart rate strap is not properly worn | Check that the Heart rate strap is in proper contact with the skin and in the right direction |
| The distance is too far above the receiver | Keep the chest strap within **3 feet** of the console |
| The battery of heart rate strap is low or has no voltage | Replace the new lithium battery **CR2032** |

**Two figures, and they are this book's**: a 3-foot working range and a CR2032 cell. The Xterra treadmills print the same two for their chest belts (`xterra-treadmill-errors-wireless-pulse-not-working-chest-belt-3-feet-cr2032`), so they are the Dyaco strap's figures rather than a rower's - but the water rowers' books print no battery and no range for a strap at all, and the ERG550W service manual answers the same question with only a frequency (`erg550w-2023-errors-console-cannot-get-pulse-data-5-0-khz-chest-belt`).

A strap that reads only when it is very close to the console is the next row down and has a single cause, the same battery (`erg700-2022-errors-wireless-heartbeat-reception-too-short-cr2032`); a strap that reads the wrong number is a different row again (`erg700-2022-errors-heartbeat-value-incorrect-another-strap-interference-or-receiver`).

The Spirit CRW800 and XRW600 (`crw800-2024-errors-wireless-heartbeat-has-no-effect`) and the Sole SR500 (`sr500-2016-wireless-heart-rate-no-reading`) print this row with the same figures for their own rowers.
