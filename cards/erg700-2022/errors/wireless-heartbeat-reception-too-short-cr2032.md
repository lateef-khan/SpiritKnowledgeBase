**A reading that holds only close to the console - not no reading, and not a wrong one.**

The condition is printed as *Wireless heartbeat reception is too short (must be very close to the console)*:

| Cause | Solution |
|---|---|
| The battery is low | Replace the new lithium battery **CR2032** |

One cause, one part. A strap that drops out as you slide back on the rail is this row, and it is the strap's battery rather than the console's receiver; the receiver is only condemned when the number is wrong with a fresh cell (`erg700-2022-errors-heartbeat-value-incorrect-another-strap-interference-or-receiver`). The full no-reading list, including the 3-foot range, is the row above (`erg700-2022-errors-wireless-heartbeat-no-effect-strap-worn-within-3-feet-cr2032`).

The Spirit CRW800 and XRW600 print the row word for word (`crw800-2024-errors-wireless-heartbeat-reception-too-short`).
