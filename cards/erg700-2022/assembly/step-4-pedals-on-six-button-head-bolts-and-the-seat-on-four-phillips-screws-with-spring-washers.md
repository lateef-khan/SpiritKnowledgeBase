1. Use the **ALLEN WRENCH & SCREW DRIVER (127)** to tighten **6 BOLTS (129)** and **6 WASHERS (121)** to install the **LEFT AND RIGHT PEDALS (4, 5)** on the **MAIN FRAME (1)** - three bolts per pedal.
2. Use the same tool to tighten **4 SCREWS (124)** with **4 WASHERS (125)** to install the **SEAT (49)** onto the **SEAT ATTACHING BOARD (16)**.

The hardware box names them: 129 button head socket bolts, 121 flat washers, 124 Phillips head screws, 125 spring washers. This is the last assembly step; the console then takes its external power supply (linked card).
