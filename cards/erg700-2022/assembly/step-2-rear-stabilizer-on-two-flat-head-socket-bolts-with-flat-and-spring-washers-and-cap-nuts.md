Use the **WRENCH (139)** to tighten **2 BOLTS (135)** through where the **ALUMINUM TRACK (51)** meets the **REAR STABILIZER (3)** and secure with **2 WASHERS (109), 2 WASHERS (137) and 2 NUTS (136)**.

The hardware box names them: 135 flat head socket bolts, 109 flat washers, 137 spring washers, 136 cap nuts. Bolt through, flat washer, spring washer, cap nut.
