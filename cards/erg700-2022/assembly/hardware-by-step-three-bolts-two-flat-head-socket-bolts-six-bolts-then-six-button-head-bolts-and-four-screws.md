This book has no pack-checklist page; each step prints its own hardware box. No thread sizes are printed anywhere.

| Step | # | Item | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 109 | Flat washer | 3 |
| 1 | 120 | Bolt | 3 |
| 2 | 135 | Flat head socket bolt | 2 |
| 2 | 109 | Flat washer | 2 |
| 2 | 137 | Spring washer | 2 |
| 2 | 136 | Cap nut | 2 |
| 3 | 116 | Split washer | 6 |
| 3 | 123 | Bolt | 6 |
| 4 | 121 | Flat washer | 6 |
| 4 | 129 | Button head socket bolt | 6 |
| 4 | 124 | Phillips head screw | 4 |
| 4 | 125 | Spring washer | 4 |

**Tools named in the steps:** WRENCH (130) in step 1, WRENCH (139) in step 2, ALLEN WRENCH (130) in step 3, ALLEN WRENCH & SCREW DRIVER (127) in step 4. Item 130 is called a wrench in one step and an Allen wrench in the next; no tools page settles it.

The Spirit CRW800 (2024) is this rower's twin with different figures - four hex bolts at the front, five at the folding end - so its hardware card is linked as a look-alike, not a match.
