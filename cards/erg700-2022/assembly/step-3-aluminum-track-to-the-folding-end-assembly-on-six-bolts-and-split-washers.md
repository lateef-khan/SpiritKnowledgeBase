Attach the other end of the **ALUMINUM TRACK (51)** to the **FOLDING END ASSEMBLY (6)** of the Main Frame (1) and use the **ALLEN WRENCH (130)** to tighten the **6 BOLTS (123)** together with **6 WASHERS (116)** - the hardware box calls 116 split washers.

The folding end assembly is the hinge that lets the track fold up for storage (the Folding/Unfolding page, held by the maintenance section, presses the Release Lever (12) to fold it).
