- **Waking.** Pressing any key on a console that has turned off lights the **full display**, the buzzer **beeps for 2 seconds**, and the console enters **idle mode**. That is normal, not a fault.
- **Starting without a key.** When the speed sensor detects a stroke the console goes straight into **Manual** workout mode.
- **Switching off.** "In any mode during turning on, if there is not any button activated or no rowing activity lasts for **1 minute**, it turns off automatically." The book names only idle and off - no sleep state - and does not say whether the figures survive a wake.

The console runs from an **external power supply** plugged into the jack on the unit (the book's Power page: unplug it when not in use, and let a cold machine reach room temperature before plugging in, or the display or electronics may be damaged); its troubleshooting table names the supply as AC 100-240 V in, DC 12 V out, which is an errors card. There are no batteries. RESET held 3 seconds "turns on the console again" ([key card](five-keys-and-the-eleven-mode-order-behind-the-mode-key.md)). One minute is this console's figure; the water rowers sleep after about 4 minutes ([ERG600W card](../../erg600w-2021/console/power-up-sets-the-clock-calendar-and-alarm-then-the-sport-screen.md)). The Spirit XRW600 prints this page word for word ([Spirit twin](../../shared/console/xrw600-console-power-off-after-one-minute.md)).
