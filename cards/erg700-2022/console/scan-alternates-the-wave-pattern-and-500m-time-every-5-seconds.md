**500M/TIME appears only in the Manual, Distance, Time and Calorie modes.** In Manual, as the book's example: when the console starts the matrix in the middle of the LCD shows the **wave pattern** (the stroke speed), switches to **500M/TIME 00:00** across the centre after **5 seconds**, back to the wave after another 5, and repeats - "this is the function of SCAN". The console goes straight into SCAN after start.

**Press MODE** and the matrix holds the wave pattern only; press it again for 500M/TIME; press it again and SCAN resumes - the three states rotate.

The book never says what 500M/TIME measures - it prints no definition and no units beyond the 00:00 example - so do not tell a customer it is a 500 m split; the water rowers define their TIME/500M as the time to row 500 metres at the current pace ([water rower card](../../shared/console/xterra-ergw-console-readouts-time-500m-distance-to-99900-m-strokes-and-watts.md)). Keys: [key card](five-keys-and-the-eleven-mode-order-behind-the-mode-key.md); windows: [window card](window-ranges-with-watts-to-2000-as-a-decimal-and-level-1-to-16-in-dots.md).
