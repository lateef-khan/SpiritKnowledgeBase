**This is the ERG700's lever-and-knob fold. The ERG220 and ERG500 fold on a ball head pin**
(`xterra-erg-maintenance-folding-on-the-ball-head-pin-pull-it-raise-the-rail-and-reinsert-it`).

## Folding / Unfolding Procedures, as printed

1. **Hold the Handlebar (37) to lift the rower slightly and press the Release Lever (12).**
2. **Lift and fold the Aluminum Track until it locks into place.**
3. To fold the Rear Stabilizer, **turn Knob (47) counterclockwise until loosened. Pull Knob to release and fold
   Rear Stabilizer.**
4. To unfold the rower, reverse the steps.

In the drawing the release lever (12) is at the front end of the track where it meets the frame, beside the
handlebar (37); the knob (47) is on the rear stabilizer where it meets the track. The track latches itself when folded ("until it locks into place") - no
pin to fit - and the rear stabilizer folds against the track so the stored footprint is the main frame alone.

## Moving Procedures, on the same page

1. Fold the aluminum rail assembly up.
2. **Lift the rear and roll away.** The drawing shows the folded machine tipped forward onto the wheels of the
   front stabilizer - the same lift-the-rear method as every other Xterra rower
   (`xterra-rower-maintenance-moving-lift-the-rear-until-the-front-transport-wheels-touch-the-floor`).

This page is the whole storage chapter of the ERG700 book: no caution about the seat sliding, no note on how
long it may stay folded.

Spirit's XRW600 air rower prints the same two panels for its own machine
(`spirit-xrw600-assembly-folding-and-moving`). Other brand, separate card.
