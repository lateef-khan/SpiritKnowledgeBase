**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the Manufacturer's Limited Warranty page of the ERG700 owner's manual (PDF p. 27, printed 24), *Effective January 31, 2022 - ROWER LIMITED WARRANTY*. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**Five years of parts is the longest parts term on any Xterra rower page** - the next longest is two years (ERG500, ERG600W, ERG650W, ERG750W: `xterra-rower-warranty-periods-frame-5-years-parts-2-years-labor-90-days`, `sb600-2023-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`, `erg750w-2025-warranty-periods-frame-5-years-parts-2-years-labor-none`) - and it is the only rower page that pairs a lifetime frame with five years of parts; the ERG650W pairs it with two (`sb600-2023-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`). One year of labor is the longest labor term printed for a rower.

The rest of the page is the older layout: five consumer duties starting with a registration card back in ten days (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-with-a-registration-card`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover`), the nine exclusions (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-store-display-and-unregistered-products`), six service steps with a trip charge (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-service-steps-dealer-service-organization-then-the-service-department`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-owner-pays-freight-parts-shipping-and-trip-charges`), residential use only (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-use-environment-residential-only-any-other-use-voids-it`) - with an ATTENTION box on the contents page. The book is stamped *ERG700_VER6_20220131* under a *(c)2020* back cover; the effective date agrees with the stamp.
