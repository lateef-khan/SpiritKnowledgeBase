**Every interval mode runs 10 Exercise/Rest cycles.**

- **20/10 INTERVAL** - 20 seconds (Exercise) / 10 seconds (Rest). Press Start/Stop to begin.
- **10/20 INTERVAL** - 10 seconds (Exercise) / 20 seconds (Rest). Press Start/Stop to begin.
- **CUSTOM INTERVAL** - user-defined Exercise time / Rest time; **default 10 seconds / 10 seconds,
  adjusted in 1-second steps**. The value at the **left** of the matrix window flashes for the
  exercise time: set it with UP/DOWN, press Start/Stop. The value at the **right** flashes for the
  rest time: set it the same way.

The centre of the LCD shows the **count-down of the current work segment with a wave**, or the
**rest count-down with a mark**. Pressing UP or DOWN adjusts the resistance level. When the workout
completes the buzzer sounds a long beep; **if rowing continues, the count-down repeats.**

**Not the ERG800W's intervals** - that console's second fixed interval is 20/30, its custom interval
runs 5 to 599 seconds, and its cycle count is settable from 1 to 99. **Not the air bike's Tabata** -
the AIR650 runs eight fixed intervals.

**Same page on Spirit and Sole machines.** The Spirit CRW800 and XRW600 and the Sole SR500 rowers print this chapter word for word; those are other brands and other cards - see `see_also`.
