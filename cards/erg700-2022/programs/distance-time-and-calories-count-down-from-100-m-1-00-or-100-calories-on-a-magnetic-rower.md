All three are count-down modes. Choose the mode with MODE, use **UP/DOWN** to set the goal, then
press **Start/Stop** to confirm the setting and start the workout.

| Mode | Default | Increment | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Target Distance** | **100M** | **50M** up or down | not stated |
| **Target Time** | **1:00** | **1 minute** up or down | **99:00** |
| **Target Calorie** | **100** | **10** up or down | not stated |

The window for that mode counts down from the setting and shows the remaining amount. **When it
reaches 0 the workout completes and the buzzer sounds with a long beep. If rowing continues, the
count-down repeats.** In all three, pressing UP or DOWN adjusts the resistance level, and the centre
of the LCD scans every 5 seconds between the stroke wave and 500M/TIME (MODE overrides it) - see the
manual mode card.

*Slips as printed: the Target Time paragraph opens "The default **distance** is 1:00", and the
Calorie paragraph ends "If rowing continues, the **time** count-down repeats". The figures are as
tabled.*

**The water rowers set different targets** - the ERG800W from 20:00 / 1000 m / 150 cal with a
strokes program, the ERG750W with no default - see `not_to_be_confused_with`.

**Same page on Spirit and Sole machines.** The Spirit CRW800 and XRW600 and the Sole SR500 rowers print this chapter word for word; those are other brands and other cards - see `see_also`.
