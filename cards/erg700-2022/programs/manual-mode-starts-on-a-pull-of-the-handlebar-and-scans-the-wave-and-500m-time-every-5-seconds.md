Pressing **Start/Stop** begins the workout mode, or **pulling the handlebar under idle mode enters
directly into Manual workout mode**. Manual asks for nothing first - no age, weight or time.

The image at the centre of the LCD **scans every 5 seconds** between the **stroke speed as a wave
pattern** and **500M/TIME** (your split). Pressing MODE cancels the scanning and leaves the wave;
pressing MODE again shows 500M/TIME; pressing it again returns to SCAN. "Only workout modes of
Manual, Distance, Time and Calorie are with this display function."

**Pressing UP or DOWN adjusts the resistance level**, shown at the bottom right corner of the LEVEL
window; the level runs 1~16, lit as eight dots of two levels each.

Time accumulates to 99:59, distance to 9999 (then "1X.XX" format), calories to 999, watts to 2000
(over 999 shown as 1.00, 1.24, 2.00). The console turns off after 1 minute with no button and no
rowing; any key wakes it, and a speed signal takes it straight into Manual.

**Same page on Spirit and Sole machines.** The Spirit CRW800 and XRW600 and the Sole SR500 rowers print this chapter word for word; those are other brands and other cards - see `see_also`.
