**AC model: TT8 2019 ST928A-YT037, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2019 (ST928-YT035) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

1. Use an **M5 L Allen wrench** to loosen the **4 sheet metal screws** and remove the console mast cover.
2. Use an **M6 L Allen wrench** to remove the **4 button head socket bolts** from the console support.
3. Disconnect the console wiring and replace the console.
