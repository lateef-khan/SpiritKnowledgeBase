**AC model: TT8 2019 ST928A-YT037, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2019 (ST928-YT035) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

1. Follow the front and rear roller replacement procedure first.
2. Remove the left and right foot rail cap. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **6 screws** and the bracket from the back of the foot rail, then remove the foot rail in the direction of the arrows.
3. Use an **M5 L Allen wrench** to remove the **8 screws** securing the running board. Remove the running board and replace the running board or running belt. If the cushions need replacing, remove the **6 cushions** and replace them. Reassemble in the reverse order.
4. Use a **14 mm open end wrench** to adjust belt tension with a tension measuring device. **Adjust to the
   white LBS area, between 70-75 LBS.** Use a **14 mm T-shaped socket wrench** to secure and tighten the 4
   securing screws.
