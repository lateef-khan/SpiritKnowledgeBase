**AC model: TT8 2019 ST928A-YT037, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2019 (ST928-YT035) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

**This is E-50H, one code in the inverter set. It is not any of the E0 to E7 codes used by the
DC-motor TT8 2019.**

Printed description: "Error in communication between the console and inverter"

The console and the inverter are not talking. This is the cable **between** the two boxes.

Troubleshooting, in the manual's order:

1. Check whether the control wire is bad connection or damaged.
2. Replace the inverter.
3. Replace the console.
