**AC model: TT8 2019 ST928A-YT037, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2019 (ST928-YT035) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

From the troubleshooting matrix, in the manual's order:

1. The ON/OFF switch has not been switched on — switch the AC switch.
2. The safety key is in the wrong position — insert it correctly.
3. The computer cable is broken — check the wire and connect again.
4. The safety device is broken (open) — replace the safety key device.
5. Other components are faulty — replace the console.

**This list is shorter than the one in the DC-motor TT8 2019 (ST928-YT035) manual**, which also names
a 12-pin computer connector, a blown controller fuse and a blown varistor. Those steps are not printed for
this machine.

The service checklist adds four more things to check before calling it a defect: the tether cord is not in
position; the **circuit breaker on the front grill** has tripped (push it in until it locks); the plug is
disconnected from the 220 Vac wall outlet; or the breaker panel circuit breaker has tripped.
