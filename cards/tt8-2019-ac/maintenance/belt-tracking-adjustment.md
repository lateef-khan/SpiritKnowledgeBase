**AC model: TT8 2019 ST928A-YT037, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2019 (ST928-YT035) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

Performance depends on the frame sitting on a reasonably level surface. If the frame is not level the front
and back rollers cannot run parallel, and constant belt adjustment will be needed.

It is normal for some belts to drift near one side with nobody on them; after a few minutes of use the belt
should tend to centre itself. Adjust only if it keeps moving to one side **during use**.

1. A **10 mm Allen wrench** is provided to adjust the rear roller. **Make tracking adjustments from the left
   side only.**
2. Set the belt speed to about **3 to 5 kph**.
3. **Turn the bolt clockwise to move the belt to the right.** Turn only **1/4 turn** and wait a few minutes
   for the belt to settle.
4. Continue in 1/4 rotations until the belt stabilises in the centre of the running deck.

A small adjustment makes a dramatic difference. Proper belt tracking is an owner responsibility common to
all treadmills. Damage to the running belt resulting from improper tracking or tension adjustments is **not covered under the warranty**.

If the belt will not stay centred with nobody on it, the checklist notes the user may simply be favouring
one foot; where that gait is natural, track the belt slightly off-centre to the side **opposite** the belt
movement.
