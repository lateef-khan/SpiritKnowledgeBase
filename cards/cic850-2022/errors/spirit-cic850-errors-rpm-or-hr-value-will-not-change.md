1. Press the **`MODE` key** repeatedly to toggle between SPEED (SPD), DISTANCE (DIST), TIME
   (TIME) and CLOCK (CLK).
2. **Press and hold the `SET` key** repeatedly to clear a past measured value, or to go into a
   setting and leave it again.

**The manual's own answer is that the console is showing a different field, not that a sensor
has failed.** Cycle the display before looking at the transmitter.

**Only the CIC850 book prints this entry.** The XIC600 2018 and 2021 troubleshooting pages carry
four entries and this is not one of them, so do not offer a `SET` key fix to an XIC600 owner -
that manual names no `MODE` or `SET` key at all, only "the two keys on the front of the
console".

The Sole SB700-2020 and SB900-2020 service manuals print the same entry, filed there under
`section: console`: `sole-spinner-rpm-or-hr-does-not-change`. Different brand, separate card.
