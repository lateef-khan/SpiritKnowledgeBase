Section 9-2, `Noise Troubleshooting`:

> The most reason is the bolts do not tighten enough. Sometimes because parts shape change or shift then cause parts friction or operate unsmoothly.

1. **Flywheel friction on a brake block.** Turn the hex screw clockwise with the **M6 open-end wrench**. If the brake block (right) touches the flywheel, push the brake block (right) in the opposite direction **to keep the gap between the brake block (right) and the flywheel at 1.5 mm**. Check the brake block (left) the same way.
2. **Pedal noise.** Pedals are consumables; after a while their bearings wear and make noise. Replace the pedals.

**The figure is 1.5 mm each side**, and the CIC850 owner's manual does not print it - its noise advice stops at loose hardware. Setting the brake blocks is otherwise a maintenance matter (the brake knob and blocks are replaced in section 10-2 of the same book); the diagnosis here is that a scrape on the flywheel is a block that has drifted in.

A belt that slips is the belt-tension procedure on the page before it, already carded from the owner's manual as `cic850-2022-maintenance-belt-tension-behind-the-top-and-right-guards`. A console that is blank or shows no speed is the page after it: `cic850-2022-errors-display-blank-or-no-speed-five-checks`.

The Sole SB700 of 2009-2020 answers noise with one line - flywheel rubbing the leather or wool brake pad is normal - on `sole-spinner-noises`, a Sole card.
