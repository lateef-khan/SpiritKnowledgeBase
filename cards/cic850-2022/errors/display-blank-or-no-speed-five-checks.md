Section 9-3, `Display Blank or No Speed Displayed troubleshooting`, in full:

1. Check the battery of console and transmitter.
2. Check the sensor is at the right place to face magnet.
3. Check the transmitter have signal.
4. If the above items are normal, then adjust the transmitter position to face the console.
5. **Each transmitter and console are matched one to one.**

**Step 5 is the one the owner's-manual troubleshooting page does not say**: a transmitter from another bike will not drive this console until it is paired, and a console that shows `Err` at the end of the pair stage has not paired (`spirit-cic850-errors-err-transmitter-pairing-failed`). The owner's-manual rows that the same service manual reprints on its own page 20 - press any key, then both sets of batteries - are `spirit-indoor-cycle-errors-no-display-on-console`; a cadence figure that jumps because two bikes share a console code is `spirit-indoor-cycle-errors-cadence-jumps-console-code`.

The console (DT-3268F) and the speed transmitter each take their own batteries, and the wiring page of the same book shows the transmitter, its speed sensor and an `LR03` cell - the AAA size.

The Sole SB700 of 2009 prints a four-check version of this page without the pairing line: `sb700-2011-errors-display-blank-or-no-speed-four-checks`, a Sole card.
