1. Use an **M17 open-end wrench** to loosen the **nylon nut** and the combination wrench (C) to loosen **2 cross-head screws (M4 x 8)**, then remove the brake block.
2. Reverse the steps to refit.

That is the whole procedure. The book's noise answer adds the setting: if the brake block touches the flywheel, turn its hex screw clockwise with the M6 open-end wrench and push the block the other way to keep a **1.5 mm gap** between each brake block (right, then left) and the flywheel. That adjustment belongs to the maintenance section.
