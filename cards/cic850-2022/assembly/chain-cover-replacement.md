1. Remove the **2 guard (top) covers (No. 47)** on the **guard (top) (No. 46)**. Remove the **4 screws (No. 50)** and **2 screws (No. 51)**, then remove the guard (top) (No. 46). Remove the **7 screws (No. 50)** and remove the **right guard (No. 44)**. Remove the **5 screws (No. 50)** and remove the **left guard**.
2. Put the right guard back on and re-tighten its screws (No. 50); put the left guard back on and re-tighten its screws (No. 50); put the guard (top) (No. 46) back on and re-tighten the 4 screws (No. 50) and 2 screws (No. 51).

**One screw part number, No. 50, holds all three guards** (4 + 7 + 5), with two No. 51 screws on the top guard only. The screw sizes are not printed; the numbers are call-outs on the book's drawing.

The belt-tension adjustment (the linked maintenance card) removes the top guard and the right guard only, by the same screws.
