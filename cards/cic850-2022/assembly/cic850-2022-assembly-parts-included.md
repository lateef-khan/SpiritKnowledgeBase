## Three lines the CIC800 list does not have

**Tablet Holder**, **Dumbbell Holder** and **Console Bracket**. The CIC800 carton has
none of the three and no console assembly step at all. The CIC850 also renames the
CIC800's **Adjustment Handle** an **Adjustment Handlebar Knob**; it is the same star
pop-pin.

## The list undercounts the dumbbell holders

It says **1 Dumbbell Holder**. Assembly step six fits **two** - a left holder (113) and a
right holder (114) - one on each side of the seat slider. Expect two in the box.

**The console itself is not on the list** either, though step five fits a **DT-3268F**
console and the hardware table for that step names it. Only its bracket is counted here.
