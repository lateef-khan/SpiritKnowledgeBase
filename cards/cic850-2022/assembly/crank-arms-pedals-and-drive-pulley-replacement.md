**Pedals:** use a **15 mm open-end wrench** to take the pedals off. That is the whole pedal instruction; the direction is not printed here (the owner's manual says the left pedal is reverse-threaded).

**Crank and drive pulley:**

1. Take off the chain cover and the drive belt, following the flywheel, belt and drive pulley steps.
2. Loosen the **crank fixing screws** counterclockwise with **tool A**. Place the **screw (B)** on the front of **tool C** and fix it on the crank; turn tool C clockwise and pull the crank off together with the drive pulley.
3. Use an **H5 hexagon socket** and a torque wrench at **180 kgf-cm** to loosen the **5 screws (M8)** of the crank arm, then take the drive pulley off.
4. Fit the right crank and the left crank onto each side of the bottom bracket. **The right and left cranks must be set 180 degrees apart.**
5. Tighten the crank fixing screws clockwise with tool A and the torque wrench. **Set the torque wrench to 600 kgf/cm.**
6. Put the belt on the pulley and adjust it (115 to 120 Hz, the belt card).
7. Refit the right guard, left guard and guard top.

**Two torque figures in kilogram-force centimetres, printed as "kgf.cm" and "kgf/cm" on the same page:** 180 for the five M8 pulley screws (printed as the figure to loosen them at, step 3) and 600 for the crank fixing screw. 600 kgf-cm is about 59 N-m; 180 kgf-cm about 17.7 N-m. The book prints no N-m.

Tools A, B, C, D and E are the lettered tools in the photographs, whose captions are mostly Chinese: tool A is captioned as an **8 mm hex bit on a 12.5 mm (1/2") drive socket**, tool C is a **T-handle crank puller** with screw B threaded into its nose, and tool D is the **lock-nut wrench** used in the bottom-bracket procedure. The owner's-manual tool card names the four lettered tools in the carton.
