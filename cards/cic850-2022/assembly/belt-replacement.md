**Belt tension: 115 to 120 Hz.**

1. Remove the 2 guard (top) covers, the 4 screws and 2 screws, and the guard (top).
2. Remove the 7 screws and the right guard.
3. Take the drive belt off.
4. Put the rear end of the new belt into the **small pulley and idler arm on the flywheel**. Put the front end of the new belt onto the **pulley**, and **slowly turn the crank to walk the belt on**. The tension of the belt is **115 to 120 Hz**.

No refit step is printed for the guards; the chain-cover card has them. The tension is set with the screw (No. 17) and the two hex screws (No. 55) that the belt-tension maintenance card describes, and that card is where a slipping belt is adjusted without replacement.

Only the top and right guards come off for the belt; the left guard stays on (the flywheel assembly needs all three).
