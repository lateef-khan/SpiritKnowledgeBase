1. Remove the **decorative cover**, then loosen the **lock nut** counterclockwise with **tool D** and remove it.
2. Remove the **left cup** of the bottom bracket **clockwise** with **tool E**; remove the **right cup** **counterclockwise** with tool E; remove the main body of the bottom bracket.
3. Apply some **blue thread locker** to the frame's threads, left and right, as in the pictures. Tighten the bottom bracket counterclockwise with tool E.
4. **Set the torque wrench to 700 kgf/cm** and tighten the right cup with tool E and the torque wrench.
5. Set the torque wrench to **700 kgf/cm** and tighten the left cup with tool E and the torque wrench.
6. Set the torque wrench to **350 kgf/cm** and tighten the lock nut with tool D.

**The two cups thread opposite ways** - left cup off clockwise, right cup off counterclockwise - and step 3's "tighten the bottom bracket counterclockwise" is as printed. The cranks come off first (the crank card, 600 kgf-cm) to reach the bracket.

The torques are printed in kilogram-force centimetres ("kgf/cm"): 700 kgf-cm is about 68.6 N-m and 350 kgf-cm about 34.3 N-m. The book prints no N-m figures.
