1. Loosen the **star pop-pin**, then take the **seat post** off.
2. Loosen the **adjustment knob** and pull out the **safety pin**, then take the **sliding seat mount** off the seat post.
3. Use a **13 mm open-end wrench** to loosen the **seat nuts**, then take the seat off.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.

The handlebar post comes off the same way (its own card). The safety pin is the travel stop that keeps the sliding mount on the post; it has to be pulled before the mount will slide off.

The owner's manual prints no seat height or fore/aft procedure (the linked absence card); the service manual prints only this removal.
