Numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing item numbers; the letters A to D are the
supplied tools. Quantities are on
[the hardware card](cic850-2022-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Unpacking

Cut the straps, then cut along the dotted line on the bottom of the box; lift the box
over the unit and unpack. **The text says the hardware is separated into six steps; the
manual prints eight.** The identical sentence appears in the CIC800 book above five
steps. It is boilerplate, not a count.

## Step one - front stabilizer

Position the **FRONT STABILIZER (3)** on the frame bracket with the **transport wheels
facing up and toward the front**. Attach with 2 hex screws (65) and 2 flat washers (5).
Do not overtighten. Make sure the levelling feet with nut are fully screwed in.

## Step two - rear stabilizer

The **REAR STABILIZER (10)**, same two screws and two washers, same warning.

## Step three - handlebar post

Turn the **STAR POP-PIN (104)** clockwise into the frame with **wrench D**, then insert
the **HANDLEBAR POST (37)** into the frame tube and tighten firmly with the pop-pin.

## Step four - free the console screw

Remove the pre-installed **SCREW (137)** from the back of the console. It is used in step
five; this step does nothing else.

## Step five - console, bracket, bottle and tablet holders

1. Fit 2 AAA batteries in the **CONSOLE (110)**.
2. Place the **CONSOLE BRACKET (109)** on the back of the console and secure with the
   screw (137), tightened with **wrench C**.
3. Position the bracket on the **HANDLEBAR (30)**, aligning the screw holes of the
   **WATER BOTTLE HOLDER (105)**, the **TABLET HOLDER (106)** and the handlebar. Secure
   with 2 screws (103) and 2 flat washers (111), tightened with **L-hex wrench A**.
4. Pull out the **SAFETY PIN (35)** and slide the handlebar onto the **POST (37)**.

## Step six - dumbbell holders

Remove 3 screws (27) with 3 spring washers (28) from the seat slider assembly, attach the
**LEFT DUMBBELL HOLDER (113)** and refit the same three screws and washers with
**wrench C**. Repeat for the **RIGHT DUMBBELL HOLDER (114)**.

**The hardware table lists six of each; the step uses three per side.**

## Step seven - pedals

R and L on the spindles, grease if needed, R on the chain guard side clockwise, L
counter-clockwise. See
[the pedal card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-cycle-assembly-pedal-reverse-thread-no-torque-figure.md).

## Step eight - speed sensor battery

Loosen the **SCREW (119)** with wrench C, remove **LEFT CHAIN GUARD B (118)**, insert
the new battery on the sensor board, refit the guard and the screw. The console/
transmitter pairing procedure is printed on the same page and is a console fact.

## The book has no seat-position procedure

It goes straight from step eight to handlebar adjustment. See
[the absence card](cic850-2022-assembly-no-seat-position-procedure.md).
