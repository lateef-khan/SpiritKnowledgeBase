1. Remove the 2 guard (top) covers, the 4 screws and 2 screws, and the guard (top).
2. Remove the 7 screws and the right guard.
3. Remove the 5 screws and the left guard.
4. Use a **3 mm Allen wrench** to loosen **6 screws (M5 x 14)** of the **outer circle tube** and take the outer circle tube off.
5. Use a **4 mm Allen wrench** to loosen **3 screws (M6)** on each of the left and right sides of the **idler arm**, and take the **flywheel assembly** off.
6. Reverse the steps to refit.

Steps 1 to 3 are the chain-cover procedure. The belt has its own procedure with the tension figure (115 to 120 Hz); after the flywheel goes back the belt is refitted and set by that card.
