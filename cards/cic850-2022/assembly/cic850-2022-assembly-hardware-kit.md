## The cover part number is shared with the CIC800; the hardware is not

The CIC850 cover carries part number **800390**, which is the CIC800's. The hardware
tables do not follow it. The CIC800 hangs each stabilizer on **2 screws (3), 4 washers
(4) and 2 nylon nuts (5)**; the CIC850 uses **2 screws (65) and 2 washers (5) with no
nut at all**. Only the thread size, M8 x 1.25 x 55L, survives. Every other item number in
the two books differs, and the CIC850 has five steps the CIC800 does not.

**Do not order CIC850 hardware from a CIC800 parts list.**

## Rows that do not agree with their own step

- **Step 6** lists **6 screws (27) and 6 spring washers (28)**; the step fits **3 of
  each per dumbbell holder**, which is the same total read a different way.
- **Step 7** numbers the pedals **136L/R** while the drawing on the same page labels them
  **135/L** and **136/R**.
- **Step 8** gives screw **119** no type and no size.

## Steps 5 and 6 mix hardware with assemblies

Those two tables list the console, the handlebar, the holders and the post alongside the
fasteners. They are what the step touches, not what is loose in the hardware bag.
