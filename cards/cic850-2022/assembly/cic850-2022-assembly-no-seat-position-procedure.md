**The CIC850 manual has no seat height procedure and no seat fore/aft procedure.** The
book goes from assembly STEP EIGHT straight to HANDLE BAR ADJUSTMENT, then resistance,
brake and pedal straps. Its sister book, the CIC800, prints a seven-step seat height
procedure and a two-step fore/aft procedure on the page before the handlebar section.

**This looks like a dropped page rather than a machine without the adjustment.** The
CIC850's own labelled diagram calls out **SEAT HEIGHT ADJ.** and **SEAT FORWARD/AFT
ADJ.**, its INDOOR CYCLE SETUP page tells you how to change the saddle **pitch**, and
its carton holds a seat post with the same star pop-pin arrangement.

## What to use instead

The CIC800 procedure is on
[the shared card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-cycle-assembly-seat-height-and-fore-aft-position.md). It is
written for the CIC800's knobs and is not carded as covering the CIC850, because nothing
in the CIC850 book states it.

## How this absence was established

A loose-word search across the extraction for `hip height`, `SEAT HEIGHT ADJUST`,
`ADJUSTMENT OF SEAT` and `12 and 6` returned nothing, where the CIC800 extraction returns
all four. A page-by-page reading of the PDF confirms the chapter order above, and the one
near-blank page in the book (PDF p. 8) was rendered and read: it is the labelled parts
diagram, not a dropped seat page.
