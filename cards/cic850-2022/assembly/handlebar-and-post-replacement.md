1. Loosen the **star pop-pin**, then take the **handlebar post** off.
2. Loosen the **adjustment knob** and pull out the **safety pin**, then take the handlebar with its **adjustment handlebar block** off the handlebar post.
3. Use a **5 mm L-Allen wrench** to loosen **2 screws (M8 x 25L)**, then remove the handlebar and the adjustment handlebar block.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.

Same pop-pin and safety-pin sequence as the seat post; the two M8 x 25 screws are the only sized fasteners in the section. The owner's-manual card on handlebar height and reach (linked) is the adjustment; this is the removal.
