1. There is one hole on each of the left and right sides at the bottom of the brake knob: block one end and blow compressed air into the other with an air gun to pop the upper cover of the knob off.
2. Use the combination wrench (C) to loosen the **flat cross-head screw**, counterclockwise.
3. Take the brake knob off and put the new brake knob on the **brake rod**. **Turn the knob counterclockwise and the resistance returns to zero.**
4. Use the combination wrench (C) to tighten the flat cross-head screw, clockwise. Use a rubber hammer to press the upper cover of the knob back in - **note the direction of the sticker on the cover**.

**The knob cap comes off with air pressure, not a tool** - that is the only method the book gives. Tool (C) is the combination wrench from the assembly tool kit; the screw size is not printed.
