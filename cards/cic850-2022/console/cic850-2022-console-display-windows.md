**TIME.** Length of time in min/sec. It counts up or counts down while you pedal. **When pedalling
stops, the time stops counting up or down after 3 seconds.**

**CADENCE & SPEED.** Cadence is how fast the cranks are rotating in RPM. The approximate speed can
also be displayed (MPH/KPH) in the same section, and the cadence carries a **bar graph** alongside.
**Average Cadence or Speed is shown automatically once the rider stops pedalling for 3 seconds.**

**DISTANCE.** The approximate distance achieved, calculated as though the rider were on a bike whose
tyres are the same size as the bike's flywheel. It is not a road distance.

**KCAL.** An approximation of calories burned, calculated from the rider's **instantaneous heart
rate, age and weight**.

**HEART RATE** *(HR featured models only)*. The approximation of heart rate detected **from the chest
belt**. **Average Heart Rate is shown automatically after 3 seconds if the console cannot detect the
current heart rate.**

**The three-second rule is the same everywhere on this console** - time stops, cadence and speed flip
to averages, and heart rate flips to average. A rider who stops for a red light and sees a different
number is seeing the average, not a fault.

**Calories need a heart rate.** The manual says the calculation uses instantaneous heart rate, so
without a chest belt paired the figure rests on age and weight alone. The personal data setting is
what supplies those; see `cic850-2022-console-personal-data-half-pound-steps`.
