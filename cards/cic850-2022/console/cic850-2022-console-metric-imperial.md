1. Hold the **PAGE** button for **3 seconds** until the display starts flashing with **TIME**
   displayed.
2. Press **PAGE** to select **KG or LB** (metric or imperial).
3. Press **MODE** to confirm your selection.

**There is one unit switch, not three.** The weight unit *is* the units setting: the personal data
page spells out that "this change of metric units to imperial units will affect the displaying units:
**MPH/KPH, Lb/KG and ML/KM**." Choose LB and you get miles per hour and miles; choose KG and you get
km/h and kilometres.

**So a rider asking for miles must change the weight unit.** There is no separate distance or speed
setting anywhere in this manual.

**The same choice appears inside the personal data walk-through**, as step 2 of three, reached by
holding Page for 3 seconds under KCAL or DISTANCE and stepping with MODE. Both routes set the same
value; see `cic850-2022-console-personal-data-half-pound-steps`.

**The published speed range is stated in km/h only** (0 - 99 KM/H), even though the console will
display MPH.

**The service manual prints the same three steps.** A scanned support tip headed "New SB700/900 Console
| CIC X50" gives a longer route for the same change: press **Mode** to highlight **DIST**, hold **Page**
until TIME blinks, press **Mode** so a **heart** blinks, press **Page six times** to reach the blinking
KG or LB, press **Mode** to change it, then let the console reset. That walks through the personal-data
screens to the same weight-unit switch; it is a technician's note (authority 2), not a manual.
