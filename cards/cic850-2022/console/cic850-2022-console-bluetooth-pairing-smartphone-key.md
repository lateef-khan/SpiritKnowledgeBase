1. Press the **smart phone key** on the console - the centre of the three keys, labelled SMARTPHONE
   BUTTON on the console drawing.
2. On your phone, open the desired app and **search for devices to pair**.
3. Select the bike console once it is shown on your device. The app and bike console should now be
   paired.

**The protocol is BLE4.0**, published in the console specification block.

**The console is compatible with most virtual active apps available** - the manual says so as the
closing line of its Kinomap walk-through. It gives named walk-throughs for **Zwift** and **Kinomap**
only.

**The bike shows up as `SPIRIT BIKE`** in Kinomap's device list, which is the only place the manual
prints the advertised name. Expect the same name elsewhere.

**There is no PIN and no passkey** in this procedure, and no way described to unpair or to forget a
device from the console side.

**This is a different key from the pairing gesture for the speed transmitter**, which is MODE held
with PAGE/RECORD for 3 seconds and has nothing to do with your phone.
