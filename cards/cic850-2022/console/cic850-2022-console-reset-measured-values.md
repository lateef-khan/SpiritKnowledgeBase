**Press and hold the Page key under the TIME Mode for 3 seconds.**

All the measured values for **AVG SPEED, AVG PULSE, TIME, DIST and KCAL reset to zero.**

**It clears all five or none.** The manual gives no way to zero one figure and keep another.

**You have to be in the right window first.** Press MODE until the bottom window is showing TIME;
holding Page from DIST or KCAL opens the personal data and heart rate zone settings instead, and
holding it from SPEED or DISTANCE opens the countdown timer.

**The troubleshooting page words it differently.** There it reads "Press and Hold 'SET key'
repeatedly to clear past measured value or go into setting and exit" - the same gesture, named for a
key this console does not have. See `cic850-2022-console-set-key-not-on-the-console`.

**This does not clear the personal data.** Gender, weight units and bodyweight survive; the manual
describes no way to reset those to factory values, and no odometer at all.

**The service manual prints the same reset**: hold the Page key under the TIME mode for 3 seconds, and
AVG SPEED, AVG PULSE, TIME, DIST and KCAL return to zero.
