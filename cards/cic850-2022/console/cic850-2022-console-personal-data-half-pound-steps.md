Under **KCAL or DISTANCE** mode, hold the **Page** key for **3 seconds** to go into the setting, then
press **MODE** repeatedly to reach the heart rate windows.

1. **Gender.** Press the **Page** key to select **Male or Female**.
2. **Weight unit.** Press **Page** to move on, then press **Mode** to select **Kg or Lb**.
   *Note: this change of metric units to imperial units affects the displaying units MPH/KPH, Lb/KG
   and ML/KM.*
3. **Weight.** Press **Page** to move on, then press **Mode** to increase the weight **by 0.5 Kg or
   0.5 Lb** per press.

**Why it matters.** The manual says calories are calculated by measuring the rider's **instantaneous
heart rate, age and weight**, and accurate personal data makes the calories more accurate.

**But there is no age field.** The console asks for gender, weight unit and weight, and nothing else.
The calorie formula the manual describes uses an age the console never collects.

**Half a unit per press.** With no rapid-advance gesture described, a 180 lb rider is 360 presses
from zero. The manual gives no faster route.

**The Page and Mode keys swap roles here** compared with the heart rate zone on the same settings
run, where Mode increases the value and Page moves on. Read each step, do not assume.
