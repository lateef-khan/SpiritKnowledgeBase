**The console is a DT-3268F**, named as part 110 in the step 5 hardware table.

**Nine labels are drawn round the screen.** On the left, top to bottom: **WATTS**, **HEART RATE**,
**LOW BATTERY**. On the right, top to bottom: **RPM**, **SPEED**, **BPM**, and one label pointing at
the bottom line reading **TIME / DISTANCE / KCAL** - those three share a single window and the
**Mode** key swaps between them.

**Three keys, in a row under the screen:**

| Position | Label |
|---|---|
| Left | **MODE** |
| Centre | **SMARTPHONE BUTTON** |
| Right | **PAGE/ RECORD** |

**The screen carries icons as well as digits** - a lightning bolt beside the watts figure, a cadence
symbol, a heart, and a Bluetooth mark at the top right.

**The console face itself is moulded MODE on the left and PAGE on the right**, matching the drawing.

**There is no fourth key**, which matters because the manual's pairing and troubleshooting pages
repeatedly instruct you to press a **SET** key. There is no SET key on this console; see
`cic850-2022-console-set-key-not-on-the-console`.

**The console runs on batteries and has no mains lead**, so there is no power switch either.
