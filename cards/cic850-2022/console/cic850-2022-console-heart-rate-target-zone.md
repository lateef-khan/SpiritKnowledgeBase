1. Under **DIST or KCAL**, hold the **Page** key for **3 seconds** to go into the settings.
2. Press **MODE** to go to the **heart rate icon**, then press **Page** to open the heart rate target
   zone window.
3. Press the **MODE** key to increase the **maximum** heart rate limit.
4. When the maximum is set, press the **Page** key to adjust the **minimum** heart rate.

**It needs a chest belt.** This console reads heart rate from a chest belt only - it has no hand grip
pulse sensors - so with no belt the zone has nothing to act on.

**The console publishes a pulse range of 30 to 240 BPM**; the manual sets no separate limit on the
zone itself.

**The manual describes no alarm for this zone.** Unlike the XIC600, which arms a beeping heart rate
alarm from its right hand key, the CIC850 pages describe setting the limits and nothing that happens
when you cross them. Do not promise a rider a beep.

**Same doorway as the personal data settings.** Holding Page for 3 seconds under DIST or KCAL opens
one settings run; MODE steps through it and the heart rate icon is one of the stops. See
`cic850-2022-console-personal-data-half-pound-steps`.
