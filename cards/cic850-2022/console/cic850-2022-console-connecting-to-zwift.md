1. **Log in to Zwift.**
2. **Select the power source and cadence.** The system prompts you to select a machine.
3. Select the machine.
4. **Select ride to start.**
5. **Enjoy the ride!**

**Pair the console to the phone first.** Press the smartphone key on the console so it is advertising
over BLE4.0, then let Zwift find it - see
`cic850-2022-console-bluetooth-pairing-smartphone-key`.

**The bike presents as both a power source and a cadence sensor.** Zwift is asked for both in the
same step, so a rider who pairs only cadence gets no watts in game.

**The manual's steps are a picture caption sequence**, printed against five screenshots, and it names
no Zwift version, no minimum app release and no trainer difficulty setting. Anything beyond these five
steps is not in this manual.

**Kinomap has its own, much longer procedure** on the next two pages -
`cic850-2022-console-connecting-to-kinomap`. Beyond those two, the manual says only that the console
is compatible with most virtual active apps available.
