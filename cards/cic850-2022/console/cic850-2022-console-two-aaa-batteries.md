**Console - 2 AAA:**

1. Remove the battery cover from the back of the console (part 110).
2. Insert **2 AAA batteries** into the battery compartment and reinstall the cover.
3. When battery working voltage is low, the **"low battery" indicator** shows on the console display.

The manual prints this twice, in the assembly step and again at the end of the troubleshooting page.
The second copy says "from the back of the **computer**" rather than the console; same part.

**Speed sensor board - cell not specified:**

1. Loosen screw 119 with wrench C and remove **LEFT CHAIN GUARD B** (part 118).
2. **Insert the new battery on the sensor board.**
3. Replace the chain guard and secure it with the screw.

**The manual never names the sensor board's cell type**, gives no low-battery warning for it, and
gives no interval. Do not tell a customer it is AAA - that figure is only ever given for the console.

**Changing the sensor battery may break the pairing.** The pairing procedure is printed on the very
same page, directly under the battery step, headed with the note that all transmitters are paired
before shipping and these steps are only needed where the devices have been unpaired. See
`cic850-2022-console-pairing-the-speed-transmitter`.
