**Outline (printed p. 2), in capitals as printed:** SEAT HEIGHT ADJ, **TABLET HOLDER**, SEAT
CUSHION, **DUMBBELL HOLDER**, **RESISTANCE ADJUSTMENT**, HANDLEBAR ADJUSTMENT, FLYWHEEL, REAR
STABILIZER, PEDALS, FRONT STABILIZER, TRANSPORT WHEELS, **LEVELERS**, MAIN FRAME.

**Skeleton (printed p. 3):** Handlebar Mount, Drive Pulley (printed *Drive Pullev*), Handlebar,
Sliding Seat Mount, **Handlebar Post Sliding**, **Seat Post Mount**, **Outer circle tube assembly**,
Main Frame, **Brake Block**, Front Stabilizer, Rear Stabilizer, Crank Arm.

The *Resistance Adjustment* is the knob that presses the **Brake Block** onto the flywheel - a
friction brake, so there is no motor or coil on either drawing; the *Outer circle tube assembly*
is the flywheel's rim. The *Dumbbell Holder* sits behind the seat post. The capitalised outline
labels are the owner's-manual names; the skeleton uses the factory's.

No dimensions, weights or numbers are printed on either page. The Sole SB700 of 2011 is the
same kind of friction-brake cycle with a leather pad instead of a block
(`sb700-2011-specs-outline-and-skeleton-part-names`) - a different brand and machine.
