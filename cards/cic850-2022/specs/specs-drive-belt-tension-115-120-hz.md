**115-120 Hz** - "The tension of the belt is 115-120HZ", printed at the end of step 4 of the belt
replacement, after the new belt's rear end is put onto the small pulley and idler arm on the
flywheel and the crank is turned slowly to walk it onto the front pulley. No gauge is named; a
frequency means a sonic tension meter.

**The Q&A's belt-tension procedure gives no number.** It says to loosen the two M6 hex screws
(No. 55), turn screw No. 17 clockwise to tighten and anticlockwise to loosen, retighten, and
"turn the crank to see if belt runs smoothly. You can also try riding the bike to test the belt
tension". Its opening note, "make sure you adjust both sides equally", is treadmill running-belt
text: this cycle has one adjustment screw.

**Lower than every other Spirit bike figure** - the CIC850's pulley ratio and belt are not the
uprights' (180 Hz) or the JB950's (100-110 pounds, `jb950-2022-specs-belt-tension-100-110-pounds`).
