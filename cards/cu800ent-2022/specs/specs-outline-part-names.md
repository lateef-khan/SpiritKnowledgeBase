One drawing, nine labels: **Console**, **Cooling Fan**, **Seat**, **Handle Pulse Sensor**,
**Drink Bottle Holder**, **Shroud**, **Pedal**, **Rear Stabilizer**, **Moving Wheel**.

The *Moving Wheel* is the transport wheel on the front stabilizer, and the *Shroud* is the single
label for the side case. The cooling fan is called out on the console because the ENT fan sits in
the console head and its angle is adjustable (see the console section).

No skeleton drawing, dimensions or weights are printed. The generator-bike outline of the
CU800(2020) book labels different parts on the same frame: `cu800-2021-specs-outline-part-names`.
