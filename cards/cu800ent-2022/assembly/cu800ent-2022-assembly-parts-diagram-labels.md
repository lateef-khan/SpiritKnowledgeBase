Eight labels, printed on a photograph with no heading and no letters:

- **CONSOLE**
- **CONSOLE FAN**
- **HAND PULSE SENSORS**
- **CUP HOLDER**
- **SEAT**
- **FRONT STABILIZER**
- **PEDALS**
- **REAR STABILIZER**

The page carries no title, no item numbers and no key. It is a photograph of a finished
machine with eight callout lines, and the text under them is set as a flat image - it
comes out of the manual only through OCR.

## Five parts the 2024 diagram names and this page does not

The 2024 printing replaces this photograph with a lettered line drawing, A to M, adding
**Front Handlebars**, **Console Mast**, **USB Charger**, **Crank Arms** and **Main
Frame**, and renaming CONSOLE to Touchscreen and HAND PULSE SENSORS to Pulse Grips. See
[that card](../../cu800ent-2024/assembly/cu800ent-2024-assembly-parts-diagram-labels.md).

## The non-touchscreen printing labels six more, and the levelers

The CU800 2021 manual puts a line drawing on the same page with **fourteen** labels,
adding **HANDLEBARS**, **CONSOLE MAST**, **CONSOLE MAST COVER**, **DRINK BOTTLE
HOLDER**, **CRANK ARM**, **MAIN FRAME** and, alone in this whole family, **LEVELERS**,
and it calls the grips PULSE SENSORS rather than HAND PULSE SENSORS. See
[that card](../../cu800-2021/assembly/cu800-2021-assembly-parts-diagram-labels.md).

## The diagram is not a carton list

Nothing on this page tells you what is packed loose. For that see
[the parts card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-cu800ent-assembly-parts-included.md).
