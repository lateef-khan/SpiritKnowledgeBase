These are the callout numbers on the exploded view drawing on page 58. The
drawing is a picture with bare numbers on it and no text, so this table is the
only readable form of it.

**The list heads itself `CT800ENT Part List_SPIRIT`** and is date-stamped
**230809** at its foot - the same stamp the CT800-2020 list carries, so the date
does not tell the two lists apart.

**148 rows are printed.** The whole numbers that appear are 1-29, 31-70, 72, 75,
77-82, 85-94, 101-109, 111-140, 142-148, 150-156, 158-164 and 166. Numbers
outside that run are not printed in this manual at all - **30, 71, 73, 74, 76,
83, 84, 95-100, 110, 141, 149, 157 and 165 are absent, not a missing page.**

**The manual contradicts itself about which cable pair is which.** The parts list
prints item 79 as `900m/m_CSAFE Connecting Cable(Upper)` and item 80 as
`2400m/m_CSAFE Connecting Cable(Lower)`, while items 81 and 82 are only
`Connecting Wire (Upper)` and `(Lower)`. **The assembly steps say the opposite**:
Step Two pairs "the Ethernet Cable (No. 80) with the Upper Ethernet Cable
(No.79)" and "the C-safe Cable (No. 82) with the Upper C-safe Cable (No.81)", and
Step Four repeats "The Upper Ethernet Cable (No.79)" and "The Upper C-safe Cable
(No.81)". So the list calls 79/80 the CSAFE pair and the assembly text calls
79/80 the Ethernet pair. **Confirm which connector a cable lands on before
ordering 79, 80, 81 or 82.** Both pairs are the same two lengths, 900 mm upper
and 2400 mm lower, so length will not tell them apart either. This card records
the parts list wording exactly as printed and does not resolve the conflict.

**Seventeen items exist only on this list and not on the CT800-2020 list** -
77, 78, 79, 80, 81, 82, 85, 86, 87, 88, 89, 90, 91, 92, 93, 164 and 166. They are
the entertainment console's hardware: the two CSAFE cable pairs, the Plate and
L-Plate, the Board, the Power Adaptor, the Interface Board, six short connecting
wires and two extra screws. **Item 71, the TV Adapter (5C2V), exists only on the
2020 list and is not printed here.**

of them differ in quantity and two are a genuinely different part:

and 118 - differ only in how the same part is written out: added or dropped size
prefixes, a connector code in brackets, a hyphen instead of brackets. Their
quantities match.

**No motor rating, roller size, deck size or belt size is printed for any of
these parts.** Item 55 is "Drive Motor" and item 66 "Incline Motor", each with no
horsepower or wattage; items 15, 16, 17 and 18 give the front roller, rear
roller, running deck and running belt no dimensions.
