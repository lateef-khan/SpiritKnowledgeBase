Two bullets of the safety instructions set the placement rules:

> Do not operate treadmill on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and
> treadmill may result.

> Do not block the rear of the treadmill. Provide a minimum of 1 meter clearance between the rear
> of the treadmill and any fixed object.

- **1 metre behind the machine, clear of any fixed object.**
- **Not on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet.** The reason given is damage to both the carpet and
  the treadmill, not traction or ventilation.
- The safety WARNING header adds **a flat, level surface** with a 120-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet:
  `spirit-ct800-safety-outlet-120-volt-15-amp`.

**No side clearance and no front clearance figure is printed.** This manual states only the rear
distance. If a customer needs a figure for the sides, say the manual does not give one.

**Do not fill that gap from the 2016 and 2020 CT800 manuals.** Those two print the ASTM F2115-05
clearance instead - 0.5 m (19.7 in.) on each side and 1 m (39 in.) behind
(`ct900ent-clearance-requirements`). The ENT manual cites no standard and prints no side figure, so
quoting 19.7 in. for a CT800ENT would be an invention. The rear figure happens to agree at 1 metre.

**The CT800-2012 manual prints no placement or clearance rule at all** - no carpet rule and no
distance anywhere in it.

The temperature, humidity and moisture limits for this machine are
`ct900ent-operating-environment-voltage`.
