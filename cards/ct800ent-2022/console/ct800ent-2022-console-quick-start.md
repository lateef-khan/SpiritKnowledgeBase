- Press the **Start** button to begin belt movement at **0.5 mph**, then adjust to the desired speed
  using the **Speed** up/down button.
- To slow the treadmill belt, press the **Speed down** key until the desired speed is reached.
- To stop the treadbelt, press and release the **Stop** button.

**There are no direct access speed buttons on this console and no wake-the-display step.** The other
CT800 consoles have both: twelve direct access speed buttons on the later LED console
(`ct850-2020-quick-start`), five rapid speed keys numbered 2 through 6 on the earlier one
(`spirit-ct800-console-quick-start-speed-keys-2-to-6`). Neither belongs on this machine.

The manual prints no top speed in its quick start section. What the second and third press of Stop
do is a separate behaviour: `ct800ent-2022-console-pause-stop`.

**The CT850ENT-2022 owner's manual prints this section word for word on its p. 17.** Its console
chapter and its TOUCHSCREEN OPERATION chapter are the same document as the CT800ENT-2022's, page for
page and line for line, so this card covers that machine.

**Neither ENT console has direct access speed buttons.** The CT850 2016 and 2020 LED consoles have
fifteen (`spirit-ct850-console-quick-start-speed-keys-1-to-15`) and the CT850-2018 types the speed
on a numeric buttonpad (`ct850-2018-console-quick-start-numeric-buttonpad`). Neither belongs on this
machine.
