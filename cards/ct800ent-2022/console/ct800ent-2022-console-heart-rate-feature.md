The **Pulse (Heart Rate)** value on the screen shows the current heart beats per minute.

- You must use **both** the left and right stainless steel sensors to pick up your pulse.
- Pulse values are displayed any time the computer is receiving a signal from the **hand pulse
  sensors**.
- The hand pulse sensors may be used while in Heart Rate Control.
- The treadmill will also pick up wireless heart rate transmitters that are **Polar and Bluetooth
  compatible**.

**This is the only CT800 console the manuals call Bluetooth compatible.** The LED CT800 consoles are
described as Polar compatible "including coded transmissions" and name no Bluetooth strap:
`ct850-2020-heart-rate-feature`. Do not read one statement onto the other machine.

The manual prints no pairing procedure for a strap and no list of supported strap models. How to
wear a strap is `ctsbs900-heart-rate-chest-strap-pairing`; wild or random readings are
`ctsbs900-heart-rate-chest-strap-troubleshooting`.
