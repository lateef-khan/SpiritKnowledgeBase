- Plug in the power cord and switch on the **main power switch at the front of the treadmill**. The
  **safety key must be inserted** - the machine will not operate without it.
- When the power is turned on, the screen shows a **brief loading screen** and then the **Home
  Screen**, which indicates that the machine is ready to operate.

**Begin operation by touching the icons on the Home Screen.** The quick operating buttons on the
handlebar do the basic treadmill functions instead: `ct800ent-2022-console-layout`.

**The manual does not list the Home Screen's icons.** It shows them only in a photograph, and names
them elsewhere one at a time - Language (`ct800ent-2022-console-language-selection`), Internet
(`ct800ent-2022-console-internet-apps`), Screen Mirroring from the Entertainment screen, and the
exercise programs. No icon list is printed, so do not quote one.

This console shows no odometer or software version at power-up. **The LED CT800 consoles do**, and
that sequence is `ct850-2020-power-on-sequence`; it does not describe this machine.

**The CT850ENT-2022 owner's manual prints this section word for word on its p. 16.** Its console
chapter and its TOUCHSCREEN OPERATION chapter are the same document as the CT800ENT-2022's, page for
page and line for line, so this card covers that machine.

**Its LED stablemates do show an odometer and a software version at power-up** -
`ct850-2020-power-on-sequence` covers the CT850 2016, 2018 and 2020 consoles, and does not describe
this machine.
