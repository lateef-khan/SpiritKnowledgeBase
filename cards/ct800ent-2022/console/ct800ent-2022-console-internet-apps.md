The **Internet** section offers various **streaming, news and social media** options.

- **The machine must be connected to the internet for the apps to work.**
- Click the app of your choice to connect.
- Follow any on-screen prompts to continue login or other authorizations as needed.

**The manual names no app and prints no count.** It also gives no network setup procedure for this
machine - nothing about WiFi, Ethernet, a password screen or an administrator menu - so how the
treadmill is put on a network is not answered here.

The CT900ENT manual describes a different arrangement: **6 basic internet sites** chosen through an
Internet interface, configurable from its Maintenance program (`ct900ent-internet-browsing`). That
is a different machine; do not carry the count or the site manager onto this one.
