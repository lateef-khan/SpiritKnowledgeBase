**This console is a touchscreen.** It has none of the LED windows, dot matrix or direct access
ladders of the other CT800 consoles.

The drawing labels:

| Call-out | Where it is |
|---|---|
| Touchscreen | The whole upper display |
| Tablet-friendly reading rack | Above the screen |
| Adjustable fan angle | Fan beside the screen |
| USB Charging port | Below the fan |
| Cell Phone Ledge | Left of the screen |
| Small Tray Storage | Below the cell phone ledge |
| Convenient accessory trays | Right of the screen |
| Start, Stop, Speed, and Incline controls | Keypad in the middle of the handlebar |
| Safety Key | Left of that keypad |
| Contact Heart Rate Grips | The two handlebar grips |

**The touchscreen operates most functions of the treadmill.** You can touch any button on the
screen, or use the **Quick Operating Buttons** on the handlebar instead:

- **Speed** up/down - control the speed.
- **Start** - begins the workout.
- **Stop** - pauses or stops the workout.
- **Incline** up/down - control the degree of incline.

The manual gives no rating, current or data capability for the USB charging port, and no dimension
for the reading rack or the trays.

**The CT850ENT-2022 owner's manual prints the same call-out drawing on its p. 15 and the same
FUNCTIONS OF THIS TREADMILL paragraph on its p. 17**, with the same ten labels and the same four
quick operating buttons, so this card covers that machine. Only the heading differs - CT850ENT
CONSOLE OPERATION rather than CT800ENT.

Neither manual gives a rating, current or data capability for the USB charging port.

**The CT800ENT-2024 and CT850ENT-2024 owner's manuals print a call-out drawing of ten labels on their
p. 21** - CONSOLE SCREEN - OVERVIEW - and the same FUNCTIONS OF THIS TREADMILL paragraph on their
p. 15, so this card covers those two machines. That page is a flattened image in both books and the
labels were read from the OCR supplement in `text.md`.

**Nine of the ten labels are the same. One is not.** Where the 2022 drawing labels **Convenient
accessory trays**, the 2024 drawing labels a **Water bottle holder and cargo compartment for buttons,
phone, or MP3 player** - the same words the LED CT800-2024 and CT850-2024 drawings use
(`ct850-2020-console-layout`). The 2024 books also write **Cellphone Ledge** and **Small Storage
Tray** as one word and two, where the 2022 books write Cell Phone Ledge and Small Tray Storage, and
they label the fan simply **Fan** rather than Adjustable fan angle.

**The FUNCTIONS paragraph is word for word**, including the four quick operating buttons - Speed
up/down, Start, Stop, Incline up/down - and the phrase "operating most functions of the treadmill".

**Neither 2024 manual gives a rating, current or data capability for the USB charger** either. **The
2024 ENT elliptical and bike are drawn with five call-outs, not ten**:
`spirit-ent-bike-console-layout-touchscreen`.
