**This console is a touchscreen.** It has none of the LED windows, dot matrix or direct access
ladders of the other CT800 consoles.

The drawing labels:

| Call-out | Where it is |
|---|---|
| Touchscreen | The whole upper display |
| Tablet-friendly reading rack | Above the screen |
| Adjustable fan angle | Fan beside the screen |
| USB Charging port | Below the fan |
| Cell Phone Ledge | Left of the screen |
| Small Tray Storage | Below the cell phone ledge |
| Convenient accessory trays | Right of the screen |
| Start, Stop, Speed, and Incline controls | Keypad in the middle of the handlebar |
| Safety Key | Left of that keypad |
| Contact Heart Rate Grips | The two handlebar grips |

**The touchscreen operates most functions of the treadmill.** You can touch any button on the
screen, or use the **Quick Operating Buttons** on the handlebar instead:

- **Speed** up/down - control the speed.
- **Start** - begins the workout.
- **Stop** - pauses or stops the workout.
- **Incline** up/down - control the degree of incline.

The manual gives no rating, current or data capability for the USB charging port, and no dimension
for the reading rack or the trays.
