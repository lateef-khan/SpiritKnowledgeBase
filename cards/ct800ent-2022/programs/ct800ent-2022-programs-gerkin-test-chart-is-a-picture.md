The Gerkin protocol, also known as the fireman's protocol, is a **sub-max VO2 (volume of oxygen)
test**. The test increases speed and elevation alternately **until you reach 85% of your maximum
heart rate**. The time it takes you to reach 85% determines the test score (VO2max).

The manual adds one note the button-console CT800 manuals do not print: **use a heart rate strap for
best results.**

**The Gerkin value table and the VO2max score chart are printed as pictures in this manual and did
not survive text extraction, so no stage values and no score bands are recorded for this machine.**
Two other Spirit charts exist and they do **not** agree with each other, so neither may be assumed
to be the one on this console:

- `spirit-2026t-programs-gerkin-chart` - the chart whose stage 2.1 begins at **1:15**. This is the
  chart the 2012, 2016 and 2020 CT800 manuals print.
- `ct900-gerkin-protocol` - the CT900 chart, whose stage 2.1 begins at **1:00** and whose speeds sit
  a quarter-stage earlier from stage 2.4 onward.

Read the original CT800ENT manual pages before quoting a stage speed or a VO2max score for this
machine.

**The CT850ENT owner's manual prints this same description word for word** (printed page 36),
including the heart-rate-strap note, and **its Gerkin value table (printed page 37) and VO2max score
chart (printed page 38) are pictures too**, so no stage values and no score bands are recorded for
that machine either. The same warning applies: the two Spirit charts that do exist disagree with
each other and neither may be assumed to be the one on this console.
