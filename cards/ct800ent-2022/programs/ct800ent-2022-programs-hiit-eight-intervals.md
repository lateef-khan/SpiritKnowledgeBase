**These are the figures for eight intervals and 20-to-90-second periods.** The button-console CT800
generation defaults to **10** intervals and allows **30 to 60 seconds** - see `ct900-hiit-program`.
The two are not interchangeable.

The HIIT, or High Intensity Interval Training, program has you perform short bursts of high
intensity sprinting followed by short rest periods. It is a fully customizable interval training
program: you enter the number of intervals, the time of each Sprint and Rest period, and the work
intensity of the levels.

1. Select the program then press **Enter** to begin customizing it with your personal data. You are
   asked for the **number of intervals** you want to do. **The default is 8 and the range available
   is 3 to 15.** One interval equals 1 Sprint and 1 Rest segment.
2. Next enter the **Interval time**: the Sprint time and the Rest time. **You may select the Sprint
   time or Rest time from 20 to 90 seconds.** After the interval time is set, set the intensity to
   **Sprint speed** and **Rest speed**.
3. Press **Start** to begin the HIIT program. **The program starts with a 3-minute warm-up period
   with the speed set to 50% of the sprint speed** selected previously. You can manually adjust the
   speed during warm-up if you wish.

**This manual prints no default sprint speed and no default rest speed.** The button-console
generation offers 6.0 MPH and 3.0 MPH as defaults; do not assume those figures here.
