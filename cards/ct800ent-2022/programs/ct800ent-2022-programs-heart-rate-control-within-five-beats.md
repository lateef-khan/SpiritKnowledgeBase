Heart Rate Control (HRC) uses the treadmill's **incline system**, not speed, to control your heart
rate, driven by the pulse grips or a wireless chest strap (sold separately). Increases and decreases
in elevation affect heart rate much more efficiently than changes in speed alone; the program
changes elevation gradually to achieve the programmed target.

- **Heart Rate 1 (HR1)** attempts to maintain your heart rate at **65%** of your maximum.
- **Heart Rate 2 (HR2)** attempts to maintain your heart rate at **80%** of your maximum, as
  calculated from the user data you entered.
- You may **change the target heart rate** yourself.
- **The machine attempts to keep you within five beats of your target heart rate.**

**When "No Heart Rate Detected" is displayed**, no pulse signal is being received. Check that the
sensors or the heart rate strap are being used as instructed, and check for possible interference.

Both programs are selected and started from the Exercise Programs menu like any other program - see
`ct800ent-2022-programs-entering-a-program-touchscreen`. **This manual prints no separate
step-by-step Heart Rate setup sequence**; the button-console CT800 generations do, and those steps
must not be carried across.

The CT900ENT manual states the same five-beat behaviour and a 65% default but never prints a figure
for its HR2 - see `ct900ent-hr-program`.
