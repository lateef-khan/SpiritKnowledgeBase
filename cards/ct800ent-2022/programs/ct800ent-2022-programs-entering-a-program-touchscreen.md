Tap **Exercise Programs** on the Home Screen. The screen shows the menu of programs available:
Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Interval, Heart Rate 1, Heart Rate 2, Custom, 5k Run, 10k Run, HIIT
and Fitness Test.

1. Select a program by **tapping it on the screen**, then press **Enter** to begin. Customize the
   program on the following screens by entering your data, or press **Start** to begin with the
   default settings.
2. Press **Start** to begin your workout, or press **Stop** to return to the previous screen.
3. Once the workout begins a **3-minute warm-up** commences. Press **Start** to bypass it and go
   straight to the workout. During the warm-up the clock counts down from three minutes.

**No warm-up on: 5K Run, 10K Run, HR, Custom, Fitness Tests and the manual programs.** *The
button-console generation prints the same list without the Fitness Tests - see
`ct850-2020-starting-and-customising-a-program`.*

*The manual's numbered list jumps from 1 to 3, printing no step 2. Nothing appears to be missing
from the sense of the procedure; the numbering is reproduced above as a continuous 1-2-3.*

**This console does not prompt for time, age, bodyweight and top speed in fixed console windows the
way the button consoles do.** It says only "customize the program on the following screens by
entering your data"; the individual per-program screens are described on each program's own page.

**The CT850ENT owner's manual prints this same page word for word** (printed page 22): the same
twelve-program menu, the same numbered list that jumps from 1 to 3 with no step 2, and the same
no-warm-up list including the Fitness Tests.
