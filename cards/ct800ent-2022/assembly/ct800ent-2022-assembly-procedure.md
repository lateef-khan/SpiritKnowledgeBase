**The same four steps and the same hardware as the LED CT800 and CT850 of the same
platform, with four cables where those machines have one.** The differences are all
listed at the bottom of this card; take them seriously, because two of the counts
differ.

**The CT800ENT and CT850ENT assembly chapters are the same document.** Every word of
the four steps and of all four hardware tables is identical; only the model name in
the page headings differs.

## Step one - right upright, then left

1. Thread the all Cables (No.53/80/82/84) up through the centre of the Right
   Upright Column (No.9).
2. Use five sets of Button Head Socket Bolt (No.150), Split Washer (No.133) against
   a Flat Washer (No.115) to secure the Upright Fixing Plate (No.10) to the Right
   Uprights (No.9) to the Main Frame (No.1) using the Allen Wrench (No.160).
3. Use two Phillips Head Screws (No.144) to secure the Motor Base Cap Right (No.35)
   to Main Frame (No.1) using the Screw Driver (No.158).
4. Repeat the previous two steps on the left side.

**Item 84 is not in this manual's parts list.** The list runs 82, then 85. Cables
53, 80 and 82 are all there. The manual never says what 84 is.

## Step two - locking plates, cable pairing, console support

1. On the underside of the Console Support (No.5) is where the Locking Plate
   Assembly (No. 3 & 4) can be attached. Use 2 Socket Head Cap Bolts (No.152) and 2
   Flat Washers (No.153) on each side to secure.
2. Connect all cables with the correct pairing:
   - The Computer Cable (No. 53) with the Upper Computer Cable (No.52).
   - The Ethernet Cable (No. 80) with the Upper Ethernet Cable (No.79).
   - The C-safe Cable (No. 82) with the Upper C-safe Cable (No.81).

   Note: **Be careful not to pinch the wires** and slide them into the Right
   Upright (No.9).
3. Gently slot the Console Support (No.5) into the Left/Right Uprights (No.8 & 9)
   and secure with 3 Button Head Socket Bolts (No.151), 3 Split Washers (No.133),
   and 3 Flat Washers (No.125) on both sides using the Allen Wrench (No.160).

**The parts list names those two cable pairs the other way round.** It calls 79 and
80 the CSAFE Connecting Cable, upper and lower, and calls 81 and 82 plain
Connecting Wire, upper and lower - where this step calls 79/80 Ethernet and 81/82
C-safe. Pair them by the connector and the run length, not by the name.

Step 1 uses four each of items 152 and 153; the step two hardware table lists five
of each.

## Step three - rear upright bolts, cap and safety key

1. Further secure the uprights by inserting a Button Head Socket Bolt (No.154)
   through a Split Washer (No.133), and a Curved Washer (No.155) onto the backside
   of both Left/Right Uprights (No. 8 & 9) using the Allen Wrench (No.160).
2. Plug the Round Cap (No.29) into the Right Upright (No.9).
3. Plug the Safety Key (No.72) into the Safety Key base on the Console Support
   (No.5).

## Step four - console

1. Place the Console Assembly (No.38) onto the Console Support (No.5). Secure with
   4 Socket Head Cap Bolts (No.156), 4 Split Washers (No.120), and 4 Flat Washers
   (No.153) using the Allen Wrench (No.159).
2. Connect all cables to Console Assembly (No.38):
   - The Upper Handpulse Wires (No.50 & 51).
   - The Upper Computer Cable (No.52).
   - The Grounding wire (No.46).
   - The Upper Ethernet Cable (No.79).
   - The Upper C-safe Cable (No.81).
3. Fasten the Console Cover (No.41) with 2 Phillips Head Screws (No.144) to the
   Console Support (No.5) using the Screw Driver (No.158).

## The four differences from the LED machine on the same platform

- **Cables up the upright:** four here, one there.
- **Bolt sets per side in step one:** five here, four there - out of the same ten
  in the hardware table, which only the five makes add up.
- **Cables onto the console in step four:** five here, three there. Only this
  manual names a grounding wire in the assembly.
- **Console cover screws:** two here, one there - out of the same two in the
  hardware table.

This manual also drops the "Gather Hardware for Step n" opening item that the other
one prints at the head of every step, so its item numbers all sit one lower.
