Hardware: **#98** M6 x 15mm Phillips Head Screw (4 pcs), **#99** M5 x 12mm Phillips Head Screw (4 pcs), **#105** Ø4 x 16mm Sheet Metal Screw (4 pcs), **#192** 3.5 x 12L Sheet Metal Screw (4 pcs).

1. Install the Seat (61) on the Seat Carriage (4) with the four Phillips Head Screws (98), by using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (114).
2. Install the Computer Cable (44) and Hand pulse Wire, Coiled (45) into the connector in the back of the Console Assembly (19). Install the console onto the mounting plate with the four Phillips Head Screws (99). **Be careful not to pinch the wires between the console and the mounting plate - this could short the wires and damage the electronics.**
3. Install the Drink Bottle Holders (L /39, R/38) onto **left of** the Seat Handle Bar (6) with the four Sheet Metal Screws (105).
4. Install the Pedals (L/116, R/117) in the Cranks by using the **14/15m/m Wrench (132)**. **The left Pedal (116 L) has a reverse thread** and screws into the crank in the opposite rotation from normal threads. There is an "L" stamped into the end of the threaded post of the left pedal and an "R" in the right. Make sure to tighten the pedals as much as you possibly can. It may be necessary to re-tighten the pedals if you feel a thumping during pedalling; a clicking or thumping sound during pedalling is usually caused by the pedals being too loose.
5. Install the Tablet Holder (190) to the back of the console with four Sheet Metal Screws (192), by using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (114).

**Only four #99 screws now, and no stabilizer covers.** Through 2016 this step carried eight #99 - four for the covers and four for the console. From 2019 the covers are not fitted by the owner at all.

**"onto left of the Seat Handle Bar" is what the manual says** for a pair of holders labelled L/39 and R/38. The 2011 and 2013 LCR manuals say "onto left and right sides".

The LCR 2023 prints the same five instructions but adds **four Star Washers (196)** under the four console screws.
