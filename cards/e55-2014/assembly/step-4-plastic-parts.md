Use screwdriver (160 or 161) for the plastic parts.

1. **Connecting Arm Covers (88-R) with (87-L)** on the Left Connecting Arm (8) with **two Phillips Head Screws (115)** and **one Sheet Metal Screw (126)**. Repeat for the Right Connecting Arm (9).
2. **Slide Wheel Covers (82)** above each slide wheel with **four Phillips Head Screws (115)**.
3. **Middle Stabilizer Covers (93-L & 94-R)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (115)**.
4. **Front Stabilizer Cover (79)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (115)**.
5. **Cover Holders (21)**, hole facing backward, on the Rear Rail Assembly with **four Phillips Head Screws (125)**; **Inclinable Rail Cover (91)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (115)** using driver (161).
6. **Rear Bar Cover (92)** on the rear stabilizer and the Cover Holders (21) with **four Phillips Head Screws (115)**.
7. **Set the incline level between 3 and 8**, then install the **Incline Bottom Cover (89)** on the Rail Base Assembly (2) with **two Phillips Head Screws (115)**.
8. **Front Console Cover (95)** on the mast, matched with the **Rear Console Cover (96)**, with **three Phillips Head Screws (115)** and **two Sheet Metal Screws (126)**.
