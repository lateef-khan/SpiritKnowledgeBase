1. **Slide two Wave Washers (153)** and the **Swing Arms (10-L) and (11-R)** onto each side of the Console Mast shaft; secure with **two Hex Head Bolts (100)** and **two Flat Washers (145)** using **Wrench (162)**.
2. **Connect the Upper Handle Resistance Cable (39) and Upper Handle Incline Cable (40)** with the **Lower Resistance/Incline Cables (198)**. Push the excess wire into the Console Mast (12) and **plug the Switch Wire Cap (185)** into each side of the Console Mast (12).
3. **Match the Front Handle Bar Cover (83-L) with the Rear Handle Bar Cover (84-L)** on the left Swing Arm (10) and secure with **three Sheet Metal Screws (126)** using driver (161). Repeat on the right Swing Arm (11) with the **Front Handle Bar Cover (86-L)**, **Rear Handle Bar Cover (85-R)** and three Sheet Metal Screws (126). (The book marks both right covers, including a front one, R-or-L exactly as printed here.)
