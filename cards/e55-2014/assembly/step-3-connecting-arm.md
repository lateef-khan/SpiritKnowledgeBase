**Untie the wire on the Rod End Bearing** and connect the **Left Swing Arm (10) with the Left Connecting Arm (8)**; secure with a **Hex Head Bolt (109)**, **Rod End Sleeve (19)**, **Flat Washer (157)** and **Nyloc Nut (137)** using **Wrenches (159 & 162)**. Repeat for the **Right Connecting Arm (9)** and **Right Swing Arm (11)**.
