1. **Connect the Rail Base Assembly (2) with the Rear Stabilizer of the Main Frame (1)** using **two Hex Head Bolts (107)**, **two Flat Washers (142)**, **two Nyloc Nuts (134)**, and **four Hex Head Bolts (108)** with **four Flat Washers (157)**, **four Spring Washers (158)** and **four Star Washers (151)**. Tighten with **Wrenches (159 & 162)**.
2. **Connect the connecting wires for the incline motor (48 & 49)** by matching the red, white and black color codes.
3. **Guide the Computer Cable (50) through the Console Mast Cover (72) and the Console Mast (12)**, which also goes through the Console Mast Cover (72). Pull the connector out of the Console Mast (12), insert it into the U-Base on the mainframe, and secure with the **Hex Head Bolt (101)** and **Split Washer (155)** on the U-Base plus **two Hex Head Bolts (106)** and **two Curved Washers (156)**. Use **Wrench (159)** to secure the Console Mast (12) to the U-Base. Then slide the Console Mast Cover (72) down.
4. **Plug in the Computer Cable (50), two Handpulse Cables (34 & 35), the Upper Resistance Handle Cable (39), and the Upper Incline Handle Cable (40)** on the bottom of the **Console Assembly (45)**, and secure it on the console holding plate with **four Phillips Head Screws (117)**, tightened with driver (161).
