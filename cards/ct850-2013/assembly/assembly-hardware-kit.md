Three panels of captioned drawings, one per step. **The sizes below were read from rendered pages**; the OCR of this scanned book garbles two of them.

## Step 1

| Item | Part | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 116 | Flat Washer | 3/8" x 35 x 2T | 6 |
| 117 | Split Washer | 3/8" x 16.5 x 2.0T | 6 |
| 134 | Phillips Head Screw | M5 x 12mm | 4 |
| 144 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 3/8" x 3" | 6 |

Three bolts a side, so six - and the panel says six. The 2018 book's step one lists ten of items 116, 117 and 144 for its five-a-side build.

## Step 2

| Item | Part | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 134 | Phillips Head Screw | M5 x 12mm | 4 |

The same screw as step 1's covers, bagged again for the console; eight of item 134 in all.

## Step 3

| Item | Part | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 117 | Split Washer | 3/8" x 16.5 x 2.0T | 6 |
| 125 | Flat Washer | 3/8" x 25 x 2T | 6 |
| 145 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 3/8" x 3/4" | 6 |
| 146 | Socket Cap Bolt | M8 x 12mm | 6 |

**Every count balances**: six bolts and washers on the uprights, four screws on the covers, four on the console, six and six on the brackets and mast tops. There is no step four, so the 2018 book's two 3/8" x 1-3/4" bolts (162) and curved washers (163) are not in this kit.

**The item numbers are the 2018 book's, not the 2016 or 2020 book's.** 116 is a flat washer and 144 a bolt here and in the 2018 book; in the 2016 and 2020 books 116 is a split washer and 144 a Phillips screw. No torque is printed for anything, and the book has no parts list to check a count against.
