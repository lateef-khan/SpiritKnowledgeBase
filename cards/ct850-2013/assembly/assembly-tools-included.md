Three captioned drawings under the heading **Assembly Tools**:

- **#149. 3/8" Allen Wrench**
- **#148. 8mm Allen Wrench**
- **#147. Phillip Head Screwdriver**

**The second tool is an 8 mm Allen wrench, not the 10 mm every later CT800 and CT850 carton lists.** The 2016, 2018, 2020 and 2024 books pack a 3/8" Allen, a **10mm** Allen and a Phillips screwdriver, and their belt chapters then ask for an 8 mm Allen wrench "provided in the parts package" that their tools panel never names. This book's tools panel and its belt chapter agree: the 8 mm wrench that turns the tread-belt tension and tracking bolts is the one in the carton.

The three bolt heads in the build - 3/8" x 3" and 3/8" x 3/4" button head socket bolts, and M8 x 12 mm socket cap bolts - take the 3/8" Allen and the 8 mm Allen; the M5 x 12 mm screws take the screwdriver. The assembly steps never name a tool by item number.

**No carton parts list is printed anywhere in the book**: the pre-assembly page holds the two unpacking items and this panel and nothing else, exactly as the CT800 2012 manual does.
