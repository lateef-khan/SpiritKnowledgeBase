**This is the 2018 build cut to three steps and three bolts a side.** The 2018 CT850 book uses the same part names and numbers - Retaining Plate (98), bolts (144), mounting brackets (3-L, 4-R), console (28), cables A (42) and B/F (41) - but puts five bolts through each upright, leaves them hand-tight until a fourth step, and fits the plastic covers last. This book tightens the three bolts at once and fits the covers in step one. The 2016 and 2020 CT850 books are a different build with different numbers.

## Step 1 - uprights

1. The **Right Upright (34)** has a preinstalled **Lower Computer Cable A (42)**. To install the uprights on the base frame, special care must be taken not to pinch the computer cable between the upright and frame base. Slide the **Split Washer (117)** and **Flat Washer (116)** on the **Button Head Socket Bolt (144)**. Slide the bolt through the top hole in the **Retaining Plate (98)** and the corresponding top hole in the upright tube, and screw into the base frame. Now install the other two Button Head Socket Bolts (144) together with Split Washers (117) and Flat Washers (116) and **tighten all three bolts up**.
2. Repeat this step to install the **Left Upright (33)**.
3. Attach the **Left and Right plastic covers (26-L, 27-R)** with **four Phillips Head Screws (134)**.

## Step 2 - console

1. Connect the **Upper Computer Cable B (41)**, **Hand Pulse Assembly C (37), D (38)**, **Touch Pad/Backlit Cable E (43)** and **Ground Wire F (35)** to the board on the back of the **Console (28)**.
2. Use **four Phillips Head Screws (134)** to secure the console assembly to the **Console Support (5)**.

> **Important Assembly Note.** When assembling the console mounting interface brackets (3 & 4) please be sure to **slide the brackets all the way toward the back of the console before tightening the bolts**. (Printed on its own page between steps 2 and 3, with the caption "Push mounting bracket all the way to the back of the console, then tighten the screws".)

## Step 3 - console on uprights

1. Use **six Socket Head Cap Bolts (146)** to secure the console onto the **Left and Right Mounting Brackets (3-L, 4-R)**. See the photo on the previous page.
2. Connect the **Upper Computer Cable F (41)** to the **Lower Computer Cable A (42)**.
3. Slide the assembly into the left and right uprights.
4. Slide **six Split Washers (117)** and **six Flat Washers (125)** onto **six Button Head Socket Bolts (145)** on top of the uprights. Be careful not to pinch the computer cable.

The build ends there: **no rear bolts, no curved washers, no final tightening step**, where the 2018 book adds a step four with two 3/8" x 1-3/4" bolts through the console masts. Cable 41 is called **B** in step 2 and **F** in step 3, as in the 2018 book. Item 134 is used in step 1 (covers) and step 2 (console), four each, which is why its hardware panel prints four in both steps.
