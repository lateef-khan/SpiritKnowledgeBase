**These are the 60% and 80% figures.** The 2016 and 2018 CT850 books print 60% and **85%** for the
same two programs, and the 2020 book prints **65%** and 80%
(`spirit-ct850-programs-heart-rate-control-60-and-85-percent`,
`ct800-2020-programs-heart-rate-control-65-and-80-percent`). Only the figures differ; the words around
them are the same. **On this book the heart rate training page agrees with the program** - it too
recommends 60% for fat and 80% for the cardiovascular system - so, unlike the 2016 book, there is no
clash to reconcile.

*"Heart Rate (HR) uses your treadmills incline system to control your heart rate. Increases and
decreases in elevation affect heart rate much more efficiently than changes in speed alone. The HR
program automatically changes elevation gradually to achieve the programmed target heart rate."*

During setup you choose the **Weight Control (HR-1)** program, which holds **60% of your Maximum
Heart Rate**, or the **Cardiovascular (HR-2)** program, which holds **80%**. Maximum heart rate is 220
minus your age, and the setting is calculated when you enter your age.

## Programming

1. Press the **HR** button.
2. The window shows "PRESS ENTER TO MODIFY OR START TO BEGIN WORKOUT". Enter opens the settings;
   Start runs the program as it is.
3. "PRESS 1 OR 2 TO SELECT THEN PRESS ENTER" with the dot matrix showing **1**: press **1** or **2** on
   the numeric keypad, then Enter.
4. "ADJUST TIME THEN PRESS ENTER", Time window blinking - Up/Down/Fast/Slow or the numeric keys, then
   Enter.
5. "ADJUST AGE THEN PRESS ENTER", Incline window blinking. **Adjusting age changes the target heart
   rate value.** Enter.
6. "ADJUST BODY WEIGHT THEN PRESS ENTER", Incline window blinking. Enter.
7. "ADJUST HEART RATE THEN PRESS ENTER", Speed window blinking. Enter.
8. "PRESS START TO BEGIN WORKOUT OR ENTER TO MODIFY". Start begins; Stop returns to the previous
   screen.

**"CHECK PULSE" in the message window means no pulse signal is being received and the program cannot
run** - check that the heart rate strap is working. **HR 1 and HR 2 have no warm-up**; the three
minute warm-up belongs to the profile presets.
