**This book has no exploded view and no parts list.** Its table of contents runs Product Registration, Important Safety Instructions, Important Electrical Instructions, Important Operation Instructions, CT850 Assembly Instructions, Operation of Your Console, Programmable Features, Using Heart Rate Transmitter, General Maintenance, Service Checklist - Diagnosis Guide and **Manufacturer's Limited Warranty on printed page 27**, and the warranty is the last printed page before the back cover. The pages between the maintenance section and the warranty are belt tracking, the troubleshooting table, calibration and the engineering-mode menu.

**The absence was checked twice**, because the whole book is a Lexmark scan with no text layer. The contents page was read from a render, since its OCR is noise; and a loose word search over the OCR of all thirty-two pages for `part`, `explod`, `diagram`, `dwg` and `item` finds only "warrants all its treadmill parts", "replacement parts shipped" and "This part is available through most electrical supply" - never a numbered row.

**Do not resolve a number from another CT850 book.** The 2016 and 2020 owner's manuals print a 129-item list (`ct850-2016-specs-parts-list`) and the 2018 book prints none either (`ct850-2018-specs-no-parts-list`); the numbering of the 2016 list is that machine's, and nothing in this book ties a 2013 part to it. To turn a part on a 2013 machine into an orderable number, go to Spirit Fitness with the serial number.

The book also prints no specification table - the 2013 machine's dimensions, weight and motor rating are not in it either (`spirit-ct850-specs-no-specification-table` covers the later books; this one is the same).
