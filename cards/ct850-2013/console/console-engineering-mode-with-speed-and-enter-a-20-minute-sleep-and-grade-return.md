**This menu holds the CT800's items and is entered the CT850-2016 service manual's way.** Its items -
Key Test, Security, Functions, Display Test, with Sleep, Beep, Grade Return, Units, Maintenance and
Pause under Functions - are the CT800-2012/2016 list (`spirit-ct800-console-engineering-mode-menu-with-units`),
but the CT800 books hold Start, Stop and Enter, where this book uses **Speed and Enter**, the gesture
of `ct850-2016-engineering-mode-menu`. The 2018 CT850 menu has a DA Test and no Grade Return
(`ct850-2018-console-engineering-mode-menu-with-da-test`). Do not read one onto another.

**To enter: remove the safety key, press the Speed and Enter keys at the same time, and return the
safety key until "ENGINEERING MODE" is displayed; press Enter again.** Scroll with the
**Up/Down/Fast/Slow** keys, **Enter** runs an item, **Stop** returns to the previous menu selection.

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| a. Key Test | Test all the keys to make sure they are functioning |
| b. Security | Allows the keypad to be locked to prevent unauthorized use |
| c. Functions | Press Enter to access settings |
| d. Display Test | Tests all the display functions |

Under **Functions**, as printed:

| # | Setting | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| i | **Sleep Mode** | Turn on to have the console power down automatically after **20 minutes** of inactivity |
| ii | **Beep** | Turns off the speaker so no beeping sound is heard |
| iii | **Grade Return** | Returns the elevation to lowest setting when pause is pressed - and the line runs straight on into "**Units** (Sets the display to readout in English or Metric display measurements)" |
| iv | **Units** | Set to English or Metric display readings |
| v | **Maintenance** | Resets odometer reading |
| vi | **Pause Mode** | Turn on to allow 5 minutes of pause, turn off to have the console pause indefinitely |

**Units is printed twice**, once glued to the end of the Grade Return line and once as item iv. It is a
typesetting slip on the page, not two settings; there is one Units entry on the console.

**The manual contradicts itself about the sleep time-out.** This menu says **20 minutes**; the console
chapter's TO TURN TREADMILL OFF says the display goes to sleep after **30 minutes** of inactivity
(`ct900ent-sleep-mode-auto-shutoff`). Nothing resolves it; both are reproduced as printed, as they are
for the CT800 and CT850-2018 books.

**Security is one line and no unlock.** The manual prints no Child Lock, no unlock gesture and no
"CONSOLE LOCKED" message. Its two other locks are entered with the numeric keys and the safety key,
not from this menu: the mileage lock (`ct850-2016-mileage-lock`) and the diagnostics screen that
clears the odometer (`ct850-2016-reset-odometer-and-hours`).

**Calibration is not in this menu.** It has its own gesture and its own "FACTORY SETTING" prompt
(`ct850-2013-console-calibration-with-a-20-kph-12-5-mph-maximum-and-an-incline-steps-prompt`).
