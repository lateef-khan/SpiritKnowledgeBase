**Eight steps, a maximum of 12.5 mph, a minimum printed in kph first, and no Grade return step.** The
CT800 and CT850-2018 routine is nine steps with a maximum of **12.0** and a Grade return step
(`spirit-ct800-console-calibration-with-grade-return`); the CT850-2016 owner's manual sets a maximum
of 15.0 (`ct850-2016-console-calibration-minimum-speed-0-5`). Entering another book's figure on this
console is the reason they are kept apart.

1. **Remove the safety key.**
2. **Press and hold down the Incline and Speed buttons and replace the safety key. Continue to hold the
   Start and Fast button until the window displays "FACTORY SETTING", then press Enter.**
3. Set the display to **Metric or English** (Miles vs. Kilometers) with the **Up/Down/Fast/Slow** key,
   then press **Enter**.
4. Make sure the **wheel size diameter is 2.98**, then press **Enter**.
5. Adjust the **maximum speed** (if needed) to **20.0 kph or 12.5 mph**, then press **Enter**.
6. Adjust the **minimum speed** (if needed) to **0.8 kph or 0.5 mph**, then press **Enter**.
7. The Message Window shows **"INCLINE STEPS"**. Adjust the **maximum elevation** (if needed) to **15**,
   then press **Enter**.
8. The Message Window shows **"CALIBRATION ON"**. Press **Start** to begin calibration. The process is
   automatic; **the speed will start up without warning, so do not stand on the belt.** After finishing,
   it returns to Idle Mode by itself.

**Step 2 names two different pairs of keys.** It says to hold **Incline and Speed** while replacing the
key, and then to "continue to hold the **Start and Fast** button" - the CT800 gesture. The page prints
exactly that and nothing resolves it. The CT850-2016 service manual's routine opens with Incline and
Speed; the CT800 books with Start and Fast +. Try Incline and Speed first, since that is what this
book says to be holding when the key goes in, and Start and Fast + if the prompt does not appear.

**The maximum is set before the minimum here**, the reverse of the CT800 order, and the metric figure
is printed first. Neither an adjustable range nor a default is printed for any value.

**When to run it:** the troubleshooting table says "if the belt moves, but stops after a short time and
the display shows 'Low Speed', run calibration"; if the belt never moves and the display shows Low
Speed, contact service. **If calibration does not pass, check the speed sensor alignment** - the
manual prints that procedure directly under this one; it is held with the maintenance cards
(`spirit-ct800-maintenance-speed-sensor-alignment`). The diagnostics screen that shows the software
version and clears the odometer follows it on the same page (`ct850-2016-reset-odometer-and-hours`).
