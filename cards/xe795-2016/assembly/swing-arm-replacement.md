**This is the 2016 book's two-step procedure; the 2023 XE795 book prints a different six-step one** (covers, a
`3/8" x 3/4"` pivot bolt and a wiring disconnect). Same machine name, different arm.

1. Remove the **bushing round end cap** from the **5-way pipe fixing plate** and use an **8 mm L Allen wrench** to
   release the **M8 x 20 m/m socket head cap bolt** and **M10 bushing washer**.
2. Use **12 and 13 m/m wrenches** to release the **5/16" x 1-1/4" hex head bolt**, **5/16" x 20 nyloc nut** and
   **5/16" x 20 flat washer** fixed on the rod end bearing. Separate the swing arm from the rod end bearing, then take
   out the swing arm.

The book prints no reassembly line for this section.
