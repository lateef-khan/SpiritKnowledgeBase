**p. 5, the shrouded machine:** TPR Handle Bar Axle Inner Cover, Console Assembly, Bushing Round End
Cap (twice), Upright Cover (L) and (R), Side Case (L) and (R), Pedal Foam (L) and (R), Round Disk
Cover, Round Disk, Pedal (L) and (R), Slide Wheel Cover, Handgrip End Cap.

**p. 6, the frame:** Swing Arm (L) and (R), Console Holder Assembly, Main Frame (labelled twice),
Drive Belt, **Induction Brake**, Cross Bar, Connecting Arm (L) and (R), Pedal Arm (R), Aluminum
Track, Retaining Bracket, Aluminum Track.

*Upright Cover* is this book's name for the console-mast covers, and *Induction Brake* its name
for the generator flywheel's brake unit that the wiring chapter calls the generator brake. No
pedal arm (L) is labelled. The Outlines chapter prints **no dimensions, no machine weight and no
user weight limit**, and no part numbers. The 2023 book replaces both drawings with numbered
tables (`xe795-2023-specs-outline-numbered-part-names`).
