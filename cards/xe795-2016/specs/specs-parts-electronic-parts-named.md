**2-1 Upper Controllers (p. 8)** - **Speaker**, **Cooling FAN** and **DISPLAY**. No thumb switch is
labelled on this page, though the chapter-6 wiring page shows a LEVEL THUMB SWITCH connector on the
display board.

**2-2 Lower Controller and Driver (p. 9)** - **SPEED SENSOR**, **Driver board** and **HYBRID GENERATOR**,
the flywheel with its coil pack that the Electrical Configurations page calls the GENERATOR BRAKE (working
voltage DC 0.4-14 V).

**This is the only 2016 residential elliptical book with a generator instead of a tension motor.** The
XE195, XE295, XE395 and XG400 name a TENSION MOTOR (the gear motor on a steel rope)
(`spirit-xe395-xe895-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`,
`spirit-xe195-xe295-xg400-2016-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`). The commercial CE900 books name
the same generator, sensor and driver board with a fan and display and no speaker
(`spirit-ce900-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`).

**The 2023 XE795 book labels five parts, not six, and names them differently** - Cooling Fan and Speaker
on the console, Speed Sensor, Induction Brake and Brake Controller below, with no display callout
(`xe795-2023-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`).

**No part number or rating is printed on these pages**; the generator's 6-pin connector and the driver
board's CN2 output are on the specs cards for chapters 3 and 6. The only tool the book lists is a
multi-meter (`spirit-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`).
