**This is the XE795 (2016) list: an M14 rod end, a cross bar at 500-550 lbs, and an M8x15 cap bolt
on the bushing washer. The XE195/XE295/XE395/XE395ENT books print an M12 rod end, a bushing-housing
bolt at 500-600 lbs and the cross bar at 550-600 lbs
(`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-slip-check-four-joints-500-to-600-and-550-to-600-lbs`). The figures
differ; do not mix the two lists.**

"Slipping problems can be verified by the following steps."

| Joint | Check |
|---|---|
| **M14 x P1.75 Rod End Bearing** | The M14 nut on top must be securely tightened with the Pedal Bar Assembly, and the rod end bearing needs to be **vertical** |
| **Cross Bar** | The CAP Socket Head Cap Bolt M8x40 m/m on top of the cross bar must be tightened to **500~550Lbs** |
| **Bushing washer** | The **M8x15 socket head cap bolt** fixed on the bushing washer has to be tightened up |
| **Belt** | "This issue appears quite often, because after a period of time, most belts will become loose from use depending on usage time. Solution will depend on weight and adjustment methods, refer to belt disassembly procedures" |

There is no bushing-housing row on this machine: its pedal arm rides on a 5-way pipe fixing plate
with an M10 bushing washer, not on the bushing housing of the smaller XE models. The torque is
printed as "Lbs" with no unit of length.

The belt adjustment it points to is the hook-screw cap to 190 Hz -
`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-belt-slipping-hook-screw-cap-13-mm-190-hz`. The XE795 (2023) service
manual prints no Slip Problem section at all.
