**HRC on this console controls the incline, not the speed.**

1. Press the **PROGRAM** buttons to select the HRC mode you want and press **Enter**.
2. The preset time is **30:00**, range **05:00 to 99:00**. Press the Fast/Slow keys to modify
   and press **ENTER** to continue.
3. Adjust the **weight** and press Enter.
4. Adjust the **age** and press Enter.
5. Adjust the **target heart rate**.
6. Press **START** to execute the program (the book adds "or press START button to start
   training directly").
7. Press **STOP** to stop the workout, or pull the safety key away to shut down the computer.

**How it regulates:** when the heart rate is **lower** than the target the incline
**increases** to raise the heart rate; when the heart rate **reaches** the target no change is
made; when the heart rate is **higher** than the target the incline **decreases**. **If the
heart rate does not lower and the incline cannot go any lower, the machine stops to protect
the user.**

The book gives no default target heart rate and no percentage; it prints the 220-minus-age
training-zone page separately.
