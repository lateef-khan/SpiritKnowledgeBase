1. The display automatically turns off (goes to sleep) after **5 minutes** with no key operation. In sleep mode the treadmill draws very little current — "about as much as your television when it is turned off".
2. Remove the tether cord.
3. Turn off the main switch on the front of the treadmill, below the motor cover.

Every other 2021-2024 book of this layout prints 30 minutes here ([30-minute card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-display-sleeps-after-30-minutes.md)); the TR300 is the only one that prints 5.
