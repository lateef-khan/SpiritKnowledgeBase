1. While the treadmill is running, press the red **Stop** key once to pause. The tread-belt slowly decelerates to a stop and the **incline goes to zero percent**. The Time, Distance and Calorie readings hold while the unit is paused.
2. To resume, press **Start** while paused; the speed and incline return to their previous settings.
3. If Stop is pressed a **second time**, the program ends and a **workout summary** is displayed.

Unlike the TR150-style books, this one prints no five-minute pause limit and no third press to idle. The TR6.6 and TR6.4 print a longer sequence ([card](../../shared/console/xterra-tr-console-pause-second-stop-summary-third-stop-idle-hold-resets.md)).
