**You must use a wireless chest strap transmitter (not included) to use the heart rate program. Do not use a hand pulse sensor for heart rate control.** There must be a steady intensity signal value in the heartbeat window.

1. Press the **PROGRAM** key to select **HR PROGRAM** and press **ENTER**. **TARGET HR 55 PERCENT** is displayed. Press PROGRAM to switch to **TARGET HR 65 PERCENT**, **TARGET HR 75 PERCENT**, **TARGET HR 85 PERCENT**. Press ENTER to confirm your choice.
2. The Message Center asks for your **Age**. Enter it with the Speed + / - keys, then press Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight** with the Speed + / - keys, then press Enter.
4. Adjust the **Time** with the Speed + / - keys, then press Enter.
5. Adjust the **Heart Rate Target** - the heart rate level you will strive to maintain during the program - with the Speed + / - keys, then press Enter. *The heart rate that appears is based on the % you accepted in step 1; changing this number increases or decreases that percentage.*
6. Press **Start** to begin your workout, or press **Stop** to go back and modify your settings.
7. To increase or decrease the workload at any time during the program, press the **Speed + / -** key on the console or the **right handlebar**. This changes your target heart rate at any time during the program.
8. When the program ends, press **Start** to begin the same program again, or **Stop** to exit the program.

The XT385, XT485 and XT685 present the same four targets as named programs HR1 to HR4, keep you within 3-5 beats of the target, and change the workload from the **Incline** keys on the **left** handlebar instead - see the linked card. The XT185 has no heart rate program at all.
