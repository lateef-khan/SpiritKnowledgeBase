The console overview diagram labels these parts:

- **LCD Window**
- **Adjustable Fan**
- **USB Charging** port
- **Hand Pulse Grip**
- **Cup Holder**
- **Safety Key**
- **Program Buttons**
- **Handrail Lock**
- **Child Lock**
- **Direct Speed & Incline Buttons**

**The diagram labels no handlebar incline or speed controls** - those appear only on the XT385,
XT485 and XT685 panels. The XT185 panel has neither the USB port nor the handrail lock.
