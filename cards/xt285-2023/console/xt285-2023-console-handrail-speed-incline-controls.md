This treadmill **allows you to make speed and incline changes using the side handrails**, and lets
you **turn those handrail controls off** if you frequently hold on to the rails. **When the indicator
light is lit, the handrail switches are disabled**, so you can use the full length of the handrails
without activating the speed or elevation controls.

**The manual gives the disable control two different locations and never reconciles them:**

| Where the manual says it is | Section |
|---|---|
| "the disable button on **the left side of the lower portion of the console**" | Handrail Speed and Incline Adjustments, page 23 |
| "**To the right of the Stop button** there is a Handrail control switch and an indicator light next to it" | Handrail Buttons Disable Switch, page 24 |

The console overview diagram on page 20 labels a **Handrail Lock** in the lower button row. Confirm
the position on the machine before telling a customer where to press.

The XT385, XT485 and XT685 place the equivalent button next to the child lock button - see
`xt-2023-console-handlebar-speed-incline-controls`.
