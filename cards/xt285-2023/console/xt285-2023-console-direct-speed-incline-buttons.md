**You are able to set your speed and incline setting quickly by pressing the buttons on the
console.** Choose the desired speed and/or incline level and the treadmill automatically adjusts to
that level, so you do not have to hold a button down until it reaches the value.

- **Speed: press the Direct Speed key, then 1 through 12.**
- **Incline: press the Direct Incline key, then 0 through 12.**

**The incline range is unique to this machine in the family.** The XT185 stops at incline 10, and the
XT385, XT485 and XT685 reach incline 15. Do not quote their numbers here.
