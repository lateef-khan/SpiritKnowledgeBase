**The 2008 dealer manual prints the XE100 exploded view on its page 15 and this list on pages 16-18.** The list is native text; the drawing is line art with the callout numbers only, rotated on its page, and no drawing stamp was found on it at 300 dpi in any of the four rotations. The columns are headed *Part # / Description / Part #* - the first is the callout number on the drawing, the last is the six-digit Spirit part number - and **there is no quantity column**. Sub-items are written `30~1`, `36~4`; an `X` means the book prints no number for that piece (the handpulse halves and the rear handle bar covers, sold as part of the item above them).

- Items 57 and 58, the left and right connecting arm covers, are bracketed *SAME PART* and both carry 006099. Item 58 is printed `Connecting Arm Cover ®` - the book sets a registered-mark glyph where `(R)` is meant, here and on the XE300, XE400 and XE500 lists.
- Items 13 (Idler Wheel Assembly), 82 (J Bolt) and 98 (Drink Bottle Assembly) have no part number; 98~1, the bottle alone, is 030059.
- Items 106 to 109 are the tools packed with the machine - a 13/14 mm wrench (022596), short and long Phillips screwdrivers (023109, 023091) and a 12 mm wrench (000342) - and item 112 is the lubricant (022271). They are parts, ordered like any other.
- The tilde `˜` printed in front of a size (`˜38×˜31×˜25×17+3T`, `˜72_Slide Wheel`) is where a diameter sign belongs; the book prints it that way on every list.
- Five rows at the end are not on the drawing at all: the Hardware Kit (006005), the Side Decal (006067), the Footplate Replacement Kit (006153), its Locking Pin Bracket alone (006238) and the 5 x 10 plated Phillips screw (000051) that goes with it.

**Order against the serial number.** The same book's parts purchase order treats a line without a serial number as a stock order (`spirit-xt-2007-policy-parts-purchase-order-form`); the owner's manual for this machine prints drawings but no list at all (`spirit-ce-assembly-exploded-view-and-parts-list`), so this is the only place its part numbers are printed. Which elliptical service manuals print a list is on `spirit-elliptical-specs-parts-which-service-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.
