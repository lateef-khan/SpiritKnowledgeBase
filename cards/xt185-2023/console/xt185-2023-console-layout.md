The console overview diagram labels these parts:

- **LCD Window**
- **Adjustable Fan**
- **Hand Pulse Grip**
- **Cup Holder**
- **Safety Key**
- **Program Buttons**
- **Child Lock**
- **Direct Speed & Incline Buttons**

**The diagram labels no USB charging port and no handrail lock**, and this manual never mentions a
USB port, Bluetooth or the Spirit+ app anywhere. The other four machines in this family label more
parts on the same panel.
