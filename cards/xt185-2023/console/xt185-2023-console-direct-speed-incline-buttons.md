**You are able to set your speed and incline setting quickly by pressing the buttons on the
console.** Choose the desired speed and/or incline level and the treadmill automatically adjusts to
that level, so you do not have to hold a button down until it reaches the value.

- **Speed: press the Direct Speed key, then 1 through 10.**
- **Incline: press the Direct Incline key, then 0 through 10.**

**This machine has the lowest preset range in the family.** The XT285 reaches speed 12 and incline
12; the XT385, XT485 and XT685 reach speed 12 and incline 15. Do not quote their numbers here.
