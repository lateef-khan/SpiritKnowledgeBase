**Two ways, and the manual leads with the QR code.** The product registration page in the front matter prints a QR code and says:

> Use your smartphone to scan the QR code above to quickly and easily register your new Spirit Fitness machine. You can also go to **spiritfitness.com/warranty** under the Support tab to register online.

**The deadline is ten days, and it is stated on the warranty page rather than here** - item 2 of RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE FACILITY:

> The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within **10 days** of the original purchase, **or completed online**, to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.

**This manual is the first in the Spirit rower and ergometer set to accept online registration as satisfying the deadline.** The five rower manuals require the card to be *returned to the address listed on the card* within ten days and offer the website separately; this one puts *or completed online* inside the same sentence.

**The three things to have ready** are printed as blank boxes on the registration page - **Serial Number**, **Date of Purchase**, and **Dealer / Place of Purchase**.

**The serial number sticker is on the front stabilizer**, below a barcode, and the manual says to record it *for the purpose of registering your Upper Body Ergometer's warranty*.

**Failing to register is not itself an exclusion on this machine.** The exclusion list reserves only the right to ask for proof of purchase where no warranty record exists - `85ue-2025-warranty-exclusions-nine-items`. That is the water rower position, not the air rower one, where an unregistered product is excluded outright.

**The registration page also carries the book's own version stamp** - *Version: 1.0, Revision: 12.15.25* - which agrees with the December 15, 2025 effective date on the warranty page and with the © 2025 on the back cover. All three dates agree on this manual, which is not true of every Spirit book.
