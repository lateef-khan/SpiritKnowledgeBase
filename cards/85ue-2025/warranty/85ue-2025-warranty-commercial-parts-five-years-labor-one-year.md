**One row, two columns**, under a heading that reads **WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL**:

| Warranty | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial | 5 Years | 1 Year |

The prisons footnote is printed **above** the table on this manual, between the *Commercial Warranty* heading and the column row, rather than beneath it: *\*Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage*.

**There is no frame column.** The manual warrants Parts and Labor and states no period for the frame at all - not Lifetime, not a number. Every Spirit rower manual gives the frame its own column; this one does not. Do not tell a customer the frame is covered for life on an 8.5UE; the book does not say it.

**There is no residential row, and the word "residential" appears nowhere in the manual.** This is a commercial-only warranty. Yet its exclusion 2 still withholds service-call reimbursement for an ergometer "not in ordinary household or **light commercial** use", wording carried over from the residential books - a phrase that names two environments the table has no row for. The manual reconciles nothing. `85ue-2025-warranty-exclusions-nine-items`.

**Five years parts and one year labor is not the rower figure.** The CRW800 commercial rower row reads five years parts and **two** years labor; this one reads one. Read the machine's own table.

**Labor at one year still carries the twelve-and-eighteen-month SERVICE paragraph**, which dates the labor term from the bill of sale or, failing that, eighteen months from factory shipping - the one place in this manual where the two figures do not contradict each other outright, since eighteen months exceeds the one-year column. The manual does not say which governs.
