**Nine items:**

1. **CONSEQUENTIAL, COLLATERAL, OR INCIDENTAL DAMAGES** such as property damage and incidental expenses resulting from any breach of this written or any implied warranty. *Some states do not allow this exclusion, so it may not apply to you.*
2. **Service call reimbursement to the consumer.** Reimbursement to the dealer is also excluded where it does not involve malfunction or defects in workmanship or material, for units beyond the warranty period, for units beyond the service call reimbursement period, for an Upper Body Ergometer not requiring component replacement, or an Upper Body Ergometer not in ordinary household or light commercial use.
3. **Damages caused by anyone other than an authorized Spirit Fitness service company**, by parts other than original Spirit Fitness parts, or by external causes - corrosion, discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, or acts of God.
4. **Products whose original serial number has been removed or altered.**
5. **Products that have been sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party.**
6. **Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase** if no warranty record exists for the product.
7. This warranty is **expressly in lieu of all other warranties** expressed or implied, including merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose.
8. **Product used in prisons and correctional facilities.**
9. **Warranties outside of the United States may vary.** Contact your local dealer.

**Item 2 names two environments the warranty table has no row for.** The table is commercial-only, headed *WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL*, with no residential row and no use of the word "residential" anywhere in the manual - yet this item withholds service-call reimbursement for a machine *not in ordinary household or light commercial use*. The phrase is carried over from Spirit's residential books. The manual never says what it means here, and it is the only place the 8.5UE's environment is restricted apart from item 8. Do not read it as an offer of household cover.

**Not registering is not an exclusion.** Item 6 reserves the right to ask for proof of purchase and nothing more, even though the facility is told at responsibility 2 to return the registration card within ten days: `85ue-2025-warranty-registration-qr-code`. The Spirit air rower manuals do make an unregistered product an exclusion outright; this one does not.

**Prisons are excluded twice** - here at item 8, and again in the footnote printed above the warranty table: `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.

**This list is not the water rower list even though both run to prisons at item 8.** The water rowers stop at eight items and print no outside-the-United-States line; this one adds it at nine. And this list keeps the *not in ordinary household or light commercial use* clause in item 2 that the water rowers drop.

**The 48-contiguous-states paragraph is printed twice in this manual**, once at the foot of this page and once at the foot of the SERVICE page, with one word different: this page reads *does not **include** Alaska or Hawaii*, the SERVICE page reads *does not **apply** to Alaska or Hawaii*. The rule is the same either way.
