Once the machine is assembled and standing on a flat floor:

1. Use a **½" wrench** to **loosen the top nut of the leveler**.
2. **Adjust the levelers by hand** as necessary to remove any wobble.
3. **Tighten the top nut** against the bottom of the stabilizer tube.
4. Make sure the **bottom nut remains cinched against the levelling foot**.

## The page gives no count and no location

It says "the leveler" in the singular in step 1 and "the levelers" in the plural in step 2.
The machine diagram labels **Q. Leveling glides** once, without a count, and no step of the
five-step build fits or adjusts one - they arrive on the machine. See
[the diagram card](85ue-2025-assembly-parts-diagram-labels.md).

## The same book prints a second, different wrench for the same nut

The MACHINE CARE noise item, thirty-three pages later, says the pads are **2, on the bottom
of the front and rear stabilizer**, and to **"use a 14mm wrench (or adjustable wrench) to
loosen the top nut of the levelers"**, then repeats the top-nut and bottom-nut sentences
word for word.

**½" is 12.7 mm and 14mm is not**, and the two pages describe the same nut. **Neither
wrench is named in the build** - the largest tool the steps call for is the 13/14mm (item
612), which opens to 14 but not to 12.7. **An adjustable wrench answers both.** The care
page is also the only page that gives a count, and it is not the page that tells you how to
level the machine.

## The care page is written for five machines at once

Its opening line names **8.0T, 8.0R, 8.0U, 8.5S and 8.5UE** together, and its second remedy
is "the crank arm nut and/or the pedals need to be retightened" - this machine has crank
arms but no pedals. **Take the levelling sentence from that item and nothing else.**

## The Spirit 8.5S stepper prints this page word for word

Its setting-up page carries the same four steps, the same ½" wrench and the same absence of
a count. **That is a different product line and a different machine**; this card and
[the 8.5S card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-85s-assembly-levelling-glides-by-hand.md) hold
the same instruction for two lines that nothing filters apart at retrieval.

**The rehabilitation steppers print a longer version** that counts six glides and sets the
middle pair separately. **Do not borrow the six.** See
[that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-rehab-stepper-assembly-levelling-six-glides.md).

## This is not the sentence most Spirit machines print

The usual one - two pads under the **rear** stabiliser, **14mm**, no top nut - is on
[that card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md), and the 8.5UE
is deliberately not on it.
