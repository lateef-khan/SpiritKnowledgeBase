**6.4.1 Remove the seat back.** As shown in figure 6.4.1, **insert a flat-head screwdriver between the plastic cover and the seat back, then pry it open**. As shown in figure 6.4.2, remove the bolts by turning them counterclockwise.

Install the seat back in reverse order following figures 6.4.1 and 6.4.2.

No bolt count or size is printed. The page also carries a stray heading in Chinese, "6.5 椅背拆除" (seat back removal), between the two steps - the book's own 6.5 is the seat back steel cable. The component drawing names a **Seat Back Cover (13)** and a **Seat Back (16)**.
