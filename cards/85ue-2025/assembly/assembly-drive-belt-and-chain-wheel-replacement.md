**6.15.1 Remove the drive belt and chain wheel.**

1. As shown in figure 6.15.1, use the tool to **loosen the chain wheel**.
2. As shown in figure 6.15.2, use the tool to remove the screws and remove the **sensor sheet metal**.
3. As shown in figure 6.15.3, **remove the drive belt**.
4. As shown in figures 6.15.4 and 6.15.5, use the tool to remove the screws and nuts, then remove the **chain wheel assembly**.

Install the drive belt and chain wheel in reverse order following figures 6.15.1 to 6.15.5.

**No belt tension figure is printed anywhere in the book**, and no screw sizes. The sensor sheet metal carries the Hall sensor that reads the magnet on the drive pulley; the no-revolutions troubleshooting sets its gap at 2 to 3 mm, centred between the two arrow marks, when it goes back. The 8.0U and 8.5R bikes slide their belt out of an idler wheel assembly after two C rings; not this machine.
