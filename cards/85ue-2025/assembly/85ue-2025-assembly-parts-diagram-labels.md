**This is the closest thing the book has to a parts list.** There is no carton checklist
and no tools panel; this diagram is where a part gets its name.

## Four callouts name things no other page explains

- **P, Moving wheels** - named here and nowhere else. No page says how to use them; see
  [that card](85ue-2025-assembly-no-moving-procedure.md).
- **M, Seat carriage stops with assisted grab bar** - the phrase appears once, here.
- **N, Wheelchair anchor** - the operating page adds that it "incorporates a strap that
  secures the wheelchair at the wheel or frame member", and no page says how to fit or use
  the strap.
- **T, UART Port** - for stress testing; the data-transfer chapter uses the USB port, not
  this one.

## Q is the only levelling reference outside the two levelling paragraphs

**Leveling glides**, once, with no count. Two pages of the book tell you how to turn them
and neither says how many there are. See
[the levelling card](85ue-2025-assembly-levelling-glides.md).

## The lettering is not the assembly numbering

The build uses numbers - 095, 173, 508, 605 - and this diagram uses letters. **No page maps
one onto the other.**
