**6.14.1 Remove the chain.** As shown in figure 6.14.1, use the tool to **loosen the chain wheel**. As shown in figure 6.14.2, use the **needle-nose pliers** to remove the **chain fasten** (the chain fastener). As shown in figure 6.14.3, remove the **metal plate** and disengage the **connecting clip** to take off the chain.

Install the chain in reverse order following figures 6.14.1 to 6.14.3.

No tool sizes are printed; needle-nose pliers are on the book's list of eighteen required maintenance tools. **The sound of the chain and flywheel in forward and reverse rotation is normal**, the troubleshooting chapter says. The bikes of the same generation have no chain - a belt runs from the crank pulley - so their cards do not apply.
