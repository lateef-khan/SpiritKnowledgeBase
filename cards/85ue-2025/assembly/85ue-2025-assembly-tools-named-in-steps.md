Five tools, named only inside the steps:

| Item | Tool | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| 174 | L Allen wrench | step 1 |
| 175 | Phillips screwdriver | steps 1, 5 |
| 611 | 8mm Allen wrench | step 2 |
| 612 | 13/14mm wrench | steps 2, 4 |
| 613 | 5mm Allen wrench | steps 2, 3, 4 |

Each step page also draws the tools it uses at the foot of the page, beside their item
numbers.

## There is no TOOLS panel and no PARTS INCLUDED panel

The pre-assembly page says **"Locate the hardware package. Remove the tools first"** and
then never lists a tool. **The book has no carton checklist of any kind** - the table of
contents runs Parts of your unit, Assembly Instructions, Setting up your Ergometer, and
nothing between them counts what is in the box.

**So the manual never states that these five tools are supplied.** It tells you to take
them out of the hardware package, and it names five; that is the whole basis for the list
above. An owner cannot check a delivery against this book.

## Item 174 has no size

Step one calls it only the **"L Allen Wrench"**. Its six screws are **M5 x 5L**, which
takes a 3mm key, and the book never says so. Items 611 and 613 are given sizes; 174 is
not.

## The 13/14mm is not the wrench that levels the machine

The setting-up page calls for a **½" wrench** on the levellers' top nut and the machine
care page calls for a **14mm** on the same nut. **½" is 12.7mm and 14mm is not**, and the
13/14mm named here opens to 14 but not to 12.7. See
[the levelling card](85ue-2025-assembly-levelling-glides.md).

## Nothing here is a torque wrench

No step of this build gives a torque figure; step four says "firmly" twice instead.
