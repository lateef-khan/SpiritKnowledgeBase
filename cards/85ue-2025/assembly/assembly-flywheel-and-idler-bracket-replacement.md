**6.16.1 Remove the flywheel and idler bracket.** As shown in figure 6.16.1, remove the **drive belt**. As shown in figure 6.16.2, use **needle-nose pliers** to remove the **spring**. As shown in figure 6.16.3, use the tool to remove the screws at the red-circled area, then remove the idler bracket.

Install the flywheel and idler bracket in reverse order following figures 6.16.1 to 6.16.3.

No screw counts or sizes are printed, and the flywheel's own fasteners are not named - the heading promises the flywheel and the steps end at the idler bracket. The 8.0U and 8.5R bikes print 1/4" hex head bolts on both sides of their flywheel and M8 nuts on the idler; those figures are not this machine's. The sound of the flywheel in forward and reverse rotation is normal, the troubleshooting chapter says.
