**6.1.1 Remove the electronic console.** As shown in figure 6.1.1, remove **2 pcs of bolts** from the **rear tube cover**. As shown in figures 6.1.2 and 6.1.3, remove **4 pcs** from the back of the console, and then disconnect the cable to remove the console.

Install the console in reverse order following figures 6.1.1 to 6.1.3.

No sizes are printed and the four fasteners at the back are not named. The 8.5S stepper's console comes off the same way (two screws on a rear tube cover, four underneath); the 8.0U and 8.5R bikes lose two M4 screws from the cover sides and four M5 from a console bracket instead. The O-ring under the rotating structure is reached from the console mast, not from here.
