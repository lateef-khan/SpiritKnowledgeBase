**6.9.1 Remove the cantilever steel cable.**

1. As shown in figure 6.9.1, use the tool to remove the **cable tie**.
2. As shown in figure 6.9.2, remove the **steel cable end**.
3. As shown in figure 6.9.3, use the tool to remove the screws, and then remove the steel cable end.
4. As shown in figures 6.9.4 and 6.9.5, use the tool to remove the screws.
5. As shown in figure 6.9.6, remove the plastic cover at the red-circled area, then use a tool to remove the screws. After that, you can open the **ring grip cover**.
6. As shown in figure 6.9.7, use the tool to remove the screws at the red-circled area, and then pull out the steel cable.

Install the cantilever steel cable in reverse order following figures 6.9.1 to 6.9.7.

No screw counts, sizes or cable length are printed; the positions are carried by the red circles in the figures. The **leg-position** cantilever cable is a shorter, separate procedure (6.13) and its own card; the cantilever's gas cylinder and lift adjustment is 6.18.
