**On refit the #197 wave washer goes in before the circular ring, and the eight figures of 6.12 give the order.** The 8.0U and 8.5R bikes pull their crank arms with a puller and torque the nut to 60 N-m; this machine has a retaining sleeve and a flange nut and prints no torque.

**Crank arm (6.10).**

1. As shown in figure 6.10.1, use a **No. 5 hex wrench** to loosen the **flat head socket screw** counterclockwise.
2. As shown in figures 6.10.2 to 6.10.4, use a **13 mm open-end wrench** to loosen the **crank retaining sleeve** counterclockwise, then remove the crank retaining sleeve.
3. As shown in figure 6.10.5, use a **nut remover** to loosen the **M10 flange nut** counterclockwise.

Install the crank arm in reverse order following figures 6.10.1 to 6.10.5.

**Circular ring handle assembly (6.11).** As shown in figures 6.11.1 and 6.11.2, use a **No. 6 hex wrench** to loosen the **M10 button head socket bolt** counterclockwise. Install in reverse order.

**Crank arm assembly steps (6.12).** As shown in figures 6.12.1 to 6.12.8, install in the order shown. **Before reassembling the circular ring, make sure to place the #197 wave washer in first**, then follow the sequence of the eight figures to install the crank arm onto the cantilever.

The preventative-maintenance page checks the circular ring's screws and its smoothness when it rotates: too loose or too tight, tighten or loosen the screws accordingly, the same method on the left and right. The component drawing names the **Cantilever (01)**, the **Circular Ring (02)** and the **Crank Arm (18)**.
