Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. Fastener sizes and counts are
on [the hardware card](85ue-2025-assembly-hardware-by-step.md) - **the manual prints them
only as pictures.**

## Before you start

1. Cut the straps, then lift the box over the unit and unpack.
2. Remove all parts and inspect for damage or missing parts; contact your dealer
   immediately if anything is damaged or missing.
3. **Locate the hardware package. Remove the tools first.**
4. Take the hardware for each step as needed to avoid confusion.
5. **The equipment is heavy. Use care and additional help when unpacking and moving it** -
   failure to do so could result in injury and damage.

## Step one - console articulating arm and console

1. **Connect the connectors (63, 66, 68, 82 lower terminal) to (64, 67, 69, 83)** between
   the console mast and the console articulating arm.
2. **Plug all the connectors (64, 67, 69, 83)** from the top of the articulating arm into
   the back of the console.
3. Attach the **rotating structure assembly** onto the main frame with **6 screws (171)**
   using the **L Allen Wrench (174)**. *If the rotating structure will not move smoothly,
   loosen 4 of the screws (171) by about half a turn to a turn.*
4. Install the **Console (61)** onto the articulating arm and attach the **leather cover
   (198)** with **2 screws (172)** at the **top right and top left**, using the **Phillips
   Screwdriver (175)**. Then fit **2 more bolts (172)** at the **bottom right and bottom
   left** - **the leather cover must not be in between** at the bottom.
5. Attach the **yellow cover (97)** and the leather cover (198) to the articulating arm
   with **2 screws (170)**, Phillips screwdriver.

## Step two - seat back tube, mast cover, bottle holder, arm pad

1. Install the **seat back tube (508)** into the rear of the **seat set (509)** with **2
   bolts (587), 2 flat washers (588) and 2 nuts (610)**, using the **8mm Allen Wrench
   (611)** and the **13/14mm Wrench (612)**.
2. Slide the **mast cover (551)** onto the seat back tube and down onto the main body of
   the seat (553).
3. Attach the **bottle holder (550)** to the right side of the seat by **snapping it into
   the locking sockets** on the bottom of the right support arm (510).
4. Install the **support arm pad** onto the **support arm (536)** with **4 bolts (615)**,
   using the **5mm Allen Wrench (613)**.

## Step three - seat back

1. Install the **seat back (537)** onto the seat back tube (508) with **4 bolts (609)**,
   5mm Allen wrench.
2. Install the **seat back cover (552)** onto the seat back assembly by **snapping it into
   the locking sockets**.

## Step four - seat set onto the main frame

1. Install the **rear seat set to the front main frame** with **2 bolts (606), 2 flat
   washers (608) and 2 nuts (607)**, using **both** the **5mm Allen Wrench (613)** and the
   **13/14mm Wrench (612)**, **firmly**.
2. Install **2 bolts (605) on the right and left side** of the front main frame with the
   5mm Allen wrench, **firmly**. *That is four bolts, and the hardware table says four.*

## Step five - foot rest plate

Attach the **foot rest plate (095)** to the frame with **8 screws (173)**, using the
**Phillips Screwdriver (175)**.

## The build ends here and the setting-up page begins

No step levels the machine, plugs it in or fits the moving wheels. What follows is
**SETTING UP YOUR UPPER BODY ERGOMETER**: plugging in and the power switch, levelling the
glides, and the seat and console adjustments. See
[the levelling card](85ue-2025-assembly-levelling-glides.md).

**Two parts are snapped in rather than fastened** - the bottle holder in step two and the
seat back cover in step three - and neither has a removal instruction anywhere in the book.

**Nothing in the build touches the crank arms, the handles or the arm supports.** They
arrive assembled; the adjustable crank arm and the rotating handles are described on the
operating page instead.
