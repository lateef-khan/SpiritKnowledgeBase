**6.5.1 Remove the seat back steel cable.** As shown in figure 6.5.1, use a tool to loosen the area marked by the red circle, then remove the **cable head and the screw**. As shown in figure 6.5.1, **cut the cable tie**, then use both hands to apply force in the direction of the arrows shown in figure 6.5.2 to remove and replace it.

Install the seat back steel cable in reverse order following figures 6.5.1 and 6.5.2.

No tool, screw size or cable length is printed, and the book cites figure 6.5.1 twice where the second should be 6.5.2. The fore/aft seat cable is the next procedure and starts with an E-clip; the 7.0R bike and the 7.0S / 7.5S steppers back an adjuster out under the release handle instead.
