**6.3.1 Remove the seat pad.** As shown in figures 6.3.1, 6.3.2 and 6.3.3, **remove the screws counterclockwise in order**.

Install the seat pad in reverse order following figures 6.3.1 to 6.3.3.

No screw count or size is printed; the order is carried by the figures. The plastic-cover procedure begins by removing the seat this way. The seat back pad is the next procedure and pries a plastic cover off first.
