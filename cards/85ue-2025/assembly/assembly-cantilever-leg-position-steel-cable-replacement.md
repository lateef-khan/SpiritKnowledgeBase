**6.13.1 Remove the cantilever steel cable (leg position).** As shown in figure 6.13.1, use the tool to remove the **cable tie**. As shown in figure 6.13.2, use the tool to remove the screws and remove the **steel cable end**. As shown in figure 6.13.3, use the tool to remove the screws and remove the steel cable end, then remove the steel cable.

Install the cantilever steel cable (leg position) in reverse order following figures 6.13.1 to 6.13.3.

No screw counts, sizes or cable length are printed. This is the shorter of the two cantilever cables; the arm-position cable (6.9) runs through the ring grip cover and is its own card.
