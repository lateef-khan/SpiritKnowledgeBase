**6.7.1 Remove the seat gas cylinder.** As shown in figure 6.7.1, use a tool to loosen the screw and remove it. As shown in figure 6.7.2, use a tool to loosen the screw and remove it, and then remove the seat gas cylinder.

Install the seat gas cylinder in reverse order following figures 6.7.1 and 6.7.2.

Two screws, no sizes. This is the cylinder under the seat; the cantilever has its own gas cylinder and lift adjustment, the last procedure in the book. The 7.0R bike and the steppers reach their seat cylinder through four cushion screws and a safety cover; not this machine.
