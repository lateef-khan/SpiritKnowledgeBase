**Two people: the machine is tilted onto its side to reach the pedal cover screws.**

1. As shown in figure 6.8.1, remove the seat.
2. As shown in figure 6.8.2, **have someone assist you in tilting the machine to the side**, then use a tool to loosen and remove the screws (**4 pcs in total**) to take off the **pedal cover**.
3. As shown in figures 6.8.3 and 6.8.4, **insert a flat-head screwdriver into the gap where the covers join and pry them open**. Apply some force to release the clips and the locking grooves.
4. As shown in figure 6.8.5, use a tool to loosen and remove the screws (**10 pcs in total**) to take off the **pedal rear shroud**.
5. As shown in figure 6.8.6, **twist the top cover with both hands** to remove it.

Install the plastic covers in reverse order following figures 6.8.1 to 6.8.6.

No screw sizes are printed. The component drawing names a **Front Upper Cover (05)**, **Rear Shroud (06)**, **Front Lower Cover (07)** and **Pedal Cover (09)**; the "pedal" here is the crank handle of the ergometer, item 08. The chain, belt, flywheel and controller procedures all sit behind these covers and none of them says so.
