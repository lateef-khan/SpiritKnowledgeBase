**6.6.1 Remove the seat steel cable.** As shown in figure 6.6.1, **use the C-clip (tool) to remove the E buckle** (the E-clip), then use a tool to loosen the screw and remove it. As shown in figures 6.6.2 and 6.6.3, use a tool to loosen the screw and remove it. As shown in figure 6.6.4, **remove the steel cable head in the direction of the arrow** to take out and replace the cable.

Install the seat steel cable in reverse order following figures 6.6.1 to 6.6.4.

No tool sizes, screw sizes or cable length are printed. The 8.0U parts list for the same seat family carries an **E5 E-clip (592)**. The seat back cable is the previous procedure and has no clip.
