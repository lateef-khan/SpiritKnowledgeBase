**The machine has moving wheels and the manual never says how to use them.**

Callout **P. Moving wheels** on the parts diagram is the only place they are named. There
is no lifting point, no tipping instruction, no folding, no storage page and no transport
section.

## The two sentences that come closest

- **Pre-assembly item 5**: "Our equipment is heavy, use care and additional help if
  necessary when unpacking and moving. Failure to follow these instructions could result in
  injury and damage to the equipment."
- **The application specification**: "Transport and store the 8.5UE at a temperature of
  -20° ... + 50° C."

Neither describes moving the assembled machine across a floor.

## How the absence was checked

*Fold* appears twice in the document, both times inside the word *folder* on the
data-transfer software pages, and never about the machine. *Transport* appears only in the
temperature line above. *Wheel* appears as
flywheel, wheelchair and this one callout. Every page from 13 to 22 - the build and both
setting-up pages - was rendered at 300 dpi and compared against the extraction; none is a
flattened image hiding a panel.

**This is a real absence, not a gap in the extraction.** The hardware tables on the same
pages *are* flat images and were recovered by rendering; a moving procedure is not among
what came back.

## The levelling glides are what the machine stands on afterwards

Whatever you move it with, the glides have to be reset on the new floor. See
[that card](85ue-2025-assembly-levelling-glides.md).
