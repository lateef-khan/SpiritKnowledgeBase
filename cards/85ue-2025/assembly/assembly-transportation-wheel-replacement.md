**6.2.1 Remove the transportation wheel.** As shown in figure 6.2.1, remove the bolt by turning it counterclockwise (the right and left sides are the same method). As shown in figure 6.2.2, **secure one side and loosen the other side** by turning it counterclockwise.

Install the transportation wheel in reverse order following figures 6.2.1 and 6.2.2.

No bolt size is printed. The 8.0U and 8.5R bikes name the fasteners (a button head socket bolt and a nylon nut) and pair the wheel with the adjustment foot in one procedure; this book replaces the wheel alone, and adjusts the **four adjustment feet** only in its wobbling answer. The owner's manual's levelling page is the setting-up card.
