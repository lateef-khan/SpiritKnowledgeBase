**6.18.1 Remove the cylinder and lift adjustment.**

1. As shown in figure 6.18.1, use the tool to remove the **cable tie**.
2. As shown in figure 6.18.2, remove the **steel cable end**.
3. As shown in figure 6.18.3, use a tool to **remove one of the screws and loosen the other so that the bracket can be moved aside**.
4. As shown in figure 6.18.4, use a tool to remove the **axle and the screw**.
5. As shown in figure 6.18.5, use a tool to remove the axle and the screw, then remove the idler bracket gas cylinder and lift adjustment.

Install the cylinder and lift adjustment in reverse order following figures 6.18.1 to 6.18.5.

No sizes are printed. Step 5 calls the part "the Idler Bracket gas cylinder" as printed; it is the cantilever's cylinder - the idler bracket of the drive is 6.16. The seat has its own, separate gas cylinder (6.7).
