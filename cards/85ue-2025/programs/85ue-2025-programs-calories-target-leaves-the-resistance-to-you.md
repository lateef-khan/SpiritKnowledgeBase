*"Calories Target uses your 8.5UE's resistance system to help you achieve your calories target.
Increases and decreases in resistance change the efficiency of burning calories. **You need to change
the resistance manually** and the program ends when the calories consumption reached the target."*

Setup takes two things: the **workload type** and the **program time**.

**This is the one target program the console does not drive for you.** Heart Rate Target and METs
Target both *"automatically change resistance gradually"* to hold their target; Calories Target says
plainly that you change it by hand. See
`85ue-2025-programs-three-workload-types-constant-power-isokinetic-and-constant-resistance` for what
the workload type setting does while you do.

**No default calorie figure, increment or maximum is printed**, and the page states no behaviour for
what happens when the program time runs out before the calorie target is met.
