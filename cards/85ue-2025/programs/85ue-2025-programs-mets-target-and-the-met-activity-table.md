*"METs stands for metabolic equivalent, which is one way that exercise physiologists estimate how
much work is being done during physical activity. This program controls the patient workload to
maintain a steady MET level throughout the exercise session. **Perfect for heart patients who are
prescribed exercise in METs by their cardiologist.**"*

**1 MET** is the amount of energy produced relative to body mass while at rest - sitting and reading
is 1 MET. **1 MET is equivalent to approximately 3.5 millilitres of O2 per kg of body weight per
minute.**

*"METs Target uses your 8.5UE's resistance system help you to achieve your METs target. The program
automatically changes resistance gradually to maintain the METs at target."*

1. Set your **target METs** and **exercise time**.
2. Press **Start** to begin the exercise.

*"Using 1 MET as the reference value, light activities burn up to 3 times as many calories as rest,
moderate activities burn 3-6 times as many and vigorous exercise turns over more than 6 times as much
energy as rest."*

the stepper book, not a guide to what a MET means on this machine - see
