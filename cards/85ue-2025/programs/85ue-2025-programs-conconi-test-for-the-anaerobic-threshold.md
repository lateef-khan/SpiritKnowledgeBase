*"The Conconi test is for measuring subject's anaerobic threshold heart rates and **doesn't require
taking blood samples**. **Heart rate sensor is required and the test should be supervised by the
therapist.**"*

Setup is two values:

- the **resistance increment**, and
- the **period for every stage**.

The screen shows a **test status** panel while the test runs, and **a button ends the test**.

**That is the whole of what this manual prints about the Conconi Test.** It states no warm-up, no
starting resistance, no end condition and no reported result. The 8.0T treadmill's Conconi page gives
a **ten minute self-adjusted warm-up** and says the test ends when the user presses STOP - see
`80t-2026-programs-conconi-test` - but that is a treadmill running on speed increments, not an arm
crank running on resistance increments, and none of its figures may be carried across.

*A page defect, recorded and not repaired: the screenshot printed beside the Conconi Test text is a
**Steep Ramp Test** screen. Its title bar reads "Steep Ramp Test" and it carries that test's
"Endure the longest time possible at pedaling speed over 60 rpm" prompt. The captions beneath it -
"Set the resistance increment and period for every stages", "Click this button to end the test" -
belong to the Conconi Test. The picture is only visible in a render; the native text layer of the page
carries no screen labels at all.*
