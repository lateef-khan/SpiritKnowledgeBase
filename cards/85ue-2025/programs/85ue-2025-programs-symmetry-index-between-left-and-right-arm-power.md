*"The Symmetry mode may aid in achieving a more balanced pedaling stroke for patients with **upper
limb** deficiencies, such as neurological patients and **post-op shoulder** patients."* It measures
left and right power through the cycle range and displays the watt measurements on screen, with a
graph, a watt value and a symmetry index.

**The Symmetry Index is the difference in percentage between the left and right arm power. If the
left and right arms produce the same power the Symmetry Index will be 0%.**

The worked example: the screen shows the **right arm producing more power than the left, 120 vs. 85
watts**, and displays the higher wattage of the right arm.

## For best results

**The Symmetry program starts at level 1 and the resistance needs to be increased manually.** *"Make
sure to set the resistance to a level where the patient is doing enough work to generate a meaningful
measurement. It is recommended to set the resistance as high as the patient can perform without
discomfort, but low enough so they can complete full pedal revolutions. **Very low resistance
settings result in erratic or inconsistent measurements.**"*

**Note: the watt readings are not force measurements.** *"The Symmetry program employs a proprietary
algorithm using a power table and velocity calculations to generate the watt readings; they are not
from direct force measurements."*

**Symmetry is a Features page item, not one of the thirteen programs**, and it is reached from the
workout screen - the page footer reads "Click to show symmetry page". See
`85ue-2025-programs-thirteen-programs-on-the-touchscreen`.

**The 8.5S stepper prints this page almost word for word about legs**, with the same 120 vs. 85 watt
example and a screenshot showing a Symmetry Index of 98% - see
`85s-2025-programs-symmetry-index-and-watt-measurement`. The 8.5UE page is about arms and shoulders;
do not answer one machine from the other.
