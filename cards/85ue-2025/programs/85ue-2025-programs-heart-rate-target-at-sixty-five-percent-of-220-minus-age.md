*"Heart Rate Target uses your 8.5UE's resistance system to control your heart rate based on
information from a heart rate sensor (not included). Increases and decreases in resistance affect
heart rate efficiently. **The program automatically changes resistance gradually to achieve the
programmed target heart rate.**"*

**The default target is:**

```
MHR = (220 - age) x 65%
```

*"Your maximum heart rate is based upon a formula that subtracts your age from a constant of 220.
Your target heart rate setting is automatically calculated during the setup mode when you enter your
age and you can adjust from there."*

On the setup screen:

- A **switch changes the target heart rate setting between BPM and % of the user's maximum heart
  rate**, so the same target can be entered either way.
- Set the **target heart rate** and the **program time**, then **Start**.

**65% is a program default, not a training recommendation.** The 8.5UE manual prints **no maximum
heart rate chapter, no training zone, no Target Heart Rate Training Chart and no RPE scale** - the
whole book was searched for *Borg*, *perceived*, *exertion* and *training zone* and returns nothing,
and its only other mention of 220 is a part number and a CR1220 battery type. Do not quote 65% as
the zone to train in.

**The 8.5S stepper prints the same page with the same formula** - see
`85s-2025-programs-heart-rate-target-defaults-to-sixty-five-percent-of-220-minus-age` - and **the
8.5S-Fit defaults differently**, 65% *or* 85% with a five-beat tolerance. The console family is
shared; the figures are not interchangeable across machines.
