The Programs Setup page lists **thirteen**:

- **Manual** - *"allows you control the workload manually yourself, not controlled by the
  computer."* Choose the workout time, press Start.
- **Timer** - *"a quick set-up, large font, egg timer type display for patient warm-ups."* Set a
  target exercise time and press Start; **+** and **-** adjust the session time during the workout.
- **Hill** and **Plateau** - the two **Pattern** programs. *"Pattern programs are preset exercise
  programs that have been designed for a variety of goals. The initial built in level of difficulty
  is set to a relatively easy level. You may select the level before beginning."* Setup takes a level
  of difficulty, a workload type and a workout time, and **the unit of measurement can switch between
  RPM and deg/sec.**
- **HIIT**
- **Wingate Test**
- **Facility** - build and save a custom program under your facility's name, or save any preset you
  have completed as one. Set the resistance type, max level and total time, then touch each segment
  to shape the profile and Save.
- **Heart Rate Target**
- **Calories Target**
- **METs Target**
- **Conconi Test**
- **Steep Ramp Test**
- **Power Distribution**

**"When a program is selected you have the option of modifying the settings. If you want to begin
without entering new settings, just press the start key."** Skipping data entry uses the data from
the **set up menu**.

**Symmetry is not one of the thirteen.** Like the workload types it sits on the Features pages and is
reached from the workout screen - see
`85ue-2025-programs-symmetry-index-between-left-and-right-arm-power`.

**This is not the 8.5S stepper's list**, even though the two books are near-twins. The 8.5S has
**eleven** programs: it carries **Steps Target**, which an arm crank cannot measure, and it has **no
Conconi Test, no Steep Ramp Test and no Power Distribution program**. See
`85s-2025-programs-eleven-programs-on-the-touchscreen`.

**Measured, not assumed.** Compared as 8-word phrase shingles over the two program chapters, the
8.5UE and the 8.5S share **76.6%** of the 8.5S's phrases - the same book with *leg* changed to *arm*
and three tests added. That closeness is exactly why the two program lists must not be quoted for
each other.
