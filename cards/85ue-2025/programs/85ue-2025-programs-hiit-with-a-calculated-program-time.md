*"The HIIT (High, Intensity Interval Training) program takes advantage of the latest trend in
wellness. During the program you will perform short bursts of high intensity sprinting followed by
short rest periods."* *(the stray comma in "High, Intensity" is printed)*

*"HIIT is a highly customizable interval training program. You can select the number of intervals,
time of each interval Sprint and Rest periods and the work intensity of the levels."*

1. **Set the resistance level for sprint and rest.**
2. **Click Next for next step.**
3. **Set the sprint time, rest time and how many intervals you like.**
4. **Program time calculate automatically.** *(printed as shown)*

**Levels come first on this machine, times second** - the reverse of the order most Spirit HIIT pages
use.

**No default and no range is printed for any of the four settings.** There is no stated sprint level,
rest level, sprint time, rest time or interval count, and **no warm-up and no cool-down are
mentioned at all**. Spirit's treadmill and bike HIIT pages do print those figures - 20 to 90 second
segments, eight intervals, a three minute warm-up - and **none of them may be quoted for this
machine**, which prints none.

**The total program time is not something you set.** The console works it out from the sprint time,
the rest time and the interval count.
