**A boxed WARNING of six bullets, addressed to whoever is supervising the user rather than to the
user.**

> **! WARNING!**

- **Never use this equipment during an electrical storm.** Surges may occur in your facility power
  supply that could damage this equipment's components.
- **All users should have medical clearance before starting any rigorous exercise program.** This is
  especially important for persons with a history of heart disease or other high risk factors.
- **The user should adjust the seat, crank arm length and the arm angle** to a position that is
  comfortable during exercise.
- **Start the user at a safe exercise level.** Do not allow the user to be over exerted. Symptoms to
  watch for, but not limited to, are: shortness of breath or difficulty in breathing, pain or
  discomfort, feeling faint.
- **Make sure the user warms up and cools down properly** to avoid over taxing the cardio vascular
  system. Allow **three to five minutes** of warm up and cool down during each exercise session.
- The contraindication list, carded separately
  (`85ue-2025-safety-who-may-not-use-the-ergometer`).

> SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!

**The electrical storm bullet is about the machine, not the patient.** The risk named is damage to
the components from a supply surge, not shock. The manual gives no other surge protection advice and
names no surge protector.

**Three to five minutes is the figure to quote**, for the warm up and again for the cool down, each
session.

**Three things are adjusted before a session** - the seat, the crank arm length and the arm angle -
and safety item 18 adds `To avoid injury please observe all minimum and maximum adjustment
settings`. The settings themselves are in the setting-up chapter, not here.

**The contraindications are printed in the same box.** They are the sixth bullet of this WARNING and
are the machine's clinical exclusions - severe osteoporosis, non-union fractures, debilitating
dizziness, poor safety awareness or cognition, a patient over 440 lb, and acute conditions including
BP over 180/110 Hg. A reader who takes only the five bullets above has half the page
(`85ue-2025-safety-who-may-not-use-the-ergometer`,
`85ue-2025-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lbs`).

**The Spirit 2026 treadmills print a similar block and are not this machine.** Their medical
clearance and exertion card covers the 70T, 80T and the MT200 rehabilitation treadmills
(`spirit-2026t-safety-medical-clearance-and-exertion`); the wording overlaps but the electrical
storm bullet, the crank arm adjustment and the contraindication list are the 8.5UE's own.
