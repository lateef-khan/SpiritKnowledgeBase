**440 lb, and this manual is the only one in the Spirit range that prints its weight limit four
times. All four agree.**

| Where | Printed as |
|---|---|
| SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS, item 23 | `User Weight Limit: 440 lbs.` |
| APPLICATION SPECIFICATION, intended patient population | `Maximal patient's weight is 440 lb.` |
| IMPORTANT OPERATION INSTRUCTIONS | `Do not use for patients weighing greater than 440 lb (bariatric).` |
| SPECIFICATIONS, printed page 55 | `Patient weight capacity 200 kgs (440 lbs)` |

**The operation instructions turn the figure into a contraindication.** Where every other Spirit
manual states a limit and says nothing about exceeding it, this one lists a patient above 440 lb
among the conditions that rule the machine out, alongside severe osteoporosis and acute fractures
(`85ue-2025-safety-who-may-not-use-the-ergometer`). Treat 440 lb here as a clinical exclusion, not
as a recommendation.

**200 kgs is the manual's own rounding.** 440 lb is 199.6 kg and 200 kg is 441 lb; the specification
page prints the pair as `200 kgs (440 lbs)` and neither figure is a separate limit.

**Do not answer from the other Spirit 440 lb cards.** Two other Spirit families print the same
number and neither covers this machine: the 2026 4.0T, 7.0T and 8.0T treadmills and the MT200
rehabilitation treadmills (`spirit-2026t-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lbs`), and the 7.0S, 7.5S,
8.5S and 8.5S-Fit steppers, which print it again as `200 kgs` in their specification table
(`spirit-climber-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lbs`). The 8.5UE is neither a treadmill nor a stepper.

**No Spirit rower shares this figure.** The rowers print 450 lb, 350 lb, 330 lb or nothing at all;
never quote 440 lb for one (`spirit-rower-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lb`,
`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`,
`crw900-2021-safety-user-weight-limit-contradiction`).
