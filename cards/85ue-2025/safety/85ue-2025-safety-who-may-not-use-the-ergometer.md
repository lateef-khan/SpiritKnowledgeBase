**This machine has printed contraindications. No other Spirit product does.**

> 8.5UE should not be used for patients with severe osteoporosis, non-union fractures, debilitating
> dizziness, or poor safety awareness/cognition. Do not use for patients weighing greater than
> 440 lb (bariatric). Do not use for patients with acute conditions such as pulmonary embolus,
> thrombus, acute MI, acute fractures, or BP over 180/110 Hg.

| Ruled out | |
|---|---|
| Severe osteoporosis | |
| Non-union fractures | |
| Debilitating dizziness | |
| Poor safety awareness / cognition | |
| Weight over **440 lb** (bariatric) | (`85ue-2025-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lbs`) |
| Acute pulmonary embolus, thrombus, acute MI, acute fractures | named as examples of acute conditions |
| **BP over 180/110 Hg** | the manual's own unit, printed as `Hg` |

**`180/110 Hg` is what the manual prints.** The unit is normally mmHg; quote the figures and the
manual's wording rather than correcting it silently.

**The list of examples is not closed.** `acute conditions such as` means the four named are examples,
so a clinician's judgement governs anything not on the list. The manual does not say what to do in
a borderline case.

**Who the machine is for, from its APPLICATION SPECIFICATION:**

> This product is intended to be used to redevelop muscles, restore motion to joints, in aiding in
> the physical rehabilitation process for patients with orthopedic and neurological problems. Also
> used in sports medicine, wellness and general conditioning programs.

- **Male and female adults.** Children only at the discretion of a qualified physical therapist
  and/or with parental consent.
- **The patient must be ambulatory.**
- **The patient should have medical clearance before starting any rigorous exercise program**,
  especially with a history of heart disease or other high risk factors.
- **Open kinetic chain exercise only**: the patient is *not restrained or connected to* the
  equipment.
- **Contact site: hands, feet and trunk**, and the condition should not have any trauma.
- **Intended for hospital, clinic and home use, and research in academic institutions.**

**A wheelchair user is an intended user.** The machine's removable seat rolls away for direct
wheelchair access, its swivel seat turns 180 degrees in both directions to help move a patient onto
it, and a wheelchair anchor strap secures the chair at the wheel or frame member. Those are features
described elsewhere in the book; the safety chapter never restricts use to the seat.

**This is the opposite of what the Spirit water rowers say.** The CRW900 and CRW800H2O manuals print
`Note: Rower is not suitable for therapeutic purposes`
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-not-suitable-for-therapeutic-purposes`). Both are seated pulling
machines from the same brand and only one is a rehabilitation device. Never answer a clinical
question about one from the other's cards.

**The ordinary Spirit capability bullet is not printed here.** `This appliance is not intended for
use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities` appears in the rower and
elliptical manuals (`spirit-ce-safety-who-may-use-the-elliptical`) and **not in this book** - which
is consistent, since reduced capability is what this machine is bought for. Its own list above is
the exclusion.
