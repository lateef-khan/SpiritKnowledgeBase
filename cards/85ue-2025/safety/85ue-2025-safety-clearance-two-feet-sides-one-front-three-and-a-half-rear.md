> Ensure there is a minimum space on the sides of this equipment of two feet for proper operation,
> easy access and to prevent possible injuries to others standing or walking nearby. There should be
> a minimum of at least one foot of free space at the front and three and a half feet at the rear.

| Side | Minimum free space |
|---|---|
| Each side | **2 ft** |
| Front | **1 ft** |
| Rear | **3 ft 6 in** |

- **These are minimums**, in the manual's own words, and the reason given for the side figure is
  access and the safety of people standing or walking nearby.
- **No metric conversion is printed** and no ceiling height or room size appears with them.
- **The rear figure is the largest** because the seat carriage and the wheelchair approach are at
  the back of the machine.

**The machine's own footprint is on the specification page**, not here: 170.8 cm (67.2 in) long by
76 cm (29.9 in) wide by 167.5 cm (65.9 in) high. Add the clearances to those to size a room; the
manual never does the sum for you.

**A wheelchair user approaches from the rear.** The removable seat rolls away for direct wheelchair
access and the wheelchair anchor strap secures the chair, so the three and a half feet behind the
machine is working space rather than a margin
(`85ue-2025-safety-who-may-not-use-the-ergometer`).

**Two Spirit treadmill cards print the same three figures and do not answer for this machine.** The
70T-2026, MT200-2010 and MT200-2022 manuals give two feet at each side, one at the front and three
and a half at the rear (`70t-2026-safety-clearance-around-the-treadmill`) - the same numbers for a
machine with a belt running behind the user. Read the figure off the book for the machine in front
of you.

**The Spirit rowers say something different or nothing.** The two water rowers ask for
`an area no less than 2ft kept clear around the rower` with no directional split
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-two-feet-of-clearance`), and the four air rowers name no clearance at all
(`spirit-ce-safety-no-placement-or-clearance-requirement`).
