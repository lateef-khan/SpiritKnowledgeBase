**This is a medical device's climatic specification, in metric, with five figures instead of one.**

| Limit | Figure |
|---|---|
| Temperature, in use | **+10 to +36 degrees C** |
| Relative humidity | **30 to 90 %**, non-condensing |
| Air pressure | **700 to 1060 mbar** |
| Maximum operating altitude | approx. **10,000 feet (3000 m)**, without pressurization |
| Transport and storage temperature | **-20 to +50 degrees C** |

The heading over the block reads:

> If not stated otherwise **Dyaco MED products** are designed for operation in normal climatic
> surroundings (IEC 60601-1)

**`Dyaco MED` is the OEM's name left in a Spirit-branded manual**, the same printing defect the 8.5S
stepper book carries. It is not evidence of a second product.

The same page adds three absolute exclusions:

> Never expose this equipment to rain or moisture. This equipment is not designed for use outdoors,
> near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.

> This equipment is not protected against the ingress of water or particulate matter.

> This equipment is not suitable for use in an oxygen rich environment.

**The figures are printed twice and the second printing adds the room it is used in.** The
APPLICATION SPECIFICATION page repeats `Temperature range: 10°C ~ 36°C` and
`Relative humidity range: 30% R.H. ~ 90% R.H., non-condensing` under INTENDED CONDITIONS OF USE, and
adds `standard ambient room lighting is sufficient`, a viewing distance of **1 m** and a viewing
angle of **120°**. Both halves are the same fact; a reader who finds only one has the temperature
but not the lighting.

**Do not answer from the Fahrenheit card.** The Spirit XRW600 and CRW800 rowers give
`40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit, and humidity is 95% non-condensing`
(`ct900ent-operating-environment-voltage`) - a wider band, a higher humidity ceiling and no pressure
or altitude figure. 10 to 36 C is 50 to 97 F, so this machine is the stricter by a wide margin at
both ends.

**The two Spirit water rowers state nothing at all.** Their manuals print no electrical page and no
temperature or humidity figure anywhere
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-four-aa-batteries-and-no-electrical-page`). That is an absence, not a
figure to fill from here.

**The Spirit 7.0S, 7.5S, 8.5S steppers and the MS300 print the identical block**
(`spirit-climber-safety-operating-environment-iec-60601`) - the same Dyaco MED platform, a different
product line. The figures agree; quote this card for an ergometer.
