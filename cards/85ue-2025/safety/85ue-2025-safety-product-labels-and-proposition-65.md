**Two labels, and this manual is one of the few Spirit books that says where each one is.**

| Label | Where the manual says it is | What it reads |
|---|---|---|
| PROPOSITION 65 WARNING | `on the shroud near the plug-in` | `WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm - www.P65Warnings.ca.gov` |
| SERIAL NUMBER STICKER | `on the front stabilizer` | a barcode with the number printed below it, and the Spirit wordmark; the specimen part number beside it is `935-1107` |

> **PROPOSITION 65 WARNING** - This sticker will be found on the shroud near the plug-in. The State
> of California requires us to inform you that this unit was manufactured using chemicals that could
> cause harm with improper use.

> **SERIAL NUMBER STICKER** - This sticker will be found on the front stabilizer. Please record the
> number below the barcode for the purpose of registering your Upper Body Ergometer's warranty.

**It is the number *below the barcode* that gets recorded**, not the barcode itself. The
registration block on the previous page provides ruled boxes for the serial number, the date of
purchase and the dealer, and a QR code to spiritfitness.com/warranty.

**This manual names no chemical.** Every Spirit rower, bike, treadmill and elliptical manual that
prints a Proposition 65 box names **Toluene and Acrylamide**
(`xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`); this book replaces that with `manufactured using chemicals
that could cause harm with improper use` and lists none. Do not supply the two chemical names for an
8.5UE - they are not in its book.

**There are only two labels.** No exercise warning sticker, no keep-clear-of-moving-parts decal, no
made-in country sticker and no class or weight decal appears on the page. The 2024 black-unit
manuals show three labels and the CRW900 water rower shows three of its own
(`crw900-2021-safety-warning-labels-and-serial-decal`).

**No certification mark is shown on any label.** The machine's standards are printed as a
CERTIFICATIONS block on its specification page instead - a genuine list, unlike the Spirit rowers,
whose manuals carry none (`crw800-2024-safety-no-compliance-page`).

**Four Spirit rowing and pulling machines put the serial number in four different places**, so never
carry one across:

| Machine | Serial number |
|---|---|
| 8.5UE | front stabilizer |
| CRW900-2021, CRW800H2O | centre floor tube behind the crank (`spirit-water-rower-safety-serial-number-on-the-centre-floor-tube`) |
| XRW600-2019, XRW600-2021 | shown only as an unlabelled arrow in a drawing (`spirit-ce-safety-serial-number-location`) |
| CRW800-2021, CRW800-2024 | not stated at all |

**The page's text is in the PDF's text layer but the stickers are pictures.** Printed page 3 returns
70 native words against 92 from a 300 dpi render; the decal wording above was read from the render.

**The manual dates itself on this spread.** The registration block prints `Version: 1.0` and
`Revision: 12.15.25`, which agrees with the December 15, 2025 warranty effective date.
