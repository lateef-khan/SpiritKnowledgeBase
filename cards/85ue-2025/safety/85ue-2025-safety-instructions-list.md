**This is a medical device's safety list, and it is the longest in the Spirit range.** It runs to 23
numbered items against 13 on the CRW800 rower and 14 or 15 on the XRW600.

> To reduce risk of injury to persons: Read all instructions before using this appliance.
>
> **! WARNING - To reduce the risk of electric shock,** disconnect your 8.5UE from the electrical
> outlet prior to cleaning and/or service work.
>
> Read all instructions before using this appliance.

| 2 | To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons, install this equipment on a flat level surface with access to a 100 to 240-volt AC, 50/60 Hz, 15-amp grounded outlet. Do not use an extension cord unless it is 16awg or larger, with only one outlet on the end. This equipment should be the only appliance in the electrical circuit. Do not attempt to disable the grounded plug by using improper adapters, or in any way modify the cord set; a serious shock or fire hazard may result along with computer malfunctions. |

Beside the list is the boxed note the 2024 and 2025 Spirit manuals all carry:

> Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of
> your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine.

**Item 20 is the safety item unique to this machine.** The flywheel has **no freewheel** and is
directly coupled to the handles, so the cranks keep turning while the flywheel is turning - a user
cannot simply stop pulling and let it coast. Nothing else in the Spirit range prints this sentence.

**Item 3 is a medical-device requirement**, not the ordinary grounded-outlet sentence: the supply
main must have *protective earth*. Its grounding page repeats the rule at length
(`spirit-product-must-be-grounded`), and its outlet figure is carded separately
(`85ue-2025-safety-outlet-100-to-240-volt-50-60-hz-15-amp`).

**Items 16 and 17 restrict who may touch the machine.** Servicing beyond this manual, and
installation and assembly, are for trained personnel only. No rower manual in the range says this.

**Item 13 says heart rate monitoring may be inaccurate and that over exercise may result in injury
or death.** The Spirit rower manuals instead say a chest strap *is* an accurate method; this book
takes the opposite position.

**The list does not say who may not use the machine.** That is printed two pages later, in the
operation instructions and the application specification, as a set of contraindications
(`85ue-2025-safety-who-may-not-use-the-ergometer`). A reader who stops at this list will miss it.
