The 8.5UE schedules by **period**, and its weekly row is written in facility terms — *Weekly / Daily
(Daily member Use)* — because this is a rehabilitation machine used by many people a day.

| Period | Task | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **After Each Use** | General cleaning | Use a soft, damp cloth or paper towel to clean the console, and to wipe down the equipment surface, main body cover, **handles, cushion, seat back** and frame. |
| **Weekly / Daily (Daily member Use)** | Functional & safety checking | Ensure the equipment is functioning properly. Check around the equipment and **inspect the crank arm with handles for loosening**. Wipe the full surface and console clean with a soft, damp cloth or paper towel. |
| **Monthly** | Foot Rest Plate cleaning | No cleaners or abrasives. A mild soap and water solution with a **nylon scrub brush** to clean the top of the textured plates (**10:1 water to soap**). |
| **Monthly** | Handles checking | **Check the handles for tightness and wear.** Check the cushion, seat back and handles for signs of wear. |
| **Quarterly** | General cleaning, Foot Rest Plate cleaning, Functional & safety checking | Do a round of the above tasks. |

**There is no yearly row and no cover-off task.** The 8.0T rehabilitation treadmill's table ends with
a yearly motor-cover vacuum; this machine's ends at quarterly
(`80t-2026-maintenance-cleaning-schedule`).

## The notes still say "pedals"

The Foot Rest Plate row's own note asks you to clean "the top of the textured **pedals**", and the
cleaning instructions above the table say the same. **An 8.5UE has no pedals** — it is an arm-crank
ergometer, and the parts the row means are the **foot rest plates** its own task name gives. The
task name is right and the note is a carry-over from the stepper book.

## Read it with the three cleaning instructions above it

The table sits under three numbered steps: switch the machine off at the mains and unplug it, use
**water only** with a soft damp cloth on all surfaces and most-touched points, and use the **10:1**
soap solution with a nylon brush on the textured plates, letting them dry before use. Those, and the
nine warnings on the next printed page, are `80t-2026-maintenance-cleaning-cautions`. The
seven-step disinfection routine on the previous page is
`80t-2026-maintenance-disinfection-procedure`.

## Not the 8.5S table

The 8.5S stepper prints a table of the same shape with different rows — *Pedals cleaning*, *Pedals
checking*, a **handlebar arm and footplates** tightness check, and a **tread belt cleaning** task on
a machine with no tread belt (`spirit-85s-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`). Do not read one
table's rows onto the other machine.
