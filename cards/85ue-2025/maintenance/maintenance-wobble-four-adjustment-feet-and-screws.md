**Four feet, then screws.** 7.1.1 Machine Adjustment, as printed:

1. "As shown in Figure 7.1.1, **adjust the height of the 4pcs adjustment foot until the unit is balanced**."
2. "As shown in Figure 7.1.2, and 7.1.3, **check these screws are tightened**." The figures point at the screws;
   the text names no assembly - read them from the page.

**The service manual counts four feet and gives no wrench.** The owner's manual's care page, carded with the
8.5S, says two leveling pads under each of the front and rear stabilizers - the same four - turned with a
**14 mm** wrench on the top nut (`spirit-85s-maintenance-pedals-and-front-and-rear-stabiliser-pads`). The counts
agree; only this book leaves the spanner out.

The screws of step 2 are the ones the preventative chapter looks at on every visit
(`85ue-2025-maintenance-preventative-check-screws-on-pedal-circular-ring-rail-o-ring-and-seat-carriage`).

**The only other troubleshooting item is a noise**, and it is not a fault
(`85ue-2025-maintenance-chain-and-flywheel-noise-in-both-directions-is-normal`). The book has no belt-slip
section and no tension figure. The 8.0U and 8.5R bikes split sway three ways - handgrip, seat, whole bike
(`spirit-med-bike-maintenance-sway-handgrip-seat-and-whole-bike`); this book has the one row.
