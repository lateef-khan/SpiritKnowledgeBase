The first sentence of the machine care page reads:

> Regular cleaning and maintenance are essential to keep your **Dyaco** equipment (**8.0T, 8.0R,
> 8.0U, 8.5S, 8.5UE**) operating for their best for lasting years.

**This is the right manual.** The 8.5UE is the last model in its own list. **Dyaco International** is
the manufacturer named on the 8.5UE's own specification page as well, so the sentence is the
factory's wording rather than a mistake about the machine.

**The other four are not this machine** and their figures must not be quoted from this book: the
8.0T is a rehabilitation treadmill, the 8.0R and 8.0U are bikes, the 8.5S is a stepper.

## The 8.5S book prints a different list

The 8.5S 2025 and 8.5S-FIT 2026 manuals print the same sentence with **8.0T, 8.0U, 8.5R, 8.5S,
8.5UE** — an **8.5R** where this book has an **8.0R**
(`spirit-85s-maintenance-care-page-names-dyaco-machines`). The two lists disagree by one model id,
and the rest of the two pages are identical at difflib word level 0.996. **Neither book is evidence
that a machine of the other name exists**; quote the list only as the page prints it.

## The rest of the page is shared

Everything below this sentence — the noise checks, the levelling pads, the disinfection steps — is
word for word the 8.5S text and is carded with it:
`spirit-85s-maintenance-pedals-and-front-and-rear-stabiliser-pads` and
`80t-2026-maintenance-disinfection-procedure`. Only the care schedule table differs
(`85ue-2025-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`).
