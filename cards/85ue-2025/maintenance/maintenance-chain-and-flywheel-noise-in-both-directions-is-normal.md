7.2.1, as printed: "During use, **the sounds generated by the chain and flywheel during forward and backward
rotation are normal**."

That is the whole of the noise section. **Do not replace a part for it, and do not oil it** - the book names no
lubricant for the chain or anything else.

**Two differences from the bikes' version of this sentence.** The 8.0U and 8.5R service manuals say the flywheel
sound is normal *when pedaling in reverse* only, and follow it with a foot-feeling remedy - tighten the crank arms
and pedals (`cu900ent-noise-and-feet-feeling`). This machine has a **chain** as well as a belt, names both as
sources, and says **both directions** are normal; and it prints no rough-feeling remedy at all.

A noise that comes with the machine rocking is the other troubleshooting item
(`85ue-2025-maintenance-wobble-four-adjustment-feet-and-screws`).
