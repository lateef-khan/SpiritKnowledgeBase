**Three radios and two wired ports, each with its own job.**

| Link | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Wi-Fi** | **Upload data to the cloud** |
| **BLE** | **FTMS protocol for 3rd party apps** |
| **BLE and ANT+** | **Connect with Heart Rate sensor** |
| **USB Type B port** (call-out U) | **Stress Testing & Data Transfer** |
| **UART port** (call-out T) | **Stress Testing** |

**"This provides possibilities no matter what third part Heart Rate sensor unit the user wants to
connect to."** That is the whole of the strap-compatibility claim. **The manual names no brand and no
pairing procedure** for the sensor - the status bar simply carries a **Heart Rate Sensor Connection**
icon (`85ue-2025-console-screen-overview-and-status-bar`).

**ANT+** is *"a communication protocol using very low energy and able to broadcast the signal to
several types of equipment at the same time"*, and **"Dyaco 8 series products support ANT+ natively,
having an integrated ANT+ chipset."**

**FTMS** is the **FiTness Machine Service** wireless protocol *"to allow third party software to
control exercise equipment, such as Ergometer trainers, Ergometers and rowing machines"*. The manual
adds that making Bluetooth machines available to connect **"is difficult because each sensor
historically required a specific protocol"** - it names **no app that has been tested**.

**Real-time streaming of the patient's workout data is available for download.** That is the
manual's own sentence, and it says no more: no destination, no format, no rate.

**The console still prescribes a 5 kHz chest strap in its heart rate chapter.** *"Most transmitters
that operate at 5kHz frequency will also work"*, and the replacement cell is a **CR2032** with an
estimated **2500 hours** of transmitter life. **Three radio standards named in one book - BLE, ANT+
and 5 kHz analogue - and it never says which the built-in receiver actually uses.**

**A Spirit book calling the machine a Dyaco product**, and its own model rosters disagree. The console
page says **"MED 8 series products (8.0T, 8.0R, 8.0U, 8.5S, 8.5UE)"** and the machine-care page
repeats that list, but the data transfer page says the software **"works with 8.0T, 8.0U, 8.5R, 8.5S
and 8.5UE"** - **8.0R in one list, 8.5R in the other, and neither list has both.** Both are
reproduced as printed. **Quote the Spirit model when you answer; the 8 series names are the OEM's.**

**The 8.5S recumbent stepper prints this section in the same words**
(`85s-2025-console-wifi-ble-ftms-and-ant-plus-connectivity`), and that card quotes the roster with
**8.5R**. It is a **climber** card; this one is the ergometer's.
