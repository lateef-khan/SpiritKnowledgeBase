**Call-out A on the parts drawing is a 10" touch panel console.** It is the only display on
the machine, and there is no LED panel, no keypad strip and no second screen.

**It moves in three ways, each with its own call-out:**

| Call-out | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| **B** | **Vertical viewing angle adjustment** |
| **C** | **Console articulating arm horizontal adjustment** |
| - | **360 degree swivel** on the arm itself |

**Why the arm swivels:** *"This provides the ability for clinicians to operate the console from any
position around the machine, and allowing a suitable viewing distance for the patient. The tilted
view angle adjustment meets a wide variety of user heights."* **The console is meant to be operated
by the clinician standing beside the patient**, not by the patient facing it.

**The patient has a second set of controls on the handle.** Call-out **E** is the **Multi-Function
Handle**: *"This handle is in close patient proximity and has remote quick keys to easily operate the
console, and contains a tower height adjustment lever."* **The manual never lists which keys those
are.** It says "quick keys" and stops - no count, no labels, no diagram. **Do not tell a caller which
functions are on the handle.**

**Four sockets sit on the front end of the machine, below the console:**

| Call-out | Port |
|---|---|
| **R** | **Power socket** |
| **S** | **Power ON/OFF** |
| **T** | **UART Port**; for Stress Testing |
| **U** | **USB Type B Port**; for Stress Testing **& Data Transfer** |

**The USB port carries data on this machine** and has its own procedure
(`85ue-2025-console-usb-type-b-data-transfer-to-a-csv-file`). **Call-outs R and S are on the front of
the machine, not the console**, and the console has no power switch of its own.

**The 8.5S recumbent stepper runs the same console software on the same 10" panel**, and this
manual's own connectivity and care pages list the two machines together as MED 8 series
(`85s-2025-console-settings-and-the-four-workout-views`). **They are separate cards because they are
separate product lines** - an ergometer answer must not be served for a stepper or the reverse - but
the screens themselves match. **The 8.5S-Fit is a different, 15.6" console** and matches neither.
