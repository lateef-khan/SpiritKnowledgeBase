**You cannot start until you have chosen a login method, and Guest is one of them.** The
console powers up on the **login method page**, and *"you may now begin to use the machine after
choosing the login method"*. The connectivity list names the three: **"Multiple access method login:
NFC, PIN or QR Code."**

**Three ways to log in, plus guest:**

- **QR code** - *"The user scans the QR code with their own phone and log in."*
- **NFC** - *"The console is equipped with an RFID reader. To log in, hold your phone/RFID tag to the
  symbol located on the bottom left of the console."*
- **Pin code** - *"The clinician generates the pin code in the Dyaco Insight App and provides it to
  the user. The User can log in using the pin code for one day."*
- **Guest** - *"Choose the preferred log in method or enter the system as a guest. Please make sure
  the WiFi connection is available, or you will enter the system as a guest in offline status."*

**A user can log in after the session instead.** *"You could also log in to the system to record your
exercise data after finishing the session if you didn't log in the beginning."*

**First-time setup runs before any of this, in four screens:**

1. **Select language & WiFi.** Select the preferred language and click Next; turn WiFi on; choose the
   network and click Next; enter the password and click Connect.
2. **Organization account.** Sign in with the organization account's **email and password**, or click
   **"No account yet"** at the lower left and **scan a QR code** to create a new organization
   member's account.
3. **Product Registration.** Enter the **administrator's email and password** to sign in, then **the
   product's serial number**.
4. **Time and Date.** Set the local time and date and click **Start** to finish setup.

**The clinician-side app is named once and never explained.** The **Dyaco Insight App** appears only
in the pin code bullet - no download link, no platform, no account instructions.

**Losing the Wi-Fi drops the user to guest**, and the manual nowhere says what happens to a session
recorded offline.

**The 8.5S recumbent stepper prints this chapter in the same words**
(`85s-2025-console-login-by-qr-code-nfc-or-pin`). That is a **climber** card and will not be served
for an ergometer question; this one is the ergometer's.
