**The Beacon uses brightly coloured lights on the top of the console** to gauge exercise
effort and **remind physical therapists of their patients' progress at a glance**.

**It can be set to show one of three measures**: **heart rate**, **power**, or **progress**. The
bands are the same five colours for all three:

| Colour | Heart rate | Watts | Workout progress |
|---|---|---|---|
| **White** | 0-59% | 0-20% | 0-24% |
| **Blue** | 60-69% | 21-40% | 25-49% |
| **Green** | 70-79% | 41-60% | 50-74% |
| **Yellow** | 80-89% | 61-80% | 75-99% |
| **Red** | 90-100% | 81-100% | 100% |

**What the manual says the colours mean:** *"White & Blue: initial condition of warm up or low stage.
Green: ready for use or medium stage. Yellow: the high stage caution or close to the end. Red: the
highest stage warning or the final stage."*

**The function may be turned on or off in the setup**, from **Settings**
(`85ue-2025-console-function-keys-and-the-four-viewing-modes`). **The manual does not say which of
the three measures is the default.**

**Note printed beside it:** *"There is a thin protective sheet of clear plastic on the overlay of the
console that should be removed before use."* **A new machine whose screen looks hazy or unresponsive
usually still has that sheet on.**

**Only the MED 8 series has this.** No other Spirit console lights its housing. The **8.5S recumbent
stepper** prints the identical table (`85s-2025-console-beacon-progress-indicator-colour-bands`) -
that is a **climber** card, and this one is the ergometer's.
