| Figure | Printed |
|---|---|
| **Length** | **170.8 cm (67.2")** |
| **Width** | **76 cm (29.9")** |
| **Height** | **167.5 cm (65.9")** |
| **Product weight** | **149 kgs (328.5 lbs)** |

**All four convert correctly**, which is worth saying because the 8.5S and
8.5S-FIT steppers of the same family print a height row whose two figures differ
by seven inches. **This page does not have that fault** - 170.8 cm is 67.2", 76 cm
is 29.9", 167.5 cm is 65.9" and 149 kg is 328.5 lb, all to the printed precision.
Both figures on every row were confirmed against a 300 dpi render of the page.

**Height is the dimension to plan around, not length.** At **167.5 cm the machine
is nearly as tall as it is long**, because the console and crank tower stand up
rather than lying along the frame - the 8.5S recumbent stepper of the same family
is 220 cm long and 131 cm high, the other way round. The tower is
height-adjustable and the manual says to set it "in line with the center of the
chest". Check the doorway against the height.

## No shipping weight and no carton size

**Neither is printed anywhere in the book**, and neither is a folded or
transport dimension - the machine does not fold. 149 kg is the assembled machine
alone. For a freight quote, get the gross weight and carton dimensions from Spirit
Fitness.

## The clearance figures are a separate answer

The manual asks for **two feet at the sides, one foot in front and three and a half
feet behind** as free space around the machine. That is a safety requirement, not
a dimension - see
`85ue-2025-safety-clearance-two-feet-sides-one-front-three-and-a-half-rear`.

## Do not read a stepper's figures across

The 8.5S and 8.5S-FIT recumbent steppers are **220 x 86 cm** and 150.9 kg, and the
MS300, 7.0S and 7.5S differ again. They are a different product line on the same
Dyaco MED platform, and a customer quoting 220 cm has a stepper, not an ergometer.
