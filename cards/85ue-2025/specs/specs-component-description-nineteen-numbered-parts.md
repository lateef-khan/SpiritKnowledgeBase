The **Cantilever (1)** is the arm that carries the crank tower out over the user, and the **Circular
Ring (2)** the ring handle on it that the preventive-maintenance chapter checks for smooth rotation;
the replacement chapter has its own procedures for the cantilever's steel cables and the gas cylinder
and lift adjustment that set the tower's height. The
**Rotating Structure Assembly (4)** is the pivot under the console, with the O-ring the
troubleshooting chapter replaces. Items 8 and 9, *Pedal* and *Pedal Cover*, name the foot plate and
its shroud - the book keeps the word pedal from the bikes.

**No part numbers, quantities or dimensions here**; the owner's manual's A-to-U labelled drawing is
`85ue-2025-assembly-parts-diagram-labels`. The 8.5S stepper book numbers nineteen components on a
different machine (`85s-2025-specs-component-description-nineteen-numbered-parts`).
