1. Use a Phillips Head Screw Driver to loosen the 5 tapping screws on motor cover, then remove the motor cover.
2. Disconnect all lower controller wiring. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the controller, fit the new lower controller and reconnect all wiring.

The manual gives no calibration step in this section. The calibration procedure is section 8.9.
