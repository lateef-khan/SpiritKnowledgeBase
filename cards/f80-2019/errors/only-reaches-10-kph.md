This indicates the motor should be receiving power to operate, and that the **AC voltage reaching the treadmill is low**.

- **Do not use an extension cord.** If one is required it should be as short as possible and heavy duty, **16 AWG minimum**.
- Low household voltage - contact an electrician or your dealer.
- A minimum of 220 volt AC current, 50 hz is required. (on 120Vac electronic power system need have 110Vac)
