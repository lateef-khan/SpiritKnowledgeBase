The diagnosis guide lists four things to check.

1. Noise coming from the rollers. (The manual's sentence stops there.)
2. Noise while running **at the lowest level of incline** can be too much pressure on the **incline cylinder**. This applies only at the lowest incline level.
3. A **knocking noise during the workout** - check that all bolts are tightened.
4. A **thumping noise while the belt runs** happens with a brand new treadmill, or one that has not been used for a long time. The belt has been shaped by the rollers and hardened by low temperature. Running the belt for tens of minutes makes the thumping gradually go away.
