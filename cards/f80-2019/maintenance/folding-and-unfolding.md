**Do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and locked position.** Be sure the power cord is secured to avoid damage. Use both handrails to manoeuvre the unit.

**To fold**: make certain the treadmill is at **minimum incline**. Lift the running deck until it is secured by the locking telescoping tube assembly in the centre back of the base.

**To unfold**: apply slight forward pressure on the running deck with one hand, at the rear roller area, to relieve pressure on the locking system. Pull down on the unlocking lever and slowly lower the deck to the floor. The deck lowers unassisted once it reaches about waist high.
