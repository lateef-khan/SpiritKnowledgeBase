**The user's gait.** The user may be walking while favouring or putting more weight on the left or right foot. If that walking pattern is natural, **track the belt slightly off-centre to the side opposite from the belt movement**.

Uneven tension across the belt is the other cause, and the tracking and tension procedures correct it.
