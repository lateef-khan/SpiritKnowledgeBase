The adjustment is made **from the rear roller**. The adjustment bolts are at the end of the step rails in the end caps, reached through a small hole in the end cap.

1. Tighten the rear roller bolts **only enough to prevent slippage at the front roller**.
2. Turn **both** tread-belt tension adjustment bolts in increments of **1/4 turn each**.
3. Inspect the tension by walking on the belt at a low speed, checking that the belt does not slip.
4. Keep tensioning until the belt stops slipping.

If the belt feels tight enough but still slips, the problem may be a **loose motor drive belt under the front cover**.

**DO NOT OVERTIGHTEN.** Over-tightening causes belt damage and premature bearing failure. Damage to the running belt resulting from improper tracking or tension adjustments **is not covered under the warranty**.
