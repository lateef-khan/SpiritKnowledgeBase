1. Install the 2 AA batteries (included); the computer **beeps for 2 seconds** and enters **CLOCK & CALENDAR MODE**.
2. The CLOCK flashes: UP / DOWN sets the hour, ENTER confirms; UP / DOWN sets the minutes, ENTER confirms. Then set the **YEAR (in the STROKES window), MONTH (in the CALORIES window) and DAY (in the PULSE window)**, ENTER after each.
3. **ALARM** blinks. Press ENTER to skip it, or press UP to turn the alarm on (an arrow appears beside ALARM), press ENTER, and set the alarm time in the flashing CLOCK window with UP / DOWN and ENTER.
4. The computer goes to the **SPORT screen**, where MANUAL and RACE blink: UP or DOWN to choose, ENTER to confirm (programs cards).

**In sleep mode** the computer displays the **calendar** (year, month, day), the **current room temperature** and the **clock**. **The alarm only works while the computer is in sleep mode and will not sound during exercise.** To set it later, **press and hold RESET** to return to the clock screen. **Sleep mode begins after about 4 minutes of inactivity.**

This is the only Xterra rower book with a clock; its siblings boot into a water-level prompt instead ([water-level card](../../shared/console/xterra-ergw-console-power-up-asks-for-the-water-level-one-to-six.md)) and this book never asks for the water level.
