Only the ERG600W among the Xterra rowers prints a bungee adjustment, and its service manual gives the
symptom it cures.

## The adjustment, from the owner's manual

> There are 3 hooks (A, B, C) for the bungee cord, located on the inside of the Frame (No.1) under the water
> tank. If you hook the Bungee Cord (No.39) on to B or C, it increases the assisted return of the mesh belt.
> **C is the fastest return.** Use caution when adjusting the bungee cord.

The drawing looks up under the tank from below: hook A is nearest the front pulley, B and C further back
along the frame rail, so each hook back stretches the cord more. The service manual's reassembly step 8 hangs
a new cord "in the first hook rack at bottom of main frame" - hook A is the factory position, the lightest
return.

## The symptom, from the service manual Q&A "Strap can't retract"

> Q: When you do exercise, the handlebar strap can't retract smoothly?
>
> 1. Check if bungee cord loses elasticity, **adjust bungee cord to another hook to improve its elasticity.**
> 2. Check if handlebar can't return to the handlebar seat, you need to **change the mesh belt wheel** as
> above related Manual.

So a strap that comes back slack or slowly is first a cord moved one hook further back (A to B, B to C). A
handle that will not return to its seat at all is a mesh belt wheel replacement, which is the service
manual's procedure (the assembly section's card), not an adjustment.

No tension figure, no cord life and no replacement interval is printed in either ERG600W book. The ERG550W,
ERG650W, ERG750W and ERG800W books print no bungee page at all; the ERG800W's return is a spring box inside the
drive assembly.

Spirit's CRW900 water rower prints a bungee tension procedure of its own
(`crw900-2021-maintenance-bungee-recoil-tension-adjustment`). Other brand, separate card.
