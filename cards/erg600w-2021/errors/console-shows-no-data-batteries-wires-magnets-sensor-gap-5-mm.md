**This is the list with a sensor gap - 5 mm - and it is the ERG600W's figure only.** The ERG550W's version of the question has no gap and starts with the length of the stroke (`erg550w-2023-errors-console-shows-no-data-full-stroke-of-1-m-then-batteries-wires-two-magnets`); the ERG750W's table says under 6 mm (`erg750w-2025-errors-no-speed-sensor-connection-gap-under-6-mm-cables-magnet`).

The service manual's answer, in its order. The question is printed as *The console without date* (sic) and the answer opens *When there is no display, check as below*:

1. Check if the batteries have enough power. If not, change new one to try again.
2. Check if the wires are connected properly. If not, try to connect all the wires again.
3. Check if the magnets are installed properly in the belt wheel. If not, install a new magnet in the hole of the belt wheel.
4. Check the distance to confirm whether the sensor bar has moved away from the magnet. **If the distance is over 5 MM, move the sensor bar to keep the standard distance.**
5. Check if the wire has breakage. If yes, change new wire.
6. If all is OK, change a new console.

(The book numbers the first two steps 1 and 1, then 2 to 5; they are six steps.)

**The photo beside the answer draws a red line labelled `25MM` along the sensor bar, from its fixing screw to the sensor tip.** The text's only figure is the 5 mm gap. The two are not the same measurement: the 5 mm is how far the sensor may sit from the magnet before it must be moved back, and the 25 mm marks the sensor bar's reach on the bracket, which the text does not explain. This card follows the text - 5 mm is the fault limit - and records the photo's figure so that a reader who sees `25MM` on the page does not take it for the gap.

The owner's manual describes the same fault in three rows - sensor wire, replace sensor wire, replace monitor - with no gap and no magnets (`xterra-rower-errors-no-count-or-distance-sensor-wire-then-monitor`). A console that shows nothing at all is a shorter question on the same page: batteries, then a new console (`xterra-rower-errors-monitor-does-not-display-install-batteries-then-computer-wires`).

The Spirit CRW800H2O service manual prints this list without the gap step (`crw800h2o-console-shows-no-data`); the Spirit CRW900 gives 2-3 mm and six magnets for its own sensor (`crw900-2021-errors-speed-sensor-gap-2-to-3-mm-and-six-magnets`). Neither figure is printed for this machine.
