**Where the water comes from decides the answer, and only one of the three cases is a fault-free one.** The service manual asks three questions:

| Where | Printed answer | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A little water from the **middle of the upper tank when you stand the rower up** | "this is a normal phenomenon but may check if you put too much water. You can go on running this machine." | Check the fill level; keep using it |
| From the **bottom** of the tank | "Maybe the lower tank has a little crack, please replace tank as above related Manual." | Replace the tank (the tank/impeller pages of this manual) |
| From **between** the upper tank and the lower tank | "Sealing ring is defective or not properly assembled, change a new sealing ring to assemble tank." | Replace the sealing ring and reassemble the tank |

The first and third cases are both at the join of the two halves. What separates them is that the harmless one is a little water **only when the rower is stood on end** and is answered by the fill level, while a leak at the join with the machine sitting flat is the sealing ring. Cloudy water is a different question with a tablet for an answer and belongs to maintenance.

The ERG550W service manual, this rower's sibling, prints no leak question. The Spirit CRW800H2O service manual prints these three cases in the same words (`crw800h2o-water-leaking`).
