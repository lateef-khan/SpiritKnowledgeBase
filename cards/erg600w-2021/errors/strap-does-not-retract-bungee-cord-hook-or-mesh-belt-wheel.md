**Two causes, one adjustment and one part.** The question is printed as *When you do exercise, the handlebar strap can't retract smoothly?*

1. Check if the bungee cord has lost elasticity. **Adjust the bungee cord to another hook** to improve its elasticity.
2. Check if the handlebar cannot return to the handlebar seat. If so, **change the mesh belt wheel**, following the mesh belt wheel replacement pages of this manual.

The hooks are the three - A, B, C - inside the front of the frame that the owner's manual's assembly pages show for the bungee cord; moving the cord to a hook that stretches it further restores the return. The mesh belt wheel is the spring-loaded reel the strap winds onto, and a handle that will not come home to its seat at all is that reel, not the cord.

The Spirit CRW800 and XRW600 answer the same symptom with a latch and spring latch on a different mechanism (`spirit-crw800-errors-ribbon-roll-does-not-spring-back-or-gets-stuck-latch-and-spring-latch`), and the Spirit CRW900 water rower prints a row for a belt that slips off its bungee pulley (`crw900-2021-errors-belt-slips-off-the-bungee-pulley`). Neither is this machine's.
