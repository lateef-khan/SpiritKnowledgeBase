**One labelled picture, fourteen labels:**

| Label | Label |
|---|---|
| Console | Seat |
| Decorative cover | Slide Rail |
| Handlebar | Front stabilizer |
| Mesh Belt Wheel | Impeller |
| Pedal Strap | Rear Stabilizer |
| Pedal | Tank |
| Stopper | End Cap |

The *Mesh Belt Wheel* is the reel the handle strap winds onto, with the bungee cord inside it; the *Impeller* is
the paddle in the *Tank*; the *Stopper* is the pair of rubber blocks (owner's manual part 35) bolted to the rail
sides to stop the seat; the *End Cap*s are the front-stabilizer caps (25 and 26) and the rear rail cap. The
*Decorative cover* sits behind the console on the main frame and comes off with four screws when the belt wheel is
replaced.

No dimension or weight is printed. The service manual prints no parts list, wiring diagram or block diagram; its
contents are fifteen replacement procedures (the assembly section's) and three Q&A pages - console without data,
strap will not retract, leaking water (the errors section's). The ERG550W book's drawing names twelve parts
(`erg550w-2023-specs-outline-twelve-labels-console-to-adjust-knob`).
