**NOTE: "We recommend having 2 people to assemble the product."**

1. Turn over the **Slide Rail (3)** as shown. Attach the **Rear Stabilizer (4)** to the slide rail with **4 Washers (71) and 4 Outer Hex Screws (66, M8 x 15)**.
2. Tighten with the **Spanner (75)** - the 13-17 mm open wrench, because these are hex-head screws, not Allen screws.
