1. Take the seat assembly out of the slide rail as on the seat page.
2. Turn the seat assembly over; use two spanners to release the roller set (figure 1).
3. Use a screwdriver and an Allen wrench to remove the roller, then pull the roller shaft out from the roller (figure 2).
4. Reverse the steps to replace the defective roller - "maybe one or more".

No sizes are printed. Check every roller while the seat is out; the seat has to come out again to reach the next one.
