Attach the **Console (46)** to the **Main Frame (1)** using **4 Washers (71) and 4 Screws (60, M8 x 18)**. Tighten with the Allen wrench (74).

The step says nothing about wires; the service manual shows the sensor wire and console wire meeting at the console support, and the console's 2 AA batteries are fitted in the console chapter (the console section's card). Filling the tank (linked card) is what remains before rowing.
