1. Slide the **Seat (36)** onto the **Slide Rail (3)**.
2. Attach the slide rail to the **Main Frame (1)** using **4 Flat Screws (58, M8 x 15)**.
3. Secure the **Stopper (35)** with **2 Screws (60, M8 x 18) and Washers (87, OD16 x ID8.5 x 1.5)**. **Insert all 6 screws partially into the holes first**, then tighten with the Allen Wrench (74).
4. Attach **Rail endcap L (82) and Rail endcap R (83)** to the slide rail.

The bag holds two stoppers (35); the step names "the Stopper" with two screws - one stopper each side of the rail, one screw each, as the service manual's seat procedure confirms ("two M8 x 90 bolts to remove two stoppers from the sides of the aluminum rail" - the service book prints a different bolt length from the owner's book's M8 x 18; both agree on two bolts).
