1. Use Allen wrench S6 and a spanner to release **one M10 x 55 bolt** to take the guide roller apart (figure 1).
2. To refit, insert the M10 x 55 bolt and one nylon nut through the guide roller, then secure them with the S6 key and spanner (figure 2).
3. **Make sure the strap is on the right side.**

The Spirit CRW800H2O's page is the same bolt with two spacers named and an S5 key printed (linked).
