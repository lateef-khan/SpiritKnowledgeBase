Attach the **L and R End Caps (25 and 26)** to the **Front Stabilizer (2)**, then tighten with **2 Cross Screws (68, M4 x 12)**.

The step says "using the Spanner (75)"; a cross (Phillips) screw takes a screwdriver, and the S5 Allen wrench (74) in the bag has no Phillips end. Bring a Phillips screwdriver.
