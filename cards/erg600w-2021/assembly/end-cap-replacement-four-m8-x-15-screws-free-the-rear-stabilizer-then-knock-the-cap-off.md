1. Use the spanner to remove **4 M8 x 15 screws** and take the rear stabilizer out - the four outer-hex screws (66) of assembly step 1.
2. Remove the cross screws from the bottom of the end cap.
3. Knock the end cap off the rear stabilizer and fit the new one.

These caps are the levelers the owner's manual turns to balance the rower.
