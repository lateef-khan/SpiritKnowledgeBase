1. The **upper pedal** can be replaced directly by slipping it down off the lower pedal.
2. To replace the **lower pedal**, use a screwdriver and Allen wrench to remove two M5 cross screws.
3. Reverse the steps to fit the new pedal.

Two-part pedal: establish which half is broken before ordering. Identical on the Spirit CRW800H2O (linked).
