1. Use Allen wrench S5 and Spanner S13-S17 to release **one M8 x 70 button head bolt**, then disconnect the sensor wire and wire from the console and take the console apart (figure 1).
2. Use a Phillips screwdriver to release **four M5 x 12 Phillips head screws** (figure 2).
3. To refit: secure the Support plate with the four M5 x 12 screws onto the back of the console, connect the sensor wire and wire, and secure the console assembly onto the console support with the M8 x 70 bolt.

The owner's manual mounts the console support to the frame on four M8 x 18 screws; this bolt is the pivot between the support and the console. The Spirit CRW800H2O's page has the same four M5 x 12 screws but no pivot bolt.
