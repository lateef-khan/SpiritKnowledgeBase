1. Use Allen wrench S5 and a spanner to release **one M8 x 30 bolt** to take the bungee pulley apart (figure 1). **"Make sure the bungee cord not to be loosen when you remove the small pulleys."**
2. Reverse the steps to refit, and **make sure the bungee cord stays right in the curve of the pulley.**

The strap's return pull on this rower is a bungee cord over four small pulleys; the owner's manual's three hooks (A, B, C) set its tension - the maintenance section's card. The Spirit CRW800H2O uses a volute spring instead and has no bungee pulley page.
