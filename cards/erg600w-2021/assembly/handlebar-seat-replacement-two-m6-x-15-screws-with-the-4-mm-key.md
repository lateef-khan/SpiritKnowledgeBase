1. Use Allen wrench S4 to release **two M6 x 15 screws** to take the defective handlebar seat off (figure 1).
2. Fit the new handlebar seat and secure it with the two M6 x 15 screws.

The shortest page in the book, and identical on the Spirit CRW800H2O (linked). The handlebar seat is the cradle the handle rests in - not the rower's seat, not the handlebar.
