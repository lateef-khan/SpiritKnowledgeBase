1. Turn the **Main Frame (1)** upright. Attach the **Front Stabilizer (2)** to the main frame using **8 Washers (71) and 8 Screws (60, M8 x 18)**. Tighten with the Allen Wrench (74, S5).
2. "You can put 3~4 screws into the holes first, and start tightening by hand. After all the screws are put in, then tighten with the tool."

Eight screws - the most of any step on this rower; four each side.
