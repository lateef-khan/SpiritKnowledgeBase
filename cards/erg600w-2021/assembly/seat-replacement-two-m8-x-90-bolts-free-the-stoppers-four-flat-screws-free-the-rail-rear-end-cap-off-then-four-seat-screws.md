1. Use Allen wrench S5 to release **two M8 x 90 bolts** to remove the two stoppers from the sides of the aluminum rail (figure 1).
2. Stand the rower up; use Allen wrench S5 to release **four M8 x 15 flat screws** to take the rail out from the main frame (figure 2).
3. Remove the rear rail end cap and slide the seat assembly out (figure 3).
4. Turn the seat assembly over; use Allen wrench S4 to release **four M6 x 15 screws** to take the seat off (figure 4).
5. Reverse the steps to refit.

**The stopper bolt length disagrees between books:** the owner's manual fits the stoppers with two M8 x 18 screws (60); this page removes M8 x 90 bolts. Both agree on two bolts, one per stopper, and the stoppers must come off before the seat can leave the rail. The Spirit CRW800H2O's seat comes off around one M10 x 55 roller bolt - a different carriage.
