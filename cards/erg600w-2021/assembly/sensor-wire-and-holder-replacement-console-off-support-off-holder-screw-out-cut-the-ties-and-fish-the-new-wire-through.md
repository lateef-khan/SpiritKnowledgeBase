1. Use Allen wrench S5 and Spanner S13-S17 to release **one M8 x 70 button head bolt**, then disconnect the sensor wire and wire from the console and take the console off the console support (figure 1).
2. Use Allen wrench S5 to release **four M8 x 15 screws** to take the console support off the main frame (figure 2).
3. Use Allen wrench S5 and the spanner to release **one M8 x 75 button head bolt** (figure 3).
4. Use an Allen wrench and screwdriver to release **one M4 x 12 cross screw** to take the sensor holder off, taking the sensor bar out at the same time - "you may need to clear the glue on the surface at first".
5. Cut the two black ties on the support, then pull the two sensor wires out of the support tube.
6. Put the new sensor wire through the tube; "if difficult, thin iron wire can help".
7. Fix the sensor holder, plug in the sensor bar and secure the bolts at both sides of the support tube.

The Q&A page's rule for the refit: the sensor bar must sit within **5 mm** of the belt wheel magnet. The Spirit CRW800H2O's sensor comes out on four M3 x 6 screws behind the volute spring set - a different route.
