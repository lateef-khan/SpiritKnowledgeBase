1. Use a socket wrench and Allen wrench to remove the 2 stoppers at the side of the aluminum rail (figure 1).
2. Remove the rear rail end cap, then slide the seat assembly out (figure 2).
3. Use a screwdriver to release **five screws on each side** of the aluminum rail and take the two aluminum rails apart (figure 3).
4. Reverse the steps to refit.

"Aluminum Rail" here means the two strips the rollers run on, not the whole slide rail. The Spirit CRW800H2O's strip is glued under two covers - a different fixing.
