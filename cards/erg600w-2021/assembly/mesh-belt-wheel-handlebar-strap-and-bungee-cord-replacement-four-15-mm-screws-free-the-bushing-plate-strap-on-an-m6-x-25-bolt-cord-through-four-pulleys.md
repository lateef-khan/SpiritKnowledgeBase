1. Use the S5 Allen wrench to release **4 M8 x 15 screws** to take the bushing plate off the main frame (figure 1).
2. Take the defective mesh belt wheel off the shaft - **loosen the bungee cord first**.
3. Remove the strap: if the handlebar strap is as picture 1 in figure 2, tear it down directly; if as picture 2, remove the fixed screw in the mesh belt wheel.
4. Remove the bungee cord - pull it straight out of the mesh belt wheel.
5. Fix the strap on the mesh belt wheel with an **M6 x 25 bolt** through the front end of the strap (figure 3). Rotate the wheel to wind the strap onto it. Put the bungee cord through the hole in the wheel.
6. Pass the handlebar through the main frame, then pull the mesh belt to the front of the storage knob (figure 4). Put the mesh belt wheel on the POM magnet seat, adding some oil if needed.
7. Fit the bungee cord (figure 5): **the cord must pass the 4 small pulleys in the right order and direction.** Fix the end of the cord with the aluminum clasp or a white tie; the clasp needs special pliers or a fixture (figure 6).
8. **Hang the bungee cord in the first hook rack at the bottom of the main frame** - hook A of the three on the owner's manual's adjustment page.

The Spirit CRW800H2O's page replaces a volute spring and its axle instead of a bungee - a different mechanism.
