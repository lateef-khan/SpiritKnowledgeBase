1. Use a screwdriver to remove **two M5 screws** and take the defective pedal strap out of the pedal (figure 1).
2. Put the new pedal strap through from one side of the plate to the other, then secure it with the two screws (figure 2).

No screw length is printed. Identical on the Spirit CRW800H2O (linked).
