1. Use Allen wrench S5 to release **four M8 x 15 screws** to take the console assembly apart, then take the decorative cover out of the main frame (figure 1).
2. Use Allen wrench S6 and a spanner to release the **M10 x 185 bolt** to take the belt wheel apart, "and make sure two long spacers there for assembling the new belt wheel" (figure 2).
3. Refit the belt wheel including the bearing and magnet (figure 3).
4. Put the decorative cover and the console assembly back on the main frame.

The magnet in the belt wheel is what the sensor bar reads; the Q&A page says the sensor bar must sit within 5 mm of it. The Spirit CRW800H2O's wheel is held by a nylon nut each side, not one through-bolt.
