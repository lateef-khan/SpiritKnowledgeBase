**The whole manual is 482 words and has no specification page.** Its contents
are Parts Included (3), Assembly (4), Parts List (6). Nothing else is in it.

**It never names a treadmill.** The cover reads "XTHRAILS EXTENDED HANDRAILS"
and the assembly text refers only to "the Unit" and "the treadmill". No model
number, no model range and no compatibility list appears anywhere in the book,
so it cannot be used to answer whether the kit fits a particular machine. Ask
Spirit Fitness or the dealer, and say the manual does not state it.

None of these is printed either:

- assembled or packed dimensions of the rails, or how far they extend
- kit weight or shipping weight
- a user weight limit, or any load rating for the rails
- a torque figure for any of the fourteen bolts
- a materials or finish specification

**What the book does state.** A parts list of eleven items
(`xth-rails-specs-parts-list`), a four-step assembly procedure, the fold and
unfold instructions, and the transport-wheel note. The only tools named are the
M5 combination Allen wrench and Phillips screwdriver supplied in the kit.

**Two clues about fitment, neither of them a model list.** The assembly requires
the treadmill to have a Handgrip Base to bolt the connecting tubes into and a
Square End Cap on each handgrip that comes off first, and the fold and unfold
instructions describe a deck with a yellow release sticker and four transport
wheels. A machine without those parts is not covered by this book.
