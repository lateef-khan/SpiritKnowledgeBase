The kit's parts list runs 001 to 011 with no gaps. There is no exploded view
drawing beside it; the only illustrations in the book are the assembly
photographs.

| No. | Part description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 001 | Left Handgrip Connecting Tube | 1 |
| 002 | Right Handgrip Connecting Tube | 1 |
| 003 | Handgrip Stabilizer | 2 |
| 004 | Stabilizer | 1 |
| 005 | PVC Handgrip (L) | 1 |
| 006 | PVC Handgrip (R) | 1 |
| 007 | Rubber Foot Pad | 3 |
| 008 | Square End Cap | 2 |
| 009 | 5/16" × 3/4"_Button Head Socket Bolt | 14 |
| 010 | M5_Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench | 1 |
| 011 | M10 x 5T Nut | 3 |

**Item 009 is used for two different things in this book.** The parts list gives
it as the button head socket bolt above, but the assembly text also writes
"Handgrip Base (009)" for a part of the treadmill the rails bolt on to. Read
`xth-rails-specs-item-009-two-parts` before ordering against 009.

**Two entries the parts list and the front of the book disagree about.**

- Item 004 is "Stabilizer" here. The Parts Included page on printed page 3 calls
  the same part "1 Stabilizer Base" and the assembly text on page 5 calls it the
  "U-shaped Stabilizer Base (004)".
- Item 010 is "M5_Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench" here. Everywhere else in the
  book it is the "Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver", and
  the assembly text calls it the "M5 L Allen Wrench (010)".

**Items 005, 006, 007, 008 and 011 are never mentioned in the assembly steps.**
The four assembly steps name only 001, 002, 003, 004, 009 and 010. The PVC
handgrips, rubber foot pads, square end caps and M10 nuts appear in the parts
list only, so this table is the only place their quantities are stated.

**Counting the bolts.** The parts list gives 14 of item 009, and the Parts
Included page agrees ("Button Head Socket Bolt (14pcs)"). The assembly steps
account for all 14: 4 into the stabilizer base, 6 into the handgrip base, then
4 more.

The manual is version 1.0, revision 03/17/23.
