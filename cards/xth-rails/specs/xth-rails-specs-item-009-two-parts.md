**The manual gives the number 009 to two different things, and never says so.**

| Where it appears | What 009 is called there |
|---|---|
| PARTS LIST, page 6 | `009  5/16" × 3/4"_Button Head Socket Bolt  14` |
| PARTS INCLUDED, page 3 | `#9. 5/16"×3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt (14 pcs)` |
| ASSEMBLY step 3, page 5 | "using 4 pcs of 5/16" × 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolts (009)" |
| ASSEMBLY step 3, page 5 | "into the **Handgrip Base (009)** of the Unit" |

The same sentence uses 009 both ways: *"Insert the Left Handgrip Connecting Tube
(001) and Right Handgrip Connecting Tube (002) into the Handgrip Base (009) of
the Unit and use the M5 L Allen Wrench (010) to tighten 6 pcs of 5/16" × 3/4"
Button Head Socket Bolts (009) to secure them together."*

**Which one is the real 009.** The parts list and the parts-included page agree
with each other, and only the bolt is a part of this kit. Treat **009 as the
5/16" × 3/4" button head socket bolt, quantity 14**.

**The handgrip base is not a part of this kit at all.** The assembly text calls
it "the Handgrip Base (009) **of the Unit**" - it is on the treadmill the rails
are fitted to, not in the box. The kit's own parts list has no handgrip base in
it. There is no item number for it in this book, so do not order a handgrip base
against 009 or against any number from this manual.

**Do not read the kit's numbering as a treadmill's.** These are 001-011 callouts
local to a three-page accessory book. A treadmill parts list that also has a 009
is a different list.
