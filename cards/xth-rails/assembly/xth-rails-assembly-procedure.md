The whole kit goes on with one tool, the **M5 L Allen Wrench (010)** supplied
with it.

**1. Set your treadmill into the folded position.** To fold it, lift the deck
until the latch clicks in place. The fold and unfold procedure is on its own
card.

**2. Take the square end caps off the handgrips.** Use the M5 L Allen Wrench
(010) to remove the screws and take away the Square End Cap on the hand grip
(R, L).

**3. Build the base, then hang the tubes on the unit.** Three tightenings, 14
bolts in all:

- Install the **Handgrip Stabilizers (003)** into the **U-shaped Stabilizer Base
  (004)** using **4 pcs of 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolts (009)**, secured
  with the M5 L Allen Wrench (010).
- Insert the **Left Handgrip Connecting Tube (001)** and **Right Handgrip
  Connecting Tube (002)** into the **Handgrip Base of the unit** and use the M5 L
  Allen Wrench (010) to tighten **6 pcs of 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolts
  (009)** to secure them together.
- After that, use the M5 L Allen Wrench (010) to tighten **4 pcs of 5/16" x 3/4"
  Button Head Socket Bolts (009)**.

**4. The treadmill can now be unfolded for use.** To unfold it, press the tube
with your foot at the yellow sticker to release the latch, push the running deck
forward with your left hand while pulling the release lever downward with your
right, and gently lower the deck to the floor supporting it with a hand.

## Two warnings about this manual

**The Handgrip Base is printed as "(009)", and 009 is also the bolt.** The step
text above reproduces the manual, which uses 009 for both the bolt and the
treadmill's handgrip base in one sentence. Order 009 as the bolt only, and read
`xth-rails-specs-item-009-two-parts` before ordering anything against that
number.

**The manual never says what the last 4 bolts fasten.** The third tightening is
printed exactly as written above, with no part named. It brings the total to the
14 bolts the kit contains, which is the only check available.

The manual names **no treadmill model**, so it does not say which machines the
kit fits. It does show a treadmill that folds, has a latch released by a yellow
sticker on the tube, has a release lever, and rolls on four transport wheels that
engage when it is folded.
