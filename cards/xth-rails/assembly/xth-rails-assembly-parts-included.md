## Tools included

- Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screwdriver (item 010)

That is the only tool in the kit, and the only tool the assembly steps ever call
for. **You do not need anything else.**

## Parts included

The PARTS INCLUDED panel names three assemblies and two hardware items:

| Qty | Part | Item |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Handgrip Stabilizers | 003 |
| 2 | Handgrip Connecting Tubes | 001, 002 |
| 1 | Stabilizer Base | 004 |
| 14 | 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt | 009 |
| 1 | Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver | 010 |

**All 14 bolts are used.** The assembly steps call for 4 into the stabilizer
base, then 6 for the connecting tubes, then 4 more - 14 in all.

## Two things this panel does not tell you

- The panel calls item 004 the **Stabilizer Base**; the kit's own parts list
  calls the same item simply **Stabilizer**, and the assembly step calls it the
  **U-shaped Stabilizer Base**. One part, three names.
- The panel numbers the bolt **#9** and the wrench **#10**, while the parts list
  numbers the same two items **009** and **010**. Same items.

The kit's full parts list runs to eleven numbered items, several of which are
not named in this panel - the PVC handgrips, the rubber foot pads, the square end
caps and the M10 nuts. It is held on `xth-rails-specs-parts-list`.
