The Pulse (heart rate) readout displays your current heart rate in beats per minute during the workout. You must use both stainless steel sensors on the side hand rails to display your pulse; the value displays any time the upper display is receiving a pulse signal. You can also monitor your pulse with **the included telemetric chest strap**.

The heart-rate programs page adds that the telemetric chest strap is recommended for the best results with those programs. How to wear it, and what interferes with it, are on [the strap card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-wearing-the-chest-strap-and-its-2500-hour-battery.md) and [the erratic-reading card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-erratic-heart-rate-and-the-seven-interference-sources.md). The TR6.4 book of 2024 prints the same pulse-grip text without the chest strap sentence ([pulse grips card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-pulse-grips-both-stainless-sensors.md)).
