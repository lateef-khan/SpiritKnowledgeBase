**Step 4 - Cables.** "This step is easier if you have someone else helping to hold the console while
you connect the wires."

| Connect | to |
|---|---|
| computer cable (38) | mating connector (37) of the console |
| cables (127) | mating cables (53) on the right side |
| cable (152) | mating cable (131) |
| cables (128) | mating cables (54) |

**Step 5 - Console Assembly.** Insert **four washers (148)** and **four button head socket bolts (90)**
underneath the console and tighten with the combination Allen wrench and Phillips screwdriver (96).

The step 5 hardware box calls bolt #90 "3/8" x 3/4"" where the pack checklist says 5/16" x 3/4" - the
book prints both. The book does not name what cables 127/128/152 are (the drawing labels them only by
number).
