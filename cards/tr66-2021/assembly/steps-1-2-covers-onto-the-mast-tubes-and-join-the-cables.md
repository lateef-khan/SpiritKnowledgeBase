1. **Main Base Frame.** Remove all the parts from the carton and lay the main base frame on a flat level
   surface.
2. **Covers & Cables.** Slide the plastic covers (**R-42 & L-43**) over the left and right console mast
   upright tubes. Connect the **upper cable (38)** to the **lower cable (39)**.

**The covers go on before the mast tubes are fitted** - they cannot be slid on afterwards - and they are
screwed down only at step 6. Step 3 (mast tubes into the base frame) follows.
