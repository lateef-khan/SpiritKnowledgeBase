1. Go back and **fully tighten the six button head bolts** that connect the left and right console mast
   tubes to the base frame (left part-tightened at step 3), using the combination Allen wrench and
   Phillips screwdriver (96).
2. Attach the **console mast covers** to the **upright tubes (42 & 43)** with **four tapping screws (88, 5 x
   16 mm)** and **two Phillips head screws (95, M5 x 10 mm)**. Tighten with the same tool (96).

This is the last assembly step. **The book has no "final process" or power-on step** - the assembly
chapter ends here and the next page is folding and transport. The two front bolts with curved washers
from step 3 are not named in the "go back and tighten" sentence, which counts six.
