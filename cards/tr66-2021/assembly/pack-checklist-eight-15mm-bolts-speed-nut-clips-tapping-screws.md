| Step | Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | #93 | 5/16" x 15 mm Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 |
| 3 | #94 | 5/16" Curved Washer | 2 |
| 3 | #132 | M5 Speed Nut Clips | 4 |
| 5 | #148 | Ø8 mm x 1.5T Split Washer | 4 |
| 5 | #90 | 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 |
| 6 | #88 | 5 x 16 mm Tapping Screw | 4 |
| 6 | #95 | M5 x 10 mm Phillips Head Screw | 2 |

**Assembly tools:** #96 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver; #97 M6 L Allen
Wrench.

**Bolt #90 is printed two ways.** The checklist calls it 5/16" x 3/4"; the step 5 hardware box on the
instruction page calls it **3/8" x 3/4"**. The manual does not say which is right. The checklist also
prints the split washer's number as "148.." rather than "#148".
