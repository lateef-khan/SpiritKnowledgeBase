**Setup**

1. Press the **Fitness** key, then **Enter**. The display prompts you through the programming.
2. **Age (default 35)** - your age sets your recommended maximum heart rate for the bar graph
   and heart-rate features. Adjust, press Enter.
3. **Body Weight (default 150 lbs)**. The book notes the calorie count in this program "is only
   an estimate because the machine can't calculate calories expended while strength training".
4. **Preset top speed (3 mph or 5 kph)**; adjust, press Enter.
5. **Preset top incline (1.0%)**; adjust, press Enter.
6. **Number of intervals (default 10; choose 10, 20 or 30)**.
7. **Interval time (default 1:00)** - the duration of both the cardio and the strength
   intervals. On average you complete 15 to 20 repetitions of the strength exercise in a 0:30
   interval; the longer the interval, the less weight and speed required.
8. **Recovery time** after completing both the cardio and strength intervals.

**Program example, as printed** (10 intervals = 5 cardio and 5 strength; cardio and strength
intervals 0:30, recovery 1:00):

- 3:00 warm-up: 1:00 at 1 mph, 1:00 at 2 mph, 1:00 at 3 mph.
- 1st cardio interval, 0:30; the display then reads "STRENGTH INTERVAL 1 BEGIN DUMBBELL ROW".
- The user steps off to perform the strength exercise; the console counts down to 0:00 and
  beeps 3x to signal a return to the treadmill.
- "PRESS START TO BEGIN RECOVERY"; the user walks at 2 mph for 1:00.
- The 2nd cardio interval follows, and so on until 5 cardio, 5 strength and 5 recovery
  intervals are done; the five strength exercises run in the order listed in the manual.
- The last 2:00 are a cool-down walking at 2 mph.

With **20 intervals** you perform each strength exercise twice before moving to the next; with
**30** you perform each once, then repeat the sequence of all five a 2nd and 3rd time.

**Caution:** select a pair of dumbbells you can manoeuvre safely and effectively for the
strength interval time you chose.

The five exercises are on their own card. Spirit's Fusion program is the same idea with
different defaults (ten intervals of two minutes); see `not_to_be_confused_with`.
