Performed in this order, each "for the duration of the strength interval".

**1. Dumbbell Bent Over Row** - emphasis: mid/upper back and front of arms.
1. Grasp the dumbbells with an overhand grip, arms fully extended in front of the thighs, feet
   shoulder width apart.
2. Maintain a slightly arched lower back throughout.
3. Draw your elbows up and out until there is a 90-degree bend in your elbows.
4. Slowly lower the dumbbells to the start position.
5. Repeat.

**2. Push Up** - emphasis: chest, shoulders and back of arms. Two variations: if you cannot
perform it as illustrated, place your knees on the floor, a padded mat or a pillow. A wider
hand position emphasises the chest and shoulders; a narrower one the triceps.
1. Place your hands on the floor or on a set of dumbbells (caution: dumbbells may roll,
   especially if the ends are round); draw your stomach muscles towards your spine to keep a
   straight line between ankles and shoulders.
2. Begin with your elbows bent at 90 degrees.
3. Fully straighten your arms.
4. Repeat.

**3. Dumbbell Shoulder Press** - emphasis: shoulders.
1. Hold the dumbbells at shoulder height with an overhand (palms forward) or neutral (palms
   facing one another) grip.
2. Press straight overhead until the arms are fully extended.
3. Slowly lower to the start position.
4. Repeat.

**4. Dumbbell Tricep Overhead Extension** - emphasis: back of arms.
1. Hold the dumbbells at shoulder height behind your head with a neutral grip; elbows pointed
   straight ahead.
2. Raise the dumbbells overhead until the arms are fully extended; the upper arms stay
   stationary, pivoting at the elbows.
3. Slowly lower to the start position.
4. Repeat.

**5. Dumbbell Bicep Curl** - emphasis: front of arms.
1. Hold the dumbbells with an underhand grip (palms forward) beside your thighs.
2. Tuck your upper arms into your ribcage.
3. Curl the dumbbells to shoulder height, pivoting at the elbows.
4. Slowly lower to the start position.
5. Repeat.

Spirit's Fusion program uses the same five exercises; see `see_also`.
