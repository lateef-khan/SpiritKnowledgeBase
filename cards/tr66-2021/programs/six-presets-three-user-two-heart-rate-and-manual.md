The book states: "The TR6.6 Treadmill has **six factory preset programs, three user defined
programs, two heart rate programs and one manual program**."

- **Six presets** (pictured as Work and Incline profiles): Ascent, Glute Blast, Adventure,
  Xtreme, Interval and **X Country**. X Country's work picture is a rolling profile of
  moderate steps with a dip near the end; its incline picture has a raised block in the
  second half.
- **User programs**: the text says three, but the User Programs page names only **User 1 and
  User 2** and says a program "will be stored under either User 1 or User 2". **The count of
  three is not borne out anywhere else in the book.**
- **Two heart rate programs**: HR-1 Weight Control and HR-2 Cardiovascular.
- **One manual program.**
- A **Fitness program** (cardio and strength intervals with dumbbells) has its own key and its
  own pages but is not counted in the sentence above.

The TR6.4 has the same console family with five presets (no X Country), two user programs and
Manual, and no heart-rate or Fitness program.
