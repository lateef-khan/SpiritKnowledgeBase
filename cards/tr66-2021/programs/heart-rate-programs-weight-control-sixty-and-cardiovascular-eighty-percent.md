**The heart-rate programs use the incline, not the speed, to move your heart rate.** The book:
"Increases and decreases in elevation affect heart rate much more efficiently than changes in
speed. Additionally, changes in incline keep you in control of the machine's speed instead of
the machine controlling you."

- **Weight Control (HR-1)** tries to hold your heart rate at **60% of your Maximum Heart Rate**.
- **Cardiovascular (HR-2)** tries to hold it at **80%**.
- Maximum Heart Rate = **220 minus your age**, entered during setup.

**Caution, as printed:** the target used in HR-1 and HR-2 is a suggestion only for normal,
healthy individuals. Do not exceed your limits; you may not be able to reach your chosen
target. If in question, enter a higher age, which sets a lower target.

**Setup**

1. Press the **HR1/HR2** key: once shows HR1, twice shows HR2.
2. **Age (default 35)** - adjust and press Enter, or press Enter to accept.
3. **Body weight (default 150)** - adjust or accept, Enter.
4. **Time (default 30:00)** - adjust, Enter.
5. The display blinks the **default heart rate for this program**; adjust if you wish, Enter.
6. Press **Start**.

**You must maintain contact with the pulse sensors or wear the chest strap for the program
to work.** The book recommends the telemetric chest strap for the best results.

The TR65 also has HR1 at 60% and HR2 at 80%, but there HR1 adjusts the speed and HR2 the
incline; see `not_to_be_confused_with`.
