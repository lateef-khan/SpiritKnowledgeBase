Hardware step 3: **8 curved washers (#5), 8 Allen screws (#21) M8*16**; pre-assembled, 4 screws (20) on the computer and 2 screws (54) on the front post.

1. Connect the upper extension computer wire (19) to the lower computer wire (32).
2. Connect the front extension hand pulse wire (18) to the middle extension hand pulse wire (33).
3. Attach the front post (9) to the main frame. Secure using four curved washers (5) and four Allen screws (21). *Tip: avoid pinching the extension wires and handpulse wires.*
4. Attach the left stationary handlebar (12) to the front post (9). Secure using two curved washers (5) and two Allen screws (21). Repeat for the right stationary handlebar (11).
5. Connect the front extension hand pulse wire (18) to the pulse wire from the computer (10).
6. Connect the upper extension computer wire (19) to the wire from the computer (10). *Tip: avoid pinching the extension wires and pulse wires.*
7. Attach the computer (10) to the top bracket of the front post (9). Secure using four screws (20) which are pre-assembled on the back of the computer.
8. Attach the bottle holder (46) to the front post (9). Secure using two screws (54) which are pre-assembled on the front post (9).

Wires 18 and 19 are named twice: once joined to the lower wires at the base of the post (steps 1-2) and once joined to the computer at the top (steps 5-6). They are extension leads that run inside the post.
