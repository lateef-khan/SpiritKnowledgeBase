Hardware step 1: **2 Allen screws (#2) M8*75, 2 curved washers (#5), 2 spring washers (#4)**.

1. Attach the front stabilizer (3) to the main frame (1), securing with two Allen screws (2), two spring washers (4) and two curved washers (5).

The 2024 successor drops the spring washers and shortens the screws to M8*50 (the linked card).
