Hardware step 6: pre-assembled on the covers, **5 self-tapping screws (#53)**.

1. Attach the left rear support cover (47) to the left side of the support tube (40). Secure using two self-tapping screws (53).
2. Attach the right rear support cover (48) to the right side of the support tube (40). Secure using three self-tapping screws (53).

Two on the left, three on the right - five in all, and the covers are handed.
