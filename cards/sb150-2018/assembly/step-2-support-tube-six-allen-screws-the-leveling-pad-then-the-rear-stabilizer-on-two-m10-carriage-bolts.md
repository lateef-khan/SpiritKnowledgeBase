Hardware step 2: **6 Allen screws (#21) M8*16, 6 flat washers (#38), 2 carriage bolts (#23) M10*75, 2 curved washers (#24) 10*22*1.5, 2 domed nuts (#31) M10**, and the leveling pad (39).

1. Connect the middle extension hand pulse wire (33) with the rear extension hand pulse wire (34). Attach the support tube (40) to the main frame (1) and tighten with 6 sets of Allen screw (21) and flat washer (38). **Attach the leveling pad (39) to the bottom of the main frame (1).**
2. Attach the rear stabilizer (6) to the support tube (40) and tighten with 2 sets of carriage bolt (23), curved washer (24) and domed nut (31).

"Tip: Avoid pinching the Extension Wires."

The leveling pad is a single foot screwed into the underside of the main frame where the support tube joins it; the rear stabilizer's own end caps are the adjustable ones the user-direction page turns to stop a wobble.
