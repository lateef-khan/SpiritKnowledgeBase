**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

The treadmill uses a high-efficiency low-friction bed, and performance is best when the bed is kept clean.

- **Monthly**: wipe the edge of the belt and the area between the belt edge and frame with a soft damp cloth
  or paper towel, reaching as far under the belt edge as is practical. **Use water only — no cleaners or
  abrasives.** A mild soap and water solution with a nylon scrub brush will clean the top of the textured
  belt. Let the belt dry before use.
- **Belt dust** happens during break-in until the belt stabilises. Wipe the excess off with a damp cloth.
- **Monthly**: vacuum underneath the treadmill so dirt, dust and pet hair do not block air inlets or build
  up on the running belt.
- **Once a year**: remove the black motor hood and vacuum out accumulated dirt. **Unplug the power cord
  before this task.**
