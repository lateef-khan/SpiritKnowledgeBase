**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

The checklist entry is "computer shuts off when console is touched (on a cold day) while walking/running".

**The treadmill may not be grounded — static electricity is "crashing" the computer.** Go back to the
grounding instructions and confirm the outlet is properly installed and grounded.
