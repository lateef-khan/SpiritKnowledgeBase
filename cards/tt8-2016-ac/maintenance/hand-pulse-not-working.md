**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

No pulse displayed on the monitor:

1. Hands not on the hand pulse sensors, or only one hand on a sensor — **hold both hand pulse grips**.
2. The connector between the HANDPULSE wire and the console is not connected properly — connect the cable
   again.
3. The wires were damaged when connecting the HANDPULSE wire to the console — replace the cable.
4. The hand pulse board is broken — replace the console or the hand pulse board.
