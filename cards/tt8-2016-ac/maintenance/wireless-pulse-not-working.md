**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

**No pulse displayed**

1. The chest belt is not worn properly — check it has proper skin contact and is oriented correctly.
2. The distance is too far and exceeds the receiver range — use the chest belt **in front of the console,
   within 3 feet**.
3. The chest belt battery is weak or dead — replace it with a new **CR2032 lithium battery**.

**Erratic pulse display**

1. Another chest belt is in use around the treadmill — check for other chest belt use nearby.
2. Other magnetic field disturbance — change the position or direction of the treadmill.
3. The receiver is broken — replace the receiver.

A chest belt held too close to the treadmill points at a weak battery: again, a new **CR2032**.
