**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

The matrix treats the FAST/SLOW speed adjustment switch and the UP/DOWN incline adjustment switch as one
fault with one list:

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The connector of the SPEED CABLE and CONSOLE is not connected properly | Connect the cables again |
| The connector of the SPEED CABLE and the SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/CABLE is not connected properly | Check the wire of the speed adjustment switch; replace with a new cable |
| The button of the SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH is broken | Replace with a new speed adjustment switch |
| The connector of the SPEED CABLE or the SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/CABLE is damaged | Replace with a new cable |
