**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

First clean between the belt and deck to remove trapped debris. Use a clean, non-fraying rag, t-shirt or
light towel: halfway between the end of the treadmill and the motor cover, push the garment under the belt
until you can grasp it on both sides, drag it the length of the entire belt **1-2 times**, and remove it.

**Do not lubricate with anything other than approved lubricant.** The treadmill comes with one tube of
"Lube"; extra tubes and the Lube-n-Walk kit are available from an authorised dealer.

**Interval: the belt and deck come pre-lubricated, and subsequent lubrication should be done every 90 hours
of use, or sooner if the deck looks dry.** Reach between the belt and deck to verify lubrication is present
**every other month**.

If the lubricant dries out, friction between belt and deck rises and stresses the drive motor, drive belt
and electronic motor control board, which can cause catastrophic failure of those expensive components.
**Failure to lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty.**
