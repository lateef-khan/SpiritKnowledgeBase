**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

**Do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and locked position.** Secure the power cord to
avoid damage, and use both handrails to manoeuvre the unit.

**To fold**: make certain the treadmill is at **minimum incline**. Lift the running deck until it is secured
by the **locking telescoping tube assembly** in the centre back of the base.

**To unfold**: apply slight forward pressure on the running deck with one hand — at the rear roller area, to
relieve pressure on the locking system. Pull down on the **unlocking lever** and slowly lower the deck to
the floor. The deck will lower unassisted once it reaches about waist high.
