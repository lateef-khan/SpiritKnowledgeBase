**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| House breaker tripped | Reset it |
| Treadmill breaker tripped | Reset the treadmill breaker |
| Treadmill controller shut down and the LED would be ON | Belt/deck lubrication; turn the ON/OFF switch off and turn the power on again |

If the machine also stops immediately after START is pressed, the matrix puts that down to a broken
controller: turn the AC switch off and on again, then replace the controller and calibrate it.
