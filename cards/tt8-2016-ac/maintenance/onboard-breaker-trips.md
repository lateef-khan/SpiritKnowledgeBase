**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

The checklist row reads: **"Treadmill trips on board 10 amp circuit"** — high belt/deck friction.
The fix is belt and deck lubrication.

**The amp rating printed here differs between the TT8 variants.** This manual says 10 amp. Check the
rating stamped on the machine rather than assuming it matches another TT8.
