**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

- The incline motor is a **230 volt AC motor**. This manual gives no separate figure for a 120 Vac electronic power system, unlike its DC twin.
- It has **four wires: red, black, white and green**, plus one **3-pin position sensor cable**.
- **AC voltage on the red wire (UP)** increases the incline.
- **AC voltage on the black wire (DOWN)** decreases the incline.
- The **white wire (COM)** is neutral.
- The **green** wire is grounding.

**The decline motor works the same way**, driven from the decline control board rather than the main
controller.

When you refit an incline motor, land the wires on the controller as **red to UP, white to COM, black to
DOWN**.
