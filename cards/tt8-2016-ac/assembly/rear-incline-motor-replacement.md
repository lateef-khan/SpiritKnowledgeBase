**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

1. Remove the **Rear Adjustment Base (R)** and the **LOGO Cover (R)**, then remove the old incline motor.
2. **The rear incline motor must be adjusted to 210 mm minimum prior to installation.**
3. Use a combination **M8 Allen wrench** and **14 mm and 17 mm wrenches** to assemble the new incline motor.
4. Connect the incline motor wiring to the controller:
   - **Red wire to "UP"**
   - **White wire to "COM"**
   - **Black wire to "DOWN"**

The rear incline motor is a separate part from the front one and takes a **different length setting**
(210 mm rear versus 235 mm front). Do not carry one figure over to
the other motor.
