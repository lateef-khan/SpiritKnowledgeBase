**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to loosen the **2 Phillips head screws and 2 tapping screws** on the motor cover, then remove the
   motor cover.
2. Disconnect all lower controller wiring, use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the controller, fit the
   new lower controller and reconnect all wiring.

**The controller is drive-type specific.** This machine runs an AC motor through an inverter and a power board; a DC-motor controller will not work in it.
