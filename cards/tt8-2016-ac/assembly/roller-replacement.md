**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to loosen the **2 screws** on the rear adjustment base.
2. Use an **M6 L Allen wrench** to loosen the **2 screws** on the rear roller.
3. Remove the motor cover. Use a **19mm T-shaped socket wrench** to loosen the **4 screws**.
   Use a **14 mm open end wrench** to loosen the **1 belt tension screw** and loosen the drive belt. Use a
   **13 mm wrench** to loosen the front roller screws. Remove the front and back rollers and fit the new
   ones. Reassemble in reverse order; the running belt tension needs to be adjusted and centred.
4. Use the **14 mm open end wrench** to adjust belt tension with a tension measuring device. **Adjust to the
   white LBS area, between 70-75 LBS.** Use a **14 mm T-shaped socket wrench** to secure and tighten the 4
   securing screws.

**The manual contradicts itself on the socket size**: step 3 calls for a 19 mm T-shaped socket wrench on the 4 screws, and step 4 tightens the same 4 screws with a 14 mm T-shaped socket wrench. Every other TT8 manual uses 14 mm in both steps.
