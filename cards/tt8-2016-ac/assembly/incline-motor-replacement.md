**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

1. Adjust the treadmill to the **folded position**, then remove the old incline motor.
2. **The incline range must be adjusted to 235 mm minimum prior to installation.**
3. Use a combination **M8 Allen wrench** and a **14 mm wrench** to assemble the new incline motor.
4. Connect the incline motor wiring to the controller:
   - **Red wire to "UP"**
   - **White wire to "COM"**
   - **Black wire to "DOWN"**

Recalibrate after fitting.

**This manual gives two different figures for the same measurement.** The Installation of the Incline Motor section says the incline range must be adjusted to **225 mm minimum** prior to installation, while step 2 of the incline motor replacement says **235 mm minimum**. Both figures are printed in this manual; the other TT8 manuals of this generation say 235 mm. Confirm against the machine before fitting.
