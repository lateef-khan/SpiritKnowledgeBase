**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

**This is E-53H, one code in the inverter set. It is not any of the E0 to E7 codes used by the
DC-motor TT8 2016.**

Printed description: "Machine (front) incline motor Error"

The **front** incline motor. The rear incline motor has its own, differently shaped code: a bare E3.

Troubleshooting, in the manual's order:

1. Check whether the VR wire of the incline motor is connected or bad in contact.
2. Check if the power cord of the incline motor is properly connected/connected or poorly exposed.
3. Check if the incline motor is lock dead or bad in contact.
4. Re-calibration.
5. Replace the incline motor.
6. Replace the inverter.

The troubleshooting matrix prints this code without the hyphen, as **E53H**, in the row "GRADE window displays E53H": check the connector of the cable, calibrate the console, then replace the incline motor or the decline motor.
