**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

**This is E-29H, one code in the inverter set. It is not any of the E0 to E7 codes used by the
DC-motor TT8 2016.**

Printed description: "Ht High temperature display"

Ht as a display, not the E-42H warning. The manual gives no field check for this one.

Troubleshooting, in the manual's order:

1. Replace the inverter.
