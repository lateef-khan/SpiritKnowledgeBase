**AC model: TT8 2016 ST925A-YT030, AC drive motor driven by an inverter. The DC drive motor TT8 2016 (ST925-YT021) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

1. Press and hold **START, STOP and ENTER** together until the display shows **"Engineering mode"** (it may
   say maintenance menu, depending on version). Press the ENTER key.
2. Scroll the menu with the **Speed ▲/▼** keys. Use the STOP key to return to previous menu selection.

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| KEY TEST | Tests all the keys to make sure they are functioning |
| DISPLAY MODE | Turn off to have the console power down automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity |
| PAUSE MODE | On allows 5 minutes of pause; off pauses indefinitely |
| LUBE MESSAGE RESET | Resets the lube message and the odometer readings |
| KEY TONE | Turns the key beep on and off |
| CHILD LOCK | Locks the keypad. With child lock enabled the keypad will not operate until you press and hold **START and ENTER for 3 seconds** |
