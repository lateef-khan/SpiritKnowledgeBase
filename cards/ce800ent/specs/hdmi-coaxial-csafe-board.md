A separate small board in the console carries three connections, called out left to right:

- **Coaxial cable**
- **HDMI cable**
- **C-SAFE (5 PINS)**

This is a different board from the console transfer board on p. 18, which has its own HDMI socket
and its own 5-pin C-SAFE connector.
