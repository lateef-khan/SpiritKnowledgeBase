**Upper controllers (p. 7)** - **Display**, **Cooling Fan**, **Thumb Switch**, **Earphone Port**,
and, oddly, **Induction Break** with a photo of the flywheel brake.

**Lower controller and driver (p. 8)** - **Controller**, **Speed RPM Sensor**,
**PCB & Power Converter**.

Putting the induction brake under "Upper Controllers" is an error in the source: the brake is at
the flywheel, and the same photo is used again on p. 23 under "Flywheel definition function".
The manual spells it "Induction Break" on p. 7 and "Induction Brake" on p. 23.
