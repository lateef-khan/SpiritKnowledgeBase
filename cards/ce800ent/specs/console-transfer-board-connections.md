Seven connections are called out on the board in the console back cover:

- **HDMI cable** - the HDMI socket
- **Ethernet cable** - the RJ45 socket
- **Hand pulse (4 pins)** - the connector silkscreened **HP-RIGHT** (four ways)
- **Hand pulse (3 pins)** - the connector silkscreened **HP-LEFT**, JK3 (three ways)
- **C-SAFE cable (5 pins)** - the white five-way connector under the **C-SAFE** silkscreen
- **System cable (4 pins)** - the four-way header under the **+12V / JK5** silkscreen
- **System cable (6 pins)** - the manual's leader for this one ends on the shroud rather than on
  a connector; the only unused header of that size is the one silkscreened **CONTROL**

Other silkscreen visible on the board: **GND**, **SAFETY**, **JK2** beside HP-RIGHT.
