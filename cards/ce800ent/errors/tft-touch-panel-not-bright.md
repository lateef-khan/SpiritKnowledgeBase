| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Power cord isn't plugged into outlet | Check power cord to line equipment |
| TFT touch panel is broken | Check the power has 120VAC. Replace new console. |

**This row asks for 120VAC**, and unlike the CT850 and CVC800 versions it starts at the power cord
rather than at the panel. The CU900ENT prints a TFT row too, but with different causes and no
voltage at all: `cu900ent-tft-not-bright`. The LCD versions are on
`spirit-lcd-dim-or-incomplete` and the LED version on `ct850-2020-led-dim-or-incomplete`.

**This manual has no second row for dead segments.** The CT850, CVC800 and CU900ENT manuals all
carry one.
