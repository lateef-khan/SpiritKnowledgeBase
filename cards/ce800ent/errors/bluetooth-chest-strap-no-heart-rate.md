The condition is printed as
`Bluetooth chest strap heart rate monitor no function (No pulse displayed on monitor)`.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| No heart rate display on console | 1. Check the Bluetooth chest strap of power is enough. 2. Turn on Bluetooth on the console of system, tap the name of the Bluetooth device you want to pair with your Bluetooth chest strap. |

**Only the CE800ENT manual carries this row.** The CT850, CU900ENT and CVC800 manuals cover the
older 5 kHz analogue chest belt instead, on `spirit-wireless-chest-belt-no-pulse`, and that card
names a CR2032 battery and a 3-foot range. Neither figure is printed for the Bluetooth strap here.

Bluetooth itself is enabled under **Maintenance** in engineering mode.
