The manual calls out five things.

| Call-out | What it is |
|---|---|
| Bluetooth Heart rate Icon | Small icon at the bottom left of the touch screen |
| Stars, Stop, and LEVEL Control Keys | The physical keypad below the screen |
| TFT LCD Touch Panel & Program Menu | The screen itself |
| Adjustable fan angle | The fan grille in the SPIRIT badge panel under the screen |
| USB Charging port | Right of the fan grille |

**The call-out really is printed "Stars".** It labels the START key; the keypad in the drawing reads
**LEVEL down, START, STOP, LEVEL up**, with a headphone socket at its right-hand end.

The Home screen in the drawing carries four tiles - **Exercise Programs, Internet, Screen
Mirroring, Language** - and the line "To begin workout press START on the keypad below."

Screen Mirroring appears only on this drawing. The manual's Operation text never mentions it.

---

**The owner's manual draws the same console face and labels it differently** (`spirit-elliptical-ce800ent-owners-manual`,
p. 15). Its five call-outs are **Touchscreen**, **Adjustable fan angle**, **Tablet-friendly reading
rack**, **USB Charging port**, and **Start, Stop, and Level controls**.

Two things follow from comparing the two drawings:

- **The tablet-friendly reading rack is named only in the owner's manual**, and the **Bluetooth heart
  rate icon** and the **headphone socket** are named only in the service manual. Both parts are on the
  machine; neither document lists all of them.
- The owner's manual **does not print "Stars"**. It writes the keypad as **Start, Stop, and Level
  controls**, which confirms the service manual's "Stars" call-out is a typo for START.

**The claim above that Screen Mirroring is never mentioned in the Operation text is true only of the
service manual.** The owner's manual devotes two full pages to it, with separate iOS and Android
procedures - see `ct800ent-2022-console-screen-mirroring-ios` and `ct800ent-2022-console-screen-mirroring-android`. It also places it differently: the owner's
manual says Screen Mirroring is selected from the **Entertainment screen**, while this drawing puts it
on the **Home screen**. Neither document resolves the other.

**What the owner's manual adds about power-up** (p. 16, STARTING OPERATION): when the power is turned
ON the screen shows a **brief loading screen** and then the **Home Screen**, which indicates the
machine is ready to operate. Its captions read "Home Screen: Begin operation by touching the icons"
and "Quick operating buttons are conveniently located for basic Elliptical functions".

**Two pages of this owner's manual had no text layer and have since been recovered by OCR at 300 dpi.**
They add two things to this card.

**The quick operating buttons are named.** The workout screen shot on PDF page 24 (printed p. 22)
prints a bar across the bottom reading **ENTERTAINMENT | PROGRAM | DISPLAY | INTERNET**, and above it
a data row labelled **Distance, Calories, Time Elapsed, Age, RPM** with **LEVEL** at the left. That
bar is what the p. 16 caption means by "quick operating buttons conveniently located for basic
Elliptical functions". **The screen's own numbers in that shot are the manual's sample values, not
anything about a real machine.** The three view buttons below it read **Simple**, an unreadable middle
word, and **Dashboard**; the text of the manual names the middle one **Track**
(`ce800ent-console-workout-display-views`).

**The console drawing's screen is partly legible.** PDF page 17 (printed p. 15) OCRs a **Home** label
at the top of the screen and two tiles - **Exercise Programs** and **Internet**. **A third tile is
present but its label does not survive OCR**, and no fourth tile can be read. The service manual
drawing above lists four tiles; only two of them can be confirmed from this document, so do not treat
this page as a second source for the Screen Mirroring or Language tiles.
