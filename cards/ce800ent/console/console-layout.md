The manual calls out five things.

| Call-out | What it is |
|---|---|
| Bluetooth Heart rate Icon | Small icon at the bottom left of the touch screen |
| Stars, Stop, and LEVEL Control Keys | The physical keypad below the screen |
| TFT LCD Touch Panel & Program Menu | The screen itself |
| Adjustable fan angle | The fan grille in the SPIRIT badge panel under the screen |
| USB Charging port | Right of the fan grille |

**The call-out really is printed "Stars".** It labels the START key; the keypad in the drawing reads
**LEVEL down, START, STOP, LEVEL up**, with a headphone socket at its right-hand end.

The Home screen in the drawing carries four tiles - **Exercise Programs, Internet, Screen
Mirroring, Language** - and the line "To begin workout press START on the keypad below."

Screen Mirroring appears only on this drawing. The manual's Operation text never mentions it.
