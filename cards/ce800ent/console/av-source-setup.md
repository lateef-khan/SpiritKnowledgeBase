The sixth engineering mode entry sets the audio/visual source. Two choices, word for word:

1. **C-Safe protocol.** HDMI transfer to screen on TV of Entertainment function.
2. **CAB protocol.** Connect a set-box transfer to screen on TV of Entertainment function.

The manual gives no cable specification, no port location and no instruction on which to pick. The
bike in the same console family says explicitly that **only one of C-SAFE or CAB may be chosen**.
