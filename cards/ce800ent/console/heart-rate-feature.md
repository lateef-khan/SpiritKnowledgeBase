The **Pulse (Heart Rate)** value on the screen shows the current heart beats per minute.

- You must use **both** the left and right stainless steel sensors to pick up your pulse.
- Pulse values are displayed any time the computer is receiving a signal from the hand pulse sensors.
- You may use the hand pulse sensors while in **Heart Rate Control**.
- The console will also pick up **wireless heart rate transmitters that are Polar and Bluetooth
  compatible**.

**The sentence about wireless transmitters names the CU800ENT, not the CE800ENT.** That is a
copy-and-paste defect in the same paragraph block that is headed "FUNCTIONS OF THIS UPRIGHT BIKE".
Read it as the CE800ENT: the console drawing on the previous page carries a Bluetooth heart rate
icon, which supports the claim being about this machine.

The manual gives no chest strap pairing procedure for this console. Bluetooth is enabled under
**BT Setup** in the Maintenance menu.
