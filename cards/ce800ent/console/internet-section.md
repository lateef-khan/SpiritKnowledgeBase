> The Internet section offers various streaming, news, and social media options. The machine must be
> connected to the internet in order for the apps to work. Simply click on the app of your choice to
> connect. Follow any on-screen prompts to continue login or other authorizations as needed.

**The manual names no individual app.** It gives no list, no version and no statement about which
services are licensed in which territory.

The machine reaches the internet over Wi-Fi or Ethernet, both set up under **Maintenance** in
engineering mode. `System & Apps` in that same menu is described as "Update APPS (internet function
of APPs)".
