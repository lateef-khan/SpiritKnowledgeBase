The console has a built-in **earphone sound output jack**.

> The Jack is not an audio input jack.

Volume must be controlled on the **speaker icon** on the screen, not on the jack.

So you cannot feed audio from a phone into the console through this socket. The drawing on the
Display Windows page puts the socket at the right-hand end of the keypad.
