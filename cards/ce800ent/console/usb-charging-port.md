The on-console USB port charges a personal device while you work out. The equipment power must be on.

The manual's three notes, word for word in substance:

- **A USB charging cable is not included.** Make sure a compatible one is used.
- Your device's charging icon may or may not show. Depending on the current your device needs, the
  icon may not light while the device is still charging, though possibly at a lower rate.
- The port provides up to **5 Vdc / 1.0 amp** and meets **USB 2.0** regulations.

> You will not be able to save your workout data to a USB via this port; it is used for charging
> purposes only.

The USB slot used for software updates is a different thing and is reached through the Maintenance
menu.
