The touchscreen operates all functions. Touch any button on the screen, or use the quick buttons
along the bottom. On the lower part of the console there is a **Start** button to begin the workout,
a **Stop** button to pause or stop programs, and a **Level** button to change workload.

**Quick start** is the quickest way to begin:

1. Wait for the console to power up.
2. Press **Start**.

In Quick Start the **Time counts up from zero**, all workout data starts to accrue, and the workload
may be adjusted manually by pressing on the screen or the **Up and Down buttons on the lower control
panel**.

defect: this is the elliptical service manual. The instructions themselves are consistent with the
console drawn on the previous page.

**The owner's manual settles the copy-and-paste defect.** In `spirit-elliptical-ce800ent-owners-manual`
p. 17 the same paragraph is headed **"FUNCTIONS OF THIS ELLIPTICAL"**, not "FUNCTIONS OF THIS UPRIGHT
BIKE". The body text is otherwise word for word identical - Start to begin, Stop to pause or stop
programs, Level to change workload, Quick Start counting up from zero, and adjusting the workload on
the screen or on the Up and Down buttons on the lower control panel.

So the instructions on this card are confirmed by a second document for this machine, and the bike
heading in the service manual is a heading error only.
