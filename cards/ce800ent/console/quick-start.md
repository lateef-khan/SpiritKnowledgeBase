The touchscreen operates all functions. Touch any button on the screen, or use the quick buttons
along the bottom. On the lower part of the console there is a **Start** button to begin the workout,
a **Stop** button to pause or stop programs, and a **Level** button to change workload.

**Quick start** is the quickest way to begin:

1. Wait for the console to power up.
2. Press **Start**.

In Quick Start the **Time counts up from zero**, all workout data starts to accrue, and the workload
may be adjusted manually by pressing on the screen or the **Up and Down buttons on the lower control
panel**.
