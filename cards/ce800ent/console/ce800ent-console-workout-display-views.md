Once a workout begins, **three preset views appear as buttons along the bottom of the screen**. Tap
one to switch. You may change view at any time during the workout.

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Simple** | A **muscle activation profile** for the current workout, an overview of the **Resistance profile**, plus elapsed Distance, Calories/Hr, Total Time, Heart Rate and RPM |
| **Track** | A **virtual track** that corresponds with the current workout, plus elapsed Distance, number of **Laps**, Calories, Total Time, Heart Rate and RPM |
| **Dashboard** | A **virtual dashboard** mimicking a typical elliptical display, with elapsed Distance, Calories/Hr, Calories, Total Time, Heart Rate and RPM |

All three lists end "as well as other exercise data", and the manual never says what that other data
is.

**The Simple view is the only one that shows a resistance profile**, and the **Track view is the only
one that counts laps**. **Calories/Hr appears on Simple and Dashboard; plain Calories on Track and
Dashboard.**

**This machine has no dot matrix track ring.** The track here is drawn on the touchscreen as one of
these three views, not built from lights around a window.
