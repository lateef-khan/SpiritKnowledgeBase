> Each of the programs can be customized with your personal information and changed to suit your
> needs. Some of the information asked for is necessary to ensure the readouts are correct. You will
> be asked for your **Age** and **Weight**.

- **Age** is necessary during the **Heart Rate control** program, to make sure the settings are
  right for you. Otherwise the work settings could be too high or too low.
- **Weight** helps calculate a more correct **Calorie** reading. The manual is explicit that it
  cannot provide an exact calorie count, only as close as possible.

**This service manual prints no program list.** It names Heart Rate control here, and the console
drawing has an "Exercise Programs" tile, but there is no table of program names, no profile chart
and no per-program setup sequence anywhere in the manual.

**All ten Spirit commercial elliptical owner's manuals open their programs chapter with this same
paragraph** - CE800 2013 printed page 15, CE800 2021 page 17, CE800 2023 page 18, CE850 2016 page
20, CE850 2020 page 23, CE850 2022 page 24, CE900 2021 page 17, CE900 2025 page 19, CES880 2025
page 39, and the CE800ENT owner's manual. Three wordings differ:

- The CE800 2013, CE850 2016, CE850 2020, CE850 2022 and CE900 2021 add one sentence: entering your
  **Age** also ensures the **Heart Rate bar graph** shows the correct number.
- The CES880 2025 shortens it: entering your Age is necessary during the **Heart Rate programs**,
  and your weight is used to give a more accurate calorie count.
- The CE900 2025 drops the closing "Some good news..." sentence from the calorie note.

**Nine of the ten also print a CALORIE NOTE**, which the service manual does not: calorie readings
on every piece of exercise equipment, in a gym or at home, are not accurate and tend to vary widely.
They are meant only as a guide to monitor your progress from workout to workout; the only accurate
measurement is in a clinical setting connected to a host of machines, because every person burns
calories at a different rate. Most add that you continue to burn calories at an accelerated rate for
at least an hour after finishing. **The CE800ENT owner's manual does not print the calorie note.**

**The "no program list" sentence above is true of the *service* manual only.** The CE800ENT
**owner's** manual does print a program list - ten programs on the touchscreen menu, in
`ce800ent-programs-touchscreen-program-menu`.
