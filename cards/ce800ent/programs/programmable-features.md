> Each of the programs can be customized with your personal information and changed to suit your
> needs. Some of the information asked for is necessary to ensure the readouts are correct. You will
> be asked for your **Age** and **Weight**.

- **Age** is necessary during the **Heart Rate control** program, to make sure the settings are
  right for you. Otherwise the work settings could be too high or too low.
- **Weight** helps calculate a more correct **Calorie** reading. The manual is explicit that it
  cannot provide an exact calorie count, only as close as possible.

**This service manual prints no program list.** It names Heart Rate control here, and the console
drawing has an "Exercise Programs" tile, but there is no table of program names, no profile chart
and no per-program setup sequence anywhere in the manual.
