**Two figures on this machine differ from every other Spirit commercial elliptical.** Sprint and Rest
times run **20 to 90 seconds** here, not 20 to 60; and the 3-minute warm-up runs at **50% of the
sprint speed selected previously**, not at a fixed resistance level 5.

1. Select the program, then press **Enter** to begin customising it with your personal data. You are
   asked for the number of **intervals**: default **10**, range **3 to 15**. One interval equals 1
   Sprint and 1 Rest segment.
2. Enter the **Interval time, Sprint time and Rest time**. Sprint or Rest time may be selected from
   **20 to 90 seconds**. After the interval time is set, set the intensity training to **Sprint
   level** and **Rest level** - the resistance levels you will experience during the workout.
3. Press **Start** to begin. The program starts with a **3-minute warm-up** at a resistance level
   set to **50% of the sprint speed selected previously**. You may adjust the resistance manually
   during warm-up.

**The manual states no cool-down for this machine.** It also gives no pedal-rpm target and does not
describe the dot matrix as a speed display, all of which the button-console manuals do.
