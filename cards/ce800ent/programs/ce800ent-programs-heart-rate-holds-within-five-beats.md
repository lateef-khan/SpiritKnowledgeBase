The default value is **65% of your projected rate maximum**. You have the option of changing your
target heart rate. **The machine will attempt to keep you within five beats of your target heart
rate.**

That is the whole of what the owner's manual says about this program. It prints **no numbered
setup steps**, unlike the button-console CE800, CE850, CE900 and CES880 manuals, and it offers no
65%-or-80% pair of programs - the touchscreen menu lists a single "Heart Rate".

The 65% default agrees with this manual's own training-zone page, which recommends 65% of maximum
heart rate for burning fat - see `ce800ent-programs-target-zone-of-117-to-153-beats`.
