Selecting **Exercise Programs** on the Home Screen opens a menu of ten programs:

Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, HIIT, Interval, Heart Rate, Custom, Constant Power, and Fitness Test.

To select and start one:

1. Tap the program on the screen, then press **Enter** to begin. Customise the program on the
   following screens by entering your data, or press **Start** to begin the program using the
   default settings.
2. Press **Start** to begin your workout, or **Stop** to return to the previous screen.

*The manual numbers these steps 1 and 3; there is no step 2 on the page.*

**There is no Strength program on this machine** and no numbered program keys - selection is by
tapping the touchscreen.

**This corrects an earlier card.** `ce800ent-programmable-features` records that the CE800ENT
*service* manual prints no program list. The owner's manual does; this is it.

Manual mode on this console is described in one line: the level of resistance is controlled by the
user, and you may increase or decrease levels at any time during your workout.

**The Time, Age and Weight tabs.** On the Manual setup screen you press the **Time** tab to select
the workout duration and enter a value **between 5 and 99 minutes**, then repeat for **Age** and
**Weight**, then press **Start** to begin. *(This page is printed as a picture; the figures come
from an OCR recovery of PDF page 24, printed page 22.)*

The program-profile screens carry one more instruction: **press Enter to begin the workout, or use
the arrows to view other Program Profiles.**
