To find the benefit range you want to train in, first work out your **Maximum Heart Rate**:
**220 minus your age**. Your heart rate training zone is **50% to 90%** of that maximum.

**This manual prints 65% and 85%.** 65% of your MHR is recommended for burning fat, 85% for
strengthening the cardiovascular system, and 65% to 85% is the zone to stay in for maximum benefit.

Worked example for someone **40 years old**:

- 220 - 40 = **180** (maximum heart rate)
- 180 x .65 = **117** beats per minute (65% of maximum)
- 180 x .85 = **153** beats per minute (85% of maximum)
- So for a 40 year old the training zone would be **117 to 153** beats per minute.

If you enter your age during programming the console performs this calculation automatically.

**This figure agrees with the machine's Heart Rate program**, whose default target is 65% of
projected maximum - the only one of the ten Spirit commercial elliptical manuals where the
training-zone page and the heart rate program agree.

The page ends by saying that with all Spirit Fitness Ellipticals you may use the heart rate monitor
feature without using the Heart Rate program, and that when it is used **with** the Heart Rate
programs the machine will automatically adjust **resistance** to maintain the desired heart rate.

**Four different figure pairs appear across the ten Spirit commercial elliptical owner's manuals** -
see the not-to-be-confused-with links.
