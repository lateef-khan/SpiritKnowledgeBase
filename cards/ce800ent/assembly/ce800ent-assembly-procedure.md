## Step one - rear rail and rail support

1. Slide the Rear Rail Assembly (No.2) under the rear oval-shaped stabilizer tube on
   the Main Frame (No.1). From the top of the stabilizer secure with 2 Button Head
   Socket Bolts (No.127) and 2 Flat Washers (No.130). From the side secure with 4
   Button Head Bolts (No.128) and 4 Curved Washers (No.129).
2. Install the Rail Support Assembly (No.3) onto the Main Frame (No.1) and Rear Rail
   Assembly (No.2) using 2 Button Head Socket Bolts (No.127) and 2 Flat Washers
   (No.130) on top, and 2 Button Head Bolts (No.128) and 2 Curved Washers (No.129) at
   the rear.
3. Install the 4 Tube Stabilizer Covers (No.83/84, 2 of each) using 4 Screws (No.97).

**There is no centre aluminium rail step here.** The LED CE800 bolts a Center Aluminum
Rail (No.72) on in its step one; this machine has a **Rail Support Assembly (No.3)**
instead, and its carton list still says "1 Center Aluminum Rail". The two are not the
same part and item 72 is not in this manual's parts list.

## Step two - footplates and pedal arms

1. Attach the Footplates (No. L 67) to the pedal mounting plate with 4 Screws
   (No.131).
2. Slide the Pedal Arm Assembly (No.5) onto the crank axle of the main frame. Do not
   force it - the bearings can be damaged. Secure with a Bolt (No.126) and a Flat
   Washer (No.106).
3. Install the Cover (No.64) over the pedal arm connection at the rotation axle with
   a Screw (No.119).
4. Install the Roller Wheel Cover (No.69) with 2 Screws (No.97).
5. Repeat all steps with the opposite Pedal Arm Assembly (No.6).

## Step three - console mast, swing arms and bottle holder

1. Unravel the Computer Cable (No.152), CSAFE Connecting Wire (No.150) and
   Communication Cable (No.149), and snake them through the bottom of the Console
   Mast (No.11) and out the top. To install the mast tube, first insert **3** Socket
   Head Cap Bolts (No.132) into the mainframe, locate the mast on its position and
   tighten those three. The **last** bolt (No.132) goes in with the Flat Washer
   (No.107).
2. Mount the Console Mast Covers (No. L80 / R81) with 4 Screws (No.134).
3. Install 2 Wave Washers (No.133) onto the console mast axle, then install the Swing
   Arms (L9) (R10) onto the axles. Do not force them or use a hammer. Secure with a
   Bolt (No.135) and Washer (No.138) on each arm.
4. Attach the bottom of the swing arms to the rod ends of the pedal arms using 2
   Shoulder Bolts (No.137) and 2 Nylon Nuts (No.136).
5. Attach the Water Bottle Holder Clamp (158) to the console mast (11) with 2 M5x12mm
   Philips head screws (97), then mount the Water Bottle Holder (159) to the clamp
   using 2 more (97).

**Three cables go up this mast, not one.** The LED CE800 snakes only the computer
cable; this machine adds the CSAFE connecting wire and the communication cable. Get
all three up before the mast is bolted down.

## Step four - console and beauty covers

1. Plug the Computer Cable (No.152), CSAFE Connecting Wire (No.150), Communication
   Cable (No.149) and Handpulse w/Cable Assembly (No.40/41) into the corresponding
   connectors on the back of the Console. Mount the console to the mast with 4 Screws
   (No.97).
2. Install 4 Swing Arm Covers (No.77/76 left & 78/79 right) with 6 Screws (No.134).
3. Install 4 Covers (No.74 x 2 & 75 x 2) to the ends of the pedal arms with 4 Screws
   (No.97) and 4 Screws (No.134). **Do not install these upside down** - there is an
   arrow on the inside of each cover showing the direction.
4. Install the 4 Covers (No.72 x 2 & 73 x 2) at the bottom of the swing arms using 4
   Screws (No.134) and 4 Screws (No.97).
5. Install the Front Stabilizer Cover (No.66) with **4** Screws (No.97).

*The LED CE800 fits its front stabilizer cover with two screws. This one says four.*

## The console is given two item numbers in one step

Step four item 1 says to plug the cables into "the back of the **Console (No.31)**"
and then to mount "the **Console (No. 30)**" to the mast. The parts list has **31
Console Assembly, quantity 1**, and no item 30 at all. **Item 31 is the console;
30 is a misprint.**

## What the service manual says about the same joints

The CE800ENT service manual carries replacement procedures for the console, the
console mast and the swing arm. Two of its figures do not agree with this manual -
see [the console card](console-replacement.md) and
[the swing arm card](swing-arm-replacement.md). The console mast figures do
agree: four M8x25mm bolts, the flat washer on the last one.

## The 2024 redesign reprints this build

The **CE800ENT 2024** manual prints these four steps word for word and its four
hardware tables row for row. The rail support assembly is still item **No.3** and the
carton panel still promises a **Center Aluminum Rail** the build never fits.

**No cable was added or dropped.** Step three still threads the Computer Cable
(No.152), the CSAFE Connecting Wire (No.150) and the Communication Cable (No.149), and
the word HDMI appears nowhere in either printing. One machine in the same 2024
redesign did lose a cable - see
[the CR800ENT card](../../cr800ent-2024/assembly/cr800ent-2024-assembly-hdmi-wire-dropped.md).
