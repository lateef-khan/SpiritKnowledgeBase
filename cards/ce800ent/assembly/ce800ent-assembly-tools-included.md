- 13/14mm Wrench
- 14/17mm Wrench
- Phillips Screwdriver
- L Allen Wrench
- M12 Allen Wrench

## The parts list carries all five, and gives the L wrench no size

| # | Printed as |
|---|---|
| 139 | 13/14mm Wrench |
| 140 | L Allen Wrench |
| 141 | M12 L Allen Wrench |
| 142 | 14/17mm Wrench |
| 143 | Phillips Head Screw Driver |

**The panel and the parts list agree here**, five tools and five items - unlike the
LED CE800 manuals, whose panel lists a 12mm wrench their parts list has no item for.

**Item 140 is given no size anywhere.** The build needs an Allen key for the M8x25mm
socket head cap bolts of the console mast and the 5/16"x15mm socket head cap bolts of
the pedal arms; item 140 is the one, and the manual never says what it fits.

## This is not the LED CE800 carton

The LED CE800 cartons hold six tools - a 12mm wrench, a 17mm wrench and a combination
M6 Allen/screwdriver this machine does not have. This carton adds a **14/17mm**
wrench in place of the plain 17mm. See
[the LED tools card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ce800-assembly-tools-included.md).

**No 14mm wrench on its own.** The noise item says to adjust the two levelling pads
with a 14mm wrench; both wrenches above have a 14mm end.
