The drawing is not headed and the labels are not lettered. Eight words point at the
machine:

- Console
- Console fan
- Hand pulse sensors
- Swing arms
- Water bottle holder
- Flywheel
- Pedals
- Slide rails

**Three labels the LED CE800 drawing carries are absent here**: console mast, console
mast cover and the front, main and rear frame labels. The LED drawing names eleven
parts (twelve on the 2023 printing); this one names eight and adds **slide rails**,
which no LED drawing labels at all. The LED drawing calls the grips **pulse sensors**;
this one calls them **hand pulse sensors**. Same part, different words.

The **water bottle holder is on this drawing**, as it is on the 2023 LED drawing and
not on the 2021 one. See [the bottle holder card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ce-assembly-water-bottle-holder.md).

**Nothing on this diagram is levellers or transport wheels**, although the parts list
carries item 91, Transportation Wheel, quantity 2, and the noise item names two
levelling pads.
