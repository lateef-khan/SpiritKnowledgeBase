**Step 1:** Follow the steps of **"Idler Wheel Assembly replacement"** to remove idler wheel
assembly.

**Step 2:** Use an **11mm open-end wrench** to remove flywheel **4 locking bolts (`1/4"*3/4"` hex head
bolt)**, **4 flat washers (`1/4"*19`)**, and **4 spring washers (1/4")**. **Unplug the power cable**
then remove the flywheel.

**Step 3:** To do the reverse of above steps to reassemble the flywheel and other parts.

**The flywheel on this machine is powered.** Step 2 unplugs a power cable at the flywheel - this is a
generator flywheel, not a free one. Unplug it before you pull the wheel.

The belt tension figure that belongs to the refit is printed in 8-7, not here. Set it after the idler
wheel assembly is back on.

**The CVC800 climber's flywheel step is a different job.** It uses a **15mm** wrench on flywheel nuts
and a **17mm** box wrench to centre the belt, and it names no power cable.
