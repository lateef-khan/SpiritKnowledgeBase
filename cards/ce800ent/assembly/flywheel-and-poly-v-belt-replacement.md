**Step 1:** Follow the steps of **"Idler Wheel Assembly replacement"** to remove idler wheel
assembly.

**Step 2:** Use an **11mm open-end wrench** to remove flywheel **4 locking bolts (`1/4"*3/4"` hex head
bolt)**, **4 flat washers (`1/4"*19`)**, and **4 spring washers (1/4")**. **Unplug the power cable**
then remove the flywheel.

**Step 3:** To do the reverse of above steps to reassemble the flywheel and other parts.

**The flywheel on this machine is powered.** Step 2 unplugs a power cable at the flywheel - this is a
generator flywheel, not a free one. Unplug it before you pull the wheel.

The belt tension figure that belongs to the refit is printed in the idler wheel section (8-7 here, 10-7 in the
CE800 (2020) book; the XE890B and XE795 books set 190 Hz in their idler sections), not here. Set it after the
idler wheel assembly is back on.

**The CVC800 climber's flywheel step is a different job.** It uses a **15mm** wrench on flywheel nuts
and a **17mm** box wrench to centre the belt, and it names no power cable.

**The CE800 (2020) book prints this word for word as 10-8.** The 2017 CE800 (XE890B) book and both XE795 books print
the same job with the same hardware - **11 mm wrench, `1/4" x 3/4"` hex head bolt, `Ø1/4" x 19 m/m` flat washers,
`Ø1/4"` spring washer** - but call the part the **Induction Brake**, say "remove power controller wire" for the
power cable, print no count of four, and add a refit instruction: **adjust the position of the drive belt to the
centre of the drive pulley** (according to the idler wheel assembling procedure). The 2023 XE795 book lists the
prerequisites as 9.3, 9.4, 9.6, 9.7 and 9.8.
