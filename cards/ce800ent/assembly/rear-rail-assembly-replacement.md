**Step 1:** Use an **L-Allen wrench** to remove the bolts, flat washers, and spring washers which
locking the **rail support assembly**.

**Step 2:** Use a screwdriver to remove the **rear stabilizer cover (A) & (B)**.

**Step 3:** Use a **6mm L-Allen wrench** to remove all the bolts, flat washers, **curve washers**
which locking the rear rail assembly.

**Step 4:** To do the reverse of the above steps to reassemble the rear rail assembly.

**This procedure gives no fastener sizes and no counts.** Every other procedure in section 8 prints
them; 8-9 prints only "the bolts, flat washers, and spring washers". It also does not say what size
L-Allen wrench step 1 needs - only step 3 names 6mm. Bag the hardware as it comes off.

Step 3 is the only place in this manual that names a **curve washer**. It is a different part from
the flat and spring washers in step 1; keep the three kinds apart.
