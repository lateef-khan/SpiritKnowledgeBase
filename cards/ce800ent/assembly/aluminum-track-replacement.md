**Step 1:** Remove the **rear stabilizer cover (A) & (B)**.

**Step 2:** Use a **12mm open-end wrench** to remove **3 bolts (`5/16"*3/4"` hex head bolt)** then
remove the aluminum track.

**Step 3:** To do the reverse of the above steps to reassemble.

Three bolts hold the track. This is the last procedure in the CE800ENT manual - section 8 ends here - and the last in the CE800 (2020), XE890B and XE795 books too.

**Four more books print the same three bolts.** The CE800 (2020) book prints this word for word as 10-10. The 2017
CE800 (XE890B) book and both XE795 books word step 2 as "Use 12mm wrench to remove `5/16" x 3/4"` hex head bolt x
3pcs and **retaining bracket**, aluminum track"; the XE890B book keeps the rear stabilizer cover step ("refer to the
method of removing Rail Assembly"), and the two XE795 books print no cover step at all.
