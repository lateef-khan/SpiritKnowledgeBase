**Step 1:** Remove the **rear stabilizer cover (A) & (B)**.

**Step 2:** Use a **12mm open-end wrench** to remove **3 bolts (`5/16"*3/4"` hex head bolt)** then
remove the aluminum track.

**Step 3:** To do the reverse of the above steps to reassemble.

Three bolts hold the track. This is the last procedure in the manual - section 8 ends here.
