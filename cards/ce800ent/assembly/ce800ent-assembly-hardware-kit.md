## One item number, forty screws

Item **97, M5 x 12mm Phillips head screw**, appears in all four panels and the parts
list totals **40** of them. Item **134**, the 3.5 x 12mm sheet metal screw, totals
**18**. The four panels above add to 28 of item 97 and 18 of item 134, so the extra
twelve screws of item 97 go to parts fitted at the factory.

## The wave washer count does not close

Step three fits **2** wave washers, item 133, onto the console mast axle. The parts
list says the machine carries **3** of item 133 (Ø25 wave washer). The service manual
tells you to remove a wave washer from **both** the left and right axle. Two go on
here; the third is elsewhere on the machine and this manual never says where.

## The panel and the service manual disagree on the swing arm bolt

This panel says the bolt that secures each swing arm to the mast axle is item **135,
a 3/8" x 3/4" hex head bolt**, and the parts list agrees. The service manual's swing
arm procedure calls the same fastener a **3/8" x 15mm** hex head bolt. Three-quarters
of an inch is about 19mm, not 15. See [the swing arm card](swing-arm-replacement.md)
before quoting a size.

**The console mounting screw disagrees too.** Step four mounts the console with 4
Screws (No.97), which this panel and the parts list both call **M5 x 12mm**. The
service manual's console replacement says **4 bolts M5 x 10mm**. Item 131 in this
manual is the M5 x 10mm screw, and it is used on the footplates, not the console.

## The 2024 redesign reprints all four tables

The **CE800ENT 2024** manual prints these four tables row for row - the same item
numbers, the same descriptions and the same quantities. The counts that do not close
in the older book do not close in this one either.
