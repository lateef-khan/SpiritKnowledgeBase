**Step 1:** Use a screwdriver to remove **3 screws (`3.5*12mm`)** of handle bar cover then remove the
covers.

**Step 2:** Use a **14mm open-end wrench** to remove the **hex head bolt (`3/8"*15mm`)** and
**flat washer (`3/8"*30*2.0T`)**.

**Step 3:** Use a screwdriver to remove the **connecting arm cover (A) and (B)**.

**Step 4:** Use a **17mm open-end wrench** and **12mm L-Allen wrench** to remove the bolt of
**rod end bearing**.

**The manual prints no reassembly step here.** Every other procedure in section 8 ends with a
"reverse of the above steps" line; 8-2 stops at step 4. Reassembly is the reverse, but the manual
does not say so, and it names no torque.

Two later procedures tell you to come back here, and both misprint the reference as **"10-2"**.
There is no section 10 in this manual - they mean this section, 8-2.
