| Flywheel | Steel cable | SN ranges |
|---|---|---|
| K500040 | K020046 | 14 ranges, e.g. 5350182011036369~036956 |
| K500021 | K020005 | 29 ranges, e.g. 5350182011035781~036368 |

The sheet carries one "Before 2021/08/19 is K500021+K020005" note, on a row already assigned K500040+K020046. Match by SN range, not by date. The full range table is the source text.
