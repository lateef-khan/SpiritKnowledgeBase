Use screwdriver (185 or 186) for the plastic parts. Unlike the 2016 book, the incline bottom cover here fits without powering up first, and there is a tablet holder step.

1. **Connecting Arm Covers B (L) and (R), (112 and 111)**, on the Connecting Arm (L) (8) with **two Phillips Head Screws (139)** and **two Sheet Metal Screws (142)**; then **Connecting Arm Covers A (R) and (L), (109 and 110)**, on the lower Lower Handle Bar (L) (13) the same way using the Short driver (185). Repeat on the other side.
2. **Slide Wheel Covers (104)** above each side of the Slide Wheel with **four Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
3. **Middle Stabilizer Covers (117 and 118)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
4. **Front Stabilizer Cover (103)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
5. **Cover Holders (25)**, bent-tab hole facing rearward, with **four Phillips Head Screws (145)**; **Inclinable Rail Cover (115)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
6. **Rear Bar Cover (116)** on the Rear Stabilizer and the Cover Holders (25) with **four Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
7. **Incline Bottom Cover (113)** on the Incline Rail Assembly (2) with **four Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
8. **Handle Bar Covers (105 & 106, 107 & 108)** with **eight Sheet Metal Screws (142)**.
9. **Console Mast Covers (95 & 96)** with **four Sheet Metal Screws (142)**.
10. **Tablet Holder (93)** on the back of the console with **four Sheet Metal Screws (142)**.
