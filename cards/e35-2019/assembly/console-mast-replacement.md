1. Remove the connecting arm and pedal arm first (steps 11-2 and 11-3).
2. Following the owner's manual assembly instructions in reverse, use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the console mast cover.
3. Following the owner's manual assembly instructions in reverse, remove the console mast.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **two 3x20 tapping screws** which secure the hand pulse assembly, pull it out and disconnect the cables.
5. Unplug the round cap under the hand pulse tube and pull out the hand pulse cables to take the hand pulse sensor assembly apart.
6. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
