1. **Install the Incline Rail Assembly (2) into the U-channel bracket of the Main Frame (1).** From the sides install **two Hex Head Bolts (133)** with **two Flat Washers (164)** and **two Nylon Nuts (157)**. From the top install **four Hex Head Bolts (135)**, **four Split Washers (182)**, **four Flat Washers (170)**, and **four Star Washers (183)**. Tighten with **Wrenches (184 & 187)**.
2. **Connect the 3 wire harness: Incline Motor Power Cord (48)** to the female receiver from the Incline Rail Assembly (2). Connect the **Three pin position Sensor connector / Incline Motor Connecting Wire (49)** the same way.
3. **Run the Computer Cable (50) through the Console Mast (12)**, install the mast into the receiving bracket, and fit **four Socket Head Cap Bolts (125)** with **four Split Washers (180)** and **four Flat Washers (164)**. Tighten with the **L Allen Wrench (217)**, pulling slightly on the cable at the top so it does not get pinched and shorted.
4. **Install the Bottle Holder (94) on the Console Mast (12) with two Phillips Head Screws (141)** using driver (186). **The bottle holder must be assembled before console assembly.**
5. **Plug the connectors into the back of the console: Computer Cable (50), two Hand pulse Cables (57, 57-1), Resistance Switch Wire (60) and Incline Switch Wire (61).** Secure the **Console (45)** with **four Phillips Head Screws (141)** using driver (186).
