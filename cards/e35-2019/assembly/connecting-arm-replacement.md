1. Remove the swing arm assembly and swing arm first (step 11.2). If you only need the pedal tube, remove the swing arm with the rod end bearing still attached.
2. Use a **12mm open end wrench** to remove the **hex socket screw 5/16" x 15mm** and **flat washer 5/16" x 20**. Pull out the pedal carriage bolts to remove the pedal bar assembly.
3. To disassemble the adjustable pedal, use a **14mm wrench** to release the **3/8" x 19 hex head bolt**, **3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washer** and **3/8" x 7T nylon nut**.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **M5x10mm Phillips head screws** and the **19 x 14 x 10 x (5+4)T bushing**, then pull out the axle of the locking pin. Use C-ring pliers to release the C-ring and pull out the locking pin.
5. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **Phillips head screw M5x10mm** securing the pedal, and remove the pedal.
6. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
