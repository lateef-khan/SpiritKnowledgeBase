1. An elliptical is full of moving parts with joints and the parts are related to each other, so proper lubrication matters. **Lubricating with grease while maintaining or replacing parts can eliminate potential problems.**
2. There are many causes for un-smoothness. **Slide wheels moving parallel and freely on the rail is the key factor**, which is affected by the pedal arm being parallel to the rail and perpendicular to the bearing housing.
3. Swing arms must connect freely to the connecting arms; the slide wheels must be a true circle without slippage, and the rod end (fish-eye) bearing must be undamaged and tight enough to eliminate noises. If necessary, use a **19mm wrench** to replace the rod end bearing.
4. To maintain, start from the slide wheels by replacing them or applying lubricant on the curvature of the slide wheels. Keeping parallel parts parallel to each other is the key to judging which part needs adjustment or replacement.
