If there is no resistance, check whether the console functions properly and whether the steel cable is damaged.

Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **5x19 tapping screws** to replace the steel cable.

If the console also shows E2, work the E2 card first: a dead tension motor and a broken steel cable produce the same complaint from a customer.
