There are many places noise can come from and it is not easy to isolate one. The manual lists, at the front: chain cover and round disk, EPE, round disk, sleeve, cross bar, belt and belt pulley, idler wheel plate, swing arm, swing arm axle, flywheel, steel cable, slide wheel and crank arm; and at the rear the inclinable rail assemblies and the pedals.

The main reasons are **not enough lubrication, unsmooth rotations, and loose screws**.

Because the product uses a linked design, resolving a noise varies from machine to machine depending on the actual situation. There is no single fix.
