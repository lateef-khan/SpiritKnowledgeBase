The likely cause is friction creating an abnormal sound. Check whether the large disk is not centred or is offset.

1. If the disc is **not centred** and causing friction, remove the left and right chain cover and reassemble, following the assembly and disassembly procedures.
2. If the disc is **offset** and causing friction, remove the left and right chain cover, then with a Phillips screwdriver remove the **5x16mm tapping screw** and the **1/4" x 19mm flat washer**. Place the round disk and the cross bar on a flat surface and check whether either is deformed. If deformed, check whether the deformity can be fixed manually.

Deformity is mostly caused by external force. The production line runs a quiet room test to a standard of **+/-3mm** before shipment.
