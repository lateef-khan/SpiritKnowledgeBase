Ten letters on a line drawing headed PARTS OF YOUR UPRIGHT BIKE:

| Letter | Part |
|---|---|
| A | Console |
| B | Tablet Rack |
| C | Pulse Grips |
| D | Water Bottle Holder |
| E | Seat |
| F | Fore/Aft Seat Adjustment |
| G | Vertical Seat Adjustment Knob |
| H | Crank Arm |
| I | Rear Stabilizer |
| J | Pedal |

It names the two seat adjustments the setting-up page describes and a tablet rack the steps do not mention; it does not label the console mast, the handlebars or the levelling glides. **The Dyaco PT edition of the same bike labels a numbered set of six**; that is its own card.
