**Four M8 x 65 mm socket head cap bolts hold this handlebar, on an Allen wrench and a 13 mm wrench.** The CU800 and XBU55 handlebar sits on two 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts and a 12 mm wrench; that is the linked card, not this one.

1. Take apart the console.
2. Use an **Allen wrench** and a **13 mm open wrench** to release **4 pcs of M8 x 65L socket head cap bolts**, **4 pcs of split washers** and **4 pcs of flat washers**, and the **handlebar assembly** can be released.
3. Use the Allen wrench to release **2 pcs of M8 bolts** and **2 pcs of flat washers** and remove both **handlebar (R, L) assemblies**, then fit the new handlebar assembly.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.
5. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **four 3 x 20 mm tapping screws** securing the **hand pulse w/cable assembly**, separate the halves of the hand pulse w/cable assembly from each other and remove.
6. Reverse the step to refit.

The owner's manual's step three fits the same handlebar with **4 bolts (60), M8 x 65L socket head cap bolts, 4 lock washers (64), 8 washers (57) and 4 nuts (84)** - eight washers and four nuts the service step does not mention; the parts list carries the bolt as item 60 (4), the Ø8.5 x Ø18 x 1.5T flat washer as item 57 (8) and the M8 x 7T nylon nut as item 84 (5). The 2 pcs of M8 bolts in step 3 are not sized further.
