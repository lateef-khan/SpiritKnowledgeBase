**Outlines (2-1)** - the assembled upright with twelve call-outs:
Console Assembly, Handgrip Side Cap, Hand pulse, Seat, Seat Post Cover, Console Mast Cover, Chain
Cover (L), Chain Cover (R), Front Stabilizer Cover, Rear Stabilizer Cover, Round Disk, Pedal.

**Skeleton (2-2)** - the bare frame with twelve call-outs:
Console Mast, Seat Handlebar, Seat Slide Bracket, Inner Slide, Adjustment Knob, Chain Cover Bracket,
**Generator/Brake**, Idler Wheel Assembly, Main Frame, Crank, Drive Pulley, Rear Stabilizer.

The *Inner Slide* is the seat post that telescopes in the mast under the *Adjustment Knob*; the
replacement chapter calls the same parts *Seat Slider* and *Locking Knob*, and the parts list *Seat
Slider* (6) and *Fore Aft Adjustment Knob* (86). The *Round Disk* on the outline is the crank-cover
disc the chain-cover procedure removes first.

Unlike the recumbent, **no controller is called out** - the *Generator/Brake Controller* (parts list
21) sits inside the chain covers and the skeleton shows only the *Generator/Brake* unit itself. The
recumbent 4.0R's drawings name a different set of parts (`40r-2025-specs-outline-and-skeleton-part-names`).
