**The staircase keeps rotating, so the manual makes stopping it a separate step from getting off.**

> Familiarize yourself with the location of the STOP buttons on the console. If you experience
> difficulties during the workout, pushing the STOP button will bring the machine to a stop.

> Use care when getting on or off the machine. Always use the handrails. Do not step off of the
> machine while it is moving. Press the STOP button and wait for the machine to come to a complete
> stop before dismounting.

- **Press STOP, wait for a complete stop, then dismount.** Not one action but three.
- **Always use the handrails**, mounting and dismounting.
- **STOP buttons**, plural, on the console.

**There is no safety key and no tether cord on this machine.** The STOP button is the only stop the
manual names. Do not offer a CSC900 owner the tether-cord procedure written for a Spirit treadmill
or for the CTSBS900 sled (`ctsbs900-safety-stop-key-and-tether-cord`).

The same paragraphs carry the clothing rule that belongs with a moving staircase:

> Wear proper exercise clothing and athletic shoes during a workout. Avoid wearing loose clothing.
> Tie back long hair and keep towels away from the moving parts. Face forward at all times and never
> attempt to turn around while the machine is moving.

- **Face forward at all times** and never turn around while it is moving.
- **Keep towels away from the moving parts** - the manual names towels specifically.

**Do not operate the machine if the motor shroud is removed or if the power cord is damaged in any
manner**, and **keep all air openings free from dirt or any material that would block the flow of
air to the motor.**

None of the other thirteen 2024 manuals prints any of this. The steppers, whose pedals also move
under the user, print no mounting or dismounting rule at all
(`spirit-2024-safety-instructions-list-stepper`).
