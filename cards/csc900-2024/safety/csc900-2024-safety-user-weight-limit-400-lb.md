**400 lb.** Each manual prints it in two places, and only one of the two gives a metric figure.

| Where | 2024 | 2019 and 2021 |
|---|---|---|
| Safety instructions | item 8 - `User Weight Limit: 400 lb.` | a bullet - `Maximum User Weight: 400 lbs / 180 kg.` |
| Electrical safety, club rules | `Do not exceed the maximum allowable weight limit of 400 lbs. / 180 kg.` | the same sentence |

**The figure has not moved since 2019.** All three CSC900 printings say 400 lb, so this is not a
change the 2024 book made; it is what a CSC900 has always been. The 2019 and 2021 books give the
metric figure in both places, and the 2024 book in only one.

180 kg is 397 lb, so the two statements are the same allowance. The manual does not say what happens
above it.

**This is the only one of the fourteen 2024 machines that is not 450 lb.** The four treadmills, the
three ellipticals, the three bikes, the two steppers and the rower all print 450
(`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`). Never quote 450 lb for a CSC900 and never quote
400 lb for one of the others.

**It is not the CES880 figure either.** The CES880-2025 suspension elliptical also prints 400 lbs,
but beside **181 kgs**, not 180 kg, and on its cover rather than in its electrical chapter
(`ces880-2025-safety-user-weight-limit-400-lbs`). The two books round the same allowance
differently; quote the machine's own manual.

The boxed note beside the safety list asks the reader to review and adhere to the user weight
restrictions **and power requirements** of the machine, and says failure to do so may result in
serious injury or damage to the machine (`csc900-2024-safety-instructions-list`).

**The CSC880 that replaces it is 330 lb**, not 400
(`spirit-climber-safety-user-weight-limit-330-lb`).

**The CVC800 and XS895 are also 400 lb but print no metric figure**, so do not carry 180 kg onto them
(`spirit-climber-safety-user-weight-limit-400-lb-no-metric`).
