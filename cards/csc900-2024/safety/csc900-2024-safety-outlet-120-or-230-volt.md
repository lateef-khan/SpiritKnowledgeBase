**There is no single voltage to give. The machine is built for one of two, and the plug on the cord
tells you which.**

> This product will be wired for either 120 VAC nominal power input OR 230 VAC nominal input. It is
> factory-equipped with a specific electric cord and plug to permit connection to the proper
> electric circuit. Make sure that the product is connected to a dedicated power line having an
> outlet with the same configuration as that of the plug.

- **120 VAC nominal or 230 VAC nominal**, decided at the factory, not by the installer.
- **Match the outlet to the plug already fitted.** That is the manual's test, and it prints no plug
  type, no NEMA number and **no amperage or breaker rating** for either option.
- **A dedicated power line.** Nothing else on that circuit.

**No adapter may be used.**

> Improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric shock.
> Check with a qualified electrician or serviceman if you are in doubt as to whether the product is
> properly grounded. Do not modify the plug provided with the product. **NO adapter should be used
> with this product.**

This is the opposite of what the 2024 treadmill, CE850 and CS800 grounding pages say. Those describe
a temporary two-pole adapter and how to earth it with a metal screw
(`spirit-temporary-adapter-for-a-two-pole-receptacle`). **Never offer that procedure to a CSC900
owner.**

**Changing the circuit is a service job.**

> If the product must be reconnected for use on a different type of electric circuit, the
> reconnection should be made by qualified service personnel in order to avoid a hazard.

**None of the three manuals has a GROUNDING INSTRUCTIONS page.** Items 3, 4 and 5 of the safety list
are the whole of what any of them says about supply and earthing.

**All three CSC900 printings say the same thing.** The 2019 and 2021 books print these sentences as
bullets rather than numbered items; the words and both voltages are identical, and neither adds an
amperage. The 2019 and 2021 safety chapters match at 98.4% on an order-free word count of the printed
pages.

**The CSC880-2025 that replaces the CSC900 dropped half of the choice.** Its item 3 reads
`wired for either 120 VAC nominal power input` and stops - the 230 VAC alternative is gone, and the
word *either* was left behind (`spirit-climber-safety-outlet-120-vac-only`). Do not offer a CSC880
owner a 230 volt option and do not offer a CSC900 owner only 120.
