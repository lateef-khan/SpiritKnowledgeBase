Nine items, five intervals, and **no annual column** — the table stops at Biannually.

## The unplug line calls the machine a stepper

The CSC900 is a rotating-staircase **stairclimber**, not a stepper, and every other heading in the
manual calls it a StairClimber. The unplug line is the wording from the CS800 stepper book. Read it
as "unplug the CSC900"; it does not mean a different machine.

## It is not the stepper table, though it is close

The **CS800** and **CRS800S** steppers print nine rows too, and every interval mark is the same, but
three things differ: their first row is **Entire Stepper** rather than *Entire Unit*, their **Unit
Hardware** row comes before **Detailed Cleaning** rather than after it, and they have an
**Accessory Tray** where this machine has **Accessory Cups**. The CRS800S also leaves its Emergency
Switch row blank. See `cs800-2024-maintenance-machine-care-schedule` and
`crs800s-2024-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`.

## What this table does not have

- **No Levelers row**, and unlike the steppers the CSC900's Machine Care text does not mention
  levelling either (`csc900-2024-maintenance-one-reason-and-owner-responsibility`). **There is no
  levelling instruction anywhere in this book.**
- **No USB Port row**.
- **Nothing for the staircase drive.** The Steps row covers cleaning and inspecting the steps
  themselves; the chain, belt or drive that rotates them is not a row and has no interval in this
  manual.
