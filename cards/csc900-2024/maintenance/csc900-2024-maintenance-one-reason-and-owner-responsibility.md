**Every Spirit stair climber manual in the repository prints this page, and none of them prints the
standard Spirit noise block. It names one reason instead of two, and drops every figure the rest of
the range carries.** The wording has not changed since 2019.

Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each workout. If a squeak, thump,
clicking or rough feeling develops, the main cause is most likely **the following reason**:

1. **The hardware was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.** All bolts that were installed
   during assembly need to be tightened as much as possible. It may be necessary to use a larger
   wrench than the one provided if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently.
2. With durable, high performance components, this equipment is designed for **heavy usage with
   minimal maintenance required**. To keep it in top condition, perform the regular **daily, weekly
   and monthly preventive maintenance routines** "outlined below". On the **CSC900 2024** and the
   **CSC880 2025** that schedule is on the next page —
   `csc900-2024-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`. **On the CSC900 2019 and 2021 it was never
   printed** — `spirit-csc900-maintenance-schedule-promised-not-printed`.
3. The safety and integrity of this machine can only be maintained when the equipment is
   **regularly examined for damage and wear and repaired**. It is the **sole responsibility of the
   owner** of this equipment to ensure that regular maintenance is performed. **Worn or damaged
   parts must be replaced immediately, or the equipment removed from service until the repair is
   made.**

**Items 2 and 3 are not reasons for a squeak.** The CSC900 2024 and the CSC880 2025 number them
into the same list anyway, so the lead-in promises one reason and the list runs to three items; the
CSC900 2019 and 2021 print the same four paragraphs unnumbered, which reads correctly. The other
thirteen 2024 New Black Units manuals say "one of **two** reasons" and print exactly two items, with
the levelling check folded into the second - the CSC900 2024 is the only one of the fourteen whose
count does not work out.

## Four figures the other thirteen print and this book does not

The standard Spirit block gives **90%** of noise calls traced to loose hardware, a **crank arm nut**
to retighten, **2 leveling pads** on the rear stabilizer and a **14 mm wrench** to adjust them
(`ct800-2016-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`, `cu900ent-general-maintenance`).

**None of the four appears anywhere in any of the four stair climber manuals.** All four sources
were searched for "90%", "crank arm", "leveling pad" and "14mm"; the only hit is the CSC880's
parts-diagram label "J. Leveler". So for a CSC900 or a CSC880:

- There is **no printed leveller count** and **no wrench size** for adjusting them.
- There is **no levelling step at all** in the maintenance chapter, and where a machine care table
  exists it has no Levelers row. The CSC880 draws a leveller on its parts diagram and still never
  says how to turn it.
- Do not quote the 90% figure. It is a Spirit sentence, but not one this machine's manual makes.

## What the owner is on the hook for

Item 3 is the strongest maintenance-responsibility wording in the 2024 range: regular maintenance is
**the owner's**, and a worn or damaged part means the machine comes **out of service** until it is
repaired. Sole's indoor cycles carry a similarly worded rule in their own manuals
(`sole-spinner-maintenance-owner-responsibility`) — that is a different brand's sentence and should
not be quoted for a Spirit machine, or the reverse.

## What the CSC900 2024 does share with the other thirteen

The four-step post-workout routine — store the machine, damp cloth, microfiber on the screen, keep
debris out of the cupholders — is printed here word for word
(`spirit-2026t-maintenance-post-workout-machine-care`), as are the sanitizing bullets
(`ct900-sanitizing-equipment`), which it prints **without** the wear-parts warning the four
treadmills attach to them.

## What the four books do not share with each other

| | CSC900 2019 | CSC900 2021 | CSC900 2024 | CSC880 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The four paragraphs above | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| Sanitizing bullets (`ct900-sanitizing-equipment`) | **no** | yes | yes | yes |
| Four-step post-workout routine | **no** | **no** | yes | yes |
| Machine care schedule table | **no** | **no** | yes | yes |

The 2019 book stops at these four paragraphs: its maintenance chapter is one page, and the warranty
begins on the next. **Do not offer a sanitizing method for a CSC900 2019 from its own manual** —
`ct900-sanitizing-equipment` is a company-wide card and reaches it, but the 2019 book itself prints
none.
