**This manual prints none of the heart rate training pages the rest of the 2024 range carries.**
Specifically, it has:

- **No maximum heart rate formula** and no 220-minus-age page. A loose search of the whole book for
  "maximum heart", "MHR" and "220" returns nothing.
- **No target heart rate training zone**, no percentage pair and no Target Heart Rate Training
  Chart.
- **No Rate of Perceived Exertion page.** A loose search for "Borg" and "perceived" returns nothing.
- **No chest strap fitting page** and no chest strap interference warning. The word "strap" appears
  only in the console overview, which says the machine reads a **Bluetooth enabled heart rate chest
  strap** as well as contact heart rate.

The table of contents confirms it: the Programs chapter runs from **printed page 20 to page 21**,
and the next entry is **Machine Care on page 32**.

**The one heart rate figure in the book is the WFI test's Target Heart Rate**, and it uses a
different formula from every other 2024 Spirit manual - `[208 - (0.7 * age)] * 85` rather than 220
minus age. See `csc900-2024-programs-wfi-stairclimber-protocol`.

**Do not answer a heart rate training question for this machine from a Spirit stepper card.** The
steppers print 60% and 85% and a 108-to-153 zone; this manual prints no zone at all.
