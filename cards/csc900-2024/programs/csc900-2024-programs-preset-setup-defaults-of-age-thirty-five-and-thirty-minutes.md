Every one of the five preset profiles is set up the same way.

1. Press the program's own button (FAT BURNING, TRAINING, ROLLING HILLS, CHALLENGE or INTERVAL).
2. The console displays **"Start Or Press OK To Set Up"**. Press **OK**.
3. **Enter Your Age** - the default **35** blinks. Use the Level +/- keys or the numerical keypad.
   Press OK.
4. **Enter Your Weight** - the default is **65 kg (145 lbs)** and blinks.
5. Adjust with the Level +/- keys or the numerical keypad. Press OK.
6. **Enter Time** - the default **30 minutes** blinks. **The minimum is 5 minutes and the maximum is
   99 minutes.** Press OK.
7. Press **START** to begin.
8. The console **counts down from 3 seconds**, then the stairs unlock and the program begins. The
   program profile displays on the dot matrix display.
9. **Resistance level begins at 23 steps per minute (SPM).**
10. Adjust the program's resistance with the Level +/- keys, the numerical keypad, **or the
    Resistance +/- buttons at the contact heart rate handle ends**.

*The manual prints this list with steps 4 and 5 split across one instruction, so the numbering runs
to ten for a nine-action sequence. It is reproduced as printed.*

The same ten steps are printed once for each of the five programs, identical apart from the button
pressed in step 1.
