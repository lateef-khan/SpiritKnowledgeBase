**The CPAT test is pass/fail and does not measure heart rate or determine a submaximal VO2 level.**
That is what separates it from the WFI test on the same button - see
`csc900-2024-programs-wfi-stairclimber-protocol`.

The StairClimber test was designed to **simulate climbing stairs in full protective clothing while
carrying fire fighting equipment**, challenging aerobic capacity, lower body muscular endurance and
balance. It simulates a fire fighter walking up stairs carrying **a high-rise pack or a hose
bundle**.

1. Press the **FITNESS TESTS** button.
2. The console displays **WFI**. **Press START/STOP again for CPAT.** Press **OK**.
3. Press **START**. The test begins when the StairClimber unlocks and begins to rotate.
4. **The Level +/- keys are not operational during the test.**
5. Pressing **START/STOP** terminates the test immediately.

**The test has only 2 segments:**

| Segment | Steps per minute | Time | Description | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 50 | 0:20 | Warm-up | StairClimber unlocks and begins to rotate |
| 2 | 60 | 3:00 | Test | Automatic transition from warm-up to test |
| 3 | 0 | 0 | Test stops, stairs lock | **No integrated cool down** |

**The test ends after the 3-minute segment.** Unlike the WFI test there is no three minute cool
down; the stairs simply stop and lock.

*The console spells the test **CAPT** in the button instruction at step 2 and **CPAT** everywhere
else. Recorded as printed.*

**The 2021 CSC900 prints this test identically**, including the same **CAPT** misspelling at step 2.

The **2019 CSC900** has no Fitness Tests button and no CPAT page. The **2025 CSC880** has a FITNESS
TEST key on its console overlay but prints no CPAT page either - see
`csc880-2025-programs-five-profiles-a-target-and-a-user-button`. Do not serve this card for a
CSC880.
