**The Target Heart Rate on this test is not 220 minus your age.** It is:

```
THR = [208 - (0.7 * age)] * 85
```

That is the only place in the 2024 Spirit range where this formula appears; every other 2024 manual
uses **220 minus your age** on its heart rate training page, and this manual prints no such page at
all. Do not substitute one for the other.

The WFI StairClimber Protocol was adopted as a **VO2 submaximal field test for fire service
personnel**. The test is a series of **one minute intervals with increasing workloads**. The goal is
for the participant's heart rate to **achieve the Target Heart Rate and exceed it for 15 seconds**.

1. Press the **FITNESS TESTS** button.
2. The console displays **WFI**. Press **OK**.
3. **Enter Your Age** - the default **35** blinks. Use the Level +/- keys or the numerical keypad.
   Press OK.
4. **Enter Your Weight** - the default **65 kg (145 lb)** blinks. Press OK.
5. **Enter Your Height** - the default is **175 cm (70 in)**. Press OK.
6. Press **START**. The test begins when the StairClimber unlocks and begins to rotate.
7. **The Level +/- keys are not operational during the test.**
8. Pressing **START/STOP** terminates the test immediately.

## How the test runs

| Level | Time | Description | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 2:00 | Assessment starts / warm-up | StairClimber unlocks and begins to rotate |
| 5 | 1:00 | Warm-up continues | |
| 7 | 1:00 | Test begins | After the 3-minute warm-up, the test begins at level 7 |
| 3 | 1:00 | Cool down | Begins once HR exceeds THR for 15 seconds |
| 3 | 1:00 | Cool down | Console displays estimated VO2 Max |
| 3 | 1:00 | Cool down | StairClimber automatically stops after the 3-minute cool down |

- Assessment continues following the protocol table until the participant's heart rate **exceeds the
  THR for 15 seconds**.
- **If HR drops below THR during that 15 second period, the assessment continues.**
- The assessment is not complete until HR exceeds THR for 15 seconds.
