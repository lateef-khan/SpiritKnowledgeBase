**This console has a dedicated button per program**, not a single Program key you scroll with.

**Five preset profiles:**

- **FAT BURNING** - keeps the user at low to moderate intensity for a prolonged workout. The focus
  is on aerobic endurance.
- **TRAINING** - lets the user gradually ramp up intensity until reaching a level for steady state
  exercise. The focus is on aerobic endurance.
- **ROLLING HILLS** - a traditional program giving segments of medium and medium-high intervals.
  Trains aerobic capacity.
- **CHALLENGE** - a prolonged gradual warm-up followed by sustained training at a higher intensity.
  **Introduces anaerobic training within an aerobic workout.**
- **INTERVAL** - alternates high intensity anaerobic sprints with low intensity segments for
  recovery. Trains anaerobic capacity.

**Plus three more buttons:**

- **TARGET** - Target Training, where you choose Time, Vertical Distance or Calories as the goal.
- **USER PROGRAM** - build your own across all 24 segments of the dot matrix display.
- **FITNESS TESTS** - the WFI and CPAT firefighter protocols.

**There is no Manual program, no Hill, no Fat Burn, no Cardio, no Strength and no HIIT** on this
machine, and **no heart rate control program**. The program set shares no name with the Spirit
steppers - see `spirit-stepper-programs-five-programs-claimed-and-which-are-described` - and must
not be answered from them.
