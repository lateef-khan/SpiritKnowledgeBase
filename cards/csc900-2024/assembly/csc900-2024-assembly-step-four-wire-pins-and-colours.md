Step four says to insert the wires "with different PINS and colors according to the
instructions in the diagram". The diagram is five labelled connectors:

| Wire | Pins | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Communication Wire | 5PIN | White |
| Handrail Wire | 7PIN | Yellow |
| Handrail Wire | 7PIN | White |
| Emergency Stop Wire | 4PIN | Red |
| Heart Rate Wire | 4PIN | White |

**Pin count alone does not separate them and colour alone does not either.** Two wires
are 7PIN and two are 4PIN; three are white. Only the two together identify a connector:

- the two **handrail** wires are the 7PIN pair, and they are told apart by colour -
  yellow and white;
- the two **4PIN** wires are told apart by colour - the emergency stop is the red one
  and the heart rate the white one;
- the **5PIN** is unique and is the communication wire.

**The red one is the emergency stop.** It is the only red connector on the machine and
the only 4PIN red, which makes it the one to check first if the stairs will not run.

## The diagram is the only place this is printed

The parts list gives item numbers for wires but no pin counts and no colours, and the
step text says only to follow the diagram. There is no wiring table anywhere else in the
book, and the troubleshooting chapter never refers to a wire by pin count or colour.

## The diagram is on the same page as the step, not the next one

Step four's item 1 says the diagram is "on the follow page". It is printed at the foot
of the same page as step four. Nothing is missing; the cross-reference is wrong.
