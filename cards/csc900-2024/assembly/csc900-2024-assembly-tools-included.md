Two tools, and nothing else:

- 5mm Allen Wrench
- 6mm Allen Wrench

**This is the smallest tools panel of the fourteen 2024 commercial machines.** The next
smallest holds three; the CRS800S holds seven.

## Step four needs a screwdriver this carton does not hold

Step four fastens the handrails with **2 ST4.2 x 15L screws** and the handrail covers
with **4 M4 x 30L screws**. Both are driven screws, not socket bolts, and **there is no
screwdriver in the carton**. Every other 2024 commercial machine that fits a driven
screw packs a screwdriver for it; this one does not, and the manual never says to
supply your own. See [the build](csc900-2024-assembly-procedure.md).

## Everything else on the machine is an M8 socket bolt

Steps one to four fit M8 x 20L, M8 x 30L, M8 x 40L and M8 x 55L bolts. The 6mm key is
what those take; the manual never says which of the two keys goes with which bolt, and
never gives a hex size for any fastener.

## No wrench, and no levelling tool

Unlike the other thirteen machines of this redesign, **the CSC900 manual prints no
levelling instruction at all** and so calls for no wrench. Its machine diagram labels
levellers as callout I and no page in the book says how to adjust one. See
[the absence card](csc900-2024-assembly-no-levelling-or-moving-instruction.md).

## Nothing here is a torque wrench

No step of this build gives a torque figure.

## Three printings, and only one row ever changed

`spirit-climber-csc900-2019-owners-manual` (pp. 7-12) and
`spirit-climber-csc900-2021-owners-manual` (pp. 7-12) print this chapter unchanged from
the 2024 book, step for step and table for table, including the M6/M8 disagreement in
step one. Compared from the three PDFs' own text.

**One row moved.** The handrail-cover screw is **M4 x 25L** in the 2019 printing - in
its table, in its step text and on its drawing - and **M4 x 30L** in the 2021 and 2024
printings. See
[the handrail cover screw card](../../csc900-2019/assembly/csc900-2019-assembly-handrail-cover-screw-m4-x-25.md)
before ordering one for a 2019 machine.

The 2024 book also adds the five-connector wire diagram under step four. Neither the
2019 nor the 2021 printing has it; their step four ends at item 3.
