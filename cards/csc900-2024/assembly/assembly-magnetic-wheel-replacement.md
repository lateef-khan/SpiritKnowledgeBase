1. Remove the side cover.
2. Remove a set of pedals - *fix one end of the connecting shaft with a wrench and the other end with a power
   screwdriver with a socket, rotate it while pulling it outward*. Pull out the pedal fixing shaft from one side.
3. Remove the belt tensioning plate.
4. Remove the bolts.
5. Remove the connection wire of the control board.
6. Take off the magnetic wheel.

No counts, sizes or refit step. This is the 2022 magnetic-system machine; the V1.0 alternator book has a
generator and a brake in its place, and its pedal removal is a seven-step procedure with its own card. The CSC880
magnetic control wheel comes out without removing pedals.
