Eleven lines, with quantities as printed:

| Qty | Part |
|---|---|
| 1 | Main Frame |
| 1 | Console |
| 1 | Console Mast |
| 1 | Column Group |
| 1 | Decorative Ring |
| 1 | Handlebar Group |
| 2 | Side Handlebars |
| 2 | Decorative Rings |
| 1 | Handlebar Reinforcement |
| 1 | Hardware Kit |
| 2 | Handlebar Covers |

## Three decorative rings, on two separate lines

The panel lists **1 Decorative Ring** and, four lines later, **2 Decorative Rings**.
They are different parts in different places: the single one goes on the console mast in
step one and the pair go on the side handlebars in step three. The panel gives no item
numbers to tell them apart, and neither do the hardware tables - see
[the hardware card](csc900-2024-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## The panel lists no power cord

**Every other 2024 commercial machine that runs on mains power lists a power cord on
this panel; this one does not.** The book does show a Power Input on its quick-start
page, so the machine takes one. Nothing on this panel accounts for it.

## The panel lists no step base, and step two fits one

Step two attaches a **Step Base** to the lower main frame with three bolts, and the
step-two hardware table counts it as a line - "STEP BASE, 1". This panel does not
mention it.

## The panel lists no levellers

The machine diagram on the next page labels **Levelers** as callout I, and no step of
the build fits one. This is the only one of the fourteen 2024 manuals that prints no
levelling instruction anywhere. See
[the absence card](csc900-2024-assembly-no-levelling-or-moving-instruction.md).

## What the panel does not say

It gives no carton count, no shipping weight and no box dimensions.

## Three printings, and only one row ever changed

`spirit-climber-csc900-2019-owners-manual` (pp. 7-12) and
`spirit-climber-csc900-2021-owners-manual` (pp. 7-12) print this chapter unchanged from
the 2024 book, step for step and table for table, including the M6/M8 disagreement in
step one. Compared from the three PDFs' own text.

**One row moved.** The handrail-cover screw is **M4 x 25L** in the 2019 printing - in
its table, in its step text and on its drawing - and **M4 x 30L** in the 2021 and 2024
printings. See
[the handrail cover screw card](../../csc900-2019/assembly/csc900-2019-assembly-handrail-cover-screw-m4-x-25.md)
before ordering one for a 2019 machine.

The 2024 book also adds the five-connector wire diagram under step four. Neither the
2019 nor the 2021 printing has it; their step four ends at item 3.
