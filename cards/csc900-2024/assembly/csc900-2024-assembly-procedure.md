**This build names no item numbers at all.** Every other manual in the 2024 commercial
redesign refers to parts by an assembly-drawing number in parentheses; this one names
parts in words and fasteners by thread and length. Its hardware tables have no PART
column either - see [the hardware card](csc900-2024-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Step one - console mast, decorative ring and column group

1. Insert the console mast wire through the **Decorative Ring**, then round to insert
   through the rear of the tube. Connect the communication wires between the **Main
   Frame** and the **Console Mast**.
2. Attach the Decorative Ring to the Console Mast using **1 M8*20 bolt, 1 M8*65 bolt and
   2 ø8 flat washers**.
3. Attach the **Column Group** to the lower Main Frame using **3 M8*55 bolts and 3 ø8
   flat washers**.

**The step-one hardware table calls that second bolt M6 x 65L, not M8*65.** The step
text and the drawing call-out both say M8. See
[the bolt-length card](csc900-2024-assembly-step-one-bolt-length-contradiction.md)
before ordering one.

## Step two - step base

1. Attach the **Step Base** to the lower main frame using **2 M8*20 bolts, 2 ø8 flat
   washers and 1 M8*30 bolt**.

That is the whole of step two. The single M8*30 is the only one in the book, and the
drawing shows it going in from the opposite side to the pair of M8*20s.

## Step three - side handlebars and handlebar group

1. Connect wires with the same terminal.
2. Attach the **Side Handlebars** to the **Handlebar Group** and with the **Decorative
   Rings** using **4 M8*20 bolts and 4 ø8 flat washers**.
3. Attach the Handlebar Group to the Console Mast with the **Reinforcement Plate** using
   **2 M8*40 bolts and 2 ø8 flat washers**.
4. Attach the handlebars to the **Column Groups** using **2 M8*20 bolts**.

*Item 4 fits two bolts and no washers, and the table's washer count of six matches items
2 and 3 alone. That is consistent, not an error - but it means two of the six M8*20
bolts in this step go in bare.*

## Step four - console and handrail covers

1. Connect the **Console** using **4 M8*20L bolts**. Insert the wires with different
   pins and colours according to the diagram on the following page - see
   [the wire chart](csc900-2024-assembly-step-four-wire-pins-and-colours.md).
2. Secure the handlebars on each side using **2 ST4.2*15L screws**.
3. Attach the **Handrail Covers** on each side using **4 M4*30L screws** - **M4*25L on
   the 2019 printing**, which says 25 in its table, its step text and its drawing alike.

**The carton holds no screwdriver for items 2 and 3.** Its only two tools are a 5mm and
a 6mm Allen wrench. See [the tools card](csc900-2024-assembly-tools-included.md).

**The drawing beside step four disagrees with the text twice.** Its call-outs read
**ST4.2*16 Screw** where the text and the table say 15, and **M4*25 Screw** where the
text says M4*30L and the table says M4 X 30L. Two of three printings say 15 and 30, so
those are the figures to work to; the manual never reconciles them.

## The build ends here

There is no SETTING UP page - the only 2024 machine without one. Step four is followed
directly by QUICK START, which carries the Power Input drawing and nothing else about
putting the machine together. No step fits a leveller, and the book prints no moving or
transport procedure. See
[the absence card](csc900-2024-assembly-no-levelling-or-moving-instruction.md).

## Three printings, and only one row ever changed

`spirit-climber-csc900-2019-owners-manual` (pp. 7-12) and
`spirit-climber-csc900-2021-owners-manual` (pp. 7-12) print this chapter unchanged from
the 2024 book, step for step and table for table, including the M6/M8 disagreement in
step one. Compared from the three PDFs' own text.

**One row moved.** The handrail-cover screw is **M4 x 25L** in the 2019 printing - in
its table, in its step text and on its drawing - and **M4 x 30L** in the 2021 and 2024
printings. See
[the handrail cover screw card](../../csc900-2019/assembly/csc900-2019-assembly-handrail-cover-screw-m4-x-25.md)
before ordering one for a 2019 machine.

The 2024 book also adds the five-connector wire diagram under step four. Neither the
2019 nor the 2021 printing has it; their step four ends at item 3.
