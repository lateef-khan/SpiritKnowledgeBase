**The manual prints both, on the same page.**

- The **step text** says: attach the Decorative Ring to the Console Mast using the Bolt
  **M8*20** (1pc), **M8*65** (1pc) and ø8 flat washer (2pc).
- The **drawing call-out** beside it labels the same bolt **M8*65 bolt**.
- The **Hardware for Step 1 table** on the same page lists it as **BOLT, M6 X 65L, 1**.

Two of the three printings say M8 and one says M6. The rest of the build is M8
throughout - M8*20, M8*30, M8*40 and M8*55 - and the ø8 flat washer the step pairs with
this bolt is an 8mm washer, which fits an M8 shank and is loose on an M6.

**Work to M8 x 65.** The M6 in the table is the outlier and nothing else in the book
supports it.

## Why this is easy to miss

The table is the natural place to look when you are counting the bag out, and it is the
only one of the three that gives a thread the parts list can be searched on. **This
manual's hardware tables carry no part numbers**, so there is no item number to
cross-check the row against - see
[the hardware card](csc900-2024-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## The page is native text, not an OCR reading

Both figures are in the PDF's own text layer, and a 300 dpi render of the page reads
them the same way. This is what the book prints, not a scanning error.

## This is not the only place the CSC900 prints a fastener two ways

Step four's drawing labels an **ST4.2*16 Screw** where its text and table both say
ST4.2*15L, and an **M4*25 Screw** where both say M4*30L. Same pattern, different page,
and there the drawing is the odd one out rather than the table.
