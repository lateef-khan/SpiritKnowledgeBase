1. Unplug the old communication wire from the console and fix the new communication wire to it with tape.
2. Cut off the fixed tie at the bottom of the upright frame.
3. Pull the communication wire from the bottom of the upright frame.
3. (printed twice) Pull the control wire from the bottom to the right position.
4. Remove the tape.
5. Connect the communication wire to the console and the control board.

The old wire is the pull-through for the new one. Only the 2022 book prints this; the V1.0 book tests the wire for
continuity, same-numbered pin to pin, and replaces it without saying how. A damaged communication wire is the
first thing both books' troubleshooting names for ER11 and ER12 - those are errors cards.
