1. Remove the side cover.
2. Remove the connection wire of the control board.
3. Remove the bolts.

*Before replacing the control board, pay attention to take pictures to record the wire sequence.*

Three captions and the note; no counts, sizes or refit step. This is the 2022 magnetic-system board. The V1.0
alternator book's controller has five steps, a resistor harness and a same-batch rule, and the CSC880's
controller lists seven cables - do not carry those across.
