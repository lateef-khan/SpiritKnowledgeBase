1. Remove the rear cover of the console.
2. Remove the fixed plate.
3. Unplug the connection wire.
4. Unplug the membrane key wire.
5. Remove the bolts.
6. Take off the console board to replace it.

Six captions, no counts or sizes. The CSC880 book prints the same six moves as "replace the console clip"; the
V1.0 CSC900 book's "console core" is three steps with a ground wire. Each has its own card.
