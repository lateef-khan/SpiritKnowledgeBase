**These four tables have no PART column.** Every other hardware table in the 2024
commercial redesign gives an assembly-drawing item number for each row; these give a
TYPE, a DESCRIPTION and a QTY and nothing else. There is no way to order a fastener from
this book by number - only by thread and length.

They also mix assemblies in with fasteners: the first row of each table is the major
part the step fits.

## Hardware for step 1

| Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| Console mast | | 1 |
| Bolt | M8 x 20L | 1 |
| Bolt | **M6 x 65L** | 1 |
| Bolt | M8 x 55L | 3 |
| Washer | Ø8 | 5 |

**The M6 row contradicts the step and the drawing**, which both call that bolt M8*65.
See [the bolt-length card](csc900-2024-assembly-step-one-bolt-length-contradiction.md).

|---|---|---|

**Step four is the only step with no washers.** Four bolts hold the console on, bare.

## The whole build is metric

Thirteen of the fourteen 2024 commercial manuals mix imperial bolts with metric screws;
**this one is metric throughout** - M4, M8 and a self-tapping ST4.2, with Ø8 washers.
There is not an inch measurement in any of the four tables.

## Every count closes against the steps

Step one uses two washers on the decorative ring and three on the column group, against
a table of five. Step three uses four washers in its item 2 and two in its item 3,
against a table of six, and six M8*20 bolts across items 2 and 4, against a table of
six. Step two and step four match their steps line for line. Nothing is over or under -
which makes the M6/M8 disagreement in step one the only real defect in the four tables.

## The two screw rows disagree with the drawing beside them

The step four drawing labels an **ST4.2*16 Screw** where this table says ST4.2 x 15L,
and an **M4*25 Screw** where this table says M4 x 30L. The step text agrees with the
table on both. Work to 15 and 30.

## What the tables do not print

**No torque figure for any bolt**, and no item number for anything at all.
