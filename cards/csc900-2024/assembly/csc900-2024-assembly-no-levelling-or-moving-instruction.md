**The CSC900 manual never tells you how to level the machine, and never tells you how
to move it.**

The word **Levelers** appears once, as **callout I** on the machine diagram. No page
gives a count, a location, a wrench size or a method, and no step of the four-step build
fits or adjusts one.

## This is the odd one out of fourteen

The other thirteen manuals of the 2024 commercial redesign all print the same sentence
in their maintenance chapter - *there are 2 leveling pads on the bottom of the rear
stabilizer, use a 14mm wrench (or adjustable wrench) to adjust the levelers* - as the
last remedy in the noise item. See
[the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

**The CSC900 rewrote that item and dropped the sentence.** Its noise item keeps the
first remedy word for word - the hardware was not sufficiently tightened during
assembly, and it may be necessary to use a larger wrench than the one provided - and
then goes to generic preventive-maintenance text instead of the crank-arm and levelling
remedy the other thirteen print. Nothing replaced it.

## How the absence was checked

A loose search for "level" returns only resistance levels, the two "level surface"
placement lines and the diagram callout. A search that tolerates letter-spacing
finds nothing more. Every page of the PDF whose text layer is thin was rendered at
300 dpi and read - pages 21, 24, 41, 42, 43 and 44 - and none of them carries a
levelling or moving instruction; pages 41 and 43 are blank but for a folio number.

## What the book does say about lifting it

The pre-assembly page carries two warnings that are the nearest thing to a moving
instruction: **do not attempt to move the unit by yourself** - have at least one other
person help - and **do not grasp any plastic parts while lifting and moving**, because
the covers are non-structural and will not carry the machine's weight. That is a
warning about lifting, not a procedure for rolling it. See
[the pre-assembly card](csc900-2024-assembly-pre-assembly-six-items.md).

The carton panel lists no transport wheels and no levellers, and the machine diagram
labels no transport wheel.

## Do not answer this from the CS800 or the CRS800S

Both are climbers of the same redesign and both do print the levelling sentence. The
**CS800** carton even counts four levellers on its panel. Neither figure belongs to
this machine.

## Every CSC900 printing is like this, and so is the CSC880

`spirit-climber-csc900-2019-owners-manual` and
`spirit-climber-csc900-2021-owners-manual` print no levelling instruction and no moving
procedure either. In both, the whole word "level" returns only resistance levels, the
two "hard, level surface" placement lines and - in the 2021 book - nothing else at all;
neither prints the diagram callout the 2024 book has, because neither has a machine
diagram. Their maintenance chapters carry no noise item with a levelling remedy.

The **CSC880 2025** stair climber is the same: its diagram labels **J. Leveler** and no
page says how to adjust one. See
[the CSC880 diagram card](../../csc880-2025/assembly/csc880-2025-assembly-parts-diagram-and-oem-label.md).

**So this is a stair climber trait rather than a 2024 one.** Four Spirit stair climber
manuals across seven years, and not one of them says how to level the machine.
