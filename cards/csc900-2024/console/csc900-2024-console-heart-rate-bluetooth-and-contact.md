**The StairClimber has capabilities for both a Bluetooth sensor for telemetric heart rate and contact
heart rate.**

- **Wearing a Bluetooth-enabled heart rate chest strap:** **the console automatically detects the
  closest sensor** and displays the heart rate in the **upper left side window**. There is no pairing
  step, no device list and no PIN - the manual describes none.
- **Contact heart rate:** **grasp the hand pulse sensors** and the console calculates and displays
  the rate. **This may take a few seconds.** **Press the scroll button until Heart Rate is
  displayed** - heart rate is the second data point of window one, not the default
  (`csc900-2024-console-four-display-windows`).

**"The closest sensor" is the whole of the pairing story, and it is a problem in a gym.** The manual
does not say what happens when two people wearing straps stand at neighbouring machines, and offers
no way to choose a strap or lock one out.

**This is the only Spirit commercial machine described this way.** The eight LED machines of the same
2024 range claim **Polar compatibility including coded transmissions** and name no Bluetooth strap
(`spirit-ce-console-pulse-window-polar-coded-straps`); the four ENT touchscreens are **Polar and
Bluetooth compatible** and pair a strap from a **Heart Rate icon** in the status bar
(`ct800ent-2022-console-heart-rate-feature`). **This manual never mentions Polar at all.**

**It prints no bpm range** and no troubleshooting for a strap that will not read. How to wear a strap
is `ctsbs900-heart-rate-chest-strap-pairing`.

**The CSC900-2021 and the CSC880-2025 print this paragraph word for word** - CSC900-2021
printed p. 15 and CSC880-2025 printed p. 19, both under BASIC INFORMATION - so the automatic
closest-sensor behaviour is four years older than the 2024 book and is still current in 2025. Same
two capabilities, same upper left side window, same scroll button, same "may take a few seconds",
and the same silence about what happens when two straps are in range.

**None of the three manuals mentions Polar.**

**The CSC880-2025 is the only one of the three that says what radio it is.** Its compliance page
lists **Bluetooth (with FTMS)** among the standards the product is certified to -
`csc880-2025-console-bluetooth-with-ftms-declared-with-no-pairing-procedure`. The two CSC900
printings declare no such thing anywhere.

**The CSC900-2019 is the printing to keep off this card.** It describes a **built-in heart rate
monitoring system** with contact sensors only and no Bluetooth at all:
`csc900-2019-console-heart-rate-is-contact-only-with-no-bluetooth-strap`.
