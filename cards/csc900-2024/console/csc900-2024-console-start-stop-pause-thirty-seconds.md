**Thirty seconds, not five minutes, and you do not come back at the level you left.**

Once the StairClimber has started rotating the staircase, the PAUSE function can be used.

- **Press START/STOP once** - the staircase **slows, pauses and locks in place**. **The pause lasts
  30 seconds.**
- **Press START/STOP again within those 30 seconds** - the staircase **unlocks and begins slowly
  rotating again at resistance level one**. **You have to raise the resistance back to your previous
  level yourself.**
- **Do nothing for 30 seconds** - **the program ends automatically and the console resets.**
- **Press START/STOP twice** - the staircase slows and stops, the program ends and the console
  resets.

**Do not quote the other Spirit consoles' Stop behaviour at this machine.** The 2024 LED ellipticals,
bikes and steppers pause for **5 minutes**, resume at the level you were on, and reset on a
**three-second hold** (`spirit-ce-console-stop-button-press-once-twice-hold`); the treadmills hold
their readings for 5 minutes and return speed and incline to their previous settings
(`ct850-2020-pause-stop-reset`). **None of that is true here.**

**The manual prints no hold-to-reset and no step-back-during-data-entry behaviour** for this key, and
does not say whether the workout data survives the 30 seconds.
