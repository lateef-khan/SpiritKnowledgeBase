**The display is separated into 4 separate windows with twelve total data points.** There is a
**button below each window**, and pressing it changes that window's data point.

| | Window one | Window two | Window three | Window four |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Vertical Distance | Calories | Time Elapsed | Steps Per Minutes |
| **2** | Heart Rate | Calories / Minute | Time Remaining | Steps |
| **3** | METs | Watts | Total Program Time | Floors |

**Vertical distance counts up from 0 to 999 feet or meters, then rolls over to 1 to represent
1,000.** A light in the decimal space illuminates for each increment of 1,000 and **changes position
with each 1,000 reached**.

**Changing from imperial to meters is done by pressing 1, 2, 3, OK.** That is the whole instruction;
the manual prints no menu, no confirmation message and no way to see which system is currently set.
**This console has no engineering or maintenance mode** - the words appear nowhere in the manual - so
this key sequence is the only unit switch it has.

**There is no Scan key and no automatic cycling.** Each window is changed on its own with its own
button, and nothing steps all four at once. That is the opposite of every other Spirit LED console in
the 2024 range, where one Up/Down Scan key swaps a whole row of four readings at a time
(`spirit-stepper-console-led-data-windows-steps-and-vertical-distance`,
`spirit-ce-console-led-data-windows-scan`).

**The manual gives no ranges** - no maximum for Floors, Steps, Watts or METs - and does not say how a
floor is counted.
