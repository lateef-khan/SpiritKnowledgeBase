**An adjustable vent, two-speed fan is integrated into the console.** **The fan is Off by default.**

| Press | What happens |
|---|---|
| First press | Fan on, on **High** |
| Second press | Switches to **Low** |
| Third press | **Shuts the fan off** |

So the cycle is off, High, Low, off - **it starts at the fastest speed**, which is the opposite way
round from the CE900, CR900 and CU900 fan, where each press *increases* the airflow through three
speeds before switching off (`spirit-ce900-console-three-speed-fan`). Telling a CSC900 owner that the
second press makes it blow harder is wrong.

**The manual does not say where the fan button is** - only that the vent angle is adjustable - and
gives no airflow figure for either speed.

**No other machine in the 2024 New Black Units range describes a fan control at all.** The eight LED
machines and the four ENT touchscreens label a **Fan** on their console drawings and print no switch,
no speed and no default.
