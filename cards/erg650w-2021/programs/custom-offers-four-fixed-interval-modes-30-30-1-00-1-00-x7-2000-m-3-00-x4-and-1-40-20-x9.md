Choose **Custom**, then press ENTER to confirm and choose one of four custom modes. They are fixed;
nothing in them is adjustable:

| As shown on the console | Meaning as printed |
|---|---|
| **V:30/:30R** | Training 30 seconds, Rest 30 seconds |
| **V1:00/1:00R...7** | Training 1 minute, Rest 1 Minute, Repeat 7 times |
| **V2000m/3:00R...4** | Training 2000m, Rest 3 Minute, Repeat 4 times |
| **V1:40/:20R...9** | Training 1:40 minutes, Rest 20 seconds, Repeat 9 times |

The first mode prints no repeat count. "Custom" here means a choice among these four, not a mode
you build yourself; an interval with your own work and rest values is **Target Intervals** - see
`see_also`.
