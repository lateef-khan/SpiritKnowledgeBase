Choose **Target Single**, press ENTER to confirm, and choose one of four values to target:

| Selection | Sets | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Single Time** | only a time value | press Start to begin |
| **Single Distance** | only a distance value | press Start to begin |
| **Single Calories** | only a caloric value | press Start to begin |
| **Single THR** | only a Target Heart Rate, **90 - 200 BPM** | "If you exceed the THR, a beeping sound will occur. **Only usable with a wireless chest strap**" |

The ranges and steps for the first three are the ones on the functions page: **TIME 1 ~ 99 min in
1-minute steps; DISTANCE 0 ~ 99900 meters in 100-meter steps; CALORIES 10 ~ 9990 Cal in 10-calorie
steps** (strokes, 0 ~ 9990 in tens, are also settable there but are not a Target Single choice).

**Two heart rate ranges in one book.** The functions page's PULSE paragraph says that "In Manual
Mode" a target heart rate can be set from **30 to 240 in 1 BPM increments**, beeping and blinking
PULSE while out of range. The ERG650W has no Manual mode in its program list - that paragraph is
carried over word for word from the ERG600W book, whose console does have one. This card follows
the Programs page: **the ERG650W's own heart rate target is Single THR, 90 to 200 BPM.** The
30-to-240 figure belongs to the ERG550W and ERG600W - see `not_to_be_confused_with`.

The console does not change the load to hold the target; on a water rower the resistance is the
tank level.
