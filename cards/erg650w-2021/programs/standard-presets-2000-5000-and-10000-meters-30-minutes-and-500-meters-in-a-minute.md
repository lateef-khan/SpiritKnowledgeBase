Choose **Standard**, then press ENTER to confirm and begin your desired training mode:

- **2000m**
- **5000m**
- **10000m**
- **30:00**
- **500m/1:00**

Press START to begin the program. **The preset value counts down to 0; the other values (calories,
strokes, etc.) count up.** Pressing RESET brings you back to standby mode and all values reset to 0.

The book says nothing more about **500m/1:00** - whether it is a 500 m piece, a one-minute piece, or a
pace target of 500 m per minute is not stated; the console's TIME/500M readout is the split it
would relate to. The distances are meters; the ERG650W's functions page counts distance to 99900
meters in 100-meter steps.

These are the only preset workouts on the console; there is no profile behind any of them.
