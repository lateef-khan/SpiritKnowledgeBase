1. Turn over the **Slide Rail (3)** as shown. Attach the **Seat's Rollers (39)** to the slide rail, making sure the plate touches the rollers. **To secure the slide rails between the rollers you'll need to insert it at an angle and rotate** as shown - the two rail halves go on one at a time around the roller set, upside down.
2. Secure the **Rear Stabilizer (2)** to the slide rails using **2 Screws and 2 Washers on both sides** - four M8 x 45 screws (63) and four washers (74) in all.

**The step text swaps the two part numbers**: it prints "2 Screws (No. 74) and 2 Washers (No. 63)", while the page's own hardware callout and the pack checklist both print **63 = M8 x 45 screw** and **74 = OD20 x ID8.5 x 1.5 washer**. This card follows the callout and the pack.
