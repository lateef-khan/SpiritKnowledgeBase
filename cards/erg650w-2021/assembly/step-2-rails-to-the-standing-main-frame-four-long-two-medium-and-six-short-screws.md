**Note: "We recommend having two people help during the next step in assembly - especially someone to prevent the seat from sliding down."**

Standing the **Main Frame (1)**, attach the **Slide Rail (3)**. Tighten using **4 Long Screws (99, M8 x 57)** on the "Top" or seat side of the frame, **2 Medium Screws (77, M8 x 27)** on the "Outer Sides" of the frame, and **6 Short Screws (65, M6 x 15)** along the "Bottom" and "Inner Side" of the frame.

Twelve screws in three lengths; the drawing labels the rail halves 3L and 3R.
