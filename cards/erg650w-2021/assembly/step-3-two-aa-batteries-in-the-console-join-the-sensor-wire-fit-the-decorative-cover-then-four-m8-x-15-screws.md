1. Place the **2 AA batteries** into the **Console (54)** - before it goes on, because the back is covered once mounted.
2. Turn the **Main Frame (1)** upright. Connect the **Sensor Wire (56)** to the **Connection Wire (96)**. **Be careful not to pinch the wires.** Feed any extra length of wire to lay inside the main frame.
3. Plug the **Decorative Cover (26)** into the main frame.
4. Secure the console to the main frame using **4 Small Screws (68, M8 x 15)**.

The console then prompts for the water level (L1-L6) - the console section's card - and the tank is filled on the next page (linked card).
