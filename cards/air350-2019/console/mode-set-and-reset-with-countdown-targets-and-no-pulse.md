| Readout | Range as printed |
|---|---|
| TIME | 0:00 ~ 99:59 (minute:second) |
| SPEED | 0 ~ 999.9 "K per hour" |
| CALORIES | 0.0 ~ 999.99 ~ 9999 Kcal |
| DISTANCE | 0.1 ~ 999.9 K |

Battery type (2) two AAA; operating temperature 0-40 C (32-104 F); storage -10 to 60 C (14-140 F). **No pulse or heart-rate readout is printed anywhere in the book** - the words do not appear.

**Keys.** **MODE** selects functions; hold two seconds to reset everything to zero except ODOMETER. **SET**, in STOP mode, sets target values for TIME, CALORIES and DISTANCE; hold two seconds and the value climbs on its own until any key is pressed. **RESET**, in STOP mode, resets the setting values to zero; hold two seconds to zero everything except ODOMETER.

**Functions.** SCAN (press MODE until SCAN shows) cycles TIME, CALORIES, ODOMETER, SPEED and DISTANCE, 6 seconds each. TIME, CALORIES and DISTANCE count up, or count down from a preset. The calorie readout is an estimate for an average user, for comparison between workouts on this unit only. **ODOMETER does not reset unless you remove the batteries.**

**Countdown.** Press MODE until the function you want shows (not in SCAN), press SET to raise the value (hold for auto-increase), press any key to stop at the value. The console turns on when you pedal or press a key and off after 4 minutes of inactivity ([auto-off card](../../shared/console/xterra-console-battery-console-wakes-on-pedalling-and-sleeps-after-4-minutes.md)); the blank-display troubleshooting table on the next page belongs to the Errors cards.
