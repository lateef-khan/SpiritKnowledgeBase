Hardware: **#35 D washer (2), #59 large screw (2), #61 M16 wave washer (2)**; tool #93 combination wrench (2 pcs) and the Allen wrench.

1. **Apply some of the included grease to the large screw (80) before assembly.** (The part the text calls "Large Screw (80)" is the Moving Shaft (80) of the parts list; the "Large Screws (59)" of step 3 below are the two knurled sleeves on the parts list.)
2. Attach the left and right handrail arms (3 and 2) to the main frame (1) by first sliding the large screw (80) through the right handrail arm (2) and placing 1 wave washer (61) and 1 D washer (35) against the large screw. Then place the right handrail arm (2) at the connection point on the main frame (1). Tighten the screw in the direction shown in the illustration with the Allen wrench (93).
3. **Tightening the large screw (59): insert the wrench into the holes on the large screws (59) and turn them in opposite directions to thoroughly tighten the large screw (59).**

Two combination wrenches (93) ship in the bag for this reason - one in each large screw, turned against each other.
