Hardware: **#76 M8 x 35 screw (4), #89 arc washer OD 20 x ID 8.5 x 1.5 (4), #71 M8 nylon lock nut (4), #88 spring washer OD 12 x ID 8.5 x 2.0 (4)**. Tool: #91 S5 Allen key.

1. Using 2 screws (76), 2 arc washers (89) and 2 spring washers (88) attach the left handrail arm (3) to the lower handrail arm (4). Then secure with 2 nylon nuts (71).
2. Repeat this process to attach the parts of the right handrail arm (2).

**Note: when connecting the upper arms to the lower arms the powder washer (70) is on the outside of the arms, and the connection points labeled "A" in the diagram should face inwards towards each other.**

The powder washer (70) is not in the hardware bag; it comes on the arm.
