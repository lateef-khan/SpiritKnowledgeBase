Hardware: **#77 M10 washer OD 20 x ID 10.5 x 1.5 (1), #22 knob (1)**, and the three washers (69) and nylon nuts (71) that come on the seat; tool #93 wrench.

1. Unscrew 3 washers (69) and 3 nylon nuts (71) from the seat (19) using the wrench (93).
2. Attach the seat (19) to the seat slider (7) with the washers (69) and nylon nuts (71).
3. Attach the seat slider (7) to the seat post (6) with the knob (22) and M10 washer (77).
4. Insert the seat post (6) into the main frame (1) and lock it at the desired height with the adjustment knob (24).

**CHECK ALL BOLTS AND NUTS ARE TIGHTENED BEFORE USING THE MACHINE.** The height and fore/aft fit is set on the first-time-user page (linked).
