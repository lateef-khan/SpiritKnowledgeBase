Hardware: **#57 M8 x 55 screw (4)** - the callout reads "M8x55xS5", a 5 mm Allen head - and **#69 M8 washer, OD 16 x ID 8.5 x 1.5 (4)**. Tool: #91 S5 Allen key.

1. Using 2 sets of screws (57) and M8 washers (69) attach the front stabilizer (9) to the main frame (1).
2. Repeat the same steps to attach the rear stabilizer (10) to the main frame (1).

The book does not say which stabilizer carries the transport wheels, and has no moving paragraph.
