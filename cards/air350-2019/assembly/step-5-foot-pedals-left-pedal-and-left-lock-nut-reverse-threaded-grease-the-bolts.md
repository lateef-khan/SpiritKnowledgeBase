Hardware: **#72 M14 washer (4), #92 M14 wave washer (4), #74 right lock nut (1), #75 left lock nut (1)**; tool #93 combination wrench (2 pcs).

1. **The left pedal (14) and the left lock nut (75) have reverse threading and have to be turned counterclockwise to tighten.** Apply some of the included grease to the bolt of the left and right foot pedal (14 and 15) before assembly. Turn the belt pulley with crank (18) to the front to make it horizontal.
2. Insert the left foot pedal (14) through the washer (72), wave washer (92), the connection piece (8) and into the crank. Turn the left pedal (14) counterclockwise as tightly as you can with your hands. Make sure the bolt on the left pedal (14) penetrates the crank fully. Lock the left pedal (14) with the left lock nut (75). Finish tightening with the wrench (93).
3. Repeat on the right side, making sure to turn the pedal clockwise.

The drawing puts a washer (72) and wave washer (92) on each side of the connection piece (8) - four of each in the bag for two pedals. No torque figure is printed.
