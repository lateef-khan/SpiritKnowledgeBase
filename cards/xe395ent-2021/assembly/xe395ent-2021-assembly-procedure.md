**Step one is printed as two facing panels, 1-A and 1-B**, both headed "HARDWARE FOR
STEP 1". They are one step done in two halves.

## Step one-A - incline rail into the main frame

1. Slide the **INCLINE RAIL ASSEMBLY (2)** into the U channel of the **MAIN FRAME
   (1)**, taking care not to damage the wires leaving each part.
2. Connect it **horizontally** with 2 **HEX HEAD BOLTS (104)**, 2 **FLAT WASHERS
   (137)** and 2 **NYLOC NUTS (130)**. Secure it **vertically** with 4 **HEX HEAD
   BOLTS (185)**, 4 split washers, 4 flat washers and 4 **STAR WASHERS (154)**.
   Tighten with wrenches 155 and 158.
3. Connect the **INCLINE MOTOR WIRES (46 & 47)** to the harness and the black wire
   leaving the incline rail assembly, and push the excess inside the U channel.

*The manual's own sentence swaps two item numbers here - see the note at the foot of
this card and the hardware card.*

## Step one-B - console mast, console and end caps

1. Slide the **CONSOLE MAST COVER (72)** onto the **CONSOLE MAST (12)**, facing the
   correct way, and tighten 3 **SHEET METAL SCREWS (120)** on the cover (72/72-1).
2. Feed the twist tie wire and the **COMPUTER CABLE (48)** into the bottom of the
   mast and out of the top.
3. Install the mast into the receiving bracket on top of the main frame, without
   pinching the cables. One bolt is already in the bracket and is tightened **last**.
4. **SPLIT WASHER (152)** onto **HEX HEAD BOLT (105)**, hand tightened through the
   **left** side. A **CURVED WASHER (153)** onto each **HEX HEAD BOLT (103)**, both
   into the **front**. Front bolts as tight as possible first, then the two left side
   bolts.
5. Connect the 2 **HAND PULSE CABLES (53)**, the **RESISTANCE CABLE (55)** and the
   **INCLINE CABLE (56)** to the back of the **CONSOLE (43)**.
6. Fit the console to the mast bracket with 4 **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (116)**.
7. Insert 4 **OVAL END CAPS (93)** into the **MAIN FRAME (1)**.

## Step two - lower swing arms and connecting arms

1. Two **WAVE WASHERS (196)** onto each side of the swing arm axle, then the **LOWER
   SWING ARMS (10 LEFT, 11 RIGHT)**, secured with 2 **BOLTS (184)** and **FLAT WASHERS
   (141)**. They were fitted at the factory; jiggle, do not force.
2. Remove the tie holding the spacer in the rod end of the **RIGHT CONNECTING ARM
   (9)**, line it up with the bracket at the bottom of the **LOWER RIGHT SWING ARM
   (11)**, slide the **BOLT (186)** through bracket, rod end and spacer, add the
   **FLAT WASHER (142)** and **NYLOC NUT (192)** and tighten hard. Repeat on the left.

## Step three - upper swing arms, both of them wired

1. **RUBBER SLEEVE (162)** onto the **LEFT (16)** and **RIGHT (17)** upper swing arms,
   wide part at the bottom.
2. Join the **RESISTANCE CABLE (55)** from the right upper swing arm and the
   **INCLINE CABLE (56)** from the left upper swing arm to the 2 **HANDLE CABLES
   (57)** leaving the console mast tube. Slide the 2 **SWITCH WIRE CAPS (161)** on,
   wide side facing the swing arm.
3. Insert each upper swing arm into its lower arm and fasten with **3 HEX HEAD BOLTS
   (159)**, **2 CURVED WASHERS (160)** and **3 NYLOC NUTS (127)**.

**Both arms are wired on this machine**, one for resistance and one for incline, and
the bag holds two caps. The XE295 wires only the right arm and holds one.

## Step four - covers, and the incline goes up

1. Two **WHEEL COVERS (79 & 80)** above the rollers with 4 **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS
   (115)**.
2. **Plug the power cord in, switch on, press start and raise the incline to level
   8.** Fit the **COVER (85)** to the mid-stabilizer tube with 2 screws (115).
3. **LEFT HANDLE BAR COVERS (81 & 82)** and **RIGHT HANDLE BAR COVERS (83 & 84)** over
   the axle connections, **4 SHEET METAL SCREWS (119) on each side**.
4. A **STEEL BRACKET (180)** on the left and right of the rear stabilizer tube, single
   hole facing the rear, 4 **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (122)**.
5. The front **REAR STABILIZER COVER (87)** with 2 screws (115), then the back **REAR
   STABILIZER COVER (88)** with 4 screws (115).
6. Check the four floor levellers - see
   [the levelling card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xe-assembly-levelling-top-nut.md).
7. **Return the elevation to flat with the Incline down key.**

## The incline has to be up before one cover goes on

Job 2 is the only reason this build needs mains power. The **XE395 2010** does the
same thing at the same point, and the **XE395 2023** does it too - but the 2023 book
loses the level number from the sentence.

## Step one-A swaps two item numbers in its own sentence

The sentence says "4 SPLIT WASHERS (142), 4 FLAT WASHERS (151)". Its own panel says
**142 is the flat washer** (5/16" x 20 x 1.5T) and **151 is the split washer**
(8 x 1.5T). The two numbers are the wrong way round in the text. The **XE395 2010**
manual prints the same joint the right way round, and so does the **XE395 2023**.
See [the hardware card](xe395ent-2021-assembly-hardware-by-step.md).
