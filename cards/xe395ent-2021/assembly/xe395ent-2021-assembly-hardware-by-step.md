## The two step-1 panels repeat three items

Items **130**, **137** and **142** are listed in **both** step-1 panels, at the same
quantities each time - 2, 2 and 4. Step 1-A uses the two nyloc nuts and the two 3/8"
flat washers on the incline rail; step 1-B's jobs use neither. **Do not read the two
panels as two separate bags of the same parts** - there is one step-1 bag, printed
twice because the step needed two pages.

## Items 142 and 151 are the wrong way round in the step text

Step 1-A's sentence secures the incline rail vertically with "4 SPLIT WASHERS (142),
4 FLAT WASHERS (151)". The panel above has **142 as the flat washer** and **151 as
the split washer**. Trust the panel: the XE395 2010 manual builds the identical joint
with "four split washers (178), four Flat Washers (100)" the right way round, and the
XE395 2023 manual prints it correctly as "4 SPLIT WASHERS (151), 4 FLAT WASHERS
(142)". This 2021 printing is the only one of the three with the swap.

## Step four is the biggest bag on any residential XE

**Twelve** M5 x 12mm Phillips screws where the XE295 supplies four and the XE195
eight - because this machine has a mid-stabilizer cover, two rear stabilizer covers
and two wheel covers to fit. Its four **M6 x 10mm** screws (122) are for the two steel
brackets only and are not interchangeable with item 115.

## Item 122 exists only on the incline machines

The XE195, XE295, XG400 and XE795 2021 have no steel bracket, no rear stabilizer
cover and no item 122. The XE395 2010 fits the same brackets with its own item 157,
also M6 x 10mm.
