**When the power cord is connected to the elliptical, the console will automatically power up.**
That single sentence is the whole of the manual's POWER section - **it prints no self-test, no
software version, no odometer and no stand-by timeout**, unlike every LED-console XE.

**To start a workout, press Start Workout on the screen or press the START key.** Either initiates
Quick Start: **the Time counts up from zero** and the workload is adjusted by hand with the **Level
Up/Down keys**.

**There are 20 levels of resistance**, banded exactly as on the other 20-level XE ellipticals: 1-5
very easy, 6-10 more challenging with small steps, 11-15 getting tough as the jumps grow, 16-20
extremely hard and suited to short interval peaks and elite athletic training.

**Stop pauses for five minutes, twice ends the workout.** Press **Stop** once during a program and it
**pauses for 5 minutes**; press **Start** to resume. Press **Stop** twice and the program ends on a
**Workout Summary** - Total time, Avg. Speed, Avg. Power, Avg. HR, total Laps.

**The manual stops there.** It does not describe a three-second hold, a third press, a complete
reset, or a previous-screen function, all of which the LED-console XE manuals print. **Absence is
the answer**: see `spirit-xe-console-stop-key-summary-then-reset` for what those other machines do,
and do not carry it onto this one.

**During a workout, Stop is also on screen** - the bottom right corner pauses or finishes the
workout: `xe395ent-2021-console-workout-views-goal-profile-and-track`.
