**The section holds a variety of third party apps to keep you entertained during your workout.**

1. **Make sure the machine is connected to WiFi** (the manual points back to its own p. 21).
2. **Choose the app you want by clicking on it.**
3. Press the full screen icon to enter full screen mode, the exit icon to leave it, and the **home
   icon to choose another app**.

**Some apps may require a subscription to access.** The manual says so plainly, and **it names no
individual app** - no list, no version, and nothing about which services are licensed where.

**Without WiFi this tab does nothing.** The set-up page states that without WiFi, saving workout data
to the cloud, using apps and casting content will not work:
`xt485ent-2023-console-wifi-first-time-setup`.

**The XT485ENT treadmill prints a shorter version of this page** - "connect to the WiFi and choose
the app icon" - **with no subscription warning at all**. See `xt485ent-2023-console-internet-and-apps`.

**Internet and Apps also works during a workout**, and behaves the same way there:
`xe395ent-2021-console-workout-views-goal-profile-and-track`.
