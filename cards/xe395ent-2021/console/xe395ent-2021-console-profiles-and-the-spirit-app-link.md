**To create a profile, press the + button on the starting screen.** A **maximum of 9 profiles** can
be created; you may also continue as a **Guest**.

**If you already have a Spirit APP account:** open the app, select **CONNECT TO MACHINE**, then use
your **phone's camera to scan the QR code that appears on the console screen**. That uploads your
existing personal info to the machine **and** links the two profiles, so your workout history is
fully displayed in the Spirit app. **One Spirit app account cannot be linked to multiple profiles on
one machine.**

**If you do not have a Spirit account**, press the **I DON'T** button and build one by filling in the
form.

**Three tabs:**

| Tab | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **INFO** | Your personal data - the screen shows **Username, Height, Age, Gender, Weight, Spirit Account** and a **Delete Profile** button. Tap any field, change it, then press **Save**. **You can also link a Spirit account here**, by pressing the "Spirit Account" field |
| **HISTORY** | **Ten records of the latest activity**, and only workouts completed by the current user. Tap a record preview for detail. **All records are in the Spirit app account if the profile is linked** |
| **FAVORITES** | Programs the current user labelled as favorites; they can be seen and deleted here, and **they also appear on the Homescreen** |

**Different users can choose different unit systems**, select their own favourite programs and save
their own templates.

**The XT485ENT treadmill prints almost the same page with a shorter app step** - "you can scan the QR
code appearing on the screen", with no CONNECT TO MACHINE instruction and no mention of the phone's
camera. Everything else, including the 9 profiles and the ten history records, is word for word. See
`xt485ent-2023-console-profile-tabs`.
