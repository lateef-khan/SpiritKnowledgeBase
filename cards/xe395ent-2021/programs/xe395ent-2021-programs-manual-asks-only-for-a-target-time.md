The MANUAL program **requires a time frame set before starting**. Use the scale or the arrows to
adjust the target time, then press **START** to begin the workout.

**Level and incline are adjusted manually while running**, from the arrows in the right-hand panel of
the workout screen.

**That is the whole of it.** The XE395ENT manual asks for no Age and no Weight in Manual mode - those
live in the user profile - and prints none of the seven-step sequence the keyed XE consoles use. See
`spirit-ce-programs-manual-mode` for those.

During the workout the bottom panel offers three views: **GOAL VIEW** (the parameter that ends the
workout - for a profile program, time counting down), **PROFILE VIEW** (your workout profile and your
position in it), and **TRACK VIEW** (laps on a **1/4 mile track**, showing progress by distance
covered).
