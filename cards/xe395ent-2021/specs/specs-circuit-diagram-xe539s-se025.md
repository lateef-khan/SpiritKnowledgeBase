The sheet is headed **XE539S-SE025-01 ELLIPICAL CIRCUIT DIAGRAM** (spelt so) - the XE539S code of
the XE395 family with the SE025 suffix, where the 2016 book's sheet was SE019.

- **Console** at the top, with two leads to the **hand-pulse** plates and two to the **thumb
  switches**, and a loom down to the controller.
- **Mains**: cord and plug into an **appliance inlet with a fuse**; two spades to the
  **Controller**.
- **Controller** in the middle. **TENSION MOTOR** hangs off its left side on a plugged lead.
  **INCLINE MOTOR** hangs off its right side on a bundle of spade leads, with the actuator's
  position-sensor lead in the same run.
- A single **speed sensor** lead drops from the bottom of the controller.

**No cable pin counts and no wire colours are printed** on the sheet, and the console loom is not
labelled with its six pins - those are on the pin-define page
(`xe395ent-2021-specs-console-to-driver-board-6-pin-definition`). The controller's sockets are
named on the driver-board page (`xe395ent-2021-specs-driver-board-cs51005-connections`). The 2016
XE395 sheet draws the same machine with the CS62004 board, its 317-020004 gear motor and UP / COM /
DOWN spades labelled (`spirit-ce850-2016-xe395-xe895-specs-circuit-diagram`).
