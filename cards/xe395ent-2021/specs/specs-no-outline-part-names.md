**The page holds a heading, one line drawing of the shrouded machine and a page number, and
nothing else.** There are no leader lines and no part names, in the text layer or on the render.

Proved twice: the text layer of PDF p. 3 has two lines (the heading *1. Outlines* and the folio),
and the 300 dpi render shows the drawing without a single callout. Neither is a flattened-text
case - the XE395-2016 book's equivalent pages extract their labels natively and this one has
none to extract.

Where the names are: the XE395-2016 book labels the same frame on two drawings
(`xe395-2016-specs-outline-part-names`), and the ENT book's own chapter-2 photographs name the
electronic parts (`spirit-xe395-xe895-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`). No book in the family
prints dimensions or weights on its outline page.
