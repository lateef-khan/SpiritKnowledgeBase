"There are 3 selections for target pulse:", exactly as printed:

```
HRC - 65%   TARGET H.R = 60% of (220-AGE)
HRC - 75%   TARGET H.R = 75% of (220-AGE)
HRC - 85%   TARGET H.R = 85% of (220-AGE)
```

**The first line contradicts itself**: the selection is labelled 65% and its formula says
60%. The other two lines agree with their labels, and the SB2.5r-family console this one is
cut down from lists 55/65/75/85 with matching formulas, so this card treats the selection as
**HRC-65% = 65% of (220 - AGE)** and the "60%" as the misprint. The console's own label is
what you will see when you select it.

Setup and behaviour are the SB2.5r family's: select the program, ENTER, "Time" flashes,
step through the parameters (a target time locks out distance), START/STOP. "If your Pulse
measurement is above or below (+/- 5) the Target HR setting, the computer will adjust the
resistance load automatically; it will check approximately every 10 seconds. If the heart
rate signal disappears, the computer will keep the resistance load constant for 60 seconds
then it will decrease the resistance load 1 level every 10 seconds."
