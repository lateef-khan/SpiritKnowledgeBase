**PROGRAM:** 13 different programs. **LEVEL:** the programs have **16 levels of work displayed in 8 bars in each column**; each column is 1 minute of workout (unless time is set to count down) and **each bar represents 2 levels**.

**Workout selection.** After power-up use UP/DOWN to select a program and press ENTER. Six basic programs: Manual, Pre-Programs (6 profiles), Body Fat, Target Heart Rate, Heart Rate Control and User. There is no Watt program on this console.

**Setting workout parameters.** ENTER makes "Time" flash; set it with UP/DOWN, ENTER moves to the next parameter; START/STOP starts. Time and Distance cannot both be set in one workout.

The setting range, default and step of each parameter (time, distance, calories, watt, age, pulse) are on the programs card `xterra-programs-workout-parameter-ranges-time-to-99-minutes-calories-to-9995-watts-40-to-250`; this card covers the keys and the display.

The SB2.5r, FS2.5, FS3.5 and RSX1500 share the keys but hold 24 programs and 24 levels with calories in steps of 5 and a watt range ([card](../../shared/console/xterra-console-24-programs-and-24-levels-in-8-bars-of-3.md)).
