> The facility is responsible for the items listed below:

1. The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within 10 days of the original purchase, or completed online to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.
2. Proper use of the fitness equipment in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual
3. Proper installation in accordance with instructions provided with the fitness equipment and with all local electric codes.
4. Expenses for making the fitness equipment accessible for servicing, including any item that was not part of the fitness equipment at the time it was shipped from the factory.
5. Damages to the fitness equipment finish during shipping, installation or following installation.
6. Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual.

**The list is addressed to a facility only.** There is no "consumer" in the heading or the lead sentence, and the manual's warranty table has no residential row, so this is the one Spirit elliptical manual in this knowledge base whose warranty section never addresses a home owner at all.

**Six items. There is no grounded-power-supply duty on this list.** The CE800 2021, CE800 2023, CE800ENT, CE900 2025 and CES880 lists all carry "Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage, replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects in house or facility wiring" - which puts blown fuses and defective house wiring on the owner. **This manual does not print it.** Do not quote it to a CE900 2021 owner; the source does not impose it.

**Item 1 offers both routes.** A card returned within 10 days, **or** online registration. The three CE850 manuals offer only the card; the CE900 2025 manual offers only online. See `spirit-ce-warranty-registration`.

Item 4 makes getting the machine to a place where it can be worked on the facility's expense, not a warranty cost (`xt-2023-warranty-owner-costs`).

**This is not the 2025 CE900 list.** That manual heads it RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE FACILITY without the word NORMAL, runs to seven items, adds the grounded-power-supply duty, and replaces item 1 with online registration only (`spirit-ce-2025-warranty-facility-responsibilities`).

This list is only half of what governs the warranty. The other half is the eight exclusions on the next page, `ce900-2021-warranty-exclusions`.
