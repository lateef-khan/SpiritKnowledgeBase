| WARRANTY | FRAME | GENERATOR BRAKE | PARTS | LABOR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial | Lifetime | 5 Years | 3 Year | 3 Years |

The manual prints *3 Year* singular in the Parts column and *3 Years* in Labor; both periods are three years.

**There is one row and it is not qualified.** The label is plain **Commercial** - not *Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities)* as the CE800 2021 manual of the same effective date prints - and **no Residential row is printed anywhere in this manual**. So this manual states no home warranty at all. Do not supply one from a CE800 or CE850 card; those are different machines with a different table.

Under the table:

> \*Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage.

**Three years of parts, not five, and three years of labor, not two.** Both figures are unique to this machine among the ten Spirit elliptical manuals in this knowledge base. Answering a CE900 2021 parts question from `spirit-ce-warranty-commercial-periods` adds two years the manual does not give; answering a labor question from it takes a year away.

**The 2025 CE900 manual replaced this table outright.** Effective October 01, 2024 the CE900 prints three rows - dues-paying commercial, non-dues-paying commercial and residential - a 10-year frame rather than a lifetime one, and a Wear Items column. See `spirit-ce-2025-warranty-coverage-periods`. A CE900 owner's answer depends on which manual came with the machine.

**Which facility this row covers is not settled by the row label.** The label says Commercial with no qualification, but exclusion 2 refuses reimbursement for equipment "not in non-dues paying facility or household use" - and the exclusion list carries **no** dues-paying exclusion. See `spirit-ce-warranty-use-environment` and `ce900-2021-warranty-exclusions`.

The opening paragraph warrants "this elliptical trainer" rather than "all its elliptical trainer parts" and dates the periods from the date of sale, or eighteen months from factory shipping when there is no receipt - `xt-2023-warranty-start-date`.
