The overview drawing names five things:

- **Integrated adjustable fan** for workout comfort
- **Premium, ultra-bright BLUE LED display**
- **Easy to read lap counter**
- **LED heart rate graph**
- **Large LED matrix window** to track workout progress

The drawing also carries a bilingual **WARNING** label - *keep children under the age of 13 away from
machine* / *gardez les enfants ages de moins de 13 ans loin de l'appareil* - and a SPIRIT badge.

**Blue, not white.** The 2025 CE900 prints the same list of call-outs but says **white**:
`ce900-2025-console-screen-overview`. That is the one word that separates the two drawings, and it is
the thing a person looking at a console can actually check.

**This drawing does not name a tablet rack.** The 2025 drawing adds a **Tablet friendly reading rack
holder** call-out; this one does not, so do not tell a 2021 owner their console has one on the
strength of the later manual.

**No key is named on this drawing**, and the 2021 manual has no engineering mode section anywhere, so
nothing about a maintenance menu can be quoted for this machine.
