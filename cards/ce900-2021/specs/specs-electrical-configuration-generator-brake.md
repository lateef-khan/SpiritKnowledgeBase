Section 3 names three parts:

- **CONSOLE** - "interface that controls all functions of the Elliptical"; it contains the key
  controls and an **LED display**.
- **MAIN CONTROLLER** - "the circuit board consist of the DC power supply for console"; includes the
  power supply and the driver control circuit.
- **GENERATOR BRAKE** - "it can change to increase or decrease resistance level of brake".

**No voltage, current or resistance figure is printed anywhere in the section.** The elliptical has
no adapter: the generator in the flywheel powers the console, and the operation page says the
machine "does not need to be plugged into an AC outlet" - you pedal to switch it on.

The chapter-2 photographs caption **Cooling FAN** and **DISPLAY** as the upper controllers, and
**HYBRID GENERATOR**, **SPEED SENSOR** and **Driver board** as the lower ones. The 2018 CR900 and
CU900 bikes print the same three paragraphs word for word
(`spirit-cr900-cu900-2018-specs-electrical-configuration-generator-brake`); the CE900ENT replaces
the generator brake with an EMS flywheel fed from a 24 V adapter
(`ce900ent-specs-electrical-configuration-ems-flywheel-and-10-1-inch-tft`).
