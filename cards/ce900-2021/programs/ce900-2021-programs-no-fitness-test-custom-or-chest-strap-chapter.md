**Absence, verified across the whole document.** The CE900 2021 owner's manual is the thinnest of the
ten Spirit commercial elliptical owner's manuals, and four chapters the others carry are simply not
in it:

- **No Fitness Test.** The words YMCA and VO2max appear nowhere in the document.
- **No Custom or User program.** There is no program-building or segment-editing section.
- **No chest strap chapter.** No fitting instructions, no battery figure, and no Erratic Operation
  page.
- **No target heart rate / training zone page.** The 220-minus-your-age formula and the worked
  40-year-old example are absent, and so is the Rate of Perceived Exertion (Borg) scale.

What the manual **does** carry, in order: Programming the Console, Entering a Program & Changing
Settings, Manual, five Preset Programs (Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, Interval), Programming
Preset Programs, HIIT, Heart Rate Program Operation, and the Constant Watt Program.

The 2025 CE900 owner's manual adds the chest strap chapter, the Erratic Operation page, the training
zone page and the Borg scale, but **still has no Fitness Test and no Custom program**.
