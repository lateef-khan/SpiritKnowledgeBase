**The XBU55ENT service manual has no "Parts Replacing Guide", no "Troubleshooting" disassembly chapter and no exploded-view repair section.** Its seven sections are: Outlines, Electronic Parts, Electrical Configurations, Product Operation, Unit Block Diagrams, Basic Connections and Wiring, and Error Messages / Troubleshooting (the E2 tension-motor test, the circuit diagram, the engineer-mode menu and the troubleshooting matrix). The book is 33 pages and the matrix is its last page.

Checked three ways: the word "replace" appears only in the troubleshooting tables ("Replace with new LCD or console", "Replace lower controller"); no page carries a heading about disassembling, assembling or replacing; and a render of the final pages shows the matrix and nothing after it.

**Where to look instead.** The XBU55 2016 (XU319-SB011) and XBU55 2023 (XU319A-SB030) service manuals print a ten-section parts replacement chapter for the same upright frame - console, handlebar, console mast, seat and sliding seat mount, inner slide, crank and pedals, shrouds, end cap, drive pulley and bearing housing, flywheel and steel cable - and the XBR55ENT book prints a full chapter for the recumbent twin. The XBU55ENT book does not say that its frame is the XBU55's, so those cards are the nearest evidence, not this machine's own.
