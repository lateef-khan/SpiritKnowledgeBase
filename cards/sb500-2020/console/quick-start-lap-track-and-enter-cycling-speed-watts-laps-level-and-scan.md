**Quick Start.** After the console powers up, press **Start**. Time counts up from zero and resistance is adjusted with the Level up/down keys.

**Display.** The dot-matrix shows the resistance profile. Pressing **Start during a program switches the display to a lap track a quarter mile long**. Pressing **Enter** switches the data in the message window: it first shows the program name, then each press steps through **Speed, Watts, Laps, Current Level/Max Level, Scan**, and repeats. To the left of the dot-matrix a number shows the resistance level.

**Data displayed.** Calories - an accumulation of Kcal, an estimate from the exercise type, resistance level, pace and body weight. Time - elapsed if counting up, remaining if counting down. Pulse - your heart rate while grasping the hand pulse sensors ([heart icon card](../../shared/console/xterra-console-heart-icon-blinks-when-the-grips-or-strap-are-read.md)). RPM - pedalling speed. Speed - mph, or km/h if "Km" shows. Distance - accumulated mileage.

Pausing and resetting with Stop/Reset is on [the pause card](../../shared/console/xterra-console-stop-once-pauses-5-minutes-twice-ends-with-a-summary-held-3-seconds-resets.md). The FS5.8e/FS5.9e version of this page adds incline and altitude ([card](../../shared/console/xterra-console-quick-start-with-level-and-incline-keys-and-enter-cycling-altitude.md)); the SB4.5r version is older and simpler ([card](../../sb45r-2013/console/quick-start-with-a-quarter-mile-track-and-enter-switching-speed-rpm-and-level.md)).
