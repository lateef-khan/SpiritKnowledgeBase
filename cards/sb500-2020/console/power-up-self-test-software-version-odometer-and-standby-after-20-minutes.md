The bike is supplied with an external power supply; when power is connected the console powers up by itself. **With no input for 20 minutes the console goes to stand-by mode to save energy; press any key to turn it on.**

**Power-up sequence.** The console performs an internal self-test with all the lights on. When they go off the dot-matrix shows a **software version (e.g. VER 1.0)** and the time and distance windows show an **odometer reading - the hours the bike has been used and the virtual miles it has gone.** The odometer stays a few seconds, then the start-up display scrolls the workout profiles and operating instructions.

The 2014 printing of this book says the same. Sleep can be turned off in engineering mode, where the timer is written as 30 minutes ([engineering card](engineering-mode-start-stop-enter-5-seconds-with-lcd-test-type-set-and-key-tone.md)) - the two figures disagree; the card follows the 20 minutes printed on the customer page for the timer a rider sees, and notes the 30 on the menu. The SB4.5r, FS5.8e and FS5.9e print 30 minutes on their customer page ([card](../../shared/console/xterra-console-power-up-self-test-software-version-odometer-and-standby-after-30-minutes.md)).
