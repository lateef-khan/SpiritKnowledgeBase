**Fit it on the console mast (No.2) with two Phillips head screws (No.83).** The 2021
manual never says so; the 2024 printing of the same build does, as item 4 of step three.

## What the 2021 book prints

The carton panel lists **1 Water Bottle Holder**. Step three has three items - the
console mast covers, the handlebar caps and the console - and none of them is the bottle
holder. The step-three hardware table lists **10 screws (No.83), M5 x 10MM**, and the
three items spend **eight**:

| Item | Screws (No.83) |
|---|---|
| 2, handlebar caps, two a side | 4 |
| 3, console | 4 |
| **Table total** | **10** |

**The two that are left over are the bottle holder's.** The bag is right and the chapter
is one item short.

## The 2024 printing supplies the missing item

`spirit-climber-cs800-2024-owners-manual` prints step three with the same first three
items word for word and adds:

> 4. Secure the Water Bottle Holder (No.113) on the Console Mast (No.2) with two
>    Phillips Head Screws (No.83) by using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (No.118).

Its hardware table still says ten, and with item 4 the count closes. The holder is item
**No.113** in the parts list; the 2021 chapter never gives it a number.

## The CVC800 ships the same carton and does fit it

The CVC800 vertical climber prints the same twelve-line carton panel as both CS800
printings, and its step four item 6 installs the **Beverage Holder (No.78)** with two
tapping screws. See [that build](../../cvc800/assembly/cvc800-assembly-procedure.md).

## Nothing else in the chapter changed

Step zero, step one, step two, step two continued and the three hardware tables are word
for word the same in the 2021 and 2024 printings, compared from the two PDFs' own text -
including the flat washer printed **3/4"** in the step one table and the bolt printed
**3/8 x 2-14"** in the step two table. See
[the hardware card](../../cs800-2024/assembly/cs800-2024-assembly-hardware-kit.md).
