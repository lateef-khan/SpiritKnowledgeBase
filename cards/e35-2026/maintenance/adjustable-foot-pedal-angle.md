**Pull the red quick release handle, located under each footpad, towards the rear** to change the angle. There are **three footpad angle settings**:

| Position | Angle at the bottom of the elliptical stroke |
| --- | --- |
| **Lowest** | **zero (0) degrees**, or flat |
| **Second** | **five (5) degrees** |
| **Top** | **ten (10) degrees** |

Each footpad has its own handle, so both must be set to match.

This is a **mechanical adjustment on the pedal itself and has nothing to do with the power incline**, which is driven by a motor from the console or swing arm buttons. Changing the pedal angle does not change the incline reading.
