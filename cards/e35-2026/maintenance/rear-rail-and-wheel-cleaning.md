**Dirt build-up on the rear rails and polyurethane wheels is a source of noise.**

1. **Clean the rails and wheels with a lint free cloth and rubbing alcohol.**
2. **Stubborn build-up can be removed with your thumbnail or a non-metallic scraper, like the back edge of a plastic knife.** Nothing metal.
3. **After cleaning, apply a small amount of lubricant on the rails with your fingers or a lint free cloth.**
4. **You only need a thin coat of lubrication. Wipe off any excess.**

The manual **does not name the lubricant** - no type, no brand, no part number, and no interval for repeating this. It gives only "a small amount of lubricant" and "a thin coat".

Do not treat this like the sweat-path wipe-down. That is a damp cloth after each workout; this is alcohol and then a light lubricant, on the rails and wheels only.
