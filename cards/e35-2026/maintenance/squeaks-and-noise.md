**If a squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling develops, the main cause is most likely one of two reasons.**

**I. The hardware was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.**

- **All bolts that were installed during assembly need to be tightened as much as possible.**
- **It may be necessary to use a larger wrench than the one provided if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently.**
- The manual is emphatic about the share: **"90% of calls to the service department for noise issues can be traced to loose hardware or the rear rails being dirty."**

**II. Dirt build-up on the rear rails and polyurethane wheels.**

- Build-up on the rails **can cause a thumping sound that you would swear is coming from inside the main body of the machine, because noise travels and is amplified in the tubing of the frame.** Do not chase the sound to where you hear it.
- The cleaning and lubrication procedure has its own card.

**If squeaks or other noises persist, check that the unit is properly leveled before calling the service department.** Level it on the six adjustment feet fitted during assembly.

Assembly step 1 deliberately leaves the frame bolts loose - "Please Tighten All Screw After All Components Assembly Complete" - so a machine assembled step by step and never gone back over is exactly the machine this section describes.

This owner's manual lists no other noise sources. The E35 **service** manuals of earlier model years work through a longer list of internal sources; those are different machines and neither list mentions the other's causes, so a technician should work through both.
