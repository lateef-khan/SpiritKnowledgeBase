**Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each workout.**

That is the whole of the printed instruction. The manual names **no cleaning product, no forbidden solvent and no frequency other than "after each workout"**, and it prints no preventative maintenance schedule table for this machine.

Two rules from elsewhere in the manual apply while you do it:

- **Disconnect the elliptical from the electrical outlet prior to cleaning and/or service work.**
- The warranty excludes damage from **corrosion (RUST)** and from **improper maintenance**, so a sweat path left wet is the owner's cost, not SOLE's.

The rails and wheels take a different treatment - rubbing alcohol and a light lubricant, not a damp cloth.
