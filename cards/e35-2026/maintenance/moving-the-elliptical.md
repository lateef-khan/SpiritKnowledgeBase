**The elliptical is equipped with two transport wheels which are engaged when the rear of the elliptical is lifted.**

That is the whole of the manual's transport instructions. It does **not** print a weight, a two-person requirement, a lifting point, a maximum tilt angle, or an instruction to unplug first - although the safety instructions require the machine to be disconnected from the outlet before cleaning or service work, and the cord runs to the front left of the unit.

This machine does not fold. There are no folding instructions in this manual.

After moving it, re-level the machine on its six adjustment feet; the maintenance section says to check levelling before calling about persistent noise.
