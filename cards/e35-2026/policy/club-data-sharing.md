**By joining your first event, you agree to share your username, profile image, and workout data - such as workout dates, distances, active time, and calories - with other participants.**

The consent is given by the act of joining the first event. The manual describes no separate opt-in screen, no way to withdraw the consent later, and no way to take part in an event without sharing.

It applies to **other participants** in the event, which is not the same audience as the fitness cloud sites the SOLE+ App syncs to.

Viewing all available events also requires the console profile to be linked to a SOLE+ account.
