**The service department assists in troubleshooting prior to sending a field technician. SOLE may request pictures and video via email of issues to help with diagnosing.**

The same requirement is written into the owner's responsibilities: **provide pictures or video of any issues via email as requested by the service technician, and assist with troubleshooting and diagnosing prior to a home visit.**

Declining is not neutral. **Expenses for making the elliptical accessible for servicing** are the consumer's responsibility, and a service call that does not involve a defect in workmanship or material is an excluded reimbursement.
