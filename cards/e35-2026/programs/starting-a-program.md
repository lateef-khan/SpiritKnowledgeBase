Once you have decided on the program, **press the "Start This Program" button on the screen, or the physical "START" key on the machine, to begin your workout.**

Either one works; the physical key is not a different mode.

**After a short countdown the console switches to workout mode.** Warm-up and cool down phases, where the program has them, may be skipped by pressing **"Skip"**.
