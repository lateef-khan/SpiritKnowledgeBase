**Templates are preset programs with a preserved set of parameters.** They sit in the additional tab menu at the top of the Programs page.

Two ways to make one:

- **Save a program as a template upon completion** of the workout; or
- **create one from scratch in the Templates tab**: tap the **"New Template"** button, choose your base program, then save it with adjusted properties.

**A maximum of 12 template programs can be stored.**

**To remove unwanted templates, press "Edit" in the Templates tab.**

A template is not a favorite. A favorite is a bookmark on a program; a template stores the parameters you chose with it. The console keeps up to 12 of each, counted separately.
