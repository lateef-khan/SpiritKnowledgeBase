**HIIT is a customizable interval program where you can manually choose:**

- **the duration and resistance of the sprint interval**;
- **the duration and resistance of the rest interval**;
- **the total number of intervals.**

It is listed separately from **Interval**, which is one of the five preset pattern programs and is not user-configurable in the same way. Two different programs, similar names.

The manual prints no limits for the interval durations, the resistance values or the interval count.
