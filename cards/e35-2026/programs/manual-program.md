**Manual program is a timed workout with resistance and incline controlled manually at any time during the workout.**

Nothing in the machine changes the load for you; you set the time and then drive resistance and incline yourself from the console keys, the side control panels or the swing arm buttons.

The Home screen also offers a shortcut straight to **a manually controlled timed workout**, and video classes are **manually controlled workouts of fixed duration** in the same sense.

You can use the heart rate monitor feature during manual mode without running the Heart Rate program.
