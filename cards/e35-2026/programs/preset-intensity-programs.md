Five programs share one description in this manual:

**Hill, Fatburn, Cardio, Strength and Interval follow preset resistance and incline changing patterns.**

**The Total Time and Max Resistance settings define the duration and overall intensity of your workout.**

**This owner's manual prints no individual profile for any of the five** - no segment chart, no per-minute level table and no description of how one differs from another. The five names and the single sentence above are everything the manual gives, which is why they share a card here rather than getting one each.

If you want a shape you control, use **Custom**, where you draw the resistance and incline pattern yourself.
