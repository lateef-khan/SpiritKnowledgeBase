**Keep your bill of sale. Twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale, or eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number, establishes the labor warranty period should service be required.**

**If service is performed, it is in your best interest to obtain and keep all receipts.**

**The same period is printed twice on this page and both halves apply.** The warranty component table on the same page carries a **Labor 1 Year** row; the Service paragraph quoted above is where the 12 months is tied to the bill of sale and where the 18-month fallback for a machine with no bill of sale is stated. The table row alone does not tell you what to do without a receipt.

Labor is stated separately from parts. The **Parts** term for this machine is **2 Years** and the **Frame Weldments** term is **Lifetime**; both live in that table.

Even inside the labor period, **service call reimbursement to the consumer is an exclusion**, as is a dealer service call that does not involve a malfunction or a defect in workmanship or material.
