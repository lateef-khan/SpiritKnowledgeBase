**Warranty will be void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment. (Examples: Garage, outside, carport, etc.)**

This is stricter than the operating specification and the two are easy to confuse. The electrical instructions permit **40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit and 95% non-condensing humidity** as operating limits; the warranty asks for a **climate-controlled, insulated** room regardless. **An uninsulated garage that never leaves 40-120 degrees F still voids the warranty under item 10.**

The manual gives no definition of "climate-controlled" beyond the three examples of what fails it.
