**The warranty period runs from the date of retail sale, as determined by the sale receipt.**

**In the absence of a sales receipt, it runs from eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date.**

Read that carefully: with no receipt, you do not get a shorter warranty measured from purchase - the clock is set by the factory shipping date instead, which SOLE determines **from the serial number**. A machine that sat in a warehouse loses that time.

The Service section states the same rule for labor in its own words: **twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale, or eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number**, establishes the labor warranty period.

**Keep your bill of sale.** It is the only thing that puts the start date at your purchase rather than at the factory.
