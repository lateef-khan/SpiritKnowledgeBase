**The warranty in this manual is effective February 1, 2026** and applies to the elliptical product line.

What is printed about the cover itself:

- **SOLE warrants all its elliptical parts for a period of time listed** in the warranty table: **Frame Weldments Lifetime, Parts 2 Years, Labor 1 Year, Cosmetic Items 90 Days.** Those four rows have their own card.
- **SOLE's responsibilities include providing new or remanufactured parts, at SOLE's option, and technical support to our independent dealers and servicing organizations.** A remanufactured part is a valid fulfilment; the choice is SOLE's, not the owner's.
- **In the absence of a dealer or service organization, these warranties will be administered by SOLE directly to a consumer.**
- A footnote marks the cosmetic exception: **"Cosmetic items including, but not limited to the following: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals."**

The component-and-period table is a graphic in the PDF, so it is **not in the ingested text of this source** - the text carries "The warranty period applies to the following components:" and then only the cosmetic footnote. The four periods were recovered from the rendered page image and are carded separately; searching the ingested text for them will turn up nothing.

Two limits are worth knowing before quoting a period. **The table breaks the machine into four rows only** - frame weldments, parts, labor and cosmetic items - so there is no separate electronics, console or motor term to quote. And **the periods are not shared across the elliptical line**: the E95-2026 table gives Parts 3 Years where this one gives Parts 2 Years.
