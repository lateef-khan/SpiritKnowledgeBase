**The warranty does not cover products that have been sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party. Warranties DO NOT transfer.**

A second owner has no manufacturer's warranty on this machine, however much time is left on the original period, and whether money changed hands or not - a gift is named alongside a sale.

The registration is tied to the original purchase as well: the warranty does not cover **products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at SOLE**, and registration is validated by the original purchase date or proof of purchase.
