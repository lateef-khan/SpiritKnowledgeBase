**THIS ELLIPTICAL IS INTENDED FOR RESIDENTIAL USE ONLY AND IS WARRANTED FOR THE APPLICATION. ANY OTHER APPLICATION VOIDS THIS WARRANTY IN ITS ENTIRETY.**

The notice is printed on the front matter page, before the safety instructions, and the exclusions repeat it twice more:

- **Product use in any environment other than a residential setting** is excluded.
- The warranty **applies only to products in ordinary household use**, and reimbursement is excluded for units **not in ordinary household use**.

There is no light-commercial tier in this manual. A machine placed in an apartment building's shared gym, an office, a studio or a hotel is outside the warranty, and the wording is "**in its entirety**", not partially.
