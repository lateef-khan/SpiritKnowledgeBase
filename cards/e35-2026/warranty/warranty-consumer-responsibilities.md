**This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household use**, and the consumer is responsible for:

1. **Returning the warranty registration card** within 10 days of the original purchase, or registering later with proof of purchase.
2. **Proper use in accordance with the instructions in this manual, including general maintenance.**
3. **Proper installation in accordance with the instructions and with all local electric codes.**
4. **Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage, replacement of blown fuses, and repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring.**
5. **Expenses for making the elliptical accessible for servicing**, including any item that was not part of the elliptical at the time it shipped from the factory.
6. **Damage to the elliptical finish during shipping, installation or following installation.**
7. **Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual.**
8. **Providing pictures or video of any issues via email as requested by the service technician, and assisting with troubleshooting and diagnosing prior to a home visit.**

**Item 2 does not describe this machine.** It reads in full: "Proper use of the **treadmill** in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual, including general maintenance (i.e. **belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under motor cover, and lubrication.** See all components under General Maintenance section)". This is an elliptical owner's manual, and its **General Maintenance section contains none of those four items** - it covers wiping the sweat path, tightening loose hardware, and cleaning and lubricating the rear rails. The clause appears to be carried over from a treadmill warranty. `sole-elliptical-e35-2026-owners-manual` prints both the treadmill wording on page 27 and the elliptical maintenance list on page 26. This card picks no winner and reproduces the clause as printed.
