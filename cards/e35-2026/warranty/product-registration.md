**Record the serial number** of the machine in the space provided on page 1 of the manual. The manual does not say where the serial number label is on the machine.

Two ways to register:

- **The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to SOLE**; or
- **register online at www.soletreadmills.com under the support tab.**

**The registration card must be returned within 10 days of the original purchase to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.** Outside that window, **the warranty can still be registered if you have proof of purchase** - a receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation.

Registration is not optional in practice. The exclusions say the warranty does not cover **products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at SOLE**, and SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists. A separate exclusion voids coverage on **products whose original serial number has been removed or altered.**

The manual also asks you to record the **dealer name, dealer telephone number and purchase date** on the inside page for future contact.
