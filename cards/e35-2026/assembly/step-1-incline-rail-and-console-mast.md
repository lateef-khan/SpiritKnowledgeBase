**Do not fully tighten anything in this step.** The manual prints the note inside step 2 of the sequence: **"NOTE: Please DON'T Tighten All Screws"** and **"Please Tighten All Screw After All Components Assembly Complete"**.

1. **Install 6pcs Adjustment Feet (90)** to the Front Stabilizer and Middle Stabilizer of the Main Frame (1) and the Rear Stabilizer of the Incline Rail Assembly (2).
2. **Install the Incline Rail Assembly (2) into the U-channel bracket of the Main Frame (1).** From the sides install **4pcs Hex Head Bolts (127) with 2pcs Hex Head Bolts (126)**, **6pcs Split Washers (169)**, **6pcs Flat Washers (165)** and **4pcs Star Washers (195)**, as shown in figure 1, and tighten with **Wrench (178)**.
3. **Connect the wire harness.** Connect the **Incline Motor Power Cord (48)** to the female receiver cable coming from the Incline Rail Assembly (2). Connect the **three-pin position sensor connector, Incline Motor Connecting Wire (49)**, to the female receiver coming from the Incline Rail Assembly (2).
4. **Run the Computer Cable (50) through the Console Mast (12)**, pulling the twist tie up through the mast until the cable exits the top. **Install the Console Mast (12) into the receiving bracket on the top of the Main Frame (1)**, fit **4pcs Socket Head Cap Bolts (131)** with **4pcs Split Washers (169)** and **4pcs Flat Washers (165)**, and tighten with the provided **L Allen Wrench (177)**. **Pull slightly on the computer cable at the top of the mast while installing.** This ensures the cable does not get pinched and shorted during console mast assembly.
5. **Install the Bottle Holder (92) to the Console Mast (12) with 2pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)** and tighten with the provided Phillips Head Screw Driver (176).

Two wordings in this step do not add up, and the manual does not resolve either:

- Step 2 says "**Secure with the four bolts**" and then names **4pcs (127) plus 2pcs (126) - six bolts.** The washer counts (6 split, 6 flat) match six bolts, not four.
- Step 3 says "**Connect the 3 wire harness**" and then describes **two** connections.

The six adjustment feet fitted in step 1 are what you level the machine on later; the maintenance section says to check levelling before calling about persistent noise.
