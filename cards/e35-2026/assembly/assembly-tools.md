Five tools are supplied:

| Item | Tool |
| --- | --- |
| #175 | Short Phillips Head Screw Driver |
| #176 | Phillips Head Screw Driver |
| #177 | 8mm L Allen Wrench |
| #178 | 13/14m/m Wrench |
| #179 | 12/14m/m Wrench |

The instructions call for **two wrenches at once** in the steps that use a bolt-and-nut pair, so keep both #178 and #179 to hand.

The General Maintenance section warns that the supplied wrenches may not be enough at final tightening: **"It may be necessary to use a larger wrench than the one provided if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently."**
