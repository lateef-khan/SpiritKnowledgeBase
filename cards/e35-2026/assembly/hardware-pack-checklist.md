The hardware is bagged per assembly step. Numbers in parentheses in the instructions are the drawing item numbers below.

**Hardware Step 1**

| Item | Description | Qty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #126 | 3/8" × 3/4" Hex Head Bolt | 2 pc |
| #127 | 3/8" × 2-1/4" Hex Head Bolt | 4 pc |
| #131 | 3/8" × 3/4" Socket Head Cap Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #136 | M5 × 10m/m Phillips Head Screw | 2 pcs |
| #165 | Ø 3/8" × Ø 19 × 1.5T Flat Washer | 10 pcs |
| #169 | Ø 10 × 2T Split Washer | 10 pcs |
| #195 | Ø 10 Star Washer | 4 pcs |

**Hardware Step 2**

| Item | Description | Qty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #122 | 5/16" × 15mm Hex Head Bolt | 10 pc |
| #123 | 5/16" × 1-1/4" Hex Head Bolt | 2 pc |
| #136 | M5 × 10mm Phillips Head Screw | 4 pc |
| #149 | 5/16" × 7T Nylon Nut | 2 pc |
| #160 | Ø 5/16" × Ø 20 × 1.5T Flat Washer | 6 pc |
| #162 | Ø 5/16" × Ø 23 × 3T Flat Washer | 4 pc |
| #164 | Ø 8.5 × Ø 26 × 2.0T Flat Washer | 2 pc |

**Hardware Step 3**

| Item | Description | Qty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #124 | 5/16" × 1-3/4" Hex Head Bolt | 6 pcs |
| #149 | 5/16" × 7T Nylon Nut | 6 pcs |
| #168 | Ø 8 × 23 × 1.5T Curved Washer | 4 pcs |

**Hardware Step 4**

| Item | Description | Qty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #136 | M5 × 10mm Phillips Head Screw | 18 pcs |
| #139 | 3.5 × 12mm Sheet Metal Screw | 10 pcs |
| #141 | M6 × 10mm Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |

**Item #136 and item #149 appear in more than one step bag.** #136 is called for in steps 1, 2 and 4 (2 + 4 + 18 pieces) and #149 in steps 2 and 3 (2 + 6 pieces). Do not read a single line as the total for the machine.

The manual prints **M5 × 10m/m** in the Step 1 list and **M5 × 10mm** in the Step 2 and Step 4 lists for the same item #136; that is a spelling difference in the manual, not two parts.
