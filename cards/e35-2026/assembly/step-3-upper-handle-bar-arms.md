**Insert the left Top Handle Bar (19) onto the left Lower Handle Bar and secure with 3pcs Hex Head Bolts (124), 2pcs Curved Washers (168) and 3pcs Nylon Nuts (149), using two wrenches (179 & 178). Secure the right Top Handle Bar (20) on the right Lower Handle Bar the same way.**

The step is a single instruction in the manual with no numbered sub-steps.

Counts per side against the Hardware Step 3 bag: **3 bolts × 2 sides = 6 (#124, 6 pcs)**, **3 nuts × 2 sides = 6 (#149, 6 pcs)**, **2 curved washers × 2 sides = 4 (#168, 4 pcs)**. All three match, so there is **one bolt per side that takes no curved washer**; the manual does not say which of the three.
