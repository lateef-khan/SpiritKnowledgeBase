1. **Align the plate of the Left Connecting Arm (8) with the foot pad axle of the Left Pedal Arm (6) and put it in.** Secure the **Fixing Axle (25)** with **2pcs Flat Washers (162)** and **2pcs Hex Head Bolts (122)** using **Wrench (179)**. Do the same for the Right Connecting Arm (9) on the Right Pedal Arm (7).
2. **Install the left and right Lower Handle Bars (10) (11) onto the left and right console mast shafts** and secure with **Hex Head Bolts (122)** together with **Flat Washers (164)** using **Wrench (179)**.
3. **Untie the rod end bearing on the left Connecting Arm (8).** Insert **Hex Head Bolt (123)** through the rod end bearing bracket on Swing Arm (10) and use wrenches **(178 & 179)** to tighten together with **Flat Washer (160)** and **Nylon Nut (149)** in the rod end bearing bracket on the left Swing Arm. Fix the right Swing Arm (11) and right Connecting Arm (9) the same way with the same hardware.
4. **Run the Handle Wire, Incline (62) from the Left Handpulse Assembly (15)** through the left round hole of the console mast and pull it out at the top. Secure to the console mast left side with **2pcs Hex Head Bolts (122)** and **2pcs Flat Washers (160)** using **Wrench (179)**. Do the same on the right with the **Handle Wire, Resistance (61) from the Right Handpulse Assembly (16)**.
5. **Plug all of the connectors into the back of the console: Computer Cable (50), Handle Wire Resistance (61) and Handle Wire Incline (62).** Secure the **Console (45)** on the console mounting plate with **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)**, tightened with Phillips Head Screw Driver (176).

Two things the manual leaves open:

- **Parts (10) and (11) are called "Lower Handle Bars" in step 2 and "Swing Arm" in step 3.** Same item numbers, two names, in consecutive sentences.
- **Step 2 does not state how many Hex Head Bolts (122) to use.** The step's bag holds 10 pcs of item #122; steps 1 and 4 of this page account for 4 and 4, which leaves 2 for step 2, one per side, matching the 2 pcs of Flat Washer #164 in the bag. That arithmetic is this card's, not the manual's - the manual prints no number there.

Left and right are wired to different functions: **incline on the left handpulse, resistance on the right.**
