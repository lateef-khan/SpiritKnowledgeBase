1. **Match the Connecting Arm Covers (L) and (R), (110) and (111), on Connecting Arm (L) (8)** and secure with **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)** and a **Sheet Metal Screw (139)** using the Short Phillips Head Screw Driver (175). Repeat for the other side.
2. **Install the Slide Wheel Covers (95) above each side of the Slide Wheel Urethanes** and secure with **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)** using Phillips Head Screw Driver (176).
3. **Secure the Front Stabilizer Cover (112) on the front stabilizer with 2pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)**, using Phillips Head Screw Driver (176).
4. **Install the Cover Holders (23), with the hole for mounting the plastic cover on the bent tab facing rearward**, and secure them on the Incline Rail Assembly (2) with **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (141)**. Install the **Inclinable Rail Cover (115)** on the rail base with **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)**. Secure the **Rear Bar Cover (116)** on the Rear Stabilizer and the Cover Holders (23) with **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)**, using Phillips Head Screw Driver (176).
5. **Secure the Bottom Cover (113) on the Incline Rail Assembly (2) with 4pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)**, using Phillips Head Screw Driver (176).
6. **Install the Handle Bar Covers (106 & 107, 108 & 109) over the handle bar axle connections** and secure with **4pcs Sheet Metal Screws (139)** using Phillips Head Screw Driver (176).
7. **Match the right and left Console Mast Covers (100 & 101) and install them on the Console Mast (12) with 4pcs Sheet Metal Screws (139).**
8. **Plug both Oval End Caps (76) into both ends of the stabilizer tube.**

**Step 4 is the point at which everything can be tightened.** Step 1 told you to leave the frame bolts loose until all components are assembled; this is the last assembly page.

The **cover holder orientation in sub-step 4 - bent tab facing rearward - is the only orientation the manual calls out**, and getting it wrong leaves nowhere to mount the rear bar cover.

Item #141 (M6 × 10mm) is used only here, in sub-step 4. Everything else on this page is #136 or #139.
