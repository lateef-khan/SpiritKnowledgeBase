**"Before returning your Sole product to any retailer, or if you require any assistance with assembly or technical support, please call us first for assistance at 866-780-7653."**

The notice is printed twice - on the front matter page and again on the back cover - which is the manual's own emphasis, not this card's.

**866-780-7653** is the same number given for the service department and for questions about where to obtain service.

Calling first is also how the manual routes an assembly problem. If parts are damaged or missing at unpacking, it tells you to **contact your dealer immediately**.

This is separate from shipping a unit back to the factory, which additionally requires a return authorization number.
