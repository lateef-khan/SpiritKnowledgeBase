- **Keep hands away from all moving parts.**
- **Never drop or insert any object into any openings.**
- The machine has **obvious pinch points and other caution areas that can cause harm**; that is the manual's stated reason for keeping children under 13 away.

The manual gives no procedure for retrieving an object that has fallen inside. It does say to disconnect the machine from the outlet before any cleaning or service work.
