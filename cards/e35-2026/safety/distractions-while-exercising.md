**Use caution while participating in other activities while using your elliptical, such as watching television, reading, etc. These distractions may cause you to lose balance, which may result in serious injury.**

The safety page and the console section pull in opposite directions and the manual never reconciles them: this warning tells you distractions may cause you to lose balance, while the touchscreen section invites you to watch video classes, use pre-installed apps and **mirror your phone screen** onto the console during a workout. The manual states both and resolves neither.
