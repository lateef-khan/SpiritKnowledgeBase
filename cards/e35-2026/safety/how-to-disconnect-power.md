1. **Turn all controls to the off position.**
2. **Remove the plug from the outlet.** That is the manual's stated way to disconnect.

Rules that go with it:

- **DANGER - to reduce the risk of electric shock, disconnect your SOLE elliptical from the electrical outlet prior to cleaning and/or service work.**
- **NEVER remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power.**
- **Unplug the elliptical during an electrical storm** as a precaution.

The power connector is on the **front, left side of the unit**, and the power switch sits where the line cord plugs into the machine on the left side near the front.
