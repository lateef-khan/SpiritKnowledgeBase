**NEVER use your elliptical during an electrical storm.** Surges may occur in your household power supply that could damage elliptical components.

**Unplug the elliptical during an electrical storm as a precaution.**

The warranty exclusions back this up: elemental damage including **power surges and lightning storms** is not covered.

The same two sentences are printed in the AE, 2012-2015 and WE55/WE95 books and in every stencil book except the E20 pair. The E98-2011 (`facility or household`), WE25 (`lightening strikes`), 2007/2008 and E20 books (no unplug sentence) word it differently and are not covered here. The warranty sentence above describes the 2026 book.
