- **Never operate the elliptical if it has a damaged cord or plug.**
- **If the elliptical is not working properly, call your dealer.**
- **Keep the cord away from heated surfaces.**
- Do not disable the grounded plug with improper adapters or in any way modify the cord set.

The manual gives no cord replacement procedure for the owner. Repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring is listed as the **consumer's** responsibility, not SOLE's.
