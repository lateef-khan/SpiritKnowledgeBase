- **Never expose this elliptical to rain or moisture.** The product is **not** designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.
- **Operating temperature specification: 40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit.**
- **Humidity: 95% non-condensing** - no water drops forming on surfaces.
- The safety instructions state the same rule more briefly: **do not use outdoors.**

The warranty adds a harder rule of its own: it is **void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment**, and it names a garage, outside and a carport as examples. A garage can sit inside 40-120 degrees F and still void the warranty.
