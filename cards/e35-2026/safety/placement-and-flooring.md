- Install the elliptical on a **flat level surface**.
- **Do not operate the elliptical on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both the carpet and the elliptical may result.**

The manual does not prescribe an equipment mat, a clearance distance around the machine, or a floor loading. It does say elsewhere that if squeaks or other noises persist you should check that the unit is **properly leveled** before calling the service department; the six adjustment feet fitted during assembly are what you level it with.
