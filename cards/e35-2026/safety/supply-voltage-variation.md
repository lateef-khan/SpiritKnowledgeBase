**If voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more, the performance of your elliptical may be affected. Such conditions are not covered under your warranty.**

If you suspect the voltage is low, contact your local power company or a licensed electrician for proper testing.

The warranty exclusions repeat the point in their own words: damage from an **inadequate power supply** is excluded, as is damage from power surges and lightning storms.

All 32 B books print the ten-percent rule with the same not-covered consequence; the 2007/2008 books phrase it `If A.C. voltage varies ...`. The warranty-exclusion sentence above describes the 2026 book.
