**Do not operate where aerosol spray products are being used or where oxygen is being administered.**

**Sparks from the motor may ignite a highly gaseous environment.**

The machine has both an incline motor and a resistance gear motor, so the warning is not limited to one location on the frame.

The 2011-2015 native books (including the AE pair) and every stencil book except the E20 pair print both sentences with `may ignite`. The 2007/2008 books print the first sentence with no sparks sentence, the WE books read `could ignite`, and the E20 pair prints the first sentence with no sparks sentence - none of those join this card. The motor-location paragraph above describes the 2026 machine; the E20-2013/2014/2015 machines share their book's list but have no incline motor and are not covered here.
