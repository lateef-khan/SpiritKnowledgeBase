**The hand pulse sensors are not medical devices.**

Various factors, **including the user's movement**, may affect the accuracy of heart rate readings. The pulse sensors are **intended only as exercise aids in determining heart rate trends in general.**

The Heart Rate program drives the machine from this reading, so the same caveat applies to it. A Bluetooth chest strap is an alternative source of heart rate on this console.
