**Always hold on to a handle bar while making control changes (incline, level, etc.).**

Two related rules on the same page:

- **NEVER operate this elliptical without reading and completely understanding the results of any operational change you request from the computer.**
- Changes in resistance and incline do **not** occur immediately, so expect the machine to keep moving under you while the command is carried out.

Both the console keys and the swing arm buttons can change the incline, so this applies wherever you make the change from.
