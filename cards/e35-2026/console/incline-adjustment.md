This machine has a **computer controlled power incline**.

- **The power incline can be controlled by the buttons on the console or by the buttons on the swing arms.**
- **It will automatically adjust via the incline motor when you exercise using a built-in workout program.**
- At its **lowest position you get a normal elliptical workout**. As the incline increases you feel your knees rise higher with each step, which involves more muscle fibers due to the increased range of motion.

The manual prints **no incline range, no number of incline levels and no degree figures** for this machine. Programs on the touchscreen treat incline values as absolute and settable directly, but the manual never says what the endpoints are.

Do not confuse the incline with the **foot pedal angle**, which is a separate three-position mechanical adjustment under each footpad.
