**On the Home page, choose from a variety of pre-installed Apps, or use the Mirroring function to cast the content from your smartphone directly to the console screen.**

Once the content is on, **use the bottom panel for navigation and view settings.** Three view layouts are available:

| Layout | What stays on screen |
| --- | --- |
| **Full interface** | status bar, control panels, real-time stats |
| **Partial interface** | status bar, real-time stats |
| **Minimal interface** | full-screen content with all the panels hidden |

The same logic applies while watching video classes. **Navigate through the video by sliding on the progress bar, or skip 10-second intervals** with the skip icons. **The "Pause" button pauses both your workout and the video at the same time.**

The safety page warns that distractions such as watching television or reading **may cause you to lose balance**. The manual prints both and reconciles neither.
