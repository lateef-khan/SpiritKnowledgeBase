**The exercise equipment's Bluetooth device can also be connected to a Bluetooth wireless heart rate chest strap transmitter.**

**The chest strap transmitter can still connect to the exercise equipment even though the App has already been connected with the exercise equipment.** The app connection and the strap connection do not exclude one another.

The manual names no strap model, no protocol version and no pairing procedure for the strap itself.

The **Heart Rate program requires a heart rate monitor**, and the hand pulse sensors carry an accuracy disclaimer, so a strap is the more reliable source for that program.
