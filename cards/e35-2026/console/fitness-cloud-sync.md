The SOLE+ App can sync your workout data with one of the fitness cloud sites SOLE supports:

- **Apple Health**
- **Google Health Connect**
- **Strava**
- **MapMyFitness**

The manual adds "**with more to come**", so the list is the state at printing rather than a closed set.

The machine itself does not reach these sites; the app does. The console's own history holds only the last 10 workouts, so the app is also how you keep a longer record.
