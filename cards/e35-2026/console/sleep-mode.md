The console ships with **Sleep mode** and offers three choices:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **ON** | The unit goes into an energy-saving state after **15 minutes** of inactivity. |
| **OFF** | The console will stay lit while the power is on. |
| **RETAIL** | The console will run the demo video after **3 minutes** of inactivity. |

**On TFT displays, click "Sleep Mode" under Settings to switch among the three.**

RETAIL is a showroom setting. A machine that starts playing a demo video three minutes after you stop is in RETAIL, not broken.
