1. **Scan the QR code on the console screen** to download the mobile app first.
2. **Install the app and complete registration.**
3. **Open the QR code scanning camera in your SOLE+ app and scan the second QR code** to link your local profile with the SOLE+ account.

Two rules printed with the procedure:

- **Your console profile cannot be linked to multiple SOLE+ accounts at the same time.**
- **If you skip this step you can access it again later in Profile settings.**

Linking is what unlocks the full workout history beyond the 10 records held on the console, and viewing all available **Club** events requires a linked profile.
