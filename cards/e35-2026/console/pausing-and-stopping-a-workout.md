**Stop anytime by pressing the physical "STOP" key or by tapping the red pause button in the bottom right corner of the screen.**

**After 5 minutes of inactivity in the Pause mode, the workout will end automatically.**

That five-minute timeout is a common surprise: a workout you paused and walked away from is finished, not waiting. It is separate from **Sleep mode**, which blanks the console after 15 minutes of inactivity when it is set to ON.

When you pause during a video class, **the "Pause" button pauses both your workout and the video at the same time.**
