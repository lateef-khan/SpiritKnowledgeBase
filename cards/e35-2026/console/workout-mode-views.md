Once the workout has started, **after a short countdown the console appears in its workout mode.**

- **Warm-up and cool down phases may be skipped by pressing "Skip".**
- During the workout, **switch between Stats (Focus), Charts and Track views** to check on your progress.
- Go to the **Media** section for media content, or open **Garmin** to observe your Garmin smart watch real-time data.
- **In the Focus view you can select which parameters to display by tapping on any of the number blocks.**
- **Use the side control panels or the physical keys to adjust resistance and incline.**

Stopping and pausing are covered separately, including the five-minute rule that ends a paused workout on its own.
