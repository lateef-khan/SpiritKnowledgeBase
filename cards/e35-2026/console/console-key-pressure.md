**Do not use excessive pressure on console control keys. They are precision set to function properly with little finger pressure.**

**If you feel the buttons are not functioning properly with normal pressure, contact your SOLE dealer.**

The manual gives no key test, no self-diagnostic and no engineering menu for the owner to check the keys with. Reporting the symptom to the dealer is the only route it offers.
