The control panel diagram labels four items besides the display:

- **Cooling fan**
- **Fan power switch** - the fan has its own switch, separate from the machine's power switch
- **Speaker**
- **Volume control**

The page is a labelled photograph with callout lines. **The extraction kept the labels but lost the picture and the leader lines**, so the exact position of each control on the console face is not recoverable from the text. The labels themselves are printed as listed above.

The manual gives no fan speed settings, no automatic fan behaviour and no volume range.
