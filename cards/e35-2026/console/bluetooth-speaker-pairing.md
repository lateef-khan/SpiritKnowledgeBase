**The exercise equipment can play music wirelessly via Bluetooth.**

1. **Turn on your mobile phone's or tablet's Bluetooth function.**
2. **Search for the name "BT-Speaker" in your device's Bluetooth menu.**
3. **Tap to connect.** Your device can now transmit music to the exercise equipment.

"BT-Speaker" is the machine's audio device name and is separate from the name the SOLE+ App looks for when it searches for Sole equipment. A device paired to BT-Speaker is paired to the speaker, not to the workout data connection.

Volume is set with the **volume control** on the console.
