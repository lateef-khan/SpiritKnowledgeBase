**When power is connected to the elliptical, the console will automatically power up.** There is no separate console on key.

- **This model is connected directly to 115 VAC.**
- **The power switch is located where the line cord plugs into the unit, on the left side near the front.**
- The operation instructions label the same place: **POWER CONNECTOR - FRONT, LEFT SIDE OF UNIT.**

If the console appears dark but power is on, check **Sleep mode** before assuming a fault: with Sleep mode ON the unit goes into an energy-saving state after 15 minutes of inactivity.

The grounding section of this same manual calls the supply a **nominal 110-volt** circuit while this page says 115 VAC. Both figures are printed in this manual.
