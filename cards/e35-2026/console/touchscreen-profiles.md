Launched for the first time, the console prompts you to **either enter as a guest or create a new profile**.

- **Tap + to create a new profile.**
- **A maximum of 9 profiles can be created.**
- **Each profile can be protected with a passcode.**

The manual recommends setting up your own profile because it lets the machine remember:

- your **physical parameters**;
- your **favorite programs and templates**;
- your **own custom program with an adjustable intensity pattern**;
- **workout records that can be synchronized with the SOLE+ mobile app.**

In **Guest mode** you can still set age and weight, from the Home screen, so the machine can calculate your workout summary more accurately.
