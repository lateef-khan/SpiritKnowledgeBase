Access video classes from the **"Videos" tab of the bottom navigation bar**.

- **Video classes are pre-recorded manually controlled workouts of fixed duration.** The class does not drive the machine; you control resistance and incline yourself.
- There are **hundreds of videos of 7 activity types: Elliptical, Yoga, Stretching, Boxing, Sculpt, Meditation and Full Sweat.**
- **Filter classes by instructor, duration, difficulty level, etc.**
- The additional tab menu at the top of the page features **Collections - sequences of video classes that follow a certain theme.**

The manual does not say whether the video library needs a subscription, a network connection or an app account.
