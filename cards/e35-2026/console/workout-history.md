**Press "History" on the Home page** to see your latest workout records.

- **Up to 10 records can be stored locally on the console.**
- **You can access your full workout history if you link your profile to the SOLE+ mobile app.**
- **Tap on a record preview to see the full summary.**

A workout that has dropped off the console list is not lost if the profile was linked; it is in the app. If the profile was never linked, the eleventh workout pushes the first one out and the manual describes no way to get it back.

The Type-C port on the console **cannot** be used to save workout data. It charges only.
