Hardware: **1 crossed head self-tapping screw (No. 22) M4 x 10L, 1 limited bracket (No. 27)**. Tool: Allen key 73.

1. **Lift up the adjustable bar (No. 08)** and insert the backrest support bracket (No. 3) into the main frame (No. 1) as shown. **Ensure that the adjustable bar (No. 08) is pointing upright when inserting for proper assembly.** The page draws a "Yes" and a "No" picture - bar up, bar down.
2. Attach the limited bracket (No. 27) onto the main frame (No. 01) and tighten with 1 crossed head self-tapping screw (No. 22).

The limited bracket is the end stop on the seat rail. The service document repeats the order: "Assemble Seat and Latch" then "NOTE: Lift up on adjustable Bar, then insert backrest support bracket/seat to main frame."
