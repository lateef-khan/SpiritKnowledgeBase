Hardware: pre-installed on the front post, **4 Allen screws (No. 17) and 4 arc washers (No. 33)**. Tools: Allen key 73 and wrench 72.

1. Remove the pre-installed 4 Allen screws (No. 17) and 4 arc washers (No. 33) from the front post (No. 02) and connect the upper sensor wire (No. 10) with the lower sensor wire (No. 11) (fig. 3.2).
2. Connect the upper pulse sensor wire (No. 12) with the lower pulse sensor wire (No. 13) (fig. 3.3).
3. Insert the front post (No. 02) into the main frame (No. 01) and secure with the 4 Allen bolts (No. 17) and 4 curved washers (No. 33).

The page is headed "Tension and Pulse Wires" but this bike's tension knob (No. 51) is on the frame and no tension cable is connected here; the upright's version of this step does connect one.

The service document: **"Do not PINCH sensor / hand pulse wires in mast tube"**, with a photo captioned "secure sensor wires - potential pinch point with assembly". A pinched sensor wire is the usual reason the console shows no speed.
