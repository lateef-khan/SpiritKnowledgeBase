Hardware: **2 Allen bolts (No. 19) M8 x 65L, 2 Allen bolts (No. 16) M8 x 40L, 4 curved washers (No. 33) 8.5 x 20 x 1.5t, 2 nuts (No. 31) M8**. Tools: Allen key 73 and wrench 72.

1. Attach the front handlebar (No. 04) to the front post (No. 02), tighten with 2 Allen bolts (No. 19), 2 curved washers (No. 33) and 2 nuts (No. 31).
2. Attach the rear handlebar (No. 05) to the backrest support bracket (No. 03), tighten with 2 Allen bolts (No. 16) and 2 curved washers (No. 33).
3. **Remove the sticker (No. 36) from the main frame (No. 01), gently pull the middle pulse sensor wire (No. 14) out of the upright and connect it to the lower pulse sensor wire (No. 21).**

The service document: "Attaching the Handlebars - NOTE: Must remove sticker to access middle pulse sensor wire, then connect into Lower pulse wire." The rear handlebar carries the hand pulse grips; a rear handlebar with no heart-rate reading is this connection, still under its sticker.
