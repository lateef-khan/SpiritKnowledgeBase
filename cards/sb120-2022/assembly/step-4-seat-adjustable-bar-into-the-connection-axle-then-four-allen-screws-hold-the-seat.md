Hardware: **4 Allen screws (No. 32) M8 x 15L**; pre-installed on the connection axle, **2 Allen bolts (No. 20) and 2 flat washers (No. 34)**. Tool: Allen key 73.

1. Remove the pre-installed 2 Allen bolts (No. 20) and 2 flat washers (No. 34) from the connection axle (No. 09).
2. Insert the adjustable bar (No. 08) into the connection axle (No. 09) as shown, then tighten with the 2 Allen bolts (No. 20) and 2 flat washers (No. 34).
3. Attach the seat (No. 25) to the backrest support bracket (No. 03) and tighten with the 4 Allen screws (No. 32).

The adjustable bar (the parts list calls it the "adjusted bar", 16 x 2t x 305L) is the lever that locks the seat position on the main frame; it is lifted in the next step.
