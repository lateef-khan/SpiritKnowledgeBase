Hardware: pre-installed on the back of the console, **2 crossed head screws (No. 23)**. Tool: Allen key 73 (the page names it; the screws are Phillips).

1. Remove the pre-installed 2 crossed head screws (No. 23) from the back of the console (No. 24).
2. Connect the upper sensor wire (No. 10) and the upper pulse sensor wire (No. 12) to the console (No. 24), then attach the console (No. 24) to the front post (No. 02) and secure with the 2 crossed head screws (No. 23).

**NOTE: CHECK TO ENSURE THAT ALL BOLTS AND NUTS ARE TIGHTENED BEFORE USING THE MACHINE.**

The console runs on batteries; there is no adapter to plug in as a last step.
