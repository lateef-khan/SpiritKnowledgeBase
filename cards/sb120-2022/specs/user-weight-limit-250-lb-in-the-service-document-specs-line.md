**250 lb.** The service document's whole *Specs* block is that one line: "User weight limit- 250 lbs". It agrees
with the owner's manual, which prints "Weight Limit: 250 lbs." as item 10 of the Important Safety Instructions - the
safety section holds that rule. Nothing else on the SB120 is specified anywhere: no dimensions, weight, flywheel
weight or belt size beyond the parts list's "Belt 310 J5".

The same document also records that the serial number is on the unit at the rear base frame and that no registration
card ships with the owner's manual, and that the frame and parts carry a one-year residential warranty with no labour
(the warranty section holds the terms).
