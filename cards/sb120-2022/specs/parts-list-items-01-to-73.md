**What the list tells a service call.** The console is item 24; the 8-level tension knob item 51; the flywheel assembly item 66 and its adjusters items 68-69; the belt item 59, **310 J5**; the pedals items 28-29, **JG-16B**; the five sensor and pulse wires items 10-14 and 21; the hand-pulse sensors item 64; the seat item 25 and back cushion item 26. Three rows are printed as a bare *End cap* (48, 49, 50). The packed tools are the last two rows, items 72 (multifunctional wrench) and 73 (Allen key) - the December 2019 SB120 service document sizes them as *13&15mm* and *5mm*.

The UB120 upright bike prints the same layout with 55 items and `UB120-BK1036-PARTnn` serials (`ub120-2023-specs-parts-list-items-1-to-55`); which other Xterra books print a list is `xterra-specs-parts-which-bike-elliptical-and-climber-manuals-print-an-exploded-view-and-parts-list`.
