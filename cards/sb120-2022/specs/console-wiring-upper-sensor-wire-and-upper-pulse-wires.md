**Two plugs at the console, labelled in the service document's photograph: 1 - Upper sensor wire, 2 - Upper Pulse
Wires.** Behind them the owner's manual's parts list has six wires:

| Part | Name | Joins |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Upper sensor wire | the console (speed), down the front post to 11 |
| 11 | Lower sensor wire | from the sensor bracket (41) at the crank |
| 12 | Upper pulse sensor wire | the console (hand pulse), down the front post |
| 13 | Pulse sensor wire | the front-handlebar grips; step 3 calls it the "Lower Pulse Sensor Wire (No. 13)" |
| 14 | Middle pulse sensor wire | runs inside the main frame to the seat; joins 21 |
| 21 | Lower pulse sensor wire | the rear-handlebar grips (hand pulse sensors 64) |

Step 3 joins 10 to 11 and 12 to 13 before the front post goes into the main frame; step 6, after the rear handlebar
is on, says "Remove the Sticker (No. 36) from the Main Frame, gently pull the Middle Pulse Sensor Wire (No. 14) out
of the upright and connect it to the Lower Pulse Sensor Wire (No. 21)"; step 8 plugs 10 and 12 into the console. The
service document's tips: "Do not PINCH sensor/ hand pulse wires in mast tube" and "Must remove sticker to access
middle pulse sensor wire, then connect into Lower pulse wire" - a missed sticker is a recumbent with pulse from the
front grips only.

The parts list and step 3 disagree on part 13's name (Pulse sensor wire vs Lower Pulse Sensor Wire); the card
follows the parts list, since 21 is the one the list and step 6 both call lower. The console runs on two AAA cells;
the same platform's UB120 upright uses an Extension Sensor Wire (7) and two Pulse Wires (27) instead.
