1. Remove the **4 console assembly locking bolts** with an **M5 L-Allen wrench**, and use a **13 open-end
   wrench** to remove the **2 hex bolts of the console support**.
2. Unplug the **computer cable** and the **Rapid key wires**, then replace the console.

**The section is misheaded.** It is printed as "9-2 Controller replacement" - the same words as 9-1 -
in both the table of contents and the page heading; the steps are the console's. The TRX5500 book's
twin procedure has the four Allen bolts but not the two hex bolts.
