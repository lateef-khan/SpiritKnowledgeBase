In the standby state, press the **speed deceleration (Speed ▼) key and the Program key at the same time**. The buzzer sounds **twice** to confirm the switch.

The book prints no display prompt; the buzzer is the only confirmation. The service manual's calibration procedure for this console ([card](calibration-program-key-eng-wheel-42-max-16.md)) sets a minimum of 1.0 and a maximum of 16.0 with no unit step, so this key combination is the only unit switch either book prints.
