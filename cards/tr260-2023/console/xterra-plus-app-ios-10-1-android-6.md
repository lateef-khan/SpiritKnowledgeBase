The machine has a Bluetooth transceiver that lets it interact with selected phones or tablets through the free **Xterra + App** from the Apple Store or Google Play.

1. Download the App.
2. Open it on your phone or tablet and make sure Bluetooth is enabled on the device.
3. In the App, click the Bluetooth icon in the top right corner to search for your Xterra + equipment.
4. In the Bluetooth scan result list, select the machine to connect. When the App and the equipment are synced, the **Bluetooth icon on the console display lights up**. Click "DISPLAY" to start using the App; the DISPLAY button shows the current workout data.
5. When your exercise is complete, press "END & SAVE WORKOUT" in the App to store the workout data. You will be prompted to sync it with each available fitness cloud site — Strava, Map My Fitness or Fitbit, "with more to come" — after installing that site's own app so its icon is active.

Your device needs at least **iOS 10.1 or Android 6.0** for the Xterra + App. The 2023-2025 books raise that to iOS 13.1 / Android 8.0 ([later app card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-xterra-plus-app-ios-13-android-8.md)), and the TRX3500/TRX4500 books print an older "Xterra App" with iOS 7 / Android 4.4 ([card](../../shared/console/xterra-trx-console-xterra-app-ios-7-android-4-4.md)).
