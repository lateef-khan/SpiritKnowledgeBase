1. Attach the Safety key to enable the display (if not already on).
2. Press the **Start** key to begin belt movement. Adjust the speed using the Speed ▲/▼ keys, or press one of the **rapid SPEED buttons 2, 4, 6, 8, 10**.
3. To incline the deck, press the Incline ▲/▼ key to the desired level, or press one of the **rapid INCLINE buttons 0, 3, 5, 7, 10**.
4. To stop the tread-belt, press the **Stop** key or pull away the Safety key.

The console pictured in the owner's manual has the incline quick keys (10, 7, 5, 3, 0) in a column left of the LCD and the speed quick keys (10, 8, 6, 4, 2) in a column to its right, with Incline ▼/▲, Enter and Speed ▼/▲ under the LCD and Stop, Program, Start below those.

The service manual for this console (Dyaco GT75A-NT050) says the same rapid keys — speed 2/4/6/8/10 kph (mph) and incline 0/3/5/7/10 — but its Up key description says "the maximum incline position is 16", which contradicts the incline 0-10 keys, the calibration step that sets the maximum incline to 10 levels, and the owner's manual. Treat 10 as the working maximum ([service-manual keys card](keys-in-ready-and-run-mode-per-the-service-manual.md)).
