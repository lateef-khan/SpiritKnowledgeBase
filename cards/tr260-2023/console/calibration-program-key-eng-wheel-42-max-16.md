The owner's manual prints no calibration procedure; this is from the Dyaco GT75A-NT050 service manual.

1. Remove the Safety Key.
2. Press and hold down **PROGRAM** and at the same time replace the Safety Key; "**Eng**" appears in the speed window.
3. Press **START**, adjust the wheel size diameter to **42**, then press START.
4. Set the minimum speed to **1.0** and press START to confirm and move on.
5. Set the maximum speed to **16.0** and press START to confirm and move on.
6. Set the maximum incline value to **10 levels**.
7. Press Start to begin calibration. The process is automatic; the belt starts without warning, so **do not stand on the belt**.

The procedure has no unit step: the owner's manual's key combination is the only unit switch printed ([units card](switch-kilometers-and-miles-with-speed-down-and-program.md)). The 1.0 and 16.0 read as kilometres per hour (the TR150 service manual's identical procedure says "1km (0.5Mi)" and "16km (10Mi)"); the service manual's own speed function gives 0.5 to 10.0 mph as the work range ([ranges card](display-modes-and-window-ranges-per-the-service-manual.md)).
