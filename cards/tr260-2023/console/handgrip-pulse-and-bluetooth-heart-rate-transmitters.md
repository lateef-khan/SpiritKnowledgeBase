Any time the treadmill is operating, grasping a handgrip shows the pulse feature and the heart-beat value is displayed. Grasp firmly while using this feature, otherwise the value fluctuates. The console will also pick up wireless heart-rate transmitters that are **Bluetooth compatible**.

The book prints no chest strap in the box and no wearing instructions. The service manual's block diagram shows a wireless HR receiver on the display board, and its Pulse function says the reading comes "by using hand pulse or receiver" ([service-manual card](display-modes-and-window-ranges-per-the-service-manual.md)).
