Recovery measures **how fast your heart rate returns to normal after exercise**. The faster it
returns, the better aerobic shape you are in.

1. Press the **Recovery** button. A **60 second countdown** starts.
2. **Hold the hand pulse sensors and do not exercise** during the countdown.
3. At the end of the 60 seconds a score is displayed.

The score runs from **F1 to F6**, with **F1 the highest score and F6 the lowest**.

**This is the only fitness test on any of the eleven Spirit residential XE elliptical owner's
manuals.** None of them has a Gerkin, WFI, military or YMCA test, and none has a VO2max score - the
XE350, XE550, XE395, XE195, XE295, XG400, XE795 and XE395ENT manuals print no fitness test of any
kind.

The manual gives no interpretation of F1 to F6 beyond "highest" and "lowest" - no age bands, no
target ranges - so this KB holds none.
