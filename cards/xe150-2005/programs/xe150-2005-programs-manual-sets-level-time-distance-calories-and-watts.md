Use the Up/Down keys to highlight the **Manual** icon at the top of the display and press **Mode**.
Then set five things in turn, pressing Mode after each:

1. **Work level** - the profile blinks; Up/Down adjusts it.
2. **Time**.
3. **Distance** - a target distance.
4. **Calories**.
5. **Watts**.

After all five are set, press **Start** to begin.

**What setting Watts does.** If you set a Watt figure the machine **automatically adjusts the
workload to maintain continuous work** at that figure. Set Watt to 100 and it tries to keep you
working at a rate equal to 100 Watts - the rate that would keep a 100 Watt light bulb burning. Watts
are determined by how much work you are doing, which on this machine is the speed you are pedalling
combined with the workload. **In Watt mode, if you change your speed the machine changes the workload
to hold the same Watt level: pedal faster and the workload decreases, pedal slower and it
increases.**

*The manual's Watt paragraph names the machine as "the **ZE50** Elliptical". There is no ZE50 in this
book or in this product line; it is a stray name in the source and is recorded here only so a reader
who meets it knows it is not a different machine.*

**Manual mode on the XE350 and XE550, in the same manual, is a different flow** - Age, Weight and
Time only, starting at level one. See `spirit-ce-programs-manual-mode`.
