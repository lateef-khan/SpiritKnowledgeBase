The Target HR program sets a target heart rate and the machine **automatically adjusts the work load
to safely reach that target and maintain it**.

1. Use Up/Down to select the **Target HR** program, then press **Mode**.
2. The display shows a number with the **age** icon blinking. Set your age with Up/Down, then Mode.
3. The display shows a blinking percentage, or the letters **THR**. Up/Down chooses between
   **55%**, **75%**, **90%** and **THR**.
   - Choosing a percentage sets the target automatically as that percentage of your maximum heart
     rate, worked out from your age. **This is why the age must be right.**
   - Choosing **THR** makes the **Pulse** window blink so you can set your own target heart rate with
     Up/Down, then Mode.
4. The **Time** window blinks; program it and the rest of the data exactly as in any other program.

**The formula the console uses is 220 - age = maximum HR.** The manual's worked example: a 30 year
old has a maximum HR of **190 BPM** (220 - 30 = 190), so at 75% their target HR is **142 BPM**.

**These are not the figures the same manual's training-zone page gives.** That page recommends 60%
and 80% and works a 40 year old out to 108-144 beats per minute; this program offers 55%, 75% and
90%. The source does not reconcile them - see
`spirit-ce-programs-target-zone-of-108-to-144-beats`.

**The XE350 and XE550, in the same manual, have HR 1 and HR 2 keys set to 60% and 80% instead** - see
`spirit-ce-programs-heart-rate-control-operation`. **On the XE150 the heart rate chest strap
transmitter is optional**, where the XE350 and XE550 chapters do not say so.
