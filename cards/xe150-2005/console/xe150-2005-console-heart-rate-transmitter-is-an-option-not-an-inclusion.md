**The manual marks the transmitter as an option for this machine and this machine only.** In the
sentence that explains how the console picks up a pulse it writes:

> ... or wearing the heart rate transmitter (**optional on XE150**) ...

**So a chest strap is not in the box.** On the XE350 and XE550 in the same manual the same sentence
carries no such qualifier.

**The hand pulse sensors are fitted regardless.** Grasping the stainless sensors on the stationary
handlebars is the built-in way to read a pulse on all three machines, and the XE150's own console
section lists a **PULSE** window that "displays your current heart rate when you grasp the hand
sensors or wear the chest strap transmitter".

**The manual gives no part number for the optional transmitter**, and no dealer or ordering
instruction. It does describe how to wear one and warns about interference; that section is written
for the series as a whole.

**Do not read this onto the other two machines.** The parenthesis names the XE150 alone.
