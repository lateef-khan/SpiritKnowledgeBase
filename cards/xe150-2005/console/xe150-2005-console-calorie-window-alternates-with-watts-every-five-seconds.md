**Two of this console's windows share themselves between two readings.**

- **The Calorie window switches between Calories and Watts every 5 seconds.** Calories is a total and
  is approximate. **Watts indicates how much work you are doing** - if the Watt reading is 100, you
  are doing enough work to light a 100 watt light bulb.
- **The bottom left data window switches between RPM and Speed every 5 seconds.** **RPM is your
  actual pedal rotation speed**; the **Speed readout is in MPH and indicates approximate road speed
  as if pedalling a bicycle in 7th gear.**

**The other windows do not alternate.** Distance shows miles travelled, based on pedal speed. Pulse
shows beats per minute while you hold the hand sensors or wear a chest strap transmitter.

**The manual gives the interval as both five and six seconds.** The Quick Start page says the RPM and
Speed window switches **every 5 seconds**; the FUNCTIONS list four pages later says **"Automatically
toggles between SPEED and RPM every 6 seconds"**. Both are printed and **the manual never resolves
it**, so do not quote a fixed interval to a customer - say it alternates every few seconds.

**Watts can also be a target.** In the Manual mode the Watt icon is one of the values you can set,
and once it is set **the machine adjusts the workload to hold that wattage** - pedal faster and the
workload drops, pedal slower and it rises.

**The XE350 and XE550 in the same manual do not work like this.** Their readings are grouped into
four sets stepped by a Display button, not alternated in place:
`spirit-xe-console-display-button-steps-through-four-sets-of-readings`.
