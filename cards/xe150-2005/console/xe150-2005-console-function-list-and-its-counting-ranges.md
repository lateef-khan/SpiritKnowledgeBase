| Window | What it does |
|---|---|
| **TIME** | Counts **up from zero to 99:59**, or **down from a preset time to zero** |
| **SPEED / RPM** | Toggles automatically between the two. **Speed is in miles per hour (or kilometres per hour)** |
| **DISTANCE** | Counts **up from 0.00 to 99.99 miles**, or **down from a preset distance to zero** |
| **CALORIES** | Accumulates **from 0 to 9999** |
| **WATTS** | The amount of work being done, in Watts |
| **PULSE** | Your current heart rate, from the hand sensors or the chest strap transmitter |
| **LEVEL** | Shown next to the workout profile. **The level of work being done, from 1 to 16** |

**Levels 1 to 16 is the only resistance range printed anywhere in this manual**, and it is printed on
the XE150 pages. **Do not quote it for the XE350 or the XE550** - their console section gives no
range at all. The later XE ellipticals print 20 levels, and the XE795 prints 40.

**The calorie figure is explicitly not a measurement.** The list says so itself: "This is an
estimated number only to be used from workout to workout to compare your progress. Actual calorie
burn cannot be measured accurately on any consumer exercise equipment because every individual burns
calories at a different rate."

**The SPEED/RPM interval is given as six seconds here and five seconds on the Quick Start page.** See
`xe150-2005-console-calorie-window-alternates-with-watts-every-five-seconds`; the manual does not
resolve it.
