Six numbered items on a drawing headed "4.0 U-Upright bike", under **Parts and adjustments**:

1. Console
2. Hand pulse grips
3. Fore / aft seat adjustment
4. Vertical seat adjustment
5. Pedal
6. Leveling glides

And under **Optional parts (not shown)**: **2.1 Adjustable crank set**.

**This is the Dyaco "PT" edition of the 4.0U** (an undated user manual, February 2016 PDF). It names the levelling glides that the 2025 Spirit book's lettered diagram does not, and leaves out the tablet rack, water bottle holder, seat, crank arm and rear stabilizer that the Spirit diagram names. The optional adjustable crank set appears only here.
