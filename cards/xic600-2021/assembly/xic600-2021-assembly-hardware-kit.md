Steps four (pedals) and five (console and transmitter) have no hardware table.

## The handlebar bolt is not the one in the 2018 book

The 2018 printing of the same machine calls for **BOLT (88), 5/16" x 15mm** in step
three. **This is the only hardware change between the two printings** - every other row
above is identical, and the two assembly chapters otherwise match at 91% at word level.
5/16" x 3/4" is about 19mm, so the 2021 bolt is the longer of the two. See
[the 2018 card](../../xic600-2018/assembly/xic600-2018-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

Neither manual prints a parts list, so there is no exploded view to check the item
number against.
