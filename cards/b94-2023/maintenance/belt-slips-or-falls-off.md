**The belt slips when pedalling**

1. Take apart both left and right chain covers and crank arms.
2. Use an **11 mm open end wrench** to tighten the **1/4" x 8T nyloc nut** until a sound wave tension gauge reads **450 N**. Because this model is belt driven, the user's weight or the way it is ridden can make the belt slip. Under normal use it will not.
3. The drive belt is malleable and expendable. It is normal for it to loosen over time.

**The belt falls off when pedalling**

Follow section 9.8 to install the belt, then test by revolving forward and backward after adjustment. If the belt falls off, **adjust it one groove toward the direction it is falling** and test again. If it still falls off, the machine may have been dropped hard enough to deviate a part dimension: try replacing the drive pulley, the bearing housing or the flywheel. If the problem persists, the mainframe could be deformed and the whole unit should be replaced. That is unlikely, because all units are tested before shipping.
