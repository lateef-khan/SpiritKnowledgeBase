1. Use a **13/15 mm open end wrench** to take the pedals apart: turn the **left pedal clockwise** and the **right pedal counterclockwise**.
2. To refit the pedals, turn them in the reversed directions respectively.
3. Take the crank arm end cap apart and use a **plug wrench** to release the nut.
4. Apply the **crank tool** on the crank to hold it and use an **Allen wrench** to release the crank.
5. To refit the cranks (16L, 16R), use an air crank tool or a rubber hammer to fix the crank on the axle, tighten the nut on the crank axle and plug in the crank arm end cap.
