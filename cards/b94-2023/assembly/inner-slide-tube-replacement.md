1. Remove the **locking knob**.
2. Pull the seat slider and the seat post cover up. Take the centre spatial wrap apart from the main frame with a **rubber hammer**, then use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **2 pcs of 4 x 12 mm sheet metal screws**. Remove the slide spacer with the rubber hammer to take the seat slider and centre spatial wrap apart.
3. To refit, return the centre spatial wrap to the seat slider, return the slide spacer into the seat slider and tighten it with the two 4 x 12 mm sheet metal screws, then insert it into the main frame. Use the rubber hammer to return the centre spatial wrap and refit the locking knob.
