1. Follow section 9.7 to remove chain cover L.
2. Remove the steel cable from the flywheel. **Release the aluminium plate gently: it deforms easily and then causes noise.**
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **two M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screws** on the gear motor and take the gear motor apart.
4. Release the steel cable from the gear motor to take it apart.
5. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screw** and take the reed switch apart.
6. Use the console to set the moving range when installing the steel cable. After fitting the cable, drive the tension to maximum from the console, then adjust the steel cable so the exposed cable is tight. **At maximum tension the aluminium plate sits at its lowest position.** Use **two 8 mm open end wrenches** to tighten the adjusting screw when done.
