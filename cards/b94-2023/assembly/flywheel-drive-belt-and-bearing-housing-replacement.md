1. Follow section 9.7 to remove chain covers L and R.
2. Use an **11 mm open end wrench** to release the **1/4" x 8T nyloc nut** to the end, and a **6 mm Allen wrench** to remove the **M10 x P1.25 x 15L button head socket bolt**. Take the bearing housing apart.
3. Take the steel cable down off the flywheel. **Handle the aluminium sheet on the steel cable with care**: it is an aluminium sheet with a spring, and if it distorts it rubs and makes noise. The manual calls this a very important point.
4. Take the belt down from the drive pulley, then use a **15 mm wrench** to loosen **2 pcs of 3/8"-UNF26 nuts** on the flywheel. Take the flywheel and the belt apart.
5. To refit, secure the bearing housing on the main frame with the **M10 x P1.25 x 15L button head socket bolt**. Make sure the nut stopper is securely on the main frame.
6. Return the drive belt onto the flywheel and back to the main frame. Align the flywheel and the drive pulley in line. Turn the **3/8"-UNF26 x 4T nut** by the drive pulley outward until it touches the main frame, and turn the other **3/8"-UNF26 nut** inward. Use **15 mm and 17 mm open end wrenches** to tighten both. Do the same for the **3/8"-UNF26 x 3T nut** and the 3/8"-UNF26 nut on the other side.
7. Return the drive belt to the drive pulley and the flywheel. Turn the drive pulley to centre the belt. If it is not centred, loosen the 3/8"-UNF26 nut on the flywheel and adjust the nuts. Use an **11 mm open end wrench** to tighten the **1/4" x 8T nyloc nut** until the drive belt is tight with the bearing housing. Use a sound wave tension gauge to make sure the reading is **450 N**. Turn the drive pulley to confirm the belt stays centred, then return the steel cable to the flywheel.

The belt tension figure and the dangling "(Remark)" that follows it are covered in a separate card.
