1. Follow section 9.1 to remove the console.
2. Use a **5 mm L-Allen wrench** to remove **4 pcs of 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts**, **4 pcs of 5/16" x 18 mm x 1.5T flat washers** and **4 pcs of 5/16" x 1.5T split washers**, which secure the handlebar assembly (L/R).
3. Remove **four Ø3 x 10 mm tapping screws** to remove the handgrip side caps (top) and (bottom).
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **four 20 mm tapping screws** securing the hand pulse with cable assembly, separate the assemblies from each other and remove them.
5. Reverse the steps to refit.

The section heading names the console mast, but the steps stop at the handlebar and the hand pulse. The console mast itself comes off in section 9.3.
