**450 Newton.** The manual prints the same figure in both places, section 9.8 step 7 and the belt slipping answer in section 10.2.

Set it by tightening the **1/4" x 8T nyloc nut** with an **11 mm open end wrench** until a sound wave tension gauge reads 450 N.

**The manual has a dangling reference.** Step 7 reads "use sound wave tension gauge to make sure the reading is 450 N (Remark)", but no Remark paragraph follows. The B94 2016 manual, which is otherwise the same procedure, has that Remark and it says **a brand new drive belt must read 540 N**, because the belt is elastic and loses tension over time. The B94 2019 manual has the same dangling "(Remark)". Treat 540 N as the likely figure for a new belt on this machine, and confirm with the technical department.

The LCB and LCR bikes are set by frequency instead, 180 to 205 Hz. Do not carry that to this machine.
