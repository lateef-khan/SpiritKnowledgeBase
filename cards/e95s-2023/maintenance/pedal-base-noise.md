The manual calls the left and right pedal bases and pedal arms **the most important parts of this model** and says to pay attention to their maintenance.

1. If there is noise, check whether something is rubbing or something has dropped in, then check the **aluminum track**. If the track is damaged, replace it as soon as possible.
2. Check the **diameter 38 urethane slide wheel** for damage. If it is damaged, replace it directly.
3. Clean the aluminum track and the diameter 38 urethane slide wheel, and add some lubricant.
4. If it is loose, use an **11mm wrench** to adjust the diameter 38 urethane slide wheel on the adjustment wheel fixing plate until it is appropriately tight.
