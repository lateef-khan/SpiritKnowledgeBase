The manual names two general flywheel problems: **the adjusted resistance does not work**, and **noise**.

1. **Resistance does not change while the gear motor is working.** Check whether the **steel cable** is working with the flywheel or has gone loose.
2. **Noise while the flywheel spins.** Check whether the flywheel, the **diameter 330 drive pulley** and the idler wheel assembly are rubbing. Disassemble following the flywheel steps and reset them. If the noise is from the flywheel itself, replace it.
3. **Poly-V belt slipping.** Adjust the cap on the hook-type screw with a **#13 wrench**. Refer to the disassembly procedures for the adjustment.
4. **Poly-V belt worn or damaged.** Replace it with a new part.

Point 3 gives no frequency. The belt tension figure, **190Hz (+/- 10)**, is printed only in section 9.8 step 12 and has its own card.
