1. The incline motor, controller and gear motor are all electronic components. **Do not disassemble them if you are unfamiliar with these parts.**
2. When the incline motor is not functioning, check every wire for a secure connection, and check whether the **incline controller** and the **power adaptor** are damaged or broken. Replace damaged or broken parts.
3. **To return the incline motor, make sure it has been reset.** Turn the tubing **clockwise to the end**, then turn the same tubing **counterclockwise two and a half circles**, or set the centre between the two holes to **245 +/- 1 mm**.
4. If there is no resistance, first check whether the console is functioning normally. Next check the **gear motor cable**; if it is worn or damaged, remove the **steel cable**, use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **tapping screw 5 x 19** securing the gear motor cable, and replace the cable with a new one.

**The 245 +/- 1 mm figure is specific to this machine.** The 2023 incline ellipticals - E25, E35, E95 and E98 - use 206mm or 207mm with **one and a half** circles. Do not carry those figures onto this model.
