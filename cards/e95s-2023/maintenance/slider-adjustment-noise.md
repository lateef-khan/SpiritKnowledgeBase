The slider adjustment is **the most important part of the stride adjustment**.

1. Check whether the **diameter 40 adjustment transportation wheel** is damaged. If it is, replace it following the disassembly steps.
2. Check whether the **swing assembly (#16)** is defaced on the surface. Wipe it and apply some lubricant.
3. Adjust the wheelbase following the disassembly steps if there is a space between the two parts, and adjust it to the standard scales.
