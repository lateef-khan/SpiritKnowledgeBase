If the elliptical loses power or will not start, check the fuse **located on the motor controller**.

**DANGER: turn the power switch off and unplug the elliptical to reduce the risk of an electric shock.**

1. Remove the fuse holder.
2. Remove and replace the fuse on the holder.

The manual gives no fuse rating and no part number.
