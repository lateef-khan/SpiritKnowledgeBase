The matrix splits this into two rows.

**Both the incline adjustment switches, up and down, do not function**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The connector of INCLINE CABLE and CONSOLE not connected properly. | Connect the wires again. |
| The connector of INCLINE CABLE and INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/CABLE not connected properly. | Connect the wires again. |

**One of the incline adjustment switches, either up or down, does not function**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The connector of INCLINE CABLE or INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH CABLE got damage. | Replace buttons. Replace the cable. |
| Button of INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH is broken. | Replace buttons. Replace the cable. |

The second row's reason column repeats the damaged-connector line three times over. That is how the table is printed.

If the screen also shows an incline message, see the INCLINE ERR card.
