**190 Hz, plus or minus 10 Hz**, measured with a sonic device.

The manual gives a feel test as the equal alternative: tighten the **nylon nut M8 x 9T on the screw shackle** and flick the belt until it makes a **crisp sound**, or use the sonic device to measure 190Hz (+/- 10).

After tensioning, turn the crank arm assembly so the belt and flywheel rotate smoothly, then reassemble the other parts.
