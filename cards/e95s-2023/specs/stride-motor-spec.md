The manual calls this part the **incline motor**, and on this machine it sets the **stride length**.

It is a **115-volt AC motor**. It has four wires - red, black, white and green - plus one **3-pin position sensor cable**.

- AC voltage on the **red** wire (UP) makes the motor increase the setting.
- AC voltage on the **black** wire (DOWN) makes the motor decrease the setting.
- The **white** wire (COM) is neutral.
- The **green** wire is ground.

**The manual contradicts itself on what this motor does.** Section 3 Electrical Configurations says "User can control Stride length by console within the Controller." The General Information table under it describes the same motor as increasing and decreasing **the incline**. The wording is inherited from the incline machines; this machine has no incline.

Zeroing a replacement motor is a different figure from the incline models. See the controller and motor card.
