**Work voltage: DC 4.5~7.5V.** It controls resistance increases and decreases.

The gear motor pulls a **steel cable** that sets the magnetic flywheel's resistance. Removing the gear motor means taking the steel cable out first.

Engineer Mode's SERVICE page has a **Motor Test** entry for fine-tuning and lifespan testing of this motor.

**This manual prints no meter procedure for the gear motor** - no drive board output figures and no probe placement.
