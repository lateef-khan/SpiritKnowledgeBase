- Install the elliptical on a flat level surface with access to a **120-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet**, with only the elliptical plugged into the circuit.
- Do not use an extension cord unless it is **16 AWG or better with only one outlet on the end**. Do not disable the grounded plug with improper adapters or modify the cord outlet.
- **Never use a GFCI wall outlet with this elliptical.** As with any appliance with a large motor, the GFCI will trip often. Route the power cord away from any moving part, including the elevation mechanism and transport wheels.
- To reduce the risk of electric shock, disconnect the elliptical from the outlet before cleaning or service work.
- The product must be grounded. Improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric shock. The grounding instructions call it a **nominal 120-volt circuit**.
- A temporary adapter may be used on a 2-pole receptacle only until a properly grounded outlet is installed by a qualified electrician, and it must be held in place by a metal screw.

The 2019 elliptical manuals print **115-volt** in the same sentence. This manual prints 120-volt in both the safety and the grounding sections.
