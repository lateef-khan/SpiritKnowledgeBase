1. **Put the 2pcs Flat Washers (176) on the 2pcs Hex Head Bolts (137)** and hand-tighten them through the top of the Rear Stabilizer Tube (Main Frame) into the Rear Rail Assembly (2) using **Wrench (194)**.
2. **Put the 4pcs Curved Washers (187) on the 4pcs Hex Head Bolts (139)** and hand-tighten them through the front of the Rear Stabilizer Tube (Main Frame) into the Rear Rail Assembly (2) using **Wrench (194)**.
3. **Use the L Allen Wrench (193) to release 2pcs Socket Head Cap Bolts (145)** and take apart the side back. **Pierce the Computer Cable (68) from the bottom of the mast tube through it** and pull it out of the top. Secure with **4pcs Socket Head Cap Bolts (145)**, **4pcs Spring Washers (186)** and **4pcs Flat Washers (176)**.
4. **Install the Bottle Holder (206) on the Console Mast (12) with 2pcs Phillips Head Screws (150)** using driver (192). **The bottle holder must be assembled before console assembly.**
5. **Untie the Computer Cable (68); connect two Handpulse W/Cable Assembly (58 & 207) and the Handle Wire (Upper) Resistance/Incline (61, 62)** with the **Console Assembly (55)**. Place the Console on top of the Mast and tighten **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (150)** with driver (192).
