1. Remove the joint assembly following the slider adjustment disassembly steps.
2. Use a **no. 12 wrench** to remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** from the **B diameter 178 x 41L rotate axle** fixed on the swing assembly on the main frame.
3. Pull out the B diameter 178 x 41L rotate axle.
4. Use a **12mm wrench** to release the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **diameter 8.5 x 26 x 2.0T flat washer**, and remove the swing assembly and slider adjustment which are fixed at the adjusting lever on the moving range adjusting assembly.
5. Use the **12mm wrench** to release the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **diameter 8.5 x 26 x 2.0T flat washer** fixed at the adjusting lever on the slider adjustment, then remove the adjusting lever.
6. Use a round bar smaller than diameter 17 to knock the **J4FM-1719-09 bushing** out of the adjusting lever.
7. Use a **13mm wrench** to release the **M8 x 6.3T nut** fixed on the wheelbase and release the **M8 x 20L socket head cap bolt**. The swing assembly can then be pulled out.
8. Use a round bar smaller than diameter 17 to knock the **J4FM-1719-09 bushing** out of the swing assembly.
9. Reverse the procedures to return the parts.
10. When returning the swing assembly to the main frame, the **B diameter 178 x 41L rotate axle must be aligned with the flat portion of the notch on the U-seat**, then the screw locked.
11. When locking the **diameter 40 adjustment transportation wheel** back, the **5/16" x 7T nyloc nut must be in the same position as the crank** locked in the slider adjustment axle.
12. When every part is assembled, use a torque wrench to lock the **M8 x 20L socket head cap bolt to 150lbs** for the wheelbase, lock the **M8 x 6.3T nut** tight, and put the slider cover back on the slider adjustment.

The manual writes the torque as "150lbs" with no length unit, the same way it writes "550lb" for the bushing housing. Confirm the intended unit before torquing to a number.
