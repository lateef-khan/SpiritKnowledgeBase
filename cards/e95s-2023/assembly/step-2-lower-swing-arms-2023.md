1. Fit the **left and right Lower Handle Bars (13, 14)** together with **2pcs Wave Washers (204)** onto the left and right shafts of the mast tube, then tighten with **2pcs Hex Head Bolts (136)** and **2pcs Flat Washers (175)** using **Wrench (194)**.
2. **Untie the Rod end Bearing on the left Connecting Arm (8)** and pierce the **Hex Head Bolt (134)** through the rod end holes and rod end bearing, then tighten with the **Flat Washer (180)** and **Nylon Nut (168)** using **Wrench (194)** and **Wrench (195)**. Do the same for the right Connecting Arm (9) and right Lower Handle Bar (14).
