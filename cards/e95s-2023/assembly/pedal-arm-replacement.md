1. Remove side case (L) and (R) and pedal arm (L) and (R) following the disassembly steps.
2. Pull out the **diameter 17 x 34L rotate axle A** from the inside. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **diameter 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** from the outside of rotate axle A for slider adjustment (L) and (R) of the joint assembly with a **12mm wrench**.
3. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **diameter 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** from the crank arm assembly for the bushing housing and pedal arm with a **12mm wrench**.
4. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **diameter 8.5 x 26 x 2.0T flat washer** from the rear rail assembly for the joint assembly with a **12mm wrench**.
5. To remove the **J4FM-1719-09 bushing** from the joint assembly, use a pole smaller than diameter 17 and hit the bushing out.
6. Use a **#8 hex wrench and a #14 wrench** to remove the **gap socket screw 3/8" x 2-1/4"**, **flat washer 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T** and **nyloc nut 3/8" x 11T** connecting the inclinable rail assembly and the rotating block, to remove the inclinable rail assembly.
7. To remove the **WFM-2528-21 bushing** from the bushing housing and pedal arm, use a pole smaller than diameter 25 and hit the bushing out.
8. To remove the **WFM-1719-12 bushing** from the rear rail assembly, use a pole smaller than diameter 17 and hit the bushing out.
9. Remove the two **M5 x 15L Phillips head screws** from the slide wheel cover with a Phillips head screwdriver when taking apart the **diameter 78 urethane slide wheel**.
10. Remove the **diameter 17 C ring** and **diameter 17 x 0.5T wave washer** from pedal arm (L) and (R) of the diameter 78 urethane slide wheel using the C ring pliers.
11. Reverse the above procedures to resume the pedal arm.
12. When assembling the diameter 78 urethane slide wheel, follow this order: **wave washer, slide wheel, wave washer, C ring**.
13. Line up the wheelbase with the surface of the diameter 17 x 34L rotate axle A when assembling the joint assembly and adjusting lever, and lock it with the screw.
14. **Lock the bushing housing and pedal arm onto pedal arm (L) and (R) using a torque wrench, up to 550lb.**

The manual writes that torque as "550lb" with no length unit. It prints the slider adjustment torque the same way, as "150lbs". Confirm the intended unit before torquing to a number.
