1. Follow section 9.1 to remove the seat handlebar and lower handle mast assembly.
2. Remove the two **diameter 4 x 15L sheet metal screws** of the console mast cover with a Phillips head screwdriver.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **eight sheet metal screws 4 x 15L** and remove the left chain cover.
4. Unscrew side case (R) from the main frame with a Phillips head screwdriver.
5. Remove the **5 x 19L tapping screw** on side case (L) of the main frame with a Phillips head screwdriver.
6. **Remember the position of the AC input module and the colour of the cables.** Remove the two connecting wires and the ground wire, and the side case (L) can come off.
7. Remove the **5 x 19L tapping screw** on the side case rear shroud of the main frame with a Phillips head screwdriver.
8. Reassemble following the steps above in reverse. Do not clip the cables. Fit side case (R) and (L) and the side case rear shroud, and lock the screws.

Step 1 calls the part the "seat handlebar" although this machine has no seat, and step 6 prints its final clause twice. That is how the manual is written.
