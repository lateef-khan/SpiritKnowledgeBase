1. Remove the steel cable from the magnetron flywheel following the gear motor disassembly steps.
2. Use a **13mm wrench** to release the **nylon nut M8 x 7T** fixed at the idler wheel assembly on the main frame.
3. Use a **13mm wrench** to release the adjust screw, **nylon nut M8 x 9T**, rod end sleeve and **flat washer 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T** fixed on the idler wheel assembly.
4. Pull the drive belt off the drive pulley.
5. Release the **nylon nut M8 x 7T**, **flat washer 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T** and **carriage bolt M8 x 20L** fixed on the idler wheel assembly, then remove the idler wheel assembly.
6. To remove the idler wheel **bearing 6203**, use C clip pliers to release the **C ring diameter 17** fixed on the idler wheel assembly.
7. Use a **15mm wrench** to release the **flange nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 11T** fixed on the magnetic flywheel.
8. Remove the magnetic flywheel together with the belt from the oblique side of the main frame.
9. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
10. When reloading the magnetic flywheel and belt, and when reversing the drive pulley, **the belt and drive pulley must stay in the middle position**.
11. If the belt is biased to one side, release the **flange nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 11T** and use a **17mm wrench** to adjust the nut fixed on the inner side of the magnetic flywheel. The belt and drive pulley must stay in the middle position, then fasten the flange nut again.
12. Use the **nylon nut M8 x 9T on the screw shackle** to tighten up, and flick the belt for a **crisp sound**, or use a sonic device measured at **190Hz (+/- 10)**. Then turn the crank arm assembly so the belt and flywheel rotate smoothly, and reassemble the other parts.
