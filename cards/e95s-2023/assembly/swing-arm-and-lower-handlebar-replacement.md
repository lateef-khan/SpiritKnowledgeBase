1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **sheet metal screws 3.5x12mm** securing the swing arm covers, front and back.
2. Remove the **3/8" x 2-1/4" socket head cap bolt (#137)**, the **diameter 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washer (#176)** and the **3/8" x 7T nyloc nut (#165)** that fix the upper handlebar to the lower handlebar, using **two 14mm wrenches**.
3. Disconnect the wiring connecting the upper and lower swing arms.
4. When the keys on the handrails are not functioning, first remove the stickers, then use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **M5x20mm flat head socket screws** securing the keys to the swing arm.
5. Separate the cables of the resistance button and the **450mm handle wire (upper)** and get the handle switch.
6. Use a **14mm wrench** to release the **3/8" x 3/4" hex head bolt** together with the **3/8" x 30 x 1.5T flat washer** which secure the swing arms.
7. Release the **M5 x 15L Phillips head screws** of connecting arm cover A and connecting arm cover B with a Phillips screwdriver, and remove both covers.
8. Use **#12 and #13 open end wrenches** to hold the swing arm assembly and remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 1-1/4"**, **flat washer 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T** and **nyloc nut 5/16" x 7T**. Remove the swing arm assembly.
9. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
