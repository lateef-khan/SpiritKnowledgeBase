1. First remove the pedal bar assembly, then use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **Phillips head screws M5x10mm** securing the cover to the pedal arm joint, and remove the pedal arm joint cover.
2. Pull out the end of the slider cover by hand.
3. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **diameter 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T curved washer** of the connecting component with a **12mm wrench**. Separate the connecting component and the swing assembly.
4. Pull out pedal base (L) and (R) to the back and separate them from connecting arm (L) and (R).
5. Release the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **diameter 8.5 x 26 x 2.0T flat washer** of the connecting component with a **12mm wrench**.
6. Use a **12mm open end wrench** to remove the **hex socket screw 5/16" x 1/2"** and **flat washer 8.5 x 26 x 1.5T**. Pull out the pedal carriage bolts to remove the pedal bar assembly.
7. Release the **5/16" x 15L hex head bolt** of the pedal arms with a **12mm wrench**. This frees the **diameter 38 urethane slide wheel**.
8. Remove adjustment wheel fixing plate (L) and (R) with the **12mm wrench** and release the **5/16" x 15L hex head bolt** and **diameter 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** from the left and right pedal base.
9. Assemble again following the steps above.
10. **Lock the diameter 8 x 16 x 2T flat washers in the middle of the urethane slide wheel and adjustment wheel fixing plate (L) and (R)** when assembling the diameter 38 urethane slide wheel.
11. Adjust the **diameter 38 urethane slide wheel** and the aluminum track with an **11mm wrench** and the **1/4" x 1/2" hex head bolt** while locking the left and right pedal base back onto pedal arm (L) and (R).

Steps 5 and 6 give two different flat washer thicknesses for the same connecting component, **2.0T** and **1.5T**. Both are printed.
