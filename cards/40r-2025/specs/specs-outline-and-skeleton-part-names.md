**Outlines (2-1)** - a line drawing of the assembled recumbent with fifteen call-outs:
Console, Console Mast Cover, Handle, Seat, Hand Pulse Sensor, Seatback, Cup Holder (L), Cup Holder (R),
Aluminum Rail, Rear Chain Cover (L), Rear Chain Cover (R), Rear Stabilizer Cover, Bottom Cover,
Front Stabilizer Cover, Front Chain Cover (L), Front Chain Cover (R), Pedal.

**Skeleton (2-2)** - the frame with the covers off, thirteen call-outs:
Mast Handle bar Assembly (called out twice, once at each grip), Seat Carriage, Seat Handle Bar, Seat
Back Frame, Aluminum Axle, **CONTROLLER** (under the seat carriage), Drive Pulley, Crank Arm, Idler
Wheel Assembly, **GENERATOR**, Main Frame, Rear Stabilizer.

The two drawings are the only place the book names the covers; the parts list numbers them (Front
Shroud (L)/(R) 29-30, Rear Shroud (L)/(R) 35-36, Console Mast Cover 31) under different names - the
outline says *Chain Cover* where the list says *Shroud*. The controller sits in the seat base and the
generator (the induction brake) inside the front shrouds, which is why the replacement chapter takes
the rear shrouds off to reach one and the front shrouds off to reach the other.

The upright 4.0U's drawings name a different set of parts
(`40u-2025-specs-outline-and-skeleton-part-names`).
