**Two bolts hold the seat back frame here; the CR800 books print six of the same bolt.** The owner's manual's step two also fits this frame with two bolts (119, 3/8" x 1-3/4") and two nuts, so two is this machine's figure.

1. Use a **14 mm open wrench** to unscrew **2 pcs of 3/8" x 3/4" hex head bolts** and **2 pcs of 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washers** and take the **seat back frame** apart.
2. Remove the **seat back cover** first, then use an **M5 Allen wrench** to release the **M8 x 15 mm button head socket bolt** and take the **seatback cushion** apart.
3. Reverse the steps to refit.

**No screws are printed for the seat back cover** - step 2 says only "remove the seat back cover first". The CR800 books release four M5 x 15 screws for theirs. The parts list carries a **Seat Back Cover (128)**, a **Seat Back (63)** and **four M8 x 15 mm button head socket bolts (179)**, where the step names one.

**The two books do not agree on the frame bolt.** This procedure prints 3/8" x 3/4" hex head bolts with flat washers and no nuts; the owner's manual's step two fits the same seat back frame to the seat carriage with two 3/8" x 1-3/4" hex head bolts (119), two flat washers (84) and two nyloc nuts (89). Neither book explains the other; check the bolt in the frame before ordering.
