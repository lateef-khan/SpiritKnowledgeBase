**Step 3 refits the halves with six 3.5 x 16 screws and four 5 x 16 screws; step 4 of the same procedure refits them with seven and two.** Both counts are printed on the facing pages. Seven and two is what comes off in step 1, and the parts list carries fourteen Ø3.5 x 16 sheet metal screws (103) and eight Ø5 x 16 tapping screws (101) for the front and rear covers together.

1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet metal screws** and **2 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws** on the **left rear shroud**. Unplug the **300 mm hand pulse wire, coiled**, the **hand pulse wire** and the **switch cable (upper)**, and the left rear shroud can be taken apart.
2. Release the **Ø3.5 x 20 sheet metal screw** with its **3/16" x 15 x 1.5T flat washer** that secures the **right rear shroud**, then release **2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws** on the right rear shroud, and take it off.
3. To refit, place a **5/16" x 16 x 1T flat washer** on the plastic column of the rear shroud (R), pass the column through its hole into the main frame and fix it lightly with **one Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet metal screw** and the 3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T flat washer; join the left and right rear shrouds and lock them with **6 pcs of Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet metal screws**; then adjust the bottom cover position and fix it to the main frame with **4 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws**.
4. Combine the rear shroud (L) and rear shroud (R) together and secure with **7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet metal screws** and **2 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws**.

**Three cables come off the left shroud**, as on the XBR recumbents (which name them the 300 mm coiled hand-pulse wire, the 2100 mm coiled hand-pulse wire and the 2100 mm upper switch cable); the 2009 and 2020 CR800 books unplug the hand-pulse wire only. Step 3 is the XBR refit wording, washer (127) included; step 4 is the CR800 wording. The book does not say which to follow. The screw on the right-hand frame post is Ø3.5 x 20 with a 3/16" washer, as the 2009 XR898 prints it.
