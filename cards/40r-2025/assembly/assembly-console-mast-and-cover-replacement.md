**The handlebar comes off two M8 x 25 mm socket head cap bolts with an Allen wrench, not the two 5/16" hex head bolts and 12/14 mm wrench of the CR800 and XBR recumbents.** The mast bolts below them are the same six as on those machines.

1. Take the console apart first.
2. Use an **Allen wrench** to release **2 pcs of M8 x 25 mm socket head cap bolts**, **2 pcs of split washers** and **2 pcs of flat washers**, and the **handlebar assembly** can be released.
3. Reverse the step to refit the handlebar.
4. Separate the **console mast cover** from the **front shrouds (L, R)** at the left and right sides of the seam with a fingernail or something flat, release the latches that lock the cover and the shrouds together, and pull the console mast cover up.
5. Unscrew **6 pcs of 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts**, **4 pcs of 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T flat washers** and **2 pcs of 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T curved washers** to pull the **console mast** out. The book says to do this "with the Allen wrench"; hex head bolts take a wrench, and the owner's manual fits the same six bolts (68) with the wrenches in the carton.
6. To refit the console mast, guide the **computer cable** and the **hand pulse sensor assembly w/cable** through the console mast and out of the console securing plate, then tighten the 6 pcs of 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts, **6 pcs of 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T flat washers** and 2 pcs of 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T curved washers with the Allen wrench.

**Four flat washers come off in step 5 and six go back in step 6**, exactly as the CR800 books print it.

The owner's manual's step three fits the same handlebar with **two socket head cap bolts (113), M8 x 25 mm**, two split washers (82) and two flat washers (76), which agrees with step 2 here. No torque is printed anywhere.
