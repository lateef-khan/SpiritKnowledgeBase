**This book prints a 14/15 mm wrench for the pedals and a 14 mm T wrench for an M10 x 1.25 crank nut.** The 2020 CR800 books print a 13/15 mm wrench and an M10 x 1.0 nut, the 2009 XR898 a 13/15 mm wrench and M10 x 1.25, the XBR books no nut size at all; the 4.0U upright prints a 13/15 mm wrench too. Read the nut before ordering.

1. Use the **14/15 mm wrench** to turn the **left pedal clockwise** and the **right pedal counterclockwise** to remove both pedals.
2. Turn the pedals the opposite way to refit them.
3. Take off the **crank arm end cap** and release the **M10 x 1.25 nut** with the **14 mm T wrench**.
4. Use the plug tool that matches the cranks and turn a hex wrench through it to release the cranks.
5. To refit the cranks, use a hammer to seat the cranks tightly on the **drive pulley axle**, turn the nut tightly onto the axle, and plug in the crank arm end cap.

**The left pedal is left-hand threaded** - it comes off clockwise. No torque is printed for the pedals or the nut; the owner's manual says only to tighten the pedals as much as you possibly can.

The parts list of the same book carries the nut as **item 108, M10 x P1.25 x 10T** (2 pcs), a **14/15 mm wrench (132)** and a **12/14 mm wrench (112)** - both among the tools the owner's manual packs.
