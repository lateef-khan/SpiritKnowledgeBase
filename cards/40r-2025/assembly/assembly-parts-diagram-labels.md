Ten letters on a line drawing headed PARTS OF YOUR RECUMBENT BIKE:

| Letter | Part |
|---|---|
| A | Console |
| B | Tablet Rack |
| C | Pulse Grips |
| D | Console Upright |
| E | Seat |
| F | Side Rail |
| G | Cup Holders |
| H | Crank Arm |
| I | Rear Stabilizer |
| J | Pedal |

**It names a tablet rack, a console upright and a side rail** - words the assembly steps do not use: the steps call the upright the console mast (2), and the side rail is the aluminium track of the seat carriage. It does not label the levelling glides, the seat back, the gas shock or the front handlebars.

**The Dyaco PT edition of the same bike labels a different, numbered set** of seven parts and adjustments; that is its own card. The CR800 diagram names thirteen parts in words, with no letters.
