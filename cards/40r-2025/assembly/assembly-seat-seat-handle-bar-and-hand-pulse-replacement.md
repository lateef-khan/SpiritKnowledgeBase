**The seat screws are M6 x 18 mm here, not the M6 x 15 mm the XR898 and XBR recumbents print.** Everything below the seat matches the XR898 / XBR version: two long bolts, four 3/8" x 2" bolts, six nyloc nuts and six washers.

1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **four M6 x 18 mm Phillips head screws** and take the **seat** apart.
2. Use **two 14 mm open-end wrenches** to release **two 3/8" x 2-3/4" hex head bolts**, **four 3/8" x 2" hex head bolts**, **six 3/8" x 7T nyloc nuts** and **six 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washers**, which secure the **seat carriage**, and take the **seat handle bar** apart.
3. Take apart the **HGP wire grommet** that secures the hand-pulse assemblies. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to release **four Ø3 x 20 mm tapping screws** and take the hand-pulse assemblies apart.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.

The owner's manual fits the same seat cushion (61) with **four screws (186), M6 x 18 mm**, and its step one puts the same two bolts (175) and four bolts (71) through the seat carriage, so the service manual and the build agree. The 2020 CR800 books print the four shorter bolts as 3/8" x 1-3/4"; that is the other linked card.

The seat handle bar's bolts run through the seat carriage, which stays on its track.
