The dot matrix display shows graphic feedback and has **three basic displays** for
most programs.

- When you begin a program it shows a **speed profile**.
- **To the left of the dot matrix there is a key labeled display.** Pressing it
  switches to an **incline grade**, and then to a **track**.
- **When the LEDs are blinking the graph scans through the three displays.**

**The dot matrix has 24 columns of lights.** In the Quick start program **each
column represents 1 minute** - the time per column can be modified in other
programs. **At the end of the 24th column (24 minutes of work) the display wraps
around and restarts at the first column.**

**Both MT200 manuals print this word for word** - the 2010 manual on its p. 13, the
2022 manual on its pp. 22-23.
