Calibration sits under Service Mode. `MW` is the message window.

1. MW shows **START LEFT SIDE**.
2. When **Start** is pressed the MW shows **`AD _ _ _  TGT 65`**.
3. **The speaker beeps slowly when the magnet distance is too far, quickly when
   near, and steady when correct.**
4. **Press enter to continue.**
5. MW shows **START RIGHT SIDE**.
6. When **Start** is pressed the MW shows **`AD _ _ _  TGT 65`**.
7. The same beep pattern applies: slow when too far, quick when near, steady when
   correct.
8. **Press enter or stop to end calibration.**

**This calibrates the step sensors, not the grade.** Grade calibration is the last
step of the Factory settings sequence; see `70t-2026-console-factory-settings`.

**Both MT200 manuals print this routine word for word**, including the `TGT 65`
target - the 2010 manual on its p. 40, the 2022 manual on its p. 63.
