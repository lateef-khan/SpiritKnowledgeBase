Under Service Mode, **MW shows "SECURITY"**, then **"CHILD LOCK ON" or "OFF"**.
This sets the Child Lock function.

**The function locks out the keypad until a pre-determined key sequence is
pressed.**

**Unlock sequence: hold Start and Enter down together until unlocked.**

A console that will not respond to any key is the first thing to check against
this setting.

**Both MT200 manuals print the same Security item and the same unlock sequence** -
the 2010 manual on its p. 40, the 2022 manual on its p. 63.

**The MT200-2022 service manual prints the same Security item and the same Start and Enter unlock.**

**The 2025 printing of the 7.0T owner's manual (Revision 01.10.25, warranty effective October 23, 2024) prints this section word for word on the same page**, so this card covers the 70t-2025 as well.
