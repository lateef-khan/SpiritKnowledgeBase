**These machines take a 10mm Allen wrench. The 8.0T takes an 8mm one for the same job, and the
CT850 2016 gives no wrench size and a 1/4 turn rather than 1/4 to 1/2.**

1. Adjustment must be made from the **rear roller**. The adjustment bolts are at the end of the
   step rails, in the end caps. *Adjustment is through a small hole in the end cap.*
2. Tighten the rear roller bolts only enough to prevent slippage at the front roller.
3. Turn **both** tread-belt tension adjustment bolts with a **10mm Allen wrench** in increments of
   **1/4 to 1/2 turn each**.
4. Inspect for proper tension by walking on the belt at a low speed, making sure the belt does not
   slip.
5. Keep tensioning the bolts until the belt stops slipping.

**Do not overtighten.** Over tightening will cause belt damage and premature bearing failure.

If you feel the belt is tight enough but it still slips, the problem may be a **loose motor drive
belt** under the front motor cover.

The same bolts set tracking — `70t-2026-maintenance-treadbelt-tracking-adjustment`.

Damage caused by a bad tension adjustment is not a warranty item —
`spirit-2026t-warranty-belt-adjustment-damage`.

## The two MT200 manuals print the same procedure

The MT200 2010 and MT200 2022 owner's manuals print these steps in the same words: the same
**10mm** wrench, the same **1/4 to 1/2 turn**, the same *"Adjustment is through small hole in the
end cap"* note, the same **do not overtighten** warning and the same loose-motor-drive-belt
caveat. **Neither MT200 manual carries the duplicated block described next** — that damage is in
the 7.0T manual only.

## A second block on the same page prints different figures

Page 41 carries a duplicated "GENERAL MAINTENANCE" block over the manual's own column. That block
says "in increments of 1/4 turn each" and names no wrench. The figures above are the ones in the
7.0T's own text.
