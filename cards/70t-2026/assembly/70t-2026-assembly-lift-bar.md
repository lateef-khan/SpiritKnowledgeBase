The lift bar makes raising and lowering the handrails easier. It is fitted in
assembly step four: the Lift Bars (192) go onto the parallel bars and are
secured with the knobs (207). Both MT200 years use those same two numbers.

**You can remove the lift bars for better patient access** by loosening the
locking knobs. **Hold the bar with one hand and loosen the knobs with the
other** so the lift bar does not suddenly fall to the floor.

All three manuals print this paragraph word for word. The MT200 parts lists name
item 192 a **P.Bar Lift Assist Handle** and item 207 a **Brake Tension Knob**;
the assembly step calls them the lift bar and the knobs. They are the same two
parts under two names.

The 8.0T has no lift bar. Its handrails are raised and lowered by hand on
quick-release levers.
