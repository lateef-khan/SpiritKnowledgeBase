## To lift

1. Turn both the left and right vertical adjustment knobs **counterclockwise one
   or two rotations**.
2. Grab the lift bar and pull up. The locking pins in the knobs will
   automatically ratchet into the indexing holes in the tubes.
3. Tighten the knobs when the desired position is set.

There is a **numbered scale on the tubes** for repeatable settings.

## To lower

1. Loosen the two knobs, then **pull them out and rotate slightly until the knob
   remains out on its own**. The pin should now be disengaged from the tube.
2. Use the lift bar to lower the rails. **Lower the rail past the position you
   desire**, then rotate the knob again so the pin can engage the holes in the
   tube.
3. Now lift the rails to the desired position and lock the knob.

The 7.0T and both MT200 years print this procedure word for word.

**This is the knob-and-pin handrail, not the quick-release one.** The 8.0T
adjusts height with a quick-release lever, has no ratcheting pin and no lift
bar, and its procedure does not include the lower-past-and-come-back-up move.
The horizontal adjustment is the same on all of these machines and is held on
its own card.
