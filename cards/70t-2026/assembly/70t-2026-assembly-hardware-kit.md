The hardware is photographed on one page, bagged by step. The panel gives the
item number, the size and the count; it gives no part-type words such as "bolt"
or "washer", so the types below are only what the assembly steps call them.

## Step one

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 104 | 3/8" x 3-3/4" | 6 |
| 58 | Ø10 x 2T | 6 |
| 59 | 3/8" x 25 x 2T | 6 |

## Step two

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 153 | 3/8" x 3/4" | 6 |
| 58 | Ø10 x 2T | 6 |
| 59 | 3/8" x 25 x 2T | 6 |
| 108 | M8 x 12L | 6 |
| 73 | M5 x 12L | 4 |

## Step three

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 110 | M5 x 10L | 2 |
| 112 | M5 x 30L | 2 |

## Step four

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 154 | 3/8" x 3/4" | 8 |
| 155 | 3/8" x 25 x 2T | 8 |
| 226 | 3/8" x 2" | 8 |

Items **58 and 59 are bagged twice**, six for step one and six for step two, so
the carton holds twelve of each.

Steps two and four both use a 3/8" x 3/4" bolt but they are **different item
numbers**, 153 and 154, and the counts differ - six against eight.

## Numbers that mean something else on a sibling machine

- **137** is the 5mm Allen wrench here. On the 4.0T it is a Bolt, M8 x 35mm.
- **207** is a handrail lift-bar knob here. On the 8.0T it is a Bolt, M10 x 20L.
- **259** is the 6mm Allen wrench here. On the 8.0T the number 259 labels a
  console connector.
- **3, 4 and 5** are the two console mounting brackets and the console frame
  here. On the 4.0T, 3 is the Lower Console Assembly, 4 the Console Frame and 5
  the Handle Bar Frame.

The handrail hardware does the same job on the 8.0T under three different
numbers: its 213, 215 and 214 stand where this machine's 154, 155 and 226 do,
at the same three sizes.
