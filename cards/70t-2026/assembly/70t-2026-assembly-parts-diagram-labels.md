| Label | Part |
|---|---|
| A | Hand grips |
| B | Electronic console |
| C | Safety Key |
| D | Adjustable handrails |
| E | Handrail horizontal adjustment |
| F | Handrail vertical adjustment |
| G | Handrail lift bar |
| H | Rear step |
| I | Rear incline |
| J | Front incline |

**These letters are this machine's letters only.** The 4.0T diagram runs A to J
as well and means different parts by them - its C is the safety tether cord
placement and its D is the water bottle holder. The 8.0T runs A to Q.

There is no water bottle holder on this diagram. The 8.0T diagram has one; this
machine's does not.
