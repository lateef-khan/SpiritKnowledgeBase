The A.C. mains input connector is **in the front of the machine**. The input
module holds three things:

- an input connector for the line cord,
- a power switch,
- a **20 amp circuit breaker**.

**Turn the power switch to off when the machine is not in use.**

**The breaker rating is 20 amp on these machines and 15 amp on the 8.0T**, whose
Plugging in paragraph is otherwise word for word the same. Check the machine in
front of you before quoting a rating.

The MT200 manuals of both years print this same paragraph under the heading
"Connecting to A.C. power", naming the machine rather than saying "the unit".
The 2022 manual's copy is broken mid-sentence by the page layout and loses the
words "switch and a 20 amp circuit breaker. Turn the power switch to off"; the
2010 manual prints the sentence whole, and that is the wording above.

Neither manual prints anything here about what the console shows at power-up.
That sequence is not in this section.

The **2025 printing** of this book (Revision 01.10.25, `spirit-treadmill-70t-2025-owners-manual`) prints this page word for word; its assembly chapter is 98.2% the 2026 chapter at word level, the difference being two item numbers in step three.
