The Readouts line of the specification page names eleven values:

Time, Grade, Distance, Speed, Pulse, METs, Calories, Pace, Cadence, Left and
Right Step Length, Symmetry Index.

This is the spec sheet's list of what the machine measures. How each one is
shown, paged through or reset is a console question and the console section of
the manual covers it.

**Left and Right Step Length and Symmetry Index are the two that mark this
machine out** - they are what the Symmetry program measures. The 8.0T's
specification page carries no Readouts line at all, so this list must not be
quoted for it.
