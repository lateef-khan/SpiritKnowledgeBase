**Certifications, exactly as printed.**

- CAN/CSA -C22.2 No. 60601-1:14
- ANS/AAMI ES60601-1:2005+A2 (R2012) +A1
- IEC 60601-1-2:2014
- EN 60601-1-2:2015
- IEC 60601-1:2005+A1:2012
- EN 60601-1:2006+A1:2013+A12:2014
- EN ISO 20957:2013
- ISO 14971; 2019

The page really does print "ANS/AAMI" rather than ANSI/AAMI, and a semicolon in
"ISO 14971; 2019". Both are reproduced here as the manual has them.

**No EU MDR entry appears in this list.** The 8.0T's list does carry EU MDR
2017/745 Class I, along with EN ISO 20957-1 and EN ISO 20957-6 and later
amendment years throughout. The two lists are not interchangeable; see
`80t-2026-specs-certifications`.

**Disposal.** "Reference should be made to local regulations concerning the
disposal of this product at the end of useful life." The manual gives no take-back
scheme, no WEEE marking and no dismantling instruction.

**The 2025 owner's manual prints the identical block** - the same eight standards, the same
"ANS/AAMI" and "ISO 14971; 2019" spellings, no EU MDR entry - and the same Disposal sentence.
