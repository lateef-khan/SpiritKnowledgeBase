> 14. Ensure there is a minimum space on the sides of the 7.0T of two feet for proper operation,
>     easy access and to prevent possible injuries to others standing or walking nearby. There
>     should be a minimum of at least one foot of free space at the front and three and a half
>     feet at the rear.

| Side | Minimum free space |
|---|---|
| Each side | two feet |
| Front | one foot |
| Rear | three and a half feet |

The reason the manual gives for the side figure is other people: proper operation, easy access,
and preventing injuries to anyone standing or walking nearby.

**The 8.0T's rear figure is nearly twice this.** The same numbered instruction in the 8.0T manual
asks for **six feet, six inches** at the rear, with the same two feet at the sides and one foot at
the front: `80t-2026-safety-clearance-around-the-treadmill`. The two machines are not
interchangeable on this figure - do not quote six feet six for a 7.0T, or three and a half feet
for an 8.0T.

**The 4.0T manual prints no clearance distances at all** (`40t-2026-safety-placement-and-floor`),
and the XT and CT900 families print different figures again
(`xt-2023-safety-placement-and-clearance`, `ct900ent-clearance-requirements`).

**Both Spirit MT200 owner's manuals ask for the same three figures**, in the same sentence, with
`treadmill` in place of the machine's name:

> Ensure there is a minimum space on the sides of the treadmill of two feet for proper operation,
> easy access and to prevent possible injuries to others standing or walking nearby. There should
> be a minimum of at least one foot of free space at the front and three and a half feet at the
> rear.

Two feet each side, one foot at the front, three and a half feet at the rear - the 7.0T's figures,
not the 8.0T's, and the same reason is given for the side figure. **The MT200 manuals print no
other clearance figure**, and say nothing about carpet, furniture or keeping children and pets out
of the area.

**The 2025 printing of the 7.0T manual prints this page word for word**, so it holds for the 70t-2025 as well. The January 2025 book (revision 01.10.25, version 2.0) was compared with the 2026 book page by page; the only safety-page changes between them are the outlet voltage and plug line (`70t-2025-safety-outlet-115-volt-20-amp-and-a-nema-5-15p-line`), a fuller operator profile, and the contact site on the application page, which the 2025 book gives as `hands, feet, and trunk` where the 2026 book says `hands and feet`.
