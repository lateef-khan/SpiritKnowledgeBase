**This is the 7.0T E8 (Over Torque) - not E33 (Over Trque), not E7 (Thrm Ovrload), not the CT900 E8 (Drive HW ERR) and not the Sole E8 (controller EEPROM fault).**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Code | E8 |
| Name, as printed | Over Torque |

**The causes and remedies the table lists under it, in printed order:**

1. Brake locked
2. Deck Lube
3. Bad Drive
4. Bad Motor

The table prints nothing else for this code - no description, no test procedure and no part number. The wording above is the manual's own, abbreviated as it prints it.
