**This is the 7.0T E1 (Over Current) - not E9 (Over I Speed), not E10 (Over I Accel), not the CT900 E1 (Over Current), not Spirit CVC800 E-1 (display-board RAM), not Spirit XT E1 (motor not responsive after Start), not Sole E01 (over current protection) and not the Sole E1 (no speed signal).**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Code | E1 |
| Name, as printed | Over Current |

**The causes and remedies the table lists under it, in printed order:**

1. Deck Lube
2. Bad Drive or Motor
3. Check Brake

The table prints nothing else for this code - no description, no test procedure and no part number. The wording above is the manual's own, abbreviated as it prints it.
