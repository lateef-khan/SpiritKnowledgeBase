**This is the 7.0T E5 (IGBT Fault) - not E18 (IGBT O-Heat), not E4 (Ground Fault), not the CT900 E5 (Thermal Overload), not Spirit CE800ENT E5 (console to controller communication) and not the Sole E5 (console and controller not talking).**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Code | E5 |
| Name, as printed | IGBT Fault |

**The causes and remedies the table lists under it, in printed order:**

1. Check wiring
2. Replace Drive

The table prints nothing else for this code - no description, no test procedure and no part number. The wording above is the manual's own, abbreviated as it prints it.
