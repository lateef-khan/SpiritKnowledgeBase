**The 7.0T offers one fitness test only.** Its Vo2 test is the **Gerkin protocol** - also known as the
fireman's protocol - a sub-max Vo2 (volume of oxygen) test. There is no menu of military or Chester
tests on this console; the 4.0T offers nine tests and the 8.0T ten.

**Fitness test programming**
1. Press the **Vo2** key and press **enter**.
2. The message window asks for your **age**, shown in the Incline window. Adjust with the up and down
   keys, then press enter.
3. Enter your **weight**, shown in the distance window, then press enter.
4. Press **start** to begin the test.

Gender and height are not asked for. The test increases speed and elevation alternately until **85% of
Max heart rate** is attained; the time taken to reach 85% determines the score. The stage chart and the
before/during/after guidance are on separate cards.

Both MT200 owner's manuals print the same four setup steps and the same 85% rule, and offer the
**same single fitness test** - the 2010-generation manual on printed pages 19-20, the
2022-generation manual on printed pages 31-32. Neither asks for gender or height either.

**The 7.0T 2025 owner's manual (Revision 01.10.25) prints this page word for word** as the 2026 book does; only the folio differs.
