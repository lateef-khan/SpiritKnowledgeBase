If the incline motor does not function, first check the proper connection of the cables, then check the incline controller, the transformer and the console for damage. Replace the part which is damaged.

That is the whole of section 9-8 in this manual. The detailed nine-step electrical test, the LED and relay checks and the potentiometer voltages are in the E3 test procedure card.
