1. A problem with this part is rare. In most cases it is **improper steel cable adjustment** that causes noise, or shifting caused by improper assembly. Follow the procedures for replacing the flywheel to adjust the steel cable.
2. If there is noise with the flywheel, check whether there is foreign material or abnormal rubbing. **Replace with a new flywheel if the noise cannot be fixed.**
