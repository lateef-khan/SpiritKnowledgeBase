- Install the elliptical on a flat level surface with access to a **115-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet**, with only the elliptical plugged into the circuit.
- Do not use an extension cord unless it is **16 AWG or better with only one outlet on the end**. Do not disable the grounded plug with adapters or modify the cord outlet.
- **Never use a GFCI wall outlet with this elliptical.** As with any appliance with a large motor, the GFCI will trip often. Route the power cord away from any moving part, including the elevation mechanism and transport wheels.
- To reduce the risk of electric shock, disconnect the elliptical from the outlet before cleaning or service work.
- The product must be grounded, and the grounding instructions state it is **for use on a nominal 115-volt circuit**. Improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric shock.
