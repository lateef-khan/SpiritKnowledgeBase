The manual's cover code is **SE685S-YE030**. Section 1 is titled "SE685S-YE030 Outlines" and the table of contents calls it the "SE685S Elliptical".

This is the Sole E55 of model year 2019. The Spirit database names SKU **555018** as this machine. The year comes from that name, not from the digits of the number.

The machine has a console, a main controller (DC power supply, incline driver and tension motor driver), a tension motor that changes brake resistance, and an AC incline motor.

The parts drawings name the main plastic and steel parts: console, console mast cover, side case L and R, round disk and round disk cover, pedal arm covers, incline cover, inclinable rail cover, rear bar cover, middle stabilizer cover, slide wheel cover; console mast, console holder assembly, upper and lower handle bars, main frame, drive pulley, cross bar, idler bracket, bushing housing pedal arm, adjustable pedal, rear rail assembly, flywheel, connecting arms, pedal arms, inclinable rail assembly and incline motor.
