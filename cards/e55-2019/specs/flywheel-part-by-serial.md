Match the machine's serial number against the SN range column in the source table. Two pairs cover the sheet:

| Flywheel | Steel cable | SN ranges |
|---|---|---|
| K500020 | K020005 | 9 ranges, e.g. 5550182011005041~005544 |
| K500042 | K020046 | 3 ranges, e.g. 5550182011005545~006048 |

The sheet carries one "Before 2021/08/19 is K500020+K020005" note, on a row already assigned K500042+K020046. Match by SN range, not by date. The full range table is the source text.
