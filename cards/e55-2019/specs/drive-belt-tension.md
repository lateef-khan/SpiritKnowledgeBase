**190 Hz, plus or minus 10 Hz**, read on an acoustic gauge while the belt is plucked with a finger.

Set it by tightening the **M8 x 9T nut on the M8x170mm J-bolt**, then tighten the M8 x 7T nut. The belt must be on the drive pulley and flywheel first.

The manual prints this figure once, inside the Poly-V belt replacement procedure. The belt slipping entry in the troubleshooting section does not repeat it; it only tells you to adjust the cap on the hook-type screw with a #13 wrench.
