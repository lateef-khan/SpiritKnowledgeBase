1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release two **Phillips head screws M5x15mm** and take the slide wheel cover off first.
2. Use **circlip pliers** to remove the **circlip 17** and remove the sliding wheels.
3. Reverse the above steps to refit the pedal arm, and return the **17 wavy washers** back to the left and right slide wheels.
