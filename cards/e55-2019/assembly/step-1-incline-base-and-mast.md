1. **Install the Incline Rail Assembly (2) into the U-channel bracket of the Main Frame (1).** From the sides install **two Hex Head Bolts (128)** with **two Flat Washers (171)** and **two Nylon Nuts (158)**. From the top install **four Hex Head Bolts (124)**, **four Split Washers (176)**, **four Flat Washers (166)**, and **four Star Washers (177)**. Tighten with **Wrenches (186 & 189)**.
2. **Connect the 3 wire harness (43)** to the female receiver from the Incline Rail Assembly (2). Connect the **Three pin position Sensor connector (44)** the same way.
3. **Slide the Console Mast Cover (95) onto the Console Mast (12).** Tie the twist tie to the **Computer Cable (45)**, pull it up through the mast, and install the mast into the receiving bracket, pulling slightly on the cable at the top so it does not get pinched and shorted.
4. **Install four Socket Head Cap Bolts (129) with four Split Washers (175) and four Flat Washers (171)** and tighten with the **L Allen Wrench (197)**, pulling slightly on the cable at the top.

The checklist draws and labels #129 a Hex Head Bolt while this step says socket cap and calls for the L Allen wrench. The drawing plus the pack list wins; treat the bolt as drawn and keep the Allen key handy in case the bag follows the step text.
5. **Plug the connectors into the back of the console: Computer Cable (45), two Hand pulse Cables (52), Resistance switch wire (55) and Incline switch wire (56).** Secure the **Console (40)** with **four Phillips Head Screws (140)**.
6. **Install the Bottle Holder (105) on the Console Mast (12) with two Phillips Head Screws (140)** using driver (188). **The bottle holder must be assembled before console assembly.**
