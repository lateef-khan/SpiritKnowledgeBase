1. Follow the procedures to take the rail base assembly apart.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **M5x15mm Phillips head screws** securing the rear bar case, inclinable rail cover and incline cover.
3. Use **two 14mm wrenches** to release the **3/8" x 2-1/2" hex head bolts**, **3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washers** and **3/8" x 7T nyloc nuts** which secure the rear rail assembly and incline motor holder.
4. Use **two 17mm wrenches** to release the **M10 x 130 m/m hex head bolts**, **3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washers** and **M10 x 8T nyloc nuts** which secure the incline motor and incline device.
5. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **5x16 tapping screw** and the ground wire, and pull it out after cutting the wire tie, to take the incline motor apart.
6. Use a **12mm wrench** to release the **5/16" x 1 hex head bolt** and **5/16" x 35 x 2T flat washer** which secure the rail assembly. Pull out the locking tube assembly and take the rail assembly apart.
7. Release the **M5x15mm Phillips head screw** securing the aluminum rail to take the rail apart, if necessary.
8. Reverse the above steps to refit the parts.

This manual gives no zeroing distance for the incline motor.
