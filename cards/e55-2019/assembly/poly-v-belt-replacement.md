1. Follow 11-2, 11-3, 11-4 and 11-5 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, both chain covers and the cross bar.
2. Use a **13 mm wrench** to loosen the **M8 x 9T nut on the M8x170mm J-bolt** and on the idler wheel, and another **M8 x 7T nut**; the belt is then released.
3. To reassemble, return the belt on the drive pulley and flywheel, then tighten the M8 x 9T nut until the **acoustic gauge reads 190 Hz (+/- 10 Hz)** when the belt is plucked with a finger, then tighten the M8 x 7T nut.
4. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.

The table of contents calls section 11.6 "Flywheel & Poly-V Belt Replacement", but the body of 11-6 is the belt only; the flywheel is section 11-8.
