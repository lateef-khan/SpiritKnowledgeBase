**The manual heads this section "11-9. Drive Pulley Replacement", but the table of contents calls 11.9 Bushing Housing Replacement and the steps are the bushing housing.** Read the heading as a misprint.

1. Follow 11-2 and 11-3 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, left and right side cases, cross bar and the belt.
2. Use a **12 mm wrench** to release the **5/16" x 15 m/m hex head bolt** with the **5/16" x 35 x 1.5T flat washer**, and the bushing housing is released.
3. To reassemble, be sure that the **25 wave washer** is returned onto the shaft, then return the bushing housing onto the shaft and tighten with the 5/16" x 15 m/m hex head bolt and 5/16" x 35 x 1.5T flat washer.
4. Follow 11-3 to 11-4 and 11-2 to 11-5 to return the pedal arm and connecting arm.
