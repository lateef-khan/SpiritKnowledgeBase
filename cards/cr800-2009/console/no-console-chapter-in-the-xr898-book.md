**The XR898 service manual describes how to take the console off and how to test its cables, and
nothing else about it.** No page tells you what the display shows, what a key does, how to change
units, or how to reach an engineering or maintenance menu.

This is an absence that was checked three ways, not a gap in the extraction:

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Contents list | Part I, eleven disassembly procedures (Console; Front Handlebar and Console Mast; ...); Part II, Q&A on Console, Slippage, Noises |
| Loose-word search for engineer, maintenance menu, key test, sleep, battery, monitor | 0 hits each; "console" 25 hits, all in the removal procedure and the Q&A |
| Same search allowing spaces between the letters | 0 hits |
| Every page's word count from the PDF | none of the 18 pages is under 25 words; no page is a flattened image hiding a chapter |

**What the book does say about the console** is held elsewhere: removing it - four M5 x 12 mm
screws, then unplug the computer cable and the handgrip sensor cables - is with the assembly cards,
and the three Q&A answers (display will not come on, no speed readout, no pulse) are with the error
cards. They name a **9P computer cable**, a **Hall Sensor** and a magnet, and tell the technician
to use a multi-meter.

**No owner's manual for this machine is in the repository**, so nothing on this card is corroborated
or contradicted by one. The later CR800 (2020) service manual prints a full Product Operation chapter
and a maintenance menu (`spirit-ce800-console-maintenance-menu-function-and-service`); **none of it
may be quoted for this 2009 bike**, whose brake is an induction brake and whose book calls it the
XR898.
