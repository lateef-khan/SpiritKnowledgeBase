**Outline, a photograph with leader lines:** Seat Back, Seat Back Cover, Seat, Console, Drink
Bottle Holder (L), Rear Stabilizer Cover, Step Cover, Front Shroud (R), Pedal, Rear Shroud (L),
Front Shroud (L), Handgrip Pulse Sensor, Rear Shroud (R), Drink Bottle Holder (R), **F. Stabilizer
Cover**.

**Skeleton:** Seat Handle Bar, Mast Handle Bar Assembly, Crank Arm, Seat Back Frame, Seat
Carriage, Console Mast, Rear Stabilizer, **Aluminum Axle** (the seat track), Drive Pulley, Drive
Belt, Idler Wheel Assembly, **Controller**, Main Frame, **Induction Brake**.

**This is the whole of the book's electrical content.** The XR898 manual is eleven replacement
procedures and three Q&A pages; it has **no electronic-parts, electrical-configuration,
block-diagram, wiring or circuit-diagram chapter** - checked page by page, and the only diagram
pages are the two above. The Q&A names a *9P computer cable (44)* and a *Hall Sensor (46)*, which
is as close to wiring as it gets. For the induction brake's controller wiring the nearest labelled
book is the 2016 XBR95's (`spirit-xbr95-2016-cu800-2012-specs-generator-controller-031101b-connections`),
a different machine.
