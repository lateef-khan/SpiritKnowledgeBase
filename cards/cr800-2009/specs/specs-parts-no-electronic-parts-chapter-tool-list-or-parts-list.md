**The XR898 book is eleven replacement procedures and three Q&A pages, and nothing else.** It has no
"Electronic Parts" chapter, no "Prepare tools" line and no parts list - three things every later CR800
service manual prints in some form. Both text layers were searched: the native text has no *prepare*,
*tools required* or *multi-meter* line, and the OCR supplements add none.

**What it does name.** Its first page draws an Outline and a Skeleton with part names on them (console,
shrouds, seat carriage, crank arm, aluminum axle, drive pulley, drive belt, idler wheel assembly,
**controller**, **induction brake**), and its steps cite parts by number in brackets - Console (19),
computer cable (44), Hall Sensor (46), magnet (56), Induction Brake (55), Controller (43) in the
skeleton - **against an exploded view the book does not print**. Those numbers match the numbering the
later CR800 books use for the same parts, but no page here lets a reader check them.

**The one tool it names is a multi-meter, twice, inside the Q&A** - "Use multi-meter to check output
voltage at each connect contact" and "use multi-meter to check cable continuity" - never as a list. The
wrench sizes stay inside the steps they belong to.

**This is the induction-brake CR800, not the generator one.** The 2020 CR800 book names a Generator Brake
and a Controller on its chapter-2 pages (`spirit-cr800-cu800-2020-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`); which
CR800 carries which is on `spirit-bike-specs-generator-brake-or-induction-brake`.
