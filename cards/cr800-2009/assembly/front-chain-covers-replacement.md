1. Follow the crank procedure to take the **cranks (51L, 51R)** and the **console mast cover (31)** off.
2. To take the **left front shroud (29)** off, release **seven Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet-metal screws (103)** and **two 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (101)**.
3. Unscrew the **front shroud (30)**: **two Ø3.5 x 20 mm sheet-metal screws (107)**, **two 3/16" x 15 x 1.5T flat washers (78)** and **two 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (101)**, and take the front shroud (30) off.
4. To refit, secure the front shroud (30) onto the mainframe by slightly tightening the two Ø3.5 x 20 mm sheet-metal screws (107) with the two 3/16" x 15 x 1.5T flat washers (78) temporarily, then tighten the two 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (101) and the Ø3.5 x 20 mm sheet-metal screws (107).
5. Match both front shrouds (29 and 30) and secure with the seven Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet-metal screws (103) and the two 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (101).

**The 2020 CR800 and CR800ENT books print the same procedure with the Ø3.5 screws as x 12 rather than x 16, and add a sixth step about trapping the on/off switch (159) between the shroud halves.** This 2009 book has no switch step and no round disk step (the XBR books add one). The screw counts and the washers are the same in all of them.

**The Spirit Medical 4.0R (FR800-SB022-03 book) prints these five steps with no switch step either**, so it belongs here rather than with the 2020 CR800. Its printing variances: step 2 calls the seven screws Ø3.5 x 16 sheet metal screws and step 5 calls the same seven Ø3.5 x 12 self tapping screws; step 3 prints the right-hand washers as 3/16" x 16 x 1.5T and step 4 as 3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T; step 3 ends "and unplug", the 2020 wording. Counts, the 5 x 16 screws and the two Ø3.5 x 20 frame screws are the same. The 4.0R book prints no part numbers in this chapter.
