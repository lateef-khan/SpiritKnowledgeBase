1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet-metal screws (103)** and **2 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (101)** on the **left rear shroud (35)**. Unplug the hand-pulse cables, and the left rear shroud (35) can be taken off.
2. Release the **Ø3.5 x 20 sheet-metal screw (107)** with its **3/16" x 15 x 1.5T flat washer (78)** that secures the **right rear shroud (36)**, then release **2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws (101)** on the right rear shroud (36), and take it off.
3. To refit, put a **5/16" x 16 x 1T flat washer (127)** on the post of the right rear shroud (36), locate the post into the hole in the mainframe and slightly tighten the Ø3.5 x 20 sheet-metal screw (107) with the 3/16" x 15 x 1.5T flat washer (78). Then tighten the two 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (101), and tighten the Ø3.5 x 20 sheet-metal screw (107) again.
4. Match the left rear shroud (35) with the right rear shroud (36) and secure with the seven Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet-metal screws (103) and the two 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (101).

**The washer (127) on the right shroud's post is printed here and in the XBR books, and not in the 2020 CR800 books.** The XBR books refit the halves with six Ø3.5 x 16 screws plus four 5 x 16 screws where this book keeps seven and two; the XBR version is its own card.

The book's noise answer says a right front shroud rubbing the drive pulley can be cured by adding one more 5/16" x 16 x 1T flat washer (127) to the left front shroud (29) - the same washer.
