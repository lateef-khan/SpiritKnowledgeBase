Hardware: **#51** 5/16" x 5/8" Hex Head Bolt (7 pcs), **#72** 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T Flat Washer (6 pcs), **#99** 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T Curved Washer (1 pc).

1. Unravel the **Computer Cable (29) and the Ground Cable (32)**, then pull the twist tie wire through the Console Mast Cover (38) - see the illustration for the correct orientation of the cover - and the Console Mast (2), until the cable connector comes out the top opening of the console mast.
2. Attach the Console Mast (2) onto the Main Frame (1) with the seven Hex Head Bolts (51). Place **six Flat Washers (72) on the side bolts** and **one Curved Washer (99) on the front bolt**. Tighten using the **12/14mm Wrench (100)**.

**This model has a ground cable (32) to route as well as the computer cable.** It is named in this step and again in step 4, where it plugs into the back of the console. No B94 manual of any year, and no other LCB manual, mentions a ground cable in the assembly steps - so do not carry it across.

Seven bolts but only six flat washers: the seventh, front bolt takes the single curved washer.
