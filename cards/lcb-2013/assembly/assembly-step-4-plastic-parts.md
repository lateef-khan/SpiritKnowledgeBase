Hardware: **#58** M5 x 12mm Phillips Head Screw (6 pcs).

1. **Untie the twist tie that holds the two cables together and discard it.** Insert the Computer Cable (29), the **Ground Cable (32)** and two Hand pulse cables (26 & 28) into the connectors in the back of the Console (34).
2. Attach the Console (34) onto the Console Mast (2) with four Phillips Head Screws (58). Tighten using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (93).
3. Attach the **Front** Stabilizer Cover (40) to the Main Frame (1) with two Phillips Head Screws (58). Tighten with the Phillips Head Screw Driver (93).
4. Install the Pedals (45 L, 46 R) onto the Cranks by using the **13/15mm Wrench (92)**. **The left pedal has a reverse thread** and screws into the crank in the opposite rotation from normal threads. There is an **L** stamped into the end of the threaded post of the left pedal and an **R** on the end of the right post. Tighten the pedals as much as you possibly can. It may be necessary to re-tighten the pedals if you feel a thumping while pedalling; a clicking noise or thumping feeling is usually caused by loose pedals.
5. Attach the Seat (19) to the **Seat Post (7)** using the **12/14mm Wrench (100)**.

**Four connections into the back of the console, not three.** This is the only LCB year that has a separate Ground Cable (32); it is routed up the console mast in step 2 and plugged in here.

The rear stabilizer cover is fitted in step 1 on this year, not here.
