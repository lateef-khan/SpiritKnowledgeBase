**This product must be grounded.** If the unit should malfunction or breakdown, grounding provides a path of least resistance for electric current, reducing the risk of electric shock. This product is equipped with a cord having an equipment-grounding plug. The plug must be plugged into an appropriate outlet that is properly installed and grounded in accordance with all local codes and ordinances.

**DANGER** - Improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric shock. Check with a qualified electrician or serviceman if you are in doubt as to whether the product is properly grounded. Do not modify the plug provided with the product if it will not fit the outlet; have a proper outlet installed by a qualified electrician.

This product is for use on a nominal 110-volt circuit, and has a grounding plug that looks like the plug illustrated in the manual. A temporary adapter that looks like the adapter illustrated may be used to connect this plug to a 2-pole receptacle if a properly grounded outlet is not available. The temporary adapter should be used only until a properly grounded outlet can be installed by a qualified electrician. The green colored rigid ear-lug, or the like, extending from the adapter, must be connected to a permanent ground such as a properly grounded outlet box cover. Whenever the adapter is used, it must be held in place by a metal screw.

> Note: Other parts of this manual (troubleshooting and the parts list) reference 220V AC / a minimum of 210 volt AC current and "220V USE" hardware. This manual does not resolve that inconsistency with the 110-volt statement above - see [electrical power requirements](../specs/electrical-power-requirements.md) for both statements as printed.

If your treadmill's computer shuts off when touched (especially on a cold, dry day) while walking or running, this is a grounding problem - see [computer shuts off from static](../maintenance/computer-shuts-off-static-cold.md).
