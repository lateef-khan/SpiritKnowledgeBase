Your CT900 has three labels on it:

## Serial number sticker
Found on the back of the motor cover. Record the number below the barcode - you'll need it to register your treadmill's warranty. The label artwork also carries the manufacturer contact number (870) 935-1107; the barcode number shown on it is a sample, not your machine's serial number.

## Proposition 65 warning
Found on the left side of the treadmill's upright. California requires notice that this unit was manufactured using chemicals that could cause harm with improper use.

## Safety warning sticker
Found on the left side of the treadmill's upright. Text:

**WARNING / AVERTISSEMENT - SERIOUS INJURY COULD OCCUR IF THESE PRECAUTIONS ARE NOT OBSERVED.**
- Consult your physician before use.
- Stop immediately if you become dizzy or experience chest pains and consult your physician.
- Heart Rate monitoring systems can be inaccurate; use them for reference only.
- Read all warnings on the unit and be properly instructed or read the Owner's Manual on how to use before beginning.
- Inspect this machine for damage prior to use.
- Keep body, clothing, and fitness accessories clear of moving parts.
- Risk of personal injury - Keep children under the age of 13 away from machine.

This same warning text is repeated in full in the manual's [Safety Instructions](safety-instructions.md).

**The CTSBS900 carries the same four label types in different places** - its serial number and
Proposition 65 stickers are on the side frame near the plug-in rather than on the motor cover and
upright, and it adds a console warning sticker in the accessory pocket. Do not read a location off
one machine for the other: `ctsbs900-product-labels`.

**The CT900ENT manual prints no product-labels page at all.** It gives the serial number location
in one line - at the front of the treadmill by the power switch
(`ct900ent-serial-number-and-product-registration`) - and prints the Proposition 65 text as a
warning box rather than describing a sticker (`ct900ent-prop65-warning`). It never says where the
safety warning sticker is on that machine. That is an absence, not a different location.
