Your CT900 has three labels on it:

## Serial number sticker
Found on the back of the motor cover. Record the number below the barcode - you'll need it to register your treadmill's warranty.

## Proposition 65 warning
Found on the left side of the treadmill's upright. California requires notice that this unit was manufactured using chemicals that could cause harm with improper use.

## Safety warning sticker
Found on the left side of the treadmill's upright. Text:

**WARNING / AVERTISSEMENT - SERIOUS INJURY COULD OCCUR IF THESE PRECAUTIONS ARE NOT OBSERVED.**
- Consult your physician before use.
- Stop immediately if you become dizzy or experience chest pains and consult your physician.
- Heart Rate monitoring systems can be inaccurate; use them for reference only.
- Read all warnings on the unit and be properly instructed or read the Owner's Manual on how to use before beginning.
- Inspect this machine for damage prior to use.
- Keep body, clothing, and fitness accessories clear of moving parts.
- Risk of personal injury - Keep children under the age of 13 away from machine.

This same warning text is repeated in full in the manual's [Safety Instructions](safety-instructions.md).
