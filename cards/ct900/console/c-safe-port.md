Your console is equipped with a C-SAFE Port. The Power (POWER) port can be used for powering a remote controlled audio-visual system by connecting a cable from the remote to the Power port at the back of the console.

**The 2025 CE900 elliptical owner's manual prints exactly this much and no more** (p. 16, FEATURES -
C-Safe Features), so this card covers that machine and is no longer treadmill-only. **The word COMM
does not appear anywhere in that document.**

**That is what separates this card from `ct850-2020-c-safe-ports`.** Nine other Spirit machines print
a second sentence naming a **Communication (COMM)** port for fitness software applications. Do not
add a COMM port to an answer for these two machines; neither manual says they have one.

**The 2025 CR900 and CU900 bike owner's manuals print exactly this much too** - CU900 p. 15 and
CR900 p. 15, both headed **C-Safe Features** under FEATURES - so this card covers them as well and is
no longer treadmill-and-elliptical only. **The word COMM appears nowhere in either document.**

**Their own 2018 printings did name a COMM port.** The CR900-2018 and CU900-2018 manuals print the
two-port version on `ct850-2020-c-safe-ports`. The sentence was dropped for 2025; the 2018 wording
must not be quoted for a 2025 machine, or the 2025 wording for a 2018 one.
