1. Press any button to wake the display up if not already on.
2. Press the **Start** button to begin belt movement at 0.5 mph, then adjust to the desired speed using the **Speed +/-** buttons, or by typing the desired speed on the numeric keypad. Once the setting desired is selected, press **Enter**.
3. To stop the treadbelt, press and release the **Stop** button.

For what happens when you press Stop once vs. twice vs. hold it, see [Pause / Stop / Reset](pause-stop-reset.md).
