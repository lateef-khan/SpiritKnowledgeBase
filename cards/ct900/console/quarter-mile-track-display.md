The 1/4-mile track (0.4 km) will be displayed around the dot matrix window. The flashing dot indicates your progress. In the center of the track there is a lap counter for reference.

**The CT800 2012, 2016 and 2020 owner's manuals print this paragraph word for word** - 2012 p. 12,
2016 p. 18, 2020 p. 19 - so this card covers those machines.

**The CT850-2020 puts its track in a different place** - to the left of the dot matrix window, not
around it - so do not read that machine's layout onto these: `ct850-2020-quarter-mile-track`. **The
CT800ENT-2022 has no track ring**; its touchscreen draws a virtual track as one of the workout
views.
