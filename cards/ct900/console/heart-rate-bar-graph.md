Displays a graphical representation of your heart rate as a percentage of your estimated maximum heart rate. When you enter your age during programming, the console will calculate your maximum heart rate then light up the graph to show the percent of estimated maximum heart rate you are currently achieving.

For how maximum heart rate is calculated and what training zone percentages mean, see [Heart Rate Exertion](../programs/heart-rate-exertion-target-zones.md).
