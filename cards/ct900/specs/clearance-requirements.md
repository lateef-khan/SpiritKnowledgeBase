Per ASTM F2115-05 Specifications 6.1.2.11, the recommended minimum clearance required around each treadmill for access to, passage around, and emergency dismount is:

- **0.5 m (19.7 in.)** on each side of the treadmill
- **1 m (39 in.)** behind the machine

This is stated as item 15 of the WARNING list in [Safety Instructions](../safety/safety-instructions.md).
