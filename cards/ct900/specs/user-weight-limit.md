**User Weight Limit: 450 lb.**

This is listed as item 16 of the WARNING section in [Safety Instructions](../safety/safety-instructions.md). The manual notes: "Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine."
