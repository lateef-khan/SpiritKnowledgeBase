**This manual is inconsistent about the required voltage. Both statements below are printed exactly as they appear in the source; this document does not resolve the conflict.**

- [Grounding Instructions](../safety/grounding-instructions.md) (page 7) states: "This product is for use on a nominal **110-volt** circuit," and shows a standard grounded 3-prong plug and 2-pole adapter.
- The [Troubleshooting](../maintenance/top-speed-limited-low-voltage.md) table (page 40) states the display "Motor should be receiving power to operate. Low AC voltage to treadmill... A minimum of **210 volt AC current** is required," and separate troubleshooting rows (page 41) reference plugging into a "**220 VAC** wall outlet" and checking that "AC power is 220V."
- The Parts List and Exploded View Diagram (page 49-51) include several components explicitly labeled "**220V USE**" (a choke, connecting wires, screws), suggesting this manual may be shared across a 110V (US) and a 220V (export) version of the unit without being fully adapted for each market.

**Extension cords**: Do not use an extension cord if it can be avoided. If one is required, it should be as short as possible and heavy duty 16 gauge minimum. See [top speed limited to ~7 mph / low voltage](../maintenance/top-speed-limited-low-voltage.md).

**Environmental spec** (page 6): Temperature specification is 40 degrees C, and humidity is 95%, non-condensing (no water drops forming on surfaces). Not for outdoor or high-humidity use - see [Electrical Safety](../safety/electrical-safety.md).
