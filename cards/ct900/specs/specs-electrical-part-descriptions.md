- **Safety key** - fits on the console and activates all functions. With no safety key the console cannot
  be controlled.
- **Console** - the interface that controls all functions of the treadmill; key controls and an LED display.
- **Main controller** - the circuit board that carries the AC power supply for the console, the incline
  driver and the AC motor driver, and links the console to output the voltages for the "DC control Board".
  The general-information line lists power supply, AC motor, incline motor, AC control Board control
  circuit and incline control circuit.
- **AC motor** - controls speed increases and decreases. **No work voltage is printed.**
- **Incline motor** - a **220 volt AC** motor with four wires (red, black, white, green) plus one 3-pin
  position-sensor cable. AC on **red = UP**, AC on **black = DOWN**, **white = COM (neutral)**, **green =
  ground**.

**220 volts is this book's figure, and the book is a 220-volt book.** Its safety page asks for a 220-volt
10-amp outlet, its troubleshooting says "Check the voltage of power is 220V", and its circuit diagram is
the 220-volt sheet - yet the same safety chapter's grounding paragraph still says "nominal 120-volt
circuit", and its service checklist says "110 VAC wall outlet". The CT900ENT book prints a **120 volt**
incline motor and an AC 120V~ drive motor. Read the incline voltage from the book for the machine in hand.
