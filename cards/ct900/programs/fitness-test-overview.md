When the **Fit Test button** is pressed the DM (Dot Matrix Message Center) displays: **GERKIN**. This is the first of 9 different tests available: Gerkin, WFI, Army (pft), Navy (prt), Air Force (prt), Marines (pft), Law Enforcement (peb), U.S. Coast Guard, and U.K. Chester Fireman (Performance & Prediction protocols). The initial test is GERKIN; to select a different test press the **Fitness Test** button again and the next test will be shown. Continue to press the **Fitness Test** button until the test you want is shown in the message window. To select your desired fitness test, press **Enter**.

## Fit test operation

1. For each program the Message Window will prompt you to enter your personal data such as **Age, Weight, Height, Gender**. Adjust the settings using the keypad or Up and Down buttons then press the **Enter** button to accept the new number and proceed on to the next screen.
2. After all required data is entered you may now press **Start** to begin the test.
