Heart Rate Control (HRC) uses your treadmill's incline system to control your heart rate via information from pulse grips or wireless chest strap. Increases and decreases in elevation affect heart rate much more efficiently than changes in speed alone. The HRC program automatically changes elevation gradually to achieve the programmed target heart rate.

**Selecting a Heart Rate Control Program**: You have the option, during setup mode, to choose either the **Weight Control (HR 1)** program or the **Cardiovascular (HR 2)** program. The Weight Control program will attempt to maintain your heart rate at 60% of your Maximum Heart Rate. The Cardiovascular program will attempt to maintain your heart rate at 85% of your Maximum Heart Rate. Your Maximum Heart Rate is based upon a formula that subtracts your age from a constant of 220. Your HR setting is automatically calculated during the setup mode when you enter your age.

## Heart Rate Control programming steps

1. Press **HR button** to enter heart rate program.
2. The window will show PRESS ENTER TO MODIFY OR START TO BEGIN WORKOUT. You can either press the **Enter** button for settings or press the Start button to execute the program.
3. When Enter is pressed, the Message Window shows PRESS 1 OR 2 TO SELECT THEN PRESS ENTER. At the same time, the dot matrix window shows "1" and you can press numeric buttonpad 1 or 2, then Enter.
4. Message Window shows ADJUST TIME THEN PRESS ENTER with Time Window blinking. Use Up/Down buttons or numeric buttons to adjust. After setting time, press Enter again.
5. Message Window shows ADJUST AGE THEN PRESS ENTER with Incline Window blinking. Use Up/Down/Fast/Slow buttons or numeric buttons to adjust. Adjusting age will change the HR value (per the MHR formula above). After age is set, press Enter again.
6. Message Window shows ADJUST BODY WEIGHT THEN PRESS ENTER with Incline Window blinking. Use Up/Down buttons or numeric buttons to adjust. After weight is set, press Enter again.
7. Message Window shows ADJUST HEART RATE THEN PRESS ENTER with Speed Window blinking. Use Up/Down buttons or numeric buttons to adjust. After the target heart rate value is set, press Enter again.
8. Message Window shows PRESS START TO BEGIN WORKOUT OR ENTER TO MODIFY. Press Start to begin the workout or Stop to return to the previous screen.

**NOTE**: When the Message Window shows CHECK PULSE, there is no pulse signal received and the program cannot be performed. Check and make sure that the heart rate strap functions normally - see [heart rate chest strap setup](heart-rate-chest-strap-setup.md).

With all Heart Rate programs you may use the heart rate monitor feature without using the Heart Rate program - this can be used during manual mode or during any of the nine fitness test programs.
