**Yes, a dues-paying gym is covered - and it is the only Spirit treadmill warranty in
this knowledge base that says so.** The table names three environments and gives each
its own periods, so the answer to "how long is it covered" depends on where the machine
stands:

| Warranty | Frame | Motor | Deck | Parts | Labor | Wear Items |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial (Dues Paying Facilities) | 10 Years | 5 Years | 3 Years | 3 Years | 3 Years | 6 Months |
| Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | 10 Years | 5 Years | 5 Years | 5 Years | 3 Years | 6 Months |
| Residential | Lifetime | Lifetime | Lifetime | 10 Years | 3 Years | 6 Months |

Dues-paying and non-dues-paying differ only in **Deck** and **Parts** - 3 Years against
5. Frame, Motor, Labor and Wear Items are the same in both commercial rows. The full
table with its footnotes is `ct900-warranty-coverage-terms`.

**Do not carry the CT800 or CT850 answer across.** Those eight manuals exclude a
dues-paying facility outright - "Product used in a dues paying facility (YMCA, Health
Club, Etc.)" is item 8 of their exclusion list. No such exclusion is printed in this
manual; its item 8 is prisons. A machine that is refused cover on a CT800 is warranted
here, on the shorter of the two commercial rows
(`spirit-ct800-warranty-use-environment`).

**The manual still contradicts itself twice about home and light commercial use.**
Neither sentence is reconciled anywhere in the source, so quote both rather than
choosing:

- The opening paragraph warrants "all its **home use** Treadmill parts" - residential
  wording heading a table whose first two rows are commercial.
- Exclusion 2 refuses service call reimbursement for "units not in **ordinary household
  or light commercial use**" - which reads as a limit on the very dues-paying placement
  the table's first row covers. The list is `ct900-warranty-exclusions`.

**What the manual never says.** It does not define what makes a facility dues-paying,
gives no examples, caps no hours of use per day, and defines neither "light commercial"
nor "ordinary household". Do not import the XT five-hours-a-day limit
(`xt-2023-warranty-use-environment`); it is not printed in this manual.

**Prisons and correctional facilities are outside all three rows**, by the footnote
under the table and again by exclusion 8 - `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.
