1. Install **MAIN PANEL (3)** onto the uprights using 6 **BOLTS (63)**, 6 **LOCK WASHERS (79)** and 6 **FLAT WASHERS (74)**.
2. Run the wiring harness through the **MAIN PANEL (3)** as shown in the illustration.
3. Tighten all bolts and washers on both uprights (7 and 8) from the previous step.

## Hardware for step 2

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 63 | BOLT | M10XP1.5X 35L | 6 |
| 74 | FLAT WASHER | Ø3/8"X Ø25X3T | 6 |
| 79 | LOCK WASHER | Ø10 | 6 |

**The two manuals disagree on how the flat washer is printed.** The CT900ENT table
prints item 74 as `3/8"x 2.5`, which names neither an outside diameter nor a
thickness. The CT900 table and the CT900 parts list both give **Ø3/8" x Ø25 x 3T**,
and that is the figure above. Item numbers and quantities are identical in both
books; the CT900ENT also writes the bolt as `M10x1.5x35mm` and the lock washer as
`10`.
