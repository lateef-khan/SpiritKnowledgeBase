The power input is located at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood.

Power the treadmill on by plugging it into an appropriate wall outlet, then turn on the power switch located at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood. Ensure that the safety button (safety key) is installed, as the treadmill will not power on without it - see [safety tether cord and safety key](../safety/safety-tether-cord-and-key.md).

When the power switch is turned on, the treadmill console will take around 10 seconds to power on. The console will then enter idle mode, which is the starting point for operation.

**The CTSBS900 manual prints this whole paragraph word for word** at its p. 18, calling the same part the safety *key* rather than the safety *button*, and adding a switch-position line that contradicts the location above. It has its own card - see [powering on the CTSBS900](../../ctsbs900/assembly/powering-on.md).

**The CT900ENT does not power up like this.** Its setting-up page names no power switch, no motor-hood location and no safety key; it plugs in at the **front base of the unit** and goes straight to a Home screen - see [starting the treadmill and the Home screen](../../ct900ent/console/starting-the-treadmill-home-screen.md).

Next: [Quick Start](../console/quick-start.md).

If the display does not light up at all after this, see [display won't turn on](../maintenance/display-wont-turn-on-or-is-dim.md).
