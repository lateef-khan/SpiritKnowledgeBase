1. Loosen the 8 screws holding the **MOTOR COVER (37)** in place and remove the cover.
2. Uncoil wiring harness and run wires through the **RIGHT UPRIGHT (7)**.
3. Use 5 **BOLTS (65)** and 5 **LOCK WASHERS (79)** to attach the **RIGHT UPRIGHT (7)** to the **MAIN FRAME (1)**. Do not pinch wires. Do not tighten before putting the console on.
4. Use 5 **BOLTS (65)** and 5 **LOCK WASHERS (79)** to attach the **LEFT UPRIGHT (8)** to the **MAIN FRAME (1)**. Do not tighten before putting the console on.

## Hardware for step 1

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 65 | BOLT | M10XP1.5X60L | 10 |
| 79 | LOCK WASHER | Ø10 | 10 |

**Both manuals print the same two item numbers and the same quantities.** The CT900ENT
table writes them `M10x1.5x60mm` and `10`, dropping the thread-pitch "P" and the
diameter symbol; the CT900 parts list confirms item 65 is an M10 x P1.5 x 60mm socket
head cap bolt and item 79 a Ø10 x 2T spring washer. Same hardware, two printings.

The CT900ENT text says only "the motor cover" and gives it no item number; the CT900
text calls it **(37)**. It is the same cover.
