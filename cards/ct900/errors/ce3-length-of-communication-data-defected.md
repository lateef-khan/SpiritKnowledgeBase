**This is cE3 (warning code #3), not cE1, cE2, cE4, or cE10.** Press RESET to clear any Warning Code.

**Corrective actions**:
1. Verify if the length of communication data fits the specifications of the motor drive.
2. Verify the communication cable and the communication quality.
3. Clear the fault and then press RESET button.
