**This is the inverter's oSL warning code (warning #12, Over Slip), not oSP (warning #13, Over Speed), and not the console's [E23 OVER SLIP](e23-over-slip.md) error code.** Press RESET to clear any Warning Code.

**Corrective actions**:
1. Verify if the motor drive is overload.
2. Verify the setting of Pr04-21 ~Pr04-23.
