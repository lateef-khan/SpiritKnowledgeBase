**This is E2 (OVER VOLTAGE), not E14 (DC LOW VOLT) and not E25 (STOP OVER VOLT).**

The manual's Error Codes table gives no distinct description or solution for E2 beyond its name. Per the table: "Please follow to AC MOTOR DRIVER inverter VFD-TM Error and Warning codes' descriptions corresponding table" for both description and solution.
