**This is the inverter's toH warning code (warning #17), not the console's [E32 MOTOR TEMP](e32-motor-temp.md) or [E3 IGBT OVER TEMP](e3-igbt-over-temp.md) error codes.** Press RESET to clear any Warning Code.

**Corrective actions**:
1. Verify if the motor is overheated.
2. Verify the wiring of motor's temperature protection switch.
