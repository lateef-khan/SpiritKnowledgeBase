**This is InC3 (warning #16, up-down reversed) - not InC1 (warning #14, up-down not responding) or InC2 (warning #15, up-down loss).** Press RESET to clear any Warning Code.

**Corrective actions**:
1. Verify the wiring between the up-down motor and the motor drive.
2. Verify the settings of Pr02-20 ~Pr02-30.
