**This is InC1 (warning #14, up-down not responding) - not InC2 (warning #15, up-down loss) or InC3 (warning #16, up-down reversed).** Press RESET to clear any Warning Code.

**Corrective actions**:
1. Verify the wiring between the up-down motor and the motor drive.
2. Verify the settings of Pr02-20 ~Pr02-30.
3. Verify if the up-down reaches the impassable point.
