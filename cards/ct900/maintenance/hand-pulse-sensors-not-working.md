**Symptom**: Hand pulse lost its function. (No pulse displayed on monitor)

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Hands not on the hand pulse sensors, or only one hand on sensor | Hold the hand pulse with two hands |
| Connector of HANDPULSE W/WIRE and Console not connected properly | Connect the cable again |
| Wires got damaged when connecting the HANDPULSE W/WIRE and Console | Replace with new cable |
| Hand pulse board is broken | Replace console or Hand pulse board |

For wireless chest strap issues instead of grip sensors, see [wireless heart rate not working / erratic pulse](wireless-heart-rate-and-erratic-pulse.md).
