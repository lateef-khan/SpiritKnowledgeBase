## Display does not light at all
Possible causes and solutions, in order:
1. Tether cord (safety key) not in position - see [safety tether cord and safety key](../safety/safety-tether-cord-and-key.md).
2. Circuit breaker on front grill tripped. Push circuit breaker in until it locks.
3. Plug is disconnected. Make sure plug is firmly pushed into the wall outlet.
4. Breaker panel circuit breaker may be tripped.
5. Treadmill defect. Contact your Spirit Fitness dealer.

## No display even with the safety key inserted
| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Haven't switched ON/OFF switch | Switch the AC switch |
| Safe key inserted in wrong position | Insert the safe key in the right position |
| Console connector not plugged in properly | Check the wire and connect again |
| Console cable is broken | Replace console cable |
| Fuse on controller is blown | Replace fuse or controller |
| Varistor on controller is blown | Replace varistor or controller |
| Safety device is broken (open) | Replace safety key device |
| Other components are faulty | Replace console |

## LED not bright, incomplete, or imperfect
| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| LED light is broken | Replace with new LED or console |
| Power to console too low | Check AC power is 220V; check power to console; replace lower controller |
| LED displays are broken (general) | Replace with new console |

These are technician-level diagnostic tables from the manual's Troubleshooting section; contact your Spirit Fitness dealer before opening the unit.
