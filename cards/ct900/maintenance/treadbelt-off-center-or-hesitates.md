## Tread-belt does not stay centered / hesitates when walked/run on
The user may be walking while favoring or putting more weight on either the left or right foot. If this walking pattern is natural, track the belt slightly off-center to the side opposite from the belt movement. Adjust as necessary - see [treadbelt tracking adjustment](treadbelt-tracking-adjustment.md).

## Tread belt does not run in center
**Reason**: Tread belt tension not even across tread belt. **Solve**: See [treadbelt tracking adjustment](treadbelt-tracking-adjustment.md) and [belt tension adjustment](belt-tension-adjustment.md).

## Tread belt hesitates while being stepped on
**Reason** (as printed in the source): "Insufficient lubricant on tread belt. Tread belt tension insufficient." **Solve** (as printed): "See treadmill belt lubrication."

**Ambiguity note**: the source's troubleshooting table refers readers to a "treadmill belt lubrication" section, but no such section or procedure exists anywhere else in this manual. This also appears to contradict [Belt and deck cleaning](belt-deck-cleaning.md), which states this treadmill uses a hard waxed deck and explicitly instructs: "Do not apply any type of lubricant or wax to the surface." This document preserves both statements exactly as printed and does not attempt to resolve the contradiction - if the belt is hesitating, check [belt tension adjustment](belt-tension-adjustment.md) first, and contact your Spirit Fitness dealer before applying any lubricant.
