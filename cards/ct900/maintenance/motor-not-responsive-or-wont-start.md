## Motor is not responsive after pressing Start
1. If the belt moves, but stops after a short time and the display shows "LS", run calibration - see [calibration procedure](calibration-procedure.md).
2. If you press start and the belt never moves, and the display shows LS, contact service.

## When you press "START", the treadmill doesn't start
| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Motor wire isn't connected in the right position | Check and plug again |
| Motor is broken | Replace motor or check the wire/connector |
| Treadmill controller shut down and LED would be ON | Turn off the AC switch and turn on power again |

## After pressing "START", the treadmill stops immediately (both source rows below, essentially duplicate entries)
| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Controller is broken | Turn off the AC switch and turn on power again; replace controller and calibrate it |
| Controller was broken | Replace with new controller and calibrate it |

## Treadmill stops or shuts off by itself
| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| House breaker tripped | Reset it |
| Treadmill breaker tripped | Reset treadmill breaker |
| Treadmill controller fuse is broken | Replace with new fuse |
| Treadmill controller shut down and LED would be ON | Turn off the AC switch and turn on power again |
