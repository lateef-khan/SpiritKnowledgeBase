**Symptom**: Treadmill trips on board 20 amp circuit.

**Cause**: High belt/deck friction. See [belt and deck cleaning](belt-deck-cleaning.md). If cleaning doesn't prevent this from reoccurring, check the amp draw of the motor. If this is high and there are signs of significant wear of the deck, it may need to be flipped if it is on its original side.

Not to be confused with the **house** breaker (not on the treadmill itself) tripping - see [house breaker trips (AFCI/GFCI)](house-breaker-trips-afci-gfci.md) for that separate symptom.
