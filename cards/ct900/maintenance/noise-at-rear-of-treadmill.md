**Symptom**: Noise in the rear of the treadmill.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Rear roller bearings are defective | Replace with new rear roller |
| Rear roller misaligned | Adjust rear roller position |

For noise coming from the front/motor area instead, see [noise under motor cover](noise-under-motor-cover.md).
