**Symptom**: Circuit breaker trips, but not the treadmill circuit breaker.

**Cause/Solve**: Need to replace the breaker with a "High In-rush current" type breaker. See [Electrical Safety](../safety/electrical-safety.md) for full details.

This is the same AFCI/GFCI nuisance-tripping issue described in [Electrical Safety](../safety/electrical-safety.md): these breakers may trip occasionally during exercise because of the high inrush currents of the unit's drive electronics and motor - this affects all treadmill brands, not just the CT900. If you have AFCI/GFCI breakers/outlets and experience nuisance tripping, check whether other devices (fluorescent lights with electronic ballasts, coffee makers, space heaters, hair driers) share the same circuit.

Not to be confused with the treadmill's **own onboard** 20 amp breaker tripping, which is a different symptom - see [onboard circuit breaker trips](onboard-circuit-breaker-trips.md).
