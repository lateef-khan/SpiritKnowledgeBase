**This describes the safety key device failing, not the console's E21 SAFETY KEY error code** - see [error E21 SAFETY KEY](../errors/e21-safety-key.md) for that formal fault code.

## With no safety key, treadmill still displays or operates
**Reason**: Safety device is broken (short). **Solve**: Replace the safety key device or console.

## After removing safety key, treadmill can't stop
**Reason**: The safety key device is broken. **Solve**: Replace with new safety key device.

Under normal operation, the treadmill should refuse to power on or operate at all without the safety key/magnet installed, and pulling the magnet should stop the belt within a step or two - see [safety tether cord and safety key](../safety/safety-tether-cord-and-key.md). Either of the malfunctions above (won't power-limit without the key, or won't stop when the key is pulled) is a safety-critical fault - stop using the treadmill and contact service.
