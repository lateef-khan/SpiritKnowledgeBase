## Wireless lost its function (No pulse displayed on monitor)
| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Chest belt not worn properly | Check chest belt has proper contact with skin and is oriented correctly |
| Distance is too far and exceeds range of receiver | User chest belt in front of console within 3 feet |
| Chest belt battery is weak or dead | Replace with new lithium battery type CR2032 |

## Chest belt too close to the treadmill
**Reason**: Weak battery. **Solve**: Replace with new lithium battery type CR2032.

## Erratic pulse display
**Reason**:
1. Another chest belt in use around the treadmill.
2. Other magnetic field disturbance.
3. Receiver is broken.

**Solve**:
1. Check for other chest belt use around the treadmill.
2. Change the position or direction of the treadmill.
3. Replace with a new receiver.

A fuller list of interference sources for erratic/wild heart rate readings (grounding, microwaves, fluorescent lights, security systems, pet fences, nearby transmitters) is documented at [chest strap warning - erratic operation](../programs/chest-strap-erratic-operation-interference.md). The CR2032 battery and 2500-hour estimated battery life are also documented in [heart rate chest strap setup](../programs/heart-rate-chest-strap-setup.md).
