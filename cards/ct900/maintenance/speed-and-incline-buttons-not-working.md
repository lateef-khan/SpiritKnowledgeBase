## FAST/SLOW button of SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH can't be used / only Fast works / only Slow works
| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Connector of SPEED CABLE and CONSOLE not connected properly | Connect cables again |
| Connector of SPEED CABLE and SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH w/cable not connected properly | Connect cables again |
| Connector of SPEED CABLE or SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH/cable is damaged | Connect cable again |
| Button of SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH is broken | Replace with new buttons |
| Connector of SPEED CABLE or SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH/cable is damaged | Replace with new cable |

## UP/DOWN button of INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH can't be used / only Up works / only Down works
| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Connector of INCLINE CABLE and CONSOLE not connected properly | Connect the wires again |
| Connector of INCLINE CABLE and INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH w/cable not connected properly | Connect the wires again |
| Connector of INCLINE CABLE or INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH cable got damaged | Replace the cable |
| Button of INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH is broken | Replace buttons |
| Connector of INCLINE CABLE or INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH cable got damaged/damaged | Replace the cable |
