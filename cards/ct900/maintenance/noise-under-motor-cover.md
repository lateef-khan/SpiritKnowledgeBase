**Symptom**: Noise under motor cover.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Worn brushes or bearings on motor | Replace with new motor |
| Front roller bearings are defective | Replace with new front roller |
| Drive belt is misadjusted (too tight or too loose) | Adjust motor position |

For noise coming from the back of the machine instead, see [noise at rear of treadmill](noise-at-rear-of-treadmill.md).
