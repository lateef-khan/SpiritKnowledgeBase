For a wireless chest strap transmitter bought from Sole:

1. Attach the transmitter to the elastic strap using the locking parts.
2. Adjust the strap as tightly as possible while keeping it comfortable.
3. Position the transmitter with the **SOLE logo in the middle of your body, facing away from your chest**. Some people may have to position it slightly left of centre. Attach the end of the elastic strap by inserting the round end, and secure the transmitter and strap around your chest with the locking parts.
4. Position the transmitter **immediately below the pectoral muscles**.
5. **Sweat is the best conductor**, but plain water can be used to pre-wet the electrodes - the two ribbed oval areas on the reverse side of the belt and both sides of the transmitter. Wearing the strap a few minutes before the workout to warm it up helps; because of body chemistry some users have a more difficult time achieving a strong, steady signal at first. **Wearing clothing over the strap does not affect performance.**
6. **Stay within range of the console.** The range length may vary, so stay close enough to keep readings strong and reliable. Wearing the transmitter immediately against bare skin assures proper operation; to wear it over a shirt, moisten the areas of the shirt where the electrodes will rest.

**The transmitter switches itself on and off.** It activates automatically when it detects activity from the user's heart and deactivates when it receives none.

**It is water resistant, not waterproof.** Moisture can create false signals, so dry the transmitter completely after use to prolong battery life.

The strap is **optional** and is not supplied with the machine. If the reading is wild or missing, work through the interference card as well.
