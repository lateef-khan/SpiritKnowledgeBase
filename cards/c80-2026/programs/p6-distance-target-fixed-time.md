**P-6 Mode - Distance Target Exercise Mode, Time Fixed.**

1. Press the **"Set"** button to set the target distance. **Range: 1.0-10.0 km (miles).**
2. **Exercise time is fixed at 10 minutes.**
3. After setting, press the **"Start/Pause"** button to start or pause.
4. During exercise, you can press buttons to check relevant exercise data.
5. Press and hold the **"Start/Pause"** button to end the exercise and return to the IDEL mode.

**The fixed 10 minutes cannot be changed**, and the manual does not say what happens if the distance is not covered inside it. A customer who wants the same distance with no time limit uses P-2, whose range is printed as **1.00-10.00** - the same bounds at different printed precision.
