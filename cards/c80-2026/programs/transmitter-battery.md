- **Estimated transmitter battery life: 2500 hours.**
- **Replacement battery: a Panasonic CR2032.**

Dry the transmitter completely after use to prolong battery life - it is water resistant, and moisture can create false signals.

**This is not the console battery.** The console takes **four AA cells** and has its own compartment and its own low-battery indication; see `c80-2026-console-batteries` and `c80-2026-battery-low-warning`. A customer asking "what battery does my treadmill take" may mean either one.

The manual prints no procedure for opening the transmitter, and the strap is an optional accessory that is not supplied with the machine.
