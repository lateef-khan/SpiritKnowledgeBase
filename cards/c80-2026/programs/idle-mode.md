**IDEL Mode is the unlimited exercise mode** - the default, with no time, distance, calorie or heart rate target.

1. Press the **"Start/Pause"** button to start or pause.
2. During exercise, press buttons to check related exercise data.
3. **Press and hold the "Start/Pause"** button to end exercise and return.

**"IDEL" is the manual's own spelling** and is used on every page that names a mode; read it as idle. Every one of the six numbered programs ends by returning here.

The six numbered target programs are P-1 to P-6, each on its own card.
