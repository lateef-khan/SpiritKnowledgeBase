**Do not use the machine for Heart Rate unless a steady, solid Actual Heart Rate value is being displayed.** High, wild, random numbers indicate a problem.

Areas to look for interference, as printed:

1. Microwave ovens, TVs, small appliances, etc.
2. Fluorescent lights.
3. Some household security systems.
4. Perimeter fence for a pet.
5. Some people have problems with the transmitter picking up a signal from their skin. **If you have problems, try wearing the transmitter upside down.** Normally the transmitter is oriented so the SOLE logo is right side up.
6. The antenna that picks up your heart rate is very sensitive. If there is an outside noise source, **turning the whole machine 90 degrees may de-tune the interference**.
7. **Loose console or loose bolts in the upright tube.**
8. Another individual wearing a transmitter **within 3 feet** of your machine's console.

If problems continue, contact your dealer.

**WARNING printed in capitals: do not use the heart rate program if your heart rate is not registering properly on the display.**

Item 7 is the one a technician can act on directly - the console mounting screws and the upright bolts. The assembly instructions tighten the upright bolts only at the end of Step 3, so a machine assembled by the customer is a fair suspect.
