| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Maximum operating temperature | **40 degrees C** |
| Humidity | **95% non-condensing** (no water drops forming on surfaces) |

**Never expose the machine to rain or moisture.** It is not designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.

Two points to note:

- The manual gives **no minimum operating temperature** and no storage-temperature range.
- The warranty adds a separate and stricter condition - the warranty is void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment, with a garage or carport named as an example. Meeting the 40 degrees C / 95% figures above does **not** on its own keep the warranty valid.

The page is headed "Important Electrical Instructions" although this machine has no mains supply; the table of contents calls the same page "Important Electrical Information".
