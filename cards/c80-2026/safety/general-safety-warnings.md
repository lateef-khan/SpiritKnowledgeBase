The general bullets from the safety list. The placement, user-age, aerosol and environment bullets are on their own cards.

- **Keep hands away from all moving parts.**
- **Never drop or insert any object into any openings.**
- **Do not use outdoors.**
- **Do not attempt to use the machine for any purpose other than the purpose it is intended for.**
- **Use the handrails provided; they are for your safety.**
- **Wear proper shoes.** High heels, dress shoes, sandals and bare feet are not suitable. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue.

The page closes with **"SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!"**
