**Do not operate the machine where aerosol spray products are being used or where oxygen is being administered.**

**The printed reason does not fit this machine.** The manual gives the reason as *"Sparks from the motor may ignite a highly gaseous environment"* - but this is a **curved manual treadmill with no drive motor**, as the card `c80-2026-model-overview` records from the service manual and as this manual's own title says. The rule appears to be carried over from Sole's powered-treadmill manuals.

Both statements are printed. This card picks no winner: the restriction is stated, but the mechanism named for it is a motor the machine does not have. Treat the restriction as printed and do not repeat the motor-spark explanation to a customer.
