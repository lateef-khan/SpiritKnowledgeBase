- **Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine.** The manual gives the reason: there are obvious pinch points and other caution areas that can cause harm.
- The appliance **is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge**, unless a person responsible for their safety has given them supervision or instruction concerning its use.

The manual sets **no maximum user weight and no maximum user height** anywhere. If a customer asks for a weight limit, this manual does not answer it.
