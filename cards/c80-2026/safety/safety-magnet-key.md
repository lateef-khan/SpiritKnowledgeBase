The key and the pin work by magnetism. There are two positions:

1. **Lock** - press the pin all the way in. This locks the resistance at the **maximum** level.
2. **Release** - use the magnet key to draw the pin out of the lock position, so the resistance lever is released.

**Lock the machine with the safety magnet key to prevent unauthorised operation.** The general maintenance section repeats this as a required first step: lock the machine with the safety magnet key **before** vacuuming or wiping the belt.

The assembly instructions name the same part from the other end: *"The Magnetic buckle Assembly (53) can be used to unlock the gear lock function. Use it as needed depending on your workout requirements."* Item **53, Magnetic buckle Assembly** in the service manual parts list is this key.

**Note a disagreement between the two manuals.** The card `c80-2026-model-overview`, taken from the service manual, says nothing in that manual describes a safety key. This owner's manual devotes a headed section to the Safety Magnet Key, and the service manual parts list does carry item 53. Neither source is corrected here - the owner's manual describes a resistance lock, and the service manual has no procedure for it.
