Two placement rules, both printed in the safety list:

- **Leave a minimum of 3 1/2 feet of clearance between the rear of the machine and any fixed object.** Do not block the rear of the machine.
- **Do not operate it on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet.** The manual says damage to both the carpet and the machine may result.

The manual also says **do not use it outdoors**. The temperature and humidity limits are on their own card, and the warranty adds a climate-control condition that is stricter than the safety list.

The manual prints **no room-size, ceiling-height or side-clearance figure**, and no machine footprint or weight anywhere in this owner's manual.
