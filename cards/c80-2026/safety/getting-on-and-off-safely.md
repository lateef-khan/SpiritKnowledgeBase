**NEVER mount or dismount the machine while the belt is moving. Always hold on to a handrail or hand bar.**

On a curved manual treadmill the belt is driven by the user's own feet, so it does not stop on a console command - it stops when it is no longer being driven. The manual states no belt-stopping procedure and lists no safety tether cord or stop key. What it does describe is a **Safety Magnet Key** that locks the resistance at maximum to prevent unauthorised operation; that is a lock for an unattended machine, not an emergency stop.

The manual also warns that distractions such as watching television or reading may cause you to lose balance or stray from walking in the centre of the belt.
