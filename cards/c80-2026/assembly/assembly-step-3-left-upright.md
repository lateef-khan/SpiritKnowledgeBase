1. Place the **Left Upright (5)** on the left side of the treadmill. Connect it to the **Main Frame Left side (2)** and the **Front armrest console support (6)**.
2. Using the **L Allen Wrench (96, 97)**, install the **Button Head Socket Bolt (92), Split Washer (57), Flat Washer (56), Button Head Socket Bolt (94), Curved Washer (93), Button Head Socket Bolt (95), Split Washer (88)** and **Flat Washer (79)** onto the Left Upright (5).
3. **After completing STEP 1 to STEP 3, tighten all screws securely.**

**This is the step that closes the "do not tighten them yet" instruction** given in Steps 1 and 2. Nothing is tightened until all three uprights and the console support are in place.

The hardware for this step is bag 3, and it carries the highest count of any bag - four of item 92 and six of item 57. The manual prints **no torque figure** for any fastener.
