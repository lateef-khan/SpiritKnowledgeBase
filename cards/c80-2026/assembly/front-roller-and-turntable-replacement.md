1. **Front roller assembly.** Using **two 17 mm open-end wrenches**, remove the **four mounting screws and lock nuts** from the front roller, then remove the front roller assembly.
2. **PJ turntable.** Using a **3 mm Allen wrench**, loosen and remove the **set screw** on the PJ turntable, then replace it with a new one.
3. **The removal and installation procedures for the pulley and bearing housing follow steps 4 and 5** of the section "Replacing the Pedal, Rear Roller, Pillow Block Bearing, Pulley".
4. Reinstall by reversing the above steps.

**The speed sensor magnet lives on this roller.** After any work here, check that the gap between the speed sensor and the magnet is less than 2 mm before refitting the front right cover.
