1. Follow the section "Replacing the Pedal, Rear Roller, Pillow Block Bearing, Pulley" to **remove 2-3 pedals**, then **rotate the unit to the position requiring replacement**. There are 14 limit wheels around the track, so you turn the deck to bring the faulty one into the opening.
2. Using a **6 mm Allen wrench and a 13 mm open-end wrench**, remove the **belt idler pulley or 608 bearing** at the location requiring replacement, then replace it with a new one.
3. Reinstall by reversing the above steps.

**This section is missing from the table of contents**, which stops at "Replacing the Front Roller, PJ Turntable".

**The bearing size here is not in the parts list.** The heading and step 2 both name a **608 bearing**, but the parts list names only "Bearing (16) x120", "6203_Bearing (25) x2" and "6001_Bearing (27) x2". The 120 unnumbered bearings are the likely match, but the manual does not say so.
