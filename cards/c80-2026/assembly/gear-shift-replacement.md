1. Using a **flathead screwdriver**, gently pry open the **indicator cover** at the recessed area of the **rotary cover**.
2. **Move the shifter to the foremost position.** Using a Phillips screwdriver, remove the **two M5 x 12L pan head Phillips screws** inside the rotary cover.
3. Remove the shifter assembly from the **right upright tube**, then disconnect the cable assembly. The shifter assembly can then be removed and replaced with a new one.
4. Reinstall by reversing the above steps.

This is item **40, Gear Shift Assembly** in the parts list. It sets the resistance by pulling the steel cables on the magnetic resistance holders; there is no electrical resistance control on this machine.
