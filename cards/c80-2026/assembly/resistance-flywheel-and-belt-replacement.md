The three jobs run in order; the flywheel needs the resistance assembly off, and the belt needs the flywheel off.

1. **Resistance assembly.** Using a **5 mm Allen wrench and a 10 mm open-end wrench**, remove the **cable mounting screw**. Then use **8 mm and 13 mm open-end wrenches** to remove the **resistance assembly shaft**. Remove the resistance assembly and replace it with a new one.
2. **Flywheel.** After removing the resistance assembly, use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **six pan head Phillips screws** from the flywheel, then replace it with a new one.
3. **Belt.** After removing the flywheel, use an **11 mm open-end wrench** to loosen the **mounting screw of the belt guide pulley**. The belt can then be removed and replaced with a new one.
4. Reinstall by reversing the above steps.
