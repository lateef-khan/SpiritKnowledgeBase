1. Connect the **Connecting line (Upper) (48)** from the **Front armrest console support (6)** to the **control wire on the Console Assembly (47)**.
2. Using the **Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (97)** and the **Phillips Head Screw (78)**, attach the **Console Assembly (47)** to the Front armrest console support (6).
3. Then use the same wrench (97) and Phillips Head Screw (78) to attach the **Drink Bottle Holder (52)** to the **Left Upright (5)**. **Assembly is now complete.**

**Note printed with the step:** the **Magnetic buckle Assembly (53)** can be used to unlock the gear lock function. Use it as needed depending on your workout requirements.

**Screw counts are not printed here.** The bag holds **six** item 78 screws. The service manual's replacement procedure splits them as **four** into the console and **two** into the bottle holder, which accounts for all six - see `c80-2026-console-replacement`. This owner's manual states neither number, so the split above comes from the other manual, not from this page.
