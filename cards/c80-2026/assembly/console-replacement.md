1. Using a Phillips screwdriver, remove the **four M5 x 12L pan head Phillips screws** from the **front handlebar console bracket**. **Disconnect the wire harness**, then remove the console and replace it with a new one.
2. Using a Phillips screwdriver, remove the **two M5 x 12L pan head Phillips screws** from the front handlebar console bracket. The **bottle holder** can then be removed and replaced with a new one.
3. Reinstall by reversing the above steps.

The console is item **47, Console Assembly** in the parts list and the drink bottle holder is item **52**.
