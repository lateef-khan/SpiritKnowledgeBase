The four jobs in this section run in order; each later step needs the earlier ones.

1. **Pedal replacement.** Using an **8 mm open-end wrench**, remove the **M5 lock nuts and retaining washers from both sides**, then replace the pedal with a new one.
2. **Rear roller replacement.** Remove **the 10 pedals** by following step 1. Then, using **two 17 mm open-end wrenches**, loosen the **four mounting screws and lock nuts** of the rear roller assembly and remove the rear roller assembly.
3. Following steps 1 and 2, **the entire pedal assembly can be removed**.
4. **Pillow block bearing replacement.** Using a **3 mm Allen wrench**, loosen the **set screw** on the pillow block bearing, then remove the bearing and replace it with a new one.
5. **Pulley replacement.** Using a **3 mm Allen wrench**, loosen the **set screw** on the pulley. Then use **snap ring pliers** to remove the retaining ring (C-clip), and replace the pulley with a new one.
6. Reinstall by reversing the above steps.

**The deck carries 60 pedal slats in total** (parts list item 19), so the "10 pedals" in step 2 is the number that must come off to free the rear roller, not the number on the machine.

Steps 4 and 5 of this section are the ones the front roller procedure sends you back to for its pulley and bearing housing.
