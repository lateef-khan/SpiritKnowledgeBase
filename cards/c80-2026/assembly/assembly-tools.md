Two tools are supplied and the manual names no others:

| Item | Tool |
|---|---|
| 97 | M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench |
| 96 | L Allen Wrench |

Item 97 is the tool the manual uses for the Phillips head screws in Assembly Step 4 as well - it calls for "the Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (97) and the Phillips Head Screw (78)", so that tool carries a Phillips bit.

**No size is printed for the L Allen Wrench (96)**, and the manual does not say a customer needs any tool of their own. Assembly Steps 2 and 3 write "the L Allen Wrench (96, 97)", which pairs the L wrench label with item 97 even though item 97 is listed separately as the M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench.
