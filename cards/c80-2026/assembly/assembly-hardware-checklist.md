**Bag 4 - printed as "Hardware Step 5"**

| Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 78 | M5 x P0.8 x 12mm Phillips Head Screw | 6 |

**Watch the numbering on the fourth bag.** The checklist labels it item 4 in its own sequence but heads it **"HARDWARE STEP 5"**, while the assembly instruction that uses it is **Assembly Step 4** and calls for **"HARDWARE STEP 4"**. There are only four bags and only one of them holds the Phillips screws, so the fourth bag is the one Assembly Step 4 needs, whatever the heading says.

The Ø diameter symbol is written **O** above because the printed symbol does not survive extraction cleanly.

The manual also prints a **"Scan Here for Assembly Video"** QR code on page 4. The extraction does not carry the code image or its address.
