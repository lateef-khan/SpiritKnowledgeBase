The three parts come off in a fixed order: left upright, then front handlebar console bracket, then right upright.

1. **Left upright.** Using **5 mm and 6 mm L-shaped Allen wrenches**, remove the socket head cap screws from the **three locations**. Lift the left upright upward to remove it, then replace it with a new one.
2. **Front handlebar console bracket.** After removing the left upright tube, use **5 mm and 6 mm L-shaped Allen wrenches** to remove the socket head cap screws from the **two locations**. **Disconnect the wire harness**, then replace the bracket with a new one.
3. **Right upright.** After removing the left upright and the front handlebar console bracket, use a **6 mm L-shaped Allen wrench** to remove the socket head cap screws. Lift the upright upward, **disconnect the cable assembly and the wire harness**, then replace it with a new one.
4. Reinstall by reversing the above steps.

The right upright carries the gear shift cable as well as the console wiring, which is why it is the one with a cable assembly to disconnect.
