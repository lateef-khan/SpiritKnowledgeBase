1. Place the **Right Upright (4)** on the right side of the treadmill. **Make sure the Gear Shift Assembly (40) is positioned at the end of the arrow direction.** Connect the **Connecting line (Middle) (49)** and **Steel Cable (38)** from the bottom of the Right Upright (4) to the **Connecting line (Lower) (50)** and **Steel Cable (39)** on the **Main Frame (1)**.
2. After connecting, install the Right Upright (4) onto the **Main frame right side (1)**. Using the **L Allen Wrench (96)**, insert the **Button Head Socket Bolt (92), Split Washer (57), Flat Washer (56), Button Head Socket Bolt (94)** and **Curved Washer (93)** onto the Main Frame, **but do not tighten them yet.**

**Leave every bolt loose.** Steps 1, 2 and 3 all fit loose, and the instruction to tighten comes only at the end of Step 3: "After completing STEP 1 to STEP 3, tighten all screws securely."

The hardware for this step is bag 1. The item numbers above are parts list numbers from the service manual, so `c80-2026-parts-list` resolves any of them.
