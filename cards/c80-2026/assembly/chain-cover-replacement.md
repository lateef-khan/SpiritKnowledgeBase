1. **Remove the upper upright tube first.**
2. Using a Phillips screwdriver, remove the screws from the chain cover, then replace the chain cover as needed.
3. Reinstall by reversing the above steps.

There are four covers in the parts list: **Front Chain Cover (R) 43**, **Rear Chain Cover (R) 44**, **Front Chain Cover (L) 45** and **Rear Chain Cover (L) 46**. The manual does not print a screw count or size for them; the tapping screws in the list are Ø5 x 16mm (90) and Ø5 x 30mm (98).

**The front right cover is the one to remove to reach the speed sensor.**
