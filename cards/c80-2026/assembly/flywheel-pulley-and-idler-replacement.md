1. **Flywheel pulley.** Using a **5 mm Allen wrench**, remove the **fixing screw** of the flywheel pulley and take out the pulley assembly. **If bearing replacement is required**, use **snap ring pliers** to remove the **C-clip** and replace the bearing.
2. **Belt idler pulley.** Using a **6 mm Allen wrench**, remove the **mounting bracket** of the belt idler pulley and take out the assembly. If bearing replacement is required, use snap ring pliers to remove the C-clip and replace the bearing.
3. Reinstall by reversing the above steps.

The parts list carries **6203 bearings x2 (25)** and **6001 bearings x2 (27)** alongside the **Flywheel Pulley (26)**, plus the **Idler Adjustment Carriage Bolt (22)** and **Idler Axle (23)**.
