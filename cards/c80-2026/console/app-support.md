The manual names **three** apps:

- **SOLE+ APP**
- **Kinomap APP**
- **ZWIFT APP**

That is the whole statement. **No pairing procedure is printed** - the manual gives no console menu, no pairing code, no Bluetooth name, no supported phone or operating system, and no note on which readings each app receives. If a customer asks how to pair, this manual does not answer it.
