**This product supports Bluetooth heart rate belts.** Wear the heart rate belt correctly.

That is all the page says. **No pairing procedure, no protocol name and no compatible-device list is printed here.**

Three other cards carry the rest of the heart rate story, and none of them repeats this one:

- Wearing an optional Sole wireless chest strap: `c80-2026-chest-strap-wearing-instructions`
- Wild or missing readings and what interferes with them: `c80-2026-chest-strap-erratic-operation`
- Whether a Garmin device can be used: `c80-2026-garmin-heart-rate-compatibility`

**There are no hand pulse grips described anywhere in this manual.** PULSE on the display is fed by a belt.
