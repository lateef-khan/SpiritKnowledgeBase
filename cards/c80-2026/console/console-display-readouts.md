| Display | Description | Range |
|---|---|---|
| **TIME** | Exercise Time | 0:00~99:59 min~sec |
| **DIST** | Exercise Distance | 0.00~99.99 km (miles) |
| **PULSE** | Exercise PULSE | Heart Rate 40~240 bpm |
| **CALO** | Exercise Calories | 0-9999 kcal |
| **SPEED** | Exercise Speed | 0.0~99.9 km (miles) |

Two cautions on this table:

- The page prints it as **two side-by-side column groups**, and the extraction interleaves their headers into one mangled row. TIME, DIST, PULSE and CALO are in the left group and SPEED alone is in the right group; each label, its description and its range sit together on one printed line, which is how the rows above are recovered.
- The distance and speed rows are printed as **"km (miles)"**. The manual never says which unit the console is set to, and it prints **no procedure for switching between metric and imperial** anywhere.

The manual states no accuracy figure for any reading, and the total distance and total time counters are separate from these - they are on their own card.
