1. In the **IDEL** mode, press **both the "Set" button and the "Mode" button for 3 seconds** to enter the **ODO** display.
2. The console shows **total distance and total time**.
3. **Hold the "Start/Pause" button to reset the data to 0.**
4. Press **both the "Set" button and the "Mode" button for 3 seconds again** to return to the IDEL mode.

**The reset is not confirmed and not reversible in the manual** - there is no "are you sure" step printed, and no way to read the old figure back. Warn a customer before they hold Start/Pause on this screen.

The manual writes the first button as **"Set"** here and as **"Settings"** in the button table on page 10. It is the same button.
