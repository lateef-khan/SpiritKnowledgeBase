1. The system **enters sleep mode after 1 minute of inactivity and no operations**. The display turns off.
2. **Any operation wakes it up.**

**A dark display is not a fault on its own.** Press any button, or start moving the belt, before treating it as a blank-screen problem. If the console stays dark after an operation, the blank-screen diagnosis starts at the batteries - see `c80-2026-blank-screen`.

The manual gives **no way to change or disable the one-minute timeout**, and no separate deeper power-off state.
