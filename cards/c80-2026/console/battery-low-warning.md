1. **When the battery icon flashes, the battery is about to run out.** Replace the batteries in time.
2. **Push the battery cover upward to remove it.**
3. **Replace the 4 AA batteries** following the direction shown in the illustration.
4. Reinstall the battery cover.

The illustration showing the cell orientation does not survive extraction, so check the polarity marks moulded into the compartment.

This card is the warning and the access method. The card `c80-2026-console-batteries`, taken from the service manual, covers what the cells are and where the compartment is - it puts the cover **on the rear of the console**, which is consistent with pushing it upward here. Neither manual mentions the other's detail, so read both.

**Do not confuse these cells with the chest strap transmitter battery**, which is a single Panasonic CR2032 and has its own card.
