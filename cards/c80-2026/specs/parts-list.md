**Three faults in the printed numbering, left as printed.**

- **Item 1 is used twice**, once for "Frame Welding" and once for "Main frame right side". Every later frame part may therefore be one number out from the exploded view.
- **There is no item 14** - the list runs 13 PJ turntable, then 15 Sleeve.
- **There is no item 55** - the list runs 54 M8 x 45mm Button Head Socket Bolt, then 56 Flat Washer.

Check a number against the exploded view before ordering.
