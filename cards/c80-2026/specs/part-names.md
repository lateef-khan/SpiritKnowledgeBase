The labelled photograph on page 3 names thirteen parts. Use these names when reading the parts replacing guide.

| No. | Name | No. | Name |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Console | 8 | Drink Bottle Holder |
| 2 | Gear Shift Assembly | 9 | Left Upright |
| 3 | Front armrest console support | 10 | Pedal |
| 4 | Right Upright | 11 | Rear Chain Cover (R) |
| 5 | Front Chain Cover (R) | 12 | Foot Pad |
| 6 | Moving Wheel | 13 | Handle |
| 7 | Foot Pad | | |

**These numbers are not the parts list numbers.** The main parts indication numbers 1 to 13 belong to the photograph only. In the parts list the console is item 47, the gear shift assembly is item 40 and the handle is item 7. Quote the parts list number when ordering.

Two other naming differences to know: **Foot Pad appears twice** in this list, as 7 and as 12; and the part the photograph calls the **Moving Wheel** is called the **Transportation Wheel** in the parts list.
