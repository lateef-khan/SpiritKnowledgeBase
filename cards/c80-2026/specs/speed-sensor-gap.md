**The gap between the speed sensor and the magnet on the front roller must be less than 2 mm.**

To see it, remove the **front right plastic cover of the main frame** using the parts replacing guide.

If the sensor is correctly positioned and there is still no reading, **the speed sensor should be replaced**. The full no-reading diagnosis, starting at the cable, is in its own card.
