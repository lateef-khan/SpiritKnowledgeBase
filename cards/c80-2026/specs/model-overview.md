The cover reads **C80**, **TK700-SK001**, **MANUAL CURVED TREADMILL**.

**This machine has no drive motor and no incline motor.** The belt is a curved slat deck the user drives with their own feet. Nothing in the manual describes a motor, a controller, a speed control, an incline mechanism, a safety key, a lubrication schedule or a mains power supply, and there is no error code list. **Do not apply powered-treadmill procedures or error codes to it.**

What it does have:

- A **battery-powered console** - four AA cells in the back of the console. There is no mains cord.
- A **Gear Shift Assembly** on the right upright, a lever that sets resistance through a steel cable.
- **Magnetic resistance**: magnetic resistance holders, outer magnetic resistance holders, eight braking magnets and magnet support shafts, pulled by a 790mm and a 185mm steel cable.
- A **speed sensor** reading a magnet on the front roller, wired to the console by three connecting lines - 650mm upper, 1950mm middle, 250mm lower.
- A flywheel, flywheel pulley, drive belt, belt idler pulley, front and rear rollers, 60 pedal slats on a crawler-type track, limit wheels and a PJ turntable.

**No SKU has been matched to this manual.** "C80" and "TK700-SK001" do not appear in the Spirit database under brand Sole, so do not quote a six digit model number for it.

The manual is at **Version 1.0, initial release 2026/6/10**, and runs to 17 pages.
