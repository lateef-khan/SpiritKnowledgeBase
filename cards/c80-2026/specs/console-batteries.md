The console runs on **four AA batteries**. **They are located on the back of the console**, behind a battery cover on the rear.

To change them, open the battery cover on the rear of the console and **replace all four AA batteries with new ones**. The manual says to replace all four, not just the flat one.

**The machine has no mains cord.** Depleted batteries are the usual cause of a blank screen, and the blank-screen card carries the rest of that diagnosis.
