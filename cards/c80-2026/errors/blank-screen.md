**This is usually caused by depleted batteries.** This machine has no mains supply; the console runs on batteries alone.

1. Open the **battery cover on the rear of the console**.
2. **Replace all four AA batteries with new ones.**
3. If the batteries are confirmed to have sufficient power and the screen still does not display anything after installing new ones, **the console is defective and should be replaced**.

The manual lists no other cause and no error code for a blank screen.
