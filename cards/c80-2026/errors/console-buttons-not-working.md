The manual gives one instruction, printed as a note under the troubleshooting section:

**"If the console buttons are damaged or not functioning, please replace the console with a new one."**

There is no key test, no repair of individual buttons and no separate keypad part in the parts list - the **Console Assembly (47)** is a single part.
