- **Warranties outside of North America may vary.**
- **Sole USA warranty coverage is limited to the Continental United States.**
- **Sole USA does not sell or service equipment in Hawaii, Alaska, or outside of the United States.**
- For warranty availability in any other region, **contact your local dealer**.

So Hawaii and Alaska are outside Sole USA's coverage even though they are US states, and "may vary" is as far as the manual goes for the rest of North America - it names no other country's terms.
