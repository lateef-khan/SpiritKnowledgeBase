**"Warranty will be void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment. (Examples: Garage, outside, carport, etc)"**

This is stronger than an exclusion: it voids the warranty for the whole machine, not just the damaged part, and the three examples the manual gives are a **garage, outside, and a carport**.

**It is also stricter than the safety limits.** The safety page allows operation up to 40 degrees C and 95% non-condensing humidity - meeting those figures does not satisfy this clause, because an uninsulated garage can sit inside them and still void the warranty. See `c80-2026-operating-environment`.

Exclusion 8 runs alongside it: the warranty does not cover product use in any environment other than a residential setting.
