The warranty runs **from the date of retail sale, as determined by the sale receipt**.

**With no receipt, it runs from eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date** instead. That is a fixed substitute start point, not an extension: a machine with no receipt is treated as having been sold 18 months after it left the factory.

**Keep the receipt.** The service section says the same in different words: keep your bill of sale, because twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale, or eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number, establishes the labour warranty period. Both halves are printed on the same page; the labour-period wording has its own card.

The warranty text is dated **effective February 1, 2026**.
