All nine exclusions as printed.

1. **Consequential, collateral or incidental damages** such as property damage and incidental expenses resulting from any breach of this written or any implied warranty. *Note printed with it: some states do not allow this exclusion or limitation, so it may not apply to you.*
2. **Service call reimbursement to the consumer.** Service call reimbursement to the dealer is also excluded where it does not involve malfunction or defects in workmanship or material, for units beyond the warranty period, for units beyond the service call reimbursement period, for machines not requiring component replacement, or for machines not in ordinary household use.
3. **Damages caused by service performed by anyone other than an authorised SOLE service company; use of parts other than original SOLE parts; or external causes** - corrosion (RUST), discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance including lack of consistent lubrication of the deck, inadequate power supply, natural disasters, or elemental damage such as power surges, lightning storms, water damage or fire damage.
4. **Products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered.**
5. **Products that have been sold, transferred, bartered or given to a third party.** Warranties do not transfer - see `c80-2026-warranty-transfer`.
6. **Products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at SOLE.** SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists - see `c80-2026-warranty-registration`.
7. **This warranty is expressly in lieu of all other warranties expressed or implied**, including the warranties of merchantability and/or fitness for a particular purpose.
8. **Product use in any environment other than a residential setting** - see `c80-2026-residential-use-only`.
9. **Warranties outside of North America may vary** - see `c80-2026-warranty-geographic-coverage`.

Printed after the nine: **the warranty is void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment**, with a garage, outside, or a carport named as examples. That has its own card.

**Exclusion 3 names lubrication of the deck.** This manual documents no lubrication anywhere and the maintenance section prescribes none - see `c80-2026-cleaning-schedule` for that conflict.
