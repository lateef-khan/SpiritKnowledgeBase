Effective **February 1, 2026**.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Frame** | **Lifetime** |
| **Parts & Labor** | **1 Year** |
| **Cosmetic Items** | **90 days** |

**Cosmetic items** include, but are not limited to, **grips, console overlays, and labels/decals**.

SOLE's responsibilities are **providing new or remanufactured parts, at SOLE's option, and technical support** to its independent dealers and servicing organisations. Where there is no dealer or service organisation, SOLE administers the warranty directly to the consumer.

**The warranty covers only residential use** and there are conditions that void it entirely - each is on its own card. When the clock starts is on `c80-2026-warranty-start-date`, and the SERVICE paragraph on the same page restates the labour period in different words on `c80-2026-labor-warranty-period`.
