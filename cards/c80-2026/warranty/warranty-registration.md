**The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.

**Missing the 10 days is not fatal.** Outside the 10-day period the warranty can still be registered **if you have proof of purchase** - the manual names a receipt, a bank statement, a credit card statement, or an email order confirmation.

Exclusion 6 is the other half of this rule: the warranty does not cover **products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at SOLE**, and **SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists** for the product.

**How to register** - by card or online - is on `c80-2026-product-registration`. This card is the deadline and the consequence; that one is the method.
