Two ways to register:

1. **Complete the self-addressed product registration card in full and return it to SOLE.**
2. **Or register online** at **www.soletreadmills.com** under the **support** tab.

**Record the serial number** of the product in the space provided on page 2 before you do either. Page 1 also gives a panel to record the **name of the dealer, the dealer's telephone number, and the purchase date** for easy contact in future.

**The manual never says where the serial number sticker is on the machine.** It gives a space to write it but no location, so this manual cannot answer "where do I find it".

**Registration is time limited** - the card has to be back within 10 days of purchase to validate the warranty, and after that proof of purchase is needed instead. That rule and its exclusion are on `c80-2026-warranty-registration`.
