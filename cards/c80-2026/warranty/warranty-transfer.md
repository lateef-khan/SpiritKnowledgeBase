**No. Warranties do not transfer.**

The warranty does not cover **products that have been sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party**. The manual prints "(Warranties DO NOT transfer.)" beside that exclusion.

So a second owner has no manufacturer's warranty on the machine, however much of the original period is left, and a machine given away as a gift after purchase is in the same position.

The related exclusion is **item 6** - a product with no warranty registration card on file at SOLE is also excluded, and registration is in the original purchaser's name.
