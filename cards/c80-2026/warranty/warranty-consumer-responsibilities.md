The warranty applies **only to products in ordinary household use**. The consumer is responsible for:

- Returning the **warranty registration card within 10 days**, or registering later with proof of purchase.
- **Proper use in accordance with the instructions in this manual, including general maintenance** - the page lists this as *"belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under motor cover, and lubrication"*.
- **Proper installation** in accordance with the instructions provided and **with all local electric codes**.
- **Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage, replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring.**
- **Expenses for making the machine accessible for servicing**, including any item that was not part of it when it shipped from the factory.
- **Damages to the finish during shipping, installation or following installation.**
- **Routine maintenance** as specified in this manual.
- **Providing pictures or video of any issues by email** as requested by the service technician, and **assisting with troubleshooting and diagnosing prior to a home visit**.

**Two of these do not describe this machine, and both are printed as they stand.**

- The maintenance list names **belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under a motor cover, and lubrication**. The General Maintenance section on page 17 prescribes none of those; it prescribes monthly vacuuming and weekly wiping only, and this machine has **no motor and no motor cover**. See `c80-2026-cleaning-schedule`.
- The electrical items name a **grounded power supply, blown fuses, house wiring and local electric codes**. The machine has **no mains cord**; the console runs on four AA cells. See `c80-2026-console-batteries`.

No winner is picked here. Both passages are printed, and a technician quoting the warranty should quote the tasks the manual actually documents.
