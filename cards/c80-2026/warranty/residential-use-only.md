**The warranty is residential only.** Two statements say so:

- The warranty **applies only to products in ordinary household use**.
- Exclusion 8: the warranty does not cover **product use in any environment other than a residential setting**.

Exclusion 2 adds the practical consequence - service call reimbursement to the dealer is excluded for machines **not in ordinary household use**.

**The manual gives no light-commercial rating, no hours-per-day or hours-per-week figure, and no commercial warranty option** for this machine. Putting one in a gym, a workplace, a hotel or an apartment building's shared room removes warranty coverage; the manual does not say the machine is unsafe there, only that it is not covered.
