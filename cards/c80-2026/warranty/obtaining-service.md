The manual says service must be obtained **by following these steps, in order**:

1. **Contact your selling authorised SOLE dealer.** OR
2. **Submit the service request online** at **www.soletreadmills.com/serviceparts.html**, including the **serial number, contact information and a brief description of the problem**.
3. If there is a question as to where to obtain service, **contact the SOLE service department on 866-780-7653**.
4. **If no local service is available**, SOLE will repair or replace the parts, at SOLE's option, within the warranty period **at no charge for parts**. **All transportation costs, both to the factory and on return, are the owner's responsibility**, and so is adequate packaging. SOLE is not responsible for damage that occurs during shipping - make freight damage claims with the freight carrier. **Do not ship any unit without a return authorisation number.**
5. **The service department assists in troubleshooting before sending a field technician**, and SOLE may request pictures and video by email of the issue to help with diagnosing.

**Expect the troubleshooting step.** Step 5 and the consumer responsibilities both say a home visit comes after remote diagnosis, and supplying photographs or video on request is listed as the owner's obligation.

The return authorisation rule in step 4 is strict enough to have its own card.
