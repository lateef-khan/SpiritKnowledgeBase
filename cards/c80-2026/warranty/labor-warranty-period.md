**Keep your bill of sale.**

**Twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale**, or **eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number**, establishes the labour warranty period should service be required.

If service is performed, it is in your best interest to obtain and keep all receipts.

**This is the second place the manual states the same period.** The warranty table on the same page lists **Parts & Labor - 1 Year**, and the paragraph above it gives the receipt-or-shipping start rule; this SERVICE paragraph is where the serial number is named as the way the factory shipping date is determined. Neither passage refers to the other, so read both - the table gives the length and this gives the way the start date is proved.

This written warranty gives you specific legal rights, and you may also have other rights that vary from state to state.
