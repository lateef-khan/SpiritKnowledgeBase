**Lock the machine with the safety magnet key before either task.** The manual prints this in capitals.

| Task | How often |
|---|---|
| **Vacuum the treadmill** | **monthly** |
| **Wipe the belt** with a soft damp cloth or paper towel | **weekly** |

The reason given: dirt, dust and hair can block air inlets and accumulate on the slat belt, and vacuuming prevents excess build-up of dirt that can get sucked up.

**That is the entire maintenance section - two tasks and no others.** It prescribes no lubrication, no belt tension adjustment, no belt tracking adjustment, no bolt-retightening interval and no parts inspection.

**The warranty page disagrees with this.** On page 18 the consumer's responsibilities list general maintenance as *"belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under motor cover, and lubrication"*, and exclusion 3 excludes damage from *"lack of consistent lubrication of the deck"*. None of those four tasks appears in this maintenance section, and this machine has no motor and therefore no motor cover. Both passages are printed in the same manual. This card picks no winner: the maintenance section is the only place that gives an actual schedule, and the warranty page names tasks the manual never explains how to do.
