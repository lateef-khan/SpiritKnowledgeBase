**"When the product is not used for a long time, the batteries should be removed to prevent battery leakage that could damage the product."**

The manual sets **no threshold** for "a long time" and gives no other storage instruction - no cover, no position, no temperature figure for storage.

The batteries are the **four AA cells** behind the console cover; how to reach them is on `c80-2026-battery-low-warning`.
