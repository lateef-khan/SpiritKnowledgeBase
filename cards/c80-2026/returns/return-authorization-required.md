**DO NOT SHIP ANY UNIT TO THE FACTORY WITHOUT A RETURN AUTHORIZATION NUMBER. All units arriving without a return authorisation number will be refused.**

The rest of the rule, as printed:

- **All transportation costs, both to the factory and on return to the owner, are the responsibility of the owner.**
- **The owner is responsible for adequate packaging** on the return to SOLE.
- **SOLE is not responsible for damages that occur during shipping.** Make all freight damage claims with the appropriate freight carrier.
- Parts themselves are at no charge within the warranty period, at SOLE's option to repair or replace, and this route applies **only where no local service is available**.

**The manual does not say how to obtain the number.** It gives no form, no department and no deadline for it - the route into the process is the service steps on `c80-2026-obtaining-service`.

This is a repair return, not a consumer right of return. **A machine being taken back to a shop is a different question** and is on `c80-2026-call-before-returning-to-retailer`.
