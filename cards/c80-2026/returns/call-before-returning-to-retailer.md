**"Before returning your Sole product to any retailer, or if you require any assistance with assembly or technical support please call us first for assistance at 866-780-7653."**

The manual prints this panel **twice** - once on the cover and once as the closing panel of the warranty section - which is as close as it comes to an instruction to call before a return.

**It is not a returns policy.** The manual sets **no return window, no restocking fee, no refund terms and no condition requirements**; a retail return is the retailer's policy, not this manual's. What the manual offers is the same number for assembly help and technical support before the customer gives up on the machine.

**Sending a unit to the factory is a separate process** with a return authorisation number that the factory will refuse a shipment without - see `c80-2026-return-authorization-required`.
