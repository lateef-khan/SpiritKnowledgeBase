Model 563812. AC switch to controller path.

| Item | Ref | Part number | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power cord | 51 | E060001 | LT-202+511 SJT 14A/3C black 105D 2M |
| Power socket | 43 | F010007 | DB-14/15A/125VAC. White, black and green ground wires already attached. Black runs to the breaker, white to the switch |
| Breaker | 42 | F020011 | |
| Black wire, 100mm | 45 | E010747 | Breaker to A/C switch |
| A/C switch | 44 | F030001 | On/Off switch RF-1004-NBR#W1A 5mmT (F030008) |
| White wire, 300mm | 46 | E010754 | Switch to controller |
| Black wire, 300mm | 47 | E010755 | Switch to controller |
| Motor controller | 36-1 | D020105 | YJ-2256L (PA-AE00070L) without adaptor |
| Adaptor | 36-2 | D022218 | Transformer, Z controller (115V) choke filter |

**Computer cables**

| Item | Ref | Part number | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Upper | 48 | E020002 | 800 mm, for XT 185 and 285 models upper/lower |
| Middle | 50 | E020052 | 1200 mm. E020053 may be substituted if stock is zero. **Check the serial number for the correct version on 563812 and 560813** |
| Lower | 49 | E020048 | 1400 mm. This one plugs into the motor controller |
