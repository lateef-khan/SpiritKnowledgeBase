Dirt build-up on the **rear rails** and **polyurethane wheels** is a source of noise. The build-up
can make a thumping sound that seems to come from inside the main body of the machine, because
noise travels and is amplified in the tubing of the frame.

1. Clean the rails and wheels with a **lint free cloth and rubbing alcohol**.
2. Stubborn build-up can be removed with your thumbnail or a **non-metallic scraper**, like the
   back edge of a plastic knife.
3. **After cleaning, apply a small amount of lubricant on the rails** with your fingers or a lint
   free cloth. You only need a **thin coat**; wipe off any excess.

**This is the only Spirit elliptical owner's-manual procedure that asks for a lubricant.** The
owner's manuals name no brand, type or part number for it, and give no interval; the 2008 dealer
service manual for the XE100-XE500 does give a part number, 022271, see below. The 2016 service
manuals for the residential XE, CE800 and CE850 ask for lubricant on the swing arm axle, the
connecting-arm joints and the rail as well - those are the `spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-*` cards. Do not substitute a treadmill deck
lubricant: **an elliptical has no tread belt and no deck**, and none of the Spirit tread-belt or
deck lubrication cards applies to these machines. None of the eleven residential XE owner's
manuals contains the words deck, tread belt, running belt, silicone or wax.

The CE900 manuals print the same cleaning steps for their **aluminium tracks** and stop before the
lubricant - see `spirit-ce900-maintenance-track-and-wheel-cleaning`. The CE800 2021/2023, CE800ENT
and all three CE850 manuals print no rail or track cleaning at all.

**The XE795 and the XE895 print none of this.** The XE795 2018 printing, both XE795 2021 printings
and the XE895 2018 manual drop the paragraph and go straight from loose hardware to the crank arm
nut; the 2023 XE395 and XE795 manuals replaced the whole chapter with a four-step post-workout
routine; and the XE150/XE350/XE550 manual has no maintenance chapter at all. Do not quote a rail
lubricant for any of those machines.

**Nothing changed between the printings that do carry it.** The XE395 2018 and the XE195, XE295 and
XG400 2019 books print this paragraph word for word with their 2021 reprints - same cloth, same
alcohol, same thin coat, and still no brand, type, part number or interval for the lubricant.

## The 2008 dealer manual: two troubleshooting rows and a part number for the lubricant

The Spirit Manufacturing dealer service manual that covers the **XE100, XE200, XE300, XE400 and
XE500** does not print the owner's-manual paragraph. It prints the same care as two rows of a
mechanical troubleshooting table:

| Symptom | Remedy, in the book's order |
|---|---|
| **Thumping noise** | 1. **Clean and lube wheels and rails.** 2. If there is still a thump, check for loose bolts. 3. If there is still a thump, order new pedal arm bushing housings with IGUS plastic bushing (printed "IGOS") |
| **Slide wheels jumping off of the track** | 1. **Clean wheels and tracks with rubbing alcohol. All of the sticky and/or dirty lube must be removed from both the wheels and the tracks.** 2. If the aluminum track has been damaged, order a new aluminum track |

So on these five machines the dealer book confirms both halves of the owner's paragraph - alcohol
on the wheels and the tracks, then lubricant - and adds the warning that **old lubricant gone
sticky is itself the cause of a wheel leaving the track**, so it comes off completely before fresh
goes on. The third thump remedy is a part swap and belongs to the errors section.

**The dealer book also names the lubricant by part number.** Every one of its five parts lists
carries a row **"Lubricant - 022271"** (item 112 on the XE100, XE200 and XE300, item 138 on the
XE400 and XE500). No brand or type is printed with it, but it is an orderable Spirit part, which no
owner's manual gives. The XE400 and XE500 have no owner's manual in the repository; this dealer
book is their only statement of the procedure.

The same dealer book's one adjustment, the brake cable for a resistance that is too high at level
one, is `spirit-xe-2007-maintenance-brake-cable-adjustment-for-resistance-too-high`. The 2016
service manuals for the later XE machines answer a rough motion with the same alcohol wipe and
re-lubrication (`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-rough-motion-loose-screws-then-alcohol-wipe-and-re-lubricate-the-rail`).
