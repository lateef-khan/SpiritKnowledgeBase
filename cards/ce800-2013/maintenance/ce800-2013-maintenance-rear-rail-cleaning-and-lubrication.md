Dirt build-up on the **rear rails** and **polyurethane wheels** is a source of noise. The build-up
can make a thumping sound that seems to come from inside the main body of the machine, because
noise travels and is amplified in the tubing of the frame.

1. Clean the rails and wheels with a **lint free cloth and rubbing alcohol**.
2. Stubborn build-up can be removed with your thumbnail or a **non-metallic scraper**, like the
   back edge of a plastic knife.
3. **After cleaning, apply a small amount of lubricant on the rails** with your fingers or a lint
   free cloth. You only need a **thin coat**; wipe off any excess.

**This is the only Spirit elliptical procedure that asks for a lubricant.** The manuals name no
brand, type or part number for it, and give no interval. Do not substitute a treadmill deck
lubricant: **an elliptical has no tread belt and no deck**, and none of the Spirit tread-belt or
deck lubrication cards applies to these machines. None of the eleven residential XE owner's
manuals contains the words deck, tread belt, running belt, silicone or wax.

The CE900 manuals print the same cleaning steps for their **aluminium tracks** and stop before the
lubricant - see `spirit-ce900-maintenance-track-and-wheel-cleaning`. The CE800 2021/2023, CE800ENT
and all three CE850 manuals print no rail or track cleaning at all.

**Three residential XE manuals also print none of this.** The XE795 2021 February and November
printings drop the paragraph and go straight from loose hardware to the crank arm nut, and the
2023 XE395 and XE795 manuals replaced the whole chapter with a four-step post-workout routine. The
XE150/XE350/XE550 manual has no maintenance chapter at all. Do not quote a rail lubricant for any
of those six machines.
