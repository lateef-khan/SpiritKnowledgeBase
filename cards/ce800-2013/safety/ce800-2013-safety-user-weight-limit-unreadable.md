**Do not quote a user weight limit for a CE800-2013.**

The manual does print one - it is the last bullet of its safety instructions, in the same position
as on every later CE800 - but this manual survives only as an **image-only scan read by OCR**, and
the figure in that bullet cannot be verified. No PDF is on disk to check it against.

Two things about it are known and neither settles the number:

- **The unit is metric.** The bullet reads `kg`, and `kg` is not a plausible misreading of `lb`.
  Every later CE800 manual prints the limit in pounds.
- **It is not the same figure as the later manuals.** The 2021 and 2023 CE800 manuals print
  `Maximum User Weight: 450 lb` (`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`), which is 204 kg.
  The digits in this scan do not read as 204, so 450 lb must not be carried back onto a 2013
  machine either.

The rest of that page's OCR carries visible damage - the running header comes out as
`CE8O00 Elliptical Trainer` and the bullet markers as stray punctuation - which is why a bare digit
on it is not evidence.

**What to tell a customer:** the manual states a limit but this copy of it cannot be read reliably,
so confirm the figure against the printed page or with Spirit Fitness before relying on it. Do not
offer a number from another CE800 manual as a substitute.

The rest of this manual's safety list is legible and is carried on
`spirit-ce800-safety-instructions-list`.
