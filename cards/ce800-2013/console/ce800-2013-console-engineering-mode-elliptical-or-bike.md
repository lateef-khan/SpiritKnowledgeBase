The console has built-in maintenance/diagnostic software - it can switch the display between English
and Metric, and turn off the key beep, among other things.

**Press and hold Start, Stop and Enter for about 5 seconds.** The Message Center displays
**Engineering Mode Menu**. Press **Enter** to open the menu.

- **a. Key Test** - test all the keys to make sure they are functioning
- **b. Security** - locks the keypad so no unauthorised use is allowed. *Off to turn On, press Level
  Up, then the Enter key.*
- **c. Functions** (press Enter to access settings)
  1. **Sleep Mode** - turn on to have the console power down automatically after **20 minutes of
     inactivity**
  2. **Elliptical or Bike** - sets the console software calculations
  3. **DA Test** - tests the brake resistance
  4. **Beep** - the beep when a key is pressed
  5. **Units** - English or Metric
  6. **Odometer Reset**
  7. **Pause Mode** - on allows 5 minutes of pause; off makes the console pause indefinitely
- **d. Display Test** - tests all the display functions

**The Roman numerals in the source are OCR-damaged** - they read i, ii, ii, iv, v, vi, vi. The order
of the seven entries above is the printed order; only the numbering is unreadable, and it has been
renumbered 1-7 here rather than guessed at.

**Twenty minutes, not thirty.** The CE850-2016 menu is laid out almost identically but prints a
**30-minute** sleep, and has a SAFETY entry where this one has Elliptical or Bike:
`ce850-2016-console-engineering-mode-security-lock`.

**An Elliptical-or-Bike setting also exists on the 2025 CE900**, where it is called **Unit Type** and
sits at the top level of a flat nine-item menu:
`ce900-2025-console-engineering-mode-unit-type`.
