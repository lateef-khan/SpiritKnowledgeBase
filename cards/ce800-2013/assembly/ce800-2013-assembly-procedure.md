**This manual is an OCR of an image-only scan.** Numbers below are given only where
the scan reads cleanly. Where it does not, this card says so rather than guessing.

## Step 1 - rear rail and pedal arms

1. Slide the rail assembly (2) under the rear oval stabilizer tube of the main body.
   Bolt the two together with two bolts (106) and washers (109) from the top of the
   tube, and four bolts (107) and curved washers (108) through the front of the oval
   stabilizer. Make sure all six bolts are tightened very securely.
2. Attach the two footpads (48 left & 49 right) to the pedal mounting plates with
   eight screws (110).
3. Assemble the pedal arm assemblies (5 & 6) to the crank axles of the main body. The
   bearings should slide onto the axles - do not force them. Secure the pedal arms
   with two bolts (97) and washers (87).
4. Install the two covers (45 right, 46 left) over the pedal arm connection at the
   axle with two screws (103). Install the two wheel covers (50) with four screws
   (78).
5. Install the four mounting brackets (17) and (26) to the rails with four bolts
   (76). Install the two steel covers (15 right & 16 left): lift the pedal arms so
   the wheels are off the tracks, slide the pedal arms wheels-first through the
   opening of the steel cover, align the cover with the mounting brackets and secure
   with eight screws (78). Install the four small covers (2 x 19 & 2 x 20) with ten
   screws (78).
6. Install the centre aluminium step rail (72) with two bolts (107) and curved
   washers (108) at the rear and two bolts (106) and flat washers (109) at the front.
   Install the two stabilizer tube covers (64 left & 65 right) with two screws (78).

## Step 2 - console mast and swing arms

1. Unravel the computer cable (30-15) and snake it through the bottom of the console
   mast (12) and out the top. Secure the console mast to the main frame with four
   bolts (75). Mount the console mast covers (61 left & 62 right) and secure with four
   screws (113).
2. Install two wave washers (90) onto the console mast axle, then install the swing
   arms (10 left & 11 right) onto the axles. Do not force them or use a hammer - the
   bearings can be damaged. Secure the arms with two bolts (117) and flat washers
   (120). Attach the bottom of the swing arms to the rod ends of the pedal arms using
   two shoulder bolts (119) and nylon nuts (118).

*The scan renders the right-hand swing arm as "IE right" and bolt 117 as "E17".
Item numbers 11 and 117 are read from the surrounding text and the hardware panel,
which print them cleanly.*

## Step 3 - console and beauty covers

1. Plug the computer cable (30-15) and hand pulse cables (35 x 2) into the
   corresponding connectors on the back of the console (30). Mount the console on the
   mast with four screws (78).
2. Install the four swing arm pivot point covers (57, 58 left & 59, 60 right) with
   six screws (113).
3. Install the four covers (53 x 2 & 54 x 2) to the ends of the pedal arms with four
   screws (78). **These covers can be installed upside down** - there is an arrow on
   the inside of each cover showing the correct direction.
4. Install the four covers (55 & 56, 2 pcs each) at the connection point at the bottom
   of the swing arms using four screws (113) and four screws (78). Install the front
   stabilizer cover (47) with two screws (78).

## This is not the later CE800 build

The pre-assembly page says the hardware is **separated into four steps**. The manual
then prints **three**. Take the step count from the steps.

Two whole jobs on this machine are on no later CE800:

- **Four rail mounting brackets, items 17 and 26, on four bolts (76)**, and
- **two steel covers, items 15 and 16, threaded on over the pedal-arm wheels.**

The 2021, 2023 and CE800ENT manuals have no bracket step, no steel covers and no item
76 in any hardware panel. Do not look for these parts on a later machine, and do not
tell a 2013 owner to skip them.

The later manuals also split this build differently: what is step 2 here becomes
their step three, and their step two is the footplates and pedal arms that are
folded into step 1 here.

The wave washer is **item 90** here and **item 148** on the 2021 and 2023 printings.
Same part, renumbered.
