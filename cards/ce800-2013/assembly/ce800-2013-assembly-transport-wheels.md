The elliptical trainer is equipped with **two transport wheels, which engage when the
rear of the machine is lifted**.

**That is the whole of what the manual says.** It gives no weight, no lifting point,
no warning about how many people it takes, and no instruction to unplug first - this
machine has no cord to unplug, being generator-powered.

## No other CE800 manual prints this at all

The CE800 2021, 2023 and CE800ENT manuals have no transportation section, no moving
instruction and no transport wheel on their parts diagrams; the CE800ENT parts list
nevertheless carries item **91, Transportation Wheel, quantity 2**. The CE850 and
CE900 manuals also print nothing about moving, although the CE850 carton list
includes **2 Transport Wheels** and the CE900 2025 diagram labels one.

**The CES880 is the only other Spirit elliptical with a printed moving instruction**,
and it is a different one - hold the base frame with two hands and tow. See
[its card](../../ces880-2025/assembly/ces880-2025-assembly-moving-and-clearance.md).
Do not carry that across; the CES880 has a base frame this machine does not.
