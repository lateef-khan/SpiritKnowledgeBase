tabulated.** Each item is a picture with a caption under it. Only the captions are
recoverable, so there are no column headers to check row lengths against. Every
figure below is one that the scan renders cleanly; nothing here is reconstructed.

## Item 76 exists only on this machine

The **four 5/16" x 3/4" hex head bolts, item 76**, hold the four rail mounting
brackets on. No CE800 printing after this one has a bracket step, and no later
hardware panel carries an item 76 at all. See
[the assembly procedure](ce800-2013-assembly-procedure.md).

## The M10 shoulder bolt reads 38mm here, and that is the majority figure

The scan prints item 119 as "MIO x 38mm" - M10 x 38mm. The 2021 and 2023 parts lists
independently print item 119 as **M10 x 1.5 (38L)**. Only the step-three hardware
panel of those two manuals says 28mm, and it is outnumbered. See
[the later hardware card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ce800-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Two counts do not close

Item 78 appears in both the step 1 and step 3 panels at 14 pieces each, and step 1
alone calls for four, eight and ten of them - twenty-two in a bag of fourteen. The
manual prints no total and has no parts list to check against. Count what you have
before you start, and expect the panel to be a per-picture caption rather than a bag
count.
