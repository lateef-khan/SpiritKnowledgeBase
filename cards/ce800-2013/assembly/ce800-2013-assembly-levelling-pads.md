There are **4 levelling pads on the bottom of the rear rails**. Use a **14mm wrench**
(or an adjustable wrench) to adjust the levellers until the unit sits properly level.

## Four pads, and under the rails, not the stabiliser

Every later CE800 - the 2021, the 2023 and the CE800ENT - prints **2** levelling pads
and puts them **on the bottom of the rear stabilizer**. This manual says four, and
says rails. Both the number and the place are different; do not carry the later
figure back to a 2013 machine, or this one forward.
See [the two-pad card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md)
and [the six-pad CE900 card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ce900-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

## Where the manual prints it, and what else is in that item

It is not in the assembly chapter. It is the **third remedy in the troubleshooting
item about squeaks and other noises**, after tightening the hardware. The noise fault
itself is a maintenance question and belongs on its own card; what is here is the
answer to "how do I level it".

**The carton holds no plain 14mm wrench.** The 13/14mm wrench in the tools panel is
the one to use - see [the tools card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ce800-assembly-tools-included.md).

## The CU800 2012 bike prints the same sentence, rails and all

The **CU800 2012** upright bike manual prints it word for word - four pads, bottom of
the **rear rails**, 14mm wrench - as item 3 of the same noise fault. Its two OCR scans
agree on both numbers; one scan reads the wrench as "[4mm" and the cleaner scan as
"14mm".

**An upright bike has no rear rails.** The two items above it in the same list are
about dirt on "the rear rails and polyurethane wheels", which an upright bike does not
have either, so the whole item reads as carried over from a rail machine. The number
and the place are recorded here as printed; on that machine look for the levellers
under the rear stabiliser and check what is actually there before quoting four.

**Every later Spirit bike says two, under the rear stabiliser.** All twelve of the
2018-and-later commercial bike manuals print that instead. Do not carry four forward.
See [the two-pad card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).
