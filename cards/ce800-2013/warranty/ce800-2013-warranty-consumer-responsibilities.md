> This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household or in commercial facilities (excluding health clubs, YMCAs, community recreation centers). The consumer or commercial facility is responsible for the items listed below:

1. The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within 10 days of the original purchase to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.
2. Proper use of the elliptical trainer in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual
3. Expenses for making the elliptical trainer accessible for servicing, including any item that was not part of the elliptical trainer at the time it was shipped from the factory.
4. Damages to the elliptical trainer finish during shipping, installation or following installation.

**Four items. Every other Spirit elliptical manual in this knowledge base prints six or seven.** Three duties the later manuals impose are simply **not on this list**:

- **no proper-installation duty** (local electric codes) - added by the CE850 2016 onwards;
- **no grounded-power-supply duty** (blown fuses, loose connections, defective house wiring) - added by the CE800 2021 onwards;
- **no routine-maintenance duty** - added by the CE850 2016 onwards.

Do not quote any of those three to a CE800 2013 owner. The source does not impose them, and the routine-maintenance one in particular is often the reason a claim is refused on a later machine.

**The lead sentence is where this manual names the excluded facilities.** Health clubs, YMCAs and **community recreation centers** are named here rather than in the exclusion list, and community recreation centres appear in no other of the ten Spirit elliptical manuals. Its exclusion 8 is the bare "Product used in a dues paying facility" with no examples - `spirit-ce-warranty-use-environment`.

**Item 1 has no online route.** The card must be returned to the address printed on it within 10 days. The manual's own Register Your Purchase page does offer online registration, and the two are not reconciled - `spirit-ce-warranty-registration`.

Item 3 makes getting the machine to a place where it can be worked on the owner's expense, not a warranty cost (`xt-2023-warranty-owner-costs`).

**This manual is an image-only scan and its OCR is imperfect.** Item 1's "10 days" prints as "|0 days" and the exclusion numerals carry similar damage. The wording above is the recovered text; where a digit could not be read with confidence it has been left out of this card rather than guessed.

This list is only half of what governs the warranty. The other half is the nine exclusions below it, `ce800-2013-warranty-exclusions`.
