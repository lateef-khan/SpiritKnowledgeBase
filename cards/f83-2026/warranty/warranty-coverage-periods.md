**Effective February 1, 2026.**

| Component | Period |
| --- | --- |
| Frame / Drive motor | Lifetime |
| Parts | 3 years |
| Labor | 1 year |
| Cosmetic items | 90 days |

**Cosmetic items include, but are not limited to: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.**

**SOLE's responsibilities** are to provide **new or remanufactured parts, at SOLE's option**, and technical support to independent dealers and servicing organizations. In the absence of a dealer or service organization, the warranty is **administered by SOLE directly to the consumer**.

**This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household use.**
