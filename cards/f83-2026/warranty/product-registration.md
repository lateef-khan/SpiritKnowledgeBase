- **Record the serial number** of the fitness product in the space provided on page 1 of the manual.
- **The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to SOLE.**
- **You can also register online** at www.soletreadmills.com under the **support tab**.

The manual also asks you to record the **name of the dealer, the dealer's telephone number, and the purchase date** for easy contact in future.

Registration matters for the warranty: the card must be returned **within 10 days** of the original purchase to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty, and products with no warranty registration card on file at SOLE are excluded from cover.
