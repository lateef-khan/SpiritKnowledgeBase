**The warranty runs from the date of retail sale, as determined by the sale receipt.**

**If there is no receipt, it runs from eighteen (18) months after the original factory shipping date.**

The Service section states the same rule for the labour period: "**Twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale, or eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number, establishes the labor warranty period** should service be required."

**Keep your bill of sale.** If service is performed, it is in your best interest to obtain and keep all receipts.
