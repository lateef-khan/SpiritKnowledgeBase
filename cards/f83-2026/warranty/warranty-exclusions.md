The warranty does not cover:

1. **Consequential, collateral or incidental damages** such as property damage and incidental expenses resulting from any breach of this written or any implied warranty. Some states do not allow this exclusion, so it may not apply to you.
2. **Service call reimbursement to the consumer.** Service call reimbursement to the dealer is also excluded where it does not involve malfunction or defects in workmanship or material; for units beyond the warranty period; for units beyond the service call reimbursement period; for a treadmill not requiring component replacement; or for a treadmill not in ordinary household use.
3. **Damage caused by services performed by persons other than authorized SOLE service companies**; use of parts other than original SOLE parts; or external causes such as **corrosion (rust), discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance including lack of consistent lubrication of the deck, inadequate power supply, natural disasters, or elemental damage** (power surges, lightning storms, water damage, fire damage).
4. **Products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered.**
5. **Products that have been sold, transferred, bartered or given to a third party.**
6. **Products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at SOLE.** SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists.
7. **This warranty is expressly in lieu of all other warranties, expressed or implied**, including the warranties of merchantability and/or fitness for a particular purpose.
8. **Product use in any environment other than a residential setting.**
9. **Warranties outside of North America may vary.**
10. **The warranty is void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment** (for example a garage, outside, or a carport).

Separately, the maintenance section states that **damage to the running belt resulting from improper tracking or tension adjustments is not covered**, and that **failure to lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty**. The electrical section adds that performance problems caused by **house voltage varying by 10% or more are not covered**.
