**The warranty will be void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment.** The examples printed are **a garage, outside, and a carport**.

The electrical section sets the physical limits separately: never expose the treadmill to rain or moisture; it is not designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment; maximum operating temperature 40 degrees C; humidity 95% non-condensing.
