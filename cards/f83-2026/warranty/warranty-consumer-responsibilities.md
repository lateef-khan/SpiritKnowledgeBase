The warranty applies only to products in **ordinary household use**, and the consumer is responsible for:

- **Returning the warranty registration card within 10 days**, or registering later with proof of purchase.
- **Proper use in accordance with this manual, including general maintenance** — belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under the motor cover, and lubrication.
- **Proper installation in accordance with the instructions and all local electric codes.**
- **Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage**, replacement of blown fuses, and repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring.
- **Expenses for making the treadmill accessible for servicing**, including any item that was not part of the treadmill when it shipped from the factory.
- **Damage to the treadmill finish during shipping, installation, or following installation.**
- **Routine maintenance of the unit as specified in this manual.**
- **Providing pictures or video of any issues by email as requested by the service technician**, and assisting with troubleshooting/diagnosing prior to a home visit.
