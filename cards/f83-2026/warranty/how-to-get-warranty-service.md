**Keep your bill of sale.** Service under this warranty must be obtained by following these steps, **in order**:

1. **Contact your selling authorized SOLE dealer.** OR
2. **Submit the service request online** at http://www.soletreadmills.com/serviceparts.html, including **serial number, contact information and a brief description of the problem**.
3. **If there is a question as to where to obtain service, contact the service department at 866-780-7653.**
4. **If no local service is available**, SOLE will repair or replace the parts, at SOLE's option, within the warranty period **at no charge for parts**. **All transportation costs, both to the factory and on return to the owner, are the owner's responsibility.** The owner is responsible for adequate packaging on return to SOLE; SOLE is not responsible for damage during shipping. **Make all freight damage claims with the freight carrier.**
5. **The service department assists with troubleshooting before sending a field technician**, and may request pictures and video of issues by email to help with diagnosis.

**Do not ship any unit to the factory without a return authorization number.**

This written warranty gives you specific legal rights; you may also have other rights that vary from state to state.
