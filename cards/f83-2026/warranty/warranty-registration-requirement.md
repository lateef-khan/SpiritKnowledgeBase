**The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within 10 days of the original purchase to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.**

**If you are outside the 10-day period, the warranty can still be registered if you have proof of purchase** — a receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation.

Separately, the exclusions list says the warranty does not cover **products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at SOLE**, and that SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.
