The printed cause: **high belt/deck friction.**

**The printed remedy is cut off in this extraction.** The cell reads only "High belt/deck friction. See General Maintenance" and the reference it points to is not visible; the same cause is answered elsewhere in the guide with "See General Maintenance section on lubrication". No missing text is invented here.

The breaker itself is **on the front grill**; the display-does-not-light row says to **push the circuit breaker in until it locks** to reset it.
