**This is E05, two digits. It is not E3 (incline adjustment) and it is not E31 (overtemperature).**

**The codes on this console look alike and mean different things. E3 is the incline adjustment error. E01, E02, E03, E04, E05 and E06 are the two-digit controller codes. E22 and E23 are communication codes and E31 is overtemperature.**

**Meaning: undervoltage.**

1. **Check whether the input power voltage is normal.**
2. **Check whether the connector of the power socket is loose.**

The electrical section adds that if voltage varies by ten percent or more, performance may be affected, and that such conditions are not covered under warranty.
