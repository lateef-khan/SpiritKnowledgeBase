**This is E03, two digits. It is not E3 (incline adjustment), not E31 (overtemperature), and not E01 (the overload protection).**

**The codes on this console look alike and mean different things. E3 is the incline adjustment error. E01, E02, E03, E04, E05 and E06 are the two-digit controller codes. E22 and E23 are communication codes and E31 is overtemperature.**

**Meaning: the hardware current is too large.**

1. **Exceeding the rated load leads to excessive current**; the system self-protection shows the error code, and re-powering recovers it.
2. **Check whether the connection between the power socket plug and the motor is loose.**
3. **Replace the controller.**
