**This is E04, two digits. It is not E3 (incline adjustment) and it is not E31 (overtemperature).**

**The codes on this console look alike and mean different things. E3 is the incline adjustment error. E01, E02, E03, E04, E05 and E06 are the two-digit controller codes. E22 and E23 are communication codes and E31 is overtemperature.**

**Meaning: phase loss.**

1. **The motor is not connected properly, or is not connected to the controller.**
2. **Replace the motor.**
3. **Replace the controller.**

The printed cause and remedy list is identical to the one for E02.
