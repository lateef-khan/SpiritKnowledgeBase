**This is E23. It is not E22, which is the LED-watch-to-control-board error, and it is not the plain ERROR message, which is the TFT communication failure.**

**The codes on this console look alike and mean different things. E3 is the incline adjustment error. E01, E02, E03, E04, E05 and E06 are the two-digit controller codes. E22 and E23 are communication codes and E31 is overtemperature.**

**Meaning: the TFT electronic watch has a communication error with the LED electronic watch.**

1. **Check whether the cable between the LED electronic watch and the TFT electronic watch is loose.**
2. **Check whether the LED electronic watch and the TFT electronic watch are damaged.**
3. **After 2 minutes of power failure, power it on again.**

If the TFT display cannot be used at all, the LED display can be run on its own.
