**This is E31. It is not E3 (incline adjustment) and it is not E03 (hardware current too large).**

**The codes on this console look alike and mean different things. E3 is the incline adjustment error. E01, E02, E03, E04, E05 and E06 are the two-digit controller codes. E22 and E23 are communication codes and E31 is overtemperature.**

**Meaning: overtemperature.**

**This manual prints no solution or cause list for E31** — the Solution/Cause cell for this row is empty. Do not assume the steps from a neighbouring code apply.

High belt/deck friction is the cause the diagnosis guide gives for the related heat-driven symptoms, and the lubrication section warns that dried-out lubricant raises friction and stresses the drive motor, drive belt and motor control board.
