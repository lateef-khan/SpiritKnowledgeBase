The console message is printed as: "**Safety key removed, machine stopped, click Replace the key to Operate**".

**Put the safety key back on the console to continue using the treadmill.**

The treadmill will not start or operate without the key, and removing it also secures the machine from unauthorized use. When the key is pulled while running, the belt stops with a **one to two step coast, depending on speed**.
