**This is the word ERROR with no number. It is not E23, which is the TFT-to-LED communication error, and it is not any of the numbered codes.**

**Meaning: TFT electronic watch communication failure.**

1. **Check whether the TFT electronic watch is damaged.**
2. **After 2 minutes of power failure, power it on again.**

If the TFT display is damaged and cannot be used, the LED display can be unplugged from it and run independently.
