**This is E01, two digits. It is not E3 (incline adjustment) and it is not E31 (overtemperature).**

**The codes on this console look alike and mean different things. E3 is the incline adjustment error. E01, E02, E03, E04, E05 and E06 are the two-digit controller codes. E22 and E23 are communication codes and E31 is overtemperature.**

**Meaning: over-current protection. The treadmill is overloaded and the controller protection is activated.**

1. **Exceeding the rated load leads to excessive current**; the system self-protection shows the error code, and re-powering recovers it.
2. **A part of the treadmill is stuck**, so the motor cannot turn, the load is too heavy and the current is too large, and the system reports an error. **Adjust the treadmill to restart the operation, or increase the lubricating oil.**
3. **Check whether there is a flowing sound or burning smell when the motor is running, and replace the motor.**
4. **Replace the controller.**
