**This is E3, a single digit. It is not E03 (hardware current too large) and it is not E31 (overtemperature).**

**The codes on this console look alike and mean different things. E3 is the incline adjustment error. E01, E02, E03, E04, E05 and E06 are the two-digit controller codes. E22 and E23 are communication codes and E31 is overtemperature.**

**Meaning: incline adjustment error.**

1. **Check whether the incline cable is properly connected.**
2. **Connect the incline cable and power it on.**
3. **Connect the incline wire and re-calibrate it.**
