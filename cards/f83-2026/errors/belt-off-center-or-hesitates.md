The printed cause: **the user may be walking while favouring or putting more weight on either the left or right foot.**

**If this walking pattern is natural, track the belt slightly off-centre to the side opposite from the belt movement**, then see the General Maintenance section and adjust as necessary.

Two things to know about this row:

- **This diagnosis-guide remedy — deliberately tracking the belt off-centre to suit an uneven gait — is not mentioned in the maintenance section**, which asks instead for the belt to be stabilised in the centre of the running deck. Neither list mentions the other's advice, so work through both before deciding.
- The row points the reader at "General Maintenance section on **Tread-belt Tension**", but the procedure it describes is the **Tread-belt Tracking Adjustment** on page 30. The tension procedure is on page 29.
