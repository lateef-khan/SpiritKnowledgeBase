**Meaning: a reminder to check lubrication under the walking deck.** It is not a fault.

1. **Check the lubrication under the walking belt.** The manual points to the "belt deck lubrication procedure on page 28"; that procedure is actually printed on **page 31** of this manual.
2. **Power the treadmill three times to reset the lube message.**

The maintenance section adds that if you check and find no lubrication present, you should lubricate **even though the "Lube" indicator is not lit**.
