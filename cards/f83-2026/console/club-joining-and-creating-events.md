- **To view all available events your profile must be linked to your SOLE+ account.**
- **To find events more easily**, press the **filter icon in the upper menu** and filter by **format, duration and focus parameter**.
- **To join a private event**, use **"Join via ID"**: enter the event ID shared with you by the event's creator and you automatically become a participant.
- **To create your own challenge**, press the **+ icon in the upper menu**. You choose the **event format, focus parameter, dates, event name and an optional description**, and set the event **private or public**.

Joining your first event carries a data-sharing consent, described separately.
