The **Club tab in the bottom navigation bar** holds community challenges created by other members of the SOLE community.

- **Shared goals** are challenges with a **predefined target score** that must be achieved **within a limited time period** to complete the challenge.
- **Rankings** are competitions in which users try to **earn the highest score possible within a limited time period**, to defeat as many competitors as possible.

**The score can be one of these focus parameters: calories, distance, active time, or workout count.**
