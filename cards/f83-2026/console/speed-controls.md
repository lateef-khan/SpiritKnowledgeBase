1. **Press the Start key to begin belt movement.**
2. **Use the speed knob to adjust to the desired speed.**
3. You may also use the **speed keys 3, 6, 9, 12** to jump straight to a speed.
4. **To slow the belt down**, use the speed knob, or press the speed adjust keys.

The manual describes these keys twice and not identically: item 2 calls them "the speed keys 3, 6, 9, 12", item 3 calls them "the speed adjust keys, 3 through 12".

Speed can also be adjusted from the touchscreen's side control panels during a workout.
