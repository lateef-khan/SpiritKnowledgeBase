1. **Scan the QR code on the console screen** to download the mobile app.
2. **Install the app and complete registration.**
3. **Open the QR code scanning camera in the SOLE+ app** and **scan the second QR code** to link your local profile with the SOLE+ account.

- **A console profile cannot be linked to multiple SOLE+ accounts at the same time.**
- **If you skip this step you can reach it again later in Profile settings.**

Linking is also what unlocks the full workout history and the full list of Club events.
