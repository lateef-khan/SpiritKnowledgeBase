The console ships with a **sleep mode** that has three settings:

- **ON** — the unit goes into an energy-saving state after **15 minutes of inactivity**.
- **OFF** — the console stays lit while the power is on.
- **RETAIL** — the console runs the **demo video after 3 minutes of inactivity**.

How to switch:

- **LED displays:** hold **Stop** and **Display** for **5 seconds** to switch between ON and OFF.
- **LCD displays:** press and hold **Stop, Enter and Display** for **5 seconds** to switch between ON and OFF.
- **TFT displays:** click **"Sleep Mode" under Settings** to switch among the three settings.

The manual prints the LCD instruction here even though this treadmill is described on page 24 as having TFT and LED displays.
