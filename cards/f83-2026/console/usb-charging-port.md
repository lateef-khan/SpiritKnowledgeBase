The console has an **on-console type-C USB port** for charging a personal device during a workout.

1. **Make sure the fitness equipment power is on.**
2. **Connect your type-C USB charging cable** to the USB power port and to your device.
3. **Check that your device icon indicates it is charging.**

Notes printed with the procedure:

- **The USB charging cable is not included.** Make sure a compatible cable is used.
- **The device's charging icon may or may not indicate charging.** Depending on the current your device needs, the icon may be off while the device still charges, possibly at a lower rate.
- **The port provides up to 5Vdc / 1.5 amp** and is capable of powering USB devices.
- **You cannot save workout data to a USB stick through this port — it is for charging only.**
