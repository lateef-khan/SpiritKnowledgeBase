The machine has a **Bluetooth transceiver** that lets it interact with compatible iOS and Android smartphones or tablets through the **SOLE+ App**.

1. **Download the free SOLE+ App** from the Apple Store or Google Play.
2. **Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your device.**
3. **Tap the icon at the top right corner to search for Sole equipment.**
4. **When the equipment is detected, tap Connect.**
5. When the app and equipment are synced, the **Bluetooth icon on the console lights up**.

The app can also sync workout data with **Apple Health, Google Health Connect, Strava or MapMyFitness**, with more supported over time.
