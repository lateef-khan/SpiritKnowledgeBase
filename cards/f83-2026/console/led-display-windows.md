This treadmill has **both a TFT touchscreen and an LED display**. During operation the **LED display only mirrors the motion data of the TFT display**.

- The **four LED windows show INCLINE, CALORIE, PACE and SPEED**.
- **Pressing the DISPLAY/ENTER key switches them to LAPS, DISTANCE, TIME and PULSE.**
