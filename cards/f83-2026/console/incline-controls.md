- **Incline may be adjusted any time after belt movement.**
- **Use the incline knob** to adjust to the effect you want.
- For a more rapid increase or decrease, **select the desired key 0, 5, 10 or 15 on the left-hand side of the console**.

Incline changes are applied gradually, not instantly. Pausing with the red Stop key returns incline to zero percent.
