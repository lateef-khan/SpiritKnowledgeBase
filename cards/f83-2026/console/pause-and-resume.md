**To pause:** press the **red Stop key once** while the treadmill is running, or tap the **red pause button in the bottom right corner** of the touchscreen.

What happens:

- The **tread-belt decelerates slowly to a stop**.
- The **incline goes to zero percent**.
- The **Time, Distance and Calorie readings hold** while the unit is in pause mode.
- **After 5 minutes the display resets and returns to the start-up screen** (page 24). The touchscreen section states the same limit as "after 5 minutes of inactivity in the Pause mode, the workout will end automatically" (page 21).

**To resume:** while in pause mode, **press the Start key**. **The speed and incline return to their previous settings.**
