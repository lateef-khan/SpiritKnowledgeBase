Once a workout starts, after a **short countdown** the console enters workout mode. **Warm-up and cool-down phases may be skipped by pressing "Skip".**

During the workout you can switch between:

- **Stats (Focus)**, **Charts** and **Track** views to follow your progress;
- the **Media** section for your media content;
- **Garmin**, to see real-time data from a Garmin smart watch.

**In the Focus view you can choose which parameters are displayed by tapping any of the number blocks.**

**Speed and incline are adjusted from the side control panels or the physical keys.**
