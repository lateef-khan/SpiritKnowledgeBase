From the Home page you can choose from a variety of **pre-installed Apps**, or use the **Mirroring function to cast content from your smartphone directly to the console screen**.

Once content is playing, use the **bottom panel for navigation and view settings**. There are **three view layouts**:

- **full interface** — status bar, control panels, real-time stats;
- **partial interface** — status bar, real-time stats;
- **minimal interface** — full-screen content with all panels hidden.
