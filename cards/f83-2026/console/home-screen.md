The Home screen displays an **overview of your activity** and offers shortcuts to:

- an **immediate start**;
- a **manually controlled timed workout**;
- your **favourite programs and video classes**;
- **club events** you take part in or might want to join.

**In Guest mode you can set your age and weight here**, which helps the machine calculate your workout summary more accurately.

**The blocks on this page are customizable.** Press **"Edit Home"** to configure a layout that suits you: optional blocks can be enabled or disabled and their order changed.
