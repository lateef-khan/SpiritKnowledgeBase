The exercise equipment can play music wirelessly over Bluetooth.

1. **Turn on Bluetooth on your phone or tablet.**
2. **Search for the name "BT-Speaker"** in your device's Bluetooth menu.
3. **Tap to connect.**

Your device can now transmit music to the exercise equipment. The Bluetooth Connectivity page also advertises pairing with a **Premium Bluetooth Speaker Soundbar**.
