**Heart rate programming, caution: heart rate programs are intended for wireless Bluetooth wearables only.** You **must receive a strong, steady value in the Heart Rate window** before relying on them.

The Programs section adds that **a heart rate monitor is required** for both the Heart Rate program and the Gerkin Protocol test.

Note that page 2 also refers to **pulse sensors** on the machine, described as exercise aids that are not medical devices. The manual does not say whether those sensors can drive a heart rate program; page 24 says the programs are for Bluetooth wearables only. Both statements are printed and neither is corrected here.
