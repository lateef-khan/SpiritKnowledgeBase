**The Heart Rate program uses incline, not speed, to control your heart rate.**

- **The incline gradually increases until you reach your target heart rate**, then **adjusts automatically to keep you within 5 bpm of your goal**.
- **A heart rate monitor is required for this program.**
- On the setup page you can **set the target bpm directly**, or **select 60% or 80% of your age-predicted maximum heart rate** and let the machine calculate the target. If you choose the second option, **make sure the machine knows how old you are**, otherwise the calculation is based on default values.
- **The program finishes when the time is up, or if your heart rate is 20% higher than the target.**
