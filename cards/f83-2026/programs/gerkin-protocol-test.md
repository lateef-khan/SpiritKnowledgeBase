**Gerkin Protocol is a sub-maximal VO2 (volume of oxygen) test.**

- It **increases speed and incline alternately until you reach 85% of your max heart rate**.
- **A heart rate monitor is required for this test.**
- **The time it takes you to reach 85% determines the test score (VO2 Max).**
- **The test starts with a 3-minute warm-up at a lower speed.**
