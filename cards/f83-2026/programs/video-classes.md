**Access video classes by selecting the "Videos" tab of the bottom navigation bar.**

- Video classes are **pre-recorded, manually controlled workouts of fixed duration**.
- There are **hundreds of videos of 7 activity types: Running, Yoga, Stretching, Boxing, Sculpt, Meditation and Full Sweat.**
- **Filter classes by instructor, duration, difficulty level, music genre**, and so on.
- The tab menu at the top of the page also has **Collections** — sequences of video classes that follow a certain theme.
