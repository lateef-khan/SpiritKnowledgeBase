**Templates are preset programs with a preserved set of parameters.** The Templates tab is on the top of the Programs page.

- **Programs can be saved as templates on completion**, or
- **create one from scratch**: tap **"New Template"**, choose your base program, then save it with the properties you have adjusted.
- **A maximum of 12 template programs can be stored.**
- **To remove unwanted templates, press "Edit" in the Templates tab.**
