**HIIT is a customizable interval program.** You manually choose:

- **the duration and speed of the sprint intervals**;
- **the duration and speed of the rest intervals**;
- **the total number of intervals**.

This is not the same as the preset **Intervals** program, which follows a fixed printed pattern.
