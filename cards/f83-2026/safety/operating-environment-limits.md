- **NEVER expose the treadmill to rain or moisture.**
- The product is **NOT designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment**.
- **Maximum operating temperature: 40 degrees C.**
- **Humidity: 95% non-condensing** (no water drops forming on surfaces).

The warranty is separately voided if the unit is not kept in a climate-controlled, insulated environment.
