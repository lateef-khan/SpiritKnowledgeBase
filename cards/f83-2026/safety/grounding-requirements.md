**This product must be grounded.** If the treadmill malfunctions or breaks down, grounding provides a path of least resistance for electric current, reducing the risk of electric shock. The product is equipped with a cord having an **equipment-grounding plug**, which must be plugged into an appropriate outlet that is properly installed and grounded in accordance with all local codes and ordinances.

**DANGER — improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric shock.** Check with a qualified electrician or serviceman if you are in doubt as to whether the product is properly grounded.

- **Do not modify the plug.** If it will not fit the outlet, have a proper outlet installed by a qualified electrician.
- The product is for use on a **nominal 120-volt circuit**.
- A **temporary adapter** may be used to connect this plug to a 2-pole receptacle **only until a properly grounded outlet can be installed** by a qualified electrician.
- When an adapter is used, the **green colored rigid earlug** extending from it must be connected to a permanent ground such as a properly grounded outlet box cover, and the adapter **must be held in place by a metal screw**.
