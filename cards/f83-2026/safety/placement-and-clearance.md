**This manual gives the rear clearance twice and the two figures differ. Neither is picked here.**

- **Page 2, Important Safety Instructions:** "Do not block the rear of the treadmill. Provide a minimum of **3 1/2 feet** clearance between the rear of the treadmill and any fixed object."
- **Page 8, Assembly Step 1:** "The rear should be at least **3'** from any wall."

The rest of the placement rules are stated once:

- Install on a **flat, level surface** (page 2); position it on a **smooth, level floor** during assembly (page 8).
- **Do not operate the treadmill on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet.** Damage to both the carpet and the treadmill may result.
- **Do not use outdoors.**
