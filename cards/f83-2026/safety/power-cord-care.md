- **Route the power cord away from any moving part of the treadmill**, including the elevation mechanism and the transport wheels.
- **NEVER remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power.**
- **Never operate the treadmill if it has a damaged cord or plug.** If the treadmill is not working properly, call your dealer.
- **Keep the cord away from heated surfaces.**
