**Avoid AFCI/GFCI circuit breakers if possible.** These breakers may trip occasionally during exercise because of the **high inrush currents of the treadmill drive electronics and motor**. This is an issue that affects all treadmill brands.

New laws in your area may require these breakers. If you have them and are experiencing nuisance tripping:

- **Check whether any other device is plugged into the same circuit.** Examples that may also cause tripping: fluorescent lights with electronic ballasts, coffee maker, space heater, hair drier.
- **Optimally the treadmill should be the only device plugged into the circuit.**

Sole treadmills have **surge suppressors built in** to help avoid nuisance tripping. Breakers and outlets tested by Sole against their products: **Eaton (Cutler Hammer Series)**, **Leviton (Smart lock pro)** and **Schneider Electric (Canadian home series)**. These did not trip in testing when connected to Sole treadmills, **as long as no other devices were plugged into the same circuit**.
