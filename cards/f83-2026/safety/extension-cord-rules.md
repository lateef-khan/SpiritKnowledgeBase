**This manual states the extension cord rule twice and the two statements do not agree. Neither is picked here.**

- **Page 2, Important Safety Instructions:** "DO NOT USE AN EXTENSION CORD UNLESS IT IS A **14AWG OR BETTER**, WITH ONLY ONE OUTLET ON THE END."
- **Page 32, Service Checklist - Diagnosis Guide** (treadmill only reaches about 7 mph): "Do not use an extension cord. If an extension cord is required it should be as short as possible and **heavy duty 16 gauge minimum**."

Both passages agree on the rest:

- **Do not attempt to disable the grounded plug** by using improper adapters, or in any way modify the cord set.
- A serious **shock or fire hazard** may result, along with computer malfunctions.
- Only **one outlet on the end** of the cord.
