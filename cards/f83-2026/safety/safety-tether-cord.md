A safety tether cord is provided with this unit and **should be used at all times**. It is for your safety should you fall or move too far back on the tread-belt; **pulling the safety tether cord stops tread-belt movement**.

1. **Place the safety key into position on the console control head.** The treadmill will not start or operate without it. Removing the safety key also secures the treadmill from unauthorized use.
2. **Fasten the plastic clip onto your clothing securely** to assure good holding power. The clip should be attached securely so that it does not come off. Be familiar with its function and limitations. The treadmill will stop, **depending on speed, with a one to two step coast**, any time the safety key is pulled off the console. **Use the red Stop switch in normal operation.**
3. If the console displays "**Safety key removed, machine stopped, click Replace the key to Operate**", put the safety key back to continue using the treadmill.

**Remove the tether cord after use to prevent unauthorized treadmill operation.**
