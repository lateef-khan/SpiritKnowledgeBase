- **NEVER mount or dismount the treadmill while the belt is moving.**
- Sole treadmills **start at a very low speed**, so it is unnecessary to straddle the belt during start-up. Simply standing on the belt during slow acceleration is proper once you have learned to operate the unit.
- **Always hold on to a handrail or hand bar while making control changes** (incline, speed, etc.).
- **Use the handrails provided; they are for your safety.**
- **Use caution when doing other things while walking** — watching television, reading, and so on. These distractions may cause you to lose balance or stray from walking in the centre of the belt, which may result in serious injury.
