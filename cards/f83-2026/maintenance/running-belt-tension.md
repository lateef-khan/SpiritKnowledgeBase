**Belt tension is not critical for most users**, but it matters for joggers and runners in order to provide a smooth, steady running surface.

- The adjustment is made **from the rear roller with the M6 L Allen wrench (262)** supplied in the parts package.
- The **adjustment bolts are at the end of the step rails**. **Adjustment is through the small hole in the end cap.**
- **Tighten the rear roller only enough to prevent slippage at the front roller.**
- **Turn the tension adjusting bolts 1/4 turn each**, then walk on the belt and check that it is not slipping or hesitating with each step.
- **Turn the bolts on both sides evenly**, or the belt could start tracking to one side instead of running in the middle of the deck.

**DO NOT OVERTIGHTEN.** Over-tightening will cause belt damage and premature bearing failure.

**If you tighten the belt a lot and it still slips**, the problem could be the **drive belt under the motor cover** that connects the motor to the front roller. A loose motor belt feels similar to a loose walking belt. **Tightening the motor belt should be done by a trained service person.**

Damage to the running belt resulting from improper tension adjustments is not covered under the Sole warranty.
