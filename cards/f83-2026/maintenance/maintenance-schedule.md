| Task | Interval |
| --- | --- |
| Vacuum under motor cover and check wiring | Every other month |
| Clean and inspect deck for lubrication | Every other month |
| Lubricate deck | Every 90 hours, or sooner if dry |
| Inspect belt tracking, adjust if necessary | Monthly |

The chart has a **Date** column for recording each service. The general cleaning text on page 29 also asks for the area **underneath the treadmill to be vacuumed monthly**, and the belt and deck to be wiped **once a month**.
