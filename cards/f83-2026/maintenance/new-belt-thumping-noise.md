**This is normal on a new belt and does not need a service call.**

When a treadmill belt is brand new and has been in the package for a few months before use, **the area of the belt that wraps around the rollers takes on the shape of the roller**. On start-up, that shape causes a **thumping sound each time the belt moves past the rollers**.

**Once the treadmill has been used for a few workouts, the belt wears in and the thumping sound goes away.** The manual says this noise is normal on new higher-end belts, so there is no need to contact service; the new belt just requires a wear-in period.
