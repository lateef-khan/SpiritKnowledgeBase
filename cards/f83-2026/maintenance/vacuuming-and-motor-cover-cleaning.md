Dirt, dust and hair can **block air inlets** and accumulate on the running belt.

- **Vacuum underneath the treadmill monthly** to prevent excess build-up of dirt that can be sucked into the inner workings under the motor cover.
- **Every other month, remove the motor cover and carefully vacuum out** the dirt and hair that may accumulate.
- **UNPLUG THE POWER CORD BEFORE THIS TASK.**

The instruction says "use the chart on Pg. 5 to record your maintenance"; the preventative maintenance chart is actually printed on page 30.
