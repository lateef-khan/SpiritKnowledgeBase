**First, clean between the belt and deck.** Use a clean, non-fraying rag, t-shirt or light towel. Halfway between the end of the treadmill and the motor cover, push the garment under the belt until you can grasp it on both sides, drag it the length of the entire belt 1-2 times, then remove it.

**What to use.** Only **Sole Fitness approved lubricant: 100% food grade silicone lubricant for treadmills**. **DO NOT USE SILICONE CAULKING FOR BATHROOM AND KITCHEN PROJECTS, OR A LUBRICANT THAT HAS A SPRAY/FOAM BASE.** The treadmill comes with **one tube of "Lube"**; extra tubes can be ordered from Sole Fitness or an authorized Sole Fitness dealer, and a **Lube-n-Walk kit** is available from both. **Use 2-3 ounces of lubrication each application.**

**The procedure**

1. **Turn the power switch off and unplug the power cord from the wall outlet.**
2. **Measure 18" from the edge of the motor cover**; kneel down and reach under the belt approximately **4-6" from one edge**.
3. **Squirt a line of lubricant about 1/8" wide by 15" long in an "S" pattern**, perpendicular to the motor cover.
4. **Repeat on the opposite side.**
5. **Plug the cord back in and turn the power switch on.**
6. **Walk on the belt at a moderate speed for five minutes** to distribute the silicone evenly.
