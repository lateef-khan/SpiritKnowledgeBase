The treadmill is designed so that the tread-belt **remains reasonably centred while in use**. It is normal for some belts to drift near one side depending on a user's gait and whether they favour one leg. **If the belt continues to move toward one side during use, adjustment is necessary.**

1. Use the **6 mm Allen wrench (262)** provided.
2. **Make tracking adjustments on the left side bolt.**
3. **Set belt speed at 3 mph.**
4. **If the belt is too close to the left side, turn the bolt 1/4 turn to the right (clockwise)** and **wait a few minutes** for the belt to adjust itself. Continue with 1/4 turns until the belt stabilises in the centre of the running deck.
5. **If the belt is too close to the right side, turn the bolt counter-clockwise.**

A small adjustment can make a dramatic difference, which may not be apparent right away. The belt may need periodic tracking adjustment depending on use and walking/running characteristics; adjustments become less of a concern as the belt is used. **Proper belt tracking is an owner responsibility, common with all treadmills.**

**ATTENTION: damage to the running belt resulting from improper tracking / tension adjustments is NOT covered under the Sole warranty.**
