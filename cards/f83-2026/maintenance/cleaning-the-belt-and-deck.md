The treadmill uses a **very high-efficiency, low-friction deck**. Performance is maximised when the deck is kept as clean as possible.

- Use a **soft damp cloth or paper towel** to wipe the edge of the belt and the area between the belt edge and the frame. Also reach as far as practical directly under the belt edge.
- **Do this once a month** to extend belt and bed life.
- **A mild soap and water solution with a nylon scrub brush** will clean the top of the textured belt. **Allow it to dry before using.**
