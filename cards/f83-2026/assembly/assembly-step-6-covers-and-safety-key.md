1. **Attach the Frame Base Cover (R) (62) and Frame Base Cover (L) (63)** using: Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (253), Phillips Head Screw (252) 4 pcs.
2. **Fit the Rotate Axle Mast Cover Assembly (13) onto the Console Assembly (10).**
3. **Insert the Safety Key (32) into the Console Outer Cover Assembly (11).**

This is the step that installs the safety key. Page 13 tells the reader to "see assembly step 11 for reference" for the safety key, but this manual has only seven assembly steps.
