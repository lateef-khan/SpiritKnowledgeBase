**Serious injury could occur if this folding treadmill is not unpacked properly.**

**The deck restraint SHIPPING strap.** A strap is installed around the treadmill base to prevent the treadmill from unfolding accidentally during shipping. If it is not removed properly the treadmill could spring open unexpectedly and injure someone standing nearby.

1. **Position the treadmill flat on the ground**, in the orientation it would be in if you were using it.
2. **Do not tilt the treadmill up on its side while removing the shipping strap** — this could cause the folding mechanism to spring open.
3. **Pull up on the strap and cut it with a pair of scissors.**

**The safety pin.** A safety pin is locked on the treadmill base to prevent the treadmill from unfolding accidentally during shipping. **It has to be removed before powering on or using the incline function**, to avoid damaging the machine. **Pull it away from the treadmill base completely.**
