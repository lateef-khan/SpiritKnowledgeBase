**Step 5-1**

1. **Connect the Console Rotate Axle Assembly (12) Computer Cable (Middle) (12-06) to the Console Assembly (10) Computer Cable (Upper) (10-05).**
2. **Attach the Console Rotate Axle Assembly (12) and Console Assembly (10)** using: M5_Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (253), Phillips Head Screw (252) 4 pcs.
3. **Remove the Locking Pin (52), then lift up the running deck.**
4. After lifting the running deck, **use the M6_L Allen Wrench (262) and secure with Button Head Socket Bolts (263) 4 pcs and Flat Washers (264) 4 pcs.**

**After the above assembly, tighten the screws in place.**

**Step 5-2**

1. **Cover the screw holes on the Console Outer Cover Assembly (11) with the Repair Mast Cover (81), 2 pcs.**

The assembly pack checklist on page 6 calls part #263 a socket head cap bolt; the step calls it a button head socket bolt.
