The manual prints a **"Scan Here for Assembly Video" QR code** on the assembly title page. The hardware is bagged per step, and each step's bag is listed on the assembly pack checklist.

Before step 1, remove the shipping strap and the safety pin as described in the unpacking warning.

1. **Step 1** — remove the treadmill from the carton and position it on a smooth, level floor.
2. **Step 2** — connect the middle computer cable to the lower one and bolt the left and right uprights on.
3. **Step 3** — connect the middle computer cable to the upper one and the light strip to the connecting wire, then bolt the uprights to the console outer cover assembly.
4. **Step 4** — connect the console rotate axle assembly cable and attach that assembly to the console outer cover assembly.
5. **Step 5** — connect the console assembly cable, screw the console on, remove the locking pin, lift the running deck and bolt it, then cover the screw holes with the repair mast covers.
6. **Step 6** — fit the frame base covers, the rotate axle mast cover assembly, and insert the safety key.
7. **Step 7** — insert the end cap.

Steps 2, 3 and 4 each end with a note **not to fully tighten the screws yet**, and to check that the cables just connected are not pinched between the steel tubing. The screws are tightened in place at the end of step 5.
