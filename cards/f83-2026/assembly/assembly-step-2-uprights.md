1. **Connect the Computer Cable (Middle) (79) on the Right Upright (4) to the Computer Cable (Lower) (78).**
2. **Attach the Left and Right Upright (5, 4)** using: M5_Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (253), Button Head Socket Bolt (250) 8 pcs, Split Washer (214) 8 pcs.

**Do not fully tighten the screws yet.** Before attaching the hardware in step 3, make sure the cables you just connected do not get pinched between the steel tubing — pinched cables may prevent the treadmill from operating properly.
