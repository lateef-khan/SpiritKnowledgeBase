1. **Remove the treadmill from the carton** and position it aside on a smooth, level floor. The rear should be **at least 3' from any wall**.
2. **Do not remove the Velcro belt** until you have removed the plastic wrap and Styrofoam from beneath the unit.

**Turning the unit on its side after removing the belt may cause the unit to fold up and cause serious injury.**

Note that page 2 asks for a minimum of 3 1/2 feet of clearance behind the treadmill in use, which is not the 3' given here.
