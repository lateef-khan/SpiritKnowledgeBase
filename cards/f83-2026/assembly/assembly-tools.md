The **ASSEMBLY TOOLS** panel on page 7 is an illustration with no text, so the packed tool list cannot be read from the extraction. The tools named in the assembly steps themselves are:

- **Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (253)**, written as "M5_Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench" — used in steps 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6.
- **L Allen Wrench (262)**, written as "M6_L Allen Wrench" in step 5 and as "a 6 mm Allen wrench" in the maintenance section — used in step 5 and for belt tension and tracking afterwards.

A **pair of scissors** is needed before step 1 to cut the shipping strap.
