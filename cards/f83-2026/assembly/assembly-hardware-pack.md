Two things printed in this manual do not line up, and neither is corrected here:

- **The step 3 entry on the checklist lists no bolt**, but the step 3 instruction on page 9 calls for **#220, 5/16" × 65mm button head socket bolt, 4 pcs**.
- **#263 is called a "Socket Head Cap Bolt" on the checklist (page 6) and a "Button Head Socket Bolt" in the step 5 instruction (page 10).**
