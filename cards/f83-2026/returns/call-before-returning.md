The manual prints this notice on both the inside front cover and the back cover:

**"Before returning your Sole product to any retailer, or if you require any assistance with assembly or technical support, please call us first for assistance at 866-780-7653."**

The same number is given for the service department in the introduction and in the warranty's service steps.
