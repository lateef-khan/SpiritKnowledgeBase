**Loose screws first, then the carriage bolt if a pedal wobbles, then the leveling-foot thumb screw if the whole unit
rocks.** The same three sentences are printed for Spirit's 2016 residential ellipticals
(`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-shaking-loose-screws-and-pedal-carriage-bolts`) and for Sole's SC200 stepper
(`sc200-2016-shaking-problem`); this is the CS800 2016 stepper's copy.

11-3 Shaking Problem, as printed:

> The major cause for this problem is loose screw. Possible cause of loose screw at the joint is either wearing due
> to time duration of usage or assembly faults. Wobble pedal is mostly caused by assembly fault of the carriage bolt.
> For unit wobble, it can normally be fixed by adjusting the Thumb Head Socket Screw for Leveling Foot beneath the
> unit. Be sure to apply thick lubricant when replacing Leveling foot, as shown in figure 1.

Three things follow from it:

1. **A joint that has developed play** - from wear over time or from a fault at assembly - is a loose screw.
   Tighten it.
2. **A pedal that wobbles** is a carriage bolt that was not tightened at assembly. Look to that bolt before anything
   else on the pedal.
3. **The whole unit rocking** is a levelling job: turn the thumb-head socket screw of the leveling foot under the
   unit. **When a leveling foot is replaced, its thread gets a thick lubricant** - the only lubricant this book
   names, and it is for the leveller thread, not the belt or the rails.

No pad count and no wrench size are printed: the foot is turned by its thumb screw. This is the last page of the
book; the table of contents promises an *11.4 Smooth Problem* after it, and the 72-page PDF has no such page.

A machine that makes a noise rather than shaking is the previous Q&A (`cs800-2016-maintenance-noise-bearings-flywheel-rear-slide-wheel-corrugated-washer`); a belt that slips is
`cvc800-drive-belt-slipping`.
