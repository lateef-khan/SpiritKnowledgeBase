**Three places and two checks.** The book's Q&A gives noise one short paragraph; the chapter-10 sections give the
flywheel and the connecting arm their own answers.

11-1 Noise, as printed:

> The product is QC inspected in the plant before delivery. Possible locations of noises may include bearings,
> Flywheel, and Slide Wheel on the rear-side. Check if corrugated washer is installed; check for damaged bearing or
> even Pedal, etc.

So: **the bearings, the flywheel and the slide wheel at the rear** are where a noise comes from; **the corrugated
(wave) washer** is the thing to confirm is fitted, and **a damaged bearing or a damaged pedal** the things to look
for. Nothing is lubricated and nothing is adjusted on this page - it is an inspection list, and the remedy is the
replacement procedure for whatever is found (chapter 9, assembly cards).

The same book answers two noises more specifically:

- **A noise while the flywheel turns** - 10-2: check the flywheel, drive pulley and idler wheel assembly for foreign
  objects or friction first; take the flywheel off and refit it; if the noise persists, replace the flywheel
  (`cvc800-resistance-not-changing-or-flywheel-noise`).
- **A slide wheel that feels textured** - 10-5: the bearing is damaged; replace the bearing. A connecting arm with
  any other problem is replaced whole.

The XS895 and CVC800 books answer the same question with a four-item list - bearings on joint areas, flywheel,
slide wheels, pedals - and no washer (`cvc800-noise-troubleshooting`). Sole's SC200 2016 prints this page word for
word (`sc200-2016-noises`). A machine that shakes rather than makes a noise is `cs800-2016-maintenance-shaking-loose-screws-carriage-bolts-and-leveling-foot-thumb-screw`.
