1. Unplug the motor connection cable (printed "development motor").
2. **Start the machine, turn the belt while pushing in the direction of the arrow, and remove the motor belt.**
3. Remove the generator fixing bolts in turn - **there are 4 bolts to be removed**, as shown by the arrows.
4. Remove the generator.

The book heads this "Motor replacement" and the captions say generator; on the V1.0 machine the alternator is
the drive's load and the resistor its brake. Reaching it needs one set of pedals off first, as the pedal-removal
card says. No bolt size, no belt tension and no refit step are printed. The 2022 magnetic-system machine has a
magnetic wheel in its place.
