**Replace it with a controller from the same batch** - the board carries a programmed program - and keep the
original wire sequence, fixed with cable ties.

1. Remove the side triangle shroud.
2. Remove the controller screws.
3. Disconnect the cables (**power-off brake cable, motor cable, proximity switch cable**).
4. Remove the wiring harness at both ends of the **resistor**.
5. Unplug the **controller wire, power wire and magnetic switch wire** connectors and remove the controller.

**Controller replacement notes.** 1. Turn off the power supply when disassembling and assembling. 2. The internal
circuit board of the controller has a programming program: replace the controller with the same batch. 3. Pay
attention to the line sequence; when reinstalling, follow the original line sequence and fix with cable ties.

This is the V1.0 alternator-drive machine, whose controller also feeds a braking resistor; the 2022
magnetic-system book's control board has three steps and no resistor, and the CSC880's controller has seven
numbered cables. Each has its own card.
