1. Disconnect the direct connection cable between the controller and the power failure brake.
2. Remove a set of pedals (the pedal-removal card) and remove the power-off brake fixing screws from the front -
   **three power-off brake fixing screws**.
3. Remove the power failure brake.

No screw size and no refit step are printed. To test a brake before replacing it, the same book says to put
DC 24 V from the power adapter across the two ends of the brake power line: a "snap" means it is good, silence
means it is bad - that check is an errors card. The magnetic 2022 book has no separate brake procedure.
