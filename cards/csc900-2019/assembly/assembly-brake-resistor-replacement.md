1. Remove the side triangle shield, use a **short-handle screwdriver**, and remove the fixing screw.
2. Remove the fixing screws at both ends of the resistor and remove the resistor.

No sizes or refit step are printed. The photograph shows a gold finned resistor with a printed rating; read it
from the part in hand rather than from the picture. A resistor is judged bad in the same book when a meter across
it does not read close to 0.5 ohm - an errors card. The controller procedure disconnects the harness at both ends
of this resistor.

**Only the V1.0 alternator machine has a resistor.** The 2022 magnetic-system book has none.
