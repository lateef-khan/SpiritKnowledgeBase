**The machine stays powered on** so the pedals can be turned to the front, and the gaskets go back exactly where
they came from. *When replacing internal accessories such as the power-loss brake and generator, one group of
pedals can be disassembled. The machine is powered on and turned; turn the pedals to bring the removed pedals to
the front of the machine so the internal parts can be reached from the front.*

1. Remove the left and right triangle shields.
2. Drip lubricant onto the inner shaft of the pedal.
3. Use a wrench on one side.
4. Use a wrench or a power tool on the other side.
5. Turn the connecting rod while pulling it out.
6. Use a suitable tool to connect the chain to the pedal disassembly position.
7. Turn the pedal to the front and remove the fixed-chain tool at the notch position.

**Disassembly points.** (1) When pulling the connecting shaft outward, force it outward while rotating. (2) Pay
attention to the position and specification of the gaskets - take pictures, record, and reassemble in the
original position. (3) When the pedal is turned, the machine should be powered on.

The 2022 magnetic-system book condenses the same job into one caption inside its magnetic-wheel procedure: *fix
one end of the connecting shaft with a wrench and the other end with a power screwdriver with a socket, rotate
it while pulling it outward*, then *pull out the pedal fixing shaft from one side*.
