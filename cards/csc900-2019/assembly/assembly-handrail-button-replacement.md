Six photographs with one-line captions and no numbered steps: **remove the fixing screws**; **internal wiring**
(two pictures); **handrail breathing light board**; **button**; **button sleeve**.

That is all the page says. The order of the pictures is the order of disassembly. A handrail button that has
failed is replaced as a unit in the troubleshooting table ("the handrail button has failed - replace the handrail
button"). Only the V1.0 book has this section.
