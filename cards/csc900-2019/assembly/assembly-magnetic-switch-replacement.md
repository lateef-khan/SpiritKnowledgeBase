1. Disassemble a set of pedals (the pedal-removal card) and remove the fixing screws from the front.
2. Disconnect the cable.

Two captions, no sizes, no refit step. The same book's troubleshooting names a shifted or faulty magnetic safety
switch when the machine runs but speed and distance stay at 0 for 10 to 30 seconds, and tests it with a magnet
and a meter on the buzzer setting - errors cards. This is not the proximity switch, which has its own card.
