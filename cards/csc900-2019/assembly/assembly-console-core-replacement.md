1. Remove the decorative screws.
2. Disconnect the ground wire and the iron plate fixing screw.
3. Remove the fixed iron plate.

Three captions and a picture of the internal wiring; no sizes, no refit step. The page after it is a drawing of
the AI3 console's internal circuit (a specs matter). The 2022 book's "console board replacement" is a six-step
sequence for a different board and has its own card.
