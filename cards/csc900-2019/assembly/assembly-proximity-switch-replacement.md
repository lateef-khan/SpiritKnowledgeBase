1. Disconnect the proximity switch cable.
2. Cut off the tie.
3. Use a short-handle screwdriver to remove the fixing screws.
4. Remove the proximity switch.

No sizes and no refit step. The troubleshooting table's "incorrect pedal position when stopping - defective
stopper or loose wire - re-plug or replace the stopper" is this part. The render of the page shows a TL-N20ME
proximity sensor beside the caption. The magnetic safety switch is a different part with its own card.
