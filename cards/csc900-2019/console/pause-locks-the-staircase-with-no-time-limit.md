**No thirty seconds, no timeout, and you come back at the level you left.**

- **Press Stop once during a program** - the **rotating staircase locks** and the program pauses.
  **The staircase remains locked until the program is continued.** The manual sets no time limit at
  all.
- **Press Start** to unlock the stairs and continue stepping.
- **Press Stop twice during a workout** - the program ends and the console **returns to the start-up
  screen**.

**This is the key on which the 2019 machine differs most from every later printing.** From the
**CSC900-2021** onwards the same button is called START/STOP, the pause lasts **30 seconds**, the
staircase restarts at **resistance level one** so you have to climb back to your level yourself, and
if you do nothing for 30 seconds **the program ends automatically and the console resets**
(`csc900-2024-console-start-stop-pause-thirty-seconds`). **None of that is in the 2019 book.**

**The manual prints no hold-to-reset**, no step-back-during-data-entry behaviour and no statement
about whether the workout data survives the pause.
