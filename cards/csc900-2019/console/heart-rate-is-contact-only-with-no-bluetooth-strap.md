**"The StairClimber has a built-in heart rate monitoring system."** That is the whole
claim, and it is a contact system.

- **Grasp the hand pulse sensors** and the console calculates and displays Heart Rate in the **upper
  left side window**. **This may take a few seconds.**
- **Press the scroll button until Heart Rate is displayed** - heart rate is the second data point of
  window one, not the default (`csc900-2024-console-four-display-windows`).
- The Heart Rate window then reads your pulse **in beats per minute**.

**The word Bluetooth appears nowhere in this manual, and neither does Polar, ANT+, chest strap or
telemetric.** This machine's console chapter describes no wireless heart rate of any kind.

**That changed in the very next printing.** The **CSC900-2021** book replaces this paragraph with one
that says the console "has capabilities for both a Bluetooth sensor for telemetric heart rate and
contact heart rate" and **automatically detects the closest sensor** - and the 2024 and the CSC880
books keep it (`csc900-2024-console-heart-rate-bluetooth-and-contact`). **Do not promise a 2019
owner that their machine will pick up a strap.**

**The manual prints no bpm range** and no troubleshooting for a pulse that will not read.
