Check these four places, in the manual's order, and use the matching replacement section for whichever one is at fault:

1. Bearings on the joint areas.
2. Flywheel.
3. Slide wheels.
4. Pedals.

Related answers that name a noise cause of their own:

- Track wheel bearing damage at the strap hold down — card `cc81-2020-strap-hold-down-noise-or-rough`.
- Drive pulley and idler wheel assembly interfering, or the flywheel itself — card `cc81-2020-no-resistance-or-flywheel-noise`.
