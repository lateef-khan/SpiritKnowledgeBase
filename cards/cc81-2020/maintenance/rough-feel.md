Check two things:

1. Whether all the screws are tight.
2. Whether any foreign material is stuck on the **rail tubes** and the **track wheels**.

**There is a second answer for this same symptom elsewhere in the manual.** Section 9-2 says a rough feel at the strap hold down is answered by cleaning the strap hold down, replacing the track wheel, and adjusting the track wheel tightness. See the card `cc81-2020-strap-hold-down-noise-or-rough`. Work both lists.
