**Noise.** If the noise comes from a damaged track wheel bearing, replace the slide wheel. Follow the strap hold down replacement steps to get at it.

**Rough feel.** Check these three, in the manual's order:

1. Clean the strap hold down.
2. Replace the track wheel.
3. Adjust the tightness of the track wheel.

A rough feel has a second answer elsewhere in the manual: section 9-9 says to check that every screw is tight and that nothing is stuck on the rail tubes and track wheels. See the card `cc81-2020-rough-feel`.
