Take off **chain cover (R)**, loosen the **idler wheel assembly**, then reassemble the drive belt.

Then check these two with the machine **running at low speed**:

1. If the drive pulley swings too much, replace it.
2. If the drive belt, the drive pulley and the flywheel are not in line, adjust the flywheel to bring them into line.

Set the belt tension again afterwards. The figure is **190 Hz, plus or minus 10** — see the card `cc81-2020-drive-belt-tension`.

A belt that stays on but slips is a different answer — see the card `cc81-2020-drive-belt-slipping`.
