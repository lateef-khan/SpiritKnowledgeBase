**No resistance.** Check both of these:

1. The gear motor is operating properly.
2. The steel cable and the flywheel are mounted properly.

**Noise from the flywheel.** Check both of these:

1. The drive pulley and the idler wheel assembly are interfering with each other. Reassemble the flywheel.
2. The noise is coming from the flywheel itself. Replace it.

If the console also shows an error while resistance is dead, work the error first — see the card `cc81-2020-e2-tension-motor-failure`.
