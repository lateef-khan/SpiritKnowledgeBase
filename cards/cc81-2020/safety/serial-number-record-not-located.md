The CC81-2019 book (the CC81-2020 machine) says **RECORD YOUR SERIAL NUMBER**, asks the owner to fill in the blank with the dealer name, phone and purchase date, and wants the self-addressed product registration card completed and mailed or registered online at www.soletreadmills.com. Service requests must include the serial number, and an altered or removed serial voids warranty coverage.

No on-machine location is ever named on the registration or safety pages - no decal, plate, frame or console position. Registration and warranty terms themselves belong to the warranty section. The same fact for the SC200/SC300 line lives on the linked elliptical card.
