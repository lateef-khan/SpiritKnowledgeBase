**Check the power to the console before you replace anything.**

| Reason | What to do |
|---|---|
| The LCD light is broken. | Replace with a new LCD, or a new console. |
| Power to the console is too low. | Check the AC power is **100 to 240 V**. Check the power reaching the console. Replace the lower controller. |

**The manual prints this condition twice.** The second row reads "LCD displays not bright, incomplete or imperfect", gives one reason ("LCD displays are broken") and one fix ("replace with new console"). The two rows are near duplicates and the manual does not say how they differ. Work the first row, which is the fuller one.

If the console has no light at all, that is a different answer — see the card `cc81-2020-console-no-power`.
