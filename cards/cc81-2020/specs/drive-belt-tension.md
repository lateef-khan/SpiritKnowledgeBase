**190 Hz, plus or minus 10, measured with a sonic belt tension meter.**

Set it by adjusting the **idler wheel assembly**. Do this every time the flywheel or the drive belt has been off.

The manual prints one figure and prints it in one place only. It is a frequency, not a Newton figure. The Sole spin bikes use Newtons instead, so do not carry a bike number over to this machine.

Before you set the tension, adjust the **inner nut on the flywheel axle with a 17 mm wrench** so the drive belt sits on the **centre of the drive pulley**, then tighten the nuts.
