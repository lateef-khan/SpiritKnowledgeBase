Do the handlebar job first — see the card `cc81-2020-handlebar-replacement`.

1. Follow the handlebar replacement steps to take the handlebar off.
2. Remove the screws on the **right driving assembly** with a **12 mm wrench**, then pull the axle out.
3. Remove the screws on the **rod end bearing of the right connecting arm** with a **17 mm wrench and an M6 open-end wrench**, then take the right driving assembly off.
4. Remove the screws on **slider covers A and B** with a screwdriver, then take slider covers A and B off.
5. Loosen the nut and bolt on the **idler wheel assembly** with a **13 mm wrench and an M6 open-end wrench**.
6. Remove the screws on the **rear rail assembly** with an **M4 open-end wrench**, then take the rear rail assembly off.
7. Remove the screws on the **axle for the slide wheel** with **two 2 mm wrenches**, then pull the axle out. Take off the **track wheel and the plastic wheel**.
8. Remove the screws on **console master covers R and L** with a screwdriver, take the covers off, then take the **strap hold down** off.
9. Remove the screws that hold the **connection welding group and the strap hold down** together with a **14 mm wrench**.
10. Remove the screws that hold the **connection welding group on the main frame** with an **M8 open-end wrench**, then take the connection welding group off.
11. Remove the screws that hold the **strap hold down on the main frame** with an **M8 open-end wrench**, then take the strap hold down off.
12. Refitting: follow the steps in reverse.

**Three notes for refitting**

- **Note 1:** make sure the axle and the L-plate are lined up before tightening the screws.
- **Note 2:** the plastic wheels must be assembled **diagonally**.
- **Note 3:** after reassembling the **handgrip stabilizer assembly**, adjust the screws on the idler wheel assembly with an **M6 open-end wrench** so the handgrip stabilizer assembly slides smoothly with no swing.
