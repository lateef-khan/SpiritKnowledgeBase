The steps are written for the right hand side. The left side is built the same way.

1. Remove the screws on the **slide wheel cover** with a screwdriver, then take the slide wheel cover off.
2. Remove the screws on the **outside of the axle** that hold **linked assembly R and connecting arm R** with a **12 mm wrench**. Pull the axle out and separate linked assembly R from connecting arm R.
3. Remove the screws on the **inside of the axle** that hold **swing assembly R and connecting arm R** with a **12 mm wrench**. Pull the axle out, separate swing assembly R from connecting arm R, then take connecting arm R off.
4. When reassembling the axle, make sure it lines up with the **linked bracket**.
5. Remove the **C ring and wave washer** on the slide wheel with **circlip pliers**, then separate the slide wheel from the connecting arm.
6. Remove the screws that hold the **pedal** to the connecting arm with a screwdriver, then take the pedal off.
7. Remove the screws that hold **swing assembly R** on the main frame with a **12 mm wrench**, then take swing assembly R off.
8. Remove the screws that hold **linked assembly R and the crank arm** with a **12 mm wrench**, then take linked assembly R off.
9. Refitting: follow the steps in reverse.

Step 4 is printed in the middle of the removal steps, between steps 3 and 5. It is a refitting instruction. It is kept here in its printed position.

This job is step 1 of the rail tube job and of the flywheel and drive belt job.
