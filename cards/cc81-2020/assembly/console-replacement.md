1. Remove the screws on the console with a screwdriver.
2. Disconnect the computer cable.
3. Refitting: connect the computer cable **first**, before anything else.
4. Fix the console on the unit by tightening the screws.

**Note:** none of the wires may be pinched.

The manual gives no screw count and no screw size for this step.
