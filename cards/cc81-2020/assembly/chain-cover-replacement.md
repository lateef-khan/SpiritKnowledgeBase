1. Remove the screws on **console master cover R and L** with a screwdriver, then take the covers off.
2. Remove the screws on **chain cover R and L** with a screwdriver.
3. Remove the screws that hold the chain cover on the **main frame** with a screwdriver.
4. Remove the screws that hold the right chain cover and the left chain cover together with a screwdriver.
5. Remove the nut on the **DC power cable** with a screwdriver, then take the left chain cover off.
6. Remove the screws that hold the **rear shroud** on the main frame with a screwdriver, then take the rear shroud off.
7. Refitting: follow the steps in reverse.

**Two notes**

- None of the wires may be pinched.
- Make sure the joint area of chain cover R and L and the rear shroud is tight before tightening the screws.
