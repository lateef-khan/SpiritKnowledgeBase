**The copy of the FS5.8e owner's manual in this knowledge base has no warranty page.** The file is a 24-page scan with no text layer (`FS5.8e_OM_1558012.pdf`, Producer *3-Heights PDF Security Shell*, Title *Scanned Document*). Its contents page (PDF p. 2, printed 1) lists *Manufacturer's Limited Warranty* as the last section, after *General Maintenance*, but the last scanned page (PDF p. 24) is printed page 22, *Rate of Perceived Exertion*, and no page in the file carries *Effective*, *LIMITED WARRANTY*, *EXCLUSIONS* or the Jonesboro address. Every page was OCR'd at 300 dpi in all four rotations and searched; the sibling FS5.9e book, laid out the same way, runs to 28 PDF pages and prints its warranty on PDF p. 27 (printed 25), so the FS5.8e scan is short by about four pages - the warranty page, the general-maintenance page and the back cover among them.

**So this book states no warranty period, no exclusions and no service steps for the FS5.8e.** What it does print:

- the **ATTENTION** box on the contents page - *THIS ELLIPTICAL IS INTENDED FOR RESIDENTIAL USE ONLY AND IS WARRANTED FOR THIS APPLICATION. ANY OTHER APPLICATION VOIDS THIS WARRANTY IN ITS ENTIRETY* (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-use-environment-residential-only-any-other-use-voids-it`);
- the **Product Registration** page (PDF p. 3, printed 1), stamped `FS58e_20130605`: *The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to XTERRA Fitness. You can also go to www.xterrafitness.com/warrantyreg.html to register online*, and customer service at **(870)336-4286** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover`, `xterra-warranty-support-contacts-which-phone-number-each-bike-elliptical-and-climber-book-prints`);
- the electrical page's sentence that a ten-percent voltage variation is *not covered under your warranty* (`xterra-warranty-what-the-electrical-and-console-pages-say-is-not-covered`).

**Do not lend it the FS5.9e figures.** The FS5.9e page, effective August 1, 2012, gives the frame and brake for life, seven years on parts and a year of labor (`fs59e-2014-warranty-periods-frame-and-brake-lifetime-parts-7-years-labor-1-year`); the two books share a layout and a print date, and the FS5.8e stamp (`20130605`) is a year after that effective date, but nothing in the FS5.8e file confirms its table. Treat the FS5.8e period as unconfirmed until a complete copy of the book or a warranty card is found.
