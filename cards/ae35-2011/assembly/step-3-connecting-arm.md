**Untie the wire holding the Sleeve Spacer (24)** in place on the ends of the **Connecting Arms (8 & 9)**. Align the rod-end holes with the holes in the brackets of the left and right **Handle Bars (10 & 11)**, connector arm ends inside the brackets. Secure both sides with a **Hex Head Bolt (134)**, **Flat Washer (170)** and **Nylon Nut (159)** using **Wrenches (184 & 187)**. The WE35 books print this step word for word with the same numbers; see that card.
