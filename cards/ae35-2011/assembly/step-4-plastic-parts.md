1. **Console covers (94 & 95)** with **three Phillips Head Screws (139)** and **two Sheet Metal Screws (142)**.
2. **Outer Connecting Arm Covers (109 & 110)** matched with the pre-installed inner covers **(111 & 112)**; each side with **one Phillips Head Screw (139)** and **two Sheet Metal Screws (142)**, arrows pointing up or they break.
3. **Sliding Wheel Covers (104)**, each side with **four Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
4. **Stabilizer Covers (117 and 118)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
5. **Front Stabilizer Cover (103)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
6. **Incline Cover brackets (25)**, bent-tab hole facing rearward, with **four Phillips Head Screws (145)**; **Rear incline bar cover (115)** with **two Screws (139)**.
7. **Rear Stabilizer Cover (116)** with **four Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
8. **After the elliptical power is plugged in, elevate the incline to Level 8** and install the **Incline Rail front cover (113)** up against the middle stabilizer tube with **four Phillips Head Screws (139)**.

These steps consume 23 of the bag's 29 Phillips screws and 6 of its 10 sheet metal screws; the pre-installed inner covers (111 & 112) take none in print, and the book does not assign the rest.
