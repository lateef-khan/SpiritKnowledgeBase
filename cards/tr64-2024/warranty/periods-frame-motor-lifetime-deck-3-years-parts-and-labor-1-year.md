**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the warranty page of this book, under the heading *Effective June 07, 2024 ... LIMITED WARRANTY*. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**The three-year deck is unique to this book in the Xterra range.** Its sibling TR6.6 of 2021 prints deck 5 years and parts 2 years (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-periods-frame-motor-lifetime-deck-5-years-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`); the TR300 prints deck 1 year (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-periods-frame-motor-lifetime-deck-parts-labor-1-year`). The page is otherwise the TR300/TR6.6 text with the effective date moved to June 07, 2024 and *Parts 1 Years* printed with the stray plural the TR300 also has.
