The book states: "The TR6.4 Treadmill has **five factory preset programs, two user defined
programs and one manual program**."

- **Five presets**: Ascent, Glute Blast, Adventure, Xtreme and Interval, each pictured as a
  Work (speed) and an Incline profile.
- **User 1 and User 2.**
- **Manual.**

**No heart-rate program and no Fitness program.** The setup text still says entering your age
"affects the Heart Rate Bar Graph Display and the Heart Rate programs" - a sentence carried
over from the TR6.6 book, which does have HR-1 and HR-2. The TR6.4 book prints no HR program
page; a loose search of the whole text for "HR1", "HR-1", "fitness" and "heart rate program"
finds only that setup sentence and the generic training-zone page.
