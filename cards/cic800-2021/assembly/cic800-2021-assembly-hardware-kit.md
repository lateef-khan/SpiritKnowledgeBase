Step four prints "Gather HARDWARE FOR STEP 4" but **no table**. Step five is headed
**NO HARDWARE FOR STEP 5**.

## The step one screw is a typo for M8

The step one table calls part **3** an **M5X1.25X55L** screw. The step two table calls
the **same part number** an **M8X1.25X55L**, and both steps drive it through M8 flat
washers into M8x1.25 nylon nuts. M5 thread does not take a 1.25 pitch. The parts list
settles it: item **113** is a **Hex-socket head screw SUS304 M8*P1.25*55L, qty 4** -
exactly the four the two stabilizer steps consume between them.

**Read step one as M8 x 1.25 x 55L.** This was confirmed by re-extracting PDF page 9 on
its own, so it is the printed page and not an extraction artefact.

## Item 6 in the parts list is a different screw

The parts list also carries item **6, Hex screw M8*1.25*45L, qty 2** - ten millimetres
shorter. That page is a flattened image whose digits come from OCR, so do not order from
it; item 113 at 55L is the one the assembly steps and both hardware tables agree on.

## The CIC850 uses none of these numbers

Its stabilizers take screw **65** with washer **5** and **no nylon nut at all**. The two
machines share a cover part number but not a hardware kit; see
[that card](../../cic850-2022/assembly/cic850-2022-assembly-hardware-kit.md).
