Numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing item numbers. Quantities are on
[the hardware card](cic800-2021-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Unpacking

1. Cut the straps, then **cut along the dotted line on the bottom of the box**; lift the
   box over the unit and unpack.
2. Locate the hardware package. Remove the tools first, then the hardware for each step
   as you reach it.

**The unpacking text says the hardware is separated into six steps. The manual prints
five.** There is no STEP SIX anywhere in the book. Treat the sentence as boilerplate -
the CIC850 manual carries the identical sentence above **eight** steps.

## Step one - front stabilizer

1. Position the **FRONT STABILIZER (1)** on the frame bracket. **The transport wheels
   must face up and toward the front of the bike.**
2. Attach with 2 hex screws (3), 4 flat washers (4) and 2 nylon nuts (5). **Do not
   overtighten** - the stabilizer can deform.
3. Make sure the **levelling feet with nut are fully screwed into the stabilizer**.

## Step two - rear stabilizer

Identical hardware and the same two cautions, on the **REAR STABILIZER (2)**. No
orientation note, because the rear stabilizer carries no wheels.

## Step three - bottle holder

Assemble the **BOTTLE HOLDER (12)** with 2 socket hex screws (11) and 2 flat washers (13).

## Step four - handlebar and post

1. Pull out the **SAFETY PIN (2)** and slide the **HANDLEBAR** onto the **POST (11)**.
2. Insert the handlebar assembly into the frame tube and tighten firmly with the
   **star pop-pin**.

**The item numbers in this step contradict step three**, where (11) is a socket hex screw
and (13) a washer. The parts list gives no item 2 or 11 matching a safety pin or a post
either. Work from the parts in front of you here.

## Step five - pedals

No hardware. R and L are stamped on the spindles; grease the threads if you can; the R
pedal goes on the chain guard side. Full detail on
[the pedal card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-cycle-assembly-pedal-reverse-thread-no-torque-figure.md).

## What comes next

Levelling, the adjustment knobs, the saddle pitch and moving the bike are all on the
INDOOR CYCLE SETUP page that follows; see the linked cards.
