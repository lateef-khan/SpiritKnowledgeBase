**No console is listed.** The CIC800 has no console in the carton list and no console
assembly step; its display, if fitted, is not part of this build. Contrast the CIC850,
whose list names a Console Bracket and whose step five fits a DT-3268F console.

The **Adjustment Handle** is the star pop-pin that step four tightens.

## The CIC850 list has three more lines

The CIC850 carton adds a **Tablet Holder**, a **Dumbbell Holder** and a **Console
Bracket**, and renames the Adjustment Handle an **Adjustment Handlebar Knob**. See
[that card](../../cic850-2022/assembly/cic850-2022-assembly-parts-included.md).
