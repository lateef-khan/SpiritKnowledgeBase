Three tools, printed as a TOOLS INCLUDED panel beside the unpacking steps:

- **4mm Allen Wrench**
- **5mm Allen Wrench**
- **13/17mm Combination Wrench**

**The tools are not lettered and not numbered** - no #85, no A/B/C. The assembly steps
never name a tool either, so which wrench goes with which fastener is left to the reader:
the 13/17mm covers the M8 nylon nuts of steps one and two, and the Allen wrenches the
socket screws of step three.

## The carton does not hold the wrench the maintenance chapter asks for

Levelling needs a **14mm wrench**, and the 13/17mm combination wrench does not open to
14. You will need your own. See
[the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

## The CIC850 carton holds a fourth tool

The CIC850 ships the same three, **letters them A, B and C**, and adds an **M22 Wrench
(D)** for the star pop-pin. The CIC800 has no M22 wrench and no lettering.
