Hardware step 4: **1/1 hinge bolt (#10L/R), 2 wave washers (#11), 2 flat washers (#12), 1/1 nylon lock nut (#51L/R - the hardware box prints "#54L/R", the pedal tube cover's number), 2 hex head bolts (#20) M10*78, 2 washers (#17), 2 nylon nuts (#16), 2 lock knobs (#15) - the pack holds four - 2 sleeves (#18)**; pre-installed, 2 screws (#55) on each cover and 1 self-tapping screw (#56) on each tube.

1. Attach the left pedal tube (9L) to the left crank arm (24L). Secure using one left hinge bolt (10L), one wave washer (11), one flat washer (12) and one nylon lock nut (51L).
2. Repeat for the right pedal tube (9R) to the right crank arm.
3. Attach the left pedal tube (9L) to the left bottom handlebar (21L). Slide the sleeve (18) into the left pedal tube (9L) and the left bottom handlebar (21L). Secure using one hex head bolt (20), one washer (17), one nylon nut (16). Attach the cover for pedal tube (54L/R) to the pedal tube, secure using two screws (55) and one self-tapping screw (56). **Attach the left pedal (13L) to the left pedal tube (9L), secure using hex head bolts (14) and two lock knobs (15).**
4. Repeat for the right pedal tube (9R) to the right bottom handlebar (21R).

The pedals sit on two M8*45 hex bolts each and are held by lock knobs turned by hand, so the pedal can be repositioned; the hybrid bikes bolt theirs with carriage bolts and nylon nuts.
