Hardware step 5: **2 Allen bolts (#23) M8*16, 2 curved washers (#7)**; pre-assembled on the console, 4 screws (#48).

1. Attach the stationary handlebar (39) to the front post (22). Secure using two Allen bolts (23) and two curved washers (7).
2. Insert the hand pulse wires (46) through the hole on the front post (22), and then pull them out from the top of the front post (22). Connect the computer wire and hand pulse wire with the computer (38) accordingly.
3. Attach the computer (38) to the front post (22) and fasten with four screws (48).

No seat on this machine; the hybrid bikes fit theirs in this step.
