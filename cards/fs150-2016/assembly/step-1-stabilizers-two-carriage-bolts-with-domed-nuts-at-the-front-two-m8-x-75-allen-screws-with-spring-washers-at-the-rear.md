Hardware step 1: **2 carriage bolts (#6) M10*75, 2 curved washers (#49) 10*22, 2 domed nuts (#8) M10; 2 Allen bolts (#27) M8*75, 2 spring washers (#25), 2 curved washers (#7) 8*20*1.5**.

1. Attach the front stabilizer (3) to the front of the main frame (1), fasten with two carriage bolts (6), two curved washers (49) and two domed nuts (8).
2. Attach the rear stabilizer (2) to the rear of the main frame (1), fasten with two Allen bolts (27), two spring washers (25) and two curved washers (7).

Two different fastenings, front and rear; the hybrid bikes use carriage bolts at both ends.
