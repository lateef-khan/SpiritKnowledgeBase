**Outlines:** Console Assembly, Handgrip Foam, Console Mast Cover, Mesh Seat Back, Pedal,
**Handgrip Resistance Label**, Round Disk, Aluminum Track, Crank Arm, Seat, Drink Bottle Holder,
Front Shroud (L), Bottom Cover, Drink Bottle Holder (R), Handgrip End Cap.

One label on the page is left in Chinese beside its English: "橢圓斜管塞 Handgrip End Cap" - a
plug for an elliptical's angled tube, which is what the part is called at the factory.

**Skeleton:** Mast Handle bar Assembly, Seat Back Frame, Console Mast Cover, Seat Handle Bar, Drive
Pulley, Seat Carriage, Crank Arm, Idler Wheel Assembly, **Console Display Board**, Flywheel, Rear
Stabilizer.

The *Handgrip Resistance Label* marks the up/down resistance buttons in the seat handles - the
feature the XBR95 and XBR55 share and the XBR25 lacks. No dimensions or weights are printed.
