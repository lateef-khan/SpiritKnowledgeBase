**Upper Controllers (p. 8)** - **Speaker**, **Cooling FAN** and **DISPLAY**, all on the console. The speaker
is the one console part the XBR25, XBR55 and XBU55 books of the same year do not label.

**Lower Controller and Driver (p. 9)** - **SPEED SENSOR**, **Driver board** and **HYBRID GENERATOR**, all
photographed inside the shroud.

**This is the self-powered set.** The other three 2016 residential books name a **tension motor** in place
of the generator and driver board (`spirit-xbr-xbu-2016-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`); the commercial
CU900 and CR900 books name the same generator-and-driver-board pair without the speaker
(`cu900ent-electronic-parts-locations`). The same book calls the generator a **Generator Brake** on its
Electrical Configurations page and prints its work voltage there as DC 0.4~14 V; which bikes carry a
generator and which a gear motor is on `spirit-residential-bike-specs-gear-motor-or-generator-brake`.

**The 2023 XBR95 renames one of these.** Its page labels Cooling Fan, Speaker, Speed Sensor, **Brake
Controller** and Hybrid Generator - the driver board under a new name
(`xbr95-2023-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`).

No part number or rating is printed on either page.
