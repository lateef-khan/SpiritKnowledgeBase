| Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| #79 | Ø8 x Ø18 x 1.5T Flat Washer | 6 |
| #87 | 5/16" x 1/2" Button Head Socket Bolt | 10 |
| #88 | Ø8 x Ø19 x 1.5T Curved Washer | 4 |
| #104 | Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Philips Head Screw Driver | 1 |
| #105 | Lubricant | 1 |

Six bolts and all six flat washers go into the uprights at step 3; the other four bolts and the four
curved washers hold the console support at step 5. **No safety key is drawn on this checklist**, unlike
the TR150 and TR260 pages. The curved washer is Ø8 x Ø19 here; the TR150/TR260 washer is Ø8 x Ø23.
