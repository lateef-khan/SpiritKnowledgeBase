**This step uses six flat washers and no curved washers** - the TR150/TR260 version of the same step
uses four flat and two curved.

Insert the **uprights (L, R) (3, 4)** into the **frame base (2)** with the **6 button head socket bolts
(87)** and **6 flat washers (79)**, using the Allen wrench. "Please secure the screw without tightening
it." The book notes the step may work better with a second person assisting.

The hardware sidebar prints "Bolt (87) Qty: 6" and "Flat Washer (79) Qty: 6". The step 5 page adds
"Please tighten all screws after assembly is complete", which is when these six are tightened.
