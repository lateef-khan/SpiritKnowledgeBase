The manual covers the speed switch and the incline switch, and the "only up" or "only down" cases, with one list.

**Speed adjustment switch**

1. The connector of the SPEED CABLE and the CONSOLE is not connected properly. Connect the cables again.
2. The connector of the SPEED CABLE and the SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH with cable is not connected properly. Check the wire of the switch.
3. The button of the SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH is broken. Replace with a new cable, or replace the switch.
4. The connector of the SPEED CABLE or the switch cable is damaged. Replace with a new cable.

**Incline adjustment switch** - the same list with INCLINE CABLE in place of SPEED CABLE.

Run the console **Key Test** from the engineering menu first: it tells you whether the console is seeing the button at all.
