The motor is getting power but not enough of it. The cause is low AC voltage at the treadmill.

- **Do not use an extension cord.** If one is unavoidable it must be as short as possible and heavy duty, **16 AWG minimum**.
- The machine needs **a minimum of 220 volt AC at 50 Hz**. **On a 120 Vac machine it needs 110 Vac.**
- If the household supply is low, contact an electrician or your dealer.

If the belt speed and the displayed speed disagree at every speed rather than only at the top, that is a calibration problem instead.
