| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1 House breaker tripped | Reset it |
| 2 Treadmill breaker tripped | Reset the treadmill breaker |
| 3 Treadmill controller shut down and the LED would be ON | Belt and deck lubrication; turn off the ON/OFF switch and turn the power on again |

Reason 3 is the one worth reading carefully: the manual answers a latched controller with **lubrication** as well as a power cycle, because friction is what makes the controller latch.
