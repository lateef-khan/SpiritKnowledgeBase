| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Console is not calibrated | 1. Calibrate the console. 2. Replace the controller. |

**If the belt tops out around 10 kph while the display reads higher, that is a supply voltage problem instead**, not a calibration one; the checklist answers that case with the extension cord and household voltage.
