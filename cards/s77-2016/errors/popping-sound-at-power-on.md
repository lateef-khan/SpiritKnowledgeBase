| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Incorrect input power, varistor is blown broken on controller | Check the voltage of power is **220V**. Replace controller. **On a 120 Vac machine it needs 110V.** |

The varistor is the surge component on the controller's input; a blown one is a sign the supply is wrong or unstable, so measure the outlet before fitting a new controller.
