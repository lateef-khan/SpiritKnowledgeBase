The matrix carries this condition twice, with two answers:

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Controller is broken | 1 Turn off the AC switch and turn the power on again. 2 Replace the controller and calibrate it. |
| Controller was broken | Replace with a new controller and calibrate it. |

Try the power cycle first; it costs nothing. **A new controller always needs a calibration afterwards.**
