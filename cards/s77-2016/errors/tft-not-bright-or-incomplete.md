The manual splits this into two rows.

**TFT LCD not bright, incomplete or imperfect**

1. Connector fell off. Check the connector again.
2. TFT LCD light is broken. Replace with a new LCD or console.
3. Power to console too low. Check the power to the console; replace the lower controller.

**TFT LCD displays not bright, incomplete or imperfect**

1. The TFT LCD displays are broken. Replace with a new console.

Start with the connector: it is free to check and it is the first cause listed.
