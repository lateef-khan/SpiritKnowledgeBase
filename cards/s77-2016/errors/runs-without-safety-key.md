| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Safety device is broken (short) | Replace the safety key device or the console |

**This is a safety fault.** The safety key is the emergency stop; a shorted safety device means it cannot stop the belt.

The opposite fault, a safety device broken **open**, gives E0 or a dead display instead.
