| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1 Motor **M+ or M-** wire isn't connected into the right position | Check and plug it in again |
| 2 Motor is broken | Replace the motor, or check the wire and connector for damage |
| 3 Treadmill controller shut down and the LED would be ON | Turn off the ON/OFF switch and turn the power on again |

Reason 3 is the cheap one to try first: the controller has latched off and a full power cycle at the AC switch clears it.

If the belt moves and then stops with **E1** on the display, that is the calibration and speed sensor path instead.
