| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1 Hands not on the sensors, or only one hand on a sensor | Hold **both** hand pulse grips |
| 2 The connector of the HANDPULSE wire and the console is not connected properly | Connect the cable again |
| 3 The wires were damaged when connecting the HANDPULSE wire and the console | Replace with a new cable |
| 4 Hand pulse board is broken | Replace the console or the hand pulse board |

Reason 1 is the usual one.
