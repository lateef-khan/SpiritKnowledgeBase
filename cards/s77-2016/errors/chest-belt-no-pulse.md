| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1 Chest belt not worn properly | Check the belt has proper contact with the skin and is oriented correctly |
| 2 Distance too far, beyond the receiver's range | Use the chest belt in front of the console, within **3 feet** |
| 3 Chest belt battery weak or dead | Replace with a new lithium battery, type **CR2032** |

A separate row covers a belt that only reads when held very close to the treadmill: that is also a weak battery, same CR2032 replacement.
