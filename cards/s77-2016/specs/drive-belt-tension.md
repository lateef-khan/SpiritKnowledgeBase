| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Setting | The **white LBS area, between 70 and 75 LBS** |
| Measured with | A tension measuring device (belt tension gauge) |
| Adjusted with | A 14 mm open end wrench on the belt tension screw |
| Locked with | A 14 mm T-shaped socket wrench on the 4 motor securing screws |

**The manual repeats this same figure after three different jobs**: after replacing the motor, after replacing the front or rear roller, and after replacing the running deck, belt or cushions. It has to be reset every time the motor has been loosened.

A drive belt that is too loose shows up as a running belt that still slips when its own tension is correct.
