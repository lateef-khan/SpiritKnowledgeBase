| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Variable speed DC motor |
| Voltage | 0 to 180 V DC; **0 to 90 V DC on a 120 Vac machine** |
| Wires | Three: red, black, green |

| Wire | Terminal | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Red | M+ | DC voltage here turns the motor clockwise |
| Black | M- | DC voltage here turns the motor counter-clockwise |
| Green | Ground | - |

The higher the voltage, the faster the motor turns.

**One line of the manual names a colour that is not in the wire list.** The general information paragraph reads "If there is DC voltage on the Red (white) wire (M+)", while the same paragraph lists the motor's wires as red, black and green. The replacement procedure in section 11-3 confirms the pair as **red M+ and black M-**, with a greenish yellow grounding wire.
