| Field | Normal version | CE version |
|---|---|---|
| Power input | 110 AC | 230 VAC |
| Filter choke circuit | Not fitted | **Added** |
| Functions and outlines | The same | The same |

The manual says the two versions are "exactly the same in functions and outlines" apart from those two points, and shows the extra filter choke on the circuit diagram.

**This matters when you read voltages elsewhere in the manual.** Most of the electrical text is written for the 230 V machine with the 120 Vac figure in brackets: the drive motor is 0-180 VDC (0-90 VDC on the 120 Vac machine), the incline motor is 230 V AC (115 V AC on the 120 Vac machine), and the outlet requirement is 220 V 10 A (110 V 15 A on the 120 Vac machine).

**The note's heading and its first line disagree on the model.** The heading says ST725 and the sentence says ST535.
