Some household circuit breakers are not rated for the high inrush current a treadmill draws when it is first switched on, or even during use.

**The tell-tale**: the house breaker trips while the breaker on the treadmill itself does not.

**The fix**: replace the home breaker with a **high inrush type**. The manual names two examples, available from most electrical supply stores:

- Grainger part # 1D237
- www.squared.com part # QO120HM

**This is not a warranty defect.** The manual states it is a condition the manufacturer has no ability to control.
