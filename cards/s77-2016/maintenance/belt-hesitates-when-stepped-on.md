Three causes across the manual's two entries:

1. Insufficient lubricant on the tread belt. See belt and deck lubrication.
2. Tread belt tension insufficient. See the belt tension adjustment.
3. **The motor drive belt may be loose.** This is the one people miss: if the running belt is tight enough and still slips, the drive belt under the front cover is the cause. It is set with a tension gauge to the white LBS area, between **70 and 75 LBS**.
