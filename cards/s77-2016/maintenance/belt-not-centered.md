Two causes in the manual.

**The user's stride.** Someone who favours one foot moves the belt. If that walking pattern is natural for them, track the belt slightly **off-centre to the side opposite the belt movement**.

**Belt tension.** The matrix row reads "Tread belt tension not even across tread belt" and sends you to the belt adjustment.

The tracking adjustment itself is a separate procedure: 10 mm Allen wrench, rear roller, **left side only**, belt at 3 to 5 kph, quarter turn at a time, clockwise moves the belt to the right.
