**Adjust from the rear roller only.** The adjustment bolts are at the ends of the step rails, inside the end caps; you reach them through a small hole in the end cap.

1. Tighten the rear roller bolts only enough to stop the belt slipping at the front roller.
2. Turn **both** tension bolts in **quarter turn** increments.
3. Walk on the belt at a low speed after each pair of turns and check that it does not slip.
4. Keep going until the slipping stops.

**Do not overtighten.** Over tightening causes belt damage and premature bearing failure.

**If the belt feels tight enough and still slips**, the problem may be a loose motor drive belt under the front cover. The drive belt is set with a tension gauge to the white LBS area, between 70 and 75 LBS.

**Damage caused by improper tracking or tension adjustment is not covered by the warranty.**
