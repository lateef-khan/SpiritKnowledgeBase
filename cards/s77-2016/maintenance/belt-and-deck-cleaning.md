**Monthly**: wipe the edge of the belt and the area between the belt edge and the frame with a soft damp cloth or paper towel, reaching as far under the belt edge as is practical. **Use water only** - no cleaners, no abrasives. A mild soap and water solution with a nylon scrub brush cleans the top of the textured belt. Let the belt dry before using it.

**Monthly**: vacuum underneath the treadmill. Dirt, dust and pet hair block the air inlets and build up on the running belt.

**Yearly**: remove the black motor hood and vacuum out the dirt inside. **Unplug the power cord before this task.**

**Belt dust** happens during normal break-in until the belt stabilises. Wipe the excess off with a damp cloth.
