| Group | Values |
|---|---|
| Incline quick keys | 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 12, 15 |
| Speed quick keys | 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9, 12 |

The same quick key blocks are repeated on the handrails.

Other keys: **Function key** (sets data), **Fan key** (cooling fan on or off), **Enter**, **Display**, **Start**, **Stop** and the **Safety key** socket, which is the emergency function.

**The manual miscounts the quick keys.** Both entries in the ready mode section are printed as "5 preset buttons" while each list holds **seven** values.
