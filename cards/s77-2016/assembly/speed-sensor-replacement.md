1. Remove the motor top cover.
2. Remove the speed sensor wiring and replace the sensor **including its wire**.
3. **After replacement a test is required to check that the sensor registers the magnet.**

Set the gap to less than **3 mm** and line the sensor up with the magnet on the face of the front roller pulley. The controller's SPEED LED and a calibration are the two ways to confirm the result.
