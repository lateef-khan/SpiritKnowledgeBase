Eight illustrated assembly steps for the S77-2016 (hardware item numbers in brackets). Unlike the S77-2019 book there is no fixing-plate removal in step 2, no gussets named in step 3, six (not four) cover screws in step 6, and no tablet step.

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill from the carton onto a smooth, level floor with the rear at least 3 ft from any wall. Do not remove the Velcro belt until the wrap and Styrofoam are out; turning it on its side may cause it to fold up.
2. Remove the left/right Flat Head Socket Bolts (102) and the two Fixing Plate Stabilizers (101). Slide Console Mast Tube Covers (99 & 52 onto tube 5; 98 & 53 onto tube 4) onto the left and right mast tubes with the holes of 99 & 98 facing outward. Connect Lower Computer Cable (33) to Middle Computer Cable (32) on the right side. Do not pinch the cables.
3. Attach the left (5) and right (4) mast tubes with brackets (7 left, 8 right) to the base frame: three Flat Washers (79), three Split Washers (86) and three Button Head Socket Bolts (123) per side, plus two Button Head Socket Bolts (100) with two Curved Washers (124) at the front of each side. Partially tighten; leave loose until step 7 is finished. Then connect Speed Switch Cable (37) to upper Speed Cable (34), Computer Cable (32) to upper Computer Cable (31), and Incline Switch Cable (38) to upper Incline Cable (35). Have someone hold the console while connecting; do not pinch the cables.
5. Attach the Console Assembly (36) onto the uprights (5 & 4) with two Button Head Socket Bolts (91) and two Split Washers (86) per side; tighten completely with the M6 wrench.
6. Attach the left (52) and right (53) lower handlebar covers to the console with six Sheet Metal Screws (125).
7. Attach the support tube (3) between the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (100), place the Beverage Holder (120) on top, place the safety-key magnet (62) between Start and Stop, and check every bolt and screw is tight.
8. Slide the mast tube covers (99 & 98) to the bottom of each tube and fix with two Phillips Head Screws (127) per side.
