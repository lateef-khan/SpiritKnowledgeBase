1. Follow the front and rear roller procedure first.
2. Remove the left and right **foot rail caps**. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **6 screws** and the bracket from the back of the foot rail, then slide the foot rail off in the direction of the arrows.
3. Use a **combination M5 Allen wrench** to remove the **8 screws** securing the running board. Take the running board out and replace the board or the belt. If the cushions need replacing, remove the **6 cushions** and fit new ones. Reassemble in reverse order.
4. Set the drive belt tension with a tension measuring device to the **white LBS area, between 70 and 75 LBS**, then tighten the 4 securing screws.

Set the running belt tension and tracking after this job.
