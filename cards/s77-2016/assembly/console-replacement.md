1. Use an **M5 L Allen wrench** to loosen the **six sheet metal screws** and remove the console mast cover.
2. Use an L Allen wrench to remove the **4 button head socket bolts** from the console support.
3. Disconnect the console wiring and fit the new console.

**Calibrate after fitting a new console.**

The later ST728 manual for this machine family gives **four** sheet metal screws for the mast cover, not six, and calls for an M6 L Allen wrench on the support bolts.
