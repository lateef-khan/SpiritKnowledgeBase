1. Fold the treadmill first, then remove the old incline motor.
2. **Adjust the incline range to 225 mm minimum before installing the new motor.** The manual prints this twice, once in its own section and again in the replacement procedure.
3. Use a **combination M8 Allen wrench and a 14 mm wrench** to assemble the new incline motor.
4. Connect the incline motor wiring to the controller:

| Wire | Terminal |
|---|---|
| Red | UP |
| White | COM |
| Black | DOWN |

**Calibrate after fitting the new motor.**
