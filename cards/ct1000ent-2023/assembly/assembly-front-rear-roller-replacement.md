1. Use a screwdriver to remove the left and right **side adjustment screws** (figure 8.50).
2. Use the **No. 8 L-type hex wrench** (8 mm) to remove the **two rear wheel screws** - the rear roller's
   (figure 8.51).
3. Remove the motor cover (step 8.43 of the guide).
4. Release the belt as in step 8.47 of the motor procedure. "Please refer to 8.50 and 8.51 for the assembly of
   the left and right chain cover" - printed so; the covers meant are whatever steps 1 and 2 took off.
5. Remove the front roller with the No. 8 L-shaped hex wrench (figure 8.52).
6. Fit the new front and rear rollers, and assemble in the order of removal.
7. When assembled, adjust the running belt tension so the belt runs true.

**This book prints no drive-belt tension figure in the roller procedure.** The CT900 books close the same
procedure with "13 T-type sleeve, 120 ~ 130HZ"; this machine's motor procedure sets its belt to 80-90 lbs
instead, and that is the figure to use after step 4 here. No torque is printed.

The guide is a machine translation. Its words, as used on this card: **electronic watch** is the console,
**cross screwdriver** is a Phillips screwdriver, **umbrella head screws** are the guide's name for the
console and cover screws (no size is printed), and a **17th T-Shirt** or **13th T-sleeve** is a 17 mm or 13 mm
T-handle socket.
