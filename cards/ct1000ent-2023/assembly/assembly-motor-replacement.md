**Belt tension is 80-90 lbs on this machine - printed "80-90BLS".** The CT900 and CT900ENT books, which share
the rest of this guide, give 120 ~ 130 Hz for theirs. Different figure, different unit; do not swap them.

1. Remove the motor cover (step 8.43 of the guide: eight umbrella head screws).
2. Use a screwdriver to remove the motor ground wire (**green yellow**) and the motor's wires from the control
   panel (figure 8.45).
3. Use a **17 mm wrench** to hold the point marked with the red arrow in figure 8.46 and release the screws.
4. Use the **17 mm T-socket** ("17th T-shirt") to loosen the **four M10 caps**, then remove the belt and the
   motor (figure 8.47).
5. Fit the new motor by step 4 in reverse. **Do not secure the caps at first.** The motor needs to be pushed
   forward and the belt hooked back on.
6. Use the 17 mm wrench to adjust the belt tension to **80-90 lbs**. Then secure the caps.
7. Reconnect the motor ground wire and the motor wires to the control panel.

The steps are printed unnumbered under figure numbers; they are numbered here in the printed order. No
torque is printed for the caps, the tension gauge is not named, and the motor wires are not colour-coded in
the text.

The guide is a machine translation. Its words, as used on this card: **electronic watch** is the console,
**cross screwdriver** is a Phillips screwdriver, **umbrella head screws** are the guide's name for the
console and cover screws (no size is printed), and a **17th T-Shirt** or **13th T-sleeve** is a 17 mm or 13 mm
T-handle socket.
