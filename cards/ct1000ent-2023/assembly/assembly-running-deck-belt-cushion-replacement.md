1. Remove the front and rear rollers (steps 8.50 to 8.52 of the guide).
2. Use a screwdriver to remove the screws on the **rear cover** (figure 8.53).
3. Use the **No. 6 L-shaped hex wrench** (6 mm) to loosen the **8 screws of the retaining strip** (figure 8.54).
4. Remove the left and right **trims** in the direction of the arrow (figure 8.55).
5. Use the **No. 6 L-type hexagonal wrench** to remove the **eight running plate fixing screws** (figure 8.56).
   Lift the running board off and replace the board or the running belt. To change the cushions, remove the
   **8 buffers** and fit new ones (figure 8.57). Reassemble in the order of removal.
6. When assembled, adjust the running belt tension so the belt runs true.

Step 5 is where this book parts from the CT900 books: eight deck screws on a 6 mm key here, one screw on a
4 mm key there. The buffer count, eight, is the same in all three.

No screw count is printed for the rear cover, and no torque anywhere.

The guide is a machine translation. Its words, as used on this card: **electronic watch** is the console,
**cross screwdriver** is a Phillips screwdriver, **umbrella head screws** are the guide's name for the
console and cover screws (no size is printed), and a **17th T-Shirt** or **13th T-sleeve** is a 17 mm or 13 mm
T-handle socket.
