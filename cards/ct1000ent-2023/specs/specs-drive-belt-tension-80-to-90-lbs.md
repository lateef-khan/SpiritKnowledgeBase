**"Use 17th wrench to adjust the required belt tension, and the value can be adjusted to 80-90BLS."**

That is the whole statement, printed once, in the motor-replacement steps after the four M10 caps are
loosened with a "17th T-shirt" (a 17 mm T-handle socket) and the belt is hooked back. **BLS is printed as
written**; the CT800, CT850, 40T and XT books print the same step as "70~75 Lbs" on a belt gauge, so the
unit is almost certainly pounds, but this book never spells it out and never names a gauge.

This is the belt between the drive motor and the front roller, not the tread belt. The figure sits between
the CT900's **120 ~ 130 HZ** (a sonic-meter frequency, a different unit) and the 70-75 lbs of the rest of
the family; quote neither for this machine.
