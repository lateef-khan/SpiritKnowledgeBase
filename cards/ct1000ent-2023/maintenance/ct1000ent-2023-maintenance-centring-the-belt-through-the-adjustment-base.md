**This is the whole of what the CT1000ENT service manual prints on belt tracking, and it gives no wrench size, no turn fraction and no belt speed.** No owner's manual for this machine is in the knowledge base.

Check periodically that the running belt is in the middle. If it is not:

1. Remove the left and right side adjustment screw and take out the **Adjustment Base** (Figure 8.31).
2. Then adjust the screws to keep the running belt in the middle (Figure 8.32).

The "adjustment base" is the cover over each rear-roller adjustment bolt; the roller replacement procedure in the same chapter (8.4, Figure 8.50) starts by removing the same "left and right side adjustment screw". The book does not say which side to turn, which direction moves the belt which way, or how much to turn at a time.

## What the CT900 books add

The CT1000ENT is the 230 V ENT sibling of the CT900 platform, and the CT900 and CT900ENT service manuals print the full tracking procedure for their rear roller: a 10 mm Allen wrench, adjustments from the left side only, 3 to 5 kph, clockwise moves the belt right, a quarter turn at a time (`ct850-2016-treadbelt-tracking-adjustment`). **The CT1000ENT book does not print those figures**, so they are not carded for it; they are the nearest printed guidance, not the CT1000ENT's own.

## Nothing else on belt care

The same book has no tread-belt tension, cleaning, lubrication or deck-life text — `spirit-ct-maintenance-no-general-maintenance-chapter-in-service-manual`. Its matrix row for a belt that hesitates says "see treadmill belt lubrication", and no such section exists.
