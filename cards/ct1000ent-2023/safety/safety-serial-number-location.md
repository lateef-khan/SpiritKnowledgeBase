**Section 8.1 is two photographs and a heading; the manual names no part in words.** What the photographs
show:

- The label is on the **front face of the motor cover** - the face that carries, from left to right,
  the power inlet with its round push-button breaker and rocker switch under an `AC230V` label, the
  four-socket port panel (two network jacks, an HDMI socket and a coaxial connector), and then the
  label, with a `MADE IN` mark to its right.
- It sits **immediately to the right of the port panel**, on the same face, at the same height.

**The label photographed is a MAC and model label, and its serial-number line cannot be read.** At
300 dpi it reads `MAC: DB:6C:EC:1A:E7:70`, `MODEL: CT1000-E770`, a Chinese year-month-of-manufacture
line reading `202303`, and a QR code. The manual calls this the serial number location; if the number
you need is not on that label, read the QR code or the label beside it on the machine and confirm it.

**No owner's manual for the CT1000ENT is on file**, so this photograph is the only statement of where
the number is. Other Spirit treadmills put it elsewhere - the CT900 service manual photographs its
label on the face with the power switch too (`ct900-product-labels`), while the 2023 XT owner's
manuals say the rear of the motor cover (`xt-2023-safety-serial-number-sticker`) and the CT800 and
CT850 owner's manuals say the front of the treadmill by the power switch
(`spirit-ct800-safety-serial-number-and-product-labels`).
