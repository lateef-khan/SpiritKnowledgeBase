> **Warranty will be void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment. (Examples: Garage, outside, carport, etc)**

Both conditions have to hold: the space must be **climate-controlled** *and* **insulated**. A garage, a carport and any outside location are named as examples that fail the test.

This is a **separate and stricter rule** from the operating environment limits on page 3, which allow **40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit** and **95% non-condensing humidity**. A space can sit inside those operating limits and still void the warranty under this exclusion.
