> **Warranties outside of North America may vary. Sole USA warranty coverage is limited to the Continental United States. Sole USA does not sell or service equipment in Hawaii, Alaska, or outside of the United States. Please contact your local dealer for details regarding warranty availability in your region.**

Read the exclusion carefully: **Hawaii and Alaska are named alongside "outside of the United States"** as places Sole USA does not sell or service, even though they are US states - coverage there is a local dealer question, not a Sole USA one.

The Service section separately makes the owner responsible for **all transportation costs both to the factory and back**, which matters most for anyone outside the Continental United States.
