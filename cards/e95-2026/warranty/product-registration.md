The manual states the registration rule in two places, and each half adds something.

**Page 1, Product Registration**

- **Record the serial number** of this fitness product in the space provided on that page.
- The **self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to SOLE**.
- You can also go to **www.soletreadmills.com** under the **support tab** to register online.

**Page 27, Normal Responsibilities item 1** - the registration card must be returned to the address on the card **within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the warranty; **outside the 10 day period** the warranty can still be registered **if you have proof of purchase** (receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation).

The Exclusions add the teeth: the warranty does not cover **products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at SOLE**, and **SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists**. Exclusions also void coverage on **products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered**, which is why page 1 asks you to write the number down.

The dealer name, dealer telephone number and purchase date are recorded on the unnumbered welcome page at the front of the manual.
