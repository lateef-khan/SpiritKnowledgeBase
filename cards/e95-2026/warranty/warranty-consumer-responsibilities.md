This warranty applies **only to products in ordinary household use**, and the consumer is responsible for:

1. The **warranty registration card** must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card **within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty; or, if outside the 10 day period, the warranty **can be registered if you have proof of purchase** (receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation).
2. Proper use **of the treadmill** in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual, including general maintenance (belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under motor cover, and lubrication - see all components under the General Maintenance section).
3. **Proper installation** in accordance with the instructions provided with the elliptical and with **all local electric codes**.
4. **Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage**, replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring.
5. **Expenses for making the elliptical accessible for servicing**, including any item that was not part of the elliptical at the time it was shipped from the factory.
6. **Damages to the elliptical finish** during shipping, installation or following installation.
7. **Routine maintenance** of this unit as specified in this manual (see all components under General Maintenance).
8. **Provide pictures or video of any issues via email as requested by the service technician.** Assist with troubleshooting and diagnosing prior to a home visit.

**Item 2 is printed as written above and does not match this machine.** It says "proper use of the **treadmill**" and lists **treadmill** maintenance - belt tracking, belt tension and vacuuming under a motor cover - none of which exist on an elliptical. The General Maintenance section this item points at (page 26) asks instead for wiping the sweat path, tightening assembly hardware, and cleaning and lubricating the rear rails. Both passages are printed in this manual; the wording above is not corrected anywhere.
