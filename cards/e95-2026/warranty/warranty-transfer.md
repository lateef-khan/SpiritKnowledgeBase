The warranty does not cover:

> **Products that have been: sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party. (Warranties DO NOT transfer.)**

All four routes are named, so a gift is treated the same as a sale. The manual offers **no transfer fee, no re-registration path and no exception** for a second owner.

The warranty is also tied to a **registration card on file at SOLE** and to the **original serial number**, both of which point at the first purchaser.
