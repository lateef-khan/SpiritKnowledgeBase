**Keep your bill of sale.**

The **labor warranty period** is established by whichever of these applies:

- **Twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale**, or
- **Eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping**, as determined by the serial number.

If service is performed, **it is in your best interest to obtain and keep all receipts.**

This written warranty gives you specific legal rights; **you may also have other rights that vary from state to state**.

**The same one year is stated in two places, and each adds something.** The warranty period table at the top of the page gives **Labor 1 Year** flat. This Service paragraph gives the same year but offers the **eighteen (18) months from factory shipping** alternative, which the table does not mention. Neither passage repeats the other, so both apply.

This is the **labour** clock only. The parts periods - Frame Weldments Lifetime, Parts 3 Years, Cosmetic Items 90 Days - are on the coverage periods card.
