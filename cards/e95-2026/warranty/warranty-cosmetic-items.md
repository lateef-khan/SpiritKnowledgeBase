**Cosmetic items are covered for 90 Days** - the shortest term in the warranty table, against Lifetime for frame weldments, 3 Years for parts and 1 Year for labor.

The table row carries a footnote defining what counts:

> **\* Cosmetic items including, but not limited to the following: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.**

**"Including, but not limited to"** means the three named items are examples, not the whole list, so other trim can fall under the same 90 day term.

The 90 day figure is a coverage period, not an exclusion - cosmetic items are covered, briefly. What *is* excluded outright is damage from external causes, and the separate Exclusions list names **discoloration of paint or plastic** among them.
