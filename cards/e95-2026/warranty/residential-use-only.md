The manual states this twice.

- **Inside front cover (ATTENTION)** - "**THIS ELLIPTICAL IS INTENDED FOR RESIDENTIAL USE ONLY AND IS WARRANTED FOR THE APPLICATION. ANY OTHER APPLICATION VOIDS THIS WARRANTY IN ITS ENTIRETY.**"
- **Page 27, Exclusions item 8** - the warranty does not cover "**Product use in any environment other than a residential setting.**"

The Normal Responsibilities section adds that the warranty **applies only to products in ordinary household use**, and Exclusions item 2 refuses service call reimbursement for an elliptical **not in ordinary household use**.

There is **no light-commercial tier** offered anywhere in this manual.
