**Heart Rate program uses incline to control your heart rate.** The incline **gradually increases until you reach your target heart rate**, then **adjusts automatically to keep you within 5 bpm of your goal**. **A heart rate monitor is required for this program.**

On the program setup page you can either:

- **set the target bpm directly**, or
- **select 60% or 80% of your age-predicted maximum heart rate**, letting the machine calculate your target automatically. If you choose this option, **make sure you have let the machine know how old you are**, otherwise the calculations will be based on the **default values**.

**The program is finished when the time is up, or if your heart rate is 20% higher than the target.**

**The manual contradicts itself about what this program controls.** Page 18 says the Heart Rate program uses **incline** to control your heart rate. Page 24, under "Heart Rate Programs", says "The Heart Rate program **automatically controls resistance at the pedals**." Both sentences are printed in this manual and neither is corrected by the other, so treat the control variable as unconfirmed.
