The fitness test is on the **Programs** page. **Tap on the test card** to open the test setup page.

**Make sure that the physical parameters displayed on this page are correct**: this data may affect the course of the test and its results. If the data is not accurate, press **"Edit Parameters"** to adjust the numbers.

Before the test:

- **make sure you are in good health**; check with your physician before performing any exercise if you are **over the age of 35** or have any **pre-existing health conditions**;
- **make sure you have warmed up and stretched** before taking the test;
- **do not take in caffeine before the test.**

The manual **does not print the test protocol, its duration, or how the result is scored**.
