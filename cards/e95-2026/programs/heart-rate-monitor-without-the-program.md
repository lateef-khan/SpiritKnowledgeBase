**With all SOLE heart rate elliptical machines you may use the heart rate monitor feature without using the Heart Rate program.** This function can be used **during manual mode or during any of the nine different programs**.

The manual's own program list adds up to those nine: **Manual, Hill, Fatburn, Cardio, Strength, Interval, HIIT, Heart Rate and Custom.**

The same page also says **"The Heart Rate program automatically controls resistance at the pedals."** That contradicts page 18, which says the Heart Rate program uses **incline** to control your heart rate. Both sentences stand in this manual; the other half of the pair is on the heart rate program card, and neither passage overrules the other.

**If you enter your age during programming, the console will perform the maximum-heart-rate calculation automatically.** Entering your age is used for the Heart Rate programs.
