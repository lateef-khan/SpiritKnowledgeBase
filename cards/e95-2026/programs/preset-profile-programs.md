**Hill, Fatburn, Cardio, Strength and Interval follow preset resistance and incline changing patterns.**

Two settings shape any of them:

- **Total Time** - defines the duration of your workout.
- **Max Resistance** - defines the overall intensity of your workout.

**The manual prints no profile graph and no per-segment resistance or incline values for these five programs**, so the shape of each pattern is not recoverable from this manual. If you need a specific pattern, the **Custom** program lets you draw one.

These five, plus Manual, HIIT, Heart Rate and Custom, make the **nine programs** the manual counts on the heart rate page.
