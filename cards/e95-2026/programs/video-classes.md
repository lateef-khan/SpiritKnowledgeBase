Access video classes by selecting the **"Videos" tab of the bottom navigation bar**.

Video classes are **pre-recorded, manually controlled workouts of fixed duration**. There are **hundreds of videos of 7 activity types**:

**Elliptical, Yoga, Stretching, Boxing, Sculpt, Meditation and Full Sweat.**

For easier search, **filter classes by instructor, duration, difficulty level** and so on.

The additional tab menu at the top of the page features **Collections** - sequences of video classes that follow a certain theme.

Because a class is *manually controlled*, the machine does not drive resistance or incline during it; you do.
