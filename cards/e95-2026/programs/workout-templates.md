**Templates are preset programs with a preserved set of parameters.** The Templates tab is in the additional tab menu at the top of the Programs page.

Two ways to make one:

- **Save a program as a template upon completion** of the workout, or
- **create one from scratch in the Templates tab**: tap the **"New Template"** button, choose your **base program**, then **save it with adjusted properties**.

**A maximum of 12 template programs can be stored.** To remove unwanted templates, press **"Edit" in the Templates tab**.

The **12 templates** limit is separate from the **12 favourites** that fit on the Home page, and from the **9 user profiles** the console holds.
