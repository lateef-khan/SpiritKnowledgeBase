**Manual program is a timed workout with resistance and incline controlled manually at any time during the workout.**

That is the whole of the manual's description. The Home screen also offers a shortcut to **an immediate start** and to **a manually controlled timed workout** without going through the Programs tab.

Manual is one of the **nine programs** the manual counts elsewhere: Manual, Hill, Fatburn, Cardio, Strength, Interval, HIIT, Heart Rate and Custom.
