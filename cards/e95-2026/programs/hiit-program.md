**HIIT is a customizable interval program.** You can manually choose:

- the **duration** of the sprint interval,
- the **resistance** of the sprint interval,
- the **duration** of the rest interval,
- the **resistance** of the rest interval,
- the **total number of intervals**.

This is a different program from **Interval**, which is one of the five preset-pattern programs and is not user-editable in the same way.

The manual prints no default values and no maximum interval count for HIIT.
