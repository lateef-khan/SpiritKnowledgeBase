1. Clean the **rails and wheels** with a **lint free cloth and rubbing alcohol**.
2. Stubborn build-up can be removed with your **thumbnail or a non-metallic scraper**, such as the back edge of a plastic knife.
3. After cleaning, **apply a small amount of lubricant on the rails** with your fingers or a lint free cloth. **You only need a thin coat of lubrication - wipe off any excess.**

The manual **does not name the lubricant** and gives no interval for this. Do not substitute a metal scraper; the manual is explicit that the scraper must be non-metallic.
