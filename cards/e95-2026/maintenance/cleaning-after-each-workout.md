**Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each workout.**

That is the only routine cleaning the manual asks for on a fixed schedule; the rail and wheel cleaning is done when a noise develops, not on an interval. The warranty exclusions list **corrosion (rust)** as an external cause that is not covered, which is why the sweat path matters.
