To adjust the footpad angles, turn the **red knob on the rear of both pedals**.

- Turning **clockwise** moves the pedal **UP**.
- Turning **counterclockwise** moves the pedal **DOWN**.

**Make sure to adjust both pedals to the same position.**

The manual gives no numbered detents and no angle range for this adjustment.
