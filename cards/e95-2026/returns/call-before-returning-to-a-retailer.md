The manual prints the same notice on the inside front cover and again at the end of the warranty section:

> **Before returning your Sole product to any retailer, or if you require any assistance with assembly or technical support please call us first for assistance at 866-780-7653.**

The welcome page adds: **if there is a question as to where to obtain service, contact our service department at 866-780-7653** - the same number.

This is a request to call before a retail return, not a statement of the retailer's return policy; the manual does not print a return window, a restocking fee, or any refund terms.
