- Install the elliptical on a **flat level surface**.
- **Do not operate the elliptical on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet.** Damage to both the carpet and the elliptical may result.
- **Do not use outdoors.**
- **Do not operate where aerosol spray products are being used or where oxygen is being administered.** Sparks from the motor may ignite a highly gaseous environment.

The manual gives no clearance dimensions and no floor-mat requirement.
