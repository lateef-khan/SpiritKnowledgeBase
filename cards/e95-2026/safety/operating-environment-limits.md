**NEVER expose this elliptical to rain or moisture.** It is **not** designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.

| Limit | Printed value |
|---|---|
| Operating temperature | **40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit** |
| Humidity | **95% non-condensing** (no water drops forming on surfaces) |

The warranty section adds a separate rule that the warranty is void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment; that is a different limit and lives on its own card.
