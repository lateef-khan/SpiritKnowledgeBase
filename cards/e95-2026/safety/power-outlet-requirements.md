To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock or injury:

- Install the elliptical on a **flat level surface** with access to a **115-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet**, with **only the elliptical plugged into that circuit**.
- **Do not use an extension cord unless it is 14AWG or better, with only one outlet on the end.**
- Do not attempt to disable the grounded plug by using improper adapters, and do not modify the cord set in any way. A serious shock or fire hazard may result, along with computer malfunctions.

The manual cross-references the Grounding Instructions on page 3 for the rest of the electrical rules; that half is on its own card.
