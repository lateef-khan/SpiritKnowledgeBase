If voltage varies by **ten percent (10%) or more**, the performance of the elliptical may be affected. **Such conditions are not covered under your warranty.** If you suspect the voltage is low, contact your local power company or a licensed electrician for proper testing.

The same page also warns: **never remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power.**
