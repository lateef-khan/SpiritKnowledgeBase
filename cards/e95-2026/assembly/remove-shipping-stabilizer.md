Use the **L Allen Wrench (182)** - called an M8 L Allen wrench on this page - to release the **two Socket Head Cap Bolts** on the mainframe that secure the mast tube, then **remove and abandon the Side Back (196)**.

"Abandon" is the manual's own word: the Side Back is packaging, not a part of the finished machine, so it is not reused later in the assembly.
