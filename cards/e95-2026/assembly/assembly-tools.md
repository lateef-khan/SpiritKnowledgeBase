| Item | Tool |
|---|---|
| **180** | Short Phillips Head Screw Driver |
| **181** | Phillips Head Screw Driver |
| **182** | L Allen Wrench |
| **183** | 13/14m/m Wrench |
| **184** | 12/14m/m Wrench |
| **204** | Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench |

The tool list is printed as picture captions, so the sizes above are exactly as printed. Item 182 is called an **M8** L Allen wrench on page 7 but is listed with no size here.

The maintenance section separately warns that **it may be necessary to use a larger wrench than the one provided** if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently, and that a razor knife or box cutter you supply yourself is needed to open the carton.
