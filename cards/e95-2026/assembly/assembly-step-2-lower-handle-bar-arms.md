Hardware for this step: 127 (8), 128 (2), 131 (2), 156 (2), 166 (6), 168 (4), 171 (2), 203 (4). Tools: Wrench (183), Wrench (184), Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (204).

1. Align the plate of **Pedal Bar Assembly (L) (8)** and the foot pad axle of **Left Pedal Arm (6)**, then put it in. Secure the **Fixing Axle (25)** with **2pcs Flat Washer (168)** and **2pcs Hex Head Bolt (127)** using **Wrench (184)**. Same way to secure **Pedal Bar Assembly (R) (9)** on the **Right Pedal Arm (7)**.
2. Install the left and right **Lower Handle Bars (10) (11)** onto the left and right side of the console mast shafts respectively, and secure with **Hex Head Bolts (131)** together with **Flat Washers (171)** using **Wrench (183)**.
3. Untie the rod end bearing on Pedal Bar Assembly (L) (8). Insert **Hex Head Bolt (128)** through the rod end bearing bracket on **Lower Handle Bar (L) (10)** and use **wrench (183 & 184)** to tighten together with **Flat Washer (166)** and **Nylon Nut (156)** in the rod end bearing bracket on the left swing arm. Fix **Lower Handle Bar (R) (11)** and **Pedal Bar Assembly (R) (9)** the same way with the same hardware.
4. Run the **Handle Wire, Incline (63)** from the **Left Handpulse Assembly (15)** through the **left** round hole of the console mast and pull it out to the top of the console mast. Secure to the console mast left side with **2pcs Hex Head Bolt (127)** and **2pcs Flat Washer (166)** using **Wrench (184)**. Same way, run the **Handle Wire, Resistance (62)** from the **Right Handpulse Assembly (16)** through the **right** round hole of the console mast, pull it out to the top, and secure to the console mast right side.
5. Insert **Computer Cable (205)** through the **Console Mast (12)**. Then tighten the stabilizer plate of the Console Mast (12) and the stabilizer plate of the **Pivot Tube Welding Assembly (43)** with **4pcs Button Head Socket Bolt (203)** and the **Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (204)**.

The incline wire runs on the **left** and the resistance wire on the **right**; the manual is explicit about which side each goes on.
