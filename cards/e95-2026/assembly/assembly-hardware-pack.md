Each row is a picture caption on the printed page, so the order within a step is the reading order of the printed grid, not a sequence to follow.

**The step 4 counts do not reconcile.** This checklist supplies **20** sheet metal screws (146), but the step 4 instructions on page 11 consume only **16** of them (8 on the connecting arm covers, 4 on the handle bar covers, 4 on the console mast covers). Item 143 (22) and item 148 (4) both reconcile exactly. The manual is printed both ways and picks no winner, so expect four spare sheet metal screws rather than a missed step.
