Hardware for this step: 143 (22), 146 (20), 148 (4). Tools: Short Phillips Head Screw Driver (180), Phillips Head Screw Driver (181).

1. Match **Connecting Arm Covers B (L) and (R), (121) and (120)**, on **Pedal Bar Assembly (L) (8)** and secure with **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)** and **2pcs Sheet Metal Screws (146)**. Then match **Connecting Arm Covers A (R) and (L), (118) and (119)**, on the lower portion of **Lower Handle Bar (L) (10)** and secure with **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)** and **2pcs Sheet Metal Screws (146)** using the **Short Phillips Head Screw Driver (180)**. **Repeat for the other side.**
2. Install the **Slide Wheel Covers (95)** above each side of the slide wheel urethanes and secure with **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)** using the **Phillips Head Screw Driver (181)**.
3. Use **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)** to secure the **Front Stabilizer Cover (112)** on the front stabilizer with the **Phillips Head Screw Driver (181)**.
4. Install the **Cover Holders (23)**, with the hole for mounting the plastic cover on the bent tab **facing rearward**, and secure them on the **Incline Rail Assembly (2)** with **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (148)**. Install the **Inclinable Rail Cover (115)** on the rail base with **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)**. Secure the **Rear Bar Cover (116)** on the rear stabilizer and the Cover Holders (23) with **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)** using the **Phillips Head Screw Driver (181)**.
5. Secure the **Bottom Cover (113)** on the **Incline Rail Assembly (2)** with **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)** using the **Phillips Head Screw Driver (181)**.
6. Install the **Handle Bar Covers (106 & 107, 108 & 109)** over the handle bar axle connections and secure with **4pcs Sheet Metal Screws (146)** using the **Phillips Head Screw Driver (181)**.
7. Match the right and left **Console Mast Cover (100 & 101)**. Install the covers with **4pcs Sheet Metal Screws (146)** to the **Console Mast (12)**.
8. Install the **Rotate Axle Mast Cover (211)** at the back of the **Console Assembly (45)**. **Press** to secure it to the Console Assembly (45) - no screws.
9. Plug in both **Oval End Caps (76)** onto both ends of the stabilizer tube.

**These steps use only 16 sheet metal screws (146), while the hardware checklist on page 6 supplies 20.** The manual is printed both ways; do not force the extra four anywhere. Item 143 (22) and item 148 (4) reconcile exactly.

Assembly step 1 warns not to fully tighten the frame screws until every component is assembled, so this is the point at which everything gets its final tightening.
