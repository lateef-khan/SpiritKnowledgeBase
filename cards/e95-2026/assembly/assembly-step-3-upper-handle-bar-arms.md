Hardware for this step: 133 (6), 143 (8), 159 (6), 170 (8), 173 (4). Tools: Wrench (183), Wrench (184), Phillips Head Screw Driver (181).

1. Insert the left **Top Handle Bar (19)** onto the left Lower Handle Bar and secure with **3pcs Hex Head Bolts (133)**, **4pcs Flat Washers (170)**, **2pcs Curved Washers (173)** and **3pcs Nylon Nuts (159)** using **2 wrenches (183 & 184)**. Same way to secure the right **Top Handle Bar (20)** on the right Lower Handle Bar.
2. Untie the **Computer Cable (50)** from the magic belt. Connect **Computer Cable (50)**, **Handle Wire, Resistance (62)**, **Handle Wire, Incline (63)** and **Computer Cable (205)** to the **Center Console (190)**. Then place the Center Console (190) on the console fixing plate of the **Console Mast (12)** and secure it with **4pcs Phillips Head Screw (143)** using the **Phillips Head Screw Driver (181)**. *(Note: make sure the wires are collected inside the Console Mast (12) and are not pinched.)*
3. Untie the **Computer Cable (205)** from the magic belt and connect it to the **Console Assembly (45)**. Then place the Console Assembly (45) onto the **Console Fixing Plate Assembly (73)** and secure it with **4pcs Phillips Head Screw (143)** using the **Phillips Head Screw Driver (181)**. *(Note: make sure the wires are collected inside the Pivot Tube Welding Assembly (43) and are not pinched.)*

This machine has **two** console parts: a **Center Console (190)** on the mast and a separate **Console Assembly (45)** on the pivot tube. Four screws go to each, which is where the eight item 143 screws in this step's pack go.
