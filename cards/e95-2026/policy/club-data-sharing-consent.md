**By joining your first event, you agree to share your username, profile image, and workout data (such as workout dates, distances, active time, and calories) with other participants.**

The consent is given by **joining the first event**, not by a separate prompt, and the manual describes **no way to opt out** while remaining in an event, and **no way to withdraw the data afterwards**.

Viewing all available events also requires the profile to be linked to a SOLE+ account, which is a separate account link.
