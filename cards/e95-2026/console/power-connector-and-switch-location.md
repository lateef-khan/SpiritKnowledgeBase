The manual states this in two places and each half adds something.

- **Page 4** - "**POWER CONNECTOR - FRONT, LEFT SIDE OF UNIT.**"
- **Page 13** - the model is connected directly to **115 VAC**, and there is a **power switch located where the line cord plugs into the unit, on the left side near the front**.

**When power is connected to the elliptical, the console will automatically power up** - there is no separate console power button.
