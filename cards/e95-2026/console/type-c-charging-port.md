Charge a personal device during your workout using the on-console Type-C port. **The fitness equipment power must be on.**

1. Connect your Type-C charging cable (**not included**) to the Type-C power port and to your device.
2. Check to make sure your device icon indicates it is charging.

Notes printed with the procedure:

- A Type-C charging cable is **not included**; make sure a compatible Type-C charging cable is used.
- Your device's "charging" icon **may or may not** indicate it is charging. Depending on the amount of current your device requires, the icon may not be on but the device is still charging, possibly at a **lower charge rate**.
- The port provides up to **5Vdc / 1.5A** of power. **You will not be able to save your workout data to a Type-C via this port; it is used for charging purposes only.**

**The console page calls the same port a "USB CHARGER"** in its control panel callouts. The rating above is the only one printed anywhere in the manual.
