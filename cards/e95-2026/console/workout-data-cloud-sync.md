The SOLE+ app lets you sync your workout data with one of several fitness cloud sites. The manual names four, **with more to come**:

- **Apple Health**
- **Google Health Connect**
- **Strava**
- **MapMyFitness**

Syncing goes through the **app**, not directly from the console, so the machine has to be paired with the app first. The console itself only stores a limited number of workout records locally.
