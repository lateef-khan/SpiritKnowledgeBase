The machine ships with a **Bluetooth transceiver** that lets it interact with compatible **iOS and Android** smartphones or tablets through the **SOLE+ app**.

1. Download the free **SOLE+ app** from the **Apple Store** or **Google Play**.
2. Make sure **Bluetooth is enabled** on your device.
3. **Tap the icon at the top right corner** of the app to search for Sole equipment.
4. After the equipment is detected, **tap Connect**.
5. When the app and equipment are synced, the **Bluetooth icon on the equipment's console will light up**.

That lit Bluetooth icon is the manual's only stated confirmation that the pair worked.

A second, separate pairing exists on this machine for **audio**, and it uses a different device name; see the speaker card. Linking a console **profile** to a SOLE+ *account* is a third, separate step done with QR codes.
