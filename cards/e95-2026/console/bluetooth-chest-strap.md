The exercise equipment's Bluetooth device can also be connected to a **Bluetooth wireless heart rate chest strap transmitter**.

**The chest strap transmitter can still connect to the exercise equipment even though the app has already been connected to it** - the two connections do not conflict.

The manual names **no brand, model or Bluetooth profile** for the strap, and gives **no pairing steps** for it. A heart rate monitor is required for the Heart Rate program.
