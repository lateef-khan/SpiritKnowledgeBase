The exercise equipment can play music wirelessly over Bluetooth.

1. Turn on your mobile phone or tablet's **Bluetooth** function.
2. Search for the name **"BT-Speaker"** in your device's Bluetooth menu.
3. **Tap to connect.**

Your device can now transmit music to the exercise equipment.

**This is a separate connection from the SOLE+ app pair.** The app searches for the equipment from inside the app; the audio link is made from your phone's own Bluetooth menu against the name **BT-Speaker**. Volume is set with the console's volume control.
