The machine has an incline feature that increases the variety of your workouts. **At its lowest position you get a normal elliptical workout. As the incline increases you will feel your knees rise higher with each step**, which means you are involving more muscle fibers due to the increased range of motion.

The incline is a **computer controlled power incline**. It can be controlled by:

- the buttons on the **console**, or
- the buttons on the **swing arms**.

It will **automatically adjust via the incline motor** when you exercise using a built-in workout program.

The manual prints **no incline level range and no percentage grade** anywhere. It also does not say that incline changes are gradual on this page - that warning is on the operation instructions card.
