**Joining a private event** - use the **"Join via ID"** function. Enter the **event ID that was shared with you by the creator** of the event, and you will automatically become a participant.

**Creating your own challenge** - press the **+ icon in the upper menu**. You then choose:

- **event format** (shared goal or ranking),
- **focus parameter** (calories, distance, active time or workout count),
- **dates**,
- **event name**,
- an **optional description**.

**Your event can be private or public depending on your preferences.**

Joining your first event triggers a data-sharing agreement, which is on its own card.
