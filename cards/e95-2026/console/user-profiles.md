Launched for the first time, the console prompts you to either **enter as a guest** or **create a new profile**. Setting up your own profile is recommended: it lets the machine remember your physical parameters, favourite programs and templates, gives you a custom program with an adjustable intensity pattern, and lets your workout records synchronise with the SOLE+ mobile app.

- **Tap +** to create a new profile.
- **A maximum of 9 profiles can be created.**
- **Each profile can be protected with a passcode.**

If you stay in **Guest mode**, you can set your **age and weight** on the Home screen; that helps the machine calculate your workout summary more accurately.

The console holds three different limits that are easy to confuse: **9 profiles**, **12 templates** and **12 favourites**, plus **10 locally stored workout records**.
