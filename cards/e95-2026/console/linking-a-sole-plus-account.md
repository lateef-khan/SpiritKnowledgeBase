1. **Scan the QR code on the console screen** to download the mobile app first.
2. Once the app is installed and **registration is complete**, open the **QR code scanning camera in your SOLE+ app**.
3. **Scan the second QR code** to link your local profile with the SOLE+ account.

**Your console profile cannot be linked to multiple SOLE+ accounts at the same time.**

If you skip this step, you can **access it again later in Profile settings**.

Linking is what unlocks two other things: your **full workout history** beyond the ten records held on the console, and the ability to **view all available Club events**.
