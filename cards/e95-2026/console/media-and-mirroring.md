On the Home page, choose from a variety of **pre-installed apps**, or use the **Mirroring** function to **cast content from your smartphone directly to the console screen**.

Once the content is on, use the **bottom panel** for navigation and view settings. There are **three view layouts**:

| Layout | What is shown |
|---|---|
| **Full interface** | Status bar, control panels, real-time stats |
| **Partial interface** | Status bar, real-time stats |
| **Minimal interface** | Full-screen content with all the panels hidden |

The manual **names none of the pre-installed apps** and does not say which phones the Mirroring function supports.
