Access community challenges by selecting the **"Club" tab in the bottom navigation bar**. Events there are created by other members of the SOLE community.

**To view all available events, your profile must be linked to your SOLE+ account.**

There are two event formats:

| Format | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Shared goals** | Challenges with a **predefined target score**, which must be achieved within a limited time period to complete the challenge. |
| **Rankings** | Competitions in which users try to earn the **highest score possible** within a limited time period, to defeat as many competitors as possible. |

The score can be represented by one of these **focus parameters**: **calories, distance, active time and workout count**.

To find events, press the **"filter" icon in the upper menu** and filter items by **format, duration and focus parameter**.

**Joining an event shares personal data with other participants**; that consent is on its own card.
