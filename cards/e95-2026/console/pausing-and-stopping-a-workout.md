- Use the **side control panels or the physical keys** to adjust resistance and incline.
- **Stop anytime** by pressing the **physical "STOP" key** or by tapping the **red pause button in the bottom right corner** of the screen.
- **After 5 minutes of inactivity in the Pause mode, the workout will end automatically.**

That five-minute timer is a separate thing from the 15-minute sleep-mode timer and the 3-minute retail-demo timer, which apply when no workout is running.

When a **video class** is playing, the **Pause button pauses both your workout and the video at the same time**.
