Once the workout has started, after a **short countdown** the console appears in its **workout mode**.

- **Warm-up and cool down phases may be skipped by pressing "Skip".**
- During the workout, switch between **Stats (Focus)**, **Charts** and **Track** views to check your progress.
- Go to the **Media** section to enjoy media content.
- Open **Garmin** to observe your Garmin smart watch real-time data.
- In the **Focus** view, you can **select which parameters to display by tapping on any of the number blocks**.

Resistance and incline are adjusted during the workout with the **side control panels or the physical keys**.
