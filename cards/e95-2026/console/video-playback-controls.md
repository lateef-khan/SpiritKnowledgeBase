Video classes use the same view logic as the other media content.

- **Navigate through the video by sliding on the progress bar.**
- **Skip 10-second intervals** by pressing the skip icons.
- **The "Pause" button pauses both your workout and the video at the same time.**

The two skip icons were printed as symbols that did not survive the text extraction, so the sentence reads "by pressing and icons" in the extraction; only the **10-second** interval is recoverable, not which icon is forward and which is back.
