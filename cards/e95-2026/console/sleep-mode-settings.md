The console ships with a **Sleep Mode** setting that has three choices:

| Setting | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **ON** | The unit goes into an energy-saving state after **15 minutes of inactivity**. |
| **OFF** | The console will **stay lit** while the power is on. |
| **RETAIL** | The console will run the **demo video after 3 minutes of inactivity**. |

**On TFT displays, click "Sleep Mode" under Settings to switch among the three features.**

The manual does not print which of the three is set at the factory, and it does not describe the rest of the Settings page.
