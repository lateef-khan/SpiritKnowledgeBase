The console callouts printed around the control panel photograph are:

- **Cooling fan**
- **Fan power switch**
- **Volume control**
- **Speaker**
- **USB charger**

The photograph itself did not survive the text extraction, so the callout **positions on the console are not recoverable** - only the list of features above. The manual gives no further description of the fan, the volume control or the speaker anywhere else in the text.

**The charging port is named two different ways.** This page labels it **"USB CHARGER"**, while the charging section on page 12 calls it a **Type-C port** and gives Type-C instructions and a Type-C rating. The manual is printed both ways and picks no winner; the charging card carries the electrical rating.
