To see your latest workout records, press **"History" on the Home page**.

- **Up to 10 records can be stored locally on the console.**
- You can access your **full workout history** only if you **link your profile to the SOLE+ mobile app**.
- **Tap on the record preview** to see the full summary.

The charging port on this machine is charge-only, so there is **no way to export a workout to a USB stick** - the app is the only route off the console.
