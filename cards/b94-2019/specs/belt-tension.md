**450 Newton.** The manual prints the same figure in both places, section 9-8 step 7 and the belt slipping answer in section 9-12.

Set it by tightening the **1/4" x 8T nyloc nut** with an **11 mm open end wrench** until a sound wave tension gauge reads 450 N.

**The manual has a dangling reference.** Step 7 reads "make sure the reading is 450 N (Remark)", but no Remark paragraph follows. The B94 2016 manual, which is otherwise the same procedure, has that Remark and it says **a brand new drive belt must read 540 N** because the belt is elastic and loses tension over time. Treat 540 N as the likely figure for a new belt on this machine, and confirm with the technical department.
