**If the belt slips:**

1. Take both right shrouds and the right crank arm apart.
2. Use an **11 mm open end wrench** to tighten the **1/4" x 8T nyloc nut** until a sound wave tension gauge reads **450 N**.
3. Because this model is belt driven, the rider's weight or the way they ride can make the belt slip. It should not slip in normal use.
4. The belt is malleable and expendable, so it is normal for it to loosen over time.

**If the belt falls off:** follow section 9-8 to fit the belt, then test by turning the pedals forward and backward. If it falls off, move it **one groove toward the direction it fell** and test again. If it still falls off, the machine may have been dropped hard enough to move a part out of tolerance. Try replacing the **drive pulley**, the **bearing housing** or the **flywheel**. If the problem stays, the mainframe may be deformed and the whole unit should be replaced. That is unlikely, as all units are tested before shipping.
