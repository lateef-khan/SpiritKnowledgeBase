Hardware: **#50** Carriage Bolt (2 pcs), **#135** Cap Nut (2 pcs), **#71** Flat Washer (2 pcs).

1. Attach the Rear Stabilizer (5) to the Main Frame (1) by sliding two Cap Nuts (135) and two Flat Washers (71) onto two Carriage Bolts (50), then into the rear stabilizer.
2. Tighten using the **12/14 mm Wrench (100)**.
