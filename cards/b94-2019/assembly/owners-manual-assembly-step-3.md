Hardware: **#74** Button Head Socket Bolt (4 pcs), **#72** Flat Washer (4 pcs), **#103** Split Washer.

1. Secure the Seat Handle Bar (3) onto the Console Mast (2) with four Button Head Socket Bolts (74), four Flat Washers (72) and **two** Split Washers (103), using the Combination M5 Allen Wrench (76).
2. Feed the two Hand Pulse w/Cable Assemblies (26) through the front hole on the Console Mast (2) and outside.

**Split washer count is contradictory in the source.** This instruction says **two** Split Washers (103). The hardware checklist on page 6 lists **4 pcs** of #103 for this step. The manual never reconciles the two. Count what is in your hardware pack.

The bolt size is also printed two ways: `5/16" x 5/8"` in the checklist and `5/16" x18 x 5/8"` in this step.
