Hardware: **#58** M5 x 12mm Phillips Head Screw (6 pcs), **#161** 3.5 x 12L Sheet Metal Screw (4 pcs).

1. Insert the Computer Cable (29) and two Hand Pulse cables (26) into the connectors in the back of the Console (34).
2. Attach the Console (34) onto the Console Mast (2) with four Phillips Head Screws (58). **Be careful not to pinch the cables when fastening.**
3. Install the Pedals (45 L, 46 R) onto the cranks using the **13/15mm Wrench (92)**. **The left pedal has a reverse thread** and screws into the crank in the opposite rotation from normal threads. There is an **L** stamped into the end of the threaded post of the left pedal and an **R** on the right. Tighten the pedals as much as you possibly can.
4. Install the Seat Mount Slide (7) onto the track on top of the seat tube, with the seat post facing forward. Place the Nut Plate (8) onto the seat slide behind the seat post. Place the Flat Washer (71) onto the Fore/Aft Adjustment Knob (86) and thread it into the nut plate from underneath the seat tube track. Attach the Seat (19) to the seat post using the **12/14mm Wrench (100)**.
5. Secure the Beverage Holder (116) onto the Console Mast (2) with two Phillips Head Screws (58), using the Phillips Head Screwdriver (93).
6. Install the Tablet Holder (160) to the back of the console with four Sheet Metal Screws (161), using the Phillips Head Screwdriver (93).

**A clicking noise or thumping feeling while pedalling is usually caused by loose pedals.** It may be necessary to re-tighten them. The maintenance section repeats this and adds the crank arm nut and levelling as the other causes.
