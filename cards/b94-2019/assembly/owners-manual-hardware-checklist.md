Hardware supplied, by assembly step. The number in `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #50 | 3/8" x 53L | Carriage Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #135 | 3/8" x16 x 12.5T | Cap Nut | 2 pcs |
| #71 | 3/8" x 25 x 2T | Flat Washer | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #51 | 5/16" x 5/8" | Hex Head Bolt | 7 pcs |
| #72 | 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #99 | 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T | Curved Washer | 1 pc |

**Hardware step 3**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #74 | 5/16" x 5/8" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #72 | 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| #103 | 5/16" x 1.5T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 4**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #58 | M5 x 12mm | Phillips Head Screw | 6 pcs |
| #161 | 3.5 x 12L | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |

**The manual contradicts itself on the step 3 split washers.** This checklist says **4 pcs** of #103. The step 3 instruction says to use **two** of them. The manual does not say which is right. Count what is in your box and see the step 3 card.
