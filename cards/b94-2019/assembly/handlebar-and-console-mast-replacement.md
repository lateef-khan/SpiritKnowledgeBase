1. Take the console apart first.
2. Use a **5 mm Allen wrench** to release **four 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts**, **two 5/16" x 18 mm x 1.5T flat washers** and **two 5/16" x 1.5T split washers** holding the **handlebar assembly R/L (3, 4)**, then remove them.
3. Release **four 3 x 10 m/m tapping screws** to remove the **handgrip side caps, top and bottom**.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **four 20 m/m tapping screws** holding the **handpulse cable assembly**, separate the assembly and remove it.
5. Reverse the steps to refit.

This machine has a **split left and right handlebar assembly (3, 4)** held by four bolts and a 5 mm Allen wrench. The 2016 machine has a single handlebar assembly (3) held by two bolts and a 12 mm wrench.
