1. Use a **14 mm wrench** to loosen both hex head bolts and remove the **seat**.
2. Use the **14 mm wrench** to remove the **cap nut**.
3. Turn the **brake tension knob** off the **fix plate**, then remove the **sliding seat mount**.
4. To refit, install the sliding seat mount on the seat slider, then put the fix plate on it. Install the brake tension knob from the bottom upward into the fix plate and tighten with the **3/8" x 7T nut**. Finally insert the seat into the sliding tube and tighten both hex head bolts with the 14 mm wrench.

The cap nut in step 2 is new on this machine. The 2016 manual has no such step.
