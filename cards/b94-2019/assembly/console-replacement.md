1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **four M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screws**.
2. Disconnect the console and both hand pulse sensor cables, then take the console off.
3. Reverse the steps to refit the console.

This manual drops the part numbers that the 2016 manual carried.
