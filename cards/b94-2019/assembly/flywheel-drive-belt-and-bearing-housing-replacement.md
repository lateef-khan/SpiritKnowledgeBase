1. Remove the **chain covers L and R**.
2. Use an **11 mm open end wrench** to release the **1/4" x 8T nyloc nut** to the end, then use a **6 mm Allen wrench** to remove the **M10 x P1.25 x 15L button head socket bolt** and take the **bearing housing** apart.
3. Take the steel cable off the flywheel. **Handle the aluminium sheet of the steel cable with care. It is an aluminium sheet with a spring. If it is not handled with care it distorts and then rubs and makes noise. This is a very important point.**
4. Take the belt off the drive pulley, use a **15 mm wrench** to loosen **two 3/8"-UNF26 nuts** on the flywheel, then take the flywheel and the belt apart.
5. To refit, secure the bearing housing on the main frame with the M10 x P1.25 x 15L button head socket bolt. Make sure the **nut stopper** is secure on the main frame.
6. Return the drive belt onto the flywheel and back to the main frame. Line the flywheel up with the drive pulley. Turn the **3/8"-UNF26 x 4T nut** by the drive pulley outward until it touches the main frame and turn the other **3/8"-UNF26 nut** inward. Use **15 mm and 17 mm** open end wrenches to tighten both. Do the same for the **3/8"-UNF26 x 3T nut** and the 3/8"-UNF26 nut on the other side.
7. Return the drive belt to the drive pulley and the flywheel. Turn the drive pulley to centre the belt. If it is not centred, loosen the 3/8"-UNF26 nut on the flywheel and adjust the nuts. Use the **11 mm open end wrench** to tighten the **1/4" x 8T nyloc nut** until the belt is tight against the bearing housing. Use a sound wave tension gauge to reach **450 N**. Turn the drive pulley to check the belt is centred and does not come off. Return the steel cable to the flywheel.
