1. Use a **13/15 mm open end wrench** to take the pedals apart. Turn the **left pedal clockwise** and the **right pedal counterclockwise**.
2. To refit the pedals, turn them the opposite way.
3. Take the **crank arm end cap** off and use a plug wrench to release the nut.
4. Put a crank tool on the crank to hold it, then use an Allen wrench to release the crank.
5. To refit the cranks (16L, 16R), use an air crank tool or a rubber hammer to seat the crank on the axle, tighten the nut on the crank axle, and plug in the crank arm end cap.
