1. Remove the **chain covers L and R** and the **drive belt**.
2. Use C ring tweezers to remove the **20 mm C ring** on the **crank arbor**.
3. Use two **11 mm open end wrenches** to remove **four 1/4" x 3/4" hex head bolts** together with **four 1/4" x 8T nyloc nuts** on the crank arbor and the drive pulley, then take the drive pulley apart.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.
