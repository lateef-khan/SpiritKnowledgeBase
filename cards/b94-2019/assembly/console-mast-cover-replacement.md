1. Take the console and the handlebars R/L apart.
2. Press the console mast cover lightly to separate it from the left and right chain covers.
3. Use a **12 mm wrench** to release **seven 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts**, **six 5/16" x 18 mm x 1.5T flat washers** and **one 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T curved washer**.
4. To refit, guide the computer cable through the console mast and out of the console mounting plate, then put the console mast onto the mainframe. Use the 12 mm wrench to tighten the seven hex head bolts, six flat washers and one curved washer, then refit the console mast and the chain covers.
