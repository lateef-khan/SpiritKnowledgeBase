1. Take the pedals and the crank arm apart. Separate the console mast cover, then use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **nine 3.5 x 16 m/m sheet metal screws** and **three 5 x 16 m/m tapping screws** holding the left shroud.
2. Use a **15 mm open end wrench** to take the **DC power cord** apart, then take the left shroud off.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **three 5 x 16 m/m tapping screws** to remove the **chain cover R**.
4. To refit, put the right side case on the main frame. Line up the centre of the wheel with the centre of the axle and secure it with **three 5 x 16 mm tapping screws**, then reconnect the DC power cord. Match the left side case with the right side case and secure with **nine 3.5 x 12 mm self tapping screws** and **three 5 x 16 mm tapping screws**. Follow section 2.1 to refit the cranks and pedals.

**The screw sizes do not agree between the removal and the refit steps.** Step 1 says nine 3.5 x **16** m/m sheet metal screws come out. Step 4 says nine 3.5 x **12** mm self tapping screws go back. The manual does not explain the difference.
