1. Use the **M5 L Allen wrench** to remove the **6 sheet metal screws** from the upright cover.
2. Use the **M6 L Allen wrench** to remove the **4 button head socket bolts** from the console support.
3. Disconnect the console wiring and fit the new console.

**Check the Android version before ordering.** This manual documents two different display boards, Android 6 and Android 10, with different wiring pages and different PCB layouts, and the amplifier board exists only on the Android 6 console.

Calibrate the console after fitting a new one.
