1. Remove the motor hood, fold the treadmill, then remove the incline motor.
2. **The incline range must be adjusted to 225 mm minimum before installation.** The manual prints this twice, once as its own section 11 and once as step 2 here.
3. Use the **M8 L wrench and a 14 mm wrench** to assemble the new incline motor.
4. Connect the incline motor wiring to the controller.

**Step 4 has no wiring list in this manual.** The 2016 and 2019 manuals for the neighbouring builds print it under the same step as **red to UP, white to COM, black to DOWN**, and this manual gives the same mapping on its section 3 and incline test pages.

Recalibrate the incline after fitting a new motor, and check Grade Return afterwards.
