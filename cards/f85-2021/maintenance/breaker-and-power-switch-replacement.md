The manual gives both parts one line each, and both are the same job.

**Breaker (section 12-4)**

1. Remove the breaker connection wiring.
2. Replace the part and reconnect the wiring.

**AC power switch (section 12-5)**

1. Disconnect the wiring to the AC power switch.
2. Replace the switch and reconnect the wiring.

Before replacing either part, check the simple causes. The breaker on the front grill can simply be tripped; push it in until it locks. An unlit switch may be an unplugged cord rather than a failed switch.
