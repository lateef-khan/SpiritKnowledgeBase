1. Remove the motor top cover.
2. Remove the speed sensor wiring and fit the replacement parts.
3. **After replacement, test that the sensor registers the magnet.** This test is required, not optional.

Set the gap between the sensor and the magnet to **under 3 mm** while you have the cover off.
