**How often.** The belt and deck come pre lubricated. Lubricate again **every 90 hours of use**, or whenever the deck is noticeably dry. Reach between the belt and the deck to check that lubricant is present **every other month**.

**First, clean between the belt and the deck.**

1. Take a clean, non fraying rag, t-shirt or light towel.
2. Halfway between the end of the treadmill and the motor cover, push the garment under the belt until you can grasp it on both sides.
3. Drag it the length of the entire belt **1 to 2 times**, then remove it.

**Then lubricate.** Do not use anything other than the approved lubricant. The treadmill ships with one tube of Lube; more can be ordered from an authorised dealer, and a Lube-n-Walk kit is also available.

**Why it matters.** If the lubricant dries out, friction between the belt and deck rises and puts stress on the drive motor, the drive belt and the electronic motor control board, which can destroy those expensive parts. **Failure to lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty.**

**If the treadmill trips its own on board 15 amp circuit**, the cause is high belt and deck friction. Lubricate.

**The console can be set to remind the user.** Lube Setup defaults to 90 hours and can be set anywhere from 90 to 200 hours, so a machine in the field may not be prompting at the interval this page assumes. See the lube reminder card.
