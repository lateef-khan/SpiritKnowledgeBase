**Do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and locked position.** Secure the power cord first so it is not damaged, and use **both handrails** to manoeuvre the unit.

**To fold**

1. Make certain the treadmill is at **minimum incline**.
2. Lift the running deck until it is secured by the **locking telescoping tube assembly** in the centre back of the base.

**To unfold**

1. Apply slight forward pressure on the running deck with one hand, **at the rear roller area**, to relieve pressure on the locking system.
2. Pull down on the **unlocking lever** and slowly lower the running deck to the floor.
3. The deck lowers unassisted once it reaches about waist high.

Grade Return, if it is on, drops the incline to zero when the machine is powered on and at the home page, which puts the deck at minimum incline ready to fold.
