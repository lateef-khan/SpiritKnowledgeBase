**This is a display that lights but is wrong. A display that stays completely dark is a different card.**

The matrix prints two rows for this, one about the backlight and one about the panel itself.

**Not bright, incomplete or imperfect**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| A connector has fallen off | Check the connector again. |
| The backlight is broken | Replace with a new display or console. |
| Power to the console is too low | Check the power to the console. Replace the lower controller. |

**Displays not bright, incomplete or imperfect**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The displays themselves are broken | Replace with a new console. |

**Before ordering a console**, establish whether the machine has the Android 6 or the Android 10 board. They are documented as separate parts in this manual.
