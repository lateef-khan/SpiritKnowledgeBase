**GR Mode is Grade Return. Default: ON. Setting: ON or OFF.**

**If the incline does not return to zero while GR Mode is ON**, switch it to OFF and back to ON, then go back to Setting. The incline should then return to zero.

**With the setting ON, the incline goes to zero when:**

- the treadmill is powered on
- the user pauses the machine during a workout
- the workout ends and the display is at the SUMMARY page
- the display is at the HOME page
- the safety key is plugged **in**

**With the setting ON, the incline does not return to zero when the safety key is unplugged.**

**With the setting OFF:**

- the incline is not at zero when the treadmill is powered on
- pausing during a workout leaves the incline at the current level
- the incline is not at zero at the end of a workout, on the SUMMARY page
- pressing START begins at zero or at the current set level
- unplugging and re-plugging the safety key leaves the incline where it is, not at the basic level

**Europe.** On the 2016 and 2019 manuals for the neighbouring builds, grade return is set during calibration and the note reads: for sale in Europe, EU standards require it to be **off**. This manual documents the setting without repeating that note.
