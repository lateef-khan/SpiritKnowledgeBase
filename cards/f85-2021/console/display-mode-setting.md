**Display Mode covers the sleep function and the safety key behaviour. Default: ON.**

| Setting | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| ON | The console **does not** power down when the user removes the safety key. |
| OFF | The console **does** power down when the user removes the safety key, and the display turns off to power the console down automatically after **30 minutes** of inactivity. |

Pressing any key wakes the console.

So a console that stays lit with no safety key in it is behaving as designed with Display Mode ON, not faulting.

**Beep Mode**, on the same screen, is the buzzer switch. Default: ON. It controls the sound the **touch panel** makes under a finger, not the physical console keys. ON gives a buzz on every touch; OFF silences it.
