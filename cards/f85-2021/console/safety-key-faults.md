**This is a safety fault. The machine should be taken out of use until it is fixed.**

Both symptoms come from the same part failing the opposite way to an E0. An E0 means the safety switch loop is **open**; these two mean it is stuck **closed**.

| Symptom | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| With no safety key the treadmill can still display or operate | The safety device is broken, shorted | Replace the safety key device or the console. |
| After removing the safety key the treadmill cannot stop | The safety key device is broken | 1. Replace with a new safety key device. 2. Replace the controller. 3. Replace the console. |

**A lit console with the key out is not this fault.** Display Mode set to ON is designed to keep the console powered when the key is removed. This card is about the machine still **running or able to operate**, not about the screen staying lit.
