**This is a reading that appears but is unstable. A reading that never appears at all is a different card.**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Another chest belt is in use near the treadmill | Check for another chest belt in use around the treadmill. |
| Other magnetic field disturbance | Change the position or the direction of the treadmill. |
| The receiver is broken | Replace with a new receiver. |

A separate row covers a **chest belt held too close to the treadmill**: the cause is a weak battery, and the fix is a new **CR2032** lithium battery.
