**This is a completely dark display. A display that lights but is dim or patchy is a different card.**

**From the troubleshooting matrix, when the safety key is inserted and nothing appears:**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The ON/OFF switch has not been switched on | Switch the AC switch. |
| The safety key is in the wrong position | Insert the safety key in the right position. |
| The 12 PIN computer connector is not plugged in properly | Check the wire and connect it again. |
| The 12 PIN computer cable is broken | Replace the 12 PIN computer cable. |
| The fuse on the controller is blown | Replace the fuse or the controller. |
| The varistor on the controller is blown | Replace the varistor or the controller. |
| The safety device is broken, open circuit | Replace the safety key device. |
| Other components are faulty | Replace the console. |

**From the diagnosis guide, for "display does not light":**

1. The tether cord is not in position.
2. The circuit breaker on the front grill has tripped. Push it in until it locks.
3. The plug is disconnected. Make sure it is firmly pushed into the 220 Vac wall outlet (110Vac on a 120Vac electronic power system).
4. The breaker panel circuit breaker may be tripped.
5. Treadmill defect. Contact your dealer.

**Count the pins before ordering a cable.** This matrix calls the console cable a **12 PIN** computer cable, the same wording as the 2016 manual. Elsewhere in this same manual the console to controller link is described as a **6 pin main wire** carrying TX/RX. Check the connector on the machine.
