1. On the **Settings** page, **press 10 times on the word "Settings"** to enter engineering mode.
2. Press **Engineering Mode** to enter Calibration.
3. **Hit the block** for the value you want to change and enter it with the **digital keyboard**.
4. Press **ENTER** to save the value and close the keyboard.

**If the value you type is outside the range, the display shows the maximum or minimum instead.**

| Units | Maximum | Minimum |
|---|---|---|
| English | 12.0 | 0.5 |
| Metric | 22.0 | 1.0 |

If the belt does not turn during calibration the console throws E1, because calibration is the one time the RPM sensor signal is required.

**Grade Return is not part of this screen.** On the 2016 and 2019 manuals for the neighbouring builds, grade return is step 4 of the calibration. On this console it is a separate setting under Machine Information, and it has its own card.
