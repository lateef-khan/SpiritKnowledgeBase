**This is the drive motor, the DC motor that turns the belt. It is not the incline motor, which is AC.**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Variable speed DC motor |
| Voltage on the 230V system | 0 to 180 volts DC |
| Voltage on the 120Vac system | 0 to 90 volts DC |
| Wires | three: red, black, green |

- DC voltage on the **red** wire, **M+**, turns the motor clockwise.
- DC voltage on the **black** wire, **M-**, turns the motor counter-clockwise.
- The higher the voltage, the faster the motor turns.
- The **green** wire is ground.

**Source wording.** The manual writes the positive wire as "the Red (white) wire (M+)". Everywhere else in the same manual, including the four replacement procedures, the positive lead is red and the negative is black.
