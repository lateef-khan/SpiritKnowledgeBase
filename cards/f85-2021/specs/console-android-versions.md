**This one manual documents two different consoles**, and names them by their Android version.

| Section | Android 6 | Android 10 |
|---|---|---|
| 6.1 Display board wire connections | a page of its own | a separate page of its own |
| 6.2 Display board PCB, board bottom | "For Android 6 Console" | "For Android 10 Console" |
| 6.3 Amplifier board wire connections | **"Only for Android 6"** | no amplifier board page |

**The amplifier board is an Android 6 part.** The manual gives amplifier board wiring only for the Android 6 console.

The E0 page likewise prints the safety pin position on the console board **"For Android 6"** only.

**Why this matters when ordering parts.** A display board, an amplifier board or a complete console for one of these machines is not one part number. Establish which Android version the console runs before ordering.

**Android 6 consoles have lost their media apps.** Netflix, YouTube and Chrome no longer work on the first generation Android 6 touchscreens, and there is no fix on the machine side. See the media apps card.

Both consoles are **10.1" TFT TOUCH** panels.
