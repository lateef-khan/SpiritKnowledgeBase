**This is E4, not E1 (no RPM signal) and not E5 (communication).**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Code | E4 |
| Cause, as printed | Power wire of motor error. |
| Definition | Motor power wire error. |
| Cause of E4 | Power wire of Motor does not insert lower controller. |

| Part | What to do |
|---|---|
| Lower controller | Insert the power wire of the motor. |
| Motor | Replace the motor. |
| Display board | Replace the upper control board. |

The red motor wire lands on **M+** and the black on **M-**.
