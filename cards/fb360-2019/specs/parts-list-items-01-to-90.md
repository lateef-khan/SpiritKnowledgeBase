**What the list tells a service call.** The console is item 08, model **X3529**; the seat item 07, **DD-306**; the pedals item 09, **JG-16B**; the flywheel item 39; the drive belt item 65 (quantity 2); the tension knob item 47; the hand-pulse sensors item 25. The back-rest parts the FB160 does not have are items 74-90: the back cushion adjustment set (74), back cushion support tube (75), rear handlebar (76), back cushion (77), their bolts and washers (78-84), end caps (85-86), rear-handlebar foam grips (87), protective cover (88) and remote-control holder (89). The two packed tools are items 72 (Allen key) and 73 (hex head wrench).

**Against the FB160 list** (`fb160-2019-specs-parts-list-items-01-to-73`): items 01-73 carry the same names, but the FB360 prints *Club knob M12* for item 10, six curved washers (13) and five domed nuts (14) for four and four, five nylon nuts (17) for three, four handlebar end caps (23) for two, six ST4.2x12L screws (31) for two, and *Flat washer φ8.3xφ25* for item 55 where the FB160 has *Self-tapping screw ST2.9x8L* (which the FB360 moves to item 90). Order by the FB360 serial number, not the FB160 one - the `665H` suffix is the difference.
