**This is the XT385-2015 figure, and it is the only one of the five 2015 XT manuals that reads
18AWG.** Printed in capitals in the Important Safety Instructions:

> DO NOT USE AN EXTENSION CORD UNLESS IT IS A 18AWG OR BETTER, WITH ONLY ONE OUTLET ON THE END

- **18AWG minimum**, as printed.
- **Only one outlet on the end.**

**The other four 2015 XT manuals print 14AWG in the same sentence, in the same place on the page**
- see `xt-2015-safety-extension-cord-14-awg-or-better`. 18AWG is a much thinner conductor than
14AWG, and the XT385 draws from the same 110-volt, 15-amp circuit the other four do
(`xt-2015-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`).

**The manual never reconciles the two.** It prints 18AWG once and says nothing else about
extension cords. This card quotes it as printed rather than correcting it. Treat the figure as
suspect before advising a customer to run an XT385-2015 on an 18AWG cord, and quote the machine's
own manual if you quote it at all.

Other Spirit manuals print 14 AWG (`ct850-2020-extension-cord-14-awg-or-better`) and 16 AWG
(`spirit-extension-cord-16-awg-or-better`) for other machines. None of those is an XT385-2015
figure either.
