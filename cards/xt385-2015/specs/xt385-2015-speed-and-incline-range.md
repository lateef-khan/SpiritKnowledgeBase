| | Maximum you can enter | Increment |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | **12.0 mph** | .1 mph |
| Incline | **level 15** | .5 level |

The manual prints it in one sentence, word for word: "The maximum value you may
input for speed is 12.0 mph and incline level 15. Speed values can be entered in
.1 mph increments and Incline in .5 level increment."

**This machine is kept separate from the XT485-2015 and XT685-2015 on purpose.**
Those two manuals print the maximum as "12.0 mph or 18.0 kmph and incline 15.0%".
This manual prints no metric speed figure and no incline percentage - it states
the incline as a **level**. Do not carry their 18.0 kmph or their 15.0% onto this
machine, and do not treat level 15 and 15.0% as the same stated figure.

This is the only place the manual states a speed or an incline figure. It contains
no specification table, so there is no motor rating, deck size, dimension or unit
weight to go with it.
