Hardware: **#71** 3/8" x 1-3/4" Hex Head Bolt (2 pcs), **#77** 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T Flat Washer (4 pcs), **#89** 3/8" x 7T Nyloc Nut (4 pcs), **#175** 3/8" x 2-3/4" Hex Head Bolt (2 pcs).

1. Install the Seat Handle Bar (6) onto the Seat Carriage (4) with four Hex Head Bolts (2 - 71 & 2 - 175), four Flat Washers (77) and four Nyloc Nuts (89), using the **12/14mm Wrench (112)** and the **14/15mm Wrench (132)**.
2. Plug the two Hand pulse Cables (21 & 27) into the two rear receiver ports (26) located on the **left side rear cover, just under the seat**.
3. **Be sure the connectors are plugged into the correct ports.** Each connector has a tag that indicates either **L for Left, which plugs into port (42)** in the drawing, or **R for right, which plugs into port (26)**.

**Only the LCR 2013 says the hand pulse plugs are handed.** The LCR 2011 gives no port guidance, and the LCR 2016, 2019 and 2023 say the opposite - that the two hand pulse plugs look the same and can go into either hand pulse socket, and that only the odd plug (for the handlebar switches) is distinct. Do not carry the 2013 rule onto another year.
