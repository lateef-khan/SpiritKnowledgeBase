**The CE1000ENT plugs into a wall socket through an external 100 W power adapter. The service
manual states no outlet voltage, amperage, breaker, cord gauge or grounding rule, and the one voltage
word in the book is a title on its circuit diagram: `230V`.**

The 7.4 Circuit Diagram is headed `SE8880-SB028 230V Elliptical CIRCUIT DIAGRAM` and draws the supply
end to end: an `AC POWER INPUT` plug, a power cable and connector, a `100W Power Adapter` marked
`OUTPUT DC 24V / 5A`, a `DC JACK`, the controller marked `INPUT DC 24V / 5A`, and `POWER OUT TO TFT
POWER` from the controller to the 15.6-inch touchscreen. The Driver Board function page labels the
same feed `DC 24V INPUT`.

- **230V is a drawing title, not an instruction.** No 120-volt or 230-volt outlet sentence, no amp
  figure, no NEMA type, no breaker, no extension-cord gauge and no grounding paragraph appear
  anywhere, and the book has no Product Safety Instructions chapter at all - its contents run
  Outlines, Electronic Parts, Electrical Configurations, Product Operation, Unit Block Diagrams,
  Basic Connections and Wiring, Error Messages, Engineering Mode, Software update, General
  Maintenance, Disassembling and Troubleshooting. The words *outlet*, *GFCI*, *extension cord* and
  *AWG* do not appear in it; *ground* appears once, as the `GROUND` connector on the I/O board; and
  the only voltage figures are the `230V` title and the `DC 24V` labels on the circuit diagram and
  driver board. Say the manual does not state an
  outlet requirement, and read the rating off the adapter's own label.
- **The adapter is the console's supply.** The Electrical Configurations page says the main
  controller `consists of the DC power supply for console` and that an `EMS Flywheel` sets the
  resistance; the parts photographs label a `HYBRID GENERATOR` beside the driver board, and the
  Outlines list item 14 as a `Power Switch Cover`. Unlike the CE800 and CE900 service manuals, this
  one never says the elliptical runs without mains
  (`spirit-ce-safety-no-mains-outlet-needed`), and its touchscreen is fed from the DC jack.
- **No user weight limit is printed anywhere in the book**
  (`xt-2015-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`).

**The only safety text the book prints is the wear WARNING under General Maintenance** - the
safety level of the equipment is maintained only if it is examined regularly for damage and wear;
replace defective components immediately or keep the machine out of use until repaired; the parts
most susceptible to wear are the belt, PU wheel, bearing and idler. Its `Serial Number Location`
photographs are read in `spirit-ce-safety-serial-number-location`.

**The CU1000ENT-2023 upright bike is the same arrangement page for page**
(`cu1000ent-2023-safety-mains-powered-through-a-100-w-adapter`): the same 100 W, 24 V / 5 A adapter
on a diagram titled `SU8880-SB028 230V`. The CE900ENT plugs in through a 100-240 V adapter with a 24
V / 4.17 A output (`spirit-xe-safety-no-outlet-figure-printed`). **The CT1000ENT treadmill is not**:
its service manual states a 230-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet as a requirement
(`ct1000ent-2023-safety-outlet-230-volt-15-amp`); this book prints a drawing title and no requirement.
