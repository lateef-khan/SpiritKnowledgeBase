**This is the CE1000ENT procedure. The CE900 / CE900ENT books remove a TV tube dummy plug and a four-screw cover
first and have no keyboard step.**

1. Unbolt the **console-mast tube cover** by removing its **2 screws** (figures 11.11 and 11.12).
2. Unbolt the **console** by removing the **4 screws** and disconnecting the **connectors to the PCB** (figures 11.13
   and 11.14).
3. Unbolt the **keyboard** by removing the **4 screws** and disconnecting the **connectors to the keyboard** (figures
   11.15 and 11.16).
4. Reinstall the console by reversing processes 11.11 to 11.16.

No screw sizes and no torque are printed. The CU1000ENT bike prints the same template with its own counts; it is
carded separately.
