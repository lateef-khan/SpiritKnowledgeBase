Section 3 names three parts:

- **Console** - the interface that controls all functions of the elliptical; it contains the key
  controls and a **TFT display**.
- **Main Controller** - "the circuit board consists of the DC power supply for console"; the general
  information line adds that it includes the power supply and the driver control circuit.
- **EMS Flywheel** - "it can change to increase or decrease resistance level of brake".

**The screen is a 15.6" TFT Display** - the only size the book gives, on the Window Display page.
The chapter-2 photographs name the console parts **DISPLAY** and **NFC log in & Shortcut key**, and
the lower parts **HYBRID GENERATOR**, **SPEED SENSOR** and **Driver board**.

**No voltage, current or resistance figure is printed in the section.** The machine's supply is on
the circuit diagram - a 100 W adapter at DC 24 V / 5 A - and the flywheel named *EMS* here is
captioned *Hybrid Generator* two pages earlier and *Brake Flywheel* on the block diagram: one unit,
three names. The CU1000ENT bike book prints the same three paragraphs
(`cu1000ent-2023-specs-electrical-configuration-tft-console-and-hybrid-generator`).
