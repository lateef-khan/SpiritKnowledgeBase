One drawing of the shrouded machine with red call-out numbers, and an **Item / Description /
Required Q'ty** table. Items 5 and 6, 9 and 10, and 16 and 17 share one leader each - a left and
right part pointed at once.

| No. | Description | Qty | No. | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Console | 1 | 15 | Handle Bar Cover | 2 |
| 2 | Console Chin Cover (Front) | 1 | 16 | Cover (L) | 1 |
| 3 | Console Chin Cover (Rear) | 1 | 17 | Cover (R) | 1 |
| 4 | Drink Bottle Holder | 1 | 18 | Pedal (L) | 1 |
| 5 | Console Mast Cover (L) | 1 | 19 | Pedal (R) | 1 |
| 6 | Console Mast Cover (R) | 1 | 20 | Pedal Arm Cover A (L) | 1 |
| 7 | Side Case (L) | 1 | 21 | Pedal Arm Cover B (L) | 1 |
| 8 | Side Case (R) | 1 | 22 | Pedal Arm Cover A (R) | 1 |
| 9 | Side Case Plate (L) | 1 | 23 | Pedal Arm Cover B (R) | 1 |
| 10 | Side Case Plate (R) | 1 | 24 | Rear Shroud | 2 |
| 11 | Console Mast Inner Cover (L) | 1 | 25 | Slide Wheel Cover A | 1 |
| 12 | Console Mast Inner Cover (R) | 1 | 26 | Slide Wheel Cover B | 2 |
| 13 | Front Shroud | 1 | 27 | Cap | 6 |
| 14 | Power Switch Cover | 1 | 28 | TVC Cover | 1 |

These are cover and trim names, not a parts list: **no part numbers are printed** and the book
has no exploded view. The **Power Switch Cover (14)** sits low on the front shroud, which is
where the circuit diagram puts the DC jack. No dimensions, machine weight or user weight appear
on the page.

The CE900-2021 and CE900ENT books print a near-identical 33-row list under the heading
*Component Description* inside their disassembly chapter, with the same names plus a reading
rack holder, cooling fan, end cap, chin covers (L) and (R) and a TV protective rubber; that list
belongs with those machines' parts cards. The CU1000ENT bike's twenty-item outline is
`cu1000ent-2023-specs-outline-numbered-part-names`.
