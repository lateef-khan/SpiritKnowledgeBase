**2.1 Console (p. 5)** names two things on the 15.6-inch console: **DISPLAY**, and **NFC log in & Shortcut
key** - one callout for the NFC reader and the row of physical keys below the screen. No fan, speaker or
thumb switch is labelled on this console.

**2.2 Controller and Driver parts (p. 6)** names three: **HYBRID GENERATOR** (the flywheel with its coil
pack), **SPEED SENSOR** and **Driver board**, each called out on its own photograph.

**These are the same five names the CU1000ENT upright bike book prints**
(`cu1000ent-2023-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`) - the two 2023 ENT books share one chapter-2 template.
Among the ellipticals, the CE900 and CE900ENT name a Cooling FAN and DISPLAY on the console with the same
three lower parts (`spirit-ce900-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`); the CE1000ENT drops the fan callout
and adds the NFC key. The CE800ENT book names eight parts including a thumb switch and earphone port
(`ce800ent-electronic-parts-locations`).

**No part number, rating or dimension is printed on either page.** The generator's role and the driver
board's connections are on the specs cards for chapters 3 and 6 of this book; the only tool the book lists
is a multi-meter (`spirit-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`).
