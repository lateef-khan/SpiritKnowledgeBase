The Console page labels three things: **DISPLAY**, and below it **NFC log in & Shortcut key**. The
Window Display page carries one call-out, **15.6" TFT Display**. The Function Locations heading on
the same page sits over a picture with no readable labels. The General Information line says the
console "Contains Key controls and TFT Display".

**So the screen is 15.6 inches** - larger than the 10.1" TFT of the CE900ENT
(`ce900ent-console-layout-10-1-inch-tft-fan-start-level-stop-enter-and-headphone-socket`) - and
log-in is by **NFC**: the Service tab of Maintenance Mode has an NFC Test that reads "correspond to
the location of the NFC sensor, and when the sensor is detected, the information will be displayed
on the screen"
(`ce1000ent-2023-console-service-tab-key-nfc-brake-test-twice-sensor-tests-and-error-log`).

**The shortcut keys are a separate keyboard.** The Part Replacement Guide unbolts a **Keyboard**
with four screws behind the console, and the I/O board has a **Key Board CONNECT (20 PINS)** socket;
those are held with the assembly and specs cards. **The manual never names the individual keys**,
and it prints no Quick Start, no power-up step and no key-by-key operation for this machine.

**Nothing in this book describes how the NFC log-in works**, what card or phone it reads, or what a
user sees after logging in. The owner's manual for this machine is not in the repository.

**The CU1000ENT-2023 bike prints the same two pages** with the same three labels
(`cu1000ent-2023-console-15-6-inch-tft-with-nfc-login-and-shortcut-keys`).
