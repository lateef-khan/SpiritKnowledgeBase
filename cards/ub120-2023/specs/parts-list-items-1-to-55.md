**What the list tells a service call.** The console is item 18; the 8-level tension knob item 20; the flywheel assembly item 36 and its adjusters items 32 and 49; the belt item 42, **310J J5**; the pedals items 23-24 on a **1/2" axle**; the seat cushion item 14, **KX6614**; the sensor wire item 12, extension sensor wire item 7, lower tension wire item 13, pulse wires item 27 and pulse sensors item 53. The packed tools are the last two rows, items 54 (Allen key 80Lx30W) and 55 (multifunctional wrench).

The SB120 recumbent bike prints the same layout with 73 items and `SB120-RB1036-PARTnn` serials (`sb120-2022-specs-parts-list-items-01-to-73`); which other Xterra books print a list is `xterra-specs-parts-which-bike-elliptical-and-climber-manuals-print-an-exploded-view-and-parts-list`.
