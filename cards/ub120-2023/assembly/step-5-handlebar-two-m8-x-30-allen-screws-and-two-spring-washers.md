Hardware: **2 Allen screws (No. 10) M8 x 30L, 2 spring washers (No. 21) M8**. Tool: Allen key 54.

1. Connect the handlebar (No. 04) to the front post (No. 02), secure with 2 spring washers (No. 21) and 2 Allen screws (No. 10).

The pulse wires (27) from the handlebar are routed and connected in the next step.
