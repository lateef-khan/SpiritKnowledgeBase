Hardware: **1 cover for handlebar (No. 25)**; pre-installed on the back of the console, **2 crossed head screws (No. 16)**. Tool: Allen key 54.

1. Remove the pre-installed 2 crossed head screws (No. 16) from the back of the console (No. 18), and remove the grommet (No. 28) from the front post (No. 02).
2. Pull the pulse wires (No. 27) through the grommet (No. 28) and then through the hole of the front post (No. 02); finally plug the grommet (No. 28) into the front post (No. 02).
3. Connect the pulse wire (No. 27) and the extension sensor wire (No. 07) with the wire from the console (No. 18), then tighten the console (No. 18) with the 2 crossed head screws (No. 16) to the front post (No. 02).
4. Press the cover for handlebar (No. 25) into the handlebar.

**NOTE: CHECK TO ENSURE THAT ALL BOLTS AND NUTS ARE TIGHTENED BEFORE USING THE MACHINE.**
