Hardware: pre-installed on the front post, **4 Allen screws (No. 11) and 4 arc washers (No. 19)**. Tool: Allen key 54.

1. Remove the pre-installed 4 Allen screws (No. 11) and 4 arc washers (No. 19) from the front post (No. 02).
2. **Turn the tension knob (No. 20) to the 8th (highest) level, then connect it to the lower tension wire (No. 13).** At level 8 the knob's cable is pulled to its shortest, which is the only position where the two cable ends reach each other.
3. Connect the extension sensor wire (No. 07) with the sensor wire (No. 12).
4. Insert the front post (No. 02) into the main frame (No. 01) and secure with the 4 Allen screws (No. 11) and 4 arc washers (No. 19).

The tension knob lives on the front post on this bike, so its cable has to be joined before the post goes in; the recumbent's knob is on the frame and its step joins only the sensor and pulse wires.
