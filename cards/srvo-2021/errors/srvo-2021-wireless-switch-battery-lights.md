**This card is about the battery lights. A switch that simply will not pair is a different fault (`srvo-wireless-switch-not-connecting`).**

From the Diagnosis Guide, item 1, quoted in full: make sure the battery is installed correctly. If blue and red lights are flashing at the same time, the battery was not installed properly — reinstall it. If only the blue light is showing, the battery was installed correctly and the switch is ready to pair with the SRVO again.

This is an orientation check, not a replacement step: the book names no battery type here and does not say the battery is dead.
