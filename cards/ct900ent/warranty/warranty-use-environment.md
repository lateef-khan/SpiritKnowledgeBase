**The manual does not answer this cleanly, and the two halves of the section point
different ways.** Answer it from the source rather than from the row label.

- The table prints **one row, labelled plain "Commercial"**, with no dues-paying
  qualifier and no residential row to fall back on: Frame Lifetime, Drive Motor
  10 Years, Parts 3 Years, Labor 3 Years (`ct900ent-warranty-coverage-terms`).
- Everything around it addresses a facility - NORMAL RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE FACILITY,
  facility wiring, the facility's routine maintenance
  (`ct900ent-facility-responsibilities`).
- **Exclusion 2 nevertheless refuses service call reimbursement for "fitness equipment
  not in non-dues paying facility or household use."** That is the CT800 and CT850
  wording, and it narrows the unqualified "Commercial" row to a facility that charges
  no dues, or a home (`ct900ent-exclusions`).

**A plain "Commercial" row does not mean a dues-paying gym is covered. The CT850 2018
manual is the precedent for that trap.** It labels its row plain Commercial too and
still excludes a dues-paying facility elsewhere in the same section
(`spirit-ct800-warranty-use-environment`).

**But this manual is not as harsh as the CT800 and CT850 either.** Those eight print an
outright exclusion - "Product used in a dues paying facility (YMCA, Health Club, Etc.)"
as item 8 of a ten-item list. **This list has eight items and no such entry**; its item 8
is prisons. So a dues-paying placement here loses service call reimbursement under
exclusion 2 but is never excluded from cover by name. The manual does not reconcile the
two, and no card should decide it for the reader - refer the question to the dealer.

**Do not answer it from the CT900 table either.** The CT900 covers a dues-paying gym in
as many words, on its own row of periods, and adds a Residential row; neither is printed
here (`ct900-warranty-use-environment`).

**What the manual never says.** It does not define a dues-paying facility, caps no hours
of use per day, and prints no residential table. Do not import the XT five-hours-a-day
limit (`xt-2023-warranty-use-environment`) - that sentence is not in this manual.

**Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded**, by the footnote under the table
and again by exclusion 8 - `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.
