## Serial number

Please record the serial number of this fitness product. The serial number is located **at the front of the treadmill by the Power switch**.

## Register your purchase

The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to Spirit Fitness. You can also register online at [www.spiritfitness.com/warranty](https://www.spiritfitness.com/warranty) under the Support tab.

Registering your purchase is one of the [facility's normal responsibilities](../warranty/facility-responsibilities.md) required to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.
