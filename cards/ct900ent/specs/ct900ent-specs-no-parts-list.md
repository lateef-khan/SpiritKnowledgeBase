**This manual has no exploded view and no parts list.** Its table of contents runs
`13 CONSOLE OPERATION`, `22 TOUCH SCREEN OPERATION`, `30 PROGRAMMING THE CONSOLE`,
`48 GENERAL MAINTENANCE`, `49 MANUFACTURER'S LIMITED WARRANTY`, and the book ends
with the warranty pages and a NOTES page at printed page 53. No whole-machine
table of item number, description and quantity appears anywhere in it, and no
part in the book carries an orderable part number.

**The only item numbers in this book are the four HARDWARE FOR STEP tables and the
callouts in the assembly text.** Every number it uses is one of these
twenty-three:

`1, 3, 7, 8, 37, 39, 40, 41, 42, 45, 46, 47, 48, 49, 54, 63, 65, 68, 69, 74, 75,
79, 83`

The pre-assembly page says these are "the item number from the assembly drawing
for reference" - but that numbered drawing is not printed in this book.

## The four hardware tables, exactly as printed

| Step | Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 65 | BOLT | M10x1.5x60mm | 10 |
| 1 | 79 | LOCK WASHER | 10 | 10 |
| 2 | 63 | BOLT | M10x1.5x35mm | 6 |
| 2 | 74 | FLAT WASHER | 3/8"x 2.5 | 6 |
| 2 | 79 | LOCK WASHER | 10 | 6 |
| 3 | 68 | BOLT | M5x8x12mm | 8 |
| 4 | 68 | SCREW | M5x.8x12mm | 4 |
| 4 | 69 | SCREW | M5x.8x25mm | 2 |
| 4 | 75 | FLAT WASHER | 5.5x19x1.5 | 8 |
| 4 | 83 | SCREW | 3.5x12mm | 18 |

**Item 68 is printed two different ways in the one book** - `M5x8x12mm` in the Step
3 table and `M5x.8x12mm` in the Step 4 table. The second is the thread pitch
written properly; both are left as printed. Item 74's `3/8"x 2.5` is missing a
digit or a unit and is left as printed too.

## These twenty-three numbers do agree with the CT900 list

Unlike the CT850 books, whose 2018 manual runs a numbering scheme of its own,
**every one of the twenty-three numbers above names the same part in the CT900
parts list** (`ct900-full-parts-list-and-exploded-view`). Items **63, 65 and 75**
match that list's description *and* its quantity exactly; 79 is its
`Ø10 × 2T Spring Washer`; 83 its `Ø3.5 × 12m/m Sheet Metal Screw`; 7 and 8 the
right and left uprights; 45/46 and 47/48 the right and left handrail covers;
39/40 and 41/42 the right and left base covers and rear end caps.

Four of them carry a plainer name here than there - **3** is "MAIN PANEL" here and
`Console Mounting Bracket` there, **49** is "CONSOLE BRACKET COVER" here and
`LED Console Rear Cover` there, **54** is "CONSOLE" here and `Console Assembly`
there, and **37** is "MOTOR COVER" here where the CT900 list splits the cover into
37 `Motor Top Cover` and 38 `Motor Bottom Cover`.

**That agreement covers only these twenty-three numbers.** It is not a licence to
read a CT900 number this book never prints - this is the entertainment-console
machine and the CT900 list carries LED-console and 220V parts. Get a CT900ENT part
number from Spirit Fitness with the serial number.
