1. Cut the straps and open box.
2. Locate the hardware package. The hardware is separated into four steps. Remove the tools first. Remove the hardware for each step as needed to avoid confusion. The numbers in the instructions that are in parenthesis (#) are the item number from the assembly drawing for reference.

**Two printed items, and that is the whole of it.** This manual does not tell you to
inspect the parts for shipping damage and does not mention any protective film.

## This is not the CT900 wording

The CT900 prints a **four**-item list that adds lifting the box over the unit and
inspecting for damage or missing parts; the CT800 and CT850 manuals print this
two-item form but add "then along the dotted line on the bottom of the box" to item
1. All three are different instructions - see the linked cards rather than mixing
them.

The tools and the carton contents are on their own cards: [tools included](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ct900-assembly-tools-included.md) and [parts included](../../ct900/assembly/parts-included-and-tools.md), both shared with the CT900.
