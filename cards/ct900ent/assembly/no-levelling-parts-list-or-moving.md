This manual's assembly chapter is six pages - unpacking, then four build steps - and
then it goes to console operation. It does not contain, anywhere in its 53 pages:

- **Levelling.** The words leveller, levelling foot and levelling pad appear **zero**
  times. No pad count, no wrench size, no procedure.
- **Folding.** The word "fold" appears **zero** times.
- **Moving or transporting the machine.** Transport wheels are named once, inside the
  electrical warning that tells you to route the power cord away from moving parts,
  and nowhere else. There is no procedure for rolling the machine.
- **A parts list or an exploded view.** The book has neither, so there is no way to
  look an item number up in it. The numbers in parenthesis in its four assembly steps
  refer to an assembly drawing that is not reproduced.
- **A "parts of your treadmill" labelled diagram.** Its equivalent page labels console
  features, not machine parts.

**The CT900ENT service manual does print one instruction for moving it**: lift the treadmill
at the rear roller area by the two side end caps and roll it away, on
[the transportation card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ct900-assembly-transportation-instructions.md).
That book also holds a nine-procedure part replacement guide, but still no parts list and no exploded view,
and nothing on levelling.

Otherwise absence is the answer here, not a gap to fill from a sibling machine.

**The CT900 item numbers are almost certainly the same** - the two manuals print the
same four steps with the same items 63, 65, 68, 69, 74, 75, 79 and 83 - but the
CT900ENT manual never confirms it, so treat the CT900 parts list as a cross-reference
and not as this machine's own document.

**Do not answer a levelling question from a CT800, CT850, CT900 or CTSBS900 card.**
Those manuals print levelling instructions; this one does not, and the frames differ.

The one setting-up instruction this manual does print is on p. 15: plug the power cord
in at the **front base of the unit**, and the screen shows an initial image and then
the Home screen. It names no power switch and no safety key on that page, though the
machine has both - the main power switch is named in the sleep-mode section and the
tether cord on its own page. See [starting the treadmill and the Home screen](../console/starting-the-treadmill-home-screen.md) and the [safety tether cord card](../safety/safety-tether-cord.md).
