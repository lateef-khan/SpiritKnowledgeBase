## Hardware for step 2

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 63 | Bolt | M10x1.5x35mm | 6 |
| 74 | Flat Washer | 3/8"x2.5 | 6 |
| 79 | Lock Washer | (10) | 6 |

## Steps

1. Install MAIN PANEL (3) onto the uprights using 6 BOLTS (63), 6 LOCK WASHERS (79) and 6 FLAT WASHERS (74).
2. Run the wiring harness through the MAIN PANEL (3) as shown in the illustration.
3. Tighten all bolts and washers on both uprights (7 and 8) from the previous step (see [step 1](step-1-attach-uprights.md)).
