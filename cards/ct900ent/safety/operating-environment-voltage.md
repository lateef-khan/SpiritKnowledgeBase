NEVER expose this treadmill to rain or moisture. This product is NOT designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.

- **Operating temperature:** 40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit
- **Humidity:** 95% non-condensing (no water drops forming on surfaces)

If voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more, the performance of your treadmill may be affected. Such conditions are not covered under your warranty. If you suspect the voltage is low, contact your local power company or a licensed electrician for proper testing.
