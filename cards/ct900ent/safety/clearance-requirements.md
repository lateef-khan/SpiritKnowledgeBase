Per ASTM F2115-05 Specifications 6.1.2.11, the recommended minimum clearance required around each treadmill for access to, passage around, and emergency dismount is stated below.

- **0.5 m (19.7 in.)** on each side of the treadmill
- **1 m (39 in.)** behind the machine
