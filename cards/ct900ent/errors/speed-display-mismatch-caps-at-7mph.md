**Problem:** Treadmill will only achieve approximately 7 mph but shows higher speed on display.

**Cause / fix:** This indicates the motor should be receiving power to operate. Do not use an extension cord. If an extension cord is required, it should be as short as possible and heavy duty, 16-gauge minimum, low voltage. Contact an electrician or your Spirit Fitness dealer. A minimum of 110 volt AC current is required.

The XT185, XT285, XT385, XT485 and XT685 owner's manuals print the same two numbers in both
the 2015 and the 2023 revision - **16 gauge minimum** for an extension cord and **a minimum
of 110 volt AC** - and name the cause as low AC voltage to the treadmill.

**The gauge in this row disagrees with the safety page of the same manual.** The 2015 XT185,
XT285, XT485 and XT685 safety pages demand **14AWG or better**
(`xt-2015-safety-extension-cord-14-awg-or-better`) and the XT385 safety page demands
**18AWG** (`xt385-2015-safety-extension-cord-18-awg-or-better`), while this troubleshooting
row says 16 gauge minimum. Nothing in the manuals reconciles the two figures. The safety
page is the stricter instruction on four of the five machines, so quote that when someone
asks what cord to buy, and quote 16 gauge only as what this row prints.

**Do not carry these numbers to another Spirit treadmill.** The CT850 manuals print 12 gauge
and 110 volt AC 60 Hz for 2016, and 16 gauge and 100 volt AC for 2020.
