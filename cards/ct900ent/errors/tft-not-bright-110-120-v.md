| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| TFT light is broken | Replace with new TFT or console |
| Power to console too low | Check AC power is 110-120V. Check power to console. Replace lower controller. |

The condition is printed `TFT not bright, incomplete or imperfect`. The CT900ENT service manual is the only book that puts **110-120V** against the TFT version of this row; the CT800ENT 2022, CT850ENT 2022 and the two 2024 ENT treadmills print it against 120V (`spirit-2024-errors-tft-not-bright-incomplete-or-imperfect`), and the CU900ENT bike prints no figure (`cu900ent-tft-not-bright`).

Dead segments rather than a dim light are the next row down, `cu900ent-tft-displays-not-bright`.
