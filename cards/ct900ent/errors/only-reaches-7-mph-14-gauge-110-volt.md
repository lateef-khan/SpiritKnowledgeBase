**The CT900ENT service manual prints its service checklist twice and the two copies give different figures for this row.**

First printing, in chapter 7:

> This indicates motor should be receiving power to operate. Low AC voltage to treadmill. Do not use an extension cord. If an extension cord is required it should be as short as possible and heavy duty **14 gauge** minimum. Low household voltage. Contact an electrician or your dealer. A minimum of **110 volt AC current, 50/60 hz** is required.

Second printing, in chapter 9, with the condition worded `approximately 10 kph`:

> ... heavy duty **16 AWG** minimum. Low household voltage. Contact an electrician or your dealer. A minimum of **110 volt AC current, 60 hz** is required.

The second copy is the XT485 2015 checklist pasted in - it also asks for a `110 VAC` outlet in its display row, a `15 amp` on-board breaker and an `LS/LOW SPEED` message, where the first copy says 120 VAC, 20 amp and `LS`. The 14 gauge figure agrees with the book's own safety page, which asks for a 14 AWG or better cord; the owner's manual's row for this machine prints 16 gauge and 110 volt (`ct900ent-speed-display-mismatch-caps-at-7mph`).
