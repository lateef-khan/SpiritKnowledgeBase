**Problem:** Circuit breaker trips, but not the treadmill circuit breaker.

**Cause / fix:** Need to replace the facility breaker with a "high in-rush current" type breaker. This is not a warranty defect — see the full explanation in [Electrical requirements - GFCI outlets and circuit breakers](../safety/electrical-requirements-gfci-breakers.md).
