**Problem:** Display does not light.

**Possible causes:**

1. [Tether cord](../safety/safety-tether-cord.md) not in position.
2. Circuit breaker on front grill tripped. Push circuit breaker in until it locks.
3. Plug is disconnected. Make sure plug is firmly pushed into 115 VAC wall outlet.
4. Breaker panel circuit breaker may be tripped.
5. Treadmill defect. Contact your Spirit Fitness dealer.

**Step 5 is printed in all ten XT owner's manuals - the 2015 revision as well as the 2023
one.** The CT900ENT list stops at step 4. Steps 1 to 4, including the 115 VAC outlet, are
printed the same in every one of these manuals; the five 2015 XT manuals carry this row
word for word.

Other Spirit treadmills print a different outlet voltage in this same row - 120 VAC on the
CT850 (`ct850-2016-display-does-not-light`) - so do not carry the figure across machines.
