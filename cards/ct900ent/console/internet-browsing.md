When the **Internet button** on the Home page is pressed, you are directed to the Internet interface.

There are **6 basic internet sites** to choose from. You may change or add other sites through the Maintenance program — see [Adding or removing internet sites](internet-site-manager.md) for how those site icons are configured.

**Note:** An internet cable connection or WiFi connection is required — see [WiFi and Ethernet network setup](wifi-ethernet-setup.md).
