Reached via [Maintenance menu](maintenance-menu-overview.md) > WiFi/Ethernet Setup. Press the desired button for setting up WiFi or Ethernet.

## WiFi setup

Turn WiFi on and press Scan for available WiFi connections. Press the desired connection and follow the on-screen directions.

## Ethernet setup

Press **Ethernet Configuration** to set up a new connection.

Select **DHCP** to search for a connection, or **Static IP** to enter the IP address, netmask, gateway address, and DNS address manually.
