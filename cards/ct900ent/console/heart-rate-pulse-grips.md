The **Pulse (Heart Rate)** value on the screen shows the current value of heart beats per minute. You must use both left and right stainless steel sensors to pick up your pulse. Pulse values are displayed anytime the computer is receiving a **Grip pulse** signal. You may use the Grip pulse feature while in Heart Rate Control.

The CT900ENT will also pick up wireless heart rate transmitters that are **Polar compatible**.

For the full Heart Rate Control program that uses this pulse data to automatically adjust incline, see [Heart Rate Control program](../programs/heart-rate-control-program.md).

Note: the hand pulse sensors are not medical devices — see [general safety warnings](../safety/general-safety-warnings.md).

**The CR900ENT-2021 (p. 25) and CU900ENT (p. 23) bike owner's manuals print this paragraph word for
word**, headed HEART RATE FEATURE, so this card covers those bikes as well and its product line is no
longer treadmill-only.

**They add two words the treadmill manual does not: 5 khz.** Both say *"The console will also pick up
wireless heart rate transmitters that are **5 khz** Polar compatible"*, where the CT900ENT manual says
only "Polar compatible". Same claim, narrowed to the analogue 5 kHz band - **neither bike manual
mentions Bluetooth or ANT+ for this window**, even though both consoles pair a phone over Bluetooth
for audio (`ct900ent-bluetooth-pairing`).

**They also write "The console" rather than naming the machine.**

**The 2022 and 2023 ENT bikes claim more.** Their manuals say the console picks up transmitters that
are **Polar and Bluetooth compatible**: `ct800ent-2022-console-heart-rate-feature`. Do not read one
statement onto the other machine.
