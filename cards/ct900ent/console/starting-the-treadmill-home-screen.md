Plug in the power cord located at the front base of the unit. When the power is turned on, the screen will show the initial image and then enter the **Home screen**.

## Home screen

Begin operation by touching the icons:

- **TV**
- **Language**
- **Exercise Programs**
- **Internet**

To begin a workout, press the **START** key on the keypad below the screen. To view an Exercise Program or change the Language, select either key above.

## Quick operating buttons

Quick operating buttons (INCLINE +/-, SPEED +/-, START, ENTER, STOP) are conveniently located below the screen for basic treadmill functions.
