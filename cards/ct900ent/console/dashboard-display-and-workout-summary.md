## Dashboard

The **Dashboard display** shows a larger display area of key information (calories, elapsed time, calories per hour, and other workout values).

## Stop button / workout summary screen

Pressing the Stop button twice ends the program and enters the summary screen — see [Pause, Stop, and Reset button behavior](pause-stop-reset.md) for the exact button-press sequence that triggers this.

On the summary screen, all accumulated values concerning the workout are displayed (total distance, total calories, total time, total laps, average heart rate, average speed). You can touch the **Home** button at left top, or press the **Stop** button, to return to the home screen.
