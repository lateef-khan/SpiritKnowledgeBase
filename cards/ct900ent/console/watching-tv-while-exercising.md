This is the everyday, user-level TV feature reached by touching **TV** on the Home screen. For the admin channel-scanning setup, see [TV tuner setup](tv-tuner-setup.md).

When the **TV button** on the Home screen is pressed, it directly enters the TV interface.

- If you want to exercise while watching TV, press the **Start button** at the front of the treadmill to initiate **Quick Start**, or press **Programs** to select a workout.
- Change the TV channel by pressing the channel up/down buttons on the right side of the screen.
- Use the **Volume Up/Down** buttons to change the sound levels. To silence the audio, touch the **Mute** button.
- Touch **Max Screen** to switch to full screen viewing. Touching anywhere on the screen will return it to the original screen size.
