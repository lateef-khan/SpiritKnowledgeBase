The touch-control screen is used for operating all functions of the treadmill. You can directly touch any button on the screen, or use the quick buttons on the forward pod to control functions. On the forward pod there are Fast/Slow buttons to control speed, a Start button to begin the workout, a Stop button to pause/stop running, and Incline Up/Down buttons to control the incline of the treadmill.

## Quick Start

Press any button to wake the display up if not already on.

- Press the **Start button** to begin belt movement at **0.5 mph**, then adjust to the desired speed using the **Speed +/-** button, or by typing the desired speed on the numeric keypad. Once the desired setting is selected, press **Enter**.
- To stop the treadbelt, press and release the **Stop button**.
