This is the full setup procedure for the Heart Rate Control program. For a quick overview of the default HR program, see [HR program - quick overview](hr-program.md).

Heart Rate Control (HRC) uses your treadmill's incline system to control your heart rate via information from pulse grips or a wireless chest strap. Increases and decreases in elevation affect heart rate much more efficiently than changes in speed alone. The HRC program automatically changes elevation gradually to achieve the programmed target heart rate.

## Selecting a Heart Rate Control program

You have the option, during setup mode, to choose either the **Weight control (HR1) program** or the **Cardiovascular (HR2) program**.

- The **Weight control program** will attempt to maintain your heart rate at 60% of your Maximum Heart Rate.
- The **Cardiovascular program** will attempt to maintain your heart rate at 85% of your Maximum Heart Rate.

Your Maximum Heart Rate is based upon a formula that subtracts your age from a constant of 220. Your HR setting is automatically calculated during setup mode when you enter your age.

## Heart Rate Control programming steps

1. Press **HR button** to enter heart rate program.
2. The window will show PRESS ENTER TO MODIFY OR START TO BEGIN WORKOUT. You can either press the **Enter** button for settings, or press the **Start** button to execute the program.
3. When the Enter button is pressed, the Message window shows PRESS 1 OR 2 TO SELECT THEN PRESS ENTER. At the same time, the dot matrix window shows "1"; you can press numeric keypad 1 or 2, then press **Enter**.
4. Now the Message Window shows ADJUST TIME THEN PRESS ENTER with the Time Window blinking. Use Up/Down buttons or numeric keys to adjust. After setting time, press Enter again.
5. Now the Message window shows ADJUST AGE THEN PRESS ENTER with the Incline Window blinking. Use Up/Down/Fast/Slow buttons or numeric keys to adjust. Adjusting age will change the HR value (per the HR formula above). After age is set, press Enter again.
6. Now the Message window shows ADJUST BODY WEIGHT THEN PRESS ENTER with the Incline Window blinking. Use Up/Down buttons or numeric keys to adjust. After the weight is set, press Enter again.
7. Now the Message window shows ADJUST HEART RATE THEN PRESS ENTER with the Speed Window blinking. Use Up/Down buttons or Numeric keys to adjust. After the target heart rate value is set, press the Enter button again.
8. The Message window then shows PRESS START TO BEGIN WORKOUT OR ENTER TO MODIFY. Press **Start** to begin the workout, or **Stop** to return to the previous screen.

**Note:** When the Message Window shows CHECK PULSE, there is no pulse signal received and the program cannot be performed. Please check and make sure that the heart rate strap functions normally, or that you are gripping the [hand pulse sensors](../console/heart-rate-pulse-grips.md) correctly.
