When the **Fit test button** is pressed the DM (dot matrix) display shows: **GERKIN**. This is the first of **8 different tests** available: Air Force, Army, Coast Guard, Gerkin, PEB, Marine Corps, Navy, and WFI. The initial test is GERKIN; to select a different test, press the Fitness Test button again and the next test will be shown. Continue to press the Fitness test button until the test you want is shown in the message window. To select your desired fitness test, press **Enter**.

## Fit Test operation

1. For each program the Message Window will prompt you to enter your personal data such as **Age**, **Weight**, **Height**, **Gender**. You may adjust the settings using the keypad or Up and Down buttons, then press the **Enter** button to accept the new number and proceed to the next screen.
2. After all required data is entered, you may now press **Start** to begin the test.

## Detailed pass/fail tables in this manual

This manual provides detailed maximum-time-to-pass tables for:

- [Air Force](fitness-test-air-force.md)
- [Army](fitness-test-army.md)
- [Navy](fitness-test-navy.md)
- [Marines](fitness-test-marines.md)
- [Gerkin (fireman's protocol)](fitness-test-gerkin.md)

**Coast Guard, PEB, and WFI are selectable on the console as test options, but this manual does not include score/pass tables for those three tests.**

For guidance on preparing for and taking any of these tests, see [Before, during, and after the fitness test](fitness-test-before-during-after-guidelines.md).
