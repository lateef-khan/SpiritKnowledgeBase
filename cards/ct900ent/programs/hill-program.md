**Speed:** This program follows a triangle or pyramid type of gradual progression from approximately 10% of maximum effort (the level that you choose before starting this program) up to a maximum effort which lasts for 10% of the total workout time, then a gradual regression of Speed back to approximately 10% of maximum effort.

**Incline:** The deck elevation is a more gradual and sustained progression. Maximum elevation is in the middle of the workout and lasts for 10% of the duration.

For the full segment-by-segment speed and incline values, see [Preset program speed/incline chart](preset-speed-incline-chart.md).
