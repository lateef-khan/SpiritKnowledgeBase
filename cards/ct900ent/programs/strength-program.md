**Speed:** This program has a gradual progression of speed up to 100% of maximum effort that is sustained from 25% of workout duration. This will help build strength and muscular endurance in the lower body and glutes. A brief cool down follows.

**Incline:** There is a quick climb to a moderate, sustained elevation that lasts the majority of the workout length.

For the full segment-by-segment speed and incline values, see [Preset program speed/incline chart](preset-speed-incline-chart.md).
