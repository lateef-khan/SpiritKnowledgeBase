When the **Exercise Program button** on the Home page is pressed, the fitness program menu appears.

There are **12 programs** to choose from:

- [Manual](manual-program.md)
- [Hill](hill-program.md)
- [Fat Burn](fat-burn-program.md)
- [Cardio](cardio-program.md)
- [Strength](strength-program.md)
- [5K Run](5k-run-program.md)
- [Interval](interval-program.md)
- [HR](hr-program.md)
- [Custom](custom-program.md)
- [Fitness Test](fitness-test-overview.md)
- [Calories](calorie-program.md)
- [10K Run](10k-run-program.md)

Press the desired program button and follow the on-screen directions. For **Manual Program** as an example, just touch the Manual button on the screen to enter the programming for Manual.
