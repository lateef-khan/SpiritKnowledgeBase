**This is the Army test, not Air Force, Navy, Marines, or Gerkin — see [Fitness Test overview](fitness-test-overview.md) for all tests.**

A timed 2 mile run. You control the speed manually. Maximum time allowed to pass the test:

| Age | Male | Female |
|---|---|---|
| 17-21 | 16:36 | 19:42 |
| 22-26 | 17:30 | 20:36 |
| 27-31 | 17:54 | 21:42 |
| 32-36 | 18:48 | 23:06 |
| 37-41 | 19:30 | 24:06 |

For more detailed information, visit: http://bit.ly/SF-Army
