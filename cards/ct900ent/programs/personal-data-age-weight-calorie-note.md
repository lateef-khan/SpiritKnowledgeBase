Each of the programs can be customized with your personal information and changed to suit your needs. Some of the information asked for is necessary to ensure the readouts are correct.

- Entering your **Age** ensures that the Heart Rate bar graph shows the correct number. Your Age is also necessary during the [Heart Rate control program](heart-rate-control-program.md) to ensure the correct settings are used for your Age. Otherwise the work settings could be too high or low for you.
- Entering your **Weight** aids in calculating a more correct Calorie reading. Although Spirit Fitness cannot provide an exact calorie count, they aim to be as close as possible.

**Calorie note:** Calorie readings on every piece of exercise equipment, whether in a gym or at home, are not accurate and tend to vary widely. They are meant only as a guide to monitor your progress from workout to workout. The only way to measure your calorie burn accurately is in a clinical setting connected to a host of machines. This is because every person is different and burns calories at a different rate. Some good news is that you will continue to burn calories at an accelerated rate for at least an hour after you have finished exercising!
