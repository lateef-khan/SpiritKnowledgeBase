| Condition | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| The incline position doesn't match console | Console is not calibrated. | Calibrate the console. |
| STRIDE ERR, STRIDE window displays "E3". | Position sensor value of stride motor is wrong. | 1. Turn off the AC switch and turn on power again. 2. Calibrate the monitor. |

**The first row is printed with the word "incline" even though this machine adjusts stride, not incline.** The rest of the matrix, and the machine itself, use STRIDE.

The full E3 diagnosis is in its own card.
