1. Remove side case (L) and (R) and pedal arm (L) and (R) following the disassembly steps.
2. Pull out the **17 x 34L rotate axle A** from the inside. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** from the outside of rotate axle A for slider adjustment (L) and (R) of the joint assembly, with a **12 m/m wrench**.
3. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** from the crank arm assembly for the bushing housing, pedal arm, with a 12 m/m wrench.
4. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **8.5 x 26 x 2.0T flat washer** from the rear rail assembly for the joint assembly, with a 12 m/m wrench.
5. To remove the **J4FM-1719-09 bushing** from the joint assembly, use a pole smaller than 17 and hit the bushing out.
6. Use a **#8 hex wrench and #14 wrench** to remove the **cap socket screw 3/8" x 2-1/4"**, **flat washer 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T** and **nyloc nut 3/8" x 11T** connecting the inclinable rail assembly and rotating block, to remove the inclinable rail assembly.
7. To remove the **WFM-2528-21 bushing** from the bushing housing, pedal arm, use a pole smaller than 25 and hit the bushing out.
8. To remove the **WFM-1719-12 bushing** from the rear rail assembly, use a pole smaller than 17.
9. Remove the two **M5 x 15L Phillips head screws** from the slide wheel cover when taking apart the **78 slide wheel, urethane**.
10. Remove the **17 C ring** and **17 x 0.5T wave washer** from pedal arm (L) and (R) of the 78 slide wheel, urethane.
11. Reverse the above procedures to refit the pedal arm.
12. When assembling the 78 slide wheel, urethane, follow the order: **17 x 0.5T wave washer, 78 slide wheel urethane, 17 x 0.5T wave washer, 17 C ring**.
13. Line up the wheel base with the surface of the 17 x 34L rotate axle A when assembling the joint assembly and adjusting lever, and lock with the screw.
14. **Lock the bushing housing, pedal arm onto pedal arm (L) and (R) using a torque wrench up to 550 lb.**
