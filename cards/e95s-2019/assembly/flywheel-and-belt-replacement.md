1. Following the gear motor disassembly steps, remove the steel cable from the magnetron flywheel.
2. Use a **13mm wrench** to release the **nylon nut (M8 x 7T)** fixed at the idler wheel assembly on the main frame.
3. Use a 13mm wrench to release the **adjust screw**, **nylon nut (M8 x 9T)**, **rod end sleeve** and **flat washer (5/16" x 23 x 1.5T)** fixed on the idler wheel assembly.
4. Pull the drive belt off the drive pulley.
5. Release the **nylon nut (M8 x 7T)**, **flat washer (5/16" x 23 x 1.5T)** and **carriage bolt (M8 x 20L)** fixed on the idler wheel assembly, then remove the idler wheel assembly.
6. To remove the **idler wheel bearing 6203**, use a C clip tool to release the **C ring 17** on the idler wheel assembly.
7. Use a **15mm wrench** to release the **flange nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 11T** on the magnetic flywheel.
8. Remove the magnetic flywheel together with the belt from the oblique side of the main frame.
9. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
10. When reloading the magnetic flywheel and belt, the belt and drive pulley must stay in the **middle position**.
11. If the belt is biased to one side, release the flange nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 11T and use a **17mm wrench** on the adjusting nut inside the magnetic flywheel, then re-tighten the flange nut.
12. Use the **nylon nut (M8 x 9T) on the screw shackle** to tighten up, and flick the belt for a crisp sound or **use a sonic device measured at 190 Hz (+/- 10)**. Then turn the crank arm assembly so the belt and flywheel rotate smoothly, and reassemble the other parts.
