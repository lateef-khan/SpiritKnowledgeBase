1. Following the slider adjustment instructions, remove the slider adjustment, and use an **M6 Allen wrench** to release the **socket head cap bolt (M8 x 40L)** securing the fixing piece on the main frame.
2. **Plug in the power supply before removing the incline motor and use the console to adjust the program to 21"**. If the gear motor is also coming out, **adjust the resistance control to max (level 20)** first, then cut off the power.
3. Use **two 17 wrenches** to release the **incline set screws (10 x 62L)**, **flat washer (3/8" x 19 x 1.5T)**, **nylon nut (M10 x 8T)** and two **nylon washers (24 x 10 x 3T)** from the moving range adjusting assembly.
4. Release the **incline set screws (10 x 40L)**, **flat washer (3/8" x 19 x 1.5T)** and **nylon nut (M10 x 8T)** which fix the incline motor.
5. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **4 pcs of tapping screw (5 x 19L)** and the circuit cover from the main frame.
6. Cut the cable ties with diagonal pliers, release the **tapping screw (5 x 19L)** from the incline motor, and unplug the wire from the incline motor to the controller. **Remember the wire position.** The incline motor wire can then be pulled out and the motor removed.
7. **Take out the steel cable before removing the gear motor.**
8. Release **2 pcs of tapping screw (5 x 19L)** from the fixed gear motor. Pull out the connected wire, then remove the gear motor.
9. **Remember all the wire positions before releasing the controller**, then pull out all the wires and remove **2 pcs of tapping screw (5 x 19L)** from the control plate on the main frame.
10. To remove the spring, use **two 14 wrenches** to release the **hex head bolt (3/8 x 19L)**, **washer (3/8" x 19 x 1.5T)** and **nylon nut (3/8" x 7T)** on the moving range adjusting assembly.
11. Use a **12mm wrench** to release the **hex head bolt (5/16" x 1/2")** and **washer (5/16" x 23 x 1.5T)** on the incline rotate axle A 17 x 108L.
12. Pull out the incline rotate axle 17 x 108L from the other side, then remove the moving range adjusting assembly.
13. Use a round bar smaller than 17 to knock down the J4FM bushing.
14. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
15. When reassembling the incline rotate axle, **align the notch plane and then fasten the screw**.
16. Reassemble the incline motor with **2 pcs of nylon washer (24 x 10 x 3T)**.
17. When the incline motor and all the wires are fastened, **plug in the power supply, use the console to adjust the program to 18"**, then fasten the **socket head cap bolt (M8 x 40L)** on the spring, and fasten all the parts.

Zero a replacement motor before fitting it: turn the tubing clockwise to the end then counterclockwise two and a half circles, or set the centre distance between the two holes to **245 +/- 1 mm** (section 9-4).
