1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **Phillips head screws M5x10mm (4 pcs)** securing the console. Unfasten all connected wires and remove the console.
2. Reassemble in the reverse order. Be sure not to crush or damage wiring during the process.
3. To remove both the upper and lower console cover, remove the **sheet metal screws 3.5x12** securing the console. The manual says this should only be done by a professional.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **Phillips head screws M5x10mm (2 pcs)**, then move out the drink bottle holder.
5. Remove the seat handle bar and lower handle mast assembly.
6. Remove the two **4 x 15L sheet metal screws** of the console mast cover.
7. Following the owner's manual assembly instructions in reverse, remove the console mast.
8. Release the **two 3x20 tapping screws** which secure the hand pulse assembly, pull it out and disconnect the cables.
9. Unplug the round cap under the hand pulse tube and pull out the hand pulse cables to take the hand pulse sensor assembly apart.
10. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
