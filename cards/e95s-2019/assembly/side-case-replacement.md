1. Remove the seat handle bar and lower handle mast assembly (step 11-1).
2. Remove the two **4 x 15L sheet metal screws** of the console mast cover.
3. Remove eight **sheet metal screws 4x15L** and remove the left chain cover.
4. Unscrew side case (R) from the main frame.
5. Remove the **5 x 19L tapping screw** on side case (L) of the main frame.
6. **Remember the position of the AC input module and the colour of the cables.** Remove the two connecting wires and the ground wire, then remove side case (L).
7. Remove the **5 x 19L tapping screw** on the side case rear shroud of the main frame.
8. Reassemble following the above steps in reverse. **Do not clip the cables** when refitting side cases (R) and (L) and the side case rear shroud, and lock the screws.
