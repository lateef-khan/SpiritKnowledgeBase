1. First remove the pedal bar assembly, then use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **Phillips head screws M5x10mm** securing the cover to the pedal arm joint, and remove the pedal arm joint cover.
2. Pull out the end of the slider cover by hand.
3. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T curved washer** of the connecting component with a **12 m/m wrench**. Separate the connecting component and the swing assembly.
4. Pull out pedal base (L) and (R) to the rear and separate them from connecting arm (L) and (R).
5. Release the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **8.5 x 26 x 2.0T flat washer** of the connecting component with a 12 m/m wrench.
6. Use a 12mm open end wrench to remove the **hex socket screw 5/16" x 1/2"** and **flat washer 8.5 x 26 x 1.5T**. Pull out the pedal carriage bolts to remove the pedal bar assembly.
7. Release the **5/16" x 15L hex head bolt** of the pedal arms with a 12 m/m wrench, at the **38 slide wheel, urethane**.
8. Remove the adjustment wheel fixing plate (L) and (R) with a 12 m/m wrench, releasing the **5/16" x 15L hex head bolt** and **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** from the left and right pedal base.
9. Reassemble following the above steps.
10. **Lock the 8 x 16 x 2T flat washers in the middle of the slide wheel, urethane, and the adjustment wheel fixing plate (L) and (R)** when assembling the 38 slide wheel, urethane.
11. Adjust the **38 slide wheel, urethane** and the aluminum track with an **11 m/m wrench** and a **1/4" x 1/2" hex head bolt** while locking the left and right pedal base back onto pedal arm (L) and (R).
