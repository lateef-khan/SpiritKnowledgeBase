1. Remove the left and right pedal bases following the disassembly steps. Separate the screws of the **M12 x P1.75 rod end bearing** from lower handle bar (L) and (R) and pedal arm (L) and (R).
2. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** from pedal arm (L) and (R) and the rear rail assembly with a **12 m/m wrench**.
3. Pull out the **pedal axle (17 x 95L)** from the inside of pedal arm (L) and (R).
4. Remove six **M5 x 6L Phillips head screws** from the frame cover and brush.
5. Remove the **M5 x 6L Phillips head screws** from pedal arm (L) and (R) of the aluminum axle end cap.
6. Remove the three **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolts** from pedal arm (L) and (R) of the aluminum track with an **M5 L Allen wrench**.
7. Release the nut from the **M12 x P1.75 rod end bearing** with a **19 m/m wrench**.
8. Reassemble following the above steps.
9. **Line up the pedal axle with the surface of the breach of the fixing plate and lock it with the screw.**
