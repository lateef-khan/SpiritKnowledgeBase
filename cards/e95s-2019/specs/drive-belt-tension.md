**190 Hz, plus or minus 10 Hz.** The manual writes it as "flick belt with a crisp sound or use sonic device measured at 190HZ(+/-10)".

Set it with the **nylon nut (M8 x 9T) on the screw shackle**. After setting it, turn the crank arm assembly to make the belt and flywheel rotate smoothly, then reassemble the other parts.

Before tensioning, the belt and drive pulley must be centred: if the belt is biased to one side, release the **flange nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 11T** and use a **17mm wrench** on the nut inside the magnetic flywheel, then re-tighten the flange nut.
