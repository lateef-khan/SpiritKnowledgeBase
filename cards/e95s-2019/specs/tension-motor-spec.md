**Work voltage: DC 4.5~7.5V.** It controls resistance increases and decreases.

The drive board output during a level change is a different figure: **Level UP +5VDC, Level DOWN -5VDC**, with a normal meter reading of +5~6.0VDC and -5~6.0VDC at the motor control wire. Those numbers live in the E2 tension motor cards.

The manual also calls this part the **gear motor** in the troubleshooting and disassembly sections.
