Check the flywheel, the **330 drive pulley** and the **idler wheel assembly** to see whether they are rubbing while the flywheel is spinning and causing the noise. Then follow the flywheel disassembly steps and reset them.

**If the noise is caused by the flywheel itself, the manual recommends replacing it directly.**
