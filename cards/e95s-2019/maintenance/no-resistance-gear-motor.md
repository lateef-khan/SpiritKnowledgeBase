The manual gives the same complaint in two places.

**Section 9-4, step 4.** If there is no resistance, first check whether the console is functioning normally. Next check the **gear motor cable**; if it is worn or damaged, remove the steel cable. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **tapping screw 5x19** securing the gear motor cable and replace the cable with a new one.

**Section 9-5, step 1.** There are two kinds of flywheel problem in general: adjusted resistance does not work, and noise. When the user adjusts resistance on the console and it does not work **while the gear motor is working**, check whether the **steel cable works with the flywheel or is loose**.

If the console also shows E2, work the E2 card first.
