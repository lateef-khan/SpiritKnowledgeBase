1. The **CAP socket head cap bolt 3/8" x 2-1/4"** on top and the **nyloc nut 3/8" x 11T** must be tightened securely.
2. **Belt** - this appears quite often, because after a period of time most belts become loose from use. The solution depends on weight and adjustment method; use the belt disassembly procedure.

This manual's slip section lists only these two items, where the E35, E55 and E98 manuals list four. It gives no force figure here, but the pedal arm assembly step does: **lock the bushing housing, pedal arm onto the pedal arm with a torque wrench up to 550 lb**.
