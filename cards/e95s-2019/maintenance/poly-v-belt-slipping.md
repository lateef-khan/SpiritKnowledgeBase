If the Poly-V belt is slipping, **adjust the cap on the hook-type screw with a #13 wrench**. The manual sends you to the disassembly procedure for the adjustment itself.

If the Poly-V belt is worn or damaged, replace it with a new part.

The manual does not repeat the tension figure here. The target is **190 Hz plus or minus 10 Hz**; that number is printed only in the flywheel and Poly-V belt replacement procedure and has its own card.
