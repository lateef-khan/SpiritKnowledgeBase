If the elliptical loses power or will not start, check the fuse located on the **motor controller**. The manual labels it **FUSE 5A**.

DANGER: turn the power switch off and unplug the elliptical to reduce the risk of an electric shock.

1. Remove the fuse holder.
2. Remove and replace the fuse in the holder.

The console problem section names the same part differently: it says to remove the **AC adapter fuse** to check for damage and replace it if damaged.
