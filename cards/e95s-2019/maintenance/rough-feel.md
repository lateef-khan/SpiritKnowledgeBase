Check for loose screws if the machine motion feels unsmooth. Check whether there are foreign objects on or around the sliding rail; use an **alcohol wipe** to remove them and **re-lubricate with lubricant**.

On this machine the running surface is the **aluminum track** under the pedal bases; the pedal base card covers cleaning, lubricating and adjusting the slide wheels that run on it.
