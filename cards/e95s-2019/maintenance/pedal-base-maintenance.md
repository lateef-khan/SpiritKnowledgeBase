1. The **left and right pedal bases and pedal arms are the most important parts of this model.** Pay attention to their maintenance.
2. If there are noises, check whether something is rubbing or has dropped in, then check the **aluminum track**. If it is damaged, replace it as soon as possible.
3. Check the **38 slide wheel, urethane**. If it is damaged, replace it directly.
4. Clean the aluminum track and the 38 slide wheel, urethane, and add some lubricant.
5. If it is loose, use an **11 m/m wrench** to adjust the 38 slide wheel, urethane, on the adjustment wheel fixing plate, tightening it appropriately.
