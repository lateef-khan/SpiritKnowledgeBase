**Slider adjustment is the most important part of stride adjustment.**

1. Check whether the **40 adjustment transportation wheel** is damaged. If it is, replace it following the disassembly steps.
2. Check whether the **swing assembly (#16)** is defaced on the surface. Wipe it and apply lubricant.
3. Adjust the **wheel base** following the disassembly steps if there is space between two parts, and adjust to the standard scales.
