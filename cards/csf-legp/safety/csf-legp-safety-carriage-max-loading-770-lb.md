**770 lb in total, and no more than 385 lb on either side.** The CSF-LEGP 45 degree leg press prints
the split figure as item 20 of its safety instructions, in the slot other machines use for the user
weight limit:

> 20. Carriage Max Loading Capacity: 770 lb (385 lb per side)

Its safeguards page repeats the total without the split: *The maximum carriage weight should not
exceed 770 pounds.* No metric figure is given in either place.

**The per-side half of the rule is the part that gets lost.** 385 lb is exactly half of 770, so an
evenly loaded carriage at the total limit is already at the per-side limit; the sentence exists to
forbid an *uneven* load that stays under 770 lb overall. Quoting only the total gives a reader
permission to put 500 lb on one sleeve.

**This is not the user weight limit.** How heavy the lifter may be is on the serial number decal:
180 kg / 396 lb, which the manual text never states.

The CSF-LEGP also asks for a 180 cm floor perimeter beyond its training area, the largest in the
range alongside the CSF-HRAC.
