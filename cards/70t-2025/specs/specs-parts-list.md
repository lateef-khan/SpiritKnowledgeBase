**The 2025 7.0T owner's manual (Rev 01.10.25) prints a parts list; the 2026 printing of the same book does not** (`spirit-2026t-specs-no-parts-list`). This is the only 7.0T-badged owner's manual with one. The numbers are the balloon numbers on its exploded view; **no Spirit part number is printed.** 193 rows run 1 to 308 with 118 whole numbers absent - gaps, not a missing page. The console is item 36 with sub-parts 36~15, 36~16 and 36~17 (bottom cover, top cover right and left).

## The same frame as the MT200, numbered the same, worded differently

The item numbers are the MT200 2022 owner's manual's (`mt200-2022-specs-parts-list`), and most rows agree. **Seven rows of that list are not printed here at all: 71, 72, 76, 195, 276, 277, 278** - the motor cooling fan (276), its two 500 mm connecting cables (277, 278), its mounting plate (195) and the three fasteners that hold it (71, 72, 76). The 2025 7.0T list has a `Fan Back Plate` at 193 and no fan. Whether the medical 7.0T ships without the cooling fan or the list merely omits it, the book does not say; do not order a fan by an MT200 number for this machine without checking.

## What the list names that the prose does not

- Drive and electronics: 279 `AC Motor`, 274 `Inverter`, 275 `Encoder`, 265 `AC Electronic Module`, 268 `Filter`, 139 `Controller L-Plate`, 285 `Communication Cable`.
- The parallel bars: 187 / 188 `P. Bar Horizontal Adjustment Arm (L) / (R)`, 189 `P.Bar Horizontal Adj. Cap`, 206 `Locking Knob`, 207 `Brake Tension Knob`, 257 and 261 the two brake pads, 262 `EVA Foam for Rail Strap`.
- Step sensing: 209 `Sensor Mounting Plate`, 210 `Magnet Plate`, 211 `Braking Magnet`, 251 `Ø20 × M6 × 20L_Step Magnet Holde` (printed truncated).
- Tools packed as items: 137 `L Allen Wrench`, 259 `L Wrench`, 113 `Phillips Head Screw Driver`. No separate tool list is printed.
