**The 2025 printing of the 7.0T manual gives four electrical figures on two facing pages, and the
2026 printing changed three of them. This card is the 2025 book; the 2026 book is
`spirit-2026t-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`.**

Safety item 2, printed page 4:

> To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons, install the 7.0T on a
> flat level surface with access to a **115-volt AC, 60 Hz, 20-amp grounded outlet**. Do not use an
> extension cord unless it is **12 awg(2.5mm) or larger**, with only one outlet on the end. The 7.0T
> should be the only appliance in the electrical circuit. Do not attempt to disable the grounded
> plug by using improper adapters, or in any way modify the cord set; a serious shock or fire hazard
> may result along with computer malfunctions.

Electrical safety, printed page 6:

> Never remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power. If voltage varies by 10% or more
> outside the specified range **(100 to 240V)**, the performance of your 7.0T may be affected. Such
> conditions are not covered under your warranty.

> Motor: 3.0HP AC
> Power: **120 volts, 60 Hz, dedicated 20-amp circuit (NEMA 5-15P** standard power plug/ receptacle
> type)

and beside that line a drawing of a plug captioned **NEMA 5-20P**.

| Where | 2025 book (this card) | 2026 book |
|---|---|---|
| Safety item 2, outlet | **115-volt AC, 60 Hz, 20-amp** | 120-volt AC, 60 Hz, 20-amp |
| Electrical page, tolerance range | **(100 to 240V)** | no range printed |
| Electrical page, plug type in words | **NEMA 5-15P** | NEMA 5-20P |
| Electrical page, plug drawing | NEMA 5-20P | NEMA 5-20P |
| Extension cord | 12 awg(2.5mm) or larger | 12 awg(2.5mm) or larger |
| Amps | 20, in both places | 20, in both places |

- **The amp figure is the one that never moves: 20 amp, dedicated.** A 20-amp circuit is what an
  installer should provide from either book.
- **NEMA 5-15P and NEMA 5-20P are different plugs.** A 5-15P has two parallel blades and fits any
  household 15-amp receptacle; a 5-20P has one blade turned sideways and fits only a 20-amp
  T-slot receptacle (`spirit-commercial-safety-treadmill-plug-nema-5-20p-into-a-5-20r-t-slot`). The
  2025 page names one and draws the other, and the 2026 page corrected the words to match the
  drawing. Look at the plug on the machine before ordering a receptacle.
- **115 and 120 volts name the same North American supply**; the 2026 book brought item 2 into line
  with the electrical page's 120. The 2025 book's `(100 to 240V)` tolerance range is the medical
  bikes' and ergometer's universal-supply sentence left in a treadmill whose own page says 120
  volts, 60 Hz; the 2026 book removed the bracket (`xt-2023-safety-supply-voltage-variation`).

**Nothing in the 2025 book reconciles its four figures.** The rest of the page - the cover rule,
rain and moisture, ingress, oxygen-rich environment and the IEC 60601-1 climatic block - is
unchanged between the two printings (`spirit-2026t-safety-operating-environment-limits`), as is the
grounding page (`spirit-product-must-be-grounded`) and the 12 awg cord
(`spirit-2026t-safety-extension-cord-12-awg-or-larger`).

**The 4.0T-2025 book has no such change**: it prints 120-volt, 20-amp, NEMA 5-20P in both its
February and May 2025 printings, exactly as its 2026 book does
(`spirit-2026t-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`).
