Press **"History" on the Home page** to see your latest workout records.

- **Up to 10 records are stored locally on the console.**
- **Link your profile to the SOLE+ mobile app to access your full workout history.**
- Tap a record preview to see the full summary.

That 10-record limit is the reason to link a SOLE+ account: once the eleventh workout is done, the console alone
cannot show the first.
