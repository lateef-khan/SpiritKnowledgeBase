The console ships with **Sleep mode**, which has **three settings**:

| Setting | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| ON | The unit goes into an energy-saving state after **15 minutes of inactivity**. |
| OFF | The console stays lit while the power is on. |
| RETAIL | The console runs the **demo video after 3 minutes of inactivity**. |

**How to change it depends on the display type:**

- **LED displays**: hold the **Stop and Display keys for 5 seconds** to switch between ON and OFF.
- **LCD displays**: press and hold the **Stop, Enter and Display keys for 5 seconds** to switch between ON and OFF.
- **TFT displays**: tap **"Sleep Mode" under Settings** to switch among the three settings.

**Only the TFT touchscreen can reach RETAIL.** The LED and LCD key combinations toggle ON and OFF only, so a
machine showing the demo video has to be taken out of RETAIL from the touchscreen. This page is printed for the
whole console family, not only for this treadmill's touchscreen.

The manual does not say which setting the machine leaves the factory in.
