The Home screen shows an overview of your activity and offers shortcuts to:

- an **immediate start**;
- a **manually controlled timed workout**;
- your **favourite programs and video classes**;
- **club events** you take part in or might be interested to join.

**In guest mode you can set age and weight here**, which helps the machine calculate your workout summary more
accurately.

**The blocks on this page are customisable.** Press **"Edit Home"** to configure a layout that suits you; you can
enable or disable optional blocks and change their order.

History and Favorites are also reached from the Home page.
