The machine has a **Bluetooth transceiver** that lets it interact with compatible **iOS and Android** smartphones
and tablets through the **SOLE+ App**.

1. Download the free **SOLE+ App** from the **Apple Store or Google Play**.
2. Make sure **Bluetooth is enabled on your device**.
3. **Tap the icon at the top right corner** of the app to search for Sole equipment.
4. When the equipment is detected, **tap Connect**.
5. When the app and equipment are synced, **the Bluetooth icon on the equipment's console lights up**.

**Cloud sites the app can sync workout data to:** Apple Health, Google Health Connect, Strava, MapMyFitness, "with
more to come".

Linking a console *profile* to a SOLE+ *account* by QR code is a separate operation on its own card; this card is
the Bluetooth pairing between app and machine.
