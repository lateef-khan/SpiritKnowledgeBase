On its first launch the console prompts you to either **enter as a guest** or **create a new profile**.

Sole recommends creating your own profile. A profile lets the machine remember your physical parameters, favourite
programs and templates; gives you a custom program with an adjustable intensity pattern; and lets your workout
records synchronise with the SOLE+ mobile app.

- **Tap + to create a new profile.**
- **A maximum of 9 profiles can be created.**
- **Each profile can be protected with a passcode.**

**In guest mode**, set your **age and weight on the Home screen** — that helps the machine calculate your workout
summary more accurately.

Fitness tests also read the physical parameters, and their setup page has an "Edit Parameters" button of its own.
