**To start.** Once you have chosen the program, press the **"Start This Program" button on the screen**, or the
**physical "START" key** on the machine.

After a **short countdown** the console enters workout mode.

- **Warm-up and cool-down phases may be skipped by pressing "Skip".**
- Switch between **Stats (Focus), Charts and Track views** to check progress.
- Go to the **Media** section for media content, or open **Garmin** to see real-time data from a Garmin smart
  watch.
- **In the Focus view, tap any of the number blocks to choose which parameters are displayed.**

**Controls during the workout.** Use the **side control panels** or the **physical keys** to adjust speed and
incline. Stop at any time with the **physical "STOP" key** or by tapping the **red pause button in the bottom
right corner** of the screen.

**After 5 minutes of inactivity in Pause mode the workout ends automatically.**

The LED display's own pause behaviour is described separately and gives a different consequence for the same 5
minutes — see the pause and resume card.
