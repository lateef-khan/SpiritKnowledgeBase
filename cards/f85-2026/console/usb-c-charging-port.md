Charge a personal device during a workout from the **on-console type-C USB port**. **The fitness equipment power
must be on.**

1. Connect your type-C USB charging cable to the **USB Power Port** and to your device.
2. Check that your device icon indicates it is charging.

**Notes the manual prints:**

- **The USB charging cable is not included.** Make sure a compatible USB charging cable is used.
- **Your device's "charging" icon may or may not appear.** Depending on the current your device wants, the icon
  may not come on while the device is still charging, possibly at a lower charge rate.
- **The port provides up to 5Vdc / 1.5 amp.**
- **You cannot save workout data to a USB drive through this port; it is for charging only.**
