Community challenges are on the **"Club" tab of the bottom navigation bar**. Events there are created by other
members of the SOLE community.

**To view all available events, your profile must be linked to your SOLE+ account.**

Two event formats:

| Format | What it is |
|---|---|
| Shared goals | A challenge with a predefined target score, to be achieved within a limited time period. |
| Rankings | A competition to earn the highest score possible within a limited time period and beat as many competitors as possible. |

**The score can be one of four focus parameters:** calories, distance, active time, workout count.

- **Filter** events by format, duration and focus parameter with the "filter" icon in the upper menu.
- **Join a private event** with **"Join via ID"** — enter the event ID shared by the event creator and you
  automatically become a participant.
- **Create your own** with the **+ icon** in the upper menu: choose event format, focus parameter, dates, event
  name and an optional description, and make it private or public.

**Data sharing.** "By joining your first event, you agree to share your username, profile image, and workout data
(such as workout dates, distances, active time, and calories) with other participants."
