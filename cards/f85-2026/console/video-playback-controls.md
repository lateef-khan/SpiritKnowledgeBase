Video classes use the same layout logic as media content — the same three view layouts and the same bottom panel.

- **Navigate through the video by sliding on the progress bar.**
- **Skip 10-second intervals** by pressing the skip icons.
- **The "Pause" button stops the treadmill.** It pauses your workout and the video at the same time.

That last point is worth knowing before you touch it: pausing the video is not a media-only action, it stops the
belt.
