Press the **red Stop key once** while the treadmill is running to pause it.

- The tread-belt **decelerates slowly to a stop**.
- **The incline goes to zero percent.**
- **Time, Distance and Calorie readings hold** while the unit is in pause mode.
- **After 5 minutes the display resets and returns to the start up screen.**

**To resume**, press the **Start key** while still in pause mode. **Speed and incline return to their previous
settings.**

**The touchscreen page describes the same five minutes differently.** Page 21 says "After 5 minutes of inactivity
in the Pause mode, the workout will end automatically", where this page says the display resets and returns to
the start up screen. Both are printed in this manual; the manual does not reconcile them.
