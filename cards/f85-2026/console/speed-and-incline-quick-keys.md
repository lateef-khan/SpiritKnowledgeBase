**Speed.**

1. Press the **Start key** to begin belt movement.
2. Use the **speed knob button** to adjust to the desired speed.
3. You may also use the **speed keys 3, 6, 9, 12**.
4. To slow down, use the speed knob, or press the **speed adjust keys, 3 through 12**.

**Incline.** Incline may be adjusted **any time after belt movement**. Use the **incline knob**. You may also
choose a more rapid increase or decrease with the keys **0, 5, 10, 15 on the left hand side of the console**.

**Read the quick key values with care.** The manual gives four speed keys (3, 6, 9, 12) and then says "the speed
adjust keys, 3 through 12", and it gives four incline keys (0, 5, 10, 15). It never states the units for the
speed keys or the range they span, and the console picture on page 13 carries no text in this extraction. Do not
assume these are the same values as an earlier F85 console — this manual prints no other figure.

Speed and incline changes do not happen immediately; the console obeys the command gradually.
