The exercise equipment can play music wirelessly over Bluetooth.

1. Turn on the **Bluetooth function** of your mobile phone or tablet.
2. Search for the name **"BT-Speaker"** in your device's Bluetooth menu.
3. **Tap to connect.**

Your device can now transmit music to the exercise equipment.

**"BT-Speaker" is a separate Bluetooth device from the machine's SOLE+ app connection.** The app pairing and the
speaker pairing are described as two different connections, and the speaker is the one that appears under that
name in the phone's Bluetooth menu.
