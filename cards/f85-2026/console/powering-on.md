1. Plug the treadmill into an appropriate wall outlet.
2. Turn on the **power switch located at the front of the treadmill, below the motor hood**.
3. **Ensure the safety key is installed** — the treadmill will not power on without it.

**The manual's cross-reference is wrong.** This page says "(see assembly step 7 for reference)" for the safety
key, but the safety key is inserted in **assembly step 6**; step 7 is the end cap.

The Getting Familiar With The Control Panel page and the F85 console picture on page 13 are images only; the
extraction carries no key names or console layout text from them, so this knowledge base has no card for the
physical button layout of this console.
