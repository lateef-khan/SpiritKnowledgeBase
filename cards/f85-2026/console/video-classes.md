Video classes are on the **"Videos" tab of the bottom navigation bar**.

They are **pre-recorded, manually controlled workouts of fixed duration** — the class does not drive the belt.
There are **hundreds of videos across 7 activity types**:

- Running
- Yoga
- Stretching
- Boxing
- Sculpt
- Meditation
- Full Sweat

Classes can be filtered **by instructor, duration, difficulty level, music genre and so on**.

The tab menu at the top of the page also has **Collections** — sequences of video classes that follow a theme.

Any class or collection can be marked a Favorite. Playback controls during a class are on their own card.
