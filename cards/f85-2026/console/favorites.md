**Any class, collection or program can be labelled a Favorite.**

- To "like" a program, open the **program setup page** and tap the **"ribbon" icon in the upper left corner** of
  the screen.
- **Up to 12 favourite programs appear on the Home page** for easier access.
- Manage previously labelled programs on the **Favorites page**, reached from the Home page or the user Profile
  page.
- To remove the label, **un-tap the ribbon icon**.

Templates are a separate list with its own limit of 12; do not confuse the two.
