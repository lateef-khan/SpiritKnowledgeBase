The equipment's Bluetooth device **can also be connected to Bluetooth wearables**.

- **The wearable can be received even when the app is already in use** — the app connection does not block it.
- **Wear the Bluetooth wearable first, then commence connection.**
- While connected, **the console shows the heart rate value via Bluetooth**.

**Heart rate programs will only accept this source.** Page 24 restricts the Heart Rate programs to wireless
Bluetooth wearables and requires a strong, steady value in the Heart Rate Window before starting.

The manual names no brands, no protocol version and no supported-device list.
