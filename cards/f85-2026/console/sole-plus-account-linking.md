1. **Scan the QR code on the console screen** to download the mobile app.
2. Install the app and complete registration.
3. Open the **QR code scanning camera in the SOLE+ app**, and **scan the second QR code** on the console to link
   your local console profile with the SOLE+ account.

**A console profile cannot be linked to multiple SOLE+ accounts at the same time.**

If you skip this step during first launch, you can reach it again later in **Profile settings**.

Linking is what unlocks two other things: **full workout history** beyond the 10 records the console stores
locally, and **viewing all available Club events**.
