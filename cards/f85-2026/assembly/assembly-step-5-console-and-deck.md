Hardware for this step: **Split Washer (214) 4 pcs, Flat Washer (215) 4 pcs, Button Head Socket Bolt (251) 4 pcs,
Button Head Socket Bolt (263) 4 pcs, Flat Washer (264) 4 pcs.** Tools: **M5 Allen wrench head screw wrench (253)**
and **M6 L Allen wrench (262)**.

1. Connect the **Console Rotate Axle Assembly (12) Computer Cable (Middle) (12-06)** to the **Console Assembly
   (10) Computer Cable (Upper) (10-05)**.
2. Attach the **Console Rotate Axle Assembly (12)** and **Console Assembly (10)** using Button Head Socket Bolt
   (251) 4 pcs, Split Washer (214) 4 pcs and Flat Washer (215) 4 pcs, with the M5 Allen wrench head screw wrench
   (253).
3. **Remove the Locking Pin (52), then lift up the running deck.**
4. With the running deck lifted, use the **M6 L Allen wrench (262)** and fasten with **Button Head Socket Bolts
   (263) 4 pcs and Flat Washers (264) 4 pcs**.
5. Cover the screw holes on the **Console Outer Cover Assembly (11)** with the **Repair Mast Cover (81) 2 pcs**.

**After the above assembly, tighten the screws in place** — this is the step where the loosely started screws from
steps 2, 3 and 4 get fully tightened.

Part #251 is named only in the step text and in the step sidebar; the assembly pack checklist pages do not list it.
