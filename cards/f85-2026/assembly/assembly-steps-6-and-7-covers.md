These are the closing steps; the end cap step is a single instruction, so both are on one card.

**Step 6.** Hardware: **Phillips Head Screw (252) 4 pcs** (M5 × P0.8 × 12mm). Tool as printed: **M5 Allen wrench
head screw wrench (253)**.

1. Attach the **Frame Base Cover (R) (62)** and **Frame Base Cover (L) (63)** with Phillips Head Screw (252) 4 pcs.
2. Fit the **Rotate Axle Mast Cover Assembly (13)** onto the **Console Assembly (10)**.
3. Insert the **Safety Key (32)** into the **Console Outer Cover Assembly (11)**.

**Step 7.** Insert the **End Cap (49)**. The page prints "Note: After above assembly" with nothing after it; the
sentence is cut off in this extraction and no further text survives.

**The manual points at the wrong step for the safety key.** The Getting Started page (page 13) says to make sure
the safety key is installed and adds "(see assembly step 7 for reference)". The safety key is inserted in **step
6**; step 7 is the end cap. Both statements are printed in this manual.

Step 6 tells you to drive a Phillips head screw with an Allen wrench; that is how the manual prints it.
