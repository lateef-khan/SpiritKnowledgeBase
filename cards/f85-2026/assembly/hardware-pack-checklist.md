Hardware is bagged by assembly step. Part numbers are the numbers printed with a `#`.

| Step | Part | Description | Quantity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | #214 | Ø 8 × 1.5T Split Washer | 8 pcs |
| 2 | #250 | 5/16" × 3" Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 pcs |
| 3 | #214 | Ø 8 × 1.5T Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #215 | Ø 5/16" × Ø 18 × 1.5T Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| 4 | #215 | Ø 5/16" × Ø 18 × 1.5T Flat Washer | 2 pcs |
| 4 | #214 | Ø 8 × 1.5T Split Washer | 2 pcs |
| 5 | #215 | Ø 5/16" × Ø 18 × 1.5T Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #214 | Ø 8 × 1.5T Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #263 | 3/8" × 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #264 | Ø 3/8" × Ø 25 × 2T Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| 6 | #252 | M5 × P0.8 × 12mm Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |

**The checklist is incomplete against the step pages.** Three fasteners the step instructions call for do not
appear anywhere on the checklist pages:

| Part | Called for by | Quantity the step text gives |
|---|---|---|
| #220 | Assembly step 3 | 5/16" × 65mm Button Head Socket Bolt, 4 pcs |
| #235 | Assembly step 4 | Button Head Socket Bolt, 4 pcs |
| #251 | Assembly step 5 | 5/16" × 1/2" Button Head Socket Bolt, 4 pcs |

Do not read the missing rows as "no bolt is needed" — the step pages are the ones that name the bolt.

**Tools.** The step instructions name two, both supplied: the **M5 Allen wrench head screw wrench (253)** and the
**M6 L Allen wrench (262)**. The M6 L Allen wrench is also the belt tension and belt tracking tool. The Assembly
Tools panel on page 7 is a picture with no caption, so nothing further is recoverable from the extraction.
