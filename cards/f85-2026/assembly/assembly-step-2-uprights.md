Hardware for this step: **Split Washer (214) 8 pcs, Button Head Socket Bolt (250) 8 pcs.** Tool: **M5 Allen wrench
head screw wrench (253)**.

1. Connect the **Computer Cable (Middle) (79)** on the **Right Upright (4)** to the **Computer Cable (Lower)
   (78)**.
2. Attach the **Left and Right Upright (5, 4)** using Button Head Socket Bolt (250) 8 pcs and Split Washer (214)
   8 pcs.

**Do not fully tighten the screws yet.** Before moving on to step 3, make sure the cables you just connected are
**not pinched between the steel tubing** — pinched cables may prevent the treadmill from operating properly.
