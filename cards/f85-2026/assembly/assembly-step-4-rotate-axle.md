Hardware for this step: **Split Washer (214) 2 pcs, Flat Washer (215) 2 pcs, Button Head Socket Bolt (235) 4
pcs.** Tool: **M5 Allen wrench head screw wrench (253)**.

1. Connect the **Computer Cable (Middle) (12-06)** on the **Console Rotate Axle Assembly (12)** to the **Computer
   Cable (Lower) (11-24)** on the **Console Outer Cover Assembly (11)**.
2. Attach the **Console Rotate Axle Assembly (12)** and the **Console Outer Cover Assembly (11)** using Button
   Head Socket Bolt (235) 4 pcs, Split Washer (214) 2 pcs and Flat Washer (215) 2 pcs.

**Attach the two upper screws (235) loosely first** — it makes it easier to align and hang the Console Rotate Axle
Assembly. **Do not fully tighten the screws yet.**

Note the mismatch as printed: four bolts, but only two split washers and two flat washers. Part #235 does not
appear on the assembly pack checklist pages at all.
