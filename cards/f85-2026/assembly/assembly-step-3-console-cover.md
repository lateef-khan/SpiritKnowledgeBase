Hardware for this step: **Split Washer (214) 4 pcs, Flat Washer (215) 4 pcs, Button Head Socket Bolt (220) 4 pcs**
(#220 is the 5/16" × 65mm bolt). Tool: **M5 Allen wrench head screw wrench (253)**.

1. Connect the **Computer Cable (Middle) (79)** on the **Right Upright (4)** to the **Computer Cable (Upper)
   (11-28)** on the **Console Outer Cover Assembly (11)**.
2. Connect the **Monochrome light strip (80)** on the Left and Right Upright (5, 4) to the **Connecting Wire
   (11-32)**.
3. Attach the **Left and Right Upright (5, 4)** using Button Head Socket Bolt (220) 4 pcs, Split Washer (214) 4
   pcs and Flat Washer (215) 4 pcs.

**Do not fully tighten the screws yet.** Before moving on to step 4, make sure the cables you just connected are
**not pinched between the steel tubing**.

**The manual prints "Attach the Left and Right Upright (5, 4)" for both step 2 and step 3**, with different
hardware each time. It does not say what step 3 attaches the uprights to. The picture is not recoverable from this
extraction, so work from the machine.

Part #220 is not listed on the assembly pack checklist pages; only the step text names it.
