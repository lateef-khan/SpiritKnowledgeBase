1. Remove the treadmill from the carton and position it aside on a **smooth, level floor**. The rear should be at
   least **3 feet from any wall**.
2. **Do not remove the Velcro belt until you have removed the plastic wrap and Styrofoam from beneath the unit.**
   Turning the unit on its side after removing the belt may cause the unit to fold up and cause serious injury.

The shipping strap and the shipping safety pin have their own warnings before unpacking — read that card first.

**The clearance figure differs from the safety page.** Step 1 says at least 3 feet from any wall; the safety page
(page 2) says a minimum of 3 1/2 feet between the rear of the treadmill and any fixed object.
