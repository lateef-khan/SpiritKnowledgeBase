**Serious injury could occur if this folding treadmill is not unpacked properly.**

**The shipping strap.** A deck restraint shipping strap is installed around the treadmill base to stop the
treadmill unfolding during shipping. If it is not removed properly the treadmill could spring open unexpectedly and
injure someone standing nearby.

- Position the treadmill **flat on the ground, in the orientation it would be in if you were using it**.
- **Do not tilt the treadmill up on its side while removing the strap** — that can make the folding mechanism
  spring open.
- To remove it, pull up on the strap and cut it with a pair of scissors.

**The safety pin.** A safety pin is locked on the treadmill base to stop the treadmill unfolding during shipping.
**It has to be removed before powering on or using the incline function**, to avoid damaging the machine. When
removing it, pull it away from the treadmill base completely.

**The manual does not say whether this shipping safety pin is the same part as the Locking Pin (52)** that
assembly step 5 tells you to remove before lifting the running deck. Step 5 names a part number; this page does
not. Treat them as possibly the same pin and check the machine.
