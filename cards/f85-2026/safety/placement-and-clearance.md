- Install the treadmill on a **flat level surface**.
- **Do not block the rear of the treadmill. Leave a minimum of 3 1/2 feet of clearance between the rear of the
  treadmill and any fixed object.**
- Do not operate the treadmill on **deeply padded, plush or shag carpet** — damage to both the carpet and the
  treadmill may result.
- Do not use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.

**Assembly step 1 gives a different figure.** It says to position the treadmill so the rear is *at least 3 feet*
from any wall, where the safety page says *3 1/2 feet* from any fixed object. Both numbers are printed in this
manual; the manual does not say which governs.

The warranty is void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment — a garage, a carport or
outdoors will void it.
