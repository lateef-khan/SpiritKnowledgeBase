- Install the treadmill with access to a **120-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet**, with **only the treadmill plugged
  into that circuit**.
- **Do not use an extension cord unless it is 14AWG or better, with only one outlet on the end.** Do not disable
  the grounded plug with an adapter and do not modify the cord set in any way. A serious shock or fire hazard may
  result, along with computer malfunctions.
- Route the power cord **away from any moving part**, including the elevation mechanism and the transport wheels.
- **Never remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power.**
- If voltage varies by **ten percent (10%) or more**, treadmill performance may be affected, and **such conditions
  are not covered under the warranty**. Contact the power company or a licensed electrician for testing.
- Never expose the treadmill to rain or moisture. It is not designed for outdoor use, or near a pool or spa.
- **Maximum operating temperature is 40 degrees C; humidity is 95% non-condensing** (no water drops forming on
  surfaces).
- Never use the treadmill during an electrical storm; unplug it as a precaution.

**This manual contradicts itself on the extension cord gauge.** The safety page (page 2) says **14AWG or better**.
The diagnosis guide (page 32, "Treadmill will only achieve approximately 7 mph") says an extension cord should be
"as short as possible and heavy duty **16 gauge minimum**". 14AWG is the heavier wire of the two. Both are printed
in this manual and neither page acknowledges the other.
