**This product must be grounded.** If the treadmill malfunctions or breaks down, grounding gives electric current
a path of least resistance and reduces the risk of shock. The treadmill has a cord with an equipment-grounding
plug, and that plug must go into an outlet installed and grounded to all local codes and ordinances.

**DANGER** — improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric shock.
Check with a qualified electrician or serviceman if you are in doubt whether the product is properly grounded.

- **Do not modify the plug.** If it will not fit the outlet, have a proper outlet installed by a qualified
  electrician.
- The product is for use on a **nominal 120-volt circuit**.
- A **temporary adapter** may be used to connect the plug to a 2-pole receptacle only until a properly grounded
  outlet can be installed by a qualified electrician. Whenever the adapter is used, the green rigid earlug
  extending from it **must be connected to a permanent ground**, such as a properly grounded outlet box cover, and
  the adapter **must be held in place by a metal screw**.
