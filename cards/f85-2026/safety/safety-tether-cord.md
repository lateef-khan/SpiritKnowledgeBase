A safety tether cord is supplied and **should be used at all times**. It stops the tread-belt if you fall or move
too far back on the belt.

1. Place the safety key into position on the console control head. **The treadmill will not start or operate
   without it.** Removing the key also secures the treadmill from unauthorised use.
2. Fasten the plastic clip onto your clothing securely to assure good holding power. The clip should be attached
   securely so it does not come off. Be familiar with its function and limitations. **Depending on speed the
   treadmill stops with a one to two step coast** any time the safety key is pulled off the console. Use the red
   Stop switch in normal operation.
3. If the console displays **"Safety key removed, machine stopped, click Replace the key to Operate"**, put the
   safety key back to continue using the treadmill.

Remove the tether cord after use to prevent unauthorised operation.

**A fast stop when the tether is pulled is a symptom, not a feature.** The diagnosis guide lists "tread-belt stops
quickly/suddenly when tether cord is pulled" as high belt and deck friction — see that card.
