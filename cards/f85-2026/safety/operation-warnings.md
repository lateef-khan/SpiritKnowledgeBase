- **Never** operate the treadmill without reading and completely understanding the result of any operational
  change you request from the computer.
- **Speed and incline changes do not happen immediately.** Set the value on the console and release the adjustment
  key; the computer obeys the command gradually.
- **Never** use the treadmill during an electrical storm. Surges in household power could damage treadmill
  components. Unplug it as a precaution.
- Use caution when doing anything else while walking — watching television, reading and so on. These distractions
  may cause loss of balance or straying from the centre of the belt, which may result in serious injury.
- **Never mount or dismount while the belt is moving.** Sole treadmills start at a very low speed, so straddling
  the belt during start up is unnecessary. Simply standing on the belt during slow acceleration is proper once you
  have learned to operate the unit.
- Always hold a handrail or hand bar while making control changes such as incline and speed.
- **Do not use excessive pressure on the console control keys.** They are precision set to work with light finger
  pressure; pushing harder does not make the unit go faster or slower. If the buttons do not work with normal
  pressure, contact your Sole dealer.
