**Read all instructions before using the machine.** To reduce the risk of electric shock, disconnect the
treadmill from the outlet before cleaning or service work.

- Do not operate the treadmill on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Both the carpet and the treadmill can be
  damaged.
- **Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine.** There are obvious pinch points and other caution
  areas that can cause harm.
- Keep hands away from all moving parts.
- Never operate the treadmill with a damaged cord or plug. If it is not working properly, call your dealer.
- Keep the cord away from heated surfaces.
- Do not operate where aerosol spray products are being used, or where oxygen is being administered. Sparks from
  the motor may ignite a highly gaseous environment.
- Never drop or insert any object into any opening.
- Do not use outdoors.
- To disconnect: turn all controls off, remove the tether cord, then remove the plug from the outlet.
- Do not use the treadmill for any purpose other than the one it is intended for.
- **The pulse sensors are not medical devices.** User movement and other factors affect accuracy. They are an
  exercise aid for heart rate trends only.
- Use the handrails provided; they are for your safety.
- Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals and bare feet are not suitable. Quality athletic shoes are
  recommended to avoid leg fatigue.
- The appliance is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or
  lacking experience and knowledge, unless supervised or instructed by a person responsible for their safety.

**Remove the tether cord after use** to prevent unauthorised operation.

Placement, clearance and the electrical requirements are on their own cards.
