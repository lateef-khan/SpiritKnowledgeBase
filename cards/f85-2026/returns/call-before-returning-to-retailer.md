**"Before returning your Sole product to any retailer, or if you require any assistance with assembly or technical
support please call us first for assistance at 866-780-7653."**

The manual prints this twice — once inside the front cover under the table of contents, and again on the back
cover — and gives the same number a third time in the Service section as the number to call **if there is a
question as to where to obtain service**.

The same number appears at the front of the manual as the **service department** number, and again in the welcome
letter as the number to call with a question about where to obtain service.

For a warranty claim rather than a return, the service steps have their own order: selling dealer first, then the
online service request, then this number.
