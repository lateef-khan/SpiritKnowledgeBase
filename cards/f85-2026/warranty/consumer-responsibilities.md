This warranty applies only to products in **ordinary household use**. The consumer is responsible for:

- **Returning the warranty registration card** to the address on the card **within 10 days of the original
  purchase** to validate the warranty — or, outside that period, registering with proof of purchase (receipt, bank
  statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation).
- **Proper use of the treadmill in accordance with this manual, including general maintenance** — the manual names
  **belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under the motor cover, and lubrication**, and points at all components
  under the General Maintenance section.
- **Proper installation** in accordance with the instructions and **all local electric codes**.
- **Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage**, replacement of blown fuses, and repair
  of loose connections or defects in house wiring.
- **Expenses for making the treadmill accessible for servicing**, including any item that was not part of the
  treadmill when it shipped from the factory.
- **Damage to the treadmill finish** during shipping, installation, or following installation.
- **Routine maintenance** of the unit as specified in this manual.
- **Providing pictures or video of any issue by email** as requested by the service technician, and **assisting
  with troubleshooting and diagnosis before a home visit**.

Two of these have teeth elsewhere in the manual: **failure to lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the
warranty**, and **damage to the running belt from improper tracking or tension adjustment is not covered**.
