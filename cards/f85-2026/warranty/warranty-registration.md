**Record the serial number.** Page 1 asks the owner to write the serial number of the fitness product in the space
provided in the manual.

**Register the purchase.** Two routes are given:

- The **self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to Sole**.
- Or register online at **www.soletreadmills.com under the support tab**.

**The deadline, and what happens if you miss it.** The warranty page states the registration card must be
completed and returned **within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited
warranty — **or, if outside the 10 day period, the warranty can be registered if you have proof of purchase**
(receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation).

**Exclusion 6 is the other half of this rule**: products that do **not** have a warranty registration card on file
at Sole are excluded, and **Sole reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists**.

Warranties **do not transfer** — a product sold, transferred, bartered or given to a third party is excluded, and
so is a product whose original serial number has been removed or altered.
