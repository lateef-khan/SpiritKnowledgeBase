**Effective February 1, 2026.**

Coverage runs **from the date of retail sale, as determined by sale receipt**, or, in the absence of a receipt,
**eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date**.

| Component | Warranty period |
|---|---|
| Frame / Drive motor | Lifetime |
| Parts | 3 years |
| Labor | 1 year |
| Cosmetic Items | 90 Days |

**Cosmetic items** include, but are not limited to, **grips, console overlays, and labels/decals**.

Sole's responsibilities are **providing new or remanufactured parts, at Sole's option**, and technical support to
its independent dealers and servicing organisations. Where there is no dealer or service organisation, Sole
administers these warranties directly to the consumer.

**This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household use.** The consumer's own responsibilities, and the
exclusions, are on separate cards — several of them can void this coverage.
