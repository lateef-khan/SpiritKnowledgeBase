**Keep your bill of sale.** The **labor warranty period** is established by **twelve (12) months from the date on
the bill of sale**, or **eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial
number**. If service is performed, keep all receipts.

**Follow these steps, in order:**

1. **Contact your selling authorised SOLE dealer.** OR
2. **Submit the service request online** at http://www.soletreadmills.com/serviceparts.html, including **serial
   number, contact information and a brief description of the problem**.
3. If there is a question as to where to obtain service, **contact the service department at 866-780-7653**.
4. **If no local service is available**, Sole will repair or replace the parts, at Sole's option, within the
   warranty period **at no charge for parts** — see the return authorization card for who pays freight.

**Expect to troubleshoot first.** The service department assists in troubleshooting **before** sending a field
technician, and **Sole may request pictures and video of the issue by email** to help with diagnosis. The
consumer responsibilities list makes assisting with this a condition of the warranty.

This written warranty gives you specific legal rights; you may also have other rights that vary from state to
state.
