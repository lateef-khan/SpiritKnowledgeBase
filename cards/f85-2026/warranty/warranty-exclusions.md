The warranty does not cover:

1. **Consequential, collateral or incidental damages** such as property damage and incidental expenses resulting
   from any breach of this written or any implied warranty. (Some states do not allow this exclusion, so it may not
   apply to you.)
2. **Service call reimbursement to the consumer.** Service call reimbursement to the dealer is also excluded where
   it does not involve a malfunction or defect in workmanship or material; for units beyond the warranty period;
   for units beyond the service call reimbursement period; for a treadmill not requiring component replacement; or
   for a treadmill not in ordinary household use.
3. **Damage caused by service performed by anyone other than an authorised Sole service company**; use of parts
   other than original Sole parts; or **external causes** such as corrosion (rust), discoloration of paint or
   plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, **improper maintenance including lack of
   consistent lubrication of the deck**, inadequate power supply, natural disasters, or elemental damage (power
   surges, lightning storms, water damage, fire damage).
4. **Products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered.**
5. **Products that have been sold, transferred, bartered or given to a third party. Warranties do not transfer.**
6. **Products with no warranty registration card on file at Sole.** Sole reserves the right to request proof of
   purchase if no warranty record exists.
7. This warranty is **expressly in lieu of all other warranties expressed or implied**, including the warranties
   of merchantability and/or fitness for a particular purpose.
8. **Product use in any environment other than a residential setting.**
9. **Warranties outside North America may vary.** Sole USA warranty coverage is limited to the **Continental
   United States**; Sole USA does not sell or service equipment in **Hawaii, Alaska, or outside the United
   States**. Contact your local dealer for warranty availability in your region.
10. **The warranty is void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment** — a garage, outside
    or a carport are the examples given.

Two more exclusions are printed outside this list: **voltage varying by ten percent or more is not covered**, and
**damage to the running belt from improper tracking or tension adjustment is not covered**.
