**This is E03, not E3 (incline adjustment error) and not E31 (overtemperature). All three are printed in the same
table on the same page.**

**Meaning.** The hardware current is too large.

1. Exceeding the rated load leads to excessive current; the system self-protects, shows the error code, and
   **re-power recovery** clears it.
2. **Check whether the connection between the power socket plug and the motor is loose.**
3. Replace the controller.

**E01 and E03 are both over-current faults with different first remedies.** E01 blames load and lubrication and
sends you to the motor; E03 sends you to the socket-to-motor connection. Work through both lists if the code is
not clearly legible on the display.
