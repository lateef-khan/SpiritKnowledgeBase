**This is a reminder, not a fault.** It means: check lubrication under the walking deck.

1. **Check lubrication under the walking belt.** The lubrication procedure is on its own card.
2. **Power the treadmill three times to reset the lube message.**

**The manual's page reference is wrong.** This row says "Reference belt deck lubrication procedure on page 28";
the lubrication procedure is on **page 31**. Page 28 is the rate of perceived exertion page.

The lubrication card also notes the reverse case: if you reach under the belt and find no lubricant, lubricate
**even though the LUBE indicator is not lit**.
