**This is E01 on the 2026 console. It is not E03 (hardware current too large), and it is not the E1 of the older
Sole DC controller code list, which meant no speed signal.**

**Meaning.** Over Current Protection — the treadmill is overloaded and controller protection is activated.

1. Exceeding the rated load leads to excessive current; the system self-protects, shows the error code, and
   **re-power recovery** clears it.
2. **A certain part of the treadmill is stuck**, so the motor cannot turn, the load is too heavy and the current
   too large. Adjust the treadmill to restart the operation, **or increase the lubricating oil**.
3. **Check whether there is a flowing sound or burning smell when the motor is running**, and replace the motor.
4. Replace the controller.

High belt and deck friction is the usual cause of an overload on a treadmill this manual otherwise treats as
healthy — the lubrication card is the first thing to work through.
