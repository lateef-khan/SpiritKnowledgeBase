**This is E05, undervoltage. E06 is the opposite fault, overvoltage.**

**Meaning.** Undervoltage.

1. **Check whether the input power voltage is normal.**
2. **Check whether the connector of the power socket is loose.**

The electrical page adds context this row does not: **if voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more the
treadmill's performance may be affected, and such conditions are not covered under the warranty.** A minimum of
120 volt AC is required, and an extension cord is a common cause of a voltage drop.
