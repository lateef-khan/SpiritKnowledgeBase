**The manual's answer is a supply voltage answer**, printed as:

> This indicates motor should be receiving power to operate. Low AC voltage to treadmill. Do not use an extension
> cord. If an extension cord is required it should be as short as possible and heavy duty 16 gauge minimum. Low
> household voltage. Contact an electrician or your SOLE dealer. A minimum of 120 volt AC current is required.

So:

1. **Do not use an extension cord.** If one is unavoidable, it must be **as short as possible and heavy duty, 16
   gauge minimum**.
2. Suspect **low household voltage**. **A minimum of 120 volt AC is required.**
3. Contact an electrician or your SOLE dealer.

**This manual contradicts itself on the cord gauge.** The safety page (page 2) says an extension cord must be
**14AWG or better**; this row says **16 gauge minimum**. 14AWG is the heavier wire. Both figures are printed in
this manual and neither page mentions the other.

The first sentence, "This indicates motor should be receiving power to operate", is printed as shown; it does not
parse cleanly and nothing has been supplied to complete it.
