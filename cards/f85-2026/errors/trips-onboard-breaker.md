**The manual's cause is high belt and deck friction.** Lubricate the deck.

**The printed answer is truncated.** The row reads "High belt/deck friction. See General Maintenance" and stops
there, where the neighbouring row for the same cause completes the sentence as "See General Maintenance section on
lubrication". Nothing has been added; that is the natural completion and it is stated here as an observation, not
as manual text.

To reset the on-board breaker: the display-does-not-light row says **push the circuit breaker on the front grill
in until it locks**.

A breaker in the **house** panel tripping while the treadmill's own breaker holds is a different fault with a
different answer.
