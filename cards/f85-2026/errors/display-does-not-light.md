Work through the manual's list in order:

1. **Tether cord not in position.**
2. **Circuit breaker on front grill tripped. Push the circuit breaker in until it locks.**
3. **Plug is disconnected.** Make sure the plug is firmly pushed into the AC household wall outlet.
4. **Household circuit breaker may be tripped.**
5. **Treadmill defect. Contact your SOLE dealer.**

The safety key is the first item for a reason — the treadmill will not power on without it.

If the on-board breaker keeps tripping rather than simply being tripped once, that is a different entry in the
same guide and points at belt and deck friction.
