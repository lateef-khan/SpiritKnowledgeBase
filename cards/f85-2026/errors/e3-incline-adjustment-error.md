**This is E3, the incline fault. It is not E03 (hardware current too large) and not E31 (overtemperature). All
three are in the same table on page 33.**

**Meaning.** Incline adjustment error.

1. **Check whether the incline cable is properly connected.**
2. **Connect the incline cable and power it on.**
3. **Connect the incline wire and re-calibrate it.**

**Calibration is the third step and the manual prints no calibration procedure.** This owner's manual does not
describe how to calibrate the incline on this machine, so that step cannot be completed from this document alone.

E3 is the one code that means the same thing here as in the older Sole E1 to E8 family — the shared incline card
covers the wider troubleshooting tree for it.
