**This is E23, the touchscreen to LED display link. E22 is the different link, between the LED display and the
control board.**

**Meaning.** The TFT electronic watch (the touchscreen board) has a communication error with the LED electronic
watch (the LED display board).

1. **Check whether the cable between the LED electronic watch and the TFT electronic watch is loose.**
2. **Check LED electronic watch and the TFT electronic watch is damaged** (printed exactly so).
3. **After 2 minutes of power failure, power it on again.**

If the touchscreen itself is dead rather than out of sync, the manual has a documented workaround: unplug the TFT
connection cable and run the machine from the LED display alone.
