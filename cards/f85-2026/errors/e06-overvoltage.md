**This is E06, overvoltage. E05 is the opposite fault, undervoltage.**

**Meaning.** Overvoltage.

1. **Check whether the input power voltage is normal.**

**That is the whole solution column as printed.** Unlike E05, which also asks you to check the power socket
connector, E06 has a single step. Nothing has been added here.

The electrical page adds that a voltage variance of ten percent (10%) or more can affect performance and is not
covered under the warranty; contact the local power company or a licensed electrician for proper testing.
