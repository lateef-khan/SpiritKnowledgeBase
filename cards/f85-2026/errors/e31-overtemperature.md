**This is E31, not E3 (incline adjustment error) and not E03 (hardware current too large). All three are printed
in the same table on page 33.**

**Meaning.** Overtemperature.

**The manual prints no solution for this code.** The solution column of the E31 row is blank. Everything else in
the table has a numbered remedy list; this row does not. Nothing has been supplied here, and nothing should be
read across from a neighbouring row.

What the manual does say elsewhere about heat: **the maximum operating temperature specification is 40 degrees C**
and humidity is 95% non-condensing, and dirt and hair can **block air inlets**, which is why the manual asks for
monthly vacuuming under the machine and a motor-cover vacuum every other month.
