**The manual's cause is high belt and deck friction**, and its remedy is the lubrication section under General
Maintenance.

Why it is a symptom at all: the safety instructions say that when the safety key is pulled, **the treadmill stops
with a one to two step coast**, depending on speed. A belt that stops dead instead of coasting is telling you the
belt and deck are dragging.

1. Check for lubricant between the belt and deck.
2. Lubricate to the procedure on page 31 if there is none, or if the deck is dry.

The same friction shows up elsewhere in the guide as the treadmill tripping its own 15 amp on-board circuit.
