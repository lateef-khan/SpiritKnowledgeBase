**The manual's answer is that something else is sharing the circuit.** As printed: "Check that the treadmill is
the only appliance in the circuit."

1. **Make the treadmill the only device on that circuit.** The electrical page names fluorescent lights with
   electronic ballasts, a coffee maker, a space heater and a hair drier as examples of devices that cause tripping.
2. If the breaker is an **AFCI or GFCI** type, read the AFCI/GFCI card — those breakers can trip on the
   treadmill's inrush current, and Sole names the brands it has tested.
3. The treadmill needs a **120-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet** with only the treadmill on the circuit.

**The manual points at a section title that does not exist under that name.** The row says to see "Important
Electrical Information in the front of this manual"; the table of contents uses that title, but the actual heading
on page 3 is **IMPORTANT ELECTRICAL INSTRUCTIONS**. Same section, two names.

If it is the treadmill's own on-board breaker that trips, that is a different fault with a different cause.
