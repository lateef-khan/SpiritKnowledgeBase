**This is E02 on the 2026 console. It is not E01 (over current) and not E03 (hardware current too large). It is
also not the E2 of the older Sole DC controller code list, which meant an overcurrent trip.**

**Meaning.** Hall mistake — as printed. The manual gives no further definition of the term.

1. **Motor is not connected properly, or not connected to the controller.**
2. Replace the motor.
3. Replace the controller.

Check the motor connection first; two of the three printed remedies are part replacements.
