**Manual** is a timed workout with speed and incline controlled manually at any time during the run.

**Hill, Fatburn, Cardio, Strength and Interval follow preset speed and incline changing patterns.** The **Total
Time and Max Speed** settings you choose before starting define the duration and overall intensity.

**How to read the numbers.**

- **Speed values are a percentage of the max speed set before the start.** If max speed was set to 10.0 mph and
  the chart says 20, that segment runs at 2.0 mph. If the chart says 100, that segment equals the max speed you
  set.
- **Incline values are absolute.**

**What is ambiguous.** The printed header row carries **22 labels** — "Warm up", segments **1 to 20**, then "Cool
down" — but every data row carries **26 values**. So at least one label spans more than one column, and the
extraction does not preserve enough column alignment to say which. The values above are in the exact order the
manual prints them; **the mapping of value to labelled segment is not recoverable and has not been guessed.**

Note also that the manual writes the program names two ways: "Fatburn" and "Interval" in the prose, "FAT BURN" and
"INTERVALS" in the chart.
