Six US military tests are on the Programs page:

- Air Force
- Army
- Navy
- Coast Guard
- PEB
- Marine Corps

**They measure the time required for you to cover a certain distance**, and **the speed is controlled manually**.

**The manual does not print the distance for any of them**, nor any scoring standard, so this knowledge base
cannot say how far an Army test runs on this console.

Read the fitness test preparation card before running one.
