**Templates are preset programs with a preserved set of parameters** — the program plus the settings you chose for
it. The Templates tab is in the tab menu at the top of the Programs page.

Two ways to make one:

- **Save a program as a template upon completion**, or
- **create one from scratch**: in the Templates tab tap **"New Template"**, choose your base program, then save it
  with adjusted properties.

**A maximum of 12 template programs can be stored.** To remove unwanted templates, press **"Edit"** in the
Templates tab.

**Templates are not Favorites.** Favorites are a "liked" list of programs, classes and collections marked with the
ribbon icon, also capped at 12 on the Home page. The two lists are separate features with separate limits that
happen to share a number.
