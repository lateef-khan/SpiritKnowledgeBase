Fitness tests are on the **Programs page**. Tap a test card to open its setup page.

**Check your physical parameters first.** The data shown on the setup page **may affect the course of the test and
its results**. If it is not accurate, press **"Edit Parameters"** to adjust the numbers.

**Before the test:**

- **Make sure you are in good health. Check with your physician before performing any exercise if you are over the
  age of 35, or have any pre-existing health conditions.**
- **Make sure you have warmed up and stretched** before taking the test.
- **Do not take in caffeine before the test.**

The individual tests — the Gerkin protocol and the military tests — are on their own cards.
