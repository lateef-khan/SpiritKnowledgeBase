**HIIT is a customisable interval program.** You manually choose:

- the **duration** of the sprint and rest intervals,
- the **speed** of the sprint and rest intervals,
- the **total number of intervals**.

**This is not the same as the "Intervals" preset.** The Intervals preset follows a fixed pattern printed in the
program chart, where speed alternates between roughly half and full of your set max speed on a schedule you do not
choose. HIIT lets you set the intervals yourself.

The manual gives no limits for interval duration, speed or count.
