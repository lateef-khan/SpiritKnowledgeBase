**The Heart Rate program uses incline, not speed, to control your heart rate.**

- The **incline gradually increases until you reach your target heart rate**, then **adjusts automatically to keep
  you within 5 bpm of your goal**.
- **A heart rate monitor is required for this program.**
- **The program finishes when the time is up, or if your heart rate is 20% higher than the target.**

**Setting the target.** On the program setup page you can either **set the target bpm directly**, or select
**60% or 80% of your age-predicted maximum heart rate** and let the machine calculate the target. If you choose
the second option, **make sure the machine knows how old you are** — otherwise the calculation uses the default
values.

**Which monitor will work is stated elsewhere in this manual and is narrower than "a heart rate monitor".** Page
24 restricts Heart Rate programs to **wireless Bluetooth wearables only**, and requires a strong, steady value in
the Heart Rate Window. That half is on its own card.
