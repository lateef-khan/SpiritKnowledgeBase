Preset programs are on the **"Programs" tab of the bottom navigation bar**.

1. **Tap a program card** to open its **Program Setup page**, where you can read about the program and set the
   properties of your workout.
2. Adjust a workout parameter in any of three ways: **drag the ruler**, **tap the + and - buttons**, or **tap the
   number to open a numpad and type the value**.
3. Press **"Start This Program"** on the screen, or the **physical "START" key**, to begin.

**Total Time and Max Speed** are the two settings that define the duration and overall intensity of the patterned
programs.

The "ribbon" icon in the upper left of this page is how you mark a program a Favorite.

Fitness tests have their own setup pages, reached the same way from the Programs tab.
