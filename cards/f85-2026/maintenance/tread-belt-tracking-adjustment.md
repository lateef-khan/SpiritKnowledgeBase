**This is belt tracking — which way the belt sits across the deck. Belt tension, how tight it is, is a different
adjustment on its own card.**

The treadmill is designed so the tread-belt stays reasonably centred in use. **Some drift toward one side is
normal**, depending on the user's gait and whether they favour one leg. Adjust only if **the belt continues to
move toward one side during use**.

1. Use the **6 mm Allen wrench (262)** provided.
2. **Make tracking adjustments on the left side bolt.**
3. Set belt speed at **3 mph**.
4. If the belt is **too close to the left side**, turn the bolt **1/4 turn to the right (clockwise)** and wait a
   few minutes for the belt to adjust itself. Continue in 1/4 turns until the belt stabilises in the centre of the
   running deck.
5. If the belt is **too close to the right side**, turn the bolt **counter-clockwise**.

**A small adjustment can make a dramatic difference, which may not be apparent right away.** The belt may need
periodic tracking adjustment depending on use and walking or running characteristics; adjustments become less of a
concern as the belt wears in. **Proper belt tracking is an owner responsibility common with all treadmills.**

**ATTENTION: damage to the running belt resulting from improper tracking or tension adjustments is not covered
under the Sole warranty.**

**The diagnosis guide sends you to the wrong section.** For "tread-belt does not stay centered" the page 32 table
says "See General Maintenance section on Tread-belt Tension", but centring is this section, Tread-belt Tracking.
