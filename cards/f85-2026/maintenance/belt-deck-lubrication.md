**How often.** The belt and deck come **pre-lubricated**. Lubricate again **every 90 hours of use, or every 90
days regardless of usage** (it can dry out), or whenever you notice the deck is dry. **Reach between the belt and
deck to verify lubricant is present every other month.** If there is none, lubricate **even though the "Lube"
indicator is not lit on the console**. Otherwise lubricate when the console's lubrication reminder lights after 90
hours of use.

**First, clean between the belt and deck.**

1. Use a clean, non-fraying rag, t-shirt or light towel.
2. Halfway between the end of the treadmill and the motor cover, shove the garment under the belt until you can
   grasp it on both sides of the belt.
3. Drag the garment the length of the entire belt **1-2 times**, then remove it.

**What to use.** Only **Sole Fitness approved lubricant — 100% food grade silicone lubricant for treadmills**. DO
NOT use silicone caulking for bathroom and kitchen projects, or a lubricant with a spray/foam base. The treadmill
comes with **one tube of "Lube"**; extra tubes come from Sole Fitness or an authorised dealer, and a **Lube-n-Walk
kit** is also available. **Use 2-3 ounces of lubrication each application.**

**How to apply.**

1. Turn the power switch off and unplug the power cord from the wall outlet.
2. Measure **18 inches from the edge of the motor cover**; kneel down and reach under the belt approximately
   **4-6 inches from one edge**.
3. Squirt a line of lubricant about **1/8 inch wide by 15 inches long in an "S" pattern**, perpendicular to the
   motor cover.
4. Repeat on the opposite side.
5. Plug the cord back in and turn the power switch on.
6. **Walk on the belt at a moderate speed for five minutes** to distribute the silicone evenly.

**Why it matters.** If the lubricant dries out, friction between belt and deck rises and puts undue stress on the
drive motor, drive belt and electronic motor control board, which could cause catastrophic failure of these
expensive components. **Failure to lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty.**
