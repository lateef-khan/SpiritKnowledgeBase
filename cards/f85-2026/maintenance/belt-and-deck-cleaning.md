The treadmill uses a very high-efficiency low-friction deck, and **performance is maximised when the deck is kept
as clean as possible**.

1. Use a **soft, damp cloth or paper towel**. Wipe the edge of the belt and the area between the belt edge and the
   frame.
2. Reach as far as practical **directly under the belt edge**.
3. Do this **once a month** to extend belt and bed life.
4. The top of the textured belt can be cleaned with **a mild soap and water solution and a nylon scrub brush**.
5. **Allow to dry before using.**

Vacuuming under the machine and under the motor cover is a separate task on its own card, and lubrication is a
third task again.
