**This is normal on a new belt and no service call is needed.**

When a treadmill belt is brand new and has been in the package for a few months before use, the area of the belt
that wraps around the rollers **takes on the shape of the roller**. On starting the treadmill that shape causes a
**thumping sound each time the belt moves past the rollers**.

Once the treadmill has been used for a few workouts the belt wears in and the thumping goes away. The manual calls
this noise normal on new higher-end belts and says there is no need to contact service; **the new belt just
requires a wear-in period**.

Black belt dust during break-in is a separate normal effect, on its own card.
