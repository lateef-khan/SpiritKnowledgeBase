Dirt, dust and hair block air inlets and accumulate on the running belt.

- **Vacuum underneath the treadmill monthly** to prevent build-up of dirt that can be sucked into the inner
  workings under the motor cover.
- **Every other month, remove the motor cover and carefully vacuum out dirt and hair.**
- **UNPLUG THE POWER CORD BEFORE THIS TASK.**

**The page reference in this section is wrong.** The heading reads "GENERAL CLEANING (Use the chart on Pg. 5 to
record your maintenance)", but the preventative maintenance chart is printed on **page 30**; page 5 is the
unpacking warning and the assembly cover page.
