The treadmill has **transport wheels that are engaged when the treadmill is folded**. After folding, simply roll
the treadmill away.

Fold and lock the deck first — the folding card carries the warning that the unit must not be moved unless it is
in the folded and locked position, and that the power cord must be secured.
