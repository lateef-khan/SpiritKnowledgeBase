The chart is a blank log with four columns and a date column, for the owner to tick off. Its interval labels are:

| Task | Interval printed |
|---|---|
| Vacuum under motor cover & check wiring | Every other month |
| Clean & inspect deck for lubrication | Every other month |
| Lubricate deck | Every 90 hours or sooner if dry |
| Inspect belt tracking, adjust if necessary | Monthly |

The chart itself carries no filled-in values — the boxes are for the owner to date.

**The lubrication section gives one interval this chart does not.** Page 31 adds "**or every 90 days, regardless
of usage**" alongside the 90-hour figure. The chart prints only "every 90 hours or sooner if dry".

**The manual points at the wrong page for this chart.** The General Cleaning heading on page 29 says "Use the
chart on Pg. 5 to record your maintenance"; the chart is on page 30.
