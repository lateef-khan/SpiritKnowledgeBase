**This is the running belt at the rear roller. The drive belt under the motor cover, between the motor and the
front roller, is a different adjustment and is not an owner task.**

Belt tension is **not critical for most users**, but it matters for joggers and runners, who need a smooth, steady
running surface.

Adjustment is made **from the rear roller** with the **M6 L Allen wrench (262)** supplied in the parts package.
The adjustment bolts are at the end of the step rails, and **the adjustment is through a small hole in the end
cap**.

1. Tighten the rear roller **only enough to prevent slippage at the front roller**.
2. Turn the tread-belt tension adjusting bolts **1/4 turn each**.
3. Inspect for proper tension by **walking on the belt** and checking it is not slipping or hesitating with each
   step.
4. **Turn the bolts on both sides evenly.** Uneven bolts make the belt track to one side instead of running in the
   middle of the deck.

**DO NOT OVERTIGHTEN.** Over-tightening causes belt damage and premature bearing failure.

**If you tighten the belt a lot and it still slips**, the problem could be the **drive belt under the motor
cover** that connects the motor to the front roller. A loose drive belt feels similar to a loose walking belt.
**Tightening the motor belt should be done by a trained service person.**

Damage to the running belt from improper tracking or tension adjustment is not covered under the Sole warranty.
