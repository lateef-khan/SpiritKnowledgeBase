**This is normal.** Belt dust occurs during normal break-in, or until the belt stabilises. Sometimes the black
dust from the belt will appear on the floor behind the treadmill.

No action is required, and the manual does not ask the owner to contact service for it. Vacuuming under the
treadmill monthly is recommended anyway, for a different reason — dirt getting into the works under the motor
cover.
