**150 kg / 330 lb**, and it is on the serial number decal only. The CSF-HRAC manual's own text gives
no user weight limit anywhere: item 20 of its safety instructions and its safeguards line both carry
a **safety-catch** figure instead, which is a different fact.

The decal, read from a 500 dpi render of printed page 4 (`pdftotext` returns only fragments of it):

```
MODEL   NUMBER:  SP-4202-P
PRODUCT       :  Half Power Rack with Platform
COMPLIES WITH EN 957-1/2                    CE
CLASS: S STUDIO
MAX USER WEIGHT  : 150 KG / 330 LBS
SERIAL   NUMBER:  SP-4202P2206001
                                 MADE IN CHINA
```

150 kg is 331 lb, so the two halves agree. **330 lb is the lowest user weight limit in the Spirit
strength range**, 30 lb below a CSS station and 70 lb below the CSF-FUNT.

Do not confuse it with the machine's load capacity: the safety catches are rated at **650 lb**,
which is what may rest on them, not how heavy the lifter may be. Both figures belong in an answer
about "how much will the rack take".

A Spirit CSC880-2025 stair climber also carries a 330 lb / 150 kg limit. Different product line,
different machine — the numbers coinciding means nothing.
