**650 lb.** The CSF-HRAC half power rack prints it twice, in the two slots where other machines in
the range put a user weight limit:

| Where | Printed as |
|---|---|
| Safety instructions, item 20 | `Safety Catches Max: 650 lb.` |
| Safeguards, Proper Usage | `The maximum weight limit of the safety catches should not exceed 650 pounds.` |
| Specification table | `Max Load    650 lb` |

No metric figure is given for it anywhere. The manual does not say whether 650 lb is a static rating
or a drop rating, and it does not distinguish the catches from the J-hooks; it says only *safety
catches*.

**This is not the user weight limit.** How heavy the lifter may be is on the serial number decal, and
it is 150 kg / 330 lb — a separate allowance on a separate label. Give both when someone asks what
the rack will take.

The CSF-HRAC also asks for the largest floor perimeter in the range, 180 cm beyond its training area.
