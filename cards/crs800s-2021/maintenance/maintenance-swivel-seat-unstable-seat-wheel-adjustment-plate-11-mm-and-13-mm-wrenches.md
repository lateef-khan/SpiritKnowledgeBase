**The seat is unstable because there is a gap between the Seat Wheel Adjustment Plate and the aluminium track. The
fix is an adjustment, not a part.**

9-4 The Swivel Seat is not stable, as printed:

> The reason of the swivel seat is not stable is because there are gaps between Seat Wheel Adjustment Plate and
> Aluminum Track. The solution is as below:

1. **Adjustment tools:** a Phillips head screwdriver, an **11 mm open-end wrench** and a **13 mm open-end wrench**.
2. **How to adjust.**
   - **Step 1:** "Use Phillips Head Screw Drive to remove the screws" - the figure shows the screwdriver at the plate
     on the seat rail, which exposes the adjustment plate's nuts.
   - **Step 2:** "Use 11mm wrench, right side is 13mm wrench. The turning direction for front screws as shown and for
     the rear screws as shown." Two pictures: for the **front screws** the 11 mm wrench is on the left nut and the
     13 mm on the right; for the **rear screws** the 13 mm is on the left and the 11 mm on the right. In each picture
     the two wrenches turn in opposite senses, as a nut on a bolt needs, and the sense is given only by the red
     arrows on the page - read them from p. 41, not from this text.

Nothing says how far to turn or what gap to leave: the test is that the seat stops moving. No torque is printed.
Refit the screws of step 1 afterwards.

The CRS800S's other seat instruction is cleaning - the step rails and carriage wheels behind the mast and centre
covers (`crs800s-2024-maintenance-step-rail-and-carriage-cleaning`) - and the noise page of its owner's manual sends
a wobble to the four leveling pads (`crs800s-2021-maintenance-four-pads-front-and-back`). A seat that shakes on a
CR900 or CR1000ENT recumbent bike is taken up on its wheels with a wrench lifted while retightening
(`spirit-cr900-maintenance-shaking-handlebars-seat-wheels-levellers`); a medical stepper seat that will not lock is
a lever cable (`spirit-med-stepper-errors-seat-will-not-lock-thumb-nuts`).
