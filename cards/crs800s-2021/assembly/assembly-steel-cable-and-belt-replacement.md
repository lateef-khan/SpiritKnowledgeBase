Eight steps, and the book says the same strip-down serves for **the driving belt, the cable drive pulley, the
cable and the optical sensor board**.

1. Remove the screws of the left and right handles and then both handles; remove the screws and the console.
2. Take out the screws underneath the pedals and both pedals.
3. Remove the screws and remove the console mast, its cover and then the top cover.
4. Remove the screws of the left and right shrouds and the shroud covers.
5. Remove the screws of the **sensor bracket** (pic 7), then disconnect the connecting wires and the sensor
   brackets.
6. Release the **cable guide wheel axle** and detach the **wire cable end on the spring**, then remove the
   **shoulder screws** and the cable.
7. Use **retaining ring pliers** to detach the **retaining rings (C type)** and remove the **cable drive pulley**.
   Adjust the **idle pulley** to loosen the driving belt.
8. Remove the screw and the **flying wheel** (flywheel) to change the driving belt.

No screw sizes, counts or belt tension are printed here; the belt tension figure the same book gives is in its
"belt slippery" answer - **380 N on the front idle pulley, set with an Allen wrench** - and a belt that still slips
after that has a bad one-way bearing in the cable drive pulley, "notice the direction of the one-way bearing" when
you refit it. Winding the new cable back on is the next section and has its own card.

The page is laid out in two columns and `pdftotext` interleaves them; the order above is the printed step
numbering, confirmed on a render.
