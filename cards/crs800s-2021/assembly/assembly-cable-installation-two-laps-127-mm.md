**Two laps and 127 mm.** The 7.5S and 8.5S books wind their cable three turns and set 9 to 10 cm; this machine's
figures are its own.

1. Push the pedals to the end before installing the cable.
2. Locate the cable into the **first ditch (groove) on the right side** of the cable drive pulley.
3. **Rotate 2 laps** to hook with the spring.
4. Adjust the **cable guide wheel axle** to make the distance from the **centre of the screw to the wire cable
   end 127 mm**.

The 127 mm is drawn on the figure as the length from the screw centre to the cable end, with the spring beside
it. The same page then moves on to the optical sensor board: its distance to the cable drive pulley should be
7 to 9 mm and the hall sensor's to its magnet 1 to 2 mm, which belong to the "speed, watt and calories abnormal"
troubleshooting rather than to this card.
