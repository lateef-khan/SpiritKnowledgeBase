One drawing of the assembled stepper, thirteen leader-line labels, **no item numbers, part numbers
or quantities**:

| Left column | Right column |
|---|---|
| Pedal Foam | Console Assembly |
| Pedal | Console Mast Cover |
| Seat Back Cover | Top Cover |
| Drink Bottle Holder | Right side Shroud |
| Rear Shroud (L) | End Cap |
| End Cap, Oval | Rear Shroud (R) |
| | Bottom Step Cover |

The **Top Cover** is the lid over the drive at the front, the **Right side Shroud** the case beside
it, and the two **Rear Shrouds** the seat-carriage covers; the **Bottom Step Cover** is the plate
under the pedals. The **End Cap, Oval** is on the seat-rail stabilizer, the plain **End Cap** on
the front stabilizer.

Part numbers for these covers are in the owner's-manual parts list
(`crs800s-2024-specs-parts-list`), whose item names differ from these labels. The CS800 upright
stepper's outline is a different drawing (`cs800-2021-specs-outline-part-names`).
