**Step 3, Connecting the Main Frame** - hardware: #55 8 x 21 x 2t flat washer (2), #36 M8 x 15 Phillips head screw (2).

1. Connect the two handpulse wires (No. 79 & 80) together. Be careful not to pinch the wires, and feed them back into the connected frame.
2. Assemble the front main frame (No. 1) to the rear main frame (No. 2) using 2 flat washers (No. 55).
3. Secure on top with 2 Phillips head screws (No. 36).

**Step 4, Console Mast** - hardware: #55 flat washer (2), #39 M5 x 15 Phillips head screw (2), #54 M8 cup washer (1); plus 2 screws (36).

1. Run the two cables (No. 74 & 78) through the console mast (No. 3). Connect the two computer cables (No. 74 & 75) and the two handpulse wires (No. 78 & 79) together.
2. Secure the console mast (No. 3) to the main frame using 2 Phillips head screws (No. 36), 2 flat washers (No. 55) and 1 cup washer (No. 54).
3. Secure the drink bottle holder (No. 68) to the console mast (No. 3) using 2 Phillips head screws (No. 39).

**Two readings of the frame join.** The printed page says two screws and two washers at step 3 and two screws, two washers and a cup washer at step 4. The same page carries an unprinted Chinese layer - a leftover from the OEM file, not on paper - that says the frame join takes six M8 x 15 screws and six flat washers, and the mast five hex screws, two M5 screws, four flat washers and a cup washer. This card follows the printed page, which is what the customer sees; the pack holds only four washers (55) in total, which fits the printed figures and not the ghost ones.
