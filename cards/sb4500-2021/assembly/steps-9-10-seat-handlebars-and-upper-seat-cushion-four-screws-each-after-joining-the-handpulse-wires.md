**Step 9, Seat Handlebars** - hardware: #36 M8 x 15 Phillips head screws (4).

1. Connect the handpulse wires (No. 81 & 83).
2. Secure the seat handle bar L (No. 6) and the seat handle bar R (No. 7) using 4 Phillips head screws (No. 36).

**Step 10, Upper Seat Cushion** - hardware: #36 M8 x 15 Phillips head screws (4).

1. Secure the seatback cushion (No. 66) to the seat back bracket (No. 9) using 4 Phillips head screws (No. 36).

The seat handle bars carry the hand-pulse grips; wire 83 (the seat-tube section) was joined to wire 80 at step 7 and to wire 81 (the handlebar section) here.
