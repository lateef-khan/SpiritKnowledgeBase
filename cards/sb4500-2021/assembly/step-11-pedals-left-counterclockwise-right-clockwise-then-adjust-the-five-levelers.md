1. Secure the pedal L (No. 64) and pedal R (No. 65) using the Phillips head screwdriver & wrench (No. 61). **The left pedal (No. 64) should be tightened counter-clockwise and the right pedal (No. 65) clockwise.**
2. **Secure all screws and adjust the 5 levelers to sit evenly on your floor.**

Five levelers - two on each stabilizer tube and one under the frame, by the drawing. No torque and no wrench size are printed; tool 61 has 13, 14 and 15 mm ends.
