**Step 7, Seat Back Bracket** - hardware: #52 M8 x 40 button head socket screws (4) (the pack list calls them flat head socket screws).

1. Secure the seat back bracket (No. 9) to the sliding seat plate using 4 button head socket screws (No. 52). Connect the two handpulse wires (No. 83 & 80).

**Step 8, Lower Seat Cushion** - hardware: #36 M8 x 15 Phillips head screws (4).

1. Secure the seat cushion (No. 67) to the seat back bracket (No. 9) using 4 Phillips head screws (No. 36).
