**Step 5, Console** - hardware: #39 M5 x 15 Phillips head screws (4).

1. Connect the computer cable (No. 74) with the handpulse wire (No. 78). Feed the excess wire into the tube. Be careful not to pinch the wires.
2. Secure the console (No. 70) to the mast using 4 Phillips head screws (No. 39).

**Step 6, Front Handlebars** - hardware: #36 M8 x 15 Phillips head screws (4).

1. Secure the mast handle bar L (No. 4) and the mast handle bar R (No. 5) using 4 Phillips head screws (No. 36).

Step 5 item 1 reads as printed - it "connects" the computer cable to the handpulse wire; the two are the leads that step 4 fed up the mast, and the drawing shows both going into the back of the console.
