**Eighteen screws (36) against twenty called for.** The steps use 2 (step 3) + 2 (step 4) + 4 (step 6) + 4 (step 8) + 4 (step 9) + 4 (step 10) = 20. The file's unprinted Chinese layer describes an earlier revision in which some of these screws come pre-assembled on the parts and are removed first ("first remove the pre-assembled screws"), which would account for the two; the printed English says nothing of it. Count the bag before starting.

The book calls screw 36 a "Phillips Head Screw" on this page and a "Philips Head Screw" in the steps; step 7's screw 52 is a "Flat Head Socket Screw" here and a "Button Head Socket Screw" in the step.
