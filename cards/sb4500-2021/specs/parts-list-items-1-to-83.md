The SB4500 book prints an **Exploded View** (PDF p. 27, printed 25) and a two-page **SB4500 Parts List** (PDF pp. 28-29, printed 26-27) headed *Item / Description / Usage*. The drawing's callouts are circled numbers keyed to the *Item* column; no serial or ordering number is printed for any part, unlike the SB120, UB120 and FB lists. Items run 1-83 with **37, 53 and 56 not printed** and two sub-items, 15-1 and 82-1.

**What the list tells a service call.** The console is item 70, **7" TFT**; the motor (the resistance motor) item 72 with its cable item 69; the power cord item 71 and DC power cord item 73; the computer cables items 74-75; the speed sensor item 76 with its housing item 77; the flywheel item 62 and drive belt item 63; the idler wheel assembly item 13 and drive pulley item 10; the seat carriage assembly item 14 on the aluminum track item 8; the handpulse wires items 78-81 and 83 and the handlebar sensors item 82. The packed tools are items 60 (L Allen wrench 6 mm) and 61 (Phillips head screwdriver and 13/14/15 wrench).

**The list carries the book's typos as printed**: *Drive Pully* (10), *Philips* (61, 82-1), *mml* for mm on several dimensions. The SB500, the SB4500's predecessor, prints no exploded view and no list (`xterra-specs-parts-which-bike-elliptical-and-climber-manuals-print-an-exploded-view-and-parts-list`).
