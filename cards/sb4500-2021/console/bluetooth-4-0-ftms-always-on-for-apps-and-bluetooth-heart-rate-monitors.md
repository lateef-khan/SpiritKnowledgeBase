**Apps.** "Your SB4500 Fitness Bike has BLUETOOTH 4.0 FTMS built-in and will work with any FTMS-enabled 3rd party applications. Bluetooth on your SB4500 is always on and ready to pair." Open the app of your choice on your mobile device and follow its instructions to connect; **once paired, the Bluetooth icon on the screen lights up in BLUE.** The book names no app of Xterra's own and prints no XTERRA+ page.

**Heart-rate monitors.** The same radio works with any Bluetooth-enabled heart rate monitor; pairing depends on the monitor's make and model, so follow its own instructions. Once paired, the reading shows in the **Pulse** section of the screen. The chest-strap page is headed "(Sold Separately)" and prints the usual wearing steps but **no battery type and no battery life** ([the wearing steps on the strap card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-wearing-the-chest-strap-and-its-2500-hour-battery.md) are the same; only the note at the end is shorter). Interference is on [the erratic card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-erratic-heart-rate-and-the-seven-interference-sources.md), and the hand grips on [the grips card](hand-pulse-grips-beside-the-seat-need-10-seconds-of-contact.md).
