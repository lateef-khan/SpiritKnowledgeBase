The built-in hand pulse grips are on the handles next to either side of the seat. **Grasp both grips with steady contact between your hands and the metal contact pads for at least 10 seconds**; once the machine detects a pulse, the heart rate shows in the Pulse section of the screen.

The page adds a note on normal resting heart rate: about 75 beats/min on average (60-100), faster in women than men, over 130 in newborns, slower in people who do daily physical work or exercise, and moved by drugs, emotion and sleep. A Bluetooth strap is the other source ([Bluetooth card](bluetooth-4-0-ftms-always-on-for-apps-and-bluetooth-heart-rate-monitors.md)).
