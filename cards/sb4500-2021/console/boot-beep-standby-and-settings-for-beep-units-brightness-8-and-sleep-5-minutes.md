**Boot.** After power is connected the console enters boot mode: the buzzer beeps once and the boot screen shows, then the console goes to the standby interface. **Tap the middle of the screen** to reach the start interface, where START, MODE, PROGRAM and SET are offered.

**Settings (SET).** The settings interface lets you turn the buzzer on or off (**default on**), change the system to metric or imperial (**default imperial**), adjust the screen brightness (**default 8**) and set the sleep time for the display (**default 5 minutes**); the page also shows the **software version** (V1.0 in the picture).

The book prints no engineering or calibration mode and no wheel-size entry for this console. The buttons are on [the console card](touchscreen-with-tablet-tray-usb-port-and-nine-on-screen-buttons.md).
