The console drawing numbers ten program keys, five each side of the blue LED matrix:

| Key | Left column | Key | Right column |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MANUAL | 6 | INTERVAL |
| 2 | HILL | 7 | CUSTOM 1 |
| 3 | FAT BURN | 8 | CUSTOM 2 |
| 4 | CARDIO | 9 | HR 1 |
| 5 | STRENGTH | 0 | HR 2 |

Two Custom keys and two heart rate keys, and no Fitness Test or Constant Power key - which is what
separates this face from the CE800 of the same year (`ce800-2016-programs-ten-numbered-program-keys-with-fitness-test-and-constant-power`). The Quick Start
spec on p. 16 names the same lineup in its own words: "MANUAL, PROGRAM, CUSTOM1, CUSTOM 2, HRC1,
HRC2".

**The caption beside the drawing leaves Cardio out** - "(Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Strength,
Interval, 2 Custom, 2HR)", nine keys - while the drawing labels key 4 CARDIO. Five 2016 service
manuals do this; see `spirit-2016-programs-service-manual-captions-leave-cardio-off-the-program-buttons`.

**Two owner's-manual cards describe this console with one key where the face has two.** The 2016
owner's manual's Custom procedure begins "Press the Custom key"
(`spirit-ce-programs-custom-twenty-segments`), and its heart rate procedure is recorded as a single
HR key with no percentage choice (`spirit-ce-programs-heart-rate-control-operation`). The service
manual's drawing and its spec both say two of each. **Neither book says how Custom 2 or HR 2 differ
from Custom 1 and HR 1.** The XE895, whose 2016 service manual is this book with the residential
name, has USER-1, USER-2, HR-1 and HR-2 in the same four positions, and its 2018 owner's manual
gives HR 1 as 60% and HR 2 as 80% of maximum
(`spirit-xe-programs-heart-rate-programs-hold-you-within-three-to-five-beats`) - that figure is the
XE895's and is not claimed for the CE850.

The five preset keys are Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength and Interval - the five the 2016 owner's
manual describes under a heading that counts seven
(`spirit-ce850-programs-seven-preset-work-level-profiles`). The owner's manual's own console
drawing labels no key names (`spirit-ce-console-blue-led-console-face`); this is the first drawing
of the face on which the keys are named.

**The service manual prints nothing else about programs**: no profile chart, no setup steps, no
segment count, no percentage. The DISPLAY key above the program keys, with its PROFILE and STRIDE
indicator lights, is a console control, not a program.
