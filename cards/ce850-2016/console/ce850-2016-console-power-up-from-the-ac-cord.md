**When the A.C. power cord is connected to the elliptical, the console automatically powers up.**

- In **stand-by mode** the console display turns off.
- **To turn the console on, press any key.**

The self-test then runs in this order:

1. **All the lights turn on.**
2. When the lights go off, the Message Center shows the **software version (i.e. VER 1.0)**.
3. The window shows the **distance total and the total hours of use**.
4. The odometer stays up for **only a few seconds**.
5. The console goes to the start-up display: the dot matrix scrolls the program profiles and the
   Message Center scrolls the start-up message.

**This machine is mains powered.** It does not have the pedal-to-power generator the CE800-2013,
CE800-2021, CE800-2023, CE900 and CES880 manuals describe - see
`spirit-ce-console-pedal-to-power-up`. Do not tell a CE850-2016 owner to pedal to wake the console.
