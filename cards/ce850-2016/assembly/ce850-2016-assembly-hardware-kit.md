## Three items are a different size from the later CE850

| Part | 2016 | 2020 and 2022 |
|---|---|---|
| 134, the connecting-arm rod-end bolt | 5/16" x 1-1/4" hex head | **M10 x 1.5** hex head |
| 163, the rod-end nut | 3/8" x 8T nyloc | **M10 x 8T** nyloc |
| 175, the lower-swing-arm washer | 3/8"x30x2.0T **curved** washer | 3/8"x30x2.0T **flat** washer |

**Same item numbers, different parts.** A 5/16" bolt will not fill an M10 hole. Do not
carry the later figures back to a 2016 machine or the 2016 figures forward. See
[the later hardware card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ce850-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

Item **152** is printed **M5X15L** here and in the later step-four panels, and the
later step-*one* panel calls the same item M5x12mm. The 2016 figure agrees with the
majority.

## What the panels do not supply

- Step three's text calls for a **flat washer (180)** and a **nyloc nut (168)** at the
  rod end. Neither number is in any of the six panels, and this manual has **no parts
  list** to check them against. See
  [the procedure card](ce850-2016-assembly-procedure.md).
- Step six's text uses screw 153 for the console mast covers and never uses the two
  pieces of screw **154** the panel supplies.
- **No panel supplies the six levellers** the carton list contains, and no step fits
  them.
