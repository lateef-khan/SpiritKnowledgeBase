**This is a six-step build.** The 2020 and 2022 CE850 manuals print five stages
numbered zero to four and put the work in a different order. The two are not
interchangeable.

## Step one - rear rail

1. Gather hardware for step 1.
2. Put the 2 flat washers (176) on the 2 hex head bolts (137) and hand-tighten them
   through the **top** of the **middle stabilizer tube** into the Rear Rail Assembly
   (2), with the wrench (194).
3. Put 4 curved washers (187) on the 4 hex head bolts (139) and hand-tighten them
   through the **front** of the middle stabilizer tube into the Rear Rail Assembly
   (2), with the wrench (194).

*The later manuals call the same tube the **rear** stabilizer tube. This one says
middle throughout the step.*

## Step two - side back off, computer cable up, console on

1. Gather hardware for step 2.
2. Use wrench (193) to release 2 socket head cap bolts and take the side back apart -
   **disposed**. Pierce the 14P computer cable (68) from the bottom of the mast tube
   through it and pull it out of the top. Use 4 socket head cap bolts (145), 4 spring
   washers (186) and 4 flat washers (176) to secure.
3. Untie the computer cable (68), connect the 2 Handpulse w/Cable Assemblies (58) and
   the Handle Wire (Upper), Resistance/Incline (White/Red) (61/62) to the Console
   Assembly (55). Then place the console on top of the mast and use the Phillips head
   screwdriver (192) to tighten 4 Phillips head screws (150).

**The side back is thrown away here as part of step two.** The later manuals promote
that job to its own STEP ZERO and give it its own page.

## Step three - lower swing arms and connecting arms

1. Gather hardware for step 4. *(Printed "STEP 4" under the STEP THREE heading. It
   means step 3; every step in this manual opens with this line and this one is
   misnumbered.)*
2. Locate the left and right lower swing arms (13, 14) together with 4 wave washers
   onto the left and right shafts of the mast tube, then tighten with 2 hex head bolts
   (136) and two flat washers (175), with the wrench (194).
3. Untie the rod end bearing on the left connecting arm (8) and pierce a hex head bolt
   (134) through the rod end holes and rod end bearing, then tighten with a **flat
   washer (180) and nyloc nut (168)**, with wrench (194) and wrench (195). Do the same
   for the right connecting arm (9) and right lower handle bar (14).

**Items 180 and 168 are not in this step's hardware panel**, which lists a nyloc nut
as item **163** and a curved washer as item **175**. This manual has no parts list to
settle it with. The later manuals do the same job with **nyloc nut 163** and no
washer at the rod end. Do not order 168 or 180 on the strength of this sentence.

## Step four - upper swing arms and the handle wires

1. Gather hardware for step 4.
2. Insert the left upper swing arm (10) onto the left lower swing arm (13) and secure
   with 3 hex head bolts (138), 4 flat washers (176), 2 curved washers (187) and 3
   nyloc nuts (165), with wrench (194) and wrench (195). Do the same for the right
   upper swing arm (11) and right lower swing arm (14).
3. Connect Handle Wire (Upper), Resistance (white, 61) and Handle Wire (Upper),
   **Stride** (red, 62) to the 2 Handle Wires (Lower), **Resistance/Stride** (63), save
   the excess wire in the mast tube, and plug the switch wire caps onto the mast tube.

**The red handle wire is a STRIDE wire on this machine and an INCLINE wire on the 2020
and 2022 printings.** Same item numbers, 61, 62 and 63; different function printed
against them. This manual's console chapter also asks for a Max Stride the later ones
do not. Do not tell a 2016 owner their red wire is an incline wire.

## Step five - connecting arm, slide wheel and rear stabilizer covers

1. Gather hardware for step 5.
2. Secure the left connecting arm cover B (121) on the left connecting arm with a
   Phillips head screw (152), then the left connecting arm cover A (119) with 2
   Phillips head screws (152) on the lower handle bar. Do the same on the right (122,
   120).
3. Secure 2 slide wheel covers (99) on both left and right pedal arms with 4 Phillips
   head screws (152).
4. Secure 2 rear stabilizer covers (A) (104) and 2 rear stabilizer covers (B) (105) on
   both sides of the rear tube of the rear rail assembly with 4 Phillips head screws
   (152).

## Step six - mast covers, handle bar covers and end caps

1. Gather hardware for step 6.
2. Match the left and right console mast covers (111, 112) with the left and right
   side cases and secure with 2 sheet metal screws (153).
3. Match the front handle bar cover (100) and rear handle bar cover (101) on the left
   lower handle bar and tighten 4 sheet metal screws (153). Do the same for the right
   (102, 103). **Be aware not to pinch the wire.**
4. Plug both oval end caps (89) onto both ends of the front stabilizer tube.

**Step six item 2 uses screw 153 where the later manuals use screw 154**, a 4 x 15mm
sheet metal screw, for the same two console mast covers. This manual's step-six panel
carries both 153 and 154 and its text calls for 153 only; item 154's two pieces go
nowhere it prints.

## No water bottle holder, whatever the diagram says

None of these six steps fits one, and the carton list does not contain one - but the
machine diagram on p. 9 labels a **WATER BOTTLE HOLDER**. See
[the bottle holder card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ce-assembly-water-bottle-holder.md).
