- **LEVEL 2:** based on Level 1, speed increases by **1.0 miles per segment**, incline
  unchanged.
- **LEVEL 3:** based on Level 2, speed increases by 1.0 miles per segment, incline unchanged.

**P3 and P4 reach 8.0 mph at Level 1**, above the 7.5 mph running ceiling the same book gives
for the console; the book does not address this. The dot-matrix pictures beside the table
label their speed axis in kph bands (0.8~1 up to 11.1~12.0) while the table is in mph.
