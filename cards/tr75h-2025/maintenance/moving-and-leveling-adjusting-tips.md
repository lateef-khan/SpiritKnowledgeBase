There are **2 adjusting tips at the front bottom of the main frame**, marked L and M:

1. **Moving**: to move the machine, turn both tips **counterclockwise** (M).
2. **Leveling**: to level the frame for operation, turn both tips **clockwise** (L).

The drawing shows the tip under the front corner of the frame; the companion drawing shows a wheel at the rear of the frame beside the power inlet and switch. **The book prints no folding instructions** - a loose search for *fold*, *wheel*, *transport* and *move* finds only this page, the LUBE-alert wording and the boilerplate cord-routing line - and its post-workout routine omits the "store folded" step the other new-layout books print (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-post-workout-care`).

**The TR95H's page reads the opposite way round**: its SwitchPro knobs are turned clockwise to move and counterclockwise to level (`tr95h-2024-maintenance-moving-and-leveling-switchpro-knobs`). The page was read from the render; the text layer carries the page twice over. The care schedule's daily row, "adjust balancer to make it stably positioned on the ground", refers to these tips (`xterra-tr-maintenance-care-schedule-daily-to-six-monthly`).
