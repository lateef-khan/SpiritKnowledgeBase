This book replaces the drag-a-cloth procedure the other Xterra manuals print (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-cleaning-between-belt-and-deck-with-a-cloth`) with three short routines on one page.

**Cleaning and inspecting the running belt**

1. Turn off power and unplug from the electrical outlet.
2. Carefully position the treadmill on its side.
3. Use a damp cloth to wipe off the inside of the running belt. Carefully rotate the running belt by hand to clean the entire inside face. Return the treadmill to its upright position.
4. If running belt edges are frayed, check the running belt adjustment.
5. If seams are splitting, call your retailer or nearest authorized service center.

**Inspect fasteners and wiring**: check that all fasteners are properly tightened and all wiring is securely in place. To avoid damaging fasteners, do not over tighten.

**Cleaning**

1. Make sure the treadmill is off and unplugged.
2. To remove dust, use a small vacuum nozzle to carefully vacuum around all visible components.
3. To remove film or dust, use a slightly damp rag with a **mild cleaning agent sprayed onto the rag only**.
4. Be careful not to immerse any treadmill component in any liquid.

The same book's care schedule says "do not use solubility cleaners" and to use water on plastic parts (`xterra-tr-maintenance-care-schedule-daily-to-six-monthly`).
