**This book prints two lubrication procedures.** Page 41 (this card) is the JKEXER chapter; page 42 repeats the two-S-line boilerplate the other Xterra books print (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-deck-lubrication-s-line-18-inches-from-the-motor-cover`). Neither page mentions the other.

**Why**: if the lubricant dries out, friction between belt and deck rises and stresses the drive motor, drive belt and motor control board, which could result in catastrophic failure.

**How**

1. Lift the running belt as high as possible and wipe the deck and the edge of the belt with a clean, lint-free cloth to remove dirt on both sides.
2. Insert the straw from the silicone bottle from either side of the belt to reach the deck surface **about 18 inches from the motor cover**. Pump the silicone across the deck, parallel to the motor cover, in **about a one-foot line** in the middle of the deck, at approximately equal distance from both belt edges.
3. Repeat to distribute onto **three spots** on the deck, as drawn (the drawing labels *cover full width* and *lift belt as high as possible*).
4. Once applied, **walk, not run, at low speed for at least 5 minutes** so it spreads evenly.

Aim for the spot your feet hit the belt when walking, about 18 inches from the motor cover; walk on the treadmill to see where your feet land. If you mostly run, the spot may differ.

Notes: if the belt is stuck under a side rail, use a screwdriver to lever it out for easy lubrication; if the belt is not centred, see `tr75h-2025-maintenance-belt-tracking-opposite-quarter-turns-at-1-9-mph`. **If a static symptom happens, adding silicone on the running deck will also help solve it.**

The lubricant is a 1 oz tube of silicone in the box and a 7 oz pump bottle with a 14-inch applicator from the distributor (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-approved-lubricant-and-where-to-order`); the interval is 112 miles or 3 months (`xterra-tr-maintenance-lubrication-interval-every-112-miles`).
