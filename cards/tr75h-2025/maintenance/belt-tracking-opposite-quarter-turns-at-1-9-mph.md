This book turns **both** bolts, in opposite directions, and tells you to make **equal numbers of turns on the right and left bolts to prevent overtightening**. Running style and a non-level surface are the two causes it names for drift.

1. Press the Master Power Switch (at the front of the treadmill) to ON, make sure the safety key is on the console, press START, then increase speed to **1.9 mph**.
2. Stand right behind the treadmill to determine which side the belt is drifting to.
3. **Drifts to the right**: turn the **right** adjustment bolt **clockwise** a quarter turn and the **left** bolt **counterclockwise** a quarter turn (Fig. A). The belt moves to the left gradually. Repeat until it is back at the centre.
4. **Drifts to the left**: turn the **right** bolt **counterclockwise** a quarter turn and the **left** bolt **clockwise** a quarter turn (Fig. B). The belt moves to the right gradually. Repeat until it is back at the centre.

**DO NOT OVERTIGHTEN** - over tightening will cause belt damage and premature bearing failure; if the belt still slips after a lot of tightening, the drive belt under the motor cover may be the problem and is a trained-service job.

**The text says the two bolts are "at the front of the treadmill"; the two figures beside it draw hands at the rear corners of the deck, where the roller end caps are.** The page was read from the render. The TR95H book prints the same procedure with the bolts "at the rear" and 2 mph: `tr95h-2024-maintenance-belt-tracking-rear-bolts-opposite-quarter-turns-at-2-mph`.
