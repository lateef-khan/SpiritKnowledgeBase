1. **2 people are required for assembly.** Before beginning, note that **the end with the wire on the
   front post (#2) is for the right side of the main frame (#1)**. Have one person hold the front post and
   the other connect the wire from the main frame and the front post. Next, tuck the connector into the
   tube of the main frame.
2. Use **bolts (#9, M8 x 15 L)** and **washers (#10, 8.2 x 18 x 1.6t)** to fix the front post on the main
   frame. The parts list supplies eight #9 bolts, and the two handlebars take none, so all eight go
   here - four per side; the drawing shows bolts and washers at both the inner and outer faces of each
   foot bracket.

"Please make sure not to damage the wire and connector while assembling." No tool is named. The
drawing also labels wire 57 at the base and frame part numbers 46, 104 and 100 from the exploded view.
