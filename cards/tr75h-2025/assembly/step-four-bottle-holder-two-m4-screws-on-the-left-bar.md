Use **screws (#13, M4 x 10L, two)** to attach the **bottle holder (#6)** onto the **left bar of the front post
(#2)**.

That is the whole step. Step five closes the assembly: make sure the safety key is attached and the
power cable is plugged in, then "start your enjoyable workout" - see the powering-on card.
