There are **2 adjusting tips at the front bottom of the main frame**.

1. **Moving:** to move the machine, turn both tips **counterclockwise**.
2. **Leveling:** to level the frame for operation, turn both tips **clockwise**.

The drawing labels the two positions **L** and **M** with a curved arrow. **This is the reverse of the
TR95H Hiker**, whose SwitchPro knobs turn clockwise to move and counterclockwise to level. The PDF's text
layer carries a ghost copy of the paragraph headed "SETTING UP YOUR HIKER TREADMILL"; the printed page
says the same thing under "SETTING UP YOUR TREADMILL".
