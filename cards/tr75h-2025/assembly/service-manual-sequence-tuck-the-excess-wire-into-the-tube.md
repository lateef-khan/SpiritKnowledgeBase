Chapter 1 of the TR75H service manual (V1.0, January 2026) is three sub-steps, each with the same
caution: **insert the excess wire length into the tube so the signal cables are not compressed and
damaged.**

1. **1-1 Front post.** Connect the signal cables on the **right** front post first, then insert the excess
   wire into the tube.
2. **1-2 Console set.** Connect the cables; place the console set on the front post (excess wire into the
   tube); fix the console set by fastening the screws.
3. **1-3 Left/right upper handrails.** Connect the left and right fast-key and hand-pulse signal cables;
   fix the handrails with screws - excess wire into the tube first.

**The heading of 1-2 still reads "Assembling console, storage box and desktop rack"** - copied from the
TR95H book - but this machine has no storage box or desk rack and the three steps under it mention
neither. The owner's manual fits the handlebars (its step two) *before* the console (its step three);
the service manual lists them the other way round. No screw sizes are given here; the owner's manual's
parts table has them.
