1. Use the **knob (#8, console angle adjusting knob)** and **washer (#14, ø8.5 x ø20 x 2.5t)** to fix the
   **bracket (No. E)** of the **console set (#5)** on the bar of the **front post (#2)**.
2. Connect the wires from the front post and the console set.
3. Use **screws (#15, ø4 x 10L, six)** to fix the console set together.
4. Put on the **safety key (#7)**.

"Please make sure not to damage the wire and connector while assembling." The bracket is drawn
separately (label e) with the knob and the single washer going through it onto the crossbar, and the
six screws closing the console housing around it. The knob is what later sets the console angle.
