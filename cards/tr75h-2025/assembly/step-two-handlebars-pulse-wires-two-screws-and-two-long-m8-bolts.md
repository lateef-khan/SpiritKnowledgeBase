1. Connect the **pulse wires** from the **front post (#2)** and the **handlebars (#3 & #4)**.
2. Use **bolt (#12, screw ø5 x 15)** to fix the **upper side** of the handlebars.
3. Use **bolt (#11, M8 x 100 L)** and **washer (#10)** to fix the **bottom side** of the handlebars to the front
   post.

"Please make sure not to damage the wire and connector while assembling." The step calls #12 a bolt
where the parts page calls it a screw; there are two of each fastener, one per handlebar. The drawing
labels the handlebar wires 11, 14, 16 and 17 from the exploded view.
