From the TR75H service manual (V1.0, January 2026); the owner's manual prints no engineering mode.

**A. Entering.** Remove the SAFETY KEY. Press and hold **ENTER + START** together while turning on the power.

**B. In engineering mode** the dot matrix shows "**En**", the Heart Rate window shows the software version and the Time window the software date.

**C. Metric/imperial switching.** Press ENTER to open the unit interface; the window shows "Km" (metric) or "Mile" (imperial). Press **FAST** to toggle between them. Press ENTER to save and proceed to the total time display. To exit and restart, replace the SAFETY KEY.

**D. Total time.** The Time window shows the total accumulated time, range 0-9999; it does not reset to 0 by itself after 9999. To reset, **long-press STOP for three seconds**. Press ENTER to proceed to total distance, or replace the SAFETY KEY to restart.

**E. Total distance.** The Distance window shows the total accumulated distance, range 0-9999, likewise never resetting on its own. To reset, long-press STOP for three seconds. Press ENTER or replace the SAFETY KEY to restart the machine.

The 112-mile LUBE reminder is cleared separately with STOP + SPEED (−) ([lube alert card](screen-function-windows-speed-bar-and-lube-alert.md)). The TR95H's two books print nothing of this kind ([TR95H absence card](../../tr95h-2024/console/no-engineering-or-calibration-mode-is-printed.md)).
