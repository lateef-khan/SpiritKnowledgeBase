**The TR75H is the one Xterra book that asks for 24 inches, not 20.**

> After assembling your Hiker Treadmill, you'll need to make sure you've installed it in a safe area.
> We recommend that you leave an area of at least 24in on the front, and sides of your machine. The
> front of the unit is where the motor is located. We recommend that you leave an area of at least 6ft
> behind the Hiker Treadmill.

| Requirement | Figure |
|---|---|
| Front and both sides | at least **24in** |
| Behind | at least **6ft** |

- Install it where children and pets cannot access it.
- Keep the area around it clear of furniture, exercise equipment and other debris.
- Do not install it on deeply padded, plush, or shag carpet.

**The plan drawing does not fully agree with the text.** Three of its four labels read `24in`, `6ft`
and `24in`; the fourth still reads `20in`, left over from the panel the other new-layout books print
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-location-20-in-front-and-sides-6-ft-behind`). The text is the deliberate
figure; quote 24 inches and say the drawing keeps one old label.

Both figures are recommendations, not stated minimums. The TR95H, the other Hiker treadmill, is a
20-inch book.
