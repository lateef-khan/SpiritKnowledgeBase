**The manual states it in one line: "The XE350 has a heart icon only in the main screen."** The
sentence sits immediately after the description of the XE550's Heart Icon **and** Bar Graph, so the
contrast is the manual's own.

- The **heart rate monitoring system is the same**: grasp the hand pulse sensors on the stationary
  handlebars, or wear the transmitter, and the **Heart Icon blinks** after a few seconds. The
  **Message Window** shows the pulse in beats per minute.
- **There is no percentage of maximum heart rate on this console**, because the bar graph that
  carries it is not fitted.

**The console drawing agrees.** The XE350 call-outs are **Data display**, **Dot matrix**, **Message
window**, **Fan**, **Program buttons / numeric key pad**, **Control keys**, **Pulse sensors** and
**Bottle holder**. The XE550 drawing on the same page adds **Heart rate indicator**, **Heart rate bar
graph**, two **Display view** buttons and a **Fan power switch**.

**Entering your age still matters on this machine**, but for the Heart Rate control programs rather
than a bar graph.

**The XE550 and the 2007 XE300 do have the bar graph**:
