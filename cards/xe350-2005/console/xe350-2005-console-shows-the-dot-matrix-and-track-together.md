**The manual states it in one line: "The XE350 shows the dot matrix and track at the same time."**

There is nothing to switch. The XE350 has **no Display button beside the dot matrix** and **no Track
indicator light**, and the console drawing on p. 20 labels only a **Data display**, a **Dot matrix**,
a **Message window**, a **Fan**, **Program buttons / numeric key pad**, **Control keys**, **Pulse
sensors** and a **Bottle holder**.

**The XE550 is the machine that switches.** Press its Display button once for a quarter mile track
and again for a scan that alternates the two:
`spirit-xe-console-dot-matrix-switches-between-profile-and-a-quarter-mile-track`.

**This is the clearest of the three differences the 2005 manual prints between the XE350 and the
XE550.** The other two are the **Heart rate bar graph**, which only the XE550 has
(`xe350-2005-console-has-a-heart-icon-but-no-bar-graph`), and the **Auto-pilot** key, also XE550 only.

**The rest of the section covers both machines.** The manual says so directly: "Although the XE350
and XE550 look different they actually operate in a similar manner. For space consideration we will
describe the operation and note where the two differ."
