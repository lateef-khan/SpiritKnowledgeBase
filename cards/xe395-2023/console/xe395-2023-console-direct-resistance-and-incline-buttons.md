**You can set your resistance and incline level settings quickly by pressing the buttons on the
console.** Choose the desired level of resistance and incline and **the elliptical automatically
adjusts to that level**. **This saves time because you do not have to press and hold, or hold a
button down, until reaching the desired level.**

**The console drawing labels them "Direct Resistance Level & Incline Buttons"**, in the row below the
LCD window between the **Child Lock** key and the **Program Buttons**.

**The manual does not say how many buttons there are or which levels they carry.** It prints no list
and no numbering. **Do not tell a customer which button is which level** - only that the row exists
and jumps straight to a value.

**The XE795-2023 has the same feature for resistance only**, because it has no incline:
`xe795-2023-console-direct-resistance-buttons`. Its console drawing labels **Direct Resistance Level
Buttons** and its workout screen reads **Current Speed** where this machine's reads **Current
Incline**.

**Incline reaches several places on this console.** The workout-mode drawing labels **Current
Incline** twice and a **Resistance Program Profile**:
`xe395-2023-console-overview-and-workout-mode-call-outs`.
