**The machine has a child lock feature which disables the buttons on the console to prevent
unauthorized use.**

1. **To turn it on, press Child Lock.** **The LED lights up.**
2. **The elliptical is then in idle mode and cannot be operated.**
3. **To unlock, hold Child Lock for 3 seconds.**
4. **After the LED light turns off, press START to operate.**

**A lit Child Lock LED and a machine that will not start is the lock, not a fault.**

**Child Lock is a labelled key on the console face**, at the left of the row of buttons below the LCD
window, alongside the **Direct Resistance Level & Incline Buttons** and the **Program Buttons**:
`xe395-2023-console-overview-and-workout-mode-call-outs`.

**There is a second Child Lock in engineering mode.** The engineering menu's item 4 is also called
Child Lock and locks the keypad the same way:
`xe395-2023-console-engineering-mode-with-a-child-lock`. The manual does not say whether the two are
the same setting.

**The XE795-2023 has neither.** No Child Lock key, no Child Lock menu item, and no keypad lock
described anywhere in its manual - so **there is no equivalent procedure to offer that owner**.
