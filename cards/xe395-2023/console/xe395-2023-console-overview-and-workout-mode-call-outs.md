**The console face, as labelled on the overview drawing:** **LCD Window**, **Adjustable Fan**, **USB
Charging**, and along the button row **Child Lock**, **Direct Resistance Level & Incline Buttons**
and **Program Buttons**.

**The workout screen, as labelled on the workout-mode drawing:**

| Where | Call-out |
|---|---|
| Top row | **Total Calories Burned**, **Current Heart Rate**, **Cumulative Total Mileage**, **Current Resistance Level**, **Current Revolutions per minute**, **Total Laps Completed** |
| Left edge | **Current Incline** |
| Right edge | **Current Resistance Level**, **Maximum Heart Rate Percentage** |
| Left column of icons | **USB Charge Icon**, **Bluetooth Speaker**, **Bluetooth Heart Rate Icon**, **Bluetooth On/Off**, **Message Center** |
| Bottom | **Current Incline Level**, **Current Time (Elapsed or Countdown based on Program)**, **Resistance Program Profile** |

**Current Resistance Level is labelled twice** on the drawing, at the top and at the right edge, and
**Current Incline** likewise. Both are the manual's own labelling.

**There are three separate Bluetooth marks.** A **Bluetooth Speaker** icon, a **Bluetooth Heart Rate**
icon and a **Bluetooth On/Off** control - see `xt-2023-console-spirit-plus-app-pairing` and
`xt-2023-console-bluetooth-speaker` for what each connects to.

**The numbers printed inside the drawing are the manual's sample values**, not anything about a real
machine.

**The XE795-2023 drawing is the same layout with incline removed.** It labels **Current Speed** where
this one labels **Current Incline**, and its button row has no Child Lock:
`xe795-2023-console-overview-and-workout-mode-call-outs`.
