## Step one - incline rail, console mast and console, in one step

1. Slide the **INCLINE RAIL ASSEMBLY (2)** into the U channel of the **MAIN FRAME
   (1)**, taking care not to damage the wires leaving each part.
2. Connect it **horizontally** with 2 **HEX HEAD BOLTS (104)**, 2 **FLAT WASHERS
   (137)** and 2 **NYLOC NUTS (130)**. Secure it **vertically** with 4 **HEX HEAD
   BOLTS (185)**, 4 **SPLIT WASHERS (151)**, 4 **FLAT WASHERS (142)** and 4 **STAR
   WASHERS (154)**, tightened with wrenches 155 and 158.
3. Connect the **INCLINE MOTOR WIRES (46 & 47)** to the harness and the black wire
   leaving the incline rail assembly, and push the excess into the U channel.
4. Slide the **CONSOLE MAST COVER (72)** onto the **CONSOLE MAST (12)**, facing the
   correct way.
5. Feed the twist tie wire and the **COMPUTER CABLE (53)** into the bottom of the mast
   and out of the top.
6. Install the mast into the receiving bracket on top of the main frame, without
   pinching the cables. The bolt already in the bracket is tightened **last**. Tighten
   3 **SHEET METAL SCREWS (120)** on the console mast cover (72/72-1).
7. **SPLIT WASHER (152)** onto **HEX HEAD BOLT (105)**, hand tightened through the
   **left** side. A **CURVED WASHER (153)** onto each **HEX HEAD BOLT (103)**, both
   into the **front**. Front bolts as tight as possible first, then the two left side
   bolts.
8. Connect the 2 **HAND PULSE CABLES (53)**, the **RESISTANCE CABLE (55)** and the
   **INCLINE CABLE (56)** to the back of the **CONSOLE (43)**.
9. Fit the console to the mast bracket with 4 **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (116)**.
10. Insert 4 **OVAL END CAPS (93)** into the **MAIN FRAME (1)**.

## Step two - lower swing arms, connecting arms and the tablet holder

1. Two **WAVE WASHERS (196)** onto each side of the swing arm axle, then the **LOWER
   SWING ARMS (10 LEFT, 11 RIGHT)**, secured with the 2 **BOLTS (184)** and **FLAT
   WASHERS (141)**. They were fitted at the factory; jiggle, do not force.
2. Remove the tie holding the spacer in the rod end of the **RIGHT CONNECTING ARM
   (9)**, line it up with the bracket at the bottom of the **LOWER RIGHT SWING ARM
   (11)**, slide the **BOLT (186)** through bracket, rod end and spacer, add the
   **FLAT WASHER (142)** and **NYLOC NUT (192)** and tighten hard. Repeat on the left.
3. Place the **Tablet Holder (200)** on the console bottom cover and tighten **3 M5 x
   12mm Phillips Head Screws (200-16)**.

## Step three - upper swing arms

1. **RUBBER SLEEVE (162)** onto the **LEFT (16)** and **RIGHT (17)** upper swing arms,
   wide part at the bottom.
2. Attach the **WIRE (55)** from the right upper swing arm to the **WIRE (57)** leaving
   the console mast tube, and slide the **SWITCH WIRE CAP (161)** on, wide side facing
   the swing arm.
3. Insert the upper swing arm into the lower one and fasten with **3 HEX HEAD BOLTS
   (159)**, **2 CURVED WASHERS (160)** and **3 NYLOC NUTS (127)**.
4. Repeat on the left side.

## Step four - covers, and the incline goes up

1. Two **WHEEL COVERS (79 & 80)** above the rollers with 4 **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS
   (115)**.
2. **Plug the power cord into the machine and a grounded wall outlet, switch on, press
   start and elevate the incline.** Fit the **COVER (85)** to the mid-stabilizer tube
   with 2 screws (115).
3. **LEFT HANDLE BAR COVERS (81 & 82)** and **RIGHT HANDLE BAR COVERS (83 & 84)** over
   the axle connections, **4 SHEET METAL SCREWS (119) on each side**.
4. A **STEEL BRACKET (180)** on the left and right of the rear stabilizer tube, single
   hole facing the rear, 4 **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (122)**.
5. The front **REAR STABILIZER COVER (87)** with 2 screws (115), then the back **REAR
   STABILIZER COVER (88)** with 4 screws (115).
6. Check the four floor levellers - see
   [the levelling card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xe-assembly-levelling-top-nut.md).
7. **Return the elevation to flat with the Incline down key.**

## The incline level is missing from step four

The sentence reads "Press the start button and elevate the incline to level Attach
the COVER (85) to the mid-stabilizer tube". **The number has dropped out of the
printed sentence.** The **XE395ENT 2021** manual, which is otherwise the closest book
to this one, prints the same job as "elevate the incline to level 8", and the
**XE395 2010** manual says level 8 too. Both name 8; this book names nothing.

## What is new since the XE395ENT

- **Step one is one step.** The ENT split the same jobs across a STEP ONE-A and a
  STEP ONE-B facing page, with two panels both headed HARDWARE FOR STEP 1.
- **A tablet holder**, item 200, fitted in step two with three screws of item 200-16.
  It is on the carton list as *1 Tablet Rack Holder*.
- **Items 142 and 151 are the right way round.** The ENT's step-one sentence swapped
  them; this book prints "4 SPLIT WASHERS (151), 4 FLAT WASHERS (142)", which is what
  both hardware panels say.
- **One switch wire cap in the text, two in the bag.** The ENT wired both upper swing
  arms and named two caps; this book names one and its panel supplies two.
