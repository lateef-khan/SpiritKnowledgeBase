| Item | Tool | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 155 | 13/14mm Wrench | 1 |
| 157 | Phillips Head Screwdriver | 1 |
| 158 | 12/14mm Wrench | 1 |

**Three tools, and that is the whole panel.** Both wrenches are double ended, so the
carton covers 12, 13 and 14mm.

## One tool fewer than the machine it replaces

The XE395ENT 2021 carton held four - these three plus a **combination M5 Allen wrench
and Phillips screwdriver**. The 2023 build drops it, and nothing in the 2023 steps
asks for an Allen key of any size. See
[the 2021 tools card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xe-assembly-tools-2021.md).

## The levelling job names a wrench number that is not a wrench

Step four's last job says to use "the WRENCH (109)" on the leveller lock nut. **There
is no item 109 in this tools panel**, and the XE395ENT parts list gives item 109 as a
*5/16" x UNC18 x 15L Flat Head Socket Bolt*. Use the 13/14mm wrench, item 155. See
[the levelling card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xe-assembly-levelling-top-nut.md).

## The XE795 2023 carton is not this carton

The XE795 2023 holds **five** tools, adding an 8mm L Allen wrench and a second L
Allen wrench, because its swing arms and connection brackets take socket head bolts.
Nothing on this machine does.
