did. The 2023 hardware is a spread of captioned pictures, one group per step, and the
captions below are what those pictures say.

## Step two is two wave washers short of its own job

Its job 1 says "Slide **2** WAVE WASHERS (196) onto **each side** of the Swing Arm
Axle" - four washers. **The panel supplies two.** The XE395ENT 2021, with the same
sentence, supplies four. Expect to be short by two, or to be fitting one per side.

## One switch wire cap in the text and two in the bag

Step three job 2 fits **the SWITCH WIRE CAP (161)** on the right upper swing arm and
job 4 says to repeat on the left. **The bag holds two**, which matches two sides. The
XE295 2021 has the reverse defect - two sides named, one cap supplied.

## Item 200-16 is the tablet holder screw

Three **M5 x 12L Phillips head screws**, and the only place in this book the tablet
holder's own item number, **200**, appears. Neither number exists on any 2021 XE.

## The split and flat washers are the right way round here

The step-one sentence says "4 SPLIT WASHERS (151), 4 FLAT WASHERS (142)" and the panel
agrees - 151 is the split washer and 142 the flat washer. **The XE395ENT 2021 printed
those two numbers the wrong way round in the same sentence.**

## What can be checked against the parts list

This manual carries a numbered parts list at pp. 39-42, which is ordinary text and is
trustworthy. Its exploded view at p. 38 is line art; **do not read a callout off it by
machine.**
