1. Fit the **Lower Handle Bar (L) (13) and Lower Handle Bar (R) (14)** on the **Upright Bar Axle (24)** with **two Button Head Socket Bolts (103)** through **two Flat Washers (130)** using the **Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (144)**.
2. **Secure the Connecting Arm (L) (4) on the Pedal Arm (L)** with **one Button Head Socket Bolt (103)** through a **Flat Washer (136)** using the Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (144). Do the same for the **Connecting Arm (R) (5)**.
3. **Remove the Rod End Shaft and Screw** on the Connecting Arms (L/R) (4/5).
4. **Connect the Lower Handle Bars (L/R) (13/14) with the Connecting Arms (L/R) (4/5) by the Rod End Shaft (25)**, then secure the screw from the Connecting Arms (L/R) (4/5) using **Wrench (141)**.
