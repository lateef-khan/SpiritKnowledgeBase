This book has no pre-assembly or unpacking section; assembly starts here.

1. **Pull the tie on the Console Mast (2)** to run the **Computer Cable (48)** through the mast from the bottom and out of the top. **Secure the Console Mast on the mounting plate on the Main Frame** with **four Socket Head Cap Bolts (107)** through **four Split Washers (135)** and **four Flat Washers (129)** using the **8mm L Allen Wrench (143)**.
2. **Attach the Front Stabilizer (17) to the Main Frame (1)** with the transportation wheels forward, then secure **two Carriage Bolts (139)** through **two Curved Washers (140)** and **two Cap Nuts (138)** with **Wrench (141)**, from the bottom to the top through the Front Stabilizer (17).
3. **Drink Bottle Holder (90)** on the console mast with **two Phillips Head Screws (110)** using driver (142).
4. **Plug the four Oval End Caps (65)** into the stabilizer tubes.
