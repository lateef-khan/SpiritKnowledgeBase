1. **Pull two cables of the Handpulse W/Cable Assembly (54)** from the **Handpulse Assembly (L) (8)** and **Handpulse Assembly (R) (9)** through the round hole from the Console Mast, with **four Button Head Socket Bolts (103)** using the **Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (144)**.
2. **Plug all of the connectors into the back of the Console Assembly (46): Computer Cable (48)** and the **two cables of the Handpulse W/Cable Assembly (54)**. Secure the Console Assembly (46) on the console mounting plate with **four Phillips Head Screws (110)** using driver (142).
