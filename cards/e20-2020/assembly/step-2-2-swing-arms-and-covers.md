1. **Install the Swing Arm (L) (15) on the Lower Handle Bar (L)**, then secure **three Button Head Socket Bolts (103)** through **three Curved Washers (137)** using the Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (144). Do the same for the **Swing Arm (R) (16)**.
2. **Install the Console Mast Cover (L) (87) on the Chain Cover (L)** and the **Console Mast Cover (R) (88) on the Chain Cover (R)**, combine them, and secure with **three Sheet Metal Screws (114)** using driver (142).
3. **Install the Slide Wheel Cover (L) (62) and Slide Wheel Cover (R) (63)** on the Pedal Arms (L/R), and secure with **four Phillips Head Screws (110)** using driver (142).
