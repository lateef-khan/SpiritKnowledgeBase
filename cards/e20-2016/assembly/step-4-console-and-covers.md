Tighten the hardware in this step with the **M5 Allen Wrench/Phillips Head Screw Driver (108)**. The book numbers its sub-steps 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 (no step 2 is printed).

1. **Locate the Console Assembly (31) and the four Phillips Head Screws (78).** Two Hand pulse wires (37) and one Computer Cable (32) sit at the top opening of the Console Mast (10). Connect them to the mating keyed connectors on the back of the Console Assembly (31); do not force them.
3. **Store the excess wire back into the Console Mast (10)**, install the Console Assembly (31) onto the mounting plate of the Console Mast (10), and secure with the **four Phillips Head Screws (78)**.
4. **Two Slide Wheel Covers (52)** with **four Phillips Head Screws (79)** using driver (110).
5. **Rear Stabilizer Cover (L) (61)** on the Pedal Arm (L) and Rail Tube with **one Phillips Head Screw (79)** using driver (110); repeat for **Rear Stabilizer Cover (R) (62)**.
6. **Middle Stabilizer Cover (59)** on the Pedal Arm (L/R) and Rail Tube with **two Phillips Head Screws (79)** using driver (110).
7. **Drink Bottle Holder** on the Console Mast tube with **two M5 x 12m/m Phillips Head Screws (68-3)**.
