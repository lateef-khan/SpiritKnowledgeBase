This book has no pre-assembly or unpacking section; assembly starts here.

1. **Slide the Console Mast Cover (41) onto the Console Mast (10)**, facing the correct way.
2. **Unravel the Computer Cable (32)** at the top opening of the Main Frame (1) and feed it into the bottom of the Console Mast tube (10) and out of the top opening.
3. **Install the Console Mast (10) into the receiving bracket** in the top of the Main Frame (1). **Put two Split Washers (120) and two Curved Washers (149) onto one Hex Head Bolt (119) and one Hex Head Bolt (121)**, then install the screw at the front of the Console Mast. **Put two Flat Washers (94) and two Split Washers (120) onto the two Hex Head Bolts (119)**, install, and tighten with the **12/14m/m Wrench (111)**.
4. **Do not damage or pinch the Computer Cable (32)** running through the Console Mast Tube (10) during this procedure.
5. **Install the Front Stabilizer (15)** on the front stabilizer holding plate at the bottom of the main frame with the transportation wheels facing forward, and secure with **two Carriage Bolts (148)**, **two Curved Washers (149)** and **two Cap Nuts (150)** using the **12/14m/m Wrench (111)**.
