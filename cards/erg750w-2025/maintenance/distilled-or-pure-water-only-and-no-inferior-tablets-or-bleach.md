**This is the ERG750W rule: distilled or pure water. The ERG550W, ERG600W and ERG650W books say "tap water
only"** (`xterra-ergw-maintenance-tap-water-only-never-pool-chlorine-or-bleach`) **and the ERG800W says
"clean and filtered", distilled only where the supply is poor.** Three books, three answers; quote the one for
the machine in front of you.

The NOTE under "How to Fill and Empty the Tank", as printed:

- **"Only distilled water or pure water can be injected into the water tank. Also, can add the water
  purification tablets. Do not use inferior water purification tablets or bleach. They will damage the water
  tank and are exempt from the warranty."**
- "If you are using purification tablets, It is necessary to replace it every six months. If the water quality
  is very cloudy, please replace the water in time." - the interval card is
  `xterra-ergw-maintenance-water-purification-tablet-every-six-months`.
- "The water replaced in the water tank is not suitable for re-consumption, please press the sewage treatment."
  - that is, pour the old water down the drain; it is not for drinking.

The tablet is **optional** in this wording ("can add"); what is forbidden is a cheap tablet or bleach, and the
book says the tank damage that follows is not a warranty matter. No brand, dose or part number for the tablet
is printed anywhere in the ERG750W book.

Step 4 of the same page adds the one cleaning instruction this book has: after plugging the tank, **"dry the
water on and around the table and adjustment knob with a dry cloth."** The water level and the litre figures
are `erg750w-2025-specs-water-tank-capacity-8-to-12-7-liters-min-standard-max`.
