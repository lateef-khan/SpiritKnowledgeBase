"PULSE: **Use Bluetooth to connect with the heart rate monitor and detect a heartbeat. The heartbeat scan will turn off after 90 seconds if a Bluetooth heartbeat is not detected. Press any button to wake up the Bluetooth heartbeat.** Heart monitor accessories are sold separately."

So put the strap on and wake the scan with a key press if the console has sat idle for a minute and a half. There are no hand-pulse grips, and the book prints no wearing page, no strap model and no analogue (5.3 kHz) option - the only Xterra rower book that names a Bluetooth strap alone; the ERG800W takes either kind ([ERG800W card](../../erg800w-2023/console/heart-rate-from-a-5-3-khz-or-bluetooth-chest-belt-not-included.md)). The service manual's table gives one cause for "No heart rate": "bad detection from wireless transmitter - exchange the battery of the wireless transmitter and test again" (an errors card). The resistance level takes turns with the pulse in the same window, 6 seconds each ([readouts card](readouts-distance-in-100-m-steps-strokes-in-25-and-the-level-shown-in-the-pulse-window.md)); the Bluetooth symbol beside the calories window is about apps, not the strap ([symbol card](bluetooth-symbol-flashing-means-an-app-can-connect-and-gone-means-power-saving.md)).
