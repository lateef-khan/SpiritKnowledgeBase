**What the books do say.** The LCD carries a **battery symbol** beside the SPM window ([key card](mode-reset-up-down-keys-and-the-six-window-lcd.md)). The owner's manual's troubleshooting table opens "Monitor does not display - Batteries not Installed - Install Batteries". The service manual's console table, headed "TZ-8145-2AA Trouble Shooting", answers "No action (no sound, no display)" with "check whether the specification of the battery is right or there are enough power in the battery or there are batteries on the computer - remove the batteries and place new & correct ones" and "check connection wires between battery case and PA Board", and "LCD display lightly" with "check if the batteries are in correct position - reset the batteries or place new ones".

**What neither book says:** the cell size, how many, whether they are in the box, or where the case is - the owner's manual has no battery page, no assembly step that loads cells, and its parts list shows no adapter or power cord; the "2AA" at the end of the service table's title reads like a cell count, but it is part of a model code and neither book says so. Until a book or the console itself is checked, the answer is "battery-powered; type unconfirmed". The older Xterra water rowers take two AA, included ([ERG550W family card](../../shared/console/xterra-ergw-console-two-aa-batteries-included-and-changing-them-resets-every-value.md)); the Spirit water rowers take four AA ([Spirit card](../../shared/console/spirit-water-rower-console-four-aa-batteries.md)); do not carry either across. The console mount comes off with the rotating-shaft covers and the console-stand screws (an assembly card).
