"Your new XTERRA Fitness unit has Bluetooth connectivity ... Follow the instructions on page 19 to learn more." "Downloading the **XF Connect** app will help unlock more features - such as tracking workouts and sharing data via **Google Fit and Apple Fitness**. Simply search for 'XF Connect' in the app store on your smartphone or tablet, or scan the QR code on the right." The panel adds: "The XF Connect app is available on Google Play and the Apple App Store", with two QR codes and the two store badges.

**The page the book points to (19) is the Kinomap / FTMS page** ([Kinomap card](../../shared/console/xterra-rower-console-kinomap-pairing-and-the-console-goes-blank-while-an-app-is-connected.md)); there is no "Using the XF Connect app" page, no minimum iOS or Android version and no step that names the rower in the app. The book's text layer still carries the older "XTERRA+ App offers a new exercise tracking experience" artwork under the panel, but the printed page says XF Connect throughout - this is the only Xterra rower book that names that app, where the ERG180 and ERG800W print the XTERRA+ page with iOS 13.1 / Android 8.0 ([XTERRA+ card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-xterra-plus-app-ios-13-android-8.md)). The Bluetooth symbol that says the console is ready for an app is on [the symbol card](bluetooth-symbol-flashing-means-an-app-can-connect-and-gone-means-power-saving.md).
