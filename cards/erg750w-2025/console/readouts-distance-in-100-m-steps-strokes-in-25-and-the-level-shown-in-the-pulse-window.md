| Reading | As printed |
|---|---|
| **TIME** | Set a target time with UP and/or DOWN, **1 min ~ 99 min in 1-minute increments** |
| **TIME / 500M** | "Your average time to reach 500 meters is automatically displayed" |
| **DISTANCE** | Preset a target, **0 ~ 9999 meters, 100-metre increments** |
| **STROKES** | Set a target, **0 ~ 9999 strokes, 25-stroke increments** |
| **WATT** | "Displays WATT value; **alternates between STROKE & WATT values**" - the two share one window |
| **CALORIES** | Set a target, **0 ~ 999 Cal, 20-calorie increments** |
| **PULSE** | From a Bluetooth heart-rate monitor ([pulse card](pulse-from-a-bluetooth-heart-rate-monitor-that-stops-scanning-after-90-seconds.md)) |
| **SPM** | Strokes per minute |

**RESISTANCE:** "Adjust the resistance to the desired level by adjusting the knob found on the water tank. **The PULSE section of the console will display the resistance level (L1-L6) for 6 seconds, then the pulse will display for 6 seconds.**" So a reading such as L3 in the pulse window is the tank level, not a heart rate, and the console is told the level by the knob rather than by a prompt at power-up. "If you stop during the exercise, resistance will be paused for 4 seconds, and SPM will go to 0."

The setting ranges differ from the older water rowers (distance to 99900 m in 100s, strokes in 10s, calories 10 to 9990 in 10s - [the ERG550W family card](../../shared/console/xterra-ergw-console-readouts-time-500m-distance-to-99900-m-strokes-and-watts.md)); how a count-down and the INTERVAL program run is a programs question.
