Three states, as printed on the programs page:

1. "**The Bluetooth symbol flashing on the display indicates that the APP can be connected.**"
2. "The Bluetooth symbol flicker on the console indicates that the APP can be connected, **and the LCD turns off to enter the power saving**" - once an app is driving the workout the console screen goes dark, as the Kinomap page also says ([Kinomap card](../../shared/console/xterra-rower-console-kinomap-pairing-and-the-console-goes-blank-while-an-app-is-connected.md)).
3. "**The disappearance of the Bluetooth symbol represents the Bluetooth entering the energy saving mode, which can be awakened by pressing any button.**"

So an app that cannot see the rower after a rest needs a key press on the console first. The symbol sits beside the CALORIES window ([key card](mode-reset-up-down-keys-and-the-six-window-lcd.md)). The book gives no time for either sleep; the heart-rate scan has its own 90-second timer ([pulse card](pulse-from-a-bluetooth-heart-rate-monitor-that-stops-scanning-after-90-seconds.md)). The app the book names is XF Connect ([XF Connect card](xf-connect-app-named-with-a-qr-code-but-no-pairing-steps.md)).
