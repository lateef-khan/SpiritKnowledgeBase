**The screen** (Console Screen - Overview): a dark LCD in two rows. Top row: **DIST. / INTERVAL** (four digits), **TIME/500M / REST** (mm:ss) and **CALORIES** (three digits) with a **Bluetooth symbol** beside it. Bottom row: **SPM** (three digits) with a **battery symbol** beside it, **STROKES / WATT** (four digits) and **PULSE** (three digits) with a **heart symbol**. A tablet rack sits above the console (Parts of Your Rower). The page prints no picture of the keys.

| Key | As printed |
|---|---|
| **MODE** | "When the unit is not in use, press the MODE button to enter the settings (distance, time, calories, strokes). **Hold the MODE button for 3 seconds to enter the INTERVAL setting.**" |
| **RESET** | "Press the RESET key to zero the set point. **Hold the RESET key for 2 seconds to restart the console.**" |
| **UP** | "Add values to the distance, time, calories, and strokes settings by pressing the UP arrow." |
| **DOWN** | "Reduce the values of the distance, time, calories, and strokes settings by pressing the DOWN arrow." |

There is no START, ENTER or RECOVERY key on this console. What the windows read and how resistance shows is on [the readouts card](readouts-distance-in-100-m-steps-strokes-in-25-and-the-level-shown-in-the-pulse-window.md); the Bluetooth symbol's states on [the symbol card](bluetooth-symbol-flashing-means-an-app-can-connect-and-gone-means-power-saving.md); the pulse source on [the pulse card](pulse-from-a-bluetooth-heart-rate-monitor-that-stops-scanning-after-90-seconds.md); the count-down settings and the INTERVAL program are programs cards. The battery symbol is the only place either book admits the console runs on cells ([battery card](battery-powered-but-neither-book-prints-the-cell-type-or-where-it-goes.md)).
