1. Pull out the tank plug from the upper tank.
2. **Fill** as in Figure A: insert the water funnel into the tank and fill from a cup or bucket. Measure the water in the tank against the **digital mark on the side of the tank**.
3. **Pump out** as in Figure B: put a bucket next to the rower and pump the water from the tank into the bucket (the funnel and pump are item 132 in the box).
4. Plug the tank and dry the water on and around the tank and the adjustment knob with a dry cloth.

**Water level.** "Do not exceed the water label. Water added beyond the maximum water level is exempt from the warranty." The gauge marks are **Min, Standard and Max** - not the level 1 to 6 scale of the ERG550W / 600W / 650W - and the litres at each mark are a specs card.

**Notes.** "Only **distilled water or pure water** can be injected into the water tank" - this book, unlike the level-6 rowers' tap-water rule. Water purification tablets may be added; "do not use inferior water purification tablets or bleach", which damage the tank and void the warranty. Tablets are replaced every six months and cloudy water changed - the maintenance section's card. Tank water is not for drinking.

Resistance on this rower is set by the **knob on the water tank**, which the console reads as L1-L6 - the console section's card.
