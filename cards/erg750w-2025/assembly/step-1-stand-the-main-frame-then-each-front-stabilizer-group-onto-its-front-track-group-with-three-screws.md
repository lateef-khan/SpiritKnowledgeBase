**NOTE: "We recommend having 2 people to assemble the product. Please be careful when assembling. The Main Frame is easy to topple."**

1. Stand the **Main Frame Group (1)** as shown.
2. Place the **Left-Front Stabilizer Group (20)** at the screw holes of the **Left-Front Track Group (5)** and fix it with **3 Flat-Head Inner Hexagon Screws (124, M8 x 15)** using the Hexagon Wrench (129). In the same way lock the **Right-Front Stabilizer Group (21)** to the **Right-Front Track Group (4)** with 3 more screws.

Six screws for the step. The front track groups (4, 5) are part of the main frame group; the stabilizer halves bolt to their outer faces.
