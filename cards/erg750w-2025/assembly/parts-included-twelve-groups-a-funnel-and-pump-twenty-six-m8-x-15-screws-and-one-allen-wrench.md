**Parts included:** Main Frame Group (1), Right Rear Track Group (2), Left Rear Track Group (3), Console Group (6), Rear Connection Group (15), Left Front Stabilizer Group (20), Right Front Stabilizer Group (21), Seat Cushion Group (24), Left Rotating Shaft Trim Cover (32), Right Rotating Shaft Trim Cover (33), Funnel + Pump (132).

**Hardware:** Screws (124, **M8 x 15**) x **26 pcs** and one Allen Wrench (129, 5 x 5).

One screw size for the whole build, spent 6 + 8 + 0 + 8 + 4 across steps 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 - twenty-six exactly. Step 3 reuses screws that arrive fitted to the rail's rubber pad and end plugs.

"Some of the parts for this machine are preassembled on the unit. Please read your manual to ensure everything is there."
