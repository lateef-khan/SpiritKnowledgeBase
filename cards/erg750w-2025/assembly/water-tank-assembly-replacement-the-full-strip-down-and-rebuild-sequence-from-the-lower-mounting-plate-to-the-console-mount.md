This book prints no screw sizes; it is a photo sequence with one line per picture. "Some figure may be different from the machine."

**Strip-down**
1. Stand the machine upright and remove the **lower water tank mounting plate**.
2. Console mount: remove the left and right rotating shaft decorative covers, a hex wrench on the console stand's fixing screws, then disconnect the console terminals "with caution to prevent wire harness damage".
3. Main frame upper cover: the hex socket and Phillips screws out, the foot pads off the cover, then thread the handlebar and console terminal wires out of the cover.
4. Handlebar: remove the aluminum plugs and foam, then take out the webbing.
5. Main frame assembly: remove its fixing screws (front connecting plate, water tank mounting plate) and lift the assembly off.
6. Upper water tank mounting plate assembly: remove the fixing plate connection assembly's screws and the assembly; a hex wrench on the shaft's fixing screws, then take off the **bearing and belt pulley assembly**; remove the screws between the upper mounting plate assembly and the tank, and take the plate off.
7. **Remove the water tank assembly - "Please see the referencing video."** The book prints no steps for the swap itself.

**Rebuild**
8. Place the upper tank mounting plate on the new tank and tighten its screws. Put a flat washer and the bearing on top of the belt pulley and tighten the screws. **Pre-tension the belt pulley two turns, then pull out the webbing.**
9. Fit the mounting plate connection assembly into the upper tank mounting assembly and retighten. Place the main frame assembly over the tank; tighten the tank screws and the screws to the front connecting plate. **Thread the VR wire into the main frame assembly and make sure the webbing passes through the webbing guide wheel inside it.**
10. Thread the webbing through the upper cover, seat the cover, reattach the webbing to the handlebar, fit the foam and tighten the aluminum plug screws.
11. Route the VR cable out of the upper cover and tighten the cover screws. Connect the console stand terminals to the VR cable and refasten the stand.
12. Refasten the lower water tank mounting plate.

The pedal, front connecting plate and front stabilizer pages sit inside this sequence in the book (pp. 10-12) and are separate cards; the "VR" is the console's resistance sensor wire the book names only by that abbreviation.
