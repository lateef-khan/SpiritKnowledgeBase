1. Align the **Left-Rear Track Group (3)** with the **Left-Front Track Group (5)**, insert the combination, align the screw holes and tighten with **4 Screws (124, M8 x 15)** using the Inner Hexagon Wrench (129).
2. The **Right-Rear Track Group (2)** and the **Right-Front Track Group (4)** are installed in the same way.

Eight screws for the step. The rails are two-piece on each side; this joins the halves while the frame is still standing on its front.
