1. Remove the stop rubber pads and the rail end caps from the rail (the seat rubber pad 40 and end plugs 41, 42 of assembly step 3).
2. Remove the rail aluminum strip and **clean the rail thoroughly. Apply 3M adhesive tape to the new aluminum strip**, then install it into the rail.
3. Refasten the rail stop rubber pads and end caps.

The strip is glued, not screwed - the only rower in this family whose book says so. The book does not name the tape grade.
