With the seat assembly out of the rail (the rail card):

**Off**
1. Remove the screws connecting the seat and the seat mounting plate.
2. Remove the screws connecting the seat axle and the seat mounting plate.
3. Remove the seat rollers from the seat axle and replace them with new ones.
4. Remove the auxiliary wheels and the components as shown in the diagram.

**On**
5. Reinstall the seat auxiliary wheels.
6. Insert the seat axle into the seat rollers, then fasten it to the seat mounting plate.
7. Place the seat under the seat mounting plate and refasten it.

No sizes printed. The auxiliary wheels are the small side wheels that keep the carriage centred on the two rails; the rollers are the load wheels on the axle.
