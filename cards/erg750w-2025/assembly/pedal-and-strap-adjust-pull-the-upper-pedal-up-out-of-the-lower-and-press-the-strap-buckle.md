Both the foot strap and the foot pedal adjust to the user's foot size.

**Pedal:** pull from the upper part of the foot pedal to separate it from the lower part. Adjust it up or down to fit your foot, then reinsert it into the lower part to secure.

**Strap:** press the buckle on the strap hook and pull it to the right. After pressing the buckle, pull the strap to the left to loosen it. Once the size is right, fasten the buckle.

This is a buckle strap, not the Velcro strap of the other ERG rowers, and the two-part pedal has no numbered positions - the ERG800W's has seven (linked cards).
