1. Line the **Rear Connection Group (15)** up with the screw holes of the Left and Right Rear Track Groups (3, 2) in the direction of the arrow. Attach it to the **Right-Rear Track Group (2)** with **4 Screws (124, M8 x 15)** and the Inner Hexagonal Wrench (129).
2. Fasten the rear connection group to the **Left-Rear Track Group (3)** the same way.

Eight screws for the step. The rear connection group is the rear stabilizer; its adjustment rubber pads (43) are the rower's levelers.
