1. Remove the four screws from the pedal assembly and take the assembly off.
2. Remove the screws fixing the pedal and the pedal strap; the pedal and strap can then be replaced with new ones.
3. Fasten the pedal assembly back in its original position.

No sizes printed. The two-part pedal and its buckle strap are the owner's manual's adjustment page (linked).
