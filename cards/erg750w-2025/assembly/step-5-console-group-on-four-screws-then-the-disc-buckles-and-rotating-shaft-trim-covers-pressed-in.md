1. Align the screw holes on the **Console Group (6)** with those on the **Main Frame Cover Top (19)** and lock it with **4 Screws (124, M8 x 15)** and the Internal Hexagonal Wrench (129).
2. Place the **Disc Buckle (74)** in the **Left and Right Rotating Shaft Trim Covers (32, 33)** at the corresponding hole on the main frame cover top and press in the direction of the arrow. Tighten and fix, as in small figure (1); finished, it looks like small figure (2).

The last four of the twenty-six screws. The book does not say to connect a wire here; the service manual's console-mount procedure is where the console terminals unplug (linked card). Filling the tank is the next page.
