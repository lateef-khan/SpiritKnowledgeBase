1. Place the rower flat for this step.
2. With the Hexagonal Wrench (129), remove the **Seat Rubber Pad (40)** and the **Track End Plug Left (41)** and **Right (42)**, insert the **Seat Cushion Group (24)** into the track group in the direction of the arrow, and **re-install the screws** with the wrench.

No screws from the bag: the pad and the plugs come off on their own fitted screws (the exploded view labels them 125) and go back on the same way. The pad and plugs are the seat's end stops - the rail has no seat without them.
