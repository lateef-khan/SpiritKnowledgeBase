1. Remove the screws connecting the front connecting plate and the front bracket assembly.
2. Remove the two Phillips screws securing the front connecting plate and the decorative cover, then remove the cover.
3. Reinstall in reverse.

The front connecting plate is one of the two plates the main frame assembly bolts to (the other is the water tank mounting plate); the tank sequence takes it off on the way down.
