**Off:** remove the handlebar aluminum plugs and foam, then take out the webbing.

**On:** thread the webbing through the main frame upper cover and seat the cover on the main frame assembly; reattach the webbing to the handlebar, install the handlebar foam, then tighten the screws for the handlebar aluminum plugs.

The webbing end anchors under the plug at each end of the bar; the plugs are screwed, not pressed. The book prints no screw size.
