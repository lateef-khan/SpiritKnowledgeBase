**Off**
1. Remove the left and right rotating shaft decorative covers - the trim covers pressed in at assembly step 5.
2. Use a hex wrench to remove the fixing screws of the console stand - the four M8 x 15 screws of step 5.
3. Disconnect the console terminals. "Exercise caution during this procedure to prevent wire harness damage."

**On:** connect the terminals of the console stand assembly to the VR cable, then refasten the console stand assembly.

The console-VR troubleshooting page (the errors section's card) checks these same terminals first when the console misbehaves.
