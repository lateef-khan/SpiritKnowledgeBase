**Off**
1. Remove the screws at the connection between the rail and the main unit (the four a side of assembly step 2).
2. Disconnect the rail from the main unit.
3. Remove the rear connecting plate (the four screws a side of step 4), separate the rails, and take out the seat assembly.

**On:** place the seat onto both sides of the rail, refasten the rear connecting plate, connect the rail assembly to the main unit rail and tighten the screws.

The seat leaves the rail sideways once the two rail halves are apart - there is no end stop to remove.
