1. Remove the front stabilizer tube from the front bracket assembly - the three M8 x 15 screws a side of assembly step 1.
2. Use an Allen wrench and an external hex wrench to remove the transport wheel.
3. Reinstall in reverse.
4. "Install the water tank assembly back into place" - this page sits inside the tank strip-down sequence, so the book closes it by putting the tank back.
