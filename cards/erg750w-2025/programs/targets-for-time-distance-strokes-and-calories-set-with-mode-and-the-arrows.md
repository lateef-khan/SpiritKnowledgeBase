When the unit is not in use, press the **MODE** button to enter the settings - distance, time,
calories, strokes - and use **UP** to add and **DOWN** to reduce the value. **RESET** zeroes the set
point; holding RESET 2 seconds restarts the console.

| Setting | Range | Increment |
|---|---|---|
| TIME | **1 min ~ 99 min** | **1 minute** |
| DISTANCE | **0 ~ 9999 meters** | **100 meters** |
| STROKES | **0 ~ 9999 strokes** | **25 strokes** |
| CALORIES | **0 ~ 999 Cal** | **20 calories** |

That is all the console pages say about targets - no default, and nothing about what the console
does when a target is reached. TIME/500M, WATT (alternating with strokes), SPM and PULSE are readouts.
Holding MODE for 3 seconds enters the INTERVAL program instead - see `see_also`.

**The ERG800W's targets are narrower and have defaults** (20:00, 1000 m, 150 cal, 300 strokes), and
the ERG550W/ERG600W count to 99900 meters; each console is its own card.
