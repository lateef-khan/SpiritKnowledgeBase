**"VR" is the book's name for the variable resistor cabled to the console, and the book never expands it or says what it reports.** What the owner's manual does say is that resistance on this rower is set by hand at a knob on the water tank and that the console *displays* the level, L1 to L6, in the PULSE window for six seconds at a time. Read together, a console that shows the wrong level, or none, while the knob turns is the fault this page is for - a VR-side connection, not the resistance itself. That pairing is this card's reading; the service manual prints the three checks under the heading and nothing else.

The service manual's page, headed *Common Problems of Console VR*, lists three checks and shows each connector in a photo:

1. Check if the connection between the **console and VR cable terminals** is secure and normal.
2. Check if the connection between the **VR terminal and coiled cord terminal** is secure and normal.
3. Check if the **upper terminals of the console** are loose or detached.

That is all the page prints: no resistance figure, no calibration and no part to replace. The VR cable is routed through the main frame and out of its upper cover to the console stand, as the manual's assembly pages show, so a loose plug at either end is what the three checks are looking for.

**The page's Model row reads `R48`, not ERG-750W** - the same OEM numbering that names the ERG800W's service manual `R80`. It sits in the ERG-750W book between that book's own pages and is treated as that book's.

The Spirit CRW900 water rower answers trouble with its resistance by a six-step VR setting routine (`crw900-2021-errors-vr-setting-for-trouble-with-resistance`); nothing of the kind is printed here.
