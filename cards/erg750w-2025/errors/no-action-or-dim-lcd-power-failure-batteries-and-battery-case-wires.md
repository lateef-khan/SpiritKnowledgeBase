**This is the service manual's version of the dark console, and it adds one thing the owner's manual's row does not: the wires from the battery case to the PA board.** The owner's manual's row is batteries, then computer wires (`xterra-rower-errors-monitor-does-not-display-install-batteries-then-computer-wires`).

Two rows of the console troubleshooting table (the console is named by its part number, TZ-8145-2AA) share one cause, *Power failure*:

| Problem | Things to check | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| No action (no sound, no display) | 1. Check whether the specification of the battery is right, whether there is enough power in the battery, and whether there are batteries in the computer | Remove the batteries and place new and correct ones |
| | 2. Check connection wires between battery case and PA board | Repair the connection wire or change the new battery case |
| LCD display lightly (faint LCD) | Check if the batteries are in the correct position | Reset the batteries or place new ones |

So a console that gives neither a beep nor a display is fresh, correct cells first and the battery-case lead second; a console that lights faintly is cells the wrong way round or nearly flat. The book names the PA board and the battery case as the two ends of that lead and no other part; it prints no voltage.

The same table's other two rows are the strap (`erg750w-2025-errors-no-heart-rate-exchange-the-wireless-transmitter-battery`) and the speed sensor (`erg750w-2025-errors-no-speed-sensor-connection-gap-under-6-mm-cables-magnet`).
