**The book never says.** The owner's manual mentions batteries once, in the troubleshooting table - "Monitor does
not display: Batteries not Installed - Install Batteries" - and prints no type, count, spec block or installation
step. The console pages describe the buttons, functions and Bluetooth heart-rate scan, nothing about power.

The service manual's console troubleshooting table is headed **TZ-8145-2AA** and tells a technician to "check
whether the specification of the battery is right or there are enough power in the battery" and to check the
"connection wires between battery case and PA Board", again without naming the cell. The "2AA" in the console's
designation is the only hint that it takes two AA cells; it is a part code, not a statement, so treat the count as
unconfirmed until the cells are seen.

The other Xterra water rowers that print a type all take two AA cells
(`xterra-rower-specs-console-runs-on-two-aa-batteries`). The Xterra FB150 and FB350 bike books have the same gap
(`xterra-fb-specs-console-battery-type-not-printed`).
