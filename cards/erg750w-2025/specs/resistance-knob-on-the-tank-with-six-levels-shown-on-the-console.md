**A knob on the tank, six levels on the screen.** "RESISTANCE: Adjust the resistance to the desired level by
adjusting the knob found on the water tank. The PULSE section of the console will display the resistance level
(L1-L6) for 6 seconds, then the pulse will display for 6 seconds." The book never says what the knob moves inside
the tank; the water itself is filled to a Min, Standard or Max mark
(`erg750w-2025-specs-water-tank-capacity-8-to-12-7-liters-min-standard-max`), and the knob is not the fill level.

How the console knows the setting: the service manual routes a **VR cable** (VR, a variable resistor - the knob's
position sensor) from the tank up through the main frame and its upper cover to the console stand, where "the
terminals of the console stand assembly" join it. Its console-VR fault list is three checks: the connection between
the console and VR cable terminals; between the VR terminal and the **coiled cord** terminal; and the upper
terminals of the console for looseness. Those pages are headed "Model R48", the OEM's name for the machine; the
rest of the book says ERG-750W.

When rowing stops, "resistance will be paused for 4 seconds, and SPM will go to 0" - a display behaviour, the load
on the handle does not change. The other Xterra water rowers set their load by the water level alone on a six-mark
gauge (`xterra-ergw-specs-water-resistance-set-by-the-tank-level-on-a-six-mark-gauge`), and the ERG800W by a
Min-Max label (`erg800w-2023-specs-water-resistance-set-by-the-tank-level-between-min-and-max-marks`). The Spirit
CRW900's ten-position adjuster moves water between two chambers (`crw900-2021-specs-ten-level-tank-adjuster`) -
a different device with a different count.
