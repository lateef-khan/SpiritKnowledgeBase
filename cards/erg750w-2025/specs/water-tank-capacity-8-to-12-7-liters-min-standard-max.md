The tank carries three marks, and the book prints the volume at each:

| Mark | Litres | US gallons | Quarts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Min | 8.0 | 2.11 | 8.46 |
| Standard | 9.7 | 2.56 | 10.25 |
| Max | 12.7 | 3.35 | 13.4 |

"Do not exceed the water label. Water added beyond the maximum water level is exempt from the warranty." The tank
takes **only distilled or pure water** on this machine (the 2021-2023 water rowers say tap water), with a
purification tablet replaced every six months; the maintenance section holds the filling and emptying steps.

The water level is not the resistance control on the ERG750W - a knob on the tank sets the level the console shows
(`erg750w-2025-specs-resistance-knob-on-the-tank-with-six-levels-shown-on-the-console`). The ERG550W tank is
smaller, 6.5 to 10 litres over six numbered marks
(`erg550w-2023-specs-water-tank-capacity-6-5-to-10-liters-by-level`).
