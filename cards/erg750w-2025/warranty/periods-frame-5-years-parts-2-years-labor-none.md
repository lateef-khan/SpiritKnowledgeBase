| Warranty | Frame | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | 5 Years | 2 Years | None |

**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the first warranty page of the ERG750W owner's manual (PDF p. 24, printed 23), *Effective November 12, 2025 - ERG750W Water Rowing Machine LIMITED WARRANTY*. **Labor is None**: XTERRA supplies the part inside two years and the owner fits it or pays a technician. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**This is the only Xterra rower page with these three figures.** The ERG500 and ERG600W give the same frame and parts terms with 90 days of labor (`xterra-rower-warranty-periods-frame-5-years-parts-2-years-labor-90-days`); the ERG650W gives two years of parts with a lifetime frame and a year of labor (`sb600-2023-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`).

**The warranty runs two pages and stops.** Page 24 prints the table and the five consumer duties without a registration item (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-without-registration`); page 25 the nine exclusions that stop at *residential setting* (`fb180-2025-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-residential-only-with-proof-of-purchase-and-outside-us-items`) and the 48-states sentence (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-48-contiguous-states-not-alaska-or-hawaii`). **There is no SERVICE page** - no steps, no return-authorization rule, no trip charge, no service-department number (`fb180-2025-warranty-service-steps-not-printed-use-the-cover-contacts`); the cover's customer-care line is the contact (`xterra-warranty-support-contacts-which-phone-number-each-rower-and-strength-book-prints`). Registration is by QR code or the Support tab, with no deadline (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-qr-code-and-support-tab-no-deadline`). The tank page says water beyond the maximum mark, inferior tablets or bleach are *exempt from the warranty* (`xterra-ergw-warranty-tank-chlorine-bleach-or-filling-past-the-mark-voids-it`).

**Two dates.** The cover stamp is *ERG750W_175926_OM_20250409*; the registration page prints *Version: 1.0, Revision: 11.12.2025* and the warranty is effective the same November 12, 2025. The revision names the book; the April date is the earlier export.
