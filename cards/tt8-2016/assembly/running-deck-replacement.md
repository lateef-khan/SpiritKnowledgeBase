**DC model: TT8 2016 ST925-YT021, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2016 (ST925A-YT030) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

1. Follow the front and rear roller replacement procedure first.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to loosen **6 screws** and remove the top step cover in the direction of the arrows, then remove **8 screws** on the front and back of the foot rail and take the front and back foot rails off in the direction of the arrows.
3. Use a combination **M5 Allen wrench** to remove the **8 screws** securing the running board. Remove the running board and replace the running board or running belt. If the cushions need replacing, remove the **6 cushions** and replace them. Reassemble in the reverse order.
4. Use a **14 mm open end wrench** to adjust belt tension with a tension measuring device. **Adjust to the
   white LBS area, between 70-75 LBS.** Use a **14 mm T-shaped socket wrench** to secure and tighten the 4
   securing screws.
5. After changing the running deck, **walk an S shape for 1 km and a square shape for 3 km, about 10 minutes in total**, so the wax spreads evenly on the running deck.
