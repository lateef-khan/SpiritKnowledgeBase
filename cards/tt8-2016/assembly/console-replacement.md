**DC model: TT8 2016 ST925-YT021, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2016 (ST925A-YT030) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

1. Use an L Allen wrench to remove the **6 button head socket bolts** from the upright cover.
2. Use an L Allen wrench to remove the **4 button head socket bolts** from the console support.
3. Disconnect the console wiring and replace the console.
