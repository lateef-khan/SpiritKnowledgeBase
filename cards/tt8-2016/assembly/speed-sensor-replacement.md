**DC model: TT8 2016 ST925-YT021, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2016 (ST925A-YT030) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

1. Remove the motor top cover.
2. Remove the speed sensor wiring and replace the part.
3. **After replacement a test to check the sensor registers the magnet is required.**

Set the gap before you close the cover: the sensor must sit as close to the pulley as possible without touching, aligned with the magnet, and **less than 3 mm away**.
