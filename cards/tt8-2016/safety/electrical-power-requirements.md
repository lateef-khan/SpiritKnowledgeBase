**DC model: TT8 2016 ST925-YT021, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2016 (ST925A-YT030) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

- Disconnect the treadmill from the electrical outlet before cleaning or service work.
- Install the treadmill on a flat level surface with access to a **220-volt, 10-amp grounded outlet**, with
  only the treadmill plugged into that circuit. **On a 120 Vac electronic power system it is a 110-volt,
  15-amp outlet.**
- **Do not use an extension cord** unless it is **16 AWG or better with only one outlet on the end.** Do not
  disable the grounded plug with adapters or modify the cord outlet.
- **Never use a GFCI wall outlet with this treadmill.** As with any appliance with a large motor, the GFCI
  will trip often.
- Route the power cord away from any moving part, including the elevation mechanism and the transport
  wheels.
