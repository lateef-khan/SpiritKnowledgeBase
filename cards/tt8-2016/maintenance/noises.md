**DC model: TT8 2016 ST925-YT021, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2016 (ST925A-YT030) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

**Noise under the motor cover**

1. Worn brushes or bearings on the motor — replace the motor (the checklist says replace the motor brushes).
2. Front roller bearings defective — replace the front roller.
3. Drive belt misadjusted, too tight or too loose — adjust the motor position.

**Noise at the rear of the treadmill**

1. Rear roller bearings defective — replace the rear roller.
2. Rear roller misaligned — adjust the rear roller position.

**General noises**

1. Noise coming from the rollers.
2. Noise while running at the **lowest** level of incline can be too much pressure on the incline cylinder;
   this only happens at the lowest incline level.
3. Knocking noise during a workout — check that all bolts are tightened.
4. Thumping while the belt runs happens on a brand new treadmill, or one that has not been used for a long
   time, because the belt has been shaped by the rollers and hardened by low temperature. **Running the belt
   for tens of minutes makes the thumping gradually go away.**

**Black particles collecting under the treadmill** is the drive belt breaking in — vacuum under the
treadmill periodically.
