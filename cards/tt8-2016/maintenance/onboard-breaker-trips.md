**DC model: TT8 2016 ST925-YT021, DC drive motor. The AC inverter TT8 2016 (ST925A-YT030) is a different machine and this card does not apply to it.**

The checklist row reads: **"Treadmill trips on board 15 amp circuit"** — high belt/deck friction. The fix
is belt and deck lubrication.

**The row points at a section this manual does not contain.** It says "See General Maintenance section on
Belt/Deck Lubrication", but section 9 of this manual covers cleaning, tension and tracking only — there is
no lubrication procedure in it. Take the procedure from the owner's manual or from the AC or 2019 TT8
service manual.

**The amp rating printed here differs between the TT8 variants.** This manual says 15 amp. Check the
rating stamped on the machine rather than assuming it matches another TT8.
