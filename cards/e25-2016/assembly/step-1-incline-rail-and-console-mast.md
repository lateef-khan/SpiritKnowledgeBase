Do not fully tighten the rail bolts until the end of this step; the mast bolts get fully tightened here.

1. **Install the Incline Rail Assembly (2) into the U-channel bracket of the Main Frame (1).** From the sides install **two Hex Head Bolts (128)** with **two Flat Washers (171)** and **two Nylon Nuts (158)**. From the top install **four Hex Head Bolts (124)**, **four Split Washers (176)**, **four Flat Washers (166)**, and **four Star Washers (177)**. Tighten with **Wrenches (186 & 189)**.
2. **Connect the three wire harness (43)** to the female receiver cable from the Incline Rail Assembly (2). Connect the **Three pin position Sensor connector (44)** the same way. **Mate the red, white, and black wires together.**
3. **Slide the Console Mast Cover (95) onto the Console Mast (12).** Tie the free end of the twist tie exiting the bottom of the mast to the **Computer Cable (50)** at the top opening of the Main Frame, pull it up through the mast until it exits the top, and install the mast into the receiving bracket. **Pull slightly on the cable at the top while installing** so it does not get pinched and shorted.
4. **Put one Split Washer (175) onto the Long Hex Head Bolt (129)** through the left side of the receiving bracket, and **two Curved Washers (179) onto the two Short Hex Head Bolts (127)** through the front. Using **Wrench (186)**, tighten the (127) bolts first, then the (129) bolt, and lastly the pre-installed fourth bolt, firmly. **This is the main joint of the unit; if not tightened sufficiently it leads to noise and instability.**
5. **Plug the connectors into the back of the console: Computer Cable (45), two Hand pulse Cables (52 & 53), Resistance switch wire (56) and Incline switch wire (55).** Secure the **Console (40)** with **four Phillips Head Screws (140)** using driver (188). **An empty four-pin port stays empty; this model does not use it.**

The book numbers the resistance wire (56) and the incline wire (55) here, but its own handlebar step calls the Upper Resistance wire (55) and the Upper Incline wire (56). Follow each step's own numbers. This book has no bottle-holder sub-step; the 2019 book adds one.
