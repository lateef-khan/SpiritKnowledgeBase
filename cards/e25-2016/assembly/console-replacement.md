1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **Phillips head screws M5x15mm (3 pcs)** securing the bottom cover of the console, front and back, and the **sheet metal screws 3.5x12mm (2 pcs)**. Remove the bottom console cover.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **Phillips head screws M5x10mm (4 pcs)** securing the console. Unfasten all connected wires and remove the console.
3. Reassemble in the reverse order. Be sure not to crush or damage wiring during the process.
4. To remove both the upper and lower console cover, remove the **sheet metal screws 3.5x12** securing the console. The manual says this should only be done by a professional.
