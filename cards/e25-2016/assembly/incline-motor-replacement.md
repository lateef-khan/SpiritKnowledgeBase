1. Remove the chain covers.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **3 Phillips head screws M5x15mm** from the incline cover, then remove the parts.
3. Use **two 14mm wrenches** to remove the **hex head bolt 3/8" x 2-1/2"** together with the **flat washer 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T**, **nyloc nut 3/8" x 7T** and **two nylon washers 3/8" x 35 x 5T** which secure the incline tube and incline motor.
4. Use **two #14 open end combination spanner wrenches** to remove the **hex head bolt 3/8" x 2-1/2"**, **flat washer 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T** and **nyloc nut M10 x 8T** from the outer rail tube and incline adjustable assembly. Remove the inclined adjustable assembly.
5. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **tapping screw 5x16** from the incline motor grounding wire. **Cut the wire** and remove the incline motor.
6. Use a **12mm wrench** to remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 1** and **flat washer 5/16" x 35 x 2T** from the rail assembly, pull out the locking tube assembly and remove the rail assembly.
7. If removal of the aluminum rail is needed, remove the **M5x15mm Phillips head screws** from the aluminum rail.
8. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.

Note the nut sizes in steps 3 and 4: the same 3/8" x 2-1/2" hex head bolt is listed with a **3/8" x 7T** nyloc nut in one step and an **M10 x 8T** nyloc nut in the other. This manual gives no zeroing distance for the new motor.
