Use screwdriver (187 or 188) for the plastic parts. This book has no tablet-holder step and no power-on positioning step.

1. **Match the console covers (105 & 104)** and secure with **three Phillips Head Screws (138)** and **two Sheet Metal Screws (145)**.
2. **Match the Inner Connecting Arm Covers (93 & 92)** with **two Sheet Metal Screws (145)** and **two Phillips Head Screws (138)**.
3. **Match the outer Connecting Arm Covers (108 & 109)** onto the arms, each side with **two Sheet Metal Screws (145)**, arrows pointing up or they break.
4. **Slide Wheel Covers (94)**, each side with **four Phillips Head Screws (138)**.
5. **Stabilizer Covers (115 & 116)** on the middle stabilizer bar with **two Phillips Head Screws (138)**.
6. **Front Stabilizer Cover (110)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (138)**.
7. **Incline Cover brackets (21)**, bent-tab hole facing rearward, on the Incline Rail Assembly (2) with **four Phillips Head Screws (146)**; **Rear incline bar cover (113)** with **two Screws (138)**.
8. **Rear Stabilizer Cover (114)** with **four Phillips Head Screws (138)**.
9. **Front Handle Bar Cover (L-106) with Rear Handle Bar Cover (L-107)** on the left arm, **three Sheet Metal Screws (145)** with driver (188); repeat for **108 & 109** on the right.
