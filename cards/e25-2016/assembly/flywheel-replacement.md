1. Follow 11.2, 11.3, 11.4, 11.5, 11.6 and 11.7 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, both side covers, cross bar, the belt and the idler wheel assembly.
2. **Plug in the power and adjust the resistance to level 20**, then release the steel cable.
3. Use a **#15 wrench** to loosen the **nyloc nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 9T** on the flywheel and main frame. Remove the flywheel and belt.
4. To reassemble, return the belt onto the grooves on the flywheel and return the flywheel onto the mainframe.
5. Tighten both **3/8"-UNF26 x 9T nuts**. Return the steel cable onto the flywheel **at 45 degrees**, then return the idler wheel assembly.
6. Turn the drive pulley and check the belt is secured. Adjust both **3/8"-UNF26 x 4T nuts** to position the belt in the middle of the drive pulley, then tighten all other screws. Adjust the belt to a proper tension.
7. Follow 11.7, 11.6, 11.5, 11.4, 11.3 and 11.2 to return the other parts.

Note the nut sizes: this manual loosens a **9T** nut in step 3 and tightens **9T** nuts in step 5. The E55, E95 and E95S manuals of the same year loosen an 11T nut at the same point. Check the nut on the machine before ordering.
