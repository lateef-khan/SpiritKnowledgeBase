1. Remove the swing arm and inclinable rail assembly.
2. Press the console mast cover slightly to take it apart from the side cases.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **7 pcs of 4x19 self tapping screws** and **3 pcs of 5x16 x 3 tapping screws** to take the right side case apart.
4. For the left side case, release the **4x15 self tapping screws with 1/4" x 19 flat washers** on the main frame and **3 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws**. Then disconnect two red jumpers and the white grounding. **Mark the red jumpers before disconnecting them.**
5. On reinstallation, connect the red jumpers and the grounding back on the AC electronic module.
6. Return the left side case, with one **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** on it, onto the main frame. Use a 4x15 self tapping screw with a 1/4" x 19 flat washer to hold it, not too tight at first, then tighten with 3 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws, making sure the side case matches the round disk. Tighten the 4x15 self tapping screws.
7. Match both side cases with each other and use **7 pcs of 4x19 self tapping screws** and **3 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws** to secure them.
8. Reverse the above to refit the pedal arm and connecting arm.
