1. The elliptical uses a **linked structure design**, so a problem in one link affects other parts of the structure. Every part matters. As well as replacing a faulty part, **lubricate frequently**; the manual advises lubricating during maintenance or repair to prevent future problems.
2. The main footing issue is whether the sliding wheel of the linked structure slides smoothly and parallel on top of the inclinable rail assembly. The main cause would be the side inclinable rail assembly and the inclinable rail assembly not being parallel, or the side inclinable rail assembly and the main frame bottom bracket rotating sleeve not meeting at **90 degrees**.
3. Check whether the left and right connecting arm and left and right step are connected properly, whether the sliding wheel has the correct rotation, and whether there is slippage, bearing damage or unsecured connections causing noise.
4. Maintenance should generally start from the sliding wheels: replace them or add lubrication to the top of the sliding wheel arcs. Check the main structure for abnormalities and for parallel parts being out of parallel, and make sure the problem is with the part and not the assembly before replacing anything.
