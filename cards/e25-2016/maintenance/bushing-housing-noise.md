1. A problem with the bushing housing is rare. A few units may develop a noise problem over time. **Applying some grease on the 25 x 111.5 L shaft on the cross bar assembly** may help.
2. If the plastic bushing has worn out, replace it with a new one.

The bushing housing removal steps are printed under the heading "11.9 Drive Pulley Replacement"; see that card.
