Slipping problems can be verified by these checks.

1. **M12 x P1.75 rod end bearing** - the M12 nut on top must be securely tightened with the pedal bar assembly, and the rod end bearing needs to be vertical.
2. **Bushing housing, pedal arm** - the CAP socket head cap bolt **3/8" x 2-1/4"** on top and the **nyloc nut 3/8" x 11T** must be tightened securely.
3. **Cross bar** - the CAP socket head cap bolt **M8 x 40 m/m** on top of the cross bar.
4. **Belt** - this appears quite often, because after a period of time most belts become loose from use. The solution depends on weight and adjustment method; use the belt disassembly procedure.

**This manual prints no pounds-force figures.** The E35, E55, E95 and E98 manuals of the same year suggest 500~600 lbs at the bushing housing and 550~600 lbs at the cross bar. Do not carry those numbers over without confirming them.
