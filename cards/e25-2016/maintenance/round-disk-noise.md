Friction here creates abnormal sounds. To find it, check whether the large disk is not centred or is offset.

1. If the disc is not centred and causing friction, remove the left and right chain cover and reassemble, following the assembly and disassembly procedures.
2. If the disc is offset and causing friction, remove the left and right chain cover, use a Phillips screwdriver and remove the **5x16mm tapping screw** and the **1/4" x 19mm flat washer**. Place the round disk and the cross bar on a flat surface to check whether either is deformed. If deformed, check whether the deformity can be fixed by hand.

Deformity is mostly caused by external force. The manual notes the product goes through a quiet room test on the production line to ensure a standard of **+/- 3mm** is met before shipment.
