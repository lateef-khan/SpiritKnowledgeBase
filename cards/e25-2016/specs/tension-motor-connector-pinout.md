Main control side, 5 pins:

| Pin | Signal |
|---|---|
| 1 | M+ |
| 2 | M- |
| 3 | +5V |
| 4 | VR |
| 5 | GND |

The drawing numbers the mating side 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 against these, and shows the steel rope leaving the motor.
