numbers appear on both lists; **55 of those 137 carry a different description or
a different quantity, and on more than twenty the number names an altogether
different component.** These are not near-misses:

**On the printed numbering.** 169 rows are printed. The whole numbers that
appear are 1-6, 8-38, 41-64, 66-73, 75, 77, 79-90, 92-115, 117-122, 124-128,
130-136, 138-142, 145, 148, 150-153 and 168, plus the sub-numbers 21-3, 39-1 to
39-5 and 52-1 to 52-35 (with 52-6, 52-9, 52-14, 52-15, 52-17, 52-20 and 52-28 to
52-30 absent). Any number outside that run is not printed in this manual at all
- it is not a missing page. The manual writes the sub-numbers with a tilde on
pages 58-59 (`39~1`, `52~10`) and with a hyphen on the rest of page 59
(`52-31`, `52-34`); they are one numbering and are written with hyphens
throughout above.

**Three printing faults, and what was done with them.**

- **Item 117's row runs into a stray drawing code.** The page prints
  `117 Ø5 × 16L_Tapping Screw 55 201215`. The quantity is 55; `201215` is a
  document number sitting at the foot of the column, not a quantity, and it is
  not reproduced in the table.
- **Item 131 is printed across two lines**, as "M5_Combination M5 Allen Wrench &
  Phillips Head" then "Screw Driver". It is rejoined here as one description.
- **Items 45, 46 and 47 are all 100m/m connecting wires, and 45 and 47 are both
  black.** The manual prints them that way; check the exploded view before
  ordering either.

**This machine's list carries parts no other XT list has**: a Chest Strap (138),
a 400m/m Audio Cable (145), a USB Audio module (52-26), a W/Receiver HR (52-25)
and a Book Rack (52-8).
