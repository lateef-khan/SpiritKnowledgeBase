**The CUSTOM program lets you create a speed and incline changing profile by sliding up and down on
the profile columns.** Once the profile is defined, choose the target time and the maximum speed, so
the intensity adapts accordingly.

**Saving it:**

- **Every program can be saved as a template once adjusted, or once completed.** Press **Save as** to
  save the program with its current settings.
- Saved programs are managed in **TEMPLATES**. To delete one, tap it, then press **Delete** in the
  top right corner.
- **A maximum of 12 template programs can be stored.**
- **A Template program is only available to the user who created it.**

A completed workout can also be saved as a template from the workout summary screen, using the
**Save as** button there.

The XT685ENT builds its own programs a different way - by connecting dots on a grid, under a
**Custom** tab, with the speed profile required and the incline profile optional. Its limit is also
12, but the two consoles are not the same.
