**On this machine Fusion is a preset speed and incline profile chosen from the touchscreen. The
manual mentions no dumbbells, no strength intervals off the machine, and no interval-count or
recovery-time prompts.**

**Speed:** The FUSION program takes you through high levels of cardio & strength intensity followed
by recovery periods of low intensity. It utilises and develops your "Fast Twitch" muscle fibres,
which are used when performing tasks that are intense and short in duration. These deplete your
oxygen level and spike your heart rate, followed by periods of recovery and heart rate drop to
replenish oxygen, so your cardiovascular system gets programmed to use oxygen more efficiently.

**Incline:** This program will spike similar to the speed profile, but in different segments
(columns); this means that all of your lower extremity muscles will be equally challenged throughout
this program. **The incline alternates between 25 & 65 % of maximum elevation.**

**The description is printed word for word in the button-console XT385, XT485 and XT685 manuals -
but on those machines Fusion is a dumbbell-interval program** you set up with a number of intervals,
an interval time and a recovery time, and you step off the treadmill for five named strength
exercises. None of that is in this manual, so the two are kept as separate cards. See
`xt-2023-programs-fusion-program-xt385-xt485-xt685` and `xt-2023-programs-fusion-workout-structure`.

**This manual prints the same description twice.** The HIIT program on page 40 carries the identical
speed and incline paragraphs, with only "cardio & strength" removed. The source gives no way to tell
the two profiles apart; both are recorded as printed.
