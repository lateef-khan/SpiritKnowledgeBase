**This console has two heart rate programs, not four. HR1 is set to 60% and HR2 to 80% of the
maximum heart rate.** Both operate the same way and are programmed the same way; the target is the
only difference.

**You must wear a heart rate transmitter strap for these programs.**

To start one: select the **HR1** or **HR2** program, then the **Next** button, and follow the
directions on the screen.

**Do not confuse this with the button-console XT385, XT485 and XT685, which have four heart rate
programs at 55%, 65%, 75% and 85%** - see `xt-2023-programs-heart-rate-program-xt385-xt485-xt685`.
The XT685ENT has a single Heart Rate target program that offers 55%, 65%, 75% or 85%, or a bpm value
typed in directly - see `xt685ent-2023-programs-heart-rate-program`. The three consoles print three
different sets of percentages.

The manual prints no further setup steps for this program and does not say which of speed or incline
the console adjusts to hold the target. For how the 60% and 80% figures are arrived at, see
`xt-2023-programs-heart-rate-training-zone`.
