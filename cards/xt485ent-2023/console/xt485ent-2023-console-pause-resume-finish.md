- **Press the "Stop" button once and the belt slows down gradually until it fully
  stops.**
- **After 5 minutes the display resets and shows the workout summary on the screen.**
- **Press "Resume" to continue the workout from the pause.**
- **Press "Finish" to end the workout.**

**This is not the base XT Stop key behaviour.** On the XT185, XT285, XT385, XT485 and
XT685 the Stop key pauses on the first press, ends the program and shows a summary on
the second, returns to idle on a third, and resets the console if held for more than
3 seconds; see `xt-2023-console-pause-stop-reset`. This console counts no presses -
it puts Resume and Finish on the screen instead.

**The XT685ENT wording is different again**: there the pause offers a cool down as
well, and **after 5 minutes of inactivity in Pause mode the workout ends
automatically**, where this manual says the display resets and shows the summary. See
`xt685ent-2023-console-workout-mode-views`.
