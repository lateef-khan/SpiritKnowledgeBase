The Homescreen **provides an activity overview and suggests shortcuts to the training
options**.

- **In Guest mode you can provide your physical data here**, which helps the machine
  calculate your workout summary more accurately. **Use the scales or arrows to
  adjust your age and weight.**
- **The Start Workout button starts the workout immediately.**

**Across the top**, the drawing labels: **Log out**, **Pair HR device**, **Adjust
volume**, **Fan on/off**, **Activity Overview**, **Current User**, and **Wifi
Settings**. It also marks a **User data setting** area and a **Quick Start**
shortcut.

**Along the bottom**, six tabs: **Homescreen**, **Workout Programs**, **Profiles**,
**Internet & Apps**, **Screen Mirroring**, **Settings**.

**The XT685ENT home screen is not this one.** It offers an immediate Start, a
manually timed workout called **"Set Timer"**, and **3 to 6 favorite programs**; see
`xt685ent-2023-console-home-screen`.
