**You type the value; you do not press a level button.**

1. Press either the **Speed** or the **Incline** Direct Access key.
2. **Select either 2, or 3 digits.** The treadmill adjusts to that value
   automatically, so you do not have to press and hold a button until it reaches the
   value.

The manual prints these examples:

| Press | Result |
|---|---|
| Select Incline, then 1, 0 | Incline Level 1.0 |
| Select Incline, then 3, 5 | Incline Level 3.5 |
| Select Speed, then 8, 0 | 8.0 mph or kmph |
| Select Speed, then 0, 8 | 0.8 mph or kmph |

**The maximum value you may input is 12.0 mph or 18.0 kmph for speed, and 15.0% for
incline.** The manual states no minimum and no increment.

**The base 2023 XT consoles do not work this way.** On the XT385, XT485, XT685 and
XT685ENT you press a single level button - 1 through 12 for speed, 0 through 15 for
incline - with no digit entry at all; see
`xt-2023-console-direct-speed-incline-buttons`.

**The 2015 XT385, XT485 and XT685 take the same two or three digits**, with the same
four examples, but their keys are labelled **Select Speed** and **Select Incline**
rather than Direct Access; see `xt-2015-console-select-keys-two-or-three-digits`.
