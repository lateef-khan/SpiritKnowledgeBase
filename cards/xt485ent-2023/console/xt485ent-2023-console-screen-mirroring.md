To display content from your smartphone or tablet on the machine console, **open the
Screen Mirroring section and follow the instructions provided on screen** - they
differ **depending on whether you use an iOS or an Android device**.

**Once the mirroring has started**, press the full screen icon to enter full screen
mode and the exit icon to leave it.

**The manual prints no iOS or Android steps of its own.** It defers entirely to what
the console shows, so this card cannot list them.

**Casting needs the WiFi connection.** The set-up page states that without WiFi,
casting content will not work; see `xt485ent-2023-console-wifi-first-time-setup`.
