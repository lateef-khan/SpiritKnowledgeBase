The treadmill lets you **make speed and incline changes on the side handrails**. You
can turn those controls off if you frequently hold on to the rails.

- **Press the Handrails On/Off button, on the right side of the lower portion of the
  console.**
- **When the indicator light is lit, the handrail controls are disabled.**

**The button is in a different place, and has a different name, on the other machines
in this family.** On the XT385, XT485, XT685 and XT685ENT it is the **Handrail
Control Button, next to the child lock** - see
`xt-2023-console-handlebar-speed-incline-controls`. This manual never mentions a
child lock at all, so do not look for the button beside one.
