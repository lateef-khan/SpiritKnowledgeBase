- **Press any button to wake the display up** if it is not already on.
- **Press the Start Workout button on the screen to begin. The belt starts at 0.5
  mph.**
- To adjust speed, use the **Speed +/- keys on the screen**, or the **keys at the
  bottom of the console**.
- **To slow the tread-belt, press and hold the Slow - key** (console or hand rail) to
  the desired speed.
- **To stop the tread-belt, press and release the "Stop" button.**

**The start is a tap on the screen, not a press of the Start key.** On the base 2023
XT consoles you press and release the Start key twice - once to wake the display and
once to begin - and the tether key also wakes the console; see
`xt-2023-console-quick-start`. This manual mentions neither.
