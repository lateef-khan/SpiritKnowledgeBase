- **The incline is changeable any time during the workout.**
- **Press and hold the Incline up or down buttons on the handrail, or the Incline +
  or - buttons on the screen**, to change the incline to the desired level.
- **The incline may also be changed with the Direct Access Incline buttons on the
  console below the touchscreen** - see
  `xt485ent-2023-console-direct-access-keys-two-or-three-digits`.

**Two things the other XT manuals print are missing here.** This manual does **not**
say the display moves in **0.5** increments, and it does **not** say the incline
**returns to zero** at the end of a workout. Those two statements appear in every
other 2015 and 2023 XT manual - see `xt-2023-console-incline-adjustment` - and this
card does not claim them for this machine because the source never states them.
