The console drawing labels nine things:

- **USB Port**
- **Touchscreen Display**
- **Tablet Rack**
- **Integrated Speakers**
- **Swivel Fan**
- **Direct Access Speed and Incline Buttons**
- **Handrail Control Buttons**
- **3.5mm Audio-input only (no output)**
- **Easy-Touch Control Keys**

The manual then lists the **physical buttons**, which are separate from anything you
tap on the screen:

- **Direct Access Incline/Speed keys 0~9**
- **Speed + or -** buttons to control the speed
- **Incline + or -** buttons to control the incline
- **Start** button to begin a workout
- **Stop** button to end, or pause, the workout
- **Fan** button to control the fan speed
- **Handrail Controls** button to enable or disable the incline and speed controls on
  the handrails

**This is not the base XT485 panel.** That console has an LCD window, a cup holder, a
safety key and program buttons in the drawing; see `xt-2023-console-layout`. **Nor is
it the XT685ENT panel**, which is a touchscreen too but labels a cup holder, a child
lock, a hand pulse grip and a handrail lock instead; see
`xt685ent-2023-console-panel-overview`.

**The drawing does not label a safety key or a cup holder**, although the safety key
is described elsewhere in the manual.
