Once the workout has started, **after a short countdown the console appears in its
workout mode**. **Warm-up and cool-down phases may be skipped by pressing Skip.**

- **The left panel is fixed and shows your current WORKOUT STATS.** More related
  stats are revealed by **pressing the + icon**.
- **SPEED AND INCLINE CONTROLS are adjusted by tapping arrows in the right panel.**
- **The top panel holds volume, fan and WiFi.** **When the workout is paused you can
  also reach the HR device pairing settings there.**

**The bottom panel offers three views, left to right:**

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **GOAL VIEW** | The parameter that defines your workout finish - time counting down for profile programs, distance left for distance programs |
| **PROFILE VIEW** | Your workout profile and your current position in it |
| **TRACK VIEW** | Laps on a **1/4 mile track**, with progress by distance covered |

**INTERNET and SCREEN MIRRORING work the same way as in non-workout mode.** **To
pause or finish, press Stop in the bottom right corner.**

**After the workout finishes a summary appears.** **Slide left for more detail, or
press Done to go back to the Home screen.** **Press Save as to save the completed
program as a template** - the record then appears in the **History tab** of the
Profile section.

**The XT685ENT names its views differently** - Stats, Charts and Track, with the
parameters chosen by tapping a number - and it requires the **physical** keys for
speed and incline rather than on-screen arrows; see
`xt685ent-2023-console-workout-mode-views`.
