This section holds **a variety of third party apps** to keep you entertained during
your workout.

1. **Connect to the WiFi** - see `xt485ent-2023-console-wifi-first-time-setup`.
2. **Choose the app icon.**
3. Press the full screen icon **to enter full screen mode**, and the exit icon **to
   leave it**.
4. **Press the house icon to choose another app.**

**The manual names no app.** It never lists which third party apps are installed, and
this card does not guess at one.

**The CT900ENT is a different thing again** - that console browses the open web from
its Home screen; see `ct900ent-internet-browsing`.
