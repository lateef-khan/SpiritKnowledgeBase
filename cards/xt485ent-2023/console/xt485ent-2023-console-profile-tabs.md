**To create a profile, press the + button on the starting screen.** A **maximum of 9
profiles** can be created; you may also continue as a **Guest**.

- **If you already have a Spirit APP ACCOUNT, scan the QR code that appears on the
  screen.** That uploads your existing personal info to the machine **and** links the
  two profiles, so your workout history is fully displayed in the Spirit app.
- **One Spirit app account cannot be linked to multiple profiles on one machine.**
- **If you do not have a Spirit account, press the "I DON'T" button** and build one by
  filling in the form.

**Three tabs:**

| Tab | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **INFO** | Your personal data. Tap any field, change it, then press **Save**. **You can also link a Spirit account here**, by pressing the "Spirit Account" field. |
| **HISTORY** | **Ten records of the latest activity.** Only workouts completed by the current user. Tap a record preview for detail. **All records are in the Spirit app account if the profile is linked.** |
| **FAVORITES** | Programs the current user labelled as favorites; they can be seen and deleted here |

**Different users can choose different unit systems**, select their own favorite
programs and save their own templates.

**The XT685ENT sets a profile up the other way round** - download the app first, then
press **Link** on the console and scan the QR code from inside the app - and it adds a
**passcode** this manual never mentions; see
`xt685ent-2023-console-profiles-and-account-link`.
