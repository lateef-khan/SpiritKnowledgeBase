**This build has two belt tension screws - a side tension screw and a rear drive-belt tension screw - where
the other XT books loosen one.** Everything else matches them: five cover screws, a 14mm T-socket on four
motor screws, 70-75 lbs on a gauge, red to M+ and black to M-.

The steps are printed unnumbered; they are numbered here in the printed order.

1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **5 tapping screws**, then disassemble the motor cover.
2. Remove the motor grounding wire (**greenish yellow**), the motor wire (+) **red** and the motor wire (-)
   **black**.
3. Use a **14mm T-shaped socket wrench** to loosen the **4 screws**, and use the 14mm T-shaped socket wrench
   to loosen **a side tension screw as well as a rear drive belt tension screw**. Remove the motor and replace
   it with the new one.
4. Reassemble in reverse order of disassembly and re-hook the belt (readjust the belt to be parallel with the
   main frame after re-hooking). **Do not tighten the 4 securing screws yet.**
5. Use a **14mm open end wrench** to adjust the belt tension with a tension measuring device to the **white LBS
   area between 70-75 LBS**. Use the 14mm T-shaped socket wrench to secure and tighten the 4 securing screws.
6. Connect the grounding wires and the motor wires (**red M+, black M-**).

Step 3 names the T-socket for the tension screws and step 5 names an open end wrench for adjusting them; the
book does not reconcile the two. The step 5 and 6 paragraph is reprinted word for word at the end of this
book's roller and deck procedures, motor-wire line included.
