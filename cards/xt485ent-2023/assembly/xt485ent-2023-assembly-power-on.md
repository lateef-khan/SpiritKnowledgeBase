1. Plug the treadmill into an appropriate wall outlet.
2. Turn on the power switch, **located at the front of the treadmill below the
   motor hood**.
3. **Ensure the Safety Key is installed** - the treadmill will not power on
   without it.

When the power is turned on, the screen shows a **brief loading screen** and then
the **WELCOME screen**, which indicates that the machine is ready to operate.

**This is the touchscreen startup, not the self-test one.** The XT385-2023,
XT485-2023, XT685-2023 and XT685ENT manuals describe an internal self-test with
all the lights coming on, a software version in the Message Window, an odometer
in the Distance window and hours in the Time window. **None of that is printed
for this machine**, and this machine has no such windows. Do not answer a
question about VER 1.0 or an odometer reading from this card.

Two things this manual does not say, and neither should be filled in from another
machine:

- **It never says where the power switch is beyond "below the motor hood"**, and
  it prints no circuit-breaker instruction here. The troubleshooting table
  elsewhere in the manual mentions a circuit breaker on the front grill that can
  be pushed in until it locks.
- **It has no setting-up section at all**, so there is no levelling, folding or
  moving instruction to go with this one. That absence has its own card.
