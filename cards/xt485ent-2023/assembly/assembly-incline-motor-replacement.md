**Set the new incline motor to 225 mm before you fit it.** The figure is printed twice - inside the procedure
and as the book's chapter 11 over a drawing - both times as "Incline Range must be adjusted to 225mm minimum
prior to installation".

The steps are printed unnumbered; they are numbered here in the printed order.

1. Remove the motor hood and fold the treadmill, then remove the incline motor.
2. Set the new motor to **225mm**.
3. Use the **M6 and M8 L wrenches and a #14 wrench** to assemble the new incline motor.
4. Connect the incline motor wiring to the controller: **red wire to UP, white wire to COM, black wire to
   DOWN**.

The tool list is what sets this card apart from the XT385 and XT485 books, which name a 14mm wrench alone
for the same 225 mm motor. The Sole F85 2016 book, for the same Dyaco platform, names the same three tools.
No torque is printed and the drawing does not say between which points the 225 is measured, though it
dimensions the motor body's pivot hole to the hole at the end of the extended tube.
