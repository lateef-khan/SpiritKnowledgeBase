**This diagram carries no letters and no numbers.** Each part is named on the
drawing itself, so there is no A-to-N key to quote. Nine parts are called out, in
this order down the page:

- Speakers
- Console
- Console fan
- Uprights
- Handles
- Motor cover
- Belt
- Side rails
- End caps

**It is a different diagram from the one the other XT machines print.** The
XT385-2023, XT485-2023, XT685-2023 and XT685ENT print a lettered diagram running
A to M or N, which names a tablet holder, an accessory tray, cup holders, hand
pulse sensors, levellers and transport wheels. **None of those six appears on
this diagram**, and this one calls out speakers, a console fan and handles, which
that one does not. A callout quoted as a letter did not come from this manual.

The page has no heading of its own; it sits between the PRE ASSEMBLY page and
STEP ONE.

The console's own callouts - USB port, touchscreen display, tablet rack,
integrated speakers, swivel fan, direct access speed and incline buttons,
handrail control buttons, the 3.5mm audio input and the easy-touch control keys -
are a separate drawing on p. 17 under CONSOLE OPERATION, not this one.
