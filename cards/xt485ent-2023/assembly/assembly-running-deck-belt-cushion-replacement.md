The heading adds "(Be sure to disassemble the cylinder prior to this replacement procedure)". The steps are
printed unnumbered; they are numbered here in the printed order.

1. After folding the treadmill, use an **M5 L Allen wrench** and a **12mm wrench** to release the screw that
   secures the cylinder, and take it apart.
2. "Perform item (F), procedures to replace front and rear rollers." **There is no item (F) in this book** -
   the roller procedure is section 12.6, and it is the one meant.
3. Remove the left and right **foot rail caps**. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **6 screws and
   the bracket** from the back of the foot rail. Remove the foot rail in the direction of the indicating arrows.
4. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **8 screws** securing the running board. Remove the running
   board and replace the board or the running belt. If the cushions need replacing, remove the **6 cushions**
   and replace them. Reassemble in the reverse order.
5. Use a 14mm open end wrench to adjust the drive belt tension with a tension measuring device to the **white
   LBS area between 70-75 LBS**. Use a 14mm T-shaped socket wrench to secure the 4 motor screws. "Connect
   grounding wires and motor wires (red M+, black M-)."

The last sentence is the motor procedure's closing line, reprinted here; nothing in this procedure disconnects
a motor wire. The foot-rail caps and the bracket are this build's own steps - the XT485 2015 book removes six
tapping screws from the foot rail and nothing else. No torque is printed.
