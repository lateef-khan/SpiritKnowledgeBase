The hardware is not photographed on one page. **Each assembly step carries its
own small panel**, and step 1 carries two of them - the nut clips on page 11 and
the upright bolts on page 12.

| Step | Item | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (p. 11) | 128 | Nut clip | M5 | 6 |
| 1 (p. 12) | 130 | Button head socket bolts | 5/16" x 15mm | 8 |
| 2 | 133 | Button head socket bolts | 3/8" x 1-1/2" | 4 |
| 2 | 113 | Split washer | Ø10 x 2.0T | 4 |
| 3 | 120 | Tapping screws | 5 x 16mm | 6 |
| 4a | 142 | Sheet metal screws | Ø3.5 x 16L | 8 |
| 4b | 150 | Button head socket bolts | 5/16" x 3/4" | 4 |

**Two panels are both headed HARDWARE FOR STEP 4**, on pages 15 and 16, because
the manual numbers two different steps as step four. They hold different
fasteners and both are used - 8 of item 142 for the console mast covers, then 4
of item 150 for the handrail support.

## Numbers to be careful with

- The step 3 panel prints item 120 as **5" X 16MM**. The step text and the parts
  list both give it as **5 x 16mm**, a millimetre screw. The inch mark in the
  panel is a typo.
- The step 2 panel calls item 113 a **SPLIT WASHER**; the parts list calls the
  same item a **Ø10 x 2.0T Spring Washer**. One part.
- The step 4b panel spells item 150 **BUTTON HEAD BUTTON BOLTS**; the step text
  and the parts list both call it a **5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt**.

## The parts list totals are larger than the bagged counts

The parts list gives whole-machine quantities: item **120** is listed 15 times
against the 6 the panel bags for step 3, item **113** 8 times against 4, and item
**142** 8 times, which matches. Only the counts in the table above are the
assembly counts.

The three tools - the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver
(131), the L Allen Wrench (132) and the Phillips Head Screw Driver (148) - are
not in these panels. They are on the parts-included page and have their own card.
