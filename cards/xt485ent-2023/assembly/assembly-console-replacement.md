**On this machine the left and right handlebar covers come off before the console bolts.** The XT485 2023
book takes an upright cover off with eight screws instead; this is not that procedure.

1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the left and right **handlebar covers**.
2. Use an **M6 L Allen wrench** to remove the **4 button head socket bolts** from the console support.
3. Disconnect the console wirings and replace with the new console.

The steps are printed unnumbered. No count is given for the handlebar cover screws, no torque is printed,
and the procedure does not say to unplug the machine first.
