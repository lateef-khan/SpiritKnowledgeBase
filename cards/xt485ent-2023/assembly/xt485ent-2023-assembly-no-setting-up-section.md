**This manual has no "Setting Up Your Treadmill" section.** The assembly chapter
ends with the handrail support on page 16 and the console chapter starts on
page 17. So the manual says nothing about:

- **Levelling.** No levelling foot, no leveller, no wrench size, no adjustment.
  The word does not appear anywhere in the manual.
- **Folding or unfolding.** **The word "fold" is not printed anywhere in this
  manual**, and there is no Velcro shipping strap warning.
- **Moving or transporting the machine.** Transport wheels are named once, inside
  the electrical warning that tells you to route the power cord away from moving
  parts including the elevation mechanism and transport wheels.
- **Storage.**

**The silence is worth noticing, because the parts list says the machine folds.**
It carries a **Release Lever (17)**, a **Cylinder (19)**, a **Dual Torsion-Spring
(16)**, a **ChenChin Torsion-Spring (18)** and **four transport wheels** - two of
item 57, Transportation Wheel (A), and two of item 58, Transportation Wheel (B).
The hardware for a folding deck is all listed; the instructions for using it are
not printed.

Absence is the answer here, not a gap to fill from a sibling machine. **Do not
answer these questions from the XT485-2023 card.** That manual does document an
M14 levelling wrench, a fold-and-roll procedure and a Velcro strap warning, and
it is a different generation of machine with different hardware - this one's
assembly, console and carton list are all its own.

What the manual does document about first power-up - wall outlet, power switch
below the motor hood, safety key - is on its own card.
