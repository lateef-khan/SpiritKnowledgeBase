**The printed procedure never says how the front roller comes out.** Its 2015 siblings loosen the front roller
screws with a 13mm wrench and then say "remove front and back rollers, and replace with new"; this book stops
after loosening the drive belt and goes straight to re-tensioning. Read it with that gap in mind.

The steps are printed unnumbered; they are numbered here in the printed order.

1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to loosen the **2 screws** on the rear adjustment base.
2. Use an **M6 L Allen wrench** to loosen the **2 screws** on the rear roller.
3. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to remove the screws and disassemble the **left and right upright bracket
   cover**. Remove the motor cover.
4. Use a **14mm T-shaped socket wrench** to loosen the **4 securing screws**. Use a **14mm wrench** to loosen
   **a side tension screw as well as a rear drive belt tension screw** in order to loosen the drive belt.
5. When reassembled, the running belt tension needs to be adjusted and centred.
6. Use a 14mm open end wrench to adjust the belt tension with a tension measuring device to the **white LBS
   area between 70-75 LBS**. Use the 14mm T-shaped socket wrench to secure and tighten the 4 securing screws.
   "Connect grounding wires and motor wires (red M+, black M-)."

That last sentence is copied from the motor procedure; nothing in this procedure disconnects a motor wire.
The upright bracket covers in step 3 are this build's own - the sibling books do not remove them - and no
count is printed for their screws. No torque is printed.
