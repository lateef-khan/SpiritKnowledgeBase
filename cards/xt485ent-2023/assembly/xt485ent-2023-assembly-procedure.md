**The manual prints two different steps both headed STEP FOUR**, on pages 15 and
16. They are not the same step and neither is optional. They are set out below as
4a and 4b in the printed order. There is no step five in the manual.

Item numbers in brackets are from the assembly drawing.

## Step 1 - nut clips and uprights (pp. 11-12)

1. Take **6 Speed NUT CLIPS (128)**, M5, and attach them at the front and each
   side of the unit.
2. Guide the right upright through **FRAME BASE COVER RIGHT (62)** and **CONSOLE
   MAST COVER RIGHT (67)**.
3. Take the **RIGHT UPRIGHT (4)** and attach the **MIDDLE COMPUTER CABLE (50)**
   to the **LOWER COMPUTER CABLE (49)**. **Be careful not to pinch the cable** or
   damage may occur to the system.
4. Slide the RIGHT UPRIGHT (4) to sit into the frame base.
5. Repeat from step 2 on the left, using the left-hand covers and the **LEFT
   UPRIGHT (5)**.
6. With both uprights in the **FRAME BASE (2)**, use the **COMBINATION M5 ALLEN
   WRENCH AND PHILLIPS HEAD SCREW DRIVER (131)** to tighten **8 BUTTON HEAD
   SOCKET BOLTS (130)**, 5/16" x 15mm, securing the uprights to the frame base.

## Step 2 - console (p. 13)

1. Connect the **SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/ CABLE (54)** to the **UPPER SPEED
   CABLE (37)**.
2. Connect the **INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/ CABLE (55)** to the **UPPER INCLINE
   CABLE (38)**.
3. Connect the **MIDDLE COMPUTER CABLE (50)** to the **UPPER COMPUTER CABLE
   (48)**.
4. **Be sure not to pinch any of the cables** or damage may occur to the system.
5. Insert the **CONSOLE ASSEMBLY (52)** onto the right and left uprights (4, 5)
   and secure with **4 BUTTON HEAD SOCKET BOLTS (133)**, 3/8" x 1-1/2", and **4
   SPLIT WASHERS (113)**, Ø10 x 2.0T. Use the **ALLEN WRENCH (132)** to tighten.

## Step 3 - frame base covers (p. 14)

Install **FRAME BASE COVER RIGHT and LEFT (62, 61)** onto the **FRAME BASE (2)**
using **6 TAPPING SCREWS (120)**, 5 x 16mm. Tighten with the **PHILLIPS HEAD
SCREW DRIVER (148)**.

## Step 4a - console mast covers (p. 15)

Slide **CONSOLE MAST COVER RIGHT and LEFT (67, 66)** up the uprights. Secure them
with **8 SHEET METAL SCREWS (142)**, Ø3.5 x 16L, using the PHILLIPS HEAD SCREW
DRIVER (148).

## Step 4b - handrail support and beverage holder (p. 16)

Place the **HANDRAIL SUPPORT (119)** between the left and right uprights (5, 4)
and use the **COMBINATION ALLEN WRENCH & PHILLIPS HEAD SCREW DRIVER (131)** to
tighten **4 BUTTON HEAD SOCKET BOLTS (150)**, 5/16" x 3/4". Install the
**BEVERAGE HOLDER (121)** onto the handrail support (119).

**NOTE: please tighten all screws after all components assembly complete.**

## What the manual prints inconsistently

- **Two steps are both numbered four**, as above.
- The step 3 hardware table prints the tapping screw as **5" X 16MM**; the step
  text above the table and the parts list both give it as **5 x 16mm**. It is a
  millimetre screw; the inch mark in the table is a typo.
- The prose heading above step 4a says the sheet metal screws (142) fix the
  upright covers **and Console Support (6)**; the numbered instruction beside the
  drawing names only the console mast covers. Both are printed on the same page.
- **Items 66 and 67 are called two things.** The numbered instructions in steps 1
  and 4a call them the **Console Mast Cover** left and right, and so does the
  carton list; the prose heading above step 4a and the parts list call them
  **Upright Cover (L)** and **Upright Cover (R)**. One pair of parts.
