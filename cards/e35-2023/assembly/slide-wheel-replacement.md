1. To take the slide wheel apart, use a Phillips head screwdriver to release two **Phillips head screws M5x15mm** and take off the slide wheel cover first.
2. Use a **12mm open wrench** to remove the hex head bolts and remove the sliding wheels.
3. Reverse the above steps to resume the pedal arm, and return the **diameter 17 wavy washers** back to the left and right slide wheels.
