1. Follow 9.2, 9.3 and 9.4 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm and both side cases.
2. Take off the round disk cover using a tapering stick.
3. Remove the elliptical side cover round disk cover. Use a **12mm wrench** to remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 15mm** and **flat washer 5/16" x 35 x 1.5T** securing the cross bar. Use a **13 wrench** and loosen the outer bolt **M8 x 6.3T (steel level 10)**. Use a **#13 wrench and an M6 hex wrench** to loosen the inner bolt and remove the cross bar.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **8 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws with 1/4" x 19 flat washers** to separate the cross bar from the round disk cover.
5. To resume, secure the round disk on the cross bar and return it on the crank axle. Align the cross bar with the square hole, put the **7x7x25L Woodruff key** in the hole and tighten the **M8x40 socket head cap screw** together with two **M8 x 6.3T nuts** **until it reaches 500 Kg-cm**. Return and tighten the **5/16" x 15mm hex head bolt** with the **5/16" x 35 x 1.5T flat washer**.
6. Follow the above procedures to resume the connecting arm, pedal arm and both side cases with the round disk cover.
