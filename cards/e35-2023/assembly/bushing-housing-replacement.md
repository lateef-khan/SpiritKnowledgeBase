1. Follow 9.2 and 9.3 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, left and right side cases, cross bar and the belt.
2. Use a **12 mm wrench** to release the **5/16" x 15mm hex head bolt** with the **5/16" x 35 x 1.5T flat washer**, and the bushing housing can be released.
3. To resume, be sure that the **diameter 25 wave washer** is returned onto the shaft, then return the bushing housing onto the shaft and tighten with the **5/16" x 15mm hex head bolt** and **5/16" x 35 x 1.5T flat washer**.
4. Return the pedal arm and connecting arm.

**Step 4 points at sections that do not exist in this manual.** It reads "Follow procedures 11-2, 11-5 and 11-3, 11-4", which is the numbering of an older manual. In this manual the pedal arm is 9.3 and the connecting arm is 9.2.
