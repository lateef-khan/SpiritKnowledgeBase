**Work voltage: DC 4.5~7.5V.** It controls resistance increases and decreases.

Engineer Mode's SERVICE page has a **Motor Test** entry for fine-tuning and lifespan testing of this motor.

**This manual prints no meter procedure for the gear motor.** Unlike the 2023 E25 manual, it has no E2 section, no drive board output figures and no probe placement. If a technician needs those, they are in the keypad-console manuals, and they were written for a different driver board.
