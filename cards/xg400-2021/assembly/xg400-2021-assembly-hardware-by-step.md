## Two bolts of the same size with different item numbers

Item **42** and item **55** are both **M10 x 20MM**. Item 42 holds the console mast
down, four of them; item 55 is the **shoulder bolt** that pins the swing arm bracket
to the pedal arm, two of them, and the step calls it a shoulder bolt where the panel
does not. Do not swap them.

## The only inch fasteners in the build are the transport wheel bolts

Steps 1, 2, 4 and 5 are entirely metric - M5, M8 and M10. Step 3's **5/16" x 1-3/4"
bolt and 5/16" nut** are the only imperial parts on the machine, and they are the
same two the XE795 2021 uses for its transport wheels, as its own items 92 and 99.

## Step two supplies ten M5 screws for ten screw holes

Eight for the foot pedals and two for the water bottle holder - the panel's ten
close exactly. **Item 45 is M5 x 10mm and item 112 is M5 x 12mm**; the two are two
millimetres apart and are used in different steps.

## The levellers and their caps are hardware here, not parts

**Item 83, six levellers**, and **item 71, six round caps**, are in the step-five
hardware bag. On the XE195, XE295 and XE395ENT the six levellers are on the carton
parts list and no step fits them. See
[the levelling card](xg400-2021-assembly-levelling-six-levellers.md).

## No parts list to check any of this against

This manual prints no numbered parts list and no exploded view, so every item number
above exists only in these five panels and the step text.
