**Its cover calls this machine the XG400 E-Glide Trainer**, and so does its own step
five. It is the only machine of the eleven with a hand brake, and the only one whose
build ends by unlocking something.

## Step one - console mast, console and hand brake lever

1. Feed the **COMPUTER CABLE (95)** up through the console mast tube - pulse grip
   posts at the top - using the twist tie attached to it.
2. Place the console mast tube onto the mating plate of the main frame, without
   pinching the computer cable between the two plates.
3. Secure the mast with the 4 stainless steel **BOLTS (42)** and **SPLIT WASHERS
   (50)**, tightened with the **8mm Allen wrench**.
4. Fit the **CONSOLE (78)** to the bracket with 4 **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (112)**.
5. Fit the **HAND BRAKE LEVER (90)**: undo the screw threaded into the nut on the
   back of the hand brake, put the bracket around the **front of the right handlebar
   vertical tube**, put the bolt through the back of the bracket and thread it back
   into the nut.

**Four bolts and four split washers hold this mast on**, not the one-side-and-front
arrangement every other XE uses, and they take an 8mm Allen key rather than a wrench.

## Step two - swing arms, foot pedals and the bottle holder

1. Remove the foam from the console mast axles and the plastic ties from the tubes
   that slide onto them.
2. Slide the **TWO SWING ARMS (8 RIGHT & 9 LEFT)** onto the axle through a **WAVE
   WASHER (31)** each. They are marked R and L. **Do not force them or use a hammer** -
   the bearings can be damaged; the fit is snug because they were fitted at the
   factory.
3. Secure the arms with the stainless steel **bolts (40)** - **flat side of the
   washers on the outside** - and **CUP WASHERS (24)**. Snap the **DECORATIVE CAPS
   (67)** over the cup washers.
4. Fit the **FOOT PEDALS (72 LEFT & 73 RIGHT)** with **8 PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (45)**.
   They are marked L or R on the bottom.
5. Fit the **WATER BOTTLE HOLDER (66)** to the console mast tube with 2 **PHILLIPS
   HEAD SCREWS (45)**, putting the **BRAKE CABLE (90)** behind the holder to keep it
   out of the way.

## Step three - transport wheels

1. Attach the **TRANSPORT WHEELS (77)** to each bracket with the 2 **BOLTS (44)** and
   **NUTS (34)**. **Only partially tighten them** - over tightening stops the wheels
   turning.
2. Snap the 4 **END CAPS (67)** onto the cup washers on the left and right pedal arm
   assemblies.

## Step four - pedal arms to swing arms

Assemble the brackets at the bottom of the swing arms to the pedal arms with a
shoulder **BOLT (55)** and a **NUT (56)** on each side, tightened securely with the
two wrenches.

## Step five - levellers, end caps and the shipping lock

1. Tilt the machine to one side, support it, and install the **3 LEVELERS (83)** for
   that side; repeat on the other side. Level it once it is in its final position -
   see [the levelling card](xg400-2021-assembly-levelling-six-levellers.md).
2. Press the **6 PLASTIC END CAPS (71)** into the oval stabilizer tubes.
3. **Unlock the black lever near the flywheel at the end of the BRAKE CABLE (90).**
   It is locked for shipping, and it also serves as a safety lock to stop unauthorised
   use of the machine.

## The 2019 printing builds identically

The XG400 2019 manual prints the same five steps, the same five hardware panels, the
same item numbers and the same counts, word for word - hand brake, shipping lock and
all. The only difference anywhere in its assembly chapter is the machine diagram's
pulse label, **PULSE GRIPS** in 2019 against the 2021 book's stray-backtick
`PULSE SENSORS\``.

## The shipping lock is the last job of the build and nothing else mentions it

If the machine will not move, this lever is the first thing to check. **No other XE
elliptical has one**, and no other part of this manual explains it.

## Item 67 is two different parts in two steps

Step two calls item **67** a **DECORATIVE CAP** and snaps two of them over the cup
washers at the swing arms; step three calls item **67** an **END CAP** and snaps
**four** onto the cup washers on the pedal arms. Six of one item number in two
different places, under two names. Item **71**, the plastic end caps for the oval
tubes, is a different part again.
