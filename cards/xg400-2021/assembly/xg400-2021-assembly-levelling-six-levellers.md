1. **Tilt the machine to one side and put something under it for support**, then
   install the **3 LEVELERS (83)** for that side. Tilt it the other way and install
   the other three.
2. Level it **once it is standing where it will be used**.
3. **Screw the two middle levellers all the way in.**
4. **Screw the front and rear levellers all the way in, then back them out about two
   or three turns.**
5. Level the machine using **only the front and rear levellers first**.
6. Then adjust the middle levellers so they **touch** the floor.
7. **MAKE SURE TO LOCK THE LEVELERS WHEN FINISHED.**

## Six levellers, and this is the only XE that says where they go

Three per side, six in all, and the middle pair does a different job from the other
four - they are set last and only to touch the floor, not to carry the levelling. The
XE195, XE295, XE395 2010, XE395ENT and XE395 2023 all say to check "the four floor
levelers underneath the middle and rear" and never say how many are fitted or where.
The **XE795** builds fit six the same way, three a side, and give no setting order at
all.

## The manual names no wrench and no lock nut

It says "lock the levellers" without saying with what. There is no wrench number in
this job, and the carton's four tools are a 13/14mm wrench, a 12/14mm wrench, a
Phillips screwdriver and a combination M5 Allen and Phillips tool.

## The six end caps are a different part

Job 3 of the same step presses **6 PLASTIC END CAPS (71)** into the oval stabilizer
tubes. They are not levellers and they are a separate item number. Both sixes are in
the same step-five hardware bag.

## This is not the CE900 six-pad instruction

The commercial CE900 also has six levelling pads, adjusted with a **14mm wrench**,
and its manual prints that as a remedy in the noise item rather than as an assembly
job. Different machine, different method; see
[that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ce900-assembly-levelling-pads.md).
