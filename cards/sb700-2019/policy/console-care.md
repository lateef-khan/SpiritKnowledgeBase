Three rules for the Sole Indoor Cycling Console. Breaking any of them voids the warranty.

1. The console is a **sealed unit**. Open it only to fit batteries. Any opened unit voids the warranty.
2. Clean it with a **clean damp cloth**. Caustic cleaning solutions void the warranty.
3. The console is **not waterproof, only water resistant**. Excessive exposure to water voids the warranty.

The SB700 2020 and SB900 2020 manuals give the same three rules for their console, but rule 2 there says to spray an authorised cleaning or disinfectant solution and wipe dry. Do not use the spray method on this console.
