**If the belt slips:** follow section 3.3 and move the **idler axle (74)** downward by turning the **1/4" x 3"L hex head bolt (69) counterclockwise**. Tighten the **belt (40)** until belt tension reads **700 to 750 Newton**.

**If the belt falls off:** move the **idler axle (74)** upward by turning the **1/4" x 3"L hex head bolt (69) clockwise**, refit the **belt (40)** and adjust the tension. While adjusting, make sure the **6203 bearing (65)**, the **drive pulley (41)** and the **hub pulley (35)** line up straight. Run the belt for **5 minutes**, then refit the **outer chain cover (60)** if there is no problem.

The SB700 2020 manual carries this answer further, telling you to replace the idler axle or carriage bolt, and then the main frame. This manual stops at the 5 minute run.
