**700 to 750 Newton.**

To set it, move the **idler axle (74)** downward by turning the **1/4" x 3"L hex head bolt (69) counterclockwise** until the gauge reads 700 to 750 N.

The same figure is printed twice in this manual, in section 3.3 step 9 and again in the belt slippage answer in section 4.1. The SB700 2020 manual gives 1000 N for the belt slippage answer, so do not carry that figure to this machine.
