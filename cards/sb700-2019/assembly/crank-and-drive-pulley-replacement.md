1. Follow sections 3.1 and 3.3 to remove the **outer chain cover (60)** and the **belt (40)**.
2. Use the **14/15 m/m wrench (85)** to take the left **pedals (25)** apart.
3. Remove the **crank arm dust cap (44)** on the left **crank arm (42)**. Use a **14 m/m plug wrench** to release the **M10 x P1.25 x 7T nut (80)**, then take the left crank arm (42) off.
4. Use C-ring pliers to remove the **20 mm C ring (68)**, then take the **crank axle (45)**, **drive pulley (41)** and **pulley cover (63)** apart.
5. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **three M5 x 10 m/m tapping screws (93)** holding the **inner chain cover (61)**, then take it off.
6. Use C-ring pliers to release another **20 mm C ring (68)**.
7. Remove the **crank arm dust cap (44)** on the right **crank arm (43)**. Use a **14 m/m plug wrench** to release the **M10 x P1.25 x 7T nut (80)**, then take the right crank arm (43) off.
8. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew the **5 x 10 m/m tapping screw (90)** and take the **pulley cover (63)** off.
9. Use an **M5 Allen wrench** to unscrew the **5/16" x 1/2"L button head socket bolt (46)** and separate the **crank axle (45)** from the **drive pulley (41)**.
10. Reverse the steps to refit. Follow section 3.3 to set belt tension.

The manual's own table of contents spells this section "Clank Arms".
