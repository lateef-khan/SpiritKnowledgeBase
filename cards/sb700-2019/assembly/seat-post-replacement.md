1. Release the **8 x 40 m/m quick release lever (19)** to take the **seat post (5)** and the **sliding seat mount (7)** apart.
2. Release the **8 x 25 m/m quick release lever (20)** by hand and take it off. Separate the seat post (5) and the sliding seat mount (7).
3. Use a **14 m/m open wrench** to release the nut holding the **seat (27)**, then take the seat off.
4. Take the **8 x 40 m/m quick release lever (19)** and the **spring (14)** apart before you assemble the seat post (5).
5. Reverse the steps to refit.
