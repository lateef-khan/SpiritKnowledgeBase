1. Use the Phillips head screwdriver (86) to unscrew **four M6 x 15 mm Phillips head screws** and take the **flywheel fender (62)** off.
2. Take the **safety sleeve (96)** apart. Use a **30 m/m open wrench** to release the **34.5 x 15L-M30 x P1.25 nut (12)** together with the **brake tension knob (16)** and the **spring (94)**.
3. Use a hex wrench and the **14/15 m/m wrench (85)** to release the **M8 nylock nut** holding the **brake pad bracket (9)**, then take the bracket off.
4. Use the Phillips head screwdriver (86) to unscrew **two M5 x 10 m/m tapping screws (93)** and take the **leather brake pad (10)** off.
5. Reverse the steps to refit.
