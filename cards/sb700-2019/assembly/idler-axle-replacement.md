1. Follow sections 3.1 and 3.3 to remove the **outer chain cover (60)** and the **belt (40)**.
2. Use an **M6 hex wrench** to take the **idler axle (74)** apart.
3. Take the **idler adjustment carriage bolt (75)** apart together with the **1/4" x 3"L hex head bolt (69)**.
4. Use C-ring pliers to remove the **17 mm C ring (67)** and take the **6203 bearing** off the idler axle (74).
5. Reverse the steps to refit. Follow section 3.3 to set belt tension.
