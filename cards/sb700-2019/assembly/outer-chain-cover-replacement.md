1. Use the Phillips head screwdriver (86) to unscrew **two 5 x 16 m/m tapping screws (29)** and **three 3.5 x 12 m/m sheet metal screws**, then take the **outer chain cover (60)** off.
2. Reverse the step to refit the outer chain cover (60).

The manual does not give a part number for the three sheet metal screws in this section.

**The 2009 SB700 book (the sb700-2011 machine, filed as XIC600 / SB700, SB702) prints the same step:** two 5 x 16 m/m tapping screws (29) and three 3.5 x 12 m/m sheet metal screws, no part number for the three, with the combination M5 Allen wrench and Phillips head screwdriver (86).
