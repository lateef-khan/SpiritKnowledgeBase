1. Take the target post tube apart.
2. Put a Phillips head screwdriver into the holes at the bottom of the post tube to release the **end cap (56)**.
3. Put the screwdriver into the holes on the mainframe tube to take the **plastic slide insert (57)** apart.
4. Tap around the **bottom end cap (58)** to take it off.
