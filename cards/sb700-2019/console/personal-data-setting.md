1. **Gender.** Press the **left hand key** until SPEED is displayed, then press the **right hand key for 3 seconds**. The TIME digits flash. Press the **left hand key** until the gender icon blinks. Press the **right hand key** to select gender.
2. **Weight unit.** Press the **left hand key** to reach the measuring value for weight (Lb or Kg), then press the **right hand key** to select it.
3. **Bodyweight.** Press the **left hand key** to reach the bodyweight setting. Press the **right hand key** to raise the weight. Hold the right hand key for rapid advance.

The manual prints this block under a second "HEART RATE TARGET ZONES" heading. The steps are the personal data settings, not the heart rate zone.
