**To turn the heart rate alarm on:** press the **right hand key**. It only registers a reading if the chest strap is worn. The strap is sold separately. When the alarm is on, the alarm icon flashes and a beep sounds when the heart rate goes above or below the selected target zone.

**To clear the workout values:** press the **left** key until TIME is displayed, then **press and hold the right key** until the data resets. This clears exercise TIME, AVG SPEED, AVG PULSE, DISTANCE and KCAL.
