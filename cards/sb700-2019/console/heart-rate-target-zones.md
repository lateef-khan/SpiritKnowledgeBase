1. Press the **left hand key** until "SPEED" is displayed, then **hold the right hand key for three seconds**. The TIME digits flash.
2. Press the **left hand key** to select the heart rate target zone settings.
3. Press the **right hand key** to raise the **maximum** heart rate limit.
4. When the maximum is set, press the **left hand key** to adjust the **minimum** heart rate limit.
