| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| TIME | Measured in min:sec. There is a time of day clock and a workout timer. The workout time counts up or down while you pedal. When you stop pedalling it stops counting after 3 seconds. |
| RPM / CADENCE | How fast the cranks turn, in RPM. Approximate speed can also show in MPH or KPH. The RPM section also has a bar graph for tracking approximate RPM. |
| DISTANCE | Virtual distance travelled, based on riding a bike whose tyres are the same size as the flywheel. |
| KCAL | Approximate calories burned. An estimate only. |
| HEART RATE | Approximate heart rate from the chest strap, which is sold separately. |
