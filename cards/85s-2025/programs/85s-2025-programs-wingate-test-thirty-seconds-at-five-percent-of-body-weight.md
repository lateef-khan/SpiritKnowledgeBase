*"The industry standard test for measuring lower body power and fatigue rates. It can also be used
for anaerobic assessment."*

The Wingate typically involves **30 seconds of maximal exercise** on either an arm-crank or leg-cycle
ergometer. **Even though 30 seconds is the standard duration, protocols can range from 10 to 300
seconds** depending on the sport or event. At the U.S. Olympic Training Centers, Wingate tests are
predominantly used to assess cyclists: sprint track cyclists typically perform either a 30- or
18-second test, while road and endurance track cyclists usually perform a 30-second test.

## How the console runs it

- **Set the user's weight, and the force changes to 5% of the user's weight.** The force can also be
  changed manually.
- **Test time can be set to 15~300 seconds.**
- **There is a 5 minute warm-up before the test starts.** A button skips it, and another button
  changes the warm-up resistance.
- **A three second countdown begins the test after the warm-up is over.** Ask the user to pedal as
  fast as they can.
- The screen shows a **power-time chart** during the test, plus the remaining time and current
  watts. A button aborts the test.

## The protocol the page describes

Following a five-minute warm-up, **which includes three sprints at varying resistances**, the user
may get off the Stepper during a **three-minute recovery** or stay on and spin lightly. The user then
pedals as fast as possible with little or no resistance; **within three seconds a fixed resistance
is applied to the flywheel** and the user continues "all out" for the duration of the test.

**The resistance is a percentage of the user's body weight**, applied by adding a predetermined
amount of electromagnetic force. *"For example, a 70kg user with a load of 5% of his/her total body
weight would have 5.25kg added to the flywheel."*

*The manual's own example is arithmetically wrong: 5% of 70 kg is 3.5 kg, not 5.25 kg. 5.25 kg is
7.5% of 70 kg. Recorded as printed.*

**The 8.5S-Fit describes the same test with different figures** - a 75 g to 90 g per kg load rather
than 5% - and lists four named outputs this book does not. See
`85s-fit-2026-programs-wingate-test-inputs-and-outputs`.
