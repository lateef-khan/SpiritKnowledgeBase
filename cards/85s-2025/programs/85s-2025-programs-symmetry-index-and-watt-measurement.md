*"The Symmetry mode may aid in achieving a more balanced pedaling stroke for patients with lower
limb deficiencies, such as neurological patients and patients with limited lower body function."* It
measures left and right power through the cycle range and displays the watt measurements on screen,
with a graph, a watt value and a symmetry index.

**The Symmetry Index is the difference in percentage between left and right leg power. If the left
and right legs produce the same power the Symmetry Index will be 0%.**

The worked example: the screen shows the right leg producing more power than the left, **120 vs. 85
watts**, and displays the higher wattage of the right leg. A separate screenshot shows a
**Symmetry Index of 98%** with a watt measurement of **85**.

## For best results

**The Symmetry program starts at level 1 and the resistance needs to be increased manually.** *"Make
sure to set the resistance to a level where the patient is doing enough work to generate a
meaningful measurement. It is recommended to set the resistance as high as the patient can perform
without discomfort, but low enough so they can complete full pedal revolutions. **Very low resistance
settings result in erratic or inconsistent measurements.**"*

**Note: the watt readings are not force measurements.** *"The Symmetry program employs a proprietary
algorithm using a power table and velocity calculations to generate the watt readings; they are not
from direct force measurements."*

**Symmetry is a Features page item on this machine, not one of the eleven programs**, and it is
reached from the workout screen rather than from the program list - see
`85s-2025-programs-eleven-programs-on-the-touchscreen`.

**The dot matrix rehabilitation steppers report no Symmetry Index.** Their version is a Symmetry
*key* among the program keys, graphs the same left/right comparison on a dot matrix, and works its
example at **41 vs. 34 watts** in favour of the *left* leg - see
`spirit-rehab-stepper-programs-symmetry-measures-left-and-right-leg-power`. The 8.5S-Fit names
Symmetry only in its feature list and prints no description of it at all.
