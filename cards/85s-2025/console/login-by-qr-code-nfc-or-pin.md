**You cannot start until you have chosen a login method, and Guest is one of them.** The
console powers up on the **login method page**, and *"you may now begin to use the machine after
choosing the login method"*.

**Three ways to log in, plus guest:**

- **QR code** - the user scans the QR code with their own phone and logs in.
- **NFC** - the console has an **RFID reader**. Hold a phone or RFID tag to **the symbol at the
  bottom left of the console**.
- **Pin code** - **the clinician generates the pin code in the Dyaco Insight App** and gives it to
  the user. **The pin is good for one day.**
- **Guest** - *"Please make sure the WiFi connection is available, or you will enter the system as a
  guest in offline status."*

**A user can log in after the session instead.** *"You could also log in to the system to record your
exercise data after finishing the session if you didn't log in at the beginning."*

**First-time setup runs before any of this:** select the language, turn **WiFi** on, choose the
network and enter its password; then either **sign in to an organization account** with an email and
password, or **scan a QR code to create a new organization member's account**; then **register the
product** with the administrator's email and password and **the machine's serial number**; then set
the local **time and date** and press **Start** to finish.

**The clinician-side app is named once and never explained.** The **Dyaco Insight App** appears only
in the pin code bullet - no download link, no platform, no account instructions.

**Losing the Wi-Fi drops the user to guest.** The manual says so once, in the User log in panel, and
nowhere says what happens to a session recorded offline.
