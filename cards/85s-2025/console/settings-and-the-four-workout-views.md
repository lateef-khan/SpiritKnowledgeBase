**Settings** is reached from the status bar and holds, in the manual's own list: **Beacon
feature setting, language, time & date, WiFi, Standby or Pause mode, and units of measurement**.
The screenshot shows **Units** offered as **Metric** or **Imperial**.

**The workout screen has four modes and one icon steps between them:** **Status**, **Diagram**,
**Symmetry** and **Controls**. *"You may also switch the mode between Status, Diagram, Symmetry and
Controls by pressing the icon."*

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | View the value of the current parameters |
| **Diagram** | Display the selected parameter's progress chart |
| **Symmetry** | The left/right power comparison |
| **Controls** | Adjust the parameter |

**Select the parameter to display on the screen** first, then press Diagram or Controls for it. The
readings the screenshot shows are **Time Elapsed, Workload, Speed (rpm), Power (watts), Calories and
Heart Rate (bpm)**.

**Close Up Mode** is a separate toggle that **changes the appearance of the screen** - the manual
says no more than that, and does not say what it enlarges.

**During the programs you can scroll through the data in the display.**

**Quick Start needs a login first.** *"After the console powers up **and user is logged in**, just
press the Start key to begin"*; the time counts up from zero and the workload is adjusted **by
sliding to the desired number** or with the **Up and Down keys**
(`85s-2025-console-login-by-qr-code-nfc-or-pin`).

**Pausing:** press **STOP** to pause or stop the exercise; the pause screen offers **Resume** to
continue the session and **Finish** to end it.

**The function keys under the screen are +/- for resistance, START & PAUSE (one touch start, then
pause and touch start to resume) and ENTER to confirm each setting.**

**The console is a 10" touch panel** (call-out A on printed p. 12), on an articulating arm with
vertical and horizontal adjustment. **The 8.5S-Fit's is 15.6" and its screens are laid out
differently** - do not answer one from the other
(`85s-fit-2026-console-workout-views-and-finishing-with-two-stop-presses`).

**The service manual's Settings screenshot lists more than the owner's manual's sentence.** Its
panel reads **Progress Beacon, Acceleration & Deceleration, Language, Time & Date, WiFi, Standby,
Pause Mode, Account, Units (Metric / Imperial)** and **Software**, the last opening the Android,
Firmware, LCB and Console App versions with their update buttons
(`85s-2025-console-firmware-lcb-and-console-app-update-from-a-usb-root-or-over-wifi`). Acceleration
& Deceleration and Account are named nowhere in the owner's manual. The troubleshooting menu is a
separate Maintenance Mode behind the status bar
(`85s-2025-console-maintenance-mode-on-the-wifi-icon-once-and-the-clock-six-times`).
