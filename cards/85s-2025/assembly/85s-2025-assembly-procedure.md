Numbers in parentheses are assembly-drawing item numbers. **Twelve steps, printed on
nine pages** - steps one and two share a page, so do four and five and seven and eight.

## Steps one and two - the arms

1. Install the **left arm** onto the main frame with **two split washers (195) and one
   bolt (187)** on one side of the bottom of the arm, using the **6mm Allen wrench
   (167)**, and the same on the other side of the same arm.
2. Install the **washer (71), flat washer (184) and bolt (140)** on the left arm.
3. **Tighten these bolts very securely so the arm does not loosen during use.**
4. Attach the **cup (58)** on the bottom of the left arm.
5. Install the **right arm** in the same manner.

Hardware: 71 washer Ø9 x Ø49 x 1.5T (2), 140 bolt M8 x P1.25 x 20L (2), 187 bolt M10 x
16mm (4), 195 split washer Ø10 x 2T (8), 184 flat washer Ø8 x 35 x 3T (2).

## Step three - the console mast

**Keep tension on the computer cables** and pull the slack out as you work, so they are
not pinched between the tubes or caught between the main frame and the mast. Snake the
cables through the mast and install the mast onto the main frame with **four bolts (140),
four split washers (127) and four flat washers (125)**. **The cables must exit the top
opening and stay on the outside of the top of the mast for the whole of the rest of the
build** - the manual says so three times in one step.

## Steps four and five - the top covers

Attach the **front top cover (76)** and then the **rear top cover (77)** onto the main
cover.

## Step six - the console articulating arm

1. Connect the connectors **(89, 91, 104)** to **(88, 90, 103)** between the console mast
   and the console articulating arm.
2. Mount the arm onto the mast with **five slotted set screws (150), M5 x 5L**, using the
   **2.5mm Allen wrench (169)**. **Do not tighten them firmly** - back each off a quarter
   turn, and adjust for a proper swivel if the arm feels too tight.

## Steps seven and eight - the console

Plug the connectors (89, 91, 104) from the top of the articulating arm into the console.
Install the **console (86)** onto the arm and attach the **leather cover (622)** with
**two bolts (623), M5 x 10L**, at the top right and top left, then two more at the bottom
right and bottom left, using the **Phillips screwdriver (170)**. **The leather cover
should not be in between.**

## Step nine - the yellow cover

Attach the **yellow cover (176)** and the leather cover (622) to the articulating arm
with **two screws (134), 3.5 x 10 mm**.

## Step ten - the seat back tube, mast cover, bottle holder and arm pad

1. Install the **seat back tube (508)** into the rear part of the **seat set (509)** with
   **two bolts (587) 3/8" x 1-3/4", two flat washers (588) and two nuts (610)**, using the
   **8mm Allen wrench (611)** and the **13/14mm wrench (612)**.
2. Slide the **mast cover (551)** onto the seat back tube and down onto the main body of
   the seat (553).
3. Attach the **bottle holder (550)** to the right of the seat by snapping it into the
   locking sockets under the right support arm (510).
4. Install the **support arm pad** onto the **support arm (536)** with **four bolts (615),
   M6 x 12L**, using the **5mm Allen wrench (613)**.

## Step eleven - the seat back

Install the **seat back (537)** onto the seat back tube with **four bolts (609), M8 x
20L**, then snap the **seat back cover (552)** into the locking sockets on the seat back.

## Step twelve - the seat onto the frame

1. Install the **rear seat set** to the front main frame with **two bolts (606) M8 x
   P1.25 x 60L, two flat washers (608) and two nuts (607)**, using both the 5mm Allen
   wrench (613) and the open wrench (612), **firmly**.
2. Install **two bolts (605), M8 x P1.25 x 20L**, on the right and left of the front main
   frame, **firmly**. Its table counts **four** of item 605.

**The seat goes on last, and it comes off the same way.** This is the machine whose
feature list says "the removable seat provides accessibility - roll the removable seat
away to allow for direct wheelchair access". Step twelve is the joint that lets it.

## What the build never does

No step fits a levelling glide or a moving wheel, though the parts diagram labels both -
**P. Moving wheels** and **Q. Leveling glides**. Step twelve is followed by SETTING UP
YOUR STEPPER, which covers plugging in, levelling and the seat adjustments. See
[the levelling card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-85s-assembly-levelling-glides-by-hand.md).

## The tools are named in the steps and promised nowhere

Six of them - a 2.5mm, 5mm, 6mm and 8mm Allen key, a 13/14mm wrench and a Phillips
screwdriver. There is no TOOLS INCLUDED panel. See
[that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-rehab-stepper-assembly-no-tools-panel.md).
