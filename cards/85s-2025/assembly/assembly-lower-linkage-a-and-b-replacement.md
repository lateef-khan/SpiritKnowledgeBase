1. As shown in figures 6.6.1 and 6.6.2, loosen the screw to replace the **lower linkage**.
2. As shown in figures 6.6.3 and 6.6.4, loosen the screw to replace **Lower Linkage A**.
3. As shown in figures 6.6.5 and 6.6.6, loosen the screw to replace **Lower Linkage B**.
4. Install the lower linkage, lower linkage A and lower linkage B in reverse order following figures 6.6.1 to 6.6.6.

No screw sizes are printed. The 7.5S book names its three as lower, handle bar and upper linkage and nests them;
this book does not say one must come off before another. The linear slider procedure starts by sending you here.
