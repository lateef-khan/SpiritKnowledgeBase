1. As shown in figure 6.2.1, remove the screws to replace the part.
2. Install the handlebar in reverse order following figures 6.2.2 to 6.2.8.

That is the whole printed procedure: one removal picture and seven refit pictures, no screw size, no count and
no torque. The component drawing names **Handle Bar (R)** and **Handle Bar (L)** as items 6 and 7.

If the handlebar wobbles while pedalling, the troubleshooting chapter (7-2) says to tighten the bolts in figure
7.2.1 and, if it persists, to check that the quick-release lever on the handle slider is tightened and in the
locked position. The preventative-maintenance page says to check both handlebars for looseness.
