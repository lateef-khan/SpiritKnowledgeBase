**Drive belt tension 80 N**, one belt. The 7.5S has two belts at 320 N and 240 N; do not carry those figures
across.

1. As shown in figure 6.9.1, use an **8 mm hex wrench** to turn the screw **counterclockwise** to loosen the idler
   pulley.
2. Figure 6.9.2 shows the drive before the belt is replaced and figure 6.9.3 after.
3. Install the drive belt in reverse order following figures 6.9.1 to 6.9.3. As shown in figure 6.9.1, adjust
   the tightness of the bolt to set the drive belt tension: **80 N**.

The troubleshooting chapter (7-1) says a slipping belt is tightened clockwise on the same screw; if it still
slips, the one-way bearing in the steel cable slide wheel is suspect. The flywheel procedure starts by loosening
this idler.
