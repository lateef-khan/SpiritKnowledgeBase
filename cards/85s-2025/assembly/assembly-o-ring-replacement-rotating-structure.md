Loosen the **four screws securing the Console Mast** (figure 7.3.1), then pull out the **Rotating Structure
Assembly** (figure 7.3.2). After disconnecting the **three wire connectors**, the O-ring can be removed (figures
7.3.3 and 7.3.4).

The book files this under Troubleshooting as "7-3 O-ring Broken", but it is a replacement: there is no diagnosis,
only the disassembly. No refit step and no O-ring size are printed. The rotating structure is item 5 on the
component drawing and the console mast (upper) is item 4.
