1. As shown in figures 6.3.1 and 6.3.2, **loosen the screw by turning it counterclockwise**.
2. Install the pedal in reverse order following figures 6.3.1 and 6.3.2.

No screw size is printed and no short screwdriver is called for, unlike the 7.5S. The preventative-maintenance
page says to check whether the pedals are loose.
