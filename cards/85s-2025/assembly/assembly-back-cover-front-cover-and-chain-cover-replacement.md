1. Use a **flat wrench** and insert it into the latch shown in figure 6.4.1. Pry it upward from bottom to top to
   push the **rear upper cover** upward. Then follow figures 6.4.2 and 6.4.3 to remove the **back cover**.
2. As shown in figure 6.4.4, **pull the front cover outward** to remove it.
3. As shown in figure 6.4.5, loosen the screws on the **Chain Covers (L and R)**. As shown in figures 6.4.6 and
   6.4.7, first loosen the front and rear screws securing the left Chain Cover (L). Then pull the outer housing
   outward (figure 6.4.8) to remove the left Chain Cover (L).
4. Install the chain covers in reverse order following figures 6.4.1 to 6.4.8.

The book numbers its steps 1, 2, 2, 4. No screw sizes or counts are printed. The 7.5S's covers are a different
set - console mast cover, a top cover on four latches, and left and right shrouds with a DC connector inside.
The troubleshooting chapter still says "open the left shroud", a name this book's component drawing does
not use; the left chain cover is the only side cover it has.
