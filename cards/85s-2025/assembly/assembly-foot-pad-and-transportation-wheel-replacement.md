1. As shown in figure 6.11.1, **turn the footpad counterclockwise** to remove and replace it. There are four foot
   pads underneath the machine.
2. As shown in figure 6.11.2, remove the **nylon nut and the bolt** to replace the transportation wheel.

No sizes are printed. Setting the levelling glides by hand is the owner's-manual card; the 7.5S removes its
rubber feet by an iron nut instead.
