**6-1-1** As shown in figures 6.1.1 and 6.1.2, remove the **2 screws at the bottom of the rear tube cover**, then
take off the rear tube cover (figure 6.1.3).

**6-1-2** As shown in figures 6.1.4 and 6.1.5, remove the **4 screws underneath the console**, then disconnect the
wire connectors to remove the console.

Install the console in reverse order following figures 6.1.1 to 6.1.5.

No screw sizes and no connector count are printed. The 7.5S has no rear tube cover step - four screws and one
connector. The O-ring under the rotating console structure is reached from the console mast, not from here.
