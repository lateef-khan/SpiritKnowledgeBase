1. Before replacing the flywheel, first loosen the idler pulley (figure 6.9.1), then remove the drive belt. Use a
   tool to loosen the **four screws securing the flywheel** (figures 6.10.1 and 6.10.2). The flywheel can then be
   replaced.

No screw size and no refit step are printed; the belt goes back at 80 N as the drive belt card says. The 7.5S
service manual has no flywheel procedure.
