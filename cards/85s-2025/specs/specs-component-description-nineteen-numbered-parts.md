The **Rotating Structure Assembly (5)** is the pivot under the console that lets the console mast
turn - the O-ring replacement page pulls it out after the four console-mast screws. Items 14 and
15 are the two yellow levers on the seat: **Seat Rotation Release Lever (14)** swivels the seat,
**Seat Release Lever (15)** unlocks it along the rail; the **Adjusting Rod (16)** is the seat-back
angle rod, and **Seat Front Rear Adjusting (19)** the fore-aft slide at the end of the rail.

**No part numbers, quantities or dimensions are printed here**; the 8.5S's specification page is
in the owner's manual (`spirit-climber-85s-specs-dimensions-and-weight`). The 7.5S book numbers
ten components on its older platform (`7-5s-med-specs-component-description-ten-numbered-parts`).
