**An electromagnet, not a permanent magnet, and no gear motor.** The manual states
the mechanism in full:

> When the workload change is requested, and electromagnet is energized at field
> strengths relative to the workload requested. The magnetic field generated by
> the coil is induced into a flywheel creating eddy currents to flow in the
> flywheel. The eddy currents then create an opposing magnetic field to the
> electromagnetic field creating more or less resistance at the pedals and arms,
> determined by workload setting by the operator.

(The "and electromagnet" is the manual's own wording; it means "an
electromagnet".)

**Nothing moves to change the resistance.** The coil current changes and the
braking changes with it, which is why this machine can offer a **constant**
resistance mode alongside the isokinetic one, and fifty levels rather than twenty
- see `spirit-climber-specs-constant-and-isokinetic-fifty-levels`.

The programs chapter says the same thing a second way, describing the Wingate
test: "The resistance is applied to the flywheel by adding a predetermined amount
of electromagnetic force."

## The 7.0S, 7.5S and MS300 brake the opposite way

Those three swing **four permanent Braking Magnets** against an **Aluminum Brake
Disc** with a **Gear Motor** - see
`spirit-climber-specs-rehabilitation-stepper-brake-magnets-on-a-disc`. **Do not
carry their part names onto an 8.5S.** The 8.5S manual prints no parts list at
all, so it names no brake component and no item number - see
`spirit-climber-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.

## The 8.5S-FIT is deliberately not on this card

The 8.5S-FIT-2026 manual prints **no Operating Principle section and no
Application Specification section**. Its specification page gives the same
`Constant and Isokinetic with 50 levels of effort` resistance row as the 8.5S, so
it is very likely the same brake - but **its book never says so**, and the two
differ on the power supply row. Say the 8.5S-FIT manual does not describe its
resistance mechanism rather than quoting this paragraph for it.

## An eddy-current brake is not an induction brake card

Spirit's ENT ellipticals and bikes also brake by induction and have their own
cards with their own parts. This machine is a rehabilitation stepper on a medical
platform; do not serve `ce800ent-flywheel-and-induction-brake` for it.
