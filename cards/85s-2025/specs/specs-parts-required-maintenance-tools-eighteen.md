**The 8.5S (MS2000-SB036-01) service manual is the only Spirit service manual that prints a
numbered tool list, and it runs to eighteen items.** The page is headed *4-5 List of Required
Maintenance Tools* and reads, in the book's order:

| No. | Tool Name |
|---|---|
| 1 | Multimeter |
| 2 | Cordless Impact Driver Set |
| 3 | Phillips Screwdriver |
| 4 | Diagonal Cutting Pliers |
| 5 | Needle-nose Pliers |
| 6 | Electric Soldering Iron |
| 7 | Solder Wire |
| 8 | Anti-static Tweezers |
| 9 | Heat Shrink Tubing |
| 10 | Utility Knife |
| 11 | Hex Key Set (Allen Wrench Set) |
| 12 | Socket Wrench Set |
| 13 | Cable Ties (Minimum Length: 20 cm) |
| 14 | Electrical Insulation Tape |
| 15 | Lead Wire (for guiding cable through tubing) |
| 16 | Insulated Gloves |
| 17 | Anti-static Wrist Strap |
| 18 | Crank Removal Tool |

**Only one size is printed on the whole list** - the cable ties, minimum 20 cm. No hex-key
size, no socket size and no multimeter range is given here; the sizes the procedures actually
use are inside the procedures (an 8 mm hex wrench for the idler pulley, a flat wrench to pry
the back-cover latch).

**Six of the eighteen are consumables or protective gear, not tools** - solder wire, heat shrink
tubing, cable ties, insulation tape, insulated gloves and the anti-static wrist strap - and
the list makes no distinction. Read it as "have these on the bench", not "carry these".

**A crank removal tool is listed and no crank procedure is printed.** The replacement chapter
covers the console, handlebars, pedals, covers, steel cable, linkages, linear slider, slide
wheel, drive belt, flywheel, foot pads and transport wheels; nothing in it pulls a crank. The
Maintenance Mode's *Crank Calibration* item puts the right crank at 6 o'clock but does not
remove it.

**This is not the one-multimeter page** the other Spirit stepper and rower service manuals print
(`spirit-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`), and it is not the
six-photograph page of the stair climber books
(`spirit-climber-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-six-tools`). It shares with them only
the multimeter and the soldering iron.
