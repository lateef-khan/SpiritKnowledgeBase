There are **2 SwitchPro knobs at the front bottom of the main frame**, marked M and L:

1. **Moving**: to move the machine, turn both knobs **clockwise** (M).
2. **Leveling**: to level the frame for operation, turn the knob **counterclockwise** (L).

The drawing shows the knob above a foot pad; the companion drawing shows a wheel at the rear of the frame beside the power inlet and switch. **The book prints no folding instructions** - a loose search for *fold*, *wheel*, *transport* and *move* finds only this page, the storage-box assembly steps and the boilerplate cord-routing line - and its post-workout routine omits the "store folded" step the other new-layout books print (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-post-workout-care`).

**The TR75H's page reads the opposite way round**: its adjusting tips are turned counterclockwise to move and clockwise to level (`tr75h-2025-maintenance-moving-and-leveling-adjusting-tips`). The page was read from the render; the text layer is overprinted with assembly-step text that is not on the page. The care schedule's daily row, "adjust balancer to make it stably positioned on the ground", refers to this knob (`xterra-tr-maintenance-care-schedule-daily-to-six-monthly`).
