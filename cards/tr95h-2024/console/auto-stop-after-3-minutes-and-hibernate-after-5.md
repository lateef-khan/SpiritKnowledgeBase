From the book's "Other Instructions":

- If the machine **stops operation for 20 minutes**, the system is reset.
- When the machine starts but **no user runs on the deck for 3 minutes**, the machine stops; 20 minutes after that the whole system is reset.
- In standby mode, if the machine is **not operated for 5 minutes**, the computer screen **hibernates**. Press **START** to wake it up — the button list says the START key is "for starting the machine or waking up the sleeping mode".

Switching between the five heart-rate zones while running is also listed there: switching from a low to a high zone keeps speed and incline and lifts the HR target; switching from high to low cuts speed and incline by 50% and lowers the target (the heart-rate programs themselves belong to the Programs section).
