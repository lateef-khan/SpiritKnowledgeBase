For the heart-rate programs, wear a transmitter (**sold separately**) or hold both hands on the **pulse plates** on both handlebars to detect the heartbeat. If the computer does not detect a heartbeat for **20 continuous seconds**, the machine stops and **NO PULSE** shows on the console. The Fat program prints the same NO PULSE message when the plates give no reading.

How to measure your resting heart rate, as the book prints it: on the day after a rest day, after getting up in the morning, drink 250-300 cc of water, then stand and check your pulse for 3 continuous minutes.

The PULSE window's ranges are on [the windows card](windows-and-ranges-in-running-and-climbing-mode.md); the heart-rate zone programs themselves belong to the Programs section; wearing the strap is on [the strap card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-chest-strap-not-included-with-a-cr2032-battery.md).
