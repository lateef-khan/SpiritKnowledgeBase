The owner's manual (54 pages, Rev. 01/19/24) and the JKEXER 337 service manual (17 pages, September 2024) print **no engineering mode, no calibration procedure and no way to change the speed unit**. The owner's manual lists "Speed Unit: KM or MILE (Default Setting KM)" among the console features and nothing more; the service manual covers assembly sequence, belt and deck replacement, rollers, the controller group, the motor, the incline motor, wiring, the PCB sockets and three error messages.

This was checked with loose searches for "eng", "calib", "factory", "wheel", "metric" and "kilomet" in both extractions, a spacing-tolerant search, and a word count of every page of the owner's manual; the pages under 25 words are the blank page 2, the parts drawing on 11, the console overview on 20 and the exploded drawing on 49, all of which have been rendered and read.

The 2025 sibling, the TR75H, does have an engineering mode in its own service manual ([TR75H engineering card](../../tr75h-2025/console/engineering-mode-enter-and-start-units-and-total-time-reset.md)); nothing says it applies to this machine.
