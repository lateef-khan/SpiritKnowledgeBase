To start operation, press either the **Running Mode** or the **Climbing Mode** button. Then press **Start** for quick start (or press Incline to choose a user profile U0-U3 for program operation, which the Programs section covers).

In quick start, press Incline Up/Down and Speed Up/Down to adjust:

| Mode | Speed | Incline |
|---|---|---|
| Running | 0.5 – 7.5 mph | Level 0 – 15 |
| Climbing | 0.5 – 3.7 mph | Level 15 – 40 |

"Lap 00" appears on the left and one dot flashes on the right side of the LCD. One dot represents **8 meters (0.00497 miles)**; one circle represents **400 m (0.25 miles)**. When 00 jumps to 01 you have run 400 meters.

You may switch from Running Mode to the quick start of Climbing mode, or from Climbing to Running, at any time. The Cool Down button, the auto-stop timers and the console windows are on their own cards ([cool down](cool-down-button-drops-speed-to-2-mph.md), [timers](auto-stop-after-3-minutes-and-hibernate-after-5.md), [windows](windows-and-ranges-in-running-and-climbing-mode.md)).
