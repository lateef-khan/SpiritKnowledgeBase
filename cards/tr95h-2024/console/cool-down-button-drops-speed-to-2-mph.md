1. After pressing **Cool Down**, the incline lowers to the lowest level and the speed lowers to **2 kph / 2.0 mph**.
2. Time, Calories and Distance keep accumulating.
3. Pressing Cool Down again resumes the original program.

**Step count**, printed on the same page: the machine detects and accumulates total walking steps toward the user's own count goal. Counting starts about 20 seconds after the user steps on and walks around 20-30 steps. Accuracy is lower for people who walk lightly; for users over 100 lb walking 1.2-3.7 mph with stable steps it is around 97%.
