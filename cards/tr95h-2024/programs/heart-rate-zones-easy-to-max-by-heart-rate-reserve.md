**Zones, as a percentage of heart rate reserve (HRR):**

| Intensity | HRR |
|---|---|
| Easy | 59 to 74% |
| Marathon | 74 to 84% |
| Threshold | 84 to 88% |
| Interval | 88 to 95% |
| Max | 95 to 100% |

**Target Heart Rate Zone = (MHR - RHR) x HRR% + RHR**, where **MHR = 220 - age for a male,
226 - age for a female**, and RHR is the resting heart rate you keyed in. Easy is the lowest
and Max the highest zone.

**How to detect your RHR accurately, as printed:** on the day after a rest day, after getting
up in the morning, drink 250 to 300 cc of water, then stand and check your pulse for 3
continuous minutes.

**How the machine regulates** (wear a transmitter, sold separately, or hold both pulse
plates):

- Heart rate **lower** than target: speed rises **0.2 km** per detection. At the highest speed
  (printed as "10 kph") the incline rises 1 level per detection. At the highest speed and
  incline 40 with the heart rate still low, the machine keeps running at the maximum.
- Heart rate **higher** than target: speed drops 0.2 km per detection; at the lowest speed the
  incline drops 1 level; at the lowest speed and incline the **machine stops automatically**.
- **No heartbeat for 20 continuous seconds: the machine stops and shows NO PULSE.**

**Switching zones mid-workout:** low to high keeps the speed and incline and lifts the HR
target; high to low cuts speed and incline by 50% and lowers the target.

**Inconsistencies in the book:** the HR program range is given as 0.5 to 6.2 mph, the
regulation text says the highest speed is "10 kph" (6.2 mph), while the console's running
range is 12 kph; and the incline ceiling of 40 applies to Climbing mode only. The 220-minus-age
training-zone page elsewhere in the same book uses 60% and 80% of MHR with no resting heart
rate; see `see_also`.
