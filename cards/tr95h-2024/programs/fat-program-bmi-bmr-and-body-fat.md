1. Press Incline or Speed up/down to choose the **FAT** program.
2. Press **Enter** and then **Start** to begin detecting. **About 12 seconds later** the computer
   shows your **BMI, BMR, FAT% and BODY TYPE**. Hold both hands on the pulse plates; the LCD
   shows **NO PULSE** if no heartbeat is detected. The data are for reference only and not for
   medical purposes.

**BMI (Body Mass Index)** = weight in kg / (height in m)^2; the book's example: 60 kg and
165 cm gives BMI 22 = 60 / (1.65 x 1.65). BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat.

| BMI (kg/m2) | |
|---|---|
| Underweight | BMI < 18.5 |
| Normal | 18.5 <= BMI < 25 |
| Overweight | 25 <= BMI < 30 |
| Moderately Obese | 30 <= BMI < 35 |
| Severely Obese | 35 <= BMI < 45 |
| Very Severely Obese | 40 <= BMI |

**The last two rows overlap as printed** (35 to 45 and 40 upward); the book gives no
resolution.

**BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate)** - the energy needed to maintain basal metabolism, from sex,
height, weight and age:

- Male = 66 + (13.7 x weight kg) + (5 x height cm) - (6.8 x age)
- Female = 655 + (9.6 x weight kg) + (1.7 x height cm) - (4.7 x age)

**Body type 1 to 5:** 1 Skinny, 2 Underweight, 3 Normal, 4 Overweight, 5 Extremely
Overweight.

**Fat %:**

| | Normal | Overweight | Extremely overweight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Male | 10 to 20% | 20 to 25% | over 25% |
| Female | 15 to 25% | 25 to 35% | over 35% |
