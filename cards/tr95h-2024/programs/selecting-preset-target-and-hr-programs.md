After pressing **Running** or **Climbing Mode**, press **Start** for a quick start, or press
**Incline up/down** to choose **U0 to U3** and key in user data for program operation.

**For program operation**

1. Press Incline up/down to choose U0, U1, U2 or U3.
2. Press **Enter**, then Speed up/down to key in gender, age, height, weight and resting heart
   rate (RHR).
3. Press Enter to confirm.
4. Press Start to begin, or press Incline up/down or Speed up/down to choose a Preset, Target
   or HR program.

**Preset program**

1. Press Incline or Speed up/down to choose **P1 to P12** for Running (**P1 to P5** for
   Climbing).
2. Press Enter; **L1** flashes; choose **L1 to L3** (no difficulty level in Climbing mode).
3. Press Enter; the default time **30** flashes; set the desired workout time.
4. Press Start.

**Target program**

1. Choose target distance, time or calories.
2. Press Enter, then set the target value.
3. Press Start.

**Heart rate (HR) program** (0.5 to 6.2 mph and level 0 to 15 in Running; 0.5 to 2.5 mph and
level 15 to 40 in Climbing)

1. Press **Max, Interval, Threshold, Marathon or Easy**. The LCD shows the heart rate zone;
   then choose Program or Start.
2. Press Enter and Incline or Speed up/down to set the workout time.
3. Press Start. The computer compares your heart rate with the set heart rate and adjusts
   speed and incline as described on the heart-rate zones card.
