The console has two modes, chosen with the **Running Mode** and **Climbing Mode** buttons.

| Mode | Speed and incline range | Programs |
|---|---|---|
| **Running** | 0.8 to 12 kph / 0.5 to 7.5 mph, incline level 0 to 15 | **12 presets with Difficult Level 1 to 3**; 3 targets (time, distance, calories); HR programs (Max, Interval, Threshold, Marathon, Easy) at 0.5 to 6.2 mph and level 0 to 15; FAT |
| **Climbing** | 0.8 to 6 kph / 0.5 to 3.7 mph, incline level 15 to 40 | **5 presets** (no difficulty level); 3 targets; HR programs at 0.5 to 2.5 mph and level 15 to 40; FAT |

**Defaulted data:** Gender Male, Age 30, Height 67 in, Weight 154 lb, Time 30 minutes,
Resting Heart Rate 72 bpm, Target Distance 1.0 mile, Target Calories 10 kcal, Target Time
30 minutes.

**Users U0 to U3:** the Incline keys choose U0 to U3 to key in personal data (gender, age,
height, weight, resting heart rate). U0 takes data but has no memory; U1, U2 and U3 remember
three users' data, deleted by pressing and holding Enter for 3 seconds.

The Program window shows Running Mode 1 to 12 or Climbing Mode 1 to 5, and can also show BMI
and U0 to U3; the Incline window shows the Difficult Level 1 to 3. You may switch from
Running Mode to a Quick Start of Climbing mode, or from Climbing to Running, at any time.

The preset profiles are printed as numeric tables (four cards), the heart-rate zones, the
FAT program and Cool Down mode each have a card, and the selection steps are on a card of
their own.
