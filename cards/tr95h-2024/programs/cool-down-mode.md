1. After pressing **Cool Down**, the incline lowers to the lowest level and the speed lowers to
   **2 kph / 2.0 mph** (as printed; 2 kph and 2.0 mph are not the same speed and the book
   does not say which applies in each unit setting).
2. Time, calories and distance **keep accumulating**.
3. **Pressing Cool Down again resumes the original program.**

The button description adds that Cool Down lowers speed and incline "instantly".
