7. Use **2 plates (18)** and **screws (16, 5/32" x 1/2")** to fix the **storage box (4)** on the **desk set (5)**
   from underneath. The drawing shows two screws per plate.
8. Fix the storage box on the desk set with **screw (23, Ø4 x 10 mm)** from the hole underneath as shown.
   Connect **wire (113)** from the computer with **wire (110)** from the **safety key box (9)** as shown.

The safety key box (9) is not in the parts table - it arrives fitted under the desk set, with its
tether drawn hanging below. Tool drawn: the screwdriver/wrench (21). The PDF's ghost text of the
older edition says "Screw (16)" for step 8 where the printed page says screw (23).
