11. Ask someone to hold the **right handlebar (6)**; connect **wire (102)** from the assembled desk set with
    **wire (123)** from the right handlebar. Tuck the connector back into the handlebar. Be sure not to
    damage the wire or the connector.
12. Attach the bottom end of the right handlebar to the right side of the desk set with **parts 13 (M8 x
    15 mm bolts) and 15 (M8 washers)** as shown, **but do not tighten the bolts yet**.
13. Fix the middle section of the right handlebar onto the right side of the desk set and firmly
    secure it with **parts 10 (M8 x 45 mm bolt) and 17 (spacer)**. **Now tighten the parts 13 and 15**
    from step 12 securely. Follow the same procedure to fix the **left handlebar (7)** on the left side
    of the desk set, and tighten its bolts accordingly.
14. Plug in the **power cable (22)** on the **main switch located on the right side of the main frame (1)**.

The drawing labels the left handlebar's wire **103** to **119** where the text only names the right
side's 102 to 123. The printed step 13 sentence refers to "the parts (13, 15) on Step 13" - it means
the bolts left loose at step 12. Step 14 is the end of assembly; the setting-up page (moving,
levelling, powering on) follows.
