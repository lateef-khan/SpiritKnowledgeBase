There are **2 SwitchPro knobs at the front bottom of the main frame**.

1. **Moving:** to move the machine, turn both knobs **clockwise**.
2. **Leveling:** to level the frame for operation, turn the knob **counterclockwise**.

The drawing labels the knob's two positions **M** (moving) and **L** (leveling) with a curved arrow
between them. **The TR75H Hiker turns the opposite way** - counterclockwise to move, clockwise to level -
so do not carry this card's directions across.
