Chapter 1 of the JKEXER 337 service manual is five sub-steps, each ending with the same precaution:
**push the excess wire length into the tube so the signal cables are not compressed and damaged.**

1. **1-1 Front post.** Connect the signal cables on the **right** front post first, then insert the excess
   wire into the tube.
2. **1-2 Console, storage box and desktop rack.** Connect the cables; **put the storage box onto the
   console stand first**; place the console stand and storage box assembly on the desktop rack at the
   same time (insert the excess wire into the tube); fix the console stand assembly with its screws.
3. **1-3 Storage box.** Turn the desktop assembly over and lock the storage box from behind.
4. **1-4 Desktop assembly onto the front posts.** Connect the signal cable; place the desktop assembly on
   the front post and lock it with screws - tuck the excess wire in first.
5. **1-5 Left/right upper handrails.** Connect the left and right fast-key and hand-pulse signal cables;
   fix the handrails with screws - excess wire into the tube first.

**The order differs from the owner's manual**, which installs the computer set on the desk set (step 4)
and only then mounts the storage box on top (step 5). The service manual gives no screw sizes or
counts; the owner's manual's parts table and steps do.
