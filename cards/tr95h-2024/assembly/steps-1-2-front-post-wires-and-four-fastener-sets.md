1. **The end of the front post (2) with wire (98) coming out of it is the right side of the main frame
   (1).** Ask one person to hold the front post while you connect **wire (57)** from the main frame to
   **wire (89)** from the front post. Tuck the connector back into the hole on the main frame. Be careful
   not to damage the wire or the connector.
2. Mount the front post on each right and left side of the main frame and secure it with **parts 11, 15,
   14 and 13** as shown - that is **M8 x 50 mm bolts (11), M8 washers (15), M8 nylon nuts (14) and M8 x 15
   mm bolts (13)**. The drawing shows, per side, long bolts (11) through the post foot into the frame
   with washers and nylon nuts, and short bolts (13) with washers; it does not print a count per side.

Tools drawn for the step: the M6 Allen key (20) and the screwdriver/wrench (21). The book gives no
torque. The service manual's precaution for this step: connect the signal cables on the right post
first, then push the excess wire into the tube so it is not crushed.
