9. Ask someone to hold the assembled **desk set (5)** on top of the **front post (2)**. Connect **wire (116)**
   from the desk set with **wire (98)** from the top of the front post. Tuck the connector back into the
   hole on the front post. Be sure not to pinch the wires or the connector.
10. Attach the assembled desk set to the top of the front post and firmly secure it with **parts 12, 14
   and 15** - the **four M8 x 55 mm bolts (12)** from above, with **M8 washers (15)** and **M8 nylon nuts
   (14)** below - as shown. Be sure not to pinch the wire or the connector.

Tools drawn: the M6 Allen key (20) and the screwdriver/wrench (21). Wire 116 is the one joined to desk
wire 104 at step 3, so the console's cable path runs computer set - desk set - front post - main frame.
