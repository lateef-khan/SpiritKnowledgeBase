3. Loosen the cable tie to separate the **5 wires (102, 102a, 103, 103a, 104)** out of the **desk set (5)**
   and the **5 wires (114, 114a, 115, 115a, 116)** out of the **computer set (3)**, and connect the ten:

   | Desk set wire | Computer set wire |
   |---|---|
   | 104 | 116 |
   | 103 | 114 |
   | 103a | 114a |
   | 102 | 115 |
   | 102a | 115a |

   "Make sure no mismatch." Tuck the connectors one by one back into the hole on the desk set. Do not
   damage the wires or the connectors.
4. Install the **computer set (3)** on the **desk set (5)** and secure it with **parts 11, 15 and 14** (M8 x 50
   mm bolts, M8 washers, M8 nylon nuts) in the order shown - the drawing shows two bolts with washers
   into the console bracket from the front and two nuts with washers behind.
5. Mount the **storage box (4)** on top of the desk set.
6. Put on the **back cover (8)** and lock it with **parts 16** (5/32" x 1/2" screws; the drawing shows
   four).

The service manual's own sequence puts the storage box onto the console stand *first* and places the
two on the desk rack together - a different order from the owner's manual's steps 4 and 5.
