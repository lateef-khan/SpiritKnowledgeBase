| Warranty | Frame | Parts |
|---|---|---|
| Residential | 1 Year | 90 Days |

**Home Use Limited Warranty, with no Labor column.** The ERG180 owner's manual's first warranty page (PDF p. 33, printed 32; *Effective August 01, 2023 - ERG180 Rower LIMITED WARRANTY*) prints a *Warranty / Residential* row headed *Frame / Parts* and nothing for labor - not *None*, not a period. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`); the SERVICE page's *twelve (12) months ... establishes the labor warranty period* is the boilerplate every Online-Support book prints, and no labor figure exists for it to describe.

**Same figures as the ERG160, one column fewer.** The ERG160, the 2020 sibling, prints *Frame 1 Year / Parts 90 Days / Labor None* (`xterra-bike-warranty-periods-frame-1-year-parts-90-days-labor-none`); the ERG180 drops the column. In practice both give the owner a part inside 90 days and no technician. The other no-labor-column pages carry different figures (`xterra-ergw-warranty-periods-frame-3-years-parts-1-year-no-labor-column`, `xterra-warranty-periods-frame-5-years-parts-1-year-no-labor-column`).

The rest is the Online-Support page: five duties without a registration item (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-without-registration`), nine exclusions admitting a non-dues paying facility at five hours a day or less (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-with-a-non-dues-facility-allowance`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-use-environment-residential-or-non-dues-facility-5-hours-a-day`), five service steps at (870) 935-1107 with a trip charge (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-service-steps-dealer-service-organization-then-the-service-department`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-owner-pays-freight-parts-shipping-and-trip-charges`), registration by QR code with no deadline (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-qr-code-and-support-tab-no-deadline`). The book prints no exploded-view parts list; the service manual does (`erg180-2023-specs-parts-list-items-1-to-95-from-the-service-manual`).
