1. Remove the adjustable cushion (79) - the screw-in leveler.
2. Remove the end cap from the rear stabilizer.
3. Reassemble the components in the reverse order.

Unscrewing the cushion first is what frees the end cap (33). The tube itself comes off the rail on the four M8 x 20 Allen bolts of assembly step 2.
