**Tools included:** Allen Wrench (84, S5) and Wrench (63, S13-14-19).

**Parts included:** Main Frame (1), Slide Rail (2), Front Stabilizer (3), Rear Stabilizer (4), Pedals L and R (12), Seat (38), Console (91).

**Hardware, by step**

| Step | # | Item | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 34 | Limited pad | 1 |
| 1 | 65 | Cross-head screw M6 x 10 | 2 |
| 2 | 81 | Allen bolt M8 x 20 | 4 |
| 2 | 52 | Spring washer ID8.5 | 4 |
| 2 | 55 | Flat washer OD16 x ID8.5 x 1.5 | 4 |
| 3 | 45 | Allen bolt M8 x 45 | 2 |
| 3 | 52 | Spring washer ID8.5 | 2 |
| 3 | 55 | Flat washer OD16 x ID8.5 x 1.5 | 2 |
| 4 | 92 | Flat head screw M8 x 15 | 4 |
| 4 | 46 | Allen bolt M8 x 15 | 2 |
| 4 | 55 | Flat washer OD16 x ID8.5 x 1.5 | 2 |
| 5 | 50 | Hex head bolt M12 x 160 | 4 |
| 5 | 64 | Pedal bushing Ø18 x Ø13 x 26 | 2 |

Screw 65 is also the console screw: four of them arrive pre-fitted on the back of the console (step 6) and the service manual's parts list counts 65 as "Screw, 4".

The sister ERG160 (linked card) uses flat head screws (62) for the limit pad, two stop brackets with self-tapping screws in step 4, and a 20 mm washer (44) where this book uses 55.
