1. Use an open-end wrench to loosen the nuts on both sides of the pulley assembly.
2. Remove the belt before taking out the pulley assembly.
3. After reinstalling the pulley assembly, **check the tension of the belt, which should be maintained between 200 and 230.**
4. Reassemble in the reverse order.

**The tension figure has no unit printed.** The book gives "200 and 230" bare; the ERG800W service manual, from the same publisher, prints its belt tension in Hz from a belt tension meter (335-350 Hz), so a sonic tension meter reading in Hz is the likeliest meaning - but this card records the figure as printed. The ERG160 book gives no tension figure and instead pre-loads the pulley by hand three turns (linked card).
