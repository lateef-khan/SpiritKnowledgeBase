1. Remove the two screws from the front stabilizer tube (the M8 x 45 Allen bolts of assembly step 3).
2. Remove the screws securing the floor mat, then take off the floor mat (72, two of them).
3. Use an Allen wrench and an open-end wrench to remove the **moving wheel (74)**.
4. **When reinstalling the moving wheel, ensure it can rotate smoothly after tightening.**
5. Reassemble all components in the reverse order.

The floor mats and moving wheels are new in the 2023 parts list; the 2020 ERG160 has end caps instead.
