1. Use appropriate tools to remove the belt wheel screws.
2. Reassemble or reinstall the components in the reverse order.

Two lines and a photo; the book names neither tool nor screw. The belt wheel (28) is the POM wheel the mesh belt runs over under the handlebar seat - the ERG160 book reaches it by removing the handlebar seat first (linked card).
