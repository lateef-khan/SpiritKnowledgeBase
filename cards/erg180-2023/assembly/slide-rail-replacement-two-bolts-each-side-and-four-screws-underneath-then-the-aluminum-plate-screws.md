1. Remove the two bolts from both sides of the slide rail, and the four screws from the bottom. Then dismount the slide rail from the main frame.
2. Remove the screws on both sides of the aluminum plate.
3. Remove the aluminum plate from the slide rail.
4. Reassemble all components in the reverse order.

The two side bolts are the M8 x 15 Allen bolts (46) and the four bottom screws the M8 x 15 flat head screws (92) of assembly step 4. The rear stabilizer and seat are not mentioned here - their own procedures cover them.
