1. Loosen the console terminals first.
2. Use an Allen wrench and an open-end wrench to remove the screws securing the console - a bolt and nut through the bracket, so a tool on each side.
3. Reassemble the console by following the steps in reverse.

No size is printed; the photo shows one through-bolt at the bracket pivot. The ERG160 service book frees its console by unscrewing the adjust knob instead (linked card).
