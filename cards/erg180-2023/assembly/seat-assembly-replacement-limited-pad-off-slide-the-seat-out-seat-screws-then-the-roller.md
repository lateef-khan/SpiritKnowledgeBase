1. Remove the **limited pad (34)** from the slide rail, then slide the seat assembly out of the rail.
2. Remove the screws securing the seat to the seat assembly.
3. Use an Allen wrench and an open-end wrench to remove the roller from the seat assembly.
4. Reassemble or reinstall in the reverse order.

The limited pad is the seat stop fitted with two cross-head screws in assembly step 1, so the seat comes off the open end of the rail without taking the rail off the frame.
