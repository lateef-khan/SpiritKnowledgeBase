**The owner's manual prints the drawing; the service manual prints the list.** The ERG180 owner's manual's **EXPLODED VIEW DIAGRAM** (PDF p. 32, printed 31) carries callouts 1 to 95 with the pedals as *12L* and *12R*, and two tool callouts in a box at the top left - **84 (S5)** and **63** - read from a 100 dpi render, because the numbers are artwork and the text layer of the page holds only the heading. The book prints no list under it. The ERG180 service manual (V1.0, 2025) prints the same drawing (PDF p. 4) and a two-page **Parts List** (PDF pp. 5-6) headed *P/N / DESCRIPTION / Qty Per unit*, with **no specification column** - no thread sizes, no bearing numbers.

**What the list tells a service call.** The console is item 91; the flywheel item 89; the belt item 17; the tension control knob item 30; the sensor wire item 40 and sensor magnet item 69; the one-way bearing item 80; the seat item 38 on three seat rollers (60); the compression spring item 44 and the volute spring item 39; the moving (transport) wheels item 74 and the adjustable cushions (levelers) item 79. The two packed tools are item 63, the **multi-function wrench**, and item 84, the **spanner** (the *S5* Allen key on the drawing). The replacement chapters of the service manual (pp. 7-14) cite these numbers.

**Against the ERG160 list** (`erg160-2020-specs-parts-list-items-1-to-100-with-a-spec-column`): items 1 to 10 carry the same names on both machines, but the numbering parts at item 11 - *Self-tapping screw* here, *Stop Brackets R* there - and never lines up again (the ERG160 spanner is item 100, its Allen key 99; here the flat washers are 41, 55 and 62, the aluminum plates 57). Answer an ERG180 callout from this table only. For the sizes the ERG160 list prints and this one does not, the Parts Included page of the owner's manual names the packed tools as an Allen wrench and a wrench without sizes.
