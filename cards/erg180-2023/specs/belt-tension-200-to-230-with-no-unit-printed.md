**200 to 230, unit not printed.** The service manual's pulley procedure ends: "After reinstalling the pulley
assembly, check the tension of the belt, which should be maintained between 200 and 230." No unit, no gauge and no
measuring point are named. The range is in the same place and shape as the ERG800W book's acoustic figure
(335 to 350 **Hz**, `erg800w-2023-specs-belt-tension-335-to-350-hz-measured-at-three-points`), so it is most
likely a belt-tension-meter reading in hertz, but the page does not say so; do not quote a unit for it.

The belt (parts list item 17) runs from the drawstring pulley to the belt wheel; the procedure loosens the nuts on
both sides of the pulley assembly with an open-end wrench, removes the belt, and reassembles in reverse (the
assembly section holds those steps). The ERG160 book gives no tension figure at all - its belt-pulley procedure
says only to "hand-tighten it clockwise through 3 full rotations, then pull the Meshbelt outward". The Sole SR500
and SR550 rowers print their figure as "70~80BLS" (`sr500-2016-drive-belt-tension`) - a different gauge and a
different machine.
