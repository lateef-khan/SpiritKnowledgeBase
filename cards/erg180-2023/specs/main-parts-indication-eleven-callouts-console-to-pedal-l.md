**One labelled photograph, eleven callouts.**

| No. | Name | No. | Name |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Console | 7 | Tension Control Knob |
| 2 | Pedal R | 8 | Decorative Cover |
| 3 | Seat | 9 | Chain Cover |
| 4 | Slide Rail | 10 | Front Stabilizer Tube |
| 5 | Rear Support Tube | 11 | Pedal L |
| 6 | Handlebar | | |

The *Tension Control Knob* is the resistance knob the owner's manual letters C
(`xterra-erg-specs-magnetic-resistance-from-a-manual-tension-knob-with-no-level-count-printed`); the *Decorative
Cover* is the trim (parts list item 31, two of them) on the front of the frame; the *Rear Support Tube* is what
the owner's manual calls the rear stabilizer, with its adjustable cushions (79) as levelers. The owner's manual
letters the same machine A Console, B Handlebar, C Resistance Knob, D Pedals, E Seat, F Slide Rail, G Rear
Stabilizer, H Front Stabilizer, I Levelers.

No dimension or weight is printed on the drawing. The 95-item parts list and the exploded view (PDF pp. 4-6) are
the parts section's; the belt tension figure on the same book's pulley page is
`erg180-2023-specs-belt-tension-200-to-230-with-no-unit-printed`.
