**This is the ERG180 page, which lists two certifications - "FCC part 15" and "Bluetooth (with FTMS)" - and no UL
line; the ERG800W and SB600 pages add "UL".** Then the declaration: "This device complies with part 15 of the FCC
Rules. Operation is subject to the following two conditions: (1) this device must not cause harmful interference,
and (2) this device must not accept any interference received, including interference that may cause undesired
operation."

The NOTE: tested to "the limits for a **Class B digital device**, pursuant to Part 15 of the FCC Rules", designed
for "reasonable protection against harmful interference in a residential installation", with the four remedies -
reorient or relocate the receiving antenna; increase the separation between the equipment and receiver; connect the
equipment to an outlet on a different circuit from the receiver; consult the dealer or an experienced radio/TV
technician.

CAUTION: keep "a separation distance of at least **20 cm** ... between the antenna of this device and all persons",
and the transmitter "must not be co-located or operating in conjunction with any other antenna or transmitter".

Condition (2) is printed "must **not** accept", as on the SB240 and SB600 pages, where the standard Part 15
sentence reads "must accept"; the card keeps the printed wording and reads it as a misprint. There is no Grantee
note. The Bluetooth radio pairs with the XTERRA+ app and FTMS apps such as Kinomap; the console section holds that.

**Among the Xterra rowers only the ERG180 and the ERG800W print a compliance page** - the ERG800W's is the SB600
page word for word, with UL
(`sb600-2023-specs-fcc-notice-class-b-digital-device-ul-and-bluetooth-ftms-and-20-cm-rf-exposure`). The ERG160,
ERG220, ERG400, ERG500, ERG550W, ERG600W, ERG650W, ERG700 and ERG750W books, the six rower service manuals and the
four dumbbell and stand books print none, including the Bluetooth-equipped ERG550W and ERG750W.
