> When using an electrical appliance, basic precautions should always be followed, including the
> following: Read all instructions before using this appliance.

> **DANGER - To reduce the risk of electric shock:** Always unplug this appliance from the
> electrical outlet immediately after using and before cleaning.

> **WARNING - To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons,** install the
> Elliptical on a flat level surface with access to a 120-volt AC, 15-Amp. grounded outlet. With
> only the Elliptical plugged into the circuit.

> DO NOT USE AN EXTENSION CORD UNLESS IT IS 14 AWG OR LARGER, WITH ONLY ONE OUTLET ON THE END. The
> Elliptical should be the only appliance in the electrical circuit.

> DO NOT ATTEMPT TO DISABLE THE GROUNDED PLUG BY USING IMPROPER ADAPTERS, OR IN ANY WAY MODIFY THE
> CORD SET. A serious shock or fire hazard may result along with computer malfunctions.

`metal capabilities` in item 13 is printed that way; the word intended is *mental*. Every Spirit
manual in the CE800/CE850 line prints the same slip.

Beside the list is the boxed note printed on every one of the fourteen 2024 manuals:

> Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of
> your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine.

**This page is word for word the CU800ENT-2024 and CR800ENT-2024 bike page**, with `Elliptical`
substituted for the bike's own name and nothing else changed - same header, same fifteen items, same
order, same 120-volt AC 15-Amp figure, same `metal` slip
(`spirit-bike-ent-safety-instructions-list`). It is kept as its own card because the machine noun
runs through every quoted line; the rules are the same rules.

**It is not the earlier CE800ENT page.** The 2024 book asks for **120-volt AC, 15-Amp**; the
CE800ENT owner's manual that preceded it asks for **110-volt, 5-amp**, prints its list as bullets
rather than numbers, and closes with `SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!`
(`spirit-ce850-safety-instructions-list`, `spirit-ce850-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`).
Never quote 5 amps for a 2024 machine or 15 amps for the earlier one.

**This manual has no GROUNDING INSTRUCTIONS page.** The grounded-plug sentence above is the whole of
what it says about grounding.
