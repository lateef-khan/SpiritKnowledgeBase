These are the callout numbers on the exploded view drawing. The drawing is a
picture with bare numbers on it and no text, so the table below is the only
readable form of it.

**139 rows are printed.** The whole item numbers that appear are 1-13, 21, 23-41, 43-45, 47-51, 53, 56-85, 88-103, 105-122, 124-144, 148-150, 152-153, 155, 157-159, 163. Any number outside that run is **not printed in this manual at all** - 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 22, 42, 46, 52, 54, 55, 86, 87, 104, 123, 145, 146, 147, 151, 154, 156, 160, 161, 162 are absent, not a missing page.

## What changed from the July 2024 CE800ENT list

The predecessor is only three months older - the CE800ENT owner's manual stamped
`800054_OM_20240703`, carded as `ce800ent`. Both books print **exactly 139 rows
with the same numbering**. **Four rows changed:**

| Item | July 2024 printing | October 2024 printing |
|---|---|---|
| 32 | **500 mm** sensor with cable | **600 mm** sensor with cable |
| 33 | **300 mm** wire brake coil harness (red) | **900 mm** wire brake coil harness (red) |
| 158 | **Water** Bottle Holder Clamp | **Drink** Bottle Holder Clamp |
| 159 | **Water** Bottle Holder | **Drink** Bottle Holder |

Two cable lengths changed and nothing else did. **Check which printing you are
holding before quoting the length of item 32 or 33** - they are the two rows that
would send the wrong part.

## What tells this list apart from the two LED elliptical lists

This is the touchscreen machine and it plugs into the wall:

- **Item 44 is `EMS Controller`, item 45 `Power Adaptor`, item 43 `Power Cord`
  and item 47 `AC Electronic Module`.**
- **The list names no brake unit.** Item 29 is `Flywheel` and item 30 `Magnet`,
  and item 33 is the wire brake coil harness; there is no `Induction Brake` row
  the way the CR800ENT-2024 list has one at item 55, and no
  `Generator/Brake Controller` the way the CE800-2024 list has one at item 31.
- The plain CE800-2024 list has neither an EMS controller nor a power adaptor.

**Items 105 and 130 are printed identically**, both
`Ø3/8" x Ø19 x 1.5T_Flat Washer` quantity 4.

**No flywheel weight, screen size or power rating is printed against any row.**
The CE800ENT *service* manual describes the same flywheel and brake in words -
see `ce800ent-flywheel-and-induction-brake` - and gives no figures either.
