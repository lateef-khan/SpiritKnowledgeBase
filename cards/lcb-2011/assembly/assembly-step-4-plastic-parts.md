Hardware: **#58** M5 x 12mm Phillips Head Screw (**8 pcs**).

1. Insert the Computer Cable (29) and two Hand pulse cables (**26 & 28**) into the connectors in the back of the Console (34).
2. Attach the Console (34) onto the Console Mast (2) with four Phillips Head Screws (58).
3. Attach the **Front Stabilizer Cover (40) and the Rear Stabilizer Cover (41)** to the Main Frame (1) with four Phillips Head Screws (58). Tighten with the Phillips Head Screw Driver (93).
4. Install the Pedals (45 L, 46 R) onto the Cranks by using the **13/15mm Wrench (92)**. **The left pedal has a reverse thread** and screws into the crank in the opposite rotation from normal threads. There is an **L** stamped into the end of the threaded post of the left pedal and an **R** on the end of the right post. Tighten the pedals as much as you possibly can. It may be necessary to re-tighten the pedals if you feel a thumping while pedalling; a clicking noise or thumping feeling is usually caused by loose pedals.
5. Attach the Seat (19) to the **Seat Post (7)** using the **12/14mm Wrench (100)**.

**A fixed seat post, not a sliding seat mount.** The B94 of the same year fits a seat mount slide, a nut plate and a fore/aft adjustment knob in this step; the LCB 2011 just bolts the seat to the post. From 2016 the LCB gains a Sliding Seat Mount and a Brake Tension Knob instead.
