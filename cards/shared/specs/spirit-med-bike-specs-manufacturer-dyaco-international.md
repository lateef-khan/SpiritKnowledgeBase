**Dyaco International Inc., No. 1, Gong 1st Rd., Hemei Township, Changhua County 50843, Taiwan.**

All five plug-in books carry the entry; on the 7.0R-2025 and 7.0U-2025 pages it sits beside the
factory symbol used on medical labelling, on the 8.0U and 8.5R pages it is set as plain text under
*Manufacturer*. The product-registration page of every 2025 book opens "Thank you for your recent
purchase of the ... Spirit by Dyaco".

**The 2021 Dyaco MED book adds a European representative:** "Dyaco Europe GMBH, Friedrich-Ebert-
Strasse 75, 51429 Bergisch Gladbach, Germany, TEL: +49 (0) 2204 844300", beside the EC REP symbol,
and a CE mark with notified-body number 0123. None of the four 2025 Spirit books prints a European
address.

**The 4.0R and 4.0U owner's manuals name no manufacturer and print no address** - they have no
specification page. The same Dyaco entry is on the 7.0T and 8.0T treadmills
(`spirit-2026t-specs-manufacturer`) and the rehabilitation steppers.
