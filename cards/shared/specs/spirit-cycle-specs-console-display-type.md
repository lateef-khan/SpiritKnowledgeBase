Five different consoles sit across these eight machines, **and not one manual
states a screen size.**

| Machines | What the manual calls the display |
|---|---|
| XIC600-2018, XIC600-2021 | **Large LCD Matrix Window**, with a built-in heart rate receiver and two Easy-Touch keys |
| AB900-2018, AB950-2024 | **four labelled LCD panels** - Interval, Target Time/Distance/Calories, Watts/Speed/RPM, and Heart Rate. The AB950 parts-of-your-bike drawing calls it an **LCD Screen** |
| CIC850-2022 | a panel the book **never names**: no LCD, no LED, no matrix. It is identified only by its part number, **DT-3268F** |
| JB950-2022 | **23 LED panels**, plus LED colour lights for the HR Zone and the Power Zone |
| **CB900-2013, CIC800-2021** | **no console, no display and nothing to read** |

**None of the eight is a touchscreen.** The word does not appear in any of them.
If a customer expects a touchscreen on one of these, they are thinking of a
Spirit commercial ENT bike - see `spirit-bike-specs-console-display-type`, which
covers the CR and CU machines and their 10.1 inch screens. **10.1 inches belongs
to those machines and to no machine on this card.**

## Two of these bikes have no console at all

The CB900-2013 and the CIC800-2021 have no display of any kind. The word
*console* appears zero times in either manual, neither prints a battery
instruction, and the CIC800's parts-of-your-bike drawing labels a seat,
handlebar, resistance adjustment, pedals, main frame, flywheel, levelers,
stabilizers and transport wheels - **and no console**. Both were checked page by
page against 300 dpi renders, so this is not a page that failed to extract. See
`spirit-bike-console-no-console-at-all-tension-knob-only`.

## The JB950 display is a segmented LED wing, and the manual counts its panels

The Maintenance Mode **DISPLAY TEST** exists *to ensure that each of the 23 LED
panels are fully functional*, and cycles every panel through `1` to `9` and `A`
to `Z`. The console photograph on printed p. 33 - **a flat image that extracts to
two words** - shows the readouts labelled around it: **LEVEL, RPM, TIME and
DISTANCE** along the top and **WATTS and HEART RATE** below, with the panel
scrolling `JOHNNY G. METHOD`. That labelling was read from a render, not from the
text.

## What none of the eight manuals gives

**No screen size in inches or centimetres, no resolution, no brightness figure,
no refresh rate, and no operating system.** The panel technology itself is stated
only loosely: *LCD* on the XIC600 and the two air bikes, *LED* on the JB950, and
nothing at all on the CIC850. Nobody prints TFT, OLED or a backlight type.

**This card answers the display specification only.** The console face drawings,
the buttons, the modes and the setup procedures are `console` questions - see
`spirit-bike-console-xic600-face-and-two-keys` and the other
`spirit-bike-console-*` cards for the machine in front of you. For the console's
part number on the CIC850 see
`spirit-cic850-specs-dt3268f-console-part-number`.
