Two profiles, seven rows each. Neither restricts **age, gender, height, weight, ability or
culture**.

| | **Intended operator** | **Intended installer** |
|---|---|---|
| Education | **University or above** | **High school or above** |
| Knowledge | Should read the user's manual before use | Shall be able to manipulate the product properly |
| Discipline | **Should receive training from the manufacturer before use** | **Shall be given specific training by the manufacturer** |
| Experience | **Must have experience in physical therapy** | Must have experience in product assembly and disassembly |
| Background | **Must be a major in physical therapy** | **Must be electro-mechanically trained** |
| Professional competence | **Should have the physical therapist licence** | Normal vision ability required |

**This is the strictest operator profile in the Spirit corpus.** No other Spirit manual asks for a
professional licence to run the machine. It is why the safety list also says installation and
assembly are *"for trained personnel only"* -
`spirit-med-stepper-safety-instructions-list-eighteen-bullets`.

**The wording is `should`, not `shall`, on the operator's licence and training.** The installer's
training is `shall`. Read the difference as the manual prints it and do not upgrade either.

**A patient is not the operator.** Nothing here bars a patient from using the machine; it says who
is expected to set it up and supervise. The patient population is a different list -
`spirit-rehab-stepper-specs-application-specification-medical-purpose-and-patient-population`.

**Neither profile mentions the machine's own maintenance menu.** The Engineering / Maintenance mode
is reachable with three keys and no code, by anyone -
`spirit-rehab-stepper-console-maintenance-menu-with-a-five-window-sensor-test`.
