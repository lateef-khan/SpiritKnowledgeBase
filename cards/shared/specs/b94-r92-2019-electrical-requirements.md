**Never remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power.**

- If voltage varies by **ten percent (10%) or more**, the performance of the fitness bike may be affected. Such conditions are **not covered under warranty**. If you suspect the voltage is low, contact your local power company or a licensed electrician for proper testing.
- Never expose the fitness bike to rain or moisture. It is **not** designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.
- Operating temperature: **40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit**.
- Humidity: **95% non-condensing** (no water drops forming on surfaces).

What the bike actually runs on is stated separately — see the power-up card for the 12V/3.3A DC supply and 115 VAC input.
