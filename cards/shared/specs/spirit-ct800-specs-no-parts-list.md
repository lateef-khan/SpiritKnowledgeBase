**Neither the 2012 nor the 2016 CT800 owner's manual contains an exploded view
diagram or a parts list.** The 2012 table of contents runs to Manufacturer's
Limited Warranty 25 and the 2016 table of contents to Manufacturer's Limited
Warranty 41; neither has an Exploded View or Parts List entry, and no
whole-machine table of item number, description and quantity appears anywhere in
either book.

**Do not answer a 2012 or 2016 part number from the 2020 or 2022 parts list. The
two generations number the same parts differently, and the clash is silent.** The
2012 and 2016 assembly steps and the 2020/2022 parts lists both use small
two- and three-digit item numbers, so a number looks valid against either. It is
not:

| Item no. | In the 2012 / 2016 assembly steps | In the 2020 / 2022 parts list |
|---|---|---|
| 19 | right console mast tube | Cushion A |
| 21 | retaining plate | Drive Belt |
| 58 | 3/8" split washer | Fan |
| 59 | 3/8" flat washer | AC Electronic Module |
| 104 | 3/8" x 3-3/4" stainless steel bolt | Ø8.5 x Ø26 x 2.0T flat washer |
| 105 | 3/8" x 3/4" button head socket bolt | M10 x 65m/m hex head bolt (the 2020 list writes it M10 x P1.5 x 65m/m) |
| 108 | M8 x 12mm socket cap bolt | Ø3/8" x Ø19 x 1.5T flat washer |
| 125 | M5 star washer | Ø3/8" x Ø25 x 2.0T flat washer |

Ordering "part 58" for a 2012 machine against the 2020 list gets a fan instead of
a washer. Get 2012 and 2016 part numbers from Spirit Fitness or the dealer parts
list, and say the owner's manual does not print them.

**What the two manuals do print instead.**

- A **HARDWARE box at each of the three assembly steps**, giving the item number,
  description and quantity of only the fasteners used in that one step. The 2016
  manual sets them out as a PART / TYPE / DESCRIPTION / QTY table; the 2012
  manual prints the same information as captions under the drawing.
- **Assembly text citing item numbers in parentheses** - "console mast tube (19)",
  "computer cable A (87)", "retaining plate (21)". The pre-assembly page says
  these are "the item number from the assembly drawing", but the numbered
  assembly drawing they belong to is not printed as a readable list in either
  book; the step illustrations carry bare numbers with no key.
- **A TOOLS INCLUDED and PARTS INCLUDED box, in the 2016 manual only**, on the
  pre-assembly page. It lists the major sub-assemblies in the carton - 2 console
  masts, 2 side covers, 1 base frame, 1 console support, 1 console, 1 safety
  button, 1 power cord, 1 tube of lubricant (1 application), 1 hardware kit - and
  gives no item numbers. The 2012 manual has no such box; it lists only the three
  assembly tools.
- **A console overview illustration** labelled with part *names* - console mast,
  motor cover, running belt/deck, side rails - not with item numbers.

**The four later printings of the same machine do carry a full list**, one each
and not interchangeable with one another. See `ct800-2020-specs-parts-list`,
`ct800ent-2022-specs-parts-list`, `ct800-2024-specs-parts-list` and
`ct800ent-2024-specs-parts-list`. The 2024 pair is the New Black Units reissue:
its CT800 list carries the same 132 item numbers as the 2020 list with four rows
altered, and its CT800ENT list drops item 118 from the 2022 list and swaps four
cable names. **A number that checks out against one of the four is not thereby
right for another.**
