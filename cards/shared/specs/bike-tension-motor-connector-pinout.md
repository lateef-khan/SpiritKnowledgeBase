The tension motor pulls a **steel rope** to change resistance. Its main control connector is numbered 5 down to 1 on the board side.

| Pin | Signal |
|---|---|
| 1 | M+ |
| 2 | M- |
| 3 | +5V |
| 4 | VR |
| 5 | GND |
