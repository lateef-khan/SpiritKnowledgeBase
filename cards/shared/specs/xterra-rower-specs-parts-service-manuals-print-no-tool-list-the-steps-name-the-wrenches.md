**None of the six Xterra rower service manuals prints a tools-required page** - checked in the text layer, in a spacing-tolerant search for *tool*, *wrench*, *spanner* and *multimeter*, and in the OCR supplements of every page. What each book does is name the tool inside the step that uses it. Collected from every step:

| Service manual | Tools the steps name |
|---|---|
| ERG160 (erg160-2020) | an open-end wrench (pedals, belt and drawstring pulleys, seat rollers); a screwdriver (chain covers); *a tool* to pry the foam grip open |
| ERG180 (erg180-2023) | an open-end wrench (pedal bolts, pulley nuts); a Phillips screwdriver (chain covers); an Allen wrench with an open-end wrench (the seat roller, the moving wheel on the front stabilizer); *appropriate tools* for the belt wheel screws |
| ERG550W (erg550w-2023) | a cross (Phillips) wrench; an open-end wrench; an Allen wrench - **S5** for the M8*15 slide-rail screws, **S4** for the M6*15 tank screws; locking pliers with an open-end wrench on the pulley; *a tool* to knock out the front stabilizer end cap |
| ERG600W (erg600w-2021) | Allen wrenches **S4**, **S5** and **S6**; a spanner **S13-S17**; a screwdriver; a socket wrench |
| ERG750W (erg750w-2025) | a hex wrench (console stand screws); an Allen wrench with an external hex wrench (transport wheel) - no sizes anywhere in the book |
| ERG800W (erg800w-2023) | Allen wrenches **4 mm (S4)**, **5 mm (S5)** and **6 mm (S6)**; a Phillips screwdriver and, for the truss-head screws on the strap guiding wheel and belt pages, an *electric* Phillips screwdriver; open-end wrenches **#10** (idler nut M6), **#13** (M8 nuts, two of them for the belt pulley and saddle wheels) and **#17** (the M10x30 screw) |

**How to read the sizes.** *S4/S5/S6* is the Allen key across-flats size in millimetres; *S13-S17* an open-end spanner spanning 13 to 17 mm; *#13* a 13 mm open-end wrench. The ERG800W's sizes pair with its parts list (`erg800w-2023-specs-parts-list-lettered-codes-from-the-service-manual`): 6 mm for the M8x16 screws J3, 5 mm for the M6x30 screws J1 and the M6x75 console screw H7, 4 mm for the M6x12 stainless screws on the handlebar seat. **Do not confuse these with the tools packed with the rower** - the owner's manuals' Parts Included pages and parts lists show the carton's own Allen key and wrench (ERG160 items 99-100, ERG550W items 96-97, ERG180 items 63 and 84; the ERG800W's *6mm Allen Wrench, 5mm Allen Wrench, 13/15mm Wrench*), which are on the assembly and parts-list cards.

The Xterra treadmill service manuals list one tool, a multimeter (`xterra-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`); the SB600's lists five with grease (`sb600-2023-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-five-tools-and-grease`). No rower book names a multimeter, a torque figure or a lubricant.
