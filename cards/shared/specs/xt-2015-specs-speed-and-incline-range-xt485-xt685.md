**These are the highest figures in the 2015 XT family.**

| | Maximum you can enter |
|---|---|
| Speed | **12.0 mph or 18.0 kmph** |
| Incline | **15.0%** |

Both manuals print it word for word: "The maximum value you may input for speed is
12.0 mph or 18.0 kmph and incline 15.0%". Neither manual states a speed or an
incline increment - the sentence ends there, unlike the XT185, XT285 and XT385
manuals, which go on to give increments.

**The two speed figures do not convert into each other.** 12.0 mph is about 19.3
kmph, not 18.0. Both numbers are printed exactly as shown here in both manuals;
quote whichever unit the console is set to and do not calculate one from the
other.

**Only these two 2015 manuals state the incline as a percent.** The XT385-2015
gives "incline level 15" and the XT185-2015 and XT285-2015 give "incline level
9.5". Do not read a level as a percent.

This is the only place either manual states a speed or an incline figure. Neither
contains a specification table, so there is no motor rating, deck size, dimension
or unit weight to go with it.
