**These are the highest figures in the 2015 XT family, and the XT485ENT prints
them too.**

| | Maximum you can enter |
|---|---|
| Speed | **12.0 mph or 18.0 kmph** |
| Incline | **15.0%** |

Three manuals print it word for word: "The maximum value you may input for speed
is 12.0 mph or 18.0 kmph and incline 15.0%". None of them states a speed or an
incline increment - the sentence ends there, unlike the XT185, XT285 and XT385
manuals, which go on to give increments.

**The two speed figures do not convert into each other.** 12.0 mph is about 19.3
kmph, not 18.0. Both numbers are printed exactly as shown here in both manuals;
quote whichever unit the console is set to and do not calculate one from the
other.

**Only these two 2015 manuals state the incline as a percent.** The XT385-2015
gives "incline level 15" and the XT185-2015 and XT285-2015 give "incline level
9.5". Do not read a level as a percent.

This is the only place any of the three manuals states a speed or an incline
figure. None of them contains a specification table, so there is no motor rating,
deck size, dimension or unit weight to go with it.

**The XT485ENT-2023 carries the same sentence, in a 2021-revision book.** Its
manual is dated Revision 04.16.2021 and prints the figures in the Direct Access
Speed and Incline Buttons paragraph, with the console's worked examples beside
them ("Press the Select Speed button, then 8, 0 = 8.0 mph or kmph"). **The base
XT485-2023 manual does not print this sentence at all**, so this figure belongs
to the 2015 books and the ENT book, not to the whole 2023 generation.

**Do not extend this to the XT685ENT.** That manual's Direct Speed & Incline
Buttons paragraph stops before naming any maximum, and no speed figure appears
anywhere in it.
