Both hiking-treadmill books print the same SPEED / INCLINE RANGE block on the Console Features page:

| Mode | Speed | Incline |
|---|---|---|
| Running Mode | 0.5 to 7.5 mph (the TR95H book also prints 0.8 to 12 kph) | Level 0 to 15 |
| Climbing Mode | 0.5 to 3.7 mph (0.8 to 6 kph) | Level 15 to 40 |

Speed changes in 0.1 mph steps from the keys. The Function Display list on the same page repeats the figures: INCLINE
window "Level 0 ~ 15 for Running mode, Level 15 ~ 40 for Climbing Mode"; SPEED window "Running Mode 0.8 ~ 12 kph,
Climbing Mode 0.8 ~ 6 kph (0.5 – 7.5 mph / 0.5 – 3.7 mph)". The TR75H book's console-window table says the same:
"Running mode: 0.8 ~ 12 KPH (0.5 ~ 7.5 mile), Hiking mode: 0.8 ~ 6 KPH (0.5 ~ 3.7 mile)".

**The same books contradict those tops on their program pages, and the contradiction is printed, not an extraction
error:**

- TR95H, heart-rate program line (p. 21 and again p. 24): "0.5 MPH – 6.2 MPH, LEVEL 0-15 FOR RUNNING / 0.5 MPH – 2.5
  MPH, LEVEL 15-40 FOR CLIMBING". 6.2 mph is 10 kph.
- TR95H, preset-program notes (p. 25): in an HR program "When the speed is at highest 10kph ... and incline also at
  the highest level 40, ... machine will keep running following the highest speed and incline."
- TR75H, heart-rate program lines (p. 22): "HRR (E, M, T, A, I) (0.5~6.2 mph, Level 0~ 15)" for running and
  "(0.5~6.2 mph , Level 15~ 40)" for climbing - the climbing HR line prints 6.2 mph where the TR95H prints 2.5 mph.

So the console's stated range is 0.5 to 7.5 mph running and 0.5 to 3.7 mph climbing, while the heart-rate programs are
described as capped at 6.2 mph (10 kph) and, on the TR95H, 2.5 mph in climbing mode. Neither book explains the
difference. The incline figures (levels 0 to 15 running, 15 to 40 climbing) agree everywhere.

Neither book prints a specification table; these figures come from the console pages. The walking treadmills state a
different range on their own card.
