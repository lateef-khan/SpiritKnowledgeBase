**None of the four strength books prints a specification table.** No book states the assembled size of a
dumbbell or of the stand, the total weight of a dumbbell with its tray, the stand's own weight, or a load rating
for the stand.

What is printed instead:

| Book | Weight figures | Size figures |
|---|---|---|
| ADB12.5PR | the five settings, 2.5 to 12.5 lb (1.13 to 5.57 kg), on the instructions page; sixteen plates of "0.55Kg" in the parts list | base "308*122*40" in the parts list, unit not stated (millimetres by the screw sizes beside it) |
| ADB25 | the five settings, 5 to 25 lb (2.3 to 11.3 kg); eight plates of "1.1Kg" | base "325*156*38" |
| ADB55 | the five settings, 11 to 55 lb (5 to 25 kg); eight plates of "2.5Kg" | base "406*210*47" |
| DBSTAND | none - no capacity, no weight | stabilizers "38*38*515", stand tubing "38*38*900" (two), in the parts list |

The weight settings and plate counts are on
`adb125pr-2022-specs-five-weight-settings-2-5-to-12-5-lb-per-hand-with-sixteen-plates`,
`adb25-2022-specs-five-weight-settings-5-to-25-lb-with-eight-plates` and
`adb55-2022-specs-five-weight-settings-11-to-55-lb-with-eight-plates`; the stand's compatibility rule and its
missing load rating are on `dbstand-2022-specs-holds-only-xterra-adjustable-dumbbell-cradles-and-prints-no-load-rating`.
The parts lists themselves belong to the parts section.

Two of the books carry **ghost text**: the ADB25 and ADB55 pack-list pages hold the 12.5 lb book's chart figures in
their text layer, invisible on the printed page. They are not figures for those dumbbells.
