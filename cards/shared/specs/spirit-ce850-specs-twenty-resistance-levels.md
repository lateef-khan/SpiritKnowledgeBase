**20 levels**, shown on the console as 10 rows of lights. Level 1 is the easiest.

The manuals describe the levels in four bands:

| Band | What the manual says |
|---|---|
| 1 to 5 | very easy workloads, small steps, a good progression for de-conditioned users |
| 5 to 10 | more challenging, but the increase from one level to the next stays small |
| 10 to 15 | start getting tough, the levels jump more dramatically |
| 15 to 20 | extremely hard; good for short interval peaks and elite athletic training |

All three CE850 printings agree, including the 2022 printing that added the water
bottle holder.

**Not to be confused with the 40 levels of the CE800, CE900 and CES880.** Those
manuals print the same paragraph with 40, 10, 20, 30 and 40 in place of 20, 5, 10,
15 and 20 - see `spirit-ce-specs-forty-resistance-levels`. A CE850 console will not
accept a level above 20.

**These are console levels, not a physical resistance rating.** No Spirit
commercial elliptical manual states resistance in watts, newtons or kilograms, and
none prints a level-to-watts table.

**Several Sole ellipticals also run 1 to 20, and their cards are not this
machine.** `e25-2023-console-data-ranges` and `e95-2016-console-data-ranges` give
the same LEVEL range for a Sole home elliptical, from a different console with a
different brake. The number matching is a coincidence; quote this card for a
Spirit CE850 and theirs for a Sole.
