**Forty levels of resistance, and the same four bands in all four printings:**

- **The first 10 levels** are very easy workloads; the changes between levels are
  set to a good progression for de-conditioned users.
- **Levels 11-20** are more challenging, but the increases in resistance from one
  level to the next remain small.
- **Levels 21-30** start getting tough, as the levels jump more dramatically.
- **Levels 31-40** are extremely hard, and are good for short interval peaks and
  elite athletic training.

This paragraph is word for word the same in the 2016, 2018, 2021 and 2023 books.
The 2023 book restates it as a numbered list rather than a paragraph; the figures
do not change.

The 2023 console confirms the scale on its faceplate. Its ten Direct Resistance
Level buttons are marked **2, 6, 10, 14, 18, 22, 26, 30, 34 and 40** - note that
the last step jumps four levels further than the rest, from 34 to 40 rather than
to 38. The markings were read from the console drawing rendered at 400 dpi, since
that page is a picture with no text behind it.

## Every other residential bike in this range has twenty, not forty

The XBR25, the XBR55, the XBU55 and both ENT touchscreen bikes print the same
paragraph with **20** levels and bands of five - see
`spirit-residential-bike-specs-twenty-resistance-levels`. A level number means a
different workload on the two scales, so **never quote a level from one on the
other**. Level 20 is the maximum on those bikes and only halfway up on this one.

## The commercial bikes print forty too, with bands that overlap

The Spirit commercial CR and CU bikes also say 40 levels, but their banding is
printed as **1-10, 10-20, 20-30 and 30-40** - overlapping at each boundary - and
their paragraph adds "displayed as 10 rows of lights", which this one does not.
That is a different printing of a different book; see
`spirit-bike-programs-forty-levels-of-resistance-shown-as-ten-rows`. **Do not
merge the two bandings.**

## No physical rating goes with the number

Level 1 to 40 is a console scale, not a measurement. **No Spirit residential bike
manual prints a level-to-watts table**, and the Watts readout reports the work the
rider is doing rather than the setting. There is no resistance figure in watts,
newtons or kilograms anywhere in the range - see
`spirit-residential-bike-specs-no-specification-table`.
