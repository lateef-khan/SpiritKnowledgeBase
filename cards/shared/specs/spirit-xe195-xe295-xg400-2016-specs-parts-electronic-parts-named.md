**Three books, five parts, the same names.** The two entry-level ellipticals and the XG400 glider of the
2016 residential range label exactly the same electronic parts on their chapter-2 pages.

**2-1 Upper Controllers** - **Cooling FAN**, **THUMB SWITCH** (the level keys on the swing-arm handlebar)
and **DISPLAY**.

**2-2 Lower Controller and Driver** - **TENSION MOTOR** (the gear motor that pulls the steel rope to the
brake magnet) and **SPEED SENSOR**. **No controller is named on this page**: the Electrical Configurations
page of each book says the main controller's power supply and motor driver are inside the console.

**The motor is what sets these three apart from the XE395 and XE895**, which add a motor controller,
an incline motor and a speaker (`spirit-xe395-xe895-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`), and from the
XE795, which has a hybrid generator and driver board and no tension motor at all
(`xe795-2016-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`). The XG400 book is the only one of the three to name
its gear motor by type, YJ-9902, in its error pages
(`xg400-2016-specs-parts-gear-motor-and-belts-named-by-type`).

**No part number or rating is printed on the chapter-2 pages**; the tension motor's 4.5-7.5 V DC working
voltage and its five-pin connector are on the specs cards for chapters 3 and 6. The only tool each book
lists is a multi-meter (`spirit-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`).
