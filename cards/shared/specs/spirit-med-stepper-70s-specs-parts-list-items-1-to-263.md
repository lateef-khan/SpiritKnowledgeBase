**This is the 2021-generation list, printed in the 2025 service manual.** The RS9500-SS021-02 book's list runs items 1 to 263 in 259 rows and is, row for row, the list in the 2021 Dyaco MED 7.0S owner's manual - `Seat Slider (R)` and `(L)` at 7 and 8, `Flywheel Mass` at 43, the console's nineteen sub-parts 29-01 to 29-19. It is **not** the 2025 owner's manual's list, which renumbers the rotating mass and seat rails and runs to item 337 (`spirit-rehab-stepper-specs-two-different-machines-are-called-7-0s`, `spirit-climber-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`).

**The service manual's own drawing does not fit its list.** The drawing is stamped `RS9500-SS021-02 20251210` and its balloons run past 300 - 300, 305, 309 to 313, 330 to 337 are all on the page - while the list stops at 263. A number above 263 read off that drawing cannot be found in this book; the 2025 owner's manual's list is the one that decodes it. Numbers 263 and below name the part in this list, but check the drawing agrees before ordering.

**No Spirit part number is printed.** 24 whole numbers between 1 and 263 are absent (59, 65, 78, 91, 103, 113, 114, 115, 128, 159, 171, 180, 182, 196, 206, 208, 238, 248, 253, 254, 255, 256, 257, 260); item 18 is lettered 18L / 18R.

## Where the two printings of this list differ

The service manual and the MED owner's manual print the same rows; the wording moves in two places and the rest is spacing (`m/m` for `mm`, `( R )` for `(R)`):

| Item | Service manual (this book) | 2021 MED owner's manual |
|---|---|---|
| 112 | Steel Cable(84.5×76cm), 1 | Steel Cable (760L), 1 |
| 157 | M5 × P0.8 × 70L_Phillips Head Screw, 1 | M5 × P0.8 × 70L_Socket Head Cap Bolt, 1 |

The MS300 book prints the same item numbers and quantities as the MED 7.0S (`spirit-rehab-stepper-specs-two-different-machines-are-called-7-0s`).

## What the list names that the prose does not

- The brake: 44 `Gear Motor`, 45 `Braking Magnet` (four), 61 `Aluminum Brake Disc`, 43 `Flywheel Mass`, 48 `Gear Motor Spring(Ø 13×20.3L)`.
- Sensors and boards: 221 `Optical Sensor Board (CS63008-00)` and 222 `Optical Sensor Board (CS63008-10)` - the only board numbers in the book - with 223-228 the console, encoder, DC power, hall-sensor and hand-pulse cables by length, and 229 `Power Adapter`.
- Seven tools packed as items and drawn beside the machine: 209 `12/14m/m_Wrench`, 210 `13/14m/m_Wrench`, 211 `Wrench, 10mm`, 212 `M5_L Allen Wrench`, 213 `M8_L Allen Wrench`, 214 `Phillips Head Screw Driver`, 242 `Short Phillips Head Screw Driver`. The book prints no separate tool list.

**The 7.5S is a different list** with the same 263 ceiling (`7-5s-med-specs-parts-list`): pedal plates and swing arms are numbered the same, the seat and rail parts are not.
