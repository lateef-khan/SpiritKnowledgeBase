**Upper Controllers runs over two pages in both books.** Page 5 labels **DISPLAY** - a photograph of the
touchscreen console. Page 6 draws the whole bike and labels the console parts on it: **Cooling FAN** and
**Speaker** in both books, and **Thumb Switch** in the XBR55ENT book only. The XBU55ENT page 6, checked on a
render, carries two callouts and no thumb switch.

**Lower Controller and Driver (p. 7), both books** - **SPEED RPM SENSOR** and **TENSION MOTOR**, photographed
inside the shroud.

**The tension motor is the same part the 2016 LED books name** (`spirit-xbr-xbu-2016-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`);
the same ENT books give its work voltage as DC 4.5~7.5 V on the Electrical Configurations page and describe
the main controller there as the DC supply for the console, the incline driver and the tension motor driver.
**The XBU55ENT's Electrical Configurations page also describes an "INCLINE MOTOR - an AC motor" that the
upright bike does not have**; the paragraph is carried over from an elliptical book and the chapter-2 pages
name no such part.

**Do not read this against the commercial ENT bikes.** The CR800ENT and CU800ENT name an induction brake,
an earphone port, a controller and a power converter (`spirit-cr800ent-cu800ent-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`).

No part number or rating is printed on these pages.
