**Six marks, and the water is the resistance.** All three books print the same block: "The water level gauge is
on the side of the tank. The maximum fill level is 6. Never fill it over this limit. Filling the tank over this
limit will void the warranty. The resistance depends on the water level in the tank. Water level 1 is the lowest
resistance. Level 6 is the highest resistance." There is no knob, lever or console setting that changes the load;
to row harder, add water, to row easier, pump some out.

What the console does with the level differs by book:

| Book | Console and the water level |
|---|---|
| ERG550W | after the cells go in, "the console will prompt you to enter the Water Level (L1~L6)"; press up or down to select, confirm, and the console enters training mode |
| ERG650W | the console asks for the "Workout Intensity Level (this is determined by how much water you've filled the tank with) from L1 to L6 ... The default level is L6" |
| ERG600W | no level prompt: the console goes into clock and calendar setting after the cells go in, then to the sport screen |

The level entered is what the console uses for its watt and drag-force figures; it does not change the load. The
ERG600W book also describes three **bungee cord hooks (A, B, C)** under the tank: "If you hook the Bungee Cord
(No.39) on to B or C, it increases the assisted return of the mesh belt. C is the fastest return." That sets how
fast the handle retracts, not the rowing resistance.

Only the ERG550W prints how much water each mark holds - 6.5 to 10 litres
(`erg550w-2023-specs-water-tank-capacity-6-5-to-10-liters-by-level`); the ERG600W and ERG650W print no volume.
Filling, emptying and the water-treatment tablet are the maintenance section's. The ERG750W instead has a knob on
the tank and shows six levels on the console
(`erg750w-2025-specs-resistance-knob-on-the-tank-with-six-levels-shown-on-the-console`); the ERG800W has a Min-Max
label and no numbered marks (`erg800w-2023-specs-water-resistance-set-by-the-tank-level-between-min-and-max-marks`).
The Spirit CRW800H2O uses the same six-step gauge (`crw800h2o-specs-six-water-fill-levels`); the Spirit CRW900's
ten-position adjuster is a different mechanism (`crw900-2021-specs-ten-level-tank-adjuster`).
