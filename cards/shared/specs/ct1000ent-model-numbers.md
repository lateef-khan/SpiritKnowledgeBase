The numbers come from the customer-service model table (`dbo.MODEL` in `CustService`); a manual rarely prints one.

The table names this machine **CT1000ENT**; its 2023 service manual is titled "CT1000(2023) ST8880-ST018 ENT Treadmill". A search for CT1000 and a search for CT1000ENT mean the same treadmill.
