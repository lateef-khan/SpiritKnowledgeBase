Two separate devices, with separate ratings. Do not order one against the
other's numbers. **Both MT200 manuals print the same figures.**

**Fuse rating - the circuit breaker.**

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Circuit breaker | AC 125V, 20A |
| Mode of tripping | Thermal |
| Operating speed | 0.25 ~ 3.0 sec |
| Breaking capacity | 1000 A, 125 VAC |

**Fuse on inverter.**

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Voltage | 125 Vac |
| Current | 1 A |
| Operating speed | 0.4 ~ 1.0 sec |
| Breaking capacity | 50 A, 125 Vac |
| Size | 2.65 mm × 7.1 mm |

**The breaker is not the 8.0T's.** The 2026 8.0T is rated **AC 250 V, 10 A** with
a 500 A breaking capacity - half the current at twice the voltage, and a fifth of
the breaking capacity. Only the inverter fuse is the same part on both machines
(125 Vac, 1 A, 0.4~1.0 sec, 50 A, 2.65 × 7.1 mm). See
`80t-2026-specs-fuse-and-breaker-ratings`, and note that the 2026 7.0T and 4.0T
manuals print no fuse or breaker rating at all.

**Neither manual gives a part number for either device, and neither gives a
replacement procedure.** The breaker is item 292 on the parts list, described
only as "Breaker". The A.C. input module that carries it is item 265.

**Where the breaker is.** The A.C. mains input connector is at the front of the
unit; the input module holds the line-cord connector, the power switch and the
20 amp circuit breaker.

The 20 A here is the breaker inside the machine. The 20 A grounded outlet the
manual asks the machine to be plugged into is a separate requirement, on the
safety page.
