**Interface boards (section 6.3).** Two boards, both marked **AA02260-00T-01**:

- **K1 - "Inside of the console"**: KEYPAD, a **16PIN CABLE** and an **8 PIN CABLE** to the DISPLAY BOARD,
  HAND PULSE, GROUNDING. The display board end is drawn with the sockets **JK3**, **RF_JK5** and **JK8**
  and a SAFETY KEY plug.
- **K2 - "Inside of console bracket"** (the XT685 says "Beside of the console"): USB CHARGING, HAND PULSE
  (GRIP), INCLINE UP/DOWN (HANDRAIL), SPEED FAST/SLOW (HANDRAIL), HR RECEIVER, SAFETY KEY.

**Amplify board (section 6.4).** Marked **AG0003-V1.2-220513**, with four plugs: **SPEAKER** (twice),
**BLE MUSIC POWER** and **BLE MUSIC SIGNAL**. The XT685 book prints the same four names over a photograph
of the same board.

No pin definitions are printed for any of these plugs. The XT185 and XT285 books of 2023 have no
interface boards and no amplify-board section; the 2015 XT385 / XT485 amplifier is a different board
(8509) with AUDIO IN / OUT jacks.
