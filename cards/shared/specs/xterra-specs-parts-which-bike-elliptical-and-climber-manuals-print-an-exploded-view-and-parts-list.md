**Two books print a drawing with no list.** The SB240's two exploded pages number 81 parts and the FB180's one page 74, and nothing in either book says what the numbers are. The FB180 numbering matches the FB160 list on every callout checked (08 console, 07 seat, 39 flywheel, 65 belt, 72 Allen key, 73 wrench, 09L/R pedals, 15L/R cranks), and the book adds a 74 the FB160 list does not have; that is a useful hint for a service call, not a list - the FB180 (SKU 118425) is a 2025 bike and the FB160 list is from 2019. A customer asking for "part 44 on my SB240" cannot be answered from the manual.

**Where a list exists, the packed tools are its last rows** - an Allen key and a hex or multifunctional wrench (FB160/FB360 items 72-73, SB120 items 72-73, UB120 items 54-55, SB4500 items 60-61). The SB600 service manual's own tool list is five tools (`sb600-2023-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-five-tools-and-grease`).

**Twenty-two books print neither**, and the absence was checked in the native text, in a spacing-tolerant search (*E X P L O D*, *P A R T S L I S T*, *Part No*, *Serial No*, *BOM*) and in the render-vs-text sweep that appended every image-only page - including the six scans, whose every page is OCR. The *Part No. / Description / Q'TY* tables those searches do find on pages 6-8 are the assembly pack checklists, which list the carton contents and hardware bag against numbers from an unprinted drawing; they are on the assembly cards. The Xterra treadmill books split the same way (`xterra-treadmill-specs-parts-which-manuals-print-an-exploded-view-and-parts-list`); the Spirit cycle and bike books are on `spirit-cycle-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list` and `spirit-bike-specs-parts-which-service-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.
