Five of the six Xterra rower service manuals open with a labelled drawing of the machine; the ERG750W's does not (its contents page lists *Console mount assembly* and *Water Tank Assembly* and goes straight to the photo steps). Every label they print is below, read from the text layer and checked against the OCR of the page.

| Service manual | Heading and page | Labels |
|---|---|---|
| ERG160 (erg160-2020) | *Contents* drawing, PDF p. 2 - the owner's manual's *Before you begin* drawing reused | Console, Handlebar, Front Stabilizer, Pedals, Serial Number Sticker (with the (870) 333-5500 number), Warning Label, Rear Stabilizer |
| ERG180 (erg180-2023) | *GENERAL INFORMATION - Main Parts Indication*, PDF p. 3, numbered 1-11 | 1 Console, 2 Pedal R, 3 Seat, 4 Slide Rail, 5 Rear Support Tube, 6 Handlebar, 7 Tension Control Knob, 8 Decorative Cover, 9 Chain Cover, 10 Front Stabilizer Tube, 11 Pedal L |
| ERG550W (erg550w-2023) | *ERG550W Outlines*, PDF p. 3 (the contents page is bilingual, Chinese and English) | Console, Handlebar & Strap, Chain cover, Seat, Slide Aluminum Plate, Tank, Plastic Impeller, Roller, Front Stabilizer, Rear Stabilizer, Pedal Strap, Adjust Knob |
| ERG600W (erg600w-2021) | *ERG600W Outlines*, PDF p. 3 | Console, Decorative cover, Handlebar, Mesh Belt Wheel, Pedal Strap, Pedal, Seat, Slide Rail, Front stabilizer, Impeller, Rear Stabilizer, Tank, Stopper, End Cap |
| ERG800W (erg800w-2023) | *GENERAL INFORMATION - Main Parts Indication*, PDF p. 3, numbered 1-13 | 1 Console, 2 Mesh Belt Wheel, 3 Pedal Strap, 4 Impeller, 5 Tank, 6 Front Stabilizer, 7 Handlebar, 8 Pedal, 9 Seat, 10 Slide Rail, 11 Stopper, 12 End Cap, 13 Rear Stabilizer |

**These numbers and names are not part codes.** The ERG180 drawing's 1-11 are not the 1-95 of its parts list (the console is 91 there, the tension control knob 30 - `erg180-2023-specs-parts-list-items-1-to-95-from-the-service-manual`); the ERG800W drawing's 1-13 are not its lettered codes (the console is H, the tank A64, the stopper C5 - `erg800w-2023-specs-parts-list-lettered-codes-from-the-service-manual`). The ERG550W and ERG600W drawings are the only place their service manuals name a part at all, because neither prints a list (`xterra-specs-parts-which-rower-and-strength-manuals-print-an-exploded-view-and-parts-list`). The words are the ones the replacement chapters use: the *Mesh Belt Wheel* is the drum the pull strap winds onto, the *Stopper* the block at the end of the slide rail that the seat stops against, the *Decorative cover* the trim over the console mast joint, the *Impeller* the paddle inside the tank.

The ERG800W service manual also prints a **CONSOLE - Appearance** page (PDF p. 4) that labels the Tablet Holder, LCD Window and the RESET, ENTER, START, STOP, UP, DOWN and program buttons; no other rower service manual has an electronic-parts chapter, and none prints a part number on these pages. The Xterra treadmill and bike service manuals open the same way (`xterra-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-outlines-and-electronic-parts-named`, `sb600-2023-specs-parts-main-parts-indication-thirty-named-parts`).
