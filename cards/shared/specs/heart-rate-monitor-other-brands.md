**Scope.** Every declared SOLE model except the nine 2016 to 2020 bikes with Dyaco service manuals. Those bikes are out of scope because no source says a watch or a third-party strap connects to their consoles.

**Connect directly, no app needed**

- Polar Series HRM
- Scosche Rhythm HRM
- Sigma Sport HRM

**Need an app open to connect to the equipment**

| Watch | App needed |
|---|---|
| Galaxy Watch 4 | SOLE+ Android version |
| Galaxy Watch 5 | SOLE+ Android version |
| Galaxy Watch 6 | SOLE+ Android version |
| Galaxy Watch 7 | SOLE+ Android version |
| Apple Watch Series | SOLE+ iOS version |

The source lists no model numbers for the Polar, Scosche or Sigma straps, and no Apple Watch series numbers. It names the brands and families only.

For the Apple Watch setup steps and its August 2021 machine limit, see `sole-apple-watch-heart-rate`.
