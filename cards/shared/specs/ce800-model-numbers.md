The numbers come from the customer-service model table (`dbo.MODEL` in `CustService`); a manual rarely prints one. A row reading *not confirmed* has no settled number yet. The 2016 row is the "XE890B-AE10M" service manual's machine; the 2013, 2021 and 2023 owner's manuals have no settled row.
