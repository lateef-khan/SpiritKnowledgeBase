- **NEVER** expose this fitness bike to rain or moisture. This product is **NOT** designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.
- Operating temperature specification: **40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit**.
- Humidity: **95% non-condensing** (no water drops forming on surfaces).

**These five manuals have no electrical instructions page.** They print the environment limits inside Important Operation Instructions instead. They do **not** contain the warning about removing covers with AC power connected, and they do **not** contain the clause about voltage varying by ten percent (10%) or more not being covered under warranty. Those two statements belong to the B94/R92, B74/R72, LCB 2011, LCR 2011 and LCB/LCR 2023 manuals, which do carry an Important Electrical Instructions page.
