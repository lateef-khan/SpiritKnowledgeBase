Section 3 names three parts:

- **CONSOLE** - the interface that controls all functions of the bike; it contains the key
  controls and an **LED display**.
- **MAIN CONTROLLER** - the circuit board that holds the **DC power supply for the console**, and
  the driver control circuit.
- **GENERATOR BRAKE** - "it can change to increase or decrease resistance level of brake".

**No voltage, current or resistance figure is printed anywhere in the section.** The bike has no
adapter: the generator in the flywheel powers the console, so the "DC power supply" is fed by
pedalling (see `spirit-bike-safety-no-mains-outlet-needed`).

The chapter-2 photographs (the five parts they name are on `cu900ent-electronic-parts-locations`)
caption the flywheel with its coil pack **HYBRID GENERATOR**. So the one unit is *Generator Brake*
in section 3, *Hybrid Generator* on the photograph and the circuit diagram, and
*Generator/Resistance* on the outline drawing.

**Not the ENT.** The CU900ENT and CR900ENT books name an *EMS flywheel* fed from a 24 V adapter;
see `cu900ent-electrical-part-descriptions`.
