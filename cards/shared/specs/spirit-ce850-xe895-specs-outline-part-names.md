Three drawings in every book. The XE895-2016 book is the CE850-2016 book under its export name and
prints the three pages unchanged; the CE850-2020 redraws the first page around a photograph of the
newer machine and keeps the other two.

**Shrouded machine (CE850-2016 and XE895 p. 5, CE850-2020 p. 4):** Handle Bar Cover (R), Console
Assembly, Console Mast Cover (L) and (R), Side Case (L) and (R), Side Case Rear Shroud, Right
Slider Cover, Pedal (L) and (R), Connecting Arm Cover (R)(A), Rear Stabilizer Cover, Slide Wheel
Cover. The CE850-2020 prints the left one as **Mast Cover (L)**.

**Frame (p. 6 / p. 5):** Seat Handle Bar (L), Console Mast, Swing Arm (R), Lower Handle Bar (L) and
(R), **Moving Range Adjusting Assembly**, Flywheel, Main Frame, Connecting Arm (L) and (R), Left
Pedal Base, Right Pedal Base, Pedal Arm (L) and (R), Rear Rail Assembly.

**Stride mechanism (p. 7 / p. 6):** Adjusting Lever, Swing Assembly, **Incline Motor**, Slider
Adjustment (L) and (R), Wheel Base (twice; the CE850-2020 labels the right one **(R) Wheel Base**),
Bushing Housing, Pedal Arm, Crank Arm Assembly, Left Driving Assembly, Right Driving Assembly,
Joint Assembly (twice), **Gear Motor**, connecting Component.

**The actuator is labelled Incline Motor on a machine with no incline.** The wiring chapter of the
same books calls it the *stride motor*: it moves the *Moving Range Adjusting Assembly* to set the
stride length. The *Gear Motor* is the tension motor. The Outlines chapter prints **no dimensions,
no machine weight and no user weight limit**. The XE395's frame carries a real incline motor and
its own labels (`xe395-2016-specs-outline-part-names`).
