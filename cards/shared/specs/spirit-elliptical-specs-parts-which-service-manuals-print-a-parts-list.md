**Of sixteen Spirit elliptical service manuals, one prints parts lists with Spirit part numbers: the 2008
dealer manual for the XE100, XE200, XE300, XE400 and XE500.** It prints an exploded view and a numbered
list for each of the five - 136, 147, 165, 182 and 197 rows with six-digit numbers and no quantity column
(`xe100-2007-specs-parts-list`, `xe200-2007-specs-parts-list`, `xe300-2007-specs-parts-list`,
`xe400-2007-specs-parts-list`, `xe500-2007-specs-parts-list`).

**The fifteen Dyaco-template books print no list and no Spirit part number anywhere.** What each prints
instead:

| Book | Closest thing to a parts list |
|---|---|
| CE900 (SE8800-SE026), CE900ENT | a 33-row *Component Description* of the plastic covers with call-out numbers and quantities, no part numbers (`spirit-ce900-specs-parts-component-description-33-rows`) |
| CE1000ENT 2023 | a 28-row *Outlines* item table of covers with quantities, call-out numbers only |
| XE795 2023 | two *Outlines* tables, plastic parts and structures, numbered names only |
| CE800 2016 (XE890B), CE800 (2020), CE850 2016 (XE898), CE850 (2020), XE195, XE295, XE395, XE395ENT, XE795 2016, XG400, XE895 | *Outlines* drawings with part names as callouts - no numbers of any kind |

**Three books name parts by code inside their procedures, and that is all the identity they give:**

- The CE850 2016 and XE895 books name three bushings by maker's code - **J4FM-1719-09** in the joint
  assembly, adjusting lever and swing assembly, **WFM-2528-21** in the pedal arm's bushing housing and
  **WFM-1719-12** in the rear rail assembly - and cite item numbers **#137, #176, #165** and **#16** from
  a parts list neither book prints. (The Sole E95S books, built on the same template, print the same
  three codes: `e95s-2019-pedal-arm-replacement`.)
- The XG400 book names its gear motor **YJ-9902** and its two belts by type
  (`xg400-2016-specs-parts-gear-motor-and-belts-named-by-type`).
- Most of the books name bearings by size - 6005 at the crank or flywheel axle, 6203 in the idler wheel -
  and every one names fasteners by thread inside its replacement steps; those stay with the procedures.

**Board silkscreen codes are not part numbers.** The chapter-6 photographs show PCB codes (CS-series
CoreStar boards) on the display, interface and driver boards; they identify a board revision, not an
orderable part, and belong with the wiring cards.

**The owner's manuals are a separate question.** Which of them print a list is on
`spirit-ce-assembly-exploded-view-and-parts-list`: for the five 2007 XE machines the owner's book prints
drawings and no list, so the dealer manual is the only printed source of their part numbers. The bike
service manuals follow the same pattern - only the 2008 dealer book prints numbers
(`spirit-bike-specs-parts-which-service-manuals-print-a-parts-list`).
