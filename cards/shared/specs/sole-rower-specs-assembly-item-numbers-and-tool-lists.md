None of the three books prints a standalone parts list; parts information lives in the assembly pack checklist and the assembly steps, whose callouts name the MAIN FRAME (1) and FRONT STABILIZER (2) alongside hardware numbers. All callouts below verified on the render (the OCR confuses Ø with O/©/@ — the diameters here are as printed).

**Shared steps 1-3 (all three books).** Step 1: #109 Ø3/8" × Ø19 × 1.5T Flat Washer (4); #121 Ø10 × 2T washer (4) — **Spring Washer** in both SR500 books, **Split Washer** in the SR550 book (both verified on the render, carded as printed); #120 3/8" × 3/4" Hex Head Bolt (4). Step 3 (SR500 numbering): #122 Ø5/16" × Ø18 × 1.5T Flat Washer (4); #123 M8 × P1.25 × 12L Button Head Socket Bolt (5).

**Divergences.** The SR500 books keep step 2 as #129 M6 × P1.0 × 10L Button Head Socket Bolt (4) alone, while the SR550 book folds #122 (4), #123 (5) and #129 (4) into its step 2. Step 4 in the SR500-2019 book: #125 Ø1/4" Spring Washer (4), #122 Flat Washer (6), #124 M6 × 15L Phillips Head Screw (4), #123 Button Head Socket Bolt (6), #85 M5 × 10L Phillips Head Screw (3). The SR500-2022 book adds #134 Ø5 × 0.6T Star Washer (2), #133 M5 × 15L Phillips Head Screw (2) and #136 Ø5.5 × Ø19 × 1.5T Flat Washer (2). The SR550 book instead closes with console-mast hardware: #85 M5 × P0.8 × 10L Phillips (6), #147 M8 × 20L / #148 Ø8 × Ø19 × 1.5T Flat Washer, #149 Ø8 × Ø23 × 1.5T Curved Washer (2), #150 4 × 16mm Sheet Metal Screw (5).

**Assembly tools (all three books).** #128 13/14m/m Wrench (sic — genuinely printed "m/m", verified on the render); #127 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver; #126 M4 Allen Wrench.
