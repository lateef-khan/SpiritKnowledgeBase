**The two books do not print the same power supply row.**

| Manual | Power supply row |
|---|---|
| **8.5S-2025** | **100 ~ 240 Vac, 1.76 ~ 0.71 Amps, 50/60Hz** |
| **8.5S-FIT-2026** | **120V/15AMP Cord/Outlet** |

**These are two different statements, not two wordings of one.** The 8.5S row
describes what the machine will accept - any mains supply between 100 and 240
volts, at 50 or 60 Hz, drawing 1.76 A at the bottom of that range and 0.71 A at
the top. The 8.5S-FIT row describes the socket it must be plugged into, names one
voltage, and gives a circuit rating instead of a draw.

**Do not tell an 8.5S-FIT owner their machine is 100-240 V**, and do not tell an
8.5S owner they need a 15 amp circuit. Quote the row from their own book, and
where a customer needs to know whether the machine will run abroad, refer them to
Spirit Fitness rather than reading the older row across.

The two books differ on the product weight row as well - the 8.5S prints
150.9 kg and the 8.5S-FIT leaves the figure blank - so this is not the only place
the FIT diverges. See `spirit-climber-85s-specs-dimensions-and-weight`.

## Neither machine has an external adapter

There is no power brick and no Sinpro part number on either specification page.
The MS300, 7.0S and 7.5S of the same family do have one, and it is not a part for
these machines - see
`spirit-climber-specs-twelve-volt-dc-from-a-sinpro-supply`.

## Both take the same fuse

Both books print the same fuse row, and it is the only fuse specified anywhere in
the family - see `spirit-climber-specs-fuse-rating`.

## The outlet and circuit are also a safety question

Each book's electrical safety page states the grounding and outlet requirements
in its own words. That page belongs to the `safety` section; this card holds only
what the specification page prints.
