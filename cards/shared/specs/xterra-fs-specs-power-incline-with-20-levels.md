**Twenty incline levels.** "Incline/Ramp shows incline level from 1 to 20." The books describe it as "an exciting
electronic incline feature ... When the incline is at its lowest position you get a traditional elliptical workout.
As the incline increases you will feel your knees rise higher with each step, which changes the emphasis of the
muscles being used. The power incline is controlled by keys on the console and also automatically during the built-in
workout programs." (The FS5.8e page misprints its own model as "FS 5.6e" in that sentence.)

Hardware the assembly pages name: an **Incline Rail Assembly** that slides into the main frame's U channel, an
**incline motor** whose wires join the rail harness "by matching the colors red, white and black", an **Incline
Cable** into the console's Incline port beside the Resistance cable in the port labelled "Load", and **incline
buttons on the front of the left upper swing arm** (wire 166) with the resistance buttons on the right (wire 165).
The console shows an Altitude (elevation gain) readout and an incline profile, and a preset program asks for a Max
Incline as well as a Max Level; the FS5.9e book adds an incline calibration (Incline Up and Start held five seconds)
that the console section holds.

The percentage grade is not printed. No other Xterra elliptical, bike or stepper in this wave has an incline; the
Xterra WS300 walking treadmill's range is on `ws300-2023-specs-incline-range-0-to-10-percent`.
