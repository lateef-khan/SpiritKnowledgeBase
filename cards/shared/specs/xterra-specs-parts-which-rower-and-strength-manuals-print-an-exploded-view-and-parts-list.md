**Six rower books cite part numbers they never define.** The ERG220 assembly pack checklist names items 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 12 and 38 and hardware #11, #15, #34, #44, #46, #50, #52, #55, #59, #62, #64, #65, #81, #97, #98 (and a code *RE-6030* on its hardware page); the ERG500 checklist names 1/91, 3, 8L/R, 9, 11L/R, 13, 14L/R, 35, 45, 53, 54, 55 and hardware 19, 34, 36-38, 40L/R, 41-43, 92, 93; the ERG600W checklist names 1, 2, 3, 4, 25, 26, 35, 36, 46, 76, 77, 82, 83 and its tank page No. 12, 33, 76, 77; the ERG650W tank page No. 24, 50, 52, 53; the ERG700 steps #6, #51, #109, #116, #120, #123, #130, #135, #136, #137, #139; the ERG400 scan hardware #93-#95 and tools #96-#97 under *ALL OTHER PARTS PRE-ASSEMBLED ON UNIT*. None of those six prints the drawing or the list the numbers belong to, so a customer asking what part 46 is on an ERG600W cannot be answered from the book. Those checklists are the carton and hardware contents and sit on the assembly cards.

**The four rower service manuals without a list are photo-step books.** The ERG160, ERG550W, ERG600W and ERG750W service manuals replace parts by photograph; three of them open with an outline drawing that names the main parts (`xterra-rower-specs-parts-service-manual-outlines-and-main-parts-indication-names`) and none prints a tool list (`xterra-rower-specs-parts-service-manuals-print-no-tool-list-the-steps-name-the-wrenches`).

**Absence was checked** in the native text, in a spacing-tolerant search (*E X P L O D*, *P A R T S L I S T*, *Part No*, *S/N*, *spare*), in the OCR supplements the render sweep appended, and by measuring the ink on every page the text layer shows as blank (the ERG600W's pp. 25-27, the ERG650W's pp. 22-23 and the ERG550W's pp. 30-31 carry a folio and nothing else). The Xterra bike and treadmill books split the same way (`xterra-specs-parts-which-bike-elliptical-and-climber-manuals-print-an-exploded-view-and-parts-list`, `xterra-treadmill-specs-parts-which-manuals-print-an-exploded-view-and-parts-list`); the Spirit rowers are `spirit-rower-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list` and `spirit-rower-specs-parts-which-service-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.
