**Three footpad angle settings: zero (0) degrees (flat) at the lowest position,
five (5) degrees in the middle, ten (10) degrees at the top**, measured at the
bottom of the elliptical stroke. The adjustment supports the heel to relieve
pressure on the nerves in the balls of the feet.

**The mechanism differs by book while the values stay 0 to 10:**

- **Pull-pin or red quick-release handle** (three fixed positions): 2007 E55 /
  E75 / E95, VE25, VE35 / VE95, all WE models, AE35, E35 and E95 of 2013-2015,
  and E35 of 2016 / 2019 / 2023.
- **Red knob, 0 to 10 degrees with a side scale** (infinitely adjustable):
  VE55. The AE55 / AE95 books use the same knob and describe three settings
  across the same 0-10 range.

**Attribution follows the book headers.** The 2007 books limit the feature to
E55 (locking pins) and E75 / E95 respectively; the ae25-ae35-2011 book marks it
E35 only. No E20, E25 (2012+), E55 (2013+), E95 (2016+), E95s or E98 book prints
adjustable footpads at all. The step-by-step adjustment lives with the
maintenance foot-pedal procedure card; the Xterra twin prints the same three
angles
(`fs59e-2014-specs-adjustable-footplate-with-three-pedal-angles-0-5-and-10-degrees`).
