On these three machines the service manual's *Outlines* chapter is a **product photograph and nothing else**: no
numbered or boxed callouts, no part names, no dimensions.

| Book | Page | What is on it |
|---|---|---|
| TR150 (GT65-NT014) | PDF p. 5 | a colour photograph of the folded-frame treadmill, three-quarter view |
| TRX3500 / TRX4500 (GT90C-NT023 / GT90D-NT024) | PDF p. 5, headed **GT90C-NT023** | a colour photograph of the TRX3500 |
| TRX3500 / TRX4500 | PDF p. 6, headed **GT90D-NT024** | a colour photograph of the TRX4500 |

The TR150 book therefore names no parts anywhere in chapter 1; its chapter 2 (Electronic Parts) captions only
"Console" and "Lower Controller Area". The TRX3500/TRX4500 book likewise names parts only in chapter 2 ("SPEED
SENSOR", "Lower Controller", "Incline Motor", "DC Motor") and in the wiring chapter. The TR260, TRX1400, TRX2500 and
TRX5500 books, by contrast, print callouts on their outline drawings and have their own cards.

No Xterra service manual in this wave prints a dimension on its outline page.
