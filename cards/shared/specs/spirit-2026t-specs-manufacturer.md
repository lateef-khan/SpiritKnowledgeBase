**Dyaco International Inc., No. 1, Gong 1st Rd., Hemei Township, Changhua County
50843, Taiwan.**

Both specification pages carry the identical entry. On the 7.0T page it sits
inside the factory symbol used on medical labelling; on the 8.0T page it is set
as plain text under the heading "Manufacturer".

**The 4.0T manual names no manufacturer and prints no address** - it has no
specification page at all. Do not assume the same entry applies to it without a
source; see `40t-2026-specs-no-specification-table`.

The 2025 7.0T page carries the identical entry, inside the factory symbol.
