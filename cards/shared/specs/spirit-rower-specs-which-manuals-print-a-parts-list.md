**Three of the six print a parts list. Three print nothing of the kind.**

| Manual | Exploded view | Parts list | Numbering |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CRW800-2021** air rower | printed p. 40 (PDF p. 42) | printed pp. 41-42 (PDF pp. 43-44) | items **1 - 139**, with gaps |
| **CRW800-2024** air rower | printed p. 35 (PDF p. 37) | printed pp. 36-37 (PDF pp. 38-39) | items **1 - 139**, with gaps |
| **CRW900-2021** water rower | printed pp. 55-60 (PDF pp. 55-60) | on the drawings themselves | **five-digit part numbers**, no item numbers |
| **XRW600-2019** rower | **none** | **none** | - |
| **XRW600-2021** rower | **none** | **none** | - |
| **CRW800H2O-2021** water rower | **none** | **none** | - |

## The CRW800-2021 table of contents is off by one

Its contents page promises `39 EXPLODED VIEW` and `40 PARTS LIST`. **Printed p. 39
is the warranty SERVICE page.** The exploded view is printed p. 40 and the parts
list printed pp. 41-42, each confirmed from the page's own printed folio. **Do not
conclude the pages are missing because printed p. 39 is warranty text**, and do
not carry the contents-page numbers into an answer.

The exploded-view page itself is a flat image with no heading - a 300 dpi render
of it returns only the callout numbers `126 127 128` and the `Spirit Fitness`
footer. The drawing is unreadable as text; the parts list beside it is not, and
both of its pages carry a clean native text layer.

## The CRW800-2021 list is headed with the factory drawing code

Its first parts page is headed **`CW800B-YR003-01 Part List_SPIRIT`**, and the
last row is followed by a bare **`200428`** which is a drawing date code, not an
item. **`CW800B` is the factory's code for this machine and it is not a model
number** - the 2024 book dropped the header, printing only `PARTS LIST`, and
carries `CW800B` invisibly in the text layer of its exploded-view page instead.
See `crw800-2024-specs-parts-list`.

The `... Part List_SPIRIT` header is the same form the 2021 commercial bike and
stepper manuals carry - see `spirit-bike-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`
and `spirit-climber-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.

## The CRW900 does not have a parts list in the sense the others do

It prints **seven numbered drawing sheets over six pages**, each drawing with its
own `PN / Qty / Description` table beside it: Assembly Overview, Main Frame
Assembly (two sheets on one page), the tank, the Sliding Footplate Assembly, the
handle and belt/bungee pulley, the seat and side rails, and the Elevate Kit. The
footer of each sheet carries a sheet number running **2 to 8**; **no sheet 1 is
printed.**

**Its part numbers are five-digit numbers and lettered assembly codes, not item
numbers**: 93 five-digit parts from `10052` to `90322`, five sub-assembly codes
**`A159`, `A385`, `A386`, `A387` and `A1078`**, and one kit code **`EVK`** for the
Elevate Kit. A number read off a CRW800 list means nothing here and the reverse is
equally true. **`A1078`, the 10 Level Tank Complete Assembly, is marked
`Not Sold Separately`** - see `crw900-2021-specs-ten-level-tank-adjuster`.

## The XRW600 books point at a drawing they do not contain

Both XRW600 manuals say, on their pre-assembly page:

> The numbers in the instructions that are in parenthesis (#) are the item number
> from the assembly drawing for reference.

**There is no assembly drawing with item numbers in either book.** The folding
page then calls out `Handlebar (37)` and `Knob (47)`, and the reader has nothing
to look them up in. A search of both whole documents - native text and every one
of the 17 and 18 OCR-supplemented pages - returns no `Main Frame ... 1` row, no
item-number column and no occurrence of *exploded*, *parts list* or *part list*.
Both tables of contents end at the warranty on printed p. 38, followed by the back
cover.

**Do not answer an XRW600 callout number from the CRW800 list.** They are
different books and the numbers do not line up: CRW800 item 37 is a `Belt`, not a
handlebar.

## The CRW800H2O's only parts content is a hardware page

Its printed p. 13, headed `CRW800H2O PARTS`, is a flat image. A 300 dpi render of
it returns fastener callouts - `#63 M8*45 4pcs`, `#65 M6*15 6pcs`, `#68 M8*15
4pcs`, `#74 OD20*ID8.5*1.5 4pcs`, `#77 M8*27 2pcs`, `#93 S5 1pc`, `#94 S13-17
1pc`, `#99 M8*57 4pcs` - and nothing else. **That is the hardware kit, not a parts
list**; it names no frame, tank or console part. The assembly parts checklist on
printed p. 12 names nine items by number (1, 2, 3R/3L, 38, 52, 53, 54) and stops
there.

For part names on a CRW800H2O the only source in this repository is the service
manual's outline drawing - `crw800h2o-outlines-part-names`.

## The running count across the Spirit ranges

This is the rower and ergometer count. The other lines are counted separately
because `product_line` is a single value and a card cannot span two:

- **3 of 6** rowers - this card.
- **8 of 16** steppers, stair climbers and vertical climbers -
  `spirit-climber-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.
- **8 of 13** commercial bikes - `spirit-bike-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.
- **6 of 14** residential bikes - `spirit-residential-bike-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.
- **3 of 8** indoor cycles and air bikes - `spirit-cycle-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.
- The **8.5UE upper body ergometer** prints two exploded-view drawings and **no
  list at all** - `85ue-2025-specs-exploded-view-with-no-parts-list`.

## The service manuals, counted separately

Four rower **service** manuals were read the same way and none prints a list. The CRW800
(2020) service manual prints the `CW800B` exploded view - the same drawing, balloons 1 to 139 -
with no list beside it, so the owner's manual's list above is what decodes it; the CRW800 2016,
XRW600 and CRW900 service manuals print neither. See
`spirit-rower-specs-parts-which-service-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.
