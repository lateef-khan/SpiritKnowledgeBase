**There are two different machines called 7.0S and two called 7.5S.** They are five to six years
apart, and every book of the older pair prints figures the newer pair does not. Getting the wrong
one gives the customer the wrong footprint and the wrong watt ceiling.

| | **2021 books** (MED-badged; MS300) | **2025 books** (Spirit-badged) |
|---|---|---|
| 7.0S footprint | **67" x 30" x 48"** | **69.7" x 33.5" x 48"** |
| 7.5S footprint | **80.3" x 33.1" x 46.9"** | **77.6" x 33.5" x 46.5"** |
| Work load ceiling | **650 watts** | **1000 watts** |
| Machine weight | 257.4 lb / 305.8 lb | the same two figures |
| Revision stamp | **Rev. 1.2.1 2021/10/14** on both MED books | **12.18.2025** (7.0S), **12.15.2025** (7.5S) |

**The weight will not tell them apart.** Both 7.0S books print 257.4 lb and both 7.5S books print
305.8 lb. **Measure the length.**

## The MS300 and the MED 7.0S are the same machine under two badges

Not a resemblance - the same book. The MS300's specification page prints **67" x 30" x 48",
257.4 lb, 5 to 650 watts, Sinpro HPU32A-105** and so does the MED 7.0S's, row for row. Their parts
lists share every item number and every quantity; their four assembly steps and four hardware
panels are the same. And the MS300's own data-transfer chapter shows the software reporting the
connected product as **`Connected 7.0S`**, on its own page. The MS300 book is the Spirit-badged
edition of the MED 7.0S book, and its cover carries a stray **`7.0 S`**.

The two differ in three places only: the MED book adds a **CE 0123 / MDD 93/42/EEC Class Im** row
and a **Dyaco Europe GmbH** authorised-representative address, the MS300 adds a **Class II
measuring, Type BF** classification line, and the MED book **prints no warranty page at all**.

## What actually changed between the generations

The 2025 machines are not a re-badge. Their parts lists differ from the 2021 ones in the rotating
mass and the seat rails:

| Item | 2021 books | 2025 books |
|---|---|---|
| 7, 8 | Seat Slider (R), (L) *(7.0S)* | Handle Slider (R), (L) |
| 25, 26, 27 | Front Connecting Cable, Rear Connecting Cable, Drive Cable x2 | Steel Cable 845L, 935L, 1820L x2 |
| 42, 43 | Slider Sleeve x2, **Flywheel Mass x1** | Hollow Plug x2, **Curved weight Block x3** |
| 181 (pedal screws, 7.5S) | **M5 x 15L**, 12 pcs | **M5 x 12L**, 12 pcs |

The braking parts did not change - **item 44 Gear Motor, item 45 Braking Magnet x4, item 61
Aluminum Brake Disc** are the same in all of them
(`spirit-climber-specs-rehabilitation-stepper-brake-magnets-on-a-disc`).

## Telling them apart without a tape measure

- **A cover reading `MED` with `Dyaco` in the body and no mention of Spirit** is the 2021 book.
- **A book with a warranty chapter** is not a MED book; the MED books have none.
- **A book that says `5 watts up to 1000 watts`** is 2025.
- **A book with a `Rev. 1.2.1 2021/10/14` line on its last page** is a MED book. The MS300's last
  page carries **`2020© Spirit Fitness`** and no revision line; the 2025 books carry a
  `Revision:` date.

**Both MED PDFs were exported on 19 October 2021 from Microsoft Word 2010, five days after the
revision date they print.** The MS300 PDF was exported on 24 November 2021 from Adobe Illustrator.
Three files, one generation.

**The owner has ruled that a MED-badged Dyaco book is still a Spirit machine.** Only a manual that
says XTERRA is a different brand. These are carded as `brand: spirit`.
