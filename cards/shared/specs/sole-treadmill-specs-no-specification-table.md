**Neither the twenty-five legacy books nor the twenty-four stencil-era books print a specification table.** No book states the running-surface size, the drive-motor horsepower or wattage, the assembled, folded or shipping dimensions, the product weight, the roller diameter, a user-weight figure, a wiring or circuit diagram, or an FCC/IC page. The word *specification* occurs in prose only: the electrical page's temperature/humidity sentence and the warranty page's "Product features or specifications as described or illustrated are subject to change without notice".

What the books print instead, and where the figure lives:

| Figure a spec table would carry | What the books print | Card |
|---|---|---|
| Speed range | 0.5 to 12.0 mph calibration defaults (0.5 to 10 on F60-2016; 0.5 to 12 in F60-2020 engineering mode; 0.5 to 4.0 on the TD80 desk treadmill) | `sole-treadmill-specs-speed-range-0-5-to-12-mph`, `f60-2016-specs-speed-range-0-5-to-10-mph`, `td80-2019-specs-speed-range-0-5-to-4-mph` |
| Incline range | 15 max elevation (0 to 12 levels on F60-2016/2020; 0 to 15 on F80-2023 Custom; -6 to +15 elevation on F85/F89/TT8-2023; none on TD80) | `sole-treadmill-specs-incline-range-to-15-percent`, `f60-2016-specs-incline-range-0-to-12-levels`, `sole-treadmill-specs-elevation-minus-6-to-plus-15`, `td80-2019-specs-no-incline` |
| Start speed | 0.5 mph (1.0 km on F63/UF63-2006; also F60-2020 and the 2021 touchscreens; other stencil books state none) | `sole-treadmill-specs-belt-start-speed-0-5-mph`, `sole-f63-specs-belt-start-speed-1-0-km` |
| Sleep power draw | Very little, about a TV turned off (stencil button books; other stencil books print no draw figure) | `sole-treadmill-specs-sleep-mode-power-draw` |
| Outlet, plug, circuit rating | 110- or 120-volt grounded outlet/circuit prose | the safety section's electrical cards |
| Operating temperature and humidity | 40 C / 95% or 40-120 F / 95% prose | the safety section's electrical cards |
| User weight limit | No figure anywhere; only program bodyweight entries | the safety section (no legacy figure to card) |
| Motor rating, belt size, dimensions, weight, wiring diagrams, FCC | Nowhere | this card is the whole answer |

The Xterra treadmill books lack a spec table in the same way (`xterra-treadmill-specs-no-specification-table-in-nineteen-owners-manuals`).
