**Asleep, the treadmill draws very little power, about as much as a television that is turned off, so leaving the main power switch on in sleep mode is fine.** Three wordings print the same figure: the 2011-2015 and WF63 books say "When the console is 'asleep' the treadmill draws very little power, about as much as your television when it is turned off"; the 2007-2010 UF/VF/VS/VTT/WF80/WS/WTT8 books say "There is only a tiny amount of current used in sleep mode (about the same as your TV when it is turned off)"; the TT9-2018 book says "very little electric current (like a TV turned off) in the sleeping mode". The sleep timeout itself (30 minutes, 10 on the WF63) is a console setting, not this card. The outlet and circuit rating that feed the machine are safety-section cards. The F60-2016, F63/UF63-2006, F80-family-2006, TT8/UTT8-2006 and VF63-2007 books print no sleep power sentence and are not on this card.

The stencil-era button books print the same figure in two inherited wordings: the 2016/2019 books say "about as much as your television when it is turned off" and the 2023/2025 books say "for about as much as a television turned off". Their timeouts differ (30 minutes on 2016/2019, 15 on 2023+) but the timeout is a console setting, not this card. The F60-2020, TD80, 2021 touchscreen and 2023 TFT books print no draw sentence and are not on this card.
