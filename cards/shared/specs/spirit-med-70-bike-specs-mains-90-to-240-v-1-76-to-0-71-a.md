**Power - 90-240V ~: 50/60 Hz: 1.76-0.71A.** The same row in all three books. The set-up page
explains the range: "The 7.0R has a built-in universal power supply. You can plug the 7.0R into any
A.C. power source from 90 to 240 volts, 50 to 60 Hz. The A.C. input is located in the front of the
7.0R. The input module has an input connector for the line cord, a power switch and a 5 amp fuse."
The current figure is the machine's draw, highest at the lowest voltage.

**The 2021 Dyaco MED book's symbol page prints "AC power 90-240 Volt, 15Amps, 50Hz"** beside the
mains symbol - 15 A being the circuit its safety page asks for, not the draw, and 50 Hz only
where the specification row says 50/60. Its safety instructions, like the 2025 books', call for a
"90 to 240-volt AC, 50/60 Hz, 15-amp grounded outlet"; the outlet, cord and grounding rules are on
the safety cards.

**The 8.0U and 8.5R print 100 to 240 V** for the same current
(`spirit-med-8-bike-specs-mains-100-to-240-v-1-76-to-0-71-a`); the 4.0R and 4.0U need no outlet at
all. What the supply does inside the machine, and the 24 V and 12 V it makes, are on the service
manual card (`spirit-med-70-bike-specs-mains-power-through-an-ac-input-module-and-switching-power-supply`).
