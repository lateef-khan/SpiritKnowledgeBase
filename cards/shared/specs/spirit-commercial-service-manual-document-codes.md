The six Spirit commercial service manuals identify themselves inconsistently. These are the codes
actually printed inside them, and which page each is on.

| Manual | Code as printed | Where |
|---|---|---|
| CT850 2016 | **ST8100-CT001** | cover, and in the circuit-diagram title "SPIRIT #CT850(ST8100-CT001)" |
| CT850 2020 | **CT850_ST8600-YT58** | circuit-diagram title, p. 53 |
| CT850 2020 | **ST8600-YT058** | exploded-view drawing title, p. 55, dated 220713 |
| CU900ENT | **(CU900-ENT)** | circuit-diagram title "UPRIGHT CIRCUIT DIAGRAM" |
| CVC800 | **XS550** | the console, on the circuit diagram, p. 32 |
| CE800ENT | **CE800 ENT, 2020 ver.** | cover |
| CRW800H2O | **CRW800H2O ROWER** | cover |

Note the two spellings of the 2020 CT850 driver family: **YT58** on the circuit diagram,
**YT058** on the exploded view, and **YT057 / YT058** as the variant tags beside the connectors on
the console boards. They refer to the same driver generation.

The 2016 CT850 cover also prints **(110V)** under the title, while its own circuit diagram gives
the input as 120V/60HZ.
