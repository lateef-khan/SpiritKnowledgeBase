**None of the thirteen Spirit commercial elliptical owner's manuals contains a
specification table.** All thirteen were checked. No table of contents has a
Specifications entry, and none of these figures is printed anywhere in any of
them:

- **machine weight** or shipping weight - no figure, in any unit, in any manual
- **flywheel weight**, flywheel diameter, or a gear ratio
- assembled dimensions or floor footprint - **except the CES880-2025**, which is
  the only one of the ten that prints any, see
  `ces880-2025-specs-machine-dimensions-and-operating-area`
- **stride length** - **except the four CE850 manuals**, see
  `spirit-ce850-specs-stride-length-18-to-24-inches`
- a resistance range stated in watts, newtons or kilograms; resistance is given
  only as a count of console levels
- a carton size, a pedal spacing, a Q factor, or a step-up height

Get these figures from Spirit Fitness or the product spec sheet, and say the
owner's manual does not state them. **Do not carry a figure across from one
generation to another, or in from a Sole elliptical.**

**The residential XE and XG books print no specification table either**, and their
absences are not the same as these - see `spirit-xe-specs-no-specification-table`.
The CE850's 18-to-24-inch stride is the only stride length any Spirit elliptical
manual states, and it is not an answer for a home machine.

## The seven service manuals print no specification table either

The CE800-2016, CE800-2021, CE850-2016, CE850-2020, CE900-2021, CE900ENT and
CE1000ENT-2023 service manuals were checked the same way (the CE800ENT's is on
`ce800ent-outlines-part-names`). **None has a Specifications chapter; none prints a
dimension, a machine weight, a user weight limit or a flywheel weight.** Their
Outlines chapters are part-name drawings without measurements
(`ce1000ent-2023-specs-outline-numbered-part-names`, `spirit-ce900-specs-outline-part-names`).

## Two traps inside the CE800 name

**The CE800ENT needs a wall outlet; the other three CE800 books say the machine
never does.** The CE800-2013, CE800-2021 and CE800-2023 manuals all state that
"Spirit Fitness commercial elliptical trainers have a built-in generator for
power and do not need to be plugged into an AC outlet", and none of the three
lists a power cord. The CE800ENT owner's manual instead requires "a 110-volt,
5-amp grounded outlet with only the elliptical plugged into the circuit", asks
for a dedicated 5 amp circuit breaker, and its parts list carries item 43, Power
Cord. The CE850-2016, CE850-2020 and CE850-2022 manuals require the same
110-volt / 5 amp outlet. The CE900-2021, CE900-2025 and CES880-2025 are
generator-powered like the plain CE800.

**The CE800ENT owner's manual never says how many resistance levels the machine
has.** The words do not appear, in either the earlier printing or the October
2024 one. Do not answer it with the 40 levels of the plain CE800; that figure
comes from a different console.

## The 2024 reissue added no specification page, and moved the ENT to 120 volts

The CE800-2024, CE850-2024 and CE800ENT-2024 books are the New Black Units
reissue, warranted September and October 2024. Measured on native PDF text, only
about 51% of the CE800-2023 text, 52% of the CE850-2022 text and 45% of the
earlier CE800ENT text survives into them - **and none of the new half is a
specification page.** Their whole
native-plus-OCR text was searched: *specification* appears only in warranty
boilerplate, *dimension* not at all, and the only weight figures are the 450 lb
user weight limit and the `kg-m/min` fitness-test unit.

**Two figures did change, and neither is on this page.** The CE800ENT-2024 asks
for a **120-volt AC, 15-amp** outlet where every earlier commercial elliptical
book asks for 110 volts and 5 amps - a `safety` fact. And the parts lists of all
three 2024 books rename the **water** bottle holder the **drink** bottle holder,
which is the same part under a new name.

**All three 2024 books print an exploded view and a parts list**, and each list
differs from its predecessor's in a handful of rows - see
`ce800-2024-specs-parts-list`, `ce850-2024-specs-parts-list` and
`ce800ent-2024-specs-parts-list` before quoting an item.

## What the CE800ENT service manual already said

`ce800ent-outlines-part-names` records that the CE800ENT **service** manual
carries "no dimensions, no machine weight, no maximum user weight and no power
rating". That is still true of the service manual. The CE800ENT **owner's**
manual, read here for the first time, does supply two of those four - a 450 lb
weight limit and a 110-volt / 5 amp supply - and still supplies no dimensions and
no machine weight. The two manuals do not contradict each other; the owner's book
simply fills part of the gap.

## The 2013 scan

The CE800-2013 manual is OCR from an image-only scan. Its maximum-user-weight line
reads as a figure in **kilograms**, not the pounds every later printing uses, and
the digits are not legible enough to quote. **Do not give a customer a weight
limit for a CE800-2013 from this book.** Every other CE800 printing states 450 lb.

## The water bottle holder changed nothing here

The CE800-2023 and the CE850-2022 are the later printings of near-identical
books, and each adds a water bottle holder to the assembly steps and the parts
list. Neither printing changes a dimension, a weight, a stride length or a
resistance count, and neither adds a specification table. One card covers both
printings of each pair for every figure on this page.
