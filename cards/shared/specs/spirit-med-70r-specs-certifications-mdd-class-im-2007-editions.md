**The block, exactly as printed in both books:**

> CAN/CSA-C22.2 No. 60601-1:14 , ANS/AAMI ES60601-1:2005+A2 (R2012) +A1
> MDD 93/42/EEC Class Im
> IEC 60601-1-2:2007 , EN 60601-1-2:2007 , IEC 60601-1:2005
> EN 60601-1:2006+A11+A12 , EN ISO 20957:2013 , ISO 14971; 2012

(The 2021 book prints *CAN/CAS-C22.2* for CSA; both print *ANS/AAMI* and a semicolon in *ISO 14971;
2012*. All reproduced as the pages have them.)

**Declared under the Medical Device Directive as Class Im** - Class I with a measuring function -
not under the MDR the 8.0U and 8.5R cite. **The 7.0U's 2025 list is not this list**: it cites the
2014/2015 EMC editions and adds "NB: 0123" (`70u-2025-specs-certifications-mdd-class-im-nb-0123`), so
the two 7-series books of the same year were not set from one block.

**The 2021 Dyaco MED book adds what the symbols on the label mean:** a CE mark over **0123** (the
notified body), "Product certificate logo, CE MDD 93/42/EEC Class Im", and **"Type B applied parts.
This is used for parts applied to the patient that require only ordinary electrical protection, e.g.
headphones."** - the hand-pulse grips and pedals are Type B applied parts. The 2025 book drops the
symbols page.

**No notified-body certificate number, no UDI and no FDA listing** appears in either book. The
operating conditions the 60601 standards imply - 10 to 36 °C, 30 to 90 % RH, no ingress protection -
are on the safety page, and the EMC declaration on the following page
(`spirit-med-bike-specs-emc-declaration`).
