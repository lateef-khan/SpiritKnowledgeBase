**The rating is not the same on every machine, and several print none at all. Use the
row for yours.**

| Machines | What the manual states |
|---|---|
| B94-2013, R92-2013, B94-2016, R92-2016 | `12V/1.5A` DC from the power pack, connected to `115 VAC` |
| B94-2019, R92-2019 | `12V/3.3A` DC from the power pack, connected to `115 VAC` |
| B94-2023, R92-2023, SB1200-2023 | `DC 12V/3.3A (12V/3A)`, connected to `115 (230) VAC` |
| LCB-2019, LCR-2019 | `24V/2.7A` DC from the power pack, connected to AC power. **The only 24 volt machine in the range.** The manual does not state the mains voltage. |
| B94-2011, R92-2011, LCB-2011, LCR-2011, LCB-2016, LCR-2016 | **No DC power pack.** The manual says the models are connected **directly to 115 VAC**. |
| LCB-2013, LCR-2013 | **No mains cord, no switch and no power pack at all.** "The console power is created by a built in generator." |
| B74-2015, R72-2015, LCB-2023, LCR-2023 | No supply rating printed. |

Where the cord plugs in also moves:

| Machines | The manual says |
|---|---|
| B94-2019, R92-2019, B94-2023, R92-2023, SB1200-2023 | power **socket**, where the line cord plugs into the unit, on the **front** |
| B94-2013, R92-2013, B94-2016, R92-2016 | power **switch**, where the line cord plugs into the unit, on the **front** |
| B94-2011, R92-2011, LCB-2011, LCR-2011, LCB-2016, LCR-2016 | power **switch**, where the line cord plugs into the unit, on the **left side** near the front |
| LCB-2023, LCR-2023 | power **switch** at the **front** of the bike |
| LCB-2013, LCR-2013 | Nothing to plug in — the console is generator powered |

Do not read a figure from one row onto another machine. The 2019 B94 rating is more
than double the 2013 and 2016 rating, and the LCB/LCR 2019 is double again.
