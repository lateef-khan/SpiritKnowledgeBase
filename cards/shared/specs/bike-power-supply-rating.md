**The rating is not the same on every year. Use the row for your machine.**

| Machines | Supply the manual states | Mains |
|---|---|---|
| B94-2013, R92-2013, B94-2016, R92-2016 | 12V/1.5A DC from the power pack | 115 VAC |
| B94-2019, R92-2019 | 12V/3.3A DC from the power pack | 115 VAC |
| B94-2023, R92-2023 | DC 12V/3.3A (12V/3A) | 115 (230) VAC |

The other manuals in this family do not print a supply rating.

Where the cord plugs in also moves:

| Machines | The manual says |
|---|---|
| B94-2019, R92-2019, B94-2023, R92-2023 | power **socket**, where the line cord plugs into the unit, on the **front** of the bike |
| B94-2013, R92-2013, B94-2016, R92-2016 | power **switch**, where the line cord plugs into the unit, on the **front** of the bike |
| B94-2011, R92-2011, LCB-2011, LCR-2011, LCB-2016, LCR-2016 | power **switch**, where the line cord plugs into the unit, on the **left side** near the front |
| LCB-2023, LCR-2023 | power **switch** at the **front** of the bike |

Do not read a figure from one row onto another machine. A supply that is right for
a 2019 B94 is more than double the 2013 and 2016 rating.
