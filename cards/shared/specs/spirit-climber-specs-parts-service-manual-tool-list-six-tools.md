**All three stair climber service manuals - the CSC900 V1.0 (alternator drive), the CSC900
2022 (magnetic system) and the CSC880 (magnetic) - open with the same six-tool page**, six
photographs each with a caption and nothing else:

| Caption as printed | What the photograph shows |
|---|---|
| Screwdriver | a single Phillips screwdriver with a red handle |
| Socket head wrench | a fanned set of L-shaped hex (Allen) keys |
| Clyburn spanner | an adjustable (crescent) wrench |
| Pliers | combination pliers |
| Multi-meter | a hand-held digital multi-meter with its two leads |
| Electric soldering iron | a mains soldering iron with its cord |

**No sizes are printed** - not a hex-key size, not a jaw width - and the captions are the whole
text of the page. "Clyburn spanner" is the book's name for the adjustable wrench in the
picture; "socket head wrench" is its name for a set of hex keys, not for a socket set.

**This is the only tool list of its kind in the Spirit service manuals.** The Dyaco-format
books for the treadmills, bikes, ellipticals, rowers and steppers name exactly one tool, a
multi-meter (`spirit-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`); the
8.5S medical stepper names eighteen (`85s-2025-specs-parts-required-maintenance-tools-eighteen`).
The three stair climber books come from a different OEM - the CSC880 page tells the reader to
use "Anyfit's original parts" - and their page is that OEM's, the same six photographs and
captions in all three books.

**The soldering iron is listed and never used.** No procedure in any of the three books solders
anything; the replacement chapters unplug connectors and remove screws. The multi-meter is
used - the CSC900 V1.0 book's "Determination of defective accessories" pages measure the
communication line for continuity, the brake with a 24 V adapter and the resistor at close to
0.5 ohms - and those steps belong to the error cards.

**Do not confuse this with the tools packed in the carton.** The CSC900 2024 owner's manual's
tools panel is `csc900-2024-assembly-tools-included`; the service manuals do not repeat it.
