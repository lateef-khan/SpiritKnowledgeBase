**None of the thirty-five legacy owner's manuals prints a specification table.**
Every table of contents runs Registration, Safety, Electrical, Operation,
Assembly, Programs, Heart Rate, Maintenance, Warranty — no dimensions,
footprint, product or shipping weight, flywheel weight, power rating, rated
resistance-level count, fixed stride length, maximum-user-weight figure,
wiring diagram, or FCC statement appears in any of them.

**What the books do state, and where that lives:**

- **Adjustable stride 18 to 24 inches** (E95s books only) —
  `sole-elliptical-specs-adjustable-stride-18-to-24-inches`.
- **Adjustable footpads 0 / 5 / 10 degrees**, and a **2-degree inward footpad
  angle** in the pre-2012 books (except as noted there) —
  `sole-elliptical-specs-adjustable-footpad-angles-0-5-and-10-degrees`,
  `sole-elliptical-specs-footpads-2-degree-inward-angle`.
- **A floor is 16 steps, and step height is a key, not a figure** (SC200-2016,
  SC200-2019, SC300-2017) - the SC300 book adds Level / Step-height keys but
  prints no inch figure and no level count; the SC200 books power the climber
  from an external DC adapter while the SC300 connects directly to 120-volt
  mains (outlets and circuits are carded under safety).
- **Resistance level mapped to watts** (E98 2011 / 2013 / 2015 / 2019 fitness
  test: Level 1 = 9W through Level 15 = 122W, 1 watt = 6.11829727787
  kg-m/min) — carded with the fitness-test program procedure (programs
  section), not repeated here.
- **115 VAC line power, 15-amp grounded outlet, 40-to-120 °F operating range
  with 95% non-condensing humidity** — electrical requirements, carded under
  safety.
- **User-profile, template, favorite and history counts, USB charging, and the
  E98 engineering-mode brake-test PWM range (0-512, L1-L20)** — console
  features and menus, carded under console.
- **Harness-plug steps** (position sensor, computer cable, handpulse and
  switch wires) appear in assembly with no diagram behind them; the assembly
  section concurs no wiring diagram is printed.

**Get missing figures from Sole or the product spec sheet**, never from a
different brand's twin: the Spirit commercial elliptical absence card
(`spirit-ce-specs-no-specification-table`) and the Xterra footplate card hold
their own brands' facts only.
