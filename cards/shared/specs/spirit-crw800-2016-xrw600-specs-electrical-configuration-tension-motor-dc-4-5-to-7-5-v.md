**Both books print DC 4.5-7.5 V; the 2020-version CRW800 book prints DC 4.0-6.0 V**
(`crw800-2021-specs-electrical-configuration-tension-motor-dc-4-0-to-6-0-v`). Quote the figure
from the book for the machine in front of you.

| Part | Description printed |
|---|---|
| Console | Interface that controls all functions of the *Elliptical* (2016 CRW800 book) / of the *Rower* (XRW600 book) |
| Tension motor | It can change to increase or decrease resistance level of brake |

**General information**

| Part | Description printed |
|---|---|
| Console | Contains Key controls and LCD Display. Main controller Include power supply and motor driver control circuit |
| Tension motor | **Work voltage: DC 4.5~7.5V.** Control resistance increases and decreases |

**The 2016 CRW800 book says "Elliptical" on its console line** - and again on its whole safety
page - because the page was lifted from an elliptical template; the XRW600 reprint corrected the
word. It is a rower on every other page.

The E2 test pages of both books expect a different reading from this range: **+5.5 to 6.5 V DC on
LEVEL UP and -5.5 to 6.0 V DC on LEVEL DOWN** across the motor's blue and green wires. The working
voltage and the test reading are two different figures in these books; the 2020-version book
makes them one.

The same 4.5-7.5 V range is printed for the Spirit XE ellipticals and XB bikes of the same
generation (`spirit-xe-2016-specs-tension-motor-working-voltage-dc-4-5-to-7-5-v`,
`spirit-xb-specs-gear-motor-working-voltage-dc-4-5-to-7-5-v`); each is its own product line's
card.
