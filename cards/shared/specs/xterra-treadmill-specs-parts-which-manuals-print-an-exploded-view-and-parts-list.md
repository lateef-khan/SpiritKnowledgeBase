**Four books print a drawing with no list.** The TR75H, TR95H, WS200 and WS300 exploded views are numbered - the WS200 to 126, the WS300 to 119, the TR75H past 100 - and nothing in the book says what the numbers are. The only key is indirect: the two hiker books' Parts Included page cites *BOM Part No.* against each assembly part, and those numbers are the drawing's callouts (the assembly cards hold that page). A customer asking for "part 44 on my WS200" cannot be answered from the manual.

**Ten books print neither**, and the absence was checked in the native text, in a spacing-tolerant search (`E *X *P *L *O *D`, `P *A *R *T *S`) and in the render-vs-text sweep that appended every image-only page: the 2021 batch, the TR260, the TR6.4 and the three 2024 TRX books have no drawing and no list. Their assembly pages nonetheless number the parts they use, so a reader sees *(87)* and *(104)* with nowhere to look them up.

Where a list does exist the packed tools are rows in it (a 13 mm wrench, an M5 Allen-head screw wrench, an M6 L Allen wrench, lubricant); the service manuals' own tool list is one multimeter (`xterra-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`).
