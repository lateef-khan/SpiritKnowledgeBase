**Outlines (2015 books)** - three drawings: **Console Assembly**, **Handpulse Sensor**, **Speed Adjustment
Switch W/Cable**, **Incline Adjustment Switch W/Cable**, **Drive Motor**, **Incline Motor**, and a part
labelled **Console Display Board** whose Chinese caption names the lower control board. The XT185 and
XT285 books stop there.

**XT385-2015 and XT485-2015 add chapter 2**: upper controllers **Cooling FAN**, **Speaker**, **DISPLAY**;
lower controller and driver **SPEED SENSOR**, **INCLINE MOTOR**, **DC MOTOR**, **Motor Controller**, under
a heading "Driver system".

**XT485ENT** - outlines **Driving Motor**, **Incline Motor**, **Lower Controller**; upper controllers
**Cooling FAN**, **Speaker**, **Speed control on handrail**, **Incline control on handrail**, **DISPLAY**;
lower **INCLINE MOTOR**, **Motor Controller**, **DC MOTOR**.

None of these chapters carries a dimension, weight, user weight or power rating. The 2023 books replace the
drawings with a numbered outline of up to twenty parts, on its own card.
