| Figure | 8.5S-2025 | 8.5S-FIT-2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 220 cm (86.6") | 220 cm (86.6") |
| Width | 86 cm (33.8") | 86 cm (33.8") |
| Height | **131 cm (44.4")** - see below | **131 cm (44.4")** |
| Product weight | **150.9 kgs (332 lbs)** | **the heading is printed and the figure is blank** |

**The height row cannot be right.** 131 cm is **51.6 inches**, and 44.4 inches is
**112.8 cm**. The two numbers on that line differ by about seven inches. Length
and width on the same page convert correctly, so the fault is in the height row
alone and it is printed identically in both books.

**Do not quote either height as fact.** Measure the machine, or get the figure
from Spirit Fitness, and tell the customer the manual prints two heights that
disagree. Both figures were read from a 300 dpi render of the printed page, not
from the text extraction, so this is what the page says.

## The 8.5S-FIT prints no weight at all

Its specification page carries the words `Product weight` and then nothing. The
row is blank on the page - confirmed on a render, so it is not something the
extraction lost. **Do not fill it in with the 8.5S's 150.9 kg.** The two books
also differ on the power supply row - one takes 100 to 240 volts and the other a
120 volt, 15 amp outlet - so they are not the same build, and a weight carried
across would be a guess. See `spirit-climber-85s-specs-mains-power-supply`.

## Neither book prints a shipping weight

No gross weight, no carton size, no step height and no seat travel appears in
either manual. Only the assembled dimensions and, on one of the two, the product
weight.

## The user weight limit is a different figure

Both pages print **Patient weight capacity 200 kgs (440 lbs)**, which converts
correctly. That is a safety limit and lives in the `safety` section, not here.
