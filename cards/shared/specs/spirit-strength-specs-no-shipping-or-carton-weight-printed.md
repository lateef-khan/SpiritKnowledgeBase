**Not printed anywhere in the range. Do not re-run this check.**

The `Weight` row in the page 11 specification table is the **assembled machine**. There is no gross
weight, no crated weight, no carton count and no carton dimensions in any of the thirty-nine books,
and the ST800 three have no specification table at all.

The words *carton* and *box* do appear, but only in the unpacking instruction - *"Remove all parts
from the shipping carton and foam inserts"* and *"inspect the packing material and the box for items
that may have been overlooked"*. Neither carries a figure.

**Consequences for a parts or freight desk:**

- **The assembled weight is a floor, not the shipping weight.** A 531 lb CSS-ABDO ships with a
  weight stack, shrouds, pads and a pallet on top of that.
- **A CSD dual station or a CSS lat machine will not be one carton.** The manuals say nothing about
  how many, so do not guess a number for a customer.
- Assembled weights and footprints are at
  `spirit-strength-specs-machine-weight-and-overall-dimensions`.

If a customer needs freight figures, they have to come from the dealer order or the packing list,
not from the owner's manual.
