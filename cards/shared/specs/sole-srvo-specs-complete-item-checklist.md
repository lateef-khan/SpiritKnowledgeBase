The SRVO COMPLETE ITEM CHECKLIST (printed page 4) lists three entries:

- Foldable workout bench: 1 set
- Extension safety cable 3 pairs with buckle
- Short bar

A rule line follows the cable entry with no fourth item printed; carded as printed. No hardware counts, no tool list, and no part numbers accompany the list — this is an in-box checklist, not a parts list.
