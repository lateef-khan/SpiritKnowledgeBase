**18 inches at the shortest setting, 24 inches at the longest.** The stride is
adjustable, not fixed.

- The lowest (shortest) setting gives **18 inches**, which the manual says closely
  simulates a walking motion.
- The highest (longest) setting gives **24 inches**, which simulates the longer
  stride of a running motion.
- The stride can also be **computer controlled** in some programs, and set by hand
  from the console at any time with the stride up/down keys.

All four CE850 printings state the same two figures, the 2024 New Black Units
reissue included - it rewrote about half the book and left this paragraph
untouched, adding only that the stride "can be controlled by buttons on the left
swing arm and also can be automatically adjusted during the built-in workout
programs".

**No other Spirit commercial elliptical manual prints a stride length at all.**
The CE800, CE800ENT, CE900 and CES880 owner's manuals state none, and none of
them describes an adjustable stride. Do not quote 18 to 24 inches for any of
those machines - it is a CE850 figure and the CE850 is the only one of the thirteen
with the adjustable-stride feature. See
`spirit-ce-specs-no-specification-table`.

The owner's manuals give no intermediate step size, no count of stride positions and
no metric equivalent.

**The service manuals add the step and the counter.** The CE850-2016 book's Function
page reads *Stride - Display the stride position from 18 to 24. STRIDE preset value is
18 to 24. Press UP or DOWN to adjust stride, each increment and decrement is 0.5* - so
the stride moves in **half-inch steps, thirteen positions from 18 to 24**. The
stride-motor test page adds that the STRIDE window, once speed calibration ends, shows
the position sensor as a counter: **20 for max stride, 0 for lowest stride**. The
CE850-2020 service manual reprints the owner's-manual paragraph word for word and
prints no step. The actuator itself is a 115 V AC stride motor
(`spirit-ce850-xe895-specs-stride-motor-115-v-ac-four-wires-and-position-sensor`).

**The XE895-2016 service manual is the CE850-2016 book under its export name** and
prints the same 18-to-24, 0.5-step Function page, so the 2016 XE895 carries this
figure. That does **not** extend to the XE895-2018 home elliptical, whose owner's manual
adjusts the stride per workout segment and prints **no length at all** - see
`xe895-2018-specs-powered-stride-set-per-segment-with-no-length-printed`. Answering a
2018 XE895 with 18 to 24 inches carries a figure from a different book onto a machine
whose own manual states none.
