**Six of the fourteen print both an exploded view and a numbered parts list. Eight
print neither.** Every book that has one is either a 2023 printing or a
touchscreen ENT printing; **no book printed between 2016 and 2021 with an LED
console has a parts list at all.**

| Manual | Exploded view | Parts list | Highest item number |
|---|---|---|---|
| XBR55ENT 2021 | printed p. 47 (PDF 49) | printed pp. 48-50 (PDF 50-52) | 191 |
| XBU55ENT 2021 | printed p. 47 (PDF 49) | printed pp. 48-49 (PDF 50-51) | 150 |
| XBR25 2023 | printed p. 30 (PDF 32) | printed pp. 31-33 (PDF 33-35) | 191 |
| XBR55 2023 | printed p. 37 (PDF 39) | printed pp. 38-41 (PDF 40-43) | 192 |
| XBR95 2023 | printed p. 36 (PDF 38) | printed pp. 37-40 (PDF 39-42) | 192 |
| XBU55 2023 | printed p. 37 (PDF 39) | printed pp. 38-40 (PDF 40-42) | 161 |
| **XBR95 2016** | **none** | **none** | - |
| **XBR95 2018** | **none** | **none** | - |
| **XBR25 2019** | **none** | **none** | - |
| **XBR55 2019** | **none** | **none** | - |
| **XBU55 2019** | **none** | **none** | - |
| **XBR55 2021** | **none** | **none** | - |
| **XBU55 2021** | **none** | **none** | - |
| **XBR95 2021** | **none** | **none** | - |

Item numbers are **not consecutive** - the XBR25 2023 list jumps from 148 straight
to 160, and every list has gaps. Do not infer that a missing number means a missing
part.

For the eight with none, the table of contents ends at the warranty and a search of
the whole document returns no `Main Frame ... 1` row and no item-number column.
**The only part numbers those eight give are the hardware callouts inside the
assembly chapter**, which cover fasteners for assembly and nothing else. Their service manuals add
nothing: none of the 2016, 2021 ENT or 2023 XBR/XBU service books prints a parts list either
(`spirit-bike-specs-parts-which-service-manuals-print-a-parts-list`). The one residential bike book that
prints exploded views with Spirit part numbers is the 2008 dealer manual for the earlier XBR25 and XBR55
(`xbr25-2007-specs-parts-list`, `xbr55-2007-specs-parts-list`), and those numbers belong to those machines.

## The running count across the whole Spirit bike range

Three cards hold this answer, one per family, because the families print different
things above their lists. Together they cover **35 Spirit bike owner's manuals, of
which 17 print a parts list**:

- **6 of 14** residential recumbent and upright bikes - this card.
- **8 of 13** commercial upright and recumbent bikes -
  `spirit-bike-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.
- **3 of 8** indoor cycles and air bikes -
  `spirit-cycle-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.

## No factory document code above these lists

The six residential lists are headed plainly. The four 2023 books print just
`PARTS LIST` at the top of each page. The two ENT books print a model line -
`XBR55ENT Parts List` and, on the upright, **`XBU55ENTENT Parts List`, with ENT
typed twice**. None of the six carries the `-XR880-SB023-01 Part List_SPIRIT`
style factory code that the Spirit *commercial* bike lists print; see
`spirit-bike-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list` for those.

**One row was left untranslated.** Item 169 of the XBR55ENT 2021 list reads
`M5 × 25m/m_皿頭十字螺絲` - Chinese for a flat-head Phillips screw. The equivalent row
in the XBR25 2023 list reads `M5 × 25mm_Flat Head Socket Screw`.

## Do not read one bike's list for another

Measured as the overlap of item names on the parts pages alone:

| Pair | Shared part names |
|---|---|
| XBR55 2023 vs XBR95 2023 - two recumbents | **91.8%** |
| XBR55 2023 vs XBR55ENT 2021 - two recumbents | **78.0%** |
| XBU55 2023 vs XBU55ENT 2021 - two uprights | **72.0%** |
| XBR55 2023 vs XBU55 2023 - recumbent against upright | **49.1%** |
| XBR55ENT 2021 vs XBU55ENT 2021 - recumbent against upright | **43.0%** |

**Item numbers are not stable across books either.** The Transformer Power Cord is
item **109** in the XBU55 2023 list and item **123** in the XBU55ENT 2021 list. The
Flywheel is item **55** on the recumbents and item **20** on the uprights. Quote an
item number only from the book for the machine in front of you.

## What separates the two closest lists

XBR55 2023 and XBR95 2023 share 91.8% of their part names, no quantity differs
between them, and the whole difference is the resistance unit: the XBR55 list
carries a **Gear Motor**, a **Power Adaptor**, a **Transformer Power Cord** and a
**Sensor Rack**; the XBR95 list carries a **Generator/Brake Controller**, a
**Generator Wire Harness**, a **Computer Cable** and a **Fixing Plate** instead,
and no mains adaptor of any kind. See
`spirit-residential-bike-specs-gear-motor-or-generator-brake`.
