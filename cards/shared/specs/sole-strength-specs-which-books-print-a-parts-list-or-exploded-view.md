Only one of the six strength books prints a parts list or exploded view:

- **SW100-2023: yes.** A 26-item PART LIST plus an exploded view whose callouts key to it: `sole-sw100-specs-part-list-26-items-and-exploded-view`.
- **SW121-2023, SW155-2023, SW180-2023, SRVO, SRVO-2021: no.** Zero hits for "exploded", "parts list", "part list" or "replacement part" in any of the five texts; their tables of contents run Product Registration, Precautions/Safeguards, Checklists, Assembly, Warranty (where present) — no parts chapter; the SW121 pages without OCR supplements render as assembly steps, not hidden parts pages.

What the five books do print instead: the SW121/SW155/SW180 assembly pack checklists name hardware per step (assembly kits, not carded here), and the SRVO Complete book carries an in-box item checklist (`sole-srvo-specs-complete-item-checklist`). No tool list is printed in any of the six books; the only tools named anywhere are the SW100 list's own items A (Wrench) and B (Allen Key).
