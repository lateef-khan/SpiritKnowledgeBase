| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Compatible battery type | **CR1220** |
| Nominal battery voltage | **3V** |

"Non-rechargeable batteries are not to be recharged."

**The same page says the machine "Contains a non-removable battery. Only professionals are allowed
to disassemble it."** So the page names a compatible cell and in the next breath says the cell cannot
be removed by the user. Both statements are printed; read the CR1220 as the cell a service technician
fits, not as a user-replaceable part. The page ends "Power off after use to prevent unauthorized
device operation."

The rest of the page is battery handling and disposal text - recycle or dispose of used cells per
local regulations, keep away from children, never bin or incinerate them, never force-discharge,
recharge, disassemble, heat above 85 °C or incinerate - warnings rather than a specification.

The 8.0T treadmill on the same console prints the identical page (`80t-2026-specs-battery-type`); the
7.0R and 7.0U books print no battery type.
