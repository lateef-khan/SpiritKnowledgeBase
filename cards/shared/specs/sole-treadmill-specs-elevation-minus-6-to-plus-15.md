**This is -6 decline to +15, not the 0 to 15 of the other 2023 books.** The F85-2023, F89-2023 and TT8-2023 Custom-program pages state "Elevation values are absolute and vary from -6 to +15. Elevation pattern is optional", and the elevation chart prints rows below zero. Speed values in the same paragraph are relative ("maximum can be changed before each workout"), so no absolute top speed is printed. The ST90-2023 and button-generation books state no numeric incline range at all.
