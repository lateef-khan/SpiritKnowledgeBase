**The manual prints no weight-stack rating.** This total is the sum of the plate quantities its
assembly hardware table lists, and it is not a figure the manufacturer states anywhere in the book -
say so when you quote it. Why that is, and how it was checked, is at
`spirit-strength-specs-no-weight-stack-rating-printed`.

Four machines with the identical stack, and the only CSS stacks with **no ten-pound plates at all**:

| No. | Description | Qty | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| - | `20lb weight stack` | 11 | 220 lb |
| - | `20lb top weight stack` | 1 | 20 lb |
| | **Total** | **12** | **240 lb** |

The item numbers differ between the four books (089/090 on CSS-DELT, 062/063 on CSS-SCPR, 057/058 on
CSS-SHPR, 052/053 on CSS-TROT) but the descriptions and quantities are word for word the same.

**A uniform 20 lb step from bottom to top.** These four are the machines to quote when a customer
asks for a stack that steps evenly - every other CSS stack mixes plate sizes.

The optional 5 lb increment weight rides on top of the stack on the machines that have one -
`spirit-strength-specs-five-pound-increment-weight`.
