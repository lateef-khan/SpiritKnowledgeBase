Three different console panels sit across the fourteen residential bikes, and
**none of the fourteen manuals gives a screen size.**

| Machines | What the manual calls the display |
|---|---|
| XBR95 2016, XBR95 2018, XBR95 2021, XBR25 2019, XBR55 2019, XBR55 2021, XBU55 2019, XBU55 2021 | **Large Blue-LED Matrix Window**, with a Message Center below it |
| XBR25 2023, XBR55 2023, XBR95 2023, XBU55 2023 | **LCD Window** on the console page, **LCD Screen** in the assembly step - no colour given |
| XBR55ENT 2021, XBU55ENT 2021 | **Touchscreen** - no size given anywhere in either book |

**No size, no resolution, no brightness figure and no panel technology beyond the
words above appears in any of the fourteen.** A search of all fourteen extracts for
an inch measurement or the phrase *screen size* returns nothing. In particular
**the 10.1 inch figure printed for the Spirit commercial CR900ENT and CU900ENT does
not belong to an XBR55ENT or an XBU55ENT** - see
`spirit-bike-specs-console-display-type`. Where an ENT book names iOS or Android it
is describing the *customer's phone*, never the console.

## Seven books call the same panel two different things

The eight blue-LED books label the panel **Large Blue-LED Matrix Window** on the
console call-out page. Seven of those same books then say, in the *Heart Rate %
Profile* paragraph a few pages later, that "**the console LCD screen** will display
your current heart rate anytime a pulse is detected", and that the Bar Graph sits
"to the right of the LCD screen".

**That is the same panel described twice, in two chapters, under two names.** The
call-out page is the display specification and the heart-rate paragraph is
boilerplate carried over from another console. If a customer quotes "LCD" from the
heart-rate page for a 2016-2021 bike, they are not describing a different machine.

The eighth blue-LED book, the **XBR25 2019, prints no Heart Rate % Profile section
at all** and so never uses the word LCD. Its console page has no Heart Rate %
Profile call-out either.

## What else the call-out page names, and where that lives

**This card answers the display specification only** - what kind of panel, what
colour, what size. The rest of the drawing around it is a console question and
lives in the `console` section: the Message Center, the cargo compartment for keys,
phone or MP3 player, the fan, the Easy-Touch Control Buttons, and the programme
count printed beside them - **"Six innovative programs" on the XBR25 2019 against
"Ten innovative programs" on the XBR55 and XBR95 books**. The 2023 console page
labels an LCD Window, an Adjustable Fan, Program Buttons and Direct Resistance
Level Buttons instead.

**One hardware difference on that page is worth stating here, because it is a
missing part rather than a missing paragraph.** The XBR55 2023, XBR95 2023 and
XBU55 2023 console pages also label a **USB Charging** port, and each of those
books carries a *Charge Portable Devices with USB port* feature paragraph and a
**USB Board** row in its parts list. **The XBR25 2023 has none of that: the word
`USB` does not appear anywhere in the book**, its console page has no such
call-out, and its parts list has no USB Board. Do not promise a customer a USB
charging port on that machine.

For anything beyond the words *Blue-LED*, *LCD* and *Touchscreen*, go to Spirit
Fitness. See `spirit-residential-bike-specs-no-specification-table` for the rest of
the missing specification data.
