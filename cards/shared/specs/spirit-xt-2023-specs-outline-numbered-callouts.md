Each 2023 book opens with one numbered drawing. The first sixteen numbers are common to all six:

1 Console, 2 Foot Pad, 3 Heart rate sensor, 4 Fan, 5 Speaker, 6 Drink Bottle Holder (L), 7 Drink Bottle
Holder (R), 8 Base Frame, 9 Upright (L), 10 Upright (R), 11 Motor Cover, 12 Running Belt, 13 Base Frame
Cover (L), 14 Rear Adjustment Cover (L), 15 Rear Adjustment Cover (R), 16 Main Frame.

| Book | Numbers after 16 | Differences inside 1-16 |
|---|---|---|
| XT185-2023 | 17 Foot Rail (17 items) | - |
| XT285-2023 | 17 Incline Handlebar Key, 18 Speed Handlebar Key, 19 Foot Rail | - |
| XT385-2023, XT485-2023 | 17 Incline Handlebar Key, 18 Speed Handlebar Key, 19 Foot Rail, 20 Pad Holder | - |
| XT685-2023, XT685ENT | 17 Incline Handlebar Key, 18 Speed Handlebar Key, 19 Foot Rail, 20 Pad Holder | **8 Incline Frame**, **13 Motor Cover (L)** |

**The parts pages behind it (chapter 2)** name the electrical parts: **Cooling Fan** and **Speaker** on
the upper controller; on the lower controller **Speed Sensor** and **Drive Motor** (XT185 / XT285),
**Speed Sensor, Drive Motor, Incline Motor, Controller** (XT385 / XT485), **Speed Sensor, Controller**
(XT685) and **Controller** alone (XT685ENT).

None of these pages carries a dimension, weight, user weight or power rating. The 2015 books and the
XT485ENT use un-numbered drawings with different names.
