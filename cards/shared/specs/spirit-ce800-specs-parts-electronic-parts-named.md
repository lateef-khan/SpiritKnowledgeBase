**Two CE800 books, six parts, the same six.** The 2016 (XE890B-AE10M) and the 2020 books label the same
electronic parts on their chapter-2 pages, with one label that changes.

**2-1 Upper Controllers** - **Cooling FAN**, **Thumb Switch** and **DISPLAY**, plus the brake photographed at the foot of the same page: the 2016 book calls it **EMS BRAKE**,
the 2020 book **GENERATOR BRAKE**. It is the flywheel assembly, not a console part; putting it under
"Upper Controllers" is the template's error, repeated in the CE800ENT book (`ce800ent-electronic-parts-locations`).

**2-2 Lower Controller and Driver** - **SPEED RPM SENSOR** (2016) / **Speed Sensor** (2020), and
**CONTROLLER** / **Controller**, the driver board.

**The CE800ENT book names eight parts, not six** - it adds an Earphone Port and a "PCB & Power Converter"
and spells the brake "Induction Break" (`ce800ent-electronic-parts-locations`). The CE850 books name a
tension motor, motor controller and stride motor instead of a generator
(`spirit-ce850-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`); the CE900 books name a Hybrid Generator and driver
board with no thumb switch (`spirit-ce900-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`).

**No part number or rating is printed on these pages.** The generator's function and the driver board's
connectors are on the specs cards for chapters 3 and 6; the 2016 book prints no tool list and the 2020
book lists one multi-meter (`spirit-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`).
