**All six Dyaco-format Xterra treadmill service manuals name exactly one tool, and it is the same one: a multi-meter.** Each prints it once, at the head of its error-message chapter, and prints no other tool list anywhere:

| Service manual | How the page puts it | Page |
|---|---|---|
| TR150 (GT65-NT014) | *Prepare: Picture / Tool name / Multi-meter*, under the E0-E9 error-code table | PDF p. 32 (printed 33) |
| TR260 (GT75A-NT050) | *8-2 Prepare tools: Picture / Tool name / Multi-meter* | PDF p. 30 (printed 30) |
| TRX1400 (T3-NT053-01) | *Prepare: Picture / Tool name / Multi-meter*, under the E0-E7 and Err table | PDF p. 37 (printed 35) |
| TRX2500 (GT90B-NT022) | *Prepare: Picture / Tool name / Multi-meter*, under the E0-E7 and ER table | PDF p. 34 (printed 33) |
| TRX3500 / TRX4500 (GT90C/D-NT023/024) | *Prepare: Picture / Tool name / Multi-meter*, under the E0-E7 and ERR table | PDF p. 39 (printed 38) |
| TRX5500 (GT90D-NT041) | *7-2 Prepare tools: Multi-meter*, under the 7-1 Error Codes table | PDF p. 31 (printed 30) |

The picture is a hand-held digital multi-meter. **What it is for is on the pages that follow, not on this one**: the E1 procedures say *use the multi-meter, and turn to the buzzer setting of Ω gear* to check the speed-sensor and motor wires, and the E7 pages say *use Multi-meter transform into AC 1000V to check wall outlet volt*. Those steps belong with the error code they test.

**The two JKEXER service manuals print no tool list at all.** The TR75H (V1.0, January 2026) and TR95H (JKEXER 337, September 2024) books name one tool, inside a step: *use a #13 wrench to remove the screws fixing the motor*. No list, no multi-meter page.

**Do not confuse this with the tools packed in the carton.** The owner's-manual parts lists carry the packed 13 mm wrench, M5 Allen-head screw wrench, M6 L Allen wrench and the lubricant as rows 090/102/103/104 (`tr65-2023-specs-parts-list-items-001-to-188`, `tr75-2024-specs-parts-list-items-1-to-188`, `tr85-2024-specs-parts-list-items-1-to-188`, `trx1400-2023-specs-parts-list-items-001-to-121`, `trx5500-2024-specs-parts-list-items-1-to-188`), and the older owner's manuals name a 6 mm and a 5 mm Allen wrench for belt tension and tracking on their maintenance pages; the service manuals do not repeat them. The Spirit service manuals say the same one thing (`spirit-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`).
