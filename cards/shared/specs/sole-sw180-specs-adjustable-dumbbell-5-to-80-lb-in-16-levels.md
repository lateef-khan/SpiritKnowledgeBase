**The SW180 dumbbell adjusts from 5 LB to 80 LB in 16 levels:**

| LB | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 | 30 | 35 | 40 | 45 | 50 | 55 | 60 | 65 | 70 | 75 | 80 |
| KG | 2.28 | 4.55 | 6.8 | 9.1 | 11.4 | 13.6 | 15.9 | 18.2 | 20.5 | 22.7 | 25 | 27.2 | 29.5 | 31.8 | 34 | 36.3 |

Choose the weight by rotating the middle of the handle grip; the handle
must be fully inserted into the dumbbell tray to adjust.

**The book contradicts itself on the kilogram floor.** The body line reads
"from 5 LB to 80 LB as in 4.55 KG to 36.3 KG", but the table starts at 2.28
and 5 lb is 2.27 kg - the 4.55 figure was carried over from the 10-lb book.
Ruling: the table governs. Both readings confirmed on the render, so this
is a print error, not an OCR confusion.
