**All three rower service manuals name the same two parts by their industry sizes, inside the
flywheel-pulley replacement steps, and nowhere else in either book.**

- **Drive belt: `6PJ-787L`, item 24** - a six-rib J-section Poly-V belt, 787 mm long. The step
  reads "put the Drive Belt (6PJ-787L)(24) on the Flywheel Pulley (25)". The owner's-manual
  parts list prints item 24 as `Drive Belt` with no size, so the size is in the service manuals
  only.
- **Flywheel-pulley bearing: `6201 UOU`, item 31** - a 6201 deep-groove ball bearing, two of
  them, one in each attaching plate (8L and 8R). The step reads "remove the Attaching Plate
  (8R) with Bearing (6201 UOU)". The owner's-manual list agrees: item 31, `Bearing(6201 UOU)`,
  quantity 2.

**The three books print these identically** - the CRW800 (2020) book, the CW800-YR001 book
that is the 2016 CRW800, and the DW400-YR002 book that is the XRW600 - in the same step of
the same procedure. The XRW600 is the CRW800-2016 book at 89% word overlap, and this is one of
the pages where the two agree word for word.

**The tension the belt is set to is a separate figure**: the same step ends "Adjust the Drive
Belt to the Tension value of 70~80BLS" - 70 to 80 lb on a belt-tension gauge - and that
figure lives with the tensioning procedure, not here.

**A second belt exists and is not sized.** Step 8 of the CRW800 (2020) procedure mounts a
"generator belt" onto the generator pulley and tensions it by the idle-wheel screw; the book
never names its section or length, and the 2016 and XRW600 books, which have a power adapter
rather than a generator, have no such belt.

**The Sole SR500 and SR550 rowers are the same Dyaco platform and their service note names the
same belt** - `sole-rower-drive-belt-tension-procedure` cites "Drive Belt (6PJ-787L) (24)" in
the same words. That is a Sole card for Sole machines; the sizes agree because the factory is
the same, not because the manuals are.
