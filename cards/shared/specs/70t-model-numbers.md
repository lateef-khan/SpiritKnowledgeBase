| Year | Model number | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 770881 | 70t-2025 |
| 2026 | 770885 | 70t-2026 |

The numbers come from the customer-service model table (`dbo.MODEL` in `CustService`); a manual rarely prints one. The table has two 7.0T rows, 770881 and 770885; the 2026 owner's manual prints 770885, so the 2025 printing is the 770881 machine.
