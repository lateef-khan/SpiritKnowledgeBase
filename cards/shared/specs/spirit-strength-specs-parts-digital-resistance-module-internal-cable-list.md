**The Digital Resistance Module's maintenance manual prints one numbered parts table, and it
is a cable-routing key, not an exploded-view list.** Chapter 4 "Internal cable" draws the
module's insides with fourteen numbered balloons and prints a three-column table beside them -
序号 (number), 名称 (name) in Chinese, and an English name:

| No. | Chinese name | English name as printed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 电源线 | power line |
| 2 | 船型开关 | rocker switch |
| 3 | 电源输入线 | Power input line |
| 4 | 电源板 | power panel |
| 5 | 电源输出线 | Power output line |
| 6 | 电控 | Controller |
| 7 | 电机 | electrical machinery |
| 8 | 散热电阻 | Heat dissipation resistance |
| 9 | 风扇 | fan |
| 10 | 风扇转接线 | Fan adapter cable |
| 11 | 灯带 | Light strip |
| 12 | 通讯线下段 | Offline communication section |
| 13 | 通讯线中段 | Middle section of communication line |
| 14 | 显示屏 | display screen |

**Three English names are machine translations and the Chinese is the clearer reading.** Item 7
"electrical machinery" is 电机, the **motor** - the DC servo motor the same book specifies at
2000 W. Item 12 "Offline communication section" is 通讯线下段, the **lower section of the
communication cable**; item 13 is its middle section. Item 8 is the **cement braking resistor**
(the Anatomical Diagram on p. 7 calls it *Cement resistor*), and the drawing shows two of them
under one number. Item 9 is drawn as two cooling fans, and the disassembly chapter removes "the
2 cooling fans" with 8 screws.

**No part number, length or rating is printed for any row**, and there is no quantity column.
The numbers are the balloons on this one drawing; they are not Spirit part numbers and they
do not match the numbered screws in the disassembly chapter, which restart at (1) on every page.

**The rest of the module's parts are named without numbers.** Chapter 2 calls out the Screen,
Screen holder, Upper cover, Light strip, Left cover, Front panel, Steel rope and Foot pad on
the front view; the Screen back cover, Right cover, Rear cover, AC Switch and Power inlet on the
back view; and the PFC Module, Controller module, Cement resistor, AC Switch, Fan, Motor module
and Power inlet on the Anatomical Diagram. **"Power panel" in the table above and "PFC module"
in the diagram are the same board** - item 4 sits where the PFC module is drawn, between the
rocker switch and the controller.

**Which machines this module belongs to.** The i-Strength console cards and the CSI owner's
manuals treat the module as part 72 of the CSI-CPSP chest press and CSI-LROW low row; the
maintenance manual itself never names a host machine.

**Tools are named only inside the disassembly steps** - a Phillips screwdriver on every page, a
6 mm and a 4 mm hex wrench for the motor module, circlip pliers for the reel spring - and the
book prints no tool list.
