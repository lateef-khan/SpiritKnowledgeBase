| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Length | 94" (239 cm) **with rear step** |
| Width | 36" (91.4 cm) |
| Height | 56.5" (143.5 cm) |
| Product Weight | 514.8 Lbs. (234 Kgs) |

**Both MT200 manuals print the same four figures**, the 2010 revision effective
July 2013 and the 2022 revision, so one set serves both generations.

**The length is the figure with the rear step fitted.** The manual gives no
length without it, and no folded or shipping dimension of any kind. It gives
no shipping weight either - 514.8 lb is the product weight.

**These are not the 2026 7.0T or 8.0T figures.** The 7.0T is 94.8" long, 48.7"
wide, 57.5" high and 496 lb; the 8.0T is larger again at 213.3 cm (84") and
252.5 kgs (556.7 lbs). The 7.0T is more than a foot wider than this machine.
Never carry a dimension between them - see
`70t-2026-specs-dimensions-and-product-weight`.

**Related figures the specification page does not print.** No running deck or
belt size, no step-up height, no motor rating and no wheelbase. The step-up
height is stated elsewhere in the book, in the Rear Step and Deck-Lift sections,
not in the spec table.

**Clearance is a separate figure.** The manual asks for a minimum of two feet at
each side, one foot at the front and three and a half feet at the rear; that is
in the Important Safety Instructions, not in this table.
