**No XT owner's manual contains a specification table.** Twelve manuals were
checked - the five 2015 books, the five 2023 books, and the two ENT-console
books, the XT485ENT and the XT685ENT. There is no Specifications page in any of
their tables of contents, and none of these figures is printed anywhere in any
of them:

- motor rating (no horsepower, CHP, or wattage figure appears at all)
- running surface / deck size
- assembled or folded dimensions, and floor footprint
- unit weight or shipping weight

Do not answer these from another Spirit model's manual, and do not carry a
figure across from one XT to another, or from one generation to the other - the
figures these manuals *do* print differ machine by machine and year by year. Get
them from Spirit Fitness or the product spec sheet, and say the manual does not
state them.

**What the 2023 manuals give.**

- The **user weight limit**, as item 21 of the Safety Instructions, different on
  almost every machine: XT185 275 lbs, XT285 350 lbs, XT385 375 lbs, XT485 and
  XT685 425 lbs each.
- A **full parts list** with an exploded view diagram, one per machine.
- A **speed figure, but only for three machines and only inside the Calibration
  Procedure.** The XT385, XT485 and XT685 calibration steps say to set the
  maximum speed to 12.0 mph and the minimum speed to 0.5 mph. The XT185 and
  XT285 calibration procedures omit those steps and print no speed figure at
  all. Nothing in any of the five states a maximum incline; the calibration
  step only says the maximum elevation value is shown in the incline window.

**What the 2015 manuals give, and where they differ.**

- A **maximum speed and a maximum incline for every one of the five machines**,
  printed in the Select Speed & Incline Buttons paragraph of the console section.
  This is the one specification the 2015 books state and the 2023 books do not.
  The figures are not the same across the family and the incline is not stated in
  the same unit on every machine, so read them from the three cards linked above
  rather than quoting one number for the range.
- **No parts list and no exploded view diagram at all** - the opposite of the 2023
  books. See `xt-2015-specs-no-parts-list`.
- A **user weight limit in the XT685-2015 manual only.** The other four 2015
  manuals print none; the safety cards hold the figure.

**A maximum incline percent appears only in the XT485-2015 and XT685-2015
manuals.** No other manual of either generation prints an incline percentage.

**The two ENT-console manuals are no different.** The XT485ENT book runs
TROUBLESHOOTING 55, WARRANTY 56, EXPLODED VIEW 57, PARTS LIST 58 and the
XT685ENT book runs Exploded View Diagram 48, Parts List 49, Warranty 53,
Troubleshooting 56. Neither has a specification page, and neither prints a motor
rating, a deck size, a dimension or a unit weight. Both do print a full parts
list with an exploded view - `xt485ent-2023-specs-parts-list` and
`xt685ent-2023-specs-parts-list` - and **neither list matches the base machine's
list**, so do not read one against the other.

- The **XT485ENT** prints the same maximum-speed sentence the 2015 XT485 and
  XT685 books print - 12.0 mph or 18.0 kmph and incline 15.0%, inside the Direct
  Access Speed and Incline Buttons paragraph. That figure is held by
  `xt-2015-specs-speed-and-incline-range-xt485-xt685`, which now names this
  machine too.
- The **XT685ENT** prints **no speed figure anywhere**. Its Direct Speed &
  Incline Buttons paragraph stops before giving a maximum, and its user weight
  limit is 425 lbs, as item 21 of the Safety Instructions, the same as the base
  XT685-2023. The only incline bound in the whole book is in the Custom program
  section - "Incline values are absolute and vary from 0 to 15" - which is a
  program limit, not a machine specification, and should be quoted as such.

**Belt tension is a procedure in every one of these manuals, never a figure.**
None of the twelve gives a tension in turns from a datum, in newtons, or as a
deflection - only "turn the bolts 1/4 turn each and inspect by walking on the
belt". There is no belt tension figure to quote for any XT.
