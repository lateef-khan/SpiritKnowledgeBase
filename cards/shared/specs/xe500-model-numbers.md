| Year | Model number | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| 2007 | 500007 | xe500-2007 |

The numbers come from the customer-service model table (`dbo.MODEL` in `CustService`); a manual rarely prints one. The table's first-production date is 20 August 2007, the batch date of every 2007 Spirit XE and XT row; the only manual is the 2008 XE100–XE500 dealer service manual.
