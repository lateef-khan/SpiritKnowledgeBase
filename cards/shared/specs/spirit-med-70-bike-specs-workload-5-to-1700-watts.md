**The specification page:** *Work load - 5 watts up to 1700 watts.*

**The features page, eleven pages earlier:** "Work range up to **750 watts** (60 RPM) when level
settings are at 15 watts per level (see Setup under Console section for Level settings)." The 750 W
sentence is the 2021 Dyaco MED book's, whose specification page also says 750
(`70r-2021-specs-workload-5-to-750-watts`); the 2025 books changed the specification row to 1700 and
left the features page as it was. **Both are printed and nothing reconciles them.** Quote 1700 W as
the specification and say the same book's features page gives 750 W.

**What the arithmetic says.** The Level Scale menu sets 5, 10 or 15 watts per level at 60 RPM, and
the console has fifty levels: 50 x 15 W is 750 W, which is where the features-page figure comes from.
1700 W is not reachable from the level scale as printed and must be the brake's rating rather than a
console setting.

**No level-to-watts table** is printed in either book beyond the three per-level scales; the Watts
readout reports the work the rider is doing, left and right leg, not the setting
(`spirit-med-70-bike-specs-console-readouts`). The 8.0U and 8.5R print 5 to 1200 W
(`spirit-med-8-bike-specs-workload-5-to-1200-watts`); the rehabilitation steppers 5 to 1000 W
(`spirit-climber-specs-workload-5-to-1000-watts`).
