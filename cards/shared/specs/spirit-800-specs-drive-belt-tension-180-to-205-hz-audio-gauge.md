**180 to 205 Hz**, read with an **audio (sound wave) tension gauge**. It is a frequency, not a
force: no figure in newtons or pounds is printed for these four bikes.

How the books set it: hook the **J-bolt** on the main frame and tighten its **M8 x 7T nut with a
13 mm wrench** until the belt is "with acceptable tension", check the gauge reads inside the
range, then turn the drive pulley to confirm the belt sits centred on the pulley.

**One misprint to know about.** The CR800(2020) book's belt-slipping answer prints the range as
"**180~20HZ**"; its own replacement chapter, and the other three books in every place, print
180~205HZ. Read the 20 as a dropped digit, not a lower limit.

The 2020-version generator bikes and the 2020-version ENT bikes share this figure; the 2012
XU878 book gives **170 ±10 Hz** (`cu800-2012-specs-drive-belt-tension-170-hz`) and the 900-series
books give forces in newtons.
