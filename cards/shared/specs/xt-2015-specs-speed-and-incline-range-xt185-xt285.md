**Three machines share the lowest ceiling any XT console accepts: the
XT185-2015, the XT285-2015 and the XT685-2010.** Do not quote the XT385-2015,
XT485-2015 or XT685-2015 numbers here.

| | Maximum you can enter | Increment |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | **9.9 mph or KPH** | 0.1 mph |
| Incline | **level 9.5** | .5 level |

The two 2015 manuals print it in one sentence, word for word: "The maximum value
you may input for speed is 9.9 mph or KPH and incline level 9.5. Speed values can
be entered in 0.1 mph increments and Incline in .5 level increments." The
XT685-2010 manual prints the same sentence with the same four figures, writing
the metric unit as "kmph" and the increment as ".1 mph"; its worked examples are
the same two-digit entries, "Press the Quick Speed button, then 8, 0 = 8.0 mph or
kmph" and "then 0, 8 = 0.8 mph or kmph".

**The XT685 ceiling changed between model years, and only the year tells the two
apart.** The XT685-2010 console accepts 9.9 mph and incline level 9.5; the
XT685-2015 console accepts 12.0 mph and 15.0%. Establish the year before
answering an XT685 speed or incline maximum - the 2015 figure is held by
`xt-2015-specs-speed-and-incline-range-xt485-xt685`, and neither figure may be
quoted for the other machine.

**The incline is printed as a level, not as a percent.** None of the three
manuals states the entry maximum as a percentage. The XT485-2015 and XT685-2015
manuals do print a percent (15.0%); do not convert one into the other. **The
XT685-2010 book is ambiguous about its own unit** - it calls the entry maximum
"incline level 9.5" here, while its Incline paragraph says "the display will
indicate incline percent in increments of .5". Quote the figure as the book
prints it, 9.5, and say the manual uses both words for it.

**The XT685-2010 book prints higher numbers in two other places and never
reconciles them with this one.** Its Calibration Procedure tells a technician to
set the maximum speed to 12.0 and the maximum elevation to 15, and its muscle
activation figure describes elevation bands of "0-4.5% Elevation" and "5-15%
Elevation". Those are a calibration setting and a program illustration. The 9.9
mph and level 9.5 above are what the manual says you may **input at the keypad**,
which is the question a user asks; quote it as that, and say the book's own
calibration page carries a different pair of numbers rather than treating either
as the machine's rating.

This is the only place any of the three owner's manuals states a console entry
maximum. None of them contains a specification table, so there is no motor rating,
deck size, dimension or unit weight to go with it.

**The XT185-2015 and XT285-2015 service manuals contradict this figure.** Their
Function tables give a speed WORK range of **1.0 to 16.0 km/h or 0.5 to 12 mph** and
an incline WORK range of **0 to 15 in steps of 1** - not 9.9 mph and level 9.5 in
half steps. Neither book mentions the other's figures. The service-manual table is
held by `spirit-xt-2015-console-data-ranges-16-kmh-incline-steps-of-1`; quote the
owner's-manual figure as what the keypad accepts and the service-manual figure as
the work range, and say the two books disagree.

**The 2013 XT485 prints the low ceiling too, and prints the incline as a percent.** Its Quick Speed &
Incline Buttons paragraph reads "The maximum value you may input for speed is 9.9 mph or kmph and
incline **9.5%**", with the same worked examples (8, 0 = 8.0 mph; 0, 8 = 0.8 mph; 3, 5 = incline
level 3.5) and no increment sentence. So the XT485 went from 9.9 mph / 9.5 in 2013 to 12.0 mph /
15.0 % in 2015 (`xt-2015-specs-speed-and-incline-range-xt485-xt685`), as the XT685 did between 2010
and 2015 - establish the year before answering. Like the XT685-2010, the 2013 book's muscle-activation
page describes elevation bands up to "5-15% Elevation" that it never reconciles with the 9.5 % entry
maximum. Its Calibration Procedure, by contrast, sets a wheel size of 3.01, a minimum of 0.5 mph, a
maximum of **12.0 mph / 18.0 kmph** and a maximum elevation of 15 - technician settings, the same
as the XT685-2010's, not a rated range.
