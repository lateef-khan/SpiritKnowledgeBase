**These are the lowest figures in the 2015 XT family. Do not quote the XT385,
XT485 or XT685 numbers here.**

| | Maximum you can enter | Increment |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | **9.9 mph or KPH** | 0.1 mph |
| Incline | **level 9.5** | .5 level |

Both manuals print it in one sentence, word for word: "The maximum value you may
input for speed is 9.9 mph or KPH and incline level 9.5. Speed values can be
entered in 0.1 mph increments and Incline in .5 level increments."

**The incline is printed as a level, not as a percent.** These two manuals never
state an incline percentage. The XT485-2015 and XT685-2015 manuals do print a
percent (15.0%); do not convert one into the other.

This is the only place either manual states a speed or an incline figure. Neither
manual contains a specification table, so there is no motor rating, deck size,
dimension or unit weight to go with it.
