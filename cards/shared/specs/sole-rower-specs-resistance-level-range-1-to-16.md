**The SR400, SR500-2019 and SR500-2022 rowers have 16 resistance levels,
L1 to L16.** The SR500 books add that levels 1-2 light the first dot, 3-4
the second, and so on across eight dots.

The SR400 wording sits under a toggle-switch figure: press START/STOP, use
UP/DOWN to select the load. The SR500 books repeat the 1-16 range in every
program section ("Press UP or DOWN to adjust resistance level").

**The SR550 book prints no level count** - see
`sole-rower-specs-no-specification-table`. The 2016 rower's 1-16 range comes
from its service manual (`sr500-2016-console-data-ranges`); the Spirit rower
twin prints the same range
(`crw800-2024-specs-level-range-1-to-16`).
