These bikes change resistance with a **tension motor** that pulls a steel cable.

- **Working voltage: DC 4.5 ~ 7.5 V.**
- The motor increases and decreases brake resistance.
- The console holds the key controls and the LCD display. The main controller holds the power supply and the motor driver control circuit.
