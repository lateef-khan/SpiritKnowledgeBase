The CC81-2019 book (evidence for the CC81-2020) prints no exploded view, no replacement-parts list, and no parts-ordering procedure. Proven four ways: zero hits for "exploded", "parts list" or "replacement part" in the full text; the table of contents runs Product Registration, Safety, Electrical, Operation, Assembly, Console, Warranty — no parts chapter; the OCR supplements are the assembly pack checklist, tool drawings and assembly figures, not parts pages; and the assembly chapter's numbered figures are step drawings whose callouts belong to the assembly procedure.

What the book does print is hardware item numbers in parentheses plus the tool list — those live on `sole-climber-specs-assembly-item-numbers-and-tool-lists`, not here. Parts reach the owner through warranty service (return-authorization steps on the warranty cards), never through an order form in the book.
