**The transfer board on the back of the console (p. 18)** carries seven call-outs:

- **HDMI Cable**
- **Ethernet Cable**
- **Hand Pulse (4 Pins)**
- **C-SAFE Cable (5 Pins)**
- **Hand Pulse (3 Pins)**
- **System Cable (6 Pins)**
- **System Cable (4 Pins)**

The two system cables together are the **6+2+2-pin computer cable** that the power bridge board
and the schematic name - one 6-pin plug and two smaller plugs on one loom.

**The HDMI / coaxial cable / C-SAFE PCB board (p. 21)** carries three: **Coaxial Cable**, **HDMI
Cable**, **C-SAFE (5 PINS)**. It sits at the bottom of the mast where the audio-visual cables
enter the machine; the schematic draws it as *HDMI/ETHERNET/C-SAFE PCB* with sockets HDMI, JK1
and C-SAFE.

**No pin definitions are printed** for any of these connectors. The treadmill ENT books
photograph the same HDMI/coaxial/C-SAFE board: `spirit-ct-ent-specs-console-hdmi-coaxial-csafe-board-and-converter`.
