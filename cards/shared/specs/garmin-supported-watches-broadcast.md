**This is the broadcast list for LED and LCD consoles, dated 17 May 2025.** A touchscreen console uses a different and longer list. See `sole-garmin-supported-watches-touchscreen`.

**Broadcast HR must be set to ON in the watch settings.** Without that the watch sends nothing.

42 Garmin devices are on the list.

- **D2** — D2 Air, D2 Air X10, D2 Air Mach 1
- **DESCENT** — Descent G1 Series, Descent G2, Descent MK2 Series, Descent MK3 Series
- **ENDURO** — ENDURO Series
- **EPIX** — EPIX (GEN 2) Series
- **FENIX** — FENIX 6 Serries, FENIX 7 Series, FENIX 8 Series, FENIX E
- **FORERUNNER** — FORERUNNER 55, FORERUNNER 165, FORERUNNER 245, FORERUNNER 255, FORERUNNER 265, FORERUNNER 570, FORERUNNER 745, FORERUNNER 945, FORERUNNER 945 LTE, FORERUNNER 955 Series, FORERUNNER 965, FORERUNNER 970
- **INSTINCT** — INSTINCT 2 Series, INSTINCT 3 Series, INSTINCT E, INSTINCT CROSSOVER Series
- **MARQ** — MARQ Series, MARQ (Gen 2) Series
- **QUATIX** — QUATIX 6 Series, QUATIX 7 Series
- **TACTIX** — TACTIX DELTA, TACTIX 7, TACTIX 8
- **VENU** — VENU 2 Series, VENU 3 Series, VENU SQ 2 Series
- **VIVOSMART** — VIVOSMART 5
- **VIVOACTIVE** — VIVOACTIVE 5, VIVOACTIVE 6

**Why the 2025 date.** The newer 25 May 2026 workbook has no broadcast sheet at all. The 17 May 2025 list is still the newest broadcast list we hold. See `sole-garmin-support-list-versions`.

**Source spelling.** The workbook prints the family headings as "VIVISMART" and "VICOACTIVE", and "FENIX 6 Serries". Those are typing errors in the source. The correct spellings are used above.
