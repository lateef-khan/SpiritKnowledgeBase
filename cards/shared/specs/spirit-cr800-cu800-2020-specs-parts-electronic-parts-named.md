**Both "(2020)" books name the same six parts, on two pages, with the same photographs.**

**2-1 Upper Controllers** - **Display** (a photograph of the LED console head with its dot-matrix window),
**Cooling FAN** and **Thumb Switch** (both leaders land on the console), and **Generator Brake** - a
photograph of the flywheel with its coil pack, called out on the whole-bike drawing inside the front
shroud. The brake is not in the console: the page draws the entire machine and labels the console parts
and the brake on it together, which is why it appears under "Upper Controllers".

**2-2 Lower Controller and Driver** - **Controller** (a bare green circuit board) and **Speed RPM Sensor**
(a reed switch beside the magnet), both photographed inside the shroud.

The CR800 page draws a recumbent and the CU800 page an upright; the parts named, and the photographs of
them, are identical. The same book's Electrical Configurations page calls the brake a **Generator
Flywheel** and its wiring chapter labels it **GENERATOR** - three names for the one part
(`spirit-bike-specs-generator-brake-or-induction-brake`).

**Neither page prints a part number, a rating or a location dimension.** What the controller does and how
it is wired is on the specs cards for chapter 6; this card holds only what the parts are called.

The treadmill books of the same template name a different set - drive motor, incline motor, converter -
under the same headings (`spirit-ct800-specs-electronic-parts-and-outline`).
