The fitness bike is equipped with **two transport wheels** which are engaged when the **rear of the fitness bike is lifted**.

That is the whole of what these manuals say about moving the machine. They give no weight, no wheel location beyond "engaged when the rear is lifted", and no warning about the floor.

**Only the LCB and LCR manuals carry this section.** The B94, R92, B74 and R72 owner's manuals have no Transport Instructions page at all. The SB900 2023 and SB1200 2023 indoor cycles describe a different arrangement: two wheels on the **front** floor mount, tilted forward by gripping the back floor mount.
