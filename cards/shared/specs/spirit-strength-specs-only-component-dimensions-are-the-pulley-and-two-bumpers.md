**The specification table on page 11 names no materials at all.** The only component dimensions in
the whole range are four entries buried in the Specifications column of the assembly hardware tables:

| Component | Printed spec | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| `Pulley sets` and `Pulley Set with Magnets` | **φ89*32** | 89 mm outside diameter, 32 mm wide |
| `Rubber bumper` (stack bottom) | **φ64*φ32*φ20*35** | 64 mm OD, 32 mm and 20 mm bores, 35 mm tall |
| `Rubber bumper` (guide-rod top) | **φ38*φ21*10** | 38 mm OD, 21 mm bore, 10 mm thick |
| `Guide Rod` on CSF-FUNT only | **φ20*1726** | 20 mm rod, 1726 mm long |

**φ89 x 32 is the standard pulley across the selectorized and dual-station machines**, and it is the
same part whether or not it carries the rep-counter magnets. Two machines print a second, larger
pulley: CSS-PRLC lists a `Pulley Φ116*Φ10*47` and CSS-LATP a `Pulley Set φ114*25.8`.

**CSF-FUNT uses a different bumper**, `φ62*φ20*26`, and is the only machine anywhere in the range
whose guide-rod length is printed.

## What is not printed, on any of the thirty-nine

- **No cable diameter and no cable length.** Cables appear in the assembly tables as `Cable`,
  `Cable 1`, `Cable 2` or `Cable Set` with a quantity and an **empty Specifications column**. A
  customer ordering a replacement cable cannot get the size from the manual.
- **No frame or tubing specification** - no gauge, no wall thickness, no section size, no steel
  grade. The word *gauge* does not appear.
- **No upholstery specification** - no material, no foam density, no colour. *Upholstery* appears
  only as a warranty line (6 months) and a maintenance-schedule row.
- **No total pulley count.** Pulleys are counted per assembly step, never summed, and several steps
  add one or two, so a machine total would have to be reconstructed step by step. The manuals never
  state one.
- **No finish or paint specification.**

The warranty page is the only place these components are named as a class - *Frame / Welds / Cams /
Plates* at 10 years, *Bushings / Bearings / Shocks / Pulleys / Guide Rods* at 5, *Cables / Grips* at
1 and *Upholstery* at 6 months. Those are warranty terms, not specifications, and they are carded
under `section: warranty`.
