All three books label the same parts.

**Outlines** - two drawings. The console drawing calls out **Console** and **Hand Pulse**. The base drawing
calls out **AC Motor**, **Inverter Board**, **Incline Motor** and **Lower Control Board** - so this family
has a lower control board *and* an inverter under the hood.

**Upper controllers** - **Cooling FAN**, **Safety key**, **DISPLAY** (the CT1000ENT prints only DISPLAY
and Safety key under "2.1 Console").

**Lower controller and driver** - **AC MOTOR**, **INCLINE MOTOR**, **INVERTER**.

None of the three chapters carries a dimension, a machine weight, a maximum user weight or a power rating.
