**The same list is printed in three books - the 2025 Spirit owner's manual, the 2025 service manual (FR800-SB022-03) and the undated Dyaco "PT" 4.0 R user manual - and all three drawings carry the same stamp, `2019/06/06`.** The numbers below are the balloon numbers on that drawing; the drawing itself is a picture with bare numbers, so this table is the only readable form of it. **No Spirit part number is printed anywhere** - an item number, a description and a quantity is all any of the three books gives.

**173 rows are printed and the item numbers run 1 to 191** with 35 whole numbers absent (64, 117, 120, 121, 124, 130, 131, 133, 134, 135, 137, 138, 140, 142, 144, 145, 146, 147, 149, 150, 151, 152, 155, 156, 158, 159, 174, 176, 181, 182, 183, 184, 187, 188, 190) - they are gaps in the numbering, not a missing page. Two positions are lettered rather than numbered: 9L / 9R (the seat wheel adjustment plates) and 51L / 51R (the crank arms). **The console is item 19 and its fifteen sub-parts are numbered 19~01 to 19~15** - top and bottom covers, book rack, fan assembly, two interface boards (19~05 and 19~11 both read `Interface Board`), display board, resistance button with cable, deflector fan grill, Bluetooth, CSAFE board, faceplate label, fan grill anchors, wind duct and end cap.

## What the list names that the prose does not

- The brake is item 55 `Induction Brake` and its electronics item 43 `Generator/Brake Controller`; the service manual's electronic-parts chapter photographs the same two as HYBRID GENERATOR and DRIVE BOARD (`cu900ent-electronic-parts-locations`).
- The wiring is all in the list by length: 44 `2100m/m_Computer Cable`, 45 `2100m/m_Hand Pulse Sensor Assembly W/Cable`, 46 `1500m/m_Sensor W/Cable`, 47 `850m/m_Wire Brake Coil Harness (Red)`, 48 `1100m/m_Generator Wire Harness`, and four hand-pulse leads (21, 26, 27, 42).
- Four tools are packed as list items: 112 `12/14m/m_Wrench`, 114 `Phillips Head Screw Driver`, 122 `Combination M6 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver` and 123 `13m/m_Wrench`, plus 132 `14/15m/m_Wrench`. The books print no separate tool list.
- Item 49 is the seat `Gas Cylinder`, item 50 the `Steel Cable` that releases it, item 40 the `Release Lever`.

## Where the three printings differ

Only one row differs in substance: **item 170 is `Ø15 × Ø6 × 4T_Nylon Washer` in the owner's manual and the PT edition, and a bare `Nylon Washer` in the service manual.** The service manual also writes `Ø 5` with a space where the owner's manual writes `Ø5`, and the owner's manual wraps item 122 onto two lines ("...Phillips Head Screw" / "Driver"); both are typesetting, not different parts. The word `Podwer` in item 59 (`Podwer metallurgy Sleeve`) is printed that way in all three, and the PT edition prints row 127 twice, at the foot of one page and the head of the next.

**Do not read the 4.0U list for this bike.** The upright's list (`spirit-40u-specs-parts-list-items-1-to-150`) puts the console at item 34 and the induction brake at item 20; the same number names a different part on the two machines.
