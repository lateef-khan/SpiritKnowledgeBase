The distance between the pedals is called the **Q factor**. Typical stationary exercise bikes are wider than a road bike to make room for the braking mechanism, pulleys, drive components and plastic covers, which widens the Q factor.

Sole designed the pedal system so the Q factor is the smallest in the industry, and additionally custom designed a pedal with a **two degree inward tilt** to compensate for the Q factor not being perfect. A small Q factor plus the two-degree inward tilt puts the user into a biomechanical neutral alignment, so feet, ankles, knees and hips line up properly.

The inward tilt is intentional. It is not a bent pedal or a fault.
