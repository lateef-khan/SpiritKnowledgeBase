## Medical purposes

- **Patient warm-up** before a physical therapy session.
- Have the patient **walk to improve ambulation and range of motion** after knee, hip or ankle
  surgery or neurological conditions. *(The MED books word this "have the patient pedal to improve
  range of motion"; the 7.5S adds shoulder and wrist surgery.)*
- Allow patients to perform **cardiovascular exercise**.
- **Used for open kinetic chain exercise only** - the patient is not restrained or connected to the
  machine.
- A device intended **to redevelop muscles or restore motion to joints**.

## Intended patient population

- **Male and female adults. Children at the discretion of a Qualified Physical Therapist and/or
  parental consent.** The manual sets no minimum age.
- Maximal patient's weight: **200 kg** in the MED books and the MS300, **440 lb** in the 2025 books.
  The same allowance - `ms300-2021-safety-patient-weight-limit-200-kg`.
- **The patient must be ambulatory.**
- Medical clearance before any rigorous exercise programme, especially with a history of heart
  disease or other high risk factors.

**Who is excluded is a separate list on a different page** -
`spirit-rehab-stepper-safety-contraindications-osteoporosis-fractures-and-acute-conditions`.

## The part of the body it is applied to

- **Contact site: hands, feet, and trunk.**
- **Condition: should not have any trauma.**

## Intended conditions of use

| | Specified |
|---|---|
| Environment | Indoors. Not outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in high humidity |
| Ambient luminance | Standard ambient room lighting is sufficient |
| Viewing distance | **1 m** |
| Viewing angle | **120°** |
| Temperature | **10 °C to 36 °C** |
| Relative humidity | **30% to 90% RH, non-condensing** |
| Hygienic requirements | **There is no particular restriction** |
| Frequency of use | Dependent on the therapist's plan |
| Location | Hospital, clinic, home, and research in academic institutions |
| Mobility | **The product is intended to be fixed** |

**"There is no particular restriction on hygienic requirements" is the manufacturer's answer to a
clinic's infection-control question**, and there is no disinfection procedure anywhere in these
books - `ms300-2021-maintenance-hygienic-requirements-and-cleaning`.

**"Intended to be fixed" is not "bolted down".** No manual in this family prints a floor fixing, and
all of them fit transport wheels. Read it as *not intended to be moved during use*.

**The 8.5S-Fit prints no Application Specification section at all**, so none of this can be quoted
for that machine - `spirit-climber-specs-medical-certification-list`.
