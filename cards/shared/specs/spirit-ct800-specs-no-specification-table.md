**None of the four CT800 owner's manuals contains a specification table.** All
four were checked - the 2012, 2016 and 2020 CT800 books and the 2022 CT800ENT
book. No table of contents has a Specifications entry, and none of these figures
is printed anywhere in any of them:

- **motor rating** - no horsepower, CHP, wattage or motor part number appears at
  all, for either the drive motor or the incline motor
- running surface / running deck size, and roller diameters
- assembled dimensions, floor footprint, or shipping carton size
- unit weight or shipping weight
- a speed range or an incline range stated as a machine specification
- a certifications list

Do not carry a figure across from one generation to another, or in from a CT850,
CT900 or XT manual. Get these figures from Spirit Fitness or the product spec
sheet, and say the owner's manual does not state them.

**There is no belt tension figure for any CT800.** All four manuals give belt
tension only as a procedure - an 8 mm Allen wrench on the rear roller adjustment
bolts, "1/4 turn each", judged by walking on the belt. There is no tension in
newtons, no deflection, and no number of turns from a datum. The drive belt under
the motor cover has no figure either; all four say only that "Tightening the
motor belt should be done by a trained service person." **Do not answer a CT800
belt tension from `spirit-ct850-drive-belt-tension`.** That card's 70 to 75 lbs
on a gauge is a CT850 service-manual figure for a different machine, and no CT800
book states it.

**The CT850 speed and incline range is not the CT800's either.** The CT850
manuals print a working range of 0.5 to 12 mph and 0 to 15 incline as a machine
specification; the CT800 manuals print the same three numbers only as calibration
defaults a technician types in. They look identical and are not the same claim -
see `spirit-ct850-speed-and-incline-range`.

**The only speed and elevation bounds in the books are calibration settings, not
ratings.** The 2012, 2016 and 2020 Calibration Procedure steps say to set the
wheel size diameter to 2.98, the minimum speed to 0.5, the maximum speed to 12.0
and the maximum elevation to 15. Those are values a technician types into the
console during calibration; the manuals never present them as the machine's
rated range. **The CT800ENT-2022 manual has no calibration procedure at all**, so
it prints no speed or elevation bound anywhere - its Adjusting The Speed Sensor
page and its troubleshooting table both refer to a calibration that is not in the
book.

**What the four books do print, and where each fact lives.**

| Figure | 2012 | 2016 | 2020 | ENT 2022 | Section |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| User weight limit | **not printed** | 450 lbs | 450 lbs | 450 lb | safety |
| Operating temperature / humidity | 40-120 F, 95% non-condensing | same | same | same | safety |
| Nominal circuit | 110-volt / 15 amp | 110-volt / 15 amp | **120-volt** / 15 amp | **120-volt** / 15 amp | safety |
| Minimum clearance around the machine | not printed | 0.5 m each side, 1 m behind | 0.5 m each side, 1 m behind | 1 m behind only | safety |
| Deck life before flipping | 20,000 miles per side | 20,000 miles per side | **4,000 hrs** per side | **4,000 hrs** per side | maintenance |
| Whole-machine parts list | **none** | **none** | 132 items | 148 items | specs |

The 2012 manual is the odd one: it states no user weight limit at all, and it is
the only one of the four with no clearance dimension.

**The parts lists name the motors but do not rate them.** In both the 2020 and
the 2022 list, item 55 is "Drive Motor", quantity 1, and item 66 is "Incline
Motor", quantity 1, with no horsepower, no wattage and no manufacturer's part
number beside either. Items 15, 16, 17 and 18 - the front roller, rear roller,
running deck and running belt - carry no dimensions either.
