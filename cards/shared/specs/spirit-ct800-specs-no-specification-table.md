**None of the six CT800 owner's manuals contains a specification table.** All
six were checked - the 2012, 2016, 2020 and 2024 CT800 books and the 2022 and
2024 CT800ENT books. No table of contents has a Specifications entry, and none of
these figures is printed anywhere in any of them:

- **motor rating** - no horsepower, CHP, wattage or motor part number appears at
  all, for either the drive motor or the incline motor
- running surface / running deck size, and roller diameters
- assembled dimensions, floor footprint, or shipping carton size
- unit weight or shipping weight
- a speed range or an incline range stated as a machine specification
- a certifications list

Do not carry a figure across from one generation to another, or in from a CT850,
CT900 or XT manual. Get these figures from Spirit Fitness or the product spec
sheet, and say the owner's manual does not state them.

**The 2024 redesign changed none of this.** The CT800-2024 and CT800ENT-2024
books are the New Black Units reissue, warranted September and October 2024, and
only about 41% of the CT800ENT-2022 text and 55% of the CT800-2020 text survives
into them. **Neither reissue added a specification page.** Their whole
native-plus-OCR text was searched: *specification* appears only in warranty
boilerplate, *dimension* not at all, *shipping* only in warranty terms, and the
only weight-shaped figure in either book is the 450 lb user weight limit.

**There is no belt tension figure in any CT800 owner's manual.** All six give
tread-belt tension only as a procedure - an 8 mm Allen wrench on the rear roller
adjustment bolts, "1/4 turn each", judged by walking on the belt - and say of the
drive belt only that "Tightening the motor belt should be done by a trained
service person." **The service manuals do print the drive-belt figure**: the
CT800-2020 and CT800ENT-2022 service manuals set it at **70 to 75 lbs in the white
range of a belt tension gauge**, the same step the CT850 books print, and that
figure now stands on `spirit-ct850-drive-belt-tension` for those two machines. The
CT800-2016 service manual prints no belt figure at all, and there is no service
manual for the 2012 or 2024 books; there is still no tread-belt tension figure for
any CT800.

**The speed and incline range is printed as a specification only in the service
manuals.** The CT800-2016 service manual's Function list gives a WORK range of
0.5 to 12 mph and incline 0 to 15; the CT800-2020 and CT800ENT-2022 service
manuals print the same three numbers as factory or engineering defaults. The
owner's manuals print them only as calibration values a technician types in. The
figures and their contradictions are on `spirit-ct850-speed-and-incline-range`,
which now covers the CT800 books too.

**The service manuals name the drive motor and rate the driver board, not the
motor.** The CT800-2016 service manual describes a DC motor with a work voltage
of DC 90V~ and its circuit diagram names it a Ya Cheng YC782; the CT800-2020 and
CT800ENT-2022 service manuals caption the driver board **PA-AE00301L-002,
3.5HP / 110V**. That is the board's rating - no book prints a horsepower for the
motor itself.

**The only speed and elevation bounds in the books are calibration settings, not
ratings.** The 2012, 2016 and 2020 Calibration Procedure steps say to set the
wheel size diameter to 2.98, the minimum speed to 0.5, the maximum speed to 12.0
and the maximum elevation to 15. Those are values a technician types into the
console during calibration; the manuals never present them as the machine's
rated range. **The CT800ENT-2022 manual has no calibration procedure at all**, so
it prints no speed or elevation bound anywhere - its Adjusting The Speed Sensor
page and its troubleshooting table both refer to a calibration that is not in the
book.

**What the six books do print, and where each fact lives.**

| Figure | 2012 | 2016 | 2020 | ENT 2022 | 2024 | ENT 2024 | Section |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| User weight limit | **not printed** | 450 lbs | 450 lbs | 450 lb | 450 lb | 450 lb | safety |
| Operating temperature / humidity | 40-120 F, 95% non-condensing | same | same | same | **40 degrees C**, 95% | **40 degrees C**, 95% | safety |
| Nominal circuit | 110-volt / 15 amp | 110-volt / 15 amp | **120-volt** / 15 amp | **120-volt** / 15 amp | see the safety card | see the safety card | safety |

The 2012 manual is the odd one: it states no user weight limit at all, and it is
the only one of the six with no clearance dimension.

**The parts lists name the motors but do not rate them.** In the 2020, 2022, 2024
and ENT-2024 lists, item 55 is "Drive Motor", quantity 1, and item 66 is "Incline
Motor", quantity 1, with no horsepower, no wattage and no manufacturer's part
number beside either. Items 15, 16, 17 and 18 - the front roller, rear roller,
running deck and running belt - carry no dimensions either.

**The four lists are four different documents.** The 2024 CT800 list carries the
same 132 item numbers as the 2020 list with four rows altered, and the ENT-2024
list drops item 118 from the ENT-2022 list and swaps four cable names. Read a number
only from the book for the machine in hand - see `ct800-2024-specs-parts-list` and
`ct800ent-2024-specs-parts-list`.
