**Three books, eight parts, the same eight.** The XE395 and XE895 of 2016 and the XE395ENT label the same
electronic parts on their chapter-2 pages; only two labels are worded differently.

**Upper Controllers** - **Speaker**, **THUMB SWITCH** (the incline and level toggles on the swing arms),
**Cooling FAN** and **DISPLAY**. The XE395ENT spreads them over two pages, the display on one and the
switch, fan and speaker on the next.

**Lower Controller and Driver** - **SPEED SENSOR** (the XE395ENT: *SPEED RPM SENSOR*), **TENSION MOTOR**
(the gear motor on the steel rope to the brake magnet), **MOTOR CONTROLLER** (the XE395ENT: *CONTROLLER*
- the driver board with transformer and fuse) and **INCLINE MOTOR**, the 115/120 V AC motor with its
three-wire position sensor.

**The speaker is the difference from the CE850 books**, which print the same seven other parts and no
speaker; the CE850-2020 also labels the AC motor a STRIDE MOTOR
(`spirit-ce850-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`). The XE195, XE295 and XG400 name a tension motor and
speed sensor with no controller, incline motor or speaker
(`spirit-xe195-xe295-xg400-2016-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`).

**No part number or rating is printed on these pages.** The tension motor's 4.5-7.5 V DC working voltage,
the incline motor's wire colours and the 14-pin (XE395, XE895) or 6-pin (XE395ENT) console connector are on
the specs cards for chapters 3 and 6; the only tool the three books list is a multi-meter
(`spirit-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`).
