**The block, exactly as printed on both pages:**

> CAN/CSA-C22.2No. 60601-1:14, ANSI/AAMI ES60601-1:2005+A2 (R2012)+A1,
> IEC 60601-1-2:2014, EN60601-1-2:2015, IEC60601-1:2015+A1:2012,
> EN60601-1:2006+A1:2013+A12:2014, **EN ISO 20957-1**, **EN ISO 20957-5**,
> **EU MDR 2017/745 Class I**, ISO 14971:2012

**Declared under the current European medical device regulation as Class I**, where the 7.0R and
7.0U are declared under the older MDD 93/42/EEC as Class Im
(`spirit-med-70r-specs-certifications-mdd-class-im-2007-editions`,
`70u-2025-specs-certifications-mdd-class-im-nb-0123`). No notified-body number is printed.

**EN ISO 20957-5 is the part for stationary exercise bicycles and upper body crank training
equipment**, cited by part number here where the 7-series books cite the undivided EN ISO 20957:2013.
The block is word for word the 8.5UE ergometer's (`85ue-2025-specs-certifications`), which also
cites part 5; the 8.5S stepper's cites part 8 and the 8.0T treadmill's part 6 - the digit after the
hyphen is what tells the lists apart.

**No notified body number, no UDI, no declaration of conformity date and no FDA listing.** The
operating conditions the standards imply are on the safety page; the page after the certifications
is the battery page (`spirit-med-8-bike-specs-battery-type-cr1220`), not an EMC declaration - these
two books print no EMC tables.
