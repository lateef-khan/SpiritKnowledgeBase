**Eight levels on a manual knob.** The parts lists call it an "8-Level Tension Knob" (UB120 item 20, SB120 item 51)
and an "8-Level Tension Control Knob" (EU100, item 17 on the front post). "Use the Tension Knob to adjust the
resistance. Level 1 is the minimum resistance."

The knob pulls a cable: at assembly it is turned "to the 8th (highest) level" and hooked to the **Lower Tension
Wire** (UB120 part 13) or **Lower Tension Cable** (EU100 part 59) that runs down the front post to the brake. The
books never name the brake type; no motor appears in the SB120 or UB120 parts list, so the console (two AAA cells)
only reads the sensor and does not set the load.

The console-driven machines have 24 or 16 levels; the folding FB bikes also have a tension knob but print no level
count.
