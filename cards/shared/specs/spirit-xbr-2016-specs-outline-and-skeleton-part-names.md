Both 2016 recumbent books print the same two drawings with the same labels.

**Outlines:** Console Assembly, Mast Handle bar Assembly, Mesh Seat Back, Crank Arm (L), Pedal (R),
**Round Disk**, **Cable Assembly(L)**, Seat, Pedal (L), Drink Bottle Holder (R), Aluminum Track,
Front Shroud (L), Rear Shroud (R), Bottom Cover, **Handgrip End Cap**.

**Skeleton:** Handle Bar Cover, Seat Back Bracket, Console Mast, Seat Carriage, **Crank Arm End
Cap**, Seat Handle Bar, Flywheel, Idler Wheel Assembly, Drive Pulley, Main Frame, Rear Stabilizer.

The *Round Disk* is the crank-boss cover the replacement chapter removes by hand and refits with
its FRONT mark forward; the *Cable Assembly(L)* is the hand-pulse lead into the left seat handle.
The XBR55 has thumb switches in its handgrips and the XBR25 does not, but the outline drawing
does not show that difference - the *Handgrip End Cap* is labelled on both.

No dimensions or weights are printed. The 2023 XBR55 book numbers its outline instead:
`xbr55-2023-specs-outline-numbered-part-names`.
