Every figure below is transcribed exactly as printed, including the manuals' own inconsistent
spacing of units. **`Weight` is the assembled machine, not the shipping carton** - no manual in this
range prints a carton or gross weight.

| CSF-FUNT | 666.5lb 302.3kg | 60" x 33" x 83" / 1520 x 840 x 2120mm |
| CSF-HRAC | 549 lb / 249 kgs | 1508x1690x2500 mm - **millimetres only** |

| CSF-UPRB | 50 lb / 23 kgs | 1202x767x1062 mm - **millimetres only** |

**CSF-UPRB at 50 lb is the lightest machine in the range and CSF-LEGP the only one with no metric
weight at all.** Five of the six CSF books give no inch dimensions - if a customer needs inches for
these, convert and say you converted.

| CSI-LROW | 198.6 lb / 90 kg | 59.7"x47.6"x43.6" / 1516 x 1210 x 1108 mm |

These two carry no weight stack, which is why they are so much lighter than a selectorized machine
of the same footprint - see `spirit-strength-specs-i-strength-has-no-weight-stack`.

## ST800

**ST800DR3, ST800FI and ST800FT print no weight and no dimensions.** That absence is carded at
`spirit-strength-specs-product-specifications-table-on-page-eleven`.

## Four books disagree with themselves

On CSS-ABDO, CSS-BCUR, CSS-BEXT and CSS-TEXT the pound figure and the kilogram figure are not the
same weight. Do not convert one into the other for those four -
`spirit-strength-specs-pound-and-kilogram-weights-disagree`.
