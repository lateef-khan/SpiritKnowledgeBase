Four books photograph the same board for section 6.1 and print **no call-outs at all** - the page is the
heading and a picture. What the picture shows: a display board silkscreened **PA-AA02251-00T-01**
(sticker 112AA0225100T0134; a second sticker reads A512 2302220 with a 15/220 suffix), with the LCD
ribbon, a row of white JST sockets along one edge and a shielded module in one corner.

Section 6.2 "Display Board PCB Component Locations" is another bare photograph. The XT385 and XT485
books add a section 6.3 for the interface boards inside the console and the console bracket, which do
carry names; the XT185 and XT285 books have no interface board and go straight to the driver board.

The 2015 XT185 / XT285 console board is the AA01382 with a Bluetooth module and printed call-outs; the
XT685-2023 prints its own board with call-outs.
