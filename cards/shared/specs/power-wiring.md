- The **power socket** has its wires permanently attached.
  - Green is ground and goes to the frame.
  - Black goes to the top prong of the **breaker**.
  - White goes to the top left prong of the **on/off switch or AC electronic module**, looking at the back of it.
- The **breaker** comes with no wires. A small black wire runs from the bottom prong of the breaker to the top right prong of the on/off switch or AC electronic module, looking at the back.
- The **on/off switch or AC electronic module** comes with no wires. Its top prongs should be filled by the wires from the power socket and the breaker.
  - A white wire runs from the bottom left prong to the **filter**. If the machine has no filter, it goes straight to the motor controller.
  - A black wire runs from the bottom right prong to the filter, or straight to the motor controller.
