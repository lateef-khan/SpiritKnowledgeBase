The numbers come from the customer-service model table (`dbo.MODEL` in `CustService`); a manual rarely prints one. A row reading *not confirmed* has no settled number yet. The 2013 row is "XT485-2013" in the table (FP 8/13/2012) and the SKU is in the 2013 owner's manual file name; the 2015 and 2023 rows are not settled.
