**Four Spirit rower service manuals were read to their last page. None prints a parts list.**
One prints the exploded view; the other three print neither.

| Book | Exploded view | Parts list |
|---|---|---|
| CRW800 (2020) | **yes** - chapter 10, one drawing titled `CW800B`, balloons 1 to 139, three hand tools drawn as items 126-128, drawing code `1090414AM1105`, PDF p. 43 | **none** |
| CW800-YR001 (the 2016 CRW800) | none | none |
| DW400-YR002 (the XRW600) | none | none |
| CRW900 water rower | none | none |

**The CRW800 (2020) drawing is the owner's manual's drawing without the owner's manual's
list.** Its balloons are the same item numbers the CRW800 owner's-manual parts list decodes -
71 and 72 the chain covers, 43 the console assembly, 35 the gear motor, 36 the controller
assembly, 24 the drive belt - and the replacement chapter of the service manual cites them by
those numbers in every step ("Chain Cover (L) (71)", "500m/m Computer Cable (Upper) (44)"). To
turn a number into a name, use `crw800-2024-specs-parts-list`, minding the two rows that
changed between the 2021 and 2024 printings (`spirit-rower-specs-crw800-parts-list-2021-vs-2024`).
The service manual adds two identities the list lacks - the belt size and the bearing number
(`spirit-crw800-specs-parts-drive-belt-6pj-787l-and-bearing-6201-uou`).

**The 2016 CRW800 and the XRW600 books cite the same item numbers with no drawing anywhere** -
neither the service manual nor, for the XRW600, either owner's manual prints one
(`spirit-rower-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`). Their steps read "Chain Cover (L) (71)"
exactly as the 2020 book's do, and where the two books differ they differ in the number: the
XRW600 book's rear-stabilizer steps cite a Locking Knob (47), bolts (101), (102), (120) and
washers (109), (122) that the CRW800 books do not, and its seat steps name a Rear cover (18)
and a rear folding end assembly (9) where the CRW800 books name a Folding End Assembly (6). A
number from one book is not safe in the other.

**The CRW900 service manual has no parts content at all** - its 19 pages are a speed-sensor
guide, a handle-belt guide and a lubrication note - and the water rower's part numbers are in
its owner's manual's drawing sheets, which use five-digit part numbers rather than item numbers.

**The only tool any of the four lists is a multi-meter**, and the CRW900 lists none
(`spirit-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`).
