**Forty-nine Spirit service manuals - twenty-one treadmill, fourteen bike, fourteen elliptical - name exactly one tool, and it is the same one: a multi-meter.** Each book prints it once, at the head of its error-message chapter, and prints no other tool list anywhere - no wrench sizes, no torque tool, no crank puller, no tension gauge, though the replacement chapters call for them. The wording moves between three layouts and the fact does not. The tables below are per product line; each row is that book's own page.

The `8-2 Prepare tools` pages and the `Prepare:` blocks set the tool out as a Picture column and a Tool name column - on the CT800-2020 page, read from a render, the picture is a hand-held digital multi-meter - and the `Tools Required` line is a single sentence under the chapter heading. None of the twenty-one adds a second row.

**What the multi-meter is for is stated elsewhere in each book, not on this page.** The troubleshooting pages that follow, and the harness-continuity procedures in the CT800-2016 and XT175 books, tell the reader to set it to ohms for harness continuity, to AC volts (the 2015 XT books say "transform into AC 1000V") to check the wall outlet, and to a short-circuit or continuity range to test the safety key contact; those steps belong with the error code or the harness they test, and this card holds only the tool list.

**Three books in the same family print no tool list at all.** The 2021 7.0T service manual for the MT200 has no prepare-tools page - its only tools are three items in its parts list, a Phillips screwdriver, a 5 mm and a 6 mm Allen wrench (`mt200-2022-specs-parts-mt7000-service-manual-list`). The CTSBS900 slat-belt service manual names tools only inside its replacement steps. The 2008 XT175 / XT275 / XT375 / XT475 / XT675 dealer manual says "set your multimeter to read ohms" in its harness procedure and lists no tools; the Allen wrenches and combination driver it does name are rows in its parts lists.

**Do not confuse this with the tools packed in the carton.** The owner's manuals' TOOLS INCLUDED panels (the assembly cards) list the Allen wrenches and screwdrivers shipped with the machine; the service manuals do not repeat them.
