**None of the thirty Xterra bike, elliptical and seated-stepper owner's manuals in this wave prints a
specification table.** No book states the assembled dimensions, the product or shipping weight, the flywheel weight,
the stride length or step height, or a power rating beyond the adapter or batteries the console runs on. The two
service documents (FB160, SB120) and the SB600 service manual print none either; the SB120 service document's whole
*Specs* block is one line, the user weight limit
(`sb120-2022-specs-user-weight-limit-250-lb-in-the-service-document-specs-line`).

What the books print instead, and where each figure lives in this knowledge base:

| Figure a spec table would carry | What the books print | Card |
|---|---|---|
