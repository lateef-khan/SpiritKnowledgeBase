**The calibration defaults allow 0.5 minimum and 12.0 maximum speed (mph).** Every pre-stencil book in `applies_to` prints the same three engineering-calibration lines: "Adjust the minimum speed (if needed) to 0.5", "Adjust the maximum speed (if needed) to 12.0" and "Adjust the maximum elevation (if needed) to 15".

The stencil-era F60-2020 book states the same 0.5 to 12 span in its own engineering mode ("speed (0.5mph to 12mph)"), which runs the calibration itself, and on its console page ("from starting at 0.5 mph to 12.0 mph"). No other stencil-era owner's book prints an absolute speed span: the button books print only 2-through-12 presets, the touchscreen books print only percentages of a user-set maximum, and the TD80 prints its own 0.5 to 4.0 span on a separate card.

Ruled contradiction: the F60-2013/2014 book's Quick speed keys top out at 10 ("Quick speed keys 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, or 10"), while its calibration page still allows 12.0. The keys are presets, not the limit, so this card follows the calibration maximum of 12.0. The calibration key sequence itself is a console procedure, carded by the console section; this card holds only the figures.
