**Outlines (p. 5), both books** - the drawing calls out **Console**, **Cooling Fan**, **Safety Key**,
**Drink Bottle Holder**, **Handle Pulse Sensor**, **Adjustable Foot Pad**, **Running Belt**, **Rear Roller
End Caps** and **Moving Wheel**. It is the richest outline drawing in the CT family; the 2020 books label
nothing.

**Upper controllers (p. 7), both books** - **Display** and **Earphone Port**.

**Lower controller and driver (p. 8)** - the CT800ENT labels **Drive Board**, **DC Drive Motor**,
**Converter**, **Incline Motor** and **Power Bridge Board**. The CT850ENT labels **Inverter**, **AC Drive
Motor**, **Converter**, **Incline Motor** and **Power Bridge Board**, and its photograph carries a
"WARNING! HAZARDOUS VOLTAGE!" sticker on the inverter.

Neither chapter carries a dimension, a machine weight, a maximum user weight or a power rating.
