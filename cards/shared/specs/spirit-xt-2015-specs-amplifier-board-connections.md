Section 6.3 in each book is one photograph of the amplifier board with its plugs named.

**XT185-2015 and XT285-2015** - board **YJ-8511**: **AUDIO OUT**, **AUDIO IN**, two **SPEAKER (2 PINS)**
plugs, and two multi-way plugs labelled **JK1** and **JK2** (one is the 4-pin AMP wire from the display
board).

**XT385-2015 and XT485-2015** - board **8509**: **AUDIO OUT**, **AUDIO IN**, **JK4** and **JK5** each
labelled **SPEAKER (2 PINS)**, **JK7** and **JK8**, and **JK2 POWER**.

Neither book prints a pin definition for any plug. On the block diagram the amplifier's LINE IN is the
console's audio input jack and, on the XT385 / XT485 and the CT800-2016, LINE OUT is the headphone jack.
The 2023 XT385 / XT485 / XT685 use a different amplify board (AG0003) with BLE music plugs instead of
audio jacks.
