The scanned owner's manual is filed as ERG400_OM_140914 and stamped ERG400_20150205 (its warranty page still reads effective August 1, 2012); the table's "XTERRA ERG400" rows (two serial-number ranges, one number) are 140914, first production 18 August 2014.
