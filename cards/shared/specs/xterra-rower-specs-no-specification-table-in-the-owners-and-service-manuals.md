**None of the eleven Xterra rower owner's manuals, and none of the six rower service manuals, prints a
specification table.** No book states the assembled length, width or height, the folded size, the product or
shipping weight, the rail length or inseam limit, or the flywheel, impeller or tank weight.

Two pages look like one and are not:

- The ERG180 book has a page headed **SPECIFICATIONS** (printed p. 23). It is the console block - display ranges,
  "Battery Type 2pcs of Size AA", operating and storage temperature - and nothing about the machine
  (`xterra-rower-specs-console-runs-on-two-aa-batteries`).
- The ERG800W book's text layer carries three lines on PDF p. 8 - "Package Dimension: 124 cm (L) x 91 cm (W) x
  38 cm (H)", "Working Dimension: 216 cm (L) x 55 cm (W) x 120 cm (H)", "Storage Dimension: 82 cm (L) x 55 cm (W)
  x 216 cm (H)". **They are not printed.** The rendered page shows only the free-area diagram (an 85 in by 21 in
  training area with 23 in of free area on each side; the safety section holds it). The figures are an
  invisible OEM layer left in the file, so this knowledge base does not quote them as a specification.

The parts lists (ERG160, ERG180, ERG550W, ERG800W) print hardware sizes and a few part dimensions - the ERG160's
Ø150 flywheel, the ERG550W's 925 mm aluminium rail plates - but no machine dimension; the parts section holds
them. The Spirit rower books lack a spec table in the same way (`spirit-crw800-specs-no-specification-table`), as
do the Xterra treadmill and bike books.
