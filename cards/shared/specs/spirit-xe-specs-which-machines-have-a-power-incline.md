**Only the three XE395 machines have a powered incline ramp.** The XE395-2010, the
XE395ENT-2021 and the XE395-2023 each have an incline rail assembly, an incline
motor and an incline controller in the assembly steps and the parts list, incline
keys on the console, and an incline reading on the display.

The XE395-2010 manual states it outright: "The XE395 has a motorized incline ramp
feature that allows you to simulate walking uphill."

**The other eight manuals never use the word incline once.** No incline key, no
incline rail in the assembly, no incline motor in any parts list, and no incline
readout described on any console:

| Machine | Powered incline |
|---|---|
| XE395-2010, XE395ENT-2021, XE395-2023 | yes |
| XE795, both 2021 printings and the 2023 | no - the word does not appear |
| XE195-2021, XE295-2021 | no - the word does not appear |
| XG400-2021 E-Glide Trainer | no - the word does not appear |
| XE100-2007, XE200-2007, XE300-2007 | no - the word does not appear |
| XE150-2005, XE350-2005, XE550-2005 | no - the word does not appear |

**Only the XE395-2010 book gives an incline range.** It is 40 levels in half-level
steps - see `xe395-2010-specs-incline-forty-levels-in-half-steps`. Neither the
XE395ENT-2021 nor the XE395-2023 states how far its ramp travels, in levels,
degrees or percent, and their consoles are not the XE395-2010's. **Do not quote the
2010 figure for either of them.**

The XE395ENT-2021 comes closest and still gives no absolute figure: its program
descriptions say things like "the incline alternates between 25 & 65 % of maximum
elevation", which is a share of a maximum the manual never states.

**A raised ramp is not an adjustable stride.** No machine in this group has one -
the word *stride* appears in none of the eleven manuals. The Spirit commercial
CE850 is the elliptical with an adjustable stride, and its card is not an answer
for a home machine.
