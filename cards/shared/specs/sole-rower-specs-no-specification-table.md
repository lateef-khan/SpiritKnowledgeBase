**None of the four Sole rower owner's books prints a specification table.**
No dimensions, footprint, product or shipping weight, rail length, resistance
type, maximum-user-weight figure, wiring diagram, or FCC statement appears in
any of them. Every "wiring" hit is warranty boilerplate about defects in
house wiring.

**What the books do state, and where that lives:**

- **16 resistance levels, L1 to L16** (SR400, SR500-2019, SR500-2022) -
  `sole-rower-specs-resistance-level-range-1-to-16`. The SR550 book prints no
  level count.
- **Watt windows 0-999 (SR400) and 0-2000 (SR500 pair)** - console readouts,
  carded under console.
- **Folding aluminum rail assembly** - folding procedure, carded under
  assembly.
- **SR550 runs on DC 12V/3.3A from a power pack on 115 VAC** - already carded
  under safety (`sr550-2023-electrical-power-requirements`) and console
  (`sr550-2023-power-up`).
- **SR400 slide track named "UPPER SLIDE TRACK 1500MM"** in its parts list -
  part names, carded under parts.
- **110-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet, 40-to-120 °F operating range** -
  electrical requirements, carded under safety.
