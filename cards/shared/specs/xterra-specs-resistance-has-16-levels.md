**Sixteen levels.** The FS1.5 book: "LEVEL: The programs have 16 levels of work displayed in 8 bars in each column
... each bar represents 2 levels of work", and "All program profiles have 16 levels of resistance." The SB600 book
prints it as a range in its value table: **Load 1 - 16** (the same table gives Watt 10-350 and RPM 15-999; the
console section holds the display ranges).

Both are console-driven: the level is chosen with the UP/DOWN arrows and, in the heart-rate programs, "the load level
will adjust according to your current heart rate". The SB2.5r, SB4500, FS2.5, FS3.5 and RSX1500 have 24 levels.

**Two console-driven rowers print the same count.** The ERG400 book: "The eight vertical blocks represent
resistance. Each block represents two levels of resistance, with a maximum of sixteen levels of resistance", set
with the UP/DOWN keys one level per press. The ERG700 book: "The level window shows the current resistance level.
The level range is 1~16. Level 1 and 2 would light the first dot, level 3 and 4 would light the second dot, etc."
On the ERG700 a tension motor fed at DC 12 V moves the brake
(`erg700-2022-specs-power-adapter-ac-100-to-240-v-in-dc-12-v-out-feeding-the-console-and-tension-motor`); the
ERG400 book names no motor. The Spirit CRW800 and XRW600 air rowers print the same sixteen levels on eight dots
(`crw800-2024-specs-level-range-1-to-16`). The knob-adjusted Xterra rowers print no count
(`xterra-erg-specs-magnetic-resistance-from-a-manual-tension-knob-with-no-level-count-printed`).
