**Sixteen levels.** The FS1.5 book: "LEVEL: The programs have 16 levels of work displayed in 8 bars in each column
... each bar represents 2 levels of work", and "All program profiles have 16 levels of resistance." The SB600 book
prints it as a range in its value table: **Load 1 - 16** (the same table gives Watt 10-350 and RPM 15-999; the
console section holds the display ranges).

Both are console-driven: the level is chosen with the UP/DOWN arrows and, in the heart-rate programs, "the load level
will adjust according to your current heart rate". The SB2.5r, SB4500, FS2.5, FS3.5 and RSX1500 have 24 levels.
