**None of the six Sole strength owner's books prints a specification table.**
No dimensions, footprint, product or shipping weight, tube gauge, weight
capacity, wiring diagram, or parts-list dimension callout appears in any of
them. The SW100 book adds an exploded view and parts list with no figures
behind them.

**What the books do state, and where that lives:**

- **User weight 330 pounds (150 kilograms)** in five books -
  `sole-strength-max-user-weight-330-lbs`; **265 pounds
  (120 kilograms)** in the SRVO complete bench book -
  `sole-strength-max-user-weight-265-lbs`. The rules themselves
  are carded under safety.
- **Adjustable dumbbell 10-55 LB in 10 levels (SW155)** and **5-80 LB in 16
  levels (SW180)** -
  `sole-sw155-specs-adjustable-dumbbell-10-to-55-lb-in-10-levels`,
  `sole-sw180-specs-adjustable-dumbbell-5-to-80-lb-in-16-levels`.
- **FCC Part 15 Class B warning** (SRVO-2021 book, printed p. 10) -
  `sole-srvo-specs-fcc-part-15-class-b-warning`.
- **Three resistance modes with knob selection** (SRVO-2021) - console
  operation, carded under console.
- **Live area at least 140 cm (55.12 in) beyond the training area** -
  installation safeguard, carded under safety.
- **Exploded views, hardware kits and pack lists** - carded under parts.

Note: the SRVO complete book (cover "SRVO complete", footer SRB101 Ver.A,
"CONGRATULATIONS ON YOUR NEW BENCH") is a bench book, not the powered
cable trainer the SRVO-2021 book covers.
