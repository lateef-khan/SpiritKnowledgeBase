**None of these seven has a weight stack, a selector pin, guide rods or plates.** Their assembly
hardware tables contain no plate row of any kind, checked in the text layer and again on a render of
every assembly page.

| Machine | What it is | What its specification row says instead |
|---|---|---|
| CSF-AABB Ab / Back Bench | bench | `Maximum Loading Capacity 600 lb` |
| CSF-BEXT Back Extension | bench | `Maximum Loading Capacity 600 lb` |
| CSF-UPRB Utility Bench | bench | `Maximum Loading Capacity 600 lb` |
| CSF-HRAC Half Rack | rack | `Safety Catches Max Load 650 lb` |
| CSF-LEGP Leg Press | plate-loaded carriage | `Maximum Carriage Capacity 770 lb` |
| ST800FI Flat Incline Bench | bench | `WEIGHT LIMITS: 1,000LBS TOTAL; USER - 300LBS; LIFTING WEIGHT - 700LBS` |
| ST800DR3 Dumbbell Rack | rack | `TOTAL WEIGHT LIMIT: 1,000LBS` |

**Those are static load ratings, not resistance.** They tell an installer what the frame will hold,
not what the user is lifting. The figures themselves and the rules around them are carded under
`section: safety`; this card exists so that a stack question about these seven gets *"there is no
stack"* rather than a plate count borrowed from a neighbouring machine.

**CSF-LEGP is plate-loaded**, so its resistance is whatever Olympic plates the facility puts on the
carriage, up to 770 lb. The manual specifies no plates and ships none.

**CSF-BEXT is not CSS-BEXT.** The functional back extension here has no stack; the selectorized back
extension has a 280 lb one - `spirit-strength-specs-stack-of-280-lb`. Two different machines, two
different answers.

The two other stackless machines in the range are the i-Strength pair, which have a motor instead -
`spirit-strength-specs-i-strength-has-no-weight-stack`.
