| Year | Model number | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| 2021 | not confirmed | 70r-2021 |
| 2025 | 770145 | 70r-2025 |

The numbers come from the customer-service model table (`dbo.MODEL` in `CustService`); a manual rarely prints one. A row reading *not confirmed* has no settled number yet. The 2021 row is the Dyaco "MED" rehabilitation-bike manual, Rev. 1.2.1 of 14 October 2021, with no row of its own.
