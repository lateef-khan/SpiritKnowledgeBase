**Both CE900 books print the same 33-row table** beside a drawing of the machine's plastic covers, headed
*Part Number / Part Description / Qty per unit* and split into two columns of 16 and 17 rows. The
CE900ENT copy prints a Chinese name beside each English one, and that column is what tells the two
"Console Assembly" rows apart.

**Items 1 and 2 are both printed *Console Assembly*.** The CE900ENT's Chinese column reads 電子錶上殼 and
電子錶下殼 - the console's upper and lower shells - so the two rows are the two halves of the console
case, not two consoles. Item 3, *reading race holder*, is 書報架, a book rack; item 4, *cooling fan*, is
風扇導流片, the fan's deflector grille; item 5's *End Cap* is 轉接孔塞, a plug for an adapter hole; item 8's
*TV Protective Rubber* is TV 管端塞, the end plug of the TV tube; item 32's six *Caps* are 管塞, tube plugs.

**These are call-out numbers on a drawing, not Spirit part numbers, and the list covers only the covers.**
Neither book prints a number to order against, a quantity for anything but plastics, or a drawing of the
drive, the rails or the electronics - those are named on the chapter-2 pages instead
(`spirit-ce900-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`). The owner's manual for the CE900 prints no exploded
view and no list at all (`spirit-ce-assembly-exploded-view-and-parts-list`).

**The bike books under the same heading print different lists** - 29 rows for the CR900/CR900ENT with a
beam cover and an off-switch bracket (`spirit-cr900-specs-parts-component-callout-numbers`), 20 for the
CU900/CU900ENT with seat covers and an electronic module (`cu900ent-component-callout-numbers`). Both of
those also open with two Console Assembly rows; only the elliptical ENT book explains them.
