Both books print the same two drawings, and the labels are identical.

**p. 4, the shrouded bike:** Console Assembly, Seat Back, Pedal, Seat, Console Mast Cover,
Drink Bottle Holder, Front Shroud (labelled twice, left and right), Rear Shroud.

**p. 5, the frame with the shrouds off:** Handle Bar, Console Mast, Back-up Lever, Drive Pulley,
Crank Arm(L), Crank Arm(R), Main Frame, **Generator/Resistance**, Seat Back Bracket, Handlebar,
Iron Board, Seat Back Frame, Lower Controller Plate, Rear Stabilizer.

**Generator/Resistance is the label on both books**, including the CR900ENT, whose own block
diagram calls the same unit a *brake flywheel* and whose circuit diagram feeds the bike from a
24 V adapter rather than a generator. The skeleton drawing was carried over from the 2018
generator bike unchanged.

The Outlines chapter prints **no dimensions, no machine weight and no user weight limit** in
either book.
