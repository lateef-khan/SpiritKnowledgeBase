The numbers come from the customer-service model table (`dbo.MODEL` in `CustService`); a manual rarely prints one. A row reading *not confirmed* has no settled number yet. Two numbers in one cell are the plain and the C-SAFE build of the 2009 machine, both named "CR800 2009" in the table.
