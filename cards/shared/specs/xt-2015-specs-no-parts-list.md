**None of the five 2015 XT owner's manuals contains an exploded view diagram or a
parts list.** There is no Parts List or Exploded View entry in any of their tables
of contents, and no whole-machine table of item number, description and quantity
appears anywhere in them. The documents run from the safety pages to the warranty
and stop.

**Do not answer a 2015 part number from a 2023 manual.** The 2023 XT manuals each
carry a full parts list, and those item numbers are numbered per manual - the
XT185-2023 Drive Motor is 027, the XT385/XT485-2023 Drive Motor is 34, the
XT685-2023 Drive Motor is 20. Nothing establishes that any of those numbers applies
to a 2015 machine. Get 2015 part numbers from Spirit Fitness or the dealer parts
list, and say the owner's manual does not print them.

**What the 2015 manuals do print instead.**

- A **PARTS INCLUDED** and **TOOLS INCLUDED** box on the PRE ASSEMBLY page, listing
  the major sub-assemblies in the carton and the tools supplied. It gives no item
  numbers.
- A small **HARDWARE FOR STEP n** table at each assembly step, with the columns
  PART / TYPE / DESCRIPTION / QTY. These cover only the fasteners used in that one
  step. The XT185, XT285 and XT685 manuals have five such steps; the XT385 and
  XT485 manuals have six.
- Assembly text that cites item numbers in parentheses, for example
  "RIGHT UPRIGHT (4)" and "MIDDLE COMPUTER CABLE (38)". The PRE ASSEMBLY page says
  these are "the item number from the assembly drawing", but **the numbered assembly
  drawing those numbers belong to is not printed in the manual**. The only
  whole-machine illustration is the overview page, and it is labelled with part
  *names* - console, motor cover, belt, side rails - not with item numbers. See
  `xt-2015-assembly-parts-diagram-labels`.
