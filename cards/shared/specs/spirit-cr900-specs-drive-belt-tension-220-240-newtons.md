**220 to 240 newtons**, which the books also print as **50 to 55 lbs**. That is a force, not a
sonic-meter frequency, and no gauge part number is given.

Both recumbent books print it twice: when the Poly-V drive belt goes back on after the idler is
refitted (6.6.2), and in the slipping-belt remedy (7.1), where the top nut on the **J-bolt** is
tightened to load the tension spring until the slipping stops, then the bottom jam nut is
re-tightened.

**The uprights on the same frame use a lower figure.** The CU900-2018, CU900ENT and CU1000ENT
books print **180 to 210 N** - see `cu900ent-drive-belt-tension`. Do not carry the recumbent
figure onto an upright or the reverse.

**The CR1000ENT-2023 prints the same sentence** in its drive-system replacement - "make sure the
belt tension is set to 220 ~ 240 Newtons (50-55 Lbs.) and keep the belt in the middle of pulley"
- with the same J-bolt adjustment nut and tension spring, so this card covers that machine too.
Its book prints the figure once, not twice; the slipping-belt page (13.1) only points back to the
adjustment.
