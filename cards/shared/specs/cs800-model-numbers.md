| Year | Model number | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| 2016 | 800645 | cs800-2016 |
| 2021 | not confirmed | cs800-2021 |
| 2024 | 800625 | cs800-2024 |

The numbers come from the customer-service model table (`dbo.MODEL` in `CustService`); a manual rarely prints one. A row reading *not confirmed* has no settled number yet. The 2016 number is the row named "Spirit CS800 2016", the machine of the "XS200-SS003" service manual; the table also lists "Spirit CS800 2020" 800640, not confirmed for cs800-2021.
