**Two CE850 books, seven parts, one label that changes.**

**2-1 Upper Controllers** - **THUMB SWITCH** (the stride and level toggles on the swing arm), **Cooling FAN**
and **DISPLAY**.

**2-2 Lower Controller and Driver** - **SPEED SENSOR**, **TENSION MOTOR** (the gear motor on the steel rope
to the brake magnet), **MOTOR CONTROLLER** (the driver board with the transformer and fuse) and the AC
motor that changes the stride length - labelled **INCLINE MOTOR** in the 2016 (XE898-SE011) book and
**STRIDE MOTOR** in the 2020 book. It is the same part: both books describe it on their Electrical
Configurations page as the STRIDE MOTOR, a 115 V AC motor with a three-wire position sensor, and the
2016 book's own error chapter calls its fault a STRIDE ERR. The 18-24 inch stride it moves is on
`spirit-ce850-specs-stride-length-18-to-24-inches`.

**The XE895 (XE895-SE022), the residential twin of the 2016 book, names an eighth part** - a Speaker on the
console - and so do the XE395 and XE395ENT (`spirit-xe395-xe895-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`). The
CE800 books have no tension or stride motor at all; they name a generator brake and a controller
(`spirit-ce800-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`).

**No part number or rating is printed on these pages.** The tension motor's 4.0-6.0 V DC working voltage,
the stride motor's wire colours and the 14-pin console connector are on the specs cards for chapters 3
and 6; the only tool either book lists is a multi-meter
(`spirit-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`).
