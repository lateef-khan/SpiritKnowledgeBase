| Book | Specification page | What it prints |
|---|---|---|
| **7.0R-2025**, **7.0U-2025** (Spirit, Rev. 12.23.25 / 12.09.2025) | yes, two pages | dimensions, weight, resistance and levels, work load, patient weight, power, fuse, readouts, manufacturer, certifications, disposal (7.0U), then an EMC declaration |
| **8.0U-2025**, **8.5R-2025** (Spirit, Rev. 12.03.25 / 12.15.25) | yes, two pages | dimensions, fuse, product weight, resistance and levels, power rating, patient weight, workload, manufacturer, certifications, then a battery page - **no readouts line** |
| **7.0R-2021** (Dyaco MED, Rev. 1.2.1) | yes, three pages | the 7.0R-2025 list with different dimensions and work load, a European representative, an EMC declaration printed as text, and a labelling-symbols page with the Type B applied-part mark |
| **4.0R-2025**, **4.0U-2025** (Spirit, Rev. 10.03.25) | **no** | nothing - see below |
| **4.0R-PT**, **4.0U-PT** (Dyaco "PT", undated) | no page | one line under the last assembly step: *Size* and *Weight* |

**The two Spirit 4.0 books print no specification of any kind.** Their tables of contents run
Machine Care, Exploded View, Parts List, Warranty; the word *specification* occurs only in the
operating-temperature line on the electrical-safety page and in the warranty boilerplate, and no
dimension, machine weight, wattage or fuse appears anywhere in either book - checked in the text
layer and in the OCR of every page. They are self-powered generator bikes with 40 console levels
(`spirit-ce-specs-forty-resistance-levels`) and a 440 lb user limit on the safety page. Their service
manuals add the wiring but no figures either
(`spirit-med-40-bike-specs-electrical-configuration-generator-brake`). The Dyaco PT editions of the
same two bikes are the only source of a size and weight (`40r-pt-specs-size-and-weight`,
`40u-pt-specs-size-and-weight`).

**Where each figure lives**

- Dimensions and weight: `70r-2025-specs-dimensions-and-weight`, `70u-2025-specs-dimensions-and-weight`,
  `70r-2021-specs-dimensions-and-weight`, `80u-2025-specs-dimensions-and-weight`,
  `85r-2025-specs-dimensions-and-weight`.
- Resistance: `spirit-med-70-bike-specs-eddy-current-brake-and-fifty-levels`,
  `spirit-med-8-bike-specs-eddy-current-brake-and-one-hundred-levels`.
- Work load: `spirit-med-70-bike-specs-workload-5-to-1700-watts`, `70r-2021-specs-workload-5-to-750-watts`,
  `spirit-med-8-bike-specs-workload-5-to-1200-watts`.
- Power and fuse: `spirit-med-70-bike-specs-mains-90-to-240-v-1-76-to-0-71-a`,
  `spirit-med-8-bike-specs-mains-100-to-240-v-1-76-to-0-71-a`,
  `spirit-med-bike-specs-fuse-rating-5-a-250-v-fast-acting-glass`.
- Readouts, manufacturer, certifications, EMC, battery: `spirit-med-70-bike-specs-console-readouts`,
  `spirit-med-bike-specs-manufacturer-dyaco-international`, `spirit-med-70r-specs-certifications-mdd-class-im-2007-editions`,
  `70u-2025-specs-certifications-mdd-class-im-nb-0123`, `spirit-med-8-bike-specs-certifications-eu-mdr-class-i`,
  `spirit-med-bike-specs-emc-declaration`, `spirit-med-8-bike-specs-battery-type-cr1220`.

The patient weight capacity (440 lb / 200 kg on every plug-in book) is a safety figure and lives
with the safety cards. The 8.5UE upper body ergometer, on the same page layout as the 8 series, has
its own page (`85ue-2025-specs-specification-page`); the rehabilitation steppers have theirs
(`spirit-climber-specs-rehabilitation-stepper-specification-page`).
