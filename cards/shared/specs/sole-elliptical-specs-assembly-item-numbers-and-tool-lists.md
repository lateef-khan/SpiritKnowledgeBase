The books print three kinds of parts information, all tied to assembly steps — never a standalone list:

- **Hardware-pack item numbers.** Every step names hardware as name plus parenthesized item number: "5/16\" Nylon Nut (**137**)", "Computer Cable (**50**)", "Console Mast (**12**)", "Main Frame (**1**)". The WE books spell out the convention: "The numbers in the instructions that are in parenthesis (#) are the item number from the assembly drawing, for reference." The assembly drawings themselves belong to the assembly section; only the numbering convention is recorded here.
- **Isolated part-number callouts.** The only "part # / P/N" hits in the wave are in the WE books' plastic-parts steps: "Connecting Arm Covers (**part # 109&110**) with the pre-installed inner connecting arm covers (**P/N 111 & 112**)" (WE55/WE95 book), and "(part # 109&110)" with "P/N 111 & 112" (WE25/WE35 book). Carded digit-exact as printed.
- **Tool lists.** The 2011 native books open assembly with an ASSEMBLY TOOLS block: Phillips Head Screwdriver, Short Phillips Head Screwdriver, 12/14mm Wrench and 13/14mm Wrench with item numbers (AE25/AE35: #159–#162; E98-2011: #169–#172). The WE books print a Tools block with the same four tools (#159 13/14mm, #162 12/14mm, 1 pc each). The 2007 books print no tool list — steps just say "Remove the tools first" and name wrenches inline ("Using the 13/14m/m Wrench", "two sided wrench", "Phillips head screwdriver"). The stencil books number tools inside hardware steps instead (E25-2016: #186 13/14mm Wrench 160mm, #189 12/14mm Wrench, #188/#187 Phillips drivers; E20-2016: #108 M5 Allen/Phillips combo, #111 12/14mm Wrench; E95S-2015: #193 M8 Allen, #194/#195 wrenches; E25-2023: #177 8mm L Allen, #178/#179 wrenches).

Two standing instructions, same in every era: "Remove the tools first. Remove the hardware for each step as needed" from the hardware package, and tighten bolts "as much as you possibly can" — "It may be necessary to use a larger wrench than the one provided if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently."

The three SC climber books print the same convention ("The numbers in the instructions that are in parenthesis (#) are the item number from the assembly drawing for reference") with their own numbers: SC200 checklist hardware #100/#106/#108 washers, #78/#81 bolts, #83/#85 screws, #90 nut, #113/#74 (SC200-2016 and SC200-2017 books share every number); SC300 hardware #136/#142/#144 washers, #106/#110/#113 bolts and screws, #102/#117/#124/#143. Tool blocks: SC200 #118 Phillips Head Screw Driver, #119 M8 L Allen Wrench, #116 13/14mm Wrench, #117 12/14mm Wrench; SC300 #151 driver, #152 M8 L Allen, #149 13/14mm, #150 12/14mm.
