**Every one of these bikes brakes a magnet against a flywheel. What differs is
what moves the magnet, and whether the bike needs an outlet.**

Only the six manuals that carry a parts list name the parts at all - see
`spirit-residential-bike-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`. The other eight
name no resistance part anywhere.

| Manual | What moves the magnet | Mains parts listed |
|---|---|---|
| XBR25 2023 | **Gear Motor** (item 43) | Power Adaptor (131), Transformer Power Cord (41) |
| XBR55 2023 | **Gear Motor** (item 43) | Power Adaptor (131), Transformer Power Cord (41) |
| XBR55ENT 2021 | **Gear Motor** (item 43) | Power Adaptor (131), Transformer Power Cord (41) |
| XBU55 2023 | **Gear Motor** (item 21) | Power Adaptor (104), Transformer Power Cord (109) |
| XBU55ENT 2021 | **Gear Motor** (item 21) | Power Adaptor (104), Transformer Power Cord (123) |
| **XBR95 2023** | **Generator/Brake Controller** (item 43), with a **Generator Wire Harness** (48) and an **800mm Wire Brake Coil Harness** (47) | **none - no Power Adaptor and no Transformer Power Cord in the whole list** |

The Flywheel and the Magnet are item **55** and **56** on every recumbent list and
item **20** and **22** on both upright lists. The two upright lists also carry a
**Brake Tension Knob**, item **86**; neither recumbent list has one.

## The XBR95 powers itself; every other bike in the range plugs in

This is the same split, stated in the console chapter, and it covers all fourteen
books rather than only the six with parts lists.

- **All four XBR95 printings** say the bike "has a built-in generator for power and
  does not need to be plugged into an AC outlet. To power up the bike simply start
  to pedal, the console will turn on automatically."
- **The other ten books** say only "When the power cord is connected to the
  equipment, the console will automatically power up."

**The 2016, 2018 and 2021 XBR95 books word it as "Spirit Fitness commercial Semi -
Recumbent Bikes have a built-in generator"** - the word *commercial* is left over
from another book and does not mean the reader has a commercial machine. The 2023
printing corrects it to "The XBR95 Recumbent Bike has a built-in generator".

**Every one of the fourteen books still carries the boilerplate electrical safety
page telling you to unplug the appliance from the outlet when not in use**, XBR95
included. That page contradicts the XBR95 POWER paragraph and does not override
it. Whether the machine needs an outlet, and at what voltage, is a safety question
and lives in the `safety` section.

## No resistance figure goes with any of this

The manuals never state a resistance rating in watts, newtons or kilograms.
Resistance is given only as a count of console levels - **40 on the XBR95 and 20 on
everything else**. See `spirit-residential-bike-specs-no-specification-table`.

## Do not carry a commercial bike's brake part across

The Spirit commercial CR and CU bikes use a **Generator/Brake** unit or an
**Induction Brake with an EMS Controller**, with their own item numbers. Neither
name appears in any residential list, and no residential machine has an EMS
Controller. See `spirit-bike-specs-generator-brake-or-induction-brake`.
