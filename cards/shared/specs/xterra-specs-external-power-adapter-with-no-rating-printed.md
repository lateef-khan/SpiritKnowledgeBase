**Sixteen of the thirty machines run from an external adapter, and fifteen of their books never print its
rating.** Only the FS3.0 states one - 9 V, 1000 mA (`fs30-2018-specs-power-adaptor-rated-9-v-1000-ma`). No voltage,
current, polarity or plug size is printed for the machines on this card, so a replacement adapter has to be
matched to the label on the original.

Where the books put the jack, in their own words:

| Machine | What the book says |
|---|---|
| SB150, SB250, SB2.5r, EU150, FS150, FS1.5, FS2.5, FS3.5 | "This product uses an external power supply. The adapter must be plugged into the power jack, located on the front of the unit near the stabilizer tube." Unplug when not in use; warm a cold machine to room temperature first; never use a damaged supply, plug or cord. The SB250 adds that the monitor "will only function while you insert adaptor" |
| SB500, SB4.5r, FS5.8e, FS5.9e | "supplied with an external power supply. When power is connected ... the console will automatically power up"; the FS5.8e and FS5.9e electrical page labels a **power switch** beside the plug-in |
| RSX1500 | the electrical page carries only a "Plug-In" label on its photograph |
| SB4500 | no adapter paragraph; the parts list has a **Power Cord** (71), a **DC Power Cord 500 mm** (73) and a resistance **Motor** (72), and the console boots "after connecting to power" |
| SB600 | "Adapter" is in the carton list; "The adaptor port is located at the rear of the Main Frame", and the serial sticker sits on the rear stabilizer "near the adaptor port". The service manual lists the adaptor (S) and a 600 mm DC wire (A46) |
| SB240 | "Adaptor" is in the carton list as part 82; the book prints nothing else about it |

The adapter feeds the console and the gear motor that sets the resistance
(`xterra-specs-resistance-set-from-the-console-by-a-gear-motor-on-a-magnetic-brake`); the battery consoles on the
other fourteen machines need no adapter. Outlet, grounding and surge rules are on the safety section's electrical
cards. The Sole SB1200 prints its supply as DC 12 V (`sb1200-2023-power-supply`); that figure is not evidence for
any Xterra machine.
