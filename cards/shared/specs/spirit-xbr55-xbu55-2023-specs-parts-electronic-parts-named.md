**Both 2023 books put chapter 2 on one page and label the same four parts.** The page is a picture with no
text layer; the labels were read from a render.

**2.1 Console** - **Cooling Fan** (the vent slot above the level keys) and **Speaker** (the grille at the
foot of the console). No display, fan switch or thumb switch is labelled.

**2.2 Controller and Driver parts** - **Speed Sensor** (a photograph of the reed switch and magnet inside
the shroud) and **Gear Motor** (a photograph of the motor with its cable), called out on a line drawing of
the bike; the XBR55 page draws a recumbent and the XBU55 page an upright.

**"Gear Motor" is this generation's name for what the 2016 books labelled TENSION MOTOR**
(`spirit-xbr-xbu-2016-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`); the same book prints its work voltage as DC
4.5~7.5 V on the Electrical Configurations page. The XBR95 of the same year is the odd one out: its page
labels a Brake Controller and a Hybrid Generator and no gear motor
(`xbr95-2023-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`).

**Nothing on the page is a part number or a rating.**
