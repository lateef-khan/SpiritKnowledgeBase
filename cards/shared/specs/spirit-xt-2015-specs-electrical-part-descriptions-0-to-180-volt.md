- **Safety key** - fits into the console to activate all functions; without it the console cannot be
  controlled.
- **Console** - the interface that controls all functions; key controls and an **LCD** display.
- **Main controller** - the circuit board with the DC power supply for the console, the incline driver and
  the DC motor driver; power supply, motor driver control circuit and incline control circuit.
- **Treadmill motor** - a variable-speed DC motor controlled over **0-180 DC volts (0-90 DC volts on a
  120Vac electronic power system)**. Three wires, red, black and green. DC on the **red (white) wire (M+)**
  turns the motor clockwise; DC on the **black wire (M-)** turns it counter-clockwise; the higher the
  voltage the faster it turns; green is ground.
- **Incline motor** - a **230 volt AC** motor **(115 volts AC on a 120Vac electronic power system)**; four
  wires (red, black, white, green) plus one 3-pin position-sensor cable. AC on **red = UP**, AC on **black
  = DOWN**, **white = COM (neutral)**, **green = ground**.

This is the 220-volt-first wording. The XT185 / XT285 books of the same year say "0-90 (or 0-180)" and
"110 or 230", and the 2023 XT385 / XT485 books go back to that form; the figures are the same two builds
described from opposite ends.
