Five books, one drawing, three labels. It is a wiring-harness picture rather than a schematic: no
connector numbers, no pin numbers, no wire colours.

- The **console** (the 2016 books label it in Chinese, "XR329 電子錶" - the XR329 console) has one
  long **computer cable** down the mast that splits at the bottom into three plugs: one to the
  **tension motor** box, one to the **SPEED SENSOR**, and one to a **DC jack** that mates with the
  lead from the **AC adapter** block.
- Two coiled **hand-pulse leads** run from the console to the grip sensors, and two more from the
  handgrip switches to the seat-side pulse plates on the recumbents.

**The tension-motor box carries a part label that changes:** **317-020004** on all three 2016
books, **D4A05** on both ENT books.

**Titles to know.** The XBR55ENT drawing is headed **ELLIPICAL CIRCUIT DIAGRAM** (sic) under its
XR329-SB013-01 code - a heading error, not a sign of the wrong document; the XBU55ENT one is
headed XBU55 ENT CIRCUIT DIAGRAM and the 2016 books BIKE CIRCUIT DIAGRAM.

The 2023 XBR55/XBU55 books redraw the same harness as a block schematic with pin counts:
`spirit-xbr55-xbu55-2023-specs-circuit-diagram-10-pin-computer-cable`.
