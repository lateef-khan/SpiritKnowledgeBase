**"Replace with only 5A, 250V glass fuse. Fast acting 5.2 x 20 mm."** The 7-series pages print the
diameter with a psi glyph for the diameter sign; the 8-series pages set the two lines as *Replace
with only - 250V, 5A glass fuse / Fast acting - 5.2 x 20 mm*. The figures are the same on all five
books.

**Where it is.** "The input module has an input connector for the line cord, a power switch and a 5
amp fuse" - the module at the front of the machine where the cord plugs in. The no-power
troubleshooting says to "check the fuse in the Input module (located between the power switch and
line cord input)". The service manuals tell a technician to meter it - "the fuse should be short" -
but print no rating of their own.

**The same fuse, the same wording, on the Dyaco MED steppers and the 8.5UE**
(`spirit-climber-specs-fuse-rating`, `85ue-2025-specs-fuse-rating`). The 4.0R and 4.0U have no
fuse, no cord and no input module - they run off a generator. **Do not read the 5 A fuse as the
circuit rating**: the safety pages ask for a 15-amp grounded outlet.
