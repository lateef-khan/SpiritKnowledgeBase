Three Garmin support lists exist. **No single one is complete.**

| List | Date | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `SOLE_GarminWatch_SupportList_20260525.xlsx` | 25 May 2026 | Touchscreen and Companion SDK support. **Newest.** |
| `NEW Garmin HRM_Supported_20250517.xlsx` | 17 May 2025 | Broadcast HR for LED and LCD consoles, plus Samsung, Apple, Polar, Scosche and Sigma. |
| `_OLD_ SOLE Garmin CompanionSDK...xlsx` | no date, marked OLD | Nothing, except the two facts below. |

**Why the 2025 list is still needed.** The 2026 workbook dropped the broadcast sheet and the non-Garmin brands. For a non-touchscreen console, the 17 May 2025 list is the newest one we hold.

**Two facts only the OLD list carries**

1. The first-generation **ENDURO** was Companion SDK supported. The 2026 list names ENDURO 2 and ENDURO 3 only.
2. **INDEX S2** and **VIVOFIT 4** were marked as **not** supported. The newer lists simply leave them out, which is not the same as saying no.

**Renames, not removals.** "TACTIX 7" became "TACTIX 7/7 AMOLED", "MARQ" became "MARQ Series", and "FORERUNNER 955" became "FORERUNNER 955 Series". No watch lost support in those changes.
