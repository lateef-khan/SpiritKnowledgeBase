**The manual prints no weight-stack rating.** This total is the sum of the plate quantities its
assembly hardware table lists, and it is not a figure the manufacturer states anywhere in the book -
say so when you quote it. Why that is, and how it was checked, is at
`spirit-strength-specs-no-weight-stack-rating-printed`.

**Four dual stations, four different plate mixes, the same 200 lb.**

| Machine | Plates printed | Total |
|---|---|---|
| CSD-CPSP | 10 x `15LB weight plate`, 4 x `10LB weight plate`, 1 x `10LB top weight plate` | 200 lb |
| CSD-PFRD | 10 x `15lb weight stack`, 4 x `10lb weight stack`, 1 x `10lb top weight stack` | 200 lb |
| CSD-LPSR | 6 x `15lb weight stack`, 10 x `10lb weight stack`, 1 x `10lb top weight stack` | 200 lb |
| CSD-ACBE | 6 x `15lb selected iron set`, 9 + 1 x `10lb selected iron set`, 1 x `10lb counterweight set` | 200 lb |

**CSD-ACBE names its plates in translated Chinese** - `selected iron set` and `counterweight set` -
so a search for *weight stack* or *weight plate* finds nothing in that book. Its four rows are 47
(counterweight, 1), 48 (10lb, 1), 49 (15lb, 6) and 50 (10lb, 9).

The plate mix changes the step size: CSD-LPSR steps mostly in 10 lb, CSD-CPSP and CSD-PFRD mostly in
15 lb. The manuals do not say which end of the stack each size sits at.

The optional 5 lb increment weight rides on top of the stack on the machines that have one -
`spirit-strength-specs-five-pound-increment-weight`.
