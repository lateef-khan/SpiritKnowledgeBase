**Every selectorized and dual-station machine in the range has one, and the manual never gives its
weight except on the parts-of-your-unit callout**, where fourteen of the twenty-eight books label it
`5lb Increment Weight`.

**What it does:** it sits above the stack and the cable runs through it, so it adds 5 lb to whatever
the selector pin picks up. On a 20 lb stack that turns a 20 lb step into a 10 lb one; on a 15 lb
dual-station stack, a 7.5 lb one. The assembly Note says where it goes in the build order:

> *"Please install the Weight Stacks, Top Weight Stack, incremental weight then the guide rod fixed
> plate (12) in sequence."*
>
> *"The cable has to run through the incremental weight set."*

## Which manuals label it and which do not

| Callout printed | Machines |
|---|---|
| **`5lb Increment Weight`** (14) | CSD-ACBE, CSD-BCTE, CSD-CPSP, CSD-ITOT, CSD-LELC, CSD-LPCE, CSS-ABDO, CSS-BCUR, CSS-LEXT, CSS-PRLC, CSS-SCEX, CSS-SLGC, CSS-SLGP, CSS-TEXT |
| **Not labelled** (14) | CSD-LPSR, CSD-PFRD, CSD-PUDA, CSS-BEXT, CSS-DELT, CSS-GLUT, CSS-LATP, CSS-LATR, CSS-LROW, CSS-SCPR, CSS-SHPR, CSS-SQSC, CSS-SROW, CSS-TROT |

**The fourteen unlabelled machines still have the part.** Their assembly step prints the same
*"incremental weight"* instruction and the same *"the cable has to run through the incremental weight
set"* note - only the picture on page 11 omits the arrow. Checked by rendering all twenty-eight
parts-of-your-unit pages, not by searching the extracted text.

**Nowhere does a manual say the increment weight is removable or optional, and nowhere is it priced
or given a part number.** It is drawn as part of the machine. Do not tell a customer it is an
accessory to buy.

**It is not included in the reconstructed stack totals** on the per-machine stack cards, because the
assembly hardware tables do not list it as a plate.

The i-Strength pair and the benches, racks and ST800 machines have no stack and therefore no
increment weight.
