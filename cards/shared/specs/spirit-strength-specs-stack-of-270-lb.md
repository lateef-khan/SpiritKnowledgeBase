**The manual prints no weight-stack rating.** This total is the sum of the plate quantities its
assembly hardware table lists, and it is not a figure the manufacturer states anywhere in the book -
say so when you quote it. Why that is, and how it was checked, is at
`spirit-strength-specs-no-weight-stack-rating-printed`.

| Machine | Plates printed | Total |
|---|---|---|
| CSS-ABDO | 12 x `20lb weight stack`, 1 x `10lb weight stack-A`, 1 x `10lb weight stack-B`, 1 x `10lb top weight stack` | 270 lb |
| CSS-LROW | 12 x `20lb weight stack`, 2 x `10lb weight stack`, 1 x `10lb top weight stack` | 270 lb |
| CSS-SQSC | 13 x `20lb selection of iron components`, 1 x `10 pounds of counterweight` | 270 lb |

**CSS-ABDO and CSS-LROW hold the identical stack** - CSS-ABDO simply splits its two ten-pound plates
across part numbers `-A` and `-B`.

**CSS-SQSC names its plates in translated Chinese.** `20lb selection of iron components` and
`10 pounds of counterweight` are weight plates and a top plate; a search for *weight stack* or
*weight plate* returns nothing in that book, and its assembly step is split across two pages with the
table on the first and the drawing on the second.

CSS-SQSC also reaches 270 lb with **one fewer piece and no ten-pound step** - thirteen 20 lb plates
under a 10 lb counterweight, against fourteen or fifteen pieces on the other two.

The optional 5 lb increment weight rides on top of the stack on the machines that have one -
`spirit-strength-specs-five-pound-increment-weight`.
