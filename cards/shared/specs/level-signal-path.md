The level signal goes round a short loop:

1. **Console** sends the signal down the **computer cable**.
2. **Gear motor** receives it and moves.
3. **Console** receives the signal back up the **computer cable**.

Only three things are in the loop, so a level fault is the console, the computer cable, or the gear motor.

**This is the level loop, not the incline or speed loop.** Treadmills use a motor controller in the middle; see `sole-incline-signal-path` and `sole-speed-signal-path`.

**Machines without a gear motor are not covered.** Spin bikes that set resistance with a knob or an EMS brake, such as the sb700, sb900 and sb1200, have no level loop of this shape.
