The numbers come from the customer-service model table (`dbo.MODEL` in `CustService`); a manual rarely prints one. A row reading *not confirmed* has no settled number yet. The 2016 row (FP 3/8/2016) is the only XE895 row after the 2014 one; the 2018 owner's manual has no row of its own.
