Only one of the four rower books prints a parts list and exploded view:

- **SR400: yes.** A full P/N parts list (A through J series with quantities) plus an exploded-view drawing whose callouts key to that list: `sole-sr400-specs-parts-list-and-exploded-view`.
- **SR500-2019, SR500-2022, SR550-2023: no.** Zero hits for "exploded", "parts list" or "replacement part" in any of the three texts; their tables of contents run Product Registration, Safety, Electrical, Operation, Assembly, Programs, Maintenance, Warranty — no parts chapter; their supplements are checklists, tool drawings and assembly figures.

What the three books do print is hardware item numbers in parentheses plus a tool list — those live on `sole-rower-specs-assembly-item-numbers-and-tool-lists`, not here. Parts reach the owner through warranty service (return-authorization steps on the rower warranty cards), never through an order form in those books.
