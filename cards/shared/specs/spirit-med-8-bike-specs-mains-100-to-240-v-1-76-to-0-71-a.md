**Power rating - 100 ~ 240 Vac, 1.76 ~ 0.71 Amps, 50/60Hz.** The set-up page of each book says the
bike "has a built-in universal power supply" that plugs into "any A.C. power source from 90 to 240
volts, 50 to 60 Hz", with an input module holding the line-cord socket, a power switch and a 5 amp
fuse - so the set-up page starts the range at 90 V and the specification page at 100 V. Quote the
specification row.

**The 7.0R and 7.0U print 90-240 V** for the same 1.76-0.71 A
(`spirit-med-70-bike-specs-mains-90-to-240-v-1-76-to-0-71-a`); the 8.5S stepper's page prints 100
to 240 on one printing and 90 on another (`spirit-climber-85s-specs-mains-power-supply`). The supply
inside is a Mean Well RPS-120S rated 100-120 / 200-240 VAC, which is where the 100 V floor comes from
(`spirit-med-8-bike-specs-power-supply-module-mean-well-rps-120s-24-v`). The outlet and grounding
rules are on the safety cards.
