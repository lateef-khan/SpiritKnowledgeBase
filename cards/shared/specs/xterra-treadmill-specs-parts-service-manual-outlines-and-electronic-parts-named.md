The six Dyaco-format Xterra service manuals open with two picture chapters. **Chapter 1 Outlines** is a product drawing or photograph; **chapter 2 Electronic Parts** is a *2.1 Upper Controllers* console photograph and a *2.2 Lower Controller and Driver* photograph of the motor compartment. Every label they print is below, read from renders of the pages because the text layer of these books carries labels that are not printed (see the last paragraph).

| Service manual | 1 Outlines | 2.1 Upper Controllers | 2.2 Lower Controller and Driver |
|---|---|---|---|
| TR150 (GT65-NT014), pp. 4-8 | one photograph, no call-outs | **Console** | **Lower Controller Area** - a photograph of the filter, choke, controller board and drive motor under the hood; no part is labelled individually |
| TR260 (GT75A-NT050), pp. 3-7 | a 13-row *No. / Parts Name* table: 1 Console, 2 Locking Knob, 3 Transportation Wheel, 4 Cylinder, 5 Running Belt, 6 Motor Cover, 7 Foot Rail, 8 Incline/Hand Pulse Complex, 9 Speed/Hand Pulse Complex, 10 Rear Adjustment Base (R), 11 Rear Adjustment Base (L), 12 Left Upright, 13 Right Upright | **LCD Display**, **Safety key** | **Speed Sensor** (a photograph of the sensor under the front-roller pulley), **Drive Motor**, **Motor Controller** |
| TRX1400 (T3-NT053-01), pp. 6-11 | two drawings: **Console Assembly**, **Speed/Hand Pulse Complex**, **Incline/Hand Pulse Complex**; then **Motor Controller Board**, **Incline Motor**, **Drive Motor** | **Console** | **Speed Sensor** (at the front-roller pulley), **Lower Controller Area** |
| TRX2500 (GT90B-NT022), pp. 4-9 | two drawings: **Console**, **Speed adjustment button**, **Incline adjustment button**; then **Lower Controller**, **Incline Motor**, **Driving Motor** | the console photograph, unlabelled | **Lower Controller**, **Incline Motor**, **DC Motor**, and an unlabelled inset of the speed sensor at the front-roller pulley |
| TRX3500 / TRX4500 (GT90C-NT023 / GT90D-NT024), pp. 4-10 | two product photographs captioned **GT90C-NT023** and **GT90D-NT024**, no call-outs | two console photographs, the TRX4500 face on p. 8 and the TRX3500 face on p. 9, unlabelled | **DC Motor**, **Incline Motor**, **Lower Controller**, and an unlabelled inset of the speed sensor at the front-roller pulley |
| TRX5500 (GT90D-NT041), pp. 4-9 | eleven call-outs on a photograph: **Console Assembly**, **Speed Rapid Key with Hand pulse Sensor**, **Incline Rapid Key with Hand pulse Sensor**, **Upright(R)**, **Upright(L)**, **Drive Motor Cover**, **Foot Rail**, **Running Belt**, **Adjustment Base Cover(R)**, **Adjustment Base Cover(L)**; then **Drive Motor**, **Incline Motor**, **Controller** | the console photograph, unlabelled | **Speed Sensor** (inset at the front-roller pulley), **Drive Motor**, **Incline Motor**, **Controller** |

**What this tells a technician.** On every one of these machines the speed sensor sits at the front roller, reading a magnet on the roller pulley, under the motor cover; the lower controller (motor controller board) and the drive motor share the motor compartment, with the incline motor between them on the incline machines (TRX1400, TRX2500, TRX3500, TRX4500, TRX5500). The TR150 and TR260 photographs also show a mains **filter and choke** beside the controller, which the TR150 text explains: the 220 V / CEGS version has a filter and choke in the lower-controller area.

**No part number is printed in either chapter of any of the six books**; the only part numbers in these manuals are the display-board stickers photographed in the wiring chapter (`xterra-treadmill-specs-parts-display-board-part-numbers-on-the-pcb-stickers`). No book has a parts list (`xterra-treadmill-specs-parts-which-manuals-print-an-exploded-view-and-parts-list`).

**The text layer of these pages holds labels that are not on the page.** The TRX1400 book carries Chinese captions (電子錶, 速度開關與手握心跳複合組, 揚升馬達, 下控板) under its English ones; the TRX2500 and TRX3500/4500 books carry the words *SPEED SENSOR* over an inset that prints no label. Neither appears on a render, so neither is on this card as printed text. The Spirit CT800 and XT books use the same two chapters (`spirit-ct800-specs-electronic-parts-and-outline`, `spirit-xt-specs-electronic-parts-callouts`).
