**The same list is printed in the 2025 Spirit owner's manual, the 2025 service manual (FU800-SB022-03) and the undated Dyaco "PT" 4.0 U user manual, and the drawing in all three carries the stamp `2019/06/17`.** These are the balloon numbers of that drawing; **no Spirit part number is printed** in any of the three.

**107 rows are printed and the item numbers run 1 to 150** - 62 whole numbers are absent (10, 12, 24, 25, 27, 33, 44, 46, 48, 49, 77, 87, 88, 89, 90, 91, 92, 94, 95, 98, 101, 103, 104, 105, 108, 109, 110, 111, 112, 113, 115, 119, 120, 121, 122, 123, 124, 125, 126, 127, 128, 129, 130, 131, 132, 133, 134, 135, 136, 137, 138, 139, 140, 141, 142, 143, 144, 145, 146, 147, 148, 149), gaps in the numbering rather than a missing page. Three positions carry letters: 3(A), 3(B) and 3(C) are the left and right seat handle bars and the rear folding end assembly, and 16L / 16R the crank arms. **The console is item 34, with fifteen sub-parts 34~01 to 34~15**, the same fifteen names the recumbent numbers 19~01 to 19~15 - two rows both read `Interface Board` (34~05 and 34~11).

## What the list names

- Item 20 `Induction Brake`, 21 `Generator/Brake Controller`, 22 `Magnet`, 23 `Sensor W/Cable` - the service manual photographs the same parts as HYBRID GENERATOR, Driver board and SPEED SENSOR (`cu900ent-electronic-parts-locations`).
- Two hand-pulse leads are given with their connector types: 26 `950m/m_Handpulse Wire (XHP-3)` and 28 `950m/m_Handpulse Wire (XHP-4)`.
- Tools packed as items: 81 `6_L Allen Wrench`, 93 `Phillips Head Screw Driver`, 100 `12/14m/m_Wrench`, 117 `13/15m/m_Wrench`. No separate tool list is printed.
- Seat adjustment: 86 `Fore Aft Adjustment Knob`, 106 `Knob Adjustor` with its own 106~2 screws, 107 `Slider Sleeve`, 114 `Slide Spacer`.

## Where the printings differ

**No row differs in substance.** The service manual writes `mm` where the owner's manual and PT edition write `m/m`, `Ø 5` for `Ø5`, and **`Nylon Nut` where the other two print `Nyloc Nut`** (items 79, 80 and 84) - the same nut under two spellings. The PT edition is the owner's manual list word for word.

**Do not read the 4.0R list for this bike.** The recumbent's console is item 19 and its brake item 55 (`spirit-40r-specs-parts-list-items-1-to-191`).
