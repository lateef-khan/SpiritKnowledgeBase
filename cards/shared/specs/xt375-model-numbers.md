| Year | Model number | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| 2007 | 375807 | xt375-2007 |

The numbers come from the customer-service model table (`dbo.MODEL` in `CustService`); a manual rarely prints one.

The table's first-production date for this row is 20 August 2007, the batch date shared by every 2007 Spirit XE and XT row. The only manual on file is the 2008 XT175 / XT275 / XT375 / XT475 / XT675 dealer service manual, which prints no year.
