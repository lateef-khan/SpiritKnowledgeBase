**The CC81 owner's book (CC81_2019 Ver.C) prints no specification table.** No
dimensions, footprint, product or shipping weight, rated resistance-level
count, step-height figure, maximum-user-weight figure, wiring diagram, or FCC
statement appears in it.

**What the book does state, and where that lives:**

- **Resistance via a Level knob**, with no level count in this book. The 20
  working resistance levels come from the service manual, carded on
  `cc81-2020-model-overview`.
- **110-to-240-volt AC, 5-amp grounded outlet, 40-to-120 °F operating range
  with 95% non-condensing humidity** - electrical requirements, carded under
  safety.
- **Program LEVEL profile charts** are images with no numeric count behind
  them - program procedures, carded under programs.
- **Maintenance menu in the console software** - carded under console.
