**`XS895` and `XE895` are two different machines whose model numbers differ by
one letter.**

| | XS895 | XE895 |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | an **incline stepper** | an **elliptical** |
| Model ids in this repository | `xs895-2018`, `xs895-2021` | `xe895-2018` |
| Its manual calls it | "XS895 INCLINE STEPPER", "your new Incline Stepper" | an elliptical |
| Product line facet | `climber` | `elliptical` |

**Read the second letter before answering anything about an 895.** `S` is the
stepper; `E` is the elliptical.

## What they genuinely share

Both run **20 resistance levels in four bands of five**, described in the same
paragraph, and that one card correctly covers both - see
`spirit-ce850-specs-twenty-resistance-levels`. That is the trap: a search for
"895" and "levels" returns a card that is right for either machine, which makes
it easy to assume every other figure carries across too.

## What they do not share

- The **XE895 has a powered stride** set per segment in its custom programs. The
  XS895 has an **incline** instead, with its own calibration routine started from
  the console. They are different controls on different mechanisms.
- Their assembly, parts and maintenance chapters are unrelated.
- **Neither manual prints a specification table**, so there is no dimension or
  weight figure on either that could be checked against the other - see
  `spirit-climber-specs-no-specification-table`.

**Never carry a part number, a dimension or a procedure between them.** The only
thing established as common is the resistance level scale.

## Both XS895 printings are the same book

The 2018 and 2021 XS895 owner's manuals are 40 pages each, end at the warranty on
printed p. 36 followed by two blank pages and a back cover, and differ in their
warranty effective date - August 22, 2018 against February 19, 2021. **A figure
from one holds for the other**, which is not true of the XE895.
