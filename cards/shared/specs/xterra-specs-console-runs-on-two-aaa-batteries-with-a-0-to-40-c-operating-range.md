Six bike books print the same console spec block under the console drawing:

| Line | Value |
|---|---|
| BATTERY TYPE | **2 AAA or UM 4** (the AIR350 book writes it "(2) Two AAA"; UM 4 is the JIS name for an AAA cell) |
| OPERATING TEMPERATURE | **0 C to +40 C, 32 F to 104 F** |
| STORAGE TEMPERATURE | **-10 C to +60 C, 14 F to 140 F** |

The cells are **not in the carton**: the FB160 service document lists "2- AAA Batteries" among the tools the buyer
must have and says "Console- MUST purchase QTY 2 AAA Energizer batteries", and the AIR350's first assembly step is
"Place 2 AAA batteries into the Console (11)". The odometer "does not reset unless you remove the batteries", and a
faint or blank display is the books' cue to replace them (the errors section holds that table).

The same block also lists the display ranges (TIME 0:00 to 99:59, SPEED to 999.9 km/h or mph on five books and
99.9 on the FB180, PULSE 40 to 240 BPM on all but the AIR350, which has no pulse grips); the console section holds
those.

Different figures on other Xterra machines: the EU100 hybrid takes **two AA** cells, the AIR650 **four AA**, the
MBX2500 **one CR2032**, and the FB150 and FB350 books never print a type. The Spirit and Sole indoor cycles also run
two AAA cells (`spirit-indoor-cycle-maintenance-console-two-aaa-batteries`, `sole-spinner-console-aaa-batteries`).
