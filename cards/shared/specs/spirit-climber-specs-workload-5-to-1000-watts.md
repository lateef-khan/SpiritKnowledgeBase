**5 watts up to 1000 watts.** The 7.0S and 7.5S print it as
`5 watts up to 1000 watts.`; the 8.5S and 8.5S-FIT print it as
`5 watts up to 1000 watts` under a `Workload` heading. Same range, four machines.

**The MS300 stops at 650 watts** and is the only one of the five that does - see
`ms300-2021-specs-workload-5-to-650-watts`. It is otherwise the closest machine
to the 7.0S in the family, so this is exactly the figure a reader will carry
across by mistake.

## This is a rehabilitation-machine figure and no fitness climber prints one

No Spirit stepper, stair climber or vertical climber outside these five states
resistance in watts at all. The CS800, CRS800S, CVC800, XS895, CSC900 and CSC880
give resistance only as a count of console levels - see
`spirit-climber-specs-no-specification-table`. **Do not answer "how many watts"
for one of those machines from this card.**

## There is no level-to-watts table

None of the four books says which console level produces which wattage. The
console *displays* watts during a workout, left and right separately, but the
manual gives only the end points of the range. A customer asking what wattage
level 12 is cannot be answered from the manual.
