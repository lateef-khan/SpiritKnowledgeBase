**The type is not printed.** The feature list says only "User Changeable Batteries (Sensor and Receiver)"; the
assembly step says "Load the transmitter with batteries ... (Replace console battery if no digits are shown on
LCD)"; and the troubleshooting page says "Ensure that the batteries are installed properly in the console and
transmitter. If they are, install fresh batteries." No size, chemistry or count appears anywhere in either book,
in the native text or the OCR of the scanned MB500.

What the books do say about the two units: the console is a **wireless receiver** and the carton "consists of a
computer console and a speed sensor transmitter" that counts a magnet on the crank and sends a coded signal; the
console "is designed as a sealed unit and not meant to be opened other than for the sole purpose of installing
batteries" (opening it otherwise voids the warranty), and it is "NOT waterproof, only water resistant". The
odometer returns to zero when the battery is taken out.

The MBX2500, the next indoor cycle in the range, prints its cell (one 3 V CR2032) and uses a wired sensor instead.
The Spirit indoor cycles print two AAA cells for their transmitter
(`spirit-indoor-cycle-speed-transmitter-two-aaa-batteries`); that figure is not evidence for these two machines.
