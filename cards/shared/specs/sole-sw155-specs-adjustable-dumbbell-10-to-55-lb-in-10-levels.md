**The SW155 dumbbell adjusts from 10 LB to 55 LB (4.55 KG to 25 KG) in 10
levels:**

| LB | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 | 30 | 35 | 40 | 45 | 50 | 55 |
| KG | 4.55 | 6.8 | 9.1 | 11.4 | 13.6 | 15.9 | 18.2 | 20.5 | 22.7 | 25 |

Choose the weight by rotating the middle of the handle grip; the handle
must be fully inserted into the dumbbell tray to adjust.
