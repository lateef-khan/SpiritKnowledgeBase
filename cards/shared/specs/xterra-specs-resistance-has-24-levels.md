**Twenty-four levels.** The SB2.5r, FS2.5, FS3.5 and RSX1500 books say it the same way twice: "LEVEL: The programs
have 24 levels of work displayed in 8 bars in each column ... each bar represents 3 levels of work", and "All program
profiles have 24 levels of resistance." The SB4500's touchscreen book says "press the +/- keys to adjust resistance
(range 1 -24)".

The level is set from the console (UP/DOWN keys, or +/- on the SB4500) and the console drives the brake; these books
name no manual knob. The FS1.5 and SB600 have 16 levels, the EU100, SB120 and UB120 an 8-position knob, and fourteen
other books print no count.
