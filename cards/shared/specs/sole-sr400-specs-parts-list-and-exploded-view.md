The SR400 book is the only rower book in this wave with a parts list (P/N, description, quantity) and an exploded view. The view on PDF page 15 carries letter callouts A through J that key to this list: A main frame assembly, B front stabilizer, C rear stabilizer, D slide track, E saddle, F pedals, G pedal base frame, H console, I fixing pin, J hardware.

Tools named in the assembly figures: a **14mm** wrench (fixing-pin nut) and an **8mm** tool for the console angle; the battery cover is A72. H1 and H4 are both printed "SENSOR WIRE" — carded as printed.

**How this was read.** The stencil OCR confuses E/I with £/&/]/1 (tesseract letter confusion); the series letters above are confirmed against the exploded-view drawing on PDF page 15, which prints A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I and J callouts. Four rows stay partly unclear in OCR and are carded as read: A3's bushing dimensions, A5's screw size, E1 ("LAGGING", possibly LEGGING), and A65 (name and quantity run together).
