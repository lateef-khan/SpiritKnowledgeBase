The book prints no standalone parts list; parts information lives in the assembly pack checklist and the assembly steps, whose parenthesized numbers are item numbers from the assembly drawing ("The numbers in the instructions that are in parenthesis (#) are the item number from the assembly drawing for reference"). All callouts below verified on the render (the OCR confuses Ø with O/©/@ — the diameters here are as printed).

**Hardware steps.** Step 1: 126. Ø10 × 2T Split Washer (10 pcs, printed without a #); #125 Ø3/8" × 23 × 2.0T Curved Washer (4); #119 Ø3/8" × Ø19 × 1.5T Flat Washer (8); #90 3/8" × 3/4" Hex Head Bolt (2); #96 3/8" × 3/4" Socket Head Cap Bolt (8); #93 3/8" × 3-3/4" Hex Head Bolt (2). Step 2: #98 M10 × P1.5 × Ø12 × 20L Bolt (2); #119 Flat Washer (2); #112 M10 × 8T Nylon Nut (2). Step 3: #91 3/8" × 2-1/4" Hex Head Bolt (4); #111 3/8" × UNC16 × 7T Nylon Nut (4); #119 Flat Washer (4); #92 3/8" × UNC16 × 2" Hex Head Bolt (2); #118 Ø10 × Ø25 × 1.5T Flat Washer (2). Step 4: #100 M5 × P0.8 × 10L Phillips Head Screw (8); #102 3.5 × 12mm Sheet Metal Screw (6).

**Assembly tools.** #137 Phillips Head Screw Driver; #138 8mm L Allen Wrench; #140 6mm L Allen Wrench; #136 12/14mm Wrench; #135 13/14mm Wrench; #139 17mm Wrench.

Standing instruction, same as the other lines: "Remove the tools first. Remove the hardware for each step as needed" from the hardware package.
