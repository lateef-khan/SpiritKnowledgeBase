**The SRVO-2021 book prints a full FCC warning (printed p. 10).** The
device complies with Part 15 of the FCC Rules: it may not cause harmful
interference, and it must accept any interference received. Changes not
expressly approved void the user's authority to operate it. The equipment
is a Class B digital device, with limits designed to protect a residential
installation.

If it interferes with radio or television reception, the book gives four
remedies: reorient or relocate the receiving antenna, increase separation,
plug into an outlet on a different circuit, or consult the dealer or an
experienced radio/TV technician.

**The transmitter antenna must stay at least 20 cm from all persons** and
must not be collocated with any other antenna or transmitter.

No other book in this wave prints an FCC page.
