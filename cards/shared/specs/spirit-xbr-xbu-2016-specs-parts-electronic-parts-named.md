**Three books, four parts, the same names.** Two recumbents and one upright of the 2016 residential range
label exactly the same electronic parts on their chapter-2 pages.

**Upper Controllers** - **Cooling FAN** and **DISPLAY**, both on the console.

**Lower Controller and Driver** - **SPEED SENSOR** (a reed switch beside the magnet) and **TENSION MOTOR**
(the gear motor that pulls the steel cable and swings the magnet plate), photographed inside the shroud.

**The tension motor is what sets these three apart from the XBR95 of the same year**, which names a
hybrid generator and a driver board instead (`xbr95-2016-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`). The same
books call the motor a **Tension motor** on the Electrical Configurations page (work voltage DC 4.5~7.5 V)
and **Gear Motor** in the replacement chapter; the 2023 books drop "tension" and label it **Gear Motor**
on this page too (`spirit-xbr55-xbu55-2023-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`). Which residential bikes carry
a gear motor and which a generator is on `spirit-residential-bike-specs-gear-motor-or-generator-brake`.

**No part number or rating is printed on the chapter-2 pages**; the motor's connector pin-out is on the
specs cards for chapter 6.
