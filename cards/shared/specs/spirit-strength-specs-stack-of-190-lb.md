**The manual prints no weight-stack rating.** This total is the sum of the plate quantities its
assembly hardware table lists, and it is not a figure the manufacturer states anywhere in the book -
say so when you quote it. Why that is, and how it was checked, is at
`spirit-strength-specs-no-weight-stack-rating-printed`.

**These two are the lightest selectorized stacks in the range**, and the two manuals describe the
same 190 lb differently.

CSS-TEXT:

| No. | Description | Qty | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 058 | `20lbs weight stack` | 7 | 140 lb |
| 059 | `10lbs weight stack` | 4 | 40 lb |
| 055 | `10lbs top weight stack` | 1 | 10 lb |
| | **Total** | **12** | **190 lb** |

CSS-BCUR:

| No. | Description | Qty | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 068 | `20lb weight stack assembly` | 7 | 140 lb |
| 065 | `10lb top weight assembly` | 5 | 50 lb |
| | **Total** | **12** | **190 lb** |

**CSS-BCUR's row reads "top weight assembly" with a quantity of 5**, which no other manual in the
range does - every other book lists exactly one top weight. The quantity is genuinely 5 on the
printed page. Read against CSS-TEXT, the same twelve pieces are there: one top plate and four
ten-pound plates lumped under one part number. Both machines come to 190 lb either way.

The optional 5 lb increment weight rides on top of the stack on the machines that have one -
`spirit-strength-specs-five-pound-increment-weight`.
