**Not one of the four machines is a touchscreen, and only one of the four books
states a display colour.** None of the four states a screen size.

| Machine | What the manual calls the display |
|---|---|
| **CSC900-2024** stair climber | **"Premium, ultra-bright Blue LED display"**, with a "Large LED Matrix window to track workout progress" and a separate "Resistance level display window" |
| CRS800S-2024 recumbent stepper | "LED Data Display Windows" plus a "Dot Matrix Message Center" - **no colour, no size** |
| CS800-2024 stepper | the same two labels, word for word - **no colour, no size** |
| CRW800-2024 rower | **"Large LED Matrix Window"** on the console drawing - **no colour, no size** |

**Blue is the CSC900's alone.** Do not quote it for a stepper or a rower. It is
the same marketing wording the 2018 CR900 and CU900 bikes used before those
machines went white in 2025 - see `spirit-bike-specs-console-display-type` - so a
search on "ultra-bright blue LED" returns bikes as well as this stair climber.

## The rower's manual calls the same screen both LED and LCD

The CRW800's **console overview drawing** on printed p. 16 labels a
**"Large LED Matrix Window"**. Its **window-functions text** on printed p. 14 and
its program pages call the same screen an **LCD** throughout - "Wake the console
by pressing any button and the LCD will ...", "After 30 seconds of inactivity,
the LCD ...", "the image at the center of LCD will scan every 5 seconds".

**The book contradicts itself and this card does not resolve it.** Say the
manual uses both words for one screen rather than picking one. A customer telling
you their rower has an LCD is quoting their manual correctly, and so is one who
says LED.

The CR800-2024 bike book has the same slip in one line of its troubleshooting
table - "LED displays not bright, incomplete or imperfect. 1. LCD displays are
broken." - so it is a house habit, not a fault report.

## What the two stepper books add around the display

Both the CRS800S and the CS800 console pages label, besides the two windows: a
**Scan Button for LED Windows**, a **Scan Button for Dot Matrix**, Up, Down and
Program controls, a **Fan**, a **USB Charger**, and Start, Stop and Level
controls. The console chapter adds that there is "a Strides Per Minute window for
pedal speed and a Level window indicating machine resistance".

**That call-out drawing is a `console` fact, not a specification.** This card
answers only what kind of panel each machine has.

## What none of the four manuals gives

**No screen resolution, no brightness figure, no diagonal measurement and no
operating system.** Where a book names iOS or Android it is describing the
customer's phone for the app, never the console. For anything past the words
above, go to Spirit Fitness - see `spirit-climber-specs-no-specification-table`
and `spirit-crw800-specs-no-specification-table`.
