**Two of the seventeen machines are touchscreens; the rest are LED or LCD panels
with a dot matrix. Three books state a display colour and no book anywhere states
a screen size.**

| Machine | What the manual calls the display |
|---|---|
| **CSC900-2019, -2021 and -2024** stair climber | **"Premium, ultra-bright Blue LED display"**, with a "Large LED Matrix window to track workout progress" and a separate "Resistance level display window" |
| **CSC880-2025** stair climber | **"Premium, ultra-bright White LED display"** - the same sentence with the colour changed - plus the same "Large LED Matrix window" and "Resistance level display window" |
| CRS800S-2021 and -2024 recumbent stepper | "LED Data Display Window" plus a "Dot Matrix Message Center" - **no colour, no size** |
| CS800-2021 and -2024 stepper | the same two labels, word for word - **no colour, no size** |
| **CVC800-2021** vertical climber | **"LCD Data Display Window"** - the only climber console the books call an LCD on its own drawing |
| XS895-2018 and -2021 incline stepper | a **"Dot Matrix Center Display"** of "Twenty columns of boxes (10 high)" - **no colour, no size, and never called LED or LCD** |
| MS300-2021, 7.0S-2025, 7.5S-2025 rehabilitation stepper | a **"Dot matrix display"** with a heart-rate bar graph beside it - **no colour, no size** |
| **8.5S-2025** rehabilitation stepper | a **"Touch Screen Display"**: "All information required for operation is explained on screen" |
| **8.5S-FIT-2026** rehabilitation stepper | a **touchscreen** - the manual has a whole `Touchscreen Operation` chapter and a Home page reached by pressing "Hello Guest" ten times |
| CRW800-2024 rower | **"Large LED Matrix Window"** on the console drawing - **no colour, no size** |

**The colour word is the tell between the two stair climbers.** The CSC900 is
blue in 2019, 2021 and 2024; the **CSC880 of 2025 is white**. Nothing else in the
sentence changes, so a customer quoting "premium, ultra-bright ... LED display"
has told you which machine they have as soon as they read the colour. Do not
quote either colour for a stepper, a climber or a rower.

That wording is also the same marketing line the 2018 CR900 and CU900 bikes used
before those machines went white in 2025 - see
`spirit-bike-specs-console-display-type` - so a search on "ultra-bright blue LED"
returns bikes as well as this stair climber.

**The CVC800 is the odd one among the LED machines.** Its own console drawing
labels an **LCD** Data Display Window, and the power-up text repeats "the LCD Data
Display Window will show the software version". The CS800 and CRS800S drawings of
the same generation say LED in that place. **Do not correct the CVC800's manual to
LED**; it says LCD twice, consistently.

**Two machines really are touchscreens.** If a customer describes tapping the
screen, they have an 8.5S or an 8.5S-FIT and none of the other cards in this
family apply to their console.

## The rower's manual calls the same screen both LED and LCD

The CRW800's **console overview drawing** on printed p. 16 labels a
**"Large LED Matrix Window"**. Its **window-functions text** on printed p. 14 and
its program pages call the same screen an **LCD** throughout - "Wake the console
by pressing any button and the LCD will ...", "After 30 seconds of inactivity,
the LCD ...", "the image at the center of LCD will scan every 5 seconds".

**The book contradicts itself and this card does not resolve it.** Say the
manual uses both words for one screen rather than picking one. A customer telling
you their rower has an LCD is quoting their manual correctly, and so is one who
says LED.

The CR800-2024 bike book has the same slip in one line of its troubleshooting
table - "LED displays not bright, incomplete or imperfect. 1. LCD displays are
broken." - so it is a house habit, not a fault report.

## The rehabilitation steppers state a Readouts list instead of a display type

The MS300, 7.0S and 7.5S specification pages print a **Readouts** row - "Time and
Segment time remaining, RPM, Watts (Left and Right), METS, Symmetry Index, Heart
Rate, Calories, Work Level" - which says what the panel can show and not what kind
of panel it is. The 8.5S and 8.5S-FIT specification pages have no Readouts row at
all. See `spirit-climber-specs-rehabilitation-stepper-specification-page`.

## What the two stepper books add around the display

Both the CRS800S and the CS800 console pages label, besides the two windows: a
**Scan Button for LED Windows**, a **Scan Button for Dot Matrix**, Up, Down and
Program controls, a **Fan**, a **USB Charger**, and Start, Stop and Level
controls. The console chapter adds that there is "a Strides Per Minute window for
pedal speed and a Level window indicating machine resistance".

**That call-out drawing is a `console` fact, not a specification.** This card
answers only what kind of panel each machine has. **The CSC880-2025's full console
face - every key in the labelled row - is answered by
`csc880-2025-console-white-led-face-with-a-fan-target-and-user-prog-key-row` in the
`console` section.** Send a customer asking "what are the buttons on my stair
climber" there, and use this card only for "what kind of screen is it".

## What none of the four manuals gives

**No screen resolution, no brightness figure, no diagonal measurement and no
operating system - on any of the seventeen, touchscreens included.** Neither the
8.5S nor the 8.5S-FIT gives a screen size, and their specification pages have no
display row. Where a book names iOS or Android it is describing the customer's
phone for the app, never the console. For anything past the words above, go to
Spirit Fitness - see `spirit-climber-specs-no-specification-table`,
`spirit-climber-specs-rehabilitation-stepper-specification-page` and
`spirit-crw800-specs-no-specification-table`.
