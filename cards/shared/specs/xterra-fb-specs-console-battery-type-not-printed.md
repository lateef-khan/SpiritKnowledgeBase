**The type is not printed.** Both folding-bike books say the console has batteries - "If the display is faint or
shows no figures, please replace the batteries" - and nothing more: no count, no size, no spec block, and no parts
list to name a cell. The console turns on when pedalling starts or a key is pressed and shuts off after 4 minutes
without a signal.

The sister books that do print a type all say **two AAA (UM 4)** - the FB160 and FB360 of 2019 and the FB180 of
2025, on the same console layout
(`xterra-specs-console-runs-on-two-aaa-batteries-with-a-0-to-40-c-operating-range`). That is the likely answer, but
it is not evidence for these two machines.
