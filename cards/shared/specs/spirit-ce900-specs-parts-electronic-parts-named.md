**Both CE900 books print the same five names.**

**Upper Controllers (p. 8)** - **Cooling FAN** and **DISPLAY**, both in the console head. Neither book
labels a thumb switch or speaker on this page.

**Lower Controller and Driver (p. 9)** - **HYBRID GENERATOR** (the flywheel with its coil pack, the part
the Electrical Configurations page calls the GENERATOR BRAKE in the CE900 book and the EMS FLYWHEEL in the
CE900ENT book), **SPEED SENSOR** and **Driver board**.

**These are the five names the CU900, CU900ENT, CR900 and CR900ENT bike books print**
(`cu900ent-electronic-parts-locations`) - one Dyaco template across the SE8800 and SU/SR8800 machines. The
CE1000ENT of 2023 keeps the three lower parts, drops the fan callout and adds an NFC key
(`ce1000ent-2023-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`); the CE800 books name a thumb switch and a
controller instead (`spirit-ce800-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`).

**No part number is printed on these pages, and none anywhere in either book.** The numbered parts the
two books do print are the 33 cover call-outs of their Component Description table
(`spirit-ce900-specs-parts-component-description-33-rows`); the only tool they list is a multi-meter
(`spirit-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`).
