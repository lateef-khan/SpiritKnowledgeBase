| 2020 | 150314 | sb500-2020 |

The numbers come from the customer-service model table (`dbo.MODEL` in `CustService`) and, where the owner's manual prints one on its cover, from the manual itself; a row reading *not confirmed* has no settled number yet. Two printings of one book: the scan filed as SB500_OM_150314 ((c)2014, stamp 20140811, warranty effective August 1, 2014) and the 2020 export (SB500_20200706, (c)2018, warranty effective July 6, 2020) are 98.5% identical word for word, so one id, named by the newer printing, covers both. The table's "XTERRA SB500" row is 150314 (first production 1 August 2014).
