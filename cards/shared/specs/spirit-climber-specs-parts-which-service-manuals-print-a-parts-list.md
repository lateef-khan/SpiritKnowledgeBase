**Eleven Spirit stepper and climber service manuals were read to their last page. Three print an
exploded view and a parts list: the 7.5S medical stepper book of 2021 and the 2025 revisions of the
7.0S and 7.5S books.** One prints a numbered
component key and a wiring diagram with part numbers; seven print nothing that resembles
either.

| Book | Exploded view | Parts list | Card |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7.5S (RS9600-SS021-01) | yes - one drawing dated 2018.11.29, PDF p. 46 | **yes - 257 rows, items 1-263**, PDF pp. 47-54 | `7-5s-med-specs-parts-list` |
| 7.5S (RS9600-SS021-03, 2025) | yes - a new drawing stamped 20251208 whose balloons run past 330, PDF p. 47 | **the same 257 rows, items 1-263**, PDF pp. 48-55 - it does not reach the new drawing's numbers | `7-5s-med-specs-parts-list` |

**The 7.5S list is the same list its owner's manual prints**, on printed pp. 60-67 of that
book; the service manual's copy is the one with a clean text layer on every page.

**The bracketed numbers inside the replacement steps of the CS800 and CRS800S books are not from
these books.** "Ø 17 C-ring", "M5 x 10mm Phillips Head Screw" and the like are sizes, not
items; where a step does cite an item number, it is the owner's manual's exploded-view number
for the same machine - the CS800-2021 and CRS800S-2021 owner's manuals print lists to items
160 and 247 (`spirit-climber-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`). **The XS895 and the
CSC900 have no list in any book, owner's or service**, so a number in their steps points at a
drawing that exists nowhere.

**The 8.5S wiring diagram's `#` numbers are part numbers of a kind** - `#105, Lower Control
Board`, `#097, Power Supply Module`, `#092, AC Power Entry Module with Switch and Fuse`,
`#087, Data Transfer Board`, `#106 IR Reflective Sensor Board`, `#086, Console`, `#085, Brake
and Angle Sensor`, and cables `#088` to `#104` - but the book prints no list to go with them,
and its own text disagrees with the diagram once: the circuit-board page says "Power Supply
#081 provides 24 VDC" where the diagram labels the same module `#097`. The 8.5S owner's manual's
exploded view carries balloons in the 500s and does not use these numbers.

**A callout number is never a Spirit part number.** The only Spirit part numbers any of these
nine books print are the two optical-sensor board numbers in the 7.5S list, CS63008-00 and
CS63008-10.

**The two 2025 service manuals print 2021 lists under 2025 drawings.** The RS9500-SS021-02 (7.0S) and RS9600-SS021-03 (7.5S) books both carry a freshly stamped exploded view with balloons above 330 and a list that stops at item 263 - the lists of the 2021 MED owner's manuals. A number above 263 on either drawing is decoded only by the 2025 owner's manuals' 337-row lists (`spirit-climber-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`).
