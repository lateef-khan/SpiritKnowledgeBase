**None of the seventeen Spirit residential owner's manuals contains a specification
table.** All seventeen were checked. No table of contents has a Specifications
entry, and none of these figures is printed anywhere in any of them:

- **stride length** - no book states one, in any unit. The word *stride* appears in
  exactly one of the seventeen, the XE895-2018, and there it names a **powered
  stride adjustment**, never a length; see the XE895 section below
- **machine weight** or shipping weight
- **flywheel weight**, flywheel diameter, or a gear ratio
- assembled dimensions, floor footprint, carton size or required operating area
- pedal spacing, Q factor or step-up height
- a resistance rating in watts, newtons or kilograms

Get these figures from Spirit Fitness or the product spec sheet, and say the
owner's manual does not state them. **Do not carry a figure across from one
generation to another, from a commercial CE, or in from a Sole elliptical.**

**Six of the seventeen were found late.** The XE395-2018, XE795-2018, XE895-2018,
XE195-2019, XE295-2019 and XG400-2019 owner's manuals were filed under Bikes and
missed by the first elliptical sweep. Each was re-checked the same way, from the PDF
rather than from the extracted text alone: `SPECIFICATION` occurs twice in each book
and both are the operating-temperature sentence and the "subject to change without
notice" boilerplate; `DIMENSION`, `PARTS LIST`, `EXPLODED` and `WEIGHT LIMIT` occur
zero times in all six; `lbs` occurs once across all six and it is the console's
default body weight of 150 lbs at program entry, not a machine weight. **Do not
re-run this check.**

**Watts on the console is not a resistance rating.** Every one of these books
describes a Watts readout - "if 100 watts is displayed, you are doing enough work
to keep a 100-watt light bulb lit" - and none prints a level-to-watts table or a
maximum wattage.

## The XE895-2018 is the one book that says *stride*, and it still gives no length

The XE895-2018 owner's manual is the only one of the seventeen in which the word
*stride* appears at all - fifteen times. **Every one of them is the powered stride
adjustment, not a length.** It names a STRIDE wire in the assembly wiring, asks for
a stride level per segment when you build a custom program, prints a Stride Profile
picture beside the Work Profile for each preset program, and gives a Stride
Calibration routine.

**It states no stride length in inches or centimetres, no shortest and longest
setting, and no count of stride levels.** Do not answer an XE895 stride length with
the CE850's 18-to-24-inch figure: that is a commercial machine with a different
mechanism, and its manual is not evidence about this one. See
`xe895-2018-specs-powered-stride-set-per-segment-with-no-length-printed`.

## The XG400 is not an elliptical

Its cover calls it the **XG400 E-Glide Trainer** and its own manual never uses the
word elliptical for it. Both printings are the same in this respect - *elliptical*
occurs zero times in the XG400-2019 book, which calls the machine the E-Glide
Trainer throughout. It shares the console text of the elliptical books, so its
resistance count is carded with theirs, but nothing in either manual gives it a
stride length either.

## Four printings of the XE795, and only one carries the weight limit

The 2018, February 2021, November 2021 and 2023 XE795 printings are the same
machine. **Only the November 2021 printing carries "Recommended Weight Limit: 400
lb" on its safety page**; the 2018 and February 2021 printings have no weight limit
line anywhere. None of the four adds a specification table, a dimension or a weight
for the machine itself.

## The console strip on the XE395-2023 is a picture

The XE395-2023 console overview page has no text layer and its direct-select
button strip - a printed row of LEVEL numbers and a row of INCLINE numbers - is
legible only as OCR. **The digits on that strip are not readable well enough to
quote as an incline range,** and no sentence anywhere in that manual states one.
Do not answer an XE395-2023 incline range with the XE395-2010 figure; the console
is a different unit.
