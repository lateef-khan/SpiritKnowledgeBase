**None of the eleven Spirit residential owner's manuals contains a specification
table.** All eleven were checked. No table of contents has a Specifications entry,
and none of these figures is printed anywhere in any of them:

- **stride length** - the word *stride* does not appear once in any of the eleven
  books, in any unit
- **machine weight** or shipping weight
- **flywheel weight**, flywheel diameter, or a gear ratio
- assembled dimensions, floor footprint, carton size or required operating area
- pedal spacing, Q factor or step-up height
- a resistance rating in watts, newtons or kilograms

Get these figures from Spirit Fitness or the product spec sheet, and say the
owner's manual does not state them. **Do not carry a figure across from one
generation to another, from a commercial CE, or in from a Sole elliptical.**

**Watts on the console is not a resistance rating.** Every one of these books
describes a Watts readout - "if 100 watts is displayed, you are doing enough work
to keep a 100-watt light bulb lit" - and none prints a level-to-watts table or a
maximum wattage.

## What the eleven books do print, and where each fact lives

| Figure | Where it is printed | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Resistance levels | 20 on the XE195, XE295, XG400, XE395-2010, XE395ENT-2021 and XE395-2023; 40 on both XE795 printings and the XE795-2023; **not stated at all in the XE100/XE200/XE300 or XE150/XE350/XE550 books** | specs |
| Power incline range | **XE395-2010 only** - 40 levels in half-level steps | specs |
| Which machines have a power incline | the three XE395 machines only | specs |
| User weight limit | 350 lb XE195; 400 lb XE295, XG400, XE795 November printing, XE395-2023 and XE795-2023; **no figure at all** in the XE100/XE200/XE300, XE150/XE350/XE550, XE395-2010, XE395ENT-2021 or the XE795 February printing | safety |
| Supply voltage and circuit | a 15-amp grounded outlet in seven books, written as 110-volt in some sentences and 115-volt in others **inside the same book**; **no supply figure at all** in the XE100/XE200/XE300, XE150/XE350/XE550 or either 2023 book | safety |
| Operating temperature / humidity | "40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit, humidity 95% non-condensing" in seven books; the XE395-2023 prints "40 degrees C" instead; **none** in the XE795-2023, XE100/XE200/XE300 or XE150/XE350/XE550 | safety |
| Certifications | **the two 2023 books only** - FCC part 15, UL, Bluetooth (with FTMS) | safety |
| Foot pedal angle positions | XE100/XE200/XE300 and the XE350/XE550; **the XE150 has no adjustable foot pedal** | maintenance |
| Exploded view and parts list | the XE395ENT-2021, XE395-2023 and XE795-2023 books | assembly |

## The XG400 is not an elliptical

Its cover calls it the **XG400 E-Glide Trainer** and its own manual never uses the
word elliptical for it. It shares the console text of the 2021 elliptical books, so
its resistance count is carded with theirs, but nothing in its manual gives it a
stride length either.

## Two printings of the XE795 2021, and only one carries the weight limit

The February and November 2021 XE795 printings are the same machine. The November
printing adds "Recommended Weight Limit: 400 lb" to the safety page; the February
printing has no weight limit line anywhere. Neither adds a specification table, a
dimension or a weight for the machine itself.

## The console strip on the XE395-2023 is a picture

The XE395-2023 console overview page has no text layer and its direct-select
button strip - a printed row of LEVEL numbers and a row of INCLINE numbers - is
legible only as OCR. **The digits on that strip are not readable well enough to
quote as an incline range,** and no sentence anywhere in that manual states one.
Do not answer an XE395-2023 incline range with the XE395-2010 figure; the console
is a different unit.
