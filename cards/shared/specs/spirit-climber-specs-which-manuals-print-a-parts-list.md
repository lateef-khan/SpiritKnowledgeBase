**The Spirit rowers are counted separately** - three of six print a list -
because `product_line` is a single value and one card cannot span two lines. See
`spirit-rower-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`. The **8.5UE upper body
ergometer** is a third line again and prints two drawings with no list -
`85ue-2025-specs-exploded-view-with-no-parts-list`, which makes **four** Spirit
machines whose books show a diagram they give you no way to read.

## A drawing with no list is not a parts list

The 8.5S, the 8.5S-FIT and the CSC880 each print an exploded-view drawing whose
balloons carry item numbers, and then print nothing that says what any number
means. **A number read off one of those drawings cannot be turned into a part.**
The CSC880's drawing is the worst of the three - see
`csc880-2025-specs-exploded-view-with-no-parts-list`. Go to Spirit Fitness for
the part, and say the owner's manual prints a diagram but no list.

The three drawings are dated in their own corners: the 8.5S pages carry
`2025.11.05` and `2025/06/11`, and the 8.5S-FIT carries `20260413`. Those are
drawing revision dates, not the manual's date.

## The CSC900 prints neither, in every year it was printed

Its 2019, 2021 and 2024 tables of contents all end at maintenance, warranty and
nothing else. A search of all three whole documents - native text and the OCR
supplement of every page - returns no `Main Frame ... 1` row, no *nylon nut* row,
no *hex head bolt* row and no item-number column anywhere. **The only part
numbers a CSC900 book gives are the step-by-step hardware callouts inside its
assembly chapter.**

The XS895 is the same: both printings end at the warranty on printed p. 36,
followed by two blank pages and the back cover.

## The 2021 lists carry a factory document code and the 2024 lists do not

The 2021 books head their first parts page with the factory's own drawing code:

- CRS800S-2021: **`CRS800S-RS8100A-SS004-01 Part List_SPIRIT(SAP)`**
- CS800-2021: **`CS800-XS200B-SS005-01 Part List_SPIRIT(SAP)`**
- CVC800-2021: **`CVC800 Parts List`** - the model name only, no drawing code

That `... Part List_SPIRIT(SAP)` header is the same form the 2021-2023 commercial
bike manuals carry - see `spirit-bike-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.
**The 2024 replacements dropped it**: every parts page in the CRS800S-2024 and
CS800-2024 is headed simply `PARTS LIST`, as are the 7.0S and 7.5S pages of 2025.
Both 2021 lists end with bare drawing codes under the last row, which are not
items: **`200304`** on the CS800, and **`200224`** followed by
**`200304(AM1090301/R10903004)`** on the CRS800S.

**Count the CRS800S-2021 list to printed p. 44, not p. 43.** Its fourth page
carries only eleven rows - items 222, 223 and 241 to 247 - and it is easy to stop
one page early and report the highest item as 221 when it is **247**, a
`Transformer Power Cord (220V,Optional)`. The CS800-2021 list has the same shape:
its highest item is **160**, a `Foot Pad Adjusting Plate` in the right-hand
column, not the 102 the left-hand column ends on. **Read both columns of the last
page.**

**Read the CS800-2021 code from the native text layer, not from the OCR.** The
`=== OCR SUPPLEMENT ===` block of that page reads it as
`CS800-X5200B-55005-01 Part List_SPIRITISAP]`, turning S into 5 three times and
the brackets into square ones. The page has a real text layer; the OCR of it is
wrong.

## The exploded-view page itself is untitled in the 2021 books

The CRS800S-2021, CS800-2021 and CVC800-2021 tables of contents all promise
`EXPLODED VIEW`, and the page they point at carries **no heading at all** - only
the drawing, the printed page number and the `Spirit Fitness` footer. Do not
conclude the page is missing because a search for the words "exploded view"
returns only the table of contents. In those three books it always does.

## Do not read one climber's list for another

The CRS800S is a recumbent stepper, the CS800 a standing stepper and the CVC800 a
vertical climber; the MS300, 7.0S, 7.5S and 8.5S are rehabilitation machines on a
different platform again. Their item numbers overlap heavily and mean different
parts, so a number read from the wrong book is almost certainly the wrong part.
**No list in this table answers for a CSC900 or a CSC880**, whose stairs, chain
and generator brake appear in none of them.
