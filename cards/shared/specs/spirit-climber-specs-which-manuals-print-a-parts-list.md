**Two of the three print both an exploded view and a numbered parts list. The
CSC900 stair climber prints neither.**

| Manual | Exploded view | Parts list | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| CRS800S-2024 recumbent stepper | printed p. 37 (PDF p. 39) | printed pp. 38-41 (PDF pp. 40-43) | 239 |
| CS800-2024 stepper | printed p. 38 (PDF p. 40) | printed pp. 39-40 (PDF pp. 41-42) | 139 |
| **CSC900-2024 stair climber** | **none** | **none** | - |

The CSC900 table of contents ends at Machine Care 32, Troubleshooting 34,
Warranty 36, and a search of the whole document - native text and the OCR
supplement of every page - returns no `Main Frame ... 1` row, no *nylon nut* row,
no *hex head bolt* row and no item-number column anywhere. **Its 44 pages carry
no whole-machine parts table of any kind.**

**The only part numbers the CSC900 book gives are the step-by-step hardware
callouts inside its assembly chapter**, which cover fasteners for assembly and
nothing else. For anything past that, go to Spirit Fitness, and say the owner's
manual does not print a parts list.

## Do not read one climber's list for another

The CRS800S is a recumbent stepper and the CS800 a standing stepper. Their lists
share 118 item numbers and agree on **two** of them, so a number read from the
wrong book is almost certainly the wrong part. The recumbent carries a seat
carriage, an aluminium seat track, eight seat track wheels, a locking gas
cylinder and a rotate disk that the standing stepper has no equivalent of, and
its list runs to 239 rows against the CS800's 139.

**Neither list answers for the CSC900**, whose stairs, chain and generator brake
appear in neither.

## Neither book prints a document code above the list

Every parts page in both books is headed simply `PARTS LIST`, including the
continuation pages. **There is no `... Part List_SPIRIT` header line** of the kind
the 2021-2023 commercial bike manuals carry - see
`spirit-bike-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list` - and no factory drawing
code on either exploded-view page.

The four **entertainment-console** manuals of the same 2024 range head their
continuation pages `PARTS LIST - CONTINUED` instead. That wording is a reliable
tell that you are looking at an ENT book, not a stepper one.
