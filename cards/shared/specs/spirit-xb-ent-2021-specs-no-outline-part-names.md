**Neither ENT book labels a single part.** The chapter is a title page followed by one colour
photograph of the assembled bike - recumbent in the XBR55ENT book, upright in the XBU55ENT
book - with no leader lines, numbers or names. The page extracts as a bare page number, and a
150 dpi render confirms there is nothing to read. There is no skeleton drawing either.

For part names on these frames use the nearest labelled books: the 2023 XBR55 and XBU55
outlines (`xbr55-2023-specs-outline-numbered-part-names`,
`xbu55-2023-specs-outline-numbered-part-names`) and the 2016 skeletons
(`spirit-xbr-2016-specs-outline-and-skeleton-part-names`) - but the ENT console, its mast and the
fan are not on any of them.

The replacement chapters of the ENT books do name parts, with item numbers, procedure by
procedure; those live in the assembly section.
