**120 ~ 130 HZ**, adjusted with a "13 T-type sleeve" on the belt tensioner after the motor is fitted and
the belt hooked back.

This is the belt between the drive motor and the front roller, not the tread belt. Both books print the
figure twice, in the motor-replacement step and again in the roller-replacement step, word for word:
"use 13 T-type sleeve, adjust the required belt tension, the value can be adjusted to 120 ~ 130HZ".

It is a **frequency read on a sonic tension meter**, the unit the CVC800 and CE800ENT books also use.
The CT800, CT850, 40T and XT books give a **force of 70 to 75 lbs on a mechanical gauge** for the same
belt, the CT1000ENT gives "80-90BLS", and the CTSBS900 gives a belt sag in millimetres. None of those
converts into this figure; use the book for the machine in hand.
