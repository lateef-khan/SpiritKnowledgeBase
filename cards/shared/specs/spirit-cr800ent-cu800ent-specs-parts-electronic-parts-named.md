**Both ENT books name the same eight parts, word for word, on two pages.**

**Upper Controllers (p. 7)** - **Display**, **Cooling Fan**, **Thumb Switch** and **Earphone Port** on the
touchscreen console, and **Induction Break** - printed with that spelling - called out on the whole-bike
drawing inside the front shroud. It is the induction brake; the same book spells it **Induction Brake** on
its flywheel-definition page (p. 23) and **INDUCTION BRAKE** on its block diagram (p. 16). The brake is drawn
under "Upper Controllers" because the page draws the entire machine and labels the console parts and the
brake on it together.

**Lower Controller and Driver (p. 8)** - **Controller** and **Speed RPM Sensor** inside the shroud, and
**PCB & Power Converter** - the mains-side board pair the wiring chapter later calls the POWER BRIDGE PCB
board and the POWER CONVERTER.

**This is the induction-brake, mains-powered set.** The LED CR800 (2020) and CU800 (2020) books of the same
template name a **Generator Brake** and no converter, earphone port or power board
(`spirit-cr800-cu800-2020-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`); which bikes carry which resistance unit is
on `spirit-bike-specs-generator-brake-or-induction-brake`.

**Neither page prints a part number or a rating.** What the converter and the bridge board do, with their
connector labels, is on the specs cards for chapter 6.

The treadmill ENT books of the same year name display, earphone port, converter, incline motor and power
bridge board under the same headings (`spirit-ct-ent-specs-electronic-parts-and-outline`).
