**This is the touchscreen list, dated 25 May 2026.** A non-touchscreen console uses a different and shorter list. See `sole-garmin-supported-watches-broadcast`.

55 Garmin devices are on the list. A **†** marks a device that has Companion SDK support only, with no customizable data logging: FORERUNNER 45, HRM 200, HRM 600, INSTINCT, INSTINCT SOLAR and LILY. Every other device on the list has both.

- **D2** — D2 Air X10
- **ENDURO** — ENDURO 2, ENDURO 3
- **EPIX** — EPIX (GEN 2), EPIX PRO (GEN 2)
- **FENIX** — FENIX 6, FENIX 7, FENIX 7 PRO, FENIX 8, FENIX 8 SOLAR, FENIX E
- **FORERUNNER** — FORERUNNER 45 †, FORERUNNER 55, FORERUNNER 165, FORERUNNER 245, FORERUNNER 255, FORERUNNER 265, FORERUNNER 570, FORERUNNER 745, FORERUNNER 945, FORERUNNER 945 LTE, FORERUNNER 955, FORERUNNER 965, FORERUNNER 970
- **HRM** — HRM 200 †, HRM 600 †
- **INSTINCT** — INSTINCT †, INSTINCT SOLAR †, INSTINCT 2, INSTINCT 2 APAC, INSTINCT CROSSOVER, INSTINCT 3 AMOLED, INSTINCT 3 SOLAR, INSTINCT E
- **LILY** — LILY †
- **MARQ** — MARQ, MARQ (Gen 2)
- **TACTIX** — TACTIX DELTA, TACTIX 7/7 AMOLED, TACTIX 8 AMOLED, TACTIX 8 SOLAR
- **VENU** — VENU, VENU 2 / 2 PLUS, VENU 3, VENU SQ, VENU SQ 2, VENU X1
- **VIVOACTIVE** — VIVOACTIVE 4, VIVOACTIVE 5, VIVOACTIVE 6
- **VIVOMOVE** — VIVOMOVE 3 / STYLE, VIVOMOVE SPORT, VIVOMOVE TREND
- **VIVOSMART** — VIVOSMART 4, VIVOSMART 5

**This list changes.** An older list also named the first-generation **ENDURO**, which the 25 May 2026 list does not. See `sole-garmin-support-list-versions` before telling a customer a watch is unsupported.
