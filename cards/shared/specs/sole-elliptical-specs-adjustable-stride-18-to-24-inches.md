**The E95s stride adjusts from 18 inches at the lowest setting to 24 inches at
the top setting.** At 18 inches the book recommends slow pedaling or quick
bursts at very high resistance; 24 inches suits higher-speed pedaling. Any
comfortable length in between can be selected.

**Control is by buttons on the console or the left swing arm**, and the stride
can also change automatically inside the built-in workout programs. Which
programs move it is a programs question; stride calibration is a console
procedure.

**No other legacy Sole elliptical book prints a stride length.** Fixed-stride
models (E20, E25, E35, E55, E95, E98 and all 2007/2008/2009/2011/2012-2015
books) state no inch figure anywhere; see
`sole-elliptical-specs-no-specification-table`. The Spirit commercial twin
prints the same 18-to-24 range
(`ce850-2020-console-stride-length-18-to-24-inches-buttons-on-the-left-swing-arm`).
