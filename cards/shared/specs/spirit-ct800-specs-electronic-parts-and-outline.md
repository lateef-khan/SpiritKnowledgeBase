**CT800-2016.** The Outlines drawing labels only **Console** and **Hand Pulse Sensor**. Upper controllers
(p. 8): **Cooling FAN**, **Safety key**, **DISPLAY**. Lower controller and driver (p. 9): **INCLINE
MOTOR**, **SPEED SENSOR**, **DC MOTOR**, **DRIVER BOARD**, **TRANSFORMER** - the transformer is a separate
part under the motor hood, wired to the driver board's JK5 / JK6 / JK7.

**CT800-2020.** The Outlines pages are product photographs with no call-outs. Upper controllers (p. 7):
**Cooling Fan**, **Safe Key**, **Display**. Lower controller and driver (p. 8): **Incline motor**, **DC
motor**, **DRIVER BOARD**. There is no transformer and no separate speed-sensor call-out.

Neither chapter carries a dimension, a machine weight, a maximum user weight or a power rating, and no
such figure appears elsewhere in either book.
