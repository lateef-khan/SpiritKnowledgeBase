**180 ±10 Hz**, "measured by Sonic Tension Meter", set after the drive pulley and belt are back on
and the belt is confirmed to sit in its groove in a straight line between the drive pulley and
the flywheel. Both upright books print this in the drive-pulley replacement step.

**The 2016 book contradicts itself.** Its Q&A for a slipping belt says to tighten the M6 nyloc nut
"until sound wave tension gauge reads **450N**" - the recumbent figure, in the wrong unit for a
sound-wave gauge. The 2023 book has no Q&A chapter and prints only the 180 Hz figure. Set an
XBU55 by the replacement step, 180 ±10 Hz.

The recumbents in the same family are set in newtons
(`spirit-xbr-specs-drive-belt-tension-450-newtons-540-when-new`); the same 180 ±10 Hz figure
appears on the generator recumbents and on the 2012 CU800 as 170 ±10 Hz.
