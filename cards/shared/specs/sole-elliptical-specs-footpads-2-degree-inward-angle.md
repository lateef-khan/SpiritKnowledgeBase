**The footpads are built with a 2-degree inward angle.** Sole credits research
with Richard DeKok, P.T., M.T.C.: riders push outward on the power stroke,
stressing the outer foot and throwing ankles, knees and hips out of alignment,
and the inward angle puts the joints back into neutral alignment.

**This is the design angle, not the adjustable tilt.** The adjustable 0 / 5 /
10-degree positions are a separate figure
(`sole-elliptical-specs-adjustable-footpad-angles-0-5-and-10-degrees`); the
E98-2011 book prints only this 2-degree figure with no adjustable positions.
Books from 2012 on (except the AE 2011 pair) print neither figure.
