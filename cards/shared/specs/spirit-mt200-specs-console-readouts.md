The Readouts line of the specification page names eleven values:

Time, Grade, Distance, Speed, **Pause**, METs, Calories, Pace, Cadence, Left and
Right Step Length, Symmetry Index.

**Both MT200 manuals print the same eleven, and both print "Pause".** The 2010
manual spells the sixth "METs" and the 2022 manual "METS"; nothing else differs.

**The 2026 7.0T's list prints "Pulse" in the fifth position, not "Pause".**
Everything either side of it is identical on all three specification pages. The
manuals never reconcile the two words, and neither MT200 book gives a reason for
"Pause". Both readings are reproduced as printed; do not quote one list for the
other machine. See `70t-2026-specs-console-readouts`.

**Pulse is a real MT200 reading even though the spec line does not name it.**
The console section lists Pulse among the message-window measurements, as a
heart rate monitor value from 0 to 240 bpm, and the console has a heart icon and
a percentage-of-maximum bar graph beside it. So a customer asking whether the
MT200 shows heart rate should be told yes, from the console pages, not from this
line.

This is the spec sheet's list of what the machine measures. How each value is
shown, paged through with the Display key or reset is a console question and the
console section of the manual covers it.

**Left and Right Step Length and Symmetry Index are what mark this machine out** -
they are what the Symmetry program measures, from the two step sensors under the
deck.
