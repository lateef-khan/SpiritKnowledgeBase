**Motor: 3.0HP AC**, printed word for word in the Electrical Safety bullets of both
the 2026 7.0T and the 2026 8.0T owner's manuals, and in the same bullet on the
Grounding Instructions page of the **CTSBS900** slat belt sled treadmill manual.

Neither 2026 Specifications page repeats it, and the CTSBS900 manual has no
specification table at all, so this bullet is the only motor figure in any of the
three books. No wattage, no continuous-duty rating and no motor part number is
given.

**The CTSBS900 is the only one of its own family that prints a motor rating.** The
CT900 and CT900ENT owner's manuals print none - see
`spirit-ct900-specs-no-specification-table` - so never read this figure across to
them.

**The 4.0T of the same 2026 Spirit line is 4.0HP AC** - a larger number on the
smaller machine. Never carry either figure to the other; see
`40t-2026-specs-drive-motor-rating`.

The bullet directly below gives the power requirement: 120 volts, 60 Hz,
dedicated 20-amp circuit, NEMA 5-20P plug and receptacle type. That is the
electrical-safety question rather than this one.
