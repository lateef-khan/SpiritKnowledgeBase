**Motor: 3.0HP AC**, printed in the Electrical Safety bullets of both the 2026
7.0T and the 2026 8.0T owner's manuals, word for word.

Neither Specifications page repeats it, so this bullet is the only motor figure
in either book. No wattage, no continuous-duty rating and no motor part number is
given.

**The 4.0T of the same 2026 Spirit line is 4.0HP AC** - a larger number on the
smaller machine. Never carry either figure to the other; see
`40t-2026-specs-drive-motor-rating`.

The bullet directly below gives the power requirement: 120 volts, 60 Hz,
dedicated 20-amp circuit, NEMA 5-20P plug and receptacle type. That is the
electrical-safety question rather than this one.
