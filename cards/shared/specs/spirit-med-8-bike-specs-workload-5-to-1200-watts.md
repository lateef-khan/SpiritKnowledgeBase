**Workload - 5 watts up to 1200 watts**, the same row on both pages.

Neither book prints a level-to-watts table, a watts-per-level scale or a cadence for the figure -
unlike the 7.0R and 7.0U books, which set a watts-per-level scale in the console and whose two
figures disagree (`spirit-med-70-bike-specs-workload-5-to-1700-watts`). The console shows the
rider's watts with a beacon light bar colour-coded by percentage of target watts (white 0-59 % to
red 90-100 %), and the Symmetry feature splits them left and right; that is a console question.

**Three different ceilings on one brake across the Dyaco MED range** - 1700 W on the 7-series
bikes' specification page, **1200 W here**, 1000 W on the 8.5S stepper and the 8.5UE ergometer
(`spirit-climber-specs-workload-5-to-1000-watts`, `85ue-2025-specs-workload-5-to-1000-watts`), 650 W
on the MS300. Quote the figure from the book for the machine in front of you.
