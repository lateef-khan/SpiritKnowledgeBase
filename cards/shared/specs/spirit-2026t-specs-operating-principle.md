Four manuals print the same paragraph, with only the model name changed: the
2026 7.0T and 8.0T, and both MT200 books.

**Speed.** The operator uses the speed keys to control the motors. When the speed
key is pressed, a signal is sent to the **AC motor inverter** to request a
change. The inverter then sends the appropriate amount of power to the **AC drive
motor** to reach the requested speed. The motor drives the **front roller**,
which in turn moves the walking belt, through **pulleys and a fan belt**.

**Incline.** The incline motor is operated when the operator presses the incline
keys. A command is generated and transmitted to the **incline control board**,
where a relay is energised sending AC power to the motor until it reaches the
desired position.

This is the manual's own description of the drive chain, and it is what names the
inverter as the part between the console and the drive motor. It is not a
diagnostic procedure; the error-code cards cover inverter faults.

**The 2010 MT200 book prints two typos in this paragraph**, "AC motor **invert**"
for inverter and "through **pullers** and fan Belt" for pulleys. The 2022 MT200
and both 2026 books print inverter and pulleys. The wording above is the correct
form; nothing else in the paragraph differs.

**The parts these four manuals name for it.** On the MT200 parts lists the
inverter is item 274 ("Inverter, A.C. Motor"), its encoder item 275, the drive
motor item 279, the drive motor belt item 16 ("Drive Motor Belt, Poly-V"), the
front roller item 10, and the front and rear incline motors items 280 and 281.
The MT200 has two incline motors, front and rear, because it declines as well as
inclines.
