The SW100 book is the only strength book in this wave with an exploded view (printed page 9) and a part list (printed page 10 — the book heads it "PART LIST", sic). The view's numbered callouts key to this list, verified digit-exact on the render (the OCR confuses Ø/Φ with ©/o/® — the diameters here are as printed).

|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stand Leg | 2 |

| 3 | Rear Left Stabilizer | 1 |
| 4 | Rear Right Stabilizer | 1 |
| 5 | Cross Brace | 1 |
| 6 | Dumbbell Platforms | 2 |
| 7 | Water Bottle Tray | 1 |
| 8 | Connect Plate | 1 |
| 9 | Adjustable bar | 1 |
| 10 | Media Tray | 1 |
| 11 | Leveler | 4 |

| 22 | M10 Nylon Nut | 10 |

Items A and B (one Wrench, one Allen Key) close the list and are the only tools the book names; the assembly hardware pack repeats them per step.
