| Warranty | Frame\* | Generator Brake | Parts, Labor | Wear Items\*\* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | 10 Years | 5 Years | 3 Years | 6 Months |

\*Frame is defined as the welded metal base of the unit and does not include removable parts, wear items, or coatings.

\*\*Wear items include: plastic covers, overlay, grips, hardware, pull pins/knobs, decals/stickers, headphone jack, USB port, paint/coatings/finish, straps, and pedals.

> Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage.

Effective **September 27, 2024**, headed **WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL** with the table titled *Commercial Limited Warranty*.

**There is one row and no Residential row anywhere in the manual.** This is the only one of the fourteen 2024 machines whose table states no home warranty at all; the other thirteen print a Residential row beside the commercial one (`spirit-2024-warranty-coverage-periods-parts-labor`). **Do not supply a home period from a sibling manual** - the CSC900 is a different machine with a different table. The printed page was rendered at 300 dpi and read by OCR: there is one row of figures, with white space below it and the frame footnote beginning under that.

**Parts and labor share one column at 3 Years.** The manual prints no separate labor period, so the 2 Years the other 2024 machines give labor does not apply here.

**The generator brake has its own column at 5 Years**, as it does on the CE900 2021 - which is a different table and a different machine: its frame is **Lifetime** and its parts and labor are two columns of 3 years, not one (`ce900-2021-warranty-coverage-periods`).

**The frame is 10 Years.** No cell in this table says *Lifetime*.

**The opening paragraph does not match the heading or the table.** The page is headed WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL and warrants "all its **home use** Stair Climber parts", administered "directly to a **consumer**", above a duty list addressed to a **facility** - on the one manual of the fourteen whose table has no home row at all. The manual never reconciles it: `spirit-2024-warranty-use-environment`.
