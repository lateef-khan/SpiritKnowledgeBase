**What the numbers are.**

- **(870) 333-5500** - customer service and the service department in the ERG160, ERG220, ERG500, ERG550W, ERG600W and ERG650W books and on their back covers; the service department in the ERG700's step 3; customer service in the ERG180; the number printed on the serial-number sticker drawings of the ERG160, ERG180, ERG220 and ERG750W label pages; the number the water-rower tank pages give for buying purification tablets.
- **(870) 336-4286** - the ERG400 (2015) and the ERG700 registration page and back cover, the ERG800W registration and label pages, and every number in the four strength books. It is the number every Xterra treadmill book prints for customer service (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-support-contacts-three-phone-numbers-email-and-addresses`).
- **(870) 935-1107** - the service department in SERVICE step 3 of the two Online-Support rower books that print steps, the ERG180 and ERG800W; the number the Spirit manuals print for Spirit's service department.
- **(800) 258-8511** / **1-800-258-8511** - customer care on the cover and back cover of the three Online-Support books (ERG180, ERG750W, ERG800W), with **questions@xterrafitness.com**.
- **(870) 335-5500** - printed once, on the ERG750W registration page; its label page prints 333-5500 and the FB180 folding bike book prints the same pair (`xterra-warranty-support-contacts-which-phone-number-each-bike-elliptical-and-climber-book-prints`). **Treat it as a misprint of 333-5500.**

**The ERG700 disagrees with itself**: 336-4286 for customer service on its registration page and back cover, 333-5500 for the service department in step 3. Both are XTERRA Fitness numbers; use the one the step you are on prints.

**Email, fax, street and postal addresses.** *questions@xterrafitness.com* appears only on the three Online-Support covers. Every back cover prints **3000 Nestle Road, Jonesboro, AR 72401**, **Fax 870-935-7611** and **www.xterrafitness.com** - the ERG400 scan has no back cover. Every warranty page that prints SERVICE steps closes with the postal address for the service department, **XTERRA Fitness, Inc., P.O. Box 2037, Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037**; the ERG750W, which prints no steps, is the one book without it (`fb180-2025-warranty-service-steps-not-printed-use-the-cover-contacts`). The steps that say when to call are `xterra-treadmill-warranty-service-steps-dealer-service-organization-then-the-service-department`; the registration pages that carry the customer-service number are `xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover` and `xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-qr-code-and-support-tab-no-deadline`; the bike, elliptical and climber books are `xterra-warranty-support-contacts-which-phone-number-each-bike-elliptical-and-climber-book-prints` and the treadmills `xterra-treadmill-warranty-support-contacts-three-phone-numbers-email-and-addresses`.
