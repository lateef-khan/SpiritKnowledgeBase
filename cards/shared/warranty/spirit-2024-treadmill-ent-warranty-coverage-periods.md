| Warranty | Frame\* | Motor, Deck | Parts | Console Parts, Labor | Console Labor | Wear Items\*\* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | 10 Years | 5 Years | 5 Years | 2 Years | 1 Year | 6 Months |
| Residential | 10 Years | Lifetime | 10 Years | 2 Years | 1 Year | 6 Months |

\*Frame is defined as the welded metal base of the unit and does not include removable parts, wear items, or coatings.

\*\*Wear items include: plastic covers, overlay, grips, hardware, pull pins/knobs, decals/stickers, headphone jack, USB port, paint/coatings/finish, straps, and pedals.

> Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage.

Both manuals are effective **October 07, 2024**, headed **WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL** with the table titled *Commercial Limited Warranty*. The CT800ENT and CT850ENT print it identically, figure for figure.

**This is the non-ENT table with a console split off it.** Against `spirit-2024-treadmill-warranty-coverage-periods` the frame, motor, deck and parts figures are unchanged; what is new is a console covered **2 Years** and a **Console Labor** column of **1 Year**, and the disappearance of a general Labor column.

**There is no general Labor column.** The table warrants Frame, Motor and Deck, Parts, the console and Wear Items, and states no labor period for anything but the console - where the non-ENT CT800 and CT850 of three weeks earlier print Labor 2 Years for the whole machine. The only other labor figure these manuals print is the SERVICE paragraph's clock, twelve months from the bill of sale or eighteen from factory shipping (`spirit-ct800-warranty-labor-period`), and neither manual says whether that is the labor warranty this table omits. **Say the table states none rather than quoting the non-ENT figure.**

**Two column headings overlap and the manuals do not resolve them.** The fourth column reads **Console Parts, Labor** at 2 Years and the fifth reads **Console Labor** at 1 Year, so read literally the console's labor is covered for two years by one column and one year by the other. The 2022 CT800ENT and CT850ENT print the same pair as *CONSOLE PARTS* 3 Years and *CONSOLE LABOR* 1 Year (`ct800ent-2022-warranty-console-periods`), which is what the pair is elsewhere in the range, so the likeliest reading is console parts 2 years and console labor 1 year - **but neither manual says so.** Quote both headings and do not silently drop the word *Labor* from the fourth. The CU800ENT 2024, CE800ENT 2024 and CR800ENT 2024 print the same overlapping pair.

**Console parts dropped from 3 years to 2**, as they did on the other 2024 ENT machines.

**The Residential motor cell reads plain *Lifetime*** under a **Motor, Deck** heading, so it covers both components without the parenthesis the non-ENT books use - and the Parts column beside it reads 10 Years in the same row. Every figure above was read off a 300 dpi render of the printed page as well as out of the extracted text.

**The opening paragraph does not match the heading or the table**: WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL over "all its **home use** Treadmill parts" administered "directly to a **consumer**", above a duty list addressed to a **facility** - `spirit-2024-warranty-use-environment`.
