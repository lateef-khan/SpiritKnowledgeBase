Exclusion 10, printed verbatim:

> Warranty will be void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment. (Examples: Garage, outside, carport, etc)

A garage, the outdoors and a carport are the manual's own examples of places that void it. The manual gives no temperature or humidity limits with this exclusion and states no exceptions.

**This exclusion appears for the first time in the 2023 revision.** It is in the b94-2023, r92-2023, lcb-2023, lcr-2023, sb900-2023 and sb1200-2023 manuals and in no Sole bike or indoor cycle manual before them, so it cannot be quoted against an earlier machine.

Exclusion 3 of the same list reaches the same ground from another direction: it excludes damage from **corrosion (RUST)**, natural disasters, and elemental damage such as power surges, lightning storms, water damage or fire damage. A machine kept in a garage that rusts is refused twice over.
