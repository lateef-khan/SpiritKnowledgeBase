**Wear items are covered for 1 Year**, in every row of the table on all three manuals - a gym, a non-dues-paying facility and a home get the same year. What the phrase covers is not the same list:

| Manual | Column heading | The footnote names |
|---|---|---|
| CB900 2013 | Wear Items* | grips, end caps, **floor tube rubber guards**, and **calf stretch cover** |
| CIC800 2021 | Wear Items* | grips, end caps, and **toe cage straps** |
| CIC850 2022 | **Console & Wear Items*** | grips, end caps, and **toe cage straps** |

**Only grips and end caps appear on all three.** Everything else is one manual's list or the other's, and no manual says its list is exhaustive - the CIC800 and CIC850 both hedge it as "items **such as** grips, end caps, and toe cage straps", while the CB900 states its four flatly with no such-as.

**The CB900 names two parts the later manuals do not**, floor tube rubber guards and a calf stretch cover, and **the CIC pair names one the CB900 does not**, the toe cage straps. Read the footnote off the manual that came with the machine rather than merging the two lists: telling a CB900 owner their toe cage straps are a wear item, or a CIC800 owner their calf stretch cover is, quotes a list their book does not print.

**The CIC850 puts the console in this column.** Its heading reads *Console & Wear Items* where the other two read *Wear Items*, so on that machine the console carries the same **1 Year** as the grips. The footnote itself never mentions the console - only the heading does. The CB900 and CIC800 warrant no console period anywhere (`spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-three-row-periods`).

**A shorter period than the parts warranty, on parts a rider touches every session.** Parts are 3 Years on all three machines; the grips, end caps and straps are 1 Year. That is the practical answer to "are the grips covered" a year and a day after purchase: no.

## The 2019 warranty sheet prints a fourth list

**Spirit's Cardio Warranty sheet, Revision 03.07.2019, footnotes the CIC800's Wear Items row with "grips, seats and pedal
straps".** Grips are on every list; **the seat is a wear item on the sheet and on none of the
three manuals**, and the end caps that all three manuals name are not on the sheet. "Pedal
straps" is the sheet's phrase for the CIC pair's toe cage straps. It is the shortest of the
four lists and, like the CB900's, it is stated flatly with no *such as*. Quote the document
the customer holds: `spirit-commercial-warranty-sheet-2019-footnotes`.

## The other five bikes of this range print no wear-items column at all

The **XIC600** pair, the **AB900**, the **AB950** and the **JB950** warranty tables have no wear-items column and no footnote defining one. Nothing in those five manuals says a grip or an end cap is covered for a shorter time than any other part - and nothing says it is covered for the full parts period either. The manuals are simply silent; do not answer them from this card in either direction.

**The CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 have a Wear Items column at 6 Months**, half the period here, and define it differently again (`spirit-bike-2024-warranty-coverage-periods`).
