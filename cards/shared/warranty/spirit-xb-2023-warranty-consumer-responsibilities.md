Under the heading **RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE CONSUMER**:

> This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household use, and the **consumer/facility** is responsible for the items listed below:

1. Proper use of the Recumbent Bike in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual
2. Proper installation in accordance with instructions provided with the Recumbent Bike and with all local electric codes.
3. Expenses for making the Recumbent Bike accessible for servicing, including any item that was not part of the Recumbent Bike at the time it was shipped from the factory.
4. Damages to the Recumbent Bike finish during shipping, installation or following installation.
5. Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual.

**The five items are word for word identical in all four 2023 manuals** apart from the machine noun - *Recumbent Bike* in the XBR25, XBR55 and XBR95 books, *Upright Bike* in the XBU55 book. The older XB manuals call it a generic *fitness bike* whatever the machine.

## Two duties the older manuals imposed are gone

The ten printings before 2023 carry **seven** items (`spirit-xb-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`). Two were deleted:

- **The registration card.** Old item 1 required the card returned within **10 days of purchase** to validate the warranty. **No 2023 manual asks the owner to register at all**, sets any deadline, or mentions a card - and its exclusion list no longer excludes an unregistered bike either (`spirit-xb-2023-warranty-exclusions`). The Warranty Registration page at the front still invites registration, with a QR code, but imposes no time limit (`spirit-xb-warranty-registration`).
- **The electrical duty.** Old item 4 made the owner responsible for a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage, **blown fuses, loose connections and defects in house wiring**. That item is gone from all four. Do not read it to a 2023 owner; their manual does not impose it.

## The lead sentence contradicts two of the four manuals

All four say *ordinary household use*, and address a **consumer/facility** rather than the older manuals' bare *consumer*. But the **XBR95 2023** and **XBU55 2023** print a Light Commercial table two inches above this sentence, and all four books' exclusion 2 speaks of "ordinary household **or light commercial** use". Neither the table nor the exclusion list agrees with the lead sentence here. Say what the manual says, in all three places: `spirit-xb-warranty-use-environment`.

## The one cost in the list

Item 3 makes the owner pay to make the bike reachable for a service call, and to deal with anything that was not in the box. It is one of five charges the owner carries during the warranty period; the other four are in the SERVICE steps (`xt-2023-warranty-owner-costs`).

**These five items are the same five the 2023 Spirit XE ellipticals and the 2023 XT treadmills print**, with the machine noun swapped (`spirit-xe-2023-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`). **They are not the Spirit commercial list**, which runs to seven, keeps the registration item with an "or completed online" clause, and extends the electrical duty to facility wiring (`spirit-ct800-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`).
