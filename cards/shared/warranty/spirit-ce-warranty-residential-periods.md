| Warranty | Frame | Brake / Electronics | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | Lifetime | 10 Years | 10 Years | 2 Years |

**The row is word for word the same in all seven manuals** - CE800 2013, 2021 and 2023, the CE800ENT, and the CE850 2016, 2020 and 2022. Only the effective date printed above it changes: March 1, 2012; November 08, 2021; June 08, 2023; July 3, 2024 for the CE800 range, and December 23, 2015; November 08, 2021; July 06, 2022 for the CE850 range.

**The second column is named differently in each range** - *Electronics* on a CE800, *Brake* on a CE850, *EMS Brake* on the CE800ENT - and reads 10 Years on this row in every one. No manual states a separate figure for the two.

**Ten years is the home figure and five is the commercial one.** The only two columns that change between the two rows are the second one and Parts, both doubling from 5 Years to 10 Years. Frame stays Lifetime and Labor stays 2 Years in both. The commercial row is `spirit-ce-warranty-commercial-periods`.

**The CE800ENT prints two further columns** - Console Parts 3 Years and Console Labor 1 Year - and they are the only figures in that manual that do **not** change between the commercial and the residential row. Read them from `ct800ent-2022-warranty-console-periods`.

**Three of these ten elliptical manuals print no residential row at all or a different one.** The CE900 2021 table has one Commercial row and nothing else, so it states **no** home period (`ce900-2021-warranty-coverage-periods`). The CE900 2025 and CES880 2025 residential rows read Frame Lifetime, Brake **5 Years**, Parts 10 Years, Labor 2 Years and add Wear Items 6 Months - a five-year brake at home, not ten (`spirit-ce-2025-warranty-coverage-periods`).

**A CE800 or CE850 in a home is the second row of a commercial machine's table, not a separate warranty document.** The same exclusions, responsibilities and service steps govern both rows.

When the clock starts is in the paragraph above the table (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`); the two-year labor figure is dated separately in the SERVICE paragraph (`spirit-ct800-warranty-labor-period`).
