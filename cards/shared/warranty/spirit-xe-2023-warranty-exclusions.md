This warranty does not cover the following:

1. CONSEQUENTIAL, COLLATERAL, OR INCIDENTAL DAMAGES SUCH AS PROPERTY DAMAGE AND INCIDENTAL EXPENSES RESULTING FROM ANY BREACH OF THIS WRITTEN OR ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY. *Note: Some states do not allow the exclusion or limitation of incidental or consequential damages, so this limitation or exclusion may not apply to you.*
2. Service call reimbursement to the consumer. Service call reimbursement to the dealer that does not involve malfunction or defects in workmanship or material, for units that are beyond the warranty period, for units that are beyond the service call reimbursement period, for Elliptical not requiring component replacement, or Elliptical not in ordinary household or light commercial use.
3. Damages caused by services performed by persons other than authorized Spirit Fitness service companies; use of parts other than original Spirit Fitness parts; or external causes such as corrosion, discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, or acts of God.
4. Products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered.
5. Products that have been: sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party.
6. **Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.**
7. THIS WARRANTY IS EXPRESSLY IN LIEU OF ALL OTHER WARRANTIES EXPRESSED OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING THE WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND/OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.
8. Product use in any environment other than a residential setting or non-dues paying facility with 5 hours use or less per day.
9. Warranties outside of the United States may vary. Please contact your local dealer for details.

Printed under the list:

> Product features or specifications as described or illustrated are subject to change without notice. All warranties are made by Spirit Fitness, Inc. This warranty applies only in the 48 contiguous United States. NOTE: This does not include Alaska or Hawaii.

**The two 2023 manuals print these nine items word for word.** A word-level comparison of the two blocks comes back identical. Only the effective dates above them differ: August 07, 2023 for the XE395 and June 02, 2023 for the XE795.

## Item 6 is the change, and it is in the customer's favour

Every earlier XE manual - 2005, 2007, 2010 and the whole 2021 family - opens item 6 with a flat exclusion: *Products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at Spirit Fitness.* **The 2023 manuals delete that sentence.** What remains only reserves a right to ask for a receipt. An unregistered machine under the 2023 terms is **not** named as something the warranty excludes, and neither manual asks for registration in its responsibilities list either (`spirit-xe-warranty-registration`).

**Do not answer a 2021 machine's registration question from this card**, and do not answer a 2023 machine's from `spirit-ce850-warranty-exclusions`, whose item 6 still excludes outright.

## What did not change

Item 8 is the same two-limit sentence the 2021 manuals print: a home **or** a facility that charges no dues, capped at five hours a day. The XE395's table nevertheless prints a Residential row and nothing else, so a facility placement it permits has no periods stated anywhere in that manual; only the XE795 prints a Light Commercial row (`spirit-xe-warranty-use-environment`).

Items 4 and 5 are unchanged and are asked about on their own - `xt-2023-warranty-transfer`. Item 9 and the 48-states note are `xt-2023-warranty-geographic-coverage`.

**Item 2 calls the machine an *Elliptical*, capitalised**, where the 2021 manuals say *elliptical trainer* and the Spirit XT treadmill list of the same shape says *treadmill*. The rule is the same; the noun is the manual's own.
