This warranty applies only to products in **ordinary household use**. The consumer is responsible for:

1. Completing and returning the **warranty registration card** within 10 days of the original purchase to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty, **or**, outside that 10 day period, registering with proof of purchase (receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation).
2. *"Proper use of the treadmill in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual, including general maintenance (i.e. belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under motor cover, and lubrication. See all components under General Maintenance section)"*.
3. Proper installation in accordance with instructions provided with the indoor cycle.
4. Expenses for making the indoor cycle accessible for servicing, including any item that was not part of the indoor cycle at the time it was shipped from the factory.
5. Damages to the indoor cycle finish during shipping, installation or following installation.
6. Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual (see All Components under General Maintenance).
7. Providing pictures or video of any issues by email as requested by the service technician, and assisting with troubleshooting and diagnosing prior to a home visit.

**Item 2 is quoted exactly as printed, and it is treadmill text sitting in an indoor cycle manual.** It names a treadmill, belt tracking, belt tension, a motor cover and lubrication — none of which an indoor cycle has. Read it as proper use of the cycle in accordance with this manual, including the general maintenance the manual specifies.

**The SB900 manual numbers the list 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 — it skips 7.** The SB1200 manual numbers the same seven items 1 to 7. Nothing is missing from the SB900 list; only its numbering is wrong.

The pre-2023 indoor cycle manuals have six items, with a plain "proper use of the indoor cycle", no proof-of-purchase fallback, and no pictures-and-video duty.
