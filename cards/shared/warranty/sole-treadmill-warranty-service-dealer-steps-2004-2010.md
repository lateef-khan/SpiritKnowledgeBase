**Service under this warranty must be obtained by following these steps, in order:**

1. **Contact the selling authorized Sole dealer.** OR
2. **Contact the local authorized Sole service organization.**
3. If there is any question where to obtain service, call SOLE — the number depends on the book’s era:

| Books | Step-3 number |
|---|---|
| F63/UF63, F80 family, TT8/UTT8 (effective 2004) | **(800) 258-4555 ext. 634** |
| UF80/83/85, VF63, VF80/83/85, VS73/77, VTT8 (effective 2007) | **866-MYSOLE1 (697-6531)** |
| WF63, WF80/83/85, WS73/77, WTT8 (effective 2008) | **1-866-780-SOLE (7653)** for product/warranty questions, **866-MYSOLE1 (697-6531)** for technical problems (the WF63 book writes "your new elliptical", sic, in a treadmill manual) |

4. **If no local service is available**, SOLE repairs or replaces the parts, at its option, at no charge for parts. **Transportation both ways and adequate packaging are the owner’s responsibility**; freight claims go to the carrier. **Do not ship any unit to the factory without a return authorization number.**
5. By mail: **SOLE (Sole) Fitness, P.O. Box 2037, Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037.**

**Keep the bill of sale** and all service receipts. Where the warranty is honoured is separate — see `sole-treadmill-warranty-geographic-coverage`.
