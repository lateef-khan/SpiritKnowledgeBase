Two warranty sentences sit outside the warranty pages of these ten books. They are what consumer responsibility 3, *proper connection to a power supply of sufficient voltage*, and responsibility 2, *proper use ... including maintenance*, mean in practice (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-with-a-registration-card`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-without-registration`).

| What the page says | Where | Books |
|---|---|---|
| **If A.C. voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more, the performance of your [machine] may be affected. Such conditions are not covered under your warranty.** If you suspect the voltage is low, contact your local power company or a licensed electrician for proper testing. | Important Electrical Instructions (older layout) / Electrical Safety (Online-Support layout) | SB4500 p. 5, SB4.5r p. 5 (OCR), SB500 p. 5 (both printings), RSX1500 p. 6 (2021) / p. 5 (2017, OCR), FS5.8e p. 5 (OCR), FS5.9e p. 5 (OCR), SB240 p. 8, SB600 p. 8 |
| **PLEASE NOTE: 1. The XTERRA Fitness Indoor Cycling Console system is designed as a sealed unit and not meant to be opened other than for the sole purpose of installing batteries. Any opened units will void the warranty. 2. To clean the XTERRA Fitness Indoor Cycling Console use a clean damp cloth. Use of any caustic cleaning solutions will void the warranty. 3. The XTERRA Fitness Indoor Cycling Console system is NOT waterproof, only water resistant. Any excessive exposure to water will void the warranty.** | Getting Familiar with the Control Panel | MB500 p. 7 (OCR), MB550 p. 12 |

**Only the plug-in machines print the voltage sentence** - the ones with an *Important Electrical Instructions* or *Electrical Safety* page: the SB500, SB4500, SB4.5r, RSX1500, FS5.8e, FS5.9e, SB240 and SB600. The battery-powered and adapter-powered bikes and ellipticals (AIR, FB, MB, MBX, SB120, SB150, SB250, SB2.5r, UB120, EU, FS1.5-3.5, FS150) print no electrical page and no such sentence; checked by a loose grep for *covered*, *void* and *ten percent* across all thirty books. The SB240 and SB600 sentence sits on the same page as the AFCI/GFCI breaker advice.

**Only the two indoor cycles with a battery console print the three console sentences** - the MB500 and MB550. The MBX2500, their sibling, prints no *PLEASE NOTE* block; its console page was checked in the text layer and the render sweep. The sentences make three things a warranty matter that exclusion 3's *improper maintenance* and *inadequate power supply* only imply (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-store-display-and-unregistered-products`): opening the sealed console for anything but a battery change, cleaning it with a caustic solution, and letting it get wet beyond splash level.

Together with exclusion 3 these are the grounds a warranty claim on a console that failed after being opened or doused, or on electronics that failed on a brown-out, is refused. The treadmill books print the same voltage sentence with two more of their own about the belt and deck (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-what-the-maintenance-and-electrical-pages-say-is-not-covered`).
