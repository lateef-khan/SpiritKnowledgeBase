This warranty does not cover the following:

1. CONSEQUENTIAL, COLLATERAL, OR INCIDENTAL DAMAGES SUCH AS PROPERTY DAMAGE AND INCIDENTAL EXPENSES RESULTING FROM ANY BREACH OF THIS WRITTEN OR ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY. *Note: Some states do not allow the exclusion or limitation of incidental or consequential damages, so this limitation or exclusion may not apply to you.*
2. Service call reimbursement to the consumer. Service call reimbursement to the dealer that does not involve malfunction or defects in workmanship or material, for units that are beyond the warranty period, for units that are beyond the service call reimbursement period, for elliptical not requiring component replacement, or elliptical not in ordinary household use.
3. Damages caused by services performed by persons other than authorized Spirit service companies; use of parts other than original Spirit parts; or external causes such as alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, or acts of God.
4. Products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered.
5. Products that have been sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party.
6. Products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at Spirit Mfg. Spirit reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.
7. THIS WARRANTY IS EXPRESSLY IN LIEU OF ALL OTHER WARRANTIES EXPRESSED OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING THE WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND/OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

**The two books print these seven items word for word**, in the same order - a word-level comparison of the whole EXCLUSIONS block of the 2005 XE150/XE350/XE550 manual against the 2007 XE100/XE200/XE300 one comes back **identical**. Only the effective dates above them differ: October 15, 2005 and October 15, 2007.

## Seven items, not nine. Three things later manuals exclude are absent here

- **No use-environment item.** Nothing in this list bars a gym, an office or an apartment building. The limit is carried instead by the ATTENTION notice on page 2 and by the words *ordinary household use* in item 2 and in the responsibilities lead sentence - and the notice is the harsher of the two, voiding the warranty in its entirety (`spirit-xe-warranty-use-environment`).
- **No outside-the-United-States item.** The geographic limit is a sentence under the list, not a numbered exclusion (`spirit-xe-warranty-geographic-fifty-states`).
- **No prisons item and no prisons footnote.** The words *prison* and *correctional* appear nowhere in either document. Do not quote that exclusion to the owner of one.

**Item 3 is shorter than every later version.** It names *alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, or acts of God* and stops there. From the 2010 XE395 onwards the same item opens with **corrosion, discoloration of paint or plastic** as well. Neither of these two books excludes corrosion or fading anywhere.

**Item 6 names a different company.** The registration card is on file at **Spirit Mfg**, and it is **Spirit** that reserves the right to ask for proof of purchase - not Spirit Fitness, which is the name every manual from 2010 on uses. The rule is unchanged: the first sentence excludes an unregistered product outright and the second only reserves a right to ask for a receipt. The two do not say the same thing and neither book reconciles them (`spirit-xe-warranty-registration`).

**Item 5 has no colon.** It reads *Products that have been sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party* where every Spirit manual from 2010 on prints *Products that have been: sold, ...*. The rule is the same - a sale, a transfer, a barter or a gift ends the cover, and item 4 excludes a removed or altered serial number on its own (`xt-2023-warranty-transfer`).

This list is only half of what governs the warranty. The other half is the responsibilities list above it, in `spirit-xe-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-electrical-duty`.
