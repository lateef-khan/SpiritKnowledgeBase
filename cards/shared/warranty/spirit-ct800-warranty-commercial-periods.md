| Warranty | Frame | Motor/Deck | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial | Lifetime | 5 Years | 5 Years | 2 Years |

**All four figures are word for word the same in all four CT800 manuals** - the 2012, the 2016, the 2020 and the CT800ENT. Only the row label and the printed effective date move:

| Manual | Row label | Effective date |
|---|---|---|
| CT800 2012 | Commercial | March 1, 2012 |
| CT800 2016 | Commercial | December 23, 2015 |
| CT800 2020 | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | August 10, 2023 |
| CT800ENT 2022 | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | March 15, 2024 |

**Motor and deck share one column** in every CT800 manual - a single *Motor/Deck* heading, printed *MOTOR & DECK* in the CT800ENT - so the manuals state no separate deck period. The 2016 and 2020 manuals print the labor figure as "2 Year" and the 2012 and CT800ENT as "2 Years"; the period is two years in all four.

**"Commercial" here means a non-dues-paying facility.** The 2020 and CT800ENT manuals say so in the row label itself, and all four exclude a dues-paying gym elsewhere in the same section - `spirit-ct800-warranty-use-environment`. The 2020 and CT800ENT manuals also print a footnote under the table: *Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage* (`spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`).

**The CT800ENT prints two columns this row does not have** - Console Parts 3 Years and Console Labor 1 Year, between Labor and the end of the table. Nothing above is wrong for a CT800ENT; it is incomplete. Read the console periods from `ct800ent-2022-warranty-console-periods` and never fold them into Parts 5 Years.

These are **not** the other Spirit commercial figures. The CT900 warrants the frame 10 Years, the motor 5 and the deck 3, and adds a Wear Items column; the CT900ENT warrants the frame Lifetime, the drive motor 10 Years and parts 3 Years; the 2026 4.0T, 7.0T and 8.0T print Parts 5 Years and Labor 1 Year and no frame or motor column at all. None of those tables is this one.

Two things the table does not tell you on its own. When the clock starts is in the paragraph above it (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`), and the two-year labor figure is dated separately in the SERVICE paragraph two pages later (`spirit-ct800-warranty-labor-period`).
