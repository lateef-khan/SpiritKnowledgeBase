| Warranty | Frame | Motor/Deck | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial | Lifetime | 5 Years | 5 Years | 2 Years |

**All four figures are word for word the same in all eight manuals** - the four CT800 owner's manuals and the four CT850 ones. Only the row label and the printed effective date move:

| Manual | Row label | Effective date |
|---|---|---|
| CT800 2012 | Commercial | March 1, 2012 |
| CT800 2016 | Commercial | December 23, 2015 |
| CT800 2020 | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | August 10, 2023 |
| CT800ENT 2022 | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | March 15, 2024 |
| CT850 2016 | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | November 12, 2021 |
| CT850 2018 | Commercial | December 23, 2015 |
| CT850 2020 | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | August 10, 2023 |
| CT850ENT 2022 | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | July 03, 2024 |

**The effective date does not track the model year.** The CT850 2016 manual prints a warranty effective **November 12, 2021** and the CT850 2018 manual one effective **December 23, 2015** - the older manual carrying the later terms. The date is the date the warranty was written, not the year of the machine, so it is no guide to which manual is newer.

**Motor and deck share one column** in every one of the eight - a single *Motor/Deck* heading, printed *MOTOR & DECK* in the two ENT manuals - so no manual states a separate deck period. Six of the eight print the labor figure as "2 Year" (CT800 2016 and 2020, CT850 2016, 2018 and 2020) and the CT800 2012, CT800ENT and CT850ENT as "2 Years"; the period is two years in all eight.

**"Commercial" here means a non-dues-paying facility.** Five of the eight say so in the row label itself - CT800 2020, CT800ENT, CT850 2016, CT850 2020 and CT850ENT - and all eight exclude a dues-paying gym elsewhere in the same section, whatever the row label says: `spirit-ct800-warranty-use-environment`. Those same five print a footnote under the table: *Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage* (`spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`). The CT800 2012, CT800 2016 and CT850 2018 manuals print neither the parenthesis nor the footnote.

**The two ENT manuals print two columns this row does not have** - Console Parts 3 Years and Console Labor 1 Year, between Labor and the end of the table, in the CT800ENT and the CT850ENT alike. Nothing above is wrong for an ENT machine; it is incomplete. Read the console periods from `ct800ent-2022-warranty-console-periods` and never fold them into Parts 5 Years.

These are **not** the other Spirit commercial figures. The CT900 warrants the frame 10 Years, the motor 5 and the deck 3, and adds a Wear Items column; the CT900ENT warrants the frame Lifetime, the drive motor 10 Years and parts 3 Years; the 2026 4.0T, 7.0T and 8.0T print Parts 5 Years and Labor 1 Year and no frame or motor column at all. None of those tables is this one.

Two things the table does not tell you on its own. When the clock starts is in the paragraph above it (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`), and the two-year labor figure is dated separately in the SERVICE paragraph two pages later (`spirit-ct800-warranty-labor-period`).
