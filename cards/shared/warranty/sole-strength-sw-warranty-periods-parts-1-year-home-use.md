**Home Use Limited Warranty: Parts 1 Year.** The three books print this single line — no effective date, no start-date rule, no labor term, no service-steps section, and no cosmetic row.

What the page does print besides the line: a three-duty consumer list (`sole-strength-sw-warranty-responsibilities-3-item`) and a nine-item exclusion list (`sole-strength-sw-warranty-exclusions-9-item`). Registration is a card plus online signup (`sole-strength-sw-warranty-registration-card-online`). The SW121 half-rack book prints no warranty page at all (`sole-sw121-prints-no-warranty-page`).
