This warranty does not cover:

1. **Consequential, collateral or incidental damages** (property damage, incidental expenses). Some states bar this exclusion.
2. **Service call reimbursement** to the consumer, and to the dealer where there is no malfunction or defect, the unit is beyond the warranty or service-call reimbursement period, no component replacement is required, or the treadmill is not in ordinary household use.
3. **Damage from non-authorized service, non-original parts, or external causes** — corrosion (**RUST**), discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, **improper maintenance (see general maintenance instructions section), including lack of consistent lubrication of the deck**, inadequate power supply, natural disasters, or elemental damage (power surges, lightning storms, water damage, fire damage).
4. **Serial numbers removed or altered.**
5. **Products sold, transferred, bartered or given to a third party.**
6. **No warranty registration card on file** (SOLE may request proof of purchase).
7. **All other warranties disclaimed**, including merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose.
8. **Use in any environment other than a residential setting.**
9. **Warranties outside of North America may vary** — contact the local dealer. **Shipping charges may apply to Alaska and Hawaii customers.** In the F63-2025 book this item instead reads: Sole USA coverage is **limited to the Continental United States** and Sole USA **does not sell or service equipment in Hawaii, Alaska, or outside of the United States** — the 2026 wording (see `f63-2026-warranty-geographic-coverage`).
10. **Warranty void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment** (garage, outside, carport, etc). The F89-2023 book writes "if unit is not" (sic — missing "the").

Same in all eight 2023/2025 books (effective February 1, 2023 and February 1, 2025) except the item-9 split above. The TT8-2023 book omits the "including lack of consistent lubrication of the deck" clause from item 3 — carded as printed. The older 9-item list without items 3b and 10 is on `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-9-item-residential`; the 2026 list is on `f63-2026-warranty-exclusions`.
