| Warranty | Frame | Electronics / Brake | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | Lifetime | 10 Years | 10 Years | 2 Years |

**All four figures are the same in all six manuals**, and only the second column's heading and the printed effective date move - the row label is the bare word *Residential* on every one of them.

| Manual | Second column heading | Effective date |
|---|---|---|
| CU800 2012 | Electronics | March 1, 2012 |
| CR800 2021 | Electronics | November 8, 2021 |
| CU800 2021 | Electronics | November 12, 2021 |
| CU800ENT 2022 | EMS BRAKE | February 08, 2022 |
| CR800 2023 | Electronics | June 21, 2023 |
| CR800ENT 2023 | BRAKE | June 21, 2023 |

**A home doubles the second column and the parts period, and does not move the frame or the labor.** Against the commercial row - 5 years and 5 years - a home gets 10 and 10. Frame is Lifetime in both rows and Labor is 2 years in both (`spirit-bike-warranty-commercial-periods`).

**The CR800 2021, CU800 2021 and CR800 2023 print the second column as "10Years" with no space**, and their labor as "2 Year". The CU800 2012, CU800ENT 2022 and CR800ENT 2023 space it correctly. Ten years and two years on all six.

**Every one of the six warrants a home, including the two whose duty list never addresses a home owner.** The CU800ENT 2022 and CR800ENT 2023 head their responsibilities list NORMAL RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE FACILITY and administer the warranty "directly to a facility", yet still print this Residential row. Neither manual reconciles the two (`spirit-ct800-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`).

**The two ENT bikes print two columns this row does not have** - Console Parts 3 Years and Console Labor 1 Year, the same figures in the Residential row as in the Commercial one. Read them from `ct800ent-2022-warranty-console-periods` and never fold them into Parts 10 Years.

**Four Spirit bikes have no residential row at all.** The CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900ENT and CU900ENT warrant one Commercial row and nothing else, so this card answers nothing about them and neither does any other - their manuals state no home terms (`spirit-bike-900-warranty-commercial-periods`).

**The 2024-terms bikes changed this row.** The CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 keep a Lifetime frame at home but cut the brake to 5 Years and add Wear Items at 6 Months (`spirit-bike-2024-warranty-coverage-periods`); the CU800ENT 2024 cuts the frame itself to 10 Years (`cu800ent-2024-warranty-coverage-periods`).

**The seven Spirit elliptical manuals print this row's four figures exactly, and they are still a different fact.** The CE800, CE800ENT and CE850 residential row reads Frame Lifetime, Brake or Electronics 10 Years, Parts 10 Years, Labor 2 Years - the same numbers under the same kind of headings (`spirit-ce-warranty-residential-periods`). They are a different machine on a different product line; the agreement is a coincidence of the terms, not evidence that a bike part and an elliptical part are covered alike. Answer a bike from this card and an elliptical from that one. **The CT800 and CT850 row is different in one column** - Motor and Deck are *Lifetime* where this table's second column is 10 Years (`spirit-ct800-warranty-residential-periods`).
