Keep the bill of sale. Service under this warranty must be obtained in order:

1. Contact the selling authorized SOLE dealer. OR
2. Submit all service requests — serial number, contact information, brief problem description — online at **http://www.soletreadmills.com/serviceparts.html**.
3. Warranty questions: **1-866-780-SOLE (7653)**. Technical problems with the fitness climber: **866-MYSOLE1 (697-6531)**.
4. With no local service, SOLE repairs or replaces parts at its option within the warranty period at no charge for parts; the owner pays all transportation both ways and never ships without a **return authorization number**.
5. By mail: **SOLE Fitness, P.O. Box 2037, Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037**.

Same steps in all three books. The 2016-to-2020 E-family twin with the same steps is `sole-elliptical-warranty-service-dealer-online-serviceparts-2016-2020`; this card exists because that card's title range excludes the 2015 and 2017 effective dates printed here.
