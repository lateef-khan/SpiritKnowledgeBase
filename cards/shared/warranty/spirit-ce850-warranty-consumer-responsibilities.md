> This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household or Light Commercial use (see restrictions above), and the consumer/facility is responsible for the items listed below:

1. The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within 10 days of the original purchase to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.
2. Proper use of the elliptical trainer in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual
3. Proper installation in accordance with instructions provided with the elliptical trainer and with all local electric codes.
4. Expenses for making the elliptical trainer accessible for servicing, including any item that was not part of the elliptical trainer at the time it was shipped from the factory.
5. Damages to the elliptical trainer finish during shipping, installation or following installation.
6. Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual.

**The six items are word for word identical in all three CE850 manuals** - 2016, 2020 and 2022 - heading and lead sentence included.

**Six items, not seven. There is no grounded-power-supply duty on this list.** The CE800 2021, CE800 2023 and CE800ENT manuals carry a seventh item - "Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage, replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects in house or facility wiring" - as item 4 of their list, and it puts blown fuses and defective house wiring on the owner. **No CE850 manual prints it.** Do not quote it to a CE850 owner from `spirit-ct800-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`; the source does not impose it.

**Item 1 has no online option.** It requires the card returned to the address within 10 days. The CE800 2021 and later manuals add "or completed online"; the CE850 list does not, even though all three CE850 manuals print a REGISTER YOUR PURCHASE page offering online registration. The two halves are not reconciled in any of them - see `spirit-ce-warranty-registration`.

**"See restrictions above" points at something the 2020 and 2022 manuals no longer print.** The CE850 2016 table row reads *Light Commercial (5 Hours use or less in a non-dues paying facility)*, so the lead sentence has a restriction to point at. The 2020 and 2022 manuals relabel that row *Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities)* and keep the lead sentence unchanged, leaving the reference dangling. The five-hour cap survives only in exclusion 8 of those two - `spirit-ce-warranty-use-environment`.

Item 4 makes getting the machine to a place where it can be worked on the owner's expense, not a warranty cost (`xt-2023-warranty-owner-costs`).

This list is only half of what governs the warranty. The other half is the exclusions on the next page, `spirit-ce850-warranty-exclusions`.

## Nine Spirit XE and XG residential model years print the same six items

The **XE295** (2019 and 2021), the **XE395** 2018, the **XE395ENT**, the **XE795** (2018 and both 2021 printings), the **XE895** 2018 and the **XG400** (2019 and 2021) carry the same heading, the same lead sentence and the same six items, in the same order, with no grounded-power-supply duty - ten manuals, August 2018 to November 2021. The XE795 2018 and XE895 2018 blocks compare **100.0% identical** to the XE795 February 2021 block at word level.

**The lead sentence is not identical on all of them, and it is the sentence that says what use is covered:**

| Manual | Lead sentence |
|---|---|
| XE795 2018, XE895 2018, XE795 2021 both printings | ordinary household **or Light Commercial** use (see restrictions above), and the consumer/facility... |
| XE395 2018, XE295 2019, XG400 2019, XE295 2021, XG400 2021, XE395ENT 2021 | ordinary household use **(see restrictions above)**, and the consumer/facility... |

**The two lead sentences track the warranty table, and only for the XE795 and the XE895.** Those are the two machines whose tables print a Light Commercial row, and theirs are the two lead sentences that name Light Commercial use (`spirit-xe795-warranty-light-commercial-periods`).

**On the other six the "see restrictions above" points at nothing.** The XE395 2018, XE295 2019, XG400 2019, XE295 2021, XG400 2021 and XE395ENT 2021 tables print a Residential row and no commercial row at all, so there is no restriction above to see - and their exclusion 8 nevertheless permits a non-dues-paying facility of five hours a day that their table gives no periods for. None of the six reconciles it (`spirit-xe-warranty-use-environment`).

**The XG400 substitutes one noun, in both printings** - *E•Glide Trainer* for *elliptical trainer* in items 2, 3, 4 and 5 - because neither of its manuals ever calls the machine an elliptical.

**Four XE manuals are not on this card.** The **XE195** - in its 2019 printing as in its 2021 one - and the **XE395 2010** print **seven** items including the grounded-power-supply duty that puts blown fuses and house wiring on the owner (`spirit-xe-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-electrical-duty`); the **two 2023 manuals** print **five** and drop the registration item entirely (`spirit-xe-2023-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`).
