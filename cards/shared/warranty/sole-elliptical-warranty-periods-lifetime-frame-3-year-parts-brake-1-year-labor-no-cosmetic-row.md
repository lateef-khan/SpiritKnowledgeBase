**Climber warranty, effective August 1, 2015** (SC200-2016 book) and **effective August 1, 2017** (SC200-2017 book covering the SC200-2019, and SC300-2017 book) — same figures under both dates. The books print **no cosmetic-items row**; that is how the table reads, not an omission here.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Frame** | **Lifetime** |
| **Parts & Brake** | **3 Years** |
| **Labor** | **1 Year** |

The clock starts on the date of retail sale by receipt, or eighteen (18) months from factory shipping without one — the same rule as the other Sole books. The SERVICE paragraph's twelve-months-from-bill-of-sale labor rule agrees with the table's 1 year, so unlike the CC81 books there is no labor conflict here.

**This is not the lifetime-brake E-family term.** The brake here gets 3 years inside the combined Parts & Brake row, while the table's Frame row names the frame alone (not "Frame Weldments"). The nearest E-family twin with a 3-year brake is `sole-elliptical-warranty-periods-lifetime-frame-3-year-brake-parts-1-year-labor`.
