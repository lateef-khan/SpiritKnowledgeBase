**No.** Both manuals say so in as many words, at the end of the pre-assembly instructions:

> CAUTION: Damage to the bike during assembly is not covered as part of the limited Spirit Fitness warranty. Take care not to drop or lean the bike on its side. Carefully stand the bike up in the normal upright position on a stable surface so it will not tip over during assembly.

**The caution is in the assembly section, not the warranty section.** Neither manual's EXCLUSIONS list repeats it, so a reader who goes straight to the warranty pages will not find it. It is still the manual's own statement of what the warranty does not cover, and it is the only place either book says so.

**It names three specific ways to cause it** - dropping the bike, leaning it on its side, and standing it somewhere unstable so it tips - and both manuals immediately advise propping the frame on the large foam packaging while the stabilisers go on.

**This is separate from the finish clause in the warranty pages.** Both manuals also make the owner responsible for "damages to the fitness equipment finish during shipping, installation or following installation", as an item in the responsibilities list. The assembly caution is broader: it excludes damage to the **bike**, not only to its finish. Quote both when someone asks.

## The other six bikes of this range print no such caution

The CB900, XIC600 2018 and 2021, CIC800, CIC850 and AB950 manuals carry the same pre-assembly instructions without the CAUTION paragraph. **The AB950 does not print it even though the AB900 does** - the same machine family, six years apart. Do not read it to an AB950 owner as though their manual said it; their book excludes assembly damage nowhere, and only the finish clause in the responsibilities list applies.

Which periods each of these machines actually carries is `spirit-air-bike-warranty-periods` and `spirit-jb950-warranty-commercial-only-periods`.
