Keep your bill of sale. Service under this warranty must be obtained by following these steps, **in order**:

1. Contact your selling authorized **Spirit** dealer. **OR**
2. Contact your local authorized Spirit service organization.
3. If there is a question as to where to obtain service, contact our service department at **(870) 935-1107**.
4. **If no local service is available**, Spirit will repair or replace the parts, at Spirit's option, within the warranty period **at no charge for parts**. **All transportation costs, both to our factory and upon return to the owner, are the responsibility of the owner.** The owner is responsible for adequate packaging upon return to Spirit. Spirit is not responsible for damages in shipping. Make all freight damage claims with the appropriate freight carrier. **DO NOT SHIP ANY UNIT TO OUR FACTORY WITHOUT A RETURN AUTHORIZATION NUMBER. All units arriving without a return authorization number will be refused.**
5. For any further information, or to contact our service department by mail, send your correspondence to:

> Spirit Manufacturing, Inc.
> P.O. Box 2037
> Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037

**Five steps, not six**, and word for word identical in the two books - a word-level comparison differs only in an apostrophe the conversion dropped.

## What this step 4 does and does not charge

**It charges all transportation both ways** if the unit goes back to the factory, and it charges nothing else by name.

| Charge | This manual |
|---|---|
| Freight to and from the factory | **the owner's**, in as many words |
| Adequate packaging for a return, and any freight damage claim | the owner's |
| Parts themselves, when no local service is available | **no charge** |
| Shipping and handling on a replacement part | **not mentioned** |
| A trip charge for in-home service | **not mentioned** |
| An additional charge beyond 100 miles from a service center | **not mentioned** |
| Preauthorization of a repair | **not mentioned** |

**Do not quote a trip charge or a 100-mile surcharge to the owner of one of these six machines.** Those sentences enter the Spirit range with the 2010 XE395 manual and run through every later XE and XG book (`xt-2023-warranty-owner-costs`). Neither is printed here, and quoting one would invent a cost the source does not impose. Exclusion 2 does refuse **service call reimbursement to the consumer** (`spirit-xe-2005-warranty-exclusions`).

**These two books are also the only XE manuals that promise parts at no charge**, and they attach it to a condition - *if no local service is available*. From 2010 on the equivalent sentence is a different promise: Spirit Fitness's obligation is limited to repairing or replacing the product through an authorized service center, with all repairs preauthorized (`xt-2023-warranty-service-steps`).

**The dealer and the return address are named "Spirit", not "Spirit Fitness".** Steps 1 and 2 look for an authorized **Spirit** dealer or service organization, and step 5's address is **Spirit Manufacturing, Inc.** The service department number, **(870) 935-1107**, is the same one every later Spirit manual prints.

**Neither book prints a general customer-care number, an email address or a street address** anywhere in this knowledge base - the P.O. Box in step 5 is the only postal address either gives. Do not read one out of a sibling manual; if you give one, say which manual it came from.

The opening paragraph dates the period from the bill of sale or the serial number, and unlike every later Spirit manual it calls that period the **warranty** period rather than the **labor** warranty period - see `spirit-xe-2005-warranty-two-year-base-period` for the table it is meant to date, and note that the two do not agree.
