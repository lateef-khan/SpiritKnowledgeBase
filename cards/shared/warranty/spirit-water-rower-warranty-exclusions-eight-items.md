**Eight items, the same eight in both water rower manuals** - the CRW900 and the CRW800H2O:

1. **CONSEQUENTIAL, COLLATERAL, OR INCIDENTAL DAMAGES** such as property damage and incidental expenses resulting from any breach of this written or any implied warranty. *Some states do not allow this exclusion, so it may not apply to you.*
2. **Service call reimbursement to the consumer.** Reimbursement to the dealer is also excluded where it does not involve malfunction or defects in workmanship or material, for units beyond the warranty period, for units beyond the service call reimbursement period, or for fitness equipment not requiring component replacement.
3. **Damages caused by anyone other than an authorized Spirit Fitness service company**, by parts other than original Spirit Fitness parts, or by external causes - corrosion, discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, or acts of God.
4. **Products whose original serial number has been removed or altered.**
5. **Products that have been sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party.**
6. **Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase** if no warranty record exists for the product.
7. This warranty is **expressly in lieu of all other warranties** expressed or implied, including merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose.
8. **Product used in prisons and correctional facilities.**

**Item 2 stops one clause short of every air rower manual's, and the missing clause matters.** It ends at *not requiring component replacement* and never adds *or not in ordinary household or light commercial use*. On the air rowers that closing clause is the only place the warranty restricts **where** the machine may be used, and these two books drop it - then print no use-environment item at item 8 either. **On a water rower the only environment excluded anywhere is a prison.** A dues-paying gym, a hotel, a school: the manual bars none of them.

**Not registering is not an exclusion here.** These two carry only the proof-of-purchase sentence at item 6. The air rower manuals add a separate item excluding any product with no registration card on file, which these do not: `spirit-rower-warranty-exclusions-nine-items`.

**There is no outside-the-United-States item.** The air rower and ergometer lists close with *Warranties outside of the United States may vary*; these eight do not. The same restriction still reaches the owner through the closing paragraph on the SERVICE page - *This warranty applies only in the 48 contiguous United States* - so the answer is unchanged, but it is not in the list.

**Prisons are excluded twice on these two**, as item 8 and again as an asterisked footnote under the warranty table: `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.

**Water damage and the tank are not excluded here.** Neither manual excludes water, leakage, corrosion of the tank or the seals as such. The CRW800H2O does void the warranty for two specific acts, but those are printed in the tank-filling instructions rather than in this list: `crw800h2o-warranty-void-chlorine-and-overfilling`. The CRW900 prints no such rule anywhere - checked across the whole document and on a 300 dpi render of its tank-filling page.
