**Treadmill warranty, effective October 15, 2004.** The head warrants all parts for 5 years from retail sale (receipt, or 18 months from factory shipping, whichever comes first), then extends some components:

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Drive Motor** | **30 Years** |
| **Deck Wear** | **Lifetime** |
| **Electronics / Belt / Rollers** | **5 Years** |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **All Other Components** | **5 Years** |

**These two books print no Labor row in the table** — unlike the F80-family book of the same year, which prints Labor 2 Years (see `sole-treadmill-warranty-periods-30-year-motor-2-year-labor-2004-2007`). The SERVICE section still says twelve months from the bill of sale, or eighteen months from factory shipping by serial number, establishes the warranty period should service be required.

When the clock starts is separate; see `sole-treadmill-warranty-start-date-receipt-or-18-months-shipping`. What is not covered is on `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-7-item-2004-2010`.
