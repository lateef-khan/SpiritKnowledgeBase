This warranty applies only to products in **ordinary household use**. The consumer is responsible for:

1. **Proper use of the adjustable dumbbell** per this manual, including maintenance.
2. **Expenses for making the adjustable dumbbell accessible for servicing**, including any item not part of the adjustable dumbbell when shipped from the factory.
3. **Damages to the adjustable dumbbell finish** during shipping, installation or following installation.

No registration-card duty, no installation or power-supply duties — three items only, identical in all three books.
