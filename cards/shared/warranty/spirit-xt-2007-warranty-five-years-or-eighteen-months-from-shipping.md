**Spirit Manufacturing Inc. warrants all its treadmills' parts for 5 years from the date of retail sale, as determined by the sale receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date, whichever comes first.** That is the base term the 2008 dealer service manual prints above its per-model tables; the tables then extend it component by component (see the per-machine cards below). The page before adds that **all warranties are valid from the original date of purchase by the original purchaser**.

**The 18-month clock is the one a dealer has to watch.** A machine that sat in stock for a year and a half before it was sold is out of the base term on the day it is sold, whatever the sale receipt says. The later XT owner's manuals keep this two-date rule only for the labour period (`xt-2023-warranty-labor-period`); here it governs the parts term as well.

**What Spirit undertakes** is to provide new or remanufactured parts, at Spirit's option, and technical support to its independent dealers and servicing organisations. In the absence of a dealer or service organisation the warranty is administered by Spirit directly to the consumer.

**The extended periods differ by machine**, and one of them differs by use:

| Machine | Card |
|---|---|
| XT175 | `xt175-2007-warranty-home-periods` |
| XT275 | `xt275-2007-warranty-home-periods` |
| XT375, XT475, XT675 at home | `spirit-xt-2007-warranty-home-periods-xt375-xt475-xt675` |
| XT675 in commercial use | `xt675-2007-warranty-commercial-periods` |

This is a dealer document: it prints no consumer exclusion list, no registration card and no transfer rule. The dealer-facing policy - what is not covered, what Spirit pays for labour and how parts come back - is on `spirit-xt-2007-warranty-what-the-dealer-policy-does-not-cover`, `spirit-xt-2007-warranty-labor-reimbursement-80-dollar-flat-fee` and `spirit-xt-2007-warranty-parts-returns-and-30-day-billing`.
