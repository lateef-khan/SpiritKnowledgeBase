**Ten days either way, but the deadline is counted from a different event and the method
is not the same.**

| Manuals | Deadline runs from | Method |
|---|---|---|
| All CSD, CSS, CSF and CSI books (36) | the **delivery date** | online only - "must be completed online within 10 days of the delivery date" |
| ST800DR3, ST800FI, ST800FT (3) | the **original purchase** | return the **registration card** to the address on the card, **or** complete it online |

**On the thirty-six current books there is no card.** Their product registration page
prints a QR code instead:

> Use your smartphone to scan the QR code above to quickly and easily register your new
> Spirit Fitness unit. You can also go to spiritfitness.com/warranty under the Support tab
> to register online.

The same page gives blank fields to record the serial number, date of purchase, dealer or
place of purchase, and date of installation.

**On the three ST800 books the card is the primary route** - "The self-addressed product
registration card must be completed in full and returned to Spirit Fitness" - with
www.spiritfitness.com/warranty under the Support tab as the alternative. They also ask
the owner to record the serial number, which on the ST800FI is at the base of the bench.

**Missing the deadline has a stated consequence.** Exclusion 6 excludes products with no
warranty registration on file, and reserves the right to request proof of purchase where
no record exists: `spirit-strength-warranty-exclusions-eight-items`.

Registration is the first of five duties the facility owns:
`spirit-strength-warranty-facility-responsibilities`.
