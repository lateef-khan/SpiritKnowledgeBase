**Rower warranty, effective February 1, 2019** (SR500-2019 book) and **effective February 1, 2022** (SR500-2022 book) — same figures under both dates.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **All Other Components** | **3 Years** |
| **Labor** | **1 Year** |

Below the table both books print a dangling cosmetic footnote — "*Cosmetic items including, but not limited to the following: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals" — with **no cosmetic row and no period attached** (verified on the render of both pages). Carded as printed: no cosmetic cover length exists on these pages.

The SERVICE paragraph's twelve-months-from-bill-of-sale labor rule agrees with the table's 1 year, so there is no labor conflict here. The 2023 book shortens parts to 2 years: `sole-rower-warranty-periods-lifetime-frame-2-year-parts-1-year-labor-2023`.
