**Two starting points, and which one applies depends on the book.**

| Manuals | The clock starts at |
|---|---|
| All CSD, CSS, CSF and CSI books (36) | the **date of installation**, as determined by sale receipt |
| ST800DR3, ST800FI, ST800FT (3) | the **date of sale**, as determined by sale receipt |

**With no sales receipt, both fall back on the same rule:** eighteen (18) months from
the original factory shipping date. The shipping date is read off the serial number.

That fallback is a ceiling rather than a grace period. A machine that sat in a warehouse
for a year and then has no receipt is left with six months of a one-year term, not
eighteen.

**Do not confuse the warranty start date with the labour clock**, which is a separate
sentence in the SERVICE paragraph and uses *twelve* months rather than the component
period: `spirit-strength-warranty-labor-period`.

**Nor with the registration deadline**, which runs from the delivery date on the
thirty-six and from the original purchase on the three ST800 books:
`spirit-strength-warranty-registration`.

Spirit Fitness may ask for proof of purchase when no registration record exists, which
is what makes the receipt worth keeping:
`spirit-strength-warranty-exclusions-eight-items`.
