Keep your bill of sale. Twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale, or eighteen (18) months from the factory shipping date as determined by the serial number, establishes the labor warranty period.

Service must be obtained by following these steps, **in order**:

1. Contact your selling authorized SOLE dealer, **or**
2. Submit all service requests — serial number, contact information and a brief description of the problem — online at `www.soletreadmills.com/technical.php?p=service`.
3. For questions about the product or the warranty contact SOLE Fitness at **1-866-780-SOLE (7653)**. For a technical problem contact SOLE technical service at **866-MYSOLE1 (697-6531)**.
4. If no local service is available, SOLE will repair or replace the parts, at SOLE's option, within the warranty period at no charge for parts. **All transportation costs, both to the factory and on return to the owner, are the owner's responsibility.** The owner is responsible for adequate packaging on return. SOLE is not responsible for damage that occurs during shipping; make all freight damage claims with the freight carrier. **Do not ship any unit to the factory without a return authorization number — units arriving without one will be refused.**
5. For any further information, or to contact the service department by mail:

```
SOLE Fitness
P.O. Box 2037
Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037
```

**The web address in step 2 is printed corrupted in the SB900 manuals.** The SB900 2014/2016, 2019, 2020 and 2021 manuals print `www.soleindoor cycles.com/technical.php?p=service` — a domain with a space in it, left behind by a find-and-replace of "treadmill" with "indoor cycle" run over the SOLE boilerplate. The SB700, B94, R92, LCB and LCR manuals of the same years print `www.soletreadmills.com/technical.php?p=service`. Neither form is the address the later manuals use.

The 2016 and 2019 manuals replaced step 2 with `http://www.soletreadmills.com/serviceparts.html`, and the 2023 manuals dropped the mailing address and both older phone numbers in favour of **866-780-7653**. Use the steps printed in the manual that shipped with the machine, and check the current SOLE service page before quoting a URL to a customer.
