| Frame & Brake | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|
| Lifetime | 3 Years | 1 Year |

**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the Manufacturer's Limited Warranty page of the SB4500 and SB500 recumbent bikes and the RSX1500 seated stepper. The two bikes head the labor column *In-Home Labor*; the RSX1500 heads it *Labor*. The frame column is *Frame & Brake* in all three - the magnetic brake (the resistance mechanism) is covered for life alongside the frame. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**Two of these books exist in two printings, and the table did not change between them.** The SB500 book of 2020 (stamp `SB500_20200706`, *Effective July 06, 2020*) is 98.5% the SB500 book of 2014 (scan, stamp `SB500_20140811`, *Effective August 1, 2014*); the RSX1500 book of 2021 (stamp `RSX1500_20210712`, *© 2021*) still prints *Effective August 21, 2018* on its warranty page, while the 2017 scan (stamp `RSX1500_20171222`) prints *Effective December 20, 2017*. Same figures, same responsibilities, same exclusions in each pair; only the effective date moved. A 2021 RSX1500 therefore carries 2018 warranty terms, and the 2021 stamp is a re-export date.

The three books print the registration card duty and the nine-item exclusion list (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-store-display-and-unregistered-products`), the full SERVICE steps with the trip charge (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-service-steps-dealer-service-organization-then-the-service-department`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-owner-pays-freight-parts-shipping-and-trip-charges`), and the electrical page's ten-percent voltage sentence (`xterra-warranty-what-the-electrical-and-console-pages-say-is-not-covered`). The SB4.5r prints the same frame-and-brake column with five years on parts (`sb45r-2013-warranty-periods-frame-and-brake-lifetime-parts-5-years-labor-1-year`), the FS5.9e with seven (`fs59e-2014-warranty-periods-frame-and-brake-lifetime-parts-7-years-labor-1-year`). The Spirit recumbent range prints a different table (`spirit-bike-warranty-residential-periods`).
