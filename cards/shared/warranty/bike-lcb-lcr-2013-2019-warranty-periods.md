The same schedule is printed in the 2013, 2016 and 2019 manuals. Only the effective date changes.

| Component | Residential warranty (all service performed in-home) | Light commercial warranty (all service performed in-facility) |
|---|---|---|
| Parts/Electronics | 5 Years | 3 Years |
| Frame | Lifetime | Lifetime |
| Labor | 2 Years | 1 Year |

| Machines | Effective date |
|---|---|
| lcb-2013, lcr-2013 | August 1, 2012 |
| lcb-2016, lcr-2016 | February 1, 2016 |
| lcb-2019, lcr-2019 | February 1, 2019 |

Light commercial means a **non-dues paying facility with 5 hours of fitness bike use or less per day**, as the consumer-responsibilities paragraph defines it.

SOLE's responsibilities include providing new or remanufactured parts, at SOLE's option, and technical support to independent dealers and servicing organizations. Where there is no dealer or service organization, SOLE administers the warranty directly to the consumer.

**These are not the B94 and R92 periods.** Those manuals print a single residential schedule of Parts/Electronics 3 Years, Frame Lifetime, Labor 1 Year, and have no light commercial column at all.
