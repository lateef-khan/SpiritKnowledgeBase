This warranty applies only to products in **ordinary household use**, and the consumer is responsible for:

1. Returning the **warranty registration card within 10 days of the original purchase** — or, outside the 10 day period, registering with **proof of purchase** (receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation).
2. **Proper use of the treadmill** per this manual, including general maintenance (belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under the motor cover, lubrication) — the book reads "treadmill" with treadmill maintenance examples twice (items 2 and 3 are duplicates, sic), carded as printed on a rower page.
3. Same treadmill-use paragraph a second time, verbatim (sic).
4. **Proper installation** per the instructions with the Rower and all local electric codes.
5. **Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage**, replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring.
6. **Expenses for making the Rower accessible for servicing**, including any item not part of the Rower when shipped from the factory.
7. **Damages to the Rower finish** during shipping, installation or following installation.
8. **Routine maintenance** as specified in this manual (all components under General Maintenance).
9. **Providing pictures or video** of issues by email as requested, and assisting with troubleshooting/diagnosing before a home visit.

The 2023 treadmill twin with the same photo duty is `sole-treadmill-warranty-responsibilities-8-item-photo-duty-2023`. The SR500 books keep the older seven-duty list: `sole-rower-warranty-responsibilities-7-item-2019-2022`.
