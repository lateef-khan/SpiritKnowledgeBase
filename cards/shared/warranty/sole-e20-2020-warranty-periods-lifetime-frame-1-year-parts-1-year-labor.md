**Elliptical warranty on the Sole E20-2020, effective February 1, 2016** as printed — a stale date carried over into the 2020 book. Every row verified character by character on the render (PDF p. 20).

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Labor** | **1 Year** |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Brake** | **1 Year** |
| **Cosmetic Items** * | **1 Year** |
| **All Other Components** | **1 Year** |

\* Cosmetic items including, but not limited to: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.

Against the E20-2016 table this book lengthens labor from 90 days to 1 year and the frame from 1 year to lifetime, and leaves brake, cosmetic and all-other at 1 year. See `sole-e20-2016-warranty-periods-1-year-frame-90-day-labor` for the 2016 terms. The book's service steps also merge the no-local-service paragraph into step 3; see `sole-elliptical-warranty-service-dealer-online-serviceparts-2016-2020`.
