**Two acts void the warranty on these water rowers, and neither is on the warranty page.** Both are printed on the *How to Fill and Empty the Tank* page.

| Book | Water | Level | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| ERG550W (erg550w-2023) | *Fill the tank only with tap water. Never use pool chlorine or chlorine bleach. This will damage the tank and **void the warranty**.* Add a Water Purification Tablet every 6 months or as needed. | *The water level gauge is on the side of the tank. The maximum fill level is 6. Never fill it over this limit. Filling the tank over this limit will **void the warranty**.* | PDF p. 15 (printed 13) |
| ERG600W (erg600w-2021) | the same two sentences | the same, maximum level 6 | PDF p. 14 (printed 12) |
| ERG650W (erg650w-2021) | the same two sentences | the same, maximum level 6 | PDF p. 13 (printed 10) |
| ERG750W (erg750w-2025) | *Only distilled water or pure water can be injected into the water tank. Also, can add the water purification tablets. Do not use inferior water purification tablets or bleach. They will damage the water tank and are **exempt from the warranty**.* Replace the tablet every six months. | *Do not exceed the water label. Water added beyond the maximum water level is **exempt from the warranty**.* The label's Max is 12.7 litres (3.35 gallons). | PDF p. 14 (printed 13) |

**On the three older water rowers level 6 is also the maximum resistance** - *water level 1 is the lowest resistance, level 6 the highest* - so adding water past the gauge voids the warranty for nothing the machine offers. The three books tell the owner to buy water cleaning tablets from www.xterrafitness.com or 1-(870) 333-5500 (`xterra-warranty-support-contacts-which-phone-number-each-rower-and-strength-book-prints`); the ERG550W adds the six-month interval the ERG600W and ERG650W leave out, and the ERG750W says *distilled or pure water* where the older three say *tap water*.

**"Voids the warranty" is stronger than anything the warranty page says.** The nine exclusions name abuse, misuse and improper maintenance in general terms and never chlorine, bleach, tablets or a fill level (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-store-display-and-unregistered-products`, `fb180-2025-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-residential-only-with-proof-of-purchase-and-outside-us-items`). A reader who only reads the warranty pages never sees these sentences.

**The ERG800W prints no such sentence.** Its *Water Tank & Storage Warning* page says to use clean and filtered water, distilled where the supply is poor, to change the water each half year and to fill between the Min and Max marks - and states no consequence. Checked with a loose search for *void*, *warrant*, *exempt* and *covered* over the whole book and in the OCR of the page: the only warranty sentence outside its warranty pages is the electrical page's ten-percent voltage clause (`xterra-warranty-what-the-electrical-and-console-pages-say-is-not-covered`). Do not tell an ERG800W owner the tank voids anything; the book does not say it.

The Spirit CRW800H2O water rower prints the ERG550W sentences word for word (`crw800h2o-warranty-void-chlorine-and-overfilling`); the routine tank care behind them is on the maintenance cards.
