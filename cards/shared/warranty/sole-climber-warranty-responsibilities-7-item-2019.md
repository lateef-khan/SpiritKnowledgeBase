This warranty applies only to products in **ordinary household use**, and the consumer is responsible for:

1. Returning the **warranty registration card within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.
2. **Proper use of the climber** per this manual.
3. **Proper installation** per the instructions with the climber and all local electric codes.
4. **Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage**, replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring.
5. **Expenses for making the climber accessible for servicing**, including any item not part of the climber when shipped from the factory.
6. **Damages to the climber finish** during shipping, installation or following installation.
7. **Routine maintenance** of the unit as specified in this manual.

This is the older seven-duty printing. The CC81-2026 book replaces item 1's plain deadline with a proof-of-purchase alternative and replaces item 7 with a photo-and-troubleshooting duty: `cc81-2026-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`. The SC200/SC300 climber books print these same seven duties; being elliptical-line machines they join the elliptical twin `sole-elliptical-warranty-responsibilities-7-item-2011-2019`.
