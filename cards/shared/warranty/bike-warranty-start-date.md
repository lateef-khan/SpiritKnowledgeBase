SOLE warrants the parts for the period listed in the machine's warranty table, counted **from the date of retail sale, as determined by the sale receipt**.

**In the absence of a sales receipt** the period is counted from **eighteen (18) months after the original factory shipping date**.

This wording is identical in every Sole bike and indoor cycle owner's manual from 2011 to 2023. Only the periods in the table change from year to year and model to model.

The **labor** warranty has its own clock, stated separately in the SERVICE paragraph — twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale, or eighteen (18) months from the factory shipping date as determined by the serial number.

The B74 and R72 2015 owner's manual prints no warranty section at all, so it states no start date for those two machines.
