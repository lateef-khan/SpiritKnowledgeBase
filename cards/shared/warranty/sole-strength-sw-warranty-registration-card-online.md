All four SW books carry the same Product Registration page:

- **Record the serial number** in the space provided ("RECORD YOUR SERIAL NUMBER ... in the space provided below"), with spaces for the **dealer name, dealer telephone number and purchase date**.
- "The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to SOLE." Or register online at **www.soletreadmill.com under the support tab** (this family prints the host without the s — carded as printed).
- No registration deadline is printed on this page; the SW100/SW155/SW180 warranty pages only require a registration on file, with proof of purchase as fallback.

The SW121 book has this page but no warranty page at all (`sole-sw121-prints-no-warranty-page`).
