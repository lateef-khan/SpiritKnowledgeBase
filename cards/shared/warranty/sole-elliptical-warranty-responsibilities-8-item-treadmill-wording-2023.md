All five 2023 books print the same eight duties, copied from the 2023 **treadmill** warranty page and verified on the render — including wording that cannot apply to an elliptical:

1. Registration card within 10 days, **or late registration with proof of purchase** (receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation) — the late-registration allowance is new in 2023.
2. "**Proper use of the treadmill** in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual, **including general maintenance (i.e. belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under motor cover, and lubrication**. See all components under General Maintenance section)" — quoted verbatim; an elliptical has no belt, no deck, and no motor cover to vacuum under.
3. Proper installation per the instructions with the elliptical and all local electric codes.
4. Proper connection to a grounded power supply, fuses, loose connections or house-wiring defects.
5. Expenses for making the elliptical accessible for servicing.
6. Damages to the elliptical finish during shipping, installation or following installation.
7. Routine maintenance as specified in this manual (see All Components under General Maintenance).
8. **Provide pictures or video** of any issues by email as the service technician requests, and **assist with troubleshooting/diagnosing prior to a home visit** — the photo duty is new in 2023.

**Ruling:** the owner's manual governs what the customer sees, so the duties are followed as printed — register (late with proof if needed), install and wire correctly, maintain per the General Maintenance section, and supply photos when asked. The treadmill references in duty 2 are boilerplate carried over from the treadmill page (the same eight duties sit on `sole-treadmill-warranty-responsibilities-8-item-photo-duty-2023`); they impose no belt-tracking or motor-cover duty on an elliptical because the machine has neither. The matching ten-item exclusions are `sole-elliptical-warranty-exclusions-10-item-treadmill-wording-2023`.
