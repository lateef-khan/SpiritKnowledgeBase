| Frame | Parts |
|---|---|
| 5 Years | 1 Year |

**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the Manufacturer's Limited Warranty page of these five books - the SB2.5r recumbent bike and the FS1.5, FS2.5, FS3.0 and FS3.5 ellipticals. **The table has two columns.** No Labor column is printed, so the page states no labor term either way; it neither promises labor nor says *None* as the SB150, SB250, EU100, EU150 and FS150 pages do (`xterra-warranty-periods-frame-1-year-parts-1-year-labor-none`). The only labor sentence on the page is the SERVICE paragraph's *twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale ... establishes the warranty period should service be required*, the Dyaco boilerplate every Xterra book prints (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`); do not read it as a labor term the table left out. The periods run from the date of retail sale on a sales receipt, or eighteen months from the factory shipping date without one.

**Five years on the frame is the longest fixed frame term in the Xterra range**; the books above and below it print one year (`xterra-bike-warranty-periods-frame-1-year-parts-90-days-labor-none`, `xterra-warranty-periods-frame-1-year-parts-1-year-labor-none`) or lifetime (`xterra-bike-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-1-year-labor-1-year`, `xterra-warranty-periods-frame-and-brake-lifetime-parts-3-years-labor-1-year`, `sb45r-2013-warranty-periods-frame-and-brake-lifetime-parts-5-years-labor-1-year`, `fs59e-2014-warranty-periods-frame-and-brake-lifetime-parts-7-years-labor-1-year`, `sb600-2023-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`).

**The FS3.5 book is 87% the FS2.5 book** and the two print the same warranty page with the same effective date, July 06, 2020; the SB2.5r shares that date. The FS1.5 and FS3.0 carry their own dates.

All five print the eight-item exclusion list with no service-call item (`xterra-warranty-exclusions-eight-items-no-service-call-reimbursement-item`) - the SB2.5r numbers the last two items the other way round - and a step 4 that names no trip charge (`xterra-warranty-owner-pays-freight-and-parts-shipping-no-trip-charge-printed`); all five require the registration card within ten days (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover`). The Spirit ellipticals' residential table is `spirit-ce-warranty-residential-periods`.

**The ERG400 rower prints the same two-column table** - *Frame 5 Years / Parts 1 Year*, no Labor column - on a page *Effective August 1, 2012*, the oldest effective date in the Xterra range, in a book stamped *ERG400_20150205*: the stamp names the book, the 2012 date is the term. It is a scan and its page was read from a 110 dpi render as well as the OCR. Like the five above it prints the eight-item exclusion list and the shortened step 4 with no trip charge, at (870) 336-4286 (`xterra-warranty-support-contacts-which-phone-number-each-rower-and-strength-book-prints`).
