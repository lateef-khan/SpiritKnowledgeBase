**NORMAL RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE CONSUMER** - *This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household use, and the consumer is responsible for the items listed below:*

1. The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within **10 days** of the original purchase to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.
2. Proper use of the treadmill in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual
3. **Proper installation** in accordance with instructions provided with the treadmill and with all local electric codes.
4. Proper connection to a **grounded** power supply of sufficient voltage, replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring.
5. Expenses for making the treadmill accessible for servicing, including any item that was not part of the treadmill at the time it was shipped from the factory.
6. Damages to the treadmill finish during shipping, installation or following installation.
7. **Routine maintenance** of this unit as specified in this manual.

**Four books print this list word for word**: TRX2500, TRX3500, TRX4500, TRX5500. It is the older five-item Xterra list (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-with-a-registration-card`) with items 3 and 7 added and *grounded* inserted in item 4; it is also, item for item, the Sole 2026 list minus Sole's eighth item about photographs and video (`f65-2026-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`). No online registration URL is printed in item 1; the registration page of these four books carries the URL instead (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover`).

The nine exclusions under this list are `xterra-trx-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-floor-models-corrosion-and-discoloration`; what *routine maintenance* means for the deck and belt is spelled out in the maintenance chapter, whose warranty sentences are collected in `xterra-treadmill-warranty-what-the-maintenance-and-electrical-pages-say-is-not-covered`.
