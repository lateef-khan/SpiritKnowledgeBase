The dealer manual's Warranty Policy page lists, under **What is Not Covered**:

1. Failures created due to improper installation or maintenance.
2. Repairs requiring only simple adjustments, fuses, or calibrations.
3. Damages sustained from shipping - freight claim must be filed with your carrier.
4. Repairs that are made beyond the warranty period.
5. Repairs where the customer is paying the base labor fee to the dealer.
6. Units that are dealer stock or floor models.
7. Trips for diagnostic visit.
8. Residential units in a commercial setting.

**This is a dealer list, and it reads differently from the consumer exclusion lists in the later owner's manuals.** Items 2, 5, 6 and 7 are about what Spirit will pay a dealer for - an adjustment, a fuse or a calibration earns no labour credit, a diagnostic trip earns none, and a machine still on the dealer's floor earns none - rather than about what a customer can claim. The consumer-facing nine-item list that the 2010 and 2015 XT owner's manuals print (`xt-2015-warranty-exclusions`) is not in this book, and nothing here mentions consequential damages, serial-number removal, transfer to a third party or registration.

**Item 8 is the only use restriction the 2007 books print.** Only the XT675 has a commercial table (`xt675-2007-warranty-commercial-periods`); an XT175, XT275, XT375 or XT475 in a facility is a residential unit in a commercial setting and is not covered. The 2008 dealer manual for the XBR25 and XBR55 recumbent bikes prints the same eight items word for word, and its warranty table has a "Home use" column only (`xbr25-2007-warranty-home-periods`, `xbr55-2007-warranty-home-periods`), so item 8 excludes either bike from any commercial setting. The 2008 dealer manual for the XE100-XE500 ellipticals prints the eight items word for word too, and its XE ELLIPTICAL WARRANTY table has no commercial column either (`spirit-xe-2007-warranty-periods-five-year-components`, `xe300-2007-warranty-periods-ten-year-components`), so all five ellipticals are residential units under item 8.

The three further conditions under which labour credit is refused - parts not returned within 30 days, paperwork incomplete, work not completed - sit with the $80 fee on `spirit-xt-2007-warranty-labor-reimbursement-80-dollar-flat-fee`.
