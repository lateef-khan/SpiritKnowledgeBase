The SRVO Complete owner's manual (SRB101 Ver.A) prints **no warranty page and no warranty terms**. Proven four ways: the table of contents runs Product Registration, Important Precautions, Important Safeguards, SRVO complete item checklist, Assembly/Folding & Unfolding — no warranty chapter; a loose-word search finds "warranty" nowhere and "warranties" once, in the congratulations boilerplate ("backed by one of the most comprehensive warranties available"); PDF page 2, the only page without an OCR supplement, is blank on the render; and the book ends at printed page 8 with the 866-697-6531 support line.

The only warranty text touching this hardware is the seminar's bench cover (1-year bench and accessories, 90-day cosmetic): `srvo-bench-warranty`. What ships in the box is on `sole-srvo-specs-complete-item-checklist`.
