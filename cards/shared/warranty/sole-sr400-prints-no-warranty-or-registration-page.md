The thin SR400 owner's manual prints **no warranty page, no warranty terms, no registration page, and no support number**. Proven four ways: zero hits for "warranty", "guarantee", "registration", "service call" or "return author" across the full text; a spacing-tolerant search finds "Fit" only as assembly verbs ("Fit the front stabilizer"); all 17 PDF pages are accounted for — cover, parts-list drawing, assembly figures, console operation, the P/N parts list, and the exploded view (PDF page 15, verified on the render); and the book simply ends at the parts list with no back-page support block.

For SR500/SR550 warranty terms see the rower warranty cards; nothing on those cards comes from this book.
