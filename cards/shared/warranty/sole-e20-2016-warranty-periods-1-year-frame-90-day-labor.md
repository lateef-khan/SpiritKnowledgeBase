**Elliptical warranty on the Sole E20-2016, effective February 1, 2016.** Every row verified character by character on the render (PDF p. 22); the OCR layer agrees.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Labor** | **90 Days** |
| **Frame Weldments** | **1 Year** |
| **Brake** | **1 Year** |
| **Cosmetic Items** * | **1 Year** |
| **All Other Components** | **1 Year** |

\* Cosmetic items including, but not limited to: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.

**Contradiction, ruled:** the SERVICE paragraph on the same page still says "Twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale ... establishes the labor warranty period". The table is the more specific statement of the labor term, so this card follows the table's 90 days and records the SERVICE sentence as boilerplate carried over from the 1-year-labor books. The E20-2020 book revises the frame to lifetime and labor to 1 year: `sole-e20-2020-warranty-periods-lifetime-frame-1-year-parts-1-year-labor`.
