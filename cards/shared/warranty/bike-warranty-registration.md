The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card **within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.

The 2023 manuals add a fallback: outside that 10 day period **the warranty can still be registered if you have proof of purchase** — a receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation.

| Manuals | Rule |
|---|---|
| 2011 to 2021 (b94-2011 to b94-2019, r92-2011 to r92-2019, lcb-2011 to lcb-2019, lcr-2011 to lcr-2019, all sb700, sb900-2014 to sb900-2022) | Registration card within 10 days. No fallback is stated. |
| 2023 (b94-2023, r92-2023, lcb-2023, lcr-2023, sb900-2023, sb1200-2023) | Registration card within 10 days, **or** registration afterwards with proof of purchase. |

**This fact is printed in two places in every manual, and the second half is the one that bites.** It is item 1 of the consumer's responsibilities, and it returns as exclusion 6: products that **do not have a warranty registration card on file at SOLE** are not covered, and *SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product*.

So the older manuals give no written path back if the card was never sent, only SOLE's stated right to ask for proof of purchase; the 2023 manuals turn that into an explicit alternative.
