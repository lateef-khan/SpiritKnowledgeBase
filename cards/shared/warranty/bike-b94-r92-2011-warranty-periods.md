Effective **January 1, 2011**. The warranty period runs from the date of retail sale as determined by the sales receipt, or, with no receipt, from eighteen (18) months after the original factory shipping date.

One schedule is printed, headed **B94 or R92**, in this order:

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| Labor | 2 Years |
| Frame Weldments | Lifetime |
| Brake | Lifetime |
| Cosmetic Items* | 90 Days |
| All Other Components | 5 Years |

\* Cosmetic items including, but not limited to the following: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.

SOLE's responsibilities include providing new or remanufactured parts, at SOLE's option, and technical support to independent dealers and servicing organizations. Where there is no dealer or service organization, SOLE administers the warranty directly to the consumer.

**This schedule exists only in the 2011 manual.** The 2013, 2016 and 2019 manuals for the same two models replace it with Parts/Electronics 3 Years, Frame Lifetime, Labor 1 Year — no Brake row, no Cosmetic Items row, and one year of labor instead of two.
