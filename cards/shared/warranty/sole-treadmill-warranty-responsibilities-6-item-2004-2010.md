This warranty applies only to products in **ordinary household use**. The consumer is responsible for items 1–6 of `sole-treadmill-warranty-responsibilities-7-item-household` — 10-day registration card, proper use including belt tracking, proper installation, grounded supply and house wiring, accessibility expenses, finish damage — **with no seventh routine-maintenance item**. Identical across all twelve books (effective October 15, 2004, January 1, 2007 and January 1, 2008); only Sole/SOLE capitalisation varies.
