All six 2025 medical bike manuals print **one** warranty row, and it is the same row:

| Warranty | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial | 5 Years | 1 Year |

\*Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage - printed above the table, and again as exclusion 8 (`spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`).

**There is no Frame, Brake or Wear Items column and no second row.** Parts and labor are the only two periods these manuals state. Do not answer a frame or brake question from another Spirit bike table: the CR900 and CU900 2025 warrant the frame for lifetime and the brake 5 years (`spirit-bike-2024-warranty-coverage-periods`), the CR800 and CU800 commercial row reads frame lifetime and electronics or brake 5 years (`spirit-bike-warranty-commercial-periods`). None of those figures is printed for these six machines.

**There is no residential or home-use table in any of the six.** The section is headed WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL and it is the only table printed - the same one-row shape as the 2026 4.0T, 7.0T and 8.0T treadmills (`spirit-2026t-warranty-commercial-periods`), the 2025 steppers (`spirit-stepper-2025-warranty-parts-labor`) and the 8.5UE (`85ue-2025-warranty-commercial-parts-five-years-labor-one-year`).

Only the effective date and the product name change between the six:

| Manual | Effective date | Revision |
|---|---|---|
| 4.0R Recumbent Bike | October 03, 2025 | 10.03.25 |
| 4.0U Upright Bike | October 03, 2025 | 10.03.25 |
| 7.0R Recumbent Bike | December 23, 2025 | 12.23.25 |
| 7.0U Upright Bike | December 09, 2025 | 12.09.2025 |
| 8.0U Upright Bike | December 03, 2025 | 12.03.25 |
| 8.5R Recumbent Bike | December 15, 2025 | 12.15.25 |

**A sentence in the source is left dangling**, as in the 2026 treadmill books: the paragraph above the table ends "The warranty period applies to the following components:" and no component list follows - the next thing printed is the Commercial Warranty heading. That is the printed page.

**The Dyaco PT and MED editions of the same bikes print no warranty at all** (`spirit-med-bike-dyaco-edition-warranty-the-book-prints-no-warranty-page`).

When the clock starts is in the paragraph above the table (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`); the one-year labor figure is dated separately in the SERVICE paragraph two pages later (`xt-2023-warranty-labor-period`); the facility's duties, the nine exclusions, registration, the service steps and what the owner pays are `spirit-ce-2025-warranty-facility-responsibilities`, `spirit-ce-2025-warranty-exclusions`, `spirit-ce-warranty-registration`, `xt-2023-warranty-service-steps` and `spirit-2026t-warranty-owner-costs`.
