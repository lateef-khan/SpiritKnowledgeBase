The SW121 Half Rack owner's manual ($W121 Ver.A) prints **no warranty page and no warranty terms**. Proven four ways: the table of contents runs Product Registration, Important Precautions, Important Safeguards, Assembly Pack Checklist, Assembly Instruction — no warranty chapter; zero hits for "warranty" anywhere in the text (the congratulations page's "most comprehensive warranties available" is marketing boilerplate, not terms); PDF pages 10-12, the pages without OCR supplements, render as assembly STEPS 3 through 8, not hidden warranty text; and the book ends at printed page 11 with the 866-697-6531 support line.

Registration still exists — the book carries the standard card-plus-online page: `sole-strength-sw-warranty-registration-card-online`. The sibling SW100/SW155/SW180 books' 1-year parts terms do not cover this rack.
