Yes. Four charges stay with the customer while the machine is inside its warranty period, and one reimbursement is refused outright.

| What the customer pays | Where the manual says so |
|---|---|
| Freight **to and from** a service center, if the product is shipped to one | SERVICE step 4 |
| **Shipping and handling** on replacement parts shipped while the product is under warranty | SERVICE step 4 |
| Adequate packaging for a return, and any freight damage claim, which goes to the carrier | SERVICE step 5 |
| Expenses for making the equipment accessible for servicing, including anything not part of it when it left the factory | Responsibilities of the facility, item 6 |

And exclusion 2 refuses **service call reimbursement to the consumer**.

**These manuals charge no trip charge.** Step 4 stops at shipping and handling on replacement parts. Do not answer this question from `xt-2023-warranty-owner-costs`: the XT version of the same step goes on to charge the customer a trip charge for in-home service, and an additional one beyond 100 miles from the nearest service center. Neither sentence is printed in the 4.0T, 7.0T or 8.0T. Quoting them would invent a charge the source does not state.

The manuals print **no figure** for any of these charges and no way to have one waived.

Blown fuses, loose connections and defects in house or facility wiring are the facility's to fix as well - item 5 of the same list, in `ct900ent-facility-responsibilities`. The step order itself is `ct900-warranty-service-claims-process`.
