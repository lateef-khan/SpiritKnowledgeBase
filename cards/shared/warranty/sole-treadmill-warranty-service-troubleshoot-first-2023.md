**Service under this warranty must be obtained by following these steps, in order:**

1. **Contact the selling authorized SOLE dealer.** OR
2. **Submit the service request online**, with serial number, contact information and a brief description of the problem, at `http://www.soletreadmills.com/serviceparts.html`.
3. If there is a question where to obtain service, **contact the service department at 866-780-7653** — a single number for product and technical questions, where the older books split 1-866-780-SOLE and 866-MYSOLE1.
4. **If no local service is available**, SOLE repairs or replaces the parts, at its option, at no charge for parts. **All transportation costs both ways and adequate packaging are the owner’s responsibility**; freight claims go to the carrier. **Do not ship any unit to the factory without a return authorization number.**
5. **The service department assists in troubleshooting before sending a field technician**, and may request pictures and video by email to help with diagnosis. (The F89-2023 book prints this as unnumbered prose — "before they are able to send a field technician under warranty ... provide the media via email, if asked" — same step, carded as printed.)

**Keep the bill of sale**, and keep all receipts if service is performed. **Before returning the product to any retailer, call 866-780-7653 first.** No Jonesboro mail address is printed — unlike the older books (see `sole-treadmill-warranty-service-online-request-steps`). The 12/18-month service clock is on `sole-treadmill-warranty-start-date-receipt-or-18-months-shipping`; the 2026 procedure is on `f63-2026-how-to-get-warranty-service`.
