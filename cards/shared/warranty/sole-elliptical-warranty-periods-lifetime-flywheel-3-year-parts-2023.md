**Elliptical warranty, effective February 1, 2023**, identical tables in the E95-2023 and E95S-2023 books. No brake row is printed; the flywheel row (new in 2023) carries the lifetime term.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Flywheel** | **Lifetime** |
| **All Other Components** | **3 Years** |
| **Labor** | **1 Years** (sic — "1 Years", as printed) |
| **Cosmetic Items** * | **90 Days** |

\* Cosmetic items including, but not limited to: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.

Against the older E95S table (lifetime frame *and brake*, 5-year parts, 2-year labor) this book drops the brake row, adds the flywheel row, and shortens parts to 3 years and labor to 1 year: `sole-e95s-warranty-periods-frame-brake-lifetime-5-year-parts`. The books' duties and exclusions pages are treadmill copy — see `sole-elliptical-warranty-responsibilities-8-item-treadmill-wording-2023`.
