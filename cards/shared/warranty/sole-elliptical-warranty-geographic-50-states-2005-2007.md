The E25/E55-2007, E35/E75/E95-2007 and VE-2008 books close the warranty with: "**Product features or specifications as described or illustrated are subject to change without notice. All warranties are made by Sole. This warranty applies only in the 50 states of the U.S.A.**"

Two documented boundaries, carded as printed:

- The **WE-2009-2010 books print no geographic sentence at all** — neither the 50-states line nor the outside-North-America item. Their warranty closes with "All warranties are made by SOLE." An absence proven by loose-word search (no "50 states", no "North America" anywhere in either WE text.md).
- From the **2011 books on**, the geographic term moves into the exclusions list as item 9 ("Warranties outside of North America may vary. Please contact your local dealer for details") — see `sole-elliptical-warranty-exclusions-9-item-2011-2019` — and the 2023 books add AK/HI shipping charges. The treadmill geographic card is `sole-treadmill-warranty-geographic-coverage`.
