This warranty does not cover the following:

1. CONSEQUENTIAL, COLLATERAL, OR INCIDENTAL DAMAGES SUCH AS PROPERTY DAMAGE AND INCIDENTAL EXPENSES RESULTING FROM ANY BREACH OF THIS WRITTEN OR ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY. *Note: Some states do not allow the exclusion or limitation of incidental or consequential damages, so this limitation or exclusion may not apply to you.*
2. Service call reimbursement to the consumer. Service call reimbursement to the dealer that does not involve malfunction or defects in workmanship or material, for units that are beyond the warranty period, for units that are beyond the service call reimbursement period, for units not requiring component replacement, or units not in ordinary household or light commercial use.
3. Damages caused by services performed by persons other than authorized Spirit Fitness service companies; use of parts other than original Spirit Fitness parts; or external causes such as corrosion, discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, or acts of God.
4. Products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered.
5. Products that have been: sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party.
6. Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.
7. THIS WARRANTY IS EXPRESSLY IN LIEU OF ALL OTHER WARRANTIES EXPRESSED OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING THE WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND/OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.
8. Product used in prisons and correctional facilities.
9. Warranties outside of the United States may vary. Please contact your local dealer for details.

**All nine items are word for word identical in the CE900 2025 and CES880 manuals**, under the heading *What is not covered?* on the WARRANTY - CONTINUED page of each.

**Item 6 is the softest registration rule any Spirit elliptical manual prints.** Every earlier one - CE800 2013, 2021 and 2023, the CE800ENT, all three CE850 manuals and the CE900 2021 - opens item 6 with "Products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at Spirit Fitness", which excludes an unregistered machine outright. **These two drop that sentence entirely** and keep only the reserved right to ask for proof of purchase. An unregistered CE900 2025 or CES880 is not excluded by this list. That is a real change and it is the most useful thing on this card - `spirit-ce-warranty-registration`.

**No dues-paying exclusion, because the table covers one.** These are the only two Spirit elliptical manuals here that give a dues-paying facility its own row of periods, so nothing in the list excludes a health club or a YMCA. `spirit-ce-warranty-use-environment` sets that against the seven manuals that do exclude one.

**Item 2 still contradicts the table.** It refuses service call reimbursement for "units not in ordinary household or **light commercial** use", which reads as a limit on the very dues-paying placement the table's first row covers, and neither manual defines "light commercial" or reconciles the two. Quote both.

Printed under the list in both manuals:

> Product features or specifications as described or illustrated are subject to change without notice. All warranties are made by Spirit Fitness, Inc. This warranty applies only in the 48 contiguous United States. NOTE: This does not include Alaska or Hawaii.

**Nine items is not eight and not ten.** The CE900 2021 list runs to eight with the outside-the-US sentence unnumbered (`ce900-2021-warranty-exclusions`); the CE850 lists run to nine or ten with a five-hour cap (`spirit-ce850-warranty-exclusions`); the CE800 2021, 2023 and CE800ENT lists run to ten with a dues-paying exclusion (`spirit-ct800-warranty-exclusions`).

Items 4 and 5 are asked about on their own - `xt-2023-warranty-transfer`. Item 8 is `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`; item 9 and the 48-states note are `xt-2023-warranty-geographic-coverage`.

## Three Spirit commercial bike manuals print the same nine items

The CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 print this list word for word - the comparison is exact at word level - and the CU800ENT 2024 prints it with the same nine items in the same order. All three head it *What is not covered?* above EXCLUSIONS, exclude prisons at item 8, put the outside-the-United-States sentence at item 9, and **keep only the proof-of-purchase sentence at item 6**, so an unregistered bike is not excluded outright (`spirit-ce-warranty-registration`).

**Item 2 narrows cover to "ordinary household or light commercial use" on the CR900 2025 and CU900 2025** - the phrase the CE900 2025 and CES880 use - above tables whose first row is a **dues-paying** commercial facility. The manuals never reconcile the two; quote both (`spirit-bike-warranty-use-environment`).

**The CU800ENT 2024 item 2 breaks off mid-sentence on the printed page.** It runs "...for units not requiring component replacement, or units" and stops there, and the phrase *ordinary household* appears nowhere in that manual. **This is a defect in the manual, not in the extraction.** Page 58 of `CU800ENT_OwnersManual_800365_20241009.pdf` was rendered at 300 dpi and read by OCR: the rendered page ends item 2 at the same word, with white space after it and item 3 beginning below. **Do not complete the sentence from a sibling manual** - the words are not on the page to recover. Tell a CU800ENT 2024 owner that their printed item 2 is incomplete and read the use restriction off the table rows instead (`cu800ent-2024-warranty-coverage-periods`).

**Do not answer the other ten bikes from this card.** The CR800, CU800 and the two 800ENT bikes print **ten** items including an outright exclusion of a dues-paying facility (`spirit-ct800-warranty-exclusions`); the four 900-series bikes print **eight** (`ce900-2021-warranty-exclusions`).
