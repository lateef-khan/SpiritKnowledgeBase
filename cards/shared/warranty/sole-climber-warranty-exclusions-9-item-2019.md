This warranty does not cover:

1. **Consequential, collateral or incidental damages** from any breach of written or implied warranty (some states disallow this exclusion).
2. **Service-call reimbursement** to the consumer, or to the dealer where no malfunction or defect is involved, the unit is beyond the warranty or service-call-reimbursement period, the fitness bike needs no component replacement, or it is not in ordinary household use (the book reads "fitness bike" twice here, sic — carded as printed).
3. Damage from **non-authorized service**, non-SOLE parts, or external causes — corrosion, discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, acts of God.
4. Products with **removed or altered serial numbers**.
5. Products **sold, transferred, bartered or given** to a third party.
6. Products with **no warranty registration card on file** (SOLE may request proof of purchase).
7. All other warranties, **merchantability and fitness** disclaimers.
8. Use **outside a residential setting**.
9. Warranties **outside North America** vary — contact the local dealer.

This printing has nine items and no climate-control exclusion; the CC81-2026 book adds corrosion (RUST), natural-disaster wording, a no-transfer parenthesis, continental-US limits and a tenth climate-void item: `cc81-2026-warranty-exclusions`. The SC200/SC300 climber books print these same nine items; being elliptical-line machines they join the elliptical twin `sole-elliptical-warranty-exclusions-9-item-2011-2019`.
