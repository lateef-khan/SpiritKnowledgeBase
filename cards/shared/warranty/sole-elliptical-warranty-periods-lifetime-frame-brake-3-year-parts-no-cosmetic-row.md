**Elliptical warranty, effective January 1, 2007** (VE book) and **effective January 1, 2008** (WE book) — same figures under both dates. Both books print **no cosmetic-items row**; that is how the table reads, not an omission here.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Labor** | **1 Year** (books print "1 year"/"1 years") |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Brake** | **Lifetime** |
| **All Other Components** | **3 Years** |

Both books cover their models under columns headed **E25** as printed (the VE/WE naming, not AE); the E25 column applies to the VE25-2007, WE25-2009 and WE25-2010. Carded as printed. The AE25-2011 printing adds a 90-day cosmetic row: `sole-elliptical-warranty-periods-lifetime-frame-brake-3-year-parts-1-year-labor`.
