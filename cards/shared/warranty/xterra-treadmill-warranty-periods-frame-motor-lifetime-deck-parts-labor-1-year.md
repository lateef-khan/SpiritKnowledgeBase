**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the warranty page of these 2 books, under the heading *Effective March 13, 2021 ... LIMITED WARRANTY*. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**Two books print these five figures in two layouts.** The TR300 (2021) sets them as *Frame & Motor - Lifetime, Deck - 1 Years, Parts - 1 Years, Labor - 1 Year*, four columns with frame and motor sharing one. The TRX2500 (2024) sets them as a *Residential* row under five headings - Frame Lifetime, Motor Lifetime, Deck 1 Year, Parts 1 Year, Labor 1 Year. The periods are identical; the responsibilities and exclusions printed under them are not (the TR300 uses the five-item older list, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-with-a-registration-card` / `xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-store-display-and-unregistered-products`; the TRX2500 the seven-item 2024 list, `xterra-trx-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-seven-items` / `xterra-trx-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-floor-models-corrosion-and-discoloration`).

**Do not read the deck as one year on the TR6.4, TRX3500, TR6.6, TRX4500 or TRX5500** - those books print 3, 2 and 5 years for the deck (`tr64-2024-warranty-periods-frame-motor-lifetime-deck-3-years-parts-and-labor-1-year`, `trx3500-2024-warranty-periods-frame-motor-lifetime-deck-2-years-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-periods-frame-motor-lifetime-deck-5-years-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`).
