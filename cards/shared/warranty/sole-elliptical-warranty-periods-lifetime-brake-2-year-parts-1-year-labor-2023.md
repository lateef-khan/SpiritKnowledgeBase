**Elliptical warranty on the Sole E25-2023, effective February 1, 2023.** The E25 is the only 2023 elliptical that keeps a **brake** row; its siblings print a flywheel row instead.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Brake** | **Lifetime** |
| **All Other Components** | **2 Years** |
| **Labor** | **1 Years** (sic — "1 Years", as printed) |
| **Cosmetic Items** * | **90 Days** |

\* Cosmetic items including, but not limited to: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.

The E35-2023 sibling swaps the brake row for a lifetime flywheel with the same 2-year others: `sole-elliptical-warranty-periods-lifetime-flywheel-2-year-parts-2023`. The book's duties and exclusions pages are treadmill copy ("Proper use of the treadmill", "belt tracking") — see `sole-elliptical-warranty-responsibilities-8-item-treadmill-wording-2023`.
