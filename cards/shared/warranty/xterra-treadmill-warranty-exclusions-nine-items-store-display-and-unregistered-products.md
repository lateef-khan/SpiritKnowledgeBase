**EXCLUSIONS** - *This warranty does not cover the following:*

1. CONSEQUENTIAL, COLLATERAL, OR INCIDENTAL DAMAGES SUCH AS PROPERTY DAMAGE AND INCIDENTAL EXPENSES RESULTING FROM ANY BREACH OF THIS WRITTEN OR ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY. *Note: Some states do not allow the exclusion or limitation of incidental or consequential damages, so this limitation or exclusion may not apply to you.* (The note is printed twice, one under the other, in all seven books.)
2. Service call reimbursement to the consumer. Service call reimbursement to the dealer that does not involve malfunction or defects in workmanship or material, for units that are beyond the warranty period, for units that are beyond the service call reimbursement period, for treadmills not requiring component replacement, or treadmills not in ordinary household use.
3. Damages caused by services performed by persons other than authorized XTERRA service companies, use of parts other than original XTERRA parts, or external causes such as alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, or acts of God.
4. Products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered.
5. Products that have been; sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party.
6. Products that are used as **store display models**.
7. Products that do not have a **warranty registration on file** at XTERRA, Inc. XTERRA reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.
8. Product use in any environment other than a residential setting.
9. THIS WARRANTY IS EXPRESSLY IN LIEU OF ALL OTHER WARRANTIES EXPRESSED OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING THE WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND/OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

**Seven treadmill books print these nine items in this order**: TR150, TR200, TR260, TR300, TR6.4, TR6.6, TRX1000 - **and fifteen bike, elliptical and seated-stepper books print them word for word with the machine noun swapped**: AIR350, AIR650, FB150, FB350, FB160, FB360, MB500, MB550, MBX2500, SB120, SB4500, SB500, UB120, RSX1500, FS5.9e (both printings of the SB500 and RSX1500). Eleven other older-layout bike and elliptical books print **eight** items instead: the SB150, SB250, SB2.5r, EU100, EU150, FS150, FS1.5, FS2.5, FS3.0 and FS3.5 drop item 2, the service-call item (`xterra-warranty-exclusions-eight-items-no-service-call-reimbursement-item`), and the SB4.5r drops item 8, the residential-setting item (`sb45r-2013-warranty-exclusions-eight-items-no-residential-setting-item`). The TR150 and TR200 write *XTERRA* and *XTERRA, Inc.*; the other five *XTERRA Fitness* and *XTERRA Fitness, Inc.* Item 3 in these seven does **not** list corrosion or discoloration; the 2024 TRX books add them and swap items 8 and 9 (`xterra-trx-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-floor-models-corrosion-and-discoloration`). The Online-Support books, and the FB180, SB240 and SB600, drop items 6 and 7 - no display-model exclusion and no registration exclusion - and admit a non-dues facility in item 8 (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-with-a-non-dues-facility-allowance`).

Item 5 is the transfer rule (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-not-transferable-sold-bartered-or-given-away`); item 7 is the teeth behind the 10-day registration card (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover`); item 8 is the use rule (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-use-environment-residential-only-any-other-use-voids-it`). The consumer duties above this list are `xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-with-a-registration-card`.

**Seven rower books print the nine items too**, with *Rowers* in item 2 and in this order: ERG160, ERG220, ERG500, ERG550W, ERG600W, ERG650W, ERG700 (the ERG400 prints the eight-item list without the service-call item, `xterra-warranty-exclusions-eight-items-no-service-call-reimbursement-item`; the ERG180, ERG750W and ERG800W print the Online-Support lists, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-with-a-non-dues-facility-allowance`, `fb180-2025-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-residential-only-with-proof-of-purchase-and-outside-us-items`). **The four strength books print them too** - ADB12.5PR, ADB25, ADB55 (*adjustable dumbbells* in item 2) and DBSTAND (*Dumbbell Stands*) - with one phrase dropped from item 3: **inadequate power supply is not among its causes**, the products having no electrics; the store-display item 6, the registration item 7 and everything else are printed as above. The water rowers' tank pages add two acts that void the warranty outright and are not on this list (`xterra-ergw-warranty-tank-chlorine-bleach-or-filling-past-the-mark-voids-it`).
