**Twelve of the thirteen print a date. One does not.**

| Machine | Warranty effective | The book's own date stamp |
|---|---|---|
| XS895 2018 | **August 22, 2018** | © 2018, no revision line |
| CSC900 2019 | **December 1, 2019** | Revision: **12.01.2019**, © 2019 |
| XS895 2021 | **February 19, 2021** | © 2021, no revision line |
| CRS800S 2021 | **October 8, 2021** | © 2021, no revision line |
| CS800 2021 | **November 12, 2021** | © 2021, no revision line |
| CSC900 2021 | **November 19, 2021** | Revision: **11.19.2021**, © 2021 |
| CVC800 | **November 19, 2021** | © 2021, no revision line |
| MS300 | **none printed** | no copyright line and no revision line anywhere |
| CSC880 2025 | **February 14, 2025** | `CSC880_880665_OM_`**`20251030`**, ©2025 |
| 7.5S | **October 23, 2024** | `7.5S_775545_OM_`**`20251215`**, ©2025 |
| 8.5S | **December 15, 2025** | `8.5S_785545_OM_`**`20251201`**, ©2025 |
| 7.0S | **December 18, 2025** | `7.0S_770545_OM_`**`20251218`**, ©2025 |
| 8.5S-Fit | **May 20th, 2026** | `FIT_785525_OM_`**`20260520`**, ©2026 |

**The two CSC900 books are the clean pair.** Each stamps a revision line on its back cover that matches its own warranty effective date to the day - 12.01.2019 and 11.19.2021. That is what makes a stamp that *disagrees* worth noticing on the other machines.

## The 7.5S is the one to be careful with

**Its warranty is effective October 23, 2024; its book was made on December 15, 2025.** The stamp on its own Warranty Registration page reads `7.5S_775545_OM_20251215`, the filename repeats that date, and the copyright line reads ©2025.

**December 15, 2025 is the document's own date. October 23, 2024 is the warranty's.** The stamp names when this printing of the book was produced; the Effective line names when the terms inside it began, and those terms were carried forward unchanged from a printing more than a year earlier. Quote **October 23, 2024** to a customer asking which terms apply to a 7.5S, and use the 2025 stamp only to say which printing they are reading.

It is the only one of the five 2025 and 2026 books whose warranty predates its own printing by more than a few weeks. Its sibling the 7.0S, stamped three days later, carries a warranty effective the same day it was stamped.

## Two of the 2025 books date their warranty *after* they were printed

The **8.5S** was stamped `20251201` and prints terms effective **December 15, 2025** - two weeks later. The **CSC880** is the reverse and by a wider margin: stamped `20251030` and printing terms effective **February 14, 2025**, eight months earlier.

**Nothing in any of these books reconciles the two dates**, and neither date changes a figure in the table. Where they disagree, the Effective line is the one the terms are written under.

## The MS300 prints no effective date at all

**Its warranty page is headed simply *Product warranty*.** There is no *Effective &lt;date&gt;* line above it, no *WARRANTY EFFECTIVE* heading, no copyright year anywhere in the document and no revision stamp on its back cover. It is the only one of the thirteen with none of the four.

The absence was proved twice. A loose search for the word *effect* across the whole 71-page document returns one hit, in a sentence about moisture and false heart-rate signals, and a spacing-tolerant search for the letters `E f f e c t i v e` returns nothing. Then PDF pages 67 and 68 - the whole of the warranty - were rendered at 300 dpi and read with `tesseract --psm 4`: the heading is *Product warranty* and the paragraph under it begins *Spirit Fitness warrants all of the products and their components*. **The page is not a flattened image hiding a date; the date is not printed.**

So there is no date to quote for an MS300. What the manual does state is when the warranty *period* starts - the date of sale, or eighteen months from factory shipping when there is no receipt - and that clock is a different question from these terms' effective date (`ms300-2021-warranty-period-twenty-four-months`).

## The 8.5S-Fit does print one, contrary to the source manifest

The manifest entry for this source says the 8.5S-Fit "prints no warranty effective date". **It does.** PDF page 55 was rendered at 300 dpi and read by OCR, and the line above its table is unambiguous:

> Effective May 20th, 2026- 8.5S-FIT Recumbent Stepper LIMITED WARRANTY

It is the only one of the thirteen that writes the day as an ordinal - *May 20th* rather than *May 20* - and the only one whose effective date, cover stamp and filename are all the same day.

## A filename year is not the answer to this question on any of them

Every one of the eight 2018-to-2021 books is named for a date that is *not* its warranty effective date except by coincidence, and the CVC800 owner's manual carries no year in its model id at all. Read the Effective line off the warranty page rather than the filename.

The 2024 machines - the CRS800S 2024, CS800 2024 and CSC900 2024 that replaced four of these - date themselves the same two ways and disagree on five of fourteen: `spirit-2024-warranty-effective-dates`.
