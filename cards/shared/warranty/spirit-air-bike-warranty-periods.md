| Warranty | Frame | Console | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial | **5 Years** | 2 Years | 2 Years | 1 Year |
| Residential | **5 Years** | 2 Years | 2 Years | 1 Year |

**The two rows are identical.** Both air bike manuals print a Commercial row and a Residential row and give them the same four figures, so on these two machines the facility makes no difference to the periods at all. No other Spirit bike table in this knowledge base does that.

**The frame is 5 Years, not Lifetime.** Every other Spirit bike range gives a home owner a lifetime frame - the CR800 and CU800 in both rows, the CIC800, CIC850 and CB900 in their Residential row, the XIC600 in its only row. The air bikes do not. It is the shortest frame period of any Spirit bike here, and it is the same figure in a gym as at home.

**These two manuals are the only Spirit bikes of this wave with a Console column**, and it warrants the console for 2 years in both rows. The CIC850 folds a console into its 1-year wear-items column (`spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-three-row-periods`); the CB900, CIC800, XIC600 and JB950 state no console period at all.

**AB900 2018 and AB950 2024 print the same eight figures six years apart.** What moves is the heading and the effective date, not the table:

| Manual | Warranty section heading | Effective date | Back cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| AB900 2018 | AB900 AIR BIKE WARRANTY EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 1, 2018 | October 1, 2018 | (c) 2018, Revision: 12.13.2018 |
| AB950 2024 | WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL, then *Effective April 23, 2024- AB950 Air Bike LIMITED WARRANTY* | April 23, 2024 | (c) 2024, **no revision line** |

**The AB950 heads its warranty page WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL and then prints a Residential row underneath it.** The manual never reconciles the two. Quote the table, which grants the home row, rather than the page heading that appears to withhold it.

**The AB950 warranty page is four days newer than the book around it.** Its PRODUCT REGISTRATION page stamps *Version: 1.0, Revision: 04.19.2024* and its internal file stamp reads `AB950_950744_OM_20240419`, while the warranty is effective **April 23, 2024**. The warranty page was dated after the rest of the book was laid out - the reverse of the CIC850 and JB950, where the warranty page is the older half (`spirit-jb950-warranty-commercial-only-periods`).

**A fan-resistance machine still gets a parts and labor warranty.** Neither manual carves out the fan, the belt or the flywheel, and neither prints a wear-items column that would name them.

**Spirit's Cardio Warranty sheet, Revision 03.07.2019, prints the AB900 with these figures** - Frame 5 Years, a single *Parts /
Console* line at 2 Years, Labor 1 Year - and puts it under **COMMERCIAL (ALL FACILITIES)**,
beside the CIC800, which on the sheet means a dues-paying gym is covered. The sheet prints no
residential row for anything; see `spirit-commercial-warranty-sheet-2019-all-facilities-terms`.

## These are not the other Spirit bike figures

**The indoor cycles double the parts period and change the frame.** The CB900, CIC800 and CIC850 warrant Parts for **3 Years** with a frame of 10, 15 or Lifetime by row (`spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-three-row-periods`); the XIC600 pair warrants Parts 3 Years on a Lifetime frame (`spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-residential-only-periods`). Only the Labor figure - 1 Year - is shared with the air bikes.

**The JB950 shares the air bikes' 2-year parts and electronics periods but not the frame.** Its single Commercial row reads Frame **10 Years** (`spirit-jb950-warranty-commercial-only-periods`).

**The CR800, CU800 and 800ENT bikes are a different table entirely** - Frame Lifetime, an Electronics or Brake column at 5 or 10 years, Parts 5 or 10 Years and Labor **2 Years** (`spirit-bike-warranty-commercial-periods`).

**Damage done while putting an AB900 together is excluded in as many words**, on a caution the AB950 does not print: `spirit-bike-warranty-assembly-damage-excluded`. When the clock starts is `xt-2023-warranty-start-date`, and what each manual asks of the owner before it will pay is `spirit-ce-warranty-registration`.
