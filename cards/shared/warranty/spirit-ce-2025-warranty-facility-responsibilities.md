> The facility is responsible for the items listed below:

1. The warranty registration must be completed online to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty. *(CE900 2025 wording - see below)*
2. Proper use of the fitness equipment in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual
3. Proper installation in accordance with instructions provided with the fitness equipment and with all local electric codes.
4. Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage, replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects in house or facility wiring.
5. Expenses for making the fitness equipment accessible for servicing, including any item that was not part of the fitness equipment at the time it was shipped from the factory.
6. Damages to the fitness equipment finish during shipping, installation or following installation.
7. Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual.

**Items 2 to 7 are word for word the same in both manuals. Item 1 is not.**

| Manual | Item 1 |
|---|---|
| CE900 2025 | The warranty registration must be completed **online** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty. |
| CES880 2025 | The warranty registration **card** must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card **within 10 days** of the original purchase, **or** completed online to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty. |

**The CE900 2025 manual is the only Spirit elliptical manual in this knowledge base that names no registration card and no deadline.** The CES880, printed two and a half months later, restores both. Read the deadline off the manual in hand rather than from its sibling - `spirit-ce-warranty-registration`.

**The CES880 numbering is damaged.** Its list numbers the lead sentence itself as item 1 - "1. The facility is responsible for the items listed below:" - and the seven duties then run 2 to 8. The duties and their order are unchanged; only the numerals are off by one. Cite them by wording, not by number, when quoting from that manual.

**Item 4 puts the electrical supply on the facility.** Blown fuses, loose connections and defects in house or facility wiring are the facility's to fix, not warranty work. The CE900 2021 and all three CE850 manuals carry **no** such item, so this duty is not a range-wide Spirit elliptical rule.

Item 5 makes getting the machine to a place where it can be worked on the facility's expense, not a warranty cost (`spirit-2026t-warranty-owner-costs`).

**The heading dropped a word.** Both manuals head the list **RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE FACILITY**; every earlier Spirit elliptical manual heads it NORMAL RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE CONSUMER or NORMAL RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE FACILITY. Nothing in the duties changes with the heading.

**The list is addressed to a facility on a manual that still warrants a home.** The table's third row is Residential and the opening paragraph says the warranty is administered "directly to a consumer", yet no part of this list addresses a home owner. Neither manual reconciles that - `spirit-ce-2025-warranty-coverage-periods`.

This list is only half of what governs the warranty. The other half is the nine exclusions on the next page, `spirit-ce-2025-warranty-exclusions`.
