**This is the only warranty in the Spirit XE range with a stated base period.** The opening sentence warrants **all** elliptical parts for **2 years**, and the table then lists an extended period for three of them:

| | Period |
|---|---|
| Labor | 1 Year |
| Frame Weldments | Lifetime |
| All Other Components | 5 Years |

Printed above it: **Effective October 15, 2005**.

**The two halves do not agree and the manual never reconciles them.** The paragraph says two years on all parts; the table then gives *All Other Components* five years under the heading *An extended warranty period applies to the following components*. Read literally, every part that is not a frame weldment is covered for five years and the two-year sentence covers nothing at all. **Quote both to a customer** rather than picking one.

**There is no brake row.** The 2007 XE100/XE200/XE300 table and every 2021 and 2023 XE table warrant the brake for its own period; this one does not name a brake anywhere in the warranty section. Do not read a lifetime brake onto these machines from a sibling manual.

**One column, not three. This table names no machine.** The XE150, XE350 and XE550 differ from each other elsewhere in this manual - three separate console sections, parts marked *XE150, XE350 only* and *XE550 only*, an optional heart rate transmitter on the XE150 - but the warranty table names none of them and states one set of figures for all three.

The clock runs from the date of retail sale as determined by sale receipt, **or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date, whichever comes first** (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`).

**This manual is the only one in the range that covers Canada.** It is honoured in the 50 states of the U.S.A. **and Canada**; every other XE manual stops at the United States (`spirit-xe-warranty-geographic-fifty-states`).

**Getting a warranty repair costs the owner differently here too.** This manual promises parts at no charge where no local service is available and puts **all** transportation costs on the owner, with no trip charge and no 100-mile surcharge named: `spirit-xe-2005-warranty-service-and-transport-costs`.
