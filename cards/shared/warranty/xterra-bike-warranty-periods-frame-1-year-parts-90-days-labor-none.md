| Frame | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Year | 90 Days | None |

**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the Manufacturer's Limited Warranty page of these seven bike books. The FB180 prints it as a two-row table headed *Warranty / Residential*; the other six print one three-column row. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`) - except in the FB160 and FB360, whose SERVICE paragraph says *three (3) months from the date of factory shipping* instead (`xterra-fb-warranty-start-date-bill-of-sale-or-3-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**Labor is None.** No labor is covered at any time: XTERRA supplies a replacement part inside the 90 days and the owner fits it or pays a technician. Ninety days is the shortest parts cover in the Xterra range, shared with the treadmill books that print 90 days for parts (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-motor-1-year-parts-and-labor-90-days`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-and-labor-90-days-no-motor-column`) - those at least give 90 days of labor too.

**Five of the seven books also print a shorter statement at the front, and two of them disagree with this table.** The FB150 and FB350 registration page carries a *Manufacture's One-Year Limited Warranty* paragraph that says the bike *is warranted for one year from the date of purchase against defects in material* (`xterra-fb-warranty-front-page-one-year-statement-against-a-90-day-parts-table`); the FB160, FB360 and FB180 carry the same paragraph as a *90 Day Limited Warranty*, which agrees with this table (`xterra-fb-warranty-front-page-90-day-statement-not-transferable-and-no-commercial-use`). Where the two disagree this card follows the table: it is the page headed *Manufacturer's Limited Warranty*, it carries the effective date, and it is the page every other term on this card comes from.

**The SB120 service document says otherwise too.** The December 2019 SB120 service sheet (`xterra-bike-sb120-2022-service-document`, PDF p. 1) summarises the warranty as *Frame- 1 year, Parts- 1 year, Labor- None*. The owner's manual, effective July 19, 2022, prints 90 days for parts. This card follows the owner's manual: it is the later document, it is the page the customer holds, and a printed warranty table beats a technician's summary. The FB160 service document of November 2019 (`xterra-bike-fb160-2019-service-document`) agrees with its manual: *90 Days from date of purchase*.
