This warranty does not cover the following:

1. CONSEQUENTIAL, COLLATERAL, OR INCIDENTAL DAMAGES SUCH AS PROPERTY DAMAGE AND INCIDENTAL EXPENSES RESULTING FROM ANY BREACH OF THIS WRITTEN OR ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY. *NOTE: Some states do not allow the exclusion or limitation of incidental or consequential damages, so this limitation or exclusion may not apply to you.*
2. Service call reimbursement to the facility. Service call reimbursement to the dealer that does not involve malfunction or defects in workmanship or material, for units that are beyond the warranty period, for units that are beyond the service call reimbursement period, for fitness equipment not requiring component replacement, or fitness equipment not in non-dues paying facility or household use.
3. Damages caused by services performed by persons other than authorized Spirit Fitness service companies; use of parts other than original Spirit Fitness parts; or external causes such as corrosion, discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, or acts of God.
4. Products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered.
5. Products that have been: sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party.
6. Products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at Spirit Fitness. Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.
7. THIS WARRANTY IS EXPRESSLY IN LIEU OF ALL OTHER WARRANTIES EXPRESSED OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING THE WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND/OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.
8. **Product used in prisons and correctional facilities**

Then, printed below the list and **not numbered**:

> Warranties outside of the United States may vary. Please contact your local dealer for details.

**The 2019 and 2021 lists are word for word identical, all eight items.** The only change to the whole warranty section between the two printings is the effective date and the prisons footnote the 2021 book adds under its table.

**Item 2 begins "reimbursement to the facility", not "to the consumer"** - the only manual of the thirteen climbers and steppers that words it that way. It is a commercial-only book with no residential row (`spirit-csc900-warranty-commercial-periods`), and the wording follows.

**There is no use-environment item.** Nothing here caps use at five hours a day and nothing bars a dues-paying gym by name, unlike the XS895, CRS800S, CS800 and CVC800 lists, whose item 8 does both (`spirit-ce850-warranty-exclusions`). The only restriction on *where* the machine may be used is the tail of item 2 - "fitness equipment not in non-dues paying facility or household use" - and it refuses a service call reimbursement rather than excluding the machine. Read it to a customer as what it is: `spirit-climber-warranty-use-environment`.

**The outside-the-US sentence is not an exclusion here.** Every sibling manual numbers it as the last item; the CSC900 leaves it loose under the list. It says the same thing.

**Item 6 excludes an unregistered machine outright.** The two sentences in that item do not say the same thing - the first excludes the product, the second only reserves a right to ask for proof of purchase - and neither manual reconciles them. Say both. The 2025 books drop the first sentence: `spirit-ce-2025-warranty-exclusions`.

**Item 8 is prisons on this list.** On the XS895, CRS800S, CS800 and CVC800 lists item 8 is the use-environment cap instead, and on the CVC800 prisons is item **9**. Cite the number off the manual in hand.

**The 2019 book already carries the prisons exclusion**, effective December 1, 2019, at item 8 - and prints no footnote under its table. The 2021 book prints both halves: `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.
