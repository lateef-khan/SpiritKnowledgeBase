**Three tables, headed XT375 (Home Warranty), XT475 (Home Warranty) and XT675 (Home Warranty), print the same four rows word for word:**

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| Frame/Motor/Deck | Lifetime |
| Electronics/Belt/Rollers | 5 Years |
| All other components | 10 Years |
| Labor | 1 Year |

**This is the only 2007 table with an "All other components" row**, and it is a ten-year row: anything that is not frame, motor, deck, electronics, belt, roller or labour is covered for ten years. The XT175 and XT275 tables have no such row - they name drive motor, frame, electronics, belt, rollers, deck and labour and nothing else (`xt175-2007-warranty-home-periods`, `xt275-2007-warranty-home-periods`).

**The XT675 has a second table for commercial use, and it is much shorter** - motor 5 years, parts and deck 3 years (`xt675-2007-warranty-commercial-periods`). The XT375 and XT475 print no commercial column, so this manual gives them no commercial cover in writing at all.

The base term every table sits on - parts 5 years from retail sale or 18 months from factory shipping, whichever comes first - is on `spirit-xt-2007-warranty-five-years-or-eighteen-months-from-shipping`.

**Do not answer this from a 2015 or 2023 XT table.** Those print Frame / Motor / Deck / Parts / Labor with Parts at 10 Years and no electronics row (`xt-2023-warranty-residential-coverage-periods`); the five-year electronics figure here has no counterpart there.
