The sheet prints four footnotes at the foot of its second page, marked with one to four
asterisks against the machine names and table headings on both pages:

> \* Warranty applies to 48 contiguous states. Products used in prisons and correctional
> facilities are excluded from warranty coverage. See owner's manuals for complete warranty
> details.
>
> \*\* X Series warranty covers up to 5 hours use or less in a non-dues paying facility.
>
> \*\*\* Wear Items are grips, seats and pedal straps.
>
> \*\*\*\* Consoles are covered for 3 years parts and 1 year labor.

**`*` is against every table heading on both pages, so the 48-states limit and the prisons
exclusion apply to all thirty-two machines the sheet names** - including the XE795, XBR95,
XS895, CE850 and MT200, whose own 2013-2018 manuals print no prisons exclusion anywhere
(`spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`). Read with the sheet, a March 2019 sale of
any of them excluded prisons whatever the book in the carton said. The 48-states sentence is the
one every Spirit manual prints (`xt-2023-warranty-geographic-coverage`); the sheet does not name
Alaska or Hawaii and does not say what applies outside the United States.

**`**` is against the XT685, XE795 and XBR95 only** - the sheet's X Series - and it is the same
five-hour, non-dues-paying envelope those machines' manuals put in their Light Commercial row
label (`spirit-xe795-warranty-light-commercial-periods`). **The XS895 carries no `**`** although
its own manuals give it the same five-hour label (`spirit-xs895-warranty-coverage-periods`); on
the sheet it sits in the non-dues-paying tier with no hours cap stated.

**`***` is against the CIC800's Wear Items row on both pages, and the sheet's definition is not
the CIC800 manual's.** The sheet says *grips, seats and pedal straps*; the CIC800 2021 manual's
footnote says *grips, end caps, and toe cage straps* (`spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-wear-items`).
Grips are on both lists; the seat is a wear item on the sheet and not in the manual, and end
caps are a wear item in the manual and not on the sheet. Both are Spirit's words. Quote the one
the customer holds and say the other exists.

**`****` is against the CT850ENT, CE800ENT, CR800ENT and CU800ENT, and matches their manuals** -
Console Parts 3 Years and Console Labor 1 Year in the commercial row
(`ct800ent-2022-warranty-console-periods`). The CT900ENT, CE900ENT, CR900ENT and CU900ENT on
page one carry no `****` and the sheet states no console period for them; their manuals print
none either.

**The sheet defers to the manuals.** "See owner's manuals for complete warranty details" is the
sheet's own instruction, and it is right: the sheet prints no exclusions list, no service
steps, no residential row and no effective dates. Where the sheet and a manual disagree - the
CT900's frame and motor, the XS895's frame, the MT200's labor - both are on the table cards.
