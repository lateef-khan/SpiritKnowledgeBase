> Commercial Warranty
>
> \*Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage

| Warranty | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial | 5 Years | 1 Year |

**Two columns and one row is the whole table.** There is no Frame column, no Brake column and no Wear Items column, and no Residential row. The word *Residential* appears nowhere in any of the three manuals.

**So these manuals state no frame period at all.** A customer asking how long the frame is covered has no answer in the book, and *Lifetime* must not be carried across from the CRS800S or CS800 (`spirit-climber-2021-warranty-coverage-periods`) or from the MS300 (`ms300-2021-warranty-coverage-periods`). Tell them the table gives parts and labor only.

**The three tables are identical.** Measured on eight-word phrase shingles the 7.0S, 7.5S and 8.5S warranty sections agree **97.3% to 97.4%** - the difference is the effective date and the machine's name in the heading. The whole-document overlap between the 7.0S and the 7.5S is 82.8%, so the warranty section is far more alike than the books are.

**The prisons footnote is printed above the table, not below it**, in all three - between the *Commercial Warranty* heading and the column row. It is the footnote for a table it precedes. The exclusion also appears as item 8 of the list on the next page: `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.

**The 8.5S-Fit is not on this card.** Its table has the same two columns and the same single row, and its parts figure is **3 Years**, not five: `85s-fit-2026-warranty-parts-labor`. The two machines' warranty sections agree 96.4% - and the four percent that differs is the number a customer is calling about.

**Labor is 1 Year.** The 2021 steppers gave two years and the CSC900 three. There is no range-wide figure.
