**Four different answers, by manual.** Read the row labels and exclusion 2 off the manual that came with the machine.

| Manuals | Spin studio / health club (dues-paying) | Hotel, office, apartment (non-dues-paying) | Home |
|---|---|---|---|
| CB900 2013 | **Covered, with its own row** at a 10-year frame | Covered - the unqualified *Commercial* row, 15-year frame | Covered - Residential row |
| CIC800 2021, CIC850 2022 | **Covered, with its own row** at a 10-year frame | Covered - 15-year frame | Covered - Residential row |
| XIC600 2018, XIC600 2021 | **Excluded** by exclusion 8 | Covered only up to **5 hours use per day**, and no row states terms | Covered - the only row |
| AB900 2018, AB950 2024 | Not excluded, not named - one unqualified *Commercial* row | Covered - Commercial row | Covered - Residential row |
| JB950 2022 | Not excluded, not named - one unqualified *Commercial* row | Covered - Commercial row | **No terms stated** - there is no Residential row |

**Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded on six of the eight** - all but the CB900 2013 and the XIC600 2018, which carry no such clause anywhere (`spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`).

## The three indoor cycles with three rows cover a dues-paying gym and charge for it

The CB900, CIC800 and CIC850 are the only Spirit bikes in this knowledge base whose **best** frame term goes to a home and whose **worst** goes to a health club: 10 years in a dues-paying gym against 15 in a hotel and Lifetime at home (`spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-three-row-periods`).

**But their exclusion 2 narrows reimbursement to the facilities the table just covered.** The CIC800 and CIC850 refuse service-call reimbursement for equipment "not in **non-dues paying facility or household use**" - which is exactly what a health club is not, on a manual whose first row is a health club. Neither manual reconciles the two. **Say both to a customer**: the periods are not conditioned on the facility, only the service-call reimbursement is.

**The CB900 does not have that contradiction.** Its exclusion 2 reads "not in ordinary household or **Commercial** use", which matches its rows. It is the only one of the eight whose environment clauses agree with its own table.

## The XIC600 pair excludes a gym outright, and caps everything else at five hours a day

> 8. Product use in any environment other than a residential setting or non-dues paying facility with 5 hours use or less per day.

**That is the strictest environment clause of the eight**, and it is on the two machines whose table has no commercial row to begin with (`spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-residential-only-periods`). A spin studio charging dues is outside cover on an XIC600. A hotel or office gym is inside it only while the bike is used **five hours a day or less** - a cap no other manual in this range imposes.

**The five-hour cap is stated nowhere but in that exclusion.** Their responsibilities list opens "This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household (**see restrictions above**)" - and no restriction appears above it, only below. Read the cap to the customer even though the sentence pointing at it points the wrong way.

Their exclusion 2 also refuses reimbursement for a bike "not in ordinary household or **light commercial** use", a third phrase neither the table nor exclusion 8 defines.

## The two air bikes and the JB950 name no facility at all

The AB900 and AB950 print a bare **Commercial** row and a **Residential** row with **identical figures**, so on those two machines the environment changes nothing that is written down (`spirit-air-bike-warranty-periods`). **The AB900's exclusion 2 carries no environment clause whatsoever** - it ends at "fitness equipment not requiring component replacement" - so that manual states no use restriction anywhere except its prisons item. **The AB950 adds one**: "not in ordinary household or **light commercial** use", on a page headed *WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL*.

**The JB950 warrants one Commercial row and nothing else**, so a home owner has no stated terms at all (`spirit-jb950-warranty-commercial-only-periods`). Its exclusion 2 carries no environment clause either. Its prisons footnote is the only place the book restricts where the bike may live.

## Do not carry another Spirit range's answer across

**The CR800, CU800 and 800ENT bikes exclude a dues-paying gym outright** by their item 8, and the 900-series bikes state no home terms (`spirit-bike-warranty-use-environment`) - three more answers, on machines a customer will call "a Spirit bike" in the same breath. The CT800 and CT850 treadmills exclude a dues-paying gym (`spirit-ct800-warranty-use-environment`); the ellipticals move from excluded to covered across ten manuals (`spirit-ce-warranty-use-environment`); the CTSBS900 covers **only** a dues-paying facility (`ctsbs900-warranty-use-environment`).
