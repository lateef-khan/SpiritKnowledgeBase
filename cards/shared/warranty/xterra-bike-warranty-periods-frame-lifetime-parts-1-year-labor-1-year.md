| Frame | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|
| Lifetime | 1 Year | 1 Year |

**Home Use Limited WARRANTY**, as printed on the Manufacturer's Limited Warranty page of these four books - the AIR650 Airbike Pro and the three indoor cycles. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**These are the only Xterra bikes in this knowledge base with a lifetime frame and a year of labor together.** The AIR350, the AIR650's sibling, gets one year on the frame and no labor at all (`xterra-warranty-periods-frame-1-year-parts-1-year-labor-none`); the recumbent and upright bikes get 90 days or a year on parts with no labor (`xterra-bike-warranty-periods-frame-1-year-parts-90-days-labor-none`, `xterra-warranty-periods-frame-1-year-parts-1-year-labor-none`); only the SB4500, SB500, SB4.5r, SB600 and RSX1500 pages come close, and those add *& Brake* to the frame column or give more years on parts (`xterra-warranty-periods-frame-and-brake-lifetime-parts-3-years-labor-1-year`, `sb45r-2013-warranty-periods-frame-and-brake-lifetime-parts-5-years-labor-1-year`, `sb600-2023-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`).

**The AIR650 book is a 2021 printing of a 2020 warranty.** Its registration page stamps `AIR650_20210125 Version 5.0`; its warranty page reads *Effective December 12, 2020* and its back cover *© 2020*. The MB500 book is a 2014 scan and its warranty page was read from the render; the figures on it are the ones printed here.

These four books print the registration card duty and the nine-item exclusion list (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-store-display-and-unregistered-products`) and the full six-step SERVICE list with the trip charge (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-service-steps-dealer-service-organization-then-the-service-department`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-owner-pays-freight-parts-shipping-and-trip-charges`). The MB500 and MB550 console pages add three sentences of their own about what voids the warranty - opening the sealed console, caustic cleaners, water (`xterra-warranty-what-the-electrical-and-console-pages-say-is-not-covered`). The Spirit air bike prints different figures (`spirit-air-bike-warranty-periods`); the Spirit indoor cycles print theirs on `spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-residential-only-periods`.
