**Direction Technology** warrants the Universal Group Cycling Monitor free of defects in materials and workmanship.

- The **monitor, chest belt and transmitter carry a one-year warranty**.
- **Batteries are not warranted.**
- The warranty does not cover any defect caused by negligence, misuse, accident, alteration or improper maintenance.
- Only authorised Direction Technology replacement parts may be used, or the warranty and safe operation are not maintained.
- Report problems to the Customer Service Department.

Direction Technology reserves the right to change and improve products without altering products already sold.
