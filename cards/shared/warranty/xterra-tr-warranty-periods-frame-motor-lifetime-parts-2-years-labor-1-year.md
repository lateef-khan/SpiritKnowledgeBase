**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the warranty page of these 2 books, under the heading *Effective June 07 2024 ... LIMITED WARRANTY*. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**The TR75 and TR85 books are one text apart from the model name and the cover SKU** (175873 and 185873); the three warranty pages differ from each other only in the folio. No deck column is printed. The 2023 TR65 in the same layout prints parts 1 year (`tr65-2023-warranty-periods-frame-motor-lifetime-parts-and-labor-1-year`); the two hiker books print the same 2-year parts figure but drop the motor column (`xterra-tr-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-2-years-labor-1-year-no-motor-column`).

The TR75 page dates itself *Effective June 07 2024* without a comma; the TR85 prints *June 07, 2024*.
