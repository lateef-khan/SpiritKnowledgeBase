This warranty applies only to products in **ordinary household use**. The consumer is responsible for:

1. Returning the **warranty registration card within 10 days of the original purchase** — or, **outside the 10-day period, registering with proof of purchase** (receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation).
2. **Proper use including general maintenance**: belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under the motor cover, and lubrication (see all components under General Maintenance).
3. **Proper installation** per the instructions and all local electric codes.
4. **Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage**, replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring.
5. **Expenses for making the treadmill accessible for servicing**, including any item not part of the treadmill when shipped.
6. **Damage to the treadmill finish** during shipping, installation or following installation.
7. **Routine maintenance** as specified in the manual (see all components under General Maintenance).
8. **Providing pictures or video of any issue by email** as requested by the service technician, and **assisting with troubleshooting and diagnosis before a home visit**.

Same in all eight 2023/2025 books (effective February 1, 2023 and February 1, 2025). The F89-2023 book words item 8 differently — "the service department assists in troubleshooting before they are able to send a field technician under warranty ... provide the media via email, if asked" — same duty, carded as printed. This list matches the 2026 duties in substance (see `f63-2026-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`); the older 7-item list without duties 1b and 8 is on `sole-treadmill-warranty-responsibilities-7-item-household`.
