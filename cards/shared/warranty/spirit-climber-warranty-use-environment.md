**There is no single answer for this range. Four different books give four different ones.**

| Machines | Rows the table prints | Is a dues-paying gym covered? |
|---|---|---|
| **CSC880 2025** | Commercial (Dues Paying), Commercial (Non-Dues Paying), Residential | **Yes - it has its own row** |
| **XS895 2018 and 2021** | Light Commercial (5 Hours use or less in a non-dues paying facility), Residential | No, and exclusion 8 bars it |
| **CRS800S, CS800, CVC800 2021** | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities), Residential | No, and exclusion 8 bars it |
| **CSC900 2019 and 2021** | Commercial | Not stated either way |
| **7.0S, 7.5S, 8.5S, 8.5S-Fit** | Commercial | Not stated either way |
| **MS300** | commercial | Not stated either way |

## The CSC880 is the only one that covers a dues-paying club

Its table gives a dues-paying facility its own row of periods - **and that row is not simply the worst one**: it gives three years of labor where the other two rows give two. A YMCA or health club buying a CSC880 has terms; on any other machine in this range it does not (`csc880-2025-warranty-coverage-periods`).

## On the XS895 and the three 2021 machines a gym is excluded outright

Their exclusion list item 8 reads:

> Product use in any environment other than a residential setting or non-dues paying facility with 5 hours use or less per day.

**That is two conditions, and the second is easy to miss.** Even a facility that charges no dues loses cover above **five hours of use per day**. On the XS895 the row label repeats the cap - *Light Commercial (5 Hours use or less in a non-dues paying facility)* - so the machine states it twice; on the CRS800S, CS800 and CVC800 the row label says only *Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities)* and the five-hour cap appears only in item 8, where a customer reading the table alone will not find it. The list is `spirit-ce850-warranty-exclusions`.

## On the other nine the manual simply does not say

**The CSC900, the four 2025-and-2026 steppers and the MS300 print one commercial row and no dues-paying item anywhere in their exclusion lists.** So there is no row of periods for a health club and no sentence excluding one, and none of these books ever says which it means.

**Tell a customer the table gives them no row rather than telling them they are excluded.** What can be read to them is the tail of exclusion 2, which refuses service call reimbursement for equipment "not in ordinary household or light commercial use" - on the CSC900, in its own wording, "not in non-dues paying facility or household use". That is a refusal of one reimbursement, not an exclusion of the machine.

**On the MS300 even that sentence is absent.** Its exclusion 2 stops at "or units not requiring component replacement" and never reaches the household-or-light-commercial clause, so nothing in the MS300 restricts where it is used at all except the prisons footnote (`ms300-2021-warranty-exclusions`).

## Prisons are excluded on eleven of the thirteen

The two XS895 manuals state it nowhere; the other eleven do, in a footnote under the table, in the exclusion list, or both. **It is the only environment the 2025 and 2026 books exclude by name**: `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.

## Do not answer a 2024 machine from this card

The CRS800S, CS800 and CSC900 were reissued in September 2024 with tables that changed. `spirit-2024-warranty-use-environment` holds those.
