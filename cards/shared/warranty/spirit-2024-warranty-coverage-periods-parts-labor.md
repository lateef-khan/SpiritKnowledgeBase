| Warranty | Frame\* | Parts | Labor | Wear Items\*\* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | 10 Years | 5 Years | 2 Years | 6 Months |
| Residential | 10 Years | 10 Years | 2 Years | 6 Months |

\*Frame is defined as the welded metal base of the unit and does not include removable parts, wear items, or coatings.

\*\*Wear items include: plastic covers, overlay, grips, hardware, pull pins/knobs, decals/stickers, headphone jack, USB port, paint/coatings/finish, straps, and pedals.

> Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage.

**Seven machines of four different kinds print this table figure for figure** - two ellipticals, a recumbent bike, an upright bike, a semi-recumbent stepper, a stepper and a rower. Each was read both out of the extracted text and off a 300 dpi render of its own printed page; the four value columns and the two footnotes are the same on all seven, and only the machine's name in the sentence above the table changes.

**The frame is 10 Years in both rows, a home included.** No cell in this table says *Lifetime*. Every earlier Spirit CE800, CE850, CR800 and CU800 manual warrants the frame for **Lifetime** in both rows (`spirit-ce-warranty-commercial-periods`, `spirit-bike-warranty-commercial-periods`), and the CE900 2025 keeps Lifetime in its Residential row. The 2024 range dropped it.

**There is no brake or electronics column.** These manuals state no separate period for the brake their predecessors warranted for 5 years, so a brake is covered by the Parts column and nothing else.

**Parts are 5 Years in a gym and 10 at home**; labor is **2 Years** in both rows, and wear items **6 Months** in both.

**The CRS800S, the CS800 and the CRW800 have no predecessor here.** They are new machines, not reprints, so nothing in this knowledge base offers an older figure to fall back on - and the CRW800 is **not** the CRW800H2O water rower, whose warranty is a different document about a different machine.

**The opening paragraph does not match the heading or the table.** Each page is headed WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL and warrants "all its **home use**" parts, administered "directly to a **consumer**", above a duty list addressed to a **facility**. None of the seven reconciles it - `spirit-2024-warranty-use-environment`.

The nine exclusions on the next page are `spirit-ce-2025-warranty-exclusions`, and the CRS800S and CS800 are two of the six whose exclusion 2 breaks off mid-sentence. The duty list under the table is `spirit-ce-2025-warranty-facility-responsibilities`.
