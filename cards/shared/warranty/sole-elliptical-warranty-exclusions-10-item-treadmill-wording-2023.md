All five 2023 books print the same ten exclusions, copied from the 2023 treadmill page and verified on the render. Items 1–2 and 4–7 match the older nine-item list; new or reworded:

1. Consequential/collateral/incidental damages (unchanged).
2. Service-call reimbursement (unchanged).
3. Damage from non-authorized service or non-SOLE parts, "**or** external causes such as **corrosion (RUST)**, discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, **improper maintenance (see general maintenance instructions section), inadequate power supply, natural disasters, or elemental damage (i.e. power surges, lightning storms, water damage, or fire damage)**" — quoted nearly verbatim; the RUST callout and the disaster list are new in 2023.
4. Removed or altered serial numbers (unchanged).
5. Sold, transferred, bartered or given away — now with "**(Warranties DO NOT transfer.)**".
6. No registration card on file (unchanged).
7. Merchantability/fitness disclaimer (unchanged).
8. Non-residential use (unchanged).
9. Outside North America vary, **plus "Shipping charges may apply to Alaska and Hawaii customers"** — new in 2023.
10. "**Warranty will be void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment. (Examples: Garage, outside, carport, etc)**" — new in 2023.

**Ruling:** followed as printed — the rust, no-transfer, AK/HI-shipping and climate clauses are real 2023 terms even though the page was copied from the treadmill book (same ten items sit on `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-10-item-rust-climate-2023`). An elliptical kept in a garage or carport is outside cover under item 10.
