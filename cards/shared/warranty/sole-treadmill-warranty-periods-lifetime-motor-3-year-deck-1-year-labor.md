**Treadmill warranty, effective August 1, 2012** on the F60-2013, F60-2014, F63-2013 and F63-2015 books; **effective January 1, 2008** as printed in the AF63/AF65-2011 books; **effective February 1, 2016** (F63-2016/F65-2016 book); **effective February 1, 2019** (F63-2019, F65-2019 and TD80-2019 books); and **effective February 1, 2023** (F80-2023, F85-2023 and F89-2023 books) — same figures under all dates.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Drive Motor** | **Lifetime** |
| **Deck** | **3 Years** |
| **All Other Components** | **3 years** |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Labor** | **1 Year** |
| **Cosmetic Items** * | **90 Days** |

*Cosmetic items including, but not limited to: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.

The F80/F85/F89-2023 books print the Frame row first and the TD80-2019 book — a desk treadmill — still carries these ordinary household-use terms, carded as printed. The AF63/AF65 books carry a stale page: their own footer revision stamp is F63_65_20111222 (December 2011) while the warranty head still reads January 1, 2008. Carded as printed, per the ruling: the printed table wins over the revision stamp.

When the clock starts is separate; see `sole-treadmill-warranty-start-date-receipt-or-18-months-shipping`. What is not covered is on `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-9-item-residential`.
