**All six manuals close the warranty section with the same two sentences:**

> Product features or specifications as described or illustrated are subject to change without notice. All warranties are made by \<the company\>. **This warranty applies only in the 48 contiguous United States. NOTE: This does not apply to Alaska or Hawaii.**

**Alaska and Hawaii are named and excluded.** Not "may vary" - excluded. An owner in either state is outside the warranty on all six machines, and no manual offers an alternative arrangement.

**Canada, Mexico and everywhere else fall outside the 48 states too**, and four of the six add a numbered exclusion saying so - *Warranties outside of the United States may vary. Please contact your local dealer for details.* The two **water rower** manuals print no such item; their eight-item list stops at prisons. The 48-states sentence still reaches their owners through this closing paragraph, so the answer is the same, but the customer reading only the exclusion list will not find it.

**The two water rowers give an overseas owner a route the others do not**, in their CUSTOMER SUPPORT box and again inside SERVICE step 3: *For international customers, please contact your local distributer*: `spirit-rower-ergometer-warranty-service-steps`.

**The company named in the sentence is not the same in all six**, on the same page of the same document family:

| Manual | "All warranties are made by..." |
|---|---|
| XRW600 2019, XRW600 2021, 8.5UE | **Spirit Fitness, Inc.** |
| CRW800 2021, CRW900, CRW800H2O | **Spirit Manufacturing, Inc.** |

**The CRW800 manual uses both names on the same page** - *SPIRIT FITNESS, INC.* in the mailing address two lines above and *Spirit Manufacturing, Inc.* in the closing sentence. The manuals never explain the relationship between the two entities. Report both names as printed rather than picking one; a customer quoting "Spirit Manufacturing" is not quoting a different company's warranty.

**The 8.5UE prints the paragraph twice**, at the foot of its exclusions page and again at the foot of its service page, and the two differ by one word: *does not **include** Alaska or Hawaii* on the first, *does not **apply** to Alaska or Hawaii* on the second. The rule is unchanged.
