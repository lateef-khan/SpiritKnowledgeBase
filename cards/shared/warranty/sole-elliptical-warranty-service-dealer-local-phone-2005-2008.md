Keep the bill of sale. Service under this warranty must be obtained in order:

1. Contact the selling authorized Sole dealer. OR
2. Contact the local authorized Sole service organization.
3. "If there is a question as to where to obtain service, contact our service department at **(870) 935-1107**" in the 2007 books; the VE-2008 books print **866-MYSOLE1 (697-6531)** here instead. Carded per book.
4. With no local service, Sole repairs or replaces parts at its option within the warranty period at no charge for parts; the owner pays all transportation both ways, packs adequately, claims freight damage with the carrier, and never ships without a **return authorization number**.
5. By mail: **Sole, P.O. Box 2037, Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037**.

There is **no online service-request step** — that arrives with the WE books in 2009. The treadmill twin of these steps is `sole-treadmill-warranty-service-dealer-steps-2004-2010`.
