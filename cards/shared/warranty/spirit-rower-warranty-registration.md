## The deadline, and who states it

Three of the five state one, as item 1 of the responsibilities list on the first warranty page - both XRW600 printings and the CRW800:

> The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within **10 days** of the original purchase to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.

**The two water rower manuals state no deadline at all.** Their responsibilities list is a six-bullet list with no registration bullet. A CRW900 or CRW800H2O owner is told to register on the front-matter page and is never given a date.

## Where to register

Every one of the five prints a *Register Your Purchase* page in the front matter, and **the address is not the same on all five**:

| Manual | Page the manual sends you to |
|---|---|
| XRW600 2019 | https://www.spiritfitness.com/residentialwarrantyregistration.html |
| XRW600 2021 | www.spiritfitness.com/warranty |
| CRW800 2021 | http://www.spiritfitness.com/warranty |
| CRW900 2021 | https://www.spiritfitness.com/commercialwarrantyregistration.html |
| CRW800H2O | https://www.spiritfitness.com/commercialwarrantyregistration.html |

All five add the same instruction first: *The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to Spirit Fitness*, with the online page offered under the **Support** tab as the alternative.

**The two water rowers send the owner to the commercial page even though both sell to homes.** Their tables carry a Residential row. Do not read the URL as a statement about which warranty applies.

## What happens if nobody registers

**This is where the range splits, and the difference is real cover, not paperwork.**

On both XRW600 printings and the CRW800, failing to register is itself an **exclusion** - item 6 of the list:

> Products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at Spirit Fitness. Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.

On the CRW900 and the CRW800H2O, item 6 keeps only the second sentence:

> Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.

So an unregistered air rower is excluded on the face of the book, and an unregistered water rower is not - it is a machine Spirit may ask for a receipt about. **Read the customer's own manual before saying either.**

**A dated bill of sale is the fallback in every case.** The SERVICE paragraph on all five tells the owner to keep it, and dates the term from it - or, failing that, from eighteen months after factory shipping as determined by the serial number.
