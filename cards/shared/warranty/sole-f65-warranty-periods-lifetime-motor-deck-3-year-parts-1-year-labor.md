**Treadmill warranty, effective August 1, 2012.**

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Drive Motor** | **Lifetime** |
| **Deck** | **Lifetime** |
| **All Other Components** | **3 years** |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Labor** | **1 Years** (sic — the book writes "Years") |
| **Cosmetic Items** * | **90 Days** |

*Cosmetic items including, but not limited to: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.

This is the F65-only middle grade: a lifetime deck like the F80 books but 3-year parts and 1-year labor like the F63 books. Do not confuse it with `sole-treadmill-warranty-periods-lifetime-motor-3-year-deck-1-year-labor` (3-year deck) or `sole-treadmill-warranty-periods-lifetime-motor-deck-5-year-parts-2-year-labor` (5-year parts, 2-year labor).

When the clock starts is separate; see `sole-treadmill-warranty-start-date-receipt-or-18-months-shipping`. What is not covered is on `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-9-item-residential`.
