**Six steps, in order, and the manual says to follow them in order. All thirty-nine
strength books print them identically.**

1. Contact your selling authorized Spirit Fitness dealer. **OR**
2. Contact your local authorized Spirit Fitness service organization.
3. If there is a question as to where to obtain service, contact the service department
   at **(870) 935-1107**.
4. Spirit Fitness' obligation is limited to repairing or replacing the product, at Spirit
   Fitness' option, through one of its authorized service centers. **All repairs must be
   preauthorized by Spirit Fitness.**
5. The owner is responsible for adequate packaging on return. Spirit Fitness is not
   responsible for damages in shipping; make all freight damage claims with the freight
   carrier. **No unit may be shipped to the factory without a return authorization
   number** - see `spirit-strength-returns-authorization-required`.
6. For further information, or to contact the service department by mail:

> SPIRIT FITNESS, INC.
> P.O. Box 2037
> Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037

**Step 4 is the one that costs money if it is skipped.** An unauthorized repair is
excluded outright by exclusion 3, along with any non-original part:
`spirit-strength-warranty-exclusions-eight-items`.

**The P.O. Box is a mailing address, not a returns address.** Step 6 is for
correspondence; a physical return needs the authorization number from step 5 first. The
three ST800 books print a separate street address on their back page - 3000 Nestle Road,
Jonesboro, AR 72401 - with 800.258.4555 and spiritservice@spiritfitness.com, and the
thirty-six current books print 1-800-258-8511 on their cover.

**The three ST800 books add one sentence ahead of step 1**: "Keep your bill of sale."
That belongs to the labour clock rather than the steps:
`spirit-strength-warranty-labor-period`.
