Spirit Fitness warrants the parts of these treadmills for the periods below, in **ordinary household use**.

| Warranty | Frame | Motor | Deck | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | Lifetime | Lifetime | Lifetime | 10 Years | 1 Year |

**The row is word for word the same in all five 2023 XT manuals.** Only the effective date printed above it changes:

| Manual | Effective date |
|---|---|
| XT185 | July 6, 2023 |
| XT285 | July 06, 2023 |
| XT385 | September 12, 2023 |
| XT485 | September 12, 2023 |
| XT685 | September 12, 2023 |

The XT685 manual prints a **second** table below this one, a Light Commercial row with shorter motor, deck and parts periods. The other four manuals print no commercial table at all - see `xt685-2023-light-commercial-warranty-periods`.

Two things this table does not tell you on its own. When the clock starts is stated in the paragraph above it (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`), and the one-year labor figure is dated separately in the SERVICE paragraph three pages later (`xt-2023-warranty-labor-period`).

The periods here are **not** the Spirit commercial figures. A CT900 residential row reads 3 Years labor and adds a Wear Items column; these manuals have neither.
