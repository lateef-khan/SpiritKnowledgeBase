**Treadmill warranty, effective December 1, 2020** (ST90-2021 book, covering ST90-2020 and ST90-2021). Read off the page render (PDF p. 29, printed 27).

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Frame** | **5 years** |
| **Motor** | **5 years** |
| **Deck** | **3 years** |
| **Electronics** | **3 years** |
| **Parts** | **12 Months** |
| **Labor** | **12 Months** |

*Cosmetic items including, but not limited to the following: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.* **No cosmetic period is printed** — the footnote names the items but gives no days or months, unlike every other stencil book.

Duties, exclusions, service and registration are the ordinary 7-item/9-item/serviceparts set — see `sole-treadmill-warranty-responsibilities-7-item-household`, `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-9-item-residential` and `sole-treadmill-warranty-service-online-request-steps`. Do not confuse with the 2023 ST90 terms (`st90-2023-warranty-periods-5-year-frame-motor-3-year-parts`), which drop the electronics row and stretch parts to 3 years.
