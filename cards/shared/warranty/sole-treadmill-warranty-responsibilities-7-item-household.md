This warranty applies only to products in **ordinary household use**. The consumer is responsible for:

1. Returning the **warranty registration card within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the warranty.
2. **Proper use** per the manual, **including belt tracking**.
3. **Proper installation** per the instructions and all local electric codes.
4. **Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage**, replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring.
5. **Expenses for making the treadmill accessible for servicing**, including any item not part of the treadmill when shipped.
6. **Damage to the treadmill finish** during shipping, installation or following installation.
7. **Routine maintenance** as specified in the manual.

Identical in all ten books (effective January 1, 2008 stale, August 1, 2012, February 1, 2016 and February 1, 2018). The TT8 books word items 1 and 4 for facilities instead — see `sole-tt8-warranty-responsibilities-facility-wording`. The 2026 books add late registration with proof and photo/video duties — see `f63-2026-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`.
