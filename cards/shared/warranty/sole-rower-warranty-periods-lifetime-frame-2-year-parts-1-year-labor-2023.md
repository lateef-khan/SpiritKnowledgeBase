**Rower warranty, effective February 1, 2023.**

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **All Other Components** | **2 Years** |
| **Labor** | **1 Year** |

No cosmetic row and no cosmetic footnote are printed on this page. The clock starts on the date of retail sale by receipt, or eighteen (18) months from factory shipping without one. The SERVICE paragraph's twelve-months-from-bill-of-sale labor rule agrees with the table's 1 year, so there is no labor conflict here.

**This is not the 3-year SR500 term.** The SR500-2019 and SR500-2022 books give all other components 3 years: `sole-rower-warranty-periods-lifetime-frame-3-year-parts-1-year-labor`.
