**The FB160 and FB360 SERVICE paragraph reads: *Keep your bill of sale. Warranty begins from the date on the bill of sale or three (3) months from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number should service be required.*** Every other Xterra book in this knowledge base prints *Twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale or eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping ... establishes the warranty period* in that place (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**The same page contradicts itself.** Its opening paragraph, two inches above, says the periods run *from the date of retail sale, as determined by a sales receipt or in the absence of a sales receipt, eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date* - the standard sentence. So without a receipt the top of the page gives eighteen months from shipping and the bottom three. This card records both and follows the **three-month** sentence for these two books: it is the sentence written for them (a 90-day parts warranty on a bike that ships and sells within a season), it is the one a service department would apply, and the eighteen-month sentence is the Dyaco boilerplate that opens every Xterra warranty page unchanged. A customer with a receipt is unaffected - the date on the bill of sale governs either way.

**What three months means in practice.** With 90 days on parts (`xterra-bike-warranty-periods-frame-1-year-parts-90-days-labor-none`) and no receipt, a bike that shipped from the factory three months before the claim is already out of its parts warranty. The FB160 service document of November 2019 (`xterra-bike-fb160-2019-service-document`) puts the term the customer sees as *90 Days from date of purchase* and *Must register WARRANTY*; the ten-day registration card is how the purchase date gets on file (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover`).

The FB150, FB350 and FB180 - the other folding bikes - print the twelve/eighteen-month sentence or none at all (the FB180 has no SERVICE paragraph, `fb180-2025-warranty-service-steps-not-printed-use-the-cover-contacts`).
