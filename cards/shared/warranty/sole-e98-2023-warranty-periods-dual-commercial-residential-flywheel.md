**E98 warranty on the Sole E98-2023, effective August 1, 2023**, verified on the render (PDF p. 30). The brake rows of the 2011–2019 table are gone; both columns now carry a lifetime **flywheel**, and the columns split only on other-components.

| Component | Commercial (5 hours or less use per day) | Residential |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame Weldments** (commercial) / **Frame Weldment** (residential, sic) | **Lifetime** | **Lifetime** |
| **Flywheel** | **Lifetime** | **Lifetime** |
| **All Other Components** | **2 Years** | **3 Years** (printed "3Years", sic) |
| **Labor** | **1 Year** | **1 Year** |
| **Cosmetic Items** * | **90 Days** | **90 Days** |

\* Cosmetic items including, but not limited to: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.

Unlike the 2011–2019 E98 table, labor is 1 year in *both* columns and the residential column no longer carries 2-year labor. The book's duties and exclusions pages are treadmill copy ("Proper use of the treadmill", "belt tracking") — see `sole-elliptical-warranty-responsibilities-8-item-treadmill-wording-2023`.
