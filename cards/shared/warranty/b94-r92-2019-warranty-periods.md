Warranty runs from the date of retail sale as determined by sales receipt, or in the absence of a receipt, **eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date**.

Both models carry the same residential warranty, and all service is performed in-home.

**The effective date printed above this table is not the same in every year:** the 2013 manual prints *Effective January 1, 2011*, the 2016 manual *February 1, 2016* and the 2019 manual *February 1, 2019*.

**Other years and the LCB/LCR are different and are not covered here.** The 2023 B94 and R92 print Parts/Electronics **2 Years**. The LCB and LCR of 2013, 2016 and 2019 print **5 Years**, and the 2023 LCB/LCR prints a two-column table, Residential 3 Years and Light Commercial 2 Years.

| Component | B94 | R92 |
|---|---|---|
| Parts/Electronics | 3 Years | 3 Years |
| Frame | Lifetime | Lifetime |
| Labor | 1 Year | 1 Year |

SOLE's responsibilities include providing new or remanufactured parts, at SOLE's option, and technical support to independent dealers and servicing organizations. Where there is no dealer or service organization, SOLE administers the warranty directly to the consumer.

Warranties outside North America may vary. Contact your local dealer.
