**The same four steps, in the same order, in all six manuals.** Take them in order; the manuals say *in order*.

1. **Contact your selling authorized Spirit Fitness dealer.** OR
2. **Contact your local authorized Spirit Fitness service organization.**
3. **If there is a question as to where to obtain service**, contact the service department at **(870) 935-1107**.
4. Spirit Fitness' obligation is limited to **repairing or replacing, at Spirit Fitness' option**, through one of its authorized service centers. **All repairs must be preauthorized by Spirit Fitness.**

Then the packaging and return-authorization step (`spirit-rower-ergometer-returns-authorization-required`), and a sixth: for anything further, write to **Spirit Fitness, Inc., P.O. Box 2037, Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037**.

**Repairing or replacing is Spirit's choice, not the owner's.** No manual here offers a refund, a credit or a replacement on demand.

**Nothing may be done to the machine before it is preauthorized.** A repair the owner arranges themselves is excluded twice over - once as unpreauthorized, and again as a repair by someone other than an authorized Spirit Fitness service company.

**The two water rowers print a second number and an international route, and no other manual here does.** The CRW900 and CRW800H2O add a **CUSTOMER SUPPORT** box - *(870) 935-1107 (Local) or (800) 258-8511* - and repeat *For international customers, please contact your local distributer* twice, once in the customer support box and once inside step 3. The four other manuals give (870) 935-1107 alone and route an overseas owner only through the exclusion line *Warranties outside of the United States may vary. Please contact your local dealer.*

**The number to give an 8.5UE facility is (800) 258-8511.** The ergometer's back page prints *3000 Nestle Road Jonesboro, AR 72401 | Phone: 800-258-8511 | Fax: 870-935-7611*, while its SERVICE step 3 still says (870) 935-1107. Both are in the same book.

**The rower back covers print a third number, 800.258.4555**, alongside `spiritservice@spiritfitness.com` on the XRW600 books and `Questions@spiritfitness.com` on the CRW900. That is a sales and general-enquiry number on the cover, not the number the SERVICE list names. When a customer says "the number in the manual", ask which page.

**The SERVICE paragraph above the list is the same six lines in all six manuals**: keep your bill of sale; twelve months from the date on it, or eighteen months from factory shipping as determined by the serial number, establishes the labor warranty period; keep all receipts; this warranty gives specific legal rights and others vary from state to state.
