**No.** Both water rower manuals print the same caution at the head of their assembly instructions:

> **CAUTION:** Damage to the rower during assembly is not covered as part of the limited Spirit Fitness warranty. Take care.

**This is the only place either manual says it.** The eight-item exclusion list on the warranty page does not mention assembly at all - it covers abuse, misuse, accident and improper maintenance in general terms and stops there: `spirit-water-rower-warranty-exclusions-eight-items`. A reader who goes straight to the warranty section will not find this rule.

**The related duty is on the warranty page, and it is not the same rule.** The responsibilities list makes the owner responsible for *damages to the fitness equipment finish during shipping, installation or following installation* - finish only. This caution is broader: any damage during assembly, not only to the finish.

**No air rower manual prints it.** The words do not appear in either XRW600 printing or in the CRW800 2021 manual. Do not quote this caution to the owner of one; their answer has to come from the general exclusions instead.

**The manuals do not say who assembles the machine.** Neither requires professional assembly, and neither says that dealer assembly transfers the risk. If a dealer assembled it and damaged it, the manual is silent on who bears the cost.

**Do not answer this from the bike card.** `spirit-bike-warranty-assembly-damage-excluded` states the same rule for the Spirit AB900 and JB950 - the same brand but a different product line, and its wording and its machines are its own.
