Keep your bill of sale. Twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale, or eighteen (18) months from the factory shipping date as determined by the serial number, establishes the labor warranty period.

Service must be obtained by following these steps, **in order**:

1. Contact your selling authorized SOLE dealer, **or**
2. Submit all service requests — serial number, contact information and a brief description of the problem — online at `http://www.soletreadmills.com/serviceparts.html`.
3. If there is a question as to where to obtain service, contact the service department at **866-780-7653**.
4. If no local service is available, SOLE will repair or replace the parts, at SOLE's option, within the warranty period at no charge for parts. **All transportation costs, both to the factory and on return to the owner, are the owner's responsibility.** The owner is responsible for adequate packaging on return. SOLE is not responsible for damage that occurs during shipping; make all freight damage claims with the freight carrier. **Do not ship any unit to the factory without a return authorization number — units arriving without one will be refused.**
5. The service department assists in troubleshooting before sending a field technician. SOLE may request pictures and video of the issue by email to help with diagnosing.

**The 2023 manuals print no mailing address and no other phone number.** The 2011 to 2019 manuals end this list with the SOLE Fitness P.O. Box in Jonesboro, AR and give two support numbers, 1-866-780-SOLE (7653) and 866-MYSOLE1 (697-6531). The 2023 revision replaced the mail step with the troubleshooting step above and leaves **866-780-7653** as the only number in the warranty section.

Assisting with troubleshooting and supplying pictures or video is also listed as a consumer responsibility in these manuals, not merely as an offer of help.
