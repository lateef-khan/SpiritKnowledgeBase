**The only table is headed Commercial, and nothing in the book says which commercial setting it means.** The six 2025 medical bike manuals print one row - Parts 5 Years, Labor 1 Year (`spirit-med-bike-warranty-commercial-parts-five-years-labor-one-year`) - and address the duties list to "the facility". No row is labelled dues-paying or non-dues-paying, no hours-per-day limit is printed, and the words *clinic*, *hospital*, *therapy* and *gym* do not appear on the warranty pages. A physical-therapy clinic, a hospital gym and a health club are all simply "commercial" here; the manual draws no line between them.

**Prisons are the one named exclusion.** The asterisk above the table and item 8 of the exclusions bar prisons and correctional facilities (`spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`).

**A home is not given a row, but the book keeps talking about one.** The opening paragraph says the warranty is administered "directly to a consumer" where no dealer exists, and exclusion 2 refuses service-call reimbursement for a bike "not in ordinary household or light commercial use" - wording carried over from manuals that print a Residential row. These six print none. A home buyer's question has no period in the source; say so rather than reading one off the commercial row, and do not promise the "light commercial" phrase means anything more than the row that is printed.

This is the same shape as the 2026 4.0T, 7.0T and 8.0T treadmills (`spirit-2026t-warranty-use-environment`). It is not the commercial bike books, which split a dues-paying gym from a non-dues-paying facility and a home across three rows (`spirit-bike-warranty-use-environment`).
