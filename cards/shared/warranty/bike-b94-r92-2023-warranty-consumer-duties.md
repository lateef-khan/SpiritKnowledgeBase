This warranty applies only to products in **ordinary household use**. The consumer is responsible for:

1. Completing and returning the **warranty registration card** within 10 days of the original purchase to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty, **or**, outside that 10 day period, registering with proof of purchase (receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation).
2. *"Proper use of the treadmill in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual, including general maintenance (i.e. belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under motor cover, and lubrication. See all components under General Maintenance section)"*.
3. Proper installation in accordance with instructions provided with the fitness bike and with all local electric codes.
4. Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage, replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring.
5. Expenses for making the fitness bike accessible for servicing, including any item that was not part of the fitness bike at the time it was shipped from the factory.
6. Damages to the fitness bike finish during shipping, installation or following installation.
7. Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual (see All Components under General Maintenance).
8. Providing pictures or video of any issues by email as requested by the service technician, and assisting with troubleshooting and diagnosing prior to a home visit.

**Item 2 is quoted exactly as printed, and it is treadmill text sitting in a bike manual.** It names a treadmill, belt tracking, belt tension, a motor cover and lubrication — none of which this bike has. Read it as proper use of the bike in accordance with this manual, including the general maintenance the manual specifies for the bike.

The 2011 to 2019 manuals for these two models have seven items, with a plain "proper use of the fitness bike", no proof-of-purchase fallback, and no pictures-and-video duty.
