**E95S warranty, effective August 1, 2016** (E95S-2015-2016 book) and **effective August 1, 2019** (E95S-2019 book) — same figures under both dates. A single **RESIDENTIAL** column only; no commercial column and no cosmetic row are printed.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Frame & Brake** | **Lifetime** |
| **Parts** | **5 Years** |
| **Labor** | **2 Year** (sic — "2 Year", as printed in both books) |

The E95S-2023 book replaces this table with lifetime frame plus lifetime flywheel, 3-year others and 1-year labor: `sole-elliptical-warranty-periods-lifetime-flywheel-3-year-parts-2023`.
