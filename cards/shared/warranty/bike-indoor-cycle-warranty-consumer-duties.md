This warranty applies only to products in **ordinary household use**. The consumer is responsible for:

1. Completing and returning the **warranty registration card** to the address on the card **within 10 days** of the original purchase, to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.
2. Proper use of the indoor cycle in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual.
3. Proper installation in accordance with instructions provided with the indoor cycle.
4. Expenses for making the indoor cycle accessible for servicing, including any item that was not part of the indoor cycle at the time it was shipped from the factory.
5. Damages to the indoor cycle finish during shipping, installation or following installation.
6. Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual.

Six items. Item 3 stops at "provided with the indoor cycle" — unlike the B94, R92, LCB and LCR manuals it does not add "and with all local electric codes", and there is no grounded-power-supply item at all.

**This paragraph says household use only, while the warranty table on the same page prints a LIGHT COMMERCIAL column.** Exclusion 8 sides with this paragraph: "product use in any environment other than a residential setting". The manual does not reconcile them, so do not promise light commercial cover from the table alone.

The exclusions list is separate and says what the warranty does not cover at all.
