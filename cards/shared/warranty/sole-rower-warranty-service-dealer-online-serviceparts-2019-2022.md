Keep the bill of sale. Service under this warranty must be obtained in order:

1. Contact the selling authorized SOLE dealer. OR
2. Submit all service requests — serial number, contact information, brief problem description — online at **http://www.soletreadmills.com/serviceparts.html**.
3. Warranty questions: **1-866-780-SOLE (7653)**. Technical problems with the Rower: **866-MYSOLE1 (697-6531)**.
4. With no local service, SOLE repairs or replaces parts at its option within the warranty period at no charge for parts; the owner pays all transportation both ways and never ships without a **return authorization number** (the 2019 book prints "RO. Box 2037" by OCR confusion; both renders read **P.O. Box 2037, Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037**).
5. By mail: **SOLE Fitness, P.O. Box 2037, Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037**.

Same five steps in both books. The 2023 book routes questions through 866-780-7653 and adds troubleshooting before a technician visit: `sole-rower-warranty-service-troubleshoot-first-2023`.
