This warranty does not cover:

1. **Consequential, collateral or incidental damages** from any breach of written or implied warranty (some states disallow this exclusion).
2. **Service-call reimbursement** to the consumer, or to the dealer where no malfunction or defect is involved, the unit is beyond the warranty or service-call-reimbursement period, the adjustable dumbbells need no component replacement, or they are not in ordinary household use.
3. Damage from **non-authorized service**, non-SOLE parts, or external causes — alterations, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, acts of God (no corrosion or power-supply wording here).
4. Products with **removed or altered serial numbers**.
5. Products that have been **sold, transferred, bartered, or given** to a third party (the books print "been; sold", sic).
6. Products **used as store display models**.
7. Products with **no warranty registration on file** (SOLE may request proof of purchase).
8. Use **outside a residential setting**.
9. All other warranties, **merchantability and fitness** disclaimers.

Same nine items, same order, in all three books. The store-display item is unique to this family among the Sole strength books.
