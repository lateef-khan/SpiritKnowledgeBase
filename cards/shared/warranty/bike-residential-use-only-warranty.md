The cover page carries an ATTENTION panel:

> THIS FITNESS BIKE IS INTENDED FOR RESIDENTIAL USE ONLY AND IS WARRANTED FOR THE APPLICATION. ANY OTHER APPLICATION VOIDS THIS WARRANTY IN ITS ENTIRETY.

The SB700 manuals print the identical sentence with **INDOOR CYCLE** in place of FITNESS BIKE.

**On the LCB and LCR bikes this panel contradicts the warranty page in the same manual.** Those manuals print a LIGHT COMMERCIAL warranty column; define light commercial as a non-dues paying facility with 5 hours of bike use or less per day; and, from 2013 onward, exclude only "any environment other than a residential or light commercial (as defined above) setting". The cover panel says residential only and nothing reconciles the two. Do not answer a light commercial question about an LCB or LCR from this panel alone — read the warranty page of that manual.

**The same friction exists on the SB700 and the pre-2023 SB900.** Their warranty table also carries a light commercial column, while this panel, the consumer-responsibilities paragraph and exclusion 8 all say residential only. Here the balance of the manual favours residential only: two of the three passages agree with the panel.

For the B94, R92 and the 2011 LCB and LCR the panel and exclusion 8 agree — residential only.

The panel is **absent** from the SB900 2014/2016, 2019, 2020 and 2023 manuals and from the SB1200 2023 manual, so it cannot be quoted for sb900-2014, sb900-2016, sb900-2019, sb900-2020, sb900-2023 or sb1200-2023. Exclusion 8 of those manuals still limits cover to a residential setting.

This notice is the whole of what the B74 and R72 2015 owner's manual says about warranty: that manual prints no warranty section, no periods, no exclusions and no service steps.
