## The deadline

Unlike the 2023 XT manuals, these three print one:

> The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within **10 days** of the original purchase, or **completed online**, to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.

It is item 2 of RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE FACILITY, on the same page as the warranty table.

## How to register

- Scan the **QR code** on the Warranty Registration page with a smartphone, **or**
- go to **spiritfitness.com/warranty**, under the **Support** tab, and register online, **or**
- post the registration card to the address printed on the card.

The PRODUCT REGISTRATION page asks the buyer to record **Serial Number**, **Date of Purchase**, and **Dealer / Place of Purchase**. The serial number sticker is on the **back of the motor cover**, and the manual says to record the number below the barcode "for the purpose of registering your Treadmill's warranty".

Customer care, on the opening pages: **1-800-258-8511**, **service@spiritfitness.com**. That address is not the one the 2023 XT manuals print, which is questions@spiritfitness.com.

## What happens if nobody registers

**The manuals do not say the warranty is void.** Registration is called a condition that "validates" the limited warranty, but the exclusion list never excludes an unregistered product. What it says instead is exclusion 6:

> Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.

The two sentences are not reconciled anywhere in the source. Treat an unregistered machine as exposed to a proof-of-purchase demand rather than as uncovered, and keep the bill of sale - which is also what fixes the labor period (`xt-2023-warranty-labor-period`) and what the clock runs from when there is no receipt (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`).

**Do not carry a deadline across from another manual, or the absence of one.** The 2023 XT manuals impose no deadline at all; the 2015 XT manuals impose a 10-day card deadline **and** exclude an unregistered product outright; the CTSBS900 requires online registration with no day count. Only the CT900ENT matches these three word for word.

**The 2025 printings of the 4.0T (February and May) and 7.0T say the same** - the QR code, spiritfitness.com/warranty, the ten-day card-or-online duty and the proof-of-purchase exclusion - word for word.
