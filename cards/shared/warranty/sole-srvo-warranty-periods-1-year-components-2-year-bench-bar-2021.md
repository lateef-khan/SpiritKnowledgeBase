**SRVO warranty, effective February 1, 2021.**

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **All Components** | **1 year** |
| **Labor** | **1 years** (sic — the book prints the plural) |
| **Bench / Straight bar** | **2 Years** |

The clock starts on the date of retail sale by receipt, or eighteen (18) months from factory shipping without one — the same rule as the other Sole books. The SERVICE paragraph's twelve-months-from-bill-of-sale labor rule agrees with the table's 1 year, so there is no labor conflict here.

**This is not the seminar warranty.** The seminar slides give the SRVO unit, labor and accessories 1 year each with a separate 1-year bench / 90-day cosmetic bench term: `srvo-warranty` and `srvo-bench-warranty`. This book's 2-year bench/straight-bar row replaces both of those for the SRVO-2021.
