**Five dates, not four.** Each strength manual prints its own effective date on the
warranty page, and the date follows the family rather than the machine.

| Effective date | Machines | Family |
|---|---|---|
| December 29, 2023 | CSF-FUNT | Functional trainer |
| February 21, 2024 | CSD-ACBE, CSD-BCTE, CSD-CPSP | Dual station |
| February 22, 2024 | CSD-ITOT, CSD-LELC, CSD-LPCE, CSD-LPSR, CSD-PFRD, CSD-PUDA | Dual station |
| March 05, 2024 | CSF-AABB, CSF-BEXT, CSF-HRAC, CSF-LEGP, CSF-UPRB | Functional and bench |
| July 02, 2024 | all nineteen CSS selectorized machines | Single station |
| January 01, 2026 | CSI-CPSP, CSI-LROW | Newest generation |
| November 24, 2021 | ST800DR3, ST800FI, ST800FT | Benches and rack |

**The dual-station books split over two consecutive days.** Three carry February 21
and six carry February 22, 2024. Anyone quoting one date for the whole CSD line will
be wrong about six machines.

**The ST800 books do print an effective date.** All three read
*EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 24, 2021* in capitals, on the printed page - it is in the native
text layer and it survives a 300 dpi render. It is the oldest warranty in the strength
range by more than two years.

**A later revision did not move the effective date.** The CSS manuals were re-issued
through 2025 - their product registration page stamps *Version: 2.0* with revisions
running from March to June 2025 - and every one still carries *Effective July 02, 2024*.
The revision stamp names the printing; the effective date names the warranty.

Those revision stamps are not written in one date format. Several CSS books read
`23/04/2025`, `13/05/2025` and `19/03/2025`, which can only be day-first, while others
read `06/26/2025`, which can only be month-first. Do not read a day out of a CSS
revision stamp without checking that the other number is above twelve.

The coverage each date buys differs by family; see the period cards linked below.
