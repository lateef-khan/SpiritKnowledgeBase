Three warranty sentences sit outside the warranty page, in the maintenance and electrical chapters of every Xterra treadmill book. They are what consumer responsibility *proper use ... including maintenance* / *routine maintenance* means in practice (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-with-a-registration-card`, `xterra-trx-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-seven-items`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-without-registration`).

| What the page says | Where | Books |
|---|---|---|
| **ATTENTION: Damage to the running belt resulting from improper tracking / tension adjustments is not covered under the XTERRA Fitness warranty.** (The TR150 and TR200 say *the XTERRA warranty*.) | Tread-belt tracking page | 12 books: TR150, TR200, TR260, TR300, TR6.4, TR6.6, TRX1000, TRX2500, TRX3500, TRX4500, TRX5500, TRX1400 |
| **NOTE: FAILURE TO COMPLETE PERIODIC MAINTENANCE WILL VOID THE WARRANTY. SEE THE MAINTENANCE & CARE SECTION OF THE MANUAL.** and, on the same page, *PLEASE NOTE WITHOUT DOING PERIODICAL MAINTENANCE MIGHT VOID THE WARRANTY.* | Setting Up Your Treadmill, p. 17 | TR75H only |
| **Failure to lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty.** (the older books: *Failure to clean and lubricate the deck ...*) | Belt/deck lubrication page | all 19 |
| **If voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more, the performance of your treadmill may be affected. Such conditions are not covered under your warranty.** | Important Electrical Information / Electrical Safety page | all 19 |

**Seven Online-Support books print no belt-adjustment warranty sentence at all** - the TR65, TR75, TR85, TR75H, TR95H, WS200 and WS300 belt-adjustment pages carry only *DO NOT OVERTIGHTEN*, checked on a render of the TR65 page (its text layer also holds a ghost line naming an *L Allen Wrench (132)* that is not printed). Only the lubrication and voltage sentences reach all nineteen books.

Together with the warranty page's *improper maintenance* and *inadequate power supply* in exclusion 3 (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-store-display-and-unregistered-products`, `xterra-trx-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-floor-models-corrosion-and-discoloration`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-with-a-non-dues-facility-allowance`), these are the grounds a warranty claim on a slipping, frayed or worn belt, a burnt drive belt or motor, or a controller that failed on a brown-out is refused. The Dyaco service manuals for the TRX1400, TRX2500 and TRX3500/4500 repeat the tension-adjustment sentence in capitals inside their belt-replacement pages.

How to track, tension and lubricate the belt are maintenance procedures on their own cards, not here; this card holds only what the sentences say about cover. The Sole and Spirit books print the same three sentences (`spirit-2026t-warranty-belt-adjustment-damage`).
