This warranty does not cover:

1. **Consequential, collateral or incidental damages** from any breach of written or implied warranty (some states disallow this exclusion).
2. **Service-call reimbursement** to the consumer, or to the dealer where no malfunction or defect is involved, the unit is beyond the warranty or service-call-reimbursement period, the Rower needs no component replacement, or it is not in ordinary household use.
3. Damage from **non-authorized service**, non-SOLE parts, or external causes — corrosion (**RUST**), discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance (see general maintenance instructions), inadequate power supply, natural disasters, elemental damage (power surges, lightning storms, water damage, fire damage).
4. Products with **removed or altered serial numbers**.
5. Products **sold, transferred, bartered or given** to a third party. **(Warranties DO NOT transfer.)**
6. Products with **no warranty registration card on file** (SOLE may request proof of purchase).
7. All other warranties, **merchantability and fitness** disclaimers.
8. Use **outside a residential setting**.
9. Warranties **outside North America** vary — contact the local dealer. Shipping charges may apply to Alaska and Hawaii customers.
10. **Warranty void outside a climate-controlled, insulated environment** (garage, outside, carport, etc.).

The 2023 treadmill twin with the same ten items is `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-10-item-rust-climate-2023`. The SR500 books print the older nine-item list without the climate item: `sole-rower-warranty-exclusions-9-item-2019-2022`.
