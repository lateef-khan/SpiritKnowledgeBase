**Nine items, the same nine in all three air rower manuals** - both XRW600 printings and the CRW800:

1. **CONSEQUENTIAL, COLLATERAL, OR INCIDENTAL DAMAGES** such as property damage and incidental expenses resulting from any breach of this written or any implied warranty. *Some states do not allow this exclusion, so it may not apply to you.*
2. **Service call reimbursement to the consumer.** Reimbursement to the dealer is also excluded where it does not involve malfunction or defects in workmanship or material, for units beyond the warranty period, for units beyond the service call reimbursement period, for a Rower not requiring component replacement, or a Rower not in ordinary household or light commercial use.
3. **Damages caused by anyone other than an authorized Spirit Fitness service company**, by parts other than original Spirit Fitness parts, or by external causes - corrosion, discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, or acts of God.
4. **Products whose original serial number has been removed or altered.**
5. **Products that have been sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party.**
6. **Products with no warranty registration card on file at Spirit Fitness.** Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists.
7. This warranty is **expressly in lieu of all other warranties** expressed or implied, including merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose.
8. **Product use in any environment other than a residential setting or non-dues paying facility with 5 hours use or less per day.**
9. **Warranties outside of the United States may vary.** Contact your local dealer.

**Item 6 is the harshest one and it is not on every Spirit rower.** On these three, *not registering* is itself an exclusion - the product is out of warranty, and proof of purchase is a discretion Spirit reserves rather than a right the owner has. The two water rower manuals and the 8.5UE drop that clause and keep only the proof-of-purchase sentence: `spirit-water-rower-warranty-exclusions-eight-items`. Read the customer's own book before telling them an unregistered machine is uncovered.

**Item 5 ends the warranty at the first resale**, by gift as well as by sale. A second-hand XRW600 or CRW800 has no manufacturer's warranty.

**Item 8 is the use limit, and it is the whole of the environment answer on these three** - there is no separate dues-paying item: `spirit-rower-warranty-use-environment`.

**Prisons are not in this list.** The CRW800 and the November 2021 XRW600 exclude prisons in a footnote under the warranty table instead, and the February 2019 XRW600 excludes them nowhere at all: `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.

**Item 2 is printed in full on all three.** The final clause - *or a Rower not in ordinary household or light commercial use* - is present in every one. The truncation that stops the sentence at "not requiring component replacement" is on the two **water** rower manuals, not on these.

**"Inadequate power supply" at item 3 has a second half in the safety pages.** Both XRW600 manuals state on printed p. 6 that a voltage variation of ten percent or more is *not covered under your warranty*, and that a house breaker tripping while the machine's own breaker holds is *not a warranty defect* - the fix being a high-inrush breaker on a dedicated 15 amp circuit. A customer arguing a supply fault is answered by that page as well as by this one: `spirit-house-breaker-needs-a-high-inrush-type`. The CRW800 manual prints neither passage; its only statement is item 3 here.
