## The deadline

All four CT800 manuals, all four CT850 manuals and three Spirit **elliptical** manuals - the CE800 2021, the CE800 2023 and the CE800ENT - print one, as item 1 of the responsibilities list on the first warranty page:

> The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within **10 days** of the original purchase, or completed online to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.

## How to register

The front-matter page says the same thing twice over:

> The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to Spirit Fitness. You can also go to \<the registration page\> under the **Support** tab to register online.

**The registration page is not the same address in all eleven manuals.** Read it off the manual that came with the machine:

| Manual | Page the manual sends you to |
|---|---|
| CT800 2012 | https://www.spiritfitness.com/residentialwarrantyregistration.html |
| CT800 2016 | http://www.spiritfitness.com/commercialwarrantyregistration.html |
| CT800 2020 | http://www.spiritfitness.com/warranty |
| CT800ENT 2022 | http://www.spiritfitness.com/warranty |
| CT850 2016 | http://www.spiritfitness.com/warranty |
| CT850 2018 | http://www.spiritfitness.com/commercialwarrantyregistration.html |
| CT850 2020 | http://www.spiritfitness.com/warranty |
| CT850ENT 2022 | http://www.spiritfitness.com/warranty |
| CE800 2021 | http://www.spiritfitness.com/warranty |
| CE800 2023 | http://www.spiritfitness.com/warranty |
| CE800ENT | http://www.spiritfitness.com/warranty |

**The 2012 CT800 manual sends the owner of a commercial treadmill to the residential registration page**, and the 2016 manual for the same machine sends them to the commercial one. Neither manual explains the difference, and neither URL resolves to a documented form in this knowledge base. Treat the 2012 link as a documentation defect rather than as evidence the CT800 is a residential machine - the same manual prints a Commercial warranty row and excludes dues-paying facilities.

**On the CT850 the older-looking manual carries the newer address.** The CT850 2018 manual sends the owner to `commercialwarrantyregistration.html` while the CT850 2016 manual sends them to `/warranty`, the address the 2020 and ENT manuals use. Model year is no guide to which link is current; read the link off the manual in hand.

None of the eleven manuals prints a QR code, a registration phone number or an email address on that page. The three CE800 elliptical manuals head their page REGISTER YOUR PURCHASE and print the Record Your Serial Number box on it, as the CT800 2020 and the two ENT treadmills do. Each prints a Record Your Serial Number box - on the same page as Register Your Purchase in the CT800 2012, 2020 and CT800ENT and in the CT850 2016, 2020 and CT850ENT, and elsewhere in the CT800 2016 (a page earlier) and the CT850 2018 (beside the operation instructions, p. 6). The CT800 2016 and 2020 and the CT850 2016 and 2020 manuals say the serial number is **at the front of the treadmill by the Power switch**; the CT800ENT and the CT850ENT print a Serial Number Location illustration instead, and the CT800 2012 and the CT850 2018 state no location at all.

## What happens if nobody registers

**These manuals say the product is excluded.** This is the harsh version of the exclusion, and it is exclusion 6 in all eleven:

> Products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at Spirit Fitness. Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.

**The two sentences of item 6 do not say the same thing** - the first excludes the product outright, the second only reserves a right to ask for proof of purchase - and none of the eleven manuals reconciles them. Say both to a customer. Keeping the bill of sale matters either way: it is what fixes the labor period (`spirit-ct800-warranty-labor-period`) and what the clock runs from when there is no receipt (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`).

**Do not carry another Spirit manual's registration rule across.** The 2023 XT manuals impose **no** deadline and drop the outright exclusion entirely; the CT900 and CTSBS900 require online registration with no day count; the CT900ENT and the 2026 machines print the 10-day card deadline but not the outright exclusion. The other seven Spirit elliptical manuals differ again - three CE850 manuals offering no online route, a CE900 2025 naming no card and no deadline at all, and two 2025 manuals that drop the outright exclusion (`spirit-ce-warranty-registration`). Only the 2015 XT manuals, the 2010 XT685 and the XT485ENT match the CT800 and CT850 on both halves - and the 2010 XT685 sends the owner to a third address again, `residentialwarrantyregistration.html` with no Support tab named. See `xt-2015-warranty-registration`.

## Six Spirit commercial bike manuals state the same rule

The CU800 2012, the CR800 2021 and 2023, the CU800 2021, the CU800ENT 2022 and the CR800ENT 2023 print the 10-day item 1 and the two-sentence exclusion 6 word for word. Their registration pages:

| Manual | Page the manual sends you to |
|---|---|
| CU800 2012 | www.spiritfitness.com/warranty.asp, under the **support** tab |
| CR800 2021, CU800 2021, CU800ENT 2022, CR800 2023, CR800ENT 2023 | http://www.spiritfitness.com/warranty |

**The CU800 2012 address is the only Spirit address in this knowledge base ending `.asp`, and it is legible on the page.** Its extracted text mangles it - `wwwspiritfitness.com/warranty.asp` in one OCR pass and `wwwsspiritfitness.com/warranty.asp` in the other - but page 5 of `CU800_OM_)800343.pdf`, rendered at 300 dpi and rotated 180 degrees, prints it cleanly as **www.spiritfitness.com/warranty.asp**, underlined, "under the support tab to register online". The lost dot and the doubled `s` are OCR damage, not what the manual says.

None of the six prints a QR code, a registration phone number or an email address on that page. All six head it REGISTER YOUR PURCHASE with a RECORD YOUR SERIAL NUMBER box.

**The other seven bikes state the rule differently.** The CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900ENT and CU900ENT keep the 10-day card deadline and exclusion 6 but send the owner to two different addresses; the CR900 2025, CU900 2025 and CU800ENT 2024 print a QR code, require online registration with no day count, and drop the outright exclusion. All seven are at `spirit-ce-warranty-registration`.
