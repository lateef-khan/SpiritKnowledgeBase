## The deadline

All four CT800 manuals print one, as item 1 of the responsibilities list on the first warranty page:

> The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within **10 days** of the original purchase, or completed online to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.

## How to register

The front-matter page says the same thing twice over:

> The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to Spirit Fitness. You can also go to \<the registration page\> under the **Support** tab to register online.

**The registration page is not the same address in all four manuals.** Read it off the manual that came with the machine:

| Manual | Page the manual sends you to |
|---|---|
| CT800 2012 | https://www.spiritfitness.com/residentialwarrantyregistration.html |
| CT800 2016 | http://www.spiritfitness.com/commercialwarrantyregistration.html |
| CT800 2020 | http://www.spiritfitness.com/warranty |
| CT800ENT 2022 | http://www.spiritfitness.com/warranty |

**The 2012 manual sends the owner of a commercial treadmill to the residential registration page**, and the 2016 manual for the same machine sends them to the commercial one. Neither manual explains the difference, and neither URL resolves to a documented form in this knowledge base. Treat the 2012 link as a documentation defect rather than as evidence the CT800 is a residential machine - the same manual prints a Commercial warranty row and excludes dues-paying facilities.

None of the four manuals prints a QR code, a registration phone number or an email address on that page. Each prints a Record Your Serial Number box - on the same page as Register Your Purchase in the 2012, 2020 and CT800ENT manuals, and a page earlier in the 2016 one. The 2016 and 2020 manuals say the serial number is **at the front of the treadmill by the Power switch**; the CT800ENT prints a Serial Number Location illustration instead, and the 2012 manual states no location at all.

## What happens if nobody registers

**The CT800 manuals say the product is excluded.** This is the harsh version of the exclusion, and it is exclusion 6 in all four:

> Products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at Spirit Fitness. Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.

**The two sentences of item 6 do not say the same thing** - the first excludes the product outright, the second only reserves a right to ask for proof of purchase - and the manuals never reconcile them. Say both to a customer. Keeping the bill of sale matters either way: it is what fixes the labor period (`spirit-ct800-warranty-labor-period`) and what the clock runs from when there is no receipt (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`).

**Do not carry another Spirit manual's registration rule across.** The 2023 XT manuals impose **no** deadline and drop the outright exclusion entirely; the CT900 and CTSBS900 require online registration with no day count; the CT900ENT and the 2026 machines print the 10-day card deadline but not the outright exclusion. Only the 2015 XT manuals and the XT485ENT match the CT800 on both halves - see `xt-2015-warranty-registration`.
