| Residential | Lifetime | 2 Years | 1 Year |

**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the warranty page of these 2 books, under the heading *Effective May 29, 2025 ... LIMITED WARRANTY*. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**The two Hiker Treadmill books print no Motor column** - three figures only, verified on a render of the TR75H page. No motor period is stated anywhere in either book, so the drive motor falls under the two-year Parts figure. The TR75 and TR85 of the same layout print *Motor Lifetime* as a fourth column (`xterra-tr-warranty-periods-frame-motor-lifetime-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`); the TRX1400, WS200 and WS300 use this three-column shape with 90 days (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-and-labor-90-days-no-motor-column`).

Both books call the machine a *Hiker Treadmill* throughout the warranty text where every other Xterra book says *treadmill*; the terms are otherwise the same three-page block. The TR95H page is effective January 03, 2024; the TR75H page May 29, 2025.
