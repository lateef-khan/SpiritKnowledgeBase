This warranty does not cover items 1–7 of `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-9-item-residential` — consequential damages, service-call reimbursement, non-authorized service and external causes, altered serial numbers, sold/transferred/bartered products, no registration card on file, all other warranties disclaimed — **with no residential-setting and no outside-North-America items**. Two immaterial wordings: item 3 says "authorized Sole servicers" in the 2004/2007 books and "authorized SOLE service provider(s)" in the 2009/2010 books, and it never lists corrosion or discoloration. Geography for these books is a separate statement, not an exclusion item — see `sole-treadmill-warranty-geographic-coverage`.
