**Elliptical warranty on the Sole E25-2007 and E55-2006, effective October 15, 2005.** The book warrants "all its elliptical parts for a period of **3 years**" from retail sale (or 18 months from factory shipping without a receipt), then extends three rows:

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Labor** | **2 Years** |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Brake** | **Lifetime** |
| **All Other Components** | **3 Years** |

No cosmetic row is printed. The SERVICE paragraph on the same page says twelve (12) months from the bill of sale establishes the labor warranty period — the table's 2 years is the more specific statement and this card follows it.

These are the oldest terms in the wave: 6 consumer duties (no routine-maintenance item), 7 exclusions (no residential-setting and no outside-North-America items, and no corrosion/discoloration clause), service through the dealer or a local service organization at (870) 935-1107 with no online step, and cover in the 50 states only — each on its own card, linked above.
