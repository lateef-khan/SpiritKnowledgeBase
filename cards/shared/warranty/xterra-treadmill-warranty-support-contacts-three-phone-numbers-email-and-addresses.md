**Two different service-department numbers are printed, and the layout decides which.** The eleven older-layout and 2024 TRX books give **(870) 336-4286** for both customer service and the warranty service department. The eight Online-Support books keep (870) 336-4286 on the registration and labels pages but give **(870) 935-1107** in warranty step 3 - the number the Spirit manuals print for Spirit's service department (`xt-2023-warranty-service-steps`) - and add the toll-free 1-800-258-8511 and the email on the cover. No book explains the difference; the older books never print the 800 number, the email, the fax or the Nestle Road street address at all.

The steps that tell the owner when to call are `xterra-treadmill-warranty-service-steps-dealer-service-organization-then-the-service-department`; the registration pages that carry the (870) 336-4286 number are `xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover` and `xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-qr-code-and-support-tab-no-deadline`.
