**Every Spirit rower warranty prints its own effective date in the heading.** That date, not the copyright year and not the filename, is what says which terms a machine is sold under:

| Machine | Warranty heading | Effective |
|---|---|---|
| XRW600 (2019 printing) | Rower Warranty | **February 04, 2019** |
| CRW800 | ROWER WARRANTY | **October 08, 2021** |
| CRW900 | CRW900 WATER ROWER WARRANTY | **October 22, 2021** |
| CRW800H2O | CRW800H2O WATER ROWING MACHINE WARRANTY | **November 12, 2021** |
| XRW600 (2021 printing) | Rower Warranty | **November 30, 2021** |

**Four of the five fall inside seven weeks of autumn 2021.** The odd one out is the February 2019 XRW600, and it is the only rower manual here that warrants the brake for life and the only one that says nothing about prisons.

**The two XRW600 printings are the same machine.** A customer with an XRW600 has one of two warranties, and the manual is the only way to tell them apart: the 2019 book has a **Brake** column, the 2021 book does not.

**The CRW900's date belongs to the page, not to the book.** Its back cover stamps *© 2019 All Rights Reserved / Revision 4: 10.11.2019*, two years before the warranty page it is bound into - `crw900-2021-warranty-effective-date-against-a-2019-back-cover`.

**The CRW800H2O has no year in its model id, and that is deliberate.** Only one CRW800H2O manual exists in this knowledge base; its warranty is effective November 12, 2021.

**Do not read a CRW800 date off this card if the customer's book is a 2024 one.** The CRW800 was reissued in autumn 2024 on a different warranty page: `spirit-2024-warranty-effective-dates`. October 08, 2021 is the predecessor.

**An effective date is a floor, not a print date.** It says when these terms took effect, not when the book was printed - which is why the CRW900 can carry a 2021 date on a 2019 book.
