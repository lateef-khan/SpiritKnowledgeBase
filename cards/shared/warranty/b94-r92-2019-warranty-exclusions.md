The warranty does **not** cover:

1. Consequential, collateral, or incidental damages such as property damage and incidental expenses resulting from any breach of this written or any implied warranty. *Some states do not allow this exclusion, so it may not apply to you.*
2. Service call reimbursement to the consumer. Also service call reimbursement to the dealer where the call does not involve malfunction or defects in workmanship or material; for units beyond the warranty period; beyond the service call reimbursement period; where no component replacement is required; or where the bike is not in ordinary household use.
3. Damage caused by service performed by anyone other than authorized SOLE service companies; use of parts other than original SOLE parts; or external causes such as corrosion, discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, or acts of God.
4. Products with original serial numbers removed or altered.
5. Products that have been **sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party**.
6. Products without a warranty registration card on file at SOLE. SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists.
7. This warranty is expressly in lieu of all other warranties expressed or implied, including merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose.
8. **Product use in any environment other than a residential setting.**
9. Warranties outside of North America may vary.

Voltage varying by 10% or more is also called out separately as not covered.
