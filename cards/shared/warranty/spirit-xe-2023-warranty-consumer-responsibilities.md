> This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household use, and the **consumer/facility** is responsible for the items listed below:

1. Proper use of the Elliptical in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual
2. Proper installation in accordance with instructions provided with the Elliptical and with all local electric codes.
3. Expenses for making the Elliptical accessible for servicing, including any item that was not part of the Elliptical at the time it was shipped from the factory.
4. Damages to the Elliptical finish during shipping, installation or following installation.
5. Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual.

**Five items, where every earlier XE manual prints six or seven.** Two duties were dropped and neither returns:

- **Registering the warranty is no longer on the list.** The 2005, 2007, 2010 and 2021 manuals all open the list with a registration card due within 10 days. Neither 2023 manual names registration as a responsibility, and neither prints a deadline anywhere. Their exclusion 6 drops the outright exclusion of an unregistered product as well - see `spirit-xe-warranty-registration` before telling a 2023 customer they had to register.
- **The grounded-power-supply duty is gone.** Blown fuses, loose connections and defects in house wiring are not put on the owner by these two manuals (`spirit-xe-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-electrical-duty` holds the version that does).

**The lead sentence differs between the two manuals, and it is the sentence that says what use is covered.** The XE795 2023 reads "ordinary household **or light commercial** use", matching the Light Commercial table its manual prints. The XE395 2023 reads "ordinary household use" only - while its exclusion 8 still permits a non-dues-paying facility of five hours a day that its table gives no periods for. The manual never reconciles the two (`spirit-xe-warranty-use-environment`).

**Both manuals address the "consumer/facility"** in a list that is otherwise about a home.

Two items bite in practice. **Item 3** makes getting the machine to a place where it can be worked on the owner's expense, not a warranty cost. **Item 4** puts finish damage during shipping and installation on the owner, so inspect the machine before the carrier leaves. The full bill is `xt-2023-warranty-owner-costs`.

The other half of what governs the warranty is the nine exclusions on the next page, in `spirit-xe-2023-warranty-exclusions`.
