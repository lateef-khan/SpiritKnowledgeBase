This warranty does not cover:

1. **Consequential, collateral or incidental damages** (some states disallow this exclusion).
2. **Service-call reimbursement** to the consumer, or to the dealer where no malfunction or defect is involved, the unit is beyond the warranty or service-call-reimbursement period, the elliptical needs no component replacement, or it is not in ordinary household use.
3. Damage from non-authorized service, non-SOLE parts, or external causes — alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, acts of God. The 2007 books' clause reads "authorized **Spirit** service companies" (sic) and omits the corrosion / paint-discoloration phrase the VE, WE and all later books carry. Carded as printed.
4. Products with **removed or altered serial numbers**.
5. Products **sold, transferred, bartered or given** to a third party.
6. Products with **no warranty registration card on file** (SOLE may request proof of purchase).
7. All other warranties, **merchantability and fitness** disclaimers.

There is **no residential-setting item and no outside-North-America item** — those arrive with the 2011 nine-item list (`sole-elliptical-warranty-exclusions-9-item-2011-2019`). The 50-states geographic sentence stands apart on `sole-elliptical-warranty-geographic-50-states-2005-2007`. The treadmill twin of this list is `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-7-item-2004-2010`.
