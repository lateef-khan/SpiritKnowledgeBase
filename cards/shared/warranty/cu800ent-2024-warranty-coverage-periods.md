| Warranty | Frame\* | Parts | Console Parts, Labor | Console Labor | Wear Items\*\* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | 10 Years | 5 Years | 2 Years | 1 Year | 6 Months |
| Residential | 10 Years | 10 Years | 2 Years | 1 Year | 6 Months |

\*Frame is defined as the welded metal base of the unit and does not include removable parts, wear items, or coatings.

\*\*Wear items include: plastic covers, overlay, grips, hardware, pull pins/knobs, decals/stickers, headphone jack, USB port, paint/coatings/finish, straps, and pedals.

> Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage.

Effective **October 08, 2024**, headed **WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL** with the table itself titled *Commercial Limited Warranty*.

**There is no general Labor column.** The table warrants Frame, Parts, the console and Wear Items, and states no labor period for anything but the console. The only other labor figure the manual prints is the SERVICE paragraph's clock - twelve months from the bill of sale, or eighteen months from factory shipping (`xt-2023-warranty-labor-period`) - and the manual never says whether that is the labor warranty this table omits. **Say that the table states none rather than quoting a period from another manual.**

**There is no brake or electronics column either**, so the manual states no separate period for the EMS brake its 2022 predecessor warranted for 5 years.

**The frame is 10 Years in both rows, a home included.** Every earlier CU800 and CR800 manual warrants the frame for **Lifetime** in both rows (`spirit-bike-warranty-commercial-periods`), and the CR900 2025 and CU900 2025, dated a week earlier, keep Lifetime in two of their three rows. This is the only Spirit bike table in this knowledge base with no Lifetime in it at all.

**Two column headings overlap and the manual does not resolve them.** The third column reads **Console Parts, Labor** at 2 Years and the fourth reads **Console Labor** at 1 Year. Read literally the console's labor is covered for two years by one column and one year by the other. The 2022 CU800ENT prints the same two columns as *CONSOLE PARTS* 3 Years and *CONSOLE LABOR* 1 Year, which is what the pair is elsewhere in the range (`ct800ent-2022-warranty-console-periods`), so the likeliest reading is console parts 2 years and console labor 1 year - **but this manual does not say so.** Quote both headings to a customer and do not silently drop the word *Labor* from the third.

**Console parts dropped from 3 years to 2.** The CU800ENT 2022 and CR800ENT 2023 warrant CONSOLE PARTS for 3 Years; this table's console column reads 2 Years in both rows.

**No dues-paying row.** Unlike the CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 tables of the same season, this one prints Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) and Residential only, so a dues-paying gym has no row here (`spirit-bike-warranty-use-environment`).

**The opening paragraph does not match the heading or the table.** The page is headed WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL and warrants "all its **home use** Upright Bike parts", administered "directly to a **consumer**", above a responsibilities list addressed to a **facility**. The manual never reconciles them.

**Every figure and heading above was read off the rendered PDF page, not only out of the extracted text.** Page 57 of `CU800ENT_OwnersManual_800365_20241009.pdf` was rendered at 300 dpi and read both by eye and by OCR: the five value columns, the absent Labor column and the two overlapping console headings are exactly as the manual prints them. The same manual's exclusion 2 also genuinely breaks off mid-sentence on its own printed page (`spirit-ce-2025-warranty-exclusions`).
