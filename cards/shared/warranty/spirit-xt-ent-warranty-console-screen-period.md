**Two years in a home.** Both ENT manuals add a console screen column to the warranty table that no other Spirit XT manual prints, and both give it **2 Years** in the residential row.

| Manual | What the column is called | Residential | Light commercial |
|---|---|---|---|
| XT485ENT | Console Parts | 2 Years | *no light commercial table printed* |
| XT685ENT | Touchscreen | 2 Years | 1 Year |

The column sits between Parts and Labor in the printed table. **It is not the Parts period.** Parts is 10 Years residential and 3 Years light commercial on these machines; the screen is two years and one year. Quoting the Parts column for a dead console promises eight years the manual does not give.

**Light commercial halves it.** The XT685ENT is the only one of the two with a second table, and its touchscreen row there reads **1 Year** - the same as labor, and the shortest period anywhere in that manual. The XT485ENT prints no light commercial table at all, so its manual states no screen period for any use outside a home.

The other five figures in these tables - Frame, Motor, Deck, Parts, Labor - are word for word the shared XT rows and are held elsewhere: `xt-2023-warranty-residential-coverage-periods` for the residential row and `xt685-2023-light-commercial-warranty-periods` for the light commercial one. Neither of those cards carries a screen column, because the ten manuals they were written from do not print one.

**No Sole card in this knowledge base holds a console screen period**, and the base XT485 and XT685 manuals do not print one. Do not read this 2 Years onto a non-ENT machine. The commercial Spirit ENT machines print console periods of their own and they are different figures - `ct800ent-2022-warranty-console-periods`.

Two things the manuals do not say. They never define what counts as "Console Parts" or "Touchscreen" - whether the figure covers the whole console assembly or only the display - and they never say which column governs a fault that could be either. Neither manual reconciles them.

## The Spirit XE395ENT elliptical prints the same figure

Its Elliptical Trainer Warranty table, effective **February 25, 2021**, adds a column headed simply **Console** between Parts and Labor, and gives it **2 Years** in its single Residential row - the same period the two XT ENT treadmills give theirs.

| Manual | What the column is called | Residential | Light commercial |
|---|---|---|---|
| XE395ENT | **Console** | 2 Years | *no light commercial table printed* |

**It is not the Parts period on that machine either.** Parts is 10 Years and the brake is Lifetime; the console is two years. Quoting the Parts column for a dead console promises eight years the manual does not give. The other four figures in its row - Frame Lifetime, Brake Lifetime, Parts 10 Years, Labor 1 Year - are the shared XE residential row and are held at `spirit-xe-warranty-residential-periods-ten-year-parts`, which carries no console column.

**The XE395ENT is the only Spirit XE or XG residential manual with a console column.** The base XE395 manuals of 2010 and 2023 print none, and neither do the XE195, XE295, XG400 or XE795. Do not read this 2 Years onto a non-ENT machine.

**A commercial Spirit ENT machine splits the figure in two and neither half is this one.** The CT800ENT, CT850ENT and CE800ENT print *Console Parts 3 Years* and *Console Labor 1 Year* - `ct800ent-2022-warranty-console-periods`. The XE395ENT prints one Console column and no separate console labor figure at all.

**The same two things go unsaid on the XE395ENT.** It never defines what counts as the Console - the whole assembly or only the display - and never says which column governs a fault that could be either.

## The two Spirit ENT bikes print the same column, and the same 2 Years

The **XBR55ENT** and the **XBU55ENT**, both effective November 30, 2021, add a **Console** column between Parts and Labor of their warranty table and give it **2 Years**:

| Warranty | Frame | Brake | Parts | Console | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | Lifetime | Lifetime | 10 Years | **2 Years** | 1 Year |

**Neither prints a light commercial table**, so on these two there is no second console figure to halve - the XT485ENT is in the same position. **It is not the Parts period**: parts is 10 Years here, and quoting that column for a dead console promises eight years the manual does not give (`spirit-xb-warranty-residential-periods`).

**No other Spirit XB bike manual has a console column.** The XBR95, XBR55, XBR25 and XBU55 books print none, in any of their twelve printings, so a console fault on one of those has no stated period at all. Do not read this 2 Years onto a non-ENT bike.

**The two ENT bikes are the only XB manuals with a prisons footnote under the table**, printed directly below this row (`spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`).

**They never define what counts as the Console** - the whole assembly or only the display - and never say which column governs a fault that could be either. That is the same silence the XE395ENT leaves.
