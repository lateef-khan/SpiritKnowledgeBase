**Two years in a home.** Both ENT manuals add a console screen column to the warranty table that no other Spirit XT manual prints, and both give it **2 Years** in the residential row.

| Manual | What the column is called | Residential | Light commercial |
|---|---|---|---|
| XT485ENT | Console Parts | 2 Years | *no light commercial table printed* |
| XT685ENT | Touchscreen | 2 Years | 1 Year |

The column sits between Parts and Labor in the printed table. **It is not the Parts period.** Parts is 10 Years residential and 3 Years light commercial on these machines; the screen is two years and one year. Quoting the Parts column for a dead console promises eight years the manual does not give.

**Light commercial halves it.** The XT685ENT is the only one of the two with a second table, and its touchscreen row there reads **1 Year** - the same as labor, and the shortest period anywhere in that manual. The XT485ENT prints no light commercial table at all, so its manual states no screen period for any use outside a home.

The other five figures in these tables - Frame, Motor, Deck, Parts, Labor - are word for word the shared XT rows and are held elsewhere: `xt-2023-warranty-residential-coverage-periods` for the residential row and `xt685-2023-light-commercial-warranty-periods` for the light commercial one. Neither of those cards carries a screen column, because the ten manuals they were written from do not print one.

**No other Spirit or Sole card in this knowledge base holds a console screen period**, and the base XT485 and XT685 manuals do not print one. Do not read this 2 Years onto a non-ENT machine.

Two things the manuals do not say. They never define what counts as "Console Parts" or "Touchscreen" - whether the figure covers the whole console assembly or only the display - and they never say which column governs a fault that could be either. Neither manual reconciles them.
