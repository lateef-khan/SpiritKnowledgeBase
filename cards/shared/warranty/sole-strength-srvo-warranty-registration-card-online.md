Both SRVO books carry the same Product Registration page:

- **Record the serial number** in the space provided ("RECORD YOUR SERIAL NUMBER ... in the space provided below"), with spaces for the **dealer name, dealer telephone number and purchase date**.
- "The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to SOLE." Or register online at **www.soletreadmills.com under the support tab** (the Complete book prints "www. Soletreadmills.com" with a space, sic).
- The SRVO-2021 warranty-duties list adds the deadline: **within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the warranty. The Complete book prints no warranty page, so no deadline exists for it.
