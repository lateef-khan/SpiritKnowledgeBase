**Treadmill warranty, effective February 1, 2016** (TT8-2016 book, covering TT8-2016 and TT8-2016-AC), **effective February 1, 2019** (TT8-2019 book, covering TT8-2019 and TT8-2020) and **effective February 1, 2020** (TT8-2021 book) — same table under all three dates. The TT8-2021 page was read off the render (PDF p. 34, printed 32).

| | **Residential** | **Light commercial (5 hours or less use per day)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame** | **Lifetime** | **Lifetime** |
| **Drive Motor** | **Lifetime** | **5 Years** |
| **Parts** | **5 Years** | **3 Years** |
| **Labor** | **2 Year** (sic — the books write "Year") | **1 Year** |

**This is a lifetime residential motor, not the 5-year motor** of the older dual card `sole-tt8-warranty-periods-dual-residential-light-commercial` (ATT8/TT8C-2011, TT8-2013). Do not merge them.

Tension, carded as printed: these books print the ordinary-household 7 duties and the residential 9-item exclusions — exclusion 8 bars "product use in any environment other than a residential setting" — alongside the light-commercial column. The table is followed as the more specific rule for light-commercial cover; see `sole-treadmill-warranty-responsibilities-7-item-household` and `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-9-item-residential`.

When the clock starts is separate; see `sole-treadmill-warranty-start-date-receipt-or-18-months-shipping`.
