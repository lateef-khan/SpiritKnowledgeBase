Effective **January 1, 2011**. Two schedules are printed side by side, and they are not the same.

**COMMERCIAL (5 Hours or less use per day)**

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| Frame | Lifetime |
| Brake | 5 Years |
| Parts | 3 Years |
| Labor | 1 Year |

**RESIDENTIAL**

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| Frame & Brake | Lifetime |
| Parts | 5 Years |
| Labor | 2 Years |

The residential column has three rows, not four: the brake is covered for life residentially, but for 5 Years commercially.

SOLE's responsibilities include providing new or remanufactured parts, at SOLE's option, and technical support to independent dealers and servicing organizations. Where there is no dealer or service organization, SOLE administers the warranty directly to the consumer.

**This schedule exists only in the 2011 manual.** The 2013, 2016 and 2019 manuals rename the columns Residential / Light Commercial, drop the Brake row entirely, and print Parts/Electronics 5 Years and 3 Years.
