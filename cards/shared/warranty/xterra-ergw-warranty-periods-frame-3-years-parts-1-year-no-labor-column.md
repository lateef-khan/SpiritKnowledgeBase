| 3 Years | 1 Year |

**Home Use Limited Warranty, with no Labor column.** The ERG550W (older layout, one two-column row, effective January 1, 2023) and the ERG800W Torpedo (Online-Support layout, a *Warranty / Residential* row headed *Frame / Parts*, effective September 26, 2023) print the same two figures and **no labor term of any kind** - not *None*, not a period. Nothing else on either page promises labor; the ERG800W SERVICE paragraph's *establishes the labor warranty period* sentence is the boilerplate every Online-Support book prints (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`). Tell a customer the frame is covered three years and parts one year, and that no in-home labor is written into the warranty.

**Why this is not the ERG180 or ERG400 card.** Those two also print no labor column, but with different figures - one year and 90 days (`erg180-2023-warranty-periods-frame-1-year-parts-90-days-no-labor-column`), five years and one year (`xterra-warranty-periods-frame-5-years-parts-1-year-no-labor-column`). The water rowers with a labor term are the ERG600W (90 days, `xterra-rower-warranty-periods-frame-5-years-parts-2-years-labor-90-days`), the ERG650W (1 year, `sb600-2023-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`) and the ERG750W (*None*, `erg750w-2025-warranty-periods-frame-5-years-parts-2-years-labor-none`).

**The two pages otherwise differ by layout.** The ERG550W prints the older page: registration card within ten days (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-with-a-registration-card`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover`), nine exclusions with store display and unregistered products (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-store-display-and-unregistered-products`), six service steps at (870) 333-5500 (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-service-steps-dealer-service-organization-then-the-service-department`), and an ATTENTION box on its registration page (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-use-environment-residential-only-any-other-use-voids-it`). The ERG800W prints the Online-Support page: five duties without registration (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-without-registration`), QR-code registration (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-qr-code-and-support-tab-no-deadline`), nine exclusions admitting a non-dues facility at five hours a day (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-with-a-non-dues-facility-allowance`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-use-environment-residential-or-non-dues-facility-5-hours-a-day`), five service steps at (870) 935-1107. The ERG550W tank page voids the warranty for chlorine or over-filling (`xterra-ergw-warranty-tank-chlorine-bleach-or-filling-past-the-mark-voids-it`); the ERG800W's electrical page says a ten-percent voltage swing is not covered (`xterra-warranty-what-the-electrical-and-console-pages-say-is-not-covered`).
