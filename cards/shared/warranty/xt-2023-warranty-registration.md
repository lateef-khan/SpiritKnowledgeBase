## How to register

- Scan the **QR code** on the Warranty Registration page with a smartphone, **or**
- go to **spiritfitness.com/warranty**, under the **Support** tab, and register online.

The Product Registration page asks you to record, at the time of purchase: **Serial Number**, **Date of Purchase**, and **Dealer / Place of Purchase**. The serial number sticker is on the **rear of the motor cover**; the manual says to record the number below the barcode "for the purpose of registering your treadmill's warranty".

Customer care, on the same opening pages: **1-800-258-8511**, questions@spiritfitness.com.

**The XT685ENT prints all of this**, QR code included, and repeats the same three boxes - Serial Number, Date of Purchase, Dealer / Place of Purchase - with the serial sticker on the rear of the motor cover and the service number (870) 935-1107 beside it. Its manual names no registration deadline either.

## Is registration required?

**These manuals never say it is.** Registration is not one of the five numbered Responsibilities of the Consumer, and no registration deadline is printed anywhere in the warranty section. What the manual does say is exclusion 6:

> Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.

So an unregistered machine is not declared void. The owner is instead exposed to being asked for proof of purchase - which is why the SERVICE paragraph says to keep the bill of sale, and why the warranty clock falls back to eighteen months from factory shipping when there is no receipt (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`).

**The XT485ENT is not on this card, and it does impose a deadline.** Its manual is a 2021-generation document requiring the registration card back within 10 days and excluding an unregistered product outright - see `xt-2015-warranty-registration`. A shared console name is not a shared warranty page.

**Do not carry a registration deadline over from another manual - including the earlier XT manual for the same machine name.** The **2015** XT manuals impose a 10-day registration card deadline and exclude an unregistered product outright (`xt-2015-warranty-registration`); everything on this card is about the 2023 manuals only. The Spirit commercial manuals impose a deadline too - the CTSBS900 requires registration "completed online to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty", and the CT900ENT requires the card returned within 10 days of purchase. Sole's manuals impose their own 10-day card. None of that is printed in the 2023 XT manuals, and quoting it to an XT customer would invent a requirement the source does not state.
