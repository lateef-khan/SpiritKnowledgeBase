This warranty applies only to products in **ordinary household use**, and the consumer is responsible for:

1. Returning the **warranty registration card within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.
2. **Proper use of the elliptical** per this manual.
3. **Proper installation** — the E35/E75/E95-2007 book requires installation **by an authorized Sole service company**; the VE and WE books (and every later book) require installation per the instructions with the elliptical and all local electric codes.
4. **Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage**, replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring.
5. **Expenses for making the elliptical accessible for servicing**, including any item not part of the elliptical when shipped from the factory.
6. **Damages to the elliptical finish** during shipping, installation or following installation.

There is **no routine-maintenance item** — the seventh duty of the 2011 books does not exist here. Otherwise the six items match the treadmill 2004–2010 six-duty list almost word for word (that card's twin: `sole-treadmill-warranty-responsibilities-6-item-2004-2010`); the newer seven-duty list is `sole-elliptical-warranty-responsibilities-7-item-2011-2019`.
