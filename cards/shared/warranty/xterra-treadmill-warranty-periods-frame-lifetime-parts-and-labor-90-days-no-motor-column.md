| Warranty | Frame | Parts | Labor |

| Residential | Lifetime | 90 Days | 90 Days |

**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the warranty page of these 3 books, under the heading *Effective March 08, 2023 ... LIMITED WARRANTY*. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**These three books print no Motor column and no Deck column.** The table is three figures wide - Frame, Parts, Labor - so the manual states no separate motor period at all; the drive motor is a part and falls under the 90-day Parts figure. This was verified on a render of the TRX1400 page, not only on the text layer. The TR150, TR200, TRX1000 and TR260 books print the same 90-day parts and labor figures but add a *Motor 1 Year* column (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-motor-1-year-parts-and-labor-90-days`), and the TR75H and TR95H hiker books use this same three-column shape with 2 Years parts (`xterra-tr-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-2-years-labor-1-year-no-motor-column`).

**The three books are otherwise one text**: the same three-page WARRANTY - RESIDENTIAL block, the same five consumer duties, the same nine exclusions and the same six service steps. The WS200 and WS300 are folding walking treadmills; the TRX1400 is a full-size folding treadmill. The 2023 WS200 and WS300 books date the warranty *August 07 2023*; the WS200 cover stamps 20230807 and the WS300 cover 20231009, and both registration pages say Version 2.0, Revision 10/09/23.

**The SERVICE paragraph on the third warranty page contradicts the table**: it says twelve months from the bill of sale or eighteen months from factory shipping *establishes the labor warranty period*, while the table gives labor 90 days. The book does not reconcile them (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).
