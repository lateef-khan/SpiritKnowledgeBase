**Page two of Spirit Fitness's "Cardio Warranty" sheet, Revision 03.07.2019, is headed
COMMERCIAL (NON-DUES PAYING FACILITIES)*. Every machine on it is covered in a facility that
charges no dues - an office, a hotel, a fire station - and not in a health club.** The tier
covered in any facility is page one (`spirit-commercial-warranty-sheet-2019-all-facilities-terms`).
The sheet names machines without years and prints periods only.

| Machines | Frame | Second column | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CT850ENT****, CT850, CT800 | Lifetime | Motor 5 Years | 5 Years | 2 Years |
| XT685** | Lifetime | Motor 5 Years | 3 Years | 1 Year |
| CE800ENT****, CE850, CR800ENT****, CU800ENT**** | Lifetime | EMS Brake 5 Years | 5 Years | 2 Years |
| CE800, CR800, CU800 | Lifetime | Generator 5 Years | 5 Years | 2 Years |
| XE795**, XBR95** | Lifetime | Generator 5 Years | 3 Years | 1 Year |
| CRW800, CRS800S, CS800 | Lifetime | - | 5 Years | 2 Years |
| CIC800 | **15 Years** | - | 3 Years | 1 Year, Wear Items*** 1 Year |
| XS895 | **5 Years** | - | 3 Years | 1 Year |

The four markers are the sheet's footnotes (`spirit-commercial-warranty-sheet-2019-footnotes`):
`**` an X Series machine is covered up to 5 hours' use a day, `***` wear items are grips, seats
and pedal straps, `****` the ENT consoles are covered 3 years parts and 1 year labor.

## Where the sheet and the machines' own manuals agree

Every figure in the first six rows is the figure the machine's owner's manual prints in its
commercial or light-commercial row: the CT800 and CT850 family
(`spirit-ct800-warranty-commercial-periods`, with the ENT console columns on
`ct800ent-2022-warranty-console-periods`), the XT685 (`xt685-2023-light-commercial-warranty-periods`),
the CE800, CE800ENT and CE850 (`spirit-ce-warranty-commercial-periods`), the CR800, CU800 and
their ENT versions (`spirit-bike-warranty-commercial-periods`), the XE795
(`spirit-xe795-warranty-light-commercial-periods`), the XBR95
(`spirit-xb-warranty-light-commercial-periods`), the CRW800
(`crw800-2021-warranty-commercial-and-residential-periods`), the CRS800S and CS800
(`spirit-climber-2021-warranty-coverage-periods`) and the CIC800's non-dues row
(`spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-three-row-periods`).

**The sheet drops columns the manuals print.** It has no Deck column for the XT685 (3 Years in
the manual), no Brake column for the CRW800, CRS800S and CS800 (5 Years in their manuals), and
it heads the CE850's and the CE800/CR800/CU800's second column *EMS Brake* and *Generator*
where their manuals say *Brake* and *Electronics*. The figures under the headings do not move.

## Where they do not - the XS895 frame

**The sheet warrants the XS895 frame for 5 Years. Both XS895 owner's manuals - August 2018 and
February 2021 - warrant it for Lifetime** in the very row this page describes, Light Commercial
(5 hours use or less in a non-dues paying facility), with Brake 5 Years, Parts 3 Years and Labor
1 Year (`spirit-xs895-warranty-coverage-periods`). Parts and labor agree; the frame does not, and
nothing in either document explains the other. Quote the document the customer holds and say
the other exists.

## Which model ids the names map to

The sheet prints no years. This card is tagged to the id of each name whose year is 2019 or
earlier, taking the latest such year, plus the year-less `ce800ent`: CT850 `ct850-2018`, CT800
`ct800-2016`, XT685 `xt685-2015`, CE850 `ce850-2016`, CE800 `ce800-2016`, CR800 `cr800-2009`,
CU800 `cu800-2012`, XE795 `xe795-2018`, XBR95 `xbr95-2018`, CRW800 `crw800-2016`, CS800
`cs800-2016`, XS895 `xs895-2018`. **Five names have only later ids and are not tagged**: CT850ENT
(`ct850ent-2022`), CR800ENT (`cr800ent-2023`), CU800ENT (`cu800ent-2022`), CRS800S
(`crs800s-2021`) and CIC800 (`cic800-2021`); their manuals print the same figures, so the answer
for them is the same. The XS895's 2021 manual is the same machine as its 2018 one and prints
the same lifetime frame. The CR800 and CU800 ids of 2009 and 2012 are the only pre-2019 ones on
file, and their books are a generation older than the sheet.
