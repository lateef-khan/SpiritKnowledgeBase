| Warranty | Frame\* | Brake | Parts | Labor | Wear Items\*\* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial (Dues Paying Facilities) | 10 Years | 5 Years | 3 Years | 3 Years | 6 Months |
| Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | 10 Years | 5 Years | 5 Years | 2 Years | 6 Months |
| Residential | Lifetime | 5 Years | 10 Years | 2 Years | 6 Months |

\*Frame is defined as the welded metal base of the unit and does not include removable parts, wear items, or coatings.

\*\*Wear items include: plastic covers, overlay, grips, hardware, pull pins/knobs, decals/stickers, headphone jack, USB port, paint/coatings/finish, straps, and pedals.

> Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage.

**Yes, a dues-paying gym is covered** - and these are the only two Spirit elliptical manuals in this knowledge base that say so. Every other one either excludes a dues-paying facility outright or never mentions one. Which environment each row covers is `spirit-ce-warranty-use-environment`.

**The brake column is headed differently on the two machines and reads the same.** The CE900 prints **Generator Brake**, the CES880 prints **Brake**; 5 Years in all three rows on both. The effective dates differ - **October 01, 2024** for the CE900 and **December 17, 2024** for the CES880 - and every figure and both footnotes are otherwise identical.

**The dues-paying and non-dues-paying rows differ in Parts and Labor only**, and they move in opposite directions: a dues-paying facility gets 3 years of parts against 5, but 3 years of labor against 2. Frame, Brake and Wear Items are the same in both.

**A 10-year frame is not a lifetime frame.** The commercial rows read 10 Years where the earlier CE800 and CE850 manuals read Lifetime in every row (`spirit-ce-warranty-commercial-periods`). Only the residential row here keeps Lifetime, and the footnote narrows what "frame" means: the welded metal base only, with removable parts, wear items and coatings all outside it.

**Wear items are 6 months in every row**, and the list under the table is closed and specific. Grips, pedals, straps, decals, the headphone jack, the USB port and the paint are wear items - so a USB port fault at seven months is outside the parts period whichever row applies.

**The opening paragraph does not match the table.** Both manuals warrant "all its **home use** Elliptical parts" (CE900) or "all its **Suspension Elliptical** parts" (CES880) and say the warranty is administered "directly to a **consumer**", above a table whose first two rows are commercial and a responsibilities list addressed to a **facility**. Neither manual reconciles the two. Quote both rather than choosing.

**These are not the CE900 2021 figures.** That manual prints one unqualified Commercial row - Frame Lifetime, Generator Brake 5 Years, Parts 3 Year, Labor 3 Years - and no residential row (`ce900-2021-warranty-coverage-periods`). A CE900 question has two possible answers; ask which manual came with the machine.

**Do not answer this from the CT900 treadmill table.** It has the same three row labels and the same wear-items footnote, but separate Motor and Deck columns and different Parts and Labor figures (`ct900-warranty-coverage-terms`).
