**Elliptical warranty, effective August 1, 2012** (the E20/E25 multi-year book covering E20-2013 through E25-2015); **effective February 1, 2016** (E25-2016 book); and **effective February 1, 2019** (E25-2019 book) — same figures under all dates. Both columns (E20 and E25) print identical figures.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Labor** | **1 Year** (stencil books print "1 Years", sic) |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Brake** | **3 Years** |
| **Cosmetic Items** * | **90 Days** |
| **All Other Components** | **3 Years** |

\* Cosmetic items including, but not limited to: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.

**This is not the lifetime-brake E25.** The AE25-2011, VE25-2007 and WE25 books give the brake a lifetime term; this family gives it 3 years. See `sole-elliptical-warranty-periods-lifetime-frame-brake-3-year-parts-1-year-labor` for that older term.
