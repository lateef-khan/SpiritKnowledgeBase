**Elliptical warranty, effective January 1, 2008** as printed in the AE55/AE95-2011 and AE25/AE35-2011 (E35 column) books; **effective August 1, 2012** (E35/E95-2012-2015 and E55-2013-2014 books); **effective February 1, 2016** (E35/E55/E95-2016 books); and **effective February 1, 2019** (E35/E55/E95-2019 books) — same figures under all dates.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Labor** | **2 Years** |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Brake** | **Lifetime** |
| **Cosmetic Items** * | **90 Days** |
| **All Other Components** | **5 Years** |

\* Cosmetic items including, but not limited to: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals. The 2016/2019 stencil books print "Labor 1 Years" style singulars elsewhere but this table reads "Labor 2 Years" (sic kept as printed).

The AE25/AE35-2011 book covers the AE models but its two warranty columns are headed **E25** and **E35** as printed; the E35 column carries these figures and applies to the AE35-2011. Carded as printed.

When the clock starts is separate; see `sole-elliptical-warranty-start-date-receipt-or-18-months-shipping`. What is not covered is on `sole-elliptical-warranty-exclusions-9-item-2011-2019`. The treadmill twin with different figures is `sole-treadmill-warranty-periods-lifetime-motor-3-year-deck-1-year-labor`.
