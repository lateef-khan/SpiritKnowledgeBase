**Treadmill warranty, effective February 1, 2023** (F63-2023 and F65-2023 books) and **effective February 1, 2025** (F63-2025 book) — same table under both dates. Read off the page render, not just the OCR extraction.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Drive Motor** | **Lifetime** |
| **Deck** | **2 Years** |
| **All Other Components** | **2 Years** |
| **Labor** | **1 Year** |
| **Cosmetic Items** * | **90 Days** |

*Cosmetic items including, but not limited to the following: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.

This is the step down from the 3-year deck and parts the same lines carried in 2016/2019 — see `sole-treadmill-warranty-periods-lifetime-motor-3-year-deck-1-year-labor`. Do not confuse it with the 2023 F80/F85/F89 books, which kept the 3-year deck.

When the clock starts is separate; see `sole-treadmill-warranty-start-date-receipt-or-18-months-shipping`. The 2023 duties, exclusions and service steps are on `sole-treadmill-warranty-responsibilities-8-item-photo-duty-2023`, `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-10-item-rust-climate-2023` and `sole-treadmill-warranty-service-troubleshoot-first-2023`.
