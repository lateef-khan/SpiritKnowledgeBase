Keep your bill of sale. **Twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale**, or **eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number**, establishes the labor warranty period should service be required.

If service is performed, it is in your best interest to obtain and keep all receipts. This written warranty gives you specific legal rights; you may also have other rights that vary from state to state.

**This paragraph and the warranty table do not always agree, and the manual never says which governs.** Several machines have a labor row longer than twelve months:

| Machines | Labor row in the warranty table |
|---|---|
| b94-2011, r92-2011 | 2 Years |
| lcb-2011, lcr-2011 | Residential 2 Years, commercial 1 Year |
| lcb-2013, lcb-2016, lcb-2019, lcr-2013, lcr-2016, lcr-2019 | Residential 2 Years, light commercial 1 Year |

For every other machine in this family the table says 1 Year and the two agree. Where they differ, quote the labor row of the machine's own warranty table and treat this paragraph as the claim-handling rule, not as a shorter labor warranty.
