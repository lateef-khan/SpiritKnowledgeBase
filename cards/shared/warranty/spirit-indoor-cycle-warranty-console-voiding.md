Three, and both XIC600 printings state them in the same words on the console page:

1. **Opening the monitor.** The Cycling Monitor system is a **sealed unit**, meant to be opened only to install batteries. *Any opened units will void the warranty.*
2. **Caustic cleaners.** Clean the monitor with a clean **damp cloth**. *Use of any caustic cleaning solutions will void the warranty.*
3. **Water.** The monitor is **not waterproof, only water resistant**. *Any excessive exposure to water will void the warranty.*

**Installing batteries is the one exception**, and it is the only reason the manual gives for opening the case at all. The console and the speed sensor transmitter both take **AAA** batteries in the 2021 printing; the 2018 printing describes the same two battery compartments.

**"Excessive" is not defined.** The manual sets no figure, no splash rating and no guidance on sweat, and it does not say whether wiping the console with a damp cloth - which item 2 requires - counts. Quote the three sentences as printed rather than drawing a line the source does not draw.

**These sit on the console page, not in the warranty section.** Nothing in either manual's EXCLUSIONS list repeats them, and nothing in the warranty table warrants the console for any period at all. So the only thing an XIC600 manual says about console cover is how to lose it (`spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-residential-only-periods`).

## The other six bikes of this range say none of this

The CB900, CIC800, CIC850, AB900, AB950 and JB950 manuals print **no** sealed-unit warning, **no** caustic-cleaner clause and **no** waterproofing statement. Do not tell an owner of one of those that opening or washing their console voids cover - their manuals do not say so, and the CIC850 in fact warrants its console for a year (`spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-three-row-periods`).

**Do not answer this from the Sole indoor cycle cards.** The Sole SB700 and SB900 monitors carry a near-identical warning and a separate one-year warranty from **Direction Technology**, a different warrantor entirely (`sole-spinner-monitor-care`, `sole-spinner-monitor-warranty`). Those are another brand's terms.

Damage done while assembling the bike is excluded on two other machines in this range: `spirit-bike-warranty-assembly-damage-excluded`.
