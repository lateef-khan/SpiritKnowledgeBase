**Treadmill warranty, effective August 1, 2012** on the F80-2013, F85-2013 and S77-2013 books, and **effective January 1, 2008** as printed in the AF80/AF83/AF85-2011 and AS77-2012 books.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Drive Motor** | **Lifetime** |
| **Deck** | **Lifetime** (the AS77 book writes **Deck Wear**) |
| **All Other Components** | **5 years** |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Labor** | **2 Years** |
| **Cosmetic Items** * | **90 Days** |

*Cosmetic items including, but not limited to: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.

The 2011/2012 books carry a stale page: the AF80 book footer is stamped 2011 and the AS77 footer S77_20120123 (January 2012) while the warranty head still reads January 1, 2008. Carded as printed.

When the clock starts is separate; see `sole-treadmill-warranty-start-date-receipt-or-18-months-shipping`. What is not covered is on `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-9-item-residential`.
