**Eight items, in this order, in every one of the thirty-nine books:**

1. Consequential, collateral or incidental damages - property damage and incidental
   expenses arising from any breach of this written or any implied warranty. Some states
   do not allow that exclusion, so it may not apply.
2. Service call reimbursement to the facility, and to the dealer where no malfunction or
   defect in workmanship or material is involved, where the unit is past the warranty
   period or past the service call reimbursement period, where no component needs
   replacing, or where the equipment is not in non-dues-paying facility or household use.
3. Damage from service by anyone other than an authorized Spirit Fitness service company;
   from parts that are not original Spirit Fitness parts; or from external causes -
   corrosion, discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse,
   misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, or acts of God.
4. Products whose original serial numbers have been removed or altered.
5. **Products that have been sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party.**
6. Products with no warranty registration on file at Spirit Fitness. Spirit Fitness
   reserves the right to request proof of purchase where no warranty record exists.
7. This warranty is expressly in lieu of all other warranties expressed or implied,
   including merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose.
8. Product used in prisons and correctional facilities.

Below the list, unnumbered: *Warranties outside of the United States may vary. Please
contact your local dealer for details.*

**Item 5 ends the cover on resale.** A second-hand machine has no warranty, however much
of the term is left.

**One word differs across the range.** On the three ST800 books item 6 reads
"warranty registration **card** on file", matching the card those manuals ask for; the
thirty-six CSD, CSS, CSF and CSI books say "warranty registration on file" because they
register online. Nothing else in the list changes - the eight items are otherwise word
for word identical in all thirty-nine.

**Item 2 is not truncated in any of these books.** It runs to "...or household use." in
all thirty-nine, checked on the PDF text layer of every one. The mid-sentence break at
"...or units" seen on some autumn-2024 Spirit manuals and on the 2021 water rowers does
not occur anywhere in the strength range.

Item 8 also appears as a footnote under the coverage table on the three ST800 books:
`spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`. What item 2 implies about where the machine may
stand is on `spirit-strength-warranty-use-environment`.
