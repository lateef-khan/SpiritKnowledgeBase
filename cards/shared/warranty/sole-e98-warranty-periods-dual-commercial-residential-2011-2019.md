**E98 dual-column warranty — effective January 1, 2011** (E98-2011 book), **effective August 1, 2012** (E98-2013 book), **effective August 1, 2016** (E98-2015 book), and **effective August 1, 2019** (E98-2019 book) — same figures under all four dates. The only elliptical in the wave with a commercial column.

| Component | Commercial (5 hours or less use per day) | Residential |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame** / **Frame & Brake** | **Lifetime** | **Lifetime** |
| **Brake** / **Parts** | **Brake 5 Years** | **Parts 5 Years** |
| **Parts** / **Labor** | **Parts 3 Years** | **Labor 2 Years** |
| **Labor** | **1 Year** | — |

No cosmetic row is printed in any of the four books. The E98-2011 duties paragraph is the only one in the wave that admits a "non-dues paying, light commercial facility with 5 hours elliptical use or less per day" alongside ordinary household use; the E98-2013/2015/2019 duties paragraphs revert to household use only even though their tables keep the commercial column. Carded as printed — the table is followed for the commercial terms.

The E98-2023 book replaces the brake rows with a lifetime flywheel and splits other-components 2/3 years: `sole-e98-2023-warranty-periods-dual-commercial-residential-flywheel`.
