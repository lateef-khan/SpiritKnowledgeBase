**All thirty-nine strength manuals close with the same two sentences:**

> Product features or specifications as described or illustrated are subject to change
> without notice. All warranties are made by Spirit Manufacturing, Inc.
>
> This warranty applies only in the 48 contiguous United States. NOTE: This does not apply
> to Alaska or Hawaii.

**Alaska and Hawaii are named and excluded**, so a US address is not on its own enough.

**Outside the United States there is no stated term.** The line under the exclusion list
says only:

> Warranties outside of the United States may vary. Please contact your local dealer for
> details.

The manuals name no international distributor and print no overseas contact, so the local
dealer is the only route given.

**Two company names appear in the same warranty.** The opening sentence warrants the unit
in the name of **Spirit Fitness**, the service address is **SPIRIT FITNESS, INC.**, and
this closing line makes the warrantor **Spirit Manufacturing, Inc.** The manuals do not
explain the relationship. Quote the name the customer's own manual prints.
