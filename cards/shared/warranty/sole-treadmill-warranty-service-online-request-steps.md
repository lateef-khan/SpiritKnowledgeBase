**Service under this warranty must be obtained by following these steps, in order:**

1. **Contact the selling authorized SOLE dealer.** OR
2. **Submit the service request online**, with serial number, contact information and a brief description of the problem, at `www.soletreadmills.com/technical.php?p=service` (2011–2013 books) or `http://www.soletreadmills.com/serviceparts.html` (F60-2016, TT9-2018).
3. Questions about the product or warranty: **SOLE Fitness at 1-866-780-SOLE (7653)**; technical problem: **866-MYSOLE1 (697-6531)**. The F60-2012/2014 books add: if purchased at Sears, **1-800-469-4663**.
4. **If no local service is available**, SOLE repairs or replaces the parts, at its option, at no charge for parts. **All transportation costs both ways and adequate packaging are the owner’s responsibility**; freight claims go to the carrier. **Do not ship any unit to the factory without a return authorization number.**
5. By mail: **SOLE Fitness, P.O. Box 2037, Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037.**

**Keep the bill of sale** and all service receipts. The 12/18-month service clock is on `sole-treadmill-warranty-start-date-receipt-or-18-months-shipping`. The 2026 procedure differs (different URL, troubleshooting with photos first) — see `f63-2026-how-to-get-warranty-service`.
