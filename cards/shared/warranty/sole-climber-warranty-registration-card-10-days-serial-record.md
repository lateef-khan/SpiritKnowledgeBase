The Product Registration page (printed page 3):

- **Record the serial number** in the space provided ("RECORD YOUR SERIAL NUMBER ... in the space provided below"), with spaces for the **dealer name, dealer telephone number and purchase date**.
- **REGISTER YOUR PURCHASE:** "The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to SOLE." Or register online at **www.soletreadmills.com under the support tab**.
- The warranty-duties list adds the deadline: **within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the warranty. This printing offers no late proof-of-purchase alternative (the CC81-2026 book does).
