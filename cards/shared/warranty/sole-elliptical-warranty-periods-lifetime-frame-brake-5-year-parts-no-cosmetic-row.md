**Elliptical warranty, effective January 1, 2007** (VE25/VE55-2008 book) and **effective January 1, 2008** (WE25/WE35 and WE55/WE95-2009-2010 books) — same figures under both dates. The VE and WE books print **no cosmetic-items row**; that is how the table reads, not an omission here.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Labor** | **2 Years** (WE books print "2 year", VE book "2 years") |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Brake** | **Lifetime** |
| **All Other Components** | **5 Years** |

The VE25/VE55-2008 book covers the VE models but its two columns are headed **E25** and **E55** as printed; the E55 column carries these figures and applies to the VE55-2007. The WE books head theirs E35/E55/E95 likewise. Carded as printed.

This is the no-cosmetic-row printing of the 5-year-parts terms; the printing with the 90-day cosmetic row is `sole-elliptical-warranty-periods-lifetime-frame-brake-5-year-parts-2-year-labor`. When the clock starts is on `sole-elliptical-warranty-start-date-receipt-or-18-months-shipping`.
