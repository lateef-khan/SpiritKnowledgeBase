**Treadmill warranty, effective October 15, 2004** (F80/F83/F85, S73/S77 and US73/US77-2006 books) and **effective January 1, 2007** (UF80/UF83/UF85-2006, VF80/VF83/VF85, VS73/VS77 and VTT8-2007 books) — same terms under both dates.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Drive Motor** | **30 Years** |
| **Deck Wear** | **Lifetime** |
| **Electronics / Belt / Rollers** | **5 Years** |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Other Components** | **5 Years** |
| **Labor** | **2 Years** |

The 2007 printings say "SOLE" where the 2004 book says "Sole"; the figures are identical. Do not confuse with `sole-treadmill-warranty-periods-30-year-motor-lifetime-deck-wear-2004`, whose F63/TT8 tables print no Labor row.

When the clock starts is separate; see `sole-treadmill-warranty-start-date-receipt-or-18-months-shipping`. What is not covered is on `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-7-item-2004-2010`.
