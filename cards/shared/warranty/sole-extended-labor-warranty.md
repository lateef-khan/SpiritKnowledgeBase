**Extra labor only, starting when the factory labor runs out.** The agreement adds 1 or 2 years of labor coverage on top of the manufacturer's labor warranty; the exact length for a machine is in its warranty registration documents.

Covered: technician labor costs and extended trip fees, and repairs for mechanical or electrical failures that meet the warranty's conditions. All other terms, exclusions and limitations are the manufacturer's warranty in the owner's manual, also posted at soletreadmills.com/pages/service-and-manuals.

Not covered: cosmetic items (grips, console overlays, labels/decals), damage from misuse or unauthorized repairs, and machines outside the Continental US.

To claim: call **866-780-7653**, 7am-5pm MST Monday-Friday (option 2 for Service and Warranty), or file at soletreadmills.com/pages/warranty-request-form. Claims must be filed inside the coverage period. Owners must use and maintain the machine per the manual, keep the warranty paperwork, and report malfunctions promptly.

The base labor period this extends is per manual; see `sole-bike-labor-warranty-period` for the bike rule.
