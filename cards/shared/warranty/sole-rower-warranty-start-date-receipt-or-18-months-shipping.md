SOLE warrants all its Rowers' parts for the listed periods **from the date of retail sale, as determined by sale receipt**, or, in the absence of a receipt, **eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date**.

This is why every book asks you to keep the bill of sale and to record the serial number: without a receipt, the factory shipping date, determined by the serial number, is what dates the machine. The treadmill twin with the same rule is `sole-treadmill-warranty-start-date-receipt-or-18-months-shipping`.
