**A dues-paying gym is excluded, in as many words.** Exclusion 8, in all four CT800 manuals:

> Product used in a dues paying facility (YMCA, Health Club, Etc.)

The rest of the section points the same way. The table's commercial row is labelled **Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities)** in the 2020 and CT800ENT manuals. The responsibilities list is addressed to "the consumer or non-dues paying facility" - "the non-dues paying facility" in the CT800ENT. And exclusion 2 refuses service call reimbursement for "fitness equipment not in **non-dues paying facility or household use**".

So the covered envelope is a home, **or** a facility that charges no dues. Prisons and correctional facilities are outside it as well, by exclusion 9 - `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.

**This is the sharpest thing to know about a CT800 warranty.** The machine is a commercial treadmill and its manual prints a Commercial warranty row, but the word commercial in that row does **not** mean a fee-charging gym. A CT800 bought for a health club has a warranty table that appears to cover it and an exclusion two pages later that does not. The manuals never reconcile the two, and they never define what makes a facility dues-paying beyond the two examples in the parenthesis.

**The manuals cap nothing by hours.** No CT800 manual states a hours-per-day limit, and none defines "light commercial". Do not import the XT limit - "any environment other than a residential setting or non-dues paying facility with **5 hours use or less per day**" is XT wording and is not printed in any CT800 manual (`xt-2023-warranty-use-environment`). Do not import the 2026 position either: the 4.0T, 7.0T and 8.0T never mention dues-paying facilities at all (`spirit-2026t-warranty-use-environment`).

Which figures apply to which use is `spirit-ct800-warranty-commercial-periods` and `spirit-ct800-warranty-residential-periods`; the full exclusion list is `spirit-ct800-warranty-exclusions`.
