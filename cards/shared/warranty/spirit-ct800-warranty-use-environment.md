**A dues-paying gym is excluded, in as many words.** Exclusion 8, in all four CT800 manuals and all four CT850 ones:

> Product used in a dues paying facility (YMCA, Health Club, Etc.)

The rest of the section points the same way. The table's commercial row is labelled **Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities)** in five of the eight - CT800 2020, CT800ENT, CT850 2016, CT850 2020 and CT850ENT. The responsibilities list is addressed to "the consumer or non-dues paying facility" - "the non-dues paying facility" in the two ENT manuals. And exclusion 2 refuses service call reimbursement for "fitness equipment not in **non-dues paying facility or household use**".

**Yes, the CT850 commercial warranty is a non-dues-paying-facility warranty, exactly as the CT800's is.** The CT850 2016, 2020 and CT850ENT manuals say so in the row label; the CT850 2018 manual labels the row plain **Commercial** but prints the same exclusion 8, the same exclusion 2 and the same "consumer or non-dues paying facility" lead sentence, so the envelope is the same. The row label is the only thing that moves.

So the covered envelope is a home, **or** a facility that charges no dues. Prisons and correctional facilities are outside it as well, by exclusion 9 - `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.

**This is the sharpest thing to know about a CT800 or CT850 warranty.** Both are commercial treadmills and both manuals print a Commercial warranty row, but the word commercial in that row does **not** mean a fee-charging gym. A CT800 or CT850 bought for a health club has a warranty table that appears to cover it and an exclusion two pages later that does not. None of the eight manuals reconciles the two, and none defines what makes a facility dues-paying beyond the two examples in the parenthesis.

**The manuals cap nothing by hours.** No CT800 or CT850 manual states an hours-per-day limit, and none defines "light commercial". Do not import the XT limit - "any environment other than a residential setting or non-dues paying facility with **5 hours use or less per day**" is XT wording and is not printed in any CT800 or CT850 manual (`xt-2023-warranty-use-environment`). Do not import the 2026 position either: the 4.0T, 7.0T and 8.0T never mention dues-paying facilities at all (`spirit-2026t-warranty-use-environment`).

Which figures apply to which use is `spirit-ct800-warranty-commercial-periods` and `spirit-ct800-warranty-residential-periods`; the full exclusion list is `spirit-ct800-warranty-exclusions`.

**The CT850 2013 book is the same**: a plain Commercial row label, exclusion 2 refusing service-call reimbursement for equipment "not in non-dues paying facility or household use", and exclusion 8 barring a dues-paying facility outright.
