Effective **February 1, 2023**.

| Component | Residential warranty (all service performed in-home) | Light commercial warranty (all service performed in-facility) |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | Lifetime | Lifetime |
| Parts/Electronics | 3 Years | 2 Years |
| Labor | 1 Years | 1 Year |

The residential labor cell is printed **1 Years**; the light commercial cell reads **1 Year**. Both are one year — the manual offers no other reading — but the residential cell is quoted here exactly as printed.

SOLE's responsibilities include providing new or remanufactured parts, at SOLE's option, and technical support to independent dealers and servicing organizations. Where there is no dealer or service organization, SOLE administers the warranty directly to the consumer.

**The 2023 revision cut every parts and labor period.** Residential parts fell from 5 Years to 3, residential labor from 2 Years to 1, and light commercial parts from 3 Years to 2. Check which manual shipped with the machine before quoting a period.
