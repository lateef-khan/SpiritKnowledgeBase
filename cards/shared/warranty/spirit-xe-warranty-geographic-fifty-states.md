**These three books do not say 48 contiguous states.** The sentence printed under their warranty section reads:

| Manual | The note | Warrantor named |
|---|---|---|
| XE150/XE350/XE550 2005 | This warranty applies only in the **50 states of the U.S.A. and Canada**. | Spirit Manufacturing, Inc. |
| XE100/XE200/XE300 2007 | This warranty applies only in the **50 states of the U.S.A.** | Spirit Manufacturing, Inc. |
| XE395 2010 | This warranty applies only in the **50 states of the U.S.A.** | Spirit Fitness, Inc. |

**Alaska and Hawaii are inside the covered area on all three**, because fifty states includes them. Every Spirit XE and XG manual from 2021 on says the opposite - *only in the 48 contiguous United States. NOTE: This does not apply to Alaska or Hawaii* - so answering an older machine's question from `xt-2023-warranty-geographic-coverage` takes cover away that these manuals give.

**The 2005 book is the only manual in the whole Spirit XE and XG range that names Canada.** The 2007 book for the next three machines drops it, and no later manual restores it. The 2005 manual states no separate Canadian terms, no Canadian dealer and no Canadian address - only that the warranty applies there.

## Only the 2010 manual has an outside-the-area exclusion

Its EXCLUSIONS item 9:

> Warranties outside of **North America** may vary. Please contact your local dealer for details.

**That sentence and the 50-states note do not cover the same ground.** North America includes Canada and Mexico; the note limits the warranty to the fifty states. The manual never reconciles them, so a Canadian owner of a 2010 XE395 is told both that the warranty applies only in the U.S.A. and that warranties outside North America may vary. Quote both.

**The 2005 and 2007 books carry no such exclusion at all.** Their lists run to seven items and none of them is geographic (`spirit-xe-2005-warranty-exclusions`). The only geographic statement in those two manuals is the note above.

The words *North America* appear again in only one later manual, the XE195 - see `spirit-xe-warranty-exclusions-residential-setting-only`. Every other 2021 and 2023 manual reads *outside of the United States*.

**None of the three says what the warranty outside the covered area actually is.** The 2010 manual names the local dealer as the authority; the 2005 and 2007 books name nobody.
