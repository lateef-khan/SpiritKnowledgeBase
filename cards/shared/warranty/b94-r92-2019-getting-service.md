Keep your bill of sale. **Twelve (12) months** from the date on the bill of sale, or **eighteen (18) months** from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number, establishes the labor warranty period.

Follow these steps, in order:

1. Contact your selling authorized SOLE dealer, **or**
2. Submit the service request online at `http://www.soletreadmills.com/serviceparts.html`, including serial number, contact information and a brief description of the problem.
3. For questions about the product or the warranty, contact SOLE Fitness at **1-866-780-SOLE (7653)**. For a technical problem, contact SOLE technical service at **866-MYSOLE1 (1-866-697-6531)**.
4. If no local service is available, SOLE will repair or replace parts at SOLE's option within the warranty period at no charge for parts. **All transportation costs, both to the factory and on return, are the owner's responsibility.** The owner is responsible for adequate packaging on return. SOLE is not responsible for shipping damage — make freight damage claims with the carrier.

**Do not ship any unit to the factory without a Return Authorization number. Units arriving without one will be refused.**

By mail:

```
SOLE Fitness
P.O. Box 2037
Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037
```

The front and back covers repeat: before returning your Sole product to any retailer, or for assistance with assembly or technical support, call **866-697-6531** first.
