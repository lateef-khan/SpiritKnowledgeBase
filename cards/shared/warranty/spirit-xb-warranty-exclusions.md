This warranty does not cover the following:

> CONSEQUENTIAL, COLLATERAL, OR INCIDENTAL DAMAGES SUCH AS PROPERTY DAMAGE AND INCIDENTAL EXPENSES RESULTING FROM ANY BREACH OF THIS WRITTEN OR ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY. *Note: Some states do not allow the exclusion or limitation of incidental or consequential damages, so this limitation or exclusion may not apply to you.*

1. Service call reimbursement to the consumer. Service call reimbursement to the dealer that does not involve malfunction or defects in workmanship or material, for units that are beyond the warranty period, for units that are beyond the service call reimbursement period, for fitness bike not requiring component replacement, or **fitness bike not in ordinary household use**.
2. Damages caused by services performed by persons other than authorized Spirit Fitness service companies; use of parts other than original Spirit Fitness parts; or external causes such as corrosion, discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, or acts of God.
3. Products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered.
4. Products that have been: sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party.
5. **Products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at Spirit Fitness.** Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.
6. THIS WARRANTY IS EXPRESSLY IN LIEU OF ALL OTHER WARRANTIES EXPRESSED OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING THE WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND/OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.
7. **Product use in any environment other than a residential setting.**
8. Warranties outside of **North America** may vary. Please contact your local dealer for details.

**The consequential-damages paragraph is not numbered.** It sits above item 1 as an unnumbered block in all ten manuals, so this list runs to **eight** numbered items where the equivalent Spirit elliptical and treadmill lists run to nine with the same content. Quote it by its words, not by a number.

**The ten lists are word for word identical**, over five and a half years and six machines - nothing moves at all, not even the machine's name. Item 1 reads *fitness bike* on every one of the ten, even on the manuals whose warranty table two pages earlier calls the machine a Semi - Recumbent Bike or an Upright Bike. The 2023 manuals are the ones that change it, to *Recumbent Bike* or *Upright Bike*.

## Item 7 is the item that contradicts five of these manuals

It allows a **home and nothing else**. Yet the **XBR95 2016, 2018 and 2021** and the **XBU55 2019 and 2021** print a Light Commercial row of periods in their warranty table (`spirit-xb-warranty-light-commercial-periods`). Item 1 narrows it the same way a second time, refusing service-call reimbursement outside "ordinary household use". Neither the table nor the list acknowledges the other. Say both: `spirit-xb-warranty-use-environment`.

**Item 7 grows a second clause in 2023.** The four 2023 manuals read "a residential setting **or non-dues paying facility with 5 hours use or less per day**", and number it 8 in a nine-item list: `spirit-xb-2023-warranty-exclusions`.

## Item 5 excludes an unregistered bike outright, and the 2023 books delete that

**The two sentences of item 5 do not say the same thing.** The first excludes the product; the second only reserves a right to ask for proof of purchase. None of the ten reconciles them, so say both. The registration duty they enforce, and the deadline, are in `spirit-xb-warranty-registration`.

**The 2023 manuals dropped the first sentence.** Their item 6 reads only *Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product*, so an unregistered 2023 bike is not named as excluded anywhere.

## Item 8 says North America, not the United States

All ten read **outside of North America**; all four 2023 manuals read **outside of the United States**. Neither says what the warranty outside that area actually is, only that it may differ and that the local dealer is the authority. The 48-contiguous-states note printed under the list is a separate sentence: `xt-2023-warranty-geographic-coverage`.

## Two items about the machine's history

Item 3 and item 4 sit next to each other and are worth reading together: a bike sold on with its serial sticker peeled off is outside the warranty on both counts. Item 4 names four routes out of the original buyer's hands - a sale, a transfer, a barter or a gift - and the manual states no way to reinstate the cover (`xt-2023-warranty-transfer`).

**Do not answer this from a Spirit commercial machine's list.** The CT800, CT850, CE800, CR800 and CU800 lists run to **ten** numbered items, and their extra two are a dues-paying facility and prisons - neither of which appears anywhere in these ten manuals (`spirit-ct800-warranty-exclusions`).
