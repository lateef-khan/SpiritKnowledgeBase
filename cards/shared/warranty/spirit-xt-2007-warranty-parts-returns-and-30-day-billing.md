**Have the serial number, not just the model number, when you order.** If a serial number is provided when a warranty parts order is placed, the required parts are sent at no charge. If the unit is no longer covered, the parts are billed on 30-day payment terms. (An order with no serial number is treated as a stock order - see `spirit-xt-2007-policy-parts-purchase-order-form`.)

**If a replacement part has to come back, the packing list says so** - and it also states the amount the dealer will be invoiced if the part is not returned within 30 days.

**Parts required to be returned for credit: upper and lower electronics - XT Series.** That is the whole list the page prints; consoles and controllers go back, and nothing else is named.

**Paperwork that must travel with the defective part:** a copy of the invoice or packing list, and the diagnostic form if one was included with the replacement part. Without them there is no warranty consideration.

Parts not returned within 30 days are also one of the three grounds on which Spirit refuses labour credit (`spirit-xt-2007-warranty-labor-reimbursement-80-dollar-flat-fee`), and the labour credit form itself repeats that all parts must be returned to the factory unless instructed otherwise (`spirit-xt-2007-policy-warranty-labor-credit-form`). Parts orders go by fax only (`spirit-xt-2007-policy-contacting-customer-service`).
