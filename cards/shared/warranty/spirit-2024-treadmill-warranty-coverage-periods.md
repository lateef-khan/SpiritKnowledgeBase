| Warranty | Frame\* | Motor | Deck & Parts | Labor | Wear Items\*\* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | 10 Years | 5 Years | 5 Years | 2 Years | 6 Months |
| Residential | 10 Years | Lifetime (Motor & Deck) | 10 Years | 2 Years | 6 Months |

\*Frame is defined as the welded metal base of the unit and does not include removable parts, wear items, or coatings.

\*\*Wear items include: plastic covers, overlay, grips, hardware, pull pins/knobs, decals/stickers, headphone jack, USB port, paint/coatings/finish, straps, and pedals.

> Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage.

Both manuals are effective **September 17, 2024** and head the page **WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL** with the table titled *Commercial Limited Warranty*. The CT800 and CT850 print the table identically, figure for figure.

**The frame is 10 Years in both rows, a home included.** Every earlier CT800 and CT850 manual warrants the frame for **Lifetime** in both rows (`spirit-ct800-warranty-commercial-periods`, `spirit-ct800-warranty-residential-periods`). The lifetime promise has moved off the frame and onto the motor and deck, and only in a home.

**The Residential motor cell covers two components at once.** It reads *Lifetime (Motor & Deck)* under the **Motor** heading, so the deck is named there as well as in the *Deck & Parts* column beside it, which reads 10 Years in the same row. Read literally the deck is lifetime by one cell and 10 years by another. **Quote both cells to a customer**; the manual never reconciles them. The rendered page was read at 300 dpi and the parenthesis is exactly as printed - it is not an extraction artefact.

**Deck and parts share one column in a gym and are 5 Years.** There is no separate parts period.

**Labor is 2 Years in both rows** and the table states it plainly, unlike the ENT tables which give the console its own labor year (`spirit-2024-treadmill-ent-warranty-coverage-periods`).

**The opening paragraph does not match the heading or the table.** The page is headed WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL and warrants "all its **home use** Treadmill parts", administered "directly to a **consumer**", above a responsibilities list addressed to a **facility**. Neither manual reconciles them - `spirit-2024-warranty-use-environment`.

The nine exclusions on the next page are `spirit-ce-2025-warranty-exclusions`; the duty list under the table is `spirit-ce-2025-warranty-facility-responsibilities`.
