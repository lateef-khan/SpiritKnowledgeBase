Every Xterra treadmill warranty page ends with the same sentence: *Product features or specifications as described or illustrated are subject to change without notice. All warranties are made by XTERRA Fitness, Inc. **This warranty applies only in the 48 contiguous United States. NOTE: This does not apply to Alaska or Hawaii.*** (The 2024 TRX books and the second page of the Online-Support books write *does not include Alaska or Hawaii*; the meaning is the same.)

**The eight Online-Support books add exclusion 9**: *Warranties outside of the United States may vary. Please contact your local dealer for details* (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-with-a-non-dues-facility-allowance`) - so a machine sold abroad has whatever terms its local dealer gives, not this page. The Online-Support books print the closing sentence twice, at the foot of both the second and the third warranty page. The eleven older-layout books say nothing about other countries beyond the 48-states line.

Neither Canada nor any other country is named. The Sole 2026 pages say *outside of North America* where these say *outside of the United States* (`f65-2026-warranty-geographic-coverage`); the Spirit XT pages match these word for word (`xt-2023-warranty-geographic-coverage`).
