**Five numbered items, the same five in all thirty-nine books:**

1. The warranty registration - see below, this is the one item that is worded differently
   across the range.
2. Proper use of the fitness equipment in accordance with the instructions provided in
   the manual.
3. Expenses for making the fitness equipment accessible for servicing, **including any
   item that was not part of the fitness equipment at the time it was shipped from the
   factory**.
4. Damages to the fitness equipment finish during shipping, installation or following
   installation.
5. Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in the manual.

**Item 4 is the one owners are most often surprised by.** Marks to the finish put on the
machine while it is being delivered or built are the facility's cost, not a warranty
claim - and the wording covers damage *following* installation as well.

**Item 3 makes the cost of reaching the machine the facility's.** A machine on an upper
floor, behind a fixed rack, or with cladding added after delivery is made accessible at
the facility's expense.

**Item 1 is the only one whose wording changes.** The thirty-six CSD, CSS, CSF and CSI
books require online registration within 10 days of the delivery date; the three ST800
books require the registration card returned within 10 days of the original purchase, or
completion online. The full text of both is on
`spirit-strength-warranty-registration`.

The heading differs in case only - THE FACILITY IS RESPONSIBLE FOR THE ITEMS LISTED BELOW
on the thirty-six, sentence case on the three ST800 books.
