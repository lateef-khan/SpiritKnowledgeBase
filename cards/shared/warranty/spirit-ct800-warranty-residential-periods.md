| Warranty | Frame | Motor/Deck | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | Lifetime | Lifetime | 10 Years | 2 Years |

**The row is word for word the same in all four CT800 manuals** - the 2012, the 2016, the 2020 and the CT800ENT. Only the effective date printed above it changes: March 1, 2012; December 23, 2015; August 10, 2023; March 15, 2024.

Motor and deck share one column, so the manuals state no separate deck period. The 2016 and 2020 manuals print the labor figure as "2 Year" and the 2012 and CT800ENT as "2 Years"; the period is two years in all four.

**Labor is the figure that separates this table from the Spirit XT one.** The XT residential row also reads Lifetime, Lifetime, Lifetime, Parts 10 Years - and **Labor 1 Year**. Answering a CT800 labor question from `xt-2023-warranty-residential-coverage-periods` halves it. The XT table also splits Motor and Deck into two columns where this one does not.

**The CT800ENT prints two further columns** - Console Parts 3 Years and Console Labor 1 Year. Its Frame, Motor/Deck, Parts and Labor figures are exactly the four above. Read the console periods from `ct800ent-2022-warranty-console-periods`.

**A CT800 in a home is the second row of a commercial machine's table, not a separate warranty document.** The same exclusions, responsibilities and service steps govern both rows. The commercial row is `spirit-ct800-warranty-commercial-periods`; which use each row covers is `spirit-ct800-warranty-use-environment`.

When the clock starts is in the paragraph above the table (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`); the two-year labor figure is dated separately in the SERVICE paragraph (`spirit-ct800-warranty-labor-period`).
