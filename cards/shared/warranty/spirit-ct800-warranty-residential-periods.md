| Warranty | Frame | Motor/Deck | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | Lifetime | Lifetime | 10 Years | 2 Years |

**The row is word for word the same in all eight manuals** - the four CT800 owner's manuals and the four CT850 ones. Only the effective date printed above it changes: March 1, 2012; December 23, 2015; August 10, 2023; March 15, 2024 for the CT800 range, and November 12, 2021; December 23, 2015; August 10, 2023; July 03, 2024 for the CT850 range.

Motor and deck share one column, so no manual states a separate deck period. Six of the eight print the labor figure as "2 Year" (CT800 2016 and 2020, CT850 2016, 2018 and 2020) and the CT800 2012, CT800ENT and CT850ENT as "2 Years"; the period is two years in all eight.

**Labor is the figure that separates this table from the Spirit XT one.** The XT residential row also reads Lifetime, Lifetime, Lifetime, Parts 10 Years - and **Labor 1 Year**. Answering a CT800 labor question from `xt-2023-warranty-residential-coverage-periods` halves it. The XT table also splits Motor and Deck into two columns where this one does not.

**The two ENT manuals print two further columns** - Console Parts 3 Years and Console Labor 1 Year, in the CT800ENT and the CT850ENT alike. Their Frame, Motor/Deck, Parts and Labor figures are exactly the four above. Read the console periods from `ct800ent-2022-warranty-console-periods`.

**A CT800 or CT850 in a home is the second row of a commercial machine's table, not a separate warranty document.** The same exclusions, responsibilities and service steps govern both rows. The commercial row is `spirit-ct800-warranty-commercial-periods`; which use each row covers is `spirit-ct800-warranty-use-environment`.

When the clock starts is in the paragraph above the table (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`); the two-year labor figure is dated separately in the SERVICE paragraph (`spirit-ct800-warranty-labor-period`).

**The CT850 2013 book prints the same Residential row** - Lifetime, Lifetime, 10 Years, 2 Years - under the heading *Treadmill Warranty - Effective November 1, 2013*, the earliest effective date of the nine.
