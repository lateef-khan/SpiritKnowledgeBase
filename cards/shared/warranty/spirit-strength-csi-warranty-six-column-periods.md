| Period | Components |
|---|---|
| 10 Years - Parts | Frame / Welds / Cams |
| 5 Years - Parts | Bushings / Bearings / Shocks / Pulleys |
| **2 Years - Parts** | **Console / Resistance Module** |
| 1 Year - Parts | Paint & Finishes / Cables / Grips / Pull Pin Components / Rails Wheels / Attachments / All other parts |
| 6 Months - Parts | Upholstery |
| **1 Year - Labor** | **Console / Resistance Module** |

**This is the only strength table with six columns, and the only one that separates
parts from labour.** Every other Spirit strength manual prints four columns and folds
*Labor* into the one-year parts cell. Here labour has a column of its own and it covers
the console and resistance module only - the manual names no labour term for the frame,
the pulleys or the upholstery.

**Two components the older tables never mention.** The console and the resistance module
are new with this generation, and they are the only items carrying a two-year term
anywhere in the Spirit strength range.

**The ten-year and five-year cells are shorter than the older books'.** Plates are not
named at ten years and guide rods are not named at five, where the CSD and CSS tables
name both. Do not read the older cells across.

Both books carry *Effective January 01, 2026* - the newest warranty in the range:
`spirit-strength-warranty-effective-dates`.
