Items 1–7 and 9 are word-for-word `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-9-item-residential` (item 2 here reads "ordinary facility or household use"). Item 8 differs:

8. **Product used more than 5 hrs per day and/or in a dues paying facility.**

That cap is the other half of the dual columns in `sole-tt8-warranty-periods-dual-residential-light-commercial`: up to 5 hours a day in a non-dues-paying room takes the light-commercial column; beyond it there is no cover. Effective January 1, 2011 (ATT8, TT8C) and August 1, 2012 (TT8-2013).
