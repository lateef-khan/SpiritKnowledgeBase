**The three air rower manuals set the limit, and the two water rower manuals do not.** That is the whole answer, and it splits the range in half.

## The three air rowers: home, or a non-dues-paying facility at five hours a day

Both XRW600 printings and the CRW800 exclude, at item 8 of their exclusion lists:

> Product use in any environment other than a residential setting or **non-dues paying facility** with **5 hours use or less per day**.

Two conditions, and both have to hold. A machine in a facility that charges its users - a gym, a health club, a studio - is outside the warranty regardless of hours. A machine in a facility that does not charge - a corporate gym, an apartment or hotel room, a fire station - is inside it only while it is used five hours a day or less.

**The CRW800's warranty table says the same thing in its row label**, so the rule is printed twice on that machine: its commercial row reads *Commercial (Non-dues paying facility)*, and its exclusion 8 repeats the bar. The XRW600 tables have no commercial row at all, so item 8 is the only statement either XRW600 book makes about where the machine may go.

**Nobody counts the hours.** The manuals give no meter reading, no hour counter and no method of proof. Do not tell a customer their hours will be checked, and do not tell them they will not be.

## The two water rowers: no limit anywhere

**The CRW900 and CRW800H2O exclusion lists carry no use-environment item.** Their item 8 is the prisons exclusion, and their item 2 stops one clause short of the air rowers' - it never adds *or not in ordinary household or light commercial use*: `spirit-water-rower-warranty-exclusions-eight-items`.

So on a water rower the only environment excluded by name is a prison or correctional facility. What the manual does instead is price the environment: both tables carry a **Commercial** row with shorter periods than the residential one, the CRW800H2O labelling its row *(Non dues paying facility.)* and the CRW900 labelling its row **Commercial** with no qualifier at all. **The CRW900 is the one machine here where a dues-paying club is neither excluded nor named.** The book does not answer that question; do not answer it for them.

**Do not carry this card onto another Spirit line.** The 2024 machines, the XB bikes and the climbers all word their item 8 differently, and several of them exclude a dues-paying facility in a separate item this range does not have.
