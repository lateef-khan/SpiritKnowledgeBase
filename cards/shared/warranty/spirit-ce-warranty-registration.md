## The deadline, and the two manuals that state none

| Manual | Responsibilities item 1 says | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| CE800 2013 | card completed and returned to the address on the card | **10 days** |
| CE850 2016, 2020, 2022 | card completed and returned to the address on the card | **10 days** |
| CE900 2021 | card returned to the address on the card, **or completed online** | **10 days** |
| CE900 2025 | "The warranty registration must be completed **online**" | **none stated** |
| CES880 2025 | card within 10 days, **or** completed online | **10 days** |

**The CE900 2025 manual names no card and no deadline anywhere.** It is the only one of the seven that does. The CES880, printed two and a half months later, restores both. Read the rule off the manual in hand.

**Three CE850 manuals offer no online route in item 1** - only the card, returned to the address printed on it - even though all three print a REGISTER YOUR PURCHASE page that does offer online registration. No CE850 manual reconciles the two halves. Say both to a customer.

## Where to register

| Manual | Page the manual sends you to |
|---|---|
| CE800 2013 | the residential warranty registration page under the Support tab |
| CE850 2016 | https://www.spiritfitness.com/commercialwarrantyregistration.html |
| CE850 2020, CE850 2022 | http://www.spiritfitness.com/warranty |
| CE900 2021 | www.spiritfitness.com/warranty |
| CE900 2025, CES880 2025 | a **QR code** to scan, or spiritfitness.com/warranty |

**The CE800 2013 link is OCR-damaged and is not quoted here as a literal URL.** The scan reads the host and the `.htm` suffix with commas in place of full stops and a broken final character, so only the path word `residentialwarrantyregistration` is legible with confidence. Send a CE800 2013 owner to the current Support tab rather than typing that address out of the scan.

**The 2013 manual sends the owner of a commercial elliptical to the residential registration page** and the 2016 CE850 sends them to the commercial one. Neither explains the difference. Treat it as a documentation defect, not as evidence about the machine.

**Only the two 2025 manuals print a QR code**, on their PRODUCT REGISTRATION page beside the Serial Number, Date of Purchase and Dealer boxes. Their serial sticker is on the **front** stabilizer on the CE900 2025 and on the **rear** stabilizer on the CES880. No other manual of the seven prints a QR code, a registration phone number or an email address on that page.

## What happens if nobody registers

**Five of the seven exclude the product outright.** Exclusion 6 in the CE800 2013, all three CE850 manuals and the CE900 2021:

> Products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at Spirit Fitness. Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.

**The two sentences do not say the same thing** - the first excludes the product, the second only reserves a right to ask for proof of purchase - and none of the five reconciles them. Say both.

**The CE900 2025 and CES880 dropped the first sentence.** Their item 6 reads only "Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product." An unregistered machine under the 2024 terms is **not** excluded by that list: `spirit-ce-2025-warranty-exclusions`.

Keeping the bill of sale matters either way: it is what the periods are dated from when there is a receipt, and eighteen months from factory shipping is the fallback when there is not - `xt-2023-warranty-start-date`.

**Do not carry another Spirit manual's registration rule across.** The CE800 2021, CE800 2023 and CE800ENT manuals state the rule in the CT800 and CT850 wording and are carded with them at `spirit-ct800-warranty-registration`, which lists their registration pages. The XT, CT900 and 2026 rules differ again.

## Seven Spirit commercial bike manuals, and the address changes twice

**The four 900-series bikes print the CE900 2021 rule exactly** - item 1 offering a card returned within **10 days** *or* online registration, and the two-sentence exclusion 6 that excludes an unregistered product outright. **The CR900 2025, CU900 2025 and CU800ENT 2024 print the CE900 2025 rule** - "The warranty registration must be completed **online**", no card and no day count anywhere - and, like the CE900 2025 and CES880, their item 6 keeps only the proof-of-purchase sentence, so an unregistered machine is not excluded by that list.

| Manual | Page the manual sends you to |
|---|---|
| CR900 2018, CR900ENT 2021 | www.spiritfitness.com/warranty |
| CU900 2018, CU900ENT | www.spiritfitness.com/**commercialwarrantyregistration.html** |
| CR900 2025, CU900 2025, CU800ENT 2024 | a **QR code** to scan, or spiritfitness.com/warranty |

**The recumbent and the upright of the same generation do not send the owner to the same page.** The CR900 2018 and CR900ENT send them to `/warranty`; the CU900 2018 and CU900ENT, whose warranty pages are otherwise word for word the same, send them to `commercialwarrantyregistration.html`. Neither manual explains the difference. Read the link off the manual in hand.

**The three 2024-terms bikes print the QR code** on their PRODUCT REGISTRATION page beside the Serial Number, Date of Purchase and Dealer boxes, and all three put the serial sticker on the **front stabilizer**. No other bike manual prints a QR code.

The six CR800, CU800, CU800ENT 2022 and CR800ENT bikes state the rule in the CT800 wording and are carded with the treadmills at `spirit-ct800-warranty-registration`.
