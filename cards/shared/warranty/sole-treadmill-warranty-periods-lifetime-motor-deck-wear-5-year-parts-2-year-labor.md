**Treadmill warranty, effective January 1, 2008.**

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Drive Motor** | **Lifetime** |
| **Deck Wear** | **Lifetime** |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **All Other Components** | **5 Years** |
| **Labor** | **2 Years** |

**No cosmetic-items row is printed.** Same periods as `sole-treadmill-warranty-periods-lifetime-motor-deck-5-year-parts-2-year-labor` except for the missing cosmetic row and "Deck Wear" wording — kept as its own card so neither states a figure the other book does not print.

When the clock starts is separate; see `sole-treadmill-warranty-start-date-receipt-or-18-months-shipping`. What is not covered is on `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-7-item-2004-2010`.
