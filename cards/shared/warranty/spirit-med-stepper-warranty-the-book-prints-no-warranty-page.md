**Neither book prints one.** No warranty page, no coverage table, no period, no exclusion list, no
registration card, no return-authorization procedure and no serial-number location.

## How the absence was checked

A loose search for the **word** `warrant` - not a phrase - returns **three hits in each book**, and
none of them is a warranty term:

| Where | What it says |
|---|---|
| Introduction, PDF p. 2 | *"It is backed by one of the most comprehensive **warranties** in the industry."* |
| Introduction, PDF p. 2 | *"Your **warranty** and service needs will be addressed either through your regional sales representative or our highly trained service technicians."* |
| Important electrical information, printed p. 15 | supply voltage more than 10% outside 100-240 V may affect performance, and *"Such conditions are not covered under your **warranty**."* |

`registrat`, `return authoriz` and `serial` return **zero hits each**, in both books.

**The introduction promises a warranty and the book never states it.** That is the finding: the
terms exist somewhere, and not in this document.

## Where the terms actually are

The **MS300** is the same machine under a Spirit badge
(`spirit-rehab-stepper-specs-two-different-machines-are-called-7-0s`), and its book carries the
whole chapter - **twenty-four months from the bill of sale, not transferable**, a coverage table
with a lifetime frame and a five-year EMS brake, an exclusion list with a clinician-liability
clause, and service contacts. See `ms300-2021-warranty-period-twenty-four-months`,
`ms300-2021-warranty-coverage-periods` and `ms300-2021-warranty-exclusions`.

**Do not quote those terms as this book's**, because a MED-badged machine may have been sold
through a different distributor under different terms; the manual is silent. Say the manual prints
none and refer the customer to their selling dealer or to Spirit Fitness.

## The only exclusion these books do print

Out-of-range supply voltage. If mains varies by 10% or more outside 100-240 V, *"the performance of
your 7.0S may be affected. Such conditions are not covered under your warranty."* That is a
condition, not a period, and it is on the electrical page rather than a warranty page.
