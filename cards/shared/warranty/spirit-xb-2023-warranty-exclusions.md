This warranty does not cover the following:

1. CONSEQUENTIAL, COLLATERAL, OR INCIDENTAL DAMAGES SUCH AS PROPERTY DAMAGE AND INCIDENTAL EXPENSES RESULTING FROM ANY BREACH OF THIS WRITTEN OR ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY. *Note: Some states do not allow the exclusion or limitation of incidental or consequential damages, so this limitation or exclusion may not apply to you.*
2. Service call reimbursement to the consumer. Service call reimbursement to the dealer that does not involve malfunction or defects in workmanship or material, for units that are beyond the warranty period, for units that are beyond the service call reimbursement period, for Recumbent Bike not requiring component replacement, or **Recumbent Bike not in ordinary household or light commercial use**.
3. Damages caused by services performed by persons other than authorized Spirit Fitness service companies; use of parts other than original Spirit Fitness parts; or external causes such as corrosion, discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, or acts of God.
4. Products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered.
5. Products that have been: sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party.
6. **Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.**
7. THIS WARRANTY IS EXPRESSLY IN LIEU OF ALL OTHER WARRANTIES EXPRESSED OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING THE WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND/OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.
8. **Product use in any environment other than a residential setting or non-dues paying facility with 5 hours use or less per day.**
9. Warranties outside of **the United States** may vary. Please contact your local dealer for details.

**Item 2 names the machine by type, not generically.** Item 2 is quoted above from a Recumbent Bike manual: the XBR25, XBR55 and XBR95 books read *Recumbent Bike* in both places, and the **XBU55** book reads *Upright Bike* in both. The rule is the same. This is the change the older manuals' equivalent item does not make - they all read the generic *fitness bike*, whatever the machine.

**The four lists are word for word identical apart from that noun.** The XBR25 is effective July 25, 2023 and the other three August 07, 2023; nothing in the list moves between them.

## Three changes from the older XB manuals

The ten printings before 2023 carry an eight-item list (`spirit-xb-warranty-exclusions`). Three things changed:

- **The consequential-damages paragraph is now numbered.** It was an unnumbered block above item 1; it is item 1 here, which shifts every later item by one.
- **An unregistered bike is no longer excluded.** The old item 5 read *Products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at Spirit Fitness. Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase...* The first sentence is **gone**. What is left, as item 6, only reserves a right to ask for a receipt. Nothing else in a 2023 manual excludes an unregistered machine, and nothing in it sets a registration deadline either (`spirit-xb-2023-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`).
- **Item 8 gained a second environment.** The old item 7 allowed a residential setting and nothing else; item 8 here also allows a **non-dues paying facility with 5 hours use or less per day**.

**Item 9 changed area too** - *outside of North America* became *outside of the United States*, which narrows what the manual treats as covered ground. The 48-contiguous-states note is a separate sentence and these manuals print it **twice**, once under this list and once under the service steps (`xt-2023-warranty-geographic-coverage`).

## Item 8 contradicts two of the four tables

The **XBR25 2023** and the **XBR55 2023** print **no Light Commercial table at all** - one Residential row and nothing else - yet item 8 allows a non-dues-paying facility at five hours a day, and item 2 speaks of "ordinary household or light commercial use". So those two manuals permit an environment for which they state no periods. The XBR95 2023 and XBU55 2023 do print a Light Commercial table and are consistent. See `spirit-xb-warranty-use-environment`.

**Nothing in any of the four lists mentions a dues-paying gym, a YMCA or a health club**, and nothing in the list mentions prisons - the prisons exclusion on the XBR95 and XBU55 is a **footnote to the Light Commercial table**, not a numbered item (`spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`).

Items 4 and 5 are worth reading together: a bike sold on with its serial sticker peeled off is outside the warranty on both counts, and the manual names no way to reinstate cover for a second owner (`xt-2023-warranty-transfer`).
