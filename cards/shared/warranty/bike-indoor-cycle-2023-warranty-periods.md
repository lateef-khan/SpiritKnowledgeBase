Effective **March 1, 2023**. One schedule only — the 2023 indoor cycle manuals print no residential and light commercial split.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| Frame Weldments | Lifetime |
| All Other Components | 2 Years |
| Labor | 1 Year |

SOLE's responsibilities include providing new or remanufactured parts, at SOLE's option, and technical support to independent dealers and servicing organizations. Where there is no dealer or service organization, SOLE administers the warranty directly to the consumer.

**This is not the pre-2023 indoor cycle schedule.** Those manuals print Parts/Electronics 3 Years in two columns, residential and light commercial. The 2023 manuals print a single "All Other Components 2 Years" row and name the frame row "Frame Weldments".

The console is a separate matter. Where an SB900 carries the Universal Group Cycling Monitor, that monitor is warranted for **one year by Direction Technology**, not for two years by SOLE.
