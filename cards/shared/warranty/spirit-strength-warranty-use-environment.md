**The manuals name two settings and only two: a non-dues-paying facility, and household
use.** No strength manual states a term for a dues-paying gym or health club - it is
neither granted nor excluded. Exclusion 2 is the only place the question is answered,
and it answers it by describing what reimbursement is *not* available for:

> ...for fitness equipment not requiring component replacement, or fitness equipment not
> in non-dues paying facility or household use.

**The thirty-six CSD, CSS, CSF and CSI books print one row, headed Commercial**, with no
home row beside it. A reader looking only at the table sees a commercial machine with no
household terms at all; the household wording is on the next page, in the exclusion. Both
halves are the answer. The exception is the CSF-FUNT, whose single row is headed
*Light Commercial* and which the manual never defines:
`spirit-strength-csf-funt-light-commercial-warranty`.

**The three ST800 books print both rows.** They carry a **Residential (vendor defects
only)** row of lifetime terms above a **Commercial (non-dues paying facilities)** row,
so the household case is stated in the table rather than only in the exclusion, and the
commercial row is explicitly qualified.

A prison or correctional facility is excluded outright, everywhere in the range:
`spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.
