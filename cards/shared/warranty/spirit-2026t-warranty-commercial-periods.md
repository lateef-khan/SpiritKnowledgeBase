The 2026 4.0T, 7.0T and 8.0T print **one** warranty row, and it is the same row in all three manuals:

| Warranty | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial | 5 Years | 1 Year |

\*Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage - see `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.

**There is no Frame, Motor, Deck or Wear Items column, and no second row.** Parts and labor are the only two periods these manuals state. Do not answer a frame or motor question from another Spirit table: the CT900 warrants the frame 10 Years and the motor 5, the CT900ENT the frame for Lifetime and the drive motor 10 Years, and the XT residential table reads Lifetime on frame, motor and deck. None of those figures is printed for these three machines.

**There is also no residential or home-use table in any of the three manuals.** The section is headed WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL and it is the only warranty table printed. A home buyer's question about these machines has no answer in the source.

Only the effective date and the model name change between the three:

| Manual | Effective date | Version / revision |
|---|---|---|
| 4.0T | December 08, 2025 | Version 1.2, revision 01.08.2026 |
| 7.0T | November 18, 2025 | Version 3.0, revision 11.18.25 |
| 8.0T | November 18, 2025 | Version 1.0, revision 11.20.25 |
| 4.0T, 2025 printings | October 31, 2024 | revision 02.04.25 (February) and 05.19.25 (May), (c) 2024 |
| 7.0T, 2025 printing | October 23, 2024 | revision 01.10.25, (c) 2024 |

**A sentence in the source is left dangling.** The paragraph above the table ends "The warranty period applies to the following components:" and no component list follows it - the next thing printed is the Commercial Warranty heading. This is the printed page, not a conversion loss: the page was rendered from the PDF at 300 dpi and the gap is blank there too, in all three manuals.

When the clock starts is in the paragraph above the table (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`), and the one-year labor figure is dated separately in the SERVICE paragraph two pages later (`xt-2023-warranty-labor-period`). Neither is on this card.

**The 2025 printings of the 4.0T and 7.0T owner's manuals print the same row - Parts 5 Years, Labor 1 Year - and the same dangling sentence.** Only the effective date moves, back to October 31, 2024 (4.0T) and October 23, 2024 (7.0T); the two 4.0T printings of February and May 2025 are word for word identical. The 2025 medical bikes print the same one-row table (`spirit-med-bike-warranty-commercial-parts-five-years-labor-one-year`).
