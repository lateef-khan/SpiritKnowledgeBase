**Yes, and the deadline is ten days.** Three passages in each of these eleven books say so:

- **Product Registration page** (p. 3 or 4): *The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to XTERRA Fitness.* Most books add *You can also go to www.xterrafitness.com/warranty to register online.* The page also asks the owner to write down the **serial number**, the **purchase location** and the **purchase date**, and to have the model and serial number handy when calling **(870) 336-4286**.
- **Consumer responsibility 1**: *The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within 10 days of the original purchase to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty* (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-with-a-registration-card`, `xterra-trx-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-seven-items`).
- **Exclusion 7**: *Products that do not have a warranty registration [card] on file at XTERRA Fitness. XTERRA Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product* (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-store-display-and-unregistered-products`, `xterra-trx-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-floor-models-corrosion-and-discoloration`).

So an unregistered machine is, by the letter of these books, outside the warranty, with proof of purchase as the fallback XTERRA may ask for.

**Which online address each book prints** - the URL is not the same everywhere, and two books print none:

| Book | Registration page | Online registration address printed |
|---|---|---|
| TR150 (tr150-2021) | p. 4 | www.xterrafitness.com/warranty |
| TR200 (tr200-2021) | p. 4 | www.xterrafitness.com/warranty |
| TR260 (tr260-2023) | p. 3 | **none** - the card only |
| TR300 (tr300-2021) | p. 3 | www.xterrafitness.com/warranty; item 1 adds http://www.xterrafitness.com/warrantyreg.html |
| TR6.4 (tr64-2024) | p. 3 | www.xterrafitness.com/warranty; item 1 adds http://www.xterrafitness.com/warrantyreg.html |
| TR6.6 (tr66-2021) | p. 3 | www.xterrafitness.com/warranty; item 1 adds http://www.xterrafitness.com/warrantyreg.html |
| TRX1000 (trx1000-2021) | p. 3 | **none** - the card only |
| TRX2500 (trx2500-2024) | p. 3 | www.xterrafitness.com/warranty |
| TRX3500 (trx3500-2024) | p. 3 | www.xterrafitness.com/warranty |
| TRX4500 (trx4500-2024) | p. 3 | www.xterrafitness.com/warranty |
| TRX5500 (trx5500-2024) | p. 3 | www.xterrafitness.com/warrantyreg.html |

The TRX1000 and TR260 give the owner only the mailed card; the TRX5500 sends the owner to `warrantyreg.html` on its registration page where its siblings say `/warranty`; the TR300, TR6.6 and TR6.4 print both forms, one on each page.

**The eight Online-Support books do not impose this deadline.** They print a QR code and a Support-tab address and never make registration a duty (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-qr-code-and-support-tab-no-deadline`). Do not tell a TR65, TR75, TR85, TR75H, TR95H, TRX1400, WS200 or WS300 owner they had ten days.

The same ten-day rule in the same words is on the Spirit CT800 (`spirit-ct800-warranty-registration`) and the 2015 XT (`xt-2015-warranty-registration`) pages, and on the Sole 2026 pages (`f65-2026-warranty-registration-requirement`).
