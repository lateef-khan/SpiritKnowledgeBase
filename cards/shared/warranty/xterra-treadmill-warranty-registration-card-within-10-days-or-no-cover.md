**Yes, and the deadline is ten days.** Three passages in each of these eleven treadmill books - and in the twenty-six older-layout bike, elliptical and seated-stepper books with a warranty page - say so:

- **Product Registration page** (p. 3 or 4): *The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to XTERRA Fitness.* Most books add *You can also go to www.xterrafitness.com/warranty to register online.* The page also asks the owner to write down the **serial number**, the **purchase location** and the **purchase date**, and to have the model and serial number handy when calling **(870) 336-4286**.
- **Consumer responsibility 1**: *The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within 10 days of the original purchase to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty* (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-with-a-registration-card`, `xterra-trx-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-seven-items`).
- **Exclusion 7** (6 in the ten eight-item bike and elliptical books): *Products that do not have a warranty registration [card] on file at XTERRA Fitness. XTERRA Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product* (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-store-display-and-unregistered-products`, `xterra-trx-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-floor-models-corrosion-and-discoloration`).

So an unregistered machine is, by the letter of these books, outside the warranty, with proof of purchase as the fallback XTERRA may ask for.

**The SB120 service document says no card is packed.** The December 2019 SB120 service sheet notes *Serial Number on unit, no registration card with OM* - so an SB120 owner may find no card to mail even though the manual's consumer responsibility 1 demands one within ten days; the online address is the way to register. The FB160 service document says only *Must register WARRANTY*.

**The eight Online-Support treadmill books, and the FB180, SB240 and SB600, do not impose this deadline.** They print a QR code and a Support-tab address and never make registration a duty (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-qr-code-and-support-tab-no-deadline`). Do not tell a TR65, TR75, TR85, TR75H, TR95H, TRX1400, WS200, WS300, FB180, SB240 or SB600 owner they had ten days.

The same ten-day rule in the same words is on the Spirit CT800 (`spirit-ct800-warranty-registration`) and the 2015 XT (`xt-2015-warranty-registration`) pages, and on the Sole 2026 pages (`f65-2026-warranty-registration-requirement`).
