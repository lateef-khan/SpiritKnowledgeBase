## The deadline, and the four printings that state none

Ten of the fourteen manuals make registration **item 1** of the responsibilities list, word for word:

> The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card **within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.

**The four 2023 manuals name no deadline and no card at all.** Registration is not one of their numbered responsibilities and nothing in their warranty section asks the owner to register (`spirit-xb-2023-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`). Their Warranty Registration page still invites it - with a QR code - but imposes no time limit.

**None of the ten offers an online route inside item 1.** Every one requires the *card returned to the address printed on it*, even though all ten print a registration page a few pages earlier offering online registration. No XB manual reconciles the two halves. Say both to a customer.

## Where to register

**The address changes twice across the range. Read it off the manual that came with the machine.**

| Manuals | What the registration page gives |
|---|---|
| XBR95 2016, XBR95 2018, XBR25 2019, XBR55 2019, XBU55 2019 | card returned to Spirit Fitness, or **http://www.spiritfitness.com/residentialwarrantyregistration.html** under the Support tab |
| XBR95 2021, XBR55 2021, XBU55 2021 | card returned to Spirit Fitness, or **http://**www.spiritfitness.com/warranty under the Support tab |
| XBR55ENT 2021, XBU55ENT 2021 | card returned to Spirit Fitness, or www.spiritfitness.com/warranty under the Support tab, with no scheme |
| XBR25 2023, XBR55 2023, XBR95 2023, XBU55 2023 | a **QR code** to scan, or **spiritfitness.com/warranty** under the Support tab |

**The five 2018-and-2019 printings are the only ones that name a dedicated registration page rather than a warranty path**, and the switch happens between February 2019 and February 2021 - the XBU55 2019 prints the long URL and the XBU55 2021, the same machine two years later, prints `/warranty`. **No XB manual prints any address other than these three**; the range introduces no fourth.

**Only the four 2023 books print a QR code.** The ten older ones print none - the two ENT manuals do show QR codes elsewhere, for pairing the Spirit+ app, and those are nothing to do with registration.

## What the page asks you to record

**The ten older manuals ask for the serial number and nothing else.** The page is headed *Record Your Serial Number*, gives one blank line, and prints a **Serial Number Location** illustration beside it.

**The four 2023 manuals ask for three things** - **Serial Number**, **Date of Purchase**, and **Dealer / Place of Purchase** - and say the sticker is on the **front stabilizer tube**, with the number to record being the one below the barcode.

**Each 2023 book stamps its own version on that page**, which is the only place the manual dates itself:

| Manual | Version | Revision stamp |
|---|---|---|
| XBR25 2023 | 3.0 | 04.18.24 |
| XBR55 2023 | 6.0 | 04.16.23 |
| XBR95 2023 | 4.0 | 08.07.23 |
| XBU55 2023 | 4.0 | 04.12.23 |

**The XBR55 2023 stamp predates its own warranty effective date.** Its page-2 revision reads 04.16.23 and its warranty page reads *Effective August 07, 2023*, so the warranty was replaced without the version stamp being bumped. Do not date that book's warranty terms from its version stamp.

## What happens if nobody registers

**Ten of the fourteen exclude the product outright**, as exclusion 5:

> Products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at Spirit Fitness. Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.

**The two sentences do not say the same thing.** The first excludes the product; the second only reserves a right to ask for proof of purchase. None of the ten reconciles them. Say both.

**The four 2023 manuals deleted the first sentence.** Their item 6 reads only *Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product*. An unregistered 2023 bike is **not** named as excluded anywhere in that manual (`spirit-xb-2023-warranty-exclusions`).

## The support numbers on the page changed with the book

The ten older manuals print **800.258.4555** and **spiritservice@spiritfitness.com** on the back cover; the four 2023 manuals print **1-800-258-8511**, **questions@spiritfitness.com** and a fax on 870-935-7611. The service department number inside the warranty section is **(870) 935-1107** on all fourteen (`xt-2023-warranty-service-steps`).

Keeping the bill of sale matters on every one of the fourteen: it is what the periods are dated from, and eighteen months from factory shipping is the fallback when there is no receipt (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`).

**Do not carry a Spirit commercial machine's registration rule across.** The CT800, CT850, CE800 and CR800 manuals add "or completed online" to item 1, and one of them sends the owner to a *commercial* warranty registration page (`spirit-ct800-warranty-registration`).
