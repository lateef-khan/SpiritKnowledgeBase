**Elliptical warranty on the Sole E35-2023, effective February 1, 2023.** First appearance of a **flywheel** row in a Sole elliptical warranty table, verified on the render (PDF p. 28). No brake row is printed.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Flywheel** | **Lifetime** |
| **All Other Components** | **2 Years** |
| **Labor** | **1 Years** (sic — "1 Years", as printed) |
| **Cosmetic Items** * | **90 Days** |

\* Cosmetic items including, but not limited to: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.

The E25-2023 sibling keeps a lifetime brake row with the same 2-year others; the E95/E95S-2023 pair keep the flywheel row with 3-year others — each on its own card, linked above. The book's duties and exclusions pages are treadmill copy ("Proper use of the treadmill", "belt tracking") — see `sole-elliptical-warranty-responsibilities-8-item-treadmill-wording-2023`.
