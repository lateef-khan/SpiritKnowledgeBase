1. The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within **10 days** of the original purchase **(the two CSC900 manuals add "or completed online")** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.
2. Proper use in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual.
3. Proper installation in accordance with the instructions provided and with all local electric codes.
4. Expenses for making the machine accessible for servicing, including any item that was not part of it at the time it was shipped from the factory.
5. Damages to the finish during shipping, installation or following installation.
6. Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual.

**Six items, and item 4 of the seven-item list is not among them.** These four manuals ask nothing about a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage, blown fuses, or loose connections and defects in house or facility wiring. Every other Spirit climber and stepper does (`spirit-ct800-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`, `spirit-ce-2025-warranty-facility-responsibilities`). **So do not tell an XS895 or CSC900 owner that their wiring is their responsibility under this list** - the list does not say it. Their exclusion 3 still refuses damage caused by inadequate power supply, which is a different sentence and a narrower one.

**The item numbers do not line up with any sibling manual.** Accessibility is item **4** here and item **5** or **6** elsewhere. Cite the duty, not the number.

## The heading names a different person on each machine

| Manual | Heading | Who the lead sentence addresses |
|---|---|---|
| XS895 2018, XS895 2021 | NORMAL RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE **CONSUMER** | "This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household or Light Commercial use (see restrictions above), and the **consumer/facility** is responsible..." |
| CSC900 2019, CSC900 2021 | NORMAL RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE **FACILITY** | "The **facility** is responsible for the items listed below:" |

**The XS895 lead sentence carries a condition the CSC900 one does not.** It says the warranty applies *only* to ordinary household or Light Commercial use and points back to the restrictions above the list - the five-hours-a-day cap in its own table row. The CSC900 sentence carries no such qualifier, because that manual has only a Commercial row (`spirit-climber-warranty-use-environment`).

**Only the two CSC900 manuals offer an online alternative to the card.** The XS895 books require the card returned to the address printed on it, within ten days, full stop - even though both XS895 manuals print a *Register Your Purchase* page that does offer online registration and never reconciles the two. Say both to a customer (`spirit-ce-warranty-registration`).

**Failing to register is an exclusion on all four.** Item 6 of each manual's exclusion list excludes a product with no registration card on file - `spirit-csc900-warranty-exclusions` for the CSC900, `spirit-ce850-warranty-exclusions` for the XS895.

**All four lists are word for word identical within their pair, and the two pairs differ only in the noun.** The XS895 books name the *Incline Stepper*; the CSC900 books say *fitness equipment*.
