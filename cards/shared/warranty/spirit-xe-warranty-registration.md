## The deadline, and the two manuals that state none

Fifteen of the seventeen manuals make registration **item 1** of the responsibilities list, word for word:

> The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card **within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.

**The two 2023 manuals name no deadline and no card at all.** Registration is not one of their numbered responsibilities, and nothing in the warranty section asks the owner to register (`spirit-xe-2023-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`). Their PRODUCT REGISTRATION page still invites it - with a QR code - but imposes no time limit.

**None of the seventeen offers an online route inside item 1.** Every one that states the duty requires the *card returned to the address printed on it*, even though fifteen of the seventeen print a registration page offering online registration a few dozen pages earlier. No XE or XG manual reconciles the two halves. Say both to a customer.

## Where to register

**The address is different in six of them. Read it off the manual that came with the machine.**

| Manual | What the registration page gives |
|---|---|
| XE150/XE350/XE550 2005 | card returned to **Spirit Manufacturing, Inc**, or www.spiritfitness.com |
| XE100/XE200/XE300 2007 | card returned to Spirit Manufacturing, Inc, or www.spiritfitness.com/warranty.htm |
| XE395 2010 | card returned to Spirit Manufacturing, Inc., or www.spiritfitness.com/warranty.asp under the **support** tab |
| XE395 2018, XE795 2018, XE895 2018, XE195 2019, XE295 2019, XG400 2019 | card returned to Spirit Fitness, or **https://www.spiritfitness.com/residentialwarrantyregistration.html** under the Support tab |
| XE795 2021 (both printings), XE395ENT 2021, XG400 2021 | card returned to Spirit Fitness, or www.spiritfitness.com/warranty under the **Support** tab |
| XE195 2021, XE295 2021 | card returned to Spirit Fitness, or **https://**www.spiritfitness.com/warranty under the Support tab |
| XE395 2023, XE795 2023 | a **QR code** to scan, or spiritfitness.com/warranty under the Support tab |

**The six 2018 and 2019 printings are the only ones that name a dedicated registration page rather than a warranty path.** Their sentence reads *You can also go to https://www.spiritfitness.com/residentialwarrantyregistration.html under the Support tab to register online* - a longer URL than the `/warranty` one every 2021 and 2023 manual uses, and one that no XE or XG manual after February 2019 prints. The sentence before it is the one every XE manual prints: *The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to Spirit Fitness.*

**The 2005 book sends the owner to the bare home page**, with no warranty path at all - the only manual of the seventeen that does. **Only the two 2023 manuals print a QR code**; the six 2018 and 2019 printings print none. None of the seventeen prints a registration telephone number or an email address on that page.

Every one of the seventeen asks the owner to record the **serial number** in a space on the same page. The 2023 manuals add that the sticker is on the **front stabilizer** and that the number to record is the one below the barcode; all six 2018 and 2019 printings and five of the six 2021 manuals print a Serial Number Location illustration - the XE395ENT is the exception and states no location; the three older books state none either.

## What happens if nobody registers

**Fifteen of the seventeen exclude the product outright.** Exclusion 6 - numbered 5 in the two XE195 printings - in the 2005, 2007, 2010 books, the six 2018 and 2019 printings and all six 2021 manuals:

> Products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at Spirit Fitness. Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.

**The 2005 and 2007 books name a different company** - the card is on file at **Spirit Mfg**, and **Spirit** reserves the right to ask - and the 2010 XE395 names **Spirit Fitness Inc.** The rule does not move.

**The two sentences of item 6 do not say the same thing.** The first excludes the product; the second only reserves a right to ask for proof of purchase. None of the fifteen reconciles them. Say both.

**The two 2023 manuals deleted the first sentence.** Their item 6 reads only *Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.* An unregistered 2023 machine is **not** named as excluded anywhere in that manual (`spirit-xe-2023-warranty-exclusions`).

Keeping the bill of sale matters on every one of the seventeen: it is what the periods are dated from, and eighteen months from factory shipping is the fallback when there is no receipt (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`).

**Do not carry a Spirit commercial elliptical's registration rule across.** The CE800 2021 and later manuals add "or completed online" to item 1, the CE900 2025 names no card and no deadline, and the CE850 range differs again: `spirit-ce-warranty-registration`, `spirit-ct800-warranty-registration`.
