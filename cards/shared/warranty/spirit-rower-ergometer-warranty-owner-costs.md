**A warranty repair is not a free repair.** Five of the six manuals - both XRW600 printings, the CRW800, the CRW900 and the CRW800H2O - make the owner responsible for four things:

- **Freight to and from a service center**, if the product is shipped to one.
- **Shipping and handling on replacement parts** sent while the product is under warranty.
- **A trip charge for in-home service.**
- **A further trip charge** if the customer is more than **100 miles** from the nearest service center.

**The 8.5UE upper body ergometer stops after the second.** Its step 4 ends at *the customer will be responsible for shipping and handling charges* and says nothing about a trip charge or about 100 miles. On that machine the manual states no call-out fee at all. Do not quote one to a facility from a rower book.

**No figure is given anywhere.** None of the six names a dollar amount for a trip charge, for the second trip charge, or for shipping and handling. The manuals establish only that the owner pays. Do not invent a rate.

**The 100-mile line is measured to the nearest service center, not to the dealer**, and the manuals give no way to find out which service center that is - step 3 tells the owner to ask the service department on (870) 935-1107.

**"Repairing or replacing, at Spirit Fitness' option" comes in the same sentence.** The owner does not choose between a repair and a replacement, and the cost items above apply either way.

**Do not carry a Spirit treadmill or climber figure onto these machines**, or the reverse. Several Spirit ranges stop at shipping and handling and several add the trip charges; the split does not follow the product line. Read the machine's own step 4.
