**Three different answers, by manual.** Read the row labels off the manual that came with the machine.

| Manuals | Dues-paying gym (YMCA, health club) | Non-dues-paying facility (hotel, office, apartment, fire station) | Home |
|---|---|---|---|
| CU800 2012; CR800 2021, 2023; CU800 2021; CU800ENT 2022; CR800ENT 2023 | **Excluded** by exclusion 8 | Covered - Commercial row | Covered - Residential row |
| CR900 2018; CU900 2018; CR900ENT 2021; CU900ENT | **Not excluded anywhere**; two of them say *All Facilities* in the row label | Covered - Commercial row | **No terms stated** - there is no Residential row |
| CR900 2025; CU900 2025 | **Covered, with its own row** at reduced periods | Covered | Covered |
| CU800ENT 2024 | No row and no exclusion - **the manual does not say** | Covered - Commercial row | Covered - Residential row |

**Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded on all thirteen**, whatever the rest of the table says (`spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`).

## The six CR800, CU800 and 800ENT manuals exclude a dues-paying gym outright

> 8. Product used in a dues paying facility (YMCA, Health Club, Etc.)

It is item 8 on all six, and their exclusion 2 withholds service-call reimbursement for equipment "not in non-dues paying facility or household use". Five of the six say *Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities)* in the row label as well; the **CU800 2012 leaves the label as the bare word Commercial**, and it is exclusion 8 that narrows it there. A CU800 2012 in a health club is outside cover.

## The four 900-series manuals contradict themselves

Nothing in the CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900ENT or CU900ENT excludes a dues-paying facility - their exclusion lists run to eight items and no item names one - and the two ENT manuals label the row **Commercial (All Facilities)** in as many words. **But exclusion 2 withholds service-call reimbursement for equipment "not in non-dues paying facility or household use"** on all four, which is the narrowing their tables do not make. The manuals never reconcile the two.

**Say both to a customer.** The warranty periods themselves are not conditioned on the facility anywhere in those four manuals; only the service-call reimbursement is. And because none of the four prints a Residential row, a home owner of a CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900ENT or CU900ENT has **no stated terms at all** - do not answer them from a CR800 or CU800 Residential row (`spirit-bike-900-warranty-commercial-periods`).

## The CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 cover a dues-paying gym and charge for it

They are the only two Spirit bike manuals in this knowledge base that give a dues-paying facility its own row, and it is the worst row: frame 10 Years against Lifetime, parts 3 Years against 5. Labor moves the other way - 3 Years against 2 (`spirit-bike-2024-warranty-coverage-periods`). Their exclusion 2 still narrows reimbursement to "ordinary household or light commercial use", which is not what a dues-paying gym is; the manuals do not reconcile that either.

## The CU800ENT 2024 answers neither way

Its table has a Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) row and a Residential row and **no dues-paying row**, and its exclusion list carries **no dues-paying item** - the CR800-style item 8 is gone. Its exclusion 2, which is where the other manuals put the narrowing, **breaks off mid-sentence on the printed page** - confirmed by rendering that page at 300 dpi - and never states a use restriction (`spirit-ce-2025-warranty-exclusions`). So the manual neither covers nor excludes a health club in writing. Tell a customer the manual is silent rather than inferring an exclusion from a sibling.

## Do not carry a treadmill or elliptical answer across

The CT800 and CT850 manuals exclude a dues-paying gym (`spirit-ct800-warranty-use-environment`); the elliptical range moves from excluded to covered across ten manuals (`spirit-ce-warranty-use-environment`); the CTSBS900 covers **only** a dues-paying facility. Those are different machines with different tables, and a bike question has three possible answers of its own.
