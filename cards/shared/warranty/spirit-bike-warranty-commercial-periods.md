| Warranty | Frame | Electronics / Brake | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | Lifetime | 5 Years | 5 Years | 2 Years |

**All four figures are the same in all six manuals.** What moves is the name of the second column, the row label, and the printed effective date:

| Manual | Second column heading | Row label | Effective date |
|---|---|---|---|
| CU800 2012 | Electronics | Commercial | March 1, 2012 |
| CR800 2021 | Electronics | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | November 8, 2021 |
| CU800 2021 | Electronics | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | November 12, 2021 |
| CU800ENT 2022 | EMS BRAKE | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | February 08, 2022 |
| CR800 2023 | Electronics | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | June 21, 2023 |
| CR800ENT 2023 | BRAKE | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | June 21, 2023 |

**The column heading changes but the figure does not.** A CR800 or CU800 manual calls it *Electronics*, the CU800ENT calls it *EMS BRAKE* and the CR800ENT calls it *BRAKE*. No manual prints both columns and none says whether *Electronics* and the brake column cover the same components, so answer "the second column is 5 years" rather than naming a part the manual in hand does not name.

**The labor figure is printed two ways and means one thing.** The CR800 2021, CU800 2021 and CR800 2023 print **2 Year**, singular; the CU800 2012, CU800ENT 2022 and CR800ENT 2023 print **2 Years**. Two years on all six. The singular *2 Year* was read off the rendered page of the CR800 2021 manual, not only out of the extracted text, and it is what the manual prints. The SERVICE paragraph dates it separately (`spirit-ct800-warranty-labor-period`).

**A recumbent and an upright of the same generation share this table.** The CR800 2021 and the CU800 2021 print the same four figures under the same headings, five weeks apart; so do the CU800ENT 2022 and the CR800ENT 2023. The row is not a CR or a CU fact.

**The CU800 2012 figures were read off the rendered page, not off its OCR.** That source is a Lexmark scan whose pages sit upside down in the PDF, so the extracted text carries damage the printed page does not - it renders the effective date as "March 11" in one pass and closes the spaces in "2Years" and "10Years". Rendered at 300 dpi and rotated, page 31 of `CU800_OM_)800343.pdf` prints **Effective March 1, 2012** and a clean **Commercial | Lifetime | 5 Years | 5 Years | 2 Years**.

**That book was typeset in 2013, and its warranty page still says 2012.** Its pages carry the internal revision stamp **CU800_20130729**, legible on the rendered page 5. The `cu800-2012` id takes its year from the warranty effective date, which is what governs a claim; the document around it is a year newer. Do not read the id as the year the manual was printed, and do not use it to date any other fact in that manual.

**"Commercial" here means a non-dues-paying facility on all six, including the CU800 2012 whose row label does not say so.** That manual's exclusion 8 bars a dues-paying facility (YMCA, Health Club, Etc.) in the same words the other five use: `spirit-bike-warranty-use-environment`.

**The two ENT bikes print two columns this row does not have** - Console Parts 3 Years and Console Labor 1 Year. Nothing above is wrong for a CU800ENT 2022 or a CR800ENT; it is incomplete. Read the console periods from `ct800ent-2022-warranty-console-periods` and never fold them into Parts 5 Years.

**These are not the other Spirit bike figures.** The CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900ENT and CU900ENT print one Commercial row reading Frame Lifetime, Brake 5 Years, Parts 3 Years, Labor 3 Years and no residential row (`spirit-bike-900-warranty-commercial-periods`). The CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 print three rows with a Wear Items column (`spirit-bike-2024-warranty-coverage-periods`), and the CU800ENT 2024 prints a 10-year frame and no general Labor column at all (`cu800ent-2024-warranty-coverage-periods`).

**Do not answer this from a Spirit treadmill or elliptical card.** The CT800 and CT850 tables read the same four figures but their second column is *Motor/Deck* (`spirit-ct800-warranty-commercial-periods`), and the CE800, CE800ENT and CE850 tables read the same four with an *Electronics* or *Brake* column (`spirit-ce-warranty-commercial-periods`). Those are different machines whose figures happen to agree.

The home row of the same table is `spirit-bike-warranty-residential-periods`. When the clock starts is in the paragraph above the table (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`).
