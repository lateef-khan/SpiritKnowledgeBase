**Labor is covered for 2 years**, in both rows of the table - commercial and residential - and in all four CT800 manuals and all four CT850 ones. Six of the eight print it as "2 Year" and the CT800 2012, CT800ENT and CT850ENT as "2 Years"; the period is two years in every one.

**Two years is a CT800 and CT850 figure. Do not answer it from an XT card.** The Spirit XT residential and light commercial tables read **Labor 1 Year**, and so does the single commercial row of the 2026 4.0T, 7.0T and 8.0T - see `xt-2023-warranty-labor-period`. The CT900 and CT900ENT read 3 Years. Only the CT800 and CT850 ranges read two.

**The ENT manuals print a second labor figure, and it is not this one.** The Console Labor column reads **1 Year** in both rows of the CT800ENT and the CT850ENT. A console repair on either is covered for one year, not two - `ct800ent-2022-warranty-console-periods`.

What sets the date is stated in a different place, the SERVICE paragraph, word for word the same in all eight manuals:

> Keep your bill of sale. Twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale or eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number establishes the labor warranty period should service be required.

**That sentence does not agree with the table it is meant to date.** It names twelve months and eighteen months on a machine whose Labor column reads two years, and it does not say which of the two dates governs when they disagree. None of the eight manuals reconciles the paragraph with the table. Quote both to a customer rather than picking one.

The manual's own advice follows from it: keep the bill of sale, and if service is performed, obtain and keep all receipts. It adds that this written warranty gives specific legal rights and that other rights vary from state to state.

**This fact is printed in two places.** The two-year figure is in the table on the first warranty page; the dating rule is in the SERVICE paragraph two pages later. The tables are `spirit-ct800-warranty-commercial-periods` and `spirit-ct800-warranty-residential-periods`; the rest of the SERVICE paragraph is `xt-2023-warranty-service-steps`.
