**Treadmill warranty, effective January 1, 2008**, in two columns:

| Component | **Home use** | **Light commercial** |
|---|---|---|
| **Drive Motor** | **Lifetime** | **5 years** |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** | **Lifetime** |
| **All Other Components** | **5 Years** | **2 years** |
| **Labor** | **2 Years** | **1 year** |
| **Deck Wear** | **Lifetime** | **---------** (no commercial figure printed) |

The book otherwise reads household-only (responsibilities header, no commercial exclusion item), so the two columns are the only commercial terms it gives; carded as printed.

When the clock starts is separate; see `sole-treadmill-warranty-start-date-receipt-or-18-months-shipping`. What is not covered is on `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-7-item-2004-2010`.
