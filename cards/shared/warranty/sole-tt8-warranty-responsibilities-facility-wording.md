This warranty applies to products in **ordinary household use or in a non-dues paying, light commercial facility with 5 hours treadmill use or less per day**. The consumer or light commercial facility is responsible for the same seven duties as `sole-treadmill-warranty-responsibilities-7-item-household`, except:

- Item 4 reads defects in **house or facility wiring**.
- The duties bind the **consumer or light commercial facility**.

Otherwise word-for-word: 10-day registration card, proper use including belt tracking, proper installation, accessibility expenses, finish damage, routine maintenance. Effective January 1, 2011 (ATT8, TT8C) and August 1, 2012 (TT8-2013).
