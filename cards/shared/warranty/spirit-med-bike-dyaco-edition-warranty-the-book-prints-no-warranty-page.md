**None of the three books prints one.** No warranty page, no coverage table, no period, no exclusion list, no registration card, no return-authorization procedure and no serial-number location. The PT 4.0 R and 4.0 U user manuals end at Maintenance; the MED 7.0R ends at Specifications and a page of packaging and labelling symbols.

## How the absence was checked

A loose search for the **word** `warrant` returns two hits in each PT book and three in the MED book, and none is a term:

| Where | What it says |
|---|---|
| Introduction, PDF p. 2, all three | *"It is backed by one of the most comprehensive **warranties** in the industry."* |
| Introduction, PDF p. 2, all three | *"Your **warranty** and service needs will be addressed either through your regional sales representative or our highly trained service technicians."* |
| Important electrical information, printed p. 15, MED 7.0R only | supply voltage more than 10% outside 100-240 V may affect performance, and *"Such conditions are not covered under your **warranty**."* |

`guarant`, `registrat`, `return authoriz` and `serial` return **zero hits** in all three, in the native text and the OCR supplements alike.

**The introduction promises a warranty and the book never states it.** The MED-badged rehabilitation steppers do the same (`spirit-med-stepper-warranty-the-book-prints-no-warranty-page`).

## Where the terms actually are

The 2025 Spirit editions of the same machines carry a full warranty chapter - a commercial-only table of Parts 5 Years and Labor 1 Year, effective October 3, 2025 for the 4.0R and 4.0U and December 23, 2025 for the 7.0R (`spirit-med-bike-warranty-commercial-parts-five-years-labor-one-year`). **Do not quote those terms as the Dyaco book's**: a Dyaco-badged machine may have been sold through a different distributor under different terms, and the manual is silent. Say the manual prints none and refer the customer to their selling dealer or to Spirit Fitness.
