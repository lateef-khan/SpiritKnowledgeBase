Keep the bill of sale. Service under this warranty must be obtained in order:

1. Contact the selling authorized SOLE dealer. OR
2. Submit all service requests — serial number, contact information, brief problem description — online at **http://www.soletreadmills.com/serviceparts.html** (some books print https or a trailing "treadmills.com" line break; the host and path are the same).
3. Warranty questions: **1-866-780-SOLE (7653)**. Technical problems: **866-MYSOLE1 (697-6531)**. In the **E20-2020** book the no-local-service paragraph ("If no local service is available, SOLE will repair or replace the parts...") is merged into this step 3 rather than standing as its own step 4 — carded as printed.
4. With no local service, SOLE repairs or replaces parts at its option within the warranty period at no charge for parts; the owner pays all transportation both ways and never ships without a **return authorization number**.
5. By mail: **SOLE Fitness, P.O. Box 2037, Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037**.
