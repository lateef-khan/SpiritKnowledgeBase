| Warranty | Frame | Brake | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | Lifetime | Lifetime | 10 Years | 1 Year |

**The row is word for word the same in thirteen manuals over seven and a half years**, from January 2016 to August 2023. Only the effective date, the machine's noun and the heading above the table move:

| Manual | Heading above the table | Effective date |
|---|---|---|
| XBR95 2016 | Semi - Recumbent Bike Warranty | January 25, 2016 |
| XBR95 2018 | Semi - Recumbent Bike Warranty | August 22, 2018 |
| XBR55 2019 | **Recumbent** Bike Warranty | February 4, 2019 |
| XBU55 2019 | Upright Bike Warranty | February **04**, 2019 |
| XBR55 2021 | Semi - Recumbent Bike Warranty | February 4, 2021 |
| XBU55 2021 | Upright Bike Warranty | February 4, 2021 |
| XBR95 2021 | Semi - Recumbent Bike Warranty | March 16, 2021 |
| XBR55ENT 2021 | Recumbent Bike Warranty | November 30, 2021 |
| XBU55ENT 2021 | Upright Bike Warranty | November 30, 2021 |
| XBR25 2023 | XBR25 Recumbent Bike LIMITED WARRANTY | July 25, 2023 |
| XBR55 2023 | XBR55 Recumbent Bike LIMITED WARRANTY | August **07**, 2023 |
| XBR95 2023 | XBR95 Recumbent Bike LIMITED WARRANTY | August 07, 2023 |
| XBU55 2023 | XBU55 Upright Bike LIMITED WARRANTY | August 07, 2023 |

**The same machine is called three different things.** The XBR55 is a *Recumbent Bike* in 2019, a *Semi - Recumbent Bike* in 2021 and a *Recumbent Bike* again in 2023. Nothing about the periods changes with the noun.

**Two manuals print "1Year" with no space** - the XBU55 2019 and the XBU55 2021, in this row only. It is one year on all thirteen; the closed space is a typesetting slip, not a shorter period.

**The position of this row is not fixed.** Where a Light Commercial row is printed as well, the XBR95 2016, 2018 and 2021 manuals put that row **above** this one and the XBU55 2019 and 2021 manuals put it **below**; the two 2023 manuals that carry one print it as a **separate table** underneath. Read the row label, not the position.

## The XBR25 2019 is not on this card

Its table reads **Parts 5 Years**, half of this, and a shared card would state the wrong number for it: `xbr25-2019-warranty-residential-five-year-parts`. **Its own successor undoes the cut** - the XBR25 2023 prints the full 10 Years, so the parts period on an XBR25 depends entirely on which of its two manuals came with the machine.

## The two ENT bikes print a fifth column this row does not have

Between Parts and Labor the XBR55ENT and XBU55ENT add **Console 2 Years**. Their Frame, Brake, Parts and Labor figures are exactly the four above, so nothing here is wrong for an ENT bike - it is incomplete. Read the console period from `spirit-xt-ent-warranty-console-screen-period` and never fold it into Parts 10 Years.

## Seven of the thirteen print a second row as well

The **XBR95** in 2016, 2018, 2021 and 2023, and the **XBU55** in 2019, 2021 and 2023, add a Light Commercial row with a 5-year brake and 3-year parts: `spirit-xb-warranty-light-commercial-periods`. The XBR55, the XBR25 and both ENT bikes print this row and nothing else, in every printing.

## These are not the other Spirit bike figures

**The commercial CR800 and CU800 range reads differently in two of the four columns** - second column 10 Years rather than Lifetime, Labor **2 Years** rather than 1 (`spirit-bike-warranty-residential-periods`). The **XIC600** indoor cycle prints Frame Lifetime, Parts **3 Years**, Labor 1 Year and no brake column at all (`spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-residential-only-periods`), and the **AB900 and AB950** air bikes give a **5 Year** frame (`spirit-air-bike-warranty-periods`). Answering an XB question from any of those is wrong on at least one column.

**The Spirit XE and XG home ellipticals print these four figures exactly, and they are still a different fact.** Their residential row also reads Frame Lifetime, Brake Lifetime, Parts 10 Years, Labor 1 Year (`spirit-xe-warranty-residential-periods-ten-year-parts`). Same brand, different product line; the agreement is a coincidence of the terms, not evidence that a bike part and an elliptical part are covered alike. Answer a bike from this card and an elliptical from that one.

Two things this table does not tell you on its own. When the clock starts is in the paragraph above it (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`), and the one-year labor figure is dated separately in the SERVICE paragraph (`xt-2023-warranty-labor-period`).
