**Twelve months, whichever way the clock is set.**

| Manuals | Twelve months from | Or eighteen months from |
|---|---|---|
| All CSD, CSS, CSF and CSI books (36) | the date of **installation** | the date of factory shipping, as determined by the serial number |
| ST800DR3, ST800FI, ST800FT (3) | the date on the **bill of sale** | the date of factory shipping, as determined by the serial number |

The three ST800 books open the paragraph with an instruction the other thirty-six do not
print: **"Keep your bill of sale."**

**The labour figure is not always the same as the table's.** On the thirty-six current
books *Labor* also appears inside the one-year parts column of the coverage table, and
the two agree at one year. On the three ST800 books the table has no labour entry at all,
so this sentence is the only place a labour term is stated for a bench or rack.

**The CSI generation is the exception that states it twice and differently.** Its table
carries a *1 Year - Labor* column covering the console and resistance module only, while
this sentence sets a twelve-month labour period generally:
`spirit-strength-csi-warranty-six-column-periods`. Read both.

All thirty-nine add the same advice: if service is performed, obtain and keep all
receipts.
