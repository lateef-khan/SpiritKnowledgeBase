**Two rows, and a dues-paying gym has neither.** Every one of these tables is headed *Commercial Limited Warranty* and prints exactly two rows:

> Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities)
> Residential

So the warranty covers a **facility that charges no dues** - a corporate gym, a hotel, a fire station, an apartment building - and a **home**. **A health club, a YMCA or any other dues-paying facility has no row of periods at all.**

**Nothing in these manuals excludes a dues-paying facility in as many words.** Their nine-item exclusion list has no dues-paying item, where the CT800, CT850, CE800 and CU800 manuals that came before them exclude one outright at item 9 of a ten-item list (`spirit-ct800-warranty-exclusions`). So the 2024 books state no period for a health club and no exclusion of one, and never say which they mean. **Tell a customer the table gives them no row rather than telling them they are excluded** - and read exclusion 2 to them as well, which refuses service call reimbursement for "units not in ordinary household or light commercial use".

**On six of the fourteen manuals that sentence is not there to read.** Exclusion 2 breaks off mid-sentence on the printed page of the CT800ENT, CT850ENT, CE800ENT, CR800ENT, CRS800S and CS800, ending at "...or units" - so those six state no use restriction anywhere outside the table's row labels (`spirit-ce-2025-warranty-exclusions`).

**Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded, twice**, in a footnote under the table and again as exclusion 8. That is the only environment any of these manuals excludes by name: `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.

**No manual here caps daily use.** The five-hour-a-day limit the CE850 and XE manuals put on a non-dues-paying facility is printed in none of the fourteen.

**The CSC900 stair climber has no Residential row.** Its table gives one row, Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities), so that manual states no home warranty at all - `spirit-2024-stair-climber-warranty-coverage-periods`.

**The page contradicts itself and none of the fourteen resolves it.** Each is headed **WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL**, warrants "all its **home use**" parts in the paragraph above the table, says the warranty is administered "directly to a **consumer**", and then prints a duty list headed **RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE FACILITY** that never addresses a home owner (`spirit-ce-2025-warranty-facility-responsibilities`). Quote the row labels, which are the only part of the page that states a use environment consistently.

**Do not answer an earlier machine of the same name from this card.** The CT800, CT850, CE800, CE850, CR800 and CU800 manuals before 2024 exclude a dues-paying facility outright (`spirit-ct800-warranty-use-environment`, `spirit-ce-warranty-use-environment`, `spirit-bike-warranty-use-environment`); the CE900 2025, CES880, CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 go the other way and give a dues-paying facility its own row.
