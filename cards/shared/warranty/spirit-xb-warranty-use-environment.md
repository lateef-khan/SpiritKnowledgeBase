**Three different answers, and seven of the fourteen manuals contradict themselves.** Read the table and the exclusion list off the manual that came with the machine.

| Manuals | Home | Office, hotel, apartment or fire-station gym (non-dues paying) | Health club or YMCA |
|---|---|---|---|
| XBR25 2019; XBR55 2019, 2021; XBR55ENT and XBU55ENT 2021 | Covered - Residential row | **Excluded** by exclusion 7, and no row of periods exists | Excluded by exclusion 7 |
| XBR95 2016, 2018, 2021; XBU55 2019, 2021 | Covered - Residential row | **The manual says both.** A Light Commercial row of periods, and an exclusion 7 that bars it | Excluded by exclusion 7 |
| XBR25 2023; XBR55 2023 | Covered - Residential row | **The manual says both.** Exclusion 8 allows it at 5 hours a day, and no row of periods exists | Not addressed |
| XBR95 2023; XBU55 2023 | Covered - Residential row | Covered - Light Commercial table, capped at 5 hours a day by exclusion 8 | Not addressed |

**No XB bike manual mentions a dues-paying facility, a YMCA or a health club anywhere.** Unlike the CR800 and CU800 range, which excludes one by name, these fourteen never use the phrase. Before 2023 a health club is out because *every* non-residential setting is out; from 2023 the manual simply does not say.

**Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded on four of the fourteen** - the two ENT bikes and the two 2023 machines that print a Light Commercial table - and are not mentioned at all on the other ten (`spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`).

## The contradiction in the five older XBR95 and XBU55 manuals

Those five print a **Light Commercial** row of periods - Frame Lifetime, Brake 5 Years, Parts 3 Years, Labor 1 Year (`spirit-xb-warranty-light-commercial-periods`) - and then, two pages later, exclusion **7**:

> Product use in any environment other than a **residential setting**.

And their exclusion **2** refuses service-call reimbursement for a bike "not in ordinary **household** use" - the same narrowing again, with no light-commercial clause.

**The lead sentence of the responsibilities list splits these five in two.** The three XBR95 manuals read "products in ordinary household **or light commercial** use"; the two XBU55 manuals read "products in ordinary household use" only, while printing the same Light Commercial row. So an XBU55 2019 or 2021 grants light commercial periods in its table and never once admits light commercial use in its prose.

**None of the five reconciles the two.** Say both to a customer: the table names periods for a light commercial installation, and the exclusion list withdraws the cover the table grants.

## The contradiction in the two 2023 XBR25 and XBR55 manuals

It runs the other way. Those two print **no Light Commercial table**, yet their exclusion 2 reads "not in ordinary household **or light commercial** use" and their exclusion **8** reads:

> Product use in any environment other than a residential setting **or non-dues paying facility with 5 hours use or less per day**.

So the exclusion list permits an environment for which the table states no periods at all. A customer with an XBR55 2023 in a small office gym is inside exclusion 8 and outside every row of the table at the same time.

## The three manuals that agree with themselves

The **XBR25 2019**, the **XBR55 2019** and the **XBR55 2021** print one Residential row, an "ordinary household use" lead sentence, an exclusion 2 that says household use, and an exclusion 7 that bars everything else. On those three the answer is simply: a home, and nowhere else.

**The two ENT bikes agree with themselves too**, and they are the strictest of the fourteen - a Residential row with a Console column, a household-use lead sentence, exclusion 7 barring any other environment, and a prisons footnote on top.

## Do not carry another Spirit bike's answer across

The **CR800, CU800 and 900-series** commercial bikes cover a non-dues-paying facility on a row of their own and exclude a dues-paying gym by name (`spirit-bike-warranty-use-environment`); the **XIC600, CIC800, CIC850, CB900, AB900, AB950 and JB950** split four further ways (`spirit-indoor-cycle-air-bike-warranty-use-environment`). Those are different tables with different exclusion lists.

The lists themselves are `spirit-xb-warranty-exclusions` for the ten manuals before 2023 and `spirit-xb-2023-warranty-exclusions` for the four from 2023.
