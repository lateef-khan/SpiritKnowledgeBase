Keep the bill of sale. Service under this warranty must be obtained in order:

1. Contact the selling authorized SOLE dealer. OR
2. Submit all service requests — serial number, contact information, brief problem description — online. The URL varies by book: the WE and AE books and the E98-2011 book print **www.soletreadmills.com/technical.php?p=service**; the E35/E95-2012-2015 book and the E98-2013 book print **www.soleellipticals.com/technical.php?p=service**; the E20/E25-2012-2015 book and the E55-2013-2014 book say "online" with **no URL at all**. Carded as printed per book.
3. Warranty questions: **1-866-780-SOLE (7653)**. Technical problems: **866-MYSOLE1 (697-6531)**.
4. With no local service, SOLE repairs or replaces parts at its option within the warranty period at no charge for parts; the owner pays all transportation both ways, packs adequately, claims freight damage with the carrier, and never ships without a **return authorization number**.
5. By mail: **SOLE Fitness, P.O. Box 2037, Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037**.

The treadmill twin prints the same five steps for treadmills: `sole-treadmill-warranty-service-online-request-steps`. The 2016+ books move the online step to a new URL (`sole-elliptical-warranty-service-dealer-online-serviceparts-2016-2020`); the 2023 books drop the mail step (`sole-elliptical-warranty-service-4-step-2023`).
