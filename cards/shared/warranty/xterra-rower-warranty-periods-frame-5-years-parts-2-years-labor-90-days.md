| Frame | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|
| 5 Years | 2 Years | 90 Days |

**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the Manufacturer's Limited Warranty page of the ERG500 magnetic rower (effective August 21, 2018) and the ERG600W water rower (effective May 11, 2021) - the same three-column row in both. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`); the SERVICE paragraph's *twelve (12) months* sentence does not match the 90-day labor figure and is boilerplate.

**Ninety days of labor** is the shortest labor term on any Xterra rower page that prints one at all: the ERG700 and ERG650W print a year (`erg700-2022-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-5-years-labor-1-year`, `sb600-2023-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`), the ERG160 and ERG220 *None* (`xterra-bike-warranty-periods-frame-1-year-parts-90-days-labor-none`, `xterra-warranty-periods-frame-1-year-parts-1-year-labor-none`), and the ERG180, ERG400, ERG550W and ERG800W no labor column (`erg180-2023-warranty-periods-frame-1-year-parts-90-days-no-labor-column`, `xterra-warranty-periods-frame-5-years-parts-1-year-no-labor-column`, `xterra-ergw-warranty-periods-frame-3-years-parts-1-year-no-labor-column`). Two years of parts is what the ERG650W and ERG750W also give (`sb600-2023-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`, `erg750w-2025-warranty-periods-frame-5-years-parts-2-years-labor-none`).

Both books print the older-layout page: five consumer duties starting with a registration card back in ten days (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-with-a-registration-card`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover`), the nine exclusions with store-display and unregistered products (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-store-display-and-unregistered-products`), six service steps with a trip charge (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-service-steps-dealer-service-organization-then-the-service-department`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-owner-pays-freight-parts-shipping-and-trip-charges`), residential use only (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-use-environment-residential-only-any-other-use-voids-it`). The ERG600W tank page adds two acts that void the warranty outright (`xterra-ergw-warranty-tank-chlorine-bleach-or-filling-past-the-mark-voids-it`).
