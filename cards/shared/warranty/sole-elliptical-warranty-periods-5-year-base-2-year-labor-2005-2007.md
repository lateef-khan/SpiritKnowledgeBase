**Elliptical warranty, effective October 15, 2005** (E35/E75/E95-2007 book) and **effective January 1, 2007** (VE35/VE95-2008 book) — same figures under both dates. The books warrant "all its elliptical parts for a period of **5 years**", then extend two rows. **No brake row and no cosmetic row are printed.**

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Labor** | **2 Years** |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **All Other Components** | **5 Years** |

As printed quirks, carded verbatim: the 2007 book's duty 3 requires "Proper installation by an authorized Sole service company" (the VE book and every later book say "in accordance with instructions provided with the elliptical"), and the 2007 book's exclusion 3 names "authorized **Spirit** service companies" (sic) where every other book says SOLE. The VE book's SERVICE paragraph uses 866-MYSOLE1 while the 2007 book uses (870) 935-1107; see `sole-elliptical-warranty-service-dealer-local-phone-2005-2008`.
