**Spirit Fitness's two-page "Cardio Warranty" sheet, Revision 03.07.2019, sorts the commercial
range into two tiers. Page one is the tier covered in *all facilities* - the sheet's own
heading - which includes a dues-paying health club; page two is the tier covered only in a
non-dues-paying facility (`spirit-commercial-warranty-sheet-2019-non-dues-paying-terms`).** The
sheet names machines without years and prints periods only; its four footnotes are
`spirit-commercial-warranty-sheet-2019-footnotes`.

## COMMERCIAL (ALL FACILITIES)*

| Machines | Frame | Second column | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CT900ENT, CT900 | Lifetime | Motor 10 Years | 3 Years | 3 Years |
| CE900ENT, CR900ENT, CU900ENT | Lifetime | EMS Brake 5 Years | 3 Years | 3 Years |
| CE900, CR900, CU900 | Lifetime | Generator 5 Years | 3 Years | 3 Years |
| CIC800 | **10 Years** | - | Parts / Console 3 Years | 1 Year, Wear Items*** 1 Year |
| AB900 | **5 Years** | - | Parts / Console 2 Years | 1 Year |

## COMMERCIAL/MEDICAL (ALL FACILITIES)*

| Machines | Frame | Second column | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MS350, MS300, MR100, MU100 | Lifetime | EMS Brake 5 Years | 3 years | 3 Years |
| MT200 | Lifetime | Motor 10 Years | 3 Years | 3 Years |

**The CIC800 is on both pages with two frame periods** - 10 Years here, 15 Years on the
non-dues-paying page - which is exactly the split its own manual prints, a shorter frame in a
dues-paying gym than in a hotel (`spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-three-row-periods`). It is the
only machine the sheet lists twice.

## Where the sheet and the machines' own manuals agree

The CT900ENT (`ct900ent-warranty-coverage-terms`), the CR900, CU900, CR900ENT and CU900ENT
(`spirit-bike-900-warranty-commercial-periods`), the CE900 (`ce900-2021-warranty-coverage-periods`),
the CIC800, the AB900 (`spirit-air-bike-warranty-periods`) and the MS300
(`ms300-2021-warranty-coverage-periods`) print these same figures in their owner's manuals. The
MS300 manual adds a Wear Items column at 6 months the sheet omits; the AB900 manual splits
"Parts / Console" into a Console column and a Parts column, both 2 Years.

## Where they do not - two findings

**The CT900.** The sheet gives the CT900 the CT900ENT's terms: Frame Lifetime, Motor 10 Years,
Parts and Labor 3 Years, in any facility. The CT900 owner's manual on file, effective October
01, 2024, prints three rows with a **10-year frame, a 5-year motor, a Deck column and Wear
Items at 6 months** (`ct900-warranty-coverage-terms`). Nothing reconciles them; a 2019 buyer
was shown this sheet, a 2024 buyer the manual. Quote the document the customer holds and say
the other exists.

**The MT200.** The sheet's MT200 row - Lifetime frame, 10-year motor, 3 years parts and labor -
is the table the **2022** MT200 manual prints (`mt200-2022-warranty-commercial-periods`), not
the one in the 2010 manual, whose warranty is effective July 15, 2013 and reads Frame Lifetime,
Mech. Parts 3 years, Electronics 3 years, Wear Items 1 year and **Labor 1 year** with no motor
column (`mt200-2010-warranty-coverage-periods`). So Spirit had moved the MT200 to the later
terms by March 2019, three years before the manual that prints them.

## Which model ids the names map to

The sheet prints no years. This card is tagged to the id of each name whose year is 2019 or
earlier, taking the latest such year, plus the year-less ids: CT900ENT `ct900ent`, CT900 `ct900`,
CE900ENT `ce900ent`, CU900ENT `cu900ent`, CR900 `cr900-2018`, CU900 `cu900-2018`, AB900
`ab900-2018`, MT200 `mt200-2010`. **Four names on this page have no id at all in this knowledge
base: MS350, MR100 and MU100 have no manual on file, and the MS300's only manual is the 2021
one (`ms300-2021`)** - as are the only manuals for the CR900ENT (`cr900ent-2021`), CE900
(`ce900-2021`) and CIC800 (`cic800-2021`). Those four later ids are not tagged here because a
2019 sheet cannot vouch for a 2021 printing, but every one of their manuals prints the same
figures, so the answer for them is the same.

**"All facilities" is the sheet's phrase and the manuals' is weaker.** The CR900, CU900 and
CE900 manuals print a bare "Commercial" row; only the two 900ENT bike manuals say "All
Facilities" in the row label. The sheet applies the phrase to every machine on this page.
