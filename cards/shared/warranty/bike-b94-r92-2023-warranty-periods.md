Effective **February 1, 2023**. Residential warranty; all service is performed in-home. The period runs from the date of retail sale as determined by the sales receipt, or, with no receipt, from eighteen (18) months after the original factory shipping date.

| Component | B94 | R92 |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | Lifetime | Lifetime |
| Parts/Electronics | 2 Years | 2 Years |
| Labor | 1 Year | 1 Year |

**Parts/Electronics is 2 Years here, not 3.** The 2013, 2016 and 2019 manuals print 3 Years for the same two models; the 2023 revision cut it to 2. Check which manual shipped with the machine before quoting a parts period.

SOLE's responsibilities include providing new or remanufactured parts, at SOLE's option, and technical support to independent dealers and servicing organizations. Where there is no dealer or service organization, SOLE administers the warranty directly to the consumer.
