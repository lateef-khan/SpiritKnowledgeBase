Exclusion 9, in every manual:

> Warranties outside of North America may vary. Please contact your local dealer for details.

The 2023 manuals extend it:

> ... **Shipping charges may apply to Alaska and Hawaii customers.**

| Manuals | Wording |
|---|---|
| 2011 to 2021 (b94-2011 to b94-2019, r92-2011 to r92-2019, lcb-2011 to lcb-2019, lcr-2011 to lcr-2019, all sb700, sb900-2014 to sb900-2022) | Outside North America may vary; contact your local dealer |
| 2023 (b94-2023, r92-2023, lcb-2023, lcr-2023, sb900-2023, sb1200-2023) | The same, plus shipping charges may apply to Alaska and Hawaii customers |

**The manuals never say what the warranty outside North America actually is** — only that it may differ, and that the local dealer is the one to ask. Do not quote the North American periods to a customer outside North America as though they applied.

Inside North America, the warranty is silent about Alaska and Hawaii before 2023; from 2023 it says shipping charges may apply there. The general rule elsewhere in the manual still holds: parts are supplied at no charge within the warranty period, but transportation costs are the owner's.
