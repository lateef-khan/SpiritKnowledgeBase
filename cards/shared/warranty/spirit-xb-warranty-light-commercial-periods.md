| Warranty | Frame | Brake | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light Commercial | Lifetime | **5 Years** | **3 Years** | 1 Year |

**Light commercial is shorter than a home on two of the four components.** The brake drops from Lifetime to 5 Years and parts from 10 Years to 3 Years. Frame stays Lifetime and labor stays 1 Year (`spirit-xb-warranty-residential-periods`).

## Only two of the six machines print this table, and one of them not at first

| Machine | Printings with a Light Commercial row | Printings without |
|---|---|---|
| **XBR95** | 2016, 2018, 2021, 2023 - **all four** | none |
| **XBU55** | 2019, 2021, 2023 - **all three** | none |
| **XBR55** | none | 2019, 2021, 2023 |
| **XBR25** | none | 2019, 2023 |
| **XBR55ENT / XBU55ENT** | none | 2021 |

**The XBR55 and the XBR25 have no light commercial terms at all**, in any printing. Their manuals warrant one Residential row and nothing else, so putting an XBR55 or an XBR25 in an office or a hotel gym is an environment the book states no periods for - and their exclusion list goes further and bars it outright before 2023 (`spirit-xb-warranty-use-environment`). Do not read this table to their owners.

**The absence was proved twice on each of the five non-ENT manuals that lack it** - a loose-word search of the whole document, and a 300 dpi render of the warranty-table page read with `tesseract --psm 4`, whose word count matched the native extraction to within two words. The row is not hiding in a flattened image.

## The figures never move, but the layout does three times

**The four figures are identical in all seven manuals over seven and a half years**, from January 2016 to August 2023. What changes:

- **Position.** The XBR95 2016, 2018 and 2021 manuals print this row **above** the Residential one; the XBU55 2019 and 2021 manuals print it **below**. Read the row label, not the position.
- **Its own table.** The two 2023 manuals lift it out of the shared table and print it as a **second table headed *Light Commercial Warranty\**** under one headed *Home Use Limited Warranty*. Same four figures.
- **The definition.** None of the seven defines *light commercial* in the row label. The XE795 elliptical's older manuals spell it out as "(5 Hours use or less in a non-dues paying facility)"; **no XB bike manual does**. On the four 2023 books the envelope is set only by exclusion 8 - "a residential setting or non-dues paying facility with 5 hours use or less per day" - and on the five older ones exclusion 7 contradicts the row outright.

**Spirit's Cardio Warranty sheet, Revision 03.07.2019, prints these same four figures for the XBR95** with the brake column headed
*Generator*, and supplies the definition no XB manual prints: its footnote reads "X Series
warranty covers up to 5 hours use or less in a non-dues paying facility". **The sheet does not
name the XBU55**, so the definition reaches it only by inference. See
`spirit-commercial-warranty-sheet-2019-non-dues-paying-terms`.

## Prisons

The footnote printed with the table in the **XBR95 2023** and the **XBU55 2023**:

> \*Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage

**The five older manuals carry no such footnote**, and no XB bike manual carries a prisons item in its numbered exclusion list. The words *prison* and *correctional* appear nowhere in the XBR95 2016, 2018 or 2021 or in the XBU55 2019 or 2021. Do not quote it to the owner of one: `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.

## These are not the Spirit commercial bike figures

A CR800, CU800, CU800ENT or CR800ENT commercial row reads Frame Lifetime, Electronics or Brake **5 Years**, Parts **5 Years** and Labor **2 Years** (`spirit-bike-warranty-commercial-periods`); the CR900 and CU900 read Parts 3 Years and Labor **3 Years** (`spirit-bike-900-warranty-commercial-periods`). Two columns differ in each case.

**The Spirit XE795 and XE895 home ellipticals print these four figures exactly**, and they are a different product line (`spirit-xe795-warranty-light-commercial-periods`). Answer a bike from this card.

The one-year labor figure is dated separately in the SERVICE paragraph (`xt-2023-warranty-labor-period`).
