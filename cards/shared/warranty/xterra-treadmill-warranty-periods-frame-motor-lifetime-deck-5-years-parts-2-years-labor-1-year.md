**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the warranty page of these 3 books, under the heading *Effective March 13, 2021 ... LIMITED WARRANTY*. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**Three books print these five figures.** The TR6.6 (2021) sets them as *Frame & Motor - Lifetime, Deck - 5 Years, Parts - 2 Years, Labor - 1 Year*; the TRX4500 and TRX5500 (2024) as a *Residential* row under five headings with Frame and Motor each Lifetime. The five-year deck is the longest deck cover in the Xterra range.

**The TRX3500, sold alongside the TRX4500 in one service manual, prints a shorter table** - deck 2 years, parts 2 years (`trx3500-2024-warranty-periods-frame-motor-lifetime-deck-2-years-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`). Answering a TRX3500 deck question from this card gives three years too many.
