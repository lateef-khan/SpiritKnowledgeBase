| Period | Components |
|---|---|
| 10 Years | **Frame** |
| 5 Years | Bushings / Bearings / Shocks / Pulleys / Guide Rods |
| 1 Year | Paint & Finishes / Cables / Grips / Pull Pin Components / Rails Wheels / Attachments / All other parts / **Labor** |
| 6 Months | Upholstery |

**The ten-year column is one word.** Every other Spirit strength manual reads
*Frame / Welds / Cams / Plates* in this cell. These five print *Frame* and nothing else,
so welds, cams and plates are not named at ten years and fall to the one-year
*All other parts* line by default. That is what the table says; the manual states no
separate term for them.

The other three columns match the CSS single-station table word for word.

Four of the five are CSF machines. The fifth is the **CSS-LEXT**, which sits among
eighteen CSS books that all print the longer ten-year cell:
`spirit-strength-css-warranty-periods-ten-year-frame-welds-cams-plates`. Its two CSF
siblings the CSF-HRAC and CSF-FUNT print the longer cell too, so neither family can be
read across as a whole - check the machine.
