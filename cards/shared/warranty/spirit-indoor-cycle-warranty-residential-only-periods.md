| Warranty | Frame | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | **Lifetime** | 3 Years | 1 Year |

**That is the whole table.** Both XIC600 manuals print one row, and it is the Residential one. There is **no Commercial row, no wear-items column and no console column** on either printing, so the manual states **no terms at all** for a machine in a studio, a gym or an apartment building - and exclusion 8 goes further and bars the environment outright (`spirit-indoor-cycle-air-bike-warranty-use-environment`).

**Do not fill the gap from a sibling manual.** The CIC800 and CIC850, which are Spirit indoor cycles printing the same Parts 3 Years and Labor 1 Year, warrant a dues-paying gym for ten years and a non-dues-paying facility for fifteen (`spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-three-row-periods`). Reading those rows to an XIC600 owner gives them commercial cover their book does not grant and their exclusion list denies.

## The 2018 and 2021 printings warrant the same three figures

The tables are identical. What separates the two printings on this page is one line **below** the table:

| Printing | Effective date | Prisons footnote under the table | Back-cover stamp |
|---|---|---|---|
| XIC600 2018 | August 22, 2018 | **absent** | Revision: 08.22.2018 |
| XIC600 2021 | November 30, 2021 | **present** | Revision: 11.30.2021 |

**The 2021 printing adds the prisons and correctional facilities exclusion; the 2018 printing has no such clause anywhere in the book** (`spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`). Neither printing's warranty periods move.

**Both back covers carry a revision stamp that matches the warranty effective date to the day** - 08.22.2018 and 11.30.2021 - so on this machine the two dates corroborate each other rather than disagreeing.

## The opening sentence names a different warrantor from the other six bikes of this wave

Both XIC600 manuals read **Spirit Fitness, Inc. (Spirit Fitness) warrants all its Indoor Cycle parts**, naming the corporate entity in the first clause. The CB900, CIC800, CIC850, AB900, AB950 and JB950 open with the bare **Spirit Fitness**. The clock the sentence sets is the same on all eight (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`), and the footer that names who actually makes the warranty disagrees with the opening clause on four of the eight (`xt-2023-warranty-geographic-coverage`).

## Three things void the console warranty on these two machines

Opening the sealed monitor, cleaning it with a caustic solution, or exposing it to more than incidental water each void the warranty in as many words - `spirit-indoor-cycle-warranty-console-voiding`. Neither table above warrants the console for any period, so the only thing either manual says about console cover is how to lose it.

**Labor is 1 Year here and the SERVICE paragraph dates it separately** from the bill of sale or the serial number (`xt-2023-warranty-labor-period`). When the clock starts is `xt-2023-warranty-start-date`.
