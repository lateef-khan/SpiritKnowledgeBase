| Frame | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Year | 1 Year | None |

**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the Manufacturer's Limited Warranty page of these six books - one air bike, two recumbent bikes and three ellipticals. The AIR350 prints the frame column as *1 Year*; the SB150, SB250, EU100, EU150 and FS150 print *1 Years*. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**Labor is None**: XTERRA supplies the part inside the year and the owner fits it or pays a technician. The SERVICE paragraph on the same page still says *twelve (12) months ... establishes the warranty period should service be required*; that sentence is the Dyaco boilerplate every Xterra book prints and it does not add a labor term the table withholds.

**The SB250 book is a 2024 printing of a 2019 warranty.** Its cover stamps `SB250_125313_OM_20240418` and its contents page `SB250_VER9_20240418`, but the warranty page still reads *Effective June 21, 2019* and the back cover *© 2019*. The 2024 date is the print run; the terms are the 2019 terms.
