| WARRANTY | FRAME | GENERATOR BRAKE | PARTS | LABOR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial | Lifetime | 5 Years | 3 Year | 3 Years |

**There is one row and no Residential row in either manual.** The CSC900 states no home warranty at all. Do not supply one from a sibling machine.

**The 2019 and 2021 tables carry the same five figures.** Measured on eight-word phrase shingles the two warranty sections agree **96.3%**; the difference is the effective date and the prisons footnote the 2021 book adds under the table.

**PARTS reads "3 Year", singular, in both manuals.** It is a typographical slip in the source, not a shorter period - the LABOR cell beside it reads "3 Years" for the same three years. Both books print it the same way, so it has stood since December 2019.

**Labor is 3 Years, which is unusual for a Spirit climber.** The XS895 gives 1 year, the CRS800S, CS800 and CVC800 give 2, and the 2025 steppers give 1. Read the machine's own row.

**The generator brake has its own column** because the CSC900 generates its own braking load. Five years, in the only row there is.

**This is not the 2024 CSC900 table.** The machine was reissued in September 2024 with a table that drops the frame from Lifetime to **10 Years**, merges parts and labor into one column at 3 Years, and adds a wear-items column at 6 months: `spirit-2024-stair-climber-warranty-coverage-periods`. The frame is the figure to be careful with - *Lifetime* on a 2019 or 2021 machine, ten years on a 2024 one.

**It is not the CSC880 table either.** The CSC880, sold alongside it from 2025, prints three rows including a dues-paying gym and a lifetime frame only at home: `csc880-2025-warranty-coverage-periods`.
