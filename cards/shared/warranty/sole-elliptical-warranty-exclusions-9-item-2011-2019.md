This warranty does not cover:

1. **Consequential, collateral or incidental damages** from any breach of written or implied warranty (some states disallow this exclusion).
2. **Service-call reimbursement** to the consumer, or to the dealer where no malfunction or defect is involved, the unit is beyond the warranty or service-call-reimbursement period, the elliptical needs no component replacement, or it is not in ordinary household use.
3. Damage from **non-authorized service**, non-SOLE parts, or external causes — corrosion, discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, acts of God.
4. Products with **removed or altered serial numbers**.
5. Products **sold, transferred, bartered or given** to a third party.
6. Products with **no warranty registration card on file** (SOLE may request proof of purchase).
7. All other warranties, **merchantability and fitness** disclaimers.
8. Use **outside a residential setting**.
9. Warranties **outside North America** vary — contact the local dealer.

Same nine items, same order, in every book listed. The SC200-2016, SC200-2017 and SC300-2017 climber books print the same nine items reading "climber" for "elliptical" — and in item 2 "fitness bike not requiring component replacement, or fitness bike not in ordinary household use" (sic, carded as printed). The treadmill twin reads "treadmill" where this card reads "elliptical" and is otherwise the same list: `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-9-item-residential`. The older seven-item list is `sole-elliptical-warranty-exclusions-7-item-2005-2010`.
