**Elliptical warranty on the Sole AE25-2011, effective January 1, 2008** as printed (a stale date: the book is a 2011 printing). The book covers the AE models but its two warranty columns are headed **E25** and **E35** as printed; the E25 column carries these figures and applies to the AE25-2011. Carded as printed.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Labor** | **1 Year** |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Brake** | **Lifetime** |
| **Cosmetic Items** | **90 Days** |
| **All Other Components** | **3 Years** |

Footnote as printed: "Cosmetic Items, including but not limited to the following: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals". The sibling E35 column (2-year labor, 5-year others) belongs to the AE35-2011 and lives on `sole-elliptical-warranty-periods-lifetime-frame-brake-5-year-parts-2-year-labor`.
