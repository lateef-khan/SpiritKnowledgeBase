**Treadmill warranty, effective January 1, 2007** (VF63-2007 book) and **effective January 1, 2008** (WF63-2009/2010 book) — same terms under both dates.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Drive Motor** | **Lifetime** |
| **Deck Wear** | **3 Years** |
| **All Other Components** | **3 Years** |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Labor** | **1 Year** |

**No cosmetic-items row is printed.** Do not confuse with `sole-treadmill-warranty-periods-lifetime-motor-3-year-deck-1-year-labor`, which adds a 90-day cosmetic row.

When the clock starts is separate; see `sole-treadmill-warranty-start-date-receipt-or-18-months-shipping`. What is not covered is on `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-7-item-2004-2010`.
