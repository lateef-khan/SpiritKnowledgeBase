**The periods in the warranty table run from the date of retail sale, as determined by a sales receipt.** In the absence of a sales receipt they run from **eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date**. That sentence opens the warranty page of all nineteen Xterra treadmill owner's manuals in this knowledge base, from the 2021 TR150 to the 2025 TR75H; only the effective date and the model name above it change.

**The SERVICE paragraph restates it with a different figure.** Under *SERVICE* every book says: *Keep your bill of sale. Twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale or eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number establishes the warranty period should service be required.* The seven older-layout books (TR150, TR200, TR260, TR300, TR6.4, TR6.6, TRX1000) say *establishes the warranty period*; the four 2024 TRX books and the eight Online-Support books say *establishes the **labor** warranty period*.

**Neither sentence agrees with the tables.** Twelve months is not the labor figure on any Xterra page - the tables print 90 days (TR150, TR200, TRX1000, TR260, TRX1400, WS200, WS300) or 1 year (all the rest), and parts run from 90 days to 2 years. The books do not reconcile the 12-month sentence with the table; it is Dyaco boilerplate carried unchanged from the Spirit manuals (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`), where the labor figure it describes is also absent. Read the periods from the table cards (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-motor-1-year-parts-and-labor-90-days`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-and-labor-90-days-no-motor-column`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-periods-frame-motor-lifetime-deck-parts-labor-1-year`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-periods-frame-motor-lifetime-deck-5-years-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`, `tr64-2024-warranty-periods-frame-motor-lifetime-deck-3-years-parts-and-labor-1-year`, `trx3500-2024-warranty-periods-frame-motor-lifetime-deck-2-years-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`, `tr65-2023-warranty-periods-frame-motor-lifetime-parts-and-labor-1-year`, `xterra-tr-warranty-periods-frame-motor-lifetime-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`, `xterra-tr-warranty-periods-frame-lifetime-parts-2-years-labor-1-year-no-motor-column`); read this card for how the clock is counted.

**What the 18-month rule does in practice.** The factory shipping date is read from the serial number. A machine bought a year after it shipped, with no receipt, has already used twelve of its eighteen months. The registration cards ask for the serial number for this reason (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-qr-code-and-support-tab-no-deadline`).
