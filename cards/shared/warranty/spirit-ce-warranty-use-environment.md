**There is no one answer for a Spirit elliptical. Read the manual that came with the machine.** Across ten manuals spanning 2012 to 2025 the same question has three different answers, and one of them reversed in 2024.

| Manual | Commercial row label | Dues-paying gym | Hours cap | Prisons |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CE800 2013 | Commercial | **Excluded**, exclusion 8 | none | not mentioned |
| CE800 2021 | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | **Excluded**, exclusion 8 | none | excluded |
| CE800 2023 | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | **Excluded**, exclusion 8 | none | excluded |
| CE800ENT | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | **Excluded**, exclusion 8 | none | excluded |
| CE850 2016 | Light Commercial (5 Hours use or less in a non-dues paying facility) | **Excluded**, exclusion 8 | **5 hours a day** | not mentioned |
| CE850 2020 | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | **Excluded**, exclusion 8 | **5 hours a day** | excluded |
| CE850 2022 | Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | **Excluded**, exclusion 8 | **5 hours a day** | excluded |
| CE900 2021 | Commercial | **not mentioned either way** | none | excluded |
| CE900 2025 | three rows, one of them dues-paying | **Covered**, own row of periods | none | excluded |
| CES880 2025 | three rows, one of them dues-paying | **Covered**, own row of periods | none | excluded |

## Seven manuals exclude a dues-paying gym in as many words

Exclusion 8 in the CE800 2021, CE800 2023 and CE800ENT manuals:

> Product used in a dues paying facility (YMCA, Health Club, Etc.)

**The CE800 2013 manual says it twice and never names the YMCA in the exclusion.** Its exclusion 8 reads only "Product used in a dues paying facility", and the examples are in the responsibilities lead sentence instead: the warranty "applies only to products in ordinary household or in commercial facilities (**excluding health clubs, YMCAs, community recreation centers**)". Community recreation centres are named in that manual and in no other of the ten.

**The three CE850 manuals exclude it and cap the hours as well.** Their exclusion 8 reads:

> Product use in any environment other than a residential setting or non-dues paying facility with **5 hours use or less per day**.

The CE850 2016 manual puts the cap in the table row label too. The CE850 2020 and 2022 manuals drop it from the label while keeping it in exclusion 8 - and keep "This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household or **Light Commercial use (see restrictions above)**" as the responsibilities lead sentence, pointing at a restriction their own table no longer prints. Neither manual reconciles the two. Quote exclusion 8, which is the only place the five hours survive.

## The CE900 2021 manual answers neither way

Its row label is plain **Commercial**, its exclusion list runs to eight items and **none of them is a dues-paying facility**. The only trace of the restriction is exclusion 2, which refuses service call reimbursement for equipment "not in **non-dues paying facility or household use**" - reimbursement wording carried over from the CE800 list, on a manual whose table does not qualify its Commercial row. So the manual neither grants nor refuses cover in a health club. Say that plainly rather than importing exclusion 8 from a CE800 manual, and rather than reading the CE900 2025 answer backwards onto it.

## In 2024 the CE900 and CES880 started covering it

Effective October 01, 2024 (CE900) and December 17, 2024 (CES880) the table has three rows and the first is **Commercial (Dues Paying Facilities)**, with its own periods - parts 3 Years and labor 3 Years against 5 and 2 for a non-dues-paying facility. Neither manual carries a dues-paying exclusion at all. A placement refused cover on a CE800 is warranted here, on the shorter parts row: `spirit-ce-2025-warranty-coverage-periods`.

## What none of the ten says

**None defines what makes a facility dues-paying.** The examples in the parenthesis - YMCA, Health Club - are all any of them gives, and the CE800 2013 adds community recreation centres. **None but the three CE850 manuals caps hours**, and none defines "light commercial" or "ordinary household". Do not carry the five-hour cap onto a CE800, a CE800ENT, a CE900 or a CES880; it is not printed in any of those manuals.

**Prisons and correctional facilities are outside every one of these envelopes that mentions them** - eight of the ten. The CE800 2013 and the CE850 2016 manuals state the exclusion **nowhere**, neither as a footnote nor as a numbered item, so do not quote it to the owner of one: `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.

**Do not answer a Spirit treadmill question from this card, or the reverse.** The CT800 and CT850 envelope is the same shape but is a different set of manuals (`spirit-ct800-warranty-use-environment`), and the CT900, CT900ENT and CTSBS900 each answer differently again.
