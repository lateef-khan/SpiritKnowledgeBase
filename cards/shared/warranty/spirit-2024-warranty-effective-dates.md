**The effective date is not the revision date.** Every one of these manuals dates itself twice - once on the warranty page, as *Effective &lt;date&gt;*, and once on the PRODUCT REGISTRATION page at the front, as **Version: 1.0** with a **Revision:** stamp. On five of the fourteen the two disagree, and on three of those the revision stamp is **later** than the warranty the book prints.

| Machine | Warranty effective | Revision stamp on p. 2 |
|---|---|---|
| CT800 | September 17, 2024 | 09.16.24 |
| CT850 | September 17, 2024 | 09.16.24 |
| CR800 | September 17, 2024 | 09.16.24 |
| CE800 | September 17, 2024 | **09.19.24** |
| CU800 | September 17, 2024 | **09.19.24** |
| CE850 | September 23, 2024 | **09.19.24** |
| CRW800 | September 26, 2024 | 09.26.24 |
| CRS800S | September 27, 2024 | 09.27.24 |
| CSC900 | September 27, 2024 | **09.25.24** |
| CS800 | September 30, 2024 | 09.30.24 |
| CT800ENT | October 07, 2024 | **10.04.24** |
| CT850ENT | October 07, 2024 | **10.04.24** |
| CE800ENT | October 08, 2024 | 10.08.24 |
| CR800ENT | October 08, 2024 | 10.08.24 |

**Every one is Version: 1.0**, so the version number distinguishes nothing between them. All fourteen carry **©2024 All Rights Reserved** on the back cover with the model's own name.

**The dates fall in two clusters.** The ten machines without a touchscreen console are effective **September 17-30, 2024**; the four ENT machines are effective **October 07-08, 2024**, three weeks later. The ENT books are the ones that split a console period out of the warranty table.

**The date to quote a customer is the Effective line, not the revision stamp.** The revision stamp dates the printing of the book; the Effective line dates the warranty terms inside it, and it is the line the terms themselves are written under. Where they disagree - a CE800 effective September 17 in a book revised September 19, or a CT800ENT effective October 07 in a book revised October 04 - **nothing in the manual explains the gap**, and neither date changes a single figure in the table.

**A filename year is not evidence of either.** These manuals are named for their export date - `CT800_OwnersManual_800825_20240916.pdf`, `CSC900_OwnersManual_900665_20240925.pdf` - which matches the revision stamp rather than the effective date.

When the warranty period itself starts is a different question: the clock runs from the date of retail sale, or eighteen months from factory shipping when there is no receipt - `xt-2023-warranty-start-date`.
