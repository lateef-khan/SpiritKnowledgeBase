Three rules. Breaking any of them voids the warranty.

1. The monitor system is a **sealed unit**. Open it only to fit batteries. Any opened unit voids the warranty.
2. Clean it by **lightly spraying an authorised cleaning or disinfectant solution on the monitor and wiping it dry with a soft towel**. Caustic cleaning solutions void the warranty.
3. The monitor is **not waterproof, only water resistant**. Excessive exposure to water voids the warranty.

The SB700 2019 console has its own version of these rules, and it names a different cleaning method.
