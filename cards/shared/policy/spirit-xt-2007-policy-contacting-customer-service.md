**Two numbers, and the manual is firm about which does what.**

| Purpose | Number |
|---|---|
| Parts orders | **Fax (870) 930-9013** - fax only |
| Customer service | **Call 800-258-4555** |

**Do not call to place a parts order.** Orders go by fax on a completed parts purchase order (`spirit-xt-2007-policy-parts-purchase-order-form`). The reason the page gives is that the same technicians who take orders are the ones who answer service calls: "If taking parts orders by telephone, technical service calls will go unanswered." Standard shipping is FedEx ground; any other method is at the dealer's expense.

**Calling from the store.** Dial 1 for the first available technician, or the extension of a particular one. **Have the serial number of the machine when you call.** The extensions printed in this 2008 book:

| Technician | Extension |
|---|---|
| Charles Wood | 626 |
| Terry Jordan | 627 |
| Kevin Thomas | 628 |
| Nathan Price | 629 |
| Carla Nichols | 631 |
| Travis Lawrence | 633 |

If the technician chosen is unavailable, leave one detailed message and stop: do not call another extension and do not leave multiple messages, which the page says duplicates effort and slows every other customer's answer.

**Calling from a customer's home.** Dial 0 for the operator, say you are a dealer at a customer's location, and the first available technician takes the call.

These are the numbers a 2008 dealer manual prints; the later owner's manuals send consumers to their dealer and to Spirit Fitness (`xt-2023-warranty-service-steps`), and no other card in this knowledge base carries a named-extension list. The dealer's own obligations - to set up, diagnose and service what it sells - are stated on the same page (`spirit-xt-2007-policy-dealer-responsible-for-setup-and-service`).
