The manual states it in two sentences:

> Proper installation and regular maintenance are required to ensure user's safety.
>
> **Maintenance is the sole responsibility of the owner.**

So the routine work in the maintenance schedule (pedal and screw checks before each use, wiping the frame daily, the flywheel and brake pad weekly) is the owner's job, not a service call.

**Only the indoor cycle manuals carry this sentence.** The B94, R92, B74, R72, LCB and LCR owner's manuals do not print it anywhere.
