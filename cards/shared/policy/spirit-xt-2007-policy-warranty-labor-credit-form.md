**Claims must be returned to Spirit within 7 days of repair, by fax to (870) 930-9013.** The form's address block is P.O. Box 2037, Jonesboro, Arkansas 72402, and its heading carries one tick box: *Labor Warranty - Spirit is responsible*.

**What the dealer fills in.** Date of Repair, Model#, Serial Number; Service Company and its account number; Owner's Name and Address; Selling Dealer, its account number, phone number with area code and Date Sold; a **Problem** box and a **Repair Done** box; then one line per part - **Quantity, Part Number, Part Description, WTY** - and a labour block of **Labor**, **Trip** and **Total Labor**.

**What is shaded Factory Use Only** and left blank: Test Results, Unit Price, Work Order and Invoice on each part line; Freight and Shipping; the Authorization No.; and the Warranty Administrator's signature line.

**Three conditions printed on the form itself:**

- Warranty credit will be issued to the **dealer account** - not paid to a service company.
- **All parts must be returned to the factory unless instructed otherwise.**
- Claims must be returned to Spirit Corp. within **7 days** of repair.

The 2008 dealer manual for the XBR25 and XBR55 recumbent bikes prints the identical form on its page 11, and the one for the XE100-XE500 ellipticals on its page 14. What the credit is worth - a one-time flat $80 completion fee - and the three grounds for refusing it are on `spirit-xt-2007-warranty-labor-reimbursement-80-dollar-flat-fee`. Which parts must go back and the 30-day window for them is on `spirit-xt-2007-warranty-parts-returns-and-30-day-billing`; the Trip line on this form does not change the policy page's rule that a diagnostic trip is not covered (`spirit-xt-2007-warranty-what-the-dealer-policy-does-not-cover`).
