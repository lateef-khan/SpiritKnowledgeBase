**The form is faxed to 870-930-9013** ("Fill in completely for processing"), and every order ships **F.O.B. Spirit Mfg., Jonesboro, Arkansas** - the dealer owns the goods, and the freight risk, from the loading dock.

**Fields on the form.** A header for Dealer Name, Account No., Street & No., City / State / Zip, Phone Number and Fax Number, with a matching **Ship to** block; then Purchase Order No., Dealer Buyer's Name and Date of Order; then the order lines - **Qty., Part No., Description, Unit Price, Amount** - with a Buyer Signature and Date at the foot. **Orders must be signed.**

**The serial number is what turns a line into a warranty claim.** The form states: "All parts ordered are considered stock orders unless a serial number is listed below each parts' description box. A serial number listed will qualify it for warranty evaluation." The Order Completion note repeats it: a part not accompanied by a serial number is assumed to be for stock. Spirit encourages dealers to stock parts and promises to "always be fair to all dealers on credit for parts" - but **reserves the right to subject parts returns to a 20% restocking fee** if it determines that a dealer has excessively ordered parts randomly to repair a problem.

**Shipping.** All orders go by ground delivery unless otherwise indicated, and the buyer pays all expedited charges. The Special Instructions box offers **2nd Day Air**, **Next Day Air** and **Saturday Delivery**, each with a line to initial - and an expedited request that is not initialled ships by ground.

The part numbers to write on the lines are the six-digit Spirit numbers in the same book's five parts lists (`xt175-2007-specs-parts-list` and its siblings). What happens to the part after it arrives - free under warranty against the serial number, 30-day terms otherwise, and which parts must come back - is on `spirit-xt-2007-warranty-parts-returns-and-30-day-billing`.
