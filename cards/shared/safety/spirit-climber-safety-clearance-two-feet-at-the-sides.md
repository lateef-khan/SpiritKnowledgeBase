> Ensure there is a minimum space on the sides of the 7.0S of **two feet** for proper operation, easy
> access and to prevent possible injuries to others standing or walking nearby. There should be a
> minimum of at least **one foot** of free space at the **front and rear** of the 7.0S.

| Requirement | Figure |
|---|---|
| Each side | **two feet** |
| Front | **one foot** |
| Rear | **one foot** |

- **The reason given for the sides is other people**, not the user - injuries to others standing or
  walking nearby.
- **The manual gives no metric conversion** and no floor specification beyond `a flat level surface`
  in item 2.

**The 8.5S and 8.5S-Fit are not the same.** They ask for two feet at the sides and one foot at the
front, but **three and a half feet at the rear**
(`spirit-climber-safety-clearance-three-and-a-half-feet-at-the-rear`). Never quote one foot behind an
8.5S.

**The stair climbers are different again**, in inches: 20 inches (0.5 m) each side and 48 inches
(1.25 m) behind (`csc900-2024-safety-clearance-around-the-machine`).

**The MS300 bullet names the wrong product twice**, calling the machine a bike and then a unit -
`minimum space on the sides of the bike ... at the front and rear of the unit`. The figures are the
7.0S figures and the machine is an MS300 (`ms300-2021-safety-packaging-symbols-and-template-defects`).
