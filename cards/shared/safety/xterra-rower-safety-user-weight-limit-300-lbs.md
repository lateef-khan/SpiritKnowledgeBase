**This is the 300 lb figure**, printed once in each book as the last Fitness Equipment Safety
Instructions bullet, in three spellings:

| Book | As printed |
|---|---|
| ERG180 (2023) | `User Weight Limit: 300 LB` |
| ERG550W (2023) | `User Weight Limit: 300 LB` |
| ERG600W (2021) | `User Weight Limit: 300 LBS` |
| ERG220 (2023) | `Weight Limit: 300 lbs` |

None of the four has a specification table. The ERG180 adds the new-layout boxed note `Please ensure
that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of your new
machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine.`

**The sibling figures differ**: the ERG160, the ERG180's 2020 predecessor, prints 250 lb
(`erg160-2020-safety-user-weight-limit-250-lbs`); the ERG650W and ERG750W water rowers print 350 lb
(`xterra-ergw-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lbs`); the ERG800W prints 330 lb
(`erg800w-2023-safety-user-weight-limit-330-lbs`). Read the figure from the book of the machine in
hand. The pages are on `xterra-rower-safety-instructions-list-free-space-and-tighten-bolts-with-11-fitness-bullets`
and `xterra-erg-safety-instructions-list-13-item-plug-in-list`. Several Xterra bikes and treadmills
print 300 too (`xterra-safety-user-weight-limit-300-lbs`, `xterra-treadmill-safety-user-weight-limit-300-lbs`).

| Figure | Printed on | Where |
|---|---|---|

| 300 lb | ERG180, ERG550W | `User Weight Limit: 300 LB`, the last Fitness Equipment bullet |
