- **Keep hands away from all moving parts.**
- **Never drop or insert any object into any openings.**
- **Do not attempt to use your fitness bike for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended.**

All 14 upright and recumbent owner's manuals print these three rules in the same words. The safety page closes with **"SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!"**
