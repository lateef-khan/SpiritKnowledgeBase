**Four consecutive items of the safety list, on all four books:**

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 16 | Do not drop the dumbbells freely to the ground as it will cause damage to the product. In some cases, dropping the dumbbell freely may potentially cause a personal injury or death. |
| 17 | Do not bump the product against any other object as it will damage the product and may potentially cause a personal injury. |
| 18 | Do not take the product apart or disassemble any parts. Tampering with the product may potentially cause a personal injury. |
| 19 | This product is very heavy. For the highest level of safety place the dumbbell trays and dumbbells on the floor. |

The consequence the book names for a drop is damage first and injury second; the warranty page
lists `abuse, misuse, accident` among the causes it does not cover, so a dropped dumbbell is a
customer cost. Item 19 is the only placement rule in the books and it is the floor, not a bench or a
shelf - **the dumbbell stand's own book prints the same item**, which is the dumbbells' rule reprinted
rather than an instruction to keep the stand off the floor; the stand carries the cradles on its
brackets by design (`xterra-strength-safety-no-load-limit-or-user-weight-limit-printed`).

The selector rule that goes with these is item 15
(`xterra-strength-safety-do-not-turn-the-selector-or-press-the-lock-when-lifted-from-the-tray`); the whole
list is `xterra-strength-safety-instructions-list-20-items`.
