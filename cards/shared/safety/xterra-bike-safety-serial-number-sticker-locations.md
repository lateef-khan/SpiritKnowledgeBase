**Five bikes have a stated serial number location; three from the owner's manual, two from a
service document.** The other twenty-five books leave a blank and never say
(`xterra-safety-serial-number-location-not-printed`).

| Bike | Where | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FB160 | `Users R- side on main frame front support tube` - a 16-digit number | November 2019 service document (a technician note) |
| FB180 | `on the frame` - a barcode with the number below it | owner's manual, Product Labels page |
| SB120 | `located at rear base frame`; `Serial Number on unit, no registration card with OM` | December 2019 service document (a technician note); the location sentence is a picture caption read from the render |
| SB240 | `on the frame, near the leveler` | owner's manual, Product Labels page |
| SB600 | `on the rear of the stabilizer, near the adaptor port` | owner's manual, Product Labels page |

The FB160's location is likely the same on the FB360, FB150, FB350 and FB180, which share the front
support tube and lock pin, and the SB120's on the UB120 - but no book says so; offer them as places
to look, not as what the manual says. The Product Labels page and its two treadmill-copied sentences
are on `xterra-bike-safety-product-labels-page`.

The Xterra treadmills put the sticker on the motor cover or by the power switch
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-serial-number-on-the-rear-of-the-motor-cover`,
`xterra-tr-safety-serial-number-on-the-frame-near-the-power-switch`); the Spirit indoor cycles have their
own card (`spirit-indoor-cycle-safety-serial-number-location`).
