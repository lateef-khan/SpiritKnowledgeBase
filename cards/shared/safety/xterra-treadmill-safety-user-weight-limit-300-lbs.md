**This is the 300 lb figure**, printed three ways in three books:

| Book | How it is printed |
|---|---|
| TR65 | `21. User Weight Limit: 300 lbs.` - item 21 of the SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| TR95H | `22. User Weight Limit: 300 lbs.` - item 22, because the Hiker list adds a tablet-and-laptop rule |
| TRX2500 | `User Weight Limit: 300 lbs.` - the last bullet of the Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions |

The figure is printed once per book; none of the three has a specification table with a user
capacity.

**The TRX2500 is the only 2024 TRX at 300 lbs** - the TRX3500, TRX4500 and TRX5500 are 350
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lbs`). The TR75H, the other Hiker treadmill, is 265
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-user-weight-limit-265-lbs`).

| Figure | Printed on | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 250 lbs | TR150 | `Product Weight Limit: 250 lbs.`, a bullet in the Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions |
| 265 lbs | TRX1400, WS200, WS300 | item 21 of the SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| 265 lb | TR75H | item 22 of the SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| 300 lbs | TR65, TRX2500 | item 21 (TR65) or the last Fitness Equipment bullet (TRX2500) |
| 300 lbs | TR95H | item 22 of the SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| 350 lbs | TR75, TR85, TRX3500, TRX4500, TRX5500 | item 21 (TR75, TR85) or the last Fitness Equipment bullet (TRX books) |
| none | TR200, TR300, TR6.6, TRX1000, TR260, TR6.4 | no weight limit anywhere in the book |
