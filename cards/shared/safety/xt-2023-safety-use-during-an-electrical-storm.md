> NEVER use your unit during an electrical storm. Surges may occur in your household power
> supply that could damage unit components. Unplug the unit during an electrical storm as a
> precaution.

- **Do not use it** during the storm.
- **Unplug it** - the manual asks for this as a precaution, not only after a surge.
- The risk named is **damage to unit components** from a surge in the household supply.

The built-in surge suppressors mentioned on the same page are described as helping avoid
nuisance breaker tripping (`xt-2023-safety-afci-gfci-nuisance-tripping`); the manual does not
offer them as storm protection.
