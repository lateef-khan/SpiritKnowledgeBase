> NEVER use your unit during an electrical storm. Surges may occur in your household power
> supply that could damage unit components. Unplug the unit during an electrical storm as a
> precaution.

- **Do not use it** during the storm.
- **Unplug it** - the manual asks for this as a precaution, not only after a surge.
- The risk named is **damage to unit components** from a surge in the household supply.

The built-in surge suppressors mentioned on the same page are described as helping avoid
nuisance breaker tripping (`xt-2023-safety-afci-gfci-nuisance-tripping`); the manual does not
offer them as storm protection.

**The five 2015 XT owner's manuals print the same three sentences**, with the word `treadmill`
where the 2023 manuals print `unit`. Do not use it during the storm, unplug it as a precaution,
and the risk named is damage to components from a surge in the household supply.

**The three 2026 Spirit treadmill owner's manuals - 4.0T, 7.0T and 8.0T - print the first two
sentences and drop the third.**

The 4.0T keeps the XT wording:

> Never use your treadmill during an electrical storm. Surges may occur in your household power
> supply that could damage treadmill components.

The 7.0T and 8.0T say **facility** where every other Spirit manual says household - these are
commercial-capable machines:

> Never use the 7.0T during an electrical storm. Surges may occur in your facility power supply
> that could damage the 7.0T's components.

**None of the three prints `Unplug the unit during an electrical storm as a precaution.`** Do not
use the storm rule to tell a 2026 owner their manual asks them to unplug; it asks only that they
do not use the machine. Their manuals also describe no surge suppressor.
