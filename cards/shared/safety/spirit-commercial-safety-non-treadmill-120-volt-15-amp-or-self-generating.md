**Two columns and no third: a machine on this sheet either plugs into a 120 volt, 15 amp circuit or generates its own power.**

- **Every ENT console plugs in**, and every medical machine (MR100, MU100, MS300, MS350) plugs in. The self-generating machines are the non-ENT CE900, CE800, XE795, CR900, CR800, XBR95, AB900, CU900 and CU800.
- **The CE850 is the one non-ENT elliptical that plugs in**, and every stepper and the rower plug in.
- **The plug, the receptacle and the four-units-per-circuit rule** for the 120 volt column are on the same page (`spirit-commercial-safety-non-treadmill-nema-5-15p-four-units-per-15-amp-circuit`). The sheet gives the self-generating machines no requirement at all.

## Where the sheet and the manuals disagree

- **CE800ENT and CE850: 15 amps here, 5 amps in the owner's manuals.** Both books ask for a 110-volt, **5-amp** outlet and a dedicated 5 amp breaker (`spirit-ce850-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`); the CE850-2016 service manual says 115 volt at 15 amps. Three figures for one socket; quote the document the customer holds.
- **CE900ENT and CU900ENT get their only figure from this sheet.** Their owner's manuals print no voltage and no amperage at all (`spirit-xe-safety-no-outlet-figure-printed`).
- **AB900: self-generating here, battery powered in its manual.** The AB900-2018 owner's manual runs the console on batteries and names no generator (`spirit-indoor-cycle-safety-no-mains-outlet-or-voltage`). What the two documents agree on is that the air bike needs no outlet.
- **XE795: self-generating here, and the owner's manual still prints a 110-volt, 15-amp outlet warning** on the same generator-powered machine (`spirit-xe-safety-outlet-110-volt-15-amp`, `spirit-ce-safety-generator-produces-ac-power`). The sheet agrees with the machine, not with the boilerplate.
- **CRW800: a plug-in rower on this sheet, a battery rower in the 2021 and 2024 books.** The 2019 sheet describes the CRW800-2016, whose service manual feeds the console through a power adapter and asks for a 120-volt, 15-amp outlet (`spirit-ct800-safety-outlet-120-volt-15-amp`). The CRW800-2021 and CRW800-2024 run on two C cells and a generator and have no cord (`crw800-2024-safety-console-runs-on-two-c-batteries`). Ask the year before answering.
- **CR800: the 2009 service manual prints no power statement at all** (`cr800-2009-safety-no-safety-instructions-printed`); this sheet is the only document that calls the CR800 self-generating, and the 2021 owner's manual of the later machine agrees (`spirit-bike-safety-no-mains-outlet-needed`).
- **CS800, XS895, CR900, CU900, CU800, CE800, XBR95** agree with their own books: 120 volt at 15 amps for the two steppers (`spirit-ct800-safety-outlet-120-volt-15-amp`, `spirit-xs895-safety-outlet-120-volt-15-amp`), no outlet at all for the five self-powered machines (`spirit-bike-safety-no-mains-outlet-needed`, `spirit-ce-safety-no-mains-outlet-needed`).
- **MS300: 120 volt here, 100 to 240 volt in the 2021 manual** (`spirit-climber-safety-outlet-100-to-240-volt-15-amp`). Both are 15 amps, and 120 lies inside the range; the manual belongs to a machine two years later than the sheet.
