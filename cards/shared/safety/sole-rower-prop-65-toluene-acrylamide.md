**WARNING: This product can expose you to chemicals including Toluene and Acrylamide which are known to the State of California to cause Cancer and birth defects or other reproductive harm. For more information, go to www.P65Warnings.ca.gov.**

Printed once, under the table of contents, in all three books. The stencil OCR reads the all-caps AND as UN (`UN Acrylamide`, `UN or other reproductive harm`); quoted here as `and` per the render-verified elliptical, climber and treadmill cards holding the identical sentence (see the linked card). The SR400 book prints no Proposition 65 notice at all.
