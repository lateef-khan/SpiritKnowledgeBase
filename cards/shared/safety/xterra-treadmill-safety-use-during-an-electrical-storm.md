**All nineteen manuals print the rule; twelve of them add a second sentence.**

The seven older-layout books (TR150, TR200, TR300, TR6.6, TRX1000, TR260, TR6.4), under IMPORTANT
OPERATION INSTRUCTIONS:

> NEVER use your treadmill during an electrical storm. Surges may occur in your household power supply
> that could damage treadmill components.

The 2024 TRX books and the new-layout books, in the operation bullets on their electrical page:

> NEVER use your unit during an electrical storm. Surges may occur in your household power supply that
> could damage unit components. **Unplug the unit during an electrical storm as a precaution.**

| Books | Rule |
|---|---|
| all 19 | never use it during an electrical storm - the risk named is surge damage to components, not to the user |
| TRX2500, TRX3500, TRX4500, TRX5500, TRX1400, TR65, TR75, TR85, TR75H, TR95H, WS200, WS300 | and unplug it for the duration |

The warranty exclusions of the same books list `inadequate power supply` and `acts of God` among
the external causes not covered, and the electrical page says a supply that varies by ten percent is
not a warranty matter either (`xterra-treadmill-safety-electrical-information-page`).

The Spirit XT-2023 and Sole F65-2026 books print the same sentence for their own machines
(`xt-2023-safety-use-during-an-electrical-storm`, `f65-2026-use-during-electrical-storm`).
