**All four manuals print the same panel**, on the right-hand half of their grounding page, under a
plan drawing marked `20in` on three sides and `6ft` at one end.

> After assembling your treadmill, you'll need to make sure you've installed it in a safe area.

| Requirement | Figure |
|---|---|
| Front and both sides | at least **20in** |
| Behind the treadmill | at least **6ft** |

- **The front is the motor end.** The manual says so: `The front of the unit is where the motor is
  located.`
- **Install your treadmill in an area where children and pets cannot access it.**
- **Always keep the area around your treadmill clear** of furniture, exercise equipment and other
  debris.
- **Do not install your treadmill on deeply padded, plush, or shag carpet.**

Both figures are printed as recommendations - `We recommend that you leave an area of at least...` -
and the manual gives no minimum it calls mandatory.

**The two ENT manuals give a second, smaller rear figure and never reconcile it.** Item 2 of their
safety list says `Provide a minimum of 1 meter clearance between the rear of the treadmill and any
fixed object` (`spirit-2024-safety-instructions-list-treadmill-ent`). 6ft is about 1.83 m. Quote
both figures and say which page each is on; the manual does not choose between them.

**The CT800-2024 and CT850-2024 print no 1-metre figure at all** - this panel is the only clearance
statement in those two books, and it replaces the `ASTM F2115-05 Specifications 6.1.2.11` bullet the
2012 to 2020 manuals carried in their safety list.

**Do not carry these figures back to an older CT800.** The CT800-2012 manual states no placement,
floor or clearance requirement at all
(`spirit-ct800-safety-no-placement-or-clearance-requirement`), and the ENT-2022 manual states the 1
metre rear clearance and nothing else (`ct800ent-2022-safety-placement-and-rear-clearance`).
