**All nineteen owner's manuals print these three warnings, in this order, at the top of the electrical
page** (new-layout books say `unit` for `treadmill`; the 2024 TRX books head the page *Important
Electrical Instructions*, the older books *Important Electrical Information*, the new layout
*ELECTRICAL SAFETY*).

> **WARNING!** Route the power cord away from any moving part of the treadmill including the elevation
> mechanism and transport wheels.

> NEVER remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power. If voltage varies by ten percent (10%)
> or more, the performance of your treadmill may be affected. Such conditions are not covered under
> your warranty. If you suspect the voltage is low, contact your local power company or a licensed
> electrician for proper testing.

> NEVER expose this treadmill to rain or moisture. This product is NOT designed for use outdoors, near
> a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment. The temperature specification is 40
> degrees C, and humidity is 95%, non-condensing (no water drops forming on surfaces).

| Warning | Rule |
|---|---|
| Power cord | keep it clear of the incline mechanism and the transport wheels |
| Covers | never off with AC power connected (`xterra-treadmill-safety-disconnect-power-before-cleaning-or-service`) |
| Supply voltage | a variation of 10% or more can affect performance, is **not a warranty matter**, and is for the power company or an electrician to test |
| Environment | 40 degrees C, 95% non-condensing; never outdoors, by a pool or spa (`xterra-treadmill-safety-temperature-40-c-humidity-95-percent`) |

The rest of the page is the AFCI/GFCI paragraph (`xterra-treadmill-safety-avoid-afci-gfci-breakers-tested-brands`)
and, on the older books, the grounding instructions; the new-layout and 2024 TRX books also carry the
operation warnings on this page (`xterra-treadmill-safety-use-during-an-electrical-storm`).

The same page is on the Spirit CT900 (`ct900-electrical-safety`); that card covers Spirit machines only.
