**The Proposition 65 sticker is on the interior side of the treadmill's upright**, the same
face of the upright as the safety warning sticker.

What the manual says about it, in full:

> The State of California requires us to inform you that this unit was manufactured using
> chemicals that could cause harm with improper use.

**That is all these five manuals say.** They name no chemical, print no
`www.P65Warnings.ca.gov` address, and give no exposure advice. The Spirit CTSBS900 manual
does print the web address; these do not, so do not quote it for an XT.
