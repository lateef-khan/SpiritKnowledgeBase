**The SB120 and UB120 print the indoor-cycle list with the flywheel items and the MAX-mark clause
removed and a 250 lb limit added.** The two books differ only in where the clearance sentence sits:
the SB120 gives it its own item 9, the UB120 folds it into item 8, so the SB120 list runs to 10 and
the UB120 list to 9.

> WARNING - Read all instructions before using this appliance.

| SB120 | UB120 | Instruction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Use this equipment only for its intended use as described in this manual. Do not attempt to ride this bike at high pedal speeds until you have ridden the bike for some time and are comfortable riding at slower pedal speeds. |
| 2 | 2 | Serious injury or death may occur from over-training. Consult a medical doctor or qualified fitness instructor to determine an exercise program appropriate for your level of fitness. |
| 3 | 3 | Do not attempt to turn the pedal cranks by hand. Do not touch any driving mechanism while it is in motion as possible injury could occur. |
| 4 | 4 | In a home setting, keep children away from the bike when it is not in use. Keep children and pets away from the unit while it is in use. |
| 5 | 5 | Do not attempt to perform dip movements on handlebars. |
| 6 | 6 | Never drop or insert any object into any opening of the bike. |
| 7 | 7 | Only use the bike on a stable, level floor. |
| 8 | 8 | Follow instructions for safe use of the equipment including proper seat position, handlebar position, and use of foot positioning system of pedals. |
| 9 | (in 8) | For safe operation, allow for at least 1 foot (30cm) of free space to either side of the unit and 2 feet (60cm) of free space to the rear of the unit. (`xterra-bike-safety-clearance-1-ft-sides-2-ft-rear`) |
| 10 | 9 | **Weight Limit: 250 lbs.** (`xterra-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-250-lbs`) |

Below the list: `Regularly examine the bike for damage and wear. Inoperable components should be
replaced immediately or the equipment should not be used until it is repaired.`, the `Failure to
follow all guidelines...` sentence, the Proposition 65 warning naming Toluene and Acrylamide
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-proposition-65-warning`) and `SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!`. The
facing page is the six owner guidelines (`xterra-bike-safety-operation-instructions-owner-guidelines`).

Neither machine has a freewheel warning because neither is a fixed-gear cycle. Neither prints the
MAX-mark clause either, although the UB120 has a seat post held by a club knob and the SB120 a
sliding seat on an adjustable bar; the books give no height limit for either. Both run a two-AAA
console with no adapter (`xterra-safety-battery-console-no-power-adapter`), which is why the appliance DANGER line and
cord items of the plug-in list are absent (`xterra-safety-instructions-list-13-item-plug-in-list`).
