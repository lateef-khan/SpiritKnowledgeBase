All three rower books say **RECORD YOUR SERIAL NUMBER** with a blank, ask for the dealer name, phone and purchase date, and want the self-addressed product registration card completed and mailed or registered online at www.soletreadmills.com.

No on-machine location is ever named on any registration or safety page - no decal, plate, frame or console position. Registration and warranty terms themselves belong to the warranty section. The SR400 book prints no serial record at all (see its own card), and the same fact for the elliptical line lives on the linked card.
