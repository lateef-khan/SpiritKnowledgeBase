The three 2006-family books (F63/UF63, F80 family, TT8/UTT8) print an older, stricter rule: **access to a 120-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet** - with no "only the treadmill on the circuit" clause - and **DO NOT USE AN EXTENSION CORD**, with no gauge allowance at all. **DO NOT ATTEMPT TO DISABLE THE GROUNDED PLUG BY USING IMPROPER ADAPTERS, OR IN ANY WAY MODIFY THE CORD SET.** A serious shock or fire hazard may result along with computer malfunctions.

Every later legacy book instead allows a 14 AWG-or-better cord; those figures have their own cards.
