**Both CE900 owner's manuals print the same eleven items in the same order.** The 2021 manual sets
them as bullets under a WARNING; the 2025 manual numbers them 1 to 11 under
`WARNING - To reduce the risk of injury to persons`.

1. Before beginning this or any exercise program, consult a physician. This is especially important
   for persons over the age of 35 or persons with pre-existing health conditions.
2. Keep children away from the elliptical trainer. There are obvious pinch points and other caution
   areas that can cause harm.
3. Keep hands away from all moving parts.
4. Never drop or insert any object into any openings.
5. Do not use outdoors.
6. Do not attempt to use your elliptical trainer for any purpose other than for the purpose it is
   intended.
7. The hand pulse sensors are not medical devices. Various factors, including the user's movement,
   may affect the accuracy of heart rate readings. The pulse sensors are intended only as exercise
   aids in determining heart rate trends in general.
8. Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable for use on your
   elliptical. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue.
9. This appliance is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental
   capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge, unless they have been given supervision or
   instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible for their safety.
10. Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine.
11. User weight limit: 450 lb.

The 2021 manual prints item 11 as `Maximum User Weight: 450 lb` and closes the list with
`SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!`; the 2025 manual prints it as `User Weight Limit: 450 lb.`
and drops the closing line. The figure is the same in both
(`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`).

**Only the 2025 manual prints the appliance preamble** - `When using an appliance, basic precautions
should always be followed, including the following: Read all instructions before using this
appliance` - and a boxed note beside the list:

> Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of
> your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine.

**This list is shorter than the CE800 and CE850 lists, and the missing items matter.** Neither CE900
manual prints the deeply-padded-carpet rule, the damaged-parts rule, the aerosol and oxygen rule, or
the hand-pulse sentences about a chest transmitter strap. Do not answer a CE900 owner from
`spirit-ce800-safety-instructions-list` or `spirit-ce850-safety-instructions-list`; those lists
belong to other machines.

**These two lists name no outlet, no circuit rating and no extension cord.** The CE900 runs off a
built-in generator and does not plug into an AC outlet
(`spirit-ce-safety-generator-produces-ac-power`).

The consult-a-physician item is the same age-35 rule Spirit prints across the range:
`40t-2026-safety-consult-a-physician`.
