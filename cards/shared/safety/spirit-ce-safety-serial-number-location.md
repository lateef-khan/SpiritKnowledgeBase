**Only the CE900-2021 manual says where it is.**

> Please record the serial number of this fitness product in the space provided below. The serial
> number is located on the front of the elliptical trainer.

That is the whole statement. It does not name a stabilizer, a mast or a cover, and it does not
mention a barcode.

**The other seven manuals print the same heading and no location at all.** Each says only
`Please record the serial number of this fitness product in the space provided below` above a blank
line. **None of the eight has a product labels page**, none describes a sticker's position in words,
and none says what the serial number looks like.

**One decal is nevertheless shown.** The CE900-2021 manual's console features page draws a bilingual
`Risk of personal injury - Keep children under the age of 13 away from machine` warning decal on the
machine (`ce900-2021-safety-console-warning-decal`). It is a drawing with no caption, so it says
nothing about where the serial number is.

**The CE850-2016 manual comes closest without saying anything.** Its page 7 carries an illustration
captioned `Serial Number Location` beside the operation instructions. The caption is the only text;
the manual never puts the location into words, so there is nothing to quote. Read the drawing on
the page rather than guessing from it.

**That is an absence, not a licence to guess.** Do not tell a CE800, CE800ENT or CE850 owner the
number is on the front stabilizer, the rear stabilizer or the console - those are the 2025 machines'
locations, and each is different:

| Machine | Serial number sticker |
|---|---|
| CE900-2025 | front stabilizer (`ce900-2025-safety-product-labels`) |
| CES880-2025 | rear stabilizer (`ces880-2025-safety-product-labels`) |

**Registering the warranty.** All eight manuals ask for the completed self-addressed registration
card to be returned to Spirit Fitness, and give a web alternative under the Support tab:

- CE800 2021 and 2023, CE800ENT, CE850 2020 and 2022, CE900 2021 - `http://www.spiritfitness.com/warranty`
- CE850 2016 - `https://www.spiritfitness.com/commercialwarrantyregistration.html`
- CE800 2013 - the residential registration page; the address is damaged in that manual's scan and
  is not quoted here.
