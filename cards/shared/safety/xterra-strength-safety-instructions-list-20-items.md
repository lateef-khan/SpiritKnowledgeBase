**All four 2022 strength books print the same twenty-item list under `WARNING - To reduce the risk
of injury to persons`**, followed by the Proposition 65 warning. The stand's book substitutes
*Dumbbell Stand* for *adjustable dumbbell* in items 9 and 20 and leaves the dumbbell wording in
items 15, 16 and 19 - so the stand's page tells the reader not to drop dumbbells and to keep the
trays on the floor, which is the dumbbells' rule reprinted, not a stand rule.

| # | Instruction |
|---|---|
| 1 | This exercise equipment is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge. |
| 2 | Use this appliance only for its intended use as described in this manual. Do not use attachments not recommended by the manufacturer. |
| 3 | Never drop or insert any object into any opening. |
| 4 | The equipment is intended for household use. |
| 5 | Before beginning this or any exercise program, consult a physician. This is especially important for persons over the age of 35 or persons with pre-existing health conditions. |
| 6 | Keep hands away from all moving parts. |
| 7 | Do not attempt to use your equipment for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended. |
| 8 | Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable for use on your equipment. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue. |
| 9 | Please use the adjustable dumbbells instructions step by step to avoid unnecessary damage to the product. (*the Dumbbell Stands instructions* on the stand) |
| 10 | In the event of nausea, shortness of breath, dizziness, headache, chest distress, or any discomfort, stop exercising immediately. |
| 11 | In order to achieve significant exercise results and not to overburden the muscles, the exercise frequency should be administered within a reasonable range. Consult a personal trainer to learn correct posture, technique, methods, and frequency. |
| 12 | Inspect the equipment thoroughly before each use. Do not use the product with damaged or work components or parts. *(printed "work" for "worn")* |
| 13 | If the equipment is found to be worn, loose, or rusted, it should be repaired or replaced in a timely manner to ensure that all components are always in good working condition. |
| 14 | Please keep pets and children away from equipment. Do not allow children to play near this equipment or use it for any purpose. **The minimum age of use for this equipment is 18 years old. Everyone under the age of 18 must be under parental supervision at all times.** (`xterra-strength-safety-minimum-age-18-and-children-and-pets-away`) |
| 15 | **Do not press the safety lock or turn the weight selector dial when the dumbbell or handle is lifted from the dumbbell tray.** (`xterra-strength-safety-do-not-turn-the-selector-or-press-the-lock-when-lifted-from-the-tray`) |
| 16 | Do not drop the dumbbells freely to the ground as it will cause damage to the product. In some cases, dropping the dumbbell freely may potentially cause a personal injury or death. |
| 17 | Do not bump the product against any other object as it will damage the product and may potentially cause a personal injury. |
| 18 | Do not take the product apart or disassemble any parts. Tampering with the product may potentially cause a personal injury. |
| 19 | This product is very heavy. For the highest level of safety place the dumbbell trays and dumbbells on the floor. (items 16-19: `xterra-strength-safety-never-drop-bump-or-disassemble-and-keep-the-trays-on-the-floor`) |
| 20 | Use caution while participating in other activities while using your adjustable dumbbell (*your Dumbbell Stand* on the stand) such as watching television, reading, etc. These distractions may cause you to lose balance, resulting in serious injury. (`xterra-safety-distractions-while-exercising`) |

Then `WARNING: This product can expose you to chemicals including Toluene and Acrylamide which are
known to the State of California to cause cancer and birth defects or other reproductive harm. For
more information go to www.P65Warnings.ca.gov` (`xterra-treadmill-safety-proposition-65-warning`), and
the page closes with `Failure to follow all guidelines may compromise the effectiveness of the
exercise experience, expose yourself (and possibly others) to injury, and reduce the longevity of the
equipment.`, `SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!` and `CAUTION!! Please be careful when
unpacking the carton.`

Items 1-8 are the Dyaco appliance bullets with every cord, blanket, air-opening and outdoor item
removed; items 9-20 are the strength list. **No weight figure of any kind is on the page** - no user
weight limit and, for the stand, no load limit
(`xterra-strength-safety-no-load-limit-or-user-weight-limit-printed`). The Spirit strength range prints a
nineteen-item list and a weight-stack pin rule for its own machines
(`spirit-strength-safety-instructions-nineteen-shared-items`, `spirit-strength-safety-weight-selector-pin-rules`).
