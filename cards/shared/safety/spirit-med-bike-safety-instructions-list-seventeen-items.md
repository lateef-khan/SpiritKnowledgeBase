**Seventeen numbered items, six more than the commercial CR900 and CU900 lists, and two of the six
say the machine is not a medical device - on a bike sold as Spirit Medical.**

> To reduce risk of injury to persons: Read all instructions before using this appliance.
>
> **WARNING** - Read all instructions before using this appliance.

| # | Instruction |
|---|---|
| 1 | Before beginning exercise on this product, or any exercise program, consult a physician. This is especially important for persons over the age of 35 or persons with preexisting health conditions. |
| 2 | Keep children away from the bike. There are obvious pinch points and other caution areas that can cause harm. |
| 3 | Children should be supervised to ensure that they do not play with the device. |
| 4 | Keep hands away from all moving parts. |
| 5 | Never drop or insert any object into any openings. |
| 6 | Do not use outdoors. |
| 7 | Do not operate this product on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and product may result. |
| 8 | Do not attempt to use this product for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended. |
| 9 | The hand pulse sensors are not medical devices. Various factors, including the user's movement, may affect the accuracy of heart rate readings. The pulse sensors are intended only as an exercise aids in determining heart rate trends in general. |
| 10 | Heart rate monitoring systems may be inaccurate. Over exercising may result in serious injury or death. If you feel faint stop exercising immediately. |
| 11 | Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable for use on your bike. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue. |
| 12 | This appliance can be used by children aged from 8 years and above and persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities or lack of experience and knowledge if they have been given supervision or instruction concerning use of the appliance in a safe way and understand the hazards involved. Children shall not play with the appliance. Cleaning and user maintenance shall not be made by children without supervision. |
| 13 | This appliance is not intended for use by persons (including children) with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge, unless they have been given supervision or instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible for their safety. |
| 14 | Notes on the correct posture and the fact the pedal crank training equipment of class B and C are not suitable for therapeutic purposes. |
| 15 | The bike is not a medical device. |
| 16 | Place the machine on a level surface. For ease of access, there should be an accessible space preferentially on both sides of the machine equal to 2 ft (0.6 meters) to allow a user access to the machine from either side. Do not place the machine in any area that will block any vent or air openings. The machine should not be located in a garage, covered patio, near water or outdoors. |
| 17 | **User Weight Limit: 440 lb.** |

Beside the list, on the second page, is the boxed note the 2024 and 2025 Spirit manuals all carry:

> Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements
> of your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine.

**Items 14 and 15 are the ones to quote when a clinic asks.** `The bike is not a medical device`,
and pedal-crank training equipment of class B and C `are not suitable for therapeutic purposes`,
are printed in a book whose registration page calls the maker `one of the world's leading medical
product manufactures`. The manual does not say which class the 4.0R or 4.0U is. The plug-in 7.0R,
7.0U, 8.0U and 8.5R print the opposite - a rehabilitation device with contraindications and an
intended patient population (`spirit-med-bike-safety-contraindications-osteoporosis-fractures-and-acute-conditions`).
The two families share a catalogue and not a safety page.

**Items 12 and 13 pull in opposite directions** - one admits a supervised child of 8 and a
supervised person of reduced capability, the next says the appliance is not intended for such
persons unless supervised. Both are printed; neither is withdrawn
(`spirit-med-bike-safety-children-aged-8-and-above-if-supervised`).

**The 4.0R text layer carries a paragraph the printed page does not.** Under the two clearance
drawings on printed page 5 the extraction reads `Maximum User Weight: 159 kg (350 lbs.)` followed by
a repeat of item 16. A 110 dpi render of that page shows only the two drawings, the boxed note and
`Save these instructions - think safety!` - the paragraph is ghost text left behind from the Dyaco PT
edition, whose bullet list ends with exactly those words
(`spirit-pt-bike-safety-instructions-list-dyaco-edition`). **The printed limit is 440 lb**, item 17,
set in bold (`spirit-med-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lb`). The 4.0U text layer has no such
paragraph.

**What the eleven-item commercial list does not have.** The CR900-2025 and CU900-2025 list
(`spirit-bike-2025-safety-instructions-list`) prints no item 3, 7, 10, 12, 14, 15 or 16 and ends at
450 lb, so the medical bikes add the child-supervision rule, the carpet rule, the over-exercise
warning, the age-8 clause, the two not-a-medical-device sentences and the placement paragraph, and
take 10 lb off the limit.

**Both books print the list in the same words.** The only difference between the 4.0R and 4.0U
pages is the WARNING lead - the 4.0R sets `Read all instructions before using this appliance` on its
own line under the WARNING, the 4.0U runs it on - and the figures in the two clearance drawings
on the second page, which are each machine's own
(`spirit-med-bike-safety-placement-and-clearance-two-feet-each-side`).
