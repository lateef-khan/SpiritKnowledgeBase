> **WARNING** - Never operate this rower without feet properly secured in foot straps, or without
> the sliding portion of the slider footplate locked into position.

**Two conditions, and both must hold before a stroke is taken.**

| Condition | What it means |
|---|---|
| Feet properly secured in foot straps | the straps over both feet are done up |
| Sliding portion of the slider footplate locked into position | the adjustable half of the footplate is latched, not free to slide |

**`Never` is the manual's own word**, printed in a box of its own rather than as a bullet of the
safeguards list. The manual gives no exception - not for a short stroke, not for a test pull, and
not for setting the machine up.

**Both water rower manuals print it identically.** It is one of the four boxed warnings beside their
SAFEGUARDS list (`spirit-water-rower-safety-safeguards-list`), and the only one of the four that is
not about the tank (`spirit-water-rower-safety-tank-warnings`).

**No Spirit air rower manual prints this warning.** The CRW800 and XRW600 books say only
`Wear proper shoes` and name no strap or footplate rule at all. That is an absence in those books,
not permission to row an air rower unstrapped.

**The related safeguard sits in the list above it**: `Follow instructions provided in this manual
for correct foot position and basic rowing techniques`. The manual's own foot-position instructions
are elsewhere in the book; this page states the rule and points at them.
