**The console face carries the age-13 warning in English and French.** The Display Windows page of
both service manuals reproduces the console; in the panel under the numeric keypad, between the RPM
window on the left and the LEVEL window on the right, it prints:

> **WARNING** Risk of personal injury - Keep children under the age of 13 away from machine.
>
> **AVERTISSEMENT** - Risque de blessures - gardez les enfants âgés de moins de 13 ans loin de
> l'appareil.

- **It is the same rule the safety list prints as a bullet** (`spirit-ce-safety-who-may-use-the-elliptical`)
  and adds nothing to it; the pinch-point rule beside it in the list attaches no age
  (`spirit-2026t-safety-keep-children-away`).
- **The manual never mentions the print in words.** It is legible on the console drawing, English to
  the left of the keypad and French to the right, and neither book has a product labels page - so
  "on the console overlay" is a reading of the drawing, not a stated location.
- **The French half is quoted from a 600 dpi render** of the CR900 page; the CU900 page carries the
  same print. The CE900-2021 elliptical shows the same decal and its French half was unreadable in
  that book (`ce900-2021-safety-console-warning-decal`); the bike render reads cleanly.

**The CR800 (2020) and CU800 (2020) consoles carry a different print in the same style**, under the
dot-matrix window beside the PROGRAM key: `WARNING! Heart rate monitoring systems may be inaccurate.
If you feel faint stop exercising immediately.` with its French line - the heart-rate disclaimer
(`spirit-2026t-safety-heart-rate-readings-are-not-medical`), not the under-13 line. Do not quote one
family's console print for the other.
