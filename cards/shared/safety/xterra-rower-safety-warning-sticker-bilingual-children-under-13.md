**Both books reproduce the same sticker on the Product Labels page and say where it is** - `on the
center frame, near the pedals` (ERG180) or `on the frame behind the water tank` (ERG800W):

> SAFETY WARNING STICKER - This sticker will be found [there]. The same warning can be found in this
> manual. Please read and be aware of the precautions before operating your unit.

The sticker is a picture; its text, read from the render, is a WARNING / AVERTISSEMENT panel headed
**SERIOUS INJURY COULD OCCUR IF THESE PRECAUTIONS ARE NOT OBSERVED**:

| # | Precaution |
|---|---|
| 1 | Consult your physician before use. |
| 2 | Stop immediately if you become dizzy or experience chest pains and consult your physician. |
| 3 | Heart Rate monitoring systems can be inaccurate; use them for reference only. |
| 4 | Read all warnings on the unit and be properly instructed or read the Owner's Manual on how to use before beginning. |
| 5 | Inspect this machine for damage prior to use. |
| 6 | Keep body, clothing, and fitness accessories clear of moving parts. |
| 7 | **Risk of personal injury - Keep children under the age of 13 away from machine.** |

followed by the same seven in French (`gardez les enfants âgés de moins de 13 ans loin de
l'appareil`).

**On both rowers this sticker is the only age anywhere.** The ERG180 list says `Keep children and
pets away from the machine at all times. DO NOT leave children unattended in the same room` and the
ERG800W list names no children at all; its tank page says `Keep unsupervised children away from the
equipment` (`xterra-rower-safety-who-may-use-capability-physician-children-and-pets`). *The same warning
can be found in this manual* is not true of items 2 and 5 on either book, nor of item 3 on the ERG180,
whose list has no pulse-sensor bullet (`xterra-treadmill-safety-pulse-sensors-are-not-medical-devices`).

The ERG750W, the third new-layout rower, prints no safety sticker on its Product Labels page
(`xterra-rower-safety-product-labels-page`). The ERG160 and ERG220 carry an older English-only label
with a belt rule instead (`xterra-erg-safety-warning-sticker-with-the-yellow-belt-mark`). The Xterra
treadmills and recumbents carry this sticker on the upright
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-warning-sticker-on-the-upright`, `xterra-sb-safety-warning-sticker-on-the-upright`).
