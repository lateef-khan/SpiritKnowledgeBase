**Two lists, on two pages, and they are written for different readers.** The first is the generic
electrical-appliance list; the second is about using the machine.

> following: Read all instructions before using this appliance.

> **DANGER - To reduce the risk of electric shock:** Always unplug this appliance from the electrical
> outlet immediately after using and before cleaning and/or service work..

> **WARNING - To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons,** install the
> Incline Stepper on a flat level surface with access to a 120-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet with only
> the Incline Stepper plugged into the circuit. DO NOT USE AN EXTENSION CORD UNLESS IT IS A 14AWG OR

**The XS895 is the only Spirit climber whose manual calls it a household machine.** `The appliance is
intended for household use` is printed in both the 2018 and 2021 books. The CSC900, CSC880, CS800,
CRS800S and CVC800 manuals are written for a club, and the 7.0S, 7.5S, 8.5S and MS300 for a clinic.
Do not carry a club rule onto an XS895 or the reverse.

**The two printings are the same page.** Their safety chapters match at 99.8% on an order-free word
count, and no figure on either list differs.

**Neither list mentions the incline.** The machine's stairs rise and fall under the user and no item
on either page says anything about mounting, dismounting or holding on while that happens. The
handrail rule is printed later, on the operation page: `Always hold on to a handle bar while making
control changes (incline, level, etc.).`
