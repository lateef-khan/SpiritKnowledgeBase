**The manual's console features page shows a warning decal on the machine**, in English and French:

> **WARNING** - Risk of personal injury - Keep children under the age of 13 away from machine.
>
> **AVERTISSEMENT** - Risque de blessures - [French text follows, see below]

- The rule is the same age-13 keep-away rule the safety instructions list prints as a bullet
  (`spirit-ce-safety-who-may-use-the-elliptical`). The decal adds nothing to it.
- **This manual has no PRODUCT LABELS page**, so it never says where on the machine the decal is
  applied. The drawing places it beside the console; that is the drawing, not a statement, and the
  location must not be quoted as the manual's.

**The French half is not quoted here.** The page has no text layer in the PDF and was read by OCR,
which returned the French line damaged - `gardez les enfants Seés de moins de 13 ans|oin de
Fappareil` - with the accented words and the elision unreadable. The English half is legible and is
quoted above. Read the decal itself, or the printed page, before repeating the French to a
customer.

**No other Spirit elliptical manual shows this decal.** The CE800, CE850, CE800ENT and CE900-2025
manuals do not print it anywhere, and the CE900-2025 and CES880-2025 manuals describe their safety
warning sticker without reproducing its wording
(`ce900-2025-safety-product-labels`, `ces880-2025-safety-product-labels`).

The Spirit CT900 treadmill carries a longer warning sticker whose last bullet is this same sentence,
`Risk of personal injury - Keep children under the age of 13 away from machine`, under a
`WARNING / AVERTISSEMENT` heading (`ct900-product-labels`). That sticker also names chest pains,
heart-rate accuracy and pre-use inspection, none of which appears on the elliptical decal - do not
quote the treadmill sticker for a CE900.
