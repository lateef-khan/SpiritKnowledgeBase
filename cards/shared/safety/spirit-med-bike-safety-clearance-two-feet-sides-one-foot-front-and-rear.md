> Ensure there is a minimum space on the sides of the 7.0R of two feet for proper operation, easy
> access and to prevent possible injuries to others standing or walking nearby. There should be a
> minimum of at least one foot of free space at the front and rear of the 7.0R.

| Side | Minimum free space |
|---|---|
| Each side | **2 ft** |
| Front | **1 ft** |
| Rear | **1 ft** |

- **These are minimums**, in the manual's own words, and the reason given for the side figure is
  access and the safety of people standing or walking nearby.
- **No metric conversion is printed**, no ceiling height and no room size. The Dutch block of the
  2021 book says sixty and thirty centimetres; the English pages do not.
- **A flat level surface** is asked for in item 2 with the outlet
  (`spirit-med-bike-safety-outlet-90-to-240-volt-15-amp`). No carpet rule, no training-area drawing.

**The 8.0U and 8.5R ask for three and a half feet at the rear**, on the same sentence otherwise
(`spirit-med-bike-safety-clearance-two-feet-sides-one-front-three-and-a-half-rear`) - the 8.5R's
removable seat and wheelchair approach are at the back. Do not carry that figure onto a 7.0R.

**The self-powered 4.0R and 4.0U print a different rule** - 2 ft on both sides for access, and a
training-area drawing in millimetres (`spirit-med-bike-safety-placement-and-clearance-two-feet-each-side`).

**The Dyaco MED 7.0R of 2021 prints this card's sentence** as a bullet, word for word.
