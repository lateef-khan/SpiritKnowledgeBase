> If voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more, the performance of your unit may be
> affected. Such conditions are not covered under your warranty. If you suspect the voltage
> is low, contact your local power company or a licensed electrician for proper testing.

- The threshold is **ten percent (10%)** variation, in either direction as printed.
- The consequence named is **performance**, not damage.
- **It is not a warranty matter.** The manual says so in those words.
- The action is to have the **power company or a licensed electrician** test the supply, not
  to book a service call on the treadmill.

The nominal circuit these manuals name is **110 volt** - see `spirit-product-must-be-grounded`,
which carries the grounding page in full.
