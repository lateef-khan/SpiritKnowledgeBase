**Eleven books, four wordings for children, and only one sticker that prints an age.** No rower book
mentions pacemakers or pregnancy.

| Rule | Wording | Books |
|---|---|---|
| Capability | `This exercise equipment is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge.` | all except the ERG400 |
| Supervision (older wording) | `Close supervision is necessary when this appliance is used by, on, or near children, invalids, or disabled persons.` | ERG400 (2015) only |
| Children and pets | `Keep children and pets away from the machine at all times. DO NOT leave children unattended in the same room with the machine.` | ERG160, ERG180, ERG500, ERG550W, ERG600W, ERG650W, ERG700 |
| Unsupervised children | `Keep unsupervised children away from the equipment.` - on the water tank page | ERG800W (`erg800w-2023-safety-water-tank-and-storage-warning-8-items`) |
| Children on the label | `KEEP CHILDREN AWAY.` | the ERG160 and ERG220 warning label (`xterra-erg-safety-warning-sticker-with-the-yellow-belt-mark`) |
| **Age** | `Risk of personal injury - Keep children under the age of 13 away from machine.` | **the ERG180 and ERG800W sticker only** (`xterra-rower-safety-warning-sticker-bilingual-children-under-13`) |
| Physician | `Before beginning this or any exercise program, consult a physician. This is especially important for persons over the age of 35 or persons with pre-existing health conditions.` | all eleven |
| Stop if unwell | `If the user experiences dizziness, nausea, chest pain, or any other abnormal symptoms, STOP the workout at once. CONSULT A PHYSICIAN IMMEDIATELY.` | ERG160, ERG180, ERG500, ERG550W, ERG600W, ERG650W, ERG700; the stickers add `Stop immediately if you become dizzy or experience chest pains` on the ERG160, ERG180, ERG220, ERG800W |
| One person | `Only one person at a time should use the machine.` | ERG160, ERG180, ERG500, ERG550W, ERG600W, ERG650W, ERG700 |
| Household | `The appliance is intended for household use.` | all except the ERG160 |

**Four lists print no children rule at all** - the ERG220, ERG400 (whose supervision item is the
only mention), ERG750W and ERG800W; the ERG220 has it on its label, the ERG800W on its tank page and
sticker, the ERG750W nowhere. **No
supervised-user exception**: item 3 stops at `and knowledge` on every book that prints it, and the
ERG400's older wording allows supervised use and names no age.

The strength books are stricter and give a figure - 18, with parental supervision under it
(`xterra-strength-safety-minimum-age-18-and-children-and-pets-away`). The Xterra bikes, ellipticals and
treadmills print the same three appliance rules (`xterra-safety-who-may-use-bikes-ellipticals-and-climber`,
`xterra-treadmill-safety-who-may-use-the-treadmill`); the Spirit rowers print their own
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-not-suitable-for-therapeutic-purposes`).
