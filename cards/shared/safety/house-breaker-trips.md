Some circuit breakers used in homes are not rated for the high inrush currents that occur when the elliptical is first turned on or even during use.

If the elliptical trips the house circuit breaker even though it is the proper current rating, but the breaker on the elliptical itself does not trip, **replace the home breaker with a high inrush type**.

**This is not a warranty defect.** The manual states it is a condition the manufacturer has no ability to control. The part is available through most electrical supply stores. Examples given: **Grainger part # 1D237**, or online at www.squared.com **part # QO120HM**.
