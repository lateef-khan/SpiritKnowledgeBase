**This appliance is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge, unless they have been given supervision or instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible for their safety.**

**This clause was added in 2016.** The B74/R72 2015, B94/R92 2011 and 2013, LCB 2011 and 2013 and LCR 2011 and 2013 manuals do not contain it. Do not quote it for those machines.

Two more medical cautions are printed elsewhere in these manuals, away from the safety page:

- On the heart rate page of every one of the 14 upright and recumbent manuals: **"Consult your physician before participating in any exercise program."**
- Before the fitness test, in the LCB and LCR manuals only: **make sure you are in good health; check with your physician before performing any exercise if you are over the age of 35 or have any pre-existing health conditions.**

A reader who only sees the safety page will miss both.
