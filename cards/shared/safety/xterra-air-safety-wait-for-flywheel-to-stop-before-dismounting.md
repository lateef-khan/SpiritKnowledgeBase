**Both air bike books give the same three rules for stopping, and no brake.**

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | Do not attempt to ride this bike at high pedal speeds until you have ridden the bike for some time and are comfortable riding at slower pedal speeds. |
| 2 | Wait for flywheel to coast to a stop before dismounting the bike. |
| 4 | Do not attempt to turn the pedal cranks by hand. Do not touch any driving mechanism while it is in motion as possible injury could occur. |

There is no brake knob, no lever and no *push down to stop* sentence - the indoor cycles have those
(`xterra-mb-safety-no-freewheel-pedals-keep-turning-and-how-to-stop`); on the air bike you stop
pedalling and wait. The book never says whether the pedals keep turning while the fan coasts, so a
customer should be told to keep their feet on the pedals until it stops, which item 2 requires
either way.

**Neither book prints the word *fan*.** The moving-parts warning is item 4's *driving mechanism* and
the general `Keep hands away from all moving parts` is not on this list at all (it is on the
appliance-list books). There is no finger, hair, clothing or pet-near-the-fan sentence, and no
rule about riding standing up. Item 5 keeps children and pets away while the bike is in use
(`xterra-safety-who-may-use-bikes-ellipticals-and-climber`).

The Spirit AB900 - whose service manual the AIR650 shares by the manufacturer's note - prints a
different precautions list that does name the movement arms and standing up
(`spirit-air-bike-safety-dismount-movement-arms`, `spirit-air-bike-safety-user-precautions-list`); those are
Spirit cards and not the AIR650 owner's manual.
