**The three new-layout bike books have a PRODUCT LABELS page; the other twenty-seven books in the
wave have no such page and no sticker drawing.** The sticker images are pictures; their text was
read from the render, not the text layer.

| Sticker | FB180 | SB240 | SB600 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Serial number** - a barcode with the number under it and the Jonesboro phone number | `on the frame` - (870) 333-5500 | `on the frame, near the leveler` - (870) 336-4286 | `on the rear of the stabilizer, near the adaptor port` - (870) 336-4286 |
| **Proposition 65** - `WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm - www.P65Warnings.ca.gov` | `on the rear stabilizer` | `on the frame` | `on the rear of the motor cover` |
| **Safety warning** - the bilingual seven-point panel | not printed | `on the left side of the unit's upright` | `on the left side of the unit's upright` |

The page's own words for the serial sticker (SB600):

> This sticker will be found on the rear of the stabilizer, near the adaptor port. Please record the
> number below the barcode for the purpose of registering your **treadmill's** warranty.

and for the Proposition 65 sticker:

> This sticker will be found on the rear of the motor cover. The State of California requires us to
> inform you that this unit was manufactured using chemicals that could cause harm with improper use.

**Two of the SB600 sentences are the treadmill page's**: the bike has no motor cover, and it is not a
treadmill. This card follows the drawing and the serial sentence, which were written for the bike
(the stabilizer and the adaptor port are real), and treats *rear of the motor cover* as unverified -
the Prop 65 sticker is somewhere on the frame, as the SB240 says, and a customer who cannot find it
should look near the serial sticker. The SB240 and FB180 pages name only bike parts. The FB180 page
has no safety warning sticker and its phone number is the older (870) 333-5500.

The sticker wording is on `xterra-sb-safety-warning-sticker-on-the-upright`; where every other book puts
the serial number, or fails to, is on `xterra-bike-safety-serial-number-sticker-locations`. The
Xterra treadmills print the same page (`xterra-treadmill-safety-product-labels-page`); the Spirit AB900
and CES880 print theirs (`spirit-air-bike-safety-warning-labels-and-stickers`,
`ces880-2025-safety-product-labels`).
