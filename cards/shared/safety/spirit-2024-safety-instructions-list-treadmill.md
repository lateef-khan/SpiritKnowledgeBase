**Both manuals print this page word for word.** It is a numbered list of 21 items under a DANGER
line and a WARNING line, and it never names an outlet, a voltage or an amperage.

> When using an electrical appliance, basic precautions should always be followed, including the
> following: Read all instructions before using this appliance.

> **DANGER - To reduce the risk of electric shock:** Always unplug this appliance from the
> electrical outlet immediately after using and before cleaning.

> **WARNING - To reduce the risk of burns, fire electric shock, or injury to persons:**

| # | Instruction |
|---|---|
| 1 | An appliance should never be left unattended when plugged in. Unplug from outlet when not in use, and before putting on or taking off parts. |
| 2 | Do not operate under blanket or pillow. Excessive heating can occur and cause fire, electric shock, or injury to persons. |
| 3 | This exercise equipment is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge. |
| 4 | Use this appliance only for its intended use as described in this manual. Do not use attachments not recommended by the manufacturer. |
| 5 | Never operate this appliance if it has a damaged cord or plug, if it is not working properly, if it has been dropped or damaged, or dropped into water. Return the appliance to a service center for examination and repair. |
| 6 | Do not carry this appliance by supply cord or use cord as a handle. |
| 7 | Keep the cord away from heated surfaces. |
| 8 | Never operate the appliance with the air openings blocked. Keep the air openings free of lint, hair, and the like. |
| 9 | Never drop or insert any object into any opening. |
| 10 | Do not use outdoors. |
| 11 | Do not operate where aerosol (spray) products are being use or where oxygen is being administered. |
| 12 | Connect this appliance to a properly grounded outlet only. See Grounding Instructions. |
| 13 | The appliance is intended for household use. |
| 14 | To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position, then remove the plug from the outlet. |
| 15 | Do not operate equipment on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and equipment may result. |
| 16 | Before beginning this or any exercise program, consult a physician. This is especially important for persons over the age of 35 or persons with pre-existing health conditions. |
| 17 | Keep hands away from all moving parts. |
| 18 | The pulse sensors are not medical devices. Various factors, including the user's movement, may affect the accuracy of heart rate readings. The pulse sensors are intended only as exercise aids in determining heart rate trends in general. |
| 19 | Do not attempt to use your equipment for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended. |
| 20 | Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable for use on your equipment. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue. |
| 21 | User Weight Limit: 450 lb. |

`being use` in item 11 is the manual's own wording; the word intended is *used*.

Beside the list is a boxed note, printed on every one of the fourteen 2024 manuals:

> Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of
> your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine.

**This is not the CT800-2020 list.** It descends from it, but six rules are new and three are gone.

| Change | What |
|---|---|
| **New** | items 15, 16, 17, 18, 19 and 20 - carpet, consult a physician, hands away from moving parts, pulse sensors, intended purpose, proper shoes |
| **Gone** | `Close supervision is necessary when this appliance is used by, on, or near children, invalids, or disabled persons` |
| **Gone** | `Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine` - **these two manuals print no age rule at all** |
| **Gone** | the `ASTM F2115-05 Specifications 6.1.2.11` clearance bullet, replaced by a LOCATION REQUIREMENTS panel on the grounding page (`spirit-2024-safety-treadmill-location-requirements`) |
| **Changed** | item 3 rewrites `This appliance is not intended for use by persons...` and drops the supervised-use exception the older manuals allow |
| **Changed** | `Maximum User Weight: 450 lbs` becomes `User Weight Limit: 450 lb.` - the figure does not change |

The 2012 to 2020 list is `spirit-ct800-safety-instructions-list`; do not quote its age rule or its
ASTM clearance for a 2024 machine, and do not quote this list's item 16 or item 20 for a 2020 one.

**Item 3 has no exception clause.** Every other Spirit manual that prints this sentence ends it
`...unless they have been given supervision or instruction concerning use of the appliance by a
person responsible for their safety`. These two 2024 treadmill manuals stop at `and knowledge`
(`spirit-ce-safety-who-may-use-the-elliptical`).
