**All five grounding pages print the same sentence, and it carries a voltage but no current.**

> This product is for use on a nominal **110-volt** circuit, and has a grounding plug that looks
> like the plug illustrated below.

- **110 volt nominal.**
- **No amperage.** The word `dedicated` does not appear and no breaker rating is given.

**On the two ENT treadmills this contradicts their own safety page**, which asks the installer for
a `120-volt AC, 15-Amp. grounded outlet`
(`spirit-2024-safety-instructions-list-treadmill-ent`,
`spirit-ct800-safety-outlet-120-volt-15-amp`). The two statements are three pages apart in the same
book and neither is retracted. Quote both, and say which page each is on.

**On the CT800-2024 and CT850-2024 this sentence is the only supply figure in the book.** Their
safety list names no outlet at all - item 12 says only `Connect this appliance to a properly
grounded outlet only. See Grounding Instructions.` - and their electrical page gives no breaker
rating. **A 2024 CT800 or CT850 manual states no amperage anywhere.**

**That is a change from the CT800-2020 manual**, which printed `The electrical outlet used should
have a dedicated 15 amp circuit breaker` and `This product is for use on a nominal 120-volt/15 amp
dedicated circuit` (`spirit-ct800-safety-outlet-120-volt-15-amp`). The 2024 book drops the breaker
sentence, drops the amperage, and moves the voltage from 120 to 110. Do not carry the 2020 figures
forward.

**On the CS800-2024 it disagrees with that manual's own header too.** The safety page asks for a
`110-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet` and the electrical page for a `dedicated 5-amp circuit breaker`;
this grounding sentence adds a third statement with no current at all
(`spirit-2024-safety-outlet-110-volt-15-amp-stepper`).

**Nine of the fourteen 2024 manuals have no grounding page.** CE800, CE800ENT, CR800, CR800ENT,
CU800, CRS800S, CSC900 and CRW800 print none, and the CE850 prints one that names a
`nominal 110-volt/5 amp dedicated circuit` instead
(`spirit-ce850-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`).
