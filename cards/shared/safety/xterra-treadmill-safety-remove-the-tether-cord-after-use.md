**Ten manuals say it outright; the other nine only imply it.**

The seven older-layout books close the SAFETY TETHER CORD section in capitals:

> REMOVE TETHER CORD AFTER USE TO PREVENT UNAUTHORIZED TREADMILL OPERATION.

and repeat it as step 2 of *To Turn Treadmill Off* (`Remove tether cord.`) and in the disconnect
bullet of the safety page (`turn all controls to the off position, remove tether cord, then remove
the plug`).

The TRX2500, TRX3500 and TRX4500 say it under *To Turn Treadmill Off*:

> 1. Remove Safety Key. 2. Turn of the main switch on the front of the treadmill, below the motor cover.
> Note: Do not leave in Safety Key in treadmill when not in use. Always turn off machine.

**The TRX5500 and the eight new-layout books print no such line.** They keep only the tether section's
`Removing the magnet also secures the unit from unauthorized use`
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-tether-cord-and-key`), which states the mechanism without making the rule.
Do not quote the capitals line for them.

Since none of the Xterra lists carries an age rule except the WS200 and WS300
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-who-may-use-the-treadmill`), taking the key away is the only child-proofing
the older books offer; the TRX4500 alone adds a console child lock in engineering mode, which is a
console card.

The Spirit 2026 treadmill prints the same instruction (`spirit-2026t-safety-secure-the-tether-cord-after-use`).
