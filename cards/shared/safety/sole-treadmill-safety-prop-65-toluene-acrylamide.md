**WARNING: This product can expose you to chemicals including Toluene and Acrylamide which are known to the State of California to cause Cancer and birth defects or other reproductive harm. For more information, go to www.P65Warnings.ca.gov.**
Printed once, under the table of contents, in all 24 A2 books. This is the longer warning: the F60-2016 book prints a shorter one naming no chemical and giving no URL (`f60-2016-safety-prop-65-warning`), and the other A1-wave books print none. Older-layout Xterra treadmill books print the same Toluene/Acrylamide sentence on the safety page (see `xterra-treadmill-safety-proposition-65-warning`).
