**Every new-layout Xterra manual has a PRODUCT LABELS page** naming three stickers. The older-layout
and 2024 TRX books have no such page (`xterra-treadmill-safety-serial-number-location-not-printed`).

| Sticker | Where it is | Books |
|---|---|---|
| **Serial number sticker** - a barcode with the number under it and `(870) 336-4286` | on the rear of the motor cover | TRX1400, TR65, TR75, TR85, WS200, WS300 (`xterra-treadmill-safety-serial-number-on-the-rear-of-the-motor-cover`) |
| | on the frame near the power switch | TR95H, TR75H (`xterra-tr-safety-serial-number-on-the-frame-near-the-power-switch`) |
| **Proposition 65 warning** - `WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm - www.P65Warnings.ca.gov` | same place as the serial sticker on each book | all eight (`xterra-treadmill-safety-proposition-65-warning`) |
| **Safety warning sticker** - a bilingual English/French list of seven precautions | on the interior side of the upright | all eight (`xterra-treadmill-safety-warning-sticker-on-the-upright`) |

The page's own words for the serial sticker:

> This sticker will be found on the rear of the motor cover. Please record the number below the
> barcode for the purpose of registering your treadmill's warranty.

and for the safety sticker:

> This sticker will be found on the interior side of your treadmill's upright. The same warning can be
> found in this manual. Please read and be aware of the precautions before operating your unit.

The Hiker books say `Hiker Treadmill` throughout and move both the serial and Prop 65 stickers to the
frame by the power switch. The three sticker images on the page are pictures; their text was
recovered by reading the render, not the text layer.

The Spirit CT900 and XT books print the same page for their own machines (`ct900-product-labels`,
`xt-2015-safety-product-labels-and-serial-number`).
