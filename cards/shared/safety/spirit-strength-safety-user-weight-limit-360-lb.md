**360 lb / 163 kg.** Every one of the nineteen CSS selectorized single stations and all nine CSD dual
stations prints it, in two places on two different pages.

| Where | Printed as |
|---|---|
| Safety instructions, item 20 | `User Weight Limit: 360lb / 163kg.` |
| Safeguards, Proper Usage | `The maximum weight for individuals using the Strength Equipment should not exceed 360lb (163kg).` |

The wording drifts between books without the allowance changing. **CSS-SCPR** writes item 20 as
`Max User Weight: 360 lb/163kg.` **Six CSD manuals** — ACBE, BCTE, CPSP, ITOT, LPCE and PUDA
— write item 20 with no metric figure at all, `User Weight Limit: 360 lbs.`, and spell the safeguards
line out as `360 pounds (163 kilograms)`. **CSD-LELC** prints `360 lb / 163kg` in both places, and
**CSD-LPSR and CSD-PFRD** follow the CSS wording. Most CSS books state it a third time in the
specification table on the assembly page, labelled `Max User Weight`, `User Weight Limit` or — on
CSS-LROW alone — `Max Load`.

163 kg is 359 lb, so the pair is self-consistent. **The serial number decal is not.** On the seven
older CSD books and on CSS-SCPR the decal reads `MAX USER WEIGHT: 180 KG / 360 LBS`, and 180 kg is
397 lb — 34 kg above the manual. That contradiction has its own card; quote the manual figure.

The manuals do not say what happens above 360 lb, and there is no separate stack or load limit on
these twenty-eight machines: the weight stack figure is a specification, not a safety limit.
