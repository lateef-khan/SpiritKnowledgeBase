**Seventeen of the thirty machines in this wave plug into the wall through an external adapter;
the other thirteen have a battery console and nothing to plug in**
(`xterra-safety-battery-console-no-power-adapter`). Only one of the seventeen prints the adapter's
rating - the FS3.0, `This product requires a 9V, 1000mA adaptor`, on its console page (FS3.0 OM PDF
p. 15, line 545), which is a console card's figure; the other sixteen print none.

**Nine books print a Power paragraph under the registration blanks** - the SB150, SB250, SB2.5r,
EU150, FS1.5, FS2.5, FS3.0, FS3.5 and FS150:

> **Power** - This product uses an external power supply. The adapter must be plugged into the power
> jack, located on the front of the unit near the stabilizer tube. Next, plug the adapter into the
> appropriate wall outlet. When not in use, it is recommended to unplug the power supply from the
> wall outlet.
>
> IMPORTANT: If the product has been exposed to cold temperatures, allow the product sit in room
> temperature to warm up before plugging in the adapter. Failure to do so may result in damage to the
> display or electronic components.
>
> IMPORTANT: Do not operate the product if the power supply, its plug, or its cord has been damaged.

(The SB2.5r, FS1.5, FS2.5, FS3.0 and FS3.5 say *the power supply* for *the adapter*; the sentences are
otherwise identical.)

| Rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| Power jack | on the front of the unit near the stabilizer tube - the only location any of the nine gives |
| Unplug when not in use | a recommendation, restated as item 1 of the safety list |
| Cold machine | let it reach room temperature before plugging in; the risk named is the display and electronics |
| Damaged supply, plug or cord | do not operate; restated as item 5 of the safety list |

**The other eight plug-in machines say so elsewhere:**

| Book | Where the book says it plugs in |
|---|---|
| SB500, SB4.5r, FS5.8e, FS5.9e | console page, *Power Up*: `The [machine] is supplied with an external power supply. When power is connected to the [machine], the console will automatically power up.` |
| SB4500 | parts list: `Power Cord` (part 71) and `DC Power Cord 500mml` (part 73); console page: `After connecting to power, the console enters boot mode` |
| RSX1500 | a **Plug-In** picture callout on the electrical page, with no text |
| SB240 | `Adaptor` / `Adapter` in the Parts Included list |
| SB600 | `Adapter` in the Parts Included list; *PLUGGING IN*: `The adaptor port is located at the rear of the Main Frame of the machine.`; the serial sticker is `near the adaptor port` |

The six older books and the two new-layout recumbents print an electrical warnings page instead of
the Power paragraph (`xterra-safety-electrical-and-operation-instructions-40-to-120-f`,
`xterra-sb-safety-electrical-safety-page`); the nine with the paragraph print no electrical page and no
environment rating.

**Two of the jack locations disagree**: the nine paragraphs say *front of the unit near the
stabilizer tube*, the SB600 says *rear of the Main Frame*. Both are as printed; the SB600 is a
different frame and its own book is the one to follow for it.

The same paragraph is printed, in error, on three Xterra treadmills that have a mains cord
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-external-power-supply-paragraph`). The Sole bikes print it with an adapter
rating (`sole-bike-power-supply-rating`).
