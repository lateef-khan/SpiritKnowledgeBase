**All nineteen manuals print the same bullet.**

> Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable for use on your
> equipment. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue.

| | |
|---|---|
| Wear | quality athletic shoes |
| Never | high heels, dress shoes, sandals, bare feet |
| Reason the manual gives | leg fatigue |

It is a Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions bullet on the older-layout and 2024 TRX books, item 20
of the 21-item list, and item 21 on the two Hiker books whose list has an extra tablet rule. The
manuals give no other clothing rule beyond the sticker's `Keep body, clothing, and fitness
accessories clear of moving parts` on the new-layout machines.

The Sole F83-2026 prints the same bullet for its own machine (`f83-2026-proper-footwear`).
