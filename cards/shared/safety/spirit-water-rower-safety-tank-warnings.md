**Three boxed warnings, printed word for word the same in both water rower manuals.**

> **WARNING** - Do not fill past the calibration mark as indicated on the tank level sticker or
> water spillage can occur.

> **WARNING** - Do not insert fingers into tank.

> **WARNING** - After rowing exercise, please allow the unit to sit for 5 minutes before standing it
> up for storage.

- **The fill limit is a mark on the machine, not a number on this page.** The safety chapter names
  the *calibration mark* on the *tank level sticker* and gives no volume. The CRW900's filling
  instructions elsewhere in its book do give one - a maximum indicated level of 20 litres with the
  resistance adjuster set to `10` - and that figure belongs with the filling procedure, not here.
- **Five minutes is the figure to quote for storage**, and the reason the manual gives is storage
  rather than performance: the water has to settle before the rower is stood on end.
- **`Do not insert fingers into tank` is absolute** and is printed with no exception for cleaning or
  for adding a treatment tablet.

**These three warnings exist on no other Spirit machine.** No air rower, bike, treadmill,
elliptical or climber manual in the repository carries a tank, a fill mark or a spillage warning;
they are what makes a water rower different.

**A rower that leaks is a different question.** Water escaping from the tank in normal use is a
maintenance fault with its own procedure (`crw800h2o-water-leaking`); overfilling past the
calibration mark is what this card covers.

**The fourth boxed warning on the same page is about the feet, not the tank**
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-feet-in-straps-and-footplate-locked`), and the fifth is the Proposition
65 box (`xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`).
