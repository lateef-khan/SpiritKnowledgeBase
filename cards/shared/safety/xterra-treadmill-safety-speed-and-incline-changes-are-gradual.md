**All nineteen manuals open their operation warnings with these two bullets.**

> NEVER operate this treadmill without reading and completely understanding the results of any
> operational change you request from the computer.

> Understand that changes in speed and incline do not occur immediately. Set your desired speed on the
> computer console and release the adjustment key. The computer will obey the command gradually.

The TR150 and TR200, which have no powered incline, print `changes in speed do not occur
immediately`; every other book says `speed and incline`. The 2024 TRX and new-layout books say
`Set your desired work level` instead of `desired speed`, and `unit` for `treadmill`.

So a delay after a speed or incline key is designed behaviour, not a fault: press, release, and the
belt or deck ramps to the new setting. A belt that never reaches the speed shown is a different
problem and an errors card.

The Spirit XT-2023 and Sole F65-2026 print the same bullet for their own machines
(`xt-2023-safety-speed-and-incline-response-delay`, `f65-2026-speed-and-incline-response-delay`).
