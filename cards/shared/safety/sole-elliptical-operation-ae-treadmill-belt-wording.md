**These are treadmill sentences printed in an elliptical book - quoted as printed, not as instructions.** `Understand that changes in speed and incline do not occur immediately. Set your desired speed on the computer console ...` and `... stray from walking in the center of the belt; which may result in serious injury.` Ellipticals have no belt and no speed setting; the matching elliptical rule names resistance and gradual response.

The same copy error reaches the safety list: `To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position, **remove tether cord**, then remove the plug from the outlet.` Ellipticals ship no tether cord. Every other B book reads `then remove the plug from the outlet` with no tether.
