**This is the dump feature (the machine tilted 15 degrees). It is not tilt safety (one cable pulled further out than the other), drop safety (letting go of the cable) or static safety (holding still).**

When tilted **15 degrees, all weight releases**. The cable starts to retract using minimum force **5 seconds later**.

Only the SRVO-2021 owner's book prints this feature - the seminar and app cards cover the cable-imbalance tilt safety instead (see the linked card).
