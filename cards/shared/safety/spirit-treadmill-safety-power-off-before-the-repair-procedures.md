**The four repair procedures do not ask for the same thing.** Only the controller swap asks for the
cord to come out; the other three ask only for the switch.

| Procedure (2007 dealer manual / CT800-2016) | First step as printed |
|---|---|
| Replacing controller (7 / 6) | `Disconnect power cord from unit.` |
| Replacing wire harness (8 / 7) | `Turn power switch off.` - and the last step is `Connect to board and console, & turn power back on.` |
| Replacing drive motor (9 / 8) | `Turn power switch off.` |
| Replacing incline motor (10 / 9) | `Turn power switch off.` - then `Turn on unit. The new motor should turn until it is at "zero"` before the incline-tube bolt goes back in |

- **Controller: cord out.** The step is `Disconnect power cord from unit`, not switch off.
- **Harness, drive motor, incline motor: switch off.** The manual asks for no more than the power
  switch for these three, and the harness and incline-motor procedures end by switching back on.
- **No discharge time and no lock-out procedure** is printed for any of the four.

**The 2007 dealer manual has no safety chapter.** It is a troubleshooting guide, twelve procedures,
a warranty section and five parts lists; these four first steps are the only power-safety
instruction it prints for the XT175, XT275, XT375, XT475 and XT675, and it prints no outlet figure,
no grounding page and no GFCI rule. That is an absence - do not read the later XT safety pages onto
these machines.

**The CT800-2016 service manual prints the same four procedures in the same words**, renumbered 6
to 9, at the back of its troubleshooting chapter. Its own safety chapter asks for more - the
treadmill disconnected from the outlet before any service work
(`spirit-disconnect-from-the-outlet-before-cleaning-or-service`) - and the two statements sit in
the same book without being reconciled. Follow the safety chapter: pull the plug for all four.

**The other repair procedures print no power step at all.** Speed sensor adjustment, checking the
roller for a magnet and belt checking have none, and the torque-boost adjustment is performed with
`START` pressed and `someone standing on the walking belt` - it cannot be done unpowered.
