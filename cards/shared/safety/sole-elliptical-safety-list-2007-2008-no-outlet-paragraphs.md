The list opens with `WARNING - Read all instructions before using this appliance` and then, with **no DANGER shock paragraph and no outlet paragraph at all**:

- Carpet damage rule; **keep children away** (no age figure - pinch points and caution areas); hands away from moving parts; damaged cord/plug plus call-your-dealer; cord away from heated surfaces; **aerosol/oxygen with no sparks sentence**; nothing into openings; no outdoor use; disconnect by turning controls off then removing the plug; intended purpose only; pulse sensors not medical devices; proper shoes.
- **SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!**
- Electrical page: **NEVER remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power supply**; ten-percent voltage rule with the not-covered consequence; **NEVER expose to rain or moisture**, not for outdoors, pool, spa or high humidity - **with no temperature or humidity figure**.

Who may use, outlet, grounding, storm-unplug and weight-limit rules the later books print are all absent here; each absence has its own card.
