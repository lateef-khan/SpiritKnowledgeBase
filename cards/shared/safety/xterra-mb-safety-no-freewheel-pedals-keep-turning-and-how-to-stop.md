**These are fixed-gear machines: while the flywheel turns, the pedals turn, whether you are
pedalling or not.** All three books say so as items 2 and 3 of the safety list:

> The bike is NOT equipped with a freewheel system which means that when the flywheel is in motion,
> the pedals will be in motion. Do not attempt to stop the unit by applying backward pressure to
> pedals while they are turning as knee injury may occur. Do not attempt to remove your feet from
> pedals while they are moving.
>
> Wait for flywheel to coast to a stop before dismounting the bike. If you want to stop the
> flywheel, push down on the brake knob.

and again on the Basic Operation page, where the book names the control the machine actually has:

| Book | Resistance control | To brake | Before dismounting |
|---|---|---|---|
| MB500, MB550 | `tension knob: clock-wise for more resistance; counterclockwise for less` | `press down on the tension knob` | `apply the brake to stop flywheel, or increase resistance and let flywheel come to a stop` |
| MBX2500 | `tension lever: down for more resistance; up for less` | `press down on the tension lever to the maximum resistance setting` | `increase resistance and let flywheel come to a stop` |

**The MBX2500 contradicts itself**: its safety list says *push down on the brake knob*, but the bike
has a lever (`Adjusting the Tension - Push the tension lever (63) up or down`; no brake knob is named
anywhere else in the book). The safety list is the Dyaco boilerplate shared with the knob bikes; the Basic
Operation page describes this machine. This card follows the Basic Operation page: on the MBX2500,
push the lever down to full resistance to stop the flywheel. The two MB books have a knob and both
pages agree.

| Rule | All three |
|---|---|
| Never | push back on the pedals to stop, or lift your feet off moving pedals |
| Stop | press the brake (knob or lever) or add resistance, then wait for the flywheel to stop |
| Then | dismount |

The AIR350 and AIR650 print the coast-to-a-stop item without the freewheel item and without a
brake (`xterra-air-safety-wait-for-flywheel-to-stop-before-dismounting`).
The Spirit CIC and Sole SB indoor cycles print the same warning for their own machines
(`spirit-indoor-cycle-safety-fixed-gear-dismount`, `sole-indoor-cycle-fixed-gear-and-stopping`).
