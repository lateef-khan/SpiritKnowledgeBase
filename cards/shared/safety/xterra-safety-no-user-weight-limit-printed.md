**These seventeen manuals state no user weight limit.** Not on the safety page, not in a
specification table (none of them has one), not in the warranty. A reader asking for one must be
told the book does not give it.

How it was checked: every page of each book was read, the whole text searched for `weight`,
`limit`, `lb`, `kg` and `capacity`, and for the scanned books (MB500, SB4.5r, FS150, FS5.8e, FS5.9e)
the safety page rendered from the PDF and read as a picture. The only `weight` hits are the
body-weight prompt of the console set-up (`The default WEIGHT is 70kgs or 155lbs`, `A Weight of
"75" will flash`, `User weight of 165 lb`), which is a calorie input, not a limit, and the
`Weight Loss` program names.

| Family | Books without a limit | The sibling that prints one |
|---|---|---|
| Indoor cycles | MB500, MB550, MBX2500 | none - no Xterra indoor cycle prints a limit |
| Recumbent bikes | SB150, SB250, SB2.5r, SB4500, SB4.5r, SB500 | SB120 (250), SB240 (265), SB600 (300) |
| Ellipticals | EU100, FS1.5, FS2.5, FS3.0, FS3.5, FS150, FS5.8e, FS5.9e | EU150 (265) |

**Do not carry a sibling's figure across.** The SB250 book is the SB150 book reprinted with a 2024
cover and no limit was added; the EU150 book is the EU100's safety page with item 14 added, and the
EU100 page has no item 14.
