> To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons, install this equipment on
> a flat level surface with access to a **100 to 240-volt AC, 50/60 Hz, 15-amp grounded outlet**. Do
> not use an extension cord unless it is **16awg or larger**, with only one outlet on the end. This
> equipment should be the only appliance in the electrical circuit. Do not attempt to disable the
> grounded plug by using improper adapters, or in any way modify the cord set; a serious shock or
> fire hazard may result along with computer malfunctions.

> To avoid risk of electric shock, this equipment must only be connected to a supply main with
> protective earth.

| Requirement | Figure |
|---|---|
| Nominal supply | **100 to 240 volt AC** - a range, not one voltage |
| Frequency | **50/60 Hz** |
| Circuit | **15 amp**, and the bike the only appliance on it |
| Outlet | grounded; on the 8.0U the supply main must also have **protective earth** (item 3) |
| Extension cord | **16awg or larger**, one outlet on the end |
| Surface | flat and level |

**The machine draws far less than 15 amps.** Both specification pages print `100 ~ 240 Vac, 1.76 ~
0.71 Amps, 50/60Hz` - 1.76 A at 100 V falling to 0.71 A at 240 V. The 15 amp figure is the circuit
the manual requires, not the current the bike takes; give the customer both when they ask about a
breaker.

**The supply is universal and fused.** `The 8.0U has a built-in universal power supply. You can plug
the 8.0U into any A.C. power source from 100 to 240 volts, 50 to 60 Hz. The A.C. input is located in
the front of the 8.0U. The input module has an input connector for the line cord, a power switch and
a 5 amp fuse.` The specification page names the fuse to fit: `250V, 5A glass fuse, Fast acting,
5.2 x 20 mm`. **No NEMA plug type is printed anywhere**, and no breaker paragraph.

**The tolerance rule is on the electrical page.** `If voltage varies by 10% or more outside the
specified range (100 to 240V), the performance of your equipment may be affected. Such conditions
are not covered under your warranty` (`xt-2023-safety-supply-voltage-variation`).

**The 8.5R prints every figure the same** - item 2, the electrical page, the setting-up page and
the specification page - **and no protective-earth item.** Its safety list goes from the outlet
sentence straight to `Use this 8.5R only for it's intended use`; the grounding rule for the 8.5R is
its grounding page alone (`spirit-product-must-be-grounded`).

**The 7.0R and 7.0U ask for 90 to 240 volt**, ten volts lower at the bottom of the range, and like
the 8.5R print no protective-earth item (`spirit-med-bike-safety-outlet-90-to-240-volt-15-amp`). The 8.5UE
ergometer (`85ue-2025-safety-outlet-100-to-240-volt-50-60-hz-15-amp`) and the 7.0S, 7.5S and 8.5S
steppers (`spirit-climber-safety-outlet-100-to-240-volt-15-amp`) print this card's figures for their
own product lines; the self-powered 4.0R and 4.0U need no outlet at all
(`spirit-bike-safety-no-mains-outlet-needed`).
