> 6. Weight Limit: 300 lbs.

**300 lbs is the lowest rider limit of any Spirit bike in the repository.** Both XIC600 manuals
print it, in the same words, as item 6 of the six numbered Important Operation Instructions.

**It is not in the safety list.** The sixteen-bullet Important Safety Instructions list on the
facing page ends at the under-13 rule and states no figure at all
(`spirit-xic600-safety-instructions-list`). A reader who checks only the safety page will conclude
the manual prints no limit. It does; it is one page further on, under a different heading.

**No kilogram figure is printed** and no conversion is given, on either page or anywhere else in
either book.

## Do not carry this figure to another Spirit bike

| Machine | Printed limit |
|---|---|
| XIC600-2018, XIC600-2021 | **300 lbs** |
| CIC800-2021, CIC850-2022, AB950-2024 | 350 lb (`spirit-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lb`) |
| AB900-2018 | 350 pounds (159 kilograms), same card |
| JB950-2022 | **450 lb and 350lbs, on two different pages** (`jb950-2022-safety-user-weight-limit-450-and-350`) |
| CB900-2013 | none printed (`cb900-2013-safety-instructions-list`) |
| Commercial CR / CU bikes | 450 lb (`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`) |

The XIC600 is a lighter-framed indoor cycle than the CIC and the commercial bikes, and its limit is
50 to 150 lb below theirs. Answering an XIC600 question with a CIC or commercial figure overstates
the machine's rating by a wide margin.
