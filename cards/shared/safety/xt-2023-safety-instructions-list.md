All five 2023 XT owner's manuals print this page word for word. **Only item 21, the user
weight limit, differs between the machines** - it has its own cards, listed at the bottom.

> When using an electrical appliance, basic precautions should always be followed,
> including the following: Read all instructions before using this appliance.

**DANGER - To reduce the risk of electric shock:** Always unplug this appliance from the
electrical outlet immediately after using and before cleaning.

**WARNING - To reduce the risk of burns, fire electric shock, or injury to persons:**

1. An appliance should never be left unattended when plugged in. Unplug from outlet when
   not in use, and before putting on or taking off parts.
2. Do not operate under blanket or pillow. Excessive heating can occur and cause fire,
   electric shock, or injury to persons.
3. This exercise equipment is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical,
   sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge.
4. Use this appliance only for its intended use as described in this manual. Do not use
   attachments not recommended by the manufacturer.
5. Never operate this appliance if it has a damaged cord or plug, if it is not working
   properly, if it has been dropped or damaged, or dropped into water. Return the appliance
   to a service center for examination and repair.
6. Do not carry this appliance by supply cord or use cord as a handle.
7. Keep the cord away from heated surfaces.
8. Never operate the appliance with the air openings blocked. Keep the air openings free of
   lint, hair, and the like.
9. Never drop or insert any object into any opening.
10. Do not use outdoors.
11. Do not operate where aerosol (spray) products are being use or where oxygen is being
    administered. *("being use" is printed that way in all five manuals.)*
12. Connect this appliance to a properly grounded outlet only. See Grounding Instructions -
    `spirit-product-must-be-grounded`.
13. The appliance is intended for household use.
14. To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position, then remove the plug from the
    outlet.
15. Do not operate equipment on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet
    and equipment may result.
16. Before beginning this or any exercise program, consult a physician. This is especially
    important for persons over the age of 35 or persons with pre-existing health conditions.
17. Keep hands away from all moving parts.
18. The pulse sensors are not medical devices. Various factors, including the user's
    movement, may affect the accuracy of heart rate readings. The pulse sensors are intended
    only as exercise aids in determining heart rate trends in general.
19. Do not attempt to use your equipment for any purpose other than for the purpose it is
    intended.
20. Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable for
    use on your equipment. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue.
21. **User Weight Limit** - a different figure on each machine, see below.

The boxed note beside the list:

> Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power
> requirements of your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage
> to your machine.

**Two of these rules are also printed elsewhere in the same manuals, and the other copy says
more.**

- **Item 15, carpet.** The location requirements page repeats it and adds the rest of the
  placement rules: `xt-2023-safety-placement-and-clearance`.
- **The DANGER line about unplugging.** The Electrical Safety page adds that no cover may be
  removed without first disconnecting AC power:
  `xt-2023-safety-remove-a-cover-only-with-ac-disconnected`.

**This list has no minimum user age and no children clause.** Where the Spirit CT900 and
CTSBS900 manuals say "keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine", these five
manuals say only, on the location page, to install the treadmill where children and pets
cannot access it. Do not quote the age-13 rule for an XT.

**Item 13 says household use.** These manuals make no claim of commercial or light
commercial duty.
