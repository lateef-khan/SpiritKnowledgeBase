**Both new-layout recumbent books reproduce the same sticker and say where it is:**

> SAFETY WARNING STICKER - This sticker will be found on the left side of the unit's upright. The
> same warning can be found in this manual. Please read and be aware of the precautions before
> operating your unit.

The sticker is a picture on the page; its text, read from the render, is a WARNING / AVERTISSEMENT
panel headed **SERIOUS INJURY COULD OCCUR IF THESE PRECAUTIONS ARE NOT OBSERVED**:

| # | Precaution |
|---|---|
| 1 | Consult your physician before use. |
| 2 | Stop immediately if you become dizzy or experience chest pains and consult your physician. |
| 3 | Heart Rate monitoring systems can be inaccurate; use them for reference only. |
| 4 | Read all warnings on the unit and be properly instructed or read the Owner's Manual on how to use before beginning. |
| 5 | Inspect this machine for damage prior to use. |
| 6 | Keep body, clothing, and fitness accessories clear of moving parts. |
| 7 | **Risk of personal injury - Keep children under the age of 13 away from machine.** |

followed by the same seven in French (`gardez les enfants agés de moins de 13 ans loin de
l'appareil`). Items 3 and 6 are partly obscured in the render; the words given are the legible ones
and the French column, which prints them in full.

**On both bikes this sticker is the only age rule anywhere**: the 21-item safety list names no age
(`xterra-sb-safety-instructions-list-21-items`), and no other bike, elliptical or stepper book in the
range prints one (`xterra-safety-who-may-use-bikes-ellipticals-and-climber`). Item 2 - stop if dizzy or in
chest pain - appears otherwise only on the three indoor cycles.

*The same warning can be found in this manual* is not true of items 2, 5 and 7; the list has the
physician rule, the pulse-sensor disclaimer and *keep hands away from moving parts*, and nothing else
from the sticker.

The Xterra treadmills carry the same sticker on the *interior* side of the upright
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-warning-sticker-on-the-upright`); the Spirit XB-2023 bikes carry it too
(`spirit-xb-2023-safety-warning-sticker-text`) - a Spirit card.
