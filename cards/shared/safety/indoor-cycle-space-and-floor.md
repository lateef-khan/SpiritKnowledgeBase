- **Only use the bike on a stable, level floor.**
- **For safe operation, allow for at least 1 foot (30cm) of free space to either side of the unit and 2 feet (60cm) of free space to the rear of the unit.**

All ten indoor cycle manuals print both items in the same words.

**These manuals say nothing about carpet**, and nothing about temperature or humidity. The upright and recumbent manuals carry a carpet rule and a 40 to 120 degree Fahrenheit range; no indoor cycle manual does.
