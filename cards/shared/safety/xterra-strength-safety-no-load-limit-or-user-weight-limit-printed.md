**None of the four strength books prints a load figure for the stand or a user weight limit for the
dumbbells.** The safety list's only weight sentence is item 19, `This product is very heavy. For the
highest level of safety place the dumbbell trays and dumbbells on the floor.`
(`xterra-strength-safety-instructions-list-20-items`), and the product weights themselves are the
dumbbell increments on the Instructions page - 2.5 to 12.5 lb, 5 to 25 lb, 11 to 55 lb - which are
what the dumbbell weighs, not what a user may weigh.

**The stand's only capacity rule is about fit, not load**: `Only XTERRA Adjustable Dumbbell Cradles
can be mounted to this stand (Not included.)`, and the assembly step names the 12.5 lb cradles (four
bolts No.11) and the 25 lb or 55 lb cradles (four bolts No.13) as the things it carries. A stand
holding a pair of 55 lb dumbbells carries 110 lb plus the cradles; the book never states that or any
maximum.

How it was checked: every page of each book read; the text searched for `load`, `capacity`, `limit`,
`max`, `weight` and `lb`. The hits are the increment charts, the parts lists (`Dumbbell plate 2.5Kg`)
and item 19.

The Spirit strength range prints a 600 lb loading capacity and user limits for its own machines
(`spirit-strength-safety-max-loading-capacity-600-lb`, `spirit-strength-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lb`);
those are Spirit cards and say nothing about these products.
