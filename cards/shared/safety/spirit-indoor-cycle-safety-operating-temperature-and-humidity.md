**One paragraph, printed in the same words in all three manuals:**

> NEVER expose the bike to rain or moisture. This product is NOT designed for use outdoors, near a
> pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment. Maximum environmental ratings are 40-120
> degrees Fahrenheit, 95% humidity non-condensing (no water droplets forming on surfaces).

- **40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit**, which is about 4 to 49 degrees Celsius. No Celsius figure is
  printed.
- **95% relative humidity, non-condensing.** The manual defines non-condensing itself: no water
  droplets forming on surfaces.
- **Near a pool or spa is ruled out explicitly**, and so is outdoors, regardless of the temperature
  reading. The humidity rule is not the only bar.

**This is the whole of the IMPORTANT OPERATION INSTRUCTIONS page in these three books.** Unlike the
commercial upright and recumbent bikes, they print no resistance-delay rule, no distraction rule and
no console-key rule beside it (`spirit-bike-safety-operation-instructions`).

## The five manuals that print no range at all

**XIC600-2018, XIC600-2021, AB900-2018, AB950-2024 and JB950-2022 state no operating temperature
and no humidity limit.** A loose search over each whole book for *humidity*, *Fahrenheit*,
*temperature*, *rain* and *moisture* finds only unrelated text - a heart-rate zone called the
Temperate Zone in the JB950, a list of things that affect perceived exertion in the AB950, and the
instruction to keep moisture under the chest strap electrodes in the XIC600 pair.

The XIC600 manuals are the surprise there: they are the same class of machine as the CIC800 and
share most of its safety list, but the environmental paragraph is not in either printing. The AB900
and JB950 rule out `rainy weather outdoors, or an enclosed pool environment` in their DO NOT block
(`spirit-air-bike-safety-user-precautions-list`) and give no numbers with it; the AB950 prints only
`Do not use outdoors.`
