**Three treadmill manuals print, under the registration blanks, a paragraph written for a machine with
a plug-in power adapter.** It is on the page; the render shows it, so it is not ghost text.

> **Power** - This product uses an external power supply. The power supply must first be plugged into
> the power jack on the unit. Next, plug the power supply into the appropriate wall outlet. When not
> in use, it is recommended to unplug the power supply from the wall outlet.
>
> IMPORTANT: If the product has been exposed to cold temperatures, allow the product sit in room
> temperature to warm up before plugging in the power supply. Failure to do so may result in damage
> to the display or electronic components.
>
> IMPORTANT: Do not operate the product if the power supply, its plug, or its cord has been damaged.

**It does not describe these machines.** Every other page of the same three books describes a
treadmill with a mains cord: the grounding page says it `is equipped with a cord having an
equipment-grounding plug` for a nominal 120-volt (TR6.6) or 110-volt (TRX4500, TRX5500) circuit, the
electrical page says `Route the power cord away from any moving part`, the maintenance pages say
`UNPLUG THE POWER CORD`, and the TRX4500 and TRX5500 parts lists name a *Power Cord* and a *Power
Socket* and no adapter. The paragraph reads like the bike-manual template Xterra and Spirit use for
machines with a wall-wart supply, left on the page when the treadmill book was made from it. The
brief for this wave expected it on the TRX5500 only; the TR6.6 and TRX4500 print it word for word too.

What can be taken from it without contradiction:

| Sentence | Standing |
|---|---|
| unplug when not in use | agrees with item 1 of the safety list (`Unplug from outlet when not in use`) |
| let a cold machine warm to room temperature before plugging in | the only cold-weather instruction in any Xterra treadmill book; nothing elsewhere contradicts it |
| do not operate with a damaged plug or cord | agrees with item 5 of the safety list |
| plug a power supply into a power jack | **contradicted** - there is no power jack and no external supply on these treadmills |

The sixteen other Xterra treadmill manuals print no Power paragraph on this page. The Sole bikes are
the machines the paragraph fits (`sole-bike-power-supply-rating`).
