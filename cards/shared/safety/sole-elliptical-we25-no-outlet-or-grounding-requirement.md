The DANGER cleaning paragraph, the nominal 110-volt outlet paragraph and the 14AWG paragraph are all headed as E35-only, and the grounding section is headed E35-only. The WE25-2009 and WE25-2010 therefore have **no printed outlet, extension-cord or grounding requirement** - the manual simply never gives them one. Do not quote the E35 figures for a WE25.
