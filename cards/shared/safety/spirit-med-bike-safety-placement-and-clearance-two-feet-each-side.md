**Two feet of access on both sides, a level floor, and no garage, patio, water or outdoors - stated
in words - plus two drawn boxes that give the footprint and the free space in millimetres.**

> Place the machine on a level surface. For ease of access, there should be an accessible space
> preferentially on both sides of the machine equal to 2 ft (0.6 meters) to allow a user access to
> the machine from either side. Do not place the machine in any area that will block any vent or
> air openings. The machine should not be located in a garage, covered patio, near water or outdoors.

> Do not operate this product on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and
> product may result.

**The drawings put numbers on it.** Figure 1 is the training area, Figure 2 the free area; each is
a box with the bike inside and three dimensions:

| | Training area (Figure 1) | Free area (Figure 2) |
|---|---|---|
| 4.0R recumbent | **3440 x 740 mm, 2000 mm high** | **4640 x 1940 mm, 2500 mm high** |
| 4.0U upright | **3080 x 550 mm, 2000 mm high** | **4280 x 1750 mm, 2500 mm high** |

The free area is the training area plus **600 mm on every side** and **500 mm of height** on both
machines, which is where the 2 ft of the sentence comes from. The manuals never say this in words;
the figures are only in the drawings, and the text layer scrambles them, so they were read from a
render.

- **No front or rear figure is printed in words.** Only the sides get `2 ft`; the front and rear
  margins exist only as the 600 mm in the drawing.
- **The Dyaco PT editions print the same sentence and the same drawings**, in five languages, with
  the same millimetre figures (`spirit-pt-bike-safety-instructions-list-dyaco-edition`).

**The plug-in medical bikes say something different.** The 7.0R and 7.0U ask for two feet each side
and one foot front and rear (`spirit-med-bike-safety-clearance-two-feet-sides-one-foot-front-and-rear`);
the 8.0U and 8.5R for three and a half feet at the rear
(`spirit-med-bike-safety-clearance-two-feet-sides-one-front-three-and-a-half-rear`). None of the four
prints a training-area drawing. The 4.0T treadmill prints the same two boxes with treadmill figures
(`40t-2026-safety-placement-and-floor`).
