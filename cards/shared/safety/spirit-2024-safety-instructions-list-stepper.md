**The two manuals differ by one word.** Item 15 reads `User Weight Limit: 450 lb` in the CRS800S
book and `Recommended User Weight Limit: 450 lb` in the CS800 book. The figure is 450 in both and
neither manual says what happens above it. Nothing else on the page differs - the native text layers
of the two pages are the same words in the same order.

Beside the list is the boxed note printed on every one of the fourteen 2024 manuals:

> Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of
> your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine.

## This is the CE850 elliptical page with two words changed

The CE850 safety list (`spirit-ce850-safety-instructions-list`) is the same page: same header, same
fifteen items, same order, same extension-cord line. Two words differ, and one of them is a figure:

| | Stepper manuals | CE850 manuals |
|---|---|---|
| Outlet in the WARNING header | **110-volt, 15-amp** | **110-volt, 5-amp** |
| Item 13 | `mental capabilities` | `metal capabilities`, a slip for *mental* |

**Never carry the 5-amp figure onto a stepper or the 15-amp figure onto a CE850.** The stepper
manuals' own electrical page then asks for a **5-amp** breaker, which does not agree with the
15-amp outlet in this header; see `spirit-2024-safety-outlet-110-volt-15-amp-stepper`.

**The CSC900 stairclimber's list is a different list entirely** - eight items written for a
commercial gymnasium, a 400 lb limit and a 120/230 VAC input
(`csc900-2024-safety-instructions-list`).
