**Eighteen bullets, under an Attention and a Danger heading, and the machine is named in every
one.** The two books are the same page with 7.0S or 7.5S written throughout.

> **Attention** - Read all instructions in this manual before using this 7.0S.

> **Danger** - To reduce the risk of electric shock, disconnect your 7.0S from the electrical
> outlet prior to cleaning and/or service work.

> **Warning**

| Do not modify this equipment without authorization of the manufacturer. |
| To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons, install the 7.0S on a flat level surface with access to a 100 to 240-volt AC, 50/60 Hz, 15-amp outlet. The 7.0S should be the only appliance in the electrical circuit. |
| Use this 7.0S only for it's intended use as described in this manual. |
| Keep children away from the 7.0S. There are moving parts, obvious pinch points and other caution areas that can cause harm. |

| Do not use outdoors. |

## Three differences from the Spirit-badged books of the same family

- **The spare-parts bullet names `Dyaco`.** The MS300's names `Spirit Fitness`; the 2025 7.0S and
  7.5S name `Spirit by Dyaco`. Same instruction, three manufacturer names.
- **No `User Weight Limit: 440 lbs.` line, and no boxed note beside the list.** The 2025 books
  close with both. The limit is stated three times elsewhere in these books, as a patient weight -
  `ms300-2021-safety-patient-weight-limit-200-kg`.
- **The machine is named in every bullet.** The MS300 prints the same template with the product
  name dropped and three bullets calling the machine a bike; neither defect is in the English pages
  of these books - `treadmill` and `bike` return **zero hits each** across all 72 and 75 pages.

**The template defect survives in the translations.** The French pages of the same list say *"Ne pas
utiliser le **cycle exerciseur** à d'autres fins"* and the Dutch *"Probeer uw **fiets** niet voor een
ander doel te gebruiken"*, where the English says the 7.0S. Only the English pages were corrected.

## What this list does not contain

No carpet rule, no proper-shoes rule, no aerosol or oxygen rule, no extension-cord gauge and no
children-under-13 age - the same omissions as the MS300 and the 2025 books. **The clinical
contraindications are on a different page**, at printed p. 20, on the far side of the four
translated safety chapters, and a reader who stops here will miss them: see
`spirit-rehab-stepper-safety-contraindications-osteoporosis-fractures-and-acute-conditions`.

## The last two bullets put assembly and servicing out of the owner's hands

Installation and assembly are for trained personnel only, and servicing beyond what the manual
describes is for authorised service personnel. These are clinic machines and the book expects a
trained installer.
