**The five TRX books from the TRX1400 up print the grounding page with a nominal 110-volt circuit.**
Every other Xterra treadmill manual prints 120 volts in the same sentence
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-grounding-nominal-120-volt-circuit`). The 2024 TRX books also shorten the
first sentence to `If the treadmill should malfunction or breakdown`.

> This product must be grounded. If the treadmill's electrical system should malfunction or breakdown
> grounding provides a path of least resistance for electric current, reducing the risk of electric
> shock. This product is equipped with a cord having an equipment-grounding plug. The plug must be
> plugged into an appropriate outlet that is properly installed and grounded in accordance with all
> local codes and ordinances.

> **DANGER** - Improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric
> shock. Check with a qualified electrician or serviceman if you are in doubt as to whether the product
> is properly grounded. Do not modify the plug provided with the product if it will not fit the outlet;
> have a proper outlet installed by a qualified electrician.

> This product is for use on a nominal **110-volt** circuit, and has a grounding plug that looks like the
> plug illustrated below. A temporary adapter that looks like the adapter illustrated below may be used
> to connect this plug to a 2-pole receptacle as shown below if a properly grounded outlet is not
> available. The temporary adapter should be used only until a properly grounded outlet, (shown below)
> can be installed by a qualified electrician. The green colored rigid ear-lug, or the like, extending
> from the adapter, must be connected to a permanent ground such as a properly grounded outlet box
> cover. Whenever the adapter is used, it must be held in place by a metal screw.

The drawing under it is labelled `Grounding Pin`, `Grounded Outlet`, `Grounded Outlet Box`,
`Adapter`, `Metal Screw` and `Tab of Grounding Screw`.

| Rule | |
|---|---|
| Grounded outlet, installed to local code | required |
| Modify the plug to fit an ungrounded outlet | never |
| Temporary 2-pole adapter | allowed only until an electrician fits a grounded outlet; its green ear-lug on a permanent ground; held by a metal screw |
| Circuit | nominal 110 volts - **no amperage is given on this page** |

**110 is the only circuit figure in these five books**, and it comes with no amperage: the 2024 TRX
safety page and the TRX1400 safety page both dropped the `120-volt, 15-amp` warning of the older
layout (`xterra-treadmill-safety-no-outlet-amperage-or-extension-cord-rule-printed`). 110 and 120 name
the same North American supply; the manual does not explain the change.

**Three service manuals contradict even this.** The TRX2500 and TRX3500/TRX4500 Dyaco books print
`nominal 230-volt circuit` in this sentence, and the TRX1400 book prints 110
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-service-manual-outlet-and-extension-cord-figures`).

**The TRX4500 and TRX5500 books also print a paragraph about an external power supply and a power
jack**, which does not describe the cord and grounding plug this page describes
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-external-power-supply-paragraph`).

The Spirit CT800-2024 grounding page carries the same 110-volt sentence
(`spirit-2024-safety-grounding-page-says-110-volt`); that is a Spirit card.
