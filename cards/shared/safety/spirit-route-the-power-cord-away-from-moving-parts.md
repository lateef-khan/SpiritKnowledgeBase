> Route the power cord away from any moving part of the treadmill including the elevation
> mechanism and transport wheels.

Two parts are named because they move when the machine looks still: the **elevation (incline)
mechanism** and the **transport wheels**.

All three manuals print this sentence, the CVC800 with the word `climber` in place of `treadmill`.
It is printed as the second half of the GFCI bullet, not as a heading of its own; the GFCI half is
`spirit-never-use-a-gfci-wall-outlet`.

**The five 2023 XT owner's manuals print the same sentence** with the word `unit` in place of
`treadmill`, and print it as the opening WARNING of their Electrical Safety page rather than
inside the GFCI bullet:

> Route the power cord away from any moving part of the unit including the elevation
> mechanism and transport wheels.

The two parts named are the same two: the elevation mechanism and the transport wheels.
