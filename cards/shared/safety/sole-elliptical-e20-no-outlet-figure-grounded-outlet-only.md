The only outlet rule is list item 12: `Connect this appliance to a properly grounded outlet only.` No voltage, no amperage, no cord gauge and no grounding section are printed anywhere in either book. The console chapter powers the machine on `by plugging it into an appropriate wall outlet` - again with no figure. Do not quote 110-volt, 115-volt or 14 AWG for an E20-2016/2020.
