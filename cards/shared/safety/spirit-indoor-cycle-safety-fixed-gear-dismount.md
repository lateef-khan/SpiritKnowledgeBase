**These are fixed-gear machines. While the flywheel is turning the pedals are turning, whether the
rider is pedalling or not.** It is the single most dangerous property of an indoor cycle and every
one of these six manuals warns about it.

> The bike is NOT equipped with a freewheel system which means that when the flywheel is in motion,
> the pedals will be in motion. Do not attempt to stop the unit by applying backward pressure to
> pedals while they are turning as knee injury may occur. Do not attempt to remove your feet from
> pedals while they are moving.
>
> Wait for flywheel to coast to a stop before dismounting the bike. If you want to stop the
> flywheel, push down on the resistance/brake knob.

- **Never back-pedal to stop it.** Applying backward pressure is what causes the knee injury the
  warning names.
- **Never pull your feet out while the pedals are moving.**
- **Push down on the knob to stop it.** The CB900, CIC800 and CIC850 manuals call it the
  `resistance/brake knob`; the XIC600 manuals call it the `brake knob`. It is the same control and
  the same action.

## How each machine says to stop

| Machine | Wording | Where |
|---|---|---|
| CB900-2013, CIC800-2021, CIC850-2022 | `push down on the resistance/brake knob` | Safety list |
| CIC850-2022 | `EMERGENCY BRAKE - In case of emergency or before dismounting the indoor cycle, presses directly DOWN on the resistance knob to stop the flywheel and pedal.` | Its own section, printed page 24 |
| XIC600-2018, XIC600-2021 | `push down on the brake knob` | Safety list |
| JB950-2022 | Pedal more slowly until the pedals stop, **or** push down on the Push Brake System handle | DISMOUNTING BIKE |

**Only the CIC850 gives the stop a heading of its own**, and it is the only manual of the six to
call it an emergency brake. The sentence is printed as `presses directly DOWN`; that is the manual's
own wording, not a transcription slip.

## The Johnny G says it differently and means the same thing

The JB950 never uses the word *freewheel*. It states the consequence instead, under DISMOUNTING
BIKE:

> **WARNING:** The flywheel momentum of the bike will keep the pedals turning even after the user
> stops pedaling, or in the event the user's feet slip off the pedals. DO NOT DISMOUNT THE BIKE OR
> REMOVE YOUR FEET FROM THE PEDALS UNTIL BOTH THE PEDALS AND THE FLYWHEEL HAVE STOPPED COMPLETELY.
> Failure to comply may lead to loss of control and serious personal injury.

Its brake is a **Push Brake System handle**, not a resistance knob, and pedalling slowly to a stop is
offered as an equal alternative - neither is offered on the five cycle manuals.

**The two air bikes are not on this card.** They are fan-braked with movement arms rather than a
fixed-gear flywheel, and their dismount warning names a different moving part -
`spirit-air-bike-safety-dismount-movement-arms`.
