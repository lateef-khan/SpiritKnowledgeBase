**The two books certify different things and rate the device in different FCC classes.** Read the
first table before quoting either.

| | SB240 | SB600 |
|---|---|---|
| *This product has been certified to meet the following standards* | FCC part 15; Bluetooth (with FTMS) | FCC part 15; **UL**; Bluetooth (with FTMS) |
| FCC digital device class in the NOTE | **Class C** | **Class B** |

There is no Class C in Part 15 - the classes are A (commercial) and B (residential) - so the SB240's
*Class C* is a misprint; the paragraph it heads is the standard Class B residential notice word for
word, and the SB600, printed two weeks earlier from the same template, says Class B. This card
follows Class B for both and records the SB240's printed letter. Only the SB600 claims UL.

The rest of the page is the same on both:

> This device complies with part 15 of the FCC Rules. Operation is subject to the following two
> conditions: (1) this device must not cause harmful interference, and (2) this device must not
> accept any interference received, including interference that may cause undesired operation.

> NOTE: This equipment has been tested and found to comply with the limits for a Class [C/B] digital
> device, pursuant to Part 15 of the FCC Rules. These limits are designed to provide reasonable
> protection against harmful interference in a residential installation. This equipment generates,
> uses and can radiate radio frequency energy and, if not installed and used in accordance with the
> instructions, may cause harmful interference to radio communications. However, there is no
> guarantee that interference will not occur in a particular installation.

If it does interfere with radio or television reception, the four remedies are: reorient or relocate
the receiving antenna; increase the separation between the equipment and receiver; connect the
equipment to an outlet on a circuit different from the receiver's; consult the dealer or an
experienced radio/TV technician.

> CAUTION: To comply with FCC RF exposure compliance requirements, a separation distance of at least
> 20 cm must be maintained between the antenna of this device and all persons. This transmitter must
> not be co-located or operating in conjunction with any other antenna or transmitter.

The 20 cm rule concerns the Bluetooth antenna in the console; the rider's hands on the grips are
closer than that, and the page does not reconcile it. Pairing and the app are console matters. None
of the other twenty-eight bike, elliptical and stepper books prints a compliance page.

The Spirit CT900 and XT-2023 and the Sole spinner print the same page for their own machines
(`ct900-warnings-compliance-fcc`, `xt-2023-safety-fcc-class-b-interference`,
`xt-2023-safety-rf-exposure-separation-distance`, `sole-spinner-fcc-compliance`).
