The CSI-CPSP and CSI-LROW safeguards page prints five electrical rules that appear in **no other**
Spirit strength manual:

> - To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position and then remove the plug from the outlet.
> - NEVER remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power.
> - This product must be grounded. Improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can
>   result in a risk of electric shock. Do not modify the plug provided with the product. This
>   product is for use on a nominal 120-volt circuit.
> - Do not use excessive pressure on console control keys. They are precision set to function
>   properly with little finger pressure.

This is **printed on the page**, not a leftover in the file: the page was rendered at 250 dpi and
read, and the text is there.

**The 120-volt figure disagrees with the machine's own labels.** The rating decal on printed page 4
reads `Rating / Classement: 100-240V~ 50/60Hz 10A`; the name plate decal beside it reads
`Rating 110~220V`. Three different electrical statements in one book. Take the rating from the
decal, not from this bullet, and note that the decal pair do not agree with each other either.

**The manual never says where the power goes in.** Nothing in the assembly chapter fits a mains
lead, an adapter or a battery, and the words *adapter*, *power supply*, *battery* and *power switch*
appear nowhere in either book. So a caller asking "where do I plug it in" cannot be answered from
the owner's manual. The machine does have a console — the i-Strength — and the rating decal proves
it is mains powered, but the installation step is simply not documented.

The thirty-four CSS, CSD and CSF machines have no console and no electrical page of any kind.
