**Eight books print the warning, in two wordings; none of them forbids it.**

The six older plug-in books, under IMPORTANT OPERATION INSTRUCTIONS:

> Use caution while participating in other activities while using your [fitness bike / Seated
> Stepper / elliptical] such as watching television, reading, etc. These distractions may cause you
> to lose balance, resulting in serious injury.

The two new-layout recumbents, in the operation bullets of their Electrical Safety page:

> Use caution while participating in other activities while using your unit; such as watching
> television, reading, etc. (SB240)
> Use caution while participating in other activities **while walking on your unit**; such as
> watching television, reading, etc. These distractions may cause you to lose balance which may
> result in serious injury. (SB600 - the treadmill sentence, left unedited)

The risk named is losing your balance; on a recumbent or seated stepper that reads as a caution
about mounting, dismounting and reaching, since the rider is seated. The other twenty-two bike,
elliptical and stepper books print no distraction warning at all, and no Xterra bike book mentions a
tablet or phone in a safety rule - the FB180 and SB240 consoles have a phone ledge and say nothing
about using it while riding.

The treadmill books print the same warning about the belt
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-distractions-while-walking`); the Spirit XT-2023 prints the shorter wording for
its own machines (`xt-2023-safety-distractions-while-walking`).

**Rowers and strength.** The ERG800W prints the SB600's unedited treadmill sentence - `Use caution
while participating in other activities **while walking on your unit**; such as watching television,
reading, etc.` - on its Electrical Safety page (`erg800w-2023-safety-electrical-safety-page`); no other
rower book prints a distraction warning. The four strength books print it as item 20 of their list,
`...while using your adjustable dumbbell` (or `your Dumbbell Stand`) `such as watching television,
reading, etc. These distractions may cause you to lose balance, resulting in serious injury.`
(`xterra-strength-safety-instructions-list-20-items`) - the one Xterra case where the balance the rule
protects is a person holding a weight.
