- **DANGER** - to reduce the risk of electric shock, **disconnect the Bike from the electrical outlet prior to cleaning and/or service work**.
- **WARNING** - to reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock or injury to persons, **install the Bike on a flat level surface with access to a 115-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet, with only the Bike plugged into the circuit**.
- **DO NOT USE AN EXTENSION CORD UNLESS IT IS A 14AWG OR BETTER, WITH ONLY ONE OUTLET ON THE END.**
- **DO NOT ATTEMPT TO DISABLE THE GROUNDED PLUG BY USING IMPROPER ADAPTERS, OR IN ANY WAY MODIFY THE CORD SET.** A serious shock or fire hazard may result along with computer malfunctions.

**This manual prints two different supply voltages and never reconciles them.** Page 2, above, asks for a **115-volt** outlet. Page 3, under GROUNDING INSTRUCTIONS, says the product "is for use on a **nominal 110-volt circuit**". Both sentences are in the same manual. Neither this card nor the manual settles which is meant.

For contrast, the LCB 2011 and LCR 2011 manuals ask for **110 volts**, and the shared card built from the 2016 to 2019 bike **service** manuals says **120 volts** with a **16 AWG** extension cord. Three different supply voltages (110, 115 and 120) and two extension-cord gauges (14 AWG and 16 AWG) are printed across the Sole bike documentation. Use the figure printed for the machine in hand.
