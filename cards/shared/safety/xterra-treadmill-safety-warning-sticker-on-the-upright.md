**All eight new-layout manuals reproduce the same sticker** and say where it is:

> SAFETY WARNING STICKER - This sticker will be found on the interior side of your treadmill's
> upright. The same warning can be found in this manual. Please read and be aware of the precautions
> before operating your unit.

The sticker is a picture on the page; its text, read from the render, is a WARNING / AVERTISSEMENT
panel headed **SERIOUS INJURY COULD OCCUR IF THESE PRECAUTIONS ARE NOT OBSERVED**:

| # | Precaution |
|---|---|
| 1 | Consult your physician before use. |
| 2 | Stop immediately if you become dizzy or experience chest pains and consult your physician. |
| 3 | Heart Rate monitoring systems can be inaccurate; use them for reference only. |
| 4 | Read all warnings on the unit and be properly instructed or read the Owner's Manual on how to use before beginning. |
| 5 | Inspect this machine for damage prior to use. |
| 6 | Keep body, clothing, and fitness accessories clear of moving parts. |
| 7 | **Risk of personal injury - Keep children under the age of 13 away from machine.** |

followed by the same seven in French (`gardez les enfants agés de moins de 13 ans loin de l'appareil`).
Items 3 and 6 are partly obscured in the render; the words given are the legible ones and the French
column, which prints them in full.

**On six of these eight machines this sticker is the only age rule anywhere.** The 21-item safety
list of the TRX1400, TR65, TR75, TR85, TR95H and TR75H names no age
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-instructions-list-21-items`); only the WS200 and WS300 repeat `under the age of
13` in their list (`xterra-ws-safety-instructions-list`). Item 2 - stop if dizzy or in chest pain -
appears nowhere else in any Xterra treadmill book.

The Spirit XT-2023 treadmills and XB-2023 bikes carry the same sticker (`xt-2023-safety-warning-sticker-on-the-upright`,
`spirit-xb-2023-safety-warning-sticker-text`); those are Spirit cards.
