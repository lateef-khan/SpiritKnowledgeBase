**Eight rower books say the machine can be stood on end or folded to save space, and every one of
them prints a hazard beside the instruction.** The procedure is a storage matter; the hazards are
these.

| Hazard | Wording | Books |
|---|---|---|
| **The seat slides down the rail when the rail is raised** | `CAUTION: The Seat (38) will glide down when the Slide Rail (2) is raised.` | ERG160, ERG220 |
| | `CAUTION! Move the Seat to front of Slide Rail first or it will glide down when raising the rower up!` | ERG550W, ERG600W, ERG650W |
| | `When you stand the rower up, the seat will slide down.` | ERG180 |
| | `CAUTION! When the rails are uprighted, the Seat Cushion will slide down quickly!` | ERG750W |
| **Your head can hit the rear stabilizer or rail** | `Also take care to avoid the Rear Stabilizer (4) when moving the Slide Rail (2).` | ERG160, ERG220 |
| | `CAUTION! Move with caution when you raise the rower up, as your head may touch the Rear Stabilizer.` | ERG180, ERG550W, ERG600W, ERG650W |
| | `CAUTION! When standing the machine up, be aware that ones head may touch the track tube!` | ERG750W |
| **A folding rail does not stay up until it is pinned** | `CAUTION: The Slide Rail (2) will not stay in the vertical position before the Ball Head Pin (65) is inserted. Make sure to not let go of it before it is secured or the Slide Rail (2) may fall.` | ERG220 (`xterra-erg-safety-locking-pin-must-be-fully-engaged-during-use-and-when-folded`) |
| **The floor must suit a rower stood on its tank end** | `before storing it vertically, please check if the floor is suitable... If the floor is ragged or un-horizontal, you are not recommended to store it at this kind of floor. It is recommended to put a thin cloth between the standing rower and the floor to prevent from scratching the floor.` | ERG800W |
| **Do not tip a water rower onto one wheel** | `Don't tilt the rower left/right so that only one wheel touches the floor during the transportation. The water tank will be damaged if the rower falls left/right.` | ERG800W (`erg800w-2023-safety-water-tank-and-storage-warning-8-items`) |
| **Empty the tank before a long storage** | `If not using the rower for more than a month, empty the tank before storing.` | ERG550W, ERG600W, ERG650W |

Where the book says how to lift: the ERG550W, ERG600W and ERG650W say `Lift by the Rear Stabilizer
to raise the rower to vertical position`; the ERG180 and ERG750W say stand the machine up on the main
frame's mat. All eight also print `Always use caution when moving and storing your equipment` or
`use caution when moving` in the safety list or the transport paragraph.

**Three books print no vertical-storage instruction and no hazard**: the ERG400 and ERG500 fold at a
ball pin with no seat or head warning (`xterra-erg-safety-locking-pin-must-be-fully-engaged-during-use-and-when-folded`),
and the ERG700 folds at a release lever (`Lift and fold the Aluminum Track until it locks into place`)
with none. None of the eleven names a wall anchor, a strap or a ceiling height. The Spirit water
rowers add a five-minute wait before standing the rower up (`spirit-water-rower-safety-tank-warnings`).
