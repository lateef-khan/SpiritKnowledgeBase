**All three stair-climber service manuals open with the same three cautions, and the first is a before-service rule the owner's manuals do not print in these words.**

The CSC900-2024 and CSC880-2025 books print:

> - Turn off the power when replacing any parts such as electronic meter, brake system, generator, etc. Avoid personal or product injury that may be caused by energized operation.
> - When replacing parts, please use machine's original parts and make sure the product type and specification match. If you need to replace parts with different specifications for testing in special condition, please contact our technical staff for confirmation before operation.
> - When replacing parts, especially electrical parts, clean the parts and their surroundings before assembly to avoid damage caused by screws and other debris.

The CSC900-2019 book prints the same three, shorter: `Please turn off the power when replacing any parts such as electronic watch, brake system, generator, etc. Avoid possible personal or product injury caused by power operation`; `please use the original parts from manufacturer`; and `clean the parts and parts around before assembly to avoid damage caused by entering screws and other sundries`. It has no sentence about testing with different specifications.

- **Power off first**, and the parts named - the console (`electronic meter`, `electronic watch`), the brake system and the generator - are the electrical ones. The action is to turn the power off; none of the three books says to pull the plug, and none gives a wait time.
- **The power off is not the whole of it on a stair climber.** The CSC900-2019 owner's manual warns that the generator produces AC power while the staircase turns, whether or not the cord is in the wall (`spirit-climber-safety-generator-is-live-while-the-stairs-turn`). A staircase that is still moving is still live.
- **Original parts only**, matched by type and specification; a different specification needs the factory's technical staff first.
- **Clean before assembly**: the damage named is screws and debris getting into electrical parts.

**The CSC880 book says `Anyfit's original parts`**, naming the OEM rather than Spirit, on a page that also calls the book `the internal technical data of Spirit CSC880`. The part is the same; the name is the factory's.

**The owner's manuals' equivalents are different sentences**: the outlet and adapter rules of the safety list (`csc900-2024-safety-outlet-120-or-230-volt`, `spirit-climber-safety-outlet-120-vac-only`), and, on the other Dyaco service manuals, `disconnect ... from the electrical outlet prior to cleaning and/or service work` (`spirit-disconnect-from-the-outlet-before-cleaning-or-service`). The stair-climber books print neither.
