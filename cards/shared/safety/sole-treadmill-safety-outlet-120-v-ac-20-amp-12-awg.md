The books for the WTT8 - the only legacy treadmill in this wave on a 20-amp circuit - print their own figures: **access to a 120-volt AC, 20-amp grounded outlet**, and **DO NOT USE AN EXTENSION CORD UNLESS IT IS 12AWG OR BETTER, WITH ONLY ONE OUTLET ON THE END**. The rest of the extension-cord sentence (no improper adapters, no modifying the cord set, shock/fire/computer-malfunction warning) matches the other legacy books.

Its grounding page agrees: the product is for use on a nominal 120-volt circuit.
