**All three manuals print the same warning, and the same instruction: stop.**

The 7.0T:

> 13. Over exercise may result in injury or death. If you feel faint stop exercising immediately.

The 8.0T prints the identical sentence with a heart rate clause in front of it, and the 4.0T
prints it as item 17 with `Over exercising may result in serious injury or death`.

- **The action is to stop immediately**, not to slow down and continue.
- **The trigger the manuals name is feeling faint.** The 7.0T and 8.0T give a longer symptom list
  for the therapist to watch for - shortness of breath or difficulty breathing, pain or discomfort,
  feeling faint (`spirit-2026t-safety-medical-clearance-and-exertion`).

**The 4.0T adds a second sentence the others do not print**, as item 27 of its list:

> 27. Injuries to health may result from incorrect or excessive training.

Incorrect training as well as excessive; the 7.0T and 8.0T manuals do not print that half.

Neither the warning nor the symptom list is a substitute for the clearance each manual asks for
before starting - `40t-2026-safety-consult-a-physician` for the 4.0T,
`spirit-2026t-safety-medical-clearance-and-exertion` for the 7.0T and 8.0T.

**Both Spirit MT200 owner's manuals print the sentence with the heart rate clause in front of it**,
as the 8.0T does:

> WARNING: Heart rate monitoring system may be inaccurate. Over exercise may result in injury or
> death. If you feel faint stop exercising immediately.

The 2022 manual prints the same three sentences without the `WARNING:` prefix. The action is the
same - **stop immediately**, on the trigger of feeling faint - and both MT200 manuals give the
longer symptom list for the operator to watch for on their Important Operation Instructions page
(`spirit-2026t-safety-medical-clearance-and-exertion`).
