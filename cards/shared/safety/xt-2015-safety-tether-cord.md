All five 2015 XT manuals print this section in the same words.

> A safety tether cord is provided with this unit. It is a simple magnetic design that should be
> used at all times. It is for your safety should you fall or move too far back on the tread-belt.
> Pulling this safety tether cord will stop tread-belt movement.

**To use:**

1. **Place the magnet into position on the round metal portion of the console control head.** The
   treadmill will not start and operate without this. Removing the magnet also secures the
   treadmill from unauthorized use.
2. **Fasten the plastic clip onto your clothing securely** to assure good holding power.

The manual's own note on step 2:

> The magnet has strong enough power to minimize accidental, unexpected stopping. The clip should
> be attached securely to make certain it does not come off. Be familiar with its function and
> limitations. The treadmill will stop, depending on speed, with a one to two step coast anytime
> the magnet is pulled off the console. Use the Stop / Pause switch in normal operation.

- **Use it every time.** The manual's words are `should be used at all times`.
- **The magnet is also the key.** With it removed the treadmill will not start, which is how the
  manual says to secure it against unauthorized use.
- **A one to two step coast is normal**, and it varies with speed. The tether is not a
  dead-stop brake, and the manual asks for the **Stop / Pause** switch in normal operation.

**The 2015 XT manuals give the tether cord its own headed section on the Important Operation
Instructions page.** The 2023 XT manuals have no such section, so this is not a fact carried over
from a 2023 card.

The Spirit CT900 and CT850 manuals print the same paragraphs for those machines
(`ct900-safety-tether-cord-and-key`, `ct850-2020-safety-tether-cord-is-required`); they are
separate machines with their own cards.

## The two ENT manuals bring the section back

**The five 2023 XT owner's manuals dropped this section; both ENT manuals print it again.**

The **XT485ENT** manual prints it word for word as above, headed `SAFETY TETHER CORD` on its
Important Operation Instructions page, page 8, saying `treadmill` throughout and `to assure good
holding power` - the 2015 wording, unchanged.

The **XT685ENT** manual prints the same paragraphs on a page of its own headed `SAFETY KEY`,
page 18, with two words changed: `unit` in place of `treadmill`, and `to ensure good holding power`
in place of `to assure`. Every instruction and the one-to-two-step coast are the same.

Both manuals also state, in their power-on instructions, that the treadmill will not power on
unless the safety key is installed - the same fact the numbered step above gives.
