**600 mm (23.62") beyond the training area, on every side the machine is entered from, and the live
area has to include the room for an emergency dismount.** All nineteen CSS single stations and all
nine CSD dual stations print it under **Live Area and Training Area**:

> The live area shall be not less than 600mm (23.62") greater than the training area in the
> directions from which the equipment is accessed. The live area must also include the area for
> emergency dismount.

Seventeen of the nineteen CSS books put the inches first, `23.62" (600mm)`; CSS-ABDO, CSS-SHPR and
 all nine CSD books put the millimetres first. The figure is the same.

**How to work the figure out for a given machine.** The same page carries a plan view of the machine
with its training area drawn as a rectangle and dimensioned in millimetres, with an inch conversion
beside it. Add the perimeter to each accessed side of that rectangle. On the CSS-ABDO, for example,
the training area is 900 x 1210 mm (35.4" x 47.6") and the drawing marks 600 mm on all four sides, so
the live area is 2100 x 2410 mm.

Two things the manuals do **not** say: they set no separate wall clearance, and they set no
minimum gap between two machines other than this perimeter. The safety guidelines bullet above the
diagram is the only other statement — *provide an adequate safety perimeter between the machine,
walls and other equipment to ensure that the facility has the proper clearance for usage and
training* — and it puts no number on "adequate".


The CSF and CSI machines use larger perimeters — 130 cm to 180 cm — so never carry 600 mm across to
one of them. The three ST800 products print no live-area figure at all.
