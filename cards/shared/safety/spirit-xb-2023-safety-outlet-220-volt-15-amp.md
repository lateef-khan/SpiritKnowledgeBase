**This is the 2023 bike service manuals' figure: 220 volt at 15 amp. It is not the treadmill service
manuals' 220-volt, 10-amp figure, and the same page names a nominal 120-volt circuit three paragraphs
later.**

7.1 Important Safety Instructions:

> To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons, install the bike on a flat
> level surface with access to a 220-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet with only the bike plugged into the
> circuit.

7.3 Important Grounding Instructions, on the same page:

> This product is for use on a nominal 120-volt circuit and has a grounding plug that looks like the
> plug illustrated below.

| Book | Safety bullet | Grounding paragraph |
|---|---|---|
| XBR55-2023 (XR329A-SB030) | 220-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet | nominal 120-volt circuit |
| XBU55-2023 (XU319A-SB030) | 220-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet | nominal 120-volt circuit |
| XBR95-2023 (XR829A-SB032) | 220-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet | nominal 120-volt circuit |

- **The three pages are word for word the same.** Only the bike's part number on the cover changes.
- **Nothing on the page reconciles 220 with 120.** The treadmill service manuals that print 220 volt
  add a bracketed 120-volt-system figure beside it and open with a CE/CEGS note
  (`spirit-treadmill-safety-outlet-220-volt-10-amp`); these three bike books print no bracket and no
  such note anywhere. Quote both sentences to a customer and say the manual gives no third figure to
  settle them. **15 amp, not the treadmills' 10 amp**, is the current in all three.
- **The owner's manuals of the same three machines print no voltage and no amperage at all**
  (`spirit-xb-2023-safety-grounding-instructions-not-printed`), so this page is the only outlet
  figure on file for them.

**The XBR95-2023 is self-powered, and its service manual prints this page anyway.** The same book's
Electrical Configurations page says the main controller includes `power supply (generator power)`,
its block diagram draws a GENERATOR and GENERATOR BRAKE and no adapter, and its owner's manual says
the bike needs no outlet (`spirit-bike-safety-no-mains-outlet-needed`). The XBR55-2023 and
XBU55-2023 do plug in: their block diagram draws an `AC POWER ADAPTER`, and their product-operation
page says `When the power cord is connected to the equipment, the console will automatically power
up`. Tell an XBR95-2023 owner the bike needs no outlet and that its service manual prints one anyway.

**The rest of section 7 is the Dyaco treadmill chapter with `bike` substituted**, and each rule has
its own card: disconnect before cleaning or service, a 16 AWG extension cord, never a GFCI outlet,
the high-inrush house breaker with Grainger 1D237 and QO120HM, and the grounding and
temporary-adapter paragraphs. The cord-routing sentence even keeps `the elevation mechanism`,
which no bike has.

**The 2016 service manuals for the XBR25, XBR55, XBU55 and XBR95 print 115 volt at 15 amp in the same
sentence** (`spirit-xe-safety-outlet-115-volt-15-amp`). Different SKUs, different figure; never carry
one onto the other.
