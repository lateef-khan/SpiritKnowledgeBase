Twelve rows, over two pages at the back of the book.

| Symbol | Legend |
|---|---|
| Two figures lifting | Indicates that the package is heavy and two or more people are required to lift. |
| Broken glass | Package contains fragile electronic equipment. Care should be used when handling. The product inside the packaging could be easily damaged if dropped or handled without care and attention. The contents are fragile! |
| Crossed fork | Do not use forklift truck here. |
| Crossed spade | Do not use Spade truck here. |
| Crossed razorback | Do not use Razorback truck here. |
| Hand truck | Hand truck only. |
| Mobius loop | Indicates that an object is capable of being recycled - not that the object has been recycled or will be accepted in all recycling collection systems. |
| Arrows up | Top. This side up. |
| Sine wave | **AC power 100-240 Volt, 15Amps, 50Hz** |
| CE with 0123 | **Product certificate logo, CE MDD 93/42/EEC Class Im** |
| Open book with i | **read this entire manual carefully before operating your new 7.0S** |
| Standing figure | Type B applied parts. This is used for parts applied to the patient that require only ordinary electrical protection, e.g. headphones. |
| Reading figure | Follow Instructions for use |

**Hand truck only. Not a forklift, a spade truck or a razorback.** Three of the twelve rows are
there to stop someone lifting this carton the way a gym crate is lifted.

## This is the corrected version of the MS300's legend

The MS300 prints the same page with two rows wrong: its AC row asks for **220-240 Volt, 10Amps,
50Hz** and its manual row says *"before operating your new **treadmill**"*
(`ms300-2021-safety-packaging-symbols-and-template-defects`). **Both are right in these books** -
100-240 V at 15 A, matching the safety page, and the machine named. **When a caller quotes the
220-240 V figure, ask which book they have**; on a MED book the page is not in conflict with
anything.

The MED books also add a row the MS300 does not have at all: the **CE 0123** product certificate
logo against MDD 93/42/EEC Class Im. 0123 is a notified body number, and it is the same directive
the specification page cites - `spirit-climber-specs-medical-certification-list`.

**`Type B applied parts` is a real medical classification, not a defect.** It is the IEC 60601-1
class for parts that touch the patient and need only ordinary electrical protection.

**The 50 Hz on the AC row is narrower than the rest of the book**, which asks for 50/60 Hz
throughout. Read the safety page for the installation figure.
