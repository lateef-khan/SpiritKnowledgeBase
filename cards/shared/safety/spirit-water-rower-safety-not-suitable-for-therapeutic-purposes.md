> Note: Rower is not suitable for therapeutic purposes.

**That single sentence is the whole statement**, printed as a note beside the boxed warnings on the
SAFEGUARDS page of both water rower manuals. It gives no reason, names no standard and no
contraindication, and offers no exception for supervised use.

**Read it as a limit on what the machine is sold for.** The same chapter says the rower
`is designed for exercise in a commercial or consumer environment`
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-do-and-do-not-lists`) - a gym and a home, not a clinic.

**This is the exact opposite of what the Spirit 8.5UE upper body ergometer says**, and the two are
easily confused because both are seated pulling machines sold by the same brand. The 8.5UE manual
says its machine

> is intended to be used to redevelop muscles, restore motion to joints, in aiding in the physical
> rehabilitation process for patients with orthopedic and neurological problems

and prints an intended patient population, a list of medical purposes, and a list of conditions that
rule a patient out (`85ue-2025-safety-who-may-not-use-the-ergometer`). **Never answer a
rehabilitation question about a water rower from the 8.5UE cards, or the reverse.**

**No other Spirit rower manual says either thing.** The CRW800 and XRW600 air-rower books are silent
on therapeutic use, which is an absence rather than a permission.
