The same sentence closes the operation-instructions page of six rehabilitation stepper manuals,
each naming its own machine:

> **7.0S should not be used for patients with severe osteoporosis, non-union fractures,
> debilitating dizziness, or poor safety awareness/cognition. Do not use for patients weighing
> greater than 200kgs (bariatric). Do not use for patients with acute conditions such as pulmonary
> embolus, thrombus, acute MI, acute fractures, or BP over 180/110 Hg.**

| Group | Condition |
|---|---|
| Musculoskeletal | severe osteoporosis, non-union fractures, acute fractures |
| Neurological / cognitive | debilitating dizziness, poor safety awareness or cognition |
| Weight | greater than 200 kg (bariatric) |
| Acute cardiovascular | pulmonary embolus, thrombus, acute MI |
| Blood pressure | BP over 180/110 Hg |

- The list is written **`such as`**, so the manual does not present it as exhaustive.
- `MI` is myocardial infarction. `BP over 180/110 Hg` is printed that way; the unit for a blood
  pressure is mmHg.

## The weight is one limit printed in two units

The **MED 7.0S and 7.5S, the MS300 and the 8.5S** print `greater than 200kgs (bariatric)`. The
**2025 7.0S and 7.5S** print `greater than 440 lb (bariatric)` in the same sentence. 200 kg is
440.9 lb, so it is the same allowance, and the same figure the specification page gives as
`Patient weight capacity 440 Lbs. (200 Kgs.)`. See
`ms300-2021-safety-patient-weight-limit-200-kg`.

## The 8.5S names no machine

Its version begins **`This equipment should not be used...`** and spells non-union as
`non union`. Everything else is the same sentence.

## This is not on the safety instructions page

It sits three to fifteen pages later, under *Important operation instructions* or
*OPERATION INSTRUCTIONS*, and the safety list never refers to it. **A reader who works only from
the safety page will not see it.** Give both when a clinic asks who may use the machine.

## The 8.5S-Fit prints none of this

The fitness variant's book has no contraindication sentence at all - "osteoporosis" returns no
hits in it. **Do not carry this list onto an 8.5S-Fit** as though its manufacturer had stated it.

## The rehabilitation treadmills print the same sentence

The 7.0T, 8.0T and MT200 carry it on a different product line -
`spirit-2026t-safety-rehabilitation-contraindications`. Quote the machine's own book.
