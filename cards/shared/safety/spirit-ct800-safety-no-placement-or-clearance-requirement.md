**Neither manual states where the treadmill may stand or how much room it needs.** There is no
location page, no clearance figure, no standard cited, and no sentence about the floor. The words
*clearance*, *level surface* and *carpet* appear nowhere in either manual.

**That is an absence, not a permission.** Say the manual gives no figure, and take the clearance
from the installation standard the site works to or from Spirit, rather than from these manuals.

The only placement-adjacent rules either manual prints are environmental, and they are on the
electrical page rather than in a location section:

- **Not outdoors**, and never exposed to rain or moisture, near a pool or spa, or in any other
  high-humidity environment - 40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit, 95% non-condensing
  (`ct900ent-operating-environment-voltage`).
- **Do not use outdoors**, the same rule in short, as item 10 of the appliance safety list
  (`spirit-ct800-safety-instructions-list`).
- **A grounded outlet**, which the grounding page describes without saying anything about where the
  machine stands (`spirit-product-must-be-grounded`).

## Every other Spirit treadmill manual that answers this says something these two do not

| Manual | What it states |
|---|---|
| CT800 2016 and 2020, CT850 2016, 2018 and 2020, CT900ENT | ASTM F2115-05 6.1.2.11 - 0.5 m (19.7 in.) each side, 1 m (39 in.) behind (`ct900ent-clearance-requirements`) |
| CT800ENT-2022 | a minimum of 1 metre behind, no side figure (`ct800ent-2022-safety-placement-and-rear-clearance`) |
| XT 2015 manuals and XT485ENT | a flat level surface, and not on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet (`xt-2015-safety-placement-requirements`) |
| XT 2023 manuals | a location page with its own figures (`xt-2023-safety-placement-and-clearance`) |

**None of those figures may be quoted for a CT800-2012 or an XT685-2010.** The 19.7 in. and 39 in.
figures arrive in the CT800 family with the 2016 manual, and the flat-level-surface and carpet
rules arrive in the XT family with the 2015 manuals. Both of these manuals predate them.

**The CT800-2012 manual does require a dedicated circuit**, which is an electrical requirement and
not a placement one - a nominal 110-volt, 15-amp dedicated circuit
(`spirit-ct800-safety-outlet-110-volt-15-amp`). The XT685-2010 manual states no amp figure at all.
Neither sentence says anything about a flat or level floor; the `install the treadmill on a flat
level surface` wording belongs to the 2015 XT and ENT manuals.
