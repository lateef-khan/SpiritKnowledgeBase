The manual's Location Requirements, in full, for after the treadmill is assembled:

| Side | Clearance |
|---|---|
| Front | at least 20in |
| Each side | at least 20in |
| Behind | at least 6ft |

The manual notes that **the front of the unit is where the motor is**.

- **Install the treadmill where children and pets cannot access it.**
- **Always keep the area around the treadmill clear** of furniture, exercise equipment and
  other debris.
- **Do not install the treadmill on deeply padded, plush, or shag carpet.**

**The carpet rule is printed twice.** The safety instructions page states it as item 15 and
adds the reason: "Damage to both carpet and equipment may result"
(`xt-2023-safety-instructions-list`). Neither page mentions the other.

**These figures are not the ASTM ones printed for the commercial Spirit treadmills.** The
CT900 and CT900ENT manuals quote ASTM F2115-05 at 0.5 m (19.7 in.) each side and 1 m (39 in.)
behind. These five owner's manuals ask for 20in at the sides and **6ft** behind - a much
larger rear figure. Use the figure from the machine's own manual.
