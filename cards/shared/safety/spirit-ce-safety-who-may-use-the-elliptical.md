**Two bullets of the safety instructions answer this, and these manuals never gather them.**

> This appliance is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental
> capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge, unless they have been given supervision or
> instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible for their safety.

> Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine.

- **Under 13: keep away from the machine.** A keep-away rule, not a supervised-use rule, and it is
  the only age figure these manuals print.
- **Reduced physical, sensory or mental capability, or lack of experience and knowledge: not
  intended for that person - unless supervised or instructed** by a person responsible for their
  safety. The manuals offer that exception in their own words.
- **No maximum age, no other minimum, and no height qualification** appears in any of these eight
  manuals.

Six of the eight print `metal capabilities` where the word intended is *mental* - the CE800 2021 and
2023, the CE800ENT and the three CE850 manuals. The two CE900 manuals spell it `mental`. Nothing
about the rule changes.

**A separate bullet keeps children away from the machine as such**, with no age attached -
`Keep children away from the elliptical trainer. There are obvious pinch points and other caution
areas that can cause harm` (`spirit-2026t-safety-keep-children-away`). That rule and the age-13 rule
are printed in the same list and neither cancels the other.

## Two Spirit ellipticals are not covered by this card

**The CE800-2013 manual prints neither bullet.** Its safety list has no age rule and no
reduced-capability sentence anywhere, so neither may be quoted for a 2013 machine
(`spirit-ce800-safety-instructions-list`).

**The CES880-2025 manual prints the reduced-capability sentence without the exception, and no age
rule at all.** Item 1 of its list stops at `lack of experience and knowledge` - there is no
`unless they have been given supervision or instruction` clause - and there is no under-13 item
(`ces880-2025-safety-instructions-list`). Read as printed, that list allows no supervised use, which
is the opposite of the eight manuals above. Its only statement about children is on the cover
warning: keep children and pets away from the equipment when in use
(`ces880-2025-safety-warning-on-the-cover`).

**Do not answer an elliptical owner from the treadmill cards.** The CT800 and CT850 manuals print a
third bullet these ellipticals do not - `Close supervision is necessary when this appliance is used
by, on, or near children, invalids, or disabled persons`
(`spirit-ct800-safety-who-may-use-the-treadmill`) - and the 2015 XT manuals print the
reduced-capability sentence flatly, with no supervised exception
(`xt-2015-safety-who-may-use-the-treadmill`).
