**Every Xterra treadmill manual prints this warning.** The older-layout books say:

> Use caution while participating in other activities while walking on your treadmill; such as
> watching television, reading, etc. These distractions may cause you to lose balance or stray from
> walking in the center of the belt, which may result in serious injury.

The 2024 TRX books and the new-layout books drop the middle clause:

> Use caution while participating in other activities while walking on your unit; such as watching
> television, reading, etc. These distractions may cause you to lose balance which may result in
> serious injury.

Neither wording forbids it; both say the risk is losing your balance, and the older one names
wandering off the centre of the belt as the way it happens.

**The two Hiker treadmills go further** and forbid operating a tablet or laptop at high speed as a
numbered safety instruction (`xterra-tr-safety-no-tablet-or-laptop-at-high-speed`) - even though the
TR95H and TR75H ship with a desktop for one.

The Spirit XT-2023 prints the shorter wording for its own machines (`xt-2023-safety-distractions-while-walking`).
