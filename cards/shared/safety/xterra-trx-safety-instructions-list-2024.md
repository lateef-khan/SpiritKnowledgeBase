**All four 2024 TRX owner's manuals print this page word for word, except for the weight figure in the
last bullet.** It is the older XTERRA layout with the outlet warning, the extension-cord rule, the
rear-clearance bullet and the handrail bullet all removed.

> When using an electrical appliance, basic precautions should always be followed, including the
> following: Read all instructions before using this appliance.

> **DANGER - To reduce the risk of electric shock:** Always unplug this appliance from the electrical
> outlet immediately after using and before cleaning.

> **WARNING - To reduce the risk of burns, fire electric shock, or injury to persons:**

| # | Instruction |
|---|---|
| 1 | An appliance should never be left unattended when plugged in. Unplug from outlet when not in use, and before putting on or taking off parts. |
| 2 | Do not operate under blanket or pillow. Excessive heating can occur and cause fire, electric shock, or injury to persons. |
| 3 | This exercise equipment is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge. |
| 4 | Use this appliance only for its intended use as described in this manual. Do not use attachments not recommended by the manufacturer. |
| 5 | Never operate this appliance if it has a damaged cord or plug, if it is not working properly, if it has been dropped or damaged, or dropped into water. Return the appliance to a service center for examination and repair. |
| 6 | Do not carry this appliance by supply cord or use cord as a handle. |
| 7 | Keep the cord away from heated surfaces. |
| 8 | Never operate the appliance with the air openings blocked. Keep the air openings free of lint, hair, and the like. |
| 9 | Never drop or insert any object into any opening. |
| 10 | Do not use outdoors. |
| 11 | Do not operate where aerosol (spray) products are being use or where oxygen is being administered. |
| 12 | Connect this appliance to a properly grounded outlet only. See Grounding Instructions. |
| 13 | The appliance is intended for household use. |

`being use` in item 11 is the manual's own wording.

> SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!

Then the Proposition 65 warning (`xterra-treadmill-safety-proposition-65-warning`) and the bulleted
**Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions**:

- To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position, then remove the plug from the outlet.
- Do not operate equipment on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and equipment may result.
- Before beginning this or any exercise program, consult a physician. This is especially important for persons over the age of 35 or persons with pre-existing health conditions.
- Keep hands away from all moving parts.
- The pulse sensors are not medical devices. Various factors, including the user's movement, may affect the accuracy of heart rate readings. The pulse sensors are intended only as exercise aids in determining heart rate trends in general.
- Do not attempt to use your equipment for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended.
- Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable for use on your equipment. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue.
- **User Weight Limit: 300 lbs.** on the TRX2500; **User Weight Limit: 350 lbs.** on the TRX3500, TRX4500 and TRX5500

> Failure to follow all guidelines may compromise the effectiveness of the exercise experience,
> expose yourself (and possibly others) to injury, and reduce the longevity of the equipment.
> SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!

**What these four pages do not say**, and the 2021-layout page did: no `120-volt, 15-amp` outlet, no
`14AWG` extension cord, no `3 1/2 feet` behind the machine, no `Use handrails provided` and no
`remove tether cord` in the disconnect step. The only circuit figure in these books is the
`nominal 110-volt circuit` sentence on the grounding page (`xterra-trx-safety-grounding-nominal-110-volt-circuit`).
**No age rule is printed** anywhere in the four books.

The same page, with a different weight figure, is the Spirit CT800-2024 safety list
(`spirit-2024-safety-instructions-list-treadmill`) - a Spirit card, for Spirit machines only.
