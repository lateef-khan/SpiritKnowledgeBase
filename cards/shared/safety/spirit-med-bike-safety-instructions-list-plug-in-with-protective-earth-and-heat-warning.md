**Twenty-five items on the 8.0U and twenty-four on the 8.5R - the 7.0R's list with an
adjustment-lever rule and a heat warning added and the emergency brake renamed; the 8.0U alone
adds a protective earth rule and a shoes rule.**

> To reduce risk of injury to persons: Read all instructions before using this appliance.
>
> **! WARNING - To reduce the risk of electric shock,** disconnect your 8.0U from the electrical
> outlet prior to cleaning and/or service work.
>
> Read all instructions before using this appliance.

Beside items 19 to 21 is the boxed weight-and-power note the 2024 and 2025 Spirit manuals all carry.

**The 8.5R list is the 7.0R's 19 items, in the 7.0R order and wording, with five added at the
end.** Its items 1 to 19 are the 7.0R's 1 to 19 - no protective-earth item, the pulse-sensor item
at 8, the clearance at 9 with `three and a half feet at the rear` and no `(shown as below)`,
`recommended by Spirit by Dyaco` at 10 - and then 20 the optional crank arms, 21 the flywheel with
the manual mechanical brake lever, 22 the adjustment levers, 23 the heat warning and 24 `User
Weight Limit: 440 lbs.` It prints no shoes rule. The 8.5R writes `the 8.5R` where the 8.0U writes
`this equipment`, except in items 9 and 21.

- **`(shown as below)` points at a drawing the 8.0U book does not print.** Both pages were rendered;
  neither carries a clearance figure. The three figures are in the sentence alone
  (`spirit-med-bike-safety-clearance-two-feet-sides-one-front-three-and-a-half-rear`).
- **Item 3 is a medical-device requirement the 8.0U alone prints** - a supply main with
  protective earth - on top of the grounded outlet of item 2
  (`spirit-med-bike-safety-outlet-100-to-240-volt-15-amp`, `spirit-product-must-be-grounded`). The
  8.5R, 7.0R and 7.0U do not print it; their grounding page carries the rule instead.
- **Item 22 (8.5R item 21) names a manual mechanical brake lever** where the 7.0R and 7.0U call the
  same control an emergency brake lever, and adds `it is a speed independent system`
  (`spirit-med-bike-safety-no-freewheel-auto-brake-and-brake-lever`).
- **Item 24 (8.5R item 23) is the only heat warning in the Spirit bike range.** It names the motor or
  resistance system as the source and asks for ventilation, adjusted settings or breaks.
- **The list does not say who may not use the machine.** The contraindications are two pages later
  (`spirit-med-bike-safety-contraindications-osteoporosis-fractures-and-acute-conditions`).

**The 8.5UE ergometer prints 23 of these items** in the same words, with `handles` for `pedals`
(`85ue-2025-safety-instructions-list`) - a different product line with its own card.
