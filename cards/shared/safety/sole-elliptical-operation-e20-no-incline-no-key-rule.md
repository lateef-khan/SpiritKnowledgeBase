- **NEVER operate this elliptical without reading and completely understanding the results of any operational change you request from the computer.**
- Changes in **resistance do not occur immediately**. Set the desired level and release the adjustment key; the computer obeys gradually. No incline is named anywhere in the rule.
- **NEVER use your elliptical during an electrical storm**; household surges could damage components. **No unplug sentence is printed.**
- **Use caution with distractions** such as watching television or reading; they may cause you to lose balance, resulting in serious injury.
- **Always hold on to a handlebar while making control changes** (one word, no parenthetical).
- **No console-key pressure rule is printed** - the `little finger pressure` sentence every other B book carries is absent here, proved by full-text search plus the page render.
