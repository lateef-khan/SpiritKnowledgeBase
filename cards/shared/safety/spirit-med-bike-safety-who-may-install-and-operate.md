**Two profiles on the OPERATING PRINCIPLE page. The 7.0R and 7.0U print them in full; the 8.0U and
8.5R print a shortened form that keeps the two qualifications that matter.**

> **INTENDED OPERATOR** (7.0R, 7.0U)
> - There is no particular restriction on age, gender, height, weight, ability and culture.
> - Education: University or above
> - Knowledge: The operator should read the User's Manual before use.
> - Discipline: The operator should receive training from the manufacturer before use.
> - Experience: The operator must have experience in physical therapy.
> - Background: The operator must be a major in physical therapy.
> - Professional competence: The operator should have the physical therapist license.

> **INTENDED INSTALLER** (7.0R, 7.0U)
> - There is no particular restriction on age, gender, height, weight, ability and culture.
> - Education: High School or above
> - Knowledge: The installer shall be able to manipulate this product properly.
> - Discipline: The installer shall be given a specific training by manufacturer.
> - Experience: The installer must have experience in product assembly and disassembly.
> - Background: The installer must be electro-mechanically trained.
> - Professional competence: Normal vision ability required.

**The 8.0U and 8.5R print:**

> **INTENDED OPERATOR** - Education: medical professional or medical occupation with physical
> therapist license. Knowledge: the operator should read the user's manual before use.

> **INTENDED INSTALLER** - Education: high school or above. Knowledge/Experience: the installer shall
> be given a specific training by manufacturer. The installer must have experience in this
> equipment assembly and disassembly. Background: the installer must be electro-mechanically
> trained. Professional competence: normal vision ability required.

- **The operator is the therapist, not the patient.** `Operator` throughout these books is the person
  at the console setting the workload; the patient is the `user`. A patient exercising alone at
  home is outside the profile, though `home use` is a listed location
  (`spirit-med-bike-safety-intended-patient-population-and-medical-purpose`).
- **Manufacturer training** is asked of both operator and installer on the 7.0R and 7.0U, and of the
  installer alone on the 8.0U and 8.5R.
- **The safety list says the same about installation** - `Installation and assembly of this product
  should be performed by trained personnel only`, and servicing beyond the manual is for trained
  service personnel (`spirit-2026t-safety-servicing-only-by-trained-personnel` is the treadmill card
  for that sentence).

**The operating principle printed above the profiles**: the patient pushes the pedals; the operator
raises the workload from the console; an electromagnet energised in proportion to the workload
induces eddy currents in the flywheel, and the opposing field is the resistance at the pedal. The
8.5R says the operator selects `the increase/decrease indicators on the console`.

**The Dyaco MED 7.0R of 2021 prints the full 7.0R profile** on a page of its own headed `Intended
user profile`. The 7.0T and 8.0T treadmills print the shortened form
(`spirit-2026t-safety-who-may-install-and-operate`).
