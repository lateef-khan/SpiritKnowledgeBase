**These eight bikes have no mains plug, no power cord and no outlet requirement. There is no
voltage figure and no amperage figure to give, because they take no mains power.**

> Spirit Fitness commercial fitness bikes have a built-in generator for power and do not need to be
> plugged into an AC outlet. To power up the fitness bike simply start to pedal, the console will
> turn on automatically.

- **The rider is the power supply.** Pedalling spins the generator; the console comes up on its
  own and there is nothing to switch on.
- **No outlet, no circuit rating, no breaker, no extension cord, no grounding page.** None of the
  eight manuals prints any of these. That is an absence, not a figure of zero.
- **The generator is still a live AC source while the bike is turning**, which is why every one of
  these manuals prints a shock warning even with no wall socket in sight -
  `spirit-ce-safety-generator-produces-ac-power`.

The CR800-2021, CR800-2023 and CU800-2021 manuals name the machine rather than the range -
`The recumbent has a built-in generator` and `The bike has a built-in generator` - but the
statement and the consequence are the same in all eight.

## Not every Spirit commercial bike is self-powered

| Machine | Power | Card |
|---|---|---|
| CR900ENT-2021, CU900ENT | plugs in; **no voltage or amp figure printed** | `spirit-xe-safety-no-outlet-figure-printed` |
| CU800ENT-2022, CR800ENT-2023 | 110-volt, 5-amp grounded, dedicated circuit | `spirit-ce850-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement` |
| CU800ENT-2024 | 120-volt AC, 15-Amp grounded | `spirit-ct800-safety-outlet-120-volt-15-amp` |

The dividing line is the console, not the frame. Every bike in this wave with an entertainment
touchscreen plugs into a wall socket; every bike without one runs off its own generator. A CR800 and
a CR800ENT of the same year answer this question differently.

**Two quick checks tell the two families apart.** The five manuals here that carry a parts list -
CR800-2021, CR800-2023, CU800-2021, CR900-2025, CU900-2025 - list a `Generator/Brake`, a
`Generator/Brake Controller` and a generator wire harness, and no power cord; all five ENT manuals
list a `Power Cord` and no generator part at all. And the `IMPORTANT ELECTRICAL INSTRUCTIONS` page
tells the same story - every manual on this card opens it with the spinning-generator shock
warning, and no ENT manual prints that warning anywhere.

**The indoor cycles, air bikes and the Johnny G also need no outlet, and this card is not their
answer.** None of them has a generator - the word appears in none of those eight manuals - and the
CB900 and CIC800 have no console to power at all. See
`spirit-indoor-cycle-safety-no-mains-outlet-or-voltage`, which gives the battery type for each.
