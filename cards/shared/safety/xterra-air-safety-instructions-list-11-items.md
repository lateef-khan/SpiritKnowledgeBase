**Both air bike books print the same eleven items; only item 11, the weight limit, differs.**
It is the Dyaco indoor-cycle list (`xterra-mb-safety-instructions-list-11-items-no-freewheel`) with
the no-freewheel item removed and a weight limit added at the end.

> WARNING - Read all instructions before using this appliance.

| # | Instruction |
|---|---|
| 1 | Use this equipment only for its intended use as described in this manual. Do not attempt to ride this bike at high pedal speeds until you have ridden the bike for some time and are comfortable riding at slower pedal speeds. |
| 2 | Wait for flywheel to coast to a stop before dismounting the bike. (`xterra-air-safety-wait-for-flywheel-to-stop-before-dismounting`) |
| 3 | Serious injury or death may occur from over-training. Consult a medical doctor or qualified fitness instructor to determine an exercise program appropriate for your level of fitness. |
| 4 | Do not attempt to turn the pedal cranks by hand. Do not touch any driving mechanism while it is in motion as possible injury could occur. |
| 5 | In a home setting, keep children away from the bike when it is not in use. Keep children and pets away from the unit while it is in use. |
| 6 | Do not attempt to perform dip movements on handlebars. |
| 7 | Never drop or insert any object into any opening of the bike. |
| 8 | Only use the bike on a stable, level floor. |
| 9 | Follow instructions for safe use of the equipment including proper seat position, handlebar position, and use of foot positioning system of pedals. Do not attempt to pull up handlebar post and seat post over the 'MAX.' graduation. (`xterra-bike-safety-do-not-raise-seat-or-handlebar-post-past-max-mark`) |
| 10 | For safe operation, allow for at least 1 foot (30cm) of free space to either side of the unit and 2 feet (60cm) of free space to the rear of the unit. (`xterra-bike-safety-clearance-1-ft-sides-2-ft-rear`) |
| 11 | **Weight Limit: 250 lbs.** on the AIR350 (`xterra-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-250-lbs`); **Weight Limit: 350 lbs.** on the AIR650 (`air650-2021-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lbs`) |

Below the list, in both books: `Regularly examine the bike for damage and wear. Inoperable components
should be replaced immediately or the equipment should not be used until it is repaired.`, the
`Failure to follow all guidelines...` sentence, the Proposition 65 warning
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-proposition-65-warning`) and `SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!`.
The facing page is the six owner guidelines (`xterra-bike-safety-operation-instructions-owner-guidelines`).

**Neither book prints the word *fan*.** The moving-parts rule for the air bike is item 4 - do not
touch the driving mechanism in motion - and item 2's coasting flywheel; there is no separate
fan-guard, finger or hair warning, and no rule about riding standing up. The Spirit AB900, whose
service manual the AIR650 shares by the manufacturer's note, carries a different precautions list
with a standing-up ban (`spirit-air-bike-safety-user-precautions-list`); that is a Spirit card.
