**Both XRW600 printings carry this page and it is a mains-powered machine's page**, unlike the
CRW800 rower's (`crw800-2024-safety-instructions-list`), which opens straight at a bare WARNING and
names no supply at all.

> When using an electrical appliance, basic precautions should always be followed, including the
> following:
>
> Read all instructions before using this appliance.
>
> **DANGER - To reduce the risk of electric shock:** Always unplug this appliance from the
> electrical outlet immediately after using and before cleaning.
>
> **WARNING - To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons,** install
> the Rower on a flat level surface with access to a 110-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet with only the
> Rower plugged into the circuit.
>
> DO NOT USE AN EXTENSION CORD UNLESS IT IS A 14AWG OR BETTER, WITH ONLY ONE OUTLET ON THE END:

| # | Bullet |
|---|---|
| 1 | Do not operate Rower on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and Rower may result. |
| 2 | Keep children away from the Rower. There are obvious pinch points and other caution areas that can cause harm. |
| 3 | Keep hands away from all moving parts. |
| 4 | Never operate the Rower if it has a damaged cord or plug. If the Rower is not working properly, call your dealer. |
| 5 | Keep the cord away from heated surfaces. |
| 6 | Do not operate where aerosol spray products are being used or where oxygen is being administered. Sparks from the motor may ignite a highly gaseous environment. |
| 7 | Never drop or insert any object into any openings. |
| 8 | Do not use outdoors. |
| 9 | To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position, then remove the plug from the outlet. |
| 10 | Do not attempt to use your Rower for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended. |
| 11 | Use of a chest strap transmitter (sold separately) is an accurate method of heart rate analysis. Various factors, including the user's movement, may affect the accuracy of heart rate readings. |
| 12 | Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable for use on your Rower. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue. |
| 13 | This exercise equipment is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge. |
| 14 | Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine. |
| **15** | **Recommended Weight Limit: 350 lb - November 2021 printing only** |

> SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!

**The weight bullet is the only substantive difference between the two printings.** Measured on the
native PDF text of both safety chapters (printed pages 5 to 7), the two share **94.4%** of their
eight-word phrases; the changes are the added weight bullet, the Proposition 65 box moving from page
5 to page 6, and the registration URL. Nothing else on these pages differs. The 2019 book prints no
weight limit anywhere (`xt-2015-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`,
`spirit-rower-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lb`).

**Item 6 warns of sparks from the motor and the rower has no motor.** The sentence is carried over
from the mains-powered treadmill and elliptical manuals of the same range, and the book does not
reconcile it. The same defect sits in the CRW800 manuals.

**Item 11 says a chest strap is an accurate method.** These manuals print no counter-statement that
the sensors are not medical devices, unlike the treadmill and elliptical books of the same period.

**Item 13 allows no supervised exception.** The CRW800 rower's equivalent bullet adds `unless they
have been given supervision or instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible
for their safety`; the XRW600 books stop at `knowledge`
(`spirit-ce-safety-who-may-use-the-elliptical`).

**The electrical half of this chapter is on the next page**, under `Important Electrical
Instructions`: the ten-percent supply-voltage paragraph, the 40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit and 95%
non-condensing limits (`ct900ent-operating-environment-voltage`) and the high-inrush circuit-breaker
paragraph with its Grainger and QO120HM part numbers
(`spirit-house-breaker-needs-a-high-inrush-type`). Neither page carries a grounding page - these two
manuals have none - and neither carries a compliance or FCC page
(`crw800-2024-safety-no-compliance-page`).

**Do not answer a water rower owner from this page.** The CRW900 and CRW800H2O manuals print DO and
DO NOT lists in a different chapter shape that shares almost none of this text
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-do-and-do-not-lists`), and the 8.5UE upper body ergometer prints a
23-item medical-device list (`85ue-2025-safety-instructions-list`).
