All thirty-six CSS, CSD, CSF and CSI manuals show the same sticker on their product labels page,
headed **California Prop 65 Warning Decal (For US model only)**:

> **WARNING:** Cancer and Reproductive Harm - www.P65Warnings.ca.gov

That is the whole of it. This is California's **short-form** Proposition 65 warning: it names no
chemical, and the manuals give no further detail anywhere. A caller who wants to know which substance
is meant has to go to the P65Warnings web address; Spirit does not print it.

The decal is fitted to **US models only**, so a machine sold outside the United States may not carry
it and is not faulty for lacking it.

The three ST800 products carry a **longer and different** Proposition 65 warning that does name
chemicals - see the card for those.
