The console is tested to the limits for a **Class C Low Power Communication Device Transmitter under Part 15 of the FCC rules**.

Operation is subject to two conditions:

1. The device may not cause harmful interference.
2. The device must accept any interference received, including interference that may cause undesired operation.

There is no guarantee that interference will not happen in a particular installation. If a radio, television or other RF signal interferes with the console, increase the separation between the bike and whatever is emitting the signal.
