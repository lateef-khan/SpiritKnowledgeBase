**Both Hiker treadmill manuals move the serial sticker off the motor cover.**

> SERIAL NUMBER STICKER - This sticker will be found on the frame near the power switch. Please record
> the number below the barcode for the purpose of registering your Hiker Treadmill's warranty.

The power switch is at the front of the machine below the motor hood, so the sticker is at the front
end of the frame. The Proposition 65 sticker is beside it, `on the frame near the power switch`.

The TR75H draws the number as a run of sixteen `x`; the TR95H as `X XXXXXXXXX X`. Neither book gives
the number's format beyond that.

The other six new-layout books put it on the rear of the motor cover
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-serial-number-on-the-rear-of-the-motor-cover`). The Spirit CT800 books also
put theirs by the power switch (`spirit-ct800-safety-serial-number-and-product-labels`).
