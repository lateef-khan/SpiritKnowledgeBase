> Do not use excessive pressure on console control keys. They are precision set to function
> properly with little finger pressure.

Pressing harder is not the fix for a key that does not respond, and the manual warns against
it on the Electrical Safety page rather than in the console chapter.

If the machine does respond but the change seems slow, that is normal and is a different
thing: `xt-2023-safety-speed-and-incline-response-delay`.

**The five 2015 XT owner's manuals print the same sentence, word for word**, on their Important
Operation Instructions page, without the closing full stop. Pressing harder is not the fix in
either generation.
