> Use caution while participating in other activities while walking on your unit; such as
> watching television, reading, etc. These distractions may cause you to lose balance which
> may result in serious injury.

The manual does not forbid it. It asks for **caution**, and names the mechanism: a
distraction costs you your balance.

The safety instructions page adds the related rules that apply while you are on the machine -
keep hands away from all moving parts, and wear proper shoes
(`xt-2023-safety-instructions-list`).

**The five 2015 XT owner's manuals print the same sentence, word for word**, on their Important
Operation Instructions page. It is a request for caution in both generations, never a
prohibition, and it names the same mechanism: a distraction costs you your balance.

The 2015 manuals' related rules for while you are on the machine - hands away from moving parts,
proper shoes - are in their separately headed Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions list
(`xt-2015-safety-fitness-equipment-instructions`).

**The 2026 Spirit 4.0T owner's manual prints the same sentence**, with `while on your Treadmill`
where the XT manuals write `while walking on your unit`:

> Use caution while participating in other activities while on your Treadmill; such as watching
> television, reading, etc. These distractions may cause you to lose balance which may result in
> serious injury.

Still a request for caution, never a prohibition, and it names the same mechanism.

**The 2026 7.0T and 8.0T owner's manuals print no distraction warning.** Their Operation
Instructions page asks instead that the user face forward and not turn around or look backwards
while the belt is running (`80t-2026-safety-getting-on-and-off-the-treadmill`), which is a
different rule with a different reason.

**Both ENT owner's manuals print the same sentence.** The XT685ENT prints it on its Electrical
Safety page, page 7, saying `while walking on your unit`; the XT485ENT prints it on its Important
Operation Instructions page, page 8, saying `while walking on your treadmill`. A request for
caution in both, never a prohibition, and the same mechanism is named.

**All four Spirit CT800 owner's manuals print the same sentence**, and each names the activity
differently:

| Manual | Printed as |
|---|---|
| CT800 2012 | `while walking on your treadmilll` (the triple `l` is printed that way) |
| CT800 2016 | `while pedaling on your Treadmill` |
| CT800 2020 | `while pedaling on your treadmill` |
| CT800ENT-2022 | `while on your Treadmill` |

**`pedaling` is a defect, not a different rule.** The CT800 is a treadmill with no pedals; the 2016
and 2020 manuals carry a bike manual's verb through this whole page. Nothing else in the sentence
changes - it is a request for caution, never a prohibition, and it names the same mechanism, a
distraction costing you your balance. Read `pedaling` as `walking` for these two machines and do
not quote the word to a customer.
