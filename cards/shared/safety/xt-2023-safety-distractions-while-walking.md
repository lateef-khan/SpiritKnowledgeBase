> Use caution while participating in other activities while walking on your unit; such as
> watching television, reading, etc. These distractions may cause you to lose balance which
> may result in serious injury.

The manual does not forbid it. It asks for **caution**, and names the mechanism: a
distraction costs you your balance.

The safety instructions page adds the related rules that apply while you are on the machine -
keep hands away from all moving parts, and wear proper shoes
(`xt-2023-safety-instructions-list`).

**The five 2015 XT owner's manuals print the same sentence, word for word**, on their Important
Operation Instructions page. It is a request for caution in both generations, never a
prohibition, and it names the same mechanism: a distraction costs you your balance.

The 2015 manuals' related rules for while you are on the machine - hands away from moving parts,
proper shoes - are in their separately headed Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions list
(`xt-2015-safety-fitness-equipment-instructions`).
