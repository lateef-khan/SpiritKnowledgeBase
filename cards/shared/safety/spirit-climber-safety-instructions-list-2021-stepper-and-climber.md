**One page, printed twice for two different machines.** The CS800-2021 and CVC800-2021 safety
chapters match at 97.4% on an order-free word count of the printed pages. Three things differ and two
of them are figures.

> When using an electrical appliance, basic precautions should always be followed, including the
> following: read all instructions before using this appliance.

> **DANGER - To reduce the risk of electric shock:** always unplug this appliance from the electrical
> outlet immediately after using and before cleaning.

> **WARNING - To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons,** install the
> stepper on a flat level surface with access to a 110-volt, 5-amp grounded outlet with only the
> stepper plugged into the circuit. DO NOT USE AN EXTENSION CORD UNLESS IT IS A 14AWG OR BETTER, WITH
> ONLY ONE OUTLET ON THE END:

## What differs between the two books

| | CS800-2021 | CVC800-2021 |
|---|---|---|
| The noun | `stepper` throughout | `Vertical Climber` throughout |
| Outlet in the WARNING header | **110-volt, 5-amp** | **110-volt, 1.5-amp** |
| Item 15 | `Recommended User Weight Limit: 450 lb` | `User Weight Limit: 400 lb` |

**The CS800 book writes *Recommended* into item 15 and the CVC800 book does not.** Neither says what
happens above the limit.

**Never carry a figure across.** 450 lb and 5 amps are the CS800's; 400 lb and 1.5 amps are the
CVC800's. The CVC800's own service manual then gives a third outlet figure again
(`cvc800-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`).

**The 2024 CS800 and CRS800S print the same fifteen items with 15 amps in the header**
(`spirit-2024-safety-instructions-list-stepper`), so the amp figure is the thing to check before
answering, not the item list.

**No item on this page mentions the machine's own motion.** There is no mounting or dismounting rule,
no handrail rule and no rule about stepping off while the pedals move - on either machine.
