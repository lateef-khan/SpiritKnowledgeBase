**The live area shall be not less than 140 CM (55.12") greater than the training area in the directions from which the equipment is accessed. The live area must also include the area for emergency dismount.**

Word for word in all six books. The Complete book prints it in a bench-headed safety-instructions chapter; the other five in the strength-equipment chapter, and the SW100/SW155/SW180 books repeat the heading inside the safeguards page. This is the access-direction margin, not the SRVO-2021's 3.5-foot fixed-object clearance, which is a different figure on a different page.
