**This book disagrees with itself in the opposite direction from the E25/E35 books.** The safety list calls for a **110-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet** with a **14AWG-or-better** cord, but the grounding paragraph says the product is for use on a **nominal 115-volt circuit** - both figures render-verified in the E55-2016 and E55-2019 books. Neither book mentions GFCI or AFCI outlets.

The adapter, ear-lug and metal-screw rules match the other stencil books word for word.
