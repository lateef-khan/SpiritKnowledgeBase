**The three new-layout rower books have a PRODUCT LABELS page; the other eight rower books have no
such page** (two of them draw the stickers on their parts drawing instead -
`xterra-rower-safety-serial-number-sticker-locations`). The sticker images are pictures; their text
was read from the render.

| Sticker | ERG180 (2023) | ERG800W (2023) | ERG750W (2025) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Serial number** - a barcode with the number under it and a phone number | `on the center frame, near the pedals` - 870-333-5500 | `on the frame behind the water tank` - (870) 336-4286 | `on the frame` - (870) 333-5500 |
| **Proposition 65** - `WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm - www.P65Warnings.ca.gov` | `on the center frame, near the pedals` | `on the rear stabilizer` | `on the rear stabilizer` |
| **Safety warning** - the bilingual seven-point panel | `on the center frame, near the pedals` | `on the frame behind the water tank` | **not printed** |

The page's own words (ERG800W):

> SERIAL NUMBER STICKER - This sticker will be found on the frame behind the water tank. Please record
> the number below the barcode for the purpose of registering your rower's warranty.

> PROPOSITION 65 WARNING - This sticker will be found on the rear stabilizer. The State of California
> requires us to inform you that this unit was manufactured using chemicals that could cause harm
> with improper use.

> SAFETY WARNING STICKER - This sticker will be found on the frame behind the water tank. The same
> warning can be found in this manual. Please read and be aware of the precautions before operating
> your unit.

All three pages name rower parts - none of the treadmill sentences the SB600 page carried
(`xterra-bike-safety-product-labels-page`). The ERG180 drawing points the serial sticker and the
safety sticker at the same stretch of center frame under the pedals. **The ERG750W's registration page prints a different phone number
from its sticker** - `(870) 335-5500` on the page, `(870) 333-5500` on the sticker and on every other
Xterra book; the sticker's number is the one every other book prints, and this card follows it.

The sticker wording is on `xterra-rower-safety-warning-sticker-bilingual-children-under-13`; the older
sticker with the yellow belt mark on `xterra-erg-safety-warning-sticker-with-the-yellow-belt-mark`; the
chemicals warning's text on `xterra-treadmill-safety-proposition-65-warning`. The Spirit water rowers
print their labels for their own machines (`crw800h2o-safety-warning-labels-and-sticker-locations`).
