Printed in a box beside the Important Safety Instructions list, in all five manuals:

> **WARNING:** This product can expose you to chemicals including Toluene and Acrylamide which
> are known to the State of California to cause cancer and birth defects or other reproductive
> harm. For more information go to www.P65Warnings.ca.gov

- **Two chemicals are named: Toluene and Acrylamide.**
- **The harms named are cancer, birth defects, and other reproductive harm.**
- **The reference given is www.P65Warnings.ca.gov.** No other advice, no exposure limit and no
  service action is printed.

**These manuals print the warning in the manual, not as a sticker.** They never say a Proposition
65 label is applied to the machine, and they have no product labels page at all
(`xt-2015-safety-product-labels-and-serial-number`). Do not tell a customer where the sticker is
from this card.

## What changed by 2023

**The 2023 XT manuals print a different Proposition 65 text and put it on the machine.** Theirs
reads only `The State of California requires us to inform you that this unit was manufactured
using chemicals that could cause harm with improper use`, on a PRODUCT LABELS page describing a
sticker on the interior side of the upright. It names **no chemical** and prints **no web
address** - see `xt-2023-safety-proposition-65-sticker`.

So the two are not interchangeable in either direction: Toluene and Acrylamide are named only by
the 2015 manuals, and the sticker location is given only by the 2023 manuals.

**The XT485ENT owner's manual prints this box word for word**, beside its safety instructions list
on page 5 - Toluene and Acrylamide, cancer and birth defects or other reproductive harm, and
`www.P65Warnings.ca.gov`. Like the 2015 manuals it prints the warning **in the manual** and
describes no sticker; it has no product labels page
(`xt-2015-safety-product-labels-and-serial-number`).

**The XT685ENT manual prints the other Proposition 65 text**, on a PRODUCT LABELS page describing
a sticker on the interior side of the upright, naming no chemical and no web address:
`xt-2023-safety-proposition-65-sticker`. The two are not interchangeable in either direction.

**Three of the four Spirit CT800 owner's manuals print this box word for word** - the 2016, the
2020 and the ENT-2022 - naming Toluene and Acrylamide, cancer and birth defects or other
reproductive harm, and `www.P65Warnings.ca.gov`. They print it beside their Important Operation
Instructions rather than beside the safety list, and they capitalise `Cancer`.

Like the 2015 manuals they print the warning **in the manual** and describe no sticker; no CT800
manual has a product labels page (`spirit-ct800-safety-serial-number-and-product-labels`).

**The CT800-2012 manual prints no Proposition 65 warning at all.** It names no chemical and gives
no web address anywhere. Do not carry Toluene and Acrylamide back onto a 2012 machine.

**All four Spirit CT850 owner's manuals print this box word for word**, beside their Important
Operation Instructions as the CT800 manuals do, capitalising `Cancer` in the same place. Toluene
and Acrylamide, cancer and birth defects or other reproductive harm, and `www.P65Warnings.ca.gov`.

Like the other manuals they print the warning **in the manual** and describe no sticker; no CT850
owner's manual has a product labels page
(`spirit-ct800-safety-serial-number-and-product-labels`).

**The extracted text of the CT850ENT-2022 manual splits the box across two pages.** Its page 7
carries the opening as far as `...cause Cancer and birth defects or`, and the closing words
`other reproductive harm. For more information, go to www.P65Warnings.ca.gov` sit at the top of the
extracted page 3, beside the `Serial Number Location` illustration. The two halves are the same
sentence quoted above; every chemical, harm and web address is present.
