**All four machines rate the rider at 350 lb. Three print it as a limit; the AB900 prints it as a
maximum that should not be exceeded, and is the only one of the four to give a metric figure.**

| Machine | Exactly as printed | Where |
|---|---|---|
| CIC800-2021 | `User Weight Limit: 350 lb` | Last bullet of the safety list, printed page 6 |
| CIC850-2022 | `User Weight Limit: 350 lb` | Last bullet of the safety list, printed page 7 |
| AB950-2024 | `User Weight Limit: 350 lbs.` | Item 13 of the numbered safety list, printed page 4 |
| AB900-2018 | `The maximum weight for individuals riding the bike should not exceed 350 pounds (159 kilograms).` | SAFEGUARDS, printed page 16 |

- **159 kilograms is printed only by the AB900.** The other three give pounds alone. 350 lb is
  158.8 kg, so the AB900's conversion is the same figure rounded, not a different rating.
- **The AB950 and the CIC manuals put it in the safety list; the AB900 does not.** The AB900's
  safety chapter (printed page 5) states no weight at all, and a reader who stops there will find
  nothing - see `spirit-air-bike-safety-user-precautions-list`. The figure is eleven pages later on
  the SAFEGUARDS page.
- **The AB950 prints a boxed callout beside its list**: `Please ensure that you review and adhere to
  the user weight restrictions of your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or
  damage to your machine.` The CIC manuals print no such box.

## Three other figures are in use across this family

**Do not answer any of these machines with another's number.**

- **XIC600-2018 and XIC600-2021: 300 lbs** - `spirit-xic600-safety-user-weight-limit-300-lbs`.
- **JB950-2022: 450 lb in one place and 350lbs in another**, a contradiction inside a single book -
  `jb950-2022-safety-user-weight-limit-450-and-350`.
- **CB900-2013: no limit printed anywhere** - `cb900-2013-safety-instructions-list`.

The commercial CR and CU upright and recumbent bikes are rated 450 lb
(`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`), 100 lb above these four.
