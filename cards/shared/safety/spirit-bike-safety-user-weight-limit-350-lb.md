**All four machines rate the rider at 350 lb. Three print it as a limit; the AB900 prints it as a
maximum that should not be exceeded, and is the only one of the four to give a metric figure.**

| Machine | Exactly as printed | Where |
|---|---|---|
| CIC800-2021 | `User Weight Limit: 350 lb` | Last bullet of the safety list, printed page 6 |
| CIC850-2022 | `User Weight Limit: 350 lb` | Last bullet of the safety list, printed page 7 |
| AB950-2024 | `User Weight Limit: 350 lbs.` | Item 13 of the numbered safety list, printed page 4 |
| AB900-2018 | `The maximum weight for individuals riding the bike should not exceed 350 pounds (159 kilograms).` | SAFEGUARDS, printed page 16 |

- **159 kilograms is printed only by the AB900.** The other three give pounds alone. 350 lb is
  158.8 kg, so the AB900's conversion is the same figure rounded, not a different rating.
- **The AB950 and the CIC manuals put it in the safety list; the AB900 does not.** The AB900's
  safety chapter (printed page 5) states no weight at all, and a reader who stops there will find
  nothing - see `spirit-air-bike-safety-user-precautions-list`. The figure is eleven pages later on
  the SAFEGUARDS page.
- **The AB950 prints a boxed callout beside its list**: `Please ensure that you review and adhere to
  the user weight restrictions of your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or
  damage to your machine.` The CIC manuals print no such box.

## Three other figures are in use across this family

**Do not answer any of these machines with another's number.**

- **XIC600-2018 and XIC600-2021: 300 lbs** - `spirit-xic600-safety-user-weight-limit-300-lbs`.
- **JB950-2022: 450 lb in one place and 350lbs in another**, a contradiction inside a single book -
  `jb950-2022-safety-user-weight-limit-450-and-350`.
- **CB900-2013: no limit printed anywhere** - `cb900-2013-safety-instructions-list`.

The commercial CR and CU upright and recumbent bikes are rated 450 lb
(`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`), 100 lb above these four.

## Six Spirit residential bikes print 350 lb, and the figure was added rather than changed

| Machine | Exactly as printed | Where |
|---|---|---|
| XBR55ENT-2021 | `Recommended Weight Limit: 350 lb` | Last bullet of the safety list, printed page 4 |
| XBU55ENT-2021 | `Recommended Weight Limit: 350 lb` | Last bullet of the safety list, printed page 4 |
| XBR25-2023 | `User Weight Limit: 350 lbs.` | Item 21 of the numbered safety list, printed page 4 |
| XBR55-2023 | `User Weight Limit: 350 lbs.` | Item 21 of the numbered safety list, printed page 5 |
| XBU55-2023 | `User Weight Limit: 350 lbs.` | Item 21 of the numbered safety list, printed page 5 |
| XBR95-2023 | `User Weight Limit: 350 lbs.` | Item 13 of the numbered safety list, printed page 5 |

- **The two 2021 ENT manuals print it as `Recommended`**, not as a maximum, and say nothing about
  what happens above it. The four 2023 manuals print it as `User Weight Limit`. Same figure, two
  words for it.
- **No kilogram equivalent is printed by any of the six.** 159 kg is the AB900's conversion and
  belongs to that manual alone.
- **It is the only weight figure in all six books.** None of them has a specification table carrying
  a user capacity, so the safety list is the whole answer.
- **The four 2023 manuals repeat the boxed callout beside the list**: `Please ensure that you review
  and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of your new machine. Failure to
  do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine.`

**The eight earlier XB manuals print no weight limit at all** - XBR95 2016, 2018 and 2021, XBR55
2019 and 2021, XBU55 2019 and 2021, and XBR25-2019
(`xt-2015-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`). The figure was **added** between printings of the same
book, not changed: an XBU55-2019 owner and an XBU55ENT-2021 owner hold nearly the same manual, and
only the later one carries a limit. Do not tell an owner of one of the eight that their machine has
no limit, and do not read 350 lb back onto it as their manual's answer.

**450 lb is the Spirit commercial bike figure and never a residential one**
(`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`); 300 lbs is the XIC600's
(`spirit-xic600-safety-user-weight-limit-300-lbs`).
