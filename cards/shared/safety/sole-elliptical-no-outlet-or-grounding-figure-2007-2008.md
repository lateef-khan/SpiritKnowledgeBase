No 2007/2008 book prints an outlet voltage, an amperage, an extension-cord gauge or a grounding section. The only power sentences are the disconnect rule (`turn all controls to the off position, then remove the plug from the outlet`) and the warranty's `proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage` - a condition with no figure. Do not quote 110-volt, 14 AWG or any adapter rule for these machines; those paragraphs first appear in the 2009 WE books.
