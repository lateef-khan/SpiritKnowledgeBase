**Item 15 of the safety list, on all four books:**

> Do not press the safety lock or turn the weight selector dial when the dumbbell or handle is lifted
> from the dumbbell tray.

The Instructions page explains why it is mechanically the rule as well: `Choose the desired weight by
rotating the middle of the handle grip. The handle must be fully inserted into the dumbbell tray to
adjust weight.` and `Remove the handle directly up from the base. The unused plates will remain in
the base.` The tray holds the plates the selection leaves behind; out of the tray, a turned dial or a
pressed lock releases plates with nothing under them. The troubleshooting page adds that the dial
will not turn unless the handle is fully seated - `If not fully inserted, the locking mechanism will
not be released` - and that plates in the wrong groove or facing backwards (`the selection tongue`
must face away from the grip) jam it.

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| seat the handle fully in the tray, then turn the grip to the weight | turn the dial in the air |
| lift straight up | press the lock while lifting |
| keep the tray on the floor (item 19) | drop or bump the dumbbell (items 16-17: `xterra-strength-safety-never-drop-bump-or-disassemble-and-keep-the-trays-on-the-floor`) |

The stand's book prints the same item for dumbbells it does not include. How the selector and lock
work is an assembly and console matter; this card is the rule. The Spirit weight-stack machines have
their own pin rule (`spirit-strength-safety-weight-selector-pin-rules`).
