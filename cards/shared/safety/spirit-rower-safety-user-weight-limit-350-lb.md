**350 lb on both machines, and neither manual says what happens above it.**

| Manual | Printed as | Where |
|---|---|---|
| XRW600-2021 | `Recommended Weight Limit: 350 lb` | last bullet of the safety instructions list |
| CRW800H2O | `The maximum weight for individuals riding the rower should not exceed 350 lbs.` | a bullet of the SAFEGUARDS list |

**One calls it a recommendation and the other a maximum.** The figure is 350 on both. Quote 350 lb
either way, and do not present the XRW600 wording as a higher allowance or the CRW800H2O wording as
a hard cut-off - neither book says.

**Neither prints a metric figure.** No kilogram conversion appears in either manual, and neither
carries a specification table with a user capacity.

**Every other Spirit rower prints something different, and none of them answers for these two.**

| Rower | Printed limit |
|---|---|
| XRW600-**2019** | **none printed at all** (`xt-2015-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`) |
| CRW800-2021, CRW800-2024 | 450 lb (`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`) |
| CRW900-2021 | 450 lbs in its text and 330 lbs on its own decal (`crw900-2021-safety-user-weight-limit-contradiction`) |

**The XRW600 pair is the trap in this family.** The February 2019 and November 2021 books are the
same machine name and the same book - their safety chapters share 94.4% of their eight-word phrases
- and the 2021 printing's added weight bullet is the one substantive change between them. An owner
with the 2019 book will find no limit in it. Say which printing the 350 lb comes from rather than
telling them their machine has none.

**The CRW800H2O is not the CRW800.** They are different machines with different manuals: the water
rowing machine is 350 lbs here and the air rower 450 lb. Check which is in front of you before
quoting either.

**Do not answer from the bike or elliptical cards.** Ten Spirit indoor cycles, air bikes and XB
bikes print the same 350 lb figure (`spirit-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lb`) and the XE195
elliptical prints `Recommended Weight Limit: 350 lb` word for word
(`xe195-2021-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lb`). Those are other machines' manuals; this card is the
rowers' own.
