**All three indoor cycle books print this banner on the Basic Operation page**, after the
seat-and-handlebar fitting instructions and before the resistance and braking steps:

> WARNING! IF AT ANY TIME DURING YOUR WORKOUT, YOU FEEL CHEST PAIN, EXPERIENCE SEVERE MUSCULAR
> DISCOMFORT, FEEL FAINT, OR ARE SHORT OF BREATH, STOP EXERCISING AT ONCE. IF THE CONDITION
> PERSISTS, YOU SHOULD CONSULT YOUR MEDICAL DOCTOR IMMEDIATELY.

| Symptom | Do |
|---|---|
| chest pain, severe muscular discomfort, faintness, shortness of breath | stop at once |
| it persists | see a doctor immediately |

It is the only stop-exercising rule in any of the thirty bike, elliptical and stepper books
except the safety warning sticker on the SB240 and SB600, which says `Stop immediately if you become
dizzy or experience chest pains and consult your physician`
(`xterra-sb-safety-warning-sticker-on-the-upright`). The over-training item of the safety list and the
owner guidelines say to get medical clearance before starting
(`xterra-bike-safety-operation-instructions-owner-guidelines`); this is the rule for during the ride.

The Sole SB700 and early SB900 indoor cycles print the same banner for their own machines
(`sole-indoor-cycle-stop-exercising-if-you-feel-unwell`).
