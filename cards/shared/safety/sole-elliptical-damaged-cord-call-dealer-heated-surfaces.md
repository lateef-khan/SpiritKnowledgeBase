- **Never operate the elliptical if it has a damaged cord or plug. If it is not working properly, call your dealer.**
- **Keep the cord away from heated surfaces.**

The E20-2016/2020 pair adds dropped, damaged and dropped-in-water cases and says to return the appliance to a service center instead of calling the dealer.
