All six strength books say **RECORD YOUR SERIAL NUMBER** with a blank, ask for the dealer name, phone and purchase date, and want the self-addressed product registration card completed and mailed or registered online (soletreadmills.com, soletreadmill.com in the Complete book's OCR).

No on-machine location is ever named on any registration or safety page - no decal, plate, frame or console position. Registration and warranty terms themselves belong to the warranty section. The same fact for the elliptical line lives on the linked card.
