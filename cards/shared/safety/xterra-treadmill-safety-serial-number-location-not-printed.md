**These eleven books ask you to write the serial number down and never say where to read it.**

> RECORD YOUR SERIAL NUMBER - Please record the Serial Number of this fitness product in the space
> provided below. Serial Number: ________

The only other mentions are the welcome letter (`Please have your model number and serial number handy
when you call`) and the warranty exclusion for products `with original serial numbers that have been
removed or altered`. There is no Product Labels page and no sticker drawing.

How it was checked: every `serial` in each book's text read, and the registration page rendered and
read as a picture.

**Where it probably is**: the eight new-layout books, for machines of the same generation, put the
sticker on the rear of the motor cover (`xterra-treadmill-safety-serial-number-on-the-rear-of-the-motor-cover`)
or, on the Hiker machines, on the frame by the power switch
(`xterra-tr-safety-serial-number-on-the-frame-near-the-power-switch`). Offer those as places to look,
not as what this manual says.

The Spirit CE ellipticals have the same gap (`spirit-ce-safety-serial-number-location`).
