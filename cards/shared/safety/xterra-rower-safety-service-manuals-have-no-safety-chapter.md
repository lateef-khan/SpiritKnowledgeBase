**None of the six rower service manuals prints a safety, precautions or warnings chapter.** They are
part-replacement books - contents, an exploded view or parts list, and photo steps - and the only
cautions in them are inside a step.

| Service manual | What it is | The only caution-like lines |
|---|---|---|
| ERG160 (V1.0, 2025, 15 pp) | photo steps with short captions; its contents page reprints the owner's manual drawing with the *Warning Label* and *Serial Number Sticker* callouts (`xterra-erg-safety-warning-sticker-with-the-yellow-belt-mark`) | none |
| ERG180 (V1.0, 2025, 15 pp) | photo steps | `Use appropriate tools to remove the belt wheel screws` |
| ERG550W (November 2022, 20 pp) | mostly pictures; a Q&A page | the no-display answer: `Console set this regulation to protect children playing this rowing machine` - a console behaviour, not a rule |
| ERG600W (22 pp) | photo steps with captions | none |
| ERG750W (26 pp) | photo steps; a console troubleshooting table | `Exercise caution during this procedure to prevent wire damage` |
| ERG800W (R80 V1.0, 2025, 39 pp) | parts list, electronic system, replacement guides | `Caution: Ensure that the Sensor Cable (H1) does not drop into the Steel Tube` |

Nothing in any of them says to disconnect power, remove the batteries or drain the tank before a
repair, and none prints a weight limit, a clearance, a sticker or an outlet rule. The technician's
safety rules for these machines are the owner's manual's
(`xterra-rower-safety-battery-console-no-power-adapter`, `xterra-erg-safety-external-power-supply-and-power-jack`).

How it was checked: every page of each service manual read, including the OCR supplements that
carry most of their text; the text searched for `safety`, `warning`, `caution`, `danger`,
`precaution`, `disconnect`, `unplug`, `weight`, `serial` and `ground`. The Xterra SB600 and TRX5500
service manuals have the same gap (`sb600-2023-safety-service-manual-has-no-safety-chapter`,
`trx5500-2024-safety-service-manual-has-no-safety-chapter`).
