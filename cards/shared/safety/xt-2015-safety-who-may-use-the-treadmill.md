Three separate bullets on the Important Safety Instructions page answer this, and the manual
never gathers them:

> Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine.

> Close supervision is necessary when this appliance is used by, on, or near children, invalids,
> or disabled persons.

> This exercise equipment is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or
> mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge.

- **Under 13: keep away from the machine.** This is a keep-away rule, not a supervised-use rule.
- **Children, invalids or disabled persons near the machine: close supervision.** The rule covers
  being *near* the treadmill as well as using it.
- **Reduced physical, sensory or mental capability, or lack of experience and knowledge: the
  equipment is not intended for that person.** The manual states this flatly and offers no
  supervised exception.

**No maximum age and no minimum user age other than 13 is printed**, and no weight or height
qualification appears here. The manual's one other user-suitability rule is medical: consult a
physician before beginning, especially over the age of 35 or with a pre-existing health condition
(`xt-2015-safety-fitness-equipment-instructions`).

The XT285-2015 manual writes the supervision bullet as `when this exercise equipment is used`;
the other four write `when this appliance is used`. Same rule.

**The 2023 XT manuals dropped both children clauses.** Their 21-item safety list keeps only the
reduced-capability sentence and carries no age-13 rule and no close-supervision rule; their
location page says instead to install the treadmill where children and pets cannot access it. So
the age-13 figure is a 2015 statement - do not quote it for a 2023 XT, and do not tell the owner
of a 2015 XT that the manual is silent on children. See `xt-2023-safety-instructions-list`.

The Sole bikes carry a separate under-13 rule of their own
(`sole-bike-children-under-13`); it is a different brand's manual and not an answer for an XT.

**The XT485ENT owner's manual prints all three bullets**, on its safety instructions page, page 5,
in the `when this appliance is used` wording. Under 13 keep away, close supervision near children,
invalids or disabled persons, and the equipment not intended for a person with reduced physical,
sensory or mental capabilities.

**The XT685ENT manual keeps only the reduced-capability sentence**, as the five 2023 XT manuals do.
It carries no age-13 rule and no close-supervision rule; its location page says instead to install
the treadmill where children and pets cannot access it. The age-13 figure may not be quoted for an
XT685ENT - see `xt-2023-safety-instructions-list`.
