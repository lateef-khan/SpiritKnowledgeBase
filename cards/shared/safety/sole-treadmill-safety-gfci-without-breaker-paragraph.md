The 2006-family books and the 2007 printings print the GFCI rule but no house-breaker paragraph, so they are not named on the GFCI-and-breaker card. Three wordings:

| Books | Wording |
|---|---|
| VF63, VF80/VF83/VF85, UF80/UF83/UF85 | **NEVER use a ground fault circuit interrupt (GFCI) wall outlet with this treadmill. As with any appliance with a large motor, the GFCI will trip often. Route the power cord away from any moving part of the treadmill including the elevation mechanism and transport wheels.** |
| VS73/VS77, VTT8, WS73/WS77 | **NEVER use a GFCI wall outlet with this treadmill. Large switching currents of the drive motor will trip GFCI type outlets, as will any appliance with a large motor.** Same cord-routing sentence follows. |
| 2006 families (F63/UF63, F80 family, TT8/UTT8) | The short form: **NEVER use a GFCI wall outlet with this treadmill.** Same cord-routing sentence follows, and nothing else. |

The books that also print the high-inrush breaker paragraph are on the GFCI-and-breaker card.
