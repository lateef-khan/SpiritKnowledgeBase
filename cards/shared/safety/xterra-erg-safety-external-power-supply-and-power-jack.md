**Three of the eleven rowers plug into the wall through an external adaptor; the other eight have a
battery console and nothing to plug in** (`xterra-rower-safety-battery-console-no-power-adapter`). No
book, and not the ERG800W service manual either, prints the adaptor's voltage, current or plug type.

| Rower | Where the book says it plugs in |
|---|---|
| ERG400 (2015) | the Power paragraph on the registration page: `The power supply must be plugged into the power jack, located on the front of the unit near the stabilizer tube.` |
| ERG700 (2022) | the Power paragraph: `The power supply must first be plugged into the power jack on the unit.` - no location |
| ERG800W (2023) | `Adaptor` (K) in the Parts Included list; assembly figure 12, `How to Use the Adaptor - Plug the adaptor (K) like the below graphic.` The drawing shows a wall adaptor with its lead into a jack low on the front face of the main upright, just above the front stabilizer. The service manual replaces the `DC Power Wire` through the console tube, with a `2-Pin Connector (DC Power Cable)`. |

The ERG400 and ERG700 Power paragraph, the same one nine Xterra bikes and ellipticals print
(`xterra-safety-external-power-supply-and-power-jack`):

> Power - This product uses an external power supply. The power supply must [first] be plugged into
> the power jack [...]. Next, plug the power supply into the appropriate wall outlet. When not in use,
> it is recommended to unplug the power supply from the wall outlet.
>
> IMPORTANT: If the product has been exposed to cold temperatures, allow the product sit in room
> temperature to warm up before plugging in the power supply. Failure to do so may result in damage
> to the display or electronic components.
>
> IMPORTANT: Do not operate the product if the power supply, its plug, or its cord has been damaged.

| Rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| Unplug when not in use | a recommendation; item 1 of the ERG400 and ERG800W lists says it again |
| Cold machine | let it reach room temperature first; the risk named is the display and electronics |
| Damaged supply, plug or cord | do not operate |

**The safety lists do not match the power.** The ERG400 and ERG800W print the plug-in list with the
cord items and a grounded-outlet item (`xterra-erg-safety-instructions-list-13-item-plug-in-list`,
`erg800w-2023-safety-instructions-list-21-items`); the ERG700, which also plugs in, prints the eleven-item
list with no cord item at all (`xterra-erg-safety-instructions-list-11-items-and-9-bullets-no-weight-limit`).
Only the ERG800W has an electrical page (`erg800w-2023-safety-electrical-safety-page`), and only it
references grounding instructions it does not print
(`erg800w-2023-safety-grounding-instructions-referenced-but-not-printed`). How the ERG400 and ERG700
consoles behave when plugged in is a console matter.
