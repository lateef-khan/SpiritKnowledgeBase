**The 7.0T and 8.0T state it as one instruction:**

> 7. Keep the electrical cord away from heated surfaces and out of all travel lanes and do not
>    operate the 7.0T if the cord or plug is damaged.

**The 4.0T splits the same ground across two items, and says what to do instead:**

> 14. Never operate the product if it has a damaged cord or plug, if the product is not working
>     properly, call your dealer. If it has been dropped or damaged, or dropped into water, call
>     your dealer.

> 15. Keep the cord away from heated surfaces.

- **Heated surfaces: all three.**
- **Out of all travel lanes: the 7.0T and 8.0T only.** The 4.0T manual does not mention walkways,
  so do not present a trip-hazard rule as its instruction.
- **A damaged cord or plug stops the machine being used, on all three.**
- **Call your dealer** is the 4.0T's action, and it extends the rule past the cord - a machine that
  is not working properly, has been dropped or damaged, or has been dropped into water is a dealer
  call too. The 7.0T and 8.0T manuals name no contact in this item.

**Routing the cord away from the machine's own moving parts is a different rule** - the elevation
mechanism and the transport wheels - and only the 4.0T of these three prints it:
`spirit-route-the-power-cord-away-from-moving-parts`.

What the cord must be plugged into is `spirit-2026t-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`.
