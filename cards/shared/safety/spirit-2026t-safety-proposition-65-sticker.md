**The sticker is on the back of the motor cover** - the same place these manuals put the serial
number sticker, and **not** where the XT manuals put it.

> PROPOSITION 65 WARNING. This sticker will be found on the back of the motor cover. The State of
> California requires us to inform you that this unit was manufactured using chemicals that could
> cause harm with improper use.

All three 2026 manuals print this page in the same words.

**That is all these three manuals say about it.** They name no chemical, print no
`www.P65Warnings.ca.gov` address, and give no exposure advice.

**Do not answer a 2026 owner from the XT card.** The five 2023 XT manuals put the same sticker on
the interior side of the treadmill's upright (`xt-2023-safety-proposition-65-sticker`), and the
2015 XT manuals name two chemicals in their wording (`xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`).
Sending an owner to the upright of a machine that carries the label on the motor cover wastes the
call.

These three manuals print only two labels on this page: this one and the serial number sticker
(`xt-2023-safety-serial-number-sticker`). They describe no warning sticker on the upright, and no
compliance or FCC page.
