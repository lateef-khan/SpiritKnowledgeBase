**Seven bike books print the same clearance sentence in their safety list**, and two of them add a
second, larger figure later in the book.

> For safe operation, allow for at least 1 foot (30cm) of free space to either side of the unit and
> 2 feet (60cm) of free space to the rear of the unit.

| Side | Minimum |
|---|---|
| either side | 1 foot (30 cm) |
| rear | 2 feet (60 cm) |
| front | no figure |

It is item 10 on the AIR350 and AIR650, item 11 on the MB500, MB550 and MBX2500, item 9 on the SB120
and part of item 8 on the UB120. The sentence before it on every list is `Only use the bike on a
stable, level floor.`

**The SB120 and UB120 print a second figure** on their Final Considerations page:

> Place the bike in a such a way that there is room on all side of the bike to safely mount and
> dismount. We recommend a minimum of 2 feet all around the bike to safely access the bike.

The two do not agree - one foot at the sides against two feet all round. This card follows the
safety list as the minimum the manual requires and the Final Considerations sentence as the
recommendation for getting on and off a recumbent or upright; a customer with the room should give
two feet all round. The five other books print only the safety-list figure.

The other twenty-three bike, elliptical and stepper books print no clearance figure at all
(`xterra-safety-no-clearance-figure-printed`). The treadmill books give far larger figures
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-location-20-in-front-and-sides-6-ft-behind`). The Spirit CIC and Sole SB indoor
cycles print this sentence for their own machines (`spirit-indoor-cycle-safety-floor-and-clearance`,
`sole-indoor-cycle-space-and-floor`).
