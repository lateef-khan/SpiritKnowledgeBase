- **Never operate the fitness bike if it has a damaged cord or plug.** If the fitness bike is not working properly, **call your dealer**.
- **Keep the cord away from heated surfaces.**

**The LCB 2013 and LCR 2013 manuals print neither rule.** Their safety page has no cord or plug item of any kind, so nothing above can be quoted for lcb-2013 or lcr-2013.

A related rule lives on another page: **NEVER remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power.** That one is printed only in the nine manuals that have an IMPORTANT ELECTRICAL INSTRUCTIONS section, and it is already carried by the electrical requirements card.
