**The Dyaco service manuals print an outlet, cord and circuit page that no Xterra owner's manual
matches, and the five books do not even agree with each other.**

Section 7.1, as the TR150 book prints it:

> To reduce the risk of electric shock disconnect your treadmill from the electrical outlet prior to
> cleaning and/or service work.
> To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons, install the treadmill on a
> flat level surface with access to a 230-volt, 10-amp (110-volt, 15A) grounded outlet with only the
> treadmill plugged into the circuit.
> Do not use an extension cord unless it is a 16 AWG or better with only one outlet on the end. Do not
> attempt to disable the grounded plug by using improper adapters or in any way modify the cord outlet.

| Service manual | 7.1 outlet | 7.1 extension cord | 7.3 `nominal ...-volt circuit` | Owner's manual for the same machine |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TR150 (GT65-NT014) | 230-volt, 10-amp (110-volt, 15A) | 16 AWG | 110 | 120-volt, 15-amp; 14 AWG; nominal 120 |
| TRX1400 (T3-NT053-01) | 230-volt, 10-amp (110-volt, 15A) | 16 AWG | 110 | no amperage or gauge; nominal 110 |
| TR260 (GT75A-NT050) | 120-volt, 15-amp | 16 AWG | **230** | 120-volt, 15-amp; 14 AWG; nominal 120 |
| TRX2500 (GT90B-NT022) | 230-volt, 10-amp | 16 AWG | **230** | no amperage or gauge; nominal 110 |
| TRX3500 / TRX4500 (GT90C-NT023 / GT90D-NT024) | 230-volt, 10-amp | 16 AWG | **230** | no amperage or gauge; nominal 110 |
| TRX5500 (GT90D-NT041) | no safety chapter | - | - | no amperage or gauge; nominal 110 |

Three things to say to anyone quoting these pages:

- **The 230-volt figures are export-market text.** The same Dyaco page serves 230-volt machines; the
  TR150 and TRX1400 books keep the North American figure in brackets, the TR260 book keeps it in 7.1
  but not 7.3, and the TRX2500 and TRX3500/4500 books drop it altogether. All six machines are sold
  on a 110/120-volt supply, which is what every owner's manual prints.
- **The TR260 book's 7.3 says `If the exercise bike fails or malfunctions`** - the page came from a
  bike manual.
- **16 AWG against 14 AWG.** Where an owner's manual gives a gauge it gives 14 AWG, the heavier cord
  (`xterra-treadmill-safety-outlet-120-volt-15-amp-and-14-awg-extension-cord`). 14 AWG satisfies both.

Give the owner's manual figures to an owner. The service figures matter only to a technician reading
the Dyaco book, and then with this table beside them.

The Spirit CT-series service manuals print the same 220/230-volt, 10-amp page
(`spirit-treadmill-safety-outlet-220-volt-10-amp`).
