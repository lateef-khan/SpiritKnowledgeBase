**Four items of the safety list speak about children, and the last two contradict each other.**

> 2. Keep children away from the bike. There are obvious pinch points and other caution areas that
>    can cause harm.
>
> 3. Children should be supervised to ensure that they do not play with the device.
>
> 12. This appliance can be used by children aged from 8 years and above and persons with reduced
>     physical, sensory or mental capabilities or lack of experience and knowledge if they have been
>     given supervision or instruction concerning use of the appliance in a safe way and understand
>     the hazards involved. Children shall not play with the appliance. Cleaning and user
>     maintenance shall not be made by children without supervision.
>
> 13. This appliance is not intended for use by persons (including children) with reduced
>     physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge, unless they have
>     been given supervision or instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible
>     for their safety.

- **8 is the only age figure printed**, and it is a supervised-use age, not a keep-away age.
  Item 2 keeps children away from the bike without a number.
- **Item 12 permits and item 13 withholds**, for the same persons, on the same condition of
  supervision or instruction. Item 12 is the household-appliance clause of IEC 60335, item 13 the
  older form; the manual prints both and reconciles nothing. Read together, a supervised child of 8
  or a supervised person of reduced capability may use the bike, and an unsupervised one may not.
- **Cleaning and user maintenance are not for an unsupervised child** - the only maintenance rule in
  the safety chapter.

**The commercial bikes say 13.** The CR900 and CU900 lists print `Keep children under the age of 13
away from this machine` and no age-8 clause (`spirit-bike-safety-minimum-age-13-and-16`). The 4.0T
treadmill prints this card's two clauses (`40t-2026-safety-children-aged-8-and-above`). Answer each
machine from its own book.

**The Dyaco PT editions print all four sentences** as bullets, word for word.

**This is a self-powered bike's answer.** The plug-in 7.0R, 7.0U, 8.0U and 8.5R admit children only
`at the discretion of a Qualified Physical Therapist and or parental consent`, with no age at all
(`spirit-med-bike-safety-intended-patient-population-and-medical-purpose`).
