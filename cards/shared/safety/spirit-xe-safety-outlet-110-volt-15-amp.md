**110 volt, 15 amp, grounded, and nothing else on the circuit.** All **five** books print the same
WARNING at the head of their safety instructions - the XE395-2010, the three XE795 printings, and
the **XE895-2018**, which was filed under `Bikes` and missed until now:

> WARNING - To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons, install the
> elliptical on a flat level surface with access to a 110-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet with only the
> elliptical plugged into the circuit.

| Requirement | Figure |
|---|---|
| Nominal supply | 110 volt |
| Current | 15 amp |
| Outlet | grounded |
| Circuit | only the elliptical plugged into it |
| Surface | flat and level |

**This is not the commercial CE figure.** The Spirit CE850 and CE800ENT commercial ellipticals ask
for a nominal **110-volt, 5-amp** circuit on a dedicated **5 amp** breaker
(`spirit-ce850-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`). These residential machines ask for **15
amp**, three times that. Never answer an XE owner from the CE850 card or a CE owner from this one.

**The XE395-2010 and the XE795 books give no breaker rating.** The `dedicated 15 amp circuit
breaker` sentence does not appear in any of them (`spirit-xe-safety-outlet-115-volt-15-amp`).
**The XE895-2018 is the exception**: it prints the full circuit-breaker paragraph, with the Grainger
and QO120HM examples, and closes it `The electrical outlet used should have a dedicated 15 amp
circuit breaker` (`spirit-house-breaker-needs-a-high-inrush-type`). **No manual of the five prints a
NEMA type and none prints a frequency.**

## The XE895-2018 is a 110-volt machine with a 115-volt sibling

**The XE895-2018 and the XE395-2018 were printed on the same day, August 22 2018, and ask for
different outlets.** The XE895 asks for 110 volt with a **14AWG** extension cord; the XE395-2018
asks for 115 volt with an **18AWG** cord (`spirit-xe-safety-outlet-115-volt-15-amp`,
`xt-2015-safety-extension-cord-14-awg-or-better`,
`xt385-2015-safety-extension-cord-18-awg-or-better`). Their bulleted safety lists are otherwise word
for word the same (`spirit-xe-2021-safety-instructions-list`). Read the figure off the book for the
machine in front of you; do not carry the XE395 figure onto an XE895.

**The XE895-2018 has a full Grounding Instructions page**, on its page 6, and it names the circuit a
second time:

> This product is for use on a nominal 110-volt/15 amp dedicated circuit, and has a grounding plug
> that looks like the plug illustrated below.

**Its two statements agree at 110 volt**, unlike the XE395-2018 and the 2021 books, whose safety
page says 115 and whose grounding page says 110. The XE895 is the one residential elliptical whose
two voltage sentences match.

## What each manual says a second time

**The XE395-2010 grounding page names the circuit without an amp figure**:

> This product is for use on a nominal 110-volt circuit, and has a grounding plug that looks like
> the plug illustrated below.

No `15 amp` and no `dedicated` in that sentence - the same wording the Spirit XT685-2010 owner's
manual uses (`spirit-product-must-be-grounded`). The 15 amp figure for an XE395-2010 comes from the
safety page above and nowhere else.

**No XE795 printing has a grounding page at all**, in 2018 or in either 2021 book, so the WARNING
above is the machine's only statement about its supply.

## The XE795 has nothing to plug in

**The XE795 is generator-powered.** Its console runs off a built-in generator driven by the pedals,
and **no power cord appears in its parts list** - the XE195, XE295, XG400 and XE395ENT manuals each
list `1 Power Cord` and all three XE795 printings, 2018 included, list none
(`spirit-ce-safety-generator-produces-ac-power`).

**Both sentences are printed and the manual never reconciles them.** The outlet WARNING above, and
the 14AWG extension-cord line beside it, sit in a book for a machine with no mains plug. Say so
plainly rather than sending a customer to buy a circuit: an XE795 needs no wall socket to run, and
the requirement above is what its manual prints.

The XE395-2010 does have a power cord and does plug in, so for that machine the WARNING is a real
installation requirement.

## The other nine residential machines print a different figure or none

| Machine | Outlet requirement |
|---|---|
| XE195, XE295, XG400, XE395ENT (2021) | 115 volt, 15 amp, dedicated 15 amp breaker (`spirit-xe-safety-outlet-115-volt-15-amp`) |
| XE395-2018, XE195-2019, XE295-2019, XG400-2019 | 115 volt, 15 amp, dedicated 15 amp breaker (`spirit-xe-safety-outlet-115-volt-15-amp`) |
| XE100, XE200, XE300, XE150, XE350, XE550 | none printed - external power supply |
| XE395-2023, XE795-2023 | none printed (`spirit-xe-safety-no-outlet-figure-printed`) |

**Do not carry a Spirit treadmill figure onto an elliptical.** The CT800 family is 110 or 120 volt
at 15 amp, the CT850 is 120 volt at 20 amp and the 2026 machines need a NEMA 5-20P on a 120-volt,
20-amp circuit. Those cards answer for no elliptical and this one answers for no treadmill.

The extension cord all five of these books allow is 14AWG or better with one outlet on the end
(`xt-2015-safety-extension-cord-14-awg-or-better`). A house breaker that trips while the machine's
own does not is a different problem, and the XE395-2010 and the XE795 books do not cover it - the
XE895-2018 and the eight 115-volt books do (`spirit-house-breaker-needs-a-high-inrush-type`).

## Do not reach for a Sole elliptical card

**The Sole E95-2026 asks for 115 volt at 15 amp and the Sole E25-2023 for 120 volt at 15 amp**
(`e95-2026-power-outlet-requirements`, `e25-2023-electrical-power-requirements`). The amp figure
matches; the voltage does not, and they are a different brand's manuals. Check the brand on the
machine before quoting any of the three.

## Four Spirit residential bike manuals print the same figure

**110-volt, 15-amp, with only the bike plugged into the circuit**, in the WARNING header of the
safety instructions page: XBU55-2019, XBU55-2021, XBU55ENT-2021 and XBR25-2019. The XBR25-2019 book
writes `install the fitness bike on a flat level surface`; the three XBU55 books write `the bike`.

**Their recumbent siblings ask for 115-volt.** The XBR55-2019, XBR55-2021 and XBR55ENT-2021 manuals
are otherwise the same book as the XBU55 pair and print 115-volt in the same sentence
(`spirit-xe-safety-outlet-115-volt-15-amp`). Never read one across to the other.

**The four 2023 XB manuals print no voltage and no amperage at all.**
