Both manuals print the same four bullets, under the heading INTENDED PATIENT POPULATION:

> - Male and Female Adults. Children at the discretion of a Qualified Physical Therapist and or
>   parental consent.
> - Maximal patient's weight is 440 lb.
> - Patient must be ambulatory.
> - Patient should have medical clearance before starting any rigorous exercise program. This is
>   especially important for person with a history of heart disease or other high risk factors.

- **Adults of either sex**, without an age ceiling.
- **Children are not excluded**, but only at the discretion of a qualified physical therapist, or
  with parental consent. **No minimum age is printed.** This is the only Spirit treadmill family
  whose manual allows a child on the machine at all.
- **The patient must be ambulatory** - able to walk. The machine is for open kinetic chain
  exercise, with the patient neither restrained nor connected to it.
- **440 lb maximum**, the same figure as the safety instructions page
  (`spirit-2026t-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lbs`).

**A separate part of the body block applies to the two machines differently.** Under INTENDED PART
OF THE BODY the 7.0T names `Contact site: hands and feet`, and the 8.0T names
`Contact site: hands, feet, and trunk`. Both add `Condition: should not have any trauma`.

**Being in the intended population is not enough on its own.** The Operation Instructions page
excludes a list of conditions outright: `spirit-2026t-safety-rehabilitation-contraindications`.
The safety instructions page separately says to keep children away from the machine
(`spirit-2026t-safety-keep-children-away`), which is the rule when no therapist or parent has
authorised the child's use.
