**Five Xterra books print no Proposition 65 warning anywhere** - the 2013 SB4.5r and FS5.8e, the
2014 FS5.9e and MB500, and the 2016 FS150. All five predate the August 2018 labelling change after
which every Xterra book carries the Toluene-and-Acrylamide warning on its safety page or a sticker
on its Product Labels page (`xterra-treadmill-safety-proposition-65-warning`).

| Book | Stamp | Safety page |
|---|---|---|
| SB4.5r | SB4.5r_20130605 | thirteen items and the Fitness Equipment bullets, no warning between them |
| FS5.8e | FS58e_20130605 | same |
| FS5.9e | FS59e_20140214 | same |
| MB500 | MB500_20140925 | eleven items, `Regularly examine the bike`, `SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS` - no warning |
| FS150 | FS150_VER1_20160524 | thirteen items and the Fitness Equipment bullets, no warning between them |

**Two machines carded from a current book also have an earlier printing without it**: the SB500
book printed in 2014 (`SB500_20140811`, warranty August 1, 2014) has no warning where the 2020 book
(`SB500_20200706`) does, and the RSX1500 book printed in 2017 (`RSX1500_20171222`) has none where the
2021 book does. Those machines carry the warning on the current book's card; a customer holding the
older printing will not find it.

How it was checked: all five scanned books read from their renders and OCR supplements, and the text
searched for `P65`, `Toluene`, `Proposition` and `California`; no hits. The absence is a date, not a
model difference: the same MB500 list with the warning added is the 2018 MB550 page
(`xterra-mb-safety-instructions-list-11-items-no-freewheel`).

The Spirit XT-2015 books print the warning in the older wording for their own machines
(`xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`).

**A sixth book, from the rower wave**: the ERG400 rower scan, stamped `ERG400_20150205` with a
warranty page effective August 1, 2012, prints the thirteen-item plug-in list and seven Fitness
Equipment bullets with no warning between them (`xterra-erg-safety-instructions-list-13-item-plug-in-list`)
- the same 2013-2016 shape as the five above. Checked from the OCR supplements of all fourteen pages
and the safety page render; no `P65`, `Toluene`, `Proposition` or `California`. The 2023 ERG220,
which prints the same list, carries the warning (`xterra-treadmill-safety-proposition-65-warning`).
