The SR400 book prints no safety chapter and no safety content of any kind. The word `warning` appears nowhere in the book; there is no Proposition 65 notice, no serial-number record page, no user weight limit and no warranty chapter.

Proved four ways. A loose-word search (warning, serial, warranty, weight-limit, Prop 65, Toluene) returns only incidental words - the machine's own weight in the moving instructions, pedal straps and console load levels. A spacing-tolerant search finds no letter-spaced headings. Every one of the 19 PDF pages is accounted for: cover (p. 1); assembly figures with adaptor, moving, console-angle and pedal-strap adjustment plus battery and load-toggle figures (pp. 2-8); console operation (pp. 9-14); parts tables (pp. 16-17); and the three pages with no text supplement, all verified on the render - an exploded-view diagram (p. 15), a blank page (p. 18) and a solid red back cover (p. 19). The book has no table of contents promising anything more.

The nearest safety content is assembly advice: adjust the pedal straps to the proper position, and unfolding the rail is a two-person job. For what the other rower books print, see the linked cards. Do not quote any warning, limit or serial position for an SR400.
