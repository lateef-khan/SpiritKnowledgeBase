**Five books print the same page: an eight-item appliance list, the Proposition 65 warning, and
seven Fitness Equipment bullets.** These are the machines with a battery console and no power
adapter, and the page is the plug-in thirteen-item list
(`xterra-safety-instructions-list-13-item-plug-in-list`) with every cord, plug and outlet item
removed - the DANGER line, *unplug when not in use*, *damaged cord or plug*, *do not carry by the
cord*, *keep the cord from heated surfaces* and *grounded outlet only* are gone, and so is the
*To disconnect* bullet.

> When using an electrical appliance, basic precautions should always be followed, including the
> following: Read all instructions before using this appliance.
> **WARNING - To reduce the risk of burns, fire electric shock, or injury to persons:**

| # | Instruction |
|---|---|
| 1 | Do not operate under blanket or pillow. Excessive heating can occur and cause fire, electric shock, or injury to persons. |
| 2 | This exercise equipment is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge. |
| 3 | Use this appliance only for its intended use as described in this manual. Do not use attachments not recommended by the manufacturer. |
| 4 | Never operate the appliance with the air openings blocked. Keep the air openings free of lint, hair, and the like. |
| 5 | Never drop or insert any object into any opening. |
| 6 | Do not use outdoors. |
| 7 | Do not operate where aerosol (spray) products are being use or where oxygen is being administered. |
| 8 | The appliance is intended for household use. |

Then the Proposition 65 warning naming Toluene and Acrylamide
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-proposition-65-warning`), and:

**Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions**

| Bullet | Printed on |
|---|---|
| Do not operate equipment on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and equipment may result. | all five |
| Before beginning this or any exercise program, consult a physician. This is especially important for persons over the age of 35 or persons with pre-existing health conditions. | all five |
| Keep hands away from all moving parts. | all five |
| The pulse sensors are not medical devices... (`xterra-treadmill-safety-pulse-sensors-are-not-medical-devices`) | all five |
| Do not attempt to use your equipment for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended. | all five |
| Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable... (`xterra-treadmill-safety-proper-shoes`) | all five |
| **Weight Limit: 225 LBS.** | FB150, FB350 (`xterra-fb-safety-user-weight-limit-225-lbs`) |
| **Weight Limit: 250 LBS.** | FB160, FB360 (`xterra-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-250-lbs`) |
| no weight bullet | EU100 (`xterra-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`) |

The page closes with `Failure to follow all guidelines may compromise the effectiveness of the
exercise experience, expose yourself (and possibly others) to injury, and reduce the longevity of
the equipment.`, `SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!` and `CAUTION!! Please be careful when
unpacking the carton.`

The list is an appliance template: *air openings* and *electric shock* are printed on machines whose
only electrical part is a two-battery console (`xterra-safety-battery-console-no-power-adapter`).
The 2025 FB180 merges this page into one numbered list (`fb180-2025-safety-instructions-list-16-items`).
