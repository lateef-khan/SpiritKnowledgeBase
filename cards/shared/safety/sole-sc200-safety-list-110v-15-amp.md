- **WARNING** - read all instructions before using this appliance.
- **DANGER** - disconnect the SOLE climber from the electrical outlet before cleaning or service work.
- **WARNING** - flat level surface with access to a **110-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet**, with only the climber plugged into the circuit. **DO NOT USE AN EXTENSION CORD UNLESS IT IS A 14AWG OR BETTER.** These books print no single-outlet clause and no grounded-plug adapter clause.
- Do not operate on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet; **keep children under the age of 13 away** (obvious pinch points and caution areas); keep hands away from all moving parts; never operate with a damaged cord or plug (call your dealer); keep the cord away from heated surfaces; do not operate where aerosol spray products are used or oxygen is administered (motor sparks may ignite); never drop or insert any object into any openings; do not use outdoors; to disconnect, turn all controls off then remove the plug; do not use the climber for any other purpose than its intended one.
- **The hand pulse sensors are not medical devices.** Various factors including the user's movement may affect accuracy; they are only exercise aids for heart rate trends in general.
- **Wear proper shoes.** High heels, dress shoes, sandals and bare feet are not suitable; quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue.
- Not intended for use by persons with **reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities**, or lack of experience and knowledge, unless supervised or instructed by a person responsible for their safety.
- **SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!**

The SC200-2016 and SC200-2017 books print this list word for word. The SC300-2017 book prints the same list reading 120-volt at the outlet (see its own card), and the CC81 books print a different list on a 110-240-volt 5-amp outlet (see the CC81-2026 cards). Serial-number recording and the Proposition 65 position each have their own card.
