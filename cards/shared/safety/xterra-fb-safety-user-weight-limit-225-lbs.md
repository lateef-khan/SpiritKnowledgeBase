**This is the 225 lb figure**, the lowest weight limit printed on any Xterra machine.

Both folding-bike books print it once, as the last bullet of the Fitness Equipment Safety
Instructions on the safety page:

> Weight Limit: 225 LBS.

Neither book has a specification table, and the warranty page prints no capacity. The FB350 book
is the FB150 book with a back cushion and rear handlebar added (its footers still read *FB150*), and
the safety page is the same page, so the 225 lb limit is printed for both machines and is not a
copy of the other book's figure.

**The other folding bikes are 250 lbs** - FB160, FB360 and the 2025 FB180
(`xterra-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-250-lbs`). The FB150 and FB350 are the lighter-duty pair.
