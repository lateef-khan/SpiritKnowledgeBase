**One sentence, printed in eight of these manuals with only the machine's name changed.**

> NEVER expose this StairClimber to rain or moisture. This product is NOT designed for use outdoors,
> near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment. The operating temperature
> specification is **40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit**, and humidity is **95% non-condensing** (no water
> drops forming on surfaces).

| Limit | Figure |
|---|---|
| Operating temperature | **40 to 120 degrees F** (4 to 49 degrees C) |
| Humidity | **95 %**, non-condensing |
| Outdoors, pool, spa | never |

- **This is an operating specification only.** None of the eight manuals gives a storage or transport
  temperature, an air-pressure range or an altitude limit.
- **95% non-condensing is the ceiling**, and the manuals define it - no water drops forming on
  surfaces.

**Do not answer a 7.0S, 7.5S, 8.5S or MS300 question from this card.** Those four are specified as
medical devices at **10 to 36 degrees C, 30 to 90 % humidity**, with air-pressure and altitude
figures as well (`spirit-climber-safety-operating-environment-iec-60601`). Their band is far
narrower - 50 to 97 F against 40 to 120 F here - so quoting this card for one of them would allow a
room the manufacturer excludes.

**The 8.5S-Fit is neither.** Its electrical page prints
`The temperature specification is 40 degrees C, and humidity is 95%, non-condensing`: 95% as here,
but a single 40 degree **Celsius** figure with no lower bound. 40 C is 104 F, and the manual does not
say whether it is a ceiling or a nominal value.
