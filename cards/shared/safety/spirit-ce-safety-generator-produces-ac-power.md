> **WARNING** - Be aware that the generator is producing A.C. power while the elliptical trainer is
> being used. Do not service the elliptical trainer while the generator is spinning; serious
> electric shock could occur.

- **The hazard is present with no cord and no wall socket.** These machines have no mains plug; the
  generator that supplies the console is driven by the pedals.
- **The condition is the generator spinning, not the pedals moving.** A flywheel that is still
  coasting after the user steps off is still spinning.
- **The manuals give no wait time and no discharge figure.** They do not say how long to leave the
  machine before touching it.

The 2021 and 2023 CE800 manuals and the 2013 CE800 manual print `A.C.`; the 2021 CE900 manual prints
`AC`; the 2025 CE900 manual writes `Elliptical` where the others write `elliptical trainer`. Nothing
that decides the answer changes between them.

**These five machines have no outlet requirement because of this.** The CE900 2025 manual says so in
as many words on its setting-up page - Spirit Fitness commercial elliptical trainers have a built-in
generator for power and do not need to be plugged into an AC outlet - which is why none of these
manuals prints a voltage, an amp rating, a circuit breaker or an extension-cord gauge. The four
mains-powered Spirit ellipticals do: `spirit-ce850-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`.

**The unplug-before-cleaning DANGER line is still printed in the CE800 2021 and 2023 manuals**, on
their safety instructions page, even though there is nothing to unplug
(`spirit-ce800-safety-instructions-list`). Both sentences are printed and the manuals never
reconcile them.

**The CES880-2025 manual prints no such warning.** That machine is self-generating too - its
setting-up page prints the same built-in-generator sentence - but its manual has no electrical page
and no generator warning at all. That is an absence; do not quote this WARNING for a CES880.

**Do not answer a CE850 or CE800ENT from this card.** Those four ellipticals plug into a wall
outlet, and their electrical page states the opposite rule for the same job: never remove a cover
without first disconnecting AC power
(`xt-2023-safety-remove-a-cover-only-with-ac-disconnected`).
