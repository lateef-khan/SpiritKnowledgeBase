**Keep children away from the fitness bike.** There are obvious pinch points and other caution areas that can cause harm.

**These manuals give no age.** The rule is a bare "keep children away". The 2016 and later manuals of the same bikes replaced it with **"Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine"**, which is a different card. Do not read the age 13 figure back onto these machines - their manuals never print it.

None of these manuals describes a child lock or a key that disables the console.
