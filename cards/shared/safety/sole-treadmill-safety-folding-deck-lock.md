**Do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and locked position.** Secure the power cord against damage and use both handrails to maneuver it. (Moving the machine itself belongs to the maintenance section.)

Two lock mechanisms appear; each book prints one:

- **Locking telescoping tube** (F63/F65/F80/F85 2006-2013 books, AF63/AF65/AF80/AF83/AF85, UF80/UF83/UF85, VF63, VF80/VF83/VF85, WF63, WF80/WF83/WF85): **TO FOLD - make certain the treadmill is at minimum incline, then lift the running deck until it is secured by the locking telescoping tube assembly in the center back of the base. TO UNFOLD - apply slight forward pressure at the rear roller area with one hand, pull down on the unlocking lever, and slowly lower the deck; it lowers unassisted once it reaches about waist high.**
- **Latch with yellow sticker** (F60-2013/2014/2016): **TO FOLD - lift the deck until the latch clicks in place. TO UNFOLD - press the tube with your foot at the yellow sticker to release the latch.**

The S77-2013 and TT9-2018 books call their machines folding treadmills on the unpacking page but print no fold/unfold procedure; the non-folding models (AS77, ATT8, TT8-2013, TT8C, VS73/VS77, VTT8, WS73/WS77, WTT8, TT8/UTT8-2006) correctly print none.
