**None of the four 2024 TRX manuals says how much room the treadmill needs.** The older-layout
`3 1/2 feet` bullet is gone from their safety page, and the new-layout Location Requirements panel
was never added to their grounding page.

What they do print about where it stands:

| Page | Sentence |
|---|---|
| Important Safety Instructions | `Do not operate equipment on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and equipment may result.` |
| Pre-Assembly | `make sure the treadmill is positioned flat on the ground` - about removing the shipping strap, not about siting |

How it was checked: the safety, electrical and grounding pages read from the render, and the whole
text of each book searched for `clearance`, `feet`, `ft`, `behind` and `space`; the only hits are
`bare feet` in the shoes bullet and the shipping-strap paragraph.

A customer with one of these four machines has to be given the figure from a neighbouring book with
the caveat that their own manual prints none: 3 1/2 feet behind on the 2021 layout
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-rear-clearance-3-5-feet`), 20 inches around and 6 feet behind on the new layout
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-location-20-in-front-and-sides-6-ft-behind`).

The Spirit CT800-2012 book has the same gap (`spirit-ct800-safety-no-placement-or-clearance-requirement`).
