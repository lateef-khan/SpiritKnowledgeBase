**These eight rowers have no power adaptor, no power cord and no outlet requirement.** Their books
name no voltage, no plug and no jack; the only electrical part is the console, and every book tells
the owner to fit its batteries. A customer asking where to plug one in should be told there is
nothing to plug in.

| Rower | What the book says about power |
|---|---|
| ERG160 | console table `BATTERY TYPE 2 AA or UM 3`; `The battery symbol will light up when the battery is low` |
| ERG180 | console specifications `Battery Type 2pcs of Size AA` |
| ERG220 | `Insert the 2 AA batteries to activate the Console (95)`; `Please install 2 AA batteries in the battery case on the back of the Console` |
| ERG500 | `Install two AAA batteries in the battery case inside the console` - the one AAA console |
| ERG550W, ERG600W | `Install 2 AA batteries (included) and computer will beep for 2 seconds` |
| ERG650W | assembly step: `Place the 2 AA batteries into the Console (No. 54)` |
| ERG750W | the book never names the type - troubleshooting says only `Batteries not Installed - Install Batteries`; its service manual heads the console page `TZ-8145-2AA Trouble Shooting` and tells the technician to check `the specification of the battery` |

Which battery, how many and how to change them are console facts; this card records only that the
machine is unpowered.

**The safety pages still read like an appliance's.** Seven of the eight print `Never operate the
appliance with the air openings blocked` and `dropped into water` (the ERG160's bullet-only page has
neither); the ERG220 prints the whole plug-in list
with a DANGER-unplug line, a grounded-outlet item and the sentence `Some warnings below pertain to
plug-in models only` (`xterra-erg-safety-instructions-list-13-item-plug-in-list`); the ERG180 and
ERG750W close with the new-layout note about *power requirements* (`erg750w-2025-safety-instructions-list-16-items`).
None of the eight has an electrical page, a storm rule or an environment rating on its safety
pages; the ERG160 console table's `OPERATING TEMPERATURE 0°C ~ +40°C` is the console's own figure, a
console fact.

How it was checked: every page of each book read; the text searched for `adapter`, `adaptor`, `plug`,
`cord`, `outlet`, `volt` and `battery`. The hits are the battery sentences above, the tank *Fill
Plug*, the ERG600W *bungee cord*, and the warranty's boilerplate `Proper connection to a power supply
of sufficient voltage`, printed in every Xterra warranty, powered or not.

The three plug-in rowers are on `xterra-erg-safety-external-power-supply-and-power-jack`. The Spirit
water rowers print the same finding with four AA cells
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-four-aa-batteries-and-no-electrical-page`).
