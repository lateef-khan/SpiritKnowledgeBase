**This is the 350 lb figure, the highest any Xterra treadmill manual prints.**

| Book | How it is printed |
|---|---|
| TR75, TR85 | `21. User Weight Limit: 350 lbs.` - item 21 of the SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| TRX3500, TRX4500, TRX5500 | `User Weight Limit: 350 lbs.` - the last bullet of the Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions |

Printed once per book; none of the five has a specification table with a user capacity.

**The TR75H is not the TR75.** The Hiker book prints 265 lb
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-user-weight-limit-265-lbs`). **The TRX2500 is 300 lbs**, not 350
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-user-weight-limit-300-lbs`).

| Figure | Printed on | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 250 lbs | TR150 | `Product Weight Limit: 250 lbs.`, a bullet in the Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions |
| 265 lbs | TRX1400, WS200, WS300 | item 21 of the SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| 265 lb | TR75H | item 22 of the SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| 300 lbs | TR65, TRX2500 | item 21 (TR65) or the last Fitness Equipment bullet (TRX2500) |
| 300 lbs | TR95H | item 22 of the SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| 350 lbs | TR75, TR85, TRX3500, TRX4500, TRX5500 | item 21 (TR75, TR85) or the last Fitness Equipment bullet (TRX books) |
| none | TR200, TR300, TR6.6, TRX1000, TR260, TR6.4 | no weight limit anywhere in the book |
