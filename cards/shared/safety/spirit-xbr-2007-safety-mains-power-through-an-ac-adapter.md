**These two bikes are mains-powered through a plug-in AC transformer adapter. They are not
self-powered.** The 2008 Spirit Manufacturing dealer service manual for the 2007 XBR25 and XBR55 says
so in the first row of its troubleshooting guide:

> No power - There is no power to the console. 1. Unit must be plugged into an outlet with power.
> 2. Test adapter for DC voltage using volt meter. (procedure 1)

- **The supply is an `AC Transformer - Adapter`** that plugs into the wall and feeds the bike through
  a `1200mm AC Wire Assembly`; both parts lists name both (items 131 and 48 on each bike).
- **Procedure 1 tests the adapter with the meter set to AC volts and expects at least 24 volts at
  the two holes of its plug**; with no voltage the adapter is replaced `assuming the outlet has
  power`. The row above says DC and the procedure says AC - the book prints both and reconciles
  neither. The test is an errors-section procedure; it is quoted here only because it is the one
  place this manual says what the adapter puts out. Downstream, procedure 2 expects around 6 volts
  DC on the console harness.

**That is everything the manual says about power.** It prints no outlet voltage, no amperage, no
breaker, no extension-cord rule, no grounding page and no safety chapter of any kind: its contents
are the troubleshooting guide, five repair procedures, the dealer warranty pages and the two parts
lists. The words *safety*, *ground*, *GFCI* and *weight limit* appear nowhere in it - checked in the
text layer, with letter-spacing allowed, and in the 300 dpi renders the OCR supplements were made
from. No user weight limit is printed (`xt-2015-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`).

**Do not fill the gaps from a later book.** The 2016 service manuals for the next XBR25 and XBR55
print a 115-volt, 15-amp outlet and a full safety chapter (`spirit-xe-safety-outlet-115-volt-15-amp`);
they are different SKUs and nothing in this manual says those figures apply. And do not read the
generator bikes' answer onto these two (`spirit-bike-safety-no-mains-outlet-needed`): a 2007 XBR
with a dead console and no adapter output has no power at all.
