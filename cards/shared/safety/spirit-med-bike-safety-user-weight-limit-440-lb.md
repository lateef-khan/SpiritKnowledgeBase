**440 lb on all six 2025 Spirit Medical bikes. The self-powered 4.0R and 4.0U print it once, as the
last safety item; the plug-in 7.0R, 7.0U, 8.0U and 8.5R print it four times, and one of the four is
a contraindication.**

| Machine | Where | Exactly as printed |
|---|---|---|
| 4.0R, 4.0U | SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS item 17 | `User Weight Limit: 440 lb.` |
| 7.0R, 7.0U | SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS item 22 | `User Weight Limit: 440 lbs.` |
| 8.0U | SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS item 25 | `User Weight Limit: 440 lbs.` |
| 8.5R | SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS item 24 | `User Weight Limit: 440 lbs.` |
| 7.0R, 7.0U, 8.0U, 8.5R | OPERATION INSTRUCTIONS | `Do not use for patients weighing greater than 440 lb (bariatric).` |
| 7.0R, 7.0U, 8.0U, 8.5R | APPLICATION SPECIFICATION | `Maximal patient's weight is 440 lb.` |
| 7.0R, 7.0U | SPECIFICATIONS, Patient weight capacity | `440 Lbs. (200 Kgs.)` |
| 8.0U, 8.5R | SPECIFICATIONS, Patient weight capacity | `200 kgs (440 lbs)` |

- **On the plug-in bikes 440 lb is a clinical exclusion**, listed with severe osteoporosis and acute
  fractures, not a recommendation
  (`spirit-med-bike-safety-contraindications-osteoporosis-fractures-and-acute-conditions`).
- **200 kg is the specification page's rounding.** 440 lb is 199.6 kg; the two are one figure.
- **The self-powered bikes print no metric figure and no second statement.** Their safety page is
  the whole answer, and their contraindications are none - the same book says the bike
  `is not a medical device` (`spirit-med-bike-safety-instructions-list-seventeen-items`).

## The Dyaco MED 7.0R of 2021 prints the same limit in kilograms

**The Dyaco edition of the 7.0R (Rev. 1.2.1, 2021/10/14) prints no weight item in its safety
bullets at all.** Its figure is `Do not use for patients weighing greater than 200kgs (bariatric)`
on the operation page, `Maximal patient's weight is 200kg` on the application page, and
`440 Lbs. (200 Kgs.)` on the specification page - the same limit, metric first. The 2025 Spirit
printing added item 22 and turned the two text figures into pounds.

## Two books print a different figure for the same self-powered frame

**The Dyaco PT editions of the 4.0 R and 4.0 U say `Maximum User Weight: 159 kg (350 lbs.)`**
(`spirit-pt-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-159-kg-350-lbs`), 90 lb below this card. **The 4.0R-2025
text layer still carries that sentence** as ghost text under the clearance drawings on printed page
5; the printed page does not show it, and the printed limit is item 17's 440 lb. Quote 440 lb for a
Spirit-branded 4.0R or 4.0U and 350 lbs for a Dyaco PT one, and say which book the customer holds.

**Do not answer from the other Spirit 440 lb cards.** The 8.5UE ergometer
(`85ue-2025-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lbs`), the 7.0S, 7.5S and 8.5S steppers
(`spirit-climber-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lbs`) and the 4.0T, 7.0T and 8.0T treadmills
(`spirit-2026t-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lbs`) print the same number for other product lines.
The commercial CR900 and CU900 bikes are rated 450 lb
(`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`) and the residential XB bikes 350 lb
(`spirit-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lb`).
