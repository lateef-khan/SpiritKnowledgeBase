The two i-Strength machines, **CSI-CPSP and CSI-LROW**, are the only Spirit strength products that
print a pacemaker warning. It heads their safeguards page, boxed as a **WARNING**:

> **PACEMAKER USERS:** Magnetic/Electronic Interference Risk - Keep this device at least 6 inches
> (15cm) from your implanted pacemaker. Consult your physician before use.

*This device* means the machine, not the pacemaker. Six inches is the separation Spirit asks for
between the machine and the implant, and the manual does not say which part of the machine the
magnetic field comes from — the i-Strength resistance unit is the obvious candidate, but the book
does not say so and it should not be guessed at.

The instruction is unconditional about the physician: **consult before use**, not only if symptoms
appear.

**No other Spirit strength manual carries this warning.** The thirty-four CSS, CSD and CSF machines
use a plate stack with no powered resistance and print nothing about implanted devices. That is an
absence, not a clearance: it means their manuals do not address the question.
