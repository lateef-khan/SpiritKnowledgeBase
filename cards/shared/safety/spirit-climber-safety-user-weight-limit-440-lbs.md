**440 lbs.** All four steppers print the same figure, and each prints it twice.

| Where | 7.0S / 7.5S | 8.5S / 8.5S-Fit |
|---|---|---|
| Safety instructions | item **19** - `User Weight Limit: 440 lbs.` | item **24** - `User Weight Limit: 440 lbs.` |
| Specification table | `440 Lbs. (200 Kgs.)` | `200 kgs (440 lbs)` |

The two books round it the same way and differ only in which unit they print first.

**The 7.0S and 7.5S state it a third time, as a clinical contraindication**, because they are
rehabilitation machines:

> Do not use for patients weighing greater than 440 lb (bariatric).

and again on the APPLICATION SPECIFICATION page as `Maximal patient's weight is 440 lb`. The 8.5S
prints the same `Maximal patient's weight is 440 lb` line. None of the four says what happens above
the limit.

All four print the boxed note beside the safety list:

> Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of
> your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine.

**No other Spirit climber is 440 lb.** The CRS800S and CS800 steppers are 450 lb
(`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`), the CSC900 stairclimber and the CVC800 vertical
climber are 400 lb, and the CSC880 stair climber is 330 lb
(`spirit-climber-safety-user-weight-limit-330-lb`). Quote the machine's own manual.

**The MS300 is the same machine family and does not print this figure at all.** It gives 200 kg as a
patient weight instead, and prints no `User Weight Limit` line
(`ms300-2021-safety-patient-weight-limit-200-kg`).

**Not the 2026 treadmill card.** The 4.0T, 7.0T, 8.0T and MT200 are also 440 lbs
(`spirit-2026t-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lbs`), on a different product line and a different page.
