- **Do not operate the treadmill on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both the carpet and the treadmill may result.**
- **Do not block the rear of the treadmill. Provide a minimum of 3 1/2 feet clearance between the rear of the treadmill and any fixed object.**

Both lines are word for word identical in all 25 books. The assembly step 1 of seven folding books (AF63, AF80, F60-2012, F63-2013, F65-2013, F80-2013, S77-2013) instead says "the rear should be at least 3 feet from any wall" while positioning the carton - that is the assembler's working space, and this card follows the safety page's larger 3 1/2-foot figure for the installed machine. Use the larger figure wherever there is room.

Both lines are word for word identical in all 24 A2 books, render-verified for the 110-volt, 120-volt and TD80 books. The A2 assembly unpacking cautions repeat the smaller 3-foot working-space figure; as before, this card follows the safety page's 3 1/2-foot figure for the installed machine.
