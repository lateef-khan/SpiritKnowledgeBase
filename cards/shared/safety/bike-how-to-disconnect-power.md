**The manuals print three different versions of this step, and two of them do not print it at all. Use the row for your machine.**

| Machines | What the manual says |
|---|---|
| B74-2015, R72-2015, B94-2011, R92-2011, B94-2013, R92-2013, B94-2016, R92-2016, B94-2019, R92-2019, B94-2023, R92-2023, LCB-2016, LCR-2016, LCB-2019, LCR-2019 | To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position, **remove tether cord**, then remove the plug from the outlet. |
| LCB-2011, LCR-2011 | To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position, then remove the plug from the outlet. |
| LCB-2023, LCR-2023 | To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position and then remove the plug from the outlet. |

**The word "tether" appears exactly once in each of the manuals that use it - in this sentence and nowhere else.** Those manuals never say where a tether cord is, what it looks like or how to refit one, and none of the assembly, console or maintenance pages mentions one.

**The LCB 2013 and LCR 2013 manuals print no disconnect step at all.**
