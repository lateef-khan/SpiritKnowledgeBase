**Three stickers, described on a PRODUCT LABELS page these three manuals print and the ten older
bike manuals do not.**

| Sticker | Where it is | What it carries |
|---|---|---|
| Serial number | Front stabilizer | The number below the barcode, and the phone number (870) 935-1107 |
| Proposition 65 warning | Front stabilizer | That the unit was manufactured using chemicals that could cause harm with improper use |
| Safety warning | **Differs by machine - see below** | The same warning printed in the manual |

> **SERIAL NUMBER STICKER** - This sticker will be found on the front stabilizer. Please record the
> number below the barcode for the purpose of registering your bike's warranty.

## The safety warning sticker is in two different places

- **CR900-2025 and CU800ENT-2024:** `on the right side of the console`.
- **CU900-2025:** `on the left side of your Upright Bike's upright`.

The CR900-2025 and CU900-2025 manuals are otherwise the same book for this page, so this is a real
difference between the recumbent and the upright and not a printing slip. Give the location from the
machine's own manual.

**The Proposition 65 sticker text is the short form.** It names **no chemical** and prints **no web
address**, unlike the boxed warning the 2018 to 2023 bike manuals print in the manual itself, which
names Toluene and Acrylamide and gives www.P65Warnings.ca.gov
(`xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`). The two are not interchangeable in either direction.

**The registration block is on the previous page**, and in these three manuals it is a QR code plus
blanks for Serial Number, Date of Purchase and Dealer / Place of Purchase - not the mailed-in
registration card the older bike manuals describe. It also carries the manual's own revision stamp:
`Version: 3.0, Revision: 06.12.25` on both 2025 manuals and `Version: 1.0, Revision: 10.25.24` on
the CU800ENT-2024.

**The ten older bike manuals have no product labels page.** They print a `RECORD YOUR SERIAL NUMBER`
block with a blank and, in the 2018 and 2021 manuals, one sentence of location -
`spirit-ce-safety-serial-number-location`.
