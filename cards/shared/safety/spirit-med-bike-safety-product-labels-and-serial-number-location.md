**Every one of the six owner's manuals describes two stickers - a serial number sticker and a
Proposition 65 sticker - and no two books put the serial number in the same words.** Ask which
model before sending anyone to look.

| Machine | Serial number sticker, as the owner's manual says | Proposition 65 sticker |
|---|---|---|
| 4.0R | `on the frame` | `on the frame` |
| 4.0U | `near the front stabilizer` | `near the front stabilizer` |
| 7.0R | `on the base` | `on the base` |
| 7.0U | `on the front stabilizer` | `on the console upright` |
| 8.0U | `front stabilizer` (printed without `on the`) | `on the front end of the bike above the plug-in` |
| 8.5R | `on the front stabilizer` | `on the front stabilizer` |

> **SERIAL NUMBER STICKER** - This sticker will be found on the base. Please record the number
> below the barcode for the purpose of registering your Recumbent Bike's warranty.

> **PROPOSITION 65 WARNING** - This sticker will be found on the base. The State of California
> requires us to inform you that this unit was manufactured using chemicals that could cause harm
> with improper use.

- **Record the number below the barcode**, not the barcode itself. The label artwork shows the
  Spirit wordmark and the specimen part number `935-1107`.
- **The Proposition 65 sticker reads `WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm -
  www.P65Warnings.ca.gov`** and names no chemical, on all six; the Toluene and Acrylamide box of the
  older Spirit manuals is not printed (`xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`).
- **There is no third sticker.** The 2024 and 2025 commercial bikes describe three
  (`spirit-bike-2024-2025-safety-product-labels`); these six describe two.

## The service manuals photograph the sticker where the owner's manuals put it

Each service manual opens with a page headed `1. Serial Number Location` and a photograph, and no
words. Read from the renders:

- **4.0R**: a drawing of the bike with a callout to the **aluminium seat-slide rail at the base**,
  between the front shroud and the seat carriage. The callout shows the black `WARNING: Cancer and
  Reproductive Harm` label beside the white `SERIAL NUMBER` barcode label - both stickers side by
  side on the rail, which is what the owner's manual means by `on the frame`.
- **4.0U**: a drawing with a callout to the **lower front of the main shroud, just above the front
  stabilizer**. The callout shows the `SERIAL NUMBER` barcode label with `MADE IN TAIWAN` beside a
  larger Dyaco rating label (`4.0U`, `Exercise Bike / Upright`, a self-generator rating line, a
  date code and a bilingual warning about electrical shock and rotating parts - the smaller print
  is at the limit of legibility and is not quoted further).
- **7.0R**: a photograph looking down on the **aluminium base rail between the shroud and the seat
  carriage**; the barcode sticker, boxed in red, sits on the top of the rail. That is the base.
- **7.0U**: a photograph of the **foot of the console upright at the front of the frame**, the
  sticker boxed in red low on the moulding - the front stabilizer end of the machine.
- **8.0U**: two labels on the **front stabilizer**, one at each end, with the power inlet panel
  between them. Left end: the `SERIAL NUMBER` sticker, barcode `7802452601000002`, `SPIRIT (870)
  935-1107`, `MADE IN TAIWAN`, `U.S. Pat. See www.spiritfitness.com/legal`. Right end: a
  `Product UDI code: 04711517854111 / Serial Number: 13120798-70-26010002` label with a GS1 barcode
  `(01) 0 4711517 85411 1 (21) 13120798-70-26010002`. Two numbers on one machine: the Spirit
  serial begins with the 780245 part number, the UDI serial does not.
- **8.5R**: the same pair on the **front stabilizer** - the `SERIAL NUMBER` sticker at the left end
  beside a `Product UDI code: 04711517854104 / Serial Number: 13120798-80-26010002` label. **The
  photographed Spirit serial reads `7851452601000002`**, which begins 785145 while the service manual
  cover and the owner's manual file name carry 784145. The photograph is of a production unit; the
  manual does not explain the prefix.

**The service manuals print no safety chapter, no safety list, no Proposition 65 text and no
patient-safety page.** The words `patient`, `contraindication`, `grounding`, `outlet` and `caution`
do not occur in the six books as safety text - `ground wire` is a part name, `outlet` a
troubleshooting check, `safety` a cover part on the 7.0R - and the 4.0R and 4.0U books print only a
wear warning in their general-maintenance section. The photograph above is the only
safety-related content any of the six has. Checked in the text layer, with letter-spacing allowed,
and in the OCR supplements.

**The Dyaco PT editions of the 4.0 R and 4.0 U and the Dyaco MED 7.0R print no labels page and no
serial number panel at all** - the words `serial` and `sticker` do not occur in those three books
(`spirit-pt-bike-safety-instructions-list-dyaco-edition`).

**Other Spirit machines put it elsewhere.** The 8.5UE ergometer says front stabilizer and the
Proposition 65 sticker `on the shroud near the plug-in` (`85ue-2025-safety-product-labels-and-proposition-65`);
the steppers put it on the level bar or the shroud (`spirit-climber-safety-product-labels-stepper`).
