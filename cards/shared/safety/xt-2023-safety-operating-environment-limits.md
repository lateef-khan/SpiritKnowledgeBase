> NEVER expose this unit to rain or moisture. This product is NOT designed for use outdoors,
> near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment. The temperature
> specification is 40 degrees C, and humidity is 95%, non-condensing (no water drops forming
> on surfaces).

| Limit | Figure |
|---|---|
| Temperature | 40 degrees C |
| Humidity | 95%, non-condensing |

**Non-condensing** is the manual's own gloss: no water drops forming on surfaces.

The manual prints **one** temperature figure and no minimum, no Fahrenheit conversion and no
storage range. A garage or a porch is not named either way; what is named is rain, moisture,
outdoors, a pool or spa, and any other high-humidity environment.

The safety instructions page also says, more shortly, "Do not use outdoors"
(`xt-2023-safety-instructions-list`).
