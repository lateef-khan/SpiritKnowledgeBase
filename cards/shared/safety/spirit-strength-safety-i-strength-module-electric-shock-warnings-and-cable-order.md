**The module's maintenance manual opens with six bulleted electric-shock warnings and a two-column table giving the cable order. This is the technician's page; the owner's-manual safeguards block is a different, shorter list** (`spirit-strength-safety-csi-electrical-and-grounding-block`).

> Please read the safety information carefully before use the machine.
>
> Electrical current from power, telephone, and communication cables is dangerous. To avoid the risk of electric shock:
>
> - plug or unplug cables, nor install, maintain, or reconfigure this product during a thunderstorm.
> - Connect the power cord correctly and use a well-grounded power outlet.
> - Whenever possible, use only one hand to connect or disconnect device cables.
> - Never turn on any equipment when there are signs of fire, flooding, or house collapse.
> - Unless otherwise instructed during installation and configuration, disconnect equipment such as power cords, telecommunication systems, networks, and modems that are connected before opening the equipment cover.
> - installing, moving, or removing the back cover of this product, plug and unplug device cables as described in the following table:

| When connected, do the following: | When disconnected, do the following: |
|---|---|
| 1. Turn off all devices | 1. Turn off all devices |
| 2. Connect the power cable to the device | 2. Unplug the power cord from the power outlet |
| 3. Connect the signal cable to the corresponding port | 3. Unplug the signal cable from each connector |
| 4. Connect the power cable to the socket | 4. Unplug the power cord from the device |
| 5. Turn on the device | |

- **The first and sixth bullets are printed with their first word missing.** The page reads `plug or unplug cables, nor install ...` and `installing, moving, or removing the back cover ...`; the sense is *Do not* plug or unplug cables during a thunderstorm and *When* installing, moving or removing the back cover. Both are quoted as printed.
- **Power cable first, then signal cable, then the wall; the reverse to disconnect** - wall first, then signal, then the cable at the device. The table is headed "when connected" and "when disconnected"; it describes connecting and disconnecting.
- **One hand** whenever possible, so that a fault cannot pass across the chest.
- **Open the cover only with every cord out**, unless the installation instructions say otherwise.

**The owner's manuals say less.** The CSI safeguards page carries a grounding sentence, a nominal 120-volt circuit and `NEVER remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power`; it has no storm, one-hand or cable-order rule, and its 120-volt figure disagrees with the machine's own rating decals (`spirit-strength-safety-csi-electrical-and-grounding-block`). The maintenance manual prints no supply voltage on this page at all.

**The same chapter continues with four cautions on the module's battery, its power cords and its hazard-labelled housings** (`spirit-strength-safety-i-strength-module-power-down-battery-and-hazard-labels`).
