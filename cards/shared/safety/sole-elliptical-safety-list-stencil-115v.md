- **DANGER**: disconnect from the outlet before cleaning or service.
- **WARNING**: flat level surface with a grounded outlet (**115-volt, 15-amp**, E55 pair 110-volt), only the elliptical on the circuit; **14AWG-or-better** single-outlet cord; never defeat the grounded plug.
- Carpet damage rule; **keep children under 13 away** (pinch points); hands away; damaged cord plus call-your-dealer; heated surfaces; aerosol/oxygen with sparks that may ignite; openings; outdoors; disconnect off-then-unplug; intended purpose; pulse disclaimer; proper shoes; **not intended for persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities** without supervision or instruction.
- **SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!**

The E55-2016/2019 books print 110-volt at the outlet (their grounding paragraph says 115-volt); the E20-2016/2020 pair prints a different, older-style list with its own card.
