**A medical device's climatic specification, in metric, with five figures instead of one - the same
block on all four 2025 rehabilitation bikes and on the Dyaco MED 7.0R.**

| Limit | Figure |
|---|---|
| Temperature, in use | **+10 to +36 degrees C** |
| Relative humidity | **30 to 90 %**, non-condensing |
| Air pressure | **700 to 1060 mbar** |
| Maximum operating altitude | approx. **10,000 feet (3000 m)**, without pressurization |
| Transport and storage temperature | **-20 to +50 degrees C** |

The heading over the block names the maker rather than the brand:

> If not stated otherwise Spirit by Dyaco 7.0R Recumbent Bikes are designed for operation in
> normal climatic surroundings (IEC 60601-1)

The 7.0U reads `Dyaco 7.0U`, the 8.0U and 8.5R `Dyaco MED products`, the 2021 book `Dyaco 7.0R` -
the OEM's name left in a Spirit-branded manual, as on the 8.5UE and the steppers. It is not evidence
of a second product.

The same page adds three absolute exclusions and the cover rule:

> Never remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power.

> Never expose this 7.0R to rain or moisture. This product is not designed for use outdoors, near a
> pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.

> The 7.0R is not protected against the ingress of water or particulate matter.

> The 7.0R is not suitable for use in an oxygen rich environment.

**The oxygen sentence is the only aerosol-or-oxygen rule these books print** - a flat unsuitability,
as on the 7.0T and 8.0T treadmills (`spirit-2026t-safety-aerosol-and-oxygen`); no sentence about
sparks or aerosol sprays.

**The figures are printed twice and the second printing adds the room.** The APPLICATION
SPECIFICATION page repeats `Temperature range: 10°C ~ 36°C` and `Relative humidity range: 30% R.H. ~
90% R.H., non-condensing` under INTENDED CONDITIONS OF USE, and adds `standard ambient room lighting
is sufficient`, a viewing distance of **1 m** and a viewing angle of **120°**, `intended for hospital
use, clinic use, home use and research in academic institutions`, and `The product is intended to
be fixed` (`spirit-med-bike-safety-intended-patient-population-and-medical-purpose`).

**Do not answer from the self-powered bikes' Fahrenheit card.** The 4.0R and 4.0U print `5 to 48
degrees Celsius (40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit)` and `95% non-condensing`
(`spirit-med-bike-safety-operating-environment-5-to-48-c`) - a wider band, a higher humidity ceiling
and no pressure or altitude figure. 10 to 36 C is 50 to 97 F, so the rehabilitation bikes are the
stricter by a wide margin at both ends.

**The 8.5UE ergometer, the 7.0S, 7.5S and 8.5S steppers and the 7.0T and 8.0T treadmills print the
identical block** (`85ue-2025-safety-operating-environment-iec-60601`,
`spirit-climber-safety-operating-environment-iec-60601`,
`spirit-2026t-safety-operating-environment-limits`) - the same Dyaco MED platform, other product
lines. The figures agree; quote this card for a bike.
