**Five rower books print a boxed CAUTION on the adjustment page**, beside the balance-adjustment
note:

> CAUTION! Moving parts, such as the seat, can crush and cut. Keep hands clear of the Slide Rail
> during use. (ERG550W, ERG600W, ERG650W, ERG750W)

> ! Moving parts, such as the seat, could crush and cut your body. Please do not touch the slide
> rail when using the machine! (ERG180)

The hazard is the seat's rollers running on the rail while someone rows: a hand, or a child's hand,
on the rail is in the seat's path. It is a use-time rule - the same books tell you to keep hands
away from all moving parts in the safety list, and this is the one place they name the part.

**The other six rower books print no crush warning** - the ERG160, ERG220, ERG400, ERG500, ERG700
and ERG800W say only `Keep hands away from all moving parts`; the ERG800W adds, in an assembly step,
`the sliding basement will slide after the strap is cut off. Please be careful to not pinch
yourself.` The seat's second hazard - it slides down the rail on its own when the rower is stood up
for storage - is on `xterra-rower-safety-vertical-storage-hazards-seat-slides-down-and-head-near-the-rear-stabilizer`.
