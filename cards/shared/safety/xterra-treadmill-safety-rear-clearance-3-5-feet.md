**The seven older-layout manuals give one clearance figure, for the rear only.**

> Do not block the rear of the equipment. Provide a minimum of 3 1/2 feet clearance between the rear
> of the equipment and any fixed object.

Two more bullets on the same page settle the floor and the surface:

> Do not operate equipment on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and equipment
> may result

> ...install the treadmill on a flat level surface with access to a 120-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet.

| Requirement | Figure |
|---|---|
| Behind the treadmill | minimum **3 1/2 feet** to any fixed object |
| Sides and front | **no figure printed** |
| Floor | flat and level; not deep, plush or shag carpet |

Unlike the new-layout panel this one is a stated minimum, not a recommendation, and it gives no side
or front distance and says nothing about children or pets. The new-layout books ask for 6 feet behind
and 20 inches around (`xterra-treadmill-safety-location-20-in-front-and-sides-6-ft-behind`); the 2024 TRX
books print no figure (`xterra-trx-safety-no-clearance-figure-printed`).

The Spirit XT-2015 books print the same bullet for their own machines (`xt-2015-safety-placement-requirements`);
the Spirit 7.0T's two-feet-each-side figure is a different rule (`70t-2026-safety-clearance-around-the-treadmill`).
