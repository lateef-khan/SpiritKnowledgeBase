**The two manuals print this page word for word**, with one sentence of difference noted at the
foot of this card. Measured on the safety, electrical and operation pages together they agree at
**94% at word level**.

> **WARNING - Read all instructions before using this appliance.**

- Do not operate elliptical on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and
  elliptical may result.
- Keep children away from the elliptical. There are obvious pinch points and other caution areas
  that can cause harm.
- Keep hands away from all moving parts.
- Never operate the elliptical if it has a damaged cord or plug. If the elliptical is not working
  properly, call your dealer.
- Keep the cord away from heated surfaces.
- Do not operate where aerosol spray products are being used or where oxygen is being administered.
  Sparks from the motor may ignite a highly gaseous environment.
- Never drop or insert any object into any openings.
- Do not use outdoors.
- To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position, then remove the plug from the outlet.
- Do not attempt to use your elliptical for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended.
- The hand pulse sensors are not medical devices. Various factors, including the user's movement,
  may affect the accuracy of heart rate readings. The pulse sensors are intended only as exercise
  aids in determining heart rate trends in general.
- Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable for use on your
  elliptical. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue.

> SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!

## What this list does not contain

**No outlet, no circuit rating and no extension cord.** These six machines are supplied with an
external power supply and neither manual prints a voltage, an amp figure, a breaker rating or an
extension-cord gauge anywhere (`spirit-xe-safety-no-outlet-figure-printed`). Never answer one of
these owners from a 2010 or 2021 XE outlet card.

**No grounding page and no temporary adapter rule.** Neither manual has a Grounding Instructions
page (`spirit-product-must-be-grounded`,
`spirit-temporary-adapter-for-a-two-pole-receptacle` cover the manuals that do).

**No user weight limit** (`xt-2015-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`), **no age figure**, no
`Keep children under the age of 13` item and no reduced-capability sentence. The only rule about
children is the pinch-points bullet above, which attaches no age
(`spirit-2026t-safety-keep-children-away`). Do not carry the age 13 rule back from a 2021 XE manual.

**No Proposition 65 warning**, no chemical named and no `www.P65Warnings.ca.gov` address anywhere
in either manual.

**No chest transmitter strap sentence.** The pulse-sensor bullet stops at `heart rate trends in
general`; the strap sentence that the 2010 and 2021 manuals add is not printed here
(`spirit-2026t-safety-heart-rate-readings-are-not-medical`).

## The rest of the chapter

Two more pages follow, and both manuals print them alike:

**Important Electrical Information** - never remove a cover without first disconnecting the AC power
supply (`xt-2023-safety-remove-a-cover-only-with-ac-disconnected`); a supply that varies by ten
percent or more may affect performance and is not covered by warranty
(`xt-2023-safety-supply-voltage-variation`); never expose the elliptical to rain or moisture, and it
is not designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment
(`ct900ent-operating-environment-voltage`).

**Important Operation Instructions** - never operate without understanding the change you ask the
computer for; resistance changes are gradual rather than immediate; never use the elliptical during
an electrical storm (`xt-2023-safety-use-during-an-electrical-storm`); take care with distractions
such as television or reading; **always hold on to a handrail or hand bar while making control
changes**; and do not use excessive pressure on the console keys.

The handrail sentence is printed in these two manuals and in no other XE manual in the range.

## One sentence separates the two manuals

**The XE100/XE200/XE300 manual prints the environmental ratings; the XE150/XE350/XE550 manual does
not.** On the electrical page the 2007 manual closes with
`Maximum environmental ratings are 40-120 degrees Fahrenheit, 95% humidity non-condensing (no water
droplets forming on surfaces)`. That sentence is absent from the 2005 manual, whose moisture bullet
stops at `high humidity environment`. **There is no temperature or humidity figure for an XE150,
XE350 or XE550** (`ct900ent-operating-environment-voltage`).

**Do not blanket-apply anything else in these two manuals to all three machines they cover.** Each
has per-model content elsewhere - a separate Assembly Pack Check List per model and steps headed
`XE100 ONLY` and `XE200 ONLY` in the 2007 manual, three separate console sections and parts marked
`XE150, XE350 only` and `XE550 only` in the 2005 manual. The safety chapter above is the part that
is genuinely common to all six.
