**This is the 250 lb figure**, printed four ways in six books:

| Book | How it is printed |
|---|---|
| FB160, FB360 | `Weight Limit: 250 LBS.` - the last Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions bullet |
| FB180 | `16. User Weight Limit: 250 lbs.` - item 16 of the new-layout SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| AIR350 | `11. Weight Limit: 250 lbs.` - item 11 of the air bike safety list |
| SB120 | `10. Weight Limit: 250 lbs.` - item 10 of the recumbent safety list |
| UB120 | `9. Weight Limit: 250 lbs.` - item 9 of the upright safety list |

None of the six has a specification table; the figure is printed once per book. The SB120 service
document (December 2019) repeats it as `User weight limit- 250 lbs` under *Specs*, the only second
source for any of them.

The FB180 adds a boxed note under its list: `Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user
weight restrictions and power requirements of your new machine. Failure to do so may result in
serious injury or damage to your machine.`

**Do not carry 250 across a family.** The FB150 and FB350 folding bikes are 225 lbs
(`xterra-fb-safety-user-weight-limit-225-lbs`); the AIR650, the AIR350's bigger sibling, is 350 lbs
(`air650-2021-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lbs`); the SB150, SB250, SB2.5r and SB4500 recumbents print
no limit at all (`xterra-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`).
