**All three indoor cycle books print the same eleven items in the same words**, and none of them
prints a weight limit. The MB500 page was read from the render of the scanned book as well as the OCR.

> WARNING - Read all instructions before using this appliance.

| # | Instruction |
|---|---|
| 1 | Use this equipment only for its intended use as described in this manual. Do not attempt to ride this bike at high pedal speeds until you have ridden the bike for some time and are comfortable riding at slower pedal speeds. |
| 2 | The bike is NOT equipped with a freewheel system which means that when the flywheel is in motion, the pedals will be in motion. Do not attempt to stop the unit by applying backward pressure to pedals while they are turning as knee injury may occur. Do not attempt to remove your feet from pedals while they are moving. |
| 3 | Wait for flywheel to coast to a stop before dismounting the bike. If you want to stop the flywheel, push down on the brake knob. (items 2 and 3: `xterra-mb-safety-no-freewheel-pedals-keep-turning-and-how-to-stop`) |
| 4 | Serious injury or death may occur from over-training. Consult a medical doctor or qualified fitness instructor to determine an exercise program appropriate for your level of fitness. |
| 5 | Do not attempt to turn the pedal cranks by hand. Do not touch any driving mechanism while it is in motion as possible injury could occur. |
| 6 | In a home setting, keep children away from the bike when it is not in use. Keep children and pets away from the unit while it is in use. |
| 7 | Do not attempt to perform dip movements on handlebars. |
| 8 | Never drop or insert any object into any opening of the bike. |
| 9 | Only use the bike on a stable, level floor. |
| 10 | Follow instructions for safe use of the equipment including proper seat position, handlebar position, and use of foot positioning system of pedals. Do not attempt to pull up handlebar post and seat post over the 'MAX.' graduation. (`xterra-bike-safety-do-not-raise-seat-or-handlebar-post-past-max-mark`) |
| 11 | For safe operation, allow for at least 1 foot (30cm) of free space to either side of the unit and 2 feet (60cm) of free space to the rear of the unit. (`xterra-bike-safety-clearance-1-ft-sides-2-ft-rear`) |

Below the list: `Regularly examine the bike for damage and wear. Inoperable components should be
replaced immediately or the equipment should not be used until it is repaired.`, the `Failure to
follow all guidelines...` sentence and `SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!`.

| Book | Proposition 65 warning on this page | Facing page |
|---|---|---|
| MB500 (2014) | **none** (`xterra-safety-no-proposition-65-warning-printed`) | five owner guidelines |
| MB550, MBX2500 (2018) | yes, Toluene and Acrylamide (`xterra-treadmill-safety-proposition-65-warning`) | six owner guidelines (`xterra-bike-safety-operation-instructions-owner-guidelines`) |

**No weight limit is printed** on any of the three, here or anywhere else in the book
(`xterra-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`). The air bikes print the same list without item 2 and
with a weight limit as item 11 (`xterra-air-safety-instructions-list-11-items`).

The Spirit CIC indoor cycles and the Sole SB700/SB900 print this list for their own machines
(`spirit-cic-safety-instructions-list`, `sole-indoor-cycle-riding-and-setup-rules`).
