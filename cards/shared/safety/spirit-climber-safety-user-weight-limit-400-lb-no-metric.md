**400 lb, and neither manual prints a metric figure beside it.**

| Machine | Printed as |
|---|---|
| CVC800 vertical climber | `User Weight Limit: 400 lb` |
| XS895 incline stepper, 2018 and 2021 | `Weight Limit: 400 LBS` |

The XS895 drops the word *User* and writes the unit in capitals. The allowance is the same and each
manual states it once only. Neither says what happens above it.

**Do not answer from the stairclimber card.** The CSC900 is also 400 lb, but it prints the figure
**twice** and gives `180 kg` beside it (`csc900-2024-safety-user-weight-limit-400-lb`). If a caller
asks for the metric figure, only the CSC900 manual has one; do not convert 400 lb yourself and do
not carry 180 kg onto a CVC800 or an XS895.

**The 2018 and 2021 XS895 books are the same page.** Their safety chapters match at 99.8% on an
order-free word count of the printed pages, and every figure on them is identical.

**Neither machine is 450 lb.** The CRS800S and CS800 steppers of the same range are
(`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`).
