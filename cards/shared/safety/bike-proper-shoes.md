**Wear proper shoes.** High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are **not** suitable for use on your fitness bike. **Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue or injury.**

Twelve of the 14 manuals end that sentence at "leg fatigue or injury". **The LCB/LCR 2023 manual ends it at "to avoid leg fatigue."** and drops "or injury". Nothing else in the sentence differs.

The ten indoor cycle manuals (SB700, SB900, SB1200) print **no footwear rule at all**.
