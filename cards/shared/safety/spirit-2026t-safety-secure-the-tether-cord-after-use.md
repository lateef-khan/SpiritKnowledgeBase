**Two of the three 2026 manuals answer this, and they ask for two different actions.**

The **4.0T** says take the cord off the machine:

> 28. Remove tether cord after use to prevent unauthorized treadmill operation.

The **8.0T** says put it somewhere children cannot reach:

> 22. Keep the safety tether cord in the proper and safe location to avoid being attached by
>     children to start the equipment.

- Both rules exist for the same reason: **with the magnet in place anyone can start the machine.**
- The 4.0T's rule is about **anyone** unauthorised; the 8.0T's names **children** specifically.
- Neither manual says where "the proper and safe location" is, and neither describes a lock or a
  code.

**The 7.0T manual states no such rule.** Its tether-cord instruction covers only what the cord is
for and that it should be used at all times
(`spirit-2026t-safety-tether-cord-stops-the-belt`); it says nothing about removing or storing it
afterwards. Do not tell a 7.0T owner their manual asks for this.

The related keep-away rules are `spirit-2026t-safety-keep-children-away` and, for the 4.0T only,
the age-8 supervision clause `40t-2026-safety-children-aged-8-and-above`.
