**350 lb / 160 kg**, on both CSI machines — the CSI-CPSP chest press / shoulder press and the
CSI-LROW low row. It is the lowest user weight limit in the Spirit strength range and **10 lb below
every CSS and CSD machine**, so never carry a 360 lb answer across to a CSI.

| Where | Printed as |
|---|---|
| Safety instructions, item 20 | `User Weight Limit: 350 lb / 160 kg.` |
| Safeguards, Proper Usage | `The maximum weight for individuals using the Strength Equipment should not exceed 350 lb (160 kg).` |
| Name plate decal on the machine | `Load max.  160kg/360lb` |

**The decal and the manual disagree in pounds.** The name plate says 360 lb where the manual says
350 lb; the metric figure, 160 kg, is the same on both and converts to 353 lb. The manual's 350 lb
is the figure to quote — it is printed twice, in the two places a reader is sent to, and it is the
conservative one. A second decal on the same page, the rating label, gives `Maximum Weight: 160KG`
with no pounds at all.

The safeguards page these machines print is rewritten from the rest of the range and adds a
pacemaker warning above the weight line.
