**The 2016 and 2020 manuals name the place in words:**

> The serial number is located at the front of the treadmill by the Power switch.

The power switch on these machines is at the front, below the motor hood - the same place the
console chapter sends you to switch the treadmill on. The manuals do not mention a barcode and do
not print a sample number.

**The 2012 and ENT-2022 manuals give no location in words.** The 2012 manual prints only a
`Record Your Serial Number` panel with a blank line. The ENT-2022 manual prints the same panel
beside an illustration captioned `Serial Number Location` and names no part anywhere in its text.
For those two, point the customer at that illustration in their own manual or have them read the
number off the machine and confirm it - do not carry `by the Power switch` across, since neither
manual states it.

## None of the four manuals has a product labels page

**No safety warning sticker and no Proposition 65 sticker is described** in any CT800 owner's
manual. These manuals never say that a label is applied to the machine or where one would be, and
they print no label artwork.

**The Proposition 65 wording the 2016, 2020 and ENT-2022 manuals carry is printed inside the
manual**, on the operation instructions page, not described as a label:
`xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`. The 2012 manual prints no Proposition 65 wording at all.

## The four CT850 owner's manuals split exactly the same way

**The CT850 2016 and 2020 manuals name the place in the same words**, on their Record Your Serial
Number panel: `The serial number is located at the front of the treadmill by the Power switch`.
That is the same power switch below the motor hood the console chapter sends you to.

**The CT850 2018 and CT850ENT-2022 manuals give no location in words.** The 2018 manual prints only
a `RECORD YOUR SERIAL NUMBER` panel with a blank line, beside its Important Operation Instructions.
The ENT-2022 manual prints the same panel beside an illustration captioned `Serial Number Location`
and names no part anywhere in its text. For those two, point the customer at that illustration or
have them read the number off the machine - neither manual states `by the Power switch`.

**No CT850 owner's manual has a product labels page either.** None of the four says that a label is
applied to the machine or where one would be, and none prints label artwork. The Proposition 65
wording the four carry is printed inside the manual, on the operation instructions page, and is not
described as a sticker (`xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`).

**Do not answer a CT800 or CT850 from another family's label card.** The 2023 XT and 2026 manuals put the
serial number sticker on the rear of the motor cover
(`xt-2023-safety-serial-number-sticker`), the CT900 on the back of the motor cover with a barcode
and two upright stickers (`ct900-product-labels`), and the MT200-2010 on the front of the treadmill
with no further detail (`mt200-2010-safety-serial-number-location`). Those are other machines'
statements.
