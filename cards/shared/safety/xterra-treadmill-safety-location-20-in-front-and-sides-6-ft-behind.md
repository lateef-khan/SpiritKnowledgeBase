**Seven new-layout manuals print the same panel** on the right-hand half of the grounding page, under
a plan drawing labelled `20in` on three sides and `6ft` at one end. The TR75H prints the same panel
with 24 inches (`tr75h-2025-safety-location-24-in-front-and-sides-6-ft-behind`).

> After assembling your treadmill, you'll need to make sure you've installed it in a safe area.

| Requirement | Figure |
|---|---|
| Front and both sides | at least **20in** |
| Behind the treadmill | at least **6ft** |

- **The front is the motor end.** `The front of the unit is where the motor is located.`
- **Install your treadmill in an area where children and pets cannot access it.**
- **Always keep the area around your treadmill clear** of furniture, exercise equipment and other debris.
- **Do not install your treadmill on deeply padded, plush, or shag carpet.**

Both figures are recommendations - `We recommend that you leave an area of at least...` - and the
panel gives no minimum it calls mandatory. The TR95H prints `Hiker Treadmill` for `treadmill`
throughout the panel.

**This panel is the only clearance statement in these seven books**; their safety lists print no
`3 1/2 feet` bullet. The older-layout books print that bullet and nothing else
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-rear-clearance-3-5-feet`), and the 2024 TRX books print no clearance
figure at all (`xterra-trx-safety-no-clearance-figure-printed`).

The Spirit CT800-2024 family prints the identical panel (`spirit-2024-safety-treadmill-location-requirements`);
that is a Spirit card.
