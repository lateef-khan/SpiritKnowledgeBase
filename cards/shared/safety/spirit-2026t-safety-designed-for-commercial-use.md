Both manuals say so in their safety instructions, each naming its own machine:

> 11. This 7.0T is designed for commercial use and will meet the demands of orthopedic, sports
>     wellness and general conditioning programs.

> 12. Do not attempt to use your 7.0T for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended.

The Application Specification and the operating chapter restate the purpose at greater length. The
8.0T's version:

> This product is intended to be used to redevelop muscles, restore motion to joints, in aiding in
> the physical rehabilitation process for patients with orthopedic and neurological problems. Also
> used in sports medicine, wellness and general conditioning programs.

The 7.0T's is the same sentence written round the other way - `The 7.0T is intended to be used in
aiding in the physical rehabilitation process for patients with orthopedic and neurological
problems. Also used in sports medicine, wellness and general conditioning programs.`

- **Commercial use is the design intent, not a permitted extra.** These are the first Spirit
  treadmills in this knowledge base whose owner's manuals say so on the safety page.
- **They are rehabilitation devices.** Both manuals are written to IEC 60601-1, the medical
  electrical equipment standard, and both use the word *patient* throughout.
- **Home use is still named** as one of the intended locations:
  `spirit-2026t-safety-intended-conditions-of-use`.

**The 4.0T is not described this way.** Its manual carries the same no-other-purpose rule (item
12) but never calls the machine commercial, never mentions rehabilitation, and has no Application
Specification page. See `40t-2026-safety-instructions-list`.

**Both Spirit MT200 owner's manuals say the same thing on their safety instructions page**, with
`device` in place of the machine's name:

> This device is designed for commercial use and will meet the demands of orthopedic, sports
> wellness and general conditioning programs.

> Do not attempt to use your treadmill for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended.

Both are rehabilitation devices written to IEC 60601-1 and both use the word *patient* throughout,
and **both name home use as one of the intended locations** alongside hospitals, clinics and
academic research. The 2010 manual's title page reads `MT200 Rehabilitation Treadmill` and credits
Spirit Medical Systems Group; the 2022 manual's cover carries only the ReCare name and Spirit
Fitness branding, and its opening paragraph calls the machine a `physical rehabilitation
treadmill`. The design intent stated on the safety page is identical in both.
