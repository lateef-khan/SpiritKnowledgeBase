**Both MT200 owner's manuals print this page, and the two lists are the same list.** The 2010
manual sets it as one page of bullets; the 2022 manual runs the same bullets across three pages
under Attention, Danger and Warning headings. Nothing is added or dropped between the two.

The page opens:

> ATTENTION - Read all instructions in this manual before using this device.

> DANGER - To reduce the risk of electric shock disconnect your treadmill from the electrical
> outlet prior to cleaning and/or service work -
> `spirit-disconnect-from-the-outlet-before-cleaning-or-service`.

Three WARNINGs follow:

1. **Do not modify this equipment without authorization of the manufacturer** -
   `spirit-2026t-safety-do-not-modify-or-fit-aftermarket-parts`.
2. **Install on a flat level surface with access to a 115 volt AC, 60 Hz, 20-amp grounded outlet**;
   no extension cord unless 12awg or larger with one outlet on the end; the treadmill the only
   appliance on the circuit; never disable the grounded plug -
   `spirit-mt200-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`,
   `spirit-2026t-safety-extension-cord-12-awg-or-larger`.
3. **To avoid risk of electric shock, this equipment must only be connected to a supply main with
   protective earth** - `spirit-product-must-be-grounded`.

Then the bullets, in the manual's own order:

- Use this device only for it's intended use as described in this manual. *(`it's` is printed that
  way in both manuals.)*
- Keep children away from the treadmill; moving parts, obvious pinch points and other caution areas
  can cause harm - `spirit-2026t-safety-keep-children-away`.
- Except as instructed for use of the device, keep hands away from all moving parts.
- Keep the electrical cord away from heated surfaces and out of all travel lanes, and do not operate
  the treadmill if the cord or plug is damaged -
  `spirit-2026t-safety-power-cord-condition-and-routing`.
- Never drop or insert any object into any openings.
- Do not use outdoors.
- To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position then remove the plug from the outlet.
- This device is designed for commercial use and will meet the demands of orthopedic, sports
  wellness and general conditioning programs -
  `spirit-2026t-safety-designed-for-commercial-use`.
- Do not attempt to use your treadmill for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended.
- The pulse sensors are not medical devices -
  `spirit-2026t-safety-heart-rate-readings-are-not-medical`.
- Heart rate monitoring system may be inaccurate. Over exercise may result in injury or death. If
  you feel faint stop exercising immediately -
  `spirit-2026t-safety-over-exercise-can-cause-injury-or-death`.
- Two feet of free space at each side, one foot at the front and three and a half feet at the rear -
  `70t-2026-safety-clearance-around-the-treadmill`.
- No after market parts other than those recommended by Spirit -
  `spirit-2026t-safety-do-not-modify-or-fit-aftermarket-parts`.
- No servicing or adjustments beyond those described in this manual -
  `spirit-2026t-safety-servicing-only-by-trained-personnel`.
- Installation and assembly by trained personnel only -
  `spirit-2026t-safety-who-may-install-and-operate`.
- **Hold the handlebar for support when getting on or off the treadmill.**
- **To avoid injury please observe all minimum and maximum adjustment settings.**
- Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable for use on the
  treadmill. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue.
- The safety tether cord is a simple magnetic design that should be used at all times; pulling it
  stops tread-belt movement - `spirit-2026t-safety-tether-cord-stops-the-belt`.

**This list has no user weight item and no boxed weight-and-power note.** The 440 lb / 200 kg limit
is printed on the Application Specification and Specifications pages instead
(`spirit-2026t-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lbs`).

**Two rules here are the only mounting advice either manual gives.** Hold the handlebar when
getting on or off, and observe the minimum and maximum adjustment settings. Neither manual gives a
start-up sequence, names step rails, or says whether the belt may be moving - do not fill those in
from a 2026 machine's card.

**The wording differences between the two MT200 manuals are cosmetic.** The 2010 manual writes
`12awg (2.5mm2)`, `115 volt AC`, `WARNING: Heart rate monitoring system...` and `recommended by
Spirit`; the 2022 manual writes `12awg`, `115-volt AC`, drops the `WARNING:` prefix and writes
`recommended by Spirit Fitness`. No rule and no figure changes.

**Do not answer an MT200 from another Spirit family's list.** The 7.0T and 8.0T print a numbered
version of nearly this same page but on a **120 volt** NEMA 5-20P circuit and with a weight item
(`spirit-2026t-safety-instructions-list`); the 4.0T and the XT treadmills print completely
different lists.

**The 2022 manual adds a contraindications paragraph the 2010 manual does not have**, on its
operation instructions page rather than here:
`spirit-2026t-safety-rehabilitation-contraindications`.

*Extraction note: the converted 2022 text loses the opening of two bullets - the `To disconnect`
bullet begins mid-sentence at `plug from the outlet`, and the pulse-sensor bullet breaks off after
`Various factors, including`. Both are quoted above from the 2010 manual, which prints them
whole.*
