**Every one of the fourteen manuals prints the same PRODUCT LABELS page with three stickers on it,
and only the locations change.**

| Sticker | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Serial number sticker** | carries a barcode; record the number below the barcode to register the warranty |
| **Proposition 65 warning** | `The State of California requires us to inform you that this unit was manufactured using chemicals that could cause harm with improper use` |
| **Safety warning sticker** | the same warning that is printed in the manual; read it before operating the unit |

The serial number sticker also carries the telephone number `(870) 935-1107`.

## Where each machine carries them

| Machine | Serial number | Proposition 65 | Safety warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| CT800-2024, CT850-2024, CT800ENT-2024, CT850ENT-2024 | back of the **motor cover** | left side of the upright | left side of the upright |
| CE800-2024, CE800ENT-2024 | the **frame** | side of the console upright | **right** side of the console upright |
| CE850-2024 | **front stabilizer** | front stabilizer | left side of the upright |
| CR800-2024 | front stabilizer | front stabilizer | left side of the upright |
| CU800-2024 | front stabilizer | front stabilizer | left side of the upright |
| CR800ENT-2024 | front stabilizer | front stabilizer | **right** side of the console upright |
| CRS800S-2024 | front stabilizer | front stabilizer | left side of the upright |
| CS800-2024 | the **frame** | the **console upright** | left side of the console upright |
| CSC900-2024 | the **main frame** | the **main frame** | left side of the upright |
| CRW800-2024 | the **middle stabilizer** | **near the handlebar** | **near the handlebar** |

**Left and right are not interchangeable.** The CE800, CE800ENT and CR800ENT put the safety warning
sticker on the *right* side of the console upright; every other machine in the range puts it on the
*left*. Give the face from the machine's own manual.

**The rower is the only machine with no upright**, and it is the only one whose two warning stickers
are described `near the handlebar` rather than by a named part.

**Each manual names its own machine** in the serial-number sentence - `your Treadmill's warranty`,
`your Recumbent Bike's warranty`, `your Semi-Recumbent Stepper's warranty`, `your Stair Climber's
warranty`, `your Rower's warranty`. The CE800, CE800ENT and CE850 books all say `your Elliptical's
warranty`, and the CU800 says `your Upright Bike's`.

**The Proposition 65 text is the short wording.** No chemical is named and no web address is printed
in the manual's own text; the sticker artwork itself carries `WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm
- www.P65Warnings.ca.gov`, which is on the label and not in the manual
(`xt-2023-safety-proposition-65-sticker`).

**The earlier CT800 and CT850 manuals have no such page.** They put the serial number by the power
switch and describe no stickers at all
(`spirit-ct800-safety-serial-number-and-product-labels`); do not read the motor-cover location back
onto them.
