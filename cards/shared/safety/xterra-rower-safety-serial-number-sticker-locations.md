**Five rower books show where the sticker is; the other six leave a blank and never say**
(`xterra-safety-serial-number-location-not-printed-rowers-and-strength`).

| Rower | Where | How the book says it |
|---|---|---|
| ERG160 (2020) | the main frame beneath the pedals | a *Serial Number Sticker* callout on the Before You Begin drawing, pointing at the frame under the pedals; the sticker shows the Xterra logo and (870) 333-5500 |
| ERG220 (2023) | the main frame near the rail pivot, behind the pedals | a *Serial Number Sticker* callout on the Before You Begin drawing, next to the locking-pin sticker |
| ERG180 (2023) | `on the center frame, near the pedals` | the Product Labels page (`xterra-rower-safety-product-labels-page`) |
| ERG800W (2023) | `on the frame behind the water tank` | the Product Labels page |
| ERG750W (2025) | `on the frame` - no part named | the Product Labels page |

Every one is a barcode sticker with the number printed under the bars; the ERG180 and ERG800W pages
say `record the number below the barcode`. The two 2020-2023 drawings are pictures and their callout
lines were read from the render, not the text layer; the ERG160 render is the only source for its
location, because the sticker text is too small for the OCR sweep to have caught.

**Likely but unstated**: the ERG550W, ERG600W and ERG650W share the ERG800W's tank-under-the-console
layout and the ERG500 and ERG700 the ERG220's folding frame, so *behind the tank* and *near the pivot*
are the places to look first on those - offered as places to look, not as what their books say.

The Xterra bikes and treadmills have their own location cards
(`xterra-bike-safety-serial-number-sticker-locations`, `xterra-tr-safety-serial-number-on-the-frame-near-the-power-switch`);
the Spirit water rowers put theirs on the centre floor tube (`spirit-water-rower-safety-serial-number-on-the-centre-floor-tube`).
