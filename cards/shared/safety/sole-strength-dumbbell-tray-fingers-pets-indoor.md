The owner must ensure all users are adequately informed of all precautions. Before any exercise program, consult a physician - especially over age 35 or with pre-existing health problems. Not intended for persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lacking experience and knowledge, unless supervised or instructed by someone responsible for their safety.

- Intended for **indoor, home use only** - not commercial, rental or institutional settings.
- Place on a **firm, level surface**, with ample clearance around and above for the exercises.
- **Place only the included dumbbell on the tray.**
- **Keep children under age 13 and pets away** from this product.
- **Always wear protective footwear. Never place fingers between the dumbbell and the tray.**
- Use only as described. **Over exercising may result in serious injury or death** - stop immediately and cool down for faintness, shortness of breath or pain.

Word for word in all three books. The half-rack (SW121) and SRVO books print none of these dumbbell rules.
