**Keep children away from the elliptical. There are obvious pinch points and other caution areas that can cause harm.** No age figure is given anywhere in these books, and no reduced-capabilities paragraph is printed either. Do not quote `under the age of 13` for these machines - that rule first appears in the stencil books (linked card, which differs exactly on the age figure).
