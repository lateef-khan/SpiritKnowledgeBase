**The APPLICATION SPECIFICATION page, the same on all four 2025 rehabilitation bikes and on the Dyaco
MED 7.0R.**

> **MEDICAL PURPOSES**
> - Patient warm-up before a physical therapy session.
> - Have the patient walk to improve ambulation and range of motion after knee/hip/ankle surgery or
>   neurological conditions.
> - Allow patients to perform cardiovascular exercise.
> - Used for open kinetic chain exercise only, patient is not restrained or connected to the 7.0R.
> - 7.0R is a device intended to be used to redevelop muscles or restore motion to joints.

> **INTENDED PATIENT POPULATION**
> - Male and Female Adults. Children at the discretion of a Qualified Physical Therapist and or
>   parental consent.
> - Maximal patient's weight is 440 lb.
> - Patient must be ambulatory.
> - Patient should have medical clearance before starting any rigorous exercise program. This is
>   especially important for person with a history of heart disease or other high risk factors.

> **INTENDED PART OF THE BODY OR TYPE OF TISSUE APPLIED TO OR INTERACTED WITH**
> - Contact site: hands, feet, and trunk
> - Condition: should not have any trauma

- **`Have the patient walk`** is printed on a bike, on all five books; the `Operating the 7.0R` page
  two pages later says `pedal`. The purpose is the same - range of motion after knee, hip or ankle
  surgery or a neurological condition.
- **No age is printed.** Children are admitted at a physical therapist's discretion and/or with
  parental consent, and nothing else. The self-powered bikes say 8
  (`spirit-med-bike-safety-children-aged-8-and-above-if-supervised`).
- **Open kinetic chain only** - the patient is not strapped in or connected to the machine.
- **The weight line is the contraindication figure** (`spirit-med-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lb`,
  `spirit-med-bike-safety-contraindications-osteoporosis-fractures-and-acute-conditions`).

**Where it may be used**, from INTENDED CONDITIONS OF USE on the same page: indoors, `hospital use,
clinic use, home use and research in academic institutions`; frequency `dependent on therapist's
plan`; `The product is intended to be fixed`. The climatic figures beside them are
`spirit-med-bike-safety-operating-environment-iec-60601`.

**Item 18 of the safety list says the same in one sentence** - `This 7.0R is designed for commercial
use and will meet the demands of orthopedic, sports wellness and general conditioning programs`
(item 10 on the 7.0U, 11 on the 8.0U, 18 on the 8.5R) - and the `Operating the 7.0R` page opens
`intended to be used in aiding in the physical rehabilitation process for patients with orthopedic
and neurological problems. Also used in sports medicine, wellness and general conditioning
programs`.

**The 8.0U and 8.5R add heart rate to the purposes** - `Allow patients to perform cardiovascular
exercise, and measure heart rate with a heart rate monitor (the heart rate monitor isn't standard,
it is an optional purchase by users). The heart rate readings are not for medical purpose but for
information purposes only` - and drop the `redevelop muscles` bullet into the page's opening
paragraph.

**The Dyaco MED 7.0R of 2021 prints the same page** with `Maximal patient's weight is 200kg`.

**Who operates and installs it is a separate profile**
(`spirit-med-bike-safety-who-may-install-and-operate`). The 7.0T and 8.0T treadmills and the 8.5UE
ergometer print the same population for their own lines
(`spirit-2026t-safety-intended-patient-population`, `85ue-2025-safety-who-may-not-use-the-ergometer`).
