**They do not.** Searched across all thirty-six CSS, CSD, CSF and CSI manuals, the words *anchor*,
*anchoring*, *bolted*, *floor mount* and *rocking* do not appear anywhere — not in the safety
chapter, not in the assembly steps, not in maintenance. The one phrase that comes close, *so it will
not tip over*, is an assembly caution about standing the machine upright on a stable surface while
you build it, and appears in all thirty-six books. The pages an anchoring rule would sit on were read
as 300 dpi renders as well as from the text layer, so this is an absence on the page, not an
extraction gap.

What the manuals ask for instead is a **solid, level surface**, stated three times:

- Facility safety precautions: *Make sure that each unit is set up and operated on a solid level surface.*
- Safety guidelines: *Do not use machine if it is not located on a solid level surface or is improperly installed.*
- And the live-area perimeter, which keeps the machine clear of walls and neighbours.

So for these thirty-six machines the correct answer is that Spirit sets no floor-anchoring
requirement, and a facility that wants to anchor one is going beyond the manual rather than
complying with it.

**Two Spirit strength products do have an anchoring rule**, and both are ST800: the ST800DR3 dumbbell
rack must be anchored through the holes provided, and the ST800FT functional trainer has a whole
SECURING EQUIPMENT page with a pull-out force figure. Do not read either across to a CSS, CSD, CSF or
CSI machine.
