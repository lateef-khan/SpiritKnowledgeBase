**600 lb.** Three CSF benches print it as item 20 of their safety instructions, in the slot the CSS
and CSD machines use for the user weight limit:

> 20. Max Loading Capacity: 600 lb

The safeguards page repeats it in its Proper Usage list: *The maximum loading weight should not
exceed 600 pounds.* No metric figure is given anywhere in either book, and the manuals do not say
whether the 600 lb includes the user.

The three machines are **CSF-AABB** (abdominal crunch board), **CSF-BEXT** (back extension bench) and
**CSF-UPRB** (multi-purpose bench). Their serial decals carry a separate figure, `180 KG / 396 LBS`,
which is the *user* weight limit — a different allowance, on a different label.

The other CSF machines each print a different load figure in the same slot: CSF-LEGP a 770 lb
carriage capacity, CSF-HRAC a 650 lb safety-catch limit, CSF-FUNT a 400 lb user weight limit and no
load figure.
