- **NEVER operate this elliptical without reading and completely understanding the results of any operational change you request from the computer.**
- Changes in **resistance do not occur immediately**. Set your desired level on the console and release the adjustment key; the computer obeys gradually. No incline is named - these books predate the incline wording.
- **NEVER use your elliptical during an electrical storm**; surges in the household supply could damage components. **No unplug sentence is printed** - the precaution to unplug appears only in later books.
- **Use caution with distractions** such as watching television or reading; they may cause you to lose balance, which may result in serious injury.
- **Always hold on to a handrail or hand bar while making control changes** (no parenthetical list).
- **Do not use excessive pressure on console control keys**; pushing harder will not make the unit go faster or slower. Contact your Sole dealer if they misbehave with normal pressure.
