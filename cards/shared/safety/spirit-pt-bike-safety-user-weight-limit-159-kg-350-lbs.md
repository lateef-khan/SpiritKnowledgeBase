> Maximum User Weight: 159 kg (350 lbs.)

**That is the Dyaco PT edition's figure for the 4.0 R recumbent and the 4.0 U upright**, printed as
the last bullet but one of the safety list, in English and again in the Arabic, German, French and
Dutch blocks. 159 kg is 350.5 lb; the two halves agree.

**The Spirit-branded 2025 printing of the same machines says 440 lb.** Item 17 of the Spirit 4.0R
and 4.0U safety lists reads `User Weight Limit: 440 lb.`
(`spirit-med-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lb`). The frame is the same, the rest of the safety
list is the same, and the figure is 90 lb apart. Nothing in either book explains the change, and
the Dyaco book is undated, so which is the later statement cannot be proved from the pages - the
Spirit book stamps itself Revision 10.03.25; the Dyaco PDF was created in February 2016.

**The sentence survived into the Spirit 4.0R book as ghost text.** The 4.0R-2025 text layer carries
`Maximum User Weight: 159 kg (350 lbs.)` under the clearance drawings on printed page 5, and a
render of that page shows no such line. An answer read from the extraction alone would give a
Spirit 4.0R owner the PT figure. **Quote 350 lbs only to someone holding the Dyaco PT book.**

**Do not answer from the residential 350 lb card.** `spirit-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lb`
is the XBR, XBU, indoor cycle and air bike figure; the same number here belongs to a different
machine and a different book.
