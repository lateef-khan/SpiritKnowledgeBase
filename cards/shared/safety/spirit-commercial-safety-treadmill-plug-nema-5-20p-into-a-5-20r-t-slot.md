**A 20 amp plug into a 20 amp T-slot receptacle on every treadmill but two.**

> The power cord on treadmills uses a NEMA 5-20P plug (except for CT800 and XT685 which can use the NEMA 5-15P).

> The treadmill receptacle must be a NEMA 5-20R receptacle (except for CT800 and XT685 which can use the NEMA 5-15R).

| Treadmill | Plug on the cord | Receptacle required |
|---|---|---|
| CT900ENT, CT900, CT850ENT, CT850, MT200 | NEMA 5-20P | NEMA 5-20R T-slot |
| CT800, XT685 | NEMA 5-20P, or **can use** a NEMA 5-15P | NEMA 5-20R, or **can use** a NEMA 5-15R |

- **The sheet photographs both**: a duplex receptacle captioned `NEMA 5-20R T-SLOT`, whose neutral slot is T-shaped, and a plug captioned `NEMA 5-20P`, whose neutral blade is turned sideways. A 5-20P will not enter an ordinary 5-15R; the T-slot takes either blade. That is what the pictures show; the sheet does not say it in words.
- **"Can use" is the sheet's phrasing for the two 15 amp treadmills.** It does not say they ship with a 5-15P, only that a 5-15P and a 5-15R are acceptable for them. They are the two machines the sheet puts on a 15 amp circuit (`spirit-commercial-safety-treadmill-circuit-120-volt-20-amp-or-15-amp`).
- **The receptacle must still be on a dedicated circuit with an isolated ground** (`spirit-commercial-safety-dedicated-circuit-and-isolated-ground-defined`).

**Almost no manual names a NEMA type.** Of the treadmills on this sheet only the CT850ENT-2022 owner's manual prints one - `120 volt AC (NEMA 5-20P electrical cord) 20 amp grounded outlet` (`spirit-ct850-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`) - and the 2026 4.0T, 7.0T and 8.0T books, which postdate the sheet, print `NEMA 5-20P` beside a picture (`spirit-2026t-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`). The CT800, CT850, CT900, XT685 and MT200 books say `grounded outlet` and draw a three-pin plug without naming it (`spirit-product-must-be-grounded`).

**This contradicts the CT850-2018 owner's manual.** That book asks for a 110-volt, 15 amp dedicated circuit (`spirit-ct800-safety-outlet-110-volt-15-amp`); a 5-20P cord will not go into the 5-15R such a circuit would ordinarily have. The sheet gives the CT850 a 5-20P with no exception. Quote both and name the document.

**The temporary two-pole adapter the grounding pages permit** (`spirit-temporary-adapter-for-a-two-pole-receptacle`) is for a two-slot socket with no ground; it is not an adapter from a 5-20P to a 5-15R, and the sheet offers none.
