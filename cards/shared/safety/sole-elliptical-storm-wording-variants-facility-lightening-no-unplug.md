**This card holds the four wordings that differ from the standard NEVER-plus-unplug rule - read the machine's own book, not the standard card.**

| books | wording |
|---|---|
| E98-2011 | surges in `your facility or household power supply`, plus unplug |
| WE25-2009/2010 | surges `due to lightening strikes [sic]`, plus unplug |
| 2007/2008 (9 machines) | household surges, **no unplug sentence** |
| E20-2016/2020 | household surges, **no unplug sentence** |
