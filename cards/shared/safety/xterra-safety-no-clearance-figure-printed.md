**These twenty-three books give no clearance figure** - no distance to a wall, no space behind or
beside the machine, no footprint. The only placement rules they print are `Do not operate equipment
on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet` and `Do not use outdoors`.

How it was checked: every page of each book read, and the text searched for `clearance`, `free
space`, `feet`, `foot`, `inch`, `cm`, `around` and `behind`; the hits are the FS5.9e strength
exercises (`feet are spaced shoulder width apart`), the console height prompts (`5 feet 7 inches`)
none of which is a placement rule. The scanned books
(SB4.5r, FS150, FS5.8e, FS5.9e) were read from their OCR supplements and the safety page renders.

| Family | Books without a figure | Sibling that prints one |
|---|---|---|
| Folding bikes | FB150, FB350, FB160, FB360, FB180 | none |
| Recumbents and the upright | SB150, SB240, SB250, SB2.5r, SB4500, SB4.5r, SB500, SB600 | SB120, UB120 - 1 ft sides, 2 ft rear (`xterra-bike-safety-clearance-1-ft-sides-2-ft-rear`) |
| Ellipticals | EU100, EU150, FS1.5, FS2.5, FS3.0, FS3.5, FS150, FS5.8e, FS5.9e | none |
| Seated stepper | RSX1500 | none |

**Do not carry the indoor-cycle figure across.** The 1 ft / 2 ft sentence is printed only on the
seven bikes with the Dyaco cycle list; the appliance-list books never had it. The four 2024 TRX
treadmills have the same gap (`xterra-trx-safety-no-clearance-figure-printed`).
