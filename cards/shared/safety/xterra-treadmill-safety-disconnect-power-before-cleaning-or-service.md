**Three sentences, printed in every Xterra treadmill owner's manual, and a fourth in the service
manuals.**

| Where | Rule |
|---|---|
| DANGER line at the top of the safety page | `Always unplug this appliance from the electrical outlet immediately after using and before cleaning.` |
| Disconnect bullet on the safety page | `To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position, remove tether cord, then remove the plug from the outlet.` (older layout) or `...turn all controls to the off position, then remove the plug from the outlet.` (2024 TRX and new layout, which drop the tether-cord step) |
| Electrical page | `NEVER remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power.` |
| Service manual 7.1 (TR150, TR260, TRX1400, TRX2500, TRX3500, TRX4500) | `To reduce the risk of electric shock disconnect your treadmill from the electrical outlet prior to cleaning and/or service work.` |

The maintenance pages apply it every time they open the machine: `UNPLUG POWER CORD BEFORE THIS TASK`
before vacuuming under the motor hood, and `Ensure that your machine is off, and that the power cord
is unplugged to minimize risk of injury` before checking or applying lubricant.

**Order the older books give**: controls off, tether cord out, plug out. The power switch is at the
front below the motor hood; *To Turn Treadmill Off* adds `Turn off the main switch on the front of the
treadmill, below the motor cover` after removing the tether cord. Switching off is not disconnecting -
the manuals want the plug out before a cover comes off.

The Spirit and Sole books print the same rule for their own machines
(`spirit-disconnect-from-the-outlet-before-cleaning-or-service`, `e35-2026-how-to-disconnect-power`).
