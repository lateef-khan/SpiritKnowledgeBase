The list reads like the later native list with two wording differences: children are kept away for **pinch points, moving parts and other caution areas**, and motor sparks **could ignite** (not `may`) a gaseous environment. Otherwise: carpet, hands, damaged cord, heated surfaces, openings, outdoors, disconnect, purpose, pulse disclaimer, proper shoes.

**Scoping rule, WE25/WE35 book only.** The DANGER cleaning paragraph, the nominal 110-volt outlet paragraph and the 14AWG paragraph open with `These three warnings paragraphs are for E35 owners`, and the grounding section is headed `Grounding Instructions E35 only`. The WE25-2009/2010 machines are given no outlet or grounding requirement - see the linked card.

Electrical page: cover rule, ten-percent rule, rain/moisture rule. The WE25/WE35 book caps operating temperature at **40 degrees C (104 F)** with 95% humidity; the WE55/WE95 book prints **40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit** with a 95% humidity limit.
