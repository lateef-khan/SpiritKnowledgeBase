The manual prints this as a CAUTION:

- To comply with FCC RF exposure compliance requirements, **a separation distance of at
  least 20 cm must be maintained between the antenna of this device and all persons**.
- **This transmitter must not be co-located or operating in conjunction with any other
  antenna or transmitter.**

The manual does not say where on the machine the antenna is, and it gives no second figure
in inches. 20 cm is the only distance printed.

**The XT185 manual has no compliance page at all**, so it prints no RF exposure caution.

**The XT685ENT owner's manual prints the same CAUTION**, page 5, with the same 20 cm figure and
the same co-location prohibition, and it does not say where the antenna is either.

**The XT485ENT manual has no compliance page at all**, so it prints no RF exposure caution:
`xt-2015-safety-no-compliance-page`.
