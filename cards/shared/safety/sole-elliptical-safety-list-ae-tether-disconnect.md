The list is the standard native list - DANGER cleaning paragraph, 110-volt outlet, 14AWG cord, carpet, children with no age figure, hands, damaged cord, heated surfaces, aerosol with sparks, openings, outdoors, purpose, pulse disclaimer, proper shoes - with one treadmill leftover: `To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position, **remove tether cord**, then remove the plug from the outlet.` Ellipticals ship no tether cord.

Electrical page: cover rule, ten-percent rule, rain/moisture with 40-to-120 and 95% figures, full grounding section at nominal 110-volt. The operation page's belt wording has its own card.
