**Both manuals print this page word for word.** A WARNING header carries the supply requirement,
then eighteen numbered instructions follow.

> When using an electrical appliance, basic precautions should always be followed, including the
> following: Read all instructions before using this appliance.

> **DANGER - To reduce the risk of electric shock:** Always unplug this appliance from the
> electrical outlet immediately after using and before cleaning.

> **WARNING - To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons,** install the
> treadmill on a flat level surface with access to a 120-volt AC, 15-Amp. grounded outlet. With only
> the treadmill plugged into the circuit.

> DO NOT USE AN EXTENSION CORD UNLESS IT IS 14 AWG OR LARGER, WITH ONLY ONE OUTLET ON THE END. The
> treadmill should be the only appliance in the electrical circuit.

> DO NOT ATTEMPT TO DISABLE THE GROUNDED PLUG BY USING IMPROPER ADAPTERS, OR IN ANY WAY MODIFY THE
> CORD SET. A serious shock or fire hazard may result along with computer malfunctions.

|---|---|

| 2 | Do not block the rear of the treadmill. Provide a minimum of 1 meter clearance between the rear of the treadmill and any fixed object. |
| 3 | Keep children away from the treadmill. There are obvious pinch points and other caution areas that can cause harm. |
| 4 | Keep hands away from all moving parts. |
| 5 | Never operate the treadmill if it has a damaged cord or plug. If the treadmill is not working properly, call your dealer. |
| 6 | Keep the mains cord away from heated surfaces. |
| 7 | Do not operate where aerosol spray products are being used or where oxygen is being administered. Sparks from the motor may ignite a highly gaseous environment. |
| 8 | Never drop or insert any object into any openings. |
| 9 | Do not use outdoors. |
| 10 | To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position, remove tether cord, then remove the plug from the outlet. |
| 11 | Do not attempt to use your treadmill for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended. |

Beside the list is the boxed note printed on every one of the fourteen 2024 manuals:

> Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of
> your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine.

## What changed from the ENT-2022 list

The 2022 list (`ct800ent-2022-safety-instructions-list`) is the same list bulleted rather than
numbered, and three things differ:

| Change | What |
|---|---|
| **New** | item 16, the plug-modification rule, which the 2022 manual prints only on its GROUNDING INSTRUCTIONS page |
| **New** | item 17, `Remove tether cord after use to prevent unauthorized treadmill operation` |
| **Changed** | the DANGER line. 2022: `To reduce the risk of electric shock disconnect your SPIRIT treadmill from the electrical outlet prior to cleaning and/or service work`. 2024: `Always unplug this appliance from the electrical outlet immediately after using and before cleaning` |
| **New** | the opening sentence `When using an electrical appliance, basic precautions should always be followed` |

Nothing else is added, dropped or renumbered, and no figure changes: still 120-volt AC 15-Amp, still
14 AWG, still 1 meter behind, still 450 lb.

**The 1 meter of item 2 is not the only clearance figure these manuals print.** The GROUNDING
INSTRUCTIONS page adds a LOCATION REQUIREMENTS panel asking for 20in at the front and sides and 6ft
behind (`spirit-2024-safety-treadmill-location-requirements`). 6ft is about 1.83 m, so the two
statements do not agree and the manual never reconciles them. Quote both.

**The 120-volt figure in the WARNING header disagrees with the manual's own grounding page**, which
says `This product is for use on a nominal 110-volt circuit`
(`spirit-2024-safety-grounding-page-says-110-volt`).
