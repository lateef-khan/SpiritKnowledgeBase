In a double-insulated product, **two systems of insulation are provided instead of grounding. No grounding means is provided on a double-insulated product, nor should a means for grounding be added.** Servicing requires extreme care and knowledge of the system and should be done **only by qualified service personnel. Replacement parts must be identical to the parts they replace.** A double-insulated product is marked with the words DOUBLE INSULATION or DOUBLE INSULATED and the square-within-a-square symbol.

Both readings, one ruling. The same book's safety page demands a 110-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet with only the Rower on the circuit - that is the customer's rule for plugging the machine in. This page rules for the technician opening it: no added grounding, identical parts only.
