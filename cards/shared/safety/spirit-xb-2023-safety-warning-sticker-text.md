**These four manuals reproduce the sticker's own wording**, which the other Spirit bike manuals with
a PRODUCT LABELS page do not - the CR900-2025, CU900-2025 and CU800ENT-2024 books only say where the
sticker is (`spirit-bike-2024-2025-safety-product-labels`).

> **WARNING / AVERTISSEMENT - SERIOUS INJURY COULD OCCUR IF THESE PRECAUTIONS ARE NOT OBSERVED.**
>
> - Consult your physician before use.
> - Stop immediately if you become dizzy or experience chest pains and consult your physician.
> - Heart Rate monitoring systems can be inaccurate; use them for reference only.
> - Read all warnings on the unit and be properly instructed or read the Owner's Manual on how to
>   use before beginning.
> - Inspect this machine for damage prior to use.
> - Keep body, clothing, and fitness accessories clear of moving parts.
> - Risk of personal injury - Keep children under the age of 13 away from machine.

**Where the sticker is** differs by machine: the left side of the upright on the XBR25-2023,
XBR55-2023 and XBU55-2023, and the interior side of the upright on the XBR95-2023
(`spirit-bike-2024-2025-safety-product-labels`).

## This is the only place these four manuals print an age limit

**None of the four prints an under-13 item in its safety instructions list.** The last bullet of the
sticker is the whole of the age rule for a 2023 XB bike; a reader who stops at the safety list will
find no age at all (`xt-2023-safety-instructions-list`,
`ab950-2024-safety-instructions-list`, `spirit-ce-safety-who-may-use-the-elliptical`). The ten
earlier XB manuals print `Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine` as a bullet of
the list itself, and describe no sticker.

**Two other bullets are also printed elsewhere in the same manuals, in different words.** The
physician bullet is item 16 (item 8 in the XBR95-2023) of the safety list, which adds the age of 35
and pre-existing conditions (`40t-2026-safety-consult-a-physician`); the heart-rate bullet is
item 18 (item 10), which is longer and names the user's movement
(`spirit-2026t-safety-heart-rate-readings-are-not-medical`). The sticker's shorter forms do not
replace them.

**The manual says the same warning is in the manual, and that is not quite true.** The label page
reads `The same warning can be found in this manual` - but three of these seven bullets are on the
sticker only: `Read all warnings on the unit`, `Inspect this machine for damage prior to use` and
`Keep body, clothing, and fitness accessories clear of moving parts`. The safety list's nearest item
is `Keep hands away from all moving parts`, which is not the same rule.

## The French half

The sticker is bilingual and the French is printed beside the English. Read at 500 dpi it returns:

> Le non respect de ces directives pourrait entrainer des blessures graves, voir mortelles:
>
> - Consultez votre medecin avant de l'utiliser.
> - Arretez immediatement en cas d'etourdissements ou des douleurs a la poitrine et [consultez votre
>   medecin].
> - Les lectures de frequence cardiaque peuvent etre inexactes; utiliser a titre indicatif seulement.
> - Lisez tous les avertissements et les instructions sur l'appareil et dans le manuel du
>   proprietaire avant l'utilisation.
> - Inspectez cet appareil pour tout dommages avant de l'utiliser.
> - Garder le corps, les vetements et accessoires d'entrainement a l'ecart des pieces mobiles.
> - Risque de blessures - gardez les enfants ages de moins de 13 ans loin de l'appareil.

**The page has no text layer** - it is a flattened image, and `pdftotext` returns the surrounding
paragraphs and nothing from the label. Everything above was read by OCR from a 500 dpi render, so
the accents are lost and the second French bullet's closing words were unreadable. **Read the sticker
itself, or the printed page, before repeating the French to a customer.** The English half is legible
and is quoted verbatim.

**Do not quote the Spirit CT900 treadmill's sticker for a bike.** Its seven bullets are the same
sentences, but it sits on the left side of a treadmill upright and its manual's label page describes
a different set of stickers (`ct900-product-labels`).
