**Item 12 of both safety lists reads `Connect this appliance to a properly grounded outlet only.
See Grounding Instructions.` - and neither book prints any.**

The contents page runs *Product Label 4, Warning, Compliances, and Notices 5, Safety Instructions 6,
Electrical Safety 7* and then *Parts and Assembly*. There is no Grounding Instructions heading, the
Electrical Safety page (`xterra-sb-safety-electrical-safety-page`) says nothing about grounding, and
the word *ground* occurs nowhere else in either book. The treadmill books that share this list do
print the page - a nominal 120-volt or 110-volt circuit with a three-prong plug
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-grounding-nominal-120-volt-circuit`,
`xterra-trx-safety-grounding-nominal-110-volt-circuit`) - and the cross-reference was carried over with
the list.

How it was checked: the text of both books, including the OCR supplements, searched for `ground`
(hits: item 12 only) and `volt` (hits: the ten-percent-variation sentence only); every page heading
listed from the contents page and the running heads.

**What a customer can be told**: the bike runs from an external adapter
(`xterra-safety-external-power-supply-and-power-jack`), the book names no voltage, amperage or plug type
for it, and the only outlet rules are item 12's *properly grounded outlet* and the Electrical Safety
page's advice to avoid AFCI/GFCI breakers if possible and to give the unit its own circuit.
