The three ST800 books use a different safety page from the rest of the Spirit strength range:
**IMPORTANT SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS**, a bulleted list under the heading **USER SAFETY PRECAUTIONS**,
not the twenty numbered items the CSS, CSD, CSF and CSI manuals print. Each book swaps in its own
noun — *bench*, *rack*, *machine* — which is why the same rule reads three different ways.

**The seven bullets all three share:**

- Obtain a medical exam before beginning any exercise program.
- Stop exercising if feeling faint, dizzy or experiencing pain and consult your physician.
- Obtain instructions before using.
- Read and understand the owner's manual and all warnings posted on the [bench/rack/machine] before using.
- Keep all children (12 and under) away. Teenagers (13 and over) and disabled must be supervised.
- Keep body and clothing free from and clear of all moving parts.
- Use the [bench/rack/machine] only for the intended use. DO NOT modify it. Inspect it prior to use and
  DO NOT use if it appears damaged or inoperable. DO NOT use if any components are worn, frayed or
  damaged. DO NOT attempt to fix a broken one — notify floor staff. Report any malfunctions, damage
  or repairs to the facility. Replace any warning labels if damaged, worn or illegible.

**Four bullets only the ST800FT functional trainer carries**, because it is the only one of the
three with cables and a weight stack:

- Inspect all cables and connections prior to use. DO NOT use if any components are worn, frayed or damaged.
- DO NOT use if guards are missing or damaged.
- Be sure that the weight pin is completely inserted. Use only the pin provided by the manufacturer. If unsure seek assistance.
- Never pin the weights in an elevated position. DO NOT use this machine if found in this condition. Seek assistance from floor staff.
- DO NOT use dumbbells or other incremental weights, except those provided by the manufacturer.
- DO NOT use use this machine outdoors. *(printed with the doubled word)*

**The last bullet of each list is the weight limit, and all three differ** — see the cards for each
machine.

Two rules the ST800 books add that the rest of the range does not: **STANDARD COMPLIANCE**, *Spirit
Fitness products meet or exceed applicable ASTM and EN Standards* (no standard number is given), and
a **WARNING/CAUTION DECALS** note explaining that a warning decal means death or serious injury and a
caution decal means minor or moderate injury. Each page closes **SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK
SAFETY!**
