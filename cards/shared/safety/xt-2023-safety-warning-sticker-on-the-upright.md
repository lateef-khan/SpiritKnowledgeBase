**The safety warning sticker is on the interior side of the treadmill's upright.**

The manual says the same warning is printed in the manual itself, and asks you to read it
and be aware of the precautions before operating the unit. That text is the safety
instructions page: `xt-2023-safety-instructions-list`.

**These five manuals do not reproduce the sticker's own wording on the label page.** They
only say where it is and that the manual repeats it. The Spirit CT900 and CTSBS900 manuals
do print the sticker text in full; this family's manuals do not, so this card cannot
quote it.

A second sticker sits on the same interior side of the upright - the Proposition 65
warning: `xt-2023-safety-proposition-65-sticker`.
