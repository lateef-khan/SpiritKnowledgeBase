The Important Safety Instructions list prints the same precautions in every legacy book in this wave:

- **Keep hands away from all moving parts.**
- **Never operate the treadmill if it has a damaged cord or plug.** If the treadmill is not working properly, call your dealer.
- **Keep the cord away from heated surfaces.**
- **Do not operate where aerosol spray products are being used or where oxygen is being administered.** Sparks from the motor may ignite a highly gaseous environment.
- **Never drop or insert any object into any openings.**
- **Do not use outdoors.**
- **Do not attempt to use your treadmill for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended.**
- **Use handrails provided; they are for your safety.**

**SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!**

The outlet, clearance, children, shoes, pulse-sensor and tether lines from the same list have their own cards because their figures or wording vary.

The 28 non-desk A2 stencil books print the same eight bullets word for word (page renders spot-checked for a 110-volt, a 120-volt and the desk book). The TD80-2019 desk book differs throughout and has its own card (`td80-2019-safety-desk-precautions-list`).
