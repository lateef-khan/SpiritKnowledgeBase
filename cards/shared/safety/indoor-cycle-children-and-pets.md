**In a home setting, keep children away from the bike when it is not in use. Keep children and pets away from the unit while it is in use.**

All ten indoor cycle manuals print this in the same words. **They give no minimum age.** The upright and recumbent manuals of the same period do give one - "under the age of 13" from 2016 onward - but no indoor cycle manual repeats it.

The SB1200 2023 manual adds a second, stronger rule as item 13 of its safety list: **close supervision is necessary when this appliance is used by, on, or near children, invalids, or disabled persons.** No other indoor cycle manual carries that.
