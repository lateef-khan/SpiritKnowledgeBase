**The sheet requires both of every treadmill, and it is the one Spirit document that says what the two words mean.**

> A dedicated circuit for each unit requires each outlet does not have anything else operating on the same circuit.

> A non-looped (isolated) ground requires that each circuit has an individual neutral/ground connection from it and terminating at an approved earth ground.

- **Dedicated** is defined by the outlet, not the breaker: nothing else may be *operating* on the same circuit. It is the same rule the manuals write as `with only the treadmill plugged into the circuit` (`spirit-ct850-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`) and the 2026 books as `dedicated 20-amp circuit` (`spirit-2026t-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`).
- **Isolated ground** is defined by the wiring: each treadmill circuit has its **own** neutral/ground conductor, run from that circuit and ending at an approved earth ground, rather than a ground shared or looped between circuits. No owner's or service manual on file defines the term; the grounding pages say only that the product must be grounded through a properly installed outlet (`spirit-product-must-be-grounded`).
- **Both apply to every treadmill on the sheet**, the 15 amp CT800 and XT685 included (`spirit-commercial-safety-treadmill-circuit-120-volt-20-amp-or-15-amp`), and the sheet adds the plug and receptacle type on the same page (`spirit-commercial-safety-treadmill-plug-nema-5-20p-into-a-5-20r-t-slot`).

**Non-treadmill cardio is excused from the dedicated circuit.** The second page says the 120 volt, 15 amp bikes, ellipticals, steppers and rower `do not require a dedicated circuit` and that up to four may share one 15 amp circuit (`spirit-commercial-safety-non-treadmill-nema-5-15p-four-units-per-15-amp-circuit`); it says nothing about an isolated ground for them.

**Neither term is a GFCI.** The sheet does not mention ground-fault protection; the manuals' rule on that is separate (`spirit-never-use-a-gfci-wall-outlet`).

The sheet is written for U.S. markets and does not say who is to install or verify the isolated ground. The manuals' answer to that is a qualified electrician (`spirit-product-must-be-grounded`).
