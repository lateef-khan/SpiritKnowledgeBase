**Item 14 of the twenty-item list, printed on all four books:**

> Please keep pets and children away from equipment. Do not allow children to play near this
> equipment or use it for any purpose. The minimum age of use for this equipment is 18 years old.
> Everyone under the age of 18 must be under parental supervision at all times.

| Rule | Figure |
|---|---|
| Minimum age | 18 |
| Under 18 | only under parental supervision, at all times - the sentence after the minimum allows it |
| Children | not to play near it, not to use it for any purpose; pets kept away |

The two sentences pull against each other - *minimum age 18* and *under 18 only supervised* - and
the book does not reconcile them; the reading a customer can be given is that an under-18 may use
the dumbbells only with a parent supervising, and a child may not be near them at all. Items 1 and
5 add the capability rule and the over-35 physician rule
(`xterra-strength-safety-instructions-list-20-items`).

It is the highest age any Xterra book prints: the rowers' only age is *under 13 away* on two stickers
(`xterra-rower-safety-who-may-use-capability-physician-children-and-pets`). The Spirit strength range
says twelve and under (`spirit-strength-safety-children-twelve-and-under-kept-away`) - a Spirit card.
