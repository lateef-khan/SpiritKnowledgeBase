> **BEFORE BEGINNING THIS OR ANY OTHER EXERCISE PROGRAM, CONSULT A PHYSICIAN. THE PHYSICIAN CAN HELP YOU BETTER DETERMINE WHAT ACTIVITIES OR PROGRAMS ARE MOST SUITED FOR YOU. IF AT ANY TIME DURING THE WORKOUT YOU FEELING FAINT, OR CHEST PAINS, OR SEVER SHORTNESS OF BREATH, STOP EXERCISING IMMEDIATLELY AND CONSULT A PHYSICAN.**

The 2023 console manual prints a slightly shortened version of the same paragraph: "BEFORE BEGINNING EXERCISE PROGRAM", "WHAT ACTIVITIES PROGRAMS", and no "OR" before CHEST PAINS and SEVER SHORTNESS OF BREATH. Nothing is added or removed in substance.

The misspellings ("YOU FEELING FAINT", "SEVER", "IMMEDIATLELY", "PHYSICAN") are as printed in both booklets.

**This is the only safety text in either console manual.** They carry no electrical, outlet, grounding, temperature, footwear or children's rule. For those, read the owner's manual of the machine.

**These are third-party booklets.** The console is the Universal Group Cycling Monitor made by Direction Technology, and the warranty printed in the same booklet is Direction Technology's, not SOLE's.
