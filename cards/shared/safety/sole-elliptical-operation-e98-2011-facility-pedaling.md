- **NEVER operate this elliptical without reading and completely understanding the results of any operational change you request from the computer.**
- Changes in **resistance and incline do not occur immediately**; set the desired resistance and release the key.
- **NEVER use your elliptical during an electrical storm. Surges may occur in your facility or household power supply** - the only B book that says `facility`. **Unplug as a precaution.**
- **Use caution while pedaling** with distractions such as television or reading; they **may result in serious injury** - with no lose-balance clause.
- **Always hold on to a handrail or hand bar while making control changes (incline, resistance, etc.).**
- **Do not use excessive pressure on console control keys**; pushing harder will not make the unit go faster or slower. Contact your SOLE dealer.
