**NEVER use your fitness bike during an electrical storm.** Surges may occur in your household power supply that could damage fitness bike components. **Unplug the fitness bike during an electrical storm as a precaution.**

The LCB 2011 and LCR 2011 manuals word the middle sentence as "surges may occur in your **facility or** household power supply". Nothing else differs.

**Eight of the 22 upright and recumbent machines have no storm rule in their manual.** The LCB 2013, LCR 2013, LCB 2016, LCR 2016, LCB 2019, LCR 2019, LCB 2023 and LCR 2023 manuals print an IMPORTANT OPERATION INSTRUCTIONS page that never mentions an electrical storm. Absence is not permission; it is simply not stated.

A surge is also a warranty matter: the exclusions list damage from an inadequate power supply and from power surges.
