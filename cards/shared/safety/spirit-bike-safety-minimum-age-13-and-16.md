**Every page in this family that names an age says 13, except one: the AB900 air bike's SAFEGUARDS
page says 16. The AB900's own warning decal, two pages earlier, says 13.**

| Machine | What it says | Where |
|---|---|---|
| CIC800-2021, CIC850-2022 | `Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine.` | Safety list |
| XIC600-2018, XIC600-2021 | `Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine.` | Safety list |
| JB950-2022 | `Children under the age of 13 should not ride the bike. The bike mechanism and ergonomics are designed for adult use only.` | SAFEGUARDS, printed page 15 |
| AB900-2018 | **`Children under the age of 16 should not ride the bike.`** The bike mechanism and ergonomics are designed for adult use only. | SAFEGUARDS, printed page 16 |
| AB900-2018, AB950-2024 | `Risk of personal injury - Keep children under the age of 13 away from machine.` | Exercise warning decal on the machine |
| CB900-2013 | **no age is printed anywhere** | - |

**Give 16 for the AB900 and 13 for everything else.** The AB900 is the only machine whose manual
carries both numbers, and its 16 is the stricter of the two, so quoting 16 is safe for that machine
even though the sticker on the bike says 13. The JB950 page is otherwise the AB900 page word for
word, with the 16 changed to 13.

**The decal figures were read from a 300 dpi render of a flattened image page**, on the AB900 and
independently on the AB950, and both read 13 - consistent with each other and with the six manuals
that print 13 in native text.

## Two more exclusions, on six of the eight machines

> This appliance is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or metal
> capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge, unless they have been given supervision or
> instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible for their safety.

- **CIC800-2021 and CIC850-2022** print it exactly as above, `metal` for `mental` included, with the
  supervision clause.
- **XIC600-2018, XIC600-2021 and AB950-2024** spell `mental` correctly and **stop at `lack of
  experience and knowledge`, dropping the supervision clause**. On those three machines a supervised
  rider is not carved out; the exclusion is unconditional as printed.
- **CB900-2013 and the AB900 and JB950 manuals print no such exclusion at all.**

**All eight tell you to keep children away from a bike in use**, in one wording or another - the
five cycle manuals as `keep children and pets away from the unit while it is in use`, the AB900 and
JB950 as `Do not let unsupervised children operate the bike` in their DO NOT block and `Keep
children and pets away from the bike whenever it's in use` in SAFEGUARDS.

**Nothing in any of the eight sets an upper age**, and only the AB950 prints the over-35 physician
rule that the treadmill and commercial bike lists carry.
