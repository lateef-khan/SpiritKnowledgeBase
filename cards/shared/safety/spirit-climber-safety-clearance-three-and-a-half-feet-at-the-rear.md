> Ensure there is a minimum space on the sides of this equipment of **two feet** for proper
> operation, easy access and to prevent possible injuries to others standing or walking nearby. There
> should be a minimum of at least **one foot** of free space at the front and **three and a half
> feet** at the rear.

| Requirement | Figure |
|---|---|
| Each side | **two feet** |
| Front | **one foot** |
| Rear | **three and a half feet** |

- **The rear figure is three and a half times the front figure**, and it is the only clearance
  requirement in the Spirit stepper range that is not symmetrical front to back. The seat and its
  carriage travel rearward on these two machines; a wall one foot behind an 8.5S obstructs the seat.
- **The reason given for the sides is other people**, not the user.
- **No metric conversion is printed**, and no floor specification beyond `a flat level surface` in
  item 2.

**The 7.0S, 7.5S and MS300 ask for one foot at the rear**, not three and a half
(`spirit-climber-safety-clearance-two-feet-at-the-sides`). The item is worded identically apart from
that figure, so read to the end of the sentence before quoting it.

**The stair climbers are in inches**: 20 inches (0.5 m) each side and 48 inches (1.25 m) behind
(`csc900-2024-safety-clearance-around-the-machine`). 48 inches is four feet, close to this figure but
measured on a different machine and printed in a different unit; do not substitute one for the other.
