**This is the 300 lb figure**, printed two ways:

| Book | How it is printed |
|---|---|
| SB600 | `21. User Weight Limit: 300 lbs.` - item 21 of the new-layout SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| RSX1500 | `User Weight Limit: 300 lbs.` - the last Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions bullet, in both the 2021 book and the 2017 printing |

Neither book has a specification table. The SB600 adds a boxed note under its list: `Please ensure
that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of your new
machine.`

**The SB240, the other new-layout recumbent, is 265 lbs** (`xterra-safety-user-weight-limit-265-lbs`).
Several Xterra treadmills print 300 too (`xterra-treadmill-safety-user-weight-limit-300-lbs`); that
is a treadmill card.
