**Four rower books print the same page: a numbered appliance list with two items no other Xterra
list has - *ample free space* and *tighten all nuts and bolts* - and eleven Fitness Equipment bullets
that end in the user weight limit.** Two books print twelve numbered items, two print thirteen.

> When using an appliance, basic precautions should always be followed, including the following:
> Read all instructions before using this appliance.
> **WARNING - To reduce the risk injury to persons:**

| # (12-item) | # (13-item) | Instruction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Do not operate under blanket. (12-item) / Do not operate under blanket or pillow. (13-item) |
| 2 | 2 | **Operate the machinery with ample free space around it to easily get on and off the machine.** |
| 3 | 3 | This exercise equipment is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge. |
| 4 | 4 | Keep children and pets away from the machine at all times. DO NOT leave children unattended in the same room with the machine. |
| 5 | 5 | Use this appliance only for its intended use as described in this manual. Do not use attachments not recommended by the manufacturer. |
| 6 | 6 | Never operate this appliance if it is not working properly, if it has been dropped or damaged, or dropped into water. Return the appliance to a service center for examination and repair. |
| 7 | 7 | Keep the appliance away from heated surfaces. |
| 8 | 8 | Never operate the appliance with the air openings blocked. Keep the air openings free of lint, hair, and the like. |
| 9 | 9 | Never drop or insert any object into any opening. |
| 10 | 10 | Do not use outdoors. |
| - | 11 | Do not operate where aerosol (spray) products are being use or where oxygen is being administered. |
| 11 | 12 | The appliance is intended for household use. |
| 12 | 13 | **After assembly, make sure to tighten all nuts and bolts.** |

| Book | Numbered items | Proposition 65 text between the list and the bullets | Last bullet |
|---|---|---|---|
| ERG180 (new layout, 2023) | 12 | no - the warning is a sticker on the Product Labels page (`xterra-rower-safety-product-labels-page`) | `User Weight Limit: 300 LB` |
| ERG550W (2023) | 12 | yes, Toluene and Acrylamide | `User Weight Limit: 300 LB` |
| ERG600W (2021) | 13 | yes | `User Weight Limit: 300 LBS` |
| ERG650W (2021) | 13 | yes | `User Weight Limit: 350 LBS` |

**Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions** (eleven bullets on all four, in this order): do not operate
on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet; consult a physician before beginning, especially over 35 or
with pre-existing conditions; keep hands away from all moving parts; do not use the equipment for any
other purpose; wear proper shoes (`xterra-treadmill-safety-proper-shoes`); **wear proper clothing for
exercising, loose clothing could become caught in the machine**; **only one person at a time should
use the machine**; **if the user experiences dizziness, nausea, chest pain, or any other abnormal
symptoms, STOP the workout at once. CONSULT A PHYSICIAN IMMEDIATELY**; **do not place any sharp
object around the machine**; **always use caution when moving and storing your equipment**; the
weight limit (`xterra-rower-safety-user-weight-limit-300-lbs`, `xterra-ergw-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lbs`).

The three older-layout books close with `Failure to follow all guidelines may compromise the
effectiveness of the exercise experience, expose yourself (and possibly others) to injury, and reduce
the longevity of the equipment.`, `SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!` and `CAUTION!! Please be
careful when unpacking the carton.`; the ERG180 closes with the new-layout boxed note `Please ensure
that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of your new
machine...` - a template phrase on a two-AA-battery console (`xterra-rower-safety-battery-console-no-power-adapter`).

**No pulse-sensor bullet.** These four lists drop the `pulse sensors are not medical devices` bullet
that the ERG220, ERG400, ERG750W and ERG800W print; the ERG180's only version of it is on its warning
sticker (`xterra-treadmill-safety-pulse-sensors-are-not-medical-devices`). *Ample free space* is the
only clearance rule any of the four gives, with no figure (`xterra-rower-safety-no-clearance-figure-printed`).
The ERG500 and ERG700 print a shorter cousin of this page with nine bullets and no weight limit
(`xterra-erg-safety-instructions-list-11-items-and-9-bullets-no-weight-limit`).
