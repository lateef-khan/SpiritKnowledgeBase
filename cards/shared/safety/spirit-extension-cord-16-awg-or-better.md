> Do not use an extension cord unless it is a 16 AWG or better with only one outlet on the end.
> Do not attempt to disable the grounded plug by using improper adapters or in any way modify the
> cord outlet.

- **16 AWG minimum.**
- **One outlet on the end**, not a multi-way lead.
- **Never defeat the grounding pin** with an adapter, and never modify the cord or the outlet.

**Two other gauges are printed for these same machines.**

- The CT850 **2020** manual's safety chapter asks for **14 AWG**:
  `ct850-2020-extension-cord-14-awg-or-better`.
- The CT850 **2016** manual's own diagnosis guide (p. 56, printed 55) says
  `Do not use an extension cord. If an extension cord is required. it should be as short as
  possible and heavy duty 12 gauge minimum` - a **12 gauge** figure this safety chapter does not
  mention. See `ct850-2016-only-reaches-7-mph`.

The grounding chapter states the same no-modification rule at greater length:
`spirit-product-must-be-grounded`.
