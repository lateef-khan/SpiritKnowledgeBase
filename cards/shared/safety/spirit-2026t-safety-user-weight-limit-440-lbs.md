**All three machines have the same limit - 440 lbs.** This is not the 425 lb figure of the
XT485-2023 and XT685-2023, and not the 275, 350 or 375 lb figures of the smaller XT machines.

> 30. User Weight Limit: 440 lbs.

The 7.0T prints it as item 22 and the 8.0T as item 28, written `440 lb` without the s. Same
figure.

All three print the boxed note beside the list:

> Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements
> of your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine.

**The 7.0T and 8.0T state it a second and a third time, in kilograms as well.** Their Application
Specification page says `Maximal patient's weight is 440 lb`, and the 8.0T's specification page
has the row **Patient weight capacity 200 kgs (440 lbs)**. Their Operation Instructions page adds
the same limit as a contraindication - `Do not use for patients weighing greater than 200kgs
(bariatric)` (`spirit-2026t-safety-rehabilitation-contraindications`). 200 kg is 440.9 lb, so the
three statements agree to the rounding; the manuals never print a different number.

**Do not read the 8.0T's other weight row as a user limit.** The same specification page lists
`Product weight 252.5 kgs (556.7 lbs)`, which is the mass of the treadmill itself.

**The 4.0T states it once only.** That manual has no specification table at all, so its safety
instructions page is the whole answer for that machine.

**Both Spirit MT200 rehabilitation treadmill manuals carry the same limit, and print it in both
units.** The specifications table gives the row

> Patient Weight Capacity: 440 Lbs. (200 Kgs.)

and the Application Specification page gives `Maximal patient's weight is 200kg`. The 2010 manual
prints them on pages 42 and 6; the 2022 manual on pages 67 and 11. 200 kg is 440.9 lb, so the two
statements agree to the rounding and neither manual prints a different number.

**The 2022 manual states it a third time, as a contraindication** - `Do not use for patients
weighing greater than 200kgs (bariatric)`
(`spirit-2026t-safety-rehabilitation-contraindications`). **The 2010 manual has no
contraindications paragraph at all**, so for that machine the two statements above are the whole
answer.

**Neither MT200 manual states the limit in its safety instructions**, and neither prints the boxed
weight-and-power note the 2026 machines print. Their safety pages have no weight item
(`spirit-mt200-safety-instructions-list`).

**Do not read the MT200's other weight row as a user limit.** The same specifications table lists
`Product Weight: 514.8 Lbs. (234 Kgs)`, which is the mass of the treadmill itself.

**The 2025 printing of the 4.0T manual prints this page word for word**, so it holds for the 40t-2025 as well. The February 2025 book (revision 02.04.25) and its May 2025 reprint (revision 05.19.25) differ only in the version and revision line; both were compared with the 2026 book page by page and no rule or figure on the safety, electrical, grounding or operation pages changed.

**The 2025 printing of the 7.0T manual prints this page word for word**, so it holds for the 70t-2025 as well. The January 2025 book (revision 01.10.25, version 2.0) was compared with the 2026 book page by page; the only safety-page changes between them are the outlet voltage and plug line (`70t-2025-safety-outlet-115-volt-20-amp-and-a-nema-5-15p-line`), a fuller operator profile, and the contact site on the application page, which the 2025 book gives as `hands, feet, and trunk` where the 2026 book says `hands and feet`.
