**Every Xterra treadmill manual prints the same three rules; only two books print an age.**

| Rule | Wording | Books |
|---|---|---|
| Capability | `This exercise equipment is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge.` | all 19 (item 3 of the safety list) |
| Physician | `Before beginning this or any exercise program, consult a physician. This is especially important for persons over the age of 35 or persons with pre-existing health conditions.` | all 19 (a Fitness Equipment bullet, or item 16) |
| Household | `The appliance is intended for household use.` | all 19 (item 13) |

**Where an age rule appears:**

| Books | Age rule | Where |
|---|---|---|
| WS200, WS300 | item 3 adds `...unless they have been given supervision or instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible for their safety. **Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine.**` and item 4 `Close supervision is necessary when this appliance is used by, on, or near children, invalids, or disabled persons.` | the safety list (`xterra-ws-safety-instructions-list`) |
| TRX1400, TR65, TR75, TR85, TR95H, TR75H, WS200, WS300 | `Risk of personal injury - Keep children under the age of 13 away from machine.` | the safety warning sticker on the upright (`xterra-treadmill-safety-warning-sticker-on-the-upright`) |
| same eight | `Install your treadmill in an area where children and pets cannot access it.` | Location Requirements (`xterra-treadmill-safety-location-20-in-front-and-sides-6-ft-behind`) |
| TR150, TR200, TR300, TR6.6, TRX1000, TR260, TR6.4, TRX2500, TRX3500, TRX4500, TRX5500 | **none** - no age, no supervision clause, no children sentence anywhere in the book | - |

**On the eleven older-layout and 2024 TRX books, item 3 has no exception clause**: it stops at
`and knowledge`, so a supervised user with reduced capability is not allowed by the text. The WS books
restore the exception.

**The physician rule is the only health screening the manuals give.** The sticker on the eight
new-layout machines adds `Stop immediately if you become dizzy or experience chest pains and consult
your physician`; no Xterra book mentions pacemakers or pregnancy. The TRX2500 HRC program page
repeats `please consult your doctor or professional instructor before use` - a programs card.

The tether cord is the child-proofing the older books offer
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-remove-the-tether-cord-after-use`).

The Spirit XT-2015 and CT800 lists carry the same clauses for their own machines
(`xt-2015-safety-who-may-use-the-treadmill`, `spirit-ct800-safety-who-may-use-the-treadmill`).
