**NEVER operate this fitness bike without reading and completely understanding the results of any operational change you request from the computer.**

This is the first line of the operation page in every one of the 14 upright and recumbent owner's manuals, in the same words.

The LCB and LCR manuals put two more cautions beside it - that resistance changes happen gradually, and that distractions while pedaling can injure you. The B74/R72 and B94/R92 manuals do not print either of those.
