**At the base**, and the manuals say so in one line each:

| Machine | Printed as |
|---|---|
| ST800FI flat/incline bench | *The serial number is located at the base of the bench.* |
| ST800DR3 dumbbell rack | *The serial number is located at the base of the rack.* |
| ST800FT functional trainer | *The serial number is located at the base of the Functional Trainer.* |

Each is followed by a blank `SERIAL NUMBER:` line for the owner to write it into, and by the
registration instruction. The ST800FI and ST800FT send you to
`www.spiritfitness.com/warranty` under the Support tab; the **ST800DR3 sends you somewhere else**,
`www.spiritfitness.com/commercialwarrantyregistration.html`, which is the only manual in the range
to give that address.

That is all the location detail the ST800 manuals give — no photograph, no arrow, no side named. They
have no product labels page, so unlike the CSS, CSD, CSF and CSI machines there is no picture of the
decal to compare against.
