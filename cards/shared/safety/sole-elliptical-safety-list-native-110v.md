- **DANGER**: disconnect the SOLE elliptical from the outlet before cleaning or service.
- **WARNING**: flat level surface, **110-volt 15-amp grounded outlet**, only the elliptical on the circuit; **14AWG-or-better** single-outlet cord; never defeat the grounded plug (shock/fire hazard plus computer malfunctions).
- Carpet damage rule; **keep children away** (no age figure - pinch points and caution areas); hands away; damaged cord plus call-your-dealer; heated surfaces; aerosol/oxygen with sparks that may ignite; openings; outdoors; disconnect off-then-unplug; intended purpose; pulse disclaimer; proper shoes.
- **SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!**

The E98-2011 book prints this list word for word. The AE-2011 pair adds a tether cord to the disconnect line and has its own card.
