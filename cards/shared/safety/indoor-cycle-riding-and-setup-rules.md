- **Use this equipment only for its intended use as described in this manual.** **Do not attempt to ride this bike at high pedal speeds until you have ridden the bike for some time and are comfortable riding at slower pedal speeds.**
- **Do not attempt to perform dip movements on handlebars.**
- **Never drop or insert any object into any opening of the bike.**
- **Follow instructions for safe use of the equipment including proper seat position, handlebar position, and use of foot positioning system of pedals. Do not attempt to pull up handlebar post and seat post over the 'MAX.' graduation.**

All ten indoor cycle manuals print these four items in the same words. Several extractions render the last one as "over the 'MAX: graduation" - the printed text is **'MAX.'**, the maximum-extension mark on the posts.
