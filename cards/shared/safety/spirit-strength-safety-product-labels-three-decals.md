The **PRODUCT LABELS** page opens every CSS, CSD, CSF and CSI manual's safety chapter, with the same
wording in all thirty-six:

> The following pages show examples of Spirit(R) Fitness warning labels and communication stickers
> placed on the equipment as part of the manufacturing process. It is critical that owners maintain
> the integrity and placement of these stickers. If you find any stickers missing or damaged contact
> your local dealer or distributor for a replacement.
>
> If any instructions or information are not clear, and please contact to SPIRIT FITNESS customer
> service right away.
>
> NOTE: STICKERS AND LABELS ARE NOT SHOWN TO SCALE

**Three decals** are pictured, with an arrow to where each sits on the machine:

1. **Warning Decal** — a tall grey sticker of eight numbered rules, headed `WARNING - SERIOUS INJURY
   CAN OCCUR ON THIS EQUIPMENT. YOU MUST FOLLOW THESE PRECAUTIONS TO AVOID INJURY!`, usually on the
   weight-stack shroud or the upright.
2. **Serial Number Decal** — the machine's identity plate, plus a separate barcode label carrying the
   serial number and the Spirit service number (870) 935-1107.
3. **California Prop 65 Warning Decal (For US model only)**.

**The warning decal's eight items cannot be read out of these PDFs.** It is placed as a
low-resolution raster: rendered at 600 dpi the small print is still a blur, `pdftotext` returns
nothing from it, and the OCR supplement in the extraction returns nonsense (`sinoUs (CAN OCCUR ON
THs SQUIRT`). Answer a question about the decal's wording from the numbered safety instructions on
the next page, which cover the same ground legibly, and tell the caller to read the sticker on the
machine itself. Do not reconstruct the decal text.

Item 18 of those safety instructions makes replacement the owner's duty: *Replace any warning labels
if damaged, worn or illegible.* Replacements come from the local dealer or distributor.

The two CSI machines print **four** labels rather than three — a rating label and a name plate decal
in place of the single serial number decal. The three ST800 products have no product labels page at
all.

**The CSD-CPSP May 2025 update (version 5.0, revision 01/23/2025, file dated 2025 0521) and the CSS-DELT 2026 revision (file dated 20260821) print this page in the same words**, so the two newer books add nothing and change nothing here; they are cited so the page can be checked in either printing. The CSS-DELT 2026 text layer is a shifted font on its cover pages; its safety pages were read from the OCR supplements and the render.
