**This is the Dyaco edition of the 4.0 R and 4.0 U book - the cover reads `PT` and `Dyaco`, not
Spirit - and its safety chapter is the seventeen rules of the 2025 Spirit manuals printed as
unnumbered bullets, in five languages, with one figure changed.**

> **Attention** - Read all instructions in this manual before using this device.
>
> **Warning**

The bullets are, in order: consult a physician (over 35 or preexisting conditions); keep children
away from the bike, pinch points; children supervised so they do not play with the device; hands
away from moving parts; never drop or insert any object into openings; do not use outdoors; not on
deeply padded, plush or shag carpet; only for its intended purpose; the hand pulse sensors are not
medical devices; heart rate monitoring may be inaccurate and over exercising may result in serious
injury or death; wear proper shoes; the appliance can be used by children aged from 8 years and
above and persons of reduced capability if supervised or instructed; it is not intended for such
persons unless supervised; notes on correct posture and the fact that pedal crank training
equipment of class B and C are not suitable for therapeutic purposes; **the bike is not a medical
device**; then:

> Maximum User Weight: 159 kg (350 lbs.)

and the placement paragraph - a level surface, 2 ft (0.6 meters) of access on both sides, no
blocked vents, not in a garage, covered patio, near water or outdoors - followed by the two
clearance drawings and `Save these instructions - think safety!`.

**The weight line is the difference.** The Spirit 2025 printing of the same list ends `User Weight
Limit: 440 lb.`; this edition says 159 kg (350 lbs) and prints it in every language
(`spirit-pt-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-159-kg-350-lbs`). No other rule changes, and the
`not a medical device` sentence is in both editions
(`spirit-med-bike-safety-instructions-list-seventeen-items`).

**The same page is printed five times** - English (pp. 4-5), Arabic (pp. 6-7), German (pp. 8-9),
French (pp. 10-11) and Dutch (pp. 12-13) - and each language block ends with the weight line and
the two drawings. The Dutch and German blocks repeat `159 kg (350 lbs)`; the drawings carry the
same millimetre figures in every language.

**The book is undated.** It prints no revision stamp, no copyright year and no warranty effective
date; the PDF file carries a February 2016 creation date, which is the only date evidence there is.
Say `the Dyaco PT edition` rather than a year.

**It prints no product labels page and no serial number panel.** The words `serial`, `sticker` and
`Proposition` do not occur anywhere in either PT book, in the text layer or the OCR supplements.
The Spirit 2025 books added both
(`spirit-med-bike-safety-product-labels-and-serial-number-location`).

**The electrical and operation pages are the Spirit ones.** The generator shock warning and the 5 to
48 degree operating range (`spirit-med-bike-safety-operating-environment-5-to-48-c`), and the six
operation bullets that add `Always hold on to a handle bar while making control changes`
(`spirit-bike-safety-operation-instructions`), are printed word for word, again in five languages.
