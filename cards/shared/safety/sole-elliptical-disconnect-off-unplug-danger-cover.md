1. **Turn all controls to the off position.**
2. **Remove the plug from the outlet.** That is the manual's stated way to disconnect.

Rules that go with it: **DANGER - to reduce the risk of electric shock, disconnect your SOLE elliptical from the electrical outlet prior to cleaning and/or service work**, and **NEVER remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power**.

Two books word step 2 differently: the AE-2011 pair inserts `remove tether cord` (a treadmill leftover - ellipticals ship no tether), and the 2007/2008 books print no DANGER paragraph, so neither exception joins the DANGER half of this card.
