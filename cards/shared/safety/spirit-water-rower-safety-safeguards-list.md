> The following fitness safeguards and operating precautions are directed to purchasers and users of
> the Water Rowing Machine. Club Managers should ensure that members and fitness staff are trained
> to follow these same instructions. **Failure to follow these safeguards may result in injury or
> serious health risk.**

| # | Safeguard |
|---|---|
| 1 | Do not use any equipment in any way other than designed or intended by the manufacturer. It is imperative that equipment is used properly to avoid injury. |
| 2 | Injuries may result if exercising improperly or excessively. It is recommended that all individuals consult a physician prior to commencing an exercise program. |
| 3 | If at any time during exercise you feel faint, dizzy or experience pain, stop exercising and consult your physician. |
| 4 | Keep body parts (hands, feet, hair, etc.), clothing and jewelry away from moving parts to avoid injury. |
| 5 | Follow instructions provided in this manual for correct foot position and basic rowing techniques. |
| 6 | The maximum weight for individuals riding the rower should not exceed **350 lbs.** (CRW800H2O) or **450 lbs (204 kilograms)** (CRW900) |
| 7 | Do not use or permit use of any equipment that is damaged and/or has worn or broken parts. For all equipment use only replacement parts supplied by Spirit Fitness. |
| 8 | Cables and belts pose an extreme liability if used when frayed. Always replace any cable or belt at first sign of wear (consult Spirit Fitness if uncertain). |
| 9 | Preventative maintenance is the key to smooth operating equipment as well as to keep your liability to a minimum. Equipment needs to be inspected at regular intervals. |
| 10 | Ensure that any person(s) making adjustments or performing maintenance or repair of any kind is qualified to do so. |
| 11 | Do not attempt to use or repair any accessory approved for use with the Spirit Fitness Equipment which appears to be damaged or worn. |
| 12 | Check all belts, pulleys and bungee cords regularly for signs of wear, and replace if needed. |
| 13 | Check regularly and follow all instructions for maintenance as specified in this manual. |
| 14 | Replace immediately any defective parts and do not operate unit until all repairs are complete. |
| 15 | Keep children away from the equipment. Parents or others supervising children must provide close supervision of children if the equipment is used in the presence of children. |
| 16 | Do not allow users to wear loose fitting clothing or jewelry while using equipment. It is also recommended to have users' secure long hair back and up to avoid contact with moving parts. |
| 17 | All bystanders must stay clear of all users, moving parts and attached accessories and components while machine is in operation. |

**Item 6 is the only item whose figure differs between the two books**, and on the CRW900 it also
disagrees with that machine's own serial decal, which reads `150 kg / 330lbs`
(`crw900-2021-safety-user-weight-limit-contradiction`,
`spirit-rower-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lb`). Every other item is word for word the same in both
manuals.

**The clearance sentence is printed as an eighteenth bullet in the same run** and is carded on its
own because it is a figure a reader asks for by name:
`We recommend an area no less than 2ft kept clear around the rower for easier access and safety`
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-two-feet-of-clearance`).

**Four boxed WARNINGs sit beside this list and are not part of it** - the Proposition 65 box
(`xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`), the tank warnings
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-tank-warnings`) and the foot-strap warning
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-feet-in-straps-and-footplate-locked`) - along with the note that the
rower is not suitable for therapeutic purposes
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-not-suitable-for-therapeutic-purposes`).

**Item 8 and item 12 are the water rower's own hazards.** The bungee cord and the mesh belt are what
return the handle; a frayed one is called out as an *extreme liability* here, which no air-rower
manual in the range says.

**Do not answer an air rower owner from this page.** The CRW800 and XRW600 manuals carry no
SAFEGUARDS chapter at all (`crw800-2024-safety-instructions-list`,
`spirit-rower-xrw600-safety-instructions-list`), and the Spirit AB900 air bike's own safeguards list
is a different machine's page (`spirit-air-bike-safety-safeguards-list`).
