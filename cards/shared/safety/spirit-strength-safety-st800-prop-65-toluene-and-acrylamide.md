The three ST800 products print a **long-form** Proposition 65 warning that names the chemicals. It
sits on the product registration page, under the serial number block, not on a labels page:

> **WARNING**
> This product can expose you to chemicals including Toluene and Acrylamide which are known to the
> State of California to cause Cancer and birth defects or other reproductive harm. For more
> information, go to www.P65Warnings.ca.gov

Identical in all three books — ST800FI flat/incline bench, ST800DR3 dumbbell rack, ST800FT functional
trainer.

**This is a different warning from the one on the rest of the Spirit strength range.** The
thirty-six CSS, CSD, CSF and CSI machines carry the short form, *Cancer and Reproductive Harm -
www.P65Warnings.ca.gov*, as a sticker on the machine and name no chemical at all. Do not quote
Toluene and Acrylamide for a CSS or CSD machine: their manuals do not say it.

The ST800 books also print this as manual text rather than as a picture of a decal, so the manuals
do not say whether a matching sticker is fitted to the product.
