**None of the five 2015 XT owner's manuals has a compliance or notices page.** Their tables of
contents run Important Safety Instructions, Important Electrical Instructions, Important
Grounding Instructions, Important Operation Instructions, Product Registration, and then straight
into assembly. There is nothing between.

The words **FCC**, **UL**, **Part 15**, **Class B**, **certification** and **RF exposure** appear
nowhere in any of the five. The only mention of **Bluetooth** in the XT285, XT385, XT485 and
XT685 manuals is in the Spirit Fit App instructions, which describe a Bluetooth transceiver for
pairing a phone or tablet; no version and no certification is claimed for it. The XT185-2015
manual has no Bluetooth content at all.

**That is an absence, not a "not certified".** These manuals simply do not carry the information.
Answer a certification question from the machine's own rating label or from Spirit, not from this
manual.

## Do not fill the gap from the 2023 cards

The 2023 XT285, XT385, XT485 and XT685 manuals added a WARNINGS, COMPLIANCE AND NOTICES page
carrying all three of the following. **These are 2023 statements about 2023 machines:**

- the FCC part 15 / UL / Bluetooth (with FTMS) list and the Part 15 declaration —
  `xt-2023-safety-compliance-certifications`
- the Class B digital device notice and the four things to try for radio or TV interference —
  `xt-2023-safety-fcc-class-b-interference`
- the 20 cm RF exposure separation distance —
  `xt-2023-safety-rf-exposure-separation-distance`

The 2015 manuals never state any of them, so none of those figures may be quoted for a 2015 XT.
