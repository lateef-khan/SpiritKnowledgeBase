**Two stickers on every one of the four, and the manuals put the serial number somewhere different on
each machine.** Ask which model before sending anyone to look.

| Machine | Serial number sticker | Proposition 65 sticker |
|---|---|---|
| 7.0S | the **level bar** | the **side of the console upright** |
| 7.5S | the **level bar** | the **side of the console upright** |
| 8.5S | the **shroud near the plug-in** | the **shroud near the plug-in** |
| 8.5S-Fit | the **stabilizer between the seat and shroud** | the **shroud near the plug-in** |

> **SERIAL NUMBER STICKER** - This sticker will be found on the level bar. Please record the number
> below the barcode for the purpose of registering your Recumbent Stepper's warranty.

- **Record the number below the barcode**, not the barcode itself.
- **All four manuals call the machine a Recumbent Stepper** on this page, including the 8.5S and
  8.5S-Fit, whose covers name them only as steppers.

> **PROPOSITION 65 WARNING** - This sticker will be found on the side of the console upright. The
> State of California requires us to inform you that this unit was manufactured using chemicals that
> could cause harm with improper use.

The sticker itself reads `WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm - www.P65Warnings.ca.gov`. It names
no chemical. The XS895, CSC900 and CSC880 manuals print a longer form that does -
`Toluene and Acrylamide` (`spirit-xs895-safety-instructions-list`).

**There is no third sticker on any of the four.** The product registration page on printed page 2
carries a blank Serial Number field to write it into, beside Date of Purchase and Dealer / Place of
Purchase.

**These are not the 2024 black units.** Those thirteen machines carry a third label and put the
serial number in ten different places (`spirit-2024-safety-product-labels`).
