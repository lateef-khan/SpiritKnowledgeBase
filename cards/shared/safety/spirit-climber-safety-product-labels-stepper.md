**Two stickers on every one of the four, and the manuals put the serial number somewhere different on
each machine.** Ask which model before sending anyone to look.

| Machine | Serial number sticker | Proposition 65 sticker |
|---|---|---|
| 7.0S | the **level bar** | the **side of the console upright** |
| 7.5S | the **level bar** | the **side of the console upright** |
| 8.5S | the **shroud near the plug-in** | the **shroud near the plug-in** |
| 8.5S-Fit | the **stabilizer between the seat and shroud** | the **shroud near the plug-in** |

> **SERIAL NUMBER STICKER** - This sticker will be found on the level bar. Please record the number
> below the barcode for the purpose of registering your Recumbent Stepper's warranty.

- **Record the number below the barcode**, not the barcode itself.
- **All four manuals call the machine a Recumbent Stepper** on this page, including the 8.5S and
  8.5S-Fit, whose covers name them only as steppers.

> **PROPOSITION 65 WARNING** - This sticker will be found on the side of the console upright. The
> State of California requires us to inform you that this unit was manufactured using chemicals that
> could cause harm with improper use.

The sticker itself reads `WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm - www.P65Warnings.ca.gov`. It names
no chemical. The XS895, CSC900 and CSC880 manuals print a longer form that does -
`Toluene and Acrylamide` (`spirit-xs895-safety-instructions-list`).

**There is no third sticker on any of the four.** The product registration page on printed page 2
carries a blank Serial Number field to write it into, beside Date of Purchase and Dealer / Place of
Purchase.

**These are not the 2024 black units.** Those thirteen machines carry a third label and put the
serial number in ten different places (`spirit-2024-safety-product-labels`).

## Two service manuals photograph the sticker where the owner's manuals say it is

**The 7.5S (RS9600-SS021) and 8.5S (MS2000-SB036-01) service manuals each open with a page headed `1. Serial Number
Location` and a photograph, and no words.** Read from the renders:

- **7.5S**: the machine from the front; the barcode sticker, boxed in red, sits on the top of the grey front stabilizer
  bar at the left end, beside a small yellow-triangle WARNING label. That is the level bar the owner's manual names.
- **8.5S**: two photographs of the rear of the base shroud. The `SERIAL NUMBER` sticker, boxed in red, is on the rear
  face of the shroud directly above the power inlet panel - an IEC inlet, a rocker switch, a 9-pin port and a USB port
  in a row on the rear stabilizer - which is the shroud near the plug-in the owner's manual names. The close-up shows
  the barcode with the number `7855452601000003` printed above it (it begins with the machine's 785545 part number)
  and `SPIRIT (870) 935-1107 / MADE IN TAIWAN / U.S. Pat. See www.spiritfitness.com/legal` beneath.

**The 8.5S photograph shows four other labels the owner's manual does not describe**: a rating label reading `SPIRIT 8.5S
~100-240V, 50/60Hz, 1.76-0.71A`, `Rehabilitation Recumbent Seated Stepper`, `IEC 60601-1`, `IEC 60601-1-2`, `MEDICAL -
GENERAL MEDICAL EQUIPMENT AS TO ELECTRICAL SHOCK, FIRE AND MECHANICAL HAZARD ONLY IN ACCORDANCE ...`, a MET
Classified mark, Dyaco's Changhua address and `200 KG maximum`; a fuse label `Replacement Fuse: 5A, 250V, Breaking
Capacity: 200A, Fast Acting, Type GFE`; a `WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm - www.P65Warnings.ca.gov` label (the
owner's manual's Proposition 65 sticker, in this position); and a `Product UDI code` barcode label. The rating figures
are the 8.5S specification page's (`spirit-climber-85s-specs-mains-power-supply`); `200 KG maximum` is the 440 lb limit
(`spirit-climber-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lbs`). Read from a 300 dpi render; the smaller print on the rating label is
at the limit of legibility and is quoted only where it was clear.

**Neither service manual prints a safety chapter, a safety list, a Proposition 65 text or a patient-safety page.** The
words *patient*, *contraindication*, *warning* and *caution* appear in neither book, in the text layer, with
letter-spacing allowed, or in the OCR supplements; the photograph above is the only safety-related content either one
has.
