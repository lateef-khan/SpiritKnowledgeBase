**All three manuals print the same sentence**, the 4.0T on its Electrical Safety page and the 7.0T
and 8.0T as item 16 of their safety instructions:

> Do not attempt any servicing or adjustments other than those described in this manual. All else
> must be left to trained service personnel familiar with electro-mechanical equipment and
> authorized under the laws of the country in question to carry out maintenance and repair work.

- **The line is what the manual describes.** Anything the manual documents is the owner's to do;
  everything else is not.
- **Trained service personnel** means, in the manual's own terms, familiar with electro-mechanical
  equipment **and** authorised under the law of the country to do maintenance and repair work.
- The rule covers **adjustments** as well as repairs.

Two rules apply to any work that is permitted: disconnect from the outlet before cleaning or
service work (`spirit-disconnect-from-the-outlet-before-cleaning-or-service`), and never remove a
cover without first disconnecting AC power
(`xt-2023-safety-remove-a-cover-only-with-ac-disconnected`).

Fitting a part is a separate question with its own answer:
`spirit-2026t-safety-do-not-modify-or-fit-aftermarket-parts`. Who may assemble the machine in the
first place is `spirit-2026t-safety-who-may-install-and-operate` - trained personnel only, on the
7.0T and 8.0T.

**Both Spirit MT200 owner's manuals print the same sentence word for word**, as a bullet of their
safety instructions page. Nothing in the rule changes: what the manual describes is the owner's to
do, everything else goes to trained service personnel familiar with electro-mechanical equipment
and authorised under the law of the country to carry out maintenance and repair work, and the rule
covers adjustments as well as repairs.
