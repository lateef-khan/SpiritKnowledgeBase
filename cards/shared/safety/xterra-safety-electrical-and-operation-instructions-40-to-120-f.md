**Six books print, on printed page 3, an electrical page in the older Dyaco wording - three
electrical bullets and four operation bullets - and the two 2013-2014 ellipticals add a fifth.**
These are the six plug-in machines whose safety page does *not* carry a Power paragraph; the others
print that paragraph instead and no electrical page at all
(`xterra-safety-external-power-supply-and-power-jack`).

**Important Electrical Instructions - WARNING!**

| Bullet | Rule |
|---|---|
| NEVER remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power supply. | covers off only unplugged (`xterra-treadmill-safety-disconnect-power-before-cleaning-or-service`) |
| If A.C. voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more, the performance of your [machine] may be affected. Such conditions are not covered under your warranty. If you suspect the voltage is low, contact your local power company or a licensed electrician for proper testing. | a 10% supply variation is not a warranty matter |
| NEVER expose this [machine] to rain or moisture. This product is NOT designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment. Maximum environmental ratings are 40-120 degrees Fahrenheit, 95% humidity non-condensing (no water droplets forming on surfaces). | `xterra-safety-temperature-40-to-120-f-humidity-95-percent` |

**Important Operation Instructions**

| Bullet | Books |
|---|---|
| NEVER operate this [machine] without reading and completely understanding the results of any operational change you request from the computer. | all six |
| Understand that changes in resistance do not occur immediately. Set your desired level on the computer console and release the adjustment key. The computer will obey the command gradually. | all six |
| NEVER use your [machine] during an electrical storm. Surges may occur in your household power supply that could damage [machine] components. (`xterra-treadmill-safety-use-during-an-electrical-storm`) | all six |
| Use caution while participating in other activities while using your [machine] such as watching television, reading, etc. These distractions may cause you to lose balance, resulting in serious injury. (`xterra-safety-distractions-while-exercising`) | all six |
| **Always hold on to a handlebar while making control changes.** | FS5.8e, FS5.9e only |

The word in brackets is the product name each book uses: *fitness bike* (SB500, SB4.5r, SB4500),
*Seated Stepper* (RSX1500), *elliptical* (FS5.8e, FS5.9e). The RSX1500 prints a **Plug-In** callout
beside the electrical bullets, and the FS5.8e and FS5.9e a **Power Switch / Plug-In** callout - both
are pictures showing where the adapter enters the frame, with no text under them.

This page is the ancestor of the treadmill Electrical Information page
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-electrical-information-page`), which gives the environment as 40 degrees C
and adds cord routing and the AFCI/GFCI paragraph; the two new-layout recumbents print that treadmill
page (`xterra-sb-safety-electrical-safety-page`). The Spirit indoor cycles print this older page for
their own machines (`spirit-indoor-cycle-safety-operating-temperature-and-humidity`).
