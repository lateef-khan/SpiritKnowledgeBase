**These two manuals do not print a safety list at all. They print three short blocks - DO, DO NOT
and WARNING - addressed to the club owner rather than to the rider**, and then repeat the whole
chapter in nine more languages.

> Safety instructions are provided in the following languages (in the order shown): English, Dutch,
> French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Spanish, Swedish, Chinese, Japanese

### DO

> These safety notes are directed to you as the owner of the [machine]. Please train all your users
> and fitness staff to follow these safety instructions. DO:
>
> - Do encourage each of your users to discuss their health program or fitness regimen with a
>   healthcare professional.
> - Do stop operating your bike if you feel dizzy or faint.
> - Do perform regular preventative maintenance.
> - Do exercise slowly until you reach a level of comfort.

### DO NOT

> - Do not let unsupervised children operate the bike
> - Do not use without proper athletic shoes.
> - Do not use in rainy weather outdoors, or in an enclosed pool environment.
> - Do not drop or insert any object, hands, or feet into any opening or within the area of the
>   flywheel.
> - Do not attempt to remove any shrouds or modify the bike.

### WARNING

> - Your [machine] is designed for aerobic exercise in a commercial or consumer environment.
> - Please check with your physician prior to beginning any exercise program.
> - Do not push yourself to excess. Stop if you are feeling faint, dizzy, or exhausted. Use common
>   sense when biking.
> - Read the owner's manual in its entirety before operating the bike.
> - Failure to obey this warning can result in injury or death.

## The one difference between the two books

**The JB950 adds a sixth WARNING bullet the AB900 does not have: `Recommended User Weight Limit:
450 lb`.** Apart from that line and the machine's own name - `Air Bike manufactured by Spirit
Fitness` against `Johnny G Spirit Bike` - the English blocks are the same words. Every word either
page holds that the other does not is one of those two things.

**That 450 lb figure contradicts the 350lbs printed on the JB950's own SAFEGUARDS page**, and the
AB900's SAFEGUARDS page gives 350 pounds with no contradiction to resolve. See
`jb950-2022-safety-user-weight-limit-450-and-350` before quoting either figure.

## A rule that is in the translations and not in the English

**The English DO block runs to four bullets. The Dutch block on the facing page runs to five**, and
the fifth is `Gebruik de bike alleen voor zijn bedoelde gebruik, zoals in deze handleiding staat
beschreven` - use the bike only for its intended use, as described in this manual. Both manuals do
this. The intended-use rule is therefore printed for a Dutch reader and not for an English one; it
is a printing omission, not a difference in what is required.

**Neither manual prints a numbered SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list, an outlet or grounding rule, a carpet
rule, a clearance figure or an operating temperature range.** The AB950 air bike, six years later,
replaces this whole chapter with a thirteen-item numbered list -
`ab950-2024-safety-instructions-list`.
