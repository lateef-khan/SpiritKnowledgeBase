**Twenty-four numbered items - the nineteen-item list of the 7.0S and 7.5S with five more, and one
figure changed.**

> To reduce risk of injury to persons: Read all instructions before using this appliance.

> **WARNING - To reduce the risk of electric shock**, disconnect your 8.5S from the electrical outlet
> prior to cleaning and/or service work.

| # | Instruction |
|---|---|
| 1 | Do not modify this equipment without authorization of the manufacturer. |
| 2 | To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons, install the 8.5S on a flat level surface with access to a 100 to 240-volt AC, 50/60 Hz, 15-amp grounded outlet. Do not use an extension cord unless it is 16awg or larger, with only one outlet on the end. The 8.5S should be the only appliance in the electrical circuit. Do not attempt to disable the grounded plug by using improper adapters, or in any way modify the cord set; a serious shock or fire hazard may result along with computer malfunctions. |
| 3 | Use this 8.5S only for it's intended use as described in this manual. |
| 4 | Keep children away from the 8.5S. There are moving parts, obvious pinch points and other caution areas that can cause harm. |

| 6 | Keep the electrical cord away from heated surfaces and out of all travel lanes and do not operate the 8.5S if the cord or plug is damaged. |
| 7 | Heart rate monitoring system may be inaccurate. Over exercise may result in injury or death. If you feel faint stop exercising immediately. |
| 8 | While the user's legs are on the pedal, the user's leg is extended but not locked out it's kind of 5-degree angle or bend on the knee. Please don't extremely step hit the absorber during the exercise it is not the proper usage of the device. |

Beside the list is the boxed note:

> Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of
> your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine.

**Item 21 is the one a caller gets hurt by.** The pedals are directly coupled to the flywheel with no
freewheel: the pedals keep driving the user's legs as long as the flywheel is turning, and they
cannot be left to coast. Nothing on the 7.0S and 7.5S lists says this.

**Item 2 is the only item whose wording differs between the two machines.**

| | 8.5S | 8.5S-Fit |
|---|---|---|
| Outlet in item 2 | `100 to 240-volt AC, 50/60 Hz, 15-amp grounded outlet` | `120 Volt AC, 15-amp grounded outlet` |

Everything else on the page is word for word the same. Quote the machine's own figure
(`spirit-climber-safety-outlet-100-to-240-volt-15-amp`,
`spirit-climber-safety-outlet-120-volt-15-amp-fit`).

**The clearance at the rear is three and a half feet, not one foot.** The 7.0S and 7.5S ask for one
foot at the front *and* the rear; these two ask for one foot at the front and **three and a half feet
behind** (`spirit-climber-safety-clearance-three-and-a-half-feet-at-the-rear`).
