**Both are pictures, not sentences: the text layer of neither book carries the words.**

**CT850ENT-2022 - a label on the AC drive motor.** The photograph of the lower controller and driver
shows a white label on the motor housing, above the motor's rating plate:

> WARNING! HAZARDOUS VOLTAGE! Disconnect power Before servicing

with a yellow lightning-bolt triangle. It is on the **motor**, not on the inverter the photograph
also names - the inverter, converter and power bridge board carry no such label in the picture.

**4.0T (2026) - a silkscreen on the driver board.** The board photographed as `DRIVER BOARD PCB
Component Locations` (marked CS 56007 Rev 1.0) is printed `HIGH VOLTAGE DANGER!` with the Chinese
`觸電小心` (beware of electric shock) beside the AC IN and AC OUT spade terminals and the 30 A relay,
and carries a lightning-bolt symbol at the AC input corner.

**Neither book prints a discharge time.** The instruction both labels give is the safety chapter's:
disconnect from the outlet before service work
(`spirit-disconnect-from-the-outlet-before-cleaning-or-service`), and for the CT850ENT never remove
a cover without first disconnecting AC power
(`xt-2023-safety-remove-a-cover-only-with-ac-disconnected`).
