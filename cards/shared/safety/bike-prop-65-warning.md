> **WARNING: This product can expose you to chemicals including Toluene and Acrylamide which are known to the State of California to cause Cancer and birth defects or other reproductive harm. For more information, go to www.P65Warnings.ca.gov**

The wording is identical in every manual that carries it.

**Ten machines have no such warning in their manual:** B74-2015, R72-2015, B94-2011, R92-2011, B94-2013, R92-2013, LCB-2011, LCR-2011, LCB-2013 and LCR-2013. Those manuals predate the notice. Its absence says nothing about the product; it is simply not printed.

The extraction of several manuals splits this warning across two non-adjacent lines, so the sentence is scrambled in `text.md`. It is reassembled above in printed order.
