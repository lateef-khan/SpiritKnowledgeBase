DO: discuss the health program or fitness regimen with a healthcare professional; stop operating if dizzy or faint; perform regular preventative maintenance; exercise slowly until reaching a level of comfort.

DO NOT: let unsupervised children operate the equipment; use without proper athletic shoes; use outdoors or in an enclosed pool environment; drop or insert any object, hands or feet into any opening or within the area of the product; attempt to remove any shrouds or modify the equipment.

WARNING block, in all six books: the equipment is designed for exercise in a consumer environment; check with a physician before beginning any exercise program; do not push to excess (stop if faint, dizzy or exhausted, use common sense); read the owner's manual in its entirety before operating; **failure to obey this warning can result in injury or death.**

Two wording variants, same rules. The SW121 and SRVO-2021 books address the owner as training all users (`Please train all your users to follow these safety instructions`); the other four read `Please follow these safety instructions`. The SRVO Complete book reads `designed for exercise in a consumer environment`; the other five read `designed for the exercise in a consumer environment`.
