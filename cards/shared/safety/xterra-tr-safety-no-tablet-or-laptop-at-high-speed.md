**The two Hiker treadmills - the ones sold with a desktop - are the only Xterra books with this rule.**
It is item 20 of their safety list, pushing the shoes rule to 21 and the weight limit to 22.

> 20. Do not operate tablet or laptop when you are running, walking or climbing at high speed.

The manual does not define `high speed`. What it does say is that the desk is for slow use: the same
page's distraction bullet warns that reading or watching television can cost you your balance
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-distractions-while-walking`), and this item makes the desk's own use
conditional on pace.

`Climbing` refers to the incline - the Hiker books use it for walking at grade.

The other seventeen Xterra treadmill manuals, none of which has a desk, print no tablet or laptop
rule. The WS200 and WS300 ship with a tablet holder and the other new-layout books draw an accessory
holder on the console; none of them says anything about using it while moving.
