The operation instructions print the same walking rules in every legacy book in this wave:

- **NEVER operate this treadmill without reading and completely understanding the results of any operational change you request from the computer.**
- **Changes in speed and incline do not occur immediately.** Set the desired speed and release the adjustment key; the computer obeys gradually.
- **Use caution while participating in other activities** such as watching television or reading; these distractions may cause loss of balance or straying from the center of the belt, which may result in serious injury.
- **NEVER mount or dismount the treadmill while the belt is moving. SOLE treadmills start at a very low speed and it is unnecessary to straddle the belt during start up.** Simply standing on the belt during slow acceleration is proper once the unit is learned. (The 2006-family and 2007-printing books print "start with at a very low speed".)
- **Always hold on to a handrail or hand bar while making control changes** (incline, speed, etc.).
- **Do not use excessive pressure on console control keys.** They are precision set to work with little finger pressure; pushing harder will not make the unit go faster or slower. If the buttons do not function properly with normal pressure, contact the SOLE dealer.

The 28 non-desk A2 machines print the same six rules word for word. The TD80-2019 desk book differs throughout and has its own card (`td80-2019-safety-desk-walking-operation-rules`).
