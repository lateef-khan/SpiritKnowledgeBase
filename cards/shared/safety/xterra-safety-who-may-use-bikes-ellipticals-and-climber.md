**Thirty books, three templates, and only two of them print an age.** No book mentions
pacemakers or pregnancy.

| Rule | Wording | Books |
|---|---|---|
| Capability | `This exercise equipment is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge.` | item 2 or 3 of the appliance lists: FB150, FB350, FB160, FB360, FB180, EU100, EU150, SB150, SB250, SB2.5r, SB4500, SB240, SB600, RSX1500 (2021 book), FS1.5, FS2.5, FS3.0, FS3.5; item 4 of the owner guidelines: AIR350, AIR650, MB550, MBX2500, SB120, UB120 |
| Supervision (older wording) | `Close supervision is necessary when this appliance is used by, on, or near children, invalids, or disabled persons.` | item 3: SB500 (both printings), SB4.5r, FS150 |
| Supervision (older wording, shorter) | `Close supervision is necessary when this appliance is used by, on, or near children, or disabled persons.` | item 3: FS5.8e, FS5.9e, RSX1500 2017 printing |
| Children and pets | `In a home setting, keep children away from the bike when it is not in use. Keep children and pets away from the unit while it is in use.` | the cycle lists: AIR350, AIR650, MB500, MB550, MBX2500, SB120, UB120 |
| Physician | `Before beginning this or any exercise program, consult a physician. This is especially important for persons over the age of 35 or persons with pre-existing health conditions.` | every appliance-list book (23) |
| Over-training | `Serious injury or death may occur from over-training. Consult a medical doctor or qualified fitness instructor to determine an exercise program appropriate for your level of fitness.` plus `Obtain a complete physical examination from your medical doctor` in the owner guidelines | the seven cycle-list books (`xterra-bike-safety-operation-instructions-owner-guidelines`) |
| Household | `The appliance is intended for household use.` | every appliance-list book (23) |
| **Age** | `Risk of personal injury - Keep children under the age of 13 away from machine.` | **SB240 and SB600 only**, on the safety warning sticker on the upright (`xterra-sb-safety-warning-sticker-on-the-upright`) - not in their 21-item list |

**The MB500 has no capability rule at all**: its 2014 owner guidelines print five items and skip it.
Its only rule for who may use the bike is *children and pets away*.

**No supervised-user exception.** Where the capability wording is used, item 3 stops at `and
knowledge`; the *unless supervised* clause of the WS treadmills is not printed on any bike,
elliptical or stepper. The older *close supervision* wording allows children and disabled persons
under supervision and names no age.

The three indoor cycles add a stop-if-unwell warning during the ride
(`xterra-mb-safety-stop-if-chest-pain-faint-or-short-of-breath`); the SB240 and SB600 sticker adds
`Consult your physician before use` and `Stop immediately if you become dizzy or experience chest
pains`. The Xterra treadmills print the same three appliance rules
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-who-may-use-the-treadmill`); the Sole SB indoor cycles, the Spirit bikes and the
Spirit CE ellipticals print their own versions (`sole-indoor-cycle-children-and-pets`,
`spirit-bike-safety-minimum-age-13-and-16`, `spirit-ce-safety-who-may-use-the-elliptical`).
