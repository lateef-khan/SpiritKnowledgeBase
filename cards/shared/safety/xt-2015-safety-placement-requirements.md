**These 2015 manuals have no location or clearance page.** What they say about placement is
scattered across the safety and electrical pages, and it is only this:

- **A flat, level surface**, with access to a 110-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet with only the
  treadmill plugged into the circuit — `xt-2015-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`.
- **Not on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet.** Damage to both carpet and equipment may result
  — printed in the Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions,
  `xt-2015-safety-fitness-equipment-instructions`.
- **Not outdoors**, and never exposed to rain or moisture, near a pool or spa, or in any other
  high humidity environment; 40 degrees C and 95% non-condensing humidity are the stated limits —
  `xt-2023-safety-operating-environment-limits`.

**No clearance distance is printed in any of the five 2015 manuals** - not for the front, not for
the sides, and not behind the machine. Nothing is said about children or pets around it, and
nothing about keeping the surrounding area clear of furniture.

**Do not fill that gap from `xt-2023-safety-placement-and-clearance`.** The 2023 XT manuals added
a GROUNDING & LOCATION REQUIREMENTS page asking for at least 20in at the front, 20in each side and
6ft behind, plus rules about children, pets and clearing the area. Those figures were introduced
in the 2023 manuals; these 2015 manuals never state them, so quoting 6ft for a 2015 machine would
be an invention. If a customer needs a clearance figure for a 2015 XT, say the manual does not
give one.

The commercial Spirit CT900 family quotes ASTM figures instead
(`ct900ent-clearance-requirements`); those are a different machine family's numbers.

**The XT485ENT owner's manual has no location or clearance page either**, and says the same three
scattered things: a flat level surface with a 110-volt 15-amp grounded outlet
(`xt-2015-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`), not on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet, and
not outdoors or anywhere exposed to rain, moisture, a pool or spa or high humidity, at 40 degrees C
and 95% non-condensing (`xt-2023-safety-operating-environment-limits`).

**No clearance distance is printed in it** - not for the front, not for the sides, not behind - and
it says nothing about children, pets or keeping the area clear of furniture.

**Do not fill that gap from its model-mate.** The XT685ENT manual does have a GROUNDING & LOCATION
REQUIREMENTS page, asking for 20in at the front and each side and 6ft behind
(`xt-2023-safety-placement-and-clearance`). Those are that machine's figures.
