**All five 2015 XT manuals state this twice, on two different pages.**

The Important Safety Instructions page states it as the requirement the installer must meet:

> WARNING - To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons, install the
> treadmill on a flat level surface with access to a 110-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet with only
> the treadmill plugged into the circuit.

- **110 volt, 15 amp, grounded.**
- **Only the treadmill on that circuit.** Nothing else may share it.
- **A flat, level surface.**

The Important Grounding Instructions page states it again, as a description of the circuit the
product is built for. **Here the five manuals do not agree:**

| Manual | Grounding page wording |
|---|---|
| XT185-2015 | `a nominal 110-volt/15 amp dedicated circuit` |
| XT285-2015 | `a nominal 110-volt/15 amp dedicated circuit` |
| XT385-2015 | `a nominal 110-volt/15 amp dedicated circuit` |
| XT485-2015 | `a nominal 110-volt dedicated circuit` |
| XT685-2015 | `a nominal 110-volt dedicated circuit` |

The XT485 and XT685 grounding pages simply omit the amp figure; **their own safety pages still
say 15-amp**, so 15 amp is the figure for all five machines and the word `dedicated` is the
grounding page's own addition. Neither page contradicts the other on the voltage: it is 110 volt
in all five manuals, on both pages.

The rest of that grounding page - why the machine must be grounded, and the temporary adapter -
is `spirit-product-must-be-grounded` and
`spirit-temporary-adapter-for-a-two-pole-receptacle`.

**Other Spirit machines need a different circuit.** The commercial CT850 asks for 120 volt, 20 amp
(`spirit-ct850-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`) and the CVC800 climber for 15 amps at 120 volt
(`cvc800-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`). Do not carry those figures onto an XT.

**The 2023 XT manuals dropped the amp figure.** They print only `a nominal 110-volt circuit` on
the grounding page and no outlet requirement on the safety page at all, so the 15-amp figure is a
2015 statement. See `spirit-product-must-be-grounded`.
