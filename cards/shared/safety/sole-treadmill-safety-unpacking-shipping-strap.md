**READ BEFORE UNPACKING YOUR FOLDING TREADMILL. Serious injury could occur if this folding treadmill is not unpacked properly.** A Velcro strap around the treadmill base stops it unfolding accidentally during shipping; removing it wrongly can spring the deck open onto anyone standing nearby. **Keep the treadmill flat on the ground in its using orientation; do not turn it up on its side.** If the strap end is under the deck, reach under to grab it - never tilt the treadmill up for access.

The 2011-2013 folding books repeat the caution in assembly step 1: position the unit on a smooth level floor with the rear at least 3 feet from any wall, and do not remove the Velcro belt until the plastic wrap and Styrofoam are out from under the unit - turning it on its side after removing the belt may fold the unit up and cause serious injury.

No unpacking warning exists in the non-folding books (AS77, ATT8, TT8-2013, TT8C, VS73/VS77, VTT8, WS73/WS77, WTT8, TT8/UTT8-2006) - correctly, since they have no folding deck - nor in the F60-2016 book or the 2006 F63/UF63 book, both of which describe folding decks but print no unpacking page.
