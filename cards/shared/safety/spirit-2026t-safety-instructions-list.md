**Items 1 to 21 are the same on both machines, in the same order. Items 13 and 14 differ in
substance, and the 8.0T carries six extra items after 21.**

The page opens:

> To reduce risk of injury to persons: Read all instructions before using this appliance.
> WARNING - To reduce the risk of electric shock, disconnect your 7.0T from the electrical outlet
> prior to cleaning and/or service work.

1. Do not modify this equipment without authorization of the manufacturer -
   `spirit-2026t-safety-do-not-modify-or-fit-aftermarket-parts`.
2. Install on a flat level surface with access to a 120-volt AC, 60 Hz, 20-amp grounded outlet; no
   extension cord unless 12 awg or larger with one outlet on the end; the only appliance on the
   circuit - `spirit-2026t-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`,
   `spirit-2026t-safety-extension-cord-12-awg-or-larger`.
3. Connect only to a supply main with protective earth - `spirit-product-must-be-grounded`.
4. Use only for its intended use as described in this manual.
5. Keep children away; moving parts, pinch points and other caution areas -
   `spirit-2026t-safety-keep-children-away`.
6. Except as instructed, keep hands away from all moving parts.
7. Keep the cord away from heated surfaces and out of all travel lanes; do not operate with a
   damaged cord or plug - `spirit-2026t-safety-power-cord-condition-and-routing`.
8. Never drop or insert any object into any openings.
9. Do not use outdoors.
10. To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position then remove the plug from the outlet.
11. Designed for commercial use; meets the demands of orthopedic, sports wellness and general
    conditioning programs - `spirit-2026t-safety-designed-for-commercial-use`.
12. Do not use for any purpose other than the one it is intended for.
13. **Differs.** The 7.0T prints only `Over exercise may result in injury or death. If you feel
    faint stop exercising immediately.` The 8.0T prints the same two sentences preceded by
    `Heart Rate Monitoring systems may be inaccurate.` -
    `spirit-2026t-safety-over-exercise-can-cause-injury-or-death`,
    `spirit-2026t-safety-heart-rate-readings-are-not-medical`.
14. **Differs.** Two feet at each side and one foot at the front on both, but the rear figure is
    **three and a half feet** on the 7.0T and **six feet, six inches** on the 8.0T -
    `70t-2026-safety-clearance-around-the-treadmill`,
    `80t-2026-safety-clearance-around-the-treadmill`.
15. No after market parts other than those recommended by Dyaco.
16. No servicing or adjustments beyond those described in this manual -
    `spirit-2026t-safety-servicing-only-by-trained-personnel`.
17. Installation and assembly by trained personnel only -
    `spirit-2026t-safety-who-may-install-and-operate`.
18. Hold the handlebar for support when getting on or off.
19. Observe all minimum and maximum adjustment settings.
20. Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable. Quality
    athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue.
21. The safety tether cord is a simple magnetic design that should be used at all times; pulling it
    stops tread-belt movement - `spirit-2026t-safety-tether-cord-stops-the-belt`.

**The 7.0T list ends here**, with item 22: **User Weight Limit: 440 lbs** -
`spirit-2026t-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lbs`.

**The 8.0T list continues:**

22. Keep the safety tether cord in a proper and safe location so children cannot attach it to start
    the equipment - `spirit-2026t-safety-secure-the-tether-cord-after-use`.
23. Ensure adjustment levers and knobs (parallel handlebar width and height position levers) are
    properly secured and do not interfere with range of motion during exercise.
24. A-weighted sound pressure at typical head height, running at 12 km/h without load, no greater
    than 70dB - `80t-2026-safety-sound-pressure-level`.
25. Noise emission under load is higher than without load.
26. Installation of parts or accessories not originally intended or compatible with the equipment
    as sold will void warranty and may impact safety.
27. To prevent injuries related to heat generated by the device's motor or resistance system,
    ensure proper ventilation around the device, avoid overheating by adjusting settings or taking
    breaks, and be mindful of environmental conditions.
28. **User Weight Limit: 440 lb** - `spirit-2026t-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lbs`.

Both manuals print the boxed note beside the list:

> Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements
> of your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine.

**The 4.0T's list is a different list**, thirty items long, with an age-8 clause, a carpet rule, a
blanket rule and no commercial-use or tether-cord items: `40t-2026-safety-instructions-list`. Do
not answer one family from the other's list.

## The 2025 printing of the 7.0T manual prints the same 22 items with one figure changed

**Item 2 of the January 2025 book (revision 01.10.25) reads `a 115-volt AC, 60 Hz, 20-amp grounded
outlet`** where the 2026 book reads 120-volt; every other word of the 22 items, the WARNING lead and
the boxed note is the same, so the list holds for the 70t-2025 with that one figure
(`70t-2025-safety-outlet-115-volt-20-amp-and-a-nema-5-15p-line`).
