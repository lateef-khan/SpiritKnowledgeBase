**Both new-layout recumbent books print the treadmill Electrical Safety page**
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-electrical-information-page`) with the word *unit* throughout, and two lines
that only make sense on a treadmill were left in. Everything on it is a rule the customer can follow;
nothing on it names a voltage, an outlet or a plug for these bikes.

| Paragraph | Rule | Card |
|---|---|---|
| WARNING! Route the power cord away from any moving part of the unit **including the elevation mechanism and transport wheels.** | keep the adapter cord clear of moving parts - the bikes have transport wheels on the front stabilizer and no elevation mechanism | - |
| NEVER remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power. If voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more, the performance of your unit may be affected. Such conditions are not covered under your warranty... | covers off only unplugged; a 10% supply variation is for the power company or an electrician, not warranty | `xterra-treadmill-safety-disconnect-power-before-cleaning-or-service` |
| NEVER expose this unit to rain or moisture... The temperature specification is 40 degrees C, and humidity is 95%, non-condensing | 40 degrees C maximum, 95% non-condensing, never outdoors or by a pool | `xterra-treadmill-safety-temperature-40-c-humidity-95-percent` |
| Circuit breakers: Avoid AFCI/GFCI circuit breakers if possible... Brands we have tested are: Eaton (Cutler Hammer Series), Leviton (Smart lock pro) and Schneider Electric (Canadian home series). | avoid AFCI/GFCI if possible; three tested brands; the unit should be the only device on the circuit | `xterra-treadmill-safety-avoid-afci-gfci-breakers-tested-brands` |
| NEVER operate this unit without reading and completely understanding the results of any operational change you request from the computer. Understand that changes in resistance do not occur immediately... The computer will obey the command gradually. | resistance ramps; do not repeat a key press | - |
| NEVER use your unit during an electrical storm... Unplug the unit during an electrical storm as a precaution. | never in a storm; unplug it | `xterra-treadmill-safety-use-during-an-electrical-storm` |
| Use caution while participating in other activities **while walking on your unit** (SB600) / *while using your unit* (SB240); such as watching television, reading, etc. | distractions can cost you your balance | `xterra-safety-distractions-while-exercising` |
| Do not use excessive pressure on console control keys. They are precision set to function properly with little finger pressure. | light touch on the keys | `xterra-treadmill-safety-console-key-pressure` |

**The copy errors, so a reader is not misled:** the SB600 says *while walking on your unit* - the
SB240, printed two weeks later, corrected it to *while using* - and both name an *elevation mechanism*
neither bike has. The breaker paragraph blames `the high inrush currents of the unit drive
electronics and motor`; the bikes' only motor is the small resistance motor the service manual
lists, so the paragraph is the treadmill's and the tested-brand list was not re-tested on a bike. This card
follows the page as printed and flags the two lines rather than silently dropping them.

The page never names the adapter or its rating; the only power figure a customer gets from these
books is *Adapter* in the parts list and *the adaptor port is located at the rear of the Main Frame*
(`xterra-safety-external-power-supply-and-power-jack`). Item 12 of the safety list points to a
*Grounding Instructions* page that is not printed
(`xterra-sb-safety-grounding-instructions-referenced-but-not-printed`).

The Spirit CT900 prints the same page (`ct900-electrical-safety`); that card covers Spirit machines only.
