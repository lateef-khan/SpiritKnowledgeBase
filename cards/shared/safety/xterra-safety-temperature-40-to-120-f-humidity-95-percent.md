**Six books print the same environment limit in one sentence on the electrical page:**

> NEVER expose this [fitness bike / Seated Stepper / elliptical] to rain or moisture. This product is
> NOT designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.
> Maximum environmental ratings are 40-120 degrees Fahrenheit, 95% humidity non-condensing (no water
> droplets forming on surfaces).

| Limit | Figure |
|---|---|
| Temperature | 40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit - about 4 to 49 degrees C; no Celsius figure is printed |
| Humidity | 95%, non-condensing - the book defines it: no water droplets forming on surfaces |
| Never | outdoors, near a pool or spa, in rain or moisture, in any high-humidity room |

This is the only Xterra environment rating that gives a **lower** figure. The treadmill books and
the two new-layout recumbents give a single maximum of 40 degrees C with no minimum
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-temperature-40-c-humidity-95-percent`), and the nine plug-in machines with a
Power paragraph give no rating at all - only `allow the product sit in room temperature to warm up
before plugging in` after cold exposure (`xterra-safety-external-power-supply-and-power-jack`). The
safety list says it a second way, as item 10, `Do not use outdoors`.

The Spirit CIC indoor cycles and CT900ENT print the same figures for their own machines
(`spirit-indoor-cycle-safety-operating-temperature-and-humidity`, `ct900ent-operating-environment-voltage`).
