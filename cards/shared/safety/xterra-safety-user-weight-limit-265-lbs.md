**This is the 265 lb figure**, printed two ways:

| Book | How it is printed |
|---|---|
| SB240 | `21. User Weight Limit: 265 lbs.` - item 21 of the new-layout SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| EU150 | `14. . Max User Weight: 265 lb` - item 14, appended to the thirteen-item appliance list (the stray second full stop is on the page) |

Neither book has a specification table. The SB240 adds a boxed note under its list: `Please ensure
that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of your new
machine.`

**The SB600, the other new-layout recumbent, is 300 lbs** (`xterra-safety-user-weight-limit-300-lbs`),
and the EU100, the EU150's earlier sibling, prints no limit at all
(`xterra-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`). Several Xterra treadmills print 265 too
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-user-weight-limit-265-lbs`); that is a treadmill card.
