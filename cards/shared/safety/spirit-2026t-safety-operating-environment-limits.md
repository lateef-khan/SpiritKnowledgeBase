Both manuals print the same block, each naming its own machine, and give the standard they follow:

> If not stated otherwise Spirit by Dyaco 7.0T Treadmills are designed for operation in normal
> climatic surroundings (IEC 60601-1)

| Limit | Figure |
|---|---|
| Temperature | + 10 to + 36 degrees C |
| Relative humidity | 30 to 90 %, non condensing |
| Air pressure | 700 to 1060 mbar |
| Maximum operating altitude | approx. 10,000 feet (3000m), without pressurization |
| Transport and storage temperature | - 20 to + 50 degrees C |

The same page also states:

> Never expose this 7.0T to rain or moisture. This product is not designed for use outdoors, near
> a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.

> The 7.0T is not protected against the ingress of water or particulate matter.

**No IP rating is printed** - the manual states the absence of protection rather than a figure, so
there is nothing to quote as an ingress class. The same page adds that the machine is not suitable
for use in an oxygen rich environment: `spirit-2026t-safety-aerosol-and-oxygen`.

**These are the narrowest environmental limits of the three 2026 machines.** The 4.0T allows 5 to
48 degrees Celsius and 95% humidity (`40t-2026-safety-operating-environment-limits`), and the 2015
and 2023 XT manuals print a single 40 degrees C figure with no minimum
(`xt-2023-safety-operating-environment-limits`). A room that suits a 4.0T may be outside the
7.0T's and 8.0T's range at both ends.

The Application Specification page repeats the same two figures - `Temperature range: 10 C ~ 36 C`
and `Relative humidity range: 30% R.H. ~ 90% R.H., non-condensing` - as part of the intended
conditions of use: `spirit-2026t-safety-intended-conditions-of-use`.
