**Both walking-treadmill manuals print this under Functions, in the same words:**

> **Anti-Fall Function** - The treadmill will only operate when the uprights are in the upright
> position. If they are folded down at any time during operation, then the treadmill will
> automatically stop in 3 seconds. For safety reasons, the treadmill cannot be used with the uprights
> folded down (as it was in the original carton).

| | |
|---|---|
| Uprights folded | the treadmill will not run |
| Uprights fold during a workout | the belt stops within 3 seconds |
| Uprights up | the only working position - the machine ships folded and must be raised before first use |

Raising them is the first assembly step: lift the Upright Crossbar until it clicks at the top, then
press the red button on each handle with your thumb and lift the handle until it locks, and rotate the
Handlebar Stoppers under the frame. Folding is the reverse, with the stoppers rotated out to catch the
upright. Those steps are assembly cards; this card is the rule that the machine will not run any
other way.

**It is a second interlock beside the safety key.** The key stops the belt at once and shows `E07`
when pulled (`xterra-treadmill-safety-tether-cord-and-key`); the uprights stop it three seconds after
they drop. No other Xterra treadmill has an upright interlock; no Spirit or Sole card in the
repository describes one.
