- **NEVER operate this elliptical without reading and completely understanding the results of any operational change you request from the computer.**
- Changes in **resistance and incline do not occur immediately**. Set your desired resistance level on the console and release the adjustment key; the computer obeys gradually. The WE books read resistance-only (`Set your desired level`), with no incline mention.
- **NEVER use your elliptical during an electrical storm**; surges may damage components. **Unplug the elliptical during an electrical storm as a precaution.** The WE25/WE35 book adds `due to lightening strikes [sic]`.
- **Use caution with distractions** such as watching television or reading; they may cause you to lose balance, which may result in serious injury.
- **Always hold on to a handle bar while making control changes (incline, level, etc.).** The WE books read `handrail or hand bar` with no parenthetical.
- **Do not use excessive pressure on console control keys**; they work with little finger pressure. Contact your SOLE dealer if they misbehave. The WE55/WE95 book prints `contact your Spirit dealer` - a typo for Sole, quoted as printed.

The power-connector and transport-wheel notes on the same page belong to the assembly and maintenance sections, not this card.
