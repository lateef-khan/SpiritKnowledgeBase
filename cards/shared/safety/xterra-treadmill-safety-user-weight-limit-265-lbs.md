**This is the 265 lb figure.** Three books print it as item 21 of the SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list and
write it `265 lbs.`; the TR75H prints it as item 22 (its list has an extra tablet-and-laptop rule)
and writes it `265 lb.` It is the same limit.

> 21. User Weight Limit: 265 lbs.

Beside the list on every one of these books:

> Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of
> your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to your machine.

**The weight limit is printed in one place only.** None of these manuals has a specification table
with a user capacity, so the safety page is the whole answer.

**The TR95H, the other Hiker treadmill, is 300 lbs, not 265**
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-user-weight-limit-300-lbs`), and the TR75 without the H is 350 lbs.

| Figure | Printed on | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 250 lbs | TR150 | `Product Weight Limit: 250 lbs.`, a bullet in the Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions |
| 265 lbs | TRX1400, WS200, WS300 | item 21 of the SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| 265 lb | TR75H | item 22 of the SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| 300 lbs | TR65, TRX2500 | item 21 (TR65) or the last Fitness Equipment bullet (TRX2500) |
| 300 lbs | TR95H | item 22 of the SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| 350 lbs | TR75, TR85, TRX3500, TRX4500, TRX5500 | item 21 (TR75, TR85) or the last Fitness Equipment bullet (TRX books) |
| none | TR200, TR300, TR6.6, TRX1000, TR260, TR6.4 | no weight limit anywhere in the book |
