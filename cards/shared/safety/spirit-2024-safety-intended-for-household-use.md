**Item 13 of the safety list, in both manuals, in full:**

> The appliance is intended for household use.

That is the whole of the sentence. The manual adds no qualification, names no duty cycle, and says
nothing about a club, a gym or a hotel.

**The CT800 and CT850 are sold as commercial treadmills** and the rest of the same book is written
for a commercial machine. This sentence is the only statement of intended setting anywhere in
either manual, and it says household. **The manual does not reconcile the two.** Report it as
printed and do not soften it into "light commercial" - the manual never uses that phrase.

**The sentence is not new.** The CT800-2020 and CT850-2020 manuals print the same bullet in the same
appliance-precautions list. It survives the 2024 redesign unchanged while six other items around it
were rewritten (`spirit-2024-safety-instructions-list-treadmill`).

**Two other Spirit machines say the opposite in the same range.** The CSC900-2024 stairclimber's
electrical page opens `This equipment is designed for use in a commercial gymnasium or health club`
and writes its whole safety chapter for a club (`csc900-2024-safety-instructions-list`), and the
2026 rehabilitation treadmills are stated to be for commercial, rehabilitation and sports medicine
use (`spirit-2026t-safety-designed-for-commercial-use`). Neither statement may be carried onto a
CT800 or CT850.

**Whether it affects the warranty is not stated here.** Nothing on the safety page ties the sentence
to cover. Answer a warranty question from the warranty chapter, not from this item.
