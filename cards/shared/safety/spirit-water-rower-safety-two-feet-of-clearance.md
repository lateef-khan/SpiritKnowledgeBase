> We recommend an area no less than 2ft kept clear around the rower for easier access and safety.

**2 ft, all the way round, and that is the whole requirement.**

- **It is one figure, not four.** The manual gives no separate side, front, rear or ceiling
  distance - the 2 ft applies around the machine.
- **It is a recommendation in the manual's own words** (`We recommend`), and the reason given is
  access and safety.
- **No room size and no footprint appears with it.** Neither manual prints machine dimensions on
  this page, and neither carries a specification table.
- **No metric conversion is printed.** 2 ft is the only unit given.

**The two water rowers are the only Spirit rowers that state a clearance at all.** The CRW800-2021,
CRW800-2024, XRW600-2019 and XRW600-2021 manuals name no distance from a wall, no side or rear
space, no ceiling height and no room size (`spirit-ce-safety-no-placement-or-clearance-requirement`).
That is an absence in those four books; do not read 2 ft onto them.

**Do not read this figure onto the 8.5UE ergometer.** It asks for more, and asks for it
directionally: two feet at each side, one foot at the front and three and a half feet at the rear
(`85ue-2025-safety-clearance-two-feet-sides-one-front-three-and-a-half-rear`). The Spirit treadmill
figures are different again and are for a machine with a belt running behind the user
(`70t-2026-safety-clearance-around-the-treadmill`).
