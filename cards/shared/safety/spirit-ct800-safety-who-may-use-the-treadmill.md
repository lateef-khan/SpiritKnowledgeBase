Three separate bullets of the Important Safety Instructions list answer this, and the manual never
gathers them:

> Close supervision is necessary when this appliance is used by, on, or near children, invalids, or
> disabled persons.

> This appliance is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental
> capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge, unless they have been given supervision or
> instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible for their safety.

> Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine.

- **Under 13: keep away from the machine.** This is a keep-away rule, not a supervised-use rule,
  and it is the only age figure these manuals print.
- **Children, invalids or disabled persons near the machine: close supervision.** The rule covers
  being *near* the treadmill as well as using it.
- **Reduced physical, sensory or mental capability, or lack of experience and knowledge: not
  intended for that person - unless supervised or instructed** by a person responsible for their
  safety. The manual offers that exception in its own words.

**The supervised exception is what separates these manuals from the 2015 XT ones.** The XT-2015
manuals print the reduced-capability sentence flatly, with no `unless they have been given
supervision or instruction` clause (`xt-2015-safety-who-may-use-the-treadmill`). The rule is
otherwise the same sentence, so do not answer a CT800 from the XT card or the reverse: one allows a
supervised user and the other does not.

**No maximum age and no minimum user age other than 13 is printed**, and no height qualification
appears. The user weight limit is a separate bullet of the same list:
`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`.

## The other two CT800 manuals print less

**The CT800-2012 manual prints only the close-supervision bullet.** It carries no age-13 rule and
no reduced-capability clause anywhere, so neither may be quoted for a 2012 machine.

**The CT800ENT-2022 manual carries no age figure.** It prints `Keep children away from the
treadmill. There are obvious pinch points and other caution areas that can cause harm` with no
number, and a reduced-capability sentence that adds `(including children)` to the same supervised
exception. It also asks that the tether cord be removed after use to prevent unauthorized operation
(`spirit-2026t-safety-secure-the-tether-cord-after-use`). Do not quote 13 years for a CT800ENT -
see `ct800ent-2022-safety-instructions-list`.
