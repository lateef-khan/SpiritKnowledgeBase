- Route the power cord away from any moving part, including transport wheels. **NEVER remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power.** Voltage varying 10% or more affects performance and is not covered under warranty.
- **NEVER expose the SRVO to rain or moisture** - not outdoors, near a pool or spa, or any high-humidity environment. Maximum operating temperature **40 degrees C**, humidity **95% non-condensing**.
- **Avoid AFCI/GFCI circuit breakers if possible** - they may trip on the high inrush currents of the drive electronics and motor. New laws may require them; if nuisance tripping occurs, check for other devices on the circuit (fluorescent lights with electronic ballasts, coffee maker, space heater, hair drier). Optimally the SRVO is the only device on the circuit. Tested non-tripping brands: **Eaton (Cutler Hammer Series), Leviton (Smart lock pro), Schneider Electric (Canadian home series)**; the SRVO has built-in surge suppressors.
- **This product must be grounded**, for use on a **nominal 120-volt circuit** with the equipment-grounding plug in a properly installed and grounded outlet. **DANGER - improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in electric shock**; check with a qualified electrician in doubt. Do not modify the plug; a temporary adapter to a 2-pole receptacle may be used only until a grounded outlet is installed, with its ear-lug held by a metal screw to permanent ground.

No other strength book in this wave prints electrical pages - the bench, rack and dumbbells are unpowered.
