**Both manuals give one page to the stickers, with the same introduction:**

> The following pages show examples of Spirit Fitness warning labels and communication stickers
> placed on the equipment as part of the manufacturing process. It is critical that owners maintain
> the integrity and placement of these stickers. If you find any stickers missing or damaged contact
> your local dealer or distributor for a replacement.
>
> NOTE: STICKERS AND LABELS ARE NOT SHOWN TO SCALE.

| Decal | AB900-2018 | JB950-2022 |
|---|---|---|
| EXERCISE WARNING DECAL | yes | yes |
| SERIAL NUMBER DECAL | yes | yes |
| CALIFORNIA PROP 65 WARNING DECAL (For US model only) | yes | yes |
| **CE CERTIFICATION** | no | **yes** |

**The Johnny G is the only machine in this family whose manual shows a CE certification decal.** It
sits between the Prop 65 decal and the serial number decal on the page.

## What the exercise warning decal says

The decal is printed as a picture, in English above French. Recovered from a 300 dpi render:

> **SERIOUS INJURY COULD OCCUR IF THESE PRECAUTIONS ARE NOT OBSERVED.**
>
> - Consult your physician before use.
> - Stop immediately if you become dizzy or experience chest pains and consult your physician.
> - Heart Rate monitoring systems may be inaccurate, use them for reference only.
> - Read all warnings on the unit and be properly instructed or read the Owner's Manual on how to
>   use before beginning.
> - Inspect this machine for damage prior to use.
> - Keep body, clothing, and fitness accessories clear of moving parts.
> - Risk of personal injury - Keep children under the age of 13 away from machine.

The French half repeats the same seven points, beginning `Le non respect de ces directives pourrait
entrainer des blessures graves, voir mortelles`.

- **The decal says 13. The SAFEGUARDS page of the AB900 says 16** -
  `spirit-bike-safety-minimum-age-13-and-16`.
- **The decal warns that heart rate readings may be inaccurate.** That warning is on the machine and
  is not repeated in either manual's safety list.
- **The decal carries no weight limit.**

The serial number decal shows the words `SERIAL NUMBER`, a barcode, `SPIRIT`, `(870) 935-1107` and
`www.spiritfitness.com`. The Prop 65 decal carries only `WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm -
www.P65Warnings.ca.gov`, which is **not** the Toluene and Acrylamide text these two manuals also
print inside the book (`xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`). The two are different warnings and
neither substitutes for the other.

**Neither page says where any decal is stuck.** For where the serial number is, see
`spirit-indoor-cycle-safety-serial-number-location`. The AB950 replaces this page with a PRODUCT
LABELS page that does name locations (`ab950-2024-safety-product-labels`).

**The JB950's table of contents lists this page at 15 and SAFEGUARDS at 16. The pages are the other
way round.** The AB900's contents page is correct.
