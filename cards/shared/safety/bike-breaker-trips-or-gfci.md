**Never use a ground fault circuit interrupt (GFCI) wall outlet with this bike.** Like any appliance with a large motor, it will trip a GFCI often.

Route the power cord away from every moving part, including the elevation mechanism and the transport wheels.

**If the house breaker trips but the breaker on the bike does not:** some house breakers are not rated for the high inrush current that happens when the bike is switched on, or during use. Replace the house breaker with a **high inrush type**. This is not a warranty defect. Sole cannot control it.

The manuals name these examples of a high inrush breaker:

| Supplier | Part number |
|---|---|
| Grainger | 1D237 |
| www.squared.com | QO120HM |
