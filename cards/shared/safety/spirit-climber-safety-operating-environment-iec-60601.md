**These four are specified as medical devices, in metric, with four figures instead of one.**

| Limit | Figure |
|---|---|
| Temperature, in use | **+10 to +36 degrees C** |
| Relative humidity | **30 to 90 %**, non-condensing |
| Air pressure | **700 to 1060 mbar** |
| Maximum operating altitude | approx. **10,000 feet (3000 m)**, without pressurization |
| Transport and storage temperature | **-20 to +50 degrees C** |

The same page adds three absolute exclusions:

> Never expose this 7.0S to rain or moisture. This product is not designed for use outdoors, near a
> pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.

> The 7.0S is not protected against the ingress of water or particulate matter.

> The 7.0S is not suitable for use in an oxygen rich environment.

**The heading over the block names a different maker in each book.**

| Manual | Wording |
|---|---|
| 7.0S | `If not stated otherwise Spirit by Dyaco 7.0S Recumbent Steppers are designed for operation in normal climatic surroundings (IEC 60601-1)` |
| 7.5S | the same, naming the 7.5S, and **without** the `(IEC 60601-1)` reference |
| 8.5S | `If not stated otherwise **Dyaco MED products** are designed for operation in normal climatic surroundings (IEC 60601-1)` |
| MS300 | `If not stated otherwise **Spirit Fitness devices** are designed for operation in normal climatic surroundings`, with no standard named |

The figures are identical in all four. Only the sentence that introduces them changes, and the 8.5S
book leaves the OEM's name, *Dyaco MED*, in a Spirit-branded manual. That is a printing defect in the
Spirit book, not evidence of a second product.

**Do not answer from the Fahrenheit card.** The CSC900, CSC880, CS800, CRS800S, CVC800 and XS895
manuals give `40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit, and humidity is 95% non-condensing` instead - a wider
temperature band, a higher humidity ceiling, and no pressure or altitude figure at all
(`spirit-climber-safety-operating-environment-40-to-120-fahrenheit`). 10 to 36 C is 50 to 97 F, so
the medical machines are the stricter of the two by a wide margin at both ends.

**The 8.5S-Fit is not one of these four.** Its electrical page prints
`The temperature specification is 40 degrees C, and humidity is 95%, non-condensing` - one figure
rather than a range, and neither of the two blocks above.
