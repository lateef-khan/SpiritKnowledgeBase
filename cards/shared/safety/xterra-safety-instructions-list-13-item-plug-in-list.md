**Fifteen books print this page - every Xterra bike, elliptical and stepper that runs from a
plug-in adapter, except the two new-layout recumbents.** It is the Dyaco appliance page the older
Xterra treadmills print (`xterra-treadmill-safety-instructions-list-older-layout`) without the
treadmill's 120-volt outlet warning, tether cord and rear-clearance bullet.

> When using an electrical appliance, basic precautions should always be followed, including the
> following: Read all instructions before using this appliance.

> **DANGER - To reduce the risk of electric shock:** 1. Always unplug this appliance from the
> electrical outlet immediately after using and before cleaning.

> **WARNING - To reduce the risk of burns, fire electric shock, or injury to persons:**

| # | Instruction |
|---|---|
| 1 | An appliance should never be left unattended when plugged in. Unplug from outlet when not in use, and before putting on or taking off parts. |
| 2 | Do not operate under blanket or pillow. Excessive heating can occur and cause fire, electric shock, or injury to persons. |
| 3 | *two wordings - see the table below* |
| 4 | Use this appliance only for its intended use as described in this manual. Do not use attachments not recommended by the manufacturer. |
| 5 | Never operate this appliance if it has a damaged cord or plug, if it is not working properly, if it has been dropped or damaged, or dropped into water. Return the appliance to a service center for examination and repair. |
| 6 | Do not carry this appliance by supply cord or use cord as a handle. |
| 7 | Keep the cord away from heated surfaces. |
| 8 | Never operate the appliance with the air openings blocked. Keep the air openings free of lint, hair, and the like. |
| 9 | Never drop or insert any object into any opening. |
| 10 | Do not use outdoors. |
| 11 | Do not operate where aerosol (spray) products are being use or where oxygen is being administered. |
| 12 | Connect this appliance to a properly grounded outlet only. |
| 13 | The appliance is intended for household use. |
| 14 | **Max User Weight: 265 lb** - EU150 only (`xterra-safety-user-weight-limit-265-lbs`) |

| Item 3 wording | Books |
|---|---|
| `This exercise equipment is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge.` | SB150, SB250, SB2.5r, SB4500, RSX1500 (2021 book), EU150, FS1.5, FS2.5, FS3.0, FS3.5 |
| `Close supervision is necessary when this appliance is used by, on, or near children, invalids, or disabled persons.` | SB500 (both printings), SB4.5r, FS150 |
| `Close supervision is necessary when this appliance is used by, on, or near children, or disabled persons.` | FS5.8e, FS5.9e, RSX1500 2017 printing |

Then the Proposition 65 warning naming Toluene and Acrylamide
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-proposition-65-warning`) - **except** on the SB4.5r, FS150, FS5.8e and FS5.9e,
the 2014 SB500 printing and the 2017 RSX1500 printing, which predate it
(`xterra-safety-no-proposition-65-warning-printed`); the EU150 prints it on the contents page instead.

**Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions**

| Bullet | Printed on |
|---|---|
| To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position, then remove the plug from the outlet. | all fifteen |
| Do not operate equipment on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and equipment may result. | all fifteen; FS5.8e and FS5.9e add `You can purchase a floor mat directly from www.xterrafitness.com` (FS5.8e) / `www.xterrafitnessstore.com` (FS5.9e) |
| Before beginning this or any exercise program, consult a physician. This is especially important for persons over the age of 35 or persons with pre-existing health conditions. | all fifteen |
| Keep hands away from all moving parts. | all fifteen |
| The pulse sensors are not medical devices... (`xterra-treadmill-safety-pulse-sensors-are-not-medical-devices`) | all fifteen |
| Do not attempt to use your equipment for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended. | all fifteen |
| Wear proper shoes... (`xterra-treadmill-safety-proper-shoes`) | all fifteen |
| **User Weight Limit: 300 lbs.** | RSX1500 only (`xterra-safety-user-weight-limit-300-lbs`) |

The page closes with `Failure to follow all guidelines...`, `SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!`
and `CAUTION!! Please be careful when unpacking the carton.` (the FS5.9e omits the unpacking line).

**Thirteen of the fifteen print no weight limit anywhere** (`xterra-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`).
The DANGER line and item 1 are the whole unplugging rule for these machines; there is no tether
cord and no power switch on the books with a Power paragraph
(`xterra-safety-external-power-supply-and-power-jack`). The six older books add an electrical page
with a 40-120 F rating (`xterra-safety-electrical-and-operation-instructions-40-to-120-f`).

The Spirit XT-2015 treadmills and the Sole SB1200 print the same list for their own machines
(`xt-2015-safety-instructions-list`, `sb1200-2023-electrical-safety-rules`).
