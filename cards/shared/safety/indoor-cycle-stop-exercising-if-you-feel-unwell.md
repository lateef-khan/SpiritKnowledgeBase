**WARNING! IF AT ANY TIME DURING YOUR WORKOUT, YOU FEEL CHEST PAIN, EXPERIENCE SEVERE MUSCULAR DISCOMFORT, FEEL FAINT, OR ARE SHORT OF BREATH, STOP EXERCISING AT ONCE. IF THE CONDITION PERSISTS, YOU SHOULD CONSULT YOUR MEDICAL DOCTOR IMMEDIATELY.**

**This warning was dropped from the later indoor cycle manuals.** The SB900 2020, SB900 2021, SB900 2023 and SB1200 2023 owner's manuals have a BASIC OPERATION section with no such banner, and the words "chest pain" do not appear anywhere in them. Do not quote this warning as coming from those manuals.

For sb900-2021, sb900-2022 and sb900-2023 an equivalent instruction does survive, but in a different book: the DT3268 console manual that ships with those machines opens with a physician warning covering the same symptoms.
