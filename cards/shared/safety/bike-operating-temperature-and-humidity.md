**Operating temperature: 40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit. Humidity: 95% non-condensing (no water drops forming on surfaces).**

The same sentence adds that the bike must never be exposed to rain or moisture and is not designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.

Every one of the 14 upright and recumbent owner's manuals prints these two figures, and they are the same in all of them. **What moves is the heading they sit under.** Nine manuals print them under IMPORTANT ELECTRICAL INSTRUCTIONS; five (LCB 2013, LCR 2013, LCB 2016, LCR 2016, LCB/LCR 2019) print them under IMPORTANT OPERATION INSTRUCTIONS. **The LCB/LCR 2023 manual prints the whole sentence twice**, once on page 3 under ELECTRICAL and again on page 4 under OPERATION, word for word.

The ten SB700, SB900 and SB1200 indoor cycle manuals print **no** temperature or humidity figure at all.
