> Ensure there is a minimum space on the sides of this equipment of two feet for proper operation,
> easy access and to prevent possible injuries to others standing or walking nearby. There should
> be a minimum of at least one foot of free space at the front and three and a half feet at the
> rear (shown as below).

| Side | Minimum free space |
|---|---|
| Each side | **2 ft** |
| Front | **1 ft** |
| Rear | **3 ft 6 in** |

- **These are minimums**, in the manual's own words.
- **`(shown as below)` shows nothing.** The 8.0U prints the words and no drawing; both pages of its
  safety list were rendered and neither carries a clearance figure. The 8.5R prints the sentence
  without the bracket. The three figures are in the sentence alone.
- **No metric conversion is printed**, no ceiling height and no room size.
- **The rear figure is the largest** because the seat carriage and, on the 8.5R, the wheelchair
  approach are at the back: its removable seat rolls away for direct wheelchair access, its swivel
  seat turns 180 degrees in both directions, and a strap anchors the wheelchair at the wheel or
  frame member. The 8.0U is an upright with a step-through frame and prints the same figure.

**The 7.0R and 7.0U ask for one foot at the rear**, on the same sentence otherwise
(`spirit-med-bike-safety-clearance-two-feet-sides-one-foot-front-and-rear`). Do not carry this
card's rear figure onto them.

**The self-powered 4.0R and 4.0U print a different rule** - 2 ft on both sides for access, and a
training-area drawing in millimetres (`spirit-med-bike-safety-placement-and-clearance-two-feet-each-side`).

**The 8.5UE ergometer and the 7.0T treadmill print the same three figures**
(`85ue-2025-safety-clearance-two-feet-sides-one-front-three-and-a-half-rear`,
`70t-2026-safety-clearance-around-the-treadmill`) for other product lines.
