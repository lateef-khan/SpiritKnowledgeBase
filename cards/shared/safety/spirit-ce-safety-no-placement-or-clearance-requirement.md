**None of these nine manuals prints a clearance figure.** No distance from a wall, no side or rear
space, no ceiling height and no room size appears in any of them.

What they do say about where the machine may stand is two sentences, and not every manual carries
both:

> Do not operate elliptical on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and
> elliptical may result.

> WARNING - To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons, install the
> elliptical on a flat level surface with access to a 110-volt, 5-amp grounded outlet with only the
> elliptical plugged into the circuit.

| Manual | Shag carpet rule | Flat level surface |
|---|---|---|
| CE800 2013, 2021, 2023 | yes | not printed |
| CE800ENT, CE850 2016, 2020, 2022 | yes | yes, in the outlet WARNING |
| CE900 2021, CE900 2025 | **not printed** | not printed |

- **The flat level surface requirement is printed only as part of the outlet sentence**, so only the
  four mains-powered machines carry it (`spirit-ce850-safety-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`). Even
  there it is a condition on the installation, not a clearance.
- **Neither CE900 manual says anything at all about the floor.** No carpet rule, no level surface,
  no clearance.
- All nine say `Do not use outdoors`, and the seven with an electrical page add that the machine is
  not designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment
  (`ct900ent-operating-environment-voltage`).

**That is an absence, not a zero.** Say the manual does not state a clearance, and get a figure from
Spirit rather than from a treadmill card - the Spirit treadmill clearance figures are for a machine
with a belt running behind the user and do not transfer
(`70t-2026-safety-clearance-around-the-treadmill`, `ct900ent-clearance-requirements`).

**One Spirit elliptical does print figures.** The CES880-2025 manual requires an area of at least
**87 in (2200 mm) long by 43 in (1100 mm) wide** for safe operation, with at least **12 in (300 mm)**
of space at the front for access and passage. Those are that machine's figures alone and must not be
quoted for any of the nine above.

They are carded with the machine's own dimensions, because the manual prints the two in one
paragraph: `ces880-2025-specs-machine-dimensions-and-operating-area`. **Every other clearance card in
this repository sits in `safety`** - `ct900ent-clearance-requirements`,
`xt-2023-safety-placement-and-clearance`, `70t-2026-safety-clearance-around-the-treadmill` and
`ct800ent-2022-safety-placement-and-rear-clearance` among them - so a reader who expects to find the
CES880 figures here will not; this card is the safety-side pointer to them. The fact is written
once, there.

The levelers on the CE850, CE900 and CES880 are an assembly step, not a placement rule; they set the
machine steady on whatever floor it is standing on.
