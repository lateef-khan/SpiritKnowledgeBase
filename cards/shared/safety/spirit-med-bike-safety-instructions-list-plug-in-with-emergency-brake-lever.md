**A medical device's list: 22 numbered items opening with an electrical warning, and the same 22
sentences in the 7.0R and 7.0U books in two different orders.**

> To reduce risk of injury to persons: Read all instructions before using this appliance.
>
> **! WARNING - To reduce the risk of electric shock,** disconnect your 7.0R from the electrical
> outlet prior to cleaning and/or service work.
>
> Read all instructions before using this appliance.

The 7.0R numbering:

| # | Instruction |
|---|---|
| 1 | Do not modify this equipment without authorization of the manufacturer. |
| 2 | To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons, install the 7.0R on a flat level surface with access to a 90 to 240-volt AC, 50/60 Hz, 15-amp grounded outlet. Do not use an extension cord unless it is 16awg or larger, with only one outlet on the end. The 7.0R should be the only appliance in the electrical circuit. Do not attempt to disable the grounded plug by using improper adapters, or in any way modify the cord set; a serious shock or fire hazard may result along with computer malfunctions. |
| 3 | Use this 7.0R only for it's intended use as described in this manual. |
| 4 | Keep children away from the 7.0R. There are moving parts, obvious pinch points and other caution areas that can cause harm. |
| 5 | Except as instructed for use of the 7.0R, keep hands away from all moving parts. |
| 6 | Keep the electrical cord away from heated surfaces and out of all travel lanes and do not operate the 7.0R if the cord or plug is damaged. |

| 21 | The flywheel in the 7.0R does not have a freewheel, but is directly connected to the pedals. Beside item 18 is the boxed weight-and-power note the 2024 and 2025 Spirit manuals all carry.

**The 7.0U book prints the same 22 sentences with items 7 to 17 in another order** - 7 never drop
or insert, 8 do not use outdoors, 9 to disconnect, 10 designed for commercial use, 11 intended
purpose, 12 pulse sensors, 13 heart rate monitoring, 14 clearance, 15 after market parts
(`recommended by Dyaco`, without `Spirit by`), 16 servicing, 17 installation; 18 to 22 are the 7.0R's
13, 14, 20, 21 and 22. No rule is added or dropped.

- **Item 2 is the outlet, the cord gauge and the adapter rule in one sentence**
  (`spirit-med-bike-safety-outlet-90-to-240-volt-15-amp`).
- **Items 11 and 12 restrict who may touch the machine.** Servicing beyond this manual, and
  installation and assembly, are for trained personnel only.
- **Item 21 is the item unique to the rehabilitation bikes** - no freewheel, auto-braking software,
  and an **emergency brake lever** (`spirit-med-bike-safety-no-freewheel-auto-brake-and-brake-lever`).
  The 8.0U and 8.5R print the same paragraph and call the lever a manual mechanical brake
  (`spirit-med-bike-safety-instructions-list-plug-in-with-protective-earth-and-heat-warning`).
- **The list does not say who may not use the machine.** The contraindications are two pages later
  (`spirit-med-bike-safety-contraindications-osteoporosis-fractures-and-acute-conditions`).

## The Dyaco MED 7.0R of 2021 prints the same rules as bullets, in five languages

**The Dyaco edition (Rev. 1.2.1, 2021/10/14, CE MDD) prints every sentence above** under
`Attention`, `Danger` and `Warning` headings, unnumbered, in the 7.0R order, with `recommended by
Dyaco` - and **no weight item**. Its limit is stated elsewhere, as 200 kg
(`spirit-med-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lb`). The page is repeated in Arabic, German, French
and Dutch. Its Dutch block converts the cord to `1.5 mm (16awg)` and the clearances to sixty and
thirty centimetres; the English figures are the ones above.
