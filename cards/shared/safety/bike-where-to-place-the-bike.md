- **Do not operate the fitness bike on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet.** Damage to both carpet and fitness bike may result.
- **Do not use outdoors.**
- **Never expose this fitness bike to rain or moisture.** This product is **not** designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.

**Only four of these bikes are told to stand on a level surface.** The LCB 2011, LCR 2011, LCB 2023 and LCR 2023 manuals open the safety page with "install the fitness bike on a flat level surface with access to a ... grounded outlet". The other eleven manuals never say it. (The indoor cycles have their own, differently worded floor rule.)

The temperature and humidity numbers that go with the moisture rule are printed in the same sentence; they are on the operating temperature card so a reader asking for the figures gets them on their own.
