**Two rules, printed in the same words in all five manuals:**

> - Only use the bike on a stable, level floor.
> - For safe operation, allow for at least 1foot (30cm) of free space to either side of the unit and
>   2 feet (60cm) of free space to the rear of the unit.

**The XIC600 manuals print the clearance rule but not the stable-floor bullet.** In their place the
list is headed `WARNING - To reduce the risk of injury to persons, install the Indoor Bike on a flat
level surface.` The requirement is the same; only the sentence differs.

- **No figure is given for the front of the machine.** Only the two sides and the rear.
- **`1foot` is set without a space in every one of the five books.** It is 1 foot, 30 cm.
- **Nothing is said about carpet.** These five manuals have no carpet rule at all. The AB950 air
  bike is the only machine in this family with one: `Do not operate equipment on deeply padded,
  plush or shag carpet` (`ab950-2024-safety-instructions-list`).

## The three machines that print no clearance figure

**AB900-2018, AB950-2024 and JB950-2022 give no clearance figure anywhere.** `30cm` and `60cm`
return nothing from any of the three books, and neither does a loose search for free space around
the unit. For an air bike this matters more than for a cycle, because its movement arms sweep well
outside the frame - but the manuals simply do not state a distance. That is an absence, not a
figure of zero.

**A Sole card states the same two rules with the same figures for Sole indoor cycles**
(`sole-indoor-cycle-space-and-floor`). It is a different brand and must not be used to answer for a
Spirit machine, and this card must not be used to answer for a Sole one.
