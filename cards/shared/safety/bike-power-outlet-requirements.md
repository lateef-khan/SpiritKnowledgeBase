- Put the bike on a flat level surface with a **120-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet**, and put nothing else on that circuit.
- The R92 2016, LCR 2016, LCB 2019 and B94 2016 manuals add: **on a 220-volt supply the circuit is 10 amp**. The LCB 2016 and B94 2019 manuals print only the 120-volt, 15-amp figure.
- Do not use an extension cord unless it is **16 AWG or better with a single outlet on the end**.
- Never defeat the grounding pin with an adapter, and never modify the cord or the outlet.
- The bike must be grounded. Grounding gives electric current a path of least resistance if the bike fails.
- If a properly grounded outlet is not available, a temporary adapter may be used on a 2-pole receptacle. The rigid green ear lug must go to a permanent ground, and a metal screw must hold the adapter in place. Use the adapter only until an electrician installs a grounded outlet.
- Unplug the bike before you clean it or service it.
