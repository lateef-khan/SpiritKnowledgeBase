**All the manual says about grounding is one sentence, and it points at a section that is not in the
book:**

> 12. Connect this appliance to a properly grounded outlet only. See Grounding Instructions.

**There are no Grounding Instructions.** In each of the three manuals the word *ground* appears
exactly twice, and both occurrences are inside item 12 itself - once as `grounded outlet` and once as
the cross-reference. There is no grounding chapter, no `This product must be grounded` paragraph, no
plug diagram, no temporary-adapter rule and nothing in the table of contents between SAFETY
INSTRUCTIONS and ELECTRICAL SAFETY.

**So the answer to give is: a properly grounded outlet, and nothing further from this manual.**

- **Do not answer from the treadmill grounding cards.** `spirit-product-must-be-grounded` and
  `spirit-temporary-adapter-for-a-two-pole-receptacle` carry the text the XT and CT manuals print
  under that heading. These three bike manuals never print it, so quoting a temporary adapter or a
  plug-modification rule as their answer would be inventing one.
- **The manual gives no plug type, no voltage and no amperage either.** Its ELECTRICAL SAFETY page
  names no circuit at all; what it does say about the supply is the ten percent variation rule
  (`xt-2023-safety-supply-voltage-variation`) and the AFCI/GFCI paragraph
  (`xt-2023-safety-afci-gfci-nuisance-tripping`).

**The XBR95-2023 manual has no grounding sentence at all**, not even item 12: it is self-powered and
its thirteen-item safety list carries no cord, plug or outlet item
(`spirit-bike-safety-no-mains-outlet-needed`, `ab950-2024-safety-instructions-list`).

**The ten earlier XB manuals are in the same position.** XBR95 2016, 2018 and 2021, XBR55 2019 and
2021, XBR55ENT-2021, XBU55 2019 and 2021, XBU55ENT-2021 and XBR25-2019 print
`Connect this appliance to a properly grounded outlet only` as a bullet and have no Grounding
Instructions page either - but they do not promise one, because their bullet carries no
cross-reference (`spirit-xe-2021-safety-instructions-list`).

**The five 2023 XT treadmill manuals do print the section item 12 names**, which is why the same item
resolves for a treadmill and dangles for a bike. The cross-reference was carried across with the rest
of the page when the bike manual was built from the treadmill one.

## The service manuals print the section the owner's manuals point at

**The XBR55-2023 and XBU55-2023 service manuals carry 7.3 Important Grounding Instructions in full**
- `This product must be grounded`, the DANGER about the grounding conductor, `a nominal 120-volt
circuit`, the plug drawing and the temporary-adapter paragraph (`spirit-product-must-be-grounded`,
`spirit-temporary-adapter-for-a-two-pole-receptacle`). So the answer for those two machines is: the
owner's manual has nothing beyond item 12, and the service manual has the whole page. The same page
asks, three paragraphs earlier, for a 220-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet
(`spirit-xb-2023-safety-outlet-220-volt-15-amp`). No XBR25-2023 service manual is on file, so for that
machine the statement above is still the whole answer.
