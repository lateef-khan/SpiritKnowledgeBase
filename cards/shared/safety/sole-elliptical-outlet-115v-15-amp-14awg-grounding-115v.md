- Install the elliptical on a flat level surface with access to a **115-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet**, with only the elliptical plugged into the circuit.
- **DO NOT USE AN EXTENSION CORD UNLESS IT IS A 14AWG OR BETTER, WITH ONLY ONE OUTLET ON THE END**, with the same grounded-plug sentence as the 110-volt books.
- **This product must be grounded**, and it is **for use on a nominal 115-volt circuit** - the two paragraphs agree here, unlike the E25/E35 books. The temporary-adapter, ear-lug and metal-screw rules match word for word. No book named here mentions GFCI or AFCI outlets.
