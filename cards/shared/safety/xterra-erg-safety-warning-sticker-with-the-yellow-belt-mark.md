**Both books draw the label on the Before You Begin page, with a callout line to the main frame
beneath the pedals, and print no text for it** - the label is a picture, read here from the render.
Headed **WARNING - SERIOUS INJURY COULD OCCUR IF THESE PRECAUTIONS ARE NOT OBSERVED**, in capitals:

| # | Precaution |
|---|---|
| 1 | Consult your physician before use. |
| 2 | Stop immediately if you become dizzy or experience chest pains and consult your physician. |
| 3 | Heart rate monitoring systems can be inaccurate; use them for reference only. |
| 4 | Read all warnings on the unit and be properly instructed or read the owner's manual on how to use before beginning. |
| 5 | Inspect this machine for damage prior to use. |
| 6 | Keep body, clothing, and fitness accessories clear of moving parts. Keep children away. |
| 7 | *(a drawing of the belt with its mark)* **Do not pull the handle beyond the yellow mark on the belt. Doing so may cause the belt to come off of the wheel.** (`xterra-erg-safety-do-not-pull-the-handle-beyond-the-yellow-mark-on-the-belt`) |
| 8 | Do not remove this label. Replace if damaged. |

It is an English-only label; the bilingual sticker with the under-13 rule is on the new-layout
rowers (`xterra-rower-safety-warning-sticker-bilingual-children-under-13`). Items 2, 3, 5 and 7 are not in
either book's safety list (`erg160-2020-safety-instructions-list-13-bullets`,
`xterra-erg-safety-instructions-list-13-item-plug-in-list`) - the label is the only place the ERG160 book
says anything about heart-rate accuracy, and the only place either book names the belt mark.

The ERG220 drawing also shows a `WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm - www.P65Warnings.ca.gov`
label on the front upright (`xterra-treadmill-safety-proposition-65-warning`) and two folding-frame
stickers (`xterra-erg-safety-locking-pin-must-be-fully-engaged-during-use-and-when-folded`); the ERG160
drawing has only this label and the serial sticker (`xterra-rower-safety-serial-number-sticker-locations`).
The ERG160 service manual reprints the same drawing on its contents page.
