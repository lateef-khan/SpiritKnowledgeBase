> To reduce the risk of electric shock disconnect your treadmill from the electrical outlet prior to
> cleaning and/or service work.

All three manuals open their safety chapter with this line, the CVC800 with the word `climber` in
place of `treadmill`.

- It covers **cleaning as well as service**, not service alone.
- The action is **disconnect from the outlet**. Switching the machine's own power switch off is not
  what the sentence asks for.

**The CT850 2016 manual repeats it in capitals for one task.** Its general maintenance section says
of the yearly motor-hood clean-out: `UNPLUG POWER CORD BEFORE THIS TASK` (p. 54, printed 53). The
CT850 2020 and CVC800 manuals do not print that second warning.

Neither the safety chapter nor the maintenance sections of any of these six Spirit service manuals
state a capacitor discharge time or a lock-out procedure.

**The three 2026 Spirit treadmill owner's manuals - 4.0T, 7.0T and 8.0T - open their safety
chapter with the same sentence**, each naming its own machine and adding the reason as a lead:

> WARNING - To reduce the risk of electric shock, disconnect your 4.0T from the electrical
> outlet prior to cleaning and/or service work.

Cleaning as well as service, and disconnect from the outlet rather than switch off, in both
generations. These three manuals also carry the cover rule separately - no cover comes off until
AC power is disconnected (`xt-2023-safety-remove-a-cover-only-with-ac-disconnected`) - and, as
item 9 of the 4.0T list and item 10 of the 7.0T and 8.0T lists, how to disconnect:
`To disconnect, turn all controls to the off position and then remove the plug from the outlet`.

They state no capacitor discharge time and no lock-out procedure either.
