**Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine.** There are obvious pinch points and other caution areas that can cause harm.

**This age limit is only in the 2016 and later manuals.** The B74/R72 2015, B94/R92 2011 and 2013, LCB 2011 and 2013 and LCR 2011 and 2013 manuals print the same rule with **no age at all** - "Keep children away from the fitness bike" - and that wording has its own card. Do not quote "under the age of 13" for one of those machines.

The manuals describe no child lock and no removable key that disables the console. An age limit and adult supervision are all they give.
