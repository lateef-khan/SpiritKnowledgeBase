**DANGER - unplug immediately after using and before cleaning.** Then 13 numbered appliance rules: never leave plugged in unattended; no blankets/pillows over it; **close supervision** near children or disabled persons; intended use only; damaged-cord / not-working / dropped / dropped-in-water plus return to a service center; do not carry by the cord; heated surfaces; blocked air openings; nothing into openings; outdoors; aerosol/oxygen; **properly grounded outlet only**; household use.

Fitness Equipment rules: disconnect off-then-unplug; carpet damage; **consult a physician before beginning this or any exercise program** (over 35 or pre-existing conditions especially); hands away; **pulse sensors not medical devices** (reads `pulse`, not `hand pulse`); intended purpose; proper shoes (`your equipment`, not `your elliptical`).

Electrical page: cover rule, ten-percent rule, rain/moisture with `Maximum environmental ratings` of 40-120 and 95%. **SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!**
