**Six rower books ask you to write the serial number down and never say where to read it; the four
strength books do not even have the blank.**

The six rowers print, on the registration page:

> Record Your Serial Number - Please record the Serial Number of this fitness product in the space
> provided below. Serial Number: ________

and the welcome letter's `Please have your model number and serial number handy when you call`; the
only other mentions are the warranty exclusion for products `with original serial numbers that have
been removed or altered` and, on the ERG550W, a parts-list column headed *S/N* that is a part-number
index. There is no Product Labels page and no sticker on the Before You Begin drawing - the drawings
of the ERG500, ERG550W, ERG600W, ERG650W and ERG700 were rendered and checked; they label the
console, handle bar, pedals, seat, rail, tank and stabilizers only.

The four 2022 strength books print *Purchase Location* and *Purchase Date* blanks, the same
`serial number handy` sentence and the same warranty exclusion, and nothing else - no serial blank,
no drawing, no label page.

How it was checked: every `serial` in each book's text read; the ERG400 scan read from its OCR
supplements; the five rower drawings rendered from the PDF.

**Where it probably is**: the five rowers whose books do say put it on the frame - behind the water
tank on the ERG800W, near the pedals or the rail pivot on the magnetic rowers
(`xterra-rower-safety-serial-number-sticker-locations`). Offer those as places to look, not as what
this manual says. The Xterra bikes and treadmills have the same gap on most books
(`xterra-safety-serial-number-location-not-printed`, `xterra-treadmill-safety-serial-number-location-not-printed`).
