**400 lb.** Printed once in each manual, as the last bullet of the safety instructions list:

> Recommended Weight Limit: 400 lb

- **The manuals print it as `Recommended`**, not as a maximum, and say nothing about what happens
  above it.
- **No kilogram equivalent**, no second statement and no sticker in any of the three.

## The February 2021 XE795 printing does not carry this bullet

**Both 2021 printings of the XE795 owner's manual describe the same machine, `xe795-2021`, and only
the November 30 2021 printing prints the weight bullet.** The February 19 2021 printing's safety
list ends at `Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine` and prints **no weight
figure anywhere in the book**.

The figure was **added** between the printings rather than changed, so 400 lb is this machine's
limit. A customer reading the February book will not find it and is not holding a manual that says
something different - say which printing the figure comes from
(`spirit-xe795-2021-safety-instructions-list`).

## Why this is not the CES880 card

**The wording differs, and the wording is the whole of what these manuals say.** The CES880-2025 and
the two 2023 XE manuals print `User Weight Limit: 400 lbs.` as **item 13 of a numbered list**, and
the CES880 repeats it on its cover as `CAUTION: Weight on this product should not exceed 181 kgs/
400 lbs` (`ces880-2025-safety-user-weight-limit-400-lbs`). These three print `Recommended Weight
Limit: 400 lb` as an **unnumbered bullet**, with no kilogram figure and no cover statement.

The allowance is the same 400 lb either way. Quote the machine's own wording; do not tell an XE295
owner their manual calls it a user weight limit, and do not tell an XE395-2023 owner theirs calls it
a recommendation.

## The rest of the residential range

| Machine | Weight limit |
|---|---|
| XE195 | 350 lb (`xe195-2021-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lb`) |
| **XE295, XG400, XE795-2021** | **400 lb** |
| XE395-2023, XE795-2023 | 400 lbs (`ces880-2025-safety-user-weight-limit-400-lbs`) |
| XE100, XE200, XE300, XE150, XE350, XE550, XE395-2010, XE395ENT | none printed (`xt-2015-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`) |

**The XE395ENT manual prints no weight limit**, although it is otherwise the same 2021 book as the
XE195, XE295 and XG400 manuals. That is an absence; do not fill it from this card.

**The Spirit commercial ellipticals print 450 lb**, which belongs to no residential machine
(`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`).
