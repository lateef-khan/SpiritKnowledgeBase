- Install the elliptical on a flat level surface with access to a **110-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet**, with only the elliptical plugged into the circuit.
- **DO NOT USE AN EXTENSION CORD UNLESS IT IS A 14AWG OR BETTER, WITH ONLY ONE OUTLET ON THE END.** Do not attempt to disable the grounded plug by using improper adapters, or in any way modify the cord set. A serious shock or fire hazard may result along with computer malfunctions.
- **This product must be grounded**, and it is **for use on a nominal 110-volt circuit**. The temporary-adapter, ear-lug and metal-screw rules are printed in full.

The safety and grounding paragraphs agree in every book named here: both say 110-volt. No book named here mentions GFCI or AFCI outlets anywhere - the never-GFCI rule lives only in the service-manual cards. For the stencil books that print 115-volt at the outlet, see the linked cards.
