**All three manuals say no, and the 7.0T and 8.0T name the party whose approval is needed.**

The 7.0T and 8.0T:

> 1. Do not modify this equipment without authorization of the manufacturer.

> 15. Do not use any after market parts on this 7.0T, other than those recommended by Dyaco.

The 4.0T says it in its own words, as item 25:

> 25. Don't change any component by yourself, it maybe got injured or destroyed the treadmill.

*(That sentence is printed exactly that way. The manual's meaning is that changing a component
yourself risks injury and damage to the treadmill.)*

- **Aftermarket parts: only those Dyaco recommends.** Dyaco International is the manufacturer;
  Spirit Fitness is the brand.
- **Modification needs the manufacturer's authorization**, on the 7.0T and 8.0T. The 4.0T states
  the rule without naming an authority to ask.

**The 8.0T adds the consequence**, as item 26:

> 26. Installation of parts or accessories not originally intended or compatible with the equipment
>     as sold will void warranty and may impact safety.

**Only the 8.0T manual says it voids the warranty.** Do not carry that consequence onto a 4.0T or a
7.0T from their safety pages; their manuals state the rule without stating a warranty effect.

**The 4.0T list also carries an unfinished boilerplate line** where a replacement-parts statement
should be - item 22 reads `Suitable information about replacement parts that could affect the safe
use of the treadmill.` It names a subject and supplies no information, so there is nothing in it to
quote.

Doing the work is a separate question: `spirit-2026t-safety-servicing-only-by-trained-personnel`.

**Both Spirit MT200 owner's manuals print both rules**, and they name a different party from the
2026 manuals:

> Do not modify this equipment without authorization of the manufacturer.

> Do not use any after market parts on this device, other than those recommended by Spirit.

**The 2010 manual writes `Spirit`; the 2022 manual writes `Spirit Fitness`.** Neither writes
**Dyaco**, the name the 7.0T and 8.0T manuals use for the same permission. Quote the name from the
machine's own manual.

**Neither MT200 manual states a warranty consequence** for fitting an incompatible part - that
sentence is the 8.0T's alone. Do not carry it onto an MT200.
