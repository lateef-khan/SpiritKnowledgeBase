**Three folding rowers lock the rail to the frame with a pin, and one of them puts the rule on a
sticker.** The ERG220 draws two stickers at the rail pivot on its Before You Begin page:

> LOCKING PIN MUST BE FULLY ENGAGED DURING USE AND WHEN FOLDED UP (*Ball Head Locking Pin Sticker*)

> LIFT CENTER OF FRAME TO HELP LINE UP LOCKING PIN HOLES (*Pivot Point Sticker*)

and repeats both in the assembly and folding steps: `Insert the Ball Head Pin (65) into the lower
slot on the Main Frame (1) & Slide Rail (2). Lift center of frame to help line up locking pin holes.
This locks the rower in the unfolded position and can be removed to fold the rower up.` - then, folded,
`re-insert the Ball Head Pin (65) into its slot on the Slide Rail (2). This locks the rower into the
folded position.` **`CAUTION: The Slide Rail (2) will not stay in the vertical position before the
Ball Head Pin (65) is inserted. Make sure to not let go of it before it is secured or the Slide Rail
(2) may fall.`**

| Rower | The pin | What the book says |
|---|---|---|
| ERG220 (2023) | Ball Head Pin, part 65 | the two stickers and the caution above |
| ERG500 (2018) | Ball Pin, part 35 | `Before insert ball pin (35): 1. Slightly lift up the main frame. 2. Make sure the ball pin holes are lined up. 3. Insert ball pin (35) into holes.` and, folding, `the ball pin can be removed and the slide rail can be lifted up. Remember to replace the pin.` |
| ERG400 (2015) | Locking Pin, part 60 | `Pull out Locking Pin (60) of the Aluminum Track (2) and put on level, then return the Locking Pin (60) and lock well. Pull up the unit as shown in the figure in circled box when returning the Locking Pin (60).` |

The rule the three agree on: the pin is in whenever the rower is rowed and whenever it is left
folded, and lifting the frame slightly lines the holes up. Only the ERG220 says it in a sticker and
names the falling-rail hazard; the ERG500 and ERG400 have no sticker drawing and no caution. The
ERG700 folds at a release lever instead (`Hold the Handlebar (37) to lift the rower slightly and press
the Release Lever (12)... Lift and fold the Aluminum Track until it locks into place`) and prints no
pin. The seat-and-head hazards of standing any rower up are on
`xterra-rower-safety-vertical-storage-hazards-seat-slides-down-and-head-near-the-rear-stabilizer`.
