Thirty-three of the thirty-nine Spirit strength manuals carry a **SAFEGUARDS** page. It opens:
*The following fitness safeguards and operating precautions are directed to purchasers and users of
the Strength Equipment. Management should ensure that users and staff are trained to follow these
same instructions. Failure to follow these safeguards may result in injury or serious health risk.*

**Proper Usage**

- DO NOT use any equipment in any way other than designed or intended by the manufacturer.
- Injuries may result if exercising improperly or excessively. Consult a physician before starting.
  If you feel faint, dizzy or experience pain, STOP EXERCISING and consult your physician.
- Keep body parts (hands, feet, hair, etc.), clothing and jewelry away from moving parts.
- Follow the manual's instructions for correct foot position and basic techniques.
- The weight or load limit line, which differs by machine — see the cards linked below.

**Inspection**

- DO NOT use or permit use of any equipment that is damaged and/or has worn or broken parts. Use only
  replacement parts supplied by SPIRIT FITNESS.
- **Cables and belts pose an extreme liability if used when frayed. Always replace any cable or belt
  at the first sign of wear** (consult SPIRIT FITNESS if uncertain).
- EQUIPMENT MAINTENANCE — preventative maintenance keeps your liability to a minimum. Equipment needs
  inspecting at regular intervals.
- Ensure that any person making adjustments or performing maintenance or repair of any kind is
  qualified to do so.
- Do not use or repair any accessory that appears damaged or worn.
- Check all belts, pulleys and bungee cords regularly for signs of wear, and replace if needed.
- Replace defective parts immediately and do not operate the unit until all repairs are complete.

**Operating Warnings**

- Keep children away from the equipment. Parents or others supervising children must provide close
  supervision if the equipment is used in the presence of children.
- Do not allow users to wear loose fitting clothing or jewelry. Secure long hair back and up.
- All bystanders must stay clear of all users, moving parts and attached accessories and components
  while the machine is in operation.

**Two manuals in this range do not have this page.** **CSF-FUNT prints no SAFEGUARDS page at all** —
its contents jump from Safety Guidelines on page 8 to Parts of Your Unit on page 10, and the word
*safeguards* survives in that book only inside an unprinted layer of the file. **CSI-CPSP and
CSI-LROW print a rewritten version** with a pacemaker warning and an electrical block; those have
their own cards.

The nine CSD books word the weight line as *should not exceed 360 pounds (163 kilograms)*, spelled
out, where the CSS books write *360lb (163kg)*. Same allowance.
