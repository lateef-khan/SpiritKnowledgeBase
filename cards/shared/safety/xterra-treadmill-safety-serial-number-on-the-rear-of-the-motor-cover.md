**Six manuals put the serial number on the rear of the motor cover.**

> SERIAL NUMBER STICKER - This sticker will be found on the rear of the motor cover. Please record the
> number below the barcode for the purpose of registering your treadmill's warranty.

The sticker carries a barcode, the number under it (drawn on the page as `X XXXXXXXXX X`) and the
customer service number `(870) 336-4286`. The Proposition 65 sticker sits in the same place.

The registration page of each book has a blank labelled *Serial Number* to copy it into, and the
online registration at `xterrafitness.com/warranty` asks for it.

**The two Hiker treadmills put it on the frame near the power switch instead**
(`xterra-tr-safety-serial-number-on-the-frame-near-the-power-switch`), and the eleven older-layout and
2024 TRX books never say where it is (`xterra-treadmill-safety-serial-number-location-not-printed`).

The Spirit XT-2023 books say the same for their own machines (`xt-2023-safety-serial-number-sticker`).
