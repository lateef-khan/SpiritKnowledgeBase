> Please record the serial number of this fitness product in the space provided below (serial number
> is located on the center floor tube behind the crank).

**Both water rower manuals say it in words, in the same sentence**, and it is the only place either
book states a location. `center` is the manuals' own spelling.

- **The centre floor tube** is the long horizontal frame member the rail sits on.
- **Behind the crank** puts it toward the tank end of that tube, not under the seat.

**The two books show it in different pictures, and neither picture repeats the words.**

| Manual | What the labels page shows |
|---|---|
| CRW900-2021 | the decal reproduced full size, reading `SERIAL NUMBER: CRW90018120001` (`crw900-2021-safety-warning-labels-and-serial-decal`) |
| CRW800H2O | a drawing of the machine with a callout line to the front left foot (`crw800h2o-safety-warning-labels-and-sticker-locations`) |

**The CRW800H2O's own drawing disagrees with its own sentence.** The callout on its labels page
points at the **front left stabilizer foot**, while the sentence above points at the **centre floor
tube behind the crank**. Both are printed in the same book and it never reconciles them; the drawing
is a flat image and was read from a 300 dpi render. Tell a customer to check both places rather than
choosing one for them.

**No other Spirit rower manual names a location.** The CRW800-2021 and CRW800-2024 books leave a
blank line and say nothing, and the two XRW600 books leave a blank line and draw an arrow with no
caption (`spirit-ce-safety-serial-number-location`). The 8.5UE ergometer says the front stabilizer
(`85ue-2025-safety-product-labels-and-proposition-65`). Four Spirit rowing machines, four different
answers - never carry one across.
