**These 2015 manuals have no PRODUCT LABELS page.** What they print is a single
`Record Your Serial Number` panel:

> Please record the serial number of this fitness product in the space provided below.
>
> Serial Number: ______

Beside it is an illustration captioned **Serial Number Location**.

**The location is given only by that drawing.** The five manuals do not name the part the sticker
is on anywhere in their text, and they do not mention a barcode. So this card cannot tell a
customer which panel to look at - point them at the Serial Number Location illustration on that
page of their own manual, or have them read the number off the machine and confirm it.

**No safety warning sticker and no Proposition 65 sticker is described.** These manuals never say
that a warning label is applied to the machine or where it would be. The Proposition 65 wording
they carry is printed inside the manual, on the safety instructions page, not described as a
label: `xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`.

## What changed by 2023

**Do not answer a 2015 machine from the 2023 label cards.** The 2023 XT manuals added a PRODUCT
LABELS page that states in words where each sticker is:

| Label | 2015 XT manuals | 2023 XT manuals |
|---|---|---|
| Serial number | illustration only, part not named | rear of the motor cover, record the number below the barcode (`xt-2023-safety-serial-number-sticker`) |
| Safety warning sticker | not mentioned | interior side of the upright (`xt-2023-safety-warning-sticker-on-the-upright`) |
| Proposition 65 | printed in the manual, no sticker described | sticker on the interior side of the upright (`xt-2023-safety-proposition-65-sticker`) |

The 2023 manuals' "rear of the motor cover" is a 2023 statement. These 2015 manuals never confirm
it, so do not quote it as this machine's answer.
