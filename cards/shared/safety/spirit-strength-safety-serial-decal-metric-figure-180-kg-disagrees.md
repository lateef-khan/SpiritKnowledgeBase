**Quote 360 lb / 163 kg from the manual. The 180 kg on the decal is wrong.**

On these eight machines the serial number decal reproduced on printed page 4 reads

> MAX USER WEIGHT: 180 KG / 360 LBS

while the safety instructions on page 5 and the safeguards page on page 9 both give 360 lb with
163 kg beside it. 180 kg is **397 lb**, not 360 lb: the two halves of the decal do not convert into
each other, and the pound figure is the one that agrees with the rest of the book. The eight are
seven CSD dual stations — **ACBE, BCTE, CPSP, ITOT, LELC, LPCE and PUDA** — and one CSS single
station, **CSS-SCPR**.

Both decal readings were taken from 400–500 dpi renders of the page, not from the extraction.

The two newest CSD manuals, **CSD-LPSR and CSD-PFRD**, print `MAX USER WEIGHT: 163kg / 360lb` on the
decal and have no such disagreement. Neither do the other eighteen CSS machines.

The same 180 kg appears legitimately elsewhere in the range and does not indicate an error there:
the four CSF benches pair it with **396 lb**, which is the correct conversion, and CSF-FUNT pairs it
with 400 lb.

## The May 2025 CSD-CPSP update corrects the decal to 163 kg

**The CSD-CPSP owner's manual reissued as a May 2025 update (version 5.0, revision 01/23/2025) prints
the serial number decal as `MAX USER WEIGHT: 163kg / 360lb`** - read from a 250 dpi render of its
printed page 4 - where the February 2024 book printed 180 KG / 360 LBS. Its safety instructions
item 20 and its safeguards page still say 360 lb / 163 kg, so in that printing the decal and the
text agree. **The disagreement above is the 2024 book's**, and it stands for the other seven
machines until their manuals are reissued. Ask which printing the customer holds before saying the
decal is wrong.
