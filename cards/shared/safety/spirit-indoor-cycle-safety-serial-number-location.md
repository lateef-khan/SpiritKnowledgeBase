**Five of these eight manuals name a place in words. Three point at a drawing and name nothing.**

| Machine | Exactly as printed |
|---|---|
| AB900-2018 | `serial number is located on the center floor tube behind the crank` |
| JB950-2022 | `serial number is located on the center floor tube behind the crank` |
| AB950-2024 | `This sticker will be found near the rear stabilizer` |
| CB900-2013 | `Serial number located under rear cover.` |
| CIC800-2021 | caption `Serial Number Location` over a drawing; **no words** |
| XIC600-2018, XIC600-2021 | caption `Serial Number Location` over a drawing; **no words** |
| CIC850-2022 | **nothing at all** - a blank to write in and no drawing |

- **The AB900 and JB950 sentence is identical**, parenthesis included, and is the most specific of
  the three worded answers.
- **The XIC600 drawing puts an arrow on the underside of the main frame tube where it meets the rear
  stabilizer**, just behind the crank. That is read off the picture, not printed anywhere in the
  book, so give it as where the drawing points and not as a printed instruction. A 300 dpi render of
  that page returns no more text than `pdftotext` does; there is nothing hidden under the caption.
- **The CIC850 leaves the customer with no location at all.** Its RECORD YOUR SERIAL NUMBER block is
  a heading, one sentence and a blank line. If an owner cannot find it, the CIC800's drawing is the
  nearest guide, and the two machines share a frame layout.

## Registering the warranty

All eight print a REGISTER YOUR PURCHASE block beside the serial number blank, and they do not all
send the owner to the same place.

- **CB900-2013, CIC800-2021, CIC850-2022, XIC600-2018, XIC600-2021, AB950-2024** - mail the
  self-addressed registration card, or register at `www.spiritfitness.com` under the Support tab.
- **AB900-2018** - the same card, or
  `https://www.spiritfitness.com/commercialwarrantyregistration.html`, a commercial-only address no
  other manual in this family gives.
- **JB950-2022** - mail the card to the PO Box, **or call 800-258-8511**. It is the only one of the
  eight that prints a telephone number for registration, and the only one that gives no web address.

**None of these eight is the commercial CR or CU bike answer.** Those put the serial number sticker
on the **front** stabilizer (`spirit-bike-2024-2025-safety-product-labels`,
`spirit-ce-safety-serial-number-location`). The AB950's is on the **rear** stabilizer, and the AB900
and JB950's is under the middle of the machine. Front and rear are the wrong end of the bike to send
a customer to.
