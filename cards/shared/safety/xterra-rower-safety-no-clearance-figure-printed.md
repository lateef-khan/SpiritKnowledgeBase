**These ten books give no clearance figure** - no distance to a wall, no space behind or beside the
rower, no footprint, no folded or stored dimension. The placement rules they print are `Do not
operate equipment on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet`, `Do not use outdoors`, and on four of them
one sentence without a number:

> Operate the machinery with ample free space around it to easily get on and off the machine.
> (item 2 on the ERG180, ERG550W, ERG600W, ERG650W -
> `xterra-rower-safety-instructions-list-free-space-and-tighten-bolts-with-11-fitness-bullets`)

How it was checked: every page of each book read, and the text searched for `clearance`, `free
space`, `free area`, `feet`, `foot`, `inch`, `cm` and `around`; the hits are the pedal-strap and
foot-size instructions, the ERG400 hardware sizes and the sentence above. The ERG400 scan was read
from its OCR supplements and the safety page render.

| Family | Books without a figure | Sibling that prints one |
|---|---|---|
| Magnetic and air rowers | ERG160, ERG180, ERG220, ERG400, ERG500, ERG700 | none |
| Water rowers | ERG550W, ERG600W, ERG650W, ERG750W | ERG800W - 23 in free area around an 85 by 21 in training area (`erg800w-2023-safety-free-area-23-inches-around-an-85-by-21-inch-training-area`) |

**Do not carry the ERG800W figure across.** It is a different frame with its own diagram. The Spirit
water rowers' 2 ft rule is a Spirit card (`spirit-water-rower-safety-two-feet-of-clearance`); the Xterra
bikes and ellipticals have the same gap on most books (`xterra-safety-no-clearance-figure-printed`).
