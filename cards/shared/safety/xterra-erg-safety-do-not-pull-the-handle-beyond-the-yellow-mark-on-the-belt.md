**The rule is on the warning label on the frame, not in the safety list:**

> DO NOT PULL THE HANDLE BEYOND THE YELLOW MARK ON THE BELT. DOING SO MAY CAUSE THE BELT TO COME OFF OF
> THE WHEEL.

It is item 7 of the label, under a small drawing of the belt with its mark
(`xterra-erg-safety-warning-sticker-with-the-yellow-belt-mark`). The belt is the mesh strap the handle
pulls (`Meshbelt`, part 37 on the ERG160; the ERG220 troubleshooting calls it the belt), and the
yellow mark is printed on it near the end of its travel: a stroke that pulls the mark out past the
frame is over-length, and the consequence the label names is the belt leaving the wheel - a service
job, not a user fix.

Neither book gives a stroke length, and neither says anything else about the handle beyond
`Grab the handle` and `Keep hands away from all moving parts`. The ERG220 service manual is not in
this wave; a belt that has come off is a maintenance question on the ERG160 service manual's belt
pages.

The Spirit water rowers print a footplate-and-strap rule for their own machines
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-feet-in-straps-and-footplate-locked`); no Xterra rower book prints a
snap-back warning for the handle.
