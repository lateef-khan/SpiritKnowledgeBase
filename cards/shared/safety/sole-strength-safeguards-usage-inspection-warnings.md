Failure to follow these safeguards may result in injury or serious health risk.

Proper usage: do not use any equipment in any way other than designed or intended; injuries may result from improper or excessive exercise (consult a physician; stop exercising for faintness, dizziness or pain); keep body parts, clothing and jewelry away from moving parts; follow the manual's foot position and basic techniques.

Inspection: do not use equipment that is damaged or has worn or broken parts; use only SOLE-supplied replacement parts; inspect at regular intervals (preventative maintenance limits liability); adjustments, maintenance and repair only by qualified persons; never use or repair a damaged or worn accessory; follow all maintenance instructions; replace defective parts immediately and do not operate until repairs are complete.

Operating warnings: keep children away (close supervision required where children are present); no loose clothing or jewelry, long hair secured back and up; all bystanders clear of users, moving parts, accessories and components while the machine is in operation.

**This Strength Equipment is not suitable for therapeutic purposes.**

Variants: the SRVO-2021 and SW121 books add `Managers should ensure that users are trained to follow these same instructions`. The SRVO Complete book adds a bench-specific line the others lack: `Provide an adequate safety perimeter between the bench, walls and other equipment and ensure proper clearance for usage and training`. The per-book user weight figure lives on its own cards, not here.
