**All seven manuals print the same eleven bullets, under one `WARNING` heading, in this order:**

> - Before beginning this or any exercise program, consult a physician. This is especially
>   important for persons over the age of 35 or persons with pre-existing health conditions.
> - Keep children away from the fitness bike. There are obvious pinch points and other caution
>   areas that can cause harm.
> - Keep hands away from all moving parts.
> - Never drop or insert any object into any openings.
> - Do not use outdoors.
> - Do not attempt to use your fitness bike for any purpose other than for the purpose it is
>   intended.
> - The hand pulse sensors are not medical devices. Various factors, including the user's
>   movement, may affect the accuracy of heart rate readings. The pulse sensors are intended only
>   as exercise aids in determining heart rate trends in general.
> - Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable for use on
>   your fitness bike. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue.
> - Maximum User Weight: 450 lbs
> - This appliance is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental
>   capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge, unless they have been given supervision or
>   instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible for their safety.
> - Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine.
>
> SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!

- **Every manual writes `fitness bike`**, never `upright` or `recumbent`, so the same list is
  printed word for word on a CR and on a CU of the same generation.
- **The list is not an electrical list.** The rain, moisture, temperature and humidity rules are on
  the facing `IMPORTANT ELECTRICAL INSTRUCTIONS` block, and the resistance-delay and console-key
  rules are on the `IMPORTANT OPERATION INSTRUCTIONS` page
  (`spirit-bike-safety-operation-instructions`).
- **Nothing here says the bike must be plugged in.** Five of the seven state the opposite -
  see `spirit-bike-safety-no-mains-outlet-needed`.

**The CU900ENT-2021 manual prints `metal capabilities` where the other six print `mental
capabilities`.** It is a typographic error in that one book; no figure or rule changes.

## Three bullets these manuals do not print

- **No flat-level-surface or carpet rule**, and no clearance figure -
  `spirit-ct800-safety-no-placement-or-clearance-requirement`.
- **No aerosol or oxygen rule**, and no damaged-cord rule. Those appear only in the ENT manuals
  that plug in (`spirit-bike-ent-safety-instructions-list`).
- **No numbering.** The 2025 manuals renumber the same content 1 to 11 and drop three of these
  bullets - `spirit-bike-2025-safety-instructions-list`.

The California Proposition 65 warning is printed by all seven, in a box on the operation
instructions page rather than in this list (`xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`).
