**Do not operate where aerosol spray products are being used or where oxygen is being administered.** Sparks from the motor may ignite a highly gaseous environment.

**Only five of the 14 upright and recumbent manuals print this rule**, and all five are LCB or LCR: 2011, 2013 and 2023. The LCB 2016, LCR 2016 and LCB/LCR 2019 manuals dropped it, and no B74, R72, B94 or R92 manual has ever carried it.

The rule says "sparks from the motor" although these manuals describe no motor on the bike. It is reproduced here exactly as printed.

The SB1200 2023 indoor cycle manual prints a shorter version of the same rule as item 21 of its safety list, without the sparks sentence; that one is on the SB1200 electrical safety card.
