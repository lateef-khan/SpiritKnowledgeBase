> The console operates on 4 AA batteries. The battery compartment is on the back side of the
> console. Please dispose of batteries properly after use.

- **Four AA cells**, and both manuals say they are supplied in the box.
- **The compartment is on the back of the console.**
- **Removing the batteries returns the console to factory settings** and zeroes the ODO value, in
  both books.

**The CRW900 book contradicts itself on the count and the CRW800H2O does not.** Read from the native
PDF text of both manuals, not from OCR:

| Manual | Parts list | Assembly step | Console setup |
|---|---|---|---|
| CRW900-2021 | item 15, quantity **4** | `Install supplied **2** AA batteries [15]` | `The console operates on **4** AA batteries` |
| CRW800H2O | `AA Batteries **4**` | `Place the **4** AA batteries into` | `The console operates on **4** AA batteries` |

**Four is the figure to give.** Two of the CRW900's three statements say four, its own parts list
ships four, and its sibling book says four in all three places. The `2` in the CRW900 assembly step
is the odd one out and the manual never reconciles it - say so rather than sending someone to buy
two cells.

**There is no electrical page, and that is a bigger absence than the battery fact.** Neither manual
prints:

| | Printed? |
|---|---|
| An outlet, voltage or amperage figure | **no** |
| A grounding page or a grounded-plug rule | **no** |
| An extension-cord rule | **no** |
| An operating temperature or humidity figure | **no** |
| A ten-percent supply-voltage paragraph | **no** |
| A circuit-breaker paragraph | **no** |

The strings `volt`, `amp`, `outlet`, `grounded`, `Fahrenheit`, `Celsius` and `humidity` appear
nowhere in either book's safety chapter. The one occurrence of *grounded power supply* in each is in
the warranty chapter's owner-responsibilities list, which is a warranty term rather than an
installation figure.

**The absence was checked twice.** Every page of both manuals that extracts under 25 native words
was re-rendered at 300 dpi and read with OCR; none is an electrical page. There is nothing to
resolve - these machines have no mains supply and their books say nothing about one.

**So the environment answer is: the manual does not state one.** Do not read the air rowers'
`40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit, 95% non-condensing` onto a water rower
(`ct900ent-operating-environment-voltage`). What these books do say about where the machine may
stand is `Do not use in rainy weather outdoors, or in an enclosed pool environment`
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-do-and-do-not-lists`) and nothing more.

**Every Spirit rower is powered differently.** Read the figure off the book for the machine in
front of you:

| Rower | Console power | Outlet figure |
|---|---|---|
| CRW900-2021, CRW800H2O | **4 AA batteries** | none printed |
| CRW800-2021, CRW800-2024 | **2 C batteries** (`crw800-2024-safety-console-runs-on-two-c-batteries`) | none printed |
| XRW600-2019, XRW600-2021 | **mains, with a power cord** | 110 volt, 15 amp (`spirit-xe-safety-outlet-110-volt-15-amp`) |

**And the 8.5UE ergometer is mains again**, at 100 to 240 volt AC on a 15-amp circuit
(`85ue-2025-safety-outlet-100-to-240-volt-50-60-hz-15-amp`). Never quote an AA cell for a CRW800 or
a C cell for a CRW900.

**The CRW900-2021 service manual prints no safety chapter and no electrical page either.** It is a 486-word book of
exploded views and part-replacement drawings; the only supply words in it are a Q&A entry for a console that does not
illuminate after battery installation, which says to check the batteries are installed the right way round or replace
them. No outlet, no voltage, no amperage and no temperature figure, as in the owner's manual.
