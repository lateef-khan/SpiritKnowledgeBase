The SC200-2016 book prints no Proposition 65 warning anywhere. Proved four ways: a precise word search (proposition, Toluene, Acrylamide, P65Warnings, expose-you, birth-defects) returns nothing while the same search returns two hits in the sibling SC200-2017 book; a spacing-tolerant WARNING search finds exactly three occurrences (read-all, outlet, electrical) against four in the books that carry the warning; the contents page was read in full and holds no warning box; and the book is native text, so nothing hides in an unscanned image.

The sibling SC200-2017 book (the SC200-2019 machine) does print the Toluene and Acrylamide notice - see the linked card. Do not quote Toluene or Acrylamide for an SC200-2016.
