**Three labels, and the two warning stickers share one face.** Both 2023 manuals print this page in
the same words.

| Label | Where the manual says it is |
|---|---|
| Serial number sticker | on the **front stabilizer** |
| Safety warning sticker | on the **left side of the upright** |
| Proposition 65 warning | on the **left side of the upright** |

**Serial number sticker**

> This sticker will be found on the front stabilizer. Please record the number below the barcode for
> the purpose of registering your Elliptical's warranty.

Record the number **below the barcode**. The label artwork shows a row of `x` characters in place of
a number and the telephone number **(870) 935-1107**; neither is your machine's serial number.

**Safety warning sticker**

> This sticker will be found on the left side of your Elliptical's upright. The same warning can be
> found in this manual. Please read and be aware of the precautions before operating your unit.

The manual says the sticker repeats what the manual already prints, so the sticker adds nothing to
the safety instructions list (`ces880-2025-safety-instructions-list`).

**Proposition 65 warning**

> This sticker will be found on the left side of your Elliptical's upright. The State of California
> requires us to inform you that this unit was manufactured using chemicals that could cause harm
> with improper use.

**That is all these two manuals say about Proposition 65.** They name **no chemical** and print
**no www.P65Warnings.ca.gov address**. The 2021 residential XE manuals print the other Proposition
65 text - naming Toluene and Acrylamide and giving the web address, in the manual rather than on a
sticker (`xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`). The two are not interchangeable in either
direction.

## Every Spirit machine puts these stickers somewhere different

| Machine | Serial number sticker | Proposition 65 sticker |
|---|---|---|
| **XE395-2023, XE795-2023** | **front stabilizer** | **left side of the upright** |
| CE900-2025 | front stabilizer | side of the console (`ce900-2025-safety-product-labels`) |
| CES880-2025 | rear stabilizer | rear stabilizer (`ces880-2025-safety-product-labels`) |
| 2023 XT treadmills | back of the motor cover (`xt-2023-safety-serial-number-sticker`) | interior side of the upright (`xt-2023-safety-proposition-65-sticker`) |

**Do not read one across to another.** `Left side` and `interior side` of an upright are not the
same face, and a treadmill has no front stabilizer.

**No other residential XE manual has a product labels page.** The nine earlier manuals print a
`Record Your Serial Number` heading and a blank line; six of them add an unlabelled illustration
captioned `Serial Number Location`, and none of the nine puts the location into words
(`spirit-ce-safety-serial-number-location`). Do not tell one of those owners the sticker is on the
front stabilizer - that is this pair's answer.
