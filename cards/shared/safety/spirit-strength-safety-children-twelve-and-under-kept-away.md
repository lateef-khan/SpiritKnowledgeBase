Every Spirit strength manual prints the same rule, as item 5 of the numbered safety instructions in
the thirty-six CSS, CSD, CSF and CSI books and as a bullet in the three ST800 books:

> Keep all children (12 and under) away. Teenagers (13 and over) and disabled must be supervised.

So there are three groups, not two: **under 13, keep away entirely; 13 and over, supervised; adults,
no stated restriction.** Disabled users are named alongside teenagers as needing supervision. The
manuals set no upper age and no weight-training experience requirement.

Two other pages say the same thing more loosely, and neither repeats the ages:

- The **precautions** page: *Do not let unsupervised children operate the Strength Equipment.*
- The **safeguards** page: *Keep children away from the equipment. Parents or others supervising
  children must provide close supervision of children if the equipment is used in the presence of
  children.*

**The two CSI machines reword the safeguards line** and are the only ones in the range that do:
*Keep young children away from the equipment. Children 13 years old and older should be supervised
and taught proper form and usage.* The added duty is teaching proper form, not just watching.

The eight-item warning decal on the machine carries the rule too, without an age:
children should not use the equipment without adult supervision, and bystanders should be kept at a
safe distance while the equipment is in use.

**The CSD-CPSP May 2025 update (version 5.0, revision 01/23/2025, file dated 2025 0521) and the CSS-DELT 2026 revision (file dated 20260821) print this page in the same words**, so the two newer books add nothing and change nothing here; they are cited so the page can be checked in either printing. The CSS-DELT 2026 text layer is a shifted font on its cover pages; its safety pages were read from the OCR supplements and the render.
