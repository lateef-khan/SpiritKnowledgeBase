**Printed contraindications, the same sentence on all four 2025 rehabilitation bikes:**

> 7.0R should not be used for patients with severe osteoporosis, non-union fractures, debilitating
> dizziness, or poor safety awareness/cognition. Do not use for patients weighing greater than 440
> lb (bariatric). Do not use for patients with acute conditions such as pulmonary embolus, thrombus,
> acute MI, acute fractures, or BP over 180/110 Hg.

| Ruled out | |
|---|---|
| Severe osteoporosis | |
| Non-union fractures | |
| Debilitating dizziness | |
| Poor safety awareness / cognition | |
| Weight over **440 lb** (bariatric) | `spirit-med-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lb` |
| Acute pulmonary embolus, thrombus, acute MI, acute fractures | named as examples of acute conditions |
| **BP over 180/110 Hg** | the manual's own unit, printed as `Hg` |

- **`180/110 Hg` is what the manual prints.** The unit is normally mmHg; quote the figures and the
  manual's wording rather than correcting it silently.
- **The list of examples is not closed.** `acute conditions such as` means the four named are
  examples, so a clinician's judgement governs anything not on the list.
- **It is the last bullet of the operation-instructions WARNING box**, after the medical-clearance
  and warm-up rules (`spirit-med-bike-safety-operation-instructions-warnings`), not on the safety
  list. A reader who stops at the safety list will miss it.

**The Dyaco MED 7.0R of 2021 prints the same sentence with the weight in kilograms** - `Do not use
for patients weighing greater than 200kgs (bariatric)` - and repeats it in Arabic, German, French
and Dutch.

**Who the machine is for is on the application page** - adults, children at a physical therapist's
discretion, ambulatory, with medical clearance
(`spirit-med-bike-safety-intended-patient-population-and-medical-purpose`).

**The self-powered 4.0R and 4.0U print no contraindications** - their safety list says the bike
`is not a medical device` and admits a supervised child of 8
(`spirit-med-bike-safety-children-aged-8-and-above-if-supervised`). Never answer a clinical question
about one family from the other's cards.

**The same sentence is printed for the 8.5UE ergometer, the rehabilitation steppers and the 7.0T
and 8.0T treadmills** (`85ue-2025-safety-who-may-not-use-the-ergometer`,
`spirit-rehab-stepper-safety-contraindications-osteoporosis-fractures-and-acute-conditions`,
`spirit-2026t-safety-rehabilitation-contraindications`) - other product lines with their own cards.
