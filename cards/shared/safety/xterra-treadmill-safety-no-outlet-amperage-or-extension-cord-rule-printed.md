**None of these twelve books prints an outlet amperage or an extension-cord gauge on its safety
pages.** The 2021-layout paragraph - `120-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet ... 14AWG or better` - is gone
from the TRX 2024 batch and from every new-layout book, and nothing replaces it.

What each book does print:

| Page | What it says about power |
|---|---|
| Safety instructions | item 12: `Connect this appliance to a properly grounded outlet only. See Grounding Instructions.` |
| Electrical safety | the AFCI/GFCI paragraph: `Optimally the unit should be the only device plugged into the circuit` (`xterra-treadmill-safety-avoid-afci-gfci-breakers-tested-brands`) |
| Grounding | `This product is for use on a nominal 120-volt circuit` (TR65, TR75, TR85, TR75H, TR95H, WS200, WS300) or `nominal 110-volt circuit` (TRX1400, TRX2500, TRX3500, TRX4500, TRX5500) - a voltage with no amperage |
| Boxed note beside the safety list (new-layout books) | `adhere to the user weight restrictions and power requirements of your new machine` - and the power requirement it refers to is never stated |

How it was checked: the whole text of each book searched for `amp`, `AWG`, `gauge` and `extension`,
and the safety, electrical and grounding pages read from the render.

**The one extension-cord sentence these books do carry is in troubleshooting, not safety.** The
TRX1400, TR65, TR75 and TR85 Service Checklist row *Treadmill will only achieve approximately 8 mph*
says `Do not use an extension cord. If an extension cord is required it should be as short as possible
and heavy duty 16 gauge minimum` (TRX1400) or `14 gauge minimum` (TR65, TR75, TR85), and asks for
`a minimum of 120 volt AC current`. That row is an errors card; it is a remedy for a slow belt, not
an installation requirement, and the two gauges disagree.

**The service manuals do print figures** - a `230-volt, 10-amp` outlet and a `16 AWG` cord
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-service-manual-outlet-and-extension-cord-figures`) - but those are Dyaco
service books, not what the owner was given.

The Spirit CT800-2024 grounding page has the same shape, a voltage and no amperage
(`spirit-2024-safety-grounding-page-says-110-volt`).
