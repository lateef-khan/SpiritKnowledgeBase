**Four cautions, printed after the electric-shock warnings, and the third is the one a technician needs: the button and the supply switch may not make the module dead.**

> 1. When replacing the system battery, please use the same type of battery for replacement.
> 2. Some batteries contain lithium, which may explode if used, handled or handled improperly. To prevent accidents, do not:
>    - Throwing or immersing the battery in water
>    - Heat the battery to over 100°C (212°F)
>    - Repair or disassemble the battery without authorization
> 3. The power control button on the device and the switch on the power supply may not completely cut off the current supplied to the device; at the same time, the device may also have multiple power cords. To completely power down the device, make sure all power cords are disconnected.
> 4. Never remove any component housing that has a hazard label attached.

- **To make it dead, pull every power cord.** Neither the power control button nor the switch on the power supply is enough, and the manual says the module may have more than one cord. The cable order for doing so is on the previous page (`spirit-strength-safety-i-strength-module-electric-shock-warnings-and-cable-order`).
- **Same type of battery**, and the manual names no type, no voltage and no part number for the system battery - only that some contain lithium. Do not fill the type in from another Spirit book.
- **100°C (212°F)** is the one figure on the page.
- **A hazard label on a housing means the housing stays closed.** The manual does not say which housings carry one; the disassembly chapter that follows is written around that rule.

`handled or handled improperly` is printed that way.

**Nothing on this page names a supply voltage.** The module's rated power appears on its specification page, which is a specification and not this caution; the owner's manuals put a nominal 120-volt sentence on their safeguards page against rating decals that say otherwise (`spirit-strength-safety-csi-electrical-and-grounding-block`).
