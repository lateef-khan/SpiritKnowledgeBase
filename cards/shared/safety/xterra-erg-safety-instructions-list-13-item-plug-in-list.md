**Two rower books print the Dyaco plug-in appliance page** that the older Xterra bikes and
ellipticals carry (`xterra-safety-instructions-list-13-item-plug-in-list`) - and only one of the two
rowers plugs in. The ERG400 (2015, a scan) runs from an external power supply
(`xterra-erg-safety-external-power-supply-and-power-jack`); the ERG220 (2023) has a two-AA-battery
console (`xterra-rower-safety-battery-console-no-power-adapter`) and its book says so in a sentence the
ERG400 lacks:

> Read all instructions before using this appliance. **Some warnings below pertain to plug-in models
> only. Please use similar precautions when operating any machinery with a power source.** (ERG220)

> **DANGER** - To reduce the risk of electric shock always unplug this appliance from the electrical
> outlet immediately after using and before cleaning.
> **WARNING** - To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons:

| # | Instruction |
|---|---|
| 1 | An appliance should never be left unattended when plugged in. Unplug from outlet when not in use, and before putting on or taking off parts. |
| 2 | Do not operate under blanket or pillow. Excessive heating can occur and cause fire, electric shock, or injury to persons. |
| 3 | ERG220: `This exercise equipment is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge.` / ERG400: `Close supervision is necessary when this appliance is used by, on, or near children, invalids, or disabled persons.` |
| 4 | Use this appliance only for its intended use as described in this manual. Do not use attachments not recommended by the manufacturer. |
| 5 | Never operate this appliance if it has a damaged cord or plug, if it is not working properly, if it has been dropped or damaged, or dropped into water. Return the appliance to a service center for examination and repair. |
| 6 | Do not carry this appliance by the power supply cord or use cord as a handle. |
| 7 | Keep the cord away from heated surfaces. |
| 8 | Never operate the appliance with the air openings blocked. Keep the air openings free of lint, hair, and the like. |
| 9 | Never drop or insert any object into any opening. |
| 10 | Do not use outdoors. |
| 11 | Do not operate where aerosol (spray) products are being use or where oxygen is being administered. |
| 12 | Connect this appliance to a properly grounded outlet only. |
| 13 | The appliance is intended for household use. |

| | ERG220 | ERG400 |
|---|---|---|
| Proposition 65 text between the list and the bullets | yes - Toluene and Acrylamide (`xterra-treadmill-safety-proposition-65-warning`) | **no** - the 2015 book predates it (`xterra-safety-no-proposition-65-warning-printed`) |
| Fitness Equipment bullets | 8 | 7 |
| Last bullet | `Weight Limit: 300 lbs` (`xterra-rower-safety-user-weight-limit-300-lbs`) | *wear proper shoes* - **no weight limit** (`xterra-erg-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`) |

The seven bullets both print: to disconnect, turn all controls to the off position, then remove the
plug from the outlet; no deeply padded, plush or shag carpet; consult a physician, especially over 35;
keep hands away from moving parts; the pulse sensors are not medical devices
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-pulse-sensors-are-not-medical-devices`); no other purpose; wear proper shoes
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-proper-shoes`). Both close with `Failure to follow all guidelines...`, `SAVE
THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!` and `CAUTION!! Please be careful when unpacking the carton.`

**Item 12 on the ERG220 asks for a grounded outlet a battery rower cannot use**; the book's own
*plug-in models only* sentence is the reading to give a customer. Neither book prints a rower-specific
item - the ERG220's seat, rail and belt warnings are on its stickers
(`xterra-erg-safety-warning-sticker-with-the-yellow-belt-mark`,
`xterra-erg-safety-locking-pin-must-be-fully-engaged-during-use-and-when-folded`). The Spirit XT-2015
treadmills print the same list for their own machines (`xt-2015-safety-instructions-list`).
