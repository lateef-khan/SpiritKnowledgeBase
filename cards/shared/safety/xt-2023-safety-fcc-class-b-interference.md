The equipment is tested to the limits for a **Class B digital device** under Part 15 of the
FCC Rules. Those limits are meant to give reasonable protection against harmful interference
in a residential installation, but the manual states plainly that **there is no guarantee
interference will not occur in a particular installation**.

The test the manual gives is to **turn the equipment off and on** and see whether the
interference follows it. If it does, it lists four measures to try:

1. Reorient or relocate the receiving antenna.
2. Increase the separation between the equipment and receiver.
3. Connect the equipment to an outlet on a circuit different from that to which the
   receiver is connected.
4. Consult the dealer or an experienced radio/TV technician for help.

The manual gives no other remedy, and no service action.

**The XT185 manual has no compliance page at all**, so it prints none of this.

**The XT685ENT owner's manual prints the same notice and the same four measures**, page 5.

**The XT485ENT manual has no compliance page at all**, so it prints none of this:
`xt-2015-safety-no-compliance-page`.

## The Spirit CE900-2025 elliptical prints the same notice with three measures, not four

Its WARNINGS, COMPLIANCE AND NOTICES page carries the Class B digital device notice word for word -
the residential-installation limits, no guarantee against interference in a particular installation,
and the same test of turning the equipment off and on. **Its list of measures has three items, not
four:**

1. Reorient or relocate the receiving antenna.
2. Increase the separation between the equipment and receiver.
3. Consult the dealer or an experienced radio/TV technician for help.

**The different-circuit measure is missing.** The XT manuals' third item - connect the equipment to
an outlet on a circuit different from the receiver's - is not printed for the CE900, which is
consistent with a machine that plugs into no outlet at all
(`spirit-ce-safety-generator-produces-ac-power`). Do not offer it to a CE900 owner as their
manual's advice.

**No other Spirit elliptical manual carries this notice**
(`xt-2015-safety-no-compliance-page`).
