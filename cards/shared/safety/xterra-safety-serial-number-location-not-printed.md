**These twenty-five books ask you to write the serial number down and never say where to read it.**

> Record Your Serial Number - Please record the Serial Number of this fitness product in the space
> provided below. Serial Number: ________

The only other mentions are the welcome letter (`Please have your model number and serial number
handy when you call`), the parts lists that head a column *Serial No.* (a part-number column, not the
machine's serial), and the warranty exclusion for products `with original serial numbers that have
been removed or altered`. There is no Product Labels page and no sticker drawing.

How it was checked: every `serial` in each book's text read, and the registration page of the
scanned books (MB500, SB4.5r, FS150, FS5.8e, FS5.9e) read from the render.

**Where it probably is**: the five bikes whose books do say put it on the frame - the front support
tube of a folding bike, the rear base frame of a recumbent, near the leveler or the adaptor port
(`xterra-bike-safety-serial-number-sticker-locations`). Offer those as places to look, not as what this
manual says.

The eleven older Xterra treadmill books have the same gap
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-serial-number-location-not-printed`).
