**160 cm (63") beyond the training area, on every side the machine is entered from.** Four machines
print this figure: the CSF-AABB abdominal crunch board, the CSF-FUNT functional trainer, and both
i-Strength machines, CSI-CPSP and CSI-LROW. It is **more than two and a half times** the 600 mm the
CSS and CSD machines ask for.

The inch conversion is rounded differently in each book without the metric figure changing:

| Machine | Printed as |
|---|---|
| CSF-AABB | `not less than 160cm (62.99")` |
| CSF-FUNT | `not less than 63" (160cm)` |
| CSI-CPSP | `not less than 160cm / 63''` |
| CSI-LROW | `not less than 160cm / 63''` |

The live area must also include the area for emergency dismount.

**How to work the figure out for a given machine.** The same page carries a plan view of the machine
with its training area drawn as a rectangle and dimensioned in millimetres, with an inch conversion
beside it. Add the perimeter to each accessed side of that rectangle. On the CSS-ABDO, for example,
the training area is 900 x 1210 mm (35.4" x 47.6") and the drawing marks 600 mm on all four sides, so
the live area is 2100 x 2410 mm.

Two things the manuals do **not** say: they set no separate wall clearance, and they set no
minimum gap between two machines other than this perimeter. The safety guidelines bullet above the
diagram is the only other statement — *provide an adequate safety perimeter between the machine,
walls and other equipment to ensure that the facility has the proper clearance for usage and
training* — and it puts no number on "adequate".


**Do not take a footprint off the CSI drawings.** On CSI-CPSP the plan view is dimensioned
`1439` and `2037` in millimetres and labelled `47'3"` and `66'10"` in feet and inches, and those do
not convert into each other — 1439 mm is 56.7", 2037 mm is 80.2". CSI-LROW's drawing is labelled
`49' 8"` and `39' 8"`, and no millimetre dimensions come off that page cleanly at all. Take the machine's dimensions from the
specification table instead. This page was read from a 250 dpi render as well as the text layer.

On CSF-FUNT and both CSI manuals the PDF also carries a second, unprinted copy of this paragraph
that ends *the area for emergency escape* rather than *emergency dismount*. It is not on the page —
it is a hidden layer left in the file — and the printed wording is the one quoted above.
