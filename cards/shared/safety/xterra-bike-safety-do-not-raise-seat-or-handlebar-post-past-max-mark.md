**Five bike books print the same item:**

> Follow instructions for safe use of the equipment including proper seat position, handlebar
> position, and use of foot positioning system of pedals. Do not attempt to pull up handlebar post
> and seat post over the 'MAX.' graduation.

The MAX. graduation is a line on the post; the post is not to be raised past it. The fitting
procedure the item refers to - seat at hip height, a five-degree knee bend with the pedal at the
bottom, the seat knob or quick release tightened after every change - is on the *Adjusting The Bike
For A Proper Fit* page of each book, which is an assembly matter; this card holds the safety limit.

The SB120 and UB120 print the first sentence only and drop the MAX clause; the UB120 has a seat post
and a club knob but its book gives no height limit
(`xterra-bike-safety-instructions-list-recumbent-and-upright-10-items`).

The Sole SB700 and SB900 indoor cycles print the same item for their own machines
(`sole-indoor-cycle-riding-and-setup-rules`).
