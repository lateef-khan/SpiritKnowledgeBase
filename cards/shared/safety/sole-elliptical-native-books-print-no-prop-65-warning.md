None of the 13 native B books prints any Proposition 65 warning - the words California, Toluene and Acrylamide appear nowhere. The warning first appears in the 2015-2016 stencil books, on the contents page, and every stencil book carries it - see the linked card. Do not quote Toluene or Acrylamide for a 2007-2015 machine.
