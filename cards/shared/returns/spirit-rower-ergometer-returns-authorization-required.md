**No unit goes back to the factory without a return authorization number first.** All six manuals - the five rowers and the 8.5UE - print the identical rule:

> The owner is responsible for adequate packaging upon return to Spirit Fitness. Spirit Fitness is not responsible for damages in shipping. Make all freight damage claims with the appropriate freight carrier. **DO NOT SHIP ANY UNIT TO OUR FACTORY WITHOUT A RETURN AUTHORIZATION NUMBER. All units arriving without a return authorization number will be refused.**

**There is no split across the range.** The February 2019 XRW600 and the December 2025 ergometer word it the same; the two water rowers print it as an unnumbered bullet rather than as step 5, and the words do not change.

**None of the six states a refund window, a restocking fee, or any right to return an unwanted machine.** This is a return for service, not a return for money. A customer asking to send one back for a refund is asking a question these manuals do not answer - the dealer's own terms do.

**A refused shipment is the customer's problem.** The manuals say the unit will be refused. None says who pays the return leg of a refused delivery, and none names a fee. Do not offer one.

**Three duties come with it, all the owner's:**

- **Adequate packaging** - no specification, no material, no requirement to keep the original carton.
- **Freight damage is the carrier's**, not Spirit's. The claim goes to the carrier.
- **Repairs must be preauthorized** before the machine moves at all, from step 4 of the same list: `spirit-rower-ergometer-warranty-service-steps`.

**The number is not printed in any of the six manuals.** Get it from the selling dealer, from the local authorized service organization, or from the service department on **(870) 935-1107**.

**Freight to and from a service center is the customer's**, and so is shipping and handling on parts sent under warranty. Five of the six add a trip charge for in-home service and a further one past 100 miles; the 8.5UE does not: `spirit-rower-ergometer-warranty-owner-costs`.

**Do not answer this from the Sole card or the climber card.** `sole-bike-return-authorization-required` is a different brand, and `spirit-climber-returns-authorization-required` is a different product line - nothing filters either back out at retrieval time.
