**Nothing goes back without a number first.** All thirty-nine strength manuals print the
identical rule as step 5 of the SERVICE procedure:

> The owner is responsible for adequate packaging upon return to Spirit Fitness. Spirit
> Fitness is not responsible for damages in shipping. Make all freight damage claims with
> the appropriate freight carrier. **DO NOT SHIP ANY UNIT TO OUR FACTORY WITHOUT A RETURN
> AUTHORIZATION NUMBER. All units arriving without a return authorization number will be
> refused.**

Three obligations sit on the owner in that one step: adequate packaging, a freight claim
made with the carrier rather than with Spirit, and the authorization number obtained in
advance.

**No strength manual states a returns policy of any other kind.** None of the thirty-nine
prints a refund window, a trial period, a restocking fee, or an instruction to call before
taking a machine back to a retailer. This is not a gap in the extraction - the phrases
were searched for across every book and the warranty pages of all thirty-nine were read
from the PDF, including a 300 dpi render of the CSS-SROW pages, which are flat images. The
return authorization rule is the whole of what these manuals say about sending anything
back.

The number is obtained through the service route, which starts with the dealer:
`spirit-strength-warranty-service-steps`.
