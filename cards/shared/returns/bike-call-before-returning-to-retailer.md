The inside front cover and the back cover both carry:

> Before returning your Sole product to any retailer, or if you require any assistance with assembly or technical support please call us first for assistance at **<number>**. Thank you for your Sole purchase.

**The number depends on the machine.** Read the row for the machine in hand.

| Number | Machines |
|---|---|
| **866-697-6531** | b94-2016, b94-2019, lcb-2013, lcb-2016, lcb-2019, lcr-2016, lcr-2019, r92-2016, r92-2019, sb700-2011, sb700-2016, sb700-2019, sb700-2020, sb700-2021, sb900-2014, sb900-2016, sb900-2019, sb900-2020, sb900-2021, sb900-2022 |
| **866-780-7653** | b94-2023, lcb-2023, lcr-2023, r92-2023, sb1200-2023, sb900-2023 |

The notice is an instruction to call before a retailer return, not a statement of a return period. **The owner's manuals set out no return window, no restocking rule and no refund policy** — none of them says how long you have to return a machine or on what terms.

Manuals that do not print the notice at all: the B74/R72 2015, B94/R92 2011 and 2013, LCB 2011, and LCR 2011 and 2013. For b74-2015, r72-2015, b94-2011, b94-2013, r92-2011, r92-2013, lcb-2011, lcr-2011 and lcr-2013 use the service contacts in the warranty section instead.

Shipping a unit to the SOLE factory is a separate rule and needs a return authorization number first.
