**No unit goes back to the factory without a return authorization number first.** All thirteen Spirit climber, stepper and stair climber manuals print the same rule as step 5 of SERVICE:

> The owner is responsible for adequate packaging upon return to Spirit Fitness. Spirit Fitness is not responsible for damages in shipping. Make all freight damage claims with the appropriate freight carrier. **DO NOT SHIP ANY UNIT TO OUR FACTORY WITHOUT A RETURN AUTHORIZATION NUMBER. All units arriving without a return authorization number will be refused.**

**There is no exception and no split across the range** - the 2018 XS895 and the 2026 8.5S-Fit state it in the same words, and the MS300 states it as an unnumbered bullet in lower case. Thirteen manuals, one rule.

**A refused shipment is the customer's problem.** The manuals say the unit will be refused; none of them says who pays for the return leg of a refused delivery, and none names a fee. Do not offer one.

**Three separate duties come with it, and they are all the owner's:**

- **Adequate packaging.** The manuals give no specification, no material and no requirement to keep the original carton.
- **Damage in shipping is not Spirit Fitness's.** Any freight damage claim goes to the carrier, not to Spirit.
- **Repairs must be preauthorized** before the machine moves at all - step 4 of the same list.

**The number is not printed in the manual.** Get it from the selling dealer, from the local authorized service organization, or from the service department - **(870) 935-1107** on twelve of the thirteen, written **1-870-935-1107** on the MS300.

**This is a return for service, not a return for refund.** None of the thirteen manuals states a refund window, a restocking fee or a right to return an unwanted machine. If a customer is asking to send one back for money rather than for repair, the manual does not answer them - the dealer's own terms do.

**Freight to and from a service center is the customer's**, and so is shipping and handling on replacement parts sent while the machine is under warranty. Five of the thirteen - the two XS895 books, the CRS800S, the CS800 and the CVC800 - add a trip charge for in-home service and a further one beyond 100 miles: `xt-2023-warranty-owner-costs`. The other eight stop at shipping and handling, and the CSC900 books stop one sentence earlier still, at "All repairs must be preauthorized": `spirit-2026t-warranty-owner-costs`.

**Do not answer this from the Sole card.** `sole-bike-return-authorization-required` states the same rule for Sole bikes; it is a different brand and nothing filters a brand out at retrieval time.
