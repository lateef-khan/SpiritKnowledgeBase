**These charts differ from every other Spirit bike's in one cell.** The female chart's fourth stage
reads **175, 150, 125 and 100 watts - 1050, 900, 750 and 600 kgm/min**. The 4.0 and 7.0 medical bikes
and the commercial CR/CU bikes print **117 watts and 700 kgm/min** in that third column
(`spirit-bike-programs-fitness-test-workload-charts`). Everything else matches.

**Workload chart for males or very fit females**

| Stage | Workload |
|---|---|
| 1st stage | 50 watts, 300 kgm/min |
| HR after stage 1 | < 90 &#124; 90-105 &#124; > 105 |
| 2nd stage | 150 watts, 900 gm/min &#124; 125 watts, 750 gm/min &#124; 100 watts, 600 kgm/min *(the first two print "gm/min")* |
| HR after stage 2 | <120 &#124; 120-135 &#124; >135, under each branch |
| 3rd stage | 225 W 1350 &#124; 200 W 1200 &#124; 175 W 1050 &#124;&#124; 200 W 1200 &#124; 175 W 1050 &#124; 150 W 900 &#124;&#124; 175 W 1050 &#124; 150 W 900 &#124; 125 W 750 kgm/min |

**Workload chart for females or de-conditioned male**

| Stage | HR <80 | 80-90 | 90-100 | >100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st stage | 25 watts, 150 kgm/min | | | |
| 2nd stage | 125 W, 750 | 100 W, 600 | 75 W, 450 | 50 W, 300 |
| 3rd stage | 150 W, 900 | 125 W, 750 | 100 W, 600 | 75 W, 450 |
| 4th stage (if needed) | 175 W, 1050 | 150 W, 900 | **125 W, 750** | 100 W, 600 |

The three 3rd-stage groups of the male chart follow the three 2nd-stage branches in printed order,
as on the other bikes. The protocol text on the same page - a sub-maximal test of two to four
3-minute stages that ends at 85% of maximum - is the one every Spirit bike prints
(`spirit-bike-programs-fitness-test-ymca-protocol`).

The 8.0U and 8.5R 2025 books print the same charts; the 8.5R page is a picture too.
