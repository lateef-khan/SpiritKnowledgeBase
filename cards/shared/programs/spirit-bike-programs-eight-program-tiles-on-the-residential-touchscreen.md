The Workout Programs screen on the XBR55ENT and XBU55ENT 2021 shows **eight tiles**:

**Manual, Hill, Fatburn, Cardio, Strength, HIIT, Heart Rate and Custom.**

The section holds **two categories** - **Programs**, the ones with predefined level-changing profiles,
and **Templates**, ordinary programs saved with your own adjustments. Tap a category name to switch.
Tapping a program preview opens its description, and you can scroll sideways through the descriptions
of the whole category. Press **CHOOSE** to select and adjust one.

**Any program can be labelled a FAVORITE** and favourites then appear on the Homescreen.

**This is the only Spirit bike lineup that carries Strength and HIIT together.** The commercial 800
touchscreens list ten programs with HIIT and no Strength
(`spirit-bike-programs-ten-program-menu-on-the-touchscreen-800`); the 900 touchscreens list ten with
Strength and no HIIT (`spirit-bike-programs-ten-preset-programs-on-the-touchscreen-900`). Neither
count is eight, so do not answer an XBR55ENT or XBU55ENT owner from either.

**The preset page calls the machine an elliptical.** In both manuals it reads "The **elliptical** has
five different programs that have been designed for a variety of workouts. These five programs have
factory preset work level profiles". It is a bike, and the count of five excludes Manual, Heart Rate
and Custom, leaving Hill, Fatburn, Cardio, Strength and HIIT. The wording is carried over from a
Spirit elliptical manual.

**These two manuals are the same book on this chapter.** Measured on native PDF text over printed
pages 28 to 40, XBR55ENT against XBU55ENT is **100.0%** at word level - not one word differs. They
part company one page later, on the Erratic Operation list.
