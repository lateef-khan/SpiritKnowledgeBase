Heart rate is read from the **pulse grips** or a **wireless chest strap**.

1. Select the **HR** program - **HRC** on the CU800 2012 - and press **Enter**.
2. Enter your **Age**, then Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight**, then Enter.
4. Enter the **Time**, then Enter.
5. Enter your **target Heart Rate** - the heart rate you will try to maintain - then Enter.
6. Press **Start**. Stop steps back one screen during data entry.
7. Up or Down **during** the program changes your target heart rate, not the resistance directly.
8. The program **automatically increases or decreases the resistance** depending on whether your
   heart rate is above or below your target.
9. The Display or Scan button beside the data window scrolls through the readouts.

**The CR800 2021, CR800 2023 and CU800 2021 read heart rate from the chest strap only** at this
step: their sentence is "Heart rate information is read wireless chest strap", with no mention of
the pulse grips. Those same manuals do say elsewhere that Grip Pulse may be used while in Heart Rate
Control, so the omission is a wording slip on this page rather than a missing feature. Those three
also make you pick between two named HR programs first - see
`spirit-bike-programs-heart-rate-keys-of-sixty-five-and-eighty-percent`.

**The CU800 2012 adds a step 10**: at the end of the HRC program you may save the workout you just
completed as a custom user program by pressing a User key.

**On a touchscreen bike the HR program is set differently** - see
`spirit-bike-programs-heart-rate-defaults-to-sixty-five-percent-and-holds-within-five-beats`.

**Eleven residential XBR and XBU manuals print the same procedure**, and add a figure the commercial
books leave out: after you set your target, **the program tries to keep you at or within 3 to 5 beats
per minute of it**. Their order is Age, Weight, Time, Heart Rate Target, Start, and Up or Down during
the program moves the **target**, not the resistance.

**All eleven require a chest strap for this program.** Three of them - XBR25 2019, XBR55 2019 and
XBR55 2021 - add "(sold separately)" to the note; the other eight print it bare as "you must wear the
heart rate transmitter strap". **Seven also say the grip pulse cannot be used while a Heart Rate
program is running**: XBR55 2019/2021/2023, XBU55 2019/2021/2023 and XBR95 2023. The XBR95
2016/2018/2021 and the XBR25 2019 do not print that sentence.

**They differ in how many heart rate keys there are, and that changes the answer.** The XBR95
2016/2018/2021, XBR55 2019/2021 and XBU55 2019/2021 have two, fixed at 60% and 80%
(`spirit-bike-programs-two-heart-rate-keys-fixed-at-sixty-and-eighty-percent`). The XBR55, XBU55 and
XBR95 2023 books have four, at 55%, 65%, 75% and 85%
(`spirit-bike-programs-four-heart-rate-keys-at-fifty-five-sixty-five-seventy-five-and-eighty-five`).
The XBR25 2019 has one, defaulting to 60%
(`spirit-bike-programs-a-single-heart-rate-key-defaulting-to-sixty-percent`). Read the target
percentage off the right card before quoting one.

**The XBR25 2023 has no heart rate program at all**, although its console still reads a pulse - see
`spirit-bike-programs-a-bike-manual-with-a-pulse-readout-but-no-heart-rate-program`.

**Two contradictions worth knowing.** The XBR25 2019 has a single HR key but its step 1 still reads
"Press the HR 1 or HR 2 key", carried over from a two-key console. And the two XBU55 LED books contradict
themselves on the back key: their Manual page says **Stop** goes back one screen, their heart rate
page says **Enter** does. Every other residential book says Stop in both places.
