Heart rate is read from the **pulse grips** or a **wireless chest strap**.

1. Select the **HR** program - **HRC** on the CU800 2012 - and press **Enter**.
2. Enter your **Age**, then Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight**, then Enter.
4. Enter the **Time**, then Enter.
5. Enter your **target Heart Rate** - the heart rate you will try to maintain - then Enter.
6. Press **Start**. Stop steps back one screen during data entry.
7. Up or Down **during** the program changes your target heart rate, not the resistance directly.
8. The program **automatically increases or decreases the resistance** depending on whether your
   heart rate is above or below your target.
9. The Display or Scan button beside the data window scrolls through the readouts.

**The CR800 2021, CR800 2023 and CU800 2021 read heart rate from the chest strap only** at this
step: their sentence is "Heart rate information is read wireless chest strap", with no mention of
the pulse grips. Those same manuals do say elsewhere that Grip Pulse may be used while in Heart Rate
Control, so the omission is a wording slip on this page rather than a missing feature. Those three
also make you pick between two named HR programs first - see
`spirit-bike-programs-heart-rate-keys-of-sixty-five-and-eighty-percent`.

**The CU800 2012 adds a step 10**: at the end of the HRC program you may save the workout you just
completed as a custom user program by pressing a User key.

**On a touchscreen bike the HR program is set differently** - see
`spirit-bike-programs-heart-rate-defaults-to-sixty-five-percent-and-holds-within-five-beats`.
