The stage workloads are printed in kilogram-force metre per minute (kgm/min). **The complete pair of
charts survives only in the CU800ENT 2024 manual**; every other manual prints them as a flat image
or an OCR fragment.

**Workload chart for male or very fit female**

| Stage | Workload |
|---|---|
| 1st stage | 300 kgm/min |
| Heart rate after stage 1 | HR < 90 &#124; 90 - 105 &#124; > 105 |
| 2nd stage | 900 &#124; 750 &#124; 600 kgm/min |
| Heart rate after stage 2 | HR <120 &#124; 120-135 &#124; >135, for each of the three branches |
| 3rd stage | 1350 &#124; 1200 &#124; 1050 - 1200 &#124; 1050 &#124; 900 - 1050 &#124; 900 &#124; 750 kgm/min |

**Workload chart for female or de-conditioned male**

| Stage | Workload |
|---|---|
| 1st stage | 150 kgm/min |
| Heart rate after stage 1 | HR <80 &#124; 80-90 &#124; 90-100 &#124; >100 |
| 2nd stage | 750 &#124; 600 &#124; 450 &#124; 300 kgm/min |
| 3rd stage | 900 &#124; 750 &#124; 600 &#124; 450 kgm/min |
| 4th stage | 1050 &#124; 900 &#124; 700 &#124; 600 kgm/min |

The 3rd-stage row of the male chart is nine values in three groups of three, one group per 2nd-stage
branch, in the order printed above. The manual does not label which heart rate band leads to which
group beyond that ordering.

**What the other manuals give.** The CU800 2012 prints the female chart in full in both scans - 150;
750/600/450/300; 900/750/600/450; 1050/900/700/600, value for value the same as above - and loses
the male chart to OCR. The CU800ENT 2022 recovers the male 1st stage (300) and only three
3rd-stage values (1050 / 900 / 750). The CR800ENT 2023's chart page OCRs into unusable text
(`900 kgmimin`, `kgrvmin`). The CR800 2021, CR800 2023, CU800 2021, CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 print
the two chart headings with the charts themselves as flat pictures, so no values are recoverable.

**The CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900ENT and CU900ENT print no workload chart at all.**
