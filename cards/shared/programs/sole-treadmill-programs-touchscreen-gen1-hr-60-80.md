This variant offers **60% or 80%** of the age-calculated maximum (the F85 books offer 60%/85%: see `sole-treadmill-programs-touchscreen-gen1-hr-60-85`).

Set a target time, then **adjust speed manually while running to hold the target heart rate; the treadmill controls the incline automatically** (TT8 book: "Incline will be controlled by the treadmill automatically"). An HR monitoring device is required. The program ends when the time is up, **or if your HR runs 20% higher than target**.

Contradiction ruled: the TT8-2021 target-chart page says training at "60% or 85%" while its program paragraph says 60%/80%. The card follows the program paragraph (what the customer sets), since the setup screen governs the workout; the ST90-2021 book prints 60%/80% on both pages.
