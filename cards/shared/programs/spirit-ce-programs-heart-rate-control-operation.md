1. Choose the **HR** program, then press **Enter**.
2. Enter your **Age**.
3. Enter your **Weight**.
4. Set the **Time**.
5. Set your **target Heart Rate** - the heart rate you will try to maintain during the program.
6. Press **Start**. Enter goes back to modify settings; **Stop** goes back one level, or screen.
7. Level Up / Level Down during the program **changes your target heart rate**, not the resistance
   directly.
8. The program automatically increases or decreases the amount of resistance, depending on whether
   your heart rate is above or below your target.

**Five consoles offer two heart rate programs, not one.** On the CE800 2021, CE800 2023, CE850 2020,
CE850 2022 and CES880 2025 step 1 reads "choose the HR program (**65% or 80%**)". The CE800 2013,
CE850 2016, CE900 2021 and CE900 2025 have a single **HR** key and no percentage choice.

**Two extra steps on the older consoles.** The CE800 2013, CE850 2016, CE900 2021 and CE900 2025 add
that during the HR program the adjacent Display or Scan key scrolls the Message Window; the CE800
2013 and CE850 2016 add a final step that when the program ends you may save the workout you just
completed as a custom user program by pressing a User key.

The CE900 2025 opens the page with one sentence the others do not print: heart rate information is
read via pulse grips or wireless chest strap.

**The 65% / 80% program names clash with the same manuals' training-zone page**, which recommends
60% for burning fat and 85% for the cardiovascular system. Neither figure is reconciled in the
source - see `spirit-ce-programs-target-zone-of-108-to-153-beats`.
