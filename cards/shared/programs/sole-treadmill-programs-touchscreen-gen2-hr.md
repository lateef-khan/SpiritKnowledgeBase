The **Heart Rate program uses incline to control your heart rate**: the incline gradually rises until you reach the target, then adjusts automatically to keep you **within 5 bpm** of it. A heart rate monitor is required.

On the setup page, **either set the target bpm directly or select 60% or 80% of age-predicted maximum** and let the machine calculate it — for the percentage option the machine must know your age, otherwise it uses defaults. The program ends when the time is up, **or if your heart rate runs 20% higher than the target**.
