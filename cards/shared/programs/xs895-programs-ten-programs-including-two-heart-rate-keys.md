**Ten programs, each with its own key**, and the book counts them both ways.

- **Manual**
- **Five presets** - Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength and Interval. *"The Incline Stepper has five
  different programs that have been designed for a variety of workouts."*
- **User 1 and User 2** - two custom programs, stored as U1 and U2.
- **HR 1 and HR 2** - two heart rate programs, fixed at **60%** and **80%** of maximum heart rate.

**The manual gives two different totals.** Printed page 19 says the heart rate bar graph works from
the age *"you entered during the programming phase of any of the **10** programs"*; printed page 27
says the heart rate monitor may be used *"during manual mode or during any of the **nine** different
programs"*. Ten is the count that matches the key list; nine is what you get if manual mode is
counted separately, as that sentence does. Both are recorded as printed.

Program keys **preview** a profile: pressing a key from the start-up display draws that program's
profile so you can look at it; pressing **Enter** afterwards actually selects the program and enters
data-setting mode.

**The 2018 and 2021 printings are the same book here.** Compared as 8-word phrase shingles over the
programs chapter they share **91.6%**, and the only substantive difference is that the 2021 printing
drops the sentence *"These five programs have factory preset work level profiles for achieving
different goals."* from the Preset Programs heading and the closing calorie note from the
Programming The Console page. Every procedure, figure and percentage is identical.

**This is not the Spirit stepper program set.** The CS800 and CRS800S reach their programs through a
single Program button and have a HIIT program instead of two User keys; see
`spirit-stepper-programs-five-programs-claimed-and-which-are-described`.
