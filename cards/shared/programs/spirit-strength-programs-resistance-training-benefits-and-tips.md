This page is the **only** training advice Spirit prints anywhere in the strength range.
It appears in the ST800 bench, dumbbell rack and functional trainer books and in no
other Spirit strength manual.

The preamble: always consult a physician before starting an exercise program; determine
a few realistic short-term goals and expectations first, and choose an exercise routine
that suits your individual needs.

Then six rules, as printed:

1. **Warm up properly before engaging in resistance training.** Stretching, yoga,
   jogging, calisthenics or other cardiovascular exercise can help prepare your body for
   the heavier workload of lifting weights.
2. **Learn how to perform the exercise correctly before using heavy weight.** Correct
   form is important to avoid injury and to ensure that you work the proper muscle
   groups.
3. **Know your limitations.** If you are new to resistance training, or starting back
   after an extended layoff, start slowly and build foundational strength over a longer
   period of time.
4. **Pay attention to your breathing.** Inhale on the non-exertion part of the movement
   and exhale during the exertion portion. Never hold your breath.
5. **The recovery phase matters.** Allow a minimum of 48-72 hours before training the
   same muscles. If soreness persists beyond that, take a few additional days.
6. **Consult a fitness professional** to formulate a complete exercise program, to
   achieve maximum results and avoid possible injury.

The three books differ only in one noun - *your Spirit Fitness gym* on the functional
trainer, *bench* on the flat/incline bench, *rack* on the dumbbell rack. Everything
else is word for word identical.

**No rep count, set count or programme is given here or anywhere else.** The manual
sets no target number of repetitions, no set count and no rest interval; rule 6 hands
that job to a trainer.
