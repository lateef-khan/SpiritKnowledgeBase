Each of these manuals says the elliptical trainer has **five different programs** designed for a
variety of workouts, and that those five carry factory preset work level profiles.

**Only three of the six print all five descriptions.** The CE800 2013, and the CE900 2021, print
Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, **Strength** and Interval. The CE800 2021, CE800 2023, CE900 2025 and
CES880 2025 print only Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio and Interval; on those four the Strength profile
appears only as a caption on the strip of profile pictures, with no description beside it.

The CE800 2013 adds one sentence the others do not: the initial built-in level of difficulty for
each program is set to a relatively easy level, and you may adjust the level of difficulty (Max
Level) for each program before beginning.

These five are the *preset* programs. They are not the whole program list - the same consoles also
carry Manual, and, depending on the machine, HIIT, Constant Power, a Fitness Test, a Custom program
and one or two Heart Rate programs.

**The CE850 manuals say seven, not five** - see
`spirit-ce850-programs-seven-preset-work-level-profiles`.

**The 2024 CE800 and CE850 owner's manuals both say five** and both describe only four - Hill, Fat
Burn, Cardio and Interval - with Strength appearing only as a caption on the strip of profile
pictures. **The CE850 dropped its seven-program claim in 2024**: the 2016, 2020 and 2022 CE850
manuals open the chapter with seven (see
`spirit-ce850-programs-seven-preset-work-level-profiles`), and the 2024 CE850 prints the CE800
sentence instead.
