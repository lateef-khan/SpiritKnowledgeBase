1. Press the desired **program key**. Press **Enter** to select the program. The display prompts
   you through the programming, or just press **Start** to begin with default values.
2. If Enter was pressed, the display blinks your **Age (default 35)**. Entering your correct
   age affects the heart-rate bar graph display and the heart-rate programs. Adjust with the
   up/down keys, then press Enter. (You may press Start at any time during programming.)
3. The display blinks your **Body Weight (default 150 lbs)**. Entering the correct body weight
   affects the calorie count. Adjust, then press Enter.
4. The display blinks **Time (default 30 minutes)**. Adjust or accept, then press Enter.
5. The display blinks the **preset top speed of the selected program (3 mph or 5 kph)**. Adjust
   with the Speed keys, then press Enter. Each program has various speed changes throughout;
   this lets you **limit the highest speed the program can reach**.
6. The display blinks the **preset top incline of the selected program (3.0%)**. Adjust with
   the Incline keys, then press Enter.

You are now done and may press **Start** to begin, or **Enter** to go back one level and
change data entered.

**Calorie note, as printed:** no exercise machine can give an exact calorie count because too
many factors determine calorie burn for a particular person; even someone of the same body
weight, age and height may burn very differently. The Calorie display is a reference only, to
monitor improvement from workout to workout.

User programs use the same sequence with different defaults (top speed 0.5 mph, top incline
0%); they have their own card.
