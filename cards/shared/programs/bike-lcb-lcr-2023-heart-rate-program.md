The Heart Rate program **uses resistance to control your heart rate**. The resistance level gradually
increases until you reach your target heart rate, then adjusts automatically to keep you **within
5 bpm** of your goal.

**A heart rate monitor is required for this program.**

On the program setup page you can either:

- set the **target bpm** directly, or
- select **60% or 80% of your age-predicted maximum heart rate** and let the machine calculate the
  target for you.

If you pick the percentage option, **make sure the machine knows how old you are** — otherwise the
calculation uses the default values.

**The program finishes when the time is up, or if your heart rate is 20% higher than the target.**

This is a single program with a 60%/80% choice. There is no HR1 and no HR2 key on this console; the
LCB and LCR machines up to 2019 have two separate heart rate programs.
