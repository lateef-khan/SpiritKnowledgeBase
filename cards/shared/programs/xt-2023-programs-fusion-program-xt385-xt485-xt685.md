**Resistance:** Fusion takes you through high levels of cardio and strength intensity followed by recovery periods of low intensity. It uses and develops your "Fast Twitch" muscle fibres, which are used for tasks that are intense and short in duration. These deplete your oxygen level and spike your heart rate, followed by periods of recovery and heart rate drop to replenish oxygen, so your cardiovascular system is programmed to use oxygen more efficiently.

**Incline:** This program spikes similar to the speed profile, but in different segments (columns), so all of your lower extremity muscles are equally challenged. **The incline alternates between 25 & 65 % of maximum elevation.**

Setting it up:

1. Press the **PROGRAM** key to select the FUSION program. The display prompts you through the programming.
2. The Message Center blinks your **Age (default is 35)**. Use the Speed + or - keys to adjust, then press Enter. Your age determines your recommended maximum heart rate, which the HR Graph Display and the Heart Rate features are based on.
3. The Message Center blinks your **Body Weight (default is 150 lb / 70 kg)**. The correct body weight affects the calorie count. Use the + or - keys to adjust, then press Enter.
4. The Message Center blinks the **preset top speed** of the program (**3 mph or 5 kmph**). Use the speed + or - keys to adjust, then press Enter. Each program has various speed changes throughout, so this limits the highest speed the program can reach.
5. The Message Center blinks the **preset top incline** of the program (**1.0%**). Use the speed + or - keys to adjust, then press Enter.
6. The Message Center blinks the **number of intervals** desired (**default is 10; you may select 10, 20 or 30**). Use the speed + or - keys to adjust, then press Enter.
7. The Message Center blinks the desired **Interval time (default is 1:00)**. The time you select is the duration of **both** the cardio and strength intervals.
8. The Message Center blinks the desired **recovery time (default 0:30)** that you want after completing both the cardio and strength intervals. Use the + or - keys to adjust, then press Enter.
9. You are then done programming and may press **Start** to begin your workout.

*Note printed with step 3:* on average you will complete 15-20 repetitions of the strength exercise in a 0:30 interval. As a general rule, the longer the interval, the less weight (dumbbells) and speed (treadmill) required.
