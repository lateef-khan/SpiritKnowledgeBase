The fit test estimates **VO2 max** (how well the body uses oxygen) over **at most 11 minutes**, progressing in speed and elevation. **If actual heart rate reaches 85% of projected maximum before the end, the program ends** and the score comes from the stage reached. Higher estimated VO2 means fitter.

1. Press **Fit Test**: the Message Window shows **GERKIN PROTOCOL PRESS ENTER TO BEGIN**.
2. Enter **Age** (Up/Down), then Enter.
3. Enter **body weight**, then Enter.
4. The window shows **PRESS START TO BEGIN OR ENTER TO MODIFY**.
5. **3-minute warm-up** at **3 mph, 0% grade**; the window alternates 3 MINUTE WARM-UP with actual and target HR every 5 seconds while time counts down. Other windows accrue.
6. Speed and incline follow the stage table below.
7. **Reaching target continues the test 15 more seconds**, then a **cool-down**: incline 0%, speed 3.0 mph. Cool-down is **1 minute if the test took 5 minutes or less, 3 minutes if it took 5-12 minutes**; the window alternates 1 (or 3) MIN COOL DOWN with YOUR SCORE every 5 seconds while time counts down.
8. The score is the **final completed stage's VO2 Max**. Afterwards: **TEST OVER PRESS STOP TO END OR ENTER TO SEE SCORE**; 20 seconds idle returns to idle mode, Enter shows the score 10 seconds.
