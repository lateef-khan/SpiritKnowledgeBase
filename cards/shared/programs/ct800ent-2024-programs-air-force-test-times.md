**These are the touchscreen figures. The 2024 CT800 and CT850 button consoles print a different Air
Force table** whose first bracket is "<20" at 12:36 and which skips the 30-39 bracket entirely - see
`ct900-air-force-prt-fitness-test`.

A timed **1.5 mile / 2.4 km run**. You control the speed manually. Maximum time allowed to pass:

| Age | Male | Female |
|---|---|---|
| <30 | 13:36 | 15:50 |
| 30-39 | 14:00 | 16:57 |
| 40-49 | 14:52 | 18:14 |
| 50-59 | 16:22 | 19:43 |
| 60+ | 18:14 | 22:28 |

For more detailed information, visit: http://bit.ly/SF-AirForce
