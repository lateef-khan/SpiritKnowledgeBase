**Templates** are preset programs with preserved parameters. Save one upon completion, or create one in the Templates tab: tap **"New Template"**, pick a base program, save with adjusted properties. **Maximum 12 templates**; remove them via **"Edit Templates"**.

**Favorites.** Open a program setup page and tap the **"ribbon"** icon (upper left) to like it; all labelled programs sit under the **Favorites** tab (un-tap the ribbon, or **"Edit Favorites"**, to remove). Favorites-on-Home differs:

| Book | Favorites on the Home screen |
|---|---|
| F80-2023 | up to 3 programs (render-verified) |
| F85-2023, TT8-2023, ST90-2023 | up to 6 programs |
| F89-2023 | favorites plus video classes, no count printed |

**History.** **Up to 10 records** store locally; the full history needs a linked SOLE+ profile. Tap a preview for the summary. Each user sees only their own workouts.

**Profiles.** Maximum 9, each with an optional passcode, linked to SOLE+ by QR (see the gen-2 list card).
