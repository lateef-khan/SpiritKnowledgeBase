**CUSTOM allows a fully customized speed and incline profile by sliding up and down on the profile columns.** Once the profile is defined, choose a **target time and a maximum speed**, and the intensity adapts accordingly. Save it with "Save as" (see `sole-treadmill-programs-touchscreen-gen1-templates`).
