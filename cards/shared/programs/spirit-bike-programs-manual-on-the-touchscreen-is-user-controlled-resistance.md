On every ENT touchscreen bike Manual is described in one sentence: **the level of resistance is
controlled by the user, and you may increase or decrease levels at any time during your workout.**
There is no level profile and no level-one start rule.

**On the CU800ENT 2022 and CR800ENT 2023** the setup is: press the **Time** tab to select the workout
duration, entering a value **between 5 and 99 minutes**, then repeat for **Age** and **Weight**, then
press **Start**.

**On the CU800ENT 2024** the Program Info panel says the resistance level is controlled by the rider
and the target time is set before the start. Its setup page shows a single **Time** field. The
manual prints no duration range for it; the 5-to-99-minute range above is from the 2022 and 2023
books only.

**The CR900ENT and CU900ENT** use the shared program setup screens instead, with their own defaults -
see `spirit-bike-programs-touchscreen-setup-defaults-age-thirty-weight-seventy-max-level-five`.

**On an LED console Manual behaves differently**, asking for time, age and weight and starting at
level one - see `spirit-bike-programs-manual-asks-time-age-and-weight-and-starts-at-level-one`.

**The XBR55ENT and XBU55ENT 2021 print the same two sentences**, word for word: Manual requires a
time frame set before starting, you set it with the scale or the arrows, **level is adjusted manually
while running**, and you press START to begin. The program tile itself adds "Level is controlled by
the user. Increase or decrease level at any time during your workout."

Those two manuals are **identical to each other across the whole programs chapter** - measured at
100.0% at word level over printed pages 28 to 40.
