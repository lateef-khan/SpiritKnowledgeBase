After the start-up sequence - the clock, calendar and alarm on the ERG600W; the type display and the
Water Level L1~L6 prompt on the ERG550W - the console goes to the **SPORT screen**, where **MANUAL and
RACE blink**. Press UP or DOWN to select one and ENTER to confirm. Those two are the whole program
set:

- **MANUAL** - count one chosen value down, or count everything up (see `see_also`).
- **RACE** - you against the computer at one of fifteen fixed paces (see `see_also`).

The ERG600W also has a **RECOVERY** button for the heart rate recovery test; the ERG550W console has
no RECOVERY button. Neither book prints a preset profile, an interval program, a user program, a
heart rate control program, a watt program or a body-fat test, and neither has a Heart Rate
Exertion or perceived-exertion page (grep for *exertion*, *Borg* and *220* finds nothing).

**The LCD legend promises more than the book describes.** The console drawings on both books'
console pages (read from a render) carry the mode strip **STOP MANUAL PROGRAM H.R.C. RACE USER
RECOVERY B.M.I FAT%**, a 16-column profile matrix numbered 1 to 16, AGE / H.t / W.t fields and
55% / 75% / 90% / TAG pulse marks. Only MANUAL, RACE and (on the ERG600W) RECOVERY are explained
anywhere in either book; the ERG550W's five buttons are UP, RESET, START/STOP, ENTER and DOWN. Do not
tell a customer these consoles have a PROGRAM, H.R.C., USER, B.M.I or FAT% mode; the manuals do not
say so.

**The ERG650W's console has a six-item menu and the ERG800W ten programs** - see
`not_to_be_confused_with`.
