**These four manuals have no heart rate training-zone page.** They print no "220 minus your age"
formula, no maximum heart rate percentage guidance, no worked example, and no target heart rate
training chart. The nine other Spirit commercial bike owner's manuals all do.

The absence was checked three ways in each of the four books: a loose search for `220`, a
spacing-tolerant search for `MHR` and for "maximum heart rate", and a search for "zone". The only
hits are the CR900 2018 and CU900 2018 sentence that the console bar graph "represents the
percentage of your maximum heart rate you are currently achieving", and the CR900ENT and CU900ENT
fitness test sentence about 85% of the projected maximum heart rate. Neither is a training-zone
page.

**These four also print no rate of perceived exertion page** - see
`spirit-bike-programs-rate-of-perceived-exertion`.

**What this means for the ENT pair.** The CR900ENT and CU900ENT Heart Rate program defaults to "65%
of your projected rate maximum", but the manual never says how the projected maximum is worked out.
Take it from a manual that does print the formula: 220 minus your age.

**What to answer instead.** For the training zone figures, see the four zone cards; each one names
the machines whose manual prints it. Do not read a figure from another bike's manual onto these four
machines.
