**This is leg power, not step length.** The 7.0T treadmill's Symmetry program measures left and right
step length (`70t-2026-programs-symmetry`); on the bikes it measures the power each leg produces
around the pedal rotation.

*"The Symmetry program may aid in achieving a more balanced pedaling stroke for patients with lower
limb deficiencies, such as stroke patients and post-op knee patients."* The watt measurements show
in the message window and the dot matrix draws the leg power symmetry as visual feedback.

1. Press the **Symmetry** key, then **Enter**.
2. Enter the **Time** with the Up and Down keys or the numeric key pad, then Enter.
3. Turn the **auto-brake** on or off, then Enter. **The default is off** - *"since the auto-brake may be
   activated with severe asymmetry"*.
4. Press **Start**; Enter goes back to modify.

**For best results.** *"The Symmetry program starts at level 1 and the resistance needs to be increased
manually by pressing the Up arrow."* Set it as high as the patient can manage without discomfort but
low enough for full pedal revolutions - *"very low resistance settings result in erratic or
inconsistent measurements."*

**The worked example** shows an average of **41 watts left against 34 watts right**, so the graph
grows on the left. With equal power only two dots light at the bottom centre of the screen. Press the
Display key under the message window to see the **Symmetry Index**, the percentage difference between
the legs; **equal legs read 0%**.

*"The Symmetry program employs a proprietary algorithm using a power table and velocity calculations
to generate the watt readings; they are not from direct force measurements."*

**The 2021 MED edition contradicts itself.** Its Symmetry chapter is this page word for word, but its
"Unique uses" page says Symmetry *"measures distance between left and right step lengths"* and keeps
"even step symmetry" - the stepper sentence, left in a bike book. The 2025 7.0R and 7.0U books
corrected that page to *"balance between left and right pedal stroke"* and "even power symmetry".

The 7.0S and 7.5S steppers print the same chapter with the same 41-versus-34 example
(`spirit-rehab-stepper-programs-symmetry-measures-left-and-right-leg-power`).
