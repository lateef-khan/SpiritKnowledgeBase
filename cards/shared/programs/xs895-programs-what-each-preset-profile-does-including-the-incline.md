Each of the five presets is described twice: once for the **resistance** profile and once, under the
heading **Incline**, for what the pedal elevation does. Both halves are printed on the same page and
belong together.

- **Hill.** A triangle or pyramid type of gradual progression from approximately **10% of maximum
  effort** (the level you chose before starting) up to a maximum effort which lasts for **10% of the
  total workout time**, then a gradual regression back to approximately 10% of maximum effort.
  *Incline:* a more gradual and sustained progression; **maximum elevation is in the middle of the
  workout and lasts for 10% of the duration.**
- **Fat Burn.** A quick progression up to the maximum resistance level (default or user input) that
  is sustained for **2/3 of the workout**, challenging your ability to sustain energy output for an
  extended period. *Incline:* a quick and sustained progression up to the maximum value (default or
  user input) for **90% of the workout duration.**
- **Cardio.** A quick progression up to near maximum resistance, with slight fluctuations up and
  down to let your heart rate elevate and recover repeatedly, before a quick cool down. Builds heart
  muscle, blood flow and lung capacity. *Incline:* moderate, with several elevation spikes at
  different points; **segments 4, 9 and 14 are maximum elevation** for this program.
- **Strength.** A gradual progression of resistance up to **100% of maximum effort**, sustained for
  **25% of workout duration**, building strength and muscular endurance **in the lower body and
  glutes**; a brief cool down follows. *Incline:* a quick climb to a moderate, sustained elevation
  that lasts the majority of the workout.
- **Interval.** High levels of intensity followed by recovery periods of low intensity, developing
  "Fast Twitch" muscle fibres. *Incline:* spikes similar to the resistance profile but **in different
  segments**, so all lower extremity muscles are equally challenged; **the incline alternates
  between 25% and 65% of maximum elevation.**

**The incline half has no counterpart on any other Spirit climber**, because no other machine in the
line has a powered incline. Do not carry these descriptions to a CS800, CRS800S or CVC800; see
`spirit-stepper-programs-what-each-preset-profile-does` and
`cvc800-programs-what-each-preset-profile-does`.
