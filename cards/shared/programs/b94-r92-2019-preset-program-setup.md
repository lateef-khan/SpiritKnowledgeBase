The five preset programs — Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength and HIIT — are all programmed the same way.

1. Press the desired program button, then press **Enter**.
2. Enter your **Age** with the Level up/down keys, then Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight** with the Level up/down keys, then Enter.
4. Adjust the **Time**, then Enter.
5. Adjust the **Max Level**. This is the peak exertion level you will experience during the program — the highest coloured segment of the program profile. Then press Enter.
6. Press **Start** to begin. Press **Stop** to go back one level or screen and modify settings.
7. Press **Display** to scroll through the data in the Message Window during the program.
8. When the program ends the Message Window shows a workout summary for a short time, then returns to the start-up display.
