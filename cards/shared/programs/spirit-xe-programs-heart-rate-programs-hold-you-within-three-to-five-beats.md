These consoles have **two** heart rate programs. They operate identically; the only difference is the
target:

- **HR 1 is set to 60%** of your maximum heart rate.
- **HR 2 is set to 80%** of your maximum heart rate.

Maximum heart rate is worked out as **220 minus your age**, from the age you enter during setup.

**After you select your target the program will attempt to keep you at, or within 3 to 5 heart beats
per minute of, that value.** Follow the prompts in the Message Center to hold the rate.

**You must wear the heart rate transmitter strap for these programs.** All five manuals print that as
a note above the steps. The hand pulse grips are not offered as an alternative here.

The heart rate figure the console first shows you is derived from the percentage you chose at step 1.
**If you change that number, the percentage changes with it** - so an HR 1 program you have adjusted
is no longer at 60%.

For the keystrokes, see `spirit-ce-programs-heart-rate-control-operation`.

**The neighbouring machines differ.** The XE195 has a single HR key defaulting to 60% and no second
program. The XE395 2023 and XE795 2023 have **four** heart rate programs at 55%, 65%, 75% and 85%.
The XE100, XE200, XE300, XE350 and XE550 also use 60% and 80%, but their manuals print neither the
3-to-5-beat tolerance nor the chest strap requirement.

**Five earlier XE printings print this same HR 1 = 60% / HR 2 = 80% pairing and the same 3-to-5-beat
tolerance** - XG400 2019 (printed pages 32-33), XE295 2019 and XE895 2018 (pages 33-34), XE395 2018
(pages 34-35) and XE795 2018 (pages 35-36). All five print the same nine setup steps and the same
"you must wear the heart rate transmitter strap for these programs" note.

**The XE195 2019 is the exception**, as its 2021 printing is: one HR key, defaulting to 60%, with no
HR 2 - see `xe195-2021-programs-single-heart-rate-key-defaults-to-sixty-percent`.
