**These are the touchscreen figures. The 2024 CT800 and CT850 button consoles print a different
Navy table** whose first bracket is 17-21 at 16:36 - see `ct900-navy-prt-fitness-test`.

A timed **1.5 mile / 2.4 km run**. You control the speed manually. Maximum time allowed to pass:

| Age | Male | Female | Age | Male | Female |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <=19 | 12:30 | 15:00 | 45-49 | 16:08 | 17:23 |
| 20-24 | 13:30 | 15:30 | 50-54 | 16:45 | 17:30 |
| 25-29 | 14:00 | 16:08 | 55-59 | 17:09 | 18:34 |
| 30-34 | 14:30 | 16:45 | 60-64 | 18:52 | 19:43 |
| 35-39 | 15:00 | 17:00 | 65+ | 20:35 | 20:52 |
| 40-44 | 15:30 | 17:15 | | | |

For more detailed information, visit: https://bit.ly/SF-Navy

**Unlike the button-console table, this one does not repeat the Army times for ages 17-41.** The
duplication recorded on `ct900-navy-prt-fitness-test` is gone on this console.

**The table is a flat image**; these values were read from a 300 dpi render. See
`ct800ent-2024-programs-editorial-note-printed-in-the-manual`.
