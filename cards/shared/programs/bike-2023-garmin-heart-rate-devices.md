**"To check if your Garmin device can be used for heart rate transmission, please refer to the list of
supported devices provided on www.soletreadmills.com"**

That is the whole of what these manuals say about Garmin. There is **no list of supported devices in
the manual itself**, and no statement that any particular Garmin model does or does not work. The
answer for a specific watch has to come from the website.

The sentence is printed at the end of the erratic-heart-rate troubleshooting section, so a customer
looking for it under "compatibility" or "pairing" will not find it there.

**Only the 2023 manuals carry this line.** The 2011 to 2019 B94, R92, B74, R72, LCB and LCR manuals do
not mention Garmin at all.
