Ten keys in two rows: Manual; five presets — Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, plus Interval on the 2016 books and HIIT on the 2019 and 2023 books (same workout, renamed); User 1 and User 2 custom slots; HR 1 and HR 2 heart-rate programs. The books still print the old 'nine different programs' boilerplate in the monitor paragraph, which undercounts the ten keys on the console. Quick Start (Start key, time counts up) bypasses all of this.
