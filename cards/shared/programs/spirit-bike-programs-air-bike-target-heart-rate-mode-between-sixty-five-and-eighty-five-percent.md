**The band is 65% to 85% of maximum heart rate.** Target Heart Rate Mode "calculates the maximum
heart rate value to optimize the training target **between 65% - 85%** of the user's calculated or
theoretical maximum heart rate."

1. **Launch** - press the **Target Heart Rate Mode** button.
2. **Setup** - after the console has been reset, **enter your age**. Adjust with **▲** or **▼** and
   press **Enter**.
3. **Start** - the display shows your heart rate and progress toward the goal.
   - Below 65% of the calculated maximum, **the 65% display flashes** - the minimum goal has not been
     reached.
   - Above 85%, **the 85% display flashes** - you are over the maximum goal.
   - In between, the **Target Heart Rate Achieved** banner lights.
4. **End** - the mode ends when you stop pedalling, or when you press **Stop**. The workout summary
   follows.

**The console cannot change anything for you.** The Heart Rate Display Panel flashes a **▲ or ▼
symbol to tell you to increase or decrease your pedal speed**. An air bike has no resistance to
adjust, so this mode is a coach, not a controller - see
`spirit-bike-programs-air-bike-has-no-resistance-levels-because-the-fan-sets-the-load`.

**It needs a wireless chest strap**, and the AB900 manual never says so - see
`spirit-bike-console-air-bike-display-panels`.

**These figures are the air bikes' own.** Do not read 60%/80%, 60%/85% or 65%/80% from an upright or
recumbent bike card onto these two machines.

**Age is the only thing the mode asks for**, and neither manual prints the formula it uses to turn
age into a maximum heart rate. The AB950's Heart Rate Exertion page prints **220 minus your age**
elsewhere in the book; the AB900 prints no formula anywhere.
