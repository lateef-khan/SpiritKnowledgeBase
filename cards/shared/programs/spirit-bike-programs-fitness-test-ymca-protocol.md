The fitness test is based on the **YMCA protocol**: a sub-maximal test that uses pre-determined,
fixed work levels driven by your heart rate readings as the test progresses.

- It takes **6 to 15 minutes**, depending on your fitness.
- It ends when your heart rate reaches **85% of maximum** at any time during the test, **or** when
  your heart rate is between **110 bpm and 85%** at the end of **two consecutive stages**.
- The protocol uses **two to four, 3-minute stages** of continuous exercise.
- At the end you are given a **VO2max** score - Volume of Oxygen uptake, a measurement of how much
  oxygen you need to perform a known amount of work.

You choose **Male** or **Female** at the start, which decides the workload chart used. The manual's
caveat: a **very de-conditioned male** should choose Female, and a **very conditioned female**
should choose Male.

**Wording differs but the figures do not.** The CU800 2012, CR900 2018 and CU900 2018 open with "The
fitness test is based on the YMCA protocol"; the CR800 2021, CR800 2023, CU800 2021, the three
800ENT manuals and the CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 open with "The **VO2 program** is based on the YMCA
protocol". Every number above is identical across all eleven.

**The CR900ENT and CU900ENT print a shorter, different version** with a stage count instead of a
duration - see `spirit-bike-programs-fitness-test-on-the-touchscreen-900-is-up-to-four-stages`.
