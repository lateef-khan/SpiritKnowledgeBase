Custom is fully pattern-driven: on the program page press 'Add Pattern', then connect dots to map workload across the 20 segments, easiest at the bottom to hardest at the top. Created patterns can be edited later at any time. Each segment's duration follows the total time you set just before start. (A profile also gives you its own adjustable custom program storing an intensity pattern.)
