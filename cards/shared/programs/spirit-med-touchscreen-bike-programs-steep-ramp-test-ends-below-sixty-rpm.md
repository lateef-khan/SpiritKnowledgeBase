*"The Steep Ramp Test is used to determine and optimize the training intensity in adults and does not
require respiratory gas analysis measurements. User needs to maintain above 60 RPM as long as
possible as the resistance keeps increasing. The test should be supervised by the therapist."*

Setup is two values, and the screen names their units:

| Setting | Unit |
|---|---|
| **Power Increment** | **watts** |
| **Stage Duration** | **seconds** |

While the test runs the screen shows the **current watt**, the **current speed in RPM**, the **stage
number** and a prompt to keep the speed up; **a button aborts the test**. **The test ends when the RPM
drops below 60.**

*Two thresholds on one page, both recorded as printed: the body text and the coaching prompt say
**60 rpm** - "Endure the longest time possible at pedaling speed over 60 rpm" - while the warning
prompt in the same screenshot reads **"Speed is below 65 rpm. Speed up or finish"**. Which one the
console acts on is not stated, and both prompts exist only in the picture; the text layer has
neither.*

The 8.0U and 8.5R 2025 books print this page word for word, as does the 8.5UE ergometer
(`85ue-2025-programs-steep-ramp-test-ends-below-sixty-rpm`). No 8.5S stepper or treadmill book
carries this test.
