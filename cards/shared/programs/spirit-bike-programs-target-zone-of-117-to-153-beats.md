First work out your **Maximum Heart Rate**: **220 minus your age**. Your heart rate training zone is
**50% to 90%** of that maximum.

**These three manuals print 65% and 85%.** 65% of your MHR is "the recommended for burning fat"
while 85% is recommended for strengthening the cardiovascular system, and 65% to 85% is the zone to
stay in for maximum benefit.

Worked example for someone **40 years old**:

- 220 - 40 = **180** (maximum heart rate)
- 180 x .65 = **117** beats per minute (65% of maximum)
- 180 x .85 = **153** beats per minute (85% of maximum)
- So for a 40 year old the training zone would be **117 to 153** beats per minute.

If you enter your age during programming the console performs this calculation automatically.

**The Heart Rate program on these machines defaults to 65%**, which matches the lower figure here -
see `spirit-bike-programs-heart-rate-defaults-to-sixty-five-percent-and-holds-within-five-beats`.

**Three other figure pairs appear across the thirteen commercial bike manuals.** The CU800 2012
prints 60%/80% and 108 to 144; the CR800 2021, CR800 2023 and CU800 2021 print 60%/85% and 108 to
153; the CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 print 65%/80% with a printed answer of 117 to **225**. Four
manuals have no such page.

**The 2024 CR800ENT prints this same page and the same 117-to-153 example** (printed page 46).
**Its LED stablemates the 2024 CR800 and CU800 do not** - see
`spirit-bike-programs-target-zone-of-117-to-225-beats`.
