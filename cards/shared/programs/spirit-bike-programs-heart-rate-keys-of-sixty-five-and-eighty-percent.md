On the CR800 2021, CR800 2023 and CU800 2021 the first step of the heart rate program reads:

> Using the Program button choose the HR program (**65% or 80%**) then press the Enter key.

So the console carries **two** heart rate programs, named by the percentage of maximum heart rate
they target.

**The page immediately before contradicts them.** The training-zone page in the same three manuals
recommends **60%** of maximum heart rate for burning fat and **85%** for strengthening the
cardiovascular system, and calls **60% to 85%** the zone to stay in - see
`spirit-bike-programs-target-zone-of-108-to-153-beats`. Neither figure matches the program names.
The manual never reconciles them, and the KB does not harmonise them.

**No other commercial bike manual names its heart rate programs by percentage.** The CU800 2012,
CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 have a single HR or HRC key with a target you type
in; the five ENT bikes have a single Heart Rate program that **defaults** to 65% - see
`spirit-bike-programs-heart-rate-defaults-to-sixty-five-percent-and-holds-within-five-beats`.
