Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength and Interval are all programmed the same way.

1. Press the desired program button, then press **Enter**.
2. The message window asks for your **Age**. Adjust with the Up and Down keys, then press Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight** with the Up and Down keys, then Enter.
4. Enter the **Time**, then Enter.
5. Adjust the **Max Level** — the peak exertion level you will experience during the program, the
   highest coloured segment or box of the program profile. Then press Enter.
6. Press **Start** to begin. You can also go back and modify your settings by pressing **Stop** to go
   back one screen, **or Enter to go back to the beginning**.
7. Press the **Up or Down** keys at any time during the program to increase or decrease the resistance.
8. Press **Display** to scroll through the data in the message window.
9. When the program ends the message window shows a workout summary for a short time, then the
   console returns to the start-up display.

**Step 6 is where these consoles differ from the B94/R92 ones**: here Enter jumps back to the
beginning of the settings, which the B94/R92 manuals do not offer.
