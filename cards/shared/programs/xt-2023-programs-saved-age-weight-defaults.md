**No — the console keeps the last values entered.**

The Age and Weight default settings change when you enter a new number, so **the last Age and Weight entered are saved as the new default settings**. If you enter your Age and Weight the first time you use the treadmill, you do not have to enter them every time you work out, unless:

- your Age or Weight changes, or
- someone else enters a different Age and Weight.

That last case is why a shared machine can show a stranger's figures: whoever used it last leaves their numbers behind as the defaults.
