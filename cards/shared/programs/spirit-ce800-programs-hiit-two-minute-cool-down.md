HIIT is a fully customisable interval training program: short bursts of high intensity sprinting
followed by short rest periods. You set the number of intervals, the time of each Sprint and Rest
period, and the work intensity of the levels.

1. Select HIIT with the Program button, press Enter, then enter your **Age**.
2. Enter your **Weight**.
3. Number of **intervals**: default **10**, range **3 to 15**. One interval equals 1 Sprint and 1
   Rest segment.
4. **Sprint Level** - the resistance level during the Sprint segments.
5. **Rest Level** - the resistance level during the Rest segments.
6. **Interval time**. The display shows `Sprint Time:30` and `Rest Time:30`. The Sprint time blinks
   first; adjust it from **20 to 60 seconds** with the up and down keys, press Enter, then adjust
   the Rest time the same way.
7. The display shows the total time for the HIIT workout. Press Start. There is a **3-minute
   warm-up** before the first Sprint, at **resistance level 5**, adjustable manually.
8. During HIIT the dot matrix display is a **speed** indication display, not a power or resistance
   display. A blinking LED at the first Sprint segment is the target speed LED and indicates
   **90 pedal rpm**. The lights below it light up the faster you pedal; exceed 90 rpm and the target
   LED moves up. Maintain at least 90 rpm through the Sprint segments. The **rest** segments are set
   to **resistance level 5** and you may pedal at any speed as your heart rate recovers. Resistance
   can be adjusted manually during both Sprint and Rest.
9. After the last Sprint there is a **2-minute cool-down**. Press Stop to bypass it and show the
   workout summary.

**This is the 2-minute cool-down version.** The CE900 2021 and CE900 2025 print the identical
program with a **3-minute** cool-down; the CES880 2025 prints a **20-second** default Sprint time.
The CE800 2013 and CE850 2016 have no HIIT program at all.

**The 2024 CE850 prints all nine steps** (printed page 28), including the 3-minute warm-up at
resistance level 5, the 90 pedal rpm target speed LED and the 2-minute cool-down. **The 2024 CE800,
CR800 and CU800 stop at step 6** and print none of it - see
`spirit-2024-programs-hiit-procedure-stops-at-step-six`.
