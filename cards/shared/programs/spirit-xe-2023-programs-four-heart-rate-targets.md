These two consoles have **four** heart rate programs, not two:

| Program | Target |
|---|---|
| HR1 | **55%** of maximum heart rate |
| HR2 | **65%** |
| HR3 | **75%** |
| HR4 | **85%** |

The console labels them **HR 55%, HR 65%, HR 75% and HR 85%**. All four are programmed the same way:
press **PROGRAM** to select HR PROGRAM, press Enter, press PROGRAM again to pick the percentage, then
Enter, then Age, Weight, Time and the **Heart Rate Target**.

- **You must wear the heart rate transmitter strap for these programs.**
- After you select your target, the program will attempt to keep you at, or **within 3 to 5 heart
  beats per minute** of, that value.
- The heart rate the console first offers is derived from the percentage you picked; **changing that
  number moves the percentage with it**.
- **Level + / Level - during the program changes your target heart rate**, not the resistance
  directly.

*The manual's step 6 note refers back to "the % you accepted in **Step 2**", which is where the
percentage is chosen on these consoles; the 2021 manuals' equivalent note says Step 1. Both are
correct for their own step numbering.*

**Every earlier Spirit XE elliptical uses 60% and 80%**, or a single key defaulting to 60% - see
`spirit-xe-programs-heart-rate-programs-hold-you-within-three-to-five-beats`. **These four
percentages do not match the training-zone page in the same 2023 manuals**, which still recommends
60% and 80%; the source does not reconcile them.
