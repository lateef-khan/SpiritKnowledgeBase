1. Using the **Program** button choose the **HR program (65% or 80%)**, then press Enter.
2. Enter your **Age**, then Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight**, then Enter.
4. Set the **Time**, then Enter.
5. Set your **target Heart Rate** - the heart rate you will try to maintain during the program -
   then Enter.
6. Press **Start** to begin, or **Enter** to go back and modify. **Stop** goes back one level or
   screen while editing.
7. **Level Up/Down during the program changes your target heart rate**, not the resistance directly.
8. **The program automatically increases or decreases the resistance** depending on whether your
   heart rate is above or below your target.

**The two programs are named for the percentage of maximum heart rate they aim at: 65% and 80%.**
The same machines' heart rate training page recommends **60% for burning fat and 85% for the
cardiovascular system** - see `spirit-stepper-programs-target-zone-of-108-to-153-beats`. The manual
does not reconcile the two pairs; both are recorded as printed.

**The 2021 printings of both machines carry the same eight steps**, headed "HEART RATE PROGRAM
OPERATION", and the same 65% / 80% pair.

**The CVC800 vertical climber has no such program**, although its contents page names a
`HEART RATE PROGRAM` chapter - see `cvc800-programs-no-heart-rate-program-despite-the-chapter-title`.
