**The heart rate control program needs the wireless Bluetooth chest strap transmitter (sold
separately). Do not use the hand heartbeat sensor for heart rate control**; the program must
receive a steady signal in the heartbeat window.

1. Press **Enter/Program**, use **Speed up/down** to select **HR PROGRAM**, press
   Enter/Program to display **TARGET HR 55 PERCENT**; press up/down to choose **TARGET HR 65,
   75 or 85 PERCENT**; confirm with Enter/Program.
2. Enter your **Age** with Speed up/down, press Enter/Program.
3. Enter your **Weight**, press Enter/Program.
4. **Time** - adjust, press Enter/Program.
5. Adjust the **Heart Rate Target** - the level you will strive to maintain. The value shown is
   based on the percentage accepted in step 1; changing this number raises or lowers that
   percentage. Press Enter/Program.
6. Press **Start** to begin, or **Stop** to go back and modify.
7. To increase or decrease the workload at any time, press **Speed up/down** on the console or
   the right handlebar - this **changes your target heart rate** during the program.
8. When the program ends, press Start to begin the same program again or Stop to exit.

The book does not say whether the program adjusts speed, incline or both to hold the target.
The TR65 (speed for HR1, incline for HR2) and TRX3500/TRX4500 (incline only) books do say;
see `see_also`.
