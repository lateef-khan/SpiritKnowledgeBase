**Caution! Do not use this machine for Heart Rate programs unless a steady, solid Actual Heart Rate
value is being displayed.** High, wild, random numbers indicate a problem.

Areas to look for interference which may cause erratic heart rate:

1. Microwave ovens, TVs, small appliances, etc.
2. Fluorescent lights.
3. Some household security systems.
4. Electric fence for a pet.
5. Some people have problems with the transmitter picking up a signal from their skin. **Try wearing
   the transmitter upside down** - normally it is oriented so the Spirit Fitness logo is right side
   up.
6. The antenna that picks up your heart rate is very sensitive. If there is an outside noise source,
   **turning the whole machine 90 degrees may de-tune the interference.**
7. Another individual wearing a transmitter **within 3 feet** of your machine's console.

If problems continue, contact your dealer.

**This is the seven-item list. The 2024 CS800 and CRS800S print an eight-item one** that adds *"The
machine is not properly grounded"* as item 1 and moves everything down - see
`spirit-stepper-programs-erratic-heart-rate-interference`. The grounding item is the whole
difference; do not answer from the eight-item card and count wrong.

Two wording differences across the five machines, recorded as printed:

- The **XS895** incline stepper writes item 4 as **"Perimeter fence for a pet"**; the CS800, CRS800S
  and CVC800 write **"Electric fence for a pet"**.
- The **XS895** heads the caution *"Do not use this **elliptical trainer** for Heart Rate
  programs"* - leftover text from the XE895 elliptical whose book this one was made from. The
  machine is an incline stepper. The **CVC800** heads it "this Vertical Climber" and the **CS800**
  "this stepper".
