1. Press **Enter/Program**, use **Speed up/down** to select **TARGET PROGRAM**, press
   Enter/Program to confirm. Press up/down to choose between **TARGET TIME**, **TARGET
   DISTANCE** and **TARGET CALORIES**, and press Enter/Program to confirm.
2. **Age** - adjust, press Enter/Program.
3. **Body Weight** - adjust.
4. The message window displays the time, distance or calorie preset to be adjusted, depending
   on the target chosen. Adjust with Speed up/down, press Enter/Program.
5. Press **Start** to begin, or **Stop** to go back one level.

**The book prints no default or range for the three targets** on this console. The TR260,
TRX1400 and TRX2500 books print theirs (30:00, 5.00 miles, 500 calories); see `see_also`.
