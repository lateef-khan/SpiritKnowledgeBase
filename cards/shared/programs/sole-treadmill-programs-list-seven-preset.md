This console offers **7 factory preset programs, 2 user defined programs and one Manual program**. The Heart Rate Control programs H-1 and H-2 are part of the preset set, which is why the heart-rate chapter speaks of "the nine different programs" (seven presets plus two user slots) for background monitoring.

The presets are **not named** in the text (no Hill/Fat Burn/Cardio headings); each shows its maximum speed in the Speed window when chosen. Setup is on `sole-treadmill-programs-selecting-seven-preset`, user setup on `sole-treadmill-programs-user-basic-keypad`, and the H-1/H-2 programs on `sole-treadmill-programs-hrc-70-90-autopilot`.
