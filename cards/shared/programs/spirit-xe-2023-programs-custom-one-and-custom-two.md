There are **two** customisable programs, **Custom 1 and Custom 2**, which operate identically. You can
build one from scratch or **save any preset program you complete as a custom program**.

1. Press **PROGRAM** to select **Custom 1** or **Custom 2**, then Enter. If a program is already saved
   there it is displayed and you are ready to begin. If not, you may enter a username: the letter
   **"A"** blinks, **Level +** switches to "B" and **Level -** switches to "Z". Press Enter for each
   letter. **Maximum 7 characters.** Press **Stop** when finished.
2. If a program is already stored, pressing Start or Enter at the welcome message prompts **"Run
   Program?"** - Level + / - chooses Yes or No. Choosing No then asks whether to delete the stored
   program. **You must delete the current program to build a new one.**
3. Enter your **Age**.
4. Enter your **Weight**.
5. Set the **Time**.
6. The first column blinks (`SEGMENT > 1`). Set its resistance with the **Level +/-** key, then Enter.
7. The next segment starts at the previous segment's level. Repeat until **all twenty segments** have
   been set.
8. Press **Start** to begin, or **Enter** to go back and modify your settings.

**There is no Max Resistance Level step**, unlike the XE295, XG400 and XE795 2021, which ask for one
before the segments.

*Both manuals print the Age instruction twice - once as the tail of step 2 and again as step 3 - so
their printed list runs one step longer than the actions it describes.*
