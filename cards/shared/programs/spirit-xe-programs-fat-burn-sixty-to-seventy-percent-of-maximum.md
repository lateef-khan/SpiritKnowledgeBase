The Fat Burn program is designed to maximise the burning of fat. The manuals say most experts agree
that a **lower exertion level that stays steady** is best, and that the absolute best way to burn fat
is to keep your heart rate at around **60% to 70% of its maximum potential**.

**This program does not use heart rate.** It simulates a lower, steady, exertion workout; it does not
read your pulse and does not adjust to it.

At setup you are asked for a **Max Level**, the peak exertion level of the program. The **factory
setting is level five** - the lowest factory Max Level of the five presets.

**The 60% to 70% figure appears nowhere else in these manuals.** Their training-zone page recommends
60% to 80% as the zone to stay in for maximum benefit, and the console's own heart rate programs are
set to 60% and 80%. The source does not reconcile the three.

**The later XE ellipticals describe Fat Burn differently** - a quick progression to the maximum
resistance level sustained for 2/3 of the workout, with no heart rate percentage at all. That is
`spirit-ce-programs-fat-burn-profile`.

The Sole E25 2026 and B94/R92 2019 owner's manuals print this same wording for their own Fat Burn
programs. Those are Sole machines and are not covered by this card.
