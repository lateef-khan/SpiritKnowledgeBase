First work out your **Maximum Heart Rate**: **220 minus your age**. Your heart rate training zone is
**50% to 90%** of that maximum.

**These two manuals print 65% and 80%.** 65% of your MHR is recommended for burning fat while 80% is
recommended for strengthening the cardiovascular system, and 65% to 80% is the zone to stay in for
maximum benefit.

**The worked example is wrong as printed.** Both manuals print, for someone 40 years old:

- 220 - 40 = **180** (maximum heart rate)
- 180 x .65 = **117** beats per minute (65% of maximum)
- 180 X .80 = **225** beats per minute (80% of maximum)
- So for a 40 year old the training zone would be **117 to 225** beats per minute.

**180 x 0.80 is 144, not 225**, and 225 beats per minute is above the printed maximum heart rate of
180 for a 40 year old. The percentages are recorded here exactly as printed, and so is the wrong
figure, because that is what the page shows a reader. **Do not train to 225.**

**The same slip is in the Spirit CE900 2025 elliptical manual** - see
`ce900-2025-programs-target-zone-of-117-to-225-beats`. No other Spirit commercial bike manual
carries it.

**Three other figure pairs appear across the thirteen commercial bike manuals.** The CU800 2012
prints 60%/80% and 108 to 144; the CR800 2021, CR800 2023 and CU800 2021 print 60%/85% and 108 to
153; the three 800ENT bikes print 65%/85% and 117 to 153. Four manuals have no such page.

**The 2024 CR800 and CU800 owner's manuals print the same error** (both printed page 32): the same
65% and 80% pair, the same `180 X .80 = 225`, and the same closing sentence that the zone is 117 to
225 beats per minute. The 2024 CR800ENT prints 65% and 85% and reaches 153 - see
`spirit-bike-programs-target-zone-of-117-to-153-beats`.
