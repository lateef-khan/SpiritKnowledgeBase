Cardio presents a quick progression up to **near maximum** resistance level - the default level, or
the level you entered. It has slight fluctuations up and down to allow your heart rate to elevate
and then recover repeatedly, before beginning a quick cool down. The manuals say this builds up your
heart muscle and increases blood flow and lung capacity.

All ten Spirit commercial elliptical owner's manuals print this description word for word. The
CE800 2013 heads it "Resistance:" before the sentence.

The profile picture that goes with it is a printed image in every manual, so the KB does not hold
its column values.
