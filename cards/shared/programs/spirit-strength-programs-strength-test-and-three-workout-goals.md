**The Strength Test is offered in PURE mode only.** Select **Strength Test** from the
main screen and complete the test using explosive power to find your maximum strength.
The console then shows a **Weight Guide** with a suggested number of sets, repetitions
and a resistance for each of three goals.

| Goal | On-screen purpose | Sets | Reps |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Muscle Endurance** | maintain fitness and health | 2 - 4 | 15 - 20 |
| **Hypertrophy** | increase muscle mass and BMR | 2 - 4 | 8 - 12 |
| **Power & Strength** | improve absolute strength | 5 - 6 | 4 - 6 |

**The sets and reps are the prescription. The resistance is not a fixed figure** - it is
calculated from your own test. The manual's screenshot shows a peak result of 150 lb
giving 80, 100 and 110 lb for the three rows; those pounds are that example's, not a
specification.

The manual also defines the three:

- **Muscle Endurance** - the ability of a muscle or group of muscles to sustain repeated
  contractions over a prolonged period without fatigue.
- **Muscular Hypertrophy** - the increase in size and volume of skeletal muscles
  resulting from resistance training or other forms of exercise.
- **Muscular Strength** - the maximum force a muscle can produce in a single movement;
  **muscular power** is the ability to exert that force quickly.

Two results end the test rather than prescribing:

- **Too Heavy.** If the resistance exceeds the capabilities of the motor system the
  screen reads *So Strong!* and says the recommended value exceeds the resistance range
  of the device - exercise at the maximum setting, try another training device, or
  consult a professional coach. Select **Re-Test** and exert a little less force.
- **Too Light.** If the result is below the minimum effective training resistance the
  screen reads *Off to a Great Start* - exercise at the minimum setting, try another
  device, or consult a professional coach. Select **Re-Test** and exert more effort.

Select a goal to begin, **Re-Test** to test again, **End Session** to stop.
