The Fitness Test is based on the **YMCA Protocol** and is a **sub-maximal** test that uses
pre-determined, fixed work levels based on your heart rate readings as the test progresses.

- It takes **between 6 and 15 minutes**, depending on your level of fitness.
- It ends when your heart rate reaches **85% of maximum** at any time during the test, **or** if your
  heart rate is **between 110 bpm and 85%** at the end of the two consecutive stages.
- At the end your **VO2 max** score is displayed on the console. VO2 max stands for Volume of Oxygen
  uptake — a measurement of how much oxygen you need to perform a known amount of work.
- The protocol uses **two to four 3-minute stages** of continuous exercise.

**On the test setup page, fix your physical parameters if they are not correct — this information
determines the results of your test.**

The test setup screen also prints: **"Level changes automatically until you reach 85% of max heart
rate. Stop if you feel ill or distressed."**

**Before the test:**

- Make sure you are in good health. Check with your physician before performing any exercise **if you
  are over the age of 35 or have any pre-existing health conditions**.
- Make sure you have warmed up and stretched before taking the test.
- Do not take in caffeine before the test.

**This manual prints no score chart.** It says a VO2 max number is displayed but gives no scale to
read it against. The excellent/good/average charts exist only in the 2011 and 2013 LCB and LCR
manuals, and those describe a different console — do not quote them for this machine.
