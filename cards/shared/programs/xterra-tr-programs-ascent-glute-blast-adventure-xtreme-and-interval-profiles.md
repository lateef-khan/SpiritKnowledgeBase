The book prints each preset as **two dot-matrix pictures, "Work" (speed) and "Incline"**, on
an 18-column by 8-row grid. The rows "give a visual picture of the intensity level, but do not
necessarily indicate a specific value", so **no speed or incline figure is printed** and none
is given here. What the pictures show:

| Program | Work (speed) picture | Incline picture |
|---|---|---|
| **Ascent** | A single pyramid: climbs step by step to a peak in the middle columns and descends symmetrically | A broad, low plateau across the middle of the workout with one higher step near the centre |
| **Glute Blast** | Two humps: a rise to a peak, a dip, and a second, higher peak, then a descent | Several short raised blocks, the tallest near the centre |
| **Adventure** | High work with alternating up-and-down steps across most of the workout | Four short, isolated incline spikes spaced across the workout |
| **Xtreme** | A steady climb to a sustained high plateau in the second half, then a sharp drop at the end | A plateau across most of the workout with a higher block in the middle |
| **Interval** | Four tall equal-height blocks separated by low recovery columns | Four short incline blocks aligned with the work blocks |

The TR6.6 prints a sixth profile, **X Country**, which the TR6.4 does not have; it is
described on the TR6.6 roster card.

The preset **top speed (3 mph / 5 kph) and top incline (3.0%)** you accept or change during
setup cap these profiles.
