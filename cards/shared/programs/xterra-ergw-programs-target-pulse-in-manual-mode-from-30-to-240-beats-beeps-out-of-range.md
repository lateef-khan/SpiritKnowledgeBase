**Wireless chest strap sensor sold separately - the pulse function only works with it.**

In Manual Mode, set the target heart rate value by pressing UP/DOWN; the available range is **30 to
240 in 1 BPM increments**. The computer displays your heart rate during training. **When you are out
of range of the target value during exercise, the computer beeps and PULSE blinks until the target
range is resumed or the wireless chest strap sensor is removed.**

PULSE is one of the five functions MANUAL mode can count down (time, distance, strokes, calories,
pulse), and only one of them can be set at a time - see `see_also`. The book prints no zone width
around the target, and the console changes no load: on a water rower the resistance is the tank
level.

**The same paragraph is printed in the ERG650W book, whose console has no Manual mode and sets its
heart rate target as Single THR, 90 to 200 BPM** - that card explains the ruling. The ERG800W's
program runs 100 to 180 with a 20-to-50-beat zone; the ERG500's alarm runs 90 to 220. One card per
console.
