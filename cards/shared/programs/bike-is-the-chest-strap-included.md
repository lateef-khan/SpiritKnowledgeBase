**On these machines the manual says the chest strap is not supplied.**

| Manual | What it prints |
|---|---|
| **B74/R72 2015** | The section is headed "USING HEART RATE TRANSMITTER (Optional)" and opens: "The heart rate transmitter is an option and is **not included with your bike**. Inquire with the store where you bought your bike for purchasing this item." |
| **LCB 2011, LCR 2011, LCB 2013, LCR 2013** | The section is headed "USING HEART RATE TRANSMITTER **(SOLD SEPARATELY)**". No further wording. |
| **B94/R92 2023, LCB/LCR 2023** | The section is headed "USING HEART RATE TRANSMITTER (OPTIONAL)" and opens: "**If you purchase** a wireless chest strap transmitter from Sole, here are the instructions on how to wear it". |

**The B94/R92 2011, 2013, 2016 and 2019 manuals say nothing either way.** Their heading is a bare
"USING HEART RATE TRANSMITTER" with no parenthetical and no sentence about buying one. Absence is not a
statement that the strap is included — do not tell a customer it ships with the machine on the strength
of those manuals.

The **LCB 2016, LCR 2016 and LCB/LCR 2019** manuals carry the same parenthetical after the heading, but
the text conversion mangled it beyond safe quoting, so those four machines are deliberately left off
this card. Check the printed manual before answering for them.

The strap itself is a **Panasonic CR2032**-powered transmitter with an estimated 2500 hours of battery
life.
