Confirm physical information step by step first — results rest on age, weight, height and gender. Before starting, **"All Results"** shows the test results table.

**BEFORE THE TEST:** make sure you are in good health (physician check if over 35 or with pre-existing conditions); warm up and stretch; no caffeine; if using hand pulse sensors, hold the grips gently without tensing.

**DURING THE TEST:** the console must receive a **steady heart rate** (hand pulse sensors or chest strap) or the test cannot begin. The test starts with a **3 minute warm-up at 4.8 kph (3 mph)** before the actual test (Gerkin only). Shown during the test: elapsed time; incline in percent grade; distance in miles or km per the unit setting; speed in MPH or KPH; target and actual heart rate in the message window.

**AFTER THE TEST:** cool down one to three minutes, and **note the score** — the console returns to start-up mode after a few minutes.
