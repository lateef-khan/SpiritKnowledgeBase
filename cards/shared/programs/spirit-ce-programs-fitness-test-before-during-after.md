**Before the test**

- Make sure you are in good health; check with your physician before performing any exercise if you
  are over the age of **35**, or have pre-existing health conditions.
- Warm up and stretch before taking the test.
- Do not take in **caffeine** before the test.
- Hold the hand grips gently, do not tense up.

**During the test**

- The console must be receiving a **steady heart rate** for the test to begin. Use the hand pulse
  sensors or wear a heart rate chest strap transmitter.
- Maintain a steady **50 RPM** pedal speed. **Below 48 RPM or above 52 RPM the console emits a
  steady beeping sound** until you are back within range.
- The Scan or Display key scrolls the readings.
- The display always shows your pedal speed on the right side to help you hold 50 RPM.
- The data shown during the test is:
  - **Work in KGM** - an abbreviated form of kg-m/min., a work measurement of kilogram-force
    meter/minute.
  - **Work in Watts** - 1 watt is equal to **6.11829727787** kg-m/min.
  - **HR** is your actual heart rate; **TGT** is the target heart rate to reach to end the test.
  - **Time** is the total elapsed time of the test.

**After the test**

- Cool down for about **one to three minutes**.
- Take note of your score, because the console returns to the start-up mode automatically after a
  few minutes.

The CE850 2020 and CE850 2022 word the first bullet as "before beginning a new exercise routine";
the others say "before performing any exercise". Nothing else differs between the seven manuals.
