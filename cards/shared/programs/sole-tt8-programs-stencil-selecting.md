This differs from the A1 TT8 setup (`sole-treadmill-programs-selecting-tt8`) in two steps: the book prints a **3-minute warm-up with bypass** (the A1 card says its books print none), and the Time step states **no 20-minute default** in text — though the console screenshots on the page show **Age 35, Weight 155, Time 20, Max Speed 3.0**.

1. **Press the desired Program key**, then press **Enter** to customise, or just press **Start** to begin with the default settings.
2. The Message window shows your **Age**. Entering it correctly affects the heart rate Bar Graph Display and the Heart Rate programs. Adjust with the Grade or Speed keys, then press Enter. **You may press Start at any time during the programming** to start with what you have entered so far.
3. The Weight window shows your **body weight**; entering it correctly affects the calorie count. Adjust and press Enter. (No machine gives an exact calorie count; use it to compare workouts.)
4. The Time window shows the **total workout time desired**. Adjust with the Grade or Speed keys, then press Enter.
5. The Speed window shows the **preset top speed** of the program. Adjust and press Enter — this caps the highest speed the program will reach at its peak.
6. **Press Start to begin.** The program begins with a **3 minute warm-up**; press Start again to bypass it and go straight to the program.
