The Programs Setup page lists **fifteen**:

- **Manual** - *"allows you control the workload manually yourself, not controlled by the computer."*
  Choose the workout time, press Start. The setup screen also takes a workload type and a level.
- **Timer** - *"a quick set-up, large font, egg timer type display for patient warm-ups."* Set a
  target exercise time and press Start; **+** and **-** adjust the session time during the workout.
- **Hill** and **Plateau** - the two **Pattern** programs. *"Pattern programs are preset exercise
  programs that have been designed for a variety of goals. The initial built in level of difficulty
  is set to a relatively easy level. You may select the level before beginning."* Setup takes a level
  of difficulty, a workload type and a workout time, and **the unit of measurement can switch
  between RPM and deg/sec**.
- **HIIT**
- **Wingate Test**
- **Facility** - build and save a custom program under your facility's name, or save any preset you
  complete as one. Change the resistance type, max level or total time, tap a segment to create or
  modify the profile, and Save; Start runs it.
- **Heart Rate Target**
- **Calories Target**
- **METs Target**
- **YMCA Protocol**
- **Astrand Rhyming Test**
- **Conconi Test**
- **Steep Ramp Test**
- **Power Distribution**

*"When a program is selected you have the option of modifying the settings. If you want to begin
without entering new settings, just press the start key."* Skipping data entry uses the data from
the set up menu.

**Symmetry is not one of the fifteen.** Like the three workload types it sits on the Features pages
and is reached from the workout screen.

**This list is neither the 8.5UE arm ergometer's nor the 8.5S stepper's**, though all three share
the MED 8-series console. The 8.5UE lists thirteen - no YMCA Protocol and no Astrand Rhyming Test
(`85ue-2025-programs-thirteen-programs-on-the-touchscreen`); the 8.5S lists eleven with a Steps
Target (`85s-2025-programs-eleven-programs-on-the-touchscreen`). The 8.0T treadmill prints a
longer list again. Read each machine's list from its own book.

The 8.0U and 8.5R 2025 books print this chapter word for word; the 8.5R is a recumbent and its pages
run five later.
