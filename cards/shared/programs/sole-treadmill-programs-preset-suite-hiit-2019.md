This suite differs from the 2013-generation suite in exactly one preset: the fifth profile is renamed **HIIT** (was Interval); every other program, description and setup step is identical: see `sole-treadmill-programs-preset-suite-2013`.

The console has **ten built-in programs**: one **Manual** program, **five preset profiles (P1-P5)**, two **user defined programs (U1 and U2)** and two **Heart Rate programs (HR1 and HR2)**.

The five presets, as printed:

- **Hill.** "The Hill program simulates going up and down a hill. The workload will steadily increase and then decrease during the program."
- **Fat Burn.** "Designed, as the name implies, to maximize the burning of fat." A lower exertion level at a steady workload is best; "the absolute best way to burn fat is to keep your heart rate at around 60% to 70% of its maximum potential. This program does not use heart rate but simulates a lower, steady exertion workout."
- **Cardio.** "Designed to increase your cardiovascular function and endurance. This is exercise for your heart and lungs. It will build up your heart muscle and increase blood flow and lung capacity" with "a higher level of exertion with slight fluctuations in work."
- **Strength.** "Designed to increase muscular strength in your lower body. This program will steadily increase in workload to a high level and forces you to sustain it. This is designed to strengthen and tone your legs and glutes (muscles of the butt)."
- **HIIT.** "Takes you through high levels of intensity followed by periods of low intensity," depleting oxygen then recovering, which "aids in heart rate recovery from intense activities."

Preset profiles are drawn dot-matrix SPEED/INCLINE pictures, so there are no segment figures to quote. Setup is the menu-console setup on `sole-treadmill-programs-selecting-menu-2013` (age, weight, time, max-speed cap, 3-minute warm-up). Figures verified against the page render; the OCR layer mangles the dot profiles.
