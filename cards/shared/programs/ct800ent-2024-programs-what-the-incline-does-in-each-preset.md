These manuals describe **Speed and Incline separately for each preset program**. The button-console
manuals describe only the speed behaviour, so this half of the fact exists nowhere else.

- **Hill.** Incline: the deck elevation is a more gradual and sustained progression than the speed.
  **Maximum elevation is in the middle of the workout and lasts for 10% of the duration.**
- **Fat Burn.** Incline: a quick and sustained progression up to the maximum value (default or user
  input) for **90% of the workout duration**.
- **Cardio.** Incline: moderate, with **several elevation spikes at different points** of the
  workout.
- **Interval.** Incline: all of your lower extremity muscles are equally challenged throughout.
  **The incline alternates between 25% and 65% of maximum elevation.**
- **Manual and 5K / 10K Run.** Both speed and incline are controlled by the user.

The speed half of each description is the same text the button consoles print - see
`ct900ent-hill-program`, `ct900ent-fat-burn-program`, `ct900ent-cardio-program` and
`ct900ent-interval-program`. The printed segment chart of speed and incline values is on
`spirit-ct800-programs-preset-speed-incline-chart`.
