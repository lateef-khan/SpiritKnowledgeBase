Cardio presents a quick progression up to **near** the maximum resistance level - the default level,
or the level you entered. It has slight fluctuations up and down to let your heart rate elevate and
then recover repeatedly, before a quick cool down. The manual says this builds up your heart muscle
and increases blood flow and lung capacity.

All thirteen Spirit commercial bike owner's manuals print this description word for word. The CU800
2012 heads it `Resistance:` before the sentence.

**Cardio is a fixed resistance profile, not a heart rate program.** It does not read your pulse and
does not adjust to it. The program that holds a heart rate target is HR - see
`spirit-bike-programs-heart-rate-program-operation`.
