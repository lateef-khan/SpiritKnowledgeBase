**These two machines are the only Spirit strength products that give the exercise
instruction in words**, and the words are on the console, not in the book. The manual
reproduces the screen as a picture.

**How To Use** is a button on the console Home Screen, described there as *Learn how to
exercise on this device*. What else the Home Screen holds is
`spirit-strength-csi-console-home-screen`.

The **How To Use** screen shows, for each exercise: the exercise name, a front and back
muscle figure, an illustration of the movement, and one written instruction -

> Adjust the seat to a suitable position (vertical or slightly upward), adjust the mode
> and resistance, then sit on the bench.

**A machine with two exercises pages through them.** The chest press / shoulder press
console numbers its screens **1/2 Shoulder Press** and **2/2 Chest Press** with
**Next Exercise** and **Previous Exercise** buttons. The low row console shows one
exercise and no paging.

**That one sentence is the whole of the seat guidance.** Neither book prints a seat
height figure, a pad setting, a grip, a range of motion or a rep count anywhere; the
sets and reps come from the Strength Test instead.

**The manual's caption is broken.** It reads *"you can learn How to perform the exercise
buy selecting How To Use,"* - *buy* for *by*, and the sentence ends on a comma in both
books. Nothing is missing from the screen itself; the caption is simply unfinished.
