Manual means you control the workload, not the computer. 1. Press Manual, then Enter. 2-4. Enter Age, Weight and Time with the Incline or Level 5/6 keys (E98-2011: Incline or Resistance Up/Down), Enter each time; the E20 models in the E20/E25 book use the Level 5/6 keys throughout. 5. Press Start; Stop steps back one level. 6. You start at level one for warm-up; Level 5 raises, Level 6 lowers the load. 7. Display scrolls the Message Window. 8. At the end a workout summary shows briefly, then start-up returns.
