Touchscreen workouts run **warm-up and cool-down phases that may be skipped by pressing "Skip"** (gen-1 books print exactly that sentence). This is the opposite of the menu consoles, which have no cool-down phase at all: see `sole-treadmill-programs-no-cool-down-phase`.

Gen-2 detail: after the countdown the console enters workout mode (Stats/Charts/Track views, or Media). The physical **STOP** key pauses; from Pause, get back to running, **start a cool down**, or end and see the summary. **5 minutes idle in Pause ends the workout automatically.**
