All three CE850 owner's manuals say the elliptical trainer has **seven different programs** designed
for a variety of workouts.

**The 2016 manual contradicts itself in the very next sentence.** It says "The elliptical trainer has
seven different programs that have been designed for a variety of workouts. These **five** programs
have factory preset work level profiles for achieving different goals." The 2020 and 2022 manuals
say seven in both sentences. Nothing in any of the three reconciles the two counts.

None of the three prints seven descriptions. The 2016 manual prints five - Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio,
**Strength** and Interval. The 2020 and 2022 manuals print four - Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio and
Interval - with Strength and HIIT appearing only as captions on the strip of profile pictures.

The CE800, CE900 and CES880 manuals say **five**, not seven - see
`spirit-ce-programs-five-preset-work-level-profiles`.
