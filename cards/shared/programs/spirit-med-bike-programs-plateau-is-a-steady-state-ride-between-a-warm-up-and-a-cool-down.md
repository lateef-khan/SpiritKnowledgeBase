*"The Plateau program provides a steady state exercise with warm up and cool down periods."*

That one sentence is the whole description, printed identically in all seven books. **The manuals
give no length for the warm up or the cool down and no figure for the plateau level**: the level you
reach is the max level you enter at setup on the 4.0 bikes, and on the 7.0 bikes the peak level the
work keys set.

**No commercial Spirit bike has a Plateau program** - the CR/CU lineups use Fat Burn for a sustained
level. The 4.0T treadmill's Plateau (`40t-2026-programs-plateau`) is steady speed with varied grade; a
bike has no grade, so its Plateau is steady resistance.
