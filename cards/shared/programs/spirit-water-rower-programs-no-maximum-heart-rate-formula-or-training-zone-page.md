**Neither book prints one.** There is no *Heart Rate Monitoring* chapter, no **220 minus your age**
formula, no 50%-to-90% training zone paragraph, no Target Heart Rate Training Chart and no Rate of
Perceived Exertion scale in either manual.

**How this was checked.** Both whole manuals were searched on the loose words - *220*, *Borg*,
*perceived*, *exertion*, *maximum heart*, *training zone* - over the native PDF text **and** over the
appended OCR of every flat-image page (32 pages in the CRW900, 13 in the CRW800H2O). *Borg*,
*perceived*, *exertion* and *training zone* return **zero** hits in both. *220* returns only a fax
number and a part number `10220`. *Maximum heart* returns two hits, both inside the CRW900's own
Target Heart Rate program paragraph.

**What each machine gives you instead:**

- The **CRW900** computes the zone for you from your age and trains you between **65% and 85%** of it
  - see `crw900-2021-programs-target-heart-rate-set-by-age-between-65-and-85-percent`. The formula it
  uses is never printed.
- The **CRW800H2O** asks for a target in **beats** and holds you within **10%** of it - see
  `crw800h2o-programs-target-heart-rate-set-in-beats-defaulting-to-100`. It never mentions age or a
  maximum at all.

**The air rowers do print the missing pages**, and they do not agree with each other either: the
CRW800 gives a 40 year old **108 to 153** beats, the XRW600 **108 to 144**. Neither figure belongs on
a water rower.
