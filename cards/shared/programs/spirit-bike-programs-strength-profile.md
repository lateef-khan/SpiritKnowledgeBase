Strength has a gradual progression of resistance up to **100% of maximum effort**, sustained for
**25% of the workout duration**. The manual says this helps build strength and endurance in the
lower body and glutes. A cool down follows.

**Only three of the thirteen commercial bike manuals describe Strength**: the CU800 2012 and the two
touchscreen 900 machines, the CR900ENT and the CU900ENT. The CU800 2012 words the benefit as
"strength and **muscular** endurance" and calls the ending "a **brief** cool down"; the two ENT
manuals say "strength and endurance" and "Cool down follows". The figures are the same.

**The CR800 2021, CR800 2023 and CU800 2021 manuals name Strength but never describe it.** Their
preset chapter counts five programs and prints only four descriptions; "Strength" appears as the
fifth label in the profile-picture strip down the page margin. See
`spirit-bike-programs-five-presets-of-which-four-are-described`.

**The CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 manuals also count five presets and name no fifth program at all.**
The only place the word "strength" appears in either book is the heart rate page, in
"strengthening the cardio vascular system".

The CR900 2018, CU900 2018 and the three 800ENT bikes have no Strength program.
