HIIT takes you through high levels of intensity followed by periods of low intensity: endurance rises as your oxygen level depletes and replenishes, your cardiovascular system learns to use oxygen more efficiently, and the heart rate spikes between recovery periods aid heart rate recovery. **This is the older Interval program renamed** — same prose, new name — so an Interval card for another machine describes the same workout.
