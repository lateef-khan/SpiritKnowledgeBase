**This is the chart whose stage 2.1 begins at 1:15. The 4.0T manual prints a Gerkin chart whose stage 2.1
begins at 1:00 and whose speeds sit one quarter-stage earlier from stage 2.4 onward - see
`40t-2026-programs-gerkin-chart`. The two charts are not interchangeable.**

The Gerkin protocol, also known as the fireman's protocol, is a sub-max VO2 (volume of oxygen) test. The
test increases speed and elevation alternately until **85% of max heart rate is attained**. The time it
takes the heart rate to reach 85% determines the test score (VO2 Max).

in full.
