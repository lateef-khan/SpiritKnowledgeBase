**You must wear the heart rate transmitter strap for these programs.**

Both programs operate the same and are programmed the same way; the only difference is the target. **HR1 is set to 60% and HR2 is set to 80% of the maximum heart rate.** After you select your heart rate target the program attempts to keep you at or within **3 - 5 heart beats per minute** of that value. Follow the prompts in the Message Center to maintain your selected heart rate value.

1. **On the XT385**, press the **HR 1** key (60% of max heart rate default) or the **HR 2** key (80% of max heart rate default). **On the XT485 and XT685** there is one **HR 1-2** key: press it **once** for 60% or **twice** for 80%. Then press **Enter**.
2. The Message Center asks for your **Age**. Enter it with the Speed + or - keys, then press Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight** with the Speed + or - keys, then press Enter.
4. Adjust the **Time** with the Speed + or - keys, then press Enter.
5. Adjust the **Heart Rate Target** — the heart rate level you will strive to maintain during the program — with the Speed + or - keys, then press Enter. *The heart rate that appears is based on the % you accepted in step 1; changing this number increases or decreases that percentage.*
6. Press **Start** to begin your workout, or **Stop** to go back and modify your settings. *At any time during the editing of data you can press the Stop key to go back one screen.*
7. To increase or decrease the workload at any time during the program, press the **Incline + or -** key on the console or the **left handlebar**. This changes your target heart rate at any time during the program.
8. During the HR 1 or HR 2 programs you can scroll through the data in the Message Center by pressing **Enter**.

**On the XT285 the workload is changed from the Speed keys on the right handlebar instead**, and that manual adds a ninth step for restarting the program when it ends — see the linked card. The XT185 has no heart rate program at all.

**The 2010 XT685 also uses the Speed keys on the right handlebar, not the Incline keys on the left**, and it too prints a ninth step — one that additionally lets you save the finished workout to a User key. **The XT685 therefore changed between generations on this point.** See `xt685-2010-programs-heart-rate-program-speed-keys`; the two procedures are not interchangeable.
