**This is HR1, whose default is 60% of maximum heart rate. HR2 is the same program with a default of 80%.**

1. Press the **HR1** key. **There is no Enter press at this step** on these consoles.
2. During programming the menu displays **Age**. Enter it with the Level ▲/▼ keys **or the numeric key
   pad**, then press Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight** with the Level ▲/▼ keys or the numeric key pad, then Enter.
4. Enter the **Time**, then Enter.
5. Adjust your **Target Heart Rate** — the level you will strive to reach and maintain during the
   program. Then press Enter.
6. Press **Start** to begin. The manual says you can also go back and modify settings by pressing the
   **Enter** key, and its note in the same step says that at any time during data editing you can press
   **Stop** to go back one level or screen. Both sentences are printed; every other program in these
   manuals uses Stop to go back.
7. Press **Level ▲/▼** during the program to change your target heart rate at any time.
8. When the program ends press **Start** to run the same program again or **Stop** to exit.

**This program requires the use of the chest strap.** The manual prints that in the same section, and
adds that **an RPM signal must be received before the unit can determine the appropriate resistance
level** — so you have to be pedalling as well as wearing the strap.

These manuals call the pair the **HR programs**, not HRC, and they say 60% and 80% are the
**defaults** for HR1 and HR2 — wording the B94/R92 manuals do not use.
