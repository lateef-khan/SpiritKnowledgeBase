The CR900ENT and CU900ENT owner's manuals name the lineup in one sentence:

> The Spirit Fitness CR900ENT [CU900ENT] offers ten preset programs: **Manual, Hills, Fat Burn,
> Cardio, Strength, Interval, HR, Custom, Fitness Test, and Constant Power.**

**There is no HIIT program on either machine.** Searching both whole manuals for "HIIT", with
spacing tolerance, returns nothing.

**The same manual contradicts itself on the count.** The Exercise Programs page, describing the menu
that opens from the Home screen, says "There are **12** programs to choose from." The lineup
sentence six pages later says ten and names ten. The KB records both; the manual does not reconcile
them.

**The 800ENT touchscreen bikes have a different lineup** - ten programs with HIIT and without
Strength. See `spirit-bike-programs-ten-program-menu-on-the-touchscreen-800`.
