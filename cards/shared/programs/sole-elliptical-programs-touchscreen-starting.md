Decide on the setup page, then press 'Start This Program' on screen or the physical START key. Manual (Timer) runs user-controlled resistance and incline for the set time; presets scale every segment proportionally to the chosen Max Level. During the workout switch Stats/Charts/Track views (tap a number in Stats to choose parameters) or visit Media; use the physical keys for level/incline. STOP pauses (auto-ends after 5 idle minutes); resume, finish to the summary, or — E95s/E98 only — start a cool down.
