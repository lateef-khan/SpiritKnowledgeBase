**Four printings of the same two consoles open the Preset Programs chapter with four different
counts.** Recorded as printed:

| Manual | Opening sentence |
|---|---|
| **CS800 2021** | *"The stepper has **ten** different programs that have been designed for a variety of workouts. These **ten** programs have factory preset work level profiles for achieving different goals."* |
| **CRS800S 2021** | *"The Semi-Recumbent stepper has **seven** different programs that have been designed for a variety of workouts. These **five** programs have factory preset work level profiles for achieving different goals."* |
| **CS800 2024** | *"The fitness Stepper has **five** different programs..."* |
| **CRS800S 2024** | *"The fitness Stepper has **five** different programs..."* |

**The CRS800S 2021 sentence contradicts itself inside two lines** - seven, then five.

**What is actually on the machines.** Both consoles reach every program through one **Program**
button, and both hold:

- **five preset profiles** - Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, Interval;
- **Manual**;
- **Custom** - one on the CS800, two on the CRS800S;
- **two Heart Rate programs**, 65% and 80%;
- **HIIT**, on the CS800 only.

That is **ten** on the CS800 (counting its one Custom) and **ten** on the CRS800S (counting its
two Customs and no HIIT). The 2021 CS800's "ten" is the only figure in the four that matches a real
key count; "five" counts the preset profiles alone, and "seven" matches nothing.

The heart rate training page in all four books adds a fourth number, saying the pulse readout may be
used *"during any of the **nine** different programs"* - see
`spirit-stepper-programs-target-zone-of-108-to-153-beats`.

**Answer a "how many programs" question with the key list, not with the manual's sentence.**
