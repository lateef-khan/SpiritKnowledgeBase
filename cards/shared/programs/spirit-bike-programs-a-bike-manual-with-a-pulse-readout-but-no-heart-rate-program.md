**The XBR25 2023 owner's manual describes no heart rate program.** Its Programs chapter runs Programs
Intro, Preset (Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, HIIT, Fusion), Fusion, Countdown Mode and Custom
Workout Program, and then goes straight to Maintenance & Care. There is no HR key, no target
percentage and no HR setup procedure.

**It also prints none of the heart rate training pages** its 2023 stablemates carry: no maximum heart
rate formula, no 60%/80% training zone page, no Borg / rate of perceived exertion scale, and no chest
strap chapter. The XBR55, XBU55 and XBR95 books of the same year print all four.

**The console still reads a pulse.** The Features page says the recumbent bike has a built-in heart
rate monitoring system, that grasping the hand pulse sensors or wearing a heart rate transmitter
starts the Heart Icon blinking, and that the Pulse window shows beats per minute. The workout screen
has a Current Heart Rate readout.

**Its Features page points at a section that is not in the book.** "grasping the hand pulse sensors on
the front cross bar or wearing the heart rate transmitter (**see Using Heart Rate Transmitter
section**)" - there is no such section, and no such entry in the table of contents. The XBR55, XBU55
and XBR95 books of the same year do have one.

**Two more sentences have nothing to run on.** "You may not use the Grip Pulse feature while
in Heart Rate Programs" and "Entering your Age is necessary during the Heart Rate programs" are
carried over from the XBR55 book; this machine has no heart rate program for either to apply to.

**How the absence was checked.** The table of contents lists only two Programs entries and no heart
rate entry. Loose searches of the whole extracted text for "heart", "220", "zone", "Borg", "perceived"
and "strap" return only the Features and safety mentions above. Every PDF page from 23 to 29 was
counted for native words - the lowest is 185 and the Programs pages run 191 to 541 - so no page in the
range is a flattened image hiding a section.
