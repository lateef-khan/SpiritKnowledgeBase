1. Attach the transmitter to the elastic strap using the locking parts.
2. Adjust the strap as tightly as possible while remaining comfortable.
3. Position the transmitter with the logo centred in the middle of your body, facing away from your chest. Some people must position it slightly left of centre. Secure the strap around your chest with the locking parts.
4. Position the transmitter **immediately below the pectoral muscles**.
5. Sweat is the best conductor. Plain water can also be used to pre-wet the electrodes — the 2 ribbed oval areas on the reverse side of the belt and both sides of the transmitter. Wearing the strap a few minutes before your workout is recommended. Some users find a strong steady signal difficult at first; after "warming up" this lessens. Wearing clothing over the transmitter does not affect performance.
6. Stay within range of the console for a strong steady signal. Wearing the transmitter against bare skin assures proper operation. If worn over a shirt, moisten the areas of the shirt the electrodes rest on.

The transmitter activates automatically when it detects heart activity, and deactivates when it does not. It is water resistant, but **dry it completely after use** — moisture can create false signals and shortens battery life.
