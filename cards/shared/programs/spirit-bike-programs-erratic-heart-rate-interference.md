**Do not use the bike for Heart Rate programs unless a steady, solid actual heart rate value is
being displayed.** High, wild, random numbers indicate a problem.

Areas to look for interference:

1. The bike is not properly grounded.
2. Microwave ovens, TVs, small appliances.
3. Fluorescent lights.
4. Some household security systems.
5. A perimeter fence for a pet.
6. Some people's skin gives the transmitter trouble. Try wearing the transmitter **upside down** -
   normally it is worn with the Spirit Fitness logo right side up.
7. The antenna is very sensitive. With an outside noise source, **turning the whole machine 90
   degrees** may de-tune the interference.
8. Another individual wearing a transmitter within **3 feet** of your machine's console.

If problems continue, contact Spirit Fitness.

**Only five of the ten manuals print item 1.** The grounding item heads the list in the
CU800ENT-2022, CR800ENT-2023, CU800ENT-2024, CR900-2025 and CU900-2025 manuals. **The CU800-2012,
CR800-2021, CR800-2023 and CU800-2021 manuals print a seven-item list that begins at microwave
ovens** and never mentions grounding. Do not tell an owner of one of those four that their manual
names it - it does not, and everything above is then numbered one lower.

**On the CR900-2025 and CU900-2025 the grounding item is printed but cannot be acted on.** Those two
bikes are self-powered and have no mains plug, no power cord and no grounding conductor
(`spirit-bike-safety-no-mains-outlet-needed`), so there is nothing an owner can ground. It is
carried over from the mains-powered manuals; treat items 2 onwards as the actionable list there.

**The pet fence is named two ways.** `Electric fence` in the CR800-2021, CR800-2023, CU800-2021,
CU800ENT-2022 and CR800ENT-2023 manuals, `Perimeter fence` in the CU800-2012, CU800ENT-2024,
CR900-2025, CU900-2025 and AB950-2024. And the closing line is `contact your dealer` in the older manuals,
`contact Spirit Fitness` in the CU800ENT-2024, CR900-2025, CU900-2025 and AB950-2024.

**The CU800 2012 prints the same list with the wrong machine name**: its caution reads "Do not use
this **elliptical trainer** for Heart Rate programs", in both scans of the manual. It is a bike.

**The AB950-2024 air bike prints the seven-item version and names its own machine correctly**: "Do
not use this **Air Bike** for Heart Rate programs unless a steady, solid Actual Heart Rate value is
being displayed." Its grounding item is absent, so on that machine the list starts at microwave
ovens.

**The CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900ENT and CU900ENT print no such warning page.** **Nor does the
AB900 2018 air bike** - see `spirit-bike-programs-air-bike-2018-prints-no-heart-rate-training-page`.

**Thirteen residential XBR and XBU manuals print this page**, and they split over item 1 the same way
the commercial books do.

- **Eight-item list, headed by grounding:** XBR55 2019, XBR55 2021 and XBR55ENT 2021. Their item 1
  reads **"Treadmill not properly grounded - Proper grounding is a must!"** - the word *treadmill*,
  on a bike. It is carried over from a treadmill manual; read it as "this machine".
- **Seven-item list, starting at microwave ovens:** XBR95 2016/2018/2021 and 2023, XBR25 2019,
  XBU55 2019/2021/2023, XBR55 2023 and XBU55ENT 2021. **The XBU55ENT drops the grounding item that
  its XBR55ENT twin keeps.** Measured at word level, those two pages agree at **92.2%**, and the only
  differences are that one word and the machine name - Recumbent Bike against Upright Bike.

All thirteen name the pet fence a **Perimeter fence**. The closing line is **"contact your dealer"**
in the ten older books and **"contact Spirit Fitness"** in the four 2023 books. Every one of the
thirteen names its own machine correctly in the caution - Recumbent Bike, Semi-Recumbent Bike or
Upright Bike - unlike the CU800 2012, which calls itself an elliptical trainer.

**The XBR25 2023 prints no such page.** Its manual has no chest strap chapter and no heart rate
program - see `spirit-bike-programs-a-bike-manual-with-a-pulse-readout-but-no-heart-rate-program`.
