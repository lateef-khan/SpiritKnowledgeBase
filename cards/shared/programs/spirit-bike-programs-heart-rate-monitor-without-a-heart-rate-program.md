**You may use the heart rate monitor feature without using a Heart Rate program.** The pulse readout
works in Manual and in every other program.

**What the manuals then say a Heart Rate program adjusts is not consistent, and two of the wordings
are wrong for a bike.**

| Manual | Printed wording |
|---|---|
| CU800ENT 2022, CR800ENT 2023 | "the machine will automatically adjust **resistance** to maintain the desired heart rate" |
| CR800 2021, CR800 2023, CU800 2021, CU800ENT 2024 | "the machine will automatically adjust **speed or incline** to maintain the desired heart rate" |
| CU800 2012 | "The Heart Rate program automatically controls **resistance at the pedals**" |
| CR900 2025, CU900 2025 | no adjustment sentence; instead "This function can be used during manual mode or during any of the **nine different programs**" |

**"Speed or incline" is treadmill boilerplate.** A Spirit commercial bike has neither: what the
Heart Rate program changes is the resistance. Four manuals print it anyway.

**"Nine different programs" is also boilerplate.** The CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 count **five**
presets elsewhere in the same book, and the same sentence appears in Spirit elliptical and treadmill
manuals whose own program counts differ too. The CU800 2012 prints both the "nine different
programs" sentence and the correct resistance sentence together.

The subject of the sentence also varies: "all Spirit Fitness machines" on the CR800 2021, CR800 2023
and CU800 2021, "all Spirit Fitness **Upright** Bikes" on the CU800ENT 2022 and CU800ENT 2024, and
"all Spirit Fitness **Recumbent** Bikes" on the CR800ENT 2023.

**The CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900ENT and CU900ENT print no version of this sentence** - they have
no training-zone page for it to sit on.
