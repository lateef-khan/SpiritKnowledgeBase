**You may use the heart rate monitor feature without using a Heart Rate program.** The pulse readout
works in Manual and in every other program.

**What the manuals then say a Heart Rate program adjusts is not consistent, and two of the wordings
are wrong for a bike.**

| Manual | Printed wording |
|---|---|
| CU800ENT 2022, CR800ENT 2023 | "the machine will automatically adjust **resistance** to maintain the desired heart rate" |
| CR800 2021, CR800 2023, CU800 2021, CU800ENT 2024 | "the machine will automatically adjust **speed or incline** to maintain the desired heart rate" |
| CU800 2012 | "The Heart Rate program automatically controls **resistance at the pedals**" |
| CR900 2025, CU900 2025, AB950 2024 | no adjustment sentence; instead "This function can be used during manual mode or during any of the **nine different programs**" |

**"Speed or incline" is treadmill boilerplate.** A Spirit commercial bike has neither: what the
Heart Rate program changes is the resistance. Four manuals print it anyway.

**"Nine different programs" is also boilerplate.** The CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 count **five**
presets elsewhere in the same book, and **the AB950 2024 air bike has no manual mode at all and
eight console modes, not nine** - see
`spirit-bike-programs-air-bike-eight-console-modes-and-no-workout-profiles`; and the same sentence appears in Spirit elliptical and treadmill
manuals whose own program counts differ too. The CU800 2012 prints both the "nine different
programs" sentence and the correct resistance sentence together.

The subject of the sentence also varies: "all Spirit Fitness machines" on the CR800 2021, CR800 2023
and CU800 2021, "all Spirit Fitness **Upright** Bikes" on the CU800ENT 2022 and CU800ENT 2024, and
"all Spirit Fitness **Recumbent** Bikes" on the CR800ENT 2023.

**The CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900ENT and CU900ENT print no version of this sentence** - they have
no training-zone page for it to sit on. **Nor does the AB900 2018 air bike.**

**On the AB950 the first half of the card still holds.** Its Quick Start page says the console shows
all data "including Heart Rate (if a heart rate chest strap is detected)", so the pulse readout does
work outside Target Heart Rate Mode.

**Thirteen residential XBR and XBU manuals print the same sentence**: you may use the heart rate
monitor feature without using the Heart Rate program, during manual mode or during any of the **nine
different programs**, and the Heart Rate program automatically controls resistance at the pedals.

**"Nine" is wrong on every one of the thirteen.** The XBR25 2019 console carries six programs, the
XBR55 and XBU55 LED consoles ten, the XBR95 LED consoles twelve, and the two ENT touchscreens eight.
No residential XBR or XBU manual describes nine. Treat the number as boilerplate and the sentence -
the pulse readout works outside the heart rate program - as the fact.

**Ten of the thirteen also say the opposite about the grips.** The XBR55, XBU55 and 2023 books print
"You may not use the Grip Pulse feature while in Heart Rate Programs" on the console page, so the
readout is available in every program *except* a heart rate program, where the strap is required.

**The service manuals split the same way, and put the two sentences within one book family.** The
CR800 "(2020)" and CU800 "(2020)" service manuals say "You **may** use the Grip Pulse feature while
in Heart Rate Control", and the CU800ENT 2022 and CR800ENT 2023 service manuals say "You may use the
hand pulse sensors while in Heart Rate Control"; the XBR95 2023 service manual says "You may **not**
use the Grip Pulse feature while in Heart Rate Programs". None of the service manuals prints the
"without using a Heart Rate program" sentence itself, and neither does the AB900 2018 service
manual. The CU800 "(2020)" service manual's paragraph also names the wrong machine - "The CR800 will
also pick up wireless heart rate transmitters" - a carry-over from the recumbent book.

**The XBR25 2023 prints the grip lockout sentence but has no heart rate program to be locked out
of** - see `spirit-bike-programs-a-bike-manual-with-a-pulse-readout-but-no-heart-rate-program`.

**The 4.0R and 4.0U medical bikes add two more rows to the table above.** Their 2025 books say "With
all Spirit Fitness Recumbent Bikes you may use the heart rate monitor feature without using the Heart
Rate program" - the 4.0U, an upright, says Recumbent too - and close with the treadmill boilerplate
"the machine will automatically adjust speed or incline". Their Dyaco PT 4.0 R and 4.0 U editions say
"With all Heart Rate programs you may use the heart rate monitor feature without using the Heart Rate
program. This function can be used during manual mode or during any other different programs. The
heart rate program automatically controls resistance at the pedals" - the sentence that fits a bike,
and no count of programs.
