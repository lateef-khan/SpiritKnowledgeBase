**This is the seven-item, one-metre list the three Dyaco editions print.** The Spirit 2025 books for
the same bikes print eight items, beginning with grounding, and say three feet - see
`spirit-bike-programs-erratic-heart-rate-interference`.

**Do not use the bike for heart rate control unless a steady, solid actual heart rate value is being
displayed.** High, wild, random numbers indicate a problem. Areas to look at for interference:

1. Microwave ovens, TVs, small appliances.
2. Fluorescent lights.
3. Some household security systems.
4. A perimeter fence for a pet.
5. Some people's skin gives the transmitter trouble - try wearing it **upside down**; normally the
   logo is right side up.
6. The antenna is very sensitive; with an outside noise source, **turning the whole machine 90
   degrees** may de-tune the interference.
7. **Another person wearing a chest strap within 1 meter will interfere.**

If you continue to experience problems contact your dealer.

**No grounding item, and rightly so on the 4.0**: the PT 4.0 R and 4.0 U are generator-powered with no
mains plug. The MED 7.0R 2021 does plug in, and its list omits the item too. The 7.0S/7.5S steppers'
Dyaco-era books print this same seven-item list
(`spirit-rehab-stepper-programs-erratic-heart-rate-seven-sources-and-a-one-metre-rule`).
