The Interval program takes you through high levels of intensity followed by periods of low intensity. It increases endurance by depleting your oxygen level followed by periods of recovery to replenish oxygen, training your cardiovascular system to use oxygen more efficiently; the spikes in heart rate between recovery periods also aid heart rate recovery from intense activities.

2019 and later button-console books rename this program HIIT with the same prose.
