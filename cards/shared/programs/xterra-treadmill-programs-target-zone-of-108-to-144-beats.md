**Maximum Heart Rate (MHR) = 220 minus your age.**

- Your heart rate training zone is **50% to 90%** of your maximum heart rate.
- **60%** of your MHR is recommended for burning fat (the TRX2500, TRX3500 and TRX4500 books:
  "is the zone that burns fat").
- **80%** is recommended for strengthening the cardiovascular system.
- 60% to 80% "is the zone to stay in for maximum benefit" (every book except the TR300, TR6.4
  and TR6.6, which say the goal "can be achieved by training between 60% and 80% of your MHR").

**Worked example for a 40-year-old, as printed:**

```
220 - 40 = 180 (maximum heart rate)
180 x .6 = 108 beats per minute (60% of maximum)
180 X .8 = 144 beats per minute (80% of maximum)
```

So for a 40-year-old the training zone would be **108 to 144 beats per minute**.

"If you enter your age during programming the console will perform this calculation
automatically" - printed in the TR6.6 book and in every new-layout book (TRX1400, TR65, TR75,
TR85, TR95H, TR75H, WS200, WS300), which add "Entering your age is used for the Heart Rate
programs". The WS200 and WS300 have no heart-rate program to use it.

**Consult your physician before participating in any exercise program.**

**The chart is a picture.** The text refers to "the black columns on the chart" (MHR by age,
20 to 65) with two diagonal lines for weight loss and cardiovascular training. The columns and
lines carry no printed values beyond the axis (90 to 190 bpm, ages 20 to 65).

**Leftover wording:** the eight new-layout books say "With this **Spirit Fitness** unit you may
use the heart rate monitor feature without using the Heart Rate program" - the paragraph was
carried over from a Spirit manual and names the wrong brand. When the monitor is used with the
heart rate programs, "the machine will automatically adjust speed or incline to maintain the
desired heart rate".

The TRX5500 prints this page with **65% and 85%** (117 to 153 beats) instead; see
`not_to_be_confused_with`. The TR95H's own heart rate programs use heart rate reserve and
226 minus age for women, on a different page; see `see_also`.
