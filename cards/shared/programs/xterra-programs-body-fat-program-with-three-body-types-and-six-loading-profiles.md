The body fat program (program **23** on the SB150 and FS150, **24** on the SB250, EU150 and
SB240) "calculate[s] users' body fat ratio and ... offer[s] a specific loading profile".
Three body types are divided by the FAT% calculated:

| Type | Body fat |
|---|---|
| 1 | > 27% |
| 2 | 20% to 27% |
| 3 | < 20% |

1. Select BODY FAT with UP/DOWN; press ENTER.
2. **HEIGHT** flashes - set it (default **170 cm or 5'07"**), press ENTER.
3. **WEIGHT** flashes - set it (default **70 kg or 155 lb**), press ENTER.
4. **GENDER** flashes - **1 = male, 0 = female** (default 1), press ENTER.
5. **AGE** flashes - set it (default **35**), press ENTER.
6. Press START/STOP to begin the measurement with both hands on the grips. If the window
   shows **"E"**, your hands are not holding the grips well (or the chest belt is not
   touching properly) - fix that and press START/STOP again.
7. The display shows FAT PERCENT (the function list adds BMI and BMR).
8. Press START/STOP to begin exercise.

**One of six profiles then runs automatically**, printed as pictures with a workout time
under each. Filled cells per column on the ten-column, eight-row grid, read from a render:

| Row | 40 minutes | 40 minutes | 20 minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| upper | 2 3 5 5 5 5 4 3 3 2 | 2 4 4 6 6 6 6 4 4 2 | 3 5 5 7 8 8 7 5 4 3 |
| lower | 1 2 4 4 4 4 3 2 2 1 | 1 3 3 5 5 5 5 3 3 1 | 1 3 3 5 6 6 5 3 2 1 |

The books do not say which profile goes with which body type or gender.

**A misprint:** the SB250 heading says program 24 but its step 1 says "select the BODY FAT
(P22) program"; P22 is the Target H.R.C., and the program list and the other four books say
24, so 24 is right.
