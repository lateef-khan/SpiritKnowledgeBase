**One HR key, and it defaults to 60% of your projected maximum heart rate.** The XBR25 2019 manual
adds the reasoning: **60-80% is more conducive to fat loss goals, and 81-100% is generally used for
cardiovascular conditioning and high intensity interval training.**

There is no HR1 and no HR2 on this console. Its key strip reads MANUAL, CARDIO, HILL, FATBURN,
INTERVAL, HR, and its badge counts six programs.

**The manual contradicts its own console on this page.** Step 1 of the procedure reads "Press the **HR
1 or HR 2** key then press the Enter key", carried over unchanged from the two-key XBR55 and XBU55
books of the same year. Press the single HR key; there is no second one to choose.

**You must wear a heart rate transmitter strap (sold separately) for this program**, and after you set
the target the console tries to hold you within **3 to 5 beats per minute** of it - see
`spirit-bike-programs-heart-rate-program-operation` for the full step list.

**The XBR25 2023 dropped the heart rate program entirely** - see
`spirit-bike-programs-a-bike-manual-with-a-pulse-readout-but-no-heart-rate-program`.

**The Spirit XE195 elliptical prints this same paragraph.** Different product line, separate card -
see `xe195-2021-programs-single-heart-rate-key-defaults-to-sixty-percent`.
