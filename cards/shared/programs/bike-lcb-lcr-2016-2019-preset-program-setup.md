Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength and the fifth preset are all programmed the same way.

1. Press the desired program button. **There is no Enter press at this step** — that is the
   difference from the 2011 and 2013 consoles and from the B94/R92 consoles.
2. During the programming stage the menu displays **Age**. Adjust with the Level ▲/▼ keys, then
   press Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight** with the Level ▲/▼ keys, then Enter.
4. Enter the **Time**, then Enter.
5. Adjust the **Max Level** — the peak exertion level you will experience during the program, the
   highest coloured segment or box of the program profile. Then press Enter.
6. Press **Start** to begin. Press **Stop** to go back one level or screen and modify settings.
7. Press the **Level ▲/▼** keys at any time during the program to increase or decrease the resistance.
8. Pressing the **DISPLAY** key changes the displayed information.
9. When the program ends the window shows a workout summary for a short time, then the console
   returns to the start-up display.

Step 8 is printed as "When you workout in **Manual** program mode, pressing the DISPLAY key will
change the display information" even though it appears inside the preset-program procedure. That
wording is the manual's, not a transcription slip.
