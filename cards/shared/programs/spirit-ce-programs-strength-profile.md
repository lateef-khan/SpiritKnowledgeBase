Strength has a gradual progression of resistance up to **100% of maximum effort**, sustained for
**25% of the workout duration**. The manuals say this helps build strength and muscular endurance in
the lower body and glutes. A brief cool down follows.

**Only three of the ten manuals print this description** - the CE800 2013, the CE850 2016 and the
CE900 2021. The CE800 2021, CE800 2023, CE850 2020, CE850 2022, CE900 2025 and CES880 2025 manuals
show "Strength" only as a caption on the strip of profile pictures and print no text beside it. The
CE800ENT has no Strength program at all: its touchscreen program menu lists ten programs and
Strength is not one of them.

The profile picture that goes with it is a printed image in every manual, so the KB does not hold
its column values.
