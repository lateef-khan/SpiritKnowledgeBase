**Resistance Level 1 to 15.** Elastic behaves like rubber tubing: easy at the beginning
and harder and harder as it stretches out. The manual adds that at the top of a level it
feels like hitting a wall, and pressing further turns the effort into **isometrics**. It
recommends the mode for building joint integrity and calls it ideal for the older
population.

**A first-time user is told to start below level 15** to get comfortable with the machine
and the technique.

Set the level on the Setup Screen, press the blue **BEGIN** button, then adjust the level
mid-set by pressing another Resistance Level. **See Chart** gives a **Line Graph**
comparing the pounds of force and the time of the current repetition against the previous
one, as Isokinetic does. Press **End Set** when the repetitions are done.

**The prose and the screenshot disagree about the top of the scale.** The text states a
range of 1 to 15, and the screenshot shows a value of 15 - but the slider under it is
ticked 0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, so the drawn scale runs past 15. The prose is what the
manual states; the screen picture is a mock-up. Quote 1 to 15.
