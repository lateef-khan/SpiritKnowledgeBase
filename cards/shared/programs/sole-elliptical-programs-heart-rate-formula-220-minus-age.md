Maximum Heart Rate is 220 minus your age; the training zone is 50% to 90% of it — 60% burns fat, 80% strengthens the cardiovascular system. Example printed in every book: age 40 → MHR 180 → 108 bpm (60%) to 144 bpm (80%). Entering your age lets the console do the math automatically. The E20 books say 'predicted' Maximum Heart Rate with the same numbers. A printed chart maps both goal lines by age; train on a physician-approved schedule.
