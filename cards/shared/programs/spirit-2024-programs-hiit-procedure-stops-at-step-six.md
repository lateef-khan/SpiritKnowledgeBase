**The HIIT page in these three manuals ends at step 6**, immediately after setting the Sprint and
Rest times. Steps 7 to 10, which every other Spirit LED console prints, are simply not there. The
page was checked against the native PDF text and a render; nothing is hidden in an image, and the
rest of the page is blank.

What the three manuals **do** print (steps 1-6):

1. Choose **HIIT** with the Program button, then Enter, and enter your **Age**.
2. Enter your **Weight**.
3. Enter the **number of intervals**. **The default is 10 and the range available is 3 to 15.** One
   interval equals 1 Sprint and 1 Rest segment.
4. Set the **Sprint Level** - the resistance level during the Sprint segments.
5. Set the **Rest Level** - the resistance level during the Rest segments.
6. Set the **Interval time**. The display shows **Sprint Time:30** and **Rest Time:30**; adjust the
   **Sprint time from 20 to 60 seconds**, press Enter, then adjust the Rest time.

What is **missing**, and is therefore not stated for these three machines:

- that the display then shows the total HIIT time and you press Start;
- the **3-minute warm-up at resistance level 5**;
- that the Dot Matrix Message Center becomes a speed display with a **90 pedal rpm** target LED;
- that Rest segments sit at **resistance level 5**;
- the **2-minute cool-down** at the end of the last Sprint.

**The 2024 CE850 and CS800 print all of it.** The missing half is on
`spirit-ce800-programs-hiit-two-minute-cool-down` and `spirit-stepper-programs-hiit-on-the-stepper`,
taken from the manuals that do print it. It is very likely the same behaviour on these three
machines, but their own books do not say so, and a card must not claim they do.

*The 2021 and 2023 CE800, CR800 and CU800 manuals - the machines these replace - print the full
procedure. The truncation is new in the 2024 printing.*
