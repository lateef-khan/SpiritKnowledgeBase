**This is the 90%/70% zone family, not the 60%/80% one.** Maximum Heart Rate is **220 minus age** (auto-computed when age is entered; also feeds the heart-rate bar graph on F83/F85/S73/S77 models). Training at **90%** of maximum serves **cardiovascular fitness**; **70%** serves **weight loss** — on a schedule approved by a physician; consult one before starting.

The books print an MHR-by-age chart with two diagonal training lines (image only). The monitor works without the control program in manual mode or any of the nine programs; the control program itself drives incline (speed and elevation on most of these consoles — see `sole-treadmill-programs-hrc-70-90-autopilot` — incline only on the TT8 consoles, see `sole-treadmill-programs-hrc-70-90-incline-only`). The H-1/H-2 target caution applies: suggestion only for normal healthy individuals; a higher entered age lowers the target.

The 60%/80% zone with the worked 108-144 example is on `f63-2026-target-heart-rate-zone`; the 2009-2010 80%/60% rewording on `sole-treadmill-programs-target-zone-w-series`.
