Two charts. **Which one applies is decided by the Male/Female choice you made at the start of the
test**, and the manual's rule is that a very de-conditioned male uses the female chart while a very
conditioned female uses the male chart.

**VO2max chart for males and very fit females**

| | 18-25 years old | 26-35 years old | 36-45 years old | 46-55 years old | 56-65 years old | 65+ years old |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| excellent | >60 | >56 | >51 | >45 | >41 | >37 |
| good | 52-60 | 49-56 | 43-51 | 39-45 | 36-41 | 33-37 |
| above average | 47-51 | 43-48 | 39-42 | 35-38 | 32-35 | 29-32 |
| average | 42-46 | 40-42 | 35-38 | 32-35 | 30-31 | 26-28 |
| below average | 37-41 | 35-39 | 31-34 | 29-31 | 26-29 | 22-25 |
| poor | 30-36 | 30-34 | 26-30 | 25-28 | 22-25 | 20-21 |
| very poor | <30 | <30 | <26 | <25 | <22 | <20 |

**VO2max chart for females and de-conditioned males**

| | 18-25 years old | 26-35 years old | 36-45 years old | 46-55 years old | 56-65 years old | 65+ years old |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| excellent | 56 | 52 | 45 | 40 | 37 | 32 |
| good | 47-56 | 45-52 | 38-45 | 34-40 | 32-37 | 28-32 |
| above average | 42-46 | 39-44 | 34-37 | 31-33 | 28-31 | 25-27 |
| average | 38-41 | 35-38 | 31-33 | 28-30 | 25-27 | 22-24 |
| below average | 33-37 | 31-34 | 27-30 | 25-27 | 22-24 | 19-22 |
| poor | 28-32 | 26-30 | 22-26 | 20-24 | 18-21 | 17-18 |
| very poor | <28 | <26 | <22 | <20 | <18 | <17 |

The "excellent" row of the second chart is printed as a bare number where every other cell in both
charts is a range or a ">"/"<" bound. That is how the manual prints it.

**These charts exist only in the 2011 and 2013 manuals.** The 2016 and 2019 LCB/LCR manuals state
that their fitness test score is **not** a VO2max number and print no chart at all.
