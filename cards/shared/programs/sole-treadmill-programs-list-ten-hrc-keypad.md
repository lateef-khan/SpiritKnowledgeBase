**"The New SOLE Series of treadmills offer five preset programs, two user defined programs, two heart rate control programs and one Manual program"** — ten in all, with ten dedicated program keys.

The presets are **not named** in the text. Each shows its preset top speed when chosen so the top speed can be capped. Setup is on `sole-treadmill-programs-selecting-keypad-hrc`, user setup on `sole-treadmill-programs-user-basic-keypad`. The two heart rate programs differ by era: 70%/90% on `sole-treadmill-programs-hrc-70-90-autopilot` and its incline-only twin, 60%/80% on `sole-treadmill-programs-hrc-60-80-autopilot-wf80`.
