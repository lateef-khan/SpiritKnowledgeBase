**The running belt moves freely in Free Mode. "User may fall if not paying attention."** Pulling the safety key stops the belt immediately; re-attach it to resume Free Mode.

1. Select Free Mode to enter it.
2. The belt moves freely — **hold the hand rail and start running**.
3. Incline adjusts up **0 to 8 level**.
4. Press Stop and **wait for the incline to come down to 0**, then exit Free Mode.

The ST90-2023 Home screen shortcuts Free Mode next to Start. No other A2 treadmill prints this program.
