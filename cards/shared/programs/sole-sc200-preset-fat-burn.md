The Fat Burn program is designed to maximize the burning of fat: a lower exertion level held at a steady workload, ideally keeping the heart rate at around **60% to 70%** of its maximum potential.

**This program does not use heart rate.** It simulates a lower, steady exertion workout instead.

**About the profile chart.** The manual prints the work level profile as a dot-matrix picture with **no printed level values**. Read from the page image, its shape is a low flat plateau across the middle of the grid. On the SC300 each preset also carries a STEP HEIGHT chart, which has its own card.
