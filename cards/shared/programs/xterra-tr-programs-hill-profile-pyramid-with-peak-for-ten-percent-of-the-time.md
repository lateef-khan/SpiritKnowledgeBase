**Hill** "follows a triangle or pyramid type of gradual progression from approximately **10%
of maximum effort** (the level that you chose before starting this program) up to a
**maximum effort which lasts for 10% of the total workout time**, then a gradual regression
of resistance back to approximately 10% of maximum effort."

**Incline:** "The deck elevation is a more gradual and sustained progression. **Maximum
elevation is in the middle of the workout and lasts for 10% of the duration.**"

The book pictures the program as a **Speed** and an **Incline** dot-matrix profile with **no
figures**; the words below are the whole of what it prints. The maximum speed and incline
the program reaches are shown in the Message Center when it is chosen and can be lowered
during setup.

Sole treadmills print the same Hill description with a percentage table; see `see_also`.
