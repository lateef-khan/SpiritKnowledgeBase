1. Choose **Custom** with the Program button and press **Enter**.
2. Enter your **name**. The letter "A" blinks; Up moves to "B", Down moves to "Z". Press Enter for
   each letter. **Maximum 7 characters.** Press **Stop** when finished.
3. If a program is already stored in Custom you are asked **"Run Program?"** - Up and Down select
   Yes or No. Choosing No then asks whether to delete the saved program. **You must delete the
   current program to build a new one.**
4. Enter your **Age**, then Enter.
5. Enter your **Weight**, then Enter.
6. Enter the **Time**, then Enter.
7. Enter the **Max Resistance Level** of the program, then Enter.
8. The first column blinks. Set the resistance level for **SEGMENT > 1** with the Up button, then
   Enter.
9. Each next segment starts at the level of the one before. Repeat until **all twenty-four
   segments** have been set.
10. The display says **"New Program Saved"** and offers to start or modify the program. Stop exits
    to the start-up screen.

**Only these three LED manuals have a Custom program.** The CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900 2025 and
CU900 2025 have none - searching those four whole manuals for "custom" returns only "each of the
programs can be **customized** with your personal information" and "HIIT is a fully
**customizable** interval training program".

**The touchscreen bikes store twenty segments, not twenty-four**, and take no name - see
`spirit-bike-programs-custom-twenty-segments-on-the-touchscreen`. **The CU800 2012 stores twenty
segments under a facility name** - see
`cu800-2012-programs-custom-twenty-segments-saved-under-a-facility-name`.
