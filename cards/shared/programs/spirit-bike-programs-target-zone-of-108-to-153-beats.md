First work out your **Maximum Heart Rate**: **220 minus your age**. Your heart rate training zone is
**50% to 90%** of that maximum.

**These three manuals print 60% and 85%.** 60% of your MHR is recommended for burning fat while 85%
is recommended for strengthening the cardiovascular system, and 60% to 85% is the zone to stay in
for maximum benefit.

Worked example for someone **40 years old**:

- 220 - 40 = **180** (maximum heart rate)
- 180 x .6 = **108** beats per minute (60% of maximum)
- 180 x .85 = **153** beats per minute (85% of maximum)
- So for a 40 year old the training zone would be **108 to 153** beats per minute.

If you enter your age during programming the console performs this calculation automatically.

**These same three manuals name their two heart rate programs 65% and 80%**, which neither figure on
this page matches. See `spirit-bike-programs-heart-rate-keys-of-sixty-five-and-eighty-percent`.

**Three other figure pairs appear across the thirteen commercial bike manuals.** The CU800 2012
prints 60%/80% and 108 to 144; the three 800ENT bikes print 65%/85% and 117 to 153; the CR900 2025
and CU900 2025 print 65%/80% with a printed answer of 117 to **225**. Four manuals have no such page.
