The Heart Rate program uses resistance (not incline) to control your heart rate: resistance rises gradually to your target, then adjusts automatically to keep you within 5 bpm of it. A heart rate monitor is required. On the setup page set the target bpm directly or pick 60% or 80% of your age-predicted maximum (tell the machine your age first, or it calculates from defaults). The program ends when time is up, or if your heart rate runs 20% higher than target.
