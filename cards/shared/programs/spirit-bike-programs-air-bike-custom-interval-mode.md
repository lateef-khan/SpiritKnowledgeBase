**This is Interval Custom, not Interval 20/10 or Interval 10/20.** Those two are fixed at eight
intervals with preset segment times.

**Interval Custom Mode allows the length of the work/rest segments to be customized, as well as the
total number of intervals.**

1. **Launch** - press the **Interval Custom Mode** button.
2. **Setup** - after the console has been reset, you enter the **total number of intervals** and the
   **work/rest time interval**. Adjust each value with **▲** or **▼** and press **Enter** to confirm
   it.
3. **Start** - the interval begins with a **countdown from three**, announcing the start of each work
   interval.
4. **End** - the mode ends **immediately after the last segment of the final interval**. Pressing
   **Stop** at any time also ends it. The workout summary is shown either way.

**Neither manual prints the allowed range** for the number of intervals or for the segment times, and
neither says whether the setup survives a power cycle. The AB900 2018 service manual repeats the
owner's manual's page word for word and adds no range either. The setup step is worded "after the console
has been reset", which is the only hint: it implies the last values are kept until then.
