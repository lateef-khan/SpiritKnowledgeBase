Each of the five presets prints two dot-matrix charts with **no printed level values**: a LEVEL (resistance) chart whose shape matches the SC200 book, and a STEP HEIGHT chart beside it. Read from the page image:

- Hill STEP HEIGHT: a low broad mound.
- Fat Burn STEP HEIGHT: a low flat band, lower than its LEVEL plateau.
- Cardio STEP HEIGHT: a low base with about four narrow spikes.
- Strength STEP HEIGHT: a mid-height flat block.
- Interval STEP HEIGHT: alternating short columns, lower than the LEVEL columns.

Whether the step height changes automatically during a preset is chosen at setup step 6 of the preset procedure; with it off, step height stays manual.
