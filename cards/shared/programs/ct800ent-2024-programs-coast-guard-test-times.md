**This table is one second lower in every bracket than the one the button consoles print.** The
2024 CT800 and CT850 print 12:52 / 15:27 in the first row; this console prints **12:51 / 15:26**.
The difference is real and was confirmed against a 300 dpi render of the page. Do not substitute one
for the other - see `ct800-2020-programs-coast-guard-test-times`.

A timed **1.5 mile / 2.4 km run**. You control the speed manually. Maximum time allowed to pass:

| Age | Male | Female |
|---|---|---|
| <30 | 12:51 | 15:26 |
| 30-39 | 13:36 | 15:57 |
| 40-49 | 14:29 | 16:58 |
| 50-59 | 15:26 | 17:55 |
| 60+ | 16:40 | 18:44 |

**The manual's link for this test is wrong.** It prints "For more detailed information, visit:
https://bit.ly/SF-Marines" under the Coast Guard heading - the Marines link, not the Coast Guard
one. Every other test on these pages carries its own link. The 2020 button-console manuals print
http://bit.ly/SF-CoastGuard here.

**This is a pass/fail time table, not the Superior-to-Failure rating chart** the CT900, CTSBS900 and
2026 4.0T manuals print for the same test.
