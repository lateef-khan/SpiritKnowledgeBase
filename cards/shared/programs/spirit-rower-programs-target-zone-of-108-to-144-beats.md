**These are the 60% and 80% figures, and the arithmetic on this page is correct.**

Determine your **Maximum Heart Rate** with the formula **220 minus your age**. Your heart rate
training zone is **50% to 90%** of maximum. **60% of MHR is recommended for burning fat while 80% is
recommended for strengthening the cardio vascular system. This 60% to 80% is the zone to stay in for
maximum benefit.**

**Example**, for someone who is 40 years old:

- 220 - 40 = **180** (maximum heart rate)
- 180 x .6 = **108** beats per minute (60% of maximum)
- 180 X .8 = **144** beats per minute (80% of maximum)
- So for a 40 year old the training zone would be **108 to 144 beats per minute.**

The chart page repeats the pair: cardiovascular fitness or weight loss *"can be achieved by training
at **80% or 60%**, respectively, of your MHR on a schedule approved by your physician."*

**The XRW600 has no heart rate program to enter an age into.** Its eleven workout modes are Manual,
Distance, Time, Calories, three intervals, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength and Game - none of them targets
a heart rate. See `crw800-2024-programs-eleven-workout-modes-in-mode-button-order`.

**The 2019 and the 2021 XRW600 print this page identically.** Compared word for word over the whole
programs and heart rate chapter, the two printings differ only in line wrapping.

**The CRW800 rower prints 85% and 153, not 80% and 144** - the same page, the same worked example,
a different upper figure. See `crw800-2024-programs-target-zone-of-108-to-153-beats`. Two Spirit
rowers, two different zones; do not answer one from the other.
