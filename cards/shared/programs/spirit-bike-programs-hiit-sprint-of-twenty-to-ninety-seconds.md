HIIT is a fully customisable interval training program: short bursts of high intensity sprinting
followed by short rest periods. You enter the number of intervals, the time of each Sprint and Rest
period, and the work intensity of the levels.

**On the CU800ENT 2022 and CR800ENT 2023:**

1. Select the program, press **Enter**, and enter the **number of intervals**: default **10**, range
   **3 to 15**. One interval equals 1 Sprint and 1 Rest segment.
2. Enter the **interval time** - the Sprint time and the Rest time, each selectable from **20 to 90
   seconds**. Then set the **Sprint level** and **Rest level**, the resistances you will work at.
3. Press **Start**. The program opens with a **3-minute warm-up** at a resistance level set to
   **50% of the sprint level** you chose, adjustable manually during the warm-up.

**On the CU800ENT 2024** the order is reversed - Sprint time and Sprint Level, then Rest time and
Rest Level, and the number of intervals last - and the manual prints no default and no range for the
interval count or the segment times. Its warm-up paragraph is the same 3 minutes at 50%.

**The 2022, 2023 and 2024 manuals word the warm-up basis differently.** The CU800ENT 2022 says 50%
of "the sprint **level** selected previously"; the CR800ENT 2023 and CU800ENT 2024 both say 50% of
"the sprint **speed** selected previously". Nothing else on the page changes, and no manual defines
a sprint speed to take a half of.

**No manual in this family prints a cool-down for HIIT**, and none mentions a target rpm.

**The LED consoles run a different HIIT** - 20 to 60 second sprints, warm-up at level 5, a 90 rpm
target and a 3-minute cool-down. See `spirit-bike-programs-hiit-sprint-of-twenty-to-sixty-seconds`.
