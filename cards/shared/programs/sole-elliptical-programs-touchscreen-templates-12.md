Templates are preset programs with a preserved set of parameters. Save any program as a template when it completes, or build one from scratch in the Templates tab: tap New Template, choose the base program, save with adjusted properties. At most 12 template programs are stored; remove unwanted ones with Edit Templates in the Templates tab.
