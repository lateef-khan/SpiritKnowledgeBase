Subtract your age from 220 for the Maximum Heart Rate (MHR), then take a percentage of it. The training zone is 50% to 90% of MHR: 60% burns fat and 80% strengthens the cardiovascular system. Entering your age during programming makes the console do this calculation automatically.

Worked example the manual prints: a 40-year-old has MHR 180 (220 minus 40), so 60% is 108 and 80% is 144 beats per minute: a zone of 108 to 144.

The printed chart shows MHR as black columns per age with two diagonal lines for the two goals: weight loss at 60% and cardiovascular fitness at 80% of MHR, pursued on a schedule approved by a physician.
