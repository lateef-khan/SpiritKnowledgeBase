Press **All Programs** on the Home Screen. The prose lists the programs available as **Manual,
Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Interval, Heart Rate, Custom, 5k Run, 10k Run, HIIT and Fitness Test** -
**eleven entries, with a single Heart Rate entry.**

1. Select a program by tapping it on the screen, then press **Enter** to begin. Customize the
   program on the following screens by entering your data, or press **Start** to begin with the
   default settings.
2. Press **Start** to begin your workout, or **Stop** to return to the previous screen.
3. Once the workout begins a **3-minute warm-up** commences. Press **Start** to bypass it and go
   straight to the workout; the clock counts down from three minutes.

**No warm-up on: 5K Run, 10K Run, HR, Custom, Fitness Tests and the manual programs.**

On the screen itself the Programs page carries two tabs. **Default Programs** shows ten tiles -
Manual, Fatburn, Cardio, Hill, HIIT, Interval, 5K Run, 10K Run, Heart Rate and Custom - and
**Fitness Tests** is the second tab rather than a tile. The Home Screen shows four of those tiles
(Fatburn, Cardio, Hill, HIIT) beside Start, Set Time and an **All Programs** link.

**This is the change from the 2022 touchscreen.** The 2022 CT800ENT and CT850ENT open the same list
from **Exercise Programs**, and write the heart rate programs out separately as **Heart Rate 1** and
**Heart Rate 2**, so their printed list counts twelve - see
`ct800ent-2022-programs-entering-a-program-touchscreen`. The 2022 numbered list jumps from 1 to 3
with no step 2; **the 2024 list prints all three steps.**

The button consoles in the same 2024 range still have two heart rate programs and count twelve -
see `ct850-2020-preset-program-list`.
