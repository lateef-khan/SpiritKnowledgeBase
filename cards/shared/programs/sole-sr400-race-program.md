**This is the Sole SR400 race: SPM preset 10, distance preset 500M, and the pace levels run L1 at 1:00 per 500M up to L15 at 8:00.** The Xterra water-rower race in `not_to_be_confused_with` also has fifteen levels and PC/USER WIN but numbers them the other way round (L1 at 8:00).

1. Select Race and press ENTER.
2. Set the SPM (preset 10) and the Meters (preset 500) and press ENTER. There are 15 pace levels: L1 rows 1:00 TIME/500M and L15 rows 8:00 TIME/500M, in 0:30 steps.
3. START/STOP starts the workout; the LCD shows USER and PC status side by side.
4. When either side finishes the distance the computer stops and shows PC WIN or USER WIN, with a bi-bi alarm for 8 seconds; any key stops the alarm.
5. START/STOP pauses with all values saved; RESET returns to the main menu.
