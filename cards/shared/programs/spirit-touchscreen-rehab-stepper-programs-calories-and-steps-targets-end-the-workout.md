**Both targets work the same way: you set a number, you control the resistance yourself, and the
workout finishes when the number is reached.**

- **Calories.** *"Calories Target uses your 8.5S's resistance system to help you achieve your
  calories target. Increases and decreases in resistance change the efficiency of burning calories.
  **You need to change the resistance manually and the program ends when the calories consumption
  reached the target.**"* The 8.5S-Fit puts it as: set a target calorie burn and the workout
  automatically finishes when it is reached; its screenshot shows **168 kcal**, and the 8.5S's shows
  **100 kcal**.
- **Steps.** *"Steps Target uses your 8.5S's computer and sensor help you to achieve your steps
  target. You can also increases and decreases in resistance during the session manually and the
  program ends when the steps consumption reached the target."* The 8.5S-Fit's screenshot shows
  **1200 steps**.

**A stepper counts steps, not distance.** Neither machine offers a distance target, and neither
prints a minimum or maximum for either goal.

*The screenshot figures above are sample values on a setup screen, not defaults the manuals state in
words. Neither book prints a permitted range for calories or steps.*

**Do not answer this from the stairclimber's Target Training card.** That program offers Time,
Vertical Distance or Calories with printed ranges of 5-99 minutes and 10-990 - see
`csc900-2024-programs-target-training-goals-and-ranges`.
