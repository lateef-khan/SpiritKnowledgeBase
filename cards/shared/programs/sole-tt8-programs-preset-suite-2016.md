This book differs from the A1 TT8 suite card (`sole-treadmill-programs-preset-suite-tt8`) in the preset wording: Hill and Strength say **workload** here, not speed — otherwise the ten match.

The TT8 has **ten built-in programs**: one **Manual** program, **five preset profiles** (Interval era), one **Custom**, one **Fit Test**, and two **Heart Rate programs (HR1 and HR2)**.

- **Hill.** "The Hill program simulates going up and down a hill. The workload will steadily increase and then decrease during the program."
- **Fat Burn.** Same fat-burning text as the menu books, ending "This program does not use heart rate but simulates a lower, steady exertion workout."
- **Cardio.** Same cardiovascular text as the menu books.
- **Strength.** "This program will steadily increase in workload to a high level and forces you to sustain it. This is designed to strengthen and tone your legs and glutes (muscles of the butt)."
- **Interval.** Same oxygen-depletion/recovery text as the menu books.

Profiles are drawn **TT8 SPEED / TT8 GRADE** dot-matrix pictures (render-verified), approximate by design with no segment figures to quote. Setup, Custom, Fit Test and HRC each have their own stencil cards (see links); all differ from the A1 TT8 cards. The book documents both drive versions, so both TT8-2016 ids apply.
