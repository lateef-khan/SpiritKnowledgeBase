The rate of perceived exertion (RPE), also known as the Borg scale after Swedish physiologist G.A.V. Borg, rates intensity from 6 to 20 by feel: 6 Minimal; 7 Very, very light; 8 Very, very light +; 9 Very light; 10 Very light +; 11 Fairly light; 12 Comfortable; 13 Somewhat hard; 14 Somewhat hard +; 15 Hard; 16 Hard +; 17 Very hard; 18 Very hard +; 19 Very, very hard; 20 Maximal. Add a zero for an approximate heart rate (12 ≈ 120 bpm). Nearly every book prints 'also know as'; that is their typo, the scale is the Borg scale. The E20 books word the closing paragraph differently ('comfortable'/'difficult' pace) with the same meaning.
