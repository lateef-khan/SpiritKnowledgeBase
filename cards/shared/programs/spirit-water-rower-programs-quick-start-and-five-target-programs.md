The **CONSOLE PROGRAMS** chapter lists **six** things, and that is the whole program set:

- **Quick Start**
- **Target Time**
- **Target Distance**
- **Target Calories**
- **Target Strokes**
- **Target Heart Rate**

## Quick Start

**"As strokes are detected, the console will immediately launch QUICK START."** There is no key to
press. TIME, DISTANCE, CALORIES, SPM, WATTS, STROKES and TOTAL STROKES begin counting.
**TIME/500m and TIME/500m AVERAGE are displayed alternately every 5 seconds.**

With a heart rate input the chest-strap icon stays lit and the heart icon flashes, and the current
rate is shown; **without one, three dashes "- - -" are shown continuously.**

The 7-segment display shows the level: on the **CRW900** *"according to VR adjustments"* - the tank
adjuster - and on the **CRW800H2O** *"according to the settings"*, meaning the L1 to L6 water level
you entered with the LEVEL button.

## What these machines do not have

**No Manual mode, no Fat Burn, Cardio or Strength profile, no interval program, no Game, no fitness
test, no user profiles and no custom program builder.** Searching both whole manuals for *fat burn*,
*profile*, *Borg* and *perceived* returns nothing, and the only occurrences of *interval* are the
INTERVAL DISPLAY panel name and the CRW900's advice below.

**The CRW900 tells you to run intervals by hand instead.** Its Operation Instructions page says
*"Interval training is used by most rowers, where a period of low intensity is combined with short
intervals of high intensity. Your SPIRIT Rower allows for changes 'on the fly', to achieve
multi-level resistance profiles during a single workout."* That is coaching advice about the tank
adjuster, not a console program.

**This is not the CRW800 or XRW600 program list.** Those machines have a MODE button and eleven named
workout modes including three intervals and a Game - see
`crw800-2024-programs-eleven-workout-modes-in-mode-button-order`.

**Measured, not assumed.** Compared word for word with `difflib.SequenceMatcher` on the native PDF
text, the two water rowers' console chapters agree **86.5%**, and the two CONSOLE PROGRAMS pages
alone **81.2%** - close relatives. The CRW800 air rower and the CRW800H2O share **23.9%** of their
whole documents. Two consoles, not one.
