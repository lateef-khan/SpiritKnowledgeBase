*"Calories Target uses your 8.0U's resistance system to help you achieve your calories target.
Increases and decreases in resistance change the efficiency of burning calories. **You need to change
the resistance manually** and the program ends when the calories consumption reached the target."*

Setup takes the **workload type** and the **program time**; the screenshot's setup screen reads
"Set your target calories, then control workload manually" with a **Workload**, a **Level** and a
**Target Calories** field in kcal.

**Heart Rate Target and METs Target drive the resistance for you; this one does not.** The text
prints **no default calorie figure, no increment and no maximum**, and says nothing about what
happens when the program time runs out before the target is met.

The 8.0U and 8.5R 2025 books print this page word for word, as does the 8.5UE ergometer
(`85ue-2025-programs-calories-target-leaves-the-resistance-to-you`).
