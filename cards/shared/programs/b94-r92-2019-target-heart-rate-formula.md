Maximum Heart Rate (MHR) = **220 minus your age**.

Your heart rate training zone is **50% to 90%** of your maximum heart rate.

- **60%** of MHR is the zone that burns fat.
- **80%** of MHR is for strengthening the cardiovascular system.
- **60% to 80%** is the zone to stay in for maximum benefit.

Worked example from the manual, for a 40 year old:

| Step | Value |
|---|---|
| 220 - 40 | 180 (maximum heart rate) |
| 180 x .6 | 108 beats per minute (60% of maximum) |
| 180 x .8 | 144 beats per minute (80% of maximum) |

So a 40 year old's training zone is **108 to 144 beats per minute**.

If you enter your age during programming, the console performs this calculation automatically.

You can use the heart rate monitor feature **without** using a Heart Rate Control program, during manual mode or any other program. Only the HR programs actually control resistance from your heart rate.
