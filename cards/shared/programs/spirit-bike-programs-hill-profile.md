Hill follows a triangle or pyramid type of gradual progression from approximately **10% of maximum
effort** - the level you chose before starting the program - up to a maximum effort which lasts for
**10% of the total workout time**, then a gradual regression of resistance back to approximately 10%
of maximum effort.

All thirteen Spirit commercial bike owner's manuals print this description word for word. The CU800
2012 heads it `Resistance:` before the sentence; the wording after that is the same. The touchscreen
consoles list the program as **Hills** in the CR900ENT and CU900ENT program lineup and as **Hill**
everywhere else; the description is identical.

The profile picture printed beside it is an image in every manual, so the KB does not hold its
column values.

The Spirit commercial ellipticals print the same sentence - see
`spirit-ce-programs-hill-profile`. That is a separate card because it is a different product line.

**All fourteen Spirit residential XBR and XBU bike manuals print the same description** - XBR95 2016, 2018, 2021 and 2023; XBR25 2019 and 2023; XBR55 2019, 2021 and 2023; XBR55ENT 2021; XBU55 2019, 2021 and 2023; and XBU55ENT 2021.
The four 2023 books and the two ENT touchscreens set the name in sentence case as **Hill**; the eight
older LED books print **HILL**. The wording is otherwise identical to the commercial books.

The profile picture beside it is an image in these manuals too, so the KB does not hold its column
values.
