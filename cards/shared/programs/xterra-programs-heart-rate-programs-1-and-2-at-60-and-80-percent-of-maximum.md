"Both programs operate the same. The only difference is that **HR1 is set to 60% and HR2
is set to 80% of your maximum heart rate**." You must grasp the contact heart rate grips
with both hands (the SB500, FS5.8e and FS5.9e also accept the telemetric chest strap; the
SB4.5r book names the grips only).

1. Select **HR1** or **HR2** (Program up/down keys until the message window shows it, or the
   HR1/HR2 button on the SB4.5r) and press Enter.
2. Enter your data with the Level up/down keys, Enter after each: age and weight (the SB500
   asks age first, the other three weight first), then **Time**.
3. Select the **Target Heart Rate Level** - the console shows the rate it computed from your
   age and the program's percentage; adjust if you wish and press Enter. "During the workout
   the product will adjust resistance to keep you near this heart rate."
4. Press **Start**. Stop goes back one screen if you want to change a setting.
5. Level up/down keys raise or lower the **target heart rate** at any time during the
   program.

The percentages are of 220 minus your age; see the heart rate exertion card. The Sole B94
and R92 bikes have the same HR1 and HR2; see `see_also`.
