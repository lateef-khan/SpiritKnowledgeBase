**These four books print no heart-rate training page.** There is no "220 minus your age"
formula, no 50 to 90% zone, no 40-year-old example and no Borg scale; the fifteen other Xterra
treadmill books print all of them.

What they do say about heart rate is a console fact: the TR200 and TR260 describe the handgrip
pulse readout (the TR260 adds that the console "will also pick up wireless heart rate
transmitters that Bluetooth compatible"), and all four carry the safety-page note that
heart-rate monitoring systems may be inaccurate. **None of the four has a heart rate program.**

Proved by a loose search of each `text.md` for "heart", "pulse", "220", "zone", "borg" and
"exertion", by the same search with spaces allowed between letters, by reading the pages the
hits fall on, and by checking every OCR supplement (TR150: pages 6, 8, 24; TR200: none; TR260:
pages 3, 4, 6, 12, 25; TRX1000: pages 3, 5, 18) for a flattened heart-rate page. None holds one.
