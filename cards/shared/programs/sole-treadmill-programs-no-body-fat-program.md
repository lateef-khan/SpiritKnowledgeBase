**None of these books prints a body-fat, body-composition or BMI program.** Every program chapter was read in full: the complements run from Manual/presets/user/HR slots (all generations) through Custom, Fit Test, Gerkin, military tests, 5K/10K, CALORIE and HRC, and no chapter offers a fat-percent or BMI readout program.

Proved twice: a phrase search for body fat, bodyfat and BMI across all 25 sources returns nothing in program text (the lone substring hit is the warranty page's word "Submit"), and each generation's program chapter was read page by page. The calorie programs estimate burn from weight, not composition.
