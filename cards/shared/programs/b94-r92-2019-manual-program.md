In the Manual program you control the workload yourself, not the computer.

1. Press the **Manual** program button, then press **Enter**.
2. The Message Window asks for your **Age**. Adjust with the Level up/down keys, then press Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight** with the Level up/down keys, then Enter.
4. Enter the **Time** with the Level up/down keys, then Enter.
5. Press **Start** to begin. You can go back and modify settings by pressing **Stop** to move back one programming screen.
6. The bike starts at **level one**, the easiest level. Staying at level one for a while to warm up is recommended. Press **Level up** to increase the workload and **Level down** to decrease it.
7. Press **Display** to scroll through the data in the Message Window.
8. When the program ends the Message Window shows a workout summary for a short time, then returns to the start-up display.
