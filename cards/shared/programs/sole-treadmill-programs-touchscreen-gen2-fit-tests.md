**7 options** under the Fitness Tests tab. Tap a test card for its setup page and check the physical parameters there — wrong data skews the course and the score ("Edit Parameters" fixes them).

**Before the test:** good health first (physician check if over 35 or with pre-existing conditions); warm up and stretch; no caffeine.

**Gerkin Protocol** is a sub-maximal VO2 (volume of oxygen) test that raises speed and incline alternately until **85% of max heart rate** (a heart rate monitor is required). **Time-to-85% is the score (VO2 Max).** It starts with a 3-minute warm-up at a lower speed.

**Air Force, Army, Navy, Coast Guard, PEB and Marine Corps** are US military tests measuring **time to cover a set distance; speed is manual**. Distances as printed on the test cards (render-verified on the F80-2023 and F85-2023 books; the same seven cards appear in all five books' screenshots):

| Test | Distance |
|---|---|
| Air Force | 1.5 miles |
| Army | 2.0 miles |
| Coast Guard | 1.5 miles |
| Gerkin | 85% of max HR (not a distance) |
| PEB | 1.5 miles |
| Marine Corps | 3.0 miles |
| Navy | 1.5 miles |
