The Manual program works as the name implies: you control the workload yourself, not the computer.

1. Choose Manual, then press **Enter**.
2. Enter your **Age**, then press Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight**, then press Enter.
4. Set the **Time**, then press Enter.
5. Press **Start** to begin, or Enter to go back and modify your settings. At any time during data
   entry, **Stop** goes back one level, or screen.
6. The program automatically starts you at **level one**. That is the easiest level and it is a good
   idea to stay there for a while to warm up. Press Up to increase the workload, Down to decrease it.
7. When the program ends, press Start to run the same program again or Stop to exit.

**Which key selects Manual.** The CE800 2021, CE800 2023, CE850 2020, CE850 2022 and CES880 2025 use
the **Program** button to scroll to Manual. The CE800 2013, CE850 2016, CE900 2021 and CE900 2025
have a dedicated **Manual** key, and on those four you may also type Age and Weight on the numeric
key pad instead of the Up and Down keys.

**Two machines print two extra steps.** The CE800 2013 and CE850 2016 add, between steps 6 and 7,
that you may scroll through the data in the Message Window with the adjacent Display key and switch
between the profile display and a quarter mile track, and that when the program ends you may save
the workout you just completed as a custom program by pressing the Custom key.

**Stride length.** On the CE850 2020 and CE850 2022 step 6 adds that you can control stride length at
any time with the stride up/down keys on the left swing arm.

The CE800ENT does not print these steps. Its manual describes Manual mode in one line - see
`ce800ent-programs-touchscreen-program-menu`.

**Twelve Spirit residential XE machines print these same steps.** What each adds:

- **XE100, XE200, XE300, XE350 and XE550** (XE100/XE200/XE300 manual printed pages 23-24 and 35-36;
  XE150/XE350/XE550 manual pages 22-23) add, between steps 6 and 7, that you scroll the message
  window with the adjacent Display or Enter key, and, on the XE300, XE350 and XE550, that you may
  switch between the profile display and a quarter mile track. Those same step-7 sentences also
  mention switching to heart rate **Auto-Pilot** mode, but **only the XE550 actually has that
  button** - see `xe550-2005-programs-heart-rate-auto-pilot`, which explains why the sentence appears
  in books covering machines that do not have it. All five add a final step: you may save the workout
  you just completed as a custom user program by pressing a **User** key.
- **XE395 2010** (printed page 18) adds a whole step the others do not have: **the program starts
  with no Incline**, and you raise or lower it during the program with the Up and Down keys on the
  **left swing arm**, while resistance is changed on the console or the **right** swing arm.
- **XE195, XE295, XG400 and XE795 2021** (printed pages 22, 23, 22 and 22) print steps 1-7 and add
  the same "save it as a custom user program by pressing a User key" ending. On these the keys are
  named **Level Up / Level Down**.
- **XE395 2023 and XE795 2023** (printed page 30 and page 29) print steps 1-7 with a **PROGRAM** key
  used to scroll to Manual, and their closing step offers only Start or Stop - **the save-as-custom
  ending is gone**.

**Two XE consoles do not work this way at all.** The XE150 sets a level, a time, a distance, a
calorie target and a watt target before you start - see
`xe150-2005-programs-manual-sets-level-time-distance-calories-and-watts`. The XE395ENT touchscreen
asks only for a target time - see `xe395ent-2021-programs-manual-asks-only-for-a-target-time`.

**Six earlier XE printings, filed on disk under bikes, print these same steps** - XE795 2018,
XE195 2019 and XG400 2019 (printed page 22), XE295 2019 (page 23), and XE395 2018 and XE895 2018
(page 24). All six print steps 1-7 and add the same "save the workout you just completed as a custom
user program by pressing a **User** key" ending. Two small differences:

- **Which key goes back at step 5.** The XE795 2018 says **Stop**, and adds the note that Stop goes
  back one level or screen at any time during data entry. The other five say **Enter**.
- **What the workload keys are called.** The XE895 2018 calls them plain **Up / Down** throughout;
  the XE795 2018 says Level Up/Down for age and weight but plain Up/Down for the workload; the other
  four say **Level Up / Level Down** everywhere.

*The XE195 2019 prints that save-as-custom ending even though it has no User key and its manual has
no Custom User Defined Programs section at all - the same mismatch its 2021 printing carries. See
`xe195-2021-programs-four-presets-and-six-programs`.*
