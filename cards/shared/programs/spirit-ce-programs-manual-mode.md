The Manual program works as the name implies: you control the workload yourself, not the computer.

1. Choose Manual, then press **Enter**.
2. Enter your **Age**, then press Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight**, then press Enter.
4. Set the **Time**, then press Enter.
5. Press **Start** to begin, or Enter to go back and modify your settings. At any time during data
   entry, **Stop** goes back one level, or screen.
6. The program automatically starts you at **level one**. That is the easiest level and it is a good
   idea to stay there for a while to warm up. Press Up to increase the workload, Down to decrease it.
7. When the program ends, press Start to run the same program again or Stop to exit.

**Which key selects Manual.** The CE800 2021, CE800 2023, CE850 2020, CE850 2022 and CES880 2025 use
the **Program** button to scroll to Manual. The CE800 2013, CE850 2016, CE900 2021 and CE900 2025
have a dedicated **Manual** key, and on those four you may also type Age and Weight on the numeric
key pad instead of the Up and Down keys.

**Two machines print two extra steps.** The CE800 2013 and CE850 2016 add, between steps 6 and 7,
that you may scroll through the data in the Message Window with the adjacent Display key and switch
between the profile display and a quarter mile track, and that when the program ends you may save
the workout you just completed as a custom program by pressing the Custom key.

**Stride length.** On the CE850 2020 and CE850 2022 step 6 adds that you can control stride length at
any time with the stride up/down keys on the left swing arm.

The CE800ENT does not print these steps. Its manual describes Manual mode in one line - see
`ce800ent-programs-touchscreen-program-menu`.
