**On the CE900 the cool-down after the last Sprint is 3 minutes, not the 2 minutes the CE800, CE850
and CES880 manuals print.** Everything else in the program matches those manuals.

1. Press the **HIIT** button then Enter, and enter your **Age** (Up and Down buttons or the numeric
   button pad).
2. Enter your **Weight**.
3. Number of **intervals**: default **10**, range **3 to 15**. One interval equals 1 Sprint and 1
   Rest segment.
4. **Sprint Level** - the resistance level during the Sprint segments.
5. **Rest Level** - the resistance level during the Rest segments.
6. **Interval time**. The Message window shows `Sprint :30 | Rest :30`. The Sprint time blinks
   first; adjust it from **20 to 60 seconds** with the + - buttons, press Enter, then adjust the
   Rest time.
7. The Message window shows the total time. Press Start. There is a **3-minute warm-up** before the
   first Sprint, at **resistance level 5**, adjustable manually.
8. The dot matrix display in HIIT is a **speed** indication display, not a power or resistance
   display. The blinking LED at the first Sprint segment is the target speed LED and indicates
   **90 pedal rpm**. Maintain at least 90 rpm through the Sprints. The **rest** segments are set to
   **resistance level 5**.
9. At the end of the last Sprint there is a **3-minute cool-down**. Press Stop to bypass it and show
   the workout summary.

The 2021 and 2025 CE900 manuals print this program identically.
