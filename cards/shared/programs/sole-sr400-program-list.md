In the workout setting mode MANUAL flashes in the dot matrix. Press UP or DOWN to step through Manual, Program, H.R.C., Race and User, and press ENTER to confirm the choice. Each mode gets its own card.

Pressing START/STOP from the main menu skips selection and starts rowing directly in Manual mode. After a workout finishes, RESET returns to the main menu. The console offers 16 load levels, L1 to L16.
