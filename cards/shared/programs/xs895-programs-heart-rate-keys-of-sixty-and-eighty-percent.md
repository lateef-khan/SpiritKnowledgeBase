**You must wear the heart rate transmitter strap for these programs (sold separately).** The manual
also says plainly: *"You may not use the Pulse Grip feature while in Heart Rate Programs."*

**Both programs operate the same way. The only difference is that HR 1 is set to 60% and HR 2 is set
to 80% of the maximum heart rate.**

After you select your heart rate target the program **attempts to keep you at or within 3-5 heart
beats per minute of that value**, and **automatically controls resistance at the pedals** to do it.

1. Press the **HR 1** or **HR 2** key, then **Enter**.
2. Enter your **Age** with the Level +/- keys, then Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight**, then Enter.
4. Set the **Time**, then Enter.
5. Set the **Heart Rate Target** - the heart rate you will strive to maintain - with the Level +/-
   keys, then Enter. *"The heart rate that appears is based on the % you accepted in Step 1. If you
   change this number it will either increase or decrease the % from Step 1."*
6. Press **Start** to begin, or **Enter** to go back and modify. At any time during data editing,
   Enter goes back one screen.
7. **Level +/- during the program changes your target heart rate**, not the resistance directly.
8. The Enter key scrolls the data in the Message Center during the program.
9. When the program ends, **Start** repeats it and **Stop** exits.

**The percentages are 60 and 80, matching this machine's own training page** - see
`xs895-programs-target-zone-of-108-to-144-beats`. **The Spirit steppers' keys are 65% and 80%** and
must not be substituted; see
`spirit-stepper-programs-heart-rate-keys-of-sixty-five-and-eighty-percent`.
