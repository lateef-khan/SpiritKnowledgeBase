**The chest strap is sold separately and is not in the box.**

1. Attach the transmitter to the elastic strap using the **interlocking button**.
2. Adjust the strap as tightly as possible while ensuring it is still comfortable.
3. Position the transmitter with the logo centred in the middle of your torso facing away from your
   chest - some people must position it slightly left of centre. Attach the final end of the elastic
   strap by inserting the round end and, using the locking parts, secure the transmitter and strap
   around your chest.
4. Position the transmitter directly below the pectoral muscles.
5. Sweat is the best conductor for the very small electrical signals of a heart beat, but plain
   water can be used to pre-wet the electrodes - the 2 ribbed oval areas on the reverse side of the
   belt and both sides of the transmitter. Wear the strap a few minutes before your workout: some
   users, because of body chemistry, have a harder time getting a strong steady signal at first, and
   after "warming up" the problem lessens.
6. Your workout must be within range - the distance between transmitter and receiver - to get a
   strong steady signal. Stay close enough to the console. Wearing the transmitter directly on bare
   skin assures proper operation; over a shirt, wet the areas of the shirt the electrodes rest on.

**Note.** The transmitter activates automatically when it detects activity from the user's heart and
deactivates when it does not. It is water resistant, but moisture can create false signals, so dry
it completely after use to prolong battery life. Estimated transmitter battery life is **2500
hours**. The replacement battery is **CR2032**.

**Third party straps.** The CE800 2021, CE800 2023, CE850 2020 and CE850 2022 manuals say straps
that operate using **Bluetooth or ANT+** should also work with this machine. **The CE800ENT manual
says Bluetooth only and does not mention ANT+.**

**A different wording covers the other five machines.** The CE800 2013, CE850 2016, CE900 2025 and
CES880 2025 print the Spirit treadmill version of this page - "interlocking key", "Panasonic
CR2032", no third-party sentence - which is `ct900-heart-rate-chest-strap-setup`. The CE900 2021
owner's manual has no chest strap chapter at all.
