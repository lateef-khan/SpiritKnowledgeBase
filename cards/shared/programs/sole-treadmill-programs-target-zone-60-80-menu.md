"To determine the benefit range in which you wish to train, you must first determine your Maximum Heart Rate. This can be accomplished by using the following formula: 220 minus your age." The training zone is **50% to 90%** of maximum; **60% of MHR burns fat, 80% strengthens the cardiovascular system**, and "this 60% to 80% is the zone to stay in for maximum benefit."

Worked example, printed identically in all listed books: a 40-year-old has MHR 180; 180 x .6 = **108 beats per minute** (60%); 180 x .8 = **144 beats per minute** (80%); "so for a 40 year old the training zone would be 108 to 144 beats per minute." Entering age during programming makes the console do the calculation automatically. "Consult your physician before participating in any exercise program."

This is the menu-console zone. The 2009-2010 consoles share the 60/80 pair (see `sole-treadmill-programs-target-zone-w-series`); the early consoles use 90/70 instead. Touchscreen books state their percentages on the Heart Rate program cards, not this chart.
