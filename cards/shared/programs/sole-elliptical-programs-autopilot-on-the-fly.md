Auto-pilot is a heart rate program you enter on the fly: at any time, during any program, press Auto-pilot and the console switches to heart rate control, holding your heart rate at whatever it is the moment you press. Example printed in all three books: in Manual at 150 bpm, pressing Auto-pilot keeps you at 150 bpm automatically. Scope: E75/E95-2007, the VE95 chapter and the WE95 chapter only — the smaller consoles in the same books have no Auto-pilot.
