These two manuals print a second paragraph, headed **Incline**, beside each preset program's
resistance description. What each program does with pedal elevation:

- **Hill** - the pedal elevation is a more gradual and sustained progression. **Maximum elevation is
  in the middle of the workout and lasts for 10% of the duration.**
- **Fat Burn** - a quick and sustained progression up to the maximum value, default or user input,
  **for 90% of the workout duration**.
- **Cardio** - moderate elevation with several spikes at different points. **Segments 4, 9 and 14 are
  maximum elevation for this program.**
- **Strength** - a quick climb to a moderate, sustained elevation that **lasts the majority of the
  workout length**.
- **Interval** (printed as **HIIT** in the XE395ENT) - spikes similar to the resistance profile but
  in different segments, so all of your lower extremity muscles are equally challenged. **The incline
  alternates between 25 & 65% of maximum elevation.**

*The XE395ENT wording says the HIIT incline spikes "similar to the **speed** profile". That machine
has no speed profile; the phrase is carried over from a treadmill manual. The XE395 2010 says
"resistance profile".*

**The pictures beside these paragraphs are printed images** with no numeric columns, so this KB holds
no segment-by-segment incline values beyond the segment numbers quoted above.

**No other Spirit elliptical prints incline profiles.** The XE195, XE295, XG400, XE795 and the 2023
machines print the resistance paragraph only, and no Spirit commercial elliptical manual has an
incline chapter at all.
