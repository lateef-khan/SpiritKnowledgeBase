The XBR95 2016, 2018 and 2021 manuals say the Semi-Recumbent Bike has **seven different programs**
with factory preset work level profiles, and describe all seven:

**Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, Interval, Calories and Fusion.**

**Twelve programs are described in the book altogether**, once the ones that are not presets are
counted: the seven above, **Manual**, **User 1** and **User 2**, and **HR1** and **HR2**. The callout
beside the keypad still reads "Ten innovative programs offer a variety of work-outs", and the key
legend pairs them - USER1-2 and HR1-2 are one key each on the drawing - which is how ten keys carry
twelve programs. See `spirit-residential-bike-console-face-ten-programs` for the console face itself.

**Calories and Fusion each have their own setup procedure** rather than the ordinary preset flow -
see `spirit-bike-programs-calories-program-counts-a-calorie-goal-down-to-zero` and
`spirit-bike-programs-fusion-alternates-bike-intervals-with-off-bike-strength`.

**All three printings are the same book on this chapter.** Measured on native PDF text over the whole
programs chapter, the 2016 and 2018 books match at **99.9%** at word level and the 2018 and 2021 books
at **98.7%**, the 2021 difference being an extra copy of the Quick Start paragraph and one dropped
clause in the Manual introduction. No program name, no default and no percentage changed in five
years.

**The XBR95 2023 replaced Interval with HIIT, dropped Calories and User, and added Target and
Custom** - see `spirit-bike-programs-six-presets-adding-hiit-and-fusion`. The XBR55 and XBU55 of the
same era have five presets, not seven.

**The Spirit XE795 elliptical console carries the same seven.** Separate card, different product line
- see `xe795-2021-programs-seven-presets-including-calories-and-fusion`.
