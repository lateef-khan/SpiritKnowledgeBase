**These books print no workout programs.** The SW100, SW121, SW155 and SW180 owner's manuals contain registration, safety precautions and safeguards, assembly instructions and the warranty, and nothing else. The word program appears in them only in the safety warnings about consulting a physician before starting an exercise program.

There is no console chapter because these machines have no console: resistance is the plates on the rack, so there is no preset, custom, heart rate or watt program to card.

The SRVO Complete bench manual (SRB101) is the same shape: an item checklist plus fold/unfold steps, no programs. The SRVO power station itself does have three training modes, which live on their own cards and are deliberately not listed here.
