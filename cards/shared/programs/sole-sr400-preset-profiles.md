Program mode offers twelve preset profiles, P1 to P12. Select Program, pick a profile with UP/DOWN, preset the TIME the same way, then START/STOP to row. UP/DOWN adjusts the resistance level from 1 to 16 during the workout; pausing saves all values with WATT and TIME/500M as averages, the countdown ends with an 8-second alarm, and RESET returns to the main menu.

**About the profile pictures.** The manual prints all twelve as small LCD pictures with **no numeric level values**. Read from the page image: P01 ramps up, P02 stays low with small bumps, P03 peaks in the middle, P04 shows twin peaks, P05 and P06 climb in steps (P06 higher), P07 rolls, P08 holds a high plateau, P09 ramps, P10 holds a tall plateau, P11 peaks late, P12 stays low and rolling.
