**Neither walking treadmill has a program of any kind.** The console pages describe Start/Pause,
Stop, the Speed 3 and Speed 6 shortcut keys, Speed + and -, (on the WS300) Incline Up and
Down, and a **Quick Start**: insert the safety key, turn on the power, press Start/Stop, a
3-2-1 countdown with a buzzer, then the motor starts at 0.6 mph and you adjust the speed with
+ and -. The "How to Use the Treadmill" page repeats the same sequence. There is no PROGRAM
key on the console overview.

The only program-section content in either book is the generic **220-minus-age training zone
page** and the **Borg scale** (see `see_also`), which say entering your age "is used for the
Heart Rate programs" - a leftover sentence, as these consoles have no heart rate program and
no age entry.

Proved by a loose search of both `text.md` files for "prog", "preset", "interval", "target",
"mode" and "P1" to "P9" (only the safety-page phrase "exercise program", the app page's
"training progress" and the lubrication "intervals" match), by reading the console pages
(PDF 19 to 22), and by checking the OCR supplements for pages 19, 20 and 22, which repeat the
native text and add nothing.
