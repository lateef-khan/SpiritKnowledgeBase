Hill follows a triangle or pyramid type of gradual progression from approximately **10% of maximum
effort** - the level you chose before starting the program - up to a maximum effort which lasts for
**10% of the total workout time**, then a gradual regression of resistance back to approximately 10%
of maximum effort.

All ten Spirit commercial elliptical owner's manuals print this description word for word. The
CE800 2013 heads it "Resistance:" before the sentence; the wording after that is the same.

The profile picture that goes with it is a printed image in every manual, so the KB does not hold
its column values.
