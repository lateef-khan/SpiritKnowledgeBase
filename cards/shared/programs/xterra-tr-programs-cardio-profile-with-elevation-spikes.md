**Cardio** "presents a quick progression up to **near maximum resistance level** (default or
user input level). It has **slight fluctuations up and down** to allow your heart rate to
elevate, and then recover repeatedly, before beginning a quick cool down. This will build up
your heart muscle and increase blood flow and lung capacity."

**Incline:** "The elevation in this program is moderate. There are **several elevation
spikes** at different points of the workout. **Segments 15 are maximum elevation** for this
program" (sic - printed as "Segments 15").

The book pictures the program as a **Speed** and an **Incline** dot-matrix profile with **no
figures**; the words below are the whole of what it prints. The maximum speed and incline
the program reaches are shown in the Message Center when it is chosen and can be lowered
during setup.
