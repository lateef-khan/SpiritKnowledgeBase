*"METs stands for metabolic equivalent, which is one way that exercise physiologists estimate how
much work is being done during physical activity. This program controls the patient workload to
maintain a steady MET level throughout the exercise session: **perfect for heart patients who are
prescribed exercise in METs by their cardiologist**."*

**1 MET** is essentially the amount of energy produced relative to body mass while at rest - sitting
and reading is 1 MET. **1 MET is equivalent to approximately 3.5 millilitres of O2 per kg of body
weight per minute.**

Using 1 MET as the reference: **light activities burn up to 3 times as many calories as rest,
moderate activities 3 to 6 times, and vigorous exercise more than 6 times.**

**The two machines control it differently:**

- **8.5S** - *"METs Target uses your 8.5S's resistance system to help you achieve your METs target.
  The program automatically changes resistance gradually to maintain the METs at target."* Set the
  target METs and the exercise time, then Start.
- **8.5S-Fit** - *"A METs program is a controllable constant METs whose level adjusts when the RPM is
  changed. Choose either faster RPM at a lower resistance level or slower RPM at a higher resistance
  level."*

**The 8.5S manual prints a reference table** of activities against MET values, from sleeping at 0.9
and watching television at 1.0, through walking 3.0 mph at 3.3 and stationary bicycling at 100 watts
at 5.5, up to jogging at 7.0 and rope jumping at 100/min at 11.0.

*Two page defects, recorded and not repaired. The 8.5S setup screenshot is captioned "Set your target
METs. **Speed and incline** will change automatically" - a treadmill sentence on a stepper with
neither - and the target figure shown in that screenshot reads **110**, which is not a plausible MET
value. Neither is treated here as a specification.*
