**Any program can be labelled a Favorite, and favourites appear on the Home screen so you can reach
them at any time.**

The XT685ENT manual gives the detail:

- **3 to 6 favourite programs appear on the Home screen, depending on your display size.**
- To "like" a program, open its **program setup page** and tap the **"ribbon" icon in the upper left
  corner** of the screen.
- All labelled programs are listed under the **Favorites** tab.
- To remove the label, press **Edit** in the Favorites tab, or open the program setup page again and
  un-tap the ribbon icon.
- Once you have decided on a program, press **Start This Program** on the screen, or the physical
  **START** key, to begin.

The XT485ENT manual prints only the shorter statement - any program can be labelled FAVORITE, and
favourite programs appear on the Homescreen so you can access them any time. **It gives no count, no
ribbon icon and no removal step,** and on that machine favourites are also visible in the
**FAVORITES** tab of the Profile section, where they can be deleted.
