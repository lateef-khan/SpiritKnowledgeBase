The Cardio program is designed to increase cardiovascular function and endurance, exercising the heart and lungs: it builds up the heart muscle and increases blood flow and lung capacity through a higher level of exertion with slight fluctuations in work.

**About the profile chart.** The manual prints the work level profile as a dot-matrix picture with **no printed level values**. Read from the page image, its shape is a stepped climb to a broad uneven plateau with slight dips, then a stepped fall. On the SC300 each preset also carries a STEP HEIGHT chart, which has its own card.
