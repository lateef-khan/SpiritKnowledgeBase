Interval takes you through high levels of intensity followed by recovery periods of low intensity.
The manuals say it uses and develops your "Fast Twitch" muscle fibres, which are used when
performing tasks that are intense and short in duration. Those deplete your oxygen level and spike
your heart rate, followed by periods of recovery and heart rate drop to replenish oxygen; your
cardiovascular system gets programmed to use oxygen more efficiently.

All ten Spirit commercial elliptical owner's manuals print this description word for word. The
CE800ENT writes "fast twitch" without the quotation marks the others use.

**Interval is not HIIT.** Interval is a factory preset profile with no settings of its own. HIIT is
a separate, fully customisable program on eight of the ten machines - see
`spirit-ce800-programs-hiit-two-minute-cool-down`.

The profile picture that goes with Interval is a printed image in every manual, so the KB does not
hold its column values.
