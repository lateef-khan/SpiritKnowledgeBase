Interval takes you through high levels of intensity followed by recovery periods of low intensity.
The manuals say it uses and develops your "Fast Twitch" muscle fibres, which are used when
performing tasks that are intense and short in duration. Those deplete your oxygen level and spike
your heart rate, followed by periods of recovery and heart rate drop to replenish oxygen; your
cardiovascular system gets programmed to use oxygen more efficiently.

All ten Spirit commercial elliptical owner's manuals print this description word for word. The
CE800ENT writes "fast twitch" without the quotation marks the others use.

**Interval is not HIIT.** Interval is a factory preset profile with no settings of its own. HIIT is
a separate, fully customisable program on eight of the ten machines - see
`spirit-ce800-programs-hiit-two-minute-cool-down`.

The profile picture that goes with Interval is a printed image in every manual, so the KB does not
hold its column values.

**Eight Spirit residential XE machines print this description word for word too**, but three of them
print it under a different name.

- Printed as **Interval**: XE395 2010 (printed page 21), XE195 (page 24), XE295 (page 25), XG400
  (page 24) and XE795 2021 (page 29).
- Printed as **HIIT**: XE395ENT (page 38), XE395 2023 (page 32) and XE795 2023 (page 31). Those three
  print no program called Interval. The XE395 2023 and XE795 2023 also print a **Fusion** program
  whose text is this same paragraph with "high levels of cardio & strength intensity" in place of
  "high levels of intensity".

**This HIIT is not the CE800 and CE850 HIIT.** On those machines HIIT is a separate configurable
program with its own interval and cool-down settings; on these three XE machines HIIT is simply the
Interval preset profile under a new name.

The XE395 2010 and XE395ENT add a pedal **Incline** paragraph saying the incline alternates between
**25 and 65% of maximum elevation** - see `xe395-2010-programs-incline-profiles`.

**The two oldest XE consoles describe Interval in wording that a Spirit treadmill card already
holds** - see `40t-2026-programs-interval`.

**Six earlier XE printings print this same Interval description, word for word** - XE195 2019
(printed page 24), XG400 2019 (page 24), XE295 2019 (page 25), XE395 2018 (page 26), XE895 2018
(page 28) and XE795 2018 (page 29).

**Two of the six print a second profile beside it.** The XE395 2018 adds an **Incline:** paragraph to
every preset - see `xe395-2010-programs-incline-profiles`. The XE895 2018 prints a **Stride Profile**
chart beside every Work Profile but describes none of them in words - see
`xe895-2018-specs-powered-stride-set-per-segment-with-no-length-printed`.
