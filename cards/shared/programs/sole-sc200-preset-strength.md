The Strength program is designed to increase muscular strength in the lower body. It steadily increases resistance to a high level and forces you to sustain it, which strengthens and tones the legs and glutes.

**About the profile chart.** The manual prints the work level profile as a dot-matrix picture with **no printed level values**. Read from the page image, its shape is a steady staircase rising to a high point and then dropping back. On the SC300 each preset also carries a STEP HEIGHT chart, which has its own card.
