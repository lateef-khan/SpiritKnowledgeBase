The fitness test assesses your fitness level and gives a score at the end.

**The score is not a VO2max number.** The manual says so plainly: it is "just a number to use as a
reference". Take the test every month and the score should rise as your fitness improves. There is no
score chart in these manuals.

- The test takes **6 to 15 minutes**.
- You are asked to maintain a pedal speed of **50 RPM**; the console adjusts the work load
  automatically from your heart rate response to set work levels.
- It ends when you reach your target heart rate, which is **85% of your maximum heart rate
  (Max HR = 220 - your age)**, **or if your HR exceeds 110 BPM for 2 consecutive stages**.

**Programming the test**

1. Press the **Fit-test** button and press Enter.
2. Enter your **Age** with the LEVEL Up and Down keys, then Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight** with the LEVEL Up and Down keys, then Enter.
4. Press **Start** to begin the test, or **Enter** to modify your settings.

There is **no Gender step** on these consoles; the 2013 LCB and LCR manuals have one and these do not.

**Before the test**

- Make sure you are in good health. Check with your physician before performing any exercise **if you
  are over the age of 35 or have pre-existing health conditions**.
- Make sure you have warmed up and stretched.
- Do not take in caffeine before the test.
- Hold the hand grips gently; do not tense up.

**During the test**

- The console must be receiving a **steady heart rate** for the test to begin. Use the hand pulse
  sensors or wear a heart rate chest strap transmitter.
- Maintain a steady **50 RPM**. Below **48 RPM** or above **52 RPM** the console beeps steadily until
  you are back in range.
- The message window always shows your pedal speed on the right side.
- Data shown: **Work in KGM** (kg-m/min, kilogram-force meter/minute), **Work in Watts** (1 watt equals
  **6.11829727787** kg-m/min), **HR** (actual heart rate) and **TGT** (target heart rate that ends the
  test), and **Time**.

**After the test**

- Cool down for about **one to three minutes**.
- Take note of your score: the console returns to the start-up mode on its own after a few minutes.
