The Strength program is designed to increase muscular strength in your lower body. It **steadily
increases resistance to a high level and then keeps you there**, which the manuals say is designed to
strengthen and tone your **legs and gluteus**.

At setup you are asked for a **Max Level**, the peak exertion level of the program. The **factory
setting is level eight** - the highest factory Max Level of the five presets.

**The later XE ellipticals describe Strength differently** - a gradual progression to 100% of maximum
effort sustained for 25% of the workout duration, followed by a brief cool down. That is
`spirit-ce-programs-strength-profile`.
