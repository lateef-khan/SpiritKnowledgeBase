Preset setup on the SC300 follows the SC200 sequence (program button, Enter, Age, Weight, Time, then Max Level: the peak exertion, the highest colored segment of the profile), with one extra choice.

After Max Level, choose whether to turn the Step height profile on for this program. With it off, step height stays manually controlled during the workout but the automated step height changes are off. Press Enter when finished selecting, then Start to begin; Stop steps back one screen. During the workout the Level or Step height keys change resistance or step height at any time, the Display key scrolls the data, and the workout ends with a summary.
