**The omission is in the caption, not on the machine.** Each of these five Dyaco service
manuals draws the console on its Function Button Locations page and prints a "PROGRAM BUTTONS
(...)" caption beside it. The drawing labels a CARDIO key on every one of them; the caption names
every key except Cardio.

| Book | Caption prints | Drawing labels |
|---|---|---|
| CE850 2016 (XE898-SE011) | Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Strength, Interval, 2 Custom, 2HR | MANUAL, HILL, FAT BURN, **CARDIO**, STRENGTH, INTERVAL, CUSTOM 1, CUSTOM 2, HR 1, HR 2 |
| XE895 2016 (XE895-SE022) | Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Strength, Interval, 2 User, 2HR | MANUAL, HILL, FAT BURN, **CARDIO**, STRENGTH, INTERVAL, USER-1, USER-2, HR-1, HR-2 |
| XE195 2016 (XE509S-SE021) | Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Interval, HR | MANUAL, **CARDIO**, HILL, FAT BURN, INTERVAL, HR |
| XE395 2016 (XE539S-SE019) | Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Strength, Interval, 2 User, 2HR | MANUAL, HILL, FAT BURN, **CARDIO**, STRENGTH, INTERVAL, USER-1, USER-2, HR-1, HR-2 |
| XE795 2016 (XE815-SE024) | Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Strength, Interval, Calories, Fusion, 2 User, 2HR | MANUAL, HILL, FAT BURN, **CARDIO**, STRENGTH, INTERVAL, CALORIES, FUSION, USER 1-2, HR 1-2 |

Three sibling books from the same year get it right: the XE295 and XG400 captions read "(Manual,
Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, Interval, User1~2, HR1~2)", and the CE800 2016 caption names
Cardio too.

The same stale caption sits in the XE395 **2010** owner's manual
(`xe395-2010-programs-console-diagram-omits-cardio-from-the-program-buttons`) and in the XBR95 2016
bike service manual (`spirit-bike-programs-seven-presets-adding-calories-and-fusion`). It is a
template line copied between books, not a statement about any one console.

**Read the key strip, not the caption, when a customer asks whether their machine has Cardio.** The
later owner's manuals for these machines describe Cardio in full: XE195 2019 and 2021
(`xe195-2021-programs-four-presets-and-six-programs`), XE395 2018 and XE895 2018
(`spirit-xe-2021-programs-five-presets-and-ten-programs`), XE795 2018 and 2021
(`xe795-2021-programs-seven-presets-including-calories-and-fusion`), and the CE850 2016 owner's
manual (`spirit-ce850-programs-seven-preset-work-level-profiles`).

**None of the five service manuals describes any program.** They print the key strip, a one-line
Quick Start spec naming the keys, and nothing else about programs. The captions are text the
extraction reads cleanly; the drawings are pictures whose OCR comes out garbled or upside down, so
anyone working from the text layer alone sees only the caption.
