All three 2026 manuals print this guidance for their fitness test. It covers the Gerkin protocol on all
three machines and the WFI test on the 4.0T and 8.0T.

## Before the test
- Make sure you are in good health; check with your physician before performing any exercise if you are
  over the age of 35, or have pre-existing health conditions.
- Make sure you have warmed up and stretched before taking the test.
- Do not take in caffeine before the test.
- **Hold the hand grips gently, do not tense up** (printed in the 4.0T and 8.0T manuals; the 4.0T adds
  "if you don't wear your wireless chest strap transmitter". The 7.0T manual omits this bullet).

## During the test
- **The console must be receiving a steady heart rate for the test to begin.**
  - 4.0T: you may use the hand pulse sensors or wear a heart rate chest strap transmitter, **although the
    chest strap transmitter is recommended**.
  - 7.0T: you may wear a heart rate chest strap transmitter (**Polar T31/T34**).
  - 8.0T: you may wear a heart rate sensor. Heart rate accessories are not included.
- **The test starts with a 3 minute warm-up at 3 mph (4.8 km/hr)** before the actual test begins.
- The data shown during the test (7.0T and 8.0T): total elapsed time; incline in percent grade; distance
  in miles or kilometres; speed in mph or km/h; **target heart rate and actual heart rate**.

## After the test
- **Cool down for about one to three minutes.** On the 8.0T a cool down period starts automatically when
  the test completes successfully.
- **Take note of the score, because the console returns to the start-up mode automatically after a few
  minutes.**
