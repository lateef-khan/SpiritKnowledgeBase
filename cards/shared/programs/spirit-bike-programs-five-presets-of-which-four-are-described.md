Five owner's manuals open the preset chapter with **"The fitness bike has five different programs
that have been designed for a variety of workouts. These five programs have factory preset work
level profiles for achieving different goals."** and then describe only **four**: Hill, Fat Burn,
Cardio and Interval.

**On the CR800 2021, CR800 2023 and CU800 2021 the fifth is Strength.** It is named in the strip of
profile pictures printed down the outside margin of those pages, which reads Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio,
Interval, **Strength**, HIIT. The word never appears in the running text, so the manual gives the
name and no description. The description that belongs there is on
`spirit-bike-programs-strength-profile`, taken from the manuals that do print it.

**On the CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 the fifth program is not named anywhere.** Those two books have
no profile strip and no Strength program; searching either whole manual for "strength" returns only
"strengthening the cardio vascular system" on the heart rate page.

**The CR900 2018 and CU900 2018 count four and describe four.** Their chapter is otherwise the same
text. See `spirit-bike-programs-four-presets-hill-fat-burn-cardio-and-interval`.

**The 2024 CR800 and CU800 print the same sentence and the same four descriptions** (printed page
23), with Strength again named only in the strip of profile pictures.
