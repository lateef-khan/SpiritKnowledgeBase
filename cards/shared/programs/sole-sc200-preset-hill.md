The Hill program simulates going up and down a hill. The resistance in the pedals steadily increases and then decreases during the program.

**About the profile chart.** The manual prints the work level profile as a dot-matrix picture with **no printed level values** (labelled SC200 WORK in the SC200 books, LEVEL in the SC300 book). Read from the page image, its shape is a symmetrical stepped pyramid that rises to a peak in the middle and falls away again. On the SC300 each preset also carries a STEP HEIGHT chart, which has its own card.
