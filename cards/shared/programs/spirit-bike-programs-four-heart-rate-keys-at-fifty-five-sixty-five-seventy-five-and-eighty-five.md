**Four heart rate programs, named by the percentage of maximum heart rate they target: HR1 55%, HR2
65%, HR3 75% and HR4 85%.** The XBR55 2023, XBU55 2023 and XBR95 2023 manuals print this sentence
identically, and label the choices on screen **HR 55%, HR 65%, HR 75%** and **HR 85%**.

All four operate the same way and are programmed the same way:

1. Press **PROGRAM** to select HR PROGRAM, then **ENTER**.
2. Press **PROGRAM** again to pick target HR 55%, 65%, 75% or 85%, then **Enter**.
3. **Age**, then Enter. 4. **Weight**, then Enter. 5. **Time**, then Enter.
6. **Heart Rate Target** - the number shown comes from the percentage you chose in step 2, and
   changing it raises or lowers that percentage - then Enter.
7. **Start**. Stop goes back one screen during data entry.
8. Level + or - **during** the program moves your target heart rate, not the resistance.

The console then tries to hold you **within 3 to 5 beats per minute** of the target. **You must wear
the heart rate transmitter strap for these programs**; it is not included.

**None of these four percentages appears on the training page two spreads earlier**, which still
recommends 60% and 80% and works its example at 108 to 144 beats - see
`cu800-2012-programs-target-zone-of-108-to-144-beats`. The manuals never reconcile the two, and the KB
does not harmonise them.

**The XBR25 2023, printed in the same style and the same year, has no heart rate program at all** -
see `spirit-bike-programs-a-bike-manual-with-a-pulse-readout-but-no-heart-rate-program`. The earlier
XBR55, XBU55 and XBR95 books had two keys, at 60% and 80%.

**The Spirit XE ellipticals of 2023 print the same four percentages.** That is a separate card, for a
different product line - see `spirit-xe-2023-programs-four-heart-rate-targets`.
