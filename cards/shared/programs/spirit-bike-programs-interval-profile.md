Interval takes you through high levels of intensity followed by recovery periods of low intensity.
The manual says it develops your **"Fast Twitch"** muscle fibres, used for tasks that are intense and
short in duration: these deplete your oxygen level and spike your heart rate, followed by periods of
recovery and heart rate drop to replenish oxygen, so your cardiovascular system is programmed to use
oxygen more efficiently.

All thirteen Spirit commercial bike owner's manuals print this description word for word. The
CU800ENT 2022, CR800ENT 2023 and CU800ENT 2024 drop the quotation marks around "fast twitch"; the
sentence is otherwise unchanged.

**Interval is a preset profile; HIIT is a separate, customisable program.** Interval has a factory
profile you cannot edit segment by segment; HIIT asks you for the number of intervals and the sprint
and rest times. Ten of the thirteen bikes have both. See the two HIIT cards linked below.
