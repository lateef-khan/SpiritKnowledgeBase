This book differs from the TT8-2016 suite in exactly one preset: the fifth profile is renamed **HIIT** (was Interval), and the test is headed **Fitness Test** (was Fit Test); descriptions and composition otherwise match `sole-tt8-programs-preset-suite-2016`.

The TT8 has **ten built-in programs**: one **Manual** program, **five preset profiles**, one **Custom**, one **Fitness Test**, and two **Heart Rate programs (HR1 and HR2)**. Hill and Strength say **workload** (not speed). The HIIT text is the old Interval text verbatim: "takes you through high levels of intensity followed by periods of low intensity," depleting oxygen then recovering, which "aids in heart rate recovery from intense activities."

Profiles are drawn TT8 SPEED/GRADE dot-matrix pictures with no segment figures. Setup, Custom, Fitness Test and HRC follow the stencil cards (see links). One SKU covers both listed ids.
