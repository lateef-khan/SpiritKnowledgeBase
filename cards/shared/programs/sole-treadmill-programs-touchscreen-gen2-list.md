Console UI images reflect the software at production and may change with internet updates. First launch offers guest entry or a new profile (recommended); **maximum 9 profiles**, each protectable with a passcode. A **SOLE+** account links by scanning the console QR code in the SOLE+ app (one console profile links to one SOLE+ account at a time).

The Home screen shows activity overview plus shortcuts: immediate **Start**, a manually controlled timed **Timer**, and favorite programs. Tabs across the top: **Programs, Fitness Tests, Templates, Favorites** (the F89 adds **Classes** and **Collections** tabs: see its classes card).

- **Manual (Timer)**: timed workout, speed and incline user-controlled at any time.
- **Hill, Fatburn, Cardio, Strength, HIIT**: preset speed/incline patterns; **Total Time and Max Speed** define duration and intensity. Segment levels are charted (see the two chart cards; F80/ST90 print the 20-percent chart, F85/F89/TT8 the 40-percent chart).
- **5K Run, 10K Run**: target distance set automatically (5/10 km); the setup page shows the **estimated total time** for the chosen target speed. Ends at the distance.
- **Heart Rate** and **Custom** have their own cards; finished or adjusted programs save as templates (see links). Tap any program card for its setup page; adjust with ruler drag, +/-, or the numpad. **"Start This Program"** (screen) or the physical **START** key begins the workout.

Model notes: the ST90 Home screen also offers **Free Mode** (see `sole-st90-programs-free-mode`); the TT8 books say **Elevation** where the others say Incline.
