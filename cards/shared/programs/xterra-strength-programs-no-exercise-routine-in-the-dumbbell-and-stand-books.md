**No.** The four books hold a safety list, a pack list, the weight-selection (or assembly) steps, a
troubleshooting entry, the warranty, an exploded view and a parts list. None prints an exercise, a
set-and-rep scheme or a routine.

The only sentence about training is safety item 11, the same in all four: *"In order to achieve
significant exercise results and not to overburden the muscles, the exercise frequency should be
administered within a reasonable range. **Consult a personal trainer to learn correct posture,
technique, methods, and frequency.**"* Item 5 adds the usual "Before beginning this or any exercise
program, consult a physician."

Proved twice: the contents pages list no exercise section; grep for *exercise*, *routine*, *curl*,
*press*, *squat*, *rep*, *set*, *workout* and *train* hits only the safety page; and the two
low-word pages of each book (the exploded-view spread and the back cover), rendered, hold no text
beyond the folio and the address line.
