1. Select the program with the Program up/down keys (the profile shows in the dot-matrix
   window) and press **Enter**.
2. Enter your personal data with the Level up/down keys, pressing Enter after each:

   | Book | Order of prompts |
   |---|---|
   | SB500 | Age, Weight, Time, Max Level |
   | SB4.5r | Weight, Age, Time, Max Level |
   | FS5.8e, FS5.9e | Weight, Age, Time, Max Level, then **Max Incline** with the Incline keys |

   **Max Level** "is the peak exertion level you will experience during the program (work
   level at the top of the hill)"; the profile is scaled to it.
3. Press **Start** to begin.
4. Level up/down during the program changes only the work level of the **current segment**.
5. Press Enter to switch the data displayed.
6. At the end the message window shows a workout summary, then returns to the start-up
   display.

The presets themselves are on their own cards: seven on the SB500, seven on the SB4.5r,
eight on the FS5.8e and FS5.9e.
