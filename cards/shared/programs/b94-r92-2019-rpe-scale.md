The Rate of Perceived Exertion (RPE), also known as the **Borg scale**, was developed by Swedish physiologist G.A.V. Borg. It rates exercise intensity from **6 to 20** depending on how you feel.

| Rating | Perception of Effort |
|---|---|
| 6 | Minimal |
| 7 | Very, very light |
| 8 | Very, very light + |
| 9 | Very light |
| 10 | Very light + |
| 11 | Fairly light |
| 12 | Comfortable |
| 13 | Somewhat hard |
| 14 | Somewhat hard + |
| 15 | Hard |
| 16 | Hard + |
| 17 | Very hard |
| 18 | Very hard + |
| 19 | Very, very hard |
| 20 | Maximal |

You can get an approximate heart rate for each rating by **adding a zero**. A rating of 12 is roughly 120 beats per minute.

Your RPE varies with stress level, physical and emotional health, temperature, humidity, time of day, and when and what you last ate.
