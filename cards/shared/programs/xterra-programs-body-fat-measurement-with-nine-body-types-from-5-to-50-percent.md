1. Select **Body Fat** with UP/DOWN and press ENTER.
2. "**Male**" flashes - change the gender with UP/DOWN, press ENTER.
3. A height of "**175**" flashes - adjust, press ENTER.
4. A weight of "**75**" flashes - adjust, press ENTER.
5. An age of "**30**" flashes - adjust, press ENTER.
6. Press START/STOP to start the measurement, holding the hand pulse grips. **After 15
   seconds** the display shows Body Fat %, BMR, BMI and BODY TYPE.
7. Press START/STOP to return to the main display.

The books print the height and weight defaults without units (175 and 75 read as
centimetres and kilograms).

**Nine body types** by the FAT% calculated:

| Type | Fat % | Type | Fat % | Type | Fat % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5-9 | 4 | 20-24 | 7 | 35-39 |
| 2 | 10-14 | 5 | 25-29 | 8 | 40-44 |
| 3 | 15-19 | 6 | 30-34 | 9 | 45-50 |

"BMR: Basal Metabolic Rate is the amount of daily energy expended at rest. BMI: Body Mass
Index is a general body type based on height and weight."

The RSX1500 climber prints the same test with imperial defaults (5'8", 165 lb); see
`see_also`.
