*"METs stands for metabolic equivalent, which is one way that exercise physiologists estimate how much
work is being done during physical activity. This program controls the patient workload to maintain a
steady MET level throughout the exercise session: perfect for heart patients who are prescribed
exercise in METs by their cardiologist."*

**1 MET** is the energy produced relative to body mass at rest - sitting and reading is 1 MET - and
**is equivalent to approximately 3.5 millilitres of O2 per kg of body weight per minute**. Light
activities burn up to 3 times as many calories as rest, moderate 3-6 times, vigorous more than 6.

*"METs Target uses your 8.0U's resistance system to help you achieve your METs target. The program
automatically changes resistance gradually to maintain the METs at target."*

1. Set your **target METs** and **exercise time**.
2. Press **Start**.

*The setup screenshot on the second page is a treadmill's: its caption reads "Set your target METs.
**Speed and incline** will change automatically." A bike has neither. The body text above is the
sentence that fits the machine.*
