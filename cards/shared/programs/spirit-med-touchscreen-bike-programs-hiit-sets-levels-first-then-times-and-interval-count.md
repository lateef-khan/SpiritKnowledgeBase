*"The HIIT (High, Intensity Interval Training) program takes advantage of the latest trend in
wellness. During the program you will perform short bursts of high intensity sprinting followed by
short rest periods."* *(the stray comma is printed)* *"HIIT is a highly customizable interval training
program. You can select the number of intervals, time of each interval Sprint and Rest periods and
the work intensity of the levels."*

1. **Set the resistance level for sprint and rest.**
2. **Click Next for next step.**
3. **Set the sprint time, rest time and how many intervals you like.**
4. **Program time calculate automatically.** *(printed as shown)*

**Levels first, times second** - the reverse of the LED-console bikes, whose HIIT takes 20 to 60
second sprints after a three minute warm-up (`spirit-bike-programs-hiit-sprint-of-twenty-to-sixty-seconds`).
**No warm-up, cool-down, default or range is printed in the text.**

**What the two screenshots show, read from the render** - the text layer carries none of it: the
first screen has **Sprint Level 10** and **Rest Level 5** on pickers; the second has **Sprint Time
00:30**, **Rest Time 00:30** and **Intervals 10**, with the time pickers stepping in **five second**
increments and a program time badge reading **30:00**. Whether those are defaults or an example the
page does not say, and ten intervals of thirty plus thirty seconds make ten minutes, not thirty. The
second screen's graph is captioned "Level profile, km/h" - a treadmill legend on a bike page.

The 8.0U and 8.5R 2025 books print this page word for word, as does the 8.5UE arm ergometer
(`85ue-2025-programs-hiit-with-a-calculated-program-time`).
