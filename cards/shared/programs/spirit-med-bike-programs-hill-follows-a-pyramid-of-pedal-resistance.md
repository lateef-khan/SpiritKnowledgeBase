*"This program follows a triangle or pyramid type of gradual progression to simulate going up and
down a hill. The resistance in the pedals will steadily increase and then decrease during the
program."* (4.0R, 4.0U, 7.0R and 7.0U 2025)

**The three Dyaco editions print the shorter form**: *"The Hill program simulates going up and down
a hill. The resistance in the pedals will steadily increase and then decrease during the program."*
(PT 4.0 R, PT 4.0 U, MED 7.0R 2021). Same profile, one clause fewer.

**No percentage figures are printed** - not the "10% of maximum effort" start, not the "peak that
lasts a tenth of the workout" that the commercial CR/CU bike books give for their Hill
(`spirit-bike-programs-hill-profile`). Do not carry those figures onto these machines. On the
treadmills Hill moves speed and grade; here it moves resistance only.
