The i-Strength electronic resistance system lets a user train in **four distinct
modalities**, chosen from the console home screen:

| Mode | What the home screen says it does |
|---|---|
| **PURE** | Traditional weight training |
| **ECCENTRIC** | Increased resistance on return |
| **ISOKINETIC** | Constant speed movement, regardless of force applied |
| **ELASTIC BAND** | Resistance grows as range increases |

A user can **switch mode from the summary screen at the end of a set** - the other three
modes sit along the bottom of that screen.

The system is described as electronically adaptive rather than a weight stack: the
manual's claim is that a member can sit down and start as fast as on a stack machine,
with benefits that exceed one.

**These are Spirit modes, not Sole SRVO modes.** The Sole SRVO has three numbered modes
- Standard, Eccentric, Isokinetic - and its eccentric figure is a fixed 10:7 ratio. The
Spirit console has four named modes and its eccentric increase is user-set from +10% to
+50%. The names overlap; the numbers do not. See `srvo-mode-2-eccentric`.
