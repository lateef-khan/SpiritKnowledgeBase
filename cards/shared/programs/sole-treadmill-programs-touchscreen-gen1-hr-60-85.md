This is the F85 variant offering **60% or 85%** of age-predicted maximum heart rate. The TT8-2021 and ST90 books offer 60% or 80% instead: see `sole-treadmill-programs-touchscreen-gen1-hr-60-80`.

Set a target time, then **adjust speed manually while running to hold the target heart rate; the treadmill controls the incline automatically**. An HR monitoring device is required. The program ends when the time is up, **or if your HR runs 20% higher than target**. "Do not exceed your limits!"
