**HR auto pilot only works in the Fitness programs - Manual, Hill, Plateau, Interval and Custom.**
When you are exercising in one of them and decide to hold the heart rate you are at, press **auto
pilot**: the console switches to HR control and maintains your current heart rate.

- **You must wear a heart rate strap to enter this mode.** If no heart rate is detected the message
  window shows **NO HEART RATE**.
- At the end of the HR auto pilot program a workout summary is displayed in the message window.

The 4.0R and 4.0U 2025 books and the Dyaco PT 4.0 R and 4.0 U editions print this page in the same
words. The 4.0T treadmill prints the same feature with 5K added to its list of fitness programs
(`40t-2026-programs-hr-auto-pilot`). **The XE550 elliptical's Auto-pilot is a different feature** -
it works at any time in any program and its manual states no strap requirement - and must not be
answered from this card.
