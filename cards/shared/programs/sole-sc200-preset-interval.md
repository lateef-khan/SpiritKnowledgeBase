The Interval program takes you through high levels of intensity followed by periods of low intensity. It builds endurance by depleting the oxygen level and then giving recovery periods to replenish it, so the cardiovascular system learns to use oxygen more efficiently; the spikes in heart rate between recovery periods also aid heart rate recovery from intense activity.

**About the profile chart.** The manual prints the work level profile as a dot-matrix picture with **no printed level values**. Read from the page image, its shape is a row of tall narrow columns over a low base. On the SC300 each preset also carries a STEP HEIGHT chart, which has its own card.
