HIIT is a fully customisable interval training program: short bursts of high intensity sprinting
followed by short rest periods.

1. Select **HIIT**, press Enter, then enter your **Age**.
2. Enter your **Weight**.
3. Number of **intervals**: default **10**, range **3 to 15**. One interval equals 1 Sprint and 1
   Rest segment.
4. **Sprint Level** - the resistance level during the Sprint segments.
5. **Rest Level** - the resistance level during the Rest segments.
6. **Interval time**. The display shows `Sprint :30 | Rest :30` (the CR800, CU800 and CU900 2025
   print it as `Sprint Time:30` and `Rest Time:30`). The Sprint time blinks first: adjust it from
   **20 to 60 seconds**, press Enter, then adjust the Rest time the same way.
7. The display shows the total time for the HIIT workout. Press Start. There is a **3-minute
   warm-up** before the first Sprint, at **resistance level 5**, adjustable manually.
8. During HIIT the dot matrix is a **speed** display, not a power or resistance display. A blinking
   LED at the first Sprint segment is the target speed LED and marks **90 pedal rpm**; lights below
   it come on the faster you pedal, and past 90 rpm the target LED moves up. Maintain at least
   90 rpm through the Sprints. The **Rest** segments are set to **resistance level 5** and you may
   pedal at any speed while your heart rate recovers. Resistance can be adjusted manually in both.
9. After the last Sprint there is a **3-minute cool-down**. Stop bypasses it and shows the summary.

**Steps 7 to 9 are printed only by the CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900 2025 and CU900 2025.** The
CR800 2021, CR800 2023 and CU800 2021 manuals end their HIIT chapter at step 6 and never print the
warm-up, the 90 rpm target or the cool-down. The steps above are safe for those three only in that
the first six match word for word.

**The touchscreen 800ENT bikes run a different HIIT** - 20 to 90 second segments and a warm-up at
half the sprint level. See `spirit-bike-programs-hiit-sprint-of-twenty-to-ninety-seconds`. The
CR900ENT and CU900ENT have no HIIT at all.
