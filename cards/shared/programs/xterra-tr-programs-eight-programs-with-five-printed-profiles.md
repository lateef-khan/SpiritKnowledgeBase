The Preset Programs page says: "The treadmill has **eight different programs** that have been
designed for a variety of workouts. These eight programs have factory preset work level
profiles for achieving different goals." **It then names, pictures and describes five:**
Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength and HIIT. The selection step also lists only those five
("Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, or Hiit").

The other programs the book documents are set up through the same Enter/Program key:
**Fusion**, **5K** and **10K**, **Countdown (target time, distance or calories)**,
**Custom 1 and Custom 2**, and the **Heart Rate program**. Which of these the book counts
among the eight is not stated.

The profile pictures on pages 26 and 27 (printed 25 and 26) are dot-matrix speed and incline
pictures with **no figures**; the console is 20 columns by 10 rows and the boxes "only show
an approximate level".

Each preset program has a maximum speed and incline that is displayed in the Message Center
when the workout is chosen.
