1. Select a program, then press **Enter** to begin customizing it with your personal data, or just
   press **Start** to begin the program with the default settings.
2. The **Time** window blinks with the default value of **20 minutes**. Adjust with any of the
   Up/Down keys, then press **Enter**.
3. The **Incline** window now blinks a value indicating your **Age**. Entering the correct age
   affects the Heart Rate Bar Graph accuracy and is also needed for the HR programs. Adjust, then
   press Enter.
4. The **Distance** window now blinks a value indicating your **Bodyweight**. Entering your correct
   bodyweight affects the Kcal readout accuracy. Adjust, then press Enter.
5. The **Speed** window now blinks, showing the preset top speed of the selected program. Adjust,
   then press Enter. Each program has various speed changes throughout; this limits the highest
   speed the program will attain during your workout.
6. Press **Start** to begin your workout.
7. A **3 minute warm-up** begins. Press Start to bypass it and go straight to the workout. During
   the warm-up the clock counts down from 3 minutes.

You may press **Start at any time** during programming to begin with only the settings you have
modified at that point.

**This console gives no list of programs that skip the warm-up.** The later twelve-program console
does, and it also adds a Program button to step through the list and a Stop button that returns to
the previous screen - see `ct850-2020-starting-and-customising-a-program`.
