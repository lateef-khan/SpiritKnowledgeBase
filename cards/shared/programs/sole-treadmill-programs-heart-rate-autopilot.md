**"This unique feature allows the user to instantly enter the Heart Rate Control mode at any time with the press of a button."** The **HR2 program button doubles as the Auto-Pilot button**.

- **During any program**, pressing Auto-Pilot enters HRC immediately.
- **The heart rate at the moment of pressing is held automatically** as the new target.
- The **elevation/incline up/down buttons change the target** while in Auto-Pilot mode.

Auto-Pilot needs the same steady chest-strap signal as the programmed HRC (see `sole-treadmill-programs-hrc-70-90-autopilot`). The 2011/2013 menu consoles and the WF63 drop this feature; the 2026 consoles have no equivalent button.
