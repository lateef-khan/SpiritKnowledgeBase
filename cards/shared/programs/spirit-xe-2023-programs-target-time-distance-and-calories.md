Target programs count a chosen item **down** to zero and then end the workout.

1. Press **PROGRAM** to select **TARGET PROGRAM**, then Enter.
2. Press **PROGRAM** to pick **Target Time**, **Target Distance** or **Target Calories**, then Enter.
   You may instead just press **Start** to begin with the default values.
3. If you pressed Enter, the Message Center blinks your **Age (default is 35)**. Adjust with + / -,
   then Enter.
4. It blinks your **Body Weight (default is 155 lbs / 70 kgs)**. Adjust, then Enter.
5. The target item blinks:
   - **Target Time** - default **5 minutes**
   - **Target Distance** - default **3.00 mile**
   - **Target Calories** - default **300**

   Adjust it, or accept the default, then press Enter.
6. Press **Start** to begin your workout.

**The body weight default here is 155 lbs**, which is also what the 5K and 10K programs in the same
manual use. That figure differs from the 100 and 150 lbs printed by the XE795 2021 console.

**This replaced the XE795 2021's Calories program**, which asked for an average pedal RPM and a top
resistance level and calculated the workout time for you - see
`xe795-2021-programs-calories-program-counts-down-to-a-calorie-goal`. The 2023 Target Calories program
does none of that.
