**These are the 60% and 85% figures.** The twelve-program CT800 generation targets **65% and 80%**
instead - see `ct800-2020-programs-heart-rate-control-65-and-80-percent`.

Heart Rate Control (HRC) uses the treadmill's **incline system**, not speed, to control your heart
rate. Increases and decreases in elevation affect heart rate much more efficiently than changes in
speed alone. The HRC program changes elevation gradually to achieve the programmed target heart
rate.

During setup you choose either the **Weight Control (HR-1)** program or the **Cardiovascular (HR-2)**
program.

- **Weight Control (HR-1)** attempts to maintain your heart rate at **60% of your Maximum Heart
  Rate.**
- **Cardiovascular (HR-2)** attempts to maintain your heart rate at **85% of your Maximum Heart
  Rate.**

Your Maximum Heart Rate is a formula that subtracts your age from a constant of **220**. Your HR
setting is calculated automatically during setup when you enter your age.

## Programming steps

**You must receive a strong, steady value in the heart rate window or the program will not start.**

1. Press the **HR1** or **HR2** button.
2. The **Pulse** window blinks, showing the default HR for this program. Adjust it and press
   **Enter**, or just press Enter to accept the default.
3. The **Calorie** window blinks, showing bodyweight. Adjust and press Enter, or press Enter to
   accept the default.
4. The **Pulse** window blinks, showing age. Adjust and press Enter, or press Enter to accept the
   default. **Adjusting age changes the Target HR value.**
5. The **Time** window blinks. Adjust the time and press Enter.
6. Press **Start** to begin the program.
7. During the program you may **increase or decrease the target heart rate with the Incline
   Up/Down buttons.**

*As printed, both manuals show the target heart rate in the Pulse window at step 2 and the age in
the Pulse window again at step 4, with bodyweight in the Calorie window. That is what the source
says and it is preserved.*

The CT900 targets the same 60% and 85% but is set up from a numeric keypad with different on-screen
prompts - see `ct900-heart-rate-control-hrc`. The two procedures are not interchangeable.
