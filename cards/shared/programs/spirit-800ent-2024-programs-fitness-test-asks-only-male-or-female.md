The VO2 program is based on the **YMCA protocol** and is a sub-maximal test using pre-determined,
fixed work levels based on your heart rate readings as the test progresses. It takes **6 to 15
minutes** depending on your fitness.

**The test ends** when your heart rate reaches **85% of maximum** at any time during the test, **or**
when your heart rate is between **110 bpm and 85%** at the end of two consecutive stages. A VO2max
score is given at the end. The protocol uses **two to four 3-minute stages** of continuous exercise.

You are asked to choose **Male or Female** at the beginning; the choice decides which workload chart
is used. The program tile adds a caption the LED consoles do not print: *level changes
automatically until you reach [target]; **stop if you feel ill or distressed***.

**This console asks for nothing else.** The LED consoles in the same 2024 range ask for gender,
then age, then weight - see `spirit-ce-programs-fitness-test-entry-steps` and
`spirit-bike-programs-fitness-test-entry-gender-age-and-weight`. On the touchscreen, age and weight
come from the home screen (illustrated at 36 years, 155 lb), not from a test setup step.

**The manual does not print the de-conditioned-male / very-fit-female advice** the LED manuals give
about choosing the other chart. The workload chart values themselves are on
`spirit-bike-programs-fitness-test-workload-charts`.
