The VO2 program is based on the **YMCA protocol** and is a sub-maximal test that uses pre-determined,
fixed work levels based on your heart rate readings as the test progresses.

- It takes **6 to 15 minutes**, depending on your level of fitness.
- It **ends** when your heart rate reaches **85% of maximum** at any time during the test, **or**
  when your heart rate is between **110 bpm and 85%** at the end of **two consecutive stages**.
- At the end a **VO2max** score is given. VO2max stands for Volume of Oxygen uptake, a measurement
  of how much oxygen you need to perform a known amount of work.
- The YMCA protocol uses **two to four 3-minute stages** of continuous exercise.
- You are asked to choose **Male or Female** at the beginning. The choice decides which workload
  chart the test uses.

**The advice on which option to pick is worded two ways.** The CE800 2013, CE800 2021 and CE800 2023
say the only caveats are that a very de-conditioned **Male** needs to choose option Female, and a
very conditioned **female** needs to choose option Male. The CE850 2020, CE850 2022 and CES880 2025
soften it to "you may want to choose". The CE800ENT prints no caveat sentence at all.

**The CE800 2013 heads it "Fit-Test Program" and calls it "the fitness test", not "the VO2
program".** Everything after that first phrase is the same.

**Three machines have no Fitness Test**: the CE850 2016, the CE900 2021 and the CE900 2025 owner's
manuals never mention YMCA or VO2max.
