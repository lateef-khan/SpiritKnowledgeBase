The XBR55 2019, XBR55 2021, XBU55 2019 and XBU55 2021 manuals say the bike has **five different
programs** with factory preset work level profiles, and **describe all five**:

**Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength and Interval.**

**Ten programs in all**, and here the badge is right: the five presets plus **Manual**, **User 1**,
**User 2**, **HR1** and **HR2** make the "Ten innovative programs offer a variety of work-outs" the
console badge claims. The key strip is printed as a flat image; OCR of it on the
XBR55 2019 console page reads MANUAL, HILL, FATBURN, CARDIO, STRENGTH, INTERVAL, USER, USER-2 and two
HR keys, which is the same ten.

**Count five described, not four.** The commercial CR800 and CU800 books count five and print only
four descriptions, which is a different fact about a different console - see
`spirit-bike-programs-five-presets-of-which-four-are-described`. On these four residential books the
count and the descriptions agree.

**The two 2021 printings are the same book as their 2019 twins on this chapter.** Measured on native
PDF text over the programs pages, XBR55 2019 against XBR55 2021 is **99.1%** at word level and XBU55
2019 against XBU55 2021 is **98.9%**; the only change of substance is the removal of the named
third-party apps, which is a console fact and not a program one. **No program name, level or
percentage changed.**

**The XBR55 and XBU55 2023 books moved to six presets** - Strength stayed, Interval became HIIT, and
Fusion arrived - see `spirit-bike-programs-six-presets-adding-hiit-and-fusion`. The XBR25 2019 of the
same era has four presets and no Strength.
