*"The symmetry program may aid in achieving a more balanced exercise stroke for patients with
bi-lateral deficiencies, such as stroke patients and post-op knee patients."* It measures left and
right power through the pedal range and shows a graph of leg power symmetry on the dot matrix, as
visual feedback for improving the involved limb's strength.

## Running it

- Press the **symmetry key**, then **enter**.
- The message window prompts for **age, weight and time**. Enter each with the up and down keys or
  the numeric key pad, pressing enter to accept.
- Press **start** to begin, or **enter** to go back and modify; **stop** goes back one screen.
- During the program the **enter** key scrolls through **watts, symmetry and METS** in the message
  window.

## For best results

**The symmetry program starts at level 1 and the resistance must be increased manually** with the up
arrow. *"Make sure to set the resistance to a level where the patient is doing enough work to
generate a meaningful measurement. It is recommended to set the resistance as high as the patient
can perform without discomfort, but low enough so they can complete full pedal strokes. **Very low
resistance settings result in erratic or inconsistent measurements.**"*

## Reading the graph

The sample the manual prints shows an average watt measurement where **the left leg is producing
more power than the right, 41 vs. 34 watts**, and the graph reflects the higher wattage of the left
leg. **If the power is equal in both legs only two dots are lit on the bottom centre of the graphic
screen.** Press **Enter** to view the Symmetry data.

**It also works for upper body only.** *"When pushing the handles, the graph will be reversed (left
side will actually be displaying right arm information). It will be correct if the patient is
pulling."*

**Note: the watt readings are not force measurements.** *"The symmetry program uses a power table
and velocity measurements to generate the watt readings; they are not from direct force
measurements."*

**The 8.5S touchscreen version reports a Symmetry Index percentage as well** and uses a different
worked example - see `85s-2025-programs-symmetry-index-and-watt-measurement`.
