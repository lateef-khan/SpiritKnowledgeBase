To find the benefit range you want to train in, first work out your **Maximum Heart Rate**:
**220 minus your age**. Your heart rate training zone is **50% to 90%** of that maximum.

**These manuals print 60% and 80%.** 60% of your MHR is the zone for burning fat, 80% for
strengthening the cardiovascular system, and 60% to 80% is the zone to stay in for maximum benefit.

Worked example for someone **40 years old**:

- 220 - 40 = **180** (maximum heart rate)
- 180 x .6 = **108** beats per minute (60% of maximum)
- 180 x .8 = **144** beats per minute (80% of maximum)
- So for a 40 year old the training zone would be **108 to 144** beats per minute.

If you enter your age during programming the console performs this calculation automatically.

**The CE800 2013 words the first figure differently**: "60% of your MHR is the **zone that burns
fat** while 80% is **for** strengthening the cardio vascular system", where the other two say
"recommended for". The numbers are the same.

**Four different figure pairs appear across the ten Spirit commercial elliptical owner's manuals.**
The CE800 2021, CE800 2023, CE850 2020 and CE850 2022 print 60%/85% and 108 to 153; the CE800ENT
prints 65%/85% and 117 to 153; the CE900 2025 prints 65%/80% with a printed answer of 117 to
**225**. The CE900 2021 owner's manual has no training-zone page at all.

**The CES880 2025 also names its heart rate programs 65% and 80%**, which its own page here does not
match. The CE800 2013 and CE850 2016 have a single HR key and no such clash.

**All eleven Spirit residential XE owner's manuals print this same 60% / 80% guidance and the same
108-to-144 example, value for value** - the XE150/XE350/XE550 (printed page 29), XE100/XE200/XE300
(page 29), XE395 2010 (page 24), XE195 (page 25), XE295 (page 31), XG400 (page 30), both XE795 2021
printings (page 30), XE395ENT (page 40), XE395 2023 (page 26) and XE795 2023 (page 25). **No XE
manual carries the CE900 2025's arithmetic slip** (that manual prints "180 x .80 = 225"); every XE
manual prints 180 x .6 = 108 and 180 x .8 = 144.

They split into the same two wordings the commercial manuals use. The XE150/XE350/XE550,
XE100/XE200/XE300 and XE395 2010 say "60% of your MHR is the **zone that burns fat** while 80% is
**for** strengthening the cardio vascular system", as the CE800 2013 does; the eight later XE
manuals say "**recommended for**".

**Every one of the eleven closes the page by saying the heart rate monitor may be used without a
Heart Rate program, "during manual mode or during any of the nine different programs".** No XE
machine actually has nine programs - the count printed elsewhere in the same manuals is four, five,
six, seven, eight or ten depending on the model - so that sentence is boilerplate, not a lineup. The
2012 and 2016 CT800 and the 2018 CT850 manuals carry the same sentence.

The XE100/XE200/XE300, XE150/XE350/XE550 and XE395 2010 manuals end that sentence with "The Heart
Rate Control program automatically controls resistance at the pedals", which on an elliptical is
correct; the same sentence appears as bike boilerplate on several Spirit treadmills.

**Where this page sits in the XE150/XE350/XE550 manual matters.** That book prints its heart rate
chapter once, inside the XE350 and XE550 operation section. The XE150 console has no HR 1 / HR 2
keys at all: its heart rate program offers **55%, 75%, 90% or a custom target** - see
`xe150-2005-programs-target-hr-program`. The 60% / 80% figures on
this page are the general training-zone guidance, not the XE150's program settings.
