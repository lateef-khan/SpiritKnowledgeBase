1. Press the **Fit Test** button and press **Enter**. On the CR800 2021, CR800 2023 and CU800 2021
   there is no Fit Test button: use the single **Program** button to choose the Fitness Test program,
   then Enter.
2. The display shows **Gender Male**, with "Male" blinking. Use the Up and Down arrows to change it
   to Female, then Enter. **The choice of gender determines which workload chart is used for the
   test.**
3. Enter your **Age**, then Enter.
4. Enter your **Weight**, then Enter.
5. Press **Start** to begin the test, or Enter to modify your settings.

**The CR800 2021, CR800 2023 and CU800 2021 stop at "Now press Start to begin the test"** and do not
offer the Enter-to-modify option at step 5. They also print only "The Dot Matrix Message Center will
display Gender" without naming Male as the value shown first.

**On the touchscreen bikes the fields are tapped on a setup screen instead.** The CR900ENT and
CU900ENT print no gender step at all - see
`spirit-bike-programs-fitness-test-on-the-touchscreen-900-is-up-to-four-stages`.
