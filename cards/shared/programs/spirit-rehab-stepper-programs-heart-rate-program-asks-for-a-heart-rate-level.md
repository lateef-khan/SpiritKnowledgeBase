Press **HR**, then **Enter**, and answer four questions in the message window:

1. **Age** - up/down keys or the numeric key pad, then Enter.
2. **Weight** - up/down keys or the numeric key pad, then Enter.
3. **Time** - adjust, then Enter.
4. **Heart rate Level** - *"the heart rate level you will experience during the program"*. Adjust,
   then Enter.

Then press **Start**. Pressing **Enter** instead goes back to modify the settings, and **Stop** goes
back one screen at any point during data entry.

**During the program the Up and Down keys move the target heart rate**, not the workload directly -
the manual says pressing them *"will allow you to change your target heart rate at any time during
the program"*. Scrolling the message window data is the Enter key on the MS300 and the MED books,
and the **Display** key on the 2025 books.

At the end, **Start** repeats the program and **Stop** exits; the finished session can be saved to a
User key (MS300, MED books) or the **Facility** key (2025 books).

## The manual gives no number to set it to

There is **no default level, no percentage of maximum, and no formula** anywhere in any of these
books - see
`spirit-rehab-stepper-programs-no-maximum-heart-rate-formula-and-no-training-zone-page`. The HR
percent bar graph beside the display is scaled 50% to 90% and needs the correct age entered in Set
Up to mean anything. **Do not import the 8.5S's 65%-of-220-minus-age default**, which belongs to a
different console.

## The pulse it holds is not a medical measurement

Grip the hand pulse sensors or wear a 5 kHz chest strap. Every one of these books says in its safety
list that **the pulse sensors are not medical devices** and that the heart rate reading is an
exercise aid only. A rehabilitation patient whose heart rate matters clinically should not be
monitored by this console.

## This page was reported absent once and is not

An earlier reading of the MS300, 7.0S and 7.5S books concluded the HR key had no described program.
It does, in all five: search for **`HEART RATE PROGRAM OPERATION`** in the 2025 books and
**`Heart rate program operation`** in the MS300 and MED books.
