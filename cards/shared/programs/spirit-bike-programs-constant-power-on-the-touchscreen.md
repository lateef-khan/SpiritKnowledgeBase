All five ENT touchscreen bikes describe it in two sentences:

> A watts program is a controllable constant power whose level adjusts when the speed is changed.
> Choose either **faster pedalling at a lower resistance level** or **slower pedaling at a higher
> resistance level**.

**The CU800ENT 2024 renames the program Watts**; the other four call it **Constant Power**. The
description is identical.

**None of the five manuals prints a setup procedure or a target watt figure** for this program - no
default, no range, no steps. The LED consoles do: see `spirit-bike-programs-constant-watt-program`.
