**In Fusion you alternate mid-to-high intensity cardiovascular intervals on the bike, strength
exercises off the bike, and low intensity recovery intervals on the bike.** You need a pair of objects
that can be comfortably held in your hands - dumbbells, water jugs, soup cans - for **4 of the 5**
strength exercises the program uses.

Setting it up:

1. Select **Fusion**, then **Enter**.
2. **Age** (default **35**). Enter.
3. **Body Weight**. Enter. The manual warns that the calorie count in this program will not be
   accurate, because the machine cannot calculate calories expended while strength training.
4. **Top resistance level** (default and minimum **Level 5**). This is the level for **all** cardio
   intervals, and it can be changed at any time during the program. Enter.
5. **Number of intervals** (default **10**): **10** = 5 cardio and 5 strength, **20** = 10 and 10, or
   **30** = 15 and 15. Enter.
6. **Interval time** (default **2:00**) - the length of each cardio interval. As a general rule the
   longer the interval the less resistance you need, and the more repetitions you do the less weight
   you need. Enter.
7. **Recovery time** (default **0:30**), taken after both the cardio and the strength interval. Enter.

**Caution printed with it:** the shorter the recovery, the longer your heart rate stays elevated. If
you are new to exercise, or have just resumed after a long break, make the recovery interval at least
as long as the cardio interval.

**Seven residential bike manuals print this program**: the three LED XBR95 books of 2016, 2018 and
2021, and all four 2023 books - XBR25, XBR55, XBU55 and XBR95. Every figure above is identical in all
seven.

**The 2023 books also print a one-paragraph profile description for Fusion** on the preset page,
which is the HIIT/Interval paragraph with "cardio & strength intensity" in place of "intensity". The
XBR95 LED books print the same paragraph under FUSION.

**The XBR25 2019, XBR55, XBU55 and the two ENT touchscreens have no Fusion program.**

**The Spirit XE ellipticals print the same procedure.** Separate card, different product line - see
`spirit-xe-programs-fusion-alternates-cardio-intervals-with-dumbbell-work`.
