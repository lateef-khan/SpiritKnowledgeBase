To start the HR program, press the **HR** key then the **Enter** button and follow the directions in
the message window.

1. Press the **HR** key, then press the **Enter** key.
2. The message window asks for your **Age**. Enter it with the Up and Down keys or the numeric key
   pad, then press Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight** with the Up and Down keys or the numeric key pad, then press enter.
4. Enter the **Time**, then press enter.
5. Adjust the **Heart rate Level** - the heart rate level you will experience during the program -
   then press enter.
6. Press **Start** to begin. You can also go back and modify your settings by pressing the **Enter**
   key. *At any time during the editing of data you can press the **Stop** key to go back one level,
   or screen.*
7. **To increase or decrease the workload at any time during the program, press the Up or Down key.
   This changes your target heart rate at any time during the program.**
8. During the HR program you can scroll the data in the message window with the adjacent **Display**
   key.
9. When the program ends you may press **Start** to run it again, **Stop** to exit, or the
   **Facility** key to save what you just did as a custom user program.

**The manual gives no percentage presets for this program.** Unlike the XT and 4.0T consoles, which
offer fixed targets such as 55%, 65%, 75% and 85% of maximum heart rate, the MT200 asks for a heart
rate level directly. The age entered in Set Up is what the console's heart rate bar graph is scaled
against.

Both MT200 owner's manuals print these steps in the same words. The 7.0T owner's manual has no
heart rate program section at all.
