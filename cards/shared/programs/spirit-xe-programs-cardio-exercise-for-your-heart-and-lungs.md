The Cardio program is designed to increase your **cardio vascular function** - simply said, exercise
for your heart and lungs. It builds up your heart muscle and increases blood flow and lung capacity.
It achieves that by incorporating a **higher level of exertion with slight fluctuations in work**.

At setup you are asked for a **Max Level**, the peak exertion level of the program. The **factory
setting is level seven**.

**The later XE ellipticals describe Cardio differently** - a quick progression to near maximum
resistance with fluctuations that let your heart rate elevate and recover repeatedly before a quick
cool down. That is `spirit-ce-programs-cardio-profile`.

The Sole CC81 2026 owner's manual prints this same "exercise for your heart and lungs" wording for
its own Cardio program. That is a Sole machine and is not covered by this card.
