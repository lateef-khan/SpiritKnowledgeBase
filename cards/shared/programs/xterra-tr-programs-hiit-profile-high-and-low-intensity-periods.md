**HIIT** "takes you through **high levels of intensity followed by periods of low
intensity**. This program increases your endurance by depleting your oxygen level followed by
periods of recovery to replenish oxygen. Your cardiovascular system gets programmed to use
oxygen more efficiently this way. This program also forces your body to become more efficient
due to spikes in heart rate, between recovery periods. This aids in heart rate recovery from
intense activities."

**No interval length, sprint time or interval count is printed** for HIIT; unlike the other
four profiles it has no separate incline paragraph.

The book pictures the program as a **Speed** and an **Incline** dot-matrix profile with **no
figures**; the words below are the whole of what it prints. The maximum speed and incline
the program reaches are shown in the Message Center when it is chosen and can be lowered
during setup.
