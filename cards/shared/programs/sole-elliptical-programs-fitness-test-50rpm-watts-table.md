The test scores your fitness (not a VO2max number — except the 2023 touchscreen YMCA test, which does show VO2max) as a monthly reference over 6-15 minutes. Programming: Fit-test, Enter; Age (LEVEL/RAMP Up/Down); Weight; Start (or Enter to modify). Before: be healthy (physician check over 35 or with conditions), warmed up, no caffeine, loose grip. During: keep a steady 50 RPM — below 48 or above 52 beeps until corrected — with a steady heart rate signal (grips or strap); the window always shows pedal speed at right; data readings are work in KGM (kg-m/min), Watts (1 W = 6.11829727787 kg-m/min), HR vs TGT, and Time. It ends at 85% of max (220 − age) or HR over 110 bpm for 2 consecutive stages. After: cool down 1-3 minutes and note the score before start-up returns. Workload per stage (render-verified; OCR '7W' is 87W):

| | HR<80 | HR 80-90 | HR 90-100 | HR>100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st stage | Level 1 (9W), 54 kgm/min ||||
| 2nd | L12 (74W) 444 | L11 (64W) 384 | L7 (42W) 252 | L3 (28W) 168 |
| 3rd | L14 (104W) 624 | L13 (87W) 522 | L11 (64W) 384 | L7 (42W) 252 |
| 4th | L15 (122W) 732 | L14 (104W) 624 | L12 (74W) 444 | L11 (64W) 384 |

(kgm/min.) The 2019 console shows TARGET TIME HAS BEEN COMPLETED, then YOUR SCORE (e.g. 76.3) with 'Press Stop to back to home'; the 2011/2013 books only say to take note of the score.
