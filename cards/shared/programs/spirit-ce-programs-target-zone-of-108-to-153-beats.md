To find the benefit range you want to train in, first work out your **Maximum Heart Rate**:
**220 minus your age**. Your heart rate training zone is **50% to 90%** of that maximum.

**These manuals print 60% and 85%.** 60% of your MHR is recommended for burning fat, 85% for
strengthening the cardiovascular system, and 60% to 85% is the zone to stay in for maximum benefit.

Worked example for someone **40 years old**:

- 220 - 40 = **180** (maximum heart rate)
- 180 x .6 = **108** beats per minute (60% of maximum)
- 180 x .85 = **153** beats per minute (85% of maximum)
- So for a 40 year old the training zone would be **108 to 153** beats per minute.

If you enter your age during programming the console performs this calculation automatically.

**This contradicts the same manuals' own Heart Rate programs**, which are named **65% and 80%** on
the program list. Nothing in any of the four manuals reconciles the two pairs of figures.

**Four different figure pairs appear across the ten Spirit commercial elliptical owner's manuals.**
The CE800 2013, CE850 2016 and CES880 2025 print 60%/80% and a 108-to-144 zone; the CE800ENT prints
65%/85% and 117 to 153; the CE900 2025 prints 65%/80% with a printed answer of 117 to **225**. The
CE900 2021 owner's manual has no training-zone page at all.

The closing sentence differs between these four. The CE800 2021 and CE800 2023 say the heart rate
monitor feature may be used during manual mode or during any of the nine different programs, and
that the Heart Rate program automatically controls resistance at the pedals. The CE850 2020 and
CE850 2022 instead say that when the heart rate monitor feature is used with the Heart Rate
programs, the machine will automatically adjust **speed or incline** to maintain the desired heart
rate - **wording carried over from a treadmill manual; this machine has neither**.
