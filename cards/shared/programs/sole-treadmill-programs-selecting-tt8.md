1. **Press the desired program from the 10 available program keys**, then press **Enter** to customise with your data, or just press **Start** to begin with the default settings.
2. The Message window shows your **Age**. Entering it correctly affects the heart rate Bar Graph Display and the Heart Rate programs, which work off a percentage of maximum heart rate. Adjust with the Up/Down keys, then press Enter.
3. The Message window shows your **body weight**; entering it correctly affects the calorie count. Adjust and press Enter. (No machine gives an exact calorie count; use it to compare workouts.)
4. The Time window shows the default of **20 minutes**. Adjust, then press Enter.
5. The Message window blinks the **preset top speed** of the program. Adjust and press Enter — this caps the highest speed the program will reach.
6. **Press Start to begin, or Enter to modify your settings.**

Unlike the menu consoles, these books print **no 3-minute warm-up and no warm-up bypass**. Age here also feeds the heart-rate bar graph, which the menu-console setup does not mention.
