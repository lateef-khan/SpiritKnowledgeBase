**These consoles have no workout programs.** There is no program list, no profile, no preset, no
program key, no manual program, no heart rate control program, no fitness test and no custom
program.

The word "program" appears in all three manuals only in safety boilerplate - "consult a medical
doctor or qualified fitness instructor to determine an exercise program appropriate for your level
of fitness" and the like. A loose search for `program` returns five hits in each XIC600 manual and
one in the CIC850, and every one of them is that boilerplate.

**What the console offers instead:**

| Feature | XIC600 2018 / 2021 | CIC850 2022 |
|---|---|---|
| Quick Start | Hold any key to wake from Power Saving Mode | Press **Page** to wake from Power Saving Mode |
| Mode select | Left key cycles SPEED, DIST, TIME, CLOCK | **MODE** key cycles TIME, DIST, KCAL |
| Reset all values | Hold right key 3 s | Hold **Page** 3 s under TIME mode |
| Workout timer | Count up, or set a count-down in minutes | Count up 00:00 to 99:59, or set a count-down |
| Time-of-day clock | Yes, 12 h or 24 h | **No clock** |
| Heart rate target zone | Manual upper and lower limit | Manual upper and lower limit |
| Heart rate alarm | Yes | **No** |
| Personal data | Gender, weight unit, bodyweight | Gender, weight unit, weight |
| App pairing | **None** | Bluetooth (BLE 4.0), including Zwift |

**The CIC850 2022 service manual's console chapter says the same.** Its four Product Operation
pages show the display windows (Watts, RPM, Speed, Heart Rate BPM, Time, Distance, Kcal), Quick
Start from Power Saving Mode with the Page key, the MODE key cycling TIME, DIST and KCAL, and the
readouts; a loose search of the whole book for `program` returns nothing, and the heart rate
heading again reads "apply to HR featured models only". It repeats the KCAL sentence about age too.

**Resistance is set by hand at the knob on the machine.** Nothing on the console changes it for you,
so a heart-rate-controlled or profile-driven workout is not possible on these bikes, and there are no
numbered resistance levels to name.

**The heart rate target zone is not a program.** You type in a maximum and a minimum limit in beats
per minute and the console watches them; it never asks your age, prints no "220 minus your age"
formula and offers no percentage-of-maximum choice. **Do not quote a percentage pair onto these
machines** - the 60%/80%, 60%/85%, 65%/85% and 65%/80% figures belong to Spirit upright, recumbent
and air bikes whose consoles do the arithmetic. For the key sequences see
`spirit-bike-console-xic600-heart-rate-target-zone` and the CIC850's own console card.

**The pulse comes from a chest belt, not the handlebars.** Both manuals describe heart rate as "the
approximation of heart rate detected from the **chest belt**". The XIC600 safety page says a chest
strap transmitter is **sold separately**; the CIC850 heading reads "HEART RATE (apply to **HR
featured models only**)", so not every CIC850 reads a pulse at all.

**One CIC850 sentence claims an age the console never asks for.** Its KCAL description says calories
"are calculated by measuring the rider's instantaneous heart rate, **age**, and weight" - but there is
no age field anywhere in that book. A loose search for `age` in the CIC850 manual returns only the
"children under the age of 13" safety line and that one sentence. Treat it as carried-over wording.
The XIC600 manuals make no such claim: they say only that the calories "are an estimate only".

**Do not answer an indoor cycle question from an upright or recumbent bike card.** Hill, Fat Burn,
Cardio, Strength, Interval, Constant Watt, Custom, HR1/HR2 and the YMCA fitness test belong to the
CR800, CU800, CR900 and CU900 consoles and exist on none of these.

**The JB950 Johnny G Spirit Bike is the Spirit indoor cycle that does have programs** - Warm Up,
Manual/HR Zone, Power Zone with an FTP test, and a Lap program. It is deliberately not listed on this
card.

**The Sole indoor cycles state the same fact on their own card**,
`sole-bike-spin-console-has-no-workout-programs`. That is a different brand and a different console;
the two are linked, not merged.
