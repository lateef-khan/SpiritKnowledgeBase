**HR1 is set to 60% and HR2 is set to 80% of your maximum heart rate.** Nine Spirit residential bike
manuals print that sentence: XBR95 2016, 2018 and 2021; XBR55 2019 and 2021; XBU55 2019 and 2021; and
the XBR55ENT and XBU55ENT touchscreens of 2021.

Both programs are otherwise the same and are programmed the same way. The percentage only sets the
**starting** target: the console works it out from the age you enter, shows it as a Heart Rate Target
you can adjust, and moving that number up or down moves the percentage with it.

**These two percentages match the training-zone page in the same manuals**, which recommends 60% for
burning fat and 80% for the cardiovascular system and gives 108 to 144 beats for a forty year old -
see `cu800-2012-programs-target-zone-of-108-to-144-beats`. That agreement is worth noting because it
does **not** hold on the commercial CR800 and CU800 2021, whose keys read 65% and 80% on a page that
recommends 60% and 85%.

**On the two touchscreens you pick the percentage on the Heart Rate screen**, which shows MAX HR
beside a TARGET HR (% OF MAX) control offering 60% and 80%, then press Next.

**The same family answers three different ways.** The XBR55, XBU55 and XBR95 **2023** books replaced
these two keys with four, at 55%, 65%, 75% and 85%
(`spirit-bike-programs-four-heart-rate-keys-at-fifty-five-sixty-five-seventy-five-and-eighty-five`);
the XBR25 2019 has a single key defaulting to 60%
(`spirit-bike-programs-a-single-heart-rate-key-defaulting-to-sixty-percent`); and the XBR25 2023 has
no heart rate program at all.
