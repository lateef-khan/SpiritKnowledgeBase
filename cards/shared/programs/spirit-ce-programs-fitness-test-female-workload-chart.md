**This is only the "Workload Chart For Female Or De-Conditioned Male".** The male / very fit female
chart is still unrecovered - see
`spirit-ce-programs-fitness-test-workload-charts-are-pictures`.

The first stage is fixed. From the second stage on, the console picks a column from your heart rate
at the end of the previous stage. Four heart-rate columns, four values in every row:

| Stage | HR < 80 | HR 80-90 | HR 90-100 | HR > 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st stage | 150 kgm/min for everyone | | | |
| 2nd stage | 750 kgm/min | 600 kgm/min | 450 kgm/min | 300 kgm/min |
| 3rd stage | 900 kgm/min | 750 kgm/min | 600 kgm/min | 450 kgm/min |
| 4th stage | 1050 kgm/min | 900 kgm/min | 700 kgm/min | 600 kgm/min |

**Two manuals print these same kgm/min figures**, so every value above is corroborated: the CES880
2025 and the CE800ENT. Both pages are printed as pictures and were recovered by OCR; the CE800ENT
scan read the 3rd-stage first cell as "990", which the CES880 scan reads as 900 - **900 is the
value**, and it is also what the 4th stage's second column repeats.

**The CES880 also prints a resistance Level and a watt figure beside each kgm/min value.** The
CE800ENT does not, so these are uncorroborated:

- 1st stage - Level 5 (25W)
- 2nd stage - Level 26 (125W), Level 23 (100W), Level 18 (75W), Level 12 (50W)
- 3rd stage - Level 29 (150W), Level 26 (125W), Level 23 (100W), Level 18 (75W)
- 4th stage - Level 32 (175W), Level 29 (150W), **Level 25 (117W)**, Level 23 (100W)

**Treat `Level 25 (117W)` with care.** Every other watt figure in the chart is a round multiple of
25 W, and 700 kgm/min divided by the manual's own conversion (1 watt = 6.11829727787 kgm/min) is
114 W, not 117. Nothing in any other CE manual corroborates that cell.

The CE800 2013 owner's manual, an image-only scan, carries the same kgm/min ladder - 150; 750, 600,
450, 300; 900, 750, 600, 450; 1050, 900, 700, 600 - but its OCR attached it under the *male* heading
with a heart-rate header reading `HR <120 / HR 120-135 / HR>135`, so its column mapping is not
recoverable and it is not claimed here.
