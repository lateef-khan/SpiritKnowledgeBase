**These are the 65% and 85% figures.** The 2022 touchscreen states the same pair as **65% and 80%**,
and the 2024 button consoles state **65% and 80%** too. The three must not be swapped.

> The default value is **65% or 85% of your projected rate maximum**. You have the option of
> changing your target heart rate. The machine will attempt to keep you **within five beats** of
> your target heart rate.

The program tile is captioned **"Follow incline"** on the screen: the treadmill steers your heart
rate with elevation, not speed. The setup screen offers **Target Heart Rate** and **Time**.

**This console has one Heart Rate program, not two.** The 2022 touchscreen and the button consoles
both split it into HR-1 (weight control) and HR-2 (cardiovascular); see
`ct800ent-2024-programs-all-programs-menu-of-eleven-entries`.

**HR has no warm-up.**
