1. **Press the desired PROGRAM key**, then press Enter; the display prompts through programming, or press **Start** to begin with default values.
2. The Time window blinks the default of **20 minutes**. Adjust with the up/down keys, then press Enter. (You may press Start at any time to start.)
3. The Calorie window blinks your **body weight**; entering it correctly affects the calorie count. Adjust and press Enter. (No machine gives an exact calorie count; use it to compare workouts.)
4. The Heart Rate window blinks your **Age**. Entering it correctly affects the heart rate Bar Graph Display (F83/F85/S73/S77 models) and the Heart Rate Control programs, which work off a percentage of maximum heart rate. Adjust and press Enter.
5. The Speed window blinks the **preset top speed** of the program. Adjust and press Enter — this caps the highest speed the program can reach.

These books print **no warm-up phase**. The H-1/H-2 setup continues on `sole-treadmill-programs-hrc-70-90-autopilot`.
