Manual, five presets (Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, plus Interval on the 2015 book and HIIT on the 2019 book), one CUSTOM slot, FIT-TEST, and a 2HR key pair driving on-screen HR1/HR2 programs. Setup is menu-driven throughout, with up to 6 user profiles, Sole App Bluetooth sync, and per-workout SUMMARY averages.
