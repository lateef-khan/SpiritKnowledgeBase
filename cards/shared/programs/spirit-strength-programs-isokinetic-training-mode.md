Isokinetic training is *all about speed of movement*: the resistance is fixed and you
train by increasing how fast you move. The manual says it is traditionally used for
rehabilitation and is ideal for training a joint through a full range of movement.

**Five Movement Speed levels, named on the buttons:**

| Level | Name |
|---|---|
| 1 | Low |
| 2 | Moderate |
| 3 | Normal |
| 4 | Fast |
| 5 | Max |

**A first-time user is told to start between level 1 and level 3** to get comfortable
with the machine and the technique.

Set the speed on the Setup Screen, press the blue **BEGIN** button, and the Progress
Screen collects elapsed time, total lb, KCAL and repetitions. The speed can be changed
mid-set by pressing another Movement Speed button.

**Its chart is different from Pure and Eccentric.** Pressing **See Chart** here gives a
**Line Graph** comparing the pounds of force and the time of the current repetition
against the previous one - force vertical, time horizontal - not the concentric/eccentric
bar graph the other two modes draw. Press **End Set** to finish.

**This is not the Sole SRVO's Isokinetic mode**, which offers only slow (0) and fast (1);
see `srvo-mode-3-isokinetic`.
