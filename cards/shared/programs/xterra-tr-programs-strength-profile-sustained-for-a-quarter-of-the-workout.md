**Strength** "has a gradual progression of resistance up to **100% of maximum effort** that is
**sustained for 25% of workout duration**. This will help build strength and muscular
endurance in the lower body and glutes. A brief cool down follows."

**Incline:** "There is a quick climb to a moderate, sustained elevation that lasts the majority
of the workout length."

The book pictures the program as a **Speed** and an **Incline** dot-matrix profile with **no
figures**; the words below are the whole of what it prints. The maximum speed and incline
the program reaches are shown in the Message Center when it is chosen and can be lowered
during setup.
