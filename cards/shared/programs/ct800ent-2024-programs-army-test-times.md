**These are the touchscreen figures. The 2024 CT800 and CT850 button consoles print a completely
different Army table** whose first bracket is 17-21 at 16:36 - see `ct900-army-pft-fitness-test`.
The two must not be swapped.

A timed **2 mile / 3.2 km run**. You control the speed manually. Maximum time allowed to pass:

| Age | Male | Female |
|---|---|---|
| <=21 | 20:12 | 23:48 |
| 22-26 | 21:54 | 25:30 |
| 27-31 | 22:30 | 26:30 |
| 32-36 | 24:12 | 26:30 |
| 37+ | 25:12 | 26:30 |

For more detailed information, visit: https://bit.ly/SF-Army

**The table is a flat image**; these values were read from a 300 dpi render. See
`ct800ent-2024-programs-editorial-note-printed-in-the-manual` - the page carries an unfinished
editorial comment beside these tables saying they do not match the current document.
