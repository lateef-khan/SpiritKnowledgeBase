The Gerkin protocol, also known as the fireman's protocol, is a **sub-max VO2 test**. Speed and
elevation increase alternately **until you reach 85% of your Max heart rate**; the time taken to
reach 85% is the score (VO2max). The manual adds: **use a heart rate strap for best results.**

**This chart's stage 2.1 begins at 1:00.** The 2024 CT800 and CT850 button consoles print the other
Spirit chart, whose stage 2.1 begins at **1:15** and whose speeds sit a quarter-stage later - see
`spirit-2026t-programs-gerkin-chart`. Within one 2024 range the two consoles print two different
charts, so read the chart for the console in front of you.

The page prints the chart **twice side by side**, once in KPH and once in MPH. The stage, time,
grade and VO2 Max columns are identical in both; only the speed unit differs.
