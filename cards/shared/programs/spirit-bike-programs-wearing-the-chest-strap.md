**The chest strap is not in the box.** These five manuals say so twice: once in the page heading and
again in the note at the foot of the page, "The chest strap is sold separately."

**The two heading wordings are the same fact.** The CR800 2021 and CR800 2023 head the page
"(NOT INCLUDED)"; the CU800 2021, CU800ENT 2022 and CR800ENT 2023 head it "(SOLD SEPARATELY)". All
five print the identical body sentence underneath, so the difference is a heading style, not a
difference in what you get.

1. Attach the transmitter to the elastic strap using the **interlocking button**.
2. Adjust the strap as tightly as possible while it is still comfortable.
3. Position the transmitter with the logo centred in the middle of your torso, facing away from your
   chest. Some people must position it slightly left of centre. Insert the round end of the elastic
   strap and secure it around your chest with the locking parts.
4. Position the transmitter **directly below the pectoral muscles**.
5. **Sweat is the best conductor**; plain water may also be used to pre-wet the electrodes - the two
   ribbed oval areas on the reverse of the belt and both sides of the transmitter. Wear the strap a
   few minutes before your workout: some users take longer to give a strong steady signal at first,
   and this lessens after "warming up".
6. Stay within range of the console. Wearing the transmitter on bare skin assures proper operation;
   over a shirt, wet the areas of the shirt the electrodes rest on.

**The transmitter switches itself on** when it detects activity from the user's heart and off when
it does not. It is water resistant, but moisture can create false signals, so dry it after use to
prolong battery life. **Estimated transmitter battery life is 2500 hours; the replacement battery is
CR2032** - these five do not name a make.

**Third party straps, and the one difference between the five.** The CR800 2021, CR800 2023 and
CU800 2021 say straps that operate using **Bluetooth or ANT+** should also work with this machine.
The CU800ENT 2022 and CR800ENT 2023 say **Bluetooth** only and do not mention ANT+.

**The CU800ENT 2022 and CR800ENT 2023 also print the supply statement a third time**, on the safety
page: "Use of a chest transmitter strap (sold separately) is a much more accurate method of heart
rate analysis."

**A different page covers four other bikes.** The CR900 2025, CU900 2025 and CU800ENT 2024 print the
Spirit treadmill version - interlocking **key**, **Panasonic** CR2032, no third-party sentence - and
the CU800 2012 prints an older version with no supply statement at all. See the two cards linked
above.

**The CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900ENT and CU900ENT have no chest strap chapter**, and no supply
statement anywhere in the book.
