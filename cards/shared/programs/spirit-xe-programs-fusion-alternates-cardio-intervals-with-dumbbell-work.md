In Fusion you alternate between **mid-high intensity cardiovascular intervals on the elliptical**,
**strength exercises off the elliptical**, and **low intensity recovery intervals on the elliptical**.
You need a pair of objects that can be comfortably held in your hands - dumbbells, water jugs, soup
cans - for **4 of the 5** strength exercises the program uses.

1. Select **Fusion**, then press **Enter**.
2. The Message Center blinks your **Age (default is 35)**. Adjust with Level Up/Down, then Enter.
   Your age sets your recommended maximum heart rate, which the bar graph and the heart rate programs
   are percentages of, so it must be right.
3. It blinks your **Body Weight**. Adjust, then Enter. **The calorie count in this program will not be
   accurate, because the machine cannot calculate calories expended while strength training.**
4. It blinks the preset **top Resistance level - default and minimum is Level 5**. Adjust, then Enter.
   **This value is the Level for all cardio intervals**, and the resistance can be changed at any time
   during the program.
5. It blinks the **number of intervals - default is 10**. You may select **10** (5 cardio and 5
   strength), **20** (10 cardio and 10 strength) or **30** (15 cardio and 15 strength). Adjust, then
   Enter.
6. It blinks the **Interval time - default is 2:00**. The time you select is the duration of **each
   cardio interval**. *As a general rule, the longer the interval the less resistance is required,
   and the more repetitions you perform the less weight you need.*
7. It blinks the **recovery time - default is 0:30** - taken after completing both the cardio and
   strength intervals. Adjust, then Enter.

**Caution.** The shorter the recovery time, the longer your heart rate stays elevated. If you are new
to exercise, or have resumed after a long break, it is recommended that **your recovery interval
length matches or exceeds the length of the cardio interval**.

**The XE795 2021 manual gives 150 lbs as the body weight default at step 3; the two 2023 manuals
print no default for that step.** Every other figure above is identical in all three.

**These are not the treadmill Fusion figures.** The Spirit XT485 and XT685 version sets a top speed
and a top incline instead of a resistance level and defaults the interval time to **1:00**, not 2:00 -
see `xt-2015-programs-fusion-program-xt485-xt685`. The five dumbbell exercises and the worked
warm-up-to-cool-down example are printed only in the treadmill manuals; **no XE manual lists which
five exercises they are**, so this KB holds none for the ellipticals.

**The XE795 2018 printing (printed pages 24-25) prints all of the above, figure for figure**,
including the **150 lbs** body weight default at step 3 that the 2021 printing gives and the 2023
manuals omit. Fusion is one of the two programs that separates the XE795 from every other XE
elliptical of its generation; no XE395, XE895, XE295, XE195 or XG400 manual has it.
