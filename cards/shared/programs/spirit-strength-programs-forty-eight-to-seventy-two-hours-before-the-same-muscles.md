**A minimum of 48 to 72 hours before training the same muscles.**

The manual's wording: *Keep in mind how important the recovery phase is in achieving
your goals. The general rule is to allow a minimum of 48-72 hours before training the
same muscles. If you still experience soreness after this period of time has elapsed,
take a few additional days until the soreness has subsided.*

So the figure is a floor with an override: **if it still hurts after 72 hours, wait
longer.** The manual gives no upper limit and no weekly session count.

**Only the ST800 bench, dumbbell rack and functional trainer books print this.** No CSS,
CSD, CSF or CSI manual carries any recovery figure at all; those books print no training
advice whatsoever. Do not quote 48-72 hours as a Spirit figure for a selectorized
machine - it is stated for these three products only.
