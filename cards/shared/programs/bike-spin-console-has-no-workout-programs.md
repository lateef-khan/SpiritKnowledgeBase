**These consoles have no workout programs.** None of the indoor cycle owner's manuals lists a program,
a profile, a preset or a program key. The word "program" appears in them only in the safety warnings
about consulting a physician before starting an exercise program.

What the console does offer instead:

- **Quick Start** — hold any key to wake the console from power saving mode and start.
- **Mode / Page select** — cycle the bottom readout between SPEED, DIST, TIME, KCAL and CLOCK.
- **A count-down timer** — set a COUNT DOWN TIME and the timer counts down to zero, then counts up.
- **Heart rate target zones** — an upper and a lower heart rate limit with an alarm, not a program that
  controls resistance.
- **Personal data** — gender, weight unit and bodyweight, which only make the calorie estimate better.
- On the 2020, 2021 and 2023 machines, pairing with third-party apps such as Kinomap and Zwift.

**Resistance is set by hand, at the tension or brake knob on the machine.** Nothing on the console
changes it for you, so there is no heart rate control program, no fitness test and no custom program.

**Do not answer an indoor cycle question from an upright or recumbent bike card.** Hill, Fat Burn,
Cardio, Strength, Interval/HIIT, User 1, User 2, HR1, HR2, Custom and Fit-Test belong to the B94, R92,
B74, R72, LCB and LCR consoles and exist on none of these.

The **SB1200-2023** is the exception among Sole indoor cycles and is deliberately not listed on this
card: it has a touchscreen with Timer, Distance, Calories and Heart Rate modes.
