The Fat Burn program is designed to maximize the burning of fat: most experts agree a lower exertion level that stays at a steady workload is best, and the absolute best way to burn fat is to keep your heart rate at around 60% to 70% of its maximum potential. This program does not use heart rate but simulates a lower, steady exertion workout.
