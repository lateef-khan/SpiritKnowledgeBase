**On these 2015 machines Fusion has its own dedicated Fusion key. On the 2023 XT385, XT485 and XT685 you reach it by stepping the single PROGRAM key to FUSION instead — see the linked card.**

**Resistance:** This program takes you through high levels of cardio and strength intensity followed by recovery periods of low intensity. It uses and develops your "Fast Twitch" muscle fibres, which are used when performing tasks that are intense and short in duration. These deplete your oxygen level and spike your heart rate, followed by periods of recovery and heart rate drop to replenish oxygen, so your cardiovascular system is programmed to use oxygen more efficiently.

**Incline:** This program spikes similar to the speed profile, but in different segments (columns), so all of your lower extremity muscles are equally challenged. **The incline alternates between 25 & 65 % of maximum elevation.** The manual notes the speed and incline profile graphs are similar to those of the Interval program.

Setting it up:

1. Press the **Fusion** key. The display prompts you through the programming.
2. The Message Center blinks your **Age (default is 35)**. Use the speed + or - keys to adjust, then press Enter.
3. The Message Center blinks your **Body Weight (default is 150)**. Use the + or - keys to adjust, then press Enter. **The calorie count displayed in this program will not be accurate, because the machine cannot calculate the calories expended while strength training.**
4. The Message Center blinks the **preset top speed** of the program (**3 mph or 5 kmph**). Use the speed + or - keys to adjust, then press Enter.
5. The Message Center blinks the **preset top incline** of the program (**1.0%**). Use the speed + or - keys to adjust, then press Enter.
6. The Message Center blinks the **number of intervals** desired (**default is 10; you may select 10, 20 or 30**). Use the speed + or - keys to adjust, then press Enter.
7. The Message Center blinks the desired **Interval time (default is 1:00)**. The time you select is the duration of **both** the cardio and strength intervals. *On average you will complete 15-20 repetitions of the strength exercise in a 0:30 interval; as a general rule, the longer the interval, the less weight (dumbbells) and speed (treadmill) required.* Use the + or - keys to adjust, then press Enter.
8. The Message Center blinks the desired **recovery time (default setting is 0:30)** that you want after completing both the cardio and strength intervals. Use the + or - keys to adjust, then press Enter.

The manual stops at step 8 and prints no closing "press Start" step here.

For how a Fusion session runs from warm-up to cool-down, and for the five dumbbell exercises it calls out, see the linked cards — the 2015 manuals print both of those word for word as the 2023 manuals do.
