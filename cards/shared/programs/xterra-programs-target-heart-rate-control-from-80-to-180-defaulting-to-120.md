Program 22 is the **Target H.R.C.** program - "workout by your target heart rate value"
instead of a percentage of 220 minus age.

1. Select P22 with UP/DOWN; press ENTER.
2. **TARGET PULSE** flashes - set it between **80 and 180** with UP/DOWN (default **120**),
   press ENTER.
3. TIME, DISTANCE and CALORIES flash in turn - set each, press ENTER.
4. Press START/STOP.

As in programs 18 to 21, the computer raises the resistance level (about every 20 seconds)
while your detected heart rate is below the target and lowers it while it is above. Hold both
pulse grips or wear a chest strap.
