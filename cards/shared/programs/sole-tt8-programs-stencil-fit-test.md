This differs from the A1 Gerkin card (`sole-tt8-programs-gerkin-fit-test`) at the end of the test: there is **no TEST OVER message and no 20-second score recall** — after the test, "cool down for about one to three minutes" and "take note of your score because the console will automatically return to the start-up mode after a few minutes." Protocol, stages and cool-down timing otherwise match.

The fit test estimates **VO2 max** (how well the body uses oxygen) over **at most 11 minutes**, progressing in speed and elevation. **If actual heart rate reaches 85% of projected maximum before the end, the program ends** and the score comes from the stage reached. Higher estimated VO2 means fitter.

1. Press the **Fit-test button** and press Enter.
2. Enter **Age** (Grade keys), then Enter.
3. Enter **body weight**, then Enter.
4. The window shows **PRESS START TO BEGIN OR ENTER TO MODIFY**.
5. **3-minute warm-up** at **3 mph, 0% grade**; the window alternates 3 MINUTE WARM-UP with actual and target HR every 5 seconds while time counts down. Other windows accrue.
6. Speed and grade follow the stage table below.
7. **Reaching target continues the test 15 more seconds**, then a **cool-down**: grade 0%, speed 3.0 mph. Cool-down is **1 minute if the test took 5 minutes or less, 3 minutes if it took 5-12 minutes**; the window alternates 1 (or 3) MIN COOL DOWN with YOUR SCORE every 5 seconds while time counts down.
8. The score is the **final completed stage's VO2 Max**.

Before the test: make sure you are in good health (check with a physician if over 35 or with pre-existing conditions); warm up and stretch; no caffeine; hold the hand grips gently.
