This is the F80 variant: the setup steps match the menu-console `sole-treadmill-programs-hr-60-80-menu` card, but the program **drives the treadmill Incline** to reach the target and speed stays manual throughout.

**CAUTION: heart rate programs are intended for the wireless transmitter chest strap. Do not use the Pulse Grip bar for Heart Rate.** You must receive a strong, steady value in the heart rate window.

- **HR1** defaults to **60%** of projected maximum; geared for **fat loss**.
- **HR2** defaults to **80%** of projected maximum; geared for improving **cardiovascular and lung function**.

1. Select **HR1 or HR2** via the Program keys, then press Enter.
2. The Message window blinks your **age**. Adjust or accept; **adjusting age changes the Target HR value**.
3. The Message window blinks the default **body weight**. Adjust or accept.
4. Adjust the **HR1/HR2 target**: 60% (HR1, 220 - age x .60) or 80% (HR2, 220 - age x .80); higher or lower allowed. Adjust with the Speed keys (console or handrails) and press Enter.
5. The Time window blinks. Adjust the time and press Enter.
6. **Press Start to begin.** Press **Stop to go back one level or screen** and re-edit.
7. **Speed may be changed at any time during the program** with the Speed keys.
8. **Display scrolls** the message data; at the end a **summary** shows briefly, then the console returns to start-up.
9. Press Start to begin the program.

**The program controls the treadmill Incline** to raise heart rate to target automatically (upwards of five minutes; fitter takes longer). **You can make manual adjustments to the speed throughout the program.**
