**Three presets, and they are Hill, Plateau and Interval.** There is no Fat Burn, Cardio or Strength
profile on these consoles, unlike the commercial CR/CU bikes.

*"The bike has three different programs that have been designed for a variety of workout goals.
These programs factory preset work level profiles for achieving different goals. The initial
built-in level of difficulty for each program is set to a relatively easy level. You may adjust the
level of difficulty (max level) for each program before beginning."* (4.0R and 4.0U 2025, and the
Dyaco PT 4.0 R and 4.0 U editions word for word)

The 7.0R and 7.0U 2025 books and the Dyaco MED 7.0R 2021 book say the same thing with *"three
preset exercise programs"* and add a paragraph on why the dot-matrix picture never changes when you
press the work keys - see `spirit-med-bike-programs-preset-setup-asks-only-for-time-and-the-auto-brake`.

**The three profiles are described one card each**, and each description is printed identically in
all seven books except the Hill sentence, which the two Dyaco 4.0 PT editions and the 2021 MED
edition shorten. Setting a preset up differs between the two console families: the 4.0 bikes ask
for age, weight, time and a max level (`spirit-bike-programs-preset-setup-steps`); the 7.0 bikes ask
only for time and whether the auto-brake is on.

**Beside the three presets** every one of these bikes also has Manual, a Custom/Facility program,
the VO2 test and a heart rate program; the 4.0 family adds METs and HR auto pilot, and the 7.0
family adds Constant Power, Isokinetic and Symmetry.
