1. **Press the Program keys** to view the programs, then press **Enter** to select one. Or just press **Start** to begin with the preset values for age, weight and other program data.
2. The window shows **Age**. Adjust and press Enter. **You may press Start at any time during the programming** to start with what you have entered so far.
3. The window shows your **body weight**; entering it correctly affects the calorie count. Adjust and press Enter. (No machine gives an exact calorie count; use it to compare workouts.)
4. The Time window shows the **total workout time desired**. Adjust and press Enter.
5. The window shows the **preset max speed** of the program. Adjust and press Enter — each program varies speed throughout, and this caps the highest speed it will reach at its peak.
6. **Press Start.** The program begins with a **3 minute warm-up**; press Start again to bypass it and go straight to the program.

Key and window names vary by book without changing the steps: the F60-2012 book says **Speed 5/6 keys**, the AF63/AF65 book says the **Calorie window (F63) or Message window (F65)**, and the AF80/AF83/AF85 book says the **Up/Down (Up5/6Down) keys** and the **Speed window** for the max-speed step. The S77-2013 book also says Program keys with a Speed window for step 5.

This is the menu-console setup; the 2026 console numbers its presets P2-P6 with Display/Enter keys instead: see `f63-2026-selecting-a-preset-program`. The max-speed cap and the 3-minute warm-up work the same way.
