**CAUTION: heart rate programs are intended for the wireless transmitter chest strap only. Do not use the Pulse Grip bar for Heart Control.** You must receive a strong, steady value in the heart rate window.

- **HR1** defaults to **60%** of projected maximum; geared for **fat loss**.
- **HR2** defaults to **80%** of projected maximum; geared for improving **cardiovascular and lung function**.

1. Select **HR1 or HR2** via the Program (Speed on the F60) 5/6 keys, then press Enter.
2. The Message window blinks your **age**. Adjust and press Enter (or accept the default); **adjusting age changes the Target HR value**.
3. The Message window blinks the default **body weight**. Adjust or accept.
4. Adjust the **HR1/HR2 target**: the default is 60% (HR1, 220 - age x .60) or 80% (HR2, 220 - age x .80); a higher or lower number may be chosen. Adjust with the Speed 5/6 keys (console or handrails) and press Enter.
5. The Time window blinks. Adjust the time and press Enter.
6. **Press Start to begin.** Press **Stop to go back one level or screen** and re-edit.
7. **Speed may be changed at any time during the program** with the Speed 5/6 keys.
8. **Display scrolls** the Message-window data; at the end a **summary** shows briefly, then the console returns to start-up.
9. Press Start to begin the program.

The program then **steadily raises heart rate to the target automatically, which can take upwards of five minutes**; fitter people take longer. Unlike the TT8, this chapter never says the program drives the incline: see `sole-treadmill-programs-hr-60-80-tt8` for the incline-driving twin, and `f63-2026-heart-rate-programs` for the 2026 Bluetooth H1/H2.
