Manual means you control the workload, not the computer. 1. Press Manual, then Enter. 2-4. Enter Age, Weight, Time with Level UP/DOWN, Enter each time. 5. Start begins; Stop steps back. 6. Level-one warm-up; Level UP raises, Level DOWN lowers. 7. E20-2016 only: Enter scrolls the Message Window. 8. A summary ends the session, then start-up returns.
