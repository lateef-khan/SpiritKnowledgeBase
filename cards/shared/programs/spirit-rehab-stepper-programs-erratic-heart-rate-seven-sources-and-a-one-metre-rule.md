> **Caution! Do not use the machine for heart rate control unless a steady, solid actual heart rate
> value is being displayed.** High, wild, random numbers being displayed indicate a problem.

Areas to look at for interference, as bullets rather than numbers:

1. Microwave ovens, TVs, small appliances, etc.
2. Fluorescent lights.
3. Some household security systems.
4. **Perimeter fence for a pet.**
5. Some people have problems with the transmitter picking up a signal from their skin. **Try wearing
   the transmitter upside down** - normally it is oriented so the logo is right side up.
6. The antenna is very sensitive. If there is an outside noise source, **turning the whole machine
   90 degrees may de-tune the interference.**
7. **If there is another person wearing a chest strap within 1 metre, it will interfere.**

If problems continue, contact your dealer.

## Two figures differ from the rest of the range, and one item is missing

| | This card (MS300, MED books) | 2025 7.0S and 7.5S | CS800 / CRS800S 2024 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Items | **7, as bullets** | 8, numbered | 8, numbered |
| Another strap | **within 1 metre** | within 3' | within 3' |
| Grounding item | **not printed** | item 1, *"Recumbent Steppers is not properly grounded"* | item 1 |
| Logo named | "the logo" | "the Spirit Fitness logo" | "the Spirit Fitness logo" |

**One metre is 3.28 feet, so the two ranges are close but not the same figure.** Quote the book in
front of you.

**Grounding is the item this list does not have.** On the 2025 books it is the first thing to
check; here it is not mentioned at all, and the machine still must be grounded -
`spirit-product-must-be-grounded`.
