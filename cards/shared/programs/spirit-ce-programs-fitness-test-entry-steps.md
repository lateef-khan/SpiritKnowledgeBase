1. Find the **Fitness Test** program and press **Enter**.
2. The display shows **Gender**. Use the Up and Down arrows to change it, then press Enter. **The
   choice of gender determines which workload chart is used for the test.**
3. Enter your **Age**, then Enter.
4. Enter your **Weight**, then Enter.
5. Press **Start** to begin the test.

**The CE800 2013 differs in two places.** It names the key **Fit-Test**; and its gender screen opens
at `Gender Male` with the word Male blinking, to be changed to Female with the Up and Down arrows.
Its step 5 also offers Enter to modify your settings, which the later manuals do not print.

**The CE800ENT prints no entry steps.** Its Fitness Test page gives the protocol and the before /
during / after guidance only; selection is by tapping the program on the touchscreen menu.
