**The increased eccentric resistance is adjustable from +10% to +50%.** The console's
setup screen offers **five** steps - **+10%, +20%, +30%, +40%, +50%** - and converts each
percentage to pounds under the button, so with a 50 lb concentric resistance the row
reads 55, 60, 65, 70 and 75 lb.

**The +30% step is easy to lose.** It is the selected button in the manual's screenshot,
which is drawn dimmer than the rest, and a tesseract pass over that page returns the row
as *+10% +20% +40% +50%*. The step exists; read the rendered page rather than the OCR.

The manual's analogy: eccentric training is when a training partner helps you lift the
weight and you lower it yourself under control. The i-Strength software lets you set the
intensity of **both** directions instead - *from entry level eccentrics to body building
hard*.

To run a set:

1. On the Setup Screen set the **Concentric Resistance** with **+**, **-** or the slide
   bar.
2. Choose the **Increased Eccentric Resistance** percentage.
3. Press the blue **BEGIN** button.
4. Mid-set only the **concentric** resistance can be changed from the Progress Screen.
5. **See Chart** gives a Bar Graph of the concentric and eccentric movements against
   milliseconds and repetitions.
6. Press **End Set** when the repetitions are done.

**AUTO ADJUST works here too**, and it moves the concentric resistance - see
`spirit-strength-programs-auto-adjust-for-drop-sets`.

**This is not the Sole SRVO's Eccentric mode.** The SRVO fixes the concentric at 70% of
the eccentric, a 10:7 ratio the user cannot change. The Spirit console instead adds a
percentage the user picks. Do not quote one figure for the other; see
`srvo-mode-2-eccentric`.
