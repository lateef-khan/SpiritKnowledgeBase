*"Each of the programs can be customized with your personal information and changed to suit your
needs. Some of the information asked for is necessary to ensure the readouts are correct."*

**You will be asked for your Age and your Weight**, and the manual gives one reason for each:

- **Age** is also necessary during the **Heart Rate control program** *"to ensure the correct
  settings are in the program for your Age. Otherwise the work settings could be too high or low for
  you."* The two heart rate keys work from a maximum heart rate derived from that age - see
  `spirit-stepper-programs-heart-rate-keys-of-sixty-five-and-eighty-percent`.
- **Weight** *"aides in calculating a more correct Calorie reading."*

**The calorie figure is an estimate by the manual's own admission:** *"Although we cannot provide
an exact calorie count we do want to be as close as possible."* Do not promise a customer an
accurate calorie number from this console.

Where the age and weight are entered - as steps 2 and 3 of every program's setup, before the time
and, on a preset, the Max Level - is on
`spirit-stepper-programs-preset-setup-asks-age-weight-time-and-a-max-level` and
`spirit-stepper-programs-manual-starts-at-level-one-and-can-be-saved-as-custom`.

**Both 2020-edition service manuals print the paragraph word for word**, under the heading
PROGRAMMABLE FEATURES, in the Product Operation chapter, and both 2021 owner's manuals print the
same paragraph under the same heading. The service manuals add nothing the owner's manuals do not
say.

The Spirit bikes carry the same Dyaco paragraph and their own card for it -
`spirit-bike-programs-age-and-weight-set-the-heart-rate-bar-and-the-calorie-count`; that card is a
different product line and is not evidence for a stepper.
