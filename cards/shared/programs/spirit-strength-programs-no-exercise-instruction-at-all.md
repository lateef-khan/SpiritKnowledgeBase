**No. These three books have no exercise page of any kind.** The half rack, the leg
press and the upright bench go straight from Safeguards to Assembly, and their
contents pages list only Product Registration, the safety chapters, Assembly,
Maintenance & Care, the Exploded View Diagram and Warranty.

That makes them the exception in the range. The other 31 Spirit strength manuals each
carry a **START WORKOUT** page holding a picture of the machine's workout placard - see
`spirit-strength-programs-exercise-instruction-is-the-machine-placard`. These three do
not, and they print no placard part number either: the word *placard* does not occur
anywhere in any of the three.

**This absence was checked on the rendered pages, not on the extraction.** Every page
of all three PDFs was rendered at 300 dpi and compared word for word against the text
layer. The only text the render adds is contents-page noise and the Proposition 65 /
serial-number product label. Nothing exercise-related is hidden as artwork.

**Do not read the CSF-AABB or CSF-BEXT placard back onto these machines.** Those two
CSF benches do have a placard page; the half rack, leg press and upright bench simply
have none.
