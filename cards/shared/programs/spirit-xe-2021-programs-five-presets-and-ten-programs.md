The lineup is **Manual**, five presets - **Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength and Interval** - **User 1
and User 2**, and **HR 1 and HR 2**. That is **ten**, which matches both the feature callout ("Ten
innovative programs offer a variety of work-outs") and the heart rate bar graph page, which speaks of
"the programming phase of any of the **10** programs".

The preset page says "the elliptical has **five** different programs that have been designed for a
variety of workouts. These five programs have factory preset work level profiles."

**The XG400 is not sold as an elliptical.** Its cover reads "XG400 E•Glide Trainer" and its preset
page opens "The **E•Glide** has five different programs". Everything above is printed identically in
both manuals.

*Both manuals still carry the boilerplate sentence about "any of the **nine** different programs" on
their heart rate page. Both machines have ten.*

**The XE195, from the same year, has only four presets and six programs**; the XE795 2021 has seven
presets and adds Calories and Fusion.

**Four earlier printings print the same lineup and the same two counts** - XG400 2019 (printed page
16 callout, page 23 presets), XE295 2019 (page 17 callout, page 24 presets), XE895 2018 (page 17
callout, page 27 presets) and XE395 2018 (page 18 callout, page 25 presets). All four print "Ten
innovative programs offer a variety of work-outs" on the machine drawing, ten program keys - Manual,
Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, Interval, User 1, User 2, HR 1, HR 2 - and a preset page opening
"has **five** different programs". All four also carry the same stale "any of the **nine** different
programs" sentence on the heart rate page.

**Two of them add a second dimension the others do not have.** The XE395 2018 raises and lowers the
pedal incline and prints an incline profile for every preset. The XE895 2018 changes stride length
instead and prints a stride profile for every preset. Neither adds a program.

*The XE395 2018 console drawing does label a **CARDIO** key. The XE395 2010 drawing left it out - see
`xe395-2010-programs-console-diagram-omits-cardio-from-the-program-buttons`, which stays a
2010-only card.*
