The lineup is **Manual**, five presets - **Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength and Interval** - **User 1
and User 2**, and **HR 1 and HR 2**. That is **ten**, which matches both the feature callout ("Ten
innovative programs offer a variety of work-outs") and the heart rate bar graph page, which speaks of
"the programming phase of any of the **10** programs".

The preset page says "the elliptical has **five** different programs that have been designed for a
variety of workouts. These five programs have factory preset work level profiles."

**The XG400 is not sold as an elliptical.** Its cover reads "XG400 E•Glide Trainer" and its preset
page opens "The **E•Glide** has five different programs". Everything above is printed identically in
both manuals.

*Both manuals still carry the boilerplate sentence about "any of the **nine** different programs" on
their heart rate page. Both machines have ten.*

**The XE195, from the same year, has only four presets and six programs**; the XE795 2021 has seven
presets and adds Calories and Fusion.
