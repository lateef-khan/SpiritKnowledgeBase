**Before the test**

- Make sure you are in good health; check with your physician before performing any exercise if you
  are over the age of **35**, or if you have pre-existing health conditions.
- Warm up and stretch first.
- **Do not take in caffeine** before the test.
- Hold the hand grips gently; do not tense up.

**During the test**

- The console must be receiving a **steady heart rate** for the test to begin. Use the hand pulse
  sensors or wear a heart rate chest strap transmitter.
- Maintain a steady **50 rpm** pedal speed. Below **48 rpm** or above **52 rpm** the console emits a
  steady beeping sound until you are back in range.
- The data window always shows your pedal speed on the right side to help you hold 50 rpm.
- The readouts are:
  1. **KGM** - short for kg-m/min., a work measurement in kilogram-force metre/minute.
  2. **Watts** - 1 watt equals **6.11829727787** kg-m/min.
  3. **HR** is your actual heart rate; **TGT** is the target heart rate that ends the test.
  4. **Time** is the total elapsed time of the test.

**After the test**

- Cool down for about **one to three minutes**.
- Note your score: the console returns to start-up mode by itself after a few minutes.

All thirteen manuals print this page. The CU800 2012 is the only one whose OCR damages the watt
conversion - it reads `6.1 1829727787` in both scans, with a space that is not in the figure - and
its scan 1 also OCRs "HR is your actual heart rate" as "**R** is your actual heart rate". Both scans
agree on every digit that is legible.

**The nine Spirit medical bike books print this page with one bullet swapped.** Where the commercial
books say "hold the hand grips gently, do not tense up", the medical books say instead: **"Adjust the
seat to the proper position so that when your leg is extended during pedaling there is a slight bend
at the knee of about 5 degrees."** The 50 rpm rule, the 48 and 52 limits, the KGM and watt
readouts, TGT and the one-to-three minute cool down are unchanged. **The 8.0U and 8.5R touchscreens
say the console "will show an alarm" rather than emit a beep when the speed leaves the range**, say
only "You may wear a heart rate sensor" for the steady heart rate the test needs, and print "Take
not of your score".
