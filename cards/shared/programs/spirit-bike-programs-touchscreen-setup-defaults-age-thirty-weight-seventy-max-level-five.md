Selecting a program on the CR900ENT or CU900ENT and pressing Enter opens the setup screens. The
manual prints a default and a range for each field:

| Field | Default | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Age | 30 years | 10 to 110 |
| Weight | 70 KG | 10 to 150 |
| Max Level | 5 | 5 to 40 |

**Time is asked first** - tap the Time window and use the key pad - and the manual gives no default
or range for it.

You may press **Start** at any point during setup to begin with only the settings changed so far.

**Entering the correct Age affects the heart rate bar graph accuracy and is needed for the HR
programs. Entering the correct bodyweight affects the Kcal readout accuracy.**

**The 800ENT bikes print different figures.** The CU800ENT 2022 and CR800ENT 2023 give the Manual
program a workout duration range of **5 to 99 minutes** and no defaults for the other fields; the
CU800ENT 2024 prints no ranges at all and describes a Program Setup page where you drag a scale, use
plus and minus, or tap the number for direct input.
