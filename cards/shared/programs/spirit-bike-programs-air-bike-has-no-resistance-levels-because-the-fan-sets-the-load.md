**There are no resistance knobs to adjust and no numbered resistance levels.** The resistance is
*isokinetic*: it comes from the movement of air against the fan-shaped flywheel, so **you control it
with your own pace**.

- **Pedal and push slower** and the flywheel gives less resistance - a cardiovascular endurance
  workout you can sustain for a long time.
- **Pedal faster and push/pull the movement arms harder** and the flywheel gives more - a muscle
  endurance or anaerobic workout.

Because of this the console cannot hold a level, cannot raise or lower one for you, and has no level
display. **Every mode on the console measures your effort; none of them sets it.** That includes
Target Heart Rate mode, which flashes an arrow telling you to pedal faster or slower rather than
changing anything itself.

**Anaerobic exercise and High Intensity Interval Training (HIIT) are much more strenuous** and both
manuals say they should only be undertaken by users who have already established a baseline fitness
level. Discuss your fitness regimen with a healthcare professional.

**The bike can also be used arms-only.** Put your feet on the front foot pegs either side of the
flywheel and work the movement arms alone. **The pedals keep rotating while you do this**, so keep
your feet on the platforms and clear of them.

**Do not read a level figure from a magnetic bike onto this machine.** The forty-level and
twenty-level cards describe magnetically braked bikes; an air bike has no equivalent number.
