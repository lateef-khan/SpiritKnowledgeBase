The fitness test is based on the **YMCA protocol** and is a **sub-maximal** test that uses
pre-determined, fixed work levels driven by your heart rate readings as the test progresses.

- It takes **between 6 and 15 minutes**, depending on your level of fitness.
- It ends when your heart rate reaches **85% of maximum** at any time during the test, **or** when
  your heart rate is **between 110 bpm and 85%** at the end of two consecutive stages.
- At the end you are given a **VO2max** score. VO2max stands for Volume of Oxygen uptake — a
  measurement of how much oxygen you need to perform a known amount of work.
- The protocol uses **two to four 3-minute stages** of continuous exercise.

**Male or Female.** You are asked to choose one at the start; the choice decides which workload chart
the test uses. The manual's two caveats: **a very de-conditioned male should choose Female**, and
**a very conditioned female should choose Male**.

**Programming the test**

1. Press the **Fit-test** button and press Enter.
2. *(2013 manuals only)* The message window displays **Gender Male** with "Male" blinking. Use the up
   and down arrows to change it to Female if you need to, then press Enter. The choice of Gender
   determines which workload chart is used for the test.
3. Enter your **Age** with the Up and Down keys, then Enter.
4. Enter your **Weight** with the Up and Down keys, then Enter.
5. Press **Start** to begin the test, or **Enter** to modify your settings.

The 2011 manuals have no Gender step in this numbered list — they go straight from Fit-test to Age —
even though the same page says you will be asked to choose Male or Female.

**Before the test**

- Make sure you are in good health. Check with your physician before performing any exercise **if you
  are over the age of 35 or have pre-existing health conditions**.
- Make sure you have warmed up and stretched.
- Do not take in caffeine before the test.
- Hold the hand grips gently; do not tense up.

**During the test**

- The console must be receiving a **steady heart rate** for the test to begin. Use the hand pulse
  sensors or wear a heart rate chest strap transmitter.
- Maintain a steady **50 RPM** pedal speed. If your pedal speed drops **below 48 RPM** or goes
  **above 52 RPM** the console beeps steadily until you are back in range.
- The message window always shows your pedal speed on the right side to help you hold 50 RPM.
- Data shown during the test: **Work in KGM** (an abbreviation of kg-m/min, kilogram-force
  meter/minute), **Work in Watts** (1 watt equals **6.11829727787** kg-m/min), **HR** (your actual heart
  rate) and **TGT** (the target heart rate that ends the test), and **Time** (total elapsed time).

**After the test**

- Cool down for about **one to three minutes**.
- Take note of your score: the console returns to the start-up mode on its own after a few minutes.

The two workload charts the Gender choice selects are printed as pictures only. Their numbers are not
in the converted text, so this card cannot give the work levels for each stage.
