1. Press the desired program button, then press Enter.
2. Enter your Age with the Level keys, then press Enter.
3. Enter your Weight with the Level keys, then press Enter.
4. Set the Time, then press Enter.
5. Adjust the Max Level, then press Enter. This is the peak exertion level of the program: the highest colored segment (box) of the program profile.
6. Press Start to begin. Press Stop to go back one screen and change a setting.
7. Press the Display key to scroll through the data in the Message Window during the program.
8. When the program ends the Message Window shows a workout summary for a short time, then the console returns to the start-up display.
