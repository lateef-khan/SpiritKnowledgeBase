"The Manual program allows you to adjust your workload throughout the workout to meet your
personal fitness needs" - you control the workload, not the computer.

1. Select **MANUAL** (SB500, FS5.8e, FS5.9e: press the Program up/down key until the message
   window shows MANUAL; SB4.5r: press the Manual program button) and press **Enter**.
2. The message window asks for your personal data - adjust each with the Level up/down keys
   and press Enter:

   | Book | Order of prompts |
   |---|---|
   | SB500 | Age, then Weight, then Time |
   | SB4.5r, FS5.8e, FS5.9e | Weight, then Age, then Time |

3. Press **Start** to begin.
4. The machine starts at **level one** - on the ellipticals "level one for both Incline and
   Resistance". The SB4.5r adds: "this is the easiest level and it is a good idea to stay at
   level one for a while to warm up." Raise or lower the workload at any time with the Level
   up/down keys (SB4.5r: on the console or the stationary hand grips); on the ellipticals
   change the incline with the Incline up/down keys.
5. Press Enter during the workout to switch the data shown in the message window.
6. When the program ends the message window shows a summary of your workout for a short
   time, then the console returns to the start-up display.
