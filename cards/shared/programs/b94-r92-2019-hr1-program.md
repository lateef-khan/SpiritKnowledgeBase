**This is HR1, set to 60% of maximum heart rate. HR2 is the same program set to 80%.**

1. Press the **HR1** key, then press **Enter**.
2. Enter your **Age** with the Level up/down keys, then Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight**, then Enter.
4. Adjust the **Time**, then Enter.
5. Adjust your **Target Heart Rate** — the level you will strive to reach and maintain. Then Enter. The heart rate that appears is based on the percentage this program sets; changing the number increases or decreases that percentage.
6. Press **Start** to begin. At any time during data editing you can press **Stop** to go back one level or screen.
7. Press **Level up/down** during the program to change your target heart rate at any time.
8. Press **Display** to scroll through the data in the Message Window.
9. When the program ends, press **Start** to run the same program again or **Stop** to exit.

The Heart Rate Control program automatically controls resistance at the pedals.

A Bluetooth chest strap connected through the Sole app does **not** work in HRC mode.

The manual prints one contradictory line in this procedure: step 6 says to go back and modify settings by pressing the **Enter** key, while the note in the same step says **Stop** goes back one screen. Every other program in the manual uses Stop to go back.
