**This is a different test from the older E98 Fit-Test: it reports a VO2max score** (volume of oxygen uptake for a known amount of work). The sub-maximal YMCA protocol uses two to four 3-minute stages at fixed work levels driven by your heart rate readings, taking 6-15 minutes. It ends when your heart rate reaches 85% of maximum at any time, or sits between 110 bpm and 85% at the end of two consecutive stages. Setup: fix your physical parameters first — they determine the result. Before: healthy (physician check over 35 or with conditions), warmed up, no caffeine.
