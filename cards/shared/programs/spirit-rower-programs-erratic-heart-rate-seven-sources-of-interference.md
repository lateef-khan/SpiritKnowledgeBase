**Caution! Do not use this machine for Heart Rate readings unless a steady, solid Actual Heart Rate
value is being displayed.** High, wild, random numbers being displayed indicate a problem.

Areas to look for interference which may cause erratic heart rate:

1. Microwave ovens, TVs, small appliances, etc.
2. Fluorescent lights.
3. Some household security systems.
4. A fence for a pet - see the wording note below.
5. Some people have problems with the transmitter picking up a signal from their skin. **Try wearing
   the transmitter upside down** - normally it is oriented so the Spirit Fitness logo is right side
   up.
6. The antenna that picks up your heart rate is very sensitive. If there is an outside noise source,
   **turning the whole machine 90 degrees may de-tune the interference.**
7. Another individual wearing a transmitter **within 3 feet** of your machine's console.

If you continue to experience problems, contact your dealer.

**This is the seven-item list. The 2024 CRW800 prints an eight-item one** that adds *"Rowers is not
properly grounded"* as item 1 and pushes everything down by one - see
`crw800-2024-programs-erratic-heart-rate-interference`. Quoting "item 4" from the wrong card names
the wrong thing.

Two wording differences across the three machines, recorded as printed:

- Item 4 is **"Electric fence for a pet"** in the 2021 CRW800 and **"Perimeter fence for a pet"** in
  both XRW600 printings.
- The caution line names the wrong machine in the 2021 CRW800: *"Do not use this **stepper** for
  Heart Rate **programs**"* - leftover text from a stepper book, on a rower that has no heart rate
  program at all. The XRW600 books read *"Do not use this **Rower** for Heart Rate **monitoring**"*,
  which is what the machine actually offers.
