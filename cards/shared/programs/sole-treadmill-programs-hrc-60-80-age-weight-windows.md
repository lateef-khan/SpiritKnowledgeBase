This stencil-console variant differs from `sole-treadmill-programs-hr-60-80-menu` twice: it asks for **time before the target** in dedicated **AGE/WEIGHT windows**, and the program **drives the Incline** while speed stays manual. Verified word-for-word in all six books (TT8 books say Grade where the others say Incline).

**CAUTION: heart rate programs are intended for the wireless transmitter chest strap. Do not use the Pulse Grip bar for Heart Rate.** You must receive a strong, steady value in the heart rate window.

- **HR1** defaults to **60%** of projected maximum; geared for **fat loss**.
- **HR2** defaults to **80%** of projected maximum; geared for improving **cardiovascular and lung function**.

1. Select **HR1 or HR2** via the Program keys.
2. The **AGE window** shows your age. Adjust and press Enter (or accept); **adjusting age changes the Target HR value**.
3. The **WEIGHT window** shows the default body weight. Adjust or accept.
4. The **Time window** shows a value. Adjust the time and press Enter.
5. Adjust the **HR1/HR2 target**: 60% (HR1, 220 - age x .60) or 80% (HR2, 220 - age x .80); higher or lower allowed. Adjust with the Speed keys (console or handrails) and press Enter.
6. Settings done: **press Start**; **Stop goes back one level or screen**.
7. **Speed may be changed anytime** with the Speed keys (console or handrails).
8. **Display shows** the data-window data; at the end a **summary** shows briefly, then the start-up display.
9. Press Start to begin the program.

**The program controls the treadmill Incline (Grade on the TT8)** to raise heart rate to target automatically (upwards of five minutes; fitter takes longer). **You can make manual adjustments to the speed throughout the program.**
