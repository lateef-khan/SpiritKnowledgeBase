**Nine programs.** Press **"All Programs"** on the Home screen to reach them. Tabs across the top of
that screen switch between **All Programs, Templates and Favorites**.

| Program | What the manual says |
|---|---|
| **Manual** (also called **Timer**) | A timed workout with the resistance level controlled manually at any time during the workout. |
| **Hill** | Follows a preset level changing pattern. |
| **Fatburn** | Follows a preset level changing pattern. |
| **Cardio** | Follows a preset level changing pattern. |
| **Strength** | Follows a preset level changing pattern. |
| **HIIT** | Follows a preset level changing pattern. |
| **Heart Rate** | Uses resistance to control your heart rate. A heart rate monitor is required. |
| **Custom** | A fully customisable intensity pattern you draw yourself. |
| **Fitness Test** | The YMCA sub-maximal test. |

Tap a program card to open its **Program Setup** page, where you can read more about the program and
set the properties of your workout, or keep browsing with the arrow buttons in the upper top right
corner. Adjust a parameter by dragging the ruler, tapping the **+** and **-** buttons, or tapping the
number to open a numpad and typing the value.

**This console is not the earlier LCB/LCR console.** There are **no HR1 and HR2 buttons** — there is
one Heart Rate program with a 60%/80% choice — and there is **no Interval** program. The five preset
patterns are Hill, Fatburn, Cardio, Strength and HIIT.

**The five preset descriptions printed in the 2011-2019 LCB and LCR manuals do not appear in this
manual at all.** Nothing here says what shape Hill follows, and the sentence about burning fat at
60% to 70% of maximum heart rate is not printed in this document. Do not carry it over.
