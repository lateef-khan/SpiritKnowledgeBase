1. Attach the transmitter to the elastic strap using the **locking parts**.
2. Adjust the strap as tightly as possible while it stays comfortable.
3. Position the transmitter with the logo centred in the middle of **your body**, facing away from
   your chest; some people must position it slightly left of centre. Attach the final end of the
   elastic strap by inserting the round end and, using the locking parts, secure the transmitter and
   strap around your chest.
4. Position the transmitter **immediately below the pectoral muscles**.
5. Sweat is the best conductor, but plain water can be used to pre-wet the electrodes - **the 2 black
   square areas on the reverse side of the belt and either side of the transmitter**. Wear the strap
   a few minutes before your workout; some users, because of body chemistry, have a harder time
   getting a strong steady signal at first, and after "warming up" the problem lessens. **Wearing
   clothing over the transmitter or strap does not affect performance.**
6. Your workout must be within range - the distance between transmitter and receiver - to get a
   strong steady signal. Stay close enough to the console. Wearing the transmitter directly against
   bare skin assures proper operation; over a shirt, moisten the areas the electrodes rest on.

**The two manuals differ on two points.**

- **Logo.** The XE100/XE200/XE300 manual says the **POLAR** logo is centred on your body. The
  XE150/XE350/XE550 manual says the **Spirit** logo.
- **Battery.** Only the XE150/XE350/XE550 manual prints the note: the transmitter activates
  automatically when it detects heart activity and deactivates when it does not; it is water
  resistant, but moisture can create false signals, so dry it completely after use to prolong battery
  life. **Estimated battery life 2500 hours; replacement battery Panasonic CR2032.** The
  XE100/XE200/XE300 manual prints no battery information at all, so this KB records none for those
  three machines.

**On the XE150 the transmitter is optional**, which its manual states beside the pulse description.
Neither manual says the strap is or is not included in the box.

**Every later XE elliptical prints a different strap page** - "2 **ribbed oval** areas" and an
"interlocking key". That is `ct900-heart-rate-chest-strap-setup`.
