Manual means you control the workload, not the computer. 1. Press Manual, then Enter. 2-4. Enter Age, Weight and Time with the Ramp or Level Up/Down keys, Enter each time. 5. Press Start; Stop steps back one level. 6. You start at level one for warm-up; Level Up raises, Level Down lowers the load. 7. Display scrolls the message window. 8. At the end a workout summary shows briefly, then start-up returns.
