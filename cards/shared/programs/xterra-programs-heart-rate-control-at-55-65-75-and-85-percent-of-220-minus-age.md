Programs 18 to 22 are the heart rate control programs; 22 is the Target program (its own
card). The four percentage programs set the target from your age:

| Program | Target heart rate |
|---|---|
| 18 | 55% Max H.R.C. = (220 - AGE) x 55% |
| 19 | 65% Max H.R.C. = (220 - AGE) x 65% |
| 20 | 75% Max H.R.C. = (220 - AGE) x 75% |
| 21 | 85% Max H.R.C. = (220 - AGE) x 85% |

"The computer will adjust the resistance level according to the heart rate detected. For
example, the resistance level may **increase every 20 seconds** while the heart rate
detected is lower than the TARGET H.R.C. Also the resistance level may **decrease every 20
seconds** while the heart rate detected is higher."

1. Select P18 to P21 with UP/DOWN; press ENTER.
2. **AGE** flashes - set it (default **35**), press ENTER.
3. TIME, DISTANCE and CALORIES flash in turn - set each, press ENTER.
4. Press START/STOP. Hold both pulse grips (or wear a chest strap) so the console has a
   heart rate to work from.
