The Watts program maintains a **constant work load**: the resistance **Level adjusts by itself when
your pedal speed changes**, so the power output stays where you set it.

1. Select the **Watt** or **Constant Power** program and press **Enter**.
2. Enter your **Age**, then Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight**, then Enter.
4. Enter the **Time**, then Enter.
5. Enter the **Target Watt Level** - the constant power you will work at - then Enter.
6. Press **Start**. Stop steps back one screen during data entry.
7. Up or Down **during** the program changes your **target Watt level**, not the resistance level
   directly.
8. The Scan button beside the data window scrolls through the readouts.
9. When the program ends, Start begins the same program again and Stop exits it.

**The CR900 2018 and CU900 2018 service manuals print the same nine steps** under the same
"Constant Watt Program" heading, with the Watt key, and their step 8 calls it "the Constant Power
program" in passing - the two names are one program. They are the only service manuals in the KB
that print it.

**The button is named differently on different machines.** The CU800 2012, CR800 2021, CR800 2023
and CU800 2021 call it **Constant Power**; the CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900 2025 and CU900 2025
call it **Watt** and head the chapter "Constant Watt Program". The steps are the same.

**The CR800 2021, CR800 2023 and CU800 2021 open with different wording** - "A Watts program is a
controllable constant power whose Level adjusts when the speed is changed" - which is the sentence
the ENT touchscreen bikes use. See
`spirit-bike-programs-constant-power-on-the-touchscreen`.
