Three program categories: **Programs** (predefined speed/incline profiles), **Fitness Tests** and **Templates** (regular programs saved with personal adjustments). Tap a category, tap a program preview for its description (scroll sideways for all), then **Choose** to select and adjust it.

- **MANUAL** requires a timeframe first (scale or arrows for target time). Speed and incline stay manual while running.
- **HILL, FATBURN, CARDIO, STRENGTH, HIIT** have preset speed/incline profiles shown on the preview. **Time and maximum speed are required**; intensity adapts to the chosen max speed. (The HIIT setup screen additionally offers interval count, Sprint/Rest times and work intensity.)
- **5K, 10K** are distance programs: set a target speed and the workout ends once the distance is reached.
- **HEART RATE** and **CUSTOM** have their own cards (see links); every program can be saved as a template and labelled FAVORITE (favorites appear on the Homescreen).

Wording notes: the TT8-2021 book says **elevation** where the others say incline ("speed and elevation profile", "Speed and Elevation are adjusted manually") and prints "programs has"; the facts match. The ST90 books add a **FREE MODE** program: see `sole-st90-programs-free-mode`.
