*"The Symmetry mode may aid in achieving a more balanced pedaling stroke for patients with lower limb
deficiencies, such as neurological patients and patients with limited lower body function."* It
measures left and right power through the cycle range and shows a graph, the watt values and a
symmetry index on screen.

**The Symmetry Index is the difference in percentage between the left and right leg power; equal legs
read 0%.** The worked example shows the **right leg producing more power than the left, 120 vs. 85
watts**, and the screen shows the higher wattage of the right leg.

**Auto brake.** You may turn it on or off during program setup. *"Since the auto brake may be
activated with severe asymmetry the auto brake default setting is off."*

**For best results** the program starts at level 1 and the resistance must be raised manually: high
enough for a meaningful measurement, low enough for full pedal revolutions, because *"very low
resistance settings result in erratic or inconsistent measurements."* The watt readings come from
*"a proprietary algorithm using a power table and velocity calculations"*, not direct force
measurement.

**Symmetry is a Features page item, not one of the fifteen programs**; the page footer reads "Click
to show symmetry page". Power Distribution, which is a program, answers a different question - where
in the revolution the power is made.

The 7.0 bikes print the LED-console version of this program with a 41 versus 34 watt example
(`spirit-med-bike-programs-symmetry-graphs-left-and-right-leg-power-with-a-41-vs-34-watt-example`);
the 8.5S stepper and 8.5UE ergometer print this same touchscreen page for legs and arms respectively.
