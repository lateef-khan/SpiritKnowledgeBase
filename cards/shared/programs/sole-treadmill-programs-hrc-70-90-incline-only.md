**This is the incline-only HRC: the program uses the treadmill's incline system alone** ("Increases and decreases in elevation affect heart rate much more efficiently than changes in speed alone. The HRC program automatically changes elevation gradually"), where the sibling consoles drive speed and elevation together.

- **Weight Control (HR-1)** at **70%** of maximum; **Cardiovascular (HR-2)** at **90%**. Maximum is 220 minus the entered age; the target is a suggestion for normal healthy individuals.
- **Auto-Pilot is present** (HR2 doubles as it); see `sole-treadmill-programs-heart-rate-autopilot`.
- **Setup** matches the siblings: chest strap only (the program will not start without a strong, steady value); **HR1/HR2 button**, Pulse default target, Calorie **weight**, Pulse **age**, Time, **Start**; **elevation/incline buttons change the target** mid-program.

**Contradiction ruled.** The WTT8 Target Heart Rate page says goals are met "by training at 80% or 60%, respectively". That page describes general training zones; this card follows the Selecting paragraph, which names the two programs and their maintained percentages (70%/90%). The VTT8 book agrees on both pages (90%/70% zone). The zone pages are on `sole-treadmill-programs-target-zone-w-series` (WTT8) and `sole-treadmill-programs-target-zone-90-70` (VTT8).
