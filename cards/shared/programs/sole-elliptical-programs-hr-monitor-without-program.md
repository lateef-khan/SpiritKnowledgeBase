On SOLE heart-rate ellipticals you may use the heart rate monitor feature without using a Heart Rate program — in Manual mode or during any of the nine different programs — while a Heart Rate program automatically controls pedal resistance. **The 'nine' is the books' own boilerplate and undercounts their own consoles**: the E25-2016 console photo (PDF p. 14, render-verified) carries ten program keys — Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, Interval, User 1, User 2, HR 1, HR 2 — even though its callout lists nine names.
