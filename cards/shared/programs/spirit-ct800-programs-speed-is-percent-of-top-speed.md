**A speed number in the preset program chart is a percentage of the top speed of the program, not a
speed in mph or km/h.** The top speed is the one you set during program setup.

Worked example as printed: the first Speed setting for P1 (Program 1, HILL) shows the number **20**.
That segment therefore runs at **20% of the top speed** for the program. If you set the top speed to
**10 mph**, the first segment is **2 mph**. Segment **12** shows **100**, so the speed there is 100%
of 10 mph - simply 10 mph.

All four CT800 owner's manuals print this paragraph and the same worked example, including the
CT800ENT touchscreen manual, **whose own chart of numbers is printed as a graphic and did not
survive text extraction**. For the numbers on the machines where they did, see
`spirit-ct800-programs-preset-speed-incline-chart`.

**The manuals say nothing about the incline numbers being scaled.** They are printed as percent of
grade in the chart and no manual calls them a percentage of anything you set.
