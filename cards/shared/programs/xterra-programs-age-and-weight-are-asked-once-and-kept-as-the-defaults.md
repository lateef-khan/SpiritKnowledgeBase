"Each of the programs can be customized with your personal information." You are asked for
your **age** and **weight**: age is needed by the Heart Rate programs, weight helps the
calorie estimate - "we cannot provide an exact calorie count". The books add that calorie
readings on any exercise equipment are estimates for comparing one workout with the next.

**Entering a program / changing settings**

- Press the **Program** keys to scroll through the programs; the profile of each shows in
  the dot-matrix window.
- Press **Enter** to select a program and begin customizing the settings, following the
  message window.
- To work out without entering new settings, press **Start** - "this will bypass the
  programming of data and take you directly to the start of your workout" with the default
  settings.

"Age and Weight default settings will change when you enter a new number. The last age and
weight entered will be saved as the new default settings", so you enter them once unless
they change or someone else enters different ones.

The SB4.5r book has dedicated program buttons (Manual, HR1, HR2 ...) where the SB500 and
FS5.xe scroll with Program up/down keys; the setup pages otherwise match.
