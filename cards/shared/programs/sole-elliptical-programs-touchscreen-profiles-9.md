First launch offers guest entry or a new profile (tap +): profiles remember physical parameters, favorites, templates and the personal custom program, and sync records with the SOLE+ app. At most 9 profiles, each optionally passcode-protected. Link SOLE+ by scanning the console QR code in the app (one SOLE+ account per console profile); skipping is possible via Profile settings later. Guests set age/weight on Home for accurate summaries.
