**You must wear the chest strap for these programs.** HR1 defaults to 60% of maximum ((220 − age) × .60, adjustable up or down); HR2 is programmed the same way at 80% for cardiovascular conditioning, vs HR1 for weight/body-fat loss. The E25 asks Weight then Age; the E35 asks Age then Weight (Ramp/Level or numeric keypad), then Time. During the program the machine auto-adjusts resistance toward your target: if you exceed it, the level drops to Level 1, and if you are still not within 15 beats per minute of target the program ends — a safety feature against overworking the heart. Stop steps back; Display scrolls data; a summary ends the session.
