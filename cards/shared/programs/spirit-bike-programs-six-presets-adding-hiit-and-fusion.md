The four 2023 residential bike manuals - XBR25, XBR55, XBU55 and XBR95 - all say the bike "has
multiple different programs that have been designed for a variety of workouts. **These six programs**
have factory preset work level profiles", and describe six:

**Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, HIIT and Fusion.**

Alongside the six presets the same books describe **Manual**, the three **Target** countdown programs
(Time, Distance, Calories) and **Custom 1** and **Custom 2**. Three of the four also describe **four
heart rate programs**; the XBR25 2023 does not
(`spirit-bike-programs-a-bike-manual-with-a-pulse-readout-but-no-heart-rate-program`).

**Every program is reached through one PROGRAM key** rather than a key per program: press PROGRAM
until the one you want is shown, then Enter. The level keys are labelled **+** and **-**.

**HIIT here is the old Interval program renamed.** Its paragraph is the Interval paragraph word for
word, and it takes the ordinary preset setup - it does not ask for sprint or rest lengths - see
`spirit-bike-programs-interval-profile`.

**The programs chapter is one chapter printed four times.** Measured on native PDF text over the
PROGRAMS pages, XBR55 2023 against XBR95 2023 is **100.0%** at word level and XBR55 2023 against
XBU55 2023 is **99.7%**. What separates the four machines is not the program list: the XBR95 runs to
40 resistance levels where the other three run to 20, and the XBR25 has no heart rate program.

**Interval and Calories were the casualties.** The XBR95 2016-2021 lineup had Calories and Interval
and no HIIT; the XBR55 and XBU55 2019-2021 lineup had Interval and User 1 and User 2 and no Fusion.
