- **Hill.** A triangle or pyramid type of gradual progression from approximately **10% of maximum
  effort** (the level you chose before starting) up to a maximum effort which lasts for **10% of the
  total workout time**, then a gradual regression back to approximately 10% of maximum effort.
- **Fat Burn.** A quick progression up to the maximum resistance level (default or user input) that
  is sustained for **2/3 of the workout**. It challenges your ability to sustain your energy output
  for an extended period.
- **Cardio.** A quick progression up to near maximum resistance level, with slight fluctuations up
  and down to let your heart rate elevate and recover repeatedly, before a quick cool down. Builds
  heart muscle, blood flow and lung capacity.
- **Strength.** A gradual progression of resistance up to **100% of maximum effort**, sustained for
  **25% of workout duration**. Builds strength and muscular endurance **in the lower body and
  glutes**. A brief cool down follows.
- **Interval.** High levels of intensity followed by recovery periods of low intensity, developing
  your "Fast Twitch" muscle fibres.

**The Strength description is what distinguishes these two manuals from the rest of the 2024 range.**
The 2024 CE800, CE850, CR800 and CU800 name Strength only as a picture caption and print no
description; the steppers print it in full, and the sentence about the **lower body and glutes** is
theirs alone - the elliptical and bike versions say "in the lower body".
