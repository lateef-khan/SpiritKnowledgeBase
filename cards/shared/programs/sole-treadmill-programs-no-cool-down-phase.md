**No A1 program prints a general cool-down phase.** Preset setup ends at Start (warm-up where printed), and workout end is a summary or idle return. Proved twice: a phrase search for cool down/cool-down/cooldown across all 25 sources, plus full reads of every generation's program chapter.

**Two test-only exceptions, not workout phases:** the TT8 Gerkin fit test cools down at 3.0 mph/0% for 1 or 3 minutes by test length (see `sole-tt8-programs-gerkin-fit-test`), and the TT9 prints cool-down columns in its preset chart plus a 1-3 minute post-test cool-down (see `tt9-2018-programs-preset-chart` and `tt9-2018-programs-fitness-test`). The F60-2012 "COOLING FANS" hit is the console fan, unrelated.
