1. Press **PROGRAM** to select **TARGET PROGRAM** and then **ENTER** to display **TARGET TIME**. Press **PROGRAM** to switch to **TARGET DISTANCE** and **TARGET CALORIES**, and press **ENTER** to confirm.
2. If Enter was pressed, the Message Center blinks a value indicating your **Age**. Use the + / - keys to adjust, then press Enter. Your age determines your recommended maximum heart rate, which the Bar Graph Display and the Heart Rate features are based on.
3. The Message Center blinks a value indicating your **Body Weight**.
4. The message window displays the **time, distance and calorie presets** to be adjusted, depending on the program you choose. Use the **Incline + / -** key to adjust, then press Enter.
5. You are then done programming and may press **Start** to begin your workout, or **Stop** to go back one level to change data entered in the programming phase.

**The XT185 and XT285 manuals print no default values for the targets.** The XT385, XT485 and XT685 manuals give 5 minutes, 1.0 mile and 300 calories - see the linked card - but those figures are not printed in the XT185 or XT285 manual.
