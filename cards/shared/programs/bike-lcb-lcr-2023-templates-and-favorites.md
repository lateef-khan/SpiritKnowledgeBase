**Templates are preset programs with a preserved set of parameters.**

- A program **can be saved as a template on completion**, or you can create one from scratch in the
  **Templates** tab: tap **"New Template"**, choose your base program, then save it with the properties
  you adjusted.
- **A maximum of 12 template programs can be stored.**
- To remove unwanted templates, press **"Edit Templates"** in the Templates tab.

**Favorites**

- A program can be labelled a **Favorite**. **Up to 3 favorite programs appear on the Home screen.**
- To "like" a program, open its program setup page and tap the **ribbon icon in the upper left corner**.
- All labelled programs are under the **Favorites** tab. To remove a label, press **"Edit Favorites"**,
  or open the program setup page and un-tap the ribbon icon.

Once you have decided on the program, press the **"Start This Program"** button on the screen, or the
physical **START** key on the machine, to begin your workout.
