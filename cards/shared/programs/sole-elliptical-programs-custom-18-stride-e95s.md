**The second profile here is stride, not incline.** Otherwise the menu-console procedure: 7-character username ('A' blinks; Level arrows; Stop finishes), Run-Program/delete-to-rebuild, menu-driven Age/Weight/Time, resistance profile to all eighteen segments, then the stride profile the same way, Start saves, Enter modifies, Stop exits, present-column-only mid-program changes, summary ends.
