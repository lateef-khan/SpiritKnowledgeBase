The Hill program **simulates going up and down a hill**. The resistance in the pedals steadily
increases and then decreases during the program.

At setup you are asked for a **Max Level** - the peak exertion level you will experience during the
program, **at the top of the hill**. The **factory setting is level seven**.

These five consoles print no percentages for the profile and no numeric segment values; the profile
is a picture on the dot matrix.

**The later XE ellipticals describe Hill completely differently** - a triangle or pyramid from about
10% of maximum effort to a peak lasting 10% of the workout. That is
`spirit-ce-programs-hill-profile`, and it is the description the XE395 2010 and every XE from 2021
on print.

The Sole E25 2026, CC81 2026 and B94/R92 2019 owner's manuals print this same "simulates going up
and down a hill" sentence for their own Hill programs. Those are Sole machines; a card carries one
brand, so they are not covered here.
