1. **Press the PROGRAM key** to pick the program, including Manual (P0) and P1-P5. Press Enter; the display prompts through programming, or press **Start** to begin with default values.
2. The Time window shows the default of **20 minutes**. Adjust with the up/down (fast/slow) keys, then press Enter. (You may press Start at any time to start.)
3. The Calorie window shows your **body weight**; entering it correctly affects the calorie count. Adjust and press Enter. (No machine gives an exact calorie count; use it to compare workouts.)
4. The Calorie window shows the **preset top speed** of the program. Adjust and press Enter — this caps the highest speed the program can reach.

There is **no age entry, no warm-up phase and no heart rate program** on these consoles. Later consoles add all three.
