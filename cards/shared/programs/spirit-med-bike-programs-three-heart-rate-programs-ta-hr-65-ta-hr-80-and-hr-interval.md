**Pressing the HR key offers three programs: TA HR 65%, TA HR 80% and HR interval.** The two
percentage programs target 65% or 80% of your maximum heart rate. **HR interval is named and never
described** - no page of any of the four books says what it does, how long its intervals are or
what they alternate between.

1. Press the **HR** key, choose the program, then press Confirm.
2. Enter your **age** with the Plus and Minus keys, then Confirm. *(Age sets the target: the training
   page works the maximum as 220 minus age.)*
3. Enter your **weight**, then Confirm.
4. Enter the **time**, then press Enter.
5. Adjust the **target HR** - *"the 65% / 80% heart rate level you will experience during the
   program"* - then Confirm.
6. Press **Start**. Confirm goes back to modify the settings; Stop steps back one screen during data
   entry.
7. **Plus or Minus during the program changes your target heart rate at any time.**
8. The Display key scrolls the data; when the program ends, Start runs it again and Stop exits.

**Which way the bike controls your pulse is stated twice and contradicted once.** The Dyaco PT
editions say *"The heart rate program automatically controls resistance at the pedals."* The 2025
Spirit books replace that sentence with treadmill boilerplate - *"the machine will automatically
adjust speed or incline to maintain the desired heart rate"* - on a bike that has neither. The
resistance sentence is the one that fits the machine.

The commercial CR800/CU800 consoles name two heart rate programs, 65% and 80%, with no HR interval
(`spirit-bike-programs-heart-rate-keys-of-sixty-five-and-eighty-percent`). The 4.0T treadmill lists
the same three names as this card (`40t-2026-programs-heart-rate-programs`).
