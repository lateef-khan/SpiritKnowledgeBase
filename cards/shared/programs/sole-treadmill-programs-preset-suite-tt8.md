The TT8 has **ten built-in programs**: one **Manual** program, **five preset profiles**, one **Custom**, one **Fit Test**, and two **Heart Rate programs (HR1 and HR2)**. The ten have dedicated program keys on the console.

The five presets, as printed (Hill and Strength name speed where the 2013 menu books say workload):

- **Hill.** "The Hill program simulates going up and down a hill. The speed will steadily increase and then decrease during the program."
- **Fat Burn.** Same fat-burning text as the menu books, ending "This program does not use heart rate but simulates a lower, steady exertion workout."
- **Cardio.** Same cardiovascular text as the menu books.
- **Strength.** "Designed to increase muscular strength in your lower body. This program will steadily increase in speed to a high level and forces you to sustain it. This is designed to strengthen and tone your legs and glutes (muscles of the butt)."
- **Interval.** Same oxygen-depletion/recovery text as the menu books.

**The TT8 SPEED and INCLINE profiles are drawn pictures, not numbers**, so there are no segment figures to quote.

Custom is a one-slot user-built program and the Fit Test is a Gerkin-protocol VO2 test; each has its own card: see `sole-tt8-programs-custom` (setup) and `sole-tt8-programs-gerkin-fit-test`. HR1/HR2 are on `sole-treadmill-programs-hr-60-80-tt8`.
