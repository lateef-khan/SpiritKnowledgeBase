In standby (ready) mode you can press **Start** directly, or press **ENTER** to set a
count-down target. Pressing ENTER once opens the **time** setting, a second press the
**distance** setting, a third press the **calorie** setting; adjust with **SPEED up/down**
and confirm with ENTER.

| Target | Default | Range | Step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time | 30:00 | 5:00 to 99:00 | 1:00 |
| Distance | 5.00 miles | 1.00 to 99.00 | 0.1 (TR260 wording); the TRX1400 book gives no step |
| Calories | 500 | 10 to 990 | 10 |

**The system accepts only the last parameter entered.** The TR260 book: "if the last input
is a distance, the previous parameter will be invalid and only the distance setting will be
accepted."

The workout starts at **0.5 mph** and the treadmill **stops automatically** when the
count-down reaches 0:00, 0.00 miles or 0 calories. The TR260 adds that "the number of
ascending segments is zero", i.e. Manual mode has no preset speed steps.

**Damaged text in the TRX1400 book:** both the time mode and the distance mode are headed
"Count-down time training mode", and the distance preset is printed as "5 (MPH)" with a
range of "1.00-99 (MPH)". The unit is a misprint - the window being set is the distance
window - and the figures match the TR260's 5.00 miles (1.00 to 99.00).

The TRX2500 prints the same Manual page with different limits (distance 1.00 to 9.00, calories
10 to 9990); see `not_to_be_confused_with`.
