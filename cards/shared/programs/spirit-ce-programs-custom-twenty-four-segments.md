**This is the twenty-four segment version.** The CE800 2013 and CE850 2016 store **twenty**
segments, and the CE800ENT stores **twenty** built on the touchscreen.

1. Select **Custom** with the Program button, then press Enter to begin programming.
2. Enter your name. The letter "A" blinks; Up switches to "B", Down switches to "Z". Press Enter for
   each letter. **Maximum 7 characters.** Press Stop when finished.
3. If a program is already stored in Custom, the display asks **Run Program?** - Up/Down selects Yes
   or No. Choosing No then offers to delete the stored program. **You must delete the current
   program to build a new one.**
4. Enter your **Age**.
5. Enter your **Weight**.
6. Set the **Time**.
7. Set the **Max Resistance Level** of the program, then press Enter. *(The CE850 2020 and CE850
   2022 print this step as "the Max Level and Max Stride ... the peak exertion level you will
   experience during the program".)*
8. The first column blinks. Set the resistance level for segment 1 with the Up button, then Enter.
9. The next segment starts at the previous segment's level. Repeat until **all twenty-four
   segments** have been set.
10. The display says "New Program Saved" and offers to start or modify the program. Stop exits to
    the start-up screen.
