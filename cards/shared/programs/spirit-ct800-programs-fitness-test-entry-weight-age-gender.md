1. The Message Window asks you to enter your **Weight**. The weight setting is shown in the
   **Distance** window; adjust with the Up and Down buttons, then press **Enter** to accept and go
   on to the next screen.
2. You are now asked to enter your **Age**. The age setting is shown in the **Incline** window;
   adjust, then press Enter.
3. You are now asked to enter your **Gender**. The gender setting is **also shown in the Incline
   window**; adjust, then press Enter.
4. Press **Start** to begin the test.

*As printed, both manuals put Age and Gender in the same Incline window on consecutive screens. That
is what the source says and it is preserved.*

**Weight comes before age on this console.** The 2012 console asks for age first and never asks for
gender - see `ct800-2012-programs-fitness-test-gerkin-only`. The CT800ENT touchscreen asks for a
fourth value, height - see
`ct800ent-2022-programs-fitness-test-entry-age-weight-height-gender`.

For what to do before, during and after the test itself, see `ct900-gerkin-wfi-test-procedure`.
