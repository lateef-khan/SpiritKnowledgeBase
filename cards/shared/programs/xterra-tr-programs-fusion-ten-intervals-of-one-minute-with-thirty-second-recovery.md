1. Press **Enter/Program**, select the program with **Speed up/down**; the screen prompts you
   to program it, or press **Start** to use the default values.
2. **Age** - adjust, press Enter/Program (age sets the recommended maximum heart rate).
3. **Body Weight** - adjust, press Enter/Program. The usual calorie note follows.
4. The **preset top speed** of the program - adjust, press Enter/Program.
5. The **preset top incline** - adjust, press Enter/Program.
6. The **interval motion, preset to 10** (the number of intervals) - adjust, press Enter/Program.
7. The **interval, preset to 1:00** - adjust, press Enter/Program.
8. **ENTER RECOVERY TIME, preset to 0:30** - adjust, press Enter/Program.
9. Press **Start** to begin, or **Stop** to go back one level.

**What Fusion actually does between the intervals is not described in this book** - no
strength exercises, no warm-up or cool-down structure, and nothing about stepping off the
treadmill. Spirit's Fusion (ten intervals of two minutes with dumbbell exercises) and the
TR6.6's Fitness program (ten one-minute intervals) are related programs with different
figures; see `not_to_be_confused_with`.
