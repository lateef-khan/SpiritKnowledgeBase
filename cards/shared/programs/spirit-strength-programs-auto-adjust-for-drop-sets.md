**AUTO ADJUST raises or lowers the resistance by itself, either with every repetition or
every few repetitions.** The manual presents it as an easy way to perform **drop sets** -
a popular way to challenge muscle fatigue - and tells the reader to ask a facility trainer
to learn more about drop sets.

Three settings, in the Auto Adjustment row of the Setup Screen:

| Setting | What it does | Unit shown |
|---|---|---|
| **Increment** | the weight by which the resistance changes | lb |
| **Progression** | whether the weight is increasing or decreasing | up / down arrow |
| **Frequency** | how many repetitions are completed before each change | reps |

The console's own help pop-up puts it as: *Use Auto Adjustment if you want the resistance
to gradually adjust during your set.*

**It is offered in PURE and in ECCENTRIC only.** In Eccentric it moves the **concentric**
resistance; the eccentric percentage on top of it does not change. The Isokinetic and
Elastic mode pages describe no Auto Adjust.

During the set the Progress Screen keeps showing the rule in force, on the resistance
line, so a user can see what the next change will be.
