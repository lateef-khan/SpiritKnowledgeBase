**Setup**

1. Press the **Fitness** key, then **Enter**; the display prompts you through the programming.
2. **Age (default 35)** - it sets the heart rate bar graph and the heart rate features, so
   enter it correctly.
3. **Body Weight (default 150 lbs)** - it sets the calorie count, which "won't be accurate
   because the machine can't calculate calories expended while strength training".
4. **Preset top resistance of the program (Level 5)**; adjust, press Enter.
5. **Number of intervals (default 10; choose 10, 20 or 30)**.
6. **Interval time (default 1:00)** - the duration of both the cardio and the strength
   intervals. "On average you will complete 10-12 repetitions of the strength exercise in a
   0:30 interval. As a general rule, the longer the interval, the less weight (dumbbells) and
   resistance (elliptical) required."
7. **Recovery time** after completing both the cardio and strength intervals.

**Program example, as printed** (10 intervals = 5 cardio and 5 strength; cardio and strength
intervals 0:30, recovery 1:00):

- The program begins with a **3:00 warm up**.
- 1st cardio interval, 0:30; the console counts down and the Message Center reads
  "STRENGTH INTERVAL 1 BEGIN DUMBBELL ROW".
- The user steps off the elliptical to perform the strength exercise; the console counts
  down to 0:00 and beeps 3x to signal a return to the elliptical.
- "PRESS START TO BEGIN RECOVERY".
- The 2nd cardio interval follows, and so on until 5 cardio, 5 strength and 5 recovery
  intervals are done; the five strength exercises run in the order listed in the manual.
- The last **2:00 are a Cool Down** "with the user pedaling at a low resistance".

With **20 intervals** you perform each strength exercise twice before moving on; with **30**
you perform each once, then repeat the sequence of all five a 2nd and 3rd time.

**Caution:** select a pair of dumbbells you can manoeuvre safely and effectively for the
strength interval time you chose.

The five exercises (bent over row, push up, shoulder press, tricep overhead extension,
bicep curl) are the same page as the TR6.6 treadmill's; that card now covers these two
ellipticals. The treadmill version of the program has a top speed and incline instead of a
level and quotes 15-20 repetitions; see `see_also`.
