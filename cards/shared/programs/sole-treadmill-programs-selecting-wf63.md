1. **Press the PROGRAM keys** to view the programs, then press **Enter** to select one. Or just press **Start** to begin with the preset values for age, weight and other program data.
2. The Time window shows the preset value of **20 minutes**. Adjust with the up/down or fast/slow keys, then press Enter. (**You may press Start at any time** to start with what you have entered.)
3. The Calorie window shows your **body weight**; entering it correctly affects the calorie count. Adjust, then press Enter. (No machine gives an exact calorie count; use it to compare workouts.)
4. The Calorie window shows the **preset top speed** of the program. Adjust, then press Enter — this caps the highest speed the program will reach at its peak.
5. **Press Start to begin.** The program begins with a **3 minute warm-up**; press Start again to bypass it and go straight to the program.

There is **no age entry** in this setup, unlike the 2013 menu consoles whose step 2 is age. The warm-up and its bypass match the 2013 books.
