The CR900 2018 and CU900 2018 owner's manuals say the fitness bike has **four different programs**
that have factory preset work level profiles: **Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio and Interval**. Four are
counted and four are described.

Beside those four presets the console carries its own buttons for **Manual**, **HIIT**, **Fit Test**,
**Heart Rate** and **Watt** (constant power), plus Quick Start from the Start button. Each program
has its own key: pressing a program key previews the profile, and Enter selects it.

**The console photograph OCRs the button strip badly** as
`MANUAL TATBURN IML HEART RATE CARDIO MRL INTERVAL HINESS TEST`. The legible programs in it are
Manual, Fat Burn, Heart Rate, Cardio, Interval and Fitness Test; the two garbled labels sit where
Hill and Watt belong. The KB does not card the button strip itself because the image is not
reliably readable.

**Neither manual has a Custom program**, and neither prints a target heart rate zone page - see
`spirit-bike-programs-no-target-heart-rate-zone-page`.

**The later LED manuals count five presets, not four.** See
`spirit-bike-programs-five-presets-of-which-four-are-described`.
