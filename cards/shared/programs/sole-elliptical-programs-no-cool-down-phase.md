No button-console book prints a cool-down program, phase or button: pausing offers resume (Start) or end-and-summary (Stop), and the E98 fit-test merely advises cooling down 1-3 minutes afterwards. A cool-down choice exists only in the pause menu of the 2023 E95s/E98 touchscreen consoles.
