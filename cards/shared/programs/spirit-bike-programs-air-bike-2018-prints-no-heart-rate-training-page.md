**The AB900 2018 manual has none of the three heart rate training pages the AB950 2024 prints.**
There is no "220 minus your age" formula, no percentage-of-maximum guidance, no worked example, no
target heart rate training chart, no rate of perceived exertion (Borg) page, and no chest strap
fitting page or chest strap warning page.

**How the absence was checked.**

- A loose search for `strap` returns **two** hits in the whole book: a packing instruction about
  cutting the shipping straps, and the Quick Start line about a "chect strap" being detected.
- `exert`, `borg` and `perceived` return **nothing**.
- `220` returns only two German telephone numbers.
- `zone` returns one hit, in the German safety text.
- `MHR` returns only the two Target Heart Rate Mode sentences about 65% and 85%.
- Every one of the 52 PDF pages was rendered at 200 dpi and read with OCR, then compared against the
  native extraction. The only pages where the render recovered materially more text are **13 and 14**
  (the warning label pages, already added to the source as OCR supplements) and **page 30**, which is
  the console picture. **None of them is a heart rate page.**

**What the AB900 does say about heart rate.** Only the console pages: the Heart Rate Display Panel,
and Target Heart Rate Mode, which asks for your age and aims between **65% and 85%** of the maximum
it calculates. **The manual never prints the formula it uses to get that maximum.** Take it from a
Spirit manual that does print one - 220 minus your age - and say that is where the figure comes from.

**The AB950 2024 manual prints all three pages** and its Target Heart Rate Mode is word for word the
same. If the customer has an AB950, answer from those pages instead.

**This is a different absence from the four commercial bikes.** The CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900ENT
and CU900ENT also lack a zone page - see `spirit-bike-programs-no-target-heart-rate-zone-page` - but
they are magnetic bikes with a different console.
