**This is HIIT, the preset interval profile. It is not Fusion, which alternates treadmill intervals with off-machine dumbbell exercises.**

**Speed:** The HIIT program takes you through high levels of intensity followed by periods of low intensity. It increases your endurance by depleting your oxygen level, followed by periods of recovery to replenish oxygen, so your cardiovascular system is programmed to use oxygen more efficiently. The program also forces your body to become more efficient because of the spikes in heart rate between recovery periods, which aids heart rate recovery from intense activities.

**Incline (XT385, XT485 and XT685 only):** This program spikes similar to the speed profile, but in different segments (columns), which means all of your lower extremity muscles are equally challenged throughout the program. **The incline alternates between 25 & 65 % of maximum elevation.** The XT185 and XT285 manuals print no incline paragraph for HIIT.

The XT485ENT owner's manual (page 40) prints the **same incline paragraph**, including the
25 & 65 % figures, but describes the speed profile in the Fusion manual's words: high levels
of intensity followed by recovery periods of low intensity, using and developing your "Fast
Twitch" muscle fibres. It does not print the heart-rate-recovery sentence the other 2023
manuals print. The XT685ENT manual prints no HIIT description; it gives a numeric segment
table instead.
