**You must use a wireless chest strap transmitter to use the heart rate program.**

The four heart rate programs operate the same way and are programmed the same way. The only difference is the target: **HR1 is set to 55%, HR2 to 65%, HR3 to 75% and HR4 to 85% of the maximum heart rate.** After you select your heart rate target, the program attempts to keep you at or within **3 - 5 heart beats per minute** of that value. Follow the prompts in the Message Center to maintain your selected heart rate value.

1. Press the **PROGRAM** key to select **HR PROGRAM**, then press **ENTER**.
2. Press the **PROGRAM** key to select target **HR 55%**, **HR 65%**, **HR 75%** or **HR 85%**, then press Enter.
3. The Message Center asks for your **Age**. Enter it with the Speed + or - keys, then press Enter.
4. Enter your **Weight** with the Speed + or - keys, then press Enter.
5. Adjust the **Time** with the Speed + or - keys, then press Enter.
6. Adjust the **Heart Rate Target** - the heart rate level you will strive to maintain during the program - with the Speed + or - keys, then press Enter. *The heart rate that appears is based on the % you accepted in step 2; changing this number increases or decreases that percentage.*
7. Press **Start** to begin your workout. *At any time during the editing of data you can press the **Stop** key to go back one screen.*
8. To increase or decrease the workload at any time during the program, press the **Incline + or -** key on the console or the **left handlebar**. This changes your target heart rate at any time during the program.

The manual contradicts itself at step 7: the step text says you can go back and modify your settings by pressing the **Enter** key, while the note immediately under it says the **Stop** key goes back one screen. Both are printed as shown.
