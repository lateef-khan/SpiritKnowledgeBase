These books have the same ten as the 2013 menu consoles (one Manual program, five preset profiles, U1 and U2, HR1 and HR2), but **two preset descriptions are carried over from a bike manual and do not fit a treadmill**:

- **Hill.** "The Hill program simulates going up and down a hill. **The resistance in the pedals** will steadily increase and then decrease during the program." A treadmill has no pedals; read "pedals" as the belt workload.
- **Strength.** "This program will steadily **increase in resistance** to a high level and forces you to sustain it," for the legs and glutes.
- **Fat Burn, Cardio and Interval** match the 2013 menu books word for word.

Two more book quirks. The AF63/AF65 and AF80/AF83/AF85 books print the Manual slot as **(PO)**, and the AF80/AF83/AF85 book prints **two profile-picture columns per program (F80 vs F83/85)** instead of one. The pictures are dot-matrix drawings with no numeric values, so there are no segment figures to quote.

Carded as printed; the 2013 wording without the bike carryover is on `sole-treadmill-programs-preset-suite-2013`.
