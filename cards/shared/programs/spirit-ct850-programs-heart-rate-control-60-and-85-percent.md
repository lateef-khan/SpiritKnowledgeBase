**These are the 60% and 85% figures.** The same console in its **2020** revision targets **65% and
80%** instead, with the identical wording around it - see
`ct800-2020-programs-heart-rate-control-65-and-80-percent`, which now covers the 2020 CT850. The
figures are the only thing that changed between the two printings.

Heart Rate Control (HRC) uses the treadmill's **incline system**, not speed, to control your heart
rate. Increases and decreases in elevation affect heart rate much more efficiently than changes in
speed alone. The HRC program changes elevation gradually to achieve the programmed target heart
rate.

During setup you choose either the **Weight Control (HR-1)** program or the **Cardiovascular (HR-2)**
program.

- **Weight Control (HR-1)** attempts to maintain your heart rate at **60% of your Maximum Heart
  Rate.**
- **Cardiovascular (HR-2)** attempts to maintain your heart rate at **85% of your Maximum Heart
  Rate.**

Your Maximum Heart Rate is a formula that subtracts your age from a constant of **220**. Your HR
setting is calculated automatically during setup when you enter your age.

## Programming steps

**You must receive a strong, steady value in the heart rate window or the program will not start.**
The Message Center shows **"Check Pulse"** when there is no pulse signal.

1. Select the HR program. **On the 2016 machine you reach it with the Program button; on the 2018
   machine there is a dedicated HR button.**
2. The Message Center shows "Press Enter to modify or Start to begin workout". Press **Enter** for
   settings, or **Start** to run the default program.
3. The Message Center shows "Press 1 or 2 to select then press Enter" and displays **"1"**. Choose
   HR-1 or HR-2. *On the 2018 machine the choice is made with the numeric buttonpad.*
4. "Adjust Time then press Enter", with the Time window blinking. Use the Up/Down and Fast/Slow
   buttons, then press Enter.
5. "Adjust Age then press Enter". *The 2018 manual blinks age in the Incline window and adds that
   adjusting age changes the target heart rate value.*
6. "Adjust Body Weight then press Enter". *The 2018 manual blinks it in the Incline window.*
7. "Adjust Heart Rate then press Enter". *The 2018 manual blinks it in the Speed window.*
8. Press **Start** to begin your workout, **Enter** to modify, or **Stop** to return to the previous
   screen.

**HR 1 and HR 2 have no warm-up.**

The five-preset CT800 console prints the same 60% and 85% figures but is set up from **HR1 and HR2
buttons** with the target heart rate in the Pulse window and the bodyweight in the Calorie window,
and lets you retarget with the Incline keys mid-workout - a different procedure that must not be
carried across, see `spirit-ct800-programs-heart-rate-control-60-and-85-percent`.

**A contradiction between two pages of the 2016 manual:** its heart rate training page recommends
**65%** for burning fat and **85%** for the cardiovascular system, while this page sets Weight
Control to 60%. Neither figure is reconciled in the source. See
`ct900-heart-rate-exertion-target-zones`. **The 2018 manual has no such clash** - its training page
prints 60% and 80%, see `xt-2023-programs-heart-rate-training-zone`.
