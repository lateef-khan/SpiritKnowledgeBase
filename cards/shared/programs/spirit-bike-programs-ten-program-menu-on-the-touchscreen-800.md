On the CU800ENT 2022 and CR800ENT 2023, selecting **Exercise Programs** from the Home screen opens a
menu of: **Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, HIIT, Interval, Heart Rate, Custom, Constant Power, and
Fitness Test** - ten programs.

**There is no Strength program on any of the three 800ENT bikes.** The only occurrence of the word
in any of the three manuals is "strengthening the cardio vascular system" on the heart rate page.

**The CU800ENT 2024 has a redesigned touchscreen and does not print a lineup sentence.** Its
programs open from an **All Programs** button on the home page; tapping a program opens **Preview
Mode**, where you slide left and right between programs of the same category, and tapping again
opens the **Program Setup** page. The 2024 manual then gives one page each to Manual, Hill, Fat
Burn, Cardio, Interval, **Watts**, Heart Rate, Custom, HIIT and Fitness Test - the same ten, with
Constant Power renamed Watts.

**The touchscreen 900 bikes have a different lineup** - ten programs with Strength and without HIIT.
