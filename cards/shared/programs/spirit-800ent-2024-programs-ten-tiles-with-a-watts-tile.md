Press **All Programs** on the home page to open a full selection of the preset programs. The
Programs page shows **ten tiles**:

Manual, Fatburn, Cardio, Hill, HIIT, Interval, Heart Rate, **Watts**, Fitness Test and Custom.

Tapping a tile opens a Preview page that describes the program before you commit to it, with a
**Select This Program** button.

**The power program is labelled Watts on this generation**, not Constant Power. The 2021-2023
touchscreens print the same ten-program list but name the power program **Constant Power** and reach
it from **Exercise Programs** rather than **All Programs** - see
`ce800ent-programs-touchscreen-program-menu` and
`spirit-bike-programs-ten-program-menu-on-the-touchscreen-800`.

**There is no Strength program on either machine**, and no Constant Power *and* Watts pair: the two
names are the same program.

**The elliptical and the upright bike print this page identically.** Measured on native PDF text
over the whole programs chapter the two manuals agree to **99.9%**, the residue being word order in
two lines of running text.
