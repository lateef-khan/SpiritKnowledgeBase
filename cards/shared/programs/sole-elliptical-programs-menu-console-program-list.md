The menu consoles offer Manual, five presets (Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, plus Interval on the 2015/2016 books and HIIT on the 2019 books), a fitter's Fit-Test, and two heart-rate slots: physical 2HR keys driving on-screen HR1/HR2 programs. Custom storage differs: U1/U2 (USER 1/USER 2 keys) on the E95/E95s, one CUSTOM slot on the E98 — and the E95s overlay labels the heart-rate keys HRC1/HRC2 while its operation text still says HR1/HR2. Setup runs through on-screen menus ('the menu will display Age at first item') rather than the older key-per-step flow.
