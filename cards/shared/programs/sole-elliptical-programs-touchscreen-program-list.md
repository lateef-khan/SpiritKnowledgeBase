The Training section offers an immediate Start, a manually controlled timed workout ('Timer'/Manual), up to 3 (E35) or 6 (others) favorite shortcuts on Home, and All Programs with tabs for All Programs, Templates and Favorites. Program cards: Manual (Timer), Hill, Fatburn, Cardio, Strength, HIIT, Heart Rate, Custom and Fitness Test. Tapping a card opens its setup page (program description, Total Time / Max Level via ruler, +/− or numpad, arrow keys to browse). Guest mode sets age and weight on the Home screen for accurate summaries; profiles remember parameters, favorites, templates and records.
