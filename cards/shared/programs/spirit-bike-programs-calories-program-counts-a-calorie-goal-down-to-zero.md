Only the three LED **XBR95** manuals - 2016, 2018 and 2021 - carry a Calories key.

1. Press **Calories**, then **Enter**.
2. **Age** (default **35**). It sets the heart rate bar graph and the heart rate programs, so enter it
   correctly. Enter.
3. **Body Weight**. It sets the calorie count. Enter.
4. **Calorie burn** (default **300**). Enter.
5. **Average pedal RPM** (default **50**). Enter.
6. **Top resistance** of the program (default **Level 5**). Each program changes resistance as it
   runs; this caps the highest it can reach. Enter.
7. The console shows a **Program Time it has calculated** from steps 4 to 6. Press **Start** to accept
   it, or **Enter** to go back to steps 4-6 and have the time recalculated.

While it runs:

- The program opens with a **3:00 warm up** - 1:00 at Level 1, 1:00 at Level 2, 1:00 at Level 3 - or
  press **Enter** again to skip it.
- **Both the Calorie and the Time windows count down to 0.** If the time expires before the calories
  reach zero, the Time window starts accumulating time until the calories do.
- It closes with a **3:00 cool down** - 1:00 at Level 3, 1:00 at Level 2, 1:00 at Level 1 - or press
  **Enter** to end the workout.

**The preset page gives Calories no profile of its own.** Its description there is the Fat Burn
paragraph reprinted word for word: a quick progression up to the maximum resistance level sustained
for two thirds of the workout. The manual never says whether the two profiles really are the same
shape.

**The XBR95 2023 dropped the Calories key** and offers Target Calories instead, which counts a
calorie goal down with no warm up, no cool down and no RPM step - see
`spirit-bike-programs-target-time-distance-and-calories-on-the-led-console`.

**The Spirit XE795 elliptical prints the same procedure.** Separate card, different product line -
see `xe795-2021-programs-calories-program-counts-down-to-a-calorie-goal`.
