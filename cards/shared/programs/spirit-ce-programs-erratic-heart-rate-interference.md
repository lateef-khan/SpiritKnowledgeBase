**Caution! Do not use the elliptical for Heart Rate programs unless a steady, solid Actual Heart Rate
value is being displayed.** High, wild, random numbers indicate a problem.

Areas to look for interference which may cause erratic heart rate:

1. **The elliptical is not properly grounded.** *(Printed only in the CE800ENT and CE900 2025
   manuals. The other seven begin their list at the next item.)*
2. Microwave ovens, TVs, small appliances, etc.
3. Fluorescent lights.
4. Some household security systems.
5. A fence for a pet. *(The CE800 2021, CE800 2023, CE800ENT, CE850 2020 and CE850 2022 manuals say
   **Electric** fence; the CE800 2013, CE850 2016, CE900 2025 and CES880 2025 manuals say
   **Perimeter** fence.)*
6. Some people have trouble with the transmitter picking up a signal from their skin. Try wearing
   the transmitter **upside down**; normally it is oriented so the Spirit Fitness logo is right side
   up.
7. The antenna that picks up your heart rate is very sensitive. If there is an outside noise source,
   **turning the whole machine 90 degrees** may de-tune the interference.
8. Another individual wearing a transmitter **within 3 feet** of your machine's console.

**Who to call.** The CE900 2025 and CES880 2025 manuals close with "contact Spirit Fitness"; the
other seven close with "contact your dealer".

**The CE900 2021 owner's manual has none of this** - it prints no chest strap chapter and no erratic
operation page.

The service manuals hold a shorter troubleshooting row for the same symptom, with different causes -
see `spirit-erratic-pulse-display`.
