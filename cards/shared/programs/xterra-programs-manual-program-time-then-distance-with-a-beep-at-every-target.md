Select **Manual** with UP/DOWN and press ENTER. The first parameter, "Time", flashes; adjust
it with UP/DOWN and press ENTER to save and move to the next parameter. Continue through the
parameters you want, then press START/STOP.

"**(If user sets up the target time to workout then the next parameter of Distance can not
be adjusted)**" - time and distance cannot both be targets in one workout.

"When any one of the workout parameters counts down to zero the console emits a beep sound
and the workout stops automatically. Press START KEY to continue the workout to reach the
unfinished workout goals set."

UP and DOWN raise and lower the resistance load during the workout. The same page and
wording is printed in all five books.
