**The chest strap is not in the box.** These three manuals head the page "HEART RATE CHEST STRAP*
(NOT INCLUDED)". **Unlike the 800 manuals they do not repeat it in the body**: there is no "The
chest strap is sold separately" sentence, so the heading is the only statement on this page. The
CU800ENT 2024 does say it once more on its safety page - "Use of a chest transmitter strap (sold
separately) is a much more accurate method of heart rate analysis."

1. Attach the transmitter to the elastic strap using the **interlocking key**.
2. Adjust the strap as tightly as possible as long as it is not too tight to remain comfortable.
3. Position the transmitter with the logo centred in the middle of your torso, facing away from your
   chest. Some people must position it slightly left of centre. Insert the round end of the elastic
   strap and secure it around your chest with the locking parts.
4. Position the transmitter **directly below the pectoral muscles**.
5. **Sweat is the best conductor**; plain water may also be used to pre-wet the electrodes - the two
   ribbed oval areas on the reverse of the belt and both sides of the transmitter. Wear the strap a
   few minutes before your workout: some users take longer to give a strong steady signal at first,
   and this lessens after "warming up".
6. Stay within range of the console. Wearing the transmitter on bare skin assures proper operation;
   over a shirt, wet the areas of the shirt the electrodes rest on.

**The transmitter switches itself on** when it detects activity from the user's heart and off when
it does not. It is water resistant, but moisture can create false signals, so dry it after use to
prolong battery life. **Estimated transmitter battery life is 2500 hours; the replacement battery is
a Panasonic CR2032.**

**None of the three prints the third-party strap sentence** that the 800 manuals carry, so this page
does not say whether a Bluetooth or ANT+ strap works. **For the CU800ENT 2024, another page says it
does**: the troubleshooting table has a "Bluetooth chest strap heart rate monitor no function" row
telling you to check the strap's power and to turn on Bluetooth on the console and tap the strap's
name to pair it. Read that as evidence the 2024 supports a Bluetooth strap; the CR900 2025 and
CU900 2025 manuals say nothing either way.

**A different page covers the 800 machines.** The CR800 2021, CR800 2023, CU800 2021, CU800ENT 2022
and CR800ENT 2023 print the interlocking **button** version with a bare CR2032 and a third-party
strap sentence - see `spirit-bike-programs-wearing-the-chest-strap`.

**The CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900ENT and CU900ENT have no chest strap chapter at all.**
