**Inhale on the non-exertion part of the movement, exhale during the exertion portion,
and never hold your breath.**

The manual calls this a general rule of thumb and prints nothing further - no count, no
tempo, no instruction about bracing.

In practice the exertion portion is the half of the repetition you are working against
the resistance, so the breath out goes with the effort and the breath in goes with the
return.

**Only the ST800 bench, dumbbell rack and functional trainer books print a breathing
rule.** No CSS, CSD, CSF or CSI Spirit strength manual mentions breathing anywhere.
