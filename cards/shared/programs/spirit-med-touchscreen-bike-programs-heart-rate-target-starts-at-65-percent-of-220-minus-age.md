*"Heart Rate Target uses your 8.0U's resistance system to control your heart rate based on
information from a heart rate sensor (not included). Increases and decreases in resistance affect
heart rate efficiently. The program automatically changes resistance gradually to achieve the
programmed target heart rate."*

**The default target is:**

```
MHR = (220 - age) x 65%
```

*"Your maximum heart rate is based upon a formula that subtracts your age from a constant of 220.
Your target heart rate setting is automatically calculated during the setup mode when you enter your
age and you can adjust from there."*

On the setup screen a **"Calculate % of max" switch** changes the target between **BPM** and **% of
the user's maximum heart rate**; set the target and the program time, then Start. The screenshots
show the two forms of the same screen - **110 bpm** on one, **65%** on the other, both at 20:00 -
with the pickers stepping in **5 bpm** and **5%**.

**65% is a program default, not training advice.** Neither book prints a maximum heart rate chapter,
a training zone or an RPE scale (`spirit-bike-programs-no-target-heart-rate-zone-page`). The
touchscreen CR/CU 800ENT bikes hold their target within five beats and say so; **no tolerance is
printed here** (`spirit-bike-programs-heart-rate-defaults-to-sixty-five-percent-and-holds-within-five-beats`).

The 8.0U and 8.5R 2025 books print this page word for word, as do the 8.5S stepper and 8.5UE
ergometer.
