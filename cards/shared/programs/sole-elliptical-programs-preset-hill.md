The Hill program simulates going up and down a hill. The resistance in the pedals will steadily increase and then decrease during the program.

On incline machines each preset carries a resistance profile and an incline profile (dot-matrix pictures, approximate by design; the E20 has resistance profiles only). The peak of the hill is set with Max Level during setup (see the setup card for your generation).
