1. **Press the desired program from the 10 available program keys**, then press **Enter** to customise with your data, or just press **Start** to begin with the default settings.
2. The Time window blinks the default of **20 minutes**. Adjust with the up/down keys, then press Enter. (**You may press Start at any time** to begin with only the settings modified so far.)
3. The Calorie window blinks your **body weight**; entering it correctly affects the calorie count. Adjust and press Enter. (No machine gives an exact calorie count; use it to compare workouts.)
4. The Heart Rate window blinks your **Age**. Entering it correctly affects the heart rate Bar Graph Display and the Heart Rate Control programs, which work off a percentage of maximum heart rate. Adjust and press Enter.
5. The Speed window blinks the **preset top speed** of the program. Adjust and press Enter — this caps the highest speed the program will reach.

**Now press the Start key to begin your workout.** These books print **no warm-up phase and no warm-up bypass**; the program starts directly.

The F83/F85 bar-graph note appears in books covering those models ("F83/F85 only"); the S73/S77 books say the same for their bar graph.
