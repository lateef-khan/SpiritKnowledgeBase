*"A cycle ergometer test of aerobic fitness. The subject cycles at 50 rpm for 6 min at a work load set
at a level related to the sex and condition of the subject."* The loads printed:

| Subject | Work load |
|---|---|
| unconditioned males | 50-100 watts |
| unconditioned females | 50-75 watts |
| conditioned males *(printed "mates")* | 100-150 watts |
| conditioned females | 75-110 watts |

*"The subject's heart rate is taken in the last 10 s of each of the two final minutes of exercise.
The average of these two figures, corrected for the subject's age, is used to estimate a maximal
oxygen uptake (VO2max). The estimate is based on the assumption that subjects of the same age have
a similar maximal heart rate."*

**That is everything the page prints about it.** No setup screen is shown for this test - the
screenshot on the page belongs to the Steep Ramp Test - so no entry steps, no end condition and no
result display are recorded here. The 8.0T treadmill's Astrand Rhyming is a walking test at a
constant pace (`80t-2026-programs-astrand-rhyming`) and its figures are not this bike's.

The 8.0U and 8.5R 2025 books print this page word for word. **The 8.5UE ergometer does not have this
test.**
