**This machine's pair is 60% and 80%, not 60% and 85%.** Every other Spirit climber in the range
uses 85% for the upper figure, so the answers differ by nine beats for a forty year old.

Determine your **Maximum Heart Rate** with the formula **220 minus your age**. Your heart rate
training zone is **50% to 90%** of maximum. **60% of MHR is recommended for burning fat while 80% is
recommended for strengthening the cardiovascular system. This 60% to 80% is the zone to stay in for
maximum benefit.**

**Example**, for someone who is 40 years old:

- 220 - 40 = **180** (maximum heart rate)
- 180 x .6 = **108** beats per minute (60% of maximum)
- 180 x .8 = **144** beats per minute (80% of maximum)
- So for a 40 year old the training zone would be **108 to 144 beats per minute.**

The arithmetic on this page is correct.

The page also prints a **Target Heart Rate Training Chart** - maximum heart rate as black columns by
age from 20 to 65, with a Weight Loss line at 60% and a Cardiovascular line at **80%**.

**The heart rate program keys on the same machine are named for the same two percentages**, 60% and
80%, which is unusual: the Spirit steppers name their keys 65% and 80% while their training page
recommends 60% and 85%. See `xs895-programs-heart-rate-keys-of-sixty-and-eighty-percent`.

**Do not answer this from a Spirit stepper or vertical climber card.** Those machines print 60% and
85% and a 108-to-153 zone - see `spirit-stepper-programs-target-zone-of-108-to-153-beats`.
