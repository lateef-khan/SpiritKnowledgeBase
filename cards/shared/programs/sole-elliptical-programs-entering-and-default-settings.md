Press a program key, then Enter, and follow the Message Window; or just press Start to bypass programming and work out on the saved settings. The last Age and Weight entered become the new defaults, so you only re-enter them when they change or someone else uses the machine. Printed defaults:

| Books | Age | Weight | Time | Max Levels (Hill/FatBurn/Cardio/Strength/Interval) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E25/E55-2007, VE25/VE55-2008 (e25-2007, e55-2006, ve25-2007, ve55-2007) | 35 | 155 lb | 30:00 | 7/5/7/8/7 |
| E35/E75/E95-2007, VE95-2008 ch. (e35-2007, e75-2007, e95-2007, ve95-2007) | 35 | 150 lb | 30:00 | 7/5/7/8/7 |
| VE35-2008 ch. (ve35-2007) | 35 | 155 lb | 20:00 | 7/5/7/8/7 |
| 2009-2013 books (we/ae/e-series) | not printed | not printed | not printed | not printed |

The VE35 chapter selects programs by scrolling with the Up/Down keys, and the E20 models in the E20/E25-2012 book scroll with the Level 5/6 keys instead of program buttons.
