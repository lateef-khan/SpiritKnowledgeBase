The Fat Burn program is designed to maximize the burning of fat. Most experts agree a lower exertion level held at a steady workload is best, and the absolute best way to burn fat is to keep heart rate around **60% to 70%** of its maximum potential.

**This program does not use heart rate.** It simulates a lower, steady exertion workout. If you want the console to actually control resistance from your heart rate, use an HR program instead.

It is one of the five preset programs, all of which are set up the same way.
