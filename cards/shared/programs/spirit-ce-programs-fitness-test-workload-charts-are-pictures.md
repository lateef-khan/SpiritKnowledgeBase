Every one of these manuals prints two charts under the Fitness Test heading:

- **Workload Chart For Male Or Very Fit Female**
- **Workload Chart For Female Or De-Conditioned Male**

**In all seven the charts are printed as pictures.** Two of those pictures have since been recovered
by OCR, both of them the **female / de-conditioned male** chart - on the CES880 2025 and the
CE800ENT. Those values are on
[the female workload chart](spirit-ce-programs-fitness-test-female-workload-chart.md).

**The male / very fit female chart is still unrecovered on every one of the seven machines**, so
this knowledge base does not hold its values. The test still runs from it: the Male/Female choice at
the start of the test decides which of the two charts the console uses.

**The CE800 2013 is a scan and its chart was put through OCR.** What came out is a broken jumble -
a first-stage label reading `ist Stage 300-kgni/min`, heart-rate column headings `HR <120`,
`HR 120-135`, `HR>135`, and loose values including `150`, `300`, `450`, `600`, `700`, `750`, `900`
and `1050 kgm/min` with no recoverable row-to-column mapping, plus one clearly damaged token
(`4050 kgrnémin`). The kgm/min ladder matches the recovered female chart, but the heading and the
heart-rate columns above it do not. **The mapping is not recoverable and has not been guessed at.**

The CE800ENT scan of the male chart is damaged the same way: a header reading `HR <0 | 90 = 105 >
105` over `HR WR <120  HR 120-135  HR O15`, and a single surviving row, `3rd stage 1050 / 900 / 750`
with no fourth value. **Not enough of it can be read to card it.**

What the console *does* with the chart is on `spirit-ce-programs-fitness-test-ymca-protocol`; what
the resulting score means is on `ct900-vo2max-score-interpretation`.
