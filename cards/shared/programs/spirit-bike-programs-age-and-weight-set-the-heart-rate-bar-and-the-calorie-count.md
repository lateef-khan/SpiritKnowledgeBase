Each program can be customised with your personal information. The console asks for **Age** and
**Weight** because some readouts depend on them:

- **Age** makes the heart rate **bar graph** show the correct number, and is needed by the **Heart
  Rate control program** so the work settings suit your age. Without it the settings could be too
  high or too low for you.
- **Weight** makes the **calorie** reading more accurate.

**Calorie note, printed verbatim in the manual:** calorie readings on every piece of exercise
equipment, in a gym or at home, are not accurate and vary widely. They are meant only as a guide to
monitor your progress from workout to workout. The only way to measure calorie burn accurately is in
a clinical setting connected to a host of machines, because every person burns calories at a
different rate.

**The CU800 2012, CR900 2018, CU900 2018 and CR900ENT and CU900ENT close the note** with "you will
continue to burn calories at an accelerated rate for at least an hour after you have finished
exercising"; the CR800 2021, CR800 2023, CU800 2021, CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 stop one sentence
earlier.

**Five manuals drop the sentence about the bar graph** and keep only the Heart Rate control reason
for entering age: the CR800 2021, CR800 2023, CU800 2021, CR900 2025 and CU900 2025. The CU800 2012,
CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900ENT and CU900ENT print both reasons.

**The three 800ENT manuals have no such page at all.**

**All fourteen residential XBR and XBU manuals print this page**, and all fourteen close the calorie
note with the "accelerated rate for at least an hour after you have finished exercising" sentence.

**They split the same two ways as the commercial books.** The ten LED books - XBR95 2016/2018/2021
and 2023, XBR25 2019 and 2023, XBR55 2019/2021/2023, XBU55 2019/2021/2023 - give only the Heart Rate
reason for age ("Entering your Age is necessary during the Heart Rate programs to ensure the correct
settings are in the program for your Age. Otherwise the work settings could be too high or low for
you") and say weight "aides in calculating a more correct Calorie reading". **The XBR55ENT and
XBU55ENT print the bar graph reason as well**: "Entering your Age ensures that the Heart Rate bar
graph shows the correct number."

**The XBR25 2023 keeps this page but has no heart rate program for the age to feed** - see
`spirit-bike-programs-a-bike-manual-with-a-pulse-readout-but-no-heart-rate-program`.
