Both manuals open the chapter with the same sentence: **"The fitness Stepper has five different
programs that have been designed for a variety of workouts. These five programs have factory preset
work level profiles for achieving different goals."**

**The two books then describe different sets.**

- **CS800 describes six** - Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, Interval and **HIIT** - and prints a
  full ten-step HIIT setup procedure. Six descriptions against a claim of five.
- **CRS800S describes five** - Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength and Interval. **It has no HIIT
  description and no HIIT procedure.** The word HIIT appears on that machine only as a caption on
  the strip of profile pictures printed down the outside margin. A loose search of the whole PDF for
  "HIIT" and "high intensity" returns nothing but those captions, and a 300 dpi render of the page
  where the CS800 prints HIIT shows only the Interval description.

Both strips of profile pictures read Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Interval, Strength, HIIT - **six
pictures against a claim of five programs** on both machines.

These five are the *preset* programs. Neither machine's list is the whole program list: both also
carry **Manual**, a **Custom** program and **two Heart Rate programs**, reached with the same
Program button.
