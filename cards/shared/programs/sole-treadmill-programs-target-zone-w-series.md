**Maximum Heart Rate is "220 - User's Age"**, computed automatically when age is entered (also feeds the bar graph on F83/F85 models). Cardiovascular fitness is trained at **80%** of maximum, weight loss at **60%** — on a schedule approved by a physician; consult one before starting. An MHR-by-age chart with two diagonal lines is printed (image only).

The monitor works without the control program in any program; **the Heart Rate Control program automatically controls incline**. The H-1/HR-1/H-2/HR-2 target caution applies: suggestion only for normal healthy individuals; a higher entered age lowers the target.

**Contradiction ruled.** The WS73/WS77 and WTT8 books' Selecting paragraphs maintain **70% (Weight Control) and 90% (Cardiovascular)** for the actual HR-1/HR-2 programs. This card follows the Selecting paragraphs for the programs (see `sole-treadmill-programs-hrc-70-90-autopilot` and `sole-treadmill-programs-hrc-70-90-incline-only`); the 80%/60% here is the books' general-zone artwork. The worked 108-144 example generation is on `f63-2026-target-heart-rate-zone`.
