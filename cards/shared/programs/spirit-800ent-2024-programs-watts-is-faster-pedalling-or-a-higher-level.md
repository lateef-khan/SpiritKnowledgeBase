A watts program is a **controllable constant power whose level adjusts when the speed is changed**.
Choose either **faster pedaling at a lower resistance level** or **slower pedaling at a higher
resistance level**.

The setup screen offers two values: **Time** (illustrated at 20) and **Constant Power** (illustrated
at 100).

**The manual prints no step list for this program**, and no minimum or maximum watt figure. The LED
consoles in the same range print nine numbered steps and ask for age and weight as well - see
`spirit-ce-programs-constant-power-watt` and `spirit-bike-programs-constant-watt-program`.

The tile is labelled **Watts**; the same program is called **Constant Power** on the 2021-2023
touchscreens and on every LED console.
