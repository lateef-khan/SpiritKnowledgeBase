*"The Conconi test is for measuring subject's anaerobic threshold heart rates and doesn't require
taking blood samples. Heart rate sensor is required and the test should be supervised by the
therapist."*

Setup is two values: **the resistance increment** and **the period for every stage**. A **test status**
panel shows while it runs and **a button ends the test**.

**That is the whole of what the page prints.** No warm-up, no starting resistance, no end condition
and no reported result. The 8.0T treadmill's Conconi page gives a ten minute self-adjusted warm-up
and ends on STOP (`80t-2026-programs-conconi-test`) - a treadmill on speed increments, not a bike on
resistance increments; none of its figures may be carried here.

*A page defect, recorded as printed: the screenshot beside the Conconi text is a **Steep Ramp Test**
screen - its title bar reads "Steep Ramp Test", it shows Power Increment and Stage Duration fields
and the prompt "Endure the longest time possible at pedaling speed over 60 rpm". The captions under
it ("Set the resistance increment and period for every stages", "Click this button to end the test")
belong to the Conconi Test. The 8.5UE book has the same defect.*

The 8.0U and 8.5R 2025 books print this page word for word.
