**One manual gives two answers, 18 pages apart, and neither page mentions the other.** Give the
customer both.

| | Setup page (printed 19 / 20) | Care page (printed 37 / 39) |
|---|---|---|
| How many levellers | **6** — 4 corner and 2 centre | **2** levelling pads |
| Where | leveling glides on the bottom of the machine | on the bottom of the **rear stabiliser** |
| Wrench | **½"** | **14 mm** |

A ½" spanner is 12.7 mm. The two sizes are not interchangeable on the same nut, so at most one of
them is right for the machine in front of you.

## The setup page, in full

> Once the 7.0S is assembled, and placed on a flat level floor, it may be necessary to adjust the
> leveling glides on the bottom of the 7.0S to ensure proper stability. Use a **1/2" wrench** to
> loosen the top nut of the levelers. Make sure the **two center levelers are screwed all the way
> in**. Adjust the **4 corner levelers by hand** as necessary to remove any wobble. **Unscrew the 2
> center levelers until they touch the floor.** Then tighten all the top nuts against the bottom of
> the stabilizer tubes. Make sure the bottom nut remains cinched against the levelling foot.

This is the more detailed of the two and the only one that gives an order of operations, so it is
the one to work from when a machine rocks. The care page's two rear-stabiliser pads are the standard
Spirit sentence, printed word for word on the CS800 2021, the CVC800 and both XS895 printings
(`ct800-2016-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`) — machines whose feet are arranged differently.

## Which to quote

- **Start with six levellers and a ½" wrench**, because that page describes this machine's own
  layout, names the centre pair, and tells you what order to do them in.
- **Carry a 14 mm as well.** The care page is not a typo the manual anywhere retracts, and a
  customer reading only that page will have brought one.
- **Do not carry either figure to another machine.** The 8.5S and 8.5S-FIT give 2 pads under *each*
  of the front and rear stabilisers with a 14 mm
  (`spirit-85s-maintenance-pedals-and-front-and-rear-stabiliser-pads`); the CRS800S 2021 gives 2 at
  the front and 2 at the back with no wrench size at all
  (`crs800s-2021-maintenance-four-pads-front-and-back`).

The 8.5S books carry a milder version of the same disagreement — ½" on their setup page, 14 mm on
their care page — but no clash of counts, because their setup page gives none.
