**This is the recumbent check: all of the mast's mounting screws, done as needed, behind covers.**
The upright CU900, CU900ENT and CU1000ENT check names six riser screws and excludes a shaking
vertical tube (`cu900ent-check-console-mast`); this one names no count and no exclusion.

As printed — 1.2 Check for Console Mast:

- Check that **all mounting screws are tight** (figures 3.2.1 and 3.2.2).
- **Perform as needed when handlebars or console seem loose.**
- Refer to **6-5-1 and 6-2** for the cover removal procedures to access the screws.

Section 6-5-1 is the mast-cover removal — three self-tapping screws on the left-hand mast cover,
then both covers come off — and 6-2 is the front handlebar replacement, which reaches the M8
socket screws once the rear console cover is off. Both are assembly cards.

The same book's chapter 7 gives the same answer for front handlebars that shake during pedalling:
check the bolts securing the handlebar to the mast and the mast bolts at the main frame
(`spirit-cr900-maintenance-shaking-handlebars-seat-wheels-levellers`).

The **CR1000ENT 2023** prints the check as 11.12, word for word, and sends the reader to 12.25 and 12.12 for
the covers - its numbering of the mast-cover removal and the front handlebar replacement.
