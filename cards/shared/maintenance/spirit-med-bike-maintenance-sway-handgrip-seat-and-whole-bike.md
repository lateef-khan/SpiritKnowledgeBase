**The service manuals split sway by what is swaying, and the 8.5R has no seat row because its seat does not
slide on a quick-release.**

| What sways | 8.0U (as printed) | 8.5R (as printed) |
|---|---|---|
| **Handgrip** shaking during operation | 7.3.1: "refer to 6.4 to tighten screws" - the handgrip stabilizer assembly's eight M6 screws (`80u-2025-maintenance-preventive-check-console-mast-handgrip-stabilizer-rear-stabilizer-and-seat-slide`) | 7.3.1: "refer to 3.2 to tighten screws" - the three M8 socket head cap bolts on the console mast (`85r-2025-maintenance-preventive-check-console-mast-bolts-and-rotating-structure-o-ring`) |
| **Seat** wobbling during operation | 7.3.2: "refer to 3.5 to tighten up" - press the handgrip fixing plate up, check the seat inner slide holds, pull the quick-release lever up and tighten it at the proper position | - (no row) |
| **Whole bike** wobbling | 7.3.3: "refer to Figure 6.16.2 to adjust the height of Adjust Pads" | 7.3.2: "refer to Figure 6.9.2 to adjust the height of Adjust Pads" |

The pad instruction, in both books' replacement step: **turn the adjustment foot counterclockwise to remove it**,
and "adjust the height of adjustment pads when the unit is not horizontal until balance". No wrench size is
printed in the service manuals; the owner's manuals give **two pads under each of the front and rear
stabilizers**, a **14 mm wrench** on the top nut, and a bottom nut kept cinched against the foot
(`spirit-85s-maintenance-pedals-and-front-and-rear-stabiliser-pads`) - and, in their setup chapters, a **½"**
wrench for the same nut.

**Not the CU900's three rows and not the CR900's.** The CU900 sends a shaking seat to one seat connection
screw and the whole bike to four feet (`cu900ent-shaking`); the CR900 recumbent takes seat play out of its
track wheels (`spirit-cr900-maintenance-shaking-handlebars-seat-wheels-levellers`); the 7.0U's console mast is a
knob and a quick-release with a stated tolerance
(`70u-2025-maintenance-sway-seat-screws-and-console-mast-knob-and-quick-release`).
