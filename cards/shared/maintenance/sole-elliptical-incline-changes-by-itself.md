The machine thinks a button was pressed: a shorted button wire, worn buttons sticking, or a bad incline/brake controller.

If the machine was recently assembled, disassembled or moved, replace the **Computer Cable**, the **Upper Incline Wire** and the **Lower Resistance/Incline Wire** first.

1. Ask whether the incline on the console changes at the same moment it physically changes.
   - **Yes** - disconnect the Incline handle switch from the console and try again.
     - Machine now works - replace the **Upper Incline Wire**, the **Lower Resistance/Incline Wire**, the **Handpulse Thin Film Label** and the **Incline Label**.
     - Machine still misbehaves - replace the **Console Assembly**.
   - **No** - replace the **Incline/Brake Controller**.
