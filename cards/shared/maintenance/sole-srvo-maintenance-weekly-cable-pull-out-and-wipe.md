**Once every week, pull the cable all the way out and wipe the cable down.** Make sure the machine is **unplugged** while doing so. After the cable is dried, turn the machine on and let the cable retract.

This is the weekly interval in the SRVO book's GENERAL MAINTENANCE list. The same list's after-each-workout wipe (sweat path, damp cloth) sits on the shared interval card, and its unplug/store/examine lines on the companion card (both linked). Cable faults - stuck, not retracting, no resistance - are diagnosis, not care, and belong to the errors section.
