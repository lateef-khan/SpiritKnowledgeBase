**This is the five-item list — Belt, Shaft, Bearing, Idler, Pedal.** The CR900ENT 2021, CU900ENT
and CU1000ENT 2023 service manuals print a different four — Belt, PU wheel, Bearing, Idler — under
the same heading (`cu900ent-wear-parts-inspection`), and Spirit's sanitizing guidance prints all
six (`ct900-sanitizing-equipment`). Quote the list from the book for the machine.

**WARNING** — as printed: 'The effect that the safety level of the equipment can be maintained
only if it is examined regularly for damage and wear.'

1. **Replace defective components immediately** and/or keep the equipment out of use until repair.
2. The components which are most susceptible to wear: **Belt, Shaft, Bearing, Idler, Pedal.**

No inspection interval and no wear limit is given for any of the five. The pedals do get a
tightness check in the same books' preventative-maintenance chapter — every week on the CR900
(`spirit-cr900-maintenance-weekly-pedal-check`), 'regularly' on the CU900
(`cu900ent-check-pedals`) — which is a looseness check, not a wear limit.

The manual prints the list with ideographic commas (Belt、Shaft、Bearing、Idler、Pedal。); they are
transcribed as ordinary commas above. The CR900 2018 and CU900 2018 owner's manuals print no wear
list.

## The 2025 medical 4.0R and 4.0U service manuals print the same five

**Belt, Shaft, Bearing, Idler, Pedal**, in that order, under the same WARNING and the same general-maintenance
block, in both the FR800-SB022-03 (4.0R) and FU800-SB022-03 (4.0U) books. Their owner's manuals print no
wear list at all. **The 2025 7.0R and 7.0U owner's manuals print the six-item list** - Belt, PU wheel, Bearing,
Idler, Shaft, Pedal - beside their sanitizing bullets (`ct900-sanitizing-equipment`); their service manuals print
none. Quote the list from the book for the machine.
