This is the 2023-generation schedule. It differs from the older 90-hour card in two load-bearing ways: a **90-day** time trigger alongside the hours trigger, and a named **2-3 ounce** dose. The card follows these books.

**How often**: pre-lubricated from the factory; then **every 90 hours of use, or every 90 days, regardless of usage as it can dry out**, or whenever the deck is dry. Reach between the belt and deck **every other month**; if no lubricant is present, lubricate even though the "Lube" indicator is not lit. Otherwise lubricate when the console reminder lights after 90 hours.

**What to use**: only Sole-approved lubricant - **100% food-grade silicone lubricant for treadmills. DO NOT USE SILICONE CAULKING FOR BATHROOM AND KITCHEN PROJECTS OR A LUBRICANT THAT HAS A SPRAY/FOAM BASE.** The treadmill comes with one tube of "Lube"; extra tubes and a Lube-n-Walk kit can be ordered from Sole or an authorised dealer. **Use 2-3 ounces of lubrication each application.** **Failure to lubricate at regular intervals may void the warranty**: dry running stresses the drive motor, drive belt and motor control board to catastrophic failure.

First clean between the belt and deck (non-fraying rag, t-shirt or light towel, halfway between the end and the motor cover, drag the length 1-2 times, remove).

1. Power off and unplug.
2. Measure **18 inches** from the motor cover; reach under the belt **4-6 inches** from one edge. Squirt about **1/8 inch wide by 15 inches long in an "S" pattern**, perpendicular to the motor cover.
3. Repeat on the opposite side.
4. Plug back in and power on.
5. Walk at a moderate speed for **five minutes** to spread the silicone.
6. Reset a showing "Lube" message through the engineering menu: hold **Start, Stop and Enter**, insert the safety key, keep holding until Engineering Mode Menu shows, Enter to access, Speed buttons to **Functions**, Enter, step to **Maintenance**, Enter to reset, Stop to exit.
