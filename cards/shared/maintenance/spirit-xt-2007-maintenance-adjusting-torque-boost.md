**Adjust at lower speeds to eliminate motor surging.** That is the controller diagram's own caption for the torque boost dial. The Troubleshooting Guide names it as the first thing to try when the belt surges or speeds up and slows down frequently, before the speed sensor and before replacing the controller.

1. Using a small screwdriver, turn the dial **counter clockwise until it stops**.
2. Press the START button on the console.
3. **With someone standing on the walking belt**, turn **clockwise until the belt begins to move**.
4. Increase the speed and check for smooth belt movement.

## Where the dial is

On the XT175-675 controller diagram the torque boost adjustment is item 8, next to the POWER and LIMIT indicators and the speed-sensor connection. The 2007 book prints the diagram; the CT800 2016 service manual copies the four steps word for word but its own driver-board drawings (sections 6.3 and 6.4) label MOT_DRV, SHUT_DOWN, LIMI, RPM SENSOR, PWM and POWER and do not label a torque pot. On a CT800 2016 look for the small dial on the DC driver board; the book gives no other location.

## When to stop

The order the guide gives is: torque boost, then speed sensor (`spirit-xt-2007-maintenance-speed-sensor-one-sixteenth-inch-from-roller` on the 2007 machines, `spirit-ct800-maintenance-speed-sensor-alignment` on the CT800 2016), then replace the controller. A belt that still surges after the dial is set is not a torque-boost fault.
