As part of the routine maintenance schedule, **belt lubrication and cleaning should be performed every 80 hours of use, or earlier if you notice that the deck is dry or dirty.** Also check belt lubrication **before first use**: with the machine off and unplugged, reach between the running belt and the top of the base to verify that lubricant is present.

**The TRX1000 book contradicts itself**: its preventative maintenance chart says **every 40 hours or sooner if dry** while this page says 80. Both are carded; the chart side is `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-lubrication-interval-every-40-hours`. The TRX2500, TRX3500, TRX4500 and TRX5500 books print no chart, so 80 hours is their only figure - in the owner's manual. **The TRX2500, TRX3500 and TRX4500 service manuals give a different schedule again**: half a bottle after the first 25 hours, then every 50 hours (`xterra-trx-maintenance-service-manual-lubrication-schedule-25-then-50-hours`).

Keeping the deck lubricated at the recommended intervals ensures the longest life possible; if the lubricant dries out, friction between belt and deck rises and stresses the drive motor, drive belt and electronic motor control board, which could result in failure of those components. **Failure to clean and lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty.**

The procedure is `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-deck-lubrication-half-bottle-each-side-1-foot-from-the-motor-cover`. The console's LUBE reminder and how to clear it belong to the console cards.
