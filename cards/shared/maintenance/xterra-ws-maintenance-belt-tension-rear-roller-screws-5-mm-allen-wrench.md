If the running belt slips while walking on it, adjust the tension of the running belt by turning the screws on the rear rollers of both sides with the **5 mm Allen wrench**, which is included in the hardware kit. (The pack list holds both a 5 mm and a 6 mm Allen key; the belt page names the 5 mm.)

**During adjustment, while turning the rear rollers' screws, be sure to keep running belt tension balanced on both sides, otherwise the belt shifts from the higher-tension end to the other.**

**DO NOT OVERTIGHTEN** - over tightening will cause belt damage and premature bearing failure. If you tighten the belt a lot and it still slips, the problem could actually be the drive belt under the motor cover that connects the motor to the front roller; tightening the motor belt should be done by a trained service person.

No tracking procedure is printed for these machines (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-no-belt-tracking-procedure-printed`). The troubleshooting table's belt rows are `xterra-ws-maintenance-belt-not-smooth-or-slipping-lubricant-and-tension`. The TR65/TR75/TR85 print the same paragraph with an M6 L wrench (103): `xterra-tr-maintenance-belt-tension-rear-roller-screws-m6-l-wrench-103`.
