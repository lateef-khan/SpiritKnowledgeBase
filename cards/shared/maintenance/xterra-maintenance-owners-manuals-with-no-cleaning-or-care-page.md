Seven of the wave's owner's manuals have **no maintenance, cleaning or care section**: no "General
Maintenance", no "Storage and Maintenance", no "Maintenance & Care", no cleaning agent named anywhere. Their
tables of contents run Registration - Safety - Assembly - Console - Programs - Warranty. Proved by the
contents page, by loose-word grep of every page including the OCR supplements, and by reading the pages
around the console and warranty chapters.

What each book does say that touches on care:

| Book | The only care-related lines |
|---|---|
| AIR350 OM 2019 | "Final Preparations" after assembly: adjust all four feet so the unit sits flat; tighten both pedals to the crank arms; tighten the seat and handlebar posts. Grease from the kit goes on the large screw and pedal bolts **at assembly**. No cleaning, no fan, belt or chain care, no transport instruction. |
| AIR650 OM 2021 | Same "Final Preparations" (four levelers). Its **service** care - chain oil and chain slack - comes from the Spirit AB900 service manual via the manufacturer's note: `air650-2021-maintenance-chain-lubrication-general-chain-oil-by-way-of-the-ab900-service-manual`. |
| EU150 OM 2024 | A "User Direction" page: how to move it and how to level it (`xterra-eu-maintenance-moving-pull-back-on-the-stationary-handlebar-with-the-top-handlebars-matched`, `xterra-maintenance-wobble-turn-the-leveling-caps-on-the-rear-stabilizer`). **No** cleaning paragraph and **no** weekly checks - the EU100 book prints both, the EU150 dropped them. |
| FS1.5 OM 2019, FS2.5 OM 2020 | Nothing. |
| FS3.5 OM 2020 | Only the assembly step that sets the stabilizers so the machine "is level and stable". |
| SB2.5r OM 2020 | Only the pedal step: tighten the pedals firmly or a clicking noise can occur; retighten after use if clicking occurs (`xterra-sb-maintenance-thump-clicking-or-wobble-from-loose-pedals-or-crank-arms`). |

Do not quote another Xterra book's cleaning agent, interval or lubricant to an owner of one of these
machines; the manual they hold says none of it.
