Seven of the bike and elliptical wave's owner's manuals have **no maintenance, cleaning or care section**: no "General
Maintenance", no "Storage and Maintenance", no "Maintenance & Care", no cleaning agent named anywhere. Their
tables of contents run Registration - Safety - Assembly - Console - Programs - Warranty. Proved by the
contents page, by loose-word grep of every page including the OCR supplements, and by reading the pages
around the console and warranty chapters.

What each book does say that touches on care:

| Book | The only care-related lines |
|---|---|
| AIR350 OM 2019 | "Final Preparations" after assembly: adjust all four feet so the unit sits flat; tighten both pedals to the crank arms; tighten the seat and handlebar posts. Grease from the kit goes on the large screw and pedal bolts **at assembly**. No cleaning, no fan, belt or chain care, no transport instruction. |
| AIR650 OM 2021 | Same "Final Preparations" (four levelers). Its **service** care - chain oil and chain slack - comes from the Spirit AB900 service manual via the manufacturer's note: `air650-2021-maintenance-chain-lubrication-general-chain-oil-by-way-of-the-ab900-service-manual`. |
| EU150 OM 2024 | A "User Direction" page: how to move it and how to level it (`xterra-eu-maintenance-moving-pull-back-on-the-stationary-handlebar-with-the-top-handlebars-matched`, `xterra-maintenance-wobble-turn-the-leveling-caps-on-the-rear-stabilizer`). **No** cleaning paragraph and **no** weekly checks - the EU100 book prints both, the EU150 dropped them. |
| FS1.5 OM 2019, FS2.5 OM 2020 | Nothing. |
| FS3.5 OM 2020 | Only the assembly step that sets the stabilizers so the machine "is level and stable". |
| SB2.5r OM 2020 | Only the pedal step: tighten the pedals firmly or a clicking noise can occur; retighten after use if clicking occurs (`xterra-sb-maintenance-thump-clicking-or-wobble-from-loose-pedals-or-crank-arms`). |

Do not quote another Xterra book's cleaning agent, interval or lubricant to an owner of one of these
machines; the manual they hold says none of it.

## The rower and strength books (wave X3, 2026-09-11)

Twelve more owner's manuals print **no cleaning agent, no cleaning interval and no sanitizing page**. Checked
the same way: contents page, loose-word grep of every page and OCR supplement (clean, wipe, cloth, soap,
detergent, alcohol, sanit, lubric, oil, grease, maint, care), and the low-word pages rendered and read. What
each one does carry that touches on care:

| Book | The only care-related lines |
|---|---|
| ERG160 OM 2020 | Moving, leveling and vertical storage on the Adjustment Guides pages (`xterra-rower-maintenance-moving-lift-the-rear-until-the-front-transport-wheels-touch-the-floor`, `xterra-erg-maintenance-storing-upright-raise-the-slide-rail-to-vertical-and-the-seat-slides-down`). No cleaning line. |
| ERG220 OM 2023 | Moving, leveling and the ball-pin fold (`xterra-erg-maintenance-folding-on-the-ball-head-pin-pull-it-raise-the-rail-and-reinsert-it`); the squealing row of its troubleshooting table. No cleaning line. |
| ERG400 OM 2015 (scan) | **Nothing.** Fourteen pages: registration, power, safety, five assembly steps, console, programs, warranty. No moving, storage, folding or cleaning sentence; the locking pin appears only as an assembly step. |
| ERG500 OM 2018 | One folding sentence under assembly step 8 (`xterra-erg-maintenance-folding-on-the-ball-head-pin-pull-it-raise-the-rail-and-reinsert-it`); the squealing row. No moving and no cleaning line. |
| ERG550W OM 2023, ERG600W OM 2021, ERG650W OM 2021 | The tank page - tap water, no chlorine, tablets, the 1-6 gauge - and "wipe excess water off of the frame" after filling; moving and vertical storage; empty the tank before a month's storage (`xterra-ergw-maintenance-tap-water-only-never-pool-chlorine-or-bleach`, `xterra-ergw-maintenance-changing-the-water-siphon-pump-out-then-funnel-in`, `xterra-ergw-maintenance-empty-the-tank-before-storing-for-more-than-a-month`). No cleaner for the frame, rail or seat. |
| ERG750W OM 2025 | The tank page - distilled water, tablets every six months - and "dry the water on and around the table and adjustment knob with a dry cloth"; moving and storage (`erg750w-2025-maintenance-distilled-or-pure-water-only-and-no-inferior-tablets-or-bleach`). Its "Storage and Maintenance" heading covers moving and standing the rail up and nothing else. |
| ADB12.5PR, ADB25, ADB55 OM 2022 (adjustable dumbbells) | Safety items 12 and 13 only: "Inspect the equipment thoroughly before each use", and "If the equipment is found to be worn, loose, or rusted, it should be repaired or replaced in a timely manner". No wiping, dial cleaning or storage instruction; the troubleshooting page is the dial not turning. |
| DBSTAND OM 2022 (dumbbell stand) | The same two safety items, and the assembly step that levels the footpads before the dumbbells go on. No cleaning or storage line. |

The three rower books that do print care are the **ERG180** and **ERG800W** (the new-layout post-workout and
75% isopropyl page: `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-post-workout-care`,
`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-sanitizing-with-75-percent-isopropyl-alcohol`) and the **ERG700** (the sweat-path
wipe and the noise list: `xterra-maintenance-wipe-the-sweat-path-with-a-damp-cloth-after-each-workout`). No
rower or strength book names a lubricant
(`xterra-rower-maintenance-no-lubricant-chain-oil-or-rail-lubricant-named-in-any-book`). Spirit's strength
range is in the same position (`spirit-strength-maintenance-no-cleaning-agent-and-no-storage-section`).
