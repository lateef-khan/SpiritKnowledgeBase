The hybrid bikes are tipped back by the fixed handlebar, not lifted by the rear stabilizer:

> To move the Hybrid Bike, **grasp the stationary handlebar and pull back on the handle** until the Hybrid
> Bike can be moved on the front wheels. Carefully move the Hybrid Bike to the desired location and then
> lower it. **Before moving the machine, please make sure that the top handlebars are on same position.**

The last sentence is the one specific to these machines: the two moving handlebars are to be set level
with each other before you tip the bike. The EU100 and EU150 pages are word for word
the same apart from the model name.

Every other Xterra bike and elliptical of this generation is moved by lifting the rear stabilizer -
`xterra-maintenance-moving-lift-the-rear-and-roll-on-the-front-transport-wheels`.
