The treadmill is designed so the tread-belt stays reasonably centred while in use. **Some belts drifting near one side while in use is normal**, depending on the user's gait and whether they favour one leg. Adjust only if the belt **keeps moving toward one side** during use.

The supplied wrench is a **6 mm Allen wrench**; the parts-list number for it varies by book: **132** in the 2011-batch and 2013-batch books and the AS77, **99** in the F60-2012-2014 book, **105** in the ATT8, TT8-2013 and TT8C books, **149** (an M6 L wrench) in the TT9 book, and unnumbered in the F60-2016 and W-family books.

1. **Make tracking adjustments on the left side bolt.**
2. Set belt speed at **3 mph**.
3. If the belt is too close to the left side, turn the bolt only **a 1/4 turn to the right (clockwise)** and wait a few minutes for the belt to adjust itself. A small adjustment can make a dramatic difference that is not apparent right away.
4. Keep making 1/4 turns until the belt stabilises in the centre of the running deck.
5. If the belt is too close to the right side, turn the bolt **counter-clockwise**.

The belt may need periodic tracking adjustment depending on use and walking/running characteristics; expect to adjust as required. Adjustments become less of a maintenance concern as the belt is used. **Proper belt tracking is an owner responsibility common with all treadmills.**

**ATTENTION: damage to the running belt resulting from improper tracking/tension adjustments is not covered under the Sole warranty.**

The service manuals for these generations print a different procedure (10 mm wrench, kph speeds); see `f65-2016-belt-tracking-adjustment`. This card follows the owner's manuals.
