**Turn the machine off and unplug it before you start.**

1. Loosen the 5 screws in the motor top cover. They do not have to come all the way out. Three run along the front and one is on each side. You may need to fold the deck up to reach the side screws.
2. Remove the motor top cover.
3. **Take a picture of the old motor controller board.** It helps with every step after this.
4. Disconnect all wires from the old controller. Some may have hot glue on them. Peel it away, or use a cotton bud dipped in rubbing alcohol along the edges. You do not have to re-glue the new board.
5. Remove the 2 screws holding the controller to the frame. They can be very tight, so a power drill helps.
6. Remove the old controller and fit the new one with the 2 screws.
7. **Drive motor wires**, bottom left corner: red to **M+**, black to **M-**. On some models the second wire is white instead of black.
8. **Incline motor wires**, top right: red to **UP**, white to **COM**, black to **DOWN**.
9. **Incline motor potentiometer wire** to the 3 pin connector. Depending on the model this is on the left middle of the board, or below the main computer cable connector.
10. **Main computer cable** to the biggest pin connector. The pin count varies by model. It is usually at the top of the board, towards the left.
11. **Speed sensor** to the 2 pin connector in the top left corner.
12. **Older machines only**: if there is a separate transformer beside the controller with red, blue and black wires, connect it to the pin connector near the bottom centre.
13. **On/off switch or filter wires**, bottom right corner: black to **ACN**, white to **ACL**. The labels vary a little by model.
14. Refit the motor top cover and retighten the 5 screws.
15. Plug the machine in and turn it on.
16. **Run a calibration.** A new controller always needs one. The steps differ for a button console and a touchscreen.
