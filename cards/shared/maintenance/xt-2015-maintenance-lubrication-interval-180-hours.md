**This is the 180-hour interval, printed only in the XT185 2015 and XT285 2015 manuals. The XT385 2015 and XT485 2015 manuals say 90 hours.**

- **The belt and deck come pre-lubricated**, so a new machine needs nothing applied.
- **Subsequent lubrication should be performed every 180 hours of use, or if you notice that the deck is dry.**
- **It is recommended that you reach between the belt and deck to verify there is lubrication present, every other month.**
- **If you check and there is no lubrication present, lubricate even though the "Lube" indicator is not lit on the console.**
- **Otherwise, lubricate when the console's lubrication reminder lights after 180 hours of use.**

So there are two triggers: the **hour counter** in the console, and your own **two-monthly hand check**, which overrules the counter when the deck is dry.

**Resetting the "Lube" message is a console job**, not part of the lubrication itself. The manual prints the key sequence as a note under the lubrication steps and again in the Engineering Mode Menu, where item 3-III is "Maintenance (Reset lube message and odometer readings)".

**Nothing else in these two manuals states a lubrication interval.** Unlike the 2023 manuals, there is no illustration caption giving a second, shorter figure, so there is no contradiction to reconcile here; compare `xt-2023-maintenance-lubrication-illustration-note`.

**The XT685 2015 is not on this card** and takes no lubricant at all; see `xt685-2023-belt-deck-general-maintenance`.
