The ST90's whole printed maintenance chapter is one paragraph (spelling as printed). Dirt, dust and hair block air inlets and accumulate on the slat belt. **Vacuum the treadmill monthly** against build-up that gets sucked up; **wipe the belt weekly** with a soft damp cloth or paper towel. **UNPLUG THE POWER CORD BEFORE THIS TASK.**

What the books do **not** print: no belt/deck lubrication (there is no lube schedule, no lubricant, no Lube message), no belt-tension procedure, no tracking procedure, no folding or moving procedure, and no maintenance chart. Do not card any of those for these machines from a sibling book.

Stale-pointer note: the service checklists still say `See General Maintenance section on Tread-belt Tension', but the General Maintenance chapter has no tension section. The printed chapter above governs; the pointer is boilerplate.
