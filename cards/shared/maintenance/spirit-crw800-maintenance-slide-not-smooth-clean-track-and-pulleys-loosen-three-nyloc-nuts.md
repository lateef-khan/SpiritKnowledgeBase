**Three steps, in the book's order, "until the slide works".** The same three lines with the same part keys are
printed in all three books - the CRW800 (2020/2021) service manual, the CW800-YR001 book that is the CRW800 2016,
and the DW400-YR002 book that is the XRW600.

1. **Clean the Aluminum Board (52) and the Aluminum Track Pulley (53).**
2. **Loosen the three 3/8" × 11T Nyloc Nuts (106).**
3. **Check and replace the Aluminum Track Pulley (53) or the Pulley (54).**

The numbers in brackets are the exploded-view keys. The nyloc nuts (106) are the ones on the bolts that carry the
Pulley (54) in the aluminium track, seat and rear stabilizer replacement procedure (11-8 in the 2021 book, 9-8 in
the other two - assembly cards, and where the pulleys come off for step 3). The book says only "loose", not by how
much, and never says to retighten them: back them off a little, test the slide, and only then decide on step 3.

**No cleaner and no lubricant is named** for the board or the pulleys; the instruction is *clean*. A slide that is
noisy rather than stiff gets the same cleaning as item 4 of the noise list, and both pulleys replaced if cleaning
does not cure it (`spirit-crw800-maintenance-noise-screw-kits-foot-pads-seat-knob-thread-slide-fan-flywheel`).

## Not the water rowers

The CRW900 and CRW800H2O seat rollers are looked after differently - a soft dry cloth weekly and a debris check
before every use, with no nuts to loosen (`crw900-2021-maintenance-seat-roller-and-track-cleaning`). Do not read
the nyloc-nut step onto them.

## The same text under the Sole brand

Sole's SR500 2016 and SR550 2023 rower service manuals print these three steps word for word with the same part
keys (`sr500-2016-slide-not-smooth`). Same Dyaco text, different brand; this card is the Spirit one.
