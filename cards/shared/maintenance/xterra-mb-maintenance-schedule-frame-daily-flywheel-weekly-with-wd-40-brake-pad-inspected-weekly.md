**The printed brake-pad row says N/A for cleaner and N/A for lubricant.** The Spirit XIC600 table oils its
leather pad with 3-IN-ONE or 10W (`spirit-indoor-cycle-maintenance-schedule-wd40-and-weekly-brake-pad`),
the Sole table uses silicone spray (`sole-spinner-maintenance-schedule`), and the Spirit CIC table wipes
the flywheel with water and checks the pad monthly
(`spirit-indoor-cycle-maintenance-schedule-water-and-monthly-brake-pad`). None of those figures belongs to
an Xterra MB.

| Part | Recommended action | Frequency | Cleaner | Lubricant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pedals | Ensure that pedals are tight in crank arms; that all screws on pedals are tight; and that the pedal straps are not frayed | Before each use | N/A | N/A |
| Frame | Wipe down by using a soft damp clean cloth | Daily | Water | N/A |
| Flywheel | Wipe down by spraying on a rag and applying a light coat to sides of the flywheel | Weekly | WD-40 spray | N/A |
| Brake Pad | Inspect for excessive wear or squealing | Weekly | N/A | N/A |

Footnote 1 under the table: "Use of lubricants or cleaning solutions other than those so specified will
result in diminished performance and a shorter life span for that part." So the only products the books
allow are **water** on the frame and **WD-40 sprayed onto a rag** for the flywheel sides. A squealing pad
is a wear symptom to inspect, not something to oil. None of the three books says what the pad is made of;
all three print the same four rows, and each stops the flywheel by pressing down on the tension knob or
lever.

## Read the page, not the extraction

The MB550 and MBX2500 PDFs carry **ghost text** on this table: the text layer holds "a dry or leather
brake pad" and "3-IN-ONE Oil or 10W oil. Do not use silicone-based lubricants" overlaid on the brake-pad
row - the Spirit XIC600 wording, left in the OEM file and **not printed on the page**. Both pages were
rendered at 150 dpi to confirm the row reads "Inspect for excessive wear or squealing / Weekly / N/A / N/A".
The MB500 is a scan, so its text is the render already, and it prints the same row.

## The console

The MB500 and MB550 console page adds: "To clean the XTERRA Fitness Indoor Cycling Console use a clean
damp cloth. Use of any caustic cleaning solutions will void the warranty." The console is a sealed unit,
water resistant but not waterproof, and opening it for anything but batteries voids the warranty - the
warranty terms belong to the warranty cards. The MBX2500's wired CR2032 console prints no such note.
