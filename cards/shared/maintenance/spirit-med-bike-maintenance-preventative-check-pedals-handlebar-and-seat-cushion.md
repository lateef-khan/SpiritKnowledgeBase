**No interval is printed for any of these.** The CU900 books say "regularly" and the CR900 books "every week"
for the same pedal check; these two say only to check.

| | 7.0R (MR490-SB018-03) | 7.0U (MU470-SB018-02) |
|---|---|---|
| Pedals | 3.1: check whether the pedal is loosened. **If the right pedal is loosened, lock it clockwise. If the left pedal is loosened, lock it counterclockwise** (figures 3.1.1, 3.1.2) | 3.1: the same two directions - "if the right pedal is loosened clockwise and locked, the left pedal is locked counterclockwise" (figures 3.1.1, 3.1.2) |
| Handlebar | 3.2: check if the **front handle bar** is properly adjusted or loosened; if it is loose, tighten the screw (figure 3.2.1) | 3.2: check the handle is loose; if the screws are loose, **use a screwdriver and wrench** to tighten them, **both sides the same** (figures 3.2.1-3.2.3) |
| Seat | - | 3.3: check whether the **seat cushion is too tight or too loose**; adjust with the **13 mm and 14 mm wrenches** (figures 3.3.1, 3.3.2) |

The 7.0R chapter goes on to two items the upright has no equivalent of or shares: the aluminium seat track
(`70r-2025-maintenance-aluminium-track-cleaned-regularly-to-avoid-pu-wheel-loss`) and, on both books, the
mechanical brake's release lever
(`spirit-med-bike-maintenance-brake-release-lever-loose-from-felt-wear-adjust-the-steel-cable`).

## What a loose one leads to

A pedal that clicks is the first thing both owner's manuals send you to
(`spirit-med-bike-maintenance-clicking-when-pedaling-pedal-knob-and-leveling-feet`); a 7.0U seat that shakes is
this 3.3 adjustment, and a console mast that swings is its knob and quick-release
(`70u-2025-maintenance-sway-seat-screws-and-console-mast-knob-and-quick-release`). Removal of a pedal is the
reverse of the directions above - the 7.0R's 6.2.1 takes the left pedal off counterclockwise and the right off
clockwise - an assembly card.

**No torque and no screw size** is printed for the handlebar or the seat on either book.
