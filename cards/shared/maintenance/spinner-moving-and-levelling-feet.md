**Moving the bike.** Two wheels are located on the **front floor mount** for easily relocating the bike.

- Grip the back floor mount and tilt the bike forward (1).
- While avoiding uneven surfaces, roll the bike to its new location.
- If the bike rocks after being set down, turn each **levelling foot** until it rests firmly on the floor (2).

**Equipment warning.**

- Do **not** unscrew the levelling feet **more than 1/2"**.
- Before moving your bike, adjust your handlebar and saddle settings to their lowest levels.

The manuals give no wrench size for the levelling feet and no count of how many there are.
