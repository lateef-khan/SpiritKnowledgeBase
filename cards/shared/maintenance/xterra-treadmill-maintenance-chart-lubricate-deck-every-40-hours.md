The chart is a blank log printed under the table of contents for the owner to fill in. Its four task columns and their intervals, as printed:

| Task | Interval |
|---|---|
| Vacuum under motor cover & check wiring | Every other month |
| Clean & inspect deck for lubrication | Every other month |
| Lubricate deck | Every 40 hours or sooner if dry |
| Inspect belt tracking, adjust if necessary | Monthly |

A fifth column headed **Date** records when each task was done. The General Maintenance page says "there is a maintenance schedule/chart included under the Table of Contents for convenient record keeping."

Where it is printed: the TR150 and TR200 books print it twice, on the contents page and again on the page before the back cover; the TRX1000 and TR260 books print it once, on the contents page.

**The TRX1000 book contradicts itself.** Its chart says every 40 hours, but its Belt/Deck Lubrication Procedure says every **80 hours**. Both figures are carded; see `xterra-trx-maintenance-lubrication-interval-every-80-hours`. The TR150, TR200 and TR260 books say 40 hours in both places.

The sister machines whose chart reads **90 hours** are on `xterra-tr-maintenance-chart-lubricate-deck-every-90-hours`. The TRX2500, TRX3500, TRX4500, TRX5500 books and every new-layout book (TRX1400, TR65, TR75, TR85, TR95H, TR75H, WS200, WS300) print no chart at all.
