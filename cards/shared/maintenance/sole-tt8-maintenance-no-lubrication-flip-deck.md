These are proprietary decks, not oil decks: **do not apply any type of lubricant or wax to the surface** (2019/2023 wording). The 2021 book never says it outright but prints no lubrication schedule or procedure anywhere - the only deck remedy it gives is the flip below.

Wipe the belt edge and between belt and frame monthly with a soft damp cloth or paper towel, reaching under the edge; mild soap and a nylon brush on the textured top, dried before use.

If the original side shows significant wear, it needs flipping - contact your service technician. If either of these keeps happening, follow this order:

1. Clean under the belt as described under General Maintenance.
2. Check the belt tension is not too tight; adjust as necessary.
3. Call your service provider to have the deck flipped if it is on the original side.

The two warning scenarios are: the belt **stops very quickly after the safety key is dislodged**, or the **15 amp fuse keeps blowing**.

Contradiction ruled: all three books' service checklists still point friction rows at a `section on lubrication' the books do not print, the 2021 touchscreen console defaults its Lube Setup message to 90 hours, and the 2023 checklist adds `Only lubricate with 100% silicone'. All are stale boilerplate from the folding silicon-deck books. The deck chapters above govern: no owner-applied lubricant on these decks. (The lube-message reset itself lives in the console menus.)
