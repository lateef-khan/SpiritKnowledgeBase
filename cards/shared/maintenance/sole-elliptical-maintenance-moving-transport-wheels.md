The elliptical is equipped with **two transport wheels**, which are **engaged when the rear of the elliptical is lifted**.

That is the whole of every B manual's transport instruction. No book gives a weight, a lifting technique, a two-person warning or a folding mechanism - none of these machines folds. The 2026 generation prints the same sentence (linked).
