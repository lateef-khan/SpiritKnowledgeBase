Two books give the interval, in two wordings, and both say **six months**:

| Book | Sentence, as printed |
|---|---|
| ERG550W OM 2023 | "Add a Water Purification Tablet every 6 months or as needed. Humidity and temperature can play a role. If water remains cloudy, replace the water in the tank." |
| ERG750W OM 2025 | "If you are using purification tablets, It is necessary to replace it every six months. If the water quality is very cloudy, please replace the water in time." |

Same figure, two attitudes: the ERG550W tells you to add a tablet; the ERG750W treats the tablet as optional
("can add") and only fixes the interval **if** you use one. Both say a cloudy tank means new water, not another
tablet. Neither prints a dose, a brand or a part number, and neither says a word about sunlight or algae.

Where to get them - the ERG550W (and the ERG600W and ERG650W, which print this line but **no interval**):
"To extend the life of your rower and prolong use between tank cleanings, please purchase water cleaning
tablets at www.xterrafitness.com, or by calling 1-(870) 333-5500."

What may go in the tank besides the tablet differs by machine and is carded separately - tap water only and
never chlorine on the ERG550W (`xterra-ergw-maintenance-tap-water-only-never-pool-chlorine-or-bleach`),
distilled or pure water and no inferior tablets or bleach on the ERG750W
(`erg750w-2025-maintenance-distilled-or-pure-water-only-and-no-inferior-tablets-or-bleach`). The ERG800W
names no tablet at all and changes its water every half year instead
(`erg800w-2023-maintenance-clean-filtered-water-changed-every-half-year-no-tablet-named`).

Spirit's CRW800H2O prints the ERG550W sentence for its own tank
(`crw800h2o-maintenance-purification-tablet-every-six-months`); the CRW900's interval is a different figure
altogether (`crw900-2021-maintenance-long-term-water-treatment-interval`). Other brand, separate cards.
