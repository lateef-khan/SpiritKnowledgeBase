1. Remove the battery cover from the back of the computer.
2. Insert **2 AAA batteries** into the battery compartment and reinstall the battery cover.

**When the batteries are flat:** the SB700 manuals say that when the battery working voltage is too low, the **"Low battery"** indicator will show up on the console display indicating it is time to change the batteries. The SB900 manuals put the same sentence first: when the **"Low Battery"** indicator shows up on the display it is time to change the batteries.

These consoles take replaceable cells. The SB700 2020 onward and SB900 2020 onward consoles are **rechargeable instead** and have no battery to replace.
