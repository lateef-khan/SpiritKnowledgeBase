The transport wheels are on the **front stabilizer** and touch the floor only when the **rear** of the
machine is lifted. Thirteen books say so, in two wordings.

**The procedure** (folding bikes, SB150, SB250, FS150):

> To move the [folding bike / exercise bike / elliptical], grasp the rear stabilizer and carefully lift it
> until the [machine] can be moved on the front wheels. Carefully move the [machine] to the desired
> location and then lower it.

The FB160, FB360 and FB180 say "rolled on the front wheels"; the FB150, FB350, SB150, SB250 and FS150 say
"moved on the front wheels". Same action.

**The feature sentence** (SB500, SB4.5r, FS5.8e, FS5.9e, RSX1500), printed under Features / Transportation:

> The [fitness bike / elliptical / Seated Stepper] is equipped with transport wheels, which are engaged
> when the rear of the [machine] is lifted.

Those five books give no further steps, and the **SB4500** prints neither sentence - it has no
transportation text anywhere.

Three Xterra families move differently and have their own cards: the EU100 and EU150 hybrid bikes are
tipped back by the stationary handlebar
(`xterra-eu-maintenance-moving-pull-back-on-the-stationary-handlebar-with-the-top-handlebars-matched`);
the SB120 and UB120 are tilted forward by the handlebar
(`xterra-bike-maintenance-moving-tilt-the-handlebar-forward-onto-the-front-stabilizer-wheels`); the
new-layout SB240 and SB600 are lifted from the front
(`xterra-sb-maintenance-moving-stand-at-the-front-and-lift-onto-the-built-in-transport-wheels`).

Spirit and Sole bikes use the same lift-the-rear method (`spirit-xb-2023-assembly-transport-wheels`,
`lcr-2026-moving-the-bike`, `e95-2026-moving-the-elliptical`). Other brands, separate cards.
