**This is the hinged-rail family: the main frame stays on the floor and the slide rail swings up to vertical.
The ERG800W stands the whole machine on end** (`erg800w-2023-maintenance-storing-the-rower-standing-vertically-on-a-level-floor-with-a-thin-cloth`),
**and the ERG220, ERG500 and ERG700 fold on a locking pin or a release lever**
(`xterra-erg-maintenance-folding-on-the-ball-head-pin-pull-it-raise-the-rail-and-reinsert-it`,
`erg700-2022-maintenance-folding-release-lever-at-the-handlebar-and-rear-stabilizer-knob`).

Six books say the rower "can be stored vertically to save space". Each prints its own cautions, and none
prints a latch, pin or strap that holds the rail up:

| Book | How to raise it | Cautions printed |
|---|---|---|
| ERG160 OM 2020 | "Take the Slide Rail (2) and move it into a vertical position." | "The Seat (38) will glide down when the Slide Rail (2) is raised." "Take care to avoid the Rear Stabilizer (4) when moving the Slide Rail (2)." |
| ERG180 OM 2023 | "storing the rower vertically" | "Move with caution when you raise the rower up, as your head may touch the Rear Stabilizer. When you stand the rower up, the seat will slide down." |
| ERG550W OM 2023 | "Lift by the Rear Stabilizer (No.3) to raise the rower to vertical position." | "Move the Seat (No.17) to front of Slide Rail (No.2) first or it will glide down when raising the rower up!" "Your head may touch the Rear Stabilizer (No. 3)." Empty the tank if unused for more than a month. |
| ERG600W OM 2021 | "Lift by the Rear Stabilizer (No.4) to raise the rower to vertical position." | Same two cautions, Seat No.36 / Slide Rail No.3 / Rear Stabilizer No.4. Empty the tank if unused for more than a month. |
| ERG650W OM 2021 | "Lift by the Rear Stabilizer (No.2) to raise the rower to vertical position." | Same two cautions, Seat No.38 / Slide Rail No.3 / Rear Stabilizer No.2. Empty the tank if unused for more than a month. |
| ERG750W OM 2025 | "you can stand the machine track along the direction of the arrow as shown in Figure A to save space. After lifting, the connecting group can erect the machine, and the mat on the main frame will support the ground." | "When standing the machine up, be aware that ones head may touch the track tube!" "When the rails are uprighted, the Seat Cushion will slide down quickly!" |

Two things to do every time, whichever book you hold: **push the seat to the front of the rail before you
lift** (the three water rower books say so; the others only warn that it will slide), and **keep your head
clear of the rear stabilizer** as the rail comes up past you.

The water rowers add the one-month rule
(`xterra-ergw-maintenance-empty-the-tank-before-storing-for-more-than-a-month`). The ERG750W does not print
it, and no book in this family says anything about how long the rower may stay upright, about the floor under
it, or about tank leakage when stood up - that last question is a service-manual Q&A row for the errors
section.

Spirit's CRW800H2O prints the ERG550W captions for its own rower (`crw800h2o-maintenance-empty-the-tank-before-storing`).
Other brand, separate card.
