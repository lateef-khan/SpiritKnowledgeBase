Do this when a squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling develops - there is no calendar or hours interval for it:

1. Clean the **rails and wheels** with a **lint free cloth and rubbing alcohol**.
2. Stubborn build-up can be removed with your **thumbnail or a non-metallic scraper**, such as the back edge of a plastic knife.
3. After cleaning, **apply a small amount of lubricant on the rails** with your fingers or a lint free cloth. **You only need a thin coat of lubrication - wipe off any excess.**

The manual **does not name the lubricant type** and gives no interval. Do not substitute a metal scraper. The 2007 books print a shorter version of this procedure without the scraper sentence (separate card); the 2026 generation matches this card word for word (linked).
