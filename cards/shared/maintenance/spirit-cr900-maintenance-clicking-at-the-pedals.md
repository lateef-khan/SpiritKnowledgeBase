**This is the recumbent list of four checks.** The CU900 and CU900ENT book answers the same
complaint differently — lock the pedal, then check the crank — and adds that a flywheel sound
when pedalling backwards is normal (`cu900ent-noise-and-feet-feeling`).

As printed — 7-2-2: if there is a clicking sound, or a feeling that something is loose,

1. first check that the **pedals are tight**;
2. then check that the **crank arm bolts are tight**;
3. **check for play in the axle**;
4. it is also possible that the **bearing assembly nuts are loose** — check those.

The order is the book's. The pedal check is the weekly one
(`spirit-cr900-maintenance-weekly-pedal-check`); the crank arm bolt is tightened to 60 N·m
(45 ft-lb) with an 8 mm Allen key in the pedal/crank replacement, and the bearing cartridge nut is
loosened with a spanner wrench in the drive-system replacement — both assembly cards. The general
rule for any squeak, thump or click — loose assembly hardware, then the crank arm nut and pedals,
then the levellers — is `cu900ent-general-maintenance`.

The section is numbered 7-2-2 with no 7-2-1 in either book. Handlebars, seat or whole-machine
shaking is the next section, `spirit-cr900-maintenance-shaking-handlebars-seat-wheels-levellers`.
