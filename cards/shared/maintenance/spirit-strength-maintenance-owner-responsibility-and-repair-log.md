**All thirty-nine Spirit strength owner's manuals put maintenance on the owner, in two places.**

On the facility precautions page:

> **NOTE: It is the sole responsibility of the user/owner or facility operator to ensure that
> regular maintenance is performed.**

and on the maintenance page itself:

> **SPIRIT is not responsible for performing regular inspection and maintenance actions for your
> machines.** Instruct all personnel in equipment inspection and maintenance actions and also in
> accident reporting and recording.

## The four maintenance obligations that come with it

The facility precautions page carries nine bullets and the whole page is quoted on
`spirit-strength-safety-facility-precautions-list`, which is where a reader asking *what are the
facility's safety rules* should be sent. **Four of the nine are maintenance duties**, and these are
the ones a service conversation turns on:

1. **Perform regular maintenance checks on the equipment.** Also pay close attention to all areas
   most susceptible to wear, **including (but not limited to) cables, pulleys, belts and grips**.
2. **Immediately replace worn or damaged components.** If unable to immediately replace worn or
   damaged components then **remove from service until the repair is made**.
3. **Use only Spirit Fitness supplied components to maintain/repair the equipment.**
4. **Keep a repair log of all maintenance activities.**

The SAFEGUARDS page of the books that have one adds: *"Ensure that any person(s) making adjustments
or performing maintenance or repair of any kind is qualified to do so"* -
`spirit-strength-safety-safeguards-page-usage-and-inspection`.

**The pre-use cable inspection is the fifth bullet and has its own card**, because it is an
inspection interval rather than a duty:
`spirit-strength-maintenance-cables-inspected-weekly-and-before-every-use`.

## What a gym can be told with confidence

- **The repair log is required by the manual**, not optional. No format, retention period or
  content is specified — only that one is kept for *all* maintenance activities.
- **Aftermarket parts are excluded by the manual**, not merely discouraged.
- **A machine that cannot be fixed today comes off the floor today.** "Remove from service until
  the repair is made" is unconditional.
- **The pre-use inspection is separate from the scheduled one** and falls on whoever uses the
  machine.

## The three ST800 books say it with a shorter wear list

**ST800DR3** and **ST800FI** print the same five bullets but shorten the first to *"pay close
attention to all areas most susceptible to wear"* with **no component list**, and they print **no
pre-use cable inspection bullet**. Neither machine has a cable — one is a dumbbell rack and one is a
flat/incline bench. **ST800FT** prints the full list, cables and all.

## This is the Spirit statement

Every strength card written before this wave is Sole. Sole's own parts and service policies are
separate documents and are not what these thirty-nine books say.
