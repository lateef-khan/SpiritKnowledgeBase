Lubricate the rails and wheels **once a month**. It cuts noise and wear on the aluminum rails and the slide wheels.

What you need: 100% silicone lubricant (gel form preferred) and a rag.

1. Clean the rails and slide wheels of dust and old lubricant.
2. Put a small line of lubricant on each rail.
3. Ride the machine slowly to spread it across the rails and slide wheels.

The owners books name no lubricant type and give no calendar interval - they say clean with rubbing alcohol and apply a thin coat when noise or roughness develops; see `sole-elliptical-maintenance-rail-wheel-clean-lubricate`.
