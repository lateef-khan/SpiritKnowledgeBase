Regularly check belt lubrication: with the machine off and unplugged, reach between the running belt and the top of the treadmill base to verify that lubrication is present. To lubricate:

1. Ensure the machine is off and unplugged.
2. Reach between the belt and the top of the base to verify that lubrication is present.
3. While kneeling beside the deck, use one hand to hold the belt up and away from the base just enough so your other hand can reach the lubrication underneath.
4. **Starting about 1 foot from the motor cover, apply 1/2 of your lubricant bottle in a long "S" pattern about 4-6 inches from one edge.**
5. Repeat steps 3 and 4 on the opposite side **using the remaining 1/2 of the bottle**.
6. Plug the power cord back in and turn the power switch on.
7. **Walk on the belt at a moderate speed for 5 minutes** to distribute the lubricant evenly.
8. The belt is now lubricated; normal use can resume.

Cleaning between belt and deck is printed straight after it (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-cleaning-between-belt-and-deck-with-a-cloth`). The intervals differ by book: 40 hours for the TR260 (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-lubrication-interval-every-40-hours`), 80 hours for the TRX machines (`xterra-trx-maintenance-lubrication-interval-every-80-hours`). The TRX2500, TRX3500 and TRX4500 service manuals print a different, half-bottle-under-the-centre procedure: `xterra-trx-maintenance-service-manual-lubrication-half-bottle-under-the-centre-of-the-belt`. The Spirit XT 2023 books print these eight steps word for word; that is another brand.
