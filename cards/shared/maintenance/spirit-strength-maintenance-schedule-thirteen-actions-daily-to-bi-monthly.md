**Twenty-two Spirit strength machines print exactly this table.** Thirteen actions in three
groups, on one page, and it is the whole of the maintenance chapter in each of those books.

The only other timed obligation anywhere in them is the **pre-use** cable and belt inspection on the
facility precautions page - `spirit-strength-maintenance-cables-inspected-weekly-and-before-every-use`.

The page's own preamble, word for word:

> A regular preventative maintenance schedule with all fitness equipment ensures that products are
> working at an optimal condition without affecting the end user exercise experience. To assist in
> the maintenance regiment, it is recommended to break service into: Daily, Weekly, & Monthly
> activities.
>
> All preventive maintenance activities must be performed on a regular basis. **SPIRIT is not
> responsible for performing regular inspection and maintenance actions for your machines.**
> Instruct all personnel in equipment inspection and maintenance actions and also in accident
> reporting and recording.

## Note the two things that are cleaned and inspected on different clocks

**Upholstery is cleaned daily but inspected monthly.** **The main frame is cleaned weekly but
inspected bi-monthly.** They are separate rows in separate groups; a reader who scans one column
will miss the other.

**"Bi-Monthly" is the manual's own word** and it does not say whether that means twice a month or
every two months. It is the last column, after Monthly, which reads as every two months. The book
gives no definition. Say what the table says and do not resolve it for a customer.

## Only one item on this table gets lubricated

The guide rods, monthly. **Nothing else on the machine has a lubrication row**, and the table names
no lubricant — see `spirit-strength-maintenance-guide-rods-lubricated-monthly-and-no-lubricant-named`.

## Cleaning has no method and no product

Four rows say *Clean* and none of them says with what. There is no cloth, no cleaner, no soap and
no sanitizing page anywhere in these twenty-two books — see
`spirit-strength-maintenance-no-cleaning-agent-and-no-storage-section`.

## The nearby tables that are not this one

- The **nine CSD dual-station machines** print fifteen rows, adding a bi-monthly *Internal Tower*
  clean and a monthly *Add on Track* lubrication:
  `spirit-strength-maintenance-schedule-with-internal-tower-and-add-on-track`.
- The **two CSI machines** print different intervals under a **Bi-Annually** fourth column, with
  the frame cleaned daily and everything inspected monthly:
  `spirit-strength-maintenance-schedule-with-a-bi-annual-column`.
- The **CSF-FUNT** drops both upholstery rows, the **CSF-HRAC** drops the pulley row, and the
  **CSF-UPRB** prints only six rows with no lubrication group at all. Each has its own card.
- The **three ST800 machines** print a completely different table with separate **Commercial** and
  **Home** columns.

Do not read this table onto any of them.

## This is the Spirit table, not the Sole one

Every strength card in this repository that predates this wave is a Sole SRVO card. Nothing here
was taken from one, and none of them covers a Spirit machine.
