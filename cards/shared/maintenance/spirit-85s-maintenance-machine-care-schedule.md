Read the cleaning instructions above the table first: switch the main power **OFF** and unplug the
machine, use **water only** on most surfaces, and use a mild soap solution at **10:1 water to soap**
with a nylon scrub brush on the textured pedals. The full list, with what must never be used, is
`80t-2026-maintenance-cleaning-cautions`.

| Period | Task | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| After Each Use | General cleaning | Use a soft, damp cloth or paper towel to clean the console, and to wipe down the equipment surface, main body cover, handlebar, manual mechanical brake lever, cushion, adjustable levers and frame. |
| Weekly / Daily (Daily member Use) | Functional & safety checking | Ensure the equipment is functioning properly. Check around the equipment and inspect the crank arm with pedals for loosening. Use a soft, damp cloth or paper towel to wipe clean the full surface and console. |
| Monthly | Pedals cleaning | No cleaners or abrasives. A mild soap and water solution along with a nylon scrub brush to clean the top of the textured pedals (**10:1 water to soap**). |
| Monthly | Pedals checking | Check the handlebar arm and footplates are firmly tightened. Check hand grips, manual mechanical lock lever, cushion, seat back and footplates for signs of wear. |
| Quarterly | General cleaning, **tread belt cleaning**, functional & safety checking | Do a round of the above tasks. |

Four periods, no annual row, and **no levelling row** — levelling is on the previous page instead
(`spirit-85s-maintenance-pedals-and-front-and-rear-stabiliser-pads`).

## The quarterly row asks for a tread belt this machine does not have

An 8.5S is a seated stepper. It has no tread belt, no running deck and no belt tension or tracking
adjustment anywhere in its manual, yet the quarterly cell lists **Tread belt cleaning** as one of
three tasks. It is carried over from the treadmill version of this table
(`80t-2026-maintenance-cleaning-schedule`), where the row is real. **Read the quarterly row as "do a
round of the above tasks" and drop the tread belt.**

It is the same authoring chain that prints *Dyaco* and four other models in the page's opening
sentence (`spirit-85s-maintenance-care-page-names-dyaco-machines`) and that names a *manual
mechanical brake lever*, a *saddle* and a *seat back* in the cleaning lists.

**The mismatch runs the other way in the treadmill book.** The 8.0T's cleaning instruction step 3
says "textured **pedals**" on a treadmill while its table says "textured **belt**"; here the
instruction says pedals, which is right, and the table says tread belt, which is not.

## This is not the stepper table the 2024 range prints

The **CS800 2024** and **CRS800S 2024** steppers, and the **CSC900 2024** and **CSC880 2025** stair
climbers, print a different shape entirely — an *Item* × *Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Quarterly /
Biannually* grid of nine rows with Clean and Inspect marks in the cells
(`cs800-2024-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`,
`csc900-2024-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`). This table has no item column and no biannual
interval. **The 7.0S and 7.5S print no schedule table of any kind**, only the noise text and the
four-step post-workout routine.
