**To fold**: lift the running deck until it is secured by the locking mechanism on the **left upright tube**. You will hear it pop into place when secure.

**WARNING: before unfolding the treadmill, hold the deck with one hand to prevent it from falling and release the knob with your other hand.**

**To unfold**: apply slight forward pressure on the running deck with your **right hand**. Pull out on the **round knob** and slowly lower the running deck to the floor.

**To move it**: the treadmill has **two transport wheels**. Fold the treadmill before moving. **Lean the treadmill back so that the transport wheels touch the floor**; you are then free to move the unit. **CAUTION: do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and locked position.** Remove the power cord from the front of the unit to avoid possible damage. Use both handrails to manoeuvre the unit to the desired position.

The TR260 page carries an unprinted Chinese-and-English layer in the file ("Let the transport wheels touch the floor and roll to move", "Relocation"); the printed page, read from the render, says what is above. The TR150 and TR200 use a lock pin instead of a knob (`xterra-tr-maintenance-folding-lock-pin-on-the-left-upright-and-two-transport-wheels`); the TR300 family latches on the right side and rolls on four wheels (`xterra-tr-maintenance-folding-telescoping-tube-on-the-right-and-four-transport-wheels`).
