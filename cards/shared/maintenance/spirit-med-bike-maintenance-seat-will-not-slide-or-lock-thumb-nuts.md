The manual, word for word:

> Adjust the thumb nuts located to the rear of the seat adjustment levers. If the cables attached to the levers
> stretch it is possible the latch for the seat will not disengage, or engage, properly. Adjusting the thumb nut
> can remedy this.

**One adjuster covers both failures.** A cable that has stretched can leave the latch unable to let go - the
seat will not move - or unable to catch - the seat will not lock. Both are taken up at the **thumb nuts behind the
seat adjustment levers**, and the manual names no other part, no turn count and no travel figure. Adjust, try
the lever, repeat.

**The 7.0R has two seat levers.** The seat slides fore and aft on one and the seat back reclines on the other;
its service manual adds a third cable for the **swivel** of the seat, adjusted the same way - "adjust the
adjustment screw beneath the release handle" (6.6 and 6.7 of the 7.0R service manual, replacement procedures).
Establish which adjustment is failing before turning anything.

**Not the upright.** The 7.0U troubleshooting page has no seat row; its seat post and slide are locked by knobs,
and a seat that *shakes* on it is a screw adjustment in the service manual
(`70u-2025-maintenance-sway-seat-screws-and-console-mast-knob-and-quick-release`). A 7.0R seat that shakes rather
than sticks is the seat-wheel plate (`70r-2025-maintenance-swivel-seat-sway-fixing-screw-11-mm-and-13-mm-wrenches`).

The medical recumbent steppers print this same row word for word and it is carded with their troubleshooting
table (`spirit-med-stepper-errors-seat-will-not-lock-thumb-nuts`); on the bikes it is filed here because the
remedy is an adjustment, not a part.
