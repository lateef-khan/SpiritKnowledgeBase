| Part | Recommended Action | Frequency | Cleaner | Lubricant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pedals | Ensure that pedals are tight in crank arms; that all screws on pedals are tight; and that the pedal straps are not frayed | Before each Use | N/A | N/A |
| Frame | Wipe down by using a soft damp clean cloth | Daily | Water | N/A |
| Flywheel | Wipe down by spraying on a rag and applying a light coat to sides of the flywheel | Weekly | WD-40 Spray | N/A |
| Brake Pad | Inspect for excessive wear or a dry leather brake pad | Weekly | N/A | 3-IN-ONE Oil or 10W Oil. **Do not use Silicone-based Lubricants** |

**Spray the WD-40 onto a rag, not onto the flywheel**, and apply a light coat to the sides only.

The **brake pad is leather** and the manual asks you to look for dryness as well as wear. The two
oils it names are 3-IN-ONE and 10W. **Silicone-based lubricants are forbidden.**

Both printings, 2018 and 2021, carry this table unchanged.

## This is not the CB900, CIC800 or CIC850 table

Those three print the same four rows with **no Lubricant column at all**: their flywheel is wiped
with **water**, and their brake pad is only *checked for wear*, **monthly** —
`spirit-indoor-cycle-maintenance-schedule-water-and-monthly-brake-pad`. Two of the four figures
differ, so neither card may be quoted for the other's machines.

It also contradicts the commercial-bike absence card. Twelve Spirit commercial bike manuals name no
lubricant of any kind (`spirit-bike-maintenance-no-lubricant-belt-or-battery-service`); **the
XIC600 names three** — WD-40, 3-IN-ONE and 10W. That card does not cover the XIC600 and its "no
lubrication" statement must not be carried onto one.
