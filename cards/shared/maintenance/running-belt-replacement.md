1. Remove the screw holding the cover with a Phillips screwdriver, then pull the cover towards you to slip it off.
2. Loosen the two screws underneath where the cover was.
3. Loosen the 5 screws in the motor hood, three at the back and one on each side, then remove the motor hood.
4. Slide the cover away from the aluminium rail and loosen the screw beneath it.
5. Slide the foot rail off. It is tight but it will come.
6. Remove the 4 deck bolts with an Allen wrench.
7. Remove the bolts holding the rear roller.
8. Remove the bolt holding the front roller with two 13mm wrenches, then remove the front roller.
9. Pick up the walking deck and slide the old belt out.
10. Slide the new belt onto the deck.
11. Fit the rollers but do not tighten the bolts yet.
12. Apply lubrication to the deck surface, 18 inches back from the front motor cover.
13. Tension the belt by tightening the front and rear rollers. **Test**: half way along the belt, lift it with one hand and try to reach the centre of the deck. Your fingers should just barely NOT reach. If they reach, the belt is still too loose.
14. Start the machine at 1 to 2 mph and track the belt with the rear roller adjustment on the left side. Turn the Allen wrench in the direction you want the belt to go.
