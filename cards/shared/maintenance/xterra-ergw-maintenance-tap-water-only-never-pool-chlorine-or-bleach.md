**This is the tap-water rule of the ERG550W, ERG600W and ERG650W. The ERG750W says the opposite about tap
water, and the ERG800W asks for filtered or distilled water** - see the two cards linked as not-to-be-confused.

Printed under "How to Fill and Empty the Tank", word for word in all three books:

- **"Fill the tank only with tap water. Never use pool chlorine or chlorine bleach. This will damage the
  tank and void the warranty."**
- "Water from the tank is not suitable for consumption. Dispose the water after pumping it out from the tank."
- "To extend the life of your rower and prolong use between tank cleanings, please purchase water cleaning
  tablets at www.xterrafitness.com, or by calling 1-(870) 333-5500."

So: tap water in, a purchased XTERRA cleaning tablet as the only additive, and the old water down the drain.
The chlorine sentence is the one warranty exclusion printed on this page rather than in the warranty chapter;
the other one is filling past gauge level 6 (`xterra-ergw-maintenance-water-level-gauge-1-to-6-never-above-6`).

Only the **ERG550W** goes on to give a tablet interval - every 6 months or as needed, new water if it stays
cloudy (`xterra-ergw-maintenance-water-purification-tablet-every-six-months`). The ERG600W and ERG650W print
the purchase line and no interval at all; `month`, `tablet` and `cloudy` return nothing else in either book.

Draining and refilling is `xterra-ergw-maintenance-changing-the-water-siphon-pump-out-then-funnel-in`; the
one-month storage rule is `xterra-ergw-maintenance-empty-the-tank-before-storing-for-more-than-a-month`.

Spirit's CRW800H2O prints the same page for its own tank (`crw800h2o-maintenance-filling-and-emptying-the-tank`,
`crw800h2o-warranty-void-chlorine-and-overfilling`). Other brand, separate cards.
