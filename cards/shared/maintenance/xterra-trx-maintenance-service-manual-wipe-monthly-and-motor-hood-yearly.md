**These are the service manual's figures. The owner's manuals for the same machines say every other month for both tasks.** See `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-vacuum-under-monthly-and-motor-cover-every-other-month`; neither book acknowledges the other.

Chapter 10.1 "Tread belt and Deck" of the Dyaco service manuals (GT90B-NT022, GT90C-NT023 / GT90D-NT024, T3-NT053-01):

- Use a soft, damp cloth or paper towel to wipe the edge of the belt and the area between the belt edge and frame; also reach as far as practical directly under the belt edge. **This should be done once a month** to extend belt and bed life. Use water only - no cleaners or abrasives. A mild soap and water solution along with a nylon scrub brush will clean the top of the textured belt. Allow the belt to dry before using.
- Belt dust occurs during normal break-in or until the belt stabilizes; wiping excess off with a damp cloth will minimize buildup.
- General cleaning: dirt, dust and pet hair can block air inlets and accumulate on the running belt. **On a monthly basis, vacuum underneath your treadmill** to prevent buildup. **Once a year, remove the black motor hood** and vacuum out dirt that may accumulate. Unplug the power cord before this task.

The TRX3500/TRX4500 book prints this page as a picture; it was read from the render. The Spirit XT 2015 service manuals print the same paragraph with the same yearly figure, on `xt-2015-maintenance-vacuuming-motor-hood-yearly`.
