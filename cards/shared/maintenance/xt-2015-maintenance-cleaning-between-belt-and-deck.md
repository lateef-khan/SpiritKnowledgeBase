The 2015 manuals, the XT485ENT manual **and the XT685 2010 manual** print this as the **first step of the lubrication procedure**, not as a routine of its own. They give it no interval; it is done whenever you are about to lubricate.

1. Use a **clean, non-fraying rag, t-shirt, or light towel**.
2. **Halfway between the end of the treadmill and the motor cover**, shove the garment under the belt until you can **grasp it on both sides of the belt**.
3. **Drag the garment the length of the entire belt 1-2 times.**
4. **Remove the garment.**

**This is not the 2023 procedure.** The XT 2023 manuals print a longer, seven-step version that starts by unplugging the machine, adds a visual check, requires a cloth **longer than the running belt is wide**, and tells you to **rotate the belt halfway and repeat** for heavy dirt. The 2015 text has none of those steps and does not say to unplug. See `xt-2023-maintenance-belt-and-deck-cleaning`.

**The XT685 2015 has no such procedure**, because it takes no lubricant. Its manual gives a monthly damp-cloth routine instead; see `xt685-2023-belt-deck-general-maintenance`. **The XT685 2010 is the opposite** - it lubricates, and prints this paragraph in the 2015 words, with the same leading "First, you want to clean between the belt and deck" the XT185 and XT285 manuals have.

**Damage in the source.** The XT385 and XT485 2015 manuals **and the XT485ENT manual** begin this paragraph "**These** First, you want to clean between the belt and deck", with a stray leading word the XT185 and XT285 manuals do not have. Nothing else in the paragraph differs between the five.
