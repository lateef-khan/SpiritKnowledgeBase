Every Xterra rower that prints a moving instruction moves the same way: **the wheels are on the front
stabilizer, and they only touch the floor when the rear of the machine is lifted.**

Six books print one sentence, word for word (ERG160, ERG220, ERG550W, ERG600W, ERG650W, ERG750W):

> To move the machine, lift up the rear stabilizer until the transportation wheels on the front stand touch
> the ground. With the wheels on the ground, you can transport the rower to the desired location with ease.

Three say it differently:

| Book | Wording | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| ERG700 OM 2022, "Moving Procedures" | "1. Fold the aluminum rail assembly up. 2. Lift the rear and roll away." | Fold first (`erg700-2022-maintenance-folding-release-lever-at-the-handlebar-and-rear-stabilizer-knob`); the drawing shows the folded machine tipped onto its front wheels. |
| ERG800W OM 2023, "Storage and Transportation" | "After assembly, you can move the rower by lifting the rower from the rear stabilizer to let the wheels on the front stabilizer to touch the floor. Once the wheels touch the floor, you can move the rower easily." | **"Don't tilt the rower left/right so that only one wheel touches the floor during the transportation. The water tank will be damaged if the rower falls left/right."** |
| ERG180 OM 2023, "Moving the Machine" | "The front stabilizer has built-in transport wheels. Stand at the front of the machine and lift it up until the weight of the machine is transferred to the transport wheels." | See below - the text and the drawing disagree. |

## The ERG180 sentence is a bike sentence, and the drawing wins

The ERG180 paragraph is the one printed for the SB240 and SB600 recumbents
(`xterra-sb-maintenance-moving-stand-at-the-front-and-lift-onto-the-built-in-transport-wheels`), whose
wheels are under the front and are engaged by lifting the front. On the ERG180 page the **drawing beside it
shows the user at the rear, holding the rear stabilizer up, with the front wheels on the floor** - the same
picture the other rower books print. Lifting the front of a rower whose wheels are on the front stabilizer
lifts the wheels off the floor. This card follows the drawing: on the ERG180, as on every other ERG, **lift the
rear**.

The ERG400 and ERG500 books print no moving instruction anywhere (no "move", "transport" or "wheel" sentence
in either, including the OCR supplements). The ERG400's assembly step fits parts it calls "wheel covers" to the
main frame and never says what they cover; neither book says how to move the machine, so do not tell an
owner of either that the book says how.

Xterra's bikes and ellipticals lift the rear the same way
(`xterra-maintenance-moving-lift-the-rear-and-roll-on-the-front-transport-wheels`). Spirit's rowers print
the same sentence for their own machines (`crw800h2o-assembly-storage-and-moving`,
`crw800-2024-assembly-folding-and-moving`). Other brand, separate cards.
