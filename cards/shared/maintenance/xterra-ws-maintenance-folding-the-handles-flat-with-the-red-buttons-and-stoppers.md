"Folding Your Treadmill", printed with three lettered drawings:

- **(A)** Press and hold the **red button on the handle** with your thumb and, at the same time, fold the handle in the direction of the arrow until the handle is locked, releasing the button as you raise your hand. The other side is done the same way.
- **(B)** Fold the **Handlebar down and make it parallel to the running platform**.
- **(C)** Rotate the **Handlebar Stoppers out so that the stopper catches the Upright**.

The parts diagram names a **Transport Handlebar (K)** and **Upright Stopper (L)**, but no page says how to move the machine and no transport wheels are named; the only mention of wheels is the boilerplate safety line about routing the cord away from "the elevation mechanism and transport wheels".

The Functions page adds that the treadmill **only operates with the uprights up**: if they are folded down during operation it stops automatically within 3 seconds, and it cannot be used with the uprights folded as it was in the carton. That behaviour belongs to the console cards. The post-workout routine says to store the machine folded when not in use (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-post-workout-care`).
