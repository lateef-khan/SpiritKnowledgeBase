This is the 180-hour version of the S-pattern procedure. It differs from the 90-hour card only in the interval and reminder timing; every step below is otherwise identical.

The belt and deck come pre-lubricated. After that, lubricate **every 180 hours of use**, or sooner if you notice the deck is dry. **Reach between the belt and deck every other month** to check that lubricant is present; if there is none, lubricate even though the console "Lube" indicator is not lit. Otherwise lubricate when the console reminder lights after 180 hours.

**Use only Sole Fitness approved lubricant.** The treadmill comes with one tube of "Lube"; extra tubes and a Lube-n-Walk kit can be ordered from Sole or an authorised dealer. **Failure to lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty**: dry lubricant raises the friction between belt and deck and stresses the drive motor, drive belt and electronic motor control board to the point of catastrophic failure.

First clean between the belt and deck: with a clean non-fraying rag, t-shirt or light towel, halfway between the end of the treadmill and the motor cover, push the garment under the belt until you can grasp it on both sides, then drag it the length of the belt 1-2 times and remove it.

1. Turn the power switch off and unplug the power cord.
2. Measure **18 inches** from the edge of the motor cover; kneel down and reach under the belt **4-6 inches** from one edge. Squirt a line of lubricant about **1/8 inch wide by 15 inches long in an "S" pattern**, perpendicular to the motor cover.
3. Repeat on the opposite side.
4. Plug the cord back in and turn the power switch on.
5. Walk on the belt at a moderate speed for **five minutes** to spread the silicone evenly.
6. If the "Lube" message is on the console, reset it through the engineering menu: press and hold **Start, Stop and Enter**, then insert the safety key and keep holding until the engineering menu shows; press Enter, use Speed 5 (Speed 6 goes backwards) to reach **Functions**, press Enter, then step to the **Maintenance** message and press Enter to reset it. Press Stop to exit.
