**This is the recumbent answer, with a seat-wheel adjustment.** The CU900 and CU900ENT upright's
seat fix is one seat connection screw (`cu900ent-shaking`); this seat rides on wheels in an
aluminium track and the fix is to take the play out of them.

| What shakes | What to do (as printed) |
|---|---|
| **Front handlebars** shake during pedalling or feel loose when grabbed | Check the bolts securing the handlebar to the mast and/or the mast bolts at the main frame (7-3-1) |
| **The seat** shakes during use | Check the wheels are tightened properly. It may be necessary to eliminate play between the wheels and the seat track: **loosen the bottom wheels and lift up on the wrench while retightening them.** You may need to adjust the top wheels too — loosen them, **lift the seat carriage while retightening them** (7-3-2) |
| **The whole product** shakes during use | Check that the **foot levellers** are adjusted properly (7-3-3) |

The levellers are the two pads under the rear stabilizer, turned with a 14 mm wrench
(`cu900ent-general-maintenance`). The handlebar check is the one the book's preventative-
maintenance chapter schedules as needed (`spirit-cr900-maintenance-console-mast-screws`). Cleaning
the track keeps the wheels sliding once the play is out
(`spirit-cr900-maintenance-periodic-cleaning-and-seat-track-rails`).

Neither book gives a torque or a play figure for the seat wheels; the test is that the seat no
longer shakes.
