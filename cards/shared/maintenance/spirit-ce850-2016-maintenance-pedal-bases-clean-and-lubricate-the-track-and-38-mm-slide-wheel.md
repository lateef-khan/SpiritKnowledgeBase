**This is the adjustable-stride pedal base of the CE850 and XE895, whose 38 mm wheel rides an
aluminium track inside the base. It is not the 72 or 78 mm rear slide wheel on the rear rail that
the other Spirit ellipticals clean (`ce800-2013-maintenance-rear-rail-cleaning-and-lubrication`).**

"Left and Right Pedal Bases and Pedal Arms (L) & (R) are the most important part of this model.
Pay attention to the maintenance."

1. If there is a noise, check whether something is **rubbing** or something has **dropped in**, then
   check the **Aluminum Track**. If it is damaged, replace it as soon as possible.
2. If the **diameter-38 Slide Wheel, Urethane** is damaged, replace it directly.
3. **Clean the Aluminum Track and the diameter-38 Slide Wheel, Urethane, and add some lubricants.**
4. **If it is loose, use an 11 mm wrench to adjust the diameter-38 Slide Wheel on the adjustment
   wheel fixing plate until it is appropriately tight.**

No lubricant is named and no interval is given. The wheel is set the same way when the base goes
back together after a replacement: "Adjust the 38 slide wheel and Aluminum Track by using 11m/m
wrench and 1/4" x 1/2" Hex Head Bolt while lock the Left and Right Pedal Base back on Pedal Arm."

The other half of the stride mechanism, the slider adjustment and its 40 mm wheel, has its own
check - `spirit-ce850-2016-maintenance-slider-adjustment-40-mm-wheel-and-lubricated-swing-assembly`.
The Sole E95S (2023) service manual prints this section word for word (`e95s-2023-pedal-base-noise`).
