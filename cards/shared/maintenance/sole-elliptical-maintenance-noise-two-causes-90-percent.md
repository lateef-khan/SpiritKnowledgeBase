If a squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling develops, the main cause is most likely one of **two** reasons:

**I. The hardware was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.** All bolts installed during assembly need to be tightened **as much as possible**. It may be necessary to use a **larger wrench than the one provided** if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently. The manual states that **90% of calls to the service department for noise issues can be traced to loose hardware or the rear rails being dirty**.

**II. Dirt build-up on the rear rails and polyurethane wheels.** Build-up on the rails can cause a thumping sound that seems to come from inside the main body of the machine, because noise travels and is amplified in the tubing of the frame. The cleaning procedure is on its own card.

**If squeaks or other noises persist, check that the unit is properly leveled before calling the service department.** In the VE2008 books (ve25-2007, ve55-2007, ve35-2007, ve95-2007) and the WE55/WE95 2009-2010 book (we55-2009, we55-2010, we95-2009, we95-2010) this item adds the leveler spec: **4 leveling pads on the bottom of the rear rails, adjusted with a 14mm wrench (or adjustable wrench)**. All other books on this card print the plain check-leveled sentence.

This is the whole of these manuals' noise troubleshooting - no bearing, bushing or belt is named. The 2026 generation matches this card word for word (linked).
