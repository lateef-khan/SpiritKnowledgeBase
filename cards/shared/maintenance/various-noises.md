**Rubbing noise**

1. Check the running belt is tracked to the centre.
2. If it is tracked correctly, have the customer remove the motor hood cover and check the drive belt for tracking and damage. Replace the drive belt if it is off track or damaged.
3. If the running belt is not tracked correctly, walk the customer through running belt tracking. The owner's manual has the instructions.

**Clicking, clunking, knocking or squeaking**

Ask where the noise comes from, and whether it happens all the time or only when someone is on the machine.

- Front or rear, all the time: replace the front roller or the rear roller, possibly both.
- Directly under the customer, only when they are on it: replace the deck cushions. Ask whether it is only on the left or the right, to decide how many to send.
- Front only, while they are on it, possibly one noise per footstep: have them check every fastener is tight. If they are, replace the nylon washers and the deck cushions.
