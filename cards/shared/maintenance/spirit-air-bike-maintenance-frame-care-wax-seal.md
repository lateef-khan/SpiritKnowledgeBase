It is recommended that the frame is **sealed from sweat impurities**, which may speed the corrosion
process, **at least once a month**.

1. **Cleaning.** Use a clean terry cloth dampened in **30 parts water to 1 part** non-abrasive
   detergent or car wash soap, and rub over the frame of the product.
2. **Wash / dry.** Dampen a clean terry cloth in an **only water** solution to wipe away any soap
   residue. Using a **second clean and dry terry cloth**, dry off any liquid residue.
3. **Sealing.** The frame can be sealed by applying a **wax or polishing coat** that helps repel
   liquids like human perspiration. Apply the wax or polish per the manufacturer's instructions.

The manuals name **no wax or polish brand** and no product to avoid at this step. The
petroleum-solvent and abrasive-cleaner ban from the daily wipe-down still applies —
`spirit-air-bike-maintenance-daily-dry-off-and-six-sweat-points`.

## Sealing is not sanitizing

The 75% isopropyl alcohol procedure is a separate job with a separate purpose
(`ct900-sanitizing-equipment`); the AB950 and JB950 print it, the AB900 does not. **A wax coat is
not a disinfectant, and alcohol is not a sealer.**

## No indoor cycle in this wave prints frame care

The CB900, CIC800, CIC850 and XIC600 owner's manuals name only a damp cloth and water for the
frame. They give no monthly sealing step, no 30-to-1 ratio and no wax.
