The Maintenance & Care page of the new-layout books opens with this routine:

1. **Store your machine according to the folding instructions when not in use.** (Printed by the TRX1400, TR65, TR75, TR85, WS200 and WS300 only.)
2. Use a slightly damp cloth to clean areas where sweat or oil made contact with the machine.
3. Use a microfiber cloth to clean the display and remove unwanted oils and other things that may damage the screen.
4. Avoid leaving paper or other small debris in the cupholders.

The **SB240 and SB600 recumbent bikes print the same four steps** with step 1 reworded: "Store your machine in a dry and clean area when not in use." Nothing else on their Maintenance & Care page differs from the treadmill page except that the bikes omit the treadmill's general-cleaning paragraph about vacuuming under the unit. Moving them is `xterra-sb-maintenance-moving-stand-at-the-front-and-lift-onto-the-built-in-transport-wheels`.

The **TR95H and TR75H print three steps**, dropping the folding line - those machines do not fold (`tr95h-2024-maintenance-moving-and-leveling-switchpro-knobs`, `tr75h-2025-maintenance-moving-and-leveling-adjusting-tips`).

The **ERG180 magnetic rower prints the SB240/SB600 wording of all four steps**, cupholder line included. The **ERG800W water rower prints steps 1-3 only** - no cupholder line - and follows them with its own paragraph: stop using the rower immediately if there are signs of excessive wear on the belt, pulleys, pedal straps and straps, and do not use it until they are repaired or replaced; its weekly, half-yearly and hundred-hour table is `erg800w-2023-maintenance-schedule-weekly-rails-and-frame-half-yearly-water-and-hundred-hour-belt-check`. The other nine rower books have no post-workout section (`xterra-maintenance-owners-manuals-with-no-cleaning-or-care-page`).

This is the routine sweat wipe-down only. Disinfecting is `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-sanitizing-with-75-percent-isopropyl-alcohol`. The older-layout Xterra books (TR150 through TR6.4 and the TRX 2024 books) have no post-workout section.
