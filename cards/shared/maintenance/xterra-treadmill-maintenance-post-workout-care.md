The Maintenance & Care page of the new-layout books opens with this routine:

1. **Store your machine according to the folding instructions when not in use.** (Printed by the TRX1400, TR65, TR75, TR85, WS200 and WS300 only.)
2. Use a slightly damp cloth to clean areas where sweat or oil made contact with the machine.
3. Use a microfiber cloth to clean the display and remove unwanted oils and other things that may damage the screen.
4. Avoid leaving paper or other small debris in the cupholders.

The **TR95H and TR75H print three steps**, dropping the folding line - those machines do not fold (`tr95h-2024-maintenance-moving-and-leveling-switchpro-knobs`, `tr75h-2025-maintenance-moving-and-leveling-adjusting-tips`).

This is the routine sweat wipe-down only. Disinfecting is `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-sanitizing-with-75-percent-isopropyl-alcohol`. The older-layout Xterra books (TR150 through TR6.4 and the TRX 2024 books) have no post-workout section.
