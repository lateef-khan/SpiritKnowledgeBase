Printed under "Cleaning", word for word in all nine books:

> A damp cloth is sufficient to clean most surfaces of this unit. **Be careful not to drip water on the
> monitor.** A mild liquid soap may be added if needed.

That is the whole instruction: water, a cloth, and optionally mild liquid soap. Nothing is forbidden by
name and no disinfectant is mentioned. The one caution is the monitor.

Other Xterra books say it differently, and the difference is worth keeping straight:

- The SB120 and UB120 allow a **mild non-abrasive detergent** and add **"Do not use solvents"** and
  "store the bike indoors" -
  `xterra-bike-maintenance-cleaning-with-mild-non-abrasive-detergent-no-solvents-and-indoor-storage`.
- The SB500, SB4.5r, SB4500, FS3.0, FS5.9e and RSX1500 say only "damp cloth, sweat path, after each
  workout" - `xterra-maintenance-wipe-the-sweat-path-with-a-damp-cloth-after-each-workout`.
- The new-layout SB240 and SB600 print the 75% isopropyl alcohol sanitizing page.
