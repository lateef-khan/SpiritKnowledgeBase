**Owner's manual diagnosis guide** (TR300, TR6.6, TR6.4, TRX1400, TR65, TR75, TR85), one row for two symptoms - "Tread-belt does not stay centered" and "Treadmill belt hesitates when walked/run on":

> The user may be walking while favouring or putting more weight on either the left or right foot. **If this walking pattern is natural, track the belt slightly off-centre to the side opposite from the belt movement.** See the Maintenance section on Tread-belt Tension. Adjust as necessary.

**Dyaco service manual matrix** (TR150, TR260, TRX1400, TRX2500, TRX3500/TRX4500), two rows:

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Tread belt does not run in center | Tread belt tension not even across tread belt | See treadmill belt adjustment |
| Tread belt hesitates while being stepped on | Insufficient lubricant on tread belt; tread belt tension insufficient | See treadmill belt lubrication |

The TRX1400 service manual's diagnosis guide adds "motor drive belt may be loose" to the hesitation row.

Where the pointers lead: tracking is `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-belt-tracking-6-mm-allen-wrench-left-bolt-3-mph` or `xterra-tr-maintenance-belt-tracking-5-mm-allen-wrench-left-bolt-3-mph`; tension is `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-belt-tension-quarter-turn-with-6-mm-allen-wrench`. **On the TR65, TR75 and TR85 the row sends the reader to a tracking procedure the book never prints** (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-no-belt-tracking-procedure-printed`). The TR150, TR200, TRX1000 and TR260 owner's manuals print no diagnosis guide of this kind, only an error-code table.
