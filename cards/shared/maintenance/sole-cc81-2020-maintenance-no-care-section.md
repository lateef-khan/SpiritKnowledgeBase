**The CC81 book prints no care, cleaning, lubrication or maintenance schedule.** Its table of contents goes from Operation straight to Maintenance Menu in Console Software and then the warranty. (The 2019 book is evidence for cc81-2020: same SKU 581519 on the machine.)

Two things this absence is not:

- The **Maintenance Menu** the book does print is console diagnostic software (Start + Stop + Enter, Engineering Mode) - a console-section topic, not machine care.
- Warranty item 7 makes the consumer responsible for **"routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual"** - but no page of the manual specifies any. Like the SC200/SC300 books' wipe-down sentence, that sentence is unenforceable here because there is nothing to enforce.

The SC200 and SC300 climber books do print a General Maintenance list (linked card); the CC81 book has no equivalent page. No moving or transport procedure is printed either.
