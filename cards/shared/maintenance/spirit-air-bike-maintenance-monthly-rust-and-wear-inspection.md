The monthly check is a **comprehensive inspection of the overall frame and main assembly
components**, in addition to the weekly maintenance.

## Rust and corrosion

Inspect the frame and main assembly components for rust or corrosion. **Tilt the bike or place it
upside down** to locate areas where rust and corrosion may develop. Use a **small wire brush** to
remove rust build-up in small crevasses such as leveling feet, pop pin handles and other bolt
assemblies. Particular attention to:

- Leveling feet
- Pop pin handles

## Wear items

Inspect all wear items for adjustment or possible part replacement. Particular attention to:

- **Inspect seat pad for wear.** Rips, tears or excessive movement indicates replacement is
  required.
- **Inspect pedals for play.** Excessive movement of pedals indicates replacement is required.
- Tighten seat hardware.
- Leveling feet.

**Parts replacement:** certain items may need replacing on inspection due to wear, identified
during these scheduled inspections. The manuals name **no service life, no wear limit and no hour
figure** for any of them.

## The JB950 prints neither list

The Johnny G Spirit Bike's monthly page names **pedals only** — no rust inspection, no wire brush
and no wear-item list. Confirmed by reading printed pp. 68 and 70 in a 300 dpi render as well as in
the extraction. See `jb950-2022-maintenance-weekly-check-toe-clips-and-service-tech`.

## This is not the service manual's wear-parts warning

The CU900ENT service manual names a different set — belt, PU wheel, bearing, idler — as the
components most susceptible to wear (`cu900ent-wear-parts-inspection`). That is a commercial
upright bike list and none of these four items appears on it.
