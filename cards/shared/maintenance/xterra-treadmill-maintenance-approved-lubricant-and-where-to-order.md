**Do not lubricate with anything other than XTERRA Fitness approved lubricant.** The treadmill comes with one tube; the books differ only in how they say to buy more:

| Books | What is in the box | Where to get more |
|---|---|---|
| TR300, TR6.6, TR6.4 | one tube of "Lube" | www.xterrafitness.com |
| TRX1000, TR260, TRX2500, TRX3500, TRX4500, TRX5500 | one tube of treadmill belt lubricant | www.xterrafitness.com or customer service at (870) 336-4286 |
| TRX1400, TR65, TR75, TR85, WS200, WS300 | one tube of treadmill belt lubricant | www.Xterrafitness.com or customer service at (800) 258-8511 |
| TR95H | one tube of "Hiker Treadmill belt lubricant" | www.Xterrafitness.com or (800) 258-8511 |
| TR75H | one **1 oz tube of silicone** | the distributor's **7 oz silicone in one bottle with a 14-inch applicator** - 100% silicone, non-petroleum base, no harmful solvents, non-toxic and non-flammable, pump-action spray bottle, "good for treadmill that used non-petroleum based lubricant" |

The lubricant is called **silicone** in the procedures ("silicone lube") and in the pack lists ("Silicone Oil"); no grade or part number is printed. The TR150 and TR200 books say "silicone lubricant" in the procedure but print no approved-lubricant line and no ordering instruction, so they are not on this card.

**The WS200 and WS300 pack list counts 2 Silicone Oil while the lubrication page says the treadmill comes with one tube.** The TRX1400 service manual adds that "a Lube-n-Walk kit" may also be used. The (800) 258-8511 number is the one the Spirit books print for Spirit customer service.
