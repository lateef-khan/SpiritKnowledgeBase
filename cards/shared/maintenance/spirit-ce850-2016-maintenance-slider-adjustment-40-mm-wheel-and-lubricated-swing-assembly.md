"Slider adjustment is the most important part of stride adjustments."

1. Check whether the **diameter-40 Adjustment Transportation Wheel** is damaged. If it is, replace
   it following the disassembly steps.
2. Check whether the **Swing Assembly (#16)** is **defaced on the surface**. **Wipe it, then apply
   some lubricant.**
3. **Adjust the Wheel Base** according to the disassembly steps if there is space between the two
   parts, and **adjust it to the standard scales**.

No lubricant is named and no interval is given. The "standard scales" are not printed on this page;
the slider-adjustment replacement procedure it points to says only that when everything is back
together the **M8 x 20 L socket head cap bolt on the wheel base is torqued to 150 lbs** (as printed,
no unit of length) and the M8 x 6.3T nut locked tight before the slider cover goes back on.

This is the stride-length mechanism, which the CE850 and XE895 alone have; the 38 mm wheel inside
the pedal base is the companion check
(`spirit-ce850-2016-maintenance-pedal-bases-clean-and-lubricate-the-track-and-38-mm-slide-wheel`).
The Sole E95S (2023) book prints the same three points (`e95s-2023-slider-adjustment-noise`).
