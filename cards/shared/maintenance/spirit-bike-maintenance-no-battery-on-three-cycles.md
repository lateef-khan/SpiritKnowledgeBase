**None of these three machines has a battery an owner can change.** The words *battery*, *cell*,
*AAA*, *AA*, *CR2032* and *lithium* do not appear anywhere in any of the three manuals, in the
extracted text or in a 300 dpi render of every page.

Two different reasons:

- **The CB900 2013 and CIC800 2021 have no console at all.** Neither manual contains the words
  *console*, *computer*, *monitor* or *display*, and neither parts list holds one. Resistance is a
  knob, the brake is that knob pressed down, and there is nothing electrical to power. A customer
  reporting "the display is dead" on one of these has the wrong model.
- **The JB950 2022 has a console, but its manual never names a power source.** What it does say is
  that **pedalling above 30 RPM is all that is needed to illuminate the console**. There is no
  battery compartment, no mains cord, no adapter and no charging instruction printed anywhere in
  its 76 pages. Do not send a JB950 owner looking for a battery door.

## The other five machines in this family do take batteries

- **XIC600 2018 and 2021, CIC850 2022** — console: **2 AAA**
  (`spirit-indoor-cycle-maintenance-console-two-aaa-batteries`).
- **XIC600 2018 and 2021** — speed sensor transmitter: a second **2 AAA**
  (`spirit-indoor-cycle-speed-transmitter-two-aaa-batteries`).
- **CIC850 2022** — a sensor board battery behind the left chain guard, **type never printed**
  (`cic850-2022-maintenance-sensor-board-battery-behind-left-chain-guard`).
- **AB900 2018 and AB950 2024** — console: **4 AA, not included**
  (`spirit-bike-console-air-bike-four-aa-batteries`).

The commercial Spirit bikes are a separate case again: no machine battery, only the chest strap's
CR2032 — `spirit-bike-maintenance-no-lubricant-belt-or-battery-service`. **That card does not cover
these eight machines and its statement must not be carried onto them**, because five of the eight
do take batteries and two of them name lubricants.
