**This is the elliptical and seated-stepper rule. The bike books say a thump or wobble is loose pedals
or crank arms** - `xterra-sb-maintenance-thump-clicking-or-wobble-from-loose-pedals-or-crank-arms`.

From the General Maintenance page, after the sweat-path wipe-down:

2. If a squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling develops, the main cause is most likely one of two reasons:
   1. **The hardware was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.** All bolts that were installed during
      assembly need to be tightened as much as possible. It may be necessary to use a **larger wrench than
      the one provided** if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently. The majority of calls to the service
      department for noise issues can be traced to loose hardware or the rear rails being dirty.
   2. **Dirt build-up on the rear rails and polyurethane wheels** is also a source of noise. Noise from
      build-up on the rails can cause a thumping sound that you would swear is coming from inside the main
      body of the machine, because noise travels and is amplified in the tubing of the frame. The cleaning
      procedure is `xterra-maintenance-rear-rail-and-wheel-cleaning-rubbing-alcohol-plastic-scraper-thin-coat-of-lubricant`.
3. If squeaks or other noises persist, **check that the unit is properly leveled** before calling the
   service department.

## Expect some noise

The same page closes with a paragraph headed "Elliptical Noises" (FS3.0, FS5.9e) or "Seated Stepper Noises"
(RSX1500): all such trainers, no matter who the manufacturer, have a multitude of moving linkages and parts,
and even with the tightest mechanical tolerances there can be a slight amount of play between some parts.
That play can cause slight noises during use such as clicks and small thumps. **Please expect that the
machine will not be completely silent.**

## The FS3.0 prints reason (i) only

The FS3.0 book gives item 2 as a single reason - the hardware paragraph - and keeps the sentence "loose
hardware or the rear rails being dirty" without ever printing the rail-cleaning steps. The FS5.9e and the
RSX1500 (both the 2021 book and the 2017 printing) print reasons (i) and (ii) in full.

The RSX1500 is a seated stepper, and its page is the elliptical text with the product name swapped: it
speaks of "rear rails and polyurethane wheels" and prints no step- or pedal-arm-specific maintenance of
its own.

Spirit and Sole print the same page for their ellipticals (`ce800-2013-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`,
`e95-2026-squeak-thump-or-rough-feel`). Other brand, separate cards.
