**This is the elliptical section. The CU900ENT bike's 7.2 under the same heading is a different text
about a flywheel sound when pedalling backwards (`cu900ent-noise-and-feet-feeling`).**

## A grainy feel is normal

7.2.1 as printed: "If there is a sense of graininess, such as re-treading, such as the use of the
bearing is the roller is not the general ball, so give the operation will feel the same, this is
normal." The slide-wheel bearing is a roller bearing, not a ball bearing, and a slight graininess
underfoot is how it feels. Do not replace parts for it.

## A loud abnormal noise

7.2.2: a lot of abnormal sound is "not easy to detect". It comes "from the **fish eye axis and the
fish eye bearing**" of the pedal-arm link, and "the most important reason is the **lack of
lubrication** or rotation is not smooth or **loose screws**". Because the machine is a linked
structure, the fix "must be in accordance with the actual situation" - lubricate the rod-end
(fish-eye) joint, tighten what is loose.

## Keep the slide wheels and the rails clean

7.2.3: "The surface of the **sliding wheel group** is kept clean and can be dusted for a long period
of time, need regular maintenance and cleaning, and can maintain wheel life." The main-roller
replacement procedure (6.13 / 12.41) shows how to take off the carriage base covers A and B and the
slide guard to get at them.

7.2.4: "regularly clean the **aluminum rails** to extend the service life. It is recommended to use
**alcohol** to remove the dust attached to the aluminum rail." The CE900 (2021) and CE900ENT books
print the same sentence a second time as their one preventative-maintenance item, 3.1 Clean
Regularly for Aluminum Slides; the owner's-manual version of the track cleaning is
`spirit-ce900-maintenance-track-and-wheel-cleaning`. No lubricant is named anywhere in the three
books for the rails or the wheels.

A belt that slips is 7.1 (`spirit-ce900-maintenance-belt-slips-or-falls-off-pressure-roller-280-to-310-n`);
a machine that shakes is 7.3 (`spirit-ce900-maintenance-shaking-loose-handle-screw`); a squeak from
loose hardware is the general maintenance list (`ct800-2016-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`).
