**How often**: every 3 months, or every 90 hours of use.

**What you need**: 2 ounces of 100% silicone based lubricant, preferably a gel, and a 5mm or 6mm Allen wrench.

1. Go to the back of the machine with the Allen wrench. The top hole in each end cap is the rear roller bolt used for tensioning.
2. Turn both bolts counter-clockwise 10 full turns.
3. Lift the belt.
4. Lay the lube down in an "S" pattern towards the centre of the deck, where the user runs.
5. Tighten both rear roller bolts clockwise 10 full turns to tension the belt again.
6. Run the machine at low speed, no more than 2. The belt spreads the lubricant for you.
