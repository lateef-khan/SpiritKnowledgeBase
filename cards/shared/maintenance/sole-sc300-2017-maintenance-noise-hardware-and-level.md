If a squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling develops, the manual says the main cause is most likely one of two reasons, then prints two:

**I. The hardware was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.** All bolts installed during assembly need to be tightened **as much as possible**. It may be necessary to use a **larger wrench than the one provided** if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently. The manual states that **ninety percent of calls to the service department for noise issues can be traced to loose hardware**.

**II. If squeaks or other noises persist, check that the unit is properly leveled.** There are **leveling pads on the bottom of the front and rear stabilizers**; adjust them with a **14mm wrench (or adjustable wrench)**.

Two differences from its siblings: there is **no crank arm nut** step (the SC200 books print one - linked card), and the levelers sit under **both** stabilizers rather than the rear one only. Like the SC200 books, and unlike the elliptical card (`sole-elliptical-maintenance-noise-two-causes-90-percent`), this book names no rail dirt and carries no rail-cleaning step.
