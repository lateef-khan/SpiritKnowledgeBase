> There is a set of transport wheels on the Front Stabilizer. To move the bike carefully **tilt the
> handlebar forwards and roll away.**

The SB120 names the stabilizer as part No. 6; the UB120 does not number it. With the wheels on the front
stabilizer, tilting the bike forward by the handlebar is what puts its weight on them - the other Xterra
books reach the same position by lifting the rear stabilizer
(`xterra-maintenance-moving-lift-the-rear-and-roll-on-the-front-transport-wheels`), reached from the other
end of the machine.
