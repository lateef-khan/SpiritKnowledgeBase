**This is the bike version of the noise rule. The elliptical and seated-stepper books blame assembly
hardware, dirty rails and leveling instead** -
`xterra-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking-from-loose-hardware-dirty-rails-or-leveling`.

The SB500, SB4.5r and SB4500 General Maintenance page, item 2:

> If a thump, clicking or wobbly feeling develops, the main cause is usually the pedals and/or crank arms
> being loose.

No torque figure, no wrench size and no procedure follow it. The fix the books imply is the one their
assembly page gives: the **left pedal is a reverse thread** (turn it counter-clockwise to tighten) and the
right pedal a normal thread (clockwise), and the pedals go on **"as tight as you can possibly get them"**.

## When to re-tighten - the books disagree by a unit

| Book | Sentence on the pedal step |
|---|---|
| SB500 OM, 2020 and 2014 printings, p. 12 | "It may be necessary to re-tighten them again after **a few hours** of use." |
| SB4.5r OM 2013, p. 12 | "It may be necessary to re-tighten them again after **a few months** of use." |
| SB2.5r OM 2020, p. 8 | "Make sure to tighten the pedals firmly or a clicking noise can occur during pedaling. It may be necessary to retighten the pedals **after use, if clicking occurs**." |
| SB4500 OM 2021, p. 12 | Left counter-clockwise, right clockwise; no re-tighten sentence. |

The same boilerplate sentence prints *hours* in the SB500 books and *months* in the SB4.5r. This card
follows **hours**: it is the reading of the later printings, and the SB2.5r ties the re-check to the first
click, which comes early. Check the pedals after the first few sessions; a click or wobble at any time is
the cue to re-tighten, whichever book you own. The SB4500 prints the noise rule but no re-tighten interval
at all.

The SB2.5r has no General Maintenance page; its only care sentence is the one on the pedal step above.

Other brands print the same rule for their bikes - Spirit's commercial uprights
(`cu900ent-general-maintenance`) and Sole's LCR and R92 (`lcr-2026-noise-when-pedaling`,
`r92-2026-noise-troubleshooting`). Different brand, separate cards.
