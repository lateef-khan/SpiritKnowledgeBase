**These four machines have 2 leveling pads under the rear stabilizer, and their list runs to four
causes, not two.** No other Spirit machine prints this combination.

1. **Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each workout.**
2. If a squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling develops, the main cause is most likely one of
   several reasons:
   - **The hardware was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.** All bolts installed during
     assembly need to be tightened as much as possible. It may be necessary to use a larger wrench
     than the one provided if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently. The manual is emphatic:
     **90% of calls to the service department for noise issues can be traced to loose hardware.**
   - **Dirt build-up on the rear rails and polyurethane wheels.** The cleaning steps and the thin
     coat of lubricant that follows are
     `ce800-2013-maintenance-rear-rail-cleaning-and-lubrication`.
   - **The crank arm nut needs to be retightened.**
   - If squeaks or other noises persist, check that the unit is properly leveled. There are
     **2 leveling pads on the bottom of the rear stabilizer**; use a **14mm wrench** (or adjustable
     wrench) to adjust the levelers.

The manual writes the lead-in as "one of **several** reasons" and then numbers four items, the
fourth of which is the levelling check rather than a cause. Earlier Spirit books say "one of two
reasons" over the same kind of list.

## Every neighbouring Spirit list drops one of these four items

- The **XE795 2021** February and November printings drop the **rail cleaning** and keep the crank
  arm nut, giving three items and the same 2 pads -
  `ct800-2016-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`. That card also covers the CE800 2021/2023,
  CE800ENT, CE850 and the CT800 2016 and CT850 2018 treadmills.
- The **XE100/XE200/XE300**, **XE395 2010** and **CE800 2013** drop the **crank arm nut**, keep the
  rail cleaning, and give **4** leveling pads under the **rear rails** -
  `ce800-2013-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`.
- The **CE900 2021 and 2025** give **6** pads on the bottom of the machine and no crank arm nut -
  `spirit-ce900-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`.

The wrench is 14mm on all of them. The pad count is not: 2, 4 and 6 are three different machines'
figures, and quoting the wrong one sends an owner under the wrong end of the frame.

## The XG400 is not an elliptical

Its manual cover reads **XG400 E-Glide Trainer**. This maintenance list is printed for it word for
word, and the body above says "the unit" and "the machine" because the XG400 book does. Do not
describe an XG400 to a customer as an elliptical.

## The 2023 rewrite deletes this list

The **2023 XE395 and XE795** manuals carry no noise section, no rail cleaning, no lubricant and no
leveler count. Their whole maintenance chapter is a four-step post-workout routine -
`spirit-2026t-maintenance-post-workout-machine-care`. The **XE150/XE350/XE550** manual prints no
maintenance chapter at all - `spirit-xe-maintenance-manual-prints-no-maintenance-section`.
