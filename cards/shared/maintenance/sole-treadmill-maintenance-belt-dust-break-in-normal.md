**BELT DUST occurs during normal break-in, until the belt stabilizes.** Black dust from the belt appearing on the floor behind the treadmill is normal.

This is running-belt dust, not the drive-belt break-in residue: black particles collecting *under* the machine are the drive belt breaking in (linked card). Both are normal on a new machine, neither is a fault.
