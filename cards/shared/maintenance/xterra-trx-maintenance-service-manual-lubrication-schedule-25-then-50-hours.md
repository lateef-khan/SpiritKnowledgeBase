**The service manual and the owner's manual print two different schedules for the same deck. This card is the service manual's; the owner's manual's 80-hour figure is `xterra-trx-maintenance-lubrication-interval-every-80-hours`.** Neither book mentions the other.

IMPORTANT: ONLY USE HALF THE BOTTLE OF LUBRICANT PER APPLICATION.

Lubrication schedule:

1. **After the first 25 hours of use (2-3 months)**, apply one half bottle of lubricant.
2. **Every 50 hours of use (5-8 months)**, apply one half bottle of lubricant.

Between applications the book's own check applies - lift one side of the belt and feel the treadboard; lubricate if it is dry to the touch: `xterra-trx-maintenance-service-manual-lubrication-half-bottle-under-the-centre-of-the-belt`. The TRX3500/TRX4500 book prints this page as a picture; it was read from the render. The Spirit XT185 2015 service manual prints the same schedule (`xt185-2015-maintenance-lubrication-schedule-25-then-50-hours`), another brand.
