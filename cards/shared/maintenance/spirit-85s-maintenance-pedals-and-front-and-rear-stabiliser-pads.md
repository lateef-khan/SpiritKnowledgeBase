**These two machines carry a different levelling figure from every other Spirit stepper: 2 pads
under the front stabiliser and 2 under the rear.** Do not read "2 pads on the rear stabilizer" from
a CS800 or a 7.0S onto them, and note that the pedals are named alongside the crank arm nut here.

If a squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling develops, the main cause is most likely one of two
reasons:

1. **The hardware was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.** All bolts that were installed
   during assembly need to be tightened as much as possible. It may be necessary to use a larger
   wrench than the one provided if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently. **90% of calls to the
   service department for noise issues can be traced to loose hardware.**
2. **The crank arm nut and/or the pedals need to be retightened.**
3. If squeaks or other noises persist, check that the unit is properly levelled. There are **2
   levelling pads on the bottom of the front and rear stabiliser**. Use a **14 mm wrench** (or an
   adjustable wrench) to loosen the top nut of the levellers, adjust as necessary to remove any
   wobble, then tighten all the top nuts against the bottom of the stabiliser tubes. **Make sure the
   bottom nut remains cinched against the levelling foot.**
4. To preserve the condition of your warranty, make sure that **all other normal maintenance and
   repair procedures are performed by an authorised and qualified technician.** Contact your local
   dealer support with any questions.

The lead-in still says "one of **two** reasons" over a list of four.

## The same book gives a different wrench two pages earlier

The setup chapter's *Leveling the 8.5S* says to use a **½" wrench** to loosen the top nut of the
leveller and then adjust **by hand**; this page says **14 mm**. A ½" spanner is 12.7 mm, so the two
are not interchangeable on a nut that fits one of them. The setup chapter gives no pad count at all,
so the count on this page is the only one printed in either book. **Quote both sizes and let the
customer take a wrench of each.** The 7.0S and 7.5S carry a worse version of the same disagreement —
`spirit-70s-75s-maintenance-leveller-count-and-wrench-disagree`.

## Item 2 is a carry-over; item 4 is not

Neither an 8.5S nor an 8.5S-FIT has a **crank arm** — the phrase occurs nowhere else in either
book. Send the customer to the **pedals**, which they do have, and stop there.

Item 4 is real and worth repeating: this is the only Spirit stepper page that ties the warranty to
having other work done by a qualified technician.
