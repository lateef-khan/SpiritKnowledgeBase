**These machines have 6 leveling pads on the bottom of the machine.** The CE800 2021/2023, CE800ENT
and CE850 manuals give **2**, under the rear stabiliser, and the 2013 CE800 gives **4**, under the
rear rails. The wrench is 14mm on all of them.

Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each workout. If a squeak, thump,
clicking or rough feeling develops the main cause is most likely one of two reasons:

1. **The hardware was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.** All bolts installed during
   assembly need to be tightened as much as possible. It may be necessary to use a larger wrench
   than the one provided if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently. **90% of calls to the
   service department for noise issues can be traced to loose hardware.**
2. **Dirt build-up on the rear rails and polyurethane wheels.** See
   `spirit-ce900-maintenance-track-and-wheel-cleaning`.

If squeaks or other noises persist, check that the unit is properly leveled. There are **6 leveling
pads on the bottom of the machine**; use a **14mm wrench** (or adjustable wrench) to adjust the
levelers.

## The second reason is not the crank arm nut here

The CE800 2021/2023, CE800ENT and all three CE850 manuals give **"the crank arm nut needs to be
retightened"** as reason 2 and print no rail cleaning
(`ct800-2016-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`). Neither CE900 **owner's** manual mentions a
crank arm nut anywhere. Do not send a CE800 or CE850 owner to the tracks.

## The CE900 2021 service manual disagrees with its own owner's manual

The **CE900 2021 service manual** (SE8800-SE026) prints the *other* version of this list under
GENERAL MAINTENANCE: reason 2 is **the crank arm nut and/or the pedals need to be retightened**,
there is no track-cleaning sentence, and the levelling check reads **"2 leveling pads on the bottom
of the rear stabilizer"** with the same 14 mm wrench. The CE900ENT and CE1000ENT 2023 service manuals
print it identically. That block is carded with the other 2-pad machines at
`ct800-2016-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`, and its leveling-foot procedure speaks of four
feet. So for the CE900 2021 the pad count is **6 in the owner's manual, 2 on the service manual's
maintenance page and 4 in its replacement procedure**, and the crank arm nut is named in one book
and not the other. Neither book explains the other. The track cleaning above stands on its own -
the service manual also says to clean the aluminium rails with alcohol
(`spirit-ce900-maintenance-track-and-wheel-cleaning`).

The CE900 2025 manual also carries the four-step post-workout routine
(`spirit-2026t-maintenance-post-workout-machine-care`); the 2021 manual does not.
