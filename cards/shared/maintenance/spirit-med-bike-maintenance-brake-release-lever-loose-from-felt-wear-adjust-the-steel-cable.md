**The lever loosens because the felt wears, and the cure is the cable, not the lever.** As printed:

> Usually, the tension of release lever becomes loose because of the abrasion of felt. You could use the steel
> cable to adjust the tension.

1. Loosen the **2 screws** on the cover of the mechanical brake (on the 7.0R, on its right side) and remove the
   cover (figure 3.4.1). The 7.0U calls it the steel cover.
2. **Adjust the distance of the steel cable** if it feels too loose for the release lever (figure 3.4.2).

The book gives no figure for the cable travel and no test beyond the feel of the lever: shorten the cable until the
lever bites, refit the cover. The felt is the brake pad - a **wool felt** part on the 7.0R parts list - and once it
is too thin to be taken up on the cable it is a replacement, not an adjustment.

**This is the manual brake a therapist uses to stop the flywheel, not the resistance.** Pedal resistance on
these bikes is an induction brake set by the console; a resistance complaint is answered from the electronic
troubleshooting chapter, not from here.

**The 8.5R answers the same complaint the other way round** - a brake that sticks because its **M5 × 40 mm** screw
is too tight, loosened first and only then the cable adjusted
(`85r-2025-maintenance-emergency-stop-screw-too-tight-or-adjust-the-cable`). Do not read one onto the other.
