Dirt build-up on the rear rails and the polyurethane wheels that ride on them makes a thumping sound that
carries through the frame tubing and seems to come from inside the machine. The cure, as printed:

1. **Clean the rails and wheels with a lint-free cloth and rubbing alcohol.**
2. **Stubborn build-up** can be removed with your thumbnail or a **non-metallic scraper, like the back edge
   of a plastic knife.**
3. After cleaning, **apply a small amount of lubricant on the rails** with your fingers or a lint-free cloth.
   **You only need a thin coat of lubrication; wipe off any excess.**

**No lubricant is named** - not a type, not a brand, not a part number - and no interval is given. The
instruction is triggered by noise, not by a schedule.

The FS3.0 book mentions dirty rear rails as a cause of noise but never prints these steps
(`xterra-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking-from-loose-hardware-dirty-rails-or-leveling`). The RSX1500 is
a seated stepper carrying the elliptical wording unchanged.

Spirit and Sole print the same procedure for their ellipticals
(`ce800-2013-maintenance-rear-rail-cleaning-and-lubrication`, `e95-2026-rear-rail-and-wheel-cleaning`).
Other brand, separate cards.
