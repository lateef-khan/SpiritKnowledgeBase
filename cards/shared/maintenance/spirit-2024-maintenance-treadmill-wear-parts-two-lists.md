**The same warning is printed twice in each of these four manuals, three pages apart, with two
different component lists. Neither page acknowledges the other.**

The warning itself is identical both times: *"The effect that the safety level of the equipment can
be maintained only if it is examined regularly for damage and wear."*

**On the maintenance page (printed p. 37, or p. 53 on the ENT books)** the list is five items and
it is a treadmill's:

> Belt, **Idler**, **Roller**, **Deck**, **Tread-belt**

**On the sanitizing page (printed p. 40, or p. 56)** the list is six items and it is not:

> Belt, **PU wheel**, **Bearing**, Idler, **Shaft**, **Pedal**

A CT800 or CT850 has no PU wheel, no pedal and no shaft in the sense those words carry on a bike or
an elliptical. The six-item list is Spirit's company-wide wording, printed unchanged on the
sanitizing page of the CT900, CTSBS900 and CT900ENT as well (`ct900-sanitizing-equipment`), and a
third, four-item version — Belt, PU wheel, Bearing, Idler — is in the CU900ENT service manual
(`cu900ent-wear-parts-inspection`).

**Answer a treadmill wear question from the five-item list.** It is the one printed on the
treadmill's own maintenance page and the only one whose parts all exist on the machine. Say the book
prints a second list if the customer has found it; do not merge the two.

## The maintenance page adds what to do about it

The same WARNING block continues: servicing and adjustments other than those described in the manual
must not be attempted, and **opening the motor cover must be left to trained service personnel**
familiar with electromechanical equipment and authorized under the laws of the country in question
to carry out maintenance and repair work.

The sanitizing-page version instead adds *"Replace defective components immediately and/or keep the
equipment out of use until repair"* — which the maintenance-page version does not say.

## No interval and no wear limit

**Neither list carries an inspection interval or a wear limit for any part.** The eighteen-row
schedule table is where the intervals live, and it names four of the five: **Deck** biannually,
**Drive Belt** biannually, **Front / Rear Roller** biannually and **Belt Tension / Tracking**
weekly. It has **no row for the idler** (`ct900-maintenance-schedule`).

## None of the other ten 2024 manuals prints this warning

Checked for "susceptible" across all fourteen New Black Units sources: the four treadmills print it,
and the CE800, CE850, CE800ENT, CR800, CR800ENT, CU800, CRS800S, CS800, CSC900 and CRW800 do not —
not on their maintenance page and not beside their sanitizing bullets, which those ten print without
any warning attached. There is no wear-parts list to quote for those ten machines.
