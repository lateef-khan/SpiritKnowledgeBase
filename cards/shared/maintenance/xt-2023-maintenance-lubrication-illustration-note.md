A caption printed beside the hardware illustration on the tracking and belt-adjustment pages reads:

> Use Lubricant (58) to lubricate running deck beneath running belt after using for 2 to 3 months. Only a few droplets are sufficient for lubrication. Avoid applying too much lubricant as this may catch dirt and affect electronic control system.

The item number in that caption is **58** on the XT185 and XT285 and **30** on the XT385 and XT485; the wording is otherwise identical.

**This is the second half of a fact stated twice, and the two halves disagree.** The DECK LUBRICATION section of the same manual gives the interval as **every 90 days, after 90 hours of use, or earlier if the deck is dry or dirty**, and its procedure applies **half a bottle down each side**. This caption gives **2 to 3 months** and **a few droplets**. Neither section refers to the other and neither says which is right. See `xt-2023-maintenance-deck-lubrication-interval` and `xt-2023-maintenance-deck-lubrication-procedure`.

The one instruction that appears only here is the warning that **too much lubricant catches dirt and can affect the electronic control system.**
