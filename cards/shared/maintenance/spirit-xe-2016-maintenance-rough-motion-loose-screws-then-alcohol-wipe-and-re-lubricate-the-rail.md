**This is the service manuals' answer to a rough feel, and it ends with lubricant on the rail. The
owner's-manual cleaning routine (`ce800-2013-maintenance-rear-rail-cleaning-and-lubrication`) is a
different page - lint-free cloth, a non-metallic scraper, a thin coat - and the CE900 manuals clean
their tracks with alcohol and add no lubricant at all
(`spirit-ce900-maintenance-track-and-wheel-cleaning`).**

1. **Check for loose screws if the machine motion feels unsmooth.**
2. **Check whether there are foreign objects on or around the sliding rail. Use an alcohol wipe to
   remove them and re-lubricate with lubricant.**

Two sentences; no lubricant is named and no interval is given. On the CE850 and XE895 the "sliding
rail" is the aluminium track of the adjustable-stride pedal base, and the same books tell you to
clean that track and its 38 mm urethane wheel together
(`spirit-ce850-2016-maintenance-pedal-bases-clean-and-lubricate-the-track-and-38-mm-slide-wheel`).

## The CE800 (2016) wording

The XE890B book: "Check tightness of screws and bolts if the unit **clicks**. Otherwise, there might
be foreign materials on either the **rail or Slide Wheel**. Use **rubbing alcohol** to wipe and apply
some lubricant both parts." Same two steps; it names the slide wheel as well as the rail.

## The 2023 XE795 book dropped the item

The XE795 (2023) service manual's Common Problems chapter runs belt, side cases, flywheel, swing
arm, connecting arm, shaking - and stops. It prints no smooth-motion item, no alcohol and no rail
lubricant anywhere. Its 2016 predecessor does.

The Sole E25 (2019) service manual carries the same two sentences (`e25-2019-rough-feel`).
