**This is the 2007 dealer-manual figure: about 1/16 of an inch. The later CT800 and XT service manuals say "less than 3 mm" and the owner's manuals give no figure at all.**

1. Take off the motor hood.
2. The speed sensor is located on the frame, near where the drive belt goes around the front roller.
3. The sensor should be adjusted to **about 1/16th of an inch from the front roller**.

That is the whole of Procedure 3. The manual does not say how the sensor is held or which way it moves; the later books add that a screw holds it and that it must line up with the magnet on the pulley (`xt-2023-maintenance-adjusting-the-speed-sensor`, `spirit-ct800-maintenance-speed-sensor-alignment`).

## When the manual sends you here

The Troubleshooting Guide names this procedure for two symptoms:

- **LS error after the belt moves for a few seconds** — run calibration, adjust the speed sensor, check the roller for its magnet, check the wire harness for continuity, and if the roller has a magnet and the adjustment does not fix it, replace the speed sensor.
- **Belt surging, speeding up and slowing down** — adjust torque boost first (`spirit-xt-2007-maintenance-adjusting-torque-boost`), then the speed sensor, then replace the controller.

Both symptom lists are troubleshooting rows and are carded there; this card is the adjustment itself.

## One book, five machines

The XT175, XT275, XT375, XT475 and XT675 share this dealer manual and it prints the figure once for all five. 1/16 inch is about 1.6 mm — well inside the 3 mm ceiling the 2015 and 2023 service manuals print for the XT185 through XT685ENT, but it is a different number from a different book, and the two figures should not be swapped.
