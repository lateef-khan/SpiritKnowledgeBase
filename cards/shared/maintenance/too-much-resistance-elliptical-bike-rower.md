**This card is for resistance stuck high.** For no resistance at all, see `sole-no-resistance-elliptical-bike-rower`.

1. Ask whether the level on the console changes when the buttons are pressed.
   - **Yes** - replace the **Steel Cable**.
   - **No** - replace the **Console Assembly**.

**On an elliptical, the level handle wire is a second suspect.** Disconnect the Level handle wire from the console and try the machine again. If it now works, replace the **Upper Resistance Wire**, the **Lower Resistance/Incline Wire**, the **Handpulse Thin Film Label** and the **Level Label**. If it still does not work, replace the **Console Assembly**.

**Machines without a gear motor are not covered.** Spin bikes that set resistance with a knob or an EMS brake, such as the sb700, sb900 and sb1200, are built differently.
