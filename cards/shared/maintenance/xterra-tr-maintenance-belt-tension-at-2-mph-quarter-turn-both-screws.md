Adjust the belt tension if it begins to slip on the rollers; this increases the longevity of the treadmill's components. The page opens with "Before adjusting belt tension, keep the belt loose".

1. Turn the **MASTER POWER SWITCH** to ON and make sure the safety key is pressed in.
2. Press START to start running the belt, then increase speed to **2 MPH**.
3. Turn **both adjustment screws an equal amount, approximately one-quarter turn clockwise**.
4. Slow the belt by holding onto the handrails and applying more weight as you walk (as if walking downhill), then step heavily on the belt to see if it slips.
5. If the belt slips, adjust **one-quarter turn clockwise** and repeat step 4 if necessary.

**ALWAYS BE CAREFUL NOT TO OVERTIGHTEN THE RUNNING BELT.**

The page does not say where the screws are or what tool fits them; the tracking page of each book does (`tr95h-2024-maintenance-belt-tracking-rear-bolts-opposite-quarter-turns-at-2-mph`, `tr75h-2025-maintenance-belt-tracking-opposite-quarter-turns-at-1-9-mph`). The care schedule checks "if running belt is slipping" daily before use (`xterra-tr-maintenance-care-schedule-daily-to-six-monthly`). The other Xterra books tension with a 6 mm Allen wrench and no speed: `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-belt-tension-quarter-turn-with-6-mm-allen-wrench`.
