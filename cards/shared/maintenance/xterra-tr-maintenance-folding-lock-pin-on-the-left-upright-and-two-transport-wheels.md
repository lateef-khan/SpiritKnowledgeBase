**CAUTION: do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and locked position.** Remove the power cord from the front of the unit to avoid possible damage. Use both handrails to manoeuvre the unit to the desired position.

**To fold**: pull the **Lock Pin** outward and lift the running deck until it is secured by the locking mechanism on the **left upright tube**. You will hear it pop into place when secure.

**To unfold**: apply slight forward pressure on the running deck with your **right hand**. Pull the Lock Pin outward and slowly lower the running deck to the floor.

**To move it**: the treadmill has **two transport wheels that are engaged when the treadmill is folded**. After folding, simply roll the treadmill away.

The TRX1000 and TR260 latch on a round knob instead and are leaned back onto their wheels (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-folding-round-knob-on-the-left-upright-lean-back-on-two-wheels`). The manual incline setting printed on the same page of these books (a pop pin on the TR150, a rotating foot pad on the TR200) is an operating feature, not maintenance.
