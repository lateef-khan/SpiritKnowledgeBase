The belt and deck come **pre-lubricated**. After that, as printed:

- **TR150 and TR200**: lubricate **every 40 hours of use, or if you notice that the deck is dry**. Reach between the belt and deck to verify there is lubrication present **every other month**; if there is none, lubricate.
- **TR260**: belt lubrication and cleaning **every 40 hours of use, or earlier if you notice that the deck is dry or dirty**; check belt lubrication before first use.
- **TRX1000**: the preventative maintenance chart says **every 40 hours or sooner if dry** - but the lubrication page of the same book says **every 80 hours**. The two figures sit on different pages and neither mentions the other. The 80-hour side is `xterra-trx-maintenance-lubrication-interval-every-80-hours`.

The chart in all four books repeats the 40 hours (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-chart-lubricate-deck-every-40-hours`). Keeping the deck lubricated at the recommended intervals ensures the longest life possible; if the lubricant dries out, friction between belt and deck rises and stresses the drive motor, drive belt and electronic motor control board, which could result in failure of those components. **Failure to lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty.**

The procedures: `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-deck-lubrication-s-line-18-inches-from-the-motor-cover` (TR150, TR200) and `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-deck-lubrication-half-bottle-each-side-1-foot-from-the-motor-cover` (TR260, TRX1000). The console's LUBE reminder and how to clear it belong to the console cards.
