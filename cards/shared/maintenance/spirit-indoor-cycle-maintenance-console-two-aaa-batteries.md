1. Remove the **battery cover from the back of the computer** (on the CIC850, from the back of the
   **CONSOLE (110)**).
2. Insert **2 AAA batteries** into the battery compartment and reinstall the battery cover.
3. When the battery working voltage is too low, the **"Low battery" indicator** shows up on the
   console display, indicating it is time to change them.

**Both XIC600 printings name AAA.** The 2018 and the 2021 print this page word for word; there is no
version of the XIC600 manual that leaves the cell type unstated.

## Not the same as the speed sensor transmitter

The XIC600 has a **second** set of 2 AAA cells in the speed sensor transmitter, and changing either
set forces a re-synchronisation —
`spirit-indoor-cycle-speed-transmitter-two-aaa-batteries`. The CIC850's speed sensor takes a
different route again, behind the left chain guard, and its manual **never names the cell type** —
`cic850-2022-maintenance-sensor-board-battery-behind-left-chain-guard`.

## Not the air bikes, and not a Sole cycle

The AB900 and AB950 consoles take **4 AA** cells, not 2 AAA —
`spirit-bike-console-air-bike-four-aa-batteries`. The CB900, CIC800 and JB950 have **no battery to
change at all** — `spirit-bike-maintenance-no-battery-on-three-cycles`.

Sole's SB700 and SB900 indoor cycle manuals print this same procedure word for word for **Sole**
machines (`sole-spinner-console-aaa-batteries`). That is a different brand: the two cards state the
same steps and neither covers the other's machines.
