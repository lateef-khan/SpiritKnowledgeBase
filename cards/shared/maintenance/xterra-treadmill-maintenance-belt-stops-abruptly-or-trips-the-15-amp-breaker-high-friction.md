Two rows of the diagnosis guide share one cause, **high belt/deck friction**, and the remedy is deck care.

| Problem | TR300, TR6.6, TR6.4, TRX1400 | TR65, TR75, TR85 |
|---|---|---|
| Tread-belt stops quickly/suddenly when the tether cord is pulled | See the maintenance section on cleaning the deck. If cleaning doesn't prevent this from reoccurring, check for significant wear of the deck. **If so, the deck may need to be flipped if it is on its original side.** | See Belt and Deck Cleaning and Deck Lubrication on cleaning the deck. |
| Treadmill trips on board 15 amp circuit | See the maintenance section. If cleaning doesn't prevent this from reoccurring, **check the amp draw of the motor**. If it is high and there are signs of significant wear of the deck, it may need to be flipped if it is on its original side. | See Belt and Deck Cleaning and Deck Lubrication on cleaning the deck. |

The TRX1400 service manual gives the breaker row alone: "High belt/deck friction. See General Maintenance section on Belt/Deck Lubrication." No Xterra book prints a deck-flipping procedure or a deck-life figure; the flip is mentioned only here.

The cleaning and lubrication these rows point to: `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-cleaning-between-belt-and-deck-with-a-cloth`, `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-deck-lubrication-s-line-18-inches-from-the-motor-cover`. A breaker that trips in the **house** panel rather than on the treadmill is a different row and an electrical matter.
