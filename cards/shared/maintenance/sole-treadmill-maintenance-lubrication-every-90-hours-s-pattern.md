The belt and deck come pre-lubricated. After that, lubricate **every 90 hours of use**, or sooner if you notice the deck is dry. **Reach between the belt and deck every other month** to check that lubricant is present; if there is none, lubricate even though the console "Lube" indicator is not lit. Otherwise lubricate when the console reminder lights after 90 hours.

**Use only Sole-approved lubricant.** The treadmill comes with one tube of "Lube"; extra tubes and a Lube-n-Walk kit can be ordered from Sole or an authorised dealer. **Failure to lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty**: dry lubricant raises the friction between belt and deck and stresses the drive motor, drive belt and electronic motor control board to the point of catastrophic failure.

First clean between the belt and deck: with a clean non-fraying rag, t-shirt or light towel, halfway between the end of the treadmill and the motor cover, push the garment under the belt until you can grasp it on both sides, then drag it the length of the belt 1-2 times and remove it.

1. Turn the power switch off and unplug the power cord.
2. Measure **18 inches** from the edge of the motor cover; kneel down and reach under the belt **4-6 inches** from one edge. Squirt a line of lubricant about **1/8 inch wide by 15 inches long in an "S" pattern**, perpendicular to the motor cover.
3. Repeat on the opposite side.
4. Plug the cord back in and turn the power switch on.
5. Walk on the belt at a moderate speed for **five minutes** to spread the silicone evenly.
6. If the "Lube" message is on the console, reset it through the engineering menu: press and hold **Start, Stop and Enter**, then insert the safety key and keep holding until the engineering menu shows; press Enter, use Speed 5 (Speed 6 goes backwards) to reach **Functions**, press Enter, then step to the **Maintenance** message and press Enter to reset it. Press Stop to exit.

The F60-2016 printing of this same procedure names **"FUEL Fitness approved lubricant"** and tells you to contact FUEL Fitness. That is stale sibling-brand boilerplate in an otherwise Sole book (Sole cover, Sole safety pages, Sole warranty); the card follows the book's own Sole identity and the procedure, which matches the other books word for word.

The service manual for the F60-2016 prints a different schedule (half a bottle after 25 hours, then every 50 hours); see `f60-2016-belt-deck-lubrication`. This card follows the owner's manual. The TT9-2018 chart repeats this 90-hour rule but its deck is wax-coated and must not be lubricated; see the wax-deck card.

Two stencil printings of this same 90-hour schedule differ in the details. The **F85-2020/2021 touchscreen** books keep the schedule and the S-pattern steps but print no lube-message reset in the maintenance chapter (no engineering-menu reset; the reset lives in the console menus). The **TD80-2019** printing names **100% silicone treadmill lubricant**, orders tubes from **Dyaco Canada Inc.**, and likewise prints no reset step. This card otherwise follows the books word for word; the 2023 generation adds a 90-day trigger and has its own card.
