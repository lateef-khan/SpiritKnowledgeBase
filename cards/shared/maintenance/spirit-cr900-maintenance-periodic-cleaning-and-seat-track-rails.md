As printed — 1.3 Periodic Cleaning:

- **Wipe down all surfaces with a clean damp cloth.** Mild detergents may be used on most areas.
  **Do not use abrasives or caustic cleaning materials** unless otherwise indicated.
- **Regular cleaning of the aluminium seat track rails** (figures 3.3.1 and 3.3.2) for continued
  smooth seat operation and life of the wheels. Use a clean damp cloth with mild cleaners.
  **Rubbing alcohol may be used on the aluminium tracks for removing stubborn dirt.**

No interval is printed — 'periodic' and 'regular' are the manual's only words — and **no
lubricant** is named for the rails or the wheels. The rails are cleaned, not greased.

**The uprights have no rails and no 1.3.** The CU900 2018, CU900ENT and CU1000ENT 2023
preventative-maintenance chapters carry only the pedal and console-mast checks. The sweat-path
wipe after each workout, printed in every one of these books, is `cu900ent-general-maintenance`;
Spirit's sanitizing bullets are `ct900-sanitizing-equipment`.

Two other Spirit rail instructions differ from this one and should not be read onto a CR900: the
CE900 elliptical adds a non-metallic scraper for stubborn build-up
(`spirit-ce900-maintenance-track-and-wheel-cleaning`), and the 2012 CU800 owner's manual, carried
over from an elliptical, adds a thin coat of lubricant
(`cu800-2012-maintenance-squeak-rear-rails-and-four-levelers`).

**The CR1000ENT 2023 prints only the rail sentence.** Its 11.13, headed *Periodically Clean Iron Board* (a
translation slip for the aluminium rail), says "regular cleaning of aluminum seat track rails for continued smooth
seat operation and life of wheels" (figures 11.15, 11.16) and nothing else: no damp cloth, no mild detergent, no
abrasives warning and no rubbing alcohol. Quote the rail cleaning for it and not the cleaning agents.

A seat that shakes rather than sticks is a wheel-play adjustment —
`spirit-cr900-maintenance-shaking-handlebars-seat-wheels-levellers`.
