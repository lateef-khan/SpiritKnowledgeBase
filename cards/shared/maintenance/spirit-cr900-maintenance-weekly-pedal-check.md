**This is the weekly check.** The CU900 2018, CU900ENT and CU1000ENT 2023 books print the same two
directions but say only to check 'regularly' (`cu900ent-check-pedals`); the air bikes re-torque
their pedals after the first 10 hours and every 100 hours
(`spirit-air-bike-maintenance-pedal-retorque-10-and-100-hours`).

As printed — 1.1 Check Pedals are tight:

- **Check whether the pedals are tight every week** (figures 3.1.1 and 3.1.2).
- **The left pedal tightens counter-clockwise and the right pedal tightens clockwise.**

The pedal is one of the two things the same book names for a squeak, thump, click or rough
feeling — 'the crank arm nut and/or the pedals need to be retightened'
(`cu900ent-general-maintenance`) — and the first thing its chapter 7 says to check for a clicking
sound at the pedals (`spirit-cr900-maintenance-clicking-at-the-pedals`). On the CR900 the pedal
also closes the book's list of parts most susceptible to wear
(`spirit-cr900-cu900-maintenance-wear-parts-five-items`); the CR900ENT's list drops it
(`cu900ent-wear-parts-inspection`).

Removal is the reverse: the pedal/crank replacement step takes the right pedal off
counter-clockwise and the left pedal off clockwise with a 15 mm open-end wrench.

The **CR1000ENT 2023** recumbent - its service manual is filed under Rowers - prints the same weekly check
and the same two directions as its 11.11, and the same removal as its 12.14.
