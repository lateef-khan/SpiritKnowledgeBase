| Part | Recommended Action | Frequency | Cleaner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pedals | Ensure that pedals are tight in crank arms, that all screws on pedals are tight, and that the pedal straps are not frayed. | Before each Use | N/A |
| Frame | Wipe down using a clean, soft damp cloth | Daily | Water |
| Flywheel | Wipe down using a clean, soft damp cloth | Weekly | Water |
| Brake Pad | Check for wear | Monthly | N/A |

**There is no Lubricant column on this table.** Water is the only cleaner named, and nothing on
these three machines is oiled.

The two footnotes printed under the table — pedal internals are not serviceable, and using an
unspecified cleaner shortens the part's life — are
`spirit-indoor-cycle-maintenance-pedal-internals-and-approved-cleaners`.

## This is not the XIC600 table

The XIC600 prints a five-column version of the same four rows and its figures are different: the
flywheel is wiped with **WD-40 Spray** rather than water, and the brake pad is inspected **weekly**
for excessive wear *or dryness* and oiled with 3-IN-ONE or 10W oil —
`spirit-indoor-cycle-maintenance-schedule-wd40-and-weekly-brake-pad`. Do not quote WD-40 or a
weekly brake pad interval for a CB900, CIC800 or CIC850, and do not quote water for an XIC600.

It is not the commercial bike table either. The eleven-row Machine Care schedule on the CR900 2025,
CU900 2025 and CU800ENT 2024 is a different document —
`spirit-bike-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`.

## What else these three manuals ask of an owner

The CIC800 and CIC850 also print the sweat-path wipe-down and the squeak/level checks
(`cu900ent-general-maintenance`) and the sanitizing bullets (`ct900-sanitizing-equipment`). **The
CB900 prints neither** — this table is its entire maintenance chapter, two pages long.
