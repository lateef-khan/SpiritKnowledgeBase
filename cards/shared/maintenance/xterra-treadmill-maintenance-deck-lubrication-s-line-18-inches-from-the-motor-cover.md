1. Turn the power switch off and unplug the power cord from the wall outlet.
2. **Measure 18 inches from the edge of the motor cover**; kneel down and reach under the belt approximately **4-6 inches from one edge**. Squirt a line of lubricant about **1/8 inch wide by 15 inches long in an "S" pattern**, perpendicular to the motor cover.
3. Repeat the process on the opposite side.
4. Plug the electrical cord back into the outlet and turn the power switch on.
5. **Walk on the belt at a moderate speed for five minutes** to evenly distribute the silicone lube.

The older-layout books (TR150, TR200, TR300, TR6.6, TR6.4) print a cleaning step first - drag a rag between belt and deck (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-cleaning-between-belt-and-deck-with-a-cloth`) - and the TR300, TR6.6 and TR6.4 add a step 6 on resetting the console's Lube message, which is a console matter. Checking whether lubricant is present is the same in every book: machine off and unplugged, reach between the running belt and the top of the base.

How often is on the interval cards for each book: `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-lubrication-interval-every-40-hours`, `xterra-tr-maintenance-lubrication-interval-every-90-hours`, `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-lubrication-interval-every-90-days-or-90-hours`. The TRX1000, TR260 and TRX 2024 books lubricate with half a bottle per side instead (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-deck-lubrication-half-bottle-each-side-1-foot-from-the-motor-cover`), and the TR75H prints a second, different procedure in its own lubrication chapter (`tr75h-2025-maintenance-deck-lubrication-three-one-foot-lines-with-the-straw`). The Sole F80 book prints these same five steps.
