The treadmill uses a very high-efficient low-friction deck ("high-efficient" is the books' own spelling; the 2026 books print "high-efficiency" for the same deck). Performance is best when the deck is kept as clean as possible.

- With a **soft, damp cloth or paper towel**, wipe the edge of the belt and the area between the belt edge and the frame. Reach as far as practical directly under the belt edge.
- Do this **once a month** to extend belt and bed life.
- A **mild soap and water solution** with a **nylon scrub brush** cleans the top of the textured belt. **Allow to dry before using.**

These books print no "water only" restriction; the older books that do are covered with the water-only card (see the shared `f60-2016-belt-and-deck-cleaning` card, extended to those machines). Vacuuming intervals for these machines live on the vacuuming cards.

Contradiction ruled: the service-manual cards for the 2016+ machines say `Use water only'. The owners books carded here print no water-only restriction - a soft damp cloth or paper towel on the belt edge, reaching under it, with mild soap and a nylon scrub brush on the textured top, dried before use. This card follows the owners books. (The TD80 book additionally restricts its rubberized walking surface to water only and its computer surface to anti-static compounds; see the TD80 desk-care card. The ST90 slat books have their own wipe-weekly care card.)
