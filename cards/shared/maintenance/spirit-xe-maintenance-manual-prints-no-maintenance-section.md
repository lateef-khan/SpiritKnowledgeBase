**The XE150, XE350 and XE550 owner's manual has no maintenance chapter.** Its table of contents
goes from Operation of Your New Elliptical straight to the warranty. There is no post-workout
wipe-down, no noise or squeak section, no rail cleaning, no lubricant, no leveler count, no
sanitizing page and no service interval anywhere in the book.

This is an absence in the document, not a claim that the machines need no care. The manual simply
does not say.

**Do not answer an XE150, XE350 or XE550 question from another Spirit manual.** The books that do
carry a maintenance chapter give figures that differ between machines - 2, 4 or 6 leveling pads,
with and without a crank arm nut and with and without a rail lubricant - so there is no safe
default to borrow. The nearest sibling by wording is the 2007 XE100/XE200/XE300 manual
(`ce800-2013-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`,
`ce800-2013-maintenance-rear-rail-cleaning-and-lubrication`), which shares 64% of its text with
this book, but it is a different generation and its figures are not stated for these three
machines. Quote it only as what a comparable Spirit elliptical is told to do, never as this
manual's instruction.

The one maintenance-shaped instruction this manual does give sits in the **assembly** chapter: the
console mast bolts "should be tightened as much as you possibly can". Loose assembly hardware is
what every later Spirit manual blames for 90% of noise complaints, so it is the first thing to
check on these machines too.

**The warranty chapter still expects maintenance.** It excludes damage from "improper maintenance"
without describing what proper maintenance is - a gap in the source, recorded here rather than
filled in.
