**This is the pin-locked fold of the ERG220 and ERG500. The ERG700 folds on a release lever and a knob**
(`erg700-2022-maintenance-folding-release-lever-at-the-handlebar-and-rear-stabilizer-knob`), **and the
hinged-rail rowers have no pin at all**
(`xterra-erg-maintenance-storing-upright-raise-the-slide-rail-to-vertical-and-the-seat-slides-down`).

## ERG220, as printed

1. Locate the **Ball Head Pin (65)** on the Slide Rail (2) and remove it.
2. Take the Slide Rail (2) and move it into a vertical position.
   - CAUTION: **The Seat (38) will glide down when the Slide Rail (2) is raised.**
   - Take care to avoid the Rear Stabilizer (4) when moving the Slide Rail (2).
3. Once the rail is vertical, **re-insert the Ball Head Pin (65) into its slot on the Slide Rail (2).** This
   locks the rower in the folded position.
4. CAUTION: **The Slide Rail (2) will not stay in the vertical position before the Ball Head Pin (65) is
   inserted. Do not let go of it before it is secured or the Slide Rail (2) may fall.**

The same pin locks the rower **open**: the assembly step seats it in the lower slot on the main frame and rail
("lift center of frame to help line up locking pin holes"), and a sticker on the frame reads **"LOCKING PIN MUST
BE FULLY ENGAGED DURING USE AND WHEN FOLDED UP."** Unfolding is the reverse - pin out, rail down, pin into the
lower slot.

## ERG500, as printed

One sentence, under step 8 of the assembly chapter: **"To fold the machine for storage, the ball pin can be
removed and the slide rail can be lifted up. Remember to replace the pin."** The pin is No. 35, the same one
the assembly steps pull to unfold the main frame ("slightly lift up the main frame, make sure the ball pin holes
are lined up, insert ball pin"). The ERG500 book prints none of the ERG220's cautions and does not say whether
the rail stays up unpinned; treat it as the ERG220 says.

Neither book prints a moving instruction for the folded machine - the ERG220's is on the page before
(`xterra-rower-maintenance-moving-lift-the-rear-until-the-front-transport-wheels-touch-the-floor`) and the
ERG500 has none.
