**These two books name a 5 mm Allen wrench for tracking; every other Xterra book that prints this page names 6 mm** (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-belt-tracking-6-mm-allen-wrench-left-bolt-3-mph`). Both books name a 6 mm wrench for the tension bolts on the page before, so a reader has both sizes to hand.

It is normal for some belts to drift near one side, depending on a user's gait. **Adjust only if the belt continues to move toward one side during use.**

1. A **5 mm Allen wrench** is provided. Make tracking adjustments **on the left side bolt** only.
2. Set belt speed at **3 mph**.
3. If the belt is **too close to the left side**, turn the bolt **only a 1/4 turn to the right (clockwise)** and wait a few minutes for the belt to adjust itself. Continue in 1/4 turns until the belt stabilizes in the centre of the running deck.
4. If the belt is **too close to the right side**, turn the bolt **counter-clockwise**.

A small adjustment can make a dramatic difference which may not be apparent right away. Proper belt tracking is an owner responsibility common with all treadmills.

Every one of these pages closes with: **damage to the running belt resulting from improper tracking / tension adjustments is not covered under the XTERRA Fitness warranty**.
