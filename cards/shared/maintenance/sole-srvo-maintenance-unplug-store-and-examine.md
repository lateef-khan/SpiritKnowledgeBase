The rest of the SRVO book's GENERAL MAINTENANCE list, beside the two interval wipes (linked cards):

- **Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each workout** - on the shared interval card, not repeated here.
- **Make sure to unplug the machine after use.**
- **Store the machine in a cool and dry place.**
- **Examine the equipment regularly for damage** and have it repaired. The safety and integrity designed into the machine can only be maintained this way; it is the sole responsibility of the user, owner or facility operator to ensure regular maintenance is performed. **Worn or damaged components must be replaced immediately, or the equipment removed from service until the repair is made.**

No bolt schedule, upholstery step or guide-rod care is printed - unlike the generic strength-book boilerplate (the bench and rack books print only that boilerplate and no care section at all). The SRVO-2021 warranty's item 7 ("routine maintenance as specified in this manual") is satisfiable here, because this page actually specifies some.
