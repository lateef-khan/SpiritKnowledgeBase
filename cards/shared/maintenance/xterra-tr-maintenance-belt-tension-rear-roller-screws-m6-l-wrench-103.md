The whole of the printed "Belt Adjustments" text in these three books:

If the running belt slips while walking on it, adjust the tension of the running belt by turning the screws on the rear rollers of both sides with the **M6 L Allen Wrench (103)**, which is included in the hardware kit.

**During adjustment, while turning the rear rollers' screws, be sure to keep running belt tension balanced on both sides, otherwise the belt shifts from the higher-tension end to the other.**

**DO NOT OVERTIGHTEN** - over tightening will cause belt damage and premature bearing failure. If you tighten the belt a lot and it still slips, the problem could actually be the drive belt under the motor cover that connects the motor to the front roller; a loose drive belt feels like a loose walking belt. Tightening the motor belt should be done by a trained service person.

No quarter-turn rule, no walking test and **no tracking procedure** are printed for these machines (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-no-belt-tracking-procedure-printed`). The page was read from the render: the text layer also carries an unprinted block about an "L Allen Wrench (132)" and "a few droplets" of lubricant every 2 to 3 months, none of which is on the page. The longer procedure the other Xterra books print is `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-belt-tension-quarter-turn-with-6-mm-allen-wrench`.
