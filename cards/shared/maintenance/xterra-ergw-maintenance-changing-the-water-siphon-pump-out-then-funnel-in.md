**The ERG800W drains differently - a drum pump with the rower stood upright**
(`erg800w-2023-maintenance-changing-the-water-with-the-drum-pump-and-the-rower-stood-upright`). These four
books share one page, "How to Fill and Empty the Tank", and the machine stays on the floor.

The same four steps serve the first fill and every water change afterwards:

1. **Remove the fill plug** from the upper tank.
2. **To fill (Fig. A):** insert the funnel into the tank, then use a cup - or the siphon pump and a bucket - to
   fill it. Read the **water level gauge on the side of the tank** to reach the level you want.
3. **To empty (Fig. B):** place a bucket next to the rower and use the **siphon pump** to pump the water out
   of the tank into the bucket.
4. **Refit the fill plug** and wipe the excess water off the frame. The ERG750W says it as "dry the water on
   and around the table and adjustment knob with a dry cloth."

| Book | Fill plug | Upper tank | Funnel | Siphon pump |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ERG550W OM 2023 | No. 50 | Upper Tank Cover No. 24 | No. 90 | No. 91 |
| ERG600W OM 2021 | No. 33 | Upper Tank Cover No. 12 | No. 77 | No. 76 |
| ERG650W OM 2021 | No. 50 | Upper Tank No. 24 | No. 52 | No. 53 |
| ERG750W OM 2025 | "tank plug" (no number) | "upper tank" | water funnel | pump; the pack list calls the pair "Funnel + Pump (No. 132)" |

The ERG750W's gauge is a **"digital mark on the side of the tank"** read in litres; the other three read a
scale of 1 to 6. Which level to stop at, and the warranty consequence of going past the top mark, is
`xterra-ergw-maintenance-water-level-gauge-1-to-6-never-above-6` for the three older books and
`erg750w-2025-specs-water-tank-capacity-8-to-12-7-liters-min-standard-max` for the ERG750W.

**When to do it:** every six months with a tablet, or whenever the water stays cloudy
(`xterra-ergw-maintenance-water-purification-tablet-every-six-months`), and before storing the rower for
more than a month (`xterra-ergw-maintenance-empty-the-tank-before-storing-for-more-than-a-month`). What water
to put back in is machine-specific: tap water on the three older books, distilled or pure on the ERG750W.
The pumped-out water is not for drinking - dispose of it.

Spirit's CRW800H2O prints the same four steps with its own part numbers
(`crw800h2o-maintenance-filling-and-emptying-the-tank`). Other brand, separate card.
