As printed in both new-layout books:

> The front stabilizer has built-in transport wheels. **Stand at the front of the machine and lift it up**
> until the weight of the machine is transferred to the transport wheels. You can now easily move the
> machine to a new location.

That is the whole instruction. It differs from every other Xterra bike in the repository, which lift the
**rear** by the rear stabilizer to put the front wheels down
(`xterra-maintenance-moving-lift-the-rear-and-roll-on-the-front-transport-wheels`). The card follows the
SB240/SB600 books as written; nothing else in either book describes moving.

These two books print no leveling instruction and no "store folded" line; their care page is the
post-workout wipe-down and sanitizing routine (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-post-workout-care`).
