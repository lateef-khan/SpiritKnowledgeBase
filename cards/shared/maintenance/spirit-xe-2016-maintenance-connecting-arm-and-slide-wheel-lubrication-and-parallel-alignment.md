1. "The elliptical uses linked structure design, thus if there is a problem in the link it will
   affect other parts of the structure." Beyond replacing a bad part, **it is important to lubricate
   frequently** - the book advises lubricating during any maintenance or repair to head off later
   problems.
2. The main cause of a footing problem is the **sliding wheel not sliding smoothly and parallel on
   top of the Inclinable Rail Assembly**: the side Inclinable Rail Assembly and the Inclinable Rail
   Assembly are not parallel, or the side rail and the main frame bottom-bracket rotating sleeve do
   not meet at **90 degrees**.
3. Check that the left and right connecting arm and the left and right step are connected
   properly, that the sliding wheel turns through the correct rotation, and for slipping, bearing
   damage or unsecured connections causing noise. A bad **Rod End Bearing** is removed with a
   **#19 wrench** and replaced.
4. **Maintenance should start from the sliding wheels** - replace them or **add lubrication to the
   top of the sliding wheel arcs**. Check the main structure and its parallel parts, and make sure
   the fault is in a part and not in the assembly before replacing anything.

No lubricant is named and no interval is given.

## The 2023 XE795 keeps points 1 and 2 only

The XE795 (2023) service manual prints the lubrication advice and the parallel-rail paragraph and
stops there: no connection check, no wrench size, no "start from the sliding wheels". The 2016 XE795
prints all four.

## The two CE800 books

The **CE800 (2016)** book (XE890B) has the same four points in older wording. It sends the fish
eye bearing to a **#22 open-end wrench**, not #19, and its point 4 reads "During maintenance, start
from sliding wheels by applying lubricant on wheels or replace if necessary."

The **CE800 (2020)** book compresses it to three: a) all the joints matter and "when doing the
replacement also need to **apply some grease on those joints**"; b) the commonest cause of a
**vibrating pedal** is the slide wheels not sliding smoothly on the **aluminum track**, because the
pedal arm is not aligned with the track or not perpendicular to the mainframe housing; c) check the
connecting-arm to pedal-arm connection, then the **roundness of the slide wheels**, then that the
rod end bearing is tight and not defective. No wrench size.

The rail itself is wiped with alcohol and re-lubricated when the motion feels rough -
`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-rough-motion-loose-screws-then-alcohol-wipe-and-re-lubricate-the-rail`.
The owner's-manual cleaning routine for the rails is `ce800-2013-maintenance-rear-rail-cleaning-and-lubrication`.
The Sole E55 and E95S books carry the same Dyaco text (`e55-2016-connecting-arm-lubrication`,
`e95s-2023-connecting-arm-problems`).
