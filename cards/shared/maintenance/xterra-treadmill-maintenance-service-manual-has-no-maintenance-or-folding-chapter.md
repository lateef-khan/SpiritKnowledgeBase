**Five of the eight Xterra service manuals print no General Maintenance chapter and no Folding/Unfolding chapter.** The tension, tracking, cleaning, lubrication and folding procedures for these machines are in their owner's manuals only.

| Service manual | What its contents run to |
|---|---|
| TR150 (Dyaco GT65-NT014) | Outlines, electronic parts, wiring, safety, error messages E0-E9, circuit diagram, calibration, maintenance **menu** (a console menu, not a care chapter). Its troubleshooting matrix still says "see treadmill belt adjustment" and "see treadmill belt lubrication". |
| TR260 (Dyaco GT75A-NT050) | Outlines through error messages, calibration, a troubleshooting matrix with the same two pointers, then Disassembling and Assembling. Its Lube Message note points at "page 17 (OWNER'S MANUAL)". |
| TRX5500 (Dyaco GT90D-NT041) | Outlines through error messages, circuit diagram, Parts Replacement Guide. Nothing on belt care or folding; "folding treadmill" appears only as a step in motor and deck replacement. |
| TR95H (JKEXER 337) and TR75H (V1.0) | Assembly sequence, belt and deck replacement, wiring, PCB tables, error messages. Their only care sentence is a remedy in the E1 current-limit scenario: "add lubricant between the running deck and running belt". |

The three Dyaco books that do print both chapters are the TRX1400, TRX2500 and TRX3500/TRX4500 service manuals, and their figures often differ from the owner's manuals: `xterra-trx-maintenance-service-manual-wipe-monthly-and-motor-hood-yearly`, `xterra-trx-maintenance-service-manual-lubrication-schedule-25-then-50-hours`, `xterra-trx-maintenance-service-manual-folding-locking-knob-and-tilting-on-the-rear-wheels`, `trx1400-2023-maintenance-service-manual-tracking-10-mm-allen-wrench-3-to-5-kph`. The Spirit service manuals have the same gap on `spirit-ct-maintenance-no-general-maintenance-chapter-in-service-manual`.
