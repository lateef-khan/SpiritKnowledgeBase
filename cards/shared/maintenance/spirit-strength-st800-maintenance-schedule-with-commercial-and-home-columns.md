**The two unpowered ST800 machines are on a different template from the rest of the Spirit strength
range, and it asks the question the CS books never do: gym or home?**

| ROUTINE | COMMERCIAL | HOME | LATEST DATE ENTRY |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clean: Upholstery | Daily | Weekly | |
| Inspect: All Decals | Weekly | 3 Months | |
| Inspect: All Nuts and Bolts, Tighten if needed | Weekly | 3 Months | |
| Inspect: Anti-Skid Surface | Weekly | 3 Months | |
| Clean and Wax: All Glossy Finishes | 6 Months | Yearly | |

The page's own lead-in:

> For best performance, Spirit recommends the following maintenance schedule. **Check the integrity
> and function of the following parts. Replace all worn components immediately.**

## Three things this table has that no CS table has

- **Two intervals per row.** A commercial figure and a home figure, three to twelve times apart.
- **A blank column, *LATEST DATE ENTRY*.** The table is meant to be written on. It is the printed
  form of the repair-log obligation —
  `spirit-strength-maintenance-owner-responsibility-and-repair-log`.
- **A finish-care row.** *Clean and Wax: All Glossy Finishes*, six-monthly in a gym. No wax product
  is named.

## Five rows, and what is not on them

**No cable row, no belt row, no pulley row, no guide-rod row and no lubrication of any kind.**
Neither machine has a cable or a guide rod; one is a dumbbell rack and one is a flat/incline bench.
The word *lubricate* does not appear in either book.

**Do not read the ST800FT's cable and guide-rod rows onto these two.** That machine's table has
twelve routines and a yearly cable replacement:
`spirit-strength-st800ft-maintenance-schedule-of-twelve-routines`.

## The anti-skid surface row

The ST800FT's care notes explain what the row is for, and the wording applies to the same part:
*"These surfaces are designed to supply secure footing and need to be replaced if they appear worn
or become slippery."* **That sentence is printed in the ST800FT book, not in these two** — quote it
as the range's explanation, not as these machines' own text.

## These two do print a sanitizing page

Unlike the thirty-six CS strength manuals, the ST800 books print Spirit's four sanitizing bullets in
full on the next page — **75% isopropyl alcohol** on hard plastics, a conditioner after sanitizing
on upholstery. Word for word the company-wide text: `ct900-sanitizing-equipment`.
