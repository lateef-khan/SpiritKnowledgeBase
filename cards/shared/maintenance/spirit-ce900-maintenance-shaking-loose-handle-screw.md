**The elliptical books give shaking one check, at the handle. The CU900ENT bike's 7.3 splits it three
ways - armrest, seat, whole product (`cu900ent-shaking`) - and the residential XE books send you to
loose screws and the pedal carriage bolts
(`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-shaking-loose-screws-and-pedal-carriage-bolts`).**

7.3.1 as printed: "check whether the handle is loose and shake. Please **loosen** the screw if it is
loose. (See Procedure 6.2 for disassembly wobbles cover.)"

**"Loosen" here is a translation slip**: the remedy for a loose handle is to tighten its screw, and
the procedure it points to is the handlebar replacement, whose fixings are the **M10x60L (2 pcs) and
M10x55L (1 pc)** screws under the swing cover. Take the swing cover off, tighten those, and put the
cover back. The book prints no torque for them.

If the whole unit rocks on the floor rather than the handle moving, that is the levelling check of
the general maintenance list - two pads under the rear stabilizer with a 14 mm wrench
(`ct800-2016-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`) - and the leveling-foot procedure (6.18 / 12.91),
which turns the wheel counterclockwise and "adjusts the height of the **four** feet when not flat
until the balance". **The two pages of one book disagree on the count** - two pads under the rear
stabilizer on the maintenance page, four feet in the replacement procedure - and the CE900 owner's
manual prints six. Level whatever feet the machine in front of you has.
