Dirt, dust and hair can block air inlets and accumulate on the running belt.

- **On a monthly basis, vacuum underneath the treadmill** to prevent build-up of dirt that can get sucked up into the inner workings under the motor cover.
- **Every other month, remove the motor cover and carefully vacuum out** dirt and hair that may accumulate.
- **UNPLUG THE POWER CORD BEFORE THIS TASK.**

All nineteen books print these two intervals. The older layout (TR150, TR200, TRX1000, TR260, TR300, TR6.6, TR6.4) says "dirt, dust, and **pet** hair" and "remove the **black motor hood**"; the TRX 2024 books and the new-layout books (TRX1400, TR65, TR75, TR85, TR95H, TR75H, WS200, WS300) say "remove the motor cover". The TR95H and TR75H care schedule adds a **six-monthly** "open motor cover and vacuum all dust" row (`xterra-tr-maintenance-care-schedule-daily-to-six-monthly`), and the Dyaco service manuals say **once a year** (`xterra-trx-maintenance-service-manual-wipe-monthly-and-motor-hood-yearly`); the owner's-manual page itself never varies.
