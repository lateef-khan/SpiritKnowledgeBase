**Neither the 8.0U nor the 8.5R service manual prints a belt tension figure. The 7.0R and 7.0U of the same year
do - 220-240 N and 260-280 N - and those numbers must not be read onto these two.**

7.1.1, as printed on the 8.0U: "If the belt is slipping or shedding, please refer to 6.9, 6.10 and Figure 6.10.2 to
adjust tension by tightening nut." The 8.5R says the same with its own section numbers, 6.5 and 6.6.

What those sections show:

| | 8.0U (MU2000-SB036-01) | 8.5R (MR2000-SB036-01) |
|---|---|---|
| Drive pulley and belt | 6.9: remove the **C rings** on the crank axle to remove the drive pulley; slide the drive belt out of the idler wheel assembly | 6.5: the same two steps |
| Idler and flywheel | 6.10: remove **Phillips head screws (M5 × 3)** and **nuts (M8 × 2)** to remove the idler wheel assembly (figures 6.10.1, 6.10.2); two hex head bolts each side (1/4" × 4) hold the flywheel | 6.6: the same screws and nuts (figures 6.6.1, 6.6.2); the flywheel's four 1/4" screws |

So "tightening nut" means the **two M8 nuts on the idler wheel assembly** in figure 6.10.2 (8.0U) or 6.6.2
(8.5R): tighten them until the belt stops slipping. **No newton figure, no hertz figure and no gauge is named** -
the only torque in either book is the 60 N·m crank-arm nut. Tighten until the slip stops and no further; the
books give nothing else to aim at.

**The 8.5UE ergometer's service manual has no belt-slip section at all** and no tension figure either; its
chapter 7 is a wobble and a noise (`85ue-2025-maintenance-wobble-four-adjustment-feet-and-screws`).

A rough feeling underfoot is the next section, 7.2 - the flywheel sound in reverse is normal, then tighten the
crank arms and pedals (`cu900ent-noise-and-feet-feeling`).
