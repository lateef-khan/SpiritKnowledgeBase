This is the 180-hour chart. It differs from the extended 90-hour chart card only in the lubrication column; the other three columns match.

The chart is a blank log the owner fills in. Its four task columns and their intervals, as printed:

| Task | Interval |
|---|---|
| Vacuum under motor cover and check wiring | Every other month |
| Clean and inspect deck for lubrication | Every other month |
| Lubricate deck | **Every 180 hours** or sooner if dry |
| Inspect belt tracking, adjust if necessary | Monthly |

There is a fifth column headed **Date** for recording when each task was done. The general-cleaning paragraph separately asks for a **monthly** vacuum underneath the treadmill and the belt section for a **monthly** belt-edge wipe.
