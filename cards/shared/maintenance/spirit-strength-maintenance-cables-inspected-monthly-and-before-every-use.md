**This is the monthly figure, not the weekly one.** The two CSI machines are the only Spirit
strength machines whose schedule inspects belts and cables **monthly**. The other thirty-three
cabled machines in the range say **weekly**.

Two intervals are printed, and both apply:

1. **Monthly**, as a scheduled task — the *Inspect: Belt / Cables* row, ticked in the **Monthly**
   column alongside fasteners, handgrips, upholstery, labels and pulleys.
2. **Before every single use** — *"Inspect all cables and belts and connections prior to use.
   DO NOT use if any components are worn, frayed or damaged."*

## The interval got longer while the cleaning got shorter

On the CSI table the guide rods are **cleaned weekly** where the rest of the range cleans them
monthly, and the frame is **cleaned daily** where the rest cleans it weekly — but the cable
inspection went the other way, from weekly to monthly, and the pulleys came in from bi-monthly to
monthly. See `spirit-strength-maintenance-schedule-with-a-bi-annual-column` for the whole table.

**Do not answer a CSI cable question from the weekly card.** Weekly is stricter than what the
customer's book asks for, and a gym building a PM round from the wrong figure will not match its
own documentation.

## No replacement interval here either

Like the rest of the Spirit strength range, these books give **no service life for a cable** in
months, hours or repetitions. The replacement trigger is the condition of the part:
`spirit-strength-maintenance-replace-a-frayed-cable-at-first-sign-of-wear`, which the CSI
SAFEGUARDS page prints word for word.

The **one** Spirit strength machine with a fixed cable replacement interval is the ST800FT
functional trainer — `spirit-strength-st800ft-maintenance-cables-inspected-daily-and-replaced-yearly`.
That is a different machine on a different template; do not read its yearly figure onto a CSI.
