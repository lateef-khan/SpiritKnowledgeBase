**It is a printed OEM defect, not a wrong manual.** The first sentence of the MACHINE CARE page reads, on the
Spirit-branded 8.0U:

> Regular cleaning and maintenance are essential to keep your **Dyaco** equipment (**8.0T, 8.5R, 8.0U, 8.5S,
> 8.5UE**) operating for their best for lasting years.

The 8.5R prints the same sentence with the list reordered - **8.0T, 8.0U, 8.5R, 8.5S, 8.5UE**. Each book names
itself among the five and four machines that are not it.

Two things are wrong with it:

- **Dyaco** is the manufacturer, not the brand on the machine. Dyaco International Inc. of Hemei Township,
  Changhua County, Taiwan builds these bikes; the page was written for Dyaco's own medical range and the name was
  not swapped out for Spirit.
- **Four of the five models listed are other machines.** The 8.0T is a rehabilitation treadmill, the 8.5S a seated
  stepper and the 8.5UE an upper body ergometer - all Spirit medical machines with their own manuals in this
  repository - and the other bike of the pair is the other bike.

The advice that follows the sentence **is** for the bike in hand and is safe to use - the loose-hardware, crank
arm nut, pedal and levelling checks (`spirit-85s-maintenance-pedals-and-front-and-rear-stabiliser-pads`). Read
past the list.

## What it does not mean

It does not mean the customer received the wrong book, or that an 8.0U is a rebadged 8.0T, and it is not grounds
for reading a treadmill or stepper figure onto a bike. The same authoring chain leaves other traces in the
chapter - a *manual mechanical brake lever* and a *saddle* in the cleaning lists, and a **tread belt cleaning**
row in the quarterly column of a bike with no tread belt
(`spirit-med-bike-maintenance-machine-care-schedule-after-each-use-to-quarterly`).

The 8.5S and 8.5UE books carry the same sentence with their own lists
(`spirit-85s-maintenance-care-page-names-dyaco-machines`, `85ue-2025-maintenance-care-page-names-dyaco-machines`).
