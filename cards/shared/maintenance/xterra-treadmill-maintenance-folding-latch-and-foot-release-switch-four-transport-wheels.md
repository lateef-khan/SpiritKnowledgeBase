**To fold**: lift the deck until the latch clicks in place.

**To unfold**: press the releasing switch with your foot and pull down the running deck. Gently lower the deck to the floor, supporting the deck with a hand.

**To move it**: the treadmill has **four transport wheels that are engaged when folded**. After folding, simply roll the unit away. **No need to tilt.**

The TRX1400 book prints the same page word for word; its text layer also carries an unprinted "T3 FOLDING INSTRUCTIONS" block ("press the releasing switch with foot and push down the deck", "Relocation: let the transport wheels touch the floor and roll to move") that the render does not show. The Spirit XT 2023 books print a near-twin with a yellow-sticker tube and a release lever; that is another brand (`xt-2023-assembly-folding-and-moving`).

**The TRX1400 service manual describes a different mechanism** - a locking knob, the deck lifted 30 cm, the treadmill tilted back on its rear wheels - on `xterra-trx-maintenance-service-manual-folding-locking-knob-and-tilting-on-the-rear-wheels`. Before first unfolding, a Velcro shipping strap has to come off with the treadmill flat on the floor; that is the pre-assembly page and belongs to the assembly cards. The post-workout routine says to store the machine folded when not in use (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-post-workout-care`).
