These machines do not fold; the whole transport instruction is one paragraph. That is the whole of what the books print.

**Carefully lift the treadmill at the rear roller area, grasping the two side end caps, and roll the treadmill away.**

Note the AS77-2012 trap: its table of contents advertises "Folding Instructions" on the printed page 12, but that page holds assembly steps 3 and 4; the only moving instruction the book actually prints is the lift paragraph above. The card follows what is printed, not the contents entry.
