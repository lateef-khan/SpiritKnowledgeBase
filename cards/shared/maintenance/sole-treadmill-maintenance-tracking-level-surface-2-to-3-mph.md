This is the older tracking text. It differs from the 3 mph card in three ways: the frame must first be level, the speed is a range, and an unloaded belt is expected to centre itself. The card follows this printing.

Performance depends on the frame standing on a **reasonably level surface**. If the frame is not level, the front and back rollers cannot run parallel and constant belt adjustment may be necessary.

The treadmill is designed to keep the treadbelt reasonably centred. **Some belts drifting near one side with nobody on the belt is normal**; after a few minutes of use the belt should tend to centre itself. Adjust only if the belt keeps moving toward one side **during use**.

1. A **6 mm Allen wrench** is provided for the rear roller. **Make tracking adjustments from the left side only.**
2. Set belt speed at approximately **2 to 3 mph**. A small adjustment can make a dramatic difference.
3. Turn the bolt only **a 1/4 turn** and wait a few minutes for the belt to adjust itself.
4. Keep making 1/4 turns until the belt stabilises in the centre of the running deck.

The belt may need periodic tracking adjustment depending on use and walking/running characteristics. Adjustments become less of a maintenance concern as the belt is used. **Proper belt tracking is an owner responsibility common with all treadmills.**

**ATTENTION: damage to the running belt resulting from improper tracking/tension adjustments is not covered under the Sole warranty.**
