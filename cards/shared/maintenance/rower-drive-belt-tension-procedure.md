The flywheel slides on its bracket. Loosen the mounting nuts, pull the flywheel away with the adjuster nut, then lock the mounting nuts again.

1. **Loosen the mounting nuts on both sides of the flywheel.** These are the nuts circled in blue in the source picture: the hex nut on the flywheel bracket bolt, where the bracket arm crosses the flywheel hub. There is one on each side.
2. **Tighten the adjuster nut** to pull the flywheel and tighten the drive belt. This is the nut circled in red: the smaller nut on the threaded adjuster rod, just above and outboard of the blue one.
3. **Tighten the mounting nuts on both sides of the flywheel** again.

**The tension figure is in a separate card.** Both service manuals name the same parts and the same target: tie up the **3/8" x UNF26 x 11T nut (82)**, adjust the **M6 x 6T nyloc nut (103)**, and set the **Drive Belt (6PJ-787L) (24)** to **70~80BLS** against the flywheel (23). See `sr500-2016-drive-belt-tension` and `sr550-2023-drive-belt-tension`. Read the blue nuts as part (82) and the red nut as part (103).
