**Do not apply any type of lubricant or wax to the surface.** The deck is a dual-sided hard wax
deck; it is finished at the factory and there is nothing to top up.

**No lubrication interval exists for this line.** None of the three 2026 manuals names a lubricant,
a quantity or an interval, and none of their maintenance schedules lists a lubrication task. The
same holds for the four 2024 commercial machines below.

## Where each manual prints it

- **4.0T** — in the manual's own Belt and deck text on page 43, alongside the deck life figure. See
  `40t-2026-maintenance-deck-life-and-flipping`.
- **7.0T (p. 41) and 8.0T (p. 63)** — the sentence appears only inside the duplicated "GENERAL
  MAINTENANCE" block that overlays the belt-and-deck page in both manuals. That block conflicts
  with the surrounding text on other figures (it prints an 8 mm wrench where the 7.0T's own text
  says 10 mm, and 3-5 kph where both machines' own text says 2-3 mph), so treat it as supporting
  evidence rather than as those manuals' own wording. Their own text is silent, and silence here
  means no lubricant is called for, not that one is.

## Do not carry a Sole procedure over

Sole treadmills of the same year take silicone between the belt and the deck on a stated interval.
Those figures are for Sole machines and must not be applied here — see `sole-lubricate-running-belt`.
The Spirit CT850 2016 manual contradicts itself on this point; `ct850-2016-tread-belt-lubrication`
records why.

## The four 2024 New Black Units treadmills print it in their own text

The **CT800 2024**, **CT850 2024**, **CT800ENT 2024** and **CT850ENT 2024** print
*"Do not apply any type of lubricant or wax to the surface"* on the visible page, at the end of the
deck-life sentence, not inside an overlay. Their eighteen-row schedule table lists **no lubrication
task** either (`ct900-maintenance-schedule`).

**Their deck-life figure is not the 2026 one.** Those four rate the deck at 20,000 *kilometres* a
side rather than 20,000 miles - `spirit-2024-maintenance-deck-life-20000-kilometres`. The
no-lubricant rule is the same on both; the mileage is not.
