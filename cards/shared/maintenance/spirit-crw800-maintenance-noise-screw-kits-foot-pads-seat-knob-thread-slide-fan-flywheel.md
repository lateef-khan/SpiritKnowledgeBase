**The CRW800 books print five causes; the XRW600 book prints four - it has no seat up/down adjustment knob, so item 3
is not there.** Everything else is word for word the same across the three service manuals.

"Please refer to the cause of noise to find out the problems. The main reasons and parts are below."

1. **Screw kits not fixed.** Check them.
2. **Check that all four Adjustment Foot Pads (59) are fixed to the ground**, and adjust each of their nuts.
3. *CRW800 2016 and 2021 only:* **Tighten the Seat Up/Down Adjustment Knob (47).** If it still makes a noise, **put
   some lubrication on the thread of its screw** - the figure circles the threaded stud under the seat that the knob
   screws onto. The book names no lubricant.
4. **The slide is noisy.** Clean the Aluminum Track Pulley (53), the Pulley (54) and the Aluminum Board (52). If that
   does not work, replace the Aluminum Track Pulley (53) and the Pulley (54) directly.
5. **The Fan (22) or Flywheel (23) is noisy.** Take off the Chain Cover (L) (71) and Chain Cover (R) (72) and check.

Item 3 is the only place a CRW800 book asks for a lubricant, and it is one unnamed dab on a knob thread; the seat
track and its pulleys are cleaned, not greased (`spirit-rower-maintenance-no-lubricant-named-anywhere`). On the
XRW600, part 47 is the locking knob that holds the rear stabilizer (9-10), not a seat knob, and the list gives it
nothing.

A slide that is stiff rather than noisy is the neighbouring Q&A (`spirit-crw800-maintenance-slide-not-smooth-clean-track-and-pulleys-loosen-three-nyloc-nuts`). The owner's-manual noise page for the
CRW800 2021 is a different, shorter text - loose hardware and two levelling pads with a 14 mm wrench
(`crw800-2021-maintenance-two-remedies-and-no-crank-arm-nut`) - and the XRW600 owner's manual prints the
three-remedy block (`ct800-2016-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`). This card is the service-manual list with
part numbers; the two agree on the foot pads and on tightening hardware first.
