**The life of the product is determined by how consistently the daily procedure is done.**

Dry the bike after each use to remove sweat and moisture. Use a **liquid non-abrasive cleaner
diluted with water**.

**Wipe down / cleaning:** to prevent the build-up of rust and other forms of corrosion, wipe the
bike down at the end of each day, or preferably **at the end of each class**. **Raise all posts to
the highest setting to expose moisture.** Using an absorbent cloth, give attention to:

- Handgrips
- Seat / adjustable slide for the seat
- Back leg assembly
- Guard(s)
- Pop-pins
- Leveling feet

**NOTE: Never use abrasive cleaning liquids or petroleum-based solvents when wiping down the bike.**

## This is not the indoor cycle routine

The CIC800, CIC850 and XIC600 give a one-line "wipe down all areas in the sweat path after each
workout" and then go straight to squeak and levelling checks
(`cu900ent-general-maintenance`). They name no posts to raise, no six-point list and no solvent
warning. These three machines are the only Spirit bikes in the repository that break service into
**Daily, Weekly and Monthly** activities as a stated regimen.

The AB950 additionally prints the four-step post-workout routine shared across the Spirit range —
`spirit-2026t-maintenance-post-workout-machine-care`. The AB900 and JB950 do not.

**Moving and levelling** is printed inside the GENERAL MAINTENANCE chapter on the AB900 and JB950,
and in the setup chapter on the AB950. It is carded once, as
`spirit-airbike-jb950-assembly-moving-and-levelling`: lift from the rear, roll on the front wheels,
and level on **four adjusters** under the front and rear legs.
