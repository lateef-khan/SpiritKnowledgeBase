**This is the hook-screw adjustment to 190 Hz. The CE800 (2020) book sets the same J-bolt nut to a
different printed figure (`ce800-2021-maintenance-belt-drops-off-or-slips-j-bolt-nut-13-mm`), and
the generator-driven CE900, CE900ENT and CE1000ENT have a pressure roller set to 280-310 N instead
(`spirit-ce900-maintenance-belt-slips-or-falls-off-pressure-roller-280-to-310-n`). Do not carry
a figure between the three.**

"If the Poly-V Belt is slipping, simply adjust the cap on the hook-type screw with the #13 wrench
to adjust. Refer to the disassembly procedures for adjustment."

The disassembly procedure it points at is the idler wheel replacement, and that is where the
figure lives:

- With the belt back on the drive pulley, **tighten the nyloc nuts M8x9T on top of the hook
  screws**.
- **Flick the belt for a crisp sound to check tightness, or use a sound measuring device to measure
  190 Hz (plus or minus 10).**

Nothing more is printed: no feel test beyond the flick, no turn count on the nut, no interval.

## What each book calls the part

The 2016 XE books call it the **hook-type screw** in the belt section and the **hook screws** in
the idler procedure. The CE800 (2016) book, titled XE890B, calls the same part the **J-bolt**:
"If the Belt slips, adjust the nut on J-bolt with 13 mm wrench." Its idler procedure then prints
the same nyloc nut, the same flick test and the same **190HZ (plus or minus 10)**. One part, two
names.

The CE850 (2016) and XE895 (2016) stride machines print the slipping sentence word for word and
set the belt in their flywheel procedure: "Use nylon nut (M8x9T) on screw shackle to tight up and
flick belt with a crisp sound or use sonic device measured at 190HZ (plus or minus 10), then turn
crank arm assembly to make belt and flywheel rotation smoothly." On those two the belt must also
sit **in the middle of the drive pulley** - if it runs to one side, the flange nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 11T
on the flywheel is released and the inner nut adjusted with a 17 mm wrench until it centres.

## The 2023 XE795 prints the figure but not the symptom

The XE795 (2023) service manual carries the same idler step - nyloc nuts M8x9T on the hook screws,
flick for a crisp sound, **190 Hz plus or minus 10** - but its belt section (10.1) stops after the
falling-off procedure and never says what to do when the belt slips. Quote the idler step for it.

## Why the belt loosens

The Q&A chapter of the 2016 books says it plainly: "most belts will become loose from use
depending on usage time. Solution will depend on weight and adjustment methods" - user weight and
hours are the two factors, and the answer is this adjustment
(`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-slip-check-four-joints-500-to-600-and-550-to-600-lbs`).

The Sole E25, E35, E55, E95 and E98 service manuals carry the identical Dyaco sentence and the
identical 190 Hz figure - `e95-2016-poly-v-belt-slipping`, `e25-2019-poly-v-belt-slipping`. The
Spirit CVC800 climber does too (`cvc800-drive-belt-slipping`). They are separate cards because
they are separate brands and product lines; the figure happens to agree.
