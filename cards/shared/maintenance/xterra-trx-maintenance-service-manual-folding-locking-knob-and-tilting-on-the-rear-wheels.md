**This is the service manual's chapter 9, and it does not match the owner's manuals for the same machines** - the TRX2500/3500/4500 books fold on a release pin with no moving instructions (`xterra-trx-maintenance-folding-release-pin-and-no-moving-instructions`); the TRX1400 book folds on a latch with a foot-operated release and says "no need to tilt" (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-folding-latch-and-foot-release-switch-four-transport-wheels`).

**Unfolding**: pull the locking knob and hold the running deck, and lower it down to the floor.

**Folding**: pull the locking knob with the right hand; with the left hand lift the running deck up to **30 cm**, then lift with two hands until it is locked by the locking knob.

**Transport**: before moving the treadmill, convert it to the storage position as above. Make sure the locking knob is closed fully over the frame guide.

1. Hold the upper ends of the handrails. Place one foot on the base.
2. **Tilt the treadmill back until it rolls freely on the rear wheels.** Carefully move it to the desired location; use extreme caution, and do not attempt to move the treadmill over an uneven surface.
3. Place one foot on the base and carefully lower the treadmill until it rests in the storage position.

The TRX3500/TRX4500 book prints this page as a picture; it was read from the render.
