This is the oldest schedule in the wave: one early application, then a very long interval. It differs from every other card here, which is why it stands alone.

**Re-lube with nothing other than Sole lubricant.** Sole provides one application with the unit; buy more from your Sole dealer. Some other lubricants may destroy the deck surface or belt and **void the warranty**. Friction also depends on belt/deck cleanliness, which is beyond Sole's control.

Apply the enclosed lubrication after approximately the **first 50 hours** of operation. Use **all the contents of one container (no more)** and spread it onto the middle portion of the deck (away from the edges, under the treadbelt) along its width. Lubricant is not harmful to the skin.

Walking on the belt evens the coat out: walk at **2 to 3 mph for 3 to 5 minutes** over all areas of the belt, using the handrails for support. The first few minutes polish the deck and lubricate the belt backing. **Do not run on a newly lubricated belt** until this break-in is done.

If the belt and deck are kept reasonably clean, **over 1,000 hours** can pass before further re-lubing is needed.

**CAUTION - KEEP HANDS CLEAR OF MOVING REAR PULLEY.**
