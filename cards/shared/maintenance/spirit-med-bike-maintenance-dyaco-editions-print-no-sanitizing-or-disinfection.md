**The whole of the printed cleaning instruction in these three books is one sentence: wipe down all areas in
the sweat path with a damp cloth after each use to prevent rust.** There is no sanitizing section, no
disinfectant, no alcohol strength, no dwell time, no cleaning schedule and no lubricant anywhere in the Dyaco
PT 4.0 R, the Dyaco PT 4.0 U or the Dyaco MED 7.0R owner's manual.

What each maintenance chapter holds instead:

| Book | Maintenance chapter | Cleaning it prints |
|---|---|---|
| Dyaco PT 4.0 R (undated, 62 pages) | p. 55-56: the four care bullets, then the console maintenance menu | the damp-cloth sentence |
| Dyaco PT 4.0 U (undated, 58 pages) | p. 53-54: the same four bullets and the same menu | the damp-cloth sentence |
| Dyaco MED 7.0R (Rev. 1.2.1, 2021, 90 pages) | p. 82-85: the same four bullets, the maintenance menu, the troubleshooting list | the damp-cloth sentence |

The four bullets themselves - rust, the monthly pedal check, loose hardware and the ½" wrench on two rear
levelers - are `spirit-med-bike-maintenance-rust-wipe-monthly-pedal-check-and-half-inch-wrench`.

## The MED 7.0R says so in its intended-use chapter as well

Its application specification states **"there is no particular restriction on hygienic requirements"** and lists
hospital and clinic use among the intended locations
(`spirit-med-bike-maintenance-no-restriction-on-hygienic-requirements`). The two PT 4.0 books have no
intended-use chapter and make no statement either way.

## Do not import a procedure from the Spirit-branded books

The Spirit-branded 2025 4.0R and 4.0U print the **75% isopropyl** sanitizing bullets beside the same four care
items (`ct900-sanitizing-equipment`); the 2025 8.0U and 8.5R print a **20 ml, 70%-or-greater** clinical routine
and a warning list that forbids alcohol (`80t-2026-maintenance-disinfection-procedure`). None of that is printed
in a Dyaco edition. Tell the facility what the book requires - the damp cloth - and that any infection-control
protocol beyond it is the facility's own policy, or a question for Spirit Fitness service, not something these
manuals specify. The MS300 rehabilitation stepper is answered the same way
(`ms300-2021-maintenance-hygienic-requirements-and-cleaning`).
