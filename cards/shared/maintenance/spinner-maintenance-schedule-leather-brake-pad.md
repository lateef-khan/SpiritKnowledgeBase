| Part | Recommended action | Frequency | Cleaner | Lubricant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pedals | Ensure that pedals are tight in crank arms; that all screws on pedals are tight; and that the pedal straps are not frayed | Before each use | N/A | N/A |
| Frame | Wipe down by using a soft damp clean cloth | Daily | Water | N/A |
| Flywheel | Wipe down by spraying on a rag and applying a light coat to sides of the flywheel | Weekly | WD-40 spray. | N/A |
| Brake Pad | see below | Weekly | N/A | see below |

1. Do not service internal parts of pedals. If they are found to be worn internally, we recommend replacing the pedal.
2. Use of lubricants or cleaning solutions other than those so specified will result in diminished performance and a shorter life span for that part.

**The brake pad row of this table carries two overlapping sets of words, and they contradict each other on the lubricant.** One set says the lubricant is Silicone Spray. The other set says 3-IN-ONE 10W oil and *"Do not use silicone-based lubricants"*. The extraction cannot say which one the printed page shows on top, so both are preserved here in the order they appear:

```
Brake   Inspect

 Pad        a dry or
                  leather brake
                      squealing  pad                           oil. Do not use

```

Read as two layers, that is:

- **Layer A** - Brake Pad: inspect for excessive wear or squealing, weekly, cleaner N/A, lubricant **Silicone Spray**.
- **Layer B** - Brake Pad: inspect for excessive wear or a dry or squealing leather brake pad, weekly, cleaner N/A, lubricant **Oil or 3-IN-ONE 10W oil. Do not use silicone-based lubricants.**

Do not pick one and discard the other without checking the printed manual. The newer SB700 2019 onward and SB900 2020 onward manuals print only the Silicone Spray version, with no leather brake pad and no warning against silicone.
