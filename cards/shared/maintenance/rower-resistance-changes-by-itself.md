A stuck button, a computer error, or a failing brake mechanism can all do this. Work through it in order.

1. Ask whether the machine has recently been assembled, disassembled or moved.
   - **Yes** - replace the **Computer Cable**.
   - **No** - go to step 2.
2. Ask whether the level on the console changes at the same moment the resistance physically changes.
   - **Yes** - remove the battery from the handle switch and try the machine again.
     - Machine now behaves - replace the **Handle Assembly**.
     - Machine still misbehaves - replace the **Console**.
   - **No** - replace the **Gear Motor**.

The handle battery sits under the **Battery Cover (36~3)** on the Controller Assembly (36), held by one **3.5 x 12L sheet metal screw (112)**. Both rowers are built this way.

**This card is for resistance that moves on its own.** For resistance that will not move at all, see `sr500-2016-no-adjustable-resistance` or `sr550-2023-resistance-will-not-adjust`.
