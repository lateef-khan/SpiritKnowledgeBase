This is the telescoping-tube generation. It differs from the service-manual latch generation (foot-press tube, yellow sticker) and from the 2026 cylinder generation; the card follows the owner's books.

**Do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and locked position.** Secure the power cord to avoid damage, and use both handrails to manoeuvre the unit to the desired position.

**To fold the treadmill:** make certain the treadmill is at **minimum incline**. Lift the running deck until it is secured by the **locking telescoping tube assembly in the centre back of the base**.

**To unfold the treadmill:** apply slight forward pressure on the running deck with one hand (at the rear roller area, to relieve pressure on the locking system). Pull down on the **unlocking lever** and slowly lower the deck to the floor. The deck lowers unassisted when it reaches about waist high.

**Transport:** the treadmill is equipped with transport wheels that engage when it is folded; after folding, simply roll it away. The 2006-batch, 2011-batch, VF-, UF- and W-books say **four** transport wheels; the 2013-batch and F60 books say transport wheels without a count.

No book in this group prints a re-levelling step after the move; the frame is expected to sit level wherever it lands (see the tracking cards for what an unlevel frame causes).
