Most noise comes from **loose screws or bolts**. Sometimes a part has deformed or shifted, which causes noise or rough movement.

1. Noise where the **flywheel and the wool felt on the brake pad rub each other is normal**.
2. A **worn pedal assembly** makes the movement rough and noisy. Replace worn pedals.
3. Noise happens when the **chain cover and the belt rub**. Moving the chain cover slightly is the most effective fix.

**The 2009 SB700 book (`sb700-2011`) prints item 1 only**, and names the part as a **leather brake pad** rather than wool felt: "Most noise is caused by rubbing between Flywheel (31) and Leather Brake Pad (10) which is normal." It says nothing about pedals or the chain cover; do not quote items 2 and 3 for that machine.
