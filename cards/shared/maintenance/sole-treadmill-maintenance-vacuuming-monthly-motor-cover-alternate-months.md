Dirt, dust and hair block air inlets and accumulate on the running belt.

- **Monthly**: vacuum underneath the treadmill, against build-up that gets sucked into the inner workings under the motor cover.
- **Every other month**: remove the motor cover and carefully vacuum out accumulated dirt and hair. **UNPLUG THE POWER CORD BEFORE THIS TASK.**

Contradiction ruled: the service manuals say once a year under the hood; these owners books say every other month. This card follows the owners books. (The TD80 desk book keeps the yearly hood interval and the ST90 slat book vacuums monthly with no hood step; both have their own cards.)
