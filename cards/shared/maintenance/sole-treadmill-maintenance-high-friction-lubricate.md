Two checklist rows in every wave book share one remedy: lubricate. (Row ownership agreed with the errors section: these friction rows are maintenance's; the static-shutoff row is errors'.)

- **"Tread-belt stops quickly/suddenly when tether cord is pulled"** - **High belt/deck friction. See the General Maintenance section on lubrication.** The 2006-batch, VF-, VS-, VTT8- and UF80-family books word it **"Treadbelt stops instantly (it should move about a foot or so) when tether cord is pulled while standing on the unit"**; the remedy is the same.
- **"Treadmill trips on board 15 amp circuit"** - **High belt/deck friction. See General Maintenance.** The same older families word it **"Possible High belt/deck friction."**

Lubricate per the interval card for the machine (a dry deck is the usual cause), then re-check. The neighbouring static-shutoff row ("computer shuts off when console is touched") is a grounding issue, not friction; see `f65-2016-console-shuts-off-when-touched`.
