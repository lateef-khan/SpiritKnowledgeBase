**The six 2023 XT service manuals contain no General Maintenance chapter.** Their contents run outlines, electronics, wiring, safety, error messages, calibration, the maintenance menu, the troubleshooting matrix and part replacement. Belt tension, tracking, cleaning, lubrication interval and deck life are in the owner's manuals only, and those are carded.

## What the books say instead

- **E2 / OVER CURRENT** (every one of the six): *"First, we recommend that users lubricate the bottom of the running belt according to the instructions provided in the owner's manual."* The service manual defers to the owner's manual by name. The error itself is an errors card; the instruction it points at is `xt-2023-maintenance-deck-lubrication-procedure` and `xt-2023-maintenance-deck-lubrication-interval`.
- **Troubleshooting matrix** (all but the XT685ENT): *"Tread belt does not run in center — See treadmill belt adjustment"* and *"Tread belt hesitates while being stepped on — Insufficient lubricant on tread belt / tension insufficient — See treadmill belt lubrication."* Neither section exists in the book. They are `xt-2023-maintenance-treadbelt-tracking`, `xt-2023-maintenance-treadbelt-tension` and the lubrication cards above.
- **Roller and deck replacement**: "Adjust running belt to the center is necessary when installing front/rear roller back" and "Adjust running belt tension and center it" — instructions with no figures, no wrench and no turn fraction. Use the owner's-manual cards.

## The one piece of belt care the book does print

The speed-sensor check under E1, with the **3 mm** gap ceiling the owner's manuals lack — `xt-2023-maintenance-adjusting-the-speed-sensor`.

## The XT685 2023 is a special case

Its E2 text tells the reader to lubricate the belt per the owner's manual, but the XT685 2023 owner's manual says the deck must **never** be lubricated (`xt685-2023-belt-deck-general-maintenance`). The service manual is boilerplate shared with the lubricated XT185-XT485; on an XT685 2023 follow the owner's manual.

## The 2015 books are different

The XT185, XT285, XT385 and XT485 2015 service manuals each print a General Maintenance chapter with cleaning, tension, tracking and lubrication; see `xt-2015-maintenance-lubrication-interval-90-hours`, `xt185-2015-maintenance-lubrication-schedule-25-then-50-hours` and `ct850-2016-treadbelt-tracking-adjustment`. Do not assume a 2023 book has the same chapter because its 2015 predecessor did.
