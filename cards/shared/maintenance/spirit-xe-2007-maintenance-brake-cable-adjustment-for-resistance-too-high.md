**This is the 2007 dealer manual's cable-brake adjustment for the XE100 to XE500. The XG400 (2016)
sets its magnet housing 2 mm from the flywheel instead
(`xg400-2016-maintenance-magnet-to-flywheel-gap-2-mm-and-steel-cable-tightness`); the later
gear-motor XE books print no brake adjustment at all.**

The mechanical troubleshooting table has one row for it: **Resistance too high - see Procedure 5.**

## Procedure 5: adjusting the brake cable

1. Take the right and left shrouds off.
2. On the **left side** of the unit, find the **brake inside the flywheel**.
3. With an open end wrench, **loosen the small lock nut**.
4. With the same wrench, **loosen the longer nut**. As you loosen it you will see the gap get
   smaller.
5. **The smaller the gap, the easier the resistance will be at level 1. Do not adjust the gap out
   completely.**
6. **Tighten down the lock nut.**

The book prints no measurement for the gap and no wrench size. "No resistance change" is the
opposite fault: the table diagnoses it electrically first - the gear motor potentiometer readings
and Procedure 3 - and, if the motor can be heard running, ends at this same cable adjustment. The
electrical half is carded in the errors section.

The dealer manual's other two care rows - a thump, and slide wheels jumping off the track - are
answered by cleaning and lubricating the wheels and rails
(`ce800-2013-maintenance-rear-rail-cleaning-and-lubrication`).
