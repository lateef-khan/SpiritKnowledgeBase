This is the older tension text. It differs from the both-bolts card in one load-bearing way: tension is set from the **right side only**, and every tension change must be compensated with an equal tracking change. The card follows this printing.

Belt tension is **not critical for most users**. It matters for joggers and runners, giving a smooth, steady running surface.

Adjustment is made **from the right side of the rear roller** with the supplied 6 mm Allen wrench. The adjustment bolt is at the **end of the right side rail**, reached **through the small hole of the end cap**.

Tighten the rear roller **only enough to prevent slippage at the front roller**. Turn the treadbelt tension adjusting bolt in increments of **1/4 turn** and inspect for proper tension. When a tension adjustment is made, a **tracking adjustment must also be made to compensate**: turn **both** the tension and tracking Allen bolts an **equal amount**, clockwise, by **no more than a 1/4 turn at a time**.

**DO NOT OVERTIGHTEN.** Over-tightening causes belt damage and premature bearing failure.
