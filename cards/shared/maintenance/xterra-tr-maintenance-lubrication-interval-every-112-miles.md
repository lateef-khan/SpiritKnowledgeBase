Both books tie lubrication to distance:

- **TR95H**: the care schedule's *Lubrication of Running Belt* row says "use a silicone lubricant to lubricate whenever reaching **112 miles**", and the Deck Lubrication page says the word LUBE flashes every 3 seconds on the console once accumulated running distance reaches 112 (printed "112 MPH") to remind the user.
- **TR75H**: the deck comes pre-lubricated and subsequent lubrication should be done **every 112 miles, or every 3 months of operation, whichever comes first**. The LUBE Alert page says the word appears after powering on when total usage reaches 112 miles, and the reset only works after that threshold.

**Both books also print the boilerplate "every 90 days, after 90 hours of use" on their Deck Lubrication page** (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-lubrication-interval-every-90-days-or-90-hours`) and never reconcile the two. The TR75H's Before and After Running page adds: **failure to complete periodic maintenance will void the warranty**; see the Maintenance & Care section.

The TR75H procedure is `tr75h-2025-maintenance-deck-lubrication-three-one-foot-lines-with-the-straw`; the TR95H's is the two-S-line procedure (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-deck-lubrication-s-line-18-inches-from-the-motor-cover`). The full care schedule is `xterra-tr-maintenance-care-schedule-daily-to-six-monthly`. Clearing the LUBE word belongs to the console cards.
