**This is the only lubricant any Spirit CS strength manual names, and five books out of
thirty-six name it.** Printed as step 3 of the weight-stack assembly note, word for word:

> **After the weight stack is installed, lubricate the Guide Rods using a thin coat of clear
> silicone prior to attaching the weight stack shrouds.**

CSS-LATP, CSS-LEXT, CSS-SQSC, and — new in the 2025 revision printings only —
CSD-ITOT and CSD-PUDA. The already-ingested 2024 printings of CSD-ITOT and CSD-PUDA drop the
clause, like every other CSD book; the 2025 revisions are the first dual-station manuals to
carry it.

## Read the two halves carefully

- **"A thin coat."** No volume, no coverage figure, no reapplication amount.
- **"Prior to attaching the weight stack shrouds."** This is a **build** instruction. It sits in the
  assembly chapter beside the upper-pulley drawing, not on the maintenance page, and it describes
  the moment the stack is first put together.

**The monthly *Lubricate: Guide Rods* row on the maintenance page of these same five books names
nothing.** So even here the recurring task has no specified product; what is specified is the first
application, at build.

## The other thirty-one books print the same step without the lubricant

*"After the weight stack is installed, lubricate the Guide Rods prior to attaching the weight stack
shrouds."* Same step, same position, clause removed — confirmed unchanged in the 2025 revision
printings of CSD-ACBE, CSD-LELC (February printing) and CSD-LPCE. Some CSS books do not print the
step at all, and the remaining CSF and CSI books do not print it. See
`spirit-strength-maintenance-guide-rods-lubricated-monthly-and-no-lubricant-named`, which is the
answer for those machines.

**Whether Spirit intends clear silicone on every machine in the range is not something these
manuals settle.** Five of thirty-six say it; the rest are silent. Say which is which rather than
generalising the figure.

## Clear silicone is not the ST800FT's answer

The **ST800FT** functional trainer names a **Teflon (PTFE) based lubricant** for its guide rods and
a **Silicon or Teflon based lubricant spray** for its seat sleeves —
`spirit-strength-st800ft-maintenance-teflon-guide-rods-and-a-lithium-bearing-repack`. Different
book, different template, and it prints its lubricant on the maintenance table rather than in the
assembly chapter.

## Where this fact lives, and why it is filed under maintenance

The sentence is printed in the assembly chapter, but the question it answers — *what do I put on
the guide rods?* — is a maintenance question and is asked long after the machine is built. The
assembly chapter's own cards cover the weight-stack build step as a procedure; this card holds the
lubricant.
