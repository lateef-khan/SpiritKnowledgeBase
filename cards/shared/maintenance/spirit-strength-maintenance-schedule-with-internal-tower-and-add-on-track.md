## The two extra rows

- **Clean: Internal Tower, bi-monthly.** It sits in the *Cleaning* group, not *Inspect*.
- **Lubricate: Add on Track, monthly.** The same interval as the guide rods.

**Neither term is defined or drawn anywhere else in these nine manuals**, and neither appears in
their parts lists under that name. The table is the only place either phrase is printed. Quote the
row and the interval; do not invent which part is meant.

**Neither row names a lubricant or a cleaner**, exactly as the guide-rod row does not — see
`spirit-strength-maintenance-guide-rods-lubricated-monthly-and-no-lubricant-named`.

## The two machines that print these rows and different intervals

The **CSI-CPSP** and **CSI-LROW** also carry an Internal Tower and an Add on Track row, but under a
**Bi-Annually** fourth column and with different intervals throughout:
`spirit-strength-maintenance-schedule-with-a-bi-annual-column`. The **CSF-FUNT** carries the
Internal Tower row without the Add on Track row, and the **CSF-HRAC** carries the Add on Track row
without a pulley row.

The twenty-two single-station machines carry **neither** row:
`spirit-strength-maintenance-schedule-thirteen-actions-daily-to-bi-monthly`.
