The whole of the printed folding text in the TRX2500, TRX3500 and TRX4500 books, on the last assembly page:

1. **Pull the Release Pin to lower the deck from the folded position until it locks.**
2. **Pull the Release Pin and raise the deck into the locking position to fold.**

The **TRX5500 book prints the heading "Folding Instructions" and four drawings with no words**: a hand pulling the pin at the deck pivot, the deck lowering, the deck rising, and the folded treadmill on its wheels. It was read from the render; the text layer holds only the heading.

**None of the four books prints instructions for moving the treadmill.** The only mention of wheels is the safety line "route the power cord away from any moving part of the treadmill including the elevation mechanism and transport wheels". The Dyaco service manuals for the TRX2500, TRX3500 and TRX4500 do describe moving - tilt back onto the rear wheels - on `xterra-trx-maintenance-service-manual-folding-locking-knob-and-tilting-on-the-rear-wheels`, but their folding text does not match this page.

Before first unfolding, a Velcro shipping strap around the base has to come off with the treadmill flat on the floor; that is on the pre-assembly page and belongs to the assembly cards.
