The treadmill uses a high-efficiency low-friction bed, and performance is best when the bed is kept clean.

- **Monthly**: wipe the edge of the belt and the area between the belt edge and the frame with a soft damp cloth or paper towel, reaching as far under the belt edge as is practical. **Use water only, no cleaners and no abrasives.** A mild soap and water solution with a nylon scrub brush cleans the top of the textured belt. Let the belt dry before using it.
- **Monthly**: vacuum underneath the treadmill to prevent dirt, dust and pet hair building up and blocking the air inlets.
- **Once a year**: remove the black motor hood and vacuum out the dirt inside. **Unplug the power cord before this task.**

**Belt dust** appears during normal break-in, until the belt stabilises. Wiping the excess off with a damp cloth keeps the buildup down. Black particles collecting under the treadmill are the drive belt breaking in; vacuum under the treadmill periodically.

**The A1 owner's books print the same three intervals word for word** (wipe monthly with water only; vacuum underneath monthly; black motor hood yearly, unplugged). They call the part a "bed" where the service manual says "treadboard", and their dust line reads "Wiping excess off with a damp cloth will minimize buildup". The F80-family book's lube page names "Spirit approved lubricant" (stale sibling-brand boilerplate); its cleaning page is Sole's and matches.
