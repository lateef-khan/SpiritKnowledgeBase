The two books put their only care instructions among the "Final Considerations" at the end of assembly:

- **"The bike can be cleaned with a damp cloth and mild non-abrasive detergent. Do not use solvents."**
- **"Store the bike Indoors. Excessive moisture can cause rust to the frame and damage to the
  electronics."**

Nothing else on cleaning is printed - no interval, no sanitizer, no lubricant. The same list asks you to
tighten all bolts and nuts, tighten both pedals to the crank arms as instructed, and leave 2 feet of
clearance all round to mount and dismount; those are assembly and safety facts, not care.

The other Xterra bikes of this generation allow **mild liquid soap** and warn about water on the monitor
rather than about solvents -
