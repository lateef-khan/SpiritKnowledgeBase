You need a fresh 9V battery (or a power drill battery) and a Phillips screwdriver.

1. Unplug the treadmill. The machine must be off and unplugged from the wall.
2. Loosen and remove the 5 screws holding the motor cover, then lift the cover off.
3. Follow the two red and black wires from the drive motor to the motor controller. On some models they are red and white.
4. Photograph or note where the wires plug in, then unplug both motor wires from the board.
5. Slide the plastic insulation back on the wire ends to expose the metal contacts.
6. Touch one motor wire to the positive terminal of the battery and the other to the negative terminal. It does not matter which way round. It only changes the direction the motor turns.
7. Watch the motor. **If it moves or even twitches, the motor is fine.**
