**WARNING**: "The effect that the safety level of the equipment can be maintained only if it is
examined regularly for damage and wear."

1. **Replace defective components immediately and/or keep the equipment out of use until repair.**
2. **The components which are most susceptible to wear: Belt, PU wheel, Bearing, Idler.**

Four items, no interval. The WARNING sits directly under the general maintenance list - wipe the
sweat path, tighten loose hardware and the crank arm nut, level the two rear pads with a 14 mm
wrench (`ct800-2016-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`).

## The same four items on the touchscreen bikes; five and six elsewhere

The CU900ENT, CR900ENT and CU1000ENT bike service manuals print the identical four-item list
(`cu900ent-wear-parts-inspection`). The CR900 and CU900 2018 bike books print **five**
(`spirit-cr900-cu900-maintenance-wear-parts-five-items`), and the treadmill and 2024 owner's manuals
that attach this WARNING to their sanitizing bullets print **six**, adding Shaft and Pedal
(`ct900-sanitizing-equipment`). None of the CE900 owner's manuals prints the WARNING at all - the
list exists for these ellipticals only in the three service manuals.

On an elliptical the **PU wheel** is literal: the slide wheels that run on the aluminium rails, which
the same books say to keep clean (`spirit-ce900-maintenance-track-and-wheel-cleaning`).
