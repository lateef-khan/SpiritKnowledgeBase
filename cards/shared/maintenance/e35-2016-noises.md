Noises are a general problem for ellipticals and are difficult to troubleshoot. The manual lists possible sources: rubbing between chain covers and round disk, bushing housing and the cross bar, the belt and the drive pulley, idler wheel, handle bar and console mast shaft, flywheel and the steel cable at the front; slide wheels and crank axle with wave washers, pedal arms and pedals at the rear.

Major causes are **insufficient lubricant, un-smoothness and loose screws**.

Since those are moving parts and interact, problem solving must be treated case by case.
