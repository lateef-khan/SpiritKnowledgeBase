1. **Do not service internal parts of pedals.** If they are found to be worn internally, the
   recommendation is to **replace the pedal**.
2. **Use of lubricants or cleaning solutions other than those specified will result in diminished
   performance and a shorter life span for that part.**

"Those specified" means the Cleaner and Lubricant columns of that machine's own care schedule, and
**the two schedules in this repository do not name the same substances**. The CB900, CIC800 and
CIC850 name water only
(`spirit-indoor-cycle-maintenance-schedule-water-and-monthly-brake-pad`); the XIC600 names WD-40 for
the flywheel and 3-IN-ONE or 10W oil for the brake pad, and forbids silicone
(`spirit-indoor-cycle-maintenance-schedule-wd40-and-weekly-brake-pad`). Read rule 2 against the
schedule of the machine in front of you.

## Where the sentence sits differs by machine

On the CB900, CIC800 and CIC850 these are the two footnotes beneath the maintenance table. On both
XIC600 printings they are items **1 and 2** of the numbered GENERAL MAINTENANCE list, ahead of the
sweat-path wipe and the squeak checks. The wording is the same either way; only the CB900 adds the
word "so" ("other than those so specified").

The air bikes and the JB950 print neither sentence. What they say about pedals instead is a
re-torque interval and a warning that members should not change the pedals —
`spirit-air-bike-maintenance-pedal-retorque-10-and-100-hours`.
