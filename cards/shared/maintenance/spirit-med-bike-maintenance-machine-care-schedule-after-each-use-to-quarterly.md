Read the cleaning instructions above the table first: switch the main power **OFF** and unplug the bike, use
**water only** on most surfaces, and use a mild soap solution at **10:1 water to soap** with a nylon scrub brush
on the textured pedals. The full list, with what must never be used, is `80t-2026-maintenance-cleaning-cautions`.

| Period | Task | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| After Each Use | General cleaning | Use a soft, damp cloth or paper towel to clean the console, and to wipe down the equipment surface, main body cover, handlebar, manual mechanical brake lever, **saddle** (8.0U) / **cushion** (8.5R), adjustable levers and frame. |
| Weekly / Daily (Daily member Use) | Functional & safety checking | Ensure the equipment is functioning properly. Check around the equipment and **inspect the crank arm with pedals for loosening**. Use a soft, damp cloth or paper towel to wipe clean the full surface and console. |
| Monthly | Pedals cleaning | No cleaners or abrasives. A mild soap and water solution along with a nylon scrub brush to clean the top of the textured pedals (**10:1 water to soap**). |
| Monthly | Pedals checking | **Check pedals tightening and wear.** |
| Quarterly | General cleaning, **tread belt cleaning**, functional & safety checking | Do a round of the above tasks. |

Four periods, no annual row, and **no levelling row** - levelling is on the previous page
(`spirit-85s-maintenance-pedals-and-front-and-rear-stabiliser-pads`).

## The quarterly row asks for a tread belt these bikes do not have

An 8.0U is an upright bike and an 8.5R a recumbent; neither has a tread belt, yet the quarterly cell lists
**Tread belt cleaning** among its three tasks. It is carried over from the treadmill version of this table
(`80t-2026-maintenance-cleaning-schedule`), where the row is real, exactly as it is on the 8.5S stepper
(`spirit-85s-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`). **Read the quarterly row as "do a round of the above tasks" and
drop the tread belt.** The same authoring chain prints *Dyaco* and four other models in the page's opening
sentence (`spirit-med-bike-maintenance-care-page-names-dyaco-machines`).

## Not the 8.5S table, and not the commercial bike table

The 8.5S stepper's *Pedals checking* row reads "check the handlebar arm and footplates are firmly tightened;
check hand grips, manual mechanical lock lever, cushion, seat back and footplates for signs of wear"; these two
bikes say only **"Check pedals tightening and wear."** The 8.5UE ergometer cleans foot rest plates and checks
handles (`85ue-2025-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`). The commercial CR900 and CU900 print an eleven-row
item-by-interval grid with a biannual column (`spirit-bike-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`). Do not read one
table's rows onto another machine.

**The monthly pedal check is the only printed interval for the pedals on these two bikes.** Their service manuals
say "regularly" (`cu900ent-check-pedals`).
