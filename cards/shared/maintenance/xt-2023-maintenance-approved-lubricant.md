**Do not lubricate with anything other than Spirit Fitness approved lubricant.**

- Your treadmill comes with **one tube of treadmill belt lubricant**. It is listed in Parts Included as **1 Lubricant**: item **58** on the XT185 and XT285, item **30** on the XT385 and XT485.
- **Extra tubes can be ordered directly from www.Spiritfitness.com, or by calling customer service at (800)258-8511.**

**These manuals do not say what the lubricant is made of.** They never call it silicone, never give a grade, and never name a substitute product, so no equivalent can be recommended from this source. The Sole manuals for other machines do specify 100% food grade silicone, but that is a different manufacturer's instruction; see `f80-2026-approved-lubricant`.
