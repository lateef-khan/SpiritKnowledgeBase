**There is no schedule in either book. The sentence promising one is not followed by anything.**

Both the 2019 and the 2021 CSC900 owner's manuals print, under GENERAL MAINTENANCE:

> With durable, high performance components, this equipment is designed for heavy usage with
> minimal maintenance required. To keep it in top condition, perform regular daily, weekly and
> monthly preventive maintenance **routines outlined below**.

Nothing is outlined below. On the 2019 page the rest of the sheet is blank and the warranty starts
on the next page; on the 2021 page the space below is taken by the sanitizing bullets
(`ct900-sanitizing-equipment`), which are not a schedule and name no interval.

## How this was proved

The claim is an absence, so it was checked three ways rather than by reading the extracted text:

1. Both pages were **rendered and looked at**, not searched. Neither carries a flattened table.
2. The **following PDF page** was read in both books; it is the warranty, so the section does not
   continue overleaf.
3. Both sources were searched for **quarterly**, **biannual**, **PERIOD**, **schedule** and
   **chart**. No hits in either.

## The 2019 service manual prints none either

The CSC900 service manual V1.0 - the alternator-drive book - has no maintenance chapter at all. Its *Maintenance
Cautions* are three lines about switching the power off, using original parts and cleaning debris off a part
before it is refitted, and the rest of the book is unboxing, installation, a twelve-row fault table and replacement
steps. The only lubricant in it is dripped onto the pedal shaft to get a pedal off (p. 12, a replacement step), and
"chain wheel" appears once, as a possible noise source in the fault table. **Nothing in either book lubricates the
step chain or its sprockets, or sets an interval for anything.**

## The schedule arrives in 2024

The **CSC900 2024** is the first stair climber manual to print one — a nine-row *Item* by
*Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Quarterly / Biannually* grid — and the **CSC880 2025** prints it again,
identically (`csc900-2024-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`). **Do not quote those nine rows to a
2019 or 2021 owner as if their book contained them.** If a facility needs intervals for an older
CSC900, say plainly that its manual gives none and offer the 2024 table as the manufacturer's later
guidance for the same machine, labelled as such.

The rest of the page — the one reason for a squeak, and the owner's sole responsibility for regular
maintenance — is unchanged from 2019 to 2025 and is carded at
`csc900-2024-maintenance-one-reason-and-owner-responsibility`.
