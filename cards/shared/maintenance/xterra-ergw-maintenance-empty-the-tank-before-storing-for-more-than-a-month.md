Printed inside the storage caution on the Transport and Storage page of all three books:

**"If not using the rower for more than a month, empty the tank before storing."**

That is the whole rule: a month is the threshold, and the job is the normal drain with the siphon pump into a
bucket (`xterra-ergw-maintenance-changing-the-water-siphon-pump-out-then-funnel-in`). The pumped-out water is
not for drinking.

The sentence sits under "you can save space by storing the rower vertically", so it applies whether the rower
is stood up or left flat; standing it up is
`xterra-erg-maintenance-storing-upright-raise-the-slide-rail-to-vertical-and-the-seat-slides-down`.

**Nothing about frost.** None of the three books mentions freezing, cold or a storage temperature for the
water; the one-month figure is the only reason given for emptying the tank. The **ERG750W** and **ERG800W**
books print no storage-emptying rule at all - the ERG800W's answer is a water change every half year
(`erg800w-2023-maintenance-clean-filtered-water-changed-every-half-year-no-tablet-named`).

Spirit's CRW800H2O prints the same sentence (`crw800h2o-maintenance-empty-the-tank-before-storing`). Other
brand, separate card.
