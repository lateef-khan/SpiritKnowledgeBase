Two books say it two ways, and both call it normal:

- **Owner's manuals** (TR150, TR200, TRX1000, TR260, TR300, TR6.6, TR6.4), under General Maintenance: "**Belt Dust** - This occurs during normal break-in or until the belt stabilizes. Wiping excess off with a damp cloth will minimize buildup."
- **Dyaco service manuals** (TR150, TR260, TRX1400, TRX2500, TRX3500/TRX4500), in the troubleshooting matrix: "**Black particles collecting under treadmill** - Drive belt is breaking in. - Vacuum under treadmill periodically."

Neither book gives a duration for break-in. The service manuals' chapter 10 also repeats the belt-dust sentence for the TRX1400, TRX2500, TRX3500 and TRX4500.

The new-layout owner's manuals (TRX1400, TR65, TR75, TR85, TR95H, TR75H, WS200, WS300) and the TRX2500/3500/4500/5500 owner's manuals print neither sentence.
