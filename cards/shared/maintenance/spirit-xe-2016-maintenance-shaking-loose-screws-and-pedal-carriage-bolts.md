**Shaking is mainly due to loose screws, so check and tighten loose screws first.**

Then check the connection points for gaps that have opened after prolonged use - wear and tear - or
from improper assembly. The one example the books give: **shaking of the foot pedal is caused by
loose carriage bolts, from improper assembly.**

That is the whole item. No torque, no list of which screws.

## The heading is wrong on four of the books

The XE195 and XE295 books head this paragraph **"10-3. Slip Problem"**, the same title as the
preceding item, and the XE395 and XE395ENT books head it **"10-3. Resistance & Incline Problem"**.
The text under all four headings is the shaking paragraph above. The XE795 books head it "Shaking
Problem", the CE850 and XE895 "Play". Read the paragraph, not the heading.

## The CE800 (2016) adds the front-stabilizer footpad

The XE890B book: "Wobble is usually caused by loose screw. When there is wobble, try to tighten all
screws and check for installation faults or worn out of parts when time lasts. Like a wobble pedal
is usually caused by carriage bolt not installed properly. **If the unit wobbles, adjust the screws
for footpad beneath Front Stabilizer.**" It is the only book of the nine that names a leveller here.

A little wobble at the **swing arm** is normal and is answered separately
(`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-swing-arm-noise-thick-lubricant-on-the-axle-then-the-6005-bearing`).
The generator-driven CE900 family answers shaking with one check on the handle
(`spirit-ce900-maintenance-shaking-loose-handle-screw`). The owner's manuals' noise page is
`spirit-xe-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`.
