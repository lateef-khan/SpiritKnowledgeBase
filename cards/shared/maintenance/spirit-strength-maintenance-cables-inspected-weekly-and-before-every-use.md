**Two intervals, both printed, in every one of these thirty-three books.**

1. **Weekly**, as a scheduled task. The maintenance table's *Inspect: Belt / Cables* row is ticked
   in the **Weekly** column. It is the only weekly item in the *Inspect* group; everything else
   there is monthly or bi-monthly.
2. **Before every single use**, as a condition of use. The facility precautions page says:
   *"Inspect all cables and belts and connections prior to use. DO NOT use if any components are
   worn, frayed or damaged."*

**Neither replaces the other, and the two sit twenty-five pages apart in the book.** A gym that
runs a weekly PM round is doing half of what the manual asks; the pre-use check is on the user and
the floor staff.

## What "inspect" is looking for

The wear list is printed on the same precautions page: *pay close attention to all areas most
susceptible to wear, **including (but not limited to) cables, pulleys, belts and grips***. The
SAFEGUARDS page adds *check all belts, pulleys and bungee cords regularly for signs of wear, and
replace if needed*.

The condition that fails the check is **frayed, worn or damaged**. What to do about it is one
sentence and it is absolute — see
`spirit-strength-maintenance-replace-a-frayed-cable-at-first-sign-of-wear`.

## No service life, no hour count, no cycle count

**These manuals give no replacement interval for a cable or a belt.** Not in months, not in hours,
not in repetitions. The only trigger they print is the condition of the part. Do not offer a
customer a figure; there is not one in the book.

## The machines that are not on this card

- **CSI-CPSP and CSI-LROW** tick *Inspect: Belt / Cables* in the **Monthly** column, not the
  weekly one — `spirit-strength-maintenance-cables-inspected-monthly-and-before-every-use`. Quoting
  "weekly" at a CSI owner is quoting a four-times-stricter interval than their book prints.
- **CSF-UPRB** has **no Belt / Cables row at all**, only the pre-use sentence —
  `spirit-strength-csf-uprb-maintenance-schedule-of-six-actions`.
- **ST800FT** is on a Commercial/Home table: cables inspected **daily** in a facility and **weekly**
  at home, and — uniquely in this range — **replaced on a fixed interval** —
  `spirit-strength-st800ft-maintenance-cables-inspected-daily-and-replaced-yearly`.
- **ST800DR3 and ST800FI** have no cables and no cable row.

## This is the Spirit answer, not the Sole one

Every strength card written before this wave is a Sole SRVO card, and nothing on this card came
from one.
