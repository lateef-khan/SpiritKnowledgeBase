One row of the Trouble Shooting table:

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Squealing | V-belt slipping | Adjust v-belt |

Four rower books print the same row: the **ERG220** as "Squeaking or squealing noise / V-belt slipping / Adjust v-belt", and the **ERG500, ERG550W and ERG600W** as "Squealing / Belt slipping / Adjust Belt" - the same diagnosis without the letter. The ERG160, ERG650W, ERG700 and ERG750W troubleshooting tables have no noise row, and the ERG180, ERG400 and ERG800W owner's manuals print no troubleshooting table at all.

**That is all any of the books print.** No page of any of them shows where the belt is, which nut or screw
tensions it, or how tight it should be, and no page of the owner's manual gives a procedure to reach it. Treat "adjust v-belt" as a diagnosis for the service call, not a job the book
walks you through.

The rows above it in the same table - a **grinding noise** from a defective crank bearing, idler pulley or
flywheel - are answered with "replace" and belong to the error and troubleshooting cards, not to
maintenance.

The SB600 service manual, for a different machine, does give a belt-slip adjustment (an idler wheel screw,
`sb600-2023-maintenance-belt-slips-turn-the-idler-wheel-adjustment-screw-and-a-belt-that-comes-off-goes-back-one-notch`);
nothing in these books says the SB150, SB250, FS150 or any of the rowers is built the same way. The ERG180 and ERG800W service manuals do print a belt tension figure inside their pulley replacement procedures (the assembly section's cards), but no owner's manual sends the owner there.
