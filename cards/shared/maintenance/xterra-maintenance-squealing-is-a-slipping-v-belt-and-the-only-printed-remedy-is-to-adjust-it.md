One row of the Trouble Shooting table:

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Squealing | V-belt slipping | Adjust v-belt |

**That is all the three books print.** No page of any of them shows where the belt is, which nut or screw
tensions it, or how tight it should be, and no page of the owner's manual gives a procedure to reach it. Treat "adjust v-belt" as a diagnosis for the service call, not a job the book
walks you through.

The rows above it in the same table - a **grinding noise** from a defective crank bearing, idler pulley or
flywheel - are answered with "replace" and belong to the error and troubleshooting cards, not to
maintenance.

The SB600 service manual, for a different machine, does give a belt-slip adjustment (an idler wheel screw,
`sb600-2023-maintenance-belt-slips-turn-the-idler-wheel-adjustment-screw-and-a-belt-that-comes-off-goes-back-one-notch`);
nothing in these three books says the SB150, SB250 or FS150 is built the same way.
