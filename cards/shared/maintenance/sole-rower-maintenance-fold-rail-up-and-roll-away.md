Under the heading FOLDING/UNFOLDING PROCEDURES, all three books print the same two text steps:

4. **Fold the aluminum rail assembly up.**
5. **Lift the rear and roll away.**

Steps 1-3 on the same page are figures with no readable text on the render - the OCR returns only fragments - so the knob/lever part of the procedure, if any, is not statable from these books. Nothing on the page says how to unfold the rail or lock it back down; reversing the two steps is the obvious reading and the manual does not state it.

The SR400 folds differently, on a fixing pin rather than this rail fold (linked card). None of the four rower books prints any cleaning, lubrication or care schedule (linked card).
