Dirt build-up on the **rear rails and polyurethane wheels** is a source of noise. Build-up on the
**aluminium tracks** can cause a thumping sound that seems to come from inside the main body of the
machine, because noise travels and is amplified in the tubing of the frame.

1. Clean the tracks and wheels with a **lint free cloth and rubbing alcohol**.
2. Stubborn build-up can be removed with your thumbnail or a **non-metallic scraper**, like the
   back edge of a plastic knife.

**Neither manual asks for a lubricant here, and neither states an interval for this cleaning.**
The CE900 2025 machine care table gives the tracks and wheels a weekly clean and a monthly
inspection (`ce900-2025-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`); the 2021 manual prints no table.

**The 2013 CE800 manual adds a lubricant step to the same procedure** - a thin coat on the rails
after cleaning (`ce800-2013-maintenance-rear-rail-cleaning-and-lubrication`). Do not read that step
onto a CE900; these two manuals stop at the scraper.

The CES880 suspension elliptical has no tracks or wheels to clean and its manual prints neither
this paragraph nor a Tracks / Wheels row in its schedule.

## The service manuals: the same alcohol, "regularly", and still no lubricant

The **CE900 2021**, **CE900ENT** and **CE1000ENT 2023** service manuals say the same thing in one
sentence, and the CE900 and CE900ENT books say it twice - once as their only preventative-maintenance
item, **3.1 Clean Regularly for Aluminum Slides**, and again in the noise chapter: "regularly clean
the aluminum rail to extend the service life. (It is recommended to use **alcohol** to remove the
dust attached to the aluminum rail.)" The CE1000ENT book prints it once, in its noise chapter. No
cloth, no scraper, no interval beyond "regularly", and **no lubricant** - the three books agree with
the owner's manuals on that.

The noise chapter adds the wheels: "The surface of the **sliding wheel group** is kept clean and can
be dusted for a long period of time, need regular maintenance and cleaning, and can maintain wheel
life", with the carriage base covers A and B and the slide guard coming off to reach them - the
main-roller replacement procedure shows how. The rest of that chapter, a dry fish-eye bearing and a
grainy feel that is normal, is `spirit-ce900-maintenance-noise-and-feet-feeling-graininess-normal-fish-eye-bearing-lubrication`.
The **PU wheel** those books list among the four wear parts is this wheel
(`spirit-ce900-maintenance-wear-parts-four-items-belt-pu-wheel-bearing-idler`).

The CE900ENT and CE1000ENT have no owner's manual in the repository; the service manual's sentence
is their only cleaning instruction. The residential XE service manuals answer a rough motion with an
alcohol wipe *followed by lubricant*
(`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-rough-motion-loose-screws-then-alcohol-wipe-and-re-lubricate-the-rail`) -
do not read that lubricant onto a CE900.
