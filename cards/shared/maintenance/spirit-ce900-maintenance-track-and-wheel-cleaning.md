Dirt build-up on the **rear rails and polyurethane wheels** is a source of noise. Build-up on the
**aluminium tracks** can cause a thumping sound that seems to come from inside the main body of the
machine, because noise travels and is amplified in the tubing of the frame.

1. Clean the tracks and wheels with a **lint free cloth and rubbing alcohol**.
2. Stubborn build-up can be removed with your thumbnail or a **non-metallic scraper**, like the
   back edge of a plastic knife.

**Neither manual asks for a lubricant here, and neither states an interval for this cleaning.**
The CE900 2025 machine care table gives the tracks and wheels a weekly clean and a monthly
inspection (`ce900-2025-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`); the 2021 manual prints no table.

**The 2013 CE800 manual adds a lubricant step to the same procedure** - a thin coat on the rails
after cleaning (`ce800-2013-maintenance-rear-rail-cleaning-and-lubrication`). Do not read that step
onto a CE900; these two manuals stop at the scraper.

The CES880 suspension elliptical has no tracks or wheels to clean and its manual prints neither
this paragraph nor a Tracks / Wheels row in its schedule.
