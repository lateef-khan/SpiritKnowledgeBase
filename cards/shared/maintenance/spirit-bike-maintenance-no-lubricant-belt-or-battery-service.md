These twelve owner's manuals prescribe **no lubrication of any kind**. The words *lubricant*,
*lubricate*, *grease* and *silicone* do not appear in any of them. There is nothing on the machine
for an owner to oil or grease, and no interval at which to do it. **Do not carry a treadmill deck
lubricant onto a bike** — a bike has no tread belt and no deck.

They prescribe **no drive belt work** either. A **Drive Belt** appears in the exploded parts list
(item 54 on the CR800 2021, item 35 on the CR900 2025 and so on), and nowhere else: no tension
figure, no adjustment step, no slip procedure. Every other occurrence of the word *belt* in these
books is a **chest belt** heart rate transmitter.

**There is no battery in the bike.** The only battery any of these manuals names is the **CR2032**
lithium cell in the chest strap transmitter, which is an accessory and is dealt with as a heart
rate topic, not as machine maintenance. The CR800, CR900, CU800 and CU900 do not even take a mains
cord — they run off a built-in generator and need no AC outlet.

What the manuals *do* ask of an owner is the wipe-down, the hardware and crank arm nut check and
the levelling in `cu900ent-general-maintenance`, plus, on three of them, the inspection schedule in
`spirit-bike-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`.

## The two places a figure does exist

- **The 2012 CU800** owner's manual is the one bike book that names a lubricant: a thin coat on
  the rear rails after cleaning them. It is carried over from an elliptical manual and the machine
  has no rails — `cu800-2012-maintenance-squeak-rear-rails-and-four-levelers`. It is excluded from
  this card.
- **The CU900ENT service manual**, not the owner's manual, gives a drive belt tension of
  **180 to 210 N** and the routing and idler steps that go with it —
  `cu900ent-belt-slips-or-falls-off`. That is a technician procedure for one machine. Do not quote
  it as an owner task, and do not carry the figure to another model.

The same service manual is also the only Spirit bike document that lists wear parts (belt, PU
wheel, bearing, idler) — `cu900ent-wear-parts-inspection`. **None of these twelve owner's manuals
prints that warning**, so they name no wear part and no wear limit.
