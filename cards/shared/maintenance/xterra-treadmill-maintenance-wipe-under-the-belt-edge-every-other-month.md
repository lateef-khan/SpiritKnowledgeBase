The treadmill uses a very high-efficient, low-friction deck, and performance is maximized when the deck is kept as clean as possible.

- **Every other month**, use a soft, damp cloth or paper towel to wipe under the belt. **Use water only - no cleaners or abrasives.**
- The top of the textured belt is cleaned with a **mild soap and water solution and a nylon scrub brush**. Allow the belt to dry before using.

The books describe the area to wipe in two ways:

| Books | Where to wipe |
|---|---|
| TR150, TR200, TRX1000, TR260 | "the area between the frame and 6 - 8 inches under the belt" |
| TR300, TR6.6, TR6.4 | "the edge of the belt and the area between the belt edge and frame. Also reach as far as practical directly under the belt edge" |

The same paragraph goes on to say that belt dust during break-in is normal (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-belt-dust-and-black-particles-during-break-in`) and to vacuum monthly and under the motor hood every other month (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-vacuum-under-monthly-and-motor-cover-every-other-month`). The deeper clean, dragging a cloth between belt and deck, is `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-cleaning-between-belt-and-deck-with-a-cloth`.

The Dyaco service manuals for the TRX family print the same paragraph with **once a month** instead of every other month - a different figure on a different card, `xterra-trx-maintenance-service-manual-wipe-monthly-and-motor-hood-yearly`.
