**None of the six Spirit rower owner's manuals asks for lubrication, and none names a lubricant.**
Not for the seat rollers, not for the slide rail, not for the rowing belt or the chain, not for the
flywheel bearing.

This is an absence in the documents, not permission to invent one. **Do not tell a rower owner to
oil, grease or silicone anything on the strength of a treadmill or elliptical card.**

## What the manuals ask for instead

| Machine | What the book asks for |
|---|---|
| XRW600 2019, XRW600 2021, CRW800 2021 | Wipe the sweat path with a **damp cloth** after each workout; tighten loose hardware; level the machine (`ct800-2016-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`) |
| CRW800H2O, CRW900 2021 | Wipe the seat rails and frame **weekly with a lint free cloth**; treat the tank water; check belt and bungee tension every hundred hours (`spirit-water-rower-maintenance-weekly-rails-and-hundred-hour-belt-check`) |
| CRW800 2024 | A daily-to-biannual clean-and-inspect table with **no lubrication row** (`crw800-2024-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`) |

The CRW900 is explicit about the material to use on the moving parts a customer worries about most:
**clean the wheels and tracks weekly with a soft *dry* cloth**
(`crw900-2021-maintenance-seat-roller-and-track-cleaning`). Its only oil-like instruction is
cosmetic — furniture polish on the wood frame.

## The service manuals are different books

Two Spirit rower **service** manuals do name a lubricant, and neither is for the rollers or the track. The
**CRW900 service manual** sprays "any kind of lubrication grease, such as super grease" onto the strap pulley
shaft through the frame plug (`crw900-2021-maintenance-aerosol-super-grease-on-the-strap-pulley-shaft`). The **CRW800 2016 and 2021 service manuals** answer a noise at the seat
up/down adjustment knob with "some lubrication on the position of screw" — the knob's thread, lubricant unnamed
(`spirit-crw800-maintenance-noise-screw-kits-foot-pads-seat-knob-thread-slide-fan-flywheel`); the XRW600 service manual's noise list has no such item. Everything else in those books is clean,
tighten, level or replace. This card's absence is the owner's-manual absence, and it stands.

## What a rower does have that wears

Wear is answered by **inspection and replacement**, not by lubrication: the rowing belt and the
CRW900's bungee cord are checked **every hundred hours for correct tension and signs of wear**, and
a change is a job for a service representative. A noisy machine is answered by **tightening bolts
and levelling**, and the manuals say 90% of noise calls come down to loose hardware.

## Do not carry the other product lines' answers across

Spirit treadmills have deck lubrication cards with real intervals and named lubricants; the 2026
range has a deck that explicitly takes none
(`spirit-2026t-maintenance-deck-takes-no-lubricant`). Spirit bikes have their own no-lubricant
statement (`spirit-bike-maintenance-no-lubricant-belt-or-battery-service`). Those are different
machines; this card is the rower answer.

**The 8.5UE upper body ergometer's manual also names no lubricant** — checked the same way over
its 64 pages — but it is a different product line and is answered from its own care schedule,
`85ue-2025-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`.
