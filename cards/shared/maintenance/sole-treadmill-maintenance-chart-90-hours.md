The chart is a blank log the owner fills in (with a Date column). Its four task columns:

| Task | Interval |
|---|---|
| Vacuum under motor cover and check wiring | Every other month |
| Clean and inspect deck for lubrication | Every other month |
| Lubricate deck | Every 90 hours, or sooner if dry |
| Inspect belt tracking, adjust if necessary | Monthly |

The TD80 desk book prints the same four columns (garbled in extraction, read on the render). The TT8-2016 book prints the same chart form but its deck is wax-coated and must not be lubricated (see the wax-deck card); the TT8-2019+ and ST90 books print no chart at all.
