This is the yearly-hood variant. It differs from the extended vacuuming card only in the motor-hood interval (yearly here, every other month there); the underneath vacuum matches.

Dirt, dust and pet hair can block air inlets and accumulate on the running belt.

- **On a monthly basis, vacuum underneath** the treadmill to prevent buildup of dirt that can get sucked up into the inner workings under the motor cover.
- **Once a year**, remove the **black motor hood** and vacuum out dirt that may accumulate. **UNPLUG THE POWER CORD BEFORE THIS TASK.**
