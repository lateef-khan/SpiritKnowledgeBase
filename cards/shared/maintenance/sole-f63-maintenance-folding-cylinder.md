This is the cylinder generation. It differs from the telescoping-tube generation (locking tube assembly plus hand-pulled unlocking lever) in both halves of the procedure; the card follows these books.

**Do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and locked position.**

**To fold:** make certain the treadmill is at **minimum incline**. Lift the running deck until it is **secured by the cylinder in the center back of the base**.

**To unfold:** **using your foot, firmly push against the cylinder to release the folding mechanism.**

**Transport:** the treadmill is equipped with transport wheels that are engaged when the treadmill is folded; after folding simply roll it away. No book in this group prints a re-levelling step after the move.
