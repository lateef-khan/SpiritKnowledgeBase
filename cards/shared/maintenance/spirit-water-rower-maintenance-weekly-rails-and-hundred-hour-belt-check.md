Both water rower manuals print the same paragraph and the same table under **GENERAL MAINTENANCE**.
The CRW900 table has **five** rows, the CRW800H2O **four**: the water rower without a bungee cord
has no bungee cord row.

| Item | Time frame | Instructions |
|---|---|---|
| Seat and Seat Rails | **Weekly** | Wipe down seat rails with lint free cloth |
| Frame | **Weekly** | Wipe down frame with lint free cloth |
| Tank and Water Treatment | **12m to 2 years** | Follow the water treatment instructions (see below for where each book sends you) |
| **Bungee Cord — CRW900 only** | **Check every hundred hours** for correct tension and for signs of wear | The bungee cord should last for many years. If a bungee cord change is required, contact your local service representative. |
| Rowing Belt | **Check every hundred hours** for correct tension and for signs of wear | The rowing belt should provide many years of trouble free use. If a rowing belt change is required, contact your local service representative. |

The paragraph above the table says a preventative schedule should be broken into **daily, weekly and
monthly** activities — but the table itself gives only weekly and hourly intervals plus the water
figure. There is **no daily row** in either book.

It also says plainly that **Spirit is not responsible for performing regular inspection and
maintenance on your machine**, and that a facility must train its own staff in equipment inspection,
accident reporting and record keeping.

## The two books point at different pages for the water job, and one of them is wrong

The CRW900 row reads *"Follow instructions as specified in the Water Treatment Procedure section of
the manual [pg 33]"*, and printed page 33 of that book really is **TANK FILLING & WATER TREATMENT
PROCEDURES**. Its paragraph adds *"Details on each activity can be found in the Maintenance section
of this document (see pages 33 - 38)"*, which is also right: 33 is tank filling, 34 is changing the
water, 35 to 37 are detaching and reattaching the rower belt and tensioning the bungee, 38 is the
footplates.

The CRW800H2O row reads *"Follow instructions as specified on **page 25** of the manual"*, and
printed page 25 of that book is `TARGET TIME, DISTANCE, CALORIES MODE` — a console chapter. **Its
tank instructions are on printed page 18.** The same wrong reference appears a second time in its
troubleshooting table, which is carded at `spirit-water-rower-errors-tank-water-turns-cloudy`.

## Do not carry one machine's water figures onto the other

The 12-month-to-2-year row is the only water figure the table gives, and the two books mean
different things by it. The CRW800H2O asks for a purification tablet **every 6 months or as needed**
(`crw800h2o-maintenance-purification-tablet-every-six-months`); the CRW900 expects **8 to 12 months
near a sunlit window and 2 years or more in a darker room**
(`crw900-2021-maintenance-long-term-water-treatment-interval`). Their filling procedures, tablets and
capacities are different too.

## Neither book prints a post-workout noise section

There is no sweat-path wipe, no "squeak, thump, clicking" list, no levelling pad count and no wrench
size in either water rower manual — `squeak`, `crank arm`, `leveling pads` and `14mm` all return
zero hits across both texts including their OCR supplements. This table is the whole of their
maintenance chapter, and the sanitizing bullets follow it
(`ct900-sanitizing-equipment`). The air rowers are the other way round: they print the noise section
and no table at all.

**Neither table has a battery row**, although both consoles run on AA cells.
