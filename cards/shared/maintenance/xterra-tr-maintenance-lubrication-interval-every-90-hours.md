The belt and deck come **pre-lubricated**. Subsequent lubrication should be performed **every 90 hours of use, or if you notice that the deck is dry**.

- Reach between the belt and deck to verify there is lubrication present **every other month**. If there is none, lubricate **even though the "Lube" indicator is not lit on the console**.
- Otherwise, lubricate **when the console's lubrication reminder lights after 90 hours of use**.

The preventative maintenance chart in the same three books repeats "every 90 hours or sooner if dry" (`xterra-tr-maintenance-chart-lubricate-deck-every-90-hours`). Keeping the deck lubricated at the recommended intervals ensures the longest life possible; if the lubricant dries out, friction between belt and deck rises and stresses the drive motor, drive belt and electronic motor control board, which could result in failure of those components. **Failure to lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty.**

The procedure is `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-deck-lubrication-s-line-18-inches-from-the-motor-cover`, and only the XTERRA Fitness approved lubricant goes on (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-approved-lubricant-and-where-to-order`). Resetting the reminder is done in the console's Engineering Mode and belongs to the console cards. The Sole F80 book gives the same 90 hours and the same every-other-month check, with a 90-day floor these books do not print.
