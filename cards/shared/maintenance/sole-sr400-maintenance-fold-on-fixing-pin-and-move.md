**Unfold (HOW TO EXTEND THE MACHINE):** slightly pull up the handle and pull the fixing pin (I), then slowly put down the slide track (D) until the rear stabilizer is on the ground. Pull the fixing pin again to fix the machine. **Two people are recommended for this step.**

**Fold (HOW TO FOLD UP THE MACHINE):** slightly pull up the handle and pull the fixing pin (I), then slowly put up the slide track (D) until it is fixed.

**Move (HOW TO MOVE THE MACHINE):** the front stabilizer is built with transportation wheels. Stand at the rear of the machine and lift it up; once the weight transfers onto the transportation wheels, the machine moves easily to its new location.

This pin-and-track fold is specific to the SR400 - the SR500/SR550 books fold the aluminium rail up with no pin step (linked card). Console-battery replacement and pedal-strap adjustment are printed on the surrounding pages but belong to console operation and use, not to this card.
