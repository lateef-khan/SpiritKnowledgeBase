Nine books print the same short check list under **Maintenance**, in two wordings. The items and the
interval are the same; what differs is the sentence about a defective part.

| Check | Wording A (SB150, SB250, FB150, FB350, EU100, FS150) | Wording B (FB160, FB360, FB180) |
|---|---|---|
| Seat nuts | "Check that seat nuts are secure, check before each workout." (SB150, SB250, FB150, FB350 only - **the EU100 and FS150 do not print this line**) | "Check that seat nuts are secure." |
| Pedals | "Check that pedals are tight, pedals can work loose over time." | "Check that the pedals are tightened securely as they can loosen over time." |
| Stabilizer bolts | "Check that stabilizer bolts are tight, check before each workout this." | "Check that stabilizer bolts are tight." |
| Handlebar | "Check that handlebar is secure, if not, tighten, check before each workout this." | "Check that handlebar is secure." |
| Anything else | - | "Tighten any parts that may have loosened over time before proceeding with your workout." |

**Interval.** Wording A: "The safety of this produce [sic] can be maintained only if regular periodic
checks are made. Most checks can be performed once a week. However some checks should be made before each
workout, and are indicated as such below." Wording B: "Depending on your workout regimen, checks should
be made weekly or before each workout."

**A defective part.** Wording A: "Should a part become defective, replace it immediately. Do not allow use
of the equipment until it has been repaired." Wording B: "Should any part become defective, contact XTERRA
Customer Service immediately to order replacement parts or seek advice on proper service. Discontinue use
until the defective part has been repaired." Same instruction - stop using it - with B naming who to call.

The FB350 book's page footers still read "FB150"; it is the FB150 book with the model name changed on the
cover and in the introduction, and this page is identical in both.
