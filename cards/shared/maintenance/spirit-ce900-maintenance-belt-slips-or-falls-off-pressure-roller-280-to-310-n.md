**The figure is 280 to 310 newtons, a force, set at a pressure roller. The CU900ENT bike, whose book
is otherwise this book, sets its belt to 180 to 210 N (`cu900ent-belt-slips-or-falls-off`); the
residential XE and CE800 ellipticals set a J-bolt nut to a sonic-meter frequency. Do not carry any
of the three figures to the others.**

Section 7.1 as printed: "If the belt is found to slip and fall off, please refer to Procedure
6.11.1, Procedure 6.11.2, Procedure 6.11.3, and Procedure 6.11.4, **the tension of the pressure
roller can be adjusted to adjust the tension, belt tension maintained at 280 ~ 310N**."

Those four references are, in order:

| Ref | What it covers |
|---|---|
| 6.11.1 | Crank, left and right chain covers and the shaft connecting rod off; **remove the nut and hook type screws** |
| 6.11.2 | Remove the belt - "when the belt is installed in the middle of the belt, belt tension maintained at **280 ~ 310N**" |
| 6.11.3 | **Press the roller (3 pcs)** when the roller is locked to the flywheel |
| 6.11.4 | The four flywheel screws |

That is the only belt tension figure these books print. The nut and hook-type screws on the idler
wheel bracket are what the "pressure roller" is adjusted with; the books give no wrench size and no
feel test. The removal steps themselves are replacement procedures and are carded in the assembly
section.

The CE1000ENT (2023) book numbers the same steps 12.21 and calls the belt section 13.1; the CE900ENT
book is the CE900 (2021) book renumbered. No figure changes.
