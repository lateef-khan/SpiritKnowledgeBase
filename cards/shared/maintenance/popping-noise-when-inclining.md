A popping noise **only** when the treadmill inclines is almost always the gas cylinder for the folding function drying out.

The cylinder lubricates itself, so the more the deck is folded, the better it gets.

Have the customer lift and lower the deck into the folded position 10 to 15 times. That should lubricate the cylinder and stop the noise.
