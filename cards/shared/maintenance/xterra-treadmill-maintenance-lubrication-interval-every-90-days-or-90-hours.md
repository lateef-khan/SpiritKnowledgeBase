As part of the routine maintenance schedule, **belt lubrication and cleaning should be performed every 90 days, after 90 hours of use, or earlier if you notice that the deck is dry or dirty.** Also check belt lubrication **before first use**: with the machine off and unplugged, reach between the running belt and the top of the base to verify that lubricant is present.

Keeping the deck lubricated at the recommended intervals ensures the longest life possible; if the lubricant dries out, friction between belt and deck rises and stresses the drive motor, drive belt and electronic motor control board, which could result in failure of those components. **Failure to clean and lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty.**

**Two of these books print a second, different interval.** The TR95H care schedule and LUBE reminder, and the TR75H lubrication chapter, say **112 miles** (the TR75H adds "or every 3 months, whichever comes first"): `xterra-tr-maintenance-lubrication-interval-every-112-miles`. Neither book reconciles the two. The TR65, TR75 and TR85 Lube-message paragraph reads "(Use a total of 90 hours.)" - the TR75 and TR85 misprint it as "90s hours".

The TRX1400 service manual says every 90 hours or if the deck is dry, with an every-other-month check, and names a "Lube-n-Walk kit" as an alternative lubricant. The procedure is `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-deck-lubrication-s-line-18-inches-from-the-motor-cover`. The console's LUBE reminder and how to clear it belong to the console cards. The Spirit XT 2023 and Sole F80 books print the same two figures; those are other brands.
