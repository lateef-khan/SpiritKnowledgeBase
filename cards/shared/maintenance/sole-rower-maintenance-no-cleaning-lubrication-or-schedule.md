**None of the four rower books prints any rail cleaning, seat-roller, chain/belt/strap, foot-stretcher or water-tank care, and none prints a care schedule.** (These are dry rowers; there is no water tank to care for.) The only maintenance-section topics in this wave's rower books are folding and moving (linked cards).

The warranty pages then demand what the books never specify:

- SR500-2019 and SR500-2022 make the consumer responsible for **"routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual"** - but no page specifies any.
- SR550-2023 goes further: items 2-3 require general maintenance **"i.e. belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under motor cover, and lubrication. See all components under General Maintenance section"**, and item 8 cites **"(See All Components under General Maintenance)"**. This rower has no belt, no deck, no motor cover and **no General Maintenance section anywhere in the book** - both passages are pasted treadmill boilerplate. The ruling: quote the warranty's words when asked about warranty, and say the referenced section does not exist in this book.

Proved twice per book: a loose-word search (maint, clean, lub, tighten, rail, chain, tank, daily, weekly, monthly) over native text plus all OCR supplements, and a read of every page the TOC points at.
