The books open their Q&A with a warning that a noise is hard to place, and a list of where it can
come from - front to back:

- the chain cover and the round disk, the EPE (foam) and round disk, the sleeve, the **Cross Bar**;
- the **belt and belt pulley**, the **Idler Wheel Plate**;
- the **swing arm** and **swing arm axle**;
- the **flywheel** and the **steel cable**;
- the **slide wheel** and the **crank arm**, "during assembly along with side Inclinable Rail
  Assemblys as well as pedals".

**"The main reasons are usually not enough lubrication, unsmooth rotations, and loose screws."**
Because the machine is a linked design, "resolving noise issue will vary from machine to machine
depending on actual situation."

So the three things to do are the three care items of the same chapter: lubricate the pivots
(`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-swing-arm-noise-thick-lubricant-on-the-axle-then-the-6005-bearing`,
`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-connecting-arm-and-slide-wheel-lubrication-and-parallel-alignment`),
clean and re-lubricate the rail
(`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-rough-motion-loose-screws-then-alcohol-wipe-and-re-lubricate-the-rail`)
and tighten what is loose (`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-shaking-loose-screws-and-pedal-carriage-bolts`).
A rubbing side case or round disk and a noisy flywheel are diagnosed rather than lubricated and are
carded in the errors section.

## The CE800 (2016) list

The XE890B book names the same places in fewer words - "From Chain Cover rubbing Crank Round Disk
Cover, Bushing Housing and Cross Bar, Belt and Drive Pulley, Idler Wheel Assembly, Handlebar and
Mast shaft, Flywheel and Cable to Sliding Wheels and Rail Assembly, even Pedals" - and gives the
same three causes: "Insufficient lubrication, smoothness or loose screws are major causes."

The generator-driven CE900 family answers noise with one specific bearing and one normal grainy
feel (`spirit-ce900-maintenance-noise-and-feet-feeling-graininess-normal-fish-eye-bearing-lubrication`);
the owner's manuals' page is `spirit-xe-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`.
