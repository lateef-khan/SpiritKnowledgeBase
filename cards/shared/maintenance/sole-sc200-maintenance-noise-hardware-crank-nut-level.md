If a squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling develops, the manual says the main cause is most likely one of two reasons, then prints three:

**I. The hardware was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.** All bolts installed during assembly need to be tightened **as much as possible**. It may be necessary to use a **larger wrench than the one provided** if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently. The manual states that **ninety percent of calls to the service department for noise issues can be traced to loose hardware**.

**II. The crank arm nut needs to be retightened.**

**III. If squeaks or other noises persist, check that the unit is properly leveled.** There are **2 leveling pads on the bottom of the rear stabilizer**; adjust them with a **14mm wrench (or adjustable wrench)**.

This is **not** the elliptical two-cause card: those books name dirt on the rear rails as cause II (`sole-elliptical-maintenance-noise-two-causes-90-percent`). The SC200 books name the crank arm nut instead, and carry no rail-cleaning step. The SC300 book is close but different again - no crank arm nut, levelers front and rear (linked card).
