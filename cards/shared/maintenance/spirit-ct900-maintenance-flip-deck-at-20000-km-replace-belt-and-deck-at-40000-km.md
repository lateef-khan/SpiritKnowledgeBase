**Three different deck-life figures are printed for these two machines — two in the service manual and one in the owner's manual — and this card holds the only one that also says when the belt and deck are replaced.** Read the whole card before quoting a number.

## The service manual's table

| Total using distance | 20,000 km / 12,500 miles | 40,000 km / 25,000 miles |
|---|---|---|
| Task | Flipping deck | Replacing belt and deck |

Notes printed under the table:

- **Please clean wax on roller during flipping deck or replacing belt.** (The book prints "belt/belt".)
- The low maintenance (routine monthly cleaning), dual-sided hard wax deck is designed to withstand up to **20,000 kilometer / 12,500 miles on each side**. If the original side of the deck shows significant wear, it needs to be flipped. Contact your service technician for assistance. **Do not apply any type of lubricant or wax to the surface.**

The table is a picture on the page; the figures above were read from a render, not from the text layer.

## The same book says 32,000 km two pages earlier

Section 10.1 Tread belt and Deck (9.1 in the CT900ENT book) prints the identical sentence with a different number: the deck "is designed to withstand up to **32,000 kilometers** on each side". Nothing in either book reconciles 32,000 km with the 20,000 km / 12,500 miles of the table, and the 12,500-mile figure is not a conversion of either — 20,000 km is about 12,400 miles, 32,000 km about 19,900.

## The owner's manuals say 20,000 miles

The CT900 and CT900ENT owner's manuals print **20,000 miles on each side** (`ct900-belt-deck-cleaning`, `ct900ent-belt-deck-cleaning`), which is what 32,000 km rounds to. So the service manual's prose agrees with the owner's manual in a different unit, and its table cuts the figure to 12,500 miles. Quote the owner's-manual figure to a customer; quote the table to a technician deciding when to replace parts, because it is the only place the 40,000 km replacement point is printed.

## The inspection that tells you the side is done

The schedule on the same page ticks "Inspect for deck wear — Visual" **semi-annually** (`40t-2026-maintenance-schedule`, which the CT900 and CT900ENT service manuals print). Neither book ties either distance to the console odometer.
