**This is the two-item list of the adjustable-stride CE850 and XE895 books, and it prints no torque
figure. The XE195/XE295/XE395 books print four joints with 500-600 and 550-600 lbs figures
(`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-slip-check-four-joints-500-to-600-and-550-to-600-lbs`); do not read
those numbers onto these two machines.**

1. **The CAP Socket Head Cap Bolt 3/8" x 2-1/4" on top and the nyloc nut 3/8" x 11T must be
   tightened securely.** That is the bolt through the bushing housing of the pedal arm - the one the
   pedal-arm replacement procedure sets with a torque wrench "up to 550lb" when the housing goes
   back on; the Q&A page itself gives no number.
2. **Belt:** "This issue appears quite often, because after a period of time, most belts will become
   loose from use depending on usage time. Solution will depend on weight and adjustment methods,
   refer to belt disassembly procedures for references." The adjustment is the hook-screw cap with a
   13 mm wrench to 190 Hz - `spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-belt-slipping-hook-screw-cap-13-mm-190-hz`.

There is no rod-end-bearing row and no cross-bar row on these two books.

The stride mechanism's own looseness - a slide wheel that has worked loose on its adjustment plate -
is a different check with an 11 mm wrench:
`spirit-ce850-2016-maintenance-pedal-bases-clean-and-lubricate-the-track-and-38-mm-slide-wheel`.
