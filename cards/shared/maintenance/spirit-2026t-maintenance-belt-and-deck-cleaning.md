The machine uses a very high-efficient, low-friction deck and belt. Performance is maximized when
the bed is kept as clean as possible.

- Use a soft, damp cloth or paper towel to wipe the edge of the belt and the area between the belt
  edge and the frame. Also reach as far as practical directly under the belt edge.
- Do this **once a month** to extend belt and deck life.
- **Use water only — no cleaners or abrasives.**
- A mild soap and water solution along with a **nylon scrub brush** will clean the top of the
  textured belt.
- Allow the belt to dry before using.

## The soap ratio is only printed for the 8.0T

The 4.0T and 7.0T say "a mild soap and water solution" and give **no ratio**. The 8.0T's cleaning
table gives the ratio as **10:1 water to soap** — see `80t-2026-maintenance-cleaning-schedule`.
Do not carry the 8.0T ratio onto the other two machines; their manuals do not print one.

Nothing goes onto the deck itself: see `spirit-2026t-maintenance-deck-takes-no-lubricant`.
