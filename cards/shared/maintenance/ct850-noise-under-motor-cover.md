| Condition | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| Noise under motor cover. | 1. Worn brushes or bearings on motor. | 1. Replace with new motor. |
| | 2. Front roller bearings are defective. | 2. Replace with new front roller. |
| | 3. Drive belt is misadjusted (too tight or too loose). | 3. Adjust motor position. |

Printed identically in both CT850 manuals.

Note that cause 3 is the **motor drive belt**, not the tread belt. The 2016 checklist warns
separately that a loose motor drive belt makes the tread belt feel as though it is hesitating —
see `ct850-tread-belt-hesitates-when-stepped-on`.

For a noise at the other end of the machine see `ct850-noise-in-the-rear-of-the-treadmill`.
