The drive motor sits on a mounting plate with a bolt at each corner, and a threaded tensioner stud that runs up through a cross bracket behind the motor. Loosening the four plate bolts lets the motor slide. The tensioner stud then pushes it away from the front roller.

1. Loosen the **two bolts on the front edge** of the motor mounting plate. These are the pair beside the green and yellow ground wire and the motor leads. The source marks them in **red**.
2. Loosen the **two bolts on the opposite edge** of the same plate. The source marks them in **blue**.
3. Loosen the **hex nut on top of the cross bracket**, on the threaded tensioner stud behind the motor. The source marks it in **green**.
4. Loosen the **hex bolt head below that bracket**, on the tensioner stud itself. Turning it pushes the motor away and tensions the drive belt. The source marks it in **orange**.
5. Tighten the nut on top of the cross bracket (green).
6. Tighten the two bolts on the opposite edge of the plate (blue).
7. Tighten the two bolts on the front edge of the plate (red).

**Note**: the source is a set of photographs with coloured circles and no written part names. The names above were read from the pictures.
