The treadmill is designed so that the belt remains reasonably centred while in use. It is normal for some belts to drift near one side, depending on a user's gait and whether they favour one leg. **Adjust only if the belt continues to move toward one side during use.**

1. A **6 mm Allen wrench** is provided. Make tracking adjustments **on the left side bolt** only.
2. Set belt speed at **3 mph**.
3. If the belt is **too close to the left side**, turn the bolt **only a 1/4 turn to the right (clockwise)** and wait a few minutes for the belt to adjust itself. Continue in 1/4 turns until the belt stabilizes in the centre of the running deck.
4. If the belt is **too close to the right side**, turn the bolt **counter-clockwise**.

A small adjustment can make a dramatic difference which may not be apparent right away. The belt may need periodic tracking adjustment depending on use; adjustments become less of a concern as the belt is used. Proper belt tracking is an owner responsibility common with all treadmills.

Every one of these pages closes with: **damage to the running belt resulting from improper tracking / tension adjustments is not covered under the XTERRA Fitness warranty** - except the TRX1400 book, whose printed line reads "the **Spirit Fitness** warranty".

The TRX2500, TRX3500 and TRX4500 service manuals print the same procedure (6 mm wrench, item 97) and add the target: continue until "the edge is within the range marked on the motor hood". The TRX1400 service manual gives different figures - a 10 mm wrench at 3 to 5 kph - on `trx1400-2023-maintenance-service-manual-tracking-10-mm-allen-wrench-3-to-5-kph`. The TR150 and TR260 books name a **5 mm** wrench: `xterra-tr-maintenance-belt-tracking-5-mm-allen-wrench-left-bolt-3-mph`.
