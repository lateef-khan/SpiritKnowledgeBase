**These five books print no tracking procedure** - no left-side bolt, no 3 mph, no quarter-turn rule. Their only rear-roller instruction is the tension paragraph, which says to keep tension balanced on both sides "otherwise the belt shifts from the higher-tension end to the other" (`xterra-tr-maintenance-belt-tension-rear-roller-screws-m6-l-wrench-103`, `xterra-ws-maintenance-belt-tension-rear-roller-screws-5-mm-allen-wrench`).

The TR65, TR75 and TR85 troubleshooting page still prints the row "Tread-belt does not stay centered / hesitates when walked or run on - ... track the belt slightly off-center to the side opposite from the belt movement. See the Maintenance & Care section on Tread-belt Tension" - it sends the reader to a section that only balances the two screws (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-belt-drifts-or-hesitates-under-one-foot`). The WS200 and WS300 troubleshooting table has no centring row at all.

How this was proved: a loose search of each book for *centre*, *center*, *drift*, *left side bolt* and *3 mph* found only the app's "exercise tracking", chest-strap and Message Center hits and, in the TR family, that one Q&A row; the belt-adjustment page was rendered and read (the text layer under it carries an unprinted block about an "L Allen Wrench (132)", not a tracking procedure). The Xterra books that do print the procedure are `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-belt-tracking-6-mm-allen-wrench-left-bolt-3-mph` and `xterra-tr-maintenance-belt-tracking-5-mm-allen-wrench-left-bolt-3-mph`.
