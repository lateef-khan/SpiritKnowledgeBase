**The four SW books (dumbbell stand, half rack, two adjustable dumbbells) and the SRVO-complete bench book print no upholstery cleaning, no bolt re-tightening schedule, no guide-rod or cable care, no moving procedure and no care schedule of any kind.** The whole of what they say about maintenance is the same two boilerplate lines:

- Precautions: **"Do perform regular preventative maintenance."**
- Inspection block: **"EQUIPMENT MAINTENANCE - Preventative maintenance is the key to smooth operating equipment ... Equipment needs to be inspected at regular intervals"**, plus: only qualified persons to adjust/repair, and replace defective parts immediately without operating the unit until repaired.

No interval is ever named - "regular intervals" is the closest the books get - so there is nothing to put an interval card on. Each book's warranty still references maintenance with nothing specified, the same hollow reference the rower and CC81 books make. The bench book's fold/unfold steps are assembly, not care.

This is the expected shape for these books, not a missing page: all five were read end to end (native text plus every OCR supplement) and none has a care chapter to miss. The powered SRVO unit is the exception - its book prints a real GENERAL MAINTENANCE list (linked cards) - which is why `srvo-2021` is not on this card.
