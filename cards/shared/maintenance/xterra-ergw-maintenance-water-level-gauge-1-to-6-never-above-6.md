**This is the six-step gauge of the ERG550W, ERG600W and ERG650W. The ERG750W's tank is marked in litres**
(`erg750w-2025-specs-water-tank-capacity-8-to-12-7-liters-min-standard-max`) **and the
ERG800W's only in Min and Max** (`erg800w-2023-maintenance-clean-filtered-water-changed-every-half-year-no-tablet-named`).

Printed under WATER LEVEL, the same in all three books:

- "See Fig. A. The water level gauge is on the side of the tank. **The maximum fill level is 6. Never fill it
  over this limit. Filling the tank over this limit will void the warranty.**"
- "The resistance depends on the water level in the tank. **Water level 1 is the lowest resistance. Level 6 is
  the highest resistance.**"

There is no resistance lever on these machines: the fill level *is* the resistance setting, and the ERG550W
and ERG650W consoles ask for it at power-up so that their figures match (the console section's cards). Add or siphon out water to
change it (`xterra-ergw-maintenance-changing-the-water-siphon-pump-out-then-funnel-in`).

## Only the ERG550W prints the capacity behind each mark

| Level | Litres | US gallons | US quarts |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | 6.5 | 1.72 | 6.86 |
| L2 | 7.1 | 1.87 | 7.50 |
| L3 | 7.8 | 2.06 | 8.24 |
| L4 | 8.4 | 2.22 | 8.87 |
| L5 | 9 | 2.38 | 9.50 |
| L6 | 10 | 2.64 | 10.56 |

The ERG600W and ERG650W books print the gauge rules and **no capacity figure of any kind**; do not quote
the ERG550W's litres to an owner of those machines as their book's figure.

Overfilling is one of the two warranty exclusions printed on this page rather than in the warranty chapter;
the other is chlorine (`xterra-ergw-maintenance-tap-water-only-never-pool-chlorine-or-bleach`). Spirit's
CRW800H2O prints the same 1-to-6 gauge and the same two exclusions for its own tank
(`crw800h2o-maintenance-filling-and-emptying-the-tank`, `crw800h2o-warranty-void-chlorine-and-overfilling`).
Other brand, separate cards.
