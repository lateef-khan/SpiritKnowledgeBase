**This deck is rated in kilometres, and the figure is not the same as the mileage figure Spirit
prints elsewhere.**

The low maintenance (routine monthly cleaning), dual-sided hard wax deck is designed to withstand up
to **20,000 kilometers on each side**. If the original side of the deck shows significant wear, it
needs to be flipped. Contact your service technician for assistance.

**Do not apply any type of lubricant or wax to the surface** —
`spirit-2026t-maintenance-deck-takes-no-lubricant`.

## The 2024 reissue cut the figure, it did not restate it

The manuals these four replaced print a different number in a different unit:

| Machine | Deck | Rated life a side |
|---|---|---|
| CT800 2024, CT850 2024, CT800ENT 2024, CT850ENT 2024 | dual-sided hard wax | **20,000 kilometres** |
| CT800 2012, CT800 2016, CT850 2018, CT900, CT900ENT | dual-sided hard wax | 20,000 **miles** |
| CT800 2020, CT800ENT 2022, CT850 2016, CT850 2020, CT850ENT 2022 | proprietary belt/deck | 4,000 **hrs** |

20,000 kilometres is about **12,400 miles** — roughly **62%** of the 20,000-mile figure the same
sentence carried on the earlier hard-wax machines. A customer who was told 20,000 miles for a
CT800ENT 2022 and reads 20,000 kilometres in the 2024 book is not misreading it.

**Do not convert either figure into the other.** Neither manual prints a conversion; the 2026 4.0T
is the only Spirit treadmill that prints both, as "20,000 miles (32,000 kilometers)"
(`40t-2026-maintenance-deck-life-and-flipping`). Quote the unit the customer's own manual prints.

## Why this figure was previously treated as an artefact

Until these four manuals, "20,000 kilometers on each side" appeared in the repository only inside
the duplicated *GENERAL MAINTENANCE* block that sits invisibly over the belt-and-deck page of the
CT900 and the 2026 4.0T/7.0T/8.0T, where it contradicts the visible text and is correctly
disregarded (`ct900-belt-deck-cleaning`, `40t-2026-maintenance-deck-life-and-flipping`).

**On the 2024 machines it is the visible text.** These four PDFs carry the same invisible layer,
but the kilometre sentence is printed on the page as well: it was read off a 300 dpi render and
confirmed by eye. Spirit adopted the wording that used to be the ghost.

## The inspection that tells you the side is done

The schedule table gives the **deck** a **biannual** visual inspection and the **drive belt** the
same (`ct900-maintenance-schedule`). **None of the four manuals ties the figure to the console
odometer**, and none says where to read accumulated distance for this purpose; the manuals mention
the odometer only as something the engineering menu can reset. Read the wear off the deck.
