A chirp from under the motor hood cover is usually the drive belt as it moves.

First make sure the drive belt is tensioned correctly and is not damaged. If it all looks good:

1. Take a regular bar of soap. Do not wet it.
2. Run the machine at its lowest speed.
3. Hold the bar of soap against the ribbed side of the drive belt.
4. Hold it there for a few minutes.

This lubricates the drive belt and quiets the chirp.
