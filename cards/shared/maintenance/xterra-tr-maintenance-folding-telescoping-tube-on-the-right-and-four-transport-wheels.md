**CAUTION: do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and locked position.** Remove the power cord from the front of the unit to avoid possible damage. Use both handrails to manoeuvre the unit to the desired position.

**To fold**: make certain the treadmill is at **minimum incline**. Lift the running deck until it is secured by the **locking telescoping tube assembly on the right side of the base**. You will hear it pop into place when secure.

**To unfold**: apply slight forward pressure on the running deck with your **left hand** - at the rear roller area, to relieve pressure on the locking system. Pull out on the **round knob** and slowly lower the running deck to the floor. **The deck will lower unassisted when it reaches about waist high.**

**To move it**: the treadmill has **four transport wheels that are engaged when the treadmill is folded**. After folding, simply roll the treadmill away.

The Spirit XT 2015 service manuals print nearly this page with the tube "in the centre back of the base"; that is another brand (`spirit-xt-2015-assembly-folding-unfolding-and-transport`). The lighter Xterra books latch into the left upright on a pin or knob and have two wheels: `xterra-tr-maintenance-folding-lock-pin-on-the-left-upright-and-two-transport-wheels`, `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-folding-round-knob-on-the-left-upright-lean-back-on-two-wheels`.
