Under *Intended conditions of use - Environment including hygienic requirements*, every one of these five
books states, word for word:

> **Hygienic requirements: there is no particular restriction on hygienic requirements.**

The same chapter says the bike is *intended for hospital use, clinic use, home use and research in academic
institutions*, that frequency of use is *dependent on therapist's plan*, and that the product *is intended to be
fixed*. So the manufacturer contemplates clinical use by a succession of patients and sets no hygiene restriction
in the chapter that defines the intended use. That is the answer to give, plainly, before reaching for a
cleaning procedure.

## What each book pairs the line with

The line is the same in all five; the cleaning chapter behind it is not, and the two halves must be quoted
together:

| Book | Cleaning printed in its care chapter |
|---|---|
| Dyaco MED 7.0R 2021 | the damp-cloth wipe-down only - no sanitizing, no disinfectant (`spirit-med-bike-maintenance-dyaco-editions-print-no-sanitizing-or-disinfection`) |
| Spirit 7.0R 2025, 7.0U 2025 | the damp-cloth wipe-down and the standard **75% isopropyl** sanitizing bullets with a conditioner on upholstery (`ct900-sanitizing-equipment`) |
| Spirit 8.0U 2025, 8.5R 2025 | a seven-step clinical routine - **20 ml of 70% or greater isopropyl alcohol**, a 15-minute application, a 3-to-5-minute dwell (`80t-2026-maintenance-disinfection-procedure`) - and, two pages later, a warning list that says **cleaning agents and disinfectants containing alcohol are not permitted** (`80t-2026-maintenance-cleaning-cautions`) |

So a 7.0R and an 8.0U, with the identical "no particular restriction" sentence, print different strengths,
and the 8.0U and 8.5R contradict themselves on alcohol. Read the machine's own care chapter; do not carry the
20 ml routine onto a 7.0R or the 75% bullets onto an 8.0U.

## The same sentence on the rehabilitation steppers

The MS300 2021 and the MED 7.0S and 7.5S steppers print the identical line and are carded at
`ms300-2021-maintenance-hygienic-requirements-and-cleaning`; the wording is Dyaco's medical-device template, not
a statement about any one machine.
