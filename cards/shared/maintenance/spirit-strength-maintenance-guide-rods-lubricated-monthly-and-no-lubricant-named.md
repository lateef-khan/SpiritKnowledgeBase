**Two answers, and the second one is the one people are surprised by.**

1. **The guide rods are lubricated monthly.** They are the **only** part any of these
   thirty-five machines has a lubrication row for. Nine CSD machines, the two CSI machines and the
   CSF-HRAC add one further row, *Lubricate: Add on Track*, also monthly.
2. **The maintenance page names no lubricant.** The row reads *Guide Rods*, a tick sits under
   *Monthly*, and there is no product, no type and no quantity anywhere on the page or in the
   chapter.

The guide rods are also **cleaned** on their own row: monthly on thirty-three of these machines,
weekly on the two CSI. Cleaning and lubricating are two rows in two groups.

## What the manuals do and do not tell you

| | Printed? |
|---|---|
| Interval | **Yes — monthly** |
| Which part | **Yes — the guide rods** (plus *Add on Track* on twelve of them) |
| Lubricant type | **No** |
| Quantity or coverage | **No** |
| How to apply it | **No** |
| What to clean the rod with first | **No** |

## The only lubricant named anywhere in the range, and where it is

**Three CSS manuals name one — and they name it in the assembly chapter, not on this page.** The
CSS-LATP, CSS-LEXT and CSS-SQSC weight-stack step reads *"After the weight stack is installed,
lubricate the Guide Rods using **a thin coat of clear silicone** prior to attaching the weight
stack shrouds."* See
`spirit-strength-maintenance-guide-rods-take-a-thin-coat-of-clear-silicone`.

**That is a build instruction for the initial assembly, and it is printed in three books out of
thirty-six.** The other thirty-two print the same step with the lubricant clause removed —
*"lubricate the Guide Rods prior to attaching the weight stack shrouds"* — or, on the CSD, CSF and
CSI books, do not print the step at all.

**The monthly maintenance row names no lubricant on any of the thirty-five, including those three.**

## What to say to a customer whose stack is sticking

Say what the book says: the rods are cleaned and lubricated monthly, and Spirit's own strength
manuals do not specify a product. Clear silicone is the only thing Spirit prints for a Spirit
strength guide rod, and it is printed for three machines at build time. If you go beyond that, say
you are going beyond the manual.

**Do not answer from a treadmill or elliptical card.** Deck lubricant and rail lubricant are
different parts on different machines, and a wrong product on a guide rod attracts dust into the
bushings.

## The one Spirit strength machine that does name a lubricant on its maintenance page

The **ST800FT functional trainer**: *Clean & Lubricate: Guide Rods with a **Teflon (PTFE) based
lubricant***, monthly in a facility and every 3 months at home, plus a **Silicon or Teflon based
lubricant spray** on seat sleeves and guide rods and a **light grease (lithium, super lube, etc.)**
repack for the linear bearings —
`spirit-strength-st800ft-maintenance-teflon-guide-rods-and-a-lithium-bearing-repack`. Different
machine, different template; the figure does not carry across.

## The machine with nothing to lubricate

**CSF-UPRB** has no *Lubricate* group at all and the word *lubricate* does not occur in its manual:
`spirit-strength-csf-uprb-maintenance-schedule-of-six-actions`.
