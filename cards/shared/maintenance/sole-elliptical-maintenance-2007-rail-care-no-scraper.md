**This is the 2007 short template: dirt build-up is the only cause printed - no loose-hardware cause, no `I cannot stress` sentence, no 90% figure, and no thumbnail/scraper sentence.** (The book prints `thin cost`, a typo for thin coat.)

1. Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each workout (same sentence as every other B book, separate card).
2. **If a squeak, or rough feeling, develops the cause is most likely dirt build-up on the rear rails and poly-urethane wheels.** Clean the rails and wheels with a lint free cloth and rubbing alcohol. Then apply a small amount of lubricant on the rails with your fingers or a lint free cloth. You only need a thin coat of lubrication, wipe off any excess. The lubricant type is not named.
3. **If squeaks or other noises persist, check that the unit is properly leveled. There are 4 leveling pads on the bottom of the rear rails, use a 14mm wrench (or adjustable wrench) to adjust the levelers.**
