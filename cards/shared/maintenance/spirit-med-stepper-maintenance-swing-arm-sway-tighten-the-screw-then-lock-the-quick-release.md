**Two steps in order: the pivot screw, then the quick-release lock.** Both medical recumbent steppers print the same
pair; the 7.5S calls the part a swing arm, the 8.5S a handlebar.

| | 7.5S | 8.5S |
|---|---|---|
| Step 1 | 7.2.1 "If you find the swing arm is shaking during the step-on, lock the screw as shown in Figure 7.2.1" | 7-2 item 1 "If you [find] the handlebar wobbling during pedaling, please tighten the bolts, as shown in Figure 7.2.1" |
| Step 2 | 7.2.2 "If the phenomenon of shaking still exists, check whether the **quick release telescopic tube** adjustment [is] to the locking position as shown in Figure 7.2.2" | 7-2 item 2 "If the wobbling persists, check whether the **quick release lever on the handle slider** is tightened and in the locked position, as shown in Figure 7.2.2" |

The screw of step 1 is the one the preventative check looks at (`spirit-med-stepper-maintenance-preventative-check-pedals-and-swing-arm-handlebars-for-looseness`). The quick release of step 2 is the user's
handle-length adjuster on the handle slider - parts-list item 65, *Quick Release lever*, two per machine on the
7.5S - and a swing arm that still sways with its screw tight is one whose slider was left unlocked.

No torque and no wrench size are printed for the screw. Removing the swing arm altogether is 6.2 (7.5S) / 6-2
(8.5S), assembly cards.

**Not the seat.** A seat that will not lock or slide on these machines is a stretched lever cable taken up on thumb
nuts (`spirit-med-stepper-errors-seat-will-not-lock-thumb-nuts`), and an unstable *swivel* seat on the CRS800S is a
wheel-plate adjustment (`crs800s-2021-maintenance-swivel-seat-unstable-seat-wheel-adjustment-plate-11-mm-and-13-mm-wrenches`).

## The 2025 7.0S and 7.5S service manuals print the 7.5S column word for word

The **7.0S 2025** and **7.5S 2025** books carry 7.2.1 "lock the screw" and 7.2.2 "check whether the quick release
telescopic tube adjustment [is] to the locking position" exactly as the 7.5S -01 book does, with the same two
figures. Nothing is added and no torque appears.
