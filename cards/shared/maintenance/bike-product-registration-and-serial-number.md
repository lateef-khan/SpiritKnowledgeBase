**Record your serial number.** Please record the Serial Number of this fitness product in the space provided in the manual.

**Register your purchase.** The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to SOLE. You can also go to **www.soletreadmills.com** under the **support** tab to register online.

The manuals do not say where the serial number label is on the machine, and they do not say registration is required for warranty cover.

**One bike in this family has no registration page at all:** the B74/R72 2015 owner's manual. Its contents run straight from the electrical instructions to the parts lists, with no Product Registration section and no Record Your Serial Number box. Do not tell a B74 or R72 2015 owner to look for one.

The DT-3268 console manuals and the SB900 2023 tension knob manual also carry no registration page; their machines are covered by the owner's manuals above.
