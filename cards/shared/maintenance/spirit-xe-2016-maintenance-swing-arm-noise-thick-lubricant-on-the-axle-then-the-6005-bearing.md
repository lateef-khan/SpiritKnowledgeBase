**The bearing named here is the 6005. The Sole E25 book, otherwise the same Dyaco text, names a 6003
(`e25-2019-swing-arm-noise`); do not carry a bearing number across the brands.**

"To make assembly easier for consumers, there will be minor difference in size (0.05mm~0.07mm).
Therefore, it is normal for a little shake/wobble to exist, but after prolonged use, small parts
due to friction wear will generate noise."

1. **The solution is to add thick lubricant into the top of the diameter-25 x 296 L Swing Arm
   Axle.**
2. **If the noise is emitting from the bottom bracket, the 6005 bearings need to be replaced.**

That is the whole answer for a wobble or a creak at the swing arm pivot. The 2016 XE books do not
name the lubricant, an interval, or a torque for the pivot bolt.

## Which books print the size tolerance

The **0.05 mm to 0.07 mm** clearance is printed on the XE195, XE295, XE395 and XE395ENT. The XE795
(2016) and XE795 (2023) books print the same paragraph but say only "minor difference in size"
with no figure. Nothing else changes.

## The two CE800 books say it in fewer words

The CE800 (2016) book, titled XE890B: "Handlebars are not absolutely tight for easy to assemble to
the end user. It is normal for handlebars to shift a little bit. However, noises may be caused as
time lasts because parts wear. The solution for the noise is to **apply some grease on mast
shaft**. If the noises are from button bracket, it needs to change **6005_Bearing**." The "mast
shaft" is the swing arm axle; the bearing is the same 6005.

The CE800 (2020) book has one sentence: "If there is a noise from the swing arm, maybe the bearing
is worn require **apply grease or replacement**." It names neither the axle nor a bearing number;
the 6005 above is not stated for that machine.

## Not the handrail keys

Steps 2 and 3 of the same section, on the XE195, XE295, XE395 and XE395ENT books, are about handrail keys that stop working -
remove the stickers and the M5x20mm screws, check the wire connection between the swing arm and
the lower arm. That is a wiring check, not care, and is not part of this card.
