Dirt, dust and **pet hair** can block air inlets and accumulate on the running belt.

- **Vacuum underneath your treadmill on a monthly basis** to prevent excess build-up of dirt that can get sucked up and get into the inner workings under the motor cover.
- **Once a year, remove the black motor hood and vacuum out dirt that may accumulate.**
- **UNPLUG POWER CORD BEFORE THIS TASK.**

**The yearly figure is what separates these four machines from the rest of the range.** The XT185 2015 and XT285 2015 manuals, all five XT 2023 manuals, and the **XT685ENT** manual say **every other month** for the motor cover, not once a year - see `xt-2023-maintenance-vacuuming-and-general-cleaning`. The monthly vacuum underneath is the same everywhere.

**The XT485ENT manual is on this card**, printing the same paragraph with the same **pet hair**, the same **black motor hood** and the same **once a year**.

The Spirit CT850 2016 service manual prints this paragraph with the same two intervals; that is a different machine and has its own card, `ct850-2016-tread-belt-and-deck-cleaning`.

These manuals print no maintenance chart and give no page reference for one. **The XT685 2010
manual does both**, and puts the motor-hood vacuum at **every other month** rather than once a
year - so the XT685 2015 on this card is the odd year out within its own model line, not the norm.
See `xt-2023-maintenance-vacuuming-and-general-cleaning` and `xt685-2010-maintenance-record-chart`.
