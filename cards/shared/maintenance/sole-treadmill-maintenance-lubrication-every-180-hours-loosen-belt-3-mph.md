This is the loosen-the-belt procedure with a 3 mph no-load check. It differs from the 6 mph card only in that check speed; the interval and every other step match.

The belt and deck come pre-lubricated. After that, lubricate **every 180 hours of use**. The console has a built-in lubrication reminder indicator that lights every 180 hours.

**Use only SOLE approved lubricant.** The treadmill comes with one tube; extra tubes can be ordered from SOLE, and the only approved kit is **Lube-N-Walk** (it comes with an application wand; order from Lube-N-Walk at jadfitness.com). **The proper kit is silicon based, NOT paraffin wax.** SOLE lubricant is also sold on soletreadmills.com under the products tab. **Failure to lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty**: dry lubricant raises the friction between belt and deck and stresses the drive motor, drive belt and electronic motor control board to the point of catastrophic failure.

Using the supplied 6 mm Allen wrench, loosen the two rear roller adjustment bolts in the rear end caps enough to get your hand under the belt (**5-10 turns**). Loosen both bolts the same number of turns and remember how many, because you tighten them back by exactly that amount afterwards.

1. With the belt loose, wipe the deck with a clean lint-free cloth to remove dirt.
2. Apply the **whole tube** of lubricant onto the deck about **18 inches** from the motor cover. Squeeze the contents across the deck (parallel to the motor cover) in about a **one-foot line, like toothpaste on a toothbrush**, in the middle of the deck at equal distance from both belt edges, roughly where your feet land. Walking the treadmill before loosening the belt shows you where your feet land; runners may land elsewhere.
3. Tighten the rear roller bolts back by the same number of turns.
4. Run the treadmill at about **3 mph without walking on it** for a minute or two to check the belt stays in the middle; if it tracks aside, follow the tracking procedure.
5. **Walk, not run**, on the treadmill immediately for at least 5 minutes to spread the lubricant evenly. With a silicone-based Lube-N-Walk kit, follow its own instructions.
