**No Xterra rower book tells the owner to lubricate anything.** Not the slide rail, not the seat rollers, not a
belt, strap or chain - no product, no interval, no part number, in any of the eleven owner's manuals or the six
service manuals of this range.

Two things a searcher will trip over:

- **"Chain cover"** is the name of the plastic side cover on the ERG160, ERG180, ERG550W and ERG800W (parts lists
  and the SM "Replacing the Chain Cover" pages). There is no chain behind it: these machines drive a belt and a
  handle strap or mesh belt. Xterra's air bike is the machine with a chain to oil
  (`air650-2021-maintenance-chain-lubrication-general-chain-oil-by-way-of-the-ab900-service-manual`).
- Three service-manual sentences mention oil or grease **inside a replacement procedure**, never as care:
  the ERG600W SM says "add some oil if need" where the mesh belt wheel sits on the POM magnet seat, and "brush
  some oil" on the large stainless washer when the tank goes back on; the ERG800W SM warns not to lose the white
  cross axle connector at the bottom of the tank, "which is covered with grease". None gives a product.

The one rail instruction in the range is the ERG800W's weekly wipe of the seat wheels and side rails **with a
lint-free cloth**, dry (`erg800w-2023-maintenance-schedule-weekly-rails-and-frame-half-yearly-water-and-hundred-hour-belt-check`).
The other ten owner's manuals print no rail, roller or wear instruction at all
(`xterra-maintenance-owners-manuals-with-no-cleaning-or-care-page`).

Do not carry the elliptical rail procedure - rubbing alcohol and "a thin coat of lubricant" - onto a rower
(`xterra-maintenance-rear-rail-and-wheel-cleaning-rubbing-alcohol-plastic-scraper-thin-coat-of-lubricant`);
no rower book says it. Spirit's rowers are in the same position
(`spirit-rower-maintenance-no-lubricant-named-anywhere`). Other brand, separate card.
