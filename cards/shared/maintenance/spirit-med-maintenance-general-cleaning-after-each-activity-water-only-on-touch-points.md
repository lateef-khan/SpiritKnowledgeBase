Under *General cleaning*, beside the warning list, as printed:

> Wipe down the equipment and all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth **after each activity**. After each
> activity, the most touched points (e.g. handlebar, cushion) should be cleaned with **water only**.

**Two things the rest of the Spirit range does not say.** The commercial books wipe the sweat path "after each
workout" (`cu900ent-general-maintenance`); these five say "after each activity" - a session, whoever's it was -
and add that the parts every patient touches get **water and nothing else** in that wipe-down.

## Read it with the two halves either side of it

- The **warning list** it sits beside forbids acids, household cleaners, caustic disinfectants and **any cleaning
  agent or disinfectant containing alcohol**, and says never to spray anything directly on the machine
  (`80t-2026-maintenance-cleaning-cautions`). Water only on the touch points is consistent with that list.
- The **disinfection procedure** two pages earlier asks for **20 ml of 70% or greater isopropyl alcohol** on the
  same touch points (`80t-2026-maintenance-disinfection-procedure`). The book never reconciles the two. This
  paragraph is the routine wipe; the seven steps are the disinfection; quote both and let the facility choose
  under its own policy.

The schedule table one page earlier puts the same wipe at *After Each Use* with the same water-only note for the
pedals (`spirit-med-bike-maintenance-machine-care-schedule-after-each-use-to-quarterly`,
`spirit-85s-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`, `85ue-2025-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`).

**Not the four-step post-workout routine.** The 4.0R, 4.0U, 7.0R and 7.0T books print a different list - store
the machine, damp cloth, microfiber cloth on the screen, empty the cupholders
(`spirit-2026t-maintenance-post-workout-machine-care`) - and none of these five prints that.
