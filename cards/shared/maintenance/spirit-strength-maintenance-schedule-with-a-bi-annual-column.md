**The two CSI machines print a different schedule from the other thirty-seven, and the difference
is not cosmetic.** The fourth column is headed **Bi-Annually**, not Bi-Monthly, and five rows sit
in a different column from where the rest of the range puts them.

## The five rows that move

| Row | Rest of the range | CSI |
|---|---|---|
| Clean: Main Frame | Weekly | **Daily** |
| Clean: Guide Rod | Monthly | **Weekly** |
| Clean / Inspect: Internal Tower, Main Frame | Bi-Monthly | **Bi-Annually** |
| Inspect: Belt / Cables | **Weekly** | **Monthly** |
| Inspect: Pulleys | Bi-Monthly | **Monthly** |

**Cleaning gets more frequent and inspection gets less frequent.** The two that matter to a
commercial gym pull in opposite directions: the guide rods are wiped four times as often, and the
**cables are inspected a quarter as often** — see
`spirit-strength-maintenance-cables-inspected-monthly-and-before-every-use`, which is the reason
the interval has its own card.

## Bi-Annually is a different word from Bi-Monthly

The other thirty-seven Spirit strength manuals head their fourth column **Bi-Monthly**. These two
head it **Bi-Annually**. Whatever "bi-monthly" was meant to mean, "bi-annually" is unambiguously
longer, and the internal tower and main-frame inspections move with it. Do not translate one
heading into the other.

## Everything else on the page is the same

The preamble is word for word the range preamble, including *SPIRIT is not responsible for
performing regular inspection and maintenance actions for your machines*. The row names are the
same fifteen as the CSD table. No lubricant, cleaner or torque figure is named here either.

**These are the newest and longest books in the range** — 56 and 48 pages against about 30 for the
rest, with a warranty effective January 1, 2026 — and their maintenance page is still one page.
Nothing in the extra thirty pages is about maintenance; they are the i-Strength training modes.

## The Digital Resistance Module's own maintenance manual adds nothing

The i-Strength *Digital Resistance Module Maintenance Manual* - the module the CSI owner's manuals list as part
72 - is a disassembly, wiring, specification and fault-code book despite its title: no schedule, no cleaning step,
no lubricant and no interval anywhere in its 26 pages. The only time figure in it is the controller's rated
*Service Life 5000 hours*, a specification. This table remains the only maintenance guidance for a CSI machine.
