**These are the XE195/XE295/XE395/XE395ENT figures: a bushing-housing bolt at 500-600 lbs and a
cross-bar bolt at 550-600 lbs. The XE795 (2016) book prints 500-550 lbs for the cross bar and an M14
rod end; the CE800 (2016) book 550-600 lbs with an M14 fish-eye nut and no bushing-housing figure;
the CE850 and XE895 books no figure at all. Each is its own card.**

"Slipping problems can be verified by the following steps."

| Joint | Check |
|---|---|
| **M12 x P1.75 Rod End Bearing** | The M12 nut on top must be securely tightened with the Pedal Bar Assembly, and the rod end bearing needs to be **vertical** |
| **Bushing Housing, Pedal Arm** | The CAP Socket Head Cap Bolt 3/8" x 2-1/4" on top and the nyloc nut 3/8" x 11T must be tightened securely - **recommended weight is between 500~600Lbs** |
| **Cross Bar** | The CAP Socket Head Cap Bolt M8x40 m/m on top of the cross bar must be tightened to **550~600Lbs** |
| **Belt** | "This issue appears quite often, because after a period of time, most belts will become loose from use depending on usage time. Solution will depend on weight and adjustment methods, refer to belt disassembly procedures" |

The books print the torque as "Lbs" with no unit of length. Take it as the manual prints it; the
belt adjustment it points to is `spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-belt-slipping-hook-screw-cap-13-mm-190-hz`.

A machine that **shakes** rather than slips is answered by the next Q&A item, loose screws and the
pedal carriage bolts - `spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-shaking-loose-screws-and-pedal-carriage-bolts`.

The Sole E25 (2019) service manual prints the same four joints with **no** torque figures
(`e25-2019-slip-problem`); the Sole E98 prints figures (`e98-2019-slip-problem`).
