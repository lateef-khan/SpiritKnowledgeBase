1. Remove the **left cover (112)** by removing **four screws (30)** and **two screws (93)**.
2. Without removing the transmitter from the left cover (112), remove the Battery Cover from the transmitter.
3. Install **2 AAA batteries** in the battery compartment and reinstall the battery cover.
4. When new batteries are installed in the transmitter or console you must perform the synchronizing procedure for the console to work properly.
5. After synchronizing, make sure the wire from the transmitter is wrapped around the plastic as in the manual's image.
6. Reinstall the left cover.

**The SB900 2020 and SB900 2021 manuals print this job twice.** This six-step version appears on the transmitter page; a shorter three-step version ("remove the battery cover from the transmitter") appears again a page or two later. The short version omits the cover removal. Use the six steps above on these machines.

The SB900 2014, 2016 and 2019 manuals print only this version. The SB700 manuals print only the short version.
