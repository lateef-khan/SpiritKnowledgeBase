The chart is a blank log printed once, under the table of contents, for the owner to fill in. Its four task columns and their intervals, as printed:

| Task | Interval |
|---|---|
| Vacuum under motor cover & check wiring | Every other month |
| Clean & inspect deck for lubrication | Every other month |
| Lubricate deck | Every 90 hours or sooner if dry |
| Inspect belt tracking, adjust if necessary | Monthly |

A fifth column headed **Date** records when each task was done. The General Maintenance page says "there is a maintenance schedule/chart included under the Table of Contents for convenient record keeping."

The 90-hour figure agrees with the lubrication page of the same three books (`xterra-tr-maintenance-lubrication-interval-every-90-hours`). The sister machines whose chart reads **40 hours** are on `xterra-treadmill-maintenance-chart-lubricate-deck-every-40-hours`. The Sole F80 chart carries the same four rows and the same 90 hours; that is another brand and its own card.
