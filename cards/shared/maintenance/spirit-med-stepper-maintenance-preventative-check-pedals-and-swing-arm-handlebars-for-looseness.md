**Two items, no interval.** Both medical recumbent steppers' service manuals open with a preventative-maintenance
chapter: two checks on the 8.5S, the same two plus the rail on the 7.5S (`7-5s-med-maintenance-aluminium-sliding-rail-cleaned-regularly-to-avoid-pu-wheel-loss`). Neither book says how often.

| | 7.5S (RS9600-SS021) | 8.5S (MS2000-SB036-01) |
|---|---|---|
| Pedal | 3.1 "check whether the pedal is loose, if it is loose, you need to lock the screw" (figure 3.1.1) | 3-1 "check whether the pedals are loose" (figure 3.1.1) |
| Swing arm / handlebar | 3.2 "check whether the armrests are loose or not. If it is loose, tighten the screws (Same on both sides)" (figures 3.2.1, 3.2.2) | 3-2 "check whether the left and right handlebars are loose or wobbling" (figure 3.2.1) |

The 7.5S calls the moving handles *swing arms* and their grips *armrests*; the 8.5S calls them *handlebars*. It is
the same check - the screws at the swing-arm pivot, both sides.

**Where the intervals are.** The owner's manuals carry them, not these books: the 7.5S owner's manual checks hand
grips, seat cushions and foot pedals for wear **once a month**
(`7-5s-med-maintenance-monthly-wear-check-on-grips-cushions-and-pedals`); the 8.5S owner's manual schedule inspects
the crank arm and pedals **weekly / daily** and checks the handlebar arm and footplates are tight **monthly**
(`spirit-85s-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`).

**What a loose one leads to.** A swing arm that sways in use is the same screw, plus the quick-release on the
telescopic tube (`spirit-med-stepper-maintenance-swing-arm-sway-tighten-the-screw-then-lock-the-quick-release`). The pedal screw on the 7.5S is reached with a short screwdriver from underneath (6.3); on
the 8.5S it loosens counter-clockwise (6-3) - both assembly cards.
