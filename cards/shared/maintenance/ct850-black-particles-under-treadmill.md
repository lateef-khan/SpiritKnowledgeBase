| Condition | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| Black particles collecting under treadmill. | Drive belt is breaking in. | Vacuum under treadmill periodically. |

That row is the whole answer both CT850 manuals give, and it is printed identically in each.

The 2016 manual's General Maintenance section adds a second, separate kind of dust under the
heading **Belt Dust**: "This occurs during normal break-in or until the belt stabilizes. Wiping
excess off with a damp cloth will minimize buildup." That paragraph is about the **tread belt**,
not the drive belt, and it appears only in the 2016 manual — see
`ct850-2016-tread-belt-and-deck-cleaning`.
