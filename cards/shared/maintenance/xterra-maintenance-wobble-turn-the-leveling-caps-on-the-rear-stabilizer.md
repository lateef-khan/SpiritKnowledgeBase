> The [bike / elliptical] is equipped with adjustable end caps. **If the unit is wobbly, please turn the
> leveling caps on the rear stabilizer to level the unit.**

The books name the end caps of the **rear** stabilizer only. No tool is named and no figure is given for
how far to turn them.

A wobble that survives leveling is loose hardware: the weekly checks
(`xterra-maintenance-weekly-checks-pedals-seat-nuts-stabilizer-bolts-and-handlebar`) cover stabilizer
bolts, pedals, seat nuts and handlebar. The FS3.0, FS5.9e and RSX1500 books put the same idea the other way
round - "if squeaks persist, check that the unit is properly leveled" - without saying which feet to turn
(`xterra-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking-from-loose-hardware-dirty-rails-or-leveling`).

Spirit's CS800 gives a different fix for a shaking machine (thumb screws on the leveling foot) -
`cs800-2016-maintenance-shaking-loose-screws-carriage-bolts-and-leveling-foot-thumb-screw`. Other brand.
