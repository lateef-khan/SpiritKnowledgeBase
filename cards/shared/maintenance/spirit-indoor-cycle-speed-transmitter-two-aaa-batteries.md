1. Remove the **battery cover from the transmitter**.
2. Install **2 AAA batteries** in the battery compartment and reinstall the battery cover.
3. **When new batteries are installed in the transmitter *or* the console you must perform the
   synchronising procedure** for the console to work properly.

**Synchronising the transmitter to the console** — do this *before* attaching either to the bike:

1. Press and hold the **two keys on the front of the console** for about **3 seconds** until the
   display shows **ID - -**.
2. Press and hold the **blue button on the transmitter** for **3 seconds** and release. The console
   should show **ID 0**.
3. **You must press the blue button within 10 seconds** of the console showing ID - -, or the
   console shows **ID ER**. If it does, restart the procedure.

The transmitter is attached by Velcro to the **back side of the chain cover, below the left crank**,
with the speed sensor wire plugged into it.

## Two sets of cells on this machine

The console has its own **2 AAA** cells —
`spirit-indoor-cycle-maintenance-console-two-aaa-batteries`. Changing *either* set triggers the synchronising
procedure above; if an error persists, the manual says it may be necessary to remove and reinstall
the batteries in **both** the console and the transmitter.

## Not the CIC850's sensor battery

The CIC850's speed sensor board sits **inside the machine behind left chain guard B**, is reached by
removing one screw, is paired with MODE and SET rather than a blue button, and its **cell type is
never printed** — `cic850-2022-maintenance-sensor-board-battery-behind-left-chain-guard`. Do not
promise a CIC850 owner a AAA cell or a blue button.

Sole's SB700 and SB900 manuals print the same three steps for **Sole** machines
(`sole-spinner-transmitter-battery`). Different brand; neither card covers the other's machines.
