Absence proved twice: the tables of contents list no folding or transport chapter, and a loose-word search of the full text finds only the safety-page mention of transport wheels and the warranty's "transportation costs" line.

- The **VS73/VS77-2008 and WS73/WS77-2009-2010 books print no folding and no moving instructions at all.** Their tables of contents jump from assembly/operation to General Maintenance with nothing in between. Do not card a procedure for these machines from a sibling book.
- The **S77-2013 and TT9-2018 books print orphan transport sentences** ("transport wheels that are engaged when the treadmill is folded; after folding simply roll it away") **with no folding procedure anywhere in the book.** The sentences assume a mechanism the book never describes; the card reports them as printed and marks the procedure as absent.

Neither group prints a re-levelling-after-the-move step either.
