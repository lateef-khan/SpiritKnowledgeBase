Absence proved twice: the tables of contents list no folding or transport chapter, and a loose-word search of the full text finds only the safety-page mention of transport wheels and the warranty's "transportation costs" line.

- The **VS73/VS77-2008 and WS73/WS77-2009-2010 books print no folding and no moving instructions at all.** Their tables of contents jump from assembly/operation to General Maintenance with nothing in between. Do not card a procedure for these machines from a sibling book.
- The **S77-2013 and TT9-2018 books print orphan transport sentences** ("transport wheels that are engaged when the treadmill is folded; after folding simply roll it away") **with no folding procedure anywhere in the book.** The sentences assume a mechanism the book never describes; the card reports them as printed and marks the procedure as absent.

Neither group prints a re-levelling-after-the-move step either.

A2 additions, each proved the same way (contents entry, loose-word search, page read):

- The **S77-2016** book prints orphan transport sentences (wheels engaged when folded; roll away) with no folding procedure, like the S77-2013/TT9-2018 group above.
- The **S77-2019** book prints no folding and no moving instructions at all, like the VS/WS group above - with the same contents trap as the AS77-2012: its table of contents advertises `Folding Instructions' on printed page 12, but that page holds Assembly Step 9 (tablet holder), verified on the render. The card follows what is printed, not the contents entry.
- The **TT8-2016** book (both drive versions) prints orphan transport sentences with no folding procedure, like the S77-2013/TT9-2018 group above.
- The **TT8-2019/TT8-2020** book prints no folding procedure and a one-line moving instruction of its own: `equipped with transport wheels for simply rolling the treadmill away' (PDF p.11).
- The **TT8-2021, TT8-2023 and ST90-2020/2021/2023** books print no folding and no moving instructions at all, like the VS/WS group above; their tables of contents jump from assembly/operation to General Maintenance with nothing in between.

None of these books prints a re-levelling-after-the-move step either.
