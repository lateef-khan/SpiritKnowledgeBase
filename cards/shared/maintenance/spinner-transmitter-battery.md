1. Remove the battery cover from the transmitter.
2. Install **2 AAA batteries** in the battery compartment and reinstall the battery cover.
3. When new batteries are installed in the transmitter or console you must perform the synchronizing procedure for the console to work properly.

The manuals do not name a brand or a battery life for these cells.

**Step 3 is not printed for every machine.** The SB700 and SB900 owner's manuals all carry it. The DT-3268 console manual that ships with the SB900 2023 prints only steps 1 and 2 and gives no transmitter-to-console synchronizing procedure at all, so do not promise an SB900 2023 owner that there is one to follow.

**The SB900 2023 owner's manual is no help here.** Its page 8 carries the heading "Transmitter Battery Replacement Procedure" and then nothing but a picture; the extracted text of that page is empty. The two AAA figure for that machine comes from the DT-3268 console manual shipped with it.

On the SB900 2014, 2016, 2019, 2020 and 2021, the transmitter sits behind the left cover and there is a longer procedure for reaching it.
