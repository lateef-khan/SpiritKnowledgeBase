**The test speed here is 80 to 100 RPM. The XE195, XE295 and CE800 (2016) books print 100 to 120
RPM and the XE395 books 100 to 200 RPM for the same step.**

1. **Remove the right chain cover and the Idler Wheel Plate**, then reinstall the Poly-V belt.
2. **Rotate at low speed** and watch whether the drive pulley or the belt is offset or deformed.
   Check whether the belt pulley is offset, or whether the **belt, drive pulley and magnetic flywheel
   are not aligned in a straight line**.
3. **Rotate at high speed - **80 to 100 RPM** - and test with abrupt stops** to check for abnormalities. If
   everything is fine, reassemble fully.
4. If the three parts are not in a straight line, **adjust the magnetic flywheel** to the proper
   place. The book adds that alignment is always set before the machine leaves the factory.
5. A worn or damaged belt is replaced, not adjusted.

Slipping is the other belt symptom and has its own adjustment, the hook-screw cap with a 13 mm
wrench to 190 Hz - `spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-belt-slipping-hook-screw-cap-13-mm-190-hz`.

## The 2023 book stops after the alignment

The XE795 (2023) service manual prints steps 1 to 4 as a) and b) and then ends the section: it has
**no "if the belt is slipping" sentence and no "replace a worn belt" sentence**. Its tension figure
survives in the idler-wheel replacement step, 190 Hz plus or minus 10, which is the card linked above.
