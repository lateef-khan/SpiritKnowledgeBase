The smell almost always comes from the motor controller. It is rarely the drive motor itself. Lubricating between the belt and the deck can fix it, because the controller then works less hard.

1. Ask how often the customer lubricates. Either way, check the running belt and running deck for wear. Slide a hand between belt and deck palm up and feel whether the texture in the middle is gone. Turn the palm down and feel the deck for damage.
   - Belt or deck worn or damaged: replace the running belt, replace the running deck only if it is damaged, and replace the motor controller. Then do step 2.
   - Not lubricated properly but belt and deck are good: have the customer lubricate properly. If that does not fix it, the motor controller needs replacing. Then do step 2.
   - Belt and deck good and lubrication good: do step 2. The motor controller will need replacing if nothing else is found.
2. Remove the motor hood cover. Have the customer look at the wires going into the motor controller for damage.
   - Damage found: get a photo to decide what to replace. If any wire into the controller is damaged, replace the controller as well as the damaged parts.
   - No damage: just replace the parts from step 1.
