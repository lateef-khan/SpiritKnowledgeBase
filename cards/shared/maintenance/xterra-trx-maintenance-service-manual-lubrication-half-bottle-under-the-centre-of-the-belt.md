**This is the service manual's procedure. The owner's manuals for the same machines lubricate half a bottle per side in an S pattern** (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-deck-lubrication-half-bottle-each-side-1-foot-from-the-motor-cover`), and the two schedules differ too (`xterra-trx-maintenance-service-manual-lubrication-schedule-25-then-50-hours`).

Your treadmill should require little maintenance other than periodically applying lubricant; lubricating under the treadbelt ensures superior performance and extends its life expectancy.

**How to check the treadbelt for proper lubrication**: lift one side of the treadbelt and feel the top surface of the treadboard. If the surface is **slick to the touch**, no further lubrication is required. If it is **dry to the touch**, apply one packet of lubricant or half of the bottle.

**How to apply lubricant**

1. Lift one side of the treadbelt.
2. **Pour one half of the lubricant bottle under the centre of the treadbelt** on the top surface of the treadboard.
3. **Walk on the treadmill at a slow speed for 3 to 5 minutes** to distribute the lubricant evenly.

NOTE: do not over-lubricate the treadboard; wipe off any excess that comes out. **IMPORTANT: only use half the bottle of lubricant per application.**

The TRX3500/TRX4500 book prints this page as a picture; it was read from the render. The TRX1400 service manual instead prints the owner's-manual rag paragraph and a 90-hour interval, and names a "Lube-n-Walk kit". The Spirit XT185 2015 service manual prints this procedure word for word: `xt185-2015-maintenance-deck-lubrication-half-bottle-procedure`, another brand.
