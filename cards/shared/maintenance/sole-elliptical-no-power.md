Power has to travel from the cord, through the fuse in the power socket/switch, to the incline adapter (sometimes called a filter or transformer) and the incline/brake controller, then up to the display board.

If the machine was recently assembled, disassembled or moved, replace the **Computer Cable** first.

1. Ask whether the power cord is plugged in properly.
   - **No** - plug it in properly and try again.
   - **Yes** - check the fuse between the power socket and the switch. If it is blown, the customer buys a new fuse. If it is not blown, go to step 2.
2. If the console still makes a beeping noise when the switch is turned on, replace the **Display Board** or the **Console Assembly**.
3. If the cord is in, the fuse is good and there is still no power, replace the **Incline/Brake Controller** and the **Incline Adapter**.

**The service manuals answer a dead console differently.** They check the AC switch, the console connectors, the fuse and the wiring, with no controller or adapter swap (`e95-2016-no-display`, `e35-2019-no-display`).
