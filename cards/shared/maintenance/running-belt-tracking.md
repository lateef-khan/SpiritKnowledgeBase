Only the **user's left side** end cap at the very back of the machine is used for tracking.

1. Turn the left rear roller bolt a quarter turn at a time in the direction you want the belt to move. Counter-clockwise moves it left, clockwise moves it right.
2. Run the machine at low speed, no more than 2, and watch the belt walk across. Press **Stop** when it looks centred.
3. Undo the same number of turns on the left rear roller bolt to straighten the roller again.

**Notes**: you may have to straighten the rear roller before you start. The owner's manual holds the tracking instructions for the specific machine.
