**Of the seventeen rower books and four strength books, exactly one prints an error code: the ERG700 owner's manual, with E1 (RAM ERROR, replace the console) and E2 (cable communication, check the 8-pin cable)** - `erg700-2022-errors-e1-ram-error-console-eeprom-failed-replace-the-console`. Every other console in the range reports its faults as symptoms, not codes, so a customer reading a code off any other Xterra rower is not reading one these books define.

What each book prints, and where the answer lives:

| Book | What it has | Card |
|---|---|---|
| ERG500, ERG550W, ERG600W, ERG650W, ERG750W OM | a Problem / Cause / Solution page: monitor does not display; no count or distance; (ERG500) no tension; (ERG500, ERG550W, ERG600W) squealing | `xterra-rower-errors-monitor-does-not-display-install-batteries-then-computer-wires`, `xterra-rower-errors-no-count-or-distance-sensor-wire-then-monitor`, `erg500-2018-errors-no-tension-air-damper-needs-adjustment` |
| ERG220 OM | the same page with five problems: console does not turn on; no speed or distance (plus a magnetic wheel); no tension; grinding noise; squeaking | the two cards above, `erg220-2023-errors-no-tension-computer-wires-then-tension-knob`, `erg220-2023-errors-grinding-noise-idler-pulley-or-flywheel` |
| ERG160 OM | one row only: console does not turn on | `xterra-rower-errors-monitor-does-not-display-install-batteries-then-computer-wires` |
| ERG700 OM | E1 and E2, then a table: LCD, three heart-rate rows, light sensor | the E1 card and `erg700-2022-errors-lcd-does-not-shine-backlight-or-console-power-ac-100-240-v-dc-12-v` |
| ERG550W SM, ERG600W SM | a Q&A chapter: no data, powers on by itself, no display; (ERG550W) no pulse; (ERG600W) strap does not retract, leaking water | `erg550w-2023-errors-console-shows-no-data-full-stroke-of-1-m-then-batteries-wires-two-magnets`, `erg600w-2021-errors-console-shows-no-data-batteries-wires-magnets-sensor-gap-5-mm` |
| ERG750W SM | Console VR and Sensors pages, then a four-row console table | `erg750w-2025-errors-no-speed-sensor-connection-gap-under-6-mm-cables-magnet` |
| ADB12.5PR, ADB25, ADB55 OM | one problem: the selector does not turn in the base | `xterra-adb-errors-adjustment-handle-does-not-turn-in-base-seat-handle-and-check-plates` |
| **ERG180 OM, ERG400 OM, ERG800W OM, dumbbell stand OM** | **no troubleshooting section and no code.** Their contents run from Product Registration through assembly, console and programs to the warranty with no Troubleshooting entry | - |
| **ERG160 SM, ERG180 SM, ERG800W SM** | parts lists and replacement steps only; no fault, check or Q&A page. The ERG800W book's 39 pages are a parts list and fourteen replacement procedures | - |

The books that print nothing were checked three ways: the contents page, a loose-word search of the whole text layer and every OCR supplement (`trouble`, `problem`, `error`, `err`, `fault`, `blank`, and `E` followed by a digit), and a render of every page compared against the text layer - the ERG400 book is a scan and is OCR throughout. Of the silent owner's manuals, the ERG180 describes a two-AA console like the ERG160's, whose one row is above, and the ERG800W runs on an adaptor (its assembly pages plug it in and its service manual lists an adaptor and a DC power wire) and prints only a chest-strap interference paragraph, which is a console matter.

Rows a technician would not call faults sit elsewhere: *Squealing - belt slipping - adjust belt* (ERG220, ERG500, ERG550W, ERG600W) and the ERG700's *General Maintenance* list of squeaks, thumps and clicking from loose hardware are maintenance questions; a battery symbol on the ERG160 and ERG180 display is the console's low-battery indicator, and the ERG800W service manual's 1.5 mm sensor gap is a step in its sensor-cable replacement procedure, not a fault check.

The Xterra bikes, ellipticals and climber have their own version of this card (`xterra-errors-owner-manual-has-no-troubleshooting-section-and-no-error-code-table`), and so do the Spirit rowers (`spirit-rower-errors-no-error-codes-printed`).
