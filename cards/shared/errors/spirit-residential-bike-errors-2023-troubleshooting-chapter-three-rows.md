**Three of the fourteen Spirit residential bike owner's manuals print a `TROUBLESHOOTING`
chapter. It is one page, it has three rows, and it names no code of any kind.** The three are
the **XBR25 2023, XBR55 2023 and XBU55 2023**.

The three rows, headed `Problem` and `Solution/Cause`:

| Problem | Where the answer lives |
|---|---|
| Display does not light | `spirit-residential-bike-errors-display-does-not-light-115-vac` |
| Squeak, thump, clicking sound heard | `section: maintenance` - loose assembly hardware, the crank arm nut, and the two levelling pads on the rear stabiliser adjusted with a 14 mm wrench |
| Rough feeling when using bike | the same three causes, printed again word for word |

**Rows 2 and 3 print identical text.** The manual repeats the loose-hardware, crank-arm-nut and
levelling answer under both headings rather than cross-referencing, so a reader who finds one has
already found the other. That is the source's shape, not a transcription slip.

**The three pages are the same page.** Compared word by word with bare page numbers stripped, all
three match at **100%** - 248 words each. Nothing on this card depends on which of the three
machines a caller owns.

**No error code appears anywhere in these three books.** Neither `E` followed by digits, nor
`ERR`, `EEPROM`, `LWR` or a hexadecimal code. The three consoles have an `Engineering Mode Menu`
with a Key Test, a Display Test and a `Motor test (Continually runs the tensioning gear motor)`,
and none of those returns a code either; that menu is carded under `section: console`.

**The XBR95 2023 does not have this chapter**, even though it was printed in the same August 2023
family as the XBR55 2023 and shares 91.3% of its words with it. Its table of contents ends at
Warranty, and its last printed pages are blank. So do not tell an XBR95 2023 owner to turn to
their troubleshooting page. The other ten residential printings - XBR95 2016, 2018 and 2021,
XBR25 2019, XBR55 2019 and 2021, XBU55 2019 and 2021, XBR55ENT 2021 and XBU55ENT 2021 - have no
troubleshooting chapter either. That absence is carded at
`spirit-commercial-bike-errors-no-error-codes-printed`, which was checked by rendering every page
of all fourteen manuals at 300 dpi and comparing the OCR against the text layer.

**What every one of the fourteen does print instead**, the eleven older books included, is the
`Erratic Operation` heart rate interference list - the only symptom-and-cause page in the
residential line: `spirit-ce-errors-erratic-heart-rate-interference`. The one exception is the
XBR25 2023, which has no chest-strap chapter at all.

Spirit codes are never Sole codes. A caller reading a code off a screen has a machine whose code
did not come from any of these three books; establish what the machine actually is before
answering.
