The Troubleshooting Guide's first row, `No power - There is no power to the console`, printed identically in the 2008 XT dealer manual (XT175, XT275, XT375, XT475, XT675) and the CT800 2016 service manual's *Treadmill Troubleshooting* chapter.

1. Unit must be plugged into an outlet with power.
2. Power switch must be illuminated.
3. Tether cord must be in place.
4. Check treadmill circuit breaker (`spirit-xt175-errors-checking-the-treadmill-circuit-breaker`).
5. Check "POWER" LED on controller (the XT manual points at its controller diagram).
6. Make sure wires on board are plugged in securely.
7. Check wire harness for continuity (`spirit-xt175-errors-checking-wire-harness-continuity`).
8. If LEDs on controller are not lit, test the fuse on the controller. If the fuse is good, replace the controller.
9. If LEDs are on, replace the console.

The order is the diagnosis: outlet, switch, tether, breaker, then the controller's POWER LED decides between fuse-or-controller and console. The XT manual cites its procedures by number after each step; the CT800 2016 drops the numbers and prints the same words. Neither book says what the CT800's own matrix says about a dead switch or a dead console with the key in (`ct850-2016-power-switch-not-lit-at-power-on`, `ct850-2016-no-display-when-safety-key-inserted`).
