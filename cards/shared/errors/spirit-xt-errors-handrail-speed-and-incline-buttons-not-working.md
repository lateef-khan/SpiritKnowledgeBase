The row folds two conditions - `FAST/SLOW button of SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH can't be used` and `UP/DOWN button of INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH can't be used` - into one list, and the list names only the speed cable.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The connector of SPEED CABLE and CONSOLE not connected properly | Connect cables again |
| The connector of SPEED CABLE and SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/CABLE not connected properly | Check the wire of SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH |
| Button of SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH is broken | Replace with new cable |
| The connector of SPEED CABLE or SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH/W/CABLE is damaged | Replace new SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH |

The fixes are numbered 1, 2, 2, 3 in the book and do not line up with the causes one to one; read them as a set - reconnect, inspect the switch wire, replace the cable, replace the switch. For the incline switch substitute the incline cable and switch throughout, which the row leaves the reader to do.

The 2023 XT185 to XT685, the 2015 XT385 and XT485 and the CT1000ENT 2023 service manuals print it identically. The 2015 XT185 and XT285 answer the two switches separately with different parts (`spirit-xt-2015-errors-speed-buttons-keys-stuck-or-board`, `spirit-xt-2015-errors-incline-buttons-vr-or-power-wires`); the CT books print a six-cause list for each (`ct850-2016-speed-buttons-not-working`, `ct850-2016-incline-buttons-not-working`).
