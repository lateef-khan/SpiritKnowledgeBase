| Condition | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| After turning on power, treadmill has a popping sound. | Incorrect input power, varistor is blown broken on controller. | Check the voltage of power is 110V or 230V. Replace controller. |

The 2015 XT185 and XT285 service manuals print this row identically. The 2015 XT385 and XT485 and every 2023 XT book write the same row as *220V ... (on 120Vac electronic power system need 110V)* (`spirit-xt-errors-popping-sound-at-power-on-220-v-or-110-v`); the CT800 and CT850 books print 120V, 110-120V or 200-240V. Same fault, same part, and a figure that follows the book in the customer's hands.
