**9-2 Pedal arm.** *Noise from Pedal arm. (It usually cause by the bearing is defective.)* Replace Pedal arm's bearing. Replace the Slide wheels' bearing.

**9-3 Connect arm.** *Noise from Connect arm.* Check Rod end bearing. If this bearing has clearance and cause noise please does the replacement. If the noise comes from Pedal assembly, check Pedal locking screws are secured, and the top of the assembly is taped foam tapes.

**Three bearings and one screw check.** The pedal arm pivots on a bearing and rides the rail on slide wheels with bearings of their own; the connect arm swings on a rod end bearing. Any of the three that has developed clearance is replaced - the book prints no play figure and no lubricant. The one non-bearing cause is the pedal itself: its locking screws loose, or the foam tape missing from the top of the pedal assembly, which is the pad the foot plate rests on.

**"Check Rod end bearing" is a hands-on check for clearance**, the same criterion the CS800 books use for their bushing (`spirit-cs800-errors-clearance-between-bushing-and-axle-replace-the-bushing`). The book's own index of noise sources - joint bearings, flywheel, slide wheels, pedals - is `spirit-stepper-errors-noise-sources-bearings-flywheel-slide-wheels-and-pedals`.
