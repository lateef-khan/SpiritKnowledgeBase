Procedure 5 in the XT manual (Procedure 4 in the CT800 2016), word for word the same:

1. The front roller should have a round magnet and a round counter weight.
2. Take a screw or something metallic and test each one.
3. Only one should be magnetic.

So the roller carries two identical-looking discs and only one of them is the magnet the speed sensor reads; a screw held to each tells them apart, and a roller on which neither disc holds the screw has lost its magnet. It is step 3 of the LS-after-belt-moves row (`spirit-xt175-errors-ls-error-after-belt-moves`): a missing magnet gives the sensor nothing to count and no adjustment will cure it.
