**Chapter 10 of both ENT service manuals - 10-1 The Console and Error Messages, 10-2 Belt Slipping and Falling-off, 10-3 Noises problem - is the Q&A chapter of an exercise-bike service manual, printed unchanged.** Nothing in it describes a treadmill.

- 10-1 says a blank screen should be checked at the computer cables, then *Check Power adapter connector is fully insert DC socket of Bike*, then the adapter's output voltage; a treadmill has no power adapter and no DC socket. Its no-heart-rate answer, *check hand pulse wires connection from it to console*, is the one line that applies.
- 10-2 says a slipping belt should be checked with a sonic tension meter for **250±50 Hz** after removing *the left and right shrouds* per a section 9-5 that in these books is the front and rear roller replacement; the drive belt is called a consumable, and a belt that falls off is blamed on *the bike dropped during transportation* misaligning the drive pulley, idle wheel assembly and flywheel. A treadmill has none of those parts, and its own belt tension is set with a tension gauge at 70~75 lbs in chapter 9.
- 10-3 traces noises to the pedal, the crank locking nut, and the drive pulley rubbing the front of the right shroud.

So a reader who reaches this chapter for a treadmill fault should leave it. The treadmill answers in the same books are the matrix and checklist of chapter 8 - `ct850-2016-power-switch-not-lit-at-power-on`, `ct850-2020-no-display-when-safety-key-inserted`, `spirit-2024-errors-tread-belt-does-not-run-in-center`, `spirit-2024-errors-noise-under-the-motor-cover` - and the error tables at 8-1. The 250 Hz figure must not be quoted for a treadmill drive belt.
