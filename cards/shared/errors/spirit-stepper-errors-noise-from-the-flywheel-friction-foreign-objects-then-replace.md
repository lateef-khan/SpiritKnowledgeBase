- **CS800 (2020):** *There is a noise when the flywheel is spinning.* **Solve:** Check the noise is from the flywheel friction drive pulley or idle wheel. If so follow the replacement steps to reinstall those parts. If the noise comes from flywheel, please do the replacement.
- **CS800 2016:** If there is abnormal noises emitted during flywheel rotation, check the flywheel, drive pulley, and Idler Wheel Assembly to see if there is any foreign objects or friction first. Disassemble and reassemble the flywheel, but if abnormal noises persist, replace with new flywheel.
- **XS895:** If there is noise coming when Flywheel is spinning. Check Flywheel, Drive pulley, and Idle wheel assembly friction each other causes the noise. Or Flywheel makes the noise itself.

**Three parts rub, and only one is replaced for noise.** Friction between the flywheel, the drive pulley and the idler wheel assembly - or something caught between them - is fixed by taking the parts off and refitting them along the replacement steps; a flywheel that is noisy on its own is replaced. No lubricant is named for any of the three.

**Alignment is the usual reason two of them touch.** The belt sections of the same books say the drive pulley, flywheel pulley and idler must sit in a straight line, and that the flywheel is what gets moved to put them there - so a flywheel that has been refitted after a belt change is the first suspect for a new rubbing noise.

The general noise list - bearings, flywheel, slide wheels, pedals - is `spirit-stepper-errors-noise-sources-bearings-flywheel-slide-wheels-and-pedals`; a flywheel that is quiet but gives no resistance is `spirit-stepper-errors-resistance-does-not-change-though-the-gear-motor-runs-steel-cable-on-the-flywheel`.
