1. **Hold down any key** to bring the console to "Quick Start" mode.
2. Ensure that the batteries are installed properly in the **console and the transmitter**. If they are, **install fresh batteries**.

**These consoles take replaceable batteries.** The advice for the later rechargeable consoles is different: those tell you to press any key, then check that the battery icon is *not* showing and watch for a red LED flashing on the transmitter. Do not send an owner of this console looking for a battery icon or a charging cable.
