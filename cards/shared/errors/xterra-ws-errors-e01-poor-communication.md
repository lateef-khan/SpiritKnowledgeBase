**This is E01 on the WS200 and WS300 walking treadmills**, the console-to-controller communication fault. On the Dyaco-built Xterra treadmills the same fault is E5 (`xterra-treadmill-errors-e5-console-controller-communication-poor`), and on the Sole 2026 treadmills E01 is over current (`f63-2026-e01-error-code`).

The owner's manuals print one row for it, in a table that is a flat picture read from the render:

| Questions | Possible cause | Method |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Poor communication | The signal line is not plugged, plug it again |

That is the whole printed entry. The seven-code table it comes from is `xterra-ws-errors-e01-to-e07-error-table`; the no-code "treadmill does not work" rows are `xterra-ws-errors-treadmill-does-not-work-five-causes`.
