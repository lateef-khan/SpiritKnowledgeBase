> House circuit breaker trips, but not the treadmill circuit breaker — Need to replace the house breaker with a "High In-rush current" type breaker (see the grounding instructions page for details).

The books point at their grounding page (page 4 in most, page 3 in the TT9-2018 and F60-2016 books). The F60-2016 book words the condition "Circuit breaker trips, but not the treadmill circuit breaker" with the same high-in-rush answer. A Spirit card holds the same fact for Spirit machines (`ct850-2016-house-breaker-trips-not-treadmill-breaker`) — same remedy, different brand, so a separate card.

The 2006-family, VF63-2008, UF80/83/85-2007, VF80/83/85-2008, VS73/77-2008 and VTT8-2008 books print no such row (proved by loose-word search for "breaker" across each book plus reading the tables). The on-board 15-amp trip in those books is a different row with a friction remedy and belongs to maintenance.

**Eight stencil owner's manuals print this same high-in-rush row** (all point at their page-3 grounding instructions; locator). The 2019-and-later stencil books instead say to check the treadmill is the only appliance in the circuit — that variant lives on `f65-2026-house-breaker-trips`.
