The *Service Checklist - Diagnosis Guide* in the TR300, TR6.6 and TR6.4 owner's manuals prints five checks:

*Display does not light*

1. Tether cord not in position.
2. Circuit breaker on front grill tripped. Push circuit breaker in until it locks.
3. Plug is disconnected. Make sure plug is firmly pushed into **115 VAC** wall outlet.
4. Breaker panel circuit breaker may be tripped.
5. Treadmill defect. Contact XTERRA Fitness.

The new-layout books (TR65, TR75, TR85, TRX1400) print the same five with "AC wall outlet" and "contact your dealer" (`xterra-treadmill-errors-display-does-not-light-five-checks-ac-outlet`). The service-manual version, nine causes with the breaker's small red button, is `xterra-treadmill-errors-power-switch-not-lit-nine-causes` (no service manual exists for these three machines).
