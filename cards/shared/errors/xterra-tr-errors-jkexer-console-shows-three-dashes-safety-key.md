The TR75H and TR95H service manuals print, as the second item of their error chapter: **The console is on but displays "- - -".** Cause: the safety key connection of the console is abnormal.

Troubleshooting: (a) check whether the micro switch in the safety key box is faulty; (b) check whether the wire connecting the safety key to the console is correct.

These machines print no E0; the three dashes are their missing-key indication. The display board's safety socket is a 2-pin socket (item 3 on the display board callout). The Dyaco-built Xterra treadmills show E0 or PLEASE REPLACE THE SAFETY KEY for the same condition (`xterra-treadmill-errors-e0-safety-switch-malfunction-owner-checks`).
