Nine Spirit commercial manuals print this row and all nine give the same three causes and the same
three fixes. Only the noun for the machine changes.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Another chest belt in use around the machine | Check for other chest belt use around the machine |
| Other magnetic field disturbance | Change the position or direction of the machine |
| Receiver is broken | Replace with new receiver |

Which noun each manual prints: CT850 2016 and CT850 2020 say `treadmill`; CVC800 says `climber`;
CU900ENT says `product`; the CE900 2025 owner's manual says `Elliptical`; the CR900 2025 and CU900
2025 owner's manuals say `Bike`. **The CE800ENT manual, which is an elliptical manual, prints
`treadmill` in the reason column and `bike` in the solve column.** That is a copy-and-paste defect
in the source, not a statement about the machine. **The CU800ENT 2024 bike manual carries the same
defect** - `treadmill` in the reason column, `bike` in the solve column.

**Four of the nine come from an owner's manual rather than a service manual** - CE900 2025,
CR900 2025, CU900 2025 and CU800ENT 2024 - and in all four the page is a flat picture that carries
no text layer at all.

The CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 troubleshooting pages are word-for-word identical to each other.

The customer-facing interference list for the same symptom on the ellipticals is a different and
longer answer: `spirit-ce-errors-erratic-heart-rate-interference`.

No reading at all from a chest belt is a different row:
`spirit-wireless-chest-belt-no-pulse`. No reading from the handgrips is
`spirit-hand-pulse-not-working`.

**Ten of the fourteen 2024 New Black manuals print this row with the same three causes and the
same three fixes**, so it now answers for nineteen machines. The noun they use: the CE850 2024
says `Ellipitcal` (spelled that way in the source), the CRS800S 2024 says `climber`, the CS800
2024 says `Stepper`, and the two ENT treadmills say `treadmill`. **The CE800 2024, CE800ENT 2024,
CR800 2024, CR800ENT 2024 and CU800 2024 manuals all carry the copy-and-paste defect** described
above - `treadmill` in the reason column and `bike` in the solve column - on an elliptical and on
three bikes alike. Seven manuals now share that defect, which makes it inherited rather than a
one-off.

**Four of the fourteen do not print this row at all.** The CT800 2024 and CT850 2024 treadmills
carry the short nine-row diagnosis table, which has no pulse rows in it. The CRW800 2024 rower
answers the same symptom in its own wording, on
`crw800-2024-errors-heartbeat-value-incorrect`. **The CSC900 2024 stairclimber answers it nowhere**
- its troubleshooting table has one heart rate row and that row is about the hand pulse wire
(`csc900-2024-errors-heartbeat-not-sensed`), even though the console chapter of the same book says
the machine takes a Bluetooth chest strap.
