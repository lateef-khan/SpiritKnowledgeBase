Section 7-3, `Noise or unstable feeling`, printed in the same words in both books:

- **7-3-1 Front handlebars shaking during pedalling, or loose when grabbed**: check the bolts securing the handle bar to the mast and/or the mast bolts at the main frame.
- **7-3-2 Seat shaking during use**: check the wheels are tightened properly. *It may be necessary to eliminate play between the wheels and the seat track. Loosen the bottom wheels and lift up on the wrench while retightening them. You may need to adjust the top wheels too by loosening them, lifting the seat carriage while retightening them.*
- **7-3-3 The whole product shaking during use**: check that the foot levellers are adjusted properly.

**The seat step is the one worth knowing**: the fix for a rocking seat is not just tighter wheels but wheels retightened *under lift*, so the inner and outer wheels are pressed to opposite faces of the aluminium track. The XBR25/XBR55 2007 dealer manual describes the same trick for its seat carriage - press down on the inner nut with an 11 mm wrench while tightening the 13 mm nyloc - as its one mechanical procedure.

**The CU900 2018 upright answers the same heading with its own three items** - front armrest, seat back-and-forth, whole product - and that answer is already carded from the identical CU900ENT book at `cu900ent-shaking`. A clicking at the pedals is the previous section: `spirit-cr900-errors-clicking-or-loose-feeling-at-the-pedals`.
