1. Check the chest strap is **worn correctly** and that there is **moisture under the
   electrodes**. A dry strap reads nothing.
2. **Move the bike away from anything that could interrupt the radio frequency signal** - the
   manual names a DVD player or a television as examples.

**The XIC600 books add "(sold separately)" to the chest strap**, so an XIC600 bought without a
strap has nothing to read a pulse from and never will. The CIC850 book drops that phrase.

This is the "nothing at all" case. A pulse that appears and then disappears mid-ride is a
different entry with a different fix - `spirit-indoor-cycle-errors-pulse-cuts-out-36-inches`.

The Sole SB700 and SB900 indoor cycles print the same entry:
`sole-spinner-no-heart-rate-chest-strap-consoles`. Different brand, separate card.

**The CIC850 2022 service manual reprints this entry word for word** on its own `Troubleshooting` page, and adds a `Common problems` chapter behind it with a five-check blank-display page (`cic850-2022-errors-display-blank-or-no-speed-five-checks`) and a noise page (`cic850-2022-errors-noise-brake-block-gap-1-5-mm-or-worn-pedals`).
