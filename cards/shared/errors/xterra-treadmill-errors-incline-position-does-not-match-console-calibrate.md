The troubleshooting procedure matrix in the TR260, TRX1400, TRX2500 and TRX3500/TRX4500 service manuals prints one line for this:

*Condition:* The incline position doesn't match console. *Reason:* 1. Console is not calibrated. *Solve:* 1. Calibrate the console.

That is the whole entry. The calibration procedure itself (the Eng or factory-settings menu, wheel size, minimum and maximum speed, maximum incline) is a console card for each machine. The TR150 matrix has no incline row; the belt-speed counterpart is `xterra-treadmill-errors-belt-speed-does-not-match-display-calibrate`. If calibration leaves an incline error on the display, see `xterra-treadmill-errors-incline-err-window-position-sensor-power-cycle-then-calibrate`.
