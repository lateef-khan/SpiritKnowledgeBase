Three conditions share one list: `UP/DOWN button of INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH can't be used`, `Incline button just can press UP, can't press DOWN`, `Incline button just can press DOWN, can't press UP`.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The incline VR or incline power wires not connected properly | Connect the wires again |
| The incline VR or incline power wires are damaged | Replace the cable |
| Maybe keys stuck | Replace buttons |
| Inspect whether the incline motor is stuck | Replace the Incline |
| Inspect whether the incline gears are cracked | Replace the Incline |

The fixes are numbered 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 in the book - there is no 5 - and `Replace the Incline` means the incline motor. This row treats a dead incline button as possibly the motor itself, which is what the E3 sections of the same books check (`spirit-xt-2015-errors-e3-incline-err-during-incline-action`). The 2015 XT185 and XT285 service manuals print it identically; every other Spirit treadmill book answers the incline buttons with cable, connector and button checks only (`spirit-xt-errors-handrail-speed-and-incline-buttons-not-working`, `ct850-2016-incline-buttons-not-working`).
