The 2021-batch owner's manuals print one *Service Checklist - Diagnosis Guide* table. The four books print the same seven rows, word for word, in this order:

| Console shows | Meaning printed | Pointer card |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Console shows E1 8~10 seconds after pressing "Start" button | `xterra-treadmill-errors-e1-owner-checks-8-to-10-seconds-after-start` |
| E0 | Safety Switch Malfunction | `xterra-treadmill-errors-e0-safety-switch-malfunction-owner-checks` |
| E2 | Over Current Protection. Treadmill over loaded, controller protection is activated | `xterra-treadmill-errors-e2-over-current-owner-checks-belt-lube-bearing-motor` |
| E4 | Improper Motor input voltage. Motor is not connected properly | `xterra-treadmill-errors-e4-motor-power-wires-not-in-lower-controller` |
| E5 | Communication disconnected between the console and the controller or communication error | `xterra-treadmill-errors-e5-console-controller-communication-poor` |
| E6 | Controller Malfunction | `xterra-treadmill-errors-e6-lower-controller-component-fault` |
| E7 | Abnormal Power Input | `xterra-treadmill-errors-e7-input-power-too-low-too-high-or-unstable` |

The table opens with a **LUBE** row (a reminder to check lubrication under the walking deck; hold ENTER, or on the other three books PROGRAM, for 3 seconds to reset). That is a maintenance reminder, not a fault, and is not carded here.

No E3 and no E9 are printed in these four books. The TR150 and TR260 service manuals list more codes than their owner's manuals do; see `xterra-treadmill-errors-service-manual-error-code-tables-by-book`.
