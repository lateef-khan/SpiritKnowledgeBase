Three conditions share one list: `FAST/SLOW button of SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH can't be used`, `Speed button just can press FAST, can't press SLOW`, `Speed button just can press SLOW, can't press FAST`.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Maybe keys stuck | Replace key board |
| Maybe upper control is broken | Replace upper Controller |
| Maybe lower control is broken | Replace lower Controller |

No cable is named. The 2015 XT185 and XT285 service manuals print it identically; every other Spirit treadmill book answers the same condition with cable and connector checks (`spirit-xt-errors-handrail-speed-and-incline-buttons-not-working`, `ct850-2016-speed-buttons-not-working`). The incline buttons on these two machines have their own row and a different answer again (`spirit-xt-2015-errors-incline-buttons-vr-or-power-wires`).
