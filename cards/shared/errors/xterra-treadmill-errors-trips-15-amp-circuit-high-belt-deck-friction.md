Every Xterra owner's manual with a *Service Checklist* prints this row; the remedy is worded two ways.

*Treadmill trips on board 15 amp circuit.*

- **TR300, TR6.6, TR6.4, TRX1400 owner's manual:** High belt/deck friction. See General Maintenance (TRX1400: the Maintenance & Care section). If cleaning doesn't prevent this from reoccurring, **check the amp draw of the motor**. If this is high and there are signs of significant wear of the deck, it may need to be **flipped** if it is on its original side.
- **TR65, TR75, TR85:** High belt/deck friction. See Belt and Deck cleaning and Deck Lubrication on cleaning the deck.
- **TRX1400 service manual (10.2):** High belt/deck friction. See General Maintenance section on Belt/Deck Lubrication.

No amp figure for the motor is printed in any of these books. The cleaning and lubrication procedures are maintenance cards, and the deck flip is an assembly or maintenance card where the book gives one. The house breaker tripping instead is a different row (`xterra-treadmill-errors-house-breaker-trips-but-not-treadmill-breaker`); the on-board breaker's reset button is in `xterra-treadmill-errors-power-switch-not-lit-nine-causes`. The neighbouring row - the belt stops suddenly when the tether cord is pulled, also high friction, cleaned the same way - is a maintenance card.
