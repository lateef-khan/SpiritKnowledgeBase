**Caution! Do not use this machine for Heart Rate programs unless a steady, solid Actual Heart
Rate value is being displayed. High, wild, random numbers being displayed indicate a problem.**
*(Each manual names its own machine in that caution. `elliptical trainer`: CE800 2013, CE800 2021,
CE800 2023, XE395 2018, XE795 2018, XE895 2018, XE195 2019, XE295 2019, XE195 2021, XE295 2021,
XE795 2021, XE395ENT, **and the CU800 2012 upright bike**. `elliptical` or `Elliptical`: CE800ENT,
CE850 2016, CE850 2020, CE850 2022, CE900 2025, XE395 2010, XE395 2023, XE795 2023.
`Suspension Elliptical`: CES880 2025. `E•Glide Trainer`: XG400 2019 and XG400 2021.
`fitness bike`: CR800 2021, CR800 2023, CU800 2021. `Recumbent Bike`: CR800ENT 2023, CR900 2025,
XBR55 2019, XBR55ENT 2021, XBR55 2023, XBR95 2023. `Semi - Recumbent Bike`, with the spaces:
XBR95 2016, XBR95 2018, XBR95 2021, XBR55 2021. `Semi-Recumbent Bike`, without them: XBR25 2019.
`Upright Bike`: CU800ENT 2022, CU800ENT 2024, CU900 2025, XBU55 2019, XBU55 2021, XBU55ENT 2021,
XBU55 2023.)*

**The CU800 2012 bike manual calls the machine an `elliptical trainer` in its own caution line.**
Both OCR scans of that manual agree on the wording, so it is a copy-and-paste defect in the source,
not a second machine. Do not tell a CU800 owner they have an elliptical.

Areas to look for interference which may cause erratic heart rate:

1. **The machine is not properly grounded.** *(Printed only in the CE800ENT, CE900 2025, CR800ENT
   2023, CU800ENT 2022, CU800ENT 2024, CR900 2025, CU900 2025, XBR55 2019, XBR55 2021 and XBR55ENT
   2021 manuals. Every other manual on this card begins its list at the next item. The CR900 2025,
   CU900 2025 and CU800ENT 2024 books print the machine's name as a plural against a singular
   verb - `Upright Bikes is not properly grounded` - which is a typo, not a statement about two
   machines.)*
2. **Microwave ovens, TV's, small appliances**, etc.
3. **Fluorescent lights.**
4. **Some household security systems.**
5. **A fence for a pet.**
6. Some people have problems with the transmitter picking up a signal from their skin. **Try
   wearing the transmitter upside down.** Normally it is oriented so the Spirit Fitness logo is
   right side up.
7. The antenna that picks up your heart rate is very sensitive. If there is an outside noise
   source, **turning the whole machine 90 degrees may de-tune the interference**.
8. **Another individual wearing a transmitter within 3' of your machine's console.**

**The thirteen residential XB bikes added on 2026-09-10 print the same seven items, the same
`Perimeter fence for a pet` wording and the same 3-foot figure**, so nothing on this card changes
for an XBR or XBU caller. Three of them add the grounding item at the top, and all three word it
**`Treadmill not properly grounded - Proper grounding is a must!`** on a machine that is a
recumbent bike: XBR55 2019, XBR55 2021 and XBR55ENT 2021. That is a copy-and-paste defect in the
source, the bike counterpart of the CU800 2012 `elliptical trainer` line above. **Do not tell an
XBR55 owner they have a treadmill** - read the item as "the bike is not properly grounded". The
XBU55 2019, XBU55 2021 and XBU55ENT 2021 books, printed alongside them, do not carry the item at
all, so an XBU55 list is seven items and its XBR55 twin is eight.

The three 2023 residential books close with `contact Spirit Fitness`; every earlier XB printing
closes with `contact your dealer`.

**The XBR25 2023 prints none of this.** It is the one residential bike of the fourteen with no
chest-strap chapter at all: its table of contents runs straight from Features to Programs Intro,
the words `erratic`, `chest strap warning` and `interference` appear nowhere, and every page of
the manual was rendered at 300 dpi and OCRed to confirm the section is not a flattened image.
Its console still reads a strap - the `Pulse Grip Feature` paragraph on printed page 20 says so -
but the interference list was dropped from the book.

**Every residential XE machine on this card prints the same seven items as the CE800 2013 and
CE850 2016 books, word for word.** Measured on the erratic-operation block itself rather than on
the whole manual, the XE195, XE295, XE795 2021 February and XE795 2021 November lists are identical
to each other at 8-word shingles; the XG400 and XE395ENT sit at 94-95% and the XE395 2010 at 89%,
and every difference is a line-wrap or the machine's own name. The XE395 2023 measures 95% against
the XE195 once its facing column is separated out; the remaining 5% is its closing line, and the
XE795 2023 prints the same seven items and the same closing line as the XE395 2023.

**The six earlier printings added on 2026-09-10 change nothing in this list.** Measured on the
erratic-operation block alone, as Jaccard over 8-word shingles with bare page numbers stripped,
the **XE395 2018, XE795 2018, XE895 2018, XE195 2019 and XE295 2019 blocks are 100% identical to
each other and to the XE195 2021, XE295 2021 and XE795 2021 February blocks**, and the **XG400
2019 block is 100% identical to the XG400 2021 block**. The XG400 pair differs from the other
five only in the caution line, where it names an `E•Glide Trainer`. The seven items, the
`Perimeter fence for a pet` wording, the 3-foot figure and the `contact your dealer` closing
therefore all predate 2021 unchanged, and no answer here depends on which printing a caller owns.

**The XG400 is not an elliptical.** Its manual calls the machine an `E•Glide Trainer` throughout,
so do not describe it as an elliptical to its owner. Everything else in its list is the same. The
XE395 2010 writes the logo in item 6 as `SPIRIT`; every other manual on this card writes
`Spirit Fitness`.

The two oldest residential books print a **shorter** list that never mentions a strap within 3
feet - see `spirit-xe-errors-erratic-heart-rate-shorter-interference-list`. Do not quote the 3-foot
figure at an XE100, XE150, XE200, XE300, XE350 or XE550 owner.

**The XE395 2023 and XE795 2023 manuals print this list beside a second block** headed
`Instructions For The 5KHZ Receiver Function`, which says the 5 kHz heart rate reception is off by
default and gives the key sequence to switch it on. That is a console setting, not an interference
cause, and it is carded under `section: console`.

The CE900 2021 owner's manual has **no heart rate chapter at all** and prints none of this.

**Nine of the thirteen Spirit commercial bike owner's manuals print this list.** The four that do
not are the CR900 2018, CU900 2018, CR900ENT 2021 and CU900ENT 2021 - checked three ways, on the
loose words `erratic`, `fence` and `interference`, on a spacing-tolerant `E R R A T I C`, and by
rendering and OCRing every page of those four that carried under 25 words of text. The CR900 2025
and CU900 2025 lists are word-for-word identical to each other, as are the CR800 2021 and CR800
2023 lists.

The CE800 2013 manual is an image-only scan read by OCR; its list is legible and matches the
CE850 2016 wording item for item.

**The Spirit treadmill list is not this list.** The CT900 and XT treadmill manuals end at a strap
within 3' as well, but the MT200 rehab treadmill ends at **1 metre** - see
`spirit-mt200-console-erratic-heart-rate-interference`. Quote the figure from the machine in front
of you.

This is the customer-facing interference list. The service matrix row for the same symptom names
three different causes and is on `spirit-erratic-pulse-display`.
