The service checklist of these five books prints the same three causes as the troubleshooting matrix, with one different fix:

| Problem | Solution/cause |
|---|---|
| Noise under motor cover | 1. Worn brushes or bearings on motor. **Replace with new motor brushes.** 2. Front roller bearings are defective. Replace with new front roller. 3. Drive belt is misadjusted (too tight or too loose). Adjust motor position. |

The matrix in the same books answers the first cause with `Replace with new motor` (`spirit-2024-errors-noise-under-the-motor-cover`). So each of these documents offers two fixes for worn brushes - brushes on one page, the whole motor on another - and says nothing about which. Brushes are the cheaper part and the checklist is the page written for a dealer, so start there; a noise that survives new brushes is the bearings, and that is the motor.

The 2015 XT285, XT385 and XT485, the XT485ENT and the CT900ENT print this checklist row; the CT900 service manual prints it too, and is carded under maintenance for that machine. The rear-roller row beside it reads the same in checklist and matrix (`spirit-2024-errors-noise-in-the-rear-of-the-treadmill`).
