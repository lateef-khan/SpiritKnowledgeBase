The manuals fold three conditions into one block of causes, and print it identically in 2016 and 2020:

- UP/DOWN button of INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH can't be used.
- Incline button just can press UP, can't press DOWN.
- Incline button just can press DOWN, can't press UP.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The connector of INCLINE CABLE and CONSOLE not connected properly | Connect the wires again |
| The connector of INCLINE CABLE and INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/CABLE not connected properly | Connect the wires again |
| The connector of INCLINE CABLE or INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH CABLE got damage | Replace the cable |
| Button of INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH is broken | Replace buttons |
| The connector of INCLINE CABLE or INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH CABLE got damage | Replace the cable |
| The connector of INCLINE CABLE or INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH CABLE damaged | Replace the cable |

This row is about the *buttons*. If the incline moves but the number is wrong, see
`ct850-2016-incline-position-does-not-match-console`; if the console prints a message, see
`ct850-2016-incline-err-shown-in-incline-window`.

**The CT800ENT 2024 and CT850ENT 2024 owner's manuals print this row word for word** in the
Condition/Reason/Solve matrix on printed pages 57 to 59. Both of those pages are flat pictures
with no text layer and were read from the rendered page.

All three conditions and all six reasons are unchanged, the repeated sentences included.

**Six more service manuals print the six-cause list word for word**: the CT800 2016, CT800 2020, CT800ENT 2022, CT850ENT 2022, CT900ENT and 4.0T. The XT and CT1000ENT books fold speed and incline into one shorter row (`spirit-xt-errors-handrail-speed-and-incline-buttons-not-working`); the 2015 XT185 and XT285 blame the incline wires and the motor instead (`spirit-xt-2015-errors-incline-buttons-vr-or-power-wires`).
