The owner's manuals, word for word:

> **Clicking noise when pedaling**
> - Make sure the pedal is tightened properly. It is common to have to retighten the pedal after the first few hours use.
> - Make sure the **adjustment knob for the pedal slide** is tightened as much as possible.
> - Check that the leveling feet on the bottom of the bike are adjusted properly.

**The middle item is this bike's own.** The 7.0R and 7.0U have adjustable-length crank arms - the pedal slides along the crank and is clamped by a knob - and a knob left loose clicks once a revolution. The other two are the ordinary bike answers: a pedal that needs re-torquing after the first hours, and a machine rocking on its feet.

The service manuals' *7.2 Noise / Feet Feeling* section says the same in service terms - lock the pedal (3.1), then check whether the crank is loose (6.2 on the 7.0R, 6.3 on the 7.0U) - and the general-maintenance page of every book in the range puts it in one sentence: *90% of calls to the service department for noise issues can be traced to loose hardware*. Those care procedures are carded under `section: maintenance`.

The CR900 recumbent answers a click with a four-item list that ends at the axle and the bearing nuts (`spirit-cr900-errors-clicking-or-loose-feeling-at-the-pedals`); nothing in the 7.0R/7.0U books goes past the crank.
