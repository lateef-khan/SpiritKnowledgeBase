**On the TR6.6 and TR6.4, E2 is an incline fault, not over current.** The owner's manuals print: **E2: Incline position error.** No remedy is printed against it.

On every other Xterra treadmill E2 is over current (`xterra-treadmill-errors-e2-over-current-silicone-oil-then-board-or-motor`), and the incline position fault is E3, ER or ERR. Neither machine has a service manual in the knowledge base, so no VR test or wiring check is available for this code; the Engineering Mode on the same page asks for a top incline setting of 15 and a grade-return choice, which are console facts.
