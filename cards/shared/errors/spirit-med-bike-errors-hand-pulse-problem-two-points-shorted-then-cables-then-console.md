**The 7.0R and 7.0U owner's manuals print no hand pulse row; this is the service manuals', and both print it the same.**

> **Hand Pulse Problem**
> i. Check the cables in the back of console for the possible loose. Go to next step if the problem doesn't solve.
> ii. Measure the hand pulse sensor as the picture below. **The two points should be short when the console was on the bike and any other two points shouldn't be short.** Set the hand pulse sensor to the right setting if the setup was wrong. Go to next step if the setup is right.
> iii. Open the cover and check the cables connection and possible broken cables. Replace the console if everything is good.

**The middle step is a continuity reading, not a voltage.** The two contacts the picture marks should read shorted with the console fitted, and no other pair should - a grip whose contacts read open, or read shorted to a neighbour, is a wrongly set or damaged sensor. The text says "set the hand pulse sensor to the right setting" and the picture carries the marking; the book prints no ohm figure and no part number.

The 7.5S stepper prints the same three steps with the two contacts called *the two rear sensors points* (`7-5s-med-errors-hand-pulse-problem-rear-sensor-points-shorted`). The Spirit-wide matrix row for the same symptom lists four causes rather than a procedure (`spirit-hand-pulse-not-working`), and the residential bikes' Q&A walks the wiring to a continuity test (`spirit-bike-errors-no-pulse-displayed-check-hand-pulse-wiring-then-continuity`).
