**Meaning**: abnormal voltage at the motor terminals, or a problem with the motor wiring. The drive motor wires may be loose, the motor controller may be damaged, or the drive motor may be dead.

If the error appeared after the machine was assembled, replace the computer cables first.

1. Unplug the machine and remove the motor hood cover. Check where the drive motor wires plug into the motor controller.
2. Make sure the drive motor wires are tight, then see whether the fault stays.
3. Inspect the drive motor wires and the controller for damage.
   - Visible damage: replace the damaged parts, then go to the wear checks.
   - No visible damage: run a battery test on the drive motor.
     - Motor passes: replace the motor controller.
     - Motor fails: replace the drive motor.
4. Then run the wear checks: ask about lubrication, check the running belt and deck for wear, and ask about squeaking or knocking noises. Replace worn rollers, belt or deck as found.
