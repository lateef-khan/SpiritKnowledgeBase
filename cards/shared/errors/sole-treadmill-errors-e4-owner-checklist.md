**This is the stencil-era owner's-manual E4 (abnormal motor voltage), not the 2026 E04 and not Spirit's E4.**

The six owner's manuals print the same ERROR MESSAGES table with OK/NG checkboxes. The F80-2023, F85-2023 and F89-2023 prints read 'E1_'..'E7_' on the OCR layer — the render shows a plain code beside the checkbox column.

| ERROR | MEANING | POSSIBLE CAUSE |
|---|---|---|
| Console showing E4 | Abnormal motor voltage | Motor is not connected properly or even not connected to the controller. |

The service manuals' deeper E4 procedure (motor power wire) is a separate card: see `f65-2023-e4-motor-power-wire`.
