| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Worn brushes or bearings on motor | Replace with new motor |
| Front roller bearings are defective | Replace with new front roller |
| Drive belt is misadjusted (too tight or too loose) | Adjust motor position |

Three causes, in the order the manual lists them. **The cheapest one is printed last** - a drive
belt that is merely too tight or too loose is fixed by moving the motor, with no part to buy - so
check the belt tension before condemning the motor or the roller.

A noise from the **rear** of the machine is a separate row with its own two causes:
`spirit-2024-errors-noise-in-the-rear-of-the-treadmill`.

**Sixteen treadmill service manuals print this row with the same three causes and three fixes** - the 2015 and 2023 XT185 to XT685, the CT800 2016, CT800 2020, CT800ENT 2022, CT850ENT 2022, CT900ENT, CT1000ENT 2023 and 4.0T. **The service checklist in some of the same books answers the first cause differently**: `Replace with new motor brushes` rather than a new motor (`spirit-xt-errors-noise-under-motor-cover-checklist-motor-brushes`).
