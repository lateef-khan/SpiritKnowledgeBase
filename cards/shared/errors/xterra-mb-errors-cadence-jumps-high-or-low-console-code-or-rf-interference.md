*Troubleshooting - Cadence number jumps high or low*, printed identically in the MB500 and MB550 books:

1. **Separate bikes that may be set to the same console code and are cross-talking**, or re-synchronize
   the transmitter and console (see page 7).
2. **Relocate the bike to a different part of the room**, away from any RF interference areas.

> CAUTION!! External interference may be caused by other electronic devices, such as: nearby televisions,
> stereo equipment, speakers, electrical wire cabling, etc. If you experience disturbances in the console
> display try moving your bike(s) away from potential RF interference areas.

"Console code" is the book's phrase for the transmitter identity, not a fault code: two consoles are
listening to one transmitter.

**The "(see page 7)" pointer is dead in both books.** Printed page 7 is an assembly step in both - the
bottle holder and seat in the MB550 book (PDF p. 9), the handlebars and bottle holder in the MB500 book
(PDF p. 5 of the scan) - and neither book prints a re-synchronising or pairing procedure anywhere - the sentence is carried over from the Dyaco template the Spirit XIC600 and
Sole SB700 books share, where the procedure exists. Follow the two steps that are printed: separate the
bikes and move away from interference. The Spirit and Sole cards below hold the same two steps for their
own consoles, with their pairing procedures.
