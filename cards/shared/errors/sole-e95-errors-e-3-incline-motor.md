**This is E-3 with a hyphen, the E95 incline-motor calibration error — not the Sole treadmill E3 incline fault (`sole-e3-error`), which names controllers and cables instead of a STOP bypass.**

The book prints, word for word: “If calibration is errors of the incline motor,in screen will shows “E-3”.You can press STOP key by pass.” The screen shows a red warning panel reading E-3 with “Press Stop to bypass” beneath it. If calibration passes instead, the Home page appears.

Calibration is started from the adjacent Incline Calibration paragraph (Incline-up key plus Start); the maintenance-menu procedure itself is a console fact. If the problem persists the book says to contact the service department.
