The row `Belt surging - Belt speeds up and slows down frequently`:

1. **Adjust torque boost.**
2. Adjust the speed sensor.
3. Replace the controller.

Procedure 6 in the XT manual (Procedure 5 in the CT800 2016) is the torque boost adjustment, word for word the same in both:

1. Using a small screwdriver, turn the dial counter-clockwise until it stops.
2. Press the START button on the console.
3. With someone standing on the walking belt, turn clockwise until the belt begins to move.
4. Increase the speed and check for smooth belt movement.

The XT controller diagram places the dial as item 8, *Torque Boost Adjustment - Adjust at lower speeds to eliminate motor surging*. So the dial sets how much extra drive the controller adds at low speed under load; too little lets a loaded belt stall and lurch, too much overshoots. Set it to the point where a loaded belt just starts, no further.

Printed identically in the 2008 XT dealer manual and the CT800 2016 service manual's *Treadmill Troubleshooting* chapter. No other Spirit treadmill service manual in the repository mentions a torque boost dial.
