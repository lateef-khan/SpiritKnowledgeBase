| Condition | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| After removing safety key, treadmill can't stop. | 1. The safety key device is broken. | 1. Replace with new safety key device. 2. Replace controller. 3. Replace console. |

One cause and three fixes in the order printed - the key device first, then the controller, then the console. The 2023 XT185, XT285, XT385, XT485 and XT685, the 2015 XT385 and XT485 and the CT1000ENT 2023 service manuals print it identically.

The CT800, CT850, CT900ENT, 4.0T and 2015 XT185 and XT285 books stop at the first fix (`ct850-2016-does-not-stop-after-safety-key-removed`). The opposite fault - the machine runs with no key fitted at all - is `ct850-2016-operates-without-safety-key`, which every one of these books prints the same way.
