**Caution! Do not use this elliptical for Heart Rate Control unless a steady, solid Actual Heart
Rate value is being displayed. High, wild, random numbers being displayed indicate a problem.**

Areas to look for interference which may cause erratic heart rate:

1. **Microwave ovens, TV's, small appliances**, etc.
2. **Fluorescent lights.**
3. **Some household security systems.**
4. **Perimeter fence for a pet.**
5. Some people have problems with the transmitter picking up a signal from their skin. **Try
   wearing the transmitter upside down.** Normally the transmitter is oriented so the Spirit logo
   is right side up.
6. The antenna that picks up your heart rate is very sensitive. If there is an outside noise
   source, **turning the whole machine 90 degrees may de-tune the interference.** *(Printed in the
   XE100/XE200/XE300 manual only. The XE150/XE350/XE550 manual stops at item 5.)*

If you continue to experience problems, contact your dealer.

**These two books are the only Spirit elliptical manuals that do not print "Another individual
wearing a transmitter within 3' of your machine's console".** Neither the XE150/XE350/XE550 book
nor the XE100/XE200/XE300 book states any distance at which a second strap starts to interfere, so
do not quote three feet at an owner of one of these six machines. Every later residential XE and
every commercial CE manual does print it: `spirit-ce-errors-erratic-heart-rate-interference`.

Both books call the feature **Heart Rate Control**, not "Heart Rate programs" as every manual from
the XE395 2010 onwards does, and both write the logo as `Spirit` rather than `Spirit Fitness`.

**On the XE150 the heart rate transmitter is optional**, so an XE150 may have no chest strap to
interfere with in the first place; the XE350 and XE550 sections of the same manual do not carry
that qualifier.
