**This is a message, not a code.** It is printed in the ERROR CODES table of both books, below the
numbered codes and below `Replace Safety Key`.

| Error Message | Cause | Troubleshooting |
|---|---|---|
| SAFETY LOCK | Children's Safety mode is on. | Press and hold the Incline key to relieve this mode. |

**The two books disagree about which incline key, and neither is legible as a word.** The CT800ENT
2024 prints `Press and hold Incline& key`; the CT850ENT 2024 prints `Press and hold Incline A key`.
Both are a rendering of an arrow glyph that the page draws rather than spells, and the rendered page
does not resolve which arrow it is. Read it as **hold an incline key** and try the up arrow first;
neither manual says anywhere else how the mode is switched on, so there is nothing to check it
against.

**Nothing else in either manual mentions a children's safety mode** - not the console chapter, not
the programs chapter, not the safety pages. This table row is the whole of what the documents say
about it.

The other message in the same table is `Replace Safety Key`, which is a missing tether key and a
different fault: `spirit-2024-errors-replace-safety-key-message`.
