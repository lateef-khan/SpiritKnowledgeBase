**This is E03 on the WS200 and WS300 walking treadmills**, the missing speed-sensor signal. On the Dyaco-built Xterra treadmills lost speed is E1 (`xterra-treadmill-errors-e1-no-rpm-signal-only-in-calibration`) and E3 is an incline fault; on the Sole 2026 treadmills E03 is a hardware over-current (`f63-2026-e03-error-code`).

The owner's manuals print one row for it, in a table that is a flat picture read from the render:

| Questions | Possible cause | Method |
|---|---|---|
| E03 | Non-sensing signal | Sensing wire is not plugged in properly. Plug it again correctly |

That is the whole printed entry. The seven-code table it comes from is `xterra-ws-errors-e01-to-e07-error-table`; the no-code "treadmill does not work" rows are `xterra-ws-errors-treadmill-does-not-work-five-causes`.
