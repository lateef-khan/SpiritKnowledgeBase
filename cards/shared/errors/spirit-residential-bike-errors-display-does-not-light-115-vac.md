**The manual gives two causes and stops. There is no error code, no fuse check and no breaker
check.**

| Problem | Solution/Cause |
|---|---|
| Display does not light | 1. Plug is disconnected. Make sure plug is firmly pushed into 115 VAC wall outlet. |
| | 2. Bike defect. Contact your Spirit Fitness dealer. |

**115 VAC is the figure these bikes print**, and it is the only voltage in the row. Do not carry a
Spirit treadmill's figure across: the CT900ENT and the XT books print 115 VAC, the 40T 2026 prints
110 VAC, the CT800 2020 prints 110 - 120 VAC and the CT850 service manuals print a plain 120 VAC.
The treadmill rows are also longer, and they open with the **tether cord** and the **two circuit
breakers** - the one on the machine's front grill and the one in the building's panel. **These
bikes have neither a tether cord nor a breaker on the machine**, so those steps do not exist on
the machine in front of you. The household breaker still can, and the manual's own Electrical
Safety page names the AFCI/GFCI brands Spirit has tested - but the manual does not list it as a
cause in this row, and this card does not add one.

The XBR25 2023, XBR55 2023 and XBU55 2023 print this row **word for word identically** - their
whole troubleshooting pages match at 100% once bare page numbers are stripped.

**The other eleven residential bike manuals do not print this row at all.** The XBR95 2016, 2018,
2021 and 2023, the XBR25 2019, the XBR55 2019 and 2021, the XBU55 2019 and 2021, and the XBR55ENT
and XBU55ENT 2021 have no troubleshooting chapter of any kind - see
`spirit-commercial-bike-errors-no-error-codes-printed`. For those machines the manual offers no
answer to a dead display, and the XBR95 2023 in particular is **not** covered by this card even
though its sister XBR55 2023 is.

This is a Spirit card. A Sole bike or treadmill with a dead console is a different fault list
with different figures - see `sole-no-display` and `f65-2016-display-does-not-light`, and do not
serve either of those to a Spirit owner.
