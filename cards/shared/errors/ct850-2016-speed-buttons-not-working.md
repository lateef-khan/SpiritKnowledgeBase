The manuals fold three conditions into one block of causes, and print it identically in 2016 and 2020:

- FAST/SLOW button of SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH can't be used.
- Speed button just can press FAST, can't press SLOW.
- Speed button just can press SLOW, can't press FAST.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The connector of SPEED CABLE and CONSOLE not connected properly | Connect cables again |
| The connector of SPEED CABLE and SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/CABLE not connected properly | Connect cables again |
| The connector of SPEED CABLE or SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH/W/CABLE is damaged | Connect cable again |
| Button of SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH is broken | Replace with new buttons |
| The connector of SPEED CABLE or SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH/W/CABLE is damaged | Replace with new cable |
| The connector of SPEED CABLE or SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH/W/CABLE is damaged | Replace with new cable |

The last three reasons are the same sentence printed three times; the manual gives no way to tell
them apart. The incline buttons have their own row: `ct850-2016-incline-buttons-not-working`.

**The CT800ENT 2024 and CT850ENT 2024 owner's manuals print this row word for word** in the
Condition/Reason/Solve matrix on printed pages 57 to 59. Both of those pages are flat pictures
with no text layer and were read from the rendered page.

All three conditions and all six reasons are unchanged, the sentence repeated three times
included.

**Six more service manuals print the six-cause list word for word**, the sentence repeated three times included: the CT800 2016, CT800 2020, CT800ENT 2022, CT850ENT 2022, CT900ENT and 4.0T. The XT and CT1000ENT books fold speed and incline into one shorter row (`spirit-xt-errors-handrail-speed-and-incline-buttons-not-working`).
