The only fault-and-fix line in the five folding-bike books is a NOTE under the console functions:

- **If the display is faint or shows no figures, please replace the batteries.**
- The monitor will automatically shut off if there is no signal received after 4 minutes.

So a blank console on a bike nobody has pedalled for four minutes is asleep, not broken; pedal or
press a key first. If the figures are dim or missing while riding, fit new batteries. The FB160, FB360
and FB180 books give the type as **2 AAA or UM4**; the FB150 and FB350 books print no battery type.

None of the five books has a troubleshooting section or an error code
(`xterra-errors-owner-manual-has-no-troubleshooting-section-and-no-error-code-table`).
