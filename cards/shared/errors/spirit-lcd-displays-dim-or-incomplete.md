| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| LCD displays are broken | Replace with new console |

The CT850 2016 and CVC800 manuals print this row identically.

This is the row about the **displays**, not the light behind them. The row above it offers a power
check first and is on `spirit-lcd-dim-or-incomplete`. This row offers no check at all - the
manual goes straight to a new console.

The CT850 2020 manual prints the same row about LEDs: `ct850-2020-led-displays-dim-or-incomplete`.
The CU900ENT prints a TFT version: `cu900ent-tft-displays-not-bright`.

**Three of the 2024 New Black machines print this row word for word about an LCD** - the CR800 2024
recumbent bike, the CU800 2024 upright bike and the CRS800S 2024 semi-recumbent stepper. All three
say `LCD displays are broken` and `Replace with new console`, and all three pages are flat pictures
read from the rendered page.

**On the two bikes the row above it talks about LEDs and this one about LCDs, in the same table.**
`LEDs not bright, incomplete or imperfect` sits directly above `LED displays not bright, incomplete
or imperfect`, whose reason column then reads `LCD displays are broken`. The books mix the two nouns
on one page; nothing turns on which is used.

The CE800 2024 and CE850 2024 ellipticals and the CS800 2024 stepper print the LED wording instead:
`ct850-2020-led-displays-dim-or-incomplete`.

**Ten more service manuals print this one-line row word for word** - `LCD displays not bright, incomplete, or imperfect / LCD displays are broken / Replace with new console` - the 2015 and 2023 XT185 to XT685 and the CT800 2016. The LED and TFT versions are on `ct850-2020-led-displays-dim-or-incomplete` and `cu900ent-tft-displays-not-bright`.

**Nine bike service manuals print this one-line row.** The XBR25 2016, XBR55 2016, XBU55 2016, XBR95 2016, XBR55ENT 2021 and XBU55ENT 2021 print it word for word about an LCD. The CU800 2020-book (`cu800-2021`), the CR800 2020-book (`cr800-2021`) and the CU800 2012 print it the way the 2024 bikes do - `LED displays not bright, incomplete or imperfect` as the condition, `LCD displays are broken` as the reason, `Replace with new console` as the fix - so the noun mix described above is a decade old, not a 2024 slip.
