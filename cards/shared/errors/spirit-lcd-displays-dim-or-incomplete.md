| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| LCD displays are broken | Replace with new console |

The CT850 2016 and CVC800 manuals print this row identically.

This is the row about the **displays**, not the light behind them. The row above it offers a power
check first and is on `spirit-lcd-dim-or-incomplete`. This row offers no check at all - the
manual goes straight to a new console.

The CT850 2020 manual prints the same row about LEDs: `ct850-2020-led-displays-dim-or-incomplete`.
The CU900ENT prints a TFT version: `cu900ent-tft-displays-not-bright`.
