**The book is not missing a page. There is no troubleshooting page to find.** Four owner's manuals - the Spirit Medical 4.0R and 4.0U 2025 books (Rev 10.03.25) and the undated Dyaco "PT" 4.0 R and 4.0 U user manuals they descend from - print **no error message, no error code, no condition/reason/solve matrix and no troubleshooting chapter**. Their contents pages run Heart Rate, Machine Care (or *Maintenance* in the Dyaco books), Exploded View, Parts List, Warranty, and stop.

**How the absence was checked.** Across the four books (45, 45, 63 and 59 PDF pages) the words *troubleshooting*, *error* and *fault* appear **not once** - every hit for those letters is *default* - and a letter-spaced search (`T R O U B L E`, `E R R O R`) finds nothing either. Every page of each PDF was rendered at 300 dpi and read with tesseract against its text layer; the pages where the render knows more than the text are covers, contents, safety-label art, parts diagrams and the heart-rate pages, not a fault table.

**The nearest thing the owner's manuals print** is the Machine Care page: *if a squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling develops* it is loose hardware or the crank arm nut and pedals (carded under `section: maintenance`), and the heart-rate chapter's advice that *random numbers being displayed indicate a problem* with the chest belt's contact (`section: console`).

**The service manuals fill the gap**, and they exist for both machines:

- `EEPROM ERR` - the only message the console has, and how to clear it: `spirit-med-bike-errors-eeprom-err-start-stop-fan-three-seconds-then-console`.
- A dim or partly lit display: `spirit-med-bike-errors-leds-not-bright-check-power-to-console-then-lower-controller`; dead segments: `ct850-2020-led-displays-dim-or-incomplete`.
- Pulse faults - erratic, hand pulse dead, chest belt dead: `spirit-erratic-pulse-display`, `spirit-hand-pulse-not-working`, `spirit-wireless-chest-belt-no-pulse`.
- The 4.0U book's Q&A chapter - display dead, no RPM, no heart rate, noises, the belt coming off: `spirit-bike-errors-display-wont-come-on-check-computer-cable-then-meter`, `spirit-bike-errors-no-speed-readout-hall-sensor-or-magnet-test-with-another-magnet`, `spirit-bike-errors-no-pulse-displayed-check-hand-pulse-wiring-then-continuity`, `cu800-2012-errors-noises-crank-or-pulley-rubbing-the-chain-covers-or-foreign-material`, `40u-2025-errors-belt-drop-idler-wheel-pivot-weld-or-pulley-alignment`. The 4.0R service manual has no Q&A chapter.

The 7.0R and 7.0U of the same range *do* print a troubleshooting page in their owner's manuals (`spirit-med-bike-errors-eeprom-error-replace-the-console-only-message` and the cards it links), and the 8.0U/8.5R put the fault on the touchscreen instead (`spirit-med-bike-errors-touchscreen-console-shows-the-message-and-the-fix`).
