**This is the TR150 and TRX1400 row**; the TR260 and TRX books print a six-item cable version (`xterra-treadmill-errors-speed-buttons-not-working-speed-cable-six-checks`).

*Condition:* FAST/SLOW button of SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH can't be used. Speed button can only press FAST, can't press SLOW. Speed button can only press SLOW, can't press FAST.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1. Maybe keys stuck | 1. Replace key board |
| 2. Maybe upper control is broken | 2. Replace upper controller |
| 3. Maybe lower control is broken | 3. Replace lower controller |

The incline buttons have their own row (`trx1400-2023-errors-incline-buttons-not-working-vr-wires-keys-motor-gears`; the TR150 matrix has none).
