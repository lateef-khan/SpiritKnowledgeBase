**Three of these books print no error code and no troubleshooting section at all. The fourth, the SR400, prints exactly one E-2 line.**

What each book prints instead:

- SR500-2019 (24 pages): registration, safety, assembly, console operation, programs, RPE chart, warranty. No troubleshooting chapter, no codes.
- SR500-2022 (25 pages): same shape plus a game program. No troubleshooting chapter, no codes.
- SR550-2023 (23 pages): registration, safety, assembly, touchscreen operation, folding, heart-rate programs, training chart, RPE, warranty, service. No troubleshooting chapter, no codes. Its two "troubleshooting/diagnosing" hits are warranty/service-process sentences, not a table.
- SR400 (19 pages): assembly, operation, programs P01-P12, parts lists. Its only error content is one remark under Recovery Mode: "If console display E-2, pls check if cables were well connected or damaged" (`sr400-e2-console-cable-check`).

A caller quoting E1 or E2 on an SR500-2019, SR500-2022 or SR550-2023 is quoting a different book: E1/E2 live in the SR500-2016 and SR550-2023 *service* manuals, carded separately and linked here as look-alikes, not matches.
