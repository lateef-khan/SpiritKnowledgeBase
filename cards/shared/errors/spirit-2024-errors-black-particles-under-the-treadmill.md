| Condition | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| Black particles collecting under treadmill. | Drive belt is breaking in. | Vacuum under treadmill periodically. |

**The manual treats this as normal wear-in, not a fault.** It names no threshold, no time limit and
no point at which the particles stop being expected, and it asks for no inspection of the drive
belt. The only action is to vacuum.

If the drive belt is also noisy or misadjusted, that is a different row:
`spirit-2024-errors-noise-under-the-motor-cover`.
