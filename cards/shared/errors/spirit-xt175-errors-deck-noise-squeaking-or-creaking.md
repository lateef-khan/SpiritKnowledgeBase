The Mechanical System row `Deck noise - Squeaking or creaking while walking`:

1. Tighten the deck bolts.
2. Clean and re-lube the deck.

Printed identically in the 2008 XT dealer manual and the CT800 2016 service manual's *Treadmill Troubleshooting* chapter. The CT800 2016 adds two one-line procedures beside it - *Tighten Drive Motor* (remove the motor top cover, check the motor screws are not loose, tighten) and *Grease Incline Motor Bolt* (raise the incline to its highest, remove the motor cover, clean and grease a dirty or dry incline motor bushing and bolt) - which the XT manual does not print. Both are maintenance tasks rather than diagnoses.
