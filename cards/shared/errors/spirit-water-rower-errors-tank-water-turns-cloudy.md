Both water rower manuals print this row in the same words.

| Possible Cause | Instructions |
|---|---|
| Rower is in direct sunlight or has not had water treatment | Change rower location to reduce direct exposure to sunlight. Add water treatment or change tank water as directed in this manual. Consider using distilled water to refill tank. |
| Local tap water is of poor quality | |

So three things to do, in the order the manual gives them: **move the rower out of the sunlight**,
**treat or replace the tank water**, and **refill with distilled water** where the tap water is poor.

**The CRW800H2O manual sends you to the wrong page.** Its row reads "change tank water as directed
on **page 25** of this manual". Printed page 25 of that book is `TARGET TIME, DISTANCE, CALORIES
MODE` - a console chapter. The tank filling and water treatment instructions are on printed
**page 18**, which is where its own contents page points. The CRW900 manual's reference is correct:
it names the Water Treatment Section at **pg 33**, and printed page 33 of that book is
`TANK FILLING & WATER TREATMENT PROCEDURES`.

**The two machines are treated with different tablets and different amounts, so do not carry one
figure to the other.** The CRW900 takes **1 Water Treatment Tablet per full tank**, is shipped with
**4 tablets**, and its manual says to use no tablet other than the ones supplied. The CRW800H2O
takes **1 water-purification tablet**, shipped as **1 packet**, repeated **every 6 months or as
needed**, and its manual adds the line this row does not: *if water remains cloudy, replace the
water in the tank*. Neither book prints the other's figures.

Neither manual says pool chlorine or bleach will clear the water - the CRW800H2O says the opposite,
that it **damages the tank and voids the warranty**.

Water coming *out* of the tank is a different fault: `crw800h2o-water-leaking`.
