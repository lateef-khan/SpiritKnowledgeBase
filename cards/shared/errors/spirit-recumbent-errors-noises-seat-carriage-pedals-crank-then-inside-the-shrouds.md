**Eight recumbent books open with the same sentence** - *Noises are mostly caused by loose screws/bolts, sometimes rubbing or poor smoothness due to mechanical deformity/shifting may also cause noises* - and list the same places in the same order.

1. **Seat Carriage (4)**: noises there are mostly a loose **Seat Wheel Adjustment Plate** after long use, and usually come with serious play. The seat-carriage procedure (procedure 10 in the parts chapter) is the remedy. The CR800 2020-book calls the wobble *a normal phenomenon for a long term used machine*.
2. **Pedals (116) (117)**: worn pedals cause poor smoothness and noise. Replace them. The CR800 2020-book calls them Footrests.
3. **Crank Arm (51L, 51R)** loose can also cause poor smoothness and noise, *although the chance is low*. Tighten.
4. If noises still persist after disassembling **Front Shrouds (29, 30)**, find the spot where the noise starts and replace parts when necessary.

**Two of the eight add a step between 3 and 4, and name the usual culprit.** The CR800 2009 (XR898): *in most cases it is right Front Shroud (30) and Drive Pulley (20) that are rubbing each other. Replace Drive Pulley (20) or put one more 5/16" x 16 x 1T Flat Washer (127) to left Front Shroud (29)*. The CR800 2020-book: *in most case, it is Front Shroud (30) which is rubbing against Drive Pulley (20) and the solution is to replace Drive Pulley (20)* - the washer trick is gone.

Three of the eight - the XBR55 2023, XBR95 2023 and CR800 2009 - close the chapter with *contact DYACO for problems not covered in this service manual. Any problem solving on the unit is back-up by DYACO technical team.*

The uprights list different places (`spirit-upright-errors-noises-pedals-crank-then-chain-covers-or-drive-pulley`), and the CR900 books split the same symptom into a pedal card and a shaking card (`spirit-cr900-errors-clicking-or-loose-feeling-at-the-pedals`, `spirit-cr900-errors-handlebar-seat-or-whole-bike-shaking`). The owner's-manual answer for the residential bikes - loose assembly hardware, the crank arm nut, the levelling pads - is a maintenance card and does not mention the seat carriage at all.
