The manual's answer, word for word:

> Treadmill may not be grounded. Static electricity is "crashing" the computer. Refer to Grounding Instructions.

**All four CT850 owner's manuals print this row word for word** - the 2016, 2018 and 2020
manuals and the CT850ENT 2022 manual - each pointing at its own Grounding Instructions page
rather than repeating them.

The grounding instructions themselves are in the product safety section of each manual, not here.

The CT800, CT800ENT, CT900ENT, 4.0T and XT manuals print the identical row, carded separately
as `ct900ent-computer-shuts-off-static-cold-weather`.
