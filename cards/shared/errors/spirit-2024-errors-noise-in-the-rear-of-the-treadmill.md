| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Rear roller bearings are defective | Replace with new rear roller |
| Rear roller misaligned | Adjust rear roller position |

Two causes, in the order the manual lists them. **Alignment is the second one and costs nothing**,
so check the roller position before ordering a roller.

A noise from the **front**, under the motor cover, is a separate row with three causes of its own:
`spirit-2024-errors-noise-under-the-motor-cover`.
