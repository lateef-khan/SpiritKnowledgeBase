Three causes and four fixes, in the order the manual lists them.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| House breaker tripped | Reset it |
| Treadmill breaker tripped | Reset treadmill breaker |
| Treadmill controller shut down and LED would be ON | Belt / deck lubrication. Turn off the ON/OFF switch and turn on power again |

The third cause carries two fixes: lubricate the belt and deck, then power-cycle. That is the row saying a controller that shut itself down did so under load, which is the same reasoning as the E2 over-current section (`spirit-xt-2023-errors-e2-over-current-lubricate-then-belt-then-controller`). The 2023 XT185 to XT685, the 2015 XT385 and XT485 and the CT1000ENT 2023 service manuals print it identically.

The CT800, CT850, CT900ENT, 4.0T and 2015 XT185 and XT285 books print a four-cause version with a controller fuse and no lubrication step (`ct850-2016-stops-or-shuts-off-by-itself`). A stop the moment START is pressed is a different row: `ct850-2016-stops-immediately-after-start`.
