**This is the misbehaving display, not the dark one** - a console that shows nothing at all is the troubleshooting-table row (`xterra-rower-errors-monitor-does-not-display-install-batteries-then-computer-wires`).

Two books print a one-line remedy on their console page, and the two differ in what they do with the batteries:

| Book | Printed | What it asks |
|---|---|---|
| ERG220 | "If the console displays abnormally, please re-install the batteries and try again." | Take the two AA cells out and put them back - a power-cycle reset |
| ERG600W | "If there is a problem with the display, try changing the batteries first." | Fit new cells before suspecting anything else |

The ERG600W adds the rules for the change: change both, do not mix battery types, do not mix old and new, and **changing the batteries resets all values**. The ERG220 says the same of removal: whenever the batteries are removed, all values reset to zero. So either remedy costs the stored workout values, and that is expected.

Neither book says how long to leave the cells out; the EU100 hybrid's version of this note names 15 seconds (`eu100-2018-errors-display-illegible-or-partial-segments-remove-batteries-15-seconds`), and the folding bikes' version says only to replace them (`xterra-fb-errors-display-faint-or-no-figures-replace-the-batteries`). Those figures belong to those machines.
