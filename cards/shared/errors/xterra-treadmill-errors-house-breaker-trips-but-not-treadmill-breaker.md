Every Xterra owner's manual with a *Service Checklist* prints this row:

*Circuit breaker trips, but not the treadmill circuit breaker.* Check that the treadmill is the only appliance on the circuit. See "Important Electrical Information" (new-layout books: the "Electrical Safety" section) in the front of the manual.

The **TRX1400 owner's manual adds a second line: "Need to replace the house breaker with a 'High In-rush current' type breaker."** Its service manual's checklist prints only that line - *House circuit breaker trips, but not the treadmill circuit breaker: need to replace the house breaker with a "High inrush current" type breaker (see section 7.2 for Important Electrical Instructions)* - and the electrical pages of every Dyaco service manual carry the same warning that some home breakers are not rated for high inrush current. The dedicated-circuit and breaker requirements are safety cards.

The treadmill's own breaker tripping is the other row (`xterra-treadmill-errors-trips-15-amp-circuit-high-belt-deck-friction`).
