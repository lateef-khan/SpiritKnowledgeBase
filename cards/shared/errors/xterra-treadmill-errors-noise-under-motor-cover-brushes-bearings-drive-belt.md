The troubleshooting procedure matrix prints this row in all five service manuals:

*Condition:* Noise under motor cover.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1. Worn brushes or bearings on motor | 1. Replace with new motor |
| 2. Front roller bearings are defective | 2. Replace with new front roller |
| 3. Drive belt is misadjusted (too tight or too loose) | 3. Adjust motor position |

The TRX1400 service manual prints the row a second time in its 10.2 checklist, where remedy 1 reads **"Replace with new motor brushes"** rather than a new motor. Noise from the back of the machine is `xterra-treadmill-errors-noise-at-rear-roller-bearings-or-misaligned`; the TRX1400's four-item "treadmill with noises" row is `trx1400-2023-errors-noises-rollers-incline-cylinder-bolts-thumping`. Drive belt tension and motor position adjustment are maintenance cards.
