**This is E1, not E2 (the gear motor).**

E1 is an EEPROM error in the display board. It is a software fault. A pinched computer cable or a damaged display board can cause it.

1. Ask whether the machine has recently been assembled, disassembled or moved.
2. **Yes** - replace the **Computer Cable**.
3. **No** - replace the **Display Board**.

**The service manuals answer this differently.** The 2016-generation books define E1 as an EEPROM failure and say to replace the upper controller (`e35-2016-e1-eeprom-failure`); the 2023 books say to replace the display board directly.
