One cause and one fix, printed identically in the 2016 and 2020 manuals.

| Condition | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| With no safe key but treadmill could display or operate | Safety device is broken (short) | Replace the safety key device or console |

The opposite fault - the treadmill will not stop when the key is pulled out - is a separate row on
`ct850-2016-does-not-stop-after-safety-key-removed`.
