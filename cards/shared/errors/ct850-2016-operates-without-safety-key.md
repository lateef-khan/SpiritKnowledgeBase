One cause and one fix, printed identically in the 2016 and 2020 manuals.

| Condition | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| With no safe key but treadmill could display or operate | Safety device is broken (short) | Replace the safety key device or console |

The opposite fault - the treadmill will not stop when the key is pulled out - is a separate row on
`ct850-2016-does-not-stop-after-safety-key-removed`.

**The CT800ENT 2024 and CT850ENT 2024 owner's manuals print this row word for word** in the
Condition/Reason/Solve matrix on printed pages 57 to 59. Both of those pages are flat pictures
with no text layer and were read from the rendered page.

**Sixteen more service manuals print this row word for word** - the 2015 and 2023 XT185 to XT685 books, the CT800 2016, CT800 2020, CT800ENT 2022, CT850ENT 2022, CT900ENT, CT1000ENT 2023 and 4.0T. One cause, one fix, in every one. The XT685ENT and XT485ENT service manuals print no matrix at all, so the row is absent from those two documents.
