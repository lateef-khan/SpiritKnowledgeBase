The owner's manuals of these four machines define E2 in one line: **Over the rated current. The controller is over the rated current for 3 seconds.**

The figure is 3 seconds here and **6 seconds in the TRX1400 owner's manual** (`trx1400-2023-errors-e2-overload-over-rated-current-for-6-seconds`); the 2021-batch and TR65 books print no time. What to do about it is on the service-manual card `xterra-treadmill-errors-e2-over-current-silicone-oil-then-board-or-motor` (TRX2500, TRX3500, TRX4500; the TR300 has no service manual in the knowledge base).
