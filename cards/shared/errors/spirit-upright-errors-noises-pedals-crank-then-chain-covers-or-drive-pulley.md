**Both upright books print the same four causes in the same words.**

> The causes of noises are mostly loose screws. Sometimes it is because of parts being deformed or shifting causing rubbing or unsmooth moving.

1. **Left and right pedals (45) (46)**: mostly pedal wear. Worn pedals must be replaced.
2. **Left and right crank**: sometimes the crank gets loose. Tightening it fixes the problem.
3. **Chain covers (36, 37)**: most cases are the covers deforming so that the **crank arms (16L, 16R) scratch them**. *The deform is because of the requirement by safety regulations of minimum gaps.* The solution is to replace the chain covers. In some cases the noise is a **deformed Drive Pulley (15) rubbing the right chain cover (37)**; replace the drive pulley.
4. If there are still noises with the left and right shrouds off, find where the noise comes from and replace parts if necessary.

**Cause 3 is the one the recumbent books do not have**, and the reason the book gives is worth keeping: the covers sit close to the cranks because the safety rules require a minimum gap, so a slightly deformed cover touches. The recumbents' list runs seat carriage, pedals, crank, then the inside of the front shrouds (`spirit-recumbent-errors-noises-seat-carriage-pedals-crank-then-inside-the-shrouds`).

The XBU55 2016 has two more noise entries of its own, for the flywheel (`xbu55-2016-errors-flywheel-noise-bearing-housing-wear-or-a-bent-aluminium-sheet`) and for a swaying seat post or console mast (`xbu55-2016-errors-seat-post-console-mast-or-handlebar-sway-tighten`). The CU800 2012 that preceded the CU800 2020-book says the same thing about the crank and the pulley rubbing the covers, and adds foreign material in the covers: `cu800-2012-errors-noises-crank-or-pulley-rubbing-the-chain-covers-or-foreign-material`.
