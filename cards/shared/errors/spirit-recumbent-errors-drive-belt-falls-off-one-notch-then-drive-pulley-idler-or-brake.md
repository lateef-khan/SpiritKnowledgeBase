**Eight recumbent books give the same escalation, and the last step is the whole bike.**

The XBR25 2016, XBR55 2016, XBR95 2016, XBR55ENT 2021 and CR800 2009 (XR898):

> Follow Procedure #5 in section I to install. Drive Belt (54) requires to turn back and forth to see if it falls off. **Adjust Drive Belt (54) on notch beyond against the side it falls off.** If it still keeps falling off, try to replace **Drive Pulley (20), Idler Wheel Assembly (10) or Induction Brake (55)**. If it still keeps falling off, the unit might have been seriously dropped and the frame were deformed and the whole unit requires to be replaced. It is rare that the unit fails at this part because it would be tested in the factory before shipping. This problem will occur when the unit drops from a high and incorrect way to put on the ground.

The XBR55 2023 and XBR95 2023 print the same paragraph naming **Drive Pulley (20) and Idler Wheel Assembly (10)** only - the brake is left off their parts list. The CR800 2020-book names the same three parts as the 2016 books, calling the third **Generator/Brake (55)**, and says to adjust the belt *reversely to the falling direction one notch at a time*.

So: first spin the crank both ways and watch which side the belt leaves; move it one groove the other way and test again; then the drive pulley, the idler wheel assembly and (on the books that list it) the brake; and only then a bent main frame, which the books tie to the bike having been dropped.

The "Procedure #5" all of them cite is the belt-and-idler replacement in the same book; the tension it is refitted to is on the slip card for the machine (`spirit-xbr-maintenance-belt-slip-nyloc-nut-88-450-newtons`, `spirit-recumbent-maintenance-belt-slip-nyloc-nut-88-180-hz`, `cr800-2021-maintenance-belt-slip-180-to-205-hz`). The uprights answer the same symptom differently: `xbu55-2016-errors-belt-falls-off-refit-and-test-before-the-chain-covers-go-back`, `cu800-2021-errors-belt-falls-off-one-groove-then-drive-pulley-bearing-housing-or-flywheel`.
