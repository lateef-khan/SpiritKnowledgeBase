**Only the XE400 and XE500 of the five machines in this book have a powered incline**, so this row and Procedure 6 apply to them alone.

`Incline does not work`
1. Verify that the incline motor wires are plugged in correctly where the rail assembly connects to the main body of the unit - **black to black, red to red, white to white, and the 3 wire harness connected**.
2. Check to be sure that all wires are connected to the incline controller correctly.

**Procedure 6 - incline controller wiring** - is a labelled photograph, not text: the red wire from the on/off switch, the red wires from the transformer, the black wires from the transformer, the main wire harness, the red, white and black wires from the incline motor and the harness from the incline motor, each pointed at its terminal on the controller. The book prints no voltage to measure at any of them. Procedure 9 is the XE400/XE500 wiring schematic. The parts lists name the **Incline Controller - Lower Board (006095, ALT#670100A)** and the **Incline Adaptor (Transformer) (006096)**.

**A wrong incline reading rather than a dead incline is Procedure 8** - *if the console shows an incline error or does not seem to physically be at the incline displayed, the unit needs to be calibrated*: hold START and level UP for 5 seconds, press ENTER at `Calibration`, and the system restarts when done. The XE395 2010 owner's manual prints that same hold (`xe395-2010-errors-incline-ramp-error-recalibrate`). **No message text is printed for the error**; do not tell a caller the console reads `INCLINE ERR`.

The 2016 XE395 answers a dead incline with a wire, controller and power-adaptor check and then a nine-step meter test (`spirit-xe-errors-incline-motor-not-functioning-check-wires-controller-and-power-adaptor`).
