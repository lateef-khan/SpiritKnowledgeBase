`Squeaking noise in the pedal (XE100 & XE200)`
1. Order and install a new connecting arms.

**That is the whole row** - no lubrication step, no bolt check, one part. The row is printed for the XE100 and XE200 only; the XE300, XE400 and XE500 are not named on it. The parts lists give the arms as **Connecting Arm (L) 006110 and (R) 006111** on the XE100 and **Connecting Arm (L) - Assembly 006018 and (R) 006019** on the XE200.

The other noise row on the same page, *thumping noise*, starts with cleaning and lubricating the wheels and rails and only then goes to new pedal-arm bushing housings; that row is a care answer and lives with the maintenance cards. The 2016 XE books answer a squeak from the swing arm with grease on the axle and then a 6005 bearing.
