**None of these three books prints a troubleshooting section, a diagnosis guide or an error code.**

- SW100 adjustable dumbbell: assembly instructions plus warranty. No troubleshooting heading anywhere.
- SW121 half rack: pack checklist plus assembly instructions. No troubleshooting heading anywhere.
- SRVO Complete (SRB101): registration, precautions, item checklist, assembly/folding. No diagnosis guide and no error table — unlike its sibling, the 2021 SRVO owner's book, which prints a five-item Diagnosis Guide and a 12-code Error Messages table (`srvo-error-code-table`), and unlike the SRVO service manual.

Do not read this card as "these machines have no faults": the SW155/SW180 dumbbell books *do* print one troubleshooting row (stuck adjustment handlebar, linked here), and the SRVO fault codes live on the linked table card.
