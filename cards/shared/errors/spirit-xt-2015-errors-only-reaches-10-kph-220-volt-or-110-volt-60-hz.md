The service checklist's answer, word for word:

> This indicates motor should be receiving power to operate. Low AC voltage to treadmill. Do not use an extension cord. If an extension cord is required it should be as short as possible and heavy duty 16 AWG minimum. Low household voltage. Contact an electrician or your dealer. A minimum of 220 volt AC current, 50 hz is required (of 110 volt AC current, 60 hz is required).

So **16 AWG minimum** for the cord and either **220 V at 50 Hz** or **110 V at 60 Hz** at the outlet, depending on the build. Only the XT285 2015 service manual prints the checklist; the XT185 2015 service manual has none, and sits here because the two books are otherwise one document. The 2015 XT385, XT485 and the XT485ENT word the bracket as a 120 Vac electronic power system (`spirit-xt-errors-only-reaches-10-kph-220-volt-50-hz`); the owner's manuals print 7 mph and 110 volt (`ct900ent-speed-display-mismatch-caps-at-7mph`).
