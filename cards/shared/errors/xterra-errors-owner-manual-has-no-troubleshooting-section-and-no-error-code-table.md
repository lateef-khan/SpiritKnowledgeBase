Twenty of the Xterra bike, elliptical and climber owner's manuals contain **no troubleshooting section and no
error-code table**. Their tables of contents run from Product Registration through the console and programs
to the warranty with no Troubleshooting entry, and no page of any of them prints a code of the form E plus a
digit. Checked three ways for each book: the contents page, a loose-word search of the whole text layer and
every OCR supplement (`trouble`, `problem`, `error`, `err`, `fault`, `blank`, and `E` followed by a digit,
with letter-spacing allowed), and a read of the console and maintenance pages - the scanned books among
them (SB4.5r, FS5.8e, FS5.9e, and the 2014 SB500 and 2017 RSX1500 printings) are OCR throughout.

What each book prints instead, and where the answer lives:

| Book | What it has | Card |
|---|---|---|
| FB150, FB350, FB160, FB360, FB180 | one NOTE: display faint or no figures - replace the batteries | `xterra-fb-errors-display-faint-or-no-figures-replace-the-batteries` |
| EU100 | Remark 4: illegible or partial segments - remove the batteries for 15 seconds | `eu100-2018-errors-display-illegible-or-partial-segments-remove-batteries-15-seconds` |
| EU150 | nothing, although its console is of the SB150 / SB250 / FS150 family whose books print an E1-E3 table | `xterra-errors-e1-monitor-cannot-read-the-gear-motor-signal` describes that console family |
| SB600 | no page, but `ER` on the Recovery screen; the service manual has a four-condition troubleshooting chapter | `sb600-2023-errors-er-on-the-recovery-screen-heart-rate-undetected`, `sb600-2023-errors-screen-remains-blank-console-cable-then-multimeter` |
| AIR650 | nothing; the manufacturer's service note sends you to the Spirit AB900 service manual | `air650-2021-errors-console-without-display-check-connections-power-outputs-then-four-aa-batteries` |
| SB2.5r, SB4500, SB500, SB600, FS2.5, FS3.5, FS5.9e, AIR650 | an *Erratic Operation* paragraph for the chest strap (interference sources) - a console-section matter | - |
| SB4.5r, SB500, SB4500, FS3.0, FS5.9e, RSX1500 | *General Maintenance*: a squeak, thump or clicking is loose hardware or dirty rails - a maintenance matter; FS5.9e adds an incline calibration under Engineering Mode | - |
| FS1.5, FS5.8e | nothing at all | - |

The FS5.8e scan ends at its Rate of Perceived Exertion page: the General Maintenance and warranty pages its
contents list at printed 20-21 are not in the file, so nothing can be said about what they print.

The books that **do** print a troubleshooting page are the AIR350, SB120 and UB120 (one row), the MB500,
MB550 and MBX2500 (No Display / Cadence), and the SB150, SB250, FS150 and SB240 (the E1-E3 family).
