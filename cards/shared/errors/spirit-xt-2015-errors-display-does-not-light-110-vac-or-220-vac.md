Five checks, in the order the service checklist prints them.

1. Tether cord not in position.
2. Circuit breaker on front grill tripped. Push circuit breaker in until it locks.
3. Plug is disconnected. Make sure plug is firmly pushed into **110 VAC** wall outlet. **(for 220V model is 220 VAC)**
4. Breaker panel circuit breaker may be tripped.
5. Treadmill defect. Contact your dealer.

**Only the XT285 2015 service manual prints this checklist; the XT185 2015 service manual has no checklist at all**, and the XT185 is on this card because the row is otherwise word for word the XT285's - both books are one document apart from the checklist, the folding page and a lubrication page. The 2015 XT385 and XT485 turn the figures round (`spirit-xt-errors-display-does-not-light-220-vac-or-110-vac`); the XT owner's manuals print 115 VAC (`ct900ent-display-does-not-light`).
