**XS895 - 9-6.** When the unit has noise problem, please check the followings and refer to replaced steps accordingly: 1. Bearings on joint areas. 2. Flywheel. 3. Slide wheels. 4. Pedals.

**CS800 2016 - 11-1.** The product is QC inspected in the plant before delivery. Possible locations of noises may include bearings, Flywheel, and Slide Wheel on the rear-side. Check if **corrugated washer** is installed; check for damaged bearing or even Pedal, etc.

**Four places, and each has its own page.** The list is an index, not a fix: a noisy flywheel goes to the flywheel page (`spirit-stepper-errors-noise-from-the-flywheel-friction-foreign-objects-then-replace`), a noisy pedal arm or connecting arm to its bearings (`spirit-xs895-errors-noise-from-the-pedal-arm-or-connect-arm-bearings` on the XS895, `spirit-cs800-errors-vibration-at-the-pedals-slide-wheel-bearing-and-a-broken-pedal` on the CS800), and the remedy in every case is a bearing or a part, not a lubricant - neither book names one.

**The corrugated washer is the check that costs nothing.** The 2016 book asks whether the wave washer at the joint is present at all; a joint assembled without it has end-float and knocks. It is the one cause on the list that is an assembly fault rather than wear.

**Loose fasteners are a different page.** Both books treat shaking and wobble as loose screws and the levelling feet, under their slip and shaking sections, which are care rather than diagnosis. Sole prints this list for its SC200 steppers (`sc200-2016-noises`, `sc200-2019-noises`).
