The TR75H and TR95H service manuals open their error chapter with: **The console does not light up.** Cause: the console has no power supply.

Troubleshooting: (a) check whether the AC power is supplied to the driver board; (b) check whether the upper and lower controller connection cable is connected, or damaged; (c) console or driver board failure - replace.

The owner's manuals for these two machines print no *Display does not light* row (their Troubleshooting page is the three codes and a smell note), so this is the only no-power guidance for them. The Dyaco-built Xterra treadmills' nine-cause no-power row is `xterra-treadmill-errors-power-switch-not-lit-nine-causes`.
