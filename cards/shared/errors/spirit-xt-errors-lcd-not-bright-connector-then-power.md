| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Connector fall off | Check connector again |
| LCD light is broken | Replace with new LCD or console |
| Power to console too low | Check power to console. Replace lower controller. |

The condition is printed as `LCD not bright, incomplete, or imperfect` in the XT books and `TFT LCD not bright, incomplete, or imperfect` in the CT1000ENT 2023, whose console is a touch screen. The 2023 XT185 to XT685 books number the first two causes `1.` and `1.`; the 2015 XT385 and XT485 and the CT1000ENT number them 1, 2, 3. **No supply voltage is printed** - the row says only *check power to console* - where every CT book and the 2015 XT185 and XT285 print a figure.

Dead segments rather than a dim light are the next row down, `spirit-lcd-displays-dim-or-incomplete`. The other printings of this row: 110V or 230V on the 2015 XT185 and XT285 (`spirit-xt-2015-errors-lcd-not-bright-110-v-or-230-v`), 110-120V on the CT800 2016 and CT850 2016 (`spirit-lcd-dim-or-incomplete`), 120V on the CT800 2020 family (`ct850-2020-led-dim-or-incomplete`, `spirit-2024-errors-tft-not-bright-incomplete-or-imperfect`).
