**This is the stencil-era owner's-manual E3 (incline error), not the 2026 E3 and not Spirit's E3.**

The six owner's manuals print the same ERROR MESSAGES table with OK/NG checkboxes. The F80-2023, F85-2023 and F89-2023 prints read 'E1_'..'E7_' on the OCR layer — the render shows a plain code beside the checkbox column.

| ERROR | MEANING | POSSIBLE CAUSE |
|---|---|---|
| Console showing E3 | Incline Error | 1. Is the cable between incline motor and controller connected correctly and properly? 2. Check if incline mechanism being stock [sic] or defective? 3. Is VR connected properly or with inter-mittance? |

The book prints 'stock' where it means stuck (render and OCR agree). The service manuals' deeper E3 procedure (VR voltage range) is a separate card: see `f65-2023-e3-incline-vr-voltage`.
