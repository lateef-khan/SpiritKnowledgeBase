The condition is printed as `TFT not bright, incomplete or imperfect`.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| TFT light is broken | Replace with new TFT or console |
| Power to console too low | Check AC power is 120V. Check power to console. Replace lower controller. |

**This row asks for 120V and starts at the panel**, which puts it with the CT850 2020's LED version
rather than with the ENT bike and elliptical version. Four Spirit answers to the same symptom, and
the figure is different in each: the CT850 2016 and CVC800 ask for `110-120V`
(`spirit-lcd-dim-or-incomplete`), the CT850 2020 for `120V` (`ct850-2020-led-dim-or-incomplete`),
the CE800ENT and the 2024 ENT bikes and ellipticals for `120VAC` but starting at the power cord
(`ce800ent-tft-touch-panel-not-bright`), and the CU900ENT for no figure at all
(`cu900ent-tft-not-bright`).

Dead segments rather than a dim backlight are the next row down:
`cu900ent-tft-displays-not-bright`.
