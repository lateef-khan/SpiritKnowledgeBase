**Read this first if the machine is a CU900ENT.** The CU900ENT *owner's* manual prints no codes,
but the CU900ENT **service** manual carries a full eight-row code table plus `EEPROM ERR` -
`cu900ent-error-code-messages-list`. The absence below is about the owner's manual only.

**Twenty-six Spirit bike owner's manuals print no error code, no fault code, no
condition/reason/solve matrix and no troubleshooting chapter.** Seventeen of them do print one
fault page - `ERRATIC OPERATION`, the heart rate interference list - and that is the whole of
their fault content; see the end of this card. Across the ten commercial uprights and
recumbents the word *error* appears twice in total, both times in the same sentence about FTP
settings on the two ENT touchscreen books, and the word *troubleshooting* appears in that same
sentence and nowhere else. Across the five indoor cycles and air bikes added later the word
*error* appears **once**, on the JB950's LIMIT SENSOR page, and *troubleshooting* not at all.
Across the eleven residential XB manuals added on 2026-09-10 the words *error*, *fault* and
*troubleshooting* appear **not once**; the only hit for any of the four is *diagnostic*, in the
sentence "The console has built in maintenance/diagnostic software" that opens the Engineering
Mode Menu, and the two ENT touchscreen books do not print even that. None of the twenty-six
tables of contents lists a troubleshooting or error-code section.

**Three residential bikes are *not* in that list and do print a troubleshooting chapter** - the
**XBR25 2023, XBR55 2023 and XBU55 2023**. It is three rows long, it holds no code, and it is
carded at `spirit-residential-bike-errors-2023-troubleshooting-chapter-three-rows`.

**The XBR95 2023 is the exception inside its own generation.** It was printed in the same
August 2023 family as the XBR55 2023 and shares 91.3% of its words with it, yet its table of
contents runs Maintenance & Care 34, Engineering Mode 35, Exploded View 36, Parts List 37,
Warranty 41 and stops. Printed pages 44 and 45 are blank and page 46 is the back cover. There is
no troubleshooting page in it, checked page by page against the PDF rather than by offset. Do not
quote the XBR55 2023 troubleshooting table to an XBR95 2023 owner as if it were in their book -
the advice is the same, but the page is not there.

**Three Spirit indoor cycles are *not* in that list and do print a troubleshooting page** - the
XIC600 2018, the XIC600 2021 and the CIC850 2022. Four or five symptoms each, no codes:
`spirit-indoor-cycle-errors-no-display-on-console`,
`spirit-indoor-cycle-errors-no-pulse-shown`,
`spirit-indoor-cycle-errors-pulse-cuts-out-36-inches`,
`spirit-indoor-cycle-errors-cadence-jumps-console-code` and, on the CIC850 only,
`spirit-cic850-errors-rpm-or-hr-value-will-not-change`. Those three consoles also print a
pairing-failure string - `ID ER` on the XIC600, `Err` on the CIC850.

This is a checked absence, not an unread page. Every page of every one of the ten was counted for
words, and each page under 25 words was rendered at 200 dpi and read with OCR, because Spirit prints
its troubleshooting tables as flat pictures. The low-text pages turned out to be section dividers,
exploded views, VO2max charts and NOTES pages - no hidden matrix among them.

The five added on 2026-09-09 were checked harder still: **every page of all five was rendered at
200 dpi and the OCR word count compared against the native extraction**, rather than trusting a
word-count threshold. Only three pages came back richer than the text layer - CIC800 page 24 and
AB900 pages 13 and 14 - and all three are parts lists and assembly art, already appended to the
sources as OCR supplements. No hidden troubleshooting table exists in any of the five.

**Three commercial bike owner's manuals do print fault content**, and they are the ones to reach for
when the caller's model is one of them:

- **CR900 2025 and CU900 2025** - a six-row symptom table and one code, `EEPROM ERROR`, at
  `ce900-2025-errors-eeprom-error-replace-upper-controller`.
- **CU800ENT 2024** - a seven-row symptom table and one code, `E5`, at
  `ce800ent-e5-console-controller-communication`.

**A code a caller reports on one of the ten did not come from their manual.** Establish what is
actually on the screen before answering, and do not serve a Spirit treadmill, elliptical or
climber code as the answer - those families use different codes with different fixes.

What these ten manuals print instead of a troubleshooting chapter:

- **An `ERRATIC OPERATION` page**, on six of the ten - CR800 2021, CR800 2023, CR800ENT 2023,
  CU800 2012, CU800 2021 and CU800ENT 2022. It is a seven- or eight-item list of interference
  sources for a wild heart rate reading, and it is the only symptom-and-cause content any of
  the ten carries: `spirit-ce-errors-erratic-heart-rate-interference`. The other four - CR900
  2018, CU900 2018, CR900ENT 2021, CU900ENT 2021 - print no fault page of any kind.

- **Key Test and Display Test** inside the Engineering Mode Menu (called Maintenance Mode Menu in
  the 2018 books), the nearest thing these consoles offer to a self-diagnostic. Six of the ten carry
  it: CR800 2021, CR800 2023, CR900 2018, CU800 2012, CU800 2021 and CU900 2018. The CR800 and
  CU800 2021 books add a PWM/brake test, a Csafe test and a speed sensor test. Carded under
  `section: console`.
- **On the CR900ENT and CU900ENT**, a Maintenance suite reached by pressing the `Home` icon ten
  times, and one line saying the Spirit FTP site is where error logs and remote troubleshooting are
  managed. Carded under `section: console`.
- **The CR800ENT 2023 and CU800ENT 2022 books document no service or engineering mode at all.**
  Their tables of contents run Console Operation, Touch Screen Operation, Entering Programs,
  Program Display, General Maintenance, Warranty, Exploded View, Parts List and nothing else. Those
  two are the emptiest of the ten for a technician.
- **A squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling** under general maintenance - loose assembly hardware
  and the crank arm nut. Carded under `section: maintenance`.

What the five indoor cycles and air bikes print instead:

- **The CB900 2013 and the CIC800 2021 have no console at all.** Neither manual uses the words
  console, computer or monitor once. Resistance is a tension knob, the brake is the same knob
  pressed down, and there is nothing on the machine that could display a code. For these two the
  answer to "what does the code mean" is that the bike cannot show one.
- **The AB900 2018 and AB950 2024 air bikes** carry an `ENGINEERING MODE` of three settings -
  metric/imperial, audio on/off and the odometer. No key test, no display test, no fault
  readout. They are fan-resistance machines with no resistance motor to fault. Carded under
  `section: console`.
- **The JB950 2022** has the richest service content of any bike in the repository - a fifteen
  page `MAINTENANCE MODE` (printed pages 51-65) with a key test, a display test, a brake and
  motor test, an RPM sensor test and a software updater. Two of its screens are fault work and
  are carded here: `spirit-jb950-errors-limit-sensor-test-after-motor-error`, which the manual
  says is run *only when a motor error has occurred*, and
  `spirit-jb950-errors-update-fail-or-search-update`. The rest is `section: console`. Note also
  that the JB950 has its own website, `www.johnnygspiritbike.com`, printed in place of
  `www.spiritfitness.com` on every page footer.
- **`TESTING THE BIKE`**, a post-assembly checklist on the AB900 and the JB950. It is an
  assembly sign-off, not a diagnostic. Carded under `section: assembly`.

What the eleven residential XB manuals print instead:

- **The `Erratic Operation` heart rate interference list**, on all eleven, and it is their only
  symptom-and-cause page: `spirit-ce-errors-erratic-heart-rate-interference`. Three of them -
  XBR55 2019, XBR55 2021 and XBR55ENT 2021 - open the list with **`Treadmill not properly
  grounded`** on a machine that is a recumbent bike; that is a copy-and-paste defect in the
  source, not a second machine.
- **An `Engineering Mode Menu`** on the nine LCD-console books, entered by holding Start, Stop
  and Enter for about five seconds. Key Test, an LCD Test the XBR95 2023 renames Display Test,
  and a Functions submenu; the eight older books close with Security, the XBR95 2023 with Exit.
  The component test inside Functions is **not the same on every model**: the XBR95 2016, 2018
  and 2021 books call it `DA Test (Tests the brake resistance)`, the XBR25 2019, XBR55 2019,
  XBR55 2021, XBU55 2019 and XBU55 2021 books call it `Motor Test`, and the XBR95 2023 calls it
  `PWM test (Test the Brake resistance)`. It is the nearest thing these consoles offer to a
  self-diagnostic and it returns no code, only a live reading. Carded under `section: console`.
- **The XBR55ENT 2021 and XBU55ENT 2021 touchscreen books document no service or engineering
  mode at all**, exactly as the CR800ENT 2023 and CU800ENT 2022 do. The words *error*, *fault*,
  *troubleshooting* and *diagnostic* are absent from both books entirely. Those two are the
  emptiest of the eleven for a technician.
- **A squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling** under `GENERAL MAINTENANCE` - loose assembly
  hardware, the crank arm nut, and two levelling pads on the rear stabiliser adjusted with a
  14 mm wrench. Carded under `section: maintenance`.

The eleven were checked the same way as the five added on 2026-09-09: **every page of all eleven
was rendered at 300 dpi and OCRed, and the OCR word count compared against the native
extraction**, rather than trusting a word-count threshold. 612 pages across the fourteen
residential bike manuals were swept. The only pages that came back richer than the text layer
were console screenshots, program-description pages and exploded views - eight of them on the
XBR55ENT and seven on the XBU55ENT. The strings `TROUBLESHOOTING` and `Problem Solution/Cause`
appear in the renders of exactly three manuals, and all three are the 2023 books already named
above. No hidden fault table exists in any of the eleven.

The Spirit residential ellipticals have the same gap, described at
`spirit-xe-errors-no-error-codes-printed`.

**The service manuals are a different matter.** This card is about the *owner's* manuals. On 2026-09-11 the service manuals of most of the machines above were carded, and they print what the owner's manuals do not:

- **`EEPROM ERR`, replace the upper controller** - CU900 2018, CR900 2018, XBR95 2016: `spirit-bike-errors-eeprom-err-replace-upper-controller`. **`EEPROM ERROR`, replace the upper controller** - CU800 2012, CU800 2021, CR800 2021: `ce900-2025-errors-eeprom-error-replace-upper-controller`. **`EEPROM ERR` with no fix** - CR900ENT 2021: `cu900ent-eeprom-err`.
- **The `40H`-`50H` / `EAH` / `ECH` / `EDH` list** on the CR900ENT 2021 as well as the CU900ENT: `cu900ent-error-code-messages-list`. **`E5`** on the CU800ENT 2022 and CR800ENT 2023: `ce800ent-e5-console-controller-communication`. **`E2`** on the XBR55ENT 2021 and XBU55ENT 2021: `spirit-xb-errors-e2-motor-does-not-move-on-level-key`.
- **Four worded messages on the JB950 2022** - `USING DEFAULT SETUP`, `HR SENSOR ERROR`, `MOTOR ERROR`, `ENCODER ERROR`: `jb950-2022-errors-error-message-table-four-messages`.
- **A Condition / Reason / Solve matrix in every one of those service manuals** (`spirit-erratic-pulse-display`, `spirit-hand-pulse-not-working`, `spirit-wireless-chest-belt-no-pulse` and the display rows they link to), a four-row lookup table on the AB900 2018 (`ab900-2018-errors-lcd-does-not-shine-check-1-5-v-batteries`), and Q&A chapters on the XBR, XBU, CR800 and CU800 books (`spirit-bike-errors-display-wont-come-on-check-computer-cable-then-meter` and its neighbours).

**Still true of the service manuals**: the AB900 2018 prints no code, the CR800 2009 (XR898) and XBR25/XBR55 2007 books print no code, and the XBR95 2023 owner's manual has no troubleshooting page while its service manual has a three-row matrix and a Q&A.
