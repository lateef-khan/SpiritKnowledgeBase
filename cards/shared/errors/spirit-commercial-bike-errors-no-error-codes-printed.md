**Read this first if the machine is a CU900ENT.** The CU900ENT *owner's* manual prints no codes,
but the CU900ENT **service** manual carries a full eight-row code table plus `EEPROM ERR` -
`cu900ent-error-code-messages-list`. The absence below is about the owner's manual only.

**Ten Spirit commercial bike owner's manuals print no error code, no fault code, no
condition/reason/solve matrix and no troubleshooting chapter.** Six of the ten do print one
fault page - `ERRATIC OPERATION`, the heart rate interference list - and that is the whole of
their fault content; see the end of this card. Across all ten the word *error*
appears twice in total, both times in the same sentence about FTP settings on the two ENT
touchscreen books, and the word *troubleshooting* appears in that same sentence and nowhere else.
None of the ten tables of contents lists a troubleshooting or error-code section.

| Machine | Manual |
|---|---|
| CR800 | 2021, 2023 |
| CR800ENT | 2023 |
| CR900 | 2018 |
| CR900ENT | 2021 |
| CU800 | 2012, 2021 |
| CU800ENT | 2022 |
| CU900 | 2018 |
| CU900ENT | 2021 |

This is a checked absence, not an unread page. Every page of every one of the ten was counted for
words, and each page under 25 words was rendered at 200 dpi and read with OCR, because Spirit prints
its troubleshooting tables as flat pictures. The low-text pages turned out to be section dividers,
exploded views, VO2max charts and NOTES pages - no hidden matrix among them.

**Three commercial bike owner's manuals do print fault content**, and they are the ones to reach for
when the caller's model is one of them:

- **CR900 2025 and CU900 2025** - a six-row symptom table and one code, `EEPROM ERROR`, at
  `ce900-2025-errors-eeprom-error-replace-upper-controller`.
- **CU800ENT 2024** - a seven-row symptom table and one code, `E5`, at
  `ce800ent-e5-console-controller-communication`.

**A code a caller reports on one of the ten did not come from their manual.** Establish what is
actually on the screen before answering, and do not serve a Spirit treadmill, elliptical or
climber code as the answer - those families use different codes with different fixes.

What these ten manuals print instead of a troubleshooting chapter:

- **An `ERRATIC OPERATION` page**, on six of the ten - CR800 2021, CR800 2023, CR800ENT 2023,
  CU800 2012, CU800 2021 and CU800ENT 2022. It is a seven- or eight-item list of interference
  sources for a wild heart rate reading, and it is the only symptom-and-cause content any of
  the ten carries: `spirit-ce-errors-erratic-heart-rate-interference`. The other four - CR900
  2018, CU900 2018, CR900ENT 2021, CU900ENT 2021 - print no fault page of any kind.

- **Key Test and Display Test** inside the Engineering Mode Menu (called Maintenance Mode Menu in
  the 2018 books), the nearest thing these consoles offer to a self-diagnostic. Six of the ten carry
  it: CR800 2021, CR800 2023, CR900 2018, CU800 2012, CU800 2021 and CU900 2018. The CR800 and
  CU800 2021 books add a PWM/brake test, a Csafe test and a speed sensor test. Carded under
  `section: console`.
- **On the CR900ENT and CU900ENT**, a Maintenance suite reached by pressing the `Home` icon ten
  times, and one line saying the Spirit FTP site is where error logs and remote troubleshooting are
  managed. Carded under `section: console`.
- **The CR800ENT 2023 and CU800ENT 2022 books document no service or engineering mode at all.**
  Their tables of contents run Console Operation, Touch Screen Operation, Entering Programs,
  Program Display, General Maintenance, Warranty, Exploded View, Parts List and nothing else. Those
  two are the emptiest of the ten for a technician.
- **A squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling** under general maintenance - loose assembly hardware
  and the crank arm nut. Carded under `section: maintenance`.

The Spirit residential ellipticals have the same gap, described at
`spirit-xe-errors-no-error-codes-printed`.
