**This is the TR260, TRX2500, TRX3500 and TRX4500 row**; the TR150 and TRX1400 books print a three-item keys-or-controller version (`xterra-treadmill-errors-speed-buttons-not-working-keys-stuck-or-controller`).

*Condition:* FAST/SLOW button of SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH can't be used. Speed button can only press FAST, can't press SLOW. Speed button can only press SLOW, can't press FAST.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1. The connector of SPEED CABLE (TRX books: "(UPPER)") and CONSOLE not connected properly | 1. Connect cables again |
| 2. The connector of SPEED CABLE and SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/CABLE not connected properly | 2. Connect cables again |
| 3. The connector of SPEED CABLE or SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/CABLE is damaged | 3. Connect cable again |
| 4. Button of SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH is broken | 4. Replace with new buttons |
| 5. The connector of SPEED CABLE or SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/CABLE is damaged | 5. Replace with new cable |
| 6. The connector of SPEED CABLE or SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/CABLE is damaged | 6. Replace with new cable |

Items 3, 5 and 6 are printed with the same wording and three different remedies. The incline buttons have the same six-item row (`xterra-treadmill-errors-incline-buttons-not-working-incline-cable-six-checks`).
