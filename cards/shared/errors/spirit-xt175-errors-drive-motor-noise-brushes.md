The Mechanical System row `Drive motor noise`, in the order printed:

1. Check the wires around the motor for rubbing.
2. **Remove, clean and re-insert the motor brushes.**
3. Replace the motor.

Printed identically in the 2008 XT dealer manual and the CT800 2016 service manual's *Treadmill Troubleshooting* chapter. This is the only Spirit treadmill page that says a brush can be cleaned and refitted rather than replaced; the later matrices say *replace with new motor brushes* or *replace with new motor* (`spirit-xt-errors-noise-under-motor-cover-checklist-motor-brushes`, `spirit-2024-errors-noise-under-the-motor-cover`).
