**This is E04 on the WS200 and WS300 walking treadmills**, an overload attributed to the controller or the motor. It is not the Dyaco E4 motor-wiring code (`xterra-treadmill-errors-e4-motor-power-wires-not-in-lower-controller`); on the Sole 2026 treadmills E04 is a phase loss (`f63-2026-e04-error-code`).

The owner's manuals print one row for it, in a table that is a flat picture read from the render:

| Questions | Possible cause | Method |
|---|---|---|
| E04 | Controller or motor abnormal | Overload, replace controller or motor |

That is the whole printed entry. The seven-code table it comes from is `xterra-ws-errors-e01-to-e07-error-table`; the no-code "treadmill does not work" rows are `xterra-ws-errors-treadmill-does-not-work-five-causes`.
