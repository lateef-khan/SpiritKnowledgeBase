The troubleshooting procedure matrix prints this row in all five service manuals:

*Condition:* Erratic pulse display.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1. Another chest belt in use around treadmill | 1. Check for other chest belt use around treadmill |
| 2. Other magnetic field disturbance | 2. Change the position or direction of treadmill |
| 3. Receiver is broken | 3. Replace with new receiver |

No pulse at all, rather than an erratic one, has two rows of its own: `xterra-treadmill-errors-hand-pulse-not-working-two-hands-cable-board` and `xterra-treadmill-errors-wireless-pulse-not-working-chest-belt-3-feet-cr2032`. How the console reads a pulse and pairs a strap is a console card.
