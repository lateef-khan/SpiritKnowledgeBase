**This is E02 on the WS200 and WS300 walking treadmills**, a failed power device on the controller. It is not the Dyaco E2 over-current code (`xterra-treadmill-errors-e2-over-current-silicone-oil-then-board-or-motor`), and on the Sole 2026 treadmills E02 is a hall-sensor fault (`f63-2026-e02-error-code`).

The owner's manuals print one row for it, in a table that is a flat picture read from the render:

| Questions | Possible cause | Method |
|---|---|---|
| E02 | Power assault | Power tube breakdown, replace the electronic controller |

That is the whole printed entry. The seven-code table it comes from is `xterra-ws-errors-e01-to-e07-error-table`; the no-code "treadmill does not work" rows are `xterra-ws-errors-treadmill-does-not-work-five-causes`.
