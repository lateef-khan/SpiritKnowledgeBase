The troubleshooting procedure matrix prints this row in all five service manuals:

*Condition:* Treadmill stops or shuts off by itself.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1. House breaker tripped | 1. Reset it |
| 2. Treadmill breaker tripped (TRX books: "chopper") | 2. Reset treadmill breaker |
| 3. Treadmill controller fuse is broken | 3. Replace with new fuse |
| 4. Treadmill controller shut down (TR260: "and TFT would be ON"; TRX books: "and LED would be ON") | 4. Turn off the AC switch and turn on power again |

The treadmill breaker is reset by its small red button (`xterra-treadmill-errors-power-switch-not-lit-nine-causes`). A house breaker that trips while the treadmill's own does not is an owner's-manual row (`xterra-treadmill-errors-house-breaker-trips-but-not-treadmill-breaker`); a shut-down with an E2 on the display is over current (`xterra-treadmill-errors-e2-over-current-silicone-oil-then-board-or-motor`).
