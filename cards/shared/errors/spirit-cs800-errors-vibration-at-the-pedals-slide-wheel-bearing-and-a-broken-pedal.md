Both books put two situations under the connecting arm.

**A vibration from the feet.**
- CS800 (2020): *There is a vibration feeling from feet when pedaling.* **Solve:** It's maybe the bearing of Slide wheel is defective require replacement.
- CS800 2016: *If Slide Wheel occurs texture should be bearing damage, only need to replace new bearing.*

**A broken part.**
- CS800 (2020): *The broken pedal.* **Solve:** To do the pedal replacement.
- CS800 2016: *If connecting arm had problem, only need to change to new connecting arm.*

**The slide wheel runs the connecting arm along the rail tube, and its bearing is what you feel.** A rough or pulsing feel under the feet on every stroke - the 2016 book's word is *texture* - is that bearing, not the pedal and not the pivot. The slide wheel comes off the connecting arm with a circlip (17 mm) in the connecting arm replacement steps of the same book, and only the bearing needs to be new.

**Neither book prints a test.** There is no play figure, no noise description and no interval; a vibration that was not there before is the whole diagnosis. Play at the arm's *pivot* is a different wear part - the bushing (`spirit-cs800-errors-clearance-between-bushing-and-axle-replace-the-bushing`).

The XS895 gives the same slide-wheel bearing as the second cause of a noisy pedal arm (`spirit-xs895-errors-noise-from-the-pedal-arm-or-connect-arm-bearings`). Sole prints this page for its SC200 steppers (`sc200-2016-connecting-arm-problem`, `sc200-2019-connecting-arm-problem`).
