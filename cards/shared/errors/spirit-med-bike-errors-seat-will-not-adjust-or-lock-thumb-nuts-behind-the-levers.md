The owner's manual, word for word:

> **Cannot adjust seat fore/aft or back angle, or seat adjustments will not lock in place**
> Adjust the thumb nuts located to the rear of the seat adjustment levers. If the cables attached to the levers stretch it is possible the latch for the seat will not disengage, or engage, properly. Adjusting the thumb nut can remedy this.

**One adjuster covers both failures.** A stretched cable can leave the latch unable to let go - the seat will not move - or unable to catch - the seat will not lock. Both are taken up at the thumb nuts behind the seat adjustment levers, and no other part is named. **No turn count and no travel figure is printed.**

**The 7.0U upright has no such row** - its seat goes up and down on a post with a knob, and its owner's manual drops this entry. The 7.0R 2025 and the Dyaco MED 7.0R 2021 print it identically. The service manual side of the same cables is the replacement chapter (6.6 seat steel cable, 6.7 seat rotation cable), carded under `section: assembly`.

The recumbent steppers print the same paragraph word for word (`spirit-med-stepper-errors-seat-will-not-lock-thumb-nuts`).
