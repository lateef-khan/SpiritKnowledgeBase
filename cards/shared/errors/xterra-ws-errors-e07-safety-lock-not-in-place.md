**This is E07 on the WS200 and WS300 walking treadmills**, the missing safety key. On the Dyaco-built Xterra treadmills that is E0 (`xterra-treadmill-errors-e0-safety-switch-malfunction-owner-checks`); the Dyaco E7 is an input-power fault (`xterra-treadmill-errors-e7-input-power-too-low-too-high-or-unstable`).

The owner's manuals print one row for it, in a table that is a flat picture read from the render:

| Questions | Possible cause | Method |
|---|---|---|
| E07 | Safety lock is not in place | Put the safety key in correct position |

That is the whole printed entry. The seven-code table it comes from is `xterra-ws-errors-e01-to-e07-error-table`; the no-code "treadmill does not work" rows are `xterra-ws-errors-treadmill-does-not-work-five-causes`.
