The condition is printed as `LEDs not bright, incomplete or imperfect`.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| LED light is broken | Replace with new LED or console |
| Generator Power to console too low | Check generator power connection. Replace controller. |

**These three machines have no mains supply to the console**, so the second cause names the
**generator**, not the wall, and the fix is a connection check rather than a voltage. No voltage
figure is printed anywhere in the row - do not borrow one from the mains-powered Spirit manuals that
answer the same symptom.

The other Spirit answers to this symptom, and the figure each one prints: `110-120V` on the CT850
2016, CVC800, CE850 2024 and CRS800S 2024 (`spirit-lcd-dim-or-incomplete`), `120V` on the CT850 2020
(`ct850-2020-led-dim-or-incomplete`), `120V` on the two 2024 ENT treadmills
(`spirit-2024-errors-tft-not-bright-incomplete-or-imperfect`), `120VAC` on the ENT bikes and
ellipticals (`ce800ent-tft-touch-panel-not-bright`), `220-240V or 110-120V` on the CS800 2024
stepper (`cs800-2024-errors-lcd-not-bright-220-240-or-110-120-volt`), none at all on the CE900
2025, CR900 2025 and CU900 2025
(`ce900-2025-errors-leds-not-bright-incomplete-or-imperfect`), and **pedal above 25 RPM** on the
CES880 2025 (`ces880-2025-errors-lcd-not-bright-incomplete-or-imperfect`), which is generator-powered
like these three.

Dead segments rather than a dim light are the next row down. The CE800 2024 prints it about an LED
(`ct850-2020-led-displays-dim-or-incomplete`); the CR800 2024 and CU800 2024 print it about an LCD
(`spirit-lcd-displays-dim-or-incomplete`).
