Nine causes, in the order the manual lists them. The 2016 and 2020 manuals print this row identically.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Power cord isn't plugged into outlet | Plug the power cord into outlet |
| Power cord isn't plug into unit | Plug the power cord into unit |
| The voltage of outlet is too low | Check the voltage of outlet |
| Plug or connector of power cord is open | Replace power cord |
| Connector of power cord is broken | Replace power cord |
| Connecting cable disconnected | Check if wire is disconnected, connect it again |
| Breaker tripped | Press the small red button to return to original status |
| Breaker is broken | Replace breaker |
| ON/OFF switch is broken | Replace AC switch |

A popping sound at power on is a different row, and the two manuals print **different supply voltages** for it: see `ct850-2016-popping-sound-at-power-on` and `ct850-2020-popping-sound-at-power-on`.
