Section 7.2, `Noise and/or rough feeling at the pedals`, printed in the same words in both books (the paragraph is numbered 7-2-2; there is no 7-2-1):

> If there is a clicking sound, or feeling that something is loose, **first check that the pedals are tight. Then check that the crank arm bolts are tight. Check for play in the axle. It is also possible that the bearing assembly nuts are loose, check those.**

Four places, in that order: pedals, crank arm bolts, axle play, bearing assembly nuts. The pedals tighten opposite ways - the preventative-maintenance page of the same book says the left tightens counter-clockwise and the right clockwise, and asks for a weekly check.

**The CU900 2018 upright answers the same heading differently** - it says the flywheel sound when pedalling backwards is normal, and to lock the pedal then check the crank - and that answer is already carded from the identical CU900ENT book at `cu900ent-noise-and-feet-feeling`. Handlebars, seat or the whole bike shaking is the next section: `spirit-cr900-errors-handlebar-seat-or-whole-bike-shaking`. A belt that slips under load is `spirit-cr900-maintenance-belt-slip-jam-nut-220-to-240-newtons`.

The general-maintenance page of both books puts the same diagnosis in one sentence for a customer - *90% of calls to the service department for noise issues can be traced to loose hardware* - and that page is a maintenance card.
