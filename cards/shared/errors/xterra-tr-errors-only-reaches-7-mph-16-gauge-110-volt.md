The *Service Checklist* row reads, for these books:

*Treadmill will only achieve approximately 7 mph but shows higher speed on display.* This indicates the motor should be receiving power to operate. **Low AC voltage** to the treadmill. Do not use an extension cord. If an extension cord is required it should be as short as possible and heavy duty, **16 gauge minimum**. Low voltage. Contact an electrician or XTERRA Fitness. A minimum of **110 volt AC current** is required.

The TR300 book's copy of the row is damaged: it reads "Low voltage. vvA minimum of 110 volt AC current is required." - the sentence "Contact an electrician or XTERRA Fitness" that the TR6.6 and TR6.4 books print at that point is missing and two stray letters stand in its place.

The three figures - the speed the treadmill reaches, the cord gauge and the minimum voltage - differ between books: 7 mph / 16 gauge / 110 V (TR300, TR6.6, TR6.4), 8 mph / 16 gauge / 120 V (TRX1400 owner's manual), 5 mph / 14 gauge / 120 V (TR65, TR75, TR85), 10 kph / 16 AWG / 220 V 50 Hz (TRX1400 service manual). Each set has its own card. A belt speed that is wrong but not limited is a calibration matter (`xterra-treadmill-errors-belt-speed-does-not-match-display-calibrate`); the outlet and circuit the machine needs are safety cards.
