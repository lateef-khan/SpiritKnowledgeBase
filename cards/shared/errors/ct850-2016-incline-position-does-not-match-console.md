One cause and one fix, printed identically in the 2016 and 2020 manuals.

| Condition | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| The incline position doesn't match console | Console is not calibrated | Calibrate the console |

This row is only about the *number being wrong*. If the console prints `INCLINE ERR` instead, that
is a different row: see `ct850-2016-incline-err-shown-in-incline-window`.

The 2016 manual carries a second row of the same shape for speed - `The speed of the belt doesn't
match console display` - that the 2020 manual does not print. It is on
`ct850-2016-speed-does-not-match-console-display`.

**The CT800ENT 2024 and CT850ENT 2024 owner's manuals print this row word for word** in the
Condition/Reason/Solve matrix on printed pages 57 to 59. Both of those pages are flat pictures
with no text layer and were read from the rendered page.

Neither 2024 book prints the speed version of this row.

**Sixteen more service manuals print this one-cause, one-fix row** - the 2015 and 2023 XT185 to XT685, the CT800 2016, CT800 2020, CT800ENT 2022, CT850ENT 2022, CT900ENT, CT1000ENT 2023 and 4.0T. The 2023 XT books and the CT1000ENT word the cause `Controller is not calibrated`; the rest say `Console`. The fix is `Calibrate the console` in every one.
