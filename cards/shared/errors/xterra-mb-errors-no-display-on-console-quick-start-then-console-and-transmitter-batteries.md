*Troubleshooting - No Display on Console*, printed identically in the MB500 and MB550 books:

1. **Hold down any key** to bring the console to "Quick Start" mode.
2. Ensure that the batteries are installed properly in the **console and transmitter**. If they are,
   **install fresh batteries.**

Both books also say it in the console-mounting step of assembly: *Replace console battery if no digits
are shown on LCD*. There are two battery sets to check because the speed sensor transmitter on the frame
sends a coded signal to the console - a dead transmitter cell leaves the console with nothing to show.

**No mains, no power switch, no cord.** The console is a sealed, water-resistant unit that the book says
must not be opened except to install batteries (opening it otherwise voids the warranty), so a blank
screen is only ever the key press or the batteries.

The Spirit XIC600 / CIC850 and the Sole SB700 / SB900 indoor cycles print the same two steps word for
word; those are other brands and separate cards.
