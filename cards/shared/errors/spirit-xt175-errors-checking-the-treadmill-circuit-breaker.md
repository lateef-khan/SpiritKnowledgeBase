Procedure 4 in the 2008 XT dealer manual:

1. The circuit breaker is a small, red button between the power cord and the power switch.
2. Press the button in to reset.

It is step 4 of the no-power row (`spirit-xt175-errors-no-power-to-console-nine-checks`). **The CT800 2016 service manual's copy of the same guide drops this procedure** - its numbered list goes from the harness check to the roller magnet - though its matrix says the same thing in a row: *Breaker tripped - Press the small red button to return to original status* (`ct850-2016-power-switch-not-lit-at-power-on`). The later manuals' checklists call it *the circuit breaker on the front grill* (`ct900ent-display-does-not-light`).
