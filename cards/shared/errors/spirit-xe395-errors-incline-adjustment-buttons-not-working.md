The manual folds three conditions into one block of causes.

- UP/DOWN button of INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH can't be used.
- Incline button just can press UP, can't press DOWN.
- Incline button just can press DOWN, can't press UP.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The connector of INCLINE CABLE and CONSOLE not connected properly | Connect the wires again |
| The connector of INCLINE CABLE and INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/CABLE not connected properly | Connect the wires again |
| The connector of INCLINE CABLE or INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH CABLE got damage | Replace the cable |
| Button of INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH is broken | Replace buttons |
| The connector of INCLINE CABLE or INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH CABLE got damage | Replace the cable |
| The connector of INCLINE CABLE or INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH CABLE damaged | Replace the cable |

**The last three reasons are the same sentence printed three times**, and the manual gives no way to tell them apart. The same defect is in the CE850 books' stride version of this row and in the CT850 treadmill manuals' speed and incline versions, which is where the table was copied from (`ce850-2024-errors-stride-adjustment-buttons-not-working`, `ct850-2016-incline-buttons-not-working`).

**The XE395 2016 and XE395ENT 2021 service manuals print this block word for word.** The buttons are the thumb switches on the swing arms; the same two books also say, in their swing-arm troubleshooting, that when the handrail keys do nothing the first things to check are the key wires and the wire between the upper and lower swing arm (`spirit-xe-2016-errors-handrail-keys-not-working-check-the-swing-arm-wires`).

This row is about the **buttons**. If the incline moves but the number is wrong, or the window shows `Err` / `E3`, see `xe395-2016-errors-incline-err-incline-window-err` and `xe395ent-2021-errors-incline-err-incline-window-e3`. Sole's E25 family prints the same incline row (`e25-2016-incline-buttons-not-working`). Different brand; separate card.
