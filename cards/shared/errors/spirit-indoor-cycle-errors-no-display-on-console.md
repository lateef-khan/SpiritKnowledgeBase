1. **Press and hold any key** to bring the console to "Quick Start" mode.
2. **Check the batteries in *both* the console and the speed transmitter.** If they are
   installed properly, install fresh ones.

**There are two sets of batteries, not one.** The console takes 2 AAA and the speed sensor
transmitter takes its own 2 AAA. A blank console is as often the transmitter's batteries as
its own, and after any battery change the transmitter has to be paired to the console again.

The XIC600 books word step 1 as "Hold down any key"; the CIC850 book words it "Press any
key". Same console family, same fix.

**These consoles have no mains supply to check.** None of these bikes plugs into an outlet -
there is no power switch, no cord and no breaker, so a blank display is always batteries,
never power.

The Sole SB700 and SB900 indoor cycles print this same entry word for word:
`sole-spinner-no-display-battery-consoles`. That is a different brand and a separate card.
