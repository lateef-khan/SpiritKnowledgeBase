**Not one of these five manuals prints an error code.** The words *error*, *fault* and *code* do not
appear in the XRW600 2019, XRW600 2021 or CRW800 2021 books at all, and the two water rowers use
neither. There is nothing of the form `E1`, `E-1` or `ERR` to look up in any of them.

What each book does carry:

| Manual | Troubleshooting section | Error codes |
|---|---|---|
| XRW600 2019 | none | none |
| XRW600 2021 | none | none |
| CRW800 2021 | none | none |
| CRW800H2O 2021 | four rows, printed page 34 | none |
| CRW900 2021 | five rows on printed page 53, a sixth on page 54 | none |

The three air rowers end their contents at General Maintenance and the warranty; the CRW800 2021
adds an exploded view, a parts list and three pages of NOTES after it. **The only fault-shaped
content in those three books is the chest strap "Erratic Operation" interference list** in the heart
rate chapter, which is a list of things near the machine to move, not a fault table.

**The CRW800 2021 is the trap here.** Its successor, the CRW800 2024, *does* print an error code
table - `E1` for a console EEPROM failure and `E2` for a cable tension communication error - plus a
six-row Happening / Caused / Processing Step matrix. **None of that is in the 2021 book.** Same
model name, three years apart, and the whole errors chapter arrives between them. Do not answer a
2021 caller from the 2024 table, and do not read the 2021 manual's silence as evidence that the 2024
codes are wrong.

The rows the water rowers do print are carded separately:
`spirit-water-rower-errors-tank-water-turns-cloudy`,
`spirit-water-rower-errors-inconsistent-split-time-and-stroke-rate`,
`crw900-2021-errors-belt-slips-off-the-bungee-pulley`,
`crw900-2021-errors-vr-setting-for-trouble-with-resistance`,
`crw800h2o-console-shows-no-display` and `crw800h2o-console-shows-no-data`. The CRW800H2O also has a
**service** manual, which is where its console Q&A and its leaking-tank answer come from; the
owner's manual is not the only document for that machine.

The same absence holds across the Spirit bikes (`spirit-commercial-bike-errors-no-error-codes-printed`)
and the climbers and steppers (`spirit-climber-errors-no-error-codes-printed`).

## Read this first: the service manuals do print codes

**This card is about the owner's manuals.** The service manuals for three of these five machines print `E1` and `E2` with a symptom and a remedy - the CRW800 2021 (`800940`), the CRW800 2016 (`CW800-YR001`) and the XRW600 (`DW400-YR002`) all carry `E1` (console EEPROM failure, shown as `RAM ERROR`, replace the console) and `E2` (cable tension communication error, shown as `MOTOR ERROR`, with a voltage test) - `crw800-2024-errors-e1-console-eeprom-failure`, `crw800-2024-errors-e2-cable-tension-communication-error`. So the trap described above cuts the other way as well: **the CRW800 2021 owner's manual is silent, but the CRW800 2021 service manual prints exactly the two codes the 2024 owner's manual prints.** A 2021 owner reporting `E1` or `E2` has a documented code.

The CRW900's service manual prints no code either, but it does add a speed sensor procedure with a gap figure the owner's manual lacks (`crw900-2021-errors-speed-sensor-gap-2-to-3-mm-and-six-magnets`) and the same console Q&A as the owner's manual.
