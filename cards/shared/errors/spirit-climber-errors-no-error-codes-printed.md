**Read this first if the machine is a CVC800.** The CVC800 *owner's* manual prints no codes, but the
CVC800 **service** manual carries `E-1` and `E-2` with a full diagnosis, a voltage test and a
troubleshooting matrix - `cvc800-e-1-ram-error`, `cvc800-e-2-tension-motor-error`,
`cvc800-tension-motor-voltage-test`, `cvc800-screen-does-not-light`. The absence below is about the
owner's manual only.

**Read this first if the machine is an 8.5S 2025.** Its console states the fault and a suggested fix
on screen instead of printing a code list, and the manual says so in a NOTE:
`85s-2025-errors-console-shows-the-fault-and-a-suggested-fix`.

**Nine Spirit climber and stepper owner's manuals print no error code, no fault code, no
condition/reason/solve matrix and no troubleshooting chapter.** Across all nine the word
*troubleshooting* appears **not once**, and the standalone words *fault* and *faulty* appear **not
once** - every hit for those letters is `default`. The word *error* appears **twice in the nine**,
both times in the same NOTE on the 8.5S 2025 about on-screen messages. None of the nine tables of
contents lists a troubleshooting or error-code section.

| Machine | Manual | Type | Contents page ends |
|---|---|---|---|
| XS895 | 2018, 2021 | incline stepper | General Maintenance 34, Warranty 36 |
| CSC900 | 2019 | stair climber | General Maintenance 29, Warranty 30 |
| CSC900 | 2021 | stair climber | General Maintenance 33, Warranty 34 |
| CRS800S | 2021 | recumbent stepper | General Maintenance 34, Warranty 37, Exploded View 40, Parts List 41 |
| CS800 | 2021 | stepper | General Maintenance 34, Warranty 37, Exploded View 40, Parts List 41 |
| CVC800 | 2021 | vertical climber | General Maintenance 25, Warranty 27, Exploded View 30, Parts List 31 |
| 8.5S | 2025 | recumbent stepper | Machine Care 56, Specifications 59, Exploded View 61, Warranty 63 |
| 8.5S-FIT | 2026 | recumbent stepper | Machine Care 47, Specifications 50, Exploded View 51, Warranty 53 |

## The 2024 reprints of three of these machines *do* print codes

This is the trap. **A CRS800S, CS800 or CSC900 caller may own either generation**, and only the newer
one has anything to look up:

| Machine | 2019 / 2021 book | 2024 book |
|---|---|---|
| CRS800S | nothing | `RAM ERROR` and `MOTOR ERROR`, plus a troubleshooting page (`crs800s-2024-errors-ram-error-replace-the-display-board`, `crs800s-2024-errors-motor-error-tension-motor-signal-wrong`) |
| CS800 | nothing | `EEPROM ERROR` and `Err` in the LEVEL window (`cs800-2024-errors-eeprom-error-replace-the-console`) |
| CSC900 | nothing | `ER02`, `ER05`, `ER07`, `ER11`, `ER12` on an eleven-row matrix (`csc900-2024-errors-error-code-table`) |

**So "the CSC900 has no error codes" is true of the 2019 and 2021 books and false of the 2024 one.**
Establish the machine's year before you answer, and do not read the 2024 table to a 2019 or 2021
owner as though it were in their book.

**Two further climbers in the range do print codes** and are not in the list above: the **CSC880
2025** stair climber, with `ER01` to `ER07` (`csc880-2025-errors-error-code-table`), and the **7.0S
2025, 7.5S 2025 and MS300 2021** steppers, with `EEPROM Error` and `Motor Error`
(`cs800-2024-errors-eeprom-error-replace-the-console`,
`spirit-med-stepper-errors-motor-error-press-stop-for-idle-mode`).

## What the nine print instead of a troubleshooting chapter

- **An `ENGINEERING MODE` or `Maintenance Menu`, on five of the nine** - XS895 2018, XS895 2021,
  CRS800S 2021, CS800 2021 and CVC800 2021 - entered by holding Start, Stop and Enter for about two
  to five seconds. Key Test, Display Test, a Functions submenu and a Motor Test. It is the nearest
  thing these consoles offer to a self-diagnostic and it returns no code, only a live reading.
  Carded under `section: console`.
- **A touchscreen `Maintenance Mode` on the 8.5S-FIT 2026**, reached from the Home page by pressing
  `Hello Guest` ten times, holding machine information, settings and service procedures. Also
  `section: console`.
- **Nothing at all on the CSC900 2019, the CSC900 2021 and the 8.5S 2025.** Those three books
  document no engineering mode, no maintenance menu, no key test and no display test - the words do
  not appear in them. They are the emptiest of the nine for a technician, and the two CSC900 books
  are the emptiest documents in this wave.
- **A squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling** under `GENERAL MAINTENANCE` - loose assembly
  hardware, the crank arm nut and the levelling pads. Carded under `section: maintenance`.
- **Nothing else.** No `ERRATIC OPERATION` heart rate interference page of the kind the bikes and
  ellipticals carry, on any of the nine.

## How the absence was checked

Every page of all nine - 414 pages - was counted for native words and scanned with `pdfimages` for a
raster large enough to be a flattened page. **Sixteen pages carry a raster over 0.9 megapixels**, and
every one of those sixteen was rendered at 200-300 dpi and read with `tesseract --psm 6`; so was
every page under 60 native words. They turned out to be heart rate program pages, exploded views,
parts diagrams, console screenshots, assembly art, section dividers, NOTES pages and back covers -
no hidden matrix among them, and not one of the sixteen renders contains the letters *error*,
*fault* or *troubleshoot*. The 5 to 37 OCR supplement pages already appended to each source were re-read as well.

This is not the whole-book render the residential bike sweep used. It is a raster scan of every page
plus a render of every page that could hold a flattened table; a table printed as a picture must
carry a large raster, and none of the pages that do is one.

**A code a caller reports on one of these nine did not come from their manual.** Establish what is
actually on the screen before answering, and do not serve a Spirit treadmill, elliptical, bike or
2024-generation climber code as the answer - those families use different codes with different
fixes, and Sole codes are a different brand entirely.

The Spirit residential ellipticals and the Spirit bikes have the same gap, at
`spirit-xe-errors-no-error-codes-printed` and
`spirit-commercial-bike-errors-no-error-codes-printed`.

## Read this first: the service manuals for six of these machines do print codes or a troubleshooting chapter

**This card is about the owner's manuals.** The service manuals say more:

| Machine | Service manual | What it prints |
|---|---|---|
| CSC900 2019 | `V1.0` (alternator drive, March 2020 PDF) | `ER07`, `ER11`, `ER12`, `ER22` on a twelve-row matrix, plus four component tests (`csc900-2019-errors-error-code-table`) |
| CRS800S 2021 | 2020 ver. | `RAM ERROR` (replace the display board) and `Err` / `MOTOR ERROR` with a voltage test (`crs800s-2024-errors-ram-error-replace-the-display-board`, `crs800s-2024-errors-motor-error-tension-motor-signal-wrong`) |
| CS800 2021 | CS800 (2020) | `EEPROM ERROR` and `Err` with a voltage test (`cs800-2024-errors-eeprom-error-replace-the-console`, `cs800-2024-errors-err-in-the-level-window-tension-motor-feedback`) |
| CS800 2016 | `XS200-SS003` | `E1` and `E2`, written with a hyphen in one place (`cs800-2016-errors-e1-eeprom-failure-replace-the-upper-controller`) |
| XS895 2018 / 2021 | `XS300B-YS006` | `E1`, `E2` and `E3` / `STEP ERROR` (`spirit-xs895-errors-error-code-table-three-codes`) |
| 8.5S 2025 | `MS2000-SB036-01` | no code table, but a `UART Communication Error` message, an Error Log in Maintenance Mode and three electronic procedures (`85s-2025-errors-uart-communication-error-cables-103-and-104-then-software-version`) |

**So a code reported by a caller with one of these machines may well be in their service manual**, even though it is not in the book they own. The CVC800 paragraph above is the same shape. The CSC900 2021 and the 8.5S-FIT 2026 have no service manual in the repository; for them the absence stands.
