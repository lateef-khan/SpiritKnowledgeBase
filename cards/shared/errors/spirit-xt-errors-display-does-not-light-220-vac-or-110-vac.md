Five checks, in the order the service checklist prints them.

1. Tether cord not in position.
2. Circuit breaker on front grill tripped. Push circuit breaker in until it locks.
3. Plug is disconnected. Make sure plug is firmly pushed into **220 Vac** wall outlet. **(On 120Vac electronic power system need 110Vac power)**
4. Breaker panel circuit breaker may be tripped.
5. Treadmill defect. Contact your dealer.

The 2015 XT385 and XT485 and the XT485ENT service manuals print it identically. **Their owner's manuals print 115 VAC in the same row** (`ct900ent-display-does-not-light`), so for these three machines the service and owner's documents disagree about the outlet. The 2015 XT185 and XT285 service manuals turn the figures round - 110 VAC, with 220 VAC for the 220 V model (`spirit-xt-2015-errors-display-does-not-light-110-vac-or-220-vac`) - and the CT books print 120 VAC (`ct850-2016-display-does-not-light`). The 2023 XT service manuals and the XT685ENT have no checklist.
