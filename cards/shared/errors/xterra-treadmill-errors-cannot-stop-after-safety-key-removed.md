The troubleshooting procedure matrix prints one line for this in all five service manuals:

*Condition:* After removing safe key, treadmill can't stop. *Reason:* 1. The safety key device is broken. *Solve:* 1. Replace with new safety key device.

A treadmill that starts or displays with no key in at all is `xterra-treadmill-errors-runs-without-safety-key-safety-device-shorted`. The buzzer test for the key device is `xterra-treadmill-errors-e0-safety-key-device-buzzer-test`.
