**This is the only symptom-and-remedy either CSI manual prints.** Neither book has a troubleshooting
page, an error code or a fault table - see `spirit-strength-errors-no-troubleshooting-page-printed`.
The note below sits on the assembly page, not in a troubleshooting chapter, which is why it is easy
to miss.

## The symptom, and the two things that cause it

When the resistance box and the track are **not fully assembled**:

- The unit **can be powered on, but resistance cannot be set**, and the operation interface **should
  not be used**.
- Pulling the steel cable in that state with **a force of 8 kg or more** may cause **the steel cable
  inside the resistance box to jam or even become damaged**.

So "it powers up but I cannot set a resistance" is expected behaviour on a part-built machine, not a
fault. It becomes a fault the moment someone pulls the cable hard to find out why.

## What the manual says to do

> If this occurs, open the rear cover and follow the after-sales guidance to restore functionality.

The manual prints **no** further step than that: no cable route, no re-seating procedure, no torque
or tension figure, and no part number. "After-sales guidance" is not reproduced anywhere in the book,
so this remedy is a referral, not an instruction a technician can follow from the manual alone.

## As printed

> Note: When the resistance box and the track are not fully assembled, the unit can be powered on,
> but resistance cannot be set and the operation interface should not be used. Furthermore, when
> pulling the steel cable, do so gently (with a force less than 8 kg). Excessive force is strictly
> prohibited. Violating these principles may cause the steel cable inside the resistance box to jam
> or even become damaged. If this occurs, open the rear cover and follow the after-sales guidance to
> restore functionality.

**8 kg is the printed figure and the manual gives it in kilograms only** - there is no pound
equivalent anywhere on the page.

## A caution about the text layer of this page

The PDF text layer of page 14 contains the note **twice**: once as printed, and once as an
unprinted earlier revision overlaid on the parts table, which reads "The (951) are pre-installed"
where the printed page reads "The (951)(908) are pre-installed". The figures above were read from a
render of the page, not from the extraction.

Both CSI machines - the CSI-CPSP chest press and the CSI-LROW low row - carry this note word for
word on the same page.
