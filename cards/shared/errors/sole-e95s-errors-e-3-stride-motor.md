**This is E-3 with a hyphen, the E95s stride-motor calibration error — not the Sole treadmill E3 incline fault (`sole-e3-error`), and not the E95/E98 incline-motor E-3, which names the incline motor instead of the stride motor.**

The book prints, word for word: “If calibration is errors of the stride motor,in screen will shows “E-3”.You can press STOP key by pass.” The screen shows a red warning panel reading E-3 with “Press Stop to bypass” beneath it. If calibration passes, the Home page appears.

The calibration screens step through MAX STRIDE then MIN/MAX STRIDE. If the problem persists the book says to contact the service department.
