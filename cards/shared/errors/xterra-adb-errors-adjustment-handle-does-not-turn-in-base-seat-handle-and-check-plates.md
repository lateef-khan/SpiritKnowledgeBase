**This is the one troubleshooting entry the dumbbell books print, and all three print it word for word.** The problem is given as *Adjustment handlebar doesn't turn around while handle is in base.*

The answer, in the book's order:

a. Ensure the handle is **fully inserted into the dumbbell tray base**. If it is not fully inserted, the locking mechanism will not be released.

b. Check to see if any weight plates were put into the **wrong grooves** or **face backwards**. All weight plates must have the **selection tongue facing away from the handle grip**.

So the selector locks by design whenever the handle is out of its tray, and it also jams when a plate has been returned to the wrong slot or turned round: the tongue on each plate must point outward, away from the grip, for the selector to pass it. Neither cause is a part failure and the books name nothing to replace. The 12.5 lb pair, the 25 lb and the 55 lb dumbbell differ in their plate counts and increments but not in this answer.

The dumbbell stand's book prints no troubleshooting page at all (`xterra-errors-rower-and-strength-books-print-no-error-codes-except-one-rower`).
