The *Service Checklist Diagnosis Guide* in the TR65, TR75, TR85 and TRX1400 owner's manuals prints five checks:

*Display does not light*

1. Tether cord not in position.
2. Circuit breaker on front grill tripped. Push circuit breaker in until it locks.
3. Plug is disconnected. Make sure plug is firmly pushed into AC wall outlet.
4. Breaker panel circuit breaker may be tripped.
5. Treadmill defect. Contact your dealer.

The TR300, TR6.6 and TR6.4 books print the outlet as 115 VAC and say to contact XTERRA Fitness (`xterra-tr-errors-display-does-not-light-five-checks-115-vac`); the TRX1400 *service* manual's copy of this checklist says **220 VAC** (`trx1400-2023-errors-display-does-not-light-220-vac-service-manual`). The service-manual matrix row with nine causes is `xterra-treadmill-errors-power-switch-not-lit-nine-causes`.
