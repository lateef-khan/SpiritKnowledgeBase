The Mechanical System row `Belt noise`, in the order printed:

1. Check the belt to see if it needs to be tracked.
2. Take off the motor hood to make sure the noise is from the belt.
3. Check the belt and deck for damage.
4. Clean the deck and re-lube.

Printed identically in the 2008 XT dealer manual and the CT800 2016 service manual's *Treadmill Troubleshooting* chapter. The CT800 2016 adds a *Belt Checking* procedure for a belt that touches its guide: run it at the lowest speed, 0.5 mph, and turn the left rear roller adjustment clockwise no more than a quarter turn until the noise stops - a tracking adjustment, and a maintenance fact. The motor and deck noises have their own rows (`spirit-xt175-errors-drive-motor-noise-brushes`, `spirit-xt175-errors-deck-noise-squeaking-or-creaking`).
