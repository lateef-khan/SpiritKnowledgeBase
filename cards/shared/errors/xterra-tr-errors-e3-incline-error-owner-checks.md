**This is E3 as the TR300 and TR65 owner's manuals print it: an incline error.** On the TR75H and TR95H E3 is a communication fault (`xterra-tr-errors-e3-jkexer-poor-communication-upper-lower-controller`).

The TR300 book prints one line: **E3: Incline Error.** The TR65 book prints the same meaning with three checks:

1. Is the cable between incline motor and controller connected correctly and properly? - Connect properly.
2. Check if incline mechanism being stuck (printed "stock") or defective? - Replace incline motor.
3. Is VR connected properly or with interference? - Replace incline motor.

Neither machine has a service manual in the knowledge base. The same fault on the machines that do is `xterra-treadmill-errors-e3-incline-vr-out-of-range` and its ER / ERR / Err siblings, whose troubleshooting starts at the VR wires and the 5-pin cable rather than at a new motor.
