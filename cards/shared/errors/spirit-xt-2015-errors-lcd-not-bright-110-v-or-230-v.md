| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| LCD light is broken | Replace with new LCD or console |
| Power to console too low | Check AC power is 110V or 230V. Check power to console. Replace lower controller. |

The condition is printed `LCDs not bright, incomplete or imperfect`. The 2015 XT185 and XT285 service manuals print the row identically, and they are the only XT books that name a supply figure in it; the 2015 XT385 and XT485 and all the 2023 XT books say only *check power to console* and start at a fallen-off connector (`spirit-xt-errors-lcd-not-bright-connector-then-power`). The CT850 2016 asks for 110-120V (`spirit-lcd-dim-or-incomplete`).

Dead segments rather than a dim light are the next row down, `spirit-lcd-displays-dim-or-incomplete`.
