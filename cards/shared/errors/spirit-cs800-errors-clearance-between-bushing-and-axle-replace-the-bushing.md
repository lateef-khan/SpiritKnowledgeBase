- **CS800 (2020) - 9-4 Swing Assembly.** *Situation: There is a clearance between bushing and axle.* **Solve:** The bushing may be worn after a long time of usage requires replacement.
- **CS800 2016 - 10-4 Rod End Shaft and Linked Assembly.** Rod end shaft and linked assembly has check quality and then assembly, only **Bushing** will damage when long time using, and has gap problem, simply replace the new product with reference to the disassembly step.

**One wear part, and it is the only one either book expects to wear at the pivots.** The rod end shaft, the linked assembly and the swing assembly are checked at the factory; what goes with use is the bushing between the axle and the arm, and the symptom is play - a gap you can feel or hear as the pedal arm changes direction. The fix is a new bushing, fitted along the swing or linked assembly replacement steps of the same book (a 12 mm wrench for the axle bolt and washer; make sure axle and U-plate align before tightening).

**No wear limit is printed.** The book gives no gap figure at which the bushing is due; a clearance that can be felt is the criterion.

A vibration felt through the feet rather than play at the pivot is a different bearing - the slide wheel's (`spirit-cs800-errors-vibration-at-the-pedals-slide-wheel-bearing-and-a-broken-pedal`). Sole prints this page for its SC200 steppers (`sc200-2016-rod-end-shaft-and-linked-assembly`, `sc200-2019-rod-end-shaft-and-linked-assembly`).
