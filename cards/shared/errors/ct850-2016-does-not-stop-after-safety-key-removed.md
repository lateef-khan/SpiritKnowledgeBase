One cause and one fix, printed identically in the 2016 and 2020 manuals.

| Condition | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| After removing safe key, treadmill can't stop | The safety key device is broken | Replace with new safety key device |

The opposite fault - the machine runs with no key fitted at all - is a separate row on
`ct850-2016-operates-without-safety-key`.

**The CT800ENT 2024 and CT850ENT 2024 owner's manuals print this row word for word** in the
Condition/Reason/Solve matrix on printed pages 57 to 59. Both of those pages are flat pictures
with no text layer and were read from the rendered page.

**Eight more service manuals print the one-cause, one-fix row identically**: the 2015 XT185 and XT285, the CT800 2016, CT800 2020, CT800ENT 2022, CT850ENT 2022, CT900ENT and 4.0T. The 2023 XT books, the 2015 XT385 and XT485 and the CT1000ENT print the same cause with **three** fixes - safety key device, then controller, then console - on `spirit-xt-errors-does-not-stop-after-safety-key-removed-three-parts`.
