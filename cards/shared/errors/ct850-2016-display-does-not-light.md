Five checks, in the order the manual lists them. The 2016 and 2020 service manuals print this row identically.

1. Tether cord not in position.
2. Circuit breaker on front grill tripped. Push circuit breaker in until it locks.
3. Plug is disconnected. Make sure plug is firmly pushed into 120 VAC wall outlet.
4. Breaker panel circuit breaker may be tripped.
5. Treadmill defect. Contact your dealer.

**The CT850 owner's manuals print the same five checks with a different outlet figure, and
none of them prints 120 VAC.** The 2016 and 2020 owner's manuals print step 3 as a
**110 - 120 VAC** outlet (`ct800-2020-errors-display-does-not-light`); the 2018 and CT850ENT
2022 owner's manuals print **115 VAC** (`ct900ent-display-does-not-light`). Three documented
figures for the same machines, and nothing reconciles them. Steps 1, 2, 4 and 5 are the same
in all of them, except that the owner's manuals name the dealer as *your Spirit Fitness
dealer*.

The troubleshooting matrix carries two more rows about a dead or dim console that this list does not
mention: the ON/OFF switch not lighting (`ct850-2016-power-switch-not-lit-at-power-on`) and no
display once the safety key is inserted (`ct850-2016-no-display-when-safety-key-inserted` for the
2016 manual, `ct850-2020-no-display-when-safety-key-inserted` for the 2020 manual).
