As printed, items 2 and 3 of `Swing Arm Problem` (the first item is the axle-grease and 6005-bearing noise note, which is a care question):

- **XE195 2016:** When the keys on the handrails are not functioning, first remove the stickers and pull out the Handgrip End Cap, then check the Resistance Button W/Cable is correctly connected with the Resistance Connecting Cable. Next check the wire connection between the swing arm and the lower arm is connected properly. At the same time, also check to see if the wire is broken.
- **XE295 2016:** When the keys on the handrails are not functioning, first remove the stickers, use a Phillips head screwdriver and remove the M5x20mm screws securing the keys to the Swing Arm. Next check the wire connection between the swing arm and the lower arm is connected properly. At the same time, also check to see if the wire is broken.
- **XE395 2016 and XE395ENT 2021:** the XE295 wording, plus one line between the two steps: *Check to see if the wires for the keys and swing arm are connected properly.*

**Every version is a wiring check and nothing else** - no part is named to replace, and no meter is used. The keys are the thumb switches on the swing arms; on the XE195 they come off with the end cap, on the others with two M5x20mm screws.

**The matrix answers the same symptom from the console end.** On the XE395 2016 and XE395ENT 2021 the incline switch has a six-cause row ending in *replace the cable* and *replace buttons* (`spirit-xe395-errors-incline-adjustment-buttons-not-working`); the CE850 books print the stride version. The CE850 2016 and XE895 2016 print only the access step - *when the keys on the handrails are not functioning, first remove the stickers ... remove the M5x20mm Flat Head Socket Screws securing the keys to the Swing Arm* - inside their swing-arm replacement procedure, with no wire check after it.
