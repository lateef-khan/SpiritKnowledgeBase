The manual, word for word:

> Adjust the thumb nuts located to the rear of the seat adjustment levers. If the cables attached to
> the levers stretch it is possible the latch for the seat will not disengage, or engage, properly.
> Adjusting the thumb nut can remedy this.

**One adjuster covers both failures.** A cable that has stretched can leave the latch unable to let
go - the seat will not move - or unable to catch - the seat will not lock. Both are taken up at the
**thumb nuts behind the seat adjustment levers**, and the manual names no other part.

**No turn count, no torque and no travel figure is printed.** Adjust, try the lever, repeat.

**These machines have two seat adjustments, on two levers, and the assembly page tells them apart by
cable colour**: the MS300 manual reads "the left lever with gray cable is for the fore/aft seat
adjustment and the right lever with black cable is for the seat back angle". Establish which of the
two is failing before you turn anything.

**Where this fact lives.** It is printed only inside the Troubleshooting section - the maintenance
chapter of these manuals never mentions the seat or the thumb nuts - so it is carded here with the
rest of that table rather than under `section: maintenance`, where the repository files most
adjustment work.
