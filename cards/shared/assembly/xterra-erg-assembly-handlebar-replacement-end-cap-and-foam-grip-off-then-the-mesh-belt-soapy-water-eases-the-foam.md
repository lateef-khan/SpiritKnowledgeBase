1. Remove the round end cap and the **foam grip** from the **Handlebar (5)** - the ERG160 says "using an air gun" - then remove the mesh belt.
2. Reassemble in the reverse order.

**Note (ERG160):** "Use a tool to pry the foam grip to open a bit and then inject some soapy water into it; that will be easier to remove and install." The ERG180 book gives the same two moves (end cap, then foam) without the tip. Neither says how the mesh belt fastens to the bar; the ERG550W and ERG600W service books show a screw through the belt end.
