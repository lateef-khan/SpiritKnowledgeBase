1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **two M5 x 10 mm Phillips head screws** securing the **handle bar
   covers** (the 2020 book: "remove both side handle bar covers", no count).
2. Release the **three Ø4 x 15L sheet-metal screws** securing the **console mast cover**.
3. Remove the **seven Ø4 x 15L sheet-metal screws** of the left and right covers.
4. Remove the **one Ø5 x 16L tapping screw** joining the left and right covers (the 2020 book: "a screw which locks
   both left and right chain covers").
5. Remove the **five 5 x 19 mm tapping screws** that secure the **right chain cover** to the main frame.
6. Remove the **five 5 x 19 mm tapping screws** that secure the **left chain cover** to the main frame.
7. Release the **nut of the DC power plug**, then pull the DC power cables out; the Chain Cover (L) can then be
   released. The 2020 book says to use a **needle-nose plier** on the DC socket nut, then unmount the DC power
   wire from the chain cover.
8. Reassemble in the reverse order.

**Both generations print the same screws - 2, 3, 7, 1, 5 and 5 - and the same DC socket nut.** The XS200-SS003
book (cs800-2016) prints the sizes; the 2020 book prints "ψ4*15mm" and "ψ5*19mm" for the same fasteners. Every
later procedure that says "follow the chain cover replacement" means this card. The Sole SC200 side-case card is
the same procedure for the same Dyaco chassis.
