**Hook the handle before you let go of it.** The ribbon roll (30) retracts; if the handle is not hooked on the
Hook (9) the strap goes back inside the chain cover.

1. Take out the **3.5 x 12L sheet-metal screws (112), 2 pcs**, and the **sheet-metal screws (132), 2 pcs**, from
   the **Controller Assembly (36)** with a driver, and pass the handle through the **Ribbon Roll (30)**. Pass the
   **Handle (10)** through the Ribbon Roll (30) and **fix it on the Hook (9)**, to stop the ribbon roll going back
   to the chain cover.
2. To change the **PVC Sleeve (50)**, remove the Controller Assembly (36) directly.
3. To remove only the battery, remove the **3.5 x 12L sheet-metal screw (112), 1 pc**, from the **Battery Cover
   (36~3)**.

The CRW800 (2020) book and the CW800-YR001 book print this identically. The **XRW600's handle is a different
part** - end caps, foam and centre covers on a handle bar holder - and has its own card.

The controller assembly (36) is the resistance button faceplate and RF module on the handle; "no adjustable
resistance" troubleshooting in the same books starts by opening this battery cover.
