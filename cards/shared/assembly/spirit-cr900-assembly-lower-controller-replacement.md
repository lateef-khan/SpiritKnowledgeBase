**6.8.1 Remove the lower controller.** Refer to 6.7 for the removal of the covers. Unplug the wiring connectors, then remove the **four Phillips head screws** that secure the lower controller.

That is the whole printed procedure: one step, no refit step, no screw size, no connector count. The CR900 (2018) and CR900ENT books print it identically.

Which covers: 6.7 is the rear and bottom cover procedure, so the controller is reached from the rear of the machine. The troubleshooting matrix's "replace lower controller" for a dim display and the driver-board wiring definitions are other sections; this card is the physical swap only.
