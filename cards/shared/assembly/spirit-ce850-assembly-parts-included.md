- 1 Main Frame
- 1 Console Mast
- 1 Console Mast Cover
- 2 Swing Arms
- 2 Connecting Arms
- 6 Levelers
- 1 Console
- **1 Water Bottle Holder** - 2022 printing only
- 2 Foot Pedals
- 1 Power Cord
- 1 Audio Cable
- 2 Transport Wheels
- 1 Hardware Kit

**The 2016 and 2020 cartons have twelve lines, the 2022 thirteen.** The added line is
the water bottle holder, printed between the console and the foot pedals. Everything
else is word for word the same on all three.

## The 2016 diagram promises a holder the 2016 carton does not contain

The 2016 manual's machine diagram labels a **WATER BOTTLE HOLDER**, but this panel
does not list one and none of its six assembly steps fits one. The 2020 printing drops
the label; the 2022 printing puts it back and adds the part and the step. See
[the bottle holder card](spirit-ce-assembly-water-bottle-holder.md) before promising
a 2016 owner a part.

## Six levellers, a power cord and an audio cable

This is the only Spirit elliptical carton that lists levellers - **six** of them - and
the only one that lists a power cord. The CE800 and CE800ENT panels list neither,
because those machines are generator-powered and have nothing to plug in.

**The CE850 noise item still says two levelling pads**, under the rear stabiliser,
not six. Both figures are printed in the same manual and neither page mentions the
other. See [the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

The **tools** are printed in a second panel on the same page and are on their own
card - see [tools included](spirit-ce850-assembly-tools-included.md).
