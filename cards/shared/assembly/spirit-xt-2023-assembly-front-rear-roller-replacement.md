Six books print the same five steps.

1. Remove both **Adjustment base cover** screws with a screwdriver, then take the covers off.
2. Remove the **2 Rear Roller locking bolts** with an **M6 L-Allen wrench**, then take off the rear roller.
3. Remove the drive motor cover, then unmount the drive belt from the front roller.
4. Remove the **Front Roller locking bolt** with a **13mm wrench**, then take off the front roller.
5. Adjusting the running belt to the centre is necessary when installing the front or rear roller back.

Two things this procedure does not do that the 2015 XT books do: it does not loosen the motor bolts or a
tension screw to slack the drive belt (it lifts the belt off the front roller with the cover open), and it
prints no belt-tension figure. The drive-belt figure, 70~75 lbs, is in these books' drive motor procedure.

No screw count is printed for the adjustment base covers, and no torque is printed.
