**Write down the AC switch wire colours and positions before you unplug them** (step 6).

1. Use a screwdriver to remove **2 screws**, then take off both left and right side **handle bar covers**.
2. Use a screwdriver to remove **3 screws**, then take off the **console mast cover**.
3. Use a **12 mm and a 13 mm box wrench** to remove the **upper handle bar locking bolts and washers**, then take
   off both sides of the upper handle bar.
4. Use a screwdriver to remove the chain cover locking screws, then take off the **right chain cover**.
5. Use a screwdriver to remove the **left chain cover** locking screws.
6. **Record the AC switch wires' colour and location, then unplug the wires.** Take off the left chain cover.
7. Remove the **rear side case**.
8. Reverse the steps to install everything back. *Do not pinch wires.*

The AC power switch, with its fuse, sits on the left side where the line cord plugs into this mains-powered
stepper, which is why step 6 exists. No screw sizes or chain-cover screw counts are printed. Every later procedure in the book that
starts "follow the steps of side case replacement" or "chain cover replacement" means this card.

The XS300B-YS006 service manual is one book for both XS895 owner's-manual printings; the two-column page is read by step number.
