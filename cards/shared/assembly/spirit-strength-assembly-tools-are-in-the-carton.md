**All tools required to assemble the Strength Equipment are included within the
packaging.** The unpacking step tells you to locate the hardware package and
**"Remove the tools first"**, before you take out any step's hardware.

**Five of these machines say the opposite in a later printing.** This card describes the
earlier printing of each; the table at the foot says which printing is which.

**The manual never says which tools they are.** No tool panel, no picture, no sizes - on
any of these thirteen books. The only thing you are told is that they are in the pack.

## The two i-Strength machines add a reason to use your own anyway

Their NOTE continues: *"Using professional tools will decrease the time of tightening the
hardware and will be more secure."* Same claim that the tools are supplied, with a
recommendation not to rely on them.

## Twenty-three manuals in the same range say the opposite

Every CSS selectorized station, three of the CSD dual stations and the CSF functional
trainer say the tools are **not** in the packaging and name three you must supply. Do not
carry this card onto them - see
[the supply-your-own card](spirit-strength-assembly-three-tools-you-must-supply.md).

## Five CSD dual stations changed sides in 2025

Each of these was reprinted with the NOT-included NOTE, asking for an **Allen wrench set,
a Phillips screw driver and c ring pliers**. Both printings exist, so answer by the book
in the customer's hands, not by the machine.

| Machine | Printing that says the tools ARE included | Printing that says they are NOT |
|---|---|---|
| CSD-CPSP chest press / shoulder press | February 2024 (4601US) | May 2025 update, 4601US 20250521 |
| CSD-ACBE ab crunch / back extension | February 2024, `CSD-ACBE_622233_OM_20240325` | 2025 revision, Version 5.0, 04/11/2025 |
| CSD-ITOT inner / outer thigh | February 2024 | 2025 revision, Version 4.0, 02/22/2025 |
| CSD-LPCE leg press / calf extension | February 2024 | 2025 revision, Version 4.0, 03/03/2025 |
| CSD-PUDA pull-up / dip assist | February 2024, `CSD-PUDA_678323_OM_20240619` | 2025 revision, Version 4.0, 15/05/2025 |

**The 2025 books also drop "Remove the tools first" from the unpacking list**, so they do
not contradict themselves the way the leg extension/leg curl book does. A machine built
from a 2025 book needs your own tools; see
[the supply-your-own card](spirit-strength-assembly-three-tools-you-must-supply.md) for
the wording and the one book that misprints it.
