**Install the Weight Stacks, then the Top Weight Stack, then the incremental weight, then
the guide rod fixed plate - in that sequence.**

The fixed plate is what caps the guide rods, so nothing can be added to the stack once it
is on. Getting the order wrong means taking the plate off again.

## The wording varies and two of the four items drop out of some books

Four printings exist across these machines:

- *"...Weight Stacks, Top Weight Stack, incremental weight then the guide rod fixed plate
  (12) in sequence"* - the fullest, on the abdominal, back extension, lat pulldown,
  shoulder press and seated leg press.
- *"...Weight Stacks, Top Weight Stack and Incremental Weight then the guide rod fixed
  plate (12) in sequence"* - squat/standing calf.
- *"...Weight Stacks, Top Weight Stack, incremental weight in sequence"* - seated chest
  press and the lat pulldown/seated row, with the fixed plate left off the list.
- *"...Weight Stacks, Top Weight Stack and Guide rod fixed plate in sequence"* - deltoid
  trainer, seated row and pec fly/rear delt, with the incremental weight left off.

**The order is the same in all four; the shorter ones simply omit an item.** Fit all four
in the order above whichever book you have.

Two more notes sit in the same panel: the magnet side of the upper pulley has to face the
sensor, and the guide rods are lubricated before the shrouds go on. See
[the magnet card](spirit-strength-assembly-the-magnet-side-of-the-upper-pulley-faces-the-sensor.md)
and [the lubrication card](spirit-strength-assembly-lubricate-the-guide-rods-before-the-shrouds.md).

The **CSD-CPSP chest press / shoulder press prints this note only in its May 2025 update** (4601US, 20250521); its February 2024 printing has no notes on the weight-stack page at all. Its wording is the third form - *"...Weight Stacks, Top Weight Stack, incremental weight in sequence"* - with the fixed plate left off the list, and its step-8 table has no guide rod fixed plate either. A separate note above it says **"The cable has to run through the incremental weight set."**

The **CSS-DELT's 2026 revision** (SP-4312, 20260821, whose text layer is a shifted font - read from its OCR supplements and renders) prints this exactly as its 2025 printing does.
