**Tools included:** Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screwdriver (#102), M6 L Allen Wrench
(#103), 13 mm Wrench (#90) - one of each.

**Parts included:** 1 Main Frame, 2 Uprights, 2 Frame Base Covers, 1 Console, 1 Deck, 1 Power Cord,
1 Lubricant, 1 Hardware Kit.

| Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| #80 | Ø8 x 1.5T Split Washer | 4 |
| #100 | Ø5/16" x Ø18 x 1.5T Flat Washer | 8 |
| #99 | 5/16" x UNC18 x 3/4" Hex Head Bolt | 8 |
| #125 | 5/16" x UNC18 x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 |
| #44 | Safety Key | 1 |
| #104 | Lubricant | 1 |

The hex bolts are **3/4" long**, as on the TRX5500; the TR300, TR6.4 and TRX2500-4500 use a 1/2" bolt
under the same item number. **"2 Frame Base Covers" are listed but no assembly step fits them** - the
four steps end with the handrail support and the book gives no screws for the covers.

The three books print this page word for word.
