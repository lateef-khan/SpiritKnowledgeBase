1. **Console covers (E55: 94 & 95, E95: 196 & 197)** with **three Phillips Head Screws (139)** and **two Sheet Metal Screws (142)** using the Short driver (185). **Tighten the Sheet Metal screws first.**
2. **Inner covers (111 & 112)** on the Left and Right Connecting Arms (8 & 9), each side with **two Phillips Head Screws (139)** and **two Sheet Metal Screws (142)** using the Short driver (185). **Outer covers (109 & 110)** on the left and right handlebars (10 & 11) the same way, the outer screws being Self Tapping (142). Arrows on the stickers decide the orientation.
3. **Sliding Wheel Covers (104)**, each side with **four Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
4. **Stabilizer Covers (117 & 118)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
5. **Front Stabilizer Cover (103)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
6. **Incline Cover brackets (24)**, bent-tab hole facing rearward, with **four Phillips Head Screws (145)**; **Rear incline bar cover (115)** with **two Screws (139)**.
7. **Rear Stabilizer Cover (116)** with **four Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
8. **After the elliptical power is plugged in, run the incline to position 8** and install the **Incline Rail front cover (113)** up against the middle stabilizer tube with **four Phillips Head Screws (139)**.

These steps consume all 29 Phillips screws and all 10 sheet metal screws in the bag.
