1. **Install the Incline Rail Assembly (2) into the U-channel bracket of the Main Frame (1).** From the sides install **two 3/8" x 1-1/2" Hex Head Bolts (107)** with **two 3/8" x 19mm Flat Washers (142)** and **two 3/8" x 7T Nylon Nuts (134)**. From the top install **four 5/16" x 2-1/4" Hex Head Bolts (108)**, **four 5/16" Split Washers (158)**, **four 5/16" x 20 Flat Washers (157)** and **four Star Washers (151)**, as shown in figure 1. Tighten with the **13/14 mm Wrench (159)** and **12/14 mm Wrench (162)**.
2. **Slide the Console Mast Cover (72) onto the Console Mast (12).** **Unravel the computer cable (47)** at the top opening of the Main Frame and feed it into the bottom of the console mast tube and out of the top opening. Install the Console Mast (12) into the receiving bracket. **Pull slightly on the cable at the top while installing** so it does not get pinched and shorted.
3. **Put one 3/8" x 2T Split Washer (155) onto the 3/8" x 2-1/4" Long Hex Head Bolt (101)** through the left side of the receiving bracket, and **two 3/8" x 23 Curved Washers (156) onto the two 3/8" x 3/4" Short Hex Head Bolts (106)** through the front. Using the **13/14m/m Wrench**, tighten the three bolts plus the pre-installed fourth firmly, as much as you possibly can.
4. **Plug the connectors into the back of the console: Computer Cable (47), two Hand pulse Cables (52) and Resistance switch wires (55).** Secure the **Console (46)** with **four M5 x 10m/m Phillips Head Screws (117)**.
