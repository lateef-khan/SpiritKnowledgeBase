1. Remove the **rear tapping screws (2 pcs)** from the **riser cover**, then remove the **M5 screws (2 pcs)** on both
   sides (figures 6.3.1 and 6.3.2).
2. Assemble the console mast cover by reversing the process.

The three books print this word for word. Section 6.16 sends you here ("refer to Procedure 6.3 to remove the
electronic watch") - the console removal is actually 6.1.
