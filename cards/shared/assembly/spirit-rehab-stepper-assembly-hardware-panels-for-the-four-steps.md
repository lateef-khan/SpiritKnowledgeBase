## Reading these

- **The same item number appears in more than one panel.** Items 170, 190 and 193 are split across
  steps 1 and 2, so the total in the bag is the sum. Ten of item 170 and ten of item 190 in all.
- **The parts list gives the same items in full thread form** - item 129 as
  `M6 x P1.0 x 40L Socket Head Cap Bolt`, item 190 as `3/8" x UNC16 x 7T Nyloc Nut`. Order by the
  parts list description, not by the panel's short form.
- **Item 243 is written `Ø 10 x 2T` in the MED book and `M10 x 2T` in the MS300 book.** It is a
  10 mm split washer either way; the MS300's `M` is a typo for a washer diameter.
- **Nothing in these panels is a levelling glide.** All six arrive fitted -
  `spirit-rehab-stepper-assembly-levelling-six-glides`.

## The tools are not in these panels either

Six wrenches and a screwdriver come as numbered rows in the parts list, and no panel promises them -
`spirit-rehab-stepper-assembly-no-tools-panel`.

## The 7.5S has a different pack

Nineteen items in its step-one panel against eleven here, because of the wheelchair anchors and the
seat rail - `7-5s-med-assembly-hardware-panels-for-the-four-steps`. **Do not read a 7.5S quantity
off this card.**
