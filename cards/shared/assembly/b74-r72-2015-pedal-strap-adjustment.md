Adjust each pedal strap so that they are snug around your feet. You have the option of adjusting the **strap length** on one or both sides of the pedal.

**This 2015 manual says "strap length".** Every other Sole upright and recumbent owner's manual that prints this paragraph (B94/R92 2013 onward, LCB/LCR 2016 onward) says "the **hole location** on one or both sides of the pedal" instead. The 2015 pair is the only wording of its kind, so do not answer a B74 or R72 question from the other card and do not answer another model from this one.
