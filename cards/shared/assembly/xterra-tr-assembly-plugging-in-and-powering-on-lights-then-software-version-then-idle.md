1. Plug the unit into an appropriate wall outlet.
2. Turn on the **power switch located at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood**.
3. **Ensure the safety key is installed** - the treadmill will not power on without it.

When the power is turned on, **all the lights on the display light up for a short time and the console
shows the software version**. The treadmill then enters **idle mode**, the starting point for operation.

Six books print this paragraph word for word; the TR95H and TR75H say "Hiker Treadmill" for
"treadmill". The TR75H also closes its assembly chapter with a "Step Five - Complete Installation":
"Make sure the Safety Key is attached and the power cable is plugged in. Start your enjoyable workout."
Its service manual's assembly chapter says the same thing in one line.

The books do not say what the version number will read, how long the lights stay on, or what idle mode
looks like - the console section covers that. The TR300 and TR6.4 print a different final-process
step (power switch "near the power cord", press START) - see the linked card. The WS200/WS300 print a
different paragraph again (socket on the right side, standby state).
