Hardware: **#93** M5 x 12L Tapping Screw (2 pcs), **#101** 4 x 16L Sheet Metal Screw (3 pcs).

1. Install the Tablet Holder (138) on the Handlebar (6) with **3 pcs of Sheet Metal Screws (101)**, by using the **Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (86)**.
2. Install the **Drink Bottle Holder (23)** on the Main Frame (1) with **2 pcs of Tapping Screws (93)**, by using the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (86).

The SB900 2020 wording adds the size inline in instruction 1: "3pcs of 4 x 16m/m Sheet Metal Screws (101)". Same part.

**The drink bottle holder instruction is new for 2020.** On the 2019 machines the two #93 tapping screws are supplied but no printed step uses them.

**The step number differs between the two manuals** - the SB700 2020 prints it as step 5 and the SB900 2020 as step 6 - and the SB700 2020 checklist calls the same hardware group step 6 while its own instruction page calls it step 5. The parts and instructions are the same either way.
