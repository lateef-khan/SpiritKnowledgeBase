1. **Connect the Swing Arm (L-10) to the left Lower arm (13)** and the **Swing Arm (R-11) to the right Lower arm (14)**, and secure them with **six Hex Head Bolts (206)**, **eight Flat Washers (164)**, **four Curved Washers (181)** and **six Nyloc Nuts (157)** using **Wrenches (184 & 187)**.
2. **Connect the two wires (L-61 to 62 and R-60 to 62)** on the left and right sides together, store the excess wire including the plastic connectors back inside the console mast (12), place the **rubber grommets (75)** over the wire on each side, and snap them into the holes of the console mast.
