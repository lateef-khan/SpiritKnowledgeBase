1. Take the treadmill out of the carton and lay it aside on smooth ground.
2. **Lift the upright crossbar (as shown in A) until it clicks into place at the top height.**

The crossbar is hinged to the base, so no bolts are used. Step 2 then raises the two handles and turns
the handlebar stoppers away; folding the machine later reverses the sequence. The manual gives no
warning about fingers at the hinge and does not say what to do if the crossbar does not click.
