## Height

1. Standing next to the bike, adjust the seat until it is about **hip height**.
2. Rotate the crank arms until the pedals are vertical: **12 and 6 o'clock**.
3. Put your foot in the toe cage of the lower pedal and mount. The **ball of the foot**
   goes over the centre of the pedal, and the leg should be **slightly bent at the knee,
   about 5 degrees**.
4. Leg too straight, or the foot cannot reach - lower the seat. Knee bent too much -
   raise it.
5. Dismount, then loosen the fastener on the seat post and move the seat up or down.
6. Tighten the fastener to secure the post.
7. Note the final position mark on the seat post for future reference.

## Fore/aft

1. Sit on the bike with the crank arms at **3 and 9 o'clock**. For road-bike training,
   the position is right when the **small bump at the top of your shin sits above the
   pedal axle**.
2. Dismount, loosen the fastener under the seat, slide the seat forward or back, then
   tighten.

## The fastener is not the same part on every machine

The CB900 and both XIC600 printings say **quick release lever**. The CIC800 says
**knob**. The procedure and every figure above are otherwise word for word across all
four books.

**The 2021 XIC600 adds one line the others lack:** "Seat height can change when wearing
different shoes, always wear shoes when cycling." It is at the end of step 7. The 2018
XIC600 prints it too; the CB900 and CIC800 do not.

## The CIC850 prints none of this

The CIC850 manual jumps from the last assembly step to handlebar adjustment with no
seat-position procedure at all, even though its own labelled diagram calls out SEAT
HEIGHT ADJ. and SEAT FORWARD/AFT ADJ. See
[the absence card](../../cic850-2022/assembly/cic850-2022-assembly-no-seat-position-procedure.md).
