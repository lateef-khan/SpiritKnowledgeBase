**The notch on the swing axis must line up with the U-slot before the screws go in** - both books say so.

The XS200-SS003 book (cs800-2016):

1. Remove the left and right chain covers (the side case card).
2. Use a **10 mm wrench** to release the **M6 x 6T nyloc nut** fixed on the **M6 x 45L eye bolt**.
3. Use a **12 mm wrench** to remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 3/4"** and the **flat washer 5/16" x 23 x 3T** fixed
   on the swing axis.
4. Remove the hex head bolt 5/16" x 3/4" and flat washer 5/16" x 23 x 3T that fix the swing axis and the linked
   assembly.
5. Divide the connecting U-seat from the swing axis and linked assembly, then remove the swing axis.
6. Use a 12 mm wrench to release the **5/16" x 3/4" hex head bolt** and **Ø8.5 x 26 x 2T flat washer** fixed on the
   linked assembly and the crank arm, and separate them.
7. Reassemble in the reverse order. When the swing axis goes back, the notch plane must be connected with the
   U-slot gap plane alignment, then the screws.

**The CS800 (2020) book (cs800-2021) prints it in three moves**: chain covers off; remove the outside hex head
bolt and washer locking the axle, then remove the axle; remove the bolt locking the linked assembly to the
crank, then take off the linked assembly; *make sure axle and U bracket alignment before tightening the bolts*.
No eye bolt is named.

The crank arm, idler wheel and flywheel procedures all start by sending you here. The Sole SC200 linked-assembly
card prints the XS200-SS003 wording.
