**Step 1:**

1. Attach the Leveler (11) to the Stabilizer (3/4) and the Bottom Cross Brace (2).
2. Attach the Stabilizer (3/4) and Bottom Cross Brace (2) to the Stand Legs (1), using four M10x70mm Bolts (16), eight O10mm Washers (20) and four M10 Nylon Nuts (22).

Note: **DO NOT COMPLETELY TIGHTEN THE HARDWARE AT THIS TIME.**

**Step 2:** Attach the Center Cross Bar (5) to the Stand Legs (1), using two M8x65mm Bolts (17), four 8mm Washers (21) and two M8 Nylon Nuts (23).

Nothing is fully tightened until step 5.
