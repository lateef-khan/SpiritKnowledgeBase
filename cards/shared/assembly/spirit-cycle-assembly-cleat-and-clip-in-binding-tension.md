- **Engage:** place the cleat between the bindings and **push down**.
- **Disengage:** **twist the heel outwards**, away from the bike.
- **Binding tension** is adjustable and should be set so that the cleat and shoe do not
  disengage while pedalling. Use an **Allen key** on the tension adjusting screws -
  **clockwise increases** binding tension, **counterclockwise decreases** it.

The CB900 calls the shoe interface a **cleat** and labels a Front Binding and a Rear
Binding on its drawing; the CIC850 calls the same thing an **SPD clip-in** and labels
its drawing "SPD Clip-in View". The instruction is otherwise word for word.

**No Allen key size is given**, and neither carton lists a tool for this: the CB900 ships
a 6mm Allen wrench and a combination M5 Allen/Phillips driver, the CIC850 a 4mm and a
5mm Allen wrench. None of them is named for the binding screws.

## These pedals have a strap side as well

Both machines fit a dual-face pedal: cleats on one side, a caged nylon strap on the
other. For the strap side see
[the CB900 card](spirit-cycle-assembly-pedal-straps-with-a-spring-loaded-clasp.md) and
[the CIC card](spirit-cic-assembly-pedal-straps-and-toe-clips.md).

## The CIC800 and both XIC600 printings have no clip-in instruction

The words cleat, clip-in and SPD appear nowhere in those three books.
