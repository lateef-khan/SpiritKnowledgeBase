1. Use the wrench to turn the **left pedal clockwise** and the **right pedal counterclockwise** to take both pedals off.
2. Turn the pedals the opposite way to refit them.
3. Take off the **crank arm end cap (28)**, then use a **T-wrench or plug wrench** to release the nut.
4. Take the **crank arms (51L, 51R)** off: the 2023 books say "use a crank puller"; the 2016 and ENT books say "use proper tool to secure crank arms and release them with hex wrench".
5. To refit the crank arms (51L, 51R), use a power tool or hammer to seat them, return the **nut (108)** to the **crank axle (8)** and tighten, and put the crank arm end cap (28) back.

**Wrench for the pedals:** the 2016 and ENT books print "13/15 mm wrench", the 2023 books "a 15 mm wrench". It is the 15 mm end of the same combination wrench.

**The nut (108) is not sized in any XBR book.** The 2020 CR800 books print it as M10 x 1.0 and the 2009 CR800 as M10 x 1.25; the 2008 XBR dealer parts list prints part 108 as M10x1.0. No torque is printed for the nut or the pedals; the owner's manuals say only to tighten the pedals as much as possible.

The left pedal is left-hand threaded - it comes off clockwise.
