**Belt tension on the CE900 family is a force, 280 to 310 N, not the 190 Hz frequency the XE books use.** The reed
switch goes back 2 mm from the magnet.

1. Refer to procedures 6.7 (crank), 6.6 (left and right chain cover) and 6.10 (shaft connecting rod) to remove them.
   Remove the **nut** and **hook type screws** (figures 6.11.1 and 6.11.2).
2. Remove the belt (figure 6.11.3). **When the belt is installed in the middle of the belt [pulley], belt tension is
   maintained at 280 ~ 310 N.**
3. Press the **roller (3 pcs)** when the roller is locked to the flywheel (figure 6.11.4); refer to figures 6.11.1 to
   6.11.3 to disassemble the belt.
4. The flywheel's **4 screws** can be removed to remove the flywheel (figure 6.11.5).
5. Refer to procedures 6.11.1 to 6.11.4 for the installation of the drive system parts.
6. Loosen the **reed switch screw** and remove it (figures 6.11.6 and 6.11.7). **Install it with the belt on, with a
   distance of 2 mm from the magnet.**
7. Refer to procedures 6.11.1 to 6.11.6 for the installation of the transmission system parts.

The same figure is repeated in 7.1 Belt Slips/Falls off ("the tension of the pressure roller can be adjusted ...
belt tension maintained at 280 ~ 310N"), which is the maintenance question. The three books print this section
word for word; the CU900ENT bike's template prints a different tension (180 to 210 N).
