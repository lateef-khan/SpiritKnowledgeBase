**Three turns, wound from the right, and 9 to 10 cm from the centre of the cable head to the screw.** Both books
print the same winding rule and the same distance; the wording below is the 7.5S book's, with the 8.5S
differences after it.

**Removal (7.5S: 6.5.3).** Loosen the **socket head cap bolt on the roller assembly** to release the rear
connecting cable (figure 6.5.7). Remove the **spring-side cable head** (figure 6.5.8). Remove the bolt on the other
end of the steel lock to remove the cable (figure 6.5.9).

**Refit (7.5S: 6.5.4).**

1. Install the new cable on the copper sleeve of the plastic slide (figure 6.5.10) and lock it onto the **pedal
   plate** (figure 6.5.11).
2. Slide the new wire around the fixed rear sliding wheel set (figure 6.5.12) and move the sliding platform to the
   rear (figure 6.5.13).
3. **Wind from right to left** (figure 6.5.14). Make sure the cable in the **first groove is completely inside the
   groove**, then wind **three turns** (figures 6.5.15, 6.5.16). *On the other side the winding must also start
   from the right side of the cable pulley.*
4. Pass the cable head through the **hook of the spring** (figure 6.5.17), adjust the tension of the wheel set
   (figure 6.5.18), and set the **centre of the cable head 9 to 10 cm from the contour screw** (figure 6.5.19).

**The 8.5S book (6-5, step 3 onward) prints the same job for its "Slide Wheel (Urethane)"**: loosen the screw on
the slide wheel to release the tension, remove the cable end on the spring side, remove the bolt at the other
end; then STEP 1 assemble the bolt, spring and washer in the order shown and tighten the pedal plate (R), then
(L); STEP 2 route the cable around the slide wheel and move both pedal plates to the rearmost position; STEP 3
right to left, first groove seated, wrap three times, the other side also from the right; STEP 4 cable end into
the spring hook, adjust the slide wheel tension, centre-to-bolt distance **9 to 10 cm**. Its "as shown in figure
6.5.1, move the pedal plates" in STEP 2 points at the front-cable figure; a misprint in the source.

Neither book gives a tension figure for the rear cable; only the front cable has one (40 to 50 lb).
