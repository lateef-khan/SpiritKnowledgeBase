1. *(XBR books)* Take the **left and right bottle holders (38, 39)** and the **release lever (40)** off. The 2009 XR898 book starts at step 2.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **four M6 x 15 mm Phillips head screws (98)** and take the **seat (61)** off.
3. Use **two 14 mm open-end wrenches** to release **two 3/8" x 2-3/4" hex head bolts (175)**, **four 3/8" x 2" hex head bolts**, **six 3/8" x 7T nyloc nuts (89)** and **six 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washers**, which secure the **seat carriage (4)**, to take the **seat handle bar (6)** off.
4. Take off the **HGP wire grommet (126)** that secures the **hand-pulse assemblies (27)** (the 2009 book: 21, 27). Use the Phillips head screwdriver to release **four Ø3 x 20 mm tapping screws** and take the hand-pulse assemblies apart.
5. Reverse the steps to refit.

**The 2020 CR800 and CR800ENT books print the four shorter bolts as 3/8" x 1-3/4" (71)** where these seven books print 3/8" x 2"; the long pair (175), the nuts and the washers match. That version is the linked card.

The seat handle bar's bolts run through the seat carriage, which stays on its track. The hand-pulse assembly wiring runs down inside the rear shrouds; the rear-shroud card is where the 2100 mm hand-pulse wire and the switch cable are unplugged.
