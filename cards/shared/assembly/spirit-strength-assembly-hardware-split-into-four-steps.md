Every one of these thirty-six manuals prints the same two-step unpacking instruction:

1. **Cut the straps, then lift the box over the unit** and unpack. Remove all parts from
   the shipping carton and the foam inserts and verify that all parts are included in the
   shipment.
2. **Locate the hardware package. The hardware is separated into four steps.** Remove the
   hardware for each step as needed to avoid confusion.

**The numbers in parenthesis (#) in the instructions are the item number from the
assembly drawing**, not part numbers you can order against.

## "Four steps" is the template's number, not the machine's

All thirty-six books print *separated into four steps* word for word. **Almost none of
them has a four-step build.** The step panels run to six on the abdominal, seven on the
squat/standing calf, eight on the biceps curl and the triceps extension and ten on the
leg extension and the seated leg curl; the bench-style CSF units run to three, four or
five, and the two i-Strength machines to four and six. Count the panels in the book in
front of you and ignore the sentence.

## Three of them add a step before the straps are cut

The functional trainer and both i-Strength machines are shipped standing and open with
**"Position the shipping carton so the Heavy End is located at the bottom"**, which makes
cutting the straps step 2 and locating the hardware step 3. See
[the heavy-end card](spirit-strength-assembly-heavy-end-of-the-carton-goes-down.md).

## Where the tools come from splits the range in two

Twenty-three of these books tell you to supply three tools yourself; thirteen say the
tools are in the carton and to take them out first. The split does not follow the
product family. See
[the supply-your-own card](spirit-strength-assembly-three-tools-you-must-supply.md) and
[the tools-in-the-carton card](spirit-strength-assembly-tools-are-in-the-carton.md).
