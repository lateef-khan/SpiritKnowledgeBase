Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. This is the CU800 chassis
with a touchscreen: the item numbers and the hardware are the CU800 2021 numbers, and
the steps are rewritten. Hardware quantities are on
[the hardware card](spirit-cu800-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Step one - rear stabilizer

1. Install the **Rear Stabilizer (No.5)** onto the Main Frame with 2 Bolts (No.50) and
   2 Washers (No.151).

## Step two - console mast and its cables

1. Slide the **Console Mast Cover (No.38)** through the **Console Mast (No.2)**.
2. Making sure not to pinch the wires, run them through the Console Mast (No.2) and
   Console Mast Cover (No.38). The wires are: **Computer Cable (No.29)**, **CSAFE
   Connecting Wire (No.33)**, **Network Connecting Cable (No.144)** and, on the 2022
   printing only, **HDMI Connecting Wire (No.147)**.
3. Secure the Console Mast (No.2) to the **Main Frame (No.1)** using **6 Bolts (No.51)
   and 6 Flat Washers (No.72) on the sides of the mast. On the back of the mast use 1
   Bolt (No.51) and 1 Curved Washer (No.99).**

Seven bolts in all, as on the CU800 2021 - but this step says which six go where, and
puts the odd one at the **back** where the older book says the front.

## Step three - handlebar

1. Thread the **Handpulse w/Cable (No.26)**, the **Handpulse w/Cable (No.28)** and the
   Computer Cable (No.29) through the Console Mast (No.2).
2. Secure the **Handlebar (No.3)** to the Console Mast (No.2) using 2 Bolts (No.51), 2
   Split Washers (No.103) and 2 Flat Washers (No.72).

Neither printing tells you to snap a plastic cover over the joint, and neither carton
packs a handlebar cover.

## Step four - console, beverage holder, pedals and seat

1. Connect the wires on the back of the console to their places: Computer Cable
   (No.29), Handpulse Wires (No.26 & 28), CSAFE Connecting Wire (No.33), Network
   Connecting Cable (No.144) and, on the 2022 printing only, HDMI Connecting Wire
   (No.147). Tuck excess cable into the Console Mast (No.2), careful not to pinch the
   wires.
2. Secure the **Console (No.34)** to the Console Mast (No.2) using 4 Screws (No.58)
   with the Screwdriver (No.93).
3. Fasten the **Beverage Holder (No.116)** to the Console Mast (No.2) using 2 Screws
   (No.58).
4. Install the **Pedals (No.45L/R)** onto the **Crank Arms (No. 16L/16R)** carefully
   using the Wrench (No.92) to hold the pedal pivot, then firmly fasten the pivot
   clockwise.
5. Install the **Seat (No.19)** onto the **Sliding Seat Mount (No.7)**, then fasten the
   attached nut using the Wrench (No.100).

**Item 4 prints no reverse-thread warning and no torque figure**, where the CU800 2021
and 2012 books on the same chassis print the warning. See
[the pedal card](spirit-bike-ent-assembly-pedal-no-thread-warning.md).

## The HDMI cable is the only difference between the two printings

The 2024 book drops **HDMI Connecting Wire (No.147)** from both step 2 and step 4 and
changes nothing else in any of the four steps - the hardware tables, the item numbers
and the wording are otherwise identical. Its chapter reflows onto larger pages, puts
each hardware table below its step and renames the headings from CU800ENT STEP ONE to
ASSEMBLY - STEP ONE.

The build ends here. There is no first-power-on step: these bikes are
generator-powered and the console starts when you pedal.
