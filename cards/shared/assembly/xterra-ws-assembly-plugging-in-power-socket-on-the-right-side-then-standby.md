Insert the power cord into the **socket on the right side of the treadmill**. Plug in the power supply
and turn on the power switch of the machine. The treadmill will now enter **standby state**.

That is the whole paragraph. Unlike the full-size Xterra treadmills, the book says nothing about the
safety key being needed to power on, a lamp test or a software version - and the switch is described
only as "the power switch of the machine", by the socket on the right. The folding and moving
instructions follow on the next two pages.
