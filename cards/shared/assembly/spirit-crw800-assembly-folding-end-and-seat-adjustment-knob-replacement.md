**Two rules the book prints in bold.** Never have both M8 x 15L socket head cap bolts (98) out at once - fit a new
one in the first hole before you take the second. And the 22L knob nut (46) is factory thread-locked; **add 1 to
2 cc of thread-locker** when it goes back.

1. Remove the **Aluminum Track (51)** and the **Pedal (L) (R)**.
2. Use the **6 mm wrench** to take apart the **M8 x P1.25 x 15L socket head cap bolts (98), 2 pcs**, and the
   **Folding End Assembly (6)**. *It is not allowed to take out both pieces of bolt (98): get a new M8 x P1.25 x
   15L socket head cap bolt (98) to lock the original position, then follow the same steps for the other one.*
3. Use the **10 mm wrench** to take apart the **M6 x 6T nyloc nut (103)**.
4. Use the **22 mm wrench** to take off the **22L x M16 x M22 x 37L knob nut (46)** and the **Seat Up/Down
   Adjustment Knob (47)**. *The knob nut (46) is fixed by thread-locker; add 1 to 2 cc of thread-locker when you
   reverse the steps.*
5. Use the **13 mm wrench** to remove the **M8 x P1.25 x 13T cap nuts (100), 2 pcs**, and the **M8 x P1.25 x 20L
   hex head bolts (99), 2 pcs**, and take the **Tension Spring (48)** and the **Main Frame (1)** apart.
6. Use the **8 mm wrench** to remove the **3/8" x UNC16 x 1" socket head cap bolts (101), 2 pcs**, and the
   **Ø3/8" x 20 x 3.0T flat washers (102), 2 pcs**, and take the **Seat Stop Axle (15)** and the Tension Spring
   (48) apart.
7. Reverse the steps to refit the parts.

The CRW800 (2020) and CW800-YR001 books print this identically; the Sole SR500 (2016) book prints the same seven
steps. **The XRW600 has a release lever and a dual torsion spring instead of a knob**, and its own card. If the
seat knob squeaks, the troubleshooting chapter says to tighten it and put some lubrication on the screw.
