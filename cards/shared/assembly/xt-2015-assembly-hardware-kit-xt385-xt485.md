These manuals print no single hardware panel. Each step page carries its own
table; put together they read:

| Step | Item | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 128 | Nut Clip | M5 | 6 |
| 2 | 130 | Bolts | 5/16" x 15MM | 8 |
| 3 | 120 | Tapping Screws | 5" x 16MM | 6 |
| 4 | 133 | Bolts | 3/8" x 1-1/2" | 4 |
| 4 | 113 | Split Washer | Ø10 x 2.0T | 4 |
| 5 | 142 | Sheet Metal Screws | Ø3.5 x 16L | 8 |
| 6 | 150 | Bolts | 5/16" x 3/4" | 4 |

**The XT385-2015 and XT485-2015 kits are identical**, item number for item number
and quantity for quantity. The beverage holder the XT485-2015 fits in step six
needs no fastener of its own in these tables.

Item 120 is described **"5" X 16MM"**, mixing an inch mark with a millimetre
length; the same odd description is printed for item 88 in the XT185-2015 and
XT285-2015 manuals.

**Watch item 142 and item 132 across manuals.** Item 142 is the Ø3.5 x 16L sheet
metal screw here and in the XT185-2015 and XT285-2015 manuals, but on the
XT685-2015 the same screw is item 125. Item 132 in these two manuals is the Allen
Wrench, while on the XT185-2015 and XT285-2015 it is the M5 speed nut clip.

Against the 2023 XT385 and XT485 kit, three lines are unchanged - **130 (8 pcs),
133 with 113 (4 pcs each) and 150 (4 pcs)**. Item 120 survives as a number but is
described there as an M5 x 15L Phillips head screw, not a tapping screw, and the
2023 kit adds items 160 and 166 for the two-piece console and the tablet holder,
which this machine does not have.
