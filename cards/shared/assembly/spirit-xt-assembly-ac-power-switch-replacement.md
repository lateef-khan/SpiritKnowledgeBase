The whole procedure, in every one of these eleven books, is one sentence and a photograph.

- 2015 books and XT485ENT: **"Disconnect wiring to AC power switch, replace AC power switch and reconnect
  wiring."**
- 2023 books: **"Unplug AC Power switch wires to replace and plug wires back."**

Nothing else is printed: no screws, no location, no part number, no note on which wire goes to which
terminal, and no instruction to unplug the machine first. The CT850 and CT800 commercial books print a real
procedure for the same job - a seven-screw front cover and an overload protective device - and that is a
different card.

The breaker procedure printed beside this one is the same sentence with the breaker named instead.
