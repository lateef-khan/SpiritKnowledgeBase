1. Fix the **tablet holder (D)** to the underside of the console using **six cross-recessed countersunk
   head screws** (the six 4.2 x 19 mm bolts on the pack list).
2. Place the **red safety key (E)** onto the **yellow area on the underside of the console**.

**Adjusting the angle:** slide the back panel of the bracket in the direction of the arrow to adjust the
angle, turning it to a suitable angle.

This is the last assembly step; the setting-up page (plugging in and powering on) follows. The book
does not name a screwdriver, and neither of the two Allen keys on the pack list is called for here.
