Five bullets, the same in both books:

- Tighten all bolts/nuts securely to complete assembly.
- The bike can be cleaned with a damp cloth and mild non-abrasive detergent. **Do not use solvents.**
- Make sure to tighten both pedals securely to the pedal crank arms as instructed.
- **Store the bike indoors.** Excessive moisture can cause rust to the frame and damage to the electronics.
- Place the bike in such a way that there is room on all sides of the bike to safely mount and dismount. **We recommend a minimum of 2 feet all around the bike** to safely access the bike.

The same page carries "Adjusting Resistance" (the tension knob, level 1 the minimum) and "Transporting the Bike" (the linked card). Neither book has a levelling instruction: the rear stabilizer end caps are drawn but never called adjustable.
