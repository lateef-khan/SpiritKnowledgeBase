1. Follow the user manual to take off the upright.
2. Remove the **round caps** of the handle bar, then pull the **hand pulse and rapid key wires** out of the handle
   bar.
3. Use a screwdriver to remove the **hand pulse screws**, then take off the hand pulse set.
4. Pull out the handle bar **rapid key cap**. *The rapid key cap needs to be aligned when installing.*
5. Reverse the steps to install all parts.

The upright itself comes off by the owner's-manual build in reverse - the service manual does not repeat it.
The rapid keys are the handle bar's thumb switches (the interface-board wiring page labels them THUMB SWITCH);
their wiring runs inside the bar with the pulse wires. No screw sizes are printed.

The XS300B-YS006 service manual is one book for both XS895 owner's-manual printings; the two-column page is read by step number.
