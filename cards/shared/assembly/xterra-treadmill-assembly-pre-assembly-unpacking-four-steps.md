After the shipping-strap warning, the PRE-ASSEMBLY page gives four steps:

1. Cut the straps, then lift the box over the unit and unpack.
2. Carefully remove all parts from the carton and inspect for any damage or missing parts. **If parts
   are damaged or missing, contact your dealer immediately.**
3. Locate the hardware package. **Remove the tools first.**
4. Remove the hardware for each step as needed to avoid confusion. The numbers in parenthesis (#) in
   the instructions are the item numbers from the assembly drawing.

The TRX books print a different unpacking paragraph (banding straps, a hardware pack split into
sections); see the linked card.
