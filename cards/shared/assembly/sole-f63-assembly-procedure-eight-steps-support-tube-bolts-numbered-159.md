Eight illustrated assembly steps (hardware item numbers in brackets):

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill from the carton onto a smooth, level floor with the rear at least 3 ft from any wall. Do not remove the Velcro belt until the plastic wrap and Styrofoam are out from under the unit; turning it on its side with the belt off can let it fold up and cause serious injury.
2. Fit six Speed Nut Clips (128) on the frame base, slide the lower handlebar covers (142L & 144R) onto the console masts, slide the Frame Base Caps (62L & 63R) over the upright tubes (4 & 5), and connect Lower Computer Cable (49) to Middle Computer Cable (50) on the right side.
3. Bolt the upright tubes (4 & 5) to the frame base (2) with eight Button Head Socket Bolts (130); leave them loose until step 7 is finished. Do not pinch the cables in the tubing.
4. Connect Speed Adjustment Switch Cable (54) to Speed Cable Upper (37), Incline Adjustment Switch Cable (55) to Incline Cable Upper (38), and Middle Computer Cable (50) to Computer Cable Upper (48); tuck excess cable into the handrail tubing.
5. Insert the Console Assembly (52) into the uprights and fix with four Button Head Socket Bolts (139) and four Split Washers (113) using the M6 L wrench.
6. Fix the left (141 & 142) and right (143 & 144) upper and lower handlebar covers over the upright tops with six Sheet Metal Screws (175).
7. Bolt the support tube (26) between the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (159), set the Beverage Holder (158) on it, place the safety-key magnet (75) between Start and Stop, and check every bolt and screw is tight.
8. Finish tightening the mast bolts left loose in step 2 and fix the console mast covers (62 & 63) to the frame base with six Tapping Screws (120).

In the 2016 printing the lower handlebar covers are numbered 64 & 65 (step 2) and the step-6 covers are the left (64) and right (65), versus 142L/144R and 141-144 in the 2013 printing; the step-2 cable numbers are Lower (50)/Middle (49) and Middle (49)/Upper (48) versus Lower (49)/Middle (50) and Middle (50)/Upper (48) in 2013. Kit, order and all bolt sizes match, so one card holds both printings.
