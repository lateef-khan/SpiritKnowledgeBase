1. Take the pedals and crank arms off. Separate the **console mast cover** and the **saddle cover** from the left and right shrouds, then use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **seven Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet-metal screws (63)** and **three 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (61)** that secure the left shroud, and take the left shroud off.
2. On the right side, unscrew the **4 x 19 mm self-tapping screw (64)** with its **5 x 15 x 1.5T flat washer (57)** on the main frame, and **3 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (61)** on the right side case, to release it.
3. To refit, fit the **right side case (37)** on the main frame and tighten one 4 x 19 mm self-tapping screw (64) with its 5 x 15 x 1.5T flat washer (57) **loosely for now**. **Align the centre of the wheel with the centre of the axle**, secure with 3 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (61), then add one more 4 x 19 mm self-tapping screw (64) with a 5 x 15 x 1.5T flat washer (57). Match the **left side case (36)** to the right side case (37) and secure with **7 pcs of 3.5 x 12 mm self-tapping screws (63)** and 3 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (61). Refit the cranks and pedals.

**The same screw (63) is printed as Ø3.5 x 16 mm in step 1 and 3.5 x 12 mm in the refit step**, in all four books. The count (seven) and the part number are consistent; the length is not. The 2012 CU800 book prints Ø3.5 x 12 mm for this screw.

**Per-book differences.** The CU800 (2020) and CU800ENT books print step 2 in full (the left-side screws again, then the 4 x 19 mm screw on the main frame, then the three right-side screws). The XBU55 2016 and 2023 books shorten step 2 to "Remove 3 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screw to take chain cover apart" and drop the "loosely for now" wording from the refit, but keep the same fasteners.

The alignment step (wheel centred on the axle) is the only positioning instruction printed; a cover fitted off-centre rubs the crank, which is the noise cause the troubleshooting section names.

**The Spirit Medical 4.0U (FU800-SB022-03 book) prints the XBU55 form**: the shortened step 2 ("Remove 3 pcs of 5 x 16 m/m Tapping Screw to take Chain Cover (R) apart"), the full refit with the 4 x 19 mm self tapping screw and 5 x 15 x 1.5T flat washer fitted "loosely for now", the wheel centred on the axle, and the same seven-screw length drift (Ø3.5 x 16 off, 3.5 x 12 back on). No part numbers are printed in its steps; its parts list carries seven Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet metal screws (63) and six Ø5 x 16 mm tapping screws (61).
