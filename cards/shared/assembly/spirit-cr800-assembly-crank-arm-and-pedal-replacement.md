**The three books disagree on the crank nut's thread pitch.** The 2020 CR800 and CR800ENT books print the nut (108) as **M10 x 1.0**; the 2009 XR898 book prints the same part as **M10 x 1.25**; the 2008 XBR25/XBR55 dealer parts list prints part 108 as "M10x1.0_Nut, Crank Arm". Check the nut before ordering.

1. Use the **13/15 mm wrench** to turn the **left pedal (116) clockwise** and the **right pedal (117) counterclockwise** to remove both pedals.
2. Turn the pedals the opposite way to refit them.
3. Take off the **crank arm end cap (28)** and release the **M10 nut (108)** with the wrench (the 2009 book names it a T wrench or plug wrench).
4. Use the plug tool that matches the **cranks (51L, 51R)** and turn a hex wrench through it to release the cranks.
5. To refit the cranks (51L, 51R), use a hammer (the 2009 book: "power tool or hammer") to seat the cranks tightly on the **drive pulley axle (8)**, turn the nut tightly onto the axle, and plug in the crank arm end cap.

**The left pedal is left-hand threaded** - it comes off clockwise. No torque is printed for the pedals or the crank nut.

**The 2009 XR898 page carries a stray paragraph** after step 5 - "1. Crank Axle symmetrically at each side, use air hammer to secure the Cranks and then use 14mm wrench to tighten the nut..." - which is the 2012 upright (XU878) crank text pasted onto the recumbent page. Its 14 mm wrench does not fit this nut; the numbered steps above are the recumbent procedure.
