These five books carry a small **Correct / Wrong** panel with no caption - on a CABLE
ROUTING DIAGRAM page in four of them, on the step 7 assembly page of the leg extension/leg
curl. It is the only place in the range that names the **cable limiting post**, and
nothing on the page explains what it is for.

**What the two drawings show**, read off a 600 dpi render:

- **Correct** - the cable runs straight past the pulley on the tangent, and the limiting
  post stands clear of it beside the pulley rim.
- **Wrong** - the cable has climbed around the pulley rim and is pinched between the
  pulley and the post.

The post is a short pin beside each pulley that stops the cable jumping out of the groove.
If the cable is threaded on the wrong side of it, the cable rubs the post instead of
running in the sheave, and the panel exists because that is easy to do while the machine
is still half built.

**There is no text at all in the panel** beyond the two labels, and no note anywhere else
in these books refers to it. Compare the picture in the book in front of you.

## Which books print it, and where

| Machine | Page | Where it sits |
|---|---|---|
| CSD ab crunch / back extension | printed 20 (PDF 21) | its own CABLE ROUTING DIAGRAM page, both printings |
| CSD biceps / triceps extension | printed 17 (PDF 18) | its own CABLE ROUTING DIAGRAM page |
| CSD chest press / shoulder press | printed 21 (PDF 22) | its own CABLE ROUTING DIAGRAM page, both printings |
| CSD leg press / calf extension | printed 20 (PDF 21) | its own CABLE ROUTING DIAGRAM page, both printings |
| CSD leg extension / leg curl | printed 18 (PDF 19) | on the **step 7 assembly page**, not on a routing page, in both 2025 printings |

**The inner/outer thigh and the pull-up/dip assist do not print it.** Their cable routing
page is the two-row A/B legend and nothing else - checked by grep on the whole text and
confirmed on a render of PDF p. 18 of each 2025 revision.

The page that names the two wires is a different page - see
[the cable routing legend card](spirit-strength-assembly-cable-routing-diagram-names-two-wires.md).
Getting the cable into the groove at all means taking the pulley off first on some
machines - see
[the pulley card](spirit-strength-assembly-take-the-pulley-off-to-route-the-cable.md).
