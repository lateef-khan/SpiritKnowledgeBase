1. Plug the treadmill into an appropriate wall outlet.
2. Turn on the power switch, **located at the front of the treadmill below the
   motor hood**.
3. **Ensure the Safety Key is installed** - the treadmill will not power on
   without it.

At first power-up, in this order:

1. The console performs an **internal self-test**; during it all the lights turn
   on.
2. When the lights go off, the **Message Window shows a software version**
   (i.e.: VER 1.0) and the **Distance window shows an odometer reading** - how
   many virtual miles the treadmill has gone.
3. The **Time window shows how many hours** the treadmill has been used.
4. The odometer and time stay up **for only a few seconds**, then the console
   goes to the startup display: the **dot matrix scrolls through the different
   workout profiles** and the Message Window scrolls the start-up message.
5. You may now begin to use the console.

**This is the self-test startup, not the shorter one.** The XT185-2023 and
XT285-2023 do not run this self-test; their consoles light up briefly, show
Time and Distance odometer readings, scroll the software version in the Message
Center and drop straight into idle mode. That shorter sequence is the one held
on the CT850-2020 card.

**A caution about the XT685ENT.** Its manual prints all of the above word for
word, on the same SETTING UP page - but that machine has a **touchscreen**, and
the words Message Window, Distance window, Time window and dot matrix describe
the segment-and-dot-matrix console of the non-ENT XT685. The plug-in and
power-switch half of this card is safe for it; treat the four windows as
boilerplate carried over from the other manual rather than as a description of
what an ENT screen shows. **The XT485ENT is not on this card at all**: its
manual describes a brief loading screen followed by a WELCOME screen, which is a
different sequence on its own card.
