*(Tool: Phillips screwdriver)*

1. Remove the **3 screws (4)** on the left side and the **3 screws (5)** on the right side.
2. After removing the **6 screws (1)**, the front panel and the light strip can be taken off.

Twelve screws in all. The light strip is cable 11 in the cable chapter.

The "Digital Resistance Module" is the i-Strength line's motorised resistance box, part 72 in the CSI owner's manuals; the screw numbers are the call-outs on the manual's exploded drawings.
