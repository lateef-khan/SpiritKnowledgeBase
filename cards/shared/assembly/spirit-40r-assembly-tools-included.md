Five tools, the same five in both books:

- **14/15mm wrench**
- **12/14mm wrench**
- **13m/m wrench**
- **Phillips head screw driver**
- **Combination M6 Allen wrench & Phillips head screw driver**

**Two more than the CR800 carton**, which holds the 14/15mm, the 12/14mm and the screwdriver only. The 13 mm wrench and the combination M6 Allen wrench are for the parts this build has that the CR800's does not: the two **M8 x 25 socket head cap bolts (113)** on the front handlebars take the Allen end, and the service manual uses a 13 mm wrench on the M8 x 7T nuts of the belt J-bolt and seat carriage. The parts list of the service manual numbers them **112 (12/14mm wrench), 132 (14/15mm wrench), 123 (13m/m wrench), 114 (Phillips head screw driver) and 122 (combination M6 Allen wrench and Phillips head screw driver)**.

The steps never name a tool by item number. **A 14mm is in this carton** in the 14/15mm and the 12/14mm, which is the size the machine care page asks for on the levellers - although the setting-up page asks for a 1/2" wrench for the same nut; see the levelling card. Nothing in the build takes a torque wrench and none is packed.
