Hardware in the box beside this step: **#93** M5 x 12L Tapping Screw (2 pcs), **#101** 4 x 16L Sheet Metal Screw (3 pcs).

1. Install the Tablet Holder (138) on the Handlebar (6) with **3 pcs of Sheet Metal Screws (101)**, by using the **Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (86)**.

The SB900 2019 wording adds the size inline: "3pcs of 4 x 16m/m Sheet Metal Screws (101)". Same part.

**The two #93 tapping screws are supplied but never used by any printed step in these two manuals.** On the 2020 machines the same step gains a second instruction that fits a Drink Bottle Holder (23) with them.

**The step number is printed inconsistently on the SB700 2019.** The instruction page heads it HARDWARE STEP 5 and prints it as numbered step 5; the assembly pack checklist heads the same two parts HARDWARE STEP 6. On the SB900 2019 both places say step 5.
