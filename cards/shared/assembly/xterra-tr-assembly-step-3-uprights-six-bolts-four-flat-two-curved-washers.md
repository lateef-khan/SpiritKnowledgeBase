Insert the **left and right uprights** into the **frame base** and fix them with **six button head
socket bolts, four flat washers and two curved washers**, using the combination M5 Allen wrench and
Phillips screwdriver. **Fix the screws without tightening them** - they are tightened after the console
support is on. The book notes the step "may work better with a second person assisting".

| Book | Uprights | Frame base | Bolt (x6) | Flat washer (x4) | Curved washer (x2) | Tool |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TR150 2021 | (L, R) (3, 4) | (2) | (87) | (79) | (88) | "SCREW DRIVER (104)" |
| TR260 2022 | (L, R) (3, 4) | (2) | (87) | (79) | (88) "on the rounded side of the upright" | "ALLEN WRENCH" |
| TRX1400 2023 | (L, R) (3, 4) | (2) | (94) 5/16" x 1/2" | (79) Ø5/16" x Ø18 x 1.5T | (95) Ø8 x Ø23 x 1.5T | Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (104) |

Only the TR260 says where the two curved washers go (the rounded side of the upright); the drawings in
all three books put the curved washers on the two front bolts and the flat washers on the four side
bolts. The TR260 page also carries a Chinese/English ghost layer in the PDF that is not printed; the
figures above are from the printed page.
