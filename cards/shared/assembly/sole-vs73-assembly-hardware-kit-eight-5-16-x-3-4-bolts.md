Hardware supplied in the box, identical figures in the VS73-group 2008 and WS73-group 2009 books. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #92 | M5 x 10 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 8 pcs |
| #86 | 3/8 x 2.0T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| #100 | 5/16 x 3/4 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 pcs |
| #101 | 5/16 x 1/2 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 pcs |
| #90 | 3.5 x 12 mm | Self Tapping Screw (2008 book) / Sheet Metal Screw (2009 book) | 4 pcs |
| #91 | 3/8 x 1-3/4 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #62 | - | Ellipse Safety Key | 1 pc |
| #18 | - | Lubricant | 1 pc |
| #103 | - | Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver | 1 pc |
| #104 | - | M6 Allen Wrench | 1 pc |

The 2008 book calls (90) a Self Tapping Screw and the 2009 book a Sheet Metal Screw; same number, size and count, so this card treats them as the same part. The steps consume four of the eight #100 bolts (support brace); the other four have no step use in either book.
