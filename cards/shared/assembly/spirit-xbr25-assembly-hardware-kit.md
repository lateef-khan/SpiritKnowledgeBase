**Both printings list the same items in the same quantities.** The 2019 book tabulates
them; the 2023 book draws them as labelled pictures under Step.1 to Step.4, with item 67
written **3/8" x 4" Hex Head Bolt** and item 65 **3/8" x 53mm Carriage Bolt**.

## The step-1 table and the step-1 text disagree in the 2019 book

The table lists **two** of item 84 and two **Cap Nuts (191)**. Its step-1 text reads
"Install the REAR STABILIZER (7) onto the MAIN FRAME with 2 BOLTS (65) and **4 WASHERS
(84)**" and **never mentions the cap nuts at all**. Two washers are in the bag, and two
nuts the text does not ask for. The 2023 book fixes it: "2 BOLTS (65) and 2 FLAT WASHERS
(84) and 2 CAP NUTS (191)", which is what the XBR55 and XBR95 books have said all along.

## What makes this kit different from the XBR55 and XBR95 kit

Three rows, and they are the reason the two cannot share a card:

- **Item 142, a 3/8" x 4-1/4" bolt, ×2.** No other XB recumbent has it. The XBR25 seat
  back takes four bolts through two sets of holes - two of item 67 through the top holes
  and two of item 142 through the side holes.
- **Item 77, four extra flat washers in step 1**, and **item 89 doubled to four**, for
  those extra bolts.
- **No item 70, 76 or 91 in step 4.** The XBR25 **has no gas cylinder**, so its step four
  holds only the four sheet metal screws for the bottle holders.

Its item 67 is also written **3/8"X4"** where the XBR55 and XBR95 write
**3/8"XUNC16X4"** - the same bolt with the thread spelled out on one book and not the
other. See [the XBR55 and XBR95 hardware card](spirit-xbr-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## The wrench the step names is not in the carton, in the 2023 book

The XBR25 2023 tool panel numbers **#112 (12/14mm), #132 (14/15mm) and #114 (Phillips)**
and its step three then asks for "the WRENCHES provided (**112 & 130**)". There is no
#130 in this carton; #130 is the XBR55's 13/14mm. The 2019 book says 112 & 132 and is
right. See [the tools card](spirit-cr800-assembly-tools-included.md).
