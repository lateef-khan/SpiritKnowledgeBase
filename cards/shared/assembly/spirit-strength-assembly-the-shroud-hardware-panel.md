This is the one hardware panel that is **identical on all twenty-eight weight-stack
machines**. Item numbers vary slightly between books; the descriptions, specifications
and quantities do not.

| No. | Description | Specification | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 002 | Upper shield | | 1 |
| 003 | Shield fixed plate | | 4 |
| 007 | Front shield | | 1 |
| 008 | Back shield | | 1 |
| 015 | Cross recessed countersunk head screw | M5x10 | 8 |
| 018 | Inner Hexagon Socket Head Bolt | M6x16 | 2 |
| 022 | Spring washer | M6 | 2 |
| 025 | Flat washer | Φ12xΦ6.4x1.6 | 2 |

Eight countersunk screws hold the four fixed plates; the two socket bolts with their
spring and flat washers hold the upper shield.

**Nothing else in these books is shared this cleanly.** The door-frame joint takes four
M10x35 bolts on some machines and eight on others, the pulley sets change size and the
plate count changes with the stack, so every other panel has to be read off its own book.

## It is the last step on the selectorized machines, and not always numbered the same

It is step 6 on the abdominal, step 5 on the lat pulldown/seated row, step 8 on the
biceps curl. Find it by its contents, not by its number.

## The functional trainer prints a different shroud panel

Its shields are held by **twenty ST4.2x16 cross recessed pan head tapping screws** with
twenty Φ15xΦ5.3x1.2 big washers, and it has two front shields rather than one. See
[the functional trainer card](../../csf-funt/assembly/csf-funt-assembly-two-towers-and-a-tapping-screw-shroud.md).

The order the covers go on, and the wiring you must connect first, are on
[the shroud order card](spirit-strength-assembly-connect-the-battery-before-the-shrouds-go-on.md).
