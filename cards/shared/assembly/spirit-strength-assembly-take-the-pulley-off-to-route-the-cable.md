**The pulley is pre-installed. Remove it when you route the cable and restore it after the
cable is routed.**

The pulley is fitted at the factory with the cable *outside* it, so on every one of these
machines the cable cannot be laid into the groove without taking the pulley off first. Its
item number varies by machine - 56 on the abdominal, 64 on the standing calf extension -
so read it off your own step table.

**The i-Strength low row says the same thing in one more useful sentence**: *"Remove the
pulley to route the cable between the pulley grooves and cable retaining post."* The
retaining post is what stops the cable jumping the groove, and the cable has to sit
between the two.

## Two dual stations gained this note in their 2025 reprint, and name the pulley

The **ab crunch/back extension** calls it *"The Pulley (62) is pre-installed. Please
remove it when you route the cable and restore it back after cable is routed."* The **leg
press/calf extension** calls it *"The pulley (63) has been pre-installed. Please remove it
and route the cable and then restore the pulley back."* Neither note is in the February
2024 printing of either book. **62 on one machine, 63 on the other** - read it off your
own table.

## The leg press/calf extension says it twice, and one of the two is unillustrated

Its CABLE ROUTING DIAGRAM page adds *"Please remove middle pulley inside the red circle to
be able to route cable and reinstall afterwards"* - but **there is no red circle on that
page**; a 300 dpi render shows the routing drawings with no highlight of any colour. The
**ab crunch/back extension** has the opposite defect: its routing page prints a red circle
around the middle pulley and **no note explaining it**. Read the two pages together: the
circled pulley is the one to take off, and it is the same job as the step 1 note above.

This is a different job from threading the power line through the frame, which is done
earlier in the same step - see
[that card](spirit-strength-assembly-thread-the-cable-before-joining-the-frames.md).
