**The pulley is pre-installed. Remove it when you route the cable and restore it after the
cable is routed.**

The pulley is fitted at the factory with the cable *outside* it, so on every one of these
machines the cable cannot be laid into the groove without taking the pulley off first. Its
item number varies by machine - 56 on the abdominal, 64 on the standing calf extension -
so read it off your own step table.

**The i-Strength low row says the same thing in one more useful sentence**: *"Remove the
pulley to route the cable between the pulley grooves and cable retaining post."* The
retaining post is what stops the cable jumping the groove, and the cable has to sit
between the two.

This is a different job from threading the power line through the frame, which is done
earlier in the same step - see
[that card](spirit-strength-assembly-thread-the-cable-before-joining-the-frames.md).
