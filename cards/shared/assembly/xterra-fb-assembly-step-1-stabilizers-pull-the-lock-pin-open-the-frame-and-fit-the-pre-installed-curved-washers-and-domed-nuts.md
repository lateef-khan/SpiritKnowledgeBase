Hardware: pre-assembled on the main frame, **1 lock pin (#11)**; pre-assembled on the front and rear stabilizers, **4 curved washers (#13)** and **4 domed nuts (#14)**. Tool: the hex head wrench (73).

1. Pull out the lock pin (11) from the main frame (01) - the 2019 and 2025 books call it the Front Support Tube (No. 01) - and adjust the frame to the correct (open) position, then re-insert the lock pin (11) to secure it.
2. Attach the **front stabilizer tube, the one with the wheels (05)**, to the main frame (01) and tighten with 2 curved washers (13) and 2 domed nuts (14).
3. Repeat for the rear stabilizer tube (06).

The five books print the same step with the same part numbers; the 2019 and 2025 books say "the pre-installed 2 Curved Washers", the 2021 books list them in the hardware box as pre-assembled.

The November 2019 service document for the 2019 upright adds the tool: **"Front/Rear Stabilizer - Remove Lock Pin. 2 curved washers & 2 domed nuts are preinstalled on tubes. Tighten with 13 mm wrench."**
