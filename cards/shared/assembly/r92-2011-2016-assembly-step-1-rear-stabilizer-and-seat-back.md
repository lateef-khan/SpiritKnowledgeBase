Hardware: **#65** 3/8" x 2-1/4" Hex Head Bolt (4 pcs), **#77** 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T Flat Washer (6 pcs), **#84** 3/8" x 25 x 2T Flat Washer (4 pcs), **#176** 3/8" x 3/4" Hex Head Bolt (6 pcs).

1. Attach the Rear Stabilizer (7) onto the Main Frame (1) with **four** Hex Head Bolts (65) and **four** Flat Washers (84), using the **12/14mm Wrench (112)**.
2. Attach the Seat Back Bracket (5) onto the Seat Carriage (4) with **six** Hex Head Bolts (176) and **six** Flat Washers (77), using the **12/14mm Wrench (112)**.

**Four bolts on the rear stabilizer, not two.** From 2019 the R92 uses only two #65 (renamed a carriage bolt) with two #84 washers and two #191 cap nuts, and adds an end-cap instruction. There are no cap nuts at all in this step on the 2011-2016 machines.

**No end caps in this step.** The end-cap instruction for the front stabilizer tube appears only from 2019.
