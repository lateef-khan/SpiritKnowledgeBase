Use the combination M5 Allen wrench and Phillips head screwdriver (102) to tighten **six M5 x 15 mm
Phillips head screws (126)** to secure the **right and left frame base covers (39, 38)** on the **frame
base (2)**.

This is the last hardware step; a "Finishing Assembly" page follows (check the belt lubrication before
first use, and the folding instructions). The TRX1400 fits its base covers with **four** M5 x 15 mm
screws (item 83); the TR65/TR75/TR85 list two frame base covers among the parts but print no step for
them.
