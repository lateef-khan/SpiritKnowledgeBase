1. Remove the motor cover (step 4.2.1 of the guide, 8.43 in the CT1000ENT book: eight umbrella head screws).
2. Remove the **AC power switch ground wire** with a screwdriver.
3. Remove the AC power switch module with the cross screwdriver "and switchboard 8" - printed exactly so in
   all three books. Whether "8" counts screws or names a part is not explained; the figure beside it is the
   only guide.
4. After fitting the new module, reassemble in the order of removal.

The CT900 books cite their figures as "Figure 6.4.1" and "Figure 6.4.2" in the text while labelling them
4.4.1 and 4.4.2 on the page; the CT1000ENT book cites 8.48 and 8.49 correctly. No wire colours other than the
ground are printed, and no part number.

The CT850 and CT800 books' version of this job - a seven-screw front cover and an overload protective device -
is a different procedure on a different card.

The guide is a machine translation. Its words, as used on this card: **electronic watch** is the console,
**cross screwdriver** is a Phillips screwdriver, **umbrella head screws** are the guide's name for the
console and cover screws (no size is printed), and a **17th T-Shirt** or **13th T-sleeve** is a 17 mm or 13 mm
T-handle socket.
