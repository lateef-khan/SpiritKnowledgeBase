1. Replace the **aluminum rail** with the **M6 screw (6 pcs)** attached to it (figure 6.14.1).

That is the whole section. The CE800 / XE795 aluminum track uses three `5/16" x 3/4"` hex bolts and a 12 mm wrench
instead. The three books print this word for word.
