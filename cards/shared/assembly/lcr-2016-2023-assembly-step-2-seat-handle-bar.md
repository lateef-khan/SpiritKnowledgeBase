Hardware: **#71** 3/8" x 1-3/4" Hex Head Bolt (2 pcs), **#77** 3/8" x 19mm x 1.5T Flat Washer (4 pcs), **#89** 3/8" x 7T Nyloc Nut (4 pcs), **#175** 3/8" x 2-3/4" Hex Head Bolt (2 pcs).

1. Install the Seat Handle Bar (6) onto the Seat Carriage (4) with the two Hex Head Bolts (71), two Hex Head Bolts (175), four Flat Washers (77) and four Nyloc Nuts (89), by using the **12/14m/m Wrench (112)** and the **14/15m/m Wrench (132)**.
2. Plug the two Hand pulse W/Cable Assemblies (21) and (27) into the sockets (26) on the **left side rear cover, just under the seat**. **Two of the plugs look the same; these are the hand pulse plugs and can be plugged into either hand pulse socket.** The two socket connectors on the side case below the seat have two that are the same also. **The odd plug and socket is for the switches in the handlebars.**

The 2016 printing names the tools in full (12/14m/m and 14/15m/m); the 2019 and 2023 printings shorten them to "Wrench (112)" and "Wrench (132)". Same tools.

**This contradicts the LCR 2013 manual**, which says the hand pulse connectors are tagged L and R and must go into specific ports. From 2016 onward the manual says either hand pulse plug fits either hand pulse socket.
