1. To listen to an MP3 during your workout, connect the MP3 with the computer (2018 bike: 10; 2024 bike: 6; elliptical: 10) by using the audio wire (87; 86; 85).
2. **Plug adapter into the power receptacle.**

The drawing shows the adapter plugging into a jack on the frame near the front stabilizer. Nothing else is printed on this page: no switch, no self-test and no start-up display. The registration page of each book adds where the jack is ("on the front of the unit near the stabilizer tube"), to let a cold machine warm to room temperature before plugging in, and to unplug when not in use.
