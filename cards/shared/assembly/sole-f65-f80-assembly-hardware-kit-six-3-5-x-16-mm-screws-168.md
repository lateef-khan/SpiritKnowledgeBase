Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step, identical in the F65 2013, F80 / F85 2013, F80 2016 and F85 2016 books. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | #128 | M5 | Speed Nuts Clip | 6 pcs |
| 3 | #130 | 5/16" x 1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 pcs |
| 5 | #133 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #113 | Dia 10 x 2T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 6 | #168 | 3.5 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 6 pcs |
| 7 | #151 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #75 | - | Safety Key | 1 pc |
| 8 | #120 | 5 x 16 mm | Tapping Screw | 6 pcs |

Tools in the box: #131 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver, #132 M6 L Allen Wrench.

This kit differs from the F63 2013 kit in steps 5-7: console bolts #133 (not #139), washers 2T (not 1.5T), cover screws #168 (not #175), support bolts #151 (not #159).
