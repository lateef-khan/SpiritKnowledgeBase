**Only the 2022 book prints this step; the 2019 book and its hardware pack have no tablet holder.**

Install the Tablet Holder (135) to the back of the Console with two Phillips Head Screws (133), two Star Washers (134) and two Flat Washers (136), using the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (127).
