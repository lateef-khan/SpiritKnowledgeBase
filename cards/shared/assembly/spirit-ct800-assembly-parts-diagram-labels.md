The labels are words, not letters, and the manuals do not all use the same words.
There are only three sets of labels between the seven diagrams, so the columns are
headed by the set rather than by one manual:

| CT800 2016 and CT850 2018 | CT800 2020, CT850 2016 and CT850 2020 | CT800ENT and CT850ENT |
|---|---|---|
| CONSOLE | CONSOLE | CONSOLE |
| CONSOLE FAN | CONSOLE FAN | CONSOLE FAN |
| READING RACK | READING RACK | *(not labelled)* |
| HEART RATE GRIPS | HEART RATE GRIPS | HAND PULSE SENSORS |
| DRINK BOTTLE HOLDER | BOTTLE HOLDER | CUP HOLDER |
| SAFETY Button *(CT800 2016)* / SAFETY BUTTON *(CT850 2018)* | SAFETY KEY | SAFETY KEY |
| HANDLEBARS | HANDLEBARS | HANDRAILS |
| CONSOLE MAST | CONSOLE MAST | CONSOLE MAST |
| SIDE RAILS | SIDE RAILS | SIDE RAILS |
| MOTOR COVER | MOTOR COVER | MOTOR COVER |
| RUNNING BELT/DECK | RUNNING BELT/DECK | RUNNING BELT/DECK |
| TREAD-BELT TRACKING | TREAD-BELT TRACKING | TREAD-BELT TRACKING |

**Twelve labels on the LED-console manuals, eleven on the touchscreen ones.** The
CT800ENT and CT850ENT diagrams drop the reading rack and rename three parts; those
machines still have a reading rack, which their own console pages call a
"Tablet-friendly reading rack".

Between the older and newer LED sets only two words change: **SAFETY Button**
becomes **SAFETY KEY**, and **DRINK BOTTLE HOLDER** becomes **BOTTLE HOLDER**. The
same two names drift through the rest of the manuals, which use "safety button",
"safety key" and "safety tether cord" for the same magnetic key.

**The CT850 2018 diagram is the older set, printed a model year after the CT850
2016 used the newer one.** Its machine reverts to DRINK BOTTLE HOLDER and to the
safety-button name, which it sets in full capitals - **SAFETY BUTTON** - where the
CT800 2016 prints **SAFETY Button**. That capitalisation is the only difference
between those two diagrams. Do not read the label set as a date.

The CT850 2016 and CT850 2020 diagrams are word for word the CT800 2020 diagram,
and the CT850ENT diagram is word for word the CT800ENT one.

**The CT800 2012 manual prints no such diagram.** Its equivalent page is a
photograph of the console alone with its features called out, so nothing here is
claimed for that machine.
