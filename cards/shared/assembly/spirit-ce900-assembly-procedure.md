## Step one - console mast, handlebar bracket and cup holder

1. Unwind the wiring harness and run the wires up through the **CONSOLE MAST (9)**.
   Bolt the console mast to the **MAIN FRAME (1)** using 4 **BOLTS (54)**, 4 **LOCK
   WASHERS (57)** and 4 **WASHERS (65)**. **Do not pinch wires.**
2. Run the wires from the **CENTER HANDLEBARS (11)** through the **HANDLEBAR BRACKET
   (10)**, into the console mast (9) and out the top.
3. Install the center handlebars (11) to the handlebar bracket (10) using **4 BOLTS
   (52)**.
4. Install the handlebar bracket (10) to the console mast (9) using **4 BOLTS (54)**.
5. Attach the **CUP HOLDER (99)** to the handlebar bracket (10) using 2 **SELF-TAPPING
   SCREWS (46)** and 2 **WASHERS (58)**.

**Item 52 is not in this step's hardware panel.** The panel supplies twelve of item 54
- eight for items 1 and 4 above - and no item 52 at all. Both printings do this. The
four bolts that hold the centre handlebars to the bracket have no size printed
anywhere in either manual.

## Step two - mast covers and console

1. Install 2 **U-NUTS (78)** to the **LEFT CONSOLE MAST COVER (100)**.
2. Attach the **CONSOLE MAST COVERS (100 & 101)** to the main frame (1) using 2
   **SELF-TAPPING SCREWS (47)**. Use 2 **SCREWS (49)** to connect the covers to each
   other.
3. Plug in all wires from the wiring harness to the **CONSOLE (111)**.
4. Install the console (111) to the console mast (9) using **4 SCREWS (49)**. Be
   careful not to pinch any wires.

*Item 1 names the left cover as item 100 and item 2 names the pair as 100 and 101. The
right-hand cover, item 101, gets no U-nuts.*

## Step three - side handlebars onto the swing arms

1. Install the **LEFT SIDE HANDLEBAR (2)** to the **SWING ARM (12)**:
   - a) First install 2 **longer BOLTS (44)** with a **WASHER (63)** on both sides of
     the swing arm (12) and a **NUT (71)**. **Do not tighten yet.**
   - b) Then install 1 **SHORTER BOLT (43)** with a washer (63) through the swing arm
     (12) and the **CROSSTRAINING HANDLEBAR (2)**, using a **CURVED WASHER (68)** and a
     **NUT (71)** on the back side.
   - c) **Tighten after all hardware is installed.**
2. Repeat the process for the **RIGHT SIDE HANDLEBAR (3)**.

*Item 2 is called the LEFT SIDE HANDLEBAR in step 1 and the CROSSTRAINING HANDLEBAR in
step 1b. Same part.*

## Step four - console bracket covers and swing arm hinge covers

1. Attach the **CONSOLE BRACKET COVERS (91 & 92)** to the console mast (9) using 2
   **SCREWS (49)**. Connect the covers together using 2 **SELF-TAPPING SCREWS (46)**.
2. Attach the left **SWING ARM HINGE COVER (93)** to the swing arm (12) using 2
   **SCREWS (49)**. Repeat for the right side.

## The 2021 and 2025 builds are the same build

All four steps, every item number, every count and every hardware panel are word for
word identical between the two printings. **Nothing about assembling this machine
changed.**

What did change is around the build: the 2025 manual adds a lettered whole-machine
diagram and prints the CT900-style four-item pre-assembly list, where the 2021 manual
prints the CT800-style two-item one. See
[the diagram card](../../ce900-2025/assembly/ce900-2025-assembly-parts-diagram-labels.md).

## It is a cup holder in the build and a water bottle holder on the diagram

Step one fits a **CUP HOLDER (99)**, and both carton lists say "1 Cup Holder". The
2025 manual's own parts diagram labels the same object **"F. Water Bottle Holder"**.
It is one part with two names in one book. It is **not** the clamp-and-holder
assembly the CE800 and CE850 fit to the console mast - different part, different
place, different screws. See
[the bottle holder card](spirit-ce-assembly-water-bottle-holder.md).

## Nothing here tells you to fit the levellers

The carton list has no levellers and no step fits any, yet the noise item in both
manuals says there are **six levelling pads on the bottom of the machine**. See
[the levelling card](spirit-ce900-assembly-levelling-pads.md).
