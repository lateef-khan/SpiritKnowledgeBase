**This is the stride machines' two-piece arm.** The XE295/XE395 two-piece arm splits at three `5/16" x 1-3/4"` bolts;
this one splits at a single `3/8" x 2-1/4"` socket cap bolt with part numbers from the exploded view.

1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **sheet metal screws 3.5x12mm** securing the swing arm covers (front and
   back).
2. Remove the **3/8" x 2-1/4" socket head cap bolt (#137)**, **Ø3/8" x Ø19 x 1.5T flat washer (#176)** and **3/8" x 7T
   nyloc nut (#165)** which fix the upper handle bar to the lower handle bar, using **two 14 m/m wrenches**.
3. **Disconnect the wiring** connecting the upper and lower swing arms.
4. When the **keys on the handrails** are not functioning, first remove the stickers, then use a Phillips head
   screwdriver to remove the **M5x20mm flat head socket screws** securing the keys to the swing arm.
5. Separate the cables of the **resistance button** and the **450 m/m handle wire (upper)**, and take out the **handle
   switch**.
6. Use a **14 mm wrench** to release the **3/8" x 3/4" hex head bolt** together with the **3/8" x 30 x 1.5T flat washer**
   which secure the swing arms.
7. Release the **M5 x 15L Phillips head screws** of **connecting arm cover A** and **connecting arm cover B** with a
   Phillips head screwdriver, and remove covers A and B.
8. Use **#12 and #13 open end wrenches** to hold the swing arm assembly and remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x
   1-1/4"**, **flat washer 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T** and **nyloc nut 5/16" x 7T**. Remove the swing arm assembly.
9. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.

The CE850-2016 and XE895-2016 books print this section word for word. Steps 4 and 5 are the handrail key-switch
repair folded into the arm procedure; the XE395 books print a similar step under "handle switch bracket".
