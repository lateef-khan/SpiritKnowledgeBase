Numbers in brackets are item numbers from the assembly drawing. Gather the
hardware for each step before you start that step.

**Step one - uprights into the frame base.** Take **6 Speed Nut Clips (132)** and
attach them at the front and each side of the unit. Guide the right upright
through Frame Base Cover Right (43) and Console Mast Cover Right (100). Take the
Right Upright (4) and attach the Middle Computer Cable (38) to the Lower Computer
Cable (39) - be careful not to pinch the cable or damage may occur to the system.
Slide the right upright (4) to sit into the frame base. Repeat from the "guide
the upright" step on the left.

**Step two - bolt the uprights down.** With the Right and Left Uprights (4, 5)
inserted into the Frame Base (2), use the Combination M5 Allen Wrench and
Phillips Head Screw Driver (96) to tighten **8 Button Head Socket Bolts (93)**
securing the uprights to the frame base.

**Step three - frame base covers.** Install Frame Base Cover Right and Left
(42, 33) onto the Frame Base (2) using **6 Tapping Screws (88)**. Tighten with
the Phillips Head Screw Driver (122).

**Step four - console.**

1. **XT285-2015 only:** connect the Speed Adjustment Switch w/ Cable (126) with
   the Upper Speed Adjustment Switch w/ Cable (128), and the Incline Adjustment
   Switch w/ Cable (127) with the Incline Adjustment Switch w/ Cable (129). The
   XT285-2015 step four has these two connections; the XT185-2015 step four does
   not print them.
2. Connect the Middle Computer Cable (38) to the Upper Computer Cable (37). Be
   careful not to pinch the cables.
3. Insert the Console Assembly (40) onto the Right and Left Uprights (4, 5) and
   secure with **4 Button Head Socket Bolts (90)** and **4 Split Washers (82)**.
   Use the Allen Wrench (97) to tighten.

**Step five - console mast covers.** Slide Console Mast Cover Right and Left
(100, 99) up the uprights. Secure them with **8 Sheet Metal Screws (142)** using
the Phillips Head Screw Driver (122).

That is the whole procedure. There is no handrail-support step, no tablet-holder
step and no gusset step on these two machines.

Three things in the printed pages to be aware of:

- Step one calls the right frame base cover **43**; step three calls the pair
  **(42, 33)**. The manual does not reconcile the two numbers.
- The **XT185-2015 step-three page is headed "XT185 STEP THREE" but its hardware
  table and its first instruction both read "HARDWARE FOR STEP 4"**, so that
  manual prints "STEP 4" twice. The XT285-2015 manual reads STEP 3 there. The
  hardware itself is the same on both.
- In the XT285-2015 step four the incline pair is printed as **"INCLINE
  ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/ CABLE (127) with the INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/ CABLE
  (129)"** - the word UPPER is missing from the second one, where the speed line
  above it has it.

**None of these item numbers carry over to the 2023 XT185 or XT285.** That
machine has a six-step assembly, no speed nut clips, and mounts its console on
5/16" x 15mm bolts (93) with curved washers (132) rather than the 3/8" x 1-3/4"
bolts (90) and split washers (82) used here.
