1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver (114) to release **4 pcs of M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screws (99)** and unplug the **computer cable (44)** and the **handgrip sensor cables (45)**; the **console (19)** is released.
2. Reverse the step to refit.

The 2020 CR800 and CR800ENT books print only the four screws; the 2009 XR898 book adds the two cables to the same step. Nothing else - no torque, no cable routing - is printed in any of the three.

The console mast procedure begins "take the console apart first", so this card is its first step.

**The XBR recumbents unplug different cables** (the 2100 mm hand-pulse wire and the 2100 mm upper switch cable), so their console card is separate although the screws are the same.
