Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. Hardware quantities are on
[the hardware card](spirit-xbr-2023-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Step one - stabilizer and seat back

1. Attach the **Rear Stabilizer (7)** to the **Main Frame (1)** with 2 **Bolts (65)**, 2
   **Flat Washers (84)** and 2 **Cap Nuts (191)**. Tighten completely with the **Wrench
   (112)**.
2. Attach the **Seat Back (5)** to the **Seat Carriage (4)**. Slide 1 **Bolt (67)**
   through each side, then attach a **Nylon Nut (89)** to each. **Tighten just enough
   that there is no side-to-side play but the seat back still moves freely front to
   back.** Use wrenches **112 and 130**.
3. Insert an **End Cap (32)** into each opening of the front stabilizer tube. A rubber
   mallet may be needed; **no mallet is in the carton**.

## Step two - console mast, console, tablet holder and handlebar

1. Slide the **Computer Cable (44)**, **Handlebar Resistance Control Cable (148)** and
   **Hand Pulse Cable (45)** through the bottom of the **Console Mast Cover (31)** and
   then the bottom of the **Console Mast (2)**, cover the right way up. Install the mast
   into the receiving tube of the Main Frame (1) without pinching the cables: 2 **Bolts
   (68)** and 2 **Flat Washers (76)** on each side, and 2 Bolts (68) with 2 **Curved
   Washers (83)** on the front. Tighten all six firmly with the Wrench (112). Remove the
   white styrofoam pad fitted to stop bolts dropping down the mast tube.
2. Plug the three cables into their connectors behind the **Console Assembly (19)** and
   fix the console to the mounting plate with 4 **Phillips Head Screws (99)**, using the
   **Phillips Head Screwdriver (114)**.
3. **Attach the Console Holder (153) to the Console Assembly (19) with 3 Phillips Head
   Screws (99).**
4. Attach the **Handle Bar Assembly (3)** onto the Console Mast (2) bracket with 2 Bolts
   (68), 2 **Split Washers (82)** and 2 Flat Washers (76). Tighten with the Wrench (112).

**Item 3 is the whole of what is new in this build.** The older XBR55 and XBR95 books
have no console holder and use four screws where this uses seven.

**Note the order.** The handlebar goes on **after** the console here; in the older books
it goes on before.

## Step three - seat and seat handlebar

1. Install the **Seat (61)** on the Seat Carriage (4) with 4 **Phillips Head Screws
   (98)**.
2. Attach the **Seat Handle Bar (6)** to the Seat Carriage (4) with 4 **Hex Head Bolts
   (71)**, 4 **Flat Washers (77)** and 4 **Nylon Nuts (89)**, using wrenches 112 and 130.
3. Plug the **Hand Pulse Sensor Cables (27)** into the rear two holes on the left side of
   the rear plastic cover, and the **Handlebar Resistance Cables (150)** into the
   remaining front hole.

## Step four - gas cylinder, pedals and bottle holders

1. Install the **Gas Cylinder (57)** on the **Seat Back Frame (5)** with 1 **Bolt (70)**,
   1 Flat Washer (76) and 1 **Nylon Nut (91)**, using wrenches **112 and 113**.
2. Attach the **Pedals (116)** to the **Crank Arms (51L, 51R)** with the Wrench (113).
   The **left pedal is reverse-threaded** and goes on counterclockwise. **No torque
   figure is printed** - see
   [the pedal card](spirit-bike-assembly-pedal-tighten-as-much-as-possible.md).
3. Attach the **Drink Bottle Holders (39L, 38R)** to the sides of the Seat Handle Bar (6)
   with 4 **Sheet Metal Screws (105)**.

## Then set the machine up

Unlike the older printings, these two books carry a **SETTING UP YOUR BIKE** page after
the build: levelling with an M14 wrench, powering on, and moving the bike on its
transport wheels. See
[the levelling card](spirit-xb-2023-assembly-levelling-m14-wrench.md) and
[the transport card](spirit-xb-2023-assembly-transport-wheels.md).

## The two books are the same build, and differ in one paragraph

Compared word for word, the XBR55 2023 and XBR95 2023 assembly chapters are identical
apart from page numbers - **and the powering-on paragraph**. The **XBR95 2023 has a
built-in generator and never needs an outlet**: it starts when you pedal. The **XBR55
2023 powers up when the cord is connected.** Nothing in the four assembly steps differs.

## The carton no longer lists a part the build fits

Step two fits the **Console Mast Cover (31)** and the 2023 parts panel does not list it.
See [the parts card](spirit-xbr-2023-assembly-parts-included.md).
