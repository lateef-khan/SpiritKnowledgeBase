Position the machine where it is convenient to use the adaptor. There is one adaptor hole located at the front of the machine.

This is the last assembly-adjacent step in the book: the machine is unfolded onto its rear stabilizer first (two people recommended), and only then plugged in through this hole. Folding the machine back up and wheeling it to a new location belong to the maintenance section.
