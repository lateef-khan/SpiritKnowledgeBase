Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | #128 | - | Speed Nuts Clip | 6 pcs |
| 3 | #130 | 5/16" x 1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 pcs |
| 5 | #133 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #113 | Dia 10 | Split Washer | 6 pcs |
| 6 | #134 | M5 x 10 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 8 pcs |
| 6 | #122 | 3.5 x 12 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #159 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #75 | - | Safety Key | 1 pc |
| 8 | #120 | 5 x 16 mm | Tapping Screw | 6 pcs |

Tools in the box: #131 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver, #132 M6 Allen Wrench, #30 Lubricant, #186 Phillips Screw Driver.

This kit differs from the AF63 2011 kit only in step 5-6: console bolts numbered #133 instead of #139 (same 3/8" x 1-3/4" size), eight M5 x 10 mm side-cap screws (#134) instead of M5 x 15 mm (#140), sheet-metal screws numbered #122 instead of #119, and lubricant numbered #30 instead of #134. The step-2 hardware label misprints "#128 (2 pcs)"; checklist and step text say six, and this card follows six.
