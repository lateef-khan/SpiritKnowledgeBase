Hardware: pre-installed on the main frame (the 2019 book: on the rear support tube, No. 02), **2 Allen screws (#82)**. Tool: 72 (the 2019 book labels it "Wrench" on this page and "Allen Key" everywhere else).

1. Remove the 2 screws (82) from the frame, attach the remote-control holder (89) onto the frame, and tighten with the 2 screws (82).

The holder is a tray for a television remote; neither book says anything else about it.
