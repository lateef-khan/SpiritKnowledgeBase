Hardware: **#49** 5/16" Split Washer (4 pcs), **#48** 5/16" x 16mm Flat Washer (4 pcs), **#88** 5/16" x 5/8" Screw (4 pcs).

1. Attach the handlebars - **make sure they are oriented as in the illustration** - with four Screws (88), four Split Washers (49) and four Flat Washers (48). Tighten firmly using the **Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (86)**.

**These are imperial parts.** The SB900 of the same years uses the same three item numbers for metric parts: #49 is a ø8 split washer, #48 a ø8 x 16mm flat washer and #88 an M8 x P1.25 x 20L screw. Do not swap them between models.

The SB700 2021 replaced this step entirely with a Sliding Handlebar Mount and five different fasteners.
