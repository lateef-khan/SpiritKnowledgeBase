**The only belt-tension figure in this manual is "about an inch of play in the drive belt".** No gauge, no
pounds, no hertz. The later XT service manuals set the same belt to 70-75 lbs with a tension gauge; do not read
that figure back onto these machines from this book.

1. Turn the power switch off.
2. Disconnect the **red and black motor wires** from the controller.
3. Loosen the tension bolt and remove the **four motor bolts**.
4. Take off the drive belt.
5. Insert the new motor into place.
6. Put the drive belt on.
7. Put in the four motor bolts. Do not tighten them all the way down.
8. Tighten the tension bolt until you only have **about an inch of play** in the drive belt.
9. Tighten down the four motor bolts.
10. Plug the motor wires into the controller.

The book's controller diagram (its Procedure 11) labels the two motor terminals **M+ red** and **M- black**,
which is where the red and black wires go back on. No bolt size, torque or motor part number is printed here;
the part numbers are in each machine's parts list.

The same manual covers five machines with one procedure and does not say that any of them differs.
