1. Take the **console (34)** off first (four M5 x 12 mm screws, computer cable and hand-pulse cables unplugged).
2. Use a **12 mm wrench** to release **2 pcs of 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts (51)**, **2 pcs of 5/16" x 18 mm x 1.5T flat washers (72)** and **2 pcs of 5/16" x 1.5T split washers (103)** that secure the **handlebar assembly (3)**, and remove the handlebar assembly (3).
3. Release **four Ø3 x 10 mm tapping screws (65)** to remove the **handgrip side caps, top and bottom (42) (43)**.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **four 20 mm tapping screws (62)** securing the **hand-pulse w/cable assembly (26) (28)**, separate the two halves of the assembly from each other and remove.
5. Reverse the steps to refit.

**Per-book differences.**

- The XBU55 2016 and 2023 books add "Take off the Handgrip Cap" before the 12 mm wrench in step 2, and number the hand-pulse assembly (26) only; the CU800 books number it (26) (28).
- The CU800 (2020) book heads this section "Handle Bar and Console Mast Replacement", but the console mast itself is the next section (10-3). This card is the handlebar only.

**Hardware named in these steps and nowhere else in the book:** the handgrip side caps use **Ø3 x 10 mm** tapping screws (65); the hand-pulse assembly uses **20 mm** tapping screws (62) with no diameter printed.
