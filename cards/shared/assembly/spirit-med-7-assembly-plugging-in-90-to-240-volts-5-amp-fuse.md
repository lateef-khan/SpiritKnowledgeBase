The bike has a **built-in universal power supply**. You can plug it into any A.C. power source from **90 to 240 volts, 50 to 60 Hz**. The **A.C. input is located in the front** of the bike. The input module has an **input connector for the line cord, a power switch and a 5 amp fuse**. **Turn the power switch to off when the bike is not in use.**

Three books print it word for word, each with its own model name: the 7.0R (2025 and the 2021 Dyaco MED edition, under "Connecting to A.C. power") and the 7.0U. **The 8.0U and 8.5R print 100 to 240 volts for the same module**; that is a different card. The 4.0R and 4.0U have no power input at all - they run on a generator and start when pedalled.

The service manual's no-power troubleshooting starts at the same three parts - "make sure the A.C. outlet has power (90~240VAC), the line cord plugged in securely and the power switch is on" - and ends at the fuse in the input module, which "should be short" on a meter. The electrical-safety page's outlet and grounding requirements are the safety section's.
