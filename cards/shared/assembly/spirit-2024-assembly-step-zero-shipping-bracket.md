Two Spirit machines open their assembly chapter with a step numbered **zero**, before
step one. In both it does the same job - take off a protective bracket that shipped
bolted to the mast mounting plate on the main frame - and in both you throw the bracket
away.

**The bolts are handled differently, and that is the part to get right.**

| | CE850 | CS800 |
|---|---|---|
| Bracket | Side Back (No.28) | Side Back (No.17) |
| Held by | 2 Socket Head Cap Bolts (No.145) | 2 Socket Head Cap Bolts, 3/8" x 3/4" |
| Tool | Allen Wrench (No.193) | L Allen Wrench (No.119) |
| The bolts | **Keep them** | **Discard them** |

## Why the CE850 bolts must be kept

Its **step one needs four of item 145** to secure the console mast, and its hardware bag
holds only two. The two that come out in step zero are the other two. Bin them and the
build stops at step one with nothing to finish the mast.

The CE850 manual does not say this. It says only to release the bolts and dispose of the
side back; the shortfall is visible only by comparing the step one text against the step
one table. See
[the CE850 build](spirit-ce850-assembly-procedure.md) and
[its hardware card](spirit-ce850-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Why the CS800 bolts can go

Its step zero says in so many words to **discard the bolts**, and no later step asks for a
3/8" x 3/4" socket head cap bolt from that source. Its step one mast bolts are item 81, and
the bag holds four of them. See [the CS800 build](../../cs800-2024/assembly/cs800-2024-assembly-procedure.md).

## Neither pre-assembly page mentions it

Both machines print the standard four-item pre-assembly list, which cuts the straps,
inspects for damage, takes the tools out and explains the item numbers in parentheses -
and says nothing about a bracket. See
[that card](ct900-pre-assembly.md). A reader who works from the pre-assembly page and
then looks for "step one" will walk straight past step zero.

## The CE850 2016 does the same job without a step zero

Its manual has no step numbered zero. The instruction is folded into **step two**,
worded differently - "Use WRENCH (193) to release 2 SOCKET HEAD CAP BOLTS and take apart
side back disposed" - immediately before the same four socket head cap bolts (No.145),
four spring washers (No.186) and four flat washers (No.176) that secure the mast. The
bracket and the trap are the same; the step number is not, so that machine is
deliberately not on this card.

## No other Spirit machine has one

Of the fourteen machines in the 2024 commercial redesign, only these two open with a step
zero. The CRS800S, the CSC900 and the CRW800 - the other three new shapes in that
redesign - all begin at step one and ship no such bracket.
