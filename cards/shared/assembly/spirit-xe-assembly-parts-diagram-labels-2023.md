**Both 2023 manuals print the same fourteen labels, in the same order, with the same
letters.** Nothing distinguishes the two diagrams in the text.

## The names changed from 2021 and the parts did not

| 2021 label | 2023 label |
|---|---|
| SWING ARM | C. Swinging Handlebars |
| CONNECTING ARM | I. Linkage Arms |
| PULSE SENSORS / PULSE GRIPS | F. Hand Pulse Grips |
| FOOT PEDALS | J. Pedals |
| RAILS | L. Roller Track |
| CONSOLE FAN | D. Adjustable Fan |

**The 2023 diagram drops four labels the 2021 one had** - FRONT STABILIZER, MAIN
FRAME, LEVERS and SPEAKERS - and adds four - Tablet Holder, LCD Screen, Console
Buttons and Flywheel Cover. The levellers are on both carton lists and on neither
2023 diagram.

## The steps still use the old names

The 2023 assembly steps call item 9 the **LEFT CONNECTING ARM**, not a linkage arm,
and call items 5 and 17 **swing arms**, not swinging handlebars. Three names for two
parts across one book.

## These letters are not item numbers

Nothing on this diagram is a part number. Both 2023 manuals do carry a real numbered
parts list - XE395 at pp. 39-42 and XE795 at pp. 38-40 - and those numbers are the
ones to order against. See
[the exploded view card](spirit-ce-assembly-exploded-view-and-parts-list.md).
