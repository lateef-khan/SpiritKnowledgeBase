## Six printings, eighteen rows, and not one figure moves

The XBR95 2016, 2018 and 2021 tables and the XBR55 2019, 2021 and XBR55ENT tables were
compared row by row - item number, description and quantity. **Every value is identical
on all six.** The XBR55 and the XBR95 are two machines sharing one hardware kit across
five years of printings.

**The XBR55ENT changes the type column and nothing else.** Where the other five write
BOLT it writes **CARRIAGE BOLT** for item 65 and **HEX HEAD BOLT** for items 67, 68, 71
and 70. Same part numbers, same sizes, same quantities - the head types are simply named
rather than left blank.

## Item 89 is listed twice, and it is one nut

Step 1 asks for two and step 3 for four, both **3/8"X7T** nyloc nuts. Six in the carton
in total. The two tables are not a contradiction and not two different nuts.

## Which one takes the odd tool

Item **70**, the single 5/16" x 1-1/4" bolt in step 4, holds the gas cylinder on the
seat back frame and is tightened with wrenches **112 and 113**. It is the only fastener
in the build that needs the 13/15mm, and the XBR95 carton does not list one - see
[the four-tool card](spirit-xbr55-assembly-tools-included.md).

## The XBR25 kit is not this kit

The XBR25 adds a second seat-back bolt (item **142**, 3/8" x 4-1/4") and four extra flat
washers, doubles the step-1 nyloc nuts to four, and has **no step-4 gas cylinder
hardware at all** - no item 70, 76 or 91. Do not carry these rows across. See
[the XBR25 hardware card](spirit-xbr25-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## The 2023 recumbents change one quantity

The XBR55 2023 and XBR95 2023 panels list **seven** of item 99 where these six list
four, because their build adds a console tablet holder held by three more screws. Every
other row is the same. See
[the 2023 hardware card](spirit-xbr-2023-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## This is not the CR800 kit either

The commercial CR800 step one takes a **3/8" x 135mm** bolt with a curved washer; this
one takes a **3/8" x 53mm** bolt with a flat washer and a cap nut. The two machines
share a carton list and share almost no fastener. See
[the CR800 hardware card](spirit-cr800-assembly-hardware-kit.md).
