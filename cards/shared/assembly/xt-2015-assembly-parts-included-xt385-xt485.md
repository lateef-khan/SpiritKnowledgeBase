Printed carton list:

| Qty | Part | XT385-2015 | XT485-2015 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Main Frame | yes | yes |
| 2 | Uprights | yes | yes |
| 2 | Console Mast Covers | yes | yes |
| 2 | Frame Base Covers | yes | yes |
| 1 | Console | yes | yes |
| 1 | Deck | yes | yes |
| 1 | Beverage Holder | **not listed** | yes |
| 1 | Audio Cable | yes | yes |
| 1 | Power Cord | yes | yes |
| 1 | Lubricant | yes | yes |
| 1 | Hardware Kit | yes | yes |

**The beverage holder is the only difference.** The XT485-2015 list names one and
its assembly step six fits it to the handrail support; the XT385-2015 list names
none and its step six fits only the handrail support.

An **Audio Cable** is on both lists. It is not on the XT185-2015 or XT285-2015
list, and the 2023 manuals list none for any of the five machines.

The list does not say what is inside the Hardware Kit; the fasteners are printed
step by step instead - see the hardware card for these two machines.
