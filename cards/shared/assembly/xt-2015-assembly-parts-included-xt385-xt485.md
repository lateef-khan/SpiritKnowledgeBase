Printed carton list:

| Qty | Part | XT385-2015 | XT485-2015 | XT485ENT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Main Frame | yes | yes | yes |
| 2 | Uprights | yes | yes | yes |
| 2 | Console Mast Covers | yes | yes | yes |
| 2 | Frame Base Covers | yes | yes | yes |
| 1 | Console | yes | yes | yes |
| 1 | Deck | yes | yes | yes |
| 1 | Beverage Holder | **not listed** | yes | yes |
| 1 | Audio Cable | yes | yes | yes |
| 1 | Power Cord | yes | yes | yes |
| 1 | Lubricant | yes | yes | yes |
| 1 | Hardware Kit | yes | yes | yes |

**The beverage holder is the only difference.** The XT485-2015 list names one and
its assembly step six fits it to the handrail support; the XT385-2015 list names
none and its step six fits only the handrail support. **The XT485ENT carton list
is the XT485-2015 list item for item and in the same order**, and its assembly
also fits the Beverage Holder (121) to the Handrail Support (119).

An **Audio Cable** is on all three lists. It is not on the XT185-2015 or
XT285-2015 list, and the 2023 manuals list none for any of the five machines nor
for the XT685ENT. On the XT485ENT it is item 145, a 400mm Audio Cable.

**Only the carton list is shared with the XT485ENT.** That machine's hardware,
its assembly steps and its console are its own; take the rest of the build from
its own cards.

The list does not say what is inside the Hardware Kit; the fasteners are printed
step by step instead - see the hardware card for these two machines.
