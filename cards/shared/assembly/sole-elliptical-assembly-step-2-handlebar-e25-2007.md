1. **Install 2 pcs of 17m/m Wave Washers** onto the left and right sides of the Handle Bar axle.
2. **Slide the Left and Right Handle Bars** onto the axle.
3. **Put 2 pcs of 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T Flat Washers onto 2 pcs of 5/16" x 15m/m Hex Head Screws** and tighten in the threaded holes in the ends of the axle.
