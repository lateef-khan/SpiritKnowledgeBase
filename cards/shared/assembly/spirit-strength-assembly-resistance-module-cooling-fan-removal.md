*(Tool: screwdriver)*

1. Remove the **8 screws (1)** and remove the **2 cooling fans (2)**.

Two fans, four screws each. The fans reach the power board through a fan adapter cable (cable 10 in the cable
chapter). The controller's over-temperature fault names a failed fan as one cause - an errors card.

The "Digital Resistance Module" is the i-Strength line's motorised resistance box, part 72 in the CSI owner's manuals; the screw numbers are the call-outs on the manual's exploded drawings.
