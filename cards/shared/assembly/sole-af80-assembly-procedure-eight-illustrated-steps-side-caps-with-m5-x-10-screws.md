Eight illustrated assembly steps (hardware item numbers in brackets). The sequence matches the AF63 2011 books; only the step 5-6 hardware differs.

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill from the carton onto a smooth, level floor with the rear at least 3 ft from any wall. Do not remove the Velcro belt until the plastic wrap and Styrofoam are out from under the unit.
2. Fit six Speed Nut Clips (128) on the frame base, slide the Frame Base Caps (61 & 62) over the upright tubes (4 & 5), and connect Lower Computer Cable (49) to Middle Computer Cable (50) on the right side.
3. Bolt the upright tubes (4 & 5) to the frame base (2) with eight Button Head Socket Bolts (130); leave them loose until step 7 is finished. Do not pinch the cables in the tubing.
4. Connect Speed Adjustment Switch Cable (54) to Speed Cable Upper (37), Incline Adjustment Switch Cable (55) to Incline Cable Upper (38), and Middle Computer Cable (50) to Computer Cable Upper (48); tuck excess cable into the handrail tubing.
5. Attach the Console Assembly (52) onto the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (133) and four Split Washers (113) using the M6 wrench; tighten completely.
6. Fix the side caps over the upright tubes (4 & 5) with eight Phillips Head Screws (134) and four Sheet Metal Screws (122).
7. Bolt the support tube between the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (159), set the Beverage Holder (158) on it, place the safety-key magnet (75) between Start and Stop, and check every bolt and screw is tight. (The step text numbers the tube (15) in the first sentence and (26) in the second; this card keeps both numbers as printed.)
8. Attach the console mast covers (62 & 63) to the frame base (2) with six Tapping Screws (120).
