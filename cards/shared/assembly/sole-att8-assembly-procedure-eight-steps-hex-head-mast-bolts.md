Eight illustrated assembly steps, identical in the ATT8 and TT8C 2011 books (hardware item numbers in brackets). Neither book prints a Velcro-strap unpacking warning.

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill base frame from the carton onto firm, level ground.
2. Remove the four motor-cover Phillips Head Screws (129) with the M5 driver and set the cover aside; connect the main-frame Computer Cable (32) to the Computer Cable (33) at the bottom of the right upright tube (4).
3. Bolt the upright tubes (5 & 4) to the main frame (1), hand-tight only until the console is on: two Split Washers (85) plus two Hex Head Bolts (102) in the bottom holes per tube, two Split Washers (85) plus two Hex Head Bolts (100) in the upper holes per tube, and two Curved Washers (131) on two Hex Head Bolts (101) in the two front holes per tube. Partially tighten with the 12/14 mm wrench (108). Do not pinch the right-upright harness.
4. Connect lower Computer Cable (33) to upper Computer Cable (31).
5. Fix the Console Assembly (36) on top of the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (125) and four Split Washers (85); tighten completely with the M6 wrench (105) without pinching cables.
6. Bolt the handrail support (3) between the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (99), set the Beverage Holder (48) on it, and check the safety-key magnet (63) is on the console.
7. Fully tighten the mast-to-frame bolts with the 12/14 mm wrench; refit the motor cover with its screws (129); press four Speed Nuts (84) onto the motor cover (43); fix the console mast covers (40 & 41) with six Sheet Metal Screws (92) and two Self Tapping Screws (87).
8. Fix the left (117 & 118) and right (119 & 120) handlebar covers over the upright tops with eight Phillips Head Screws (126) and four Sheet Metal Screws (89).
