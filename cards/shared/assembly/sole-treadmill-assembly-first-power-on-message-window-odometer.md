First power-on, the last step of assembly. This paragraph is identical in the F60 2012, F63 2013, F65 2013 and F80 / F85 2013 books, in the F63/F65 2016 book, and in the F63 2019, F65 2019, F80 2016 and F80 2019 books, except the F60 book omits the assembly-step cross-reference:

Power the treadmill on by plugging it into an appropriate wall outlet, then turn on the power switch located at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood. Ensure that the Safety Key is installed, as the treadmill will not power on without it (see assembly step 7 for reference; reference absent in the F60 book). When the power is turned on, a message will scroll across the Message Window showing the current software version. Then the Time and Distance Windows will display Odometer readings for a short time: the Time Window shows hours in use and the Distance Window shows miles (or kilometers in metric). The treadmill then enters idle mode, which is the starting point for operation.
