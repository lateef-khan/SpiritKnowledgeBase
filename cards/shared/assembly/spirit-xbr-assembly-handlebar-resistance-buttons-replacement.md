1. Use your hand, or a tool with a flat bead, to take the **handgrip end cap (144)** off and draw out the **resistance button w/cable (145)**.
2. Separate the resistance button w/cable (145) from the **switch cable (lower) (151)**.
3. Peel off the **up and down handgrip resistance labels (146 and 147)**, then take the **handgrip button w/cable (145)** off.
4. When refitting, mind the direction of the handgrip end cap: **the raised part on the end cap (144) must match the concave part on the handlebar (6).**

Five books print this identically; the 2016 and ENT books head it "Buttons on The Handle Bar (Remark 9)" - no remark 9 is printed in those books.

The lower switch cable (151) plugs into the upper switch cable (148), which the rear-shroud procedure unplugs and the console procedure unplugs at the console end. The XBR25 2016 has no buttons section and no switch cables in its console or rear-shroud steps as printed.
