**Holding the Rear Stabilizer, gently tilt the elliptical up and roll it away.**

Both manuals print that one sentence and nothing else, beside a picture that labels
the **Transport Wheels** and the **Rear Stabilizer**. Neither gives a weight, says
how many people it takes, or tells you to unplug first.

## Lift at the rear stabiliser, not the rail

The transport wheels are at the **front** of the machine - label **N** on the
whole-machine diagram - so tilting the rear up puts the weight onto them. Both 2023
diagrams label a **Rear Stabilizer (M)** and a **Transport Wheel (N)**. See
[the diagram card](spirit-xe-assembly-parts-diagram-labels-2023.md).

## The nine older residential XE manuals do not say this

- The **XE100/XE200/XE300** and **XE150/XE350/XE550** manuals print a
  **Transportation** heading in their features chapter saying only that the machine
  has two transport wheels which engage when the rear is lifted. Same idea, different
  words, and no instruction to hold anything. See
  [that card](../../ce800-2013/assembly/ce800-2013-assembly-transport-wheels.md).
- The **XE395 2010, XE195, XE295, XG400, XE795 2021** and **XE395ENT** manuals say
  **nothing at all** about moving the machine, although every 2021 carton list
  includes 2 Transport Wheels and the XG400 and XE795 builds bolt them on by hand.

## The XE795 2023 fits its own transport wheels in step one

"Attach the TRANSPORT WHEELS (80) to each bracket with 2 BOLTS (92) and 2 NUTS (99).
**Partially tighten** - over tightening the hardware will prevent the wheels from
rotating." If the machine will not roll, that is the first thing to check on an
XE795 or an XG400. The XE395 2023's wheels arrive fitted.

## This is not the treadmill instruction

The Spirit XT685 2023 and XT685ENT say to lift **at the rear roller area, grasping
the two end caps**. Different machine, different lifting point; see
[that card](../../xt685-2023/assembly/xt685-2023-moving-the-treadmill.md).
