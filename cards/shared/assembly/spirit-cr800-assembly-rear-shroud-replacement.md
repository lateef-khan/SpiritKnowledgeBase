1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 12 self-tapping screws (103)** and **2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws (101)** on the **left rear chain cover (35)**. Unplug the **hand-pulse wire (26)**, and the left rear chain cover is released.
2. On the **right rear chain cover (36)**, unscrew the **Ø3.5 self-tapping screw (103)** with its **Ø5 x 16 x 1.5T flat washer (78)** and **2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws (101)**, and the right rear chain cover (36) is released.
3. To refit the rear shroud (R) (36), return it onto the **main frame (1)** and slightly tighten a **Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet-metal screw (103)** with a **3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T flat washer (78)**, then use **2 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (101)** to secure it on the main frame, and lock the Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet-metal screw (103).
4. Join the rear shroud (L) (35) and rear shroud (R) (36) and secure with **7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet-metal screws (103)** and **2 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (101)**.

**Step 2 prints the right-side screw as "ø 3.5x26"** in both books. No 26 mm screw exists in these parts lists; the refit step names the same screw (103) as Ø3.5 x 16 mm, and step 1 names (103) as Ø3.5 x 12. Part (103) is printed with three lengths in one procedure; the count and the washer are consistent.

The 2009 XR898 and the XBR books put a **5/16" x 16 x 1T flat washer (127)** on the right shroud's post before it goes back on the frame and name the frame screw (107); these two books do not print (127) at all. Those are the linked cards.
