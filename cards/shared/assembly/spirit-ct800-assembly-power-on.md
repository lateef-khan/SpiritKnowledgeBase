1. Plug the treadmill into an appropriate wall outlet.
2. Turn on the power switch, **located at the front of the treadmill below the
   motor hood**.
3. **Ensure that the safety key is installed** - the treadmill will not power on
   without it.

All three manuals print these words identically. The 2016 and 2020 manuals call it
the **safety button**, the 2012 manual the **safety key**; it is the same magnetic
key, and the tether-cord pages of all three describe it the same way.

## What the console then shows is a separate question

The rest of that page - every light on the display coming up, the Time and
Distance windows showing the hours and the miles on the odometer, the software
version message, and the console settling into **idle mode** - describes the
console in normal running, not the last step of assembly. That half of the fact
is on its own card; see the linked power-on-sequence card, which holds the same
sequence word for word for other Spirit LED consoles.

If nothing lights at all after step 3, the troubleshooting table in each of these
manuals starts with the tether cord not being in position and the circuit breaker
on the front grill.

**The CT800ENT does not power up like this.** Its manual gives no motor-hood
location for the switch and shows a loading screen and a Home Screen rather than
an odometer; it has its own card.
