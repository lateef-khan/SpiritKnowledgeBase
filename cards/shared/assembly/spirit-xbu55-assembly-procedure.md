Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. Hardware quantities are on
[the hardware card](spirit-xbu55-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Step one - rear stabilizer

1. Install the **Rear Stabilizer (5)** onto the **Main Frame (1)** with 2 **Bolts (50)**,
   2 **Flat Washers (71)** and 2 **Nuts (135)**, tightening with the **Wrench (100)**.
2. Insert an **End Cap (41)** into each opening of the front stabilizer tube. A rubber
   mallet may be needed; **no mallet is in the carton**.

## Step two - console mast

1. Unravel the **Computer Cable (29)** and snake it through the **Console Mast Cover
   (38)** and the **Console Mast (2)** until the connector comes out of the top opening.
   **Be sure not to pinch the cable.** Slide the plastic cover (38) onto the mast the
   right way up.
2. Install the Console Mast (2) onto the Main Frame (1) with **7 Bolts (51)**, **6 Flat
   Washers (72) on the side bolts** and **1 Curved Washer (99) on the front bolt**.
   Tighten with the Wrench (100).

## Step three - handlebar

1. Run the **2 Hand Pulse Wires (26)** into the hole in the handlebar mounting plate and
   out through the hole in the console plate. **Remove the black plastic cover from the
   handlebars by cutting the plastic tie.**
2. Install the **Handle Bar (3)** onto the Console Mast (2) with 2 Bolts (51), 2 Flat
   Washers (72) and 2 **Split Washers (103)**, careful not to pinch the hand pulse wires.
   Tighten with the Wrench (100). **Snap the black plastic handlebar cover over the
   joint.**

## Step four - console, pedals, seat and bottle holder

1. Insert the Computer Cable (29) and the **2 Hand Pulse Cables** into the connectors
   behind the **Console (34)**. Install the Console onto the Console Mast (2) with 4
   **Phillips Head Screws (58)**, using the **Phillips Head Screwdriver (93)**.
2. Install the **Pedals (45L, 46R)** onto the **Crank Arms (16L, 16R)**. The **left
   pedal is reverse-threaded** and screws in with the opposite rotation; there is an "L"
   stamped in the end of the threaded post of the left pedal and an "R" in the right.
   Tighten with the **Wrench (92)**. **No torque figure is printed** - see
   [the pedal card](spirit-bike-assembly-pedal-tighten-as-much-as-possible.md).
3. Install the **Seat Mount Slide (7)**, **post at the front**, onto the track on top of
   the **Seat Bracket (6)**: slide a **Flat Washer (71)** onto the **Seat Adjustment Knob
   (86)**, put it through the slots of each, and fit a **Flange Nut (8)** on the end.
   Install the **Seat (19)** onto the seat post, tightening the nut on each side beneath
   the seat.
4. Install the **Drink Bottle Holder (116)** onto the Console Mast (2) with 2 Phillips
   Head Screws (58).

## The build ends here

There is **no first power-on step**: these bikes are generator-powered and the console
starts when you pedal. There is no levelling step and no way to move the machine printed
anywhere in these three books - see
[the absence card](spirit-xb-assembly-no-moving-or-transport-section.md).

**The carton packs a Front Stabilizer Cover and a Rear Stabilizer Cover that no step
fits.** See [the parts card](spirit-xbu55-assembly-parts-included.md).

## What differs between the three printings

Compared word for word, and only one thing does:

- **The XBU55 2019 book writes "2 HAND PULSE CABLES (26, 28)" in step four**; the 2021
  and ENT books write **"(26)"**. Its own step three names wire 26 only, and its hardware
  is unchanged.

Everything else is line breaks and page numbers.

## This is close to the CU800 build, but it is not it

Steps two, three and four use the same item numbers and the same hardware as the
commercial CU800 2021. **Step one does not**, and the step order in step four differs -
the CU800 fits the beverage holder before the pedals and never mentions the seat
adjustment knob, the flange nut or the plastic tie on the handlebars. See
[that card](../../cu800-2021/assembly/cu800-2021-assembly-procedure.md).
