Both books print the same five lines - Procedure 7 in the CT800 2016 book, Procedure 8 in the XT dealer manual.

1. Turn the power switch off.
2. Unplug the harness from the console and from the controller.
3. Tie the end of the new harness to the old harness (with a zip-tie or a spare wire).
4. As you pull the old harness out of the upright, you will pull the new harness in.
5. Connect it to the board and the console, and turn the power back on.

That is the whole procedure. It does not say which upright the harness runs through, does not name a harness
part number, and does not say how to open the upright - the trick is that you never do; the old harness
tows the new one.

The troubleshooting tables in both books speak of a "lower and middle wire harness" as the parts to replace
when the incline has no relay click and no diagnostic light; this procedure is the only one printed for
changing either.
