**Look closely at the four floor levellers underneath the middle and rear of the
elliptical.** If any of them is not in contact with the floor:

1. Loosen the **bottom** nut with the wrench.
2. **Turn the rubber foot clockwise** until it makes solid contact with the floor.
3. Retighten the bottom nut so it cannot move.

It is the last job of the last assembly step on all three machines, not a maintenance
item.

## The bottom nut, not the top nut

The **XE395ENT 2021** and the **XE395 2023** print this job word for word with one
change: **top** nut, loosened and retightened. Same four levellers, same clockwise
turn, opposite nut. Do not carry either instruction across; see
[the top-nut card](spirit-xe-assembly-levelling-top-nut.md).

## Four in the instruction and six in the box

Every 2021 carton list says **6 Levelers**, and this job names **four**, "underneath
the middle and rear". The XG400 and XE795 builds fit six, three per side, and say so.
This card reports what the levelling job says; the carton count is on
[the parts card](spirit-xe-assembly-parts-included-2021.md).

## The wrench number in the sentence is not a wrench

The XE195 and XE295 both say to use **"the WRENCH (109)"**. Item 109 is a *5/16" x
UNC18 x 15L flat head socket bolt* in the only 2021 XE parts list there is. Use the
**13/14mm wrench** from the carton. The **XE395 2010** names its wrench correctly, as
item **124**, its 13/14mm.

## One word differs on the 2010 machine

The XE395 2010 says to turn the **rubber pad** clockwise where the two 2021 machines
say **rubber caster**. Same part, same direction, same nut.

## This is not the CE or CT levelling instruction

The commercial Spirit ellipticals and treadmills use a different sentence entirely -
two, four or six levelling pads turned with a **14mm wrench**, printed as a remedy in
the noise item rather than as an assembly job. See
[the two-pad card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md)
and [the six-pad CE900 card](spirit-ce900-assembly-levelling-pads.md).
