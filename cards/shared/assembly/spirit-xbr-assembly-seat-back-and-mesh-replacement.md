1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **4 pcs of Ø4 x 16 mm sheet-metal screws (105)**.
2. Use **two 14 mm wrenches** to release **2 pcs of 3/8" x 4" hex screws (67)** and **2 pcs of 3/8" x 7T nyloc nuts (89)**.
3. Use a **12 mm and a 13 mm wrench** to release one **5/16" x 1-1/4" hex screw (70)** and one **5/16" x 18 mm x 1.5T flat washer (76)**, and take the **seat back assembly (5)** and the **mesh seat back (63)** off.
4. *(XBR55 2016 and ENT books)* Loosen the cable from the mesh seat back (63).
5. Reverse the steps to refit.

**The XBR25 2016 book prints a different bolt set for step 2** - two 3/8" x 4" plus two 3/8" x 4-1/4" bolts (142) with four Ø3/8" x Ø19 x 1.5T washers (77) - and has its own card.

The 2016 and ENT books head this "Disassembling/Assembling of Seat Carriage Cover and Seat Back Bracket"; the XBR95 2016 and the 2023 books "Seat Back Assembly and Mesh Seat Back". The parts are the same. Step 4 (the cable) is printed only in the XBR55 2016 and XBR55ENT books; the recline cable it refers to is the one the release-lever card removes.
