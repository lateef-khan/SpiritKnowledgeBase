One 38-page dealer manual covers five machines. Its repair chapter is thirteen numbered procedures, printed
once for all five; only the calibration procedure is split by model.

| Procedure | Title | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Calibrating the unit | console; three versions, for the XT175, the XT275, and the XT375/475/675 |
| 2 | Checking wire harness | test |
| 3 | Speed sensor adjustment | adjustment |
| 4 | Checking treadmill circuit breaker | test |
| 5 | Checking roller for magnet | test |
| 6 | Adjust torque boost | adjustment |
| **7** | **Replacing controller** | part replacement |
| **8** | **Replacing wire harness** | part replacement |
| **9** | **Replacing drive motor** | part replacement |
| **10** | **Replacing incline motor** | part replacement |
| 11 | Controller diagram | wiring |
| 12 | Wiring diagram | wiring |
| 13 | Engineering mode | console; XT375, XT475 and XT675 only |

The four replacement procedures have their own cards. The rest of the book is the troubleshooting guide
(two pages), the warranty and parts-order forms, and an exploded view with a parts list for each of the five
machines - the parts lists are the only place this manual distinguishes one machine's hardware from
another's.

The CT800 2016 service manual reprints procedures 1-3 and 5-10 of this chapter with light edits as its
chapter 11, so the two books' replacement procedures are on shared cards where the text agrees.
