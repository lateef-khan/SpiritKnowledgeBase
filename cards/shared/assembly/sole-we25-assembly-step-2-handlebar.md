1. **Install the two 17m/m Wave Washers (153)** onto the left and right sides of the Handle Bar axle.
2. **Slide the Left (10) and Right (11) Handle Bars** onto the axle. The small stickers mark L and R.
3. **Install two 5/16" Flat Washers (145) onto the two 5/16" x 15mm Hex Head Bolts (100)** and tighten in the threaded holes in the ends of the axle.
4. **Front Handle Bar Covers (83 left, 85 right) and Rear Handle Bar Covers (84 left, 86 right)** over the Handle Bar axle connections with the **six 3.5 x 12m/m screws (126)**.
