The bike has a **built-in universal power supply**. You can plug it into any A.C. power source from **100 to 240 volts, 50 to 60 Hz**. The **A.C. input is located in the front** of the bike. The input module has an **input connector for the line cord, a power switch and a 5 amp fuse**. **Turn the power switch to off when the bike is not in use.**

Both books print it word for word, each with its own model name. **The 7.0R and 7.0U print 90 to 240 volts for the same paragraph**; ten volts apart at the bottom of the range, and the books do not say why. The parts diagrams label the switch (O / S, Power ON/OFF) and the socket (P / T, Power socket) at the front, beside the USB Type B and UART ports that the service manual uses for its communication test.

The service manual's no-power troubleshooting measures the AC input at the power supply module's CN1 and checks "whether the power switch is turned on and the fuse is not blown" - the same fuse - before going on to the 24 VDC and 12 VDC checks. The electrical-safety page's outlet and grounding requirements are the safety section's.
