**These five manuals have no "Setting Up Your Treadmill" section.** The assembly
chapter ends at the last numbered step and the console chapter starts on the next
page. So the manuals say nothing about:

- **Levelling.** No levelling feet, no wrench size, no adjustment.
- **Folding or unfolding.** The word "fold" does not appear anywhere in any of
  the five manuals.
- **Moving or transporting the machine.** Transport wheels are mentioned once,
  and only inside the electrical warning that tells you to route the power cord
  away from moving parts including the elevation mechanism and transport wheels.
- **Storage.**

Absence is the answer here, not a gap to fill from somewhere else. **Do not
answer these questions from the 2023 XT cards.** Those manuals do document a
levelling foot adjusted with an M14 wrench, a fold-and-roll procedure, a Velcro
shipping strap and a lift-at-the-rear-roller method for the XT685 - none of which
is printed for a 2015 machine, and the 2015 hardware is not the same hardware.
