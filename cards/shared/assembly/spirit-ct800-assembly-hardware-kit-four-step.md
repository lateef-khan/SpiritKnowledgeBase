**All four manuals list the same items in the same quantities in all four tables.**
The CT850 2020 tables are the CT800 2020 tables word for word, and the CT850ENT
tables are the CT800ENT tables word for word. Two descriptions differ between the LED
and touchscreen books, and they are called out below.

**The CT850 2016 manual is the exception on this platform and is not covered here.**
Its step two table lists **six** of items 152 and 153 where these four list five, and
its step two instruction calls for three a side rather than two. It has its own
card - do not answer a CT850 2016 hardware question from this one.

## Hardware for step 1

| Part | Type | Description | QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 115 | Flat Washer | Ø3/8" x 35 x 2.0T | 10 |
| 133 | Split Washer | Ø10 x 2T | 10 |
| 144 | Phillips Head Screw | M5 x 12mm | 4 |
| 150 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 3/8" x 3" | 10 |

| 152 | Socket Head Cap Bolt | M8 x 12mm | 5 |

## Hardware for step 3

The Round Cap is a part, not a fastener, and it is listed in the hardware table
rather than in the carton list.

|---|---|---|---|
| 120 | Split Washer | see below | 4 |

| 153 | Flat Washer | Ø8 x Ø16 x 2T | 4 |
| 156 | Socket Head Cap Bolt | M8 x P1.25 x 20L | 4 |

## The two descriptions that differ between the manuals

- **Item 120.** The LED manuals describe it **Ø8 x 1.5T**; the ENT manuals describe
  it **5/16" x 1.5T**. Each manual's own parts list repeats its own wording. Neither
  says they are the same washer, and neither says they are different.
- **Item 153 in the step 4 table.** The ENT manuals print it as a **Flash Washer**;
  every other table in all four manuals prints **Flat Washer**, at the same
  Ø8 x Ø16 x 2T.

## Three places where a step uses fewer than the table lists

These are the manuals' own numbers, reproduced as printed:

| Table | Table says | The step uses |
|---|---|---|
| Step 1, items 115, 133, 150 | 10 each | 8 on the LED manuals (four sets a side), 10 on the ENT manuals (five sets a side) |
| Step 2, items 152 and 153 | 5 each | 4 (two a side), in all four manuals |
| Step 4, item 144 | 2 | 1 on the LED manuals, 2 on the ENT manuals |

Where the LED and ENT books disagree, the ENT figure is the one that matches the
table. No manual acknowledges the gap.
