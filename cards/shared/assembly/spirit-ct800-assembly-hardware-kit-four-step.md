**Both manuals list the same items in the same quantities in all four tables.**
Two descriptions differ, and they are called out below.

## Hardware for step 1

| Part | Type | Description | QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 115 | Flat Washer | Ø3/8" x 35 x 2.0T | 10 |
| 133 | Split Washer | Ø10 x 2T | 10 |
| 144 | Phillips Head Screw | M5 x 12mm | 4 |
| 150 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 3/8" x 3" | 10 |

## Hardware for step 2

| Part | Type | Description | QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 125 | Flat Washer | Ø3/8" x Ø25 x 2.0T | 6 |
| 133 | Split Washer | Ø10 x 2T | 6 |
| 151 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 3/8" x 3/4" | 6 |
| 152 | Socket Head Cap Bolt | M8 x 12mm | 5 |
| 153 | Flat Washer | Ø8 x Ø16 x 2T | 5 |

## Hardware for step 3

| Part | Type | Description | QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 29 | Round Cap | *(no description printed)* | 1 |
| 133 | Split Washer | Ø10 x 2T | 2 |
| 154 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 3/8" x UNC16 x 1-1/4" | 2 |
| 155 | Curved Washer | Ø10 x Ø23 x 1.5T | 2 |

The Round Cap is a part, not a fastener, and it is listed in the hardware table
rather than in the carton list.

## Hardware for step 4

| Part | Type | Description | QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 120 | Split Washer | see below | 4 |
| 144 | Phillips Head Screw | M5 x 12mm | 2 |
| 153 | Flat Washer | Ø8 x Ø16 x 2T | 4 |
| 156 | Socket Head Cap Bolt | M8 x P1.25 x 20L | 4 |

## The two descriptions that differ between the manuals

- **Item 120.** The LED manual describes it **Ø8 x 1.5T**; the CT800ENT manual
  describes it **5/16" x 1.5T**. Each manual's own parts list repeats its own
  wording, at a quantity of 14 in both. Neither says they are the same washer,
  and neither says they are different.
- **Item 153 in the step 4 table.** The CT800ENT prints it as a **Flash Washer**;
  every other table in both manuals prints **Flat Washer**, at the same
  Ø8 x Ø16 x 2T.

## Three places where a step uses fewer than the table lists

These are the manuals' own numbers, reproduced as printed:

| Table | Table says | The step uses |
|---|---|---|
| Step 1, items 115, 133, 150 | 10 each | 8 on the LED manual (four sets a side), 10 on the CT800ENT (five sets a side) |
| Step 2, items 152 and 153 | 5 each | 4 (two a side), in both manuals |
| Step 4, item 144 | 2 | 1 on the LED manual, 2 on the CT800ENT |

Where the two manuals disagree, the CT800ENT figure is the one that matches the
table. Neither manual acknowledges the gap.
