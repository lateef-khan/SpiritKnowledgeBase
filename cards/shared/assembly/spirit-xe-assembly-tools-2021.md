All five cartons list the same four tools, and nothing else:

- 13/14mm Wrench
- Phillips Head Screwdriver
- 12/14mm Wrench
- Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver

**Both wrenches are double ended**, so the carton covers 12, 13 and 14mm and gives
you two wrenches for the bolt-and-Nyloc-nut joints. The fourth tool is one object
that is both an M5 Allen key and a Phillips driver.

## The steps ask for tools that are not on this panel

- The **XE795 2021** build names a **5MM L WRENCH** in steps 1 and 4 and an **8MM L
  WRENCH** in step 3. Neither is on its tools panel, and neither is anywhere in its
  carton list.
- The **XG400** build names an **8mm Allen WRENCH (108)** in step 1.
- The **XE295** build names an **L ALLEN WRENCH (177)** in steps 1 and 4, where the
  XE195 calls the same item 177 the **COMBINATION M5 ALLEN WRENCH & PHILLIPS HEAD
  SCREW DRIVER**. Item 177 is one tool with two names across two books.

The M5 combination tool will do a 5mm socket head. **Nothing in any of these five
cartons is an 8mm Allen key**, so an XE795 or XG400 builder has to supply one.

## The item numbers for the same tool are not stable between the five books

| Tool | XE195 / XE295 / XE395ENT | XG400 | XE795 2021 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13/14mm wrench | 155 | 116 (called simply WRENCH) | named by size, no number |
| 12/14mm wrench | 158 | 110 / 111 (as a pair) | named by size, no number |
| Phillips screwdriver | 157 | 107 | named, no number |
| Combination M5 Allen / L Allen | 177 | 108 (as "8mm Allen WRENCH") | named by size, no number |

## Items 109 and 106 are bolts, not wrenches

The XE195, XE295 and XE395ENT steps all print **"the WRENCH (109)"** and the XE195
prints **"the WRENCHES provided (109 & 106)"**. The XE395ENT parts list - the only
parts list in these five books - gives item **109** as a *5/16" x UNC18 x 15L Flat
Head Socket Bolt* and item **106** as a *3/8" x 2-1/2" Hex Head Bolt*. **Neither is
a tool.** The three books agree on the tool numbers that matter (155 is the 13/14mm
wrench, 157 the Phillips driver, 158 the 12/14mm wrench, all three confirmed by that
parts list) and then hand the reader two bolt numbers in the sentences that tell you
what to tighten with.

**Item 177 is in no parts list.** It is named as a tool in the XE195 and XE295 steps
and the XE395ENT list stops without it. Use the combination tool in the box and
ignore the number.

## This is not the 2010 panel and not the 2023 panel

The XE395 2010 carton has a **short Phillips driver** where this one has the
combination Allen tool. The XE395 2023 carton has three tools and the XE795 2023
carton has five. See the linked cards.
