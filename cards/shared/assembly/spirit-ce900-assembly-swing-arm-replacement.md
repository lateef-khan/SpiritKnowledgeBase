**No screw size, no wrench and no torque are printed for this arm.** The XE and CE800 swing arms are different
joints with sized bolts; do not carry them here.

1. Remove the **screw (1 pc)** that locks on the **swing tube** (figures 6.8.1 and 6.8.2).
2. Remove the **foot rod group of fish-eye bearings** (figures 6.8.3 to 6.8.5); you can then remove the swing tube.

The three books print this word for word. No refit line is printed; section 6.9 refers back to figures 6.8.3 to
6.8.5 for the foot link.
