**Connect the Upper Swing Arm (L) (18) to the left Lower Swing Arm** and the **Upper Swing Arm (R) (19) to the right Lower Swing Arm**, and secure them with **six Hex Head Bolts (106)**, **four Curved Washers (143)** and **six Nyloc Nuts (129)** using **Wrenches (151 & 152)**.
