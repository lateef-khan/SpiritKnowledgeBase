Hardware supplied in the box with the Sole TT8-2019, TT8-2020 (one SKU 588818 covers both ids) and TT8-2021, grouped by assembly step; the two books print the identical kit. This is the four-screw printing: the step-6 handlebar-cover screws (#90) are 4 pcs and a step-9 tablet-holder step is added, unlike the TT8-2016 kit (see the linked card).

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | #85 | Dia 10 x 2T | Split Washer | 6 pcs |
| 3 | #99 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Flat Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #100 | 3/8" x 2-1/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 6 pcs |
| 3 | #102 | 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| 3 | #131 | Dia 8 x Dia 23 x 1.5T | Curved Washer | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #85 | Dia 10 x 2T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #125 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 6 | #90 | 3.5 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #99 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Flat Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #63 | - | Safety Key | 1 pc |
| 8 | #126 | M5 x 10 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| 9 | #90 | 3.5 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |

Tools in the box: #104 L Allen Wrench, #105 M6 L Allen Wrench, #188 Phillips Head Screw Driver. There is no hardware step 1, 2 or 4.
