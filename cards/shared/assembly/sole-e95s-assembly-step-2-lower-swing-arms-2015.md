1. **Install two Wave Washers (183)** onto the left and right shafts of the mast tube. Slide the **left and right Lower Swing Arms (13, 14)** onto the shafts. Install the **two 3/8" x 3/4" Hex Head Bolts (136)** and **3/8" Flat Washers (175)** and tighten with **Wrench (194)**.
2. **Remove the tie-wrap from the Rod end Bearings on the left Connecting Arm (8)** and slide it into the bracket on the lower swing arm. Secure with a **5/16" x 1-1/4" Hex Head Bolt (134)**, **5/16" Flat Washer (180)** and **5/16" Nyloc Nut (168)** using the **13/14mm Wrench (194)** and **12/14mm Wrench (195)**. Repeat for the right Connecting Arm (9) and right Lower Swing Arm (14).
