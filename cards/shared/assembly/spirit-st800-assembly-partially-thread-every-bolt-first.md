The pre-assembly note on all three machines:

**During assembly, it is recommended to ensure that all bolts are in place and partially
threaded before completely tightening any one bolt.**

Each step then says which it wants: most say **Hand Tighten the Bolts**, and the steps
that finish a joint say **Wrench Tighten the Bolts**. Follow that literally - a joint
wrench-tightened early is a joint the next bracket will not line up with.

**The dumbbell rack and the functional trainer add three more sentences** that the bench
does not print:

- *"As with any assembled part, proper alignment is critical. While tightening the
  fasteners, be sure to leave room for adjustments. Do not fully tighten the fasteners
  until instructed to do so. Be careful to assemble the components in the sequence
  presented in this manual."*
- **Assembly takes professional installers approximately one hour on the dumbbell rack and
  approximately two hours on the functional trainer**, longer if it is your first. Both
  books recommend professional installers and a second person, because some components are
  large and heavy.
- *"It is important that you assemble your product in a clean, clear, uncluttered area."*

The manuals print the word as *aproximately*; the figures are one and two hours.

**Two parts must be lubricated as they go together on the functional trainer** - the left
and right guide bars in step three, and the two weight-stack guide bars in step four. See
[that card](../../st800ft/assembly/st800ft-assembly-guide-bars-weight-plates-and-the-fifty-pound-upgrade.md).
