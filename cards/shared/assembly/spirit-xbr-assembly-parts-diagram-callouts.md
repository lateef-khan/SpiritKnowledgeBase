The drawing carries fourteen callouts, written on the picture rather than keyed to a
letter or a number:

- CONSOLE
- CONSOLE FAN
- FRONT HANDLEBARS
- CONSOLE MAST
- CONSOLE MAST COVER
- PEDALS
- CRANK ARMS
- SEAT BACK
- SEAT
- PULSE GRIPS
- CUP HOLDER
- FRONT STABILIZER
- REAR STABILIZER
- MAIN FRAME

**All seven manuals name the same fourteen parts.**

## The touchscreen book renames one

The **XBR55ENT** drawing reads **PULSE SENSORS** where the other six read **PULSE
GRIPS**. It is the same part in the same place, and the ENT assembly steps still call
its cable the Hand Pulse Sensor Cable (27), as every one of these books does.

## What the drawing does not point at

It does not point at the **levellers**, the **transport wheels**, the **gas cylinder**
or the **seat carriage** - and the seat carriage is item 4, named in two of the four
assembly steps. **The drawing shows one cup holder and the carton packs two.**

**No residential recumbent drawing in this family shows a transport wheel**, because
these machines' manuals never mention one. See
[the absence card](spirit-xb-assembly-no-moving-or-transport-section.md).

## The 2023 books letter their diagram instead

The XBR55 2023 and XBR95 2023 drawings key **A to N** to a list, the XBR25 2023 drawing
**A to M**, and both add a tablet holder, an adjustable fan, console buttons, a seat
slide rail and a transport wheel. See
[the 2023 diagram card](spirit-xbr-2023-assembly-parts-diagram-letters.md) and
[the XBR25 2023 card](../../xbr25-2023/assembly/xbr25-2023-assembly-parts-diagram-letters.md).
