Install the **front inner handlebar cover (L, R) (107, 110)** and the right and left uprights with the
**four 3.5 x 12 mm sheet metal screws (74)**, using the combination M5 Allen wrench and Phillips head
screwdriver (102), and tighten them.

The sentence is printed as "Install the Front Inner Handlebar Cover (L,R) (107,110) **and** Right and
Left Uprights" in both books - read it as fitting the covers **to** the uprights. Step 6 (frame base
covers, six screws) follows. The TRX2500 and TRX3500 have no such step.
