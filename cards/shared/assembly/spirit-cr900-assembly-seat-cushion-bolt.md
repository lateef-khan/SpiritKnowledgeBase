**Four bolts, item 90, Button head Socket Bolt, M8 x 20mm.** That is what the CR900
2025 manual prints, and it is the only one of the three printings whose step and whose
table agree.

The other two disagree with themselves:

| Printing | Step four says | Step four table lists |
|---|---|---|
| **CR900 2018** | 4 BOLTS **(90)** | **85** BOLT M10XP1.5X25MM, qty **6** |
| **CR900ENT** | 4 BOLTS **(85)** | **85** BOLT M10XP1.5X25MM, qty **6** |
| **CR900 2025** | 4 BOLTS **(90)** | **90** Button head Socket Bolt M8 x 20mm, qty **4** |

Item 85 is the short seat-back bolt used six times in step three; six of it are already
listed in the step-three table. The step-four table in the two older books repeats that
line rather than printing the cushion bolt, so the 2018 book asks for a bolt its own
table never lists and the CR900ENT book resolves the mismatch the wrong way, by
changing the step to match the stray table line.

## Take item 90 and take four of them

The 2025 book is the same build reflowed - the four steps are word for word the
2018 steps - and it is the printing that corrected this table. Its figure is the one
that fits the step: four bolts for four holes, a button head that sits flush under a
seat pad, and an M8 rather than the M10 frame bolt.

**Do not drive an M10XP1.5X25MM into the seat cushion holes on the strength of the
older tables.** If the machine came with the 2018 or CR900ENT book, check the bag: the
cushion bolts are the button-head M8s, not the hex M10s left over from step three.

## Nothing else in step four is in doubt

The pedals are the same in all three printings, item 26L and 26R into crank arms 22 and
23, left-hand thread on the left, 300 in-lbs (34 N-m).
