1. Remove the **swing cover** (figures 6.2.1 and 6.2.2), then remove the **M10x60L (2 pcs)** and **M10x55L (1 pc)**
   screws; the armrest can then be removed.
2. Reassemble the handlebar by reversing the process.

The three books print this word for word. The book calls the fixed side handlebar the "armrest".
