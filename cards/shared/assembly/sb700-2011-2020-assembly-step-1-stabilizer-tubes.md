Hardware: **#82** 3/8" Nut (4 pcs), **#81** 3/8" x 3-1/2" Button Head Socket Bolt (4 pcs), **#83** 3/8" x 23mm Curved Washer (4 pcs).

1. Attach the front and rear floor stabilizer tubes by inserting **two Button Head Socket Bolts (81) through each tube**. Fasten two Curved Washers (83) and two Nuts (82) on each tube, using the wrench (85) provided.

**The front stabilizer is the one with the wheels mounted in the bottom of the tube.** That is how the manual tells the two apart.

**The 2021 machine changed #81 to a Carriage Bolt.** The bolt for the same job is not the same part.
