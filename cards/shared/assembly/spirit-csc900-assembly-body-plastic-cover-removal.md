**Fifteen captions in the V1.0 book, fourteen in the 2022 book, the same job.** The V1.0 (alternator) numbering:

1. Disassemble the console. 2. Disassemble the handrail. 3-1. Remove the left and right handrail covers 1.
3-2. Remove the left and right handrail covers 2. 4. Remove the left and right handrails. 5. Remove the
horizontal and left and right handrails as a whole. 6. Disassembly of the console (mast). 7. Remove the console
(mast). 8. Remove the inner shield fixing screw. 9. Remove the front inner shroud. 10. Remove the rear inner
shield. 11. Remove the housing fixing screws at the step. 12. Remove the bottom shield connecting plate fixing
bolt. 13. Remove the inner guard connecting plate fixing bolts. 14. Disconnect the internal power cable and
network cable. **15. Remove the case from the rear with two people working diagonally upwards.**

The 2022 book prints the same sequence as 1 to 14 with different words - "remove the cover #1 of handle", "cover
#2 of handle", "remove the console welding" and "take off the console welding" for steps 6 and 7 (the welded
console mast), "remove the inner bolts", "removal of the housing bolts at the safety step", "remove the bolt #1 of
plastic cover" printed twice for 12 and 13, and "unplug inside power cable and network cable connector" - and
stops there, without the two-person lift.

No screw counts or sizes are printed. Steps 12 and 13 in the V1.0 book carry their Chinese captions
("拆除外壳固定螺栓", remove the housing fixing bolts) beside the English.
