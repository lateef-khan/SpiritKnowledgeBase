**Belt figure: 450 N on a belt tension gauge, and 540 N for a new belt.** The induction-brake XBR95 and 2009 CR800 print 180 +/- 10 Hz and the 2020 CR800 180 to 205 Hz for a different belt path; those are other cards.

1. Take the front shrouds (29) (30) off.
2. Use a **13 mm open-end wrench** to loosen the **nyloc nut (88)** on the **J-bolt (87)** and remove the J-bolt (87).
3. Use a **6 mm Allen wrench** and a **14 mm open-end wrench** to release the **3/8" x 3/4" button head socket bolt (81)**, the **3/8" x 7T nyloc nut (89)** and the **3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washer (77)**, then take the **idler wheel assembly (10)** off.
4. Remove the **drive belt (54)**.
5. Remove the steel cable from the **flywheel (55)** and use a **15 mm open-end wrench** to release the **3/8"-UNF26 nut (121)** on the flywheel to remove it.
6. To refit, hang the drive belt (54) on the flywheel (55), then install the flywheel on the **mainframe (1)**. Align the flywheel with the **pulley (20)** and turn the **nut (120)** outward until it touches the mainframe, then use **15 mm and 17 mm open-end wrenches** to tighten the nut (120) and the 3/8"-UNF26 nut (121). Tighten the **nut (119)** and the 3/8"-UNF26 nut (121) on the other side.
7. Use the 6 mm Allen wrench and the 14 mm open-end wrench to tighten the button head socket bolt (81), nyloc nut (89) and flat washer to secure the idler wheel assembly on the mainframe, **then turn it back a quarter turn** to free it a little, and install the J-bolt (87) with the **M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88)** onto the idler wheel assembly (10) and the mainframe (1).
8. Return the drive belt (54) to the **drive pulley (20)** and the flywheel (55), then turn the drive pulley to check the belt runs smoothly in the centre. If it does not, loosen the 3/8"-UNF26 nut (121) on the flywheel and adjust both nuts (119) and (120); secure when aligned. Use the 13 mm wrench to tighten the M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88) on the J-bolt until the idler wheel (10) presses the belt tightly. **Use a belt tension gauge to make sure the tension reads 450 N.** Turn the drive pulley (20) to make sure the belt is centred and not falling off.
9. Assemble the **steel cable (62)** to the flywheel (55) last.

**Note printed in all four books:** a new drive belt must be set to **540 N**, because the belt has ductility and the tension falls after a period of use.

"(Note 1)" after the 450 N figure points at that note. The XBR25 2016 book omits step 9 (the steel cable) from this section but its gear-motor procedure still ends by refitting the cable. Both 2016 books and the ENT book head this "Flywheel and Drive Pulley"; the 2023 book "Flywheel, Drive Belt, and Idler Wheel Assembly".
