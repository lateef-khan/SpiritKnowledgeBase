**No wrench size, no nut size and no torque are printed.** The books give directions and tools only; the 8.0U and 8.5R books on the next chassis print 60 N-m for the same nut, and that figure does not transfer here.

**Pedals (6.2.1 / 6.3.1).** As shown in the figures, **the left pedal is disassembled counterclockwise and the right pedal is disassembled clockwise.** Refit by following the step in reverse order.

**Crank arms (6.2.2 / 6.3.2).** Remove the **crank arm end cap** and **loosen the nut anticlockwise**. Use the **extractor** to withdraw the crank arm. (Both sides are the same.) Refit by following the pedal step and the crank step in reverse order.

## The pedal direction contradicts the same book's maintenance page

The preventative-maintenance page of each book says the opposite. The 7.0R's 3.1: *"If the right pedal is loosened, lock it clockwise. If the left pedal is loosened, lock it counterclockwise"* - a left pedal that tightens counterclockwise loosens clockwise, which is what every other Spirit bike book prints (the left pedal is left-hand threaded and comes off clockwise). The 7.0U's 3.1 says the same in garbled form: *"If the right pedal is loosened clockwise and locked, the left pedal is locked counterclockwise."* **The replacement step's "left pedal counterclockwise" is the odd one out** among the maintenance page, the owner's manuals ("the left pedal has a reverse thread") and the rest of the range. Check the L and R stamped on the pedal posts and turn against the thread.

The 7.0R page is a flat image in the PDF; its text was read from a render, not the extraction.
