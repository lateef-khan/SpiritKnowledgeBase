1. Refer to the shroud procedure to remove the **rear shroud (L)** (7.0R: 6.9) or the **side case** (7.0U: 6.8).
2. As shown in figure 6.11.1 (7.0U: 6.10.1), **take off all the connectors and loosen the 2 screws** to remove the controller.
3. Refit by following the steps in reverse order.

No screw size is printed. The 7.0R parts list carries the part as **Generator/Brake Controller (237)** and its OCR parts page as "Brake Controller"; the 7.0U list as **Controller, Brake (123)**, and its wiring diagram as "FO90355 Brake controller". The controller converts the 24 V from the switching power supply into the 12 V the console runs on; the voltage checks at its 6-pin and 4-pin connectors are the no-power troubleshooting, not this card.
