"This product uses an external power supply. The power supply must first be plugged into the **power jack on the unit**. Next, plug the power supply into the appropriate wall outlet. When not in use, it is recommended to unplug the power supply from the wall outlet."

Two IMPORTANT notes follow in the ERG400 and ERG700 books:

- "If the product has been exposed to cold temperatures, allow the product to sit in room temperature to warm up before plugging in the power supply. Failure to do so may result in damage to the display or electronic components."
- "Do not operate the product if the power supply, its plug, or its cord has been damaged."

Where the jack is: the ERG400 says "on the front of the unit near the stabilizer tube"; the ERG700 only "on the unit"; the ERG800W's Figure 12 draws the **Adaptor (K)** cord running to a socket at the foot of the front upright, beside the front stabilizer, and says only "Plug the adaptor (K) like the below graphic". The ERG800W lists the adaptor under Parts Included; its electrical-safety page (outlet, breakers, voltage) is the safety section's card.

None of the other rowers in this family takes an adapter - their consoles run on batteries.
