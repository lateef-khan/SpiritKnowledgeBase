- 1 Main Frame
- 1 Rail Assembly
- 1 Center Aluminum Rail
- 1 Console Mast
- 1 Console Mast Cover
- 1 Front Stabilizer Cover
- 2 Swing Arms
- 2 Connecting Arms
- 1 Console
- **1 Water Bottle Holder** - CE800 2023 and CE800ENT only
- 2 Foot Pedals
- 1 Hardware Kit

**The 2021 CE800 carton has eleven lines, the 2023 CE800 and the CE800ENT twelve.**
The one added line is the water bottle holder, printed between the console and the
foot pedals. Everything else is word for word the same on all three, the CE800ENT
included, which prints "2 Swing arms" in lower case and is otherwise identical to
the 2023 list. See [the water bottle holder card](spirit-ce-assembly-water-bottle-holder.md)
for the clamp and the screws, which the panel does not mention.

**No panel lists a power cord.** These machines are self-powered - the console runs
off a generator driven by the pedals - so there is nothing to plug in and nothing
missing. The CE850 carton, by contrast, does list a power cord and an audio cable;
that is a different machine, not a better-packed one.

**No panel lists the levellers either**, although the machine has them and the noise
item tells you to adjust them. The CE850 and CE900 panels do list six.

The **tools** are printed in a second panel on the same page and are on their own
card - see [tools included](spirit-ce800-assembly-tools-included.md). The **2013
CE800 manual prints no parts panel at all**; it has only the tools drawing.

## The 2024 redesign prints the same twelve lines

The **CE800 2024** and **CE800ENT 2024** cartons list the same twelve lines in the same
order, the water bottle holder included. The CE800ENT 2024 panel still prints
"2 Swing arms" in lower case where the CE800 prints "2 Swing Arms", exactly as the
older touchscreen book did, and is otherwise identical.

Half of each of those two books is new text; this panel is not part of it.
