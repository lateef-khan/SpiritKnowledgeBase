Every panel is headed **PART, TYPE, DESCRIPTION, QTY**, four columns, on both
printings. **Step zero has no hardware panel** - it reuses the two bolts already in
the frame.

## Hardware for step 1

|---|---|---|---|

| 139 | Hex head bolt | 3/8" x 3-3/4" | 4 |
| 145 | Socket head cap bolt | 3/8" x 3/4" | 4 |
| 150 | Screw | M5x10 | 4 |
| 176 | Flat washer | 3/8"x19x1.5T | 6 |
| 186 | Spring washer | 3/8"x2T | 4 |
| 187 | Curved washers | 3/8" x 23 x 2T | 4 |

holder; the 2020 panel has seven rows and stops at item 187.

## Hardware for step 4

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 152 | Phillips head screw | M5 x 15L | 14 |
| 153 | Sheet metal screw | 3.5 x 12L | 8 |
| 154 | Sheet metal screw | 4 x 15mm | 2 |

## Item 152 is printed at two lengths in the same manual

The step-one panel and the step-one text of the 2022 printing call item 152 **M5 x
12mm**. The step-four panel above calls the same item **M5 x 15L**, and so does the
parts list, in both printings: `152  M5 × 15L_Phillips Head Screw`. **Two printed
places against one, and the two agree across both printings.** Treat item 152 as the
M5 x 15L screw; do not order an M5x12 on the strength of the step-one panel.

The parts-list count for item 152 rose from **22** on the 2020 printing to **26** on
the 2022 - exactly the four screws the bottle holder takes. See
[the bottle holder card](spirit-ce-assembly-water-bottle-holder.md).

## Two more counts that do not close

- Step one calls for **4 Socket Head Cap Bolts (No.145)** and the panel supplies four,
  but **step zero has already taken two of them out of the frame**. Six holes, four
  bagged bolts and two salvaged ones.
- Step four item 3 asks for four screws to hold **four** covers - 2 of item 104 and 2
  of item 105 - one screw per cover.
