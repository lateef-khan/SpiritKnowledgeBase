**Hardware Step 1** - rear stabilizer and console mast

| Item | Description | Qty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 108 | 3/8" Split Washer | 6 pcs |
| 106 | 3/8" Curved Washer | 2 pcs |
| 100 | 3/8" Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| 78 | 3/8" x 3-3/4" Hex Head Bolt | 2 pcs |
| 81 | 3/8" x 3/4" Socket Head Cap Bolt | 4 pcs |

**Hardware Step 2** - swing arms, covers and console

| Item | Description | Qty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 83 | M5 x 10mm Phillips Head Screw | 8 pcs |
| 85 | 4mm x 15mm Sheet Metal Screw | 3 pcs |
| 90 | 5/16" Nyloc Nut | 6 pcs |
| 113 | 5/16" Curved Washer | 4 pcs |
| 74 | 5/16" x 1-3/4" Hex Head Bolt | 6 pcs |
