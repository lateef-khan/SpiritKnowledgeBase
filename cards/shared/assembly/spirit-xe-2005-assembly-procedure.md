**This book names no item numbers at all.** Every part is called by name and every
fastener by its inch size. It is the only Spirit elliptical manual built that way.

## Step 1 - rear rail

1. Put the two flat washers on the **2-1/4" bolts** and hand tighten them through the
   **top** of the middle stabilizer tube into the rear rail assembly.
2. Put the four curved washers on the **3-3/4" bolts** and hand tighten them through
   the **front** of the middle stabilizer tube into the rear rail assembly.
3. Tighten all six bolts securely with the wrench provided.
4. **XE150 and XE350 only:** fit the four plastic covers for the rear rail assembly
   and the one front stabilizer cover, using the six 3/4" x 1/4" screws and the
   Phillips driver.

**The XE550 has no rear rail covers at this step.** It gets a two-piece rear shroud
instead, at the very end of step 4.

## Step 2 - console mast

1. Slide the console mast cover onto the mast as far as it will go, facing the
   correct way.
2. Unravel the wire harness at the top opening of the main body, feed it into the
   bottom of the mast tube and out of the top.
3. Install the mast into the receiving bracket. **One bolt is already installed in
   the bracket** and engages the slot at the bottom of the mast; it is tightened at
   the end with the other three.
4. Flat washer onto the **2-3/4" bolt**, curved washers onto the two **1" bolts**.
   Hand tighten the 2-3/4" bolt through the **left** side of the receiving tube, then
   the two 1" bolts through the **front**. A wire runs through the tube - do not
   damage or pinch it.
5. Tighten all four bolts with the two-sided wrench, as hard as you can.
6. **Two connectors, not three:** one 4-pin (hand pulse sensors) and one 10-pin (main
   wire harness). They are keyed; do not force them.
7. Store the excess wire in the mast and fit the console with the four 1/2" x 1/4"
   Phillips head screws.

## Step 3 - handle bars

1. Two large wave washers onto the handle bar axle, one each side.
2. Slide the left and right handle bars on - each has an L or R sticker.
3. Large flat washers onto the two **5/8" x 3/8" bolts**, tightened into the threaded
   holes in the ends of the axle.
4. Fit the plastic covers over the handle bar and axle connections with the six self
   tapping Phillips head screws.

## Step 4 - foot pads and connecting arms

1. On the pedal arms, in the middle on top, two axles are already installed in the
   bushing housings. Remove the bolt (one will be loose) and washer from **one side
   of each axle**, then remove the axle and set it aside.
2. Hold the rear of the left foot pad in your right hand and the connecting arm just
   in front of it in your left, and place the foot pad over the bushing housing.
3. Line up the large hole in the side of the bracket under the foot pad with the hole
   in the bushing housing and push the axle all the way in until the washer sits flush
   against the bracket. **You may have to wiggle the axle while moving the foot pad
   slightly to get it in.**
4. Thread the bolt and washer from job 1 back into the axle and tighten with two
   wrenches - one on **each** axle bolt.
5. Line the pivoting rod end of the connecting arm up with the handle bar bracket,
   rod end **inside** the bracket. One **1-1/4" x 3/8" bolt** through the bracket and
   the rod end, then a flat washer and a Nyloc nut, tightened with a wrench on the
   nut and one on the bolt.
6. Repeat for the right side.
7. Fit the plastic covers over the rod end and handle bar joint: **the front inside
   hole takes a self tapping screw and the two rear holes take the 3/8" machine
   screws (short)**. Use the short Phillips driver for the inside screws.
8. Two wheel covers with the four 5/8" machine screws (long).
9. **XE350 and XE550 only:** fit the locking pins for the adjustable foot pads into
   the holes you want. See the Foot pad angle adjustment section of the manual.
   **The XE150's foot pads do not adjust and it has no locking pins to fit.**
10. **XE550 only:** fit the two plastic parts that make up the **rear shroud** with
    seven Phillips head screws.

## What is different between the three machines

| | Rear rail covers, step 1 | Foot pad locking pins, step 4 | Rear shroud, step 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| XE150 | yes, four covers | **no** | no |
| XE350 | yes, four covers | yes | no |
| XE550 | **no** | yes | yes, two parts, seven screws |

**The XE550 has the longest build and the XE150 the shortest.** Do not fit rear rail
covers to an XE550 and do not look for locking pins on an XE150; its foot pads do not
adjust.

## The 2007 machines have no equivalent step

The XE100, XE200 and XE300 all have adjustable foot pads, and none of them needs an
assembly job for it: the **XE100 and XE200 have a spring release pull pin** under the
rear of the foot pads and the **XE300 has an Acme crank** with a degree scale on the
sides, both fitted at the factory. Only this 2005 book asks the builder to install a
pin, and only on two of its three machines.

## Nothing here levels the machine or fits a transport wheel

There is no levelling step, no leveller in the build and no levelling instruction
anywhere in this manual. The transport wheels are already on the machine - see
[the transport wheel card](../../ce800-2013/assembly/ce800-2013-assembly-transport-wheels.md).

## The manual says "XE550 only" in a book that does not cover the XE550

Not here, but in the console and programs chapters, the **XE100/XE200/XE300** manual
repeats the phrase "(XE550 only. See Heart Rate section for details)" twelve times,
copied from this book. There is no XE550 in that manual.
