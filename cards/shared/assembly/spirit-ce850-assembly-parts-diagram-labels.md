The drawing is not headed and the labels are not lettered. Twelve words point at the
machine on the 2020 printing:

- Console
- Console fan
- Console mast (beneath console)
- Swing arm
- Pulse sensors
- Front stabilizer
- Main frame
- Connecting arm
- Foot pedals
- Levelers
- Rails
- Rear stabilizer

## The thirteenth label comes and goes

- **2016** prints thirteen: the twelve above plus **WATER BOTTLE HOLDER**, between
  the front stabilizer and the main frame. It calls the grips **PULSE GRIPS** rather
  than pulse sensors, and prints **SWING ARM** and **CONSOLE MAST** without the
  "(beneath console)" note.
- **2020** prints the twelve above. The bottle holder label is gone, and so is any
  bottle holder part.
- **2022** prints thirteen again, the label back in the same place, and this time the
  machine has one - it is in the carton list and step one fits it.

**A 2016 diagram is not evidence that a 2016 machine has a bottle holder.** See
[the bottle holder card](spirit-ce-assembly-water-bottle-holder.md).

**This is the only Spirit elliptical diagram that labels levellers and rails.** The
CE800 and CE800ENT drawings label neither.
