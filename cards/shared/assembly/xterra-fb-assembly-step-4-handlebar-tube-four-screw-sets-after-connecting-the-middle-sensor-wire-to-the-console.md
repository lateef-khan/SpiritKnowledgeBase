Hardware: pre-installed on the handlebar tube, **4 screw sets (#12)**. Tool: the 5 mm Allen key (72).

1. Remove the 4 screw sets (No. 12) from the handlebar tube (No. 04).
2. Connect the middle sensor wire (No. 19) with the console (No. 08).
3. Attach the handlebar tube (No. 04) to the rear support tube (No. 02) and tighten with the 4 screw sets (No. 12).

The console (08) comes mounted on the handlebar tube (the parts list draws them as one item, 04/08), so there is no separate console step in these three books. This is step 4 on the uprights and step 5 on the recumbent.

The November 2019 service document is written around the one thing that goes wrong here: **"DO NOT PINCH MIDDLE SENSOR WIRE TO CONSOLE! TIP: Console front support tube has cutout, and be sure to slip the wire into notch, secure Molex connection for speed sensor, and push terminals down the tubing. Then, use 5mm Allen wrench to tighten handlebars to main frame."** Its photo captions repeat it: "Console wire slips into notch, secure connection. Secure middle speed sensor wire & push down tube."
