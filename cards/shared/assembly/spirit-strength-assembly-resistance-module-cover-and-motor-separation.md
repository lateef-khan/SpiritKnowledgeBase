*(Tool: Phillips screwdriver)*

1. Remove the screw (1); the cover (2) and the connecting plate (3) can then be removed.
2. Remove the screw (4) to separate the cover (2) from the connecting plate (3).
3. Pull the drive shaft (5) from the motor (6).

*(Note: don't lose the iron on the drive shaft.)*

The "iron" is a loose piece on the drive shaft that the drawing shows and the text does not name further; it is
the one thing on this page the manual warns about.

The "Digital Resistance Module" is the i-Strength line's motorised resistance box, part 72 in the CSI owner's manuals; the screw numbers are the call-outs on the manual's exploded drawings.
