Item numbers in brackets are from the exploded view.

**Step 1 - uprights.** Install the Right and Left Uprights (4, 5) into the
Upright Cover L, R (59, 60) and Frame Base Cover (L & R) (61, 62). Connect the
Computer Cable (Lower) (49) and Computer Cable (Middle) (50). Insert the Right
and Left Uprights (4) and (5) into the Frame Base (2) and use the Combination
Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (131) to tighten **8 pcs of
5/16" x 15mm Button Head Socket Bolts (130)**.

**Step 2 - console.** Connect the Speed Cable (170-7) into the Speed Cable
(Upper) (37). Connect the Incline Cable (170-8) into the Incline Cable (Upper)
(38). Connect the Computer Cable (Middle) (50) and Computer Cable (Middle &
Upper) (50-1).

Insert Console Assembly Lower (52) into the Right and Left Uprights (4) and (5)
and secure with **4 pcs of 3/8" x 1-1/2" Button Head Socket Bolts (133)** with
**4 pcs of Ø10 x 2.0T Split Washers (113)**, using the L Allen Wrench (132).
**NOTE: do NOT tighten all screws yet.**

Then connect Computer Cable (53-23) to Computer Cable (Middle Upper) (50-1),
Connecting Cable (53-24) to Connecting Cable (52-16), Connecting Cable (Upper)
(53-25) to Connecting Cable (Lower) (52-17), and Connecting Cable (Red) (53-26)
to Connecting Cable (52-18). Place Console Assembly (Top) (53) onto Console
Assembly (Bottom) (52) and use the Phillips Head Screw Driver (148) to tighten
**4 pcs of M5 x 45mm Phillips Head Screws (160)**.

**Step 3 - handrail support.** Install the Handrails Support (119) between the
Right and Left Uprights (4, 5) with **4 pcs of 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket
Bolts (150)**, using the Combination Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (131) and the
Phillips Head Screw Driver (148).

**Step 4 - covers.** Install the Upright Cover (R) and (L), (60) and (59), on the
Right and Left Uprights (4) and (5) and Console Support (6) using **8 pcs of
3.5 x 16mm Sheet Metal Screws (142)** with the Phillips Head Screw Driver (148).
Install the Frame Base Covers (L) and (R), (61) and (62), on the Frame Base (2)
and secure with **6 pcs of M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws (120)**, same
screwdriver.

**Step 5 - tablet holder.** Place the Tablet Holder (169) on Console Assembly
(Top) (53) and use the Phillips Head Screw Driver (148) to tighten **3 pcs of
M5 x 25mm Phillips Head Screws (166)**.

**NOTE: tighten all screws only after all components have been assembled.**

The step 2 text names the speed wire 170-7 and the incline wire 170-8, while the
step 2 illustration labels the speed wire 107-7. The manual does not resolve
which number is right; the incline wire is labelled 170-8 in both places.
