Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step, identical in the ATT8 and TT8C 2011 books. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | #85 | 3/8" x 2T | Split Washer | 8 pcs |
| 3 | #100 | 3/8" x 1" | Hex Head Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #101 | 3/8" x 3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #102 | 3/8" x 2" | Hex Head Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #131 | 3/8" x 23 x 1.5T | Curved Washer | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #125 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #85 | 3/8" x 2T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 6 | #99 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #92 | 5 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 6 pcs |
| 7 | #87 | 5 x 16 mm | Self-Tapping Screw | 2 pcs |
| 7 | #84 | M5 | Speed Nut Clip | 4 pcs |
| 8 | #126 | M5 x 10 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 8 pcs |
| 8 | #89 | 3.5 x 12 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |

Tools in the box: #104 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver, #108 12/14 mm Wrench, #105 M6 Allen Wrench, #63 Safety Key. Step 7 also calls for four Phillips Head Screws (129) for the motor cover; they are cited in the step text, not the checklist panel.
