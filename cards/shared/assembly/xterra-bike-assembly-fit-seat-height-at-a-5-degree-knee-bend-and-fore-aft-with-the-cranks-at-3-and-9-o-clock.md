"Take some time to learn how to properly adjust the bike to your body ... Riding the bike when it is incorrectly adjusted can result in discomfort and increase your risk of injury."

**Seat height**

1. Standing next to the bike, adjust the seat until it is about hip height.
2. Rotate the crank arms until the pedals are in the vertical position: 12 and 6 o'clock.
3. Place your foot in the toe cage of the pedal closest to the floor (the air-bike books: "on the pedal") and mount the bike. Ensure that the ball of your foot is over the center of the pedal. **Your leg should be slightly bent at the knee, about 5 degrees.**
4. If your leg is too straight or your foot cannot touch the pedal, lower the seat. If your leg is bent too much, raise the seat.
5. Dismount. Loosen the quick release lever on the seat post (the belt-drive cycle and the air bikes: "the knob on the seat post") and adjust up or down.
6. When the seat is in the desired position, tighten the quick release to secure the seat post.
7. Note the final position mark on the seat post for future reference.

**Seat forward/aft** (the 2018 belt-drive cycle and the air bikes say "forward/back")

8. Sit on the bike with the crank arms in the 3 and 9 o'clock positions. A proper forward/aft position (for road bike training) is achieved when **the small bump at the top of your shin is above the pedal axle**.
9. Dismount. Loosen the quick release under the seat (belt-drive cycle and air bikes: "the knob under the seat") and slide the seat forward or backward as desired; tighten it.

The same page on all five books; only the name of the fastener changes - a quick release lever on the chain-drive indoor cycles, a knob on the belt-drive cycle and the air bikes. Step 6 says "tighten the quick release" even in the books whose step 5 says "knob". The indoor cycles continue with a handlebar fit section (the linked card); the air bikes stop at the seat.
