Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #82 | 3/8" | Nut | 4 pcs |
| #81 | 3/8" x 3-1/2" | **Carriage Bolt** | 4 pcs |
| #83 | 3/8" x 23mm | Curved Washer | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #91 | M5 x 10mm | Phillips Head Screw | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 4**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #47 | ø8.5 x ø16 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| #88 | M8 x P1.25 x **25L** | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #143 | 5/16" x 15L | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #144 | 5/16" x 7T | Nylon Nut | 4 pcs |
| #146 | ø8 x 1.5T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 6**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #101 | 4 x 16mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 3 pcs |

**Three changes from the SB900 2020 pack.** #81 became a Carriage Bolt; #88 grew from 20L to **25L** and the handlebar washers changed number as well as size (#47 flat washer and #146 split washer replace #48 and #49); and #143/#144 were added for the two drink bottle holders. The #93 tapping screw is gone.

**#146 is described two ways in this manual.** The step 4 hardware box calls it a `ø8 x 1.5T Split Washer`; the step 4 instruction beside it calls the same part a `5/16"x1.5T Split Washer`. The manual never reconciles the two.

**Item numbers are not shared between models.** The 2023 machines renumbered the whole pack - there `#93` is a button head socket bolt, while on the 2019-2021 machines `#93` is an M5 tapping screw. Always use the checklist printed in your own machine's manual.
