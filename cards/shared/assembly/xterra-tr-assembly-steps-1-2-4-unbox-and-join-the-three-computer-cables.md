The console cable is in three pieces - lower (in the frame), middle (in the upright) and upper (at the
console). Two of the five assembly steps are nothing but joining them, and they bracket the upright
step:

1. **Step 1.** Remove the unit from the box and put it on a flat and level floor.
2. **Step 2.** Connect the **middle** computer cable with the **lower** computer cable. The book notes
   this step "may work better with a second person assisting". (Step 3 then inserts the uprights.)
3. **Step 4.** Connect the **upper** computer cable with the **middle** computer cable - again "with a
   second person assisting". Step 5 then bolts the console support on; that page warns not to pinch
   the computer cables while attaching the console.

The callout numbers differ by book:

| Book | Upper | Middle | Lower |
|---|---|---|---|
| TR150 2021, TR260 2022, TRX1000 2021 | 37 | 38 | 39 |
| TRX1400 2023 | 46 | 47 | 45 |

No step says which way round the plug goes or how to tuck the joined connector back into the tube.
The TRX1400 book's text layer carries a duplicated ghost of these steps in a different layout
("STEP 2. Connect the Computer Cable (Middle)…"); the printed page is the numbered list above.
