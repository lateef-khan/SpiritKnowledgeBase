Hardware: **#91** M5 x 10mm Screw (2 pcs). The 2021 checklist calls the same part an M5 x 10mm **Phillips Head Screw**.

1. Install the rear stabilizer cover with two Phillips Head Screws (91).

Identical in every SB900 owner's manual from 2014 to 2021. The instruction names no tool.

**The SB700 prints a different instruction for the same job**: "Attach the Rear Stabilizer cover with two Screws (91). Tighten using the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (86)."

**The SB900 2023 has no rear stabilizer cover step at all.** Its step 2 is the sliding handlebar mount.
