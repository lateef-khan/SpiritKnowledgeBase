printings. The four panels are identical between 2021 and 2025.

## Item 52 is called for and never supplied

Step one item 3 fits the centre handlebars to the handlebar bracket "using 4 BOLTS
(52)". **No panel in either manual carries an item 52**, and the parts list does not
reach it either. Its size is printed nowhere. Both printings have the defect.

The panel does supply **twelve** of item 54, and steps 1 and 4 of that step use eight,
so four M10 x p1.5 x 20L bolts are spare in the bag - the same count as the missing
item 52. They may be the intended bolts; the manual never says so, so do not tell a
customer that they are.

## The two step-three bolt lengths matter

Item **44 is the longer bolt at M10 x 60** and goes in first, on both sides of the
swing arm, left untightened. Item **43 is the shorter at M10 x 55L** and goes through
the swing arm and the handlebar last. The manual calls them "longer" and "shorter"
without printing the lengths in the text - they are only in the panel above.

## Counts that do not close

Step three supplies **10 washers (63)** and **6 nuts (71)** for two sides, and the
printed steps use one washer per bolt side and one nut per bolt: six bolts, six nuts
and eight washer positions. Two washers are spare per the panel. Step four supplies
**6 screws (49)** and its two items use four.
