1. Plug the power cord into the treadmill and the other end into the wall outlet. Flip the **power
   switch, located near the power cord on the treadmill**, to ON. Make sure the **safety tether cord** is
   properly attached and in place, and press **START**.
2. The belt tension should be set correctly out of the box. If the belt slips when you step on it or
   slides to the side, adjust it as described in the Treadbelt Tracking Adjustment section near the end
   of the manual.

**The TR300 adds calibration**: its step 1 ends "Run the calibration procedure", and a third item says
to perform the calibration procedure in the General Maintenance section on page 19. **The TR6.4 book
prints neither sentence** - its final process is the two items above and nothing else.

The drawing on both pages shows the M6 L Allen wrench (103) twice, at the rear roller bolts - the tool
the belt adjustment uses.
