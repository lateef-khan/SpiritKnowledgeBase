Once the machine is assembled and standing on a flat level floor:

1. Use a **1/2" wrench** to **loosen the top nut** of the levellers.
2. Make sure the **two centre levellers are screwed all the way in**.
3. **Adjust the four corner levellers by hand** as necessary to remove any wobble.
4. **Unscrew the two centre levellers until they touch the floor.**
5. **Tighten all the top nuts** against the bottom of the stabilizer tubes.
6. Make sure the **bottom nut remains cinched against the levelling foot**.

**Six levellers, and the middle pair does a different job.** The four corners carry the
levelling; the centre pair is set last and only until it touches.

## The MED-badged 7.5S prints four levellers, not six, and its parts list agrees

The 2021 MED book for a machine called **7.5S** prints the same page with the centre pair
removed: *"Use a 1/2" wrench to loosen the top nut of the levelers. **Adjust the front 4
corner levelers by hand** as necessary to remove any wobble... Then tighten all the top nuts."*
No centre pair, no unscrewing step. **Its parts list carries four `Ø75 Foot Leveler` rows
(item 75) and no other adjustable foot** - and so does the 2025 7.5S parts list, which lists
`Ø75 Foot Pad` four times at the same item number while its text asks for six. **On a 7.5S,
count four.** See
[the four-leveller card](../../7-5s-med/assembly/7-5s-med-assembly-levelling-four-front-corner-glides.md).

The MED **7.0S** prints this page word for word, six levellers and all, and its parts list
carries six `Rubber Foot` rows (item 30). The count is right on that machine.

## The same book contradicts this on its maintenance page

All three manuals repeat levelling as the last remedy in their noise item, and there
they say **2 levelling pads on the bottom of the rear stabilizer**, not six:

- the **MS300** says two pads and a **½" wrench** - the same wrench size as this page;
- the **7.0S** and **7.5S** say two pads and a **14mm wrench**, which is not the same
  size as ½" (12.7 mm) and not a size any of the three cartons supplies.

Neither page mentions the other. This page is the one that tells you where the levellers
are and in what order to set them; take the count and the order from here. See
[the two-pad card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md) for
the machines that print the other sentence.

## The 8.5S and 8.5S-Fit print a shorter version of this page

Their setting-up page keeps the ½" wrench and the top nut and drops the count, the
centre pair and the order. See
[that card](spirit-85s-assembly-levelling-glides-by-hand.md).

## The levelling glides are a diagram call-out on all three

**F. Leveling glides** on the 7.0S, **K. Leveling glides** on the 7.5S and **6. Leveling
glides** on the MS300. No step of any of the three builds fits one; they arrive on the
machine.
