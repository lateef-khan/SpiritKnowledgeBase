> **CAUTION: Damage to the bike during assembly is not covered as part of the limited
> Spirit Fitness warranty.** Take care not to drop or lean the bike on its side.
> Carefully stand the bike up in the normal upright position on a stable surface so it
> will not tip over during assembly.

Word for word in both manuals, in the pre-assembly page before any step.

**Three separate instructions live in that block:** do not drop it, do not lean it on
its side, and build it standing upright on a stable surface. The JB950 adds elsewhere on
the same page that the frame should be propped on the large foam packaging while the
stabilizers go on, and that lifting the main frame always needs a second person.

## This is an assembly warning, not the warranty text

The warranty chapter of each book has its own exclusion list. This sentence is the only
place either manual ties assembly damage to warranty cover, and it is printed where the
installer will see it rather than in the warranty section.

## The AB950 dropped it

The 2024 AB950 pre-assembly page carries none of this - no caution, no warranty
sentence, no instruction about which way up to stand the machine.
