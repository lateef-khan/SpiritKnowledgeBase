1. As shown in figures 6.10.1 and 6.10.2 (8.5R: 6.6.1 and 6.6.2), remove the **Phillips head screws (M5 x 3 pcs)** and the **nuts (M8 x 2 pcs)** to remove the **idler wheel assembly**.
2. As shown in figure 6.10.3 (8.5R: 6.6.3), remove the hex head bolts on both sides to remove the **flywheel**.

**The flywheel bolt count is printed two ways.** The 8.0U prints *"remove Hex Head Bolts 2PCS (1/4"x4PCS) each on both sides"*; the 8.5R prints *"loosen the screws (1/4"x4PCS) on both sides of flywheel"*. Four 1/4" bolts in all is the reading both share; the parts list carries **eight 1/4" x UNC20 x 3/4" hex head bolts (115)** for the idler and flywheel together, with **four Ø1/4" split washers (116)** and **eight 1/4" x 13 x 1.0T flat washers (117)**.

The two M8 nuts on the idler are the belt-tension nuts: the troubleshooting chapter sends a slipping belt to "figure 6.10.2 to adjust tension by tightening nut", and prints no tension figure. Both books also say it is **normal to hear the flywheel when pedalling in reverse**. The idler wheel assembly is items 9(A), 9(B) and 9(C) with **Idler Bracket (A) (10), 2** and **(B) (11)**; the flywheel is item 86.
