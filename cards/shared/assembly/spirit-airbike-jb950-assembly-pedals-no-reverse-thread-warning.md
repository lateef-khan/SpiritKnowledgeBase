What the three manuals print for the pedals, and the whole of it:

- **AB900 and AB950:** "Use a WRENCH (100) to fasten the **LEFT PEDAL (45L)** onto the
  **LEFT CRANK ARM (16L)** and **RIGHT PEDAL (45R)** onto the **RIGHT CRANK ARM (16R)**
  respectively."
- **JB950:** "Assemble **PEDALS (54)** to bike using **15MM WRENCH (158)**. Use of a
  **large pedal wrench (not included)** is highly recommended to secure the pedals."

**None of the three mentions a reverse thread, a left-hand thread or a counterclockwise
turn, and none prints a torque figure in the assembly step.** A whole-document search of
all three books for *reverse*, *counterclockwise* and *left-hand thread* returns only
seat and handlebar handles - nothing about a pedal.

**Treat the left pedal as left-hand threaded anyway.** Every Spirit indoor cycle manual
that does mention it - the CB900, CIC800, CIC850 and both XIC600 printings - says the
left pedal is reverse-threaded on the same kind of crank. The absence of the warning is a
wording choice, not evidence of a different pedal.

## The AB900 asks for a tool the JB950 does not

The AB900 pre-assembly says all tools needed are in the packaging **except a rubber
mallet, recommended when assembling the pedals**. The JB950 says all tools are included
but recommends a **large pedal wrench** that is not. The AB950 says neither.

## Only the AB950 prints a torque, and not here

**33-37 lb-ft**, in the maintenance chapter, for the pedal-to-crank-arm bolt. It is not
in the assembly step and it is not stated for the AB900 or the JB950. See
[that card](../../ab950-2024/assembly/ab950-2024-assembly-pedal-torque-33-to-37-lb-ft.md).
