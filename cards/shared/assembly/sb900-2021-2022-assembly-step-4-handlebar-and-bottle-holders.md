Hardware: **#47** ø8.5 × ø16 × 1.5T Flat Washer (4 pcs), **#88** M8 × P1.25 × 25L Button Head Socket Bolt (4 pcs), **#143** 5/16" × 15L Button Head Socket Bolt (4 pcs), **#144** 5/16" × 7T Nylon Nut (4 pcs), **#146** ø8 × 1.5T Split Washer (4 pcs).

1. Install the Handlebar (6), the Fixing Plate (140) and the Sliding Handlebar Mount (8) with four M8 × P1.25 × 25L Button Head Socket Bolts (88), four ø8.5 × 16 × 1.5T Flat Washers (47) and four Split Washers (146). Tighten the bolts securely by using the **Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (86)**.
2. Install the Drink Bottle Holder (L) (141) and the Drink Bottle Holder (R) (142) with four 5/16" × 15L Button Head Socket Bolts (143) and four 5/16" × 7T Nylon Nuts (144), by using the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (86) and the **13/14m/m Wrench (145)**.

**#146 is described two ways in this manual.** The hardware box beside this step calls it a `ø8 × 1.5T Split Washer`; this instruction calls the same part a `5/16" × 1.5T Split Washer`. The manual never reconciles them.

**#88 got longer for 2021** - M8 × P1.25 × **25L** here against M8 × P1.25 × 20L on the SB900 2014-2020. The washers changed number as well: #47 and #146 replace #48 and #49.

The SB700 2021 does the same job with a #149 socket head cap bolt and #48/#150 washers, and the same #143/#144 for the bottle holders.
