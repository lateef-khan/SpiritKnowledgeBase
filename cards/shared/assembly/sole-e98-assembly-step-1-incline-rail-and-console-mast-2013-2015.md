1. **Install the Incline Rail Assembly (2) into the U-channel bracket of the main frame (1).** From the sides install **two Hex Head Bolts (117)** with **two Flat Washers (158)** and **two Nylon Nuts (145)**. From the top install **four Hex Head Bolts (115)**, **four Split Washers (166)**, **four Flat Washers (154)**, and **four Star Washers (168)**. Tighten with **Wrenches (169 & 172)**.
2. **Connect the three Incline Motor Power lines by matching the Red, White and Black color codes of the wires (43)** and the **3-pin position Sensor Connector (44)**. Slide the **Console Mast Cover (83)** onto the **Console Mast (12)**. Tie the twist tie to the **Computer Cable (45)**, pull it up through the mast until it exits the top, and install the mast into the receiving bracket. **Pull slightly on the cable at the top while installing** so it does not get pinched and shorted.
3. **Put one Split Washer (167) onto the Long Hex Head Bolt (118)** through the left side of the receiving bracket, and **two Curved Washers (164) onto the two Short Hex Head Bolts (116)** through the front. Using **Wrench (169)**, tighten the (116) bolts first, then the (118) bolt, and lastly the pre-installed fourth bolt, firmly. **This is the main joint of the unit; if not tightened sufficiently it leads to noise and instability.**
4. **Plug the Computer Cable (45), two Hand pulse Cables (52 & 56), the Ground Wire (192), and the Resistance & Incline wires (184 & 185)** into the back of the console. Secure the **Console (40)** with **four Phillips Head Screws (128)**, tightened with driver (170 or 171).
