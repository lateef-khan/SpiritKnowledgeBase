| Part No. | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 1/2 | Main Frame | 1 |
| 3 | Seat Post (the 2019 book: Seat Support Tube) | 1 |
| 4 | Front Handlebar Set (2019: Handlebar Tube/Console, 04/08) | 1 |
| 5 | Front Stabilizer | 1 |
| 6 | Rear Stabilizer | 1 |
| 7 | Seat | 1 |
| 8 | Computer | 1 |
| 9 | Pedal (L/R) | 1 |
| 74/75 | Back Cushion Support Tube Set | 1 |
| 76 | Rear Handlebar | 1 |
| 77 | Back Cushion | 1 |
| 89 | Remote Control Holder | 1 |
| - | Hardware Bag, Manual | 1 each |

**Hardware bag** (both books, same numbers and quantities; the 2019 book also draws the sizes):

| Part No | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Club Knob | 1 |
| 17 | Nylon Nut M8 | 2 |
| 13 | Curved Washer 8.3 x 22 x 1.5t | 2 |
| 78 | Carriage Bolt M8 x 50L | 2 |
| 81 | Allen Screw M8 x 40L | 2 |
| 55 | Flat Washer 8.3 x 25 x 2t | 2 |
| 84 | Spring Washer M8 | 4 |
| 80 | Allen Screw M8 x 45L | 4 |
| 72 | Allen Key (5 mm) | 1 |
| 73 | Hex Head Wrench | 1 |

The two curved washers (13) in the bag are for the rear handlebar; the four curved washers and four domed nuts for the stabilizers are pre-installed on the stabilizer tubes and are not in the bag. "The parts for this machine are preassembled on the unit."

The 2021 book swaps the two page headings - its "Assembly Pack Checklist" is the parts list and its "Assembly Part Checklist" is the hardware - and every footer in it still reads "FB150 Folding Bike".
