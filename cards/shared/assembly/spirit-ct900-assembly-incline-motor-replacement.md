**The lifting motor must be set to a minimum stroke of 315 mm before it is fitted.** The CT900 and CT900ENT
books print the figure twice - inside the procedure and as a chapter of their own ("Incline Range must be
adjusted to 315 mm minimum prior to installation", over a drawing); the CT1000ENT book prints it inside the
procedure only. It is the largest incline-motor setting in any Spirit treadmill book: the CT850 uses 235 mm
and the XT machines 210 or 225 mm.

1. Remove the motor cover (step 4.2.1 of the guide, 8.43 in the CT1000ENT book).
2. "Move the machine up on the paper tube" - prop the machine up - and use **two No. 17 open-end wrenches**
   to remove the screws fixing the lifting motor to the lifting frame, then the same two wrenches to remove the
   screw fixing the lifting motor to the main frame. Detach the motor and remove the wiring that goes with it.
3. Set the new lifting motor to a **minimum stroke of 315 mm**. How to get there: the CT900 books say "about
   five turns in the end"; the CT1000ENT book says "Circle to end and then circle 5 turns in reverse" - run the
   screw to its end and back it off five turns.
4. Fit the new motor in the order of removal and connect the control and grounding lines.

No torque is printed, the drawing does not say between which points the 315 is measured, and none of the
three books says to recalibrate the incline afterwards in this procedure.

The guide is a machine translation. Its words, as used on this card: **electronic watch** is the console,
**cross screwdriver** is a Phillips screwdriver, **umbrella head screws** are the guide's name for the
console and cover screws (no size is printed), and a **17th T-Shirt** or **13th T-sleeve** is a 17 mm or 13 mm
T-handle socket.
