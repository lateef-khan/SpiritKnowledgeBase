1. Use a screwdriver to remove the left and right **side adjustment screws**.
2. Use the **No. 8 L-type hex wrench** (8 mm) to remove the **two rear wheel screws** - the rear roller's.
3. Remove the motor cover (step 4.2.1 of the guide).
4. Release the drive belt as in steps 4.3.3 and 4.3.4 of the motor procedure. "Please refer to 4.5.1 and
   4.5.2 for the assembly of the left and right chain cover" - printed so; the covers meant are whatever
   steps 1 and 2 took off.
5. Remove the front roller with the No. 8 L-shaped hex wrench.
6. Fit the new front and rear rollers, and assemble in the order of removal.
7. When assembled, adjust the running belt tension so the belt runs true, and set the drive belt tension as in
   step 4.3.4 - **13 mm T-socket, 120 ~ 130 Hz**.

The CT1000ENT book prints the same six steps and then stops before the tension figure; its motor is tensioned
in pounds, so it is on its own card. No torque is printed.

The guide is a machine translation. Its words, as used on this card: **electronic watch** is the console,
**cross screwdriver** is a Phillips screwdriver, **umbrella head screws** are the guide's name for the
console and cover screws (no size is printed), and a **17th T-Shirt** or **13th T-sleeve** is a 17 mm or 13 mm
T-handle socket.
