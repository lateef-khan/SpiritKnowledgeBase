The drawing is not headed and the labels are not lettered or numbered. Eleven words
point at the machine:

- Console
- Console fan
- Console mast
- Console mast cover
- Swing arms
- Pulse sensors
- Flywheel
- Pedals
- Front stabilizer
- Main frame
- Rear stabilizer

**The 2023 printing adds a twelfth, WATER BOTTLE HOLDER**, pointing at the console
mast below the pulse sensors. That is the only change to the drawing.

**Nothing on this diagram is levellers, rails or transport wheels**, although the
machine has all three - the noise item names two levelling pads under the rear
stabiliser, and step one bolts a rail assembly and a centre aluminium rail on. The
CE850 diagram does label levellers and rails; this one does not.

The **CE800 2013 manual prints no such diagram at all** - it goes from the safety
pages straight to pre-assembly. The **CE800ENT** prints a different eight-label
drawing; see [its own card](../../ce800ent/assembly/ce800ent-assembly-parts-diagram-labels.md).
