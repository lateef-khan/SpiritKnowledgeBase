1. **Press and hold the red button on the handle with your thumb** (step B) and at the same time lift
   the handle up until it locks; release the button during the lifting. Do the same on the other side.
2. **Rotate the left and right handlebar stoppers to the bottom of the main frame** in the direction
   of the arrow (step C), so that they are not exposed, to avoid damage.

The stoppers (the parts diagram calls them **Upright Stopper**, label L) are what catch the uprights
when the machine is folded; the folding instructions turn them back out so "the stopper catches the
Upright". Step 3 (tablet holder and safety key) follows.
