**Set the resistance to the highest level before the steel cable comes off**, and centre the belt on the pulley
with the 17 mm wrench on the flywheel nuts before tensioning.

The XS200-SS003 book (cs800-2016):

1. Remove the idler wheel assembly (the idler card). Use the console to set resistance level 20 and remove the
   **steel cable**.
2. Take the steel cable off the flywheel, then take the drive belt off the drive pulley.
3. Use a **15 mm wrench** to release the **two 3/8" UNF26 x 9T nuts** securing the flywheel to the main frame.
   Take off the drive belt connected to the flywheel.
4. Reassemble the flywheel and belt in the reverse order, using the crank arm to rotate the drive pulley. Use a
   **#17 wrench** to tighten the **inner flywheel nut 3/8" UNF26 x 4T** and adjust the belt to the centre of the belt
   wheel. Tighten the nut and use the crank arm to bring the belt to the right tension.

**The CS800 (2020) book (cs800-2021)** prints the same in five steps: resistance to MAX, then power off; idle wheel
assembly off; steel cable off the flywheel, belt off the drive pulley; a **15 mm open-end wrench** for the **2 nuts**
locking the flywheel; refit in reverse, turn the drive pulley with the crank to check the belt is aligned with
the flywheel pulley, idle wheel and drive pulley, and if not use a **17 mm open-end wrench** on the flywheel nuts
to centre the belt; then set the tension with the idle wheel assembly.

The belt tension figure lives on the idler card (190 Hz in the 2016 book, none in 2020). The Sole SC200
flywheel-and-belt card is the 2016 procedure.
