1. Take both left and right shrouds (29) (30) and the **drive belt (54)** off.
2. Use **C-ring pliers** to take off the **Ø20 C-ring (86)**, and the **drive pulley axle (8)** can be released.
3. To release the drive pulley, use two wrenches to unscrew **4 pcs of 1/4" x 3/4" hex head screws (66)** with **8 pcs of 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers (72)** and **4 pcs of 1/4" x 5T nuts (90)**.
4. Follow steps 3 and 2 in reverse order to refit the crank axle.

**The same nut (90) is printed as 1/4" x 8T in the 2009 XR898 and every XBR book**, and as 1/4" x 5T here and in the 2008 dealer parts list ("1/4" x 5T Nyloc Nut"). Same part number, two printed thicknesses; the bolt, washer count and C-ring are identical, and the XBR books name an 11 mm wrench where this one says "2 pcs of wrench". The XR898 / XBR version is its own card.

After the pulley goes back the belt has to be re-tensioned to 180 to 205 Hz - the figure is printed in the belt procedure, not here.
