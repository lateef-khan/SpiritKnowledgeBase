1. First remove the **pedal bar assembly**, then use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **Phillips head screws
   M5x10mm** securing the cover to the pedal arm joint, and remove the **pedal arm joint cover**.
2. Pull out the end of the **slider cover** by hand.
3. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **Ø5/16" x 23 x 1.5T curved washer** of the **connecting component**
   using a **12 m/m wrench**. Separate the connecting component and the swing assembly.
4. Pull the **pedal base (L) and (R)** out backwards and separate them from the **connecting arm (L) and (R)**.
5. Release the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **Ø8.5 x Ø26 x 2.0T flat washer** of the connecting component using
   a 12 m/m wrench.
6. Use a **12 mm open end wrench** to remove the **hex socket screw 5/16" x 1/2"** and **flat washer Ø8.5 x 26 x 1.5T**.
   Pull out the **pedal carriage bolts** to remove the pedal bar assembly.
7. Release the **5/16" x 15L hex head bolt** of the pedal arms with a 12 m/m wrench at the **Ø38 slide wheel,
   urethane**. Take off the **M5 x 10L Phillips head screws x 4 pcs** of the left and right pedal base with a Phillips
   head screwdriver. (The XE895-2016 book drops that second sentence.)
8. Remove the **adjustment wheel fixing plate (L) and (R)** with a 12 m/m wrench and release the **5/16" x 15L hex
   head bolt** and **Ø5/16" x Ø23 x 1.5T flat washer** from the left and right pedal base.
9. Reassemble by the above steps.
10. Lock the **Ø8 x Ø16 x 2T flat washers** in the middle of the slide wheel (urethane) and the adjustment wheel
    fixing plate (L) and (R) when you assemble the **Ø38 slide wheel, urethane**.
11. Adjust the Ø38 slide wheel (urethane) against the **aluminum track** using an **11 m/m wrench** on the **1/4" x
    1/2" hex head bolt** while locking the left and right pedal base back on pedal arm (L) and (R).

**The connecting-component washer is printed three ways** in steps 3, 5 and 6: a `Ø5/16" x 23 x 1.5T` curved
washer, a `Ø8.5 x Ø26 x 2.0T` flat washer and a `Ø8.5 x 26 x 1.5T` flat washer. The books read as three separate
fasteners at three joints; keep them apart as they come off.
