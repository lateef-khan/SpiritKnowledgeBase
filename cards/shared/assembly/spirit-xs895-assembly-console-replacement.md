**Step 1.** Remove the **4 console locking screws**, then unplug all wires and take off the console.

**Step 2.** Plug all the wires first, install the console, then secure the 4 locking screws. *All wires should not
be pinched.*

No screw size is printed. The console's wire connections - amp power, fan, HR handlebar, wireless HR, key
board, system cable - are on the display-board wiring page of the same book and belong to the specs cards.

The XS300B-YS006 service manual is one book for both XS895 owner's-manual printings; the two-column page is read by step number.
