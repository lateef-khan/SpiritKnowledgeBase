The hardware is bagged per assembly step. Numbers in parentheses are assembly-drawing item numbers. Against the 2015 kit, Step 1 holds 6 (not 4) #150 screws and Step 2 uses two #204 wave washers instead of four #183.

**Hardware Step 1**

| Item | Description | Qty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #186 | 3/8" x 2T Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| #150 | M5 x 10mm Phillips Head Screw | 6 pcs |
| #145 | 3/8" x 3/4" Socket Head Cap Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #176 | 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #137 | 3/8" x 2-1/4" Hex Head Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #187 | 3/8" x 23 x 2T Curved Washer | 4 pcs |
| #139 | 3/8" x 3-3/4" Hex Head Bolt | 4 pcs |
