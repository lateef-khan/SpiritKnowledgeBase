**The 7.5S and the 8.5S are the machines in this family whose seat comes off; the 7.0S and the
MS300 are not.** On the 7.5S the manual gives the procedure as three drawing captions and nothing
else:

1. **Pull out pins, hold the seat release lever, and slide the seat assembly off the rear.**
2. **Pull the pop-pin, sliding the anchor to the desired location.**
3. **Secure the strap to the wheel of the chair, or to the chair frame.**

That is the whole of the printed procedure. There is no heading, no numbered list, no order of
operations and no warning panel beside it.

## What the manual does not say, and a clinic will ask

- **No weight or handling note for the seat assembly itself.** It gives no mass and no two-person
  instruction.
- **No re-fitting instruction.** Sliding the seat back on is not described.
- **No limit on chair type, wheel size or brake state.** The strap is specified only as going to
  "the wheel" or "the chair frame".
- **No stated anchoring load.** The strap is a positioning restraint as the manual describes it,
  not a rated tie-down.

**Do not invent any of those.** Say the manual gives three captions and refer a clinic asking about
transfer safety to their own policy and to Spirit Fitness.

## The parts behind the three captions

| Item | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 235 | Wheelchair anchor assembly | 2 |
| 245 | Strap Hold Down | 2 |
| 249 | Step Up Frame Pop Pin | 2 |
| 18 | Rail Base Frame | 1 |

The **two anchor assemblies bolt into the outside holes in the top of the frame tube** during step
one of the build, which is also why this machine presses **two** stabiliser end caps where the 7.0S
and MS300 press four - `75s-2025-assembly-procedure`.

**The rail is why the 7.5S is a foot longer than the 7.0S.** 80.3" against 67" on the MED books -
`7-5s-med-specs-dimensions-and-weight`.

## The 7.0S and the MS300 do not have this

Neither prints a removable seat, an anchor or a transfer board; their feature lists have no such
call-out and their builds fit no such part.

**The 8.5S and the 8.5S-Fit do have one**, described in words rather than captions - *"removable
seat away to allow for direct wheelchair access"* and *"the wheelchair anchor design incorporates a
strap that secures the wheelchair at the wheel or frame member"*, with **L. Wheelchair anchor** as a
diagram call-out. That is a different machine and a different console; answer an 8.5S from its own
book.
