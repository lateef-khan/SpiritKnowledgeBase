1. Take both left and right front shrouds (29) off.
2. Remove the **steel cable (62)** from the **flywheel (55)**. **Treat the aluminium plate gently - it deforms easily and then causes scratching noises.**
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **two M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screws (99)** on the **gear motor (43)** and take it apart.
4. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to remove one **M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screw (99)** and take the **reed switch sensor (46)** apart.
5. To set the cable range against the **console (19)** when refitting the steel cable (62): **raise the resistance to the maximum level with the console, then tighten the steel cable with its screw.** The aluminium plate is at its lowest position when the resistance is at maximum. Use **two 8 mm open-end wrenches** to secure it.

Four books - XBR25 2016, XBR55 2016, XBR55ENT and XBR55 2023 - print this identically. The 2023 book heads it "Gear Motor, Steel Cable, and Speed Sensor"; the others say "Reed Switch Sensor". Same part (46).

**Step 5 is the calibration.** The cable is clamped with the resistance at maximum, which puts the aluminium plate at the bottom of its travel; clamp it anywhere else and the resistance range is offset.

The XBR95 and the 2020 CR800 have no gear motor - their resistance is an induction or generator brake - so this card does not apply to them. The XBU55 upright has the same gear motor but its service manuals print no replacement procedure for it.
