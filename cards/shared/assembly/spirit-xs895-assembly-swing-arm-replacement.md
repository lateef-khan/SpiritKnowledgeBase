1. Follow the side case replacement to take off the side cases.
2. Use a **12 mm box wrench** to remove the **swing arm axle outside locking bolt**, then unmount the axle. Use a
   **17 mm box wrench and an M6 Allen wrench** to remove the **rod end bearing locking bolt**, then take off
   **Swing Arm C**.
3. Use a **12 mm box wrench** to remove the bolt locking the **bushing housing** to the mainframe, then take off
   **Swing Arm A and Swing Arm B**.
4. Reverse the steps to install all parts. *The axle needs to be aligned when installing.*

No bolt sizes are printed. Swing Arm A is also freed from the pedal arm side in the pedal arm procedure.

The XS300B-YS006 service manual is one book for both XS895 owner's-manual printings; the two-column page is read by step number.
