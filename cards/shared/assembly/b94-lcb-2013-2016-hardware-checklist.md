Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #50 | 3/8" x 2-1/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #58 | M5 x 12mm | Phillips Head Screw | 2 pcs |
| #71 | 3/8" x 25mm x 2T | Flat Washer | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #51 | 5/16" x 5/8" | Hex Head Bolt | 7 pcs |
| #72 | 5/16" x 18mm x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #99 | 5/16" x 19mm x 1.5T | Curved Washer | 1 pc |

**Hardware step 3**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #51 | 5/16" x 5/8" | Hex Head Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #72 | 5/16" x 18mm x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 2 pcs |
| #103 | 5/16" x 1.5T | Split Washer | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 4**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #58 | M5 x 12mm | Phillips Head Screw | 6 pcs |

All four packs are identical item for item, quantity for quantity. The only variation between the four printings is how the #99 size is typeset (`5/16" x 19 x 1.5T` in some, `5/16" x 19mm x 1.5T` in others).

The two #58 screws in step 1 are for the rear stabilizer cover; the six in step 4 are four for the console and two for the front stabilizer cover.

**Item numbers are not shared between models.** On the R92 and LCR recumbents `#71` is a hex head bolt and `#99` is a Phillips head screw, while on these uprights `#71` is a flat washer and `#99` is a curved washer. Always use the checklist printed in your own machine's manual.
