**The distance between the reed sensor and the magnet should be 2 mm** after refitting. The same books' troubleshooting chapter says "about 5 mm" for the same gap, and that the magnet on the pulley must line up with only one of the arrows on the sensor. Two figures, one gap: the replacement page prints 2 mm and the diagnosis page 5 mm, and neither book reconciles them.

**7.0R (6.13).**

1. Refer to 6.8 to remove the front shroud and to 6.9 to remove the rear shroud.
2. As shown in figure 6.13.1, take off the **4 screws** to remove the **step cover**.
3. As shown in figure 6.13.2, loosen the screw. As shown in figure 6.13.3, cut all the cable ties along the reed sensor w/cable. As shown in figure 6.13.4, unplug the connector of the reed sensor w/cable on the controller to remove it.
4. Refit by following the steps in reverse order. **The distance between reed sensor and magnet should be 2 mm.**

**7.0U (6.12)** prints the same job without the step cover: side case off (6.8), loosen the screw, cut the cable ties, unplug the connector at the controller, refit in reverse, 2 mm.

The 7.0R parts list carries a **1300 mm Sensor W/Cable (264)** ("1300mm_Speed Sensor W/Cable" on its OCR page); the 7.0U list a **Reed Sensor W/Cable (23)** and a **Magnet, Crank Position (22)**. This reed switch counts crank revolutions for the symmetry program; the angle sensor on the brake is a different sensor with a 3 mm gap and no replacement procedure in either book.
