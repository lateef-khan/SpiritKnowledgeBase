**Step 1.** Remove the parts from the carton and lay the main base frame / deck on a flat, level, clean
surface.

**Step 2.** Connect the **lower computer cable (55)** from the frame to the **middle computer cable (54)**
in the upright, tuck the connector back into the frame without pinching the wires, then insert the
**right and left uprights (4) and (5)** into the **frame base (2)** and tighten **eight 5/16" x 1/2" hex
head bolts (99)** and **eight Ø5/16" x Ø18 x 1.5T flat washers (100)** - four per upright - with the
**13 mm wrench (90)**. "This step may work better with a second person assisting" / "You may need
assistance holding the Uprights."

How the books phrase it:

| Book | Wording |
|---|---|
| TR300 2021 | connect 55 to 54 "in the **Right** Upright (4)"; attach the right upright with four bolts and washers, then the left upright (5) with four more |
| TR6.4 2024 | the same two sentences, but the cable is "in the **Left** Upright (4)" and the left upright is attached first; its tuck sentence is misprinted "so that you pinch the wires" |
| TRX2500 / TRX3500 / TRX4500 2024 | one sentence: connect 54 to 55, insert right and left uprights, tighten 8 bolts and 8 washers; "Be careful not to pinch the Computer Cables as this may cause damage to them" |

**The TR300 and TR6.4 disagree on which upright carries the cable** (right vs left) while both number it
(4). The TR65/TR75/TR85 and TRX5500 print the same step with a **3/4" bolt** - see the linked card.
