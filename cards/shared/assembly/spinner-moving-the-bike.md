Two wheels are located on the **front floor mount** for easily relocating the bike.

1. Grip the **back** floor mount and tilt the bike forward (1).
2. While avoiding uneven surfaces, roll the bike to its new location.
3. If the bike rocks after being set down, turn each levelling foot until it rests firmly on the floor (2).

**Equipment warning**

- Do not unscrew the levelling feet more then 1/2".
- Before moving your bike, adjust your handlebar and saddle settings to their lowest levels.

Only the SB900 2023 and SB1200 2023 owner's manuals print this section. The earlier SB700 and SB900 manuals have no "Moving The Bike" instructions at all.
