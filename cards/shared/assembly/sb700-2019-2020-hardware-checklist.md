Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #82 | 3/8" | Nut | 4 pcs |
| #81 | 3/8" x 3-1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #83 | 3/8" x 23mm | Curved Washer | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #91 | M5 x 10mm | Screw | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 4**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #49 | 5/16" | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| #48 | 5/16" x 16mm | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| #88 | 5/16" x 5/8" | Screw | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 6**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #93 | M5 x 12L | Tapping Screw | 2 pcs |
| #101 | 4 x 16L | Sheet Metal Screw | 3 pcs |

The checklist runs 1, 2, 4, 6. The assembly steps that use no pack hardware (the console step and the pedal step) have no checklist block.

**The last block is numbered differently in the two halves of the manual.** The checklist heads it **HARDWARE STEP 6**; the instruction page that uses those screws heads it **HARDWARE STEP 5** and prints it as numbered step 5, TABLET HOLDER. The parts are the same either way.

**#93 is unused on the 2019 machine.** The SB700 2019 tablet holder step uses only the three #101 sheet metal screws; nothing in the printed 2019 steps calls for the two M5 x 12L tapping screws. On the SB700 2020 the same step gained a second instruction that uses them for the Drink Bottle Holder (23).

**Item numbers are not shared between models.** The 2023 machines renumbered the whole pack - there `#93` is a button head socket bolt, while on the 2019-2021 machines `#93` is an M5 tapping screw. Always use the checklist printed in your own machine's manual.
