1. Use a screwdriver to remove the **4 umbrella head screws** of the **chin cover** (the CT900 books say "LED
   chin cover", the CT1000ENT book "console chin cover").
2. Remove the electronic control cable connector, and use a cross screwdriver to remove the console's
   **4 umbrella head screws**. The console can then be lifted off.
3. Refit the new console by the same two steps in reverse.

Three books print these steps with the same counts. The CT900ENT book prints the CT900 wording unchanged,
"LED chin cover" included, although its console is the ENT screen and not the LED console.

The hand pulse procedure in the same guide starts by removing the console this way.

The guide is a machine translation. Its words, as used on this card: **electronic watch** is the console,
**cross screwdriver** is a Phillips screwdriver, **umbrella head screws** are the guide's name for the
console and cover screws (no size is printed), and a **17th T-Shirt** or **13th T-sleeve** is a 17 mm or 13 mm
T-handle socket.
