The seat can be adjusted forward or backward to the position that is the most comfortable. To adjust the seat, **loosen the quick release knob, slide the seat to the desired position and retighten the knob.**

The same four lines in all four books. On the recumbents the knob is the one that holds the sliding tube in the seat support bracket (the 2024 bike names it a quick release handle with a ball end cap and a brake bracket, but this page still says knob); on the hybrid bikes it is the quick release knob (57) that holds the seat post in the main frame, so "forward or backward" there is really height. No fit guide (knee angle, hip height) is printed in these books.
