1. Refer to the shroud procedure to remove the front shroud (7.0R: 6.8) or the side case (7.0U: 6.8).
2. Refer to 6.10.1 (7.0U: 6.9.1) to remove the **eye bolt and tension spring**.
3. Refer to 6.10.2 (7.0U: 6.9.2) to remove the **drive belt**.
4. As shown in figure 6.10.7 (7.0U: 6.9.7), **loosen the 3 screws** to remove the idler wheel assembly.
5. Refit: as shown in figure 6.10.8 (7.0U: 6.9.8), **"luck the idler wheel assembly counterclockwise"** - lock it counterclockwise - and follow steps 1 to 4 in reverse order.

No screw size is printed. The 7.0R parts list carries an **Idler Wheel Assembly (Upper) (59)** and **(Lower) (60)**, an **Idler Bracket (10)** and a **Cantilever Anchor Assembly (58)**; the 7.0U list carries **Bracket, Idler Wheel Assembly (Upper) (126)** and **(Lower) (127)** and an **Idler Bracket (125)**. The belt tension is set afterwards on the eye-bolt nuts - 220 to 240 N on the 7.0R, 260 to 280 N on the 7.0U - and is on the eye-bolt cards, not here.
