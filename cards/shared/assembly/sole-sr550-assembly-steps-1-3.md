**This is the SR550 sequence, not the SR500 one: the track and folding-end work the SR500 splits into steps 2 and 3 is one step 2 here.**

1. Use the WRENCH (128) to tighten 4 HEX HEAD BOLTS (120) together with 4 SPRING WASHERS (121) and 4 FLAT WASHERS (109) to secure the MAIN FRAME (1) and FRONT STABILIZER (2) together.
2. Install the Rear Aluminum Track (51) onto the Rear Stabilizer (3) with four Button Head Socket Bolts (129), using the M4 L Allen Wrench (126). Attach the other end of the Aluminum Track (51) to the Folding End Assembly (6), then use the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (127) to tighten with five Button Head Socket Bolts (123) and four Flat Washers (122).
3. Install the left and right Pedals (4, 5) on the MAIN FRAME (1) with six Button Head Socket Bolts (123) and six Flat Washers (122), using the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (127) to tighten. Use it again to install the Connecting Cover (63) on the Aluminum Track (51) with three Phillips Head Screws (85). Install the SEAT (49) and Seat Attaching Board (16) with four Phillips Head Screws (124) and four Split Washers (125), using the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (127).

**Washer naming contradiction, ruled.** Step 1 calls for "4 SPRING WASHERS (121)", but the Hardware Step 1 box on PDF p.6 and the checklist on PDF p.8 both name part 121 the O10 x 2T **Split** Washer, and the seat step in this same sequence calls part 125 a Split Washer too. Both readings are transcribed as printed; the ruling is the pack list and the same-book seat step: fit the **Split** Washer (121) as packed.
