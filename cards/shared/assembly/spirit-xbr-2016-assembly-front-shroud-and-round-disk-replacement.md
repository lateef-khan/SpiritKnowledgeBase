**Five Ø3.5 x 12 and three 5 x 16 screws on the left shroud.** The XBR95 2016 and both 2023 books print seven and two for the same cover; that is the linked card.

1. Take the **crank arms (51L, 51R)** off.
2. Remove the **round disk (34)** with the fingers.
3. When refitting the round disk, pay attention to its direction: the latches are different sizes, and the **FRONT** marking faces forward.
4. Take the **console mast cover (31)** off.
5. To release the **left front chain cover (29)**, use a Phillips head screwdriver on **5 pcs of Ø3.5 x 12 self-tapping screws (103)** and **3 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws (101)**.
6. To release the **right front chain cover (30)**: on the mainframe, unscrew **2 pcs of Ø3.5 x 20 self-tapping screws (107)** with **2 pcs of Ø5 x 16 x 1.5T flat washers (78)**; then, on the right cover, unscrew 2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws and unplug it.
7. To refit, secure the right front shroud (30) to the main frame with a **Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet-metal screw (103)** and a **3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T flat washer (78)** lightly; join the left (29) and right (30) shrouds and lock them with **7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet-metal screws (103)**; then adjust the **crank axle (8)** and the **bottom cover** to their relative position and fix with the **5 x 16 mm tapping screws (101)**.

**Five screws come off the left cover in step 5 and seven go back in step 7**, printed that way in all three books - and the refit step is word for word the one the 2023 books print after removing seven. Whether the 2016 cover has five or seven Ø3.5 screws, this book does not settle; the part number (103) is the same, printed x 12 on removal and x 16 on refit.
