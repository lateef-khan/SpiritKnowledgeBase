First power-on, the last step of assembly. This paragraph is identical in the F63 2023/2025 and F65 2023 books, and differs from the older message-scroll books (see the linked card) in two places: the version message displays rather than scrolls, and the odometer uses the Time and Pace windows.

Power the treadmill on by plugging it into an appropriate wall outlet, then turn on the power switch located at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood. Ensure that the Safety Key is installed, as the treadmill will not power on without it. When the power is turned on, the message window displays the current version of the software. Then the Time and Pace Windows will display Odometer readings for a short time: the Time Window shows hours in use and the Pace Window shows miles (or kilometers in metric). The treadmill then enters idle mode, which is the starting point for operation.

What the console shows during normal running after this point is a console-section matter, not assembly.
