1. **Install two Wave Washers (175)** onto the left and right sides of the Handle Bar axle.
2. **Slide the Left (13) and Right (14) lower arms** onto the axle. The small stickers mark L and R.
3. **Install two Flat Washers (165) onto the two Hex Head Bolts (132)** in the threaded holes in the ends of the axle with wrench (187).
4. **Untie the wire holding the Sleeve Spacer (24)** on the rod-end of the Lower Handle Bar Arms (13 & 14). Align the rod-end holes with the Handle Bar (13 & 14) bracket holes, ends of the connecting arms inside the brackets, and secure with a **Hex Head Bolt (134)**, **Flat Washer (170)** and **Nylon Nut (159)** using **Wrenches (184 & 187)**.

The checklist bags 4 wave washers while the step text fits two, one per side. This card follows the step text.
