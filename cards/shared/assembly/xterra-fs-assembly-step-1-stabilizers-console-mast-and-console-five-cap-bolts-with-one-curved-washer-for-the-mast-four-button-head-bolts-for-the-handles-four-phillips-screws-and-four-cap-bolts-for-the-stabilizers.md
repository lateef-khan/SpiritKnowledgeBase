Hardware step 1: **4 button head socket bolts (43), 8 flat washers (44), 1 curved washer (45), 9 split washers (46), 4 M5 x 12 Phillips head screws (53), 9 socket head cap bolts (102)**.

1. Locate the console mast (9; the 2018 book: 122) and slide on the console mast cover (82). **Make sure the cover is facing the correct direction, as shown, before sliding onto the mast.** Install the wiring harness (94) into the bottom of the mast and out the top. Be careful when installing the console mast to the main frame so as not to pinch or cut the wiring harness; damage to the electronics may occur.
2. Install the console mast on the main frame with **five socket head cap bolts (102), four flat washers (44), five split washers (46) and one curved washer (45)**. Tighten using the L Allen wrench (103). Snap the console mast cover (82) in place against the plastic covers below.
3. Route the hand pulse wires (96) from the stationary handle bars (10, 11) through the console mast as shown. Attach the handles to the console mast with **four button head socket bolts (43)**. Use the M5 Allen wrench (68) to tighten.
4. Connect the cables (94 & 96) to the console (58) and install the console onto the mast with **four Phillips head screws (53)**. Tighten using the Phillips screwdriver (68).
5. Secure the front stabilizer (12) and rear stabilizer (13) with **four socket head cap bolts (102), four split washers (46) and four flat washers (44)**. Tighten using the L Allen wrench (103).

| Book | Differences |
|---|---|
| 2019 (FS1.5) | Prints "five Button Head Socket Bolts (43)" for the mast and "four Button Head Socket Bolts (43)" for the stabilizers - **a misprint**: the pack holds only four bolts (43) against nine cap bolts (102), and the 2020 and 2018 books say (102) in both places. The mast and stabilizers take the cap bolts. |
| 2018 (FS3.0) | The harness is pulled up the mast by a wire tie: "wrap the end of the wire tie that exits the bottom of the mast around the wire harness (94) that sits in the console mast frame bracket, slowly pull the wire tie and harness up from the bottom of the mast through the top while setting the mast into the bracket". Its step 5 names the M5 Allen wrench (103). |

Five bolts on the mast: four with flat washers and one with the curved washer, each with a split washer.
