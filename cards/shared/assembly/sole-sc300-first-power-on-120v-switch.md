**This is the 120-volt power-switch power-up, not the SC200's external DC adapter.**

When power is connected to the climber the console will automatically power up. These models are connected directly to 120-volt, and there is a power switch located where the line cord plugs into the unit on the left side near the middle.

When it is first powered on, the console will perform an internal self-test. During this time all the lights will turn on, the Message Window display will show a software version (i.e.: VER 1.0), and the Total STEPS Window will display steps. The Time Window shows how many total hours the climber has been used.
