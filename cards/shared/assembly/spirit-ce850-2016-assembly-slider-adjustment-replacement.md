**"Slider Adjustment" is the book's name for the stride-length mechanism, and this section replaces its parts.**
The care adjustment of the sliders (9-3 in the troubleshooting chapter) is a different, maintenance question.

1. Remove the **joint assembly** by the slider adjustment disassembly steps.
2. Use a **no. 12 wrench** to remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** from the
   **B Ø178 x 41L rotate axle** fixed on the swing assembly on the main frame.
3. Pull out the **B Ø178 x 41L rotate axle**.
4. Use a **12 m/m wrench** to release the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **Ø8.5 x Ø26 x 2.0T flat washer**, and
   remove the **swing assembly** and slider adjustment fixed at the **adjusting lever** on the **moving range
   adjusting assembly**.
5. Use a 12 m/m wrench to release the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **Ø8.5 x Ø26 x 2.0T flat washer** fixed at
   the adjusting lever on the slider adjustment, then remove the adjusting lever.
6. Use a round bar smaller than **Ø17** to knock the **J4FM-1719-09 bushing** out of the adjusting lever.
7. Use a **13 m/m wrench** to release the **M8 x 6.3T nut** fixed on the **wheel base**, and release the **M8 x 20L
   socket head cap bolt**. The swing assembly can then be pulled out.
8. Use a round bar smaller than Ø17 to knock the **J4FM-1719-09 bushing** out of the swing assembly.
9. Use an **M5 Allen wrench and a no. 13 m/m wrench** to remove the **5/16" x 1-3/4" button head socket bolt** and
   **5/16" x 7T nyloc nut** fixed on the **Ø40 adjustment transportation wheel** on the slider adjustment, then
   remove the wheel and the wheel base.
10. Reverse the procedures to return the parts.
11. When the swing assembly goes back on the main frame, the **B Ø178 x 41L rotate axle must be aligned with the
    flat portion of the notch on the U-seat** before the screw is locked.
12. When locking the Ø40 adjustment transportation wheel back, the **5/16" x 7T nyloc nut must be in the same
    position as the crank locked in the slider adjustment axle**.
13. When every part is assembled, **use a torque wrench to lock the M8 x 20L socket head cap bolt to 150 lb** on the
    wheel base, lock the M8 x 6.3T nut tight, and put the slider cover back on the slider adjustment.

The axle is printed `Bψ178x41L` in one step and `B Ø178x41L` in the next, in both books.
