1. Use a driver to take out the **M5 x 10L Phillips head screws (85), 3 pcs**, and the **Connecting Cover (63)**.
2. Use the **5 mm wrench** to take out the **M8 x P1.25 x 12L button head socket bolts (123), 6 pcs**, and the
   **Ø5/16" x Ø18 x 1.5T flat washers (122), 6 pcs**. This releases the **Aluminum Track (51)**, the seat sets
   **(49) (16) (53) (54)** and the **Rear Stabilizer (3)**.
3. Take the seat sets (49) (16) (53) (54) out at the front.
4. Use the **4 mm wrench** to release the **M6 x P1.0 x 18L button head socket bolts (89), 2 pcs**, and the
   **M6 x P1.0 x 10L button head socket bolts (129), 4 pcs**. Remove the **Seat Stop Cover (65)** and the Rear
   Stabilizer (3).
5. Use the **6 mm wrench and the 14 mm wrench** to take apart the **3/8" x UNC16 x 1-1/4" button head socket bolt
   (105)**, the **3/8" x 11T nyloc nut (106)** and the **Pulley (54)**.
6. **CW800-YR001 book only (crw800-2016):** use the 6 mm wrench and the 14 mm wrench to take apart the
   **3/8" x UNC16 x 4-1/4" socket head cap bolt (104)**, the 3/8" x 11T nyloc nut (106) and the Pulley (54). The
   CRW800 (2020) book (crw800-2021) does not print this step and goes straight from the 1-1/4" bolt to the seat.
7. Use a driver to take apart the **M6 x 15L Phillips head screw (124)**, the **Seat (49)** and the **Seat
   Attaching Board (16)**.
8. Reverse the steps to refit the parts.

The "5 mm", "4 mm" and "6 mm wrench" are Allen keys. The XRW600 book uses different bolts for the same job and
has its own card. The Sole SR500 (2016) book prints the eight-step version with the 4-1/4" bolt.

If the slide is rough, the troubleshooting chapter in the same books says to clean the aluminum board (52) and
track pulley (53), loosen the three 3/8" x 11T nyloc nuts (106), and replace the pulleys (53) or (54) - that is
maintenance, not this replacement.
