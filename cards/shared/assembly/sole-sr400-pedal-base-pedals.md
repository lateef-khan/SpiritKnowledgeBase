1. Fit the base frame of the pedal (G) to the main frame (A) by two screws (J1).
2. Loosen the screw from the shaft of the base frame (G1), which is pre-assembled.
3. Re-assemble the shaft (G1) into the base frame of the pedal (G) and the pedal (F) by screws.

The pedal shaft ships pre-assembled in its base; it has to come out before the pedal goes in and then be refitted through both.
