1. Adjust the resistance on the console to the highest and remove the power cord. Take out the Chain Cover (L)
   (71) and Chain Cover (R) (72).
2. Pull the **Steel Cable (66)** out of the **Flywheel (23)**.
3. Use a driver to take out the **Ø5 x 16L tapping screws (111), 2 pcs**. Take out the **Front Gear Motor Cover
   (74)** and **Rear Gear Motor Cover (75)**.
4. Use the **8 mm wrench** to remove the **M5 x 5T nyloc nut** of the Steel Cable (66).
5. Remove the cables from the **Gear Motor (35)**. Use a driver to take out the **M5 x P0.8 x 15L Phillips head
   screws (78), 2 pcs**.
6. Reverse the steps to refit the parts.

The three books print this identically. Setting the console to the highest resistance first pulls the steel
cable to its shortest, which is what lets it come off the flywheel in step 2. The tension motor's working
voltage and its connector pins are specs, not this card.
