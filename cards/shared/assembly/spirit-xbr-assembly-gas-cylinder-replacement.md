1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to loosen **2 pcs of M5 x 12 Phillips head screws (99)** and take the **seat carriage cover (125)** off.
2. Use **two 13 mm wrenches** to release one **5/16" x 2-1/2" hex head screw (69)**, one **5/16" x 18 mm x 1.5T flat washer (76)** and one **5/16" nyloc nut (91)**.
3. Use a **12 mm and a 13 mm wrench** to release one **5/16" x 1-1/4" hex head screw (70)**, one 5/16" x 18 mm x 1.5T flat washer (76) and one 5/16" nyloc nut (91).
4. Use an **8 mm wrench** to release the screws secured on the steel cable, then separate the **steel cable (58)** from the **gas cylinder (57)** and take the gas cylinder (57) off.
5. Reverse the steps to refit.

Five books print this identically; the 2016 and ENT books head it "Gas Cylinder (Remark 8)" - the remark is not printed anywhere in those books.

**The XBR25 2016 service manual has no gas cylinder section.** Its release-lever procedure holds the gas cylinder nut with the 8 mm wrench, so the machine has one, but no replacement steps are printed for it.
