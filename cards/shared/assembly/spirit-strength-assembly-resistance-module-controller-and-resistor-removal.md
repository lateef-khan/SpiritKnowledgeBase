*(Tool: Phillips screwdriver)*

1. Remove the **4 screws (6)** to remove the resistance (5) - the resistor.
2. Remove the **4 screws (4)** to remove the resistance bracket (3).
3. Remove the **4 screws (2)** to remove the controller (1).

The anatomical drawing names the resistor the **cement resistor**; it is the braking resistor the controller's
fault list refers to (regenerative braking overload, braking resistor fault protection - errors cards). The
controller's specs - RS485 at 115200 bps, FOC, 5000 hours - are specs cards.

The "Digital Resistance Module" is the i-Strength line's motorised resistance box, part 72 in the CSI owner's manuals; the screw numbers are the call-outs on the manual's exploded drawings.
