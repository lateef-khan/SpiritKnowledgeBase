The fitness bike is equipped with **two transport wheels, which are engaged when the rear of the fitness bike is lifted**.

That single sentence is the whole of the TRANSPORT INSTRUCTIONS section. The manual gives no weight, no tilt angle and no levelling instruction.

The 2011 and 2013 printings spell it "equippped"; the 2016 and later printings spell it "equipped". The instruction is otherwise identical.

**No TRANSPORT INSTRUCTIONS section is printed in the B94, R92, B74 or R72 owner's manuals.** Do not carry this statement across to them.
