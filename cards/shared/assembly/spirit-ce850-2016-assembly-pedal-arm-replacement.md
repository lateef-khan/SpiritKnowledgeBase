**Fourteen steps, three pressed bushings and one torque figure: the bushing housing goes back on the pedal arm at
550 lb.** The XE-series pedal arm has none of the bushings and no torque.

1. Remove **side case (L) and (R)** and **pedal arm (L) and (R)** by the disassembly steps.
2. Pull out the **Ø17 x 34L rotate axle A** from the inside. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **Ø5/16" x
   Ø23 x 1.5T flat washer** from the outside of rotate axle A for **slider adjustment (L) and (R)** of the **joint
   assembly**, using a **12 m/m wrench**.
3. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **Ø5/16" x Ø23 x 1.5T flat washer** from the **crank arm assembly**
   for the **bushing housing, pedal arm**, using a 12 m/m wrench.
4. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **Ø8.5 x Ø26 x 2.0T flat washer** from the **rear rail assembly** for
   the joint assembly, using a 12 m/m wrench.
5. To remove the **J4FM-1719-09 bushing** from the joint assembly, use a bar smaller than **Ø17** and knock the
   bushing out.
6. Use a **#8 hex wrench and a #14 wrench** to remove the **gap socket screw 3/8" x 2-1/4"**, **flat washer 3/8" x 19
   x 1.5T** and **nyloc nut 3/8" x 11T** connecting the **inclinable rail assembly** and **rotating block**, to
   remove the inclinable rail assembly.
7. To remove the **WFM-2528-21 bushing** from the bushing housing, pedal arm, use a bar smaller than **Ø25** and
   knock it out.
8. To remove the **WFM-1719-12 bushing** from the rear rail assembly, use a bar smaller than **Ø17** and knock it out.
9. Remove **two M5 x 15L Phillips head screws** from the **slide wheel cover** when taking the **Ø78 slide wheel,
   urethane** apart.
10. Remove the **Ø17 C ring** and **Ø17 x 0.5T wave washer** from pedal arm (L) and (R) at the Ø78 slide wheel,
    urethane, using C-ring pliers.
11. Reverse the above procedures to refit the pedal arm.
12. When you assemble the Ø78 slide wheel, urethane, build the stack in this order: **Ø17 x 0.5T wave washer, Ø78
    slide wheel (urethane), Ø17 x 0.5T wave washer, Ø17 C ring**.
13. When assembling the joint assembly and **adjusting lever**, **line up the wheel base with the surface of the Ø17
    x 34L rotate axle A** and lock with the screw.
14. **Lock the bushing housing, pedal arm on pedal arm (L) and (R) using a torque wrench, up to 550 lb.**

Step 6 names an inclinable rail assembly; on the stride machines the rail is fixed and the stride motor moves the
pedal path. The Sole E95S-2016 twin prints the same step and the same 550 lb.
