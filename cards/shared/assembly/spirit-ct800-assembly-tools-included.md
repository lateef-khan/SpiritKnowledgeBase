All eight cartons list the same three tools, and nothing else:

- 3/8" Allen Wrench
- 10mm Allen Wrench
- Phillips Screwdriver

The CT800 2012 manual heads the panel **Assembly Tools** rather than TOOLS INCLUDED
and prints "Phillip Head Screwdriver"; the tools themselves are the same three.

## Numbering, on the manuals that number them

The CT800 2020, the CT800ENT, the CT850 2016 and 2020 and the CT850ENT assembly
steps call the tools out by item number - the **Screw Driver (No.158)**, the
**Allen Wrench (No.160)** in steps one to three and the **Allen Wrench (No.159)** in
step four. The CT800 2012 and 2016 steps and the **CT850 2018** steps give no tool
item numbers at all.

**Two parts lists do not agree with their own tools panel.** The CT800ENT and the
CT850ENT each call item 159 an "L Allen Wrench(6x25x67L)" and item 160 an "L Allen
Wrench(6x26Lx76L)" - two metric-looking wrenches - where the panel on page 8 of the
same manual says 3/8" and 10mm. The CT800 2020 and the CT850 2016 and 2020 parts
lists call 159 an "L Allen Wrench" and 160 an "Allen Wrench" with no sizes at all.
The manuals never reconcile the two.

**The CT850 2018 manual has no parts list at all** to check any of this against;
its contents run from general maintenance straight to the warranty.

## An 8mm Allen wrench the panel does not list

Every one of the eight manuals says, in its belt chapter, that the tread-belt
tension and tracking bolts are turned with an **8mm Allen wrench provided in the
parts package**. That wrench is not on this tools panel in any of them. Belt
tension and tracking are maintenance questions and have their own cards; what
matters here is that the carton is said to hold a tool the tools panel does not
name.
