All four cartons list the same three tools, and nothing else:

- 3/8" Allen Wrench
- 10mm Allen Wrench
- Phillips Screwdriver

The 2012 manual heads the panel **Assembly Tools** rather than TOOLS INCLUDED and
prints "Phillip Head Screwdriver"; the tools themselves are the same three.

## Numbering, on the two manuals that number them

The 2020 and CT800ENT assembly steps call the tools out by item number - the
**Screw Driver (No.158)**, the **Allen Wrench (No.160)** in steps one to three and
the **Allen Wrench (No.159)** in step four. The 2012 and 2016 assembly steps give
no tool item numbers at all.

**The CT800ENT parts list does not agree with its own tools panel.** It calls item
159 an "L Allen Wrench(6x25x67L)" and item 160 an "L Allen Wrench(6x26Lx76L)" -
two metric-looking wrenches - where the panel on page 8 of the same manual says
3/8" and 10mm. The 2020 parts list calls 159 an "L Allen Wrench" and 160 an "Allen
Wrench" with no sizes at all. The manuals never reconcile the two.

## An 8mm Allen wrench the panel does not list

Every one of the four manuals says, in its belt chapter, that the tread-belt
tension and tracking bolts are turned with an **8mm Allen wrench provided in the
parts package**. That wrench is not on this tools panel in any of them. Belt
tension and tracking are maintenance questions and have their own cards; what
matters here is that the carton is said to hold a tool the tools panel does not
name.
