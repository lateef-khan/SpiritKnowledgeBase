Hardware step 4: **4 PVC caps (#32), 2 hex head bolts (#39) M10*78, 2 sleeves (#40), 6 washers (#41) 10*22, 6 nylon nuts (#42) M10, 4 carriage bolts (#44) M10*45, 1/1 hinge bolt (#45L/R), 2 wave washers (#46) 16, 2 washers (#47) 27*13, 1/1 lock nut (#48L/R)**.

1. Attach the left pedal tube (11) to the left side of the crank (49). Secure using one left lock nut (48L), one washer (47), one wave washer (46) and one left hinge bolt (45L).
2. Attach the left pedal tube (11) to the left bottom handlebar (14). Line up the holes and insert the sleeve (40), then secure using one nylon nut (42), one washer (41) and one hex head bolt (39). Attach the PVC cap (32) onto both sides of the pedal tube (11).
3. Attach the left pedal (9) onto the left pedal tube (11), secure using two carriage bolts (44), two washers (41) and two nylon nuts (42).
4. Repeat the procedure for the right pedal tube (12) and right pedal (10).

The hinge bolts and lock nuts are handed - 45L with 48L on the left, 45R with 48R on the right; the book does not say which way each turns. The 2016 elliptical fits its pedals with hex bolts and lock knobs instead (the linked card).
