| Part No. | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 1/2 | Main Frame | 1 |
| 3 | Seat Support Tube | 1 |
| 4 | Handlebar Tube (the 2019 and 2025 books draw it as 04/08 "Handlebar Tube/Console", 1 pc / 1 pc) | 1 |
| 5 | Front Stabilizer | 1 |
| 6 | Rear Stabilizer | 1 |
| 7 | Seat | 1 |
| 8 | Computer | 1 |
| 9 | Pedal (L/R) | 1 (the 2019 book: 1 pc / 1 pc) |
| - | Hardware Bag | 1 |
| - | Manual | 1 |

The hardware bag holds three things and no loose fasteners: **#10 Club Knob** (1), **#72 Allen Key** (1) and **#73 Hex Head Wrench** (1). The 2021 book lists them on its own "Assembly Pack Checklist" page; the 2019 book draws them with the parts and calls the key a 5 mm Allen key on the step pages; the 2025 book prints only the parts list and then names "73 Wrench", "10 Club knob" and the Allen key beside each step.

Every fastener is pre-assembled on the unit: the lock pin on the main frame, four curved washers and four domed nuts on the stabilizers, three flat washers and three nylon nuts on the seat, and the handlebar screws on the frame. "Some of the parts for this machine are preassembled on the unit. Please read your manual to ensure everything is there."

The November 2019 service document for the 2019 book adds that the tools included are a "13 & 15mm Wrench, 2-AAA Batteries, 5mm Allen Key Screw Driver combo, and Club Knob" - and separately that the customer "MUST purchase QTY 2 AAA Energizer batteries" for the console; the two sentences disagree on whether batteries ship in the box.

The recumbent folding bikes (the 350 and 360) ship four more parts and a ten-item hardware bag; see the linked card.
