1. **Install the two Wave Washers (81)** onto the Handle Bar axles.
2. **Slide the Left and Right Handle Bars (10, 11)** onto the axle. The small stickers mark L and R.
3. **Assemble the two 3/8 x 30mm Flat Washers (97) onto the two 3/8 x 3/4 Hex Head Bolts (103)** in the threaded holes in the ends of the axle; tighten with the **13/14mm Wrench (106)**.
4. **Front Handle Bar Cover (L-54) and Rear Handle Bar Cover (L-54-1)** onto the Handle Bar (L-10) with **four 3.5 x 12mm Screws (87)**.
5. Repeat step 4 for the right side Handle Bar Covers.
