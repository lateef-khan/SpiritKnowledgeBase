Hardware: pre-assembled on the main frame, **2 Allen screws (#12)**. Tool: the Allen key.

1. Remove the 2 Allen screws (12) from the main frame (01). Attach the handlebar (04) to the main frame (01) and tighten with the 2 Allen screws (12).

This is step 4 on the upright folding bike and step 5 on the recumbent folding bike (its step 4 is the back cushion). The console is fitted afterwards as a separate step. The 2019 and 2025 books use a handlebar tube with the console already on it, held by **four** screw sets - the linked card.
