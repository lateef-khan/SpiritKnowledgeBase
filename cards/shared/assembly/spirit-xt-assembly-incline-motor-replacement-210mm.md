**Set the new incline motor to 210 mm before you fit it.** The four books print the figure three ways: the
XT185 2015 book as "Zeroing the incline motor before take it apart from the machine or assemble it onto the
frame. The zeroing distance is 210mm" inside the procedure, and as "Incline Range must be adjusted to 210mm
minimum prior to installation" in its chapter 11, over a drawing; the XT285 2015 book as that "Incline Range"
sentence both inside the procedure and in its chapter 11; and the 2023 books as "To adjust spare Incline
motor to lower (210mm)". The XT385 and XT485 use 225 mm and are on another card.

1. Remove the motor cover and fold the treadmill. (XT185 2015: "First remove the motor cover and adjust
   treadmill to folded position"; XT285 2015: "First adjust treadmill to folded position"; 2023 books: "Take
   off Motor cover then folding treadmill".)
2. Unplug the incline motor wires and unmount the old incline motor.
3. Set the new motor to **210 mm**.
4. Fit the new incline motor with a **14mm wrench** (2023 books: "14mm open-end wrench").
5. Connect the incline motor wiring to the controller: **red wire to UP, white wire to COM, black wire to
   DOWN**.

The chapter-11 drawing in the 2015 books dimensions the 210 between the pivot hole on the motor body and
the hole at the end of the extended tube, with the tube run out. In the 2023 books the three wire colours are
printed only as callouts on the photograph of the controller, not in the step text.

No torque is printed, and neither generation says to recalibrate the incline afterwards in this procedure.
