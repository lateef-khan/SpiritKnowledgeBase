1. After taking the **rear chain covers (L/R) (35, 36)** off, unplug the cables on the **induction brake controller (43)**, then use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **2 pcs of 5 x 19 tapping screws** and take the induction brake controller (43) off.

One step, no refit step, printed identically in the XBR25 2016, XBR55 2016, XBR55ENT and XBR95 2016 books as the first part of "Aluminum Trail and Stabilizer Cover".

**Only the XBR95 has an induction brake.** The XBR25, XBR55 and XBR55ENT are gear-motor bikes, and their books still call the board the induction brake controller. **In those three books the number (43) is used twice**: for the gear motor in the gear-motor procedure and for this controller here. In the XBR95 2016 book, and in the 2008 XBR dealer parts list, (43) is the controller. The XBR55 2023 and XBR95 2023 books drop this section and print no controller replacement.
