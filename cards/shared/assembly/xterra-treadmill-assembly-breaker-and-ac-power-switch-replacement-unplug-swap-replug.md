Both parts are plug-in replacements and every Dyaco book gives each one sentence:

- **Breaker:** "Unplug Breaker wires to replace and plug wires back" (TR260 9-4, TRX5500 9-4); "Remove
  Breaker connection wiring, replace part and reconnect wiring" (TRX1400 12-4, TRX2500 and TRX3500/4500
  11-4).
- **AC power switch:** "Unplug AC Power switch wires to replace and plug wires back" (TR260 9-5, TRX5500
  9-5); "Disconnect wiring to AC power switch, replace AC power switch and reconnect wiring" (TRX1400
  12-5, TRX2500 and TRX3500/4500 11-5).

None of them says how the part is held in its housing, to unplug the mains cord first, or which wire
goes to which terminal. The parts sit beside the power inlet at the front of the frame.
