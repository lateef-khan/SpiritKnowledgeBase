Three books share one replacement guide, translated by machine from Chinese. The CT900 and CT900ENT books
print it **word for word the same** (1,062 words, checked); the CT1000ENT book prints a re-edited copy.

| Procedure | CT900 / CT900ENT | CT1000ENT | Where the figures differ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Console | 4.1 | 8.4 | same |
| Lower control board | 4.2 | 8.4 | same |
| Motor | 4.3 | 8.4 | **120 ~ 130 Hz** there, **80-90 lbs** here |
| A.C. input module | 4.4 | 8.4 | same |
| Front and rear rollers | 4.5 | 8.4 | tension figure printed there, not here |
| Running deck, belt and cushions | 4.6 | 8.4 | one 4 mm deck screw there, **eight 6 mm** here |
| Incline motor | 4.7 | 8.4 | same, 315 mm |
| Idler | 4.8 | **absent** | - |
| Hand pulse board and set | 4.9 | 8.4 | same |

Where the guide sits: the CT900 books list it in their contents as sub-items 1-4 under "11. Installation
of the Incline Motor" (Serial Number Location, Component Description, Preventative Maintenance, Part
Replacement Guide), with its own 4.x numbering and its own figure numbers - it is an appendix pasted in
whole. The CT1000ENT book files the same four items as 8.1 to 8.4 of its chapter 8.

**Glossary.** The words the translation uses, and what they are on the machine:

| Printed | Meaning |
|---|---|
| electronic watch, electronic form | the console |
| umbrella head screws | the console and cover screws; no size printed |
| cross screwdriver | Phillips screwdriver; "male screwdriver" once, same tool |
| 17th T-Shirt, 17th T-sleeve, 13 T-type sleeve | 17 mm or 13 mm T-handle socket |
| No. 8 / No. 6 / No. 4 L-type hex wrench | 8, 6 or 4 mm L-shaped hex key |
| No. 17 opening wrench | 17 mm open-end wrench |
| rear wheel | rear roller |
| lifting motor | incline motor |
| paper tube | a prop under the raised frame |
| buffer | deck cushion |
| press pulley | idler |
| chain cover | the side covers taken off in the roller procedure |
| switchboard 8 | printed so in the A.C. input module step; not explained |
| BLS | lbs |

No torque value appears anywhere in the guide, and no procedure says to unplug the machine first.
