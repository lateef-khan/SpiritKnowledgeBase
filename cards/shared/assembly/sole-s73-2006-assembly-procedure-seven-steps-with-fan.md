Seven unillustrated assembly steps, one hardware list for all of them.

1. Remove all parts from the carton.
2. Connect Computer Cable (Lower) to Computer Cable (Middle).
3. Hand-tighten the right and left uprights into the frame base with 8 pcs of 5/16 x 1/2 Button Head Socket Screws, using the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Screwdriver.
4. Install the Upright Support onto the left and right uprights with 4 pcs of 5/16 x 3/4 Button Head Socket Screws.
5. Connect Computer Cable (Middle) to Computer Cable (Upper); connect Speed Adjustment Switch W/Cable to Speed Cable (Upper); connect Incline Adjustment Switch W/Cable to Incline Cable (Upper).
6. Install the Console Support into the right and left handrails with 4 pcs of 5/16 x 1/2 Button Hand Socket Screws.
7. Connect the fan assembly computer cable to the console and fix the fan assembly onto the console with 3 pcs of M3 x 18 mm Self Tapping Screws.

Lock down all hardware after the accessories are assembled.
