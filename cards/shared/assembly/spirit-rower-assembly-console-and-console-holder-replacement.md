1. Take out the **3.5 x 12L sheet-metal screws (112), 4 pcs**, from the **Console (43)** with a driver, into the top
   cover of the console, then remove the **500 mm computer cable (upper) (44)** and the wireless cables or
   receivers.
2. Remove the **3/8" x UNC16 x 3-3/4" socket head cap bolt (83)** with the **8 mm L Allen wrench** if you want to
   take out the whole **Console Assembly (43)**. Separate the 500 mm computer cable (upper) (44) and the
   controller.
3. To remove the **Console Holder Assembly (7)**: take out the Chain Cover (L) and (R) first, then separate the
   **500 mm computer cable (upper) (44)** from the **500 mm computer cable (lower) (45)**. Apart from the
   **Board (34)**, take out the 3/8" x UNC16 x 3-3/4" socket head cap bolt (83); the Console Holder Assembly (7)
   and Console Assembly (43) then come away together.
4. Reverse the steps to refit the console. **The 3/8 inch bolt (83) and the upper computer cable pass through the
   same space and can interfere with each other** - the books say this "needs techniques".

The three books print this word for word. The bolt (83) is the same one the chain-cover procedure tells you to
loosen but not remove; here it does come out. The Sole SR500 and SR550 books print the same steps for the same
Dyaco chassis.
