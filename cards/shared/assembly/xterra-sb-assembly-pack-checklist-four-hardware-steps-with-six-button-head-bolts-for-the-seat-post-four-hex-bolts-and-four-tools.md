The 2013 book prints the model as **SB4.5r**; the 2020 book (and its 2014 printing) as SB500. Same pack apart from step 4.

| Step | # | Description | 2013 | 2020 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 70 | 5/16" x 18 mm Flat Washer | 6 | 6 |
| 1 | 73 | 5/16" Split Washer | 6 | 6 |
| 1 | 81 | M5 x 12 mm Phillips Head Screw | 4 | 4 |
| 1 | 90 | 5/16" x 1-1/4" Hex Head Bolt | 4 | 4 |
| 1 | 95 | 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt | 6 | 6 |
| 1 | 96 | 3/8" x 3-1/2" Carriage Bolt | 2 | 2 |
| 1 | 97 | 3/8" x 23 mm Curved Washer | 2 | 2 |
| 1 | 98 | 3/8" Cap Nut | 2 | 2 |
| 1 | 99 | M6 x 15 mm Phillips Head Screw | 4 | 4 |
| 2 | 71 | 3/8" x 19 mm Flat Washer | 4 | 4 |
| 2 | 80 | 3/8" Nyloc Nut | 4 | 4 |
| 2 | 100 | 3/8" x 3" Hex Head Bolt | 4 | 4 |
| 3 | 70 | 5/16" x 18 mm Flat Washer | 4 | 4 |
| 3 | 71 | 3/8" x 19 mm Flat Washer | 6 | 6 |
| 3 | 95 | 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt | 6 | 6 |
| 3 | 101 | 3/8" x 3/4" Hex Head Bolt | 6 | 6 |
| 3 | 102 | 5/16" x 19 mm Curved Washer | 2 | 2 |
| 4 | 70 | 5/16" x 18 mm Flat Washer | **2** | **4** |
| 4 | 73 | 5/16" Split Washer | **2** | **4** |
| 4 | 95 | 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt | **2** | **4** |
| 4 | 81 | M5 x 12 mm Phillips Head Screw | 4 | 4 |
| Tools | 103 | 12/14 mm Wrench | 1 | 1 |
| Tools | 104 | 14/15 mm Wrench | 1 | 1 |
| Tools | 105 | Phillips Head Screwdriver | 1 | 1 |
| Tools | 106 | M5 L Allen Wrench | 1 | 1 |

**Step 4 is where the two books differ**: the 2013 bike's front handlebar (3) is one piece held by two bolts; the 2020 bike's mast handlebar assembly (3L, 3R) is two pieces held by four. Everything else in the pack is identical.
