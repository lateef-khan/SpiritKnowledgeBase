Hardware: **#91** M5 x 10mm Screw (2 pcs).

1. Attach the Rear Stabilizer cover with two Screws (91). Tighten using the **Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (86)**.

Identical in every SB700 owner's manual from 2011 to 2021.

**The SB900 prints a different instruction for the same job**: "Install the rear stabilizer cover with two Phillips Head Screws (91)", and it names no tool.
