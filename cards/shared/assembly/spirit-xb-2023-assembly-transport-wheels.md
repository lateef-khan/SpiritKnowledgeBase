> The Recumbent Bike is equipped with 2 transport wheels on the front stabilizer. Lift
> the bike by the rear stabilizer and gently tilt the bike up and roll it away.

Two sentences, and the whole of it. All four manuals print them word for word; the
**XBU55 2023** writes "The Bike is equipped with..." under the heading **Moving The
Upright Bike**, and the three recumbents write "The Recumbent Bike".

**Lift the rear, roll on the front.** The wheels are on the front stabilizer and each
book's lettered diagram points at one - **N** on the XBR55 and XBR95, **M** on the
XBR25, **L** on the XBU55.

**Nothing says how heavy the machine is, how many people are needed, or how far it may be
rolled.** None of the four gives a weight for the lift, and none says to unplug the
machine first - though the XBR25, XBR55 and XBU55 run on mains power and the XBR95 does
not.

## The ten older residential bikes say nothing at all

The XBR25 2019, XBR55 2019 and 2021, XBR55ENT, XBR95 2016, 2018 and 2021, XBU55 2019 and
2021 and XBU55ENT manuals have no moving instruction anywhere, and eight of the ten never
print the word "wheel". See
[the absence card](spirit-xb-assembly-no-moving-or-transport-section.md).

## The indoor cycles say it differently

The CB900, CIC800 and CIC850 manuals print "equipped with two transport wheels, which are
engaged when the rear of the indoor cycle is lifted" - the same idea, different words,
different machines. See
[that card](spirit-cycle-assembly-transport-wheels-engage-when-the-rear-is-lifted.md).
