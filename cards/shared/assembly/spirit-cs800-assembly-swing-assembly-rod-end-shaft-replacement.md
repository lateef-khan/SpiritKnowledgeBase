**Align the flat of the swing axis with the U-slot before the bolt is locked** - both books end on that
instruction ("U-slot gap plane alignment" in 2016, "make sure axle and U-plate alignment" in 2020).

The XS200-SS003 book (cs800-2016), which prints the sizes:

1. First remove the Chain Covers (R, L) (the side case card).
2. Use a **10 mm wrench** to release the **M6 x 6T nyloc nut** fixed on the **M6 x 45L eye bolt**.
3. Use a **#12 open-end combination spanner wrench** to remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 3/4"** and **flat washer
   5/16" x 23 x 1.5T** securing the right swing arm assembly.
4. Remove the **5/16" x 3/4" hex head bolt** and **Ø5/16" x Ø23 x 3T flat washer** that fix the pedal assembly set
   and the swing axis.
5. Separate the base of the swing axis from the pedal assembly set, then remove the handle bar axle.
6. Use a **12 mm wrench** to remove the **5/16" x 3/4" hex head bolt** and **Ø8.5 x 26 x 2T flat washer** that fix
   the handle bar assembly set to the main frame, and separate them.
7. Reassemble in the reverse order. When refitting the swing axis, the flat plane must be connected with the
   U-slot gap plane alignment, then lock the screw.

**The CS800 (2020) book (cs800-2021) prints the same job in four moves** and drops the eye-bolt step: chain covers
off; a **12 mm open-end wrench** to remove the inside bolt and washer of the swing assembly axle; unmount the
axle and separate the swing assembly and connecting arm; a 12 mm wrench to remove the bolt and washer locking the
swing assembly to the mainframe. Same 12 mm, same bolt, no eye bolt named.

The washer in step 3 is printed 1.5T and in step 4 3T for the same bolt size; both are as printed. The Sole
SC200 rod-end-shaft card is this procedure word for word.
