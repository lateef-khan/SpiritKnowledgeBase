1. Take the **crank arms (51L, 51R)** and the **console mast cover (31)** off.
2. To release the **left front chain cover (29)**, use a Phillips head screwdriver on **7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 12 self-tapping screws (103)** and **2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws (102)**.
3. To release the **right front chain cover (30)**: on the mainframe, unscrew **2 pcs of Ø3.5 x 20 self-tapping screws (107)** with **2 pcs of Ø5 x 16 x 1.5T flat washers (78)**; then, on the right cover, unscrew **2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws** and unplug it.
4. To refit the right front chain cover, put it on the mainframe and locate **2 pcs of Ø3.5 x 20 self-tapping screws (103)** with **2 pcs of 3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T flat washers (78)** loosely, then tighten **2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws (101)** on the mainframe and tighten all the loose screws.
5. Match the left front chain cover (29) with the right (30) and secure both with 7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 12 self-tapping screws (103) and then 2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws (101).
6. **Pay special attention when installing the front shroud (30): the on/off switch (159) must be secured by the left and right front shrouds.** The top and bottom of the switch sit inside the two shrouds; the left and right of the switch sit outside them.

**Part-number drift inside the printed steps.** The 5 x 16 tapping screws are (102) in step 2 and (101) in steps 4 and 5; the Ø3.5 x 20 screws are (107) in step 3 and (103) in step 4; the washer (78) is Ø5 x 16 x 1.5T in step 3 and 3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T in step 4. The counts are consistent throughout.

Step 6 is the one instruction the 2009 XR898 version of this procedure does not have; the XR898 page also prints the Ø3.5 screws as x 16 rather than x 12, which is why that book has its own card.
