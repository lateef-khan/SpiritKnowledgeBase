1. Remove the console, as in steps 4.1.1 and 4.1.2 (8.41 and 8.42 in the CT1000ENT book).
2. Use a screwdriver to remove the **18 screws** from the upper bracket of the fixed bracket, and take the
   bracket cover off.
3. Unplug the **12P control cable connector** and remove the core module wire; the hand pulse control board
   can then be swapped for the new part.
4. Use the hexagonal wrench to remove the **four hexagonal screws**; the hand-held group - the hand pulse set -
   can then be replaced. Reassemble in the order of removal.

Three books print these four steps with the same counts. No hex key size is printed, and no part number.

The guide is a machine translation. Its words, as used on this card: **electronic watch** is the console,
**cross screwdriver** is a Phillips screwdriver, **umbrella head screws** are the guide's name for the
console and cover screws (no size is printed), and a **17th T-Shirt** or **13th T-sleeve** is a 17 mm or 13 mm
T-handle socket.
