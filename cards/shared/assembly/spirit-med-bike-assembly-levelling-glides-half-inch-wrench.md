Once the bike is assembled and placed on a flat level floor, it may be necessary to adjust the levelling glides on the bottom of the bike to ensure proper stability:

1. Use a **1/2" wrench** to **loosen the top nut of the leveler**.
2. **Adjust the levelers by hand** as necessary to remove any wobble.
3. **Tighten the top nut** against the bottom of the stabilizer tube.
4. Make sure the **bottom nut remains cinched against the leveling foot**.

**Seven books, one paragraph.** The 4.0R, 4.0U, 7.0R (2025 and the 2021 Dyaco MED edition), 7.0U, 8.0U and 8.5R print it word for word, each with its own model name in it; the 8.0U and 8.5R write the wrench as "½"" and drop the word "level" from "flat level floor". None gives a count or a location for the glides beyond "the bottom of the bike".

## The machine care page of the same books names a different wrench

Each book's noise remedy counts the pads and names a wrench, and not the same one. The **4.0R and 4.0U**: 2 leveling pads on the bottom of the rear stabilizer, "use a ½" (14mm) wrench (or adjustable wrench)". The **7.0R and 7.0U**: 2 pads on the rear stabilizer, "a 14mm wrench (or adjustable wrench)". The **8.0U and 8.5R**: 2 pads "on the bottom of the front and rear stabilizer", a 14mm wrench "to loosen the top nut of the levelers". The **2021 Dyaco MED 7.0R**: 2 pads and a ½" wrench. ½" is 12.7 mm and 14 mm is not, and only the 4.0 books equate them, in a bracket. The two pages describe the same nut; the care page is the only one that gives a count. **A 14 mm is in the 4.0R, 4.0U, 7.0R and 7.0U cartons** (in their 12/14 mm wrench) and the 8.5R's 13/14 mm wrench (612) opens to 14; the 8.0U names no 14 mm, and no book names a ½". An adjustable wrench answers both. The 4.0R and 4.0U Dyaco PT editions have no setting-up page and print only the 14 mm sentence, on their maintenance page.

The 8-series service manuals replace the same feet - **turn the adjustment foot counterclockwise** - and adjust their height "when the unit is not horizontal until balance"; that is the service card.
