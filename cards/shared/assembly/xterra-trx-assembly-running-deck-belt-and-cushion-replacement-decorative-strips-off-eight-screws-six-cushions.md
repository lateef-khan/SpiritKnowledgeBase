"Please take the cylinder before replacement."

1. With the running board folded, use an **M5 Allen wrench and a 12 mm wrench** to remove the securing
   screws on the **cylinder** and remove it.
2. Use a cross (Phillips) screwdriver to remove the **2 umbrella-head cross screws**, and the **4 tapping
   screws** of the left and right **decorative strips** (printed "Chinese face tapping screws" - a
   translation artefact; the book does not describe the head), then remove each strip in the arrow direction.
3. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **8 screws securing the running board**. Remove the board and
   replace the board or the running belt. If cushions need replacing, **remove the 6 cushions** and
   replace them. Reassemble in reverse order; the running belt tension then needs adjusting and
   centring.
4. Use a **14 mm open-end wrench** to adjust the drive belt tension with a tension measuring device to
   the **white LBS area between 70-75 lb**; tighten the 4 motor securing screws with the 14 mm T-shaped
   socket wrench.

The side strips are screwed, not taped - the TR260 and TRX1400 peel foam-taped foot rails instead, and
the TRX5500 undoes four foot-rail bolts. The TRX3500/4500 chapter names neither GT90C nor GT90D, so it
stands for both.
