1. Take the **rear shrouds (35, 36)** and the **seat carriage (4)** off.
2. Use a **12 mm open-end wrench** to release **6 pcs of 5/16" x 3/4" hex screws (94)**, **5/16" x 3/4" flat washers (160)** and **5/16" x 1.5T split washers (82)**, and take the **aluminium track (14)** off.

That is the whole procedure; no refit step is printed. Six books print it identically. The 2016 and ENT books put it under "Aluminum Trail and Stabilizer Cover" with the step cover and the induction brake controller; the 2023 books give it its own section, "Replacing Aluminum Track".

The flat washers (160) are printed with the same "5/16" x 3/4"" dimension as the screws; no washer count is given.
