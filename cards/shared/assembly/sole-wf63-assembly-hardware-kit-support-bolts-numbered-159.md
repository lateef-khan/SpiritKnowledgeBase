Hardware supplied in the box. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #140 | M5 x 10 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 8 pcs |
| #128 | - | Speed Nuts Clip | 6 pcs |
| #113 | Dia 10 | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| #130 | 5/16" x 1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 pcs |
| #119 | 3.5 x 12 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |
| #120 | 5 x 16 mm | Tapping Screw | 6 pcs |
| #139 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #159 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #75 | - | Safety Key | 1 pc |
| #134 | - | Lubricant | 1 pc |
| #131 | - | Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver | 1 pc |
| #132 | - | M6 Allen Wrench | 1 pc |

This kit is the VF63 2008 kit plus the four #159 support-brace bolts; the procedure gains the eighth step fitting them.
