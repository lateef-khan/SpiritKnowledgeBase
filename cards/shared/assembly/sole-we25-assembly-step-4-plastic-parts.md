1. **Connecting Arm Covers (71 and 72)** on the Left and Right Connecting Arms (8 & 9), each side with **two M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws (115)** and **one 3.5 x 12mm Sheet Metal Screw (126)** using the Short driver (160).
2. **Sliding Wheel Covers (82)**, each side with **four M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws (115)**.
3. **Stabilizer Covers (93) and (94)** on the middle stabilizer bar with **M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws (115)**. (No count is printed for these two covers.)
4. **Front Stabilizer Cover (79)** with **two M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws (115)**.
5. **Incline Cover brackets (27)**, bent-tab hole facing rearward, on the Incline Rail Assembly with **four M6 x 10mm Phillips Head Screws (125)**; **Rear Incline Bar Cover (91)** with **two M5 x 15 m/m Screws (115)**.
6. **Rear Stabilizer Cover (92)** with **four M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws (115)**.
7. **After the elliptical power is plugged in, run the incline to position 8** and install the **Incline Rail Front Cover (89)** up against the Middle Stabilizer tube with **two M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws (115)**.
