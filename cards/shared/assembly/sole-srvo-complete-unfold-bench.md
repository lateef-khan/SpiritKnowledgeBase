1. Place the adjustable knob into the bar hook. Raise the rack by pulling the adjustable knob on the top rod. After the rack is raised, tighten the knob at the bottom of the rack.
2. Unfold the front and rear stabilizer and make sure both are in place. Tighten the adjustable knob. (Printed between the labelled steps without its own step number.)
3. Unfold the SRVO stabilizer, and make sure the locking clamp is locked in securely. (Printed as UNFOLD STEP 3.)

Before any of this, remove all parts from the carton and inspect for damage or missing parts; if any are found, contact the dealer immediately. Folding the bench back down has its own card.
