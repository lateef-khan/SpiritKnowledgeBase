Attach the **Rear Stabilizer (4)** to the **Slide Rail (2)** and tighten with **4 Flat Washers (55, OD16 x ID8.5 x 1.5), 4 Spring Washers (52, ID8.5) and 4 Allen Bolts (81, M8 x 20)**. The drawing stacks them bolt - spring washer - flat washer, so the flat washer sits against the stabilizer.

Same four fasteners in all three books; the tool is the S5 Allen key (99 on the ERG160, 84 on the ERG180) and the S6 Allen key (97) on the ERG220. The 2023 ERG180 book calls the part the **Rear Support tube (4)** and its service manual "Rear Support Tube"; it is the same stabilizer.
