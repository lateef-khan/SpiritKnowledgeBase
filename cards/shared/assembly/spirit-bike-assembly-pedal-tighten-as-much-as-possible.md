**The left pedal has a reverse thread and screws in with the opposite rotation. Make
sure to tighten the pedals as much as you possibly can.**

**No torque figure is printed.** Not in the step, not in the hardware table, not
anywhere else in these four manuals. "As much as you possibly can" is the whole of the
instruction, and the carton holds no torque wrench.

The CR800 fits **Pedals (No.116 L, 117 R)** into **Crank Arms (No.51 L, 51 R)**; the
CU800 2021 and 2012 fit **Pedals (No.45 L, 46 R)** into **Crank Arms (No.16 L, 16 R)**.

## The CU800 2012 manual says two things the others do not

- **There is an "L" stamped into the end of the threaded post of the left pedal and an
  "R" in the right.** Both OCR scans of that page print it. No later manual mentions
  the stamps.
- **"It may be necessary to re-tighten the pedals if you feel a thumping during
  pedaling the bike. A noise or feeling such as a thumping or clicking is usually
  caused by the pedals being too loose."** The CR800 and CU800 2021 books drop this
  sentence from the assembly step; it survives in the maintenance chapter of several of
  these manuals as a noise remedy.

## The 900-series bikes do print a figure

**300 in-lb (34 N-m)** on all six of them. Do not carry it back to an 800-series
machine, whose crank arms and pedal item numbers are different - see
[that card](spirit-bike-assembly-pedal-torque-300-in-lb.md).

## The touchscreen books drop the warning entirely

The CR800ENT and both CU800ENT manuals, on these same chassis, print neither the torque
nor the reverse thread. See
[that card](spirit-bike-ent-assembly-pedal-no-thread-warning.md).

## Fourteen Spirit residential bikes say the same thing, and say more of it

Every one of the fourteen XB manuals - **XBR25** 2019 and 2023, **XBR55** 2019, 2021 and
2023, **XBR55ENT**, **XBR95** 2016, 2018, 2021 and 2023, **XBU55** 2019, 2021 and 2023
and **XBU55ENT** - prints the reverse-thread warning in step four and **no torque figure
anywhere in the book**.

All fourteen also print the two sentences the CR800 and CU800 2021 books dropped: the
**"L" and "R" stamped in the end of the threaded post**, and **"it may be necessary to
re-tighten the pedals if you feel a thumping while pedaling the bike. A thumping or
clicking noise is usually caused by loose pedals."** So the CU800 2012 is not the only
manual that keeps them.

**They do not all use the same words for "tight".** The ten recumbent books say
**"tighten the pedals as firmly as you can"**; the four upright books say **"as much as
you possibly can"**, which is the CR800 and CU800 wording. Same instruction, no figure
either way.

## Which pedal is which, on the XB machines

- **XBR95 2016 and 2018, XBR55 2019 and XBR55ENT:** **Pedals (116L, 117R)** into
  **Crank Arms (51L, 51R)**.
- **XBR95 2021, XBR55 2021 and XBR95 2023:** the same step, but the pedal is written
  **(116)** with no L or R and no item 117. The crank arms still read 51L, 51R.
- **XBR25 2019 and 2023:** **Pedals (L, R) (116, 117)**, tightened with the **wrench
  (132)** rather than the 113 the XBR55 and XBR95 name.
- **XBU55 2019, 2021, 2023 and XBU55ENT:** **Pedals (45L, 46R)** into **Crank Arms
  (16L, 16R)** - the CU800 item numbers, on a different machine. The XBU55 2023 book
  writes the pedal as **(45)** with no L or R.

## The residential touchscreen bikes do print the warning

The **XBR55ENT** and **XBU55ENT** carry it in full, unlike the commercial CR800ENT and
CU800ENT books. Do not read the touchscreen absence card as covering every ENT machine;
see [that card](spirit-bike-ent-assembly-pedal-no-thread-warning.md).
