**The left pedal has a reverse thread and screws in with the opposite rotation. Make
sure to tighten the pedals as much as you possibly can.**

**No torque figure is printed.** Not in the step, not in the hardware table, not
anywhere else in these four manuals. "As much as you possibly can" is the whole of the
instruction, and the carton holds no torque wrench.

The CR800 fits **Pedals (No.116 L, 117 R)** into **Crank Arms (No.51 L, 51 R)**; the
CU800 2021 and 2012 fit **Pedals (No.45 L, 46 R)** into **Crank Arms (No.16 L, 16 R)**.

## The CU800 2012 manual says two things the others do not

- **There is an "L" stamped into the end of the threaded post of the left pedal and an
  "R" in the right.** Both OCR scans of that page print it. No later manual mentions
  the stamps.
- **"It may be necessary to re-tighten the pedals if you feel a thumping during
  pedaling the bike. A noise or feeling such as a thumping or clicking is usually
  caused by the pedals being too loose."** The CR800 and CU800 2021 books drop this
  sentence from the assembly step; it survives in the maintenance chapter of several of
  these manuals as a noise remedy.

## The 900-series bikes do print a figure

**300 in-lb (34 N-m)** on all six of them. Do not carry it back to an 800-series
machine, whose crank arms and pedal item numbers are different - see
[that card](spirit-bike-assembly-pedal-torque-300-in-lb.md).

## The touchscreen books drop the warning entirely

The CR800ENT and both CU800ENT manuals, on these same chassis, print neither the torque
nor the reverse thread. See
[that card](spirit-bike-ent-assembly-pedal-no-thread-warning.md).
