**These ten manuals never tell you how to move the machine.** There is no MOVING section,
no TRANSPORTATION section and no SETTING UP YOUR BIKE page. The assembly chapter ends at
step four and the next page is console operation.

**Eight of the ten never print the word "wheel" at all.** The XBR25 2019, XBR55 2019 and
2021, XBR95 2016, 2018 and 2021 and XBU55 2019 and 2021 books use it nowhere - not in a
step, not on the machine drawing, not in a parts list.

**The two touchscreen books print it once each, in a parts list.** The XBR55ENT lists
*18 Transportation Wheel 2* and the XBU55ENT *47 Transportation Wheel 2* in their exploded
-view part tables. **So the machines have the wheels and the books never say what to do
with them.**

## How this was checked

Every one of the ten was searched, on native PDF text with hyphens rejoined, for *wheel*,
*transport*, *move*, *moving*, *roll* and *relocate* as loose words - not for a heading.
The only hits outside the two parts-list rows are "keep hands away from all **moving**
parts" on the safety page and "the Lap track will **move** in a counterclockwise
direction" in the console chapter.

Every page of every assembly chapter was also rendered at 200 dpi and read with OCR, and
the result compared against what `pdftotext` returns. **No page in any of the ten hides
text behind a picture**, so this is a real absence and not a flattened page.

## There is no levelling step either

None of the ten has a levelling instruction in its assembly chapter. All ten do carry the
Spirit noise-remedy sentence in their general-maintenance chapter - *two levelling pads on
the bottom of the rear stabilizer, use a 14mm wrench* - and **that sentence is the only
levelling instruction in these books.** See
[that card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

Their machine drawings do not point at the levellers either.

## The four 2023 residential bikes do print both

The XBR25 2023, XBR55 2023, XBR95 2023 and XBU55 2023 manuals add a **SETTING UP YOUR
BIKE** page after the last assembly step, carrying a levelling instruction, a powering-on
paragraph and a moving instruction, and their lettered diagrams point at the transport
wheel. See [the transport card](spirit-xb-2023-assembly-transport-wheels.md) and
[the levelling card](spirit-xb-2023-assembly-levelling-m14-wrench.md).

**The machines did not change.** The XBR55 2021 and XBR55 2023 are the same chassis with
the same hardware; the wheels were always there. What changed is the book.
