**Both XE795 books print the same figures.** The 2017 CE800 (XE890B) book has the same bolts but names a 12 mm
wrench for the button head bolt; the XE195/295/395 books use an outer nut and inner hex instead.

1. Remove the left and right chain cover (2016: refer to 11.6; 2023: "follow procedures 9.3, 9.4 and 9.6 to take
   apart connecting arm, pedal arm and both side cases").
2. Remove the **round disk cover** and use a **6 mm L Allen wrench** to release the **M8X40mm socket head cap bolt**
   fixed on the cross bar.
3. Use a **5 mm L Allen wrench** to remove the **5/16" x 10mm button head socket bolt** and **5/16" x 35 x 1.0T flat
   washer**, then take down the cross cranks.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **tapping screws** which secure the round disk and separate the
   cross bar and round disk from each other.
5. When reassembling, place the cross bar into the **centre of the rotating mandrel**. Next place the **Woodruff key
   with the rounded part facing inwards**. Reassemble the remaining parts in the reverse order.
