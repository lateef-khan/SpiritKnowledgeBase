**Belt tension 220 to 240 newtons (50 to 55 lbs); reed switch gap about 2 mm (3/16").** The upright CU900, CU900ENT and CU1000ENT books print 180 to 210 N and a 1 mm gap for the same-looking steps; those figures do not transfer.

**6.6.1 Release tension on the idler.** Loosen the adjustment nut on the **J-bolt** until the **tension spring** can be removed.

**6.6.2 Remove the belt.** After the idler is removed you can remove the **Poly-V drive belt**. **When re-installing the belt make sure the belt tension is set to 220 to 240 newtons (50 to 55 lbs).**

**6.6.3 Remove the idler assembly.** As shown in figures 6.6.5 and 6.6.6, loosen the **3 screws** and remove the idler assembly. **When re-installing the idler assembly, rotate the bracket fully clockwise so the screws are at the end of travel in the slots.**

**6.6.4 Remove the brake.** Remove the **4 screws** holding the brake in place and remove the brake.

**6.6.5 Replacing the bearing cartridge assembly.** As shown in figure 6.6.9, loosen the nut with a spanner wrench, or a screwdriver and hammer if necessary. **When installing the new assembly, align the key in the cartridge with the slot in the frame housing. Use Loctite thread locker** when installing the new cartridge.

**6.6.6 Remove the reed switch.** As shown in figure 6.6.10, loosen the reed switch screw and remove it. When installing the reed switch, **set the gap between the switch and the face of the magnet in the pulley to about 2 mm (3/16")**, and **offset the switch from the centre of the magnet**: the centre of the magnet should pass the edge of the switch's plastic housing, not its centre. **If the switch and magnet are not offset you can get two switch activations and the console will display double the actual pedal speed.**

**Figure 6.6.10 is a placeholder.** Both books print "Insert picture of reed switch alignment" where the drawing should be. The offset rule above is the only description of the alignment.

The belt-slipping answer in the troubleshooting chapter repeats the figure: loosen the bottom jam nut on the J-bolt, back it off a few turns, tighten the top nut to increase spring tension until the slipping stops, set 220 to 240 N (50 to 55 lbs), then re-tighten the jam nut. That adjustment belongs to the maintenance section; the replacement is here.

**The CR1000ENT book prints the same figures as 12.16** - J-bolt and tension spring, Poly-V belt at 220 to 240 N (50 to 55 lbs) kept in the middle of the pulley, three idler screws with the bracket fully clockwise, four brake screws, the keyed bearing cartridge with Loctite, and the reed switch gap "about 2 mm" - **but it omits the offset rule and the double-speed warning**, and its figure 12.40 is a real photograph of the switch on the frame, not the placeholder the CR900 books print. Set the offset as above anyway; nothing in the CR1000ENT book contradicts it.
