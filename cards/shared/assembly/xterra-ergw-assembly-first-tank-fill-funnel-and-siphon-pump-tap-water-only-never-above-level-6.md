The same page in all three books, with each book's own part numbers:

| Book | Fill plug | Upper tank (cover) | Funnel | Siphon pump |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ERG550W | 50 | 24 | 90 | 91 |
| ERG600W | 33 | 12 | 77 | 76 |
| ERG650W | 50 | 24 | 52 | 53 |

1. Remove the **fill plug** from the **upper tank cover**.
2. **To fill** (Fig. A): insert the **funnel** into the tank, then use a cup or the **siphon pump** and a bucket to fill. Use the **water level gauge on the side of the tank** to measure the level you want.
3. **To empty** (Fig. B): place a bucket next to the rower and use the siphon pump to pump the water out of the tank into the bucket.
4. Put the fill plug back into the upper tank cover. Wipe excess water off the frame.

**Water level.** The gauge is on the side of the tank. **The maximum fill level is 6. Never fill over it - filling over this limit voids the warranty.** Resistance depends on the level: level 1 is the lowest resistance, level 6 the highest.

**Notes.** Fill the tank only with **tap water**; never pool chlorine or chlorine bleach - "this will damage the tank and void the warranty". Tank water is not for drinking; dispose of it after pumping out. Water cleaning tablets are sold at www.xterrafitness.com or on 1-(870) 333-5500. The ERG550W adds a **Water Purification Tablet every 6 months or as needed** - the tablet interval, the litres-per-level table and emptying before a month's storage are maintenance and specs cards.

The console then asks for the water level (L1-L6) at first power-up so its load figures match the tank - the console section's card.
