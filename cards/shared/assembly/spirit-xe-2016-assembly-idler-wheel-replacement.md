**Seven books print this procedure with the same hardware and the same figure: 190 Hz (+/- 10).** The CE800 (2020)
/ CE800ENT books print `185Khz~210Khz` for their J-bolt idler; that card is separate.

1. Remove the cross bar (refer to 11.7; the 2023 book: "follow procedures 9.3, 9.4, 9.6 and 9.7 to take apart
   connecting arm, pedal arm, both side cases and cross bar").
2. Use a **#13 wrench** to loosen the **nyloc nut M8x9T** on the **hook screws** to remove the belt. Use the #13
   wrench to remove the **screws M8x20**, **nyloc nut M8x7T** and **flat washer 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T** to remove the
   idler wheel.
3. Reassemble the parts in the reverse order, tighten the **nyloc nuts M8x9T** on top of the hook screws, **flick the
   belt for a crisp sound** to check tightness or use a sound measuring device to measure at **190 Hz (+/- 10)**, then
   reassemble the remaining parts.

The XE395 books name the tool a "#13 Double Open-End Wrench"; the Sole E35-2016 twin calls the hook screws the
"J bolt" and the `M8x20` screws carriage bolts. The nut, screw, washer and frequency are the same in all of them.
