As shown in figure 6.12.1 (8.5R: 6.7.1), **remove the socket head cap bolt and the nut** to remove the brake pad. The 8.5R book names the tools: **an open-end wrench and an L Allen wrench**.

That is the whole procedure - one bolt, one nut, no sizes. The parts list carries the part as **Brake Pad (18)** and the lever it hangs from as **Brake Lever (4)** on an **Upright Brake Shaft (5)** with a **Steel Cable (6)**; the shaft has its own procedure on the 8.0U. The 7.0R and 7.0U call the same wear part the **felt** of the mechanical brake and adjust the cable rather than replacing the pad - that is the maintenance section's card.

**On the 8.5R the emergency-stop screw is checked, not the pad**: its preventative-maintenance page says an **M5 x 40 mm Phillips head screw** that is too tight makes the brake stick, and to loosen the screw or adjust the cable. The 8.0U prints no such check.
