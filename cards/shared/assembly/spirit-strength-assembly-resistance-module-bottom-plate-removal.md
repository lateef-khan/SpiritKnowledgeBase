*(Tool: Phillips screwdriver)*

1. First remove screw **No. 1**, then remove screw **No. 2**.
2. After keeping the foot pads properly, the bottom plate can be removed.

The order of the two screws is the manual's own. The foot pads come off with the plate and are to be kept; the
motor module is reached once the plate is off.

The "Digital Resistance Module" is the i-Strength line's motorised resistance box, part 72 in the CSI owner's manuals; the screw numbers are the call-outs on the manual's exploded drawings.
