Hardware step 3: **#113 5/16" x 35 x 3.0T flat washer (1), #114 5/16" x 23 x 2.0T flat washer (1), #112 8 x 25 quick release lever (1)**.

1. Install the handlebars (6) and handlebar post (4) together with the quick release lever (112), one flat washer (113), one flat washer (114) and one button head socket bolt (20), using the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (117).

Bolt (20) is not in the hardware pack; it comes on the post. The drawing puts the quick release (112) and the two washers on the top of the post where the handlebar clamps, and bolt (20) at the base of the post.
