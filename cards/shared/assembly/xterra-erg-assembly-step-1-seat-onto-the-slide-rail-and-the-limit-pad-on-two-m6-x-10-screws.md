1. Slide the **Seat Assembly (38)** onto the **Slide Rail (2)**.
2. Attach the **Limit Pad (34)** to the rail - the 2023 book says "to the back of" the rail - and secure it with **two M6 x 10 screws**.

| Book | Screw | Tool drawn |
|---|---|---|
| ERG160 (2020) | 62, Flat Head Screw M6 x 10 | 100, multi-function wrench |
| ERG180 (2023) | 65, Cross-head Screw M6 x 10 | (none) |

Same step, same pad, same thread; only the screw head differs, so use the screws the bag holds. The ERG160 drawing also shows a second pad and screw callout ("34 2pcs", "62 2pcs") - the parts list carries two limiter pads and four screws 62, but the step fits one pad with two screws, and the service manual for both machines removes "the limited pad" (singular) to slide the seat off.

The ERG160's seat stop brackets (11, 15) are fitted in step 4, not here; the ERG180 has none.
