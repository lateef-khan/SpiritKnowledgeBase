Hardware supplied in the box, identical in the UF80-group 2007 and VF80-group 2008 books. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #134 | M5 x 10 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 8 pcs |
| #128 | M5 | Speed nut clip | 6 pcs |
| #113 | 10 | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| #130 | 5/16 x 1/2 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 pcs |
| #122 | 3.5 x 12 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |
| #120 | 5 x 16 mm | Self Tapping Screw | 6 pcs |
| #133 | 3/8 x 1-3/4 | Button Head Socket Screw | 4 pcs |
| #75 | - | Ellipse Safety Key | 1 pc |
| #30 | - | Lubricant | 1 pc |
| #131 | - | Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver | 1 pc |
| #132 | - | M6 Allen Wrench | 1 pc |

There are no support-brace bolts in this kit and the seven-step procedure has no support-brace step; the WF80-group 2009 books add both.
