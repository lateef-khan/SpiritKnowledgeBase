**Four books, one set of figures.** The XE795 and CE800 books hold the cross bar with an `M8x40` socket head cap
bolt and a 6 mm L Allen wrench instead of this outer nut and inner hex.

1. Remove the left and right chain cover (refer to 11.6).
2. Remove the elliptical side cover **round disk cover**, then use a **12 mm wrench** (XE395: "12mm Open End
   Combination Spanner Wrench") to remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 15mm** and **flat washer 5/16" x 35 x 1.5T**
   securing the cross bar.
3. Use a **#13 wrench** to loosen the **outer bolt M8 x 6.3T (steel lvl 10)**. Use the #13 wrench and a **hex wrench
   (M6)** to loosen the **inner bolt** and remove the cross bar. (The XE395 books call the outer and inner parts
   "Nut".)
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **tapping screws** which secure the round disk and separate the
   cross bar and round disk from each other.
5. When reassembling, place the cross bar into the **centre of the rotating mandrel**. Next place the **Woodruff key
   with the rounded part facing inwards**. Reassemble the remaining parts in the reverse order.

The Sole E35-2016 twin adds a torque for the same joint (`500 Kg-cm`); none of these four books prints one.
