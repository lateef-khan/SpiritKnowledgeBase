1. **Align the plate of the Left Connecting Arm (8) with the foot pad axle of the Left Pedal Arm (6)** and put it in. Secure the **Fixing Axle (25)** with **2pcs Flat Washers (162)** and **2pcs Hex Head Bolts (122)** using **Wrench (179)**. Do the same for the Right Connecting Arm (9) on the Right Pedal Arm (7).
2. **Install the left and right Lower Handle Bars (10) (11)** onto the left and right console mast shafts and secure with **Hex Head Bolts (122)** together with **Flat Washers (164)** using **Wrench (179)**.
3. **Untie the rod end bearing on the left Connecting Arm (8).** Insert **Hex Head Bolt (123)** through the rod end bearing bracket on the Swing Arm (10) and with wrenches **(178 & 179)** tighten together with **Flat Washer (160)** and **Nylon Nut (149)**. Fix the right Swing Arm (11) and right Connecting Arm (9) the same way.
4. **Run the Handpulse W/Cable (57) from the Left Handpulse Assembly (15)** through the left round hole of the console mast and pull it out at the top. Secure to the console mast left side with **2pcs Hex Head Bolts (122)** and **2pcs Flat Washers (160)** using **Wrench (179)**. Do the same on the right with the **Handpulse W/Cable (58) from the Right Handpulse Assembly (16)**.
5. **Plug all of the connectors into the back of the console: Computer Cable (50), two Hand pulse Cables (57 & 58), Resistance switch wire (60) and Incline switch wire (61).** Secure the **Console (45)** with **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)** using driver (176).

The 2026 E35 console step plugs three connectors (computer cable plus two handle wires) with different part numbers, so that card stays separate.
