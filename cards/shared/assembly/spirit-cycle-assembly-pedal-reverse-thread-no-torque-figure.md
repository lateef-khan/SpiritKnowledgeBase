**The left pedal is reverse-threaded. It tightens counterclockwise. No torque figure is
printed in any of these five manuals** - not in the step, not in a hardware table, not
anywhere else in the book.

## Which pedal is which

- **XIC600 (both printings) and CB900:** pedals **25L** and **25R**. The XIC600 books
  add that the pedals are "identified by either an R or L stamped into the end of the
  bolt".
- **CIC800 and CIC850:** the R and L are **stamped on the spindle**, and the R pedal
  goes on the **chain guard side**. The CIC850 hardware table numbers them **136L/R**
  while its own drawing labels them **135/L** and **136/R**; the drawing and the table
  disagree, so order by side and stamp rather than by number.

## What each pair adds

- **CB900:** slide a **Flat Washer (89), 14mm x 20mm x 2T**, over the threads of each
  pedal before screwing it in, and tighten with the **14/15mm wrench (86)**. No other
  manual here fits a washer under the pedal.
- **CIC800 and CIC850:** **apply some grease to the threads** before engaging the crank.
  Both spell out the two directions - R clockwise, L counter-clockwise - rather than
  naming the thread.
- **XIC600:** no washer, no grease, no tool named.

## Do not carry a torque figure in from another Spirit bike

The Spirit 900-series upright and recumbent bikes print **300 in-lb (34 N-m)**, and the
AB950 air bike prints **33-37 lb-ft** for the pedal-to-crank-arm bolt. Neither figure is
stated for any machine on this card, and the crank arms and pedal item numbers are
different. See the look-alike cards listed above.
