1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet-metal screws (103)** and **2 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (101)** on the **left rear shroud (35)**. Unplug the **300 mm hand-pulse wire, coiled (26)**, the **2100 mm hand-pulse wire, coiled (133)** and the **2100 mm switch cable (upper) (148)**, and the left rear shroud (35) can be taken off.
2. On the **right rear chain cover (36)**, unscrew the **Ø3.5 x 16 self-tapping screw (103)** with its **3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T flat washer (78)** and **2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws (101)**, and the right rear chain cover (36) is released.
3. Use special tools to remove the nut from the **hand-pulse cable sensor assembly (26)** and the **handle switch bracket**; the left and right rear shrouds can then be separated.
4. To refit, place the **5/16" x 16 x 1T flat washer (127)** on the plastic post of the rear shroud (R), pass the post through its hole into the **main frame (1)** and fix it lightly with one Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet-metal screw and the 3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T flat washer (78); join the left and right rear shrouds (35) (36) and lock them with **6 pcs of Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet-metal screws**; then adjust the bottom cover position and fix it to the main frame with **4 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws** (printed "(01110)", a Sole-style part number in a Spirit book).

**Seven Ø3.5 screws come off the left shroud and six plus one go back**; two 5 x 16 screws come off each side and four go back. Printed that way in all six books.

**The XBR95 2016 book names the right-side frame screw differently in step 2**: "Ø3.5 x 20 sheet metal screw (107) with 3/16" x 15 x 1.5T flat washer (78)" - the 2009 XR898 wording - where the other five books print Ø3.5 x 16 (103). Its refit step is the same as the others.

The 2020 CR800 books print seven-and-two on refit and no washer (127); the 2009 XR898 prints seven-and-two with the washer. Both are linked.
