**WARNING.** A Velcro strap is installed around the treadmill base to stop the
unit unfolding accidentally during shipping. If the strap is not removed
properly the treadmill **could spring open unexpectedly** and injure anyone
standing near it.

1. Position the treadmill **flat on the ground**, in the orientation it would be
   in if you were using it.
2. **Do not turn the treadmill up on its side** while removing the shipping
   strap. That can make the folding mechanism spring open.
3. If the end of the strap - the end you need to grab - is under the treadmill
   deck, **reach under the deck to grab it. Do not tilt the treadmill up** to get
   at the strap end.

The XT185-2023 and XT285-2023 manuals print this warning twice, once under
PRE-ASSEMBLY and again as part of assembly step 1. The XT385-2023 and
XT485-2023 manuals print it once, under PRE-ASSEMBLY.

**The XT685-2023 is not covered by this card.** That manual prints no strap
warning at all and the machine does not fold.

These four manuals describe a strap only. They never mention a shipping safety
pin, which the Sole folding treadmills do have - do not go looking for one.

**The XT485 2013 manual prints the same warning, word for word, ten years earlier** - under the heading "!!ATTENTION: IMPORTANT UNPACKING INSTRUCTIONS. PLEASE READ BEFORE UNPACKING YOUR FOLDING TREADMILL!!" at the top of its assembly chapter, once. It then continues into a box-cutter unpacking paragraph and a five-section hardware pack that the 2023 manuals do not print; that is its own card. The 2015 XT manuals, between the two, print no strap warning at all.
