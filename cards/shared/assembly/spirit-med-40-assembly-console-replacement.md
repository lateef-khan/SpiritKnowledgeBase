**Six screws, not four: two hold a console chin cover that comes off first.** The CR800, CU800 and XBR consoles have no chin cover and come off on four screws; those are the linked cards.

1. Unscrew **2 pcs of M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screws** with a Phillips head screwdriver and remove the **console chin cover**.
2. Unscrew **4 pcs of M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screws** with the Phillips head screwdriver and the console can be released.
3. Unfasten all the cables connected to the console.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.

Both books print the four steps word for word. Neither names the cables (the 4.0R owner's manual connects two harnesses, 44 and 45, and fits the chin cover with two screws numbered 99; the 4.0U owner's manual connects three, 26, 28 and 29). No torque is printed.

The console mast procedure on the 4.0R and the handlebar procedure on the 4.0U both begin "take the console apart first", so this card is their first step.
