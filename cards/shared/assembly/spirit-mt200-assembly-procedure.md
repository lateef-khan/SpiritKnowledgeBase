**Step four here uses bolt 153 and washer 59.** The 7.0T runs the same four steps
in the same words but bolts its handrails on with 154 and 155 instead. Ordering
by the 7.0T numbers will get the wrong parts for an MT200.

The 2022 manual opens the chapter with one extra line the 2010 manual does not
print: read each step's instructions and study the drawing carefully to become
familiar with all the parts and procedures before beginning each step.

## Step 1 - the upright tubes

1. **The right side upright tube (19) has the computer cables pre-installed.**
   Lift the tube off the walking surface and attach it to the base frame. Gently
   pull on the computer cables as you align the upright tube into the bracket on
   the base frame, taking the slack out so the cable does not get pinched between
   the tube and the base frame. Assemble one 3/8" x 3-3/4" bolt (104), 3/8" split
   washer (58) and 3/8" flat washer (59) through the top hole in the retaining
   plate (21), and the corresponding top hole in the upright tube, and screw into
   the base frame. Now install the other two bolts (104) and washers (58 & 59)
   and hand tighten all three. **Do not torque the bolts yet until the upper
   console frame is installed.**
2. Install the left upright (18) in the same manner.

## Step 2 - console frame, mounting brackets and end caps

1. Install the two mounting brackets (3 & 4) with the six M8 x 12mm screws
   (108). **When assembling the console mounting brackets (items 3 & 4), slide
   the brackets all the way towards the back of the console frame before
   tightening the bolts.** The 2022 manual repeats this as a caption: push the
   mounting bracket all the way towards the back of the console, then tighten the
   screws.
2. Mount the console frame (5) to the upright tubes (18 & 19) with six 3/8" x
   3/4" bolts (153), 3/8" split washers (58) and 3/8" flat washers (59). Be
   careful not to pinch the computer cables.
3. Firmly tighten the six bolts (104) at the bottom of the uprights and install
   the two end caps (32 & 33) with the four M5 x 12mm screws (73).

## Step 3 - the console

1. Plug all the connectors in back of the console.
2. Mount the console (36) to the console frame (5) with two M5 x 30mm screws
   (112) in the **top** holes and two M5 x 10mm screws (110) in the **bottom**
   holes in the back side of the console frame.

## Step 4 - parallel bar handrails, lift bars and rear step

1. Install the eight 3/8" x 3/4" bolts (153) and 3/8" flat washers (59) into the
   holes on the underside of the frame. **Only thread them into the holes two or
   three turns** so the slots in the handrail brackets can slide onto the bolts
   easily. Slide the handrail onto the bolts and hand-tighten them. Thread the
   eight 3/8" x 2" bolts (226) into the sides of the handrails. Once all the
   bolts are installed, tighten all of them securely.
2. Install the Lift Bars (192) to the parallel bars and secure with the knobs
   (207).
3. Install the rear step (181) by sliding into the receiving tubes under the rear
   of the deck and secure with the two pins (217). Raising the rear incline may
   make it easier to line up the holes for the pins.

## What the two manuals do and do not settle

- The text names only one retaining plate, **(21)**, and then says to fit the
  left upright "in the same manner". It gives no separate number for the left
  plate in the words.
- **Neither manual has an unpacking or pre-assembly section.** The assembly
  chapter opens straight into the per-step hardware photographs.
- The 2022 manual's copy of these steps is broken by the page layout and loses
  whole clauses - the sentence that says to lift the tube off the walking
  surface, the bolt and washer names in step two, and the opening of step four.
  The wording above is the 2010 manual's, which prints all four steps intact.
  Nothing in the surviving 2022 text contradicts it.
