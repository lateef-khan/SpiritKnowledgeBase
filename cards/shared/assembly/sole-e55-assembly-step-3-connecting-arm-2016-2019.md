1. **Insert the left Top Handle Bar (19) onto the left Lower Handle Bar** and secure with **three Hex Head Bolts (123)**, **two Curved Washers (178)** and **three Nyloc Nuts (155)** using **Wrench (186)**. Secure the right Top Handle Bar (20) the same way.
2. **Connect the Upper Resistance Handle Wire (55) with the Lower Resistance/Incline Handle Wire (57)**, store it in the frame tube, and **snap in the Switch Wire Cap (65)** on the console mast. Then connect the **Upper Incline Handle Wire (56)** with the Lower wire (57) the same way and snap in the second cap (65).
