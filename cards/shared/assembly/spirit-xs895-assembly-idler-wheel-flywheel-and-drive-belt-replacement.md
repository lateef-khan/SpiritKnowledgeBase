**Belt tension 190 Hz, plus or minus 10 Hz, on a sonic belt tension meter**, set on the idler wheel assembly.

1. Follow the gear motor replacement to unmount the steel cable from the flywheel. Then use a **13 mm box
   wrench** to remove the adjust nut from the **J-bolt**.
2. Use a **13 mm box wrench** to remove the idle wheel **carriage bolt**, then take off the idle wheel assembly.
3. Use a **15 mm box wrench** to remove the **flywheel locking nuts**, then take off the flywheel from the
   mainframe.
4. Take the drive belt off the drive pulley.
5. Reverse the steps to install the flywheel and drive belt. Use **17 mm box wrenches** to adjust the flywheel
   location so the drive belt is centred on the drive pulley, then secure the flywheel locking nuts.
6. Adjust the idle wheel assembly to give the drive belt the right tension - **use a sonic belt tension meter;
   the right value is 190 Hz +/- 10 Hz**. Rotate the crank to check that the flywheel, drive belt and drive pulley
   run smoothly, then install all other parts.

The troubleshooting chapter's quick fixes for a slipping belt - tighten the bolt and nut on the crank arm, then
adjust the drive belt tension with a 13 mm wrench on the J-bolt nut - are the adjustment; a belt that drops is
refitted after loosening this idler and checking that the drive pulley does not swing and that pulley, belt and
flywheel are aligned. The same 190 Hz is printed for the CS800 (2016) idler and the Sole CC81.

The XS300B-YS006 service manual is one book for both XS895 owner's-manual printings; the two-column page is read by step number.
