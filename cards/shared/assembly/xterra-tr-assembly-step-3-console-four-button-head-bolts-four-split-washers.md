The same step in nine books: connect the cables at the top of the uprights, then set the **console
assembly (21)** into place on the **left and right uprights (4) and (5)** and secure it with **four 5/16"
x 3/4" button head socket bolts (125)** and **four Ø8 x 1.5T split washers (80)**, tightened with the
combination M5 Allen wrench and Phillips screwdriver (102). Be careful not to pinch the computer
cables.

What is connected first differs by book:

| Book | Connections, in printed order |
|---|---|
| TR300 2021, TR6.4 2024 | middle computer cable (54) to upper (53); Speed Cable (162) to Speed/Hand Pulse Complex (28); Incline Cable (163) to Incline/Hand Pulse Complex (29) |
| TRX2500 / TRX3500 / TRX4500 / TRX5500 2024 | middle (54) to upper (53); Speed Adjustment Switch W/Cable (Upper) (114) to Speed/Hand Pulse Complex (28); Incline Adjustment Switch W/Cable (Upper) (115) to Incline/Hand Pulse Complex (29) |
| TR65 2023, TR75 / TR85 2024 | middle (54) to upper (53); Speed Cable (Upper) (114) to (28) **(3 pin)**; Handpulse Wire (Upper) (114-01) to (28) **(2 pin)**; Incline Cable (Upper) (115) to (29) **(3 pin)**; Handpulse Wire (115-01) to (29) **(2 pin)** |

The TR65 names the (28) mate "Speed Adjustment Switch W/Cable" where the TR75/TR85 say "Speed / Hand
Pulse Complex". The TRX2500's hardware sidebar labels the split washer "#100" - a misprint for #80,
which its own sentence uses. The TR300 numbers the uprights (4) and (5) here after calling (4) the
right upright and (5) the left at step 2.
