Four quantities in these books do not balance against the joint they describe. **All four
are on the printed page** - a 300 dpi render of each page agrees with the PDF text layer,
so they are the manual's error and not an extraction fault.

| Machine | Step | Printed | Why it cannot be right |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standing calf extension | 1 | 7 bolts, 8 spring washers, 8 flat washers | one washer of each type per bolt |
| Torso rotation | 1 | 7 bolts, 11 spring washers, 11 flat washers | same, and four washers over |
| Squat/standing calf | weight stack | 3 rod end covers | two guide rods take four, two per rod |
| Shoulder press | weight stack | 11 magnet pulleys | the machine has one magnet pulley |

**Count the washers, not the bolts, on the first two.** Every other machine in the range
pairs the door-frame bolt one-for-one with a spring washer and a flat washer, and prints
4 of each or 8 of each. Eight and eleven are the counts that look deliberate; seven is
the one to distrust.

**On the shoulder press, 11 is the row above bleeding into this one** - its 20 lb weight
stack row is also 11, and the magnet pulley sits directly under it. One machine, one
magnet pulley. See
[the magnet pulley card](spirit-strength-assembly-the-magnet-side-of-the-upper-pulley-faces-the-sensor.md).

**On the squat/standing calf, fit four rod end covers.** Two rods, two ends each; the rest
of the range prints 4.

If a count in your own book looks wrong, this is the check: the door-frame joint takes
either 4 or 8 M10x35 bolts on every machine in this range, with matching washers.
