> The indoor cycle is equipped with two transport wheels, which are engaged when the
> rear of the indoor cycle is lifted.

One sentence, and the whole of it. Each manual writes its own model name into it.

**Lift the rear.** The wheels are on the **front** stabilizer - the CIC800 and CIC850
labelled diagram calls out TRANSPORT WHEELS there, and the CIC assembly step one tells
you to fit the stabilizer with the wheels facing up and toward the front. Lifting the
rear tips the machine forward onto them.

**Nothing says how heavy the machine is, how many people are needed, or how far it may
be rolled.** None of the three manuals gives a weight for the lift.

## The air bikes and the Johnny G lift from the rear too, but say it differently

Those three manuals print a MOVING & LEVELING section that names the front leg and the
four levelling adjusters. Different words, different machines - see
[that card](spirit-airbike-jb950-assembly-moving-and-levelling.md).

## The XIC600 says nothing at all

Neither XIC600 printing has a transport instruction, even though its front stabilizer
carries wheels. See
[the absence card](spirit-xic600-assembly-no-levelling-or-moving-section.md).
