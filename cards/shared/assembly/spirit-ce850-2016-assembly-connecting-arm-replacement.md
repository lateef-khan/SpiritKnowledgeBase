**The stride machines' connecting arm rides on a `Ø17 x 95L` pedal axle and an `M12 x P1.75` rod end bearing.** The
XE-series connecting arm uses a `5/16"` rod-end bolt; do not carry those figures here.

1. Remove the left and right **pedal bases** by the disassembly steps. Separate the screws of the **M12 x P1.75 rod
   end bearing** from **lower handle bar (L) and (R)** and **pedal arm (L) and (R)**.
2. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **Ø5/16" x Ø23 x 1.5T flat washer** from pedal arm (L) and (R) and
   the **rear rail assembly** using a **12 m/m wrench**.
3. Pull out the **pedal axle (Ø17 x 95L)** from the inside of pedal arm (L) and (R).
4. Remove **six M5 x 6L Phillips head screws** from the **frame cover** and **brush** with a Phillips head screwdriver.
5. Remove the **M5 x 6L Phillips head screws** from the **aluminum axle end cap** of pedal arm (L) and (R).
6. Remove the **three 5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolts** from the **aluminum track** of pedal arm (L) and (R) using an
   **M5 L Allen wrench**.
7. Release the **nut** from the **M12 x P1.75 rod end bearing** using a **19 m/m wrench**.
8. Reassemble by the above steps.
9. **Line up the pedal axle (ψ17x95L) with the surface of the notch ("breach") of the fixing plate** and lock it with
   the screw.

The XE895-2016 book prints step 9 without the axle size; the CE850-2016 book prints "(ψ17x95L)". Step 6 names an
Allen wrench for a hex head bolt; quoted as printed.
