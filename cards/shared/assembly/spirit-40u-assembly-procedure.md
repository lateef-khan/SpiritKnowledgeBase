**The 2025 Spirit book and the undated Dyaco PT edition print the same four steps word for word, item numbers included.** Item numbers in parentheses are assembly-drawing numbers; the quantities are on the hardware card.

## Step one - rear stabilizer and seat mount slide

1. Install the **rear stabilizer (5)** onto the **main frame (1)** with the four hex head bolts (50) and four flat washers (71).
2. Install the **seat mount slide (7)** onto the track on top of the seat tube with the seat post facing forward, with the **seat adjustment knob (86)**, flat washers (71), **fixing plate (8)** and **nut (78)**.

## Step two - console mast

1. Run the **wiring harness (29)** through the **console mast (2)** and **console mast cover (38)** and out the top.
2. Assemble the console mast (2) to the main frame (1) with **6 bolts (51) and 6 washers (72) on the both side screws, 1 bolt (51) and 1 curved washer (99) on the front screw**. Do not pinch any wires.

## Step three - handlebars

1. Install the **handlebars (3)** to the console mast (2) with **4 bolts (60), 4 lock washers (64), 8 washers (57) and 4 nuts (84)**.
2. Run the **2 hand pulse wires (26 and 28)** into the hole in the handle bar mounting plate and out through the hole in the console plate.

## Step four - console, covers, cup holder, pedals, seat

1. Connect the wiring harness (26, 28 and 29) to the **console (34)**. Attach the console (34) to the console mast (2) using 4 screws (75). Be careful not to pinch any wires.
2. Attach the **console bracket cover (42)** to the handlebars (3) using 2 screws (74).
3. Install the **front stabilizer cover (40)** and the **rear stabilizer cover (41)** onto the main frame (1) with 4 screws (75).
4. Attach the **cup holder (116)** to the console mast (2) using 2 screws (75).
5. Install the **left and right pedals (45 L and 45 R)** onto the **left and right crank arms (16 L and 16 R)**. The left pedal has a reverse thread and will be screwed in with the opposite rotation. **Make sure to tighten the pedals as much as possible.**
6. Install the **seat (19)** onto the **seat post (7)**.

## What differs from the CU800 build

The console mast bolts are the CU800's seven; **the handlebars are not**. The CU800 handlebars go on two 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts, this machine's on **four M8 x 65 socket head cap bolts with lock washers, eight flat washers and four nyloc nuts**, which the service manual releases with an Allen wrench and a 13 mm wrench. Step one fits the seat mount slide with a knob, fixing plate and cap nut where the CU800 steps do not mention the slide at all.

The pedal step prints no "L" and "R" stamp sentence and no thumping sentence, unlike the 4.0R. The build ends at the seat: the bike is generator-powered and the console starts when you pedal. The PT edition prints "Size: 108 x 55 x 136 cm, Weight: 60.5 kg" under its last drawing - the specs section's fact.
