**The CE900 and CE900ENT books print this word for word; the CE1000ENT's console comes off differently (two cover
screws, then a console and a separate keyboard).** The book calls the console the "electronic watch".

1. Unbolt the **TV tube dummy plug** before the next step (figure 6.1.1). Unbolt the **console-mast tube cover** by
   removing its **4 screws** (figures 6.1.2 and 6.1.3).
2. Remove the electronic watch (console) by removing the **M5 screw (4 pcs)** from the rear of it and removing the
   **wire connector** (figures 6.1.4 and 6.1.5).
3. Reinstall the console by reversing the process (the book says "reversing the processes 6.1.4 until 6.1.5" - the
   figure numbers, not step numbers).

No torque is printed. The same Dyaco template on the CU900ENT and CR900 bikes is carded separately; do not carry
their screw counts here.
