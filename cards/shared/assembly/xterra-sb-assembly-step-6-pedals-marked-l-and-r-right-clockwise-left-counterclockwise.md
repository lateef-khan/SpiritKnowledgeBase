1. The left and right pedals (2018: 37 and 36; 2024: 19L and 19R) are marked "L" and "R". Attach the straps to the pedals.
2. Connect the right pedal (36) to the crank (17) on the right hand side of the cycle as you sit on it, and repeat for the left pedal (37) - the 2024 book says it the other way round, left pedal (19L) to the left crank arm (48) first, then the right.

**Note: the right pedal should be threaded on clockwise and the left pedal on counterclockwise.**

No tool and no torque are printed. The 2024 book's step is headed "Pedals and Base Cover" but has no base cover; the 2018 book fits its two rear support covers in the same step (the linked card).
