If the seat assembly on the recumbent bike is loose and wobbles from side to side:

1. Loosen the **nyloc nut (B)** with a **13 mm wrench**.
2. Press down on **(A)** with an **11 mm wrench**.
3. While pressing down on (A), tighten the nyloc nut (B).
4. Use this procedure on **all 4 seat wheel assemblies**.

**Why it works, as the manual explains it:** there are two wheels on each assembly. Pressing down on (A) turns the assembly so that the inside wheel (which is connected to B) is pressed to the top of the track while the outer wheel (connected to A) stays pressed to the bottom of the track. The play is taken up between the two wheels and the track, not by clamping harder.

The drawings show (A) and (B) on the front and rear wheel assemblies of the left side. The page is headed "Tighten seat carraige" as printed. The same wheel-setting rule, with the directions of turn spelt out per corner, is the last step of the later XBR and CR800 seat carriage replacement procedures (linked).

This is the only mechanical repair procedure in the 2008 XBR25/XBR55 dealer manual; its other four procedures are electrical tests and the engineering mode.
