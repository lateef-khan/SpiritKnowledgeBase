1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the screws on both sides of the chain cover and take off the **Chain Cover L and R (9, 10)**.
2. Reassemble the chain covers in the reverse order.

No count is printed; the parts lists carry eleven ST4.2 x 19 and four ST4.2 x 25 self-tapping screws (ERG160) for the covers. Every procedure on the flywheel, belt, pulleys and magnet bracket starts here.
