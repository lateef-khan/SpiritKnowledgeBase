Four tables, one per step, identical in both books. Item numbers are the assembly-drawing numbers.

## Hardware for step 1

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | Hex Head Bolt | 3/8" x 2-1/4" | 4 |
| 71 | Flat Washer | Ø3/8" | 5 |
| 78 | Cap Nut | 3/8" x 12.5T | 1 |

Five washers: four under the stabilizer bolts and one on the seat adjustment knob. The knob (86) and the fixing plate (8) are parts, not listed here.

## Hardware for step 2

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 51 | Hex Head Bolt | 5/16" x 5/8" | 7 |
| 72 | Flat Washer | Ø5/16" | 6 |
| 99 | Curved Washer | Ø5/16" | 1 |

The CU800's step-two table, line for line.

## Hardware for step 3

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 57 | Flat Washer | Ø8.5 | 8 |
| 60 | Socket head cap bolt | M8 x 65L | 4 |
| 64 | Split Washer | Ø8 x 1.5T | 4 |
| 84 | Nyloc Nut | M8 x 7T | 4 |

**Not the CU800's two 5/16" x 5/8" bolts.** Four through-bolts with a washer each side and a nut.

## Hardware for step 4

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 74 | Phillips head screw | M5 x 8L | 2 |
| 75 | Phillips head screw | M5 x 12L | 10 |

Ten of item 75: four for the console, four for the stabilizer covers, two for the cup holder; the two short screws (74) hold the console bracket cover. **No torque figure is printed for any bolt, and none for the pedals.** The seat (19) goes onto the seat post with no listed fastener.
