"The bike (elliptical) is equipped with adjustable end caps. **If the unit is wobbly, please turn the leveling caps on the rear stabilizer to level the unit.**"

The same sentence in all five books; none says which way to turn the caps or names a tool. The two recumbents also screw a single **leveling pad** into the underside of the main frame during step 2 (2018: pad 39; 2024: pad 25) - that is a fixed foot, and this page is about the two threaded caps at the ends of the rear stabilizer.

Other Xterra lines say it differently: the indoor cycles and air bikes say "adjust all four levelers", the 2023 recumbent's step 1 turns "the adjustable end-cap", and the treadmills carry levelling feet with a wrench size (the linked cards).
