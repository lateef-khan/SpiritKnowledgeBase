1. **Slide the Console Mast Cover onto the Console Mast**, facing the correct way.
2. **Unravel the Computer Cable** at the top opening of the Main Frame and feed it into the bottom of the console mast tube and out of the top opening.
3. **Install the Console Mast into the receiving bracket** in the top of the Main Frame. **One bolt is already installed** in the receiving bracket and engages the slot at the bottom of the Console Mast; it gets tightened at the end with the three other console mast bolts.
4. **Put 1pc of 3/8" x 2T Split Washer onto 1pc of 3/8" x 2-1/4" Hex Head Screw** and **2pcs of 3/8" x 23 x 1.5T Curved Washers onto 2pcs of 3/8" x 3/4" Hex Head Screws**. Hand-tighten the long screw through the left side of the receiving bracket into the Console Mast (the electrical wire runs through the tube; do not damage or pinch the Computer Cable or the Console may be damaged). Hand-tighten the two short screws through the front of the receiving bracket.
5. **Using the 13/14m/m Wrench, tighten the three bolts plus the pre-installed fourth firmly, as much as you possibly can.**
6. **Locate the Console and 4 pcs of M5 x 10m/m Phillips Head Screws.**
7. **Three electrical wire connectors sit at the top opening of the console mast: two 2-pin (hand pulse sensors) and one 10-pin (main wire harness).** Connect them to the mating keyed connectors on the back of the console; do not force them. **The two 2-pin connectors can go into either console connector.**
8. **Store the excess wire back into the Console Mast**, install the Console onto the mounting plate, and secure with the 4 pcs of M5 x 10m/m Phillips Head Screws.
