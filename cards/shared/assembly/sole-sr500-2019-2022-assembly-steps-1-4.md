1. Gather HARDWARE FOR STEP 1. Use the WRENCH (128) to tighten 4 HEX HEAD BOLTS (120) together with 4 SPRING WASHERS (121) and 4 FLAT WASHERS (109) to secure the MAIN FRAME (1) and FRONT STABILIZER (2) together.
2. Gather HARDWARE FOR STEP 2. Use ALLEN WRENCH (126) to tighten 4 BUTTON HEAD SOCKET BOLTS (129) onto the ALUMINUM TRACK (51) and the Rear Stabilizer (3).
3. Gather HARDWARE FOR STEP 3. Attach the other end of the ALUMINUM TRACK (51) to the FOLDING END ASSEMBLY (6) and use COMBINATION WRENCH (127) to tighten the 5 BUTTON HEAD SOCKET BOLTS (123) together with 4 FLAT WASHERS (122).
4. Gather HARDWARE FOR STEP 4. Install the left and right PEDALS (4, 5) on the MAIN FRAME (1) with 6 BUTTON HEAD SOCKET BOLTS (123) and 6 FLAT WASHERS (122), using COMBINATION WRENCH (127) to tighten. Use COMBINATION WRENCH (127) again to install the CONNECTING COVER (63) on the ALUMINUM TRACK (51) with 3 PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (85). Install the SEAT (49) and SEAT ATTACHING BOARD (16) with 4 PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (124) together with 4 SPRING WASHERS (125), using COMBINATION WRENCH (127).

On the 2022 machine a tablet-holder sub-step follows this step 4; it has its own card. The 2019 book prints no first-power-on step: its console wakes by pressing any button, which is console-section content.
