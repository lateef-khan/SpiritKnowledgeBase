**No zeroing distance is printed. The motor finds its own zero: fit it with the tube bolt out, power the
machine on, let it run to zero, then put the tube bolt in.** The 2015 and 2023 XT service manuals zero the
motor by hand to 210mm or 225mm instead; those figures belong to those machines, not to these.

1. Turn the power switch off.
2. If possible, fold up the unit.
3. Turn the treadmill onto its side.
4. Disconnect the motor wires from the controller.
5. Remove the bolt from the incline tube.
6. Remove the bolt from the top of the incline motor and take out the motor.
7. Insert the new motor and put in the bolt on the top.
8. Reconnect the motor wires to the controller.
9. Turn on the unit. The new motor should turn until it is at "zero".
10. Insert the bolt into the incline tube.

The book's controller diagram (its Procedure 11) is where the three motor wires are named: **Down - black,
Com - white, Up - red**. No wrench size, bolt size or torque is printed, and the one procedure serves all five
machines without distinguishing them.
