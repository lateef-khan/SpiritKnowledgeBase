"Note: To perform these replacements, the Cylinder needs to be dismounted first."

1. Fold the treadmill. Unmount the cylinder with an **M5 hex wrench** and a **12mm open-end wrench**.
2. Follow section 9.6 (the roller procedure) to remove the front and rear rollers. Use a screwdriver to
   remove the **foot rail fixing screws** on both sides, then follow the direction to slide both foot rails out.
3. Remove the **8 Running Deck locking screws**, then take off the running deck. Now you can replace the
   running deck, the running belt and the cushions. Do the reverse steps to install them back.
4. Adjust the running belt tension and centre it.

Four books print these four steps word for word. What they leave out, and the 2015 XT books print: the
number of foot rail screws (six there) and the number of cushions (six there). Do not carry those counts to
these machines from the older books.

The XT685 and XT685ENT do not fold and have no cylinder; their version of this procedure is on its own card.
No torque is printed and the belt tension is not specified here.
