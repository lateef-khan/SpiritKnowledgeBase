**No assembly is printed, and none is missing.** The SRVO-2021 book covers a motorized cable trainer, not a bench: its "installation" content is electrical (grounding, outlet, surge protection) and its mechanical content is cable use, knob resistance modes, and cable care.

This absence was proved four ways: a loose-word search for assembl, hardware, bolt, install, attach, unfold and fold returns only electrical-outlet hits and the service phone line; a spacing-tolerant search finds no split headings; OCR supplements cover every content page (PDF pp.1-15); and the one unsupplemented page (PDF p.16) was rendered and read as a logo-only back cover. Do not confuse this machine with the SRVO Complete bench, which unfolds rather than assembling.
