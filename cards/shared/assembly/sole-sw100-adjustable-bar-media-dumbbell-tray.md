**Step 5:**

1. Attach the Adjustable bar (9) to the Connect Plate (8), using three 45mm Washers (19), one M10x100mm Bolt (14), two O10mm Washers (20) and M10 Nylon Nuts (22).
2. Attach the Media Tray (10) to the Adjustable bar (9), using one M10x80mm Bolt (15), two O10mm Washers (20) and M10 Nylon Nuts (22).

Note: **COMPLETELY TIGHTEN THE HARDWARE AT THIS TIME.** Everything left loose since step 1 is tightened here.

**Step 6:** Attach the Dumbbell Tray to the Dumbbell Platforms (6), using four M6x75mm Bolts (25), four O6mm Washers (26) and four M6 Nylon Nuts (24).

**Washer reading, ruled.** Step 5(1) prints "three O10mm Washers (45)", but the kit holds no item 45: item 19 is the 45mm Washer and the kit holds exactly three of them, and the step-5 drawing calls out three washers (19) at the bar joint. Both readings are transcribed as printed; the ruling is the drawing with the pack list: fit the three **45mm Washers (19)**.
