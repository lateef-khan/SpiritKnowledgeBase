1. Use the Phillips head screwdriver (114) to release **four M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screws (99)** and unplug the **computer cable (44)**, the **2100 mm hand-pulse wire, coiled (133)** and the **2100 mm switch cable (upper) (148)**, then replace the **console assembly (19)**.
2. Reverse the step to refit the console assembly (19).

Six books - XBR25 2016, XBR55 2016, XBR55ENT, XBR95 2016, XBR55 2023, XBR95 2023 - print this word for word, part numbers included.

**Three cables, not two.** The 2020 CR800 recumbent console has the computer cable and the hand-pulse cables only; the XBR consoles also carry the upper switch cable (148) for the resistance buttons on the seat handlebar. That cable and the coiled hand-pulse wire are also what the rear-shroud procedure unplugs at the other end.
