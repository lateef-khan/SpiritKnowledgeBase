1. Remove the rear cover screws (the 2022 book: "remove the bolts").
2. Push the rear cover (the 2022 book: "extend the arrow direction to push the rear cover").
3. Remove the fixing screws (the 2022 book: "remove the bolts").
4. Unplug the cables.
5. Remove the console ("electronic watch" in the V1.0 book, "take off the console" in the 2022 book).

Five captions in each book, no counts, no sizes, no refit step. The console's back cover slides off a buckle, as
the installation chapter shows. Replacing the board inside the console is a separate procedure in each book:
"console core" in the V1.0 book and "console board" in the 2022 book.
