1. **Position the shipping carton so the heavy end is at the bottom.**
2. Cut the straps, then lift the box over the unit and unpack. Remove all parts from the
   carton and the foam inserts and verify that all parts are included.
3. Locate the hardware package. The hardware is separated into steps - remove the tools
   first, then the hardware for each step as needed.

**Both books say the hardware is separated into four steps.** The AB900 does print four.
**The JB950 prints five**, so its own sentence undercounts by one.

## The JB950 adds two instructions the AB900 does not

- **"Always have assistance when lifting main frame."** Printed as item 4 of
  pre-assembly and repeated in bold on the next page. The AB900 gives no such warning.
- **"Use the large foam packaging to prop the bike frame off of the ground to allow
  assembly of the front, then rear stabilizers."** Printed twice in pre-assembly and once
  more inside assembly step one. So **do not throw the foam away** until the stabilizers
  are on.

## If something is missing

Both books say to inspect the packing material and the box for overlooked items, then
call the local Spirit Fitness service department or the distributor who sold the product,
with the serial number ready. The back of each manual lists international offices.

## The AB950 unpacks differently

The 2024 AB950 replaced this whole page with three short lines about clearing the floor
and keeping the packing until assembly is finished. See
[that card](../../ab950-2024/assembly/ab950-2024-assembly-pre-assembly-clear-the-floor-first.md).
