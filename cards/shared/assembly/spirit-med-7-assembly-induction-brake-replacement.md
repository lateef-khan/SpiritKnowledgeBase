**Two screws on each side of the brake, and on refit the brake is pushed forward to the end before they are tightened.** The XR898 and CU800 brakes come off four 1/4" x 3/4" bolts on an 11 mm wrench; not this machine.

1. Refer to the shroud procedure to remove the front shroud (7.0R: 6.8) or the side case (7.0U: 6.8).
2. Refer to 6.10.1 (7.0U: 6.9.1) to remove the **eye bolt and tension spring**.
3. Refer to 6.10.2 (7.0U: 6.9.2) to remove the **drive belt**.
4. Refer to 6.10.3 (7.0U: 6.9.3) to remove the **idler wheel assembly**.
5. As shown in figure 6.10.9 (7.0U: 6.9.9), **loosen the 2 screws on both sides of the induction brake** to remove it.
6. Refit: as shown in figure 6.10.10 (7.0U: 6.9.10), **push the induction brake forward to the end** and follow steps 1 to 5 in reverse order.

No screw size is printed, and the books do not say to unplug the brake coil harness - the troubleshooting chapter names it as "the cable that connects control board and coil on the brake", so disconnect it before the brake comes out. The parts list calls the part **Induction Brake (55)** on the 7.0R (its OCR parts page reads "Flywheel") and **Induction Brake (122)** on the 7.0U. The magnet for the angle sensor sits on the brake shaft with a 3 mm gap; that is the sensor troubleshooting's figure, not this card's.
