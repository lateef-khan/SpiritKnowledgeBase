**There is no hardware picture panel in this manual.** Each step opens with a
sentence listing what its bag should contain, and that sentence is the whole record.

## Step 1 - rear rail

- four **3-3/4" x 3/8"** bolts
- four curved washers
- two **1/4" x 3/8"** bolts
- two flat washers
- six **3/4" x 1/8"** Phillips head screws

## Step 2 - console mast

- one **2-3/4" x 3/8"** bolt
- one lock washer
- two **1" x 3/8"** bolts
- two curved washers
- four **1/2" x 1/4"** Phillips head screws

## Step 3 - handle bars

- two **5/8" x 3/8"** bolts
- two large flat washers
- two large wave washers
- four self tapping Phillips head screws

## Step 4 - foot pads and connecting arms

- two **1-1/4" x 3/8"** bolts
- two flat washers
- two Nyloc nuts
- four **5/8"** machine screws (long)
- four **3/8"** machine screws (short)
- two self tapping Phillips head screws

## Five places where the list and the step disagree

- **Step 1 lists "two 1/4" x 3/8" bolts" and its job 2 puts washers on "the 2-1/4"
  bolts".** The same two bolts are 1/4" in the list and 2-1/4" in the job. The
  step-2 list has a genuine 2-3/4" bolt, so the longer reading is the plausible one -
  but the manual prints both and this card will not choose for you.
- **Step 1's six Phillips head screws are 3/4" x 1/8" in the list and 3/4" x 1/4"
  in its job 4.** Both sizes are printed on the same page for the same six screws.
- **Step 2 lists "one lock washer" and its job 5 puts "the flat washer" on the 2-3/4"
  bolt.** One washer, two names.
- **Step 3 lists four self tapping screws and its job 5 fits the covers with six.**
  Two short.
- **Step 4 lists two self tapping screws and its job 7 needs one per side** - that
  closes - **but its job 10 and job 11 fit locking pins and, on the XE550, a rear
  shroud with seven Phillips head screws that appear in no list.** The XE550's seven
  shroud screws are in no hardware sentence in the book.

## Nothing here is an item number

No part in this manual carries a number, so a number quoted from an XE100/XE200/XE300
or XE395 manual means nothing on these machines and must not be ordered against them.
The three assembly drawings label their parts by name only. See
[the drawings card](spirit-ce-assembly-exploded-view-and-parts-list.md).
