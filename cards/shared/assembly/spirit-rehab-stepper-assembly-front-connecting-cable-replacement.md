**Cable tension 40 to 50 lb**, and the screw goes back to the length you measured before you took it apart. Both
books print those two figures.

**Removal (7.5S: 6.5.1).** As shown in figure 6.5.1, **record the length of the screw first** and remove the nut.
As shown in figures 6.5.2 and 6.5.3, remove the screw to remove the front connecting cable.

**Refit (7.5S: 6.5.2).** Adjust the tension of the front connecting cable to the length of the screw on the front
side of the machine. As shown in figures 6.5.4 and 6.5.5, tighten the fixing screws. As shown in figure 6.5.6,
adjust the nut so that the tension of the front connecting cable reaches **40 to 50 lb**.

**The 8.5S book (6-5, steps 1 and 3) prints the same procedure in fewer words**: note the screw length, remove
the nut and bolt to detach the steel cable; adjust the tension of the front steel cable to match the screw length
measured before disassembly, tighten the idle wheel screw, and adjust the nut to set the tension to 40 to 50 lb.
**Its page numbers the steps 1, 3, 3, 4 - there is no step 2 and figures 6.5.2, 6.5.3 and 6.5.6 are never
printed.** Nothing is missing from the procedure itself.

Neither book says how the tension is measured. The rear cable is a different job with a winding pattern and its
own card.
