All three cartons print the same panel, and nothing else:

- 8mm Allen Wrench
- 2 Phillips Screwdrivers

The CT900 parts list carries the same three tools as items **110 L Allen Wrench**,
**111 Phillips Head Screw Driver** and **112 Short Phillips Head Screw Driver**, one
of each. The parts list gives the Allen wrench no size; the panel above is the only
place the 8mm is printed.

## The 8mm wrench is named here, unlike on the CT800 platform

The belt chapters of the CT900 and CT900ENT manuals both say the tread-belt tension
and tracking bolts are turned with "an 8mm Allen wrench **provided in the parts
package**". On these two machines that wrench is on the tools panel, so the carton
and the belt chapter agree. On the eight CT800 and CT850 manuals the same sentence
appears and the wrench is **not** on their tools panel - see the linked CT800 tools
card before you carry that defect across.

## The CTSBS900 contradicts itself on the facing page

The CTSBS900 prints a **second panel, also headed TOOLS**, on p. 9. It lists the
hardware bag a-s, and the tools in it are:

- l. Open-end wrench 17mm
- m. L-shaped hex wrench + cross screwdriver 5mm
- n. T-shaped wrench 6mm
- o. T-shaped wrench 8mm

**No 8mm Allen wrench and no Phillips screwdriver appear in that list**, and no
17mm, 6mm or 5mm tool appears on the p. 8 panel. The manual never reconciles the two
pages. Its six assembly steps drive hex socket cap screws and cross-head screws,
which matches the p. 9 list; the word "Phillips" appears nowhere in the CTSBS900
manual outside the p. 8 panel, and "8mm Allen" nowhere outside it either. Treat the
p. 9 list as the one that matches the CTSBS900 build, and expect the carton to hold
it.

## Two tools the carton does not contain

- The machine-care pages of the CT900 and CTSBS900 say "it may be necessary to use a
  **larger wrench than the one provided**" to tighten assembly bolts sufficiently.
- The same pages send you to a **14mm wrench (or adjustable wrench)** to adjust the
  levellers. No manual in this family packs one.
