Both printings list the same nine lines.

## The list and the steps disagree about the console mast

The carton list names a **Console Mast**, but no assembly step fits one: the console
mounts to the handlebar with a clamp and a thumb screw. The list also omits the
**rear stabilizer cover** that step two installs, and the **handlebars**, which the
labelled diagram names. Treat the panel as a rough count, not a checklist.
