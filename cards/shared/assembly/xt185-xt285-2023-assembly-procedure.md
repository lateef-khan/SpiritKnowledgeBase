Item numbers in brackets are from the exploded view. Do the steps in order and
leave everything finger-tight until the end.

1. Take the treadmill deck out of the box and place it on level and clean
   ground. Remove the Velcro shipping strap as the warning on this step
   describes - the deck can spring open.
2. **You may need a second person to hold the Uprights (4 and 5) for this step.**
   Insert the Left Upright (5) through the Frame Base Cover (L) (42) and the
   Right Upright (4) through the Frame Base Cover (R) (43). Connect the Computer
   Cable (Middle) (38) to the Computer Cable (Lower) (39).
3. Insert the Right and Left Uprights (4) and (5) into the Frame Base (2) and
   use the Combination M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (96) to tighten
   **8 pcs of 5/16" x 15mm Button Head Socket Bolts (93)**.
4. Install the Frame Base Covers (L) and (R), (42) and (43), on the Frame Base
   (2) and secure with **6 pcs of 5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws (153)** using the
   Phillips Head Screw Driver (122).
5. Connect the Computer Cable (Middle) (38) and the Computer Cable (Upper) (37).
6. Insert the Console Assembly (40) into the Right and Left Uprights (4) and (5)
   and secure with **4 pcs of 5/16" x 15mm Button Head Socket Bolts (93)**, with
   **2 pcs of Ø5/16" x 19 x 1.5T Curved Washers (132)** and **2 pcs of 5/16" x 1"
   Button Head Socket Bolts (70)**, using the M6 L Allen Wrench (97).

**NOTE: tighten all screws only after all components have been assembled.**

Then check the belt lubrication before the first use - see the separate card.

The XT385-2023 and XT485-2023 have a five-step assembly with a handrail support
and a tablet holder, and the XT685-2023 a four-step one; none of the item
numbers carry across.
