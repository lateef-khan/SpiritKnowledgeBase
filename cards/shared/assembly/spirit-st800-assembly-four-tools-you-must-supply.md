All three ST800 manuals print the same TOOLS REQUIRED panel, as four pictures with four
labels and no sizes:

- **Ratchet wrench and socket**
- **Adjustable wrench**
- **Rubber mallet**
- **Hex key wrench set**

**None of them is in the carton.** No tool appears in any of the three parts lists, and
the panel is headed *required*, not *included*.

**The rubber mallet is the one that catches people out.** It is not needed for any bolt;
it is for seating the plastic tube guides, foot covers and plugs that these frames use in
quantity - the functional trainer alone lists four plastic tube guides, four foot covers
and ten plugs.

**On the functional trainer the panel does double duty**: it is headed TOOLS REQUIRED /
HARDWARE GUIDE and adds four fastener drawings - button head cap screw, socket head cap
screw, flat head cap screw and hex head bolt - so a builder can tell them apart in the
tube holes.

**Two of the cartons hold gloves.** The bench parts list counts one pair (item 24) and the
functional trainer counts five across two rows (items 32 and 33). The dumbbell rack has
none.

The rest of the Spirit strength range names three different tools, or says the tools are
in the carton. See
[the CSS tools card](spirit-strength-assembly-three-tools-you-must-supply.md) and
[the tools-in-the-carton card](spirit-strength-assembly-tools-are-in-the-carton.md).
