1. Take the front shrouds (29) (30) and the **drive belt (54)** off (the XBR95 2023 book also lists the drive pulley procedure among the prerequisites).
2. Unplug the harnesses on the **induction brake (55)** and use an **11 mm wrench** to release **4 pcs of 1/4" x 3/4" hex head bolts (66)** together with **4 pcs of 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers (72)** and **Ø1/4" spring washers (80)** on the induction brake (55). The induction brake can be taken off as soon as the bolts and washers are released.
3. Reverse the procedure to refit.

The 2020 CR800 and CR800ENT books print the identical bolts and washers for the part they call the **generator flywheel (55)** - that is the linked card. The XBR95 has a hybrid generator brake; the 2009 XR898 has a plain induction brake; the mounting is the same.

After refitting, the belt goes back to 180 +/- 10 Hz (the belt and idler wheel card).
