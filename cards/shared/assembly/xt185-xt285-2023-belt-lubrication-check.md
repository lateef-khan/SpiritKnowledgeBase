Once the treadmill is fully assembled, check the belt lubrication **before
initial use**:

1. Make sure the machine is off and the **power cord is unplugged**, to minimise
   the risk of injury.
2. Use one hand to hold the treadmill belt up just enough that your other hand
   can reach between the belt and the deck. **Verify that lubrication is
   present.**

If lubrication is present, continue with normal use. If it is not, lubricate the
belt with approved treadmill belt lubricant, following the deck lubrication
procedure the manual prints on page 41.

This note is printed only in the XT185-2023 and XT285-2023 manuals. The
XT385-2023, XT485-2023 and XT685-2023 manuals give no pre-use lubrication check
in their assembly sections.
