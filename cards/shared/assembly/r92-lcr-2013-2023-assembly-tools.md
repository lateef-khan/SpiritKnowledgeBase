| Item | Tool |
|---|---|
| #112 | 12/14mm Wrench |
| #114 | Phillips Head Screwdriver |
| #132 | 14/15mm Wrench |

**The R92 2019 ships the same three tools** and is covered by its own card.

Two recumbents of the same family ship a different set and are **not** covered here:

- **R92 2011** adds a fourth tool, #115 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver.
- **LCR 2011** prints #132 as a *13/15mm* wrench in its tool list while every instruction in the same manual calls #132 the 14/15mm wrench.

**Tool item numbers are not shared between models.** The same `#` is a different part on another Sole bike, so use the list printed in your own machine's manual. The B94 and LCB uprights of the same years ship #92, #93 and #100 instead.
