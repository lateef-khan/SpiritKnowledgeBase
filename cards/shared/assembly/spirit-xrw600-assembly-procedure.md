Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. Hardware quantities are on
[the hardware card](spirit-xrw600-assembly-hardware-by-step.md). **Every step opens with
"Gather HARDWARE FOR STEP n"** and then has exactly one instruction.

**The 2019 and 2021 printings are the same build.** Their assembly chapters agree to
99.6% at word level and their hardware tables are identical row for row.

## Before you start

1. Cut the straps, then lift the box over the unit and unpack.
2. Remove all parts and inspect for damage or missing parts; if anything is damaged or
   missing, contact your dealer immediately.
3. Locate the hardware package. **Remove the tools first**, then take out the hardware
   for each step as you reach it.

## Step one - front stabiliser

Use the **Wrench (130)** to tighten **3 Button Head Socket Bolts (120)** together with
**3 Flat Washers (109)** to secure the **Main Frame (1)** and the **Front Stabilizer
(2)** together.

*Three, not four. The CRW800 uses four here and adds spring washers.*

## Step two - aluminium track to the rear stabiliser

Use the **Allen Wrench (126)** to tighten **4 Button Head Socket Bolts (101)** through
the **Aluminum Track (51)**, securing the track and the **Rear Stabilizer (3)**
together.

## Step three - the other end of the track to the folding end assembly

Attach the other end of the **Aluminum Track (51)** to the **Folding End Assembly (6)**
of the Main Frame (1), and use the **Allen Wrench (130)** to tighten **6 Socket Head
Cap Bolts (123)** together with **6 Split Washers (116)**.

*Step one calls item 130 a plain wrench and this step calls it an Allen wrench. See
[the tools card](spirit-xrw600-assembly-tools-included.md).*

## Step four - pedals and seat

1. Use the **Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (127)** to
   tighten **6 Button Head Socket Bolts (129)** and **6 Flat Washers (121)**, installing
   the **Left and Right Pedals (4, 5)** on the **Main Frame (1)**.
2. Use the same tool to tighten **4 Phillips Head Screws (124)** together with **4
   Split Washers (125)**, installing the **Seat (49)** onto the **Seat Attaching Board
   (16)**.

**Neither the seat nor the seat attaching board is on the carton panel.** See
[the parts card](spirit-xrw600-assembly-parts-included.md).

## The build ends here

**No step fits a leveller**, although the machine diagram labels LEVELERS at both ends
and the maintenance chapter says there are two under the rear stabiliser. **No step
plugs the machine in**, although the carton holds a power cord and neither book prints
a first power-on procedure. The folding and moving procedures are printed twenty pages
later, between the programs and the heart rate chapter, not at the end of the build -
see [that card](spirit-xrw600-assembly-folding-and-moving.md).
