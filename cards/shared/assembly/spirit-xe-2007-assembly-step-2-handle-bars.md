**The manual heads two of these panels "XE100 ONLY" and "XE200 ONLY".** They are not
interchangeable and the parts are not the same size.

## XE100 only

1. Install the two **17m/m Wave Washers (83)** onto the left and right side of the
   handle bar axle.
2. Slide the **Left (10)** and **Right (11)** Handle Bars onto the matching side.
   Each carries a small sticker marked L or R.
3. Put the two **5/16" x 23mm Flat Washers** onto the two **5/16" x 15m/m Hex Head
   Bolts** and tighten them into the threaded holes in the ends of the axle.

Three items, and no wiring: **the XE100 handle bars carry no cable.**

## XE200 only

1. Install the two **28m/m Wave Washers (81)** onto the left and right side of the
   axle.
2. Slide the Left (10) and Right (11) Handle Bars on, L and R by the sticker.
3. Put the two **3/8 x 30mm Flat Washers (97)** onto the two **3/8 x 3/4 Hex Head
   Bolts (103)** and tighten them into the ends of the axle.
4. Connect the two wires together **(124, 125)** and store the excess, plastic
   connectors included, back in the console mast. Put the **rubber grommet (123)**
   over the wire and snap it into the hole in the console mast.

## XE300

Items 1 to 4 are the XE200's four items word for word, with the same item numbers,
and then one more:

5. Install the **Front Handle Bar Covers (54-1 left, 55-1 right)** and **Rear Handle
   Bar Covers (54 left, 55 right)** over the axle connections with the six
   **3.5x10m/m Self Tapping Screws (87)**.

**The XE300 fits its handle bar covers here; the XE100 and XE200 fit theirs in step
4.** That is why the XE300's step 2 bag carries eight of item 87 and the others carry
none.

## What must not be carried across

| | Wave washer | Bolt | Flat washer | Handle bar wiring |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| XE100 | 17m/m, item 83 | 5/16" x 15m/m | 5/16" x 23mm | none |
| XE200 | 28m/m, item 81 | 3/8 x 3/4, item 103 | 3/8 x 30mm, item 97 | items 124, 125, grommet 123 |
| XE300 | 28m/m, item 81 | 3/8 x 3/4, item 103 | 3/8 x 30mm, item 97 | items 124, 125, grommet 123 |

**The XE100 is the odd machine of the three.** Everything in its handle bar joint is
one size down and it has no wire to join.

## Item 81 is printed at two sizes in this book

Every step above calls item 81 a **28m/m** wave washer. Its picture in all three
Assembly Pack Check Lists is captioned **25**. Both are printed; quote the item
number. See [the hardware card](spirit-xe-2007-assembly-hardware-checklist.md).
