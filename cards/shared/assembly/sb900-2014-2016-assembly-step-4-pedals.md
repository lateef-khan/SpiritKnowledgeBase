This step needs no hardware from the assembly pack.

1. Install the Left (25L) and Right (25R) pedals to the crank arms. **The left pedal has a reverse threaded bolt and needs to be tightened** ... [see below] ... stamped into the end of the bolt.

**The middle of this instruction is missing from the converted text of this manual.** What survives is "Please note that the Left pedal has a reverse threaded bolt and needs to be tightened" followed by a gap, then "stamped into the end of the bolt." No page scan is available for this manual, so the missing words cannot be recovered from the source.

The SB900 2019 and 2020 manuals print the same instruction complete: "...needs to be tightened **in a counterclockwise direction. The pedals are identified by either an R or L**  stamped into the end of the bolt." That is the same sentence shape, but it is from a different year's manual and is quoted here only as a pointer, not as a statement about the 2014 or 2016 machine.

**What is certain from this manual: the left pedal is reverse threaded.** Do not force it clockwise.
