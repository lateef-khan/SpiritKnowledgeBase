1. Replace the **M5 screw (2 pcs)** attached to the **connecting rod cover** and replace the cover (figure 6.10.1).
2. Use the tool to open the **sun's wrench's tenon** (the CE1000ENT book prints "tension"), then remove the **Sun**
   and the **nuts** (figures 6.10.2 to 6.10.6), and see figures 6.9.1 to 6.9.3 to replace the **shaft connecting
   rod**.

That is the whole section. "Sun" and "tenon" are the book's words for the crank-side fitting and its locking tab;
the book names no tool and no size. Section 6.11 removes this rod again on the way to the belt.
