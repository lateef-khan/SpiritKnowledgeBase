tabulating it. Item numbers are the assembly-drawing numbers.

**Both manuals draw the same eighteen hardware pictures and the same four tools.**

## One quantity changed from the older printings, and only one

Compared row by row against the XBR55 2019 and 2021, XBR55ENT and XBR95 2016, 2018 and
2021 tables: **item 99 goes from four to seven.** Every other item number, size and
quantity is unchanged across ten years of printings.

The three extra screws are the ones step two uses to fit the **Console Holder (153)** to
the console assembly - the tablet holder the older machines do not have. Four hold the
console to its mounting plate, three hold the holder to the console.

See [the older hardware card](spirit-xbr-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## The measurements are written differently, not changed

The older tables write item 84 as **Ø3/8" × Ø25 × 2T** and item 67 as
**3/8"XUNC16X4"**; the 2023 pictures write **3/8" x 25 x 2T** and **3/8" x 4"**. The
diameter symbol and the thread pitch are dropped from the label, not from the part.

## The XBR25 2023 kit is a different kit

It has **no item 70, 76 or 91** - no gas cylinder - and it adds **item 142**, a 3/8" x
4-1/4" bolt, with four extra washers and four nyloc nuts in step one. Its item 99 stays
at four. See [that card](spirit-xbr25-assembly-hardware-kit.md).
