1. Take the **rear shrouds (35, 36)** off.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver on **2 pcs of M6 x 15 screws (98)** and take the **seat stop axles (11)** off. Pull up the **release lever (40)** (2009 book: the seat front/aft adjustment lever (167)) to take the **seat carriage (4)** off.
3. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to release **four M6 x 10L flat Phillips head screws (161)**, **4 pcs of 1/4" x 16 x 1.0T flat washers** and **four sleeves (162)**. Then use a **13 mm open-end wrench** to release **four M8 x 7T nyloc nuts (88)** and **four Ø8 x Ø18 x 3T knurled lock washers (79)**, and take the **seat wheel adjustment plates (9L, 9R)** off.
4. Use an **M4 Allen wrench** and a **10 mm open-end wrench** to release the **M5 x 45 mm socket head cap bolt (171)** and the **M5 x 5T nyloc nut (173)**.
5. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to release **two M5 x 25 mm flat head socket screws (169)**, then pull out the **lever anchor (168)** to take the **seat front/aft adjustment lever (167)** apart.
6. Use an **M5 Allen wrench** and an **11 mm open-end wrench** to take off the **M6 x 38 mm socket head cap bolt (93)**, the **1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washer (72)** and the **M6 nyloc nut (129)**, and take the **seat position latch (12)** off.
7. To refit the lever (167): join it to the lever anchor (168) with the two M5 x 25 mm flat head socket screws (169); place the **spring (104)** in the seat position latch (12) and secure with the M5 x 45 mm socket head cap bolt (171) passing, from top to bottom, through the latch (12), the spring (104), the **Ø15 x 6 x 4T nylon washer (170)**, the lever (167) and the **Ø3/16" x 10 x 1T flat washer (172)**, then tighten with the M5 x 5T nyloc nut (173).
8. *(XBR books)* Use the 13 mm open-end wrench to lightly tighten the 4 pcs of M8 x 7T nyloc nuts, the 4 knurled lock washers (79) and the seat wheel adjustment plates (9L, 9R) on the seat carriage (4).
9. Install the completed seat carriage (4) onto the **aluminium track (14)** and set the seat wheel adjustment plates (9L, 9R) with the 11 and 13 mm wrenches: at the **front left**, turn the 11 mm wrench **counterclockwise** first, then tighten the M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88) with the 13 mm wrench; at the **front right**, turn the 11 mm wrench **clockwise** and tighten the M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88); then at the rear, turn the hex bolt **counterclockwise on the right** and **clockwise on the left**.
10. To test the smoothness: if the seat carriage is too tight, turn the four screws back until the movement is acceptable. Tighten the four **sleeves (163)** with the four 1/4" x 16 x 1.0T flat washers and the M6 x 10L flat Phillips head screws (161). The seat stop axle (11) and the rear shrouds (35, 36) can then go back.

**Printing defects to know about.**

- The sleeves are numbered **(162)** in step 3 and **(163)** in step 10, in every one of these books.
- The XBR25, XBR55 2016 and XBR55ENT books print steps 1 to 3 twice - once as 1-3 and again as 4-6 - before continuing; it is one pass, not two.
- The XBR95 2016 book prints a **10 mm** wrench for the latch bolt (93) in step 6 where the other six books print 11 mm.
- The 2009 XR898 book lists the four 1/4" x 16 x 1.0T flat washers only at refit (step 10); the XBR books list them in step 3 as well.

**The 2020 CR800 and CR800ENT version** starts from the aluminium axle end cap (135) instead of the rear shrouds and uses Ø7 x Ø15 x 1.5T washers (162) instead of 1/4" x 16 x 1.0T; it is the linked card. The wheel-setting rule in step 9 is what the 2008 XBR dealer manual prints as its "tighten seat carriage" procedure.

**The Spirit Medical 4.0R (FR800-SB022-03 book) prints steps 1 to 7 of this procedure** - rear shrouds first, the two M6 x 15 screws on the seat stop axles, the four M6 x 10L flat Phillips screws with **4 pcs of 1/4" x 16 x 1.0T flat washers** and four sleeves (the XBR form of step 3), the 13 mm wrench on the four M8 x 7T nyloc nuts and knurled lock washers, the M4 Allen wrench and 10 mm wrench on the M5 x 45 mm bolt, the two M5 x 25 mm flat head socket screws and the lever anchor, the M5 Allen wrench and 11 mm wrench on the M6 x 38 mm latch bolt, and the top-to-bottom refit order of the lever bolt through the latch, the spring, the Ø15 x 6 x 4T nylon washer, the lever and the Ø3/16" x 10 x 1T flat washer, tightened with the M5 x 5T nyloc nut. **It stops there: the wheel-setting steps 8 to 10 (front left counterclockwise, front right clockwise) are not printed in that book**, whose troubleshooting chapter has no seat-sway answer either. Its step 4 prints "M5 x 5T flat washer and M6 nyloc nut" where the other books print the M5 x 5T nyloc nut (173) - a slip; the parts list carries item 173 as the M5 x 5T nyloc nut. The book heads the section "Sear Carriage", as the XBR95 2016 book does.
