Hardware: **#82** 3/8" Nut (4 pcs), **#81** 3/8" x 3-1/2" Button Head Socket Bolt (4 pcs), **#83** 3/8" x 23mm Curved Washer (4 pcs).

1. Install the front and rear stabilizers with four Button Head Socket Bolts (81), four Curved Washers (83) and four Nuts (82). **The front and rear stabilizer are different. Be sure to assemble the stabilizer with the wheels onto the front of the bike.**

**Damaged source text in two of these manuals.** In the SB900 2014/2016 and SB900 2019 PDFs a line is missing from the converted text of this instruction, which reads only "The front and rear stabilizer are ... the bike." The full sentence quoted above is taken from the SB900 2020 manual, where the same instruction is printed complete. Treat the middle of that sentence as unverified for the 2014, 2016 and 2019 machines.

**The 2021 machine changed #81 to a Carriage Bolt.**
