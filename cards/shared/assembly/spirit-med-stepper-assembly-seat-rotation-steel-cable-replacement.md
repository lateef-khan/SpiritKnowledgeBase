**This procedure is printed in the 2025 books only.** The 2021 7.5S book (RS9600-SS021-01) ends its steel-cable chapter at the fore/aft cable and has no 6.14.

**6.14.1 Replace the steel cable for the seat rotation adjustment.** As shown in figure 6.14.1, cut the cable tie. As shown in figures 6.14.2 and 6.14.3, **rotate the seat to an angle that makes it easier to remove the cable end and the spring**. As shown in figure 6.14.4, adjust the **adjustment screw beneath the release handle** to the position that allows the cable to be removed, then remove and replace the cable.

**6.14.2** Refit by following 6.14.1 in reverse order.

No cable length or screw size is printed. This cable has a spring on its head, like the fore/aft cable and unlike the seat back cable. The swivel latches every 45 degrees on the rear handle - the seat-adjustment card. The 7.0R medical recumbent bike prints the same page word for word for its own swivel seat; that is a bike card.

**The 7.5S -03 book's contents page lists "7.3 Sway of Swivel Seat" and its body does not print it** - its 7.3 is Abnormal SPM Reading. The 7.0S -02 book prints the swivel-seat sway answer (an 11 mm and a 13 mm wrench on the seat wheel adjustment plates) as its 7.3; that adjustment is the maintenance section's.
