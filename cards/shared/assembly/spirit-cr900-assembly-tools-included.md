Five tools, printed as a TOOLS INCLUDED list beside the unpacking steps:

- **13/15mm Wrench**
- **17mm Wrench (2)** - two of them
- **Phillips Screwdriver**
- **5mm Allen Wrench**
- **6mm Allen Wrench**

All three printings list the same five. The 2025 manual moves the panel onto its own
PARTS INCLUDED page and heads it TOOLS; the words are unchanged.

## The upright carton is not the same

The CU900, CU900ENT and CU900 2025 cartons hold **four** tools - the same list without
the two 17mm wrenches. Do not carry this list across; see
[the upright tools card](spirit-cu900-assembly-tools-included.md).

## No step names a tool, and no 14mm is in the box

Not one of the four assembly steps says which tool to use. Match the tool to the head:
the build drives M10, M8, M6 and M5 bolts and screws and 3.5mm and 4mm self-tapping
screws.

**There is no 14mm wrench in this carton**, although the levelling sentence in the
maintenance chapter calls for one. The 13/15mm will not fit it; use an adjustable, as
that sentence itself allows. See
[the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).
