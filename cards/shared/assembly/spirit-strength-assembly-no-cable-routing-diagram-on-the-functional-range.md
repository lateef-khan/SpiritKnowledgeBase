**None of these eight manuals has a CABLE ROUTING DIAGRAM page.** Their tables of contents
run straight from Assembly to Maintenance & Care, and the phrase does not appear anywhere
in the book.

**The functional trainer is the surprising one.** It is a two-tower cable machine, and its
manual still prints no routing diagram - and no cable in any of its four step tables
either. The word *cable* appears in that book five times, all of them in the safety and
maintenance chapters ("Inspect all cables and connections prior to use", "Belt / Cables"
in the care schedule, "Paint & Finishes / Cables / Grips" in the warranty). Its cables
arrive routed.

The five bench-style CSF units have no cable at all - no weight stack, no pulley, nothing
to route.

**The i-Strength machines route their cable inside a step instead.** The low row's step
four drawing carries the callout *"Remove the pulley to route the cable between the pulley
grooves and cable retaining post"*, which is the only routing instruction in either book.
See [that card](spirit-strength-assembly-take-the-pulley-off-to-route-the-cable.md).

Checked three ways before writing this: the table of contents of each book, a
case-insensitive search of the whole text for *rout* and for *cable*, and the printed
pages themselves. The twenty-eight weight-stack machines all do print the diagram - see
[that card](spirit-strength-assembly-cable-routing-diagram-names-two-wires.md).
