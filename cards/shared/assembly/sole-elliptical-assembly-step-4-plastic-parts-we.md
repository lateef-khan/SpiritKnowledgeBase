1. **Outer Connecting Arm Covers (#109 & #110)** matched with the pre-installed inner covers **(#111 & #112)**; each side with **one M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screw (139)** and **two 3.5 x 12mm Sheet Metal Screws (142)** using the Short driver (185).
2. **Sliding Wheel Covers (104)**, each side with **four M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
3. **Stabilizer Covers (117 and 118)** with **two M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
4. **Front Stabilizer Cover (103)** with **two M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
5. **Incline Cover brackets (25)**, bent-tab hole facing rearward, with **four M6 x 10mm Phillips Head Screws (145)**; **Rear incline bar cover (115)** with **two M5 x 15 m/m Screws (139)**.
6. **Rear Stabilizer Cover (116)** with **four M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
7. **After the elliptical power is plugged in, run the incline to position 8** and install the **Incline Rail front cover (113)** up against the middle stabilizer tube with **four M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
