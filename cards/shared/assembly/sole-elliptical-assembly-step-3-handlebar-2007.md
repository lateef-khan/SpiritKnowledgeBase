1. From the hardware pack for step 3 you should have **two 5/8" X 3/8" bolts**, **two large flat washers**, **two large wave washers** and **four self tapping Phillips head screws**. (The E35/E75/E95 book bags four self tapping screws here but fits six covers screws in step 5; follow the fitting step.)
2. **Install the 2 large wave washers onto the handle bar axle**, one on each side.
3. **Slide the left and right handle bars** onto the axle. The stickers mark L and R.
4. **Put the large flat washers onto the two 5/8" X 3/8" bolts** and tighten in the threaded holes in the ends of the axle.
5. **Install the plastic covers over the handle bar/axle connections** with the **six self tapping Phillips head screws**.
