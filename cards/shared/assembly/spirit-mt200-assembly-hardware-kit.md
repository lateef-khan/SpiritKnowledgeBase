The hardware is photographed bagged by step. The panel gives the item number,
the size and the count and no part-type words, so the types below are only what
the assembly steps call them. Both years print the same panel.

## Step 1 - uprights

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 104 | 3/8" x 3-3/4" | 6 |
| 58 | 3/8" x 2T x 4H | 6 |
| 59 | 3/8" x 25 x 2T | 6 |

## Step 2 - console frame, brackets and end caps

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 153 | 3/8" x 3/4" | 6 |
| 58 | 3/8" x 2T x 4H | 6 |
| 59 | 3/8" x 25 x 2T | 6 |
| 108 | M8 x 12L | 6 |
| 73 | M5 x 12L | 4 |

## Step 3 - console

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 110 | M5 x 10L | 2 |
| 112 | M5 x 30L | 2 |

## Step 4 - parallel bar handrails

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 153 | 3/8" x 3/4" | 8 |
| 59 | 3/8" x 25 x 2T | 8 |
| 226 | 3/8" x 2" | 8 |

**Three items are bagged more than once.** Item 58 is bagged twice, six and six.
Item 59 is bagged three times, six, six and eight - twenty in all. Item 153 is
bagged twice, six for the console frame in step two and eight for the handrails
in step four - fourteen in all.

## Do not read these counts off the parts list

The parts list gives whole-machine totals, which are larger than what is bagged
for assembly: item 58 is listed 19 times, item 59 32 times, item 73 31 times and
item 153 18 times. Only the counts in the tables above are the assembly counts.

## The 7.0T uses different numbers for the same job

**Step four is where the two machines part company.** The 7.0T bolts its
handrails on with items **154** and **155**; the MT200 re-uses **153** and
**59** at the same two sizes. Item 226 is the same number on both. The 8.0T uses
a third set again - 213, 215 and 214.

The panel also writes item 58 differently on the two machines: **3/8" x 2T x 4H**
here, **Ø10 x 2T** on the 7.0T. Both manuals' step text calls it a 3/8" split
washer, and the MT200 parts list names it a Ø3/8" x 2.0T Split Washer.
