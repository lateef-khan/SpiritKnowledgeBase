1. First remove the console mast (the 2020 book: "follow the user manual to take off the console mast").
2. Unplug the **round cap** under the hand pulse tube and pull out the hand pulse cables to take the hand pulse
   sensor assembly apart (the 2020 book: "remove the end caps of handle pulses; put out the handle pulse wires
   from under the tube").
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **two Ø3 x 20 tapping screws** securing the hand pulse assembly,
   pull it out and disconnect the cables (the 2020 book: "2 screws (ψ3x20mm)").
4. Reassemble in the reverse order. *Be sure not to crush or damage wiring during the process* / *do not pinch the
   wires.*

Both generations print the same two 3 x 20 screws. Neither service manual repeats how the mast bolts to the
frame - that is the owner's-manual build. The Sole SC200 console-mast card is the same procedure.
