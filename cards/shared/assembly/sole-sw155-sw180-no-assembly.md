**No assembly is printed, and none is missing.** The SW155 and SW180 books cover adjustable dumbbells, not stands: the mechanical content is seating the handle fully into the dumbbell tray base so the locking mechanism engages, plus safety and warranty pages. There is no hardware kit, no tools list and no assembly instruction page.

This absence was proved four ways: a loose-word search for assembl, hardware, bolt, step, install, attach, tighten and wrench returns only finish-damage warranty boilerplate and safety-installation precautions; a spacing-tolerant search finds no split headings; OCR supplements cover every content page (PDF pp.1-9); and the one unsupplemented page (PDF p.10) was rendered and read as a logo-only back cover. Do not confuse these dumbbells with the SW100, which is the stand that does assemble.
