The page after the carton list is a picture of the built machine with labels and no
item numbers. Thirteen labels:

**SPEAKERS**, **CONSOLE**, **CONSOLE FAN**, **SWING ARM**, **CONSOLE MAST**, **PULSE
SENSORS**, **FRONT STABILIZER**, **MAIN FRAME**, **CONNECTING ARM**, **FOOT PEDALS**,
**LEVELERS**, **RAILS**, **REAR STABILIZER**.

## Three labels are not the same on every machine

- **The XE895 2018 has a fourteenth label, WATER BOTTLE HOLDER**, between FRONT
  STABILIZER and MAIN FRAME. It is the only diagram on this card that points at one,
  and the holder is already fitted in the picture - no step in the XE895 build
  installs it and no line of its carton list counts it.
- **The XE195 2021 has no SPEAKERS label** - twelve labels, not thirteen. **The XE195
  2019 does have it**, and its console page prints "Integrated Speakers for MP3
  Player" as well. The speakers were dropped between the 2019 and the 2021 printing of
  the same machine; do not read the 2021 book's twelve labels back onto a 2019 unit.
  Every other machine on this card carries the label.
- **The pulse label is PULSE GRIPS on eight books and PULSE SENSORS on three.** All
  six 2018 and 2019 printings say PULSE GRIPS, and so do the XE395ENT and the
  **February 2021 XE795** printing; the XE195 2021, XE295 2021, XG400 2021 and the
  **November 2021 XE795** printing say PULSE SENSORS. **The word changed with the
  printing, not with the part.**

**That one word is the only difference between the two XE795 2021 printings anywhere
in the assembly chapter.** Everything else - the unpacking list, the carton list, all
six steps and all six hardware panels - is word for word identical between February
and November. They are one machine, `xe795-2021`, and the November printing's
addition is a cleaning and sanitizing section elsewhere in the book, not anything
about the build. **The XE795 2018 printing prints the same eight-step-page chapter,
also with PULSE GRIPS.**

## No item numbers here

These labels are names only. The XE395ENT is the only machine on this card with a
numbered parts list, at pp. 51-55; every other book here, including all six 2018 and
2019 printings, has neither a parts list nor an exploded view. See
[the exploded view card](spirit-ce-assembly-exploded-view-and-parts-list.md).

## The XG400 diagram is the elliptical diagram

The XG400 uses the same thirteen labels for a machine its own cover calls an
**E-Glide Trainer**. On the 2021 printing its pulse label is printed with a stray
backtick - `PULSE SENSORS\`` - in the book; the 2019 printing has the clean label
**PULSE GRIPS**. Its hand brake lever, brake cable and
water bottle holder are on no label.

## The 2023 machines use a different diagram entirely

The XE395 2023 and XE795 2023 replace this with a lettered A to N list. See
[that card](spirit-xe-assembly-parts-diagram-labels-2023.md).
