Chapter 4 is a two-page routing drawing with a fourteen-row legend, printed in Chinese with an English column:

| # | Chinese | English (as printed) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 电源线 | power line |
| 2 | 船型开关 | rocker switch |
| 3 | 电源输入线 | power input line |
| 4 | 电源板 | power panel |
| 5 | 电源输出线 | power output line |
| 6 | 电控 | controller |
| 7 | 电机 | electrical machinery (the motor) |
| 8 | 散热电阻 | heat dissipation resistance (the cement resistor) |
| 9 | 风扇 | fan |
| 10 | 风扇转接线 | fan adapter cable |
| 11 | 灯带 | light strip |
| 12 | 通讯线下段 | lower section of the communication line (printed "offline communication section") |
| 13 | 通讯线中段 | middle section of the communication line |
| 14 | 显示屏 | display screen |

The second page repeats the numbers over a photograph of the open box so each cable can be followed; it carries
no words. **The English column is a machine translation**: 7 is the motor, 8 is the braking resistor, and 12 is
the lower section of the communication line, not an "offline" one.

The drawing gives no connector pin-outs, wire colours or lengths - those belong to the specs cards where the
book prints them. The CSS and CSD owner's manuals' cable routing diagram - two wires, a dry reed pipe connection
wire and a power line - is a different drawing for the selectorized machines and has its own card.

The "Digital Resistance Module" is the i-Strength line's motorised resistance box, part 72 in the CSI owner's manuals; the screw numbers are the call-outs on the manual's exploded drawings.
