1. Follow step 2-1: remove the left and right **end cap covers**.
2. Remove the screws fixing the **back cover** and remove the back cover.
3. **Loosen the nut that adjusts the belt tightness (cannot be removed).**
4. Use a **#13 wrench** to remove the screws fixing the motor and take out the motor.

That is chapter 5 in both books. Unlike the Dyaco service manuals, nothing is said about the motor
wires, a belt tension figure, or re-fitting. The M+/M- motor leads go to the brake board, not the
driver board - see the wiring cards.
