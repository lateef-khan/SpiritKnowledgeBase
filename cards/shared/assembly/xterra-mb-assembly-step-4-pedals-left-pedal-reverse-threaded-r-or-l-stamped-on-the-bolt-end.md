| Book | Text |
|---|---|
| Indoor cycles (2014 and 2018) | Install the Left (104L) and Right (104R) pedals to the crank arms. **Please note that the left pedal has a reverse threaded bolt and needs to be tightened in a counterclockwise direction.** The pedals are identified by either an R or L stamped into the end of the bolt. |
| Belt-drive cycle (2018) | Attach the left pedal (1L) to the left crank (33). Attach the right pedal (1R) to the right crank (36). **Note: the right pedal should be threaded on clockwise and the left pedal on counter-clockwise.** CHECK ALL BOLTS AND NUTS ARE TIGHTENED BEFORE USING THE MACHINE. |

No torque figure in any of the three books. The "Before Using" page of the 2018 books repeats "make sure to tighten both pedals securely to the pedal crank arms as instructed", and the maintenance schedule checks the pedals before each use.
