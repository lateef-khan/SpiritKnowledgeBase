**Set the new incline motor to 165 mm before you fit it.** The TRX2500-5500 books say 195 mm for their
motor; do not mix the two.

**TR260 GT75A (9-8)**:

1. Take off the motor cover, then fold the treadmill.
2. Unplug the incline motor wires and unmount the incline motor.
3. Adjust the spare incline motor **to lower (165 mm)** - the drawing dimensions 165 across the motor.
4. Install the incline motor with a **14 mm open-end wrench and an M8 L-Allen wrench**.
5. Plug the incline motor wires to the controller: **Red to UP, White to COM, Black to DOWN** (the colour
   key is a callout in the drawing, not in the text layer).

**TRX1400 T3 (12-8)**:

1. First remove the motor cover and put the treadmill in the folded position, then remove the old
   incline motor.
2. **Incline range must be adjusted to 165 mm minimum prior to installation.**
3. Use a **14 mm wrench** to assemble the new incline motor.
4. Connect the incline motor wiring to the controller: **red wire to "UP", white wire to "COM", black
   wire to "DOWN"** (the page prints the key in English and Chinese).

Neither book says between which two points the 165 is measured beyond the drawing, nor a torque.
