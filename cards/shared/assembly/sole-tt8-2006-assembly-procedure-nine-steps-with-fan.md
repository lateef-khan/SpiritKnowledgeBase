Nine unillustrated assembly steps, one hardware list for all of them. This book prints no unpacking warning.

1. Remove all parts from the carton.
2. Connect Computer Cable (Lower) to Computer Cable (Middle).
3. Hand-tighten the right and left uprights into the main frame with 2 pcs of 3/8 x 2 Button Head Socket Screws, 2 pcs of 3/8 x 2T Spring Washers and 6 pcs of 5/16 x 1/2 Button Head Socket Screws, using both wrenches.
4. Install the Upright Support onto the left and right uprights with 4 pcs of 5/16 x 3/4 Button Head Socket Screws; do not tighten yet.
5. Connect Computer Cable (Middle) to Computer Cable (Upper); connect Speed Adjustment Switch W/Cable to Speed Cable (Upper); connect Incline Adjustment Switch W/Cable to Incline Cable (Upper).
6. Install the Console Support into the right and left uprights with 4 pcs of 5/16 x 1/2 Button Hand Socket Screws, fit the beverage holder onto the console support, then tighten all screws from step 4.
1. Remove all parts from the carton.
8. Install Frame Base Cap (R) and (L) with 2 pcs of 5 x 16 mm plus 4 pcs of 5 x 16 mm Self Tapping Screws.
9. Connect the fan assembly computer cable to the console and fix the fan assembly onto the console with 3 pcs of M3 x 18 mm Self Tapping Screws.

Lock down all hardware after the accessories are assembled.
