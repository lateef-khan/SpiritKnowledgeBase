"Before disassembling the rear roller, you need to complete the disassembly of the left and right end
cap covers, the motor upper and lower covers and the front roller" - chapter 2 of the same book.

1. **Loosen the nut that adjusts the belt tightness (do not remove it).**
2. **Remove the screw on the right side of the rear roller**, then **pull the rear roller out to the
   right**.

That is the whole chapter (3 in both books). Sizes, tools and the re-tensioning afterwards are not
printed. The belt/deck replacement chapter deliberately stops short of this - it loosens the right
screw only and says the rear roller need not come out to change the belt.
