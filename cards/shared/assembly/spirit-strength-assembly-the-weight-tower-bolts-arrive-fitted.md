**The screws hardware (20, 24, 27) at the weight tower bottom are pre-installed. Remove
them, attach the frame onto the door frame, then fit the screws.**

Items 20, 24 and 27 are, on the CSS books, the **inner hexagon socket head bolt, the
spring washer and the flat washer** of the door-frame joint. They arrive in the tower, not
in the hardware pack, so a builder who works only from the step table will look for them
in the pack and not find them.

**The item numbers are not stable across the range.** The trio is 20/24/27 on the
selectorized stations and the pec fly/rear delt, but the leg extension/leg curl station
words it *"HARDWARE (20, 24, 27) is pre-installed on tower. Remove hardware to attach
connecting frame (33)"* and names a different frame. Read the numbers off the book in
front of you.

**On the lat pulldown the note says "attach the frame (40) onto tower"** rather than onto
the door frame - the only machine on this card that describes the joint that way.

## How many bolts is a per-machine number

The door-frame joint takes **4** of the M10x35 bolts on most of these machines and **8**
on the abdominal, the back extension, the lat row and the seated leg press. Two books
print a count that does not balance at all - see
[the mismatched counts card](spirit-strength-assembly-fastener-counts-that-do-not-balance.md).
Take the figure from your own step table, never from another machine's.

The **CSS-DELT's 2026 revision** (SP-4312, 20260821, whose text layer is a shifted font - read from its OCR supplements and renders) prints this exactly as its 2025 printing does.
