Numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing item numbers. Quantities are on the
hardware card for your printing - the two printings do not use the same handlebar bolt.

## Unpacking

1. Cut the straps, then lift the box over the unit and unpack.
2. Remove all parts from the carton and inspect for damage or missing parts. Contact
   your dealer immediately if anything is damaged or missing.
3. Locate the hardware package. Remove the tools first, then the hardware for each
   step as you reach it.

## Step one - front and rear stabilizers

Install the **FRONT AND REAR STABILIZERS** with four **CARRIAGE BOLTS (81)**, four
**CURVED WASHERS (83)** and four **CAP NUTS (82)**.

**The front and rear stabilizer are different.** The stabilizer carrying the wheels
goes on the front of the bike.

## Step two - rear stabilizer cover

Install the **REAR STABILIZER COVER** with two **SCREWS (91)**.

## Step three - handlebars and bottle holder

1. Install the handlebars with four **BOLTS**, four **SPLIT WASHERS (49)** and four
   **FLAT WASHERS (48)**. Tighten securely.
2. Install the **BOTTLE HOLDER** to the handlebars by loosening the thumb screw,
   clamping it to the handlebars, and re-tightening the thumb screw.

**The bolt is not the same in both printings** - see the hardware cards.

## Step four - pedals

Install the **LEFT (25L)** and **RIGHT (25R)** pedals into the crank arms. The left
pedal is reverse-threaded; see
[the pedal card](spirit-cycle-assembly-pedal-reverse-thread-no-torque-figure.md).

## Step five - console, batteries and speed transmitter

Batteries, synchronising and the physical mounting are all printed as STEP FIVE across
two pages. The mounting is on
[its own card](spirit-xic600-assembly-console-and-transmitter-mounting.md).

## The chapter ends there

There is **no levelling step and no instruction for moving the bike** in either
printing. See
[the absence card](spirit-xic600-assembly-no-levelling-or-moving-section.md).
