1. **Align the hole in the end of the Connecting arm (L) (pivoting rod end)** with the hole in the bracket of the Handle Bar (L), rod end inside the bracket. **Take 1pc of 5/16" x 1-1/4" Hex Head Screw** through the Handle Bar (L) bracket and the rod end. Fit **1pc of 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T Flat Washer**, a **5/16" x 7T Nylon Nut** and the **11.9 x 8.5 x 16m/m Sleeve Spacer** on the screw and tighten firmly, using a **12m/m Wrench** on the nut and one on the screw. (The VE book adds: make sure the tie-wrap is removed from the rod end and the sleeve spacer does not fall out during assembly.)
2. **Repeat for the right side Pedal/Connecting Arm.**
