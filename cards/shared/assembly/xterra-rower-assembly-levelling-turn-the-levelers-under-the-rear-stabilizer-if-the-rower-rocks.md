Every one of these books puts the levelers at the **rear**: "If you find the machine is unbalanced before or during your workout, the Levelers can be used to correct this issue. Continuously working out on an unbalanced machine may cause damage to the machine" (ERG160 / ERG220 wording).

| Book | What it calls the leveler | Part no. |
|---|---|---|
| ERG160, ERG220 | Levelers on the Rear Stabilizer (4) | 33 (rear stabilizer end cap) |
| ERG180 | the end cap for the Rear Support Tube | 79 (adjustable cushion) |
| ERG550W | Adjustable Floor Stabilizers below the Rear Stabilizer (3) | 19 |
| ERG600W | the End Caps on the Rear Stabilizer (4) | - |
| ERG650W | "Adjust the rear feet" | - |
| ERG700 | 2 leveling pads on the bottom of the rear stabilizer, **use a 14 mm wrench (or adjustable wrench)** | - |
| ERG750W | the adjustment Rubber Pad on the Rear Track Group, "if the machine is uneven after assembly" | 43 |

Only the ERG700 names a tool, and it files the instruction under General Maintenance as the third cause of squeaks. The ERG400, ERG500 and ERG800W books print no levelling instruction; the ERG800W's step 5 instead leaves the front stabilizer bolts loose until the rail and rear stabilizer are on "to make sure the foot pad on the front stabilizer touches the ground better".
