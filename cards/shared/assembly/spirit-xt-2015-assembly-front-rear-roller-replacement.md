1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to loosen the **2 screws** on the **rear adjustment base**.
2. Use an **M6 L Allen wrench** to loosen the **2 screws** on the rear roller.
3. Remove the motor cover. Use a **14mm T-shaped socket wrench** to loosen the **4 screws** (the motor's), and a
   **14mm open end wrench** to loosen the **1 belt tension screw**, and loosen the drive belt.
4. Use a **13mm wrench** to loosen the front roller screws. Remove the front and back rollers and replace them
   with new. Reassemble in the reverse order of disassembly.
5. When reassembled, the running belt tension needs to be adjusted and centred, and the drive belt tension
   needs to be adjusted: with the 14mm open end wrench and a tension measuring device, to the **white LBS area
   between 70-75 LBS**, then tighten the 4 securing screws with the 14mm T-shaped socket wrench.

The XT185 2015 book prints step 5 as a pointer - "refer to 12.3-5 above", its motor procedure's tension
step - and the XT285, XT385 and XT485 books print the tension paragraph in full. The figures are the same.

The running-belt tension and centring that step 5 asks for are not specified in this procedure; the
belt-adjustment pages in each book's maintenance chapter hold them. No torque is printed.
