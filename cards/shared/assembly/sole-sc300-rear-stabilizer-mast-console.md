1. Attach the Rear Stabilizer with handle (13) to the mounting plate on the main frame and secure with two 3/8" x 3-3/4" Hex Head Bolts (106), two O10 x 2T Split Washers (144) and O3/8" x 23 x 2.0T Curved Washers (142), using the 13/14m/m Wrench (149).
2. Pull the tie on the Computer Cable (2) to have the Computer Cable (46) go through the mast from the bottom and out of the mast on top. Secure the Console Mast on the mounting plate on the Main Frame with four 3/8" x 3/4" Socket Head Cap Bolts (110), four O10 x 2T Split Washers (144) and four O3/8" x O19 x 1.5T Flat Washers (136), using the L Allen Wrench (152).
3. Take off the tie from the Computer Cable (46) and plug it into the Console Assembly (45) together with the two Handpulse Assemblies (51, 52) and the Resistance/Incline (White/Red) cables (61, 62). Place the Console on the mounting plate of the Console Mast and secure with four M5 x 10m/m Phillips Head Screws (113), using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (151).
