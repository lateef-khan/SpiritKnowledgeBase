## XE395 2023 - eleven lines

- 1 Main Frame
- 1 Console Mast
- 1 Console Mast Cover
- 2 Swing Arms
- 2 Linkage Arms
- 6 Levelers
- 1 Console
- 1 Tablet Rack Holder
- 2 Foot Pedals
- 2 Transport Wheels
- 1 Hardware Kit

## XE795 2023 - the same ten lines without the tablet rack holder

- 1 Main Frame
- 1 Console
- 1 Console Mast
- 1 Console Mast Cover
- 2 Swing Arms
- 2 Linkage Arms
- 6 Levelers
- 2 Foot Pedals
- 2 Transport Wheels
- 1 Hardware Kit

**Both machines fit a tablet holder during the build** - the XE395 in step two, from
its item 200 with three M5 x 12mm screws, and the XE795 in step six, from its item
136 with three M5 x 12mm screws. **Only the XE395 counts one on its carton list.**

## Connecting arms became linkage arms

The 2021 carton lists say **2 Connecting Arms**; both 2023 lists say **2 Linkage
Arms**, and both 2023 machine diagrams label the same part **I. Linkage Arms**. It is
the same part with a new name, and the 2023 assembly steps still call it a connecting
arm in the text - the XE795 step four says "LEFT CONNECTING ARM (9)".

## Neither list has a power cord or an audio cable

The 2021 lists carry **1 Power Cord** on four machines and **1 Audio Cable** on all
five. Both 2023 lists drop both lines. On the **XE795 2023** there is nothing to drop
- it is generator powered. On the **XE395 2023** there is: its step four says to
"plug the power cord into the machine and a grounded wall outlet" to raise the incline
before one cover goes on, and no line of its carton list accounts for that cord.

## How this panel reads in the file

Both 2023 manuals print the carton list in a narrow two-column block wedged between
the hardware picture panels, and it machine-reads badly - the lines interleave with
the hardware captions. **The contents above are certain; the printed left-to-right
order is not**, and it is reproduced here in the order it could be recovered. No
count was taken from a damaged line.
