**This is the gear-motor XBU55 (2016 and 2023 books), belt figure 180 +/- 10 Hz on a sonic tension meter.** The generator CU800 upright uses the same C-ring and pulley bolts but no bearing-housing step and a 180 to 205 Hz figure; the Sole B94 twin prints 450 N. Neither figure transfers.

1. Remove both shrouds (chain covers (L) (R) (36) (37)).
2. Use an **11 mm open-end wrench** to release the **1/4" x 8T nyloc nut (130)** to the end, and a **6 mm Allen wrench** to remove the **M10 x P1.25 x 15L button head socket bolt (127)**, and take the **bearing housing (10)** apart.
3. Unmount the **drive belt (14)** from the **drive pulley (15)**.
4. Use **C-ring tweezers** to remove the **Ø20 C-ring (67)** on the **crank arbor (11)**, then take the crank arbor (11) and drive pulley (15) down - they can be knocked out with a rubber hammer.
5. Use **two 11 mm open-end wrenches** to remove **four 1/4" x 3/4" hex head bolts (54)** together with **four 1/4" x 8T nyloc nuts (79)** on the crank arbor (11) and the drive pulley (15), and take the drive pulley apart.
6. Reverse the steps to refit, but check that the belt (14) sits in the groove and runs in a straight line across the drive pulley (15) and the **flywheel (20)**. **Adjust the belt tension, measured with a sonic tension meter, to 180 +/- 10 Hz.**

**The same book prints a second, different belt figure.** The slipping-belt answer in the troubleshooting chapter says to tighten the M6 nyloc nut (130) with the 11 mm wrench "until sound wave tension gauge reads 450N". 180 +/- 10 Hz is a frequency; 450 N is a force. The replacement procedure prints the frequency; the troubleshooting answer prints the force. Which one the technician's gauge reads depends on the gauge, and the book does not reconcile them. The belt-tension figure card for this machine covers the contradiction; this card carries the procedure.

The 2016 book heads the section "The Belt and Drive Pulley" and the 2023 book "Replacing the Drive Pulley"; the steps are the same.
