**The book prints the check list three times, once per machine.** Step 1 and step 3
are the same on all three. **Steps 2 and 4 are not** - do not order or count from the
wrong table.

## Step 1 - console mast - identical on all three

| Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 60 | 3/8" x 2-1/4" Hex Head Bolt | 1 |
| 66 | M5 x 10m/m Phillips Head Screw | 4 |
| 84 | 3/8" x 23 x 1.5T Curved Washer | 2 |
| 96 | 3/8" x 2T Split Washer | 1 |
| 103 | 3/8" x 3/4" Hex Head Bolt | 2 |

## Step 2 - handle bars - three different bags

**XE100**

| Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 59 | 5/16" x 15m/m Hex Head Bolt | 2 |
| 79 | 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T Flat Washer | 2 |
| 83 | 17 Wave Washer | 2 |

**XE200**

| Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 81 | 25 Wave Washer | 2 |
| 97 | 3/8" x 30 x 2T Flat Washer | 2 |
| 103 | 3/8" x 3/4" Hex Head Bolt | 2 |

**XE300** - the XE200 bag plus eight self-tapping screws, because the XE300 fits its
handle bar covers during step 2 and the XE200 does not

| Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 81 | 25 Wave Washer | 2 |
| 87 | 3.5 x 12m/m Self Tapping Screw | 8 |
| 97 | 3/8" x 30 x 2T Flat Washer | 2 |
| 103 | 3/8" x 3/4"mm Hex Head Bolt | 2 |

**The XE100 uses a smaller wave washer and a smaller bolt than the other two.** Item
83 against item 81, and a 5/16" bolt with a 5/16" flat washer against a 3/8" bolt
with a 3/8" flat washer. Nothing about the XE100 handle bar bag carries across.

## Step 3 - connecting arms

**XE100 and XE200**

| Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 71 | 5/16" x 7T Nylon Nut | 2 |
| 80 | 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T Flat Washer | 2 |
| 104 | 5/16 x 1-1/4 Hex Head Bolt | 2 |

**XE300** - the same three plus the covers it fits in this step

| Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 71 | 5/16" x 7T Nylon Nut | 2 |
| 80 | 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T Flat Washer | 2 |
| 87 | 3.5 x 12m/m Self Tapping Screw | 10 |
| 104 | 5/16 x 1-1/4 Hex Head Bolt | 2 |
| 105 | M5 x 15m/m Phillips Head Screw | 11 |

## Step 4 - plastics and covers - three different bags again

| Machine | Item 87, 3.5 x 12m/m Self Tapping Screw | Item 105, M5 x 15m/m Phillips Head Screw | Item 91, 5/16" x 3/4 Hex Head Bolt |
|---|---|---|---|
| XE100 | 10 | 17 | - |
| XE200 | 18 | 21 | - |
| XE300 | - | 26 | 4 |

**Only the XE300 has an item 91**, and it is the only machine whose step 4 bolts
anything to steel - the two W-shaped brackets under the aluminium tracks and the
centre support brace. Its step 4 carries no self-tapping screws at all, because it
used them in steps 2 and 3.

## Two things the book contradicts itself about

- **Item 81 is drawn as "25" in all three panels and called "28m/m" in every step
  that fits it.** Both numbers are printed, in the same book, for the same item.
  This card does not resolve it; quote the item number, not the size.
- **Item 87 is "3.5 x 12m/m" in every panel and "3.5x10m/m" in five of the step
  sentences that use it** - the XE100 step 4 items 1 and 4, and the XE200 step 4
  item 5. Other sentences in the same steps call the same item 3.5x12m/m.

## Two screws in the XE100 step 4 have no item number at all

Its item 1 fits the connecting arm covers "with four M5 x 15m/m Screws and two
3.5x10m/m Self Tapping Screws" and names neither item number, where the equivalent
XE200 and XE300 sentences name 105 and 87. The sizes match items 105 and 87, but the
manual does not say so.
