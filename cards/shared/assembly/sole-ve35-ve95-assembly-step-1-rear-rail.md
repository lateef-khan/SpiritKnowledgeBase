1. **Install the two 3/8 x 19mm Flat Washers (76) on the two 3/8 x 2-1/4 Hex Head Bolts (60)**, hand-tighten only, through the top of the Rear Stabilizer Tube (Main Frame) into the Rear Rail Assembly (2).
2. **Install the four 3/8 X 1 Curved Washers (84) on the four 3/8 x 3-3/4 Hex Head Bolts (61)**, hand-tighten through the front side of the Rear Stabilizer Tube (Main Frame) into the Rear Rail Assembly (2). **Tighten all six bolts firmly** with the **13/14 m/m Wrench (106)**.
3. **Front Stabilizer Cover (49)** with **two M5 x 15 m/m Phillips Head Screws (105)**.
