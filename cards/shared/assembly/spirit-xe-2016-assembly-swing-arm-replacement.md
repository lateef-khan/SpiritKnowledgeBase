**This is the two-piece swing arm with handlebar buttons.** The XE195 book's one-piece arm has no wiring and no
joint bolts; the Sole E35-2016 twin uses a `3/8" x 3/4"` pivot bolt where this arm uses `5/16" x 15mm`.

1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **sheet metal screws 3.5x12mm** securing the swing arm covers (front and
   back).
2. **Disconnect the wiring** connecting the upper and lower swing arms.
3. Use a **12 mm wrench** to release the **5/16" x 15mm hex head bolt** and **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T washer** securing the
   swing arm assembly (the XE395 books say "which secure the Handle Bar").
4. Release the **fish-eye (rod end) bearing** with **12 mm and 13 mm wrenches**: **5/16" x 1-1/4" hex head screw x 1**,
   **5/16" x 20 x 1.5T flat washer x 1** and **5/16" x 7T nyloc nut x 1**. Remove the swing arm assembly.
5. To split the assembly, use **#12 and #13 open end wrenches** to release **three hex head bolts 5/16" x 1-3/4"**
   with **three nyloc nuts 5/16" x 7T** and **two curve washers 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T**, and separate the upper swing arm
   from the lower swing arm.
6. To take the **handle switch bracket** apart, tear off the handle switch sticker first, then use a Phillips head
   screwdriver to release **two flat head socket screws M5x20mm** and the bracket; the handlebar resistance cable or
   incline cable can then be taken apart.
7. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.

The XE295 book numbers the reassembly line as step 7; the XE395 and XE395ENT books print it unnumbered.
