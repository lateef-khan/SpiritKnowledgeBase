## PEDAL STRAP ADJUSTMENT - both manuals

> Place the ball of you feet in the toe clip till the front of the shoe is snugly in the
> cage. Then tighten the strap of toe clip around your shoe by pulling **up** on the
> strap until cage of toe clip fits snugly around the shoe.

Printed word for word in both books, typo included.

## ADJUSTING THE PEDAL STRAPS - CIC850 only

The CIC850 prints a **second** strap paragraph on the next page:

> Place your feet in between the aluminum surface of the pedal and the nylon foot strap
> that wraps around it. To adjust the fit, pull the end of the adjustable strap **down**
> to tighten, or pull the strap **up** to loosen.

**The two paragraphs pull the strap opposite ways.** One says pull up to tighten the toe
clip; the other says pull down to tighten the nylon strap and up to loosen it. The
CIC850 pedal has both a caged toe clip and a nylon strap, so the two may describe
different parts of the same pedal - but neither paragraph says so, and the manual never
reconciles them. Work from the pedal in front of you, not from the page.

## Neither is the CB900 and XIC600 instruction

Those manuals describe a **spring-loaded clasp** that must be depressed before the strap
will move at all. Neither CIC paragraph mentions a clasp. See
[that card](spirit-cycle-assembly-pedal-straps-with-a-spring-loaded-clasp.md).
