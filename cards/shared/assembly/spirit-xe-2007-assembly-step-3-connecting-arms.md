1. Line the hole in the rod end of the left **Connecting Arm (8)** up with the hole
   in the bracket of the left **Handle Bar (10)**. **The rod end goes on the inner
   side of the bracket.** Make sure the ty-wrap is removed from the rod end and that
   the **sleeve spacer (22) does not fall out** during assembly. Put one **5/16" x
   1-1/4" Hex Head Bolt (104)** through the bracket and the rod end, add one **5/16"
   x 20 x 1.5T Flat Washer (80)** and one **5/16" x 7mm Nylon Nut (71)**, and tighten
   as tight as possible using the two 12m/m wrenches provided. Repeat on the right.

**That single item is the whole of step 3 on the XE100 and the XE200**, word for word
and item number for item number on both.

## The XE300 does two more things in this step

2. Install the connecting arm covers **(57, 57-1 and 58, 58-1)** over the joint of
   the rod end and the handle bars (10 and 11) with eight **M5 x 15m/m Phillips Head
   Screws (105)** and eight **3.5x12m/m Self Tapping Screws (87)**, using the Phillips
   head screw driver.
3. Install the front **(122)** and rear **(121)** console mast covers with three
   **M5 x 15m/m Screws (105)** and two **3.5x12m/m Self Tapping Screws (87)**.

**On the XE100 and XE200 those two jobs are step 4 items**, not step 3 items, and the
XE100's version of the connecting arm cover uses four screws and two self-tappers per
side rather than eight and eight. See
[the step 4 card](spirit-xe-2007-assembly-step-4-covers.md).

## The manual asks for two wrenches and the carton holds one of each size

"Tighten as tight as possible using the two 12m/m Wrenches provided" - the tools
panel supplies **one** 12m/m wrench and one 13/14m/m wrench. See
[the tools card](spirit-xe-assembly-tools-2005-2007.md).

## No torque figure is printed

Neither this step nor any other in this manual gives a torque value, for this joint
or any other. "As tight as possible" is all the book says.
