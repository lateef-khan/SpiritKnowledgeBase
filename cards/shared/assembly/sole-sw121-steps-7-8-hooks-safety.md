**These two steps are diagram-only: the page carries no written instruction, only exploded drawings with callouts.** The drawings were read on the render of PDF p.12.

**Step 7** seats the dumbbell-tower fittings (callouts 2-005 with the M8 bolts, washers, nuts and lock washers) onto the frame uprights.

**Step 8** drops the two hooks (008) and the two safety welding sets (022) onto the uprights. The Hardware Step 7 box names these as the Right hook (2 pcs) and the Right safety welding set (2 pcs); both readings are transcribed exactly as the book prints them.
