Hardware: **#51** 5/16" x 5/8" Hex Head Bolt (7 pcs), **#72** 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T Flat Washer (6 pcs), **#99** 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T Curved Washer (1 pc).

1. Let the Computer Cable (29) through the Console Mast Cover (38) and the Console Mast (2).
2. Secure the Console Mast (2) onto the Main Frame (1) with the seven Hex Head Bolts (51), six Flat Washers (72) and one Curved Washer (99) by using the Wrench (100).

Seven bolts but only six flat washers. **This short wording does not say where the odd washer goes.** The longer instruction printed in the 2011-2016 B94 and the 2019 B94 does: the six flat washers go on the side bolts and the single curved washer goes on the **front** bolt. Same seven-bolt joint, same parts.

The LCB 2016 instruction names the tool as the 12/14mm Wrench (100); the 2019 and 2023 printings shorten it to "Wrench (100)". It is the same tool.
