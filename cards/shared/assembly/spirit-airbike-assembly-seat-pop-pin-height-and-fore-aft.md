Proper setup gives a more comfortable ride and reduces the risk of injury.

## Height

Dismount the bike. Holding the seat with one hand, **loosen and pull out on the seat
height pop-pin** to release it from its current preset location. Raise or lower the seat
to the desired height, then gently release the pop-pin. Raise or lower the seat slightly
if necessary until the pop-pin engages a preset hole.

**At the proper height there should be a slight bend in your knee when you are at the
bottom of a pedal stroke.**

## Fore/aft

Once the height is right, adjust the seat forward or back so that **when the feet are at
3 o'clock and 9 o'clock the forward knee is directly over the pedal axle**.

**Recheck the height afterwards.** Moving the seat forward and backward has the same
effect as moving it higher or lower.

## The height is preset holes, not a slide

The pop-pin engages a hole, so the height is discrete. Only the fore/aft is continuous,
on the sliding seat mount (7) the seat bolts to during assembly. There is **no handlebar
adjustment on either air bike** - the arms move with the pedals and are not
height-adjustable.

## The Johnny G pop-pin is turned as well as pulled

The JB950 pin must be **turned counterclockwise** before it will pull out, and turned
clockwise to secure. These two do not say to turn theirs. See
[that card](../../jb950-2022/assembly/jb950-2022-assembly-seat-and-handlebar-tension-handles.md).
