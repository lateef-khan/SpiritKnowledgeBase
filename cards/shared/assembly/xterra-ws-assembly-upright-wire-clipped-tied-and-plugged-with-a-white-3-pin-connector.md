**No manual describes this; the card comes from an 86-second silent service video and nothing else.**
The owner's manuals only say to lift the crossbar until it clicks. The video (file name "How to assemble
the wire WS200-300") shows a technician preparing the wire in a folding upright on a bench:

1. A thin cable with **red, yellow and black** conductors is seated at the hinge-bracket end of the long
   flat steel upright.
2. Small black plastic **cable clips**, each with one black self-tapping screw driven by a cordless
   screwdriver, hold the cable along the bar - at least two, one of them at the slotted silver bracket
   where the cable exits the bar.
3. The cable is pressed into the channel along the bar and **white cable ties are threaded through the
   bar's holes** over it and pulled tight - at least three along the bar, more near the bracket end.
4. A small black rubber **grommet** (strain relief) is fitted over the cable near the motor housing.
5. The cable's small **white 3-pin connector** is mated to a matching white 3-pin connector on a lead from
   the drive-motor side.

**What the video does not establish:** which end of the machine the bar belongs to, the connector's
name on the board, the screw sizes, the tie spacing, any torque, and whether this is a factory or
field procedure. Nothing in the picture names the model; the file name is the only link to the WS200
and WS300.
