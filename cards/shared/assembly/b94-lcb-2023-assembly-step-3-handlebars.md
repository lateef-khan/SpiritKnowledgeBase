Hardware: **#74** 5/16" x18 x5/8" Button Head Socket Bolt (4 pcs), **#72** 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T Flat Washer (4 pcs), **#103** 5/16" x 1.5T Split Washer (4 pcs).

1. Plug the two Handpulse W/Cable Assemblies (26) through the Console Mast (2) and pull them out of the holes together with the connectors. Use 4 pcs of Button Head Socket Bolts (74) together with 4 pcs of Flat Washers (72) and 4 pcs of Split Washers (103) to secure **Handle Bar (L) (3)** and **Handle Bar (R) (4)** on the Console Mast (2), by using the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (76).

**Two separate handlebars, left (3) and right (4).** Every earlier B94 and LCB fits a single one-piece handlebar assembly numbered (3).

**All four split washers are used here.** The 2019 machines print the same four-piece hardware box but tell you to fit only two; that contradiction does not exist in the 2023 manuals.
