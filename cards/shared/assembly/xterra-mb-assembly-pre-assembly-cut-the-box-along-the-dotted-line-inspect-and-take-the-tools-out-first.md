1. Using a razor knife (box cutter) cut the outside, bottom edge of the box along the dotted line. Lift the box over the unit and unpack.
2. Carefully remove all parts from the carton and inspect for any damage or missing parts. If damaged parts are found, or parts are missing, contact us immediately.
3. Locate the hardware package. **Remove the tools first.** Remove the hardware for each step as needed to avoid confusion. The numbers in the instructions that are in parenthesis (#) are the item number from the assembly drawing for reference.

Three steps, word for word the same in both books. The treadmill books print a four-step version of this page (the linked card); the bike version adds the dotted line and the box cutter.
