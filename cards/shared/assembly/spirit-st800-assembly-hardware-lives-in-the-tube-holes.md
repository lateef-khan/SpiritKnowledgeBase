The ST800 unpacking instruction is two lines and neither of them mentions a hardware bag:

1. **Cut the straps, then pull the staples away from the bottom half and lift the top half
   of the carton off of the bottom.**
2. **The hardware is located in the holes of the associated tubes/brackets.**

**So each joint's bolts arrive in the joint.** Nothing is bagged and nothing is sorted by
step. Emptying every hole before you start is the practical move, because a bolt left in a
hole is a bolt the next part will not seat against.

**This is the opposite of the rest of the Spirit strength range**, where the hardware is a
pack split into four steps and the bracketed numbers are drawing item numbers. See
[that card](spirit-strength-assembly-hardware-split-into-four-steps.md).

**The carton opens differently too.** The other thirty-six machines say to cut the straps
and lift the box over the unit; these three say to pull the staples and take the top half
off, which means the machine is not lifted out at all.
