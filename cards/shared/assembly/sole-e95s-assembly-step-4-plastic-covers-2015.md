1. **Left Connecting Arm Cover (121)** on the left Connecting Arm with **M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws (152)**, then **left Connecting Arm Cover (119)** with **two (152)** on the Lower Swing Arm. Repeat for the right covers **(122)** and **(120)**.
2. **Two Slide Wheel Covers (99)** with **four (152)**.
3. **Four Stabilizer Covers (104 & 105)** on both sides of the rear tube of the rear rail assembly with **four (152)**.
4. **Left and right Console Mast Covers (111, 112)** matched with the side cases, **two 4mm x 15mm Sheet Metal Screws (154)**.
5. **Front Handle Bar Cover (100) with Rear Handle Bar Cover (101)** on the left Lower Swing Arm, **four 3.5mm x 12mm Sheet Metal Screws (153)**; same for **(102)** and **(103)** on the right. Do not pinch the wire.
6. **Two Oval End Caps (89)** onto both ends of the front stabilizer tube.
