Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. Hardware quantities are
on [the hardware card](spirit-cu900-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Step one - rear stabilizer and seat

1. Install **REAR STABILIZER (4)** to **MAIN FRAME (1)** with 3 BOLTS (61) and 3
   WASHERS (60).
2. Install **SEAT (28)** to **SEAT POST (8)** with 4 BOLTS (52) and 4 WASHERS (53).
3. Attach **SEAT BRACKET COVERS (43 & 44)** to SEAT POST (8) with 4 round head SCREWS
   (59).

Three bolts hold the rear stabilizer, not four. Their thread size changed between
printings - see
[the rear stabilizer bolt card](spirit-cu900-assembly-rear-stabilizer-bolt-size.md).

## Step two - console mast and console

1. Run the **WIRING HARNESS** through the **CONSOLE MAST (2)** and out the top.
2. Install CONSOLE MAST (2) to MAIN FRAME (1) with 6 BOLTS (52) and 6 WASHERS (112).
   Do not pinch any wires.
3. Connect **HGP CONNECTOR CABLES (87 & 102)** to **CONSOLE (48)**. Run the cables
   through the centre of the console bracket and out the back.
4. Connect WIRING HARNESS to CONSOLE (48).
5. Attach CONSOLE (48) to CONSOLE MAST (2) using 4 SCREWS (54). Be careful not to
   pinch any wires.

## Step three - handlebars

1. Install **HANDLEBARS (3)** to CONSOLE MAST (2) with 4 BOLTS (56), 4 LOCK WASHERS
   (57), 8 WASHERS (53) and 4 NUTS (58). **4 WASHERS (53) go on each side of the
   handlebars (3).**
2. Connect HGP CONNECTOR CABLES (87 & 102) to the HGP cables coming out of HANDLEBARS
   (3).
3. Attach **CONSOLE BRACKET COVER (35)** to HANDLEBARS (3) using 2 SCREWS (66).

The console goes on in step two but its bracket cover in step three, because the cover
lands on the handlebars rather than on the mast.

## Step four - cup holder, mast covers and pedals

1. Attach **CUP HOLDER (36)** to CONSOLE MAST (2) using 2 SCREWS (54).
2. Attach **CONSOLE MAST COVERS (37 & 38)** over CONSOLE MAST (2) using 4 SELF-TAPPING
   SCREWS (55).
3. Install **PEDALS (11)** into **CRANK ARMS (9 & 10)**. The left pedal has a reverse
   thread and will be screwed in counterclockwise. Tighten to **300 in-lb (34 Nm)** of
   torque.

## All three printings print the same four steps

Word for word, apart from the rear stabilizer bolt size. The CU900ENT book is the CU900
2018 book with the model name changed in the headings and nothing else - the assembly
chapters match at **99.0%** at word level, and every difference is column spacing or
the letters ENT. The 2025 manual reflows the steps onto larger pages, moves each
hardware table below its step and renames the headings from CU900 STEP ONE to
ASSEMBLY - STEP ONE. **No step in any of the three names a tool.**

The build ends here. There is no first-power-on step: these bikes are
generator-powered and the console starts when you pedal.
