Hardware: **#82** 3/8" Nut (4 pcs), **#81** 3/8" x 3-1/2" **Carriage Bolt** (4 pcs), **#83** 3/8" x 23mm Curved Washer (4 pcs).

1. Install the front and rear stabilizers with four Carriage Bolts (81), four Curved Washers (83) and four Nuts (82). **The front and rear stabilizer are different. Be sure to assemble the stabilizer with the wheels onto the front of the bike.**

**#81 is a Carriage Bolt on these machines.** On the SB900 2014 to 2020 the same item number is a Button Head Socket Bolt of the same 3/8" x 3-1/2" size. The SB700 2021 made the identical change in the same year.
