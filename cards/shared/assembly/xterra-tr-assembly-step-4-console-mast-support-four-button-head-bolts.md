Install the **console mast support (9)** - the TRX and TR65/75/85 books call it the **handrail support
(9)** under the heading "Crossbar Installation" - between the **left and right uprights (4) and (5)** and
use the combination M5 Allen wrench and Phillips screwdriver (102) to tighten **four 5/16" x 3/4" button
head socket bolts (125)**.

Every book then says to **tighten all screws after all components have been assembled**. On the TR300
and TR6.4 a "Final Process" page follows (plug in, power on). On the TRX2500, TRX3500, TR65, TR75 and
TR85 this is the last hardware step. On the TRX4500 and TRX5500 two cover steps follow.
