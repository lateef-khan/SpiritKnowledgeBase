| Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| #79 | Ø8 x Ø18 x 1.5 mm Flat Washer | 6 |
| #87 | 5/16" x 1/2" Button Head Socket Bolt (the 2022 book writes it 5/16" x UNC18 x 1/2") | 12 |
| #88 | Ø8 x Ø23 x 1.5T Curved Washer | 6 |
| #105 | Lubricant | 1 |
| #40 | Safety Key | 1 |
| #104 | Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver | 1 |

The twelve bolts are used six at step 3 (uprights) and six at step 5 (console support); the
flat washers four then two, the curved washers two then four.

**The 2021 book does not draw the tool.** Its checklist page shows only the three fasteners, the
lubricant and the safety key, yet its step 3 and step 5 say to use the "SCREW DRIVER (104)". The 2022
book draws #104 on the checklist. Both pages are flat images: the text layer of the 2021 file holds
nothing for this page and the 2022 file's text layer omits the lubricant and safety key, so the table
above was read from the page renders.
