> **To move the bike to a new location:** Lift the bike from the rear and use the front
> wheels (located on the front leg, below the handlebar) to roll the bike from one
> location to another.
>
> **To level the bike:** Use the four leveling adjusters (located on the underside of the
> front and rear legs) to compensate for uneven floor surfaces.

Word for word in all three manuals. **Four adjusters, two under each leg**, and the
wheels are on the **front** leg - so you lift the rear and the machine tips forward onto
them.

**No tool, no direction of turn and no weight are given**, in any of the three books.

## The air bikes say it a second way, in the testing checklist

Both AB manuals end the assembly checklist with "tip the bike forward **using the arms**
and roll it on a smooth surface to the final location and adjust the leveling feet so the
bike is stable" - the arms are the handle. The JB950 checklist says the same without
naming the arms, though the JB950 has no movement arms. See
[the air bike checklist](spirit-airbike-assembly-testing-checklist.md) and
[the Johnny G checklist](../../jb950-2022/assembly/jb950-2022-assembly-testing-checklist.md).

## The indoor cycles are a different instruction

The CB900, CIC800 and CIC850 have **two** transport wheels and no four-adjuster
paragraph; the XIC600 has no moving instruction at all. Do not carry this card across to
them - see
[that card](spirit-cycle-assembly-transport-wheels-engage-when-the-rear-is-lifted.md).
