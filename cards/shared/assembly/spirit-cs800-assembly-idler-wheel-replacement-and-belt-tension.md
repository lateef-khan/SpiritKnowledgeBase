**Belt tension 190 Hz +/- 10 Hz** - printed in the XS200-SS003 book (cs800-2016); the CS800 (2020) book says
"use a sonic belt tension meter" and prints no value.

The XS200-SS003 book:

1. Remove the linked assembly (the linked assembly card; the book's cross-reference "step 9.4" is right).
2. Use a **#13 double open-end wrench** to loosen the **nyloc nut M8 x 7T on the hook screws** to remove the belt.
   Use the #13 wrench to remove the **screws M8 x 20**, **nyloc nut M8 x 7T** and **flat washer 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T**
   to remove the idler wheel.
3. Reverse the steps to refit. After the idler wheel assembly is installed, make sure the **M8 x 9T nut** is so
   tight that the **sound wave tester reads 190 Hz +/- 10 Hz** when the belt is plucked with a finger.

**The CS800 (2020) book (cs800-2021)** prints five steps: the **left** linked assembly off; a **13 mm open-end
wrench** to loosen the **nut (M8*7T)** locking the idle wheel assembly to the main frame; a 13 mm wrench to remove
the nut on the **J-bolt**, then unmount the drive belt from the drive pulley; reverse to refit, *use the nut of
the J-bolt to adjust drive belt tension then use a sonic belt tension meter*; operate the crank to make sure the
belt is aligned with the drive pulley, flywheel pulley and idle wheel.

The hook screw of the 2016 book and the J-bolt of the 2020 book are the same tensioner. A slipping belt is
adjusted on the same cap nut with the #13 wrench (2016) or the J-bolt nut with a 13 mm wrench (2020) - the
maintenance answer; a worn belt is replaced by the flywheel and belt card. The Sole SC200 idler card prints the
2016 wording with the 190 Hz; the XS895 prints the same 190 Hz.
