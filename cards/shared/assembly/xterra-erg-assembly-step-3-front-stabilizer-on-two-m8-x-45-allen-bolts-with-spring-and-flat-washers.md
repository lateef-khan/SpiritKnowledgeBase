Attach the **Front Stabilizer (3)** to the **Main Frame (1)** and secure with **2 Flat Washers (55, OD16 x ID8.5 x 1.5), 2 Spring Washers (52, ID8.5) and 2 Allen Bolts (45, M8 x 45)**, using the S5 Allen key (99 / 84).

Two bolts only, one each side, and they are the long 45 mm ones - the rear stabilizer's bolts are 20 mm. The ERG220 front stabilizer takes four M8 x 20 bolts instead (linked card).
