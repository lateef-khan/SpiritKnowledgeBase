**The woodruff key goes in with its round head toward the axle.**

1. Follow the 10-8 steps (the idler, flywheel and drive belt card) to unmount the drive belt.
2. Use a **12 mm box wrench** to remove the **crank locking bolt**.
3. Use a **13 mm box wrench and an M6 Allen wrench** to loosen the nuts locking the crank on the **drive pulley
   axle**. Then take off the crank.
4. Use an **M2 Allen wrench** to loosen the **2 bolts** locking the axle on the bearing. Then take off the drive
   pulley assembly.
5. Reverse the steps to install all parts. *The direction of the woodruff key: the round head directs to the
   axle.*

**The book numbers this section 10-9, the same as the idler section before it**, and its step 1 points at "10-8",
which is the incline-motor section; the belt is unmounted in the idler section. No torque is printed for the
crank; the CS800 (2016) prints a figure for its crank bolt, the XS895 book does not.

The XS300B-YS006 service manual is one book for both XS895 owner's-manual printings; the two-column page is read by step number.
