**These two steps are diagram-only: the page carries no written instruction, only exploded drawings with callouts.** The drawings were read on the render of PDF p.9.

**Step 1** raises the two upright frames. The callouts match the Hardware Step 1 kit: the main frame risers (013) with the stick-to-tube pieces (012) and triangular connectors (014), joined with the M12x90 bolts (4-017), M12 washers (4-018) and M12 nuts (4-019).

**Step 2** adds the short riser welding groups (003) and joint pipe welding groups (004) to the uprights, joined with the M12x85 bolts (4-016), M12 washers (4-018) and M12 nuts (4-019) of the Hardware Step 2 kit.
