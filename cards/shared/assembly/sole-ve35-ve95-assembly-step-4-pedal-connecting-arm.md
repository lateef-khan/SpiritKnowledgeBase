1. **Align the hole of the rod end bearing at the end of the Connecting Arms (8 & 9)** with the holes in the bracket of the Handle Bars (10 & 11), bearings inside the U-shaped brackets. **Take two 5/16" x 1-1/4" Hex Head Screws (104)** through the left and right brackets and rod ends; fit **two 5/16" x 20mm Flat Washers (80)** and the **5/16" Nylon Nuts (71)** and tighten firmly with the **12m/m Wrench (109)** and **13/14 m/m Wrench (106)**. A sleeve spacer sits in the rod end bearing; do not let it fall out.
2. **Four sets of Connecting Arm Covers (57, 57-1 & 58, 58-1)** over the left and right rod-end and Handle Bar (10 & 11) connections with **eight M5 x 15m/m Phillips Head Screws (105)** and **eight 3.5 x 12m/m Screws (87)** using driver (108).
3. **Two Slide Wheel Covers (52)** on the left and right Pedal Arms (6, 7), above the Slide Wheels, with **four M5 x 12mm Phillips Head Screws (105)**.
4. **Four Rear Stabilizer Covers (117, 118 - 2 each)** on the Rear Stabilizers of the Rail Assembly (2) with **four M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws (105)**. (The book marks this step "for E25 only", an evident leftover; the part numbers sit in this book's E35/E95 checklists, so follow it for both machines.)
5. **E95 only: place the front half of the Rail Assembly Cover (117)** where the main body meets the rail assembly but do not fasten yet.
6. **E95 only: install the rear half of the Rail Assembly Cover (118)** on the Rear Rail Assembly (2) by lifting the pedal arms so the wheels slide through the cover openings. Slide the cover forward, rest the support tube on the front of the rail assembly under the front cover, and fit the **two M5 x 50mm Phillips Head Screws (123)** in the rear.
7. **E95 only: two M5 x 15m/m Phillips Head Screws (105)** and **two 5 x 16m/m Tapping Screws (122)** into the front half of the rail assembly cover.

The power connector sits on the front, left hand side of the unit.
