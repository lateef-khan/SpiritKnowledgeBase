**The special nut (2) comes out clockwise and, with the rope baffle rod (4), goes back counterclockwise, screwed
completely to the bottom.** Both threads are the manual's own note.

**Motor fixing plate** *(Tool: Phillips screwdriver)*

1. Remove screws (1) and (3); the flange (2), bearing (4) and motor fixing plate (5) can then be separated.

**Reel and rope** *(Tools: Phillips screwdriver, circlip pliers)*

1. First use the circlip pliers to remove the spring (1), and unscrew the **limit post (9) counterclockwise**.
2. **Twist the special nut (2) out clockwise**, remove the screws (3) and the rope baffle rod (4).
3. The whole rope reel - steel rope (5), rope wheel (6), rope stopper block (7), rope baffle (8) and screw (10) -
   can then be removed from the cover.
4. Remove the screw (10) to remove the rope (5).

*(Note: during installation, the special nut (2) and the rope baffle rod (4) need to be completely screwed to the
bottom by counter-clockwise rotation.)*

Two exploded drawings share this page and number their parts from (1) separately: the first five numbers are the
fixing-plate parts, the second ten are the reel parts. The owner's-manual maintenance rule - replace a frayed
cable at the first sign of wear - lands here.

The "Digital Resistance Module" is the i-Strength line's motorised resistance box, part 72 in the CSI owner's manuals; the screw numbers are the call-outs on the manual's exploded drawings.
