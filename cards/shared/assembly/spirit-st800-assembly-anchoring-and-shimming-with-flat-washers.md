**The functional trainer's facility safety page carries the whole rule.** In residential
use, where rocking, tipping or falling over through misuse is not a possibility, **the
equipment does not need to be anchored to the floor.** In facilities - unsupervised
settings such as hotels and apartment gyms - Spirit strongly recommends the equipment be
secured to a solid, level surface. Three notes go with that:

- **Spirit Fitness is not responsible for the actual anchoring of equipment. Consult a
  professional contractor.**
- **Use fasteners having a minimum of 500 lbs tensile capacity - 3/8" grade 2 bolts or
  better.**
- **If legs or frame do not contact the surface, DO NOT pull down with anchors. Shim any
  leg or frame not in contact with the surface using flat washers.**

**That last note is the closest thing to a levelling instruction anywhere in the Spirit
strength range.** You do not adjust the machine; you pack the gap. Pulling an unsupported
leg down with an anchor bolt loads the frame against itself.

Its own anchoring section, two pages later, says where the holes are: *"The machine has
holes in the feet, which allow for ease in anchoring to the floor."* **All anchoring
locations must be used**, the work must be done by a qualified licensed contractor, the
method and fasteners have to be verified against the flooring, and **a minimum pull-out
force of 220 lbs / 100 kgs is required for each anchor position**. Do not use the machine
until it is properly anchored.

**The dumbbell rack states the requirement and none of the detail.** Its facility safety
list says only *"Securely anchor each dumbbell rack to the floor using the anchor holes
provided in each rack"* - with no residential exemption, no fastener grade, no pull-out
figure and no shimming note. Take the three notes above from the functional trainer's
book, because the rack's book does not print them.

**The flat/incline bench prints none of this** - it has no anchor holes and no anchoring
section.

No other Spirit strength manual mentions anchoring or levelling at all. See
[that card](spirit-strength-assembly-no-levelling-instruction-anywhere.md).
