**Step 3:** Attach the Dumbbell Platforms (6) to the Stand Legs (1), using four M10x70mm Bolts (16), eight O10mm Washers (20) and four M10 Nylon Nuts (22).

**Step 4:** Attach the Connect Plate (8) and Water Bottle Tray (7) to the Dumbbell Platforms (6), using six M6x16mm Bolts (18) and six M6 Nylon Nuts (24). (The book prints "eight six M6 Nylon Nuts" with "eight" struck through; verified on the render.)

**Count contradiction, ruled.** The step demands six M6x16mm bolts and six M6 nuts, but the hardware kit on PDF p.6 holds two M6x16mm Bolts (18) and four M6 Nylon Nuts (24), and the step-4 drawing shows six bolt positions (18) with nuts (24). Both readings are transcribed as printed; the ruling is the pack list with the drawing: the box holds **fewer** of these fasteners than the step consumes, so a customer short of M6 hardware is not necessarily missing a part from a correct pack, and the book gives no correction.
