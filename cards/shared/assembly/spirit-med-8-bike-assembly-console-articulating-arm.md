The console hangs on a **360 degree swivel console articulating arm**. It provides the ability for clinicians to operate the console from any position around the machine, and allows a suitable viewing distance for the patient. **The tilted view angle adjustment** meets a wide variety of user heights. The parts diagrams name the two movements separately: **B**, the vertical viewing angle adjustment, and **C**, the console articulating arm horizontal adjustment.

**How it is fitted differs between the two bikes.** On the 8.5R the arm is mounted on the console mast in step two with **two M5 x 12 bolts (146) on a 3 mm Allen wrench (155)**, after the three lower connectors are joined to the three upper ones between mast and arm. On the 8.0U the arm arrives on the mast and step four only plugs the connectors and hangs the console on it. Both then fit the console with four M5 x 10 screws - the top two with the leather cover between, the bottom two without - and the yellow cover and leather cover with two M4 x 8 screws.

**Neither book says how stiffly the arm should swivel.** The 8.0T treadmill's book does - five socket set screws left not fully tight, loosened a quarter turn if the arm feels stiff - and that is its own card. On the bikes the arm's rotating structure sits on an **O-ring** that the service manual replaces from the console mast; that is the service card.
