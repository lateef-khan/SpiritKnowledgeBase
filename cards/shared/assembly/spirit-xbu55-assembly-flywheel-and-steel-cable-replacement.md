1. Remove both shrouds and unmount the drive belt (the drive-pulley card, steps 1 to 3).
2. Use an **11 mm open-end wrench** to release the **1/4" x 8T nyloc nut (130)** to the end and a **6 mm Allen wrench** to remove the **M10 x P1.25 x 15L button head socket bolt (127)**, and take the **bearing housing (10)** apart.
3. *(2016 book only)* Use C-ring tweezers to remove the **Ø17 C-ring (66)** of the **bearing housing (9)** and take the **axle for idler wheel 6203 (18)** and the bearing housing (9) apart.
4. Take the **steel cable** down off the **flywheel (20)**. **Handle the aluminium sheet on the steel cable with care** - it is an aluminium sheet with a spring, and if it is bent it distorts and rubs, causing noise. The book calls this "a very important point".
5. Use the **14/15 mm wrench (75)** to loosen the **2 pcs of 3/8"-UNF26 nuts** on the flywheel (20).
6. Use the 15 mm and 14 mm open-end wrenches to release the two nuts that secure the flywheel by turning counterclockwise, and take the flywheel apart.
7. Reverse the steps to refit, then set the belt position and tension as the drive-pulley card says (**180 +/- 10 Hz on a sonic tension meter**). **Note the direction of the bearing housing (9) and the nut stopper (128)** when the bearing housing goes back on the main frame (1).

**The 2016 XBU55 book prints one step the 2023 book does not:** the Ø17 C-ring (66) and the 6203 idler-wheel axle (18), step 3 above. Everything else - tools, part numbers, the warning about the aluminium sheet, the nut stopper - is the same in both.

The refit step says "notice to follow step 7 to adjust the elastic and position of belt". There is no step 7 in this section; the belt-tension instruction is the last step of the drive-pulley procedure.

The gear motor itself is not given a replacement procedure in either XBU55 book. The recumbent XBR55 books print one (gear motor, steel cable and reed switch), and the XBU55 troubleshooting chapter only says to check the steel cable and test the gear motor (21).
