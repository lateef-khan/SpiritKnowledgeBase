1. **Connect the Front Stabilizer Bar (2)** with the Main Frame at the front joining plate: tighten **two Hex Head Bolts (112)**, **two Split Washers (142)** and **two Curved Washers (144)** with **Wrench (151)**.
2. **Connect the Rear Stabilizer (3)** with the Main Frame at the rear joining plate the same way: **two Hex Head Bolts (112)**, **two Split Washers (142)** and **two Curved Washers (144)** with **Wrench (151)**.
3. **Pull the wire attached to the Computer Cable (38)** out of the **Console Mast (15)** and secure the mast on the Main Frame with the **M8 L Allen Wrench (153)**: tighten **four Socket Head Cap Bolts (113)** together with **four Split Washers (142)** and **four Flat Washers (139)**.
4. **Plug in the Computer Cable (38) and the two Handpulse Cable Assemblies (White & Red) (44, 45)** on the back of the **Console Assembly (35)**, and secure it on the console holding plate with **four Phillips Head Screws (119)**, tightened with driver (150).
