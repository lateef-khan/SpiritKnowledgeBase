Seven illustrated assembly steps (hardware item numbers in brackets):

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill from the carton onto a smooth, level floor with the rear at least 3 ft from any wall. Do not remove the Velcro belt until the plastic wrap and Styrofoam are out from under the unit.
2. Connect Computer Cable Upper/Lower (44) to Computer Cable Middle (43), insert the right and left uprights (4) and (5) into the frame base (2), and tighten eight Hex Head Bolts (97) with eight Flat Washers (76) using the wrench (100). Do not pinch the cables in the tubing.
3. Fix the frame base covers (L-33 & R-34) on the frame base (2) with six M5 x 15 mm Button Head Socket Bolts (95). (The step text misprints these as Phillips Head Screws; this card follows the checklist panel.)
4. Rest the console at height or have someone hold it; connect Computer Cable Upper/Lower (44) to Computer Cable Middle (43).
5. Insert the Console Assembly (19) into the uprights (R-4 & L-5) and fix with four Button Head Socket Bolts (94) and four Split Washers (93) using the M6 L wrench (99). Do not pinch the cables in the tubing.
6. Bolt the handrail support (9) between the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (96).
7. Press the left and right drink bottle holders (19-3) and (19-4) into the console assembly until they snap in place. No screws.
