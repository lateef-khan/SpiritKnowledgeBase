Eight assembly steps: the seven UF80/VF80 steps plus a support-brace eighth step (hardware item numbers in brackets).

1. Remove the treadmill from the carton onto smooth, level ground. Read the Velcro-strap warning first: keep the treadmill flat in its using orientation while removing the strap; never stand it on its side.
2. Install six Speed Nut Clips (128) on the frame base; fit the Frame Base Caps (61 & 62) through the upright tubes (4 & 5); connect Lower Computer Cable (49) to Middle Computer Cable (50).
3. Fix the upright tubes (4 & 5) into the frame base (2) with eight 5/16" x 1/2" Button Head Socket Bolts (130).
4. Fix the frame base caps (61 & 62) on the frame base (2) with six 5 x 16 mm self tapping screws (120).
5. Connect Speed Adjustment Switch Cable (54) to Speed Cable Upper (37), Incline Adjustment Switch Cable (55) to Incline Cable Upper (38), and Middle Computer Cable (50) to Upper Computer Cable (48).
6. Fix the Console Assembly (52) into the uprights with four 3/8" x 1-3/4" Button Head Socket Bolts (133) and four 10 mm Split Washers (113) using the M6 wrench (132).
7. Fix the left (63 & 65) and right (66 & 67) handgrip side caps on the uprights and Console Support (6) with eight M5 x 10 mm Phillips Head Screws (134) and four 3.5 x 12 mm Sheet Metal Screws (122).
8. Bolt the handrail support (15) between the uprights (5, 4) with four 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolts (152) and set the Beverage Holder (150) on the support.

Tighten all screws after all components are assembled.
