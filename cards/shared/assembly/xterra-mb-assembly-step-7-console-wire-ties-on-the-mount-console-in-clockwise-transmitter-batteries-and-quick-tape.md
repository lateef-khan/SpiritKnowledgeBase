No hardware drawn.

1. Use the wire-ties to hold the console mount and rubber protective pad on the front of the handlebar (**be aware of the console mount orientation**). Pull the wire-tie tight and cut off the excess.
2. Set the console into the console mount in a clockwise orientation. Load the transmitter with batteries, connect the sensor cable which is already on the unit, and secure the transmitter to the frame with quick tape as shown. **(Replace console battery if no digits are shown on LCD.)**

The console is a wireless cycle computer: the transmitter on the frame reads the flywheel magnet and sends to the head unit on the bar. Setting the wheel size and the console functions are on the console pages.
