**The end cap and its screw are pre-installed onto the shaft. Remove them before installing
the pad, and restore them after the pad is mounted.**

The cap retains the pad, and it is fitted at the factory so the shaft does not lose its
screw in transit. There is nothing wrong with the pad - it will not pass the cap.

**Three names for the same part and three names for the pad**, one book to the next:

| Machine | The part on the shaft | The pad | Item numbers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Abdominal | Aluminum end cap | Roll pad | 46, 88 |
| Back extension | aluminum plate | round pad (88) | 47, 79 |
| Leg extension | Aluminum end cover | Leg pad (104) | 53, 84 |
| Prone leg curl | Aluminum end cover | Round Pad (95) | 63, 80 |
| Seated leg curl | Aluminum end cover | Roll pad | 54, 91 |
| CSD ab crunch/back extension, 2025 revision only | Aluminum end cap | Leg Pad (84) | 45, 65 |
| CSD leg extension/leg curl, both 2025 printings | spring, block ring, M20 nut, pull knob, aluminum plate and socket flat head screw | leg pad (104) | 55, 49, 73, 69, 80, 91 |

**Read the item numbers off your own step table**; nothing about them carries across.
The cap itself is Φ50xΦ8.5x11 on the leg extension and prone leg curl, and its screw is
an M8x25 countersunk head hexagon socket screw on both.

## The leg extension/leg curl takes six parts off, not two

Its note reads *"The spring, block ring, M20 nut, pull knob, aluminum plate, and socket
flat head screw, are pre-installed onto the shaft (55, 49, 73, 69, 80, 91). Please remove
them before installing the leg pad (104) and please restore it after mounting the leg
pad."* The item numbers in the sentence are **not in the order the parts are named** - 55
is the spring, 49 the block ring, 73 the M20 nut, 69 the pull knob, 80 the aluminium
plate, 91 the screw. It fits **two** leg pads on that machine, not one.

## An end cap in a parts list is not always this note

The CSD ab crunch/back extension's **February 2024** printing lists an **Aluminum end
cap** (45) and its screw (65) in step 5 and prints **no note**; its **2025 revision** adds
the note above with the same nine-row table. The CSD biceps/triceps book lists the cap and
still prints no note - on that one the cap is simply a part. Check the book before you
tell someone to take a cap off.

The same panel usually carries the seat pad, the D-shape pad and the socket head cap
screws that hold them, and its quantities differ per machine.
