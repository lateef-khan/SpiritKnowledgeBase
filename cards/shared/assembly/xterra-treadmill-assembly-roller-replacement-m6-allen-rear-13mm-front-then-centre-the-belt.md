**TR260 GT75A (9-6) and TRX5500 GT90D (9-6)**, identical:

1. Remove both **adjustment base cover screws** with a screwdriver and take the covers off.
2. Remove the **2 rear roller locking bolts with an M6 L-Allen wrench** and take off the rear roller.
3. Remove the drive motor cover and unhook the drive belt from the front roller.
4. Remove the **front roller locking bolt with a 13 mm wrench** and take off the front roller.
5. **Adjust the running belt to the centre** when installing the front or rear roller back.

**TRX2500 GT90B and TRX3500/4500 GT90CD (11-6)** - the same job with the motor bolts loosened to free the
drive belt:

1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to loosen the **2 screws on the rear adjustment base**.
2. Use an **M6 L Allen wrench** to loosen the **2 screws on the rear roller**.
3. Remove the motor cover; use a **14 mm T-shaped socket wrench** to loosen the 4 motor screws and a **14
   mm open-end wrench** to loosen the 1 belt tension screw, and loosen the drive belt.
4. Use a **13 mm wrench** to loosen the front roller screws; remove the front and back rollers and
   replace. Reassemble in reverse order.
5. When reassembled, the running belt tension needs to be adjusted and centred.
6. With the 14 mm open-end wrench adjust the drive belt tension with a tension measuring device to
   the **white LBS area between 70-75 lb**, then tighten the 4 motor screws with the T-socket wrench.

**The TRX1400 uses an M5 Allen key at the rear and an 11 mm wrench at the front** - its own card. The
running-belt tension figure itself is on the maintenance cards.
