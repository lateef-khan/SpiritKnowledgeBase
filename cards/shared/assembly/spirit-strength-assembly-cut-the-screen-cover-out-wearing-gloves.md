Step two of both i-Strength builds fits the display, and one of its parts arrives as a
blank that you cut:

**Cut out the Screen Cover along the area indicated by the red arrows. Be careful not to
choose the wrong side, and wear gloves during the cutting process** - the cut edges of the
screen cover are sharp, and so is the tool.

**"Not the wrong side" is the part to slow down on.** The cover is symmetrical enough to
fit either way and the arrows are printed on one face only; cut the wrong one and the
part is scrap.

The rest of the step is conventional: the display screen, its fixing base and the lower
and middle communication cable sections go on with **four Phillips screws** and **two
M4x12 Phillips pan head screws**. The Phillips screws and the pan head screws
**arrive pre-installed on the machine** - remove them and restore them during assembly.

Step one, before it, fits the display bracket and screen mounting bracket with a single
pre-installed **M8x15 hexagon socket button head bolt** and the upper and middle
communication cable sections.

The other note on this page is the eight-kilogram limit on pulling the steel cable while
the machine is part-built - see
[that card](spirit-strength-assembly-pull-the-steel-cable-gently-until-the-machine-is-built.md).
