Three tools, and nothing else:

- 13/14mm Wrench
- Combination Wrench
- 4mm L Allen Wrench

**Both printings list the same three.** The panel is word for word identical in the
2019 and 2021 books.

## The combination tool is a screwdriver as well as an Allen key

Step four calls it, twice and in full, the **"Combination M5 ALLEN WRENCH & PHILLIPS
HEAD SCREW DRIVER (127)"**, and uses it both to drive six M8 button-head bolts into the
pedals and to drive the four Phillips screws that hold the seat down. **So this carton
does hold something that will turn a Phillips head**, even though the panel above calls
it only a "Combination Wrench".

**The CRW800 carton panel prints these same three lines and its steps do not say
that.** There the tool is only ever "COMBINATION WRENCH", and its step four sends seven
Phillips screws to a wrench that cannot drive them. The two panels agree and the two
books do not; see
[the CRW800 tools card](../../crw800-2024/assembly/crw800-2024-assembly-tools-included.md).

## The steps name four tool numbers for three tools

| Step | Tool the step names |
|---|---|
| 1 | WRENCH (130) |
| 2 | ALLEN WRENCH (126) |
| 3 | ALLEN WRENCH (130) |
| 4 | Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (127) |

**Item 130 is called a plain wrench in step one and an Allen wrench in step three.**
The panel prints no item number against any of its three lines, so the book never says
which printed tool is 126, 127 or 130, and a reader with three tools in hand cannot
resolve it from this manual.

The book is also inconsistent about the bolt heads. Step one calls item 120 **"BUTTON
HEAD SOCKET BOLTS"** while the CRW800 manual, whose table gives item 120 the same
description of 3/8"X3/4", calls the same item **"HEX HEAD BOLTS"**. A socket head takes a
hex key and a hex head takes the open-end wrench; **the two books disagree about which
this bolt is**, and neither prints a hex size for it.

## The 13/14mm is the one that levels the machine

The maintenance chapter of both books prints the range's usual sentence - two levelling
pads under the rear stabiliser, adjusted with a **14mm wrench** - and the 13/14mm in
this carton opens to 14. See
[the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

## Nothing here is a torque wrench

No step of this build gives a torque figure.
