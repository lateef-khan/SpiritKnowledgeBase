**To fold**: lift the deck until the latch clicks in place.

**To unfold**:

1. Press the tube with your foot at the **yellow sticker** to release the latch.
2. Push the running deck forward with your left hand and pull the release lever
   downward with your right hand.
3. Gently lower the deck to the floor, supporting the deck with a hand.

**To move it**: the treadmill has **four transport wheels that are engaged when
it is folded**. Fold it first, then simply roll the unit away. No need to tilt.

The manuals say nothing about unplugging the power cord first or about holding
the handrails while rolling, though the Sole treadmill manuals do.

**The XT685-2023 does not fold** and is moved a different way - see the card on
moving that machine.
