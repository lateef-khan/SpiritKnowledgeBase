1. **Connecting Arm Covers (L)(R)** over the rod-end and Handle Bar connection with **4pcs of M5 x 15m/m Button Head Socket Screws** and **2pcs of 3.5 x 10m/m Self Tapping Screws** using the Short Phillips Screw Driver.
2. **2pcs of Wheel Covers** with **4pcs of M5 x 15m/m Phillips Head Screws**.
3. **2 pcs of Rear Stabilizer Cover and 2 pcs of Middle Stabilizer Cover** for the Rear Rail Assembly with **4 pcs of M5 x 15m/m Screws** using the Phillips Head Screw Driver; then the **front stabilizer cover** with **2 pcs of M5 x 15m/m Screws**.
4. **Front Handle Bar Covers (L & R) and Rear Handle Bar Covers (L & R)** over the Handle Bars axle connections with **6 pcs of 3.5 x 10m/m Self Tapping Screws** using the Phillips Head Screw Driver.

**Ensure all fasteners are tight after the components have been assembled.** The power connector sits on the front, left hand side of the unit.
