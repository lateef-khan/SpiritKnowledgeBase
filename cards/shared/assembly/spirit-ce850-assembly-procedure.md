## Step zero - remove and throw away the side back

Use the Allen Wrench (No.193) to release the **2 Socket Head Cap Bolts (No.145)**
attached to the main frame for securing the mast tube, then **remove and dispose of
the Side Back (No.28)**.

*Keep the two bolts. Step one puts four of item 145 back in, and only two of them come
out of the hardware bag.*

## Step one - rear rail, console mast and console

1. Place 2 Flat Washers (No.176) against 2 Hex Head Bolts (No.137) and hand-tighten
   them through the **top** of the rear stabilizer tube (main frame) into the Rear
   Rail Assembly (No.2), using the Wrench (No.194).
2. Place 4 Curved Washers (No.187) against 4 Hex Head Bolts (No.139) and hand-tighten
   them through the **front** of the rear stabilizer tube into the Rear Rail Assembly
   (No.2), using Wrench (No.194).
3. Pierce the 14P Computer Cable (No.68) from the bottom of the console mast tube
   through it and pull it out of the top. Use 4 Socket Head Cap Bolts (No.145), 4
   Spring Washers (No.186) and 4 Flat Washers (No.176) to secure.
4. **2022 printing only:** attach the Water Bottle Holder Clamp (No.212) to the
   Console Mast (No.12) with 2 M5x12mm Philips head screws (No.152), then mount the
   Water Bottle Holder (No.213) to the clamp using 2 more (No.152).
5. Untie the Computer Cable (No.68), connect the 2 Handpulse w/Cable Assemblies
   (No.58) and the Handle Wire (Upper), Resistance/Incline (White/Red) (No.61/62) to
   the Console Assembly (No.55). Then place the console on top of the mast and use the
   Phillips head screwdriver (No.192) to tighten 4 Phillips head screws (No.150).

**On the 2020 printing this step has four items, not five.** Item 4 above is absent
and item 5 above is its item 4. Nothing else in the step changed.

## Step two - lower swing arms and connecting arms

1. Locate the Left and Right Lower Swing Arms (No.13, 14) together with 4 Wave Washers
   (No.183) onto the left and right shafts of the console mast tube, then tighten with
   2 Hex Head Bolts (No.136) and two Flat Washers (No.175), using Wrench (No.194).
2. Untie the rod end bearing on the Left Connecting Arm (No.8) and pierce a Hex Head
   Bolt (No.134) through the rod end holes and rod end bearing, then tighten with a
   Nyloc Nut (No.163) using Wrench (No.200) and Wrench (No.201). Repeat for the Right
   Connecting Arm (No.9) and right Lower Handle Bar (No.14).

*Wrenches 200 and 201 are not on the tools panel. See
[the tools card](spirit-ce850-assembly-tools-included.md).*

## Step three - upper swing arms and the handle wires

1. Insert the Left Upper Swing Arm (No.10) onto the Left Lower Swing Arm (No.13) and
   secure with 3 Hex Head Bolts (No.138), 4 Flat Washers (No.176), 2 Curved Washers
   (No.187) and 3 Nyloc Nuts (No.165), using Wrench (No.194) and Wrench (No.195).
   Repeat for the Right Upper Swing Arm (No.11) and Right Lower Swing Arm (No.14).
2. Connect Handle Wire (Upper), Resistance (White, 61) and Handle Wire (Upper),
   Incline (Red, 62) to the 2 Handle Wires (Lower), Resistance/Incline (No.63), shove
   the excess wire into the console mast tube, and plug the Switch Wire Caps (No.97)
   onto the mast tube to secure the wires.

**Three bolts a side, not two**, and the washer counts are not symmetrical - four flat
and two curved for three bolts.

## Step four - covers and end caps

1. Secure the Left Connecting Arm Cover B (No.121) onto the left connecting arm with a
   Phillips head screw (No.152), then the Left Connecting Arm Cover A (No.119) with 2
   Phillips head screws (No.152) onto the lower handle bar. Repeat for the Right
   Connecting Arm Cover B (No.122) and Cover A (No.120).
2. Secure 2 Slide Wheel Covers (No.99) on the left and right pedal arms with 4
   Phillips head screws (No.152).
3. Secure 2 Rear Stabilizer Covers (A) (No.104) and 2 Rear Stabilizer Covers (B)
   (No.105) on both sides of the rear tube of the rear rail assembly with 4 Phillips
   head screws (No.152).
4. Match the Left and Right Console Mast Covers (No.111, 112) with the left and right
   side cases and secure with 2 Sheet Metal Screws (No.154).
5. Match the Front Handle Bar Cover (No.100) and Rear Handle Bar Cover (No.101) on the
   left lower handle bar and tighten 4 Sheet Metal Screws (No.153). Do the same for
   the right (No.102, No.103). **Be careful not to pinch the wire.**
6. Plug both Oval End Caps (No.89) onto both ends of the front stabilizer tube.

*Item 5 is printed "Be carefeul" on both printings. Left as printed.*

## What actually changed between the two printings

**One thing.** The water bottle holder becomes item 4 of step one, the old item 4
becomes item 5, one line is added to the carton list, one label is added to the
machine diagram, and one row - `152 SCREW M5 X 12mm 4` - is added to the step-one
hardware panel. Steps zero, two, three and four are word for word identical, and the
step-four hardware panel did not change.

The 2022 extraction also breaks step one across two columns where the 2020 keeps it
in one. That is a page-layout change, not an instruction change.

**Nothing about the wiring, the bolts or the order of the build is different.**

## The 2024 redesign reprints this build

The **CE850 2024** manual prints all five stages word for word - step zero included -
and its four hardware tables row for row. It prints the **2022** form of step one, the
five-item one with the water bottle holder, not the 2020 four-item form.

Everything this card flags survives: step zero still tells you to dispose of the side
back while step one needs four of item 145 against two in the bag, and item 152 is
still M5 x 12mm in one table and M5 x 15L in another.

**The CS800 stepper of the same redesign opens with a step zero of its own**, and it
is a different instruction on a different part number. Do not answer one from the
other; see
[the step-zero card](spirit-2024-assembly-step-zero-shipping-bracket.md).
