**Every axle here must be aligned when it goes back** - the book says so twice (steps 4 and 7).

1. Follow the pedal assembly replacement to take off the pedal assembly.
2. Remove the **slide wheel covers** with a screwdriver.
3. Use a **14 mm box wrench and an M8 Allen wrench** to remove the **pedal arm locking bolt**, then take it off.
4. Use a **12 mm box wrench** to unmount the **slide wheel**. *The axle must be aligned when installing the slide
   wheel back.*
5. Use a **12 mm box wrench** to remove the bolts locking **Swing Arm A** on the pedal arm **bushing housing**,
   then take off the axle.
6. Use a **12 mm box wrench** to remove the **bushing housing locking bolt**, then take it off.
7. Reverse the steps to install all parts. *The axle needs to be aligned when installing.*

No bolt sizes are printed. Noise from a pedal arm is usually a bearing: the troubleshooting chapter says to
replace the pedal arm's bearing or the slide wheels' bearing.

The XS300B-YS006 service manual is one book for both XS895 owner's-manual printings; the two-column page is read by step number.
