**Set the new incline motor to 195 mm before you fit it.** The TR260 and TRX1400 books say 165 mm for
their motor; do not mix the two.

**TRX2500 GT90B and TRX3500/4500 GT90CD (11-9)**:

1. First adjust the treadmill to the folded position, then proceed with the old incline motor's
   removal.
2. **Incline range must be adjusted to 195 mm minimum prior to installation.**
3. Use a **14 wrench** (14 mm) to assemble the new incline motor.
4. Connect the incline motor wiring with the controller: **red wire to "UP", white wire to "COM", black
   wire to "DOWN"**.

**TRX5500 GT90D (9-9)**: take off the motor cover, then fold the treadmill; unplug the incline motor wires
and unmount the motor; adjust the spare motor **to lower (195 mm)**; install it with a **14 mm open-end
wrench**; plug the wires to the controller - **Red to UP, White to COM, Black to DOWN**.

The Sole F-series books for the same Dyaco platform also set 195 mm; the Spirit XT books set 225 or
210 mm. No torque is printed.
