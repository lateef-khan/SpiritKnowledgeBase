1. Use a **14 mm wrench** to loosen both hex head bolts and remove the **seat (19)**.
2. Turn the **brake tension knob (86)** out of the **fix plate (8)** and remove the **sliding seat mount (7)**.
3. To refit, install the sliding seat mount (7) on the **seat slider (6)**, then put the fix plate (8) on it. Install the brake tension knob (86) from the bottom upward into the fix plate (8) and tighten it with the **3/8" x 7T nut (78)**. Last, insert the seat (19) into the sliding tube and tighten both hex head bolts with the 14 mm wrench.

**The CU800 (2020) and CU800ENT books print step 2 twice** - once as "Use 14 mm wrench to take Brake Tension Knob (86) apart from Fix Plate (8)" and again as "Turn to take Brake Tension Knob (86) apart". The XBU55 books print the "Turn" version only. It is one step.

No size is printed for the two seat bolts beyond the 14 mm wrench that fits them.
