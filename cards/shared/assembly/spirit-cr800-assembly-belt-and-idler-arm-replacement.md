**Belt figure: 180 to 205 Hz on an audio tension gauge.** The 2009 XR898 and the XBR95 print 180 +/- 10 Hz for the same J-bolt; that is a different card.

1. Take both left and right front shrouds (29) (30) off.
2. Use a **13 mm wrench** to release the **M8 x 7T nut (88)** and take off the **J-bolt (87)**.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **3 pcs of M6 x 7T Phillips head screws** on the **idle arm (10)**, and the **belt (54)** can be taken off.
4. To release the idle arm (10), unscrew **3 pcs of M6 x 15 Phillips head screws (98)** with **3 pcs of Ø1/4" split washers (80)** and **3 pcs of 1/4" x 13 x 1T (72)**.
5. To refit the belt (54) and the idle arm (10), use a Phillips head screwdriver to secure the **generator flywheel (55)** with 3 pcs of M6 x 15 Phillips head screws together with 3 pcs of Ø1/4" split washers (80) and 3 pcs of 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers (72). Turn the **drive pulley (20)** to run the belt onto the pulley, hook the J-bolt on the mainframe and use the 13 mm wrench to tighten the M8 x 7T nut (88) on the J-bolt until the belt (54) has acceptable tension. **Use an audio tension gauge to make sure the frequency is within 180 to 205 Hz.** Last, turn the drive pulley (20) and make sure the drive belt (54) is set in the centre without falling off the pulley.

**Two defects in the printed steps, in both books.** Step 3 calls the idle-arm screws "M6 x 7T Phillips head screws" and step 4 calls the same three "M6 x 15 Phillips head screws (98)". Step 4 ends with "3 pcs of 1/4" x 13 x 1T (72)" and no part name; step 5 supplies "flat washers".

**The 2020 CR800 book's own belt-slipping answer prints the range as "180~20HZ"** - a dropped digit for 180 to 205 Hz. That adjustment and the belt-falling-off advice are the maintenance section's; the figure to set after this replacement is the one printed here.
