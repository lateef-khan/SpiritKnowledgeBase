1. Insert the **Slide Rail (2)** into the **Main Frame (1)** and fix it with **4 Allen Bolts (92, M8 x 15)** - the parts lists call 92 a flat head screw.
2. Fit **2 flat washers and 2 Allen Bolts (46, M8 x 15)** on the slide rail and tighten. Tool: the S5 Allen key (99 / 84).

| Book | The two washers | Extra in this step |
|---|---|---|
| ERG160 (2020) | 44, OD20 x ID8.5 x 1.5 | Stop Brackets (11 and 15) onto the rail with 2 Self-tapping Screws (59) before the washers and bolts |
| ERG180 (2023) | 55, OD16 x ID8.5 x 1.5 | none - the machine has no stop brackets |

Six bolts in total either way. The step text on both books names the washers and bolts in that order ("Attach the Flat Washers (44) and 2 Allen Bolts (46)"); the drawing puts the washers under the bolt heads.
