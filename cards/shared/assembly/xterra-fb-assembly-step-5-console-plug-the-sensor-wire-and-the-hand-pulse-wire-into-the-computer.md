No loose hardware.

| Book | Step 1 | Step 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Upright (step 5) | Insert the computer wire (8) into the handlebar (4), connect the middle sensor wire (19) with the computer (08). | Plug hand pulse wire (18) into the back of computer (8). |
| Recumbent (step 6) | Insert the computer (08) to the handlebar (04), connect the upper sensor wire (19) with the computer (08). | Plug the hand pulse wire (18) into the hole on the back of the computer (08). |

Wire 19 is called the "middle sensor wire" in one book and the "upper sensor wire" in the other; it is the same wire and the same connection. The 2019 and 2025 books deliver the console already fitted to the handlebar tube and make this connection inside the handlebar step.
