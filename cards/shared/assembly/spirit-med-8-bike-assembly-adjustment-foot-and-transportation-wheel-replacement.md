1. As shown in figure 6.16.1 (8.5R: 6.9.1), remove the **button head socket bolt and nylon nut** with an **Allen wrench and an open-end wrench** to replace the transportation wheel.
2. As shown in figure 6.16.2 (8.5R: 6.9.2), **turn the adjustment foot counterclockwise** to remove and replace it. *(Adjust the height of the adjustment pads when the unit is not horizontal, until it balances.)*

No sizes are printed. The parts list carries **Adjustment Foot (27), 3 pcs** and **Transportation Wheel (28), 2 pcs**. The troubleshooting chapter sends a bike that wobbles as a whole to figure 6.16.2 (8.5R: 6.9.2) to adjust the pads. The owner's manuals level the same feet with a half-inch wrench on the top nut; that is the setting-up card.
