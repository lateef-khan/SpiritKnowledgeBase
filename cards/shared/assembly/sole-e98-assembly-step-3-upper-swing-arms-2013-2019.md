1. **Insert the Left Upper Swing Arm (10) into the Lower Swing Arm (25).** Fasten with **three Hex Head Bolts (190)**, **four Flat Washers (158)**, **two Curved Washers (164)**, and **three Nyloc Nuts (145)** using **Wrenches (169 & 172)**.
2. **Attach the wire (186) from the left Upper Swing Arm (10, Incline buttons on front)** to the **wire (185)** exiting the Console Mast Tube (12). **Slide the switch wire cap (189)** onto the wire with the wide side facing the Swing Arm, stuff the excess wire into the tube, then snap the cap in.
3. Repeat on the right side, connecting **(186) to (184)**.
