Hardware: **#98** M6 x 15mm Phillips Head Screw (4 pcs), **#99** M5 x 12mm Phillips Head Screw (8 pcs), **#105** Ø4 x 16mm Sheet Metal Screw (4 pcs). The LCR 2011 hardware box beside this step calls #98 an **M5** x 15mm screw while its own checklist calls it M6 - see the LCR 2011 checklist card.

1. Install the Seat (61) on the Seat Carriage (4) with four Phillips Head Screws (98), using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (114).
2. Install the Front Stabilizer Cover (32) and the Rear Stabilizer Cover (37) on the Main Frame (1) with four Phillips Head Screws (99).
3. Insert the Computer Cable (44) and Hand Pulse Cable (45) into the connector in the back of the Console Assembly (19). Install the console onto the mounting plate with four Phillips Head Screws (99). **Be careful not to pinch the cables between the console and the mounting plate - this could short the cables and damage the electronics.**
4. Install the Drink Bottle Holders (39 L, 38 R) onto the left and right sides of the Seat Handle Bar (6) with four Sheet Metal Screws (105).
5. Install the Pedals (116 L, 117 R) onto the Cranks using the **14/15mm Wrench (132)**. **The left Pedal (116 L) has a reverse thread** and screws into the crank in the opposite rotation from normal threads. There is an "L" stamped into the end of the threaded post of the left pedal and an "R" in the right. Make sure to fasten the pedals as tightly as you can.
6. It may be necessary to re-tighten the pedals if you feel a thump while pedalling. A clicking or thumping noise while pedalling is usually caused by the pedals being too loose.

Eight #99 screws: four for the stabilizer covers, four for the console.

**The re-tightening advice is its own numbered instruction (6) here.** In the R92 manuals it is the tail of the pedal instruction.
