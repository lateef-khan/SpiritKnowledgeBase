**After the weight stack is installed, lubricate the Guide Rods before attaching the
weight stack shrouds.**

The order is the point. Once the front, back and upper shrouds are on, the rods are behind
them, and re-lubricating means taking the covers off again. The note appears in the same
panel as the stack fitting order, so it is easy to read past.

**Sixteen of these nineteen manuals name no lubricant in this panel.** Three do: the
**CSS-LATP, CSS-LEXT and CSS-SQSC** print the same step as *"lubricate the Guide Rods
using **a thin coat of clear silicone** prior to attaching the weight stack shrouds"* -
`spirit-strength-maintenance-guide-rods-take-a-thin-coat-of-clear-silicone`. **No manual
gives an interval here.** What the books do is put *Lubricate - Guide Rods* in the
preventative-maintenance table as a **monthly** job, and *Cleaning - Guide Rod* as monthly
too, naming no lubricant on that page in any of the thirty-five machines that have the row -
`spirit-strength-maintenance-guide-rods-lubricated-monthly-and-no-lubricant-named`. Those
are maintenance facts and live on the maintenance cards.

See [the stack fitting order](spirit-strength-assembly-weight-stack-fitting-order.md) and
[the shroud order](spirit-strength-assembly-connect-the-battery-before-the-shrouds-go-on.md).

The **CSD-CPSP chest press / shoulder press prints this note only in its May 2025 update** (4601US, 20250521); its February 2024 printing has no notes on the weight-stack page at all.

The **CSS-DELT's 2026 revision** (SP-4312, 20260821, whose text layer is a shifted font - read from its OCR supplements and renders) prints this exactly as its 2025 printing does.
