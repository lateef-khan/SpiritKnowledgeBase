Place your feet **between the aluminium surface of the pedal and the nylon foot strap**
that wraps around it.

- **Opening too narrow:** depress the **spring-loaded clasp** with one hand and pull on
  the nylon strap with the other to increase the opening.
- **Too loose, or to tighten:** depress the spring-loaded clasp, then pull on the **open
  end** of the nylon strap until it is snug around each foot.

The clasp must be depressed to move the strap in either direction. The CB900 heads the
page **Dual Function Pedals** because the same pedal takes a cleated shoe on its other
face; see
[the cleat card](spirit-cycle-assembly-cleat-and-clip-in-binding-tension.md).

## The CIC800 and CIC850 straps work differently

Those two books describe a **toe clip** you tighten by pulling the strap up, and the
CIC850 adds a nylon-strap paragraph with **no clasp at all** - "pull the end of the
adjustable strap down to tighten, or pull the strap up to loosen". Do not apply the
clasp instruction to them. See
[that card](spirit-cic-assembly-pedal-straps-and-toe-clips.md).
