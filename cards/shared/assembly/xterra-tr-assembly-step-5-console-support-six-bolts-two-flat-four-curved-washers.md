Install the **console support (5)** into the **uprights (L, R) (3, 4)** with **6 button head socket
bolts (87), 2 flat washers (79) and 4 curved washers (88)**, using the combination M5 Allen wrench and
Phillips screwdriver (104). The TR260 adds: the two flat washers go "on the flat interior side", and
"Be careful not to pinch the computer cables when attaching the console."

**Then tighten all screws** - this is the last hardware step, and the step-3 upright bolts were left
loose until now (the TR260 prints "NOTE: Please tighten all screws after assembly is complete").

**The TR260 page contradicts itself on the bolt count.** Its sentence reads "with the **4** BUTTON HEAD
SOCKET BOLTS (87) and 4 CURVED WASHERS (88), and 2 FLAT WASHERS (79)", but its hardware sidebar says
**Bolt (87) Qty: 6**, its drawing shows six bolt callouts, and its pack checklist holds twelve bolts of
which step 3 used six. The TR150 sentence, sidebar and pack list all agree on six. Six is the figure
this card carries; the TR260's "4" is a misprint. The same TR260 sidebar labels the flat washer
"(87)" where the sentence and the pack list say (79).

The TR260 file also carries an unprinted Chinese/English ghost layer that reads "6pcs … 2pcs flat …
4pcs curved" - it agrees with the sidebar, not the printed sentence.
