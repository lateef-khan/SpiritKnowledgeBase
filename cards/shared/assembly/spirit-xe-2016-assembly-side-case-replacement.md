**The XE195 and XE295 books print the same screws; they disagree on which cover gets the flat washer at refit.**
The XE395 books use nine `3.5x16` sheet metal screws and an AC power module instead - a different procedure.

1. Remove the pedal arm and console mast assembly (refer to 11.4 and 11.5).
2. Unscrew the **left chain cover** by releasing **three tapping screws 5x19mm** and disconnect **two red connecting
   wires** and **one green ground wire**, to take the left chain cover apart.
3. Unscrew the **right chain cover** on the mainframe by releasing **three tapping screws 5x19mm**.
4. Further release the **sheet metal screw 3.5x16mm** together with the **flat washer 1/4" x 19** which is inside the
   right chain cover.
5. Release the **nut of the DC power plug**, then pull out the DC power cables, and **chain cover (L)** can be released.
6. Reassemble in the reverse order.

**Which cover takes the `5/16" x 23 x 1.5T` flat washer at refit is printed two ways.** The XE195 book's refit line
says it "is to be placed on the **left** chain cover", although its own step 4 took the washer out of the **right**
cover. The XE295 book's refit line reads: "Have to pay attention that DC cable doesn't clip by (L)(R) Chain Cover
& grind to round disk. (**Right** chain cover need to put on Flat Washer 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T)". The XE295 book agrees
with itself; put the washer back where you took it from.

Both books print a broken step 2 - a run-together copy of steps 3 and 4 ("Unscrew left Chain Cover by releasing
three Tapping Screws 5x19mm and disconnect / Unscrew right Chain Cover ...") - before the real steps. It is
dropped above.
