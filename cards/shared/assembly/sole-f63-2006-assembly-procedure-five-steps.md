Five unillustrated assembly steps, one hardware list for all of them. This book prints no unpacking warning and no Velcro-strap mention.

1. Remove all parts from the carton.
2. Connect Computer Cable (Lower) to Computer Cable (Middle).
3. Hand-tighten the right and left uprights into the frame base with 8 pcs of 5/16 x 1/2 Button Head Socket Screws, using the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Screwdriver.
4. Connect Computer Cable (Middle) to Computer Cable (Upper); connect Speed Adjustment Switch W/Cable to Speed Cable (Upper); connect Incline Adjustment Switch W/Cable to Incline Cable (Upper).
5. Install the Console Support into the right and left uprights with 4 pcs of 5/16 x 1/2 Button Head Socket Screws.

Lock down all hardware after the accessories are assembled.
