Three tools, printed as a TOOLS INCLUDED list beside the unpacking steps:

- **14/15mm Wrench**
- **12/14mm Wrench**
- **Phillips Screwdriver**

All three printings list the same three, and **no Allen key of any size**. This build
uses hex-head and Phillips heads throughout; nothing in it takes a hex key.

## The upright carton reads 13/15mm, not 14/15mm

The CU800, CU800ENT and CU800 2012 cartons hold a **13/15mm** wrench where this holds
a 14/15mm; the 12/14mm and the screwdriver are the same. One character apart, and the
bolt heads differ with them. See
[the upright tools card](spirit-cu800-assembly-tools-included.md).

## The steps name tools by item number, not by size

The CR800ENT steps call for "the Wrench (No.132)" and "the Philips Screwdriver
(No.114)"; the CR800 2021 and 2023 steps name no tool at all. The item numbers are
parts-list numbers for the packed tools, not sizes.

**A 14mm is in this carton**, in the 14/15mm and the 12/14mm - unusually for a Spirit
machine, the wrench the levelling sentence asks for is actually in the box. See
[the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).
