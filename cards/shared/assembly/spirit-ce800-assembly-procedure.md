## Step one - rear rail and centre rail

1. Slide the Rail Assembly (No.2) under the rear oval stabilizer tube of the main
   frame. Secure with 2 Bolts (No.106) and 2 Flat Washers (No.109) from the top of
   the tube and 4 Bolts (No.107) and 4 Curved Washers (No.108) from the front. Make
   sure all 6 bolts are tightened securely.
2. Install the Center Aluminum Rail (No.72) with 2 Bolts (No.107) and 2 Curved
   Washers (No.108) at the rear, and 2 Bolts (No.106) and 2 Flat Washers (No.109) at
   the front.
3. Install the 2 Tube Stabilizer Covers (No.64 L & 65 R) with 2 Screws (No.78) to
   the middle stabilizer tube, and the 2 Stabilizer Covers (No.64 L & 65 R) with 2
   Screws (No.78) to the rear stabilizer tube.

*Step 3 names the same pair of item numbers, 64 and 65, for both the middle tube and
the rear tube, and asks for two screws each out of the four in the step. Both
printings do this. There is no third and fourth item number printed anywhere to
correct it with.*

## Step two - footplates and pedal arms

1. Attach Footplates (No. L 48) to the pedal mounting plate with 4 Screws (No.110).
2. Slide the Pedal Arm Assembly (No.5) onto the crank axle of the main body. Do not
   force it - the bearings can be damaged. Secure with a Bolt (No.97) and Flat Washer
   (No.87).
3. Install the Cover (No.46) over the pedal arm connection at the rotation axle with
   a Screw (No.103).
4. Install the Roller Wheel Cover (No.50) with 2 Screws (No.78).
5. Repeat all steps with the opposite Pedal Arm Assembly (No.6).

## Step three - console mast and swing arms

1. Unravel the Computer Cable (30-15), snake it through the bottom of the Console
   Mast (12) and out the top. Insert three M8x25mm Screws (75) into the mainframe,
   locate the Console Mast (12) on its position and tighten those three. The last
   M8x25mm screw (75) goes in with the 5/16" flat washer (88).
2. Mount the Console Mast Covers (L 61) (R 62) with 4 Screws (113).
3. Install 2 Wave Washers (148) onto the console mast axle, then install the Swing
   Arms (L 10) (R 11) onto the axles. Do not force them or use a hammer - the
   bearings can be damaged. Secure with a Bolt (117) and Washer (120) on each arm.
4. Attach the bottom of the swing arms to the rod ends of the pedal arms using 2
   Shoulder Bolts (119) and 2 Nylon Nuts (118).
5. **2023 printing only:** attach the Water Bottle Holder Clamp (160) to the Console
   Mast (12) with 2 M5x12mm Philips head screws (78), then mount the Water Bottle
   Holder (161) to the clamp with 2 more (78). See
   [the water bottle holder card](spirit-ce-assembly-water-bottle-holder.md).

**The 2021 printing does not tell you to fit the console mast at all.** Its step
three starts at "Mount the Console Mast Covers" - what is item 1 above is simply
absent, and so is the instruction to snake the computer cable up the mast. Its own
hardware panel for the step nevertheless lists **4 screws M8x25mm (75) and 1 flat
washer 5/16" (88)**, which nothing in its printed text uses. Follow item 1 above on
either printing; the 2023 book is the one that prints it.

## Step four - console and beauty covers

1. Plug the Computer Cable (No.30-15) and Heart Rate Cables (No.35 x 2) into the
   corresponding connectors on the back of the Console (No.30). Mount the Console to
   the mast with 4 Screws (78).
2. Install 4 Swing Arm Covers (No.57/58 L & 59/60 R) with 6 Screws (No.113).
3. Install 4 Covers (No.53 x 2 & 54 x 2) to the end of the **right** pedal arm with
   4 Screws (No.78) and 4 Screws (No.113). **Do not install these upside down** -
   there is an arrow on the inside of each cover showing the direction.
4. Install the 4 Covers (No.55 x 2 & 56 x 2) at the bottom of the **left** pedal arm
   with 4 Screws (No.113) and 4 Screws (No.78).
5. Install the Front Stabilizer Cover (No.47) with 2 Screws (No.78).

*Step 4 asks for six screws in item 2 out of a panel that supplies 14 of each type,
and steps 3 and 4 together account for sixteen. The counts do not close; fit what
the covers take.*

## What actually changed between the two printings

Three things, and only three:

1. **The water bottle holder** - a new item 5 in step three, a new line in the parts
   panel, and 4 more M5x12mm screws in the step-three hardware table.
2. **The console mast step** described above, printed for the first time in 2023.
3. **Wording.** `(No. 61L & 62R)` became `(L 61) (R62)` and `(No.10L &11R)` became
   `(L10) (R11)`. Same parts, same order, same hardware. It changes nothing.

The **hardware-table column headers did not change**: both printings head every panel
PART, TYPE, DESCRIPTION, QTY in that order. In the 2023 text extraction the step-three
header appears broken across the heading line, which reads as a swap and is not one.

Steps one, two and four are word for word identical between the printings.

## The 2024 redesign reprints this build

The **CE800 2024** manual prints these four steps word for word, the water bottle
holder step included, and its four hardware tables row for row. The two things this
card already flags are both still there in 2024: step three's console mast still goes
on with three screws and then a fourth with a washer, and step one still names items
**64 and 65** for both the middle stabilizer tube and the rear one, asking for two
screws each out of four.

**The touchscreen CE800ENT 2024 builds the same machine with different item numbers.**
Its step one secures a **Rail Support Assembly (No.3)** where this one secures a
**Center Aluminum Rail (No.72)**, and every fastener number differs, though the
quantities match line for line. Take the numbers from the book in front of you; see
[the touchscreen build](../../ce800ent/assembly/ce800ent-assembly-procedure.md).
