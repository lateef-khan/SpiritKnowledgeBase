Hardware: **#71** 3/8" x 1-3/4" Hex Head Bolt (2 pcs), **#77** 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T Flat Washer (4 pcs), **#89** 3/8" x 7T Nyloc Nut (4 pcs), **#175** 3/8" x 2-3/4" Hex Head Bolt (2 pcs).

1. Install the Seat Handle Bar (6) onto the Seat Carriage (4) with **two vertical Hex Head Bolts (71)**, **two horizontal Hex Head Bolts (175)**, four Flat Washers (77) and four Nyloc Nuts (89), using the **12/14mm Wrench (112)** and the **14/15mm Wrench (132)**.
2. Plug the two Hand pulse Cables (21) and (27) into the rear two ports (26) located on the **left side rear cover, just under the seat** - see the inset illustration.

The two bolt lengths are not interchangeable: #71 is 1-3/4" and goes in vertically, #175 is 2-3/4" and goes in horizontally.

**The R92 2019 and 2023 print the same hardware.** The 2019 instruction is word for word the same as this one and has its own card; the 2023 instruction calls #89 a "Nylon Nut" instead of a Nyloc Nut and rewrites the cable paragraph.
