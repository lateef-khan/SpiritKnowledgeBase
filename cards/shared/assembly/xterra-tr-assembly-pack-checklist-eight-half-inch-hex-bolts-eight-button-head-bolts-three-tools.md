| Step | Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | #99 | 5/16" x 1/2" Hex Head Bolt | 8 |
| 2 | #100 | Ø5/16" x Ø18 x 1.5T Flat Washer | 8 |
| 3 | #125 | 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 |
| 3 | #80 | M8 x 1.5T Split Washer (the TRX books print Ø8 x 1.5T) | 4 |
| 4 | #125 | 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 |

**Assembly tools:** #102 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver; #103 M6 L Allen
Wrench; #90 13 mm Wrench (the TRX books print "13L_Wrench").

The TR300 and TR6.4 pages sort the hardware by step as above; the TRX2500 and TRX3500 pages list the
same items in one row, with #125 as **8 pcs** and each tool as (1pc). **The TRX2500 and TRX3500 pages
mislabel #125 "BoltHex Head Bolt"** - the drawing beside it and every step that uses it show a button
head socket bolt. No safety key, lubricant or power cord is drawn on any of these four checklists.
