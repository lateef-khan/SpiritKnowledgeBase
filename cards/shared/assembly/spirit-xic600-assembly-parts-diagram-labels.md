The page carries no heading and no letters - each callout is the part name itself:

- **WATER BOTTLE HOLDER**
- **HANDLE BARS**
- **CONSOLE**
- **SEAT**
- **MAIN FRAME**
- **FLYWHEEL**
- **FRONT STABILIZER**
- **FOOT PEDALS**
- **LEVELERS** (printed twice, once on each stabilizer)
- **REAR STABILIZER**

Both printings label the same drawing identically.

## LEVELERS is the only place the word appears in the assembly chapter

The drawing names them; nothing in the five steps adjusts them, and the manual gives
no moving or levelling section. The one instruction that mentions the levelers is in
the maintenance chapter - see
[that card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

## The drawing is not a carton list

The seat, the pedals and the handlebars are drawn in place on a finished machine. For
what is packed loose see
[the parts card](spirit-xic600-assembly-parts-included.md).
