1. Remove the motor, as in steps 4.3.1 to 4.3.5 of the motor procedure.
2. Remove the **press pulley** - the idler - "by removing the C with the tool": pull the retaining C-clip with
   the tool the figure shows.
3. Fit the new idler, then reassemble in the order of removal.

That is the whole procedure. It names no clip size and no tool, and it ends before the belt is re-tensioned;
the motor procedure's step 4.3.7 (13 mm T-socket, 120 ~ 130 Hz) is where that happens on the way back.

**The CT1000ENT book has no idler procedure.** Its version of this guide goes from the incline motor straight
to the hand pulse set, so this card does not list that machine.

The guide is a machine translation. Its words, as used on this card: **electronic watch** is the console,
**cross screwdriver** is a Phillips screwdriver, **umbrella head screws** are the guide's name for the
console and cover screws (no size is printed), and a **17th T-Shirt** or **13th T-sleeve** is a 17 mm or 13 mm
T-handle socket.
