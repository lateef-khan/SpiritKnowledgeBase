Ten books, two wordings, one set of figures. The 2015 books and the 2023 books print the steps differently;
every screw count, tool size, tension and wire colour is the same.

1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **5 tapping screws** securing the motor cover, and remove it.
2. Remove the motor **grounding wire (greenish yellow)**, the **motor wire (+) red** and the **motor wire (-)
   black**.
3. Use a **14mm T-shaped socket wrench** to loosen the **4 motor screws**, and a **14mm open end wrench** to
   loosen the **1 belt tension screw**. Remove the motor and replace it with the new one.
4. Reassemble in reverse order and re-hook the belt, **readjusting the belt to be parallel with the main
   frame** after re-hooking. **Do not tighten the 4 securing screws yet.**
5. Use the 14mm open end wrench to adjust the belt tension with a tension measuring device until it reads in
   the **white LBS area between 70-75 LBS**. Then use the 14mm T-shaped socket wrench to tighten the 4
   securing screws.
6. Connect the grounding wire and the motor wires: **red to M+, black to M-**.

How the 2023 books say the same thing: "Loose 5 Motor cover locking screws with a screwdriver"; "Unmount
Drive motor ground wire and 2 input wires (black and red wire)"; "Remove 4 Drive motor Locking bolts with a
14mm T-type socket spanner and loose Drive belt. Then do the reverse move to mount Drive motor back and 4
locking bolts but do not secure"; "Adjust Drive belt tension with a 14mm open-end wrench. Measure belt
tension with a tension meter. The tension needs to be about 70~75LBS"; "Plug Drive motor 2 input wires to
Controller (Red to M+ / Black to M-) and mount ground wire". The XT185 and XT285 2023 books say "14mm
wrench" rather than "14mm T-type socket spanner" for the four bolts - same size, same bolts.

Damage in two of the sources: **the XT385 2015 and XT485 2015 pages cut step 2 off after "motor"** - the
line reads "Remove motor grounding wire (greenish yellow), motor" and stops, on the printed page as well as in
the extraction. The wire colours above come from their step 4, which names them again, and from the other
eight books.

No torque is printed for any bolt, the tension gauge is not named, and the belt-tension figure is printed
only as a range with no tolerance. The XT485ENT is not on this card: its motor procedure loosens a side
tension screw and a rear drive-belt tension screw, and has its own card.
