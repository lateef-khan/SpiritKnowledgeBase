**The pedal assembly axle must align with the pedal bracket** when it goes back (step 8).

1. Follow the chain cover replacement to remove both chain covers.
2. Use a **17 mm box wrench and an M6 Allen wrench** to remove the **rod end bolt** that links the pedal assembly and
   the pedal bar assembly.
3. Use a **12 mm box wrench** to remove the **pedal assembly axle locking bolt**.
4. Take off the pedal assembly.
5. Use a **19 mm box wrench** to loosen the **rod end bearing nut**, then remove it.
6. Use a screwdriver to remove the **4 pedal locking screws**, then take off the pedal.
7. Use a **12 mm box wrench** to remove the **pedal bar assembly locking bolt and washer**, then take it off.
8. Reverse the steps to install everything back. *The pedal assembly axle must align with the pedal bracket.*

No bolt sizes are printed, only wrench sizes. Noise from the pedal assembly, the troubleshooting chapter says, is
first a matter of the pedal locking screws and the foam tape on top of the assembly; a rod end bearing with
clearance is replaced.

The XS300B-YS006 service manual is one book for both XS895 owner's-manual printings; the two-column page is read by step number.
