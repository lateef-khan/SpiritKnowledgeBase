**The XE395 and XE395ENT books print this word for word. The new motor is zeroed to 204 +/- 1 mm between its two
hole sites; the Sole E35-2016 twin zeroes the same motor to 207 +/- 1 mm, and the CE850/XE895 stride books to
245 +/- 1 mm.**

1. Remove the rear frame (refer to 11.10).
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **screws M5x15mm** from the **rear bar cover**, **inclinable rail
   cover** and **incline cover**, then remove the parts.
3. Use **two 14 mm wrenches** to remove the **hex head bolt 3/8" x 2-1/2"** together with the **flat washer 3/8" x 19 x
   1.5T**, **nyloc nut 3/8" x 7T** and **two nylon washers Ø3/8" x Ø35 x 5T** which secure the **incline tube** and
   **rear rail assembly**.
4. Use a **14 mm wrench** to release **2 pcs M10x130mm hex head bolts**, **Ø3/8" x Ø19 x 1.5T flat washer** and
   **3/8" x 7T nyloc nut** fixed on the incline motor.
5. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **tapping screw 5x19** from the incline motor **grounding wire**. Cut
   the **wire tie mount** and remove the incline motor.
6. Use a **12 mm wrench** to remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 1** and **flat washer 5/16" x 35 x 2T** from the rail
   assembly, and remove the rail assembly.
7. If removal of the **aluminum rail** is needed, remove the **Phillips head screws M5x15mm** from the aluminum rail.
8. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly. **Be sure the grounding wire of the incline motor is firmly
   secured on the frame tube of the rear frame.**

**Zero the new motor before fitting it (printed in 9-7 Controller & Incline Motor Problem, not here):** make sure the
incline motor is reset to zero when you install a new one. Rotate the **incline barrel clockwise to the end**, then
rotate the same barrel **counterclockwise one and a half circles**. Make sure the distance of the two hole sites is
**204 +/- 1 mm**.

Step 4 names a 14 mm wrench for `M10x130mm` bolts with a `3/8"` washer and nut; the Sole twin prints two 17 mm
wrenches and an `M10 x 8T` nut at the same joint. Quoted as printed.
