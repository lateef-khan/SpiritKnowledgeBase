> Leveling
>
> Use a M14 Wrench to adjust the height of the Leveling Feet.

**One sentence, and the whole of it.** All four 2023 residential bike manuals print it
word for word in the same place, at the head of the SETTING UP YOUR BIKE page that
follows the last assembly step.

**It does not say how many feet there are, where they are, or which way to turn them.**
It does not say whether the wrench is in the carton.

## No carton on this card holds an M14

The XBR55 2023 and XBR95 2023 cartons hold a 12/14mm, a 13/15mm, a 13/14mm and a Phillips
screwdriver; the XBR25 2023 carton a 12/14mm, a 14/15mm and a screwdriver; the XBU55 2023
carton a 12/14mm, a 13/15mm and a screwdriver. **A 12/14mm is in all four and it opens to
14**, so the job can be done with what is in the box - but no wrench in any of them is
labelled M14. See [the XBR55 tools card](spirit-xbr55-assembly-tools-included.md),
[the XBR25 and XBR95 tools card](spirit-cr800-assembly-tools-included.md) and
[the XBU55 tools card](spirit-cu800-assembly-tools-included.md).

## Three of these four books answer the same question twice

The XBR25 2023, XBR55 2023 and XBU55 2023 manuals **also** print the older Spirit
sentence in their troubleshooting chapter: *there are 2 leveling pads on the bottom of
the rear stabilizer, use a 14mm wrench (or adjustable wrench) to adjust the levelers.*
That one gives the count and the location this one does not; this one is the setting-up
instruction and that one is the noise remedy. **Neither page mentions the other.** See
[that card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

**The XBR95 2023 prints only this one.** Its maintenance chapter has no noise item at
all, so nothing in that book says how many levelling feet the machine has or where they
are.

## The ten older residential bikes have no setting-up page

The XBR25 2019, XBR55 2019 and 2021, XBR55ENT, XBR95 2016, 2018 and 2021, XBU55 2019 and
2021 and XBU55ENT manuals print no SETTING UP YOUR BIKE page and no M14 instruction. See
[the absence card](spirit-xb-assembly-no-moving-or-transport-section.md).
