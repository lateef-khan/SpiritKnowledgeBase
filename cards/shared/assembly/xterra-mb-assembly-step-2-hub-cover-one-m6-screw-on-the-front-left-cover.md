Hardware step 2: **#124 M6 x 12 mm screw (1)**.

1. Install the Cover Front (L) (95) with one Phillips head screw (124) using the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (117).

The 2018 book heads this step "Hub Cover"; the 2014 book heads it "Rear Stabilizer Cover". The part is the same: the front left cover (95) drawn over the flywheel hub.
