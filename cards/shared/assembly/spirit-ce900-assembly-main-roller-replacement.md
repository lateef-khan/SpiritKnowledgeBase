**The CE900 family's rollers come out as a bolted group, not on a circlip.** The XE-series slide wheels are held
by a `Ø17` circlip and wave washers; nothing from that card applies here.

1. Loosen the **M5 screw (3 pcs)** to remove the left and right side of **carriage base cover B** (figure 6.13.1).
2. Loosen the **M5 screw (3 pcs)** to remove **carriage base cover A** (figure 6.13.2).
3. Remove the **M8 screw (4 pcs)** using the tool to remove the **slide guard** (figure 6.13.3).
4. Release the **M10 screws (6 pcs)** on both sides (figures 6.13.4 and 6.13.5); the **slide wheel group** can then
   be removed and replaced.

The three books print this word for word. No refit line is printed. The troubleshooting chapter (7.2 / 13.2) sends
you here to clean the sliding wheel surfaces.
