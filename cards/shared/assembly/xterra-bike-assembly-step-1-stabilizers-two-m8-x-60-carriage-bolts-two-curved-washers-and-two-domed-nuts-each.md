| | Recumbent (2022) | Upright (2023) |
|---|---|---|
| Carriage bolt M8 x 60L | No. 15, 4 pcs | No. 09, 4 pcs |
| Curved (arc) washer 8.5 x 20 x 1.5t | No. 33 | No. 19 |
| Domed nut M8 | No. 30 | No. 22 |
| Tool | wrench 72 | wrench 55 |

1. Attach the front stabilizer (No. 06; upright No. 05) to the main frame (No. 01) using 2 carriage bolts, 2 curved washers and 2 domed nuts.
2. Attach the rear stabilizer (No. 07; upright No. 06) to the main frame (No. 01) using 2 carriage bolts, 2 curved washers and 2 domed nuts.

The recumbent's service document adds the one thing that goes wrong: **"Stabilizer tubes w/ M8 doomnut will damage, if overtightened. To secure max stability use M8 nylon nut."** - a domed (cap) nut bottoms out on the bolt; a technician who needs it tighter swaps it for an M8 nylon lock nut.

Neither book says which stabilizer carries the transport wheels here; the Final Considerations page puts them on the front stabilizer.
