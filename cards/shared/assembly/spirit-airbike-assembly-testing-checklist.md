- **Recheck all the bolts** and make sure they are tightened to the proper torque
  specification (if indicated) and that no parts are missing.
- **Test the seat post** - it should move freely and lock at different positions.
- **Check the seat** is level and tight and does not rotate or tilt. Tighten and adjust
  as needed.
- **Test the seat's horizontal adjustment** front to rear at several settings.
- **Adjust the seat post to your needs**, then ride and test the bike for proper
  operation.
- **Pedal at a moderate pace** and test for proper, smooth movement of the pedals and
  arms.
- **When testing is complete, even the movement arms**, tip the bike forward using the
  arms, roll it on a smooth surface to its final location and adjust the levelling feet
  so the bike is stable.

> **CAUTION: The movement arms move whenever the pedals are turning. Do not dismount the
> bike until the pedals and movement arms come to a complete stop.**

The caution sits between the two halves of the list in both books.

## "the proper torque specification (if indicated)"

The first line is the only mention of torque in the AB900 assembly chapter, and **the
AB900 indicates none anywhere in the book**. The AB950 does print one figure, for the
pedal-to-crank-arm bolt, but in its maintenance chapter rather than here; see
[that card](../../ab950-2024/assembly/ab950-2024-assembly-pedal-torque-33-to-37-lb-ft.md).

## The Johnny G checklist is not this one

The JB950 prints a shorter list with no movement arms and an extra handlebar check. See
[that card](../../jb950-2022/assembly/jb950-2022-assembly-testing-checklist.md).
