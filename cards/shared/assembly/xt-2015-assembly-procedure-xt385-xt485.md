Numbers in brackets are item numbers from the assembly drawing. Gather the
hardware for each step before you start that step.

**Step one - uprights into the frame base.** Take **6 Speed Nut Clips (128)** and
attach them at the front and each side of the unit. Guide the right upright
through Frame Base Cover Right (62) and Console Mast Cover Right (67). Take the
Right Upright (4) and attach the Middle Computer Cable (50) to the Lower Computer
Cable (49) - be careful not to pinch the cable or damage may occur to the system.
Slide the right upright (4) to sit into the frame base. Repeat from the "guide
the upright" step on the left.

**Step two - bolt the uprights down.** With the Right and Left Uprights (4, 5)
inserted into the Frame Base (2), use the Combination M5 Allen Wrench and
Phillips Head Screw Driver (131) to tighten **8 Button Head Socket Bolts (130)**
securing the uprights to the frame base.

**Step three - frame base covers.** Install Frame Base Cover Right and Left
(62, 61) onto the Frame Base (2) using **6 Tapping Screws (120)**. Tighten with
the Phillips Head Screw Driver (122).

**Step four - console.**

1. Connect the Speed Adjustment Switch w/ Cable (54) with the Upper Speed Cable
   (37).
2. Connect the Incline Adjustment Switch w/ Cable (55) with the Upper Incline
   Cable (38).
3. Connect the Middle Computer Cable (50) to the Upper Computer Cable (48).
4. Be sure not to pinch any of the cables or damage may occur to the system.
5. Insert the Console Assembly (52) onto the Right and Left Uprights (4, 5) and
   secure with **4 Button Head Socket Bolts (133)** and **4 Split Washers (113)**.
   Use the Allen Wrench (132) to tighten.

**Step five - console mast covers.** Slide Console Mast Cover Right and Left
(67, 66) up the uprights. Secure them with **8 Sheet Metal Screws (142)** using
the Phillips Head Screw Driver (148).

**Step six - handrail support.** Place the Handrail Support (119) between the
Left and Right Uprights (5, 4) and use the Combination Allen Wrench & Phillips
Head Screw Driver (131) to tighten **4 Button Head Socket Bolts (150)**.
**On the XT485-2015 the same step then installs the Beverage Holder (121) onto
the Handrails Support (119).** The XT385-2015 step six stops at the bolts; that
manual's carton list names no beverage holder.

One thing printed inconsistently: **step three names the Phillips Head Screw
Driver as item 122 and step five names it as item 148.** Both steps call for a
Phillips head screw driver; the manual does not say which number is right. In
these two machines' exploded views item 148 is the Phillips Head Screw Driver
and item 122 is the number the XT185-2015 and XT285-2015 manuals use for theirs.

Carrying over to 2023: the handrail support **is** the same job with the same
parts - Handrails Support (119), 4 pcs of 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolts
(150), tool (131) - and item 130 still bolts the uprights on with 8 pcs. Nothing
else carries over. The 2023 machine has a two-piece console, a tablet holder and
a five-step order, and no speed nut clips.
