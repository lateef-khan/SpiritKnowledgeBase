**Five books, one procedure.** The CU800 (2020), CU800ENT, XBU55 2016 and XBU55 2023 service manuals print it with the same part numbers; the 2012 CU800 (XU878) book prints it without part numbers.

1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to unscrew **4 pcs of M5 x 12 mm screws (58)**, then disconnect the **computer cable (29)** and the **hand-pulse cables** to take the **console (34)** off.
2. Reverse the step to refit the console (34).

**What each book adds.**

- The XBU55 books say "both hand pulse sensor cables" where the CU800 books say "Handpulse cables" - two cables either way.
- The 2012 CU800 book prints the refit as its own step: plug in the computer cables and pulse sensor cables, then secure the console with the 4 pcs of M5 x 12 mm screws.
- No screw torque and no cable routing is printed in any of the five books.

The handlebar procedure starts by taking the console off, so this card is step one of that one.
