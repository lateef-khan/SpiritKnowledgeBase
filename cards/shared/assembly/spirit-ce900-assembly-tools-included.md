Both cartons list the same five tools, and nothing else:

- 17mm Wrench **(2 of them)**
- Phillips Screwdriver
- 5mm Allen Wrench
- 6mm Allen Wrench
- 8mm Allen Wrench

**Two 17mm wrenches, not one.** Step three tightens M10 nuts with a bolt head on the
far side, which needs both.

## The parts list carries three tools, not five

The 2025 parts list has **131 Phillips Head Screw Driver**, **132 L Allen
Wrench(5×26×120L)** and **133 6 L Allen Wrench**. There is **no item for either 17mm
wrench and none for the 8mm Allen wrench**, although the panel lists all three. The
2021 manual has **no parts list at all** to check against.

Item 132's "5×26×120L" is the only place a 5mm Allen key's dimensions are printed;
item 133 is printed with a bare "6".

## No 14mm wrench, and this machine needs one

The noise item in both manuals says to adjust the **six levelling pads** with a **14mm
wrench**. **No 14mm tool is in this carton** - the panel above has 17mm and three
Allen keys and nothing else. Bring your own or use an adjustable, as the manual
suggests. See [the levelling card](spirit-ce900-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

**This panel is on no other Spirit elliptical.** The CE800, CE800ENT, CE850 and CES880
cartons hold different tools; not one of the five above is on the CE850 panel.
