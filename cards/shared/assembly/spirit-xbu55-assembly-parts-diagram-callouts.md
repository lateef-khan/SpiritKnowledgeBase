The drawing carries thirteen callouts, written on the picture rather than keyed to a
letter or a number:

- CONSOLE
- CONSOLE FAN
- FRONT HANDLEBARS
- PULSE GRIPS
- CONSOLE MAST
- CONSOLE MAST COVER
- WATER BOTTLE HOLDER
- SEAT
- CRANK ARMS
- PEDALS
- FRONT STABILIZER
- REAR STABILIZER
- MAIN FRAME

**All three manuals name the same thirteen parts.**

## The touchscreen book renames one

The **XBU55ENT** drawing reads **PULSE SENSORS** where the other two read **PULSE
GRIPS** - the same change its recumbent sister makes. Same part, same place.

## What the drawing does not point at

It does not point at the **levellers**, the **transport wheels**, the **seat adjustment
knob** or the **seat mount slide**, and the last two are what step four fits. Nor does it
show a seat cushion, though the carton packs one separately from the seat.

**No residential upright drawing in this family shows a transport wheel**, because these
machines' manuals never mention one - see
[the absence card](spirit-xb-assembly-no-moving-or-transport-section.md). The commercial
CU800 2021 drawing is the one bike page in the whole family that points at the levelers;
see [that card](../../cu800-2021/assembly/cu800-2021-assembly-parts-diagram-labels.md).

## The 2023 book letters its diagram instead

The XBU55 2023 drawing keys **A to L** to a list and adds a tablet holder, an adjustable
fan, console buttons, a seat adjustment knob and a transport wheel. See
[that card](../../xbu55-2023/assembly/xbu55-2023-assembly-parts-diagram-letters.md).
