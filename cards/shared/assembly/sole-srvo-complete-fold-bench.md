1. Unlock the locking clamp.
2. Fold the SRVO stabilizer and make sure the stabilizers are locked in place.
3. Loosen the adjustable knob for the front and rear stabilizer. Fold the stabilizers inwards and make sure they are locked in place.
4. Loosen the adjustable knob at the bottom of the rack, and carefully lower the rack until the pull pin on the top rod is placed securely.

This is the reverse of the unfold sequence, which has its own card.
