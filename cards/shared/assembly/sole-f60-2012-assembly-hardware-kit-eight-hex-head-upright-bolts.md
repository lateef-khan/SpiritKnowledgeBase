Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | #76 | Dia 5/16" x Dia 18 mm x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 8 pcs |
| 2 | #97 | 5/16" x 1/2" | Hex Head Bolt | 8 pcs |
| 3 | #95 | M5 x 15 mm | Button Head Socket Bolt | 6 pcs |
| 5 | #93 | Dia 10 | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #94 | 3/8" x 1-1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 6 | #96 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |

Tools in the box: #98 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver, #99 M6 L Allen Wrench, #100 13 mm Wrench.

The step-3 text calls (95) "Phillips Head Screws"; the checklist panel calls them Button Head Socket Bolts, and this card follows the checklist panel.
