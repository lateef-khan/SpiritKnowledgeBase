- 2 Console Masts
- 2 Side Covers
- 1 Base Frame
- 1 Console Support
- 1 Console
- 1 Safety Button
- 1 Power Cord
- 1 Hardware Kit

**Two manuals list one more line: 1 Tube of Lubricant (1 application).** They are
the **CT800 2016** and the **CT850 2018**. The CT800 2020, the CT800ENT, the CT850
2016, the CT850 2020 and the CT850ENT print the other eight lines identically and
**do not list any lubricant**. Nothing else differs between any of the panels.

That absence is consistent with the rest of those manuals: they tell you not to
apply any lubricant or wax to the deck surface. The two manuals that pack a tube say
the same thing about the deck while still packing it.

**The lubricant does not track the model year.** The CT850 2016 has no tube and the
CT850 2018 has one, so do not infer a carton's contents from a neighbouring year of
the same machine.

**The CT800 2012 manual prints no carton list at all.** Its pre-assembly page holds
the unpacking steps and the tools panel and nothing else, so nothing on this card is
claimed for that machine.

None of the panels gives item numbers. The item numbers appear only in the assembly
steps, the hardware tables and - on the machines that have one - the parts list at
the back. The **CT850 2018 manual has no parts list**.
