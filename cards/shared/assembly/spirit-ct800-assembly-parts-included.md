- 2 Console Masts
- 2 Side Covers
- 1 Base Frame
- 1 Console Support
- 1 Console
- 1 Safety Button
- 1 Power Cord
- 1 Hardware Kit

**The 2016 manual lists one more line: 1 Tube of Lubricant (1 application).** The
2020 and CT800ENT manuals print the other eight lines identically and **do not
list any lubricant**. Nothing else differs between the three panels.

That absence is consistent with the rest of those two manuals: both tell you not
to apply any lubricant or wax to the deck surface. The 2016 manual says the same
thing about the deck while still packing a tube.

**The 2012 manual prints no carton list at all.** Its pre-assembly page holds the
unpacking steps and the tools panel and nothing else, so nothing on this card is
claimed for that machine.

None of the four panels gives item numbers. The item numbers appear only in the
assembly steps, the hardware tables and - on the 2020 and CT800ENT machines - the
parts list at the back.
