Hardware step 6: **#110 3/8" x 1-3/4" carriage bolt (1), #111 3/8" x 25 x 2T flat washer (1)**.

1. Assemble the sliding seat mount (7) on top of the seat post (5). Slide the carriage bolt (110) from the top downwards through the sliding seat mount (7), the seat post (5) and the flat washer (111). Assemble the seat fore/aft adjustment knob (103) and secure tightly.

The 2014 book prints the heading of this step as a second "Water Bottle Holder" - a misprint; the drawing and text are the seat. The 2018 book heads it "Seat".
