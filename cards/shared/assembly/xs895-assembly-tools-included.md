Four tools, each with the item number the steps call it by:

| Item | Tool |
|---|---|
| 151 | Philips Head Screw Driver |
| 152 | L Allen Wrench |
| 149 | 13/14mm Wrench |
| 150 | 12/14mm Wrench |

**Philips is the manual's own spelling**, on both printings.

## The one tool you need first is not in the box

Unpacking item 1 says to cut the banding straps **"using a razor knife (Box Cutter)"**,
then reach under the bottom edge of the carton, pull it away from the cardboard
underneath to separate the staples, and lift the box over the unit. No box cutter is
supplied, and the panel never says to provide one.

**This is the only Spirit climber or stepper carton whose unpacking instruction names a
tool.** Every other one in the range says only "cut the straps, then lift the box over
the unit and unpack".

## The L Allen Wrench has no size

Item 152 is printed as "L Allen Wrench" with nothing else. The two wrenches carry sizes
and the screwdriver a type; this one carries neither a millimetre size nor a hex figure,
and no step gives one either. It is used once, for the four 3/8" x 3/4" socket head cap
bolts (110) that hold the console mast on.

## Every driven screw in the build has a screwdriver for it

Unlike the CSC900 and the CSC880 stair climbers, this carton does hold a screwdriver -
and it needs to, because both steps drive M5 x 10mm Phillips screws (113) and step two
also drives a 3.5 x 16L sheet metal screw (117).

## No torque figure and no adjustable wrench

The build gives no torque figure for any fastener, and the noise item that tells you to
tighten every bolt afterwards says you **may need a larger wrench than the one
provided** without naming one.
