The heading adds "(Please take the gas spring apart before replacement)"; the step that does it calls the
same part the cylinder.

1. After the running board has been folded, use an **M5 L Allen wrench** and a **12mm wrench** to remove the
   securing screws on the cylinder, and remove the cylinder.
2. Perform step 12.6 (the roller procedure) and remove the front and rear rollers.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **6 tapping screws** from the front and back of the **foot
   rail**, following the indicating arrows.
4. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **8 tapping screws** of the running board. Remove the
   running board and replace the board or the running belt. If the cushions need replacing, remove the
   **6 cushions** and replace them. Reassemble in the reverse order. When reassembled, centre and adjust the
   running belt tension.
5. Use a 14mm open end wrench to adjust the drive belt tension with a tension measuring device to the **white
   LBS area between 70-75 LBS**, then tighten the 4 motor securing screws with a 14mm T-shaped socket wrench.

Only the numbering differs between the three books: the XT285 and XT385 books print the roller step as an
unnumbered line between steps 1 and 2 and number the rest 1-4; the XT485 book numbers all five. The XT185
2015 book is not on this card - its deck is held by four Allen screws under a six-screw step cover.

No torque is printed, and the running-belt tension and centring in step 4 are not specified here.
