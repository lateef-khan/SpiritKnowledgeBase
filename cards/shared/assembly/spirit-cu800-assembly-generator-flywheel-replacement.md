**This is the generator-brake upright.** The XBU55 flywheel (steel cable, two 3/8"-UNF26 nuts) and the 2012 CU800 induction brake are different parts with their own cards.

1. Take both front shrouds (36) (37) and the belt (14) off.
2. Unplug the electric wires connected to the **generator flywheel (20)**, then use an **11 mm wrench** to unscrew **4 pcs of 1/4" x 3/4" hex head screws (54)** with **4 pcs of 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers (69)** and **4 pcs of Ø1/4" split washers (76)**, and the generator flywheel (20) is released.
3. Follow the steps in reverse order to refit the flywheel.

The belt has to come off first, which means the J-bolt nut and the idle arm screws (the drive-belt card) precede this. After refitting, the belt tension has to be set again - 180 to 205 Hz on an audio tension gauge, printed in the drive-belt procedure, not here.

No torque is printed for the four hex head screws.
