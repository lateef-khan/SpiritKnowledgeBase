**This is the generator-brake upright.** The XBU55 flywheel (steel cable, two 3/8"-UNF26 nuts) and the 2012 CU800 induction brake are different parts with their own cards.

1. Take both front shrouds (36) (37) and the belt (14) off.
2. Unplug the electric wires connected to the **generator flywheel (20)**, then use an **11 mm wrench** to unscrew **4 pcs of 1/4" x 3/4" hex head screws (54)** with **4 pcs of 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers (69)** and **4 pcs of Ø1/4" split washers (76)**, and the generator flywheel (20) is released.
3. Follow the steps in reverse order to refit the flywheel.

The belt has to come off first, which means the J-bolt nut and the idle arm screws (the drive-belt card) precede this. After refitting, the belt tension has to be set again - 180 to 205 Hz on an audio tension gauge, printed in the drive-belt procedure, not here.

No torque is printed for the four hex head screws.

**The Spirit Medical 4.0U (FU800-SB022-03 book) calls the same part the Generator Induction Brake and prints the same four bolts** - 4 pcs of 1/4" x 3/4" hex head bolts with Ø1/4" spring washers and Ø1/4" flat washers on an 11 mm wrench - with two additions. Before the bolts it has you release the **3 pcs of M6 x 15 mm screws, Ø1/4" spring washers and 1/4" x 13 mm x 1T flat washers on the idler wheel assembly** and take the idler wheel assembly and J-bolt off, and it names the cable to disconnect: the **wire brake coil harness**. Its refit says to install the induction brake onto the main frame **by laying it on the floor**, secure the four bolts, refit the J-bolt, join the idler wheel assembly to the brake with the three M6 x 15 mm screws, then "follow procedures 7-3 and 5-3 to return other parts" - references to sections the book numbers 9-7 and 9-5. Its belt goes back to 180 +/- 10 Hz, not 180 to 205.
