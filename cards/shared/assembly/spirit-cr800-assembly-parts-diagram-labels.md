Thirteen labels, printed on a line drawing with no heading, no letters and no item
numbers:

- **FRONT HANDLEBARS**
- **CONSOLE**
- **CONSOLE FAN**
- **CONSOLE MAST**
- **CONSOLE MAST COVER**
- **SEAT**
- **CRANK ARMS**
- **PEDALS**
- **PULSE GRIPS**
- **CUP HOLDER**
- **FRONT STABILIZER**
- **MAIN FRAME**
- **REAR STABILIZER**

Both printings label the same thirteen. It is the page before CR800 PRE-ASSEMBLY.

## What it names that the rest of the manual does not

**CONSOLE FAN** and **PULSE GRIPS** appear only here; the steps call the grips
handpulse wires and the carton list names neither. It calls part CUP HOLDER what step
four calls the Cup Holders and the CU800 manuals call a Drink Bottle Holder.

**It does not label the levellers.** The CU800 2021 diagram does, on the same kind of
page in the same position. See
[that card](../../cu800-2021/assembly/cu800-2021-assembly-parts-diagram-labels.md) and
[the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

## The diagram is not a carton list

Nothing on this page tells you what is packed loose. For that see
[the parts card](spirit-cr800-assembly-parts-included.md).

**The touchscreen printing replaces this drawing with a photograph** carrying eight
labels, five of them different. See
[that card](../../cr800ent-2023/assembly/cr800ent-2023-assembly-parts-diagram-labels.md).
