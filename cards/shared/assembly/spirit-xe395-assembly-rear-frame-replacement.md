**The XE395 and XE395ENT books print this word for word, and the Sole E35-2016 twin prints the same bolts.** The
CE800 rear frame is a different, all-Allen-bolt job.

1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **Phillips head screws M5x15mm** below the **incline bottom cover**.
   **Disconnect the power and VR wirings for the incline motor.**
2. Use **two 14 mm double open-end wrenches** and a **12 mm double open-end wrench** to remove the **hex head bolt
   3/8" x 1-1/2"**, **flat washer 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T**, **nyloc nut 3/8 x 7T**, the **outer hex head bolt 5/16" x
   2-1/4"**, **spring washer 5/16" x 1.5T**, **flat washer 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T** and **star washer ψ5/16**. Remove the
   rear frame.
3. Reassemble the parts in the reverse order and **make sure the wirings are connected correctly (same colour wire to
   same colour socket)**.
