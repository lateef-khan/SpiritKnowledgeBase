**The wheel is held by a `Ø17` circlip with a `Ø17 x 0.5T` wave washer on each side.** The XE195 book's wheel rides
on a bolted axle with a single wave washer instead.

1. Follow **procedure 11-3** to take apart the connecting arm.
2. Disassemble the connecting arm first and use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **M5x10mm Phillips head
   screw** to take apart the **pedal arm cover**.
3. Use an **#8 (M8) hex wrench and a #14 wrench** to remove the **gap socket screw 3/8" x 2-1/4"**, the **flat
   washer(s) 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T** and **nyloc nut 3/8" x 11T** connecting the **inclinable rail assembly** and
   **rotating block**, to remove the inclinable rail assembly.
4. Use a **#12 wrench** to remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 15mm** and **flat washer 5/16" x 35 x 1.5T**, to remove
   the **bushing housing, pedal arm**.
5. To take the slide wheel apart, use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **two Phillips head screws M5x15mm** and
   take the slide wheel cover off first.
6. Use circlip pliers to remove the **circlip Ø17** and remove the sliding wheels.
7. Reassemble in the reverse order. When you assemble the **Ø78 slide wheel, urethane**, build the stack in this
   order: **Ø17 x 0.5T wave washer, Ø78 slide wheel (urethane), Ø17 x 0.5T wave washer, Ø17 C ring**.

**The washer count in step 3 is printed two ways.** The XE295 book prints **two** flat washers `3/8" x 19 x 1.5T`;
the XE395 and XE395ENT books print "flat washer" with no count. The Sole E35-2016 twin prints two. Every other
figure agrees across the three books.
