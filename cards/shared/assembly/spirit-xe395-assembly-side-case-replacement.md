**The XE395 and XE395ENT books print this word for word.** The XE195/XE295 books use three `5x19` tapping
screws per cover and a DC power plug; the XE795 books use nine `3.5x16` screws but no power module step.

1. Remove the swing arm and the inclinable rail assembly.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **nine sheet metal screws 3.5x16 mm** and remove the right chain cover.
3. Unscrew the right chain cover on the mainframe by releasing **three tapping screws 5x19mm**.
4. Further release the **sheet metal screw 3.5x16mm** together with the **flat washer 1/4" x 19** which is inside the
   right chain cover.
5. Remove **3 pcs 5 x 19 m/m tapping screws** from the left chain cover fixed on the main frame, and pull out the
   **power cable (RED, 2 pcs)** and **ground wire (green, 1 pc)**; the left chain cover can then be removed.
6. Use a screwdriver to release **M4 x 12 m/m Phillips head screws x 2 pcs** and the **M4 x 5T nyloc nut**, then
   remove the **AC power** socket.
7. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.

Steps 2 and 3 both remove the right chain cover; the book prints them in that order.
