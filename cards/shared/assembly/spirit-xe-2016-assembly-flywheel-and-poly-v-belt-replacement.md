**These four books use a cable-driven magnetic flywheel held by one `3/8"-UNF26 x 9T` nyloc nut.** The CE800 and
XE795 books bolt a powered induction brake on with four `1/4"` bolts instead; the Sole E35-2016 twin names an
**11T** nut at this point.

1. Get the tension off the cable first. XE195 and XE295 books: **use the console to set resistance level 20 and
   remove the steel cable**. XE395 and XE395ENT books: **plug in the power; the resistance should read its maximum
   value**.
2. Follow **procedure 11-8** to take apart the idler wheel, the console, and take apart the **steel cable**.
3. Use a **#15 wrench** (XE395: "#15 Double Open-End Wrench") to loosen the **nyloc nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 9T** on the
   flywheel and main frame. Remove the flywheel and belt.
4. Reverse the above steps to return all parts and **adjust the position of the drive belt to the centre of the drive
   pulley** (according to the idler wheel assembling procedure), then resume all parts.

The XE195 book prints the heading with Chinese characters before the English ("11-9 磁控飛輪與皮帶 Flywheel &
Poly-V Belt Replacement"). Step 2 reads "take apart the Idler Wheel the Console" in all four books; it is quoted
as printed.
