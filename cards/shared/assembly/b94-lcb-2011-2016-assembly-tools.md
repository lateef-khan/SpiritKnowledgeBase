| Item | Tool |
|---|---|
| #92 | 13/15mm Wrench |
| #93 | Phillips Head Screwdriver |
| #100 | 12/14mm Wrench |

Three tools, no Allen wrench. The 2019 and 2023 upright bikes add a **#76 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver**, which these do not carry.

**Tool item numbers are not shared between models.** The same `#` is a different part on another Sole bike, so use the list printed in your own machine's manual. The R92 and LCR recumbents of the same years ship #112, #114 and #132 instead.
