This step needs no hardware from the assembly pack.

The page carries a drawing with the callouts 1, 2, 3 and 4 but **no printed key naming them**; the part names below come from the instruction itself.

1. Install the mounting clamp (2) to the back of the console (1) with the clamp mounting screw (3).
2. Install and slightly tighten the thumb screw (4), then adjust the console angle for optimal visibility. Once the console is adjusted, continue to tighten the screw until it is securely attached to the handle bar.

**This is the fifth and last numbered assembly step on these machines** - after the pedals. The SB700 of the same years does the console as step 3, before the handlebar and pedals, and adds a transmitter-mounting instruction that the SB900 2014/2016 assembly chapter does not carry.

The SB900 2019 has no console assembly step at all; its step 5 is the tablet holder.
