**No cylinder step - this deck does not fold.** The four folding XT 2023 books open the same procedure by
dismounting the gas cylinder; these two go straight to the rollers.

1. Follow section 9.6 (the roller procedure) to remove the front and rear rollers. Use a screwdriver to
   remove the **foot rail fixing screws** on both sides, then follow the direction to slide both foot rails out.
2. Remove the **8 Running Deck locking screws**, then take off the running deck. Now you can replace the
   running deck, the running belt and the cushions. Do the reverse steps to install them back.
3. Adjust the running belt tension and centre it.

The XT685 book numbers these **1, 2, 4** - there is no step 3 on its page. The XT685ENT book prints the same
three steps as 1, 2, 3. Nothing is missing; the XT685 numbering skipped.

No count is printed for the foot rail screws or the cushions, no torque is printed, and the belt tension is
not specified here.
