**The recumbent and the upright print the same thirteen names in the same order**, with
only the heading changed and the callouts moved to suit the machine. It is the one page
in either assembly chapter that is common to both.

## Names that appear nowhere else in the manual

**Tablet Rack**, **Pulse Grips** and **Console Fan** are used only here. The assembly
steps never mention any of the three, the carton list names none of them, and the parts
list calls the pulse grips handpulse wires and assemblies. **Cup Holder** is the name
the recumbent carton list and steps also use; the upright carton calls the same part a
Cup Holder and the CU800 books call their equivalent a Drink Bottle Holder.

## The diagram is not a carton list

Nothing on this page tells you what is packed loose. The seat, the pedals and the
handlebars are drawn in place on a finished machine. For what is in the box see
[the recumbent parts card](spirit-cr900-assembly-parts-included.md) and
[the upright parts card](spirit-cu900-assembly-parts-included.md).

**The older printings of the same machines have no such page.** The CR900 2018, CU900
2018, CR900ENT and CU900ENT manuals print no labelled diagram at all - the four
900-series books go straight from the safety pages to pre-assembly.

## The 800-series books label their parts differently

Every CR800 and CU800 manual has a labelled page, but none of them uses letters: the
CR800 2021 and 2023 print a thirteen-label drawing, the CU800 2021 a fourteen-label
drawing that alone names the **LEVELERS**, and the CR800ENT and CU800ENT 2022 books an
eight-label photograph each. Only the CU800ENT 2024 uses this A-to-M scheme, and it
relabels two of the thirteen. See the linked cards.
