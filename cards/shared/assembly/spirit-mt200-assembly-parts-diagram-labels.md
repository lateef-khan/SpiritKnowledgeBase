**This diagram is numbered 1 to 10, not lettered.** The 7.0T diagram points at
these same ten parts in this same order but labels them A to J, so a callout
quoted as a letter did not come from an MT200 manual.

| Callout | Part |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hand grips |
| 2 | Electronic console |
| 3 | Safety lanyard |
| 4 | Adjustable hand rails |
| 5 | Handrail horizontal adjustment |
| 6 | Handrail vertical adjustment |
| 7 | Handrail lift bar |
| 8 | Rear step |
| 9 | Rear incline |
| 10 | Front incline |

**Callout 3 is named differently on the two generations.** The MT200 calls it
the **Safety lanyard**; the 7.0T calls the same callout the **Safety Key**.

There is no water bottle holder on this diagram, and no transport wheel, no
levelling foot and no motor cover. The diagram covers the ten parts a therapist
adjusts, not the whole machine.

The 2010 manual heads the page **MT200 - Rehabilitation Treadmill**; the 2022
manual heads the same page **MT200 - Treadmill**. Same diagram, same ten parts.
