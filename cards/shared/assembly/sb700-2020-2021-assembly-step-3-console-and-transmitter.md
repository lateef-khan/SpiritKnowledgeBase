This step needs no hardware from the assembly pack.

The page labels five parts: **1 Console, 2 Clamp mounting screw, 3 Mounting Clamp, 4 Velcro, 5 Speed transmitter.**

**Console installation**

1. Install the mounting clamp (2) to the back of the console (1) with the clamp mounting screw (3).
2. Install and slightly tighten the thumb screw (4), then adjust the console angle for optimal visibility. Once the console is adjusted, continue to tighten the screw until it is securely attached to the handle bar.

**Transmitter installation**

- Make sure to route the speed sensor wire behind the frame member. Plug the sensor wire into the transmitter as shown.
- Install the transmitter, at the angle shown, onto the pre-installed Velcro strip located on the back of the chain cover. Make sure the sensor wire connection is at the **top left** when mounting the transmitter.
- Make sure the sensor wire is routed **away from the spinning flywheel**.

**The callout numbers and the instruction do not agree on these two machines.** The drawing's key says **2 = clamp mounting screw and 3 = mounting clamp**, but the instruction says "install the mounting clamp (**2**) ... with the clamp mounting screw (**3**)" - the two are swapped. And the instruction's "thumb screw (**4**)" is labelled **Velcro** in the key; there is no clamping thumb screw in the key at all. The SB700 2011-2019 key does match the instruction (2 mounting clamp, 3 clamp mounting screw, 4 clamping thumb screw), so the instruction text was carried over unchanged when the drawing was renumbered. Read the parts, not the numbers.

The same step also carries the console charging note, the transmitter battery installation and the synchronising procedure, which are covered by the console section, not here.
