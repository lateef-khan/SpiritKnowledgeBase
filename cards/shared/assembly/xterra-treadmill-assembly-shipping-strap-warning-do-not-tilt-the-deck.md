"Please read before unpacking your folding treadmill. Serious injury could occur if this folding
treadmill is not unpacked properly."

A **Velcro strap** is installed around the treadmill base to stop it unfolding accidentally during
shipping. If the strap is not removed properly the treadmill **could spring open unexpectedly** and
injure anyone standing near it when the strap is removed.

1. Make sure the treadmill is positioned **flat on the ground, in the orientation it would be in if you
   were using it**.
2. **Do not turn the treadmill up on its side** while removing the shipping strap - that could make the
   folding mechanism spring open.
3. If the end of the strap you need to grab is under the deck, **reach under the deck to grab it; do not
   tilt the treadmill up** to get at it.

Eight books print this warning word for word: the four TRX books under "Pre-Assembly" on the safety
page, and the TRX1400, TR65, TR75 and TR85 under "PRE-ASSEMBLY" with a warning triangle. The 2021-batch
books (TR150, TR200, TR300, TR6.6, TRX1000), the TR260 and TR6.4, the two walking treadmills and the
two Hiker treadmills print no strap warning.
