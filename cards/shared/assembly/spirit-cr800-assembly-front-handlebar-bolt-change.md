Two answers, one per printing. Take the one that matches the book in the box.

| Printing | Console mast | Front handlebars |
|---|---|---|
| **CR800 2021** | 6 Hex Head Bolts **No.68**, 5/16"X5/8" | 2 Hex Head Bolts **No.68**, 5/16"X5/8" |
| **CR800 2023 and CR800ENT** | 6 Hex Head Bolts **No.68**, 5/16"X5/8" | 2 Hex Head Bolts **No.94**, 5/16" X 3/4" |

The 2021 step-three table lists **eight** of item 68 and no item 94. The 2023 and
CR800ENT tables list **six** of item 68 and add **2 of item 94, 5/16" X 3/4"** - an
eighth of an inch longer, on a new item number.

Everything else in step three is unchanged: the same six mast bolts, the same 4 flat
washers (No.76) on the sides and 2 curved washers (No.83) on the front, and the same 2
split washers (No.82) and 2 flat washers (No.76) on the handlebars.

## This is a real change to the build, not only to the parts list

The CR800 2021 and CR800 2023 owner's manuals are otherwise one document - their
assembly chapters match at **98.6%** at word level. **This bolt is the only difference
between them in all four steps.** A claim that the two printings differ only in their
parts lists is wrong; step three differs too, and the item number the step names
differs with it.

The CR800ENT prints the 2023 figures, which is what places it with the later printing
rather than the earlier one.

## Do not substitute one for the other

Item 68 at 5/16"X5/8" is the mast bolt on every printing and there are six or eight of
them in the bag. Fitting a 5/8" bolt where the book asks for a 3/4" leaves less thread
in the handlebar boss; fitting a 3/4" where the book asks for 5/8" can bottom out
against the mast. Read the step-three table in your own manual before you pick from the
bag.
