1. From the hardware pack for step 2 you should have **one 2-3/4" X 3/8" bolt**, **one lock washer**, **two 1" X 3/8" bolts**, **two curved washers** and **four 1/2" X 1/4" Phillips head screws**. (The E35/E75/E95 book says "one flat washer" where the E55 book says "one lock washer" for the 2-3/4" bolt.)
2. **Slide the console mast cover onto the console mast**, facing the correct way.
3. **Unravel the wire harness** at the top opening of the main body and feed it into the bottom of the console mast tube and out of the top opening.
4. **Install the console mast into the receiving bracket** in the top of the main body. **One bolt is already installed** and engages the slot at the bottom of the console mast; it gets tightened at the end with the three other console mast bolts.
5. **Put the washer onto the 2-3/4" bolt** and the **two curved washers onto the two bolts**; hand-tighten the 2-3/4" bolt through the left side of the receiving tube into the console mast (a wire runs through the tube; do not damage or pinch it or the electronic console may be damaged). Hand-tighten the **two 1" bolts** through the front of the receiving tube.
6. **Using the two sided wrench, tighten the three bolts plus the pre-installed fourth firmly, as much as you possibly can.**
7. **Locate the electronic console and the four 1/2" X 1/4" Phillips head screws.**
8. **Three electrical wire connectors sit at the top opening of the console mast: two 2-pin (hand pulse sensors) and one 10-pin (main wire harness).** Connect them to the mating keyed connectors on the back of the console; do not force them. (The E25-2007 section adds that the two 2-pin connectors can go into either console connector.)
9. **Store the excess wire back into the console mast**, install the console onto the mounting plate, and secure with the four Phillips head screws.
10. **Install the Bottle Holder onto the console mast with two M5x12mm Phillips head screws, then place the Bottle in the Bottle Holder.**
