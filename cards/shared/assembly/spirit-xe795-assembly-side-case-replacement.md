**Both XE795 books print the same screws.** The 2017 CE800 (XE890B) book's procedure uses seven `4x19` screws and a
`4x16` screw on the left cover; the XE395 books add an AC power module step.

1. Remove the parts in front of the covers: the 2016 book says "remove pedal arm and console mast assembly"; the
   2023 book says "remove the swing arm, connecting arm and pedal arm (refer to 9.2, 9.3 and 9.4)".
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **9 sheet metal screws 3.5x16mm** from the left and right chain cover
   and the **tapping screws 5x16mm** that secure the right chain cover to the mainframe. Remove the right chain cover.
3. Remove the **3.5x16mm tapping screw (1 pc)** and the **Ø1/4" x 19 m/m flat washer** fixed on the main frame from
   the left chain cover, and remove **5x16 tapping screws (3 pcs)**. The left chain cover can then be taken out.

Neither book prints a reassembly line for this section.
