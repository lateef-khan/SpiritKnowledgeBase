1. Remove the **screw (1 pc)** attached to the **connecting rod** and the **bearing bushings** (figures 6.9.1 to
   6.9.3), and refer to figures 6.8.3 to 6.8.5 (swing arm) to remove the **foot link**.
2. Remove the **M5 screws (6 pcs)** and replace the **plastic cover of the foot pedal** (figure 6.9.4).
3. Replace the **screws (8 pcs)** with **cover A, cover B** and the **rear foot pedal** attached to the foot pedal
   (figures 6.9.5 to 6.9.7).

The three books print this word for word. No sizes beyond "M5" and no refit line are printed.
