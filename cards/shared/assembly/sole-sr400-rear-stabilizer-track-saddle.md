1. Lift up the slide track (D), then assemble it with the rear stabilizer (C) by four screws (J1).
2. Lift up the slide track (D) and put it into the swivel axle (A1), and then fasten screws (J1) with washer (J2).
3. Fit the saddle (E) by 4 screws (J1).

The track goes on with the rail lifted: the machine is unfolded onto its rear stabilizer only after the pedals are on, by pulling the fixing pin and lowering the track.
