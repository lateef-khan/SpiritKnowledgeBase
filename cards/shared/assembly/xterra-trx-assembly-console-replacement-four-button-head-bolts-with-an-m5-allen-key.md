1. Use an **M5 L Allen wrench** to remove the **4 button head socket bolts** from the **console support**.
2. Disconnect the console wirings and replace with the new console.

The TRX5500 book words it "Remove 4 console assembly locking bolts with an M5 L-Allen wrench. Unplug
computer cable and Rapid key wires then replace the console." - the same two steps, naming the two
cables. **The TR260 adds two hex bolts on the console support** that these five do not have. These are
the four 5/16" x 3/4" button head bolts the owner's manual fitted at assembly step 3 (TRX2500-5500) or
step 5 (TRX1400).
