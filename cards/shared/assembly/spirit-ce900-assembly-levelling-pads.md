There are **6 levelling pads on the bottom of the machine**. Use a **14mm wrench** (or
an adjustable wrench) to adjust the levellers until the unit sits properly level.

Both printings say six, and both say "the bottom of the machine" rather than naming a
stabiliser or a rail.

## Six, not two, and not four

- The CE800 2021 and 2023, the CE800ENT and all three CE850 manuals say **2** levelling
  pads, on the bottom of the **rear stabilizer**.
- The CE800 2013 manual says **4**, on the bottom of the **rear rails**.
- This machine says **6**, and does not say where.

Three different answers in one product line. Do not carry any of them across; see the
linked cards.

## The carton does not hold the wrench

The tools panel has two 17mm wrenches and 5mm, 6mm and 8mm Allen keys, and **no 14mm
anything**. Use an adjustable, as the sentence itself allows. See
[the tools card](spirit-ce900-assembly-tools-included.md).

## Where the manual prints it, and what it does not print

It is not in the assembly chapter, and no assembly step fits a leveller. It is the
last remedy in the maintenance item about squeaks and other noises, after tightening
the hardware. The noise fault itself is a maintenance question and has its own card;
what is here is the answer to "how do I level it".

The 2025 manual's maintenance schedule also lists **Levelers - Inspect** as a routine
check, without saying how often or what "inspect" means beyond this sentence.
