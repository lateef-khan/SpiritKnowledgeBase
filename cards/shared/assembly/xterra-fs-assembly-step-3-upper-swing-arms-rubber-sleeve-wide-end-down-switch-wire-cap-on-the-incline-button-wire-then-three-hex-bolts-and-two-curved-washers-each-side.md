Hardware step 3: **6 nyloc nuts 5/16" x 7T (127), 6 hex head bolts 5/16" x 1-3/4" (159), 4 curved washers 5/16" x 23 (160), 2 switch wire caps (161)**.

1. Slide the rubber sleeve (162) onto the left (16) and right (17) upper swing arms. **Make sure the wide part is at the bottom.**
2. Attach the wire (166) from the left upper swing arm (16; incline buttons on front) to the wire (167) that exits the console mast tube (12). **Slide the switch wire cap (161) onto the wire with the wide side facing the swing arm.**
3. Insert the upper swing arm (16) into the lower swing arm. Fasten together with three hex head bolts (159) and two curved washers (160).
4. Repeat steps 3.2 and 3.3 on the right side. You will connect wires (165) to (167).

The left arm carries the incline buttons and the right arm the resistance buttons; each arm's wire meets a mast wire numbered (167). The six nyloc nuts (127) in the hardware box are never named in the step. Word for word the same in both books.
