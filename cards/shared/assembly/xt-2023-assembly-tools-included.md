Every one of the five manuals lists the same three tools. Nothing else is
needed:

- L Allen Wrench (M6)
- Phillips Head Screwdriver
- Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screwdriver

Only the item number on the exploded view changes from machine to machine:

| Tool | XT185-2023 / XT285-2023 | XT385-2023 / XT485-2023 | XT685-2023 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver | 96 | 131 | 103 |
| M6 L Allen Wrench | 97 | 132 | 104 |
| Phillips Head Screw Driver | 122 | 148 | 126 |

**Watch item 132.** On the XT385-2023 and XT485-2023 it is the M6 L Allen
Wrench. On the XT185-2023 and XT285-2023 the same number 132 is the
5/16" x 19 x 1.5T Curved Washer. The number alone does not identify the part.

The XT185-2023 and XT285-2023 panels also number the supplied lubricant as
item 58. The other three manuals list "1 Lubricant" in the carton but print no
item number for it.
