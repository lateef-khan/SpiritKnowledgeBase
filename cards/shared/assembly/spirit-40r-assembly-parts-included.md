Thirteen lines, with quantities as printed, the same in both books:

| Qty | Part |
|---|---|
| 1 | main frame |
| 1 | rear stabilizer |
| 1 | console mast |
| 1 | seat back frame |
| 1 | front handle bar |
| 1 | handle bar |
| 1 | console mast cover |
| 1 | front stabilizer cover |
| 1 | rear stabilizer cover |
| 1 | console |
| 2 | drink bottle holder |
| 2 | pedals |
| 1 | hardware kit |

**Two lines fewer than the CR800 carton**: no **seat back cover** and no separate **seat cushion** line, although step four installs a bottom seat cushion (61) and the service manual replaces a seat back cover (128). The console chin cover (125) that step four fits is not listed either. The gas shock arrives on the seat back angle adjustment bracket.

The list gives no carton count, no shipping weight and no box dimensions. The PT edition prints the machine's size and weight under its last assembly drawing instead - "144 x 74 x 120 cm, 80 kg" - which is the specs section's fact.
