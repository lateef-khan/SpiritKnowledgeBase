| #85 | 14/15m/m Wrench |
| #86 | M5 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver |

Two tools. The 2021 machines add a third, **#145 13/14m/m Wrench**, for the drink bottle holder nuts; these do not have it.

**Tool item numbers are not shared between models.**
