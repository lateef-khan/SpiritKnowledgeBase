1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **4 pcs of M6 x 15 mm Phillips head screws (98)** and take the **seat (61)** off.
2. Use **two 14 mm wrenches** to unscrew from the **seat carriage (4)**: **2 pcs of 3/8" x 2-3/4" hex head bolts (175)**, **4 pcs of 3/8" x 1-3/4" hex head bolts (71)**, **6 pcs of 3/8" x 7T nyloc nuts (89)** and **6 pcs of 3/8" x 19 mm x 1.5T flat washers (77)**, and take the **seat handle bar (6)** off.
3. Unplug the **HGP wire grommet (126)** that secures the **hand-pulse w/cable assembly (21, 27)** and use the Phillips head screwdriver to release **4 pcs of Ø3 x 20 mm tapping screws (97)**, and take the hand-pulse w/cable assembly (21, 27) apart.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.

**The four shorter bolts differ from the 2009 XR898 and the XBR books**, which print four **3/8" x 2"** hex head bolts here where these two books print 3/8" x 1-3/4" (71). The long pair (175), the six nyloc nuts (89) and the six washers (77) are the same in every book. The XR898 / XBR version, which also starts by taking the bottle holders and release lever off, is its own card.

Seat and carriage: the seat handle bar bolts pass through the seat carriage (4), so the carriage stays on the track while the handlebar comes off.
