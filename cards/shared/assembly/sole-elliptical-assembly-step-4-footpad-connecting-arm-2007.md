1. From the hardware pack for step 4 you should have **two 1-1/4" X 3/8" bolts** (the E35/E75/E95 book says 1-1/4 X 3/8), **two flat washers**, **two Nyloc nuts** (Nylon nuts in the E55 book), **four 5/8" machine screws (long)**, **four 3/8" machine screws (short)** and **two self tapping Phillips head screws**.
2. **On the pedal arms, in the middle on top, two axles sit already installed in the bushing housings.** Remove the bolt (one should be loose) and washer from one side of each axle, then remove the axle and set it aside.
3. **Holding the rear of the foot pad with your right hand and the connecting arm just in front of the foot pad with your left**, place the foot pad over the bushing housing.
4. **Line up the large hole in the side of the bracket under the foot pad** with the hole in the bushing housing and insert the axle all the way until the washer is flush against the bracket; wiggle the axle and foot pad as needed.
5. **Take the bolt removed in step 2 AND INSTALL THE NEW ANTI-ROTATION BRACKET**, then thread it into the hole in the axle. Tighten using two wrenches, one on each axle bolt, firmly.
6. **Align the hole in the end of the connecting arm (pivoting rod end)** with the hole in the handle bar bracket, rod end inside the bracket. Install **one 1-1/4" X 3/8" bolt** through the bracket and rod end with a **flat washer** and **Nyloc nut**, tightening firmly with a wrench on the nut and one on the bolt.
7. **Repeat for the right side foot pad/connecting arm.**
8. **Plastic covers over the rod-end and handle bar connection**: the front inside hole takes a **self tapping screw** and the two rear holes take the **3/8" machine screws (short)**; a short Phillips screw driver is provided for the inside screws.
9. **Two wheel covers** with the **four 5/8" machine screws (long)**.
10. **Install the locking pins for the adjustable foot pads into the desired holes** (E55 in the E55 book; E75 and E95 only in the E35/E75/E95 book). Three holes set three footpad angles; try them all, and if your toes go numb raise the angle.
11. **E95 only: install the two plastic parts of the rear shroud** with the **seven Phillips head screws.**

**Ensure all fasteners are tight after the components have been assembled.** The power connector sits on the front, left hand side of the unit.
