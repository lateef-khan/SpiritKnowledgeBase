1. Use a screwdriver to loosen the **eight umbrella head screws** that secure the motor cover, and remove the
   motor cover. (The CT900 books say "the male screwdriver"; the CT1000ENT book says "the screwdriver".)
2. Remove the wires connected to the lower controller, and use a cross screwdriver to remove the controller.
3. Fit the new board and reconnect the wires, following the two steps in reverse.

**Step 1 is the motor-cover step every other procedure in the guide points back to** - "Refer to Step 4.2.1
to remove the motor cover" in the CT900 books, "Refer to Step 8.43" in the CT1000ENT book. Eight screws is
the count for the whole family.

No board screw count, no socket names and no calibration step are printed.

The guide is a machine translation. Its words, as used on this card: **electronic watch** is the console,
**cross screwdriver** is a Phillips screwdriver, **umbrella head screws** are the guide's name for the
console and cover screws (no size is printed), and a **17th T-Shirt** or **13th T-sleeve** is a 17 mm or 13 mm
T-handle socket.
