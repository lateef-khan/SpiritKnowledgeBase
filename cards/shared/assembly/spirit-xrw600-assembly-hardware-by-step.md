## Hardware for step 4

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 121 | Flat washer | 5/16" x Ø18 x 1.5T | 6 |

| 125 | Spring washer | Ø1/4" | 4 |
| 129 | Button head socket bolt | M8 x 12L | 6 |

## Every table closes against its own step

All four steps call for exactly the counts printed above, and nothing comes off the
machine to be reused. **Forty-two fasteners in the bag and forty-two in the build** -
six in step one, four in step two, twelve in step three and twenty in step four.

## The 2019 and 2021 tables are identical

Every part number, description and quantity in all four tables matches between the two
printings, and the two assembly chapters agree to 99.6% at word level - the only
difference anywhere is a line break in the unpacking paragraph. **Nothing about the
hardware changed between them.**

## Three at the front stabiliser, where the CRW800 uses four

The step one table is the sharpest difference between the two Spirit air rowers. This
one fits the front stabiliser with **3 bolts and 3 flat washers and no spring washer**;
the CRW800 fits its own with **4 bolts, 4 flat washers and 4 spring washers**. Steps
two and three differ as well - item 101 and items 116/123 here, items 89/129 and
122/123 there. **Do not answer a hardware question about one rower from the other's
table**; see
[the CRW800 hardware card](../../crw800-2024/assembly/crw800-2024-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Split washer here, spring washer in the same book

Item 116 is a **split washer** in the step three table; item 125 is a **spring washer**
in the step four table; and step four's own text calls item 125 "4 SPLIT WASHERS(125)".
They are the same kind of part under two names. The step four table is the one that
says spring.

## Two M8 bolts three millimetres apart

**Item 123 is M8X15mm and item 129 is M8 x 12L.** Six of each, one set into the folding
end assembly in step three and one set into the pedals in step four. They look alike in
the bag; the longer one belongs to step three.

Their heads differ too, in the steps rather than the tables: step three calls item 123
"6 SOCKET HEAD CAP BOLTS", step four calls item 129 "6 BUTTON HEAD SOCKET BOLT", and
neither table names a head type at all.

## What the tables do not print

**No torque figure for any bolt**, and no note of any pre-installed fastener.
