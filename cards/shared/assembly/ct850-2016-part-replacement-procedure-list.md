**CT850 2016** - chapter 10, "Disassembling and Assembling of Parts", eight procedures:

| Contents | Body heading | Procedure |
|---|---|---|
| 10.1 | 10.1 | Lower Controller Replacement |
| 10.2 | 10.2 | Console Replacement |
| 10.3 | 10.3 | Motor Replacement |
| 10.4 | **12.4** | AC Power Switch Replacement |
| 10.5 | **12.5** | Front/ Rear Roller Replacement |
| 10.6 | **12.6** | Running Deck/ Belt & Cushion Replacement |
| 10.7 | **12.7** | Speed Sensor Replacement (including the wire) |
| 10.8 | **12.8** | Incline Motor Replacement |

The body headings jump from 10.3 straight to 12.4. That is a defect in the manual, and it makes the
manual's own cross-references wrong: the deck procedure sends you to "12.7" for the rollers when 12.7
is the speed sensor, and the roller procedure sends you to "12.3" for the belt tension when there is
no 12.3.

**CT850 2020** - chapter 11, "Part Replacement Guide", seven procedures, numbered 11-1 to 11-7 in
both the contents and the body:

| Section | Procedure |
|---|---|
| 11-1 | Lower Controller Replacement |
| 11-2 | Console Replacement |
| 11-3 | Motor Replacement |
| 11-4 | AC Power Switch Replacement |
| 11-5 | Front/ Rear Roller Replacement |
| 11-6 | Running Deck/ Belt & Cushion Replacement |
| 11-7 | Incline Motor Replacement |

What each manual has that the other does not:

- **Only the 2016 manual has a speed sensor replacement procedure.** The 2020 Part Replacement Guide
  stops at the incline motor.
- **Only the 2020 manual has an exploded view.** It is chapter 10, a single page, drawing
  ST8600-YT058, and it comes with no parts list.

Of the seven procedures both manuals carry, only two are printed identically - the **AC power switch**
and the **running deck**. The other five differ in fastener counts, tool wording or a printed value,
so each machine has its own card for them. Do not read a figure from one manual onto the other
machine.

Neither manual prints a torque value for anything, and neither prints a tool list for this chapter.

**CT800 2020, CT800ENT and CT850ENT.** Three more books carry the CT850 2020 chapter. The CT800 2020 book's
chapter 11, "Part Replacement Guide", is the CT850 2020 chapter **word for word** (11-1 to 11-7; 638 words,
one folio apart). The CT800ENT and CT850ENT books - each titled a "2020 ver." - print the same seven
procedures as chapter 9, "Disassembling and Assembling", numbered 9-1 to 9-7, with the motor headed "Drive
Motor replacement" and the roller procedure still sending you to "step 11.3 (5)", a section neither ENT book
has. So every 2020-chapter fact on the CT850 2020 cards covers those three machines as well, and the ENT
books share the CT850 2020 book's gaps: **no speed sensor procedure**. Neither ENT book has an exploded view
either; the CT800 2020 does (chapter 10, drawing ST8600-YT057, no parts list).

The 4.0T 2026 medical treadmill prints a nine-procedure version of the 2016 chapter with a rear incline
motor; that list is on its own card.
