**The owner's manuals for these machines say nothing about folding. The service manuals do.** This is the
service-manual page, printed in five books.

**Before moving it** (four of the five books): do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and
locked position. Be sure the power cord is secured to avoid possible damage. Use both handrails to manoeuvre
the unit to the desired position.

**To fold**: make certain the treadmill is at **minimum incline**. Lift the running deck until it is secured by
the **locking telescoping tube assembly** in the centre back of the base.

**To unfold**: apply slight forward pressure on the running deck with one hand - at the rear roller area, to
relieve pressure on the locking system. Pull down on the **unlocking lever** and slowly lower the running deck
to the floor. The deck will lower unassisted when it reaches about waist high.

**Transport** (XT185 and XT285 2015 books only): the treadmill is equipped with **four transport wheels**
that are engaged when the treadmill is folded. After folding, simply roll the treadmill away.

What each book prints:

| Book | "Do not move unless folded" preamble | Fold and unfold | Transport paragraph |
|---|---|---|---|
| XT185 2015 | no | yes, with figure numbers 1-4 | yes |
| XT285 2015 | yes | yes | yes |
| XT385 2015 | yes | yes | **no** |
| XT485 2015 | yes | yes | **no** |
| XT485ENT | yes | yes | **no** |

The XT385, XT485 and XT485ENT pages end after the unfold step; there is no wheel paragraph on them, checked
on the rendered page. The XT185 book heads its first section "Unfoldong The Treadmill".

The 2023 XT machines release the deck a different way - a foot on the tube at a yellow sticker and a release
lever - and are on their own card. The Sole F-series books print this same page for the same Dyaco frame.
