Hardware: **#68** 5/16" x 5/8" Hex Head Bolt (8 pcs), **#76** 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T Flat Washer (6 pcs), **#82** 5/16" x 1.5T Split Washer (2 pcs), **#83** 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T Curved Washer (2 pcs).

1. Install the Console Mast Cover (31) onto the Console Mast (2). Make sure the plastic cover is in the correct orientation.
2. Feed the Computer Cable (44) and the **Hand pulse Wire, Coiled (45)** through the bottom of the Console Mast (2) and out through the opening at the top.
3. Install the Console Mast (2) into the receiving tube of the Main Frame (1) with the **six** Hex Head Bolts (68), **four Flat Washers (76) on the sides** of the tube and **two Curved Washers (83) on the front** of the tube, by using the **12/14mm Wrench (112)**. Make sure not to pinch the wire cable, as damage to the electronics could occur.
4. **Remove the white Styrofoam pad** - factory installed to prevent bolts from being accidentally dropped into the bike.
5. Install the Mast Handle bar Assembly (3) onto the Console Mast (2) with the **two** Hex Head Bolts (68), two Flat Washers (76) and **two Split Washers (82)**, by using the **12/14m/m Wrench (112)**.

Eight #68 bolts in the bag: six for the mast, two for the handlebar.

**No plastic tie removal here.** The LCR 2011 and 2013 versions of item 5 start by removing the plastic tie holding the cover on the centre of the handlebar; from 2016 that sentence is gone.

**In the converted text of the LCB/LCR 2023 manual the hardware box beside this step reads "ARDWARE STEP 2".** The printed page reads **HARDWARE STEP 3**, which matches the checklist; this was checked against the page scan.
