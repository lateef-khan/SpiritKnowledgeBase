1. Match the Left Console Mast Cover (83) with the Right Console Mast Cover (84) on top of the left and right Chain Covers and around the Console Mast, and secure with one 3.5 x 16L Sheet Metal Screw (117) and two Phillips Head Screws (113), using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (151).
2. Attach the Upper Handle Bar (L) (24) to the mounting plates on the Left Lower Handle Bar (17) and secure with three 5/16" x 1-3/4" Hex Head Bolts (102), two 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T Curved Washers (143) and three 5/16" x 7T Nyloc Nuts (124), using the 12/14m/m Wrench (150) and the 13/14m/m Wrench (149). **Use the same way with the same quantity of bolts to secure the Upper Handle Bar (R) (25) onto the mounting plates on the Right Lower Handle Bar (18).**
3. Install the Left Handle Bar Cover (90) on the Left Lower Handle Bar and secure with two M5 x 10m/m Phillips Head Screws (113), using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (151). **Use the same way with the same quantity of bolts to secure the Right Handle Bar Cover (91) on the Upper Right Handgrip and on the Right Lower Handle Bar.**
