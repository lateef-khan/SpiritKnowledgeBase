**6.3.1 Remove the rear handle bar cover.** As shown in figures 6.3.1 and 6.3.2, remove the **2 screws** underneath the rear handle bar cover and remove it.

**6.3.2 Remove the left and right cup holders.** Figures 6.3.3 and 6.3.4 show them. Remove the **two screws from each (total of 4 screws)** and remove the cup holders.

**6.3.3 Remove the rear handle bars.** As shown in figure 6.3.5, remove the rear handle bars by removing the **8 bolts**.

**6.3.4 Disconnect the hand pulse wires.** As shown in figure 6.3.6, disconnect the hand pulse wires, then remove the handle bar assembly.

**6.3.5** Refit by following 6.3.1 to 6.3.4 in reverse order.

**The heading 6.3.1 carries an author's note that was never removed:** "(Need picture of cover from top. Difficult to know what cover from these pictures.)" It is printed in both the CR900 (2018) and CR900ENT books. No screw or bolt sizes are printed; the counts are 2, 4 and 8.

The hand-pulse wires run to the seat handlebar grips, so the wires come off last, after the eight bolts, not first.

**The CR1000ENT book prints the same counts as 12.13** - 2 screws under the rear armrest cover, two per cup holder ("about a total of 4"), 8 screws on the rear armrest, then "the two connectors from the connecting cable" - and it drops the author's note. It calls the eight fasteners screws rather than bolts.
