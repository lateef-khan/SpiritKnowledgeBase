**Both XE795 books free the connecting arm with one `M8x15mm` bolt and a 5 mm L Allen wrench.** The 2023 book
calls the bolt a hex socket screw on the **pedal axle lock plate**; the 2016 book calls it a hex head bolt fixed on the
pedal and pedal arm from the **5-way pipe fixing plate**. Same bolt, same tool.

1. Remove the swing arm assembly and swing arm (2023: refer to 9.2; 2016: "follow procedure 2"). If removing the
   pedal tube, simply remove the swing arm with the rod end bearing attached (2023 wording); if you need the
   connecting bar only, just take apart the connection between the handle bar and the fish-eye bearing (2016 wording).
2. Use a **5 mm L Allen wrench** to loosen the **hex socket screw M8x15mm** and remove the **pedal axle lock plate**
   to take off the pedal bar assembly.
3. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **Phillips head screws M5x10mm** securing the pedal and remove the
   pedal (the 2016 book counts them: **four**).
4. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly. **Ensure the pedal axle lock plate is aligned with the pedal arm.**
   (2023 book only; the 2016 book prints no reassembly line.)
