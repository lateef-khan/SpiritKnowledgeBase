Connect the **Sensor Wires (40)** from the frame to the wires on the back of the **Console (91)**, then fix the console to its bracket with the **4 Cross-head Screws (65)** that come pre-assembled on the back of the console - take them out first (the ERG180 says so), plug in, then screw down.

The screws are not in the hardware bag. The ERG160 draws the multi-function wrench (100) for this step, the ERG180 the S5 Allen wrench (84); a Phillips screwdriver is what the screw head actually needs. Neither book fits batteries here - the console specification pages say 2 x AA.

The ERG220's console is different: it pops out of the frame on a hinge (linked card).
