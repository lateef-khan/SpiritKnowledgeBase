**The stride machines have a third cover - the side case rear shroud - and an AC input module whose wire
positions you must note before unplugging.** The XE-series side cases have neither.

1. Remove the **seat handle bar** and **lower handle mast assembly** (refer to step 11-1). (The book prints "seat
   handle bar"; on this machine 11-1 is the swing arm and lower handle bar.)
2. Remove **two Ø4 x 15L sheet metal screws** of the **console mast cover** with a Phillips head screwdriver.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **eight sheet metal screws Ø4x15L** and remove the **left chain cover**.
4. Remove the **5 x 19L tapping screw** on **side case (R)** of the main frame with a Phillips head screwdriver.
5. Remove the **5 x 19L tapping screw** on **side case (L)** of the main frame with a Phillips head screwdriver.
6. **Remember the position of the AC input module and the colour of the cables.** Remove the two connecting wires
   and the ground wire, and side case (L) can then be removed.
7. Remove the **5 x 19L tapping screw** on the **side case rear shroud** of the main frame with a Phillips head
   screwdriver.
8. Reassemble by the above steps. **Do not clip the cables** between side cases (R) and (L) and the connection of the
   side case rear shroud, and lock the screws.

The XE895-2016 book prints step 4 truncated ("4. Screw on Side Case(R) of Main Frame by using Phillips head Screw
Driver.") and step 6 with a duplicated clause; the CE850-2016 book prints both in full and is quoted above.
