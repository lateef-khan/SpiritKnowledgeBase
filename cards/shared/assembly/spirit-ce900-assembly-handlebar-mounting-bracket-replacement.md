1. Remove the **M10 screws (4 pcs)** on both sides to remove the **heartbeat bracket** (figure 6.16.1). Remove the
   electronic watch (console) before dismantling - the book says "refer to Procedure 6.3", which is the mast cover;
   the console is 6.1 (the CE1000ENT book correctly says 11.11).

That is the whole section. "Heartbeat bracket" is the book's name for the hand-pulse handlebar bracket. The three
books print it word for word apart from that cross-reference.
