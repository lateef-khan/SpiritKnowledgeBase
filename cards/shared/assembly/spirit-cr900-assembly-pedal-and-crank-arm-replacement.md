**6.4.1 Remove the pedals.** As shown in figures 6.4.1 and 6.4.2, using a **15 mm open-end wrench**, remove the **right pedal counterclockwise** and the **left pedal clockwise**.

**6.4.2 Remove the crank arm retaining bolt.** As shown in figure 6.4.3, use an **8 mm Allen wrench** to remove the crank arm bolt. **When re-installing the bolt, tighten it to 60 N-m of force (45 ft-lbs).**

**6.4.3 Remove the crank.** As shown in figure 6.4.4, use a **standard bicycle crank arm removal tool** to extract the crank arm.

**60 N-m (45 ft-lbs) is the only torque figure printed in either book.** The same 60 N-m is printed for the upright CU900 and CU900ENT crank screw; the recumbent adds the Allen size (8 mm) and the imperial equivalent.

**The left pedal is left-hand threaded** - it comes off clockwise. The owner's-manual assembly card for these machines is the one that prints the pedal torque (300 inch-pounds); this service page prints only the wrench. The preventative-maintenance page says to check the pedals are tight every week.

If there is a clicking sound or a loose feeling at the pedals, the troubleshooting chapter says: pedals tight, then crank arm bolts tight, then check for play in the axle and loose bearing-assembly nuts.

**The CR1000ENT book prints the same 60 N-m as 12.14 but drops the tool sizes**: right pedal counterclockwise, left pedal clockwise (figures 12.21, 12.22); "remove the cranked screws (about the same, please use 60 N-m when locking)" (figure 12.23); "use the puller to exit the crank" (figure 12.24). No 15 mm wrench, no 8 mm Allen and no 45 ft-lbs are printed there.
