Four tools, the same four in both books:

- **13/15mm wrench**
- **12/14mm wrench**
- **6 mm L Allen wrench**
- **Phillips screwdriver**

**One more than the CU800 carton**, which holds the two wrenches and the screwdriver and no Allen key. The 6 mm L Allen wrench is for the **four M8 x 65 socket head cap bolts (60)** that hold this machine's handlebars, with the 13 mm end of the first wrench on their nuts - the service manual releases the same bolts "with an Allen wrench and a 13 mm open wrench". The service manual's parts list numbers them **117 (13/15mm wrench), 100 (12/14mm wrench), 81 (6 L Allen wrench) and 93 (Phillips head screw driver)**.

The steps never name a tool by item number. The recumbent 4.0R carton is different again - five tools, with a 14/15mm, a 13 mm and a combination M6 Allen wrench. **A 14mm is in this carton** in the 12/14mm, the size the machine care page asks for on the levellers; the setting-up page asks for a 1/2" wrench for the same nut (the levelling card).
