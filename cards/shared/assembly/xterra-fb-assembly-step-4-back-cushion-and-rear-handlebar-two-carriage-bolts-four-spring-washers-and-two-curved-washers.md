Hardware step 4: **2 nylon nuts (#17) M8, 2 carriage bolts (#78) M8 x 50L, 2 flat washers (#55) 8.3 x 25 x 2t, 4 spring washers (#84) M8, 4 Allen screws (#80) M8 x 45L**, and - in the 2021 book's step 4 only - **2 curved washers (#13) 8.3 x 22 x 1.5t and 2 Allen screws (#81) M8 x 40L**. Tools: Allen key (72) and wrench (73).

1. Attach the back cushion support tube - the 2021 book says (75), the 2019 book says the Back Cushion Adjustment Set (No. 74) - to the seat post (03), and tighten with the 2 carriage bolts (78), 2 nylon nuts (17) and 2 flat washers (55).
2. Attach the back cushion (77) onto the back cushion support tube (75) with the 4 spring washers (84) and 4 Allen screws (80).
3. *(2021 book, in this step)* Attach the rear handlebar (76) to the back cushion adjustment set (74) with 2 curved washers (13) and 2 Allen screws (81). The 2019 book prints the rear handlebar as its own step 6 with the same hardware - the linked card.

Parts 74 and 75 are one shipped item, the "Back Cushion Support Tube Set (74/75)": 74 is the adjustment set that clamps to the seat post and 75 is the tube the cushion bolts to.
