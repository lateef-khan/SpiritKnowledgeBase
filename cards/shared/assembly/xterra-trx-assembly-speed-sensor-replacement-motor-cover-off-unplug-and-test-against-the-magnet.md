**TRX2500 GT90B and TRX3500/4500 GT90CD (11-8 "Speed Sensor Replacement (including the wire)")**:

1. First remove the motor top cover.
2. Remove the speed sensor wiring and replace the part.
3. **After replacement, test that the sensor registers the magnet.**

**TRX5500 GT90D (9-8)**: "Remove Motor cover then unplug Speed Sensor wire from Controller and replace
it." - no test is printed.

The books do not give the sensor-to-magnet gap or say how the sensor is fixed to the frame. **The TR260
and TRX1400 service manuals have no speed sensor procedure** - their replacement chapters go from the
roller/deck procedures straight to the incline motor. Speed-sensor adjustment as a cure for a speed
fault is on the maintenance and errors cards.
