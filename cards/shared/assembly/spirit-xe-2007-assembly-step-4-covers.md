## XE100 only - plastics assembly, five jobs

1. Connecting Arm Covers (2 each **57**, **58**) over the rod end and handle bar
   joint, with four **M5 x 15m/m Screws** and two **3.5x10m/m Self Tapping Screws**,
   using the short Phillips driver.
2. Two **Wheel Covers (52)** with four **M5x15m/m Phillips Head Screws (105)**.
3. Two Rear Stabilizer Covers **(19, 20)** and two Middle Stabilizer Covers
   **(19, 20)** on the rear rail assembly with four **M5 x 15m/m Screws (105)**, then
   the front stabilizer cover **(49)** with two more.
4. Front Handle Bar Covers **(54-1** left, **55-1** right) and Rear Handle Bar Covers
   (**54** left, **55** right) with six **3.5x10m/m Self Tapping Screws**.
5. Front **(122)** and rear **(121)** console mast covers with three **M5 x 15m/m
   Screws (105)** and two **3.5x12m/m Self Tapping Screws (87)**.

## XE200 only - the same five jobs, bigger cover sets

The XE200's connecting arm covers are **57, 57-1 and 58, 58-1** - four pieces, not
two - and take **eight** M5 x 15m/m Phillips Head Screws (105) and **eight**
3.5x12m/m self tapping screws (87), driven with the long Phillips driver rather than
the short one. Jobs 2 to 5 are the XE100's word for word, item numbers included.

**The XE200 panel is numbered 2 to 6 and has no item 1.** The five jobs are the
XE100's five; the numbering simply starts one late. Nothing is missing from the book.

## XE300 - cover assembly, and the only steel in any of the three builds

The XE300 fitted its connecting arm covers, handle bar covers and mast covers back in
steps 2 and 3. Its step 4 is seven jobs:

1. Two **Wheel Covers (52)** with four **M5x15m/m Phillips Head Screws (105)**.
2. Front stabilizer cover **(49)** with two **M5 x 15m/m Phillips Head Screws (105)**.
3. Six **L-brackets (149)** for the steel shroud, with twelve M5x15mm Phillips head
   screws - *the manual gives their item number as (84), which its own check list
   calls a 3/8" curved washer.*
4. Two **W-shaped brackets (148)** from under the aluminium tracks, with **5/16 x 3/4
   Hex Head mounting screws (91)** inserted from above the tracks and tightened with
   a 12mm wrench.
5. Remove the ten screws (105) holding the front covers (**150-3** left, **151-3**
   right) to the two steel shrouds (**150**, **151**) and take the covers off.
6. Lift the pedal arms, slide the steel shrouds over the wheels and onto the rails,
   line them up with the brackets and secure with eight **M5 x 15m/m Phillips Head
   Screws (105)**. Re-fit the front covers with the ten screws removed in job 5.
7. Fit the centre support brace by sliding its front tab between the plastic side
   case and the stabilizer tube, and secure the rear with two **5/16 x 3/4 Hex Head
   Bolts (91)**.

**The XE300 is the only one of the three with steel shrouds, L-brackets, W-brackets
and a centre support brace.** None of items 148, 149, 150 or 151 exists on the XE100
or XE200 build.

## The last line, on all three

"PLEASE ENSURE ALL FASTENERS ARE TIGHT AFTER THE COMPONENTS HAVE BEEN ASSEMBLED."

The same page carries one more note, in a callout beside the drawing: **the power
connector is on the front, left hand side of the unit.** That is the only thing the
assembly chapter says about power; there is no first power-on step and no self-test
description in it.

## Item 84 in XE300 job 3

The screws are described as "M5x15mm Philips Head Screws (84)". Every check list in
this book gives item 84 as a **3/8" x 23 x 1.5T curved washer** and gives M5 x 15m/m
Phillips head screws as item **105**. The size in the sentence and the item number in
the sentence disagree. Order by the size, not by that number.
