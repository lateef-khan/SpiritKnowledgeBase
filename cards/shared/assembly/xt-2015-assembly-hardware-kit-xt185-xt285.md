These manuals print no single hardware panel. Each step page carries its own
table; put together they read:

| Step | Item | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 132 | Nut Clip | M5 | 6 |
| 2 | 93 | Bolts | 5/16" x UNC18 x 15L | 8 |
| 3 | 88 | Tapping Screws | 5" x 16MM | 6 |
| 4 | 90 | Bolts | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | 4 |
| 4 | 82 | Split Washer | Ø10 x 2.0T | 4 |
| 5 | 142 | Sheet Metal Screws | Ø3.5 x 16L | 8 |

**The XT185-2015 and XT285-2015 kits are identical**, item number for item number
and quantity for quantity.

Wording to expect: the step-two text calls item 93 a **Button Head Socket Bolt**
while its table calls it **Bolts**; the step-four text calls item 90 a Button
Head Socket Bolt as well. One part number, one part.

Two things printed as they are, not corrected here:

- Item 88 is described **"5" X 16MM"**, mixing an inch mark with a millimetre
  length. The same odd description is printed for item 120 in the XT385-2015 and
  XT485-2015 manuals.
- The XT185-2015 step-three table is headed **HARDWARE FOR STEP 4** even though
  the page is headed STEP THREE. It is the step-three hardware; the XT285-2015
  manual heads the same table STEP 3.

**This kit does not fit the 2023 XT185 or XT285.** That machine's kit is item 70
(5/16" x 1"), item 153 (M5 x 15mm Phillips), item 93 (12 pcs) and item 132
(curved washer) - and on this 2015 machine item 132 is the M5 speed nut clip, not
a washer.
