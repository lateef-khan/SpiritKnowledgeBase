1. Follow the pedal arm replacement to take off both pedal arms.
2. Use a screwdriver to remove the **incline cover locking screw**, then take off the incline cover.
3. Use **two 14 mm box wrenches** to remove the **rear rail assembly locking bolts**, then take off the rear rail
   assembly.
4. Use **two 14 mm box wrenches** to remove the **incline bracket locking bolt**, then take off the incline bracket.
5. Reverse the steps to install all parts.

No bolt sizes are printed. The incline motor bolts to this bracket; its own replacement is with the gear motor
and controller board.

The XS300B-YS006 service manual is one book for both XS895 owner's-manual printings; the two-column page is read by step number.
