**Four books, one set of figures.** The XE795 books use `M8x15L` bolts and a 13 mm wrench at the same joint; the
CE800 books use four `M8*25mm` bolts. Do not carry those here.

1. Follow **procedure 2** (11-2) to take apart the handle bar (the XE295 book omits this line). Use a Phillips head
   screwdriver to release **3 pcs of 3.5x16mm sheet metal screws** to take apart the left and right console mast
   covers.
2. Use a **14 mm wrench** to remove the **hex head bolts 3/8" x 3/4"**, **curved washers 3/8" x 23 x 2T**, **external
   hex head bolts 3/8" x 2-1/4"** and **spring washer 3/8" x 2T** that secure the console mast.
3. Remove the console mast and console mast cover together, pull out the **control wires and incline wires**, and
   separate the covers from the console mast.
4. To dismantle the **handpulse assembly**, take the **round cap** apart first and use a Phillips head screwdriver to
   release **two tapping screws ψ3x20mm**. Pull the handpulse w/cable out from the bottom and pull the handpulse
   top cover upward. (The XE395 books word it: remove the two `Ø3x20` tapping screws on the handpulse bar, gently
   pull the wiring from the handpulse bottom cover and disconnect the connector on the top cover.)
5. XE195 book only: when the **handgrip end cap** is removed, it only needs to be pulled up and separated from the
   **resistance connecting cable**.
6. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
