1. **Using a razor knife (Box Cutter), cut the outside, bottom, edge of the box along the dotted line. Lift the box over the unit and unpack.** (The VE books drop the comma after "bottom".)
2. **Carefully remove all parts from the carton and inspect for any damage or missing parts. If damaged parts are found, or if parts are missing, contact your dealer immediately.**
3. **Locate the hardware package. Remove the tools first. Remove the hardware for each step as needed to avoid confusion. The numbers in the instructions that are in parenthesis (#) are the item number from the assembly drawing for reference.** (The 2007 and 2008 books say "The hardware is separated into steps" instead of the item-number sentence's lead-in; the AE25, WE, and 2012-2016 books say "The hardware is separated into four steps" first. The 2011 AE books number these 1-2-3; most other books leave them unnumbered after the heading.)

**The parenthesised numbers are drawing item numbers, not order codes.** This wording covers every B book with a pre-assembly section; the two E20 books (2016, 2020) print no unpacking section at all and start at the console mast.
