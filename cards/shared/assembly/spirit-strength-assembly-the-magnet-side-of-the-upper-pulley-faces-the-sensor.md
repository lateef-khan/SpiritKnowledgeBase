**The upper pulley is the one with magnets in it. The magnet side has to face and interact
with the sensor.**

Fit it the other way round and the machine builds and runs normally, but **the repetition
counter never counts** - the reed switch has nothing passing it. That is the fault this
note prevents, and it is invisible until the machine is finished and the shrouds are on.

The sensor is the dry reed switch at the top of the tower; its lead is the *Dry Reed Pipe
Connection Wire* named on the cable routing diagram. See
[that card](spirit-strength-assembly-cable-routing-diagram-names-two-wires.md).

## The item number for the magnet pulley is not the number in the note

The step table calls it **item 210, Pulley Set with Magnets**, and on most of these
machines it is a Φ89x32 pulley, the same size as the plain pulley sets beside it. **The
lat pulldown/seated row note calls it "the upper pulley (8)"** while its own table numbers
it 210 - the note and the table on that one page disagree. Go by the description, not the
number: only one pulley in the box has magnets in it.

**On the shoulder press the printed quantity for that pulley is 11.** It is one pulley;
see [the mismatched counts card](spirit-strength-assembly-fastener-counts-that-do-not-balance.md).

**The leg extension/leg curl has two 2025 printings and only one of them numbers it.** The
February printing lists `210 Pulley Set with Magnets Φ89*32` in its weight-stack table and
prints the full note. The **March** printing - the later book - has **no item 210 anywhere
in it**, lists two plain `Pulley sets Φ89*32` instead, and shortens the note to *"The upper
pulley is with magnets."* with no instruction about which way it faces. The machine is the
same; fit the magnet side towards the sensor whichever book you have.

**The pull-up/dip assist prints the note twice.** Its magnet pulley goes on at **step 1**,
with the note beside the door-frame table, and the note is repeated in the weight-stack
step. Its step 1 table lists `013 Pulley sets Φ89*32` and `210 Pulley Set with Magnets
Φ89*32` on consecutive rows, which is the clearest statement in the range that the two are
different parts of the same size.

The **CSD-CPSP chest press / shoulder press prints this note only in its May 2025 update** (4601US, 20250521); its February 2024 printing has no notes on the weight-stack page at all. Its table numbers the magnet pulley **210, Pulley Set with Magnets, φ89*32**, beside one plain pulley set (13); the 2024 table has two plain pulley sets and no 210.

The **CSS-DELT's 2026 revision** (SP-4312, 20260821, whose text layer is a shifted font - read from its OCR supplements and renders) prints this exactly as its 2025 printing does.
