**None of the fourteen manuals prints a procedure for setting the seat or the
handlebars.** No seat height, no fore-and-aft position, no leg-extension guide, no
handlebar reach or tilt - on any of them, recumbent or upright, from 2016 to 2023.

The adjusters exist. What each book says about them is this and no more:

- **The uprights** - XBU55 2019, 2021, 2023 and XBU55ENT - install a **Seat Adjustment
  Knob (86)** during step four, with a flat washer and a flange nut, to clamp the **Seat
  Mount Slide (7)** to its track. The step is how to *build* it, not how to *use* it. The
  XBU55 2023 diagram is the only page in the family that points at the knob, lettered
  **I**.
- **The recumbents** - all ten of them - never mention a seat adjuster in any step. Their
  seat rides on a **Seat Carriage (4)** that arrives on the main frame. The XBR55 2023,
  XBR95 2023, XBR25 2023 and XBR55ENT **exploded-view parts lists** carry a **Seat
  Front/Aft Adjustment Lever (167)** with a **Lever Anchor (168)**, and the upright lists
  carry a **Seat Up/Down Adjustment Knob (106)** - parts named in a table and explained
  nowhere.
- **The 2023 diagrams** letter a **Seat Slide Rail** on the recumbents and an
  **Adjustable Pedals** callout on all three, and say nothing further about either.

## How this was checked

All fourteen PDFs were searched on native text, hyphens rejoined and whitespace
collapsed, for *adjust* as a loose word with a seat, handlebar, saddle, knob, slide,
post, position, height or fore-aft word within seventy characters. The only matches are
the assembly step above, the parts-list rows above, the diagram callouts above, the M14
levelling sentence, and a console instruction about adjusting a target time.

Every page of every assembly chapter was then rendered at 200 dpi and read with OCR and
the result compared against `pdftotext`. **No page in any of the fourteen hides text
behind a picture**, so nothing has been missed to a flattened page.

## Do not take the indoor cycle answer instead

The Spirit CIC800, CIC850 and XIC600 manuals do print a seat and handlebar fitting
procedure - hip height, a five-degree bend at the knee, the shin bump over the pedal
axle. **Those are group-cycle machines with a different seat and a different post**, and
none of the fourteen bikes here prints any of it. See
[the cycle seat card](spirit-cycle-assembly-seat-height-and-fore-aft-position.md) and
[the cycle handlebar card](spirit-cycle-assembly-handlebar-height-and-fore-aft-position.md).

The CIC850 has the same gap for the same reason and its own card records it - see
[that card](../../cic850-2022/assembly/cic850-2022-assembly-no-seat-position-procedure.md).
