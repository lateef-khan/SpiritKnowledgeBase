Hardware: **2 Allen screws (#81) M8 x 40L** and **2 curved washers (#13) 8.3 x 22 x 1.5t**. Tool: the Allen key (72).

1. Attach the rear handlebar (No. 76) onto the back cushion adjustment set (No. 74) and secure with the 2 Allen screws (No. 81) and 2 curved washers (No. 13).

The 2019 book prints this as step 6, after the handlebar and console; the 2021 book folds the same sentence into its step 4 with the back cushion.
