1. **Slide the Console Mast Cover (41) onto the Console Mast (12)**, facing the correct way.
2. **Unravel the guiding wire** at the top opening of the Main Frame (1) and feed the **Computer Cable (31)** into the bottom of the Console Mast tube (12) and out of the top opening.
3. **Install the Console Mast (12) into the receiving bracket** on the top of the Main Frame (1). **One bolt is already installed** and engages the slot at the bottom of the Console Mast; it gets tightened at the end with the three other console mast bolts.
4. **Put the 3/8" Split Washer (96) onto the 3/8" x 2-1/4" Hex Head Bolt (60)** and the **two 3/8 x 1 Curved Washers (84) onto the two 3/8" x 3/4" Hex Head Bolts (103)**. The 3/8 x 2-1/4 bolt goes through the left side of the receiving bracket, into the Console Mast (12), threading into the right side. (The Computer Cable (31) runs through the tube; do not damage or pinch it.) Hand-tighten the two 3/8" x 3/4" bolts with Curved Washers (84) through the front of the receiving bracket.
5. **Using the 13/14m/m Wrench (106), tighten the three bolts plus the pre-installed fourth firmly, as much as you possibly can.**
6. **Three connectors sit at the top opening of the Console Mast (12): two Hand pulse connectors (36) and one Computer Cable (31).** Connect them to the mating keyed connectors on the back of the **Console Assembly (30)**; do not force them.
7. **Store the excess cable back into the Console Mast (12)**, install the Console Assembly onto the mounting plate, and secure with the **four M5 x 10m/m Phillips Head Screws (66)**. Do not pinch the wires between the console back and the mounting plate; that could short the wires and damage the electronics.
8. **Remove the white Styrofoam sheet inside the opening of the main plastic shroud.** It only stops hardware falling inside during assembly; left inside, it rubs moving parts and makes noise during exercise. **Snap the console mast cover into place.**
