1. Put the **end cap (41)** on the bottom of the front and rear stabilizer.
2. Use a rubber hammer when assembling.

That is the whole procedure. There is no removal step and no fastener; the cap is a push fit.

**The 2016 book calls part (41) the "Handgrip End Cap"** and the 2023 book the "End Cap", and the 2016 wording puts it "on the button of front and Rear Stabilizer" - "button" is a misprint for "bottom". Both books put the same part in the same place: the stabilizer tube ends, not the handgrips.

The CU800 (2020) and CU800ENT books, which share the rest of this chapter with the XBU55, do not print this section.
