**Untie the rod end bearing on the left Connecting Arm (8). Insert the Hex Head Bolt (122) through the rod end bearing bracket on the Swing Arm (10)**, and tighten with the **13/14mm wrench (186)** and **12/14 wrench (189)** together with a **5/16" x 20 x 1.5T Flat Washer (166)** and a **5/16" x 7T Nyloc Nut (155)**. Assemble the right Swing Arm (11) and right Connecting Arm (9) the same way.
