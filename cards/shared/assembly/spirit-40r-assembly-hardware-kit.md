Four tables, one per step, identical in both books. Item numbers are the assembly-drawing numbers.

## Hardware for step 1

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 65 | Hex Head Bolt | 3/8" x 2-1/4" | 4 |
| 71 | Hex Head Bolt | 3/8" x 2" | 4 |
| 77 | Flat Washer | Ø3/8" | 6 |
| 84 | Flat Washer | Ø3/8" | 4 |
| 89 | Nyloc Nut | 3/8" x 7T | 6 |
| 91 | Nyloc Nut | 5/16" x 6T | 1 |
| 115 | Flat Washer | Ø8.5 | 2 |
| 118 | Hex Head Bolt | 5/16" x 1-1/4" | 1 |
| 175 | Hex Head Bolt | 3/8" x 2-3/4" | 2 |

**Nine rows, because step one also fits the gas shock (118 and 115) and the cover (24) on nuts (91).** The step attaches the cover "with two nuts (91)" and the table lists one.

## Hardware for step 2

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 84 | Flat Washer | Ø3/8" | 2 |
| 89 | Nyloc Nut | 3/8" x 7T | 2 |
| 119 | Hex Head Bolt | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | 2 |

## Hardware for step 3

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 68 | Hex Head Bolt | 5/16" x 5/8" | 6 |
| 76 | Flat Washer | Ø5/16" | 6 |
| 82 | Split Washer | Ø8 x 1.5T | 2 |
| 83 | Curved Washer | Ø5/16" | 2 |
| 113 | Socket Head Cap Bolt | M8 x 25m/m | 2 |

**The front handlebars go on two M8 x 25 socket head cap bolts (113)**, not the 5/16" hex head bolts of the CR800; the service manual releases the same two with an Allen wrench.

## Hardware for step 4

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 99 | Phillips head screw | M5 x 12m/m | 10 |
| 105 | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 x 16m/m | 4 |
| 186 | Phillips head screw | M6 x 18m/m | 4 |

Ten of item 99: four for the stabilizer covers, four for the console, two for the chin cover. The seat cushion's four screws (186) are M6 x 18, which the service manual also names.

**No torque figure is printed for any bolt, and none for the pedals**; the pedal step says only to tighten as much as you possibly can. The Ø symbol is the manuals' diameter mark.
