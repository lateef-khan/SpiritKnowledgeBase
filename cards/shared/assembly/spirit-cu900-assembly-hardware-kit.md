Four tables, one per step. Item numbers are the assembly-drawing numbers.

## Step 1

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 52 | BOLT | M8XP1.25X20L | 4 |
| 53 | FLAT WASHER | 8.5X18X1.5T | 4 |
| 59 | SCREW | M5XP0.8X12L | 4 |
| 60 | FLAT WASHER | 13X23X2.0T | 3 |
| 61 | BOLT | see the linked card | 3 |

**Item 61 is the one line the three printings do not agree on.** Take it from
[the rear stabilizer bolt card](spirit-cu900-assembly-rear-stabilizer-bolt-size.md).

## Step 2

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 52 | BOLT | M8XP1.25X20L | 6 |
| 112 | FLAT WASHER | 8.5X18X1.5T | 6 |
| 54 | SCREW | M5XP0.8X12L | 4 |

## Step 3

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 53 | FLAT WASHER | 8.5x18x1.5T | 8 |
| 56 | BOLT | M8xP1.25x65L | 4 |
| 57 | LOCK WASHER | 8x1.5T | 4 |
| 58 | NYLOCK NUT | M8xP1.25x7T | 4 |
| 66 | SCREW | M5xP0.8x6L | 2 |

Eight flat washers for four bolts, because the step puts **four on each side of the
handlebars**.

## Step 4

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 54 | SCREW | M5xP0.8x12L | 2 |
| 55 | SELF-TAPPING SCREW | 3.5x12L | 4 |

## Two items numbered 53 and 112 are the same washer

Both are printed **8.5X18X1.5T FLAT WASHER**, at four in step 1, eight in step 3 and
six in step 2 under the other number. The manuals never say why the number changes.

**No torque figure is printed for any bolt** - the only torque in the whole chapter is
the 300 in-lb for the pedals in step 4. There is no table for the pedals themselves;
they thread straight into the crank arms.

Apart from item 61, all three printings print the same four tables with the same
quantities. The CU900ENT book is the CU900 2018 book with the headings renamed.
