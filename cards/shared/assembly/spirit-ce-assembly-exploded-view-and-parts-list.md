## Three manuals have neither

**ce800-2013, ce850-2016 and ce900-2021** print no exploded view and no parts list at
all. Their contents pages go from the workout chapters to the warranty. So the item
numbers their assembly steps use - and the CE850 2016 uses items **168** and **180**
that appear in no hardware panel - cannot be checked against anything in the book. See
[the CE850 2016 hardware card](../../ce850-2016/assembly/ce850-2016-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## The CES880 has drawings and no table

The CES880 manual prints two exploded views, headed **PRODUCT PARTS DRAWING (A)** and
**(B)**, and **no numbered parts list anywhere**. Every item number its fifteen
assembly steps use - and there are about forty of them - can be found only on those two
drawings. It is the only Spirit elliptical manual built that way.

## Do not read a part number off the drawing text

**On every one of these manuals the exploded view is a line drawing with no text layer
in the PDF.** Machine-read text off those pages returns callout numbers floating over
line art and is not reliable: the same page has produced "448", "V37", "422" and "011"
for numbers that are certainly three digits or fewer. **Read a callout off the drawing
itself, or take the number from the parts list where there is one.** The numbered lists
in the six manuals that have them are ordinary text and are trustworthy.

## Two drawing dates are printed on the drawings

The CE900 2025 exploded view carries **20230816** and the CE800 2023 exploded view
carries **2022.07.05** - both older than the manual's own effective date. They are
drawing revision dates, not manual dates, and neither is a model year.
