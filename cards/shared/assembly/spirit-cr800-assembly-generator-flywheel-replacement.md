1. Take both left and right front shrouds (29) (30) and the belt (54) off.
2. Unplug the electric wires connected to the **generator flywheel (55)**, then use an **11 mm wrench** to unscrew **4 pcs of 1/4" x 3/4" hex head screws (66)** with **4 pcs of 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers (72)** and **4 pcs of Ø1/4" split washers (80)**, and the generator flywheel (55) is released.
3. Follow the steps in reverse to refit the flywheel.

The 2020 CR800 book numbers this "Parts 1. 7 Flywheel" and its steps 2 to 4 - a numbering slip; there are three steps. The CR800ENT book prints them as steps 1 to 3.

**The 2009 XR898 and the XBR95 books print the identical four bolts and washers for the part they call the induction brake (55)**; that is the linked card. The belt figure to set afterwards (180 to 205 Hz) is in the belt procedure.
