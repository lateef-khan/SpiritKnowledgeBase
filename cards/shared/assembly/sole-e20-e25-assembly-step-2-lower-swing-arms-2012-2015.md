1. **Attach the Lower Swing Arms (L) and (R), (12) and (13)**, on each side of the Console Mast shaft; secure with **two Hex Head Bolts (104)** and **two Flat Washers (138)** using **Wrench (152)**.
2. **Insert the shaft of the left Connecting Arm (10) through the bushing on the left Pedal Arm (7)** and secure with a **Button Head Socket Bolt (115)** together with a **Flat Washer (138)** using the **M5 L Allen Wrench (149)**. Do the same for the right **Connecting Arm (11)** and right **Pedal Arm (8)**.
3. **Remove the Rod End Shaft (20), Flat Washer (136) and Hex Head Bolt (104)** on the left and right Connecting Arms. **Connect the Connecting Arm with the Lower Swing Arm** and secure with the **Rod End Shaft (20)**, **Flat Washer (136)** and **Hex Head Bolt (104)** using **Wrench (152)**.
