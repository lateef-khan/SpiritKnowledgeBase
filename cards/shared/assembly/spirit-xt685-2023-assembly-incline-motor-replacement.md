**Set the new incline motor to 225 mm before you fit it, and expect to need an M8 Allen key as well as the
14mm wrench.** The folding XT 2023 books use the wrench alone and fold the deck first; this deck does not fold.

1. Take off the motor cover.
2. Unplug the incline motor wires and unmount the incline motor from the treadmill.
3. Adjust the spare incline motor to the lower setting: **225mm**.
4. Install the incline motor back with a **14mm open-end wrench** and an **M8 L-Allen wrench**.
5. Plug the incline motor wires into the controller: **red wire to the UP port, white wire to the COM port,
   black wire to the Down port** (printed as callouts on the photograph, not in the step).

Both books print the same five steps; the XT685ENT book letter-spaces some lines in the extraction but reads
the same on the page. No torque is printed, the figure is given without saying between which points it is
measured, and neither book says to recalibrate the incline afterwards in this procedure.
