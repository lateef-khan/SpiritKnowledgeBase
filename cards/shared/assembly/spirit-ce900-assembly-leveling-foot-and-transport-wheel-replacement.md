1. Remove the **screw and cap** and remove the **wheel** (figure 6.18.1).
2. Move the wheel **counterclockwise** (figure 6.18.2). **Adjust the height of the four feet when not flat, until the
   machine balances.**

That is the whole section. Step 2's "move the wheel counterclockwise" reads as turning the levelling foot; the
book prints "wheel" for both parts. The owner's-manual levelling card names six pads and a 14 mm wrench; this
book names four feet and no tool. The three books print it word for word.
