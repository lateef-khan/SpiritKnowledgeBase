Hardware step 2: **1 button head socket bolt 5/16" x 3/4" (150), 1 split washer (46), 2 nyloc nuts (55), 2 button head socket bolts 5/16" x 1-3/4" (56), 1 flat washer 8.5 x 23 x 1.5T (100)**.

1. Locate the right swing arm (upper) (8) and slide it through the swing arm bushing (23); secure it to the lower swing arm (6) with two button head socket bolts (56) and two nyloc nuts (55). Tighten using the M5 Allen wrench (68).
2. Slide the swing arm axle (22) through the console mast bushings, then through the handle bar cover (78) and lower swing arm (6). Attach with one button head socket bolt (150), one split washer (46) and one flat washer (100). Tighten using the M5 Allen wrenches (68).

The same in all three books. The axle is pushed through from the right, so the right arm goes on first; the left arm goes onto the other end of the same axle in step 3.
