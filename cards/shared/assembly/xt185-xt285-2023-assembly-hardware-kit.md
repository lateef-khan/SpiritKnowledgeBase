| Item | Description | Qty | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|
| 70 | 5/16" x 1" Button Head Socket Bolt | 2 pcs | step 6, console |
| 153 | M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screw | 6 pcs | step 4, frame base covers |
| 93 | 5/16" x 15mm Button Hex Head Bolt | 12 pcs | 8 in step 3 (uprights), 4 in step 6 (console) |
| 132 | 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T Curved Washer | 2 pcs | step 6, console |

The 12 pieces of item 93 are split 8 and 4 across two steps, so do not use all
twelve on the uprights.

Two wording traps in this manual:

- The panel caption calls item 93 a **Hex Head Bolt**; assembly steps 3 and 6
  call the same item a **Button Head Socket Bolt**. It is one part number, one
  part.
- The page heading is TOOLS INCLUDED, but the four bullets under it - Button
  Head Socket Bolt (2pcs), Philips Head Screws (6pcs), Hex Head Bolts (12pcs),
  Curved Washer (2pcs) - are the hardware above, not tools.

This kit does not fit the XT385-2023, XT485-2023 or XT685-2023; those machines
use different item numbers and different quantities.
