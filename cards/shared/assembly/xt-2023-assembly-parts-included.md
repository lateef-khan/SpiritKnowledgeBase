All five machines print the same carton list:

- 1 Main Frame
- 2 Uprights
- 2 Console Mast Covers
- 2 Frame Base Covers
- 1 Console
- 1 Deck
- 1 Power Cord
- 1 Lubricant
- 1 Hardware Kit

The list is generic; the fasteners inside the hardware kit are **not** the same
from machine to machine, so check the kit list for the machine in front of you
before you start.

The XT685-2023 is the exception to the list itself: its carton list still names
2 Frame Base Covers, but its assembly steps never fit any — that machine has
Console Mast Side Caps (98, 99) and Upright Covers (52, 53) instead. Treat the
printed line as a shared boilerplate, not as a part to hunt for.
