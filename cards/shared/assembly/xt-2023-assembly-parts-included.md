The five 2023 machines and the XT685ENT (p. 9) print the same carton list, and so
do the XT185-2015 (p. 9) and XT285-2015 (p. 9), which write "1 Console Assembly"
where the others write "1 Console" and put the two cover lines in the other
order:

- 1 Main Frame
- 2 Uprights
- 2 Console Mast Covers
- 2 Frame Base Covers
- 1 Console
- 1 Deck
- 1 Power Cord
- 1 Lubricant
- 1 Hardware Kit

The list is generic; the fasteners inside the hardware kit are **not** the same
from machine to machine, so check the kit list for the machine in front of you
before you start.

The XT685-2023 is the exception to the list itself: its carton list still names
2 Frame Base Covers, but its assembly steps never fit any — that machine has
Console Mast Side Caps (98, 99) and Upright Covers (52, 53) instead. Treat the
printed line as a shared boilerplate, not as a part to hunt for. The XT685-2015
and the XT685ENT carry the same unused line, and the XT685ENT fits the same
Console Mast Side Caps (98, 99) and Upright Covers (52, 53) in its step four.

**This card does not cover the XT385-2015, XT485-2015, XT685-2015 or XT485ENT.**
Those three 2015 cartons add an Audio Cable, the XT485-2015 and XT685-2015 add a
Beverage Holder, and the XT685-2015 list names no Lubricant at all. The XT485ENT
carton matches the XT485-2015 exactly, Beverage Holder and Audio Cable included.
Two other cards carry those lists.
