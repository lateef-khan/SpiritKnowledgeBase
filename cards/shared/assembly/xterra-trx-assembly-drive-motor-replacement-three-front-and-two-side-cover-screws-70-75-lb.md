**The motor cover comes off in two stages - three screws at the front, then fold the treadmill for two
more at the side.** The TR260 and TRX1400 use four front screws instead.

**TRX2500 GT90B and TRX3500/4500 GT90CD (11-3)**:

1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **3 tapping screws** securing the motor cover.
2. **Fold the treadmill** and remove the **2 tapping screws** securing the motor cover.
3. Remove the motor **grounding wire (greenish yellow)**, motor wire (+) **red** and motor wire (-) **black**.
4. Use a **14 mm T-shaped socket wrench** to loosen the **4 screws** and a **14 mm open-end wrench** to loosen
   the **1 belt tension screw**; remove the motor and fit the new one. Reassemble in reverse, re-hook the
   belt (readjust it parallel with the main frame). Do not tighten the 4 securing screws yet.
5. With the 14 mm open-end wrench adjust the belt tension with a tension measuring device - **to the
   white LBS area between 70-75 lb** - then tighten the 4 securing screws with the T-socket wrench.
   Connect the grounding wire and the motor wires (**red M+, black M-**).

**TRX5500 GT90D (9-3)** - the same job in Dyaco's other wording: loosen the **3 motor cover front locking
screws**; fold the treadmill and loosen the **2 motor cover side locking screws**; unmount the ground wire
and 2 input wires (black and red); remove the **4 drive motor locking bolts with a 14 mm wrench** and
loosen the drive belt, then mount the new motor and its 4 bolts **without securing them**; adjust the
belt with a **14 mm open-end wrench**, measuring with a tension meter to **about 70-75 lb**; plug the input
wires to the controller (**red to M+ / black to M-**) and mount the ground wire.

The TRX3500/4500 chapter is printed as images and names neither GT90C nor GT90D, so it stands for both
machines.
