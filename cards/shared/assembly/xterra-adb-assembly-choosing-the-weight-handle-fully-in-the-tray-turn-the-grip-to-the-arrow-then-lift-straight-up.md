The same three steps in all three books:

1. Check the chart and note the **5 weight increments** - 2.5 to 12.5 lb on the ADB12.5PR, 5 to 25 lb on the ADB25, 11 to 55 lb on the ADB55 (the kg columns are a specs card).
2. **Choose the weight by rotating the middle of the handle grip. The handle must be fully inserted into the dumbbell tray to adjust the weight.** The arrows printed on the handle show the direction to rotate to the setting you want.
3. **Remove the handle directly up from the base.** The unused plates remain in the base.

The safety page adds the rule the other way round: never press the safety lock or turn the selector dial while the handle is lifted out of the tray. If the grip will not turn with the handle in the base, the handle is not fully seated or a plate is in the wrong groove or facing backwards - "all weight plates must have the selection tongue facing away from the handle grip" (the troubleshooting page; the errors section's card).
