Hardware supplied in the box with the Sole ST90-2020 and ST90-2021 (one SKU 590820 covers both ids), grouped by assembly step. The ST90 mounts its console on a drink-bottle holder between armrest rings, so its kit matches no other Sole treadmill.

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | #120 | Dia 3/8" x 16 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 8 pcs |
| 1 | #121 | Dia 10 x 1.5T | Split Washer | 8 pcs |
| 1 | #122 | M10 x P1.5 x 15L | Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 pcs |
| 2 | #120 | Dia 3/8" x 16 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| 2 | #121 | Dia 10 x 1.5T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 2 | #122 | M10 x P1.5 x 15L | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #123 | Dia 8.5 x Dia 16 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #124 | Dia 8 x 1.5T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #125 | M8 x 35 mm | Socket Head Cap Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 4 | #126 | 4 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 5 pcs |

Tools in the box: #128 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver, #127 M6 L Allen Wrench.

Ruling: the checklist and the step-4 hardware box both list five #126 screws, but the step-4 drawing shows four screw points and the step text says "secure with four 4x16mm Sheet Metal Screw (126)". This card follows the drawing and step text (four fitted screws; the fifth is a spare in the pack) and says so.
