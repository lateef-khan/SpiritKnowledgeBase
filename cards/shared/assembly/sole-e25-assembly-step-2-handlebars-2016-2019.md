1. **Install two Wave Washers (190)** onto the left and right sides of the Handle Bar axle.
2. **Slide the Left (10) and Right (11) Handle Bars** onto the axle. The small stickers mark L and R; face them the correct way.
3. **Install two Flat Washers (167) onto the two Hex Head Bolts (120)** in the threaded holes in the ends of the axle and tighten.
4. **Connect the Upper Resistance Handle Wire (55) with the Lower Resistance/Incline Handle Wire (57)**, store it in the frame tube, and **snap in the Switch Wire Cap (65)** on the console mast. Then connect the **Upper Incline Handle Wire (56)** with the Lower wire (57) the same way and snap in the second cap (65).
