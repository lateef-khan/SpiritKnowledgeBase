## What the tables print and what they do not

The **O** above is the diameter symbol the manuals draw. "T" is thickness: a washer
written O10XO25X2.0T is 10mm bore, 25mm outside, 2.0mm thick. **No torque figure is
printed for any bolt** - the only torque in the whole chapter is the 300 in-lb for the
pedals in step 4.

**Step 1 lists 5 self-tapping screws (117) and the step uses 5** - two on the
stabilizer covers and three on the console mast covers. **Step 3 lists 16 washers
(106) and the step uses 16** - four on the seat back frame and twelve on the rear
handlebar.

The 2018 book writes the descriptions with the P for pitch (M6XP1.0X12MM); the
CR900ENT book writes the same fasteners without it (M6x1.0x12mm) and drops the
diameter symbols. **The parts are the same; only the typography changed.** The 2025
book returns to the 2018 typography.
