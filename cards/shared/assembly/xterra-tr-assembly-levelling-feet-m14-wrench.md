"Use a M14 Wrench to adjust the height of the Leveling Feet."

That sentence is the whole of the Leveling paragraph on the SETTING UP YOUR TREADMILL page in all four
books. They do not say how many feet there are, which way to turn them or how to check the machine is
level, and the **M14 wrench is not among the tools included** (the TRX1400 ships one combination tool;
the TR65/75/85 ship that plus an M6 Allen key and a 13 mm wrench).

The other Xterra treadmills print no levelling instruction at all - a loose grep for level/leveler
across the TR150, TR200, TR260, TR300, TR6.4, TR6.6, TRX1000 and TRX2500-5500 books finds nothing, and
the WS200/WS300 only label "J. Levelers" on their parts diagram - except the two Hiker models, whose
knobs turn one way to move and the other to level; see the linked cards.
