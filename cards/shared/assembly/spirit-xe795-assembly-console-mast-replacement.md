**Both XE795 books print the same figures.** The XE195/295/395 books use `3/8"` bolts and a 14 mm wrench at the
same joint; the CE800 books use four `M8*25mm` bolts.

1. Remove the swing arms (2016: refer to 11.2; 2023: refer to 9.2).
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **3 pcs of 3.5x16mm sheet metal screws** to take apart the left and
   right console mast covers.
3. Use a **13 mm wrench** to remove the **hex head bolts M8x15L**, **curved washers 5/16" x 23 x 2T** and **spring
   washer 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T** that secure the console mast.
4. Remove the console mast and console mast cover together, pull out the **control wires and incline wires**, and
   separate the covers from the console mast.
5. To dismantle the **handpulse assembly**, take the **round cap** apart first and use a Phillips head screwdriver to
   release **two tapping screws ψ3x20mm**. Pull the handpulse w/cable out from the bottom and pull the handpulse
   top cover upward.
6. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
