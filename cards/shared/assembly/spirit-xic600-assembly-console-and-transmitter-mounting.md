## Console

1. Install the **mounting clamp (2)** to the back of the **console (1)** with the
   **clamp mounting screw (3)**.
2. Install and slightly tighten the **thumb screw (4)**, then adjust the console angle
   for optimal visibility. Once the angle is right, keep tightening the screw until the
   console is securely attached to the handlebar.

## Speed transmitter

Plug the **speed sensor wire** into the **transmitter (6)**, then press the
transmitter - Velcro is pre-attached to its back - onto the Velcro half already fitted
to the bike frame.

**The frame Velcro is on the back side of the chain cover, below the left crank.**

## Batteries and pairing are not on this card

STEP FIVE also covers installing 2 AAA batteries in each of the console and the
transmitter, and the synchronising procedure that must follow any battery change. Those
are console facts: see
[the battery card](../console/spirit-bike-console-xic600-two-aaa-batteries-each.md) and
[the synchronising card](../console/spirit-bike-console-xic600-synchronising-the-transmitter.md).
This card covers only the physical mounting. The transmitter must be synchronised to the console **before** either is
attached to the bike.
