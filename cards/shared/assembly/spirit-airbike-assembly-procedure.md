Numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing item numbers. Quantities are on the
hardware card for your machine - the step three kits are not the same.

**Steps one, two and four are word for word the same in both manuals.** Step three is
not: the AB950 has armrest handles and pedal bars the AB900 does not.

## Step one - stabilizers *(both)*

1. Use **WRENCH (100)** to fasten the **FRONT STABILIZER (2)** onto the **MAIN FRAME
   (1)** with two sets of **HEX HEAD BOLTS (84)** and **FLAT WASHERS (71)**.
2. Use WRENCH (100) to fasten the **REAR STABILIZER (5)** onto the MAIN FRAME (1) with
   two sets of **CARRIAGE BOLTS (50)**, **FLAT WASHERS (71)** and **CAP NUTS (135)**.

## Step two - console base *(both)*

1. Insert the **CABLE (23)** through the hole of the **CONSOLE BASE (10)** carefully.
2. Use **SCREW DRIVER (93)** to fasten the console base onto the main frame with three
   **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (90)**.

## Step three - handlebars and rocker links

**AB900:**

1. Insert one **STAR WASHER (148)** on the **LEFT (3)** and **RIGHT (4) HANDLEBAR**
   before fastening them onto the main frame with **WRENCH (95)**, the 22mm.
2. Attach the handlebars to the **ROCKER LINKS (8)** on both sides with two sets of
   **BOLTS (136)**, **ROD END BEARINGS (64)**, **FLAT WASHERS (81)** and **NYLOC NUTS
   (75)**.
3. Fasten all bolts and washers with **WRENCH (94)** and **ALLEN WRENCH (126)**.

**AB950:**

1. Attach the **LEFT (3)** and **RIGHT (4) HANDLEBAR** to the **ROCKER LINKS (8)** with
   two sets of **BOLTS (136)**, **ROD END BEARINGS (64)**, **FLAT WASHERS (81)** and
   **NYLON NUTS (75)**, fastened with **WRENCH (94)** and **ALLEN WRENCH (126)**.
2. Attach the two **PEDAL BARS (48)** to the left and right handlebars.
3. Fasten the **LEFT ARMREST HANDLE (51)** to the left handlebar with one **SOCKET HEAD
   CAP BOLT (159)** using **ALLEN WRENCH (157)**, then insert an **OVAL END CAP (54)**
   into the opening of the handlebar. Repeat with the **RIGHT ARMREST HANDLE (52)** on
   the right.

**There is no star washer and no 22mm wrench in the AB950 build**, and the AB900 has no
armrest handles, pedal bars or end caps.

## Step four - console, pedals, seat and arm covers *(both)*

1. Connect the **CABLE (23)** to the **CONSOLE SET (34)**.
2. Fasten the console set onto the console base with four **SCREWS (58)**, using screw
   driver (93).
3. Fasten the **LEFT PEDAL (45L)** onto the **LEFT CRANK ARM (16L)** and the **RIGHT
   PEDAL (45R)** onto the **RIGHT CRANK ARM (16R)** with **WRENCH (100)**.
4. Fasten the **SEAT (19)** onto the **SLIDING SEAT MOUNT (7)** - **AB900 with WRENCH
   (100)**, **AB950 with WRENCH (158)**, the 13/15mm.
5. Fasten the **LEFT CONNECTING ARM COVER (144)** onto the left handlebar with one
   **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREW (90)**, and the **RIGHT COVER (143)** onto the right handlebar
   with another.

**The AB950 adds a note the AB900 does not print:** verify the cable harness is not
kinked and is out of the way of the brackets when connecting the console mast to the base
of the machine.

## Then test the bike
