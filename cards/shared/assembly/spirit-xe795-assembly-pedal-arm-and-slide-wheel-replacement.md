**Both XE795 books free the pedal arm from the cross bar axle with one `M10x20mm` socket bolt and an 8 mm L Allen
wrench.** The 2016 book adds the cover screw size and the wave-washer order; the 2023 book adds figure numbers.

1. First remove the connecting arm (2023: refer to 9.3; the 2016 book misprints this as "follow the procedure 2").
2. Remove the **M10 bushing round end cap** from the 5-way pipe (2016 wording) and use an **8 mm L Allen wrench** to
   release the **M10x20mm socket head cap bolt** and **M10 bushing washer**.
3. Remove the pedal arm from the **axle of the cross bar**.
4. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **slide wheel cover** (2016: **two Phillips head screws M5x15mm**).
5. Use circlip pliers (2023: "C-clamp") to remove the **C-ring** (2016: **circlip Ø17**) and remove the sliding wheel.
6. Change the slide wheel and reassemble in the reverse order. The 2016 book gives the stack: **Ø17 x 0.5T wave
   washer, Ø78 slide wheel (urethane), Ø17 x 0.5T wave washer, Ø17 C ring**.
