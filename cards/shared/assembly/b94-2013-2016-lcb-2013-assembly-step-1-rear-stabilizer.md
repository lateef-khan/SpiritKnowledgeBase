Hardware: **#50** 3/8" x 2-1/4" Hex Head Bolt (2 pcs), **#58** M5 x 12mm Phillips Head Screw (2 pcs), **#71** 3/8" x 25 x 2T Flat Washer (2 pcs).

1. Attach the Rear Stabilizer Cover (41) to the Rear Stabilizer (5) with two Phillips Head Screws (58). Tighten with the Phillips Head Screw Driver (93).
2. Attach the Rear Stabilizer (5) to the Main Frame (1) by sliding two Flat Washers (71) onto two Hex Head Bolts (50); insert the bolts through the **Rear Stabilizer Attachment Plate (131)**, then into the rear stabilizer. Tighten using the **12/14 mm Wrench (100)**.

Fit the cover before the stabilizer goes on the frame - once the stabilizer is bolted down the cover screws are hard to reach.

**The LCB 2016 prints a rewritten version of this step** with the same three parts but an extra instruction about bolt direction, and is covered by its own card. The 2019 and 2023 machines use a carriage bolt and cap nut instead of the hex head bolt.
