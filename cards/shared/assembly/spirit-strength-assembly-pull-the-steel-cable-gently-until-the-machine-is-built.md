**When the resistance box and the track are not fully assembled, the unit can be powered
on, but resistance cannot be set and the operation interface should not be used.**

**When pulling the steel cable at that stage, do so gently - with a force of less than
8 kg. Excessive force is strictly prohibited.**

Pulling harder than that **may cause the steel cable inside the resistance box to jam or
become damaged**. If it happens, the manual's remedy is to **open the rear cover and
follow the after-sales guidance to restore functionality** - it does not print the
procedure.

This is the only force limit anywhere in the Spirit strength range, and it applies only
while the machine is part-built. Once the resistance box and track are together, the
console sets the resistance and the limit no longer applies.

The note is printed beside the step two drawing, on the same page as the screen cover
cut-out - see [that card](spirit-strength-assembly-cut-the-screen-cover-out-wearing-gloves.md).
