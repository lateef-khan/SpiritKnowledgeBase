**To raise the seat:** loosen the knob one or two turns and lift up from under the rear of the seat. The knob has a **spring-loaded pin** that will automatically seat in the slots in the seat post at each position. Once the seat is at the desired setting, **tighten the knob** to ensure the seat post won't accidentally slide downward during use.

**To lower the seat:** loosen the knob, then hold under the rear of the seat and **pull the knob out** to disengage the pin from the seat post. Lower the seat position to just below the desired setting, then release the knob and **pull the seat up until the pin clicks into place**, and tighten the knob.

There is a **numbered scale on the seat post, under the seat**, for repeatable settings.

Both books print it word for word. The knob is the **Adjustment Knob, Seat Vertical (106)** of the 7.0U service manual, which replaces it by releasing it counterclockwise after the screws on both sides come off, and sends a console mast that sways to "the knob and quick release locking". **The 8.0U replaces the knob with a lever and a self-locking post**; that is its own card. The 4.0U diagram labels it G, Vertical Seat Adjustment Knob.
