1. Use an open wrench to unscrew **6 pcs of 3/8" x 3/4" hex head bolts (176)** and **6 pcs of 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washers (77)** to take the **seat back frame (5)** off.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **4 pcs of M5 x 15 mm Phillips head screws (136)** and take the **seat back cover (128)** off.
3. Use the combination M5 Allen wrench to release the **M8 x 15 mm button head socket bolt (179)** and take the **seatback cushion (63)** off.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.

**Step 4 in the 2020 CR800 and CR800ENT books reads "Follow steps in order to resume the flywheel"** - copied from the flywheel procedure. The seat back has no flywheel; the 2009 XR898 book prints "Reverse the procedures to resume parts", which is what is meant.

The 2020 books head this "Disassembling/Assembling of Seat Carriage Cover and Seat Back Bracket"; the 2009 book heads it "Seat Back, Seat Back Cover and Seat Back Frame". The parts and fasteners are the same in all three. The XBR mesh seat back uses different hardware and has its own card.
