1. **Slide two Wave Washers (212)** onto each side of the swing arm axle. **Slide the lower swing arms (25 Left, 26 Right)** onto the axles and secure with the **two Hex Head Bolts (116)** and **Flat Washers (160)**. Do not force the swing arms; they were fitted at the factory, so jiggle them to line up.
2. **Remove the tie holding the spacer (19)** in the rod at the end of the right pedal arm, line up the rod end with the bracket at the bottom of the lower right swing arm (26), and slide the **Hex Head Bolt (114)** through the bracket and the spacer (19). Fit the **Flat Washer (154)** and **Nyloc Nut (143)** and tighten as much as possible. Repeat for the left side (25), tightening with **Wrenches (169 & 172)**.
