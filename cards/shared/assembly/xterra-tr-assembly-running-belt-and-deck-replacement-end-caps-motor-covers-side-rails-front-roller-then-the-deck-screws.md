Chapter 2 of both JKEXER service manuals, "Steps to replace the running belt/running deck", in the
printed order (the TR95H numbers the sub-steps 1, 2; the TR75H letters them a, b):

1. **2-1 End cap covers.** Loosen the screws fixing the left and right end cap covers, and remove the
   covers **upwards**.
2. **2-2 Motor lower cover.** Loosen the screws fixing the motor lower cover.
3. **2-3 Motor cover.** Loosen the screws fixing the motor cover. On the back of the running deck, **loosen
   a bit the screws fixing the side rails (no need to remove them)**. Push the left and right side rails
   back. Remove the front motor cover **upwards**.
4. **2-4 Side rails.** Remove (pull) the left and right side rails **from the front**.
5. **2-5 Front roller** - "only needs to loosen the single screw for the rear roller". Loosen the front
   roller fixing screws and remove the front roller. **Loosen only the screw on the right side of the
   rear roller; there is no need to remove the rear roller.**
6. **2-6 Belt and deck.** Remove the screws fixing the deck and take off the deck and running belt.

No screw counts, sizes, tools or torque are printed for any of these steps, and nothing is said about
re-tensioning or centring the belt afterwards. Removing the rear roller itself is chapter 3 - a
separate card - and it presupposes these steps.
