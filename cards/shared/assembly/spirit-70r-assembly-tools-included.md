Eight captioned drawings under **Tools**, the same eight with the same item numbers in the 2025 Spirit book and the 2021 Dyaco MED edition:

| Item | Tool |
|---|---|
| 112 | 12.14 m/m open wrench |
| 114 | Phillips head screwdriver |
| 132 | 14.15 m/m open wrench |
| 200 | 5 m/m L Allen wrench |
| 283 | 8 m/m L Allen wrench |
| 201 | Short Phillips head screwdriver |
| 284 | 13.14 m/m open wrench |
| 280 | 10 m/m wrench single |

**Eight tools is the most any Spirit bike carton holds** - the 4.0R packs five, the 7.0U four. The short screwdriver is for the brake lever covers in step four: "the top screws need to be tightened with the short screw driver". Step one names the 12.14 mm (112) and 14.15 mm (132) wrenches for the handle bar bolts and nuts; no step names the Allen wrenches, the 13.14 mm or the 10 mm, and no step of the build uses an Allen bolt - the M8 x 30 armrest bolts of step two are driven "by using Phillips head screw driver (114)".

The service manual's parts list carries all eight under the same numbers. The 2021 book prints the panel across two pages, four tools a page, in the order 112, 114, 132, 200, 283, 201, 284, 280.
