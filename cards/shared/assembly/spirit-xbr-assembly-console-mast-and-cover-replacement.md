1. Take the console off (the console card).
2. Use the **12/14 mm wrench (112)** to release **2 pcs of 5/16" hex head screws (68)**, **2 pcs of split washers** and **2 pcs of 5/16" x 1.5T flat washers (76)**, and the **handlebar assembly (3)** is released.
3. Reverse step 2 to refit the handlebar (3).
4. Separate the **console mast cover (31)** from the **front shrouds (L/R) (29) (30)** at the left and right sides of the seam with a fingernail or something flat, release the latches that lock the cover and the shrouds together, and pull the console mast cover (31) up. Unscrew **6 pcs of 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts (68)**, **4 pcs of 5/16" x 18 mm x 1.5T flat washers (76)** and **2 pcs of 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T curved washers (83)** with the 12 mm wrench, and pull the **console mast (2)** out.
5. To refit, pull the cables through the hole in the console mast, then use the 12 mm wrench to tighten **6 pcs of 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts (68), 6 pcs of 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T washers (76) and 2 pcs of 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T curved washers (83)**.

**Four flat washers come off and six go back**, in every book.

**Which cables go up the mast is printed two ways.** The XBR25 2016, XBR55 2016 and XBR55ENT books say "guide the computer cable (44) and hand pulse sensor assembly w/cable (45) through the console mast and out of the console securing plate" - the 2020 CR800 wording. The XBR95 2016, XBR55 2023 and XBR95 2023 books say "pull computer cables (44), handgrip sensor wire, and switch cable (upper) (133) through the hole of console mast" - three cables, and they number the switch cable (133), which is the coiled hand-pulse wire's number elsewhere in the same books (the switch cable is (148) in the console and rear-shroud procedures).

**The handlebar bolt and washers are dimensioned two ways for the same part numbers.** XR329 2016 and ENT: "5/16" x 5/8" hex head screws (68), 5/16" x 1.5T split washers, 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T flat washers (76)". XBR95 2016 and the 2023 books: "5/16" x 15 hex head screws (68), Ø8 x 1.5T split washers, 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T flat washers (76)". 5/8" is about 15 mm; it is one bolt.
