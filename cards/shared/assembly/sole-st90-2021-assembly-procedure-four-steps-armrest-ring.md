Four illustrated assembly steps for the slat-belt ST90-2020/2021. The console mounts on a drink-bottle holder (6) between armrest rings, not directly on the uprights (hardware item numbers in brackets).

1. Place the left upright (5) and right upright (4) on the main frame and secure with eight M10 x P1.5 x 15L Button Head Socket Bolts (122) through eight Dia 10 x 1.5T Split Washers (121) and eight Dia 3/8" x 16 x 1.5T Flat Washers (120) on the main frame (1), using the L wrench (127).
2. Pass Cable Lower (45) and Connecting Wire (74) of the right upright (4) through Armrest Ring (R) (131) to connect with Network Connecting Cable Upper (41), Computer Cable Upper (44) and Connecting Wire (73) of the Drink Bottle Holder (6); pass Connecting Wire (74) of the left upright (5) through Armrest Ring (L) (132) to Connecting Wire (73) of the holder. Install the armrest rings (131/132) on the uprights, join them with the drink-bottle holder (6), and secure with four bolts (122) through four washers (121) and four washers (120). Then plug Upright Cover (L) (55) and Upright Cover (R) (54) into the uprights.
3. Connect Network Cable Upper (41), Computer Cable Upper (44), Connecting Wire XHP-10 (68) and Connecting Wire XHP-8 (70) of the drink-bottle holder (6) to Network Cable (83-6), Computer Cable (83-7) and Connecting Wires (83-8, 83-9) of the Console Assembly (83). Place the console (83) on the holder (6) and secure with four M8 x 35 mm Socket Head Cap Bolts (125) through four Dia 8 x 1.5T Split Washers (124) and four Dia 8.5 x Dia 16 x 1.5T Flat Washers (123).
4. Place the PAD holder on the console assembly (83) and secure with 4 x 16 mm Sheet Metal Screws (126) using the Phillips driver (128). Ruling: the checklist and hardware box list five screws but the drawing shows four screw points and the step text says "four"; this card follows the drawing and step text (four fitted, one spare).
