1. Remove the connecting arm set and the swing axis by the swing assembly and linked assembly procedures.
2. Remove the **2 pcs M5 x 10 mm Phillips head screws** fixed on the **slide wheel cover**, divide the slide wheel
   cover from the connecting arm set, then remove the swing axis from the connecting arm set.
3. Use **C-ring pliers** to release the **Ø17 C-ring** and take apart the slide wheel and pedal.
4. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **M5 x 10 mm head screws** securing the pedal and remove the pedal - the
   CS800 (2020) book counts them: **4 screws (M5*10 mm)**.
5. Reassemble in the reverse order.

**Both generations print the same screws and the same Ø17 circlip.** A broken pedal is replaced by this card
(the 2020 book's troubleshooting says so); a vibration felt through the feet when pedalling is the slide wheel's
bearing, replaced here. The Sole SC200 connecting-arm card is the same procedure.
