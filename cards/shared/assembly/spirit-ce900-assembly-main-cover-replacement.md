1. Remove the **M5 screw (8 pcs)** and remove the **left and right chain cover** (figures 6.6.1 to 6.6.3).

That is the whole section; no refit line is printed. The three books print it word for word. Eight screws is the
count for both covers together as printed; the book does not split it left and right.
