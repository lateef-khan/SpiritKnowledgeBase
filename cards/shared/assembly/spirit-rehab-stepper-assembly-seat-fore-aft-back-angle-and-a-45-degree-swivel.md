**Three adjustments, two of them on brake handles at the handle bar and one on a handle behind the
seat.**

| Adjustment | Control | Repeatable by |
|---|---|---|
| **Fore / aft** | squeeze the brake handle on the **left** side handle bar | numbered scale on the aluminium seat slide tube |
| **Seat back angle** | squeeze the brake handle on the **right** side handle bar | numbered scale just below the seat back cushion |
| **Swivel** | lift the handle **behind the seat** | latches every **45 degrees** |

- **Fore / aft.** Squeeze the left handle, move the seat, lower the handle, then move the seat
  slightly until the lock clicks into place. The position is read where **the front of the seat
  carriage lines up with the number on the scale**.
- **Seat back angle.** Squeeze the right handle and move the seat back.
- **Swivel.** Lift the rear handle to disengage the latch, rotate, and lower the handle as you
  approach the position so the latch engages. **The seat latches every 45 degrees** - eight
  positions in a turn.

**The left lever has a grey cable and the right lever a black one**, set that way during assembly,
and the levers are positioned so a patient can reach them - `ms300-2021-assembly-procedure`.

## Two straps and a rotating handle

- **Pedal straps.** Remove the ankle strap from the chrome ring, loosen the foot strap enough for
  the foot to slide under, then reattach the ankle strap and adjust both for a snug fit.
- **Seat belt.** Snap the buckles together and adjust the strap.
- **The handles rotate** so that wrist patients can use the upper body without discomfort. This is a
  free rotation, not a setting.

## If an adjustment will not lock

That is a stretched lever cable, taken up on the thumb nuts behind the levers -
`spirit-med-stepper-errors-seat-will-not-lock-thumb-nuts`. **Do not force the handle.**

## The console can set the position for you

Set Up → Position takes the patient's height and calculates the seat and arm positions -
`spirit-rehab-stepper-console-set-up-key-position-by-height-and-the-track-or-step-graph`.

## On the 7.5S the seat also comes off

Pull the pins, hold the release lever, and slide the whole seat assembly off the rear, so a
wheelchair can be anchored in its place -
`spirit-rehab-stepper-assembly-sliding-the-seat-off-and-anchoring-a-wheelchair`. That is a fourth
thing the seat does and it is not on the 7.0S or the MS300.
