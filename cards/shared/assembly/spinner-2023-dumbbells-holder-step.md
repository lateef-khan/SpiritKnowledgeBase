This step needs no hardware from the assembly pack.

1. Unscrew the Quick Release lever (38) and place the Dumbbells Holder (27) on the Sliding Seat Mount (6) to lock the Quick Release lever (38).

**The step number differs between the two machines** - step 4 on the SB900 2023, step 5 on the SB1200 2023 - but the instruction is word for word the same and uses the same item numbers.

No earlier Sole indoor cycle has a dumbbells holder; there is no equivalent step before 2023.
