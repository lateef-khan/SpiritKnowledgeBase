1. Remove the 2 screws (1)(2), pull the display (5) support upward, and disconnect the data cables (3)(4).
2. Remove the 2 screws (6) and remove the rear cover (7) of the monitor.
3. Disconnect the data cables (11)(13) and remove the 4 screws (8) to remove the screen (12); remove the 4 screws
   (10) to separate the display base (9) from the display stand (14).

No tool is named for this step. The screen is the 10.1 inch capacitive TFT panel the specs chapter describes; its
communication line runs in two sections (cables 12 and 13 in the cable chapter).

The "Digital Resistance Module" is the i-Strength line's motorised resistance box, part 72 in the CSI owner's manuals; the screw numbers are the call-outs on the manual's exploded drawings.
