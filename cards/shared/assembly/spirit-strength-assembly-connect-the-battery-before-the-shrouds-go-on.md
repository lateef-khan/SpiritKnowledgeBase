Two things in one note, in this order:

1. **Connect the batteries and the display connection before installing the covers.** The
   manual points at its own counter page for how - the repetition and time counter runs on
   **2 C batteries**, alkaline recommended, and they are not supplied.
2. **Install the Front Shroud first, then the Back Shroud, then the upper shield.**

Do the covers first and you have to take them off again to reach the battery box and the
display lead, which is the mistake the note exists to prevent.

The hardware for all four covers is
[the same eight rows on every weight-stack machine](spirit-strength-assembly-the-shroud-hardware-panel.md).

The guide rods are lubricated in the step before this one, for the same reason - they are
behind the covers afterwards. See
[that card](spirit-strength-assembly-lubricate-the-guide-rods-before-the-shrouds.md).

The counter itself, its buttons and its battery change are console and maintenance
questions and live on their own cards.

The **CSD-CPSP chest press / shoulder press prints this note only in its May 2025 update** (4601US, 20250521), word for word, under a ten-row shroud table; its February 2024 printing has no note on that page.

The **CSS-DELT's 2026 revision** (SP-4312, 20260821, whose text layer is a shifted font - read from its OCR supplements and renders) prints this exactly as its 2025 printing does.
