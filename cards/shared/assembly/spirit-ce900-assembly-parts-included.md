- 1 Main Frame
- 1 Console
- 1 Console Mast
- 1 Center Handle Bar
- 2 Side Handle Bars
- 1 Handle Bar Bracket
- 1 Cup Holder
- 2 Console Mast Covers
- 2 Console Bracket Covers
- 1 Hardware Kit
- 2 Swing Arm Hinge Covers

**Both printings list the same eleven lines, in the same order**, hardware kit second
from last. Nothing was added or removed between 2021 and 2025.

**"1 Cup Holder" is what the 2025 manual's own diagram calls a water bottle holder.**
Same part, two names in one book; see
[the bottle holder card](spirit-ce-assembly-water-bottle-holder.md).

**No power cord.** The CE900 is generator-powered - start pedalling and the console
comes on - so there is nothing to plug in and nothing missing. The CE850 carton does
list a cord; that is a different machine.

**No levellers, no transport wheel and no foot pedals on this list**, although the
2025 diagram labels a transport wheel and pedals and the noise item names six
levelling pads. Those parts arrive attached to the main frame; the panel counts only
what is loose in the carton.

The **tools** are printed in a second panel on the same page and are on their own
card - see [tools included](spirit-ce900-assembly-tools-included.md).
