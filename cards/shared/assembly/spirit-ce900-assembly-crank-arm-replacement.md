**The only torque in the CE900 family's replacement chapter: 100 N-m on the crank screw, both sides.**

1. Remove the **M10x60L screw (1 pc)** and **M10x15L screw (1 pc)** (figure 6.7.1); you can then remove and replace
   the crank.
2. Remove the **crank screw** (figure 6.7.2). **Use 100 N-m when locking it. Right side and left side are the
   same.**
3. For crank and pedal assembly the CE900 / CE900ENT books say "refer to process 6.4.1 and process 6.4.2" - 6.4 is
   the front cover and has no 6.4.2; the CE1000ENT book says "refer to process 11.71", this section itself.

The CU900 / CR900 bike books print the same template with a 60 N-m crank figure; that is a different machine and
a different number.
