**Use the M8 L Allen Wrench (182) to release the two Socket Head Cap Bolts on the mainframe that secure the mast tube, then remove and abandon the Side Back (196).** Do this before the incline-rail steps. (The E95s books describe a plainer variant with the Allen Wrench (193); see that card.)
