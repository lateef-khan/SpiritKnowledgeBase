Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. Hardware quantities are
on [the hardware card](spirit-cr800-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Step one - rear stabilizer and seat back bracket

1. Install the **Rear Stabilizer (No.7)** onto the **Main Frame (No.1)** with 2 Hex
   Head Bolts (No.65) and 2 Curved Washers (No.203).
2. Install the **Seat Back Bracket (No.5)** to the **Seat Carriage (No.4)** using 6 Hex
   Head Bolts (No.176) and 6 Flat Washers (No.77).

## Step two - rear handlebar and hand pulse wires

1. Install the **Rear Handlebar (No.6)** onto the Seat Carriage (No.4) with **4 Hex
   Head Bolts (No.71) installed through the top holes** and secured with 4 Flat Washers
   (No.77) and Nylon Nuts (No.89). Install **2 Hex Head Bolts (No.175) through the side
   holes** and secure with 2 Flat Washers (No.77) and Nylon Nuts (No.89).
2. Plug the **Left Hand Pulse Wire (No.27)** into the corresponding **Left Socket
   (No.42)** in the left plastic side case under the seat carriage, and the **Right
   Hand Pulse Wire (No.21)** into the **Right Socket (No.26)**.

**The step text asks for four bolts (No.71) and two (No.175); the hardware table for
this step lists two of each and four nylon nuts.** The table and the step disagree on
the count of No.71, in both printings. Six bolts and six nuts go through the joint by
the step's own arithmetic.

## Step three - console mast and front handlebars

1. Install the **Console Mast Cover (No.31)** onto the **Console Mast (No.2)**, facing
   the direction in the picture. Run the two wire harnesses through the bottom of the
   console mast tube and out the top opening.
2. Pull the **Computer Cable (No.44)** and the **Handpulse Wire (No.45)** through
   Console Mast (No.2).
3. Slide the Console Mast into the Main Frame (No.1), careful not to pinch the wires.
   Fasten it with **6 Hex Head Bolts (No.68)** and 4 Flat Washers (No.76) on the side
   bolts and 2 Curved Washers (No.83) on the front bolts. Snap the Console Mast Cover
   (No.31) in place.
4. Install the **Front Handlebars (No.3)** onto the Console Mast (No.2) with 2 Hex Head
   Bolts, Split Washers (No.82) and Flat Washers (No.76).

**Which bolt goes in item 4 changed between the two printings** - No.68 in the 2021
book, No.94 in the 2023 book, and the step-three table changed with it. See
[the front handlebar bolt card](spirit-cr800-assembly-front-handlebar-bolt-change.md).
The 2021 book prints "6 Hex Head Bolts (No.68)" in item 3 and lists eight of them.

## Step four - cup holders, seat cushion, pedals and console

1. Install the left and right **Cup Holders (No.39 & 38)** to the rear handlebars with
   4 Sheet Metal Screws (No.105).
2. Install the bottom **Seat Cushion (No.61)** to the Seat Carriage (No.4) with 4 Hex
   Head Bolts (No.98).
3. Install the **Pedals (No.116 L, 117 R)** into the **Crank Arms (No.51 L, 51 R)**.
   The left pedal has a reverse thread and will be screwed in with the opposite
   rotation. **Make sure to tighten the pedals as much as you possibly can.**
4. Connect the 2 Wire Harnesses (No.44 & 45) to the corresponding connectors on the
   back of the console. Install the **Console (No.19)** onto the Console Mast (No.2)
   and secure with 4 Philips Head Screws (99), careful not to pinch the wires.

**No torque figure is printed for the pedals** - see
[the pedal card](spirit-bike-assembly-pedal-tighten-as-much-as-possible.md).

## The two printings are one document

Their assembly chapters match at **98.6%** at word level and the only difference is the
step-three bolt above. The parts lists differ further - the 2023 printing adds a TV
adapter, a PU wheel and a round cap - but nothing in the four steps fits any of them.

The build ends here. There is no first-power-on step: these bikes are
generator-powered and the console starts when you pedal.
