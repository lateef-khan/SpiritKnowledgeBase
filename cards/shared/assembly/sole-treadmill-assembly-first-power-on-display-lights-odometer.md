First power-on, the last step of assembly, identical in the F63 2006, VF63 2008 and WF63 2009 books:

Power the treadmill on by plugging it into an appropriate wall outlet, then turn on the power switch located at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood. Ensure that the safety key is installed, as the treadmill will not power on without it. When the power is turned on, a message will scroll across the dot matrix showing the current software version. Then the Time and Distance windows will display Odometer readings for a short time: the Time window shows hours in use and the Distance window shows miles (or kilometers in metric). The treadmill then enters idle mode, which is the starting point for operation.
