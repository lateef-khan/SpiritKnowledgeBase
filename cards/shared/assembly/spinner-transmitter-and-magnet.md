**Battery:** take the battery cover off the transmitter, fit **2 AAA batteries**, and refit the cover.

**Transmitter placement:** depending on the bike, the transmitter can go near the drive belt cover. Its range to the console is about **2 metres**.

**Magnet:** attach the magnet firmly on the crank arm at the measured location. The maximum allowed gap between magnet and sensor is **8 mm**.

The SB900 2020 also has a magnet base with its own gap spec. That is a different part.
