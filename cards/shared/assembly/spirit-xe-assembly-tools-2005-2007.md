Four tools, and nothing else:

| Item | Tool | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 106 | 13/14m/m Wrench | 1 |
| 107 | Short Phillips Head Screw Driver | 1 |
| 108 | Phillips Head Screw Driver | 1 |
| 109 | 12m/m Wrench | 1 |

**Two screwdrivers, and the short one is not optional.** Step 4 of the XE100 build
says outright to use the short driver for the connecting arm covers, and the 2005
book says the same for the inside screws of its own cover step - the long driver
will not reach.

**Two wrenches, and some steps need both at once.** The connecting arm bolt is a
bolt-and-Nyloc-nut joint: the 2007 book says to tighten it "using the two 12m/m
Wrenches provided" and the 2005 book says "using two wrenches provided ... a wrench
on both axle bolts". The panel supplies **one** 12mm wrench and one 13/14mm wrench,
not two 12mm. Take the second from the 13/14mm end or use your own.

## The 2007 panel is printed three times and is the same three times

The XE100, XE200 and XE300 each get their own Assembly Pack Check List, and each
ends with this identical four-tool panel with the same item numbers. **The tools are
the only part of that check list that does not change between the three machines** -
their hardware does. See
[the hardware card](spirit-xe-2007-assembly-hardware-checklist.md).

## The 2005 book has no tools panel and no item numbers

The XE150/XE350/XE550 manual never prints a tools list in its text. The four tools
appear only as pictures on the corner of each machine's Step 1 & 2 assembly drawing,
captioned **13/14 mm Wrench**, **Short Screw driver**, **Screwdriver** and **12mm
Wrench** - the same four, in the same order, on all three drawings. **No item
numbers are printed for them anywhere in that book**, so do not quote 106-109 for a
2005 machine; those numbers come from the 2007 manual.

Its steps name the tools loosely - "the wrench provided", "the two sided wrench",
"a short Phillips screw driver provided". "The two sided wrench" is the 13/14mm.
