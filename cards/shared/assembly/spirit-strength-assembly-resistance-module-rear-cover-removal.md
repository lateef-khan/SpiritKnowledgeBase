*(Tool: Phillips screwdriver)*

1. Remove screws **No. 2** and **No. 3**.
2. First lift the rear cover upward, then pull it outward to remove it.

The rear cover is the panel below the screen back cover on the back view, beside the AC switch and power inlet.
This is the first of the twelve disassembly steps; the bottom plate and the motor module follow. Before any of
them, the manual's safety chapter says to turn everything off and unplug the power cord from the outlet and then
from the device.

The "Digital Resistance Module" is the i-Strength line's motorised resistance box, part 72 in the CSI owner's manuals; the screw numbers are the call-outs on the manual's exploded drawings.
