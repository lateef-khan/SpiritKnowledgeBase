Both books print the same six lines. The CT800 2016 book numbers them Procedure 6; the XT dealer manual
numbers them Procedure 7 and ends with a pointer to its wiring diagram.

1. Disconnect the power cord from the unit.
2. Remove the motor hood.
3. Remove the **two Phillips screws** holding in the controller.
4. Disconnect all wires connected to the controller.
5. Insert the new controller and secure it with the Phillips screws.
6. Reconnect the wires to the controller. (The XT book adds "See Procedure 12", its wiring diagram.)

No screw size, no torque and no board part number are printed. Neither book says to calibrate afterwards in
this procedure, though both list "run the calibration procedure" first in their troubleshooting tables.

**This is the generic dealer wording, printed unchanged for six different machines.** The CT800 2016 is an
AC-inverter treadmill and the XT machines are DC; the procedure does not distinguish them. The CT850 2016
book, the CT800's closest sibling, prints a different, Dyaco-format version of the same job with screw
counts for the motor base caps and top cover - see the linked card.
