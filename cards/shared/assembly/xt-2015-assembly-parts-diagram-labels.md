**This diagram labels its parts with words, not with letters.** The labels are:

- SPEAKERS
- CONSOLE
- CONSOLE FAN
- UPRIGHTS
- HANDLES
- MOTOR COVER
- BELT
- SIDE RAILS
- END CAPS

The **XT185-2015 diagram carries the same labels except SPEAKERS**, which it does
not print. Everything else on it is identical.

Nothing else is labelled. These diagrams name no tablet holder, no accessory
tray, no cup holder, no safety stop key, no levelers and no transport wheels.

The 2023 XT manuals print a different diagram whose parts are lettered A to M or
A to N. Do not read a letter from those cards onto a 2015 machine - the letters
do not exist here and the part list is not the same.
