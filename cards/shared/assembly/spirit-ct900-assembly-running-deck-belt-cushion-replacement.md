1. Remove the front and rear rollers (steps 4.5.1 to 4.5.5 of the guide).
2. Use a screwdriver to remove the screws on the **rear cover**.
3. Use the **No. 6 L-shaped hex wrench** (6 mm) to loosen the **8 screws of the retaining strip**.
4. Remove the left and right **trims** in the direction of the arrow.
5. Use the **No. 4 L-type hexagonal wrench** (4 mm) to remove the running plate fixing screw. Lift the running
   board off and replace the board or the running belt. To change the cushions, remove the **8 buffers** and
   fit new ones. Reassemble in the order of removal.
6. When assembled, adjust the running belt tension so the belt runs true.

Step 5 names one fixing screw and a 4 mm key; the CT1000ENT book's version of the same step names **eight**
screws and a **6 mm** key, so the two are on separate cards. The CT900 text cites "Figure 6.6.5" for the
cushions where the page labels it 4.6.5.

No screw count is printed for the rear cover, and no torque anywhere.

The guide is a machine translation. Its words, as used on this card: **electronic watch** is the console,
**cross screwdriver** is a Phillips screwdriver, **umbrella head screws** are the guide's name for the
console and cover screws (no size is printed), and a **17th T-Shirt** or **13th T-sleeve** is a 17 mm or 13 mm
T-handle socket.
