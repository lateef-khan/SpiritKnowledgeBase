1. Remove the **M10 screws (4 pcs)** on both sides (figures 6.15.1 and 6.15.2), then remove the **standpipe**. **Remove
   the electronic watch (console) before removing the riser.**

That is the whole section; no wrench size and no refit line are printed. The three books print it word for word.
