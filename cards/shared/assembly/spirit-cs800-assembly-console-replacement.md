1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **Phillips head screws M5 x 10 mm (4 pcs)** securing the console.
   Unfasten all connected wires and remove the console.
2. To refit, connect the computer cable and the hand pulse cable to the console and tighten the four M5 x 10 mm
   Phillips head screws. *Be sure not to crush or damage wiring during the process.*
3. To remove the upper and lower console covers, remove the **sheet-metal screws 3.5 x 12** that secure the console.
   *(Should only be done by a professional.)*

**Both generations print the same four M5 x 10 screws.** The CS800 (2020) book (cs800-2021) prints it as "remove 4
screws (5*10mm) which lock the console on the console mast; unplug wires; plug wires back before installing,
tighten 4 screws; do not pinch the wires", and has no step 3 - the console shell is not opened there. The Sole
SC200 books print the XS200-SS003 wording for the same Dyaco chassis.
