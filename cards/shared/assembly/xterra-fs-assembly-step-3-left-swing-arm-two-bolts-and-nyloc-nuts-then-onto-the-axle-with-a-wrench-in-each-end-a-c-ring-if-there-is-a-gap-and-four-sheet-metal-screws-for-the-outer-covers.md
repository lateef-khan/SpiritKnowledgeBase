Hardware step 3: **1 split washer (46), 1 button head socket bolt 5/16" x 3/4" (150), 4 sheet metal screws 5 x 12 (52), 2 nyloc nuts (55), 2 button head socket bolts 5/16" x 1-3/4" (56), 1 flat washer 8.5 x 23 x 1.5T (100)**.

1. Locate the left swing arm (upper) (7) and slide it through the swing arm bushing (23); secure it to the lower swing arm (5) with two button head socket bolts (56) and two nyloc nuts (55). Tighten using the M5 Allen wrench (68).
2. Slide the handle bar cover (78) and lower swing arm (5) over the swing arm axle (22). Attach with one button head socket bolt (150), one split washer (46) and one flat washer (100). Tighten using the M5 Allen wrenches (68).
   - **Note: it is important to have a wrench (68) in the bolt on both sides** - the 2018 book: "use two Wrenches (68), one on each end of the Axle (22), when tightening the Bolts (150)". The 2019 and 2020 books write the bolt as "(43)" in this note; the bolt in the step is (150).
   - **Note: if there is a gap after assembling the swing arm, please install a C-ring at Location A** (the 2018 book: Location B) **as shown.**
3. Install the outer covers (79) with four sheet metal screws (52). Tighten using the Phillips screwdriver (68).

The C-ring is not in the hardware pack; the books do not say where it ships.
