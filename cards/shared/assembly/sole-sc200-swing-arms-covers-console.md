1. Match the Left Console Mast Cover (57) with the Right Console Mast Cover (58) on top of the left and right Main Covers and around the Console Mast. Secure with three 4mm x 15mm Sheet Metal Screws (85), using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (118).
2. Attach the Left Swing Arm (15) to the mounting plates on the Left Lower Swing Arms (9) and secure with three 5/16" x 1-3/4" Hex Head Bolts (74), two 5/16" Curved Washers (113) and three 5/16" Nyloc Nuts (90), using the 12/14mm Wrench (117) and the 13/14mm Wrench (116). **Repeat for the right side.**
3. Install the Left Swing Arm Cover (64) on the Left Lower Swing Arm and secure with two M5 x 10mm Phillips Head Screws (83). **Repeat for the right side cover.**
4. Take off the tie from the Computer Cable (35) and plug it into the Console (30) along with the two Hand pulse cables (33). Place the Console on the mounting plate and secure with four M5 x 10mm Phillips Head Screws (83).
5. Push in the two Oval End Caps (52) on both ends of the Front Stabilizer.
