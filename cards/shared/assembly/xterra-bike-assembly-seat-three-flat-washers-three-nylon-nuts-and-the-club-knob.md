Hardware: **1 club knob** (#10 on the folding bikes, #26 on the upright) and, pre-assembled on the back of the seat, **3 flat washers** (#16; #15 on the upright) and **3 nylon nuts** (#17). Tools: the hex head wrench (73; 55 on the upright).

1. Screw off the 3 flat washers and 3 nylon nuts from the back of the seat (07; 14 on the upright) - the 2019 book shows this as fig. 3.1. Attach the seat to the seat support tube (03) and tighten with the same 3 flat washers and 3 nylon nuts.
2. Insert the seat support tube (03) into the main frame (01) - the 2019 and 2025 books call it the Front Support Tube - and fasten it with the club knob. The upright's book adds "line up the holes and fasten the Seat Post in right position with Club Knob".

Same hardware and order in all six books. Seat height is set by which hole the club knob goes through; none of these books prints a fit guide.
