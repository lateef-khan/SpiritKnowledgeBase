Hardware step 5: **2 Allen screws (#30) M8*16, 2 curved washers (#31)**; pre-assembled on the console, 4 screws (#62); pre-assembled on the seat post, 1 carriage bolt (#26), 1 quadrate plate (#27), 1 sleeve (#28), 1 lock knob (#29).

1. Attach the stationary handlebar (18) to the front post (13). Secure using two Allen screws (30) and two curved washers (31).
2. Insert the hand pulse wires (63) through the grommet (61) on the front post (13), and then pull them out from the top of the front post (13). Connect the sensor wire and hand pulse wire (63) with the computer (19) accordingly.
3. Attach the computer (19) to the front post (13) and fasten with four screws (62).
4. Attach the seat (22) onto the seat post (20), line up the holes then tighten with one carriage bolt (26), one quadrate plate (27), one sleeve (28) and the lock knob (29).
5. Unscrew (2018: "screw off") the quick release knob (57) from the main frame (1), insert the seat post (20) into the main frame (1). Line up the holes and secure the seat in position with the quick release knob (57). **The correct height for the seat can be adjusted after the upright elliptical is fully assembled.**

The same five steps in both books.
