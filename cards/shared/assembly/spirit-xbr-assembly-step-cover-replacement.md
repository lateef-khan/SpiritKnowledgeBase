1. Take the **rear shrouds (35, 36)** and the **front shrouds (29, 30)** off, then use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **4 pcs of M5 x 12 head screws (99)** and take the **step cover (33)** off.

One step, printed identically in six books, with no refit step. All four shrouds have to come off first, which means the crank arms and the console mast cover (front shrouds) and the seat-side cables (rear shrouds) are already out.
