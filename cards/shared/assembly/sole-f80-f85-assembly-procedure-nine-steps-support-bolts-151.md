Nine illustrated assembly steps, identical in the F80 2019, F85 2019 and F85 2021 books except for the washer thickness and the step-8 drawing noted below (hardware item numbers in brackets).

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill from the carton onto a smooth, leveled floor (F80 book) or smooth, level floor (F85 books) with the rear at least 3 ft from any wall. F80 wording: remove the plastic wrap and Styrofoam from beneath the unit before removing the Velcro belt, and tilting it sideways after removing the belt may cause it to fold and bounce upwards. F85 wording: do not remove the Velcro belt until the wrap and Styrofoam are out, and turning it on its side may cause it to fold up.
2. Install two Speed Nut Clips (128) on the left and right sides of the frame base, slide the lower handlebar covers (63 & 65) onto the console masts, slide the Frame Base Caps (161L & 162R) over the upright tubes (4 & 5), and connect Lower Computer Cable (51) to Middle Computer Cable (50) on the right side.
3. Bolt the upright tubes (4 & 5) to the frame base (2) with eight Button Head Socket Bolts (130); leave them loose until step 7 is finished. Do not pinch the cables in the tubing.
4. Connect Speed Adjustment Switch Cable (54) to Speed Cable Upper (37), Incline Adjustment Switch Cable (55) to Incline Cable Upper (38), and Middle Computer Cable (50) to Computer Cable Upper (49); tuck excess cable into the handrail tubing.
5. Attach the Console Assembly (52) onto the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (133) and four Split Washers (113); tighten completely with the M6 wrench. Washer thickness differs by book: 1.5T in F80-2019 and F85-2021, 2T in F85-2019 (see the linked kit cards).
6. Attach the left (63) and right (65) lower handlebar covers to the console with four Sheet Metal Screws (168).
7. Attach the support tube (15) between the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (151), place the Beverage Holder (150) on top of the support tube, place the safety-key magnet (75) between Start and Stop, and check every bolt and screw is tight.
8. Finish tightening the mast bolts left loose in step 2 and fix the console mast covers (161 & 162) to the frame base with two Tapping Screws (120) and two Phillips Head Screws (94). Ruling: the F85 books print a step-8 hardware drawing showing six tapping screws, but their pack checklist and this step text agree on two tapping plus two Phillips screws; this card follows the checklist and step text (two and two) and notes the drawing.
9. Insert the Tablet Holder (177) into the console assembly (52) and tighten four Sheet Metal Screws (168).
