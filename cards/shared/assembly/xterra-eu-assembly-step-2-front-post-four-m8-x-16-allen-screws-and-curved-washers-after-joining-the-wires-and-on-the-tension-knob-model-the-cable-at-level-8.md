Hardware step 2: **4 Allen screws M8*16 (#30; elliptical #23), 4 curved washers (#31; elliptical #7)**; on the 2018 hybrid, pre-assembled on the tension control knob, 1 washer (#65) and 1 bolt (#66).

| Book | Wires and knob | Post |
|---|---|---|
| 2018 hybrid (manual tension knob) | **Turn the tension control knob (17) to level 8.** Connect the tension control knob (17) with the lower tension cable (59); connect the lower sensor wire (58) with the upper sensor wire (60). Tighten the tension control knob with one washer (65) and bolt (66). | Insert the front post (13) into the main frame (1), tighten with four Allen screws (30) and four curved washers (31). |
| 2024 hybrid (console-controlled) | Connect the lower computer wire (58) with the upper computer wire (60). **Be careful not to pinch the wires while assembling as this may cause damage to the electronics.** | Same four screws (30) and washers (31). |
| 2016 elliptical | Connect the computer wire (26) from the front post (22) to the lower computer wire (28). | Insert the front post (22) into the main frame (1), four Allen screws (23) and four curved washers (7). |

Level 8 is the highest of the eight-level knob; at that setting its cable is shortest and the two ends meet. The 2024 book's tension is set from the console, so it has no knob and no cable here.
