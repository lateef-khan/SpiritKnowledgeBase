Eight illustrated assembly steps for the TT8-2016 (both drive versions share the year's book; hardware item numbers in brackets). Compared with the TT8-2013 sequence, step 2 removes fixing plates, the step-4 cable numbers change, and step 7 has no end tabs; compared with TT8-2019, step 6 uses six (not four) cover screws and there is no tablet step.

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill base frame from the carton onto firm, level ground.
2. Remove the left/right Flat Head Socket Bolts (113) with the L wrench (105), then remove the two Fixing Plate Stabilizers (112). Locate the left and right console mast tubes (5 & 4); slide Handrail Bottom Cover (117) onto the left tube and (118) onto the right tube. Connect main-frame Computer Cable (32) to Computer Cable (33) at the bottom of the right tube (4). Do not pinch the cables.
3. Attach the upright tubes (5 & 4) to the main frame (1) through the brackets (151 & 152), hand-tight only until the console is on: three Split Washers (85), three Flat Washers (102) and three Button Head Socket Bolts (100) per tube, plus two Curved Washers (131) and two Flat Head Socket Bolts (99) at the front of each tube. Tighten bolts (100) with the M6 L wrench (105) and bolts (99) with the M5 driver (104). Do not pinch the right-upright harness.
4. Connect lower Computer Cable (33) to upper Computer Cable (31), Remote Speed Cable (34) to upper Speed Cable (36-46), and Remote Incline Cable (35) to upper Incline Cable (36-47).
5. Attach the Console Assembly (36) on top of the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (125) and four Split Washers (85); tighten completely with the M6 wrench without pinching cables.
6. Attach the left (117) and right (118) lower handlebar covers to the console with six Sheet Metal Screws (90).
7. Attach the support tube (3) between the uprights (4 & 5) with four Flat Head Socket Bolts (99), slip the Beverage Holder (48) over the support, place the safety-key magnet (63) between Start and Stop, and check every bolt and screw is tight.
8. Fully tighten the mast-to-frame bolts with the M6 wrench and fix the console mast covers (40R & 41L) to each mast (5 & 4) with two Phillips Head Screws (126) per side.
