**This is the SC300 pack, not the SC200 pack: the part numbers and the sheet-metal screw count differ.**

**Hardware Step 1** - rear stabilizer, console mast and console

| Item | Description | Qty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 144 | O10 x 2T Split Washer | 6 pcs |
| 142 | 3/8" Curved Washer | 2 pcs |
| 136 | 3/8" Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| 113 | M5 x 10mm Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| 110 | 3/8" x 3/4" Socket Head Cap Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 106 | 3/8" x 3-3/4" Hex Head Bolt | 2 pcs |

**Hardware Step 2** - mast covers and handlebars

| Item | Description | Qty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 113 | M5 x 10mm Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| 117 | 3.5 x 16mm Sheet Metal Screw | 1 pc |
| 124 | 5/16" Nyloc Nut | 6 pcs |
| 143 | 5/16" Curved Washer | 4 pcs |
| 102 | 5/16" x 1-3/4" Hex Head Bolt | 6 pcs |

The curved washer in the step-1 box is printed without its "x 23 x 2.0T" middle dimensions in the checklist, but step 1 on PDF p.8 names it as the O3/8" x 23 x 2.0T Curved Washer (142); both readings are transcribed as printed.
