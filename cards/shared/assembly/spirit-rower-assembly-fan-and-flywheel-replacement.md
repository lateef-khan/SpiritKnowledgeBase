1. Adjust the resistance on the console to the highest and remove the power cord. Take out the Chain Cover (L)
   (71) and Chain Cover (R) (72).
2. Pull the **Steel Cable (66)** out of the **Flywheel (23)**.
3. Use the **10/15 mm wrench** to loosen the **M6 x 6T nyloc nut (103)** and the **3/8" x UNF26 x 11T nut (82)**.
4. Use the **6 mm L Allen wrench** to take out the **M8 x P1.25 x 20L socket head cap bolts (84), 4 pcs**. Move
   the Flywheel Pulley (25), Latch (26), Spring Latch (27) and Attaching Plates (8L) (8R) forward so the **Drive
   Belt (24)** can be removed.
5. Release the **Flywheel (23)** and **Fan (22)**.
6. Use a driver to remove the **M5 x P0.8 x 15L Phillips head screws (78), 6 pcs**; the Flywheel (23) and Fan (22)
   can then be separated.
7. Reverse the steps (the CW800-YR001 and DW400-YR002 books say "reverse step 4") to refit the flywheel pulley,
   latch and spring latch.

The three books print this identically. The belt goes back at the tension the flywheel-pulley card gives, 70 to
80 lbs; this section prints no figure of its own. The Sole SR500 and SR550 books print the same seven steps.
