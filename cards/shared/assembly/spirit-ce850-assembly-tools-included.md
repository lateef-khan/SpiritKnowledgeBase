All three cartons list the same four tools, and nothing else:

- 13/14mm Wrench
- 12/14mm Wrench
- Phillips Screwdriver
- 8mm L Allen Wrench

## The build calls for six tools by item number

The assembly steps name their tools by number, and the parts list has six:

| # | Printed as | Where the steps use it |
|---|---|---|
| 192 | Phillips Head Screw Driver | the console screws, and every cover in step four |
| 193 | L Allen Wrench | step zero, releasing the side back |
| 194 | 13/14mm Double Open-End Wrench | steps one, two and three |
| 195 | 12/14mm Double Open-End Wrench | step three |
| 200 | M12 L Allen Wrench | step two, the connecting arm rod ends |
| 201 | Open End Combination Spanner Wrench | step two, the connecting arm rod ends |

**Items 200 and 201 are on no tools panel.** Step two tells you to tighten the rod-end
nyloc nuts "by using the Wrench (No.200) and Wrench (No.201)" - an M12 L Allen wrench
and a combination spanner - and the page-9 panel lists neither. The 2016 manual's
step three does the same job with "WRENCH (194) and WRENCH (195)" instead, which are
on the panel.

**The panel's 8mm L Allen wrench has no item number.** Item 193 is printed as "L Allen
Wrench" with no size in all three parts lists. It is the one step zero calls for.

**The 2016 manual has no parts list at all** to check any of this against.

## No 14mm wrench on its own

The noise item in all three manuals says to adjust the two levelling pads with a
**14mm wrench**. Both open-end wrenches above have a 14mm end. See
[the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

**No other Spirit elliptical carton is this one.** The CE800 carton holds six tools,
the CE800ENT five, the CE900 five and the CES880 four, and no two lists overlap
completely. Do not carry this panel across.
