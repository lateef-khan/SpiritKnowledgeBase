1. Attach the (L) strap to the left pedal (No. 28; upright No. 23) and adjust the strap to the length you need. Use the same method to attach the (R) strap to the right pedal (No. 29; upright No. 24).
2. Connect the left pedal to the **left** side of the crank w/ pulley (No. 35; upright No. 08), **twist it counterclockwise to tighten**.
3. Connect the right pedal to the **right** side of the crank, **twist it clockwise to tighten**.

"Note that the straps should face outwards and the pedals should be mounted to their respective sides to be functional."

No tool and no torque are printed in either book. The recumbent's December 2019 service document supplies both: **"Confirm L/R Pedals to correct side on crankarm. L-pedal counterclockwise / reversed threads & R-pedal clockwise / right tight. Use 15mm wrench and mallet for max torque!"**
