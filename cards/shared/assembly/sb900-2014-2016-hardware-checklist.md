Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #82 | 3/8" | Nut | 4 pcs |
| #81 | 3/8" x 3-1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #83 | 3/8" x 23mm | Curved Washer | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #91 | M5 x 10mm | Screw | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 3**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #49 | ø8 | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| #48 | ø8 x 16mm | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| #88 | M8 × P1.25 × 20L | Screw | 4 pcs |

The checklist runs 1, 2, 3. The remaining assembly steps - 4 left/right pedals and 5 console - use no hardware out of the assembly pack.

**The handlebar hardware is metric here, not imperial.** #49 and #48 are ø8, and #88 is M8 × P1.25 × 20L. On the SB700 of the same years the corresponding parts are a 5/16" split washer, a 5/16" x 16mm flat washer and a 5/16" x 5/8" screw under the same item numbers. Do not carry an SB700 size onto an SB900.

The step 3 hardware box beside the instruction calls #88 a "Button Head Socket Bolt"; the checklist just calls it a "Screw". Same part, two names.

**Item numbers are not shared between models.** The 2023 machines renumbered the whole pack - there `#93` is a button head socket bolt, while on the 2019-2021 machines `#93` is an M5 tapping screw. Always use the checklist printed in your own machine's manual.
