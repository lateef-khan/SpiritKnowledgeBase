**Untie the wire holding the Sleeve Spacer (25)** in place on the rod-end bearings at the ends of the **Connecting Arms (8 & 9)**. Align the rod-end holes with the holes in the brackets of the left and right **Handle Bars (10 & 11)**, rod ends inside the brackets. Secure with a **5/16" x 1-1/4" Hex Head Bolt (109)**, **5/16" x 20mm Flat Washer (151)** and **5/16" Nylon Nut (137)** using the **13/14 mm Wrench (159)** and **12/14 mm Wrench (162)**.
