Nine illustrated assembly steps, identical in the F63 2019 and F65 2019 books (hardware item numbers in brackets). This generation adds a step-9 tablet holder to the older eight-step F63 sequence.

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill from the carton onto a smooth, leveled floor with the rear at least 3 ft from any wall. Remove the plastic wrap and Styrofoam from under the unit before removing the Velcro belt; tilting it sideways with the belt off can let it fold up and cause serious injury.
2. Install two Speed Nut Clips (128) on the left and right sides of the frame base, slide the lower handlebar covers (64 & 65) onto the console masts, slide the Frame Base Caps (62L & 63R) over the upright tubes (4 & 5), and connect Lower Computer Cable (50) to Middle Computer Cable (49) on the right side.
3. Bolt the upright tubes (4 & 5) to the frame base (2) with eight Button Head Socket Bolts (130); leave them loose until step 7 is finished. Do not pinch the cables in the tubing.
4. Connect Speed Adjustment Switch Cable (54) to Speed Cable Upper (37), Incline Adjustment Switch Cable (55) to Incline Cable Upper (38), and Middle Computer Cable (49) to Computer Cable Upper (48); tuck excess cable into the handrail tubing. Rest the console at a suitable height or have someone hold it while connecting.
5. Insert the Console Assembly (52) into the uprights and fix with four Button Head Socket Bolts (139) and four Split Washers (113) using the M6 L wrench.
6. Fix the left (64) and right (65) handlebar covers over the upright tops with four Sheet Metal Screws (175).
7. Bolt the support tube (26) between the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (159), set the Beverage Holder (158) on it, place the safety-key magnet (75) between Start and Stop, and check every bolt and screw is tight.
8. Finish tightening the mast bolts left loose in step 2 and fix the console mast covers (62 & 63) to the frame base with two Tapping Screws (120) and two Phillips Head Screws (94).
9. Insert the Tablet Holder (177) into the console assembly (52) and tighten four Sheet Metal Screws (175).
