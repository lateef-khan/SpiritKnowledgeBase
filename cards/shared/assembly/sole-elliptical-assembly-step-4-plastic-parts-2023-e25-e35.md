1. **Match the Connecting Arm Covers (L) and (R), (110) and (111)**, on the Connecting Arm (L) (8); each side with **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)** and **one Sheet Metal Screw (139)** using the Short driver (175). Repeat on the other side.
2. **Slide Wheel Covers (95)** above each side of the Slide Wheel Urethanes with **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)**.
3. **Front Stabilizer Cover (112)** with **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)**.
4. **Cover Holders (23)**, hole for the plastic cover on the bent tab facing rearward, on the Incline Rail Assembly (2) with **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (141)**. **Inclinable Rail Cover (115)** with **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)**. **Rear Bar Cover (116)** on the Rear Stabilizer and the Cover Holders (23) with **4pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)**.
5. **Bottom Cover (113)** on the Incline Rail Assembly (2) with **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)**. The 2026 book uses 4 here.
6. **Handle Bar Covers (106 & 107, 108 & 109)** with **4pcs Sheet Metal Screws (139)**.
7. **Console Mast Covers (100 & 101)** with **4pcs Sheet Metal Screws (139)**.
8. **Tablet Holder (89)** on the back of the console with **4pcs Sheet Metal Screws (139)**. The 2026 book has no tablet-holder step.
9. **Plug both Oval End Caps (76)** into both ends of the stabilizer tube.
