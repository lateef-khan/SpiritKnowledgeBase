Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. Hardware quantities are
on [the hardware card](spirit-cr900-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Step one - rear stabilizer and console mast

1. Install **REAR STABILIZER (11)** onto **MAIN FRAME (1)** with 2 short BOLTS (145),
   2 long BOLTS (139), 4 SPLIT WASHERS (138) and 4 WASHERS (106).
2. Attach left and right **STABILIZER COVERS (59 & 60)** to MAIN FRAME (1) with 2
   SCREWS (97). Join together with 2 SELF TAPPING SCREWS (117).
3. Run the **WIRING HARNESS** through the **CONSOLE MAST (2)** and out the top.
4. Attach CONSOLE MAST (2) to MAIN FRAME (1) using 6 BOLTS (92), 6 SPLIT WASHERS (111)
   and 6 WASHERS (108).
5. Place left and right **CONSOLE MAST COVERS (56 & 57)** around the base of the
   CONSOLE MAST (2). Join the covers with 3 SELF TAPPING SCREWS (117).

## Step two - front handlebar and console

1. Attach **FRONT HANDLE BAR (8)** to CONSOLE MAST (2) with 2 BOLTS (94), 2 SPLIT
   WASHERS (112) and 2 WASHERS (105).
2. Connect **WIRING HARNESS CABLES (74, 131)** to **CONSOLE (68)**.
3. Attach CONSOLE (68) to CONSOLE MAST (2) with 4 SCREWS (97). Ensure all cables do
   not become pinched.
4. Attach **CONSOLE BRACKET COVER (64)** to CONSOLE MAST (2) using 4 SCREWS (97).

**On the CR900ENT there are seven cables, not two.** Its step 2 item 2 reads "Connect
WIRING HARNESS CABLES (74, 127, 128, 129, 131, 132, 137) to CONSOLE (68)" and runs
that into the same sentence as attaching the console. Everything else in the step is
word for word the same.

## Step three - seat carriage, seat back and rear handlebar

1. Install **SEAT RELEASE LEVER (28)** onto **SEAT CARRIAGE FRAME (5)** with 2 BOLTS
   (92), 2 LOCK WASHERS (111) and 2 WASHERS (108).
2. Install **SEAT BACK FRAME (3)** onto SEAT CARRIAGE FRAME (5) with 2 short BOLTS
   (85), 2 long BOLTS (86) and 4 WASHERS (106).
3. Attach **REAR HANDLE BAR (6)** to SEAT CARRIAGE FRAME (5) using 4 long BOLTS (86),
   4 short BOLTS (85), 12 WASHERS (106) and 4 NUTS (101), and connect the WIRING
   HARNESS CABLES between REAR HANDLE BAR (6) and SEAT CARRIAGE FRAME (5).
4. Loosen 2 SCREWS (97) to remove **SEAT COVER RETAINING BRACKET (41)** from SEAT BACK
   FRAME (3).
5. Attach **REAR HANDLE BAR COVER (61)** to REAR HANDLE BAR (6) using 2 SELF TAPPING
   SCREWS (116).
6. Attach **CUP HOLDERS (120 & 121)** to REAR HANDLE BAR (6) using 4 SELF TAPPING
   SCREWS (116).
7. Attach **SEAT BACK COVER (62)** to SEAT BACK FRAME (3). Install SEAT COVER RETAINING
   BRACKET (41) using 2 SCREWS (97).

The manual prints "4 shot BOLTS (85)" in item 3; short is meant. The 2025 manual
renumbers items 5 to 7 as 1 to 3 under the heading ASSEMBLY - STEP THREE - CONTINUED
and changes nothing else.

## Step four - pedals and seat cushion

1. Install the **PEDALS (26L & 26R)** onto the **CRANK ARMS (22 & 23)**. The left pedal
   has a reverse thread and will be screwed in counterclockwise. Tighten to **300
   in-lbs. (34 N-m)** of torque.
2. Attach **BOTTOM SEAT CUSHION (42)** to SEAT CARRIAGE FRAME (5) with 4 BOLTS (90).

**The CR900ENT prints "4 BOLTS (85)" here where the other two print (90).** The
step-four hardware table disagrees with the step in two of the three books; see
[the seat cushion bolt card](spirit-cr900-assembly-seat-cushion-bolt.md) before you
reach for a bolt.

## All three printings print the same four steps

Word for word, apart from the two differences called out above. The 2025 manual reflows
them onto larger pages, moves each hardware table below its step and renames the
headings from CR900 STEP ONE to ASSEMBLY - STEP ONE. **No step in any of the three
names a tool.**

The build ends here. There is no first-power-on step: these bikes are
generator-powered and the console starts when you pedal.
