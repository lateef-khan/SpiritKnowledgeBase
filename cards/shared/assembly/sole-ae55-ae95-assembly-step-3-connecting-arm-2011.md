**Untie the wire holding the Sleeve Spacer (23)** in place on the rod-end bearings at the ends of the **Connecting Arms (8 & 9)**. Align the rod-end holes with the holes in the brackets of the left and right **Handle Bars (10 & 11)**, rod ends inside the brackets. Secure both sides with a **Hex Head Bolt (134)**, **Flat Washer (170)** and **Nylon Nut (159)** using **Wrench (184)** and **Wrench (187)**.
