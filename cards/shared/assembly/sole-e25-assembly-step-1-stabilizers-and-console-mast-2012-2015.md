1. **Connect the Front Stabilizer Bar (2)** with the Main Frame at the front joining plate: tighten **two Hex Head Bolts (109)**, **two Split Washers (142)** and **two Flat Washers (139)** with **Wrench (151)**.
2. **Connect the Incline Rail Assembly (3)** with the Main Frame at the front joining plate: tighten **two Hex Head Bolts (112)**, **two Split Washers (142)** and **two Curved Washers (144)** with **Wrench (151)**.
3. **Guide the Console Mast (15) through the Console Mast Cover (82)**, then guide the **Computer Cable (38)** through the Cover (82) and the Mast (15). Secure the mast on the Main Frame with **Wrench (151)**: tighten the **preinstalled Hex Head Bolt**, **three other Hex Head Bolts (109 & 110)**, **two Curved Washers (144)** and **one Split Washer (142)**.
4. **Plug in the Computer Cable (38), the two Handpulse Cable Assemblies (White & Red) (44 & 45), the Swing Arm Wire (Upper) Resistance (48) and the Swing Arm Wire (Upper) Incline (49)** on the back of the **Console Assembly (35)**, and secure it on the console holding plate with **four Phillips Head Screws (119)**, tightened with driver (150).
