**These two steps are diagram-only: the page carries no written instruction, only exploded drawings with callouts.** The drawings were read on the render of PDF p.11.

**Step 5** fits the four hanging bar welding groups with rubber sleeves and the two dumbbell towers (015) to the frame, joined with the M8x80 and M8x85 bolts, M8 washers, M8 nuts and M8 lock washers of the Hardware Step 5 kit.

**Step 6** fits the two rod welding sets, joined with the M8x80 bolts, M8 washers, M8 nuts and M8 lock washers of the Hardware Step 6 kit. The callouts on the drawing name the same fasteners the checklist box holds.
