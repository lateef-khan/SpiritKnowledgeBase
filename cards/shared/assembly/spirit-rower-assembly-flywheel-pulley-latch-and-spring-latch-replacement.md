**Drive belt tension 70 to 80 lbs** (printed "70~80BLS"), set on the M6 nyloc nut and locked with the 3/8 inch
nut. Set the resistance to the highest level and pull the power cord before you start.

1. Adjust the resistance to the highest on the console and remove the power cord. Take out the Chain Cover (L)
   (71), Chain Cover (R) (72), Handle (10), Controller Assembly (36) and the Console Holder Assembly (7) with the
   Console Assembly (43).
2. Loosen the **M6 x 6T nyloc nut (103)** and the **3/8" x UNF26 x 11T nut (82)** with the **10/15 mm wrench**.
3. Take out the **M8 x P1.25 x 20L socket head cap bolts (84), 4 pcs**, with the **6 mm L Allen wrench**. Move
   the **Flywheel Pulley (25)**, **Latch (26)**, **Spring Latch (27)**, **Attaching Plate (8L)** and **Attaching
   Plate (8R)** forward.
4. Hold the Flywheel Pulley (25) by hand and take out the **M6 x P1.0 x 18L button head socket bolt (89)** with the
   **4 mm L Allen wrench**. Take out the **M5 x 10L Phillips head screws (86), 3 pcs**, with a driver and remove
   the Attaching Plate (8R) with its **bearing (6201 UOU)**.
5. Take out the **M5 x 10L Phillips head screws (86), 2 pcs**, with a driver and separate the Attaching Plate (8R)
   from the bearing (6201 UOU).
6. Remove the **Ø20 x 1.2t C ring (108)** with a clip, then the **Ø20 x 29 x 0.3T wave washer (96)**, the Spring
   Latch (27) and the Latch (26).
7. On reassembly: (a) watch the position of the Spring Latch (27) against the Attaching Plate (8R); (b) fix the
   Attaching Plates (8L) and (8R) **after** putting the **drive belt 6PJ-787L (24)** on the Flywheel Pulley (25);
   (c) tie up the 3/8" x UNF26 x 11T nut (82), adjust the M6 x 6T nyloc nut (103) until the drive belt against the
   Flywheel (23) reads **70 to 80 lbs**, then tie up the 3/8" x UNF26 x 11T nut (82).

**Steps 4 and 5 both take screws (86) off the same plate, three then two.** All three books print it that way;
count what is in front of you.

**The CRW800 (2020) book (crw800-2021) adds four generator steps** the other two books do not have, because that
machine is generator-powered:

8. Mount the generator belt on the generator pulley.
9. Insert the idle wheel screw into the generator fixing plate, then lock the generator with a **bolt
   (M6 x 1.0 x 18L) and a flat washer**.
10. Adjust the nut of the idle wheel screw to the tension at which the generator belt will not skid. No figure is
    printed for it.
11. Pull the handle bar to make sure there is no noise.

The Sole SR500 and SR550 books print steps 1 to 7 for the same chassis.
