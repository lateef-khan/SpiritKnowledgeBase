## The four levers

> All four adjustment levers function the same way. Turn the lever clockwise to tighten
> and counterclockwise to loosen.

The CIC800 and CIC850 head this **ADJUSTMENT KNOBS** and write "Turn the knob" in the
second sentence while leaving "adjustment levers" in the first - the manuals use the two
words for the same part.

**The CB900 adds a sentence the CIC books drop:** if the handle is not in an easily
accessible position, **pull out on it and rotate** until you reach a workable angle for
turning. That is the standard ratchet-handle trick, and it is printed only in the CB900.

## The saddle pitch

> The pitch of the saddle can be adjusted to accommodate all users. Loosen the
> horizontal bolt under the seat to adjust the pitch, then retighten.

Word for word in all three. **Pitch is not the same adjustment as fore/aft** - the
fore/aft slide is a quick release or a knob and needs no tool, while the pitch bolt is
a separate fastener under the saddle. No size, torque or tool is given for it.

Height and fore/aft are on
[the seat card](spirit-cycle-assembly-seat-height-and-fore-aft-position.md).
