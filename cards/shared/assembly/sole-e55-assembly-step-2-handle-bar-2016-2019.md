1. **Place the left and right Lower Handle Bars (10) (11)** onto the left and right Console mast shafts and secure with **Hex Head Bolts (120)** together with **Flat Washers (170)** using **Wrench (189)**.
2. **Untie the rod end bearing on the left Connecting Arm (8). Insert the Hex Head Bolt (122)** through the rod end bearing bracket on the Swing Arm (10), and with the **13/14mm wrench (186)** and **wrench (189)** tighten together with a **Flat Washer (166)** and a **Nyloc Nut (155)**. Fix the right Swing Arm (11) and right Connecting Arm (9) the same way.
