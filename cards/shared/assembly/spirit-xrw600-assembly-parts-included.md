Nine lines, with quantities as printed:

| Qty | Part |
|---|---|
| 1 | Main Frame |
| 1 | Aluminum Rail |
| 1 | Front Stabilizer |
| 1 | Rear Stabilizer |
| 1 | Console Mast |
| 1 | Console |
| 2 | Foot Pedals |
| 1 | Power Cord |
| 1 | Hardware Kit |

**Both printings list the same nine lines.** The 2019 and 2021 XRW600 manuals are the
same book here; their whole assembly chapters agree to 99.6% at word level, and their
four hardware tables are identical row for row.

## The seat is not on the panel

Step four installs the **Seat (49)** onto the **Seat Attaching Board (16)**. Neither
part is on this list, so an owner counting the box against it has no line to tick the
seat against. The machine diagram on the facing page does label **SEAT**.

## The panel lists no levellers and no transport wheels

The diagram on p. 9 labels **LEVELERS** twice - once at the front stabiliser and once
at the rear - and the maintenance chapter says there are two levelling pads under the
rear stabiliser. This panel counts none of them, and no step of the build fits one.
Nothing in either book labels or lists a transport wheel, although the moving procedure
rolls the machine away on its front end. See
[the folding and moving card](spirit-xrw600-assembly-folding-and-moving.md) and
[the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

## A power cord, which the CRW800 carton does not have

This is the one carton line that separates the two air rowers. The Spirit CRW800
printings list eight parts and no power cord; their console runs on batteries. Do not
answer a question about one rower from the other's panel - see
[the CRW800 parts card](../../crw800-2024/assembly/crw800-2024-assembly-parts-included.md).

## What the panel does not say

It gives no carton count, no shipping weight and no box dimensions.
