Hardware supplied in the box, identical figures in the VTT8 2008 and WTT8 2009 books. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #84 | M5 | Speed Nut (2008) / Speed Nut Clip (2009) | 4 pcs |
| #85 | 3/8 x 2T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| #89 | 3.5 x 12 mm | Screw (2008) / Sheet Metal Screw (2009) | 4 pcs |
| #103 | 10 mm | Star Washer | 2 pcs |
| #87 | 5 x 16 mm | Self Tapping Screw | 2 pcs |
| #92 | 5 x 16 mm | Tapping Screw | 6 pcs |
| #126 | M5 x 10 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 8 pcs |
| #125 | 3/8 x 1-3/4 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #99 | 5/16 x 3/4 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #101 | 3/8 x 2 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #100 | 5/16 x 1/2 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 6 pcs |
| #63 | - | Safety Key | 1 pc |
| #104 | - | Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver | 1 pc |
| #105 | - | M6 Allen Wrench | 1 pc |
| #18 | - | Lubricant | 1 pc |

The 2008 book calls (84) a Speed Nut and (89) a Screw; the 2009 book calls them Speed Nut Clip and Sheet Metal Screw. Same numbers, sizes and counts, so this card treats them as the same parts.
