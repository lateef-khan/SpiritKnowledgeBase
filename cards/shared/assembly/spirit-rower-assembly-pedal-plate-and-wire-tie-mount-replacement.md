1. Use the **5 mm wrench** to take out the **M8 x P1.25 x 12L button head socket bolts (123), 6 pcs**, and the
   **Ø5/16" x Ø18 x 1.5T flat washers (122), 6 pcs**. This releases the **Pedal (L) and (R)**, the **Pedal (68)**,
   the **Pedal Plate (69)** and the **Wire Tie Mount (67)**.
2. Use a driver to take out the **M4 x 5L Phillips head screws (113), 4 pcs**, and separate the Pedal (68), Pedal
   Plate (69) and Wire Tie Mount (67).
3. Use a driver to take out the **M5 x 12L flat head socket screws (107), 4 pcs**, and the Wire Tie Mount (67).
4. Reverse the steps to refit the parts.

The three books print this identically. The "5 mm wrench" is a 5 mm Allen key. The
pedal strap itself is not a step here - it is held by the wire tie mount the screws in step 3 free.
