1. Remove the **locking knob (106)**.
2. Take the **center spatial wrap (107)** off the **main frame (1)** with a **rubber hammer** and remove the **seat slider (6)** from the main frame. Use a Phillips head screwdriver (93) to release **2 pcs of 4 x 12 mm sheet-metal screws (68)**, then knock the **slide spacer (114)** out with the rubber hammer to separate the seat slider (6) and the center spatial wrap (107).
3. To refit, return the center spatial wrap (107) to the seat slider (6), return the slide spacer (114) into the seat slider (6) and tighten it with the two 4 x 12 mm sheet-metal screws (68), then insert the assembly into the main frame (1). Use the rubber hammer to seat the center spatial wrap (107) and refit the locking knob (106).

**The XBU55 2016 and 2023 books add one preparatory step** between 1 and 2: separate the **seat post cover (39)** and the **chain covers (R/L) (36) (37)**, then pull the seat slider (6) and the seat post cover (39) up. The CU800 books do not print it.

"Center spatial wrap" is the manuals' name for the plastic spacer that sits between the slider tube and the frame.
