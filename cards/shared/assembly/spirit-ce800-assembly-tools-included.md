All three cartons list the same six tools, and nothing else:

- 12mm Wrench
- 13/14mm Wrench
- 17mm Wrench
- Combination M6 Allen wrench / Phillips screwdriver
- Phillips screwdriver
- M12 Allen wrench

The 2013 manual heads the panel **Assembly Tools** and draws the tools rather than
listing them; the six are the same, and it spells the combination tool out as
"Combination M6 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screwdriver".

## The parts list names only five of them

The 2021 and 2023 parts lists carry the tools as items **122 to 126**:

| # | Printed as |
|---|---|
| 122 | 13.14mm Wrench |
| 123 | Combination M6 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver |
| 124 | M12 L Allen Wrench |
| 125 | 17mm Combination Wrench |
| 126 | Phillips Head Screw Driver |

**There is no 12mm wrench in the parts list.** The tools panel on page 7 lists one,
the parts list has no item for it, and the assembly steps never call for a 12mm
tool by name. Item 125 is a *combination* wrench where the panel says plain 17mm.
The manual never reconciles the two. Assume the carton follows the panel and expect
a 12mm wrench you cannot order a replacement for by item number.

The **2013 manual has no parts list at all** to check any of this against.

## A 14mm wrench you may need and will not find

The noise item in all three manuals tells you to adjust the levellers with a **14mm
wrench**. The 13/14mm wrench above is the one to use - see
[the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).
The 2013 manual, which has four levelling pads rather than two, says the same 14mm.

**Every other Spirit elliptical carton is different.** The CE800ENT drops the 12mm
and the 17mm and adds a 14/17mm; the CE850 carries four tools; the CE900 carries
five, none of them shared with this list; the CES880 carries four again. Do not
carry this panel across - see the linked tools cards.

## The 2024 redesign prints the same six

The **CE800 2024** manual lists the same six tools on its PARTS INCLUDED page, in the
same order and with the same wording, the combination tool included. Nothing is added
and nothing is dropped, and the **12mm wrench is still on the panel** even though the
parts list still has no item number for it.

**The touchscreen CE800ENT 2024 carton is not this one.** It holds five tools and a
14/17mm wrench in place of the 17mm and the 12mm; see
[the touchscreen tools card](../../ce800ent/assembly/ce800ent-assembly-tools-included.md).
