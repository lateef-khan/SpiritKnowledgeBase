Four tables, one per step, in inch sizes throughout. Item numbers are the
assembly-drawing numbers.

## Step 1

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 65 | HEX HEAD BOLT | 3/8" x 135mm | 2 |
| 77 | FLAT WASHER | 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T | 6 |
| 176 | HEX HEAD BOLT | 3/8" x UNC16 x 3/4" | 6 |
| 203 | CURVED WASHER | 3/8 X 23 X 2.0T | 2 |

Item 65 is the one fastener on the card written in both systems at once - an inch
thread with a millimetre length.

## Step 2

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 71 | HEX HEAD BOLT | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | 2 |
| 77 | FLAT WASHER | O3/8" x O19 x 1.5T | 4 |
| 89 | NYLON NUT | 3/8" x 7T | 4 |
| 175 | HEX HEAD BOLT | 3/8X2-3/4" | 2 |

**The CR800 2021 and 2023 step text asks for four of item 71 and this table lists
two.** Four nylon nuts and four flat washers are listed, which suits two bolts of each
kind. The CR800ENT step text asks for two, and agrees with the table. The
contradiction is in the two older books only.

## Step 3

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 68 | HEX HEAD BOLT | 5/16"X5/8" | 6 |
| 76 | FLAT WASHER | O8 x O19 x 1.5T | 6 |
| 82 | SPLIT WASHER | O5/16" x 1.5T | 2 |
| 83 | CURVED WASHER | O5/16" x 19 x 1.5T | 2 |
| 94 | HEX HEAD BOLT | 5/16" X 3/4" | 2 |

**This is the 2023 and CR800ENT table. The 2021 table has no item 94 and lists eight
of item 68.** See
[the front handlebar bolt card](spirit-cr800-assembly-front-handlebar-bolt-change.md);
it is the only hardware difference between the two CR800 printings.

## Step 4

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 98 | HEX HEAD BOLT | M6 X 15MM | 4 |
| 99 | PHILIPS HEAD SCREW | M5X12MM | 4 |
| 105 | SHEET METAL SCREW | 4MMX16MM | 4 |

The only metric fasteners in the build, and the only step that mixes the two systems.
The CR800ENT prints the same three lines in the order 99, 98, 105 and calls item 98 a
Philips Head Screw where the CR800 books call it a Hex Head Bolt; the size is the same.

## What the tables do not print

**No torque figure for any bolt, and none for the pedals** - the pedal step says only
to tighten as much as you possibly can. The **O** above is the diameter symbol the
manuals draw.

The CR800ENT tables carry the same item numbers and the same quantities as the CR800
2023 tables throughout; only the capitalisation and the row order differ.

## The 2024 redesign reprints all four tables

The **CR800 2024** and **CR800ENT 2024** manuals print these four tables row for row -
the same item numbers, the same descriptions and the same quantities. Both print the
**2023** form of step three, with item 94 in it.

Item **98** is still typed a **Hex Head Bolt** in the CR800 book and a **Philips Head
Screw** in the CR800ENT book, at the same M6 x 15mm, and the step-two disagreement over
item 71 is still there on the CR800.
