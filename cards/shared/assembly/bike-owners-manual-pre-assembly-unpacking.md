Every Sole bike owner's manual prints the same three pre-assembly steps.

1. Using a razor knife (box cutter), cut the outside bottom edge of the box along the dotted line. Lift the box over the unit and unpack.
2. Carefully remove all parts from the carton and inspect for any damage or missing parts. If damaged parts are found, or parts are missing, contact your dealer immediately.
3. Locate the hardware package. **Remove the tools first.** Remove the hardware for each step as needed to avoid confusion.

The numbers in parenthesis (#) throughout the instructions are the item number from the assembly drawing, for reference.

**Item numbers are not shared between models.** The same `#` can be a different part on a different bike, so always work from the hardware checklist printed in your own machine's manual.

The B94 2019 and R92 2019 have their own copies of this card; this one covers the other 33 machines.

In the SB900 2014/2016 manual the converted text of step 3 has lost its first line and reads only "3. ... for each step as needed to avoid confusion." The wording above is what every other manual prints.
