1. Take the console (19) off first.
2. Use the **12/14 mm wrench (112)** to release **2 pcs of 5/16" x 15 hex head screws (68)**, **2 pcs of Ø8 x 1.5T split washers** and **2 pcs of 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T flat washers (76)**, and the **handlebar assembly (3)** is released.
3. Reverse step 2 to refit the handlebar assembly (3).
4. Separate the **console mast cover (31)** from the **front shrouds (L, R) (29) (30)** at the left and right sides of the seam with a fingernail or something flat, release the latches that lock the cover and the shrouds together, and pull the console mast cover (31) up.
5. Unscrew **6 pcs of 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts (68)**, **4 pcs of 5/16" x 18 mm x 1.5T flat washers (76)** and **2 pcs of 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T curved washers (83)** with the 12 mm wrench, and pull the **console mast (2)** out.
6. To refit the console mast (2), guide the **computer cable (44)** and the **hand pulse sensor assembly w/cable (45)** through the console mast (2) and out of the console securing plate, then tighten **6 pcs of 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts (68), 6 pcs of 5/16" x 18 mm x 1.5T flat washers (76) and 2 pcs of 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T curved washers (83)** with the 12 mm open wrench.

**Four flat washers come off in step 5 and six go back in step 6.** Both counts are printed, in all three books.

**The 2009 XR898 book prints the same procedure with these differences in wording:** it says to take off the **handlebar cover (141)** before the two handlebar bolts; it prints the handlebar bolts as **5/16" x 5/8" (68)** rather than 5/16" x 15 (the same bolt, 5/8" being about 15 mm), the split washers as **5/16" x 1.5T spring washers (82)**, and the flat washers as 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T (76). The mast bolts, washers and counts are identical. Flat washer (76) is printed as 19 mm in step 2 and 18 mm in steps 5 and 6 of the same 2020 book; it is one part.

The owner's-manual assembly step for these machines fits the handlebar with a different bolt set that changed between printings (the linked bolt-change card); this service procedure prints two bolts for the handlebar and six for the mast.
