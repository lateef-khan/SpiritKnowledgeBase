Hardware step 6: **4 Allen screws M6*12 (#38; elliptical #35), 4 carriage bolts (#37; elliptical #36, M6*20)**.

1. Connect the left top handlebar (16; elliptical 37L) to the left bottom handlebar (14; 21L). Secure using two Allen screws and two carriage bolts.
2. Repeat for the right top handlebar (64; 37R) to the right bottom handlebar (15; 21R).

The 2016 elliptical adds to this step: attach the bottle holder (84) to the front post with two self-tapping screws (59) pre-installed on the post, and connect an MP3 player to the computer with the audio wire (85). Its step 7 then plugs the adapter in (the linked card). The hybrid bikes end here; the 2018 one runs on batteries and the 2024 one's adapter is not given a step.
