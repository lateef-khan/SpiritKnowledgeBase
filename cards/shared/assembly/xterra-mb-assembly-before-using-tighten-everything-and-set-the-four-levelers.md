Four bullets, printed as "Before Using" on the indoor cycles and "Final Preparations" on the air bikes:

- Tighten all bolts/nuts securely to complete your installation.
- **Adjust all four levelers** (the fan-bike upright book: "all four feet") **so your unit sits flat on the ground for a stable ride.** If placing on a mat or on carpet, then it may take time for your unit to settle into the floor padding and will not feel as stable as if placed on a hard surface.
- Make sure to tighten both pedals securely to the pedal crank arms as instructed.
- Once proper seat and handlebar height is selected, make sure to tighten securely for a solid feel.

None of the four books says which way the levelers turn or what size they are. **The 2014 indoor-cycle book has no such page** - its assembly chapter goes from the console step straight to the console feature list, and its safety list says only "use the bike on a stable, level floor".
