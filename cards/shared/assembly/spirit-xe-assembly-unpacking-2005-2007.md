## XE100, XE200 and XE300 - three items

1. Using a razor knife (Box Cutter) cut the outside, bottom, edge of box along the
   dotted Line. Lift Box over the unit and unpack.
2. Carefully remove all parts from carton and inspect for any damage or missing
   parts. If damaged parts are found, or parts are missing, contact your dealer
   immediately.
3. Locate the hardware package. The hardware is separated into steps. Remove the
   tools first. Remove the hardware for each step as needed to avoid confusion.

**The 2007 manual prints this list twice** - once at the head of the XE100/XE200
build and once at the head of the XE300 build - word for word both times. Both
builds have four steps.

## XE150, XE350 and XE550 - the same list without item 1

1. Carefully remove all parts from carton and inspect for any damage or missing
   parts. If damaged parts are found, or parts are missing, contact your dealer
   immediately.
2. Locate the hardware package. The hardware is separated into steps. Remove the
   tools first. Remove the hardware for each step as needed to avoid confusion.

**The 2005 book never tells you to cut the box open.** It starts at "remove all
parts from carton". Nothing else differs: items 2 and 3 above are its items 1 and 2,
word for word.

## Neither book says how many steps the hardware is separated into

Both say only "separated into steps". The CT800 and XE395 2010 wording names four;
these do not, although both builds do run to four steps. Neither book explains what
a number in parenthesis means, either - the 2010 and later manuals add the sentence
about item numbers coming from the assembly drawing, and these two do not. On the
2005 book that omission matters: **its steps use no item numbers at all**, only
imperial bolt sizes.

## This is not the wording used by any other Spirit machine

The XE395 2010 uses the CT800 two-item form - cut the straps, then along the dotted
line, hardware separated into four steps. The five 2021 machines and the two 2023
machines use the four-item list that starts "Cut the straps". See
[the CT800 form](spirit-ct800-assembly-unpacking.md) and
[the four-item form](ct900-pre-assembly.md).

**A near-identical razor-knife list is printed in several Sole owner's manuals.**
It is not the same fact and those cards are a different brand; they are not linked
here and must not be widened to cover a Spirit machine.
