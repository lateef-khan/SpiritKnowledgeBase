## Hardware for step 1

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 106 | Bolt | 3/8" x 3-3/4" | 2 |
| 144 | Washer | Ø10 x 2T | 6 |
| 142 | Washer | 3/8" x 23 x 2.0T | 2 |
| 110 | Bolt | 3/8" x 3/4" | 4 |
| 136 | Washer | Ø3/8" x Ø19 x 1.5T | 4 |
| 113 | Screw | M5 x 10m/m | 4 |

Every row closes against the step: 2 bolts and 2 curved washers on the rear stabiliser,
4 bolts with 4 split and 4 flat washers on the mast, 4 screws into the console, and the
split washers split two and four across the two joints.

## Hardware for step 2

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|

| 113 | Screws | M5 x 10m/m | 6 |
| 102 | Bolt | 5/16" x 1-3/4" | 6 |
| 143 | Curved washer | 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T | 4 |
| 124 | Nyloc nuts | 5/16" X 7T | 6 |

## The step-two screw count is two short of its own step

Item 113 is listed at **6** and step two spends **8** of them:

| Where | How many |
|---|---|
| Console mast covers, item 2 | 2 |
| Left handle bar cover, item 4 | 2 |
| Right handle bar cover, item 4 | 2 |
| Drink bottle holder, item 5 | 2 |

**The bottle holder is the two that are missing.** It is the last thing item 5 does and
the only one of the four jobs that is not part of the machine's structure - which is
also how the CS800's 2021 printing loses the same part. Count eight M5 x 10mm screws
before you start; the bag holds six by the book.

Both printings print 6. This is not an extraction error: the figure is the same in the
2018 and 2021 PDFs' own text and reads the same on a 300 dpi render of the page.

## Three bolts and two washers per handlebar

Six 5/16" x 1-3/4" bolts (102) and six nyloc nuts (124) against **four** curved washers
(143) - three bolts and three nuts a side, but only two washers. The step text says the
same thing. One bolt on each handlebar has no washer under it, by the book.

## No torque figure anywhere

Neither table gives one, and no step does either.
