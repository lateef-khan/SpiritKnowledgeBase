Seven illustrated assembly steps, identical in the F80 2023 and F85 2023 books except for the step-1 unpack wording (hardware item numbers in brackets). The uprights thread through the frame base covers before any cable is connected.

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill from the carton onto a smooth, leveled floor (F85) / leveled floor (F80) with the rear at least 3 ft from any wall. F80 wording adds that the unit may fold and bounce upwards; F85 wording says fold up. Same meaning.
2. Insert the left upright (5) through Frame Base Cover (L) (61) and the right upright (4) through Frame Base Cover (R) (62); connect 6P Computer Cable Middle (153) to 6P Computer Cable Lower (154). Do not pinch the cables.
3. Insert the uprights (4) and (5) into the frame base (2) and tighten eight Flat Head Socket Bolts (86) with eight Flat Washers (106). Leave loose until step 5 is finished.
4. Connect 6P middle (153) to 6P upper (152), insert Console Assembly Bottom (53) into the uprights, and tighten four Button Head Socket Bolts (139) with four Split Washers (113). Do not pinch the cables.
5. Connect the four pairs (52-10 to 53-13, 52-11 to 53-29, 52-15 to 53-14, 52-18 to 152), place Console Assembly Top (52) onto the bottom (53), and tighten four Phillips Head Screws (127). Then tighten all screws left loose above.
6. Place the Tablet Holder (177) on the console top (52) with four Sheet Metal Screws (175).
7. Install Frame Base Cover left (61) and right (62) into the frame base (2) with four Phillips Head Screws (127) and two Phillips Head Screws (74), then pull the locking pin (157) out and insert the End Cap (156) into the hole.
