1. Attach the Rear Stabilizer (11) to the mounting plate on the main frame and secure with two 3/8" x 3-3/4" Hex Head Bolts (78), 3/8" Split Washers (108) and 3/8" Curved Washers (106), using the 13/14mm Wrench (116).
2. Run the computer cable (35) from the main body through the console mast tube (2). There is a long tie-wrap that is pre-installed in the console mast tube. Tie the end of the computer cable to the tie-wrap at the bottom of the tube and then use the other end of the tie-wrap to pull the computer cable through the tube. Bolt the console mast tube onto the main body with the four 3/8" x 3/4" Bolts (81), Split Washers (108) and Flat Washers (100).
