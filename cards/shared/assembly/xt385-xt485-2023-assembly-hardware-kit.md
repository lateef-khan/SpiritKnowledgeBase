The panel groups the hardware under four step headings:

| Panel step | Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| STEP 1 | 130 | 5/16" x 15mm Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 pcs |
| STEP 2 | 113 | Ø10 x 2T Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| STEP 2 | 133 | 3/8" x 1-1/2" Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| STEP 2 | 160 | M5 x 45L Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| STEP 3 | 150 | 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| STEP 4 | 142 | 3.5 x 16L Sheet Metal Screw | 8 pcs |
| STEP 4 | 120 | M5 x 15L Phillips Head Screw | 6 pcs |
| STEP 4 | 166 | M5 x 25L Phillips Head Screw | 3 pcs |

The panel has only four step headings but the written procedure has five steps.
Item 166 is printed under STEP 4 on the panel; the written procedure uses it in
**step 5**, for the tablet holder. Items 142 and 120 really are the step 4
fasteners.

Item 132 in these two manuals is the **M6 L Allen Wrench**, not a washer.

This kit does not fit the XT185-2023, XT285-2023 or XT685-2023.
