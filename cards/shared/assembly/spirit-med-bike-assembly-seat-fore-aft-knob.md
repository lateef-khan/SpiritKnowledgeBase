**Loosen the knob and slide the seat to the desired position.** There is a **numbered scale located on the seat slide, under the rear of the seat**, for repeatable settings.

Three books print the sentence word for word: the 4.0R, the 4.0U and the 7.0U. On the two uprights the knob is the **seat adjustment knob (86)** that step one of the build fits with the fixing plate and cap nut to the seat mount slide; the 4.0U's diagram labels it F, Fore/Aft Seat Adjustment. **On the 4.0R the sentence does not match the machine's own service manual**, which has no knob on the seat carriage - it replaces a **seat front/aft adjustment lever (167)**, a lever anchor, a seat position latch and a spring, and the CR800 chassis it shares pulls that lever up to move the seat. The owner's manual prints "loosen the knob" regardless. The 7.0R, 8.0U and 8.5R print a lever or a handle for the same adjustment; those are their own cards. The 4.0R and 4.0U Dyaco PT editions have no setting-up page and list the adjustment only on their features drawing.
