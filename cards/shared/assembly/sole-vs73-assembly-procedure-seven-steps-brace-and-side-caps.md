Seven assembly steps, identical in the VS73-group 2008 and WS73-group 2009 books except for the step-6 tray sentence (hardware item numbers in brackets). Neither book prints a Velcro-strap warning.

1. Remove the treadmill from the carton onto smooth, level ground.
2. Connect Lower Computer Cable (32) to Middle Computer Cable (33).
3. Fix the upright tubes (5, 4) into the main frame (1) with eight 5/16 x 1/2 Button Head Socket Bolts (101).
4. Connect Speed Switch Cable (37) to upper Speed Cable (34), Incline Switch Cable (38) to upper Incline Cable (35), and Middle Computer Cable (33) to upper Computer Cable (31).
5. Fix the Console Assembly (36) onto the uprights with four 3/8 x 1-3/4 Button Head Socket Bolts (91) and four 3/8 x 2.0T Split Washers (86).
6. Bolt the upright support brace (3) between the uprights with four 5/16 x 3/4 Button Head Socket Bolts (100). The 2009 books add one sentence: fit the Accessory Tray (150) onto the support brace. Part (150) appears in no pack checklist in either book.
7. Fix the left (111, 112) and right (113, 114) handgrip side caps on the uprights and Console Assembly (36) with eight M5 x 10 mm Phillips Head Screws (92) and four 3.5 x 12 mm screws (90).

Tighten all bolts securely after assembly is complete.
