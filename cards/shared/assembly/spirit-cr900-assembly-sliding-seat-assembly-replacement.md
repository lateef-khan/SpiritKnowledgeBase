Fifteen numbered sub-steps in both books, printed identically. Removal is 6.9.1 to 6.9.9; refitting and setting is 6.9.10 to 6.9.15.

**Removal**

- **6.9.1 Remove cable ties.** Cut the cable tie inside the seat carriage that restrains the **seat release cable** (figure 6.9.1), and the cable tie that restrains the **hand pulse wiring** (figure 6.9.2).
- **6.9.2 Remove the seat track cover and plastic sweat protector.** Remove the **8 screws** of the seat track cover (figure 6.9.3) and remove the cover and sweat protector.
- **6.9.3 Remove the release lever and cable.** Unscrew the **two mounting bolts** of the release lever assembly (figure 6.9.4). Remove the cable by loosening the **jam nut** (figure 6.9.5), then unscrew the **barrel** until the inside nut is off the threaded end of the barrel; remove the cable end from the lever to free the cable.
- **6.9.4 Remove the aluminum rail end plug.** Release the **two screws** at the end of the aluminium rail and the **one screw** on the outer side (figures 6.9.6 and 6.9.7).
- **6.9.5 Remove the iron plate.** Remove the **four screws** on the iron plate (figure 6.9.8).
- **6.9.6 Remove the seat carriage.** With the release lever off (6.9.3), remove the **two centre, bottom screws on both sides** of the carriage (figure 6.9.9) and **slide the carriage off the back of the unit** (figure 6.9.10).
- **6.9.7 Remove the seat lock mechanism.** Remove the **two axles** from the barrel of the locking mechanism (figures 6.9.12 and 6.9.13).
- **6.9.8 Remove the mechanical lift arm.** Loosen the **nut on the lift arm** (figure 6.9.14). On refitting, **the key points toward the console** and the lift arm faces as in figure 6.9.15; **do not lock the nut before installation** (see 6.9.15).
- **6.9.9 Remove the seat slide.** Slacken the **8 screws** to pull out the seat rails (figures 6.9.16 and 6.9.17) - the screws do not need to come out - and do not lock them yet (see 6.9.13).

**Refitting**

- **6.9.10 Install the seat slide group.** Make sure the **PU wheel** (6.9.7) is locked and will slide into the seat rails, which are still unlocked (6.9.13).
- **6.9.11 Install the cable.** The **round end** goes on the lift arm and the **nut end** passes through the side notch, with the handle as in 6.9.3 (figures 6.9.20 and 6.9.21).
- **6.9.12 Adjust the seat slide group.** **Move the carriage to the front and lock the front two screws; move it to the back and lock the rear two**; then lock the other screws (figures 6.9.22 and 6.9.23).
- **6.9.13 Combine the slide and lift arm.** Refer to 6.9.6 in reverse; the **outer screws are locked to the bottom and fixed to the inner braces**, both sides the same (figures 6.9.24 and 6.9.25).
- **6.9.14 Adjust the mechanical lift arm.** Move the carriage to the rear and lock the nut on the lift arm; move it to the front and **hand-tighten the front lift arm nut - not too tight, so it will not fall off** (figures 6.9.26 and 6.9.27).
- **6.9.15 Install the iron plate and plastic plate.** Route the heart-rate wire back, tie the strap, and set the **8 screws** (figures 6.9.28 and 6.9.29), reversing 6.9.1 and 6.9.2.

**Author's notes left in the print:** 6.9.2's heading ends "(New Picture..too many hands)" and 6.9.8 refers to "6-9-8" for its own removal. The figures skip 6.9.11 (6.9.10 is followed by 6.9.12). No screw sizes are printed anywhere in the fifteen steps.

If the seat shakes in use, the troubleshooting chapter (7-3-2) says to check the wheels are tight and, to take up play between wheels and track, loosen the bottom wheels and lift on the wrench while re-tightening, then the top wheels the same way with the carriage lifted - the same wheel-and-track adjustment, without a torque figure.
