1. Remove the pedal connecting arm by the connecting arm procedure.
2. Use a screwdriver to remove the **M5 x 10 mm Phillips head screw** fixed on the **stabilizer cover**, then remove
   the cover (the 2020 book calls it the slider cover: "a screw (M5*10mm) of slider cover").
3. Use **two 14 mm wrenches** to release the **3/8" x 2-1/4 hex head bolt**, **Ø3/8" x 23 x 2.0T curved washer**,
   **Ø3/8" x Ø19 x 1.5T flat washer** and **3/8" x 7T nyloc nut**; the curved rail tube can then be removed. The
   2020 book: "use 2 14 mm open-end wrenches to remove the bolts and nuts which lock the rail tube".
4. Reassemble in the reverse order.

Both generations print the same one M5 x 10 screw and the same two 14 mm wrenches; only the XS200-SS003 book
(cs800-2016) prints the bolt and washers. The Sole SC200 curved-rail-tube card is the same procedure.
