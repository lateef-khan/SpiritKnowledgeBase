Nine lines, with quantities as printed:

| Qty | Part |
|---|---|
| 1 | Main Frame |
| 1 | Rear Stabilizer |
| 1 | Console Mast |
| 1 | Handlebar |
| 1 | Console Mast Cover |
| 1 | Console |
| 1 | Drink Bottle Holder |
| 2 | Pedals |
| 1 | Hardware Kit |

**Both printings list the same nine lines**, in the same order.

## Two lines the non-touchscreen carton has and this one does not

The CU800 2021 panel lists a **Front Stabilizer Cover** and a **Rear Stabilizer
Cover**; this one lists neither, and no step in either manual fits them. Everything
else is the same nine lines, with Handlebar spelled as one word where the older book
spells it as two. See
[the CU800 parts card](../../cu800-2021/assembly/cu800-2021-assembly-parts-included.md).

## What the panel does not say

It gives no carton count, no shipping weight and no box dimensions, and **it does not
list the seat**, although step 4 fits the Seat (No.19) onto the Sliding Seat Mount
(No.7). Neither part is on this panel.

The panel calls item 116 a **Drink Bottle Holder**; step 4 calls the same item the
Beverage Holder.
