1. Locate the **Console Mast (12)** and **Console Mast Cover (41)** and slide the
   cover onto the mast as far as it will go. Make sure the cover is facing the
   correct way.
2. At the top opening of the Main Frame there is a **Computer Cable (31)**. Unravel
   it, straighten it, feed it into the bottom of the console mast tube and out of
   the top opening.
3. Install the console mast into the receiving bracket in the top of the Main Frame.
   **There is one bolt already installed in the receiving bracket** that engages
   with the slot at the bottom of the mast. It is tightened at the end with the
   other three.
4. Put one **3/8" x 2T Split Washer (96)** onto the **3/8" x 2-1/4" long Hex Head
   Bolt (60)**, and the two **3/8" x 23 Curved Washers (84)** onto the two **3/8" x
   3/4" short Hex Head Bolts (103)**. Hand tighten the long bolt through the **left**
   side of the receiving bracket, then hand tighten the two short bolts through the
   **front**. There is an electrical wire running through the mast tube - do not
   damage or pinch it.
5. With the **13/14m/m Wrench**, tighten the three bolts and the pre-installed fourth
   bolt firmly. "These bolts should be tightened as much as you possibly can."
6. There are three connectors at the top of the mast: two 2-pin (**hand pulse
   sensors, 36**) and one 10-pin (**main wire harness, 31**). Plug them into the back
   of the **Console (30)**. They are keyed, so do not force them; **either 2-pin
   connector goes in either 2-pin socket**.
7. Store the excess wire back in the mast and fit the console to the mounting plate
   with the four **M5 x 10m/m Phillips Head Screws (66)**. Make sure no wire is
   pinched between console and plate.

## The same seven items build all three machines

The manual prints two separate assembly chapters - one headed XE100 / XE200 and one
headed XE300 - and this step is **word for word identical** in both, item numbers
included. It is the only step of the four that is.

## The bolt order is not the order the wrench goes on

Hand-tighten the **left** bolt first and the two front bolts second, then tighten
everything with the wrench. The 2010 and later XE manuals reverse this and tighten
the front pair first, with the left side last; do not carry that order back here.
See [the XE395 2010 build](../../xe395-2010/assembly/xe395-2010-assembly-procedure.md).
