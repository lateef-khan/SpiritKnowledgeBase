**Set the new incline motor to 225 mm before you fit it.** The 2015 books print it as "Incline Range must be
adjusted to 225mm minimum prior to installation", once inside the procedure and once more as their chapter 11
over a drawing; the 2023 books print "To adjust spare Incline motor to lower (225mm)". The XT185 and XT285
use 210 mm and are on another card.

1. Fold the treadmill (2015 books: "First adjust treadmill to folded position"; 2023 books: "Take off Motor
   cover then folding treadmill").
2. Unplug the incline motor wires and unmount the old incline motor.
3. Set the new motor to **225 mm**.
4. Fit the new incline motor with a **14mm wrench** (2023 books: "14mm open-end wrench").
5. Connect the incline motor wiring to the controller: **red wire to UP, white wire to COM, black wire to
   DOWN**.

The 2015 books do not say to take the motor cover off; the 2023 books do. The chapter-11 drawing in the 2015
books dimensions the 225 between the pivot hole on the motor body and the hole at the end of the extended
tube. In the 2023 books the wire colours are printed only as callouts on the photograph of the controller.

Two other machines use 225 mm with different tools or no fold: the XT685 and XT685ENT (a 14mm open-end
wrench plus an M8 Allen key, deck not folded) and the XT485ENT (two Allen keys and a 14mm wrench). Each has
its own card. No torque is printed here.
