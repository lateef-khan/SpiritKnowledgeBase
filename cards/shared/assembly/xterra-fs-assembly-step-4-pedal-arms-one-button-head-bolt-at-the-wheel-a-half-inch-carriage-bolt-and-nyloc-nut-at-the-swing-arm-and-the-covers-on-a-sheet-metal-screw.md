Hardware step 4: **2 carriage bolts 1/2" x 70 mm (29), 2 nyloc nuts 1/2" (30), 2 flat washers (44), 2 split washers (46), 2 sheet metal screws 5 x 12 (52), 2 button head socket bolts 5/16" x 1" (98)**.

1. Locate the right side pedal arm assembly (2) and install it onto the pedal axle's large rotating wheel at the rear of the elliptical. Attach with one button head socket bolt (98), one split washer (46) and one flat washer (44). Use the M5 Allen wrench (68) to tighten.
2. Locate the carriage bolt (29) and slide it through the bracket at the front of the pedal arm assembly (2) and the lower swing arm (6). Fasten a nyloc nut (30) to the carriage bolt (29) with the 19 mm wrench (69).
3. Attach the pedal arm covers (85, 86) onto the pedal arm assembly (2) with one sheet metal screw (52). Tighten using the Phillips screwdriver (68).
4. Repeat steps 1-3 for the left side pedal arm assembly.

This is the last assembly step in both books; neither has a levelling, moving or plug-in step. The 2018 book adds a rear cover on an M5 screw (the linked card).
