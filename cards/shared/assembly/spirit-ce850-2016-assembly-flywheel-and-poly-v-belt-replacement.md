**The stride machines' flywheel is held by a `3/8"-UNF26 x 11T` flange nut and comes out with the belt from the
oblique side of the frame; the idler wheel is part of the same procedure.** The XE-series flywheel uses a **9T**
nyloc nut and a separate idler-wheel section.

1. Remove the **steel cable** from the magnetron flywheel by the gear motor disassembly steps (11-7).
2. Use a **13 mm wrench** to release the **nylon nut (M8 x 7T)** fixing the idler wheel assembly on the main frame.
3. Use a 13 mm wrench to release the **adjust screw**, **nylon nut (M8 x 9T)**, **rod end sleeve** and **flat washer
   (5/16" x 23 x 1.5T)** fixed on the idler wheel assembly.
4. Pull the drive belt off the **drive pulley**.
5. Release the **nylon nut (M8 x 7T)**, **flat washer (5/16" x 23 x 1.5T)** and **carriage bolt (M8 x 20L)** which fix
   the idler wheel assembly, then remove the idler wheel assembly.
6. To remove the **idler wheel bearing 6203**, use C-clip pliers to release the **C ring Ø17** fixed on the idler
   wheel assembly.
7. Use a **15 mm wrench** to release the **flange nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 11T** fixed on the magnetic flywheel.
8. Remove the magnetic flywheel together with the belt from the **oblique side** of the main frame.
9. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
10. When refitting the magnetic flywheel and belt, the drive pulley, belt and drive pulley must stay in the **middle
    position**.
11. If the belt runs to one side, release the flange nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 11T and use a **17 mm wrench** to adjust the nut
    fixed on the inside of the magnetic flywheel. Belt and drive pulley must stay in the middle position; then
    fasten the flange nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 11T.
12. Use the **nylon nut (M8 x 9T)** on the screw shackle to tighten up, and **flick the belt for a crisp sound or use a
    sonic device measured at 190 Hz (+/- 10)** (figure 10). Then turn the crank arm assembly to make the belt and
    flywheel rotate smoothly, and reassemble the other parts.

The CE850-2016 and XE895-2016 books print this section word for word.
