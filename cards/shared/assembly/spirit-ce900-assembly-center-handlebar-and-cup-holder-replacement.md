1. The **self-tapping screw (2 pcs)** is locked on the **cup holder**; the cup holder can be replaced after loosening
   them (figure 6.17.1).
2. Use the tool to release the **heartbeat handle** (figures 6.17.2 and 6.17.3). Refer to figure 6.17.1 and
   procedure 6.16 (the mounting bracket).

That is the whole section; the tool is not named. The three books print it word for word.
