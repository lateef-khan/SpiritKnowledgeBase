**Look closely at the four floor levellers underneath the middle and rear of the
elliptical.** If any of them is not in contact with the floor:

1. Loosen the **top** nut with the wrench.
2. **Turn the rubber caster clockwise** until it makes solid contact with the floor.
3. Retighten the top nut so it cannot move.

All three manuals then finish the build by returning the elevation to flat with the
Incline down key - this job is done with the ramp still up at the level the previous
job raised it to.

## The top nut, not the bottom nut

The **XE195**, **XE295** and **XE395 2010** print this job word for word with one
change: **bottom** nut. Same four levellers, same clockwise turn, opposite nut. See
[the bottom-nut card](spirit-xe-assembly-levelling-bottom-nut.md).

**Four XE395 printings, two answers.** The 2010 XE395 says bottom; the **2018 XE395**,
the 2021 XE395ENT and the 2023 XE395 say top. The model name is not enough to tell you
which; the year is, and the change happened between 2010 and 2018.

## A second, different levelling instruction is printed later in the same book

The **XE395 2018** manual also carries, in its GENERAL MAINTENANCE noise item, the
sentence **"There are 2 leveling pads on the bottom of the rear stabilizer, use a
14mm wrench (or adjustable wrench) to adjust the levelers."** Two pads and a 14mm
wrench there; four levellers and a carton wrench here. The two halves never mention
each other. See
[the 14mm levelling-pad card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

## Four in the instruction and six in the box

The XE395ENT carton list says **6 Levelers** and this job names **four**. The XE395
2023 and XE395 2018 carton lists say six as well. No step on any of the three fits a
leveller.

## The wrench number in the sentence is not a wrench

All three manuals say to use **"the WRENCH (109)"**. The XE395ENT's own parts list gives
item **109** as a *5/16" x UNC18 x 15L flat head socket bolt*. Use the **13/14mm
wrench** from the carton - item 155 in both books.

## This is not the CE or CT levelling instruction

The commercial Spirit ellipticals and treadmills use a different sentence entirely,
printed as a remedy in the noise item rather than as an assembly job. See
[the two-pad card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).
