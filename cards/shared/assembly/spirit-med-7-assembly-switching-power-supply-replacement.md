**The power supply is held by four plastic standoffs, not screws.**

1. Refer to the shroud procedure to remove the **rear shroud (L)** (7.0R: 6.9) or the **side case** (7.0U: 6.8).
2. As shown in figures 6.12.1 and 6.12.2 (7.0U: 6.11.1 and 6.11.2), **take off all the connectors and loosen the 4 plastic standoffs** to remove the switching power supplier.
3. Refit by following the steps in reverse order.

The 7.0R parts list carries the part as **Power Adaptor (236)** and its OCR parts page as "Switching Power Supply"; the 7.0U list as **Switching Power Supply (124)**. It takes the mains in at a 2-pin connector and puts 24 V DC out at an 8-pin connector; those measurements are the no-power troubleshooting, not this card. Isolation columns (4) are in both parts lists, which is what the standoffs are.
