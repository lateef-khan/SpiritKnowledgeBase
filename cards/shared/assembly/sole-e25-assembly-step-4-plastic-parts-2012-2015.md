1. **Connect the Swing Arm Wire (Lower) Resistance/Incline (50) with the Swing Arm Wire (Upper) Incline (49)**, and the **Swing Arm Wire (Lower) Resistance/Incline (50) with the Swing Arm Wire (Upper) Resistance (48)**. Push the excess wire into the Console Mast and **plug in the Switch Wire Cap (100)** at each side on the mast.
2. **Front (89) and rear (90) Handle Bar Covers** together on the left Swing Arm shaft with **two Sheet Metal Screws (122)** using driver (150). Repeat on the right with **(91)** and **(92)**.
3. **Connecting Arm Covers (170-L & 171-R)** on the Left and Right Connecting Arms with **four Phillips Head Screws (120)** and **two Sheet Metal Screws (122)** using the **Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (149)**.
4. **Left Slide Wheel Cover (87)** on the Left Pedal Arm with **two Phillips Head Screws (120)** using driver (150). Repeat for the **Right Slide Wheel Cover (88)**.
5. **Console Chin Cover (Front) (93)** on the mast, matched with the **Console Chin Cover (Rear) (94)**, with **three Phillips Head Screws (120)** and **two Sheet Metal Screws (122)** using driver (150).
6. **Incline Bottom Cover (165)** on the Main Frame (1) with **two Phillips Head Screws (120)** using driver (150).
7. **Front Stabilizer Cover (166)** on the Front Stabilizer Bar (2) with **two Phillips Head Screws (120)** using driver (150).
8. **Snap the Rear Bar Cover (97)** into the hole on the Incline Rail Assembly. No screws hold it.
