**Keep the motor's red, black and blue terminals apart once the wires are off.** *If any two terminals contact,
the winding wheel will not move, affecting disassembly and assembly.*

*(Tools: 6 mm hex wrench, 4 mm hex wrench, Phillips screwdriver)*

1. Pull out the **communication wire, power supply wire, ground wire and temperature sensing wire** of the motor.
2. Remove the **4 screws (3)** and gaskets with the **6 mm hex wrench** to separate the motor module from the
   resistance box.

The 4 mm hex wrench is listed among the tools but no step uses it. The motor is a 2000 W, 43 N-m DC servo with a
magnetic encoder (the specs cards); with the module out, the reel and rope, the drive shaft and the cover come
apart in the next two steps.

The "Digital Resistance Module" is the i-Strength line's motorised resistance box, part 72 in the CSI owner's manuals; the screw numbers are the call-outs on the manual's exploded drawings.
