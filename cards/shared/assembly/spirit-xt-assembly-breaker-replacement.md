The whole procedure, in every one of these eleven books, is one sentence and a photograph.

- 2023 books: **"Unplug Breaker wires to replace and plug wires back."**
- XT185 2015 and XT485ENT: **"Remove Breaker connection wiring, replace part and reconnect wiring."**
- XT285, XT385 and XT485 2015: **"Remove electrical connection wiring, replace part and reconnect wiring."**

Nothing else is printed: no location, no fastener, no rating, no part number, and no instruction to unplug
the machine first. Where the breaker sits and what it is rated are on the safety and specification cards for
each machine, not here.

The AC power switch procedure on the facing page is the same sentence with the switch named instead, and
has its own card.
