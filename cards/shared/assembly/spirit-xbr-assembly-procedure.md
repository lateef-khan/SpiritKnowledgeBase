Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. Hardware quantities are on
[the hardware card](spirit-xbr-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Step one - rear stabilizer and seat back

1. Attach the **Rear Stabilizer (7)** to the **Main Frame (1)** with 2 **Bolts (65)**,
   2 **Flat Washers (84)** and 2 **Cap Nuts (191)**. Tighten completely with the
   **Wrench (112)**.
2. Attach the **Seat Back (5)** to the **Seat Carriage (4)** of the main frame. Slide 1
   **Bolt (67)** through each side, then attach a **Nyloc Nut (89)** to each bolt.
   **Tighten just enough that there is no side-to-side play but the seat back still
   moves freely front to back.** Use wrenches **112 and 130**.
3. Insert an **End Cap (32)** into each opening of the front stabilizer tube. A rubber
   mallet may be needed; **no mallet is in the carton**.

## Step two - console mast, handlebar and console

1. Slide the **Computer Cable (44)**, **Handlebar Resistance Control Cable (148)** and
   **Hand Pulse Cable (45)** through the bottom of the **Console Mast Cover (31)** and
   then the bottom of the **Console Mast (2)**, with the cover the right way up.
2. Install the Console Mast (2) into the receiving tube of the Main Frame (1), taking
   care not to pinch the cables. Insert 2 **Bolts (68)** and 2 **Flat Washers (76)** on
   each side, and 2 Bolts and 2 **Curved Washers (83)** on the front. **Tighten all six
   firmly** with the Wrench (112).
3. Remove the white styrofoam pad, factory-fitted to stop bolts dropping down the mast
   tube.
4. Attach the **Handle Bar Assembly (3)** to the Console Mast (2) bracket with 2 Bolts
   (68), 2 **Split Washers (82)** and 2 Flat Washers (76).
5. Plug the three cables into their connectors behind the **Console Assembly (19)** and
   fix the console to the mounting plate with 4 **Phillips Head Screws (99)**, using the
   **Phillips Head Screwdriver (114)**.

## Step three - seat and seat handlebar

1. Install the **Seat (61)** on the Seat Carriage (4) with 4 **Phillips Head Screws
   (98)**.
2. Attach the **Seat Handle Bar (6)** to the Seat Carriage (4) with 4 **Hex Head Bolts
   (71)**, 4 **Flat Washers (77)** and 4 Nyloc Nuts (89), using wrenches 112 and 130.
3. Plug the **Hand Pulse Sensor Cables (27)** into the **rear two holes on the left side
   of the rear plastic cover**, and the **Handlebar Resistance Cables (150)** into the
   remaining front hole.

## Step four - gas cylinder, pedals and bottle holders

1. Install the **Gas Cylinder (57)** on the **Seat Back Frame (5)** with 1 **Bolt
   (70)**, 1 Flat Washer (76) and 1 **Nyloc Nut (91)**, using wrenches **112 and 113**.
2. Attach the **Pedals** to the **Crank Arms (51L, 51R)** with the Wrench (113). The
   **left pedal is reverse-threaded** and goes on counterclockwise. **No torque figure
   is printed** - see
   [the pedal card](spirit-bike-assembly-pedal-tighten-as-much-as-possible.md).
3. Attach the **Drink Bottle Holders (39L, 38R)** to the sides of the Seat Handle Bar
   (6) with 4 **Sheet Metal Screws (105)**.

## The build ends here

There is **no first power-on step**: these bikes are generator-powered and the console
starts when you pedal. There is no levelling step and no way to move the machine printed
anywhere in these six books - see
[the absence card](spirit-xb-assembly-no-moving-or-transport-section.md).

**The carton packs a Front Stabilizer Cover and a Rear Stabilizer Cover that no step
fits**, exactly as on the CR800. See
[the parts card](spirit-cr800-assembly-parts-included.md).

## What differs between the six printings

The six books were compared word for word. Apart from page numbers and line breaks:

- **The XBR95 2016 book misprints "Contiued on next page..."** in step two. Fixed from
  2018 on.
- **The XBR95 2021 and XBR55 2021 books write the pedal as "(116)"**; the XBR95 2016 and
  2018, the XBR55 2019 and the XBR55ENT write **"(116L, 117R)"**. Same pedals.
- **The XBR55 2019 book writes "styrofoam"; the XBR55 2021 and XBR55ENT write
  "Styrofoam".**
- The XBR55ENT names the bolt head types in its hardware tables where the others leave
  the column blank.

Nothing else in the build changed. The hardware did not move at all.

## This is not the XBR25 build

The XBR25 fixes its seat back with **four** bolts through two sets of holes rather than
two, and it **has no gas cylinder**: its step four is pedals and bottle holders only.
See [the XBR25 build](../../xbr25-2019/assembly/xbr25-2019-assembly-procedure.md).
