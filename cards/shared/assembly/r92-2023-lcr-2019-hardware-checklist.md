Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #65 | 3/8" x 53L | Carriage Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #77 | 3/8" x 19mm x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #84 | 3/8" x 25mm x 2T | Flat Washer | 2 pcs |
| #176 | 3/8" x 3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 6 pcs |
| #191 | 3/8" x16 x 12.5T | Cap Nut | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #71 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #77 | 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| #89 | 3/8" x 7T | Nyloc Nut | 4 pcs |
| #175 | 3/8" x 2-3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 3**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #68 | 5/16" x 5/8" | Hex Head Bolt | 8 pcs |
| #76 | 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #82 | 5/16" x 1.5T | Split Washer | 2 pcs |
| #83 | 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T | Curved Washer | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 4**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #98 | M6 x 15mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| #99 | M5 x 12mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| #105 | Ø4 x 16mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |
| #192 | 3.5 x 12L | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |

**The R92 2019 pack is the same list** and is covered by its own card. The **LCR 2023** pack is this list plus a fifth item in step 4, `#196 ø5 x 0.6T Star Washer (4 pcs)`.

**#65 is a Carriage Bolt in this checklist**, and the LCR 2019 and R92 2023 step 1 instructions both name it differently: the LCR 2019 calls it a **Hex Head Bolt**, the R92 2023 calls it a **Carriage Bolt**. Neither manual reconciles the wording.

**Item numbers are not shared with the B94 or LCB uprights.** Here `#71` is a hex head bolt and `#99` is a Phillips head screw; on the uprights `#71` is a flat washer and `#99` is a curved washer. Always use the checklist printed in your own machine's manual.
