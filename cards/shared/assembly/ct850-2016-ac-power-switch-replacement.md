Both manuals print this procedure word for word the same, so it is one procedure for both machines.
The 2016 manual heads it **12.4** and runs it as one paragraph; the 2020 manual heads it **11-4** and
labels the same paragraph "Step 1". Nothing else differs.

1. Use a **Phillips head screwdriver** to remove the **front cover (7 tapping screws)** and also
   remove the **AC input module cables**.
2. Use the Phillips head screwdriver again to remove the **overload protective device**.
3. Resume cable connections and all parts in reverse order after replacement.

The manual gives no torque figure and no part number for the switch or for the overload protective
device, and it does not say to unplug the machine first - do that anyway.

The manual calls the second part the **overload protective device**. The troubleshooting checklist
separately names a **circuit breaker on the front grill** that you push in until it locks when the
display is dead. Neither manual says whether those two names are the same part.
