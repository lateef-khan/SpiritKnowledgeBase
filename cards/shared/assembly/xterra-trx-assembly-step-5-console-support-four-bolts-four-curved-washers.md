Install the **console support (5)** onto the **uprights (L, R)** with **4 button head socket bolts and 4
curved washers**, using the combination M5 Allen wrench and Phillips screwdriver. "Be careful not to
pinch the computer cables when attaching the console." Then **tighten all screws** - the upright bolts
from step 3 were left loose until now.

| Book | Uprights | Bolt (x4) | Curved washer (x4) |
|---|---|---|---|
| TRX1000 2021 | (L, R) (4, 3) | (87) | (88) |
| TRX1400 2023 | (L, R) (3, 4) | (94) 5/16" x 1/2" | (95) Ø8 x Ø23 x 1.5T |

**No flat washers at this step** - the TR150/TR260 version uses six bolts with two flat and four curved.
On the TRX1400 a sixth step follows (the frame base covers); on the TRX1000 this is the last hardware
step, followed only by a note to check the belt lubrication before first use.
