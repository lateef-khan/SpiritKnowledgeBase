| No | Description | Quantity |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4.2 x 19 mm Bolts | 6 |
| 2 | 5 mm Allen Key | 1 |
| 3 | 6 mm Allen Key | 1 |
| 4 | Wrench | 1 |
| 5 | Tablet Holder | 1 |
| 6 | Power Cord | 1 |
| 7 | Safety Key | 1 |
| 8 | Silicone Oil | 2 |
| 9 | Manual | 1 |

The six bolts are the "cross-recessed countersunk head screws" that fix the tablet holder under the
console at assembly step 3 - the only fasteners the assembly uses, because the uprights and handles
arrive hinged and simply lift into place. The list does not give the wrench a size. Both books print
this page word for word.
