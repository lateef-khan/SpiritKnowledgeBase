Hardware: **#94** ø10 × ø16.5 × 2T Split Washer (4 pcs), **#93** 3/8" × 3-3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt (4 pcs), **#95** ø10 × ø23 × 1.5T Curved Washer (4 pcs).

1. Install the Main Frame (1), the Front Stabilizers (2) and the Rear Stabilizers (3) with four Button Head Socket Bolts (93), four Split Washers (94) and four Curved Washers (95), by using the **M6 L Allen Wrench (102)**.

**Three fasteners per corner group, and a split washer as well as a curved washer.** The 2021 and earlier indoor cycles use a nut, a bolt and a curved washer (#82, #81, #83) and no split washer, and they are tightened with an open wrench, not an Allen key.

**This step does not say which stabilizer goes at the front.** Every SB900 manual from 2014 to 2021 says the stabilizer with the wheels goes on the front; the 2023 manuals drop that sentence from the step. The wheels are still on the front floor mount - the "Moving The Bike" section later in both manuals says so.
