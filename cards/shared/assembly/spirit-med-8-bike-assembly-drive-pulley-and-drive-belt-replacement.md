1. As shown in figure 6.9.1 (8.5R: 6.5.1), **remove the C rings on the crank axle** to remove the **drive pulley**.
2. As shown in figure 6.9.2 (8.5R: 6.5.2), **slide the drive belt out of the idler wheel assembly** to remove the drive belt.

That is the whole procedure. **No belt tension figure is printed anywhere in either book** - the troubleshooting chapter's answer to a slipping or shedding belt is "refer to 6.9, 6.10 and figure 6.10.2 to adjust tension by tightening nut", the M8 nuts on the idler wheel assembly. The 7.0R and 7.0U print newton figures on their eye-bolt nuts; the 8-series books do not. The parts list carries **two Ø20 C rings (128)** and a **Drive Belt (30)**, and the pulley as **Drive Pulley (24-1)** on a **Crank Axle (24)** with a **Magnet (24-2)** - the magnet the Hall sensor reads, which must stay 2 to 3 mm from the sensor and aligned between its two arrows (the no-revolutions troubleshooting).

The chain covers have to be off first; the procedure does not say so.
