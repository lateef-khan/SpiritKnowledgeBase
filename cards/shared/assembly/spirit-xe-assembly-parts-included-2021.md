All eleven cartons print the same list, in the same order:

- 1 Main Frame
- 1 Console Mast
- 1 Console Mast Cover
- 2 Swing Arms
- 2 Connecting Arms
- 6 Levelers
- 1 Console
- 2 Foot Pedals
- **1 Power Cord** *(every machine except the XE795)*
- 1 Audio Cable
- 2 Transport Wheels
- 1 Hardware Kit

**Twelve lines on ten machines and eleven on the XE795.** The XE795 carton has **no
power cord**, on the 2018 printing and on both the February and the November 2021
printings, because its console runs off a built-in generator - start pedalling and it
wakes up. Nothing else is added or removed on any of the eleven.

## The list did not change between 2018 and 2021

The XE395 2018, XE795 2018, XE895 2018, XE195 2019, XE295 2019 and XG400 2019 cartons
print the same twelve lines - eleven on the XE795 2018 - in the same order as their
2021 printings. **The XE895 2018 is the only machine on this card with no later
printing**; its carton is the twelve-line list with the power cord.

## Six levellers on the list, four in the instructions

Every one of these lists says **6 Levelers**. The XE195, XE295, XE395 2018 and
XE395ENT levelling job says to look at **"the four floor levelers underneath the
middle and rear"**. The XG400 and XE795 builds fit **three per side, six in all**.
**The XE895 2018 fits none**: it lists six levellers and no step in its book installs
or adjusts one. The count on the carton list is the one that matches what is in the
box. See
[the levelling card](spirit-xe-assembly-levelling-bottom-nut.md).

## What is on the list and what is not

- **No water bottle holder line**, although the XG400, the XE795 and the XE895 all
  have one - the XG400 fits it in step 2 and the XE795 in step 5, and the XE895 has it
  on its machine diagram already fitted. It is not counted in the carton list on any of
  the three.
- **No end caps line**, although the XG400 and XE795 builds press six into the oval
  stabilizer tubes.
- **No tablet holder.** That arrives in 2023.
- The **hardware kit** is one line, last, on all five. What is inside it is bagged by
  step and is on each machine's own hardware card.
- The **tools** are printed in a second panel on the same page and are on their own
  card - see [tools included](spirit-xe-assembly-tools-2021.md).

## The commercial CE850 prints the same twelve lines

The **CE850** 2016 and 2020 cartons carry these twelve lines in this order, and the
2022 printing adds a water bottle holder as a thirteenth. That list is on
[its own card](spirit-ce850-assembly-parts-included.md), which this one does not
replace; the XE895 2018, whose build is otherwise the CE850 build, is on **this** card
because it is a residential XE.

## The XG400 list says "elliptical" parts for a machine that is not an elliptical

Its cover calls it the **XG400 E-Glide Trainer** and its own steps call it the
e-Glide, but its carton list is the elliptical list word for word, swing arms and
connecting arms included. It also arrives with a **hand brake lever** and a **brake
cable** that appear on no line of this list.
