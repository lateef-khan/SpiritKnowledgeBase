All five cartons print the same list, in the same order:

- 1 Main Frame
- 1 Console Mast
- 1 Console Mast Cover
- 2 Swing Arms
- 2 Connecting Arms
- 6 Levelers
- 1 Console
- 2 Foot Pedals
- **1 Power Cord** *(XE195, XE295, XE395ENT and XG400 only)*
- 1 Audio Cable
- 2 Transport Wheels
- 1 Hardware Kit

**Twelve lines on four machines and eleven on the XE795.** The XE795 2021 carton has
**no power cord**, on both the February and the November printing, because its
console runs off a built-in generator - start pedalling and it wakes up. Nothing else
is added or removed on any of the five.

## Six levellers on the list, four in the instructions

Every one of these lists says **6 Levelers**. The XE195, XE295 and XE395ENT levelling
job says to look at **"the four floor levelers underneath the middle and rear"**. The
XG400 and XE795 builds fit **three per side, six in all**. The count on the carton
list is the one that matches what is in the box. See
[the levelling card](spirit-xe-assembly-levelling-bottom-nut.md).

## What is on the list and what is not

- **No water bottle holder line**, although the XG400 and the XE795 both fit one -
  the XG400 in step 2 and the XE795 in step 5. It is not counted in the carton list
  on either machine.
- **No end caps line**, although the XG400 and XE795 builds press six into the oval
  stabilizer tubes.
- **No tablet holder.** That arrives in 2023.
- The **hardware kit** is one line, last, on all five. What is inside it is bagged by
  step and is on each machine's own hardware card.
- The **tools** are printed in a second panel on the same page and are on their own
  card - see [tools included](spirit-xe-assembly-tools-2021.md).

## The XG400 list says "elliptical" parts for a machine that is not an elliptical

Its cover calls it the **XG400 E-Glide Trainer** and its own steps call it the
e-Glide, but its carton list is the elliptical list word for word, swing arms and
connecting arms included. It also arrives with a **hand brake lever** and a **brake
cable** that appear on no line of this list.
