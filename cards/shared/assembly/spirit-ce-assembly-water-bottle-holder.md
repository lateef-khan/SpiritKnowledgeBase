The procedure is the same on all three machines:

1. Attach the **water bottle holder clamp** to the console mast with **2 M5x12mm
   Philips head screws**.
2. Mount the **water bottle holder** to the clamp with **2 more M5x12mm Philips head
   screws**.

Four screws in total. The manuals name no torque and no tool for it beyond the
Phillips screwdriver in the carton.

## The item numbers are different on every machine

| Machine | Clamp | Holder | Screw item | Fitted in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ce800-2023 | 160 | 161 | 78 | step three, item 5 |
| ce800ent | 158 | 159 | 97 | step three, item 5 |
| ce850-2022 | 212 | 213 | 152 | step **one**, item 4 |

**Nothing carries across.** Ordering item 160 for a CE850 gets the wrong part. On the
CE850 the holder goes on during step one, before the console is fitted, not during
step three.

## The CE850's screw is printed at two lengths

The CE850 2022 step-one hardware panel calls item 152 a **M5 x 12mm** screw, and so
does the step text. Its own step-**four** panel calls item 152 a **M5 x 15L** Phillips
head screw, and so does its parts list. It is one item number described two ways in
one manual. The parts list is the two-against-one majority; the count there rose from
**22** on the 2020 printing to **26** on the 2022, which is exactly the four screws
this holder takes.

The CE800 has no such problem: item 78 is M5 x P0.8 x 12L in the parts list, the
panel and the step alike, and its count rose from 25 to 29.

## Which machines have one at all

- **Fitted, with a step:** ce800-2023, ce800ent, ce850-2022.
- **Not fitted, no step, not in the carton list:** ce800-2013, ce800-2021,
  ce850-2020.
- **ce850-2016** is the odd one. Its machine diagram on p. 9 labels a **WATER BOTTLE
  HOLDER**, but its carton list does not include one and none of its six assembly
  steps mentions one. The 2020 printing then drops the label from the diagram, and
  the 2022 printing puts it back and adds the step. Do not promise a 2016 owner a
  holder on the strength of that diagram.
- **ce900-2021 and ce900-2025** have one, but it is a different part fitted a
  different way and both manuals call it a **CUP HOLDER (99)** in the build - two
  self-tapping screws (46) and two washers (58) onto the handlebar bracket, in step
  one. The 2025 manual's own parts diagram labels the same object "F. Water Bottle
  Holder". See [the CE900 build](spirit-ce900-assembly-procedure.md).
- **ces880-2025** has an **Accessory Tray** instead, pre-attached; there is no bottle
  holder part and no step for one.
