1. *(2019 and 2025 books only)* Connect the Left Pedal Strap (L) to the Left Pedal (No. 09L) and adjust the strap to the length you need; fit the Right Pedal Strap (R) to the Right Pedal (No. 09R) the same way.
2. Connect the left and right pedals (09L, 09R) to their crank arms (15; 15L and 15R in the later books). **The right pedal is on the right hand side of the bike as you sit on it.**
3. **The right pedal (09R) is threaded on clockwise and the left pedal (09L) counterclockwise.** "The left pedal should turn towards the front of the machine (counterclockwise) when screwing them on."

No torque figure and no tool are printed in any of the five books. The November 2019 service document for the 2019 upright supplies both: **"R-Pedal tighten CLOCKWISE & L-Pedal COUNTER CLOCKWISE with 15 mm wrench. TIP: Use rubber mallet to strike wrench, confirm max tightening!"** The maintenance page of every book says to check the pedals are tight before each workout because they work loose.

The 2021 books have no pedal straps in the step; the 2019 and 2025 books add the strap sentence and the pedals are drawn with straps.
