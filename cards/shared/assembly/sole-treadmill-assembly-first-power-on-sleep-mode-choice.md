First power-on, the last step of assembly. This paragraph is identical in the F80 2023, F85 2023, F89 2023, ST90 2023 and TT8 2023 books: unlike every older book, these print no scrolling version message and no odometer readout here, and instead describe the console sleep-mode choice.

Power the treadmill on by plugging it into an appropriate wall outlet, then turn on the power switch located at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood (at the front of the treadmill in the ST90 book). Ensure that the Safety Key is installed, as the treadmill will not power on without it (see assembly step 7 for reference; reference absent in the ST90 book). ATTENTION: the console comes in Sleep mode with three features by choice: ON puts the unit in an energy-saving state after 15 minutes of inactivity; OFF keeps the console lit while the power is on; RETAIL runs the demo video after 3 minutes of inactivity. On TFT displays, click Sleep Mode under Settings to switch among the three.

Sleep-mode power draw figures, if any, belong to the specs section (see the linked card); changing the setting itself is console-section matter.
