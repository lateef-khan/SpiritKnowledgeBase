Twelve lines, with quantities as printed:

| Qty | Part |
|---|---|
| 1 | Main Shroud |
| 1 | Console Mast |
| 1 | Console Mast Cover |
| 2 | Swing Arms |
| 1 | Water Bottle Holder |
| 4 | Levelers |
| 1 | Console |
| 2 | Foot Pedals |
| 1 | Power Cord |
| 1 | Audio Cable |
| 2 | Transport Wheels |
| 1 | Hardware Kit |

## Four levellers and two transport wheels, and no step fits either

The two-step build never mentions a leveller or a transport wheel, and neither appears
in either hardware table. The machine diagram on the next page labels **LEVELERS** at
the bottom of the frame, and the maintenance chapter counts **two** levelling pads under
the rear stabiliser, not four - see
[the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).
The panel's four and the maintenance page's two are never reconciled.

## The swing arms and the foot pedals are not fitted either

The panel lists **2 Swing Arms**, **2 Foot Pedals** and the **Main Shroud**, and no step
of the build attaches any of them. Whatever arrives already on the frame, the manual
does not say.

## The water bottle holder has two names

The panel calls it a **Water Bottle Holder** and step two item 5 calls it a **Drink
Bottle Holder (157)**. Same part, two names, one book.

## What the panel does not say

No carton count, no shipping weight, no box dimensions, and no part numbers - the panel
is the only list in the chapter that names parts without item numbers.
