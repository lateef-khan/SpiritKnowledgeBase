The whole procedure, in all six books, is one sentence and a photograph:

**"Remove Motor cover then unplug Speed Sensor wire from Controller and replace it."**

It names no screw, no gap and no test. The 2015 XT books and the CT850 2016 book print a third line for the
same job - "after replacement, a test to check if the sensor registers the magnet is required" - and these
books do not. The sensor-to-magnet gap for these machines, where a book prints one, is in the error-code
chapter, not here.
