Hardware: **#50** 3/8" x 53L Carriage Bolt (2 pcs), **#135** 3/8" x16 x 12.5T Cap Nut (2 pcs), **#71** 3/8" x 25 x 2T Flat Washer (2 pcs).

1. **Remove the Packing Board (164), the Hex Head Bolts (152), the Nylon Washers (44) and the Nuts (82) from the main frame (1).**
2. To match the Rear Stabilizer (5) with the main frame (1), use 2 pcs of Carriage Bolts (50), 2 pcs of Flat Washers (71) and 2 pcs of Cap Nuts (135), and secure with the Wrench (100).

The packing board and the four fasteners that hold it (152, 44, 82) are **shipping parts, not assembly parts**. They are not in the hardware pack checklist and they are discarded; the rear stabilizer goes on in their place.

Both 2023 manuals print "Mian frame" for "Main frame" in this step.

Only the 2023 machines print the packing-board removal. The B94 2019 and LCB 2019 step 1 does not mention it.
