1. **Front (89) and rear (90) Handle Bar Covers** together on the left Swing Arm shaft with **two Sheet Metal Screws (122)** using driver (150). Repeat on the right with **Handle Bar Covers (91) and (92)**.
2. **Console Mast Covers (L & R) (81 & 82)** on each side of the Side Cases with **two Sheet Metal Screws (122)** using driver (150).
3. **Left Slide Wheel Cover (87)** on the left Pedal Arm with **two Phillips Head Screws (120)** using driver (150). Repeat for the right **Slide Wheel Cover (88)**.
4. **Snap the Rear Bar Cover (97)** into the hole on the Incline Rail Assembly. No screws hold it.
