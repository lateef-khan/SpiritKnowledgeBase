Four tables, one per step. Item numbers are the assembly-drawing numbers.

## Step 1

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | BOLT | 3/8" X 2" | 2 |
| 71 | FLASH WASHER | Ø3/8" × Ø25 × 2T | 2 |
| 135 | NUT | 3/8" × UNC16 × 12.5T | 2 |

**"FLASH WASHER" is a misprint for FLAT WASHER**, and all three printings carry it. The
step text calls the same item a Flat Washer.

## Step 2

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 51 | BOLT | 5/16"XUNC18X5/8" | 7 |
| 72 | FLAT WASHER | Ø5/16" × Ø18 × 1.5T | 6 |
| 99 | CURVED WASHER | Ø5/16" × 19 × 1.5T | 1 |

Seven bolts, six flat washers on the side bolts and one curved washer on the front bolt.

## Step 3

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 51 | BOLT | 5/16" X UNC18 X 5/8" | 2 |
| 72 | FLAT WASHER | Ø5/16" × Ø18 × 1.5T | 2 |
| 103 | SPLIT WASHER | 5/16" X 1.5T | 2 |

Items 51 and 72 appear in both step 2 and step 3. Nine of item 51 and eight of item 72
in the carton in total; they are one bolt and one washer, not two of each.

## Step 4

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 58 | PHILLIPS HEAD SCREW | M5 X 12MM | 6 |

Six screws for the whole step: four hold the console, two the drink bottle holder. **The
only metric fastener in the build.**

## Three printings, ten rows, and not one figure moves

The XBU55 2019, XBU55 2021 and XBU55ENT tables were compared row by row - item number,
description and quantity. **Every value is identical on all three.**

**The XBU55ENT changes the type column and nothing else**, writing **CARRIAGE BOLT** for
item 50 and **HEX HEAD BOLT** for item 51 where the other two write BOLT.

## Steps 2, 3 and 4 are the CU800 tables; step 1 is not

The commercial CU800 uses the same item numbers and the same quantities in steps 2, 3 and
4. **Its step one does not match:** a **3/8" x 135mm** bolt (item 50) with a **curved
washer (151)**, against this machine's **3/8" x 2"** bolt with a flat washer and a nut.
Do not read one book's step one off the other. See
[the CU800 hardware card](spirit-cu800-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## The 2023 XBU55 changes one quantity

Its panel lists **nine** of item 58 where these three list six, because its build adds a
tablet holder held by three more screws. Every other row is the same. See
[that card](../../xbu55-2023/assembly/xbu55-2023-assembly-hardware-kit.md).
