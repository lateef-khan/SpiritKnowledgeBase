**These two steps are diagram-only: the page carries no written instruction, only exploded drawings with callouts.** The drawings were read on the render of PDF p.10.

**Step 3** spans the lower connecting pipe welding group (001) between the two upright frames, joined with the M12x90 bolts, M12 washers and M12 nuts of the Hardware Step 3 kit.

**Step 4** spans the top connection welding group (002) between the tops of the uprights, joined with the M12x85 bolts, M12 washers and M12 nuts of the Hardware Step 4 kit.
