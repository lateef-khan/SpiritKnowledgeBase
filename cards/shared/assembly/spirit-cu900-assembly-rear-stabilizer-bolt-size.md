**Three bolts, item 61, with three flat washers (60) at 13X23X2.0T.** The step is the
same in all three printings. The bolt is not.

| Printing | Item 61 as printed |
|---|---|
| **CU900 2018** and **CU900ENT** | BOLT **M10X55L** |
| **CU900 2025** | BOLT **M8X55L** |

Same length, same count, same washer, one thread size apart. The 2025 book is otherwise
the 2018 book reflowed: their assembly chapters print the same four steps word for word
and every other line of every other hardware table is identical.

## Nothing in the manuals says which is right

Neither printing corrects the other and neither mentions a change. The washer stayed at
a 13mm bore, which suits a 10mm bolt with clearance and is loose on an 8mm one - but
the washer line is unchanged in both books, so it settles nothing on its own.

**Read the step-one table in the manual that came with the machine, and check the bolt
in the bag before you drive it.** A bolt two sizes under will start in a 10mm thread
and strip.

## The recumbent uses neither

The CR900 fits its rear stabilizer with two M10XP1.5X30MM and two M10XP1.5X55MM bolts
and four split washers, a different joint entirely. See
[the recumbent hardware card](spirit-cr900-assembly-hardware-kit.md).
