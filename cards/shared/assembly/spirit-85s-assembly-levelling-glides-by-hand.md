Once the machine is assembled and standing on a flat floor:

1. Use a **½" wrench** to **loosen the top nut of the leveler**.
2. **Adjust the levelers by hand** as necessary to remove any wobble.
3. **Tighten the top nut** against the bottom of the stabilizer tube.
4. Make sure the **bottom nut remains cinched against the levelling foot**.

## The page gives no count and no order

It says "the leveler" in the singular in step 1 and "the levelers" in the plural in step
2, and never says how many there are or where they sit. The machine diagram labels
**Q. Leveling glides** on the 8.5S and **J. Leveling glides** on the 8.5S-Fit, once
each, without a count.

**Do not borrow the six from the 7.0S or 7.5S.** Those two and the MS300 print a longer
version of this page that counts six glides and sets the middle pair separately; this
page has neither the count nor the two-stage order. See
[that card](spirit-rehab-stepper-assembly-levelling-six-glides.md).

## The same book prints a second wrench size for the same nut

The maintenance chapter's noise item on both machines says **2 levelling pads on the
bottom of the front and rear stabilizer** and to **use a 14mm wrench (or adjustable
wrench) to loosen the top nut of the levelers**, then repeats the top-nut and bottom-nut
sentences word for word.

**½" is 12.7 mm and 14mm is not.** The two pages describe the same nut and name two
different wrenches, and neither refers to the other. An adjustable wrench answers both.
See
[the maintenance card](../maintenance/spirit-85s-maintenance-pedals-and-front-and-rear-stabiliser-pads.md).
