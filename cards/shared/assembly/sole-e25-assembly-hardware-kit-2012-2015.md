The hardware is bagged per assembly step. Numbers in parentheses are assembly-drawing item numbers. One book covers the E25-2013, E25-2014, and E25-2015, so one card covers all three. Steps 2 and 3 are shared with the E20 section; Steps 1 and 4 are E25-specific.

| #112 | 3/8" x 3-3/4" Hex Head Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #119 | M5 x 10mm Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
