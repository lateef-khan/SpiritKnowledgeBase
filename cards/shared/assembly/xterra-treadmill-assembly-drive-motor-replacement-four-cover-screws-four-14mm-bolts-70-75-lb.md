**Both books open the motor cover with four screws**; the TRX2500-5500 use three at the front and two
at the side. Both re-tension the drive belt to **70-75 lb** and wire **red to M+, black to M-**.

**TR260 (GT75A, 9-3)** - printed as steps 1, 2, 4, 5, 6; **there is no step 3 on the page**:

1. Loosen the **4 motor cover front locking screws** with a screwdriver.
2. Unmount the drive motor **ground wire** and the **2 input wires (black and red)**.
4. Remove the **4 drive motor locking bolts with a 14 mm wrench** and loosen the drive belt. Then do the
   reverse to mount the new motor with the 4 bolts, **but do not secure them**.
5. Adjust the drive belt tension with a **14 mm open-end wrench**. Measure it with a tension meter: **about
   70-75 lb**.
6. Plug the 2 input wires into the controller (**red to M+ / black to M-**) and mount the ground wire.

**TRX1400 (T3, 12-3)**:

1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **4 sheet metal screws** of the motor cover.
2. Remove the motor **grounding wire (greenish yellow)**, motor wire (+) **red** and motor wire (-) **black**.
3. Use a **14 mm T-shaped socket wrench** to loosen the **4 screws**, a **14 mm open-end wrench** to loosen the
   **1 belt tension screw**, remove the motor and fit the new one. Reassemble in reverse, re-hook the belt
   (readjust it to be parallel with the main frame after re-hooking). Do not tighten the 4 securing
   screws yet.
4. With the 14 mm open-end wrench adjust the belt tension using a tension measuring device - **to the
   white LBS area between 70-75 lb**. Tighten the 4 securing screws with the T-socket wrench. Connect
   the grounding wire and the motor wires (**red M+, black M-**).

Neither book says to unplug the treadmill first or names a bolt torque.
