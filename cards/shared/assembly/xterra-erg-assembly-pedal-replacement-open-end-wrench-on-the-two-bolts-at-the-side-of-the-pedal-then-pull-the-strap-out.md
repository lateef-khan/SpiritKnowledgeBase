1. Use an open-end wrench to turn the **two screws on both sides of the pedal (12)** counterclockwise and remove them - the ERG180 book: "remove the two bolts located on the side of the pedal".
2. Pull out the **pedal strap** in the direction shown (ERG160) / "remove the foot strap from the pedal" (ERG180).
3. Reassemble in the reverse order.

The two bolts are the M12 x 160 hex head bolts the owner's manual fits in step 5, one of them through a spacer sleeve - the service books do not repeat the size. Both service manuals open with the same remark: "Some figure may be different from the machine, but you can refer to the procedure to replace parts."
