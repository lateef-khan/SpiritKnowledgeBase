**Six screws, one tool, no cover.** The larger 2023 XT machines take a Phillips-screwed upright cover off first
and then four Allen bolts; these two do not.

1. Use an **M6 hex wrench** to remove the screws fastening the console assembly to the left and right
   uprights. There are a total of **six screws**.
2. Unplug all the wires, then replace the console.

No torque is printed and the procedure does not say to unplug the machine first.
