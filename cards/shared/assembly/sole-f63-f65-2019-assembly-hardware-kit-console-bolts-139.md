Hardware supplied in the box with the Sole F63-2019 and F65-2019, grouped by assembly step; the two books print the identical kit. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing. Bolt sizes read off the page rendering; the OCR supplement drops the `#130`/`#139` labels on these pages.

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | #128 | M5 | Speed Nut Clip | 2 pcs |
| 3 | #130 | 5/16" x 1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 pcs |
| 5 | #139 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #113 | Dia 10 x 1.5T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 6 | #175 | 3.5 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #159 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #75 | - | Safety Key | 1 pc |
| 8 | #120 | 5 x 16 mm | Tapping Screw | 2 pcs |
| 8 | #94 | M5 x 20 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 2 pcs |
| 9 | #175 | 3.5 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |

Tools in the box: #131 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver, #132 M6 L Allen Wrench.

This kit differs from the 2016 F63/F65 kit in steps 2, 6 and 8 (two clips instead of six, four cover screws instead of six, two tapping plus two Phillips screws instead of six tapping screws) and adds the step-9 tablet-holder screws.
