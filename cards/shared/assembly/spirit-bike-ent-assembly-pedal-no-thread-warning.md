What the three touchscreen manuals print, and the whole of it:

- **CU800ENT (2022 and 2024):** "Install the Pedals (No.45L/R) onto the Crank Arms
  (No. 16L/16R) carefully using the Wrench (No.92) to hold the Pedal pivot then firmly
  fasten the pivot clockwise."
- **CR800ENT:** "Install the Pedals (No.116L/116R) onto the Crank Arms (No. 51L/51R)
  carefully using the Wrench (No.132) to hold the Pedal pivot then firmly fasten the
  pivot clockwise."

**Neither sentence mentions a reverse thread, a left-hand thread or a counterclockwise
turn, and neither prints a torque figure.** Taken literally, "fasten the pivot
clockwise" is the wrong direction for the left pedal of any bicycle-type crank.

## The manuals for the same chassis do print the warning

These are the CR800 and CU800 chassis with a touchscreen - the same crank arm item
numbers, the same pedal item numbers. The CR800 2021 and 2023, CU800 2021 and CU800
2012 manuals all print "the left pedal has a reverse thread and will be screwed in with
the opposite rotation" for those same parts. See
[that card](spirit-bike-assembly-pedal-tighten-as-much-as-possible.md).

**Nothing in the touchscreen manuals says the pedals changed.** The absence of the
warning is a change of wording, not evidence of a different pedal; the parts lists carry
the same left and right pedal items. Treat the left pedal as left-hand threaded and
check the L and R stamps on the pedal posts before forcing anything.

## The 900-series touchscreen bikes are not like this

The CR900ENT and CU900ENT manuals keep the full sentence - reverse thread,
counterclockwise, 300 in-lb - so this is not something all Spirit touchscreen bikes do.
See [that card](spirit-bike-assembly-pedal-torque-300-in-lb.md).
