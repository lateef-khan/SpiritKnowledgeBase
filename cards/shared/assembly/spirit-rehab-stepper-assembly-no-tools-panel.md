**None of these five books prints a TOOLS INCLUDED panel.** Every other Spirit climber
and stepper manual has one beside its parts list; these have a four-item PRE-ASSEMBLY
page whose third item is the only mention of tools in the whole book:

> 3. Locate the hardware package. **Remove the tools first.**

Which tools, and how many, is never stated. **The MS300 does not print even that
sentence**: the word "tool" does not occur anywhere in its 71 pages.

## How the absence was checked

A loose search for the word **tool** - not the phrase "tools included" - over each of
the five sources returns pre-assembly item 3 and nothing else, except one console
status-bar sentence in the 8.5S-Fit. The pre-assembly page itself was read at 300 dpi in
each book rather than from the extraction, because these manuals draw parts of a page as
a flat image. There is no panel on any of them.

## What the tools are, on the 7.0S, 7.5S and MS300

They are numbered rows in the parts list, mixed in among the machine's own parts:

| Item | Tool |
|---|---|
| 209 | 12/14mm Wrench |
| 210 | 13/14mm Wrench |
| 211 | 10mm Wrench |
| 212 | 5mm L Allen Wrench |
| 213 | 8mm L Allen Wrench |
| 214 | Phillips Head Screw Driver |

Six, one of each. The 7.5S adds **208, L Allen Wrench (6 x 27 x 120L)**. The MS300 lists
the same six under its own numbering - 208 L allen wrench m6, 209 Wrench 12/14mm, 210
Wrench 13/14mm, 211 Wrench 10mm, 212 L allen wrench m5, 213 L allen wrench m8 - and no
screwdriver row, though its step 1 drives twelve M5 Phillips screws a side.

## On the 8.5S and 8.5S-Fit the tools have numbers and no list at all

Their steps call for **6mm Allen Wrench (167)**, **2.5mm Allen Wrench (169)**, **Phillips
Screwdriver (170)**, **8mm Allen Wrench (611)**, **13/14mm Wrench (612)** and **5mm Allen
Wrench (613)**. Neither book has a parts list that names them; both end in an Exploded
View chapter instead. The numbers appear only as call-outs on the assembly drawings.

**Six tools are needed and none is promised.** Have a 2.5mm, 5mm, 6mm and 8mm Allen key,
a 13/14mm wrench and a Phillips screwdriver before you start.

## Neither levelling wrench is named as supplied either

The setting-up page calls for a **½" wrench** and the maintenance page for a **14mm
wrench**, and no panel says either is in the carton. See
[the levelling card](spirit-85s-assembly-levelling-glides-by-hand.md).

## The two MED books are the MS300's case again

Neither prints a TOOLS INCLUDED panel, and neither prints a PRE-ASSEMBLY page: **the word "tool"
does not occur anywhere in either book**, across 72 and 75 pages. They supply the same six numbered
rows as the 7.0S and 7.5S - 209 to 214 - and the MED 7.5S adds **208, L Allen Wrench (6 x 27 x
120L)**, exactly as the 2025 7.5S does. What is in each step's hardware panel is carded separately:
`spirit-rehab-stepper-assembly-hardware-panels-for-the-four-steps` and
`7-5s-med-assembly-hardware-panels-for-the-four-steps`.
