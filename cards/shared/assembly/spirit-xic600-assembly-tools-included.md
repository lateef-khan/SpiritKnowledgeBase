Two tools, printed as a TOOLS INCLUDED panel beside the unpacking steps:

- **14/15mm Wrench (#85)**
- **Combination Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (#86)**

Both printings list the same two. **No Allen key size is given for the combination
tool**, and no separate Allen wrench is supplied - the two tools between them cover
every step in the chapter.

## The maintenance chapter asks for a tool the carton does not hold

Levelling the bike needs a **14mm wrench or an adjustable wrench**, which the
14/15mm wrench (#85) covers. See
[the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).
