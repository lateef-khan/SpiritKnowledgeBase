Eight illustrated assembly steps, identical in the F65 2013 and F80 / F85 2013 books except for the lower-handlebar-cover item numbers below (hardware item numbers in brackets):

7. Bolt the support tube (15) between the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (151), set the Beverage Holder (150) on it, place the safety-key magnet (75) between Start and Stop, and check every bolt and screw is tight.
2. Fit six Speed Nut Clips (128) on the frame base, slide the lower handlebar covers onto the console masts (F65 books print 65L & 67R, F80 / F85 books print 65 & 67R), slide the Frame Base Caps (161L & 162R) over the upright tubes (4 & 5), and connect Lower Computer Cable (49) to Middle Computer Cable (50) on the right side. This card follows each book's own cover numbers.
3. Bolt the upright tubes (4 & 5) to the frame base (2) with eight Button Head Socket Bolts (130); leave them loose until step 7 is finished. Do not pinch the cables in the tubing.
4. Connect Speed Adjustment Switch Cable (54) to Speed Cable Upper (37), Incline Adjustment Switch Cable (55) to Incline Cable Upper (38), and Middle Computer Cable (50) to Computer Cable Upper (48); tuck excess cable into the handrail tubing.
5. Attach the Console Assembly (52) onto the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (133) and four Split Washers (113) using the M6 wrench; tighten completely.
6. Fix the handlebar covers over the upright tops with six Sheet Metal Screws (168).
7. Bolt the support tube (15) between the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (151), set the Beverage Holder (150) on it, place the safety-key magnet (75) between Start and Stop, and check every bolt and screw is tight.
8. Finish tightening the mast bolts left loose in step 2 and fix the console mast covers (161 & 162) to the frame base with six Tapping Screws (120).
