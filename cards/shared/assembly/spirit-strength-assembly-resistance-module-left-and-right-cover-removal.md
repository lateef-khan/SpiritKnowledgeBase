*(Tool: Phillips screwdriver)*

1. After removing the **3 screws (1)**, the left cover can be taken off.
2. After removing the **3 screws (2)**, the right cover can be taken off.

The last of the twelve disassembly steps. The manual prints no reassembly chapter; every step is reversed.

The "Digital Resistance Module" is the i-Strength line's motorised resistance box, part 72 in the CSI owner's manuals; the screw numbers are the call-outs on the manual's exploded drawings.
