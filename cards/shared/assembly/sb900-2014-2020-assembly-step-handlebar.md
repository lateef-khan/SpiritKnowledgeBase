Hardware: **#49** ø8 Split Washer (4 pcs), **#48** ø8 x 16mm Flat Washer (4 pcs), **#88** M8 × P1.25 × 20L Button Head Socket Bolt (4 pcs).

1. Install the handlebars with four Button Head Socket Bolts (88), Split Washers (49) and Flat Washers (48). Tighten the bolts securely.

The instruction names no tool.

**The step number is not the same on all four machines.** It is step **3** on the 2014, 2016 and 2019 machines and step **4** on the 2020 machine. The parts and the wording are identical.

**These are metric parts.** The SB700 of the same years uses the same three item numbers for imperial parts: #49 is a 5/16" split washer, #48 a 5/16" x 16mm flat washer and #88 a 5/16" x 5/8" screw. Do not swap them between models.

The 2021 machine keeps #88 but lengthens it to **M8 × P1.25 × 25L** and replaces #48 and #49 with #47 and #146.
