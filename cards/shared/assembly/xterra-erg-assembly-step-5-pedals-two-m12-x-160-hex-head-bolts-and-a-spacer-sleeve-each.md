Each pedal takes **two Hex Head Bolts (50, M12 x 160)** into the **Main Frame (1)** - four bolts and **two Pedal Bushings / Spacer Sleeves (64)** for the pair.

The ERG180 and ERG220 say where the sleeve goes: the **upper** bolt passes through the pedal, then through a sleeve (64), then into the upper hole of the main frame; the **second bolt goes straight into the lower hole** of the frame through the lower half of the pedal. Repeat for the other pedal. The ERG160 text only says "2 Hex Head Bolts (50) and 1 Pedal Bushing (64)" per pedal, which is the same fit.

Tool: the multi-function wrench (100) on the ERG160, the screwdriver-wrench (98) on the ERG220; the ERG180 page draws its callout "#65 1pc" beside this step, a stray label from the console screw. On the ERG220 this is **step 6** (its step 5 is the rail hinge).

The sleeve's diameter is printed both Ø18 and Ø16 across these books; the pack cards say which page prints which.
