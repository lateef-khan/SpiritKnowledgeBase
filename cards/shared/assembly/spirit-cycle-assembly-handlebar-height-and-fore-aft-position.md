## Height

1. Handlebar height is a matter of preference. **Start with the handlebar at the same
   height as the seat.** Higher gives a more upright position; lower gives a more
   crouched one.
2. Loosen the fastener on the handlebar post, slide the handlebar mount up or down, then
   tighten to secure the post. Note the final position mark on the post for future
   reference.

## Fore/aft

1. Loosen the fastener under the handlebar and slide the handlebar forward or back. A
   suitable position lets you grasp the handlebar comfortably with a **slight bend at
   the elbow**.
2. Tighten to secure the handlebar assembly.

## The fastener is not the same part on every machine

The CB900 and both XIC600 printings say **quick release**. The CIC800 and CIC850 say
**knob**. Nothing else differs - the five books print this procedure word for word.

**On the CIC850 this is the whole of the fitting chapter.** That book carries no seat
position procedure; see
[the absence card](../../cic850-2022/assembly/cic850-2022-assembly-no-seat-position-procedure.md).
