1. Use a **13/15 mm open-end wrench** to take the pedals off: turn the **left pedal (45) clockwise** and the **right pedal (46) counterclockwise**.
2. To refit the pedals, turn each one the opposite way.
3. Take the **crank arm end cap (96)** off and use a **plug wrench** to release the **nut (83)**.
4. Apply a **crank tool** to the crank to hold it and use an **Allen wrench** to release the crank.
5. To refit the crank (16L, 16R), use an **air crank tool or a rubber hammer** to seat the crank on the **axle (11)**, tighten the nut on the crank axle and plug the crank arm end cap (96) back in.

**The left pedal is left-hand threaded** - it comes off clockwise. The books say it only by direction; there is no torque figure for the pedals or the crank nut, and the nut (83) is not sized.

The four books print the steps and part numbers identically. The chain-cover procedure starts by taking the pedals and crank arms off, and ends by sending you back here ("follow step 4.3 to resume cranks and pedals").
