**The left pedal has a reverse thread and screws in counterclockwise. Tighten to 300
in-lbs (34 N-m) of torque.**

All six Spirit 900-series bike manuals print it, in the last assembly step, and the six
agree to the figure. The recumbent books write "300 in-lbs. (34 N-m)" and the upright
books "300 in-lb (34 Nm)"; the number is the same.

The recumbent fits **PEDALS (26L & 26R)** into **CRANK ARMS (22 & 23)**; the upright
fits **PEDALS (11)** into **CRANK ARMS (9 & 10)**. Neither says which tool, and neither
carton holds a torque wrench.

## The 800-series bikes print no figure at all

The CR800, CR800ENT, CU800 2012, CU800 2021 and CU800ENT manuals give no torque for the
pedals. Do not carry 300 in-lb across to them - see
[the CR800 and CU800 card](spirit-bike-assembly-pedal-tighten-as-much-as-possible.md)
and [the touchscreen card](spirit-bike-ent-assembly-pedal-no-thread-warning.md).

## This is the one assembly fact the recumbent and the upright share

Their assembly chapters otherwise overlap at only **35 to 43%** at word level - every
item number, every hardware table and every step differs. This sentence is word for
word the same on both.
