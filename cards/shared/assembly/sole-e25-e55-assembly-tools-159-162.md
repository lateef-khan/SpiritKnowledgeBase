Four tools are supplied. The part numbers differ by book while the set stays the same:

| AE25 / E55-2014 / WE25 | E25-2016 / E55-2016 | Tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #159 | #186 | 13/14mm Wrench (160mm) |
| #160 | #187 | Short Phillips Head Screwdriver |
| #161 | #188 | Phillips Head Screwdriver |
| #162 | #189 | 12/14mm Wrench (160mm) |

The steps call for two wrenches at once wherever a bolt-and-nut pair is tightened. (The WE books add "(1 pc)" to each line; the contents are the same.)
