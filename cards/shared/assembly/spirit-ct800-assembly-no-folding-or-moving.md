**None of the eight manuals has a setting-up chapter.** In each one the assembly
steps run straight into console operation. So:

- **Folding.** The word "fold" is not printed anywhere in any of the eight manuals.
  These are commercial-frame treadmills and no manual suggests otherwise.
- **Moving or transporting the machine.** No procedure in any of them. Transport
  wheels are named once per manual, inside the electrical warning that tells you to
  route the power cord away from moving parts including the elevation mechanism and
  transport wheels. The CT800 2020, the CT800ENT, the CT850 2016, the CT850 2020 and
  the CT850ENT parts lists carry four Transportation Wheels as item 24; the CT800
  2012 and 2016 and the **CT850 2018** manuals have no parts list at all.
- **Storage.** Not mentioned. The CT850ENT prints the word once, as the callout
  **Small Tray / Storage** on its console diagram - that is a tray on the console,
  not a way to store the machine.

## Levelling is documented on five of the eight, and nowhere in assembly

| Manual | What it says about levelling |
|---|---|
| CT800 2012 | Nothing. The words level, leveller and levelling pad do not appear. |
| CT800 2016 | Two pads on the rear stabiliser and a 14mm wrench, printed only as a remedy for persistent squeaks in general maintenance. Its own card. |
| CT800 2020 | Pads on the bottom of the feet, no count, no wrench size, in general maintenance and in the noise row of troubleshooting. Its own card. |
| CT800ENT | Nothing. Its safety page says to install the treadmill on a flat level surface; that is a statement about the floor, not an adjustment. |
| CT850 2016 | Pads on the bottom of the feet, no count, no wrench size. Same wording as the CT800 2020; on that card. |
| CT850 2018 | Two pads on the rear stabiliser and a 14mm wrench. Same wording as the CT800 2016; on that card. |
| CT850 2020 | Pads on the bottom of the feet, no count, no wrench size. Same wording as the CT800 2020; on that card. |
| CT850ENT | Nothing. Its safety page says to install the treadmill on a flat level surface; that is a statement about the floor, not an adjustment. |

**The wording does not follow the model year.** The CT850 2016 gives the vaguer
answer and the CT850 2018 gives the specific one, the reverse of the CT800 order.
Read the manual in hand.

**Absence is the answer for the CT800 2012, the CT800ENT and the CT850ENT, not a
gap to fill from a sibling.** Do not answer a levelling question about any of those
three from the cards for the others, and do not answer any of the eight from the XT,
CTSBS900 or 2026 commercial cards - those machines have levelling feet, glides and
folding decks that these do not.

The floor-clearance figures that the manuals print are on their safety pages, not in
the assembly chapter, and belong to the safety cards.
