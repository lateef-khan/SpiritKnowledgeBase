**Neither MT200 manual documents folding or moving the machine.** Absence is the
answer here, not a gap to fill from a sibling machine.

- **Folding or unfolding.** The word "fold" appears once in each manual and only
  as a part name, **179 Folding Assembly Bracket**, in the parts list. There is
  no folding instruction, no latch, no release lever and no storage position.
- **Moving or transporting the machine.** The parts lists hold **six transport
  wheels under two item numbers** - four of item 24, Ø82 x Ø14 x 35mm, and two of
  item 199, Ø54 x 25mm - and neither manual ever says to roll the machine on
  them. There is no lift-and-roll instruction and no tilt instruction.
- **Storage.** The only storage sentence in either manual is an environmental
  limit in the compliance pages: transport and store the device at -20 to +50 °C.

**Levelling is the one set-up job these manuals do document**, and it is on its
own card - four levelling glides on the rear incline unit, half-inch wrench.

Do not answer a folding or moving question for an MT200 from the XT treadmill
cards. Those machines fold onto four transport wheels that engage when folded,
or in the XT685's case are tilted at the rear roller; none of that is printed for
an MT200, and the MT200 is a floor-standing rehabilitation machine with parallel
bars and a rear step bolted to it.
