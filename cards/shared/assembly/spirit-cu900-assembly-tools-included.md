Four tools, printed as a TOOLS INCLUDED list beside the unpacking steps:

- **13/15mm Wrench**
- **Phillips Screwdriver**
- **5mm Allen Wrench**
- **6mm Allen Wrench**

All three printings list the same four. The 2025 manual moves the panel onto its own
PARTS INCLUDED page and heads it TOOLS; the words are unchanged.

## The recumbent carton holds one tool line more

The CR900, CR900ENT and CR900 2025 cartons hold these four **plus two 17mm wrenches**,
for the seat carriage and rear handlebar bolts an upright bike does not have. See
[the recumbent tools card](spirit-cr900-assembly-tools-included.md).

## No step names a tool, and no 14mm is in the box

Not one of the four assembly steps says which tool to use. The build drives M8 and M5
bolts and screws, four M8 nylock nuts through the handlebars, and 3.5mm self-tapping
screws.

**There is no 14mm wrench in this carton**, although the levelling sentence in the
maintenance chapter calls for one. Use an adjustable, as that sentence itself allows.
See [the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).
