This step needs no hardware from the assembly pack.

1. Install the Left (**35L**) and Right (**35R**) pedals to the Crank Arms (33 & 32) by using the **14/15mm Wrench (100)**. **The Left pedal has a reverse threaded bolt and needs to be tightened in a counterclockwise direction.**

**The step number differs between the two machines** - step 5 on the SB900 2023, step 6 on the SB1200 2023 - but the instruction is word for word the same.

**The pedals are 35L and 35R on these machines**, not 25L and 25R as on the SB900 2019-2022. The crank arms are 33 (left) and 32 (right).

Unlike the 2019-2022 wording, this instruction does not say that an R or L is stamped into the end of the bolt.
