1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **5 tapping screws** securing the motor cover, and remove
   the motor cover.
2. Disconnect all the lower controller wirings. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to remove the controller,
   fit the new lower controller and reconnect all wirings.

Five books print this, with three wordings and one set of figures:

- **XT185 2015** prints the two steps above.
- **XT285, XT385 and XT485 2015** call the screws "5 button tapping screws from the motor top Cover" and run
  the second step as one line, "Disconnect all lower controller wirings, replace with new lower controller and
  reconnect all wirings".
- **XT485ENT** prints one unnumbered line, "Disassemble the motor cover and Disconnect all lower controller
  wirings, replace with new lower controller and reconnect all wirings", and gives no screw count on this page.
  Its motor procedure two pages on removes the same cover with 5 tapping screws.

No socket names, no torque and no board part number are printed in the procedure, and none of the five books
says to calibrate afterwards in this procedure - each prints a calibration procedure in its error chapter.
