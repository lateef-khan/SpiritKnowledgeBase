This step needs no hardware from the assembly pack.

1. Install the Left (**25L**) and Right (**25R**) pedals to the crank arms. **The Left pedal has a reverse threaded bolt and needs to be tightened in a counterclockwise direction.** The pedals are identified by either an **R** or **L** stamped into the end of the bolt.

**The step number is not the same on all of these machines.** It is numbered step 4 on the 2019 machine and step 5 on the 2020, 2021 and 2022 machines. The instruction is word for word the same.

The 2023 machines number the pedals **35L and 35R**, not 25L and 25R, and call for the 14/15mm Wrench (100). This instruction names no tool.
