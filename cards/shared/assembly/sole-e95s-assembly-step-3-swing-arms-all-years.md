The 2015 and 2019 books call parts (10) and (11) Seat Handle Bars; the 2023 book calls the same parts Swing Arms. The 2023 book numbers the switch wire caps (97); the older books do not number them.

1. **Insert the left Seat Handle Bar (10) onto the left Lower Handle Bar (13)** and secure with **three Hex Head Bolts (138)**, **four Flat Washers (176)**, **two Curved Washers (187)** and **three Nyloc Nuts (165)** using **Wrench (194)** and **Wrench (195)**. Do the same for the right Seat Handle Bar (11) and right Lower Handle Bar (14).
2. **Connect the Handle Wire (Upper) Resistance (White, 61) and the Handle Wire (Upper) Stride (Red, 62)** to the two **Handle Wires (Lower) Resistance/Stride (63)**, save the excess wire in the mast tube, and **plug the Switch Wire Caps (97) onto the mast tube** to secure the wire.
