Both books print the same three sentences.

1. As shown in figure 6.1.1, remove the **Phillips head screws (M4 x 2 pcs)** from both sides of the cover.
2. As shown in figures 6.1.2 and 6.1.3, remove the **Phillips head screws (M5 x 4 pcs)** that secure the **console bracket**, and remove the wiring to replace the console.
3. Assemble the console in reverse order.

No screw lengths, no torque and no connector count are printed. The owner's manuals of both machines install the console on the articulating arm with **four M5 x 10 mm screws (153 on the 8.0U, 147 on the 8.5R)** - two at the top with the leather cover in between, two at the bottom without it - which is the bracket step 2 undoes. The 8.5UE ergometer's console comes off two bolts on a rear tube cover and four at the back; that is a different card.
