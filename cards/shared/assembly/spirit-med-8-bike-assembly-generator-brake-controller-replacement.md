As shown in figure 6.14.1 (8.5R: 6.8.1), **remove the Phillips head screws (M5 x 4 pcs)** to replace the generator/brake controller (the 8.5R heads it "Lower Control Replacement").

That is the whole procedure - no connector list, no cover step. The board is the **Lower Control Board (#091)** of the circuit-board chapter, fed 24 VDC by the **Power Supply Module (#081)** and putting out 12 VDC for the console; the parts list numbers it **Generator/Brake Controller (91)** and its mounting screws are among the **M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screws (122)**. The indicator lights D5 (power) and D13 (revolutions) on this board are what the no-power and no-revolutions troubleshooting reads. The covers have to be off first; the procedure does not say so.
