**The crank nut is tightened to 60 N-m.** That is the only torque either book prints for the cranks; the pedals themselves get 300 in-lb (34 N-m) in the owner's manuals, on a separate card. The 7.0R and 7.0U print no figure at all.

1. As shown in figures 6.3.1 and 6.3.2, remove the **pedal (R) by turning counterclockwise** and the **pedal (L) by turning clockwise**.
2. As shown in figure 6.3.3, remove the **nut** (the 8.5R book: "the nut and screw inside crank"). Same on both sides. **Use 60 N-m to tighten up** on refit.
3. As shown in figure 6.3.4, remove the crank arm with a **crank puller** (the 8.5R book: "crank extractor"). Same on both sides.
4. Refit the crank arms and pedals in reverse order.

The preventative-maintenance page of each book agrees on the thread: **tighten the left pedal counterclockwise and the right pedal clockwise**, and check both pedals and the crank nuts regularly for looseness. The parts lists carry the nut as **M10 x P1.25 x 10T (26), 2 pcs**, and the tool list names a **Crank Removal Tool** among the eighteen maintenance tools.
