**Loosen the knob on the adjustable crank and pull up to disengage the pin. Slide the pedal up or down the crank arm to the desired setting, then tighten the knob.** There is a numbered scale for repeatability, and a program in the Set Up function of the console that can assist in setting up the pedal position to accommodate various patient knee angles.

**Warning: avoid wearing pants with loose fitting legs as they may get caught on the crank arm while pedaling.**

Three books print the paragraph word for word: the 7.0R (2025 and the 2021 Dyaco MED edition) under "Pedal Adjustments" / "Pedal adjustment", and the 7.0U under a heading that reads **"Seat Adjustments"** - a misprint; the text is the pedal adjustment. The operating page of each book says the adjustable crank lets patients pedal "in smaller range of knee motion, from 15 degrees to full range". The console program that assists is the console section's card; the 8.0U and 8.5R list adjustable crank arms as an option and print no adjustment paragraph.
