These three machines add a step before the straps come off:

1. **Position the shipping carton so the Heavy End is located at the bottom.**
2. Cut the straps then lift the box over the unit and unpack. Remove all parts from the
   shipping carton and foam inserts and verify that all parts are included.
3. Locate the hardware package.

**They are the three tallest and heaviest machines in the range**, and the only three of
thirty-six whose unpacking list runs to three steps rather than two. On the other
thirty-three, cutting the straps is step 1.

The rest of the page is the same on all thirty-six: the bracketed numbers are
assembly-drawing item numbers, and damage done during assembly is not covered. See
[the unpacking card](spirit-strength-assembly-hardware-split-into-four-steps.md).
