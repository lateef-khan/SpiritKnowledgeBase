Hardware: **#65** 3/8" x 53L Carriage Bolt (2 pcs), **#77** 3/8" x 19mm x 1.5T Flat Washer (6 pcs), **#84** 3/8" x 25mm x 2T Flat Washer (2 pcs), **#176** 3/8" x 3/4" Hex Head Bolt (6 pcs), **#191** 3/8" x16 x 12.5T Cap Nut (2 pcs).

1. Install the Rear Stabilizer (7) onto the Main Frame (1) with the two Hex Head Bolts (65), two Flat Washers (84) and two Cap Nuts (191), using the **12/14m/m Wrench (112)**.
2. Install the Seat Back Bracket (5) onto the Seat Carriage (4) with the six Hex Head Bolts (**167**) and six Flat Washers (77), by using the **12/14m/m Wrench (112)**.
3. Insert an end cap (32) into each opening of the **Front Stabilizer Tube**. You may need to tap them in with a rubber mallet if they are tight.

**Two contradictions in this step, both left as printed.**

- **#65 is called a Carriage Bolt in the checklist and in the hardware box beside this step, but a Hex Head Bolt in the instruction.** The R92 2023, which prints an otherwise identical step, says Carriage Bolt in its instruction too.
- **The six 3/8" x 3/4" bolts are called (167) in the instruction but #176 in the checklist** and in the hardware box. There is no item 167 anywhere in the checklist.

The R92 2019 prints this step without any cap nuts in the instruction, even though its checklist supplies two #191 - see the R92 2019 card.
