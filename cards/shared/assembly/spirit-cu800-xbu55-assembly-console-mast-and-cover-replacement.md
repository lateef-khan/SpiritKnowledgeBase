1. Take the **computer cable (29)** and the **handlebar (3)** off (console first, then the handlebar).
2. Press the **chain cover (L) (36)** so that a seam opens between it and the **console mast cover (38)**, and separate the cover from the chain cover; do the same on the right side, then lift the console mast cover (38) off upward. The XBU55 books say the same thing as "slightly press the console mast cover to separate it from left and right chain covers".
3. Use a **12 mm wrench** to unscrew **7 pcs of 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts (51)** with **6 pcs of 5/16" x 19 mm x 1.5T flat washers (72)** and **one 5/16" x 20 curved washer (99)**, and pull the **console mast (2)** out.
4. To refit, guide the computer cable (29) through the console mast cover (38) and out of the console mast (2); seat the mast on the main frame (1) and tighten the same 7 bolts (51), 6 flat washers (72) and 1 curved washer (99) with the 12 mm wrench. Secure the console mast cover (38) onto the chain covers (36) and (37).
5. Refit the console (34) and the handlebar (3) last (the XBU55 books print this as a separate step 4).

**The washer dimensions are printed inconsistently for the same part numbers.** Flat washer (72) is 5/16" x 19 mm x 1.5T here and 5/16" x 18 mm x 1.5T in the handlebar procedure of the same CU800 book; the XBU55 books print 18 mm. Washer (99) is printed as a **split washer** in the CU800 removal step, a **curved washer** in its refit step, and a **5/16" x 19 x 1.5T curved washer** in the XBU55 books. The part numbers, counts and wrench are the same in all four books; the mismatched dimensions are printing variance.

**No bolt torque is printed.** The owner's manual assembly card for these machines is separate; this is the service-manual removal.
