Two panels, printed side by side on one page.

## Folding/Unfolding Procedures

1. **Hold the Handlebar (37)** to lift the rower slightly and **press the Release Lever
   (12)**.
2. **Lift and fold the Aluminum Track until it locks into place.**
3. To fold the **Rear Stabilizer**, turn **Knob (47) counterclockwise** until loosened.
   **Pull the Knob to release** and fold the Rear Stabilizer.
4. **To unfold the Rower, reverse steps.**

## Moving Procedures

1. Fold the aluminium rail assembly up.
2. **Lift the rear and roll away.**

## Two things fold, not one

The rail locks up on a **release lever by the handlebar**, and the **rear stabiliser
folds separately** on a knob. This is the only Spirit rower whose rear stabiliser
folds; the CRW800 folds the rail alone and its knob is the rail's, not the stabiliser's.
**Do not carry either machine's steps across to the other** - see
[the CRW800 card](../../crw800-2024/assembly/crw800-2024-assembly-folding-and-moving.md).

## This one does say how to unfold

Item 4 - reverse the steps - is the whole unfolding instruction, and it is more than the
CRW800 book gives. Nothing here says at what point the release lever re-engages, or
whether the knob must be retightened once the rear stabiliser is down.

## Nothing in the book labels a transport wheel

The moving procedure rolls the machine away on its front end, and neither the carton
panel nor the machine diagram names a wheel anywhere. The page was read from a 300 dpi
render as well as the extraction; the panels are text and the pictures beside them carry
no further labels.

## The page is not in the assembly chapter

It sits at p. 31, between the last program page and the heart rate chapter, twenty pages
after the build ends. Both printings put it in the same place.
