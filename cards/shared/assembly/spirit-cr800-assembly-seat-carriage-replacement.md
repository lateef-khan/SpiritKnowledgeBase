1. Take the **aluminium axle end cap (135)** off.
2. Use two Phillips head screwdrivers to release **two M6 x 15 mm Phillips head screws (98)**, take the **seat stop axle (11)** off, and pull the **seat front/aft adjustment lever (167)** upward to take the **seat carriage (4)** off.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **4 pcs of M6 x 10L flat Phillips head screws (161)**, **4 pcs of Ø7 x Ø15 x 1.5T flat washers (162)** and **4 pcs of sleeves (163)**. Then use a **13 mm wrench** to release **4 pcs of M8 x 7T nyloc nuts (88)** and **4 pcs of Ø8 x Ø18 x 3T knurled lock washers (79)**, and take the **seat wheel adjustment plates (9L, 9R)** off.
4. Use an **M4 Allen wrench** and a **10 mm open-end wrench** to release the **M5 x 45 mm socket head cap bolt (171)** and the **M5 x 5T nyloc nut (173)**.
5. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to release **two M5 x 25 mm flat head socket screws (169)**, then pull out the **lever anchor (168)** to take the seat front/aft adjustment lever (167) apart.
6. Use an **M5 Allen wrench** and an **11 mm wrench** to release the **M6 x 38 mm socket head cap bolt (93)**, the **1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washer (72)** and the **M6 nyloc nut (129)**, and take the **seat position latch (12)** off.
7. To refit the lever (167): join it to the lever anchor (168) with the two M5 x 25 mm flat head socket screws (169); place the **spring (104)** in the seat position latch (12) and secure with the M5 x 45 mm socket head cap bolt (171) passing, from top to bottom, through the seat position latch (12), the spring (104), the **Ø15 x 6 x 4T nylon washer (170)**, the lever (167) and the **Ø3/16" x 10 x 1T flat washer (172)**, then tighten with the M5 x 5T nyloc nut (173).
8. Install the completed seat carriage (4) onto the **aluminium track (14)** and set the seat wheel adjustment plates (9L, 9R) with the 11 and 13 mm wrenches: at the **front left**, turn the 11 mm wrench **counterclockwise** first, then tighten the M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88) with the 13 mm wrench; at the **front right**, turn the 11 mm wrench **clockwise** and tighten the M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88). At the rear, turn the hex bolt **counterclockwise on the right** and **clockwise on the left** to secure.
9. Test the smoothness; if it is not acceptable, turn the four hex bolts back and re-tighten until it is. Fit the four sleeves (163) on the hex bolts and secure with the four Ø7 x Ø15 x 1.5T flat washers (162) and the four M6 x 10L flat Phillips head screws (161). Refit the seat stop axle (11) and the aluminium axle end cap (135).

**Two parts are numbered differently from the 2009 XR898 and XBR books**, which start this procedure by taking the rear shrouds off (no axle end cap), call the sleeves (162) and use **1/4" x 16 x 1.0T flat washers** under the M6 x 10L screws instead of Ø7 x Ø15 x 1.5T (162). The lever, latch, spring and wheel-setting steps are the same; that version is the linked card.

The wheel-setting direction in step 8 is the same one-line rule the 2008 XBR dealer manual prints for a loose seat carriage (press down on the 11 mm wrench, tighten the 13 mm nyloc nut, all four wheel assemblies) - the linked "tighten seat carriage" card.
