Hardware: **#68** 5/16" x 5/8" Hex Head Bolt (8 pcs), **#76** 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T Flat Washer (6 pcs), **#82** 5/16" x 1.5T Split Washer (2 pcs), **#83** 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T Curved Washer (2 pcs).

1. Install the Console Mast Cover (31) onto the Console Mast (2). Make sure the plastic cover is in the correct orientation.
2. Feed the twist tie that is attached to the Computer Cable (44) and Hand pulse Cable (45) through the bottom of the Console Mast Tube (2) and out through the opening at the top.
3. Install the Console Mast Tube (2) into the receiving tube of the Main Frame (1) with **six** Hex Head Bolts (68), **four Flat Washers (76) on the sides** of the tube and **two Curved Washers (83) on the front** of the tube, by using the **12/14mm Wrench (112)**. Pull gently upwards on the cable ends to prevent pinching the cables, because damage to the electronics could occur.
4. **Remove the white Styrofoam pad** - factory installed to prevent bolts from being accidentally dropped into the bike.
5. Remove the plastic tie from the centre of the handlebar (3). Install the Mast Handle bar Assembly (3) onto the Console Mast (2) with **two** Hex Head Bolts (68), two Flat Washers (76) and **two Split Washers (82)**, using the **12/14mm Wrench (112)**.

Eight #68 bolts in the bag: six for the mast, two for the handlebar. Six #76 washers: four on the mast sides, two on the handlebar.

**The R92 2019 prints this step word for word** and has its own card. The R92 2023 drops the Styrofoam pad instruction altogether.
