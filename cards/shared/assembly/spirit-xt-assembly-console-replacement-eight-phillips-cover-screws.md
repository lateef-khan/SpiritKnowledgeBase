Seven books print the same three steps with the same counts.

1. Use a **Phillips head screwdriver** to loosen the **8 Sheet Metal Screws**, then remove the cover. The 2015
   books call it the **console mast cover**; the 2023 books call it the **Upright cover**.
2. Use an **L Allen wrench** to remove the **4 bolts** that hold the console. The 2015 books say "4 button
   head socket bolts from the console support" and do not size the key; the 2023 books say "an M6 L-Allen
   wrench to remove 4 bolts which securing the console on the uprights".
3. Disconnect all the control wires, then replace the console assembly.

Two things the books do not agree on, neither of them a count: the Allen key is sized **M6** only in the 2023
books, and the XT285 2015 book prints a line of Chinese before its English step 3.

Not every XT takes the console off this way. The XT185 2015 book uses an M5 Allen key on the eight cover
screws, the XT185 and XT285 2023 books have no cover and take six screws straight out of the uprights, and the
XT485ENT takes off left and right handlebar covers first - each has its own card.
