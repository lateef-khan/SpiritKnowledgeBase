| Item | Tool |
|---|---|
| #85 | Wrench |
| #86 | Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver |

**#85 is printed simply as "Wrench" in these two manuals - no size.** From the SB700 2019 manual onward the same #85 is labelled "14/15m/m Wrench". Do not quote a size for a 2011-2016 machine from the later manuals.

**Tool item numbers are not shared between models.** On the SB900 2023 and SB1200 2023 the wrench is #100, not #85.
