Three lines on the page: **Handle**, **Dumbbell Tray**, **Weight Plate x 8**. Nothing is bolted; the dumbbell is used as it comes out of the box once the handle is set in the tray.

The 55 lb parts list weighs each plate at 2.5 kg; the 25 lb book prints no plate weight. The printed increments (five settings each) are on the weight-selection card and the specs card.

**Ghost text on this page.** The text layer of both books also carries the strings "2.5 12.5", "5 7.5 10 12.5" and "27 3.4 4.5 5.57" - the 12.5 lb model's dial figures, left in the file by the shared template. **They are not printed on the ADB25 or ADB55 page** (read from the render); the only figures on the page are the three part names and "X 8".
