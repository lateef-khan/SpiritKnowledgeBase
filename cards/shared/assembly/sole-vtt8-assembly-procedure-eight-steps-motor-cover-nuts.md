Eight assembly steps, identical in the VTT8 2008 and WTT8 2009 books (hardware item numbers in brackets). Neither book prints a Velcro-strap warning.

1. Remove the treadmill from the carton onto a hard, level surface.
2. Connect the Computer Cable (32) to the Computer Cable (33) from the right upright tube.
3. Fit the left and right upright tubes (5, 4) into the main frame (1), taking care not to pinch or cut the right-upright wire harness, hand-tight only until the console and hand pulse bar are on: six 5/16 x 1/2 Button Head Bolts (100), plus a Star Washer (103) on each of the two 3/8 x 2 Button Head Socket Bolts (101) in the top holes.
4. Connect Speed Switch Cable (37) to upper Speed Cable (34), Incline Switch Cable (38) to upper Incline Cable (35), and Computer Cable (33) to upper Computer Cable (31).
5. Fix the Console Assembly (36) into the top of the uprights with four 3/8 x 1-3/4 Button Head Socket Bolts (125) and four 3/8 Split Washers (85).
6. Bolt the handrail support (3) between the uprights with four 5/16 x 3/4 Button Head Socket Bolts (99) and set the Beverage Holder (48) on the support.
7. Press four Speed Nuts (84) onto the motor cover (43); fix the motor base caps (39, 40) with six 5 x 16 mm Tapping Screws (92) and two 5 x 16 mm Self-Tapping Screws (87).
8. Fix the left (117, 118) and right (119, 120) handgrip side caps on top of the uprights with eight M5 x 10 mm Phillips Head Screws (126) and four 3.5 x 12 mm Self Tapping Screws (89).

Tighten all bolts after all components are assembled.
