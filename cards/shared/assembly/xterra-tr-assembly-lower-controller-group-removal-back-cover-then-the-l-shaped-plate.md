1. Remove the screws fixing the **back cover** and remove the back cover.
2. Remove the screws fixing the **L-shaped plate** of the controller group and take out the controller
   group.

That is the whole of chapter 4 in both books. "Controller group" is the driver board and brake board
that chapters 8 and 9 map out; the books do not say to unplug the mains first, count the screws, or
describe unplugging the wires from the boards. On the Dyaco-built Xterras the controller sits under
the motor cover at the front instead.
