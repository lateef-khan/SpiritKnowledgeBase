The whole chapter is one instruction under the heading TRANSPORTATION INSTRUCTIONS:

**Carefully lift the treadmill at the rear roller area, grasping the two side end caps, and roll the
treadmill away.**

The chapter is titled "Treadmill Folding/Unfolding and Transport" in both books, but **no folding or
unfolding text is printed** - the CT900 and CT900ENT do not fold, and the chapter title is the Dyaco template's.
The page carries a drawing of a person holding the rear of the deck up with the machine tilted forward onto
its front end; the wheels are not named or counted in the text.

The CT900ENT owner's manual prints nothing about moving the machine; this is the only printed instruction
for either machine.
