**The new motor is set to 268.5 mm by running it under power before it is fitted** - not by turning it
by hand to a figure, as the Dyaco books do.

1. Place the new incline motor on the side of the machine and connect it to the power and signal
   cables of the original incline motor.
2. Press **START** on the upper controller to start the treadmill.
3. Press **Incline UP** to the **LEVEL 2** position.
4. Wait until the incline motor stops rotating, then press **Incline DOWN** to the **LEVEL 0** position.
5. Wait until the motor stops rotating, then **turn the telescopic tube** to adjust the assembly size.
   Measure it before installation: **it should be 268.5 mm**, as in the figure (the drawing dimensions
   268.5 between the two mounting holes).
6. Remove the old incline motor and install the new one.

The E6 troubleshooting in the same books repeats the figure as "268.5mm between the upper and lower
holes" for a motor whose VR value is out of range. The Dyaco-built Xterras set 165 or 195 mm and are
adjusted unpowered.
