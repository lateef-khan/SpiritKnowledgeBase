1. **Slide the Rear Rail Assembly (2) under the rear tube of the main body.** Put the **two 3/8" x 2-1/4" Bolts (137)** and **3/8" Flat Washers (176)** through the top of the main body tube and hand-tighten into the Rear Rail Assembly (2); fully tighten after the next step.
2. **Install the four 3/8" x 3-3/4" Bolts (139) and 3/8" Curved Washers (187)** through the front of the main body tube. You may need to tighten or loosen the two top bolts to align the holes. When all bolts are threaded, tighten with the Allen wrench.
3. **Run the Computer Cable (68) from the main body through the Console Mast tube (12)** using the pre-installed long tie-wrap: tie the cable end to the tie-wrap at the bottom of the tube and pull it through with the other end. **Bolt the console mast tube onto the main body** with the **four 3/8" x 3/4" Bolts (145)**, **Split Washers (186)** and **Flat Washers (176)**.
4. **Untie the Computer Cable (68) and connect it to the console** along with the **two Hand Pulse cables (58)** and the **Resistance and Stride switch cables (61/62)**. Place the Console on top of the Mast and tighten the **four M5 Phillips Head Screws (150)** with driver (192).
