**The 3/8 inch bolt in step 2 is loosened, not removed.** All three books print the warning; the covers come off
around it.

1. Remove the **Ø5 x 16L tapping screws (111), 2 pcs**, from the **Chain Cover (L) (71)** with a Phillips
   screwdriver, and take out the **3.5 x 12L sheet-metal screws (112), 9 pcs**, from the **Chain Cover (R)
   (72)**. The Chain Cover (L) (71) then comes off completely.
2. Loosen the **3/8" x UNC16 x 3-3/4" socket head cap bolt (83)** with the **8 mm L Allen wrench**, pushing the
   **3/8" x 11T nyloc nut**. *"Please do not take out the screw."*
3. Take out the other **Ø5 x 16L tapping screws (111), 2 pcs**, with the Phillips screwdriver; the Chain Cover (R)
   can then be removed.
4. Take the **galvanized iron net (L) and (R)** out of the Chain Cover (L) and (R) and straighten it out of the hole.
   A single chain cover and its net can be taken out on its own.
5. Reverse the steps to refit Chain Cover (L) (71) and Chain Cover (R) (72). **The chain cover foam must aim at
   the hole.**

**Where the three books differ.**

- The **CW800-YR001 (crw800-2016) and DW400-YR002 (xrw600-2019) books** add, at the start of step 3, *"remove
  the nut of the DC power cord with the 15 mm wrench"* - those two machines are adapter-powered and the DC socket
  sits in the right chain cover. The **CRW800 (2020) book (crw800-2021)** is the generator-powered machine and
  prints no DC-cord nut.
- The **XRW600 book** adds to step 4: *"remove the 3.5 x 12 mm sheet-metal screws (112), 22 pcs"* to free the
  net. The two CRW800 books give no screw count for the net.

The same procedure, with the DC-cord nut, is printed for the Sole SR500 (2016), which is the same Dyaco chassis
under another brand.
