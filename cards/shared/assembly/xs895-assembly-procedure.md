**The book has two steps and its own pre-assembly page says four.** Unpacking item 3
reads *"Locate the hardware package. The hardware is separated into four steps."* There
is no step three and no step four: STEP TWO is followed by XS895 CONSOLE OPERATION, and
there are two hardware tables, headed HARDWARE FOR STEP 1 and HARDWARE FOR STEP 2.

Quantities are on
[the hardware card](xs895-assembly-hardware-by-step.md). Numbers in parentheses are
assembly-drawing item numbers.

## Before you start

1. Cut the banding straps with a razor knife, separate the staples under the bottom edge
   of the carton and lift the box over the unit.
2. Remove all parts and inspect for damage or missing parts.
3. Take the tools out of the hardware package first, then take the hardware for each
   step as you need it.

## Step one - rear stabiliser, console mast and console

1. Gather HARDWARE FOR STEP 1.
2. Attach the **Rear Stabilizer with Handle (13)** to the mounting plate on the main
   frame with **2 hex head bolts (106), 2 split washers (144) and curved washers (142)**,
   using the **13/14mm wrench (149)**.
3. Pull the tie on the computer cable (2) so **computer cable (46)** runs up through the
   mast and out of the top. Secure the console mast to the main frame with **4 socket
   head cap bolts (110), 4 split washers (144) and 4 flat washers (136)**, using the
   **L Allen wrench (152)**. **DO NOT PINCH CABLE, AS THIS MAY CAUSE MALFUNCTIONS.**
4. Take the tie off computer cable (46) and plug it into the **Console Assembly (45)**
   together with the two hand pulse cables (51, 52) and the resistance/incline
   **white and red** cables (61, 62).
5. Place the console on the mounting plate of the console mast and secure with **4
   Phillips head screws (113)**, using the screwdriver (151).

*Item 5 is printed at the top right of the page, above items 1 to 4. It is still the
last thing you do in step one.*

## Step two - covers, upper handlebars and the bottle holder

1. Gather HARDWARE FOR STEP 2.
2. Match the **left (83) and right (84) console mast covers** over the left and right
   chain covers and around the console mast, and secure with **1 sheet metal screw (117)
   and 2 Phillips head screws (113)**.
3. Attach the **Upper Handle Bar (L) (24)** to the mounting plates on the **Left Lower
   Handle Bar (17)** with **3 hex head bolts (102), 2 curved washers (143) and 3 nyloc
   nuts (124)**, using the 12/14mm wrench (150) and the 13/14mm wrench (149). Repeat with
   the same quantities for **Upper Handle Bar (R) (25)** on the **Right Lower Handle Bar
   (18)**.
4. Install the **Left Handle Bar Cover (90)** with **2 Phillips head screws (113)**, and
   the **Right Handle Bar Cover (91)** the same way.
5. Install the **Drink Bottle Holder (157)** on the console mast with **2 Phillips head
   screws (113)**.

**Three bolts and two washers per handlebar.** Item 3 fits three hex head bolts and only
two curved washers on each side, and the hardware table agrees - 6 bolts, 4 washers. One
bolt a side goes in bare. That is what the manual prints on both printings.

## Both printings are the same build

The 2018 and 2021 books print this chapter word for word, compared from the two PDFs'
own text. Nothing about the assembly changed between them; what changed elsewhere is the
warranty registration URL and the electrical page's layout.

## What the build never does

No step fits the **four levellers** or the **two transport wheels** the carton panel
lists, no step fits the swing arms, foot pedals or main shroud, and there is no
first-power-on step - STEP TWO is followed straight by the console chapter. The book
prints no way to move the machine.
