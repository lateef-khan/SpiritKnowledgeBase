The 2016 manual prints these as tables headed HARDWARE FOR STEP n. The 2012
manual prints the same items and the same counts as captioned photographs under
the heading HARDWARE. **The values are identical in both.**

## Hardware for step 1

| Part | Type | Description | QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 58 | Split Washer | 3/8" | 6 |
| 59 | Flat Washer | 3/8" | 6 |
| 73 | Tapping Screw | M5 x 12mm | 4 |
| 104 | Bolt | 3/8" x 3-3/4" | 6 |

The 2012 caption calls 104 a **3/8" x 3-3/4" Stainless Steel Bolt** and 73 a
**M5 x 12mm Self Tapping Screw**.

## Hardware for step 2

| Part | Type | Description | QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 73 | Screw | M5 x 12mm | 1 |
| 110 | Screw | M5 x 10mm | 2 |
| 112 | Screw | M5 x 30mm | 2 |
| 125 | Star Washer | M5 | 1 |

The 2012 captions call 110 and 112 **Machine Screws** and 73 a **Sheet Metal
Screw**.

## Hardware for step 3

| Part | Type | Description | QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 58 | Split Washer | 3/8" | 6 |
| 59 | Flat Washer | 3/8" | 6 |
| 105 | Bolt | 3/8" x 3/4" | 6 |
| 108 | Bolt | M8 x 12mm | 6 |

The 2012 captions call 105 a **Button Head Socket Bolt** and 108 a **Socket Cap
Bolt**.

## Item 73 is given two names in the same manual

Step 1 calls it a **tapping screw** and step 2 calls it a plain **screw** or, in
the 2012 manual, a **self tapping screw** in step 1 and a **sheet metal screw** in
step 2. Both manuals print both names, both times at M5 x 12mm. It is one item
with two descriptions, five of them in the kit: four for the lower side caps and
one for the ground cable.

Items 58 and 59 are each listed at six in step 1 and six again in step 3, so the
carton holds twelve of each across the build. The steps use all of them.
