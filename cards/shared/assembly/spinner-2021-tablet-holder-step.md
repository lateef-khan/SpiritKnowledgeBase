Hardware: **#101** 4 x 16mm Sheet Metal Screw (3 pcs).

1. Install the Tablet Holder (138) on the Handlebar (6) with **3 pcs of Sheet Metal Screws (101)**, by using the **Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (86)**.

**No tapping screws in this step for 2021.** The 2019 and 2020 machines carry two #93 M5 x 12L tapping screws in the same hardware group; on these machines the drink bottle holders moved into the handlebar step and are fixed with #143 button head socket bolts and #144 nylon nuts instead, and #93 is gone from the pack.

**The step number differs between the two manuals** - step 5 on the SB700 2021, step 6 on the SB900 2021 - and the SB700 2021 checklist calls the same hardware group step 6 while its own instruction page calls it step 5.
