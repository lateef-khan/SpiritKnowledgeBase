Hardware: **#50** 3/8" x 2" Hex Head Bolt (4 pcs), **#71** 3/8" x 25 x 2T Flat Washer (4 pcs).

1. Attach the Rear Stabilizer (5) onto the Main Frame (1) with the **four** Hex Head Bolts (50) and **four** Flat Washers (71). Tighten using the **12/14mm Wrench (100)**.

**Four bolts, not two.** From 2013 onward this step uses only two #50 bolts, adds two #58 Phillips screws for the rear stabilizer cover, and routes the bolts through a Rear Stabilizer Attachment Plate (131) that the 2011 machine does not have. The 2011 step also fits no cover.
