Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #82 | 3/8" | Nut | 4 pcs |
| #81 | 3/8" x 3-1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #83 | 3/8" x 23mm | Curved Washer | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #91 | M5 x 10mm | Screw | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 4**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #49 | 5/16" | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| #48 | 5/16" x 16mm | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| #88 | 5/16" x 5/8" | Screw | 4 pcs |

**The checklist runs 1, 2, 4 - there is no hardware step 3 and no hardware step 5.** That is not a printing fault. The manual's assembly steps are 1 stabilizer tubes, 2 rear stabilizer cover, 3 console, 4 handlebar, 5 left/right pedals, and steps 3 and 5 use no hardware out of the assembly pack.

**Item numbers are not shared between models.** The 2023 machines renumbered the whole pack - there `#93` is a button head socket bolt, while on the 2019-2021 machines `#93` is an M5 tapping screw. Always use the checklist printed in your own machine's manual.
