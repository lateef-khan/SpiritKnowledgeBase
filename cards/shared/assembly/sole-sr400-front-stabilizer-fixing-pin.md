1. Fit the front stabilizer (B) to the main frame (A) with screw (J1).
2. Fasten the nut with the wrench (14mm), then fit and fasten the fixing pin (I).

The fixing pin is the pin the rail pivots and locks on: unfolding the machine later means pulling this same pin, and folding it means pulling it again.
