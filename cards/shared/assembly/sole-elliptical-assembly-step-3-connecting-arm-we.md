**Untie the wire holding the Sleeve Spacer (24)** in place on the rod-end bearings at the ends of the **Connecting Arms (8 & 9)**. Align the rod-end holes with the holes in the brackets of the left and right **Handle Bars (10 & 11)**, rod ends inside the brackets. Secure both sides with a **5/16" x 1-1/4" Hex Head Bolt (134)**, **5/16" x 20mm Flat Washer (170)** and **5/16" Nylon Nut (159)** using the **13/14 mm Wrench (184)** and **12/14 mm Wrench (187)**. The AE35-2011 book prints this step word for word with the same numbers; see that card.
