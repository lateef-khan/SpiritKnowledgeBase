The pedal strap is adjustable to the user's foot size. Three moves:

1. **Remove the Velcro end** of the strap from the mesh side by pulling it upward and then to the side (the magnetic-rower books say "then to the left"; the water-rower books "and over to the side").
2. With it free, **increase the opening** by pulling the mesh end up and to the side ("to the right" in the magnetic-rower books).
3. **To tighten**, pull the Velcro end upward, then over (right) and down to press it onto the mesh side.

The ERG650W book says "hook and loop end" for the same Velcro tab. The ERG750W's buckle strap and the ERG800W's seven-position pedal are different mechanisms (linked cards).
