Two combination wrenches (68) ship because the swing-arm axle bolt is tightened with one in each end. The nine cap bolts (102) are five for the console mast and four for the stabilizers; the four button head bolts (43) are for the handles. The 2018 book for the same family adds two M5 screws at step 4 for its rear covers (the linked card).
