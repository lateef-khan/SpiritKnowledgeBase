Hardware step 3: **2 D type washers (#36; elliptical #32), 2 washers 32*10 (#35; #31), 2 spring washers (#34; #30), 2 hex head screws M10*20 (#33; #29), 2 PVC caps (#32)**.

1. Attach the left bottom handlebar (14; elliptical 21L) to the left side axle of the front post (13; 22). Secure using one D type washer, one washer, one spring washer and one hex head screw. Attach the PVC cap onto the hex head screw.
2. Repeat for the right bottom handlebar (15; 21R) to the right side axle of the front post.

The D-type washer goes on first, against the axle's flat, then the large washer, the spring washer and the screw.

The 2016 elliptical adds two covers in the same step, on screws pre-installed on the covers: the cover for cross bar (47) onto each bottom handlebar with two self-tapping screws (33), and the cover for dual handle bar to the bottom of each with one self-tapping screw (53).
