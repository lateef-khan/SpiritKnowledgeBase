**Seven Ø3.5 x 12 and two 5 x 16 screws on the left shroud.** The XR329 2016 and ENT books print five and three for the same cover; that is the linked 2016 card.

1. Take the crank arms and pedals off.
2. Remove the **round disk (34)** by hand. Its latches are different sizes, so note its direction: the disk is marked **"FRONT"** with an arrow that faces forward when installed.
3. Take the console mast cover off.
4. To release the **left front shroud (29)**, use a Phillips head screwdriver on **7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 12 self-tapping screws (103)** and **2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws** (printed "(103)" - the 5 x 16 tapping screw is (101) everywhere else).
5. To release the **right front shroud (30)**: on the mainframe, unscrew **2 pcs of Ø3.5 x 20 self-tapping screws (107)** with **2 pcs of Ø5 x 16 x 1.5T flat washers (78)**; then, on the right shroud, unscrew 2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws and unplug it.
6. To refit, secure the right shroud to the main frame with a **Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet-metal screw (103)** and a **3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T flat washer (78)** lightly; join the left shroud (29) and right shroud (30) and lock them with **7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet-metal screws (103)**; then adjust the **crank axle (8)** and the **bottom cover** to their relative position and fix with the **5 x 16 mm tapping screws (101)**.

**Screw (103) is printed as Ø3.5 x 12 on removal and Ø3.5 x 16 on refit**, in all three books. Seven of them either way.

The 2020 CR800 books print the same fasteners with an extra step about trapping the on/off switch between the shroud halves, and no round disk; the 2009 XR898 has neither the switch nor the disk.
