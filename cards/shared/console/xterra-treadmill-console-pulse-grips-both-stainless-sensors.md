The Pulse (heart rate) readout displays your current heart rate in beats per minute during the workout. You must use **both** stainless steel sensors — on the side hand rails (TR300, TR6.4) or on the handlebar (TRX2500, TRX3500, TRX4500) — to display your pulse. The pulse value displays any time the upper display is receiving a pulse signal.

These five books name no chest strap in this section; the TRX4500 book does add a "Wearing the Chest Strap" page later ([strap card](xterra-treadmill-console-wearing-the-chest-strap-and-its-2500-hour-battery.md)). The service manuals for the TRX machines say the pulse reading comes "by using hand pulse or receiver" and that the display shows "P" after 8 seconds without a signal ([TRX2500 ranges card](../../trx2500-2024/console/display-modes-and-window-ranges-per-the-service-manual.md), [TRX3500/TRX4500 ranges card](xterra-trx-console-display-modes-and-window-ranges-per-the-service-manual.md)). The safety pages of the same books say the pulse sensors are not medical devices; the Safety section holds that.
