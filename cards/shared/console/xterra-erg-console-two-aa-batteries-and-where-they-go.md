| Book | Battery line | Where the case is |
|---|---|---|
| ERG160 | BATTERY TYPE: **2 AA or UM 3** | not stated |
| ERG180 | Battery Type: **2 pcs of Size AA** | not stated |
| ERG220 | "Please install **2 AA batteries** in the battery case on the **back of the Console** (95)" | the console pops out of the main frame on a hinge |

**ERG220 access.** Assembly step 7 (Console Connection): to insert or remove batteries the console (95) can be removed from the main frame - pop it out via the hinge, taking care not to damage the connection wires; unclip the wires and the console can be moved around freely. Insert the 2 AA batteries to activate the console. **Whenever the batteries are removed, all values reset to zero.** "If the console displays abnormally, please re-install the batteries and try again. Please properly dispose of your batteries after use."

None of the three books says whether the cells are in the box. On the ERG160 and ERG180 a **battery symbol lights on the display when the cells are low** ([auto-off card](xterra-erg-console-wakes-on-rowing-or-mode-shows-stop-after-4-seconds-and-shuts-off-after-2-minutes.md)); the ERG220 prints no low-battery symbol. Its troubleshooting table's first row, "Console does not turn on - Battery not installed - Install battery", is an errors card. The water rowers' consoles also take two AA, included ([water-rower battery card](xterra-ergw-console-two-aa-batteries-included-and-changing-them-resets-every-value.md)); the ERG500 takes two AAA ([ERG500 card](../../erg500-2018/console/two-aaa-batteries-with-two-different-access-procedures-printed.md)); the Xterra bike consoles take two AAA ([bike card](xterra-console-two-aaa-um4-batteries-and-a-faint-display.md)).
