1. Press the **ENTER** key and the **DISPLAY** key **at the same time**.
2. **Hold them down for 5 seconds.** The Message Window displays **`BLUETOOTH`**.
3. Press the **Level +/-** keys to choose **ON** or **OFF**.
4. **Press STOP or ENTER to exit.**

That is the whole paragraph as printed, in both the 2018 and the 2021 book.

**There is no DISPLAY key on this console.** Read at 300 dpi, the console drawing on printed p. 14
labels a bottom key row of **FAN, START, LEVEL - / +, STOP, ENTER**, an INCLINE rocker on the left
handgrip, a LEVEL rocker on the right, and program keys **FAT BURN, CARDIO, STRENGTH, INTERVAL,
USER-1, USER-2, HR**. **No key on the drawing is called Display**, and the word appears nowhere else
in either manual. The console's scan function is on **Enter**
(`xs895-console-message-center-scans-fpm-floors-laps-and-segment-time`), and **SCAN** is an indicator
printed on the LCD, not a key.

**So the second key in this gesture is unidentified.** Do not tell an owner where to press until the
machine in front of them has been looked at; the paragraph is inherited from a console family whose
layout this one does not share.

**The manual gives no default.** It never says whether Bluetooth ships on or off, and this toggle is
the only Bluetooth control the book describes - pairing with a phone is the separate Spirit Fit App
procedure (`xt-2015-console-spirit-fit-app-pairing`).

**This is not the CVC800's Bluetooth setting.** That vertical climber holds **Enter for 2 seconds**
in idle mode and chooses between **APP** and **BLECHESTSTRAP** - a different gesture, a different
menu and a different machine (`cvc800-bluetooth-app-or-chest-strap`).
