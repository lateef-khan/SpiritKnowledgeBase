The console has one **FUNCTIONAL BUTTON, MODE**: press it to show a different function; **hold it down for 6 seconds to reset all data**.

| Function | As printed |
|---|---|
| SCAN | Press MODE until the marker sits at SCAN; the monitor rotates through speed, distance, time, calorie, ODO and pulse, holding each **4 seconds** |
| TIME | Elapsed workout time up to 99:59 |
| SPEED | Current speed, maximum 999.9 M/H |
| DISTANCE | Distance from exercise start to end, maximum 99.99 MILE |
| CALORIE | Total calories from start to end, maximum 999.9 |
| ODOMETER | Total accumulated distance, maximum 999.9 MILE |
| PULSE RATE | Press MODE until the marker sits at PULSE, then hold both contact pads ([pulse card](xterra-console-hand-pulse-reads-after-6-to-7-seconds-and-runs-high-at-first.md)) |

Four notes follow: a faint or blank display means replace the batteries (the book names no battery type); the monitor shuts off after 4 minutes without a signal; it powers on when you start pedalling or press the button; it starts counting when you exercise and stops after you stop for 4 seconds ([auto-off card](xterra-console-battery-console-wakes-on-pedalling-and-sleeps-after-4-minutes.md)).

The two books print the console pages word for word; the FB350 book's footers still read "FB150 Folding Bike". Neither book gives a SET or RESET key - those are the FB160 and FB360 console ([card](xterra-fb-console-mode-set-and-reset-keys-with-targets-and-speed-to-999-9.md)).
