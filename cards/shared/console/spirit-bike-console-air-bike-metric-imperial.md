**The default setting is Imperial Units (mph).**

**The short form, from the console setup page:** in **Ready Mode**, press and hold **Start and Enter
for two seconds** until **KM or M is flashing**, press the **up or down arrow** to select metric (KM)
or imperial (M), then press **Enter** to confirm.

**The long form, from the engineering mode page** - the same gesture and the same setting, described
as one entry of the engineering menu:

- Start and Enter for 2 seconds enters Engineering Mode; the console beeps for 1 second and the
  **DISTANCE** banner blinks first.
- Under the DISTANCE setting, press **Enter** to get into it. **M or KM blinks**: the **M** sign
  blinks if the console is on Imperial, the **KM** sign if it is on Metric.
- Press the up or down arrow to select, then **Enter** to confirm; the console returns to Engineering
  Mode.
- With no activity for 30 seconds the console switches to STANDBY Mode. **Pressing the Reset button
  twice exits Engineering Mode.**

**The manual prints the same fact in both places** and neither half contradicts the other - the setup
page is the shortcut description, the engineering page is the full walk-through. Read them together.

**This setting survives a keypad reset.** Holding Stop for 2 seconds resets all saved settings
**except the metric or imperial units setting**. Only removing the batteries clears it.

**It changes speed and distance together**, and both are labelled from it throughout the display.

**The service manual prints both forms as well** - the Start-and-Enter shortcut in Ready Mode until KM or
M flashes, and the engineering-mode walk-through under "A, metric/imperial system switching" - and
says the factory sets the distance units in miles.
