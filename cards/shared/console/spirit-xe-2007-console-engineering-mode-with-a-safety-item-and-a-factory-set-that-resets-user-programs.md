1. **Press and hold the START, ENTER and STOP buttons until the display beeps.**
2. **"ENGINEERING MODE"** scrolls across the screen; press **ENTER**.
3. Press **UP** or **DOWN** to scroll through the sub-menus, **ENTER** to open one, **STOP** to exit
   Engineering Mode.

| Sub-menu | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Key Test** | Press ENTER, then press all buttons on the console |
| **Display Test** | Press ENTER; the console tests each segment or LED. Look for any burned out or not working |
| **Functions** | The settings below |
| **Security** | **Child Lock On/Off** - press ENTER to turn on or off |
| **Factory Set** | Press ENTER to go back to original factory settings. **This will reset saved user programs** |
| **Exit** | Press ENTER to exit Engineering Mode |

**Inside Functions, in the printed order**, each toggled or run with ENTER:

1. **Sleep Mode On/Off**
2. **Pause Mode On/Off**
3. **Odometer Reset**
4. **Units English/Metric**
5. **Beep Sound On/Off**
6. **Motor Test** - press ENTER to begin the test
7. **Safety On/Off** - "**XE300 calls it 'Inc Max'**"
8. **Exit** - leaves the Functions menu

**No timing is printed for the hold, and no minutes for Sleep Mode.** The book says only "until
display beeps".

**The owner's manual for the XE100, XE200 and XE300 prints this menu with three differences**
(`spirit-xe-console-maintenance-menu-with-a-motor-test-and-security`): its Functions list has **no
Safety item**, its Sleep Mode is timed at **20 minutes**, it holds the three keys "for about 5
seconds" until the window reads `ALTXXXX Engineering mode`, and its Factory settings entry says only
"access only allowed by manufacturing". This dealer book is the only document that explains what
Factory Set does and that the XE300 labels Safety as **Inc Max**. Neither says what Safety does.

**This is a Spirit Manufacturing dealer manual**, one book for the five models; nothing on this
page distinguishes them except the Inc Max note. The XE400 and XE500 also have an incline
calibration keystroke that is not in this menu
(`spirit-xe400-xe500-2007-console-incline-calibration-start-and-level-up-held-five-seconds`). The
XBR25 and XBR55 bikes' dealer book of the same year prints the same menu with a Brake Test and a
Bike or Elliptical switch in place of Motor Test and Safety
(`spirit-xbr-2007-console-engineering-mode-with-a-factory-set-entry`).

**The XE395-2010 owner's manual is the closest later relative**, with a Safety item it explains
(`xe395-2010-console-engineering-mode-with-an-incline-switch-and-calibration`).
