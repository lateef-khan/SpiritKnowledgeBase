The Pulse (heart rate) window displays your current heart rate in beats per minute during the workout. You must use both stainless steel sensors on the stationary grips, or the heart-rate transmitter chest strap (**sold separately**), to display your pulse; the value displays any time the upper display is receiving a pulse signal. **You may not use the Pulse Grip feature while in the Heart Rate programs.**

The console's built-in heart-rate monitoring: grasping the hand pulse sensors on the stationary handle bars or wearing the transmitter starts the **Heart Icon blinking** (this may take a few seconds), and the Pulse window then shows the rate.

The heart-rate program page says the same from the other side — the program needs the wireless Bluetooth chest strap and must receive a steady signal in the heartbeat window (the Programs section holds that). The strap itself is a BLE 5.0 device that the console can receive even while the app is connected ([BLE strap card](xterra-tr-console-bluetooth-chest-strap-ble-5-0-sold-separately.md)); how to wear it is on [the strap card](xterra-treadmill-console-chest-strap-not-included-with-a-cr2032-battery.md).
