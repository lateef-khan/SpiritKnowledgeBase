**The Message Center starts on the Program name.** In scan mode during a program it steps
through the data, **four seconds at a time**:

1. **FPM** - floors per minute
2. **FLOORS**
3. **Laps completed**
4. **Segment time**
5. **SCAN**

**Pressing the Enter key again brings you back to the beginning.**

**These are a stepper's readings and they are not the elliptical's.** The XE and XB machines on the
same console face scan **Speed, Watts, Laps, Segment time and Max level**
(`spirit-xe-console-message-center-scan-shows-laps-segment-time-and-max-level`). This machine reports
no speed and no watts anywhere in its console chapter.

**FPM and FLOORS reach the Workout Summary too**, as **Avg. fpm** and **total floors**
(`spirit-xe-console-what-the-workout-summary-lists`).

**The manual never defines a floor** - no step count, no height, no unit - and gives no range for
either reading.

**The XS895 (XS300B-YS006) service manual prints the scan word for word** - the Program name first,
then FPM (floors per minute) for four seconds, FLOORS, Laps completed, Segment time, SCAN, and Enter
to return to the beginning.
