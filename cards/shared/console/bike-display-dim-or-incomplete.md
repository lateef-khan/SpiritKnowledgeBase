Two causes, in order:

1. **The LCD light is broken.** Replace the LCD or the console.
2. **Power to the console is too low.** Check the AC supply is 110-120 V (the R92 2016, LCR 2016, LCB 2019 and B94 2016 manuals add 220-230 V for those markets). Then check the power reaching the console. If it is still wrong, replace the lower controller.

If the LCD *displays* themselves are broken, the only fix listed is a new console.
