**The Message Center starts by displaying the Program name.** In scan mode during a program it steps
through the data, **four seconds at a time**:

1. **Speed**
2. **Watts** - an indication of workload. *If 100 watts is displayed, you are doing enough work to
   keep a 100-watt light bulb lit.*
3. **Laps completed**
4. **Segment time**
5. **Max level**

**Pressing the Enter key again brings you back to the beginning.**

All five manuals print this word for word.

**The XE395-2010 scans a different list.** It shows Speed, Watts, then **Segment Time, Laps
completed and Altitude** - elevation gain based on the incline level through the program - because it
is the only one of this family with a powered ramp:
`xe395-2010-console-message-center-scan-shows-altitude`.

**The XE795-2023 has no such paragraph.** Its Basic Information section covers the Stop key only, and
nothing in the manual says what its Message Center cycles.

**This is not the two-Display-button console of the 2005 and 2007 machines.** Those step through
four fixed pairs of readings instead:
`spirit-xe-console-display-button-steps-through-four-sets-of-readings`.
