1. While the treadmill is running, press the red **Stop** key once to pause. The tread-belt slowly decelerates to a stop and the **incline goes to zero percent**. Time, Distance and Calorie readings hold while paused. After **5 minutes** the display resets and returns to the start-up screen.
2. To resume, press **Start** while paused; speed and incline return to their previous settings.
3. Stop pressed a **second time** ends the program and displays a **workout summary**. Pressed a **third time**, the console returns to the idle (start-up) screen. If Stop is **held down for more than three seconds** the console resets.

The TR75 and TR85 print this under "Pause / Stop / Preset" with identical steps; their engineering mode also has a Pause Mode setting that turns the five-minute limit off so the console pauses indefinitely ([engineering card](xterra-tr-console-engineering-mode-start-stop-enter-key-test-functions-child-lock.md)). The TR6.6 and TR6.4 books print no such setting.
