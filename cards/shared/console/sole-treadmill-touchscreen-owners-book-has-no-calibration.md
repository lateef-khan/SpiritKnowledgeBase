**Absence, proved by full-text search plus a page-by-page read of each operation chapter.** None of these owners books prints a calibration procedure; their service checklists only say to run calibration if the belt moves then stops with an error. Calibrate from the service-manual card for your machine instead. The TT8-2021 owners book is the exception: its Settings page documents a Maintenance suite with a tap-to-start Calibration.
