**First:** copyright protected content will never mirror. It shows a black, green or garbled screen, or refuses to start.

**Steps**

1. Install the **SOLE Mirroring** app, made by **Sunrise Technology Co.**, from the Google Play Store. This is **not** the SOLE+ app.
2. Put the machine and the Android device on the **same network**.
3. Make sure the machine and the app are fully up to date, with no pending software updates.
4. Grant the location permission when asked. It must be **Precise** location, not Approximate. Android only lets the app see the Wi-Fi network name and nearby devices with precise location.
5. Tap **Start Searching** in the app and pick the machine's name.

**If the customer has a mesh router**

- Turn on **802.11r fast roam**. Google Nest has this on already.
- Turn off **AP Isolation**.
