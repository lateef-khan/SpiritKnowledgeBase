**Compatibility, as the sheet prints it:**

- iPhone 4S, 5, 5C, 5S, 6 / 6 Plus, 6s / 6s Plus, 7 / 7 Plus and above; iPad 3 and above; iPod touch 5th generation and above; **iOS 7.0 and above**.
- **Android 4.3+ with Bluetooth 4.0 BLE required.** The device uses BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy).

This answer comes from the two-page **XTERRA APP Q & A** sheet of October 2018, written for the original Xterra App (iOS 7.0 / Android 4.3). The 2023-2025 books use the XTERRA+ app instead; the app card for those machines is [here](xterra-treadmill-console-xterra-plus-app-ios-13-android-8.md). The TRX3500 and TRX4500 books print the same minimum as "iOS 7 or Android 4.4" ([card](xterra-trx-console-xterra-app-ios-7-android-4-4.md)) - the sheet says 4.3, the books 4.4; the card follows the books for those two machines because they are the document in the customer's hands, and notes the sheet's figure here.
