1. Press and release the **Start** key to wake the display (if not already on). Installing the tether key also wakes the console.
2. Press and release **Start** to begin belt movement at **0.5 mph**, then adjust with the Speed Up/Down or Fast/Slow keys (console or hand rail), or press **Direct Speed** and then a number **1 through 12**.
3. To adjust speed, press and hold Speed Up/Down (console or handgrip keys), or press Direct Speed and then 1 through 12.
4. To adjust incline, press and hold Incline Up/Down (console or handgrip keys), or press **Direct Incline** and then **0 through 12** (TR75) or **0 through 15** (TR85).
5. To stop the tread-belt, press and release the **Stop** key.

**Which numbers exist.** The pictured console has ten direct speed keys (2, 4, 6, 8, 12 over 1, 3, 5, 7, 10) on both machines; the TR75 has ten direct incline keys reading 12, 8, 5, 3, 1 over 10, 6, 4, 2, 0, and the TR85 ten reading 15, 10, 6, 3, 1 over 12, 8, 4, 2, 0. The only difference between the two books in this chapter is that incline maximum, 12 versus 15.

The handlebar speed and incline controls, the fan key and the pause behaviour are on [the features card](xterra-tr-console-features-handlebar-controls-fan-and-readouts.md).
