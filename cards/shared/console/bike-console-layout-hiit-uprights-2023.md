The console carries:

- **Cooling fan** and a **fan power switch**
- **Speaker**
- A **USB port** for charging portable devices
- **Display**
- **Program buttons**: Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, **HIIT**, 2 User, 2 HR

**This panel has no audio in jack and no headphone jack.** The 2019 panel of the same shape has both, and the 2019 manual has a paragraph explaining them. This manual labels neither on the diagram and prints no audio jack paragraph anywhere. Music reaches the speaker over Bluetooth instead.

The Interval button of the earlier bikes is **HIIT** here.
