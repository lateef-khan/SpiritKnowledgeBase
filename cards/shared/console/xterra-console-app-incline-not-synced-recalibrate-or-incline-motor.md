"No synced on the INCLINE function (number) on the app" - two causes, as printed:

1. The machine needs **re-calibration**.
2. **Incline motor failure.**

The question only arises on a machine with an incline, so this card lists the app-capable treadmills; the bikes that use the app have no incline. Calibration for each treadmill is on its own console card. This answer comes from the two-page **XTERRA APP Q & A** sheet of October 2018, written for the original Xterra App (iOS 7.0 / Android 4.3). The 2023-2025 books use the XTERRA+ app instead; the app card for those machines is [here](xterra-treadmill-console-xterra-plus-app-ios-13-android-8.md).
