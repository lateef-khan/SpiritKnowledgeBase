Insert the power cord into the socket on the **right side** of the treadmill, plug it into the outlet and turn on the machine's power switch. The treadmill enters the standby state.

With the safety key on its position on the console, the **buzzer sounds** and the system enters the standby state and **displays 0:00**. Pressing Start/Pause then gives a 3-2-1 countdown before the motor starts ([quick start card](xterra-ws-console-quick-start-3-2-1-countdown-from-0-6-mph.md)).

Without the key the window shows E07 and the buzzer beeps continuously ([safety lock card](xterra-ws-console-safety-key-removed-shows-e07-and-beeps.md)).
