The console carries:

- **Cooling fan** and a **fan power switch**
- **Speaker**
- **Display**
- **Program buttons**: Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, Interval, Custom, FIT-TEST, 2 HR
- **Audio in jack** for MP3, CD or smartphone
- **Headphone jack**

**There is no USB port on this panel.** The 2019 console of the same family adds one and replaces the Interval button with HIIT.
