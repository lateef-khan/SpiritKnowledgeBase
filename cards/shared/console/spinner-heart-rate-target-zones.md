1. In **DIST** or **KCAL** mode, hold the **PAGE** key for **3 seconds** to enter the settings.
2. Press **MODE** to reach the heart rate icon, then press **PAGE** to open the target zone window.
3. Press **MODE** to raise the **maximum** heart rate limit.
4. When the maximum is set, press **PAGE** to adjust the **minimum** heart rate.
