The Pulse (Heart Rate) window shows current heart rate in beats per minute. Use **both left and right stainless steel sensors on the front cross bar**; pulse values show anytime the computer receives a **Grip Pulse signal or** while wearing an **optional chest strap transmitter** (see the book's 'Using the Heart Rate Transmitter' section for wearing directions). The TT8-2013 heads that section '(NOT INCLUDED)'.
