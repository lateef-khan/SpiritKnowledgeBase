These nine battery-powered consoles have no power switch. Every book prints the same two rules:

- **On:** the console turns on when you start pedalling or when you press a key (the FB150/FB350 wording: "auto-powered on when starting to exercise push button w/signal in").
- **Off:** the console turns off automatically after **4 minutes** with no signal received ("around four minutes" in the EU100 book). The FB150 and FB350 books add that the console stops counting when you stop exercising for 4 seconds.

A blank screen after a rest is this timer, not a fault. If pedalling or a key press does not wake it, the batteries are the next check ([2 AAA card](xterra-console-two-aaa-um4-batteries-and-a-faint-display.md); the EU100 takes two AA, [card](../../eu100-2018/console/mode-set-and-reset-with-two-aa-batteries-and-preset-alarms.md)); the "all 0's, faded numbers or blank screen" tables in the AIR350, SB120 and UB120 books belong to the Errors cards.
