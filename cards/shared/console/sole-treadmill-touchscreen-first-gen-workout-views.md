After the short countdown the console enters workout mode; warm-up and cool-down phases can be skipped with **Skip**. The **left panel is fixed** with the current WORKOUT STATS (tap **+** for more); **speed and incline controls** sit in the right panel (tapped arrows; the ST90 book says sliding scales). Top icons during the workout: **volume, fan and WiFi**; while paused, HR device pairing is also reachable. The middle area follows the bottom-panel icon, left to right: **GOAL VIEW** (the finish-defining parameter, e.g. time counting down), **DIAGRAM VIEW** (profiles with current position; speed, incline or both), **TRACK VIEW** (laps on a **400 m track** by distance covered), then INTERNET and SCREEN CASTING as outside workouts. Pause or finish with **Stop** at the bottom right. Afterwards the summary appears (slide left for details, **Done** for Homescreen, **Save as** for a template).
