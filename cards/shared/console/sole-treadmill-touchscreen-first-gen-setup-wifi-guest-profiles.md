Once the infotainment system is powered on, choose a **WiFi network or skip by pressing Back**. Without WiFi, saving workout data to the cloud, using apps and casting content are **not available**; if WiFi is skipped, set **date and time manually**. WiFi settings stay in the **top right corner** of the navigation panel. Then choose **Guest** or create a profile for a more personalised experience: a **maximum of 9 profiles** can be created.
