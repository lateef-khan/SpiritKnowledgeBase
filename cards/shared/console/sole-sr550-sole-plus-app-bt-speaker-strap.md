The machine carries a **Bluetooth transceiver** for the free **SOLE+ App** (Apple Store / Google Play), which syncs workouts to **Apple Health, Google Fit, Strava, MapMyFitness or Fitbit**. Enable Bluetooth, tap the **top-right icon**, tap **Connect**; the **console Bluetooth icon lights up** when synced.

A Bluetooth wireless heart-rate chest strap stays connected **even with the App connected**. **Wireless music**: in the phone Bluetooth menu search for **Bt-speaker** and connect; the machine plays the phone audio.

Profile linking (QR scan, one SOLE+ account per console profile) lives on the getting-started card (see_also). The book warns the touchscreen UI images may change with software updates and no longer match the manual.
