The overview drawing names six things and nothing else:

- **Integrated adjustable fan** for workout comfort
- **Premium, ultra-bright blue LED display**
- **LED heart rate graph**
- **Easy to read lap counter**
- **Large LED matrix window** to track workout progress
- **Tablet friendly reading rack holder**

**No key is named on this drawing.** The Start, Stop, Enter, Scan, program and number keys are
described only in the manual's text, never labelled on the picture. Neither is a USB port, a
headphone socket or a C-SAFE port - this console has no USB charger call-out at all, unlike the
CR800 and CU800 face (`spirit-ce-console-led-console-face`).

**The 2025 printings call the display WHITE.** The CR900-2025 and CU900-2025 drawings name the same
six things and print *Premium ultra-bright **white** LED display*:
`ce900-2025-console-screen-overview`. **Blue is the 2018 word; white is the 2025 word.** Do not quote
either colour for the other year.

**Both 2018 pages are pictures, and the OCR of the matrix call-out differs between them.** The CU900
reads "Large LED matrix window to track workout progress"; the CR900 reads "Large LED matrix to show
workout progress". The two machines print the same drawing; the wording difference is in the reading,
not in the manuals.

The drawing also carries the bilingual **keep children under 13 away** warning decal and the Spirit
logo. The decal is a safety label, not a console feature.
