The workout screen carries four status icons down the **left side**:

- **USB Charge Icon**
- **Bluetooth Speaker**
- **Bluetooth Heart Rate**
- **Bluetooth On/Off**

**The Bluetooth heart rate icon lights up, and the heart rate is displayed, when the console is
successfully connected to a Bluetooth heart rate chest strap. The icon will be off when the chest
strap is disconnected.**

**The XT185 workout screen carries none of these icons** - that manual labels no Bluetooth or USB
indicators at all.
