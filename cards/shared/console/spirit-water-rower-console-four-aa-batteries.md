**"The console operates on 4 AA batteries. The battery compartment is on the back side of the
console. Please dispose of batteries properly after use."** That is the whole section, in both books,
word for word.

**Both machines ship with the cells.** The CRW800H2O parts checklist lists **AA Batteries 4** and its
assembly step four says *"Place the 4 AA batteries into the Console (No. 54)"*; the CRW900 parts list
lists item **15, quantity 4, AA BATTERY**.

**The CRW900 assembly step says two, and it is the only place in either book that does.** Its step 5
reads *"Install supplied **2** AA batteries [15] and check computer function"*, against **4** in its
own parts list and **4** in its own console setup section. **Read from the native PDF text of both
manuals, not from OCR.** **Four is the figure to give** - two of the CRW900's three statements say
four, it ships four, and the CRW800H2O says four in all three places. **The 2 is the odd one out and
the manual never reconciles it**; say so rather than sending someone to buy two cells. The full
comparison is on `spirit-water-rower-safety-four-aa-batteries-and-no-electrical-page`.

**Neither manual gives a battery life figure, a low-battery indicator or a replacement interval**,
and neither says whether the cells should be alkaline or rechargeable.

**Removing them is a factory reset, and the manual says so in the engineering mode section:**
*"removing the batteries will return the console to original factory settings and the ODO value will
be zero."* **Warn a customer before they pull the cells to test them - the odometer does not
survive** (`spirit-water-rower-console-engineering-mode-sound-and-odo`).

**Batteries are the first thing the CRW900 troubleshooting table blames for a dark screen.** *"The
console does not illuminate after battery installation - batteries installed incorrectly or need
replacing. Check to see if the batteries are installed in the correct position. Reinstall or replace
as needed. If the LCD screen fails to illuminate, try rotating the batteries slightly in the
computer."* **Rotating the cells in place is the manual's own suggestion**, not a workaround.

**AA here, C on the air rower, and nothing at all on the XRW600.** The CRW800 air rower takes **two C
cells** (`crw800-2024-console-two-c-batteries`) and the XRW600 manuals name no power source at all
(`xrw600-console-no-power-source-is-named`). **Three Spirit rower consoles, three different answers.
Do not carry a cell size across.**

**The Spirit air bikes take four AA in the same way** and quote the same factory-reset warning
(`spirit-bike-console-air-bike-four-aa-batteries`); that is a bike card and covers no rower.
