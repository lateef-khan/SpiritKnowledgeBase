**Dot Center Matrix Display.** Twenty columns of boxes (10 high) indicate each segment of a workout. The boxes only show an approximate level of effort — an approximate percent to compare levels of intensity, not a specific value. In manual operation the dot matrix window builds a profile "picture" as values are changed.

**1/4 Mile Track.** The 1/4-mile track (one lap) is displayed around the dot matrix window. The flashing segment indicates your progress; once the 1/4 mile (**0.4 km** in metric) is complete the feature begins again. The lap track moves **counterclockwise**, and there is a **lap counter in the message window** for monitoring your distance. The LCD drawing also has a LAPS window across the top.

The TR6.6 and TR6.4 matrix is 18 by 8 with a Display key ([card](xterra-tr-console-dot-matrix-18-by-8-and-the-display-key.md)).
