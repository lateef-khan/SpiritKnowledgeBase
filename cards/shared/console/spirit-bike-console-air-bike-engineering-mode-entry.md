**To enter:** press **Start and Enter buttons for 2 seconds**. The console beeps for 1 second and the
**DISTANCE** banner blinks first.

**To move around:** press the **up or down arrow** to select the setting section. The order loops:

```
DISTANCE  ->  SOUND  ->  ODO  ->  DISTANCE
```

Press **Enter** to go into the highlighted setting.

**To leave:** **press the Reset button twice.** With no activity for 30 seconds the console switches
to STANDBY Mode.

**Three settings, no more.** DISTANCE sets metric or imperial, SOUND turns the audio on or off, ODO
reads the odometer. There is no calibration entry, no error log, no software version and no service
counter on this console.

**Exiting does not lose anything.** The console will not erase the setting value or the ODO value
under RESET mode. **However, removing the batteries returns the console to original factory settings
and turns the ODO value to zero.**

**The same gesture is printed as a units shortcut** on the console setup page, where holding Start
and Enter for two seconds in Ready Mode is described as going straight to the KM/M choice. It is the
same door: DISTANCE is simply the entry the menu opens on.

**This is not the ENT touchscreen gesture.** Those consoles open engineering mode by pressing the
Home icon ten times (`spirit-ent-console-enter-engineering-mode`), and the JB950 Johnny G bike uses
its own three-key hold. Three different Spirit bike platforms, three different doors.
