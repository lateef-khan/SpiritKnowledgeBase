**The console includes a built-in fan to help keep you cool. To turn the fan on, press the key on
the right side, front of the console.**

That is all either manual says. **No speed settings, no off instruction and no automatic behaviour
are printed.** The console drawing labels the fan at the top of the console face but does not mark
the key.

**The CE900 fan is a three-speed and its key is on the upper right**, which is a different key in a
different place: `spirit-ce900-console-three-speed-fan`.
