The console prints rapid **speed** keys **1, 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10** (2012 book: '1, 2, 4, 6, 8, or 10'), usable with the Speed 5/6 keys on the console or handgrip. The F60-2016 owner's book prints the same set; its full button tables live on the service-manual cards. Other books in the wave print different sets: 2-3-4-5-6-9-12, 2-4-6-8-12, 2-4-6-8-10-12, 2-through-10 or 2-through-12.
