The console's lubrication reminder lights after **90 hours** of use. If the "Lube" message appears, lubricate the deck (the Maintenance section holds the procedure), then reset the message:

1. To enter the Engineering Mode Menu, press and hold down the **Start, Stop and Enter** keys, and at the same time insert the safety key. Keep holding the keys down until the display shows Engineering Mode Menu. Press **Enter** to access it.
2. Press the Speed ▲ key (or Speed ▼ to go backwards) until "**Functions**" appears; press Enter.
3. Press Speed ▲ until "**Reset Lube**" appears; press Enter.
4. Press **Stop** to exit engineering mode and resume use of the treadmill.

The same three books print a *different* key combination for the calibration side of engineering mode — Enter held while the key goes in on the TR300, Start and Speed ▲ held five seconds on the TR6.6 and TR6.4 ([TR300 calibration](../../tr300-2021/console/calibration-hold-enter-wheel-60-max-10-mph-incline-10.md), [TR6.6/TR6.4 engineering](xterra-tr-console-engineering-mode-start-and-speed-up-wheel-63.md)). Neither book reconciles the two entries; both are printed as written.
