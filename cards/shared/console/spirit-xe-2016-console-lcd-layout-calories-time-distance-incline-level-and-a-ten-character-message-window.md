The LCD Layout page is a drawing of the glass with every segment lit. Top row, left to right:

| Window | Digits |
|---|---|
| **CALORIES** | 8888, with a **heart** and 888 under it for pulse |
| **TIME** | 88:88, the largest digits on the glass |
| **DISTANCE** | 88.88, with 888 **RPM** under it |

Middle: an oval **track** outline enclosing an **INCLINE** window (88.8) on the left, a **dot
matrix** of 16 columns by 10 rows in the centre, and a **LEVEL** window (88) on the right.

Bottom: a **SCAN** mark, a **ten-character** 14-segment message window, and at the right the
**KM** and **MI** unit marks with a **Bluetooth** and a **Wi-Fi** icon.

**The glass is the same on all six, so the XE195, XE295, XE795, XE895 and XG400 carry an INCLINE
window they never use.** Only the XE395-2016 has a powered ramp; the others' function tables give no
incline range and their keys have no incline function
(`spirit-xe-2016-console-keys-in-ready-and-run-mode-enter-scans-every-four-seconds`). On the XE895
the window would show the stride, which its software spec calls incline in places
(`spirit-ce850-2016-console-keys-in-ready-and-run-mode-incline-half-steps-and-alt-in-the-scan`).

**The Bluetooth and Wi-Fi icons are marks on the glass, not features the books describe.** The
XE295-2016 and XE895-2016 display-board wiring pages label a BLUETOOTH header on the board, and
that is all: no 2016 service manual in this family prints a pairing procedure or names an app. The
owner's manuals of the 2019 and 2021 successors describe the Spirit Fit app
(`xt-2015-console-spirit-fit-app-pairing`).

**The message window is what the Enter key scans**
(`spirit-xe-2016-console-keys-in-ready-and-run-mode-enter-scans-every-four-seconds`), and the
ranges each window counts to are `spirit-xe-2016-console-data-ranges-level-0-to-20-and-pulse-40-to-220`,
`xe195-2016-console-data-ranges-level-1-to-20-and-pulse-40-to-220` and
`xe395-2016-console-data-ranges-incline-0-to-20-in-half-steps-and-level-0-to-20`.
