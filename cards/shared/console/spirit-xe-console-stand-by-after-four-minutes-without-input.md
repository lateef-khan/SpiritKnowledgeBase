**If there is no input to the console for four minutes the console goes to stand-by mode.** In
stand-by the display turns off. **To turn the console on, press any key.**

**Four minutes is this manual's figure and no other Spirit elliptical manual repeats it.** The 2007
XE100, XE200 and XE300 and the XE395-2010 all print **twenty** minutes for the same behaviour:
`spirit-xe-console-stand-by-after-twenty-minutes-without-input`.

**Nothing here can be changed.** This manual documents no maintenance or engineering menu at all, so
there is no sleep or display-mode setting to turn the timeout off. The later XE consoles do have
one.

**A blank display on a machine that has been idle is normal, not a fault.** Check the power supply
only if pressing a key does nothing.
