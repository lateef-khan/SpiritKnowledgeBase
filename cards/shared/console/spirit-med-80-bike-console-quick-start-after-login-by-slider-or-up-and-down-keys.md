**"This is the quickest way to start an exercise session. After the console powers up and user is
logged in just press the Start key to begin; this will initiate the Quick Start mode."** Logging in -
or entering as a guest - comes first on this console
(`spirit-med-80-bike-console-login-by-qr-code-nfc-or-pin-or-as-a-guest`).

**"In Quick Start the time will count up from zero, all workout data will start to accrue and the
workload may be adjusted manually by sliding to desired number or by pressing the Up and Down
keys."** The slider is on the screen; the Up and Down keys are the **+ / -** hard keys under it
(`spirit-med-80-bike-console-function-keys-and-the-protective-sheet-to-peel-off`).

**That is the whole section.** Unlike the LED bikes' quick start there is no dot-matrix description, no
column count and no level count here (`spirit-ce-console-quick-start-time-counts-up`); the
specification page gives the resistance as 100 levels of effort, and the overview page's **Start**
button is this key on screen
(`spirit-med-80-bike-console-screen-overview-prints-a-factory-mode-banner-and-a-treadmill-tile`).
