Open the **right tab** of the bottom panel for **Media**: pre-installed third-party **Apps** or **Mirroring** from a smartphone (the **? icon** holds the instructions). Enjoy news/video 'on the run', or mirror the phone screen onto the console. Once content is on, a **floating panel** steers it: drag it by its top edge out of the way; **Hide Panels** goes full screen without top stats and bottom tabs, **Show Panels** brings them back; the side arrow hides/shows the text labels to shrink the panel; **Apps** returns to the source list.
