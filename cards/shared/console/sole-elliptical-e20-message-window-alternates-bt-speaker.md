No Display-key order as on the larger consoles: the Message Window **alternates automatically every 4 seconds** between **Time, Level and Distance** and **HEART RATE, RPM and Calories**. It has an **Audio In Jack** with built-in speakers but **no headphone jack is printed**; instead the book configures **Bluetooth wireless music receive** - open the phone/tablet Bluetooth device, search for **'Bt-speaker'**, connect, and the device plays through the machine. No incline calibration and no heart-rate paragraph are printed for the E20 (see_also).
