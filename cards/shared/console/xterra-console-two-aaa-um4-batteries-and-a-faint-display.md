The console table prints **BATTERY TYPE: 2 AAA or UM 4** (the AIR350 book: "(2) Two AAA", loaded into the console at assembly step 1). "If the display is faint or shows no figures, please replace the batteries." The odometer is kept until the batteries come out.

**Are they in the box?** The FB160 service document contradicts itself: its tool list reads "Tools Required (Included) - 13 & 15mm Wrench, 2- AAA Batteries, 5mm Allen Key...", and its last line reads "Console - MUST purchase QTY 2 AAA Energizer batteries." The card follows the second line - it is the specific instruction, written as a warning, while the first is a packing list - and treats the batteries as **not supplied** for the FB160; the other five books say nothing either way.

The FB150 and FB350 books only say "replace the batteries" without naming a type; the EU100 takes two AA ([card](../../eu100-2018/console/mode-set-and-reset-with-two-aa-batteries-and-preset-alarms.md)); the AIR650 takes four AA. The 4-minute auto-off that empties the screen before the batteries do is on [the auto-off card](xterra-console-battery-console-wakes-on-pedalling-and-sleeps-after-4-minutes.md).
