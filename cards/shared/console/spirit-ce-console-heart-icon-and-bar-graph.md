The console has a **built-in heart rate monitoring system** with three parts near the profile
window: a **Heart Icon**, a **heart rate number** and a **Bar Graph**.

- **Grasping the hand pulse sensors, or wearing a heart rate chest belt transmitter, starts the
  Heart Icon blinking.** The manuals warn this **may take a few seconds**.
- The window then displays your heart rate, or Pulse, **in beats per minute**.
- The **Bar Graph represents the percentage of your maximum heart rate you are currently
  achieving.**

**The bar graph is wrong until you enter your age.** All four manuals print the same note: *you must
enter your age during program setup for the Bar Graph to be accurate.* The number of beats per
minute is measured and does not depend on your age; only the percentage does.

**Where it sits differs by console.** The CE800-2013 and CE850-2016 manuals put the icon and bar
graph **below** the dot matrix display; both CE900 manuals put them **to the right** of the profile
window.

**The CE800-2021, CE800-2023, CE850-2020, CE850-2022 and CES880 consoles have no bar graph** - they
print a plain pulse window instead: `spirit-ce-console-pulse-window-polar-coded-straps`.
