At first power-on the **boot screen shows odometer readings**: total time (hours in use) and total distance (miles, or kilometres in metric). The machine then enters **idle mode**, the starting point for operation, on the main workout home page (Manual, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, Interval, User 1/2, HR 1/2 on the E95: 'select workout, then press Start to begin, or press Enter to modify'). Pressing **Display** changes the on-screen data; unless pressed a second time the screen returns to the workout screen after **3 seconds**. No VER 1.0 self-test as on the LCD family (own card).
