**There is an anatomical figure at the top of the console.** It lights the areas the bike is working,
during **any** of the programs, and you can steer it by customising the resistance profile during the
set-up phase of console programming. If you accept the default profile, the program decides.

**The manual states plainly that the upper body LEDs will not light.** That is the rule for these
machines, not a fault to chase - and it is the honest answer for a bike, where your arms rest on the
grips.

**Lower body, three degrees of engagement:** **Green** is minimal involvement, **yellow** medium,
**red** full or heavy activation. The banding is by **resistance level**:

| Levels | What lights |
|---|---|
| **1-10** | **Green** - Hamstrings and Gluteals; **Amber** - Quadriceps and Calves |
| **11-20** | **Amber** - Hamstrings and Gluteals; **Red** - Quadriceps and Calves |

**The manual names the middle colour twice and differently**, exactly as the XE795 book does: the
list of degrees calls it **yellow**, the banding table calls it **Amber**. Both mean the same lamp.

**These bands are for a twenty-level machine.** The XBR55 and XBU55 have 20 resistance levels, so the
two bands split at 10. **The XBR95 bands the identical figure at 1-20 and 21-40** because it has 40
levels; quoting that card here, or this one there, puts a customer two bands out:
`xe795-2021-console-muscle-figure-lights-by-resistance-level`.

**The XBU55 prints the heading in capitals and runs its colours together** - "Green - Hamstrings &
Gluteals light up; Amber -Quadriceps & Calves light up" with the spaces closed - where the XBR55
prints them apart. No figure differs.

**The console panel is silk-screened MUSCLE PROFILE** above the figure on all four drawings, though
the call-out list beside the drawing never names it.

**Three residential bikes have no muscle figure at all.** The XBR25 of 2019 and 2023 never had one,
and **all four 2023 bikes dropped it** - their console overview drawings label only an LCD Window, an
Adjustable Fan, Program Buttons, Direct Resistance Level Buttons and, on three of them, USB Charging.
