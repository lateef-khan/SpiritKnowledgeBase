Charge a personal device during the workout by placing it properly **inside the wireless charging box/area** (console caption: WIRELESS CHARGING AREA). Set the device in **landscape orientation** for the best result; its charging icon shows it is charging. The box gives **up to 10 watts** to phones that support wireless charging. The ST90-2023 book shortens this to placing the cell phone in the box/area with the machine on.
