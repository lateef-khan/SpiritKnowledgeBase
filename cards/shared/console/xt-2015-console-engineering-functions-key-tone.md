Press **Enter** on **Functions** to access the settings.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| I. Display Mode | **Turn off** to have the console power down automatically after **30 minutes** of inactivity |
| II. Pause Mode | **Turn on** to allow **5 minutes** of pause; **turn off** to have the console pause indefinitely |
| III. Maintenance | **Reset lube message and odometer readings** |
| IV. Units | Sets the display to readout in **English or Metric** display measurements |
| V. Key Tone | **Turns off the speaker so no beeping sound is heard** |

**The scroll key is printed differently in the two manuals**: the XT185-2015 says *"use the Speed Up
and Down keys to scroll"*, the XT285-2015 says *"Speed Up or Down arrow"*.

**Do not carry the 2023 figures onto these machines.** The 2023 XT consoles call the first entry
**Sleep Mode** and give it **15 minutes**, not 30, and they list a **BEEP** entry alongside Key Tone.
See `xt-2023-console-engineering-functions-with-key-tone`.

**The XT385-2015, XT485-2015 and XT685-2015 print a different list** - the fifth entry is called
**Beep**, not Key Tone, and the Maintenance description is not the same. See
`xt-2015-console-engineering-functions-beep`.

**Both service manuals print these five entries word for word**, including Display Mode turned off
for the 30-minute power-down, Maintenance resetting the lube message and the odometer, and Key Tone
as the fifth entry. Both scroll with the Speed ▲/▼ keys.

**What Display Mode also does, from the service manuals' E0 note:** *"When choose off, this is mean
display off after removed safety key. When choose on which is display on and appear E0 after removed
safety key."* With Display Mode OFF the console blanks when the safety key is pulled; with it ON the
display stays lit and shows E0.

**The XT285-2015 service manual walks the lube reset through this menu** in its lubrication section:
hold Start, Stop and Enter and insert the safety key, Enter, Speed ▲ until Functions, Enter, Speed ▲
until Maintenance, Enter resets the lube message, Stop exits.
