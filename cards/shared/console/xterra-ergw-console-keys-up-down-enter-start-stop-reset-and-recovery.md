| Key | As printed |
|---|---|
| **UP / DOWN** | "Press these two buttons to scroll through available selection. To adjust the function value upward and downward." On the ERG650W they are drawn as left and right **arrow keys**. |
| **ENTER** | "Press to confirm your selection. During training, press the button to cycle scan each display function." The ERG650W adds "To access the different workout programs." |
| **START / STOP** | "To start and stop your selected workout program." |
| **RESET** | "To return the computer back to the main menu. **Press and hold to reset values.**" |
| **RECOVERY** (ERG600W, ERG650W only) | "To activate the RECOVERY PROGRAM that will automatically evaluate your fitness immediately after your work out." |

**Key layouts, left to right.** ERG550W: UP, RESET, START/STOP, ENTER, DOWN - **five keys, no RECOVERY**. ERG600W: RECOVERY, RESET, START/STOP, ENTER, UP, DOWN. ERG650W: RECOVERY, RESET, ENTER, START/STOP, left arrow, right arrow. Each console is an LCD with a central dot matrix; the ERG550W's and ERG600W's mode strip reads STOP, MANUAL, PROGRAM, H.R.C., RACE, USER, RECOVERY, B.M.I, FAT% although the books describe only MANUAL and RACE; the ERG650W's reads QUICK START, STANDARD, TARGET (single, interval), CUSTOM, RACE. The ERG550W carries a phone clip on top.

What the windows read is on [the readouts card](xterra-ergw-console-readouts-time-500m-distance-to-99900-m-strokes-and-watts.md); what happens at power-up on [the water-level card](xterra-ergw-console-power-up-asks-for-the-water-level-one-to-six.md) and [the ERG600W clock card](../../erg600w-2021/console/power-up-sets-the-clock-calendar-and-alarm-then-the-sport-screen.md); the recovery test on [the ERG600W recovery card](../../erg600w-2021/console/recovery-counts-down-from-00-60-and-grades-from-outstanding-to-poor.md) - the ERG650W book describes the key and never the test. MANUAL, RACE and the ERG650W's program menu are programs cards.
