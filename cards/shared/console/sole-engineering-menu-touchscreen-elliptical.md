1. Turn the power switch on if the machine is not already on.
2. Log in to a personal account or choose the Guest profile.
3. Tap the gear icon in the top left to open Settings.
4. Tap the word "Settings" rapidly several times until the Engineering Menu appears.

Tap a selection to open it; tap the **X** at the top left to leave the menu.

The usual options:

- **Function**
  - **Unit** - miles or kilometres. Also changeable in normal settings.
  - **Odo** - hours and kilometres of lifetime use. The reset button zeroes it.
  - **Sleep Mode** - OFF, ON or RETAIL. ON sleeps after 15 to 30 minutes of inactivity; OFF keeps the screen on; RETAIL plays promo videos after 3 minutes. Also in normal settings.
  - **Beep** - ON allows beeps, OFF mutes most of them.
  - **5K HeartRate** - whether the machine listens for 5 kHz straps. OFF still allows Bluetooth/ANT+ straps.
  - **Software Update** - engineering software management. Not for the customer.
  - **Machine Type** - the model the console thinks it sits on. A wrong setting throws error codes - this is what LWR Not Match means.
- **Service**
  - **Key Test** - each press beeps and shows the button. You have to press every button to exit.
  - **Motor Test** - Manual: direct gear-motor control with plus/minus. Auto: lowest to highest automatically.
  - **Incline Test** - Manual: direct incline-motor control with plus/minus. Auto: lowest to highest automatically.
  - **Sensor Test** - live heart-rate and speed readings: HRM (Heart Rate Monitor), HP (Hand Pulse), WP (Wireless Pulse), RPM (Rotations per Minute).
- **Factory Setting**
  - **Restore Factory** - clears user profiles and saved workout data, like the login-screen reset. Does not touch media apps.
  - **First Launch** - replays the first-run tutorials.
  - **Grade Return** - ON resets the incline to 0 after boot and login. Useful after a power cut.
  - **Territory** - US, Global or Mandarin, for regional compliance.
  - **BLE Name** - the Bluetooth name broadcast to the SOLE+ app.
  - **OTA Url** - where over-the-air updates are pulled from. Engineering only.
- **Calibration** - the start button beside Incline Calibration recalibrates the incline motor. Needed whenever the console, incline controller or incline motor is replaced.

**The 2023 TFT owners books print no such menu** (`sole-elliptical-2023-tft-no-menu-no-calibration`). This card is the technician note's path, not the books'.
