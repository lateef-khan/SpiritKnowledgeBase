**These bikes plug in; nothing happens until the power switch on the input module at the front is
on.** Then "when initially powered on the console, it will perform an internal self-test. During this
time all the lights will turn on for a short time."

- **The dot matrix shows a software version** (i.e. `VER 1.0`).
- **The message window shows an odometer reading** - "how many hours the 7.0R has been used and how
  many virtual miles the 7.0R has been ridden. The display will look like this: **ODO 123 MI 123
  HRS**."
- The odometer stays "for only a few seconds", then the console goes to the start-up display, **Idle
  Mode**, with the start-up message scrolling in the message window.

**"The console will automatically power down after 20 minutes of inactivity. Press any key to wake
the console up again. Always turn off the main power switch when the [bike] is not in use."** All
three owner's manuals print 20 minutes here.

**The same books' maintenance menus print a different figure, and the service manuals a third
wording.** The Sleep mode entry says the console powers down after **30 minutes** in the 2025 7.0R
and 7.0U books and after **20 minutes** in the Dyaco MED 7.0R; both service manuals say Sleep on
means "the console will go to sleep after 30 minutes of inactivity"
(`spirit-med-70-bike-console-maintenance-mode-with-a-sleep-switch-a-keypad-lock-and-factory-settings`).
Nothing reconciles 20 with 30. Both are reproduced as printed; the Sleep entry is the only place the
books say the time-out can be switched off.

**The odometer is reset from the same maintenance menu** (Odometer reset - "Resets the odometer to
zero (Time and distance)"). The 7.0T treadmill shows the same three-stage start
(`70t-2026-console-power-on-self-test`) and the rehabilitation steppers the same 20-minute
power-down (`spirit-rehab-stepper-console-power-up-and-the-twenty-minute-auto-power-down`); the
8-series touchscreen bikes take minutes to boot and sleep after 30
(`spirit-med-80-bike-console-power-on-self-test-login-page-and-thirty-minute-power-down`).
