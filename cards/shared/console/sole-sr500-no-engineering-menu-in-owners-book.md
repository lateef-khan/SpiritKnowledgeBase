Neither owner book prints an engineering or maintenance menu: the contents run Registration, Safety, Electrical, Operation, Assembly, Operation of the rower, Folding, Warranty, with no menu chapter, and a full-text search finds no FUN display, key test, odometer reset or motor test.

The menu exists on this console family: the SR500-2016 service manual documents engineering mode (hold UP+DOWN 3 seconds in idle, FUN display, key/odometer/units/motor/manual tests). Use that card for the procedure; this card records only that the 2019/2022 owner books do not repeat it.
