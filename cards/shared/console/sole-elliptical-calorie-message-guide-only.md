Calorie readings on gym or home equipment **are not accurate and vary widely**; use them only as a guide from workout to workout. Only a clinical setting with lab machines measures burn accurately, because every body burns differently. Entering Age (needed for Heart Rate programs) and Weight keeps the figure closer. The 2007/2008 books add an after-exercise burn sentence and the WE55/95 its own variant (own cards). Worded differently from the treadmill rough-guide card (see_also).
