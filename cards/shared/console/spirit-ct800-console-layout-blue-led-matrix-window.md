**This is the earlier CT800 console.** It has no USB charger, no scan buttons and no ladders of
direct access buttons; the later CT800 and CT850 console does, and its layout is
`ct850-2020-console-layout`.

The drawing labels six things:

| Call-out | The manual's words |
|---|---|
| Fan | "Fan to keep you cool" |
| Heart Rate % Profile | The bar graph above the display |
| Contact Heart Rate Grips | The two handlebar grips |
| 20-Character Message Center | The text window |
| Large Blue-LED Matrix Window | The dot matrix panel |
| Water bottle holder and cargo compartment | "for keys, phone, or MP3 player" |

**The 2016 manual prints that last call-out as "for buttons, phone, or MP3 player".** That manual
replaces the word "key" with "button" throughout, so it reads "buttons" where the 2012 manual reads
"keys". The compartment holds keys.

Neither manual prints a numbered key map, and neither names the ports on the back of the console
beyond the C-SAFE pair (`ct850-2020-c-safe-ports`).
