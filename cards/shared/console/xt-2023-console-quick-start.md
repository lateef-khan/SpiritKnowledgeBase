1. **Press and release the Start key to wake the display up** (if it is not already on).
   *Note: installing the tether key will also wake up the console.*
2. **Press and release the Start key to begin belt movement, at 0.5 mph**, then adjust to the
   desired speed using the **Speed + / -** or **Fast / Slow** keys (console or hand rail).
3. To adjust the speed, **press and hold the Speed Up / Down keys** (console or remote handlebar
   buttons) to achieve the desired speed.
4. To adjust the incline level, **press and hold the Incline Up / Down keys** (console or remote
   handlebar buttons) to achieve the desired gradient.
5. **To stop the tread-belt, press and release the Stop key.**

All five 2023 XT manuals print these five steps identically.

Each machine also has direct preset buttons that jump straight to a level, but **the range of levels
those buttons cover is not the same on every machine in this family** - the XT185 reaches speed 10
and incline 10, the XT285 reaches 12 and 12, and the XT385, XT485 and XT685 reach 12 and 15. See the
direct speed and incline cards before quoting a number.
