After the short countdown the console enters workout mode; warm-up and cool-down phases can be skipped with **Skip**. During the workout switch between the **Stats, Charts and Track** views of the Training section, or open Media; in Stats view, tap a number to choose which parameters display. The summary after the workout works as on the first generation (details, Done, Save as template).
