Press the **Incline Up key and the Start key together and hold for 5 seconds** (E98 books: hold **until Calibration appears**), then **press Start**; calibration starts and runs automatically over a MAX/MIN INCLINE page. To leave the calibration page, **power the unit off and back on**. On the E98 books a failed calibration shows **E-3**, which Stop bypasses. The E95S books calibrate stride, not incline (own card).
