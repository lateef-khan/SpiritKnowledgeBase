The sixth engineering mode entry sets the audio/visual source. Two choices, word for word:

1. **C-Safe protocol.** (HDMI transfer to screen on TV of Entertainment function).
2. **CAB protocol** (connect a set-box transfer to screen on TV of Entertainment function).

The manual gives no cable specification, no port location and no rule for which to pick. The
owner's manuals of these two bikes say TV mode needs a set-top box on the HDMI port and is reached
from the Entertainment tile (`spirit-ent-bike-console-entertainment-tv-mode`), which is the CAB
choice described here; neither owner's manual mentions this setting.

**The other consoles in the family word the same pair differently.** The CE800ENT elliptical prints
this page (`ce800ent-av-source-setup`); the CU900ENT says only one of C-SAFE or CAB may be chosen
(`cu900ent-tv-switching-box-selection`); the CT800ENT-2022 and CT850ENT-2022 treadmills call
C-SAFE an infrared interface (`spirit-ct800ent-console-av-source-setup-cab-hdmi-or-csafe-infrared`).
The physical C-SAFE cable on these bikes is the 5-pin lead on the console's HDMI/Coaxial/C-SAFE
board, which is held with the specs cards.
