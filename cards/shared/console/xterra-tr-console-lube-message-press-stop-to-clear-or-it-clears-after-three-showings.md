When "**Lubricate Deck Press Stop to Clear Message**" appears on the LCD, check the lubrication under the walking belt (the reminder runs on a total of **90 hours**; the book prints "90s hours"). Press the **STOP** key to clear the Lube string, or let it display **three times**, after which it clears automatically.

The engineering mode's Functions menu has a **Maintenance** entry that resets the lube message and the odometer readings ([engineering card](xterra-tr-console-engineering-mode-start-stop-enter-key-test-functions-child-lock.md)). Lubricating the deck belongs to the Maintenance section.
