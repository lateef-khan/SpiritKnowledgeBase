**Eighteen columns of dots, 8 high for the TT8**, showing approximate speed/Grade effort as a profile picture. When **Speed** is lit above or below the matrix the Speed profile shows; when **Grade** is lit the Grade profile shows. Press **Enter** to swap; a **second** press switches back; a **third** press alternates the two views automatically (no five-second interval is printed, unlike the F-series books).
