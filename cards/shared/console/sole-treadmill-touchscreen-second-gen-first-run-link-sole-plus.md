At first launch the console offers **Guest** or a new profile. A profile remembers physical parameters, favorite programs and templates, gives a custom program with an adjustable intensity pattern, and syncs workout records with the SOLE+ mobile app. Tap **+** to create one: a **maximum of 9 profiles**, each protectable with a **passcode**. To link SOLE+, scan the console QR code to download the app, press **Link** at the bottom of the console screen, then scan the QR code again from inside the SOLE+ app. **One console profile cannot link to multiple SOLE+ accounts at once**; skipping is possible and the step is available again later in Profile settings.
