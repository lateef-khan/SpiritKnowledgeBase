When power is connected the console powers up automatically.

**These models have no power pack.** The manual states they are **connected directly to 115 VAC**, and there is a **power switch where the line cord plugs into the unit, on the left side near the front**.

On first power-on the **boot screen** shows odometer readings for a short time:

- **Total time** — how many hours the bike has been in use.
- **Total distance** — how many miles, or kilometres if the bike is set to metric readings, the bike has gone.

The manual illustrates the boot screen with `TOTAL TIME 20:01:30`, `TOTAL DISTANCE 153.5`, `SOFTWARE VER. 20151111 V1.0` and `INITIALIZE`. Those are example values from the printed screenshot, not this machine's readings.

The bike then enters **idle mode**, which is the starting point for operation. Idle mode is the main workout home page: select a workout and press Start to begin, or press Enter to modify it.
