The console carries:

- **Cooling fan** and a **fan power switch**
- **Speaker**
- A **USB port** for charging portable devices
- **Display**
- **Program buttons**: Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, **HIIT**, Custom, FIT-TEST, 2 HR
- **Audio in jack** for MP3, CD or smartphone
- **Headphone jack**

The Interval button of the 2016 console is **HIIT** here, and the USB charging port is new on this panel.
