**No Data Scan entry is printed**, unlike the TT8-2013 book. When the **Display key** is pressed, or when a program ends, the extra information is: **LAPS, Verticle** (the books' spelling; virtual altitude from the Grade setting), **METs, Segment Time, Max Speed**. No Pace and no Grade entries.
