**Charge your personal device from the on-console USB port; the machine's power must be on.**

The three printed notes, in the manual's order:

- **The USB charging cable is not included** - use a compatible one.
- **Your device's "charging" icon may or may not indicate it is charging.** Depending on the current
  the device wants, the icon may stay off while the device still charges, possibly at a lower rate.
- **The port provides up to 5 Vdc / 1.0 amp and meets USB 2.0 regulations.** You will not be able
  to save your workout data to a USB via this port; **it is used for charging purposes only**.

**A phone that reports "not charging" on this port is not a fault** when the phone wants more than
one amp; the manual says so in its own words.

**The owner's manuals give no rating.** Their console drawing names the USB Charging port and says
nothing about current or data (`spirit-ent-bike-console-layout-touchscreen`); this figure is from
the service manuals only. The CE800ENT elliptical prints the same block (`ce800ent-usb-charging-port`),
and the XBR55ENT, XBU55ENT and 2023 residential bikes print the same 5 Vdc / 1.0 amp figure
(`xt-2023-console-usb-charging-port`).

**Engineering mode's Update App (USB) button is a different socket.** That is the technician's
software update, described with no steps
(`spirit-ent-bike-console-maintenance-menu-seven-buttons-language-wifi-ethernet-bt-updates`); it is
not this charging port, and this port carries no data.
