**Scope.** This card is limited to the SOLE touchscreen machines. The list in `applies_to` is assembled from the machines this knowledge base already treats as touchscreen, because no source document enumerates them. A touchscreen machine that is missing from the list should be added, not read as unsupported. The nine 2016 to 2020 bikes with Dyaco service manuals are deliberately out of scope: none of them has a touchscreen.

**This is the touchscreen way.** A non-touchscreen console pairs differently. See `sole-garmin-pair-non-touchscreen`.

A Garmin watch on a supported machine works like a wireless heart rate monitor. It shows heart rate and lets the customer use the built-in heart rate programs, if the machine has any. It replaces a separate chest strap.

**CAUTION: disconnect the watch from the phone first.** Turn Bluetooth off on the phone if you need to, so the watch cannot re-connect to the phone before the machine gets it.

1. Press the **GARMIN** button at the top of the screen, then press **Add Device**. Do this the first time only.
2. Put the Garmin watch into **Pairing Mode**, and make sure it is disconnected from the phone.
   - Every Garmin model does this differently. Check the Garmin user's manual or ask Garmin Support.
   - Some Garmin watches show a QR code in pairing mode. Those do not need the Garmin Connect app.
3. The watch model name appears on the console screen. Press it to select it.
   - If the watch does not appear in the list, do step 2 again.
4. The watch shows a **PIN code**. Type it on the machine. Pairing is done.

After the first pairing the machine connects to the watch on its own, as long as the watch is disconnected from any phone.

To see every watch paired to the machine, press the **GARMIN** button at the top of the screen.
