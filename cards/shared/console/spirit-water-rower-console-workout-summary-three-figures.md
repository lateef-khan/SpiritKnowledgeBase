**"The console provides a brief workout summary after completed the workout. Data displayed
includes: Total Time, Total Distance and Total Watts."** That is the whole section, in both books.

**Three figures, and none of them is what a rower asks for.** **No total strokes, no average SPM, no
average TIME/500m, no calories and no heart rate appear in the summary** - even though the console
counts all five and Stop Mode displays them
(`crw900-2021-console-sleep-ready-pause-and-stop-modes`,
`crw800h2o-console-sleep-ready-and-stop-after-four-minutes`). **The summary and Stop Mode are two
different screens showing different things**; a customer who wants the stroke count should look at
Stop Mode, not the summary.

**"Total Watts" is not defined.** The WATTS window shows *"the power consumption during training"*,
which is an instantaneous reading; the manual never says whether the summary figure is a sum, an
average or a peak. **Do not tell a customer which.**

**It is shown whenever a target program ends** - either because the target was reached or because
RESET was pressed. Both target program sections say so in their End lines.

**On the CRW900 this summary is what Bluetooth uploads.** The Bluetooth key *"upload the workout
summary to the App"* and works **only in Stop Mode**
(`crw900-2021-console-bluetooth-uploads-the-workout-summary-to-the-app`). **The CRW800H2O has no
Bluetooth**, so its summary goes nowhere but the screen.

**The Spirit air bikes print the same three-figure summary** in the same words
(`spirit-bike-console-air-bike-workout-summary-and-reset`); that is a bike card and covers no rower.
