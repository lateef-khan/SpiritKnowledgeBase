1. Open the left chain cover (the front left shroud on the recumbents). Check the computer cable and the reed switch / sensor cable are installed properly.
2. If the installation is fine, the fault is normally the **sensor with cable** or the **magnet**. Hold another magnet near the sensor to tell which one is bad, then replace that part.

**Watch the heading in the manual.** In the B94 2016, B94 2019 and LCB 2016 manuals this answer is printed under the heading "Q: No heart rate is displayed", and the heart rate answer follows under the same heading. The R92 2016, LCR 2016 and LCB 2019 manuals label it correctly as the no-speed answer.
