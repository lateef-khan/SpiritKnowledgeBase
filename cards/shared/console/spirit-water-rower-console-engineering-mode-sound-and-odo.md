**To enter:** press **Reset and Enter together for 2 seconds**. **The console beeps for 1
second** and the **DISTANCE banner blinks first**.

**To move around:** press the **up or down arrow** to select the setting section. The manual prints
the cycle as:

```
Sound  ->  ODO  ->  Sound
```

Press **Enter** to go into the highlighted setting.

**Two settings, no more.** **SOUND** turns the console audio on or off; **ODO** reads the odometer.
**There is no calibration entry, no error log, no software version, no service counter and no units
choice on this console.**

**The DISTANCE banner blinks first, but DISTANCE is not one of the settings.** The manual says both
things in consecutive sentences and never resolves it. **The Spirit air bike's engineering mode -
the same OEM console, entered with Start and Enter - does cycle DISTANCE, Sound and ODO, and DISTANCE
there is the metric/imperial choice** (`spirit-bike-console-air-bike-engineering-mode-entry`). **On
the water rowers the DISTANCE step was left out of the printed cycle**, and neither book offers a
units choice anywhere else. Both statements are reproduced as printed; **do not promise a customer a
units setting on this menu.**

**AUDIO ON/OFF, as the manual gives it:**

- Under Engineering Mode of the Audio ON/OFF setting the **SOUND** sign blinks. Press **Enter** to
  get into it.
- After pressing Enter the console displays **ON** or **OFF**. If the audio is on, the **ON** sign
  blinks; if it is off, the **OFF** sign blinks.
- Press **Enter** to confirm and the console returns to Engineering Mode.
- **30 seconds with no activity, or pressing Reset, switches the console to STANDBY Mode.**

**ODO is an hour meter, not a distance meter.** *"The ODO feature displays the total time usage for
your machine as H : M : S."* **Once the odometer passes the 2-hour mark it displays in 1-hour
increments** - *"3H would indicate 3 hours of total usage"* - and *"when the total exercise time
exceeds 1:59:59 the LCD will display the numerical hour number next to an H"*. To exit the ODO
setting, press the up or down button to switch to the other engineering mode.

**How to leave contradicts itself in the same section.** The entry paragraph says *"pressing the
Reset button will exit the Engineering Mode"*; the ODO bullet says *"pressing the Reset button twice
will exit the Engineering Mode."* **One press or two - the manual says both.** Press once, and press
again if the menu is still up.

**Nothing here survives the batteries coming out.** *"The console settings and ODO will not be reset
using the RESET Mode, however, removing the batteries will return the console to original factory
settings and the ODO value will be zero"* (`spirit-water-rower-console-four-aa-batteries`).

**This two-second hold is not the Total Reset.** That is **Reset alone** - three seconds on the
CRW900, two on the CRW800H2O
(`crw900-2021-console-total-reset-three-second-hold`, `crw800h2o-console-total-reset-two-second-hold`).

**The air rowers have no engineering mode at all.** Neither the CRW800 nor the XRW600 manual prints
one.
