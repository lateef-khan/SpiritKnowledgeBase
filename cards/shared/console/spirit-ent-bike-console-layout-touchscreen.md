**This console is a touchscreen.** It has no LED windows, no dot matrix and no program keys.

The drawing labels five things:

| Call-out | Where it is |
|---|---|
| Touchscreen | The whole display |
| Tablet-friendly reading rack | Left of the screen |
| Adjustable fan angle | Fan beside the screen |
| USB Charging port | Below the fan |
| Start, Stop and Level controls | Keypad below the screen |

**The touchscreen operates every function.** *"You can directly touch any button on the screen or
through quick button on the bottom to control functions. On the lower portion of the console there
is the Start button to begin the workout, Stop button to pause/stop programs, Level button to change
workload."* The 2024 manual words the same sentence as **"the physical buttons below the console"**.

**There is no Fan key.** The fan is a fixed grille whose **angle** is adjustable by hand; no manual
describes a switch or a speed for it. That is the opposite of the CR900ENT and CU900ENT, which have a
**Fan** button in the keypad (`cu900ent-console-layout`).

**No safety key, no headphone socket and no speaker is named** on any of the three drawings, and none
of the manuals gives a rating, a current or a data capability for the USB charging port.

**The 2022 and 2023 drawings also show the Home Screen tiles** - Exercise Programs, Internet,
Entertainment and Language, with a Home button - but only as artwork inside the picture of the
screen. Those pages carry no text layer and the tile names were read from the image; the manuals
never list them in words.

**The CU800ENT-2024 drawing is the same five call-outs**, and its machine-overview drawing on p. 8
names the console, the **Console Fan** and the **USB Charger** among the parts of the bike. Its
screen, however, is a later software generation with a status bar and Training and Media tabs:
`cu800ent-2024-console-status-bar-and-tabs`.

**The treadmill of the same generation is drawn with ten call-outs, not five** - it adds a cell phone
ledge, tray storage, accessory trays, a safety key and heart rate grips:
`ct800ent-2022-console-layout`.
