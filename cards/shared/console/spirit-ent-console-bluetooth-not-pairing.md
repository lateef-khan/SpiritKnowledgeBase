Two things the support note tells you to check:

- **If your phone is not pairing to the console, first make sure your Bluetooth headset is NOT
  paired with your phone.** A headset already holding the phone's Bluetooth audio keeps the console
  from taking it.
- **When connecting Bluetooth headphones to the console, you may have to turn on your Bluetooth
  headphones until you hear a sound confirming they found the console and are ready to pair.**

That is the whole of the note's troubleshooting. It gives no reset procedure, no maximum number of
paired devices and no way to forget a device already in the Paired Devices list.

The pairing procedure itself is `spirit-ent-console-bluetooth-pairing`, and the same note's scope
argument applies here: it names no model.
