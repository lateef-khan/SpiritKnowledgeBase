Press any button to wake the display. Press **Start Workout** to begin belt movement at **0.5 mph**, then adjust with the **Speed + / - keys** (console or screen). To slow down, hold the **Slow - key**. Press **Stop** (physical or on-screen) once to pause: the belt slows gradually to a full stop. Press **Resume** to carry on - **resume restarts at 0.5 mph for safety**. Press **Finish** to complete the workout. Incline (Elevation on the TT8) can be changed **anytime during the workout** with the Incline + / - buttons on the console or the screen.
