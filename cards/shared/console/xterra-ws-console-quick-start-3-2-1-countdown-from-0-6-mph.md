1. Put the safety key on its position on the console and turn on the power switch; the buzzer sounds and the screen shows 0:00 in standby.
2. Stand on the left and right side bars, not on the belt, hold the handrail and press **Start/Pause**. The window shows a **3-2-1 countdown** with a one-second buzzer at each number, then the motor starts.
3. The belt starts at **0.6 mph**; step onto it holding the handlebar with at least one hand. Press **+** or **−** (short or long press) to change the speed; the adjustment stops at the maximum or minimum speed limit. Press **Speed 3** or **Speed 6** to jump straight to 3 or 6 (km/h or mph, whichever the display is in).
4. Press **Start/Pause** again to pause (the motor stops); press it again to resume, and the workout data continues from where it left off ("the motion data will be superimposed in front of the data").
5. Press **Stop** at any time to stop the motor.
6. On the WS300 only, press **Incline Up / Down** while running to adjust the incline.

The displayed speed range is 1.0-13.0 km/h (0.6-8.0 mph). If the safety key is pulled out during exercise the belt stops immediately, the console refuses any operation and the window shows E07 ([safety lock card](xterra-ws-console-safety-key-removed-shows-e07-and-beeps.md)).
