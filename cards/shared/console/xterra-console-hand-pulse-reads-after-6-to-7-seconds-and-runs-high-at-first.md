Place the palms of both hands on both contact pads (the SB120 and UB120 books: hold the pulse grips); the monitor shows your current heart rate in beats per minute **after 6 to 7 seconds**.

**The first reading is high.** "Because of the contact jamming" (FB150, FB350, FB180) or "due to contact delay" (SB120, UB120), the value **may be higher than the actual pulse during the first 2 to 3 seconds**, then returns to the normal level. The measurement cannot be regarded as the basis of medical treatment. On the FB150/FB350/FB180 you first press MODE until the marker sits at PULSE; on the FB160/FB360, SET can also set a target between 40 and 240 BPM.

The pulse-sensor disclaimer on the safety page of the same books belongs to the Safety cards. The wireless-strap consoles (AIR650, SB2.5r family, SB500 family) are on their own cards.
