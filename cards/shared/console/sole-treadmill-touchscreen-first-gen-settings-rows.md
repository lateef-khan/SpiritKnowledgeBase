Tap **UNITS** for imperial/metric; **WIFI** for the connection; **DATE/TIME** for the machine clock (it stamps the workout summary; **12 and 24 hour** formats); **SOFTWARE** to check or **Update** the version (stay on the Internet); **Media Apps** to enter and update apps. Screen buttons: **Software, Bluetooth, Media Apps, Display Mode, Display Brightness, Child Lock**. **Display mode**: the console powers off automatically after **30 minutes** of inactivity. **Display Brightness**: drag the slider. **Child lock**: locks the keypad; with it enabled nothing works until you **press and hold the Display button for 3 seconds**. The ST90 prints the same rows plus Wired Network; its service cards hold the detail.
