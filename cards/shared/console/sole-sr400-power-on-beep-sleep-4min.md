Plug in the power supply and the computer powers on with **one beep**, displaying **all LCD segments for 2 seconds** (Picture 1), then enters the workout setting mode with MANUAL flashing.

The book shows both an **adaptor hole at the front** of the machine (Figure 8) and a **battery under cover A72** (Figure 11, PDF p. 8); the adaptor is the power supply the power-on step plugs in. Adaptor routing is an assembly-side fact (see_also).

After **4 minutes with no rowing and no pulse input** the console enters power-saving mode.
