Grasp the **contact heart-rate sensors** on the handlebars (the SC300 book says the stationary handlebars) or wear the chest-strap transmitter and the **heart icon starts blinking** (this may take a few seconds). The **Pulse Window** shows beats per minute and the **HR bar graph** shows the current percentage of projected maximum heart rate.

The strap is the more accurate and reliable reading; the SC200 books call it optional. Grip readings go false with user physiology and workout habits, including how the sensors are gripped and how sweaty the hands are. The SC300 book points at the Heart Rate Programs chapter for the icon.
