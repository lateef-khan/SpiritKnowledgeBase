**These owner's manuals say fifteen direct access speed buttons, not twelve.** The CT850-2020
service manual says twelve and its card is `ct850-2020-quick-start`. The two documents describe the
same machine and disagree; count the ladder on the console in front of you before quoting a number.

- Press and release **any button** to wake the display up if it is not already on.
- Press and release the **Start** button to begin belt movement at **0.5 mph**, then adjust to the
  desired speed with the Up/Down Speed buttons. You may also use the **Direct Access Speed Buttons 1
  through 15** to set a specific speed directly.
- To slow the belt, press and hold the **Speed Down** button - on the console or on the hand rail -
  to the desired speed. The Direct Access Speed Buttons **1 through 15** work here too.
- To stop the belt, press and release the red **Stop** button.

Neither manual says what speed each of the fifteen buttons selects, and neither prints a top speed
in this section. The maximum speed is a calibration value:
`ct850-2016-console-calibration-minimum-speed-0-5` and
`ct850-2020-console-calibration-minimum-speed-0-3` both set it to 15.0.

What the second and third press of Stop do is a separate behaviour: `ct850-2020-pause-stop-reset`.

**The CT800-2020 owner's manual, whose console chapter is otherwise the same document, prints
twelve.** That machine is on `ct850-2020-quick-start` and is not covered here. **The CT850-2018
console has no ladder at all** - the speed is typed on a numeric buttonpad
(`ct850-2018-console-quick-start-numeric-buttonpad`) - and **the CT850ENT-2022 touchscreen has
neither** (`ct800ent-2022-console-quick-start`).
