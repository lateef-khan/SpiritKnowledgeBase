1. Press the **left hand key** until **time of day** is displayed.
2. Press the **right hand key for 3 seconds**. The display flashes an indication of whether the clock
   is set for **12H clock or 24H military time**. Press the right hand key to change it.
3. Press the **left hand key** to switch to the **hour** setting; use the right hand key to change
   the hours.
4. Press the **left hand key** again to switch to **minutes**; use the right hand key to change the
   minutes.
5. When you are finished, **wait a few seconds** for the display to return to the start-up screen.
   There is no confirm key.

**There is no date.** The console holds a clock only; the manual describes no calendar, no day of
the week and no way to set one.

**The clock is one of the four things the left hand key selects** at the bottom of the display -
SPEED, DIST, TIME and CLOCK - so it is also how you read the time without changing it.
