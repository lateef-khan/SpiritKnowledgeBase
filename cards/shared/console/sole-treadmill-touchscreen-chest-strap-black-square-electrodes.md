**Black square electrodes, not the ribbed ovals** on the LCD-books card - and no battery line is printed.**

1. Attach the transmitter to the elastic strap with the locking parts.
2. Tighten as far as comfortable.
3. Center **the logo** (these books say 'the logo', not the SOLE logo) facing away from the chest, slightly left if needed; join the strap end through the round end with the locking parts.
4. Sit it **immediately below the pectoral muscles**.
5. **Sweat is the best conductor**; plain water pre-wets the **2 black square areas** on the belt back and both transmitter sides. Wear it a few minutes before the workout; a weak early signal firms up after warming up. Clothing over it does not matter.
6. Stay **within range** of the console; bare skin is best, or moisten the shirt under the electrodes.
