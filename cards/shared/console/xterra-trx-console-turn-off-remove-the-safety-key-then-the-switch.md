1. Remove the Safety Key.
2. Turn off the main switch on the front of the treadmill, below the motor cover.

Note: **Do not leave the Safety Key in the treadmill when not in use. Always turn off the machine.**

These books print no sleep timer in this section. The console does have a **Sleep Mode** in engineering mode — on by default, powering the console down after 5 minutes of non-use — which can be toggled off ([engineering card](xterra-trx-console-engineering-mode-program-and-speed-keys-sleep-lube-child-lock.md)).
