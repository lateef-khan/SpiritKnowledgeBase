**Two readings share one window, on their own banner line under TIME.**

- **TIME/500m** - *"Displays the time needed to row 500 meters according to current speed and average
  speed."* The console's own shorter definition on the next page: *"The TIME/500m is time per 500
  meters."*
- **AVERAGE** - *"the average time of several TIME/500m work out."*

**They alternate rather than sit side by side.** In Quick Start *"TIME/500m and TIME/500m AVERAGE
will be alternately displayed every 5 seconds"*, and in Stop Mode they alternate *"for one minute"*
(`spirit-water-rower-console-quick-start-on-the-first-strokes`).

**SPM and WATTS sit beside it and are shown in every mode.** *"SPM and Watts will be displayed during
all modes. SPM refers to Strokes per Minute. The WATTS will show the amount of work produced in a
particular time during workout."*

**The manual gives no unit, no range and no averaging window for any of the three.** It does not say
over how many strokes the AVERAGE is taken, nor how many seconds of speed the TIME/500m figure looks
back over. **Do not invent one.**

**This is a different console from the CRW800 and XRW600 air rowers, which never define the
reading.** On those machines 500M/TIME is not a window at all - it is a state the centre dot-matrix
scans to every five seconds, and none of their four manuals says what it measures
(`crw800-2024-console-scan-500m-time`). **The definition on this card belongs to the water rowers
only.**

**The Sole SR500 is another brand.** Its own 500M/TIME card (`sr500-2016-500m-time-display`) is a
Sole answer and must not be served for a Spirit machine, or the reverse.

**If the reading is unstable, the manual blames the sensor gap.** The CRW900 troubleshooting table
lists *"Inconsistent readings on the console for 500meter split time and SPM"* against a sensor gap
issue, a faulty sensor, a sensor moved out of position or a wiring harness issue, and tells you to
use the back cover of the computer as a gap tool. **That is an errors question**, and the figure on
this card is not at fault.
