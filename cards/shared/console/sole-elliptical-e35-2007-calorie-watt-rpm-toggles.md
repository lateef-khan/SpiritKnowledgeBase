In Quick Start, time counts up from zero and distance and calories accrue from zero. The **Calorie window alternates Calories (total, approximate) and Watts (100 = work to light a 100-watt bulb) every 5 seconds**; the **bottom-left window alternates RPM and Speed every 5 seconds**; the Distance window shows miles from pedal speed; the Pulse window shows heart rate from the hand sensors or a chest strap transmitter.
