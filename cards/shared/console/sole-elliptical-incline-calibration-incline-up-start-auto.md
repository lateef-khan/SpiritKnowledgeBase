Press the **Incline Up (up-arrow) key and the Start key together and hold for 5 seconds**; the incline calibration starts and runs automatically - no further keypress. If the problem persists contact Sole service. Differs from the books that need a further Start press after the hold (own card) and from the 2011 books, which name the key Ramp Up (own card).
