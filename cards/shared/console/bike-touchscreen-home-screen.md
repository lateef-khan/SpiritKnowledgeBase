The main screen of the **Training** section shows an overview of your activity and offers shortcuts to the most frequently used training modes:

- an immediate **Start**
- a manually controlled timed workout, **Timer**
- **up to 3 programs** you have marked as favourites

**In Guest mode you set your age and weight on this screen**, which helps the machine calculate your workout summary more accurately.

The top bar of the screen carries Fan On/Off, Sound Volume, Bluetooth, Settings, Favourite Programs and Log Out, with Garmin Pairing, HRM Devices, Profile and Wi-Fi also labelled. The manual labels these on the illustration but does not explain them individually.

The bottom panel has two tabs: **Training** (programs and history) and **Media** (apps and mirroring).
