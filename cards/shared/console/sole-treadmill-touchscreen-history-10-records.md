Press **History** on the main Training screen. **Up to 10 records are stored locally** on the console; linking the profile to the SOLE+ app opens the full history. Tap a record preview for the full summary; each user sees only their own workouts. The first-generation books add that history shows only workouts the current user completed, and that a linked Sole app account holds every record.
