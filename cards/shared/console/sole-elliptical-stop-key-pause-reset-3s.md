One press during a program **pauses** it for 5 minutes; press **Start** to resume. A second press ends the program and shows a workout summary. Holding Stop for **3 seconds** performs a complete reset. During program data entry Stop steps back one screen (Previous Screen). The WE55/WE95 2009-2010 books instead need a 5-second hold for reset (own card).
