The console is a slim LED strip. The overview labels a **Start/Pause** button and a **Stop** button at the left, a **Message Center** of four digit groups in the middle, **Speed + / −** buttons and **Direct Speed** buttons (6 and 3, marked MPH) at the right; the WS300 adds **Incline ▲/▼** buttons beside Start/Pause. The window captions read CAL, HEART RATE, DISTANCE, STEPS, SPEED, (INCLINE on the WS300) and TIME, with a Bluetooth icon at the right.

**Display and key distribution specs**, as the book prints them:

| Window | Range |
|---|---|
| Time | 0:00 – 99:59 |
| Speed | 1.0 – 13.0 (imperial: 0.6 – 8.0 mph) |
| Distance | 0.00 – 99.99 |
| Calories | 0 – 9999 |
| Heart rate | 50 – 200 |
| Steps | 0 – 9999 |
| Incline (WS300 only) | 0 – 10% |

**Buttons.** Start/Pause/Resume: a short press starts the treadmill, and while running a press pauses or resumes it. Stop: a short press stops the treadmill. Speed 3 and Speed 6: a short press while running jumps to 3 or 6 (km/h or mph). Speed + and −: a short or long press changes the speed until the maximum or minimum limit. Incline Up and Down (WS300): pressed while running, raise or lower the incline.

The heart-rate grips, the anti-fall function and Bluetooth are on [the functions card](xterra-ws-console-heart-rate-grips-anti-fall-and-bluetooth-ftms.md).
