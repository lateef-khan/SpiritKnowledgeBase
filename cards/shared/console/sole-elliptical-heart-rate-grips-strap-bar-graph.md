Grasp the contact sensors on the stationary handlebars or wear the chest-strap transmitter and the heart icon starts blinking (may take seconds). The Pulse Window shows beats per minute and the HR bar graph shows percent of projected maximum. The **chest strap is more accurate and reliable**; hand sensors misread with grip, sweat and physiology. How to wear the strap is its own card. The E20 books print no heart-rate reading at all (own card).
