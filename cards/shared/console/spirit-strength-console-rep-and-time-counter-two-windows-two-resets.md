This is the **whole** of what these manuals print about the console: one page, carrying two sentences
of description, one battery line, a part number and a labelled drawing of the counter face.

## What it is

> A battery-operated (batteries not included) console is available for use on your equipment.
> The console tracks workout time and number of repetitions per set.

The parts drawing on the *Parts of your unit* page labels it **Rep & Time Counter**, with the
callout sitting between *Workout Diagrams* and *Shroud* on the weight-stack tower. The manual gives
no closer mounting description than that drawing.

## The face

| Window | Label | What it counts |
|---|---|---|
| Left | **REPS** | 1 Repetition Counter - repetitions in the current set |
| Right | **TIME** | 2 Time Counter - workout time |

**Each window has its own RESET button**, one to the left of REPS and one to the right of TIME. The
drawing shows no other control: no mode key, no start key, no power switch, no unit setting.

## What the manual does not say

There is **no** on/off procedure, **no** mounting or removal step, **no** part number for the console
itself, **no** statement of what "workout time" starts and stops on, and **no** figure for how many
reps or how much time either window will hold before it rolls over. The counter displays **no error
code** of any kind.

"Available for use on your equipment" is the manual's only word on whether the counter ships fitted
or is bought separately, and it settles neither. The counter is drawn as a part of the machine on the
parts page and the warranty table on the CSD books names a *rep counter-timer* among the one-year
parts - but the batteries are explicitly not included, and the CSS warranty table has no
counter-timer line at all.

## Not the Sole SRVO

A Sole SRVO also carries a control panel on a strength machine, and it is nothing like this: it sets
resistance electronically, runs training modes and pairs with a phone app. This counter does two
things, counts and times, and controls nothing.

**The 2026 revision of the CSS-DELT book and the May 2025 update of the CSD-CPSP book print the page unchanged** - the same two windows, the same two RESET buttons, the same two-line table.
