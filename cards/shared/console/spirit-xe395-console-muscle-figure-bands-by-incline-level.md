**This is the XE395 rule, banded by incline. It is not the pedal-direction rule of the XE295, XG400
and XE895, and not the resistance-level rule of the XE795.**

**There is an anatomical figure at the top of the console.** It lights the areas the machine is
working, during **any** of the programs.

**Lower body, three degrees of engagement:** **Green** is minimal involvement, **Amber** medium,
**Red** full or heavy. **The band is set by the incline level, not by the resistance level**, and it
then differs by pedal direction:

| Pedal direction | Incline 0-7.5 | Incline 8-20 |
|---|---|---|
| **Forward** | Gluteals and Quadriceps **Amber**; Hamstrings and Calves **Green** | Gluteals **Red**; Quadriceps **Amber**; Hamstrings and Calves **Green** |
| **Reverse** | Calves, Hamstrings and Quadriceps **Amber**; Gluteals **Green** | Calves, Hamstrings and Quadriceps **Red**; Gluteals **Green** |

**Both printings give the same four bands.** The colours, the incline split at 7.5 and the muscles
named are identical in the 2010 and the 2018 manual.

**The upper body rule is the one thing that changed between the two printings.**

- The **2010** manual lights the upper body **when you are holding the swing arms, or at any time
  your hands are not on the pulse grip sensors**, and says you steer the figure by **changing the
  pedal pattern or switching your hand position during the manual program**.
- The **2018** manual drops the swing-arm clause: the upper body LEDs light **any time your hands
  are not in contact with the pulse grip sensors**, and you steer the figure by **changing the
  incline and swinging your arms**.

**The pre-set programs drive it by adjusting the incline.** Both manuals say so in the same sentence,
which is why the bands are written against incline rather than resistance on this machine.

**Two other XE rules exist and neither is this one.**

- The **XE295, XG400 and XE895** band only by **pedal direction**, with no incline in the rule at
  all: `spirit-xe-console-muscle-figure-lights-by-pedal-direction`.
- The **XE795** never lights the upper body and bands the lower body by **resistance level 1-20 and
  21-40**: `xe795-2021-console-muscle-figure-lights-by-resistance-level`.

**The XE195 and the 2023 machines have no muscle figure at all.** The XE195 console drawing of both
the 2019 and the 2021 printing shows no Muscle Activation Profile, and neither the XE395-2023 nor the
XE795-2023 manual mentions one.
