- **Measure mode - current speed.** While you ride, the computer shows your current speed in the top window.
- **MXS Mode (max speed).** Your maximum speed from the beginning to the current point.
- **AVS Mode (average speed).** Your average speed from the beginning to the current point. If the average is above or below the current speed, the arrow symbol shows.

**"Err".** If your time or distance is over the maximum limit (**time 9 hr:59 min:59 sec, distance 999.99 km**), the console cannot measure a correct average speed and shows **Err** on the display. Once the time and distance values have been reset ([auto timer and reset card](xterra-mb-console-auto-timer-motion-symbol-and-the-3-second-set-reset.md)) the average speed shows normally. This is a display condition of the AVS mode, not a fault code. The MB500 book is a scan read from the render (the console pages are OCR); the MB550 book prints the same console chapter natively and is cited alongside.
