Charge a personal device during the workout by placing it properly inside the wireless charging box/area. Set the device in **landscape orientation** for best results; its charging icon shows it is charging. Up to **10 watts** for phones supporting wireless charging. The LCD E25-2023 has a wired USB port instead (own card).
