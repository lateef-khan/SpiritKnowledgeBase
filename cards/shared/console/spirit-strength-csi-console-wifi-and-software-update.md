## Joining a network

> To connect to WIFI, press **System Settings** at the top right of the console. Next press
> **Network & Internet**. This will take you to the WIFI connection page.

The manual names no Ethernet port, no Bluetooth pairing and no cellular option. WiFi is the console's
only network.

## Updating

> Software updates can occur automatically in the Settings when the console connects to WIFI. On the
> Settings screen, press **Update**.
>
> **Update Reminder** window pops-up. Press **OK** to download software update. Press **Not Yet** to
> delay update. Return to Settings to make future updates.

Then, once the download has finished:

> When software updates have been downloaded, pop-up window indicates to **Restart** to complete the
> update, **the console will automatically restart in 0:59 seconds**.
>
> To delay the restart, select **Not Now**. Return to Settings to make future updates.
>
> **If console reconnects to WIFI then Update Reminder will automatically pop-up.**

So *Not Yet* and *Not Now* postpone rather than cancel: the reminder returns the next time the
console reaches WiFi. **Console Restart opens to the Home Screen.**

The manual prints **no** update size, **no** duration for the download, **no** USB or offline update
route, and **no** way to see or choose a version - the version string appears on the
*Has a new version!* pop-up and in Settings under **Software & Hardware**, which the manual describes
as view-only.
