The 2023 LCD consoles pair with the **Sole+ App** (not the old Sole Fitness App): download it, open it with Bluetooth on, tap the **Bluetooth icon** to find the machine, select it, then tap **DISPLAY** in the app once the console icon lights. Finish with **END & SAVE WORKOUT**; the app offers a sync to each installed cloud app (**Apple Health, Google Fit, Strava, MapMyFitness, Fitbit** - its icon stays inactive until that app is installed). Minimum OS: **iOS 13.1 or Android 8.0**. **Wireless music**: search the phone's Bluetooth menu for **Bt-speaker**. **Bluetooth chest strap (BLE 5.0)** works even mid-App: wear it, connect, and the console shows the heart rate over Bluetooth. The strap **icon lights up** while connected and goes off when disconnected.
