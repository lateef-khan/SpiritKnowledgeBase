When you press the **Display** button the data shown on the screen changes.

**If you do not press the Display button a second time, the screen returns to the workout screen after 3 seconds.** That is the whole of what the manual says about the timing, so a screen that appears to snap back on its own is behaving as documented.

The manual illustrates the data screens with a page of screen captures labelled Segment Time, RPM, Distance, METs, Calories, Watts, Time, Pulse, Target HR, Maint 50 RPM, Work KGM, Work Watts and Target 50, grouped under Manual Program, Program/Custom, Fit-Test and HRC1/HRC2.

**The manual gives no press order for these screens.** It does not say which reading follows which, unlike the upright and recumbent manuals which print an explicit sequence. Do not invent an order from the picture captions.
