The calorie window **displays the cumulative calories burned at any given time during your
workout.**

**Note: this is only a rough guide used for comparison of different exercise sessions, and is not to
be used for medical purposes.**

Entering your weight when you program a workout is what makes the reading closer to correct; that
half of the fact is printed in the Programs section of these manuals, not here.

The five 2015 XT owner's manuals print the same two sentences word for word.

**The XT685 2010 owner's manual prints these two sentences on its p. 20**, so this card covers that
machine.

**Three residential XE ellipticals print them word for word too** - the **XE795-2021** (both the
February and the November printing), the **XE395-2023** and the **XE795-2023** - so this card is no
longer treadmill-only.

**A longer and blunter calorie warning is printed elsewhere in most XE manuals**, and it is not the
same text: `spirit-xe-console-calorie-note-you-keep-burning-at-an-accelerated-rate-for-an-hour`. The
XE795-2021 is the one machine that prints a shortened version of that one, calling the readings
"only an estimate".
