**Twenty-four columns of dots, 10 high.** The dots show approximate effort only. In Manual Operation the window builds a profile 'picture' as values change; the columns divide the total program time (1/24th each on the AS77). When **Speed** is lit the Speed profile shows; when **Incline** is lit the Incline profile shows. Press **Enter** to change views; scrolling through both profiles starts an automatic alternation. Contradiction ruled: the S77-2013 book prints 'The 20 columns are divided into 1/20th of the total time' inside its 24-column section – the card follows the section's own 24-column figure and notes the stray sentence.
