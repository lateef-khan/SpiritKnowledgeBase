1. Plug the bike into an appropriate wall outlet.
2. Turn on the **power switch located at the front of the bike**.

When power is connected the console powers up automatically.

**The manual prints no supply rating for this machine** — no DC voltage, no current, no mains figure. It names only the wall outlet and the switch at the front. Do not carry a rating from another year onto it.

**There is no self-test or odometer boot screen described for this console.** The Power Up section stops at the switch. What the console does next is governed by the Sleep mode setting, which is a separate three-way choice on this machine.
