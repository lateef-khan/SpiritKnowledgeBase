**The graphics display (dot matrix)** shows **work profiles and the symmetry graph**. When a
program begins it draws the workload profile - the resistance level changes through the program. The
**peak resistance level** is set during program setup and **can be adjusted during the workout**;
when you change it **the profile picture does not change**, but the message window shows the new
level setting.

**The data display windows** report **spm (steps per minute), calories, time, steps (total step
count) and pulse**. **Resistance level and watt measurements sit on either side of the graphic
display.**

**The message window is the main display for programming instructions and for measurements during a
program**, and **what it shows varies with the program**. The measurements it can show are:

- **average watts, left leg and right leg separately**
- **METS**
- **symmetry**
- **segment time**

**Per-leg watts and a symmetry figure are unique to this console** in the whole Spirit range. Every
other Spirit stepper reports one watts figure for the machine
(`spirit-stepper-console-led-data-windows-steps-and-vertical-distance`).

**To the right of the dot matrix is a Heart Rate Bar Graph.** Grasping the hand pulse sensors, **or
wearing a heart rate chest belt transmitter**, starts the measurement (this may take a few seconds);
the **Pulse window** then reads in beats per minute and the bar graph shows **percent of maximum
heart rate**.

**The bar graph is wrong until the age is right.** The manuals say so in the paragraph:
*"NOTE: Enter the correct age in Set Up for the Bar Graph to be accurate."* The pulse number itself
does not depend on the age; the percentage does.

**The manual names no strap brand, no protocol and no pairing step** - it says "a heart rate chest
belt transmitter" and nothing further.
