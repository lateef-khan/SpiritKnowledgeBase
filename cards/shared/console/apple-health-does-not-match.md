Both the SOLE+ app and the Apple Watch upload records to Apple Health. So Apple Health can hold two records for one workout.

If the record came from the **Apple Watch**, it will not match the data shown in the SOLE+ app.

The source shows two screenshots of the two records side by side. It does not say which record is correct, and it gives no fix.

**Related.** How to connect the watch in the first place is in `sole-apple-watch-heart-rate`.
