**"As strokes are detected, the console will immediately launch QUICK START."** There is no
key to press. Pulling the handle is the whole procedure.

**Everything starts counting at once:** **TIME, DISTANCE, CALORIES, SPM, WATTS, STROKES and TOTAL
STROKES**. **TIME/500m and TIME/500m AVERAGE are alternately displayed every 5 seconds**
(`spirit-water-rower-console-time-500m-and-average`).

**Heart rate behaves the same way as in every other mode.** *"When there's heart rate input, the icon
will stay lit and the heart icon will flash. Current heart rates will be shown. In the absence of
heart rate input, three dashes - - - will be shown continuously"*
(`spirit-water-rower-console-heart-rate-panel-three-dashes`).

**One wording difference between the two books, on the resistance reading.**

- **CRW900:** *"The 7-segment display will show level according to **VR adjustments**."*
- **CRW800H2O:** *"The 7-segment display will show level according to **the settings**."*

**On the CRW800H2O "the settings" is the LEVEL key** - you tell the console how much water is in the
tank, L1 to L6 (`crw800h2o-console-water-level-button-six-steps`). **On the CRW900 the VR is a
variable resistor**, and its own troubleshooting page prints a VR calibration under *"Trouble with
resistance"*: hold **ENTER + UP + DOWN for 2 seconds**, set the low increment, press ENTER, set the
high increment, press ENTER, and *"configured VR range will automatically be divided into 10 even
increments."* **That is an errors question**, and the CRW800H2O has no VR procedure at all.

**Quick Start is not a program and the manual gives it no end condition.** It says nothing about how
the session finishes; that is Stop Mode's job
(`crw900-2021-console-sleep-ready-pause-and-stop-modes`,
`crw800h2o-console-sleep-ready-and-stop-after-four-minutes`).

**The air rowers have no Quick Start.** On the CRW800 and XRW600 the equivalent is pressing
**Start/Stop** from idle, or simply pulling the handle so the speed sensor drops the console into
**Manual** mode (`crw800-2024-console-button-functions`). **Different console, different name.**
