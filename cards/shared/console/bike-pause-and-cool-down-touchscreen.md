Press the physical **STOP** key, or the **Pause** icon on the Level panel, to pause the workout.

From the pause screen you can:

- get back to training,
- start a **cool down**, or
- end your workout and see the summary.

**After 5 minutes of inactivity in the Pause mode, the workout ends automatically.**

The Level panel on the right side of the screen is what adjusts resistance during the workout.
