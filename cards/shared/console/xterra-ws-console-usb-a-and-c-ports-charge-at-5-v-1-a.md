The parts diagram labels **B. USB Type A & C Charger Port** on the console, next to **A. Tablet Holder** and **C. LED Screen**, with the note: "USB A and C ports are for charging purposes only; USB is **5V / 1A**."

The book says nothing about data, and the console has no way to save a workout to USB. The tablet holder itself is fitted in assembly step 3 and its angle is adjusted by sliding the back panel of the bracket (the Assembly section holds that).
