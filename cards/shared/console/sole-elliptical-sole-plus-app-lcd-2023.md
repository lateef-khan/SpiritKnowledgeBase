The machine carries a **Bluetooth transceiver** for the free **SOLE+ App** (Apple Store / Google Play), which also syncs workouts to Apple Health, Google Fit, Strava, MapMyFitness or Fitbit. Enable Bluetooth, tap the **top-right icon** to find Sole equipment, tap **Connect**; the console Bluetooth icon lights up. A Bluetooth chest-strap transmitter can connect even while the App is connected. **Wireless music**: search the phone Bluetooth menu for **Bt-speaker** and connect. Shorter than the treadmill 2023 LCD page, which adds DISPLAY/END & SAVE steps and minimum-OS figures (see_also).
