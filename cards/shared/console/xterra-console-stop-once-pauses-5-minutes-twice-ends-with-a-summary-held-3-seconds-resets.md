"The Stop/Reset key actually has several functions":

- **Press once during a program - pause for five minutes.** Get a drink, answer the phone; press **Start** to resume.
- **Press twice during a workout - the program ends** and a summary of your workout is displayed.
- **Hold for three seconds - the console performs a complete reset.**

The five-minute pause can be switched off in engineering mode, after which one press of Stop ends the program ([SB500 engineering card](../../sb500-2020/console/engineering-mode-start-stop-enter-5-seconds-with-lcd-test-type-set-and-key-tone.md), [SB4.5r and FS5.9e card](xterra-console-engineering-mode-start-stop-enter-5-seconds-with-key-test-security-sleep-and-units.md)). The SB4.5r, FS5.8e and FS5.9e books are scans read from the render; the SB500 2014 printing is a scan of the same text as the 2020 export.
