The 2007-2010 books print **no maintenance/diagnostic menu, no engineering mode and no incline calibration** - diagnosis runs through power/standby behaviour and the troubleshooting chapter (errors' territory). The first console menus appear in the 2011 books. Do not apply any Start+Stop+Enter menu or calibration card to these machines.
