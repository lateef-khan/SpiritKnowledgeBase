Plug the treadmill into an appropriate wall outlet, then turn on the power switch at the **front of the treadmill, below the motor hood**. The safety key must be installed: the treadmill will not operate without it.

The treadmill then enters **idle mode**, which the manual calls the starting point for operation. These five books print nothing about a software version or odometer readout at power-up; the books that do are on [the software-version card](xterra-tr-console-power-on-scrolls-the-software-version.md) and [the odometer card](xterra-trx-console-power-on-shows-software-version-then-odometer.md).

The 2024 book for the TRX2500 calls the section "Power Up" and says the safety key must be installed "as the treadmill will not operate without it"; the four older books say the same under "Getting Started".
