The console carries:

- **Quick level keys**, on the left and right sides of the console
- **Cooling fan** and a **fan power switch**
- **Data display**
- **Dot matrix**, which shows program profiles
- **Message window**, labelled **Time, Program Name, Distance, RPM, Calories, Laps, Level, Watts and Pulse**
- **Program buttons**: Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, Interval, Custom, Fit-Test, 2 HR
- **Control keys** and a **Display** key
- **Audio in jack** for MP3, CD or smartphone
- **Headphone jack**

The quick level keys are the row of numbered resistance shortcuts; the Quick Start section lists which numbers they carry.

The panel diagram does not label a speaker, although the manual's audio paragraph says the console has built-in speakers.
