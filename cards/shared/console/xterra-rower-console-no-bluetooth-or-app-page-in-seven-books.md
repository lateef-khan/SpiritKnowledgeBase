The ERG160, ERG220, ERG400, ERG500, ERG600W, ERG650W and ERG700 owner's manuals contain **no Bluetooth page, no app page, no Kinomap steps, no FTMS note and no compliance page listing Bluetooth**. The only wireless thing any of them names is the **chest strap** the ERG500, ERG600W, ERG650W and ERG700 take for a pulse. Every page of each book, including the rendered pages the text layer misses, was searched.

So none of these consoles pairs with a phone; a customer who wants Kinomap on an Xterra rower needs an ERG180, ERG550W, ERG750W or ERG800W ([Kinomap card](xterra-rower-console-kinomap-pairing-and-the-console-goes-blank-while-an-app-is-connected.md), [ERG800W card](../../erg800w-2023/console/app-connection-by-the-bluetooth-name-printed-on-the-back-of-the-console.md)). Note that the ERG550W's own siblings, the ERG600W and ERG650W, print no app page although their consoles look alike; the ERG550W book is the one that added it. What each of these consoles does do is on its own key card - [ERG160](../../erg160-2020/console/single-mode-key-with-scan-every-6-seconds-and-six-readouts.md), [ERG220](../../erg220-2023/console/single-mode-key-two-row-lcd-with-spm-to-120-and-an-arrow-marker.md), [ERG400](../../erg400-2015/console/start-stop-reset-enter-up-down-keys-and-the-ten-window-labels.md), [ERG500](../../erg500-2018/console/mode-reset-up-down-keys-and-six-windows-with-distance-in-miles-to-99-9.md), [water rowers](xterra-ergw-console-keys-up-down-enter-start-stop-reset-and-recovery.md), [ERG700](../../erg700-2022/console/five-keys-and-the-eleven-mode-order-behind-the-mode-key.md).
