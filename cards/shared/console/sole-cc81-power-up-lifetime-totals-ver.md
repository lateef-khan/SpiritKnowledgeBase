At power-up the console lights **all characters**. The **Time window** shows the total time the stepper has been used since new, the **Total Step window** shows the total steps since new, and the **Message Window** shows the software version (i.e. VER 1.0). After **3 seconds** the console goes to the start-up display and is ready for use.

The book abbreviates the readings **FLS** (representative floors) and **FPM** (floors per minute).
