**One minute, and it is off - not asleep.**

- **Waking it.** Pressing any key button wakes a console that has been turned off. **The LCD turns
  on with full display and beeps for 2 seconds**, then enters **idle mode**.
- **Starting without touching it.** *"When there is speed signal input with the speed sensor
  detected, it directly enters Manual workout mode."* Pulling the handle is enough.
- **Switching off.** *"In any mode during turning on, if there is not any button activated or no
  rowing activity lasts for 1 minute, it turns off automatically."*

**The two-second beep and full display is normal.** The Operating Instruction page repeats it: *"The
screen is with full display and the buzzer beeps for two seconds after turning on."* A customer
describing a two-second beep at start-up is describing the machine working.

**One minute here, four and a half on the CRW800.** The CRW800 console turns its LCD off after **30
seconds** of inactivity and only goes to sleep **270 seconds** after that - 300 seconds in total.
**Do not quote the CRW800 figure for an XRW600**; they are five times apart, and this is the single
largest difference between two console chapters that are otherwise word for word.

**The manual gives no separate sleep state and no wake-on-motion after power off.** It names exactly
two states, idle and off. A rower left mid-workout for a minute loses the display; the manual does
not say whether the workout figures survive being woken.

**There is no pause on this console.** The Reset key is valid only in stopping mode, and holding it
for three seconds *"turns on the console again"* (`crw800-2024-console-button-functions`).
