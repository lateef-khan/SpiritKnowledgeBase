| Readout | As printed |
|---|---|
| SPEED | Current training speed, maximum 99.9 KM/H or MILE/H |
| RPM | Current pedal rotations per minute |
| TIME | Accumulates 00:00 to 99:59, or counts down from a preset target |
| DIST | Accumulates 0.00 up to 999.9 KM or mile, or counts down from a preset |
| ODO | Total accumulated distance 0.0 to 999.9 KM or mile |
| CAL | Accumulates 0 to 9999, or counts down from a preset |
| WATT | Current watt |
| HEART RATE | Current heart rate in beats per minute |
| TARGET H.R. | The preset target heart rate |
| PROGRAM / LEVEL | The program number and the level bars ([24 and 24](xterra-console-24-programs-and-24-levels-in-8-bars-of-3.md) or [13 and 16](../../fs15-2019/console/13-programs-and-16-levels-in-8-bars-of-2.md)) |

**Music.** "There is an audio-input port on the top of the monitor. Users can connect an MP3, smart phone or other audio source to the port." The SB2.5r, FS2.5, FS3.5 and RSX1500 books print this line; **the FS1.5 book does not**, and its keypad picture shows no port. The display face reads START, PROGRAM, LEVEL across the top of the bar graph and TIME, SPEED (MPH), DIST, CAL, TARGET H.R. and HEART RATE along the bottom. MODE swaps RPM/SPEED, ODO/DIST and WATT/CAL in their shared windows ([keys card](xterra-console-keys-start-stop-held-3-seconds-up-down-enter-recovery-and-a-mode-toggle.md)). None of the five books prints an auto-off time for this console.
