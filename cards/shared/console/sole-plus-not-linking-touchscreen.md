**This is for a link that never worked.** If the app used to sync and then stopped, use `sole-plus-stopped-syncing-workouts` instead.

SOLE+ links to a touchscreen machine by scanning a QR code. It fails when the machine or the app is out of date, or when either one has no internet. They do **not** need to be on the same network.

1. Log in to the **Guest Profile**.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Check the treadmill is on the customer's Wi-Fi network.
4. Check for machine software updates. You may need to unplug the machine and plug it back in for it to look for updates.
5. Check the Google Play Store or Apple App Store for pending SOLE+ updates.

If SOLE+ still will not connect with the QR code, **replace the console assembly**.
