The Pulse (Heart Rate) console window shows current heart rate in beats per minute from **both stainless steel sensors on the front cross bar**; the value shows anytime the upper display receives a Grip Pulse signal. **No chest strap is printed anywhere in this book** (zero hits for chest, strap, transmitter or wireless across the full text, plus the console chapter read in full) – the only book in the wave with grips-only heart rate.
