| Model | Favorites on the Home screen |
|---|---|
| F80-2023 | Up to **3** |
| F85-2023, TT8-2023, ST90-2023 | Up to **6** |
| F89-2023 | Up to **12** (classes, collections or programs) |

To like one, open its setup page and tap the **ribbon** icon in the upper left corner. Labeled programs live under the **Favorites** tab; remove the label with **Edit Favorites** or by un-tapping the ribbon.
