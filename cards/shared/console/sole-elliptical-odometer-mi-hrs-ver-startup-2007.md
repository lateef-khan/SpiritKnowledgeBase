At first power-on all lights turn on; when they go off the dot matrix shows a software version (VER 1.0) and the message window shows an odometer of hours used plus virtual miles gone, printed like **ODO 123 MI 123 HRS** (VE/WE books: hours left, miles right). After a few seconds the console reaches the startup display with scrolling profiles and start-up message. The 2011+ LCD generation shows miles and hours in separate windows instead (own card).
