1. While the treadmill is running, press the **Start/Stop** key once to pause. The tread-belt slowly decelerates to a stop, the **incline goes to zero percent**, and the Time, Distance and Calorie readings also stop.
2. To resume, press Start/Stop again; speed and incline return to their previous settings.
3. If the **ENTER** key is **held down while paused**, the console resets and returns to the idle (start-up) screen.

The service manuals describe the same key as a cycle: first press starts (after a 3-second countdown), second press pauses, third press starts again, and so on ([service-manual card](xterra-trx-console-keys-in-ready-and-run-mode-per-the-service-manual.md)). These three books print no five-minute pause limit; their engineering Sleep Mode setting powers the console down after 5 minutes of non-use instead ([engineering card](xterra-trx-console-engineering-mode-program-and-speed-keys-sleep-lube-child-lock.md)).
