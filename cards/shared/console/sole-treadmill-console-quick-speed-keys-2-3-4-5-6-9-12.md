The console prints rapid **speed** keys **2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9 and 12** (TT8-2013 prints the same set as '2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, or 12'), usable on the console or, where fitted, the handgrip Fast/Slow keys alongside them. Other books in the wave print different sets: 2-4-6-8-12, 2-4-6-8-10-12, 1-2-4-6-8-10, 2-through-10 or 2-through-12.
