Charge a personal device from the on-console **USB Power Port** while the machine is on: connect a compatible cable (not included) and check the device icon. The port gives **up to 5 Vdc / 1.0 amp** under USB 2.0 rules; the icon may stay off on power-hungry devices that still charge slowly. **No workout data can be saved through this port; charging only.** Double the 0.5 A of the 2019 books (own card).
