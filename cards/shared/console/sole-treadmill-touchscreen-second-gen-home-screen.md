The main screen of the **Training** section shows an activity overview with shortcuts to the most-used training modes: an immediate **Start**, a manually controlled timed workout (**Timer**), and favorite programs (count differs per model; separate card). In **Guest** mode, age and weight entered here feed the workout summary. **All Programs** opens the full catalog with tabs **Programs, Fitness Tests, Templates, Favorites**; tapping a program card opens its setup page, where parameters adjust by dragging the ruler, tapping +/-, or tapping the number for a numpad. The F89 adds video classes and See All blocks; the ST90 home also offers Free Mode.
