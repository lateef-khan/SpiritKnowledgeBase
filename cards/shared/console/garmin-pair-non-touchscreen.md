**This is the non-touchscreen way.** A touchscreen console pairs differently, with Add Device and a PIN. See `sole-garmin-pair-touchscreen`.

A non-touchscreen console only supports **one directional** communication, over the wireless HRM protocol. There is no pairing menu. The watch simply broadcasts, and the machine listens.

1. On the Garmin watch, turn on **Broadcast During Activity**. Some models call it **Broadcast Heart Rate**.
   - Look under **Sensors & Accessories > Wrist Heart Rate**, or under the Bluetooth settings.
   - The exact steps change from model to model. Check the watch owner's manual.
2. Start a workout on the machine. It connects to the watch on its own.
