1. **Clear error codes**: power the treadmill off, wait a few seconds, then power it back on.
2. **Log in**: use a personal account or the Guest profile.
3. **Open the engineering menu**:
   - Tap the gear icon in the top left to open Settings.
   - At the top of the Settings screen is the word "Settings". It does not look like a button, but it is.
   - Tap that word rapidly several times until the Engineering Menu appears.
4. **Start calibration**: tap **Engineering Mode**. Step off the treadmill, because it moves on its own. Press the calibration button in the bottom right corner. The machine runs through the speed and incline ranges by itself.
5. **After**: the machine should reboot to the login screen once everything stops. If it does not, press the home button at the top to return to the settings menu.
