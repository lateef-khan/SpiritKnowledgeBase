Download the free **Sole+ App** (Apple Store / Google Play; QR code in the book). With phone Bluetooth on, tap the app's **Bluetooth icon** to find the machine and select it for connect; the console Bluetooth icon lights when synced. Tap **DISPLAY** in the app to watch the workout live. Finish with **END & SAVE WORKOUT** in the app; it offers a sync to each installed cloud app (**Apple Health, Google Fit, Strava, MapMyFitness, Fitbit** - an icon stays inactive until that app is installed). Minimum OS: **iOS 13.1 or Android 8.0**.
