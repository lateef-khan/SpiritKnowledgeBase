"This meter uses **2 AA batteries, which are included**. **Changing the batteries will reset all values.** If there is a problem with the display, try changing the batteries first. When changing the batteries, change both of them. Do not mix battery types. Do not mix old and new batteries. Dispose of old batteries according to your regional guidelines." (ERG600W.) The ERG550W's console page opens "Install 2 AA batteries (included)"; the ERG650W's assembly step 3 opens "Place the 2 AA batteries into the Console (No. 54)" and says nothing about them being included.

None of the three books says where the battery case is; the ERG600W service manual's console page shows the console coming off its support with one M8*70 bolt and four M5*12 screws holding a support plate on its back (an assembly card). Putting new cells in starts the water-level prompt (ERG550W, ERG650W) or the clock setup (ERG600W) again ([water-level card](xterra-ergw-console-power-up-asks-for-the-water-level-one-to-six.md), [clock card](../../erg600w-2021/console/power-up-sets-the-clock-calendar-and-alarm-then-the-sport-screen.md)). The Spirit water rowers take **four** AA ([Spirit card](spirit-water-rower-console-four-aa-batteries.md)); the ERG750W book names no cell at all ([ERG750W card](../../erg750w-2025/console/battery-powered-but-neither-book-prints-the-cell-type-or-where-it-goes.md)).
