**The 1/4-mile track (0.4 km) is displayed around the dot matrix window.** A **flashing dot**
indicates your progress round it, and **in the centre of the track there is a lap counter for
reference**.

All three manuals print this two-sentence section word for word.

**It is always there.** These manuals describe no button that turns the track on or off, and no
setting that switches the dot matrix between a profile and a track. The ring is a separate set of
lights around the outside of the matrix window, not a display mode of it.

**That is what separates this from the older console.** On the CU800-2012 the quarter-mile track is
drawn *inside* the dot matrix, and a **Display** button switches the window between Profile and
Track and back: `spirit-ce-console-display-button-four-window-sets`.

**The manuals give no lap count, no reset behaviour and no unit switch for this window.** Whether the
counter follows the Units setting in engineering mode is not stated
(`spirit-ce800-console-maintenance-menu-function-and-service`).
