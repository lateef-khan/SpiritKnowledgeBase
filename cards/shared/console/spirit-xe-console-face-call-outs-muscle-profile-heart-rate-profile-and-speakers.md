**The drawing that opens the CONSOLE OPERATION chapter labels eight things**, and every manual listed
here prints the same eight words:

| Call-out as printed | Where it sits |
|---|---|
| **Muscle Activation Profile** | Top of the console |
| **Large LCD with scrolling feedback and scrolling message center** | Centre |
| **Heart Rate % Profile** | The bar graph right of the LCD |
| **Integrated Speakers for MP3 Player** | Upper right of the housing |
| **Swivel Fan to keep you cool** | Left of the housing |
| **Ten innovative programs offer a variety of work-outs** | The program key row |
| **Convenient cargo compartment for keys, phone, or MP3 player** | Lower left |
| **Easy-Touch Control Keys** | Bottom |

**"Ten innovative programs" is a label on a picture, not a program list.** The manuals do not name
the ten programs in this drawing, and the key legends underneath differ from machine to machine.

**The drawing is the only place some of these are named.** On the XE295 and XG400 of both printings
the speakers appear nowhere else in the book - there is no Speakers paragraph - so this call-out plus
the audio cable in the hardware pack is the whole of what those manuals say:
`spirit-xe-console-speakers-take-an-audio-cable-and-have-no-volume-control`.

**The XE895 wears the XE795's face and the XE295's behaviour.** Its drawing carries all eight
call-outs exactly as the XE795-2018 does, but everything the console then does follows the XE295
family: **20 resistance levels** (the XE795 has 40), the muscle figure banded by **pedal direction**
(the XE795 bands by resistance level), an engineering menu ending in **Motor Test and Safety** (the
XE795 ends in DA Test), and **power from the cord** (the XE795 has a generator you pedal to wake).
**Do not answer an XE895 question from an XE795 card because the two faces match.**

**The XE195 prints a shorter drawing and is not covered here.** Both its printings drop the **Muscle
Activation Profile** and the **Heart Rate % Profile**, and label **"Six innovative programs"** rather
than ten. **The 2019 printing does carry the Integrated Speakers call-out and the 2021 printing drops
it** - the only call-out that changed between two printings of the same machine anywhere in this
family.

**The XE395-2010 drawing is a different one**, with a dot matrix and a message center called out
separately: `xe395-2010-console-panel-call-outs`. **The XE395ENT is a touchscreen** and shares none of
this: `xe395ent-2021-console-panel-call-outs-and-the-audio-jack`.

**The 2023 machines replaced the drawing.** The XE395-2023 and XE795-2023 print a FEATURES page and a
workout-mode call-out drawing instead, with no muscle profile and no cargo compartment.
