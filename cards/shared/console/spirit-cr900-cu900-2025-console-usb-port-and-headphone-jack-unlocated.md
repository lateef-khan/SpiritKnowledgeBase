**Two lists in each manual name parts the console drawing does not.**

- The **MACHINE CARE schedule** has a row **"USB Port - Inspect"**, weekly.
- The **warranty wear-items footnote** lists, among the wear items, a **headphone jack** and a **USB
  port**.
- The **CONSOLE SCREEN - OVERVIEW** drawing names neither. Its six call-outs are the fan, the
  ultra-bright white LED display, the heart rate graph, the lap counter, the LED matrix window and the
  tablet rack, and nothing else: `ce900-2025-console-screen-overview`.

**So the machine has both, and no document says where they are or what they do.** Neither manual
gives a USB rating, a current, a charging claim or any statement about whether the port carries data;
neither says whether the headphone jack is fed by a TV, a phone or anything at all - these consoles
have no tuner, no Bluetooth and no app.

**The 2018 printings of the same two bikes never mention USB at all**, in any list, and their console
drawing names no port either: `spirit-cr900-cu900-console-overview-blue-led-and-tablet-rack`.

**Do not answer this from a CR800 or CU800.** Those consoles do have a **USB Charger** labelled on the
console face, in the lower right - but that is a different console and its manuals give no rating
either: `spirit-ce-console-led-console-face`.
