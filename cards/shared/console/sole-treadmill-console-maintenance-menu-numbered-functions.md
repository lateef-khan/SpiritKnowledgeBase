The 2007-2010 books number the items instead of lettering them, and their Security item gives no unlock procedure (the 2011/2013 books unlock with Start plus Enter for 3 seconds).

1. Press and hold **Start, Stop and Enter** at the same time, until the display shows **Engineering mode** (it may say maintenance menu, depending on version). Press Enter.
2. Scroll with the **up and down** keys; **Stop** returns to the previous menu selection. The selections are:
   - **1) Calibration** (used to verify calibration).
   - **2) Factory settings** (do not change unless instructed by the Sole service department).
   - **3) Security** (locks the console keyboard).
   - **4) Functions**: **i. Sleep** (sleep mode on/off); **ii. Pause** (pause mode on/off); **iii. Maintenance** (reset lube message and odometer readings); **iv. Units** (English or Metric); **v. Grade return** (returns elevation to lowest setting when pause is pressed).
   - **5) Display test** (lights all LED lights for testing).
   - **6) Key test** (tests that all keys are functioning).
