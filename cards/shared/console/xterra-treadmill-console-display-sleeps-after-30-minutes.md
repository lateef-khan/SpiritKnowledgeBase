1. The display automatically turns off (goes to sleep) after **30 minutes** with no key operation. In sleep mode the treadmill draws very little current — "about as much as your television when it is turned off".
2. Remove the tether cord.
3. Turn off the main switch on the front of the treadmill, below the motor cover.

The TR150 and TRX1400 service manuals say that in SLEEP mode pressing any button returns the treadmill to READY mode. The TR300 book prints **5 minutes** instead ([TR300 card](../../tr300-2021/console/display-sleeps-after-5-minutes.md)), and the TR6.6 and TR6.4 ship in an in-store display mode that never sleeps until it is switched off ([display mode card](xterra-tr-console-exit-in-store-display-mode-stop-enter-display.md)).
