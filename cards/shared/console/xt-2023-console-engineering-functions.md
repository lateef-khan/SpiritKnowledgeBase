**These manuals list five settings here and have no Key Tone entry. The XT185 and XT285 list six, adding Key Tone; see `xt-2023-console-engineering-functions-with-key-tone`.**

Press Enter on **Functions** to access the settings, and the **Speed + or -** arrow to scroll.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Sleep Mode | Turn on to have the console power down automatically after **15 minutes** of inactivity |
| Pause Mode | Turn on to allow **5 minutes** of pause; turn off to have the console pause indefinitely |
| Maintenance | See the note below |
| Units | Sets the display to readout in English or Metric display measurements |
| BEEP | After closing, there is no sound for key operation |

**The Maintenance entry is not described the same way in all three manuals.** The **XT385 and XT485** manuals print it as **"Maintenance (Reset lube message and odometer readings)"**. The **XT685** manual prints the word **"Maintenance"** with **no description at all**, and that manual has no deck lubrication section and no lube message anywhere in it - see `xt685-2023-belt-deck-general-maintenance`. Nothing in the XT685 manual says whether its Maintenance entry resets a lube message, an odometer, or both.

**The XT385-2023 and XT485-2023 service manuals print this list differently, and the XT685-2023
service manual does not print it at all.** Their Functions list is: I **SLEEP Mode - Turn ON to have
the console power down automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity** (not 15); II Pause Mode, 5
minutes or indefinite; III Maintenance - reset lube message and odometer readings; IV Units; V **Key
Tone** - turns the key-press beep on or off. So the service manuals say **30 minutes and Key Tone**
where these owner's manuals say **15 minutes and BEEP**. Both are reproduced as printed; nothing
resolves the sleep figure, and Key Tone and BEEP describe the same switch.

**What Sleep Mode also does, from the same service manuals' E0 note:** *"When choose ON, this is mean
display off after removed safety key. When choose OFF which is display on and appear E0 after removed
safety key."* So with Sleep Mode ON the console blanks when the safety key is pulled; with it OFF the
display stays lit and shows E0. The service manuals say to check that setting before suspecting the
safety switch hardware. The XT685-2023 book prints the same note but the console shows "PLEASE
REPLACE THE SAFETY KEY" rather than E0, and "simulation idle mode" with the setting off.
