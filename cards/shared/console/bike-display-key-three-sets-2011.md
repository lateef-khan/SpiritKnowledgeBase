The Display Button is **to the left of the display**. Each press shows the next set of information.

1. Elapsed **Time**, **Program Name** and **Distance** (in miles) — this is what shows first.
2. **Speed** (in mph), **RPM** (pedalling speed) and **Watts**.
3. **Calories**, **Level** (work level from 1-20) and **Pulse** (heart rate in beats per minute).
4. One more press enters **scan mode**, where the displayed information changes every **4 seconds**.

The manual explains Watts as an indication of work level: a reading of 100 watts means you are doing enough work to light a 100 watt light bulb.

**This is not the order later bikes use.** From the 2013 model year the same paragraph gives a single list starting with Laps, and the number of readings and their grouping change. Do not read this list onto a later console.
