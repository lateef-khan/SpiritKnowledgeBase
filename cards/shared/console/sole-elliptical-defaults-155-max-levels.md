Defaults are **Age 35, Weight 155 lb, Time 30:00** (the VE E35 section prints Time **20:00**) with a different maximum work level per program: **Hill 7, Fatburn 5, Cardio 7, Strength 8, Interval 7**. Entering a new Age/Weight saves it as the new default (own card). The E75/E95 print 150 lb instead (own card).
