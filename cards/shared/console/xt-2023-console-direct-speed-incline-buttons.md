**You are able to set your speed and incline setting quickly by pressing the buttons on the
console.** Choose the desired speed and/or incline level and the treadmill automatically adjusts to
that level, so you do not have to hold a button down until it reaches the value.

- **Speed: press the 1 through 12 button on the console.**
- **Incline: press the 0 through 15 button on the console.**

**These ranges are not the same across the family.** The XT185 reaches speed 10 and incline 10, and
the XT285 reaches speed 12 and incline 12. Check the machine before quoting a maximum.
