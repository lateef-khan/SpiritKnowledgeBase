The Pulse (Heart Rate) window shows current heart rate in beats per minute. Use **both stainless steel sensors on the front cross bar** to display pulse, **or wear the wireless chest strap**; the value shows anytime the upper display receives a pulse signal. The AF63 book adds that the strap is **not included** with the F63 and must be console-compatible if bought elsewhere. Unlike the 2007-2010 books, these books print no ban on grip use during Heart Rate Control.
