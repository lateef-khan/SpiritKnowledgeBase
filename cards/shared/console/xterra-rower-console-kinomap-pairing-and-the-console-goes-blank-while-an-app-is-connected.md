"This console connects to 3rd party fitness apps on your phone or tablet via Bluetooth. **Once console is connected to your phone or tablet via Bluetooth, the console will not display. Your workout feedback will be displayed on your device.**" That is expected, not a fault. "This machine is compatible with other 3rd party fitness apps using FTMS protocol."

**Kinomap, as printed** (ERG550W and ERG750W): 1. Open the Kinomap app and click the lower right corner to add devices. 2. Click equipment management, then "+" to add equipment. 3. Click the "Rowing" graphic, then scroll the brand connection screen to find and select **FTMS or iConsole**. 4. Select Rowing Machine, then the Bluetooth device. 5. Press Home to return to the Home screen. 6. To select a training video, select the type of rowing and press the button to enter the training screen. 7. Start training; the pause button at the top of the screen stops. 8. From the pause screen continue or exit.

**ERG180 differences:** step 3 is "Click the 'Rower' graphic ... find and select **FTMS or XTERRA**"; step 4 "Select Rower Machine"; step 6 says "type of walk" (carried over from a treadmill book); and step 7 is replaced by the console note that after 4 seconds without rowing the display stops and shows STOP ([auto-off card](xterra-erg-console-wakes-on-rowing-or-mode-shows-stop-after-4-seconds-and-shuts-off-after-2-minutes.md)).

The ERG180 also pairs with the XTERRA+ app ([app card](xterra-treadmill-console-xterra-plus-app-ios-13-android-8.md)); the ERG750W names the XF Connect app without pairing steps ([XF Connect card](../../erg750w-2025/console/xf-connect-app-named-with-a-qr-code-but-no-pairing-steps.md)) and shows a Bluetooth symbol on its LCD ([symbol card](../../erg750w-2025/console/bluetooth-symbol-flashing-means-an-app-can-connect-and-gone-means-power-saving.md)); the ERG800W's page pairs by the Bluetooth name on the back of its console ([ERG800W card](../../erg800w-2023/console/app-connection-by-the-bluetooth-name-printed-on-the-back-of-the-console.md)). The ERG550W's siblings ERG600W and ERG650W print no app page ([absence card](xterra-rower-console-no-bluetooth-or-app-page-in-seven-books.md)). The SB600 recumbent's page is the bike version ([SB600 card](../../sb600-2023/console/kinomap-and-ftms-apps-take-over-and-blank-the-console.md)); the Spirit CIC850 is the Spirit twin ([Spirit card](../../cic850-2022/console/cic850-2022-console-connecting-to-kinomap.md)).
