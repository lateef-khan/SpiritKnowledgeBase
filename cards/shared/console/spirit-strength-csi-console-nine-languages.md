> The i-Strength console is programmed with **9 languages**: English, German, Finnish, Spanish,
> Italian, Russian, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese.

## How to change it

1. Open **Settings** from the gear symbol; it is password protected - `Dy23751545`.
2. *To adjust the desired language, select **Language** from the drop-down menu.*
3. *Select the desired Language and press **Apply**.*

The nine appear on the language panel in their own scripts - Deutsch, Suomi, Espanol, Italiano and
Russian alongside the two Chinese entries and Japanese.

Language is the only Settings item that needs an **Apply**; volume, brightness and units take effect
as they are set, and **Done** closes the whole panel.

**Nine, not twelve or thirteen.** Spirit's cardio touchscreens carry longer language lists; this
console does not, and the count is printed in the sentence itself.
