1. Press the **left hand key** until **SPEED** is displayed.
2. Hold the **right hand key for 3 seconds**. The TIME field starts flashing.
3. Press the **left hand key** to select the heart rate target zone settings.
4. Press the **right hand key** to increase the **maximum** heart rate limit.
5. When the maximum is set, press the **left hand key** to move to the **minimum** heart rate limit,
   and set it the same way.

**The zone only does anything with the alarm armed.** Pressing the right hand key from the running
display arms the heart rate alarm; with a zone set, the alarm icon flashes and the console beeps
whenever your heart rate is **above or below** the zone. See
`spirit-bike-console-xic600-mode-select-alarm-and-reset`.

**And only with a chest belt on.** This console has a built-in heart rate receiver but no hand grip
pulse sensors, and the strap is sold separately, so with no strap the heart rate field stays empty
and the zone never triggers.

**Console pulse range is 30 to 240 BPM**; the manual sets no separate limit on what the zone will
accept.

**This is the two-key gesture, not the CIC850's.** That bike reaches the same setting by holding
**Page** for 3 seconds under DIST or KCAL and stepping with **Mode**.
