The **Engineering Mode** entry of the setting page holds four settings, word for word:

| Setting | What the manual says |
|---|---|
| Units | Choice **KM or Mile**. |
| Beep Mode | Turn off beep of button, no beeping sound is heard. |
| Sleep Mode | **The default OFF.** When set ON, "the electronic watch" will go to sleep without any operation within **30 minutes**. Press any key to wake up. |
| Pause Mode | **Turn on** allow **5 minutes** of pause, **turn off** to have the console pause indefinitely. |

Note which way round Pause Mode works: turning it **off** is what stops the console ending a paused
workout after five minutes.

"The electronic watch" is the source's own term for the console throughout the Dyaco manuals.

**Thirty minutes here; fifteen on the next generation.** The CE800ENT elliptical this page was
copied from prints the same four settings and the same 30 minutes
(`ce800ent-engineering-mode-settings`), and so does the CU900ENT (`cu900ent-engineering-mode-settings`).
The 2024 CR800ENT and CU800ENT, opened by ten presses on Hello Guest, sleep after **15** minutes
(`cu800ent-2024-console-maintenance-mode-and-engineering-menu`). Do not carry either figure across.

**The treadmills of this generation put these settings somewhere else.** On the CT800ENT-2022 and
CT850ENT-2022 the Engineering Mode entry is speed and incline calibration, and Beep and Sleep live
under Machine Information (`spirit-ct800ent-console-machine-information-gs-beep-sleep-safety-zeroing`).
