Once a workout has started the console switches to workout mode.

- Switch between **Stats**, **Charts** and **Track** views of the Training section, or go to the Media section for content.
- In the **Stats** view, **tap on the number** to choose which parameters are displayed.
- Use the **Level panel on the right side of the screen** to adjust the resistance level.

Pausing and ending the workout is a separate step, and the pause screen ends the workout on its own after a set time.
