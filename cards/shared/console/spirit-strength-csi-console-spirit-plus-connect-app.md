> Download the **SPIRIT+ Connect App** by scanning the QR Code for either Apple or Android.

The page carries two QR codes under an **App Store** badge and a **Google Play** badge, and that
sentence is the whole of the printed text. **This is the only app named anywhere in the thirty-nine
Spirit strength manuals**, and only the two CSI machines print it.

## What the manual does not say

There is **no** account or sign-up step, **no** pairing procedure between the app and the console,
**no** list of what the app records or shows, **no** statement that a workout is uploaded to it, and
**no** minimum phone or operating-system version. The book introduces the app and stops.

The Settings menu carries a separate **Gym App** entry with an **Add to Gym App** action. The manual
never mentions it in prose, so whether it is the same app is not established by this book.

**Not the registration QR code.** Every CSS, CSD, CSF and CSI manual prints a QR code on its cover
and product registration page for warranty registration - see `spirit-strength-warranty-registration`.
That one is not an app download.

**Not the Sole app.** A Sole SRVO strength machine also has a phone app, with pairing, profiles and
activity tracking documented at length. It is a different brand and a different app; do not answer a
SPIRIT+ Connect question from `srvo-app-download`.
