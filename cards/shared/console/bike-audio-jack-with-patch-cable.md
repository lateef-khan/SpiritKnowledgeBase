There is an **Audio Input Jack** on the front of the console, plus built-in speakers. You may plug any low-level audio source signal into this port — MP3, iPod, portable radio, CD player, or even a TV or computer audio signal.

**An audio patch cable is included** with this machine, and there is also a **headphone jack** for private listening.

**Only this model year says a cable is supplied.** The other manuals in this range call the socket an "Audio In Jack" and say nothing about a cable being in the box.
