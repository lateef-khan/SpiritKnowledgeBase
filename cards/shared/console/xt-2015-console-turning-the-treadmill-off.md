1. **The display will automatically turn off (go to sleep) after 30 minutes with no key
   operations.** The treadmill draws **very little current** in display mode.
2. **Remove the tether cord.**
3. **Turn off the main switch on the front of the treadmill, below the motor cover.**

**The 30 minute time-out is a setting, not a fixed behaviour.** It is the **Display Mode** entry in
the engineering mode Functions menu, and that is the only place the manuals say it can be changed.
The other half of this fact is there: `xt-2015-console-engineering-functions-key-tone` for the
XT185 and XT285, `xt-2015-console-engineering-functions-beep` for the XT385, XT485 and XT685.

The standby draw is described by comparison only, and the two halves of the family use different
comparisons: the XT185 and XT285 manuals say "about as much as your screen when it is turned off",
the XT385, XT485 and XT685 manuals say "about as much as your television when it is turned off".
**No manual gives a figure in watts.**
