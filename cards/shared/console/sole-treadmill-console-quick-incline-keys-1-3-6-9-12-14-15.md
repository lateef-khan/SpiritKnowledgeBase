Incline may be adjusted anytime after belt movement. Hold the **Incline 5/6 keys** (console or handgrip), or jump straight with the rapid keys **1, 3, 6, 9, 12, 14 and 15** on the left-hand side of the console. The window shows the incline position as it changes. Other books in the wave print different sets: 1-3-6-9-12-15, 2-4-6-8-10-12, 0-12 levels, 2-through-10 or 2-through-12.
