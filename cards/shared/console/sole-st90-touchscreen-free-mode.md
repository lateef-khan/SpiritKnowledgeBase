In FREE MODE the **running belt moves freely** (non-motorized). **Hold the hand rail and take care - a user may fall if not paying attention.** Pulling the safety key stops the belt immediately; refit it to resume. Steps: select free mode to enter; run holding the rail; incline adjusts **up 0 to 8 levels**; press Stop and **wait for the incline to come back down to 0**, which exits free mode.
