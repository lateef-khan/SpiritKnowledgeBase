**The console operates on 4 x AA batteries, which are not included.** The battery compartment is on
the **back side of the console**.

**That is the whole power system.** These manuals describe no mains lead, no power switch and no
self-generating drive; the console is battery powered and the bike's resistance is a fan, so nothing
else needs electricity.

**Removing the batteries is a factory reset.** The engineering mode section says so plainly:
"removing the batteries will make the console return to original factory settings and the ODO value
will turn zero." Use that deliberately, and warn a customer before they pull the cells to test them -
the odometer does not survive.

**A reset from the keypad is not the same thing.** Holding Stop for 2 seconds resets the saved
settings but keeps the units choice, and the console does not erase the setting value or the ODO
value under that reset. See `spirit-bike-console-air-bike-workout-summary-and-reset`.

**Neither manual gives a battery life figure**, a low-battery indicator, or a replacement interval.
