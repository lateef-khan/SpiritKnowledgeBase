**This is the incline stepper's rule. It is a fourth rule, and none of the three XE rules is
it.**

**There is an anatomical figure at the top of the console.** It lights every area that is activated
while you use the machine, during **any** of the programs. You steer it by customising the resistance
profile during set-up; accept the default profile and the program itself decides, by adjusting the
resistance.

**Upper body:** the LEDs light **when you are either holding onto the swing arms, or at any time your
hands are not on the pulse grip sensors**.

**Lower body, three degrees of engagement:** **Green** is minimal involvement, **Amber** medium,
**Red** full or heavy. The band is set by the **incline level**:

| Incline | What lights |
|---|---|
| **0-7.5** | Gluteals and Quadriceps **Amber**; Hamstrings and Calves **Green** |
| **8-20** | Gluteals **Red**; Quadriceps **Amber**; Hamstrings and Calves **Green** |

**There is no pedal direction in this rule**, because the machine does not step in reverse. The
XE295, XG400 and XE895 band **only** by pedal direction
(`spirit-xe-console-muscle-figure-lights-by-pedal-direction`); the XE395 bands by the same two incline
ranges but then splits each one forward and reverse
(`spirit-xe395-console-muscle-figure-bands-by-incline-level`); the XE795 bands by resistance level
instead (`xe795-2021-console-muscle-figure-lights-by-resistance-level`). **Do not quote a reverse row
at this machine.**

**The paragraph calls the machine an elliptical trainer**, in a book whose every other page calls it
an Incline Stepper. It is boilerplate carried over from the elliptical manual, in both the 2018 and
the 2021 printing, not a different machine.
