There is an **Audio In Jack** on the front of the console, plus built-in speakers. You may plug any low-level audio source signal into this port — MP3, iPod, portable radio, CD player, or even a TV or computer audio signal.

There is also a **Headphone Jack** for private listening.

The console can also receive music over Bluetooth, which is a separate feature with its own setup.
