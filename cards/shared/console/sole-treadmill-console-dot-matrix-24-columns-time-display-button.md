**Ten rows** of dots (**Red**, tri-color on the F83/F85; tri-color throughout on the S73/S77 books; plain dots on the TT8 books) marking workout levels, with **twenty-four columns marking time** (1/24th of the program each; one minute per column counting up from zero in Quick-Start). Three LEDs – **Track, Speed, Incline** – plus a **Display button** pick the view; scrolling through all three starts an automatic scroll, four seconds each, with the matching LED blinking; one more press returns to Track.
