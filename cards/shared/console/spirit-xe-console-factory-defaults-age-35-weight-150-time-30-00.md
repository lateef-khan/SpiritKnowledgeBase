**The default computer settings are: Age = 35, Weight = 150 Lbs., Time = 30:00.**

**Max Level is set per program**, not once:

| Program | Factory max level |
|---|---|
| **Hill** | 7 |
| **Fatburn** | 5 |
| **Cardio** | 7 |
| **Strength** | 8 |
| **Interval** | 7 |

**These are the values the console uses if you press Start instead of entering your own.** Age and
weight do not stay at 35 and 150 once anybody enters different numbers - the last entered become the
new defaults: `spirit-xe-console-the-last-age-and-weight-entered-becomes-the-new-default`.

**The XE100 and XE200 default weight is 155 lb, not 150**, and it is printed in the same manual as
the XE300's 150: `spirit-xe-console-factory-defaults-with-a-155-pound-weight`. Check which console
section applies before quoting a number.

**No other residential XE elliptical prints a default set.** The XE150, the XE395-2010 and every
2021 and 2023 machine give the saving rule without the numbers.
