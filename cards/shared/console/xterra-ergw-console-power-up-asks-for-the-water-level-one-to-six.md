The console needs to know how much water is in the tank, because the water level sets the resistance the console computes with.

**ERG550W:** "1. Install 2 AA batteries (included) and computer will beep for 2 seconds. 2. The console will display the type for 2 seconds, then the console will prompt you to enter the **Water Level (L1~L6)**" - the screen reads WATER LEVEL (LOAD) and the value flashes. "3. The user can press up or down to select level. After confirming, the user will enter the training mode." The menu at the top then blinks between MANUAL and RACE: UP or DOWN to choose, ENTER to confirm (programs cards).

**ERG650W:** the full LCD shows 2 seconds after the batteries are installed and every field activates briefly; the console then prompts for the **Workout Intensity Level** - "this is determined by how much water you've filled the tank with" - **L1 to L6 with the arrow keys; the default is L6**; press ENTER to confirm and the console enters Standby.

Enter the number the tank's water-level gauge shows (level 1 the lowest resistance, level 6 the highest - the tank card is a maintenance card). **Neither book says when the console sleeps** or how to reach this prompt again without pulling the batteries; the ERG600W of the same family boots into a clock setup instead ([ERG600W card](../../erg600w-2021/console/power-up-sets-the-clock-calendar-and-alarm-then-the-sport-screen.md)) and sleeps after about 4 minutes. Keys: [key card](xterra-ergw-console-keys-up-down-enter-start-stop-reset-and-recovery.md); batteries: [battery card](xterra-ergw-console-two-aa-batteries-included-and-changing-them-resets-every-value.md). The Spirit CRW800H2O sets the same six-step level with a LEVEL key ([Spirit twin](../../crw800h2o/console/crw800h2o-console-water-level-button-six-steps.md)).
