Adjust speed and incline with the **physical keys**. Press the physical **STOP** key to pause. From the pause screen you can **get back to running, start a cool down, or end the workout and see the summary**. **After 5 minutes of inactivity in Pause mode the workout ends automatically.** The books add that the actual machine operation may differ slightly from the description.
