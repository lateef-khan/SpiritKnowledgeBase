Programs start in **Profile** mode. The Display button by the dot matrix switches to a **1/4-mile track** with a lap counter; pressing again blinks the Track light for **Scan** (Track/Profile alternate every few seconds); pressing again returns to Profile. A second Display button under the Message Window cycles four windows: Time+Distance, Pulse+Calories, Speed (RPM+MPH), Level+Watts, with its own 4-second Scan loop. Right of the dot matrix sit a **Heart Icon** (blinks once grips or strap are read) and a **Bar Graph** of percent of maximum heart rate - **enter your age or the bar graph is wrong**.
