These four books print **no maintenance/diagnostic menu and no incline/stride calibration procedure**. Console settings (sleep, fan, sound, Bluetooth, profiles) live on the touchscreen Settings page instead. Do not apply the LCD Start+Stop+Enter menu or any LCD calibration to these machines.
