**Heart rate detection.** Hold the heart-rate grips with **both hands for 3 to 5 seconds** to display the heart-rate value (50-200). Holding with one hand gives an invalid signal.

**Anti-fall function.** The treadmill only operates with the uprights in the upright position. If they are folded down at any time during operation the treadmill automatically stops within 3 seconds; it cannot be used with the uprights folded (as shipped in the carton).

**Bluetooth FTMS connection.** Open the app you want (the book names Zwift and Kinomap) and choose connect treadmill. The treadmill's Bluetooth name is its model name — **WS200** or **WS300**. The app searches for and connects to the treadmill automatically; if the connection times out, go back and repeat the previous step. The XTERRA+ app is a separate route ([app card](xterra-treadmill-console-xterra-plus-app-ios-13-android-8.md)).

The book does not say whether the pulse grips are the only heart-rate source; its chest-strap page describes a Bluetooth strap that is not included ([strap card](xterra-treadmill-console-chest-strap-not-included-with-a-cr2032-battery.md)).
