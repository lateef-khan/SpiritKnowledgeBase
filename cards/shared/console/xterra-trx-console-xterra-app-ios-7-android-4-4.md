The machine has a Bluetooth transceiver that lets it interact with selected phones or tablets through the free **Xterra App** (Apple Store or Google Play). You can view current workout data in three different display screens on your device, switch between the workout display and internet, social media or email sites via icons, and when the workout is finished the data is saved automatically to the built-in personal calendar.

1. Download the App.
2. Open it on your phone or tablet and make sure Bluetooth is enabled.
3. In the App, click the icon in the **top left** corner to search for your Xterra equipment.
4. After the equipment is detected, click Connect. When they are synced, the **Bluetooth icon on the console display lights up**.
5. When the workout is finished the data is saved automatically and you are prompted to sync it with each available fitness cloud site — iHealth, MapMyFitness, Record or Fitbit, "with more to come" — after installing that site's own app so its icon is active.

Your device needs at least **iOS 7 or Android 4.4**. These two 2024 books print the oldest app text in the range; the TRX2500 book of the same batch prints no app page and no Bluetooth at all, and the 2022-2025 books use the XTERRA+ app ([card](xterra-treadmill-console-xterra-plus-app-ios-13-android-8.md)).
