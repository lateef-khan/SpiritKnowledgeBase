**To the right of the dot matrix display there is a Heart Icon and a Bar Graph.**

- **Grasping the hand pulse sensors on the stationary handlebars, or wearing the heart rate
  transmitter, starts the Heart Icon blinking.** This **may take a few seconds**.
- The **Message Window** displays your heart rate, or Pulse, **in beats per minute**.
- The **Bar Graph represents the percentage of your maximum heart rate you are currently
  achieving**.

**The bar graph is wrong until you enter your age.** Both manuals print the same note: *you must
enter your age for the Bar Graph to be accurate.* The beats-per-minute figure is measured and does
not depend on your age; only the percentage does. The console works the percentage out from
**220 minus your age**.

**The XE350 has the heart icon but no bar graph.** Its manual says so in the same paragraph - "The
XE350 has a heart icon only in the main screen":
`xe350-2005-console-has-a-heart-icon-but-no-bar-graph`.

**The XE100 and XE200 have neither a bar graph nor this note.** Their console section describes only
the Heart Icon and a separate Pulse Display Window, and their programming page drops the sentence
about the age being needed for the bar graph.

**On the XE150 the chest strap is an optional extra**, which the same paragraph records in passing:
`xe150-2005-console-heart-rate-transmitter-is-an-option-not-an-inclusion`.
