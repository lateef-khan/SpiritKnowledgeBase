**The exercise equipment can also play music wirelessly via Bluetooth.**

1. **Turn on your mobile phone or tablet's Bluetooth function.**
2. **Search for the name "Bt-speaker"** in your device's Bluetooth menu.
3. **Tap to connect.**

Your device can now transmit music to the exercise equipment.

The manuals print the speaker's Bluetooth name as **"Bt-speaker"** and list it separately from the
Spirit+ App pairing steps.
