Pressing **Start** after the console powers up is the quickest way to begin. This initiates Start key mode.

- Time counts **up** from zero.
- The resistance level is adjusted manually with the **Level up/down** buttons.
- The dot matrix display shows a track with a blinking dot indicating your progress as it travels around the track.

The manual's wording for this section also mentions incline being adjustable. These bikes have no incline; only resistance level changes.
