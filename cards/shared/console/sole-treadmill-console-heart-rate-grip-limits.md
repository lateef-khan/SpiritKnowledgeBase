The Pulse (Heart Rate) window shows current heart rate in beats per minute from **both left and right stainless steel sensors on the front cross bar**, shown anytime the computer receives a Grip Pulse signal. Limits, as printed: **you may not use the Grip Pulse feature while in Heart Rate Control**; the books point at the Important Safety Instructions (page 3) for grip operation; and the UF80, VF80, VS73 and WS73 books add that **the grip sensors may not be accurate when running** (the other 2007-2010 books omit that sentence). A chest strap transmitter section follows in every one of these books.
