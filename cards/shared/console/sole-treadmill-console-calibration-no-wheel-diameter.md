**No wheel-diameter step.** Unlike the older books (wheel sizes 1.84-3.01 on their own cards), these books go straight from the units choice to grade return.

1. Remove the safety key.
2. Press and hold down the **Start** and **Speed up (Fast)** buttons and at the same time replace the safety key. Keep holding until the window displays **"Factory settings"**, then press **Enter**.
3. Set Metric or English (miles vs kilometres) with the **incline up/down key**, then press Enter. The maximum speed value shows in the speed window and the maximum elevation value shows in the incline window.
4. **Grade return - On.** This lets the incline return to zero when Stop is pressed; EU standards require it off for sale in Europe.
5. Press Start to begin calibration. The process is automatic; the speed will start up without warning, so do not stand on the belt.
