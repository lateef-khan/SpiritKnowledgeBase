1. Enter engineering mode - **Start and Enter for 2 seconds** - and step to the **Audio ON/OFF**
   setting with the arrows. **The SOUND sign blinks.**
2. Press **Enter** to get into it.
3. The console displays **"ON" or "OFF"**. If the audio is on, the **ON** sign blinks; if it is off,
   the **OFF** sign blinks.
4. Press the arrows to change it, then **Enter** to confirm. The console returns to Engineering Mode.
5. With no activity for 30 seconds the console switches to STANDBY Mode. **Pressing the Reset button
   twice exits Engineering Mode.**

**Neither manual states the factory default** for this setting, only that the console beeps in normal
use - for one second when engineering mode is entered, and every 30 seconds throughout Pause Mode.

**This is what silences the Pause Mode reminder**, which is the complaint that usually brings a
customer here: the console beeps every 30 seconds for the full 5 minutes of a pause. See
`spirit-bike-console-air-bike-sleep-ready-pause`.

**It survives a keypad reset.** Holding Stop for 2 seconds does not erase the setting value; only
removing the batteries returns the console to factory settings.

**The service manual calls it the buzzer sound switch** and says the ON or OFF **flashes in the watt
window** of the LCD while you change it; otherwise the same steps.
