1. Attach the transmitter to the elastic strap using the locking parts (the TR6.6 book says "interlocking button").
2. Adjust the strap as tightly as possible while still comfortable.
3. Position the transmitter with the logo centered in the middle of your torso, facing away from your chest (some people must position it slightly left of center). Attach the final end of the strap by inserting the round end and, using the locking parts, secure the transmitter and strap around your chest.
4. Position the transmitter directly below the pectoral muscles.
5. Sweat is the best conductor; plain water can also pre-wet the electrodes (the two ribbed oval areas on the reverse of the belt and both sides of the transmitter). Wear the strap a few minutes before the workout; a steady signal can take a while at first. The TRX4500 and TRX5500 add: "As noted, wearing clothing over the transmitter/strap doesn't affect performance."
6. Stay within range of the receiver — close enough to the console for reliable readings. Bare skin assures proper operation; over a shirt, wet the areas the electrodes rest on.

Note: the transmitter activates when it detects heart activity and deactivates when it receives none. It is water resistant, but moisture can create false signals, so dry it after use to prolong battery life (estimated **2500 hours**). None of these three books names the battery type.

**Whether a strap is in the box.** The TR6.6 book calls it "the included telemetric chest strap"; the TRX4500 and TRX5500 books say neither way. The 2023-2025 books that say "not included" and name a CR2032 are on [the other strap card](xterra-treadmill-console-chest-strap-not-included-with-a-cr2032-battery.md). What interferes with the reading is on [the erratic card](xterra-treadmill-console-erratic-heart-rate-and-the-seven-interference-sources.md).

**Four bike and elliptical books print the same page with 2500 hours and no battery type.** The SB2.5r introduces it with "buy an optional heart rate strap that transmits your pulse wirelessly to the console" and calls the strap the more accurate of the two sources (grips or strap); the FS2.5 and FS3.5 head it "Wearing The Chest Strap * Sold Separately"; the SB500 says neither way, and its console page says the heart icon blinks with the grips "or by wearing the chest strap" ([heart icon card](xterra-console-heart-icon-blinks-when-the-grips-or-strap-are-read.md)). The SB4500 prints the wearing steps under "(Sold Separately)" but drops the battery-life sentence altogether ([card](../../sb4500-2021/console/bluetooth-4-0-ftms-always-on-for-apps-and-bluetooth-heart-rate-monitors.md)).
