Needs **WiFi**. Pick a third-party app icon; the books' full-screen keys render as icons (OCR reads them as '4' in and '3x' out with an app-switch key). For casting, follow the iOS/Android instructions on the screen, then use the same full-screen keys. Join the facility WiFi, press **Screen Mirroring** on the device, and pick the **'SOEL-XXX'** device (the F85/TT8 books' spelling, transposed; the ST90 book spells it **'SOLE-XXX'**). The example page shows WIFI NAME TP-LINK 40CF with 'a strong connection is required' and DEVICE NAME SOEL-XXX.
