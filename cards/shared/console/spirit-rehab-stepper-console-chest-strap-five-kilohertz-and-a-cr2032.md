**The strap is not in the carton.** *"The chest strap transmitter is not a standard part, but is a
separate purchase. **Most transmitters that operate at 5kHz frequency will also work.**"* So it is
an analogue 5 kHz belt, not Bluetooth and not ANT+.

## Wearing it

1. Attach the transmitter to the elastic strap with the locking parts.
2. Tighten the strap as far as it will go while staying comfortable.
3. Centre the logo on your body, facing away from your chest - **some people must position it
   slightly left of centre**.
4. Position it **immediately below the pectoral muscles**.
5. **Wet the two black square electrodes** on the reverse of the belt. Sweat is the best conductor;
   plain water works. Put the strap on a few minutes before the workout - a weak signal at the
   start usually settles once the strap has warmed up.
6. Stay close enough to the console. Clothing over the strap does not affect it; if you wear it
   over a shirt, **moisten the shirt where the electrodes rest**.

The transmitter switches itself on when it detects heart activity and off when it stops.

## The battery

- **About 2500 hours** of transmitter battery life, estimated.
- **If the strap has a replaceable battery, the replacement is a CR2032.** The manual does not say
  which straps do.
- The transmitter is water resistant, but **moisture can create false signals** - dry it completely
  after use to prolong battery life.

## The grips are the alternative, and neither is a medical measurement

Gripping the hand pulse sensors starts the same reading; it may take a few seconds. Every one of
these manuals says in its safety list that **the pulse sensors are not medical devices** and that
the reading is an exercise aid only. For a cardiac rehabilitation patient the manual itself
recommends a chest or wrist strap over the grips - and still does not make it a clinical
measurement.

**Enter the patient's age in Set Up** or the HR percent bar graph beside the display is meaningless.
