**These machines let you make speed and incline changes on the side handrails, and you can turn
those handrail controls off.**

- **When the indicator light next to the switch is lit, the handrail switches are disabled.** This
  lets you use the full length of the handrails without fear of activating the speed or elevation
  controls.

**Each manual gives the switch two different locations, in two different places.**

| Manual | "Handrail Adjustments" says | "Handrail Buttons Disable Switch" says |
|---|---|---|
| XT285-2015 | the disable button is **on the right side of the lower portion of the console** | the switch is **to the right of the Stop button** |
| XT385-2015, XT485-2015, XT685-2015 | the disable button is **on the right side of the lower portion of the console** | the switch is **to the right of the Enter button** |

Both descriptions are reproduced as printed. The manuals never reconcile them, so look for a switch
with an indicator light beside it in the lower right of the console rather than trusting one
sentence.

**The XT185-2015 has no handrail controls.** Its manual has no Handrail Adjustments section, no
disable switch, and no indicator light - although its Quick Start steps do mention hand rail keys.

**The XT685 2010 owner's manual prints both paragraphs too** - Handrail Adjustments on its p. 16 and
Handrail Buttons Disable Switch on its p. 20 - so this card covers that machine. **It gives the same
two locations as the 2015 books, and its second location is the third variant of the three:** its
Handrail Adjustments paragraph says the disable button is **on the right side of the lower portion
of the console**, and its Handrail Buttons Disable Switch paragraph says the switch is **to the
right of the Stop button**, where the XT385, XT485 and XT685 of 2015 say to the right of the Enter
button. All three descriptions are reproduced as printed.
