**If there is no input to the console for twenty minutes the console goes to stand-by mode.** In
stand-by the display turns off. **To turn the console on, press any key.**

All four manuals print this sentence word for word.

**On these machines the timeout is a setting.** The maintenance menu carries a **Sleep mode** item -
"turn on to have the console power down automatically after 20 minutes of inactivity" - so it can be
turned off. See `spirit-xe-console-maintenance-menu-with-a-motor-test-and-security` and
`xe395-2010-console-engineering-mode-with-an-incline-switch-and-calibration`.

**The 2005 XE150, XE350 and XE550 use four minutes, not twenty**, and their manual documents no menu
at all: `spirit-xe-console-stand-by-after-four-minutes-without-input`.

**Do not read this onto the 2021 or 2023 machines.** Their manuals print no stand-by paragraph in the
power-up section; what they have instead is a Display Mode or Sleep Mode item in engineering mode,
and the 2023 pair sets that to **fifteen** minutes.
