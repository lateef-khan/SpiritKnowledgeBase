Accurate personal data makes the calorie estimate more accurate.

1. In **KCAL** or **DISTANCE** mode, hold the **PAGE** key for **3 seconds** to enter the settings, and keep pressing to reach the heart rate windows.
2. Press **PAGE** to select gender: Male or Female.
3. Press **PAGE** to move to the weight unit setting. Press **MODE** to choose **Kg** or **Lb**.
4. Press **PAGE** to move to the weight setting. Press **MODE** to raise the weight in steps of **0.5 kg or 0.5 lb**.

Changing metric to imperial changes every displayed unit: MPH/KPH, Lb/KG and ML/KM.
