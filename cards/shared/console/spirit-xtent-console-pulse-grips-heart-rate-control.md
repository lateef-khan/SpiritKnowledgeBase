**Pulse (heart rate) on the screen shows the current value of the heart beats per
minute.**

- **You must hold both the left and right stainless steel sensors to measure the
  pulse.**
- **The pulse value is shown on the screen continuously.**
- **You may use the hand pulse sensors while in Heart Rate Control.**
- **It will also pick up wireless heart rate transmitters that are Polar and
  Bluetooth compatible.**

**This is the opposite of what the base 2023 XT manuals say.** Those print *"You may
**not** use the Pulse Grip feature while in Heart Rate Programs"* - see
`xt-2023-console-pulse-grip`. **Both ENT manuals say you may.** The two statements
cannot both be right for the same family, and neither manual explains the change;
this card reports what the ENT manuals print and does not resolve the conflict.

**On the XT685ENT there is an icon for the strap**: it **lights up and the heart rate
is displayed when a Bluetooth heart rate chest strap is successfully connected**, and
**is off when the strap is disconnected**. The XT485ENT manual prints no such icon.

The XT685ENT also refers the reader to its **Important Safety Instructions, page 6**,
concerning Pulse Grip operation.
