Incline may be adjusted anytime after belt movement by selecting rapid key **2, 4, 6, 8, 10 or 12** on the left-hand side of the console. The 2016 F60 instead prints 0-12 incline levels with quick keys 10 and 12. Other books print different sets: 1-3-6-9-12-15, 1-3-6-9-12-14-15, 2-through-10 or 2-through-12.
