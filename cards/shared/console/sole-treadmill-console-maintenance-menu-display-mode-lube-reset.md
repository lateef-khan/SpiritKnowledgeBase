Entry-wording split, both readings kept: the F60-2012 and F63-2013 books say to hold the three keys; the F65, F80/F85 and S77 books add 'and install safety key'. The card follows the shorter wording and notes the addition.

1. Press and hold **Start, Stop and Enter** at the same time, until the display shows 'Engineering mode' (it may say maintenance menu, depending on version). Press Enter.
2. Scroll with the **Speed 5/6** keys; **Stop** returns to the previous menu selection. The selections are:
   - **A. Key Test** - tests all keys.
   - **B. Display Test** - tests all display functions.
   - **C. Functions** (Enter; Speed 5/6 to scroll): **I. Display Mode** (off = power down after 30 minutes inactivity); **II. Pause Mode** (on = 5 minutes of pause, off = pause indefinitely); **III. Maintenance** (reset lube message and odometer readings); **IV. Units** (English or Metric); **V. Key Tone** (beeping on/off when keys are pressed).
   - **D. Security** - locks the keypad (child lock; hold Start and Enter 3 seconds to unlock).
   - **E. Exit**.
