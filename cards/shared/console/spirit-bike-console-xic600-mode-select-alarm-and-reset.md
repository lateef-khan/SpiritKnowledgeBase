**The manual names the keys by position, the console names them by function.** The console drawing
shows the left key moulded **`MODE / RESET`** and the right key moulded **`START`** - which is worth
knowing, because the labels do not match what the keys actually do: it is the **right** key, marked
START, that resets. See `spirit-bike-console-xic600-face-and-two-keys`.

**MODE SELECT - the left hand key.** Press it to select **SPEED, DIST, TIME or CLOCK** at the bottom
of the display. That is the only way to change which value the bottom section shows, and several
other settings are entered from a particular one of those four.

**HEART RATE ALARM - the right hand key, pressed.** Press it to arm the heart rate alarm. When the
alarm is on, **the alarm icon flashes and a beep sounds** to tell you your heart rate is either
above or below the selected target zone. Set the zone first - see
`spirit-bike-console-xic600-heart-rate-target-zone`.

**RESET - the right hand key, held.** Press and hold the right hand key for **3 seconds**. All the
accumulated values for **AVG SPEED, AVG PULSE, TIME, DIST and KCAL return to zero.**

**The reset is not selective.** The manual gives no way to clear one figure and keep another; the
one gesture clears all five.

**Holding the right hand key does something different depending on where you are.** Held from the
SPEED window it opens the workout timer or the heart rate zone settings rather than resetting; held
from the TIME window it clears the exercise time. The value showing at the bottom of the display
decides which. That is why the manual keeps telling you to press the left hand key first.
