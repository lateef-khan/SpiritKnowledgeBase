Turn Display Mode **off** to have the console power down automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity. Same line in the 2012-2015 books, the E98-2013, the 2016/2019 LCD books and the E95/E98 home-page-family books (there lettered D). The E98-2011 book prints it the other way round (own card).
