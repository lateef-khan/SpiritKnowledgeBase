**There is no distance window on this console, and the start-up reading is an altimeter.**

1. **Power is connected and the console powers up on its own.** These models run on **120 volt, 15
   amp**, and the **power switch is where the line cord plugs into the unit, on the left side near
   the middle**.
2. **The console runs an internal self-test.** All the lights turn on.
3. The **Message Window** shows a **software version** (i.e. `VER 1.0`).
4. The **VERTICAL window** shows an **altimeter reading**.
5. The **Time Window** shows **how many total hours the Incline Stepper has been used**.
6. **The altimeter and time stay up for only a few seconds.** The console then goes to the start-up
   display: the dot matrix scrolls the program profiles and the Message Center scrolls the start-up
   message.

**A version number and an hours reading at power on are normal, not a fault.**

**Do not answer this from the elliptical card.** The XE and XB machines that share this console face
show a **distance total in the distance window**
(`spirit-xe-console-power-up-self-test-with-the-totals-in-the-data-windows`); this machine has no
distance window at all - its drawing labels CALORIES, TIME, VERTICAL, INCLINE and LEVEL - and reports
altitude instead. **Nothing in the manual says what unit the altimeter reads in**, or whether the
Units setting in engineering mode changes it.

**Both printings say this word for word.** The XS895-2018 and XS895-2021 console chapters match at
**99.3%** at word level, measured on the native PDF text of printed pages 14-20; the only differences
are missing spaces after full stops in the 2021 typesetting and where the page folios fall.

**The XS895 (XS300B-YS006) service manual prints this paragraph word for word with one addition**:
*"These models are connected directly to **120-volt, 15-amp or 220-volt, 10-amp**"* - a second supply
the owner's manuals never mention - with the same power switch "on the left side near the middle",
the same self-test, VER 1.0, the altimeter in the VERTICAL window and the total hours in the Time
window.
