Read from the owner's-manual console drawings and the service-manual button-location pages:

- A **blue backlit LCD** in the middle: **5.5"** on the TRX2500, **6.5"** on the TRX3500 and **7.5"** on the TRX4500 (the console badges say so, and the shared service manual says the GT90C and GT90D layouts are the same, just different sizes). The TRX2500 LCD shows Time, Calories, Incline, Program, Distance, a speed dial and Pulse; the TRX3500/TRX4500 LCD shows PACE, CALORIES, TIME, DISTANCE and PULSE windows over an incline figure, a dot matrix, a heart-rate profile bar, a speed figure and a message line.
- **Incline direct keys** in two columns left of the LCD and **speed direct keys** right of it (the values are on each machine's quick-start card), a **speaker** grille each side, and an **audio jack** at the lower left of the panel.
- Under the LCD: a **fan vent**, Incline ▲/▼ on the left, Speed ▲/▼ on the right, then the **Fan** key, **START/STOP**, the red safety key, **ENTER** and **PROGRAM**.
- Badges on the wings: "Remote Touch CONTROL" for incline (left) and speed (right, on the handrails), the LCD size, a HOME WARRANTY block, the program count (**24** on the TRX2500, **30** on the TRX3500 and TRX4500), the speed range (**0-10 mph** TRX2500, **0-12 mph** TRX3500 and TRX4500) and the incline range (**0-10%**, **0-12%**, **0-15%** respectively).

The warranty page of each book, not the badge, is the authority for the warranty terms. What the service manuals say each key does in ready and run mode is on [the service-manual keys card](xterra-trx-console-keys-in-ready-and-run-mode-per-the-service-manual.md).
