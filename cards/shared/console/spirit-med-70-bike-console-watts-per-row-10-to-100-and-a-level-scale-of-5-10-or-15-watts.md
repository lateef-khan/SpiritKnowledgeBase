Both entries live in the **Set up** menu, after Patient data and Seat position.

**WATTS PER ROW** - "Adjusts the scale of the dot matrix when power (watts) is displayed. The default
setting is **10 watts per row**. The default of 10 watts per row means the full display (**all 10 rows
lit) equal 100 watts**." It "can be adjusted from **10 to 100 watts per row** of lights on the graph" -
so a full graph can mean anything from 100 to 1000 watts.

**LEVEL SCALE** - "Set the amount of change in the level adjustment of workload (resistance at the
pedals) each time the arrow keys are pressed", for "very fine increments of resistance for physically
challenged patients or ... very high resistance levels for sports training". The default is **Fine**.

| Option | Watts per level (at 60 RPM) |
|---|---|
| 1. **Fine** | **5** |
| 2. **Medium** | **10** |
| 3. **Coarse** | **15** |

**The figures are at 60 RPM.** The operating page puts the top of the range as "up to 750 watts (60
RPM) when level settings are at 15 watts per level" - 50 levels times 15 watts - and the 2025 books'
specification page prints a workload of 5 watts up to 1700 watts, which is not 50 times any of these
three. Both are reproduced as printed.

**This setting is the first thing to check when "pedal resistance seems harder/different than
before".** The troubleshooting page says so: "Check the watts per level setting in the Set Up menu.
The default setting from the factory is 5 watts per level." The Dyaco MED 7.0R adds "(see page 22)",
a page its own Set up menu is not on.

**Watts per row is the scale of the Power graph** that the Display key beside the dot matrix
switches to (`spirit-med-70-bike-console-display-key-cycles-a-profile-a-power-graph-and-a-track`).
