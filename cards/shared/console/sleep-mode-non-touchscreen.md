1. Turn the power switch on if the machine is not already on.
2. Press and hold **Stop**, **Enter** and **Start**.
3. Insert the safety key while holding those buttons, and keep holding until the screen shows EM or Engineering Menu. It takes about 10 seconds.
4. Let go and press **Enter**. Move with speed up and down, choose with **Enter**, go back with **Stop**.
5. Press speed up until it says **Functions**, then press **Enter**.
6. Press speed up until it says **Display Mode** or **Sleep Mode**, then press **Enter**.
7. If it says **Display Mode**, press speed down to set it OFF. If it says **Sleep Mode**, press speed up to set it ON. Press **Enter** to lock it in.
8. Press **Stop** until it says Functions, press speed up until it says **Exit**, then press **Enter**.

**What happens**: the machine sleeps after 15 to 30 minutes of no use while the safety key is in. The screen also turns off when the key is removed. Press **Start** to wake it, as long as the key is still in.

The 2023 F63 and F65 use a shorter button combination. See that card instead.
