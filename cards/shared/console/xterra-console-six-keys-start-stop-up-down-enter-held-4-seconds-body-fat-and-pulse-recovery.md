| Key | As printed |
|---|---|
| START/STOP | Start or stop your exercise program; start body fat measurement |
| UP | Increase the target values; increase the tension level while exercising; select your program |
| DOWN | Decrease the preset target values; decrease the tension level; select your program |
| ENTER | Accept the desired program; accept the target value; **hold four seconds to reset all values to zero when the unit is stopped** |
| Body Fat | Have your body fat measurement (hand on the pulse grip, press the key; the monitor measures fat %, BMI and BMR) |
| Pulse RECOVERY | Have the recovery grade ([recovery card](xterra-console-pulse-recovery-grade-with-p-and-err.md)) |

The four books print this page word for word. The keypad runs across the bottom of the console: PULSE RECOVERY, UP, ENTER on the top row and BODY FAT, DOWN, START/STOP below (SB150 and EU150 pictures); the SB250 picture has the same six keys on a curved panel with a speaker grille under them. The readouts are on the windows cards ([time to 99:00 and calories to 9990](xterra-console-windows-with-time-to-99-00-calories-to-9990-and-a-distance-bar.md) for the SB150 and FS150; [with watt](xterra-console-windows-with-watt-time-to-99-59-and-calories-to-999.md) for the SB250 and EU150); the body fat program's height, weight, gender and age entry is on the Programs cards.
