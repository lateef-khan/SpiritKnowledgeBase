Before entering the Engineering Mode, turn the power off. Then hold down the **Program** key plus the **Speed Up and Speed Down** keys, and power on while holding all three. The console enters Engineering mode; press Speed Up or Down to cycle through the menu.

1. **Sleep Mode** — lets the machine enter "Sleep Mode" and power down after **5 minutes** of non-use. On by default. Press Enter to change the mode ON or OFF, then Stop to return to the menu.
2. **Lube Reset** — press Enter to reset the Lube warning message; press Stop to return to the menu.
3. **Security - Child Lock** — off by default. With the child lock on, you must **hold Start and Incline Up for three seconds** to unlock idle mode, which discourages children from turning the machine on by pressing buttons at random. Pressing Speed Up and/or Down toggles it ON/OFF.

The TRX2500 book's engineering mode uses Enter instead of Program and holds only Sleep Mode ([TRX2500 card](../../trx2500-2024/console/engineering-mode-enter-and-speed-keys-sleep-mode.md)). Calibration is a separate mode entered with Enter and Speed Up while the key goes in ([TRX3500 card](../../trx3500-2024/console/calibration-enter-and-speed-up-wheel-60-max-12-mph-incline-12.md)). The Lube message can also be cleared by holding Enter in standby ([lube card](xterra-treadmill-console-clear-the-lube-message-by-holding-enter-3-seconds.md)).
