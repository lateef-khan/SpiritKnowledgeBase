The console diagram labels:

- **Speaker**
- **Cooling fan** and a **fan power switch**
- **Display**
- A **wireless charging area**

**This machine charges a phone wirelessly, not through a USB port.** The 2019 console of the same family has a USB port instead, rated 5 Vdc / 0.5 A. Do not carry that rating here: this manual gives no rating, no charging standard and no instructions for the wireless area beyond naming it on the diagram.

**No program buttons are labelled on the diagram.** Programs are chosen on the touchscreen. The physical keys the manual names elsewhere are **START** and **STOP**.
