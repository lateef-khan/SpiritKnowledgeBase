**The source is a silent video.** It shows what to tap. It never says what the symptom looks like, gives no error message, and gives no reason why Hulu needs this. Do not promise the customer a cause.

**Get into the Android settings**

1. Log in. The **Guest** profile is enough.
2. Tap the **Wi-Fi symbol in the top right corner** of the home screen. The Android **Wi-Fi** page opens.
3. Tap the **magnifying glass in the top right** of that Wi-Fi page. A settings **Search** box opens.
4. Type `location` and tap the **Location** result.

**Turn scanning on**

5. On the **Location** page, check that **Use location** is **on**. In the video it was already on.
6. Tap **Wi-Fi and Bluetooth scanning**.
7. Turn **both** toggles on:
   - **Wi-Fi scanning**
   - **Bluetooth scanning**

   In the video both started **off**. This is the change the video is really about.
8. Go back. The Location page should now read "Both Wi-Fi and Bluetooth scanning are on".

**Give Hulu the permission**

9. On the **Location** page, tap **App permission**.
10. In the list, tap **Hulu**.
11. Choose **Allow only while using the app**. Do not choose **Deny**.

Hulu then shows under **ALLOWED ONLY WHILE IN USE**.

**Getting back out.** A small floating panel with **Apps**, **Go Back** and **Show Panels** sits on top of these screens. Use **Go Back** to leave. Drag the panel aside if it covers a toggle you need.
