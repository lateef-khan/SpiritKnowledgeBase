**After the console powers up, press the Start button.** That is Quick Start.

- The **Time counts up from zero** and all workout data starts to accrue.
- The **workload is adjusted by hand**, either **on the screen** or with the **Up and Down buttons on
  the lower control panel**.

All three manuals print this paragraph word for word. The 2024 manual adds one line to the same
page - **"Power Input Located as shown above"** - pointing at the socket in the drawing beside it.

**That is the whole section.** These manuals print no dot matrix behaviour, no column count, no
resistance level count and no wake-the-display step in Quick Start.

**Do not read the LED bikes' Quick Start onto these machines.** The CR800, CU800, CR900, CU900 and
even the CR900ENT and CU900ENT manuals print a further paragraph about the bottom row of the dot
matrix lighting, 24 columns of one minute each, and a wrap-around at the 24th:
`spirit-ce-console-quick-start-time-counts-up`. None of that is printed for the CU800ENT or the
CR800ENT, and their consoles have no matrix of lights.
