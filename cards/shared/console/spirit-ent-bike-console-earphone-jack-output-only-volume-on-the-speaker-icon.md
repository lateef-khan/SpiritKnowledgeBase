> The console has built-in an earphone sound output jack. The Jack is not an audio input jack. The
> volume must be controlled on the speaker icon.

**So it drives headphones and nothing else.** Nothing plugged into it is heard through the console,
and the jack has no volume control of its own - the on-screen speaker icon sets it.

**The owner's manuals of these two bikes never mention the jack.** Their console drawing names a
touchscreen, a reading rack, the fan, a USB charging port and the Start, Stop and Level keys, and no
headphone socket (`spirit-ent-bike-console-layout-touchscreen`). The service manuals' Upper
Controllers photograph labels an **Earphone Port** beside the Cooling Fan and a **Thumb Switch**, and
this paragraph describes it. Neither book says where on the console it is.

The CE800ENT elliptical prints the same paragraph for its own jack (`ce800ent-earphone-jack`). The
XBR55ENT and XBU55ENT residential bikes have the opposite arrangement - a 3.5 mm jack that is an
**input** only (`xe395ent-2021-console-panel-call-outs-and-the-audio-jack`); do not confuse the two.
