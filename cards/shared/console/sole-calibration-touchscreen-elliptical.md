1. **Clear error codes**: power the elliptical off, wait a few seconds, then power it back on.
2. **Log in**: use a personal account or the Guest profile.
3. **Open the engineering menu**: tap the gear icon in the top left to open Settings. At the top of the Settings screen is the word "Settings" - it does not look like a button, but it is. Tap that word rapidly several times until the Engineering Menu appears.
4. **Start calibration**: tap **Calibration** in the top right corner. Step off the elliptical, because it moves on its own. Press the start button to the right of Incline Calibration. The machine runs through the incline ranges by itself.
5. **After**: the machine should reboot to the login screen once everything stops. If it does not, press the **X** in the top left to return to the settings menu. Note any error codes and watch the behaviour, for example if it stops early or will not incline.

**The 2023 TFT owners books print no such menu.** Their console settings live on the touchscreen Settings page and they name no maintenance menu or calibration (`sole-elliptical-2023-tft-no-menu-no-calibration`). This card is the technician note's path, not the books'.
