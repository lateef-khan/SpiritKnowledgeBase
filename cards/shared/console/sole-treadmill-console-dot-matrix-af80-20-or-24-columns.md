**Twenty columns of dots, 8 high (F80), or 24 columns of dots, 10 high (F83/85).** The dots show approximate effort only and build a profile 'picture' as values change. When the word **Speed** is lit above the matrix the Speed profile shows; when **Incline** is lit the Incline profile shows. Press **ENTER** to swap between the Speed and Incline views.
