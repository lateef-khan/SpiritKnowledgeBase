Four causes, in order:

1. **Only one hand on the sensors, or hands off them.** Hold both grips.
2. **The HANDPULSE W/WIRE connector is not seated in the console.** Connect the cable again.
3. **The wires were damaged when the handpulse cable was joined to the console.** Fit a new cable.
4. **The hand pulse board is broken.** Replace the console or the hand pulse board.
