**Your device will need to be running on a minimum operating system of iOs 7 or Android 4.4 for the
Spirit Fit App to operate properly.**

"iOs 7" is printed exactly that way in all four manuals.

**These are not the Spirit+ figures.** The 2023 XT manuals require **iOS 13.1 or Android 8.0** for
the **Spirit+** App, a different app. See `xt-2023-console-spirit-plus-minimum-os`.

**The XT185-2015 owner's manual has no app section**, so it states no requirement at all.
