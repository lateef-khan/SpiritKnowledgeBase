**When to run it.** "If the console shows an incline error or does not seem to physically be at
the incline displayed, the unit needs to be calibrated."

1. **Press and hold the START and level UP keys for 5 seconds.**
2. The message window shows **"Calibration"**; press **ENTER**.
3. **The calibration process is automatic and the system will restart when finished.**

That is the whole procedure as printed - no figure to compare against, no duration, and no pass or
fail message.

**Only the XE400 and XE500 have this.** The XE100, XE200 and XE300 in the same dealer book have no
powered incline and no calibration; the book's Procedure 3 tells a technician with "an XE550" to
"go to motor test in engineering mode (Procedure 8)" - a slip, since Procedure 8 is this calibration
and the Motor Test is in Procedure 7
(`spirit-xe-2007-console-engineering-mode-with-a-safety-item-and-a-factory-set-that-resets-user-programs`).

**The XE395-2010 prints the same keystroke word for word** - Start and resistance level Up for 5
seconds, Calibration, Enter, automatic, restarts
(`xe395-2010-console-engineering-mode-with-an-incline-switch-and-calibration`). The 2016 and 2018
XE395 hold three keys - Stop, Level and Start - with no Enter
(`xe395-2016-console-incline-calibration-stop-level-and-start-held-five-seconds`). Quote the
gesture that belongs to the machine in front of you.

**The book's Troubleshooting Guide row "Incline does not work" is a wiring check, not this
calibration** - incline motor wires black to black, red to red, white to white, and the 3-wire
harness connected at the rail assembly; that row is held with the error cards.
