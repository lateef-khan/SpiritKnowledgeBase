The USB port charges a personal device during a workout.

1. Make sure the fitness equipment power is on.
2. Connect your USB charging cable (**not included**) to the USB Power Port and to your device.
3. Check that your device icon indicates it is charging.

Limits stated in the manual:

- The USB charging cable is not included. Make sure a compatible cable is used.
- Your device's "charging" icon may or may not indicate it is charging. Depending on the current your device requires, the icon may not come on but the device is still charging, possibly at a lower rate.
- The port provides up to **5Vdc / 1.0 amp** and meets **USB 2.0** regulations.
- **You cannot save workout data to a USB through this port. It is for charging purposes only.**

**1.0 amp is this year's figure, not the earlier one.** The 2019 upright, recumbent and light commercial manuals print **5Vdc / 0.5 amp** for the identical paragraph. The rest of the wording is word for word the same, so the current is the only thing that tells the two apart.
