**A power adapter, plugged into a DC jack on the machine, and no console batteries.** Neither
service manual has a battery section or a cell size for the console; what they do have, in three
places each, is an adapter:

- **The console-problem page**: if the screen does not light, *"check the power adapter is the normal
  power supply or change another one to check is work or check the adapter is correctly inserted
  into the DC jack"*, then check every wire into the console and, with the console cover off, that
  no wire is broken.
- **The quick lookup table**, under "LCD display does not shine": *"Check whether the AC power input is
  AC100 ~ 240V and the output is DC12V"*, and whether the tension motor (the CRW800-2016 book says
  cable tensioner) outputs DC12V - if not, replace it.
- **The block diagram** draws **AC ADAPTER** feeding the **POWER INPUT** of the console, and the chain
  cover procedure begins by removing **the nut of the DC power cord** with a 15 mm wrench. The parts
  drawings name it the **750 mm DC Power Cord (39)**.

**So a dead XRW600 or CRW800-2016 display is a supply question first**: adapter indicator, adapter
seated in the DC jack, 12 V at the tensioner, then the console wiring. The service manuals give no
adapter part number and no current rating.

**The one battery cover the CRW800-2016 book names is not on the console.** It is the **Battery Cover
(36~3)** of the handlebar **Controller Assembly (36)** - the radio remote that carries the level keys
and an RF module - and the no-resistance page checks "if has power of Controller Assembly" behind
it. The XRW600 book has a different handle and names no battery at all beyond the chest strap's
CR2032.

**The XRW600 owner's manuals name no power source at all** - no battery, no adapter, no switch
(`xrw600-console-no-power-source-is-named`). The service manual is the only document for that
machine that says what the console runs on.

**The CRW800 of 2021 and 2024 is a different answer.** Its owner's manuals say **two C batteries in
a compartment on the back of the console** (`crw800-2024-console-two-c-batteries`), and its 2020
service manual replaces the adapter with a **generator** and a battery cover held by one screw. The
CRW800-2016 is the adapter machine - its CW800-YR001 book prints the adapter text above and the
generator nowhere - so do not carry the C-cell answer back to it, and do not carry this adapter
forward to the 2021 machine. **The water rowers take four AA cells**
(`spirit-water-rower-console-four-aa-batteries`).
