**INTERVAL DISPLAY.** Shows all details for the Interval 20/10, Interval 10/20 and Interval Custom
modes. The upper banner tells you which interval mode is running and whether the current interval is
in a **WORK** or a **REST** period. **TOTAL TIME** shows the total run time while the large numeric
display shows the **work or rest segment time**. The **88/88** field shows the current interval and
the total number of intervals.

**TARGET TIME, DISTANCE & CALORIES DISPLAY.** A target symbol appearing means a target mode is
running. **TIME alternates between remaining and elapsed.** **DISTANCE counts down** in miles or
kilometres depending on the console setup. **CALORIES counts down** the number burned.

**WATTS, SPEED & RPM.** Shown **during all modes**. Watts is the amount of work produced in a
particular time. Speed is derived from the RPM or pedal speed, in miles or kilometres depending on
the console setup. **RPM is the speed of the spinning cranks.**

**HEART RATE.** When the console detects a signal from the wireless monitor, the **BPM** shows in the
large three-digit field and the heart icon flashes. **If no signal is detected, the field shows
`---`.** In target heart rate mode the target range is posted next to the **65% of Max HR** and **85%
of Max HR** banners; a flashing **up or down arrow** tells you to pedal faster or slower, and the
**Target Heart Rate Achieved** banner lights when you are in range.

**Three dashes is not a fault.** It means no strap signal.

**Only the AB950 manual tells you where the signal comes from.** It devotes three pages to the heart
rate chest strap, headed "(NOT INCLUDED)", plus an interference page. **The AB900 manual has no chest
strap chapter at all** - the word appears twice in the whole book, once for the packing straps and
once in a typo, "if a heart rate chect strap is detected". So the AB900 describes a heart rate panel
and two Max HR banners without ever saying what feeds them or that a strap must be bought.

**Speed and distance follow one setting.** The units choice is made once and applies to both; see
`spirit-bike-console-air-bike-metric-imperial`.
