1. **Make sure Bluetooth on your device is turned on.**
2. **Click the Bluetooth symbol on the main screen of the machine** and make sure it is turned on -
   click the toggle switch if it is off.
3. After pushing the **Bluetooth button on the console, in the bottom left hand corner**, push the
   **"box" symbol on the right hand side of the console** to get to **full screen**, which removes
   the speed and incline columns. The device list is behind those columns.
4. **Find the device name** under **"Available Devices"** if this is the first time you are pairing
   it, or under **"Paired Devices"** if it has been paired to this machine before.
5. **Click the name of the device and wait until it says "connect".** The machine and your device
   pair automatically. **Follow any prompts on the screen or on your device** to finish.

When no device is in range the list reads **"No Bluetooth device found nearby"**.

**This is a support note, not a manual.** It names no model. It is scoped to the CT800ENT-2022 and
CT850ENT-2022 because it describes a console with **speed and incline columns** - a treadmill - laid
out as those two machines' touchscreen is, and because neither of their owner's manuals prints a
Bluetooth pairing procedure at all. Do not read it onto a bike or an elliptical.

**If the device will not pair, the note has two things to check first:**
`spirit-ent-console-bluetooth-not-pairing`.

**Screen mirroring is a separate function with its own pairing route** and its own rule about which
end the headphones connect to: `ct800ent-2022-console-screen-mirroring-ios` and
`ct800ent-2022-console-screen-mirroring-android`.

One caption on the note - a sentence about turning the Bluetooth device on before nearby devices can
detect it - is too damaged in the converted text to quote, and the paired-device name in its
screenshot is unreadable. Neither is reproduced here.
