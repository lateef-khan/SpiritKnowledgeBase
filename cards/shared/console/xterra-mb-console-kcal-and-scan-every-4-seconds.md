**KCAL MODE.** Press MODE to see the record of calories consumed from the beginning to the present point. Entering your weight and gender first makes it more accurate ([user set card](xterra-mb-console-user-set-gender-weight-and-units-from-kcal-mode.md)).

**SCAN MODE.** In KCAL mode, press MODE **quickly**; the scan icon appears and the computer scans your current results through **CLK, TM, MXS, AVS, DST, ODO and KCAL**, switching from one mode to the next **every 4 seconds**. The MB500 book is a scan read from the render (the console pages are OCR); the MB550 book prints the same console chapter natively and is cited alongside.
