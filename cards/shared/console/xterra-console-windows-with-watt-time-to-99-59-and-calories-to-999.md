| Readout | As printed |
|---|---|
| Time | Elapsed time up to **99:59**; counts down from a preset target |
| Speed | Current speed up to 99.9 M/H |
| Distance | Cumulative distance up to 99.99 M; counts down from a preset |
| RPM | Current rotations per minute |
| **Watt** | The amount of mechanical power the computer is receiving from your exercise (printed twice on the page) |
| Calories | Cumulative calories up to **999**; counts down from a preset - a rough guide, not for medical purposes |
| Body fat | Fat %, BMI and BMR from the pulse grips |
| Gripped pulse | Current heart rate in BPM ([gripped pulse card](xterra-console-gripped-pulse-needs-both-hands-and-alarms-at-the-target-pulse.md)) |
| Pulse Recovery | Grade after a one-minute countdown ([recovery card](xterra-console-pulse-recovery-grade-with-p-and-err.md)) |
| DISTANCE BAR | Every bar indicates 0.1 mile |
| SPEAKER | Connect an MP3 to the monitor with the audio wire; volume is controlled by the MP3 player |
| PROGRAM | Shows the program level from P1 to P24, P23 being watt control and P24 body fat (the Programs cards) |

The SB150 and FS150 print the same page without Watt, with time to 99:00 and calories to 9990 ([card](xterra-console-windows-with-time-to-99-00-calories-to-9990-and-a-distance-bar.md)). The SB250 display face shows TIME, DISTANCE, CAL/AGE, SPEED dial, WATT, PROGRAM/LEVEL, PULSE, GENDER and an RPM dial. What happens when you stop pedalling differs between the two: [EU150](xterra-console-shuts-off-after-four-minutes-and-keeps-all-values.md) keeps its values, [SB250](../../sb250-2024/console/shuts-off-240-seconds-after-pedalling-stops-and-clears-the-values.md) clears them.
