Incline can be changed **anytime after belt movement**. Press and hold the **Incline up/down keys** (console or handgrip), or jump straight with the incline keys **1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 12 and 15** on the console. The Incline Window shows the position as it changes. The decline-capable TT8 prints different grade presets; see its card.
