**Quick Start is the quickest way to start a workout: after the console powers up, just press the
Start key.**

- The **Time counts up from zero**.
- The **workload is adjusted by hand** with the **Up or Down buttons**.
- The **dot matrix has only the bottom row lit at first**. As you increase the workload **more rows
  light**, indicating a harder workout, and **the elliptical gets harder to pedal as the rows
  increase**.

All three console sections print this paragraph word for word.

**Neither manual gives a level count in Quick Start.** The 2005 manual's FUNCTIONS list is the only
place a range is printed, and it is on the XE150 pages: **LEVEL - indicates the level of work being
done from 1 to 16** (`xe150-2005-console-function-list-and-its-counting-ranges`). Nothing states the
XE350, XE550, XE100, XE200 or XE300 range, so **do not quote a number of levels for those five
machines**.

**The XE150 does not start this way.** Its console has no Start-then-adjust Quick Start of this
shape; see `xe150-2005-console-mode-key-and-the-icon-row`.

**Later XE ellipticals print the count.** The XE395-2010 and the 2021/2023 machines say **20
levels**, the XE795 says **40**.
