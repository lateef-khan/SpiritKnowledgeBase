1. Attach the Safety key to enable the display (if not already on).
2. Press the **Start** key to begin belt movement. Adjust to the desired speed using the **Speed ▲/▼** keys (console or handgrip), or press one of the **Speed keys 2 through 10**.
3. To slow the tread-belt, press and hold Speed ▼ (console or handgrip), or press a lower Speed key.
4. To stop the tread-belt, press the **Stop** key or pull away the Safety key.

**Incline.** Incline may be adjusted any time after belt movement. Press and hold the **Incline ▲/▼** keys on the left-hand side (console or handgrip), or use the Incline keys on the left of the console for a quicker change. The display indicates the incline position as adjustments are made.

The console pictured in both books has five incline quick keys (10, 8, 6, 4, 2) on the left and five speed quick keys (2, 4, 6, 8, 10) on the right, so "2 through 10" means the even values only.
