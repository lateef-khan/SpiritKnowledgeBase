The exercise equipment's Bluetooth device (**BLE 5.0**) can also be connected to a Bluetooth wireless heart-rate chest strap transmitter (BLE 5.0). The chest strap transmitter can receive signals even when the app is already in use. Wear the Bluetooth wireless heart-rate chest strap (**sold separately**) first, then commence connection. While connected, the console shows the heart-rate value via Bluetooth.

The books print no pairing menu or button for the strap; the sentence sits at the end of the XTERRA+ app page ([app card](xterra-treadmill-console-xterra-plus-app-ios-13-android-8.md)). Wearing the strap is on [the strap card](xterra-treadmill-console-chest-strap-not-included-with-a-cr2032-battery.md). The WS200, WS300 and TRX1400 books carry the same app page without this paragraph.
