**Wheel diameter 2.98.** The 2011 books (ATT8, TT8C) carry a **Grade return – On** step; the 2007/2008 VTT8 and 2009/2010 WTT8 books do not – on those, grade return lives in the maintenance menu Functions instead.

1. Remove the safety key.
2. Press and hold down the **Start** and **Fast (speed up)** buttons and replace the safety key. Keep holding Start and Fast until the window displays **"Factory settings"**, then press Enter.
3. Set Metric or English (miles vs kilometres) with the Up/Down key, then press Enter.
4. Make sure the wheel size diameter is **2.98**, then press Enter.
5. Adjust the minimum speed (if needed) to **0.5**, then press Enter.
6. Adjust the maximum speed (if needed) to **12.0**, then press Enter.
7. Adjust the maximum elevation (if needed) to **15**, then press Enter.
8. (2011 books only) **Grade return – On**: the incline returns to zero when Stop is pressed; EU standards require it off for sale in Europe.
9. Press START to begin the calibration. The process is automatic and the belt starts up without warning, so do not stand on the belt.
