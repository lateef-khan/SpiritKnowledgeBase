Tap the **right tab of the bottom panel** to open the Media section. Choose from pre-installed third party **Apps**, or use **Mirroring** to cast content from your smartphone to the console screen. Press the **?** icon for detailed instructions.

Once content is playing, use the **floating panel** to navigate, adjust the resistance level and switch full screen mode.

- Touch the panel's **top edge** and drag it around the screen so it does not cover the content.
- Press **Hide Panels** for full screen with no stats on the top and no tabs on the bottom. Press **Show Panels** to bring them back.
- Use the **arrow button on the right side of the panel** to hide and show the text labels, which minimises the panel further.
- Press **Apps** to go back to the content source selection.
