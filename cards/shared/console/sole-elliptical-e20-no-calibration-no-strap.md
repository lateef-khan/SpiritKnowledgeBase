Both E20 books print the full Start/Stop/Enter maintenance menu yet **no incline calibration procedure**, and their console chapters print **no heart-rate paragraph and no chest-strap section** (no CR2032/transmitter text anywhere in either book). Do not apply the E25 calibration or strap cards to the E20.
