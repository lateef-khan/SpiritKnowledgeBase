**The XT185 and XT285 manuals list six settings here. The XT385, XT485 and XT685 list five - they have no Key Tone entry. See `xt-2023-console-engineering-functions`.**

Press Enter on **Functions** to access the settings, and the **Speed + or -** arrow to scroll.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Sleep Mode | Turn on to have the console power down automatically after **15 minutes** of inactivity |
| Pause Mode | Turn on to allow **5 minutes** of pause; turn off to have the console pause indefinitely |
| Maintenance | **Reset lube message and odometer readings** |
| Units | Sets the display to readout in English or Metric display measurements |
| Key Tone | Turns off the speaker so no beeping sound is heard |
| BEEP | After closing, there is no sound for key operation |

**Key Tone and BEEP are printed as two separate entries with almost the same description**, and the manual does not explain the difference between them.
