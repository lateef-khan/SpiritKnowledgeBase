**The XT185 and XT285 manuals list six settings here. The XT385, XT485 and XT685 list five - they have no Key Tone entry. See `xt-2023-console-engineering-functions`.**

Press Enter on **Functions** to access the settings, and the **Speed + or -** arrow to scroll.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Sleep Mode | Turn on to have the console power down automatically after **15 minutes** of inactivity |
| Pause Mode | Turn on to allow **5 minutes** of pause; turn off to have the console pause indefinitely |
| Maintenance | **Reset lube message and odometer readings** |
| Units | Sets the display to readout in English or Metric display measurements |
| Key Tone | Turns off the speaker so no beeping sound is heard |
| BEEP | After closing, there is no sound for key operation |

**Key Tone and BEEP are printed as two separate entries with almost the same description**, and the manual does not explain the difference between them.

**The XT185-2023 and XT285-2023 service manuals print five entries, not six, and call the first one
Display Mode.** Their list: I **Display Mode - Turn off to have the console power down automatically
after 30 minutes of inactivity** (not Sleep Mode, not 15 minutes, and turned *off* rather than on);
II Pause Mode, 5 minutes or indefinite; III Maintenance - reset lube message and odometer readings;
IV Units; V Key Tone. **There is no BEEP entry in the service manuals.** Both documents are
reproduced as printed; the 30-minute figure agrees with the 2015 books
(`xt-2015-console-engineering-functions-key-tone`), the 15-minute figure is printed only in the 2023
owner's manuals.

**What Display Mode also does, from the same service manuals' E0 note:** *"When choose off, this is
mean display off after removed safety key. When choose on which is display on and appear E0 after
removed safety key."* So with Display Mode OFF the console blanks when the safety key is pulled; with
it ON the display stays lit and shows E0. Check that setting before suspecting the safety switch.
