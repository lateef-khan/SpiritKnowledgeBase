Same two-step charging as the 0.5 A port (machine on, own cable, check the icon, icon may stay off on hungry devices, **charging only, no data**), but this port gives **up to 5 Vdc / 1.0 amp**. Printed on the 2023 LCD consoles and the first-generation touchscreens (whose layouts label it CHARGE PORTABLE DEVICES WITH USB PORT). The second generation charges wirelessly instead.
