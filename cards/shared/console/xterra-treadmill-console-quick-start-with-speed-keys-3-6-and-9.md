1. Attach the Safety key to enable the display (if not already on).
2. Press the **Start** key to begin belt movement. Adjust the speed with the **Speed ▲/▼** keys — on the TR300 these are on the console or the handgrips — or press one of the **Speed buttons 3, 6 or 9** to jump straight to that speed.
3. To slow the tread-belt, press and hold Speed ▼ to the desired speed, or press a lower speed button.
4. To stop the tread-belt, press the **Stop** key or pull away the Safety key.

**Wording versus the console.** The TR200 book says "SPEED buttons 3 through 9", but the console it pictures has exactly three quick keys, 3, 6 and 9, between the speed arrows; the TR300 book says "Speed buttons 3,6,9" and its console has 9/6/3 keys for speed on the right and 9/6/3 keys for incline on the left.

**Incline (TR300 only).** Incline may be adjusted any time after belt movement: press and hold the Incline ▲/▼ keys on the left (console or handgrip), or use the incline quick keys on the left of the console for a faster change; the display indicates the incline position as adjustments are made. The TR200's three incline settings are set by hand under the deck, not from the console.
