The console includes a **built-in fan**. **Press the button on the upper right side of the console
to turn it on.**

**It is a 3-speed fan.** Each press increases the airflow. **After the third setting, the next
press turns the fan off.** So the cycle is off, low, medium, high, off.

Both CE900 manuals print this word for word, in the paragraph directly under the NUMBER KEY PAD
heading.

**The other Spirit ellipticals do not print a fan speed.** The CE800-2013 and CE850-2016 manuals
say only that the key is on the front right of the console:
`spirit-ce-console-fan-key-on-the-front-right`. The CE800-2021, CE800-2023, CE850-2020, CE850-2022
and CES880 manuals label a **Fan** on the console drawing but never say how to switch it on. The
CE800ENT's fan is a fixed grille with an adjustable angle and no switch is described.
