**There is an anatomical figure at the top of the console.** It lights the areas the machine is
working. It lights during **any** of the programs, and you can steer it by customising the resistance
profile during set-up; accept the default profile and the program decides.

**Upper body.** The lights come on **when you are holding the swing arms, or at any time your hands
are not on the pulse grip sensors**.

**Lower body, three degrees of engagement:** **Green** is minimal involvement, **Amber** medium,
**Red** full or heavy.

| Pedal direction | What lights |
|---|---|
| **Forward** | Gluteals and Quadriceps **Amber**; Hamstrings and Calves **Green** |
| **Reverse** | Calves, Hamstrings and Quadriceps **Amber**; Gluteals **Green** |

**Five printings print this word for word**: the XE895-2018 (p. 19), the XE295-2019 (p. 18), the
XG400-2019 (p. 17), the XE295-2021 and the XG400-2021. The XG400 manuals say "E-Glide Trainer"
where the XE295 says "elliptical trainer", and the XE895 writes "upper body LED's" where the others
write "upper body lights". Nothing else differs.

**The XE895 belongs here and not with the XE795**, even though the two share a console face. Its
muscle figure follows this pedal-direction rule, not the XE795's resistance-level rule; see
`spirit-xe-console-face-call-outs-muscle-profile-heart-rate-profile-and-speakers`.

**The XE795 figure works on a completely different rule** - its upper body LEDs never light, and its
lower body is banded by resistance level rather than pedal direction:
`xe795-2021-console-muscle-figure-lights-by-resistance-level`.

**The XE395 has a muscle figure and it is banded by incline, not by pedal direction.** An earlier
reading of this card said the XE395-2010 had none. It does: both the XE395-2010 (printed p. 15) and
the XE395-2018 (p. 19) print an anatomical figure whose lower-body colours are set by **incline
level 0-7.5 and 8-20** in each pedal direction. That is a third rule and it has its own card:
`spirit-xe395-console-muscle-figure-bands-by-incline-level`. Do not answer an XE395 question from
this one.

**The XE195 has no muscle figure** in either its 2019 or its 2021 printing, and neither do the 2023
machines or anything older than 2010.
