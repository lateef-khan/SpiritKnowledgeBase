**Scope.** Every declared SOLE model except the nine 2016 to 2020 bikes with Dyaco service manuals. Those bikes are out of scope because no source says a watch or a third-party strap connects to their consoles.

Sole+ can send workouts to Strava. It sends elapsed time, average speed, max speed, calories burned, distance, elevation gain, and heart rate.

**Set it up**

1. In Sole+, open any workout activity and tap the **share** button for Strava.
2. The first time, a web browser opens. Log in with the Strava account.
3. Tick the box that says **Upload your activities**, then tap **Authorize**.

**If a red warning says activities will not contain heart rate data**

The customer must turn a Strava setting on. In Strava, go to **Profile > Settings > Health Data** and turn on recording health data. Sole+ can then send heart rate history to Strava.

**Every workout must be sent by hand.** There is no automatic sync. Each workout is synced one at a time from the share menu for that activity.
