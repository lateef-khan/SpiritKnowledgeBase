Every bike is paired at the factory. Re-pair only for commercial or maintenance work.

1. Hold **MODE and PAGE together for 3 seconds** to enter setting.
2. Wake the speed sensor by stepping on the bike. The transmitter sends its signal to the console for the pair stage.
3. **A "0" on the LCD means the pairing worked.** **"Err" means it failed** — press **PAGE** and repeat the pair stage.

The console leaves the pairing stage on its own after 10 seconds if a transmitter is already paired.

The LED on the transmitter flashes a few times when it wakes and is ready to pair.
