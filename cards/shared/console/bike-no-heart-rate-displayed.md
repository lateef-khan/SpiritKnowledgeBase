1. Check the hand pulse sensor cable is connected properly to the console, and that the hand pulse assembly cables are connected properly to the sensor wires.
2. On the recumbents (R92 2016, LCR 2016) also remove the left rear shroud and check the handpulse cable assemblies are properly joined to the hand pulse sensor assembly cable.
3. If everything is connected well, check the hand pulse assembly. Replace the cable if needed.
4. On the recumbents, if the installation is fine, probe every connection with an instrument and replace what fails.

The manuals add that the console and related parts are inspected before shipping, so a defective part is unlikely.
