**Scope:** 2023 SOLE touchscreen machines. The source names bikes, ellipticals **and** treadmills.

**This is the machine sending audio out to headphones.** It is not the phone sending audio into the machine's speakers, and it is not the SOLE+ app connecting. Those are in `sole-bluetooth-not-connecting`.

**Steps**

1. Log in to a user profile on the machine.
2. Tap the **Bluetooth** button in the **top left corner** of the screen.
3. Make sure the Bluetooth setting is switched **on**.
4. Put the headphones into pairing mode. Every brand does this differently, so follow the headphone maker's instructions.
5. Pick the headphones from the list on the screen.

A **green dot** appears next to the Bluetooth icon once the connection is live.

**If the headphones do not show up in the list**

1. Check the headphones are not already connected to something else, such as a phone. That alone will keep them out of the machine's search list.
2. If the menu has stopped searching, toggle the Bluetooth setting **off and back on** to refresh the list.
3. Try a different pair of headphones. If the second pair shows up, the problem is the first pair, not the machine.

**If the headphones connected to another device since last time**, disconnect them from that device, then pick them again by hand in the machine's Bluetooth menu.

**One audio source at a time.** The machine does not support multi-audio. It cannot play audio from two sources at once.
