"PULSE: (Wireless chest strap sensor sold separately) - In Manual Mode, set the target heart rate value by pressing up/down (available range is **30 to 240 in 1 BPM increments**). The computer will display user's heart rate during training. **When you are out of range of the target value during exercise, the computer will beep and PULSE will blink** until target range is resumed or wireless chest strap sensor is removed. **The pulse measurement function is only usable with the optional wireless chest strap sensor.**" Word for word in all three books. None of these rowers has hand-pulse grips, and none of the three owner's manuals prints a wearing page.

**Which strap.** The ERG600W recovery page says the meter "works with a **5.3 KHz** chest strap heart rate monitor (not included)"; the ERG550W service manual's Q&A answers "the console can not get pulse data" with "check if use a **5.0KHZ** chest belt, you must wear chest belt correctly". Both name an analogue (non-Bluetooth) strap; the card follows the owner's manual's 5.3 kHz - the Polar-type analogue frequency the ERG800W also names ([ERG800W card](../../erg800w-2023/console/heart-rate-from-a-5-3-khz-or-bluetooth-chest-belt-not-included.md)) - and reads the service manual's 5.0 as a rounding of the same figure. The ERG650W book names no frequency. A Bluetooth strap is not mentioned by any of the three books. The recovery test on the ERG600W needs the same strap ([recovery card](../../erg600w-2021/console/recovery-counts-down-from-00-60-and-grades-from-outstanding-to-poor.md)); the ERG650W's Single THR target runs 90 to 200 and is a programs card. The Spirit water rowers show three dashes without a strap ([Spirit twin](spirit-water-rower-console-heart-rate-panel-three-dashes.md)); these books do not say what an empty pulse window shows.
