**Three dashes mean no signal, not a fault.** *"When console detects a signal from a wireless
monitor, the BPM (beats-per-minute) will show in the large three-digit field and the heart icon will
flash. However, if no signal is detected, three dashes - - - will display to indicate no heart rate
detected."*

**Two things must both be true before a pulse appears:** *"To detect heart rate, the heart rate
receiver must be installed and a heart rate chest strap (not included) must be used."* **The receiver
is a fitted part, not standard on every build** - the CRW900 assembly chapter has its own HEART RATE
INSTALLATION step for pasting receiver [29] into place. **A machine showing dashes may simply have no
receiver in it.**

**There are no hand pulse grips on either machine.** Neither manual mentions contact grips anywhere,
and neither console drawing labels one. **A wireless strap is the only way to get a reading.**

**The Target Heart Rate arrows are part of the panel.** *"The up or down symbol will flash to indicate
that an increase or decrease of workout speed is needed to reach the desired range. When the user's
heart rate is in the target range, the icons will light."*

**The two panels are not identical, and the difference is printed on the glass.**

| | CRW900 | CRW800H2O |
|---|---|---|
| **Banners on the panel** | **AGE**, **65%**, **85%**, **Max** | none |
| **How the target range is described** | *"The target value range will post next to the 65%, 85% and Max HR banners"* | *"The target value range will vary by 10% from the default data"* |
| **What Stop Mode replays** | 85%, 65% and the max heart rate, **5 seconds each, alternately** | the **current** heart rate |

**Quote the right one.** A CRW800H2O owner has no 65% or 85% banner to look at, and a CRW900 owner
has no 10% band. **How the Target Heart Rate program computes its number is a programs question**;
this card covers only what the panel displays.

**This is not the air rowers' heart rate window.** The CRW800 and XRW600 print a numeric **40 - 220
bpm** range and show **nothing at all** when there is no signal - no dashes
(`crw800-2024-console-window-functions`). **An empty window on one console and three dashes on the
other mean the same thing.**
