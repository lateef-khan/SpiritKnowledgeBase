| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| TIME | Length of time in minutes and seconds. It counts up or down while you pedal. When you stop pedalling it stops counting after 3 seconds. |
| CADENCE & SPEED | Cadence is how fast the cranks turn, in RPM. Approximate speed can show here too, in MPH or KPH. Cadence also has a bar graph. Average cadence or speed shows automatically 3 seconds after you stop pedalling. |
| DISTANCE | Approximate distance on the bike, worked out as if the tyres had the same diameter as the flywheel. |
| CALORIES (KCAL) | Approximate calories burned, from your instantaneous heart rate, age and weight. |
| HEART RATE | Approximate heart rate from the chest belt. On HR models only. Average heart rate shows after 3 seconds if the console cannot read a current heart rate. |
| POWER (WATT) | Approximate applied force during the workout. |
