**Templates** are preset programs with a preserved set of parameters.

- A program can be saved as a template on completion, or created from scratch in the **Templates** tab: tap **New Template**, choose a base program, then save it with adjusted properties.
- **A maximum of 12 template programs can be stored.**
- To remove unwanted templates, press **Edit Templates** in the Templates tab.

**Favourites** put a program on the home screen.

- **Up to 3 favourite programs appear on the Home screen.**
- To mark one, open the program setup page and tap the **ribbon icon in the upper left corner**.
- All labelled programs are under the **Favorites** tab. To remove a label press **Edit Favorites**, or un-tap the ribbon icon on the program setup page.

To begin, press **Start This Program** on the screen or the physical **START** key on the machine.
