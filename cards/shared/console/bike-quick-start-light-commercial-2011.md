After the console powers up, press the **Start** key. This initiates **Quick Start** mode.

- Time counts **up** from zero.
- The workload and the resistance are adjusted by hand with the **Up or Down** buttons.
- The dot matrix display shows a track with a blinking dot indicating your progress as it travels around the track.

**This console has no quick level keys.** The 2013 console of the same family adds a numbered row of them and names them in this same paragraph.
