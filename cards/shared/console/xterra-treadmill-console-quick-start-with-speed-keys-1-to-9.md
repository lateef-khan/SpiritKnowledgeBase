1. Attach the Safety key to enable the display (if not already on).
2. Press the **Start** key to begin belt movement. Adjust to the desired speed using the **Speed ▲ and ▼** keys, or press one of the **SPEED buttons 1 through 9** to jump to that speed.
3. To slow the tread-belt, press and hold the Speed ▼ key to the desired speed, or press a lower SPEED button.
4. To stop the tread-belt, press the **Stop** key or pull away the Safety key.

The console face shows the nine speed keys in one row under the LCD; on the TR150 the **Enter** key sits at the end of that row, on the TRX1000 the Program and Enter keys sit to the left of the LCD. The TR150 service manual describes the same keypad as "RAPID button: 9 preset buttons for rapid speed: 0 to 9", but the owner's manual and the printed console show 1 through 9.

The same books describe pausing and resetting on [the pause card](xterra-treadmill-console-pause-with-stop-holds-readings-five-minutes.md). Incline on these two machines is a manual pop-pin adjustment, not a console function.
