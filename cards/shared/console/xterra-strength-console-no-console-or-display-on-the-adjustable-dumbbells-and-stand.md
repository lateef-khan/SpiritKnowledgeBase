None of the four 2022 strength books - the 12.5 lb pair, the 25 lb and 55 lb adjustable dumbbells, and the dumbbell stand - prints a console, a display, a monitor, a computer, a screen, a Bluetooth or an app anywhere; the word "display" appears only in the warranty's "store display models". The only control is mechanical: "Choose the desired weight by **rotating the middle of the handle grip**. The handle must be fully inserted into the dumbbell tray to adjust weight. The arrows printed on the handle will show the direction to rotate." The safety page calls it the **weight selector dial** - "Do not press the safety lock or turn the weight selector dial when the dumbbell or handle is lifted from the dumbbell tray" - and the parts list a "Handle inner cover (dial)".

So there is nothing to pair, reset or power. How the dial, tray and plates work, the weight steps, and why the handle will not turn are assembly, specs and errors cards. The rowers of the same wave that do carry a console start at [the ERG160 card](../../erg160-2020/console/single-mode-key-with-scan-every-6-seconds-and-six-readouts.md).
