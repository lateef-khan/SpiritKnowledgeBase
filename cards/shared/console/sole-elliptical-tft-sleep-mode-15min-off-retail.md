**ON**: energy-saving state after 15 minutes of inactivity. **OFF**: the console stays lit while power is on. **RETAIL** (touchscreen books): a demo video runs after 3 minutes of inactivity. Switch on TFT displays under Settings / Sleep Mode; on the LCD E25-2023 hold **Stop, Enter and Display for 5 seconds** to turn sleep ON or OFF. The E25-2023 maintenance menu also carries 'Sleep Mode - turn on to power down after 15 minutes'.
