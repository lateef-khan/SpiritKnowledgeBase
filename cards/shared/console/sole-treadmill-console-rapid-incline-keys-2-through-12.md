Incline may be adjusted anytime after belt movement. Hold the incline keys (console or handgrip), or jump straight with rapid key **2 through 12** on the left-hand side of the console. The display shows the incline position as it changes. Other books print different sets: 1-3-6-9-12-15, 1-3-6-9-12-14-15, 2-4-6-8-10-12, 0-12 levels or 2-through-10.
