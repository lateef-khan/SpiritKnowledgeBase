The SRVO Complete book (SRB101 Ver.A) covers a **folding workout bench and its accessories** (bench, extension safety cables, short bar): registration, precautions, safeguards, item checklist, assembly/folding. It prints no console, display, button or app content.

Control-panel operation for the SRVO itself (knob, flashing display, start/stop, wireless switch, SOLE+ app) lives on the SRVO console cards (see_also), sourced from the seminar, the app screenshots and the SRVO owner book.
