The machine **comes equipped with a Bluetooth transceiver** that lets it interact with selected
phones or tablets via the **Spirit+ App**. The app is **free** on the **Apple Store and Google Play** -
search for **"Spirit+"**, or scan the QR code printed in the manual.

1. **Download the app** by scanning the QR code.
2. **Open the app** on your phone or tablet and make sure **Bluetooth is enabled on the device**.
3. **In the app, click the Bluetooth icon to search for your Spirit+ equipment.**
4. **Under the Bluetooth scan result list, select the machine to connect.** When the app and
   equipment are synced, **the Bluetooth icon on the console display will light up**. Click
   **"DISPLAY"** and you may now start using the app.
5. When your exercise is complete, choose **"END & SAVE WORKOUT"** to store the workout data. You
   will be prompted to sync your data with each available fitness cloud site.

**Supported fitness cloud sites: Apple Health, Google Fit, MapMyFitness and Fitbit**, with more to
come. **You must download the matching fitness app for its icon to be active and available.**

**The XT185 owner's manual has no Bluetooth or Spirit+ section at all** - it never mentions
Bluetooth, the app, or a USB port. Do not quote this card for an XT185.
