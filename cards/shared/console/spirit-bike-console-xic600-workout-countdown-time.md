**To set it:**

1. Press and hold the **right hand key** while the bottom section of the display is showing
   **SPEED**. The **Minutes** field starts flashing.
2. Press the **right hand key** to increase the Minutes setting.

**To clear the exercise time:**

Press the **left hand key** until the **time** is displayed, then hold the **right hand key for
three seconds**.

**The window you start from decides what you get.** Held from SPEED the right hand key opens this
timer; held from TIME it clears the time. The manual never gives a single "settings" menu on this
console - each setting hangs off a different one of the four bottom-line values.

**Range.** The console's published count down range is **1 to 99 minutes** and its count up range is
**00:01 to 99:59**. See `spirit-bike-console-xic600-measuring-ranges`.

**Left alone the time counts up.** The manual describes no default countdown; without setting one
here the workout timer simply counts up while you pedal, and stops 3 seconds after you stop.
