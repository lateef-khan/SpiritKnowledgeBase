1. Download **Kinomap** or **Zwift** from Google Play or the App Store.
2. Open the app, then press the **phone icon** on the bike console.
3. When the app finds the bike console, the console shows the pairing screen.

The console uses **BLE 4.0**. The manual points to a video of the pairing process but does not give a link.
