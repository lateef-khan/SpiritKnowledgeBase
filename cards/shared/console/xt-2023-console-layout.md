The console overview diagram labels these parts:

- **LCD Window**
- **Adjustable Fan**
- **USB Charging** port
- **Handrail Lock**
- **Handlebar Incline Control** (left) and **Handlebar Speed Control** (right)
- **Hand Pulse Grip**
- **Cup Holder**
- **Safety Key**
- **Program Buttons**
- **Child Lock**
- **Direct Speed & Incline Buttons**

All three of these machines share the same labelled layout. The XT185 and XT285 panels are labelled
differently - see their own cards.
