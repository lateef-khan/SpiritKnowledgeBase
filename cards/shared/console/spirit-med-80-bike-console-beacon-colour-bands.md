**"The Beacon uses brightly colored lights on the top of the console to gauge exercise effort and
remind physical therapists of their patients' progress at a glance."** It "may be turned on or off in
the setup", and "the color indicators can be set to show three different measure: heart rate, power,
or progress".

| Colour | % of max heart rate | % of watts | Workout progress |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WHITE** | 0-59% | 0-20% | 0-24% |
| **BLUE** | 60-69% | 21-40% | 25-49% |
| **GREEN** | 70-79% | 41-60% | 50-74% |
| **YELLOW** | 80-89% | 61-80% | 75-99% |
| **RED** | 90-100% | 81-100% | 100% |

"(Color indicates: White & Blue: initial condition of warm up or low stage. Green: ready for use or
medium stage. Yellow: the high stage caution or close to the end. Red: the highest stage warning or the
final stage.)"

**The page introduces the console as the "MED 8 series products (8.0T, 8.0U, 8.5R, 8.5S, 8.5UE)"**,
and the same table is printed for the ergometer and the treadmill
(`85ue-2025-console-beacon-colour-bands`, `80t-2026-console-beacon-indicator`). The beacon setting
lives in Settings (`spirit-med-80-bike-console-settings-menu-and-the-four-viewing-modes-with-close-up-mode`)
and the service manual's Beacon Test cycles the five colours
(`spirit-med-80-bike-console-service-tab-machine-type-loopback-nfc-keypad-beacon-and-crank-calibration`).
