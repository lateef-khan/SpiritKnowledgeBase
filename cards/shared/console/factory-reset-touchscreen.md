1. On the Log In screen, tap the words "Log In" rapidly many times. If those words are not there, tap "Welcome" or the Sole logo instead.
2. A message asks "Are you sure you want to Factory Reset?". Tap **Yes**.
3. The console resets to the login screen. All profiles on the machine are gone. If the customer has a profile in the Sole+ app they can relink it and their information comes back.

**Note**: this reset only removes user profiles and clears the media apps' cache. It does not sign out of the media apps and it does not reset any app data.
