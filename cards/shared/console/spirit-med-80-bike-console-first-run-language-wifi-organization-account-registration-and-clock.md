**Select language & WiFi setting**

1. Select the preferred language, then click **Next**.
2. Turn WiFi **on**.
3. Choose the preferred WiFi network, then click **Next**.
4. Enter the password for the network, then click **Connect**.

**Organization account sign in** - "Enter Organization' account info: email and password to sign in.
If you don't have an organization account yet, please click the **'No account yet'** at the left
below."

**Create new organization member's account** - "Scan the QR code and complete the following form for
creating a new account."

**Product Registration** - "Enter the Administrator's email & password to sign in", then "enter the
product's Serial number for registration".

**Time and Date** - "Set the local time/date and click **Start** to finish setup."

**The organization account is the facility's account, not the patient's.** Patients log in
afterwards by QR code, NFC or a PIN, or exercise as a guest
(`spirit-med-80-bike-console-login-by-qr-code-nfc-or-pin-or-as-a-guest`); the PIN is generated "in
the Dyaco Insight App", which is the only place either book names the app. **Wi-Fi is what the cloud
upload rides on** (`spirit-med-80-bike-console-wifi-ble-ftms-and-ant-plus`), and the login page warns
that without it "you will enter the system as a guest in offline status".

**Language, Wi-Fi and the clock can be changed later in Settings**
(`spirit-med-80-bike-console-settings-menu-and-the-four-viewing-modes-with-close-up-mode`). The 8.0T
treadmill prints the same first run (`80t-2026-console-wifi-setup`,
`80t-2026-console-organization-account-setup`).
