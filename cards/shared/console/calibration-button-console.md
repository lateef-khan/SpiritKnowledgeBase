1. **Clear error codes**: power the treadmill off, wait a few seconds, then power it back on.
2. **Enter factory settings mode**:
   - With the machine on, remove the safety key.
   - Press and hold **Start** and **Speed Up (Fast)** on the main console. Use the console directly under the screen, not the handrail buttons.
   - Insert the safety key while holding the buttons, and keep holding for about 10 seconds.
   - The screen should show "Factory Settings". If it does not, repeat the steps.
3. **Navigate to calibration**: press **Enter** repeatedly until the screen stops changing. It should then prompt you to press Start.
4. **Start calibration**: step off the treadmill first, because it moves on its own. Press **Start**. The machine runs the speed and the incline from lowest to highest by itself.
5. **After**: on success the machine reboots to the home screen. Note any error codes that appear, and watch the behaviour, for example if it stops early or will not incline.

2026 non-touchscreen machines use a different button combination. See the 2026 calibration card.
