The bike has a Bluetooth wireless music receive function.

1. On a phone or tablet, open the Bluetooth device list.
2. Search for the Bluetooth name **"Bt-speaker"**.
3. Click to connect.

Music from the phone or tablet then plays through the exercise equipment.

This is separate from the wired Audio In Jack, which also feeds the same speakers.
