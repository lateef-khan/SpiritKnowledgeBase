**Neither bike has a console.** These two manuals describe no console, no display, no computer and
no monitor. Nothing on either bike shows speed, cadence, time, distance, calories or heart rate.

**The only rider control is the tension knob**, which sets pedalling resistance and doubles as the
brake: turn it clockwise for more resistance and counter-clockwise for less, and press down on it to
brake the flywheel.

**Do not read this as a fault.** A rider asking why their screen is blank on one of these machines
has no screen to blank. The nearest bikes that do carry a console are the XIC600 and the CIC850,
whose manuals devote a whole chapter to it.

**The CIC850-2022 is not this bike even though it looks like its successor.** It carries a DT-3268F
console with nine display windows, and its cover prints the CIC800's part number 800390, which makes
the two easy to confuse when working from a cover alone.
