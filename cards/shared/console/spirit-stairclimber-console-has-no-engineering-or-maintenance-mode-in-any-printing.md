**There is no such mode on this machine, and there never was.**

**It was not removed in 2024.** The words **engineering**, **maintenance mode**, **service mode**,
**key test**, **display test**, **odometer** and **sleep mode** appear in **none** of the four Spirit
stair climber owner's manuals - the CSC900 of 2019, 2021 and 2024 and the CSC880 of 2025. The only
occurrences of the string "unit" in any of them are in the warranty and returns chapters. All four
were searched over the whole book, native text and the OCR supplements of the flattened image pages
together, so the absence is not a page the extractor missed.

**The only setting these consoles expose is the unit system**, and it is a bare key sequence: press
**1, 2, 3, OK** from the running display (`csc900-2024-console-four-display-windows`). The manuals
print **no menu, no confirmation message and no way to see which system is currently set**.

**So there is no key test, no display test, no odometer reset, no beep control, no pause-mode
setting, no sleep-mode setting, no CSAFE protocol choice and no sensor test on any Spirit stair
climber.** Every one of those exists on the CS800 and CRS800S steppers
(`spirit-stepper-console-maintenance-menu-sleep-default-on`) and on the CVC800 vertical climber
(`cvc800-console-engineering-mode-two-second-hold`). **Do not carry a three-key hold from a stepper
to a stair climber**; there is nothing behind it.

**These manuals also print no error codes and no sleep timeout.** A technician who needs a
diagnostic on one of these machines has nothing in the owner's manual to reach for.
