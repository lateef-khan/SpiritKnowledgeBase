The console LCD screen displays your **current heart rate anytime a pulse is detected**. The graph
**to the right of the LCD screen** shows your **current heart rate % in relation to your projected
maximum heart rate**, which is **determined by the age you entered during the programming phase of
any program**.

| Percentage of maximum | Colour |
|---|---|
| **0-60%** | **White** |
| **61-70%** | **Blue** |
| **71-80%** | **Green** |
| **81-90%** | **Yellow** |
| **91% or more** | **Red** |

**The XT185 and XT285 manuals do not print this colour scale**, although their workout screens are
also labelled with a Maximum Heart Rate Percentage reading.
