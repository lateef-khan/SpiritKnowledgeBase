Past the shared calorie disclaimer (own card): 'you will continue to burn calories at about the same rate as during exercise for **20-30 minutes** after you have finished exercising!' The VE35/VE95 and WE55/WE95 books print different afterburn claims (own cards); the 2011+ books print none.
