**The console has built-in speakers and an audio input jack.**

**There is no volume control on the console. The volume must be controlled on the audio source** -
the phone, tablet or MP3 player you have plugged in.

**This is a wired jack, not Bluetooth audio.** No 2015 XT manual describes playing music over
Bluetooth; the Bluetooth transceiver in these machines is described only for the Spirit Fit App
(see `xt-2015-console-spirit-fit-app-pairing`). The 2023 XT consoles do pair as a Bluetooth speaker
under the name "Bt-speaker" - see `xt-2023-console-bluetooth-speaker`.

**The XT285-2015 console diagram labels "Integrated Speakers for MP3 Player" and an "Audio Jack"**,
but that manual prints no Speakers paragraph, so it never says whether that console has a volume
control. See `xt285-2015-console-layout`. **The XT185-2015 has neither** - its diagram labels no
speakers and no audio jack.
