The console drawing captions read **SPEAKER, COOLING FANS, AUDIO IN JACK (MP3, CD, OR SMARTPHONE), HEADPHONE JACK, INCLINE/SPEED KEYS and CONTACT HEART RATE SENSORS** (the F85 adds CHARGE PORTABLE DEVICES WITH USB PORT). **No patch cable and no Bluetooth music are printed** for these consoles, unlike the LCD books (patch cable) and the later books (Bt-speaker). The ST90 drawing captions SAFETY KEYS instead of a safety-key note.
