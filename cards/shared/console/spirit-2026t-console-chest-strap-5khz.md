**The chest strap transmitter is not a standard part - it is a separate purchase.**

**Most transmitters that operate at 5kHz frequency will also work.** The 7.0T's
Vo2 test page names a **Polar T31/T34** as an example of a strap to wear.

**The 8.0T manual contradicts itself here.** Page 24 lists the console's wireless
connectivity as **Wi-Fi, BLE and ANT+**, and says **BLE & ANT+** are what connect a
heart rate sensor - which is not a 5kHz analogue strap. Both statements are in the
same manual and this card does not resolve which is right; see
`80t-2026-console-wireless-connectivity`.

**The 4.0T is different again**, and prints Bluetooth or ANT+; see
`40t-2026-console-chest-strap-bluetooth-ant`.
