**Estimated transmitter battery life is 2500 hours.**

**If your chest strap has a replaceable battery the replacement battery is
CR2032.** Not every strap has one - the manual only says what to use if yours
does.

**Although the transmitter is water resistant, moisture can create false signals.**
Completely dry the transmitter after use to prolong battery life.

All three 2026 manuals print this note identically, inside the wearing
instructions; see `spirit-2026t-console-chest-strap-wearing`.

**Both MT200 manuals print the same note** - the 2010 manual on its p. 23, the 2022
manual on its p. 41. **The 2010 manual names the cell as a Panasonic CR2032**; the
2022 manual, like the 2026 manuals, prints only CR2032.

**The 2025 printings of the 4.0T and 7.0T owner's manuals print the same 2500 hours and CR2032 on the same pages**, so this card covers the 40t-2025 and 70t-2025 as well. The medical bikes carry the figures on `spirit-med-bike-console-chest-strap-5-khz-2500-hours-and-a-cr2032`.
