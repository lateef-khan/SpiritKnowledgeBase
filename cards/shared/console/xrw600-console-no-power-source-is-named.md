**Neither XRW600 manual says what powers the console.** There is no BATTERY INSTALLATION
section, no cell size, no compartment location, no adaptor and no power switch anywhere in either
book. **This is an absence, not an oversight in the reading.**

**How it was checked, three ways:**

1. **A loose word search** for `batter` across both manuals returns **two hits each**, and both are
   the *chest strap* transmitter: *"prolong battery life (estimated transmitter battery life is 2500
   hours). The replacement battery is Panasonic CR2032."* Nothing about the console.
2. **Every page was rendered at 300 dpi and compared against the text extraction.** No page of either
   manual hides text behind a picture - the largest gap on any of the 40 pages is 51 words, and it is
   the console drawing's own call-outs. **There is no flattened page holding a battery section.**
3. **The assembly chapter never connects the console.** All four steps bolt frame, track, pedals and
   seat; the console is already on the mast in the parts drawing. No console wire, no battery.

**What the book does print about electricity is treadmill boilerplate.** The safety page asks for *"a
110-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet with only the Rower plugged into the circuit"* and warns against a
*"damaged cord or plug"*. **Those lines appear unchanged in Spirit manuals for machines that do have
a motor**; nothing in this book identifies a cord on this rower, and the assembly chapter fits none.

**Do not borrow the CRW800's answer.** The CRW800 shares this console chapter almost word for word
and **does** print a battery section - **two C cells, compartment on the back of the console**
(`crw800-2024-console-two-c-batteries`) - but the XRW600 books do not, and the two water rowers take
**four AA** instead (`spirit-water-rower-console-four-aa-batteries`). **Three Spirit rowers, three
different answers, and for this one the manual gives none.**

**The service manual answers what the owner's manuals do not.** The XRW600-2019 (DW400-YR002)
service manual powers the console from a **12 V DC power adapter through a DC jack** - *"check the
adapter is correctly inserted into the DC jack"*, *"AC power input is AC100 ~ 240V and the output is
DC12V"* - and names no battery for the console at all
(`spirit-rower-console-powered-by-a-12v-dc-adapter-through-a-dc-jack`). The absence on this card is
the owner's manuals' alone.

**What to tell a caller with a dead XRW600 display:** the owner's manual does not name the power
source; the service manual says an adapter, so check the adapter and its DC jack first, then the
back of the console on the machine in front of you rather than quoting a cell size. **Neither of these manuals has a troubleshooting section either** - the table of contents
runs Safety, Electrical, Operation, Registration, Assembly, Console Operation, Programmable Features,
Heart Rate Transmitter, General Maintenance, Warranty, and stops.
