Three causes, in order:

1. **The belt is not worn properly.** The belt must touch skin and face the right way.
2. **The rider is out of range.** The belt must be in front of the console, **within 3 feet**.
3. **The belt battery is weak or dead.** Fit a new lithium **CR2032**.

The matrix lists one more row, "Chest belt too close to the bike", with the cause "weak battery" and the same CR2032 fix. The manual does not explain how a belt being too close causes a weak battery, so treat that row as a battery check.
