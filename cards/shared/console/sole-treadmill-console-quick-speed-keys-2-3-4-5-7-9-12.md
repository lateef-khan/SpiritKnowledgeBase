Press **Start**, then adjust with the **Speed up/down keys** (console or handgrip), or jump straight with the speed keys **2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9 or 12**. To slow down, hold the **Speed down (Slow)** key, or press the speed adjust keys **2 through 12**. The keys behave the same before and during a workout.
