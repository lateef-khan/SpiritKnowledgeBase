**Every one of the four books calls out a 7.5" LCD Display** on its Display Windows page, and names
the keys under it **FAN, START, LEVEL, STOP, ENTER** - readable only on the drawing. The Function
Button Locations page names the **PROGRAM BUTTONS**, the **CONTROL KEYS** and a **Fan Key** described
as "Cooling fan switch on or off".

**The program button row is the one thing that differs:**

| Book | Program buttons as printed | Count |
|---|---|---|
| XBR25-2016 | Manual, Cardio, Hill, Fat Burn, Interval, HR | 6 |
| XBR55-2016 | Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, Interval, User1~2, HR1~2 | 10 |
| XBU55-2016 | Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, Interval, User1~2, HR1~2 | 10 |
| XBR95-2016 | Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Strength, Interval, Calories, Fusion, 2 User, 2HR | 11 |

**The XBR95 list drops a Cardio key that its own drawing shows.** The button row on the
XBR95-2016 drawing reads MANUAL, HILL, FATBURN, CARDIO, STRENGTH, INTERVAL, CALORIES, FUSION and then
the user keys; the owner's manual's legend prints the full twelve
(`spirit-residential-bike-console-face-ten-programs`). Count the keys on the machine before naming
a program; the programs themselves are held with the programs cards.

**The XBU55 drawing is silk-screened MUSCLE PROFILE** above its anatomical figure; the XBR55 and
XBR95 drawings carry the same figure without the label being readable on the render.

**"7.5-inch LCD" is a service-manual figure.** The owner's manuals' drawing of these consoles labels
a "Large Blue-LED Matrix Window" and never gives a size; that call-out list is
`spirit-residential-bike-console-face-ten-programs`, and the XBR25's six-button version is
`spirit-residential-bike-console-face-six-programs`.
