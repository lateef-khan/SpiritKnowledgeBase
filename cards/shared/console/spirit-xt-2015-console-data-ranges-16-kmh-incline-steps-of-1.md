**These two consoles work between 1.0 and 16.0 km/h (0.5 to 12 miles) and step the incline by 1.**
The XT385-2015 and XT485-2015 reach 18.0 km/h and step the incline by 0.5
(`spirit-xt-2015-console-data-ranges-18-kmh-incline-half-steps`); the 2023 XT185 stops at 10 mph
(`xt185-2023-console-data-ranges-16-kmh-10-mph`). Do not carry one console's ceiling to another.

| Reading | Display range | Work range | Step / note |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPEED | 0.0 to 99.9 | **1.0 to 16.0 km, or 0.5 to 12 miles** | FAST / SLOW change it by 0.1 km/h (mph) |
| Incline | 0 to 99.9 | **0 to 15**; preset value 0 to 15 | UP / DOWN change it by **1** |
| TIME | 0:00 to 99:99 | 0:00 to 99:59 | Counts up by default; counts down if the user sets a time. Count-down setup range 10:00 to 99:00 |
| PACE | 99.99 | 0.00 to 99.59 | Time to walk one km or mile; shows 0.00 with no speed |
| DISTANCE | 0.00 to 99.9 | 0.00 to 99.9 | Kilometres or miles |
| CALORIES | 0.0 to 9999 | 0.0 to 999 | Cumulative |
| PULSE | 0 to 999 | **40 to 220 BPM** | Hand pulse or receiver; a chest belt must be worn to use the receiver |
| LAPS | 0 to 99 | 0 to 99 | Total working laps |

In RUN Mode, with **no pulse signal for 8 seconds** the displayed pulse becomes "0".

In RUN Mode, pressing STOP saves the time value; re-entering RUN Mode continues counting from it.

The service manual's own words for these figures are "DISPLAY range" (what the window can show) and
"WORK range" (what the machine will actually do). Quote the work range as the machine's limit.

**The incline step here is 1, and the RUN MODE key table on the next page agrees** - the UP key "each
increase is 1, the maximum incline position is 15". The 2023 books for the same two models step the
incline by 0.5.
