The Stop/Reset key has four jobs:

| What you press | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Stop once, during a program** | Pauses the program **for 5 minutes**. Press **Start** to resume. |
| **Stop twice, during a workout** | Ends the program; the console returns to the **start-up screen**. |
| **Stop held down for 3 seconds** | The console performs a **complete Reset**. |
| **Stop during data entry** | **Previous Screen** - goes back one step in the programming, one step per press. |

**The pause length is a setting on most of these machines, not a fixed figure.** Pause mode appears
in the maintenance menu of every CE listed here, and in the XE100, XE200 and XE300 maintenance menu,
and can be turned off so the console pauses indefinitely instead. See
`spirit-ce800-console-maintenance-menu-function-and-service`,
`spirit-ce850-console-maintenance-menu-cab-or-csafe` and
`spirit-xe-console-maintenance-menu-with-a-motor-test-and-security`.

**The XE350 and XE550 are the exception: their manual documents no maintenance or engineering menu
at all**, so for those two machines the five minutes is all the document offers.

**The residential XE350 and XE550 (2005) and XE100, XE200 and XE300 (2007) manuals print this
paragraph word for word**, writing "Stop/Reset key" where the CE manuals write "Stop button". Their
later residential relatives changed the behaviour: from the XE395-2010 on, a second press ends the
workout on a **Workout Summary** rather than the start-up screen and a **third press** also resets.
See `spirit-xe-console-stop-key-summary-then-reset`.

**The XE150 is not covered.** Its own console section in the same 2005 manual describes only a
**Reset** key that returns to the start-up screen, and never mentions a pause; see
`xe150-2005-console-mode-key-and-the-icon-row`.

**These manuals give you no way to resume by pedalling.** Only the Start key is named. The
CE800-2013, CE850-2016 and both CE900 manuals do let you resume by pedalling, and add what happens
when the generator stops - see `spirit-ce-console-stop-button-resume-by-pedalling`.

**A printing note.** The 2021 CE800 and 2020 CE850 print "if you need to get a drink"; the 2023
CE800 and 2022 CE850 print "if you need to get a Water" in the same sentence. Nothing about the
behaviour changed.
