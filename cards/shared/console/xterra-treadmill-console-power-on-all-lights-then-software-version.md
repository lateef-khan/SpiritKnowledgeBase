Plug the unit into an appropriate wall outlet, then turn on the power switch located at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood. The Safety Key must be installed: the treadmill will not power on without it.

When the power is turned on, **all the lights on the display light up for a short time** and the console **shows the software version**. The treadmill then enters idle mode, the starting point for operation.

The two Hiker books print the same sentence about the Hiker Treadmill; the TR75H adds on its Screen Display page that after this full display the console automatically enters Standby Mode ([TR75H screen status card](../../tr75h-2025/console/screen-status-power-on-no-key-standby-run-hike.md)).
