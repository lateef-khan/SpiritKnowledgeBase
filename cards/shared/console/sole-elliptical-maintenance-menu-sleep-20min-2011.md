Hold **Start, Stop and Enter together for about 5 seconds**; the message window shows Engineering Mode. Press Enter to enter, Level up/down to navigate. Unlike the later menus this one has **no Display Test and no Pause Mode**: A. Key Test; B. Functions (ODO Reset, Units English/Metric, **Sleep Mode: turn on to power down after 20 minutes**, Motor Test running the tensioning gear motor, Manual stepping the gear motor, Key Tone, and **Calibration** - press Enter and the motor resets itself so level 1 is the lowest resistance); C. Security (child lock: hold Start and Enter 3 seconds to unlock); D. Exit. E. Factory Settings is manufacturing-only.
