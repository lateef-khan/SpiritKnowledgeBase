**In data set-up mode the console keys double as a number pad.** When you are setting new data such
as age, weight and time, you can use these keys to enter the numbers quickly instead of holding the
Up and Down arrows.

**Where the numbers are printed differs.** The CE800-2013 and CE850-2016 manuals say the **program
keys** also act as a number key pad and that **under each program key is a number**. Both CE900
manuals head the paragraph **NUMBER KEY PAD** and simply say "you can use these buttons to enter
the numbers quickly", without saying which keys carry the digits.

**Five residential Spirit XE ellipticals have the same number pad**: the XE100, XE200 and XE300
(2007) and the XE350 and XE550 (2005). Their manuals print the CE800-2013 and CE850-2016 wording
word for word - the **program keys** double as the pad and **under each program key is a number**.
The XE350 and XE550 console drawing labels it **"Program buttons / numeric key pad"**.

**The CE800-2021, CE800-2023, CE850-2020, CE850-2022 and CES880 consoles have no number pad.** Their
manuals describe entering every value with the Up and Down keys only. Do not tell an owner of one of
those to type a number.

**No residential XE built after 2007 has one either.** The XE150 of the same 2005 manual, the
XE395-2010, and every 2021 and 2023 XE set values with the Level Up and Down keys alone. The
XE395ENT is a touchscreen and has an on-screen keyboard instead.
