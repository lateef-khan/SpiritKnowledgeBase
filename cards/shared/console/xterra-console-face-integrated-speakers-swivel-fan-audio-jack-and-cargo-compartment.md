The console page of the SB500, FS5.8e and FS5.9e labels six things: **Integrated speakers for MP3 player**, **Swivel fan to keep you cool**, **Large LCD with scrolling feedback and scrolling message center**, **Audio input jack for customized listening**, **Convenient cargo compartment for phone or MP3 player**, and **Easy-touch control buttons**. The SB500 picture shows windows for RPM, CALORIES, TIME, DISTANCE and PULSE over a dot-matrix with a LEVEL number, a heart-rate profile from 50 to 90 %, and a SPEED message line; keys FAN, STOP, PROGRAM up/down, START, ENTER at the bottom and LEVEL up/down above the compartment.

**Audio system (all four books):** "There is an Audio Input Jack on the front of the console, and built-in speakers. You may plug any low-level audio source signal into this port. Audio sources include: MP3 player, iPod, portable radio, CD player or even a TV or computer."

The SB4.5r page carries no callouts - its picture shows a fan grille, PULSE / TIME / DISTANCE / CALORIES windows over the dot-matrix, a row of MANUAL, UP, DOWN, USER 1, USER 2, HR 1 and HR 2 program keys and LEVEL keys beneath - but its text prints the same audio paragraph. The SB4.5r, FS5.8e and FS5.9e books are scans read from the render; the SB500 2014 printing is a scan of the same text as the 2020 export.
