The console ships with **Sleep mode**, which has **three** settings on this machine:

| Setting | What the manual says it does |
|---|---|
| **ON** | The unit goes to an energy-saving state after **15 minutes of inactivity**. |
| **OFF** | The console stays lit while the power is on. |
| **RETAIL** | The console runs the **demo video** after **3 minutes of inactivity**. |

**To switch it:** on TFT displays, click **"Sleep Mode" under Settings** to switch among the three.

A console that starts playing a video on its own in a home has been left in **RETAIL**, which is a showroom setting. Change it in Settings.

**The upright and recumbent bikes of the same year have only two settings** — ON and OFF, with no Retail — and they are switched by holding Stop, Enter and Display for five seconds instead of through a Settings menu. Do not carry that key press onto this touchscreen.
