None of the four books describes a console, display, keys, Bluetooth or app: their contents run product registration, precautions/safeguards, hardware/pack list, assembly (SW155/SW180 add use instructions and dumbbell-handle troubleshooting), warranty and parts, and no part in any list is electronic. The SW155/SW180 machines are adjustable dumbbells whose troubleshooting covers the handle locking mechanism, not a display.

These machines are worked with handles, pins and plates. Any resistance or weight question belongs with assembly or specs, not with this section.
