The Stop key has four jobs:

| What you press | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Stop once, during a program** | Pauses the program **for 5 minutes**. Press **Start** to resume. |
| **Stop twice, during a workout** | Ends the program; the console shows your **Workout Summary**. |
| **Stop held for 3 seconds, or a third press during the program** | The console performs a **complete Reset**. |
| **Stop during data entry** | **Previous screen or segment** - goes back to change programming data. |

**All thirteen manuals print this paragraph word for word**, the 2018 and 2019 printings included.

**The five minutes is a setting, not a fixed figure.** Every one of these machines has a **Pause
Mode** item in engineering mode - "turn on allow 5 minutes of pause, turn off to have the console
pause indefinitely".

**What the Workout Summary lists is not the same on all of them.** See
`spirit-xe-console-what-the-workout-summary-lists` before quoting the fields.

**The older residential XEs end differently.** On the 2005 XE350 and XE550 and the 2007 XE100, XE200
and XE300, a second press returns to the **start-up screen** - there is no Workout Summary and no
third-press reset. Those are covered by
`spirit-ce-console-stop-button-press-once-twice-hold`.

**The XE395ENT prints only the first two rows.** Its manual stops after the Workout Summary and
never mentions the three-second reset or the previous-screen function; see
`xe395ent-2021-console-power-up-and-quick-start`.

**Twelve Spirit residential bike printings print all four rows word for word**, so this card is no
longer elliptical-only: the XBR25, XBR55, XBR95 and XBU55 of every LED-console printing. The five
minutes, the Start key to resume, the Workout Summary on the second press, the three-second or
third-press Reset and the previous-screen behaviour during data entry are all unchanged.

**The five minutes is a setting on the bikes too.** Every one of these twelve has a **Pause Mode**
item in engineering mode, worded exactly as on the ellipticals.

**The two ENT bikes print only the first two rows.** The XBR55ENT and XBU55ENT (p. 16) stop after the
Workout Summary and never mention the three-second reset or the previous-screen function - the same
cut the XE395ENT makes. Do not tell an ENT owner to hold Stop for three seconds on the strength of
this card.

**What the Workout Summary lists is not the same on all fourteen bikes**, and the 2023 books drop a
field: `spirit-xe-console-what-the-workout-summary-lists`.

**The Spirit XS895 incline stepper prints this paragraph word for word** - XS895-2018 and
XS895-2021, p. 17, Basic Information - so this card is no longer elliptical-and-bike only. Once
pauses for five minutes, twice ends on a Workout Summary, a third press or a three-second hold
resets, and during data entry the key steps back a screen.

**What its summary lists is not what the ellipticals list**; see
`spirit-xe-console-what-the-workout-summary-lists`.
