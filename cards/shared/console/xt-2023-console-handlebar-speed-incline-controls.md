These machines let you **make speed and incline changes using the speed and incline controls located
on the handlebar**.

**You can turn those controls off** if you frequently hold on to the rails. **Press the Handrail
Control Button, which is located on the console next to the child lock button.** **When the
indicator light is lit, the handlebar speed and incline controls are disabled.** This lets you use
the full length of the handrails without fear of activating the speed or incline controls.

**The XT285 has the same disable feature but its manual puts the button somewhere else, and
contradicts itself about where** - see `xt285-2023-console-handrail-speed-incline-controls`. The
XT185 manual describes no disable control at all.
