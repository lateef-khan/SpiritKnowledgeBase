**This is for a link that worked and then stopped.** If it never linked at all, use `sole-plus-not-linking-touchscreen` instead.

SOLE+ can get unlinked from the machine on its own. New workouts then stop reaching the app. The machine and the app both need internet, but they do **not** need to be on the same network.

1. Log in to the **profile that is no longer syncing**. Not the Guest profile.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Check the treadmill is on the customer's Wi-Fi network.
4. Check for machine software updates. You may need to unplug the machine and plug it back in for it to look for updates.
5. Check the Google Play Store or Apple App Store for pending SOLE+ updates.
6. Leave Settings. Tap the customer's profile image at the top of the screen.
7. Look under the profile name:
   - **A Link button is there** — tap it and scan the QR code.
   - **It says already linked** — tap **Delete** at the top of the page to remove the profile, then link it again from the login screen.

If SOLE+ still will not connect with the QR code, **replace the console assembly**.
