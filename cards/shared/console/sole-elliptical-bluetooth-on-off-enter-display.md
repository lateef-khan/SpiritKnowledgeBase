Press **ENTER and DISPLAY at the same time and hold for 5 seconds**. The Message Window displays "BLUETOOTH". Press the **Level up/down keys** to choose ON or OFF, then press **STOP or ENTER** to exit.

This is the on/off switch for the Bluetooth transceiver the Sole Fitness App uses (see_also). It is printed outside the A-F maintenance menu in both books.
