**This is the Spirit+ app, reached a different way from the LED-console bikes' Spirit+ section.** The
steps below are the ones the two ENT bike manuals print; do not substitute
`xt-2023-console-spirit-plus-app-pairing`.

The machine **comes equipped with a Bluetooth transceiver** that lets it interact with selected
phones or tablets via the **Spirit+ App**. **Scan the QR code on the screen, or search "Spirit+" in
the App Store or Google Play, to download it.**

1. **Download the App.**
2. **Open the App** on your phone or tablet and **make sure Bluetooth is enabled on the device**.
3. **In the App, click the icon in the top right corner** to search for your Spirit equipment.
4. **After the equipment is detected, click the row the equipment is on** to connect Spirit+, then
   **click the "Display" icon to start the sync process.**
5. **When your workout is complete, the data is automatically saved and uploaded to a cloud site.**

**Three things differ from the 2023 LED bikes' Spirit+ section**, and each one is a wrong answer if
carried across:

- the search icon is in the **top right** corner, not on a Bluetooth icon in the app;
- you **tap the machine's row** and then **Display**, rather than choosing it from a Bluetooth scan
  list;
- **there is no "END & SAVE WORKOUT" step.** The upload happens by itself.

**Minimum operating system: iOs 13 or Android 8.0.** Spelled "iOs 13" in both manuals. **The 2023
XBR55, XBR95 and XBU55 print 13.1**, one decimal place apart:
`xt-2023-console-spirit-plus-minimum-os`.

**The manuals name no cloud sites.** They say only "one of many fitness cloud sites"; the 2023 bikes
name Apple Health, Google Fit, MapMyFitness and Fitbit and these two do not.

**This is not the only way workout data leaves these machines.** A profile on the console can be
linked to a Spirit APP account by scanning a QR code on screen, which carries the whole workout
history rather than one session:
`xe395ent-2021-console-profiles-and-the-spirit-app-link`. **Bluetooth chest straps, earphones and
speakers pair from the console's own Settings menu**, not through the app:
`xe395ent-2021-console-settings-menu-and-its-eight-items`.
