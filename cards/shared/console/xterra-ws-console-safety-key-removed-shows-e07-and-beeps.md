The book calls this the **Safety Lock Function**:

1. If the safety key is pulled off, the window displays **E07** and the buzzer beeps continuously.
2. When the treadmill is running, if the safety key is removed, E07 is displayed, the treadmill stops in an emergency and cannot be started.

The "How to use" page says the same: if the key is pulled out during exercise the belt stops immediately, the console refuses any operation and the window shows E07. Putting the key back on its position on the console is the cure the troubleshooting table gives for E07 ("Safety lock is not in place - put the safety key in correct position"); the Errors section holds that table and the other E0x codes.
