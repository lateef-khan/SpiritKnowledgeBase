**Tap Entertainment on the Home Screen.** The entertainment menu offers two things: **Screen
Mirroring** and **TV mode**.

**TV mode needs a set-top box.** *"When properly connected to the Set-Top Box (STP) via the HDMI port
and TV mode selected, you can enjoy TV programs after selecting TV mode from the Entertainment
screen."* **There is no tuner in this console and no channel scan anywhere in either manual** - the
box supplies the channels.

Once TV mode has begun:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **▲ / ▼** on the right of the touchscreen | Switch channels |
| **+ / -** volume on the right of the touchscreen | Sound level |
| The **⛶** button | Full screen; tap it again to go back to normal size |
| On-screen or physical **Level** controls | Change the workout while in TV mode |
| **Stop** once | Pause |
| **Stop** twice | End the workout and show the workout summary |
| **Stop** a third time | Return to the Home Screen |

Both manuals print this section word for word.

**This is not the CR900ENT or CU900ENT arrangement.** Those consoles have a **TV** tile on the Home
screen, a built-in tuner that is scanned from the Settings suite, and a **Mute** button and a **Max
Screen** button rather than a ⛶ icon: `ct900ent-watching-tv-while-exercising` and
`ct900ent-tv-tuner-setup`. **Do not offer a channel scan to a CU800ENT or CR800ENT owner.**

**No volume figure, no mute button and no headphone socket is described** for TV mode on these two
machines. For mirroring instead of TV, see `ct800ent-2022-console-screen-mirroring-ios` and
`ct800ent-2022-console-screen-mirroring-android`.
