The machine's Bluetooth device can also be connected to a **Bluetooth wireless heart rate chest strap transmitter**.

**The strap can still connect even though the app is already connected to the machine.** Both connections live at once; nothing has to be switched between them.

**This reverses the behaviour of the 2019 machines.** On those, one Bluetooth device is shared between the app and the strap, and the heart rate key on the keypad has to be pressed to move Bluetooth from one to the other. That procedure does not apply here, and telling a customer with a 2023 machine to press a heart rate key to free up Bluetooth sends them looking for a step their manual does not have.
