**Connect the machine to WiFi first** - `spirit-ent-console-wifi-setup`. The note describes this as
the thing to do once you are connected.

1. **Tap the Home button 10 times.**
2. **Select the Maintenance tab.**
3. **Select the System Apps & Updates tab** - the last tab on the very bottom.
4. **If the box on the right side of the screen is lit up and says "Update", tap it and the update
   will start.** Do this with anything that indicates it needs to be updated.

**There is nothing to check or type.** The console decides what is out of date and lights the box;
the note gives no version numbers, no download size, no time estimate and no instruction about
leaving the machine powered while it updates.

**This is the over-the-network route.** The same Maintenance menu also carries an **Update App(USB)**
button and an **Update OS** button, which the note does not open. The USB route is documented for
the bike in the same console family at `cu900ent-usb-software-update`; that is a different machine's
procedure and its screens are not claimed here.
