**The Bluetooth function will only connect when the console is in Stop Mode.** That is the whole
reason a pairing attempt fails on these bikes.

1. Put the console in **Stop Mode**.
2. Press the **Bluetooth button** on the console. **The Bluetooth symbol flashes on the display.**
3. Connect to the console through the Bluetooth function **on your device**.
4. After a positive connection, **the workout summary uploads to the app**.

**It uploads a summary, it does not stream a ride.** What crosses the link is the three-figure
workout summary - total time, total distance, total watts. Neither manual describes live data, a
virtual-ride app, a power profile or a cadence broadcast, and neither names Zwift, Kinomap or any
other app.

**Neither manual names the app.** It is called only "the APP". There is no app name, no download
link, no store listing and no account setup anywhere in either book.

**Nothing pairs mid-workout.** During a running mode the button does nothing, so a customer trying to
connect while pedalling will get nowhere.

**There is no PIN**, and no way described to unpair or forget a device from the console side.
