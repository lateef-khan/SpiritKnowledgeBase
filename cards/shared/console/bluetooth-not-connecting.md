**What is supported**: Bluetooth in the SOLE+ app works on machines built in August 2021 or later. It lets the app control the workout, and lets an Apple Watch broadcast heart rate. Machines built since 2018 also work as a Bluetooth speaker through **BT-Speaker** in the phone's system settings.

**Non-touchscreen models**

1. Check the customer's other Bluetooth devices. Something else may already be connected.
2. If another device is connected, disconnect it and connect the phone or tablet.
3. If nothing else is connected and the machine is not a treadmill, replace the console assembly.
4. If nothing else is connected and it is a treadmill, turn Bluetooth on from the console:
   - Remove the safety key.
   - Press and hold **Start** and **Incline Up**.
   - Reinsert the safety key while holding the buttons.
   - Hold until the screen shows BLUETOOTH.
   - If it shows OFF, press **Incline Up** to change it to ON.
   - Press **Enter** to confirm and exit.
5. Try connecting again. If it still fails, replace the console assembly.

**Touchscreen models**

1. Open the Bluetooth menu on the machine and check nothing else is connected.
2. If nothing is connected, make sure the software is fully up to date.
   - Up to date: replace the console assembly.
   - Not up to date: update it and try again. If it still fails, replace the console assembly.
3. If other devices are connected, disconnect them and try the customer's phone again. If the machine pairs with other devices but not with theirs, the problem is on their phone or tablet.
