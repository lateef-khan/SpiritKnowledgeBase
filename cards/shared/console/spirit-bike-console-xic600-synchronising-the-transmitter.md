Do this **after installing the batteries and before attaching the console and transmitter to the
bike**, so the two can "talk" to each other.

1. Press and hold **the two keys on the front of the console** for about 3 seconds until the display
   shows **`ID - -`**.
2. Now press and hold **the blue button on the transmitter** for 3 seconds and release.
3. The console display should show **`ID 0`**.

**You have ten seconds.** The blue button must be pressed **within 10 seconds** of the console
showing `ID - -`. Miss the window and the console shows **`ID ER`**. If it does, restart the
procedure from step 1.

**If you keep getting the error**, the manual's own next step is to remove and re-install the
batteries in **both** the console and the transmitter, then try again.

**Run this again after any battery change**, in either part - see
`spirit-bike-console-xic600-two-aaa-batteries-each`.

**Two bikes in the same room can collide.** Where the cadence figure jumps high or low, the
troubleshooting page's first suggestion is that another bike is set to the same console code and the
two are cross-talking; re-synchronising here is the fix.
