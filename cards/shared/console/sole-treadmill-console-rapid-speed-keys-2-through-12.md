The console prints rapid **speed** keys **2 through 12** to adjust the speed (2007 books: 'rapid speed keys 2 through 12 to set to a specific speed directly'; TT8 books: 'rapid speed adjust keys'), alongside the Fast/Slow keys on the console or handgrip. Other books in the wave print different sets: 2-3-4-5-6-9-12, 2-4-6-8-12, 2-4-6-8-10-12, 1-2-4-6-8-10 or 2-through-10.
