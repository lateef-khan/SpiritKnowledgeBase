**Neither manual describes an app.** Twelve of the fourteen 2024 New Black Units books carry a
**USING THE SPIRIT+ APP** page and an **ABOUT YOUR MACHINE** page before it
(`xt-2023-console-spirit-plus-app-pairing`). **The CSC900-2024 and CRW800-2024 carry neither.** The
string "Spirit+" appears **zero** times in each document; a loose search for the word "app" returns
only "appreciate", "approved" and the French "appareil"; and a spacing-tolerant search for a
letter-spaced product name returns nothing.

**Both books were rendered at 300 dpi page by page and the render appended to the extraction**, so
the absence is not a page the text layer missed: the CSC900 carries 13 OCR supplements and the
CRW800 19, and neither supplement mentions an app.

**Neither manual states a minimum phone or tablet operating system**, because neither has the closing
note that carries it (`xt-2023-console-spirit-plus-minimum-os`).

**The CSC900 does have a Bluetooth radio, and it is not for an app.** Its compliance list names
**Bluetooth (with FTMS)** and its console reads a **Bluetooth heart rate chest strap** automatically
(`csc900-2024-console-heart-rate-bluetooth-and-contact`). **Nothing in the manual says workout data
leaves the machine.**

**The CRW800 has no Bluetooth of any kind in its manual.** The word appears nowhere in the document.
Its console drawing labels a **Built-in Heart Rate Receiver** with no protocol named
(`crw800-2024-console-screen-overview`), and it is battery powered.

**Absence is the answer here.** Do not offer either owner the pairing steps from another machine in
the same range.
