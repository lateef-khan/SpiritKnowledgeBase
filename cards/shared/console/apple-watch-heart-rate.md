**Live heart rate only.** The Apple Watch sends the current heart rate to the machine through the SOLE+ app. That is the only thing it does. It lets the customer use the machine's built-in heart rate programs, if it has any, and shows heart rate during any workout.

**Only on machines built in August 2021 or later.**

**All four must be true first:**

- The SOLE+ Watch App is installed on the Apple Watch.
- SOLE+ is installed on an **iPhone**. It will not work with an Android phone.
- The Apple Watch is paired with that iPhone.
- The machine is turned on.

**Then connect the iPhone to the machine over Bluetooth:**

1. Sign in to the SOLE+ app on the iPhone.
2. Tap the arrow next to **Connect** on the main screen.
3. Tap **Add Device** in the bottom left.
4. Tap **Pair via Bluetooth**.
5. Tap **Pair** for the machine once it is discovered.

**Related.** The same source also explains why Apple Health records do not match SOLE+. See `sole-apple-health-does-not-match`.
