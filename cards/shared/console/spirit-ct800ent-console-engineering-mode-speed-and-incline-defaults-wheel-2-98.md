**The Engineering Mode entry of the setting page is where speed and incline are set and calibration
is started.** Its three printed lines:

1. **The default Speed is Minimum 0.5 (mi/hr) - Maximum 12.0 (mi/hr).**
2. **The default Incline is 15 level, Wheel size is 2.98.**
3. **Click on the calibration to start setting.**

The screen itself carries these fields: **Max. Incline**, **Wheel Size**, **Max. Speed**,
**Min. Speed**, and an **Incline AD Value** group of **Max. AD**, **Current AD** and **Min. AD**. The
AD values are the incline potentiometer readings; the manual prints no target figures for them.

**The manual does not say what the machine does when calibration runs**, whether the belt moves, or
what to do when it fails.

**The CT850ENT-2022 owner's manual prints different figures for the same machine.** Its calibration
routine, written in the LED console's words behind the same ten presses, sets a minimum of **0.3**
and a maximum of **15.0** (`ct850ent-2022-console-calibration-touchscreen`). This service manual
says 0.5 and 12.0. The wheel size (2.98) and the incline (15) agree. Nothing in either document
resolves the speeds; read the console's own screen before overwriting a value.

**The 2024 ENT treadmills print the same three lines** - 0.5 to 12.0 mi/hr and 15 level - under a
Service > Calibration entry reached by pressing Hello Guest ten times, with no wheel size at all
(`ct800ent-2024-console-calibration-defaults-0-5-to-12-0-mph`).

The elliptical and bike in this console family carry Units, Beep, Sleep and Pause on their
Engineering Mode screens instead (`ce800ent-engineering-mode-settings`,
`cu900ent-engineering-mode-settings`); on these treadmills those settings sit under Machine
Information (`spirit-ct800ent-console-machine-information-gs-beep-sleep-safety-zeroing`).
