"RECOVERY: Press to enter into heart rate Recovery function. **Only works when the computer is receiving a heart rate value.** Recovery is an indication of Fitness Level. It is a one minute measurement taken immediately after an exercise session and provides a score from 1 to 6. A score of 1 is an indication of a high level of fitness and a score of 6 indicates poor condition."

Keep the heart-rate source going - hands on the grips, or the strap on the machines that name one - or the key does nothing. The books print no P or ERR message for this console; the SB150-family consoles do ([card](xterra-console-pulse-recovery-grade-with-p-and-err.md)).
