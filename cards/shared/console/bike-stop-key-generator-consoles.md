The Stop button has several functions.

- **Press once during a program** — pauses the program.
- **Press twice during a workout** — the program ends and a Workout Summary is displayed.
- **Hold for 3 seconds** — the console performs a complete Reset.
- **During data entry for a program** — Stop performs a Previous Screen function, going back one step each time it is pressed.

**This manual gives no length for the pause and no key to resume with.** Every other manual in this range says the single press pauses for **5 minutes** and that Start resumes it. This one prints neither figure. Do not tell a customer with this machine that they have five minutes of pause on the strength of another year's manual.

**What this manual does put a five minute limit on is the generator, not the Stop key.** If you stop pedalling, the workout data is held for up to five minutes and then lost. That is a separate paragraph and a separate mechanism.
