**The Pulse (Heart Rate) window will display your current heart rate in beats per minute during the
workout.**

**Pulse value displays anytime the upper display is receiving a Pulse signal.**

**You may not use the Pulse Grip feature while in Heart Rate Programs.**

The manuals also refer the reader to the Important Safety Instructions concerning Pulse Grip
operation (page 6, and page 8 in the XT285 manual).

**One sentence in this section is printed ambiguously in all five manuals** and is reproduced here
rather than repaired: *"You must use both stainless steel sensors on the stationary grips for the
heart rate transmitter chest strap to display your pulse."* It names two different sensors - the
grip sensors and the chest strap - in one requirement, and the manuals never say which one it means.
Treat "hold both grips" as the safe reading and check with the technical department before quoting
it.

**The 2015 manuals resolve the ambiguity above.** The XT285-2015, XT385-2015, XT485-2015 and
XT685-2015 manuals print the same sentence with **"or"**: *"You must use both stainless steel
sensors on the stationary grips **or** the heart rate transmitter chest strap to display your
pulse."* The **XT185-2015** manual names only the grips - *"You must use the stainless steel sensors
on the stationary grips to display your pulse"* - and that manual never mentions a chest strap.

All five 2015 manuals add one more sentence about how the reading starts: **grasping the hand pulse
sensors on the stationary handle bars (or, on the four machines that support it, wearing the heart
rate transmitter) starts the Heart Icon blinking, which may take a few seconds**, and the Pulse
window then shows the rate in beats per minute.
