1. **After the console powers up, press the start key.** This is quick start mode.
2. **The time counts up from zero** and all workout data starts to accrue.
3. **The workload is adjusted by hand with the up or down key.** The dot matrix opens at the **lowest
   resistance**; as you raise the workload more rows light and the machine gets harder to pedal.

**The graphic display has 20 columns of lights, each column representing 1 minute** in the quick
start program - *time per column can be modified in other programs*. **At the end of the 20th column
(20 minutes of work) the display wraps around and restarts at the first column.**

**There are 20 levels of resistance displayed in 8 rows of lights.** Twenty levels drawn in eight
rows means **a row is not a level**; do not read a level number off the height of a column.

**Twenty columns, not twenty-four.** Every Spirit CS800, CRS800S, CE and CSC console runs **24
columns of one minute** and wraps at the 24th
(`spirit-ce-console-quick-start-time-counts-up`). **Do not quote 24 minutes at one of these
machines.**

**These manuals band nothing.** They print no "levels 1-5 are very easy" progression, unlike the
residential and commercial books
(`spirit-xe-console-quick-start-with-twenty-resistance-levels`), and they give no watt figure for any
level.

**The MS300-2021 says "The unit will get harder to pedal"**; the 2025 books name the machine, "The
7.0S" and "The 7.5S". Nothing else in the section differs across the five, the two MED books included.
