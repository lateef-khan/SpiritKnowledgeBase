The Calorie display shows the **cumulative calories burned** at any given time during the workout. Every one of these books adds the same note: "This is only a rough guide used for comparison of different exercise sessions, which cannot be used for medical purposes."

The TR75 and TR85 add a **CALORIE NOTE**: calorie readings on every piece of exercise equipment, in a gym or at home, are only an estimate and tend to vary widely; they are meant only as a guide to monitor progress from workout to workout. Entering your weight during program setup helps the reading (those setup steps belong to the Programs section). The TR65 prints the same note under both its Calories and its Pulse window.
