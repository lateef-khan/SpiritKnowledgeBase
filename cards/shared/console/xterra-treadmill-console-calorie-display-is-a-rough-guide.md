The Calorie display shows the **cumulative calories burned** at any given time during the workout. Every one of these books adds the same note: "This is only a rough guide used for comparison of different exercise sessions, which cannot be used for medical purposes."

The TR75 and TR85 add a **CALORIE NOTE**: calorie readings on every piece of exercise equipment, in a gym or at home, are only an estimate and tend to vary widely; they are meant only as a guide to monitor progress from workout to workout. Entering your weight during program setup helps the reading (those setup steps belong to the Programs section). The TR65 prints the same note under both its Calories and its Pulse window.

**The bikes and ellipticals say it three ways.** The SB150, FS150, SB250, EU150 and EU100 print the treadmill sentence under Calories: "This is only a rough guide used for comparison of different exercise sessions, which cannot be used for medical purposes." The SB120, UB120 and AIR350 print "The calorie readout is an estimate for an average user. It should be used only as comparison between workouts on this unit." The SB500, SB4.5r, FS5.8e and FS5.9e print the TR75/TR85 Calorie Note - estimates only, a guide from workout to workout, accurate only in a clinical setting (the SB4.5r: "connected to a host of machines") - and add that entering your weight helps the count. The SB240's line is the bare "Your computer will estimate the cumulative calories burned", so it is not listed.

**Three rowers say it too, each its own way.** The ERG160 prints the SB120 sentence - "an estimate for an average user ... only as comparison between workouts on this unit" ([ERG160 card](../../erg160-2020/console/single-mode-key-with-scan-every-6-seconds-and-six-readouts.md)); the ERG500 prints "a rough guide for comparison between different exercise sessions and should not be used as a medical standard" ([ERG500 card](../../erg500-2018/console/mode-reset-up-down-keys-and-six-windows-with-distance-in-miles-to-99-9.md)); the ERG700 prints the XRW600 wording - "based on built in estimates of the average user and may not reflect accuracy. To have a more accurate basis of caloric burn - please contact your physician" ([ERG700 card](../../erg700-2022/console/window-ranges-with-watts-to-2000-as-a-decimal-and-level-1-to-16-in-dots.md)). The other eight rower books print a calorie range and no disclaimer, so they are not listed.
