**Eighteen columns, 8 high for the F80, or 20 columns, 10 high for the F85.** The dots show approximate effort only. When **Speed** is lit above or below the matrix the Speed profile shows; when **Incline** is lit the Incline profile shows. Press **Enter** to swap profiles.
