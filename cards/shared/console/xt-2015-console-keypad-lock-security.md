**On the 2015 XT machines the keypad lock is item 4, "Security", inside the engineering mode menu.
There is no dedicated Child Lock button on the console.** To reach it, enter engineering mode - see
`xt-2015-console-engineering-mode-menu`.

**Unlocking is only described in two of the five manuals.** The **XT185-2015 and XT285-2015**
manuals print: *"When the child lock is enabled, the console will not allow the keypad to operate
unless you press and hold the Start and Enter buttons for 3 seconds to unlock the console."*

The **XT385-2015, XT485-2015 and XT685-2015** manuals print only *"Security (Allows the keypad to be
locked to prevent unauthorized use)"*. **Those three manuals do not say how to unlock it.** Do not
assume the Start-and-Enter method carries over to them; the manuals do not state it.

**None of the five manuals says how to turn the lock on** beyond selecting Security in the menu.

**This is not the 2023 arrangement.** The 2023 XT consoles have a **dedicated Child Lock button**
that lights an LED, and it is unlocked by holding **Child Lock** itself for 3 seconds, not Start and
Enter. See `xt-2023-console-child-lock`.

**The XT685 2010 owner's manual prints the same item on its p. 36** - *"Security (Allows the keypad
to be locked to prevent unauthorized use)"* and nothing more - so this card covers that machine, and
like the 2015 XT385, XT485 and XT685 manuals it does not say how to unlock it. On that machine
Security is item **b** of four, not item 4 of five; the menu it sits in is
`xt685-2010-console-engineering-mode-menu`.
