In **CLK MODE (clock mode)**:

1. Press **SET for 3 seconds** to enter setting.
2. Press SET **shortly** to choose 12 or 24 hours.
3. Then press MODE and SET to adjust the time (hour / min / sec).
4. When complete, press **MODE for 3 seconds**.

The clock is the "watch feature" of the feature list (time of day, stopwatch). The MB500 book is a scan read from the render (the console pages are OCR); the MB550 book prints the same console chapter natively and is cited alongside.
