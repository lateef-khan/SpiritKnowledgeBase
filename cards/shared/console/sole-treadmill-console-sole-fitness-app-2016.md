The machine carries a **Bluetooth transceiver** for the free **Sole Fitness App** (Apple Store / Google Play): view workout data on three Display screens, flip to internet/social/email via on-screen icons, and have finished workouts saved to the built-in calendar and synced to **iHealth, MapMyFitness, Record or Fitbit**. To sync: enable Bluetooth on the device, tap the **top-left icon** to search for Sole equipment, tap **Connect**; the console **Bluetooth icon lights up**. Minimum OS: **iOS 7 or Android 4.4**. These books print no SYNC message, no Bluetooth music and no USB charging; those arrive in 2019.
