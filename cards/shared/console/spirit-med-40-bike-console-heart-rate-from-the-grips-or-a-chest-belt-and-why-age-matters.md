"The product has a built in heart rate monitoring system. **Simply grasping the hand pulse sensors, or
wearing a heart rate chest belt transmitter will start the heart rate detection.** The message window
will display your heart rate, or pulse, in beats per minute (**this may take a few seconds**)."

**"NOTE: You must enter your correct age during program setup for heart rate control program to be
accurate."** The age is entered inside each program's set-up prompts; this console has no Set Up key
and no patient-data menu.

**There is no separate pulse window.** Heart rate appears in the message window under its **Pulse**
legend, and there is no heart icon and no percent-of-maximum bar graph on this face - both of those
belong to the 7.0 series (`spirit-med-70-bike-console-heart-icon-and-percent-of-maximum-bar-graph`).

**The paragraph does not say both grips are needed**, unlike the commercial bike and elliptical books
(`spirit-ce-console-pulse-window-polar-coded-straps`), and it names no Polar compatibility. What
strap the console reads is on its own card: 5 kHz in the 2025 Spirit books
(`spirit-med-bike-console-chest-strap-5-khz-2500-hours-and-a-cr2032`), "Bluetooth or ANT+" in the
Dyaco PT books (`spirit-med-40-pt-bike-console-chest-strap-bluetooth-or-ant-plus-with-no-frequency`).

All six books - both 2025 owner's manuals, both service manuals and both PT editions - print the
paragraph word for word.
