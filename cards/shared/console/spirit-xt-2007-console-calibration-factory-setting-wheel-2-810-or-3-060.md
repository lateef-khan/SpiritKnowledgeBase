**This routine opens with a FACTORY SETTING prompt, has a Power On/Off step no later XT routine has,
and prints two wheel diameters under chassis names rather than model names.** The XT175 and XT275
in the same dealer manual set a maximum of 10.0 and different wheel sizes
(`xt175-2007-console-calibration-wheel-2-50-max-10-0`,
`xt275-2007-console-calibration-wheel-2-30-with-grade-return`).

1. **Remove the tether cord.**
2. **Press and hold the START and FAST buttons. Replace the tether cord while holding the buttons.**
   The display shows **"FACTORY SETTING"**. Press ENTER.
3. **Power On/Off.** Press UP or DOWN to change. Press ENTER.
4. **Units English / Metric.** Press UP or DOWN to change. Press ENTER.
5. Adjust the **wheel diameter** with UP or DOWN. **2.810 for "XT200 & XT600", 3.060 for "XT800".**
   Press ENTER.
6. Adjust the **minimum speed to 0.5** with UP and DOWN. Press ENTER.
7. Adjust the **maximum speed to 12** with UP and DOWN. Press ENTER.
8. Adjust the **maximum elevation to 15** with UP and DOWN.
9. **Press START to begin calibration. DO NOT STAND ON BELT DURING CALIBRATION.**

**Step 5 names XT200, XT600 and XT800, which are not model names in this manual.** They are the
Dyaco chassis codes printed on nothing else in the book, and the manual never says which of the
XT375, XT475 and XT675 is which. The later XT685 uses 3.01 and the later XT385 and XT485 use 2.43,
which is no help either. Confirm the chassis from the frame label before entering a wheel diameter.

**Step 3, Power On/Off, is not explained.** No other Spirit calibration routine in this repository
carries it, and the manual says nothing about what it does.

The Engineering mode of these three machines is a separate menu with its own entry gesture:
`spirit-xt-2007-console-engineering-mode-six-submenus`.
