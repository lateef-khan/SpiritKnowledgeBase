1. Press the red **Stop** key once while running to pause: the belt decelerates slowly to a stop, the **incline goes to zero percent**, and the Time, Distance and Calorie readings hold. After **5 minutes** the display resets to the start-up screen.
2. Press **Start** in Pause mode to resume; speed and incline return to their previous settings.
3. A **second** Stop press ends the program and shows a workout summary; a **third** press returns to the idle (start-up) screen.
4. Holding Stop for **more than 3 seconds** resets the console.

Verified word-for-word across the 2011, 2013, 2006, 2008 and 2009 books above. The F60-2016 and the TT9 touchscreen behave differently.

The A2 LCD books (2016-2025) print the same four steps; the TT8 books say grade for incline.
