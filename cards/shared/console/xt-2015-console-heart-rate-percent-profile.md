**These colours are Amber, Green and Red. The 2023 XT consoles use a five band White / Blue / Green
/ Yellow / Red scale at different percentages - do not read one onto the other.**

The console LCD screen displays your **current heart rate anytime a pulse is detected**. The **Bar
Graph, located to the right of the LCD screen**, shows your **current heart rate % in relation to
your projected maximum heart rate**, which is determined by the **age you entered during the
programming phase**.

| Percentage of maximum | Colours lit |
|---|---|
| **50-60%** | **Amber** |
| **65-80%** | **Amber and Green** |
| **85-90% or more** | **Amber, Green and Red** |

**The scale as printed has gaps.** It says nothing about below 50%, nothing between 60% and 65%, and
nothing between 80% and 85%. All three manuals print it exactly this way; the missing bands are not
an artefact of the conversion, and no manual says what the graph shows in them.

The manuals describe the age as the one entered "during the programming phase of any of the 10
programs". **The XT485-2015 and XT685-2015 manuals elsewhere describe twelve programs** - nine
preset, two user and one manual - so that count does not agree with itself in those two manuals.

**The XT185-2015 and XT285-2015 have no heart rate bar graph section.** Their manuals mention a
"heart rate Bar Graph Display" only in the program data-entry steps and never print a colour scale.

**The XT685 2010 owner's manual prints the same paragraph and the same three colour bands on its
p. 17**, with the same gaps below 50%, between 60% and 65% and between 80% and 85%, so this card
covers that machine. It too describes the age as the one entered "during the programming phase of
any of the 10 programs", and it too describes twelve programs elsewhere - nine preset, two user and
one manual - so that count does not agree with itself in the 2010 XT685 book either.
