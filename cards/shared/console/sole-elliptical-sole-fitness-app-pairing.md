The machine carries a **Bluetooth transceiver** for the free **Sole Fitness App** (Apple Store / Google Play; minimum **iOS 7 or Android 4.4**). The app shows current workout data on three display screens, flips to internet/social/email views, saves finished workouts to a built-in calendar, and syncs to **iHealth, MapMyFitness, Record or Fitbit**.

Download the app, enable Bluetooth, tap the **top-left icon** to search for Sole equipment, tap **Connect** once the machine is detected. The **Bluetooth icon on the console lights up** when app and machine are synced.
