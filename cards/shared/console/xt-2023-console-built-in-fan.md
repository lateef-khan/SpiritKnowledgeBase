**The console includes a built-in fan to help keep you cool. To turn the fan on, press the key on
the left side of the console.**

The XT185 and XT285 console diagrams also label an **Adjustable Fan**, but **those two manuals give
no instruction for switching it on**, so this card does not claim the same key for them.
