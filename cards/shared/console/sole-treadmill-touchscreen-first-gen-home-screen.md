Captions read off the page render. The homescreen gives an activity overview (total distance) with shortcuts. In Guest mode, age and weight entered here feed the workout summary. Top icons, left to right: **Log out, Pair HR device, Adjust volume, Fan on/off, Activity overview, Current user, WiFi settings**. The red button is **Start Workout**. Program tiles read **Manual, Hill, Fatburn, See More Programs**. Bottom navigation: **Homescreen, Profile programs, Workout programs, Internet & Apps, Screen casting, Settings**. The ST90 shows different tiles (Free Mode); see its card.
