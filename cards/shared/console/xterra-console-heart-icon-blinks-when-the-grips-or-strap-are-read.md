"The machine has a built in Heart Rate Monitoring System. Simply grasping the hand pulse sensors on the stationary handlebars **or by wearing the chest strap** will start the heart icon blinking (this may take a few seconds). The Pulse Display Window will display your heart rate, or pulse, in beats per minute." - SB500, FS5.8e and FS5.9e.

**The SB4.5r names no strap.** Its sentence stops at the hand pulse sensors, and its heart-rate programs say "you must grasp the contact heart rate grips with both hands"; the other three say "or use the telemetric heart rate strap". None of the four books says a strap is in the box. How to wear one and what interferes with it are on the strap cards ([2500-hour strap](xterra-treadmill-console-wearing-the-chest-strap-and-its-2500-hour-battery.md) for the SB500, [CR2032 strap](xterra-treadmill-console-chest-strap-not-included-with-a-cr2032-battery.md) for the FS5.9e, [erratic](xterra-treadmill-console-erratic-heart-rate-and-the-seven-interference-sources.md)). The FS5.8e scan ends at printed page 22, before the General Maintenance and warranty pages its contents list promises, so what its last pages say is unknown. The SB4.5r, FS5.8e and FS5.9e books are scans read from the render; the SB500 2014 printing is a scan of the same text as the 2020 export.
