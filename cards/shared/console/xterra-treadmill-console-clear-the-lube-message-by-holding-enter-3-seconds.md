When "Lube" appears on the LCD, check the lubrication under the walking deck and lubricate if needed (the Maintenance section holds the procedure). After application, in standby mode, **hold the ENTER key for 3 seconds** to reset the message.

The TR150 book says this twice (console page and service checklist: "Hold ENTER button for 3 seconds to reset"). The TRX2500, TRX3500 and TRX4500 books say it once each, in the lubrication procedure; the TRX3500 and TRX4500 engineering mode also has a **Lube Reset** entry ([engineering card](xterra-trx-console-engineering-mode-program-and-speed-keys-sleep-lube-child-lock.md)).

The TRX1000 book of the same layout contradicts itself — ENTER on one page, PROGRAM on two others ([PROGRAM card](xterra-treadmill-console-clear-the-lube-message-by-holding-program-3-seconds.md)).
