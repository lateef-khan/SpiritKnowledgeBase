**The rapid speed keys on this console are numbered 2 through 6.** The later CT800 console has
twelve of them, numbered 1 through 12, and its card is `ct850-2020-quick-start`.

- Press and release **any key** to wake the display up if it is not already on.
- Press and release the **Start** key to begin belt movement at **0.5 mph**, then adjust to the
  desired speed with the **Up/Down** keys. You may also use the **rapid speed keys 2 through 6** to
  set a specific speed directly.
- To slow the tread-belt, press and hold the **Down** key - on the console or on the hand rail - to
  the desired speed. The rapid speed adjust keys **2 through 6** work here too.
- To stop the tread-belt, press and release the red **Stop** key.

The 2016 manual prints the same four bullets with "button" wherever the 2012 manual prints "key";
that manual replaces the word throughout, including where it means the safety key. Neither manual
says what speed each of the five rapid keys selects.

What the second and third press of Stop do is a separate behaviour: `ct850-2020-pause-stop-reset`.
