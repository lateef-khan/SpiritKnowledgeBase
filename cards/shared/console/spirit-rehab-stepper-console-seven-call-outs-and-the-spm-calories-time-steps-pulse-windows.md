**The drawing numbers seven parts and no more:**

| # | Call-out |
|---|---|
| **1** | **Graphic display** - the dot matrix, with the segmented track around it |
| **2** | **Message window** |
| **3** | **Program keys** |
| **4** | **Function keys** |
| **5** | **Data displays** |
| **6** | **HR percent** - the heart rate bar graph, right of the dot matrix |
| **7** | **Set Up key** |

**The five data display windows are labelled across the console**:
**SPM · CALORIES · TIME · STEPS · PULSE**. **SPM is steps per minute** and **STEPS is a total step
count**; the manual expands both in its Basic information paragraph.

**Resistance level and watt measurements are displayed on either side of the graphic display** - a
**LEVEL** readout on one side and **WATTS** on the other.

**The HR percent bar graph is scaled 60% to 90%** on the drawing, and reads percent of maximum heart
rate.

**The program keys are labelled** Manual, Hill, Plateau, Cardio, Interval, **Symmetry**, User 1,
User 2, **HR** and **Set up**; the function keys are **Start**, **Stop** and a **Level - / +**
rocker. **Symmetry is the key that has no counterpart on any other Spirit console** - it switches the
segmented track between a track and a foot position indicator, and displays the leg power graph.

**The MS300 drawing also labels a computer cable** entering the console; the 7.0S and 7.5S drawings
do not.

**Do not answer this from a CS800 or CRS800S console card.** Those machines have two Scan buttons, a
USB Charger, a Fan and a quarter-mile track ring, and none of those is on this drawing
(`spirit-ce-console-led-console-face`). **This console has no fan and no scan key.**
