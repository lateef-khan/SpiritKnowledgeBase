These machines run from an **external power supply (WE55/95: external AC power cord)** and the console powers up on connection. With **no input for 20 minutes** it goes to **stand-by**: the display turns off; **press any key** to turn it back on. The WE25/WE35 books instead describe a sleep draw like a switched-off TV and give the E25 no power switch (own card).
