1. Press and hold **Start, Stop and Enter** at the same time, until the display shows 'Maintenance Menu'. Press Enter.
2. Scroll with the **Fast/Slow** keys; **Stop** returns to the previous menu selection. The selections are:
   - **A. Key Test**.
   - **B. Display Test**.
   - **C. Security** - locks the keypad (child lock; hold Start and Enter 3 seconds to unlock).
   - **D. Functions** (Enter; Fast/Slow to scroll): **I. Sleep Mode** (on = power down after 30 minutes inactivity); **II. Pause Mode** (on = 5 minutes of pause, off = pause indefinitely); **III. Maintenance** (odometer readings); **IV. Units** (English or Metric); **V. Key Tone** (beep off or on when a button is pressed).
   - **E. Exit**.
