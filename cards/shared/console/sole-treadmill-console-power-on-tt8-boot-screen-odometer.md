Plug into a wall outlet, switch on the power switch at the front below the motor hood, and fit the safety key - the machine will not power on without it. **No software-version scroll is printed.** The **boot screen shows odometer readings** for a short time: total time (hours in use) and total distance (miles, or kilometres if set to metric). Then the machine enters **idle mode**, the starting point for operation.
