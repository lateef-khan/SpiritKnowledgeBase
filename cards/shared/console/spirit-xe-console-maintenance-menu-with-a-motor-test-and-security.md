**The console has built-in maintenance/diagnostic software.** It changes the display between English
and Metric and turns off the key beep, among other things.

**To open it: press and hold Start, Stop and Enter together for about 5 seconds.** The message window
displays **`ALTXXXX Engineering mode`**. Press **Enter** to reach the menu.

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **a. Key test** | Tests all the keys to make sure they are functioning |
| **b. Display test** | Tests all the display functions |
| **c. Functions** | Press Enter to reach the settings below |
| **d. Security** | Locks the keypad so no unauthorized use is allowed |
| **e. Factory settings** | Access only allowed by manufacturing |

**Inside Functions, in the printed order:**

1. **Sleep mode** - turn on to have the console power down automatically **after 20 minutes** of
   inactivity
2. **Pause Mode** - turn on to allow **5 minutes** of pause, turn off to have the console pause
   indefinitely
3. **ODO reset** - resets the odometer
4. **Units** - English or Metric display readings
5. **Beep** - turn the key beep on or off
6. **Motor test** - continually runs the tensioning gear motor
7. **Exit**

**`ALTXXXX` is what the manual prints**, and it does not say what the X's stand for. The manual also
notes the menu "may be called Engineering mode, depending on version".

**The 2005 XE150, XE350 and XE550 have no such menu.** Their manual documents none at all.

**The XE395-2010 menu looks the same but is not.** It adds an **Incline On/Off** switch, a **Safety**
item and a separate **Calibration** keystroke, and orders Functions differently:
`xe395-2010-console-engineering-mode-with-an-incline-switch-and-calibration`.

**The 2007 dealer service manual for these three machines, and the XE400 and XE500, prints this menu with a Safety item and a described Factory Set.** Its Procedure 7 holds Start, Enter and Stop "until display beeps", adds **Safety On/Off** ("XE300 calls it 'Inc Max'") to the Functions list, and says Factory Set "will reset saved user programs" - and prints no minutes for the sleep (`spirit-xe-2007-console-engineering-mode-with-a-safety-item-and-a-factory-set-that-resets-user-programs`).
