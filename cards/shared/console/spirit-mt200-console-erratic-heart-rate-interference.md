**Caution! Do not use this treadmill for Heart Rate Control unless a steady, solid
Actual Heart Rate value is being displayed. High, wild, random numbers being
displayed indicate a problem.**

Areas to look at for interference which may cause erratic heart rate:

1. **Microwave ovens, TVs, small appliances**, etc.
2. **Fluorescent lights.**
3. **Some household security systems.**
4. **Perimeter fence for a pet.**
5. Some people have problems with the transmitter picking up a signal from their
   skin. **Try wearing the transmitter upside down.** Normally it is oriented with
   the logo right side up.
6. The antenna that picks up your heart rate is very sensitive. If there is an
   outside noise source, **turning the whole machine 90 degrees may de-tune the
   interference**.
7. **If there is another person wearing a chest strap within 1 meter, it will
   interfere.**
8. **If you continue to experience problems contact your dealer.**

**Both MT200 manuals print this list identically**, except that the 2010 manual says
the transmitter is normally oriented with the **Spirit logo** right side up and the
2022 manual says only **the logo**.

**This list is not the same as the one on the other Spirit treadmills, and the
difference is in the numbers.** The CTSBS900, 4.0T, 7.0T, 8.0T, CT900 and XT lists
open with **"treadmill is not properly grounded"**, which neither MT200 manual
prints, and they end at a strap **within 3 feet**, not 1 metre. See
`ctsbs900-heart-rate-chest-strap-troubleshooting`. Quote the figure from the machine
in front of you.
