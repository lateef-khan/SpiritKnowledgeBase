**"Choose the preferred log in method or enter the system as a guest. Please make sure the WiFi
connection is available, or you will enter the system as a guest in offline status."**

| Method | What the book says |
|---|---|
| **QR code** | "The user scans the QR code with their own phone and log in." |
| **NFC** | "The console is equipped with an RFID reader. To log in, hold your phone/RFID tag to the symbol located on the **bottom left** of the console." |
| **Pin code** | "The clinician generates the pin code in the **Dyaco Insight App** and provides it to the user. The User can log in using the pin code for **one day**." |

**User log in after exercise** - "You could also log in to the system to record your exercise data
after finishing the session if you didn't log in the beginning."

**Bottom left, says the owner's manual; bottom-right, says the service manual.** Both service manuals'
NFC Sensor Test says "Place the NFC tag near the **bottom-right** corner of the console"
(`spirit-med-80-bike-console-service-tab-machine-type-loopback-nfc-keypad-beacon-and-crank-calibration`).
Nothing reconciles them; hold the tag to whichever corner carries the symbol on the console in front
of you. The 8.5S stepper's books disagree the same way.

**The Log Out icon is the left-most icon in the status bar**
(`spirit-med-80-bike-console-screen-overview-prints-a-factory-mode-banner-and-a-treadmill-tile`).
The login page is where the self-test ends
(`spirit-med-80-bike-console-power-on-self-test-login-page-and-thirty-minute-power-down`); the
organization account behind it is on
`spirit-med-80-bike-console-first-run-language-wifi-organization-account-registration-and-clock`.
The 8.5UE and 8.0T print the same three methods (`85ue-2025-console-login-by-qr-code-nfc-or-pin`,
`80t-2026-console-user-login-methods`).
