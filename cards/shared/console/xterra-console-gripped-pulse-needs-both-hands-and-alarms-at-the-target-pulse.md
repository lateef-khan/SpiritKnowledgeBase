**Gripped pulse** displays the current heart rate in beats per minute during the workout. **Both hands must hold the gripped pulse** for a reading. You may preset a target pulse, and **the computer alarms as soon as your heart rate reaches the preset figure.** This data is a rough guide for comparing sessions and cannot be used for medical purposes.

**"P" in the PULSE window means no pulse signal is coming in.** The same note names a "chest transmitter" as the other way to feed the pulse, though none of the four books prints a chest-strap page or says a strap is included. The one-minute recovery test that uses the same grips is on [the recovery card](xterra-console-pulse-recovery-grade-with-p-and-err.md).
