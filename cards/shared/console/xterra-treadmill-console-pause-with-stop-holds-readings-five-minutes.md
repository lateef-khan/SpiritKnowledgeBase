1. While the treadmill is running, press the red **Stop** key once to pause. The tread-belt slowly decelerates to a stop, and the Time, Distance and Calorie readings **hold** while the unit is in pause mode. After **five minutes** the display resets and returns to the start-up screen.
2. To resume, press the **Start** key while in pause mode; the speed returns to the previous setting.
3. Pause is executed when Stop is pressed once. If Stop is pressed a **second time**, the console resets and returns to the idle (start-up) screen.

The TR150 service manual adds that holding Stop for 3 seconds in ready mode resets the display ([service-manual keys card](../../tr150-2021/console/keys-in-ready-and-run-mode-per-the-service-manual.md)). The books with an incline motor and a workout summary say something different — see [the TR300 card](../../tr300-2021/console/pause-ends-program-with-summary-on-second-stop.md) and [the TR6.6 family card](xterra-tr-console-pause-second-stop-summary-third-stop-idle-hold-resets.md).
