For "can't connect, search shows no device list, or the connection breaks", the sheet says to make sure of these five points:

1. Is the phone or tablet **jailbroken** ("System JB")?
2. Is **Bluetooth ON** on the phone or tablet?
3. **Interference** from heavy Wi-Fi use around the machine.
4. Has **more than one device** been opened with a Bluetooth connection to the machine? (Only one can hold the link - see [the second-user card](xterra-console-app-second-user-cannot-connect-until-the-first-disconnects.md).)
5. The **Bluetooth device is damaged.**

This answer comes from the two-page **XTERRA APP Q & A** sheet of October 2018, written for the original Xterra App (iOS 7.0 / Android 4.3). The 2023-2025 books use the XTERRA+ app instead; the app card for those machines is [here](xterra-treadmill-console-xterra-plus-app-ios-13-android-8.md).
