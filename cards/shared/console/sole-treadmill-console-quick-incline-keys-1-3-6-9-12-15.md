Incline may be adjusted anytime after belt movement. Hold the **Up/Down keys** (console or handgrip; TT8 books: 'Up/Down keys' with no handgrip mention), or jump straight with the rapid keys **1, 3, 6, 9, 12 and 15** on the left-hand side of the console (TT8 books: 'Quick Incline key'). The message window shows the incline position as it changes, except the TT8 books where the space above the Incline heading on the LCD shows the deck elevation. Other books print different sets: 1-3-6-9-12-14-15, 2-4-6-8-10-12, 0-12 levels, 2-through-10 or 2-through-12.
