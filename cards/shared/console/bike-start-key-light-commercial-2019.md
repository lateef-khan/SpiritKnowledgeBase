After the console powers up, press the **Start** key. This initiates **Start key** mode.

- Time counts **up** from zero.
- The resistance level is adjusted by hand with the **Level up and down** buttons.
- The dot matrix display shows a track with a blinking dot indicating your progress as it travels around the track.

**The manual's wording here also says incline can be adjusted. These bikes have no incline.** Only resistance level changes. The same sentence appears in the 2019 upright and recumbent manual, and is wrong there too.
