"No record in the calendar data when finish workout":

1. While working out with the app connected, check whether the **Bluetooth symbol stays on the console** the whole time - and whether there was a power outage.
2. **Pressing STOP repeatedly and too fast** causes data loss while the console is transmitting to the phone. Make sure the phone shows *"Congratulations, you have completed your workout. Press STOP to exit the program."* - that message means the phone has received the data from the console.

This answer comes from the two-page **XTERRA APP Q & A** sheet of October 2018, written for the original Xterra App (iOS 7.0 / Android 4.3). The 2023-2025 books use the XTERRA+ app instead; the app card for those machines is [here](xterra-treadmill-console-xterra-plus-app-ios-13-android-8.md).
