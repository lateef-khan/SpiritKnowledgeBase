**Quick Start.** After the console powers up, press **Start**. Time counts up from zero; **Resistance and Incline are adjusted with the Level up/down and Incline up/down keys.**

**Display.** The dot-matrix shows the resistance profile; **Start during a program switches it to the incline profile, and Start again to a lap track a quarter mile long**. **Enter** switches the message window: program name first, then **Speed, Watts, Laps, Altitude (elevation gain), Current Level/Max Level, Scan**, and repeat. To the left of the dot-matrix a number shows the resistance or incline level.

**Data displayed.** Calories (Kcal, an estimate from exercise type, level, pace and weight); Time (elapsed or remaining); **Seg Time** - the time left in the current segment (column) of the profile; Pulse from the hand pulse sensors; RPM; Speed in mph or km/h if "Km" shows; **Incline/Ramp - incline level from 1 to 20**; Distance.

The heart icon, pause and reset are on their own cards ([heart icon](xterra-console-heart-icon-blinks-when-the-grips-or-strap-are-read.md), [pause](xterra-console-stop-once-pauses-5-minutes-twice-ends-with-a-summary-held-3-seconds-resets.md)); the SB500 prints the bike version without incline ([card](../../sb500-2020/console/quick-start-lap-track-and-enter-cycling-speed-watts-laps-level-and-scan.md)). The SB4.5r, FS5.8e and FS5.9e books are scans read from the render; the SB500 2014 printing is a scan of the same text as the 2020 export.
