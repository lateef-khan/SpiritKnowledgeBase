When power is connected the console powers up automatically.

**These models operate on 24V/2.7A DC**, supplied by the power pack that is connected to AC power.

**This is the only bike family in this range that uses a 24 volt pack.** The upright and recumbent bikes of the same year use a 12 volt pack, and the 2016 light commercial bikes of the same shape have no pack at all and go straight to 115 VAC. A 12 volt supply will not run this machine.

**The manual does not state the mains voltage here.** It says only "connected to AC power", and it does not say where the line cord plugs in. Do not read a socket location or a mains figure off another year.

On first power-on the **boot screen** shows odometer readings for a short time:

- **Total time** — how many hours the bike has been in use.
- **Total distance** — how many miles, or kilometres if the bike is set to metric readings, the bike has gone.

The bike then enters **idle mode**, which is the starting point for operation.
