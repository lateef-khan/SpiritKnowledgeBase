**The console will display RPM, Calories burned, Time (elapsed or countdown), Distance traveled,
Pulse, Resistance, Program Name, number of Laps completed, and Segment Time.**

**There is also a Resistance profile graph** that lets you see how hard you have worked and how
challenging the upcoming segments will be.

All four 2023 Spirit residential bike manuals print these two sentences word for word.

**RPM in place of pace or speed is what makes this the bike list.** The 2023 XT treadmills print the
same paragraph with **Pace**, **Speed** and **Incline** and a **Speed & Incline profile graph**:
`xt-2023-console-readouts`. **Neither list is a substitute for the other.**

**Laps are on the list although no 2023 bike describes a lap track.** None of the four prints a 1/4
Mile Track paragraph, and their Workout Summary reports no Laps field at all - yet the console can
show a lap count and the workout-mode drawing labels **Total Laps Completed**. **The count exists;
only the ring around it is gone.**

**This is a list of what the console can show, not an order it cycles them in.** The 2016-2021 bikes
printed a Basic Information paragraph saying the Message Center scans Speed, Watts, Laps, Segment time
and Max level four seconds apiece; **the 2023 books dropped it** and say nothing about how the reader
gets from one reading to the next:
`spirit-xe-console-message-center-scan-shows-laps-segment-time-and-max-level`.

**Watts is not on this list**, although the Workout Summary on the same page still reports **Avg.
Watts**.
