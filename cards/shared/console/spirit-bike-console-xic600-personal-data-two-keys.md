1. **GENDER.** Press the **left hand key** until **SPEED** is displayed, then press the **right hand
   key for 3 seconds**. The TIME field flashes. Press the left hand key until the **gender icon is
   blinking**, then press the right hand key to select **male or female**.
2. **WEIGHT UNITS.** Press the **left hand key** to move to the measuring value for weight, then
   press the **right hand key** to select **Lb or Kg**.
3. **BODYWEIGHT.** Press the **left hand key** to move to the bodyweight, then press the **right hand
   key** to increase the weight. **Press and hold the right hand key for rapid advance.**

**Why it matters.** Calories on this console are an approximation, and the manual calls them an
estimate only; entering the correct gender and weight is what the console has to work from.

**No age field.** The console asks for gender, weight units and bodyweight and nothing else - there
is no age entry and no automatic maximum heart rate, which is why the target zone is set as two raw
BPM limits rather than as percentages.

**The weight unit setting is the only unit switch on this console.** The manual describes no
separate metric/imperial control; MPH/KPH appear together in the RPM section by design.

**All of it lives behind the SPEED window**, reached by holding the right hand key for 3 seconds -
the same doorway as the workout timer and the heart rate zone.
