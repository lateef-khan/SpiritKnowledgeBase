The **1/4-mile track** is displayed around the dot matrix window. The flashing dot indicates your progress. Once the 1/4 mile is complete the next lap begins, and the number of completed laps is displayed in the **Laps** window.

The books print no metric equivalent for the lap here; the TR75 and TR85 books give 0.4 km ([their track card](xterra-tr-console-dot-matrix-20-by-10-and-the-quarter-mile-track.md)).
