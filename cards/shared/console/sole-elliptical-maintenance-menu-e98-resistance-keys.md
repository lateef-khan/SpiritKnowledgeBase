Hold **Start, Stop and Enter together until the display shows 'Maintenance Menu'**, then press Enter. Scroll with the **Resistance Up/Down** keys; **Stop** returns to the previous menu. Selections: A. Key Test; B. Display Test; C. Functions (Display Mode, Pause Mode, ODO Reset, Units, **Brake Test** for the brake function); D. Security; E. Factory Settings; F. Exit. The Display Mode line reads opposite between the two books (own cards).
