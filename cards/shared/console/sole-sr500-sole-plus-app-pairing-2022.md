The machine carries a **Bluetooth transceiver** for the free **SOLE+ App** (Apple Store / Google Play). Enable Bluetooth, tap the **top-right icon** to search for Sole equipment, tap **Connect** once detected; the **console Bluetooth icon lights up** when synced.

A Bluetooth wireless heart-rate chest-strap transmitter can connect to the machine **even while the App is already in use** (the book words it as the strap receiving signals with the app running). The 2019 book has no Apps page, so this card covers the 2022 machine only.
