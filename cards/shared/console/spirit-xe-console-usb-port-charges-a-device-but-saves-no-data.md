**Charge your personal device during your workout using the on-console USB port.** The fitness
equipment power must be on.

1. **Connect your USB charging cable (not included) to the USB Power Port and to your device.**
2. **Check that your device icon indicates it is charging.**

**The manual's three notes:**

- **A USB charging cable is not included.** Make sure a compatible one is used.
- The port **provides up to 5 Vdc / 1.0 amp** and **meets USB 2.0 regulations**.
- **You will not be able to save your workout data to a USB via this port; it is used for charging
  purposes only.**

All three manuals print this word for word.

**On the XE795-2023 there is a fourth note, because that machine has no mains supply.** The port only
works while you are pedalling hard enough:
`xe795-2023-console-usb-charging-needs-forty-rpm`.

**No other residential XE elliptical has a USB port.** The 2005, 2007, 2010 and 2021 manuals describe
none, and the XE395ENT drawing does not mark one even though its text describes it. **Do not tell an
owner of one of those to look for a port.**

**Workout data leaves these machines another way.** The XE395-2023 and XE795-2023 sync over Bluetooth
to the Spirit+ app (`xt-2023-console-spirit-plus-app-pairing`); the XE395ENT saves to the cloud over
WiFi against a profile (`xe395ent-2021-console-profiles-and-the-spirit-app-link`).
