"HOW TO CHANGE MILES TO KM" - as printed:

1. **Two people are needed** for this. Unplug the adaptor from the machine.
2. Press the **Start/Stop button, the Enter button and the Up button all at the same time** and connect the adaptor (one person holds the keys, the other plugs in).
3. The screen beeps and turns on.
4. Press Enter until the miles or km is flashing.
5. Press Up to change to km.
6. Press Enter until the monitor resets. Your machine is now in km.

There is no other units menu on this console. The four books print the page word for word (the FS150 says "from the elliptical").
