**TIME.** Measured in min:sec. There are **two clocks**: a time of day clock and a workout timer
clock. The workout time counts up or counts down while you pedal. **If pedalling stops, the time
stops counting after 3 seconds.**

**RPM / CADENCE.** How fast the cranks are rotating. The approximate speed of the bike can also be
shown in MPH or KPH. Beside the MPH/KPH figure the RPM section carries a **bar graph** so the rider
can keep track of approximate RPM visually.

**DISTANCE.** The **virtual** distance travelled, calculated as though the rider were on a bike
whose tyres are the same size as the group bike's flywheel. It is not a road distance.

**KCAL.** The approximation of calories burned. **The manual calls these an estimate only.**

**HEART RATE.** The approximation of heart rate detected from the chest belt during the workout.
**The chest strap is sold separately.**
