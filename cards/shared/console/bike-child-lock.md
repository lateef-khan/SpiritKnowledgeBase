Child lock is **off** by default. You switch it on or off in Engineering Mode.

**When it is on:** the message window shows "CONSOLE LOCKED", then "CHILD LOCK-ON PRESS START AND ENTER TO ENABLE OPERATION". No key works.

**To unlock:** hold **START and ENTER together**.

**The hold time is printed two different ways in the same manual.** The CHILD LOCK MODE section says "more than two seconds". The Maintenance Menu section says "3 seconds". Hold for 3 seconds and both are satisfied.

You must clear child lock before you can use Reset Mode.
