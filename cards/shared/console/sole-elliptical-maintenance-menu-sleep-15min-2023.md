Same entry as the 2016 LCD menu (hold Start, Stop and Enter 5 seconds for Engineering Mode; Level keys navigate; Key Test, Display Test, Functions, Security with the 3-second Start+Enter unlock, Factory Set, Exit) with one changed line: **III. Sleep Mode - turn on to power down after 15 minutes of inactivity** replaces the old Display Mode 30-minute line. The 2023 touchscreen books print no console menu at all (own absence card).
