> **This counter is powered by 2 C batteries. (Alkaline Battery Recommended)**

and, from the line above it on the same page, the console is **battery-operated (batteries not
included)**. So two C cells have to be bought with the machine, and alkaline is what the manual asks
for.

These machines have **no mains connection at all** - no power cord, no outlet requirement, no
voltage and no amperage anywhere in the book. The two C cells are the only power on the whole
machine.

**The battery connections are at the back of the console.** The manual never uses the words
"battery compartment". The troubleshooting page on all twenty-eight books makes its third check
*check connections at back of console for battery and switch assembly*, and every CSS assembly
chapter - with the CSD-LPSR and CSD-PFRD books - tells the builder to *connect batteries and display
connection* before the shrouds go on, so the cells go in while the tower is still open.

The manual prints **no** battery life figure, **no** replacement interval and **no** low-battery
warning behaviour. A dead counter is diagnosed by replacing the cells first - see
`spirit-strength-errors-rep-counter-and-timer-does-not-turn-on`.

**Not the CRW800 rower.** That Spirit console also runs on two C cells, in a compartment on the back
of the console, but it is a different product line with a different console and a different manual.

**The 2026 CSS-DELT revision and the May 2025 CSD-CPSP update print the closing line word for word.**
