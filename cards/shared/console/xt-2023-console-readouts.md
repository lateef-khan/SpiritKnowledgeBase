The console will display **Pace, Calories burned, Time (elapsed or countdown), Distance traveled,
Pulse, Speed, Incline, Program Name, number of Laps completed, and Segment Time.** There is also a
**Speed & Incline profile graph** that lets you see how hard you have worked and how challenging the
upcoming segments will be.

The workout-mode screen labels these readings:

| Where | Reading |
|---|---|
| Across the top | Total Calories Burned, Current Heart Rate, Cumulative Total Mileage, Lap Track, Total Completed Laps, Current Average Pace |
| Left edge | Current Incline |
| Right edge | Current Speed, Maximum Heart Rate Percentage |
| Lower left | Message Center |
| Bottom centre | Current Time (elapsed or countdown, based on the program) and the Speed & Incline Profile |

On the XT285, XT385, XT485 and XT685 the screen also carries a column of Bluetooth and USB status
icons down the left side. The XT185 screen does not.

**Scope of the table above: it is the 2023 manuals' workout-screen diagram only.** The five 2015
manuals print the readout list in the first paragraph - and nothing else. They carry no
screen-label diagram, and the only status icon any 2015 manual mentions is a Bluetooth icon that
lights when the Spirit Fit App connects (XT285, XT385, XT485 and XT685 only); see
`xt-2015-console-spirit-fit-app-pairing`. No 2015 XT manual mentions a USB port.
