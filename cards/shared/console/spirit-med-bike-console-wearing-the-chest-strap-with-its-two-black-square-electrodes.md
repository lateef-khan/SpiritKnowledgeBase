Nine bike books - the six 2025 Spirit Medical owner's manuals, the Dyaco MED 7.0R and the two Dyaco
PT 4.0 editions - print the same six bullets word for word.

1. **Attach the transmitter to the elastic strap** using the locking parts.
2. **Adjust the strap as tightly as possible** as long as it is not too tight to remain comfortable.
3. **Position the transmitter with the logo centred in the middle of your body facing away from your
   chest** (some people must position it slightly left of centre). Attach the final end of the strap by
   inserting the round end and, using the locking parts, secure the transmitter and strap around your
   chest.
4. **Position the transmitter immediately below the pectoral muscles.**
5. **Sweat is the best conductor** for the very minute heart-beat signals, but **plain water can pre-wet
   the electrodes - the 2 black square areas on the reverse side of the belt and either side of the
   transmitter**. Wear the strap **a few minutes before your workout**: some users, because of body
   chemistry, get a strong steady signal only after "warming up". Clothing over the strap does not
   affect performance.
6. **Stay within range** of the console for a strong steady signal; the range "may vary somewhat but
   generally stay close enough to the console". Bare skin assures proper operation; to wear it over a
   shirt, **moisten the areas of the shirt the electrodes rest on**.

**The electrodes are black squares on these straps**, where the treadmill books of the CT and XT
families describe ribbed oval areas; the 7.0T and 8.0T print the same black-square wording as this card
(`spirit-2026t-console-chest-strap-wearing`).

What the strap transmits on and what battery it takes are on
`spirit-med-bike-console-chest-strap-5-khz-2500-hours-and-a-cr2032` (2025 Spirit and MED 7.0R books)
and `spirit-med-40-pt-bike-console-chest-strap-bluetooth-or-ant-plus-with-no-frequency` (PT
editions).
