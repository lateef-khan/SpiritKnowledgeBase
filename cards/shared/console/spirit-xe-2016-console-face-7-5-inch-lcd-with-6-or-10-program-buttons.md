**Every one of the six books calls out a 7.5" LCD Display** on its Display Windows page, and shows
the keys under it as **FAN, START, LEVEL - and +, STOP, ENTER** - readable only on the drawing. The
Function Button Locations page names the **PROGRAM BUTTONS**, the **CONTROL KEYS** and a **Fan Key**
described as "Cooling fan switch on or off".

**The program button row is the one thing that differs, and the legends undercount it:**

| Book | Program buttons as the legend prints them | Keys on the drawing |
|---|---|---|
| XE195-2016 | Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Interval, HR (5) | MANUAL, CARDIO, HILL, FAT BURN, INTERVAL, HR (**6**) |
| XE295-2016 | Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, Interval, User1~2, HR1~2 (10) | the same ten |
| XG400-2016 | the same ten as the XE295 | the same ten |
| XE395-2016 | Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Strength, Interval, 2 User, 2HR (9) | MANUAL, HILL, FAT BURN, **CARDIO**, STRENGTH, INTERVAL, USER-1, USER-2, HR-1, HR-2 (**10**) |
| XE895-2016 | the same nine as the XE395 | the same ten as the XE395 |
| XE795-2016 | Manual, Hill, Fat Burn, Strength, Interval, Calories, Fusion, 2 User, 2HR (11) | MANUAL, HILL, FAT BURN, **CARDIO**, STRENGTH, INTERVAL, CALORIES, FUSION, USER 1-2, HR 1-2 (**10** keys; the user and HR keys are paired) |

Count the keys on the machine before naming a program; the programs themselves are held with the
programs cards.

**Five of the six drawings carry a MUSCLE PROFILE figure and a HEART RATE % PROFILE bar** (50 to 90
%) either side of the LCD, twin speakers in the fan bar, and a **3.5 mm audio socket** at the right
of the key row. The XE195 drawing has the speakers but no muscle figure, no bar and no socket. The
XE395, XE795 and XE895 books also list a Speaker among the Upper Controllers and print an Amplifier
Board page; those are specs matters.

**What the LCD itself shows is the LCD Layout page**
(`spirit-xe-2016-console-lcd-layout-calories-time-distance-incline-level-and-a-ten-character-message-window`).

**"7.5-inch LCD" is a service-manual figure.** The 2018, 2019 and 2021 owner's manuals of these
machines label the same face by its features and never give a size
(`spirit-xe-console-face-call-outs-muscle-profile-heart-rate-profile-and-speakers`). The 2016
residential bikes carry the same console
(`spirit-xbr-2016-console-face-7-5-inch-lcd-with-6-10-or-11-program-buttons`), and the CE850-2016's
commercial twin of the XE895 is drawn with a **Matrix Display** and numbered keys instead
(`spirit-ce-console-blue-led-console-face`).
