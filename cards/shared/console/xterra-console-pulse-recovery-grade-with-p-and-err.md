The computer has a recovery program to monitor your heart-rate recovery. **After your workout, press the recovery button and keep both hands on the gripped pulse.** The computer stops monitoring every function except time, which counts down from **00:60 to 00:00**. At zero the pulse window shows a grade between **F1 and F6 - F1 the best, F6 the worst** reading you can receive. Press the recovery button again to return to the main display.

**Note (as printed):** if no pulse signal is coming in the computer shows **"P"** in the PULSE window. If it shows **"ERR"** in the message window, press PULSE RECOVERY again and make sure your hands are kept well on the grips (or the chest transmitter is attached well). ERR here is a failed recovery reading, not a machine fault code.

The SB240 and FS3.0 print the same test with a table of what each grade means ([card](xterra-console-recovery-grades-from-outstanding-to-poor.md)); the SB600 shows ER instead ([card](../../sb600-2023/console/recovery-grade-and-the-er-reading.md)).
