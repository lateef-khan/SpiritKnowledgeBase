**This is the Android settings underneath the console, not the Sole engineering menu.** The engineering menu holds max speed, wheel size and calibration, and is reached a different way. See `sole-engineering-menu-touchscreen`.

The Wi-Fi page is the door. It is a real Android settings page, and its search box reaches every other Android setting.

1. Log in. The **Guest** profile is enough.
2. Tap the **Wi-Fi symbol in the top right corner** of the home screen. The Android **Wi-Fi** page opens.
3. Tap the **magnifying glass in the top right** of the Wi-Fi page.
4. Type what you need, then tap the result. Searching `location` reaches the Location page and its app permission list.

**Getting back out.** A small floating panel with **Apps**, **Go Back** and **Show Panels** sits on top of these screens. **Go Back** leaves the current screen. The panel can be dragged, and **Hide Panels** puts it away when it covers something you need to tap.

**What the source shows.** This route was recorded on a treadmill touchscreen with a GARMIN top bar, going to the Location settings. The source does not say which other settings pages are reachable or whether any are locked.
