The console powers up automatically when power is connected and runs an internal **self-test**: all the lights turn on, the **Message Window** shows the software version (i.e. VER 1.0), the **TOTAL STEPS window** shows how many total steps the climber has been used, and the **Time Window** shows how many total hours it has been used.

The totals stay up for only a few seconds, then the console goes to the startup display: the dot-matrix display scrolls through the workout profiles and the Message Window scrolls the start-up message.

The SC200 books show a DC-adapter machine (cord on the left side near the front); the SC300 book shows a **120-volt mains machine with a power switch** where the line cord plugs in on the left side near the middle. Plugging in and switching on are assembly-side facts (see_also).
