The page is headed "HEART RATE CHEST STRAP* (NOT INCLUDED)" — on the WS200 and WS300 "BLUETOOTH HEART RATE CHEST STRAP* (NOT INCLUDED)".

1. Attach the transmitter to the elastic strap using the interlocking key.
2. Adjust the strap as tightly as possible while still comfortable.
3. Position the transmitter with the logo centered in the middle of your torso, facing away from your chest (some people must position it slightly left of center). Attach the final end of the strap by inserting the round end and, using the locking parts, secure the transmitter and strap around your chest.
4. Position the transmitter directly below the pectoral muscles.
5. Sweat is the best conductor for the tiny heart-beat electrical signals, but plain water can pre-wet the electrodes — the two ribbed oval areas on the reverse of the belt and both sides of the transmitter. Wear the strap a few minutes before the workout; some users take a while to get a strong, steady signal, which lessens after warming up.
6. Stay within range of the receiver — the range varies, but stay close enough to the console for reliable readings. Wearing the transmitter on bare skin assures proper operation; over a shirt, wet the areas of the shirt the electrodes rest on.

Note: the transmitter activates automatically when it detects heart activity and deactivates when it receives none. It is water resistant, but moisture can create false signals, so dry it completely after use to prolong battery life (estimated **2500 hours**). The replacement battery is a **Panasonic CR2032**.

The three books that print this page without the CR2032 line and without "not included" are on [the other strap card](xterra-treadmill-console-wearing-the-chest-strap-and-its-2500-hour-battery.md).
