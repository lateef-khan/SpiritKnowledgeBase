The bike has a Bluetooth transceiver that works with the free **Sole Fitness App** from the Apple Store or Google Play.

1. Download the app.
2. Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your device.
3. Click the icon in the **top left corner** to search for Sole equipment.
4. After the equipment is detected, click **Connect**.
5. When the app and equipment are synced, the **Bluetooth icon on the console lights up**.

The app shows current workout data in three display screens, and saves finished workouts to a built-in personal calendar. It can also sync workout data to **iHealth, MapMyFitness, Record or Fitbit**.

**Minimum operating system: iOS 7 or Android 4.4.**

Maximum possible user profiles in the app: **6**. To make space for a new profile you select the profile you wish to delete.

If the bike is not connected when you finish, the workout is held only briefly — see the SYNC card.
