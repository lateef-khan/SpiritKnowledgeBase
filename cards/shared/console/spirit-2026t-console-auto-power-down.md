**The console automatically powers down after 30 minutes of inactivity. Press any
key to wake the console up again.** The 8.0T words it as any *function* key.

**Always turn off the main power switch when the machine is not in use.**

**Only the 7.0T says the timeout can be disabled.** Its maintenance mode has a
**Sleep** item, and with sleep off the display power is always on; see
`70t-2026-console-maintenance-mode-menu`. The 8.0T lists **Standby or Pause mode**
among its Settings, but prints no figure for it; see
`80t-2026-console-settings-menu`.

**The 4.0T manual states no inactivity timeout at all.**
