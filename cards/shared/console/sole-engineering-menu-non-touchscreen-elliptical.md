1. Turn the power switch on if the machine is not already on.
2. Press and hold **Stop**, **Enter** and **Start**. Keep holding until the screen says EM or Engineering Menu - about 10 seconds.
3. Let go of the buttons and press **Enter**.

Move with the level/resistance up and down buttons. Press **Enter** to choose, **Stop** to go back. **A changed setting must be locked in with Enter or it will not save.**

The usual options:

- **Key Test** - each press beeps and shows a number. You have to press every button to exit.
- **Display Test** - lights the whole display so dead spots show up.
- **Functions**
  - **Display Mode** (may be called **Sleep Mode**). Called Display Mode: ON keeps the screen awake, OFF lets it sleep after 15 to 30 minutes. Called Sleep Mode: the other way round.
  - **Pause Mode** - ON lets the user pause and resume within 5 minutes without losing stats. OFF makes the first Stop press end the workout.
  - **Odo Reset** - shows the odometer since the lube message was last reset. Pressing Enter resets both.
  - **Units** - ENG for miles, MET for kilometres.
  - **Motor Test** - runs the gear motor from lowest to highest resistance automatically.
  - **Manual** - direct control of the gear motor with the level buttons, showing the values sent to it.
  - **Key Tone** - ON allows beeps, OFF mutes most of them.
- **Security** (child lock) - ON shows CONSOLE LOCKED on boot or after inactivity. Hold **Start** and **Enter** for a few seconds to unlock.
- **Factory Set** - resets the engineering-menu settings to defaults. Does not touch user workouts.
- **Exit**

**The E20 owner's book prints this as a numbered F00-F11 menu** (key test, display test, odo reset, units, sleep, motor test, manual, pause, key tone, child lock, factory set, exit); see `e20-2026-engineering-mode`.
