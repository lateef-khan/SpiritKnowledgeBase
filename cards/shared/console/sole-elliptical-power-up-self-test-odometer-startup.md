These models connect directly to 115 VAC with a power switch where the line cord plugs in, left side near the front; connecting power brings the console up by itself. At first power-on it runs an internal self-test: all lights turn on, a software version shows (i.e. VER 1.0), and an odometer window shows virtual miles gone plus total hours used. After a few seconds the console goes to the startup display: the dot matrix scrolls the workout profiles and the Message Window scrolls the start-up message.

Two printed wordings, same fact: the 2011 AE books show the version in the Message window and the odometer in the Calorie window; the 2012-2015 and stencil books show the version in the Message Window and the odometer in the Distance Window. The E35-2016 book prints 'virtual kilometer' (verified on the render) where every sibling book prints miles.
