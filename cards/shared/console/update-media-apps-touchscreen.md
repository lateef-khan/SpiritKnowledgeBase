1. Make sure the machine is connected to Wi-Fi. Once it is, update notifications arrive on their own.
2. Tap the **gear** icon to open Settings.
3. Tap **Media Apps**.
4. Each app that needs updating has a red **Update** button beside it. Tap it.
5. The update takes a few moments and resets the console.
