After the console powers up, press the **Start** key. This initiates **Quick Start** mode.

- Time counts **up** from zero.
- The workload and resistance are adjusted by hand with the **Level up and down** buttons.
- The dot matrix display shows a track with a blinking dot indicating your progress as it travels around the track.

The manual calls this **Quick Start**. From the 2019 model year the same paragraph calls it **Start key mode** and adds a mention of incline, which these bikes do not have.
