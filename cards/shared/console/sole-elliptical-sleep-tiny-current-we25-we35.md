With **no speed input for 20 minutes** the console goes to sleep; press any key to wake it. Asleep it draws a tiny current, like a TV that is turned off. The **E35 connects directly to 115 VAC with a power switch** where the cord plugs in (left side near the front); the **E25 has no power switch**.
