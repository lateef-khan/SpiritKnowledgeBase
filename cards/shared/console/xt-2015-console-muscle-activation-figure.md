**There is an anatomical figure at the top of the console.** It lights all the areas that are
activated when using the treadmill, during any of the programs. You control which muscles are
activated by **changing the incline and swinging your arms**; the preset programs decide which lower
body muscles are activated by adjusting the incline automatically.

- The **upper body LEDs light any time your hands are not in contact with the pulse grip sensors**.
- The lower body lights show **three degrees of engagement**: **Green** is minimal muscle
  involvement, **Amber** is medium involvement, **Red** is full or heavy activation.

| Elevation | What lights |
|---|---|
| **0-4.5%** | Even muscle distribution - **all four muscle groups Amber** |
| **5-15%** | **Quads Amber**; **Glutes, Hamstrings and Calves Red** |

**These three colours are not the heart rate bar graph colours**, which use the same three names for
a different reading - see `xt-2015-console-heart-rate-percent-profile`.

**The XT185-2015 and XT285-2015 have no muscle activation figure.** Neither manual mentions one.

**The XT685 2010 owner's manual prints the same paragraph, the same two bullets and the same two
elevation bands on its p. 16**, so this card covers that machine.
