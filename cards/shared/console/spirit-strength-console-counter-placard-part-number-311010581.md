> **Counter Placard part number #311010581**

**This is the counter placard, not the workout placard.** Every one of these machines carries two
labels and they are ordered separately:

- the **workout placard** - the exercise picture, with a part number of its own that differs machine
  by machine (`#311008415` on the abdominal, `#311008386` on the seated chest press, and so on);
- the **counter placard** - the label beside the REPS and TIME windows - which is `#311010581` on
  every machine that prints a number for it.

The counter placard number is the **same on every book that prints it**, which is what you would
expect from a part that does not change with the exercise.

## Which books print it, and which do not

**CSD-BCTE is the only CSD manual that prints no counter placard number at all.** Its counter page
ends at the battery line, checked on the page itself at 300 dpi with `tesseract --psm 4`, not on the
extraction. Do not tell a CSD-BCTE owner that their manual gives the number; it does not.

Five other CSD books added the line in a later printing than the one this card first read. Earlier
printing on the left, whichever 2025 printing added the line on the right:

| Machine | Earlier printing | Adds the line |
|---|---|---|
| CSD-ACBE | Feb 2024 book: no placard line | 2025 revision (Ver 5.0, 04/11/2025) |
| CSD-ITOT | Feb 2024 book: no placard line | 2025 revision (Ver 4.0, 02/22/2025) |
| CSD-LELC | already printed it (March 2025 book) | Feb 2025 printing prints it too |
| CSD-LPCE | Feb 2024 book: no placard line | 2025 revision (Ver 4.0, 03/03/2025) |
| CSD-PUDA | Feb 2024 book: no placard line | 2025 revision (Ver 4.0, 15/05/2025) |
| CSD-CPSP | Feb 2024 book: no placard line | May 2025 update |

Each 2025-revision figure was read from a 600 dpi render, not the OCR extraction, because these five
books' PDFs carry no usable native text layer.

**One manual prints the wrong number.** The CSS-LATR book prints `#311008418` where the others print
`#311010581` - and `#311008418` is that machine's own *workout* placard number, printed a page
earlier. See `css-latr-console-counter-placard-repeats-the-exercise-placard-number`.
