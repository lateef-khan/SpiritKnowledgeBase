**The console LCD displays your current heart rate whenever a pulse is detected.** The **Bar Graph,
located to the right of the LCD screen**, shows your current heart rate **as a percentage of your
projected maximum**, worked out from the **age you entered during the programming phase of any of the
10 programs**.

| Percentage of maximum | Colours lit |
|---|---|
| **50-60%** | Amber |
| **65-80%** | Amber and Green |
| **85-90% or more** | Amber, Green and Red |

**All eight manuals print this word for word**, and the console drawing labels the feature **Heart
Rate % Profile**: the XE395-2018 (p. 19), XE795-2018 (p. 18), XE895-2018 (p. 20), XE295-2019 (p. 18),
XG400-2019 (p. 17) and the XE295, XE795 and XG400 of 2021. The bands did not move between the
2018/2019 printings and their 2021 successors.

**The bands leave gaps.** Nothing is printed for 61-64%, 81-84%, or anything below 50%.

**The XE195 has no bar graph in either printing.** Neither the 2019 nor the 2021 console drawing
labels a Heart Rate % Profile, and neither manual has such a section.

**The XE395-2010 has the same three bands with two differences** - the bar graph is on the **left**
of the LCD and the first colour is called **Gold**:
`xe395-2010-console-heart-rate-bar-graph-in-gold-green-and-red`. **The XE395 changed sides and
colour name between its printings**: by August 2018 its manual puts the bar graph **to the right** of
the LCD and calls the first band **Amber**, matching the rest of the family, which is why the
XE395-2018 is on this card and the XE395-2010 is not.

**On the 2023 pair the feature survives only as a call-out.** Both workout-mode drawings label a
**Maximum Heart Rate Percentage** area, but neither manual prints any colour bands for it.
