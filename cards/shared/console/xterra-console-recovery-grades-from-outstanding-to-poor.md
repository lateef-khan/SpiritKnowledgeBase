After exercising, keep holding the hand grips (the SB240: or wear the Bluetooth chest strap, sold separately) and press **PULSE RECOVERY** (FS3.0: RECOVERY). Every function stops except TIME, which counts down from **1:00 to 0:00**. At zero the screen shows the heart-rate recovery status:

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1.0 | OUTSTANDING |
| 1.0 < F < 2.0 | EXCELLENT |
| 2.0 to 2.9 | GOOD |
| 3.0 to 3.9 | FAIR |
| 4.0 to 5.9 | BELOW AVERAGE |
| 6.0 | POOR |

**SB240 only:** "P" in the PULSE window means no pulse signal; "ERR" in the message window means press PULSE RECOVERY again with your hands well on the grips or the strap well attached. The FS3.0 says press RECOVERY again to return to the main display. The SB150-family test prints the grades without meanings ([card](xterra-console-pulse-recovery-grade-with-p-and-err.md)); the SB600 shows ER on a failed read ([card](../../sb600-2023/console/recovery-grade-and-the-er-reading.md)).
