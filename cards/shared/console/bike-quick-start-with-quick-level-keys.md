After the console powers up, press the **Start** key. This initiates **Quick Start** mode.

- Time counts **up** from zero.
- The workload and the resistance are adjusted by hand with the **Up or Down** buttons, **or by pressing one of the quick level keys**.
- The dot matrix display shows a track with a blinking dot indicating your progress as it travels around the track.

**The quick level keys are printed as: 1, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 21, 24, 28, 32, 36, or 40**, on the left or right side of the console. That is the manual's list exactly, including the 20 and 21 that sit next to each other.
