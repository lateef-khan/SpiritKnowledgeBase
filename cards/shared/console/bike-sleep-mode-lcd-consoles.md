The console ships with **Sleep mode**, which has two settings:

| Setting | What the manual says it does |
|---|---|
| **ON** | The unit goes to an energy-saving state after **15 minutes of inactivity**. |
| **OFF** | The console stays lit while the power is on. |

**To switch it:** on LCD displays, press and hold the **Stop, Enter and Display** keys for **5 seconds** to turn Sleep mode ON or OFF.

**The same manual states the opposite polarity a few pages later.** Item C.III of its own Maintenance Menu reads "Sleep Mode - Turn **off** to have the console power down automatically after 15 minutes of inactivity". This block says turning it **on** is what causes the 15-minute power-down. Both statements are in this one manual and they contradict each other. Try one setting and watch the console before telling a customer which way round it is.

**A third figure exists outside this manual.** The matching service manual prints 30 minutes rather than 15 for the same menu item.
