**These two owners books print the flat six-item menu, not the nested Functions menu** their own service manuals print (Key Test, Display Mode, Pause Mode, Lube Reset, Key Tone, Child Lock here versus Key Test, Display Test, Functions, Security, Exit there).

1. Press and hold **Start, Stop and Enter** and fit the safety key at the same time, until the display shows **ENGINEERING MODE** (some versions say maintenance menu).
2. Scroll with the **Speed up/down keys**; **Stop** returns to the previous selection:
   - **A. KEY TEST** - tests every key.
   - **B. DISPLAY MODE** - turn **off** to power down automatically after **30 minutes** of inactivity.
   - **C. PAUSE MODE** - **on** allows 5 minutes of pause; **off** pauses indefinitely.
   - **D. LUBE MESSAGE RESET** - resets the lube message and the odometer readings.
   - **E. KEY TONE** - turns the key beep on or off.
   - **F. CHILDLOCK** - locks the keypad; with it enabled the keypad does nothing until you press and hold **Start and Enter for 3 seconds**.
