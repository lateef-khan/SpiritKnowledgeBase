Four notes, printed the same way in both books:

1. **The battery symbol lights when the batteries are low** - replace them then ([battery card](xterra-erg-console-two-aa-batteries-and-where-they-go.md)).
2. **The console shuts off after 2 minutes** with no signal (ERG160: "2 minutes of disuse"; ERG180: "if there is no signal received after 2 minutes").
3. **It starts by itself** when input arrives from rowing (the ERG160 book says "pedaling") or from a key press.
4. **After 4 seconds without input the display stops calculating and shows "STOP"**; when you row again it resumes and the STOP disappears. The ERG180 repeats this line at the end of its Kinomap steps.

STOP is therefore a pause marker, not a fault. Neither book says whether the values survive the 2-minute shut-off; the ERG220's own note (4.5 minutes, values kept) is on [the ERG220 card](../../erg220-2023/console/auto-on-and-4-5-minute-auto-off-that-keeps-values-and-a-2-second-reset.md). The keys and readouts are on [the ERG160 card](../../erg160-2020/console/single-mode-key-with-scan-every-6-seconds-and-six-readouts.md) and [the ERG180 card](../../erg180-2023/console/mode-and-set-keys-with-scan-every-6-seconds-and-pulse-60-to-240.md).
