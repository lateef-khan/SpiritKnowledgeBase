The console prints rapid **speed** keys **2, 4, 6, 8 and 12** to adjust the speed, alongside the Fast/Slow keys. Other books in the wave print different sets: 2-3-4-5-6-9-12, 2-4-6-8-10-12, 1-2-4-6-8-10, 2-through-10 or 2-through-12.
