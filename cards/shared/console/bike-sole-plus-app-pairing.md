The machine has a Bluetooth transceiver that works with the free **SOLE+ App** from the Apple Store or Google Play, on compatible iOS and Android smartphones or tablets.

1. Download the SOLE+ App.
2. Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your device.
3. **Tap the icon at the top right corner** to search for Sole equipment.
4. When the equipment is detected, tap **Connect**.
5. When the app and equipment are synced, the **Bluetooth icon on the equipment's console lights up**.

The app can sync your workout data to **Apple Health, Google Fit, Strava, MapMyFitness or Fitbit**, with more to come.

**This is a different app and a different corner from the earlier machines.** The 2016 and 2019 bikes use the **Sole Fitness App**, search from the icon in the **top left** corner, and list **iHealth, MapMyFitness, Record or Fitbit** as the cloud sites. Do not give a customer with a 2023 machine the older instructions.

**No minimum operating system is stated for the SOLE+ App.** The older Sole Fitness App section named iOS 7 or Android 4.4; this section names none.
