Read from the console drawing in each owner's manual (the page carries no text beyond its heading):

- A small **LCD** under the XTERRA logo with speed in a large digit group and smaller Time, Distance and Calorie fields.
- A row of nine numbered **speed keys 1 through 9**. On the TR150 a green **ENTER** key ends that row; on the TRX1000 **PROGRAM** and **ENTER** sit to the left of the LCD instead.
- **STOP** (red), **PROGRAM** and **START** (green) keys, and **Speed ▼ / ▲** arrows (below the row on the TR150, right of the LCD on the TRX1000).
- The round **safety-key** pad under the keys.
- On the two side panels, twelve printed **speed program profiles**, P1-P6 on the left and P7-P12 on the right, with a bottle holder in each wing.

There is no incline key on either console: both machines set incline by hand with a pop pin under the deck. The TR150 service manual calls the numbered keys "RAPID" keys and the LCD fields SPEED, TIME, DISTANCE, CALORIES, PULSE and PROGRAM ([service-manual ranges card](../../tr150-2021/console/display-modes-and-window-ranges-per-the-service-manual.md)), though the owner's manual prints no pulse feature for the TR150.
