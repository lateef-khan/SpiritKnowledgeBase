1. With the bike connected to the app, press the **BLE HR** icon.
2. Select the heart rate tracking device you use and press **Connect**.
3. During the workout the BLE HR icon lights up and your heart rate is shown on the display.

**This does not work in HRC mode.** The manual states that using the app with the Bluetooth chest strap does not apply to the HRC (heart rate control) programs.

Connecting a strap **without** the app is a different procedure, done with the heart rate key on the keypad.
