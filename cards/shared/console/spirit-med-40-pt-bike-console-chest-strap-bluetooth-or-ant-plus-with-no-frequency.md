**"The chest strap transmitter is not a standard part, but is a separate purchase. Most transmitters
that operate at Bluetooth or ANT+ will also work."** That is the PT wording, and it is the opposite of
the 2025 Spirit Medical books for the same two bikes, which say **5 kHz**
(`spirit-med-bike-console-chest-strap-5-khz-2500-hours-and-a-cr2032`). Nothing in either edition says
the console was changed; the two books simply name different straps for the same 4.0R and 4.0U.

**The battery sentence is printed in one PT book and not the other.**

| Book | After "dry the transmitter after use to prolong battery life" |
|---|---|
| PT 4.0 R (recumbent) | "(estimated transmitter battery life is **2500 hours**). If your chest strap has a replaceable battery the replacement battery is **CR2032**." |
| PT 4.0 U (upright) | the sentence ends at "battery life." - **no hours and no cell type** |

So a PT 4.0 U owner asking for a battery has to be answered from the recumbent book or the 2025 Spirit
books, and told so.

**The 4.0T treadmill's 2026 book says Bluetooth or ANT+ too** (`40t-2026-console-chest-strap-bluetooth-ant`),
where its 2025 printing says 5 kHz. The wearing instructions are the same in every edition
(`spirit-med-bike-console-wearing-the-chest-strap-with-its-two-black-square-electrodes`).
