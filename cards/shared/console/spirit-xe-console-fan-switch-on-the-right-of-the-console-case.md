**The consoles include a built-in fan to help keep you cool. To turn the fan on, flip the switch on
the right side of the console case.**

**It is a switch you flip, not a key you press.** The XE550 console drawing labels both a **Fan** and
a separate **Fan power switch**; the XE350 drawing labels only a **Fan**.

**No speeds, no off instruction and no automatic behaviour are printed** in any of these manuals.

**The XE100 and XE200 section calls them "the E series consoles"** where the XE300 and the 2005
manual both write "the XE series consoles". Nothing about the fan differs; it is a typo in one
paragraph of one section.

**The XE150 console section never mentions a fan.** The sentence is printed only in the combined
XE350/XE550 description, so **do not tell an XE150 owner where the fan switch is** - this manual does
not say the machine has one.

**Every later residential XE moved it to a key on the left**:
`spirit-xe-console-fan-key-on-the-left-of-the-console`. The commercial CE800-2013 and CE850-2016 put
it on the front right: `spirit-ce-console-fan-key-on-the-front-right`.
