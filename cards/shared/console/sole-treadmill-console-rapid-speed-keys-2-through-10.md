The console prints rapid **speed** keys **2 through 10** to adjust the speed, alongside the Fast/Slow keys on the console or handgrip. These books top out at 10 mph. Other books in the wave print different sets: 2-3-4-5-6-9-12, 2-4-6-8-12, 2-4-6-8-10-12, 1-2-4-6-8-10 or 2-through-12.
