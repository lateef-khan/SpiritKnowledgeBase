Plug the treadmill into an appropriate wall outlet and turn on the power switch at the front of the treadmill, below the motor hood, with the safety key installed (the treadmill will not operate without it).

When the power is turned on, **a message scrolls across the dot matrix showing the current software version**. The treadmill then enters idle mode, the starting point for operation.

The books print no way to read the version later without cycling the power; the TR6.6 and TR6.4 engineering mode also shows "software versions" on entry (see [the engineering mode card](xterra-tr-console-engineering-mode-start-and-speed-up-wheel-63.md)).
