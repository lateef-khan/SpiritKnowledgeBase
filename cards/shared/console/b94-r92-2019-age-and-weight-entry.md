- **Age** is necessary during the Heart Rate control program, to ensure the correct settings are entered.
- **Weight** aids in calculating a more correct calorie reading.

**These become the new defaults.** The last Age and Weight entered are saved as the new default settings, so you do not have to enter them every workout unless they change or someone else enters different values.

**About calories:** the manual states plainly that calorie readings on every piece of exercise equipment, in a gym or at home, are not accurate and tend to vary widely. They are meant only as a guide to monitor progress from workout to workout, because every person burns calories at a different rate.

Entering age is also what lets the console calculate your maximum heart rate automatically.
