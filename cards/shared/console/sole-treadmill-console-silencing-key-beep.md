Open the maintenance menu, take **Functions**, and set **Key Tone** (2011 books: 'Turns beep sound off or on when a button is pressed'; 2013 books: 'Will turn on/off beeping noise that is made when keys are pressed'). The F60-2016 has no maintenance menu; its 'TO TURN OFF AUDIBLE BEEP' section just points at the engineering-mode page. The AF65/AF80/AS77 and all 2007-2010 menus print no beep setting.
