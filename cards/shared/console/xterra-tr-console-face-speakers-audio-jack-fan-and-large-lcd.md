The console page of all three books names the same five features with callouts:

- **Integrated speakers** for an MP3 player.
- An **audio input jack** for customized listening (a headphone-style jack at the lower left of the keypad in the drawing).
- A **turbo fan** to keep you cool, with a Fan key on the TR6.6 and TR6.4 and a fan key between the incline and speed arrows on the TR300.
- A **large LCD** with feedback and a scrolling message center.
- A convenient **water bottle holder and cargo compartment** for a phone or MP3 player in each wing.

**Keys, read from the drawings.** The TR300 has incline quick keys 9/6/3 left of the LCD and speed quick keys 9/6/3 right of it, a 24-program profile table under the LCD, Incline ▲/▼, Fan and Speed ▲/▼, then Program, Stop, the safety key, Start and Enter. The TR6.6 and TR6.4 have program keys down each side of the LCD, a row of incline quick keys (10, 8, 6, 4, 2) and speed quick keys (2, 4, 6, 8, 10), Incline ▲/▼, a **Display** key, Speed ▲/▼, then Fan, Stop, the safety key, Start and Enter. The TR6.6 LCD windows are labelled PACE, CALORIES, TIME, DISTANCE and PULSE over an incline figure, the dot matrix, a heart-rate profile bar and a speed figure, with a message line below ([dot matrix card](xterra-tr-console-dot-matrix-18-by-8-and-the-display-key.md)).

None of the three books prints a speaker wattage, a jack size or a cable; the audio jack is described only by its callout.
