**Wireless music**: turn on the phone Bluetooth, search for **Bt-speaker** (the TT8-2023 book renders it **BT-Speaker**), tap to connect; the phone plays through the machine. **Bluetooth chest strap (BLE 5.0)** works even mid-App: wear it first, then connect; the console shows the heart rate over Bluetooth. The strap **icon lights up** on a successful connection and the rate displays; the icon is **off** when disconnected.
