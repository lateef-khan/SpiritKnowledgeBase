When "Lube" appears on the LCD, check the lubrication under the walking deck and lubricate if needed (the Maintenance section holds the procedure). After application, **hold PROGRAM for 3 seconds** to reset the message; the TR260 book says to do it in standby mode.

- **TR200:** the console page and the service checklist both say PROGRAM.
- **TR260:** the console page, the lubrication procedure, the service checklist and the service manual all say PROGRAM.
- **TR65:** "When Lube appears on the LCD check lubrication under walking belt. (Use a total of 90 hours.) After application hold PROGRAM for 3 seconds to reset the message."
- **TRX1000:** the console page (p. 14) and the service checklist (p. 18) say PROGRAM, but the lubrication procedure (p. 17) says "hold the ENTER key for 3 seconds". The book does not say which is right; try PROGRAM first, then ENTER.

The books with an ENTER reset are on [the ENTER card](xterra-treadmill-console-clear-the-lube-message-by-holding-enter-3-seconds.md); the TR300, TR6.6 and TR6.4 reset it inside engineering mode ([card](xterra-tr-console-reset-lube-from-the-engineering-mode-functions-menu.md)).
