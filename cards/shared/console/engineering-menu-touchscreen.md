1. Turn the power switch on if the machine is not already on.
2. Log in to a personal account or choose the Guest profile.
3. Tap the gear icon in the top left to open Settings.
4. Tap the word "Settings" rapidly several times until the Engineering Menu appears.

Tap a selection to open it, the back arrow to go back, and the home button to leave the menu.

The usual options:

- **Engineering Mode**
  - **Units** - miles or kilometres. Can also be changed in normal settings.
  - **Max Incline** - the highest incline allowed. Do not change unless it is set wrong.
  - **Wheel Size** - the roller diameter, not the pulley. Changing it needs a recalibration and changes the belt speed. A wrong value makes the machine run at a different speed from the one on screen.
  - **Max Speed** and **Min Speed** - do not change unless set wrong.
  - **Calibration** - locks in min speed, max speed, max incline, units and wheel size.
  - **Diagnostics** - error code history, with a button to clear codes.
  - **Machine Information**
  - **Grade Return** - ON returns the incline to 0 after boot and login. Useful after a power cut.
  - **Beep Mode** - ON beeps on button press and countdown.
  - **Child Lock** - ON locks the console on boot or after inactivity. Hold START and STOP for a few seconds to unlock.
  - **Distance/Hour** - lifetime time and distance. Separate from the lube counter.
  - **Zeroing** - resets the lifetime distance and hours.
  - **SW/FW/OS Version** - installed software, firmware and OS versions.
  - **Maintenance** - for factory engineers installing apps and firmware. Nothing useful for a customer.
- **Lube Setup**
  - **Time** - hours of use before the lube message appears. 90 hours is the recommended value.
  - **Cumulative Time/Distance** - use since the lube message was last reset.
  - **Zeroing** - resets the lube counter.
- **Key Test** - each press beeps and shows the key. You have to press every button to exit.
