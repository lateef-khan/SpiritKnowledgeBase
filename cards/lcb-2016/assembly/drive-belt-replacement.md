1. Remove the **chain covers L and R (36)(37)**.
2. Use a **13 m/m wrench** to release the **M8 x 7T nut (77)** and take off the **J-Bolt (85)**. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **three M6 x 7T Phillips head screws** on the **idle arm (9)**, and the **belt (14)** can be taken off.
3. To release the idle arm (9), unscrew **three M6 x 15 Phillips head screws (56)** with **three 1/4" split washers (73)** and **three 1/4" x 13 x 1T washers (69)**.
4. To refit the belt (14) and the idle arm (9), use a Phillips head screwdriver to secure the **generator flywheel (20)** with three M6 x 15 Phillips head screws, three 1/4" split washers (73) and three 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers (69). Turn the **drive pulley (15)** to get the belt onto the pulley, hook the J-Bolt on the mainframe, and use a **13 m/m wrench** to tighten the M8 x 7T nut (77) on the J-Bolt until the belt (14) has acceptable tension. **Use an audio tension gauge to reach 180 to 205 Hz.** Finally turn the drive pulley (15) and check the drive belt (14) sits in the centre without coming off the pulley.
