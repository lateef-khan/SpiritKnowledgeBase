Hardware: **#50** 3/8" x 2-1/4" Hex Head Bolt (2 pcs), **#58** M5 x 12mm Phillips Head Screw (2 pcs), **#71** 3/8" x 25 x 2T Flat Washer (2 pcs).

1. Secure the Rear Stabilizer Cover (41) onto the Rear Stabilizer (5) with the two Phillips Head Screws (58) by using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (93).
2. Locate the Rear Stabilizer (5) **between the Rear Stabilizer Attaching Plate (131) and the Mainframe (1)** and tighten two Hex Head Bolts (50) together with the two Flat Washers (71) **from bottom upward** by using the 12/14m/m Wrench (100).

**The bolts go in from underneath.** This is the only printing of this step that says so; the B94 2013/2016 and LCB 2013 wording just says to insert the bolts through the attachment plate. The parts are the same three items in all four manuals.
