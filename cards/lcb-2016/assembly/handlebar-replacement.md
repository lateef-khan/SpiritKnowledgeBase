1. Take the **console (34)** apart.
2. Use a **12 mm wrench** to release **two 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts (51)**, **two 5/16" x 18 mm x 1.5T flat washers (72)** and **two 5/16" x 1.5T split washers (103)** holding the **handlebar assembly (3)**, then remove it.
3. Release **four 3 x 10 m/m tapping screws (65)** to remove the **handgrip side caps, top (42) and bottom (43)**.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **four 20 m/m tapping screws (62)** holding the **handpulse cable assembly (26)(28)**, separate it and remove it.
5. Reverse the steps to refit.
