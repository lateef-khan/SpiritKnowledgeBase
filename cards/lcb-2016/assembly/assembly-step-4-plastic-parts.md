Hardware: **#58** M5 x 12mm Phillips Head Screw (6 pcs).

1. Insert the Computer Cable (29) and the two Hand pulse W/Cable Assemblies (26, 26~1) onto the Console Assembly (34).
2. Secure the Console Assembly (34) onto the Console Mast (2) with the four Phillips Head Screws (58) by using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (93).
3. Secure the Front Stabilizer Cover (40) onto the Main Frame (1) with the two Phillips Head Screws (58) by using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (93).
4. Install the Pedals (L)(R) (45)(46) onto the Crank Arms (16L)(16R) by using the **13/15mm Wrench (92)**.
5. Turn on the Brake Tension Knob (86) and put the Sliding Seat Mount (7) on the Seat Slider (6).
6. Let the Brake Tension Knob (86) through the Flat Washer (71), the Seat Slider (6) and the Sliding Seat Mount (7); adjust the position and tighten the Brake Tension Knob (86) with the Fix Plate (8).
7. Install the Seat (19) on the Sliding Seat Mount (7) by using the **12/14mm Wrench (100)**.
8. Apply the **Cap Nut (78)** onto the screw of the Brake Tension Knob (86) and tighten.

**The last instruction is printed without a number** in this manual, run on after item 7. It is a real instruction, not a note.

**#78 Cap Nut is not in the assembly pack checklist.** It is named only here. The LCB 2019 and 2023 versions of the same instruction drop the part number and just say "apply the screw of the Brake Tension Knob (86) and tighten".

**This step no longer warns about the left pedal's reverse thread.** The instruction is only "install the pedals onto the crank arms using the 13/15mm Wrench (92)". The warning is printed in the 2011-2013 B94 and LCB manuals and in every R92 and LCR manual, but not here. The left pedal is still reverse-threaded; the manual just does not say so in this step.
