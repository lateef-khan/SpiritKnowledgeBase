1. Take the pedals and the crank arm apart. Separate the console mast cover and the saddle cover from the left and right shrouds, then use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **seven 3.5 x 16 m/m sheet metal screws (63)** and **three 5 x 16 m/m tapping screws (61)** holding the left shroud, and take it off.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **seven 3.5 x 12 mm self tapping screws (63)** and **three 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (61)** on the **left side case (36)** to release it. Then unscrew the **4 x 19 mm self tapping screw (64)** with the **5 x 15 x 1.5T flat washer (57)** on the main frame. Unscrew **three 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (61)** on the **right side case** to release it.
3. To refit, install the **right side case (37)** on the main frame with **one 4 x 19 mm self tapping screw (64)** and a **5 x 15 x 1.5T flat washer (57)**, left loose for now. Line the centre of the wheel up with the centre of the axle and secure with **three 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (61)**, then add one more 4 x 19 mm self tapping screw (64) with a 5 x 15 x 1.5T flat washer (57). Match the **left side case (36)** with the right side case (37) and secure with **seven 3.5 x 12 mm self tapping screws (63)** and **three 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (61)**. Follow section 4.3 to refit the cranks and pedals.

**The screw size for the left cover is printed two ways.** Step 1 says 3.5 x **16** m/m, steps 2 and 3 say 3.5 x **12** mm, both as part (63). The manual does not explain the difference.
