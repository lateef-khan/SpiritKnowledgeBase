1. Remove the **chain covers L and R (36)(37)** and the **drive belt (14)**.
2. Use C ring tweezers to remove the **20 mm C ring (67)** on the **crank arbor (11)**.
3. Use two **11 mm open end wrenches** to remove **four 1/4" x 3/4" hex head bolts (54)** together with **four 1/4" x 8T nyloc nuts (79)** on the crank arbor (11) and the **drive pulley (15)**, then take the drive pulley (15) apart.
4. Reverse the step to refit.
