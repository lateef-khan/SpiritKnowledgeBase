1. Remove the **locking knob (106)**.
2. Take the **centre spatial wrap (107)** off the **main frame (1)** with a rubber hammer and remove the **seat slider (6)**. Use the Phillips head screwdriver (93) to release **two 4 x 12 m/m sheet metal screws (68)**, then remove the **slide spacer (114)** with the rubber hammer to separate the seat slider (6) and the centre spatial wrap (107).
3. To refit, return the centre spatial wrap (107) to the seat slider (6), return the slide spacer (114) into the seat slider (6) and tighten the two 4 x 12 m/m sheet metal screws (68), then insert it into the mainframe (1). Use the rubber hammer to seat the centre spatial wrap (107) and refit the locking knob (106).
