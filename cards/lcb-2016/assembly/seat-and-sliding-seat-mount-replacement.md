1. Use a **14 mm wrench** to loosen both hex head bolts and remove the **seat (19)**.
2. Use the **14 mm wrench** to take the **brake tension knob (86)** off the **fix plate (8)** and remove the **sliding seat mount (7)**.
3. Turn to take the brake tension knob (86) off the fix plate (8) and remove the sliding seat mount (7).
4. To refit, install the sliding seat mount (7) on the **seat slider (6)**, then put the fix plate (8) on it. Install the brake tension knob (86) from the bottom upward into the fix plate (8) and tighten with the **3/8" x 7T nut (78)**. Finally insert the seat (19) into the sliding tube and tighten both hex head bolts with the 14 mm wrench.

Steps 2 and 3 repeat the same work with a different tool. Step 3 says to turn the knob by hand.
