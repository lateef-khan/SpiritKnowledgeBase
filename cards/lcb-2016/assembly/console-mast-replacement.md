1. Take the **computer cable (29)** and the **handle bar (3)** apart.
2. Press the **chain cover L (36)** so a seam opens between it and the **console mast cover (38)**, then take the console mast cover (38) off the chain cover L (36). Do the same on the right side, then lift the console mast cover (38) upward and off.
3. Use a **12 m/m wrench** to unscrew **seven 5/16" x 5/8" hex head screws (51)** with **six 5/16" x 19 mm x 1.5T flat washers (72)** and **one 5/16" x 20 split washer (99)**, then pull out the **console mast (2)**.
4. To refit, guide the computer cable (29) through the console mast cover (38) and out of the console mast (2). Fit the console mast with the 12 m/m wrench, tightening the seven hex head screws (51), six flat washers (72) and one **5/16" x 20 curved washer (99)**. Secure the console mast cover (38) onto the chain covers (36) and (37).

Part 99 is called a **split washer** in step 3 and a **curved washer** in step 4. It is the same part.
