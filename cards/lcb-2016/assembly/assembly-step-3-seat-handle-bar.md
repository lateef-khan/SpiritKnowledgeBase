Hardware: **#51** 5/16" x 5/8" Hex Head Bolt (2 pcs), **#72** 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T Flat Washer (2 pcs), **#103** 5/16" x 1.5T Split Washer (2 pcs).

1. Secure the Seat Handle Bar (3) onto the Console Mast (2) with the two Hex Head Bolts (51), two Flat Washers (72) and two Split Washers (103) by using the **12/14mm Wrench (100)**.
2. Let the two Hand pulse W/Cable Assemblies (26) through the front hole on the Console Mast (2) and outside.

**Bolt first, then feed the cables** - this is the reverse of the order printed in the LCB 2011/2013 and the B94 manuals, which route the cables before bolting the bar on. The parts are the same three items.

The LCB 2019 replaces the two #51 hex head bolts with **four #74 button head socket bolts** driven with the Combination M5 Allen Wrench (76), so do not carry this hardware forward a year.
