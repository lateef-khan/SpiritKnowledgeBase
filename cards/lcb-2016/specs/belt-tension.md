**180 to 205 Hz, read with an audio (sound wave) tension gauge.** This machine is set by frequency, not by Newtons.

Set it by tightening the **M8 x 7T nyloc nut (77)** on the **J-Bolt** with a **13 mm wrench** until the gauge reads 180 to 205 Hz.

The manual prints the same figure in both places, section 9-8 step 4 and the belt slipping answer in section 9-12.

The B94 and R92 bikes use 450 N instead. Do not carry that to this machine.
