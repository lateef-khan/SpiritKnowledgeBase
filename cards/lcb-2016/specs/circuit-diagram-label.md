The circuit diagram page is titled "**ELLIPICAL CIRCUIT DIAGRAM**", and the closing note says "this manual can not cover all of elliptical problems".

**The manual is for a bike.** Every other page names the bike, the console section calls it a bike, and the parts are bike parts. The elliptical wording is carried over from a Dyaco elliptical manual and is a labelling error, not a sign of the wrong document.

The LCB 2019 and LCR 2016 manuals carry the same wrong heading.
