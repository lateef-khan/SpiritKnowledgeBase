1. Attach the Safety Key to enable the display (if not already on).
2. Press the **Start/Stop** key to begin belt movement. Adjust the speed with the speed ▲/▼ keys (console or handgrip), or press one of the **Speed Keys 1 – 10**.
3. To adjust the deck incline, press the incline ▲/▼ key (console or handgrip), or press one of the **Incline Keys 0 – 12**.
4. To stop, press the Start/Stop key or pull out the Safety Key — the book encourages the Start/Stop key.

**Incline.** It may be adjusted any time after the belt begins to move; press and hold the incline ▲/▼ keys on the left side (console or handgrip), or select an Incline Key 0 – 12 on the left of the console. The display indicates the incline position as adjustments are made.

**What is on the console.** The pictured face has ten speed keys (1 through 10) and ten incline keys reading 12, 10, 8, 6, 5 and 4, 3, 2, 1, 0. The console badge reads "0-12 mph speed range" and "0-12% incline range". The shared TRX3500/TRX4500 service manual also gives the Up key a maximum incline position of 12 ([service-manual card](../../shared/console/xterra-trx-console-keys-in-ready-and-run-mode-per-the-service-manual.md)).
