**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the warranty page of this book, under the heading *Effective June 07, 2024 ... LIMITED WARRANTY*. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**The TRX3500 and TRX4500 share one service manual and one owner's-manual text, and their warranty tables differ in one cell**: the TRX3500 deck is 2 years, the TRX4500 deck 5 years (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-periods-frame-motor-lifetime-deck-5-years-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`). Everything else on the page - the seven consumer duties, the nine exclusions, the six service steps, the June 07, 2024 effective date - is word for word the TRX2500 and TRX4500 page.
