This is the over-the-air firmware fix for the F63 freeze issue. It is done from a phone over Bluetooth.

1. Power the machine off and on. **Only continue when the console shows "OTA".**
2. In the app, tap **Add Device** or **+**.
3. Select **Pair via Bluetooth**.
4. On the Pair Device screen, press and hold the centre for 5 seconds. The app then scans for **AnplusOTA**.
5. If no AnplusOTA device is found, go back to step 1 and start again.
6. If a device is found, the app goes straight to the **Firmware Update** screen. Tap **Update**.
7. **Stay on this screen until it finishes.** Do not leave the screen. Keep the phone near the machine. Wait for 100%.
8. After a successful update the machine restarts. Then power it off and on again.

If it fails, go back to step 1 and start again.
