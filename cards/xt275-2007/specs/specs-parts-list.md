These are the callout numbers on the **XT275 Exploded View** on PDF page 20, a drawing printed sideways on a portrait page and stamped **960705PM1515** in small type at its edge. The drawing is a picture with bare numbers on it, so this table is the only readable form of it. It was read from the native text layer of the 2008 dealer service manual; every part number on the pages the 300 dpi OCR sweep supplemented agrees with that layer, and pages 18 and 26 were also read from a render.

**Three columns, no quantity.** The list prints `No.`, `Description` and `Spirit Part #` only - it never says how many of an item the machine uses. A part number is a six-digit Spirit number. **`X` in the part-number column is printed on the page**; the book does not say what it means, and 11 rows carry it (8, 106, 107, 108, 109, 110, 111, 112, 113, 114, 136). 5 numbered rows carry no part number at all (1, 19, 39~5, 128, 138).

**135 rows are printed.** The whole item numbers that appear are 1-30, 32-34, 36-40, 42-54, 57-67, 70-102, 106-116, 119-128, 135-140. Any number outside that run - 31, 35, 41, 55-56, 68-69, 103-105, 117-118, 129-134 - is not printed for this machine at all, not a missing page. Sub-items are written with a tilde (`27~1`, `39~1`); paired parts carry a letter (`20L`, `20R`).

**Do not read this list against the XT175 list, even though the two share one drawing.** The XT175 exploded view carries the same stamp, 960705PM1515, and the same item numbering, but 23 of the shared item numbers carry a different part number, a different description or a blank on one list and a number on the other - the running deck, running belt, motor and console among them. Rows only on this list: kit. Rows only on the XT175 list: 39~10, 39~14, 39~15, 39~16, 39~17. The row-by-row comparison is printed once, on `xt175-2007-specs-parts-list`.

**These numbers belong to the 2007 model year only.** The XT185, XT285, XT385, XT485 and XT685 of 2015 have no printed parts list anywhere (`xt-2015-specs-no-parts-list`), and the 2023 XT lists are numbered per manual with no Spirit part-number column - the XT185-2023 Drive Motor is item 027, the XT385/XT485-2023 Drive Motor is 34. Nothing establishes that any number here fits a later machine, or that a later item number fits this one.

**What this list prints that the XT175 list does not.** A `Hardware Kit 000389` row at the very end, with no item number, and part numbers for the Frame Base (007093) and Incline Bracket (007094) where the XT175 list leaves both blank. It has no 39~14 to 39~17 speaker, amplifier and washer rows. Items 87 and 89 are both `5x16m/m_Tapping Screw 010110`, as on the XT175. Item 46 is printed `%30x60_Square End Cap` on the page.

**Tools are items in the list.** Item 97 is the combination M5 Allen wrench and Phillips screwdriver (000072), item 98 the M6 combination (030029) and item 59 the lubricant (022271).
