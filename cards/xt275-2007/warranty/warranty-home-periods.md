**The XT275 is the only 2007 XT whose electronics, belt and rollers are covered for four years** - longer than the XT175's two and shorter than the five the XT375, XT475 and XT675 get. The table is headed **XT275 (Home Warranty)**:

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| Drive motor and Frame | Lifetime |
| Electronics/belt/rollers | 4 Years |
| Deck | Lifetime |
| Labor | 1 Year |

The page spells rollers `rolers` here, as in the XT175 row.

The XT175 table (`xt175-2007-warranty-home-periods`) has the same four rows with 2 Years and a 4-Year deck; the three larger machines add an *All other components 10 Years* row this table does not have (`spirit-xt-2007-warranty-home-periods-xt375-xt475-xt675`). No commercial column is printed for the XT275. The base term under every table - 5 years from retail sale or 18 months from factory shipping, whichever comes first - is on `spirit-xt-2007-warranty-five-years-or-eighteen-months-from-shipping`.

**Do not answer this from a 2015 or 2023 XT285 table**; those print Frame / Motor / Deck / Parts / Labor with Parts 10 Years and no electronics row (`xt-2023-warranty-residential-coverage-periods`).
