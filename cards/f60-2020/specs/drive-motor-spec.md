A **variable speed DC motor**: the main controller feeds it **0 to 180 volts on a 230VAC system, or 0 to 90 volts on a 120VAC system**, to raise or lower the speed of the running belt. The higher the voltage, the faster the motor turns.

**Three wires: red, black and green.**

- DC voltage on the **red (white)** wire (**M+**) turns the motor **clockwise**.
- DC voltage on the **black** wire (**M-**) turns the motor **counter-clockwise**.
- The **green** wire is ground.

The main controller carries the DC power supply for the console, the incline driver and the DC motor driver.
