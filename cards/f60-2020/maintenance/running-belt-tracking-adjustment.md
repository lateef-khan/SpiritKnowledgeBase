Performance depends on the frame standing on a reasonably level surface. If the frame is not level the front and back roller cannot run parallel, and the belt will need constant adjustment.

It is normal for a belt to drift towards one side while running with nobody on it, and after a few minutes of use it should tend to centre itself. Adjust only if the belt keeps moving to one side **during use**.

1. A **6 mm Allen wrench (item 97)** is provided for this adjustment. **Make tracking adjustments on the left side bolt.**
2. Set belt speed at **3 mph**.
3. If the belt is **too close to the left side**, turn the bolt **1/4 turn to the right (clockwise)** and wait a few minutes for the belt to adjust itself.
4. Keep making 1/4 turns until the belt is stable and its edge is **within the range marked on the motor hood**. If the belt is **too close to the right side**, turn the bolt **counter-clockwise**.

A small adjustment makes a dramatic difference, which may not be apparent right away. Belt damage from improper tracking or tension adjustment **is not covered under the warranty**.
