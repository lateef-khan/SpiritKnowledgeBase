**CAUTION: do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and locked position.** Remove the power cord from the front of the unit to avoid damage, and use both handrails to move the unit.

**To fold**: lift the deck until the **latch clicks in place**.

**To unfold**: press the tube with your foot **at the yellow sticker** to release the latch.

**Transport**: the treadmill has four transport wheels that engage when it is folded. After folding, simply roll it away.
