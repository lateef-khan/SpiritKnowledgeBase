**Adjustment must be made from the rear roller.** The adjustment bolts are at the end of the step rails, inside the end caps, reached through a small hole in the end cap.

1. Tighten the rear roller bolts **only enough to stop slippage at the front roller**.
2. Turn **both** tread-belt tension adjustment bolts in increments of **1/4 turn each**.
3. Inspect the tension by walking on the belt at a low speed and making sure the belt does not slip.
4. Keep tensioning until the belt stops slipping.

If the belt feels tight enough but still slips, the problem may be a **loose motor drive belt under the front cover**.

**DO NOT OVERTIGHTEN.** Over-tightening causes belt damage and premature bearing failure. Damage to the running belt caused by improper tracking or tension adjustment **is not covered under the warranty**.
