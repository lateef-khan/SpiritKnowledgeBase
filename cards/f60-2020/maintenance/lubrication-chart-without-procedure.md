The F60-2020 book demands lubrication in three places but never says how. Its chart calls for **Lubricate Deck (Every 90 hours or sooner if dry)**, the parts list ships **#104 Lubricant**, and the console chapter says: when `Lube' appears on the LCD, check lubrication under the walking deck **(use a total of 90 hours)**. The General Maintenance chapter - belt and deck wipe, belt dust, general cleaning, tension, tracking, engineering mode - contains no lubrication procedure and no reset procedure.

Do not substitute a sibling book's S-pattern procedure for this machine: no book prints one for it. The linked 90-hour card is what the sibling stencil books print, not this one.
