**Treadmill warranty, effective February 1, 2020.** Read off the page render (PDF p. 27, printed 27), not just the OCR extraction — the 1-year cosmetic row is the surprise.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Drive Motor** | **Lifetime** |
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Cosmetic Items** * | **1 Year** |
| **Deck** | **1 Year** |
| **Labor** | **1 Year** |
| **All Other Components** | **1 Year** |

*Cosmetic items including, but not limited to the following: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.

The 1-year cosmetic row is unique in this wave — every other stencil book prints 90 days. The 1-year deck and parts are the shortest non-TT8/ST90 terms here; the F63 books of the same era carry 3-year deck and parts (see `sole-treadmill-warranty-periods-lifetime-motor-3-year-deck-1-year-labor`). Duties, exclusions, service and registration are the ordinary 7-item/9-item/serviceparts set — see `sole-treadmill-warranty-responsibilities-7-item-household`, `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-9-item-residential` and `sole-treadmill-warranty-service-online-request-steps`.
