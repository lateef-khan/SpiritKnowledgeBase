The F60-2020 console is its own design: press **PROGRAM** to scroll **MANUAL, P1-P24** (with **FATBURN-P9, CARDIO-P12, STRENGTH-P17, HIIT-P22** named on the key list), **USER1-USER2**, **HR1-HR2**. The 24 profiles are drawn bar pictures (SPEED/INCLINE, 20 segments, levels 1-10), approximate by design with no numeric rebuild.

Preset setup: **STEP 1** Program button scrolls from Manual, Enter selects. **STEP 2** time defaults **30 minutes** (SPEED keys adjust). **STEP 3** START begins. **STEP 4** SPEED/INCLINE stay adjustable mid-program (keys or rapid keys). **STEP 5** STOP stops (safety key shuts down).

Countdown training (idle mode, ENTER key): **TIME** — 1x ENTER, time window flashes, default **30:00**, range **5-99 minutes**, belt starts at 0.5 mph, auto-stops at 00:00. **DISTANCE** — 2x ENTER, default **5.0**, range **1.0-9.0**, auto-stops at 0.0. **CALORIES** — 3x ENTER, default **50 CAL**, range **10-990 CAL**, auto-stops at 0. Quick Start runs 0.5 mph counting up with manual keys. Figures render-verified.
