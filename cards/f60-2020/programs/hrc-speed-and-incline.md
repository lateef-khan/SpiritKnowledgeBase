**STEP 1:** Program button to **HR1 (preset (220-age*60%))** or **HR2 (preset (220-age)*80%)**, Enter. **HRC1 adjusts the speed** to hold the target; **HRC2 adjusts the incline** to hold it. **STEP 2:** SPEED keys adjust age, Enter. **STEP 3:** SPEED keys adjust the target heart rate, ENTER. **STEP 4:** time defaults **30:00**, range **5:00-99:00**. **STEP 5:** START executes. **STEP 6:** STOP stops (safety key shuts down).

**Note: Handpulse sensor to be used. If no heart rate detected the program will stop.** Below target, incline or speed rises; at target nothing changes; above target they fall. The handpulse-only sensing (no chest-strap mention) is unique to this book.
