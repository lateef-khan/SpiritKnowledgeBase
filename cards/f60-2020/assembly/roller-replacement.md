1. With a Phillips head screwdriver, loosen the **2 screws** on the rear adjustment base.
2. With an **M6 L Allen wrench**, loosen the **2 screws** on the rear roller.
3. Remove the motor cover. With a **14mm T-shaped socket wrench** loosen the **4 screws**, and with a **14mm open end wrench** loosen the **1 belt tension screw** and loosen the drive belt.
4. With a **13mm wrench** loosen the front roller screws, remove the front and back rollers and fit the new ones. Reassemble in the reverse order.
5. Once reassembled, the running belt tension needs to be adjusted and the belt centred. The drive belt tension also needs setting, to **70 to 75 LBS** in the white area, as in the motor replacement steps.
