Hardware supplied in the box with the Sole F60-2020, grouped by assembly step; the assembly ends at step 4, so the kit has only steps 2 and 3 plus a step-4 bolt row. (The cover SKU 560820 is named "Sole F60 2019" in the model database, but no f60-2019 machine id exists; this card stands on f60-2020.) Sizes read off the page rendering.

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | #100 | Dia 5/16" x Dia 18 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 8 pcs |
| 2 | #99 | 5/16" x UNC18 x 1/2" | Hex Head Bolt | 8 pcs |
| 3 | #80 | Dia 8 x 1.5T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #125 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 4 | #125 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |

Tools and extras in the box: #44 Square Safety Key, #104 Lubricant, #103 M6 L Allen Wrench, #102 M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench, #90 13 mm Wrench.
