Four assembly steps for the F60-2020 (hardware item numbers in brackets). Unlike every other A2 treadmill book there are no mast-cover, support-tube/beverage-holder, safety-key or tablet steps: assembly ends with the handrail support, and the safety key (#44) only appears in the tools list.

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill from the carton onto a smooth, leveled floor with the rear at least 3 ft from any wall. Remove the plastic wrap and Styrofoam from under the unit before removing the Velcro belt; tilting it sideways with the belt off can let it fold and bounce upwards.
2. Connect Computer Cable Middle (54) to Computer Cable Lower (55), insert the right and left uprights (4) and (5) into the frame base (2), and tighten eight Hex Head Bolts (99) with eight Flat Washers (100) using the 13 mm wrench (90). Avoid pinching wires; do not pinch the cables in the tubing.
3. Connect Computer Cable Middle (54) to Computer Cable Upper (53). Insert the Console Assembly (21) into the uprights (4) and (5) and secure with four Button Head Socket Bolts (125) and four Split Washers (80), using the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screwdriver (102). Avoid pinching wires; do not pinch the cables.
4. Install the Handrail Support (9) between the left and right uprights (5) and (4) and tighten four Button Head Socket Bolts (125) with the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screwdriver (102).
