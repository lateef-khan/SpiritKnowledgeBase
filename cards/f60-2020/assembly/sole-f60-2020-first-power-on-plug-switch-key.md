First power-on, the last step of assembly, in the F60 2020 book. Unlike the F60 2016 book (see the linked card), this book prints the plug-and-switch sentence but describes no boot display at all: no version message, no odometer, no idle-mode sentence.

Power the treadmill on by plugging it into an appropriate wall outlet, then turn on the power switch located at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood. Ensure that the Safety Key is installed, as the treadmill will not power on without it.

The book continues straight to the LUBE MESSAGE (lubrication reminder), which is maintenance-section matter.
