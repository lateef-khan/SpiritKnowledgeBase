**Zero the incline motor before you take it off the machine, and before you fit it to the frame. The zeroing distance is 195 mm.**

1. Remove the motor cover and adjust the treadmill to the folded position, then remove the old incline motor.
2. **The incline range must be adjusted to 195 mm minimum prior to installation.**
3. Use a **14 mm wrench** to fit the new incline motor.
4. Connect the incline motor wiring to the controller:
   - **Red** wire to **UP**
   - **White** wire to **COM**
   - **Black** wire to **DOWN**

Section 11 of the manual repeats the same 195 mm figure.
