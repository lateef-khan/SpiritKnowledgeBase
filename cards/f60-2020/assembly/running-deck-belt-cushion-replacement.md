**Take the cylinder off before you start.**

1. With the running board folded, use an **M5 L Allen wrench and a 12 mm wrench** to remove the securing screws on the cylinder, and remove the cylinder.
2. Follow the front and rear roller procedure and remove the front and rear rollers.
3. With a Phillips head screwdriver, remove the **4 tapping screws** from the front and back of the foot rail, following the indicating arrows.
4. With a Phillips head screwdriver, remove the **8 screws** securing the running board. Remove the running board and replace the running board or the running belt. **If the cushions need replacing, remove the 6 cushions and replace them.** Reassemble in the reverse order.
5. Once reassembled, centre the running belt and adjust its tension. The drive belt tension also needs setting, to **70 to 75 LBS** in the white area.
