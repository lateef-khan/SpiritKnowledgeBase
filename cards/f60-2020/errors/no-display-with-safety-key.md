| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The ON/OFF switch has not been switched on | Switch the AC switch on |
| The safety key is in the wrong position | Insert the safety key in the right position |
| The **12 PIN** computer connector is not plugged in properly | Check the wire and connect it again |
| The **12 PIN** computer cable is broken | Replace the **5-PIN** computer cable |
| The fuse on the controller is blown | Replace the fuse or the controller |
| The controller is burned down by the varistor | Replace the varistor or the controller |
| The safety equipment is broken (open) | Replace the safety key device |
| Other components are faulty | Replace the console |

**Note on the printed table**: this manual's cause column names a **12 PIN** computer cable while its solution column says to replace a **5-PIN** computer cable, and the rest of the manual describes the console link as a 5-pin main control wire. Check the connector on the machine before ordering a cable.

The reverse fault, where the treadmill displays or operates with no safety key fitted, means the safety device is shorted. Replace the safety key device or the console.

**A dim, incomplete or imperfect LCD** is a different fault: a broken LCD light, or too little power to the console. Replace the LCD or console, check the AC power is 120V (230V), check the power to the console, and replace the lower controller.
