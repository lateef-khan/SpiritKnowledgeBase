**When you press START and the treadmill does not start**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The motor M+ or M- wire isn't connected in the right position | Check and plug it in again |
| The motor is broken | Replace the motor, or check the wire and connector if it was broken |
| The treadmill controller shut down, and its LED would be ON | Turn the AC switch off and turn the power on again |

**When the treadmill stops or shuts off by itself**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The house breaker tripped | Reset it |
| The treadmill chopper tripped | Reset the treadmill breaker |
| The treadmill controller fuse is broken | Replace it with a new fuse |
| The treadmill controller shut down, and its LED would be ON | Turn the AC switch off and turn the power on again |

**When the treadmill stops immediately after START is pressed**: the controller is broken. Turn the AC switch off and on again; then replace the controller and calibrate it.

**When the safety key is removed and the treadmill does not stop**: the safety key device is broken. Replace it.
