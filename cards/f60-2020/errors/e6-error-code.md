**This is E6, not E5 (communication) and not E2 (over current).**

**Definition**: a lower controller component is at fault, such as a transistor, an IGBT or a control module.

| Part | Troubleshooting |
|---|---|
| Lower controller | Insert power wire of motor. |
| Display board | Only replace the upper control board. |

**Note on the printed table**: the Lower controller row of this manual's E6 table reads "Insert power wire of motor", which is the wording of the E4 table. The E6 cause given above is a failed controller component.
