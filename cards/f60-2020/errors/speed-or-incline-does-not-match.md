| Condition | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| The speed of the belt doesn't match the console display | The console is not calibrated | Calibrate the console |
| The incline position doesn't match the console | The console is not calibrated | Calibrate the console |
| INCLINE ER, and the incline window displays "E3" | The position sensor value of the incline motor is wrong | Turn the AC switch off and turn the power on again. Then calibrate the monitor |

**Keys that do not work** are a separate fault. If the speed or incline adjustment switch does not work, or only one direction works, the manual blames a badly connected or damaged SPEED CABLE (UPPER) or INCLINE CABLE (UPPER), a badly connected switch cable, or a broken button. Reconnect the cables, replace the cable, or replace the buttons.
