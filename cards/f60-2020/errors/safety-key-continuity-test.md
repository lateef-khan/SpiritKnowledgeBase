1. Put the safety key in position.
2. Set the multi-meter to the **buzzer setting of the Ω gear**.
3. Place the **red probe on one safety key device pin** and the **black probe on the other** safety key pin.
4. **If the meter shows 0.00 or more, the safety key device is normal.**
5. **If the meter shows OL, the safety key device is bad.** Replace the safety key device, then check again.
