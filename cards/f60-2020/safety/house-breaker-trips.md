Some circuit breakers used in homes are not rated for the **high inrush currents** that occur when a treadmill is first turned on, or even during use.

If the treadmill trips the house circuit breaker even though the breaker is the proper current rating, **but the circuit breaker on the treadmill itself does not trip**, replace the home breaker with a **high inrush type**.

**This is not a warranty defect.** It is a condition the manufacturer has no ability to control. The part is available through most electrical supply stores. The manual names **Grainger part # 1D237**, or **www.squared.com part # QO120HM**.
