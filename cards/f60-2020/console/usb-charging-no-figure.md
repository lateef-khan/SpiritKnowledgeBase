**No volt/amp figure is printed**, unlike the 0.5 A and 1.0 A books. Charge a personal device from the on-console **USB Power Port** with the machine on: connect a compatible cable (not included) and check the device icon. The port meets USB 2.0 rules; the icon may stay off on power-hungry devices that still charge slowly. **No workout data can be saved through this port; charging only.**
