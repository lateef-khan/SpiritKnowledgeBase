1. Remove the safety key, and reinsert it after **pressing and holding the PROGRAM button for 3 seconds** to enter engineering mode.
2. Press the **ENTER** button once to select each item in turn:
   - **miles or km** - 0 is miles, 1 is km
   - **roller diameter** - 60
   - **speed** - 1.0 to 18 kph, or 0.5 to 12 mph
   - **incline** - 12
3. Press the **speed ▲ / ▼** button to change the value. Press **ENTER** to finish.
4. Press the **START** button. The treadmill calibrates itself and leaves engineering mode when the operation is completed.
