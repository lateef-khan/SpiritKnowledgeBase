In READY MODE:

- **SAFETY KEY** - fit it in the right position to power on the console. Pull it away and the computer shuts down automatically.
- **START/STOP** - one button starts and stops the treadmill. On the first press of START there is a **3 second countdown** on the window display, then the machine starts running in the Manual (normal) program. In a preset program it starts at the default speed. **A second press pauses it, a third press starts it again, and that cycle repeats.**
- **ENTER** - sets preset program item values, such as count-down time, count-down distance and count-down calories. It is also the confirm key for other settings.
- **PROGRAM** - in standby, opens the preset programs. Choose any of **P1~P24**, **HRC** or **USER MODE**.
- **FAST**, **SLOW**, **UP**, **DOWN** - non-functional unless the user has entered a setting, or while the treadmill is not working.
- **SPEED RAPID** and **INCLINE RAPID** - non-functional in this mode.
- **FAN** - turns the fan on and off.
