**No headphone jack and no patch cable are printed**, unlike the other LCD books. There is an **Audio Input Jack** on the front of the console with **built-in speakers**: plug any low-level source in - MP3 player, iPod, portable radio, CD player, even a TV or computer. For wireless music, open the phone/tablet Bluetooth device list, search for **BT-Speaker**, and connect; the phone then plays through the machine.
