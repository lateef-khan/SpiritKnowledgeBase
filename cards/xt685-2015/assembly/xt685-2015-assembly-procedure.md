Numbers in brackets are item numbers from the assembly drawing. Gather the
hardware for each step before you start that step. **There are no speed nut clips
on this machine** - the uprights bolt straight to the main frame and are braced
with gussets.

**Step one - uprights and gussets.**

1. Slide the Upright Covers (L 52, R 53) onto the Left and Right Uprights (4, 5).
2. Slide the Console Mast Side Caps (L 98, R 99) onto the Left and Right Uprights
   (4, 5) **under the Upright Covers**.
3. Connect the Lower Computer Cable (33) to the Middle Computer Cable (32). Be
   sure not to pinch the cable or damage may occur.
4. Insert the Left and Right Uprights into the Main Frame (1) and use the
   Combination Wrench and Phillips Screw Driver (103) to secure **4 Bolts (100)**
   and **4 Curved Washers (124)** into place.
5. Use the L-Shaped Allen Wrench (104) to tighten the Left and Right Gusset
   (7, 8) with **6 Bolts (123)**, **6 Flat Washers (79)** and **6 Spring Washers
   (86)**.

**Step two - console mast side caps.** Install the Console Mast Side Cap
(L 98, R 99) onto the Main Frame (1) and secure it using **4 Screws (127)** with
the Combination Wrench and Phillips Screw Driver (103).

**Step three - console.**

1. Connect the Speed Adjustment Switch w/ Cable (37) with the Upper Speed Cable
   (34).
2. Connect the Incline Adjustment Switch w/ Cable (38) with the Upper Incline
   Cable (35).
3. Connect the Middle Computer Cable (32) to the Upper Computer Cable (31).
4. Be sure not to pinch any of the cables or damage may occur to the system.
5. Insert the Console Assembly (36) onto the Right and Left Uprights (4, 5) and
   secure with **4 Button Head Socket Bolts (91)** and **4 Split Washers (86)**.
   Use the Allen Wrench (107) to tighten.

**Step four - console mast covers.** Slide Console Mast Cover Right and Left
(53, 52) up the uprights. Secure them with **8 Sheet Metal Screws (125)** using
the Phillips Head Screw Driver (126).

**Step five - handrail support and beverage holder.** Place the Handrail Support
(3) between the Left and Right Uprights (5, 4) and use the Combination Allen
Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (103) to tighten **4 Button Head Socket Bolts
(100)**. Then place the Beverage Holder (120) onto the Handrail Support (3).

Four naming points in the printed pages:

- Items **52 and 53** are called **Upright Covers** in step one and **Console
  Mast Cover** in step four. One pair of parts, two names.
- Item **86** is a **Spring Washer** in step one and a **Split Washer** in step
  three. One part.
- Item **100** is used twice - 4 pcs on the uprights in step one and 4 pcs on the
  handrail support in step five.
- Step three names the tool **Allen Wrench (107)**. The tool panel of this manual
  numbers the L Allen Wrench 104 and does not list a 107.

**The 2015 machine is not the 2023 one, and item 37 is the trap.** Here item 37
is the Speed Adjustment Switch w/ Cable; on the XT685-2023 item 37 is the Console
Assembly (Top). The 2023 machine also starts by unbolting shipping Fixing Plate
Stabilizers (101) with 4 flat head socket bolts (102), which this manual never
mentions, and has a two-piece console and a tablet holder that this one does not.
Its item 91 is described 3/8" x 1-1/2"; here it is **3/8" x 1-3/4"**.
