This manual prints no single hardware panel. Each step page carries its own
table; put together they read:

| Step | Item | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 100 | Bolts | 5/16" x 3/4" | 4 |
| 1 | 124 | Curved Washers | Ø8 x 23 x 1.5T | 4 |
| 1 | 123 | Bolts | 3/8" x 2-1/2" | 6 |
| 1 | 79 | Flat Washer | Ø3/8" x Ø19 x 1.5T | 6 |
| 1 | 86 | Spring Washer | Ø10 x 2.0T | 6 |
| 2 | 127 | Phillips Head Screws | M5 x 10MM | 4 |
| 3 | 91 | Bolts | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | 4 |
| 3 | 86 | Split Washer | Ø10 x 2.0T | 4 |
| 4 | 125 | Sheet Metal Screws | 3.5 x 16MM | 8 |
| 5 | 100 | Bolts | 5/16" x 3/4" | 4 |

Two item numbers appear twice because the same fastener is used in two steps:
**item 100** (4 pcs on the uprights in step one, 4 pcs on the handrail support in
step five) and **item 86** (6 pcs on the gussets in step one, 4 pcs on the
console in step three). The step-one table calls item 86 a **Spring Washer** and
the step-three table a **Split Washer**; it is one part.

**This machine has no speed nut clips.** Item 132 on the XT185-2015 and item 128
on the XT385-2015 are M5 nut clips; nothing of the kind is in this kit.

Against the 2023 XT685 kit, most lines carry over unchanged - **100, 124, 123, 79
and 86 in step one, 127 and 125 in the covers step, and 100 again for the handrail
support**. Two lines do not:

- Item **91** is described here as **3/8" x 1-3/4"**. The 2023 kit describes item
  91 as 3/8" x 1-1/2". The manuals disagree; neither says which is correct.
- The 2023 kit adds item 165 (M5 x 45L) for the two-piece console and item 166
  (M5 x 25L) for the tablet holder, and calls for removing 4 flat head socket
  bolts (102) that hold shipping stabilizers. This machine has none of those.
