Printed carton list:

- 1 Main Frame
- 2 Uprights
- 2 Console Mast Covers
- 2 Frame Base Covers
- 1 Console
- 1 Deck
- 1 Audio Cable
- 1 Beverage Holder
- 1 Power Cord
- 1 Hardware Kit

**No lubricant is on this list.** The XT185-2015, XT285-2015, XT385-2015 and
XT485-2015 lists all end "1 Power Cord / 1 Lubricant / 1 Hardware Kit"; this one
goes straight from Power Cord to Hardware Kit. The 2023 XT685 list does name a
lubricant. The manual does not say whether the omission is deliberate, so do not
promise an owner that a bottle is in the box.

**Two Frame Base Covers are on the list but no assembly step fits any.** The five
printed steps fit Upright Covers (52, 53) and Console Mast Side Caps (98, 99)
instead. The 2023 XT685 manual repeats the same unused line.
