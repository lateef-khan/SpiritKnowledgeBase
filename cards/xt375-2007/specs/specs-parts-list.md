These are the callout numbers on the **XT375 Exploded View** on PDF page 24, a drawing printed sideways on a portrait page and stamped **950821PM1345** in small type at its edge. The drawing is a picture with bare numbers on it, so this table is the only readable form of it. It was read from the native text layer of the 2008 dealer service manual; every part number on the pages the 300 dpi OCR sweep supplemented agrees with that layer, and pages 18 and 26 were also read from a render.

**Three columns, no quantity.** The list prints `No.`, `Description` and `Spirit Part #` only - it never says how many of an item the machine uses. A part number is a six-digit Spirit number. **`X` in the part-number column is printed on the page**; the book does not say what it means, and 9 rows carry it (9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 52~29, 159). 9 numbered rows carry no part number at all (1, 3, 6, 7A, 7B, 8, 13, 39~1, 142).

**167 rows are printed.** The whole item numbers that appear are 1-78, 81-120, 122-131, 133-135, 137-142, 144-148, 159-162. Any number outside that run - 79-80, 121, 132, 136, 143, 149-158 - is not printed for this machine at all, not a missing page. Sub-items are written with a tilde (`36~1`, `39~1`); paired parts carry a letter (`7A`, `7B`).

**Do not read this list against the XT475 list, even though the two share one drawing.** The XT475 exploded view carries the same stamp, 950821PM1345, and the same item numbering, but 29 of the shared item numbers carry a different part number, a different description or a blank on one list and a number on the other. Rows only on this list: 36~1. Rows only on the XT475 list: none. The full comparison, kept as printed on each page:

**These numbers belong to the 2007 model year only.** The XT185, XT285, XT385, XT485 and XT685 of 2015 have no printed parts list anywhere (`xt-2015-specs-no-parts-list`), and the 2023 XT lists are numbered per manual with no Spirit part-number column - the XT185-2023 Drive Motor is item 027, the XT385/XT485-2023 Drive Motor is 34. Nothing establishes that any number here fits a later machine, or that a later item number fits this one.

**Numbers that look wrong on the page, kept as printed.** Item 52~3, the Face Plate Lens Cover, is printed **`00758`** - five digits, where every other number in the book has six and the XT475 and XT675 lists give the same cover as `007058`. Item 119, the 3.5x12mm self-tapping screw, is printed **`010013`** where the XT175, XT275, XT475 and XT675 lists all give that screw as `010113`. Item 92, the 5/16" x 1" button head socket screw, is `008113` here and `000125` on the XT175, XT275 and XT475 lists (the XT675 list has no such screw). Item 51, Ground Wire, is `000555` here and `005013` on the XT475 - and `005013` is also this list's own item 160, the Console Ground Wire. Do not "correct" any of these when ordering: quote the number as printed and say which list it came from. Items 26 and 62 are printed `~10 Magnet` and `~38 Button Head End Cap` - the `~` stands where a diameter sign failed to print.

**The handgrip side caps are numbered differently from the XT475.** Here 68 (LR) is 007012, 69 (RL) is 007013 and 70 (RR) is 007011; the XT475 list gives 007011, 007012 and 007013 for the same three positions. One of the two books has the caps in the wrong order, and nothing on the page says which.

**Item 34's motor row carries a second number.** It reads `Motor 3.0HP (YC) ( Plastic Fan only #007102) 000602` - 000602 is the motor and 007102 is the plastic fan on its own.

**Tools are items in the list.** Item 141 is the combination M5 Allen wrench and Phillips screwdriver (000072), item 142 an Allen wrench with no part number, and item 30 the lubricant (022271). Item 36~1 is a transformer for the controller (022791) that the XT475 list does not have.
