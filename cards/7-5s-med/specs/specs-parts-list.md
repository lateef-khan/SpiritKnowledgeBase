These are the callout numbers on the exploded view drawing (PDF p. 46). The drawing is a
picture with bare numbers on it and no text, so the table below is the only readable form of
it. **257 rows are printed and the item numbers run 1 to 263** with 25 gaps: 19, 30, 59, 78, 85,
90, 99, 100, 102, 113-115, 129, 151, 171, 180, 183, 186, 189, 219, 220, 230, 247, 254 and 262
are not printed anywhere in the book - they are absent, not a missing page. The console is item
29 and its nineteen sub-parts are numbered 29-01 to 29-19.

**The list is printed twice in this repository's sources.** The service manual (RS9600-SS021-01)
prints it on PDF pp. 47-54 and the 7.5S owner's manual on printed pp. 60-67 under the same
heading; the rows agree wherever the owner's manual's text layer is legible. The service manual
is the cleaner copy - all eight of its pages have a native text layer - so read it from here.

## What the list names that the prose does not

- **Two optical sensor boards with board numbers**: item 221 `Optical Sensor Board(CS63008-00)`
  and item 222 `Optical Sensor Board(CS63008-10)`. The service manual's electronic-parts
  photograph (PDF p. 6) shows both boards with those numbers on their labels - the -00 board
  carries two reflective heads and the -10 board one, which is how the book's sensor-test page
  describes the step sensor (two reflector sensors) and the RPM sensor (one). See `spirit-climber-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`.
- **Two Poly-V belts by length**: item 37 `Belt (8PJ), 584mm` and item 38 `Belt (8PJ), 1032mm`.
  The replacement chapter calls them the 584L and 1032L belts.
- **A gear motor (item 44), a brake disc (61) and four braking magnets (45)** - a motor-set
  magnetic brake - and a 12 VDC power adapter (229) with an optional power cord (260): the
  machine plugs in; there is no generator row.
- **Seven tools packed with the machine sit in the list as items**: 208 and 212 L Allen wrenches
  (6 x 27 x 120L and 5 x 25 x 67L), 209 a 12/14 mm wrench, 210 a 13/14 mm wrench, 211 a 10 mm
  wrench, 213 an M8 L Allen wrench, 214 a Phillips head screwdriver, and 242 a short Phillips
  head screwdriver. The book prints no separate tool list; these rows are it.

## Rows that repeat a name

**Items 16 and 21 both read `Seat Back Bracket`, quantity 1, and items 15 and 66 both read
`Seat Carriage`, quantity 1.** Neither pair is distinguished anywhere in the book; name the
item number, not the name, when ordering. Item 101 `PU Wheel` is the largest single quantity, 27.
