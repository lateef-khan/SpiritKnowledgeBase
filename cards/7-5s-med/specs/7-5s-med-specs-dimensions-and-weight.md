| Figure | Printed |
|---|---|
| Length | **80.3" (204 cm)** |
| Width | **33.1" (84 cm)** |
| Height | **46.9" (119 cm)** |
| Weight | **305.8 lbs. (139 kg)** |

Every pair converts correctly, unlike the 7.0S page in the same family, whose inch and
centimetre figures are rounded a centimetre apart.

**No shipping weight, no gross weight and no carton size** is printed in the 75-page manual,
and neither is a step height or a seat travel.

## This is the longest machine in the family, and it is not the 2025 7.5S

The Spirit-badged **7.5S of 2025** weighs the same **305.8 lbs.** and is a different size -
**77.6" by 33.5" by 46.5"** against this book's **80.3" by 33.1" by 46.9"**. Two and a half
inches longer, four tenths narrower and four tenths taller. **The matching weight is not proof
you have the right book**; check the length. See `75s-2025-specs-dimensions-and-weight` and
`spirit-rehab-stepper-specs-two-different-machines-are-called-7-0s`.

The extra length over the 7.0S of the same book family - 80.3" against 67" - is the rear rail
the seat slides off, which the 7.0S does not have. See
`spirit-rehab-stepper-assembly-sliding-the-seat-off-and-anchoring-a-wheelchair`.

## The user weight limit is a different figure

The same page prints **Patient weight capacity 440 Lbs. (200 Kgs.)**. That is a safety limit and
lives in the `safety` section - `ms300-2021-safety-patient-weight-limit-200-kg`.
