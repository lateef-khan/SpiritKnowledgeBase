One photograph of the 7.5S (the book calls it 7.5S(RS9600-SS021-01)) with ten numbered arrows and
a table:

| Item | Description | Item | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Console | 06 | End Cap |
| 02 | Pedal | 07 | Shroud (L) |
| 03 | Console Mast Cover | 08 | Shroud (R) |
| 04 | Top Cover | 09 | Seat Back Cover |
| 05 | End Cap, Oval Stabilizer Tube | 10 | Arm Rest |

Item 5 is the oval cap on the front stabilizer tube; item 6 the plain cap on the seat rail. The
two **Shrouds** are the drive-housing sides - the wiring chapter's "open the left shroud" is
item 7, behind which sit the DC power cable connector, the sensor boards and the motor.

**No part numbers or quantities here**; the book's parts list is a separate chapter
(`7-5s-med-specs-parts-list`). The 8.5S book numbers nineteen components on a different machine
(`85s-2025-specs-component-description-nineteen-numbered-parts`).
