- **Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each use, to prevent rust.**
- **Check hand grips, seat cushions and foot pedals for signs of wear (once a month).**
- If a squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling develops, the main cause is most likely one of two
  reasons:
  - **The hardware was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.** All bolts installed during
    assembly need to be tightened as much as possible. It may be necessary to use a larger wrench
    than the one provided. **90% of calls to the service department for noise issues can be
    attributed to hardware not being tightened enough.**
- If squeaks or other noises persist, **check that the machine is properly levelled. See levelling
  instructions.**

## Three differences from the 7.0S page in the same family

| | This book | MED 7.0S and MS300 |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly item | **hand grips, seat cushions and foot pedals, checked for wear** | the pedals, checked for tightness |
| Second cause of noise | **not printed** - the list says "one of two reasons" and gives one | the crank arm nut and/or the pedals need retightening |
| Levelling | *"See leveling instructions"* | 2 pads on the rear stabiliser, ½" wrench |

**"One of two reasons" and then one reason.** The second bullet was dropped from this page and the
sentence that introduces it was not. The missing one on the 7.0S page is the crank arm nut - and a
7.5S has no crank arm either, so nothing is lost by its absence. **Do not go looking for a second
cause; check the levelling.**

**This page gives no leveller count and no wrench size.** The count is on the setting-up page:
four front corner glides and a ½" wrench -
`7-5s-med-assembly-levelling-four-front-corner-glides`. That makes this the only book in the family
whose two levelling passages do not contradict each other.

## What this page does not contain

- **No lubricant of any kind.** `lubricat` returns no hits in the whole 75-page book.
- **No schedule table.** The monthly wear check is the only interval printed anywhere in it.
- **No disinfection procedure** - `ms300-2021-maintenance-hygienic-requirements-and-cleaning`.
- **No belt, cable or bearing wear list.** The 2025 books add a WARNING naming belt, PU wheel,
  bearing, idler, shaft and pedal as the parts most susceptible to wear; this book has no such
  panel.
