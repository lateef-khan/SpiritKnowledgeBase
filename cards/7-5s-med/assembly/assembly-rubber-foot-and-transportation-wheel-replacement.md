**6.11.1 Rubber foot.** There are **four rubber feet** under the machine. As shown in figure 6.11.1, unscrew the
**uppermost iron nut** to replace the rubber foot.

**6.11.2 Transportation wheel.** As shown in figure 6.11.2, remove the **screw cap** to replace the transportation
wheel.

No sizes are printed. The parts list carries **Ø75 Foot Leveler, 4** (item 75), **Rubber Foot Pad, 6** (item 88) and
**Ø65 Transportation Wheel, 2** (item 77). Levelling the four front corner glides is an owner's-manual
adjustment with its own card.

**The 7.5S (RS9600-SS021-03) service manual prints this page word for word, four rubber feet included. The 7.0S (RS9500-SS021-02) service manual prints "There are six rubber feet under the machine"** - the one word that differs, and it agrees with the 7.0S owner's manual's six levelling glides. The iron nut, the screw cap and the rest of the sentence are the same in all three books.
