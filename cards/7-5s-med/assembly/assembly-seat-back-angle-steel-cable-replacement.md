**6.13.1.1 Remove the seat back angle cable.** For the seat cushion, refer to figure 6.12.1 (four screws under
the cushion). As shown in figure 6.13.1, loosen the adjusting cable screw and remove the cable head. As shown in
figures 6.13.2 and 6.13.3, unlock the **Velcro strap**. As shown in figure 6.13.4, turn the adjustment screw under
the release handle in the direction that lets the cable be taken out, and remove the steel cable.

**6.13.1.2** Refit by following 6.13.1.1 in reverse order.

The parts list carries **Steel Cable** (item 103) and **Steel Cable, Left (84.5 x 76 cm)** (item 112); the
procedure does not say which is this one. The fore/aft cable is the next procedure and has its own card.
