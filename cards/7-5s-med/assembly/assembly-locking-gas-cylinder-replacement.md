**6.12.1 Remove the locking gas cylinder.** As shown in figure 6.12.1, remove the **four screws under the seat
cushion**. As shown in figure 6.12.2, remove the **safety cover** by loosening its screws. As shown in figure
6.12.3, cut the cable ties. As shown in figure 6.12.4, loosen the adjustment cable and remove the cable head.
As shown in figures 6.12.5 and 6.12.6, take off the screws and caps to remove the locking gas cylinder.

**6.12.2** Refit by following 6.12.1 in reverse order.

No screw sizes are printed. The parts list carries one **Locking Gas Cylinder** (item 109) and one **Safety Cover**
(item 108). The two seat steel cables - back angle and fore/aft - start from the same four cushion screws and
have their own cards.

**The 7.0S (RS9500-SS021-02) and 7.5S (RS9600-SS021-03) service manuals print this procedure word for word**, figure numbers included. The two later books head it "Seat back Gas Cylinder Replacement" where the 2021 book says "Locking Gas Cylinder"; the steps are the same. The 7.0R medical recumbent bike prints the same six figures for its own seat; that is a bike card.
