**Two belts, two tensions: the 584 mm belt at 320 N and the 1032 mm belt at 240 N**, both set on the two idler
bolts.

**6.9.1 Loosen the idler wheel.** Refer to 6.5 to 6.8 to remove the connecting cables, linkages, linear slider
and cable guide wheel. As shown in figures 6.9.1 and 6.9.2, loosen the **socket head cap bolt and the J bolt** to
loosen the idler wheel.

**6.9.2 Remove the mounted bearing.** As shown in figures 6.9.3 and 6.9.4, loosen the locking screws to remove
the front and rear mounted bearings.

**6.9.3 Remove the belt.** Remove the **belt (584L)** (figure 6.9.5) and the **belt (1032L)** (figure 6.9.6).

**6.9.4 Belt refit.** Follow 6.9.1 to 6.9.3 in reverse. As shown in figures 6.9.1 and 6.9.2, adjust the tightness
of those two bolts to adjust the belt tension: **belt (584L) 320 N; belt (1032L) 240 N**.

**6.9.5 Mounted bearing refit.** Follow 6.9.1 to 6.9.2 in reverse.

The parts list names them **Belt (8PJ), 584 mm** and **Belt (8PJ), 1032 mm** and carries four mounted bearings.
The troubleshooting chapter (7.1.1) sends a slipping belt here to have the screw and nut tightened; if it still
slips, the cable guide wheel's one-way bearing is suspect. The 8.5S has one drive belt at 80 N.
