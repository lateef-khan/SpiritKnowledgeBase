## Reading these

- **Item 216 and item 217 appear in two panels each with different quantities**, two and twelve for
  216, six and four for 217. The bag holds fourteen of 216 and ten of 217 - and the parts list
  confirms fourteen for 216.
- **Item 217 is drawn as `3/8" x 2T` in step one and `3/8" x 3/4"` in step four**, which is how the
  page prints it. The parts list settles it: item 217 is a **flat washer**, and the step-four
  caption has taken the neighbouring bolt's size. **Order it as a washer.**
- **Item 128, not 129, is the swivel-seat-latch bolt on this machine.** The 7.0S and MS300 use 129
  for the same M6 x 40L. Order by description across the two machines.
- **The six M8 x 30L bolts and six M8 nuts of step two are six in all, three a side** - the step
  text reads *"install six M8 x 30mm bolts (196) from the top side of the armrest and assemble the
  six nuts (160)"*, and the panel supplies six of each, not twelve.
- **Item 160 is the one quantity the parts list and the panel disagree on.** The panel says
  **6**; the parts list says **20** (`M8 x 1.25 x 6.5T Luck Nut`, printed that way for *lock nut*).
  Item 196, its bolt, is 6 in both. **Fit six**, and treat the other fourteen as used elsewhere on
  the machine rather than as a shortfall in the bag.
- **Item 255 is a 3/8" nyloc nut, and it holds the seat assembly to the rail.** It appears nowhere
  in the 7.0S build.

## The tools are not in these panels

Seven wrenches and a screwdriver come as numbered rows in the parts list - this machine adds a
**208, L Allen Wrench (6 x 27 x 120L)** the 7.0S does not have. No panel promises any of them; see
`spirit-rehab-stepper-assembly-no-tools-panel`.
