**The one-way bearing has a direction, and the sensor gap is 7 to 9 mm.** Both are printed as conditions of the
refit.

**6.8.1 Remove the cable guide wheel.** Refer to 6.5.3 to remove the rear connecting cable. As shown in figure
6.8.1, loosen the screws to remove the **sensor bracket** first. As shown in figures 6.8.2 and 6.8.3, the cable
guide wheel can be removed after taking off the **C clips**.

**6.8.2 Refit the cable guide wheel.** The direction of the **one-way bearing** must be the same as in figure
6.8.4. As shown in figure 6.8.5, after fitting the sensor bracket, the distance between the sensor and the cable
guide wheel should be **7 to 9 mm**.

The troubleshooting chapter (7.1.2) sends you here when the belt still slips after its tension has been set: a
broken one-way bearing inside the cable guide wheel is the named cause. The same 7 to 9 mm appears in 7.3.1 as
the gap between the bar-code sticker and the encoder's optical sensor board.
