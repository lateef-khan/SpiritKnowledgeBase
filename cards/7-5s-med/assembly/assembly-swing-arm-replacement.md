**6.2.1** As shown in figure 6.2.1, remove the screws to remove the swing arm.

**6.2.2** Refit by following 6.2.1 in reverse order.

That is the whole printed procedure: no screw size, no count, no torque. The parts list of the same book prints
**Swing Arm (R)** and **Swing Arm (L)** as items 3 and 4 and **Swing Arm Drive Weldment, 2** as item 215.

If a swing arm shakes while stepping, the troubleshooting chapter (7.2) says to lock the screw shown in figure
7.2.1 and, if it still shakes, to check that the quick-release telescopic tube is in its locked position - an
adjustment, not this replacement. The preventative-maintenance page says to check the armrests for looseness on
both sides.
