**6.6.1 Remove the lower linkage.** As shown in figures 6.6.1 and 6.6.2, loosen the screw to remove the lower
linkage.

**6.6.2 Remove the handle bar linkage.** As shown in figures 6.6.3 and 6.6.4, loosen the screw to remove the
handle bar linkage.

**6.6.3 Remove the upper linkage.** As shown in figures 6.6.5 and 6.6.6, loosen the screw to remove the upper
linkage.

**6.6.4 Refitting.** Upper linkage: 6.6.1 to 6.6.3 in reverse. Handle bar linkage: 6.6.1 to 6.6.2 in reverse.
Lower linkage: 6.6.1 in reverse.

The three linkages nest: you cannot reach the upper one without the two below it, which is why the refit order
lengthens as you go up. No screw sizes are printed. The parts list names **Lower Linkage A (2)** and **Lower
Linkage B (2)** and a **Lower Linkage (2)**; the procedure does not say which of them "lower linkage" is. The 8.5S
book replaces its lower linkage, A and B in a separate procedure.
