**6.1.1** As shown in figures 6.1.1 and 6.1.2, remove the **four screws from the bottom of the console** ("electronic
watch" in the book's wording) and remove the **cable connector** to remove the electronic console.

**6.1.2** Refit by following 6.1.1 in reverse order.

No screw size and no connector count are printed. The 8.5S removes a rear tube cover (two screws) before its four
console screws; this book has no cover step.
