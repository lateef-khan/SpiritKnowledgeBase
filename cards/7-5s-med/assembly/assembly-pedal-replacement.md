**6.3.1** As shown in figures 6.3.1 and 6.3.2, unscrew the screw. *"The bottom of the screw is recommended to use
a short screwdriver removed around the same"* - the lower fastener sits where a normal driver will not fit, and
the tool list of the same book carries a **Short Phillips Head Screw Driver (item 242)** for it.

**6.3.2** Refit by following 6.3.1 in reverse order.

No screw size is printed. The preventative-maintenance page says to check weekly whether the pedal is loose and
lock the screw if it is.
