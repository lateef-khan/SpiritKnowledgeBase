Once the machine is assembled and standing on a flat level floor:

1. Use a **1/2" wrench** to **loosen the top nut** of the levellers.
2. **Adjust the front 4 corner levellers by hand** as necessary to remove any wobble.
3. **Tighten all the top nuts** against the bottom of the stabilizer tubes.
4. Make sure the **bottom nut remains cinched against the levelling foot**.

**Four levellers, all of them at the front corners.** There is no centre pair on this machine and
no step that unscrews one until it touches the floor.

## The parts list agrees, and the 2025 book's page does not

Item **75, `Ø75 Foot Leveler`, quantity 4** is the only adjustable foot in the parts list. Item
**88, `Rubber Foot Pad`, quantity 6**, is a pad, not a leveller.

The Spirit-badged 7.5S book of 2025 prints the **7.0S's** six-leveller paragraph instead - two
centre levellers screwed all the way in, four corners by hand, then the centre pair unscrewed
until they touch (`spirit-rehab-stepper-assembly-levelling-six-glides`). **That page carries a
count its own parts list does not support**: the 2025 7.5S list also has four `Ø75 Foot Pad` rows
at item 75. **On any 7.5S, look for four.**

## The maintenance page does not repeat a count

Where the 7.0S book ends its noise item with *"There are 2 leveling pads on the bottom of the rear
stabilizer"*, this book says only *"check that the 7.5S is properly leveled. **See leveling
instructions.**"* - so this page is the only count in the book, and nothing contradicts it.

## No panel says the wrench is in the carton

The parts list supplies a **12/14 mm** wrench (209), a **13/14 mm** wrench (210) and a **10 mm**
wrench (211). None of them is a ½" (12.7 mm). See
`spirit-rehab-stepper-assembly-no-tools-panel`.
