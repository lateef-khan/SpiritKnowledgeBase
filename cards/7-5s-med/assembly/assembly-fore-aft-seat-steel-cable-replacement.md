**6.13.2.1 Remove the fore/aft seat cable.** For the seat cushion, refer to figure 6.12.1 (four screws under the
cushion). As shown in figure 6.13.5, cut the cable ties. As shown in figures 6.13.6 and 6.13.7, untie the
**Velcro tape**. As shown in figure 6.13.8, remove the cable head and the spring. As shown in figure 6.13.9, turn
the adjustment screw under the release handle in the direction that lets the cable be removed, and remove it.

**6.13.2.2** Refit by following 6.13.2.1 in reverse order.

This cable has a spring on its head; the seat-back cable does not. Sliding the seat off the rear and the
wheelchair anchor are owner's-manual procedures with their own card.
