**6.10.1.1 Remove the magnet bracket.** As shown in figure 6.10.1, remove the **four screws under the magnet
bracket**. As shown in figure 6.10.2, take off the spring to remove the magnet bracket.

**6.10.1.2** Refit by following 6.10.1.1 in reverse order.

No screw size is printed. The bracket carries the **four braking magnets** the parts list numbers 45; the gear
motor pulls it toward the aluminium brake disc through the spring. When the gear motor goes back, the same
section says the aluminium plate must sit equidistant from the magnet on both sides and must not touch the
bracket at level 20 - see the gear motor card.

**The 7.0S (RS9500-SS021-02) and 7.5S (RS9600-SS021-03) service manuals print this procedure word for word**, figure numbers included.
