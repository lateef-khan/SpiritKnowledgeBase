**Section 3 names three parts:** **Console** - controls all functions of the bike. **Brake
Controller** - "the circuit board consist of the DC power supply for console". **Generator Brake**
- increases or decreases the resistance level of the brake. General information: the console
"contain keys control and **LED Display windows**", and the main controller includes the
"power supply (generator power), drive control circuit".

**The display line is a leftover.** The picture on the same page is captioned **7.5" LCD Display**,
the troubleshooting matrix says *LCDs*, and every XBR95 owner's-manual card records an LCD - see
`spirit-residential-bike-specs-console-display-type`. Read "LED" as copied from the commercial
900-series text.

**No working voltage is printed for the generator brake in this book.** The 2016 XBR95 book gave
one (DC 0.4-14 V, `xbr95-2016-specs-hybrid-generator-brake-working-voltage-dc-0-4-to-14-v`); the
2023 book dropped it.

The chapter-2 photographs (`xbr95-2023-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`) show only the console
and the speed sensor; the controller and the brake are photographed later, in section 6.

This is the one residential bike in the range with a generator and a separate controller; the
XBR55/XBU55 have a gear motor driven from the console.
