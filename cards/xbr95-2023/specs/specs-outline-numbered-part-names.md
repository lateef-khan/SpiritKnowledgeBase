**Upper drawing, the shrouded bike** - the same nineteen items as the XBR55-2023: 1 Console,
2 Pad Holder, 3 Console Mast Cover, 4 Front Shroud (R), 5 Front Shroud (L), 6 Round Disk,
7 Handgrip Foam, 8 Pedal (L), 9 Pedal (R), 10 Mesh Seat Back, 11 Aluminum Track, 12 Seat,
13 Drink Bottle Holder (L), 14 Drink Bottle Holder (R), 15 Bottom Cover, 16 Rear Shroud (R),
17 Rear Shroud (L), 18 End Cap, 19 Transport Wheel.

**Lower drawing, the frame:**

| No. | Name | No. | Name |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Handlebar Assembly | 10 | Drive Pulley |
| 2 | Console Mast | 11 | **Induction Brake** |
| 3 | Upright | 12 | Crank |
| 4 | Seat Back Frame | 13 | Idler Wheel Assembly |
| 5 | Seat Handlebar | 14 | Transport Wheel |
| 6 | Seat Carriage | 15 | Foot Pad |
| 7 | **Controller** | 16 | Foot Pad (middle) |
| 8 | Rear Stabilizer | 17 | Rail |
| 9 | Main Frame | | |

The **Controller (7)** is drawn inside the rear frame section under the seat, and the resistance
unit is labelled **Induction Brake** here - the same generator/brake the electrical pages call a
*Generator Brake*. Two sets of numbers, neither of them parts-list item numbers.
