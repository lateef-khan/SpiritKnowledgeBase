**One page, five parts, read from the render.**

**2.1 Console** - **Cooling Fan** (the vent slot above the level keys) and **Speaker** (the grille at the foot
of the console). This console carries direct-resistance keys from 2 to 40; no display or switch is labelled.

**2.2 Controller and Driver parts** - **Speed Sensor** (a photograph of the reed switch and magnet), **Brake
Controller** (a photograph of a red circuit board, called out low in the frame under the seat track), and
**Hybrid Generator** (a photograph of the flywheel with its coil pack, called out inside the front shroud).

**"Brake Controller" is this book's name for the board the 2016 XBR95 book called the Driver board**
(`xbr95-2016-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`); the same book's wiring chapter heads its pages "Generator
Controller" and its Electrical Configurations page pairs a **Brake Controller** with a **Generator Brake**.
Three names, one board, one flywheel.

**This is the self-powered XBR95, not the plug-in XBR55 or XBU55.** Those 2023 books label a Gear Motor and
no controller or generator (`spirit-xbr55-xbu55-2023-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`); which is which is
on `spirit-residential-bike-specs-gear-motor-or-generator-brake`.

**This book prints no tool list.** The 2023 XBR55 and XBU55 books print "Tools Required - A multi-meter"
above their error codes; the XBR95 page goes straight from its one error code to the circuit diagram
(`spirit-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`).

Nothing on the page is a part number or a rating.
