These are the callout numbers on the exploded view drawing. The drawing is a
picture with bare numbers on it and no text, so the table below is the only
readable form of it.

**162 rows are printed.** The whole item numbers that appear are 1-33, 35-39, 41-56, 61, 63-66, 68, 71-73, 75-80, 82-83, 85-95, 97-106, 108, 111-112, 114, 116, 124-126, 128-129, 131-133, 135-136, 139, 141, 143, 148, 158-173, 175-179, 185, 187-189, 192, 194-205. Any number outside that run is **not printed in this manual at all** - 56 numbers between 34 and 193 are absent, not a missing page.

## The item numbers lost their padding, and that breaks every old quote

**The 2023 CR800ENT list padded its low numbers to three digits** - `010`, `011`,
... `019`, and its console sub-items `19-01` to `19-17`. **This list prints them
plain**: `10`, `11`, ... `19`, and `19-1` to `19-17`. It also splits item 9 into
**`9L` and `9R`**, which the 2023 book did not have.

A search for "item 055" against this book finds nothing; the induction brake is
item **55** here. The two books hold the same machine's parts under two different
spellings of the same numbers, so **50 of the 152 rows in the 2023 list have no
same-spelling counterpart here** even where the part is identical.

## What else changed from the 2023 CR800ENT list

| Item | CR800ENT-2023 | CR800ENT-2024 |
|---|---|---|
| 65 | `3/8" x 135mm_Hex Head Bolt` | the same bolt, qualified `(30mm)` |
| 88 | `M8 x 7T_Nylon Nut-Blacking` | `M8 x 7T_Nylon Nut` |
| 93 | `M6 x 38m/m_Socket Head Cap Bolt (Alloy Steel)` | the alloy note dropped |
| 116 | `Pedal` | `Pedal(L/R)` |
| 159 | **`On/Off Switch Bracket`** | **`Electronic Module`** |
| 175 | `3/8" x 2-3/4"_Hex Head Bolt` | the same bolt, qualified `(12mm)` |
| 194 | `1350m/m_Sensor W/Cable` | `Sensor W/Cable` - the length dropped |

**Item 159 swapped names with the plain CR800 book.** The 2023 CR800ENT list
called 159 an On/Off Switch Bracket and the 2023 CR800 list called it an
Electronic Module; in 2024 the two names traded places. See
`cr800-2024-specs-parts-list`.

## What tells this list apart from the CR800-2024 list

- **Item 55 is `Induction Brake`, item 49 `EMS Controller`, item 47 `Power
  Adaptor` and item 41 `Power Cord`.** The plain CR800-2024 list instead has a
  `Generator/Brake` at 55 and a `Generator/Brake Controller` at 43.
- The plain CR800-2024 list carries a `TV Adapter (5C2V)` at item 194; this one
  does not.
- The two lists share 139 item numbers and agree on 93 of them, so **46 shared
  numbers carry a different part or quantity.**

**Items 3 and 6 are both printed `Handle Bar` quantity 1, and items 26 and 42 are
both printed `300mm_Handpulse Wire` quantity 1.** Neither pair can be told apart
from this list.

**No flywheel weight, machine weight, screen size or resistance rating is printed
against any row.** See `spirit-bike-specs-no-specification-table`.
