**No. Step four of the 2024 build connects four cables where the 2023 build connects
five, and the one that is gone is the HDMI Connecting Wire (No.186).**

The 2024 step four item 2 reads: connect the wires on the back of the console to their
respective places - **Computer Cable (No.44), Handpulse Wire (No.45), CSAFE Connecting
Wire (No.204), Network Connecting Cable (No.192)**. Tuck excess cable into the console
mast, careful not to pinch the wires.

The 2023 step four item 2 is the same sentence with **HDMI Connecting Wire (No.186)**
added after the network cable.

## The word HDMI appears nowhere in the 2024 manual

Not in the assembly chapter, not in the parts list, and not in the console chapter. The
2023 manual uses it twice: in step four, and in its TV Mode section, which explains that
"when properly connected to the Set-Top Box (STP) via the HDMI port and TV mode selected,
you can enjoy TV programs". **Both went in the same reissue.**

## This is the only cable dropped in the whole 2024 redesign

The **CT800ENT** and **CT850ENT** 2024 builds connect the same five cables their 2022
builds did, and the **CE800ENT** 2024 build threads the same three - computer cable, CSAFE
connecting wire, communication cable. Neither of those books ever mentioned HDMI. The
CR800ENT is the one machine of the fourteen whose console lost a connection.

## Nothing else in the build changed

The four steps are otherwise word for word the 2023 steps, and all four hardware tables
are reprinted row for row. The carton panel is unchanged too - twelve lines, and it never
listed a cable. See
[the 2023 build](../../cr800ent-2023/assembly/cr800ent-2023-assembly-procedure.md).

## What this means for a customer with a set-top box

The 2024 manual describes no HDMI port and no TV mode. It does not say the hardware was
removed - only that neither is documented any more. Do not promise a 2024 owner a TV
connection on the strength of the 2023 book, and do not tell one to look for an HDMI
cable in the mast.
