**CAUTION: heart rate programs are intended for wireless Bluetooth wearables only. You must receive a strong/steady value in the heart rate window.**

| Program | Default | Goal it is geared for |
|---|---|---|
| **H1** | **60% of your projected heart rate maximum**, (220 - age × 0.60) | Individuals with **fat loss** as a goal |
| **H2** | **80% of your projected heart rate maximum**, (220 - age × 0.80) | Individuals with a goal of **improving cardiovascular & lung function** |

1. **Select H1 or H2 via the Program keys, then press Display/Enter.**
2. The **incline window blinks showing the user's age**. Adjust and press Display/Enter, or press Display/Enter to accept the default. **Adjusting age will change the target HR value.**
3. The **incline window blinks showing the default body weight** for this program. Adjust and press Display/Enter, or accept the default.
4. **Adjust the H1 or H2 value** with the speed knob key and press Display/Enter to continue.
5. The **time window blinks.** Adjust the time and press Display/Enter.
6. You can **go back and modify your settings by pressing the Stop key to go back one level, or screen.**
7. **You can increase or decrease the speed at any time in the program by pressing the speed knob.**
8. **When the program ends the message window shows a summary of your workout.** The summary is displayed for a short time, then the console returns to the start-up display.
9. **Press Start to begin the program.**

**The program will control the treadmill to steadily increase your heart rate to the target value. This process is automatic and could take upwards of five minutes**, depending on how far your heart rate needs to go and what kind of physical shape you are in. **People who are more fit will take longer to reach the target.**

**The printed step order puts "press Start" last, at step 9, but step 6 already describes editing settings and step 7 describes changing speed during the program.** The order is reproduced exactly as printed.

**The formula is printed without brackets** as *(220 - age x 0.60)*. It is reproduced unchanged.
