**Attach Frame Base Cover (L&R) (38 & 39) to Right Upright (4) and Left Upright (5)** using **M5_Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (102)** and **Phillips Head Screw (126) - 4 pcs**.

**NOTE: Please make sure to pull Locking Pin Assembly (157) out and insert End Cap (115) into before first time operating the machine.**

**Page 5 describes the same removal in different words**, as a **safety pin** that must be removed **before powering on or using the incline function**, pulled **away from the treadmill base completely** - with **no mention of an end cap**. This step names a **Locking Pin Assembly (157)** and an **End Cap (115)** but says nothing about the incline function. **Neither passage refers to the other, and this card picks no winner.**
