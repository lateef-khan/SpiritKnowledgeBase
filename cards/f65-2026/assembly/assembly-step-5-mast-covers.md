**Attach all five covers to Console Outer Cover Bottom Assembly (22):**

| Part no. | Cover |
|---|---|
| **119** | Front Console Mast Cover (L) |
| **120** | Rear Console Mast Cover (L) |
| **121** | Front Console Mast Cover (R) |
| **122** | Rear Console Mast Cover (R) |
| **113** | Console repair Mast Cover |

Using **M5_Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (102)** and **Sheet Metal Screw (74) - 6 pcs in total**.
