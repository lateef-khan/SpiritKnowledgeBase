Assembly is printed as **seven numbered steps**, pages 8 to 11. **Steps 2 to 7 each have their own hardware bag**; step 1 needs no hardware. The hardware checklist is on pages 6 and 7.

**A QR code on page 5 is captioned "Scan Here for Assembly Video".** The code itself is an image and **no URL is printed in the text**, so the destination cannot be recovered from this extraction.

**When to tighten.** Steps 2, 3 and 4 each end with **"Please DON'T Fully Tighten All Screws Yet"**. Step 4 closes with **"Please Tighten All Screws from Steps 2 to Steps 4 After All Components Assembly Complete."**

**One tool does everything.** Every step names the **M5_Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (102)**, including steps 5, 6 and 7 whose fasteners are sheet metal and Phillips head screws. The manual names no screwdriver anywhere; see `f65-2026-assembly-tools`.
