**Remove the treadmill from the carton and position it aside on a smooth, levelled floor.** The rear should be at least **3'** from any wall.

**You must remove the plastic wrap and Styrofoam from beneath the unit before removing the Velcro belt.** **Tilting the unit sideways after removing the belt may cause the unit to fold and bounce upwards. This can cause serious injury.**

**This step's clearance figure disagrees with the safety page.** Page 2 requires **a minimum of 3 1/2 feet** between the rear of the treadmill and any fixed object; this step says **at least 3'** from any wall. See `f65-2026-placement-and-clearance`.

Page 5 gives a fuller unpacking warning, including the shipping strap and the shipping safety pin; see `f65-2026-unpacking-shipping-strap-and-safety-pin`.
