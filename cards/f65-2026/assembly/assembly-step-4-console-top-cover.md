1. **Attach Console Outer Cover TOP Assembly (21) to Console Outer Cover Bottom Assembly (22)** using:
   - **M5_Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (102)**
   - **Button Head Socket Bolt (79) - 2 pcs**
   - **Split Washer (80) - 2 pcs**
   - **Flat Washer (100) - 4 pcs**
2. **After slightly securing the above screws, secure the following parts at the middle bottom of Console Outer Cover Bottom Assembly (22)**: **M5_Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (102)**, **Button Head Socket Bolt (72) - 2 pcs**, **Split Washer (80) - 2 pcs**, **Flat Washer (100) - 2 pcs**.
3. **Connect the cables:**

| From | To |
|---|---|
| Computer Cable **(21-16)** | Computer Cable **(22-14)** |
| Hub board cable **(21-15)** | Computer Cable **(22-16)** |
| Hub board cable **(21-14)** | Computer Cable **(22-15)** |

4. **Push connected cables into Console Outer Cover Bottom Assembly (22).**

**NOTE: Please tighten all screws from steps 2 to step 4 after all components assembly is complete.**

**Cable (22-14) is claimed twice in this manual.** Step 3 mates (22-14) with Computer Cable (Middle) (117); this step mates the same (22-14) with Computer Cable (21-16). One of the two must be a misprint. **This card changes neither, and picks no winner.** See `f65-2026-assembly-step-3-console-bottom-cover`.
