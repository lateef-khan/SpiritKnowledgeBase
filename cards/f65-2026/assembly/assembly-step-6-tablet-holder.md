**Place Tablet Holder (177) on Console Outer Cover TOP Assembly (21)** and use **M5_Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (102)** to tighten **4 pcs of Phillips Head Screw (192)**.

The manual gives **no weight or size limit** for what the holder will take, and attaches no separate safety warning to it. The general caution about distraction while walking is on page 4; see `f65-2026-distractions-while-walking`.
