| Part no. | Item |
|---|---|
| **#47** | Safety Key |
| **#115** | End Cap |
| **#104** | Lubricant |
| **#103** | M6_L Allen Wrench |
| **#102** | M5_Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench |

- **#103** is the wrench the maintenance section uses for **both belt tension and belt tracking**; the tracking section calls the same part number **a 6 mm Allen wrench**. See `f65-2026-running-belt-tension` and `f65-2026-running-belt-tracking`.
- **#115 End Cap** is fitted in assembly step 7 after the locking pin is pulled out.
- **#104 Lubricant** is the one tube of "Lube" the maintenance section says the treadmill ships with.
- **No screwdriver is listed**, although steps 5, 6 and 7 use sheet metal and Phillips head screws.
