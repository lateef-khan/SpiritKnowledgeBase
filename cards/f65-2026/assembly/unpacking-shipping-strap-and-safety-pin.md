**Serious injury could occur if this folding treadmill is not unpacked properly.**

**The shipping strap.** A **deck restraint SHIPPING strap** is installed around the treadmill base to prevent the treadmill unfolding accidentally during shipping. **If this strap is not removed properly the treadmill could spring open unexpectedly and cause injury** to anyone standing near it.

1. **Position the treadmill flat on the ground, in the orientation it would be in if you were using it.**
2. **Do not tilt the treadmill up on its side while removing the shipping strap** - this could cause the folding mechanism to spring open.
3. **Pull up on the strap and cut it with a pair of scissors.**

**The safety pin.** A **safety pin is locked on the treadmill base** that prevents the treadmill unfolding accidentally during shipping. **This safety pin has to be removed before powering on or using the incline function, to avoid any damage to the machine. When removing the safety pin, pull it away from the treadmill base completely.**

**Assembly step 7 on page 11 describes the same removal differently**: *"Please make sure to pull Locking Pin Assembly (157) out and insert End Cap (115) into before first time operating the machine."* Page 5 calls the part a **safety pin** and says only to pull it away; page 11 calls it a **Locking Pin Assembly (157)** and adds fitting an **End Cap (115)** into the vacated hole. The manual never says the two are the same part.

Assembly step 1 repeats a third warning about tilting; see `f65-2026-assembly-step-1-position-and-unwrap`.
