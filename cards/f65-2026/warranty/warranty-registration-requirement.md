**The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within 10 days of the original purchase to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.**

**If you are outside the 10 day period, the warranty can still be registered if you have proof of purchase** - the manual's examples are a **receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation**.

**Exclusion 6** adds the other side of the same rule: the warranty does not cover **products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at SOLE. SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.**

How to register is on page 1; see `f65-2026-product-registration`.
