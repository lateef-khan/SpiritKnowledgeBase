- **Warranties outside of North America may vary.**
- **Sole USA warranty coverage is limited to the Continental United States.**
- **Sole USA does not sell or service equipment in Hawaii, Alaska, or outside of the United States.**
- **Please contact your local dealer for details regarding warranty availability in your region.**

The calibration procedure carries a separate regional note: **for sale in Europe, EU standards require Grade return to be off**; see `f65-2026-calibration-procedure`.
