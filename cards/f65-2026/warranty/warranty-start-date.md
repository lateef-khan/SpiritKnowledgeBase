The warranty runs **from the date of retail sale, as determined by sale receipt**, or - **in the absence of a receipt** - **eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date**.

The service section states the same rule for the **labor** period in different words: **twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale, or eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number, establishes the labor warranty period should service be required.**

**Keep your bill of sale.** If service is performed, **it is in your best interest to obtain and keep all receipts.**

The warranty effective date printed at the head of the section is **February 1, 2026**.
