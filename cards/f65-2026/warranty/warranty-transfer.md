**Warranties DO NOT transfer.**

Exclusion 5 excludes **products that have been sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party.**

Registration is tied to the original purchase and the original purchaser's proof of purchase; see `f65-2026-warranty-registration-requirement`.
