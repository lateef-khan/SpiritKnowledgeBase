**Effective February 1, 2026.** SOLE warrants all its treadmills' parts for the periods below.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Frame / Drive motor** | **Lifetime** |
| **Parts** | **2 years** |
| **Labor** | **1 year** |
| **Cosmetic items** * | **90 Days** |

**\* Cosmetic items include, but are not limited to: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.**

**SOLE's responsibilities** include providing **new or remanufactured parts, at SOLE's option**, and **technical support to our independent dealers and servicing organizations**. **In the absence of a dealer or service organization, these warranties will be administered by SOLE directly to a consumer.**

When the clock starts is a separate rule; see `f65-2026-warranty-start-date`.
