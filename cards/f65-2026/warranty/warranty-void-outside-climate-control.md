**Warranty will be void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment.** The manual's own examples of where it must not be: **garage, outside, carport, etc.**

This is stricter than the operating limits on page 3, which give a **maximum operating temperature of 40 degrees C and 95% non-condensing humidity** without naming a location. A place can be inside those numbers and still void the warranty. See `f65-2026-operating-environment-limits`.
