- **Record the serial number** of this fitness product in the space provided on page 1.
- **The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to SOLE.**
- **You can also go to www.soletreadmills.com under the support tab to register online.**

The unnumbered page facing it also asks you to record, for future contact: the **name of the dealer**, the **telephone number of the dealer**, and the **purchase date**.

**Registration is a warranty condition, not just a courtesy** - the card must be returned **within 10 days** of the original purchase, or proof of purchase held instead. See `f65-2026-warranty-registration-requirement`.
