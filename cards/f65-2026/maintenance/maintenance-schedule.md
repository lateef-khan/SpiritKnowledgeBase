| Task | Interval |
|---|---|
| **Vacuum under motor cover & check wiring** | **Every other month** |
| **Clean & inspect deck for lubrication** | **Every other month** |
| **Lubricate deck** | **Every 90 hours or sooner if dry** |
| **Inspect belt tracking, adjust if necessary** | **Monthly** |

The printed chart has a fifth column, **Date**, left blank for the owner to fill in.

**The lubrication interval is stated twice and the two do not match.** This chart says **every 90 hours or sooner if dry**. The lubrication section on page 26 says **every 90 hours of use, or every 90 days, regardless of usage**. The "or every 90 days" half appears only in the lubrication section. See `f65-2026-deck-lubrication`.

The general cleaning section asks for **vacuuming underneath the treadmill monthly**, which is not a row in this chart; see `f65-2026-vacuuming-and-motor-cover-cleaning`.
