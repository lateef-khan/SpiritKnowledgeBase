**Dirt, dust and hair can block air inlets and accumulate on the running belt.**

- **Vacuum underneath your treadmill on a monthly basis** to prevent excess build-up of dirt that can get sucked up and get into the inner workings under the motor cover.
- **Every other month, remove the motor cover and carefully vacuum out dirt and hair that may accumulate.**
- **UNPLUG THE POWER CORD BEFORE THIS TASK.**

**A page reference in this section is wrong.** The heading reads *"GENERAL CLEANING (Use the chart on Pg. 5 to record your maintenance)"*. **Page 5 is the unpacking safety page.** The Preventative Maintenance Chart, which has a Date column to write in, is on **page 25**; see `f65-2026-maintenance-schedule`.
