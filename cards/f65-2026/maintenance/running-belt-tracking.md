The treadmill is designed so that the tread-belt **remains reasonably centred while in use**. It is **normal for some belts to drift near one side** depending on a user's gait and whether they favour one leg. **Adjust only if the belt continues to move toward one side during use.**

1. **A 6 mm Allen wrench (103) is provided for this adjustment.**
2. **Make tracking adjustments on the left side bolt.**
3. **Set belt speed at 3 mph.**
4. **If the belt is too close to the left side, turn the bolt only a 1/4 turn to the right (clockwise) and wait a few minutes for the belt to adjust itself.**
5. **Continue to make 1/4 turns until the belt stabilizes in the centre of the running deck.**
6. **If the belt is too close to the right side, turn the bolt counter-clockwise.**

**Be aware that a small adjustment can make a dramatic difference which may not be apparent right away.** The belt may require periodic tracking adjustment; adjustments become less of a concern as the belt is used. **Proper belt tracking is an owner responsibility common with all treadmills.**

**ATTENTION: DAMAGE TO THE RUNNING BELT RESULTING FROM IMPROPER TRACKING / TENSION ADJUSTMENTS IS NOT COVERED UNDER THE SOLE WARRANTY.**

**The same wrench, part 103, is called two different sizes in this manual.** This section calls it **a 6 mm Allen wrench**; the tension section on page 24 calls it an **M6 L Allen wrench**, and the assembly tools list on page 7 calls it an **M6_L Allen Wrench**. See `f65-2026-running-belt-tension`.
