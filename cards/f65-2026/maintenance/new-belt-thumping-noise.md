When a treadmill belt is **brand new, and has been in the package for a few months before use**, a common occurrence can happen: **the area of the belt that wraps around the rollers will take on the shape of the roller.** On starting the treadmill, **this shape in the belt causes a thumping sound each time the belt moves past the rollers.**

**Once the treadmill has been used for a few workouts, the belt wears in and the thumping sound goes away. This noise is normal on new higher end belts so there is no need to contact service; the new belt just requires a wear in period.**
