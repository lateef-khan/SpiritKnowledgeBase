The treadmill uses a **very high-efficiency, low-friction deck. Performance is maximized when the deck is kept as clean as possible.**

1. With a **soft, damp cloth or paper towel**, wipe the **edge of the belt and the area between the belt edge and the frame**.
2. **Reach as far as practical directly under the belt edge.**
3. Do this **once a month to extend belt and bed life**.

**A mild soap and water solution along with a nylon scrub brush will clean the top of the textured belt. Allow to dry before using.**

Cleaning between the belt and deck with a rag is a separate job and is the first stage of lubrication; see `f65-2026-deck-lubrication`.
