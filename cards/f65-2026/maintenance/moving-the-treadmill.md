**The treadmill is equipped with transport wheels that are engaged when the treadmill is folded. After folding, simply roll the treadmill away.**

**Do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and locked position**, and use both handrails to maneuver it. See `f65-2026-folding-and-unfolding`.
