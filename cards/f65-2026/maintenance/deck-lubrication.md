**First, clean between the belt and deck** to remove any trapped debris. Use a **clean, non-fraying rag, t-shirt or light towel**. **Halfway between the end of the treadmill and the motor cover, shove the garment under the belt until you can grasp it on both sides of the belt. Drag the garment the length of the entire belt 1-2 times. Remove the garment.**

**What to use.** **Do not lubricate with anything other than Sole Fitness approved lubricant: 100% food grade silicone lubricant for treadmills.** **DO NOT USE SILICONE CAULKING FOR BATHROOM AND KITCHEN PROJECTS OR A LUBRICANT THAT HAS A SPRAY/FOAM BASE.** The treadmill comes with **one tube of "Lube"**; extra tubes and a **Lube-n-Walk kit** can be ordered from Sole Fitness or an authorized dealer. **Use 2-3 ounces of lubrication each application.**

**How often.** The belt and deck come **pre-lubricated**. Lubricate **every 90 hours of use, or every 90 days regardless of usage** as it can dry out, **or if you notice that the deck is dry**. **Reach between the belt and deck to verify there is lubrication present every other month**; if there is none, **follow the procedure below even though the "Lube" indicator isn't lit on the console**. Otherwise lubricate when the **console's lubrication reminder lights after 90 hours of use**.

**The procedure:**

1. **Turn the power switch off and unplug the power cord from the wall outlet.**
2. **Measure 18" from the edge of the motor cover; kneel down and reach under the belt approximately 4-6" from one edge.**
3. **Squirt a line of lubricant about 1/8" wide × 15" long in an "S" pattern perpendicular to the motor cover.**
4. **Repeat the process on the opposite side.**
5. **Plug the electrical cord back into the outlet and turn the power switch on.**
6. **Walk on the belt at a moderate speed for five minutes to evenly distribute the silicone lube.**
7. If the **"Lube" message appears on the console**, **power the treadmill three times to reset the lube message.**

**Why it matters.** If the lubricant dries out, **friction between the belt and deck rises and places undue stress on the drive motor, drive belt and electronic motor control board, which could result in catastrophic failure of these expensive components. Failure to lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty.**

**The interval is stated twice and the two do not match.** The preventative maintenance chart on page 25 says **every 90 hours or sooner if dry**, with no calendar limit; this section adds **or every 90 days, regardless of usage**. See `f65-2026-maintenance-schedule`.
