Install the treadmill on a **flat level surface with access to a 120-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet, with only the treadmill plugged into that circuit.** This is a burn, fire, electric shock and injury warning, not a suggestion.

**Extension cords.** Do not use one unless it is **14AWG or better, with only one outlet on the end.**

**Do not attempt to disable the grounded plug by using improper adapters, or in any way modify the cord set.** A serious shock or fire hazard may result, along with computer malfunctions.

The diagnosis guide gives a second, looser extension-cord rule for the "only reaches about 7 mph" fault: *"If an extension cord is required it should be as short as possible and heavy duty 16 gauge minimum."* **16 gauge is thinner than the 14AWG this page demands, and the manual never reconciles the two.** See `f65-2026-reaches-only-7-mph`.
