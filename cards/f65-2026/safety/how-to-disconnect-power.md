**To disconnect, in this order:** turn all controls to the off position, **remove the tether cord**, then remove the plug from the outlet.

- **DANGER** - to reduce the risk of electric shock, **disconnect the treadmill from the electrical outlet before cleaning and/or service work**.
- **NEVER remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power.**

Turning off the main power switch in the front of the treadmill completely removes power; simply removing the safety key only puts the console into something like sleep. See `f65-2026-turning-the-treadmill-off`.
