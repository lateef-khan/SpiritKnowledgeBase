**Use caution while participating in other activities while walking on your treadmill**, such as watching television or reading. **These distractions may cause you to lose balance or stray from walking in the centre of the belt, which may result in serious injury.**

The console has a tablet holder fitted during assembly (step 6); the manual attaches no separate warning to it.
