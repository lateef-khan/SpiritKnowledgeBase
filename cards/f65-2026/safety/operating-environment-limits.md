- **NEVER expose this treadmill to rain or moisture.**
- This product is **NOT designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.**
- **Maximum operating temperature: 40 degrees C.**
- **Humidity: 95% non-condensing** (no water drops forming on surfaces).

Separately, the warranty is **void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment** - a garage, outside or a carport are the manual's own examples. That is a warranty rule, not an operating limit, and it is on a different page; see `f65-2026-warranty-void-outside-climate-control`.
