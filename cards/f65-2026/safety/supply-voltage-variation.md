**If voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more, the performance of your treadmill may be affected. Such conditions are not covered under your warranty.**

If you suspect the voltage is low, contact your local power company or a licensed electrician for proper testing.

Low household voltage is also the manual's explanation for a treadmill that only reaches about 7 mph; see `f65-2026-reaches-only-7-mph`.
