**Avoid AFCI/GFCI circuit breakers if possible.** These breakers may trip occasionally during exercise because of the **high inrush currents of the treadmill drive electronics and motor**. This is an issue that **affects all treadmill brands**.

New laws in your area may require these breakers. If you have them and are getting nuisance tripping, **check whether anything else is plugged into the same circuit** - fluorescent lights with electronic ballasts, a coffee maker, a space heater and a hair drier are the manual's examples. **Optimally the treadmill should be the only device plugged into the circuit.**

Sole treadmills have **surge suppressors built in** to help avoid nuisance tripping. Breakers and outlets Sole has tested, which **did not trip in testing as long as no other device shared the circuit**:

| Brand | Series tested |
|---|---|
| Eaton | Cutler Hammer Series |
| Leviton | Smart lock pro |
| Schneider Electric | Canadian home series |
