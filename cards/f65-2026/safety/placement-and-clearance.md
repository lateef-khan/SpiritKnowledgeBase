- Install the treadmill on a **flat level surface**.
- **Do not operate the treadmill on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet.** Damage to both carpet and treadmill may result.
- **Do not block the rear of the treadmill. Provide a minimum of 3 1/2 feet clearance between the rear of the treadmill and any fixed object.**
- Do not use outdoors.

**This manual gives two different rear clearances.** Page 2 says a minimum of **3 1/2 feet** between the rear and any fixed object. Assembly step 1 on page 8 says *"The rear should be at least 3' from any wall."* Neither page mentions the other. The larger figure is the safer one to work to, but the manual picks no winner and neither does this card.
