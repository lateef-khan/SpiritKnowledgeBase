1. **The display automatically turns off, by going into sleep, after 15 minutes of inactivity when in the Stop mode.** Almost all power for the treadmill is off except for some circuits needed to detect a key press so the console can **wake up**. When the console is asleep the treadmill **draws very little power, about as much as a television turned off**.
2. **Removing the safety key is similar to entering sleep mode. Turning off the main power switch in the front of the treadmill will completely remove any power.**

The 15-minute timeout is the **ON** setting of Sleep Mode and can be changed; see `f65-2026-sleep-mode-settings`.
