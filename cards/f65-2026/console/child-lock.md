**Security** is item 4 of the engineering mode menu. It **allows you to lock the keypad so no unauthorized use of the machine is allowed**.

**When the child lock is enabled, the console will not allow the keypad to operate unless you press and hold the Start and Display/Enter buttons for 3 seconds to unlock the console.**

To reach the setting, enter the engineering mode menu first; see `f65-2026-engineering-mode`.

Removing the safety key is the manual's other way of securing the treadmill from unauthorized use; see `f65-2026-safety-tether-cord`.
