**Do not use excessive pressure on console control keys.** They are **precision set to function properly with little finger pressure. Pushing harder is not going to make the unit go faster or slower.**

**If you feel the buttons are not functioning properly with normal pressure, contact your SOLE dealer.**

The engineering mode menu contains a **Key Test** that exercises every key; see `f65-2026-engineering-mode`.
