The console comes with **Sleep mode** offering three choices:

| Setting | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **ON** | The unit goes to an **energy-saving state after 15 minutes of inactivity**. |
| **OFF** | The console **stays lit while the power is on**. |
| **RETAIL** | The console **runs the demo video after 3 minutes of inactivity**. |

**How to switch, by display type:**

| Display | How to switch |
|---|---|
| **LED** | **Hold the Stop and Display keys for 5 seconds** to switch between ON and OFF. |
| **LCD** | **Press and hold the Stop, Enter and Display keys for 5 seconds** to switch between ON and OFF. |
| **TFT** | **Click "Sleep Mode" under Settings** to switch among the three features. |

**Only the TFT route reaches all three settings.** The LED and LCD routes are described as switching between ON and OFF only, so on those consoles the manual gives no way to select RETAIL. **The manual never says which display type this machine has.**

Sleep Mode is also an item in the engineering mode menu; see `f65-2026-engineering-mode`.
