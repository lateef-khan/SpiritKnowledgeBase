- **Incline may be adjusted anytime after belt movement.**
- Use the **incline knob** to adjust to the effect you want. You may also choose a **more rapid increase or decrease by selecting the desired key 0, 5, 10, 15, on the left hand side of the console (incline)**.
- The **incline window display indicates incline position** as adjustments are made.

Calibration sets the **maximum elevation to 15**; see `f65-2026-calibration-procedure`. Pressing Stop once drives the incline to zero percent; see `f65-2026-pause-stop-reset`.
