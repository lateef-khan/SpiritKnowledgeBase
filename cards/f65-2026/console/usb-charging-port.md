Charge your personal device during your workout using the fitness equipment's **on-console type-c USB port**. **The fitness equipment power must be on.**

1. **Connect your type-c USB charging cable to the USB power port and to your device.**
2. **Check that your device icon indicates it is charging.**

- **The USB charging cable is not included.** Make sure a compatible USB charging cable is being used.
- **Your device's "charging" icon may or may not indicate it is charging.** Depending on how much current your device wants, the icon may not come on while the device is still charging, **possibly at a lower charge rate**.
- The port **provides up to 5Vdc/1.5 amp of power**.
- **You will not be able to save your workout data to a USB via this port; it is used for charging purposes only.**
