**The exercise equipment can also play music wirelessly via Bluetooth.**

1. **Turn on your mobile phone or tablet's Bluetooth function.**
2. **Search for the name "BT-Speaker" in your device's Bluetooth menu.**
3. **Tap to connect.**
4. **Now your device can transmit music to the exercise equipment.**

**"BT-Speaker" is the exact name the manual prints**, and it is the name to look for in a phone's own Bluetooth list.

**This is a separate connection from the SOLE+ App pairing.** The app finds the machine from a search icon inside the app; the speaker is found in the phone's Bluetooth menu. See `f65-2026-sole-plus-app-pairing`.

**The manual gives no speaker specification** - no output figure, no driver count, no unpairing instruction, and nothing about what happens if two devices try to connect.
