1. **Plug the treadmill into an appropriate wall outlet.**
2. **Turn on the power switch, located at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood.**
3. **Ensure that the safety key is installed** - the treadmill will not power on without it.

When the power is turned on:

- The **speed window displays the current version of the software.**
- The **time and distance windows then display odometer readings for a short time**: the time window shows **how many hours the treadmill has been in use**, and the distance window shows **how many miles** (or kilometres if the treadmill is set to metric readings) **the treadmill has gone**.
- The treadmill then **enters idle mode**, which is the starting point for operation.

The odometer can be reset from the engineering mode menu; see `f65-2026-engineering-mode`.
