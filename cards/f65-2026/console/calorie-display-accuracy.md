The calorie window **displays the cumulative calories burned at any given time during your workout**.

**This is only a rough guide used for comparison of different exercise sessions, which cannot be used for medical purposes.**

The programming section repeats and extends the warning: **no exercise machine can give you an exact calorie count** because too many factors determine calorie burn for a particular person. **Even for someone of the exact same body weight, age and height, calorie burn may be very different from yours.** Use the calorie display **as a reference only, to monitor improvement from workout to workout**.

**Entering the correct body weight will affect the calorie count**; the manual asks for it during program setup. See `f65-2026-manual-program-setup`.
