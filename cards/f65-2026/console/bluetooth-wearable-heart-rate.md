- **The exercise equipment's Bluetooth device can also be connected to Bluetooth wearables.**
- **The Bluetooth wearables can receive signals even when the app is already in use.**
- **Wear the Bluetooth wearable first, then commence connection.**
- **While in connection, the console will show the heart rate value via Bluetooth.**

**The paragraph above is the only part of the Bluetooth material that survived text extraction**, at the top of page 17. **`pdftotext` dropped the whole of printed page 16**, the *Bluetooth Connectivity* page, so none of it is in `sources/sole-tm-f65-2026-owners-manual/text.md`. **That page was recovered by rendering the PDF page as an image and reading it**, and it carries the app pairing, cloud sync and speaker pairing instructions: see `f65-2026-sole-plus-app-pairing`, `f65-2026-fitness-cloud-sync` and `f65-2026-bluetooth-speaker-pairing`.

**Neither page names a supported-device list, a Bluetooth version, or any wearable brand.** The manual says only "Bluetooth wearables" and "the wireless Bluetooth chest strap".

The pulse window's own requirement - that you must wear the wireless Bluetooth chest strap - is stated separately on page 15; see `f65-2026-pulse-display`.
