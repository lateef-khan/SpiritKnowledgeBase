The **pulse (heart rate) window displays your current heart rate in beats per minute during the workout**. **You must wear the wireless Bluetooth chest strap to show your pulse.** The pulse value displays **anytime the upper display is receiving a pulse signal**.

**This manual states the heart rate rule in two places.** The Bluetooth page adds how the wearable connects and that it works even while the app is in use; see `f65-2026-bluetooth-wearable-heart-rate`. Neither passage mentions the other.

**No hand-pulse grips are described anywhere in this manual.** Every heart rate source it names is a wireless Bluetooth wearable. It does not say hand grips are absent - it simply never mentions them.
