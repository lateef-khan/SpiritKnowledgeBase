1. **Attach the safety key** to wake the display up, if it is not already on.
2. **Press the Start key to begin belt movement.** Use the **speed knob button** to adjust to the desired speed.
3. To slow down the tread-belt, use the **speed knob** to adjust it to the desired speed.
4. **To stop the tread-belt, press the Stop key or pull away the safety key.**

**The manual describes the speed shortcut keys twice, and not the same way.** Step 2 says *"You may also use the speed keys 3, 6, 9, 12 to adjust the speed"*; step 3 says *"You may also press the speed adjust keys, 3 through 12."* Both sentences are on the same page. The manual does not say whether there are four shortcut keys or ten.

Incline has its own set of quick keys; see `f65-2026-incline-adjustment`.
