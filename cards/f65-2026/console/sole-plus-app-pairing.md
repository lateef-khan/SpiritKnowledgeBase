**What it is.** The machine **comes equipped with a Bluetooth(R) transceiver that will allow it to interact with compatible iOS and Android smartphones or tablet computers via the SOLE+ App.** The page is headed **"New SOLE + App to be used in conjunction with select Apple & Android devices!"**

**To sync:**

1. **Download the free SOLE+ App from the Apple Store or Google Play**, and follow the instructions in the app to sync with your exercise machine.
2. **Make sure Bluetooth(R) is enabled on your device.**
3. **Tap the icon at the top right corner to search for Sole equipment.**
4. **After the equipment is detected, tap Connect.**
5. **When the app and equipment are synced, the Bluetooth(R) icon on the equipment's console will light up. You may now start using your new Sole product.**
