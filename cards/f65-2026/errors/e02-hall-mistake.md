**This is E02 (hall mistake), not E22 (communication between the upper computer and the lower controller).**

**Meaning:** hall mistake.

1. **Motor is not connected properly or not connected to the controller.**
2. **Replace the motor.**
3. **Replace the controller.**

**E02 and E04 have the identical three-step remedy in this table.** Only the meaning line differs: E02 is a hall mistake, E04 is phase loss.
