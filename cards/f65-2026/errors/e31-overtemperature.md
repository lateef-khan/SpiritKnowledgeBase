**This is E31 (overtemperature), not E3 (incline adjustment error) and not E03 (the hardware current is too large).**

**Meaning:** overtemperature.

1. **Wait until the temperature of the machine is normal before running.**

That is the whole printed remedy. The manual gives **no waiting time, no temperature threshold and no second step**.

Page 3 gives a **maximum operating temperature of 40 degrees C**; see `f65-2026-operating-environment-limits`. High belt/deck friction is the manual's usual cause of heat build-up; see `f65-2026-deck-lubrication`.
