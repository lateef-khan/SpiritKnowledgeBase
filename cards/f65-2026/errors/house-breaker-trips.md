**"House circuit breaker trips, but not the treadmill circuit breaker"**:

**Check that the treadmill is the only appliance in the circuit.** The guide refers the reader to **"Important Electrical Information" in the front of this manual** for more details.

**The section it names is not printed under that name.** The front of the manual carries **"IMPORTANT ELECTRICAL INSTRUCTIONS"** on page 3; the table of contents lists it as **"Important Electrical Information"** on page 3. That page is where the AFCI/GFCI advice, the tested breaker brands and the dedicated-circuit rule live; see `f65-2026-afci-gfci-nuisance-tripping`.

**This is the house breaker, not the treadmill's own.** For the machine's own 15 amp breaker see `f65-2026-trips-onboard-15-amp-breaker`.

**Twenty-one stencil owner's manuals print this same only-appliance row** (locator; render-verified for F80-2023, ST90-2021, ST90-2023 and TD80-2019). The TD80 titles the problem "Circuit breaker trips, but not the treadmill circuit breaker" (no "House") with the same answer. The 2016-and-earlier stencil books instead prescribe a "High In-rush current" house breaker: see `sole-treadmill-errors-house-breaker-inrush`.
