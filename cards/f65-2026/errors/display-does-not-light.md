The diagnosis guide gives five causes, in this order:

1. **Tether cord not in position.**
2. **Circuit breaker on front grill tripped. Push circuit breaker in until it locks.**
3. **Plug is disconnected. Make sure the plug is firmly pushed into the AC household wall outlet.**
4. **Household circuit breaker may be tripped.**
5. **Treadmill defect. Contact your SOLE dealer.**

The console also **goes to sleep by itself after 15 minutes of inactivity**, which is not a fault and is not listed here; see `f65-2026-turning-the-treadmill-off`.
