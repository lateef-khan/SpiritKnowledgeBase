**LUBE is not a fault code.** The error table lists it as a **reminder to check lubrication under the walking deck**.

1. **Check lubrication under the walking belt.** See `f65-2026-deck-lubrication`.
2. **Power the treadmill three times to reset the lube message.**

**The reset instruction and the engineering mode menu do not agree on how it is done.** The error table and the lubrication page both say to **power the treadmill three times**. The engineering mode menu on page 23 has a **Lube Message Reset** item that resets it from the menu. Neither passage mentions the other. See `f65-2026-engineering-mode`.

**A page reference in the error table is wrong.** It says *"Reference belt deck lubrication procedure on page 28."* **Page 28 is the error message table itself.** The lubrication procedure is on **page 26** in this copy.

The console lights the reminder **after 90 hours of use**. The lubrication section also asks you to check by hand every other month and lubricate **even though the "Lube" indicator isn't lit** if the deck is dry.
