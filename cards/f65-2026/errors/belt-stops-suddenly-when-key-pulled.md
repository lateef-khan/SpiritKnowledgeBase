**"Tread-belt stops quickly/suddenly when tether cord is pulled"** has one cause in the guide:

**High belt/deck friction. See General Maintenance section on lubrication.**

The expected behaviour is on page 4: **the treadmill will stop, depending on speed, with a one to two step coast anytime the safety key is pulled off the console.** A dead stop instead of that coast is the symptom. See `f65-2026-safety-tether-cord` and `f65-2026-deck-lubrication`.
