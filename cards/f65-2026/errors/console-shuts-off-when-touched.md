**"Computer shuts off when console is touched (on a cold day) while walking/running"**:

**The treadmill may not be grounded. Static electricity is "crashing" the computer.**

The guide refers the reader to the **Grounding Instructions on page 3**; see `f65-2026-grounding-instructions`.
