**This is E03 (the hardware current is too large), not E3 (incline adjustment error) and not E31 (overtemperature).**

**Meaning:** the hardware current is too large.

1. **Exceeding the rated load leads to excessive current, the system self-protection shows the error code, and re-power recovery.**
2. **Check whether the connection between the power socket plug and the motor is loose.**
3. **Replace the controller.**

**E03 and E01 share cause 1 word for word**, and the manual gives no test that tells them apart. **E3 is a different fault on a different part of the machine.**
