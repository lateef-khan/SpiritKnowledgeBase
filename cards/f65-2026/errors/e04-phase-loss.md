**This is E04 (phase loss), not E4 or E4x from any other Sole error table.**

**Meaning:** phase loss.

1. **Motor is not connected properly or not connected to the controller.**
2. **Replace the motor.**
3. **Replace the controller.**

**E04 and E02 have the identical three-step remedy in this table.** Only the meaning line differs: E04 is phase loss, E02 is a hall mistake.
