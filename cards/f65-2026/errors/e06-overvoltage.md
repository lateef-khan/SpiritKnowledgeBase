**This is E06 (overvoltage), not E05 (undervoltage).** They are one digit apart and have opposite causes.

**Meaning:** overvoltage.

1. **Check whether the input power voltage is normal.**

**E06 is the only code in this table with a single step**, and unlike E05 it has no instruction to check the power socket connector.
