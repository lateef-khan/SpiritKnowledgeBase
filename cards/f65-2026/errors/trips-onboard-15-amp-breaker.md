The printed cause for **"Treadmill trips on board 15 amp circuit"** is:

**High belt/deck friction. See General Maintenance**

**The printed sentence stops there.** Every other row that names General Maintenance goes on to name the subsection - "on lubrication", "on Tread-belt Tension". **This row's cross-reference is cut off in the source and no subsection is named.** The neighbouring row with the same cause points at **lubrication**; see `f65-2026-deck-lubrication`.

To reset the breaker itself: **push the circuit breaker on the front grill in until it locks** - that instruction is printed under the dark-display row, not this one. See `f65-2026-display-does-not-light`.

**This is the treadmill's own breaker, not the house breaker.** For the house breaker see `f65-2026-house-breaker-trips`.
