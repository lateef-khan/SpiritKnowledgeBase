**This is E22 (communication between the upper computer and the lower controller), not E02 (hall mistake).**

**Meaning:** the communication between the upper computer and the lower controller is incorrect.

1. **Check whether the cable between the electronic watch and the controller is loose.**
2. **Check whether the controller is damaged.**
3. **Check whether the electronic watch is damaged.**
4. **After 2 minutes of power failure, power it on again.**

**"Electronic watch" is the manual's own wording** for the console end of that link; it appears nowhere else in the manual and is reproduced unchanged.

Assembly steps 2, 3 and 4 are where the console cables are joined, and step 2 warns that **a cable pinched between the steel tubing may prevent the treadmill from operating properly**; see `f65-2026-assembly-step-2-uprights`.
