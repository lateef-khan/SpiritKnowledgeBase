Hardware: **#125** 3/8" x 3-1/2" Carriage Bolt (4 pcs), **#126** 3/8" Curved Washer (4 pcs), **#127** 3/8" Cap Nut (4 pcs).

1. Secure the Front Stabilizer (5) and the Rear Stabilizer (6) with four Carriage Bolts (125), four Curved Washers (126) and four Cap Nuts (127), by using the **14/15mm Wrench (141)**.

**#126 is named two ways in this manual.** The assembly pack checklist on page 9 and this instruction both call it a **Curved Washer**; the hardware box printed beside this instruction calls it a **Flat Washer**. Two places say curved, one says flat, and the manual never reconciles them.

Two bolts per stabilizer. The B74 half of the same manual does the same job with #23, #24 and #30 - different numbers for the same three kinds of part.
