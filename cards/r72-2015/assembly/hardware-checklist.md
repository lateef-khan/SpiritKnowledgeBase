Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #125 | 3/8" x 3-1/2" | Carriage Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #126 | 3/8" | Curved Washer | 4 pcs |
| #127 | 3/8" | Cap Nut | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #129 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 6 pcs |
| #130 | 3/8" | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #131 | 3/8" | Nyloc Nut | 6 pcs |

**Hardware step 3**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #137 | M6 x 35mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 4**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #87 | 5/16" (given as 5/16" x 12 x 1.0T in the step 4 hardware box) | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #88 | 4mm x 12mm (given as 4 x 12L in the step 4 hardware box) | Sheet Metal Screw | 3 pcs |
| #89 | 5mm x 12mm (given as M5 x 12L in the step 4 hardware box) | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| #104 | 5/16" (given as Ø8 x 1.5T in the step 4 hardware box) | Split Washer | 6 pcs |
| #123 | 5/16" x 5/8" (given as 5/16" x 18 x 5/8" in the step 4 hardware box) | Button Head Socket Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #124 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Socket Head Cap Bolt | 4 pcs |

The checklist notes: **screws 87, 89, 104, 123 and 124 are already on the mainframe.**

**#126 is named two ways.** The checklist on page 9 calls it a **Curved Washer**, and the step 1 instruction on page 12 uses "four **Curved** Washers (126)" - but the hardware box printed beside that same instruction calls #126 a **Flat Washer**. Two places say curved, one says flat; the manual never reconciles them.

**The R72 numbering does not carry over to the B74 half of the same manual.** Here #87 is a flat washer and #104 is a split washer; in the B74 section #87 is the L Allen wrench and #104 is a button head socket bolt.
