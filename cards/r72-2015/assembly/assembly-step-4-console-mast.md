Hardware: **#124** 5/16" x 3/4" Socket Head Cap Bolt (4 pcs), **#104** Ø8 x 1.5T Split Washer (6 pcs), **#87** 5/16" x 12 x 1.0T Flat Washer (6 pcs), **#89** M5 x 12L Phillips Head Screw (4 pcs), **#123** 5/16" x 18 x 5/8" Button Head Socket Bolt (2 pcs), **#88** 4 x 12L Sheet Metal Screw (3 pcs). The hardware box notes that **screws 87, 89, 104, 123 and 124 are already on the mainframe**.

1. Pull the Upper Handpulse Wire (74) and the Computer Cable (73) through the Console Mast (2) and use the Allen wrench (142) to secure it with four Socket Head Cap Bolts (124), Split Washers (104) and Flat Washers (87) each.
2. Cover with both the left and right Console Mast Covers (27, 28) and use the Phillips Head Screw Driver (140) to secure them with three Sheet Metal Screws (88).
3. Connect the Upper Handpulse Wire (74) and the Computer Cable (73) with the Console (71) and use the Phillips Head Screw Driver (140) to secure the Console Mast (2) with four Phillips Head Screws (89).
4. Use the M5 Allen Wrench (140) to tighten the Mast Handle bar Assembly (9) and the Console Mast (2) together with two Button Head Socket Bolts (123), Split Washers (104) and Flat Washers (87) each.

**Be very careful during this step to make sure the cables do not become pinched between the parts. This could cause the wires to short, causing damage to the electronics.**

Six #104 split washers and six #87 flat washers: four used in instruction 1, two in instruction 4.

**Instruction 4 calls #140 an "M5 Allen Wrench"** while the tool list and instructions 2 and 3 call the same item the "Combination 5mm Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver". It is one tool with two heads.

**The B74 half of this manual uses these numbers for other things** - there #87 is the L Allen wrench and #104 is a button head socket bolt.
