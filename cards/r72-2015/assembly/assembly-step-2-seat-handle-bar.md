Hardware: **#129** 3/8" x 1-3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt (6 pcs), **#130** 3/8" Flat Washer (6 pcs), **#131** 3/8" Nyloc Nut (6 pcs).

1. Install the Seat Handle Bar (7) on the sliding seat carriage by using the Allen Wrench (142) to tighten **four** Button Head Socket Bolts (129), going through the Seat Handle Bar (7) and four Flat Washers (130), then use the **14/15mm Wrench (141)** to tighten four Nyloc Nuts (131).
2. Install the Seat Back Bracket (3) on the Seat Handle Bar (7) by using the Allen Wrench (142) to tighten **two** Button Head Socket Bolts (129), going through the **Bolt Washer (8)** and two Flat Washers (130), then use the 14/15mm Wrench (141) to tighten two Nyloc Nuts (131).
3. Connect the Lower and Upper handpulse wires (75, 74).

Four bolts for the handle bar plus two for the seat back bracket accounts for all six of each item in the bag.

**The R72 uses button head socket bolts and an Allen wrench here.** The R92 and LCR recumbents use two hex head bolts of two different lengths (#71 and #175) with two open-ended wrenches for the same joint. There is no interchange of parts or method.

The **Bolt Washer (8)** named in instruction 2 is not in the assembly pack checklist and arrives on the machine.
