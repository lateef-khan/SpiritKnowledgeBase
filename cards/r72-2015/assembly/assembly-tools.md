| Item | Tool |
|---|---|
| #140 | Combination 5mm Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver |
| #141 | 14/15mm Wrench |
| #142 | L Allen Wrench |

Three tools. There is no 13/15mm wrench in the R72 pack; the B74 half of the same manual has one (#79).

**Item numbers collide between the two halves of this manual.** In the B74 section #87 is the L Allen wrench; in the R72 section #87 is a 5/16" flat washer, and #104 is a split washer in the R72 pack but a button head socket bolt in the B74 pack.

**Tool item numbers are not shared between models.** The same `#` is a different part on another Sole bike, so use the list printed in your own machine's manual.
