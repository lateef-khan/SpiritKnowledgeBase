The drawing groups the leaders in pairs (6,7; 8,9; 10,11; 13,14,15; 16,17; 20,21) and boxes two
details: **Detailed drawing W** at the mast, showing the Brake Lever (19) and its cover, and
**Detailed drawing X** at the seat post, showing the Handgrip Fixing Plate (18) and the seat end caps.
The *Handgrip Stabilizer Assemblies (20, 21)* are the two swing-out handgrips on the mast, held by
eight M6 screws the preventive-maintenance chapter checks; the *Handgrip Fixing Plate (18)* is what
locks the seat inner slide.

**No part numbers, quantities or dimensions are on this page.** The recumbent 8.5R numbers fourteen
components under different names (`85r-2025-specs-component-description-fourteen-numbered-parts`).
