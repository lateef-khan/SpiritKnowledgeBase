**Neither book decodes the 8.0U's drawing.** The owner's manual prints one exploded-view page - a drawing stamped `2025.12.08` whose balloons run to 190 with lettered positions `3-1`, `3-2`, `12-L`, `12-R`, `15-A/B/C`, `45-1`, `45-2` and seven tools drawn as 159 to 165 - and goes straight to the warranty. The service manual (MU2000-SB036-01) prints the same drawing, stamped `MU2000-SB19-01 2025.06.12`, and then a seven-page list **that uses a different numbering**: in the list item 3 is a `Fixing Plate`, 4 a `Brake Lever`, 5 an `Upright Brake Shaft`, 7 `Pedal(L.R)`, 8L / 8R the crank arms and 24 the crank axle, where the drawing shows 3-1 / 3-2 as the handlebars, 5 as the seat post, 8 as the saddle, 12-L / 12-R as the pedals and 45 as the crank axle. Only the console and cable block (70 to 95) and some of the hardware numbers coincide.

**The list is the 8.5R recumbent's.** Row for row it is the list printed in the 8.5R service manual (`85r-2025-specs-parts-list`) - the same 1-170 drive-frame series and the same 501-618 rotating-seat series, seat rotation, rail assembly, gas cylinder and twenty-seven nylon wheels included, on a bike that has a fixed seat post. It differs from the 8.5R list in these rows only:

**Five rows carry quantity 0 in the 8.0U copy** - 20 `Latch Spring`, 82 `300L_Ground Wire`, 105 `M8 × 30mm_Flat head countersink Bolt`, 164 and 165 - which reads as the shared list marking recumbent-only parts the upright does not use, while the 501-618 seat rows keep their quantities.

## What to tell a caller

A balloon number read off the 8.0U drawing **cannot be turned into a part from either book**. Go to Spirit Fitness with the serial number and the number, and say which drawing it came from. Do not quote the service manual's list for the upright - item 4 is not a brake lever on the drawing, and items 501 to 618 are a seat the machine does not have. The parts the drawing does share with the 8.5R - console 70, interface board 71, cables 72 to 95, AC module 76, power supply 81, hall module 84, flywheel 86, generator/brake controller 91 - can be named from the 8.5R list with that caveat.

The 8.5UE prints its drawings without a list in the owner's manual and the list in the service manual (`85ue-2025-specs-parts-list`); the 8.5R does the same. The 8.0U is the one machine of the three whose service-manual list is not its own.
