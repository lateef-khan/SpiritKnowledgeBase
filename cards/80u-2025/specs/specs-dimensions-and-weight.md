| Figure | Printed |
|---|---|
| **Length** | **197 cm (77.6")** |
| **Width** | **85 cm (33.5")** |
| **Height** | **118.2 cm (46.5")** |
| **Product weight** | **64.6 kgs (142.4 lbs)** |

The four figures convert correctly between units, and the render agrees with the text layer.

**The shape of the figures does not fit the machine.** An upright bike with a mast-mounted
touchscreen is taller than it is long - the 7.0U upright is 110 cm long and 138 cm high
(`70u-2025-specs-dimensions-and-weight`) - and 197 cm long by 118 cm high is a recumbent's
footprint; the 8.5R recumbent on the same platform is 171 x 83 x 127 cm
(`85r-2025-specs-dimensions-and-weight`). The weight, 64.6 kg, is on the other hand far under the
recumbent's 108.9 kg and in line with an upright. Nothing else in the book gives a dimension to check
against. **Quote these as the printed figures and say the length and height look transposed or
copied from another page; get a confirmed footprint from Spirit Fitness before a doorway or a floor
plan depends on it.**

No shipping weight or carton size is printed anywhere in the book.
