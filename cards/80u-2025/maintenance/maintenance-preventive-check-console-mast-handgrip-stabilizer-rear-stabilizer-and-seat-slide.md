**Four structural checks with the fastener counts printed, and no interval for any of them.** The 8.0U is the
one medical bike whose service manual names how many screws hold each assembly.

| Check | As printed | Fasteners named |
|---|---|---|
| 3.2 Console mast | check the 8 screws on the console mast; if loose, tighten them up - "resolve Console Mast sway issue" (figure 3.2.1) | **M8 × 6 and M10 × 2** |
| 3.3 Handgrip stabilizer assembly | check the 8 screws; if loose, tighten (figures 3.3.1, 3.3.2) | **M6 × 8** |
| 3.4 Rear stabilizer | check the 2 screws secured on the rear stabilizer; if loose, tighten (figure 3.4.1) | **M12 × 2** |
| 3.5 Handgrip fixing plate and seat front-rear adjusting | **press the handgrip fixing plate upward and check that the seat inner slide holds without falling**; then **pull the quick-release lever up and tighten it at the proper position** so the seat front-rear adjustment neither wobbles nor moves (figures 3.5.1-3.5.3) | the quick-release lever |

No torque is printed for any of them. The pedals and crank arms that open the same chapter (3.1) are the
"regularly" check with the two pedal directions - `cu900ent-check-pedals`.

## What a loose one leads to

The troubleshooting chapter sends each symptom back here or to a replacement step: a shaking handgrip to 6.4, a
wobbling seat to **3.5**, a wobbling whole bike to the adjustment pads
(`spirit-med-bike-maintenance-sway-handgrip-seat-and-whole-bike`).

**The 8.5R is not this list.** Its console mast is held by three M8 socket head cap bolts, it has no handgrip
stabilizer or rear-stabilizer item, and its chapter checks the emergency-stop screw and the rotating console
structure instead (`85r-2025-maintenance-preventive-check-console-mast-bolts-and-rotating-structure-o-ring`). The
CR900 books check the mast screws whenever the console shakes (`spirit-cr900-maintenance-console-mast-screws`);
the CU900ENT's 3.2 excludes a shaking vertical tube from the mast screws altogether
(`cu900ent-check-console-mast`).

The owner's manual levels the bike on **two pads under each of the front and rear stabilizers** with a 14 mm
wrench (`spirit-85s-maintenance-pedals-and-front-and-rear-stabiliser-pads`); 3.4 above is the stabilizer's own
two bolts, not the pads.
