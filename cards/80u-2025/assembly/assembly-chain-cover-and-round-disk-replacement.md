**The covers are held by hidden clips as well as screws, and the clips break if forced.**

1. As shown in figures 6.6.1 and 6.6.2, remove the **Phillips head screws (M5 x 2 pcs)** and **unlock the hidden clips with flathead screwdrivers**. *(Please be gentle - do not apply too much force or the clips break.)*
2. Then remove the **sheet metal screws (M5 x 2 pcs)** and the **Phillips head screws (M5 x 2 pcs)** to remove the **chain cover (R)**.
3. As shown in figure 6.6.3, remove the **sheet metal screw and flat washer** to remove the **chain cover (L)**.
4. As shown in figure 6.6.4, refer to 6.3 to take the **crank arm** off first, and remove the **round disk** to replace it.

No screw lengths are printed. The parts list carries **Chain Cover (L) (37)**, **Chain Cover (R) (38)** and **Round Disk (41), 2 pcs**. The 8.5R recumbent's covers come off four screws a side and the same hidden clips, with the crank arms off before anything else; that is its own card.
