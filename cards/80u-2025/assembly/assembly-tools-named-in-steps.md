Seven tools, named only inside the steps:

| Item | Tool | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| 164 | 6 mm Allen wrench | step 1, console mast side bolts |
| 165 | 8 mm Allen wrench | step 1, console mast front bolts |
| 159 | Phillips screwdriver | steps 1, 3 and 4 |
| 162 | 19 mm wrench | step 1, rear stabilizer |
| 161 | open wrench | step 2, pedals |
| 163 | L Allen wrench | step 2, seat bolt |
| 160 | 5 mm L Allen wrench | step 3, handlebars |

Each step page also draws the tools it uses beside their item numbers.

## There is no TOOLS panel and no PARTS INCLUDED panel

The pre-assembly page says **"Locate the hardware package. Remove the tools first"** and then never lists a tool, and the book has no carton checklist of any kind - its contents run Parts of your unit, Assembly Instructions, Setting up your bike. So the manual never states that these seven are supplied; it tells you to take them out of the hardware package and names seven. **Item 161 has no size** - the pedals it drives take a 15 mm flat, which the book never says - and item 163 has no size either; its M3 seat bolt would take a small key. The 19 mm wrench for the two M12 stabilizer bolts is a size no other Spirit bike carton names.

**A 14mm is not among them**, and the machine care page asks for one on the levellers (the setting-up page asks for a 1/2" wrench for the same nut); the service manual's parts list carries a 13/14 mm wrench (612) that the owner's manual never mentions. The 8.5R recumbent names its own seven tools with different numbers; the 8.5UE ergometer names five.
