As shown in figure 6.15.1, **remove the hex head bolts (M12 x 2 pcs)** to replace the rear stabilizer.

The owner's manual's step one fits the same stabilizer (4) with **two M12 x 20L bolts (148) and two M12 split washers (149) on a 19 mm wrench (162)**, and the preventative-maintenance page says to check those two M12 screws for looseness and tighten them if the rear stabilizer moves. No torque is printed. The stabilizer carries the adjustment feet and transportation wheels, which have their own card.
