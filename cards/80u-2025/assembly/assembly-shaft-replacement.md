1. As shown in figure 6.13.1, remove the **socket head cap bolts (M8 x 3 pcs)**. Loosen the **steel cable** as in figure 6.13.2.
2. Then loosen the **socket head cap bolt (M8 x 1 pc)** and the **nylon nut (M8 x 1 pc)** as in 6.13.3 to replace the shaft.

The part is the **Upright Brake Shaft (5)** of the parts list, which carries the **Brake Lever (4)**, the **Brake Pad (18)** and the **Steel Cable (6)** with it; the three bolts are among the **M8 x 50 mm socket head cap bolts (145)** and the nut an **M8 x P1.25 x 6T nylon nut (124)**. No torque is printed. The 8.5R has no shaft procedure.
