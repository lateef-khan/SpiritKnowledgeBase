Four tables, one per step, each headed No. / Item / Description / Qty.

## Step one

| No. | Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 123 | Split washer | Ø10 x Ø18.1 x 2T | 2 |
| 145 | Bolt | M8 x 75mm | 6 |
| 146 | Split washer | Ø8.5 x 1.5T | 6 |
| 124 | Flat washer | Ø8.5 x Ø18 x 1.5T | 6 |
| 147 | Bolt | M10 x 65mm | 2 |
| 167 | Flat washer | Ø10 x Ø25 x 3T | 2 |
| 148 | Bolt | M12 x 20L | 2 |
| 149 | Split washer | M12 | 2 |
| 140 | Sheet metal screw | 3.5 x 12mm | 2 |

Six M8 bolts is two a side on three sides of the mast, vertical; the two M10 bolts go in horizontally at the inner front.

## Step two

| No. | Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 139 | Bolt | M3 x 10mm | 1 |

**The seat bolt is printed as M3 x 10 mm.** As printed; a 3 mm bolt through the pin kit (11), tightened "firmly" with the L Allen wrench (163).

## Step three

| No. | Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 151 | Bolt | M6 x 12mm | 8 |
| 152 | Split washer | M6 x 1T | 8 |
| 140 | Sheet metal screw | 3.5 x 12mm | 2 |

## Step four

| No. | Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 153 | Screw | M5 x 10 mm | 8 |
| 141 | Screw | M4 x 8 mm | 2 |

Eight of item 153: four for the console, two for the bottle holder clamp, two for the holder. **Only the pedals get a torque figure (300 in-lb, 34 Nm)** - the tables print none for any bolt. The service manual's preventative-maintenance page calls the mast joint "8 screws (M8 x 6 pcs and M10 x 2 pcs)" and the handlebar joint "8 screws (M6 x 8 pcs)", the same fasteners.
