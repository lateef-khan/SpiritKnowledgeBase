**A lever, not a knob: the seat post locks itself at each position when the lever is released.** The 4.0U and 7.0U use a knob with a spring-loaded pin; that is a different card.

**Seat height.** To raise: hold the **seat height adjustment lever** and lift up from under the seat. The seat post will automatically lock at each position after you release the lever. To lower: hold the lever and hold under the seat; lower the seat position to just below the desired setting, then release the lever - the seat post locks at each position after the lever is released. There is a **numbered scale on the seat post, under the seat**, for repeatable settings.

**Seat fore/aft.** **Loosen the handle** and slide the seat to the desired position, then **tighten the handle**. There is a numbered scale on the seat slide, under the rear of the seat, for repeatable settings (the book prints "under the rear of the eat").

The service manual's preventative-maintenance page is the other half of this: press the handgrip fixing plate upward to check that the seat inner slide holds without falling, and **pull the quick-release lever up and tighten it at the proper position** to stop the fore/aft adjustment wobbling - its cure for a seat that wobbles in use. The height feature is what the operating page calls "the up/down seat height positioning adjustment"; the seat bolt, pin kit and slide are replaced on the service-manual card.
