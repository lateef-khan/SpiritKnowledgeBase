As shown in figure 6.2.1, **remove the sheet metal screws (M5 x 2 pcs)** and then fit the new console mast cover.

That is the whole procedure. The handgrip stabilizer procedure begins here ("refer to 6.2 to remove the console mast cover"). The component drawing names a **Console Mast Cover (4)**, a **Console Mast Cover-A (6)** and a **Console Mast Cover-B (7)**; the procedure does not say which of the three it means, and the parts list carries one **Console Mast Cover (36)**.
