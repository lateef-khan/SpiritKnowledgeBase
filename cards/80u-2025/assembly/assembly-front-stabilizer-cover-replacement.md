As shown in figure 6.5.1, **remove the sheet metal screws (M5 x 2 pcs)** and then fit the new front stabilizer cover.

That is the whole procedure. The component drawing names a **Front Stabilizer Cover-A (8)** and **-B (9)**; the procedure does not say whether the two screws hold one half or both. The owner's manual fits the **front and back bottom covers (61 and 62)** onto the console mast with two 3.5 x 12 mm sheet metal screws (140) in its step one, and the top covers (59 and 60) with two more in step three.
