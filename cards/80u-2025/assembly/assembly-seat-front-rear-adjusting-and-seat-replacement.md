1. As shown in figures 6.11.1 and 6.11.2, remove the **socket head cap bolt** with an **Allen wrench**, take out the **bottom seat end cap** at the rear side, and then remove the **seat front rear adjusting** unit.
2. As shown in figure 6.11.3, **loosen the nuts from both sides** with an **open-end wrench** to remove the seat.

No bolt or nut sizes are printed. The parts list carries **Seat Front Read Adjusting (533), 2 pcs** (printed "Read"), a **Bottom Seat End Cap (17)** and a **Top Seat End Cap (16)** on the component drawing, and the seat as item 538. The owner's manual installs the seat onto the seat post with one **bolt (139), M3 x 10 mm**, through the pin kit (11) with the L Allen wrench (163) - a figure printed as M3 in that book. The preventative-maintenance page cures a wobbling fore/aft adjustment by pulling the quick-release lever up and tightening it at the proper position; the troubleshooting chapter sends a wobbling seat there, not here.
