As shown in figures 6.4.1 and 6.4.2, refer to 6.2 to remove the console mast cover. Then remove the **socket head cap bolts, 4 pcs on each side**, to remove the handgrip stabilizer assembly. **Hold the handgrip stabilizer assembly carefully to avoid danger** - it is loose once the bolts are out.

No bolt size is printed here, but the preventative-maintenance page checks the same joint as **8 screws (M6 x 8 pcs)** and says a shaking handgrip stabilizer assembly is cured by tightening them; the troubleshooting chapter sends a shaking assembly back to this section. The owner's manual's step three fits the left and right handlebars with **four M6 x 12 mm bolts (151) and split washers (152) a side on a 5 mm L Allen wrench** - the same eight bolts.
