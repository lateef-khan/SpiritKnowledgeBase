**Left and right handlebar covers (6.7).** As shown in figures 6.7.1 and 6.7.2, remove the **button head socket bolt, 1 pc on each side**, with an **Allen wrench** to take off the handlebar covers (L and R).

**Handlebar cover (6.8).**

1. As shown in figures 6.8.1 and 6.8.2, remove the **Phillips head screws (M5 x 2 pcs)**.
2. As shown in figure 6.8.3, remove the **sheet metal screw (M5 x 1 pc)** at the top of the **fixing plate**.
3. As shown in figures 6.8.4 and 6.8.5, **press the brake lever down and push the fixing plate upward**, and **rotate it 90 degrees** as in 6.8.6; remove the sheet metal screw at the other side of the fixing plate (M5 x 1 pc) to remove the handlebar cover.

The fixing plate is the **Handgrip Fixing Plate (18)** of the component drawing and the **Fixing Plate (3)** of the parts list; the preventative-maintenance page presses the same plate upward to check that the seat inner slide holds. The parts list carries **Handlebar Cover (L)** and **(R)** as items 10 and 11 of the component drawing and the centre cover as item 1. No screw lengths are printed.
