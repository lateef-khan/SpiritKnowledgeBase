The service checklist's answer, word for word:

> This indicates motor should be receiving power to operate. Low AC voltage to treadmill. Do not use an extension cord. If an extension cord is required it should be as short as possible and heavy duty 16 gauge minimum. Low household voltage. Contact an electrician or your dealer. A minimum of 120 volt AC current, 60 hz is required.

Two numbers to hold on to: **16 gauge minimum** for an extension cord and **120 volt AC at 60 Hz minimum** at the outlet.

**The CT800 2016 owner's manual prints the same row against 110 volt AC** (`ct900ent-speed-display-mismatch-caps-at-7mph`), and **the CT850 2016 service manual, otherwise the same book, prints 12 gauge and 110 volt at 60 Hz** (`ct850-2016-only-reaches-7-mph`). Nothing reconciles the three. The CT800 2020 family prints 100 volt with `/10 kph` (`ct850-2020-only-reaches-7-mph`).
