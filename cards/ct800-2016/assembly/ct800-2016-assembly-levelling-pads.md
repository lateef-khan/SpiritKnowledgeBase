There are **2 levelling pads on the bottom of the rear stabiliser**. Use a **14mm
wrench** (or an adjustable wrench) to adjust the levellers until the unit sits
properly level.

**This manual prints the instruction in one place only, and not in the assembly
chapter.** It appears as the third remedy in the general-maintenance item about a
squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling - the manual's advice is to check the
hardware first, then the levelling. The noise fault itself is a maintenance
question and has its own card; what is here is the answer to "how do I level it".

**The next-generation CT800 gives a different answer** - pads on the bottom of the
feet, no pad count and no wrench size - and the 2012 and CT800ENT manuals give
none at all. Do not carry the 14mm figure across; see the linked cards.
