There are **2 levelling pads on the bottom of the rear stabiliser**. Use a **14mm
wrench** (or an adjustable wrench) to adjust the levellers until the unit sits
properly level.

**Both manuals print the instruction in one place only, and not in the assembly
chapter.** It appears as the third remedy in the general-maintenance item about a
squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling - the advice is to check the hardware
first, then the levelling. The noise fault itself is a maintenance question and has
its own card; what is here is the answer to "how do I level it".

The CT800 2016 and CT850 2018 manuals print the sentence word for word, and both
put it in the same place in the same three-part item.

**The other manuals on this platform give a different answer or none at all.** The
CT800 2020 and the CT850 2016 and 2020 say pads on the bottom of the feet, with no
pad count and no wrench size; the CT800 2012, the CT800ENT and the CT850ENT say
nothing about levelling at all. Do not carry the 14mm figure across; see the linked
cards.
