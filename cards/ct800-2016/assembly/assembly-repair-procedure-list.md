Chapter 11, "Repair Procedures", is the Spirit dealer-style chapter - twelve short numbered procedures, not
the Dyaco "Disassembling and Assembling of Parts" chapter that the CT850 2016 book carries. **This book has no
Dyaco-format replacement chapter at all**: no motor-base-cap screw counts, no roller or deck procedure, no
speed-sensor replacement, no AC power switch procedure.

| Procedure | Title | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Calibrating the unit | console |
| 2 | Checking wire harness for continuity | test |
| 3 | Speed sensor adjustment | adjustment |
| 4 | Check roller for magnet | test |
| 5 | Adjust torque boost | adjustment |
| **6** | **Replacing controller** | part replacement |
| **7** | **Replacing wire harness** | part replacement |
| **8** | **Replacing drive motor** | part replacement |
| **9** | **Replacing incline motor** | part replacement, steps numbered 10.1-10.9 |
| 10 | Belt Checking | adjustment |
| 11 | Tighten Drive Motor | adjustment |
| 12 | Grease Incline Motor Bolt | care |

The four replacement procedures have their own cards. The others belong to the console, maintenance and
troubleshooting cards for this machine.

The chapter is the XT dealer manual's chapter edited down: the XT book has thirteen procedures including a
circuit-breaker check, a controller diagram and a wiring diagram, which this book moves elsewhere or drops.
The edit left two traces - the incline motor steps still carry the XT numbering 10.x, and the drive motor
procedure still speaks of red and black motor wires on a machine whose motor has three.
