**Eight steps, no tension bolt, no belt-tension figure.** The XT dealer manual's version of this procedure has
ten steps, loosens a tension bolt and sets "about an inch of play"; the CT850 books' version sets the belt to
70~75 lbs with a gauge. This book prints neither.

1. Turn the power switch off.
2. Disconnect the **red and black motor wires** from the controller.
3. Take off the drive belt.
4. Insert the new motor into place.
5. Put the drive belt on.
6. Put in the **four motor bolts**. Do not tighten them all the way down.
7. Tighten down the four motor bolts.
8. Plug the motor wires into the controller.

Two things to know before you follow it:

- **"Red and black motor wires" is DC wording on an AC machine.** This book is titled "Treadmill Service Manual
  (AC 120V)", its wiring chapter is built around an inverter board, and the CT850 2016 book for the same
  platform labels the motor leads **U, V and W** with a green-yellow ground. The eight lines are the XT dealer text carried
  over unchanged. Identify the leads from the machine, not from this procedure.
- Steps 6 and 7 as printed tell you to leave the bolts loose and then tighten them, with nothing in between.
  In the XT and CT850 versions the thing in between is setting the belt tension.
