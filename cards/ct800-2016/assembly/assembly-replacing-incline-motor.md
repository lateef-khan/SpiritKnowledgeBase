**No zeroing distance is printed. The motor finds its own zero: you fit it with the tube bolt out, power the
machine on, and let the motor run to zero before the bolt goes in.** The CT850 2020 book's version of this
job zeroes the motor to 235mm by hand instead; that is a different procedure for a different book.

1. Turn the power switch off.
2. Turn the treadmill onto its side.
3. Disconnect the motor wires from the controller.
4. Remove the bolt from the incline tube.
5. Remove the bolt from the top of the incline motor and take out the motor.
6. Insert the new motor and put in the bolt on the top.
7. Reconnect the motor wires to the controller.
8. Turn on the unit. The new motor should turn until it is at "zero".
9. Insert the bolt into the incline tube.

The heading reads **PROCEDURE 9** but the nine steps under it are numbered **10.1 to 10.9** - the numbering
was copied from the XT dealer manual, where the incline motor is Procedure 10. The XT version also has a step
this one lacks: "if possible, fold up unit". The CT800 does not fold, so the step was dropped and the numbering
was not.

No wrench size, bolt size or torque is printed.
