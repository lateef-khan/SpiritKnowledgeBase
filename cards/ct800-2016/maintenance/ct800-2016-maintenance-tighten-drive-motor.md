Procedure 11 is two lines long:

1. Remove the motor top cover and check if the motor screws are loose.
2. Tighten.

The book names no torque, no wrench and no screw count for this check. Its motor replacement procedure (Procedure 8) speaks of **four motor bolts**, which are the screws meant here.

## When the manual sends you here

The Mechanical System troubleshooting row for **drive motor noise** runs: check the wires around the motor for rubbing, remove, clean and re-insert the motor brushes, replace the motor. A loose motor is the mechanical cause this procedure rules out before the brushes are touched; the brushes and the replacement are carded with the troubleshooting rows.

**Unplug the treadmill before removing the cover.** The book's own safety page says to disconnect from the outlet before any cleaning or service work; Procedure 11 does not repeat it.
