**This is the service manual's four-step check, at 0.5 mph from the left rear roller. The owner's manual's tracking procedure for the same machine runs at 3 mph with an 8 mm Allen wrench — `ct900ent-treadbelt-tracking-adjustment`.**

1. Check the belt to see if it touches the belt guide.
2. If the belt touches the belt guide, turn on the unit power and press the START button on the console. Let it run at the **lowest speed, 0.5 mph**.
3. To bring the belt to the centre, use an Allen wrench to turn the **left rear roller adjustment clockwise. No more than 1/4 of a turn.**
4. When the belt is in the centre, there will be no more belt noise.

The book gives no wrench size and does not say what to do if the belt touches the guide on the other side; it names only a clockwise turn of the left bolt. The owner's manual covers both directions.

## Why this is a noise procedure

Procedure 10 is where the Mechanical System troubleshooting row for **belt noise** sends you: check whether the belt needs tracking, take off the motor hood to be sure the noise is from the belt, check the belt and deck for damage. A belt riding on its guide is the first thing that row means.

## This is the only belt-care text in the book

The CT800 2016 service manual's General Maintenance chapter is a bare title page — `spirit-ct-maintenance-no-general-maintenance-chapter-in-service-manual`. Procedures 10 to 12 are all it prints on belt and motor care; cleaning, deck life and tension are in the owner's manual only.
