1. Adjust the incline level to the **highest** setting.
2. Remove the motor cover. Check the bushing and bolt of the incline motor.
3. If the bushing or bolt of the incline motor is dirty or dry, **clean it and then grease it**.

The book does not name a grease, an interval or a symptom that calls for this; Procedure 12 stands on its own at the end of the repair procedures. Raising the incline first exposes the bolt and bushing the motor works against.

**This is the incline motor, not the deck.** The CT800 2016 owner's manual says no lubricant or wax goes on the deck surface; greasing here is confined to the incline motor's bushing and bolt. The 7.0T/MT200 service manual puts the same task on a monthly checklist as "lubricate lead screw of incline motor as needed" (`mt200-2022-maintenance-monthly-checklist`); the CT800 2016 book gives it no schedule.
