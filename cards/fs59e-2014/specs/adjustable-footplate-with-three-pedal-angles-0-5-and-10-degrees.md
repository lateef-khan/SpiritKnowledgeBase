**Three angles - 0, 5 and 10 degrees - chosen with a pull-pin under each footpad.** "The foot pedals are adjustable
to meet the users preference of pedal angle. There are three angles available with a simple pull-pin adjustment
located under the footpads. The lowest position will set the footpads to zero (0) degrees at the bottom the
elliptical stroke. The second position sets the pedal angle at five (5) degrees and the third position sets the
pedal angle at ten (10) degrees."

Why: "Some individuals get a numb foot from the compression of the nerves under your foot during an elliptical
workouts and some individuals have problems with flexibility in their Achilles tendons." And what it changes: "The
higher the angle, the more you emphasize the quadriceps. The lower the angle, the more you emphasize the hamstrings
and glutes."

The FS5.8e, the sister book from the year before, has the same power incline but does not print an adjustable
footplate. Stride length is not printed on either.
