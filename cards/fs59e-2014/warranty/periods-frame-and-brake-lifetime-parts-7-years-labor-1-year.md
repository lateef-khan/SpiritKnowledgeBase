| Frame & Brake | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|
| Lifetime | 7 Years | 1 Year |

**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the Manufacturer's Limited Warranty page of the FS5.9e book, headed *Effective August 1, 2012 - ELLIPTICAL LIMITED WARRANTY*. The book is a scan stamped `FS59e_20140214` (*© 2014* on the back cover); the page was read from a 300 dpi render. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**Seven years on parts is the longest parts term on any Xterra page in this knowledge base.** The SB4.5r, effective the same day, prints five (`sb45r-2013-warranty-periods-frame-and-brake-lifetime-parts-5-years-labor-1-year`); the SB500, SB4500 and RSX1500 three (`xterra-warranty-periods-frame-and-brake-lifetime-parts-3-years-labor-1-year`); every later elliptical one year (`xterra-warranty-periods-frame-1-year-parts-1-year-labor-none`, `xterra-warranty-periods-frame-5-years-parts-1-year-no-labor-column`).

**The FS5.8e, this machine's sibling, has no warranty page in the copy this knowledge base holds** - its scan stops at printed page 22 (`fs58e-2013-warranty-page-not-in-the-scanned-manual`). Do not lend it these figures: the FS5.8e book's contents page promises a warranty section, but nothing in the file says what it contained.

This page prints the registration card duty, the nine-item exclusion list, the full SERVICE steps with the trip charge and the 48-states sentence (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-store-display-and-unregistered-products`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-service-steps-dealer-service-organization-then-the-service-department`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-owner-pays-freight-parts-shipping-and-trip-charges`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-48-contiguous-states-not-alaska-or-hawaii`); the electrical page adds the ten-percent voltage sentence (`xterra-warranty-what-the-electrical-and-console-pages-say-is-not-covered`). The Spirit ellipticals' residential table is `spirit-ce-warranty-residential-periods`.
