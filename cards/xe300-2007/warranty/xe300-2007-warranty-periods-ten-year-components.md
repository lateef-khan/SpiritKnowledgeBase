**The XE300 owner's manual covers three machines and the table inside it has two columns. This is the right-hand one - and the 2008 dealer service manual puts the XE400 and XE500 in the same column.**

| | XE300 |
|---|---|
| Labor | 1 Year |
| Frame | Lifetime |
| All Other Components | **10 Years** |
| Brake | Lifetime |

Printed above it: **Effective October 15, 2007**.

**The dealer service manual's column is headed XE300 - XE400 - XE500** and reads Frame Lifetime, Brake Lifetime, **All Parts 10 years**, Labor 1 year (printed "1 years") - the same four figures under the name *All Parts*, with no effective date. That is the only place the XE400 and XE500 periods are printed; neither has an owner's manual in this knowledge base. The base term the column sits on - five years from retail sale or eighteen months from factory shipping, whichever comes first - is on `spirit-xt-2007-warranty-five-years-or-eighteen-months-from-shipping`.

**Only one figure separates these machines from the XE100 and XE200 in the same books.** Their column reads *All Other Components* **5 Years**. Labor, Frame and Brake are identical on all three. Quoting `spirit-xe-2007-warranty-periods-five-year-components` for an XE300, XE400 or XE500 would halve the component period.

**The column is called *All Other Components*, not *Parts*.** The manual names four rows and nothing else, so it states no separate period for a console, a belt or any other named assembly.

The paragraph above the table says the periods run from the date of retail sale as determined by sale receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date in the absence of a receipt, **whichever comes first** - see `xt-2023-warranty-start-date`.

This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household use, and the front of the manual warns that any other application voids it in its entirety (`spirit-xe-warranty-use-environment`). It is honoured in the **50 states of the U.S.A.** and the manual names nowhere else (`spirit-xe-warranty-geographic-fifty-states`).
