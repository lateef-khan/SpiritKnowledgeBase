`Adjustable pedal not staying in the desired position (XE300)`
1. Order and install new threaded bushing and crank pin.

**That is the whole row** - two parts, no adjustment. It is printed for the XE300 alone, the one machine of the five whose pedal adjusts with a knob and a crank pin rather than a pull pin: the XE300 parts list carries a **Locking Pin Assembly - Crank Pin (006036)**, a **Threaded Bushing (006037)** and a **Pedal Adjustment Handlebar Knob (006107)**, while the XE100, XE200 and XE400 list a **Pedal Adjust Pull Pin (006002)**. The XE500 lists the crank-pin assembly and threaded bushing too but is not named on the row.

The 2016 XE books have no equivalent row; their pedals are fixed or adjust with a locking pin they replace as an assembly step.
