- **Item 152 is printed with seven digits, `0030048`**, for the 5/16"×2-1/2" hex head screw; the XE400 and XE500 lists print the same screw as 030048. Kept as printed.
- Item 2, the Rail Assembly, has no number on this list (the XE100 and XE200 give it 004020). Items 82 (J Bolt) and 98 (Drink Bottle Assembly) have none either.
- Items 46~1 and 46~2 carry the change note `Starting w/#1361 use #006163 (38x380)` - from serial 1361 the 420 mm handgrip foam is replaced by the 380 mm 006163 (right, 006164).
- Item 117, the Handle Switch Assembly, reads *See Below for individual part #'s*: the overlay (006011), the switch (006012) and the plastic housing (006070) are the unnumbered rows at the end, with the Hardware Kit (006044), the Pedal Elevation Gauge Decal (006118) and the "Up/Down" pedal sticker (006106).
- Item 37 is the only adaptor on the five lists rated in the description: `9V,DV,2.0A_Adaptor - 2.0 Amp`, 000138; the XE100 and XE200 use the 1.5 A adaptor 000137.

**Order against the serial number.** The same book's parts purchase order treats a line without a serial number as a stock order (`spirit-xt-2007-policy-parts-purchase-order-form`); the owner's manual for this machine prints drawings but no list at all (`spirit-ce-assembly-exploded-view-and-parts-list`), so this is the only place its part numbers are printed. Which elliptical service manuals print a list is on `spirit-elliptical-specs-parts-which-service-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.
