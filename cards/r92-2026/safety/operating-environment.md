**NEVER expose this fitness bike to rain or moisture.** The product is **NOT designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment**.

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| **Operating temperature** | **40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit** |
| **Humidity** | **95% non-condensing** (no water drops forming on surfaces) |

**This is not the same limit as the warranty's.** The warranty is voided outright if the unit is not kept in a climate-controlled, insulated environment, and it names a garage and a carport as examples. A garage can easily sit inside 40-120 degrees Fahrenheit and still void the warranty. See the climate-control card.
