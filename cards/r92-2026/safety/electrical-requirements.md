**NEVER remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power.**

**If voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more, the performance of your fitness bike may be affected.** The manual states plainly that **such conditions are not covered under your warranty**. If you suspect the voltage is low, contact your local power company or a licensed electrician for proper testing.

The manual gives no circuit, breaker or outlet rating for this bike beyond the power pack rating; see the power supply card for what it does give.
