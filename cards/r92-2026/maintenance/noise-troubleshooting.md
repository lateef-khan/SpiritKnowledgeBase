**If a squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling develops, the main cause is most likely one of two things.**

**I. The hardware was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.**
- **All bolts that were installed during assembly need to be tightened as much as possible.**
- **It may be necessary to use a larger wrench than the one provided** if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently.
- The manual is emphatic here: **"90% of calls to the service department for noise issues can be traced to loose hardware or the rear rails being dirty."**

**II. Dirt build-up on the rear rails and polyurethane wheels.** This is the other half of that 90%. Noise from build-up on the rails can cause a thumping sound that seems to come from inside the main body of the machine, because noise travels and is amplified in the frame tubing. The cleaning and lubricating method is on its own card.

**III. If squeaks or other noises persist, check that the unit is properly levelled before calling the service department.**

**The manual gives no levelling procedure and names no levelling feet.** It only tells you to check that the unit is level.

Loose pedals are called out separately as the usual cause of a clicking or thumping sound; see the pedal card.
