**Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each workout.**

That is the entire routine-cleaning instruction in this manual. It names **no cleaning product, no disinfectant and no console-screen instruction**.

It matters for cover: the warranty excludes **corrosion (rust)** and **discoloration of paint or plastic** as external causes, and lists **routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual** as a consumer responsibility.

Cleaning the seat rails is a separate job with a different method; see the rail cleaning card.
