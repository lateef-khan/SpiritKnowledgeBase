**You can adjust the seat position while seated.**

1. **Pull up on the lever located in front of the seat.**
2. **Slide the carriage forward or backwards.**
3. **The correct position is when there is a slight bend in your knee when the pedal is at its farthest position forward** - the three o'clock position, looking at the bike from the right side.
4. **Release the lever** and you are ready to begin.

The manual describes **no seat height or backrest angle adjustment**, and gives no number of positions or travel range. The seat carriage runs on the rear rails, which is why rail cleanliness affects how the seat slides and how quiet it is.
