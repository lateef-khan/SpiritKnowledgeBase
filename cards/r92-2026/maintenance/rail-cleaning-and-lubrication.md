**Dirt build-up on the rear rails and the polyurethane wheels is a source of noise.**

1. **Clean the rails and wheels with a lint free cloth and rubbing alcohol.**
2. **Stubborn build-up can be removed with your thumbnail or a non-metallic scraper**, such as the back edge of a plastic knife.
3. **After cleaning, apply a small amount of lubricant on the rails** with your fingers or a lint free cloth.
4. **You only need a thin coat of lubrication. Wipe off any excess.**

**The manual does not name the lubricant.** It says "a small amount of lubricant" and nothing else - no type, no part number, no supplier. Do not substitute a treadmill belt lubricant on the strength of this instruction.

**Why it matters more than it sounds:** noise from build-up on the rails can cause a **thumping sound that you would swear is coming from inside the main body of the machine**, because noise travels and is amplified in the tubing of the frame.
