**This is Err in the Pulse window, and it is the only error message printed anywhere in this owner's manual.** It is not an E-number and it does not appear on the message window.

**What the manual says, in full: a heart rate monitor is asked for by the heart rate program, and the Pulse window will display Err if there is no pulse input after the Start key is pressed.**

So Err means the console started a heart rate program and never saw a heart rate - not that a part has failed.

**What the manual gives you to work with:**

- **The heart rate programs need a heart rate source.** Either grasp the Contact Heart Rate Sensors on the stationary handle bars, or wear the chest strap transmitter.
- **The heart icon blinking is the sign the console is receiving a pulse**, and it may take a few seconds to start.
- **The hand pulse sensors are subject to false readings** depending on grip and sweat, and **the chest strap is the more accurate and reliable method**.

**The manual gives no repair steps, no wiring check and no other error codes.** If a pulse never appears on any program, the hand pulse cable connection made in assembly step 2 is the one connection this manual describes.
