**The exercise equipment can play music wirelessly over Bluetooth.** The machine acts as the speaker; the phone or tablet sends the audio to it.

1. **Turn on your mobile phone's or tablet's Bluetooth function.**
2. **Search for the name "BT-Speaker" in your device's Bluetooth menu.**
3. **Tap to connect.**
4. **Your device can now transmit music to the exercise equipment.**

**BT-Speaker is paired from the phone's own Bluetooth settings, not from inside the SOLE+ App**, and it is a separate connection from the app pairing. It is also the only Bluetooth name this manual prints anywhere.

**This is the only place the manual describes the speaker's purpose.** The only other mention of a speaker is the maintenance menu's Key Tone item, which turns off the beep when a key is pressed.

**Not printed on this page:** any volume control, any console key for audio, whether the key tone setting affects music, and whether music can play while the app and a chest strap are also connected.
