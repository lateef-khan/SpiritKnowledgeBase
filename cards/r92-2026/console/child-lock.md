**Security is item F13 of the maintenance menu, not a button on the console.**

**What it does:** it **locks the keypad so that no unauthorized use of the machine is allowed**.

**To unlock:** when the child lock is enabled, **the console will not allow the keypad to operate unless you press and hold the Start and Enter buttons for 3 seconds**.

You reach the setting itself through the maintenance menu: hold Start, Stop and Enter for 3 seconds, then step to F13 with the Program key.

**This manual says nothing about what the display shows while the lock is on**, and does not say what the default setting is.
