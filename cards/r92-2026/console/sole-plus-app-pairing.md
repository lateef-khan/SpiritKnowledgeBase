**The bike comes equipped with a Bluetooth transceiver that lets it interact with compatible iOS and Android smartphones or tablet computers via the SOLE+ App.**

1. **Download the free SOLE+ App** from the **Apple Store** or **Google Play**, and follow the instructions in the App to sync with your exercise machine.
2. **Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your device.**
3. **Tap the icon at the top right corner of the app to search for Sole equipment.**
4. **After the equipment is detected, tap Connect.** You may now start using your new Sole product.

**The app asks you to sign in before any of this.** The welcome screen offers **Continue with Apple**, **Continue with Facebook** and **Continue with Google**, or **Log In** and **Sign Up** with an account, and notes that proceeding means agreeing to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

**Gaps to be aware of before promising a customer anything:**

- **"Select Apple & Android devices" and "compatible iOS and Android" are never defined.** The page gives **no supported device list**, no minimum OS version and no **Bluetooth version**.
- **The page never says what name the bike advertises for the app connection.** It says only to search from inside the app. The one Bluetooth name it does print, **BT-Speaker**, is the audio connection, not this one.
- **The two QR codes on the page point at the app stores but the codes themselves are not readable**, so no direct link can be quoted from this manual.
- **Nothing is printed about disconnecting, unpairing, or connecting a second phone.**

A chest strap can stay connected alongside the app, and music is a third, separate Bluetooth connection - both have their own cards.
