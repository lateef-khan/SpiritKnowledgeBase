**You can use a wireless Bluetooth heart rate transmitter on this machine.**

**To check if your Garmin device can be used for heart rate transmission, refer to the list of supported devices provided on www.soletreadmills.com.**

That is all page 19 says. **It gives no pairing procedure for a strap, no console menu and no protocol beyond the word Bluetooth**, and it does not reproduce the Garmin list.

**Page 14 carries the rest.** The Bluetooth Connectivity page confirms the machine's Bluetooth device can also connect to a wireless heart rate chest strap transmitter, and that **the strap can still connect even when the SOLE+ App is already connected**. That is on its own card.

**Still not printed anywhere in this manual:** which chest straps are compatible, the **Bluetooth version** of the transceiver, **which wearable brands** work, and the contents of the Garmin supported-device list - the manual points at the website for that and reproduces nothing.

The Type-C port on the console explicitly cannot transfer workout data; it charges only. Workout data leaves the machine through the app.
