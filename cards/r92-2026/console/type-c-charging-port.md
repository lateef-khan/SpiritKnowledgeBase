**Charge a personal device during your workout from the on-console Type-C port.**

1. **The fitness equipment power must be on.**
2. **Connect your Type-C charging cable to the Type-C Power Port and to your device.**
3. **Check that your device icon indicates it is charging.**

**The notes matter more than the steps:**

- **The Type-C charging cable is not included.** Make sure a compatible Type-C cable is used.
- **Your device's charging icon may or may not indicate it is charging.** Depending on how much current the device needs, the icon may stay off while the device is still charging, **possibly at a lower charge rate**. A missing icon is not a fault.
- **The port provides up to 5 Vdc / 1.0 amp.** That is well under what a modern phone or tablet will draw for fast charging, which is why a slow charge is normal here.
- **You cannot save your workout data through this port. It is for charging only.**

**Workout data leaves the machine over Bluetooth instead**, through the SOLE+ App, which syncs to Apple Health, Google Health Connect, Strava or MapMyFitness. There is no wired data path off this bike.

The tablet holder mounts to the back of the console and is fitted in assembly step 4.
