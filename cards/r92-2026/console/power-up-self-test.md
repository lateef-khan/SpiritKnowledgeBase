**When power is connected to the fitness bike the console automatically powers up.** There is no separate power switch.

**On first power up the console performs an internal self test.** During the self test:

| Where | What it shows |
|---|---|
| All lights | Turn on |
| **Level window** | **The software version** (for example, ver 1.0) |
| **Distance window** | **An odometer reading** |
| **Time window** | **How many total hours the fitness bike has been used** |

**The odometer and the hours stay on screen for only a few seconds**, then the console goes to the startup display. This is the only way the manual gives to read either figure - there is no menu item that shows them.

**On the startup display:** the dot matrix display appears in the Time and Distance windows, and the program numbers appear in the RPM window.

**The odometer can be reset from the maintenance menu**, so an odometer reading is not proof of lifetime use.
