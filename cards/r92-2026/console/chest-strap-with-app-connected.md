**Yes. The two Bluetooth connections coexist - you do not have to pick one.**

The page states it twice over:

- **The exercise equipment's Bluetooth device can also be connected to a Bluetooth wireless heart rate chest strap transmitter.**
- **The chest strap transmitter can still connect to the exercise equipment even though the App has already been connected with the exercise equipment.**

So a customer who pairs SOLE+ has not given up the chest strap, and a chest strap that stops reading after the app is connected is **not** the documented behaviour of this machine.

**This paragraph is worth knowing as specific to this manual.** The otherwise word-identical Bluetooth page in the F65-2026 manual does not carry it. Do not assume the same statement exists for another model just because the surrounding page matches.

**What the page still does not say:** which chest straps are compatible, what **Bluetooth version** the transceiver is, or **which wearable brands** work. Page 19 adds only that a wireless Bluetooth heart rate transmitter can be used and points Garmin owners at a device list on the website that the manual does not reproduce.

Music is a third, separate Bluetooth connection; the page does not say whether all three can be live at once.
