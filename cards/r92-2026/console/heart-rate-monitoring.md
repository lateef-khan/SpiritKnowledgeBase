**The fitness bike has a built in heart rate monitoring system.** There are two ways in:

- **Grasp the Contact Heart Rate Sensors on the stationary handle bars**, or
- **wear the chest strap transmitter**.

Either one **starts the heart icon blinking - this may take a few seconds** - and the **Pulse window displays your heart rate in beats per minute**.

**The chest strap is a more accurate and reliable method of heart rate reading.**

**The hand pulse sensors are subject to false readings** depending on user physiology and workout habits, **including how one grips the sensors and how sweaty their hands are**.

**The safety section states the limit more bluntly**, and it is the half people quote: **the hand pulse sensors are not medical devices**. Various factors, including the user's movement, may affect accuracy, and they are **intended only as exercise aids in determining heart rate trends in general**. Both halves are printed in this manual, in two different places; neither one mentions the other.

**The manual does not say whether a chest strap is included**, nor which strap the bike is paired to. A wireless Bluetooth transmitter is covered on its own card, and a strap can stay connected while the SOLE+ App is connected too.
