**With all SOLE Heart Rate Control fitness bike machines you may use the heart rate monitor feature without using the Heart Rate program.**

**The function can be used during manual mode or during any of the nine different programs.** In other words, the pulse readout is always available; it is not gated behind H1 or H2.

**What the Heart Rate program adds is control: it automatically controls resistance at the pedals.** Outside a heart rate program the resistance stays where you or the profile put it, and the pulse figure is information only.

The manual counts **manual mode plus nine programs** here, against the **ten program numbers** shown at power up. The two counts agree if the manual program is being counted as manual mode rather than as one of the nine.
