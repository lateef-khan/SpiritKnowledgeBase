**The age and weight default settings change when you enter a new number. The last age and weight entered is saved as the new default.**

So:

- **Enter age and weight the first time you use the fitness bike and you will not have to enter them every time** you work out.
- **You will have to enter them again if your age or weight has changed**, or **if someone else has entered a different age and weight**.

**There is one set of values, not one per user.** On a machine two people share, the values belong to whoever set up a workout last. There is no user profile and no way to store two sets.

**Age is not cosmetic on this console.** Entering your age is what the heart rate programs use to work out the target heart rate, so a stale age changes what H1 and H2 aim for.
