**The warranty does not cover products that have been sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party.** The manual states it flatly: **"Warranties DO NOT transfer."**

That covers a private sale, a gift and a swap equally - the wording is not limited to a sale.

**A second-hand machine is therefore out of warranty regardless of its age**, even where the frame weldment cover would otherwise be for life.

Registration reinforces this from the other side: exclusion 6 excludes products with no warranty registration card on file at SOLE, and registration is made in the original purchaser's name.
