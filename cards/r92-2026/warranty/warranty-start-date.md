**The clock starts at the date of retail sale, as determined by the sale receipt.**

**With no sales receipt it starts at the factory ship date instead, and the period is fixed at eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date.**

**The Service section restates it for labour specifically:** **twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale, or eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number, establishes the labor warranty period** should service be required.

**Keep your bill of sale.** If service is performed, **it is in your best interest to obtain and keep all receipts**.

**The serial number is what dates a machine when no receipt exists**, which is why the manual asks you to record it on page 1.

**The proof the warranty department will accept** is wider than a till receipt where registration is concerned - receipt, bank statement, credit card statement or email order confirmation all count. See the registration card.
