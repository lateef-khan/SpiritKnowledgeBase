**Printed on the front matter, in capitals:** **"THIS FITNESS BIKE IS INTENDED FOR RESIDENTIAL USE ONLY AND IS WARRANTED FOR THE APPLICATION. ANY OTHER APPLICATION VOIDS THIS WARRANTY IN ITS ENTIRETY."**

The warranty section says the same thing three more ways:

- **This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household use.**
- **Exclusion 8:** **product use in any environment other than a residential setting** is not covered.
- **Exclusion 2** withholds service call reimbursement to the dealer for **a fitness bike not in ordinary household use**.

**There is no light commercial tier in this manual.** It offers one application and voids the warranty in its entirety for any other. A gym, a clubhouse, an office, a hotel or a shared apartment fitness room is not residential use on this wording.

The manual does not define "residential setting" beyond the examples above.
