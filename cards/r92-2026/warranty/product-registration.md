**Record the serial number** of the product in the space provided on page 1 of the manual.

**Two ways to register:**

1. **The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to SOLE.**
2. **Register online at www.soletreadmills.com, under the support tab.**

**The deadline is printed in the warranty section, not on the registration page.** The card must be returned **within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty. **Outside the 10 day period the warranty can still be registered if you have proof of purchase** - receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation.

**Exclusion 6 is why this matters:** products that **do not have a warranty registration card on file at SOLE** are excluded, and **SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists**.

**The manual does not say where the serial number label is on the bike.** It gives only a blank space to write it in. The serial number is also what dates the machine for the labour warranty when there is no bill of sale.

Page 1 additionally asks you to record the **name of the dealer, the dealer's telephone number and the purchase date** for easy contact in the future.
