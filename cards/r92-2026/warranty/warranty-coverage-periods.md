**Fitness bike warranty, printed as effective February 1, 2023.**

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Parts** | **2 Years** |
| **Labor** | **1 Year** |
| **Cosmetic Items** | **90 Days** |

**SOLE warrants all its fitness bike parts for the periods above from the date of retail sale**, as determined by sale receipt, **or, in the absence of a sales receipt, eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date**.

**SOLE's responsibilities are** providing **new or remanufactured parts, at SOLE's option**, and technical support to independent dealers and servicing organizations. **Where there is no dealer or service organization, SOLE administers the warranty directly to the consumer.**

**This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household use.**

**Two cautions about the table as printed:**

- **The effective date reads February 1, 2023 in a 2026 manual.** It is quoted here as printed. It is not this card's job to decide whether that is the date that governs; check with the warranty department before quoting a date to a customer.
- **"Cosmetic Items" carries an asterisk with no footnote anywhere in this manual.** What counts as a cosmetic item is therefore not defined here, and this card will not borrow a definition from another model's manual.
