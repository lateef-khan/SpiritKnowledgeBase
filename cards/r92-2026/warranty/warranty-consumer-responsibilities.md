**This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household use**, and the consumer is responsible for the following eight items.

1. **The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address on the card within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty. **Outside the 10 day period the warranty can still be registered if you have proof of purchase** - receipt, bank statement, credit card statement or email order confirmation.
2. **Proper use in accordance with the instructions in this manual, including general maintenance.**
3. **Proper installation** in accordance with the instructions provided with the fitness bike **and with all local electric codes**.
4. **Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage**, replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring.
5. **Expenses for making the fitness bike accessible for servicing**, including any item that was not part of the fitness bike when it shipped from the factory.
6. **Damage to the fitness bike finish during shipping, installation or following installation.**
7. **Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual.**
8. **Provide pictures or video of any issues via email as requested by the service technician**, and **assist with troubleshooting and diagnosing prior to a home visit.**

**Item 2 is printed with treadmill wording in this bike manual.** It reads "Proper use of the **treadmill**" and gives the maintenance examples as "belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under motor cover, and lubrication". **A recumbent bike has none of those.** The bike's General Maintenance section asks for the sweat path to be wiped after each workout, the assembly hardware to be kept tight, and the rear rails and wheels to be cleaned and lightly lubricated. That is what item 2 can reasonably require of a bike owner; the printed examples cannot be met on this machine.

**Item 5 is the one that surprises people:** getting the machine to a serviceable place, and anything added to it after it left the factory, is at the owner's expense.
