**The warranty will be void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment.** The manual gives the examples itself: **garage, outside, carport, etc.**

This is a **void**, not a limitation on one component - it is written as ending the warranty, not reducing it.

**It is stricter than the operating specification, and the two are easy to confuse.** The electrical section says the bike may be operated between **40 and 120 degrees Fahrenheit at up to 95% non-condensing humidity**. A garage will often sit inside that range and still void the warranty, because the test here is climate-controlled and insulated, not a temperature reading.

Exclusion 3 backs it up from another direction by excluding **corrosion (rust)** and **water damage** as external causes.
