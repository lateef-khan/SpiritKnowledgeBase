**Q factor is the distance between the pedals.** Stationary exercise bikes are wider than a road bike because they have to make room for the braking mechanism, pulleys, drive components and plastic covers, and the wider bike puts the pedals further apart.

Sole's claims for this bike:

- The **pedal system is designed so the Q factor is the smallest in the industry**.
- The pedal is **custom designed and tooled to provide a two degree inward tilt**, to compensate for the Q factor not being perfect.
- Together these put the user into **biomechanical neutral alignment** - feet, ankles, knees and hips lined up properly.

The design work is credited to research with a leading sports scientist and physical rehabilitation expert.

**No measurement is printed.** The manual gives no Q factor figure in millimetres or inches, only the two degree tilt and the claim that the spacing is the smallest in the industry.
