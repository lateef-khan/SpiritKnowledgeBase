**The bike runs from a power pack, not from batteries.**

- **These models operate on DC 12V/3.3A (12V/3A), supplied by the power pack, which connects to 115 (230) VAC.**
- **There is a power socket located where the line cord plugs into the unit, on the front of the bike.**
- **When power is connected to the fitness bike the console automatically powers up.** There is no separate power switch described.

**The bracketed second figures are unexplained in the manual.** "DC 12V/3.3A(12V/3A)" and "115(230) VAC" read as the alternative rating for a 230 V market, but the manual never says so. Read the label on the power pack you actually have rather than quoting one of the two.

The console's own Type-C port is a separate 5 Vdc supply for charging a phone; it is not this input.
