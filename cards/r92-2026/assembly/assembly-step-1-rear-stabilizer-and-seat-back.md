Use the **HARDWARE STEP 1** pack.

1. **Install the Rear Stabilizer (7) onto the Main Frame (1)** with **2pcs of Carriage Bolts (65)**, **2pcs of Flat Washers (84)** and **2pcs of Cap Nuts (191)**, using the **Wrench (112)**.
2. **Install the Seat Back Bracket (5) onto the Seat Carriage (4)** with **6pcs of Hex Head Bolts (176)** and **6pcs of Flat Washers (77)**, using the **Wrench (112)**.
3. **Insert an end cap (32) into each opening of the Front Stabilizer Tube.** You may need to tap them in with a rubber mallet if they are tight.

**Item 3 is printed under a heading that says rear stabiliser but names the Front Stabilizer Tube.** The manual says nothing more about the front stabiliser, and no step installs one, so it appears to be already fitted to the main frame and to need only its end caps. Both readings are in the printed text; the manual does not settle it.

The manual gives no torque figures. General maintenance asks for every bolt installed during assembly to be **tightened as much as possible**.
