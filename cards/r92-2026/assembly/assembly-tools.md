**The Assembly Tools page is a picture and did not survive the text extraction.** What follows is the list of tools the four assembly steps actually name, which is verifiable from the step text. **The manual prints no tool sizes anywhere** - it names no spanner or driver size for either wrench. The closest thing to a size is the **fastener** list on the Assembly Pack Checklist, which gives 3/8" and 5/16" hex head bolts and M6, M5 and sheet metal screws; that tells you what the tools have to fit but not what the supplied ones are.

Supplied in the hardware package (remove the tools first):

- **Wrench (112)** - steps 1, 2, 3.
- **Wrench (132)** - step 2 and the pedals in step 4.
- **Phillips head screw driver (114)** - step 4.

Not supplied, but called for:

- **A razor knife (box cutter)** to open the carton.
- **A rubber mallet**, to tap the end caps (32) in if they are tight.

General maintenance adds one more: **it may be necessary to use a larger wrench than the one provided** if you cannot tighten the assembly bolts sufficiently with the supplied wrench.
