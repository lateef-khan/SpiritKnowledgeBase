Use the **HARDWARE STEP 2** pack.

1. **Install the Seat Handle Bar (6) onto the Seat Carriage (4)** with **2pcs of Hex Head Bolts (71)**, **2pcs of Hex Head Bolts (175)**, **4pcs of Flat Washers (77)** and **4pcs of Nylon Nuts (89)**, using the **Wrench (112)** and the **Wrench (132)**.
2. **Plug the two Handpulse W/Cable Assemblies (L/27, R/21) into the sockets located on the left side rear cover, just under the seat.** Then **plug the Resistance Connecting Cable (150) into the front socket.**

Two different hex head bolts are used in the same joint, **(71)** and **(175)**, two of each. **They are different lengths** - the Assembly Pack Checklist gives **(71) as 3/8" x 1-3/4"** and **(175) as 3/8" x 2-3/4"**, both packed in this step at 2 pcs. **Neither the checklist nor the step text says which of the two goes in which position**, and the assembly drawing that would show it is a picture that did not survive the text extraction. Offer both up before committing to either.

If the hand pulse grips never read afterwards, this is the connection to check first.
