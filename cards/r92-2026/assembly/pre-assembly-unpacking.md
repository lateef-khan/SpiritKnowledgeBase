1. **Using a razor knife (box cutter), cut the outside, bottom, edge of the box along the dotted line.** Lift the box over the unit and unpack.
2. **Carefully remove all parts from the carton and inspect for any damage or missing parts.** If damaged parts are found, or parts are missing, **contact your dealer immediately**.
3. **Locate the hardware package. Remove the tools first.** Remove the hardware for each step **as needed, to avoid confusion**.

**The numbers in parentheses (#) throughout the instructions are the item number from the assembly drawing**, not quantities and not part numbers you can order by.

The hardware is bagged as four packs, one per assembly step, labelled HARDWARE STEP 1 through HARDWARE STEP 4. **The Assembly Pack Checklist on pages 4 and 5 lists every item number, size and quantity**; it is reproduced on its own card, and it is what to check against when a customer reports a missing fastener.
