**Install the Pedals (L/116, R/117) into the Cranks (51L, 51R) using the Wrench (132).**

- **The left pedal (L/116) has a reverse thread** and screws into the crank **in the opposite rotation from normal threads**.
- **There is an "L" stamped into the end of the threaded post of the left pedal and an "R" in the right.** Check the stamp before you start turning.
- **Tighten the pedals as much as you possibly can.**

**If you feel a thumping while pedalling, re-tighten the pedals.** The manual says a clicking noise or thumping sound during pedalling is **usually caused by the pedals being too loose**.

This is the same symptom the general maintenance section blames on loose hardware generally, so a pedal that keeps working loose is worth checking against the wider noise card too.
