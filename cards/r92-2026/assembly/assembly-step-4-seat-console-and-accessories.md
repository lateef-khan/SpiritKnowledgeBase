Use the **HARDWARE STEP 4** pack. Items 5 and 6 of this step are the pedals and are on their own card, because the left pedal is reverse threaded.

1. **Install the Seat (61) on the Seat Carriage (4)** with **4pcs of Phillips Head Screws (98)**, using the **Phillips Head Screw Driver (114)**.
2. **Install the Computer Cable (44), Switch Cable (Upper) (148) and Handpulse Wire (45) into the connector in the back of the Console Assembly (19).** Then install the console onto the mounting plate with **4pcs of Phillips Head Screws (99)**, using the **Phillips Head Screw Driver (114)**. **Be careful not to pinch the wires between the console and the mounting plate. This could short the wires and damage the electronics.**
3. **Install the Drink Bottle Holders (L/39, R/38) onto the Seat Handle Bar (6)** with **4pcs of Sheet Metal Screws (105)**, using the **Phillips Head Screw Driver (114)**.
4. **Install the Tablet Holder (190) to the back of the console** with **4pcs of Sheet Metal Screws (192)**, using the **Phillips Head Screw Driver (114)**.

Two different sheet metal screws are used, **(105)** for the bottle holders and **(192)** for the tablet holder. They are not interchangeable in the instructions.

Once the console is on, connecting power powers it up on its own; there is no separate power switch step.
