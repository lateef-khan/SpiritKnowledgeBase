**The four hardware bags are labelled HARDWARE STEP 1 to HARDWARE STEP 4 and are opened one per assembly step.** The numbers are the item numbers from the assembly drawing.

**HARDWARE STEP 1**

| Item | Size | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **#77** | 3/8" x 19mm x 1.5T | Flat Washer | **6 pcs** |
| **#84** | 3/8" x 25mm x 2T | Flat Washer | **2 pcs** |
| **#176** | 3/8" x 3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | **6 pcs** |
| **#65** | 3/8" x 53L_ | Carriage Bolt | **2 pcs** |
| **#191** | 3/8" x16 x 12.5T | Cap Nut | **2 pcs** |

**HARDWARE STEP 2**

| Item | Size | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **#71** | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | **2 pcs** |
| **#77** | 3/8" x 19mm x 1.5T | Flat Washer | **4 pcs** |
| **#89** | 3/8" x 7T | Nyloc Nut | **4 pcs** |
| **#175** | 3/8" x 2-3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | **2 pcs** |

**HARDWARE STEP 3**

| Item | Size | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **#68** | 5/16" x 5/8" | Hex Head Bolt | **8 pcs** |
| **#76** | 5/16" x 18mm x 1.5T | Flat Washer | **6 pcs** |
| **#82** | 5/16" x 1.5T | Split Washer | **2 pcs** |
| **#83** | 5/16" x 19mm x 1.5T | Curved Washer | **2 pcs** |

**HARDWARE STEP 4**

| Item | Size | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **#98** | M6 x 15mm | Phillips Head Screw | **4 pcs** |
| **#99** | M5 x 12mm | Phillips Head Screw | **4 pcs** |
| **#105** | Ø4 x 16mm | Sheet Metal Screw | **4 pcs** |
| **#192** | 3.5 x 12L_ | Sheet Metal Screw | **4 pcs** |

**Every bag balances against the step that uses it.** Each quantity above is exactly what the printed step consumes, with one place worth reading carefully: **step 3's eight hex head bolts (68) are split across two sub-steps** - six hold the console mast in the receiving tube and two hold the mast handlebar assembly. The same split applies to its six flat washers (76): four on the sides of the tube, two at the handlebar.

**Do not add the two rows for #77 together into a machine total.** The same flat washer, 3/8" x 19mm x 1.5T, is packed in **step 1 (6 pcs)** and again in **step 2 (4 pcs)**. That is genuine reuse across two steps, not a duplicated row.

**The instructions and the checklist use two names for one part.** Item **#89** is a **Nyloc Nut** on the checklist and a **Nylon Nut** in assembly step 2. Same part.

**The checklist covers fasteners only.** The **end caps (32)** that step 1 asks you to tap into the front stabiliser tube are not on it, and neither are the tools.

**Two printed sizes carry a layout artefact:** **#65** reads `3/8" x 53L_` and **#192** reads `3.5 x 12L_`, with a trailing underscore where the length suffix should close. **#191** is printed `3/8" x16 x 12.5T`, without the space. All three are reproduced above exactly as printed.

**What the checklist still does not settle:** step 2 uses two hex head bolts **#71** (3/8" x 1-3/4") and two **#175** (3/8" x 2-3/4") in the same joint, and **neither the checklist nor the step text says which of the two goes where**. The checklist confirms both belong to step 2 at 2 pcs each; the drawing that would show the position is a picture that did not survive.
