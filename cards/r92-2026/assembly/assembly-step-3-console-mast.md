Use the **HARDWARE STEP 3** pack.

1. **Install the Console Mast Cover (31) onto the Console Mast (2).** Make sure the plastic cover is in the correct orientation.
2. **Feed the Computer Cable (44), Switch Cable (Upper) (148) and Handpulse Wire (45)** through the bottom of the **Console Mast (2)** and out through the opening at the top.
3. **Install the Console Mast (2) into the receiving tube of the Main Frame (1)** - *make sure not to pinch the wire cable, as damage to the electronics could occur* - with **6pcs of Hex Head Bolts (68)**, **4pcs of Flat Washers (76) on the sides of the tube** and **2pcs of Curved Washers (83) on the front of the tube**, using the **Wrench (112)**.
4. **Install the Mast Handle bar Assembly (3) onto the Console Mast (2)** with **2pcs of Hex Head Bolts (68)**, **2pcs of Flat Washers (76)** and **2pcs of Split Washers (82)**, using the **Wrench (112)**.

**The washer positions in item 3 are not interchangeable.** Flat washers go on the sides of the tube, curved washers on the front.

**The mast cover (31) has to go on the mast before the mast goes into the frame.** It cannot be fitted afterwards without taking the mast back out.
