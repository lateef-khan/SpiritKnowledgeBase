These are the callout numbers on the exploded view drawing on page 58. The drawing
is a picture with bare numbers on it and no text, so this table is the only
readable form of it.

**The list heads itself `CT850ENT Part List_SPIRIT`** and is date-stamped **230731**
at its foot. Unlike the LED CT850 lists it prints no ST8600 / YT058 drawing code at
all, so nothing on this page ties it to a service-manual drawing.

**143 rows are printed.** The whole numbers that appear are 1-29, 31-41, 44-53,
55-68, 70, 72-82, 85-90, 93, 94, 101-117, 119-140, 142-146, 150-156, 158-164 and 166.
Any number outside that run is not printed in this manual at all - **30, 42, 43, 54,
69, 71, 83, 84, 91, 92, 95-100, 118, 141, 147-149, 157 and 165 are absent, not a
missing page.**

**The assembly steps call for a cable numbered 84, and 84 is not in this list.** Step
One says to thread "the all Cables (No.53/80/82/84) up through the center of the
Right Upright Column"; 53, 80 and 82 are all here and 84 is one of the absent
numbers. Nothing in the manual says what 84 is. Do not guess it from a CT800ENT
list, whose 83 and 84 are also absent.

**Item 85 is printed with no part name, only the bare word `board`.** It is the only
row on the page without a proper description, and its quantity is 1. Item 93 is the
`Interface Board`; whether 85 is a second board or a lost description of the same one
is not recoverable from this page.

**Item 76 is printed `300m/m_round Wire`.** Item 46 is `300m/m_Ground Wire`, so 76 is
almost certainly a dropped G, but it is left exactly as printed below.

**79 and 80 are the network pair here; 81 and 82 are the C-safe pair.** This list
prints 79/80 as `Network Connecting Cable` upper and lower, and 81/82 as
`CSafe Connecting Wire` upper and lower, and the assembly steps agree - Step Two
pairs "the Ethernet Cable (No. 80) with the Upper Ethernet Cable (No.79)" and "the
C-safe Cable (No. 82) with the Upper C-safe Cable (No.81)". **The CT800ENT-2022 parts
list says the opposite**, printing 79/80 as the CSAFE pair; see
`ct800ent-2022-specs-parts-list`, which records that its own list and its own
assembly text disagree. Both pairs are 900 mm upper and 2400 mm lower on both
machines, so length will not tell them apart. Order 79, 80, 81 or 82 against the
connector, not against the other machine's book.

**Do not read this list against the LED CT850 lists.** 128 numbers appear on both
this and the CT850-2020 list, but 25 of them carry a different part, a different
length or a different description, and item **71** (TV Adapter) exists only on the
LED lists. The differences that change what you would order:

The remaining twelve - items 26, 49, 50, 51, 52, 62, 64, 65, 75, 94, 113 and 119 -
name the same part with different punctuation, with a qualifier such as `(Upper)` or
`(SMR-03)` dropped, or with a connector tag or a size added to the description. None
of the twelve changes a quantity.

**Items 50 and 51 are told apart here and nowhere else in the CT850 family.** This
list prints them as `400m/m_Handpulse Wire (Upper)(XHB-4)` and `(XHB-3)`. The
CT850-2016 and CT850-2020 lists print both rows identically with no connector tag,
so those two books cannot distinguish the pair.

**Item 112 is printed `3/8" × UCN16 × 2"` and item 110 `3/8" × UCN16 × 1-1/2"`, while
item 154 is printed `3/8" × UNC16 × 1-1/4"`.** UCN and UNC are the same thread callout
spelled two ways in the one list. Both spellings are left exactly as printed below;
nothing here changes a number, a size or a quantity.

**No motor rating, roller size, deck size or belt size is printed for any of these
parts.** Item 55 is "AC Motor" and item 66 "Incline Motor", each with no horsepower
or wattage; items 15, 16, 17 and 18 give the front roller, rear roller, running deck
and running belt no dimensions. See `spirit-ct850-specs-no-specification-table`.
