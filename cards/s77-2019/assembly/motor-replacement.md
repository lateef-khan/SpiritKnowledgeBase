1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to loosen the **5 tapping screws** on the motor cover and remove it.
2. Remove the **motor grounding wire (greenish yellow)**, the **motor wire (+) red** and the **motor wire (-) black**.
3. Use a **14 mm T-shaped socket wrench** to loosen the **4 screws**, and a **14 mm open end wrench** to loosen the **1 belt tension screw**. Remove the motor and fit the new one.
4. Reassemble in reverse order and re-hook the belt. **Readjust the belt to be parallel with the main frame after re-hooking. Do not tighten the 4 securing screws yet.**
5. Use the 14 mm open end wrench to set the belt tension with a tension measuring device to the **white LBS area, between 70 and 75 LBS**. Then tighten the 4 securing screws with the T-shaped socket wrench.
6. Reconnect the grounding wire and the motor wires: **red M+, black M-**.
