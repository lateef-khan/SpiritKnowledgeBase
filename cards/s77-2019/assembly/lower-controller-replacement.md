1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to loosen the **5 tapping screws** on the motor cover, and remove the cover.
2. Disconnect all the lower controller wiring, remove the controller with a Phillips head screwdriver, fit the new one and reconnect all the wiring.

**Calibrate after fitting a new controller.**
