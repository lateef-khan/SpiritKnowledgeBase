**Breaker**: remove the breaker connection wiring, fit the new part and reconnect the wiring.

**AC power switch**: disconnect the wiring to the switch, fit the new switch and reconnect the wiring.

The breaker is the one on the front grill that the troubleshooting checklist tells the user to push back in until it locks.
