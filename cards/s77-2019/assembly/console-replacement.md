1. Use an **M5 L Allen wrench** to loosen the **4 sheet metal screws** and remove the console mast cover.
2. Use an **M6 L Allen wrench** to remove the **4 button head socket bolts** from the console support.
3. Disconnect the console wiring and fit the new console.

**Calibrate after fitting a new console.**

The earlier ST725 manual for this machine family gives **six** sheet metal screws for the mast cover, not four, and does not name a wrench size for the support bolts.
