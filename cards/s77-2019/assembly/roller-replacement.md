1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to loosen the **2 screws** on the rear adjustment base.
2. Use an **M6 L Allen wrench** to loosen the **2 screws** on the rear roller.
3. Remove the motor cover. Use a **14 mm T-shaped socket wrench** to loosen the **4 screws**, and a **14 mm open end wrench** to loosen the **1 belt tension screw** and slacken the drive belt. Use a **13 mm wrench** to loosen the front roller screws. Remove the front and rear rollers and fit the new ones.
4. Reassemble in reverse order. **The running belt tension has to be adjusted and the belt centred afterwards.**
5. Set the drive belt tension with a tension measuring device to the **white LBS area, between 70 and 75 LBS**, then tighten the 4 securing screws.

Roller bearings are the cause the manual gives for noise under the motor cover (front roller) and noise at the rear (rear roller).
