Nine illustrated assembly steps for the S77-2019 (hardware item numbers in brackets). Unlike the S77-2016 book the uprights bolt through left and right gussets (7 & 8), the mast covers screw to the frame base, and a step-9 tablet holder is added.

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill from the carton onto a smooth, level floor with the rear at least 3 ft from any wall. Do not remove the Velcro belt until the wrap and Styrofoam are out; turning it on its side may cause it to fold up.
2. Remove four Flat Head Socket Bolts (102) and the two Fixing Plate Stabilizers (101). Slide the lower handlebar covers (52 & 53) onto the console masts and the Frame Base Caps (98 & 99) over the upright tubes (4 & 5). Connect Lower Computer Cable (33) to Middle Computer Cable (32) on the right side. Do not pinch the cables.
3. Attach the upright tubes (4) and (5) to the main frame (1): four Button Head Socket Bolts (100) with four Curved Washers (124), then six Button Head Socket Bolts (123) with six Flat Washers (79) and six Split Washers (86) through the left and right gussets (7 & 8). Leave loose until step 7 is finished. Then connect Speed Adjustment Switch Cable (37) to Speed Cable Upper (34), Incline Adjustment Switch Cable (38) to Incline Cable Upper (35), and Middle Computer Cable (32) to Computer Cable Upper (31); tuck excess cable into the handrail tubing. Have someone hold the console while connecting.
5. Attach the Console Assembly (36) onto the uprights (4 & 5) with four Button Head Socket Bolts (91) and four Split Washers (86); tighten completely with the M6 wrench.
6. Attach the left (52) and right (53) lower handlebar covers to the console with four Sheet Metal Screws (125).
7. Attach the support tube (3) between the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (100), place the Beverage Holder (118) on top, place the safety-key magnet (62) between Start and Stop, and check every bolt and screw is tight.
8. Finish tightening the mast bolts left loose in step 2 and fix the console mast covers (98 & 99) to the frame base (2) with four Phillips Head Screws (134). The checklist calls item 134 a button-head socket bolt while this step calls it a Phillips head screw; there are four of them either way.
9. Insert the Tablet Holder (177) into the console assembly (36) with four Sheet Metal Screws (125).
