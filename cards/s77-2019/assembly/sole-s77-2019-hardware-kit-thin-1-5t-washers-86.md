Hardware supplied in the box with the Sole S77-2019, grouped by assembly step. This is the 1.5T-washer printing: the mast split washers (#86) are Dia 10 x 1.5T, the flat washers (#79) are Dia 3/8" x Dia 19 x 1.5T and the curved washers (#124) are 8 x 23 x 1.5T, unlike the S77-2016 book (see the linked card). Sizes read off the page rendering. The step-8 screws are button-head socket bolts (#134), not Phillips screws.

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | #79 | Dia 3/8" x Dia 19 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| 3 | #86 | Dia 10 x 1.5T | Split Washer | 6 pcs |
| 3 | #124 | 8 x 23 x 1.5T | Curved Washer | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #100 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #123 | 3/8" x 2-1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 6 pcs |
| 5 | #86 | Dia 10 x 1.5T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #91 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 6 | #125 | 3.5 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #100 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #62 | - | Safety Key | 1 pc |
| 8 | #134 | M5 x 10 mm | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 9 | #125 | 3.5 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |

Tools in the box: #103 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver, #104 M6 L Allen Wrench. There is no hardware step 1, 2 or 4.
