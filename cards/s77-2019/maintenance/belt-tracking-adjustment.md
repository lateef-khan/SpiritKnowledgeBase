The frame must sit on a reasonably level surface. If it does not, the front and rear rollers cannot run parallel and the belt will need constant adjustment.

Some drift with nobody on the belt is normal; after a few minutes of use the belt should tend to centre itself. Adjust only if it keeps moving to one side during use.

1. Use the **10 mm Allen wrench** supplied, on the rear roller. **Make tracking adjustments from the left side only.**
2. Set the belt speed to about **3 to 5 kph**.
3. Turn the bolt **clockwise to move the belt to the right**.
4. Turn only a **quarter turn**, then wait a few minutes for the belt to settle.
5. Repeat in quarter turns until the belt stabilises in the centre of the deck.

A small adjustment makes a large difference. Tracking is an owner responsibility, and **damage from improper tracking or tension adjustment is not covered by the warranty**.
