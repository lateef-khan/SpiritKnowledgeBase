**By where the noise is**

| Location | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| Under the motor cover | Worn brushes or bearings on the motor | Replace the motor brushes, or the motor |
| Under the motor cover | Front roller bearings defective | Replace the front roller |
| Under the motor cover | Drive belt misadjusted, too tight or too loose | Adjust the motor position |
| Rear of the treadmill | Rear roller bearings defective | Replace the rear roller |
| Rear of the treadmill | Rear roller misaligned | Adjust the rear roller position |

**By what the noise sounds like**

1. Noise from the rollers.
2. Noise while running at the **lowest incline level only**: too much pressure on the incline cylinder. This happens only at the lowest incline.
3. **Knocking** during a workout: check that all the bolts are tight.
4. **Thumping while the belt runs**, on a brand new machine or one unused for a long time: the belt has taken the shape of the rollers and hardened in the cold. **Run the belt for tens of minutes and the thumping will gradually go away.**
