1. Remove the motor cover hood.
2. Find the sensor: it is on the **left side of the frame, right next to the front roller pulley** - the pulley with the belt round it that also goes to the motor. The sensor is small and black with one wire on it.
3. Get the sensor **as close as possible to the pulley without touching it**. The manual's limit elsewhere is a gap of **less than 3 mm**.
4. There is a **magnet on the face of the pulley**. Make sure the sensor lines up with it.
5. Loosen the screw that holds the sensor to move it, and re-tighten it when you are done.

**This machine's driver board has no SPEED indicator LED**; the earlier ST725 board does. Confirm the result with a calibration instead.
