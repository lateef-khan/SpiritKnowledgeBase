| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The drive belt is breaking in | Vacuum under the treadmill periodically |

This is expected on a new machine and is not a fault. Belt dust from the running belt during break-in is a separate, equally normal thing; wipe it off with a damp cloth.
