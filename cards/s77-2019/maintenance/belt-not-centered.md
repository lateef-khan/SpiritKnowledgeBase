The checklist gives one cause: **the user's stride**. Someone who favours one foot, or puts more weight on one foot, moves the belt. If that walking pattern is natural for them, track the belt slightly **off-centre to the side opposite the belt movement**.

The tracking adjustment itself is a separate procedure: 10 mm Allen wrench, rear roller, **left side only**, belt at 3 to 5 kph, quarter turn at a time, clockwise moves the belt to the right.
