**Clean first.** Use a clean non-fraying rag, t-shirt or light towel. Halfway between the end of the treadmill and the motor cover, push the garment under the belt until you can hold it on both sides, drag it the length of the belt once or twice, then remove it.

**Interval**: the belt and deck come pre-lubricated; lubricate every **90 hours of use**, or sooner if the deck looks dry. Reach between the belt and deck to check for lubricant **every other month**.

**Use only the approved lubricant.** The treadmill comes with one tube of Lube; more can be ordered from an authorised dealer, or as a Lube-n-Walk kit.

**Failure to lubricate at regular intervals may void the warranty.** A dry deck raises the friction and stresses the drive motor, the drive belt and the motor control board, which can destroy all three.

The lube message and odometer are reset from **Functions, Maintenance** in the engineering menu.
