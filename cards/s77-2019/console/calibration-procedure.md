1. Remove the safety key.
2. Press and hold **START** and **Speed up** and, at the same time, refit the safety key. Keep holding both until the window shows `Factory settings`, then press **ENTER**.
3. Choose **Metric or English** with the **incline up and down** keys, then press **ENTER**. The maximum speed value appears in the speed window and the maximum elevation value in the incline window.
4. **Grade return - On.** This lets the incline return to zero when STOP is pressed. EU standards require it to be **off** for machines sold in Europe.
5. Press **START** to begin the calibration.

**The process is automatic and the belt starts without warning. Do not stand on the belt.**

Calibrate after fitting a new lower controller, console or incline motor, and after an E1 or E3.

The earlier ST725 manual uses the **up and down** keys for the metric or English choice at step 3; this one uses the incline keys.
