| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Variable speed DC motor |
| Voltage | **0 to 90 volt**, or **0 to 180 volt** |
| Wires | Three: red, black, green |

| Wire | Terminal |
|---|---|
| Red | M+ |
| Black | M- |
| Green | Ground |

The higher the voltage, the faster the motor turns.

**This manual gives the two voltage ranges without saying which machine gets which.** The earlier ST725 manual is explicit: 0 to 180 V DC normally, and 0 to 90 V DC on a 120 Vac machine.

The replacement procedure confirms the pair as red M+ and black M-, with a greenish yellow grounding wire.
