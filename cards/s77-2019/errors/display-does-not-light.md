Work through the manual's list in order:

1. The tether cord (safety key) is not in position.
2. The circuit breaker on the front grill has tripped. Push it in until it locks.
3. The plug is disconnected. Push it firmly into the **220 Vac wall outlet** - **110 Vac on a 120 Vac machine**.
4. The breaker panel circuit breaker may have tripped.
5. Treadmill defect. Contact your dealer.

If the display shows **E0** rather than nothing at all, that is the safety key error and it has its own card.
