The manual gives one cause for the on-board 15 amp circuit tripping: **high belt and deck friction**. Follow the belt and deck lubrication section.

**This is the treadmill's own breaker, not the house breaker.** The house breaker tripping while the treadmill's breaker holds is a different problem with a different fix.
