| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| Outlet | **220 volt, 10 amp** grounded. **On a 120 Vac machine: 110 volt, 15 amp.** |
| Circuit | Only the treadmill on that circuit |
| Surface | Flat and level |
| Extension cord | Only if it is **16 AWG or better** with a single outlet on the end |
| GFCI outlet | **Never.** As with any appliance with a large motor, the GFCI will trip often. |

Do not disable the grounded plug with an adapter, and do not modify the cord.

Route the power cord away from every moving part, including the elevation mechanism and the transport wheels.

The machine must be grounded. Improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can cause an electric shock; if you are in any doubt, have an electrician check it.
