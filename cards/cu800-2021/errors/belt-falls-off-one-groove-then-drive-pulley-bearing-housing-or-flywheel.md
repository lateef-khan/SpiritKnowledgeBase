Section 9-2, `Belt falling off`:

> Follow the assembly procedure 4 to install the driving belt (14) and test by revolving forward and backward after adjustment. If the belt falls off, **adjust it one groove toward the direction the belt falling off** and test again. If the belt still falls off, the machine might be so heavily dropped that part dimension deviates. Try to replace the **drive pulley (15), Bearing Housing (10) or flywheel (20)**. If the problem persists, the mainframe could be deformed and the whole unit should be replaced. The possibility of such a circumstance is low as all units were tested prior to shipping.

**The parts are the upright's** - drive pulley, bearing housing, flywheel - where the recumbent books name the drive pulley, the idler wheel assembly and the brake (`spirit-recumbent-errors-drive-belt-falls-off-one-notch-then-drive-pulley-idler-or-brake`). **The direction of the one-groove move is printed the opposite way round**: this book says *toward the direction the belt falling off*, the recumbent books say *on notch beyond against the side it falls off*. Spin the crank both ways after each move; the belt itself tells you which is right.

The tension it is refitted to is the slip entry above it, 180-205 Hz (`cr800-2021-maintenance-belt-slip-180-to-205-hz`). The XBU55 2016 upright answers the same symptom with a test-before-the-covers procedure and no parts: `xbu55-2016-errors-belt-falls-off-refit-and-test-before-the-chain-covers-go-back`.
