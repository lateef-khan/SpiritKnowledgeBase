One drawing, nine labels: **Console**, **Handpulse W/Cable Assembly**, **Slider Cover B**,
**Console Mast Cover**, **Seat**, **Chain Cover (L)**, **Rear Stabilizer**, **Pedal (R)**,
**Chain Cover (R)**.

*Slider Cover B* is the cover on the seat post where it enters the frame; the replacement chapter
calls the same area the *Saddle Cover* and the seat post the *Seat Slider (6)*. The side shrouds
are *Chain Covers* here and *Side Cases* in the replacement text - the same parts.

The book prints **no skeleton drawing**, no dimensions and no weights on this page. The parts
list with item numbers is a separate document: see `spirit-bike-specs-which-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.
