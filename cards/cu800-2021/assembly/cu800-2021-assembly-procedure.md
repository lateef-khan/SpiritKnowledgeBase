Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. Hardware quantities are
on [the hardware card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-cu800-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Step one - rear stabilizer

1. Install the **Rear Stabilizer (No.5)** onto the **Main Frame (No.1)** with the 2
   Bolts (No.50) and 2 Washers (No.151).

That is the whole of step one. Two bolts, and the machine stands up.

## Step two - console mast

1. Unravel the **Computer Cable (No.29)** and snake it through the **Console Mast Cover
   (No.38)** and the **Console Mast (No.2)** until the cable connector comes out of the
   top opening of the console mast. Slide the plastic cover (No.38) onto the console
   mast, in the correct orientation.
2. Install the Console Mast (No.2) onto the **Main Frame (No.1)** with **7 Bolts
   (No.51)**, **6 Flat Washers (No.72) on the side screws** and **1 Curved Washer
   (No.99) on the front screw**, using the Wrench (No.100).

The manual prints "6 Fast Washers"; Flat is meant, and the hardware table for the step
spells it Flat Washer.

## Step three - handlebar

1. Run the **2 Hand Pulse Wires (No.26)** and the Computer Cable (No.29) into the hole
   in the handlebar mounting plate and out through the hole in the console plate.
   Install the **Handle Bar (No.3)** onto the Console Mast (No.2) with 2 Bolts (No.51),
   2 Washers (No.72) and 2 Split Washers (No.103), careful not to pinch the hand pulse
   wires. Snap the black plastic handlebar cover over the joint.

## Step four - console, beverage holder, pedals and seat

1. Insert the Computer Cable (No.29) and 2 Hand Pulse Cables (No.26, 28) into the
   connectors in the back of the **Console (No.34)**. Install the Console (No.34) onto
   the Console Mast (No.2). Secure with **4 bottom Screws (No.58)**.
2. Install the **Beverage Holder (No.116)** onto the Console Mast (No.2) with 2 Screws
   (No.58).
3. Install the **Pedals (No.45 L, 46 R)** onto the **Crank Arms (No.16 L, 16 R)**. The
   left pedal has a reverse thread and will be screwed in with the opposite rotation.
   **Make sure to tighten the pedals as much as possible.**
4. Install the **Seat Mount Slide (No.7)** onto the track on top of the seat tube with
   the seat post facing forward. Install the **Seat (No.19)** onto the seat post and
   fasten the nut using the Wrench (No.100).

**No torque figure is printed for the pedals** - see
[the pedal card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-bike-assembly-pedal-tighten-as-much-as-possible.md).

## What the build never fits

The carton list packs a **Front Stabilizer Cover** and a **Rear Stabilizer Cover** and
no step mentions either. Neither does the carton list name the seat or the seat mount
slide that step 4 fits. See
[the parts card](cu800-2021-assembly-parts-included.md).

The build ends here. There is no first-power-on step: these bikes are
generator-powered and the console starts when you pedal.

## The 2024 redesign reprints this build

The **CU800 2024** manual prints these four steps word for word and its four hardware
tables row for row. **"6 Fast Washers" is still there** in step two, and step four
still fits the seat that the carton panel never lists.

Around the build, two things changed and neither touches the steps: the pre-assembly
page is now the four-item list that tells you to inspect for shipping damage, and each
hardware table is printed below its step rather than beside it. Its carton panel and
its tools panel are unchanged - the same eleven part lines and the same 13/15mm,
12/14mm and Phillips.
