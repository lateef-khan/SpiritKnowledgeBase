Fourteen labels, printed on a line drawing with no heading, no letters and no item
numbers:

- **CONSOLE**
- **HANDLEBARS**
- **CONSOLE FAN**
- **PULSE SENSORS**
- **DRINK BOTTLE HOLDER**
- **CONSOLE MAST**
- **CONSOLE MAST COVER**
- **SEAT**
- **FRONT STABILIZER**
- **CRANK ARM**
- **LEVELERS**
- **PEDALS**
- **MAIN FRAME**
- **REAR STABILIZER**

## This is the only Spirit commercial bike page that points at the levelers

**LEVELERS** is a callout on this drawing and on no other bike diagram in the family -
not the CR800 drawing, not the CU800ENT or CR800ENT photographs, and not the lettered
A-to-M diagrams in the 2024 and 2025 books. It is also the only place in this manual
outside the maintenance chapter where the word appears.

The drawing shows where they are; it gives no count and no wrench size. Those are in
the noise item in the maintenance chapter - two pads under the rear stabiliser, 14mm
wrench. See
[the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md).

## What it names that the rest of the manual does not

**CONSOLE FAN**, **PULSE SENSORS** and **LEVELERS** appear only here in the assembly
chapters. The CR800 drawing calls the grips **PULSE GRIPS**; this one calls them
**PULSE SENSORS**, and the 2024 and 2025 books call them Pulse Grips again.

## The diagram is not a carton list

Nothing on this page tells you what is packed loose. **The carton list does not name
the seat**, which this drawing labels. For what is in the box see
[the parts card](cu800-2021-assembly-parts-included.md).

**The touchscreen printing replaces this drawing with a photograph** carrying eight
labels and no levelers. See
[that card](../../cu800ent-2022/assembly/cu800ent-2022-assembly-parts-diagram-labels.md).
