## Step one - console mast, console and rails

1. Slide the **CONSOLE MAST COVER (72)** onto the **CONSOLE MAST (12)** as far as it
   will go, facing the correct way.
2. Feed the twist tie wire and the **COMPUTER CABLE (50)** from the top opening of
   the **MAIN FRAME (1)** into the bottom of the mast and out of the top.
3. Install the mast into the receiving bracket on top of the main frame. **Do not
   pinch the cables between the tubing.** One bolt is already in the bracket and
   engages the slot at the bottom of the mast; it is tightened **last**.
4. **SPLIT WASHER (152)** onto **BOLT (105)**, hand tightened through the **left**
   side of the mast. A **CURVED WASHER (153)** onto each **BOLT (103)**, both threaded
   into the **front** of the mast tube. Tighten the front bolts as tight as possible
   with the wrench, then the two left side bolts with the same wrench.
5. Connect the 2 **HAND PULSE CABLES (48)**, the **RESISTANCE CABLE (44)** and the
   **COMPUTER CABLE (50)** to the back of the **CONSOLE (43)**. They fit one way only
   and are different sizes; do not force them. Store the excess in the mast tube.
6. Fit the console to the mast bracket with 4 **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (116)**.
7. Attach the **REAR FLOOR SUPPORT (15)** to the **RAILS (2 & 3)** with 2 **Bolts
   (176)** and **Curved Washers (153)** on each side, tightened with the combination
   M5 Allen wrench and Phillips driver.
8. Slide the rail assembly into the **MAIN FRAME (1)**. One **BOLT (176)** through
   each side, with a **FLAT WASHER (137)** and a **NYLOC NUT (130)** on the inside of
   each joint.

## Step two - swing arms

1. Two **WAVE WASHERS (150)** onto each of the left and right console mast axles.
2. Slide the **SWING ARMS (L) and (R), (10 & 11)** onto the matching side.
3. A **FLAT WASHER (141)** onto each **BOLT (99)**, fastened into the end of each
   axle and tightened with the wrench.

**The XE195's swing arms are one piece.** There is no upper and lower arm and no
handlebar wiring to join - the XE295 and XE395ENT both have both.

## Step three - connecting arms

1. Untie the twist tie holding each bushing to the connecting arm end. Line the hole
   in the end of the **CONNECTING ARMS (8 & 9)** up with the hole in the **SWING ARM**
   bracket. **The connecting arm ends go inside the swing arm brackets.** Slide a
   **HEX HEAD BOLT (101)** through each.
2. A **FLAT WASHER (142)** onto each bolt, then a Nyloc nut on each, tightened with
   the two wrenches.

## Step four - covers and levellers

1. Two **WHEEL COVERS (79)** to the rollers with 4 **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (116)**.
2. The **LEFT AND RIGHT SIDE COVER (80)** to the mid-stabilizer tube with 2 screws
   (116); the **RIGHT SIDE COVER (85)** and **LEFT SIDE COVER (86)** to the rear
   stabilizer tube with 2 screws (116).
3. **LEFT HANDLE BAR COVERS (81 & 82)** and **RIGHT HANDLE BAR COVERS (83 & 84)** over
   the handle bar axle connections, **3 SHEET METAL SCREWS (119) on each side**.
4. Check the four floor levellers under the middle and rear of the machine - see
   [the levelling card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xe-assembly-levelling-bottom-nut.md).

## The 2019 printing builds the same way with one different screw

The XE195 2019 manual prints the same four steps, in the same order, with the same
item numbers and counts everywhere except step four's Phillips screw: **item 115,
M5 x 12mm** on the 2019 printing against **item 116, M5 x 10mm** on the 2021. Every
sentence of step four that reads (116) above reads (115) in the 2019 book. Steps one
to three are word for word the same, including the phantom nut 127 below. See
[the hardware card](xe195-2021-assembly-hardware-by-step.md).

**Its machine diagram is not the same either.** The 2019 book labels **SPEAKERS** and
**PULSE GRIPS**; the 2021 book has no speakers label and says PULSE SENSORS. See
[the diagram card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xe-assembly-parts-diagram-labels-2021.md).

## Nothing in this build needs mains power

The XE195 has no incline, so no step asks you to plug the machine in part way
through. The XE395ENT and XE395 2023 builds do; this one does not. First power-on is
simply plugging the cord in - the console powers up on its own and runs a self-test.

## The nut in step three has two item numbers

The step tells you to tighten **"a NYLOC NUT (127)"**. **Item 127 is not in this
book's step three panel, or in any of its four panels.** The panel for step three
supplies **192, NYLOC NUT, 5/16" x 9T, quantity 2**, which is exactly the two nuts
this job needs. See
[the hardware card](xe195-2021-assembly-hardware-by-step.md).
