## Item 120 is supplied and never used

Three **3.5 x 16mm sheet metal screws** are in the step 1 bag and **no job in any of
the four steps fits them**. On the XE295, XE395ENT and XE395 2023 the same item 120
holds the console mast cover on, and their step text says so; the XE195's step text
does not. Three screws will be left in the bag.

## Item 127 is called for in step three and is in no panel

Step three job 3 says to tighten "a NYLOC NUT (127)" onto each bolt. The step-three
panel carries **192**, the 5/16" x 9T Nyloc nut, in the exact quantity the job needs,
and 127 appears nowhere in this book. Use item 192. The XE295 and XE395ENT manuals do
carry a separate item 127 - a 5/16" x 7T Nyloc nut - in a step this machine does not
have, which is the likely source of the number.

## Item 105 loses its closing inch mark

The panel prints item 105 as **3/8" X 2-1/4** and item 176 as **3/8" X 2-1/4"** -
the same dimension, one with the mark and one without. They are two different bolts
with the same printed size and different item numbers; order by item number.

## What closes and what does not

- Step 1's **six** of item 176 close exactly: four for the rear floor support, two
  for the rail-to-frame joint.
- Step 2's **four** wave washers close exactly: two per axle.
- Step 4's **eight** of item 116 close exactly, and its **six** of item 119 close at
  three per side.
- Step 1's **six** curved washers (153) do not close against the printed text. Job 4
  puts one on each of two bolts, and job 7 says "Curved Washers (153) on each side"
  without a count.
