The XE195's programs are **Manual**, four presets - **Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio and Interval** - and one
**HR** program. That is **six**, which matches the feature callout on the machine drawing: "Six
innovative programs offer a variety of work-outs".

The preset page says "the elliptical has **four** different programs that have been designed for a
variety of workouts. These four programs have factory preset work level profiles."

**Two things the XE195 does not have, that its 2021 siblings do:**

- **No Strength program.** The XE295, XG400 and XE795 all carry one.
- **No custom or User program.** The XE195 manual has no "Custom User Defined Programs" section, and
  none of its program steps offers to save a finished workout - unlike the XE295, XG400 and XE795,
  whose Manual step 8 does.

**It has only one heart rate program**, not two - see
`xe195-2021-programs-single-heart-rate-key-defaults-to-sixty-percent`.

*Its heart rate page still carries the boilerplate sentence about "any of the **nine** different
programs". The machine has six.*
