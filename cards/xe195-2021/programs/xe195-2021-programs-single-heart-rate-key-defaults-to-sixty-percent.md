The XE195 has **one** heart rate program, reached with a single **HR** key. There is no HR 1 / HR 2
choice.

- **The default setting is 60% of your projected maximum heart rate.**
- The manual's guidance on where to aim: the **60-80% range is more conducive to fat loss goals**,
  and **81-100% ranges are generally used for cardiovascular conditioning goals and high intensity
  interval training**.
- After you select your heart rate target the program will attempt to keep you at, or **within 3 to 5
  heart beats per minute** of, that value.
- **You must wear the heart rate transmitter strap for this program.**

The heart rate figure the console first shows is derived from the percentage you accepted; changing
that number moves the percentage with it.

**The 60-80% / 81-100% split is not the same guidance as the training-zone page in the same
manual**, which recommends 60% for burning fat and 80% for strengthening the cardiovascular system
and calls 60-80% the zone for maximum benefit. The source does not reconcile the two - see
`spirit-ce-programs-target-zone-of-108-to-144-beats`.

**The XE295, XG400 and XE795 2021, built in the same year, have two HR keys at 60% and 80%** - see
`spirit-xe-programs-heart-rate-programs-hold-you-within-three-to-five-beats`.

**The XE195 2019 printing (printed pages 29-30) prints this page word for word** - one HR key, a 60%
default, the 60-80% / 81-100% guidance, the 3-to-5-beat tolerance and the same unreconciled clash
with its own training-zone page.
