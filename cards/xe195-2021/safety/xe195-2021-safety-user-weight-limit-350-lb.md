**350 lb.** Printed once, as the last bullet of the safety instructions list:

> Recommended Weight Limit: 350 lb

- **It is the only weight figure in the manual.** No kilogram equivalent, no second statement, and
  no sticker.
- **The manual prints it as `Recommended`**, not as a maximum, and says nothing about what happens
  above it.

**350 lb is the XE195's figure alone.** It is the lowest in the Spirit residential elliptical range
and belongs to no other machine:

| Machine | Weight limit |
|---|---|
| **XE195** | **350 lb** |
| XE295, XG400, XE795-2021 | 400 lb, worded `Recommended Weight Limit` (`spirit-xe-2021-safety-recommended-weight-limit-400-lb`) |
| XE395-2023, XE795-2023 | 400 lbs, worded `User Weight Limit` (`ces880-2025-safety-user-weight-limit-400-lbs`) |
| XE100, XE200, XE300, XE150, XE350, XE550, XE395-2010, XE395ENT | none printed (`xt-2015-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`) |

**Never quote 400 lb for an XE195**, and never quote the Spirit commercial elliptical figure of
450 lb (`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`). The XE195 and XE295 manuals are otherwise
the same book - they differ in this bullet and in one word on their electrical page - so the
temptation to read one for the other is real and the figures do not transfer.
