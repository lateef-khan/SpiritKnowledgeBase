| Warranty | Frame | Brake | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | Lifetime | Lifetime | **5 Years** | 1 Year |

**The four figures are identical in both XE195 printings**, so the answer does not depend on which book the customer holds. What is printed above and below the table does:

| Printing | Heading above the table | Prisons footnote under it |
|---|---|---|
| XE195 2019 | Elliptical Trainer Warranty - Effective **February 04, 2019** | **none** |
| XE195 2021 | Elliptical Trainer Warranty - Effective **November 30, 2021** | *\*Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage.* |

**The words *prison* and *correctional* appear nowhere in the 2019 manual** - not under the table and not in its exclusion list. Do not quote that footnote to the owner of one (`spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`).

**Parts is the only figure that differs from its siblings, and it is half - in both printings.** The XE295, the XG400 and the XE795 read **Parts 10 Years** on the same row with the same Frame Lifetime, Brake Lifetime and Labor 1 Year, and they read it in the February 04, 2019 printings as well as the November 30, 2021 ones; so does the XE395 and the XE895 of August 22, 2018. Quoting `spirit-xe-warranty-residential-periods-ten-year-parts` for this machine would promise the customer five years the manual does not give, whichever year they are reading.

**Neither manual prints a commercial table.** There is no light commercial row, no hours cap in the table, and no second set of periods anywhere in either document. The exclusion list nevertheless bars any use outside a residential setting - see `spirit-xe-warranty-use-environment`.

**The warranty section calls the machine a fitness bike seven times, in both printings.** It is copy-paste from a bike manual and it does not change any figure in this table, but it is worth knowing before quoting the section to a customer: `xe195-2021-warranty-fitness-bike-wording`.

Two things this table does not tell you on its own. When the clock starts is stated in the paragraph above it (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`), and the one-year labor figure is dated separately in the SERVICE paragraph on the next page (`xt-2023-warranty-labor-period`).
