| Warranty | Frame | Brake | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | Lifetime | Lifetime | **5 Years** | 1 Year |

Printed above the table: **Elliptical Trainer Warranty - Effective November 30, 2021**. A footnote under it reads *\*Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage.*

**Parts is the only figure that differs from its 2021 siblings, and it is half.** The XE295, the XG400 and the XE795 - all three carrying the same November 30, 2021 effective date - read **Parts 10 Years** on the same row, with the same Frame Lifetime, Brake Lifetime and Labor 1 Year. Quoting `spirit-xe-warranty-residential-periods-ten-year-parts` for this machine would promise the customer five years the manual does not give.

**This manual prints one row and no commercial table.** There is no light commercial row, no hours cap in the table, and no second set of periods anywhere in the document. Its exclusion list nevertheless bars any use outside a residential setting - see `spirit-xe-warranty-use-environment`.

**The warranty section of this manual calls the machine a fitness bike seven times.** It is copy-paste from a bike manual and it does not change any figure in this table, but it is worth knowing before quoting the section to a customer: `xe195-2021-warranty-fitness-bike-wording`.

Two things this table does not tell you on its own. When the clock starts is stated in the paragraph above it (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`), and the one-year labor figure is dated separately in the SERVICE paragraph on the next page (`xt-2023-warranty-labor-period`).
