**The XT175 has the shortest cover of the five 2007 XT treadmills: two years on electronics, belt and rollers, and four on the deck.** The table is headed **XT175 (Home Warranty)** and prints four rows:

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| Drive motor and Frame | Lifetime |
| Electronics/belt/rollers | 2 Years |
| Deck | 4 Years |
| Labor | 1 Year |

The page spells rollers `rolers` in this row and the XT275 row; the word is rollers.

**The XT275 doubles the electronics period and makes the deck lifetime** (`xt275-2007-warranty-home-periods`); the XT375, XT475 and XT675 go to five years on electronics, belt and rollers and add a ten-year row for everything else (`spirit-xt-2007-warranty-home-periods-xt375-xt475-xt675`). No commercial column is printed for the XT175. The base term that sits under these rows - 5 years from retail sale or 18 months from factory shipping, whichever comes first - is on `spirit-xt-2007-warranty-five-years-or-eighteen-months-from-shipping`.

**Do not answer this from a later XT table.** The 2015 and 2023 XT books print Frame / Motor / Deck / Parts / Labor with Parts at 5 or 10 years and no electronics row at all (`xt185-2015-residential-warranty-periods`, `xt-2023-warranty-residential-coverage-periods`).
