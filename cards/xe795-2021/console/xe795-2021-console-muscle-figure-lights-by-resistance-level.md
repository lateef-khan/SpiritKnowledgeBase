**There is an anatomical figure at the top of the console.** It lights the areas the machine is
working, during **any** of the programs, and you can steer it by customising the resistance profile
during set-up.

**The manual states plainly that the upper body LEDs will not light.** That is the rule for this
machine, not a fault to chase.

**Lower body, three degrees of engagement:** **Green** is minimal involvement, **yellow** medium,
**red** full or heavy activation. The banding is by **resistance level**:

| Levels | What lights |
|---|---|
| **1-20** | **Green** - Hamstrings and Gluteals; **Amber** - Quadriceps and Calves |
| **21-40** | **Amber** - Hamstrings and Gluteals; **Red** - Quadriceps and Calves |

**The manual names the middle colour twice and differently.** The list of degrees calls it
**yellow**; the two bands call it **Amber**. Nothing distinguishes them, and the XE295 and XG400
manuals use **Amber** in both places.

**The banding needs the 40-level scale.** This is the only residential XE elliptical with 40
resistance levels, which is why the bands read 1-20 and 21-40:
`spirit-xe795-console-quick-start-with-forty-resistance-levels`.

**The XE295 and XG400 figure follows a different rule** - the upper body does light, and the lower
body is banded by pedal direction: `spirit-xe-console-muscle-figure-lights-by-pedal-direction`.
