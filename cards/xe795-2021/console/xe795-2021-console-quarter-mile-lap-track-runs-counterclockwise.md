**The 1/4-mile track - one lap - is displayed around the dot matrix window.**

- **The flashing segment indicates your progress.**
- **Once the 1/4 mile (metric: 0.4 k) is complete the feature begins again.**
- **The lap track moves counterclockwise.**
- **There is a lap counter in the message window** for monitoring your distance.

**Three manuals print this paragraph word for word**: the XE795 of August 2018 (p. 16) and of 2021,
and the **XE895-2018** (p. 19). The XE895 splits it slightly differently - it puts "The Lap track will
move in a counterclockwise direction" at the end of its Dot Matrix paragraph instead - but every fact
is the same.

**The lap count reaches other screens too.** Laps completed is one of the readings the Message Center
scans through, and **total Laps** is one of the five fields on the Workout Summary.

**The track is not a 2021 feature.** It was already in the August 2018 XE795 book, and in the XE895
of the same printing run.

**The XE795-2023 dropped it.** Its manual describes the same twenty-column dot matrix but no track,
and its Workout Summary is the only one in the family with **no Laps field** at all:
`spirit-xe-console-what-the-workout-summary-lists`.

**This is not the switchable track of the 2005 and 2007 consoles.** On the XE550 and XE300 the track
replaces the profile when you press a Display button; here it is drawn around the profile and is
always there: `spirit-xe-console-dot-matrix-switches-between-profile-and-a-quarter-mile-track`.
