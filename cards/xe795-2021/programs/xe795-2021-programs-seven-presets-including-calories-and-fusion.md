The XE795 2021 preset page lists **seven** programs: **Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Strength, Interval,
Calories and Fusion**. With **Manual**, **U1 and U2** and **HR 1 and HR 2** that is twelve
selections in all, although the feature callout on the machine drawing says "**Ten** innovative
programs offer a variety of work-outs". The manual does not reconcile the two counts.

**The page contradicts itself in its own opening sentence**: "The elliptical trainer has **seven**
different programs that have been designed for a variety of workouts. These **five** programs have
factory preset work level profiles for achieving different goals." The word "five" is left over from
the five-preset consoles; seven descriptions follow it.

The extra two:

- **CALORIES** - described with the same words as Fat Burn: a quick progression up to the maximum
  resistance level, default or user input, sustained for 2/3 of the workout, challenging your ability
  to sustain your energy output for an extended period. **The manual gives no way to tell the Calories
  and Fat Burn profiles apart**; both are recorded as printed. For how you set a calorie goal, see
  `xe795-2021-programs-calories-program-counts-down-to-a-calorie-goal`.
- **FUSION** - the Interval description with "high levels of **cardio & strength** intensity" in place
  of "high levels of intensity". For how you set it up, see
  `spirit-xe-programs-fusion-alternates-cardio-intervals-with-dumbbell-work`.

**Both 2021 printings of the XE795 manual are identical here.** The February and November printings
have word-for-word identical program chapters, 4,395 words each; the November printing's only
addition to the book is a cleaning and sanitizing section, which the February printing does not
carry.
