**The XE795 of 2021 has two printings of its owner's manual and they are the same machine.** The
February 19, 2021 printing is 40 pages; the November 30, 2021 printing is 44. Their maintenance
chapters are otherwise identical word for word.

**The one difference is that the November printing carries a SANITIZING YOUR SPIRIT FITNESS
EQUIPMENT section and the February printing does not.** February prints no sanitizing heading, no
alcohol figure and no support-article link, anywhere in the book.

The four bullets the November printing adds are the standard Spirit ones - a **75% isopropyl
alcohol** solution on hard plastics, a conditioner after sanitizing on upholstered or soft-plastic
surfaces, the option of mixing your own 75% solution from isopropyl alcohol and distilled water,
and a link to Spirit's support article. They are held by `ct900-sanitizing-equipment`, and the
November text matches the residential XE195 wording word for word.

**Do not tell an XE795 owner their manual is incomplete.** Both printings are genuine and both
describe the same machine. If a customer says their book has no sanitizing page, they have the
February printing; the procedure still applies to the machine.

## The rest of the residential XE range

The **XE195, XE295 and XG400** 2021 manuals carry the same four bullets, and the **2023 XE395 and
XE795** carry them inside a two-column Maintenance & Care page alongside the post-workout routine
(`spirit-2026t-maintenance-post-workout-machine-care`). The **XE395ENT 2021** does **not** - like
the February XE795, its GENERAL MAINTENANCE page ends at the leveling pads. The
**XE100/XE200/XE300**, **XE150/XE350/XE550** and **XE395 2010** manuals predate the section
entirely.
