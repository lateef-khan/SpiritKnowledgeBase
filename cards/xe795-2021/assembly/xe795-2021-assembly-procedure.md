## Step one - levellers, end caps, transport wheels and roller covers

1. **Tilt the elliptical to one side** - a piece of Styrofoam or a block underneath
   helps - and install the **3 LEVELERS (81)** on the underside. Repeat on the other
   side.
2. Press the **6 END CAPS (82)** into the oval stabilizer tubes.
3. Attach the **TRANSPORT WHEELS (80)** to each bracket with 2 **BOLTS (92)** and 2
   **NUTS (99)**. **Partially tighten only** - over tightening stops the wheels
   turning. Use the 13/14mm wrench and a 5mm L wrench.
4. Fit the **ROLLER WHEEL COVERS (45)** with 4 **SCREWS (55)**.

## Step two - console mast and upright covers

1. Feed the twist tie wire and **COMPUTER CABLE (27)** into the bottom of the
   **CONSOLE MAST (2)** and out of the top, without pinching it.
2. Secure the mast to the frame with **BOLTS (85)** and **FLAT WASHERS (84)** at each
   side, and a **BOLT (85)** with a **CURVED WASHER (86)** at both the front and the
   back. Tighten with the 13/14 wrench.
3. Bring **UPRIGHT COVER LEFT (43)** and **UPRIGHT COVER RIGHT (56)** together at the
   mast and secure with 3 **SHEET METAL SCREWS (56)**.

## Step three - swing arms

1. Remove the foam from the console mast axles and the plastic ties from the swing
   arm tube.
2. The arms are labelled L and R. Slide the **WAVE WASHER (110)** and the **LEFT SWING
   ARM (5)** onto the left axle. **The fit is snug; do not force it or use a hammer**,
   the bearings can be damaged.
3. Secure the arm to the mast with the **BUSHING WASHER (51)** and the **SOCKET HEAD
   CAP BOLT (89)**, tightened with the **8mm L wrench**.
4. Snap on the decorative **BUSHING COVER (79)**, then repeat on the right.

## Step four - connecting arms and pedal arms

1. Slide the axle of the **LEFT CONNECTING ARM (9)** through the rotation tube on the
   left pedal arm.
2. Secure it with the **LEFT CONNECTION BRACKET (15)** and 3 **KEY HEAD SOCKET BOLTS
   (91)**, tightened with the **5mm L wrench**.
3. Line the socket joint at the end of the pedal arm up with the bracket on the bottom
   of the swing arm and secure with the **BOLT (90)**, **WASHER (106)** and **NUT
   (87)**, tightened with the 13/14 and the 12/14 wrench.
4. Repeat on the right.

## Step five - console and water bottle cage

1. Secure the **CONSOLE (26)** to the console mast with 4 **SCREWS (55)**.
2. Secure the **WATER BOTTLE CAGE (83)** to the console mast with 2 **SCREWS (55)**.

## Step six - foot pedals

Install the **FOOT PEDALS (L 47) (R 48)** with 8 **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (55)**.

## The console goes on fifth, and it plugs into nothing you can see

**No step in this build connects a cable to the console.** Step two feeds the computer
cable up the mast and step five screws the console on; the connection is never
described. Every other residential XE build names the connectors and tells you to
plug them in.

**There is no power cord and no power step.** The console runs off a built-in
generator - it wakes when you start pedalling and goes to stand-by when the flywheel
stops.

## The 2018 printing builds identically too

The XE795 2018 manual prints the same six steps, the same six hardware panels, the
same item numbers and the same counts, word for word. **Its carton has no power cord
either**, and its machine diagram uses the February wording, PULSE GRIPS. Three
printings of this machine and one build.

## The February and November printings build identically

All six steps, all six hardware panels, every item number and every count are word
for word the same in both books. The only difference anywhere in the assembly chapter
is one label on the machine diagram, **PULSE GRIPS** in February and **PULSE SENSORS**
in November. They are one machine, `xe795-2021`. What the November printing adds is a
cleaning and sanitizing section elsewhere in the book; the February printing does not
carry it.

## Item 56 is a cover and a screw in the same sentence

Step two job 3 fits "UPRIGHT COVER RIGHT (56)" using "3 SHEET METAL SCREWS (56)".
Its hardware panel gives **56 as a 3.5 x 16mm sheet metal screw**, quantity 3. Both
printings do this.

## How many bolts hold the mast on

Step two says "8 BOLTS (85) and 6 FLAT WASHERS (84) to each side of the mast", which
reads as sixteen bolts. The panel supplies **eight**. The **XE795 2023** manual
rewrites the same sentence as "3 BOLTS (85) and 3 FLAT WASHERS (84) to each side",
which with the front and back bolts comes to eight bolts and six washers - exactly
the panel. Take the 2023 reading; see
[the 2023 build](../../xe795-2023/assembly/xe795-2023-assembly-procedure.md).
