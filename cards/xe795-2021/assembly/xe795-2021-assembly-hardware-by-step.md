## Step one fits three things the panel does not supply

The panel holds four screws, two bolts and two nuts. Step one also installs **6
LEVELERS (81)**, **6 END CAPS (82)** and the **ROLLER WHEEL COVERS (45)**, and none of
those three item numbers is in any hardware panel in the book. The levellers and end
caps are loose parts, and the carton list counts the levellers - **6 Levelers** - but
not the end caps. See
[the carton list](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xe-assembly-parts-included-2021.md).

## Items 85 and 91 are both M8 x 15L

Item **85** bolts the console mast to the frame in step two, eight of them. Item
**91** is called a **key head socket bolt** in step four and holds the connection
bracket to the connecting arm, six of them, driven with a 5mm L wrench. **Same
printed size, two item numbers, two different heads.** The 2023 manual calls item 85
a hex head bolt and item 91 a button head socket bolt, which is the clearer
description of the pair.

## Item 55 is the only screw in three of the six steps

Four in step one, six in step five, eight in step six - eighteen M5 x 10mm Phillips
screws across the build, all one item number. Every one of the three counts closes
exactly against its step.

## Item 56 is used as both a cover and a screw

Step two names "UPRIGHT COVER RIGHT (56)" and "3 SHEET METAL SCREWS (56)" in the same
job. The panel is unambiguous: **56 is the 3.5 x 16mm sheet metal screw**.

## No parts list to check any of this against

Neither XE795 2021 printing has a numbered parts list or an exploded view. The 2023
XE795 manual does, at pp. 37-40, but it renumbers most of the machine and its item
numbers must not be used on a 2021.
