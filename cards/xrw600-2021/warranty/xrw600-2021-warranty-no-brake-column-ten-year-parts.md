**One row, three columns.** The table has a single **Residential** row:

| Warranty | Frame | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | Lifetime | 10 Years | 1 Year |

and an asterisked footnote beneath it: *Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage.*

**The brake column was dropped between the two XRW600 printings, and nothing replaced it.** The February 2019 manual for the same machine prints Frame, **Brake**, Parts and Labor, and warrants the brake for **Lifetime** - `xrw600-2019-warranty-lifetime-frame-and-brake-ten-year-parts`. This one prints Frame, Parts and Labor only. On this book a brake fault is a part, covered for ten years, not for life.

**Nothing else in the table moved.** Frame is Lifetime in both, Parts is 10 Years in both, Labor is 1 Year in both. The brake column is the whole of the change, and the two warranty sections otherwise match at **98.5%** at word level. Do not treat the two printings as two warranties; treat them as one warranty minus a column.

**Which book governs is not stated.** The manual does not say whether a machine sold under the February 2019 terms keeps its lifetime brake. The effective date on the page is the only handle: `spirit-rower-warranty-effective-dates`.

**There is no commercial row**, and the use limit lives in exclusion 8 instead: `spirit-rower-warranty-use-environment`.

**Labor is one year, and the SERVICE paragraph dates it differently** - twelve months from the bill of sale or eighteen months from factory shipping, with no rule for which governs. Quote both.
