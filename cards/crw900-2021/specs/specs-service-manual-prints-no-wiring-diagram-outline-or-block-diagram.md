**The CRW900 service manual is eighteen pages and has no electrical chapter of any kind.** Its
table of contents runs *1. Troubleshooting* (speed sensor, plastic handle belt, trouble with
console) and *2. Maintenance* (lubrication grease) - and that is the whole book. Every other
Spirit rower service manual in the repository opens with an Outlines drawing and carries a block
diagram, a display-board chapter and a circuit diagram; this one has none of them, and no page
that a render recovers holds one either (eleven of its pages are flattened images and were read
from the OCR).

**What it does say about the electrics**, all of it inside troubleshooting:

- The **speed sensor** sits in a frame plug against a **magnetic ring with 6 magnets**, with a gap
  of **2-3 mm**; it pulls out of the frame after the plug and a washer come off, and "connect the
  cables" is the whole wiring instruction.
- The console runs on **batteries** ("the console does not illuminate after battery
  installation ... check to see if the batteries are installed in the correct position"). No
  cell type or count is printed here.
- The console has a **VR setting** mode for the tank adjuster's resistance range, entered with
  ENTER+UP+DOWN.

**There is no adapter, no voltage, no pin table, no board code and no part number anywhere in the
book**, and no parts list or exploded view either
(`spirit-rower-specs-parts-which-service-manuals-print-a-parts-list`). For the mechanism of the
resistance - a water tank with a ten-level adjuster - see
`crw900-2021-specs-ten-level-tank-adjuster`. Do not answer a CRW900 wiring question from the
CRW800 books; they are air rowers with a gear motor and a generator or adapter.
