**The book is a 2019 book carrying a 2021 warranty page.** Its two dates do not agree and the manual never reconciles them:

- **Warranty page, printed p. 61:** CRW900 WATER ROWER WARRANTY **EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 22, 2021**
- **Back cover, PDF p. 68:** CRW900 Owners Manual, **© 2019** All Rights Reserved, **Revision 4: 10.11.2019**

**The revision stamp names the document; the effective date names the warranty.** Spirit prints its own revision stamp on the last page under the Jonesboro address, and it is the best evidence of when a book was printed. This one says 11 October 2019. The warranty page bound into it took effect two years later, so the warranty section was replaced and the rest of the book was not.

**The stamp is invisible to text extraction.** The back cover is a flat image; `pdftotext` returns nothing from it. It has to be rendered and read. Anyone dating this manual from its extracted text alone will find no stamp at all and fall back on the filename, which says 2021.

**For a warranty question, October 22, 2021 is the answer.** The five-column table on that page is what the machine is sold under - `crw900-2021-warranty-five-column-periods-tank-and-seals`. The 2019 stamp does not shorten it or replace it.

**For anything else in the book, treat the content as 2019.** Assembly, console, programs and maintenance in this manual are the October 2019 revision. A later CRW900 revision, if one exists, is not in this knowledge base.

**This is a known Spirit pattern, not a one-off.** The CU900 manual does the same thing in the other direction - a 2018 revision stamp with a warranty page effective November 2021. When the copyright year, the revision stamp and the warranty date disagree, the stamp dates the book and the effective date dates the warranty, and nothing else on the page is evidence of either.
