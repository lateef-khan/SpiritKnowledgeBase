**Five columns - the widest warranty table of any Spirit rower**, and the only one that names the tank:

| Warranty | Frame | Parts | Tank & Seals | Wearable Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial | 10 Years | 2 Years | 3 Years | 1 Year | 1 Year |
| Residential | Lifetime | 5 Years | 5 Years | 5 Years | 2 Years |

with the footnote *\*Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage.*

**This is the only Spirit rower whose frame is not Lifetime in every row.** A CRW900 in a facility has a **10 Years** frame. Every other rower manual here prints Lifetime in both rows.

**"Wearable Parts" is named and never defined.** The manual gives no list of which parts are wearable, so there is no way to tell from the book whether a strap, a seat roller or a bungee falls in that column at one year or in Parts at two. Do not guess a list; say the manual does not print one.

**The tank and its seals are three years in a facility and five at home** - the tank's only appearance in the warranty. The sibling CRW800H2O gives the tank no column at all and covers it as a Part: `crw800h2o-warranty-coverage-periods`. Two water rowers, two different tank answers.

**A facility CRW900 is the least covered machine in the range**: two-year parts, one-year wearables and one-year labor, against five and two at home.

**The row is labelled "Commercial" with no qualifier.** Unlike the CRW800 and CRW800H2O, whose rows read *(Non-dues paying facility)*, this table does not say whether a dues-paying club counts - and this manual's exclusion list carries no use-environment item either, so the book never answers it: `spirit-water-rower-warranty-exclusions-eight-items`.

**The date on this page is not the date of the book.** The warranty page is effective October 22, 2021; the back cover stamps *Revision 4: 10.11.2019*: `crw900-2021-warranty-effective-date-against-a-2019-back-cover`.
