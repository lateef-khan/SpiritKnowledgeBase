**This rower does not fold, and the manual prints no moving procedure.**

Two **Transport Wheel Assemblies [6]** are counted on the carton panel and bolted to the
outside of the side rails in step one, with an instruction not to over-tighten the axle so
the wheel can turn. **Nothing anywhere in the book says how to use them** - no lifting
point, no direction to tip the machine, no page on relocating it.

## The one sentence that touches storage

From the SAFEGUARDS page:

> **After rowing exercise, please allow the unit to sit for 5 minutes before standing it up
> for storage.**

That is the whole of it. It implies the machine is stored **standing on end**, and it is
printed thirteen pages before the build, among the safety warnings, not with the transport
wheels. **The reason for the five minutes is not given** - the water in the tank is the
obvious candidate and the manual does not say so.

## The book stands the rower up twice, but for assembly

Step four tightens the frame bolts "from the vertical position" and step five opens with
"**Stand the Rower Vertically**". Both are build instructions. Neither says how to raise
it, how heavy it is at that point, or whether the tank should be empty.

## How the absence was checked

A loose-word search of the whole document returns *fold* nowhere, *storage* only in the
sentence above and in the tank's own "storage chamber", and *transport* only in the part
name. Every page of the assembly chapter was rendered at 300 dpi and compared against the
extraction; none of them is a flattened image hiding a panel.

## The other three Spirit rowers each answer this differently

Do not carry any of them across: the **CRW800** folds its rail on a knob and rolls away on
the rear; the **XRW600** folds the rail on a release lever *and* the rear stabiliser on a
knob; the **CRW800H2O** does not fold either, but does print a moving and upright-storage
page. See the linked cards.
