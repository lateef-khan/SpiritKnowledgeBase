**Sensor removal**

1. Remove the **frame plug**.
2. Pull the **speed sensor** out of the frame.
3. Remove the **washer** from the speed sensor.

**Sensor re-installation**

1. Fit the washer onto the new sensor.
2. Connect the cables.
3. Install the speed sensor as shown.
4. Replace the frame plug.

That is the whole procedure: seven photographs, one caption each, no tool and no screw. The pages are pictures
with the captions drawn in; `pdftotext` returns only the headings, so this card was read from the render.

Before replacing it, the same section says to check the **2 to 3 mm gap between the sensor and the magnetic
ring**, that **all 6 magnets** in the ring are present and flush, and that the internal sensor has not receded
more than a few millimetres into its housing - those checks are troubleshooting, not this replacement.
