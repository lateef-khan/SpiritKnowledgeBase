**The Rower Seat [3] arrives fully assembled** and the cushioned seat can be mounted at
various heights for added comfort and usability. **The Spacers [20] are optional.**

To mount the seat in the higher position:

1. **Remove the four pre-installed M8 x 130mm screws** and lift off the cushioned rower
   seat.
2. Place the **four Spacers [20]** between the cushioned seat and the seat frame.
3. Secure everything with the **four M8 x 160mm screws [33]**.

**Keep the four M8 x 130mm screws you removed**, in case the seat is ever returned to its
original lower position. The manual says so explicitly; nothing else in the book supplies
them again.

## This is why four long bolts are left over

Item 33 is counted on the carton panel and used by no step of the build. **Four M8 x 160mm
bolts still in the bag at the end of a standard build are correct**, not a missed joint.
Item 20, the four spacers, is the same. See
[the hardware card](crw900-2021-assembly-hardware-totals.md).

## The page says "various heights" and describes two

The heading offers a choice and the procedure gives exactly one alternative: with the
spacers or without. **No height figure is printed**, and the spacers are not dimensioned
anywhere in the book.

## It belongs between steps two and three

The page sits immediately after the seat is dropped into the rails and before the mainframe
goes on, which is the last moment the seat can be lifted out easily. The manual does not
say to decide here; it prints the option and moves on.
