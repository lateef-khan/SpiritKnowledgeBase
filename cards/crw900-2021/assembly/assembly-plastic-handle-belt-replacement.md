**Sewing face up.** The one caution on the page: the new belt goes into the handle with its sewn face upward.

**Removal**

1. Remove the **six M3 x 20 mm countersunk screws** and the **two M3 x 12 mm round sunk screws**.
2. Remove the **handle plastic cap**.
3. Remove the **belt bush** (a short pin, slid out sideways).
4. Remove the old belt.

**Installation**

1. "Installation new belt bush": the new belt goes into the handle - **CAUTION: sewing face up** (the drawing
   points at the belt's sewing face).
2. Reinstall the belt bush.
3. The belt is placed in the fixing slot and covered with the handle plastic cap.
4. Reinstall the six M3 x 20 mm countersunk screws and the two M3 x 12 mm round sunk screws.

The book gives a video for it: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R8U6uKkzips

These pages are drawings with lettered captions; the text layer holds only the headings and the link, and the
OCR supplement read the captions upside down. The screw sizes above are from the render. **The CRW800H2O
water rower keeps its strap on a mesh belt wheel with a volute spring**, a different assembly with its own card.
