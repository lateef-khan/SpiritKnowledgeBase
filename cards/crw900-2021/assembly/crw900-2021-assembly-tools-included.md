Three tools, and the manual states plainly that they are enough:

| Item | Qty | Tool |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | 1 | 5mm Allen key |
| 13 | 1 | 6mm Allen key |
| 14 | 1 | 13mm wrench |

**"NOTE: All tools required to assemble the rower are included within the packaging."**
- CRW900 PRE-ASSEMBLY, p. 19.

## There is no TOOLS panel

The three tools are items 12 to 14 of the **CRW900 BOX 1 & 2 CONTENTS** list, printed
between the M8 x 45mm bolts and the AA batteries. No page of the book separates tools from
parts, and **no step of the build names a tool at all** - not by size and not by item
number. Which key fits which bolt is left to the reader.

**The manual never pairs a tool with a fastener.** Read against the hardware sizes, the
6mm key suits the M8 bolts, the 5mm key the M6 bolts and the 13mm wrench the M8 nuts on the
far side of each joint - but that is the sizes talking, not the book.

## Item 18 is touch-up paint, not a tool

It is listed in the same panel and is the only consumable there besides the water
treatment tablets. The rower has a wooden frame and the build ends by suggesting furniture
polish; the paint is for the metalwork.

## Nothing here is a torque wrench

No step of this build gives a torque figure. Two steps say **DO NOT TIGHTEN** and leave the
fasteners loose until the machine is stood vertically - see
[the build](crw900-2021-assembly-procedure.md).

## This is not the other water rower's toolkit

The Spirit **CRW800H2O** carton holds a **5mm Allen wrench and a 13-17mm wrench**, two
tools rather than three, and its steps do name them by item number. See
[that card](../../crw800h2o/assembly/crw800h2o-assembly-tools-included.md).
