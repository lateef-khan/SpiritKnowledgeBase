**Item 33 is printed out of order**, at the end of the first panel after item 18, because
it belongs to the optional seat height - see
[the spacer card](crw900-2021-assembly-seat-height-spacers.md).

## The batteries disagree with the step that fits them

This panel counts **4 AA batteries (item 15)**. Step four says **"Install supplied 2 AA
batteries [15]"**. Both figures are printed in the native text of the PDF, four pages
apart, and neither page mentions the other. **Expect four in the box** and fit whatever
the console holder takes.

## More fasteners arrive on the machine than in the bag

Five kinds of fastener are described as pre-installed and appear on neither panel: the
bolt under the **rear brace**, the **M8 x 60mm bolt, two M8 washers and nylon nut** at
each transport wheel, the **M6 x 20mm bolts** holding the four rail bumpers, and the
**four M8 x 130mm screws** through the seat. A count of the bag against these panels will
not tell you whether the build is complete.

## Neither panel counts a leveller

The machine has none. It stands on **four elevated legs (item 23)** fitted in step five,
and the build ends by telling you to choose a flat surface rather than to adjust
anything - see [that card](crw900-2021-assembly-choose-a-flat-surface.md).

## What the panels do not say

No shipping weight, no box dimensions, and no statement of which item is in which of the
two boxes - the heading covers both at once.
