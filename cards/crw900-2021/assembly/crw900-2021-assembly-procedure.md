Item numbers in square brackets are the assembly-drawing numbers, as the manual's own
pre-assembly page explains. Quantities are on
[the hardware card](crw900-2021-assembly-hardware-totals.md). **No step of this build
names a tool.**

**This is not the CRW800H2O.** The other Spirit water rower shares no item number and no
step with this machine.

## Before you start

1. **Position the shipping carton so the heavy end is at the bottom.**
2. Cut the straps, lift the box over the unit, remove the foam inserts and check the
   parts list.
3. Locate the hardware package. Remove the tools first, then take the hardware for each
   step as you reach it.

> **CAUTION: Damage to the rower during assembly is not covered** as part of the limited
> warranty. Do not drop the rower or lean it on its side; stand it up in the normal
> upright position on a stable surface so it cannot tip over during assembly.

## Step one - rear brace, side rails and transport wheels

1. On the underside of the **Rear Brace [4]** there is a **pre-installed bolt; remove
   it.** Fit the **Bungee Hook Mount [5]** onto the rear brace. **The orientation of both
   matters** - the arrow in the illustration, which must point toward the front of the
   rower, is what holds the end of the Bungee Shock Cord [19]. Rotate the rear brace to
   the correct orientation before assembling it onto the side rails.
2. Connect the **Left/Right Side Rails [2]** using **4 M6 x 15mm Bolts [10]** and the
   Rear Brace [4]. **DO NOT TIGHTEN.** The **"Bump Stops" must face each other.**
3. Mount each **Transport Wheel Assembly [6]** onto the side rails using the pre-installed
   **M8 x 60mm bolt**, **M8 washers** and **nylon nut** - **one washer outside and one
   inside** the rail. The wheels **must be mounted on the outside of the side rails**, and
   **do not over-tighten the axle** or the wheel will not turn.

## Step two - the seat

**Spread the Left/Right Side Rails [2] slightly and drop the Rower Seat [3] into the
track**, fitting the lower seat guides into the track. It sits loosely until the mainframe
goes on. **The seat indentation must face rearward.**

*The seat can be raised on optional spacers, and that is the moment to decide - see
[the spacer card](crw900-2021-assembly-seat-height-spacers.md).*

## Step three - mainframe

Install the **Mainframe [1]** onto the side rail assembly with **8 M8 x 45mm Bolts [11]**,
**16 M8 Washers [7]**, **8 M8 Standard Nuts [8]** and **8 M8 Nylon Nuts [9]**.

**Secure the frontmost lower bracket first, then the footboard brackets, but DO NOT
TIGHTEN.** The footboard bracket bolts are hard to reach; align and loosely thread them
here and tighten them in step four, with the rower standing vertically.

## Step four - bungee cord, and the tightening the last three steps deferred

1. With the Bungee Shock Cord [19] attached to the Rear Brace [4], **tighten the footboard
   and mainframe-to-side-rail bolts** - easier to reach from the vertical position.
2. **Lower the rower back to horizontal and test the bungee recoil.**
3. Check the seat rollers and runners for dirt or debris before sitting on the seat.
4. The wooden frame can be polished with a high-grade furniture polish.
5. **Install the supplied AA batteries [15] and check the computer works.**

> **CAUTION: do not cut the bungee tie wrap before the bungee shock cord is attached to
> the rear of the rower.**

*Item 5 says "2 AA batteries" and the carton panel counts four. See
[the box contents card](crw900-2021-assembly-box-contents.md). Item 5 also sends the
reader to page 45 for the console; the console chapter starts on page 40.*

## Step five - bumpers and elevated legs

1. **Stand the rower vertically.**
2. Remove the **pre-installed M6 x 20mm bolts** and the front and rear bumpers from the
   left and right rails.
3. Mount the **Front and Rear Support Brackets [22]** and secure each with **4 M6 x 20mm
   Bolts [24]**.
4. Mount the **four Elevated Legs [23]** with **12 M8 x 45mm Bolts [11]**, **24 M8 Washers
   [7]**, **12 M8 Nuts [8]** and **12 M8 Nylon Nuts [9]**. **The arched sides of the legs
   must face the middle of the rower.**
5. **Reinstall the bumpers** to the bottom of the elevated legs with the M6 x 20mm bolts
   taken off in item 2.

## The build does not end at step five

Two more jobs follow it in the assembly chapter: **the heart rate receiver is stuck on and
cable-tied** - see
[that card](crw900-2021-assembly-heart-rate-receiver-installation.md) - and the tank has to
be filled before the machine will work, which the manual prints thirteen pages later under
its own heading rather than as a step.

**No step fits a leveller** and none is supplied; see
[that card](crw900-2021-assembly-choose-a-flat-surface.md).
