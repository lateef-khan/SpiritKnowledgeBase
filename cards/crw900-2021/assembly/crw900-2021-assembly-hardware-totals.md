**The manual prints no HARDWARE FOR STEP table.** Each step names its own quantities in
prose, and the carton panel counts the bag. They close exactly.

| Item | In the box | Step 3 | Step 5 | Step 1 | Total used |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 - M8 x 45mm bolt | 20 | 8 | 12 | - | **20** |
| 7 - M8 washer | 40 | 16 | 24 | - | **40** |
| 8 - M8 standard nut | 20 | 8 | 12 | - | **20** |
| 9 - M8 nyloc nut | 20 | 8 | 12 | - | **20** |
| 10 - M6 x 15mm bolt | 4 | - | - | 4 | **4** |
| 24 - M6 x 20mm bolt | 8 | - | 8 | - | **8** |

**Nothing is left over and nothing is short.** Two M8 washers go on every bolt, one under
the head and one under the nut, and every M8 joint takes a standard nut and a nyloc nut
both. If you have hardware in hand at the end, a joint has been missed.

## The four M8 x 160 bolts are the exception, and they are optional

Item **33**, four **M8 x 160mm bolts**, belongs to the optional raised seat and is used by
no step. If they are still in the bag when the build is finished, that is correct. See
[the spacer card](crw900-2021-assembly-seat-height-spacers.md).

## Fasteners that arrive on the machine and are never counted

Five sets are described as pre-installed and appear in no panel:

- a bolt under the **rear brace**, removed in step one to fit the bungee hook mount;
- an **M8 x 60mm bolt, two M8 washers and a nylon nut** at each **transport wheel**;
- **M6 x 20mm bolts** holding the four **rail bumpers**, taken off and refitted in step
  five;
- **four M8 x 130mm screws** through the **seat**, replaced only if the spacers are used.

The **M6 x 20mm bolts of item 24 are a separate eight**, for the support brackets. Step
five uses both sets on the same page and calls them by the same size.

## No torque figure anywhere

No step of this build gives one, and two steps deliberately leave joints loose - **DO NOT
TIGHTEN** in step one and again in step three - until the rower is stood vertically in
step four.
