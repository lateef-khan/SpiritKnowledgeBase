**One procedure, in two halves, over two printed pages. The steps are only meaningful together.**

## Detaching

1. **Pull the belt beyond the range of the normal rowing stroke** until it detaches from the belt
   bungee pulley. *You will hear the Velcro separating just before the belt detaches.*
2. **Unhook the Bungee Shock Cord [19] from the Rear Brace [4].** Push the inner clip out of the
   bungee hook, then pull the bungee through the inner clip until it is free. The cord can then be
   threaded completely out of the **Main Frame [1]** and up to the belt bungee pulley, where it is
   re-attached once the rower belt is back in position.

## Reattaching

1. Thread the belt **around the rower belt pulley with the Velcro side facing upward**.
2. Thread the belt **around the idle wheel**, then attach it to the **belt/bungee pulley** — there
   is an obvious **"lip"** at the attachment point.
3. **Wind the rower belt onto the belt/bungee pulley until the rower handle is at its furthest
   forward position.**
4. **Re-thread the Bungee Shock Cord [19]**, on the opposite side of the idle wheel, back through
   the bungee pulleys and **tie off at the attachment point**.

## Then set the tension

Step 5 of the printed sequence is the recoil-tension adjustment, and it is what the troubleshooting
table sends a customer here for: `crw900-2021-maintenance-bungee-recoil-tension-adjustment`. Tie the
slip knot described there before you reassemble the bungee hook.

## What the manual does not give

**No belt part number, no tension figure and no wear limit.** The maintenance table only says to
check the rowing belt **every hundred hours for correct tension and for signs of wear**, and that a
belt change is a job for your local service representative
(`spirit-water-rower-maintenance-weekly-rails-and-hundred-hour-belt-check`). Nothing in the book
describes replacing the belt, only re-fitting the one you have.

**This is not the CRW800H2O procedure.** That machine's belt and belt wheel are covered by its
service manual, which is a different mechanism with a volute spring rather than a bungee cord:
`crw800h2o-belt-wheel-replacement`.
