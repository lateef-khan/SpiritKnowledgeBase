**This is the one lubrication instruction in any Spirit rower book, and it is in the CRW900 service manual, not the
owner's manual.** The owner's manual names no lubricant anywhere
(`spirit-rower-maintenance-no-lubricant-named-anywhere`); the service manual's whole maintenance chapter is this
one page.

As printed, 2.1 Lubrication Grease:

> Any kind of lubrication grease, such as super grease would do the job.
> Open the cap, insert the nozzle into the frame, and spray on the shaft. And that top of the pulley below. Pull the
> handle strap slowly while putting the grease on. A little bit at a point.

What the page shows, read from the render because the photographs carry it:

- **The grease is an aerosol.** The page pictures two spray cans labelled *192 Super Grease* with extension nozzles;
  "any kind" of spray grease will do, and no brand is required.
- **The cap is the round frame plug** beside the strap pulley housing, on top of the main frame where the handle
  strap enters. The photographs show it removed and the nozzle going into the hole.
- **Spray the shaft the strap pulley turns on, and the top of the pulley below the hole.** The pulley is the spool
  the handle strap winds onto; the third photograph shows the strap coiled on it with the bungee cord under it.
- **Pull the handle strap out slowly while spraying**, so the grease reaches the whole turn of the pulley, and use a
  little at a time - "a little bit at a point".

**No interval is printed.** The manual says how, not how often. A strap that recoils badly is a bungee tension job,
not a lubrication job (`crw900-2021-maintenance-bungee-recoil-tension-adjustment`); a belt that has to come off the
pulley is `crw900-2021-maintenance-detaching-and-reattaching-the-rower-belt`.

**Do not put it on the seat rollers or the track.** Those are wiped weekly with a soft dry cloth and never
lubricated (`crw900-2021-maintenance-seat-roller-and-track-cleaning`).

The photographs are of a machine badged *Apollo* rather than Spirit; the manual presents them as this machine.
