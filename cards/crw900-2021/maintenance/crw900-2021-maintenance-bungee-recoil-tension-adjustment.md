> **Recoil tension will decrease over time as the Bungee Shock Cord [19] stretches.** To increase
> recoil tension, simply push the **inner clip out of the bungee hook from behind**, pull the
> required amount of bungee through the inner clip, and replace the inner clip into the bungee hook.

**This is expected wear, not a fault.** The cord stretches with use; taking up the slack at the
inner clip is the adjustment, and there is no tension figure, no measurement and no tool.

> **Hint:** before reattaching the inner clip and bungee hook, **tie a slip knot under slight tension
> at the lower bungee pulley**. This keeps the bungee under tension while you reassemble the bungee
> hook.

Without the slip knot the cord pulls back through your hands the moment the clip is released, and
the adjustment has to be started again.

## When to do it

The maintenance table asks you to **check the bungee cord every hundred hours for correct tension
and for signs of wear** (`spirit-water-rower-maintenance-weekly-rails-and-hundred-hour-belt-check`).
It adds that the cord should last for many years, and that a replacement is a job for your local
service representative.

## The symptom this answers

A belt that walks off the belt or bungee pulley, and a handle that comes back slowly, are the same
fault seen from two ends — the cord is not pulling hard enough. The troubleshooting table gives both
causes and sends the reader here:
`crw900-2021-errors-belt-slips-off-the-bungee-pulley`.

**Only the CRW900 has a bungee cord in this range.** The CRW800H2O maintenance table has no bungee
row, its troubleshooting table has no such fault, and neither air rower mentions one.
