**The interval depends on how much light the tank gets.**

> Water treatment schedules for your water rower will vary according to the fluid tank's exposure to
> sunlight, but expect **8-12 months near a bright, sunlit window** and **2 years or more for a
> darker location**. At the point of finding the water slightly cloudy, **add a Water Treatment
> Tablet**.

So the trigger is the water, not the calendar: **slightly cloudy means add a tablet**, and the
figures above are what to expect rather than a fixed schedule. Moving the rower out of direct
sunlight lengthens the interval, which is also the first remedy the troubleshooting table gives
(`spirit-water-rower-errors-tank-water-turns-cloudy`).

## Only the supplied tablets

> Do not use any water treatment other than the tablets supplied with this unit. For replacement
> tablets, contact your local SPIRIT distributor.

The filling page says the same thing in capitals and adds that the unit ships with **4 tablets**,
enough for several years — `crw900-2021-maintenance-tank-filling-and-initial-water-treatment`.
**Neither page says the warranty is void if another tablet is used**; that sentence belongs to the
CRW800H2O book (`crw800h2o-warranty-void-chlorine-and-overfilling`) and must not be quoted for this
machine.

**Use a drop cloth under the tank** whenever the tank plug is opened for treatment.

## The maintenance table's figure means something else

The GENERAL MAINTENANCE table on printed p. 51 gives **Tank and Water Treatment: 12m to 2 years**
(`spirit-water-rower-maintenance-weekly-rails-and-hundred-hour-belt-check`). That is the interval for
changing the water; the 8-to-12-month figure here is the interval for adding a **tablet** to water
that is staying in the tank. Treating on time is what removes the need to change it at all.

## The CRW800H2O interval is a flat six months

Its book says **every 6 months or as needed**, with no sunlight condition, and tells you to replace
the water if a tablet does not clear it —
`crw800h2o-maintenance-purification-tablet-every-six-months`. The two machines take different
tablets; do not read one interval onto the other.
