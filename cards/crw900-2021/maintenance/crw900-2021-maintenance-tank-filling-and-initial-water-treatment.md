1. **Remove the rubber fill plug** from the top of the tank.
2. Place a **large bucket of water** next to the rower. Position the **Siphon [17]** with the
   **rigid hose in the bucket and the flexible hose in the tank**.
   *Make sure the small breather valve on the siphon is closed before filling.*
3. **Set the Adjustment Knob [32] to "10".** Squeeze the siphon to begin filling. **Do not overfill.**
4. When full, **open the valve on the top of the siphon** to let excess water escape.
5. Follow the water treatment below, then **replace the tank plug**.

## Initial water treatment

**Add 1 x Water Treatment Tablet [16] per full tank.**

> DO NOT, UNDER ANY CIRCUMSTANCE, USE A WATER TREATMENT TABLET OTHER THAN THOSE SUPPLIED WITH YOUR
> UNIT.

The unit ships with **4 Water Treatment Tablets [16]**, which the manual says is enough for several
years. Additional chlorine tabs come from your regional dealer or distributor.

## The three figures printed beside the steps

- The **resistance adjuster must be set to "10"** to allow for accurate filling capacity.
- **Do not overfill beyond the maximum indicated level of 20 litres.** Refer to the **tank level
  decal** on the tank.
- Where **tap water quality is known to be poor, use distilled water**.

The knob is marked **AUTO LEVEL ADJUST, 1 to 10**. Setting **1** keeps a portion of the water in
reserve for light resistance; **10** lets the maximum amount of water reach the flywheel — which is
why filling is done at 10 and draining at 1.

## The same page numbers the tablets two ways

This page calls the tablets **[16]**, which is also what the carton parts list calls them
(*16 — 1 — WATER TREATMENT TABLETS*). The next printed page calls them **[32]**, which is the number
this page gives the **Adjustment Knob**. Both readings were taken from 300 dpi renders, so this is
the document contradicting itself and not an extraction fault. **Order tablets by name, not by
bracket number.**

## Nothing here transfers to the CRW800H2O

The other Spirit water rower is filled through a **funnel** to a gauge marked **1 to 6**, takes a
**water-purification tablet** rather than these, and prints no litre figure and no adjuster setting:
`crw800h2o-maintenance-filling-and-emptying-the-tank`. This book prints **no** warranty-voiding
sentence about the fill level or about chlorine; the CRW800H2O book does.
