1. **Set the Adjuster Knob [32] to "1".**
2. **Row at least ten strokes** to fill the storage reservoir as completely as possible. **Remove
   the tank plug.**
3. Insert the **rigid end of the siphon into the tank** and the **flexible hose into a large
   bucket**.
4. **Drain the tank using the siphon**, then refill following the tank filling instructions
   (`crw900-2021-maintenance-tank-filling-and-initial-water-treatment`, printed p. 33).
5. Follow the **Resistance Level Display guide** on the console set-up page (printed p. 53) for
   **level calibration**.

**The valve on top of the siphon must be closed to allow proper drainage** — the opposite of the
filling step, where the valve is opened at the end to let excess water out.

## About 40% of the water stays in

> *Approximately 40% of tank water will remain. It is not possible to completely drain the tank
> without disassembly.*

This is normal and is not a fault. A customer who has drained the tank and still hears or sees water
has drained it correctly. The setting to **"1"** and the ten strokes are what move as much water as
possible into the storage reservoir first, so skipping them leaves more behind, not less.

## Draining is usually unnecessary

> *Water treatment will preclude the need to change tank water if the treatment schedule is
> maintained. Additional Water Treatment Tablets are required only when discoloration appears in the
> water.*

So the first answer to cloudy water is a tablet, not a water change —
`crw900-2021-maintenance-long-term-water-treatment-interval`. The troubleshooting table adds the
other two causes, sunlight and poor tap water
(`spirit-water-rower-errors-tank-water-turns-cloudy`).

**Put a drop cloth under the tank.** The manual recommends it strongly whenever the tank plug is
opened for water treatment.

## Recalibrate the level afterwards

Step 5 is easy to miss and the reason a refilled machine can read the wrong resistance level: the
console's Resistance Level Display has to be set again from the console set-up page after the tank
is refilled.

## The CRW800H2O drains differently

It is pumped out through the fill plug with its **Siphon Pump (No. 53)** and has no adjuster knob,
no ten strokes and no stated residue: `crw800h2o-maintenance-filling-and-emptying-the-tank`. Do not
tell a CRW800H2O owner that 40% will remain — their book does not say so.
