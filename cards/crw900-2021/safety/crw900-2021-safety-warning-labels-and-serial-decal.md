> The following pages show examples of Spirit Fitness warning labels and communication stickers
> placed on the equipment as part of the manufacturing process. It is critical that owners maintain
> the integrity and placement of these stickers. If you find any stickers missing or damaged contact
> your local dealer or distributor for a replacement. NOTE: STICKERS AND LABELS ARE NOT SHOWN TO
> SCALE.

**Three labels are reproduced. The page shows what each says and does not say where any of them is
fixed.**

| Label | What it reads |
|---|---|
| EXERCISE WARNING STICKER | the Spirit wordmark above `KEEP CLEAR OF MOVING PARTS` |
| CALIFORNIA PROP 65 WARNING DECAL (For US model only) | `WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm - www.P65Warnings.ca.gov` |
| SERIAL NUMBER DECAL | the block below |

**The serial number decal, read from a 300 dpi render of printed page 15:**

```
SPIRIT
MODEL NUMBER : CRW900
CLASS : SC
MAX USER WEIGHT : 150 kg / 330lbs
SERIAL NUMBER :   CRW90018120001
                                    TÜV  GS   CE  [book-and-hand symbol]
                                    MADE IN TAIWAN
```

- **`CLASS : SC`** is the only class marking any Spirit rower manual prints.
- **`CRW90018120001` is the manual's specimen serial**, not a number to quote back to a customer.
- **The TÜV, GS and CE marks are the only certification information in the whole book.** The manual
  has no compliance page, no FCC notice and no standards list
  (`crw800-2024-safety-no-compliance-page`); these three marks on a picture are all it gives.
- **`MADE IN TAIWAN`** - the CRW800H2O's page instead shows a separate `MADE IN CHINA STICKER`
  (`crw800h2o-safety-warning-labels-and-sticker-locations`). The two water rowers are built in
  different countries.

**The decal's weight figure contradicts the manual's own safeguards page**, which says 450 lbs
(204 kilograms) - a 120 lb difference the book never reconciles
(`crw900-2021-safety-user-weight-limit-contradiction`).

**None of the text above is in the PDF's text layer.** `pdftotext` returns the three captions and
nothing from inside the decals, so this page reads as almost empty - 84 native words against 122
from a render. Read the picture, not the extraction.

**The page does not say where the stickers are.** For the serial number the manual says elsewhere
that it is `located on the center floor tube behind the crank`
(`spirit-water-rower-safety-serial-number-on-the-centre-floor-tube`); it never gives a position for
the exercise warning sticker or the Prop 65 decal.
