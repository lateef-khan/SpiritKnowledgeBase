*"The Target Heart Rate program allows the maximum beats per minute (BPM) target to be customized to
maximize cardiovascular training. The Heart Rate program calculates the maximum heart rate value
based on the user's age to optimize the training target **between 65% and 85%** of the calculated or
theoretical maximum heart rate."*

1. **Launch** - press the **Target Heart Rate** button.
2. **Setup** - after the console has been reset, **the user enters their age.** Adjust with the up
   and down arrows, then press **ENTER** to confirm.
3. **Start** - the display shows your heart rate and your progress toward the goal.
   - **Below 65% of the calculated maximum**, the **65% display flashes** - the minimum goal has not
     been reached.
   - **Above 85% of the calculated maximum**, the **85% display flashes** - you are over the maximum
     goal.
   - In range, the target-range icons light.
4. **End** - *"The target program will end when the user stops pedaling."* Pressing **RESET** at any
   time also ends it, and the workout summary follows.

**You enter an age, not a heart rate.** The console works the target out; **the formula it uses is
never printed anywhere in the manual** - see
`spirit-water-rower-programs-no-maximum-heart-rate-formula-or-training-zone-page`.

**In Stop Mode**, if a heart rate monitor was used, the console shows the heart rates **at 85%, at
65% and the maximum, for 5 seconds each, alternately.** With no monitor, no values are displayed.

**A heart rate receiver and a chest strap (not included) are required.** The manual warns that
*"maintaining a consistent signal on a rower can be a challenge due to the varying distances
experienced during the rowing stroke between the receiver/transmitter"*, and that the transmitter
frequency should be **5.3 KHz**.

**The CRW800H2O works the other way round.** It asks for a target in beats, defaulting to **100**, and
holds you within **10%** of that - no age, no percentage, no 65/85 banners. See
`crw800h2o-programs-target-heart-rate-set-in-beats-defaulting-to-100`.

*"The target program will end when the user stops pedaling" is a bike sentence in a rower book;
recorded as printed.*
