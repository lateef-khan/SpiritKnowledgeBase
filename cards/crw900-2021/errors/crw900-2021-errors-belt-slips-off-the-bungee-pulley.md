The issue is printed as `Rower belt slipping off belt/ bungee pulley`.

| Possible Cause | Instructions |
|---|---|
| Bungee not under enough tension | Tighten bungee cord following the instructions in Reattaching the Rower Belt [pg 36] section of this manual. |
| Recoil not strong enough | |

**Both causes have the same answer: tighten the bungee.** A handle that comes back slowly, or a belt
that walks off its pulley, is the same fault seen from two ends - the cord is not pulling hard
enough to keep the belt seated.

The cross-reference is good: printed **page 36** of this manual really is `REATTACHING THE ROWER
BELT`, and it begins by threading the belt around the rower belt pulley with the Velcro side facing
upward.

**The CRW800H2O prints no row for this.** Its troubleshooting table has four rows and this is not
one of them, so do not tell a CRW800H2O owner their manual covers it. The rest of that table is on
`spirit-water-rower-errors-tank-water-turns-cloudy` and
`spirit-water-rower-errors-inconsistent-split-time-and-stroke-rate`.
