**The owner's manual gives no gap figure; the service manual gives one.** The owner's-manual rows for a console that does not register and for inconsistent readings both say to check the sensor gap with the console's back cover as a gauge and print no number (`crw800h2o-console-shows-no-data`, `spirit-water-rower-errors-inconsistent-split-time-and-stroke-rate`). This book prints the checks in order, with the figure:

1. Check the gap between sensor and magnetic ring should be **2-3mm**.
2. Visually check **all 6 magnets** in the magnetic ring are present and flush (no need to remove the ring).
3. Check sensor head - the internal sensor should be **no more than a few millimeters inside the hole**. The photograph is captioned *Check internal sensor has not receded into the housing*.
4. If the above checks fail to resolve the issue continue to the next section - the sensor replacement guide, which is pictures: remove the frame plug, pull the speed sensor out of the frame, remove its washer, fit the washer to the new sensor, connect the cables, install the sensor as shown, replace the frame plug.

**Three ways the same sensor stops counting.** Too far from the ring (the gap), a ring with a magnet missing or proud (six is the full set - count them), or a sensor whose element has crept back inside its own housing so the gap is right at the face and wrong at the element. The third is the one a gap gauge cannot see.

**The figure is for this machine.** The CRW800H2O service manual, which shares the owner's-manual rows, prints no speed-sensor section and no gap; do not quote 2-3 mm at a CRW800H2O owner as though their book said it.

Resistance trouble is a different page of the same book, the VR setting routine (`crw900-2021-errors-vr-setting-for-trouble-with-resistance`).
