**It only works in Stop Mode, and the manual says so twice.** The keypad call-out reads *"Use
the Bluetooth button to upload the workout summary to the App. Work only in Stop Mode."* The
Bluetooth section repeats it: *"The Bluetooth function will only connect when the console is in the
Stop Mode."*

**The procedure, in the manual's own four steps:**

1. Get the console into **Stop Mode** (press RESET, or leave it without input)
   (`crw900-2021-console-sleep-ready-pause-and-stop-modes`).
2. **Press the BLUETOOTH button.** The **Bluetooth symbol flashes on the display** - it sits at the
   top-left of the interval panel.
3. **Connect to the console through the Bluetooth function on your device.**
4. Once a device has been paired, **the workout summary uploads to the SPIRIT Fit App**, which
   *"requires download via app store"*.

**Only the summary goes across - three figures.** Total Time, Total Distance and Total Watts
(`spirit-water-rower-console-workout-summary-three-figures`). **No live streaming, no stroke count,
no TIME/500m, no heart rate.** A customer expecting the app to mirror the console during a row will
not get it.

**Trying to pair mid-workout is the commonest reason it fails**, and the manual gives no error
message for it. If a phone cannot see the rower, check the console is showing **STOP** first.

**The manual names no protocol, no pairing code, no app store link and no supported phone version**,
and it does not say what happens if the upload fails or whether the summary is held for a later
attempt. **It gives the app one name and no more: the SPIRIT Fit App.**

**The CRW800H2O has no Bluetooth at all** - that key position is its LEVEL key
(`crw800h2o-console-screen-overview`) - and **neither air rower has any**
(`crw800-2024-console-screen-overview`). **Of the five Spirit rowers in the corpus, this is the only
one that can talk to a phone.**
