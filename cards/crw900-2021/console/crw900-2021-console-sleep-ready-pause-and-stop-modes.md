**Four modes, and this console is the only water rower of the two that has a Pause.**

| Mode | How it is entered | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **SLEEP** | **60 seconds** of inactivity | The display switches off. **Rotating the pedals** or pressing any button launches Ready Mode |
| **READY** | Waking from Sleep | Tells the user the console is powered on and ready for any mode. **60 seconds** of inactivity returns it to Sleep |
| **PAUSE** | Automatically **if there is no input** | Keeps TIME, DISTANCE, CALORIES, STROKE and TOTAL STROKES on screen. The 7-segment display shows LEVEL, and TIME/500m and its AVERAGE alternate. **After the 30-second period the buzzer buzzes for 2 seconds and it goes to Stop Mode.** Pressing RESET goes to the STOP screen immediately |
| **STOP** | RESET from Pause, or **30 seconds** with no console input | The 7-segment display shows **STOP** and every total is held |

**"Rotating the pedals" is a bike phrase on a rower.** The paragraph is inherited from the Spirit
bike and stepper manuals; on this machine it means pulling the handle. **The CRW800H2O manual
corrects it to "pulling the handlebar"** in the same sentence
(`crw800h2o-console-sleep-ready-and-stop-after-four-minutes`).

**What Stop Mode shows:** TIME the total time, DISTANCE the total distance, CALORIES the total burned,
STROKES the strokes for the workout, TOTAL STROKES the accumulative count, and **TIME/500m and
TIME/500m AVERAGE alternating for one minute**. **If a heart rate monitor was used it shows the heart
rates at 85%, at 65% and the max, five seconds each, alternately**; if none was used, **no values are
displayed**.

**Two 30-second periods that are not the same thing.** The Pause paragraph counts 30 seconds from
losing input before it buzzes into Stop; the Stop paragraph counts 30 seconds of no console input to
enter Stop directly. **The manual runs them together and never says which one is running.** Both are
reproduced as printed.

**The CRW800H2O has no Pause Mode at all and waits four minutes for Stop.** Its console section names
only Sleep, Ready and Stop. **Do not quote 30 seconds for a CRW800H2O**
(`crw800h2o-console-sleep-ready-and-stop-after-four-minutes`).

**The air rowers are a different console again** - the XRW600 simply switches off after **one minute**
(`xrw600-console-power-off-after-one-minute`) and the CRW800 sleeps 300 seconds after its LCD blanks.
