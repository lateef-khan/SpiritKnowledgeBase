**A portrait LCD with a SPIRIT badge above it, five quick mode keys beside it, and a five-key
control keypad below.**

**The drawing names six areas:** INTERVAL DISPLAY PANEL; QUICK MODE BUTTONS (labelled twice, once for
each column); TIME, DISTANCE, CALORIES, WATTS, SPEED, RPM DISPLAY PANEL; HEART RATE DISPLAY PANEL;
CONTROL KEYPAD; and BLUETOOTH BUTTON.

**Five quick mode keys, each with its own icon:**

| Side | Keys, top to bottom |
|---|---|
| **Left** | **TARGET TIME**, **TARGET DISTANCE**, **TARGET STROKE** |
| **Right** | **TARGET CALORIES**, **TARGET HR KEY** |

*"The Quick Mode buttons will go into the associated mode immediately."*

**The control keypad is five keys around one large round ENTER:** **UP** (top left), **BLUETOOTH**
(bottom left), **ENTER** (centre), **DOWN** (top right), **RESET** (bottom right).

- **UP and DOWN** - *"The arrow buttons allow the user to increase or decrease console values."*
- **ENTER** - *"used to confirm a value as making adjustments to a mode."*
- **RESET** - *"clear a value, to pause your workout, end your workout. Hold for 3 seconds for a
  Total Reset of all values (except Total Strokes)"*
  (`crw900-2021-console-total-reset-three-second-hold`).
- **BLUETOOTH** - *"upload the workout summary to the App. Work only in Stop Mode."*
  (`crw900-2021-console-bluetooth-uploads-the-workout-summary-to-the-app`).

**The bottom-left key is the whole difference between this console and the CRW800H2O's.** On the
CRW800H2O that key position is a **LEVEL** key for telling the console how much water is in the tank
(`crw800h2o-console-water-level-button-six-steps`). **The two consoles are otherwise the same
casting, the same screen and the same four other keys.** Do not answer one from the other.

**A Bluetooth symbol also sits at the top-left of the interval display panel**, above the
14-character dot-matrix row. It lights when the Bluetooth key is pressed.

**The heart rate panel on this console carries AGE, 65%, 85% and Max banners**; the CRW800H2O's does
not (`spirit-water-rower-console-heart-rate-panel-three-dashes`).

**One call-out on the drawing names windows this console does not have.** The right-hand label reads
**"TIME, DISTANCE, CALORIES, WATTS, SPEED, RPM DISPLAY PANEL"**, but the panel it points at has
**SPM** and **WATTS** windows and **no SPEED window and no RPM window** anywhere on the screen. **It
is a bike call-out left on a rower drawing** - the CRW800H2O manual prints the same label with the
same error. A caller looking for RPM on this rower is looking for something that is not there; the
stroke-rate reading is **SPM**.
