Nine items, five intervals, and **no annual column**.

## The one row that separates this table from the CE900's

The CE900 2025 prints these nine rows with the same marks **plus a Tracks / Wheels row** - clean
weekly, inspect monthly (`ce900-2025-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`). This is a suspension
elliptical with no tracks and no rail wheels, so the row is absent. That single row is the whole
difference between the two tables; every other cell matches.

**This manual prints no noise or levelling procedure.** It carries no sweat-path sentence, no "90%
of calls" figure, no leveling pad count and no wrench size - the levelers appear only as a daily
inspection in the table above. Do not borrow the CE800's two pads, the CE900's six, or the 14mm
wrench for this machine; the manual states none of them.

Its post-workout routine is the four-step list at
`spirit-2026t-maintenance-post-workout-machine-care`, and its sanitizing bullets are
`ct900-sanitizing-equipment`.
