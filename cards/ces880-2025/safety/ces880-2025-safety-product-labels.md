Three labels. **Two of the three are on the rear stabilizer.**

| Label | Where the manual says it is |
|---|---|
| Serial number sticker | on the **rear stabilizer** |
| Proposition 65 warning | on the **rear stabilizer** |
| Safety warning sticker | on the **front of the elliptical's shroud** |

**Serial number sticker**

> This sticker will be found on the rear stabilizer. Please record the number below the barcode for
> the purpose of registering your Elliptical's warranty.

Record the number **below the barcode**. The label artwork shows the sample number `1234 5678 9876`
and the telephone number **(870) 935-1107**; the sample is an illustration, not your machine's
serial number.

**Proposition 65 warning**

> This sticker will be found on the rear stabilizer. The State of California requires us to inform
> you that this unit was manufactured using chemicals that could cause harm with improper use.

**That is all this manual says about Proposition 65.** It names **no chemical** and prints **no
www.P65Warnings.ca.gov address**. The earlier Spirit elliptical manuals print the other Proposition
65 text, naming Toluene and Acrylamide and giving the web address, in the manual rather than on a
sticker (`xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`). The two are not interchangeable.

**Safety warning sticker**

> This sticker will be found on the front of your Elliptical's shroud. The same warning can be found
> in this manual. Please read and be aware of the precautions before operating your unit.

The warning the manual means is the boxed WARNING on its front cover - health risk, stop if faint or
dizzy, keep children and pets away, tighten all bolts and nuts before each use
(`ces880-2025-safety-warning-on-the-cover`). The sticker's own wording is not printed separately.

**The CE900-2025 puts all three somewhere else** - the serial number on the front stabilizer and
both the Proposition 65 and safety warning stickers on the console
(`ce900-2025-safety-product-labels`). Never read a location off one machine for the other.

**No other Spirit elliptical manual has a product labels page**
(`spirit-ce-safety-serial-number-location`).
