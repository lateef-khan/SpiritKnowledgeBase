> **WARNING**
>
> Exercise can present a health risk. Consult a physician before beginning any exercise program
> with this equipment.
>
> If you feel faint or dizzy, immediately discontinue use of this equipment. Serious bodily injury
> can occur if this equipment is not assembled and used correctly. Serious bodily injury can also
> occur if all instructions are not followed.
>
> Keep children and pets away from equipment when in use.
>
> Always make sure all bolts and nuts are tightened prior to each use. Follow all safety
> instructions in this manual.

Four rules, and three of them appear nowhere else in this manual:

- **Stop at once if you feel faint or dizzy.** The numbered safety instructions do not say this.
- **Keep children *and pets* away when in use.** This is the manual's only statement about
  children. It carries **no age figure** - the `under the age of 13` rule that every other Spirit
  elliptical manual prints is absent here
  (`ces880-2025-safety-instructions-list`). Note the qualifier: *when in use*.
- **Check every bolt and nut before each use.** A pre-use check, not an annual one.
- Consult a physician before beginning any exercise program
  (`40t-2026-safety-consult-a-physician`, which carries the age-35 form of the same rule from the
  numbered list).

The front cover also carries the weight CAUTION - `Weight on this product should not exceed
181 kgs/ 400 lbs` (`ces880-2025-safety-user-weight-limit-400-lbs`).

**The manual says this same warning is on the machine.** Its product labels page describes a safety
warning sticker on the front of the elliptical's shroud and says the same warning can be found in
the manual (`ces880-2025-safety-product-labels`). It does not print the sticker's own text
separately, so the words above are the only version there is.

**Do not answer from the CT900 or CTSBS900 label cards.** Those treadmills carry a longer warning
sticker naming chest pains, heart-rate accuracy and an under-13 rule, and it is a different text on
a different machine (`ct900-product-labels`).
