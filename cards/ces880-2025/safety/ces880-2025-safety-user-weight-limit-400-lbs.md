**400 lbs.** The manual prints it in two places, in two units that agree.

| Where | Printed as |
|---|---|
| Safety instructions, item 13 | `User Weight Limit: 400 lbs.` |
| Front cover, and beside the parts diagram | `CAUTION: Weight on this product should not exceed 181 kgs/ 400 lbs` |

181 kg is 399 lb, so the two statements are the same allowance.

**This is the only Spirit elliptical that is not 450 lb.** The CE800 2021 and 2023, the CE800ENT,
the CE850 2020 and 2022 and both CE900 manuals all print 450 lb
(`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`). Never quote 450 lb for a CES880, and never quote
400 lb for one of the others.

The cover statement is a CAUTION about the *product*, not about the user's body weight alone, and
the manual draws no distinction between the two. Nothing in the manual says what happens above the
limit.

The boxed note beside the safety list asks the reader to review and adhere to the user weight
restrictions of the machine, and says failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to
the machine (`ces880-2025-safety-instructions-list`).
