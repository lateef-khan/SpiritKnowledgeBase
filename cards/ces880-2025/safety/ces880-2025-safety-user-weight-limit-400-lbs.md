**400 lbs.** The manual prints it in two places, in two units that agree.

| Where | Printed as |
|---|---|
| Safety instructions, item 13 | `User Weight Limit: 400 lbs.` |
| Front cover, and beside the parts diagram | `CAUTION: Weight on this product should not exceed 181 kgs/ 400 lbs` |

181 kg is 399 lb, so the two statements are the same allowance.

**This is the only Spirit elliptical that is not 450 lb.** The CE800 2021 and 2023, the CE800ENT,
the CE850 2020 and 2022 and both CE900 manuals all print 450 lb
(`spirit-ct800-safety-user-weight-limit-450-lbs`). Never quote 450 lb for a CES880, and never quote
400 lb for one of the others.

The cover statement is a CAUTION about the *product*, not about the user's body weight alone, and
the manual draws no distinction between the two. Nothing in the manual says what happens above the
limit.

The boxed note beside the safety list asks the reader to review and adhere to the user weight
restrictions of the machine, and says failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to
the machine (`ces880-2025-safety-instructions-list`).

## The two 2023 residential Spirit ellipticals print the same item 13

**`User Weight Limit: 400 lbs.`** - word for word, as item 13 of the same thirteen-item list, in the
XE395-2023 and XE795-2023 owner's manuals (`ces880-2025-safety-instructions-list`).

**Neither prints a kilogram figure.** The `181 kgs/ 400 lbs` CAUTION is on the CES880's cover and
beside its parts diagram; the two 2023 XE manuals have no such cover statement and print the figure
once only, in pounds.

**The boxed note beside their list asks for one thing more than the CES880's does** - `review and
adhere to the user weight restrictions **and power requirements** of your new machine`.

## Three other residential machines print 400 lb in different words

**The XE295, XG400 and the November 2021 XE795 printing print `Recommended Weight Limit: 400 lb`** -
an unnumbered bullet, `Recommended` rather than `User`, and `lb` rather than `lbs`
(`spirit-xe-2021-safety-recommended-weight-limit-400-lb`). The allowance is the same 400 lb; quote
the machine's own wording.

**The XE195 prints 350 lb** (`xe195-2021-safety-user-weight-limit-350-lb`), and eight residential XE
manuals print no figure at all (`xt-2015-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`).
