**The CES880 opens the interval-time screen at `Sprint Time:20 and Rest Time:30`.** Every other
Spirit commercial elliptical that has HIIT opens it at `Sprint Time:30 and Rest Time:30`. The
adjustable range is the same on all of them - **20 to 60 seconds**.

1. Select HIIT with the Program button, press Enter, then enter your **Age**.
2. Enter your **Weight**.
3. Number of **intervals**: default **10**, range **3 to 15**. One interval equals 1 Sprint and 1
   Rest segment.
4. **Sprint Level** - the resistance level during the Sprint segments.
5. **Rest Level** - the resistance level during the Rest segments.
6. **Interval time**. The display shows `Sprint Time:20 and Rest Time:30`. The Sprint Time blinks
   first; adjust from 20 to 60 seconds with the up and down keys, press Enter, then adjust the Rest
   time.
7. The display shows the total time. Press Start. There is a **3-minute warm-up** before the first
   Sprint, at **resistance level 5**, adjustable manually.
8. The Dot Matrix Message Center in HIIT is a **speed** indication display, not a power or
   resistance display. The blinking LED at the first Sprint segment is the target speed LED and
   indicates **90 pedal rpm**. Maintain at least 90 rpm through the Sprints. The **rest** segments
   are set to **resistance level 5**.
9. At the end of the last Sprint there is a **2-minute cool-down**. Press Stop to bypass it.
