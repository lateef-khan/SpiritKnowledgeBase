**The 1/4-mile track (one lap) is displayed around the dot matrix window. The flashing segment
indicates your progress. Once the 1/4-mile (Metric - 0.4k) is complete, this feature begins again.**

**No lap counter is mentioned.** The CE800-2021, CE800-2023, CE850-2020 and CE850-2022 manuals print
a different paragraph for the same ring: it names a **flashing dot** rather than a segment, and puts
a **lap counter in the centre of the track**. See `ct900-quarter-mile-track-display`. Do not tell a
CES880 owner where to find a lap counter on this ring; this manual does not say there is one.
