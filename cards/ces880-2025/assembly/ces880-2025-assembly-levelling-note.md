> **LEVELING NOTE.** After placing the item in the intended location, check the
> stability of the item. If the item is not level, review the below steps:
>
> 1. Loosen the Leveler to make the Adjustment Plate become less tight.
> 2. Use one hand to adjust the Leveler for levelling.
> 3. Once the item is level, use the other hand to tighten the Adjustment Plate
>    securely against the Stabilizer to lock the Leveler in a stable position.

When first fitted, **tighten each leveller securely until the screw lines are
eliminated** - that is the manual's own test that a leveller is fully home.

## Six levellers, in two part numbers, fitted during the build

| Item | Where | Fitted in |
|---|---|---|
| 28 | 2 under the Main Frame's front stabilizer, transportation wheels on the front | step 1 |
| 28 | 2 under the Base Frame (77) | step 2 |
| 52 | 2 under the Stationary Handrail (49), called **Basic Levelers** | step 7 |

All three steps carry the note **"This step could require two people."**

## The closing instruction

> Check whether the levelers are even on the floor. **If the levelers are not even it
> will produce noise.** If not, review the LEVELING NOTE.

That sentence is printed at the end of step 15, alongside the instruction to make sure
all bolts and nuts are fully tightened before use.

## This is not the levelling instruction on any other Spirit elliptical

Every other Spirit elliptical answers "how do I level it" with one sentence in the
troubleshooting or maintenance chapter: **2 pads under the rear stabiliser and a 14mm
wrench** on the CE800 2021 and 2023, the CE800ENT and the CE850s, **4 pads under the
rear rails** on the CE800 2013, **6 pads under the machine** on the CE900s. This manual
has none of those sentences, names no wrench size, and puts the whole subject in the
assembly chapter with a locking adjustment plate no other machine has. See the linked
cards; do not carry a figure across.
