1. Place all parts from the box in a cleared area and position them on the floor in
   front of you.
2. Remove all packing materials from your area and place them back into the box. **Do
   not dispose of the packing materials until assembly is completed.**
3. Read each step carefully before beginning.

The same three sentences are printed again, as running prose rather than a numbered
list, at the head of step one.

## Nothing here is the Spirit unpacking list

Every other Spirit elliptical and treadmill manual opens with a list about **cutting
the straps and lifting the box over the unit**, and about taking the hardware out one
step at a time. **This manual prints neither instruction.** It never says to cut a
strap, never says to inspect for shipping damage, and never explains what the numbers
in parentheses mean - although its fifteen steps use them on every line.

Do not answer a CES880 question from
[the two-item list](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ct800-assembly-unpacking.md) or
[the four-item list](../../shared/assembly/ct900-pre-assembly.md). They are different
instructions for different machines.

## What this manual says instead, and says twice

The hardware page carries the rule the numbered list leaves out:

> Hardware is packaged in section A, B, C that correspond to the step with which they
> are used. **Not all steps require hardware.** Some small parts may have been
> pre-attached for shipping. **If a part is not in the hardware, check to see if it has
> been pre-assembled.**

That last sentence is the one that matters on this machine: **seven of the fifteen
steps begin by removing bolts or screws that are already fitted**, and there is no bag
for them. See [the hardware sections card](ces880-2025-assembly-hardware-sections.md).

**Also unpack the box in a clear area** and check the illustrated assembly-parts page
against what you have before you start - see
[the parts card](ces880-2025-assembly-parts-included.md).
