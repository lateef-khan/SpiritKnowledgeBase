> Unpack the box in a clear area. Follow the List of Assembly Parts and its part number
> below to check and make sure all assembly parts are present and in good condition. Do
> not dispose of the packing material until the assembly process is completed.

Sixteen illustrated entries, each with its item number in brackets:

| Part | Item |
|---|---|
| Main Frame | 1 |
| Base Frame | 77 |
| Base Cover | 76 |
| Stationary Handrail | 48, 49 |
| Inner Stationary Handrail Cover | 50, 51 |
| Outer Stationary Handrail Cover | 45 |
| Upper Handrail | 53, 54 |
| Upper Handrail Cover, and Screw Cap | 55, 56, and 64 |
| Fixed Handlebar | 78 |
| Console, and Console Bottom Cover | 86, and 88 |
| Middle Pivoting Arm Cover | 59, 60 |
| Front Swing Arm Cover | 61 |
| Bottom Swing Arm Cover | 65 |
| Pedal Arm | 74, 75 |
| Non-Slip Pad, and Pedal | 68, and 69 |
| Leveler | 28, 52 |

**Two item numbers on one line means a left and a right**, or a front and a back. The
levellers are the exception: item **28** is the four levellers under the main frame and
base frame, item **52** the two basic levellers under the stationary handrails - six in
all, fitted in steps 1, 2 and 7.

## A second, shorter panel names five more parts

The hardware page on p. 5 labels five parts the list above does not: **Console**,
**Upper Handlebar**, **Pulse Sensor**, **Accessory Tray**, **Transportation Wheel**,
**Stationary Handrail**, **Pedal** and **Leveler**. Those are callouts on the hardware
drawing rather than a second carton list.

## No quantities, and no parts list to reconcile them with

**This panel prints no counts.** Every other Spirit elliptical carton list is written
as "1 Main Frame, 2 Swing Arms"; this one is written as pictures with item numbers and
never says how many of anything. And **the manual has no parts list**, so the item
numbers above cannot be checked against a table. They are all you get.

**There is no water bottle holder in this carton.** The nearest thing is the
**Accessory Tray**, which arrives fitted; no step installs it and no item number is
printed for it. See
[the bottle holder card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ce-assembly-water-bottle-holder.md).
