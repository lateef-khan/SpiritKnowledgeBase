Fifteen steps. **This build is nothing like any other Spirit elliptical** - it has no
console mast, it fits six levellers as part of the build, and much of its hardware
arrives already in the holes.

**Step 1 - Leveler (28) and Main Frame.** Attach 2 Levelers (28) under the Main
Frame's Front Stabilizer, transportation wheels on the front. **May require two
people.** Tighten the levellers securely until the screw lines are eliminated. If it
is not level, follow the levelling note - see
[the levelling card](ces880-2025-assembly-levelling-note.md).

**Step 2 - Leveler (28) and Base Frame (77).** Attach 2 Levelers (28) under the Base
Frame (77), levelled by the same process as step 1.

**Step 3 - Base Frame (77).** Place any stationary object under the Main Frame (1),
attach the Base Frame (77) to the Main Frame (1) and fully tighten **4 Socket Head
Bolts (M10xp1.5x65mm)(130)**. **May require two people.** *Hardware section A.*

**Step 4 - Middle Pivoting Arm Cover (59, 60).** Assemble the front and back pivoting
arm covers (59, 60) to the Outer Crank Linkage (57) and fully tighten with **4 Screws,
Phillips Pan Head (M5xp0.8x15mm)(122)**. Repeat on the left. *Hardware section A.*

**Step 5 - Inner Stationary Handrail Cover (50, 51) and Stationary Handrail (48, 49).**
There is an "L" and "R" decal on the inner handrail covers. Slide the right inner cover
(51) onto the right Stationary Handrail (49); repeat on the left. **4 Socket Bolts
(M10xp1.5x60mm)(129)** and **4 Thin Nylon Nuts (M10xp1.5)(116)** are pre-attached on
the Swing Linkage Support Bracket (44) - remove them. **4 Nylon Nuts (M10xp1.5)(115)**
and **2 Socket Bolts (M10xp1.5x20mm)(128)** are pre-attached on the Base Frame (77) -
remove those too. Slide the right Stationary Handrail (49) into the Main Frame (1),
**bottom side first**, matching the handrail's bottom screw hole to the main frame
hole, then gently push the upper side in. Slightly attach 2 thin Nylon Nuts (116) and
2 Socket Bolts (129), then 2 Nylon Nuts (115) and 1 Socket Bolt (128). **Do not
tighten at this moment.** Repeat on the left.

**Step 6 - Stationary Handrail tighten process.** Fully tighten, **step a location
first**, then step b. Repeat on the left.

**Step 7 - Basic Leveler (52).** Attach 2 Basic Levelers (52) under the Stationary
Handrail (49). **May require two people.** Tighten until the screw lines are
eliminated.

**Step 8 - Inner and Outer Handrail Cover (51, 45).** Slide the right inner handrail
cover (51) up to the right Stationary Handrail (49) and fully tighten **2 Screws,
Philips Pan Head (M6xp1.0x15mm)(124)**. Then attach the Outer Handrail Cover (45) and
fully tighten with **3 Screws, Philips Truss Head (M4x20mm)(118)**. Repeat on the left.
*Hardware section B.*

**Step 9 - Front Swing Arm Cover (61).** **4 Screws (122)** are pre-attached on the
Front Swing Arm (62, 63) - remove them. Attach the Front Swing Arm Cover (61) to the
Main Frame (1) and fully tighten with **2 Screws (122)**. Repeat on the left.

**Step 10 - Base Cover, Pedal Arm (74, 75), Swing Arm Cover (65).** **2 Screws,
Philips Pan Head (M5xp0.8x30mm)(123)** are pre-attached on the Base Frame (77) - remove
them, attach the Base Cover (76) onto the Base Frame (77) and fully tighten those 2
screws (123), then press **2 Screw Caps (64)** onto the cover. Attach the Right Pedal
Arm (75) to the Swing Arm (63, 67) and fully tighten **2 Socket Head Bolts
(M10xp1.5x90mm)(132)** and **2 Nylon Nuts (M10xp1.5)(115)**; repeat on the left. Then
**16 Screws (122)** pre-attached on the Front and Back Swing Arm (63, 67) come out;
attach 4 Bottom Swing Arm Covers (65) to the Swing Arm (63, 67) and fully tighten with
**8 Screws (122)**, then press **8 Screw Caps (64)** onto the covers. Repeat on the
left. *Hardware section B.*

**Step 11 - Pedal (69) and Non-Slip Pad (68).** **8 Socket Head Bolts
(M8xp1.25x15mm)(127)** and **8 Washers (8x19x2.0t)(102)** are pre-attached on the Pedal
Arm (74, 75) - remove them. Attach the right Pedal (69) to the right Pedal Arm (75) and
fully tighten with **4 Bolts (127)** and **4 Washers (102)**. Repeat on the left.

**Step 12 - Fixed Handlebar (78).** Gently insert the Connection Wire (89) to the left
side into the Fixed Handlebar (78). Attach the Fixed Handlebar (78) to the Main Frame
(1) and slightly attach all **4 Socket Head Bolts (M8xp1.25x15mm)(127)**, **4 Lock
Washers (M8)(99)** and **4 Washers (8x16x2.0t)(101)**, then fully tighten them.
*Hardware section C.*

**Step 13 - Console (86).** Connect the console wire to the Connection Wire (89), and
the console wire to the Pulse Sensor Wire (90). **Be careful not to pinch the wires.**
Attach the Console (86) to the Fixed Handlebar (78) and fully tighten with **4 Screws,
Phillips Pan Head (M5xp0.8x20mm)(141)**.

**Step 14 - Console Bottom Cover (88).** Attach the Console Bottom Cover (88) to the
Console (86) and fully tighten with **2 Screws, Philips Pan Head (M5xp0.8x15mm)(122)**.
*Hardware section C.*

**Step 15 - Upper Handrail (53, 54) and Upper Handrail Cover (55, 56).** **4 Screws
(122)** pre-attached on the Back Swing Arm (67) come out. There is an "R" and "L" decal
on the Upper Handrail (53, 54). Attach the right Upper Handrail (54) to the right Back
Swing Arm (67) and fully tighten with **6 Bolts, Hex Head Flange (M8xp1.25x15mm)(133)**.
Attach the front and back Upper Handrail Cover (55, 56) and fully tighten with **2
Screws (122)**, then press **8 Screw Caps (64)** onto the covers. Repeat on the left.

## The manual's own closing note

> For the final step, make sure all the bolts and nuts are fully tightened before using
> the item. Check whether the levelers are even on the floor - if the levelers are not
> even it will produce noise. If not, review the LEVELING NOTE.

## Three things in this build that catch people out

1. **Seven steps begin by taking hardware out.** Steps 5, 9, 10, 11 and 15 all start by
   removing bolts or screws that were fitted for shipping, and they go back into the
   same holes. Nothing is missing from the bag; the hardware page warns of this in one
   sentence and no step repeats the warning.
2. **Step 5 leaves everything loose on purpose.** "Please do not tighten at this
   moment" - the stationary handrails are tightened in step 6, and step 6 says to
   tighten one location before the other.
3. **Step 10's heading and its text disagree.** The heading reads "Base Cover **(90)**"
   and every line of the step, and the assembly-parts page, say Base Cover **(76)**.
   There is no item 90 in this build; 76 is the base cover.

## No step numbers the covers in the step-10 removal

Step 10 item h says to "Remove the above **Bolts** (122)" where item g called the same
sixteen fasteners **Screws**. They are the M5xp0.8x15mm Phillips pan head screws.
