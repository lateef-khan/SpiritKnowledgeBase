Four tools, printed as captions under four drawings:

- **Socket wrench (13 & 17mm)**
- **Allen wrench (8mm)**
- **Phillips screwdriver (6mm)**
- **Combination wrench (130mm)**

## Two of the four captions are printed oddly, and are left as printed

- A **Phillips screwdriver** is sized by its tip, not in millimetres; "6mm" is what
  the page prints.
- **130mm** on a combination wrench reads as its overall length rather than a jaw
  size. The manual gives no jaw size for it anywhere, and no step names it.

**No step in this manual calls any tool by name or by item number.** Fifteen steps
say "fully tighten" and never say with what. Match the tool to the head: the build
drives M10, M8, M6, M5 and M4 socket-head bolts, hex-head flange bolts, Phillips pan
head and Phillips truss head screws.

## There is no parts list to check this against

**The CES880 manual has no parts list at all** - it prints two exploded-view drawings,
PRODUCT PARTS DRAWING (A) and (B), on pp. 57-58 and no numbered table anywhere. So
these four tools have no item numbers and cannot be ordered by one. Every other Spirit
elliptical manual except the CE850 2016 and the CE900 2021 carries a parts list.

**This panel is on no other Spirit elliptical.** The CE900 carton is the closest and
shares only an 8mm Allen wrench and a Phillips screwdriver; it has two 17mm wrenches
where this has one socket wrench, and 5mm and 6mm Allen keys this does not.
