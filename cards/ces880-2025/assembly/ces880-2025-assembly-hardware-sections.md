> Hardware is packaged in section A, B, C that correspond to the step with which they
> are used. **Not all steps require hardware.** Some small parts may have been
> pre-attached for shipping. **If a part is not in the hardware, check to see if it has
> been pre-assembled.**

## Section A - steps 2, 3 and 4

- Screw, Phillips Pan Head (M5xp0.8x15mm) - item 122
- Bolt, Socket Head (M10xp1.5x65mm) - item 130

## Section B - steps 8 and 10

- Nylon Nut (M10xp1.5) - item 115
- Screw, Phillips Truss Head (M4x20mm) - item 118
- Screw, Phillips Pan Head (M6xp1.0x15mm) - item 124
- Bolt, Socket Head (M10xp1.5x90mm) - item 132

## Section C - steps 12 and 14

- Lock Washer (M8) - item 99
- Washer (8x16x2.0t) - item 101
- Bolt, Socket Head (M8xp1.25x15mm) - item 127
- Screw, Phillips Pan Head (M5xp0.8x15mm) - item 122

**The panel prints no quantities**, only a picture and a size for each. It is the only
Spirit elliptical hardware panel that does not have a QTY column, and this manual has
**no parts list** to make the counts up from. Count what is in each bag before you
start.

**Section A's step list is printed "Step 2, 3, 44".** There is no step 44; the
manual's own "Use hardware section A" markers sit at steps 3 and 4, and step 2 needs no
hardware. Read it as steps 2, 3 and 4.

## Seven steps take their hardware out of the machine, not out of a bag

Steps 5, 9, 10, 11 and 15 all begin by removing fasteners that were fitted for
shipping, and put the same ones back:

| Step | Pre-attached on | What comes out |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Swing Linkage Support Bracket (44) | 4 Socket Bolts (M10xp1.5x60mm)(129), 4 Thin Nylon Nuts (M10xp1.5)(116) |
| 5 | Base Frame (77) | 4 Nylon Nuts (M10xp1.5)(115), 2 Socket Bolts (M10xp1.5x20mm)(128) |
| 9 | Front Swing Arm (62, 63) | 4 Screws, Phillips Pan Head (M5xp0.8x15mm)(122) |
| 10 | Base Frame (77) | 2 Screws, Phillips Pan Head (M5xp0.8x30mm)(123) |
| 10 | Front and Back Swing Arm (63, 67) | 16 Screws (122) |
| 11 | Pedal Arm (74, 75) | 8 Socket Head Bolts (M8xp1.25x15mm)(127), 8 Washers (8x19x2.0t)(102) |
| 15 | Back Swing Arm (67) | 4 Screws (122) |

**Nothing on that table is in section A, B or C.** If a step asks for a fastener you
cannot find in a bag, it is already in the machine.

## Two items the sections never supply

- **Step 13** mounts the console with **4 Screws, Phillips Pan Head (M5xp0.8x20mm),
  item 141**. That size is on no section list and is not pre-attached anywhere the
  manual says. It is the only M5 x 20mm screw in the build.
- **Step 15** fits each upper handrail with **6 Bolts, Hex Head Flange
  (M8xp1.25x15mm), item 133**. Item 133 is on no section list either. It is a *flange*
  bolt, not the plain M8xp1.25x15mm socket head bolt (127) that section C supplies -
  same thread and length, different head, different item number. **Do not substitute
  one for the other.**

## Two washers that are nearly the same

Item **101** is 8x16x2.0t and comes from section C, for the fixed handlebar. Item
**102** is 8x**19**x2.0t and is pre-attached on the pedal arms. Three millimetres of
outside diameter apart, and they are used in different places.
