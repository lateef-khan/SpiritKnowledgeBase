The condition is printed as `Heavy Resistance on every level`.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Generator is broken | Replace with the Generator |
| Controller is broken | Replace with the Controller |
| Console is broken | Replace with the Console |

**The generator comes first here, where the no-resistance row starts at the resistor instead.**
The two rows share the controller and the console but not the first part, so read the symptom
carefully before ordering: `ces880-2025-errors-no-resistance`.

This is the last row of the manual's table, and it sits at the very bottom of the printed page.
Nothing follows it - the next page is the maintenance menu.
