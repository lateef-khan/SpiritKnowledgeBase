The condition is printed as `Hand pulse lost its functions (No pulse displayed on the monitor)`.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Hands not on hand pulse sensors correctly | Two hands hold the hand pulse sensors tightly |
| Pulse Wire connection | Check pulse wire is connected firmly, correctly and are not damaged |

**Two causes, and neither of them is a board.** The four-cause version printed by the CE800ENT,
CT850, CU900ENT and CE900 2025 manuals ends at *Replace console or Hand pulse board*; this manual
stops at the wire: `spirit-hand-pulse-not-working`.

This two-row table is printed detached from the main error table, to the right of it, with no
heading of its own.

A pulse that reads but jumps around is a different question:
`spirit-ce-errors-erratic-heart-rate-interference`.
