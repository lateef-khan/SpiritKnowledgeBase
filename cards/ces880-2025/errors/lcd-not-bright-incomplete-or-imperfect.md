The condition is printed as `LCD is not bright, incomplete or imperfect`.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| LCD light is broken | Replace with new console |
| Power to console too low | Make sure pedal more than **25 RPM** |

**This machine powers its console from a generator, so a dim display is a pedalling-speed symptom
before it is a hardware fault.** Ask the user to pedal above 25 RPM before ordering anything.

**No other Spirit manual answers this row that way.** The CT850 2016 and CVC800 ask for
`110-120V` at the wall, the CT850 2020 asks for `120V`, and the CE900 2025 elliptical prints no
voltage at all and sends you to the lower controller. Those are mains-powered machines and their
answers do not apply here: `spirit-lcd-dim-or-incomplete`, `ct850-2020-led-dim-or-incomplete`,
`ce900-2025-errors-leds-not-bright-incomplete-or-imperfect`.

A screen showing nothing at all is a different row:
`ces880-2025-errors-lcd-screen-does-not-display-anything`.
