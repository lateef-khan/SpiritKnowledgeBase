The condition is printed as `No Resistance`.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Resistor is broken | Replace with the resistor |
| Controller is broken | Replace with the Controller |
| Console is broken | Replace with the Console |

**The resistor comes first here.** That is the load resistor the generator dumps into, not a
tension motor - this machine has no tension motor.

**The CE800ENT elliptical answers the same symptom with a different list** - control board,
generator brake resistance voltage wire, driver IC. See `ce800ent-no-resistance`; the two machines
do not share a fix.

Resistance that is heavy at every level instead of absent is the row below:
`ces880-2025-errors-heavy-resistance-on-every-level`.
