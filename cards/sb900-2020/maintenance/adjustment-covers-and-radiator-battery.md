1. Use the Phillips head screwdriver (86) to unscrew **four 5 x 10 m/m tapping screws (93)** and **four 3.5 x 12 m/m sheet metal screws (30)**, then take the **outer chain cover L/R (122/123)** off.
2. Open the **radiator cover** and replace the battery.
3. Reverse the steps to refit the outer chain cover L/R (122/123).

The manual calls these parts both "Adjustment Cover L/R" and "Outer Chain Cover L/R" in the same section, with the same part numbers (122/123). It does not say what battery type the radiator takes.
