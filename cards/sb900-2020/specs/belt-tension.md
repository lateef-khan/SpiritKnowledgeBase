**1000 Newton.**

To set it, move the **idler axle (74)** downward by turning the **1/4" x 3"L hex head bolt (69) counterclockwise** until the gauge reads 1000 N.

This manual prints the same figure in both places, section 3.5 step 11 and the belt slippage answer in section 4.1. The SB700 2019 uses 700 to 750 N, and the SB700 2020 manual prints both figures.
