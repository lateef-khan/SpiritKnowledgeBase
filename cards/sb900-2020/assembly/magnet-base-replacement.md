The SB900 changes resistance by moving a magnet base towards the flywheel. **The finished gap between flywheel and magnet base is 2 mm.**

1. Follow section 3.1 to remove the **adjustment covers L/R (122)(123)**.
2. Put a **2 mm rubber pad** or another flexible pad between the **flywheel (31)** and the **magnet base (117)**.
3. Remove the steel cable from the end of the **non-segment knob (16)**.
4. Use two open-ended wrenches to remove the **M6 x P1.0 x 45L hex head bolt (131)** and take the **M6 x P1.0 x 5.0T luck nut (132)** off it.
   **Handle the magnet base (117) gently.** Keep the rubber pad between the magnet base (117), the flywheel (31) and the hex head bolt (131) so you do not damage the flywheel.
5. Use two **13 mm open-ended wrenches** to remove the **5/16" x UNC18 x 6T nyloc nut (128)** and the **8 x 16 x 1T flat washer (48)**, then pull out the **shaft (15)** of the magnet base.
6. Take the magnet base (117) off.
7. To refit, set the gap between the flywheel (31) and the magnet base (117) back to **2 mm** with the M6 x P1.0 x 45L hex head bolt (131) and the M6 x P1.0 x 5.0T luck nut (132).

This 2 mm gap is a different part from the speed sensor magnet, which sits within 8 mm of the sensor.
