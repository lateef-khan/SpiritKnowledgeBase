1. Remove the **chain cover (outer) (60)** and both **adjustment covers (122/123)**, then turn the **non-segment knob (16)** to the lowest level, that is the least resistance.
2. Use an **11 m/m open wrench** to loosen (turn clockwise) the **1/4" x 3"L hex head bolt (69)** on the **idler axle (74)**, then take the **belt (40)** off.
3. Use the combination **M5 Allen wrench and Phillips head screwdriver (86)** to unscrew the **5/16" x 3/4" button head socket bolt (36)**.
4. Use the tool shown in the manual to take the **hub pulley (35)** apart.
5. Use the Phillips head screwdriver (86) to unscrew **six M6 x 10L Phillips head screws (39)** and take the **flywheel (31)** off together with the **axle (33)**.
6. Use the tool shown in the manual to pull out both left and right **bearing housings (32)**.
7. Use C-ring pliers to release the **20 mm C ring (68)**.
8. Use the Phillips head screwdriver (86) to unscrew **six 5/16" x 3/4"L button head socket bolts (38)** to take the **flywheel axle (33)** apart.
9. Reverse the steps to refit.
10. The **flywheel (31)** and the **flywheel axle (33)** must sit perpendicular to the frame, held by **six M6 x P1.0 x 10L flat Phillips head screws (39)**. All six screws must go fully into the main frame so the **bearing housing (32)** is tight enough.
11. Move the **idler axle (74)** downward by turning the **1/4" x 3"L hex head bolt (69) counterclockwise**, and tighten the **belt (40)** until belt tension reads **1000 Newton**.

**Note:** the bearing housing (32) and the **6004 bearing (66)** are bonded with anaerobic glue in production. If the 6004 bearing (66) fails, replace the whole set.
