1. Use a **4 mm Allen wrench** to remove the **M5 x P0.8 x 10L socket head cap screw (89)**.
2. Use the Phillips head screwdriver (86) to remove the **M4 x P0.7 x 8L Phillips head screw (121)**. The **M8 x 46L seat/handlebar adjustment locking lever (20)** can then be released and removed. Pull out the **seat post (5)** and the **seat post bracket (7)**.
3. Use a **14 m/m open wrench** to release the nut holding the **seat (27)**, then take the seat off.
4. Use the combination **M5 Allen wrench (86)** to remove the **M8 x P1.25 x 10L button head socket bolt (52)** and release the **M10 x 12L seat/handlebar adjustment locking lever (19)** to separate the seat post bracket (7) from the seat post (5).
5. Reverse the steps to refit.
6. **When you refit the seat post bracket (7) into the seat post (5), the latch (11 x 8.7L) (22) on the bracket must be parallel to the seat post**, or the bracket will not slide in.
