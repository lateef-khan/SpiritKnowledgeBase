1. Remove the **outer chain cover (60)** and the **belt (40)**.
2. Use the **14/15 m/m wrench (85)** to take the left **pedals (25)** apart.
3. Remove the **crank arm dust cap (44)** on the left **crank arm (42)**. Use a **14 m/m plug wrench** to release the **M10 x P1.25 x 7T nut (80)**, then take the left crank arm (42) off.
4. Use C-ring pliers to remove the **20 mm C ring (68)**, then take the **crank axle (45)**, **drive pulley (41)** and **pulley cover (63)** apart.
5. Use the Phillips head screwdriver (86) to release **three M5 x 10 m/m tapping screws (93)** holding the **inner chain cover (61)**, then take it off.
6. Use C-ring pliers to release another **20 mm C ring (68)**.
7. Remove the **crank arm dust cap (44)** on the right **crank arm (43)**. Use a **14 m/m plug wrench** to release the **M10 x P1.25 x 7T nut (80)**, then take the right crank arm (43) off.
8. Use the wrench (86) to unscrew the **5 x 10 m/m tapping screw (90)** and take the **pulley cover (63)** off.
9. Use the Phillips head screwdriver (86) to unscrew the **5/16" x 1/2"L button head socket bolt (46)** and separate the **crank axle (45)** from the **drive pulley (41)**.
10. Reverse the steps to refit. Follow section 3.5 to set belt (40) tension.

The manual says "follow Procedure 3" for the belt tension. On this machine the belt tension step is in section 3.5.
