1. Take the **adjustment cover L/R (122/123)** apart.
2. Pull up the **magnet base (117)** to remove the **non-segment knob (16)**.
3. Use a **10 mm open-ended wrench** to remove the nut holding the steel cable for the non-segment knob (16), then release the screw holding the knob.
4. Use the Phillips head screwdriver (86) to release **two M6 x P1.0 x 10L flat Phillips head screws**.
5. Use **12 mm and 13 mm open-ended wrenches** to remove the **5/16" x UNC18 x 2" hex head bolt (124)** and the **5/16" x UNC18 x 5.8T luck nut (125)**, then take the non-segment knob (16) and the **cable device mount (9)** off the **main frame (1)**.
6. Use the Phillips head screwdriver (86) to remove **four 3.5 x 12L sheet metal screws (30)** and separate the non-segment knob (16) from the cable device mount (9).

**Stop here if you only need to replace the knob.** Reverse the steps to refit.

7. Use **12 mm and 13 mm open-ended wrenches** to remove the **5/16" x UNC18 x 2" hex head bolt (127)** and the **5/16" x UNC18 x 5.8T luck nut (125)**, then take the **braking handle (12)** off.
8. To refit, adjust the **brake pad wool felt (134)** against the **flywheel (31)** with the 5/16" x UNC18 x 2" hex head bolt (127).

The manual writes the cover part numbers as "(123/123)" in step 1. The correct pair is (122/123).
