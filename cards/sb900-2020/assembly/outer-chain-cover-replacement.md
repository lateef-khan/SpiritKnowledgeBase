1. Remove the **adjustment cover R (123)**.
2. Use the Phillips head screwdriver (86) to unscrew **two 5 x 16 m/m tapping screws (29)** and **three 3.5 x 12 m/m sheet metal screws (30)**, then take the **outer chain cover (60)** off.
3. Reverse the steps to refit the outer chain cover (60).
