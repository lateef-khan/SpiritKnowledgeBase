Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #82 | 3/8" | Nut | 4 pcs |
| #81 | 3/8" x 3-1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #83 | 3/8" x 23mm | Curved Washer | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #91 | M5 x 10mm | Screw | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 4**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #49 | ø8 | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| #48 | ø8 x 16mm | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| #88 | M8 × P1.25 × 20L | Screw | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 6**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #93 | M5 x 12L | Tapping Screw | 2 pcs |
| #101 | 4 x 16L | Sheet Metal Screw | 3 pcs |

The checklist runs 1, 2, 4, 6. Steps 3 (console) and 5 (left/right pedals) use no pack hardware.

**Same parts as the SB900 2019, different step numbers.** The 2019 checklist calls the handlebar block step 3 and the tablet holder block step 5. The parts and quantities are identical.

Unlike the 2019 machine, both #93 tapping screws are used here: the step 6 instruction fits the Drink Bottle Holder (23) with them.

**Item numbers are not shared between models.** The 2023 machines renumbered the whole pack - there `#93` is a button head socket bolt, while on the 2019-2021 machines `#93` is an M5 tapping screw. Always use the checklist printed in your own machine's manual.
