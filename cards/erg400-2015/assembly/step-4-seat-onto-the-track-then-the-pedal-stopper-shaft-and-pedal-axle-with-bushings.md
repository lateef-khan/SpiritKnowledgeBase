1. Remove the pre-assembled **Rubber Foot (82)** and **Phillips Head Screw (83)** from both sides of the end of the aluminum track.
2. Slide the **Seat (30)** onto the **Aluminum Track (2)**. "Be sure of the correct orientation of the Seat" - the book does not say which way; the drawing has the seat's recess toward the rear.
3. Put one **Axle Bushing (91)** on the **Pedal Stopper Shaft (58)** through the **Main frame (1)** and cover it with the **Lever Sleeve (59)**.
4. Push the **Pedal Axle (8)** through the main frame, fit **two Axle Bushings (73)** and the **Pedals (72)** on both ends ("make sure pedals are correct for each side"), and tighten the shaft with **two Flat Washers (69) and two Button Head Socket Bolts (92)** using the combination M5 Allen wrench (97).

All of it is "Parts Pre-Assembled on Machine" - nothing here comes from the hardware bag. The rubber feet and screws go back on in step 5.
