Pull up the **Main Frame (1)** as shown (the track hinges up to vertical) and install the **Rear Stabilizer (3)** on the end of the **Aluminum Track (2)**. Tighten with **four Button Head Socket Bolts (92) and four Flat Washers (69)** using the combination M5 Allen wrench (97).

The bolts and washers are "Parts Pre-Assembled on Machine" - take them out of the track end, fit the stabilizer, put them back. The page heading reads "Handlebar, Seat, Pedals and Console", which is wrong; this page is the rear stabilizer only.
