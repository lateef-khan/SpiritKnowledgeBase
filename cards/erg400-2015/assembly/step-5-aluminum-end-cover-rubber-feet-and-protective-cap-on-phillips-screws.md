1. Cover the aluminum track with the **Aluminum End Cover (29)**, tightened with **two Phillips Head Screws (45)** using the combination tool (97).
2. Secure the **two Rubber Feet (82)** at both ends of the aluminum track with **two Phillips Head Screws (83)** - the ones taken off in step 4.
3. Secure the **Protective Cap (84)** at the top of the aluminum track with **two Phillips Head Screws (45)**.

This is the last mechanical step; the console then takes its external power supply (linked card). The page heading "Stabilizer Tubes and Wheel Covers" is recycled from step 1.
