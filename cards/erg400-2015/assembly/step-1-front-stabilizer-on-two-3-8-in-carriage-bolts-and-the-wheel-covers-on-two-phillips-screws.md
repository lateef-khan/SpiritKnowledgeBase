1. Mount the **Front Stabilizer (6)** onto the **Main Frame (1)** with **two Carriage Bolts (93)** through the stabilizer and the frame, **two Curved Washers (94)** and the **Cap Nuts (95)**. Tighten with the 12/14 mm open end wrench.
2. Install the right and left **Wheel Covers (54, 55)** on the twin tube ends of the main frame - "be aware not to mix up left and right" - and tighten each with a **Phillips Head Screw (45)** using the combination tool (97).

The step text says "one Cap Nut (95)"; the hardware box on the same page and the pack checklist both say **2 pcs**, one per bolt, and the drawing shows two - this card follows the drawing and the pack.

The page's own heading is used again over steps 3 and 5, and step 2 and 4 carry a "Handlebar, Seat, Pedals and Console" heading that describes neither; the headings in this scanned book are recycled and the step numbers are the only reliable guide.
