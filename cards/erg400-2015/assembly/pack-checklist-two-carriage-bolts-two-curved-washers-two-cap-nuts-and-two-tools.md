**Hardware Step 1** is the only hardware in the bag - "ALL OTHER PARTS PRE-ASSEMBLED ON UNIT":

| # | Item | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 93 | 3/8" x 59 mm Carriage Bolt | 2 |
| 94 | 3/8" x 23 x 1.5T Curved Washer | 2 |
| 95 | 3/8" x 12.5T Cap Nut | 2 |

**Assembly tools:** 97, Combination M5 Allen Wrench and Phillips Head Screw Driver; 96, Wrench (the step 1 text calls it a 12/14 mm open end wrench).

Steps 2 to 5 use fasteners that arrive fitted to the machine - button head bolts 92 and flat washers 69 on the track and pedal axle, Phillips screws 45 and 83 on the covers and rubber feet. Every step page after this one is boxed "Parts Pre-Assembled on Machine".
