The book prints each profile as a **ten-column, eight-row block picture with no numbers**. Each
column is one time segment (a tenth of the workout time) and each block is two resistance levels, so
eight blocks is level 16. The filled blocks per column, counted from a 300 dpi render of the page:

| Program | Name as printed | Column heights 1-10 (of 8) | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Polling** (Rolling is meant) | 3 4 5 4 3 4 5 4 3 4 | rolling |
| **2** | Valley | 5 5 4 3 2 1 2 3 4 5 | dip to the middle |
| **3** | Fat Burn | 1 2 3 4 4 4 4 3 2 1 | plateau |
| **4** | Ramp | 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 7 6 5 | climb, short descent |
| **5** | Mountain | 2 3 4 5 6 6 5 4 3 2 | pyramid |
| **6** | Mountain (2) | 3 4 6 3 4 6 3 4 6 3 | three peaks |

The "Reading A Program Profile" example above the six is the Rolling shape. A block is a picture of
relative resistance; the book does not print a level number for any segment, and UP/DOWN still
change the level during the workout.

**The Xterra bikes' twelve-preset consoles draw the same Rolling, Valley, Fat Burn and Mountain
shapes** on the same grid; their Ramp (2 2 3 4 5 6 6 6 5 4) and Intervals (2 3 5 2 3 5 2 3 5 2) differ
from this rower's Ramp and Mountain (2) - see `see_also`. The ERG700's Fat Burn, Cardio and Strength
are a different console and a different picture.
