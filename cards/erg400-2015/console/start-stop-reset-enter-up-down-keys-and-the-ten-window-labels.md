**The window** (the scan's callouts, clockwise): Total Workout Time, Number of Strokes, Calories Burned, Watts Produced, Program Selected, Program Profile, Rowing Position, Resistance Level, Strokes Per Minute, Distance Rowed. The program profile is a ten-column bar graph at the bottom; the rowing-position strip beside it draws a rower figure. **There is no pulse window** - the book prints no heart-rate readout, strap or grips, though its safety page carries the boilerplate "pulse sensors are not medical devices" line.

**Keys** - five, in two rows: UP, ENTER, DOWN above STOP/RESET and START.

- **START:** in non-operational mode starts **P0** (manual) exercise mode; after choosing a program, enters exercise mode; **held 3 seconds, resets all functions and values to zero.**
- **STOP/RESET:** in operational mode enters **pause**; "pressing this key at any time resets all function, the display clears and enters idle mode automatically". The two sentences are printed together and the book does not say how a pause differs from a reset; read the first press as a pause and a second press as the reset.
- **ENTER:** press for data setting - if ENTER is not pressed during an adjustment the data is accepted anyway - and to confirm a setting or value.
- **UP/DOWN:** choose the program; raise or lower a setting; **during a workout, adjust the level by 1 per press - held 2 seconds or more, the key enters "fast key mode"** and runs.

The console is fed by an **external power supply** plugged into a jack at the front of the unit near the stabilizer tube (the book's Power page; a cold machine must warm to room temperature before it is plugged in). Programs P1 to P6, the user program and the profile's sixteen resistance levels are programs cards. Miles or kilometres: [units card](change-km-to-miles-with-up-and-enter-pressed-together.md).
