"**Changing Distance KM to Mile: press the UP and ENTER keys at the same time** to change KM to Mile." That is the whole instruction - one line at the foot of the program page. The book does not say whether the same press changes miles back to kilometres, whether the setting survives a reset, or which unit the console ships in.

This is the only Xterra rower book in the range that prints a units change; the ERG160, ERG180, ERG220 and water rowers count distance in metres with no setting ([ERG160 card](start-stop-reset-enter-up-down-keys-and-the-ten-window-labels.md) for this console's other keys), and the ERG500 counts miles only ([ERG500 card](../../erg500-2018/console/mode-reset-up-down-keys-and-six-windows-with-distance-in-miles-to-99-9.md)). The X2 bikes change units with a three-key hold at plug-in ([bike card](../../shared/console/xterra-console-change-miles-to-km-with-two-people-and-three-keys-at-plug-in.md)).
