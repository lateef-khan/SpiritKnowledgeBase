1. Take the console apart.
2. Use a **5 mm L Allen wrench** to loosen the **four button head socket bolts**, then remove both side handlebars.
3. Release **four 3 x 10 m/m tapping screws** to remove the **handgrip side caps, top and bottom**.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **four 20 m/m tapping screws** holding the **hand pulse cable assembly**, separate it and remove it.
5. Reverse the steps to refit.

This machine has **two side handlebars** held by four button head socket bolts and a 5 mm Allen wrench. The 2016 machine has a single handlebar assembly held by two hex head bolts and a 12 mm wrench.
