Hardware: **#50** 3/8" x 53L (2 pcs), **#135** 3/8" x16 x 12.5T Cap Nut (2 pcs), **#71** 3/8" x 25mm x 2T Flat Washer (2 pcs).

1. Attach the Rear Stabilizer (5) to the Main Frame (1) by sliding two Cap Nuts (135) and two Flat Washers (71) onto two bolts (50), then into the rear stabilizer. Tighten using the **12/14 mm Wrench (100)**.

**The manual names #50 two different ways.** The assembly pack checklist on page 3 and the hardware box beside this step both call #50 a **Carriage Bolt**. The instruction itself calls it a **Hex Head Bolt**. The manual never reconciles the two, so the wording above leaves the part type out rather than picking a side. The B94 2019 prints the same step with "Carriage Bolt" in the instruction, and the LCB 2023 rewrites the step entirely.

The bag holds two of each item; there is no fourth fastener in this step.
