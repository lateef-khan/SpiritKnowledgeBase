Hardware: **#74** 5/16" x18 x5/8" Button Head Socket Bolt (4 pcs), **#72** 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T Flat Washer (4 pcs), **#103** 5/16" x 1.5T Split Washer (4 pcs).

1. Secure the Seat Handle Bar (3) onto the Console Mast (2) with the four Button Head Socket Bolts (74), four Flat Washers (72) and **two** Split Washers (103), by using the Combination M5 Allen Wrench (76).
2. Let the two Hand pulse W/Cable Assemblies (26) through the front hole on the Console Mast (2) and outside.

**The split washer count is contradictory in the source.** This instruction says **two** Split Washers (103). The assembly pack checklist on page 3 and the hardware box printed beside this step both say **4 pcs** of #103. The manual never reconciles the two. Count what is in your hardware pack. The B94 2019 manual has the identical contradiction; the LCB 2023 rewrote the step to use all four.

The bolt size is also printed two ways: `5/16" x 5/8"` in the checklist and `5/16" x18 x5/8"` in the hardware box beside the step.
