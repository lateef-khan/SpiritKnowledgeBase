1. Take the pedals and the crank arm apart. Separate the console mast cover, then use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **nine 3.5 x 16 m/m sheet metal screws** and **three 5 x 16 m/m tapping screws** holding the left shroud.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **three 5 x 16 m/m tapping screws** to remove the **chain cover R**.
3. To refit, put the right side case on the main frame. Line up the centre of the wheel with the centre of the axle and secure it with **three 5 x 16 mm tapping screws**, then reconnect the DC power cord. Match the left side case with the right side case and secure with **nine 3.5 x 12 mm self tapping screws** and **three 5 x 16 mm tapping screws**. Follow section 2.1 to refit the cranks and pedals.

**Two problems in this section.** The screw size changes from 3.5 x **16** m/m on removal to 3.5 x **12** mm on refit. And step 3 tells you to reconnect a DC power cord that no removal step ever tells you to disconnect. The B94 2019 manual has the same wording, and there the cord is disconnected with a 15 mm open end wrench.
