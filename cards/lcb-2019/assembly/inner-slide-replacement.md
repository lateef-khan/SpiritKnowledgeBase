1. Remove the **locking knob**.
2. Separate the **seat post cover** and the **chain covers R/L**, and pull the **seat slider** and seat post cover up. Take the **centre spatial wrap** off the **main frame** with a rubber hammer. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **two 4 x 12 m/m sheet metal screws**, then remove the **slide spacer** with the rubber hammer to separate the seat slider and the centre spatial wrap.
3. To refit, return the centre spatial wrap to the seat slider, return the slide spacer into the seat slider and tighten the two 4 x 12 m/m sheet metal screws, then insert it into the main frame. Use the rubber hammer to seat the centre spatial wrap and refit the locking knob.
