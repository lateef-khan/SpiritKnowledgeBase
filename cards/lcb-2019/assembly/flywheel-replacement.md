1. Take both left and right front shrouds (36)(37) and the **belt (14)** apart.
2. Unplug the electric wires connected to the **generator flywheel (20)**, then use an **11 m/m wrench** to unscrew **four 1/4" x 3/4" hex head screws (54)** with **four 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers (69)** and **four 1/4" split washers (76)**. The generator flywheel (20) is then released.
3. Follow the steps in order to refit the flywheel.
