The console to driver board cable on this machine is a **6-pin** cable.

The LCB 2016 and LCR 2016 use a **7-pin** cable, and their manuals print the pin list. **The LCB 2019 manual does not print the pin list for its 6-pin cable.** Do not read the 7-pin list across to this machine.

To check the cable: inspect that it is seated well, then test by swapping in a known good cable.
