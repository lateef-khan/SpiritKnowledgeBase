Most noise comes from **loose screws**. Sometimes a part has deformed or shifted and is rubbing or moving roughly.

1. **Noise from the left and right pedals** is normally pedal wear. Replace the worn pedal.
2. **Left and right crank.** A loose crank makes noise and rough movement. Tightening the crank fixes it.
3. **Chain covers.** Most cases are the chain covers deforming so the crank arms L and R scrape them. The covers deform because safety rules require a minimum gap. Replace the chain covers. In some cases a deformed drive pulley rubs the right chain cover; replace the drive pulley.
4. If there is still noise with the left and right shrouds off, find where the noise comes from and replace that part.
