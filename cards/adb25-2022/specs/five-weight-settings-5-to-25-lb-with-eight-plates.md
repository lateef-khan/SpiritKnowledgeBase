**Five settings, 5 lb apart, one dumbbell:**

| LB | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| KG | 2.3 | 4.5 | 6.8 | 9.1 | 11.3 |

"Checking the chart below, note the 5 weight increments from 5 LB to 25 LB or 2.3 KG to 11.3 KG." The carton holds
one handle, one dumbbell tray and **eight weight plates**, each "1.1Kg" in the parts list. The weight is chosen by
rotating the middle of the handle grip with the handle fully seated in its tray; the unused plates stay in the base.

The book's pack-list page carries the 12.5 lb pair's chart figures in its text layer - "2.5 12.5", "5 7.5 10
12.5", "27 3.4 4.5 5.57" - which are **not printed** on the page and are not settings of this dumbbell. No total
weight or dumbbell dimension is printed; the base is "325*156*38" in the parts list
(`xterra-strength-specs-no-specification-table-in-the-dumbbell-and-stand-manuals`). Siblings: the 12.5 lb pair
(`adb125pr-2022-specs-five-weight-settings-2-5-to-12-5-lb-per-hand-with-sixteen-plates`) and the 55 lb dumbbell
(`adb55-2022-specs-five-weight-settings-11-to-55-lb-with-eight-plates`); the 25 lb and 55 lb cradles share the
stand's longer bolts (`dbstand-2022-specs-holds-only-xterra-adjustable-dumbbell-cradles-and-prints-no-load-rating`).
