The 25 LB Adjustable Dumbbell manual prints an **Exploded View** (PDF p. 9, printed 7) and a **25LB SPARE PARTS LIST** (PDF p. 10, printed 8) headed *S/N / DESCRIPTION / SPEC / QTY*. **The quantities are for one dumbbell**: one handle (21), one base (23), eight plates (22) of 1.1 kg. The list runs 1 to 23 with **no item 13** and the spiral sleeves and locating rings split left and right (12L/12R, 15L/15R), so it has 24 rows. The exploded view is rotated artwork whose callouts the text layer does not carry; they were read from a render and match the list. The *Spec* column is the book's - millimetres for the plastic and steel parts, thread sizes for the fasteners, the plate mass in kilograms.

**What the list tells a service call.** The plates are item 22 (printed *Dumbell plate*), eight of 1.1 kg, so the dumbbell adjusts in five steps from 5 to 25 lb; the two clump weights are item 3, the selector dial item 16, the locks item 10 with square springs item 9 and ball plungers item 14, the base (tray) item 23, 325 x 156 x 38 mm. The 12.5 lb pair and the 55 lb dumbbell print their own lists with different numbering (`adb125pr-2022-specs-parts-spare-parts-list-for-the-pair-with-sixteen-plates`, `adb55-2022-specs-parts-spare-parts-list-for-one-dumbbell-with-eight-plates-of-2-5-kg`); the stand is `dbstand-2022-specs-parts-spare-parts-list-fourteen-items`.
