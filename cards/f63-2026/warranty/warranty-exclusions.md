1. **Consequential, collateral or incidental damages**, such as property damage and incidental expenses resulting from any breach of this written or any implied warranty. *Some states do not allow this exclusion, so it may not apply to you.*
2. **Service call reimbursement to the consumer.** Service call reimbursement to the dealer where the call does not involve malfunction or defects in workmanship or material; for units beyond the warranty period; beyond the service call reimbursement period; where no component replacement is required; or where the treadmill is not in ordinary household use.
3. **Damage caused by service performed by anyone other than an authorized SOLE service company; use of non-original parts; or external causes** - corrosion (RUST), discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, **improper maintenance including lack of consistent lubrication of the deck**, inadequate power supply, natural disasters, or elemental damage such as **power surges, lightning storms, water damage or fire damage**.
4. **Products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered.**
5. **Products that have been sold, transferred, bartered or given to a third party.** See `f63-2026-warranty-transfer`.
6. **Products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at SOLE.** SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists.
7. **This warranty is expressly in lieu of all other warranties, expressed or implied**, including the warranties of merchantability and/or fitness for a particular purpose.
8. **Product use in any environment other than a residential setting.**
9. **Warranties outside North America may vary.** See `f63-2026-warranty-geographic-coverage`.
10. **The warranty is void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment.** See `f63-2026-warranty-void-outside-climate-control`.

The maintenance sections add two more exclusions of their own: **damage to the running belt from improper tracking or tension adjustment is not covered**, and **failure to lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty**. Neither is numbered in this list.
