**Record the serial number** of the product in the space provided on page 1 of the manual.

**Two ways to register:**

1. **The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to SOLE.**
2. **Register online** at `www.soletreadmills.com` under the **support tab**.

**The warranty section adds the deadline**, which page 1 does not mention: the card must be returned **within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty. **Outside the 10 day period the warranty can still be registered if you have proof of purchase** - receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation.

**Exclusion 6** makes this matter: products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at SOLE are not covered, and SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists.

**The manual does not say where the serial number label is on the machine.** It only gives a blank space to write it in.

Page 1 also asks you to record the **name of the dealer, the dealer's telephone number, and the purchase date** for future contact.
