**Warranty will be void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment.**

The manual names the examples itself: **garage, outside, carport, etc.**

This is exclusion 10 of the limited warranty. It stands alongside exclusion 8, which excludes **product use in any environment other than a residential setting**.

The electrical section gives the physical limits the machine is built for - 40 degrees C maximum operating temperature and 95% non-condensing humidity, never exposed to rain or moisture. See `f63-2026-operating-environment-limits`.
