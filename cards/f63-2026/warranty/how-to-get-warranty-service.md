**Service under this warranty must be obtained by following these steps, in order:**

1. **Contact your selling authorized SOLE dealer.** OR
2. **Submit the service request online** at `http://www.soletreadmills.com/serviceparts.html`, including **serial number, contact information and a brief description of the problem**.
3. If there is a question as to where to obtain service, **contact the service department at 866-780-7653**.
4. **If no local service is available**, SOLE will repair or replace the parts, at SOLE's option, within the warranty period **at no charge for parts**. **All transportation costs, both to the factory and on return to the owner, are the owner's responsibility.** The owner is responsible for adequate packaging on return. See `f63-2026-return-authorization-required`.
5. **The service department assists in troubleshooting before sending a field technician**, and may request pictures and video by email to help with diagnosis.

**Keep your bill of sale**, and keep all receipts if service is performed.

This written warranty gives you specific legal rights, and you may also have other rights that vary from state to state.
