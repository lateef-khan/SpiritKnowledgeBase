**Warranties outside of North America may vary.**

- **Sole USA warranty coverage is limited to the Continental United States.**
- **Sole USA does not sell or service equipment in Hawaii, Alaska, or outside of the United States.**
- **Contact your local dealer** for details regarding warranty availability in your region.

This is exclusion 9 of the limited warranty.
