Hardware: #83 x 4, #64 x 4, #59 x 4.

1. Align **square hole B** of the **Console Assembly (11)** with the **L-shaped hook plates A** on the **Left Upright (4)** and **Right Upright (5)**. Install in **direction C**. After the console assembly is fully seated and flush with both uprights, **push it further in direction D**. Fix the console assembly (11) onto the uprights with **Button Head Socket Bolt (83) 4 pcs, Split Washer (64) 4 pcs and Flat Washer (59) 4 pcs** using the M5 Allen wrench head screw wrench (97).
2. Connect **Computer Cable (Middle) (110)** to the **Computer Cable (Upper) (11~26)**, then organize the computer cable to the Right Upright (5).

NOTE printed at the end of this step: "Please DON'T Tighten All Screws."

**Letters A, B, C and D refer to a figure that did not survive the text extraction.** The printed page shows which hole and which direction each letter marks.

The service manual covers removing and replacing this assembly as a repair; see `f63-2026-console-assembly-replacement`.
