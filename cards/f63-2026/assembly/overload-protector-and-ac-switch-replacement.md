**Overload protector**: disconnect the wiring from the overload protector, replace it, and reconnect the wiring.

**AC power switch**: disconnect the wiring from the AC power switch, replace it, and reconnect the wiring.

The parts replacement guide calls this part the **overload protector**; the troubleshooting matrix calls the same part the **treadmill breaker**.
