Unpack first: the shipping strap and the folding safety pin have to come off correctly before anything else. See `f63-2026-unpacking-shipping-strap-and-safety-pin`.

| Step | What it covers | Card |
|---|---|---|
| 1 and 2 | Position the base, connect the computer cable, bolt on the uprights | `f63-2026-assembly-step-1-2-uprights` |
| 3 | Hook the console assembly onto the uprights | `f63-2026-assembly-step-3-console` |
| 4 | Fit the pad top cover to the console assembly | `f63-2026-assembly-step-4-pad-top-cover` |
| 5 | Fit the mast covers, base covers and safety key | `f63-2026-assembly-step-5-covers` |
| 6 | Remove the locking pin and fold the deck | `f63-2026-assembly-step-6-locking-pin` |
| 7 | Bolt the foot pads to the running deck | `f63-2026-assembly-step-7-foot-pads` |

**The manual disagrees with itself about when to tighten.**

- Step 2 says "Do not tighten the bolts completely until Step 6 is finished" and "NOTE: Please DON'T Tighten All Screws".
- Step 3 repeats "NOTE: Please DON'T Tighten All Screws".
- Step 4 says "NOTE: After above assembly, tighten the screws in place."

Both instructions are printed. The manual does not say which wins.

Page 5 carries a "Scan Here for Assembly Video" QR code. **The code itself is an image and did not survive the text extraction**, so the video link is not recoverable from this source.
