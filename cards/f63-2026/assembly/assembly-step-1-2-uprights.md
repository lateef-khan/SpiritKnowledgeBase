**Step 1**

1. Remove the treadmill from the carton and position it on a smooth, levelled floor. **The rear should be at least 3' from any wall.** (Page 2 asks for 3 1/2 feet; see `f63-2026-placement-and-clearance`.)
2. **Remove the plastic wrap and Styrofoam from beneath the unit before removing the Velcro belt.** Tilting the unit sideways after removing the belt may cause it to fold and bounce upwards, which can cause serious injury.

**Step 2** - hardware #83 x 2, #64 x 10, #59 x 10, #93 x 8

1. Connect **Computer Cable (Lower) (111)** to the **Computer Cable (Middle) (110)**, then organize the computer cable to the **Right Upright (5)**.
2. Install the **Left Upright (4)** and **Right Upright (5)** onto the **Frame Base (2)** with **Button Head Socket Bolt (93) 8 pcs, Split Washer (64) 8 pcs and Flat Washer (59) 8 pcs**, using the M5 Allen wrench head screw wrench (97).
3. Fix the Left Upright (4) and Right Upright (5) with **Button Head Socket Bolt (83) 2 pcs, Split Washer (64) 2 pcs and Flat Washer (59) 2 pcs** using wrench (97). **Do not tighten the bolts completely until Step 6 is finished.**

NOTE printed at the end of this step: "Please DON'T Tighten All Screws." Step 4 later says to tighten; see `f63-2026-assembly-overview` for that disagreement.
