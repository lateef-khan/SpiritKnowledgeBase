1. With a Phillips screwdriver, remove the **2 rear adjustment screws**.
2. With an **M6 hex wrench**, remove the **2 rear roller bolts**.
3. Remove the motor cover. Loosen the **4 mounting bolts** with the **M6 hex wrench**, loosen the **left and right belt tension bolts** with a **13 mm wrench**, and loosen the drive belt. Remove the **front roller bolts** with the **13 mm wrench**.
4. Replace the front and rear rollers, then reassemble in the reverse order. **After assembly, adjust the running belt tension to keep it centred.**
5. Adjust the belt tension to **70 to 75 lbs (the white zone)** with a tension gauge, then tighten the 4 mounting bolts.
