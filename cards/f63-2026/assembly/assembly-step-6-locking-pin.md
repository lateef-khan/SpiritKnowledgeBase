This step uses no hardware from the assembly pack.

1. **Remove Locking Pin Assembly (26) from the Frame Base (2)**, then plug **End Cap (112)** into the hole of the Frame Base (2).
2. **Lift the running deck to fold it.**

This is the step Assembly Step 2 refers to when it says not to tighten the upright bolts completely until Step 6 is finished.

The locking pin removed here is the shipping safety pin described in the unpacking warning; see `f63-2026-unpacking-shipping-strap-and-safety-pin`.
