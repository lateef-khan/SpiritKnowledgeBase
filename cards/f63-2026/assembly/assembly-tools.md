| Item | Part |
|---|---|
| #20 | Safety Key |
| #112 | End Cap |
| #96 | 13mm Wrench |
| #98 | M6 L Allen Wrench |
| #97 | Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver |
| #116 | Lubricant |

The **M6 L Allen wrench (98)** is also the tool the maintenance section calls for when adjusting belt tension and belt tracking. The tracking section refers to it as "A6 mm Allen wrench (98)"; it is the same tool.

The **lubricant (116)** is the one tube of Sole "Lube" the treadmill ships with; see `f63-2026-deck-lubrication`.
