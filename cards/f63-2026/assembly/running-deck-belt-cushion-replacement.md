**The gas spring must come off before this procedure.**

1. Fold the treadmill. With an **M5 hex wrench and a 12 mm wrench**, remove the gas spring bolts and remove the gas spring. Then remove the front and rear rollers.
2. Remove the **2 screws at the front of the side rails**, remove the left and right side rails, and pull off the lower cover.
3. With an **M5 screwdriver**, remove the **8 deck mounting screws**. Lift the running deck; **note that there is a support tube underneath**. Replace the deck or the belt as needed. **To replace the cushions, remove 6 cushions and install new ones.** Reassemble in the reverse order and adjust the running belt tension so it stays centred.
4. Adjust the belt tension to **70 to 75 lbs (the white zone)** and tighten the mounting bolts.

**Note on the printed step**: step 1 says "Follow procedure (F) to remove front and rear rollers", but procedure (F) is the AC power switch. The roller procedure is **(G)**.
