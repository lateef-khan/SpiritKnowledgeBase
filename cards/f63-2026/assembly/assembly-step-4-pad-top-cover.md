Hardware listed for this step: **#94 5/16" x 1-1/2" Button Head Socket Bolt, 2 pcs**.

1. Connect **Connecting Wire (11~30)** to **Connecting Wire (12~7)**, then organize the wire to the right console assembly (11).
2. Place the lower edge of the **Pad Top Cover Assembly (12)** against the upper edge of the **Console Assembly (11)**. Then slide it forward to the **Hex Head Bolt (84) 2 pcs** as shown in Figure 1.
3. Install the Pad Top Cover Assembly (12) on the console assembly (11) with **Button Head Socket Bolt (94) 2 pcs** using the Allen wrench head screw wrench (97).
4. Fix the Pad Top Cover Assembly (12) on the console assembly (11) with **Hex Head Bolt (84) 2 pcs** using **Wrench (96)** - the 13mm wrench.

NOTE printed at the end of this step: "After above assembly, tighten the screws in place." Steps 2 and 3 said not to tighten until step 6; see `f63-2026-assembly-overview`.

**Hex Head Bolt (84) is not in the assembly pack checklist** on pages 6 to 7, even though this step needs 2 of them.
