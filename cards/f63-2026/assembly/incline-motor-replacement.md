1. Remove the motor cover, fold the machine into position, and remove the old incline motor.
2. **Set the incline motor to its lowest stroke position: 200 mm. The operating range is 200 to 300 mm.**
3. Install the new incline motor using a **14 mm wrench**.
4. Connect the incline motor wiring to the control board:
   - **White** cable to the **COM** terminal
   - **Red** cable to the **UP** terminal
   - **Black** cable to the **DOWN** terminal

**The 200 mm figure is specific to this model.** Earlier F63 manuals give 195 mm or 225 mm.
