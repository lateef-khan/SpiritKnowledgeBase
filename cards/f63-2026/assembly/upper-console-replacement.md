1. With an **M5 L-type hex wrench**, remove:
   - **2 M5 screws** from the rear console cover
   - **2 button head hex screws**
   - **2 hex bolts**, using a **13 mm wrench**
2. Disconnect the console control cable connector, replace the unit, then reassemble in the reverse order.

Replacing the whole console assembly is a longer job; see the console assembly card.
