**Serious injury could occur if this folding treadmill is not unpacked properly.**

**The deck restraint shipping strap**

A shipping strap is installed around the treadmill base to prevent the treadmill unfolding accidentally during shipping. If it is not removed properly the treadmill could **spring open unexpectedly** and injure anyone standing near it.

1. Position the treadmill **flat on the ground**, in the orientation it would be in if you were using it.
2. **Do not tilt the treadmill up on its side while removing the shipping strap.** This could cause the folding mechanism to spring open.
3. **Pull up on the strap and cut it with a pair of scissors.**

**The safety pin**

A safety pin is locked on the treadmill base to prevent the treadmill unfolding during shipping. **This safety pin has to be removed before powering on or using the incline function**, to avoid damaging the machine. When removing it, **pull it away from the treadmill base completely.**
