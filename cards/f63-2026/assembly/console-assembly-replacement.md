**First follow the upper console procedure to remove the upper console.**

1. With an **M5 L-type hex wrench**, remove:
   - **4 self-tapping screws** from the front and rear lower covers
   - **4 button head hex screws**
2. Disconnect the console control cable connector, replace the assembly, then reassemble in the reverse order.
