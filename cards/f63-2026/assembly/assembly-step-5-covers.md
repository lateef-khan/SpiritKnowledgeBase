Hardware: **#87 M5 x 10mm Phillips Head Screw, 6 pcs** and **#95 3.5 x 12mm Sheet Metal Screw, 4 pcs**.

1. Install **Front Console Mast Cover (L) (47), Rear Console Mast Cover (L) (48), Front Console Mast Cover (R) (49)** and **Rear Console Mast Cover (R) (50)** onto the **Console Assembly (11)** with **Sheet Metal Screw (95) 4 pcs**.
2. Install **Frame Base Cover (L) (42)** and **Frame Base Cover (R) (43)** onto the **Left Upright (4)** and **Right Upright (5)** with **Phillips Head Screw (87) 4 pcs**.
3. Install **Console Outer Cover Mast Cover (46)** onto the Console Assembly (11) with **Phillips Head Screw (87) 2 pcs**.
4. **Insert Square Safety Key (20) into the Console Assembly (11).**

The manual names the M5 Allen wrench head screw wrench (97) as the tool for all three screw steps, including the two that use Phillips head screws. Wrench (97) is a combination M5 Allen wrench and Phillips head screwdriver, so one tool covers both.
