Hardware for the step: **#83 x 2, #64 x 2, #59 x 2** - one of each per side.

1. **Rotate the foot pad (8) upward until it is against the running deck.**
2. Fix the foot pad (8) on the running deck with **Button Head Socket Bolt (83) 1 pcs, Split Washer (64) 1 pc and Flat Washer (59) 1 pcs** using the M5 Allen wrench head screw wrench (97).
3. **Install the foot pad (8) on the left side the same way as on the right side.**

This is the last assembly step.
