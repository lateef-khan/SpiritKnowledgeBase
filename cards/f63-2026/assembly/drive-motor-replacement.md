1. With a Phillips screwdriver, loosen **3 screws at the front of the motor cover** and **2 screws on the left and right sides**.
2. Disconnect the motor wires: **W (white), U (red), V (black)**.
3. With an **M6 L-type hex wrench** remove the **4 mounting bolts**. With a **13 mm wrench** loosen the **left and right belt tension bolts**. Remove the motor and fit a new one, then reassemble in the reverse order.
   - **When reinstalling the belt, make sure it is parallel to the side frame.**
   - **Do not fully tighten the 4 motor mounting bolts at first.**
   - Refer to the manual's diagram for the belt return direction and the motor rotation direction.
4. Adjust the belt tension with the **13 mm wrench**, using a belt tension gauge, to **70 to 75 lbs (the white zone)**. Tighten the 4 mounting bolts with the **M6 hex wrench**, then reconnect the ground wire and the motor wires **W / U / V**.
