| Task | Interval |
|---|---|
| **Vacuum under motor cover and check wiring** | **Every other month** |
| **Clean and inspect deck for lubrication** | **Every other month** |
| **Lubricate deck** | **Every 90 hours, or sooner if dry** |
| **Inspect belt tracking**, adjust if necessary | **Monthly** |

The printed chart is a **blank log grid** with a Date column for recording each service. **Only the column headings survived the text extraction; the grid itself carries no values.** The intervals above are exactly the headings as printed.

The belt and deck cleaning section separately asks for a **monthly** wipe of the belt edges; see `f63-2026-cleaning-the-belt-and-deck`.

The lubrication section gives a second interval alongside the 90 hours: **every 90 days regardless of usage**. See `f63-2026-deck-lubrication`.
