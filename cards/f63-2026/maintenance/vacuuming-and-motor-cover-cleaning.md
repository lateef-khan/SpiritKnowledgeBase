**Dirt, dust and hair can block air inlets and accumulate on the running belt.**

- **Vacuum underneath your treadmill monthly** to prevent excess build-up of dirt that can get sucked into the inner workings under the motor cover.
- **Every other month, remove the motor cover and carefully vacuum out dirt and hair.**

**UNPLUG THE POWER CORD BEFORE THIS TASK.** See `f63-2026-how-to-disconnect-power`.

The preventative maintenance chart pairs the motor cover job with a wiring check; see `f63-2026-maintenance-schedule`.
