The treadmill is designed so the tread-belt stays reasonably centred in use. **It is normal for some belts to drift near one side**, depending on a user's gait and whether they favour one leg. **If the belt continues to move toward one side, adjust it.**

1. Use the **6 mm Allen wrench (98)** supplied. The manual calls it "A6 mm Allen wrench (98)" here and "M6 L Allen wrench (98)" in the tension section; it is the same tool.
2. **Make tracking adjustments on the left side bolt.**
3. **Set belt speed at 3 mph.**
4. **If the belt is too close to the left side, turn the bolt only 1/4 turn to the right (clockwise)** and wait a few minutes for the belt to adjust itself. **Continue in 1/4 turns until the belt stabilizes in the centre of the running deck.**
5. **If the belt is too close to the right side, turn the bolt counter-clockwise.**

**A small adjustment can make a dramatic difference, which may not be apparent right away.**

The belt may need periodic tracking adjustment depending on use and walking or running characteristics. **Proper belt tracking is an owner responsibility, common with all treadmills**, and adjustments become less of a concern as the belt is used.

**ATTENTION: damage to the running belt resulting from improper tracking or tension adjustments is not covered under the Sole warranty.**
