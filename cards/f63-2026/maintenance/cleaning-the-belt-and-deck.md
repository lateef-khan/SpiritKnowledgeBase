The treadmill uses a very high-efficiency, **low-friction deck**. Performance is maximized when the deck is kept as clean as possible.

1. Use a **soft, damp cloth or paper towel**. Wipe the **edge of the belt** and the **area between the belt edge and the frame**.
2. **Reach as far as practical directly under the belt edge.**
3. **Do this once a month** to extend belt and bed life.
4. To clean the top of the textured belt, use a **mild soap and water solution with a nylon scrub brush**.
5. **Allow to dry before using.**

Cleaning is not lubricating. The lubricant goes between the belt and the deck on a different schedule; see `f63-2026-deck-lubrication`.
