**This occurs during normal break-in, or until the belt stabilizes.** Sometimes the black dust from the belt appears on the floor behind the treadmill. **This is normal.**

**The two manuals name different parts for the same symptom.** This owner's manual says the dust comes from **the belt**. The service manual troubleshooting matrix records "black particles collecting under the treadmill" as **the drive belt breaking in**, and says to vacuum periodically. See `f63-2026-noises`. Neither page corrects the other.
