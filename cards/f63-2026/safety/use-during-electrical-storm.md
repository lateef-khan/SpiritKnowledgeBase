**NEVER use your treadmill during an electrical storm.** Surges may occur in your household power supply that could damage treadmill components.

**Unplug the treadmill during an electrical storm as a precaution.**

The warranty does not cover damage from external causes including power surges and lightning storms; see `f63-2026-warranty-exclusions`.
