- **Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine.** There are obvious pinch points and other caution areas that can cause harm.
- **Keep hands away from all moving parts.**
- This appliance is **not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge**, unless they have been given supervision or instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible for their safety.
- **Remove the tether cord after use** to prevent unauthorized treadmill operation. Removing the safety key also secures the treadmill from unauthorized use.

The console has a separate keypad lock; see `f63-2026-child-lock`.
