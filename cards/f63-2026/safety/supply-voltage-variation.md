**If voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more, the performance of your treadmill may be affected.**

**Such conditions are not covered under your warranty.**

If you suspect the voltage is low, contact your local power company or a licensed electrician for proper testing.

The diagnosis guide gives the symptom this produces: the belt reaches only about seven miles an hour while the display shows more. See `f63-2026-reaches-only-7-mph`.
