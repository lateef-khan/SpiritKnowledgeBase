- **Maximum operating temperature: 40 degrees C.**
- **Humidity: 95% non-condensing** (no water drops forming on surfaces).
- **NEVER expose this treadmill to rain or moisture.** It is not designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.
- Do not use outdoors.
- Do not operate where **aerosol spray products** are being used or where **oxygen is being administered**. Sparks from the motor may ignite a highly gaseous environment.

The warranty section adds a separate rule that is easy to miss: the warranty is **void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment** (garage, outside, carport). That half is on `f63-2026-warranty-void-outside-climate-control`.
