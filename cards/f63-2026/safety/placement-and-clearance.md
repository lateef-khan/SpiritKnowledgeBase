- Install the treadmill on a **flat level surface** with access to a grounded outlet, with **only the treadmill plugged into that circuit**.
- **Do not block the rear of the treadmill.**
- Do not operate the treadmill on **deeply padded, plush or shag carpet**. Damage to both the carpet and the treadmill may result.

**The manual gives two different rear clearance figures.**

| Where | Figure |
|---|---|
| Important Safety Instructions, page 2 | "a minimum of 3 1/2 feet clearance between the rear of the treadmill and any fixed object" |
| Assembly Step 1, page 8 | "The rear should be at least 3' from any wall" |

Use the larger figure if you have the room. Neither page corrects the other.

**The outlet rating is not repeated on this card.** This manual names a 120-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet, and the service manual card `f63-2026-power-outlet-requirements` names different figures. Check the machine before quoting one.
