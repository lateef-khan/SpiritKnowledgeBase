**The pulse sensors are not medical devices.** Various factors, including the user's movement, may affect the accuracy of heart rate readings.

They are **intended only as exercise aids in determining heart rate trends in general.**

The calorie readout carries a similar disclaimer of its own; see `f63-2026-calorie-display-accuracy`.
