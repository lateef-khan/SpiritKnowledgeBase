A safety tether cord is supplied and **should be used at all times**. It is there in case you fall or move too far back on the tread-belt. Pulling the cord stops tread-belt movement.

1. **Place the safety key into position on the console control head.** The treadmill will not start or operate without it. Removing the safety key also secures the treadmill from unauthorized use.
2. **Fasten the plastic clip onto your clothing securely** to assure good holding power. Attach it securely so it cannot come off, and be familiar with its function and limitations. The treadmill will stop, depending on speed, with a **one to two step coast** any time the safety key is pulled off the console. **Use the red Stop switch in normal operation.**
3. If the console displays the SAFE message asking you to replace the key to operate, put the safety key back to continue using the treadmill.

A stop that is abrupt rather than a one to two step coast is a fault; see `f63-2026-belt-stops-suddenly-when-key-pulled`.
