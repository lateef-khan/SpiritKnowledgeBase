**The codes on this machine look alike and mean different things. E3 is the incline and calibration error. E01, E02, E03, E04, E05 and E06 are two-digit controller codes and are not E1 to E6. E22 is communication and E31 is overheat.**

**This is E01, two digits. It is not E1 and it is not E03 (hardware overcurrent).**

**Defect reason**: overload.

1. **Possible cause**: the load exceeds the rated capacity, so the current is excessive and the system protects itself. Restart the machine.
2. A part of the treadmill may be **jammed**, preventing the motor from rotating, which causes excessive load and current. Adjust the treadmill and restart, or apply lubrication.
3. Check for **abnormal overcurrent noise or a burning smell** during motor operation. Replace the motor if necessary.
4. Replace the controller.
