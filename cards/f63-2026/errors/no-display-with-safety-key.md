| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The ON/OFF switch has not been switched on | Switch the AC switch on |
| The safety key is in the wrong position | Insert the safety key in the right position |
| The computer cable is broken | Check the wire and connect it again |
| The safety device is broken (open) | Replace the safety key device |
| Other components are faulty | Replace the console |

The reverse fault, where the treadmill displays or operates with no safety key fitted, means the safety device is shorted. Replace the safety key device or the console.

**A dim, incomplete or imperfect LED display** is a different fault: a connector that has fallen off, a broken LED light, or too little power to the console. Check the connector, replace the LED or console, check the power to the console, and replace the lower controller. If the LED displays themselves are broken, replace the console.
