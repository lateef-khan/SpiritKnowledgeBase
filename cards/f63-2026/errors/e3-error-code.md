**The codes on this machine look alike and mean different things. E3 is the incline and calibration error. E01, E02, E03, E04, E05 and E06 are two-digit controller codes and are not E1 to E6. E22 is communication and E31 is overheat.**

**This is E3, a single digit. It is not E03 (hardware overcurrent) and not E31 (overheat).**

The error table lists E3 twice, with two different descriptions.

**E3 as the incline error**, displayed in the incline window:

1. Check whether the incline cable is properly connected.
2. Reconnect the incline cable and power on the unit again.
3. After reconnection, **perform incline calibration again**.

**E3 as the calibration error**:

1. Check whether the incline cable is properly connected.
2. After reconnection, **perform calibration again**.

Both point at the incline cable and both end in a calibration.
