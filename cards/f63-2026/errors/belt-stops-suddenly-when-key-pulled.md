**What is normal:** page 4 says the treadmill will stop, depending on speed, **with a one to two step coast** any time the safety key is pulled off the console.

**What is a fault:** the diagnosis guide lists "tread-belt stops quickly/suddenly when tether cord is pulled" with one cause - **high belt/deck friction**. It points at the lubrication section.

So an abrupt stop is a **lubrication symptom, not a safety key symptom**. See `f63-2026-deck-lubrication`.

High belt and deck friction also trips the on-board 15 amp breaker and can shut the controller down mid-workout; see `f63-2026-onboard-circuit-breaker` and `f63-2026-stops-by-itself-or-immediately`.
