**When the treadmill stops or shuts off by itself**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The house breaker tripped | Reset it |
| The treadmill breaker tripped | Reset the treadmill breaker |
| The treadmill controller shut down, and its LED would be ON | **Belt and deck lubrication.** Then turn the ON/OFF switch off and turn the power on again |

**When the treadmill stops immediately after START is pressed**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The controller is broken | Turn the AC switch off and turn the power on again |
| Check belt lubrication | Replace the controller and calibrate it |

**When the safety key is removed and the treadmill does not stop**: replace the safety key device, then the controller or power board, then the console.

**A popping sound after the power is turned on**: the input power is incorrect and the varistor on the controller has blown. Check the power is 220V and replace the controller.
