The diagnosis guide's entry, for a computer that **shuts off when the console is touched (on a cold day) while walking or running**:

> **Treadmill may not be grounded. Static electricity is "crashing" the computer.** Refer to Grounding Instructions on page 3.

Check the grounding before replacing any part. See `f63-2026-grounding-requirements`.

This is a **crash caused from outside the machine**, not a component fault. It shows up on cold, dry days when static builds up on the user.
