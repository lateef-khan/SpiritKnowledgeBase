**The codes on this machine look alike and mean different things. E3 is the incline and calibration error. E01, E02, E03, E04, E05 and E06 are two-digit controller codes and are not E1 to E6. E22 is communication and E31 is overheat.**

**This is E22. It is not E02 (Hall sensor) and it is not E2.**

**Defect reason**: communication error between the upper controller and the lower controller.

1. Check whether the **console cable and controller connection** is loose or disconnected.
2. Check whether the **controller** is damaged.
3. Check whether the **console** is defective.
4. **Power off for 2 minutes, then restart.**
