The diagnosis guide treats "tread-belt does not stay centered" and "treadmill belt hesitates when walked/run on" as **one entry with one answer**:

> The user may be walking while favoring or putting more weight on either the left or right foot. If this walking pattern is natural, **track the belt slightly off-center to the side opposite from the belt movement**. See General Maintenance section on Tread-belt Tension. Adjust as necessary.

**The cross-reference in that entry points at the wrong section.** It sends you to **Tread-belt Tension**, but tracking the belt off-centre is the **Tread-belt Tracking Adjustment** procedure. Both are on page 26.

- To move the belt sideways: `f63-2026-running-belt-tracking`.
- To stop it slipping or hesitating under load: `f63-2026-running-belt-tension`, and check lubrication.

The service manual matrix records the same two symptoms with different causes - uneven tension across the belt for off-centre running, and insufficient lubricant or insufficient tension for hesitating. See `f63-2026-noises`.
