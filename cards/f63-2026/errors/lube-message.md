**LUBE is not a fault code.** It is a **reminder to check lubrication under the walking deck**, raised after 90 hours of use.

1. **Check lubrication under the walking belt.** The checklist points at the belt/deck lubrication procedure; see `f63-2026-deck-lubrication`.
2. **Power the treadmill three times to reset the lube message.**

The engineering mode menu also has a **Lube Message Reset** item; see `f63-2026-engineering-mode`.

**LUBE is not in the service manual's error code table.** The codes on `f63-2026-error-code-list` are E3, E01 to E06, E22 and E31; this message appears only in the owner's manual diagnosis guide.

**A page reference in this manual is wrong.** The diagnosis guide says "Reference belt deck lubrication procedure on page 28." The lubrication procedure is printed on **page 27** in this copy; page 28 is the second Service Checklist.
