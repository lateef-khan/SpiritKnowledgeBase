**The treadmill has its own circuit breaker on the front grill.** If it has tripped, **push the circuit breaker in until it locks.**

**When the display does not light**, the diagnosis guide lists four causes in order:

1. **Circuit breaker on front grill tripped.** Push it in until it locks.
2. **Plug is disconnected.** Make sure the plug is firmly pushed into the AC household wall outlet.
3. **Household circuit breaker may be tripped.**
4. **Treadmill defect.** Contact your SOLE dealer.

**When the treadmill trips its own on-board 15 amp circuit**, the guide gives one cause: **high belt/deck friction**. See the lubrication procedure at `f63-2026-deck-lubrication`.

The service manual card `f63-2026-no-display-with-safety-key` gives a different set of causes for a dead display - the AC switch, safety key position, computer cable, safety key device and console. **Neither list mentions the other. Work through both.**

A house breaker that trips while this one holds is a different problem; see `f63-2026-house-breaker-trips`.
