**The codes on this machine look alike and mean different things. E3 is the incline and calibration error. E01, E02, E03, E04, E05 and E06 are two-digit controller codes and are not E1 to E6. E22 is communication and E31 is overheat.**

**This is E31. It is not E3 (incline) and it is not E03 (hardware overcurrent).**

**Defect reason**: overheat.

1. **Wait until the temperature returns to normal before operating the machine again.**

That single step is all the manual gives for this code. There is no part to replace.
