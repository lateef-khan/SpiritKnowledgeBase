**The codes on this machine look alike and mean different things. E3 is the incline and calibration error. E01, E02, E03, E04, E05 and E06 are two-digit controller codes and are not E1 to E6. E22 is communication and E31 is overheat.**

**This is E04, two digits. It is not E4.**

**Defect reason**: phase loss.

1. Check whether the **motor cable** is properly connected; reconnect it.
2. Replace the motor.
3. Replace the controller.

The brushless drive motor uses three phases at the controller: **U (red), V (black) and W (white)**. A phase loss means one of those three is not reaching the motor.
