**The Pulse (Heart Rate) window displays your current heart rate in beats per minute during the workout.**

- **You must wear the wireless Bluetooth chest strap to show your pulse.** The pulse value displays any time the upper display is receiving a pulse signal.
- The equipment's Bluetooth device **can also be connected to Bluetooth wearables**. The wearables can receive signals **even when the App is already in use**.
- **Wear the Bluetooth wearable first, then start the connection.** While connected, the console shows the heart rate value via Bluetooth.

Heart rate programs require this: they are **intended for wireless Bluetooth wearables only**, and you must receive a strong, steady value in the heart rate window before using one. See `f63-2026-heart-rate-programs`.

**This owner's manual describes only a wireless strap or wearable.** The service manual's troubleshooting matrix still carries hand pulse grip rows; that half is on `f63-2026-hand-pulse-and-chest-belt`.
