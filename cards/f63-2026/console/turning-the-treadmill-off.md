1. **The display turns itself off, going into sleep, after 15 minutes of inactivity when in the Stop mode.** Almost all power for the treadmill is off except for some circuits needed to detect a key press, so the console can wake up. **Asleep, the treadmill draws very little power - about as much as a television turned off.**
2. **Removing the safety key is similar to entering sleep mode.**
3. **Turning off the main power switch in the front of the treadmill will completely remove any power.**

To disconnect for cleaning or service, that is not enough - see `f63-2026-how-to-disconnect-power`.

The 15 minute timer can be turned off; see `f63-2026-sleep-mode-settings`.
