The console counts a **1/4 mile track**.

**The number of laps you complete is accumulated in the lap counter, which is viewed in the LAPS window.**

The service manual gives the counter's ranges: the LAPS readout displays 0 to 999 with a working range of 0 to 99. See `f63-2026-console-display-ranges`.
