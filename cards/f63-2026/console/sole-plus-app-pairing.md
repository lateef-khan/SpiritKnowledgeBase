The machine comes with a **Bluetooth transceiver** that lets it interact with compatible iOS and Android smartphones or tablets through the **SOLE+ App**.

1. Download the free **SOLE+ App** from the Apple Store or Google Play.
2. **Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your device.**
3. **Tap the icon at the top right corner** of the app to search for Sole equipment.
4. **After the equipment is detected, tap Connect.**
5. When the app and equipment are synced, **the Bluetooth icon on the equipment's console will light up.**

The app can sync workout data with **Apple Health, Google Health Connect, Strava or MapMyFitness**, with more to come.

Music playback and heart rate wearables use separate Bluetooth connections; see `f63-2026-bluetooth-speaker-pairing` and `f63-2026-bluetooth-wearable-heart-rate`.
