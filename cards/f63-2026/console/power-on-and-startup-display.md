Power the treadmill on by plugging it into an appropriate wall outlet, then turn on the **power switch located at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood**. **Ensure the safety key is installed - the treadmill will not power on without it.**

When the power is turned on:

1. The **speed window displays the current version of the software.**
2. The **time and distance windows then display odometer readings for a short time.** The time window shows **how many hours the treadmill has been in use**; the distance window shows **how many miles** (or kilometres if the treadmill is set to metric readings) the treadmill has gone.
3. The treadmill then enters **idle mode**, which is the starting point for operation.

The odometer can be reset from the engineering mode menu; see `f63-2026-engineering-mode`.
