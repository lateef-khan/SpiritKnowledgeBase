**Do not use excessive pressure on the console control keys.** They are precision set to function properly with little finger pressure.

**Pushing harder is not going to make the unit go faster or slower.**

**If you feel the buttons are not functioning properly with normal pressure, contact your SOLE dealer.**

Two other things make keys appear dead and are not a fault:

- Some keys have no function in the mode you are in. See `f63-2026-console-buttons-ready-mode`.
- The **child lock** disables the keypad until it is unlocked. See `f63-2026-child-lock`.
