**Security** is item 4 in the engineering mode menu. It **allows you to lock the keypad so no unauthorized use of the machine is allowed.**

**When the child lock is enabled, the console will not allow the keypad to operate unless you press and hold the Start and Display/Enter buttons for 3 seconds to unlock the console.**

To reach the setting, enter engineering mode first; see `f63-2026-engineering-mode`.

A locked keypad looks the same as a dead keypad. Before replacing anything, try the 3 second unlock. Removing the safety key is the other way to stop unauthorized use; see `f63-2026-who-can-use-the-treadmill`.
