The console has built-in maintenance and diagnostic software. It can change console settings such as mile to metric, and turn off the beep when a key is pressed.

**To enter:** press and hold down the **Start, Stop and Display/Enter** keys. **Re-power the machine or insert the safety key**, and the **time window will display "F2"**.

Use the **speed knob button** to make a selection and press **Display/Enter** to access the menu:

1. **Key Test** - test all the keys to make sure they are functioning.
2. **Display Test** - tests all the display functions.
3. **Functions** - press Display/Enter to access the settings, use the speed rotation button to select:
   - **Sleep Mode** - turn on to have the console power down automatically after 15 minutes of inactivity.
   - **Pause Mode** - turn on to allow 5 minutes of pause, turn off to have the console pause indefinitely.
   - **Lube Message Reset** - resets the lube message.
   - **Odometer Reset** - resets the odometer readings.
   - **Units** - sets the display to read out in Mile E or Metric H measurements.
   - **Beep** - turns off the speaker so no beeping sound is heard.
4. **Security** - locks the keypad. See `f63-2026-child-lock`.
5. **Exit**

**Engineering mode is not the calibration mode.** Calibration is entered with Start and Speed 3 and shows "F1"; see `f63-2026-calibration-values`.

The printed menu item reads "Lube Messagr Reset" - a typo in the manual for Lube Message Reset.
