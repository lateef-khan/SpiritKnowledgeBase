The console ships with a **Sleep mode** setting that has three choices:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **ON** | The unit goes into an energy-saving state after **15 minutes** of inactivity. |
| **OFF** | The console stays lit while the power is on. |
| **RETAIL** | The console runs the **demo video after 3 minutes** of inactivity. |

**How to change it depends on the display type:**

- **LED displays**: hold the **Stop and Display** keys for **5 seconds** to switch between ON and OFF.
- **LCD displays**: press and hold **Stop, Enter and Display** for **5 seconds** to switch between ON and OFF.
- **TFT displays**: click **"Sleep Mode" under Settings** to switch among the three settings.

**Only the TFT method reaches RETAIL.** On LED and LCD the shortcut switches between ON and OFF only. This machine has a white LED display.

The engineering mode menu also carries a **Sleep Mode** item; see `f63-2026-engineering-mode`.
