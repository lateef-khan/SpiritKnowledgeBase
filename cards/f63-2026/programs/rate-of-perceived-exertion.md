The **rate of perceived exertion (RPE)**, also known as the **Borg scale**, was developed by Swedish physiologist **G.A.V. Borg**. It rates exercise intensity **from 6 to 20** depending on how you feel, or the perception of your effort.

| Rating | Perception of effort |
|---|---|
| 6 | Minimal |
| 7 | Very, very light |
| 8 | Very, very light + |
| 9 | Very light |
| 10 | Very light + |
| 11 | Fairly light |
| 12 | Comfortable |
| 13 | Somewhat hard |
| 14 | Somewhat hard + |
| 15 | Hard |
| 16 | Hard + |
| 17 | Very hard |
| 18 | Very hard + |
| 19 | Very, very hard |
| 20 | Maximal |

**You can get an approximate heart rate level for each rating by adding a zero to the rating.** For example, a rating of 12 gives an approximate heart rate of **120 beats per minute**.

Your RPE varies with your stress level, physical and emotional health, temperature, humidity, time of day, and when and what you last ate. That variation is the point: when you are strong and rested your pace feels easier and you can train harder; when you are tired your pace feels harder and you train at the level right for that day.
