**CAUTION: heart rate programs are intended for wireless Bluetooth wearables only.** You must receive a strong, steady value in the heart rate window before using one. See `f63-2026-bluetooth-wearable-heart-rate`.

The console has two of them, shown as **H1** and **H2**:

| Program | Default level | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **H1** | **60%** of your projected heart rate maximum | Individuals with **fat loss** as a goal |
| **H2** | **80%** of your projected heart rate maximum | Individuals with a goal of improving **cardiovascular and lung function** |

1. Select the program via the **Program keys**, then press **Display/Enter**.
2. The incline window blinks, showing the user's **age**. Adjust and press Display/Enter, or press Display/Enter to accept the default. **Adjusting age changes the Target HR value.**
3. The incline window blinks, showing the default **body weight** for this program. Adjust and press Display/Enter, or accept the default.
4. **Adjust the target percentage.** The printed default is "60% (H1); (220 - age x 0.60); or 80% (H2); (220 - age x 0.80)". Adjust with the speed knob key and press Display/Enter.
5. The **time window** blinks. Adjust the time and press Display/Enter.
6. **Press Start** to begin. To change a setting, press **Stop to go back one level or screen.**
7. **To increase or decrease speed at any time during the program, press the speed knob.**
8. When the program ends the **message window shows a summary** of your workout, then the console returns to the start-up display.

The program then **steadily increases your heart rate to the target value. This is automatic and could take upwards of five minutes**, depending on how far your heart rate has to go and your physical condition. People who are more fit take longer to reach the target.

**The formula on this page is printed ambiguously.** "(220 - age x 0.60)" reads as one thing arithmetically; page 21 spells out the intended order - subtract your age from 220 first, then multiply. See `f63-2026-target-heart-rate-zone`.
