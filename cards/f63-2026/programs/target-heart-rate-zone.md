**Maximum Heart Rate (MHR) = 220 minus your age.**

- **Your heart rate training zone is 50% to 90% of your maximum heart rate.**
- **60% of your MHR is the zone that burns fat.**
- **80% is for strengthening the cardiovascular system.**
- **60% to 80% is the zone to stay in for maximum benefit.**

**Worked example for a 40 year old, exactly as printed:**

```
220 - 40 = 180 (maximum heart rate)
180 x 0.6 = 108 beats per minute (60% of maximum)
180 X 0.8 = 144 beats per minute (80% of maximum)
```

So for a 40 year old the training zone would be **108 to 144 beats per minute**.

**If you enter your age during programming, the console performs this calculation automatically.** Entering your age is used for the heart rate programs.

**Consult your physician before participating in any exercise program.**
