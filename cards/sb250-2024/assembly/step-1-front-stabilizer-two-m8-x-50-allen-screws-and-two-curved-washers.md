Hardware step 1: **2 Allen screws (#21) M8*50, 2 curved washers (#15)**.

1. Attach the front stabilizer (2) to the main frame (1), securing with two Allen screws (21) and two curved washers (15).

No spring washers on this bike; its 2018 predecessor used two M8*75 screws with spring washers (the linked card).
