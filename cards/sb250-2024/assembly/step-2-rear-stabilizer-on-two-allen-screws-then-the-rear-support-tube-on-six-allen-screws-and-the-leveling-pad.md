Hardware step 2: **2 Allen screws (#21) M8*50, 2 curved washers (#15), 6 Allen screws (#14) M8*16, 6 flat washers (#16), 1 leveling pad (#25)**.

1. Attach the rear stabilizer (3) to the rear support tube (7). Secure using two Allen screws (21) and two curved washers (15).
2. Connect the middle extension hand pulse wire (17) to the rear extension hand pulse wire (22).
3. Attach the rear support tube (7) to the main frame (1). Secure using six Allen screws (14) and six flat washers (16).
4. **Attach the leveling pad (25) to the bottom of the main frame (1).**

"Tip: Avoid pinching the Extension Wires."

The 2018 predecessor bolted its rear stabilizer on with M10 carriage bolts and domed nuts; this book uses Allen screws throughout.
