Hardware step 3: **2 curved washers (#15), 8 Allen screws (#14) M8*16, 2 flat washers (#16)**; pre-assembled on the back of the computer, 4 screws (12).

1. **Insert the front post (4) into the front post cover (8)** - the cover has to be on the post before the post goes into the frame.
2. Connect the extension computer wire (9) to the lower computer wire (18).
3. Connect the front extension hand pulse wire (10) to the middle extension hand pulse wire (17).
4. Attach the front post (4) to the main frame (1). **Secure the back of the post with two Allen screws (14) and two curved washers (15). Secure the side of the post with two Allen screws (14) and two flat washers (16).** *Tip: avoid pinching the extension wire and pulse wires.*
5. Slide the cover down the front post onto the main frame.
6. Attach the left handlebar (5L) to the left side of the front post (4). Secure using two Allen screws (14). Repeat for the right handlebar (5R).
7. Connect the extension computer wire (9) to the wire from the computer (6).
8. Connect the front extension hand pulse wire (10) to the wires from the computer (6).
9. Attach the computer (6) to the top bracket of the front post (4). Secure using four screws (12) which are pre-assembled on the back of the computer (6).
10. *Tip: avoid pinching the extension wire and pulse wires.*

The bottle holder goes on in step 4 on this bike, with two self-tapping screws pre-installed on the post.
