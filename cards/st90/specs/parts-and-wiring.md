The ST90 uses a **2HP inverter** (part 31, D110159) instead of a DC motor controller.

**Computer cables**: part 60-24 connects to 45-1, which connects to 45, which connects to part 31, the controller.

| Item | Part | Number |
|---|---|---|
| 60-24 | 900 mm computer cable (upper) | E020305-01 |
| 45-1 | 1500 mm computer cable (middle) | E022415-01 |
| 45 | 1500 mm computer cable | E022415 |
| 31 | 2HP inverter | D110159 |

**Speed sensor**: parts 23 and 24 run to the inverter.

| Item | Part | Number |
|---|---|---|
| 23 | Light sensor | D021224 |
| 24 | 100 mm connecting wire | E070612 |

**Power path**: part 35 to 36 and 37, then to the control board or filter. Parts 33 and 34 run from the filter to the inverter.

| Item | Part | Number |
|---|---|---|
| 35 | AC electronic module | F030073 |
| 36 | 1350 mm connecting wire (white) | E011916 |
| 37 | 1350 mm connecting wire (black) | E011917 |
| 32 | Filter | F060401 |
| 33 | 450 mm connecting wire (white) | E011901-01 |
| 34 | 450 mm connecting wire (black) | E011902-02 |

**Deck and belt**

| Item | Part | Number | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | Bearing seat | B060108A-Q7 | Sits on top of the frame, at the top of the running deck. The belt rolls over it |
| 7 | Crawler-type belt | N013010B | The belt itself. The slats sit on this |
| 8 | Pedal | M050032 | The slats |

**Handlebar buttons**

Right side is the speed level adjustment button. Left side is the incline adjustment button.

| Item | Part | Number |
|---|---|---|
| 58-1 | Key fast board outer cover (speed) | PP160008-A1-ED |
| 59-1 | Key fast board outer cover cap (speed) | PP280044-A1-E9 |
| 73 | 550 mm speed cable (blue) | E050053-01 |
| 58 | Key fast board outer cover (incline) | PP160008-A1-EC |
| 59 | Key fast board outer cover cap (incline) | PP280044-A1-EA |
| 74 | 900 mm resistance connecting cable | E050111-01 |
| 57 | Key fast board inner cover | PP160007-A1 |
