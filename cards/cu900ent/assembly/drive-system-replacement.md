**6.7.1** Remove the crank, left and right riser chain cover and left and right chain cover - please
refer to **6.3, 6.4, 6.5**. As shown in Figure 6.7.1, Figure 6.7.2, remove the **nut and hook screw**,
you can remove the **spring**.

**6.7.2** Draw the belt as shown in Figure 6.7.3, Figure 6.7.4. **(The belt is maintained in the
middle of the belt during installation, the belt tension is maintained at 180 to 210 N.)**

**6.7.3** As shown in Figure 6.7.5, loosen the **three screws** and remove the **idler**. **(The need
to install the idler counterclockwise to the end of the lock.)**

**6.7.4** As shown in Figure 6.7.6, 6.7.7, the **flywheel 4 screws** can be removed to remove the
flywheel.

**6.7.5** As shown in Figure 6.7.8, loosen the **five-way nut** and remove the **five-way turntable**.
**(Need to align the keyway in the installation, the five-nut on the lock.)**

**6.7.6** As shown in Figure 6.7.9, loosen the **reed switch screw** and remove it. **(Installation
with the belt on the magnet with a distance of 1mm.)**

**6.7.7** Please refer to 6.7.1 ~ 6.7.6 for the installation of transmission system parts.

**Belt tension is 180 to 210 N** - a force in newtons, not a frequency. Do not use a sonic belt
tension meter reading from another Spirit machine here: the CE800ENT elliptical prints
`185Khz~210Khz` and the CVC800 climber prints `190Hz+/-10Hz`. The numbers 180-210 and 185-210 look
alike and are not the same measurement.

**Reed switch to magnet gap: 1mm.** Set it on reassembly or the bike loses RPM.

**The five-way turntable is keyed.** Align the keyway before the five-way nut goes on.

The idler is installed **counterclockwise to the end of the lock**.

**Three books, one procedure and the same two figures.** The CU900 (2018) manual prints this as 6.7 and the CU1000ENT (2023) manual as 12.18: belt tension **180 to 210 N**, reed switch to magnet **1 mm**, idler counterclockwise to the end of the lock, keyed five-way turntable. The recumbent CR900 and CR900ENT print **220 to 240 N** and **2 mm** for what looks like the same drive; do not use those here.
