**6.5.1** Loose **seat riser cover 3 screws** as shown in Figure 6.5.1, Figure 6.5.2.

**6.5.2** Figure 6.5.3, Figure 6.5.4, Figure 6.5.5: the **left chain cover 7 self-tapping screws,
3 screws** removed, you can remove the left chain cover; the **right chain cover 3 screws** removed,
you can remove right chain cover. **(Please refer to 6.3 for removing crank.)**

**6.5.3** Please refer to 6.5.1, 6.5.2 for the left and right chain cover assembly.

**The two covers do not use the same number of screws.**

| Cover | Fasteners |
|---|---|
| Seat riser cover | 3 screws |
| Left chain cover | 7 self-tapping screws **plus** 3 screws |
| Right chain cover | 3 screws |

**The crank has to come off first** - 6.5.2 refers to 6.3. No screw sizes are printed.

If the belt slips or comes off, section 7.1.1 sends the technician to 6.5 and 6.7.1.
