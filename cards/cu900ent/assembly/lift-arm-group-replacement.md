**6.9.1** Set the seat adjustment group to the **highest** as shown in Figure 6.9.1.

**6.9.2** Remove the **carriage cable** as shown in Figure 6.9.2, 6.9.3, 6.9.4.

**6.9.3** As shown in Figure 6.9.5 ~ 6.9.9, will be on the handle of the **two screws**, you can
remove the **cable, handle**.

**6.9.4** As shown in Figure 6.9.10, 6.9.11, 6.9.12, 6.9.13, remove the **screw and nut from the
lifting arm** to remove the **seat riser**.

**6.9.5** As shown in Figure 6.9.14, 6.1.15, remove the **cap on the lift arm**, you can remove the
lift arm.

**6.9.6** Install the seat riser group - refer to 6.9.1 ~ 6.9.5.

**Step 6.9.1 is not optional.** The seat has to be at its **highest** setting before anything comes
apart.

**Figure reference defect:** 6.9.5 prints **"6.1.15"**. The figure printed on p. 60 is captioned
**Figure 6.9.15**. There is no figure 6.1.15 in this manual.

No screw sizes are printed. The only count is the **two screws** on the handle in 6.9.3.
