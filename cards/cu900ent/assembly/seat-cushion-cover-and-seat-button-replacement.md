**6.8.1** As shown in Figure 6.8.1, Figure 6.8.2, remove the **2 screws** from the front and rear
seats.

**6.8.2** As shown in Figure 6.8.3, Figure 6.8.4, remove the **four screws** and remove the cushion.

**6.8.3** Please refer to 6.8.2 for the cushion assembly.

**6.8.4** Please refer to 6.8.1 for the front and rear seat cover assembly.

Two fastener sets: **2 screws** hold the front and rear seat covers, **4 screws** hold the cushion.
No sizes are printed.

The heading is printed `Chair Cushions Cover、Seat Button Replacement` with an ideographic comma. The
call-out table on p. 42 names the two covers **Front Seat Cover (17)** and **Rear Seat Cover (18)**.

**The seat button is named in the heading and nowhere in the steps.** The four printed steps cover
the covers and the cushion only. If a customer needs the seat button itself, this manual does not
document it.

**Three books, one procedure.** The CU900 (2018) manual prints this as 6.8 and the CU1000ENT (2023) manual as 12.19, with the same two-then-four screws, the same ideographic comma in the heading, and no seat-button step in either.
