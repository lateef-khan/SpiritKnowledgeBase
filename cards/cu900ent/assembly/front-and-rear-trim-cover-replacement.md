**6.6.1** As shown in Figure 6.6.1, Figure 6.6.2, remove the **two screws** from the front and rear
trims and remove them.

**6.6.2** Before and after the decoration cover please refer to 6.6.1 can.
*(Printed as it stands. It means: refit the front and rear trim covers by reversing 6.6.1.)*

**Two screws.** The manual does not say whether that is two per cover or two in total, and it prints
no screw size.

**These are the front and rear covers, not the left and right ones.** The left and right trim covers
are 6.4 and use four self-tapping screws.
