**6.11.1** As shown in Figure 6.11.1, 6.11.2, loosen the **bottom 3 screws** and remove it.

That is the whole printed procedure - **one step, no reassembly step, no screw size**.

The rear stabilizer cover is part **9** on the call-out drawing on p. 42; the front stabilizer cover
is part **8**.
