**6.10.1** As shown in Figure 6.10.1, remove the **four screws from the control panel** and **release
the power** to remove the replacement.

That is the whole printed procedure - **one step, and no reassembly step**.

**"Release the power" is the safety step.** Disconnect power before the panel comes out.

No screw size, no torque, and no wiring detail is printed. The console's own error codes are covered
by the error cards, not here.
