**6.1.1** As shown in Figure 6.1.1, remove the **2 screws of the chin cover** and remove it.

**6.1.2** As shown in Figure 6.1.2 and Figure 6.1.3, remove the **four screws from the rear of the
electronic watch** and remove the **wire connector** to remove the electronic watch.

**6.1.3** Electronic table assembly in accordance with 6.1.1 and 6.1.2, you can replace.

**"Electronic watch" and "electronic table" both mean the console.** This manual's translation uses
those words throughout.

**No screw sizes are printed for either step** - only the counts, 2 and 4. That is true of every
procedure in this manual's Part Replacement Guide except the M8 cap in 6.2 and the torque figure in
6.3.

The chin cover comes off first, and 6.2 sends you back here for it.

**The CU900 (2018) service manual prints this section word for word** (two chin-cover screws, four behind the console, the wire connector), so this card covers both the CU900 and the CU900ENT. The CU1000ENT book prints **four** chin-cover screws and no second set; that machine has its own console card.
