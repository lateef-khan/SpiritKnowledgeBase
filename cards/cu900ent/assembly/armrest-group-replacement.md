**6.2.1** Refer to **Figure 6.1.1** for the removal of the **chin cover**. As shown in Figure 6.2.1,
remove the **M8 cap** and remove the armrest to remove the armrest group **(need to hold the armrest
to avoid danger)**.

**6.2.2** Please refer to 6.2.1 for armrest assembly.

**Hold the armrest before the M8 cap comes off.** The manual's own warning - the armrest is not
supported once the cap is out.

The chin cover from the console procedure (6.1.1) has to come off first.

If the front armrest moves during use, section 7.3.1 sends the technician back to this procedure to
lock the screw.
