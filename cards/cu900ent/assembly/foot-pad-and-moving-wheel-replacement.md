**6.12.1** As shown in Figure 6.12.1, loosen the **screw and cap** and remove the wheel.

**6.12.2** As shown in Figure 6.12.2, move the wheel **counterclockwise**. **(Adjust the height of
the four feet when not flat until the balance.)**

**Levelling is 6.12.2, and it is the fix for a rocking machine.** Section 7.3.3 sends a technician
here when the whole product shakes during use: adjust the height of the **four feet** until the bike
sits flat.

No screw size is printed. 6.12.1 is one step and there is no reassembly step.
