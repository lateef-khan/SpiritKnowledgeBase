**6.3.1** As shown in Figure 6.3.1, 6.3.2, remove the **right pedal counterclockwise** and remove the
**left pedal clockwise**.

**6.3.2** Remove the **crank screw** as shown in Figure 6.3.3. **(About the same, please use 60 N-m
when locking.)**

**6.3.3** As shown in Figure 6.3.4, use the **puller** to exit the crank. **(Left and right sides are
the same.)**

**6.3.4** Please refer to 6.3.1 ~ 6.3.3 for crank and pedal assembly.

**The pedals turn opposite ways.** Right pedal off **counterclockwise**, left pedal off
**clockwise**. The left pedal is left-hand threaded; forcing it the usual way strips the crank.

**Crank screw torque: 60 N-m.** That is the only torque figure printed anywhere in this manual.

A **crank puller** is needed for 6.3.3. The manual does not name a size.

**Three books, one procedure.** The CU900 (2018) service manual prints this as 6.3 and the CU1000ENT (2023) manual as 12.14, both word for word - pedal directions, the puller, and the same 60 N-m crank screw figure, which is also the only torque printed in each of those books.
