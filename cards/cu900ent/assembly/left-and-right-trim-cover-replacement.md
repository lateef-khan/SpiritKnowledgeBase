**6.4.1** As shown in Figure 6.4.1, Figure 6.4.2, remove the **four self-tapping screws** from the
left-hand cover to remove the left and right standings.

That is the entire printed procedure - **there is no 6.4.2 and no reassembly step**. Every other
procedure in this guide prints one.

The wording is the manual's: it names the **left-hand cover** in a step that removes **left and
right**. It gives no screw size, and it does not say whether the four screws are per side or in
total.

The drive system procedure (6.7.1) sends the technician here before the belt can be reached.
