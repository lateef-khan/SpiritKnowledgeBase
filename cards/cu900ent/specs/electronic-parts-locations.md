**Upper controllers (p. 8)** - **Cooling FAN** and **DISPLAY**, both in the console head.

**Lower controller and driver (p. 9)** - **Speed sensor**, inside the side case beside the
flywheel; **Hybrid generator**, the flywheel with its coil pack; **Driver board**, low down
inside the frame.

The same assembly is called the **Induction Brake** on the outline drawing (p. 6), the
**Hybrid Generator** here, and the **Brake flywheel** on the block diagram (p. 16). All three
names are for the one part.

**Three more books name exactly these five parts under the same two headings.** The CU900 (SU8800-SB008)
book prints the same photographs on the same upright; the CR900 (SR8800-SB008) book prints the same three
lower-part photographs on a recumbent, with leader lines that land on the seat and the console mast rather
than the shroud - take the locations from this card, not from that page; and the CR900ENT book names the
same five on its recumbent with a touchscreen in the DISPLAY photograph and a different driver-board
photograph. None of the four prints a part number or a rating on these pages. The CU1000ENT drops the fan
and adds an NFC key (`cu1000ent-2023-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`); the residential XBR95 of 2016
names the same generator and driver board plus a speaker (`xbr95-2016-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`).
