**Upper controllers (p. 8)** - **Cooling FAN** and **DISPLAY**, both in the console head.

**Lower controller and driver (p. 9)** - **Speed sensor**, inside the side case beside the
flywheel; **Hybrid generator**, the flywheel with its coil pack; **Driver board**, low down
inside the frame.

The same assembly is called the **Induction Brake** on the outline drawing (p. 6), the
**Hybrid Generator** here, and the **Brake flywheel** on the block diagram (p. 16). All three
names are for the one part.
