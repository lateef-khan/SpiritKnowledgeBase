**180 to 210 N**, and the belt must sit **in the middle** of the pulley as you fit it.

That is a **force in newtons**, not a frequency: the bike's belt is tensioned by the spring that
comes off with the nut and hook screw in step 6.7.1, and the manual names no sonic meter anywhere.

**This is not the 185-210 figure in the CE800ENT elliptical manual**, which is a sonic-meter
frequency despite reading almost the same, and it is not the CVC800 climber's 190 Hz plus or
minus 10. Three different machines, three different measurements, taken on three different
adjusters.

The manual prints this figure once, in brackets, inside the drive system replacement step. It
gives no tolerance beyond the range and no gauge part number.

**The 2018 CU900 and the CU1000ENT print the same bracketed sentence**, 180 to 210 N with the belt
held in the middle, in their own drive-system steps. The recumbent CR900 and CR900ENT are set
higher, 220 to 240 N (`spirit-cr900-specs-drive-belt-tension-220-240-newtons`).
