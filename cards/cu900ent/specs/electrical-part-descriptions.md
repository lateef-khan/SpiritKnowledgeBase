- **Console** - key controls and a **TFT display**.
- **Main controller** - the **DC** power supply for the console, plus the driver control circuit.
- **EMS flywheel** - increases and decreases the resistance level of the brake.

The console entry as printed reads "Interface that controls all functions of the **Elliptical**",
copied from the elliptical manual. The unit block diagram on p. 16 is titled "Elliptical
Configuration" for the same reason.

The section prints **no voltages**. The supply figures are on the circuit diagram instead: the
machine runs from an external AC adapter delivering **DC 24 V**.
