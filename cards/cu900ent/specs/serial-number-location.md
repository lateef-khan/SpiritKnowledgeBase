Two photographs, no text. They show:

1. A **SPIRIT rating label** on the rear face of the **console mast cover**, just above where the
   mast meets the front stabilizer. Beside it on the stabilizer cover is a small
   **MADE IN TAIWAN / U.S. Pat. See www.spiritfitness.com/legal** label.
2. A **barcode label** on the top of the **front stabilizer**, at the right-hand end.

The photographs are too low-resolution at 300 dpi to read the numbers printed on either label,
and the manual prints no transcription and no serial-number format.
