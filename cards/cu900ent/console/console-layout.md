The only call-out on the page is **10.1" TFT Display**.

The photograph shows a single row of physical keys below the screen, left to right:

| Key | Marking |
|---|---|
| 1 | **FAN** |
| 2 | **START** |
| 3 | **LEVEL -** |
| 4 | **LEVEL +** |
| 5 | **STOP** |
| 6 | **ENTER** |

A **headphone socket** sits at the right-hand end of that row, and a grille runs across the top of
the console housing.

**The facing page, headed "Function Locations", is not a console drawing.** It is a photograph of the
back of the console I/O board showing an RJ45 socket, an HDMI socket and the wiring connectors, with
no readable call-out labels at 300 dpi. Treat it as a connector map, not as console layout.
