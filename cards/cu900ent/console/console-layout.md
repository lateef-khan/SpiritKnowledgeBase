The only call-out on the page is **10.1" TFT Display**.

The photograph shows a single row of physical keys below the screen, left to right:

| Key | Marking |
|---|---|
| 1 | **FAN** |
| 2 | **START** |
| 3 | **LEVEL -** |
| 4 | **LEVEL +** |
| 5 | **STOP** |
| 6 | **ENTER** |

A **headphone socket** sits at the right-hand end of that row, and a grille runs across the top of
the console housing.

**The facing page, headed "Function Locations", is not a console drawing.** It is a photograph of the
back of the console I/O board showing an RJ45 socket, an HDMI socket and the wiring connectors, with
no readable call-out labels at 300 dpi. Treat it as a connector map, not as console layout.

**The owner's manuals name four things the service manual does not.** The CU900ENT owner's manual
(p. 13) and the CR900ENT-2021 owner's manual (p. 15) print the same call-out drawing, so this card
covers both machines:

- **Integrated adjustable fan** for workout comfort
- **10.1" touchscreen display** with multiple viewing modes
- **Tablet friendly reading rack holder**
- **Accessory tray** for secure placement of keys, smartphones, etc.

They also describe the keypad in words, under **FUNCTIONS OF THIS BIKE**: *"On the lower portion of
the console there is the Start button to begin the workout, Stop button to pause/stop programs,
Level button to change workload and a Fan button to turn the fan on or off."* That agrees with the
service manual's photograph - FAN, START, LEVEL -, LEVEL +, STOP, ENTER - except that neither owner's
manual mentions the **ENTER** key or the **headphone socket**, and the service manual never mentions
the fan, the reading rack or the accessory tray.

**Nothing anywhere gives a fan speed for this console.** The Fan button is described only as on or
off.
