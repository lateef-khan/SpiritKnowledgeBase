Check **regularly** whether the pedal is loose. The two pedals tighten in opposite directions:

- **Left pedal — tighten counterclockwise.**
- **Right pedal — tighten clockwise.**

The manual's wording is "lock the left pedal counterclockwise, and press the right pedal
clockwise". The curved arrows in figures 3.1.1 and 3.1.2 confirm the two directions.

A loose pedal is one of the two causes this manual gives for a squeak, thump, click or rough
feeling — see `cu900ent-general-maintenance`. It is also the first thing to check for a bad
"feet feeling" while pedalling; if the pedal is tight and the feeling persists, check the crank —
see `cu900ent-noise-and-feet-feeling`.

## Three uprights say "regularly"; the two recumbents say "every week"

The **CU900 2018** and **CU1000ENT 2023** service manuals print this check word for word —
"regularly", left counterclockwise, right clockwise — as sections 3.1 and 11.11. The **CR900 2018**
and **CR900ENT 2021** books print the same two directions but name an interval: check the pedals
**every week** (`spirit-cr900-maintenance-weekly-pedal-check`). Quote the interval from the book
for the machine; this card's three uprights have none.

## The 2025 8.0U and 8.5R say "regularly" too, and add the crank arm nuts

Both SB036 service manuals open their preventive chapter with the same check - **"check regularly if Pedals are
loose. Tighten left Pedal in counterclockwise direction and tighten right Pedal in clockwise direction"** (figures
3.1.1, 3.1.2) - and then a second sentence the CU900 books do not print: **"check regularly if Crank Arms are
loose. Tighten up the nut of Crank Arms on both sides"** (figures 3.1.3, 3.1.4). The crank nut's torque is in
their replacement step, 60 N·m. Their owner's manuals put the pedal check at **monthly** in a schedule table
(`spirit-med-bike-maintenance-machine-care-schedule-after-each-use-to-quarterly`); the service manuals give no
interval beyond "regularly". A rough feeling underfoot sends you back to this check
(`cu900ent-noise-and-feet-feeling`).

The 2025 7.0R and 7.0U print the two directions with no "regularly" at all
(`spirit-med-bike-maintenance-preventative-check-pedals-handlebar-and-seat-cushion`).
