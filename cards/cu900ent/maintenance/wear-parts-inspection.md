**WARNING** — as printed: "The effect that the safety level of the equipment can be maintained
only if it is examined regularly for damage and wear."

1. **Replace defective components immediately** and/or keep the equipment out of use until repair.
2. The components which are most susceptible to wear: **Belt, PU wheel, Bearing, Idler.**

## This list is shorter than the Spirit company-wide one

The same warning appears on the Spirit sanitizing guidance with a **six**-item list — Belt, PU
wheel, Bearing, Idler, **Shaft, Pedal** (`ct900-sanitizing-equipment`). The CU900ENT service
manual prints only the four items above. Neither list is a superset error to be smoothed over:
this card records what the CU900ENT manual actually prints.

The manual gives no inspection interval and no wear limit for any of the four parts.

None of the other five Spirit commercial service manuals in this import (CE800ENT, CRW800H2O,
CT850 2016, CT850 2020, CVC800) prints this warning at all.

## This is the only safety content in the CU900ENT manual

The CU900ENT service manual has no Product Safety Instructions chapter, no important electrical
instructions and no important grounding instructions. This WARNING is all of it. The manual prints
the wear list with ideographic commas (`Belt、PU wheel、Bearing、Idler`); they are transcribed as
ordinary commas above.
