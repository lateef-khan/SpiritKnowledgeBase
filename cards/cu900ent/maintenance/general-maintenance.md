1. **Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each workout.**
2. If a **squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling** develops, the main cause is most likely one
   of two things:
   1. **The hardware was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.** All bolts installed during
      assembly need to be tightened as much as possible. It may be necessary to use a larger
      wrench than the one provided if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently. The manual is
      emphatic here: **90% of calls to the service department for noise issues can be traced to
      loose hardware.**
   2. **The crank arm nut and/or the pedals need to be retightened** — see
      `cu900ent-check-pedals`.
3. If squeaks or other noises persist, **check that the unit is properly levelled**. There are
   **2 levelling pads on the bottom of the rear stabilizer**; use a **14 mm wrench** (or an
   adjustable wrench) to adjust the levellers.

Chapter 7 adds two more mechanical noise answers: a sound from the flywheel when pedalling
backwards is normal, and a whole-machine shake is a levelling problem. See
`cu900ent-noise-and-feet-feeling` and `cu900ent-shaking`.
