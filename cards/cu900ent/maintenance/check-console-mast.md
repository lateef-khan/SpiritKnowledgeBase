Check the **six screws on the riser** and tighten any that are loose. Figure 3.2.1 circles them as
two groups of three, at the joint between the mast and the frame.

The manual adds the parenthetical "(Vertical tube shaking problem excluded)" — that is, tightening
these six screws is not the answer to a vertical tube that shakes. For shaking complaints the
manual sends you elsewhere: the front armrest, the seat, or the levelling feet. See
`cu900ent-shaking`.
