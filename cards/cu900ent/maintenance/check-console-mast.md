Check the **six screws on the riser** and tighten any that are loose. Figure 3.2.1 circles them as
two groups of three, at the joint between the mast and the frame.

The manual adds the parenthetical "(Vertical tube shaking problem excluded)" — that is, tightening
these six screws is not the answer to a vertical tube that shakes. For shaking complaints the
manual sends you elsewhere: the front armrest, the seat, or the levelling feet. See
`cu900ent-shaking`.

## The same six screws on the CU900 2018 and CU1000ENT 2023

Both books print this check word for word, exclusion included, as sections 3.2 and 11.12. The
**recumbent** CR900 2018 and CR900ENT 2021 books say something else: check that *all* the mast's
mounting screws are tight, as needed whenever the handlebars or console seem loose, behind the
mast covers — no count of six and no exclusion
(`spirit-cr900-maintenance-console-mast-screws`).
