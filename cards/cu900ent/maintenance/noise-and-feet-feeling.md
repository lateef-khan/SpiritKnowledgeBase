## Sound from the flywheel when pedalling backwards is normal

As printed: "In the course of the use of reversing trample to hear the sound generated by the
flywheel, this is a normal phenomenon." Do not replace parts for it.

## A bad feeling underfoot

1. Lock the pedal first — section 3.1, see `cu900ent-check-pedals`.
2. If the feeling persists, **check whether the crank is loose** — section 6.3, Pedal/Crank Arm
   Replacement.

That is the same order the general maintenance section gives: hardware, then the crank arm nut and
pedals (`cu900ent-general-maintenance`).

A machine that **shakes** rather than feeling rough is a separate section — see
`cu900ent-shaking`.
