The manual splits shaking by **what** is shaking:

| What shakes | What to do |
|---|---|
| The **front armrest** during use | Tighten its screws — section 6.2, Armrest Group Replacement |
| The **seat**, moving noticeably back and forth while pedalling | Tighten the seat connection screw — section 6.8, Chair Cushions Cover / Seat Button Replacement |
| The **whole product** during use | Adjust the height of the mat — section 6.12.2, Foot Pad/Moving Wheel Replacement: move the wheel counterclockwise, adjusting the height of the four feet when not flat until balanced |

The whole-product case is the same fix the general maintenance section gives for persistent
squeaks: level the unit on its rear stabiliser pads with a 14 mm wrench —
`cu900ent-general-maintenance`.

**A shaking vertical tube is explicitly not a console-mast screw problem.** Section 3.2 excludes
it in so many words — see `cu900ent-check-console-mast`.

The **CU900 2018** service manual prints the same three rows word for word. The recumbent CR900
and CR900ENT books answer a shaking seat differently — take the play out of the seat-carriage
wheels on the aluminium track — and send loose handlebars to the mast bolts
(`spirit-cr900-maintenance-shaking-handlebars-seat-wheels-levellers`).
