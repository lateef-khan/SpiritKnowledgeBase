| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| TFT light is broken | Replace with new TFT or console |
| Power to console too low | Check power to console. Replace lower controller. |

**This row names no voltage.** The CT850 2016 and CVC800 versions ask for 110-120V, the CT850 2020
version asks for 120V, and the CE800ENT version asks for 120VAC. This one only says the power is
too low.

Dead segments rather than a dim backlight are the next row down:
`cu900ent-tft-displays-not-bright`.
