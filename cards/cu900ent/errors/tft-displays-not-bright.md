| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| TFT displays are broken | Replace with new console |

This is the row about the **displays**, not the light behind them. The row above it offers a power
check first and is on `cu900ent-tft-not-bright`. This row offers no check at all - the manual goes
straight to a new console.

The CT850 manuals print the same row about LCDs and LEDs:
`spirit-lcd-displays-dim-or-incomplete` and `ct850-2020-led-displays-dim-or-incomplete`.
