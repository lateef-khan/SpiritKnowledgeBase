**The XT685 is the only one of the five 2023 XT treadmills whose manual prints a light commercial warranty table.** The XT185, XT285, XT385 and XT485 manuals print the residential table and nothing else.

| Warranty | Frame | Motor | Deck | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light Commercial | Lifetime | 5 Years | 3 Years | 3 Years | 1 Year |

The footnote printed with the table:

> \*Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage

Light commercial is shorter than residential on three of the five components. The motor drops from Lifetime to **5 Years**, the deck from Lifetime to **3 Years**, and parts from 10 Years to **3 Years**. Frame (Lifetime) and labor (1 Year) are unchanged.

The consumer-responsibilities paragraph in this manual is worded to match: the warranty applies to products in "ordinary household use **or light commercial use**", where the other four manuals say household use only.

**This table sits awkwardly against exclusion 8**, which this manual prints unchanged along with the other four and excludes "product use in any environment other than a residential setting or non-dues paying facility with 5 hours use or less per day". The manual never defines light commercial use and never reconciles the two. See `xt-2023-warranty-use-environment`.

These are not the Spirit CT900 commercial figures either. CT900 prints a 10 Year frame and a 3 Year labor period; this table does not.
