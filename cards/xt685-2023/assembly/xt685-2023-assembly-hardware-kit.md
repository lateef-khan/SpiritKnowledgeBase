| Panel step | Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| STEP 1 | 79 | 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| STEP 1 | 86 | Ø10 x 2.0T Split Washer | 6 pcs |
| STEP 1 | 100 | 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| STEP 1 | 124 | 8 x 23 x 1.5T Curved Washer | 4 pcs |
| STEP 1 | 123 | 3/8" x 2-1/2" Button Head Socket Bolt | 6 pcs |
| STEP 2 | 86 | Ø10 x 2T Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| STEP 2 | 91 | 3/8" x 1-1/2" Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| STEP 2 | 165 | M5 x 45L Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| STEP 3 | 100 | 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| STEP 4 | 127 | M5 x 10L Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| STEP 4 | 125 | 3.5 x 16L Sheet Metal Screw | 8 pcs |
| STEP 4 | 166 | M5 x 25L Phillips Head Screw | 3 pcs |

Two item numbers are listed twice because the same fastener is used in two
steps: item 86 (6 pcs for the gussets in step 1, 4 pcs for the console in step 2)
and item 100 (4 pcs for the uprights in step 1, 4 pcs for the handrail support in
step 3).

Step 1 also calls for **4 pcs of 3/8" x 2 1/4" Flat Head Socket Bolts (102)** to
be *removed*. Those are already fitted to the machine, holding the shipping
Fixing Plate Stabilizers (101); they are not part of the kit.

The step 1 text calls item 86 a **Spring Washer**; the panel calls it a **Split
Washer**. It is one part.
