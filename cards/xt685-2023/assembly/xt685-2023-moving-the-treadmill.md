Carefully **lift the treadmill at the rear roller area, grasping the two end
caps**, and roll the treadmill away.

**All three manuals print that one sentence and nothing more**, and on the XT685
2010 it is the entire Transportation Instructions chapter - one sentence and a
picture. Its scanned page recovers no other words, so if the picture carries a
further instruction this card does not have it.

**None of the three machines folds.** Their manuals print no folding or unfolding
instructions and no Velcro shipping strap warning, so the "fold it first, then
roll it, no need to tilt" method that the XT185-2023, XT285-2023, XT385-2023 and
XT485-2023 use does not apply here - these have to be tilted.

The 2023 and ENT parts diagrams label transport wheels as N, but neither manual
says the wheels engage on their own; the only instruction is to lift at the rear
roller. **The 2010 manual has no parts diagram and no parts list**, and names
transport wheels only once, in the electrical warning that tells you to route the
power cord away from them.

One inconsistency to know about, and it is in **both 2023 manuals**: the storage
advice later on says "store your machine according to the folding instructions",
and neither manual contains any. The 2010 manual gives no storage advice at all.
