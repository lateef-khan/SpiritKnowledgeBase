Item numbers in brackets are from the exploded view. This machine does not fold,
so there is no Velcro shipping strap to cut; it is shipped with bolted-on
stabilizer plates instead.

**This is the XT685-2023, not the XT685ENT.** The two share their hardware kit
and their steps 3 and 4 word for word, but every cable connection in steps 1 and
2 is different - the ENT lands both its speed and incline cables on one Handrail
Wire (36-18) and adds a ground wire. Check which machine you have before joining
anything up.

**Step 1 - shipping plates, uprights and gussets.** Remove **4 pcs of
3/8" x 2 1/4" Flat Head Socket Bolts (102)** using the L Allen Wrench (104), then
remove the 2 Fixing Plate Stabilizers (101) from both sides of the unit.

Install the Right and Left Uprights (4, 5) into the Upright Cover (L, R) (52, 53)
and Console Mast Side Cap (L & R) (98, 99). Connect the Computer Cable (33) and
the Computer Cable (Middle) (32).

Insert the Right and Left Uprights (4, 5) onto the Mainframe (1) and use the
Combination Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (103) to tighten **four
5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolts (100)** together with **four
Ø8 x 23 x 1.5T Curved Washers (124)**. Then use the L Allen Wrench (104) to
tighten the left and right Gusset (7, 8) with **six 3/8" x 2 1/2" Button Head
Socket Bolts (123)** together with **six Ø3/8" x Ø19 x 1.5T Flat Washers (79)**
and **six Ø10 x 2.0T Spring Washers (86)**.

**Step 2 - console.** Connect the Speed Cable (170-7) into the Speed Cable
(Upper) (34). Connect the Incline Cable (170-8) into the Incline Cable (Upper)
(35). Connect the Computer Cable (Middle) (32) and Computer Cable (Middle &
Upper) (31).

Insert Console Assembly (36) into the Right and Left Uprights (4) and (5) and
secure with **4 pcs of 3/8" x 1 1/2" Button Head Socket Bolts (91)** with **4 pcs
of Ø10 x 2.0T Split Washers (86)**, using the L Allen Wrench (104).
**NOTE: do NOT tighten all screws yet.**

Then connect Computer Cable (37-24) to Computer Cable (Middle & Upper) (31),
Connecting Cable (37-27) to Connecting Cable (36-17), Connecting Cable (Upper)
(37-28) to Connecting Cable (Lower) (36-18), and Connecting Cable (Red) (37-29)
to Connecting Cable (36-19). Place Console Assembly (Top) (37) onto Console
Assembly (Bottom) (36) and use the Phillips Head Screw Driver (126) to tighten
**4 pcs of M5 x 45mm Phillips Head Screws (165)**.

**Step 3 - handrail support.** Install the Handrails Support (3) between the
Uprights (R, L) (4, 5) with **5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolts (100)**,
using the Combination Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (103). The printed sentence
reads "the 4pcs of five 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolts (100)" - the count
is garbled. The hardware panel allocates **4 pcs** of item 100 to step 3.

**Step 4 - caps, covers and tablet holder.** Install the Console Mast Side Cap
(R) and (L), (99) and (98), on the Main Frame (1) and secure with **4 pcs of
M5 x 10mm Phillips Head Screws (127)**, using the Phillips Head Screw Driver
(126). Install the Upright Cover (L) and (R), (52) and (53), on the right and
left Uprights (4) and (5) and Console Support (6) with **8 pcs of Ø3.5 x 16L
Sheet Metal Screws (125)**, same screwdriver. Place the Tablet Holder (169) on
Console Assembly (Top) (37) and tighten **3 pcs of M5 x 25mm Phillips Head Screws
(166)**.

**NOTE: tighten all screws only after all components have been assembled.**
