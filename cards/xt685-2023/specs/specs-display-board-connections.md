Section 6.1 is one photograph with ten call-outs: **HAND PULSE**, **SYSTEM CABLE**, **SAFETY**,
**KEYBOARD**, **RF**, **BLUETOOTH MODULE**, **BLE MUSIC SIGNAL**, **BLE MUSIC POWER**, **USB-POWER** and
**DC FAN**.

The BLE music pair goes to the amplify board (section 6.4), which carries the matching "BLE MUSIC POWER"
and "BLE MUSIC SIGNAL" plugs and the two speakers. No connector numbers or pin definitions are printed;
the only pin table in the book is the 6-pin system cable.

The other 2023 XT books print a bare photograph for this section; the XT685ENT prints socket names for a
different, touch-screen board.
