The numbers below are the callout numbers on the Exploded View Diagram on
page 47. The diagram itself is a picture with numbers on it and no text, so
this table is the only readable form of it.

**The XT685 numbering is its own.** Its Drive Motor is 20; on the XT385 and
XT485 it is 34 and on the XT185 and XT285 it is 027. The XT685 is also the only
XT in the range whose list carries a separate motor-compartment Fan (112) with
its two Motor Fan Connecting Cables (113, 114) and a Fan Mounting Plate (54).

**On the printed numbering.** The list is not a continuous run. The numbers
that appear are: 1-13, 15, 17, 19-39, 41-46, 48-54, 56-60, 62, 64-81, 83-91, 93-94, 96-104, 106-107, 109-110, 112-114, 117, 123-129, 131, 151-153, 163, 165-166, 168-174, 178-179, 300-301. Any number outside that run is not printed
in this manual at all - it is not a missing page.

Three printing faults, left as printed:

- **Item 36 and item 36-01 carry the same description**, "Console Top
  Cover(big)". Check the exploded view before ordering either one.
- **Item 42 is one line, "Transportation Wheel", quantity 4.** Every other XT
  splits the wheels into an (A) and a (B) part with two of each.
- **Items 65, 85 and 103 are printed across two lines** - "(Alloy" / "Steel)",
  "…_Concave" / "Washer", and "Combination Allen Wrench & Phillips Head" /
  "Screw Driver". They are rejoined here as one description each.
