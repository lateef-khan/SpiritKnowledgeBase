Applies to the 2023 F63 and F65. These use a shorter combination than the general engineering menu route.

1. Turn the power switch on if the machine is not already on.
2. Insert the safety key.
3. Press and hold **Stop** and **Enter**. While holding them, press **Display**. Sleep Mode appears.
4. Press **speed up** to change OFF to ON.
5. Press **Enter** to confirm.

**What happens**: the machine sleeps after 15 to 30 minutes of no use while the safety key is in. The screen also turns off when the key is removed. Press **Start** to wake it, as long as the key is still in.
