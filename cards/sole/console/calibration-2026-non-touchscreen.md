The 2026 series uses a different button combination from earlier non-touchscreen machines.

Applies to the 2026 F63, F65, F80 and F83. **The 2026 F85 is a touchscreen**, so use the touchscreen calibration card for that machine.

1. **Clear error codes**: power the treadmill off, wait a few seconds, then power it back on.
2. **Enter factory settings mode**:
   - With the machine on, remove the safety key.
   - Press and hold **Start** and **Speed 3** on the main console. There are 3 buttons under the Start button sticker. **Use the left or the right one. The centre button does not work.**
   - Insert the safety key while holding the buttons, and keep holding for about 10 seconds.
   - The screen should show **F1**. If it does not, repeat the steps.
3. **Navigate to calibration**: press **Display/Enter** repeatedly until the screen stops changing, then press **Start**.
4. **Start calibration**: step off the treadmill first, because it moves on its own. Press **Start**. The machine runs the speed and the incline from lowest to highest by itself.
5. **After**: on success the machine reboots to the home screen. Note any error codes, and watch the behaviour, for example if it stops early or will not incline.
