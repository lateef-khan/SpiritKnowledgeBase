Applies to the 2023 F85 (585822), F89 (589822) and TT8 (588822).

Check the wiring on the rear incline controller first. **If that does not fix it, replace the rear incline controller.**

**The part**: the rear incline controller is a small green circuit board on a metal mounting bracket, sitting low in the frame near the incline motor. A thick **red** wire lands on one white connector, and a **black and white pair** lands on a second white connector beside it. Check that both connectors are seated fully and that neither wire is pinched.

**Note**: the source is almost entirely photographs. The only written instruction in it is the rear incline controller replacement. The wiring description above was read from the picture.
