Applies to the 2023 F63 and F65.

The machine looks like it turns on, but it does not respond to the start button.

1. Press the **12** button and see whether the fan turns on.
   - **Fan turns on**: a cable between the bottom and top consoles was plugged in backwards. Unmount the top console, find the backwards cable, and plug it in the right way round. The buttons should work again.
   - **Fan does not turn on**: double check the cabling. If everything looks right and it still does not work, replace both the top and bottom console assemblies.

**The cable to check**: inside the console mast there is a **black 12-pin connector pair**, two rows of six, on a ribbon of coloured wires. A separate **white connector** sits next to it in the same channel. The black 12-pin pair is the one that goes in backwards. Its two halves fit together either way round, so check that the coloured wires run the same way on both sides.

**Note**: the source carries two photographs of a technician holding these connectors. The description above was read from them.
