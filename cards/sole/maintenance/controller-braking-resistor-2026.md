Applicable models: F63, F65, F80, F83 and F85, 2026 version.

1. Disconnect all cables from the original controller. With a Phillips screwdriver, remove the two 5 x 16 mm black Phillips pan head self-drilling screws, then remove the original controller.
2. With a power drill, drill two 3.5 mm mounting holes on the right side of the main frame for the braking resistor bracket. **Remove all metal shavings from the area afterwards.**
   - The first hole goes about **140 mm** from the front end of the right side main frame tube. This position is the same on every applicable model.
3. Fit the parts:
   - Install the braking resistor mounting bracket on the main frame with two 5 x 16 mm black Phillips pan head self-drilling screws.
   - Install the braking resistor on the bracket with two more of the same screws.
   - Install the new controller on the main frame with two more of the same screws.
   - Reconnect all cables to the new controller.

A new controller always needs a calibration afterwards.
