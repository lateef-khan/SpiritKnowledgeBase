**The manual prints no weight-stack rating.** This total is the sum of the plate quantities its
assembly hardware table lists, and it is not a figure the manufacturer states anywhere in the book -
say so when you quote it. Why that is, and how it was checked, is at
`spirit-strength-specs-no-weight-stack-rating-printed`.

| No. | Description | Qty | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 091 | `20lb weight stack` | 13 | 260 lb |
| 067 | `10lb weight stack` | 1 | 10 lb |
| 065 | `10lb top weight stack` | 1 | 10 lb |
| | **Total** | **15** | **280 lb** |

**This is the heaviest single-plate-selection stack among the nineteen CSS machines** apart from the
330 lb seated leg press.

**Not the same machine as the functional back extension.** CSS-BEXT is the selectorized one with this
stack; CSF-BEXT is a bench with no stack at all and a 600 lb loading capacity - see
`spirit-strength-specs-no-weight-stack-on-the-benches-and-racks`.

The optional 5 lb increment weight rides on top of the stack on the machines that have one -
`spirit-strength-specs-five-pound-increment-weight`.
