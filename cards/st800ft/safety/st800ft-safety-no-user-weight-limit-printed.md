**The manual does not give one, and no figure should be quoted for it.**

Where the other two ST800 products end their safety bullets with a weight limit — 1,000 lb total for
the ST800DR3 rack, and 1,000/300/700 lb for the ST800FI bench — the ST800FT's list simply ends. Its
last bullet is *DO NOT use use this machine outdoors* (printed with the doubled word).

The absence was checked two ways. A loose search of the whole manual for *weight* alongside *limit*,
*max*, *capacity*, *user*, *total* and *not exceed* returns nothing, and the only *capacity* in the
book is the fastener figure on the securing page. The only three-figure pound values anywhere in it
are **160 lbs and 210 lbs** (the weight of each stack, before and after the optional 50 lb plate pack), **220 lbs** (the
required anchor pull-out force) and **500 lbs** (the fastener tensile capacity) — none of them a
user weight limit. The ST800FT also has no product labels page, so there is no serial decal to read
a figure off, as there is on the CSF machines.

So the correct answer to a customer is that Spirit does not publish a user weight limit for this
machine in its owner's manual, and that the figure has to come from Spirit rather than be inferred
from a sibling product. **Do not carry the CSF-FUNT's 400 lb across** — that is a different
functional trainer with its own manual and its own decal.
