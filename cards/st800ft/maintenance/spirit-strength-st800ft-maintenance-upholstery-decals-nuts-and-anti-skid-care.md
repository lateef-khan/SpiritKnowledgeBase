**The ST800FT is the only Spirit strength manual with a page explaining what each inspection row
means.** Five components, printed as five short lists.

## Links, pull-pins, snap hooks, swivels, weight stack pins

- **Check all pieces for signs of visible wear or damage.**
- **Check springs in snap hook and pull-pins for proper tension alignment.**
- **If the spring sticks or has lost its rigidity, replace it immediately.**

Inspected **daily** in a facility, **weekly** at home.

## Upholstery

- **To ensure prolonged upholstery life and proper hygiene, all upholstered pads should be wiped
  down with a damp cloth after every workout.**
- **Periodically take the time to use a mild soap or an approved vinyl upholstery cleaner** to deter
  the onset of cracking or drying. **Avoid using any abrasive cleaners or cleaners not intended for
  use on vinyl.**
- **Replace ripped or worn upholstery immediately.**
- **Keep sharp or pointed objects clear of all upholstery.**

Cleaned **daily** in a facility, **weekly** at home. No product brand is named and no interval is
given for the soap step beyond *periodically*.

## Decals

- **Inspect and familiarise yourself with any safety warnings or other user information posted on
  each decal.**

Inspected **weekly** in a facility, **every 3 months** at home.

## Nuts and bolts

- **Inspect all nuts and bolts for any loosening and tightening if needed.**
- **Go through a re-tightening sequence periodically to ensure that all hardware is tensioned
  proper.**

**No sequence is printed and no torque figure is given** —
`spirit-strength-maintenance-no-torque-figure-for-any-fastener`.

## Anti-skid surfaces

- **These surfaces are designed to supply secure footing and need to be replaced if they appear worn
  or become slippery.**

Inspected **weekly** in a facility, **every 3 months** at home. Note the criterion: **slippery is a
replacement condition, not a cleaning one.**

## This page is one book out of thirty-nine

The thirty-six CS strength manuals print none of it. Their schedules say *Clean: Upholstery* and
*Inspect: Fasteners* with no method behind either —
`spirit-strength-maintenance-no-cleaning-agent-and-no-storage-section`. The advice above is sound
and it is Spirit's own, but it is printed for the ST800FT. Say so rather than attributing it to a
customer's CSS or CSD manual.

For sanitizing rather than cleaning, Spirit's company-wide instruction — **75% isopropyl alcohol** on
hard plastics and a **conditioner after sanitizing** on upholstery — is printed on the next page of
this same book: `ct900-sanitizing-equipment`.
