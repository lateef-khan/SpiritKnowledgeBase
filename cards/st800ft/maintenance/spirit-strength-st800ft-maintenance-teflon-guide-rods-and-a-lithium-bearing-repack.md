**This is the only Spirit strength manual of thirty-nine that names a lubricant on its maintenance
page.** Three parts, three products, three intervals.

| Part | Product | Commercial | Home |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guide Rods | **Teflon (PTFE) based lubricant** (clean and lubricate) | Monthly | 3 Months |
| Seat Sleeves, Turcite Bushings, Linear Bearing | lubricate — **Silicon or Teflon based lubricant spray** | Monthly | 3 Months |
| Linear Bearings | **repack with light grease (lithium, super lube, etc.)** | 6 Months | Yearly |

## How the manual says to apply each

**Seat sleeves and guide rods**, word for word:

> - Wipe down adjusting tubes with a **dust free rag before applying lubricant**.
> - Lubricate seat sleeves and Guide Rods with a **Silicon or Teflon based lubricant spray**.

**Linear bearings**, word for word:

> Referring to the Owner's Manual carefully disassemble the bearing from its housing and place **a
> finger full of light grease (lithium, super lube, etc.)** into the inside of the bearing. Using
> your finger, press the grease into the ball-bearing and their tracks. **Repeat until the
> ball-bearing tracks are full of grease.** Insert the shaft back into the bearing and wipe off
> excess grease.

No quantity is given beyond *a finger full*, and no torque or reassembly figure is given for the
housing. The manual's own cross-reference — *"Referring to the Owner's Manual"* — points back into
the same book and no page is named.

## Two spellings and one clock worth noticing

- The table says **Teflon (PTFE)** for the guide rods; the facing page says **Silicon or Teflon** for
  the same rods plus the seat sleeves. Both are the manual's own wording and it does not reconcile
  them. Either is within what the book says for a guide rod.
- The linear bearing appears **twice**, on two clocks: a **monthly** spray lubrication and a
  **six-monthly** grease repack. They are separate rows and separate jobs.

## Do not carry these products onto another Spirit strength machine

The **thirty-five CS machines with a guide-rod row name no lubricant at all** —
`spirit-strength-maintenance-guide-rods-lubricated-monthly-and-no-lubricant-named` — and the only
lubricant three of them name is **a thin coat of clear silicone**, in the assembly chapter, at build
time (`spirit-strength-maintenance-guide-rods-take-a-thin-coat-of-clear-silicone`).

Teflon spray and lithium grease are what **this** machine's book asks for. Quoting them for a CSS or
CSD machine is quoting a different manual.

## Nothing on the CSF-FUNT matches this

Spirit's other functional trainer, the **CSF-FUNT**, is on the CS template: a monthly *Lubricate:
Guide Rods* tick and no product, no bearing repack, no seat sleeve and no dust-free-rag step.
