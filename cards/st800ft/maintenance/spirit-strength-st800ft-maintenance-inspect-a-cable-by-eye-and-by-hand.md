**The ST800FT is the only Spirit strength manual that says what a cable inspection actually
consists of.** Everything else in the range says *inspect* and leaves it there.

## Belts and cables

Word for word:

> - We uses only high quality belt, and **mil-spec cables**.
> - **Visually inspect the belt and cables for fraying, cracking, peeling, or discoloration.**
> - **While the machine is not in use, carefully run your fingers along the belt or cable to feel
>   for thinning or bulging areas.**
> - **Replace belts and cables immediately at the first of damage or wear. Do not use equipment
>   until belts or cables have been replaced.**

The last bullet is printed with a word missing — *"at the first of damage or wear"* — and reads as
*at the first sign of damage or wear*, which is how the rest of the Spirit range words it. Quoted
here as printed.

## Belt and cable tension

> - Referring to the Owners Manual, when belt or cables are used **check all bolts attachments to
>   be sure they are properly attached**.
> - **Check slack in cables and re-adjust cable tension if needed.**

**No tension figure, no slack measurement and no adjustment procedure is printed.** The manual
points back at itself — *"Referring to the Owners Manual"* — and names no page. There is no cable
tension specification anywhere in the book.

## Two failure modes, and one of them you cannot see

**Thinning and bulging are hand checks.** A cable that looks intact can be failing internally, and
the manual's remedy is to run your fingers down its length with the machine at rest. That is worth
saying to a gym that inspects visually from a distance.

**Do the hand check with the stack down and the machine not in use.** The manual says so
explicitly.

## When this inspection happens

**Daily** in a facility, **weekly** at home, plus **before every use** by whoever uses the machine —
and the cable comes out **yearly** in a facility regardless:
`spirit-strength-st800ft-maintenance-cables-inspected-daily-and-replaced-yearly`.

## The rest of the range has the rule without the method

The thirty-five CS machines print *"Cables and belts pose an extreme liability if used when frayed.
Always replace any cable or belt at first sign of wear"* and no inspection method at all
(`spirit-strength-maintenance-replace-a-frayed-cable-at-first-sign-of-wear`). **The four checks
above are a sensible thing to tell any Spirit strength customer, but they are printed in one book of
thirty-nine.** Say where they come from.
