**The ST800FT is the only one of the thirty-nine Spirit strength machines whose manual gives a
cable a service life.**

| | Commercial | Home |
|---|---|---|
| **Inspect: Cables or Belts and their tension** | **Daily** | **Weekly** |
| **Replace: Cables, Belts and Connecting Parts** | **Yearly** | **3 Years** |

And, on the facility precautions page, a third interval that applies in both settings:

> **Inspect all cables and belts and connections prior to use.** DO NOT use if any components are
> worn, frayed or damaged.

## Replacement is on a clock here, not only on a condition

This is the point that separates this machine from the rest of the range. Every other Spirit
strength manual replaces a cable **only when it looks worn** and prints no interval at all
(`spirit-strength-maintenance-replace-a-frayed-cable-at-first-sign-of-wear`). This one says
**yearly in a facility** whatever the cable looks like — and *"Replace all worn components
immediately"* still applies on top of it, so a cable that fails an inspection at nine months comes
out at nine months.

**"Connecting Parts" is replaced with the cable**, not just the cable itself. The manual does not
list which parts those are.

## The gym figure is the one on the machine

The manual's lead-in says *"Your equipment comes with a commercial schedule decal. For in home use,
please follow the maintenance schedule listed below."* **The decal is the commercial column.** A
home owner following the decal would be inspecting daily and changing cables yearly — stricter than
their book asks. Ask which setting the machine is in before quoting.

## The inspection is a hand check as well as an eye check

The book tells you what a daily cable inspection is: look for fraying, cracking, peeling or
discoloration, and **run your fingers along the cable to feel for thinning or bulging areas** while
the machine is not in use. See
`spirit-strength-st800ft-maintenance-inspect-a-cable-by-eye-and-by-hand`, which also covers the
tension check the schedule row names.

## Do not carry the yearly figure onto another Spirit strength machine

Not onto the **CSF-FUNT**, Spirit's other functional trainer, whose book gives no replacement
interval at all. Not onto the thirty-three CS machines that inspect weekly, and not onto the two
CSI machines that inspect monthly. **This figure belongs to this machine's template**, which also
carries a home column no other Spirit strength manual has.
