Step five routes one Cable Assembly (No. 26) per side:

**Follow the diagram with the Cable Assembly (No. 26) through the framework as shown. Pay
special attention to make sure the cable end with the bolt is connected at the "Finish"
and the cable end with the loop is positioned at the "Start" as shown in the diagram.
Repeat this step for both sides of the machine.**

**Wrench tighten the bolts and nylon lock nuts.**

**The two ends are not interchangeable.** One is swaged to a loop and one to a threaded
bolt; the loop anchors at Start and the bolt terminates at Finish. Routed the other way
round the cable will physically fit and the travel will be wrong.

**Start and Finish are labels on the drawing, not on the machine**, and the drawing is the
only route description - the step prints no list of pulleys to pass. This is the one page
of this build that has to be read from the picture.

The parts list carries **two Cable Assemblies (No. 26)**, six big pulleys, ten small
pulleys and two Gourd Hooks Φ8 (No. 50) that the cable passes.

The rest of the Spirit strength range prints a dedicated cable routing diagram page - see
[that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-strength-assembly-cable-routing-diagram-names-two-wires.md).
The ST800 books do not; the route is inside this step.
