| | Frame | Parts | Cables | Upholstery/Grips |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Residential** (vendor defects only) | Lifetime | Lifetime | Lifetime | Lifetime |
| **Commercial** (non-dues paying facilities) | 10 Years | 5 Years | **1 Year** | 6 Months |

**Cables get a column of their own here, and only here among the ST800 books.** They are
covered for life at home and for one year in a facility. The flat incline bench and the
dumbbell rack print no cables column - which is consistent, since neither has cables.

The home row is qualified *vendor defects only*: lifetime cover for a manufacturing
fault, not for wear.

**No labour column and no labour row.** The labour term is stated separately, in the
SERVICE paragraph: `spirit-strength-warranty-labor-period`.

**A prisons footnote sits directly under this table**, in addition to exclusion 8 on the
next page: `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.

The CSF-FUNT is also a functional trainer and carries an entirely different warranty -
a single Light Commercial row with no home terms:
`spirit-strength-csf-funt-light-commercial-warranty`. Check which trainer is in front of
you before quoting either.
