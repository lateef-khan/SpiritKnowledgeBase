**This is the one machine in the thirty-nine whose manual states what the stack delivers.** Every
other book prints plate counts at best - `spirit-strength-specs-no-weight-stack-rating-printed`.

**The handle sees exactly half the weight the pin selects.**

| Weight selected | Actual resistance | | Weight selected | Actual resistance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 lb | 5 lb | | 90 lb | 45 lb |
| 20 lb | 10 lb | | 100 lb | 50 lb |
| 30 lb | 15 lb | | 110 lb | 55 lb |
| 40 lb | 20 lb | | 120 lb | 60 lb |
| 50 lb | 25 lb | | 130 lb | 65 lb |
| 60 lb | 30 lb | | 140 lb | 70 lb |
| 70 lb | 35 lb | | 150 lb | 75 lb |
| 80 lb | 40 lb | | 160 lb | 80 lb |

**So each stack runs 10 to 160 lb selected in 10 lb steps, which is 5 to 80 lb at the handle in 5 lb
steps.** A customer complaining that the machine feels light is reading the stack decal, not the
table.

## The heavy stack option takes each side to 210 lb

The manual prints a second table for it:

| Weight selected | Actual resistance |
|---|---|
| 170 lb | 85 lb |
| 180 lb | 90 lb |
| 190 lb | 95 lb |
| 200 lb | 100 lb |
| 210 lb | 105 lb |

> *"If additional 50lbs of plates are purchased to increase the weight from 160lbs to 210lbs on each
> weight stack, then these two Support Frames will be removed."*

**The upgrade is five extra 10 lb plates per side and the removal of the two Support Frames (No. 13)
and their Weight Bumpers.** The ST800FT HEAVY WEIGHT STACK page gives the procedure: strip the inner
shroud, the two guide bars, the top plate and the fifteen weight plates, take the support frames out,
add five plates to each stand and rebuild.

The standard build is **15 x `Weight Plate` (No. 51) plus 1 `Top Plate` (No. 52) per side** - the
parts list totals 30 plates and 2 top plates for the machine.
