First work out your **Maximum Heart Rate**: **220 minus your age**. Your heart rate training zone is
**50% to 90%** of that maximum.

**This manual prints 60% and 80%.** 60% of your MHR is "the zone that burns fat" while 80% is "for
strengthening the cardio vascular system", and 60% to 80% is the zone to stay in for maximum
benefit.

Worked example for someone **40 years old**:

- 220 - 40 = **180** (maximum heart rate)
- 180 x .6 = **108** beats per minute (60% of maximum)
- 180 x .8 = **144** beats per minute (80% of maximum)
- So for a 40 year old the training zone would be **108 to 144** beats per minute.

If you enter your age during programming the console performs this calculation automatically.

The page also has a **Target Heart Rate Training Chart**, a printed image with age along the bottom
from 20 to 65 and two diagonal lines labelled Weight Loss and Cardiovascular. Its values are not
recoverable from the scan.

**Both scans of this manual agree on every figure above**, digit for digit.

**Three other figure pairs appear across the thirteen commercial bike manuals**, and the KB keeps
them apart rather than harmonising them. The CR800 2021, CR800 2023 and CU800 2021 print 60%/85% and
108 to 153; the three 800ENT bikes print 65%/85% and 117 to 153; the CR900 2025 and CU900 2025 print
65%/80% with a printed answer of 117 to **225**. Four manuals have no such page at all.
