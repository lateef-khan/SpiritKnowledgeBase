1. Use a **15 mm wrench** to turn the **left pedal clockwise** or the **right pedal counterclockwise** to take the pedals off.
2. Take the crank off: use a **14 mm wrench** to release the nut that fixes the crank, then use the proper tool to take the right and left cranks off.
3. To refit, install the cranks onto the crank axle **symmetrically at each side**, use an **air hammer** to seat the cranks, then use the 14 mm wrench to tighten the nut and plug in the **crank arm end cap** to hold the crank in place. Install the pedals by turning them clockwise.

**Two figures differ from the 2020 CU800 and XBU55 books**, which use a 13/15 mm wrench on the pedals and a plug wrench (no size) on the crank nut, then an Allen wrench with a crank tool. This book prints a 15 mm pedal wrench and a **14 mm crank-nut wrench**.

**The refit sentence "Install the pedals by turning them clockwise" is wrong for the left pedal**, which this same procedure removed clockwise in step 1. The left pedal is left-hand threaded and goes on counterclockwise. The book's smoothness section adds: if a pedal comes loose, tighten it with the 15 mm wrench; if the crank clicks, tighten the crank nut.
