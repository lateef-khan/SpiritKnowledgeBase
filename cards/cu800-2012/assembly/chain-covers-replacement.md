1. Take the cranks off (procedure 4) and take the **console mast cover** and the **step covers** off the chain covers. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 12 mm self-tapping screws** and **3 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws**, and the left chain cover is released.
2. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to release the **4 x 19 mm self-tapping screw with its Ø5 x Ø15 x 1.5T flat washer** on the main frame, and **3 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws** on the right chain cover. Release **2 pcs of M3 x 10 screws** holding the **AC cable assembly**, and the right chain cover can be taken off.
3. To refit, install the right chain cover onto the main frame (with one piece of **5/16" x 19 mm x 1.5T flat washer** on the cover), secure the AC cable assembly on the cover with the 2 pcs of M3 x 10 screws, and fit one 4 x 19 mm self-tapping screw with its Ø5 x Ø15 x 1.5T flat washer **loosely for now**. **Align the crank axle with the drive pulley** and secure with 3 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws and one 4 x 19 mm self-tapping screw with its Ø5 x Ø15 x 1.5T flat washer. Match the left and right chain covers and fit the 7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 12 mm self-tapping screws and 3 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws.

**The AC cable assembly screws (two M3 x 10) are printed only in this book** - the 2012 CU800 has a plug-in AC adaptor. The 2020 CU800 and the XBU55 books share the rest of the fasteners but have no such bracket.

The book's own chain-cover troubleshooting adds: if the crank rubs a cover, both covers have to come off and be set in the middle; if the drive pulley rubs the right cover, take that cover off, release the 5/16" x 19 mm x 1.5T flat washer on the left cover, refit both and turn the crank to check.
