1. Use a **14 mm wrench** to loosen the hex bolt at both sides under the seat bottom cushion, and take the cushion off.
2. Turn the **3/8" x 25 mm knob counterclockwise** until it comes off the **fix plate**.
3. To refit, put the **seat slide bracket** (with its round tube at the front) on top of the **inner slide** and turn the 3/8" x 25 mm knob clockwise from the bottom upward to secure the fix plate. Insert the seat bottom cushion and secure it with the hex head bolts using the 14 mm wrench.

The 2020 CU800 and the XBU55 books print the same removal for their "Sliding Seat Mount (7)" and name the knob the brake tension knob (86) with a 3/8" x 7T nut (78); this book names the knob by its thread and prints no nut. The 14 mm wrench and the two seat bolts are the same.

"Seat slide bracket (with round tube at the front)" is the only orientation instruction printed for refitting the bracket.
