1. Follow procedure 3 to take off the seat bottom cushion, the seat slide bracket and the **Ø8 x M16 knob**. Take the **step cover** off the chain covers and pull out the **inner slide** together with the **Ø40 x 80 center spatial wrap**. Unplug the **ending cap** from the inner slide with a rubber hammer, and the Ø40 x 80 center spatial wrap and the step cover can be released from the inner slide.
2. To refit, return the step cover and the Ø40 x 80 center spatial wrap onto the inner slide in sequence, **with the hole for the locking knob on the right side of the step cover**. Plug the ending cap into the inner slide by hitting it with a hammer and slide the inner slide into the main frame. Insert the Ø40 x 80 center spatial wrap onto the main frame with the rubber hammer, return the step cover onto the left and right chain covers, and refit the Ø8 x M16 knob.

**Different parts from the 2020 CU800 and XBU55 books**, whose inner slide has a slide spacer (114) held by two 4 x 12 mm sheet-metal screws (68) and a locking knob (106); this book has an ending cap, a Ø40 x 80 spacer, and no screws.

The only orientation instruction is the locking-knob hole on the right side of the step cover.
