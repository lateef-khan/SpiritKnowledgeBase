**The 2012 CU800 (XU878-AB04M) book prints the handlebar and the console mast as one procedure and numbers no parts.** The 2020 CU800 and XBU55 books split it into two sections with part numbers; the fasteners are the same.

1. Take the console off (four M5 x 12 mm screws, computer cable and pulse sensor cables unplugged).
2. Take off the **handgrip cap** and use a **12 mm wrench** to release **2 pcs of 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts**, **2 pcs of Ø8 spring washers** and **2 pcs of 5/16" x 19 mm x 1.5T flat washers**, and the **seat handlebar** is released.
3. Slightly press the **console mast cover** to separate it from the left and right chain covers. Use the 12 mm wrench to release **7 pcs of 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts** with **6 pcs of 5/16" x 19 mm x 1.5T flat washers** and **one 5/16" x 20 curved washer**, and pull the console mast out.
4. To refit the console mast, let the computer cables go through the mast cover and guide them through the console mast, then put the console mast onto the main frame. Use the 12 mm wrench to tighten the 7 pcs of 5/16" x 5/8" bolts with the 6 flat washers and the one curved washer. Secure the mast cover on the left and right chain covers.

The book calls the front handlebar the "Seat Handlebar" in the heading and "Seat Handlebar" in step 2; on the drawing the part sits on the console mast, where the other uprights' books put the "Handlebar Assembly (3)".

No hand-pulse assembly steps are printed in this book - the 2020 and XBU55 books add the handgrip side caps and the four 20 mm tapping screws of the hand-pulse assembly to the handlebar procedure.
