This manual has no hardware tables. Each step ends in a **HARDWARE** panel of drawings
with a caption under each one. Every figure below was read from both OCR scans and the
two agree.

## Step 1 - rear stabilizer

- **#50. 3/8" x 2-1/4" Bolt**
- **#71. 3/8" x 25**

The step calls for **four bolts (50) and four washers (71)**. The drawings' own piece
counts did not survive either scan; the count of four comes from the step text, which
both scans print the same way. The second caption's type word is lost in both scans;
the step calls item 71 a washer.

## Step 2 - front console mast

- **#51. 5/16" x 5/8" Hex Head Bolt (7 pcs)**
- **#99. 5/16" x 19 Curved Washer (1 pc)**
- **#72. 5/16" x 18 Flat Washer (6 pcs)**

## Step 3 - handlebars

- **#51. 5/16" x 5/8" Hex Head Bolt (2 pcs)**
- **#103. 5/16" Split Washer (2 pcs)**
- **#72. 5/16" x 18 Flat Washer (2 pcs)**

## Step 4 - console, seat, covers and pedals

- **#58. M5 x 12mm Phillips Head Screw (10 pcs)**

Ten, where the 2021 and CU800ENT books list six of the same screw for a step that no
longer fits the two stabilizer covers or the console cover plate.

## Where this differs from every later CU800

**Step 1 is a different fastener in a different count**: this manual uses four of item
50 at **3/8" x 2-1/4"** with four washers numbered **71**, where the 2021 and CU800ENT
books use two of item 50 at **3/8" x 135mm** with two curved washers numbered **151**.
Steps 2 and 3 carry the same item numbers, sizes and counts as the later books.

## What the OCR could and could not read

Both scans render item 58 as "MS x 12mm" and "MS x [2mm"; the later manuals give the
same item as **M5 x 12mm**, which settles it. The piece counts in step 1 are the only
figures on this page that no scan preserved, and they are not asserted here beyond what
the step text says.
