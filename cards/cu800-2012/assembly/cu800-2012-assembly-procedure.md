Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. Every number below was
read from both OCR scans of the same page and the two agree. Hardware is on
[the hardware card](cu800-2012-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Step 1 - rear stabilizer

1. Install the **Rear Stabilizer (5)** onto the **Main Frame (1)** with the **four
   bolts (50)** and **four washers (71)**.

**Four bolts, not two.** Every later CU800 manual fits the rear stabilizer with 2 Bolts
(No.50) and 2 Washers (No.151), and its bolt is a different length. See
[the 2021 build](../../cu800-2021/assembly/cu800-2021-assembly-procedure.md).

## Step 2 - front console mast

1. Unravel the **Computer Cable (29)** and snake it through the **Console Mast Cover
   (38)** and the **Console Mast (2)** until the cable connector comes out of the top
   opening of the console mast. Slide the plastic cover (38) onto the console mast,
   in the correct orientation.
2. Install the Console Mast (2) onto the Main Frame (1) with **seven bolts (51), six
   washers (72) on the side screws and one curved washer (99) on the front screw**.

## Step 3 - handlebars

1. Run the **two hand pulse wires (26)** into the hole in the handlebar mounting plate
   and out through the hole in the console plate. Install the **Handle Bar (3)** onto
   the Console Mast (2) with two bolts (51), two washers (72) and two split washers
   (103), careful not to pinch the hand pulse wires. Snap the black plastic handlebar
   cover over the joint.

## Step 4 - console, seat, covers and pedals

1. Insert the Computer Cable (29) and two hand pulse cables (26, 28) into the
   connectors in the back of the **Console (34)**. Install the Console (34) onto the
   Console Mast (2). **Install the two bottom screws first (58).** Install the **cover
   plate (12)** with the two top mounting screws (58).
2. Install the **Front Stabilizer Cover (40)** and the **Rear Stabilizer Cover (41)**
   onto the Main Frame (1) with four screws (58).
3. Install the **Pedals (45 L, 46 R)** onto the **Crank arms (16 L, 16 R)**. The left
   pedal has a reverse thread and will be screwed into the crank in the opposite
   rotation from normal threads. There is an **"L" stamped into the end of the threaded
   post of the left pedal and an "R" in the right**. Tighten the pedals as much as you
   possibly can. It may be necessary to re-tighten them if you feel a thumping during
   pedaling; a thumping or clicking noise is usually caused by the pedals being too
   loose.
4. Install the **Seat mount slide (7)** onto the track on top of the seat tube with the
   seat post facing forward. Install the **seat (19)** onto the seat post.
5. Install the **Drink Bottle Holder (116)** onto the Console Mast (2) with two screws
   (58).

## Two things only this manual prints

- **A console cover plate (12)**, fitted with the two top screws after the two bottom
  ones. No later CU800 manual mentions it.
- **The L and R stamps on the pedal posts.** The 2021 book keeps the reverse-thread
  sentence but drops the stamps; the CU800ENT books drop both. See
  [the pedal card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-bike-assembly-pedal-tighten-as-much-as-possible.md).

**This manual fits the stabilizer covers.** The 2021 book still packs them and no
longer has a step for them.

The build ends here. There is no first-power-on step: these bikes are
generator-powered and the console starts when you pedal.
