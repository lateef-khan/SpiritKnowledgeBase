**This is the 2012 CU800 (XU878), belt figure 170 +/- 10 Hz.** The 2020 CU800 prints 180 to 205 Hz and the XBU55 180 +/- 10 Hz for what look like the same steps; do not carry either figure here.

1. Take the right chain cover off (procedure 5). Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **3 pcs of M6 x 15 mm screws** and a **13 mm wrench** to loosen the **M8 x 7T nut on the J-bolt** until the belt is loose enough to take off.
2. Use **C-ring pliers** to release the **Ø20 C-ring** on the **crank axle** and take the crank assembly off together with the **drive pulley** (a rubber hammer may help).
3. Use **two 11 mm wrenches** to release **4 pcs of 1/4" x 3/4" hex head bolts and 1/4" nuts** holding the drive pulley on the crank axle; the crank axle and the drive pulley can then be separated.
4. To refit, insert the crank axle through the bearing on the main frame, return the C-ring to its groove on the axle with the C-ring pliers, then return the drive pulley onto the crank axle. Use the 13 mm wrench to tighten the M8 x 7T nut on the J-bolt until the **idler wheel** applies some tension on the belt. **Use an acoustic tension gauge to make sure the reading is in the range 170 +/- 10 Hz.** Tighten the 3 pcs of M6 x 15 mm screws on the **idler wheel assembly**.
5. Follow procedure 5-3 to refit the covers.

The book's slippage answer repeats the same adjustment (procedures 7-1 and 7-4, tighten the M8 x 7T nut on the J-bolt) and says a belt that falls off is refitted by procedure 7; a worn idler-wheel bearing is replaced by procedure 8-1 (the induction brake card).
