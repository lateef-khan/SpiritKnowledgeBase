**This is the 2012 CU800 (XU878), which has an induction brake, not the 2020 CU800's generator flywheel.**

1. Follow procedures 5 and 7 to release the left and right chain covers and the belt. Release **3 pcs of M6 x 15 mm screws, Ø1/4" spring washers and 1/4" x 13 mm x 1T flat washers** on the **idler wheel assembly**. Take the idler wheel assembly and the **J-bolt** off.
2. Disconnect the **wire brake coil harness** and use **two 11 mm wrenches** to release **4 pcs of hex head bolts** together with **Ø1/4" spring washers and Ø1/4" flat washers** that secure the induction brake.
3. To refit, install the induction brake onto the main frame by laying it on the floor, and secure it with **4 pcs of 1/4" x 3/4" hex head bolts, Ø1/4" spring washers and Ø1/4" flat washers** using the 11 mm wrench. Install the J-bolt on the main frame, join the idler wheel assembly to the induction brake and secure with the 3 pcs of M6 x 15 mm screws, Ø1/4" spring washers and Ø1/4" flat washers. Follow procedures 7-3 and 5-3 to refit the belt (170 +/- 10 Hz) and the covers.

The book's own troubleshooting says the induction brake itself rarely fails: with no resistance, take the chain covers off and check that the wire brake coil harness is connected and unbroken before replacing anything. The idler wheel assembly's bearing is the part that usually fails, and it comes off in step 1 of this procedure.
