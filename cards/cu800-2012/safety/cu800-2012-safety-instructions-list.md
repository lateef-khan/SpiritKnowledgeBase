**This manual prints eight bullets, three fewer than every later Spirit bike manual.**

> WARNING - Read all instructions before using this appliance.
>
> - Before beginning this or any exercise program, consult a physician. This is especially
>   important for persons over the age of 35 or persons with pre-existing health conditions.
> - Keep children away from the fitness bike. There are obvious pinch points and other caution
>   areas that can cause harm.
> - Keep hands away from all moving parts.
> - Never drop or insert any object into any openings.
> - Do not use outdoors.
> - Do not attempt to use your fitness bike for any purpose other than for the purpose it is
>   intended.
> - The hand pulse sensors are not medical devices. Various factors, including the user's
>   movement, may affect the accuracy of heart rate readings. The pulse sensors are intended only
>   as exercise aids in determining heart rate trends in general.
> - Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable for use on
>   your fitness bike. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue.
>
> SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS - THINK SAFETY!

## What this list leaves out, and what that means for an answer

- **No maximum user weight.** The words *lb*, *lbs*, *capacity* and *weight limit* appear nowhere
  in the manual; every occurrence of *weight* is the body weight keyed into the console for a
  program. Do not carry the 450 lbs figure the later bike manuals print across to this machine -
  see `xt-2015-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`.
- **No age limit and no reduced-capability rule.** The `Keep children under the age of 13` and
  `not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities` bullets
  arrive with the 2018 manuals (`spirit-ce-safety-who-may-use-the-elliptical`). This manual says
  only *keep children away*.
- **No California Proposition 65 warning.** The word *California* does not appear in the manual,
  in either scan. The Toluene and Acrylamide box arrives with the 2018 manuals
  (`xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`).

Each of these is an absence, not a zero and not a permission.

**Both OCR scans in the source agree.** The source file is two scans of the one book concatenated;
the safety page appears twice and the two readings match bullet for bullet, so none of the three
omissions is a scanning loss. The warranty page of both scans is *effective March 1, 2012* and both
carry the internal stamp `CU800_20130729`.

**The bike is self-powered**, so no outlet, plug or grounding rule is printed either
(`spirit-bike-safety-no-mains-outlet-needed`).
