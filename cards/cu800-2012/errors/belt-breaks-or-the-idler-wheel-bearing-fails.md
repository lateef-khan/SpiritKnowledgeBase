Section 8-11, `The belt and idler wheel assembly`:

1. **The belt wears out eventually and may break or go out of tension.** Replace the belt by following procedure 7-1 in case this happens.
2. **It is usually a defective bearing that causes the problem with the Idler Wheel Assembly.** Replace the bearing by following procedure 8-1.

Two diagnoses, both pointing at a replacement procedure in the same book. The belt that merely slips is the next-but-one section (`cu800-2012-maintenance-belt-slip-170-hz`); a noise from the covers is 8-10 and 8-13 (`cu800-2012-errors-noises-crank-or-pulley-rubbing-the-chain-covers-or-foreign-material`).

**The procedure numbers are the parts procedures, not the Q&A sections.** "Procedure 7-1" is step 1 of parts procedure 7, `The Belt and Drive Pulley` (section 8-7 of the book), which loosens the M8 x 7T nut on the J-bolt and takes the belt off; "procedure 8-1" is step 1 of procedure 8, `Induction Brake` (section 8-8), which is the step that takes the idler wheel assembly and J-bolt apart. The refitted belt is set to **170 +/- 10 Hz** in procedure 7 step 4 - ten hertz below the 180 the XR898 and the 2020 books print.
