Section 8-15, `Smoothness`, in full:

1. **If the Crank clicks**, follow disassembling and assembling procedure 4 to **tighten the nut on the Crank**.
2. **If the pedal falls**, use a **15 mm wrench** to tighten it.

That is the book's whole answer to a rough or clicking pedal stroke. The CU800 2020-book folds the same two items into its noise list with worn pedals added (`spirit-upright-errors-noises-pedals-crank-then-chain-covers-or-drive-pulley`); this book keeps noise (8-10, 8-13 - `cu800-2012-errors-noises-crank-or-pulley-rubbing-the-chain-covers-or-foreign-material`) and smoothness apart. The CR900 books check the same clicking in a longer order - pedals, crank arm bolts, axle play, bearing nuts (`spirit-cr900-errors-clicking-or-loose-feeling-at-the-pedals`).
