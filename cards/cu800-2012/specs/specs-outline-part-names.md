**p. 5, the shrouded bike:** Handgrip Side Cap, Console Assembly, Seat, Drink Bottle Holder,
**Fore Aft Adjustment Knob**, **Seat Up/Down Adjustment Knob**, Seat Post Cover, Console Mast
Cover, Round Disk, Chain Cover, Pedal, Front Stabilizer Cover, Rear Stabilizer Cover.

**p. 6, the frame:** Seat Handlebar, Console Mast, **Inner Slide**, **Seat Slide Bracket**, Idler
Wheel Assembly, **Fix Plate**, Crank, Drive Pulley, **Induction Brake**, Main Frame, Rear Stabilizer.

So this upright has a **fore-aft seat adjustment** as well as up-down, which the 2020-version
CU800 outline (`cu800-2021-specs-outline-part-names`) does not label, and the resistance unit is
drawn as an **Induction Brake** even though the electrical pages call it a generator flywheel -
see `cu800-2012-specs-electrical-configuration-and-display-board-leads`.

No dimensions or weights are printed on either page.
