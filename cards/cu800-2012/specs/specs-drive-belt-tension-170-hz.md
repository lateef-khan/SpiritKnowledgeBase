**170 ±10 Hz** on an acoustic tension gauge - so 160 to 180 Hz.

Set it after the drive pulley is back on the crank axle: tighten the **M8 x 7T nut on the J-bolt
with a 13 mm wrench** until the idler wheel loads the belt, read the gauge, then tighten the three
M6 x 15 mm screws on the idler wheel assembly. The slippage answer (8-14) simply sends you back to
this step.

**This is the lowest figure in the family.** The 2020-version CU800 and CR800 and both ENT-800
books print **180 to 205 Hz** (`spirit-800-specs-drive-belt-tension-180-to-205-hz-audio-gauge`),
and the recumbents with the same 031101B controller print **180 ±10 Hz**
(`spirit-xbr95-specs-drive-belt-tension-180-hz-acoustic-gauge`). The book prints it once and
gives no gauge part number.
