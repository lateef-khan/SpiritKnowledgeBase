**Upper Controllers (p. 8)** - **Cooling FAN**, **Thumb Switch** and **DISPLAY** on the console, and **EMS
BRAKE** - a photograph of the flywheel with its coil pack, called out on the whole-bike drawing inside the
shroud. The brake is drawn under "Upper Controllers" because the page draws the entire machine and labels
the console parts and the brake on it together.

**Lower Controller and Driver (p. 9)** - **SPEED RPM SENSOR** and **CONTROLLER**, photographed inside the
shroud.

**"EMS BRAKE" is this book's name for the self-powered generator brake.** The same book's Electrical
Configurations page calls it a **GENERATOR FLYWHEEL** and its wiring pages label it **GENERATOR**; the 2020
CU800 book, which names the same six parts, labels it **GENERATOR BRAKE**
(`spirit-cr800-cu800-2020-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`). Which commercial bikes carry a generator and
which an induction brake is on `spirit-bike-specs-generator-brake-or-induction-brake`.

**The Electrical Configurations page of this book describes "the Elliptical"** - "Interface that controls
all functions of the Elliptical ... the elliptical functions" - on an upright-bike page; the text is carried
over from an elliptical book and the chapter-2 pages are the bike's own.

**This book prints no tool list**, unlike the 2020 CU800 book (`spirit-bike-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`).
No part number or rating is printed on either page.
