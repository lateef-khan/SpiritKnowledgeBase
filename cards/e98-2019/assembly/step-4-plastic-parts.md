Use the supplied Short and standard Phillips drivers; the book names drivers (185) and (186), which are E35 tools-list numbers, not this book's (169-172).

1. **Console Mast Covers (83 & 83-1)** on the Console Mast (12) with **four Sheet Metal Screws (132)**.
2. **Inner covers (97 & 98)** on the Left and Right Connecting Arms (8 & 9), each side with **two (129)** and **two (132)**. **Outer covers (95 & 96)** on the left and right swing arms the same way, the outer screws being Self Tapping (132). Arrows on the stickers decide the orientation.
3. **Left (91 & 92) and right (93 & 94) swing arm covers** with **eight Self Tapping Screws (132)**.
4. **Sliding Wheel Covers (22)**, each side with **four Phillips Head Screws (129)**.
5. **Stabilizer Covers (101 & 102)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (129)**.
6. **Front Stabilizer Cover (90)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (129)**.
7. **Incline Cover brackets (20)**, bent-tab hole facing rearward, with **four Phillips Head Screws (131)**; **Rear incline bar cover (100)** with **two Screws (129)**.
8. **Rear Stabilizer Cover (24)** with **four Phillips Head Screws (129)**.
9. **After the elliptical power is plugged in, run the incline to position 8** and install the **Incline Rail front cover (99)** up against the middle stabilizer tube with **four Phillips Head Screws (129)**.
10. **Tablet Holder (81)** on the back of the console with **four Sheet Metal Screws (132)**.
