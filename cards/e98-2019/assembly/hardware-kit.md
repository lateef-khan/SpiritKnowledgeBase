The hardware is bagged per assembly step. Numbers in parentheses are assembly-drawing item numbers.

**Hardware Step 1**

| Item | Description | Qty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #115 | 5/16" x 2-1/4" Hex Head Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #168 | O5/16" Star Washer | 4 pcs |
| #117 | 3/8" x 1-1/2" Hex Head Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #118 | 3/8" x 3/4" Hex Head Bolt | 4 pc |
| #128 | M5 x 10mm Phillips Head Screw | 6 pcs |
| #145 | 3/8" x 7T Nyloc Nut | 2 pcs |
| #154 | 5/16" x 20mm x 1.5T Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| #158 | 3/8" x 19mm x 1.5T Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #166 | O5/16" x 1.5T Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| #167 | O3/8" x 2T Split Washer | 4 pc |

The checklist draws and labels #118 a Hex Head Bolt, but the step-1 text fits "four Socket Head Cap Bolts (118)" with the L Allen wrench. The drawing plus the pack list wins over the step sentence, so this card lists a Hex Head Bolt and the step card carries the warning.
