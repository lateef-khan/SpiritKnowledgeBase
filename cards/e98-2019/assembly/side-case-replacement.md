1. Remove the swing arms and pedal arms first.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **M5x15mm Phillips head screws** to take apart the front stabilizer cover.
3. Release **9 pcs of 3.5x16 self tapping screws** and **3 pcs of 5x16 x 3 tapping screws** to take the right side case apart.
4. For the left side case, release the **3.5x16 self tapping screws with 1/4" x 19 flat washers** on the main frame and **3 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws**, then release the left side case.
5. On reinstallation, connect the red jumpers and the grounding back on the AC electronic module.
6. Return the left side case, with one **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** on it, onto the main frame. Use a 3.5x16 self tapping screw with a 1/4" x 19 flat washer to hold it, not too tight at first, then tighten with 3 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws, making sure the side case matches the round disk. Tighten the 4x15 self tapping screws.
7. Match both side cases with each other and use **9 pcs of 3.5x16 self tapping screws** and **3 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws** to secure them.
8. Reverse the above to refit the pedal arm and connecting arm.

Note: step 4 in this manual does not mention disconnecting the red jumpers, but step 5 tells you to connect them back. The E25, E35 and E55 manuals print the disconnect instruction, with a warning to mark the jumpers first.
