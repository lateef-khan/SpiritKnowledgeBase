1. **Install the Incline Rail Assembly (2) into the U-channel bracket of the main frame (1).** From the sides install **two Hex Head Bolts (117)** with **two Flat Washers (158)** and **two Nylon Nuts (145)**. From the top install **four Hex Head Bolts (115)**, **four Split Washers (166)**, **four Flat Washers (154)**, and **four Star Washers (168)**. Tighten with **Wrenches (169 & 172)**.
2. **Connect the three Incline Motor Power lines by matching the Red, White and Black color codes of the wires (43)** and the **3-pin position Sensor Connector (44)**. Slide the **Console Mast Cover (83)** onto the **Console Mast (12)**. Tie the twist tie to the **Computer Cable (45)**, pull it up through the mast, and install the mast into the receiving bracket, pulling slightly on the cable at the top so it does not get pinched and shorted.
3. **Install four Socket Head Cap Bolts (118) with four Split Washers (167) and four Flat Washers (158)** and tighten with the **L Allen Wrench (213)**, pulling slightly on the cable at the top.

The checklist draws and labels #118 a Hex Head Bolt while this step says socket cap and calls for the L Allen wrench. The drawing plus the pack list wins; treat the bolt as drawn and keep the Allen key handy in case the bag follows the step text.
4. **Plug the Computer Cable (45), two Hand pulse Cables (52 & 56), the Ground Wire (192), and the Resistance & Incline wires (184 & 185)** into the back of the console. Secure the **Console (40)** with **four Phillips Head Screws (128)**, tightened with driver (170 or 171).
5. **Install the Bottle Holder (82) on the Console Mast (12) with Phillips Head Screws (128)** using driver (171). **The bottle holder must be assembled before console assembly.**
