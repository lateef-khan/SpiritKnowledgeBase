The manual heads this section "11-8. Flywheel & Poly-V Belt Replacement", but its steps are the idler wheel and the belt tension. The flywheel itself is section 11-9.

1. Follow the procedures to take apart the cross bar.
2. Use a **13 mm wrench** to loosen the **M8 x 9T nut on the J-bolt** together with the **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** until the J-bolt is released. With the same wrench release the **M8 x 20 carriage bolt** securing the idler wheel assembly, together with the **5/16" x 20 x 1.5T flat washer** and **M8 x 7T nut**, and take the idler wheel assembly apart.
3. Follow the above procedures in reverse and **tighten the M8 nyloc nut on the J-bolt until the belt generates a sound wave of 190 Hz (+/- 10)**.
