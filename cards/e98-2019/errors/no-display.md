1. If there is no display, follow these steps for diagnosis.
2. Make sure the **AC power switch is turned on**.
3. If necessary, **replace the fuse of the AC power switch module**.
4. Remove the console cover and the left and right chain covers. Check that all wires are inserted in the correct positions and check whether any wires are broken.
