**190 Hz, plus or minus 10 Hz.** The manual writes it as "until the Belt generate sound wave 190HZ(+/-10)".

Set it by tightening the **M8 nyloc nut on the J-bolt** after the idler wheel assembly is back in place.

The manual prints this figure once, inside the flywheel and Poly-V belt replacement procedure. The belt slipping entry in the troubleshooting section does not repeat it.
