This is a **115 volt AC motor**. It has four wires: red, black, white and green, plus one 3-pin position sensor cable.

- AC voltage on the **red** wire (UP) makes the incline motor increase the incline.
- AC voltage on the **black** wire (DOWN) makes the incline motor decrease the incline.
- The **white** wire (COM) is neutral.
- The **green** wire is ground.

**Zeroing distance for a replacement motor: 207 +/- 1mm** between the two hole sites, reached by rotating the incline barrel clockwise to the end then counterclockwise one and a half circles.
