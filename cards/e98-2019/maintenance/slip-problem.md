The manual lists **four situations which cause slippage**.

1. **M12 x P1.75 rod end (fish-eye) bearing** - the M12 nut on top must be tightened enough to the connecting arm and perpendicular to the M12 x P1.75 rod end bearing.
2. **Bushing housing** - the 3/8" x 2-1/4" socket head cap screw and 3/8" x 11T nut on top must be tight enough, **with 500~600 lbs of force suggested**.
3. **Cross bar** - the M8x40 m/m socket head cap screw on the cross bar must be tight enough, **with 550~600 lbs of force suggested**.
4. **Belt** - this appears quite often, because after a period of time most belts become loose from use. The solution depends on weight and adjustment method; use the belt disassembly procedure.
