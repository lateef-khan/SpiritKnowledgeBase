Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. This is the CR800 chassis
with a touchscreen: the item numbers and the hardware are the CR800 2023 numbers, and
the steps are rewritten. Hardware quantities are on
[the hardware card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-cr800-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Step one - rear stabilizer and seat back bracket

1. Install the **Rear Stabilizer (No.7)** onto the Main Frame with 2 Bolts (No.65) and
   2 Washers (No.203).
2. Secure the **Seat Back Bracket (No.5)** to the **Seat Carriage (No.4)** using 6
   Bolts (No.176) and 6 Flat Washers (No.77).

## Step two - rear handlebar and hand pulse cables

1. To install the **Rear Handlebar (No.6)** to the Seat Carriage (No.4) use 2 Bolts
   (No.71), 2 Flat Washers (No.77) and 2 Nylon Nuts (No.89). **From the side** secure
   with 2 Bolts (No.175), 2 Flat Washers (No.77) and 2 Nylon Nuts (No.89).
2. Connect the **Handpulse with Cable Assembly-R (No.21)** and the **Handpulse with
   Cable Assembly-L (No.27)** to the **Handpulse Wire (No.26)** and the **Handpulse
   Wire (No.42)**.

**This says two top bolts where the CR800 2021 and 2023 books say four**, on the same
joint with the same item numbers. The hardware table here lists 2 of No.71 and 4 nylon
nuts, which agrees with two plus two; the older books' step text asks for four plus
two against the same table. Item 2 also does not say which handpulse cable goes to
which wire.

## Step three - console mast and front handlebar

1. Feed the **Console Mast Cover (No.31)** onto the **Console Mast (No.2)**.
2. Thread the **Wires (No. 44, 45, 204, 192, 186)** through the Console Mast (No.2) -
   the computer cable, the handpulse wire, the CSAFE connecting wire, the network
   connecting cable and the HDMI connecting wire.
3. Secure the Console Mast (No.2) to the **Main Frame (No.1)** using **2 Bolts (No.68)
   and 2 Curved Washers (No.83) from the back** of the mast. On the sides use **4 Bolts
   (No.68) and 4 Flat Washers (No.76)**.
4. Connect the **Front Handlebar (No.3)** to the Console Mast (No.2) using 2 Bolts
   (No.94), 2 Flat Washers (No.76) and 2 Split Washers (No.82).

This is the step that tells you where each of the six mast bolts goes; the CR800 books
say only "on the side bolts" and "on the front bolts". Item 4 uses **No.94**, as the
CR800 2023 book does and the 2021 book does not - see
[the front handlebar bolt card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-cr800-assembly-front-handlebar-bolt-change.md).

## Step four - seat, console, cup holders and pedals

1. Secure the **Seat (No.61)** to the Seat Carriage (No.4) using 4 Screws (No.98) with
   the Philips Screwdriver (No.114).
2. Connect the wires on the back of the console to their places: Computer Cable
   (No.44), Handpulse Wire (No.45), CSAFE Connecting Wire (No.204), Network Connecting
   Cable (No.192), HDMI Connecting Wire (No.186). Tuck excess cable into the Console
   Mast (No.2), careful not to pinch the wires.
3. Secure the **Console (No.19)** to the Console Mast (No.2) using 4 Screws (No.99)
   with the Screwdriver (No.114).
4. Fasten the **Drink Bottle Holders (No.39/38)** to the Rear Handlebar (No.6) using 4
   Screws (No.105).
5. Install the **Pedals (No.116L/116R)** onto the **Crank Arms (No. 51L/51R)** carefully
   using the Wrench (No.132) to hold the pedal pivot, then firmly fasten the pivot
   clockwise.

**Item 5 prints no reverse-thread warning and no torque figure**, where the CR800 books
on the same chassis print the warning. See
[the pedal card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-bike-ent-assembly-pedal-no-thread-warning.md).

## This is a rewrite of the CR800 build, not a different build

The two chapters share **50 to 53%** of their words, but every item number, every
hardware table and the order of the four steps are the same. What changed is the
wording, the five cables in place of two, and the seat moving from the middle of step
four to the start of it. Do not take a bolt count from a CR800 card without checking
the difference above.

The build ends here. There is no first-power-on step: these bikes are
generator-powered and the console starts when you pedal.
