Eight labels, printed on a photograph with no heading and no letters:

- **CONSOLE**
- **CONSOLE FAN**
- **SEAT BACKPAD**
- **FLYWHEEL**
- **PEDALS**
- **HAND PULSE SENSORS**
- **CUP HOLDER**
- **REAR STABILIZER**

The page carries no title, no item numbers and no key. It is a photograph of a finished
machine with eight callout lines, and the text under them is set as a flat image - it
comes out of the manual only through OCR.

## Three of the eight are not on the upright photograph

The CU800ENT 2022 page uses the same layout and the same eight-label count but names
**SEAT** and **FRONT STABILIZER** where this names **SEAT BACKPAD** and **FLYWHEEL**,
and it has no flywheel to point at. Five labels are common to both. See
[that card](../../cu800ent-2022/assembly/cu800ent-2022-assembly-parts-diagram-labels.md).

**FLYWHEEL** and **SEAT BACKPAD** are used only here in the whole manual. No assembly
step mentions either; the steps call the backpad the Seat Back Bracket and the Seat
Back Frame, and the carton list calls it the Seat Back Frame.

## The non-touchscreen printings label thirteen, on a drawing rather than a photograph

The CR800 2021 and 2023 manuals put a line drawing on the same page with **thirteen**
labels, adding **FRONT HANDLEBARS**, **CONSOLE MAST**, **CONSOLE MAST COVER**, **CRANK
ARMS**, **FRONT STABILIZER** and **MAIN FRAME**, calling the grips **PULSE GRIPS**, and
labelling neither the flywheel nor the seat backpad. See
[that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-cr800-assembly-parts-diagram-labels.md).

## The diagram is not a carton list

Nothing on this page tells you what is packed loose. For that see
[the parts card](cr800ent-2023-assembly-parts-included.md).
