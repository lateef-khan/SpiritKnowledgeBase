One drawing, thirteen labels: **Handle Bar**, **Seat**, **Console**, **Seat Back**, **Console
Mast**, **Console Mast Cover**, **Drink Bottle Holder**, **Handle Pulse Sensor**, **Rear Shroud**,
**Pedal**, **Rear Stabilizer**, **Moving Wheel**, **Front Shroud**.

The *Handle Pulse Sensor* is the grip on the seat handle bar, and the *Handle Bar* at the top of
the list is the front bar beside the console. Left and right are not distinguished on this
drawing; the replacement chapter numbers them (Front Shroud 29 and 30, Rear Shroud 35 and 36).

No skeleton drawing, dimensions or weights are printed.
