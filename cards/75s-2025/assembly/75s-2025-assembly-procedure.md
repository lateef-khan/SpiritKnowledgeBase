Numbers in parentheses are assembly-drawing item numbers.

## Step one - handle bar, gas shock, pedals, end caps, wheelchair anchors and seat

1. Slide the **handle bar assembly (17)** onto the receiving tubes of the seat frame and
   secure with **two 3/8" x 3-1/4" bolts (140), four 3/8" flat washers (170) and two
   3/8" nylon nuts (190)**. **Install the safety cover (108) and seat belts (233) onto
   the bolts before the washers and nuts.** Then install **four 3/8" x 2" bolts (132)**
   from the top of the tubes and secure with **eight 3/8" flat washers (170) and four
   3/8" nylon nuts (190)**.
2. Attach the **seat back gas shock** to the seat back angle adjustment bracket of the
   handle bar assembly with **one 5/16" x 1-1/4" bolt (135), two 5/16" flat washers (173)
   and a 5/16" nylon nut (193)**.
3. Assemble the **pedals (28)** to the pedal foot plates with **six M5 Phillips screws
   (181) per side**.
4. Press the **two stabilizer end caps (31)** into the stabilizer tubes.
5. Install the **two wheelchair anchor assemblies (235)** with **3/8" x 3/4" bolts (216)
   and M10 x 30 bolts (258)** into the **outside holes in the top of the frame tube**,
   with **3/8" flat washers (217) and 3/8" split washers (194) on the top of the tube**
   and **3/8" curved washers (218) on the front of the tube**.
6. Install the **seat assembly (18)** with **3/8" x 2-1/2" bolts (178)**, using **3/8"
   flat washers (217) and 3/8" split washers (194) on the top of the tube**, **3/8" flat
   washers (217) and a 3/8" nut (255) on the bottom of the tube**, and **3/8" x 3-3/4"
   bolts (206) with 3/8" curved washers (218) on the front of the tube**.

**Two end caps, not four.** The 7.0S and the MS300 both press four into the stabilizer
tubes; this machine presses two, because two of its tube ends carry a wheelchair anchor
instead.

**Items 5 and 6 exist on no other machine in this corpus.** The wheelchair anchor is
call-out **J** on the parts diagram, and the machine's own feature list says the anchor
"incorporates a strap that secures the wheelchair at the wheel or frame member".

## Step two - transport wheels, console mast and handle sliders

1. Install the **transport wheels (77)** with **5/16" x 1-3/4" bolts (152)** and **5/16"
   nylon nuts (193)**.
2. Slide the **console mast cover (74)** onto the **console mast (2)** the right way
   round.
3. Snake the computer cable through the mast onto the receiving brackets without
   pinching it.
4. Fasten the mast with **two 3/8" x 2-1/2" bolts (154), 3/8" split washers (194) and
   3/8" flat washers (170) from the left side**, secured with **two 3/8" nylon nuts
   (190)**, then **two 8mm x 16mm bolts (153) and curved washers (184)** through the
   front and rear holes.
5. Secure the **Handle Sliders (244)** using **six M8 x 30mm bolts (196) and six M8 nuts
   (160) on each side**.

**The hardware page supplies six of each, not twelve.** It prints **#196. M8 x 30L
(6pcs)** and **#160. M8 x 6.5T (6pcs)**. Read item 5 as six in all, three a side, and
count the bag first. The 7.0S prints the same sentence against the same six, under
different item numbers - 309 and 313 there, 196 and 160 here.

## Step three - seat back and cushion

1. Slide the **seat back assembly (94)** into the seat back angle adjustment bracket and
   secure with **two 3/8" x 1-3/4" bolts (197), 3/8" washers (167) and 3/8" nuts (190)**.
2. Assemble the **seat cushion (93)** onto the seat frame with **four M6 bolts (122)**.

## Step four - console and arms

1. Connect the computer cable into the back of the console and install the **console
   (29)** with **four 5mm x 12mm screws (143)**.
2. Install the **left and right arms (3 & 4)** with **3/8" x 3/4" bolts (216)**, using
   **3/8" flat washers (217) and 3/8" split washers (194)** on the sides of the arms and
   **3/8" curved washers (218)** on the front and rear. **Tighten very securely.**

## The 7.5S numbers its split washer 194 where the 7.0S numbers it 243

Both books call the part a 3/8" split washer and both use twelve of them. On the 7.0S the
item number is **243**; on the 7.5S it is **194**, and **194 is a 3/8" split washer on
the 7.0S too, at two pieces in step two**. Order by description, not by number, across
the two machines.

## Two optional parts the manual names and never fits

The parts diagram lists, as **optional parts (not shown)**, a **lower extremity
bi-lateral stabilization set (2.2)** and a **hand/wrist stabilization set (2.3)**. No
step fits either and no other page mentions them.
