| Figure | Printed |
|---|---|
| Length | **77.6" (197 cm)** |
| Width | **33.5" (85 cm)** |
| Height | **46.5" (118.2 cm)** |
| Weight | **305.8 lbs. (139 kg)** |

Every pair converts correctly. **No shipping weight, no gross weight and no
carton size** is printed in the 56-page manual.

**This is the heaviest and longest of the three dot-matrix rehabilitation
steppers** - 48 lbs heavier than the 7.0S and 10.6 inches longer than the MS300,
while being an inch and a half lower than either. It shares the 7.0S's width
exactly, at 33.5" (85 cm), so width alone will not tell the two apart.

**Do not confuse this machine with the Dyaco-branded 7.5S.** There is a separate
rehabilitation manual for a 7.5S that says "Dyaco" throughout and never says
"Spirit". It is a different document about a different product and none of its
figures belong here.

## The user weight limit is a different figure

The same page prints **Patient weight capacity 440 Lbs. (200 Kgs.)**. That is a
safety limit and lives in the `safety` section, not here.
