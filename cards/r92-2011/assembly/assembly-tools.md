| Item | Tool |
|---|---|
| #112 | 12/14mm Wrench |
| #114 | Phillips Head Screwdriver |
| #115 | Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver |
| #132 | 14/15mm Wrench |

**Four tools.** The R92 2013 and every later R92 drops #115 and ships only three. #115 is needed here because the 2011 R92 is the only year whose step 4 asks you to remove and refit the M6 button head socket screws that clamp the Seat Adjustment Lever (40).

**Tool item numbers are not shared between models.** The same `#` is a different part on another Sole bike, so use the list printed in your own machine's manual.
