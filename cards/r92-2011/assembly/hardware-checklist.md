Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #65 | 3/8" x 2-1/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #77 | 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #84 | 3/8" x 25 x 2T | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| #176 | 3/8" x 3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 6 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #71 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #77 | 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| #89 | 3/8" x 7T | Nyloc Nut | 4 pcs |
| #175 | 3/8" x 2-3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 3**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #68 | 5/16" x 5/8" | Hex Head Bolt | 8 pcs |
| #76 | 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #82 | 5/16" x 1.5T | Split Washer | 2 pcs |
| #83 | 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T | Curved Washer | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 4**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #98 | **M5 x 15mm** | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| #99 | M5 x 12mm | Phillips Head Screw | 8 pcs |
| #105 | 4 x 16mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |

**#98 is M5 x 15mm in this year only.** Every later R92 and every LCR manual calls #98 an **M6 x 15mm** Phillips head screw for the same job (fixing the seat to the seat carriage). If the four seat screws in a 2011 box do not thread, this is why.

**Item numbers are not shared with the B94 or LCB uprights.** Here `#71` is a hex head bolt and `#99` is a Phillips head screw; on the uprights `#71` is a flat washer and `#99` is a curved washer. Always use the checklist printed in your own machine's manual.
