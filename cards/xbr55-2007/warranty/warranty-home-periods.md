**The XBR55 column of the 2008 dealer manual's warranty table:**

| Component | Home use |
|---|---|
| Labor | 2 Years |
| Frame weldments | Lifetime |
| Brake | Lifetime |
| All Other Components | 5 Years |

**Labor is two years - twice the XBR25's figure in the same table, and twice what every later Spirit XB
bike manual prints.** The XBR25 column reads 1 Year (`xbr25-2007-warranty-home-periods`); the thirteen
XB owner's manuals from 2016 to 2023 all print Labor 1 Year (`spirit-xb-warranty-residential-periods`),
and their later labour clause is dated from purchase or shipping in the same two-date way as this book's
base term (`xt-2023-warranty-labor-period`). Nothing on the page explains the difference.

**"All Other Components" is 5 years, not the 10 years of the later manuals.** The XBR55's 2019, 2021 and
2023 owner's manuals print Frame Lifetime, Brake Lifetime, Parts 10 Years, Labor 1 Year
(`spirit-xb-warranty-residential-periods`); this book gives the 2007 machine half the parts period and
double the labour period. Quote from the book that came with the machine.

**The page is headed BIKE WARRANTY, but the table of contents calls it "Treadmill Warranty".** The contents
page on PDF p. 2 lists `Treadmill Warranty ... 8` under "XBR Series Warranty"; the page itself, PDF p. 8,
is headed BIKE WARRANTY and warrants "all its bike parts". The contents entry is a copy from the treadmill
dealer book of the same year.

**Home use is the only column.** The table is headed "Home use" and prints no commercial column, so this
manual gives the bike no commercial cover in writing at all; the dealer policy on the next page excludes
"residential units in a commercial setting" outright
(`spirit-xt-2007-warranty-what-the-dealer-policy-does-not-cover`).

The base term every row sits on - parts 5 years from retail sale or 18 months from factory shipping,
whichever comes first, administered by Spirit directly to the consumer only where there is no dealer - is
on `spirit-xt-2007-warranty-five-years-or-eighteen-months-from-shipping`, and what Spirit pays the dealer
for the labour year is on `spirit-xt-2007-warranty-labor-reimbursement-80-dollar-flat-fee`.
