These are the callout numbers on the **XBR55 Exploded View** on PDF page 16 - a drawing printed sideways on a
portrait page and stamped **960905AM1138** in small type at its edge. The drawing is a picture with bare
numbers on it, so the table below is the only readable form of it. Every row was read from the native text
layer of the 2008 dealer manual; none of the four list pages needed an OCR supplement.

**Three columns, no quantity.** The list prints `Parts No.`, `Description` and `Part #` only - it never says
how many of an item the machine uses. A part number is a six-digit Spirit number. **`X` in the part-number
column is printed on the page**; the book does not say what it means. Sub-items are written with a tilde
(`19~1`, `27~3`) and paired parts carry a letter (`51L`, `51R`).

**The page prints a small `˜` where a diameter sign belongs.** Rows such as `˜32 (1.8T)_End Cap` and
`˜20_C Clip` are set with that glyph on the printed page (checked on a 200 dpi render), not with the `Ø`
that the later Dyaco books use. It is kept here as printed.

**151 rows are printed**, 148 numbered and 3 without a number at the foot of the list (Hardware Kit 002184,
Console Overlay 006198, Button Overlay 006199 - the same two overlays the XBR25 list carries, under a
different hardware kit). The whole item numbers that appear are 1-20, 22, 24-40, 43-44, 46-48, 51-61,
63-73, 75-80, 82-91, 93, 95-99, 101-127, 129-131, 133-134, 140-141 and 143-153; any number outside that run
is not printed for this machine at all. One row carries `X` (19~11) and **seventeen numbered rows carry no
part number**: 1 (Main Frame), 10 (Idler Wheel Assembly), 13, 19~15, 19~16, 27, 78, 87 (J Bolt), 115 (L Allen
Wrench), 125, 126, 127, 130, 143, 149, 152 and 153. The XBR25 list prints numbers for the main frame
(002160), the idler wheel assembly (002159), the J bolt (002083), the seat carriage cover (002152) and the
grommet (002060); whether those numbers also fit the XBR55 is not something this book says.

**Three rows carry a second number inside the description.** Row 4 reads `Seat Carriage Assy #002208`
against 002187, row 9 reads `Seat Wheel Adjustment Plate Assy# 002017` against 002007, and row 19~3 reads
`SPIRIT LOGO/P020029` against 002131. The `Assy #` numbers are the assemblies the bare part goes into; the
XBR25 list prints 002017 as its seat wheel assembly.

**Five rows are tools, not bike parts** - 112 (12/14mm Wrench 030036), 113 (13/15mm Wrench 030035), 114
(Phillips Head Screw Driver 023091), 115 (L Allen Wrench, no number) and 130 (13/14mm Wrench, no number).
They are the tools packed with the machine. The dealer manual prints no other tool list; see
`spirit-bike-specs-parts-which-service-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.

**What this list has that the XBR25 list does not** is the recline seat and the handlebar resistance
buttons: Gas Cylinder 002151 (57), Seat Recline Release Cable 002045 (58), the 5/16" hex screws at 69 and
70, Pedal Foam (L) and (R) at 118 and 119, Handlebar Rubber End Cap 002162 (144), XBR Handle Switches 002161
(145), the "Up" and "Down" labels 002204 and 002205 (146, 147) and the three Adjustment Switch cables at 148,
150 and 151 (149, the switch with earphone cable, has no number).

## Do not read this list against the XBR25 list

The XBR25 exploded view is a different drawing (stamp 960907AM1130). 69 rows are identical on both lists;
17 item numbers appear only here (38, 57, 58, 69, 70, 113, 118, 119, 130, 146, 147, 148, 149, 150, 151, 152, 153); 3 appear only on the XBR25 list
(23, 132, 142); and 45 shared item numbers carry a different description or part number.
The full row-by-row comparison is printed on `xbr25-2007-specs-parts-list`. The three to watch: **pedals**
are 002053 and 002054 here but 002105 and 002106 on the XBR25; **item 140** is a Sensor Rack 022989 here
and a Sensor Bracket 002113 there; **items 144 and 145** are washers on the XBR25 list and the handlebar end
cap and switches here.
