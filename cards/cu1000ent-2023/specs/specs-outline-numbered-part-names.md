One drawing with an item table beside it; items 1 to 3 share one leader on the screen.

| No. | Description | No. | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Screen Cover(Top) | 11 | Cap |
| 2 | Screen Cover(Bottom) | 12 | Console Mast Cover-L |
| 3 | End Cap | 13 | Console Mast Cover-R |
| 4 | Keyboard | 14 | Console Mast Cover |
| 5 | Console Chin Cover (Front) | 15 | Electronic Module |
| 6 | Console Chin Cover(Rear) | 16 | Beverage Holder |
| 7 | Side Case (L) | 17 | Front Seat Cover |
| 8 | Sider Case (R) (spelt so) | 18 | Rear Seat Cover |
| 9 | Front Stabilizer Cover | 19 | Pedal |
| 10 | Rear Stabilizer Cover | 20 | Release Lever |

These are cover and trim names, not a parts list: **no part numbers and no quantities** are
printed, and the book has no exploded view. The **Electronic Module (15)** is the box on the
right-hand side of the frame behind the seat post, where the circuit diagram puts the
controller. No dimensions or weights appear on the page.
