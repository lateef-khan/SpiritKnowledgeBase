**2.1 Console (p. 5)** names two things on the 15.6-inch console: **DISPLAY**, and **NFC log in & Shortcut
key** - one callout for the NFC reader and the row of physical keys below the screen. No fan, speaker or
earphone port is labelled on this console.

**2.2 Controller and Driver parts (p. 6)** names three: **SPEED SENSOR** (a photograph of the reed switch
beside the magnet), **HYBRID GENERATOR** (the flywheel with its coil pack), and **Driver board** (a bare red
circuit board), all called out on a photograph of the upright with its shroud on.

**This is the only Spirit bike service manual that names an NFC reader.** The CU900ENT and CU900 books name
Cooling FAN and DISPLAY on the console and the same three lower parts
(`cu900ent-electronic-parts-locations`); the CU1000ENT drops the fan callout and adds the NFC key.

**No part number, rating or dimension is printed on either page.** The generator's role and the driver
board's connections are on the specs cards for chapters 3 and 6 of this book.
