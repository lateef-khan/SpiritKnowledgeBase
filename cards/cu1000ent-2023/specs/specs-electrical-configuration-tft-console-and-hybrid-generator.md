**Section 3 names three parts** (the four the chapter-2 photographs name, NFC pad included, are on
`cu1000ent-2023-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`):

- **Console** - the interface that controls all functions of the upright bike; key controls and a
  **TFT display**, which section 4 sizes at **15.6"**.
- **Main Controller** - "the circuit board consists of the DC power supply for console", plus the
  driver control circuit.
- **EMS Flywheel** - increases or decreases the resistance level of the brake.

**The photo says "hybrid generator", the text says "EMS flywheel", and the circuit diagram feeds
the bike from a 100 W adapter.** The unit is an electromagnetic brake on a flywheel, driven from
the board's MAG-BRAKE output; the *hybrid generator* caption is inherited from the 2018 CU900
photo pages. No working voltage is printed for it. The adapter figures are on
`cu1000ent-2023-specs-power-adapter-100-w-24-v-5-a`.

The 2018 CU900 and the CU900ENT print near-identical section-3 text; the CU1000ENT is the only
one of the three whose console line says "upright bike" rather than "Bike" or "Elliptical".
