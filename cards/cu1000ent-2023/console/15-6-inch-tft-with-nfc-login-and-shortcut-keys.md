The Console page labels three things: **DISPLAY**, and below it **NFC log in & Shortcut key**. The
Window Display page carries one call-out, **15.6" TFT Display**. The Function Locations heading on
the same page sits over a picture with no readable labels.

**So the screen is 15.6 inches** - larger than the 10.1" TFT of the CU900ENT and CR900ENT
(`cu900ent-console-layout`) - and log-in is by **NFC**: the Service tab of Maintenance Mode has an
NFC Test that reads "correspond to the location of the NFC sensor, and when the sensor is detected,
the information will be displayed on the screen"
(`cu1000ent-2023-console-service-tab-key-nfc-communication-brake-sensor-tests-and-error-log`).

**The shortcut keys are a separate keyboard.** The parts chapter replaces a **Keyboard** with a
20-pin cable and the hand-pulse wire behind it, and the I/O board has a **Key Board CONNECT (20 PINS)**
socket; those are held with the assembly and specs cards. The manual never names the individual
keys.

**Nothing in this book describes how the NFC log-in works**, what card or phone it reads, or what a
user sees after logging in. The owner's manual for this machine is not in the repository.
