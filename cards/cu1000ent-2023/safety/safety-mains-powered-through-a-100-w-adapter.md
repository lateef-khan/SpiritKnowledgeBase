**The CU1000ENT plugs into a wall socket through an external 100 W power adapter. The service manual
states no outlet voltage, amperage, breaker, cord gauge or grounding rule, and the one voltage word
in the book is a title on its circuit diagram: `230V`.**

The 7.4 Circuit Diagram is headed `SU8880-SB028 230V Upright Bike CIRCUIT DIAGRAM` and draws the
supply end to end: an `AC POWER INPUT` plug, a power cable and connector, a `100W Power Adapter`
marked `OUTPUT DC 24V / 5A`, a `DC JACK`, the controller marked `INPUT DC 24V / 5A`, and `POWER OUT
TO TFT POWER` from the controller to the touchscreen. The Driver Board function page labels the same
feed `DC 24V INPUT`.

- **230V is a drawing title, not an instruction.** No 120-volt or 230-volt outlet sentence, no amp
  figure, no NEMA type, no breaker, no extension-cord gauge and no grounding paragraph appear
  anywhere, and the book has no Product Safety Instructions chapter at all - its contents run
  Outlines, Electronic Parts, Electrical Configurations, Product Operation, Unit Block Diagrams,
  Basic Connections and Wiring, Error Messages, Engineering Mode, Software update, General
  Maintenance and Disassembling. The words *outlet*, *ground*, *GFCI*, *extension cord* and *AWG* do
  not appear in it; *volt* appears only on the circuit diagram and the driver-board labels. Say the
  manual does not state an outlet requirement, and read the rating off the adapter's own label.
- **The adapter is the console's supply.** The Electrical Configurations page says the main
  controller `consists of the DC power supply for console` and that an `EMS Flywheel` sets the
  resistance; the parts photographs label a `HYBRID GENERATOR` beside the driver board. Unlike the
  self-powered CR900 and CU900 books, this one never says the bike runs without mains, and its
  touchscreen is fed from the DC jack.
- **No user weight limit is printed anywhere in the book**
  (`xt-2015-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`).

**The only safety text the book prints is the wear WARNING under General Maintenance** - replace
defective components immediately or keep the machine out of use until repaired; the parts most
susceptible to wear are the belt, PU wheel, bearing and idler - which is the CU900ENT service
manual's warning word for word (`cu900ent-wear-parts-inspection`). Its `Serial Number & code
Location` photographs are read in `spirit-ce-safety-serial-number-location`.

**The CR900ENT-2021 is the same arrangement**: its service manual's circuit diagram draws a
100-240 V AC adapter with a 24 V DC output and its owner's manual prints no figure
(`spirit-xe-safety-no-outlet-figure-printed`). **The CT1000ENT treadmill is not**: its service manual
states a 230-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet as a requirement
(`ct1000ent-2023-safety-outlet-230-volt-15-amp`); this book prints a drawing title and no
requirement.

## The CR1000ENT-2023 recumbent bike is the same book with the frame changed

**The CR1000ENT (SR8880-SB028) service manual is the CU1000ENT book's shape page for page, and every supply statement in
it is the same.** Its 7.4 Circuit Diagram is headed `SR8880-SB028 230V Recumbent Bike CIRCUIT DIAGRAM` and draws the same
`AC POWER INPUT`, power cable, `100W Power Adapter`, `DC JACK` and controller; its Electrical Configurations page says the
main controller `consists of the DC power supply for console` and that an `EMS Flywheel` sets the resistance; its parts
photographs label a `HYBRID GENERATOR` beside the driver board; and its Driver Board function page labels `DC 24V
INPUT`. As on the CU1000ENT, `230V` is a drawing title and not an instruction.

- **No outlet sentence, no amp figure, no NEMA type, no breaker, no extension-cord gauge, no grounding paragraph and no
  user weight limit** anywhere in the 40 pages. The words *outlet*, *grounded*, *grounding*, *GFCI*, *extension cord* and
  *AWG* do not appear, in the text layer, with letter-spacing allowed, or in the OCR supplements; `GROUND` appears once,
  as a connector label on the I/O Board Bottom drawing (PDF p. 10). The book has no Product Safety Instructions chapter.
- **The same wear WARNING** under General Maintenance - replace defective components immediately or keep the machine out
  of use, and the parts most susceptible to wear are the belt, PU wheel, bearing and idler - is the only safety text
  it prints.
- **Its `Serial Number & code Location` photographs are the CR900's**, rating plate and all, not a CR1000ENT label
  (`spirit-ce-safety-serial-number-location`).

**The 2019 Spirit Fitness power sheet predates both machines** and lists no CU1000ENT or CR1000ENT; its rule that every
ENT console requires power is the same fact
(`spirit-commercial-safety-non-treadmill-120-volt-15-amp-or-self-generating`).
