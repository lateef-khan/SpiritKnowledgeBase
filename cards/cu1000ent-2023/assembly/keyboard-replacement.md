**12.12** Remove **four screws** from the back of the keyboard (figure 12.13). Then, as in figure 12.14, remove the **20-pin connector** and the **hand-pulse wire** on the keyboard to remove the keyboard. Refit by following figures 12.13 and 12.14 in the reverse order of removal.

**Only this book prints a keyboard procedure.** The CU900 and CU900ENT service manuals have no separate keyboard; their console procedure removes the whole console assembly. The CU1000ENT's keypad is a separate board with a 20-pin cable - the same 20-pin "Key Board CONNECT" the I/O board page of this book labels.

No screw size is printed.
