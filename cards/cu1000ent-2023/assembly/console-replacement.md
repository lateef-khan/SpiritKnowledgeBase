**Four chin-cover screws, not two.** The CU900 and CU900ENT books remove two chin-cover screws and then four more behind the console; this book prints four on the chin cover and no second set.

**12.11** As shown in figure 12.11, remove the **4 screws of the chin cover** and remove it. Remove the **wire connector** to remove the console (figure 12.12). Console assembly is 12.11 and 12.12 in reverse.

No screw size and no connector count are printed.

The keyboard (12.12) and the armrest group (12.13) are separate procedures in this book; the armrest one starts from the keyboard's hand-pulse wire and "front and rear chin cover screws".
