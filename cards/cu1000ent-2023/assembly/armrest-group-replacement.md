**A socket head cap bolt with a split washer and a flat washer, not the M8 cap the CU900 and CU900ENT books print.**

**12.13** Refer to figures 12.13 and 12.14 (the keyboard procedure) to remove the **hand-pulse wire**, and follow figures 12.15 and 12.16 to remove the **front and rear chin cover screws**. Refer to figure 12.17 to remove the **socket head cap bolt, split washer and flat washer** to remove the armrest group. Refer to figure 12.17 to assemble it back.

No bolt size, no count and no torque are printed. The CU900 books' warning to hold the armrest before its cap comes off, so it does not drop, is not printed here; the armrest is unsupported once the bolt is out on this machine too.
