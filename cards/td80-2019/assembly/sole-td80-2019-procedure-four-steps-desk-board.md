Four assembly steps for the TD80-2019 desk treadmill (hardware item numbers in brackets). Unpacking first: with the treadmill flat on the ground (never on its side), cut the banding straps, lift the carton off, and lay the unit on a level surface; the hardware pack has a tools section plus steps 1-4 sections, so open only the current step. The book proves the TD80 does fold: its folding instructions direct loosening knobs (3 & 4), folding the desk back, then folding up the deck (folding for storage belongs to the maintenance section, not this card).

1. Connect the Fixed Upright Assembly (113) to the Frame Base Tubes (L/R) (116) with sixteen 3/8" x UNC16 x 1/2" Button Head Socket Bolts (135), using the L wrench (141).
2. Lay the Desk Board (120) on the Sliding Upright Assembly (114). Secure the Desk Board Stand (115) with two Nuts (121), then fix the stand onto the sliding upright assembly with two 3/8" x UNC16 x 1-3/4" Button Head Socket Bolts (136) and two 3/8" x UNC16 x 7T Nyloc Nuts (137), using the open-end wrench (140) and the Allen wrench (141).
3. Slide Computer Cable Upper (46) into the Console Bracket Assembly (106) to connect to the Console Assembly (66); fix the console to the bracket with four M5 x P0.8 x 12L Phillips Head Screws (112) using the Phillips driver (139). Then set the bracket assembly on the right side of the desk board (120) and lock it with the Tension Knob (107).
4. Tighten four 5/16" x UNC18 x 2" Button Head Socket Bolts (70) with four 5/16" x UNC18 x 7T Nyloc Nuts (79), using the Allen wrench (142) and the wrench (140), to secure the two Limit Brackets (R/L) (118/119) through both frame base tubes (116). Afterwards the desk-to-treadmill distance adjusts by moving the unit on the limit brackets, and the console assembly locks onto the desk.
