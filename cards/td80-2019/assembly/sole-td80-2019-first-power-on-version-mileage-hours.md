First power-on, the last step of assembly, in the TD80 2019 desk-treadmill book. The display sentence matches the F60 2016 book word for word (see the linked card), but this book also prints the plug-and-switch sentence that the F60 book omits, and says "operate" where the F60 book says "power on".

Power the treadmill on by plugging it into an appropriate wall outlet, then turn on the power switch located at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood. Ensure that the Safety Key is installed, as the treadmill will not operate without it. When the power is turned on, the console will show the current software version, the total running mileage, hours. The treadmill will then enter idle mode, which is the starting point for operation.
