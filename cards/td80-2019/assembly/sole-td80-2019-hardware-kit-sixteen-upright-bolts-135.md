Hardware supplied in the box with the Sole TD80-2019 desk treadmill, grouped by assembly step. The hardware pack is separated into five sections: tools plus steps 1-4. Sizes read off the page rendering.

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | #135 | 3/8" x 1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 16 pcs |
| 2 | #136 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 2 pcs |
| 2 | #137 | 3/8" x 7T | Nyloc Nut | 2 pcs |
| 3 | #112 | M5 x 12 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| 4 | #70 | 5/16" x 2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 4 | #79 | 5/16" x 7T | Nyloc Nut | 4 pcs |

Tools in the box: #139 Phillips Head Screw Driver, #140 13/14 mm Wrench, #141 M6 Allen Wrench, #142 M5 Allen Wrench.

Note: assembly step 2 also calls for "two Nuts (121)" to secure the desk board stand (115); item 121 appears nowhere in the pack checklist, so those nuts are used as printed but are not listed in the kit above.
