**This is the TD80's 'Display does not light up' row: the same five checks as the other stencil books, worded for this machine.**

> Display does not light up — 1. Tether cord not in position. 2. Circuit breaker on front grill tripped. Push circuit breaker in until it locks. 3. Plug is disconnected. Make sure plug is firmly pushed into AC wall outlet. 4. Circuit breaker may be tripped. 5. Treadmill defect. Contact your dealer.

Check 3 names a plain AC wall outlet where the other books say 'AC household wall outlet'; check 4 says 'Circuit breaker' where they say 'Household circuit breaker'; check 5 says 'your dealer' where they say 'your SOLE dealer'. Same fault, same five remedies: see `f65-2016-display-does-not-light`. Read from the render (folio TD80 TREADMILL 23).
