The TD80-2019 book prints **no preset, user, heart rate, fitness-test or countdown programs**: operation is manual only, proved by full-text grep (program/preset/HR1 hits: none outside the belt-dust line), the OPERATION (MANUAL) chapter, and reads of every console page (all fully OCR-supplemented).

Manual operation: **STEP 1** attach the Safety key (any button wakes the display), 3-second countdown with a beep. **STEP 2** START moves the belt; SPEED keys adjust any time (0.5-4.0 MPH / 0.8-6.0 KPH). **STEP 3** ENTER pages speed/time/distance/calories/scan (scan rotates every 5 seconds). **STEP 4** STOP pauses (START resumes all data); a second STOP ends, a third resets. **STEP 5** DISPLAY shows accumulated data after training.

The three **direct-speed keys (1/2/3 kph-mph)** are shortcuts, not programs. ENTER-countdown and stride-length (30-99 cm) handling belong to the console chapter and are left there.
