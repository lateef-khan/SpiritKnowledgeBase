A desk treadmill: **no incline, no fan, no audio, no heart-rate and no Bluetooth anywhere in the book**. The console photo, left to right: **MUTE** (speaker-X) and **STRIDE LENGTH** (walker) keys, a SPEED window, **FAST / MEDIUM / SLOW** preset keys down the right, and a bottom row of **DISPLAY, START, ENTER, STOP, SLOW (down), FAST (up)**. Power on at the wall outlet plus the front power switch with the **Safety Key** in (it will not operate without it); the console shows the **software version, total running mileage and hours**, then **idle mode**. To start: fit the key to wake the display, press **START**, adjust with **SPEED up/down** or the **SLOW-MEDIUM-FAST** keys; STOP or a pulled key stops it.
