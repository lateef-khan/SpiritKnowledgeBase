**Do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and locked position**, power cord secured.

**Desk height:** loosen knobs (1 and 2) and press down on the hydraulic-cylinder handle (5) to move the desk.

**Fold / use the desk:** first check whether the console is secured on the desk or removed. Loosen knobs (3 and 4) and fold the desk back until the rubber foot pads sit near the frame uprights; to use it, pull the table down and tighten knobs (3 and 4) to lock it while in use.

**Treadmill deck:** **disconnect the power cord and the computer cable from the desk board stand**, move the unit backward or fold the desk board, then fold the deck up on its locking telescoping tube (linked card). To unfold, slight forward pressure with one hand, pull the unlocking lever, lower slowly to the floor.

**Placement and transport:** lay the deck under the desk with the transport wheels in the grooves of the limit brackets; adjust desk distance by moving the unit on the brackets, then secure the console assembly on the desk. Folded, the four transport wheels engage - roll it away.
