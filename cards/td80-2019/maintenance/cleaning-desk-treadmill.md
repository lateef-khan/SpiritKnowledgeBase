**Always unplug the treadmill before cleaning** (electrical hazard).

The belt-edge wipe matches the standard books (soft damp cloth or paper towel, monthly, reaching under the edge; mild soap and nylon brush on the textured top, dried before use - see the linked card). Everything else about TD80 cleaning is its own:

- **Walking surface: water only.** Clean the rubberized walking surface with a damp cloth (water only).
- **Never use ammonia, oils, silicones, or any other such compounds on the rubberized walking surface** - they can cause serious bodily injury and deteriorate the surface.
- **Computer surface:** dust and fingerprints come off only with chemicals made for anti-static surfaces (most computer-supply stores); harsh chemicals destroy the protective coating and build static that damages components.
- **Metal surfaces:** a soft cotton or terry cloth with a light application of car wax. **No aerosol sprays or pump bottles** - they deposit wax on the walking or computer surface.
- **Vacuum underneath monthly** against build-up reaching the inner workings under the motor cover. **Once a year**, remove the black motor hood and vacuum it out. **UNPLUG POWER CORD BEFORE THIS TASK.**
