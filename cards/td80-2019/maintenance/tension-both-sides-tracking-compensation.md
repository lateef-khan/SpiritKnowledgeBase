This is neither shared tension card: it adjusts from **both** sides like the both-bolts card, but every change needs tracking compensation like the right-side card. The card follows this printing.

Belt tension is **not critical for most users**; it matters for joggers and runners. Adjustment is made **from the right and left side of the rear roller** with the supplied **6 mm Allen wrench**; the bolts are at the **end of the right and left side rails**, reached **through the small hole of the end cap**.

Tighten the rear roller **only enough to prevent slippage at the front roller**, in increments of **1/4 turn**, inspecting for proper tension. **Every tension change must be tracking-compensated: turn both the tension and tracking Allen bolts an equal amount, clockwise, by no more than 1/4 turn at a time.**

**DO NOT OVERTIGHTEN** - over-tightening causes belt damage and premature bearing failure. If a well-tightened belt still slips, suspect the **motor drive belt under the motor cover** (trained service person only). Tracking itself follows the standard left-bolt 3 mph procedure (linked card).
