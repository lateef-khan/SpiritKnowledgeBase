The TD80-2019 desk book prints its own precautions list. Shared lines match the other A2 books except where noted: `this equipment` throughout for `this appliance`; `See Grounding Instructions if applicable` for `page 3`; `Keep children under the age of 13 away from the treadmill` for `this machine`; the disconnect line reads `... remove tether cord, and then remove the plug ...`.

Lines the other A2 books print but this one does not: the pulse-sensor disclaimer, the handrails line, and nothing else - the purpose, carpet, clearance, hands, cord, heat, aerosol, openings, outdoors and tether-after-use lines all match.

Three lines only this book prints:

- **Wear proper shoes. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue.** (No `High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet` sentence.)
- **Please verify and make sure safety key functions properly before using the treadmill. Always wear the safety key clip while in use.**
- **For energy savings, always unplug the power cord when treadmill is not in use.**
