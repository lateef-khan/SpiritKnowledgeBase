The TD80-2019 is a desk treadmill with no incline, and its operation rules are written accordingly:

- **Understand that changes in speed do not occur immediately.** Set the desired speed and release the adjustment key; the computer obeys gradually. (No incline is named anywhere on the page.)
- **NEVER use your treadmill during an electrical storm.** `Surges may occur in your power supply that could damage treadmill components` - `your power supply`, not `your household power supply`.
- **Use caution while participating in other activities while walking on your treadmill.** Distractions may unbalance you or stray you from the belt center; no television/reading examples are named.
- **NEVER mount or dismount the treadmill while the belt is moving.** `Treadmills start at a very low speed` - not `SOLE treadmills start ...`.
- **Do not use excessive pressure on console control keys.** Precision-set for little finger pressure; if the buttons misbehave with normal pressure, contact the SOLE dealer. (No `Pushing harder is not going to make the unit go faster or slower` sentence.)

No handrail rule appears anywhere in this book. The tether-cord section on the same page is on `sole-treadmill-safety-tether-cord-key-or-magnet`.
