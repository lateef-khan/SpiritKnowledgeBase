**This is the TD80 desk treadmill, not the 0.5 to 12 mph full-size range.** Its console SPEED window "Displays the current running speed from 0.8 KPH ~ 6.0 KPH. (0.5MPH~4.0MPH)", and its calibration page agrees: "Adjust the minimum speed (if needed) to 0.8kph/0.5mph" and "Adjust the maximum speed (if needed) to 6.0kph/4.0mph". Its SPEED keys are SLOW-MEDIUM-FAST presets plus DIRECT SPEED KEYS, not the 2-through-12 keys of the full-size books.
