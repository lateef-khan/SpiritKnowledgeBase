"P1 is a manual program ... **The default resistance level is 7.**" You change it at any time
with UP/DOWN during the workout.

The SB150, SB250 and FS150 books print **3** in the same sentence, and the SB240 book prints
no default. Every other line of the manual program page is identical across the five books.
