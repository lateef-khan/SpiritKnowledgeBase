The manual's whole exercise instruction for this machine is one picture of the
placard fitted to the machine. The placard is titled **LEG PRESS / CALF EXTENSION**.

It is a two-panel placard, one panel per exercise. Each panel carries a front and
a back body figure with the muscles that exercise works picked out, and a **START**
and a **FINISH** drawing of a person on the machine.

**The original printing prints no placard part number**; every other page of that
book was checked and the number is nowhere in it. **The 2025 revision prints
`Placard Part number #311006282`** beneath the same picture. A machine shipping
today carries the 2025 revision, so the number is the one to order against; the
earlier printing's silence was the book's, not the extraction's. See
`spirit-strength-console-counter-placard-part-number-311010581`
(`cards/shared/console/spirit-strength-console-counter-placard-part-number-311010581.md`)
for the *counter* placard's number, `#311010581`, printed on the next page - a
different label, not this one.

**No words go with the drawing.** The manual names no muscle, gives no starting
position in prose, no range of motion, no rep or set count and no warm-up. See
`spirit-strength-programs-exercise-instruction-is-the-machine-placard` for what
that means across the range.
