Hardware step 1: **#3 carriage bolts (4), #5 curved washers (4), #6 domed nuts (4)** - M10 x 90 bolts, 10 mm curved washers, M10 domed nuts.

1. Attach the front stabilizer (15) to the main frame (16). Secure using two carriage bolts (3), two curved washers (5) and two domed nuts (6).
2. Attach the rear stabilizer (4) to the main frame (16). Secure using two carriage bolts (3), two curved washers (5) and two domed nuts (6).

The book does not say which stabilizer carries the transport wheels, and prints no moving or levelling paragraph beyond "adjust all four levelers" on its Before Using page.
