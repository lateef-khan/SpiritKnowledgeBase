Hardware step 2: **#7 ball type quick release knob (1)**.

1. Loosen the nuts on the underside of the seat (13), place the seat (13) onto the seat post and then re-tighten the nuts.
2. Insert the seat post (10) into the main frame (16). Secure using the ball type quick release knob (7).

"Note: The seat can be horizontally and vertically adjustable after your cycle is fully assembled." The fit guide for height and fore/aft is on the first-time-user page.
