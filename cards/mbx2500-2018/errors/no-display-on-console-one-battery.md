The whole of this book's *Troubleshooting*:

**No Display on Console** - 1. Ensure that the battery is installed properly in the console. If it is,
install a fresh battery.

**One battery, in the console only.** The MB500 and MB550 books tell you to hold a key for Quick Start
and to check batteries in both the console and a separate speed transmitter
(`xterra-mb-errors-no-display-on-console-quick-start-then-console-and-transmitter-batteries`); the MBX2500
console has neither the Quick Start step nor a transmitter to feed, and the book prints no cadence-jumps
row either. The assembly instructions send you to printed page 12 for how to insert the battery.
