**Battery.** One **CR2032 3 V** battery ("Battery Spec: 3V CR2032 (1PC)"). To fit it: press the clip on the battery cover, remove the cover, tilt a new CR2032 slightly and slide it in, positive (+) facing up; to change it, gently hold the clip on the side and the battery pops out - tweezers help. On installation the screen shows the full-segment "Drawing A" with a beep, then loads the main menu ("Drawing B").

**Targets.** Set TIME, DISTANCE, CALORIES or PULSE with SET and lock each with MODE. With a signal coming in the values count up; a preset target counts down to zero, then **the monitor beeps for 8 seconds** and the function counts up from zero if you keep training.

**Sleep.** If you stop cycling for **4 minutes** the computer enters sleep mode; press any button to restart it and the original values are retained. **Reinstalling the battery resets the values.** If the display is abnormal, reinstall the battery and try again. The "No Display on Console" line of the troubleshooting page belongs to the Errors cards.
