**This console has no direct access speed ladder.** A specific speed is typed on the numeric
buttonpad. The other CT850 LED consoles have a ladder of fifteen buttons
(`spirit-ct850-console-quick-start-speed-keys-1-to-15`); the CT800 consoles have twelve
(`ct850-2020-quick-start`) or five numbered 2 through 6
(`spirit-ct800-console-quick-start-speed-keys-2-to-6`). Do not read one machine's key map onto
another.

- Press **any button** to wake the display up if it is not already on.
- Press the **Start** button to begin belt movement at **0.5 mph**, then adjust to the desired speed
  using the **Fast/Slow** buttons, or by pressing the **Speed** button and typing the desired speed
  on the numeric buttonpad. Once the setting you want is selected, press **Enter**.
- To slow the tread-belt, press and hold the **Slow** button to the desired speed. You may also use
  the numeric buttonpad to input the speed you want, then press **Enter**.
- To stop the tread-belt, press and release the **Stop** button.

The manual prints no top speed in this section. The calibration routine for this console sets the
maximum speed to **12.0**: `spirit-ct800-console-calibration-with-grade-return`.

Incline is entered the same way, by pressing the Incline button and typing the value:
`ct850-2020-incline-adjustment`. What the second and third press of Stop do is a separate
behaviour: `ct850-2020-pause-stop-reset`.
