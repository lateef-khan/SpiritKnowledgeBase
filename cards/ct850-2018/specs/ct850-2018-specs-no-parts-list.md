**This manual has no exploded view and no parts list.** Its table of contents ends
at "45 MANUFACTURER'S LIMITED WARRANTY" and the book ends at printed page 47 plus
two NOTES pages. It is the only one of the four CT850 owner's manuals without both -
the 2016 and 2020 books print a drawing on page 50 and a list on pages 51-52, and
the CT850ENT-2022 book prints them on pages 58 and 59-60.

**The only item numbers in this book are the four per-step assembly hardware tables
and the callouts in the assembly text.** Every number it uses is one of these
twenty-five:

`3, 4, 5, 26, 27, 28, 33, 34, 35, 37, 38, 41, 42, 43, 98, 116, 117, 125, 133, 134,
144, 145, 146, 162, 163`

Nothing in the book maps any of them to an orderable part number, and nothing names
a part outside those four steps. Any number a customer reads off this manual is an
assembly-step callout, never a catalogue number.

**These numbers are not the numbers in the other CT850 books.** The 2018 manual runs
its own scheme, and the same number names a different part in the 2016, 2020 and
CT850ENT-2022 lists. Quoting a number from this book against one of those lists gets
the wrong part:

| No. | What this manual calls it | What the CT850-2016 / 2020 lists call it |
|---|---|---|
| 26, 27 | left and right plastic covers | Square End Cap, Stable Wheel Spacer |
| 28 | the console | Motor Cover Anchor(D) |
| 33, 34 | left and right uprights | Motor Top Cover, Motor Base Cap (L) |
| 35 | ground wire | Motor Base Cap (R) |
| 37, 38 | hand pulse assemblies C and D | Rear Adjustment Base (R), Console Assembly |
| 41 | upper computer cable B | Console Cover |
| 116 | flat washer 3/8"X35X2T | Ø10 × 2.0T Split Washer |
| 117 | split washer 3/8"X16.5X2.0T | 3/8" × 7T Nylon Nut |
| 133, 134 | M5X12 screws | Ø10 × 2T Split Washer, 3/8" × 2-1/4" Hex Head Bolt |
| 144 | bolt 3/8"X3" | M5 × 12m/m Phillips Head Screw |
| 145 | bolt 3/8"X3/4" | M5 × 5T Nylon Nut |
| 146 | bolt M8X12 | M5 Star Washer |
| 162, 163 | bolt 3/8"X16X1-3/4", curved washer 10X23X1.5T | Ending Tape 400m/m, Ending Tape 200m/m |

Items **42, 43 and 98** - the lower computer cable A, the touch pad / backlit cable
E, and the retaining plate - have no number at all on the 2016, 2020 or CT850ENT
lists. Item **125** is the one number that means roughly the same thing in both
schemes, a Ø3/8" × Ø25 × 2.0T flat washer.

**The fastener descriptions do not carry across either.** The bolt this book numbers
162 is printed `3/8"X16X1-3/4"`; the bolt used in the matching step of the 2016,
2020 and CT850ENT books, item 154, is printed `3/8" × UNC16 × 1-1/4"`. Both are two
off, both go through a split washer and a curved washer into the back of the
uprights, and the two books give different lengths. Measure the bolt rather than
taking a length from the other book.

To turn a part on this machine into an orderable number, go to Spirit Fitness with
the serial number. Do not substitute a number from the CT850-2016, CT850-2020 or
CT850ENT-2022 list.
