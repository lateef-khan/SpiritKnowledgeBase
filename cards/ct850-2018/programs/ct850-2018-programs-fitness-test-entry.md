**This manual prints the entry sequence twice, three pages apart, and the two versions do not
agree.** Both are recorded here as printed; the source reconciles them nowhere.

## Fitness Test Operation (printed page 25)

1. The Message Window asks you to enter your **Weight**. The weight setting is shown in the
   **Incline** window; adjust with the Up and Down buttons, then press **Enter**.
2. You are asked to enter your **Age**, shown in the **Incline** window. Adjust, press Enter.
3. You are asked to enter your **Gender**, shown in the **Incline** window. Adjust, press Enter.
4. Press **Start** to begin the test.

## Fitness Test Programming (printed page 28)

1. Press the **Fit-Test** button and press **Enter**.
2. The Message Window asks you to enter your **Age**, shown in the **Incline** window. Adjust, press
   Enter.
3. You are asked to enter your **Weight**, shown in the **Distance** window. Adjust, press Enter.
4. Press **Start** to begin the test.

## What the two disagree about

- **The order.** Page 25 asks for weight first; page 28 asks for age first.
- **Gender.** Page 25 asks for it; page 28 does not mention it at all.
- **Which window shows your weight.** Page 25 puts it in the Incline window; page 28 puts it in the
  Distance window.

Page 25 also puts weight, age and gender in the same Incline window on three consecutive screens,
which is what the source says and is preserved.

**The CT800 console of the same period asks weight, then age, then gender, with weight in the
Distance window** - the page-28 window for weight but the page-25 order and the page-25 gender
prompt; see `spirit-ct800-programs-fitness-test-entry-weight-age-gender`. That is a different
machine and is not evidence for which of these two pages is right.

For which tests the button offers, see `ct800-2016-programs-fitness-test-menu-five-tests`. For what
to do before, during and after the test, see `ct900-gerkin-wfi-test-procedure`.
