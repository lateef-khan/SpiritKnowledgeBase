**None of these item numbers is the same part as the same number in the 2016 or
2020 CT850 manual.** This book numbers a flat washer 116 and a bolt 144; the other
two number a split washer 116 and a Phillips screw 144. Read the numbers only
against this manual.

The descriptions are printed without diameter symbols, exactly as shown here.

## Hardware for step 1

| Part | Type | Description | QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 116 | Flat Washer | 3/8"X35X2T | 10 |
| 117 | Split Washer | 3/8"X16.5X2.0T | 10 |
| 134 | Screw | M5X12MM | 4 |
| 144 | Bolt | 3/8"X3" | 10 |

## Hardware for step 2

| Part | Type | Description | QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 133 | Screw | M5X12 | 4 |

One row, and the whole of step two uses it.

## Hardware for step 3

| Part | Type | Description | QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 117 | Split Washer | 3/8"X16.5X2.0T | 6 |
| 125 | Flat Washer | 3/8"X25X2T | 6 |
| 145 | Bolt | 3/8"X3/4" | 6 |
| 146 | Bolt | M8X12 | 6 |

## Hardware for step 4

| Part | Type | Description | QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 117 | Split Washer | 3/8"X16.5X2.0T | 2 |
| 162 | Bolt | 3/8"X16X1-3/4" | 2 |
| 163 | Curved Washer | 10X23X1.5T | 2 |

## Every count balances, except that one item is bagged a step early

Ten of items 116, 117 and 144 in step one, and step one puts five bolts through
each of the two uprights - **ten**. Six of each item in step three, and step three
uses six and six. Four screws of item 133 in step two, and step two uses four. Two
of each in step four, and step four uses two.

That is unusual on this platform: the 2016, 2020 and CT800ENT manuals each have
steps that call for fewer fasteners than their tables list.

**The one thing that does not line up is where item 134 is bagged.** Its four
Phillips head screws sit in the step one table, and step one never calls for them.
They are used in step four, to attach the left and right plastic covers.

**Eighteen split washers of item 117 in total**, across three tables - ten, six and
two. There is no single combined hardware list in this manual, and no parts list at
the back of it to check a count against.
