**This is not the CT850 build of 2016 or 2020.** Those manuals have an Upright
Fixing Plate, a Console Support and a Locking Plate Assembly, four bolts a side,
and a wholly different set of item numbers. This one has a Retaining Plate, Console
Mounting Interface Brackets and five bolts a side. Nothing carries over - check the
item numbers against the manual in your hand before you order a part.

**The upright bolts are deliberately left loose through most of the build.** Step
one says to hand tighten only; step four is where everything comes up tight.

## Step one - uprights, hand tight only

1. Gather HARDWARE FOR STEP 1.
2. The RIGHT UPRIGHT (34) has a preinstalled Lower COMPUTER CABLE A (42). To
   install the uprights on the base frame, **special care must be taken not to
   pinch the computer cable** between the upright and frame base. Slide the SPLIT
   WASHER (117) and FLAT WASHER (116) on the BUTTON HEAD SOCKET BOLT (144). Slide
   the BOLT through the top hole in the RETAINING PLATE (98), and corresponding top
   hole in the upright tube, and screw into the base frame. Now install the other 4
   BUTTON HEAD SOCKET BOLTS (144) together with SPLIT WASHERS (117), and FLAT
   WASHERS (116). **Only hand tighten all 5 bolts. You will leave them loose until
   the console frame has been attached.**
3. Repeat this step to install the LEFT UPRIGHT (33).

## Step two - wire the console and screw it to the console support

1. Gather HARDWARE FOR STEP 2.
2. Connect the Upper COMPUTER CABLE B (41), HAND PULSE ASSEMBLY C (37), D (38),
   TOUCH PAD/BACKLIT CABLE E (43) and GROUND WIRE F (35) to the BOARD on the back
   of the CONSOLE (28).
3. Use 4 PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (133) to secure console assembly to the CONSOLE
   SUPPORT (5).

**Five cables land on the console board here**, and the console is wired on the
bench before it goes anywhere near the uprights - the reverse of the 2016 and 2020
order, where the console goes on last and is wired in place.

## Step three - brackets, cable join, and onto the uprights

1. Gather HARDWARE FOR STEP 3.
2. Use 6 SOCKET HEAD CAP BOLTS (146) to secure the console onto the LEFT AND RIGHT
   MOUNTING BRACKETS (3-L, 4-R).
3. Connect the Upper COMPUTER CABLE F (41) to the Lower COMPUTER CABLE A (42).
4. Slide the assembly into the left and right uprights.
5. Slide six SPLIT WASHERS (117) and 6 FLAT WASHERS (125) onto 6 BUTTON HEAD SOCKET
   BOLTS (145) on top of uprights. **Be careful not to pinch the computer cable.**

> When assembling the CONSOLE MOUNTING INTERFACE BRACKETS (3 & 4) please be sure to
> slide the brackets all the way toward the back of the console before tightening
> the bolts.

The manual calls cable 41 **B** in step two and **F** in step three. It is the same
Upper COMPUTER CABLE both times.

## Step four - rear bolts, final tightening, covers

1. Gather HARDWARE FOR STEP 4.
2. Slide 2 CURVED WASHERS (163) and 2 SPLIT WASHERS (117) onto 2 BUTTON HEAD SOCKET
   BOLTS (162). Slide the BOLTS through the holes on the console masts and **tighten
   all bolts, including the ones inserted in Step 1.**
3. Attach the LEFT and RIGHT PLASTIC COVERS (26-L, 27-R) with 4 PHILLIPS HEAD
   SCREWS (134).

Item 134 is bagged with step one but is not used until here; step one's own
instructions never call for it.
