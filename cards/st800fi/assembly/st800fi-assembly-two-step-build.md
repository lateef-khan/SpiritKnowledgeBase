Two steps, and both are wrench-tightened:

**Step one - attach the Rear Bracket (No. 2) to the Base Bracket (No. 1).** Secure with:

- 2 x M10 x 130 Hex Head Bolts (No. 30)
- 2 x Nylon Lock Nut M10 (No. 39)
- 4 x Washers Φ11 x Φ20 x 2 (No. 43)

**Step two - attach the Seat Pad (No. 9) to the Seat Pad Bracket (No. 5) and the Back Pad
(No. 10) to the Back Pad Bracket (No. 4).** Secure with:

- 6 x M10 x 30 Hex Head Bolts (No. 31)
- 6 x Washer Φ11 x Φ20 x 2 (No. 43)

That is the whole build. The frame arrives assembled; **only the rear bracket and the two
pads are fitted on site**, which is why this is the shortest build in the Spirit strength
range.

**Its parts list runs to 44 items**, most of them already in the frame. Item 24 is a pair
of gloves.

The hardware is in the tube holes rather than a bag, and the four tools are yours to
supply - see
[the hardware card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-st800-assembly-hardware-lives-in-the-tube-holes.md)
and [the tools card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-st800-assembly-four-tools-you-must-supply.md).

**The bench itself has no levelling foot.** Setting the back-pad angle after assembly is
an adjustment, not an assembly step, and the manual gives six settings on a pop-pin.
