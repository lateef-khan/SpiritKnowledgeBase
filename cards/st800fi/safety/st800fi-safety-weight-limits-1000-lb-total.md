The ST800FI flat/incline bench is the only Spirit strength product that splits its capacity three
ways. The last bullet of its safety instructions:

> **WEIGHT LIMITS: 1,000LBS TOTAL; USER - 300LBS; LIFTING WEIGHT - 700LBS**

- **1,000 lb total** — everything the bench carries at once.
- **300 lb user** — the lifter. This is the **lowest user weight limit of any Spirit strength
  product**, 60 lb below a CSS station and 100 lb below the CSF-FUNT.
- **700 lb lifting weight** — the bar and plates.

300 + 700 is exactly 1,000, so the three are one budget rather than three independent allowances. The
manual gives no metric figures and does not say whether a 250 lb user may therefore lift 750 lb; read
the two component limits as caps in their own right.

The ST800FI prints **no anchoring requirement** — the ST800DR3 dumbbell rack and the ST800FT
functional trainer both do, and this bench does not. Its facility precautions ask only that it be
set up and operated on a solid level surface and not on an uneven one.
