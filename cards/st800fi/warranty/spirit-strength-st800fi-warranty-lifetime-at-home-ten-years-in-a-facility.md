| | Frame | Parts | Upholstery/Grips |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Residential** (vendor defects only) | Lifetime | Lifetime | Lifetime |
| **Commercial** (non-dues paying facilities) | 10 Years | 5 Years | 6 Months |

**Three columns, two rows.** The home row is qualified *vendor defects only* - the manual
grants lifetime cover for a manufacturing fault, not for wear.

**No labour column and no labour row.** The labour term is stated separately, in the
SERVICE paragraph: `spirit-strength-warranty-labor-period`.

The two other ST800 books print the same two-row shape with different columns, so the
three cannot be read across each other:
`spirit-strength-st800ft-warranty-four-columns-adding-cables` adds a cables column, and
`spirit-strength-st800dr3-warranty-two-columns-frame-and-tray-sheets` has no parts or
upholstery column at all.

**A prisons footnote sits directly under this table**, in addition to exclusion 8 on the
next page: `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.

The CSD, CSS, CSF and CSI machines carry no residential row at all - a home term cannot
be read across from this bench to any of them.
