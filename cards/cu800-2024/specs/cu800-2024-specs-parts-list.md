These are the callout numbers on the exploded view drawing. The drawing is a
picture with bare numbers on it and no text, so the table below is the only
readable form of it.

**111 rows are printed.** The whole item numbers that appear are 1-3, 5-9, 11, 13-32, 34-38, 40-43, 45, 47, 49-56, 58, 60-63, 65-69, 71-73, 76-81, 83, 86, 92-93, 96, 99-100, 102-103, 105-107, 114, 116, 118, 122, 124, 126-127, 134-135, 138, 150-151, 174. Any number outside that run is **not printed in this manual at all** - 82 numbers between 4 and 173 are absent, not a missing page.

## What changed from the 2021 CU800 list

The 2021 book prints 113 rows and this one prints 111. **Four rows were dropped,
two added and three quantities altered:**

| Item | CU800-2021 | CU800-2024 |
|---|---|---|
| 34-16 | not printed | `Fan Grill Anchor`, qty 2 |
| 51 | 5/16" x UNC18 x 5/8" hex head bolt, **qty 9** | same bolt, **qty 7** |
| 52 | 5 x 19 mm tapping screw, **qty 2** | same screw, **qty 4** |
| 59 | `3/8" x 2"_Flat Head Socket Bolt`, qty 4 | **not printed** |
| 61 | Ø5 x 16L tapping screw, **qty 10** | same screw, **qty 8** |
| 70 | `Ø3/8" x Ø19 x 1.5T_Flat Washer`, qty 4 | **not printed** |
| 82 | `3/8" x 7T_Nut`, qty 8 | **not printed** |
| 136 | `17m/m_Wrench`, qty 1 | **not printed** |
| 174 | not printed | `5/16" x UNC18 x 3/4"_Hex Head Bolt`, qty 2 |
| 34-10 | `CASFE Board` | `CSAFE Board` - the transposition corrected |

**The 17 mm wrench is no longer in the box.** Item 136 is gone and nothing
replaces it.

## The left and right crank arms have letter suffixes

Items **16L** and **16R** are the left and right crank arms, and items **106-1**
and **106-2** are a knob nut and an M4 x 5 mm Phillips head screw printed as
sub-items of 106. The manual writes the sub-items with a tilde (`106~1`); they
are written with a hyphen here so the numbers sort.

## What tells this list apart from the two recumbent lists

- **Item 20 is `Generator/Brake` and item 21 `Generator/Brake Controller`** -
  the same pair the CR800-2024 list numbers 55 and 43. **The same part, a
  different number in each book.**
- **Item 135 is a `TV Adapter`**, where the CR800-2024 list numbers it 194.
- The upright carries no seat carriage, no aluminium seat track and no seat track
  wheels, so more than half of the recumbent list has no counterpart here: this
  list and `cr800-2024-specs-parts-list` share only 65 item numbers and agree on
  **two**.

**No flywheel weight, machine weight or resistance rating is printed against any
row.** See `spirit-bike-specs-no-specification-table`.
