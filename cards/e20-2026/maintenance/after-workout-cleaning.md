**Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each workout.**

That is the whole of the routine cleaning this manual asks for. It names no cleaning product, and warranty exclusion 3 puts **corrosion (RUST)** among the external causes the warranty does not cover.

Unplug the machine before cleaning; see `e20-2026-disconnecting-power`.
