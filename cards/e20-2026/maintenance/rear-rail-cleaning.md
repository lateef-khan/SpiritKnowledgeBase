1. **Clean the rails and wheels with a lint free cloth and rubbing alcohol.**
2. **Stubborn build-up can be removed with your thumbnail or a non-metallic scraper, like the back edge of a plastic knife.**
3. **After cleaning, apply a small amount of lubricant on the rails with your fingers or a lint free cloth.**
4. **You only need a thin coat of lubrication. Wipe off any excess.**

**The manual does not name the lubricant** - no type, no part number, no interval.

Dirty rails are one of the two causes of noise the manual names; see `e20-2026-noises-squeaks-and-thumps`.
