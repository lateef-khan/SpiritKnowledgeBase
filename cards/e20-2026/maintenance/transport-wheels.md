**The elliptical is equipped with two transport wheels which are engaged when the rear of the elliptical is lifted.**

The wheels sit at the front of the machine: assembly step 1 has you attach the **Front Stabilizer (17)** with the **transportation wheels set forward**.

The manual gives no machine weight, no two-person warning and no folding procedure - this elliptical does not fold.
