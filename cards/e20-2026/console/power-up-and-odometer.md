**Power the elliptical on by plugging it into an appropriate wall outlet.** This manual describes no power switch.

When it is first powered on the console performs an **internal self-test**: all the lights turn on, the **Distance Window displays an odometer reading** and the **Time Window shows how many total hours the elliptical has been used**. The odometer shows **how many virtual miles the elliptical has gone**.

**The odometer stays on screen for only a few seconds**, and then the console is ready to use.

The odometer can be cleared from the service menu - see `e20-2026-engineering-mode`.
