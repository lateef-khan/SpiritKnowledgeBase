**Calorie readings on every piece of exercise equipment, whether in a gym or at home, are not accurate and tend to vary widely.** They are meant **only as a guide to monitor your progress from workout to workout**.

The manual's reasoning: the only way to measure calorie burn accurately is in a clinical setting connected to a host of machines, because every person is different and burns calories at a different rate.
