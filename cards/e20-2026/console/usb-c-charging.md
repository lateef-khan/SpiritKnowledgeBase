Charge a personal device during your workout from the console's **USB Type-C port**. **The fitness equipment power must be on.**

1. **Connect your USB Type-C charging cable (not included) to the USB Type-C Power Port and to your device.**
2. **Check to make sure your device icon indicates it is charging.**

Notes printed with the procedure:

- **The USB Type-C charging cable is not included.** Make sure a compatible cable is used.
- **Your device's "charging" icon may or may not indicate it is charging.** Depending on the amount of current your device requires, the icon may be off while the device still charges, **possibly at a lower charge rate**.
- **The port provides up to 5Vdc / 1 amp.**
- **You cannot save workout data to a USB Type-C device through this port; it is for charging only.**
