**This is F03 UNITS in the engineering menu, not F04 sleep mode, not F07 pause mode and not F09 child lock.**

**F03 UNITS - 1: English, 0: Metric.** Choose English (Imperial) or Metric display readings.

Get there by holding **Start, Stop and Enter for about five seconds** and stepping through the menu with **Level Up/Down**; see `e20-2026-engineering-mode`.
