- **The equipment's Bluetooth device can also be connected to a Bluetooth wireless heart rate chest strap transmitter.**
- **The chest strap transmitter can still connect to the equipment even though the App has already been connected** to it.
- Page 22 repeats: **"You can use a wireless Bluetooth heart rate transmitter on this machine."**
- **To check if your Garmin device can be used for heart rate transmission, refer to the list of supported devices provided on www.soletreadmills.com.**

The manual does not print a pairing procedure for the strap, and does not name a 5kHz analogue strap anywhere - only Bluetooth.

The grip sensors are a separate, less reliable source; see `e20-2026-pulse-sensor-accuracy-disclaimer`.
