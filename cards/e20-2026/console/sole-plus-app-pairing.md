The machine has a **Bluetooth transceiver** that works with compatible **iOS and Android** smartphones and tablets through the **SOLE+ App**.

1. **Download the free SOLE+ App** from the Apple Store or Google Play.
2. **Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your device.**
3. **Tap the icon at the top right corner of the app to search for Sole equipment.**
4. **After the equipment is detected, tap Connect.**
5. When the App and equipment are synced, the **Bluetooth icon on the equipment's console lights up**.

The app syncs workout data with **Apple Health, Google Health Connect, Strava or MapMyFitness**, "with more to come".

A Bluetooth chest strap can stay connected at the same time as the app; see `e20-2026-bluetooth-heart-rate-strap`. Playing music is a separate pairing; see `e20-2026-bluetooth-speaker-pairing`.
