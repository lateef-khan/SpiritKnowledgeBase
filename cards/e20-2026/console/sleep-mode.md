**This is F04 SLEEP MODE in the engineering menu, not F03 units, not F07 pause mode and not F09 child lock.**

**F04 SLEEP MODE - 1: OFF, 0: ON.** **Turn on to have the console power down automatically after 15 minutes of inactivity.**

A console that goes dark after roughly a quarter of an hour of standing still is doing what this setting asks of it. Get there by holding **Start, Stop and Enter for about five seconds**; see `e20-2026-engineering-mode`.
