**This is F07 PAUSE MODE in the engineering menu, not F03 units, not F04 sleep mode and not F09 child lock.**

**F07 PAUSE MODE - 1: OFF, 0: ON. Turn on to allow 5 minutes of pause; turn off to have the console pause indefinitely.**

**The other half of this rule is on the Stop key.** One press of Stop during a program pauses it, and Start resumes it - see `e20-2026-stop-key-functions`, which states the 5 minute figure without mentioning that it is adjustable here.
