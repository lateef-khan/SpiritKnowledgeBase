**"Before returning your Sole product to any retailer, or if you require any assistance with assembly or technical support please call us first for assistance at 866-780-7653."**

The notice is printed **twice** in this manual - on the inside front cover and again on the last page, after the warranty. The front matter adds the same number for the service department: **"If there is a question as to where to obtain service, contact our service department at 866-780-7653."**
