| Item | Tool |
|---|---|
| #144 | Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench |
| #142 | Phillips Head Screw Driver |
| #143 | 8mm L Allen Wrench |
| #145 | L Allen Wrench |
| #141 | 12/14m/m Wrench |

**Only #143 has a size printed.** The manual gives no size for the Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (144) or for the plain L Allen Wrench (145).

General Maintenance adds that **it may be necessary to use a larger wrench than the one provided** if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently with the supplied tools.
