1. **Use a razor knife or box cutter to cut the outside, bottom, and the edge of the box along the dotted line. Lift the box over the unit and unpack.**
2. **Carefully remove all parts from the carton and inspect for any damage or missing parts.** If damaged parts are found, or if parts are missing, **contact your dealer immediately**.
3. **Locate the hardware package. Remove the tools first.** Remove the hardware for each step only as needed, to avoid confusion.

**The numbers in parentheses (#) in the instructions are the item number from the assembly drawing**, for reference.
