Hardware for this step: **#103 (4 pcs), #110 (6 pcs)**. See `e20-2026-assembly-hardware-pack`.

1. Use the **Phillips Head Screw Driver (142)** to secure the **Drink Bottle Holder (90)** on the console mast with **two Phillips Head Screw (110)**.
2. Pull the two cables of the **Handpulse W/Cable Assembly (54)** from **Handpulse Assembly (L) (8)** and **Handpulse Assembly (R) (9)** through the round hole from the Console Mast, with **four Button Head Socket Bolt (103)** using the **Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (144)**.
3. **Plug all of the connectors into the back of the Console Assembly (46)**: the **Computer Cable (48)** and the **two cables of the Handpulse W/Cable Assembly (54)**. Secure the Console Assembly (46) on the console mounting plate with **four Phillips Head Screw (110)** using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (142).
4. **Plug the four Oval End Cap (65) into the stabilizer tubes.**

The six #110 screws in this step's pack are two for the bottle holder plus four for the console.
