"Ø" is the diameter symbol used in the printed checklist, and "T" is the printed thickness figure.

**Three things in this checklist do not agree with the assembly steps, and the manual never reconciles them.**

- **#137** is listed here as a *Button Head Socket Bolt*, but assembly step 4 tells you to secure "three Button Head Socket Bolt (103) through three **Curved Washer (137)**."
- **#114** is listed here as an *M5 × 10m/m Phillips Head Screw* (4 pcs), but assembly step 4 calls it a **Sheet Metal Screw (114)** and uses only **three** of them.
- Step 4 lists **8 pcs of #110**, but the step 4 instructions use only four (slide wheel covers) plus two (rear bar cover) = **six**.

**#159 is listed for step 1 but no instruction names it by number.** The unnumbered "two Button Head Socket Bolt" in step 1.4 is the only place it can go.
