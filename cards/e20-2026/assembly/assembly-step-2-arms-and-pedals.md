Hardware for this step: **#98, #130, #136, #103**. See `e20-2026-assembly-hardware-pack`.

1. Attach **Pedal Arm (L) (6)** to the **left crank arm axle**, then secure **one Hex Head Bolt through one Flat Washer** using the **Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (144)**. Do the same to attach **Pedal Arm (R) (7)** to the right crank arm axle.
2. Insert the **Upright Bar Axle (24)** into the **Console Mast (2)**, then secure **Lower Handle Bar (L) (13)** and **Lower Handle Bar (R) (14)** on the Upright Bar Axle (24) with **two Head Socket Bolt (103) through two Flat Washer (130)**, using the Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (144).
3. Secure **Connecting Arm (L) (4)** on Pedal Arm (L) with **one Button Head Socket Bolt (103) through Flat Washer (136)**, using the Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (144). Do the same for **Connecting Arm (R) (5)**.
4. **Remove the Rod End Shaft and Screw which are on Connecting Arm (L.R) (4.5).** Connect **Lower Handle Bar (L.R) (13.14)** with **Connecting Arm (L.R) (4.5)** by the **Rod End Shaft (25)**, then secure the screw that came from Connecting Arm (L.R) (4.5) using the **Wrench (141)**.

The manual does not print a torque figure for any of these fasteners, and the Hex Head Bolt in step 1 is not given its item number in the instruction text - the step hardware list has it as **#98, 2 pcs**.
