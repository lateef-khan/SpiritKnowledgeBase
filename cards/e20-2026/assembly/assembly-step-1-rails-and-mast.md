Hardware for this step: **#107, #129, #135, #138, #156, #158, #139, #159, #157**. See `e20-2026-assembly-hardware-pack`.

1. **Pull the tie on Console Mast (2)** to have the **1150m/m Computer Cable (48)** go through the mast from the bottom and out of the mast on top. Secure the Console Mast on the mounting plate on the Main Frame with **four Socket Head Cap Bolt (107) through four Split Washer (135), four Flat Washer (129)**, using the **8mm L Allen Wrench (143)**.
2. Attach the **Front Stabilizer (17)** to the **Main Frame (1)** and **set the transportation wheels forward**, then secure **two Carriage Bolt (139) through two Curved Washer (156), two Cap Nut (138)** with the **12/14m/m Wrench (141)**. This step should secure the Carriage Bolt (139) **from bottom to top** through the Front Stabilizer (17).
3. Attach the **Rail Connecting Tube (10)** between the two **Rear Rail Assembly (3)**, then secure **four Button Head Socket Bolt (157) through four Curved Washer** using the **L Allen Wrench (145)**, on both sides. Then attach the two Rear Rail Assembly (3) to the **Rail Tube Connecting Seat**, and secure **two Button Head Socket Bolt (157) through two Flat Washer (129), two Nylon Nut** using the L Allen Wrench (145).
4. Use **two Button Head Socket Bolt** to secure the two Rear Rail Assembly (3) on the Rail Tube Connecting Seat, using the L Allen Wrench (145).
5. **Install four Foot Pad** on the Main Frame (1) and the Rail Connecting Tube (10).

**Steps 3 and 4 both describe fixing the rear rails to the Rail Tube Connecting Seat**, and step 4 names no item number for its two bolts. The step 1 hardware list carries **two #159 Button Head Socket Bolt** that no numbered instruction uses, which is the only candidate. The manual does not resolve this; count the fasteners in the pack before you start.

The Foot Pads in step 5 carry no item number in the text.
