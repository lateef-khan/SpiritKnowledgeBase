**WARNING: This product can expose you to chemicals including Toluene and Acrylamide which are known to the State of California to cause Cancer and birth defects or other reproductive harm.**

For more information the manual points to **www.P65Warnings.ca.gov**.

The notice is printed on the inside front cover, above the ATTENTION block about residential use.
