- **Always hold on to a handle bar while making control changes (incline, level, etc.).**
- **Use caution while participating in other activities while using your elliptical**, such as watching television or reading. These distractions may cause you to lose balance, which may result in serious injury.

The printed rule names **incline** among the control changes, but nothing else in this manual describes an incline function on this machine - the console has Level buttons and the service menu tests only a tensioning gear motor. See `e20-2026-resistance-change-timing`.
