- Install the elliptical on a flat level surface with access to a **115-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet, with only the elliptical plugged into the circuit**.
- **Do not use an extension cord unless it is 14AWG or better, with only one outlet on the end.**
- **Do not attempt to disable the grounded plug by using improper adapters, or in any way modify the cord set.** A serious shock or fire hazard may result, along with computer malfunctions.

**The manual prints two different supply figures.** Page 2 calls for a **115-volt** outlet; the Grounding Instructions on page 3 say the product **"is for use on a nominal 110-volt circuit."** Both are printed in this manual and neither corrects the other.
