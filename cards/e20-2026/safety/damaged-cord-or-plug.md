- **Never operate the elliptical if it has a damaged cord or plug.** If the elliptical is not working properly, **call your dealer**.
- **Keep the cord away from heated surfaces.**
- Do not in any way **modify the cord set**, and do not defeat the grounded plug with an improper adapter.

The manual does not give a cord part number and does not describe replacing the cord yourself.
