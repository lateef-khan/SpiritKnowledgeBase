- **NEVER expose this elliptical to rain or moisture.** It is **not** designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.
- **Operating temperature: 40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit.**
- **Humidity: 95% non-condensing** (no water drops forming on surfaces).

These are operating limits. Where the machine may be **stored and still keep its warranty** is a separate and stricter rule - see `e20-2026-warranty-void-outside-climate-control`, which voids the warranty outside a climate-controlled, insulated room. Neither passage refers to the other.
