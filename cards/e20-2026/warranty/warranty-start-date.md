**The warranty runs from the date of retail sale, as determined by the sale receipt.** **In the absence of a sales receipt, it runs from eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date.**

The Service section states the same thing for labor: **"Twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale or eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number establishes the labor warranty period should service be required."**

**Keep your bill of sale.** If service is performed, it is in your best interest to obtain and keep all receipts.
