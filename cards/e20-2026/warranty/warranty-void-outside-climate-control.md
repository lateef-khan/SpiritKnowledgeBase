**The warranty will be void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment.** The manual's own examples of what that rules out: **garage, outside, carport, etc.**

This is stricter than the operating limits printed on page 3 (**40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit, 95% humidity non-condensing**). A garage can sit inside those numbers and still void the warranty. See `e20-2026-operating-environment-limits`; neither passage refers to the other.
