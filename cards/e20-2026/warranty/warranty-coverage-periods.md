**Elliptical warranty, effective February 1, 2026.**

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** |
| **Parts** | **1 Year** |
| **Labor** | **1 Year** |
| **Cosmetic Items** | **90 Days** |

**Cosmetic items** include, but are not limited to, **grips, console overlays, and labels/decals**.

**SOLE's responsibilities** are providing **new or remanufactured parts, at SOLE's option**, and technical support to independent dealers and servicing organizations. **In the absence of a dealer or service organization, these warranties are administered by SOLE directly to the consumer.**

**This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household use**; see `e20-2026-residential-use-only`.

When the clock starts is a separate question; see `e20-2026-warranty-start-date`.
