**Warranties outside of North America may vary. Sole USA warranty coverage is limited to the Continental United States. Sole USA does not sell or service equipment in Hawaii, Alaska, or outside of the United States.** Contact your local dealer for details regarding warranty availability in your region.

**The clause disagrees with itself in one sentence:** it opens by saying coverage outside **North America** may vary, and then limits Sole USA coverage to the **Continental United States**, which excludes Hawaii, Alaska and the rest of North America outright. The manual does not reconcile the two.
