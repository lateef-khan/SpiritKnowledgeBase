**The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within 10 days of the original purchase to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.**

**Outside of the 10 day period the warranty can still be registered if you have proof of purchase** - the manual lists **a receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation**.

The matching exclusion: the warranty does not cover **products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at SOLE**, and **SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product**.

Both halves of this rule are printed on the same page, in different sections, and neither refers to the other.
