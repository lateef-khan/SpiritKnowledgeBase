- **Record the Serial Number of this fitness product in the space provided** on page 1.
- **The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to SOLE.**
- **You can also go to www.soletreadmills.com under the support tab to register online.**

The front matter also asks you to record, for future contact, the **name of the dealer, the telephone number of the dealer and the purchase date**.

**Registering is not optional if you want the warranty.** The deadline and the alternatives are a separate rule; see `e20-2026-registration-deadline-and-proof-of-purchase`.
