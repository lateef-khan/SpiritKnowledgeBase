The warranty does **not** cover:

1. **Consequential, collateral, or incidental damages** such as property damage and incidental expenses resulting from any breach of this written or any implied warranty. Some states do not allow that exclusion, so it may not apply to you.
2. **Service call reimbursement to the consumer.** Service call reimbursement to the dealer is also excluded where it does not involve malfunction or defects in workmanship or material, for units beyond the warranty period, for units beyond the service call reimbursement period, for an elliptical not requiring component replacement, or for an elliptical not in ordinary household use.
3. **Damage caused by service performed by anyone other than an authorized SOLE service company, or by use of parts other than original SOLE parts.** Also **external causes**: corrosion (RUST), discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, natural disasters, or elemental damage such as power surges, lightning storms, water damage or fire damage.
4. **Products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered.**
6. **Products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at SOLE.** SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists.
7. **This warranty is expressly in lieu of all other warranties expressed or implied, including the warranties of merchantability and/or fitness for a particular purpose.**

The remaining exclusions have their own cards, because each answers a question on its own: **5** transfer to a new owner (`e20-2026-warranty-transfer`), **8** non-residential use (`e20-2026-residential-use-only`), **9** coverage outside the Continental United States (`e20-2026-warranty-geographic-coverage`), and **10** storage outside a climate-controlled room (`e20-2026-warranty-void-outside-climate-control`).
