This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household use, and the consumer is responsible for:

1. **Returning the completed warranty registration card within 10 days**, or registering later with proof of purchase. See `e20-2026-registration-deadline-and-proof-of-purchase`.
2. **Proper use in accordance with the instructions in this manual, including general maintenance.**
3. **Proper installation in accordance with the instructions and with all local electric codes.**
4. **Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage, replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring.**
5. **Expenses for making the elliptical accessible for servicing**, including any item that was not part of the elliptical when it shipped from the factory.
6. **Damages to the elliptical finish during shipping, installation or following installation.**
7. **Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual.**
8. **Providing pictures or video of any issues via email as requested by the service technician, and assisting with troubleshooting/diagnosing prior to a home visit.** See `e20-2026-troubleshooting-before-a-home-visit`.

**Responsibility 2 describes a treadmill, not this elliptical.** It reads "Proper use of the **treadmill** in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual, including general maintenance (i.e. **belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under motor cover, and lubrication**. See all components under General Maintenance section)". **The General Maintenance section of this same manual (page 23) has no belt, no motor cover and no belt tension** - it covers wiping the sweat path, tightening assembly hardware, and cleaning and lubricating the rear rails. Both passages are printed in this manual and neither corrects the other; the only lubrication this machine's own maintenance section asks for is a thin coat on the rear rails.
