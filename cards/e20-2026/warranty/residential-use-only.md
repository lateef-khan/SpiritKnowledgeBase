**THIS ELLIPTICAL IS INTENDED FOR RESIDENTIAL USE ONLY AND IS WARRANTED FOR THE APPLICATION. ANY OTHER APPLICATION VOIDS THIS WARRANTY IN ITS ENTIRETY.**

The same rule is repeated twice more in the warranty:

- The warranty **applies only to products in ordinary household use**.
- Exclusion 8: the warranty does not cover **product use in any environment other than a residential setting**.

The safety instructions add: **do not attempt to use your elliptical for any purpose other than the purpose it is intended.**
