- **Entering your age is used for the Heart Rate programs.** If you enter your age during programming, **the console performs the training-zone calculation automatically**.
- **The Heart Rate program automatically controls resistance at the pedals.**
- **You may use the heart rate monitor feature without using the Heart Rate program.** This function can be used **during manual mode or during any of the nine different programs**.

The heart rate source can be the grip sensors or a Bluetooth strap; see `e20-2026-pulse-sensor-accuracy-disclaimer` and `e20-2026-bluetooth-heart-rate-strap`.

The manual's page of general heart-rate theory (the "220 minus your age" formula and the training-zone chart) is standard exercise-physiology text reprinted in every Sole manual, and is not reproduced here.
