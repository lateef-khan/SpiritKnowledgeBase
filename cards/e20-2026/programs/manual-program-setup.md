**The Manual program works as the name implies: you control the workload yourself and not the computer.**

1. **Press the Manual key, then press the Enter key.**
2. **Adjust the Age setting with the Level UP/DOWN keys**, then press Enter to accept the number and go to the next screen.
3. **Enter your Weight** with the Level UP/DOWN keys, then press Enter to continue.
4. **Set the Time** by pressing the keys, then press Enter to continue.
5. **Press the Start key to begin the workout.** You can go back and modify the settings by pressing the **Stop key** to go back one level of the programming screen.
6. **The program starts at level one.** This is the easiest level and it is a good idea to stay there for a while to warm up. **Level UP increases the work load, Level DOWN decreases it**, at any time.
7. **When the program ends the Message Window shows a summary of your workout.** The summary is displayed for a short time, then the console returns to the startup display.

Age and Weight are remembered as the new defaults; see `e20-2026-age-and-weight-defaults`.
