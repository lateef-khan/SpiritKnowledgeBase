Numbers in parentheses are assembly-drawing item numbers.

## Pre-assembly

Cut the straps and lift the box over the unit; inspect for damage; take the tools out of
the hardware package first and then take the hardware step by step. **The book prints no
tools panel** - see
[that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-rehab-stepper-assembly-no-tools-panel.md).

## Step one - handle bar, gas shock, pedals and end caps

1. Slide the seat carriage (20) all the way back. Slide the **handle bar assembly (17)**
   onto the receiving tubes of the seat frame and secure with **two 3/8" x 3-1/4" bolts
   (140)** from the inside hole, **four flat washers (170)** and **two nylon nuts (190)**.
   **Install the safety cover (108) and seat belts (233) onto the bolts before the
   washers and nuts.** Then install **four 3/8" x 2" bolts (132)** from the top of the
   tubes with **eight 3/8" flat washers (170)** and 3/8" nylon nuts (190).
2. Attach the **seat back gas shock (109)** to the seat back angle adjustment bracket
   with a **5/16" x 1-1/4" bolt (135), two 5/16" flat washers (173) and a 5/16" nylon nut
   (193)**.
3. Plug the hand pulse connectors from the handle bars into the mating connectors of the
   seat carriage.
4. Assemble the **pedals (28)** to the pedal foot plates with **six M5 Phillips screws
   (181) per side**.
5. Press the **four stabilizer end caps (31)** into the stabilizer tubes.

**Eight flat washers on the four 3/8" x 2" bolts, not four.** The MS300, whose step 1 is
otherwise the same sentence, says four. Both books' hardware pages agree with their own
step - twelve item 170 washers here, eight on the MS300 - so this is a real difference
between the machines and not a misprint. See
[the MS300 build](../../ms300-2021/assembly/ms300-2021-assembly-procedure.md).

## Step two - transport wheels, console mast and handle sliders

1. Install the **transport wheels (77)** with **5/16" x 1-3/4" bolts (152)** and **5/16"
   nylon nuts (193)**.
2. Slide the **console mast cover (74)** onto the **console mast (2)** the right way
   round, or it will not clip in place later.
3. Snake the computer cable through the mast and slide the mast onto the receiving
   brackets without pinching the cable.
4. Fasten the mast with **two 3/8" x 2-1/2" bolts (154), 3/8" split washers (194) and
   3/8" flat washers (170) from the left side**, secured with **two 3/8" nylon nuts
   (190)**. Install **two 8mm x 16mm bolts (153) and curved washers (184)** through the
   front and rear holes in the mast.
5. Secure the **Handle Sliders (335)** using **six M8 x 30mm bolts (309) and six M8 nuts
   (313) on each side**.

**Item 5 asks for twelve of each and the hardware page supplies six of each.** The
step-two picture prints **#309 - M8 x P1.25 x 30mm (6PCS)** and **#313 - M8 x 1.25 x
6.5T (6PCS)**. On the CRS800S, which fastens the same part, the step says three screws
and three nuts *on each side* against a table of six - which is consistent. Read item 5
as **six in all, three a side**, and count the bag before you start.

## Step three - seat back and cushion

1. Slide the **seat back assembly (94)** into the seat back angle adjustment bracket and
   secure with **two 3/8" x 1-3/4" bolts (197), 3/8" washers (167) and 3/8" nuts (190)**.
2. Assemble the **seat cushion (93)** onto the seat frame with **four M6 bolts (122)**.

## Step four - console and arms

1. Connect the computer cable into the back of the console and install the **console
   (29)** onto the mast with **four 5mm x 12mm screws (143)**.
2. Install the **left and right arms (3 & 4)** with **3/8" x 3/4" bolts (216)**, using
   **3/8" flat washers (217)** and **3/8" split washers (243)** on the sides of the arms
   and **3/8" curved washers (218)** on the front and rear. **Tighten very securely so
   the arms do not loosen during use.**

## What comes next, and what the build never does

Step four is followed by SETTING UP YOUR RECUMBENT STEPPER: plugging in - a universal
supply, 100 to 240 volts, 50 to 60 Hz, with the A.C. input at the front - then levelling
and the seat adjustments. **No step fits a levelling glide**; all six arrive on the
machine. See
[the levelling card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-rehab-stepper-assembly-levelling-six-glides.md).

**No wheelchair anchor and no seat assembly step.** The 7.5S build has both; this one has
neither. See [that build](../../75s-2025/assembly/75s-2025-assembly-procedure.md).
