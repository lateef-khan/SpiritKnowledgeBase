One rendering of the 7.0S (the book calls it 7.0S-770545 (RS9500-SS021-02)) with twelve numbered
arrows and a table:

| Item | Description | Item | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Console | 07 | Bottom Step Cover |
| 02 | Pedal | 08 | Shroud (R) |
| 03 | Console Mast Cover | 09 | Seat Back Cover |
| 04 | Top Cover | 10 | Rear Shroud (R) |
| 05 | End Cap | 11 | Rear Shroud (L) |
| 06 | Shroud (L) | 12 | End Cap, Oval Stabilizer Tube |

The two **Shrouds (6, 8)** are the drive-housing sides - the wiring chapter's "open the left shroud"
is item 6, behind which sit the DC power cable connector, the two optical sensor boards and the
tension motor. The **Rear Shrouds (10, 11)** close the seat-carriage base and the **Bottom Step
Cover (7)** the floor under the pedals; the parts list numbers them Rear Shroud (R) 68, Rear Shroud
(L) 69, Bottom Step Cover 75.

**The 7.5S book on the same platform numbers ten**, with an *Arm Rest* and no rear shrouds or
bottom step cover - `7-5s-med-specs-component-description-ten-numbered-parts`, which the 2025
RS9600-SS021-03 printing repeats. No part numbers, quantities or dimensions are on this page; the
7.0S's specification page is in its owner's manual (`70s-2025-specs-dimensions-and-weight`).
