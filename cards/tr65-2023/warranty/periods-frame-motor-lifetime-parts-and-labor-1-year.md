**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the warranty page of this book, under the heading *Effective September 28, 2023 ... LIMITED WARRANTY*. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**No deck column.** The 2023 TR65 book is the first of the Online-Support layout for the TR range and prints four figures; its 2024 siblings TR75 and TR85 print the same shape with parts 2 years (`xterra-tr-warranty-periods-frame-motor-lifetime-parts-2-years-labor-1-year`). The deck is a part here and carries the one-year parts figure.
