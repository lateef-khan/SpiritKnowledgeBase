**133 rows.** Distinct from its 2024 siblings: this list has a **Bluetooth** row (021-33), *Speed + / - Board* (021-28) and *Incline + / - Board* (021-29), a 3 x 8 mm tapping screw (021-24) and 46 of the 2.3 x 6 mm sheet-metal screws; the TR75 and TR85 lists replace those with speaker rows (`tr75-2024-specs-parts-list-items-1-to-188`, `tr85-2024-specs-parts-list-items-1-to-188`). Rows 090, 102, 103 and 104 - a 13 mm wrench, an M5 Allen-head screw wrench, an M6 L-shaped Allen wrench and the lubricant - are the tools and lube packed in the carton; the Parts Included page of the assembly chapter shows them again.

Which Xterra books print a list at all, and which print only a drawing, is `xterra-treadmill-specs-parts-which-manuals-print-an-exploded-view-and-parts-list`.
