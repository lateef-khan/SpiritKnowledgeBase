The named programs in the table: **P9 Fatburn**, **P12 Cardio**, **P14 Hill** (a speed
pyramid twice over with incline 4 to 9 and back), **P17 Strength** (incline climbs to 10 for
five segments), **P22 HIIT**. The highest speed in the table is **6.2 mph** (P11, P17, P21);
the highest incline is **10** (P17).

The book does not say how the 20 segments are spread over a program time other than the
30-minute default.
