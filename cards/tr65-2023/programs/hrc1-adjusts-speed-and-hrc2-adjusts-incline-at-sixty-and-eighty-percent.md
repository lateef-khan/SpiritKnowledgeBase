**HR1 controls the speed; HR2 controls the incline.** Both target a percentage of 220 minus
your age:

| Program | Preset target | What it adjusts |
|---|---|---|
| HR1 (HRC1) | (220 - age) x 60% | speed |
| HR2 (HRC2) | (220 - age) x 80% | incline |

**Setup**

1. Press **Program** to scroll to HR1 or HR2 and press **Enter**.
2. Press **SPEED up/down** to adjust your **age**, press Enter.
3. Press SPEED up/down to adjust the **target heart rate**, press Enter.
4. Time preset is **30:00**, range **5:00 to 99:00**; adjust with SPEED up/down.
5. Press **START**.
6. Press **STOP** to stop, or pull the safety key away to shut down the computer.

**The hand pulse sensor is to be used. If no heart rate is detected the program stops.**

**How it regulates:** when the heart rate is lower than the target, incline or speed increases
to raise it; when the heart rate reaches the target no change is made; when it is higher,
incline or speed decreases.

The TR6.6's HR-1/HR-2 use the same 60% and 80% but adjust only the incline; see
`not_to_be_confused_with`.
