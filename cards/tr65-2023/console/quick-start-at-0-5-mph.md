After the treadmill is powered on and the safety key is in place, press the **START** key. The treadmill starts at the minimum speed of **0.5 mph**, the time counts up from zero, and all data begins to accrue.

Adjust the speed with the **Fast** and **Slow** buttons and the incline with the **Up** and **Down** buttons; each press moves the speed 0.1 mph or the incline 1 level, and holding a button for 3 seconds moves it rapidly. The direct keys jump straight to a value: **speed shortcut keys 1-10** and **incline shortcut keys 0-10** ([button functions card](button-functions-fast-slow-enter-program-fan.md)).
