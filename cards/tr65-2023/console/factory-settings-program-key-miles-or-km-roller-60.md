The book prints this on its troubleshooting page under "How to enter Factory Settings to change from Miles to Kilometers":

1. Remove the safety key. Press and hold the **PROGRAM** button and reinsert the safety key. Release PROGRAM after reinserting the key.
2. Press the **ENTER** button once to select miles or km — **0 is miles and 1 is km**. Press Speed Up / Down to change the values in each menu.
3. The menus that follow hold the roller diameter (**60**), the speed (**0.5 mph to 10 mph**, or 1 km to 16 km) and the incline (**10**).
4. Stand beside the treadmill and press **START** to start the calibration procedure. The treadmill calibrates itself and leaves factory settings mode when the operation is completed.

**Do not modify any values other than English vs. Metric (miles vs. kilometers).** The book prints no other engineering menu, no sleep setting and no child lock for this machine; the lube message is cleared by holding PROGRAM 3 seconds ([lube card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-clear-the-lube-message-by-holding-program-3-seconds.md)).
