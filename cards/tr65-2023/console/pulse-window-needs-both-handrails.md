The **Pulse** window displays your current heart rate in beats per minute during the workout. To display it you must hold **both handrails**. The book adds the same note as for calories: a rough guide for comparing sessions, not for medical use.

The app page adds that the machine's Bluetooth device (BLE 5.0) can also connect to a Bluetooth wireless heart-rate chest strap transmitter (BLE 5.0), sold separately; the strap can be received even while the app is in use, and while connected the console shows the heart-rate value via Bluetooth ([BLE strap card](../../shared/console/xterra-tr-console-bluetooth-chest-strap-ble-5-0-sold-separately.md)). How to wear a strap is on [the strap card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-chest-strap-not-included-with-a-cr2032-battery.md).
