| Window | What it displays |
|---|---|
| **Speed** | The current speed, from **0.5 mph** to **10.0 mph**. |
| **Time** | Elapsed workout time in minutes up to **99:59**; counts down from a preset target time to 0:00. |
| **Distance** | Distance traveled in miles. |
| **Calories** | Cumulative calories burned (a rough guide, not for medical use). |
| **Pulse** | Current heart rate in beats per minute; you must hold both handrails. Also a rough guide. |
| **Incline** | The incline level, **0 to 10**. |
| **Program** | The program selected: Manual, P1-P24, Fatburn (P9), Cardio (P12), Hill (P14), Strength (P17), HIIT (P22), HR1, HR2, User1 or User2. |
| **Fan** | The fan cools you during the workout. |

The console overview names an LCD window, a Program button, the safety key, an accessory holder, direct incline buttons (left) and direct speed buttons (right). The drawing shows the LCD with an incline figure, a speed dial, distance, time, program, calories and pulse fields; Incline and Speed ▲/▼ either side of it; a Fan key, keys labelled PROGRAM, HILL, FATBURN, CARDIO, STRENGTH and HIIT, an ENTER key, then STOP, the safety key and START. The side panels print the profiles of Fatburn, Cardio, Hill, Strength and HIIT.
