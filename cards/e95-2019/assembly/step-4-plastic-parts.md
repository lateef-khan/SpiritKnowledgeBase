Use screwdriver (185 or 186) for the plastic parts.

1. **Console Mast Covers (96 & 96-1)** with **four Sheet Metal Screws (142)** on the Console Mast (12).
2. **Tablet Holder (196)** on the back of the console with **four Sheet Metal Screws (142)**.
3. **Inner Connecting Arm Covers (111 & 112)** with **two Sheet Metal Screws (142)** and **two Phillips Head Screws (139)** per joint.
4. **Outer Connecting Arm Covers (109 & 110)** on the arms, each side with **one Phillips Head Screw (139)** and **two Sheet Metal Screws (142)**, arrows pointing up or they break.
5. **Sliding Wheel Covers (104)**, each side with **four Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
6. **Stabilizer Covers (117 and 118)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
7. **Front Stabilizer Cover (103)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
8. **Incline Cover brackets (25)**, bent-tab hole facing rearward, with **four Phillips Head Screws (145)**; **Rear incline bar cover (115)** with **two Screws (139)**.
9. **Rear Stabilizer Cover (116)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
10. **Front Handle Bar Cover (L-105) with Rear Handle Bar Cover (L-106)** on the left arm, **four Sheet Metal Screws (142)** with driver (186); repeat for **107 & 108** on the right.
11. **After the elliptical power is plugged in, elevate the incline to Level 8** and install the **Incline Rail front cover (113)** up against the middle stabilizer tube with **four Phillips Head Screws (139)**.
