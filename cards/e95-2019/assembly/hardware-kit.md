The hardware is bagged per assembly step. Numbers in parentheses are assembly-drawing item numbers. Matches the E35-2019 kit except Step 1 #125 and the Step 2 wave washer.

**Hardware Step 1**

| Item | Description | Qty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #164 | 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #170 | 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| #157 | 3/8" x 7T Nylon Nut | 2 pcs |
| #180 | 3/8" x 2T Split Washer | 4 pc |
| #141 | M5 x 10mm Phillips Head Screw | 6 pcs |
| #125 | 3/8" x 3/4" Hex Head Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #182 | 5/16" x 1.5T Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| #183 | 5/16" Star Washer | 4 pcs |
| #133 | 3/8" x 1-1/2" Hex Head Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #135 | 5/16" x 2-1/4" Hex Head Bolt | 4 pcs |

The checklist draws and labels #125 a Hex Head Bolt, but the step-1 text fits "four Socket Head Cap Bolts (125)" with the L Allen wrench. The drawing plus the pack list wins over the step sentence, so this card lists a Hex Head Bolt and the step card carries the warning.
