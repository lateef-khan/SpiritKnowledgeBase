- **Watch item 89.** The XE500 gives the 3/8" x 7T nyloc nut 010108, which is item 94's number, the plain 3/8" x 7T nut; the XE400 list gives the nyloc nut 000080. Kept as printed.
- Sixteen numbered rows print no part number - the incline device (14), incline motor holder (15), pedal adjustment assembly (19), console holder assembly (20), on/off switch (40), cover holder (156), adjusting plate (176) and nine fasteners; 168, the controller back plate, is `X`.
- Item 150, `Incline Controller (Lower Board) ALT#670100A`, is 006095 with an alternate number printed in the description; its transformer 150~1 is 006096. The incline motor is 006144 (the XE400's is 006143); the gear motor 022170, power cord 022812 and the incline wiring (006068, 006091, 006092, 000183) are the XE400's.
- Item 32~9, `Face Plate Lens Cover` 006157, is on no other list; 129 is printed `12/14mm_Wrench` (000342, the 12 mm wrench elsewhere) and 128 `Split Washer` for the 3/8" x 2T spring washer 000053. Items 70 and 70~1 are the two round head end caps, 2.5T 000841 and 1.8T 022069.
- The three unnumbered rows are the Pedal Elevation Gauge Decal (006118), the Hardware Kit (006133) and the "Up/Down" pedal sticker (006106).

**Order against the serial number.** The same book's parts purchase order treats a line without a serial number as a stock order (`spirit-xt-2007-policy-parts-purchase-order-form`); the owner's manual for this machine prints drawings but no list at all (`spirit-ce-assembly-exploded-view-and-parts-list`), so this is the only place its part numbers are printed. Which elliptical service manuals print a list is on `spirit-elliptical-specs-parts-which-service-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.
