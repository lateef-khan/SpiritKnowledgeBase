**The service manual's console drawing labels four things: "7.5" LCD Panel", "Wireless Charger",
"FAN Button" and "Control Buttons".** The charger label points at a phone symbol on the right of the
console face, below the screen. The text under Basic Operation, word for word:

> You can charge your personal device during your workout using the fitness equipment's wireless
> charger. To charge your mobile electronics make sure the fitness equipment power is on.
> Step 1: Ensure your device is wireless chargeable.
> Step 2: Put the device in landscape, and ensure the device is in the charge area.
> Step 3: If the wireless charge does not start, please adjust the position of the device to match
> the charge circuit. A few Tablets are unavailable because of the nominal current is not enough to
> supply tablets.

**The owner's manual for the same machine describes a USB charging port instead** - "Charge
Portable Devices with USB port", 5 Vdc at 1.0 amp, no data, and a 40 RPM minimum because the
generator makes the power (`spirit-xe-console-usb-port-charges-a-device-but-saves-no-data`,
`xe795-2023-console-usb-charging-needs-forty-rpm`). Its console drawing labels **USB Charging**
where this one labels Wireless Charger
(`xe795-2023-console-overview-and-workout-mode-call-outs`). **Neither document mentions the other,
and nothing in the repository says which the shipped console has.** Look at the console: a USB
socket or a marked charge pad on the right side of the face settles it.

**The service manual contradicts itself.** Its own Display Board wire Connections page (6.1, PDF
p. 10) labels connector **J4** of display board CS11039-01ES **USB CHARGE** - the same board and
label the XBR95-2023 bike book prints - and nothing on that page is called wireless. So the book's
wiring says USB and its operation chapter says wireless.

**The wireless paragraph is the Sole one.** It is printed word for word in the Sole E35-2023
service manual and its siblings (`e35-2023-wireless-charger`), which are the Dyaco books this
chapter was assembled from; the same page calls the machine "the Recumbent Elliptical" under Direct
Resistance Buttons. The XBR95-2023 bike's service manual, otherwise this book's twin, prints no
charger paragraph of either kind.

**The drawing also answers a question the owner's manual leaves open.** Its direct level buttons
are marked **2, 6, 10, 14, 18, 22, 26, 30, 34, 40** - ten keys across the 40 levels
(`xe795-2023-console-direct-resistance-buttons`) - under a LEVEL label, with FAN, LEVEL -, START,
STOP, LEVEL +, ENTER and PROGRAM keys below them.
