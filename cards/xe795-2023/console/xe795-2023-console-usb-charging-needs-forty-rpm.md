**The manual's note, word for word: "Minimum speed must reach to 40 RPM or above to generate enough
power for USB charging."**

**This machine has no mains supply.** Its console and its USB port both run off the built-in
generator, so charging stops when you stop pedalling hard enough:
`spirit-xe795-console-power-up-by-pedalling`.

**A phone that charges while you work and stops when you rest is behaving as designed**, not
faulting.

**The XE395-2023 has no such note** because it is powered from a cord. Its identical three USB notes
are on `spirit-xe-console-usb-port-charges-a-device-but-saves-no-data`, and it carries **no RPM
condition at all**.

**40 RPM is the only figure the manual gives**, and it says nothing about what the port does between
zero and 40 RPM beyond not generating enough power.
