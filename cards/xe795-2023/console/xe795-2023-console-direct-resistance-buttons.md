**You can set your resistance level quickly by pressing the buttons on the console.** Choose the
desired level and **the elliptical automatically adjusts to that level**. **This saves time because
you do not have to press and hold, or hold a button down, until reaching the desired value.**

**The console drawing labels them "Direct Resistance Level Buttons"**, beside the **Program
Buttons**, under the LCD window.

**There is no incline on this machine and no incline button.** The workout-mode drawing labels
**Current Speed** in the two places where the XE395-2023 drawing labels **Current Incline**, and the
word incline appears nowhere in this console section.

**The manual does not say how many buttons there are or which levels they carry**, and this machine
has **40 resistance levels**, so there is certainly not one button per level. **Do not tell a
customer which button is which level.**

**The XE395-2023 version of this feature covers incline as well**:
`xe395-2023-console-direct-resistance-and-incline-buttons`.
