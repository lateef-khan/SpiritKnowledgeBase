**The console face, as labelled on the overview drawing:** **LCD Window**, **Adjustable Fan**, **USB
Charging**, **Direct Resistance Level Buttons** and **Program Buttons**. **There is no Child Lock
key.**

**The workout screen, as labelled on the workout-mode drawing:**

| Where | Call-out |
|---|---|
| Top row | **Total Calories Burned**, **Current Heart Rate**, **Cumulative Total Mileage**, **Current Resistance Level**, **Current Revolutions per minute**, **Total Laps Completed** |
| Left edge | **Current Speed** |
| Right edge | **Current Resistance Level**, **Maximum Heart Rate Percentage** |
| Left column of icons | **USB Charge Icon**, **Bluetooth Speaker**, **Bluetooth Heart Rate Icon**, **Bluetooth On/Off**, **Message Center** |
| Bottom | **Current Speed Level**, **Current Time (Elapsed or Countdown based on Program)**, **Resistance Program Profile** |

**Where the XE395-2023 labels incline, this machine labels speed.** The XE395-2023 drawing reads
**Current Incline** and **Current Incline Level** in the same two places; this one reads **Current
Speed** and **Current Speed Level**. **This machine has no incline.**

**Total Laps Completed is labelled on the drawing** even though the manual's Workout Summary for this
machine lists no Laps field, and nothing in its text describes a lap track. The drawing is the only
place laps appear.

**The numbers printed inside the drawing are the manual's sample values.**

**The XE395-2023 drawing is the same layout with incline and a Child Lock key**:
`xe395-2023-console-overview-and-workout-mode-call-outs`.

**Three 2023 Spirit residential bikes print the same two drawings**, so this card is no longer
elliptical-only: the **XBR95-2023** (pp. 17-18) and the **XBR55-2023** and **XBU55-2023** (pp. 18-19).
Same five face call-outs, same workout-screen table above, **Current Speed** and **Current Speed
Level** in the same two places, and no Child Lock key on any of the three.

**Both pages are flattened images in all three bike manuals.** `pdftotext` returns 17 and 62 words
from them; the labels were read by rendering the pages at 300 dpi.

**On the bikes, Total Laps Completed is labelled the same way and is just as unsupported.** None of
the three prints a 1/4 Mile Track paragraph and none reports a Laps field on its Workout Summary,
exactly as on the XE795-2023 - but the FEATURES page does list Laps completed among the readings the
console can show:
`spirit-residential-bike-console-readings-and-resistance-profile-graph`.

**The XBR25-2023 is deliberately not on this card.** Its overview drawing labels four things, not
five, and its workout screen carries none of the USB or Bluetooth icons and no Maximum Heart Rate
Percentage: `spirit-residential-bike-console-face-without-usb-or-bluetooth`.
