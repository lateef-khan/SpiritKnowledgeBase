One page, two drawings with red call-out numbers and a **No. / Description** table beside each.

**1.1 Plastic parts**

| No. | Description | No. | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Console | 8 | Pedal (R) |
| 2 | Handlebar Covers (R) | 9 | Pedal (L) |
| 3 | Handlebar Covers (L) | 10 | Slide Wheel Cover (R) |
| 4 | Console Mast Cover (R) | 11 | Slide Wheel Cover (L) |
| 5 | Console Mast Cover (L) | 12 | Chain Cover (R) |
| 6 | Round Disc Cover | 13 | Chain Cover (L) |
| 7 | Round Disc | 14 | *(blank)* |

**1.2 Structures**

| No. | Description | No. | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Console Holder Assembly | 10 | Cross Bar |
| 2 | Console Mast | 11 | Flywheel |
| 3 | Upper Handlebar (L) | 12 | Drive Pulley |
| 4 | Upper Handlebar (R) | 13 | Connecting Arm (R) |
| 5 | Lower Handlebar (L) | 14 | Connecting Arm (L) |
| 6 | Lower Handlebar (R) | 15 | Rear Rail Assembly |
| 7 | Main Frame | 16 | Rear Stabilizer |
| 8 | Pedal Arm (L) | 17 | Front Stabilizer |
| 9 | Pedal Arm (R) | 18 | *(blank)* |

Rows 14 and 18 are printed with a number and no name. *Chain Cover* is this book's name for the
side cases. **No part numbers, quantities, dimensions or weights** are printed. The 2016 book's
drawings carry free-text labels for a differently trimmed machine
(`xe795-2016-specs-outline-part-names`).
