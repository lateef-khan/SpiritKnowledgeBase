**2.1 Console** labels two things on a photograph of the XE795 console: **Cooling Fan** (the grille
below the LCD) and **Speaker** (the grille at the bottom corner of the console). Neither the LCD nor the
keypad gets a callout on this page.

**2.2 Controller and Driver parts** labels three on a photograph of the whole machine, each with an
inset: **Speed Sensor** (the reed switch and magnet inside the side case), **Induction Brake** (the
flywheel with its coil pack, what the Electrical Configurations page calls the Generator Brake) and
**Brake Controller** (a red driver board with a large capacitor, low in the frame).

**The 2016 XE795 book (XE815-SE024) labels six parts with different names** - Speaker, Cooling FAN and
DISPLAY above, SPEED SENSOR, Driver board and HYBRID GENERATOR below
(`xe795-2016-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`). Same machine layout, one generation apart: the 2023
book calls the driver board a Brake Controller and the generator an Induction Brake, and drops the display
callout.

**No part number or rating is printed on the page.** The board silkscreen visible in the chapter-6
photographs and the circuit diagram belong to the specs cards for those chapters; the only tool the book
lists is a multi-meter (`spirit-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`).
