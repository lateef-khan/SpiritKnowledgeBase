Section 3 names three parts:

- **Console** - interface that controls all functions; key controls and an **LCD touch panel**.
- **Controller** - "consists of the generator power supply for console, link the console to output
  appropriate voltages for **tension motor**".
- **Generator Brake** - "can change to increase or decrease resistance level of brake".

The Window Display line below names a **7.5" LCD Panel** and a **Wireless Charger**; the Function
page adds that the machine has **40 resistance levels** and powers up from the generator when
pedalled - there is no adapter and no mains cord.

**There is no tension motor on this machine.** The controller paragraph is the same boilerplate the
CE800 books carry; the block diagram, controller and circuit diagram show a generator, a
CS52005-23 board and a brake coil, and no motor. **No voltage figure is printed** - the 2016 book's
DC 0.4-14 V brake working voltage is gone
(`xe795-2016-specs-hybrid-generator-brake-working-voltage-dc-0-4-to-14-v`).

The 2023 XBR95 bike prints the same three paragraphs
(`xbr95-2023-specs-electrical-configuration-generator-brake`). The owner's manual calls the
charger a USB port (`xe795-2023-console-wireless-charger-in-the-service-manual-usb-port-in-the-owners-manual`).
