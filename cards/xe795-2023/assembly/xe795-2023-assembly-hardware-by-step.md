## Item 106 is printed as 5/6 inch

There is no 5/6" fastener. Every other washer of that family in this book is
**5/16"**, items 84, 86 and 90 among them. **The manual prints `5/6"`** and this card
does not correct it; order item 106 by its number. The XE395 2010 manual has the same
misprint on its items 74 and 99.

## Item 51 is called a flat washer here and a bushing washer in the step

The panel says **3/8" x 30 x 2T Flat Washer**; step three job 3 says to secure the
swing arm with "the **BUSHING WASHER (51)**". The 2021 panel called item 51 a bushing
washer and gave its size as M10. One part, three descriptions across two books.

## Step five supplies fourteen screws and step five uses six

Six on the console and water bottle holder in step five itself. Step six then uses
**eight more of the same item 55** on the pedals, and its own panel carries none.
The two counts together close exactly at fourteen; **the fourteen are all bagged with
step five**, so do not throw the extras away when step five is finished.

## Items 85 and 91 are both M8 x 15mm and are not the same bolt

**85 is a hex head bolt**, eight of them, holding the console mast to the frame.
**91 is a button head socket bolt**, six of them, driven with a 5mm L wrench, holding
the connection brackets to the connecting arms. The 2021 panel called both simply
"BOLT, M8 X 15L" and the step text called item 91 a *key head* socket bolt; this
printing names the heads properly.

## Items 113 to 116 exist only on this machine

They are the upper-to-lower swing arm joint, which the 2021 XE795 does not have - its
swing arm is one piece. Six bolts, six nuts, eight flat washers and four curved
washers for the two sides.

## What can be checked against the parts list

This manual carries a numbered parts list at pp. 38-40, ordinary text and
trustworthy. Its exploded view at p. 37 is line art; **do not read a callout off it
by machine.**
