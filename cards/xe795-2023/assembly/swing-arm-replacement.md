**This is the 2023 book's procedure. The 2016 XE795 book prints a different, two-step job from a five-way pipe
fixing plate with an 8 mm Allen bolt.**

1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **sheet metal screws 3.5x12mm** securing the swing arm covers (front and
   back). (Figure 1)
2. Use **#12 and #13 open end wrenches** to hold the swing arm assembly and remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x
   1-1/4"**, **flat washer 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T** and **nylon nut 5/16" x 7T**. (Figure 2)
3. **Disconnect the wiring** connecting the upper and lower swing arms.
4. Use a **14 mm wrench** to release the **3/8" x 3/4" hex head bolt** together with the **3/8" x 30 x 1.5T flat washer**
   which secure the swing arms. (Figure 3)
5. Use #12 and #13 open end wrenches to hold the swing arm assembly and remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 1-1/4"**,
   **flat washer 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T** and **nylon nut 5/16" x 7T**. Remove the swing arm assembly. (Figures 4 and 5)
6. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.

**Steps 2 and 5 print the same bolt, washer and nut.** The book removes the `5/16" x 1-1/4"` rod-end hardware
before the wiring disconnect and again after the pivot bolt. Read it as two rod-end joints (one per figure), not
as one bolt removed twice; the book does not say which.
