## Step one - levellers, end caps, transport wheels and roller covers

1. **Tilt the elliptical to one side** - Styrofoam or a block underneath helps - and
   install the **3 LEVELERS (81)** on the underside. Repeat on the other side.
2. Press the **6 END CAPS (82)** into the oval stabilizer tubes.
3. Attach the **TRANSPORT WHEELS (80)** to each bracket with 2 **BOLTS (92)** and 2
   **NUTS (99)**. **Partially tighten only.** Use the 13/14mm wrench and a 5mm L
   wrench.
4. Fit the **ROLLER WHEEL COVERS (45)** with 4 **SCREWS (55)**.

## Step two - console mast and upright covers

1. Feed the twist tie wire and **COMPUTER CABLE (27)** into the bottom of the
   **CONSOLE MAST (2)** and out of the top, without pinching it.
2. Secure the mast to the frame with **3 BOLTS (85)** and **3 FLAT WASHERS (84)** to
   **each side** of the mast, and a **BOLT (85)** with a **CURVED WASHER (86)** to
   both the front and the back. Tighten with the 13/14 wrench.
3. Bring **UPRIGHT COVER LEFT (43)** and **UPRIGHT COVER RIGHT (56)** together at the
   mast and secure with 3 **SHEET METAL SCREWS (56)**.

## Step three - swing arms onto the mast

1. Remove the foam from the console mast axles and the plastic ties from the swing arm
   tube.
2. The arms are labelled L and R. Slide the **WAVE WASHER (110)** and the **LEFT SWING
   ARM (5)** onto the left axle. **The fit is snug; do not force it or use a hammer.**
3. Secure with the **BUSHING WASHER (51)** and the **SOCKET HEAD CAP BOLT (89)**,
   tightened with the **8mm L wrench**.
4. Snap on the decorative **BUSHING COVER (79)**, then repeat on the right.

## Step four - connecting arms, upper swing arms and pedal arms

1. Slide the axle of the **LEFT CONNECTING ARM (9)** through the rotation tube on the
   left pedal arm.
2. Secure it with the **LEFT CONNECTION BRACKET (15)** and 3 **BUTTON HEAD SOCKET
   BOLTS (91)**, tightened with the **5mm L wrench**.
3. **Connect the upper swing arm to the lower swing arm** with a **3/8" x 2-1/4" Hex
   Head Bolt (113)**, a **Ø3/8" x Ø19 x 1.5T Flat Washer (115)**, a **Ø3/8" x 23 x
   2.0T Curved Washer (116)** and a **3/8" x 7T Nylon Nut (114)**, positioned as the
   drawing shows.
4. Line the socket joint at the end of the pedal arm up with the bracket on the bottom
   of the swing arm and secure with the **BOLT (90)**, **WASHER (106)** and **NUT
   (87)**, tightened with the 13/14 and 12/14 wrenches.
5. Repeat on the right.

## Step five - console and water bottle holder

1. Secure the **CONSOLE (26)** to the console mast with 4 **SCREWS (55)**.
2. Secure the **WATER BOTTLE HOLDER (83)** to the console mast with 2 **SCREWS (55)**.

## Step six - handle bar covers, pedals and tablet holder

1. Front Handle Bar Cover (L) **(132)**, Rear Handle Bar Cover (L) **(133)**, Front
   Handle Bar Cover (R) **(134)** and Rear Handle Bar Cover (R) **(135)** onto the
   swing arm with **8 3.5 x 12mm Sheet Metal Screws (117)**.
2. The Left and Right **Pedals (47)** onto the pedal plate with **4 Screws (55) on
   each side**.
3. The **Tablet Holder Assembly (136)** under the console with **3 M5 x 12mm Phillips
   Head Screws (123)**.

## Three things this build does that the 2021 build does not

- **The swing arm is two pieces.** Step four job 3 joins an upper arm to a lower arm
  with a bolt, two washers and a nylon nut - items 113 to 116, none of which exists on
  the 2021 machine. The 2021 XE795 swing arm is one piece.
- **Handle bar covers**, items 132 to 135, fitted in step six with eight sheet metal
  screws of item 117. The 2021 build fits no handle bar cover at all.
- **A tablet holder**, item 136, fitted in step six with three screws of item 123.
  **It is not on the carton list** - see
  [the 2023 carton list](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xe-assembly-parts-included-2023.md).

## The mast bolt count is stated clearly here and was not in 2021

"3 BOLTS (85) and 3 FLAT WASHERS (84) to each side" plus one bolt at the front and one
at the back comes to **eight bolts and six washers**, which is exactly what the
hardware panel supplies. The 2021 printings said "8 BOLTS (85) and 6 FLAT WASHERS (84)
to each side of the mast", which reads as sixteen. Same machine, same panel, clearer
sentence.

## Still no cable connection step, and still no power cord

As on the 2021 machine, no step plugs anything into the console: step two feeds the
computer cable up the mast and step five screws the console on. The console is
generator powered - start pedalling and it wakes up. There is no power cord in the
carton and nothing to plug in.
