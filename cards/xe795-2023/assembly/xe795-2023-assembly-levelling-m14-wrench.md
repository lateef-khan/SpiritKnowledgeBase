**Use a M14 Wrench to adjust the height of the Leveling Feet.**

That single sentence is the whole of it. The manual gives no leveller count in this
paragraph, no direction of turn, no lock nut and no order of adjustment.

## Six levellers, fitted in step one

The build itself puts **3 LEVELERS (81) on each side, six in all**, with the machine
tipped over onto a block. The carton list says **6 Levelers**. Neither the step nor
this paragraph says which of the six to set first.

## No 14mm tool is in the carton

The tools panel holds a 13/14mm wrench, a 12/14mm wrench, a Phillips screwdriver, an
8mm L Allen wrench and one unlabelled L Allen wrench. **Both double ended wrenches
carry a 14mm jaw**, so use either. See
[the tools card](xe795-2023-assembly-tools-included.md).

## The XE395 2023 has no such paragraph

Its Setting Up chapter carries Notice About Pedals, Plugging in and Powering On, and
Moving The Elliptical - **and no Leveling section**. Its only levelling instruction is
the last job of assembly step four, which names four levellers, a top nut and a
clockwise turn. See
[that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xe-assembly-levelling-top-nut.md).

## The XE795 2021 printings say nothing about levelling at all

Both 2021 printings fit the same six levellers in the same step one and then never
mention setting them. This paragraph is new in 2023.

## M14 is the manual's spelling

An M14 thread and a 14mm spanner size are not the same thing, and the sentence says
"M14 Wrench". Read it as a 14mm wrench; that is what every other Spirit levelling
instruction in the repository specifies.
