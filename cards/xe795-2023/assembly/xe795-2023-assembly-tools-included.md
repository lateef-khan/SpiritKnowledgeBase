| Item | Tool | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 93 | 13/14mm Wrench | 1 |
| 94 | Phillips Head Screwdriver | 1 |
| 95 | 8mm L Allen Wrench | 1 |
| 97 | L Allen Wrench | 1 |
| 98 | 12/14mm Wrench | 1 |

**Five tools.** Item 97's size is not printed anywhere in the manual - the panel
calls it simply an **L Allen Wrench**. The steps that use an L wrench name a **5MM L
WRENCH** for the transport wheels and the connection bracket bolts, so item 97 is
very likely the 5mm; **the manual never says so** and this card will not claim it.

## The 8mm is in the box on this machine and not on the 2021

The **XE795 2021** build names an 8MM L WRENCH in its step three and a 5MM L WRENCH in
steps one and four, and its four-tool carton holds neither. The 2023 carton supplies
both. See
[the 2021 tools card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xe-assembly-tools-2021.md).

## No 14mm wrench, and this machine asks for one

Its own Leveling note says to **use an M14 wrench** to adjust the height of the
levelling feet. **No plain 14mm tool is in this panel** - the two double ended
wrenches carry a 14mm jaw and will do it. See
[the levelling card](xe795-2023-assembly-levelling-m14-wrench.md).

## The XE395 2023 carton is not this carton

The XE395 2023 holds three tools and no Allen key at all.
