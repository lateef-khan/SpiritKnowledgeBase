**This is the XE795-2023 service manual's figure: 220 volt at 15 amp, on a page that names a nominal
120-volt circuit three paragraphs later - and the machine it is printed for has no power cord.**

7.1 Important Safety Instructions:

> To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons, install the Elliptical on a
> flat level surface with access to a 220-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet with only the Elliptical
> plugged into the circuit.

7.3 Important Grounding Instructions, on the same page:

> This product is for use on a nominal 120-volt circuit and has a grounding plug that looks like the
> plug illustrated below.

- **Nothing on the page reconciles 220 with 120.** The treadmill service manuals that print 220 volt
  put a bracketed 120-volt figure beside it and open with a CE/CEGS note
  (`spirit-treadmill-safety-outlet-220-volt-10-amp`); this book prints no bracket and no such note.
  It is the 2023 bike service manuals' page word for word, with `Elliptical` for `bike`
  (`spirit-xb-2023-safety-outlet-220-volt-15-amp`). **15 amp, not the treadmills' 10 amp.**
- **The rest of the chapter is the plug-in boilerplate**: 16 AWG extension cord, never a GFCI outlet,
  route the cord away from `the elevation mechanism and transport wheels`, the Grainger 1D237 /
  QO120HM breaker paragraph, and the grounding page with its temporary-adapter drawing - none of
  which the XE795-2023 owner's manual prints, because that book has no electrical page at all
  (`spirit-xe-safety-no-outlet-figure-printed`).

**The machine is self-powered, and the same book says so.** Its Basic Operation table opens
`Power - The console will automatically power up when the user is pedaling to generate the power`,
its Electrical Configurations page says the controller `consist[s] of the generator power supply for
console` with a `Generator Brake` for resistance, and its circuit diagram draws a `Generator` feeding
the controller with no plug, adapter or cord anywhere on it. The owner's manual's parts list has no
power cord (`spirit-ce-safety-generator-produces-ac-power`,
`spirit-xe795-console-power-up-by-pedalling`).

So a customer asking what outlet an XE795-2023 needs should be told: none - it has nothing to plug
in; the service manual prints a 220-volt, 15-amp sentence and a 120-volt sentence on one page as
carried-over boilerplate, and the owner's manual prints no figure. Quote both sentences with the book
they come from if asked, and do not send anyone to install a circuit. The XE795-2016 service manual
does the same thing with a 115-volt figure (`spirit-xe-safety-outlet-115-volt-15-amp`), and the
LED-console CE800 and CE900 service manuals say in words that no outlet is needed
(`spirit-ce-safety-no-mains-outlet-needed`).
