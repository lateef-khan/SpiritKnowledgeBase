Hardware: **#71** 3/8" x 1-3/4" Hex Head Bolt (2 pcs), **#77** 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T Flat Washer (4 pcs), **#89** 3/8" x 7T Nyloc Nut (4 pcs), **#175** 3/8" x 2-3/4" Hex Head Bolt (2 pcs).

1. Install the Seat Handle Bar (6) onto the Seat Carriage (4) with **four Hex Head Bolts (2 - 71 & 2 - 175)**, four Flat Washers (77) and four Nyloc Nuts (89), using the **12/14mm Wrench (112)** and the **14/15mm Wrench (132)**.
2. Plug the two Hand pulse Cables (21 & 27) into the two rear receiver ports (26) located on the **left side rear cover, just under the seat**.

**This wording does not say which bolt goes where.** The R92 manuals of the same period say the two #71 go in **vertically** and the two #175 go in **horizontally**. The LCR 2011 manual only gives the counts. #71 is 1-3/4" long and #175 is 2-3/4"; they are not interchangeable.

The LCR 2013 adds a third instruction about which hand pulse connector goes into which port. The LCR 2011 does not have it.
