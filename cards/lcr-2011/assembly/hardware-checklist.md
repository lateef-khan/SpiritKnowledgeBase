Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #65 | 3/8" x 2-1/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #77 | 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #84 | 3/8" x 25 x 2T | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| #176 | 3/8" x 3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 6 pcs |
| #99 | M5 x 15mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #71 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #77 | 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| #89 | 3/8" x 7T | Nyloc Nut | 4 pcs |
| #175 | 3/8" x 2-3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 3**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #68 | 5/16" x 5/8" | Hex Head Bolt | 8 pcs |
| #76 | 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #82 | 5/16" x 1.5T | Split Washer | 2 pcs |
| #83 | 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T | Curved Washer | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 4**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #98 | M6 x 15mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| #99 | M5 x 12mm | Phillips Head Screw | 8 pcs |
| #105 | 4 x 16mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |

**Three separate contradictions in this one checklist. All are left exactly as printed.**

1. **`#99` is listed twice as two different screws** - `M5 x 15mm (4 pcs)` in step 1 and `M5 x 12mm (8 pcs)` in step 4. The step 1 instruction on page 6 does not use #99 at all; it fits the seat back cover with "four Phillips Head Screws (**136**)". The LCR 2013 and 2016 checklists print `#136 M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screw (4 pcs)` in step 1, which suggests #99 in step 1 here is a misprint for #136 - but this manual never says so.
2. **`#98` is M6 x 15mm in this checklist but M5 x 15mm in the hardware box printed beside the step 4 instruction** on page 8.
3. **The step 1 instruction calls the six 3/8" x 3/4" bolts `(167)`** while the checklist and the step 1 hardware box both call them **`#176`**. No item 167 appears anywhere in the checklist.

**Item numbers are not shared with the B94 or LCB uprights.** Here `#71` is a hex head bolt and `#99` is a Phillips head screw; on the uprights `#71` is a flat washer and `#99` is a curved washer. Always use the checklist printed in your own machine's manual.
