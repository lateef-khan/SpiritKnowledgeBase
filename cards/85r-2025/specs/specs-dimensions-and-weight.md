| Figure | Printed |
|---|---|
| **Length** | **171 cm (67.3")** |
| **Width** | **83 cm (32.7")** |
| **Height** | **127 cm (50")** |
| **Product weight** | **108.9 kgs (240 lbs)** |

All four convert correctly, and the render agrees with the text layer. The width is the same 83 cm
the 7.0R recumbent prints, but this machine is 31 cm longer and 27 kg heavier
(`70r-2025-specs-dimensions-and-weight`) - the rail, the removable swivel seat and the wheelchair
anchor are the difference.

**The seat comes off.** The features page says "Roll the removable seat away to allow for direct
wheelchair access", so the 171 cm is the machine with the seat on; no figure is printed for the frame
alone or for the seat.

No shipping weight or carton size is printed. The 8.0U upright's page prints a footprint that looks
like a recumbent's and should not be trusted without confirmation
(`80u-2025-specs-dimensions-and-weight`); the 8.5S stepper on the same platform is 220 x 86 cm
(`spirit-climber-85s-specs-dimensions-and-weight`).
