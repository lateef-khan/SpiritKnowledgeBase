**The owner's manual prints the drawings and the service manual prints the list.** The 8.5R owner's manual gives two exploded-view pages - the drive frame (balloons 1 to 170, stamped `2025.11.04`) and the rotating seat (balloons 501 to 618, stamped `2025/06/11`) - and then goes straight to the warranty; nothing in that book decodes a balloon. The MR2000-SB036-01 service manual prints the drive-frame drawing again and, over six pages, **the list that decodes both drawings**. **No Spirit part number is printed in either book.**

**Two number series.** 119 rows run 1 to 170 (drive frame, console, electronics, hardware) and 112 rows run 501 to 618 (the rotating seat, its rail, gas cylinder and hardware). 392 whole numbers are absent between 1 and 618 - most of them the unused block 171 to 500 - so a missing number is a gap, not a missing page. Lettered positions: 8L / 8R crank arms, 9(A) / 9(B) / 9(C) idler wheel assemblies, 24-1 and 24-2 the drive pulley and magnet on the crank axle. The service manual's drawing writes them `008-L`, `09-A`, `24-1`.

**The seat series is shared with the 8.5UE and the 8.5S.** The 8.5UE service manual heads its copy of the second drawing `MR2000/MS2000/MZ2000` with the same `2025/06/11` date, and its rows 501-618 match this list in all but a handful of wordings and three quantities (`85ue-2025-specs-parts-list`).

**The 8.0U service manual prints this list too, and it does not fit the 8.0U** - see `80u-2025-specs-parts-exploded-view-with-no-matching-list` before quoting a number for the upright.

## What the list names that the prose does not

- Electronics: 70 `Console Assembly`, 71 `Interface Board`, 76 `AC Electronic Module`, 81 `Switching Power Supply`, 84 `Hall Module`, 86 `Flywheel`, 91 `Generator/Brake Controller`, 95 `Power Cord (Optional)`; cables 72-75 and 89-90 by length and connector (PHP-9, XHP-4, XHP-6), 87-88 brake-coil harnesses, 79 and 82 ground wires.
- The manual brake: 4 `Brake Lever`, 5 `Upright Brake Shaft`, 6 `Steel Cable`, 18 `Brake Pad`, 17 `Tension Rod Assembly`.
- The seat: 501 `Seat Rotation`, 502 `Seat Carriage`, 528 `Gas Cylinder`, 529 `Rotate Disk`, 535 twenty-seven `Ø 31 × 16.2T_Nylon wheel`, 538 `Seat`, 537 `Seat Back`, 550 `Beverage Holder`.
- Tools packed as items: 152 `Phillips Head Screw Driver`, 154 `13/15mm_Wrench`, 155 `3mm_L Allen Wrench`, 156 `6mm_ L Allen Wrench`, 611 `8mm L Allen Wrench`, 612 `13/14mm_Wrench`, 613 `L Allen Wrench`. The service manual's own tool list is a different thing - eighteen bench tools (`85s-2025-specs-parts-required-maintenance-tools-eighteen`).
