| 7 | Handgrip | 14 | Seat Back |

**Items 9 and 12 carry the same name.** On the picture 9 points to the yellow lever on the seat's
right side and 12 to the yellow loop lever at the front of the seat; the parts list separates them as
*Seat Rotation Release Lever* (505) and *Seat Release Lever* (507) with *Adjusting Lever* (511 and
517) and *Adjusting Rod* (520), so read 8 and 12 as the fore-aft and swivel releases and 13 as the
seat-back angle rod. The yellow **Brake Lever (2)** on the mast is the flywheel stop the
preventive-maintenance chapter warns not to over-tighten at its M5 x 40 mm screw.

**No part numbers, quantities or dimensions are on this page.** The upright 8.0U numbers twenty-two
components (`80u-2025-specs-component-description-twenty-two-numbered-parts`); the 8.5S stepper on the
same platform numbers nineteen (`85s-2025-specs-component-description-nineteen-numbered-parts`).
