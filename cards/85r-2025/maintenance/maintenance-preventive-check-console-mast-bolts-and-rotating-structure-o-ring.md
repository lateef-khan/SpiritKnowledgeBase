**Two structural checks, no interval, no torque.**

- **3.2 Front console mast.** Check the **socket head cap bolts (M8 × 3)** that secure the console mast; if loose,
  tighten them - "resolve Console Mast sway issue" (figure 3.2.1). The 8.0U upright's mast is held by eight
  screws, not three (`80u-2025-maintenance-preventive-check-console-mast-handgrip-stabilizer-rear-stabilizer-and-seat-slide`).
- **3.4 Rotating structure assembly.** "Check if Rotating Structure Assembly works smoothly." To reach it:
  loosen the **4 screws** that lock it on the console mast (figure 3.4.1), **pull the rotating structure assembly
  out** (figure 3.4.2), disconnect the **three wirings** and remove the **O-ring** (figures 3.4.3, 3.4.4).

The rotating structure is the console's swivel. The chapter says what to check - that it turns smoothly - and
how to get to the O-ring, and nothing about what to do with the O-ring once it is out; the same step is printed
on the 8.5UE ergometer as "Check and Troubleshooting for a console O-ring"
(`85ue-2025-maintenance-preventative-check-screws-on-pedal-circular-ring-rail-o-ring-and-seat-carriage`) and on the
8.0T treadmill as "Resolving electronic console O-ring breakage", which says the same four things. Read it as
a replacement route: a console that will not swivel smoothly has its O-ring reached this way.

The chapter's other two items are the pedals and crank arms (`cu900ent-check-pedals`) and the emergency-stop
screw (`85r-2025-maintenance-emergency-stop-screw-too-tight-or-adjust-the-cable`). A handgrip that shakes in use
is sent back to 3.2 by the troubleshooting chapter
(`spirit-med-bike-maintenance-sway-handgrip-seat-and-whole-bike`).
