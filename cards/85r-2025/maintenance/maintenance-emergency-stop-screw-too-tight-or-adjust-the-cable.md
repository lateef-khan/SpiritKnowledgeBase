**The screw before the cable.** 3.3 Check for Emergency Stop, as printed:

> As Figure 3.3.1, check if M5 × 40mm_Phillips Head Screw (1 PCS) is too tight and get the brake stuck or work
> improperly. If so, please loosen the screw. If not, please adjust the cable.

1. Find the single **M5 × 40 mm Phillips head screw** at the brake (figure 3.3.1).
2. If it is **too tight** - the brake sticks or works improperly - **loosen the screw**.
3. If the screw is not the cause, **adjust the cable**.

No cable travel, no torque and no test figure is printed; the test is that the brake stops sticking.

**The 7.0R and 7.0U answer the opposite complaint.** Their mechanical brake lever goes *loose* as the felt wears,
and the cure is to shorten the steel cable behind a two-screw cover
(`spirit-med-bike-maintenance-brake-release-lever-loose-from-felt-wear-adjust-the-steel-cable`). Here the brake
*sticks*, and the first suspect is a screw, not the cable. Do not read one onto the other.

**This is the manual stop, not the pedal resistance.** Resistance on the 8.5R is an induction brake driven by the
console; a resistance complaint is answered from the electronic troubleshooting chapter. The brake pad itself is
a replacement (6.7), reached with an open-end wrench and an L Allen wrench.
