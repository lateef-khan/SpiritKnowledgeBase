**The crank arms come off first here; on the 8.0U they come off last, and only for the disk.**

1. As shown in figure 6.4.1, refer to 6.3 to remove the **crank arms**. The **round disks** on both sides can then be removed.
2. As shown in figures 6.4.2 and 6.4.3, loosen the **four screws** that secure the chain cover at both sides.
3. As shown in figure 6.4.4, **unlock the hidden clips with flathead screwdrivers**. *(Please be gentle - do not apply too much force or the clips break.)*
4. As shown in figure 6.4.5, remove the **sheet metal screws (M5 x 2 pcs)** to remove the chain covers.
5. As shown in figure 6.4.6, remove the **sheet metal screw and flat washer** from the inner side of the chain cover.

No screw lengths are printed. The parts list carries **Chain Cover (L) (37)**, **Chain Cover (R) (38)** and **Round Disk (41), 2 pcs**, and the component drawing names the right cover item 4. The drive pulley, belt, idler, flywheel and lower control board all sit behind these covers, and none of those procedures says so.
