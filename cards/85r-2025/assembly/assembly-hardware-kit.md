Six tables, one per step except the pedal step, each headed No. / Item / Description / Qty.

## Step one

| No. | Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 145 | Bolt | M8 x 50mm | 3 |
| 121 | Flat washer | Ø8.5 x Ø18 x 1.5T | 3 |

Three mast bolts - the service manual's preventative-maintenance page checks the same "socket head cap bolts (M8 x 3 pcs)" for looseness when the mast sways.

## Step two

| No. | Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 135 | Sheet metal screw | 3.5 x 10mm | 2 |
| 146 | Bolt | M5 x P0.8 x 12L | 2 |
| 147 | Screw | M5 x 10L | 4 |

## Step three

| No. | Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 133 | Flat washer | Ø5 x Ø12 x 1.0T | 2 |
| 134 | Sheet metal screw | 3.5 x 12 mm | 2 |

## Step four

No table; the pedals thread into the crank arms and get **300 in-lb (34 Nm)**.

## Step five

| No. | Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 587 | Bolt | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | 2 |
| 588 | Flat washer | Ø3/8" x Ø25 x 2T | 2 |
| 610 | Nut | 3/8" x 7T | 2 |
| 615 | Bolt | M6 x 12L | 4 |

The one inch-size joint in the build - the seat back tube into the seat set.

## Step six

| No. | Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 609 | Bolt | M8 x 20L | 4 |

## Step seven

| No. | Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 605 | Bolt | M8 x P1.25 x 20L | 4 |
| 606 | Bolt | M8 x P1.25 x 60L | 2 |
| 607 | Nut | M8 x 7T | 2 |
| 608 | Flat washer | Ø8.5 x Ø26 x 2T | 2 |

**Four of item 605 are tabled and the step fits "2 bolts (605) on the right and left side"** - two a side, or one a side and two spare; the book does not say. No torque is printed for any bolt. The item numbers from 500 up are the seat-set numbers of the service manual's parts list, which carries the same sizes.
