**Seven steps, tools named by item number, and the rear seat set joined to the front frame last.** Item numbers in parentheses are assembly-drawing numbers; the hardware tables are on the hardware card.

## Step one - console mast

1. Run the connectors (72, 74, 89) through the **cover (36)** and the console mast tube and out the top opening. **You need to gently pull on the electrical cables as you do the steps below, taking the slack out so the cables do not get pinched between the tube and frame.**
2. Install the **console mast** with the **3 bolts (145)** and 3 flat washers (121) on the main frame. Be careful not to get the cables pinched while installing the mast.
3. Slide and install the **console mast end cover (36)** on the main body cover.

## Step two - articulating arm and console

1. Connect the connectors (73, 75, 90 - lower terminal) to (72, 74, 89) between the console mast and the **console articulating arm**.
2. Mount the console articulating arm on the console mast with **2 bolts (146)**, using the **3 mm Allen wrench (155)**.
3. Plug all the connectors (73, 75, 90) from the top of the console articulating arm to the console back.
4. Install the **console (70)** to the console articulating arm and attach the **leather cover (153)** on it with 2 bolts (147) to the top right and top left, using the Phillips screwdriver (152). Next attach 2 bolts (147) at the bottom right and bottom left, using the Phillips screwdriver (170) - **the leather cover should not be in between**.
5. Attach the **yellow cover (42)** and the leather cover (153) to the console articulating arm with 2 bolts (135), using the Phillips screwdriver (152).

## Step three - brake covers

1. Install the **left console mast top cover (39)** to the console mast with 2 sheet metal screws (134) and 2 flat washers (133), using the Phillips screwdriver (152).
2. Attach the **right brake cover (40)** to the left brake cover (39) firmly.

## Step four - pedals

1. There is an "L" stamped into the end of the threaded post of the left pedal and an "R" in the right.
2. Install the **left (7-L) and right (7-R) pedals** into the crank arms, using the **open wrench (154)**.
3. The left pedal has a reverse thread and will be screwed into the crank in the opposite rotation from normal threads.
4. Make sure to tighten the pedals as much as you possibly can. **Recommended force: 300 in-lb (34 Nm).** *It may be necessary to re-tighten the pedals if you feel a thumping during pedaling; a thumping or clicking is usually caused by the pedals not being tight enough.*

## Step five - seat back tube, mast cover, bottle holder, arm pad

1. Install the **seat back tube (508)** into the rear part of the **seat set (509)** with 2 bolts (587), 2 flat washers (588) and 2 nuts (610), using the **8 mm Allen wrench (611)** and the **13/14 mm wrench (612)**.
2. Slide the **mast cover (551)** onto the seat back tube (508). Install the mast cover down on the main body of the seat (553).
3. Attach the **bottle holder (550)** to the right side of the seat by snapping it into the locking sockets on the bottom of the **right support arm (510)**.
4. Install the **support arm pad** onto the support arm (536) with 4 bolts (615), using the **5 mm Allen wrench (613)**.

## Step six - seat back

1. Install the **seat back (537)** onto the seat back tube (508) with 4 bolts (609), using the 5 mm Allen wrench.
2. Install the **seat back cover (552)** onto the seat back assembly by snapping into the locking sockets on the seat back.

## Step seven - rear seat set onto the front main frame

1. Install the **rear seat set** to the **front main frame** with 2 bolts (606), 2 flat washers (608) and 2 nuts (607), using both the 5 mm Allen wrench (613) and the open wrench (612), firmly.
2. Install 2 bolts (605) on the right and left side of the front main frame, using the 5 mm Allen wrench (613), firmly.

**Step two names the Phillips screwdriver as item 152 and, once, as item 170** - two numbers for one tool. Step seven's table lists four of item 605 and the step fits two "on the right and left side", one a side by the step's reading. **No torque is printed except the pedals' 300 in-lb.** The 8.0U upright shares the console-and-leather-cover step and the pedal figure with this book and nothing else; plugging in, levelling and the seat adjustments are the setting-up page's cards.
