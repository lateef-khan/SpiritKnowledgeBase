**Do not remove the bolt on the right brake cover - it is the upper stop for the brake lever.**

1. As shown in figure 6.2.1, remove the **brake cover (R)** directly. **This bolt is the upper stop for the brake lever; do not remove it.**
2. As shown in figure 6.2.2, loosen the **screws and flat washer** to remove the **brake cover (L)**.

No screw sizes or counts are printed. The owner's manual fits the **left console mast top cover (39)** with **two 3.5 x 12 mm sheet metal screws (134) and two Ø5 x Ø12 x 1.0T flat washers (133)**, and snaps the **right brake cover (40)** onto it - the same two covers, so the screws of step 2 are those two. The parts list carries **Brake Cover (L) (39)** and **Brake Cover (R) (40)**. The 8.0U has no brake cover procedure.
