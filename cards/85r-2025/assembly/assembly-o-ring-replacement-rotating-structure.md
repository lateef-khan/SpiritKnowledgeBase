**The O-ring sits under the rotating structure that carries the console, and it is reached from the console mast, not from the console.**

1. Check that the **rotating structure assembly** works smoothly.
2. **Loosen the 4 screws that lock the rotating structure to the console mast** (figure 3.4.1).
3. **Pull out the rotating structure assembly** (figure 3.4.2).
4. **Disconnect the three wire connectors**, and the O-ring can be removed (figures 3.4.3 and 3.4.4).

The book files this under preventative maintenance, but it is a disassembly: no refit step, no screw size and no O-ring size are printed. The parts list carries **six O-rings (21)**, a **Rotating Structure Assembly (Top) (14)** and **(Bottom) (15)** and a **Rotate Holder Assembly (16)**; the 8.5UE ergometer prints the same page for its own rotating structure. The 8.0T treadmill's console rotation assembly comes off the same four screws with **four** connectors; the 8.5S stepper's with three - both linked.
