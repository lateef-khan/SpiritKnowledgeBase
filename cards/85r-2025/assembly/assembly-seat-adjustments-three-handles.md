**Three controls, three places: a lever at the rear right of the seat for the back angle, a handle at the front of the seat for fore/aft, and a handle behind the seat for the swivel.** The 7.0R works the same three adjustments from levers on the seat handlebar; that is a different card.

| Adjustment | Control | Repeatable by |
|---|---|---|
| **Seat back angle** | lift the lever at the **rear right side of the seat**, move the seat back, lower the lever | numbered scale just below the seat back cushion |
| **Fore / aft** | lift the handle at the **front of the seat** to disengage the latch, move the seat, lower the handle, then move the seat slightly until the lock clicks | numbered scale on the aluminum seat slide tube; the position is read where the front edge of the seat carriage lines up with the number |
| **Swivel** | lift the handle **behind the seat** to disengage the latch, rotate, lower the handle, then rotate slightly until the lock clicks | latches every **45 degrees** |

The book prints "there is a numbered scale located on the aluminum seat slide tube for repeatable settings" under the swivel paragraph as well as the fore/aft one - the same sentence twice, the second time for a rotation. The operating page adds that the swivel seat can turn **180 degrees in both directions** for assistance in moving patients onto the seat, and that the removable seat rolls away for wheelchair access - which this page does not describe.
