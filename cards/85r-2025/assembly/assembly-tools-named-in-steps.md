Seven tools, named only inside the steps:

| Item | Tool | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| 155 | 3 mm Allen wrench | step 2, articulating arm |
| 152 | Phillips screwdriver | steps 2 and 3 |
| 170 | Phillips screwdriver | step 2, once |
| 154 | open wrench | step 4, pedals |
| 611 | 8 mm Allen wrench | step 5 |
| 612 | 13/14 mm wrench | steps 5 and 7 ("open wrench (612)" in step 7) |
| 613 | 5 mm Allen wrench | steps 5, 6 and 7 |

Each step page also draws the tools it uses beside their item numbers.

**Two numbers for the Phillips screwdriver.** Step two calls it (152) for the top two console bolts and (170) for the bottom two, then (152) again; the same driver. Six tools, then, with one counted twice.

## There is no TOOLS panel and no PARTS INCLUDED panel

The pre-assembly page says **"Locate the hardware package. Remove the tools first"** and never lists a tool, and the book has no carton checklist - its contents run Parts of your unit, Assembly Instructions, Setting up your bike. So the manual never states that these tools are supplied. **Item 154 has no size**; the pedals it drives take a 15 mm flat, which the book never says. Items 611, 612 and 613 are the numbers the 8.5UE ergometer and the 8.0U service manual use for the same three wrenches.

**The 13/14 mm opens to 14**, the size the machine care page asks for on the levellers; the setting-up page asks for a 1/2" wrench for the same nut, which no tool here is. The 8.0U upright names seven tools with different numbers and a 19 mm wrench this build does not need.
