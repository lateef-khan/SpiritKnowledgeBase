| #90 | 13L Wrench | 1 |

| #103 | M6 L Allen Wrench | 1 |

**The hex head bolt #99 is 3/4" long here**; on the TRX2500, TRX3500 and TRX4500 the same item is 1/2".
Otherwise the kit is the TRX4500's. The unpacking text above the checklist drops the sentence about
the hardware pack being split into five sections that the other three TRX books print.
