"To do these replacements need to unmount Cylinder first."

1. Fold the treadmill. Unmount the **cylinder** with an **L-Allen wrench and a 12 mm open-end wrench**.
2. Remove the **4 foot rail locking bolts** and slide each foot rail off.
3. Remove the **8 running deck locking bolts** and take off the running deck. Now replace the running
   deck, running belt and cushions, and reverse the steps to install them.
4. Adjust the running belt tension and centre it.

**The foot rails are bolted here** - four bolts and they slide off. The TRX2500-4500 undo six screws to
free "decorative strips", the TR260 and TRX1400 peel foam-taped rails. The Allen key size, the number
of cushions and any torque are not printed.
