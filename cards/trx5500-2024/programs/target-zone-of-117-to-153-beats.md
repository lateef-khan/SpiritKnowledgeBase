**This is the TRX5500 page, which uses 65% and 85%.** Every other Xterra treadmill book uses
60% and 80% (108 to 144 beats for a 40-year-old).

**Maximum Heart Rate (MHR) = 220 minus your age.** Your training zone is 50% to 90% of MHR;
**65%** is recommended for burning fat, **85%** for strengthening the cardiovascular system,
and "this 65% to 85% is the zone to stay in for maximum benefit."

**Worked example for a 40-year-old, as printed:**

```
220 - 40 = 180 (maximum heart rate)
180 x .65 = 117 beats per minute (65% of maximum)
180 X .85 = 153 beats per minute (85% of maximum)
```

So for a 40-year-old the training zone would be **117 to 153 beats per minute**.

If you enter your age during programming the console performs this calculation
automatically; entering your age is used for the Heart Rate programs. "With this XTERRA
Fitness treadmill you may use the heart rate monitor feature without using the Heart Rate
program"; used with the heart rate programs, the machine automatically adjusts speed or
incline to maintain the desired heart rate.

The chart of MHR by age with the two diagonal lines is a picture. Consult your physician
before participating in any exercise program.
