The **HEART RATE** program calculates your **maximum heart rate from your age**. Set the
target time for the workout, then **adjust your speed manually** while running to maintain
the target heart rate; **the incline is controlled by the treadmill automatically**. **A heart
rate monitoring device is required.**

**The program finishes when the time is up, or if your heart rate is 20% higher than the
target.**

The program's own description on the screen, as pictured: "Choose your target heart rate and
follow speed recommendations. The machine will attempt to keep you **within 5 beats** of your
target."

The book does not print the target percentages offered. Its training-zone page uses 65% and
85% of 220 minus age (see `see_also`), unlike the 60% and 80% of the other Xterra books.
