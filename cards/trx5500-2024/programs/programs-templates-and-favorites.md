The Workout Programs section has **two categories**: **Programs** (predefined speed and
incline profiles) and **Templates** (regular programs saved with your personal adjustments).
Tap a category to switch; tap a program preview for its description and scroll sideways to
see all of them; press **CHOOSE** to select and adjust a program. The physical **Program**
button under the screen also selects a program.

- **MANUAL** requires a time frame set before starting (scale or arrows); speed and incline
  are adjusted manually while running. Press START.
- **P1 to P30** have speed and incline changing profiles shown on the program preview.
  **Time and maximum speed settings are required**; once you choose your maximum speed the
  program intensity adapts accordingly. The book prints each of P1 to P30 as a Speed and an
  Incline dot-matrix picture with **no figures and no names**.
- **HEART RATE** and **CUSTOM** have their own cards.
- **FAVORITE**: any program can be labelled a favorite; favorites appear on the Homescreen
  and in the FAVORITES tab of the user's profile, where they can be deleted.
- The Homescreen's **Start Workout** button starts a workout immediately.

The Dyaco service manual lists the PROGRAM button's choices as "P1~P30, HEART RATE, Custom
MODE".
