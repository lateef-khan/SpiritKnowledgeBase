The LED-locations page is the driver-board photograph with one callout - **LED: Power directive LED. When treadmill
power, LED will be blazed.** - pointing at a small LED on the right of the board, above the white incline VR socket
and left of the M+ terminal.

No other LED is located or named in this book; its "Controller Indicator LED debugging" table (the errors section's
card) has a single POWER row. The TRX2500 book's board locates three LEDs and the TRX3500/4500 book's two.
