**The TRX5500 service manual (Dyaco GT90D-NT041) is the one Xterra treadmill service book without a
Product Safety Instructions chapter.**

Its chapters run: 1 Outlines, 2 Electronic Parts, 3 Electrical Configurations, 4 Product Operation,
5 Unit Block Diagrams, 6 Basic Connections and Wiring, **7 Error Messages / Troubleshooting for
Electronic Issues**, 8 Circuit Diagram, 9 Parts Replacement Guide. In the TR150, TR260, TRX1400, TRX2500
and TRX3500/TRX4500 books chapter 7 is *Product Safety Instructions* - 7.1 safety, 7.2 electrical, 7.3
grounding - and the error messages come later.

So for the TRX5500 there is no service-manual `Never use a GFCI` rule
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-service-manual-never-use-a-gfci-outlet`), no high-inrush breaker part number
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-house-breaker-trips-replace-with-high-inrush-type`) and no 230-volt outlet
figure (`xterra-treadmill-safety-service-manual-outlet-and-extension-cord-figures`). The only voltage
the service book gives is in the electronic-parts and troubleshooting tables - `Check the supply
voltage is 120V (230V)` - which is a specs or errors matter.

How it was checked: both table-of-contents pages read, every page heading listed, and the text
searched for `safety`, `grounding`, `GFCI`, `volt`, `caution` and `warning`; the hits are the safety
key, the motor voltages and the E-code table.

**The owner's manual is the whole safety text for this machine**: the 2024 TRX safety page
(`xterra-trx-safety-instructions-list-2024`), its AFCI/GFCI paragraph
(`xterra-treadmill-safety-avoid-afci-gfci-breakers-tested-brands`) and its 110-volt grounding page
(`xterra-trx-safety-grounding-nominal-110-volt-circuit`).
