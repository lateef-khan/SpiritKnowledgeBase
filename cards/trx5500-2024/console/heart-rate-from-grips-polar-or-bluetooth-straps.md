Pulse (heart rate) on the screen shows the current beats per minute. Hold **both** left and right stainless steel sensors to measure the pulse; the value is shown on the screen continuously. The hand pulse sensors can be used while in Heart Rate Control. The console also picks up wireless heart-rate transmitters that are **Polar** and **Bluetooth** compatible.

Pairing runs through the touchscreen: the Homescreen's top row has a **Pair HR device** icon, the Settings menu's BLUETOOTH entry pairs chest straps, earphones or speakers, and the HR device pairing settings are also reachable from the top panel while a workout is paused ([settings card](settings-wifi-time-software-update-brightness-bluetooth.md)). The book's Heart Rate program says a HR monitoring device is required (the Programs section holds that). How to wear a strap, and what interferes with it, are on [the strap card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-wearing-the-chest-strap-and-its-2500-hour-battery.md) and [the erratic card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-erratic-heart-rate-and-the-seven-interference-sources.md).
