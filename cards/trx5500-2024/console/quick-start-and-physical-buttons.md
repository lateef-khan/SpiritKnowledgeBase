**Quick start.**

1. Attach the Safety Key to enable the display (if not already on).
2. Press any button to wake the display if it is not already on.
3. Press **Start Workout** on the Homescreen to begin belt movement at **0.5 mph**, then adjust with the Speed + or − keys.
4. To slow the treadmill, press and hold the Slow − key (console or hand rail).
5. To stop the tread-belt, press and release the **Stop** button.

**The physical buttons.** The touchscreen controls functions by touch, or by the physical buttons under the screen: Speed + and −, Incline + and −, **Start**, **Stop** (end or pause the workout), **Fan** (controls the fan speed) and **Program** (select a workout program). The handrails carry Incline ▲/▼ and Speed toggles as well; the service manual's drawing labels the console row as Incline Up/Down, Speed Up/Down, Program Key, Fan Key, Start Key, Stop Key and Safety Key, with a speaker each side.

**Incline** can be changed at any time during the workout with the handrail Incline ▲/▼ buttons or the Incline + / − buttons on the screen. Pause and the workout summary are on [the pause card](pause-resume-finish-and-five-minute-summary.md).
