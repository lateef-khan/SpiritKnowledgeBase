**Set up.** Once the console is powered on, connect to the available WiFi network by pressing the signal icon in the top right corner or from the SETTINGS menu in the bottom right corner. Without WiFi, saving workout data to the cloud, using apps and casting content are not available; if WiFi is skipped, set the date and time manually. Choose the **Guest** user or create a profile; a **maximum of 9 profiles** can be created.

**Homescreen.** It gives an activity overview and shortcuts to training. In Guest mode you can enter your age and weight here (scales or arrows) so the summary is more accurate. **Start Workout** starts immediately. The top row holds Log out, Pair HR device, Adjust volume, Fan on/off, Activity Overview, Current User and WiFi Settings; the bottom row Homescreen, Workout Programs, Profiles, Internet & Apps, Screen Mirroring and Settings.

**Profile.** Press + on the starting screen to create one. With an XTERRA app account you can scan the QR code the screen shows, which uploads your existing personal info and links the two profiles so your history shows in the XTERRA app; one app account cannot be linked to multiple profiles on one machine. Without an account press **I DON'T** and fill in the form. The Profile section has three tabs: **Info** (edit any field, then Save; link an XTERRA account from the "XTERRA Account" field), **History** (the ten latest records of workouts completed by the current user; tap a record for details; all records are in the app account if linked) and **Favorites** (programs the current user labelled as favorites, which can be deleted). Different users can choose different unit systems, favorite programs and templates.

Templates and favorites as workout programs belong to the Programs section; the Settings menu is on [the settings card](settings-wifi-time-software-update-brightness-bluetooth.md).
