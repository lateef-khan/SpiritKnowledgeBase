**Internet & Apps.** This section holds a variety of third-party apps to keep you entertained during a workout. To use them, connect to WiFi and choose the app icon. Press the expand icon to enter full-screen mode, the exit icon to leave it, and the home icon to choose another app. The screenshot in the book warns: "WHEN CLOSING THE APP, YOU ARE NOT SIGNED OUT AUTOMATICALLY."

**Screen Mirroring.** To display content from a smartphone or tablet on the console, open this section and follow the on-screen instructions for an iOS or an Android device. Once mirroring has started, press the expand icon for full screen and the exit icon to leave it. The book's screenshot reads: connect your device to the facility's WiFi network, press the Screen Mirroring button on your device, then select the **"XTERRA TRX5500"** device; a strong connection is required.

Both work the same way during a workout as outside one ([workout views card](workout-views-goal-profile-and-track.md)). The book names no app by name and prints no list of what is installed.
