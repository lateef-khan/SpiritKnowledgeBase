Plug the treadmill into an appropriate wall outlet, then turn on the power switch at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood. The Safety Key must be installed; the treadmill will not power on without it.

When the power is turned ON, the screen shows a **brief loading screen** and then the **WELCOME screen**, which indicates that the machine is ready to operate.

The service manual describes the same console (Dyaco GT90D-NT041, a 10-inch touch screen) and says that with the key out the display shows "PLEASE REPLACE THE SAFET KEY" [sic] and does not work, and with the key in the message window shows "press start workout" ([service-manual modes card](off-ready-and-run-modes-and-display-ranges.md)).
