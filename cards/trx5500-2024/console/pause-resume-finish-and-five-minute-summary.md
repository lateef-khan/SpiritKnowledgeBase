1. Press the **Stop** button once; the belt slows down gradually until it fully stops. After **5 minutes** the display resets and shows the **workout summary** on the screen.
2. Press **Resume** to continue the workout from the pause.
3. Press **Finish** to end the workout.

During a workout the Stop control also sits on the bottom right corner of the screen. When the workout is finished the summary appears: slide left for more details, or press **Done** to return to the Home screen. The completed program can be saved as a template from the summary with **Save as**, and the record appears in the History tab of the Profile section ([workout views card](workout-views-goal-profile-and-track.md)). While paused you can also open the heart-rate device pairing settings from the top panel.
