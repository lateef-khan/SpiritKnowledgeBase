Charge a personal device during a workout from the on-console USB port; the treadmill's power must be on.

1. Connect your USB charging cable (not included) to the USB power port and to your device.
2. Check that your device's icon shows it is charging.

Notes from the book: make sure a compatible USB charging cable is used. The port provides up to **5 Vdc / 1.0 amp** and meets **USB 2.0** regulations. You will **not** be able to save your workout data to a USB drive through this port; it is for charging only.

The WS200 and WS300 print the same rating for their Type A and Type C ports ([card](../../shared/console/xterra-ws-console-usb-a-and-c-ports-charge-at-5-v-1-a.md)).
