Once the workout has started, after a short countdown, the console appears in workout mode. Warm-up and cool-down phases can be skipped by pressing **Skip**.

- The **left panel** is fixed and shows the current WORKOUT STATS; press the + icon to reveal more related stats.
- **Speed and incline** are adjusted by tapping the arrows in the **right panel**.
- The **top panel** icons are the tools available during a workout: volume, fan and WiFi. When the workout is paused, the HR device pairing settings are also available there.
- The **bottom panel** has three views, left to right: **GOAL VIEW** (the parameter that defines the finish — time counting down for profile programs, distance left for distance programs), **PROFILE VIEW** (the workout profile and your current position in it) and **TRACK VIEW** (laps on a 1/4-mile track, showing progress by distance covered). Internet and Screen Mirroring work as in non-workout mode.
- To pause or finish, press **Stop** in the bottom right corner.

After the workout the summary appears: slide left for details or press Done to go back to the Home screen; Save as stores the completed program as a template, and the record appears in the Profile's History tab ([pause card](pause-resume-finish-and-five-minute-summary.md)).
