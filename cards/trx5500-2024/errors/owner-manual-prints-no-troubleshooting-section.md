The TRX5500 owner's manual (©2024, effective March 20 2024) is the only Xterra owner's manual in this wave with **no troubleshooting section**. Its table of contents runs Product Registration, Important Safety Instructions, Important Electrical Instructions, Assembly, Operation of Your Console, Charging devices using the USB port, Using a Chest Strap, Treadmill Adjustments, XTERRA APP, Exploded View, Parts List, Manufacturer's Limited Warranty - and nothing else. No page prints an error code, and the 21 pages that were rendered and OCR-read (exploded view, parts list, back cover) add none.

The machine's codes are in its service manual (Dyaco GT90D-NT041): `xterra-treadmill-errors-service-manual-error-code-tables-by-book` for the table, and the per-code cards it points to. The service manual likewise prints **no troubleshooting procedure matrix** - its chapter 7 ends at the calibration procedure and chapter 8 is the circuit diagram - so the no-power, no-display and button-fault rows carded for the other Dyaco books do not name this machine.
