**This is E1, not E2 (tension motor) and not E3 (ramp / incline VR).**

Definition: all screens are off and outputs stop when the EEPROM is damaged or malfunctions. The display message shows **"E1"**.

Troubleshooting: **replace the upper controller.** The manual gives no other step for E1.

The error table prints the meaning as "EEPROM failure".
