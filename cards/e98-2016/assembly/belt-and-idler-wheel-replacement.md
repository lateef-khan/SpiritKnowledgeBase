1. Follow the procedures to take apart the cross bar.
2. Use a **13 mm wrench** to loosen the **M8 x 9T nut on the M8x170mm J-bolt** together with the **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** until the J-bolt is released. Use the same wrench to release the **M8 x 20 carriage bolt** which secures the idler wheel assembly, together with the **5/16" x 20 x 1.5T flat washer** and **M8 x 7T nut**, to take the idler wheel assembly apart.
3. Follow the above procedures in reverse, and tighten the **M8 nyloc nut on the J bolt until the belt generates a sound wave of 170 Hz (+/- 10)**.

**170 Hz, not 190 Hz.** Every other 2016 Sole elliptical manual in this family prints 190 Hz here.

**The heading is wrong in the source.** The section is headed "Flywheel & Poly-V Belt Replacement", but these steps are the idler wheel procedure, which is what the table of contents calls 11.8. The flywheel steps are in 11-9.
