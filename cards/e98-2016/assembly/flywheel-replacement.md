1. Follow the procedures to take the idler wheel assembly apart.
2. **Disconnect all electric cables for the inductive flywheel** and cut the tie holding the cables.
3. Use an **11 mm wrench** to release **4 pcs of 1/4" x 3/4" hex head bolts** with **1/4" spring washers** and **1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers**, and take the inductive flywheel apart.
4. To reassemble, return the inductive flywheel onto the main frame and secure with **4 pcs of 1/4" x 3/4" hex head screws** with **3 pcs of 1/4" spring washer** and **3 pcs of flat washers**, then connect all electric cables back on.
5. To finish, reverse the above procedures and **adjust the belt in the centre of the drive pulley**.

**The washer counts disagree between removal and refit.** Step 3 removes four bolts with four spring washers and four flat washers; step 4 refits four bolts but names only three spring washers and three flat washers. Count the washers off the machine.
