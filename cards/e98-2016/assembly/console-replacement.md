1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **three M5x15mm Phillips head screws** and **two 3.5x12 m/m sheet metal screws** to take apart the front and rear mast covers.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **four M5x10mm Phillips head screws** and disconnect the computer cable, handle resistance wire, handle incline wire and handpulse cable to take the console apart.
3. Reassemble in the reverse order. Be sure not to crush or damage wiring during the process.
4. Release the **3.5x12 sheet metal screws** if opening the top and bottom console covers is necessary. The manual says this is to be opened by professionals only.
