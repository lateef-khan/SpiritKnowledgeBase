1. Follow the procedures for the swing arms and pedal arms.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **M5x15mm Phillips head screws** to take apart the front stabilizer cover.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **4x19 sheet metal screws** and **5x16 tapping screws** which secure the left side case, and take it apart.
4. Again, use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **4x15 sheet metal screws**, **1/4" x 19 flat washers** and **5x16 tapping screws** which secure the right side case on the mainframe.
5. Disconnect the ground wire and controller connecting wire from the AC power switch and take the right side case apart.
6. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly. The **flat washer 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T** is to be placed on the left chain cover.
