**170 Hz, plus or minus 10 Hz.** The manual writes it as "tighten the M8 Nyloc Nut on the J Bolt until the Belt generate sound wave 170HZ(+/-10)".

**This figure is unique to this manual.** Every other 2016 Sole elliptical manual in this family - E25, E35, E55, E95, E95S - and the SC200 print **190 Hz (+/- 10)**. The Sole E98 2019 service manual also prints 190 Hz. Do not substitute 190 Hz here without confirming which figure the machine in front of you was built to.

The figure is printed once, in the section headed 11-8 Flywheel & Poly-V Belt Replacement, which is in fact the idler wheel procedure. The belt slipping entry in the troubleshooting section does not repeat it.
