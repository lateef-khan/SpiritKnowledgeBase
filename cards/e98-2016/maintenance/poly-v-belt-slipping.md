If the Poly-V belt is slipping, **adjust the cap on the hook-type screw with a #13 wrench**. The manual sends you to the disassembly procedures for the adjustment itself.

The manual does not repeat the tension figure here. The target is **170 Hz plus or minus 10 Hz on a sound wave meter, not the 190 Hz the other 2016 Sole elliptical manuals print**; that number is printed only in the section headed 11-8 Flywheel & Poly-V Belt Replacement, which is the idler wheel procedure and has its own card.
