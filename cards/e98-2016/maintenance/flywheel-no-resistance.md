A problem with the **inductive flywheel** is rare. **If there is no resistance, take off both chain covers and check for disconnection or breakage of the electric cables.**

This machine brakes electrically. There is no steel cable and no tension motor to check, unlike the E25, E35, E55, E95 and E95S. Engineering Mode also has a **BRAKE TEST** entry.

If the console shows E2, see the E2 card; this manual gives no E2 troubleshooting section.
