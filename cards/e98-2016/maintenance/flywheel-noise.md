If there is noise with the flywheel, check whether there is foreign material or abnormal rubbing. **Replace with a new flywheel if the noise cannot be fixed.**

That is all this manual says about flywheel noise. **It does not mention steel cable adjustment**, unlike the E25, E35, E55 and E95 manuals of the same year, because this machine has an inductive flywheel and no steel cable.

The other half of section 9-3 is about no resistance and has its own card.
