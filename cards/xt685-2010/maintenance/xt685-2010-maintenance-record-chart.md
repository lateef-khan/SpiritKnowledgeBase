The manual prints a **maintenance record chart inside its front cover**, with a Date column to
write in each time the work is done. It is the only place in the manual that states an interval
for tracking inspection.

| Task | How often |
|---|---|
| Vacuum under motor cover & check wiring | Every other month |
| Clean & inspect deck for lubrication | Every other month |
| Lubricate deck | Every 180 hours or sooner if dry |
| Inspect belt tracking, adjust if necessary | Monthly |

**Unlike the CT800 chart, these intervals are readable.** The chart names the interval in words
under each task rather than ticking a column, so nothing depends on a mark surviving the scan. The
CT800 table gives seven tasks in columns whose ticks are lost
(`spirit-ct800-maintenance-schedule-tasks`); do not read one chart's intervals off the other.

## Each task is carded in full elsewhere

- **Vacuum under motor cover** - `xt-2023-maintenance-vacuuming-and-general-cleaning`, which adds
  the monthly vacuum *underneath* the machine and the instruction to unplug first. The chart's
  **check wiring** is the one item this manual says nothing more about anywhere: no wire to look
  at, no fault to look for.
- **Clean & inspect deck for lubrication** and **Lubricate deck** -
  `xt-2015-maintenance-cleaning-between-belt-and-deck`,
  `xt-2015-maintenance-lubrication-interval-180-hours` and
  `xt-2015-maintenance-deck-lubrication-procedure`. The 180 hours here and the 180 hours in the
  lubrication section are the same figure stated twice, and they agree.
- **Inspect belt tracking** - `xt-2023-maintenance-treadbelt-tracking`. The chart supplies the
  monthly interval that the tracking procedure itself never gives.

## No other XT685 manual has this chart

The XT685 2015, 2023 and XT685ENT manuals print no maintenance chart and no equivalent list of
intervals, and the later two do not lubricate at all. **The interval table above is specific to the
2010 machine** and must not be quoted for a later XT685.
