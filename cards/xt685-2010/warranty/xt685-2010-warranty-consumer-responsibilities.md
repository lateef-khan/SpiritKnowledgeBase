Under the heading **NORMAL RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE CONSUMER**:

> This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household or Light Commercial use (see restrictions above), and the consumer/facility is responsible for the items listed below:

1. The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card **within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.
2. Proper use of the treadmill in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual
3. Proper installation in accordance with instructions provided with the treadmill and with all local electric codes.
4. **Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage, replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects in house or facility wiring.**
5. Expenses for making the treadmill accessible for servicing, including any item that was not part of the treadmill at the time it was shipped from the factory.
6. Damages to the treadmill finish during shipping, installation or following installation.
7. Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual.

**Seven items, not six.** Every other Spirit XT manual in this knowledge base prints this list without **item 4**, and renumbers what follows: the 2015 XT manuals and the XT485ENT run to six (`xt-2015-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`) and the 2023 XT manuals to five (`xt-2023-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`). Item 4 is the one that puts the electrical supply on the owner - a bad outlet, a blown fuse, a loose connection or defective house or facility wiring is the owner's expense, not a warranty repair. Quoting either later list to the owner of a 2010 XT685 drops that duty; quoting this one to the owner of a 2015 or 2023 machine invents it.

**This is not the CT800 and CT850 list either**, although that one also runs to seven items in this order and also carries the grounded-power-supply duty. Two things differ. The CT800 list is worded for **fitness equipment** where this one is worded for the **treadmill**, in items 2, 3, 5 and 6. And CT800 item 1 offers a second route - "or completed **online**" - which this list does not: on the 2010 XT685 the printed sentence names only the card and the address on it, even though the manual's own registration page also gives a web address. Read the CT800 and CT850 list from `spirit-ct800-warranty-consumer-responsibilities`.

**The lead sentence is where the use limit is stated**, and it is the XT685 wording rather than the CT800 one: ordinary household **or Light Commercial** use. What light commercial means is printed in the table above the list rather than here - 5 hours use or less in a non-dues paying facility - and exclusion 8 repeats the same envelope. See `xt-2015-warranty-use-environment`.

This list is only half of what governs the warranty. The other half is the nine numbered exclusions that follow it, which this manual prints word for word as the 2015 XT manuals do: `xt-2015-warranty-exclusions`.
