**On this console the target heart rate is retargeted mid-workout from the Speed keys on the right
handlebar.** The 2015 XT385, XT485 and XT685 manuals put that on the **Incline** keys on the **left**
handlebar, and print no step 9 - see `xt-2015-programs-heart-rate-program-xt385-xt485-xt685`. Do not
read one procedure off the other.

**You must wear the heart rate transmitter strap for these programs.**

Both programs operate the same and are programmed the same way; the only difference is the target.
**HR1 is set to 60% and HR2 is set to 80% of the maximum heart rate.** After you select your heart
rate target the program attempts to keep you at or within **3 - 5 heart beats per minute** of that
value. Follow the prompts in the Message Center to maintain your selected heart rate value.

1. Press the **HR 1-2** key **once** for 60% of max heart rate or **twice** for 80%, then press
   **Enter**.
2. The Message Center asks for your **Age**. Enter it with the Speed + / - keys, then press Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight** with the Speed + / - keys, then press Enter.
4. Adjust the **Time** with the Speed + / - keys, then press Enter.
5. Adjust the **Heart Rate Target** - the heart rate level you will strive to maintain during the
   program - with the Speed + / - keys, then press Enter. *The heart rate that appears is based on
   the % you accepted in step 1; changing this number increases or decreases that percentage.*
6. Press **Start** to begin your workout, or **Stop** to go back and modify your settings.
7. To increase or decrease the workload at any time during the program, press the **Speed + / -**
   key on the console or the **right handlebar**. This changes your target heart rate at any time
   during the program.
8. During the HR 1 or HR 2 programs you can scroll through the data in the Message Center by
   pressing **Enter**.
9. When the program ends you may press **Start** to begin the same program again, **Stop** to exit
   the program, **or save the workout you just completed as a custom user program by pressing a User
   key** and following the instructions in the Message Center.

**Saving a finished workout to a User key is stated only in this manual.** Neither the 2015 nor the
2023 XT685 manual prints it, and the XT285 manual's step 9 offers only Start and Stop.

You may also use the heart rate readout on its own, outside these programs - see
`xt-2023-programs-heart-rate-training-zone` for the 220-minus-age formula and the training zone this
program targets, and `ct900-heart-rate-chest-strap-setup` for fitting the strap.

**The 2013 XT485 owner's manual prints this nine-step procedure word for word** - HR 1-2 key once for
60% or twice for 80%, the 3-to-5 beat tolerance, the retarget from the **Speed** keys on the console
or **right handlebar**, Enter to scroll the data, and the step 9 save to a User key. So the XT485
too changed between generations: Speed keys and a User-key save in 2013, Incline keys and no save
in 2015 (`xt-2015-programs-heart-rate-program-xt385-xt485-xt685`).
