**This build is not the XT685-2015 build and shares no item numbers with it.** The
2015 machine has gusseted uprights and five steps; this one has four steps, speed
nut clips and a cross brace. Do not read a 2015 or 2023 item number onto this
machine.

Numbers in brackets are item numbers from the assembly drawing. Unpack and lay the
hardware out first - see the linked pre-assembly card, whose two printed steps this
manual shares word for word with the CT800 manuals of the same period.

## Step 1 - console mast tubes

1. Slide the bottom console covers onto the console mast tubes (**52 onto 5**, left
   side, and **53 onto 4**, right side). Make sure they are oriented as shown in the
   illustration.
2. Connect computer cables (32 & 33) on the right side.
3. Insert the console mast tubes (4 & 5) into the frame. **Caution: be careful not to
   pinch the cables you just connected**; doing so could affect the console function.
   Pull the end of the cable tight as you insert the right console mast tube.
4. Insert **three Button Head Socket Bolts (102)** and **one Button Head Socket Bolt
   (103)** and **Split Washer (108)** on each side. Partially tighten these using the
   M6 Allen Wrench (106). **Do not completely tighten them until the end of step 3.**

## Step 2 - console

1. Connect the following cables together: Incline cables (35) to (39), Speed cables
   (34) to (38), and Computer cables (33) to (31).
2. Place the console (37) onto the brackets at the top of the console mast tubes and
   attach with **two Button Head Bolts (104)** and **two Split Washers (108)** on each
   side. **Caution: be careful not to pinch any of the cables.** Firmly tighten with
   the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (107).

## Step 3 - console covers and cross brace

1. Attach the bottom console covers (52 & 53) to the console with **five Sheet Metal
   Screws (105) on each side**. Tighten with the Phillips Head Screw Driver (109) or
   the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (107).
2. Attach the cross brace (3) to the console mast tubes (4 & 5) with **two Button Head
   Socket Bolts (102) on each side**. Tighten with the M6 Allen Wrench (106).
3. Firmly tighten the **eight** Button Head Socket Bolts inserted in step 1 with the M6
   Allen Wrench (106). Place the accessory holder tray (48) securely on top of the
   cross brace (3).

## Step 4 - side covers

1. Place **two M5 Speed Nut Clips (84) onto each side** of the frame.
2. Attach the side covers (40 & 41) to the frame with **one Self Tapping Screw (146)
   and three Sheet Metal Screws (147) on each side**. Refer to the illustration for
   screw placement. Tighten with the Phillips Head Screw Driver (109).

## Two tool item numbers the manual contradicts itself on

The tools panel on the same page as the unpacking steps numbers the **Combination M5
Allen Wrench / Phillips Head Screw Driver 106** and the **M6 Allen Wrench 107**. The
four assembly steps use those two numbers the other way round, and do so
consistently: steps 1 and 3 call **(106)** the M6 Allen Wrench and steps 2 and 3 call
**(107)** the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver. The manual
never reconciles them. Item **109**, the Phillips Head Screw Driver, is the same in
both places. Work from the tool in your hand, not from the number.

## The eight bolts in step 3 are the ones from step 1

Step 1 puts three of item 102 and one of item 103 into each side, which is the eight
bolts step 3 tells you to go back and tighten. Item 102 is then used again in step 3,
four more of them, for the cross brace; item 108 is used in step 1 and again in step
2. Counting an item once across the whole build will leave you short.

## What the scanned pages do not show

The manual's assembly drawings are images, and the copy this card was written from is
an image-only PDF read by OCR. **The two illustrations the steps tell you to refer to
- the orientation of the bottom console covers in step 1 and the screw placement in
step 4 - are not recoverable from it**, and neither is the full-page drawing between
steps 1 and 2 (PDF p. 11). Nothing on this card depends on them; the words above are
the manual's own.

The manual also opens the chapter with an **Assembly Video** note - "To assist you in
assembling your new Spirit Fitness treadmill, we have posted an assembly video at the
following link" - and the URL that follows is not legible in the scan. Do not quote a
link for it.
