This manual prints no single hardware panel. Each step page carries its own HARDWARE
panel of captioned photographs; put together they read:

| Step | Item | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 102 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 5/16" x 15mm | 6 |
| 1 | 103 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 3/8" x 2" | 2 |
| 1 | 108 | Split Washer | 3/8" x 2T | 2 |
| 2 | 104 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | 4 |
| 2 | 108 | Split Washer | 3/8" x 2.0T | 4 |
| 3 | 102 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 5/16" x 15mm | 4 |
| 3 | 105 | Sheet Metal Screw | 3.5 x 12mm | 10 |
| 4 | 84 | Speed Nut Clips | *(no size printed)* | 4 |
| 4 | 146 | Self Tapping Screw | 5 x 16mm | 2 |
| 4 | 147 | Sheet Metal Screw | 5 x 16mm | 6 |

**Every panel's count matches what its own step calls for**, once you allow for the
two items that are used in two steps:

- **Item 102** - six in step 1 (three a side on the mast tubes) and four more in
  step 3 (two a side on the cross brace), **ten in the kit**.
- **Item 108** - two in step 1 (one a side) and four in step 2 (two a side),
  **six in the kit**.

The step-1 panel writes item 108 as **3/8" x 2T** and the step-2 panel as
**3/8" x 2.0T**. It is one washer with two spellings of the same thickness.

**Items 146 and 147 are both printed 5 x 16mm** and differ only in being called a
Self Tapping Screw and a Sheet Metal Screw. Both descriptions are printed that way in
the same panel; the manual does not say what distinguishes them. Step 4 uses one of
146 and three of 147 on each side, so keep the two bags apart.

**Item 84 is the only fastener with no size on its caption.** The step calls them
**M5 Speed Nut Clips**; the panel prints only "Speed Nut Clips".

**Nothing here carries over to the XT685-2015 or XT685-2023.** Those kits are built
from items 100, 123, 124, 79, 86, 91, 127 and 125 and have no speed nut clips at all.
The numbers on this card mean nothing on those machines.
