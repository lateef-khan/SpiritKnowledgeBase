Four things this manual never prints, all of which owners ask for:

- **Levelling.** No levelling feet, no glides, no wrench size, no adjustment
  anywhere in the manual.
- **Folding or unfolding.** The word "fold" does not appear. This machine does not
  fold.
- **Storage.** Not mentioned.
- **A parts list or exploded view.** The manual's contents run from General
  Maintenance to the Service Checklist to the warranty, with no parts pages. The
  assembly steps and hardware panels are the only place item numbers appear, so an
  item number can be looked up only from the step that uses it.

There is also **no carton list**. The pre-assembly page tells you to unpack and to
lay the hardware out and nothing else, so the manual never says what should be in
the box.

**Moving the machine is the exception - that one is documented.** The manual has a
Transportation Instructions chapter; see the linked moving card.

**Absence is the answer here, not a gap to fill from a sibling manual.** The 2023 XT
manuals document a levelling foot turned with an M14 wrench and a fold-and-roll
procedure, and the 2015 and 2023 XT manuals carry carton lists and parts lists. None
of that is printed for this machine, and its hardware is not their hardware.
