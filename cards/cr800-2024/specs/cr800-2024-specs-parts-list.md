These are the callout numbers on the exploded view drawing. The drawing is a
picture with bare numbers on it and no text, so the table below is the only
readable form of it.

**154 rows are printed.** The whole item numbers that appear are 1-23, 25-33, 35-39, 42-49, 51-56, 61, 63-66, 68, 71-73, 75-80, 82-83, 85-95, 97-106, 108, 111-112, 114, 116, 124-126, 128-129, 132, 135-136, 139, 141, 143, 148, 158-173, 175-179, 185, 189, 193-195, 198, 201, 203. Any number outside that run is **not printed in this manual at all** - 68 numbers between 24 and 202 are absent, not a missing page.

## What changed from the 2023 CR800 list

The 2023 book prints 150 rows and this one prints 154. **Four rows were added and
five changed:**

| Item | CR800-2023 | CR800-2024 |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | `Ø35 x 10m/m_Rubber Foot` | `Rubber Foot` - the size dropped |
| 19-10 | `CASFE Board` | `CSAFE Board` - the transposition corrected |
| 19-16 | `Fan Anchor` | `Fan Grill Anchor` |
| 47 | `750m/m_Wire Brake Coil Harness(Red)` | `Wire Brake Coil Harness(Red)` - the length dropped |
| 65 | `3/8" x 135m/m_Hex Head Bolt` | the same bolt, now qualified `(30mm)` |
| 159 | **`Electronic Module`** | **`On/Off Switch Bracket`** |
| 175 | `3/8" x 2-3/4"_Hex Head Bolt` | the same bolt, now qualified `(12mm)` |
| 195 | not printed | `AC Input Module` |
| 198 | not printed | `80m/m_Connecting Wire (Black)` |
| 201 | not printed | `Chain Cover Attaching Plate`, qty 3 |
| 203 | not printed | `Ø3/8" x 23 x 2.0T_Curved Washer`, qty 2 |

**Item 159 is the trap, and it is worse than a rename.** The 2023 CR800 list
called item 159 an Electronic Module and the 2023 CR800ENT list called it an
On/Off Switch Bracket. **The two books swapped the name in 2024**: this list now
says On/Off Switch Bracket and `cr800ent-2024-specs-parts-list` says Electronic
Module. Quote item 159 only from the book for the machine in hand, and say which
book it came from.

## What tells this list apart from the CR800ENT-2024 list

- **Item 55 is `Generator/Brake` and item 43 `Generator/Brake Controller`.** The
  CR800ENT-2024 list instead has an `Induction Brake` at 55, an `EMS Controller`
  at 49 and a `Power Adaptor` at 47.
- **Item 194 is a `TV Adapter (5C2V)`**, which the ENT list does not carry.
- Item 159 differs, as above.

**Items 26 and 42 are printed identically**, both `300m/m_Handpulse Wire`
quantity 1.

**No flywheel weight, machine weight or resistance rating is printed against any
row.** See `spirit-bike-specs-no-specification-table`.

**The recumbent and the upright do not share a list.** This list and
`cu800-2024-specs-parts-list` share only 65 item numbers and agree on two of
them.
