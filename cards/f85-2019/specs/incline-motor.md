**This is the incline motor, the AC motor that raises the deck. It is not the drive motor, which is DC.**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | AC motor |
| Voltage | 110 or 230 volts AC |
| Wires | red, black, white, green, plus one 3 pin cable for the position sensor |

| Wire | Function |
|---|---|
| Red | UP. AC voltage here increases the incline. |
| Black | DOWN. AC voltage here decreases the incline. |
| White | COM, the neutral. |
| Green/yellow | Grounding wire. |

The refit step at the end of the manual repeats it as: red to **UP**, white to **COM**, black to **DOWN**.

**Source wording.** The line reads "All of five wire connection: red, black, white, green, and has one of 3 pins cable for position sensor." Five is the printed count, but only four colours are named. The four colours plus the separate 3 pin position sensor cable are what the wiring pages and the refit step show.
