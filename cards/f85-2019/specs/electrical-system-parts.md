| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| Safety key | Fits into the console to activate all functions and the treadmill. Without it the console cannot be controlled and the treadmill will not be activated. |
| Console | The interface that controls all functions of the treadmill. It contains the keys, the TFT-LCD display, the speaker, the fan, the hand pulse grips and the safety key. |
| Main controller (also called the lower controller or driver board) | Carries the DC power supply for the console, the incline driver and the DC motor driver, plus the motor driver control circuit, the incline control circuit and the speed sensor input. It links the console to the motors and outputs the voltages that run them. |
| Treadmill motor | A variable speed DC motor. |
| Incline motor | An AC motor. The user sets the elevation from the console, through the main controller. |

**The high voltage build has extra filtering.** The manual states: "The 220V (or CEGS) of Lower Controller Area has Filter and Chock." That filter and choke are not present on the 110V build.
