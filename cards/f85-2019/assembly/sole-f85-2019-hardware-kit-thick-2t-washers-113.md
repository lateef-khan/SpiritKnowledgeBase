Hardware supplied in the box with the Sole F85-2019, grouped by assembly step. This is the 2T-washer printing: the console split washers (#113) are Dia 10 x 2T here, while the F80-2019 and F85-2020/2021 books print Dia 10 x 1.5T for the same item number (see the linked card). Thickness read off the page rendering.

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | #128 | M5 | Speed Nut Clip | 2 pcs |
| 3 | #130 | 5/16" x 1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 pcs |
| 5 | #133 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #113 | Dia 10 x 2T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 6 | #168 | 3.5 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #151 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #75 | - | Safety Key | 1 pc |
| 8 | #120 | 5 x 16 mm | Tapping Screw | 2 pcs |
| 8 | #94 | M5 x 20 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 2 pcs |
| 9 | #168 | 3.5 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |

Tools in the box: #131 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver, #132 M6 L Allen Wrench. The assembly procedure is shared with the 1.5T books (see the linked procedure card); only this kit table differs.
