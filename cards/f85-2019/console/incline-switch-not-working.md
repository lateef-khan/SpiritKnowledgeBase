**This is the incline switch. The speed switch has its own row in the manual with different causes, and its own card.**

Three symptoms share one row:

- The UP/DOWN buttons of the INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH cannot be used.
- The incline button can press UP but not DOWN.
- The incline button can press DOWN but not UP.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The incline VR or incline power wires are not connected properly | Connect the wires again. |
| The incline VR or incline power wire is damaged | Replace the cable. |
| Maybe keys stuck | Replace the buttons. |
| The incline motor is stuck | Replace the incline. |
| The incline gears are cracked | Replace the incline. |

Check the wiring at **JK60** (incline VR) and **JK80** (incline power) on the driver board before condemning the switch. A one direction failure most often points at the incline power loom rather than the keys, because UP and DOWN are separate wires on that loom.
