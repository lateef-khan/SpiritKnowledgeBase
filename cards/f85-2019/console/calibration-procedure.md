1. Remove the safety key.
2. Press and hold **START** and **FAST** (Speed ▲) and, at the same time, replace the safety key. Keep holding both until the window displays **"Factory settings"**, then press **ENTER**.
3. Choose **Metric or English** (kilometres or miles) with the **incline UP/DOWN (▲/▼)** key, then press **ENTER**. The **maximum speed value is displayed in the speed window** and the **maximum elevation value is displayed in the incline window**.
4. **Grade return – On.** This lets the incline return to zero when STOP is pressed. For sale in Europe, EU standards require this to be **off**.
5. Press **START** to begin.

**The process is automatic and the speed starts up without warning, so do not stand on the belt.**

If the belt does not turn during calibration the console throws E1, because calibration is the one time the RPM sensor signal is required.
