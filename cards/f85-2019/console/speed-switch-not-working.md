**This is the speed switch. The incline switch has its own row in the manual with different causes, and its own card.**

Three symptoms share one row:

- The FAST/SLOW buttons of the SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH cannot be used.
- The speed button can press FAST but not SLOW.
- The speed button can press SLOW but not FAST.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Maybe keys stuck | Replace the key board. |
| Maybe the upper control is broken | Replace the upper controller. |
| Maybe the lower control is broken | Replace the lower controller. |

**Order matters.** Start with the key board, because a stuck key is the cheapest and most common of the three.
