**This is the machine's own power switch staying dark. A lit switch with a dark console is a different card.**

This row is printed in this manual and not in the 2016 or 2021 manuals for the neighbouring builds.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The power cord is not plugged into the outlet | Plug the power cord into the outlet. |
| The power cord is not plugged into the unit | Plug the power cord into the unit. |
| The voltage of the outlet is too low | Check the voltage of the outlet. |
| The plug or connector of the power cord is open | Replace the power cord. |
| The connector of the power cord is broken | Replace the power cord. |
| A connecting cable is disconnected | Check if a wire is disconnected and connect it again. |
| The breaker has tripped | **Press the small red button** to return it to its original status. |
| The breaker is broken | Replace the breaker. |
| The ON/OFF switch is broken | Replace the AC switch. |
