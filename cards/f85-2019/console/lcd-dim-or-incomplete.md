**This is a display that lights but is wrong. A display that stays completely dark is a different card.**

The matrix prints two rows for this, one about the backlight and one about the panel itself.

**Not bright, incomplete or imperfect**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The backlight is broken | Replace with a new display or console. |
| Power to the console is too low | Check the AC power is 110V or 230V. Check the power to the console. Replace the lower controller. |

**Displays not bright, incomplete or imperfect**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The displays themselves are broken | Replace with a new console. |

**This manual does not list a fallen off connector as a cause here.** The 2016 and 2021 manuals for the neighbouring builds list "connector fall off, check the connector again" as the first thing to check. It is worth checking anyway before ordering a console.
