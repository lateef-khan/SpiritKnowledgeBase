| Symptom | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| The speed of the belt does not match the console display | The console is not calibrated | Calibrate the console. |
| The incline position does not match the console | The console is not calibrated | Calibrate the console. |

**This manual offers only the calibration.** The 2016 and 2021 manuals for the neighbouring builds add "replace the controller" as a second step for the speed row.
