**This product must be grounded.** If the treadmill malfunctions or breaks down, grounding gives electric current a path of least resistance and reduces the risk of shock. The machine ships with a cord carrying an equipment grounding plug, and that plug must go into an outlet installed and grounded to all local codes.

**DANGER.** Improperly connecting the equipment grounding conductor can cause electric shock. Ask a qualified electrician or serviceman if you are unsure whether the product is grounded. Do not modify the plug. If it does not fit the outlet, have a proper outlet installed.

The product is for use on a **nominal 230 volt circuit** (110Vac on a 120Vac electronic power system).

**Temporary adapter.** A temporary adapter may be used to connect the plug to a 2 pole receptacle only until a properly grounded outlet can be installed by an electrician. The green rigid ear plug extending from the adapter must be connected to a permanent ground, such as a properly grounded outlet box cover, and the adapter must be held in place by a metal screw whenever it is used.

**Console shuts off when touched on a cold day while walking or running.** The treadmill may not be grounded, and static electricity is crashing the computer. Fix the grounding.

**A number to watch.** This page says nominal **230 volt**, while the safety page two paragraphs earlier calls for a **220 volt, 10 amp** outlet. The 2016 and 2021 manuals for the neighbouring builds both say 220 volt on this page.
