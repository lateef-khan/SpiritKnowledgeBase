1. Remove the motor hood, fold the treadmill, then remove the incline motor.
2. **The incline range must be adjusted to 225 mm minimum before installation.** The manual prints this twice, once as its own section 11 and once as step 2 here.
3. Use the **M8 L wrench and a 14 mm wrench** to assemble the new incline motor.
4. Connect the incline motor wiring to the controller:
   - **Red** wire to **UP**
   - **White** wire to **COM**
   - **Black** wire to **DOWN**

Those three land on **JK80** on the driver board. The separate 3 pin position sensor cable lands on **JK60**.

Recalibrate the incline after fitting a new motor.

**Wrench list.** This manual calls for the M8 L wrench and a 14 mm wrench. The 2016 manual for the earlier ST535 build calls for the M6 and M8 L wrenches and a #14 wrench in the same step.
