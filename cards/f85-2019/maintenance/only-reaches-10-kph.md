The display showing a higher speed than the belt reaches means the motor **is** receiving power to operate. The cause given is **low AC voltage to the treadmill**.

1. **Do not use an extension cord.** If one is unavoidable it must be as short as possible and heavy duty, **16 AWG minimum**.
2. Suspect low household voltage. A minimum of **220 volt AC, 50 Hz** is required (110Vac on a 120Vac electronic power system).
3. Contact an electrician or the dealer.

A belt that reaches full speed but shows the wrong number is a calibration problem instead, and has its own card.
