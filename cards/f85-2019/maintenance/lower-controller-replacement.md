1. With a Phillips head screwdriver, loosen the **5 tapping screws** on the motor cover, then remove the motor cover.
2. Disconnect all lower controller wiring. Use the Phillips head screwdriver to remove the board, fit the new lower controller, and reconnect all wiring.

The sockets to reconnect are **JK80** incline power, **JK60** incline VR, **JK90** main control wire, **JK50** speed sensor, plus **M+** red and **M-** black to the drive motor and the AC power input.

Calibrate the console after fitting a new controller.
