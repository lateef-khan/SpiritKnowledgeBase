**This is a machine that stops part way through use. One that cuts out the instant start is pressed is a different card.**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| House breaker tripped | Reset it. |
| Treadmill breaker tripped | Reset the treadmill breaker. |
| The treadmill controller fuse is broken | Replace with a new fuse. |
| The treadmill controller has shut down | Turn the AC switch off and turn the power on again. |

**This manual names a broken controller fuse as one of the four causes.** The 2016 and 2021 manuals for the neighbouring builds list belt and deck lubrication in that place instead. Both are worth checking: a controller that keeps shutting down is protecting itself from over current, and friction is what drives the current up.

If the **house** breaker trips but the treadmill's own breaker does not, the house breaker needs to be a high inrush type.
