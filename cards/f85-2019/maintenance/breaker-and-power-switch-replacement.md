The manual gives both parts one line each, and both are the same job.

**Breaker (section 12-4)**

1. Remove the breaker connection wiring.
2. Replace the part and reconnect the wiring.

**AC power switch (section 12-5)**

1. Disconnect the wiring to the AC power switch.
2. Replace the switch and reconnect the wiring.

Before replacing either part, check the simple causes. A tripped breaker is reset by **pressing the small red button** to return it to its original state, and the breaker on the front grill can simply need pushing in until it locks. An unlit switch may be an unplugged cord rather than a failed switch.
