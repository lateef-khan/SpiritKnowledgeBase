**This is an electrical noise at switch on, not a mechanical noise during a workout.**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Incorrect input power; the varistor on the controller has blown | Check the voltage of the power is **110V or 230V**. Replace the controller. |

Check the supply before fitting a new controller. A blown varistor is a symptom of the supply, and a new controller on the same bad supply will fail again.
