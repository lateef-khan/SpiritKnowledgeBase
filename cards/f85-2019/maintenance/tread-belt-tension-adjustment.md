**This is the running belt tension at the rear roller. The drive belt between the motor and the front roller is a different adjustment, in its own card.**

Adjustment is made **from the rear roller**. The adjustment bolts are at the end of the step rails, inside the end caps, and are reached **through a small hole in the end cap**.

1. Tighten the rear roller bolts **only enough to stop the belt slipping at the front roller**.
2. Turn **both** tread belt tension adjustment bolts in increments of **1/4 turn each**.
3. Test by walking on the belt at a low speed and checking it does not slip.
4. Keep tensioning until the belt stops slipping.

**DO NOT OVERTIGHTEN.** Over tightening causes belt damage and premature bearing failure.

If the belt feels tight enough and still slips, suspect a **loose motor drive belt under the front cover** instead.

**Belt hesitates when it is stepped on**: either the tread belt has insufficient lubricant, or the tread belt tension is insufficient.

**Damage to the running belt caused by improper tracking or tension adjustment is not covered under the warranty.**
