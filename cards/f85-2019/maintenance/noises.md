**Start by locating the noise.**

| Where | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| Under the motor cover | Worn brushes or bearings on the motor | Replace the motor, or the motor brushes. |
| Under the motor cover | Front roller bearings are defective | Replace with a new front roller. |
| Under the motor cover | Drive belt is misadjusted, too tight or too loose | Adjust the motor position. |
| Rear of the treadmill | Rear roller bearings are defective | Replace with a new rear roller. |
| Rear of the treadmill | Rear roller misaligned | Adjust the rear roller position. |

**Noises that are not a fault**

- **Noise only at the lowest incline level** while the user is running can be too much pressure on the incline cylinder. It happens only at the lowest incline level.
- **Knocking during a workout**: check that all bolts are tightened.
- **Thumping while the belt runs**, on a brand new machine or one unused for a long time. The belt has taken the shape of the rollers and hardened in the cold. Running the belt for tens of minutes makes the thumping fade.
