1. With a Phillips head screwdriver, loosen the **2 screws on the rear adjustment base**.
2. With the **M6 L Allen wrench**, loosen the **2 screws on the rear roller**.
3. With a Phillips head screwdriver, remove the screws and take off the **left and right upright bracket covers** and the **motor cover**. With a **14 mm T-shaped socket wrench**, loosen the **4 securing screws**. With a **14 mm wrench**, loosen **one side tension screw** and the **rear drive belt tension screw** to slacken the drive belt.
   - On reassembly the **running belt tension has to be adjusted and the belt centred**.
4. Use a **14 mm open end wrench** and a **tension measuring device** to set the drive belt to the **white LBS area, between 70 and 75 LBS**. Use the 14 mm T-shaped socket wrench to secure and tighten the 4 securing screws.
5. Connect the grounding wires and the motor wires: **red M+, black M-**.
