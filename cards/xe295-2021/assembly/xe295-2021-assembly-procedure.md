## Step one - console mast, console and rails

The same nine jobs as the XE195, with two changes at the end:

1-6. Cover onto mast, computer cable up the mast, mast into the receiving bracket
   with the pre-installed bolt tightened **last**, **SPLIT WASHER (152)** on **BOLT
   (105)** through the **left** side and **CURVED WASHERS (153)** on the two **BOLTS
   (103)** through the **front**, front bolts first then left; the 2 **HAND PULSE
   CABLES (48)**, the **RESISTANCE CABLE (44)** and the **COMPUTER CABLE (50)** into
   the back of the **CONSOLE (43)**; console onto the bracket with 4 **PHILLIPS HEAD
   SCREWS (116)**.
7. Attach the **REAR FLOOR SUPPORT (15)** to the **RAILS (2 & 3)** with 2 **BUTTON
   HEAD SOCKET BOLTS (176)** and **CURVED WASHERS (153)** on each side, tightened with
   the **L Allen wrench**.
8. Slide the rail assembly into the **MAIN FRAME (1)**. One button head socket bolt
   (176) through each side, with a **FLAT WASHER (137)** and item **(130)** on the
   inside of each joint.

**The XE195 uses plain bolts and a Phillips-and-Allen combination tool here; the
XE295 uses button head socket bolts and an L Allen wrench.** Same item number, 176,
different head.

## Step two - lower swing arms and connecting arms

1. Two **WAVE WASHERS (150)** onto each side of the swing arm axle. Slide the **LOWER
   SWING ARMS (10 LEFT, 11 RIGHT)** on and secure with the 2 **HEX HEAD BOLTS (99)**
   and **FLAT WASHERS (141)**. **Do not force them onto the axle** - they were fitted
   at the factory, so jiggle rather than hammer.
2. Remove the tie holding the spacer in the rod end of the **RIGHT CONNECTING ARM
   (9)**, line it up with the bracket at the bottom of the **LOWER RIGHT SWING ARM
   (11)**, slide the **HEX HEAD BOLT (101)** through the bracket, the rod end and the
   spacer, add the **FLAT WASHER (142)** and **NYLOC NUT (192)** and tighten as much
   as possible. Repeat on the left.

## Step three - upper swing arms

1. Slide the **RUBBER SLEEVE (162)** onto the **LEFT (16)** and **RIGHT (17)** upper
   swing arms, **wide part at the bottom**.
2. Join the **WIRE (50)** from the **RIGHT UPPER SWING ARM (17)** to the **WIRE (49)**
   leaving the console mast tube, and slide the **SWITCH WIRE CAP (161)** onto the
   wire **wide side facing the swing arm**.
3. Insert the upper swing arm into the lower one and fasten with **3 HEX HEAD BOLTS
   (159)**, **2 CURVED WASHERS (160)** and **3 NYLOC NUTS (127)**.
4. Repeat job 2 on the left side.

## Step four - covers and levellers

1. Two **WHEEL COVERS (79)** to the rollers with 4 **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (115)**.
2. The **LEFT AND RIGHT SIDE COVER (80)** to the mid-stabilizer tube with 2 screws
   (115); the **RIGHT SIDE COVER (85)** and **LEFT SIDE COVER (86)** to the rear
   stabilizer tube with 2 screws (115). Tighten all four with the L Allen wrench.
3. **LEFT HANDLE BAR COVERS (81 & 82)** and **RIGHT HANDLE BAR COVERS (83 & 84)** over
   the handle bar axle connections, **4 SHEET METAL SCREWS (119) on each side**.
4. Check the four floor levellers under the middle and rear of the machine - see
   [the levelling card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xe-assembly-levelling-bottom-nut.md).

## Only the right swing arm is wired on this machine

Step three job 2 joins a wire on the **right** side and its panel supplies **one**
switch wire cap. Job 4 then says "Repeat #3 from above on the left side" - and the
left upper swing arm has no wire and no second cap to fit. The **XE395ENT** does wire
both sides, a resistance cable on the right and an incline cable on the left, and its
panel supplies **two** caps. Do not carry that across.

## Handle bar covers take four screws a side here and three on the XE195

Same item 119, same 3.5 x 12mm size, different count: **4 per side on the XE295**
against 3 on the XE195, and the panel supplies 8 against 6. See
[the hardware card](xe295-2021-assembly-hardware-by-step.md).
