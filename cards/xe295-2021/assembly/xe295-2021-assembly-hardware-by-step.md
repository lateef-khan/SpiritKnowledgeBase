## Step four is four screws short of its own instructions

Its three jobs call for **4** of item 115 on the wheel covers, **2** on the
mid-stabilizer cover and **2** on the rear stabilizer covers - **eight**. The panel
supplies **four**. The XE195, which needs the same eight, supplies eight of its
equivalent item 116. Expect to be short.

## Item 130 is a bolt on this machine and a nut on the XE195

The panel calls **130** a *3/8" x 3-3/4" Bolt*, quantity 4, and step 1's last job
says to "attach a FLAT WASHER (137) and a BOLT (130) to each joint on the inside" -
a bolt threaded onto a bolt. The **XE195** panel gives item 130 as a *3/8" x 7T
Nyloc nut*, quantity 2, and its identical job says nut. **Two books, one item number,
two different parts.** Whatever is in the bag, the joint takes a washer and a fastener
on the inside; do not order item 130 across the two machines.

## Step 3 supplies one switch wire cap for a job that is repeated on both sides

Job 2 fits **SWITCH WIRE CAP (161)** on the right and job 4 says to repeat it on the
left. **One cap is in the bag**, and only the right upper swing arm carries a wire.
The XE395ENT, which wires both arms, supplies two.

## What closes exactly

Step 3's counts are the tidiest in the book: **3 bolts, 2 curved washers and 3 nuts
per side**, and the panel supplies 6, 4 and 6. Step 2's four wave washers close at
two per side, and step 4's eight sheet metal screws close at four per side.
