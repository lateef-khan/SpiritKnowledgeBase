**If there is a problem with the incline, try running the calibration.**

1. Press the **Start key, Level up key and Stop key at the same time**.
2. **Hold them down for 5 seconds.** The incline calibration then **starts and runs
   automatically**.
3. **If the problem persists, contact the service department.**

That is the whole paragraph as printed. **The manual prints no message, no code, no pass or fail
result and no duration for the calibration**, and it does not say whether anyone may be standing
on the machine while it runs. Do not tell a caller to look for a word on the screen.

**Three keys, not two.** The XE395 2010 book gives a different calibration for the same
subsystem - **Start and resistance level UP only**, then Enter, after which the system restarts -
reached as item `g` of that console's Maintenance Menu. See
`xe395-2010-errors-incline-ramp-error-recalibrate`. Quote the combination that belongs to the
machine in front of you; the two are not interchangeable.

**No later XE395 printing carries this paragraph.** The word `calibration` does not appear
anywhere in the XE395 2023, XE395ENT 2021, XE195 2021, XE295 2021, XG400 2021, XE795 2021
February, XE795 2021 November or XE795 2023 books, nor in the XE795 2018, XE195 2019, XE295 2019
or XG400 2019 printings carded in this same wave. It is printed only here and, for a different
subsystem, in the XE895 2018 book.

**This machine has a powered incline rail and no adjustable stride.** The word `stride` does not
appear in its manual at all. The matching paragraph in the XE895 2018 book calibrates the
**stride** instead, on a machine with no incline - see
`xe895-2018-errors-stride-calibration-three-key-hold`. The key hold is the same; what it
calibrates is not.

The machine still prints **no error code and no troubleshooting chapter** - see
`spirit-xe-errors-no-error-codes-printed`. The word `error` does not appear in this manual once.
A Spirit treadmill `INCLINE ERR` is a different fault on a different platform with its own causes
and its own test - see `ct850-2016-incline-err-during-incline-action`.
