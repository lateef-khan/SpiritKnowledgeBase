## Step one - incline rail, console mast and console

**One panel and one numbered list of ten jobs.** The XE395ENT 2021 splits the same
work into two facing panels, 1-A and 1-B; this book does not.

1. Slide the **INCLINE RAIL ASSEMBLY (2)** into the U channel of the **MAIN FRAME
   (1)**, taking care not to damage the wires leaving each part.
2. Connect it **horizontally** with 2 **HEX HEAD BOLTS (104)**, 2 **FLAT WASHERS
   (137)** and 2 **NYLOC NUTS (130)**. Secure it **vertically** with 4 **HEX HEAD
   BOLTS (185)**, 4 split washers, 4 flat washers and 4 **STAR WASHERS (154)**.
   Tighten with wrenches 155 and 158.
3. Connect the **INCLINE MOTOR WIRES (46 & 47)** to the wiring harness and the black
   wire leaving the incline rail assembly, and push the excess inside the U channel.
4. Slide the **CONSOLE MAST COVER (72)** onto the **CONSOLE MAST (12)** as far as it
   will go, facing the correct way.
5. Feed the twist tie wire and the **COMPUTER CABLE (53)** from the top opening of the
   main frame into the bottom of the mast and out of the top.
6. Install the mast into the receiving bracket on top of the main frame. **Do not
   pinch the cables between the tubing.** One bolt is already in the bracket and
   engages the slot at the bottom of the mast; it is tightened **last**.
7. **SPLIT WASHER (152)** onto **HEX HEAD BOLT (105)**, hand tightened through the
   **left** side. A **CURVED WASHER (153)** onto each **HEX HEAD BOLT (103)**, both
   threaded into the **front**. Front bolts as tight as possible first, then the two
   left side bolts.
8. Connect the 2 **HAND PULSE CABLES (47)**, the **RESISTANCE CABLE (49)** and the
   **COMPUTER CABLE (53)** to the back of the **CONSOLE (43)**. They fit one way only
   and are different sizes; do not force them. Store the excess in the mast tube.
9. Fit the console to the mast bracket with 4 **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (116)**.

## Step two - lower swing arms and connecting arms

1. Two **WAVE WASHERS (196)** onto each side of the swing arm axle, then the **LOWER
   SWING ARMS (10 LEFT, 11 RIGHT)**, secured with the 2 **BOLTS (184)** and **FLAT
   WASHERS (141)**. They were fitted at the factory; jiggle, do not force.
2. Remove the tie holding the spacer in the rod end of the **RIGHT CONNECTING ARM
   (9)**, line it up with the bracket at the bottom of the **LOWER RIGHT SWING ARM
   (11)**, slide the **BOLT (186)** through bracket, rod end and spacer, add the
   **FLAT WASHER (142)** and **NYLOC NUT (192)** and tighten as much as possible.
   Repeat on the left, with wrenches 155 and 158.

## Step three - upper swing arms

1. **RUBBER SLEEVE (162)** onto the **LEFT (16)** and **RIGHT (17)** upper swing arms,
   **wide part at the bottom**.
2. Join the **WIRE (50)** from the **RIGHT UPPER SWING ARM (17)** to the **WIRE (49)**
   leaving the console mast tube, and slide the **SWITCH WIRE CAP (161)** on, **wide
   side facing the swing arm**.
3. Insert the upper swing arm into the lower one and fasten with **3 HEX HEAD BOLTS
   (159)**, **2 CURVED WASHERS (160)** and **3 NYLOC NUTS (127)**.
4. Repeat on the left side.

## Step four - covers, and the incline goes up

1. Two **WHEEL COVERS (79 & 80)** above the rollers with 4 **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS
   (115)**.
2. **Plug the power cord in, switch on, press start and raise the incline to level
   8.** Fit the **COVER (85)** to the mid-stabilizer tube with 2 screws (115).
3. **LEFT HANDLE BAR COVERS (81 & 82)** and **RIGHT HANDLE BAR COVERS (83 & 84)** over
   the handle bar axle connections, **4 SHEET METAL SCREWS (119) on each side**.
4. A **STEEL BRACKET (180)** on the left and right of the rear stabilizer tube, single
   hole facing the rear, 4 **PHILLIPS HEAD SCREWS (122)**.
5. The front **REAR STABILIZER COVER (87)** to the **REAR STABILIZER TUBE (2)** with 2
   screws (115), then the back **REAR STABILIZER COVER (88)** with 4 screws (115).
6. Check the four floor levellers, **top nut** - see
   [the levelling card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xe-assembly-levelling-top-nut.md).
7. **Return the elevation to flat with the Incline down key.**

## The incline has to be up before one cover goes on

Job 2 is the only reason this build needs mains power, and it is the only place in the
whole book where the machine is switched on before the build is finished. The **XE395
2010** and the **XE395ENT 2021** do the same thing at the same point, and the **XE395
2023** does it too but loses the level number from the sentence.

## Only the right swing arm is wired, and two caps are supplied

Step three job 2 joins a wire on the **right** side and job 5 says "Repeat steps from
above on the left side". **The bag holds two switch wire caps**, so the left side has
one to fit even though this book never says what it caps. The **XE395ENT 2021** wires
both arms deliberately, a resistance cable right and an incline cable left; the
**XE295 2021** names both sides and supplies one cap. This machine is the third
variant: one wire named, two caps supplied.

## The split and flat washers are the wrong way round in step one

Job 2's sentence secures the incline rail vertically with "4 SPLIT WASHERS (142), 4
FLAT WASHERS (151)". Its own panel has **142 as the flat washer** (5/16" x 20 x 1.5T)
and **151 as the split washer** (8 x 1.5T). **Trust the panel.** The **XE395ENT 2021**
prints the same swap, so this is not a one-off typo in one book; the **XE395 2010**
and the **XE395 2023** both print the joint the right way round. See
[the hardware card](xe395-2018-assembly-hardware-by-step.md).

## Three jobs the XE395ENT build has and this one does not

- **No screws on the console mast cover.** The ENT tightens 3 **SHEET METAL SCREWS
  (120)** onto the cover in its step 1-B. This book slides the cover on and says
  nothing about screws - and still supplies three of item 120 in the step-one bag.
- **No oval end caps.** The ENT presses 4 **OVAL END CAPS (93)** into the main frame at
  the end of step one. Item 93 is nowhere in this book.
- **A computer cable where the ENT has an incline cable.** Job 8 connects a **COMPUTER
  CABLE (53)**; the ENT connects an **INCLINE CABLE (56)**. The cable item numbers
  differ throughout - 47, 49 and 53 here against 53, 55 and 56 on the ENT - so do not
  order a cable across the two books.
