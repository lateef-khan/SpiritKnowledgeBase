## Step two is two wave washers short of its own job

Its job 2 says "Slide **2** WAVE WASHERS (196) onto **each side** of the Swing Arm
Axle" - four washers. **The panel supplies two.** The **XE395 2023** has the same
sentence and the same short bag; the **XE395ENT 2021** has the same sentence and
supplies four. Expect to be short by two, or to be fitting one per side.

## Two split washers with two different sizes

Item **151** is a *8 x 1.5T (5/16" x 1.5T)* split washer, four of them, on the incline
rail joint. Item **152** is a *Ø10 x 2T* split washer, one of it, under the left side
console mast bolt. Both are called SPLIT WASHER in the same panel; they are not
interchangeable.

## Item 120 is supplied and never used

Three **3.5 x 16mm sheet metal screws** sit in the step-one bag and **no job in any of
the four steps fits them**. On the XE395ENT and the XE395 2023 the same item 120 holds
the console mast cover on and the step text says so; this book's step text does not.
Three screws will be left in the bag - the same defect the XE195 has.

## One panel where the XE395ENT prints two

This book heads a single table HARDWARE FOR STEP 1 with all thirteen rows. The
XE395ENT splits the same thirteen into a 1-A panel and a 1-B panel and repeats items
**130**, **137** and **142** in both, at the same quantities. **Read the two ENT panels
as one bag**; this book already prints it as one.

## Almost the XE395 2023 bag, without the tablet holder

Step 1, step 3 and step 4 hold the same item numbers and quantities as the **XE395
2023** picture panels, and step 2 matches it too - **except that the 2023 step-two bag
adds item 200-16, three M5 x 12L screws for the tablet holder**. There is no tablet
holder and no item 200 anywhere in this book. Step 4's item 115 is printed **M5 x
12mm** here and on the XE395ENT, and **M5 x 15L** on the 2023.

## What closes and what does not

- Step 3's counts are the tidiest in the book: **3 bolts, 2 curved washers and 3 nuts
  per side**, and the panel supplies 6, 4 and 6.
- Step 4's **twelve** of item 115 close exactly - four on the wheel covers, two on the
  mid-stabilizer cover, two and four on the two rear stabilizer covers - and its
  **eight** of item 119 close at four per side.
- Step 2's **two** wave washers do not close; the job asks for four.

## No parts list to check any of this against

This manual prints no numbered parts list and no exploded view, so every item number
above exists only in these four panels and the step text. The XE395ENT parts list
(pp. 51-55) and the XE395 2023 parts list (pp. 39-42) renumber parts of the machine
and must not be used on this one.
