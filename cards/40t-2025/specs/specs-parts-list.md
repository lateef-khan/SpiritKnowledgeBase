**This is the list in the two 2025 printings (Rev 02.04.25 and Rev 05.19.25) of the 4.0T owner's manual; the 2026 book prints a longer one.** Both 2025 printings are identical, row for row. The numbers are the balloon numbers on the exploded view, a flat drawing stamped `20221201`. **No Spirit part number is printed.** 144 rows run 1 to 180; 36 whole numbers are absent, gaps in the numbering rather than a missing page. The console is item 28 `Console Assembly` with no sub-parts listed.

## What changed for 2026

The 2026 list (`40t-2026-specs-parts-list`) keeps every row here and adds six rows at the end, **181 to 186** - `Guide wheel Assembly Bracket` (2), `Cable Wheel` (2), an M5 flat Phillips screw (2), a `C Ring` (2), a `Ø10 × Ø15 × 1T_Flat Washer` (4) and a `Ø10 × 0.3T_Wave Washer` (2). Two rows are printed differently:

| Item | 2025 printings | 2026 manual |
|---|---|---|
| 50 | Ø35 × 21 × 13L_Ferrite Core, 1 | 186, (blank) |
| 67 | 5 × 20m/m_Tapping Screw, 22 | 5 × 19m/m_Tapping Screw, 26 |
| 127 | 3/8" × UNC16 × 2-1/2"_Socket Head Cap Bolt, 1 | 3/8" × UNC16 × 2-1/2"_Socket Head Cap Bolt (Alloy Steel), 1 |

So a 2025 machine's tapping screw at item 67 is a 5 × 20 mm screw, twenty-two of them, and a 2026 machine's a 5 × 19 mm screw, twenty-six of them. Quote the year with the number.

## What the list names that the prose does not

- Drive: 10 `Front Roller W/Pulley`, 11 `Rear Roller`, 12 `Running Deck`, 13 `Running Belt`, 16 `Drive Belt`, 44 `AC Motor`, 46 `Inverter`, 47 `Filter`, 48 `Inverter Board`, 49 `Fan`, 50 `Ø35 × 21 × 13L_Ferrite Core`, 51 `AC Electronic Module`; two rows both read `Incline Motor`, 43 and 57 (the front and the rear incline motor).
- Tools packed as items: 141 `L Allen Wrench`, 142 `6mm Allen Wrench`, 143 `Phillips Head Screw Driver`. No separate tool list is printed.

**The 7.0T and 8.0T of the same year are not on this list.** The 2025 7.0T prints its own list (`70t-2025-specs-parts-list`); the 2026 7.0T and 8.0T print a drawing with no list (`spirit-2026t-specs-no-parts-list`).
