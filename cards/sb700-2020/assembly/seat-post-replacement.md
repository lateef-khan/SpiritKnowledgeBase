1. Use a **4 mm L Allen wrench** to release **three M5 x 10 mm socket head cap bolts (89)**.
2. Release the **8 x 40 m/m quick release lever (19)** by hand and take it off. Separate the **seat post (5)** and the **sliding seat mount (7)**.
3. Release the **8 x 25 m/m quick release lever (20)** by hand and take it off. Separate the seat post (5) and the sliding seat mount (7).
4. Use a **14 m/m open wrench** to release the nut holding the **seat (27)**, then take the seat off.
5. Take the **8 x 40 m/m quick release lever (19)** and the **spring (14)** apart before you assemble the seat post (5).
6. Reverse the steps to refit.

Steps 2 and 3 both describe separating the same two parts, with a different quick release lever each time. The 2019 manual has the same pair of steps.
