1. Remove the **outer chain cover (60)**, the **flywheel fender (62)** and the **braking pad holder (009)**.
2. Use an **11 m/m open wrench** to loosen (turn clockwise) the **1/4" x 3"L hex head bolt (69)** on the **idler axle (74)**, then take the **belt (40)** off.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew the **5/16" x 3/4" button head socket bolt (36)**, then use the tool shown in the manual to take the **hub pulley (35)** apart.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **six M6 x 10L Phillips head screws (39)**.
5. Use the tool shown in the manual to pull out both left and right **bearing housings (32)**.
6. Use C-ring pliers to release the **20 mm C ring (68)**.
7. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **six 5/16" x 3/4"L button head socket bolts (38)** to take the **flywheel axle (33)** apart.
8. Reverse the steps to refit.
9. Move the **idler axle (74)** downward by turning the **1/4" x 3"L hex head bolt (69) counterclockwise**, and tighten the **belt (40)** until belt tension reads **700 to 750 Newton**.

**Note:** the bearing housing (32) and the **6004 bearing (66)** are glued together in production. If the bearing needs replacing, replace the whole set.

The belt slippage answer in section 4.1 of this same manual says **1000 Newton** instead. See the belt tension card.
