1. Remove the **outer chain cover (60)** and the **belt (40)**.
2. Use an **M6 hex wrench** to take the **idler axle (74)** apart.
3. Take the **idler adjustment carriage bolt (75)** apart together with the **1/4" x 3"L hex head bolt (69)**.
4. Use C-ring pliers to remove the **17 mm C ring (67)**, then take the **6203 bearing (065)** and the **rubber pad (136)** off the idler axle (74).
5. Reverse the steps to refit. Follow section 3.3 to set belt (40) tension.

The 2019 machine has no rubber pad (136) at this joint.
