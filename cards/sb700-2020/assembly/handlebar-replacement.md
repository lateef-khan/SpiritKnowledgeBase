1. As in step 2 of section 3.6, take the **handlebar post (4)** apart.
2. Use an **M5 hex wrench** to unscrew the **5/16" x 5/8" button head socket bolt (88)** and separate the **handlebar (6)** from the **sliding handlebar mount (8)**.
3. Use an **M5 Allen wrench** to release the **5/16" x 1" button head socket bolt (97)**, then loosen the **8 x 25 m/m quick release lever (20)** to separate the sliding handlebar mount (8) from the handlebar post (4).
4. Reverse the steps to refit.
