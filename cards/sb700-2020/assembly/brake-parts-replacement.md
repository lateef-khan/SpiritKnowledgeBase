1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **four M6 x 15 mm Phillips head screws (18)** and take the **flywheel fender (62)** off.
2. Take the **safety sleeve (96)** apart. Use a **30 m/m open wrench** to release the **34.5 x 15L-M30 x P1.25 nut (12)** together with the **brake tension knob (16)** and the **spring (94)**.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **two M5 x 10 m/m tapping screws (93)** and take the **leather brake pad (10)** off.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.
5. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **four M6 x 15 Phillips head screws (18)** and take the **flywheel fender (62)** off.
6. Take the **safety sleeve (96)** apart. Use a **30 m/m open wrench** to release the **34.5 x 15L-M30 x P1.25 nut (12)** together with the **brake tension knob (16)** and the **spring (94)**.
7. Take the **two M6 x 15 mm Phillips head screws (18)** together with the **braking pad holder (009)** and the **brake pad wool felt (10)**.
8. Use a Phillips head screwdriver and a **30 m/m open wrench** to release **two M5 x 10 m/m tapping screws (93)** and take the **leather brake pad (010)** off. Reverse the steps to refit.

**The manual prints the procedure twice.** Steps 1 to 4 and steps 5 to 8 repeat the same work, with the second pass adding the braking pad holder (009). The part number for the pad is written both (10) and (010). Treat steps 5 to 8 as the fuller version.
