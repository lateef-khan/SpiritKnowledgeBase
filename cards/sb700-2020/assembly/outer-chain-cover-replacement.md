1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **two 5 x 16 m/m tapping screws (29)** and **three 3.5 x 12 m/m sheet metal screws (30)**, then take the **outer chain cover (60)** off.
2. Reverse the step to refit the outer chain cover (60).
