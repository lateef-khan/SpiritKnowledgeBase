**This manual prints two different figures and does not say which is right.**

| Where in the manual | Figure |
|---|---|
| Section 3.3 step 9, refitting the flywheel | 700 to 750 Newton |
| Section 4.1, the belt slippage answer | 1000 Newton |

The SB700 2019 manual prints **700 to 750 N** in both places. The SB900 2020 manual prints **1000 N** in both places. So the 2020 SB700 manual carries one figure from each of its neighbours.

Set the tension by moving the **idler axle (74)** downward, turning the **1/4" x 3"L hex head bolt (69) counterclockwise**.

Check with the technical department before you settle on a figure for this machine.
