**700 to 750 Newton is the standard setting.** If the belt still slips after it has been set to 700 N, take it up to **1000 N**.

The manual prints both figures and never says how they relate:

| Where in the manual | Figure |
|---|---|
| Section 3.3 step 9, refitting the flywheel | 700 to 750 Newton |
| Section 4.1, the belt slippage answer | 1000 Newton |

That is the resolution. **700 to 750 N is the figure you build to. 1000 N is the fix for a belt that keeps slipping at 700 N.**

Set the tension by moving the **idler axle (74)** downward, turning the **1/4" x 3"L hex head bolt (69) counterclockwise**.

The neighbouring machines print one figure each: the SB700 2019 gives 700 to 750 N in both places, and the SB900 2020 gives 1000 N in both.
