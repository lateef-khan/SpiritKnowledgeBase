**If the belt slips:** follow section 3.3 and move the **idler axle (74)** downward by turning the **1/4" x 3"L hex head bolt (69) counterclockwise**. Tighten the **belt (40)** until belt tension reads **1000 Newton**. Section 3.3 of the same manual says 700 to 750 N.

**If the belt falls off:**

1. Move the **idler axle (74)** upward by turning the **1/4" x 3"L hex head bolt (69) clockwise**, refit the **belt (40)** and adjust the tension.
2. While adjusting, make sure the **6203 bearing (65)**, the **drive pulley (41)** and the **hub pulley (35)** line up straight.
3. Run the belt for **5 minutes** and check whether it comes off again.
4. If there is no problem, refit the **outer chain cover (60)**.
5. If it still falls off, replace the **idler axle (74)** or the **idler adjustment carriage bolt (75)**.
6. If that does not fix it, the **main frame (1)** is deformed and needs replacing.
