**Nine steps, against ten in the 2019 and 2020 books:** this 2009 book has no inner chain cover (61) step, and its last bolt is turned with wrench (86) rather than a named M5 Allen wrench. The fasteners are otherwise the same.

1. Follow procedures 1 and 3 to remove the **outer chain cover (60)** and the **belt (40)**.
2. Use the **14/15 mm wrench (85)** to take the left **pedals (25)** off.
3. Remove the **crank arm dust cap (44)** on the left **crank arm (42)** and use a **14 mm plug wrench** to release the **M10 x P1.25 x 7T nut (80)**, then take the left crank arm (42) off.
4. Use C-ring pliers to remove the **Ø20 C-ring (68)** and take the **crank axle (45)**, the **drive pulley (41)** and the **pulley cover (63)** apart.
5. Use the C-ring pliers to release the other Ø20 C-ring (68).
6. Remove the crank arm dust cap (44) on the right **crank arm (43)** and use the 14 mm plug wrench to release the M10 x P1.25 x 7T nut (80), then take the right crank arm (43) off.
7. Use wrench (86) to unscrew the **Ø5 x 10 mm tapping screw (90)** and take the pulley cover (63) off.
8. Use wrench (86) to unscrew the **5/16" x 1/2"L button head socket bolt (46)** and separate the crank axle (45) from the drive pulley (41).
9. Reverse the steps to refit, and follow procedure 3 to set the belt tension (700 to 750 N).

The heading is printed "Clank Arms" in this book too. The belt-tension figure it points to, 700 to 750 newtons, is on the shared flywheel card.
