1. As in procedure 6 (the seat post), separate the **handlebar (6)** and the **sliding handlebar mount (8)** from the **handlebar post (4)**.
2. Use an **M5 hex wrench** to unscrew the **5/16" x 5/8" button head socket bolt (88)** and separate the handlebar (6) from the sliding handlebar mount (8).
3. Reverse the steps to refit.

**Two steps, where the 2019 and 2020 books add a third:** a 5/16" x 1" button head socket bolt released with the M5 Allen wrench before the 8 x 25 mm quick-release lever frees the sliding mount from the post. This book prints only the 5/16" x 5/8" bolt.
