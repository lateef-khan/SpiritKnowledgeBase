**Three steps and one screw, where the 2019 and 2020 books release two quick-release levers, a 14 mm seat nut and a spring.**

1. Remove the **end cap, eye tube (56)**.
2. Use an **M4 hex wrench** to release the **M5 x 10 mm socket head cap screw (89)** on the **seat post (5)** and pull the seat post (5) out.
3. Release the **8 x 25 mm quick release lever (20)** by hand and take it off; separate the seat post (5) and the **sliding seat mount (7)**.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.

The seat itself is not removed in this book's procedure - the 2019 book adds a 14 mm nut for it. The book's own Q&A says a sliding seat mount or handlebar that comes loose during a workout is cured by tightening the **8 x 40 mm quick release lever (19)** (the linked card).
