**This is the 2009 SB700 book (filed as XIC600, models SB700 and SB702).** It prints no safety sleeve and no spring at the tension knob, which the 2019 and 2020 books add; otherwise the parts match.

1. Use the combination M5 Allen wrench and Phillips head screwdriver (86) to unscrew **four M6 x 15 mm Phillips head screws** and take the **flywheel fender (62)** off.
2. Use a **30 mm open wrench** to release the **34.5 x 15L-M30 x P1.25 nut (12)** and the **brake tension knob (16)**.
3. Use a hex wrench and the **14/15 mm wrench (85)** to release the **M8 nylock nut** that secures the **brake pad bracket (9)**, and take the bracket off.
4. Use the combination wrench (86) to unscrew **two M5 x 10 mm tapping screws (93)** and take the **leather brake pad (10)** off.
5. Reverse the steps to refit.

Tool (86) is "Combinational M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screwdriver" in this book - one tool with both ends. The flywheel procedure (shared with the 2019 card) starts by sending you here.
