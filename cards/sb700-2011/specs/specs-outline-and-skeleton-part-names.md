**Unit Outline (p. 1):** Seat Cushion, **Tube Insert**, **Brake Tension Knob**, Stabilizer End Cap,
**Quick Release**, **Pulley Cover**, **Flywheel Fender**, Outer Chain Cover, Stabilizer End Cap,
Rear Stabilizer Cover, Bottom End cap, Pedal, **Eye Tube End Cap**.

**Skeleton (p. 2):** Seat Post, Sliding Seat Mount, Main Frame, Handlebar Post, **Sliding
Handlebar Mount**, Handlebar, Rear Stabilizer, Drive Pulley, **Inner Chain Cover**, Flywheel,
**Leather Brake Pad**, Front Stabilizer, Crank Arm, **Idler Axle**, **HUB PULLEY**.

The *Quick Release* levers clamp the seat post and handlebar post in their sliding mounts; the
*Brake Tension Knob* presses the *Leather Brake Pad* onto the flywheel - the only control on a
bike whose console is an option (`sb700-2011-console-console-is-an-option-in-the-2009-book`).
The *Idler Axle* (item 74 in the replacement steps) is what the belt-tension procedure moves.

The book's cover names the machine **XIC600 (SB700, SB702)**; no model number, dimensions or
weights are printed on these pages. The Spirit CIC850 of 2022 is the same kind of friction-brake
cycle with a brake block (`cic850-2022-specs-outline-and-skeleton-part-names`) - a different brand.
