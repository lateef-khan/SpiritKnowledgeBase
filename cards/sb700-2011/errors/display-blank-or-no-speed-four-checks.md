Q&A 4, `Display Blank or No Speed Displayed`, in the 2009 Dyaco book for the SB700:

1. Make sure there is power for the unit.
2. Make sure Speed Sensor and magnet are well installed.
3. Verify input/output of transmitter signal.
4. If everything is normal, change the transmitter position. **Some location may cause the signal harder to receive.**

Four checks and no pairing step - the later CIC850 book, on the same console family, adds *each transmitter and console are matched one to one* as a fifth (`cic850-2022-errors-display-blank-or-no-speed-five-checks`, a Spirit card). "Power for the unit" on this bike means the console's batteries; the owner's-manual entry for the same console - press any key, then fresh batteries in both console and transmitter - is `sole-spinner-no-display-battery-consoles`.

This book heads its console section `Display Blank (with Console Option)` in the contents: the 2009 SB700 could be sold without a console, and a bike that never had one shows nothing because there is nothing to show.

The rest of the book's Q&A is carded under maintenance from the later SB700 books, which print the same answers: belt slip and fall-off with a 700-750 N idler adjustment (`sb700-2019-belt-slips-or-falls-off`), a loose seat mount or handlebar fixed by the 8 x 40 mm quick release lever (`sole-spinner-seat-or-handlebar-loose`), and noise - the flywheel rubbing the leather brake pad is normal (`sole-spinner-noises`).
