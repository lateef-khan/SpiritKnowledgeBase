**Three tables for four stages.** Step zero has none - the two bolts it removes come out
of the machine, not out of the bag, and are thrown away.

## Hardware for step 1

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 78 | Hex head bolt | 3/8" x 3-3/4" | 2 |
| 81 | Socket head cap bolt | 3/8" x 3/4" | 4 |
| 100 | Flat washer | 3/4" x ø19 x 1.5T | 4 |
| 106 | Curved washer | 3/8" x 23 x 2.0T | 2 |
| 108 | Spring washer | ø10 x 2T | 6 |

## Hardware for step 2

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 75 | Hex head bolt | 3/8" x 3/4" | 6 |
| 76 | Button head socket bolt | 3/8" x 16 x 2" | 2 |
| 77 | Hex head bolt | 3/8 x 2-14" | 4 |
| 93 | Nyloc nut | 3/8" x 7T | 4 |
| 100 | Flat washer | 3/8" x 19"1.5T | 4 |
| 106 | Curved washer | 3/8" x 23 x 2.0T | 8 |

## Hardware for step 3

| Part | Type | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 83 | Screw | M5 x 10MM | 10 |
| 85 | Sheet metal screws | 4 x 15 | 3 |

## Item 100 is printed at two sizes in the same manual

The step one table calls item 100 a **3/4"** flat washer; the step two table calls the
same item **3/8"**. Every bolt it sits under in either step is a 3/8" bolt, and 3/8" is
what the machine takes. The 3/4" in the step one table is the odd one out and nothing
in the book corrects it.

## Two more descriptions to read carefully

- **Item 77 is printed "3/8 x 2-14""**, with no inch mark after the 3/8 and no fraction
  slash in the second figure. Every other bolt in the book is written as a fraction, so
  2-1/4" is what the printing is short of; the manual never prints it in full.
- **Item 108's description ends in a stray quotation mark** - `ø10 x 2T"`. It is a
  metric spring washer and the mark means nothing.

## Every count closes

Unusually for this range, the step arithmetic and the tables agree throughout. Step one
uses two of item 108 on the rear stabilizer and four on the mast, and the table lists
six; step two uses three of item 75 a side and the table lists six; item 106 is one a
side plus two a side in step two and two more in item 3, giving eight against a table
of eight. Nothing is over or under.

## What the tables do not print

**No torque figure for any bolt**, and no table for the two bolts step zero removes -
the manual only says to discard them.

## The 2021 printing is the same chapter but for one step

`spirit-climber-cs800-2021-owners-manual`, pp. 8-13, prints step zero, step one, step
two and step two continued unchanged, with all three hardware tables identical - the
same **3/4"** typo on flat washer 100, the same **3/8 x 2-14"** on bolt 77 and the same
counts. **Step three of the 2021 printing stops at item 3.** It has no item 4, so the
water bottle holder on its own carton panel is never fitted and its own screw count of
ten leaves two over. See
[the water bottle holder card](../../cs800-2021/assembly/cs800-2021-assembly-water-bottle-holder-never-fitted.md).
