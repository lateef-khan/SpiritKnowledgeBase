Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. Hardware quantities are
on [the hardware card](cs800-2024-assembly-hardware-kit.md). **Every step names its
tool by item number**, and the tools panel is the only place those numbers are matched
to sizes - see [the tools card](cs800-2024-assembly-tools-included.md).

## Step zero - take the side back off and throw the bolts away

Use L Allen Wrench (No.119) to remove **two 3/8" x 3/4" Socket Head Cap Bolts** that
hold the protective **Side Back (No.17)** onto the mounting plate on the main frame.
**Discard the bolts** and set the Side Back aside.

*This is not the CE850 step zero, which tells you to dispose of the bracket and keep
nothing, and whose step one then needs four of the bolt it released. Here the bolts go
in the bin and no later step asks for them. See
[the step-zero card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-2024-assembly-step-zero-shipping-bracket.md).*

## Step one - rear stabilizer and console mast

1. Attach the **Rear Stabilizer with Handle (No.11)** to the mounting plate on the main
   frame and secure with 2 Hex Head Bolts (No.78), 2 Spring Washers (No.108) and Curved
   Washers (No.106), using Wrench (No.116).
2. Pull the tie on the **Console Mast (No.2)** so the Computer Cable (No.31) and
   Handpulse Cables (No.35) go up through the mast and out of the top. Secure the mast
   to the mounting plate with **4 Socket Head Cap Bolts (No.81), 4 Spring Washers
   (No.108) and 4 Flat Washers (No.100)**, using L Allen Wrench (No.119).

## Step two - both handlebar loops

1. Place the **Upper Left Handlebar (No.15)**, with its 90-degree bend at the front,
   onto the connecting plate welded to the console mast and secure with a Hex Head Bolt
   (No.75) and a Curved Washer (No.106) - **do not tighten completely**. Insert a
   **Handlebar Connecting Tube (No.10)** into the upper left handlebar at its
   107-degree-bending end and fix it temporarily with two Button Head Socket Bolts,
   using L Allen Wrench-M6 (No.120). Attach the bottom of the connecting tube to the
   U-shaped mounting on the **left** side of the Rear Stabilizer with Handle (No.11)
   with 2 Hex Head Bolts (No.77), 2 Flat Washers (No.100), 2 Curved Washers (No.106)
   and 2 Nyloc Nuts (No.93), using Wrench (No.116) and Wrench (No.117). Tighten and
   secure all other bolts.
2. Repeat for the **Upper Right Handlebar (No.16)** and the other connecting tube.
3. Attach the **Handlebar Connecting Plate (No.13)** to the upper handlebars with 2
   Button Head Socket Bolts (No.76) and 2 Curved Washers (No.106), using Wrench
   (No.116). Tighten all hardware listed in this step.

**Leave the top bolt loose until the bottom is in.** Item 1 says so explicitly, and the
two bends - 90 degrees at the front of the upper bar, 107 degrees at the top of the
connecting tube - only line up once both ends are located.

**Item 1 calls the two temporary fasteners "Button Head Socket Bolts (No.75)", and the
step's own table types item 75 as a Hex Head Bolt.** The table lists six of item 75 and
the step uses six, so the count closes; only the name disagrees. Item 76, which the
table does type Button Head Socket Bolt, is used in item 3 and nowhere else.

## Step three - covers, console and bottle holder

1. Match the **Left and Right Console Mast Covers (No.57, 58)** on top of the left and
   right chain covers and around the console mast, and secure with **3 Sheet Metal
   Screws (No.85)**, using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (No.118).
2. Match the **Left and Right Handlebar Caps (No.68, 69)** together on the U-shaped
   mounting on the left of the rear stabilizer and tighten 2 Phillips Head Screws
   (No.83). Repeat on the right.
3. Take the tie off the Computer Cable (No.31) and plug it into the **Console Assembly
   (No.30)** together with the two Handpulse Assembly Cables (No.35). Place the console
   on the mounting plate of the mast and secure with **4 Phillips Head Screws (No.83)**.
4. Secure the **Water Bottle Holder (No.113)** to the console mast with 2 Phillips Head
   Screws (No.83).

**The build ends here.** No step fits a leveller, although the carton counts four, and
no step fits the two transport wheels the carton also lists. The setting-up page that
follows is the first power-on only: connect the AC power cord and the console comes up
by itself.
