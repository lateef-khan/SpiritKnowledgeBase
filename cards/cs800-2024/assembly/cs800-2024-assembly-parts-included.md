Twelve lines, with quantities as printed:

| Qty | Part |
|---|---|
| 1 | Main Frame |
| 1 | Console Mast |
| 2 | Console Mast Cover (Left and Right) |
| 4 | Handlebar Tubing |
| 4 | Levelers |
| 1 | Rear Stabilizer |
| 1 | Console |
| 2 | Foot Pedals |
| 1 | Power Cord |
| 2 | Transport Wheels |
| 1 | Water Bottle Holder |
| 1 | Hardware Kit |

## Four levellers on the panel, two in the same book's maintenance chapter

The panel counts **4 Levelers**. The maintenance chapter says there are **2 levelling
pads on the bottom of the rear stabilizer** and to turn them with a 14mm wrench. Neither
page mentions the other, and **no step of the three-step build fits or adjusts a
leveller at all**. See
[the levelling card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md);
take the count from neither with confidence.

The CE850 elliptical of the same redesign has the same shape of contradiction with a
different number - six on the panel, two in the maintenance chapter.

## The transport wheels are on the panel and in no step

Nothing in the build fits them, and nothing in the book says how to use them to move
the machine. The CRS800S of the same redesign is the only 2024 machine whose build
fits its transport wheels.

## "4 Handlebar Tubing" is four different parts

Step two fits two **Upper Handlebars** (No.15 left, No.16 right) and two **Handlebar
Connecting Tubes** (No.10, one a side). The panel counts them as one line of four.

## The panel does not list the side back

**Step zero removes a protective Side Back (No.17) before step one begins**, and this
panel does not mention it - reasonably, since it arrives bolted to the machine rather
than loose in the carton. It is thrown away. See
[the step-zero card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-2024-assembly-step-zero-shipping-bracket.md).

## What the panel does not say

It gives no carton count, no shipping weight and no box dimensions, and it lists no
handlebar connecting plate or handlebar caps even though step two and step three fit
both.
