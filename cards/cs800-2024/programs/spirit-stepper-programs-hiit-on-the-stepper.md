**The CS800 has this program. The CRS800S does not** - it prints no HIIT description and no HIIT
procedure, and names HIIT only as a caption on its strip of profile pictures. See
`spirit-stepper-programs-five-programs-claimed-and-which-are-described`.

1. Using the **Program** button choose **HIIT**, then press Enter.
2. Enter your **Age**, then Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight**, then Enter.
4. Enter the **number of Intervals**. **The default is 10 and the range available is 3 to 15.** One
   interval equals 1 Sprint and 1 Rest segment.
5. Set the **Sprint Level** - the resistance level during the Sprint segments - then Enter.
6. Set the **Rest Level** - the resistance level during the Rest segments - then Enter.
7. Set the **Interval time**. The LED Data Display Window shows **Sprint Time:30** and **Rest
   Time:30**, with the Sprint time blinking. **Adjust the Sprint time from 20 to 60 seconds**, press
   Enter, then adjust the Rest time.
8. The LED Data Display Window shows the total HIIT time. Press **Start**. **There is a 3-minute
   warm-up period before the first Sprint. The resistance level during warm-up is set to 5** but can
   be adjusted manually.
9. **In the HIIT program the Dot Matrix Message Center is a speed indication display, not a power or
   resistance display.** During the Sprint a blinking LED at the first Sprint segment is the target
   speed LED and **indicates 90 pedal rpm**. As you pedal faster the lights below it light up; when
   you exceed 90 rpm the target LED moves up. **You should maintain at least 90 rpm throughout the
   Sprint segments.** The Rest segments are set to **resistance level 5** and you may pedal at any
   speed while your heart rate recovers. Resistance can be adjusted manually in both segments.
10. **At the end of the last Sprint there is a 2-minute cool-down period.** Press **Stop** to bypass
    it and show the workout summary.

The 2024 CE850 elliptical prints this same ten-step procedure; the 2024 CE800, CR800 and CU800 stop
at step 6 - see `spirit-2024-programs-hiit-procedure-stops-at-step-six`.
