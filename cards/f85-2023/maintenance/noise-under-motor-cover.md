1. Worn brushes or bearings on the motor. Replace with a new motor.
2. The front roller bearings are defective. Replace with a new front roller.
3. The drive belt is misadjusted, too tight or too loose. Adjust the motor position.

Drive belt tension is about **70~75 LBS**, measured with a tension meter.
