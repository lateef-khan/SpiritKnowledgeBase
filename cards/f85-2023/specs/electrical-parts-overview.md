| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| Safety Key | Fits on the console and activates all functions. With no safety key the console cannot be controlled and the display shows "Please Replace the Safety Key". |
| Console | The interface that controls all functions of the treadmill. |
| Main Controller | Carries the DC power supply for the console, the incline driver and the DC motor driver. It links the console to the motor and outputs the voltages that control the treadmill. |
| Drive Motor | A variable speed DC motor, controlled from 0 to 180 DC volts (0 to 90 DC volts on a 120Vac electronic power system) by the main controller. It speeds the running belt up and down. |
| Incline Motor | An AC motor. The user controls elevation from the console through the main controller. |
