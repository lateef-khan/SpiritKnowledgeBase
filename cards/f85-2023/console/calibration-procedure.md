1. Open **engineering mode**, then press **Engineering Mode** to enter Calibration.
2. Hit the block to enter the value with the digital keyboard, and press **ENTER** to save and close the keyboard.
   The display shows the max or min value if the entered value is out of range.
   - **English range: Max. 12.0 / Min. 0.5**
   - **Metric range: Max. 20.0 / Min. 1.0**
3. Press the **Calibration** button to start the procedure. The machine runs the speed and incline by itself, so
   stand clear.

**This manual disagrees with itself on the metric maximum.** Section 4.2 gives the speed work range as 1.0~22.0 km/h, but the calibration range printed here is Max. 20.0. Check what the machine accepts before entering a value.
