Press the **gear icon** above the screen while in IDLE MODE.

The settings page holds: **1) Unit System, 2) Child Lock, 3) Sleep Mode, 4) Screen Brightness, 5) Date & Time,
6) Wi-Fi, 7) Language, 8) Software, 9) Media Apps, 10) Passcode.**

**As printed, the manual says "8 sub-menu modes" and then lists ten.** The list is what the console shows.

**Screen Brightness** is the one item with no card of its own: it is a slider, left for darker and right for
brighter.

A **yellow dot on the gear icon** in IDLE MODE means an update is waiting, either for the main program (Software)
or for a third-party media or mirroring app (Media Apps).
