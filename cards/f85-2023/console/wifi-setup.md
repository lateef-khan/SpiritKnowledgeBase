1. In **Settings**, turn **WIFI** on. The system scans for nearby networks automatically.
2. Tap the network name, enter its password, and the system connects using what you typed.
3. On success, pressing the **Training** button returns to the Settings page.

**If it does not connect, the device shows only "Saved"** and stays disconnected. That word is the failure state,
not a success. Re-enter the password.

The WIFI status icon shows **Connected** or **Disconnected**.
