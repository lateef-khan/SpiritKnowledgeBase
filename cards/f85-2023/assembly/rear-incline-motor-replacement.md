**This is the rear motor, the one that gives this machine its decline.** The front incline motor is
a separate part with its own card and its own zeroing length.

1. Unplug **all the rear incline motor wires from the rear incline control board**.
2. Use **two 14mm open-end wrenches** to unmount the rear incline motor.
3. **Adjust the spare incline motor to lower (205mm).**
4. Install the rear incline motor back with a **14mm open-end wrench**.
5. Plug the rear incline motor wires into its control board.

The rear incline control board is also the part the support notes name first when the START button is greyed out
on the touchscreen.
