1. Remove the **motor cover**.
2. Unplug **all** the controller wires.
3. Replace the controller.
4. Plug all the wires back.

**Calibrate the machine after fitting a new controller.** The troubleshooting matrix says so for the speed and
incline mismatch faults that follow an uncalibrated board.
