Work down the list in order.

1. The ON/OFF switch has not been switched on. **Switch the AC switch on.**
2. The safe key is in the wrong position. **Insert the safe key in the right position.**
3. The **12 PIN computer connector** is not plugged in properly. Check the wire and connect it again.
4. The **12 PIN computer cable** is broken. Replace it.
5. The fuse on the controller is blown. Replace the fuse or the controller.
6. The varistor on the controller is blown. Replace the varistor or the controller.
7. The safety device is broken (open). Replace the safety key device.
8. Other components are faulty. Replace the console.
