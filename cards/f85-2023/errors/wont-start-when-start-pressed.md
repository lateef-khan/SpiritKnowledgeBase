1. The motor **M+ or M- wire is not connected into the right position**. Check and plug it again.
2. The motor is broken. Replace the motor, or check the wire and connector for damage.
3. The treadmill controller has shut down and its LED is ON. Turn the ON/OFF switch off and turn the power on
   again.

If the console buttons are dead as a set rather than just START, check the console cabling instead.
