**If there is a problem with the incline, try running the calibration.**

1. **Press the Stop key, the Level key and the Start key at the same time.**
2. **Hold them down for 5 seconds** and the incline calibration starts and runs automatically.
3. If the problem persists, contact the service department.

That is the whole procedure as printed - **no Enter press, no figure to compare against, no
duration and no pass or fail message**, and "the Level key" does not say up or down.

**The 2018 owner's manual for this machine names the same three keys as Start, Level Up and Stop**
(`xe395-2018-errors-incline-calibration-three-key-hold`), so the two documents agree on the hold and
differ only in whether the Level key is called up. The **XE400 and XE500 of 2007** and the
**XE395-2010** calibrate on two keys, Start and Level Up, and then press Enter
(`spirit-xe400-xe500-2007-console-incline-calibration-start-and-level-up-held-five-seconds`,
`xe395-2010-console-engineering-mode-with-an-incline-switch-and-calibration`); the touchscreen
**XE395ENT** has a Calibration button in its Engineer Mode instead
(`xe395ent-2021-console-engineer-mode-factory-setting-machine-type-blename-and-incline-calibration`).
Quote the gesture that belongs to the machine in front of you.

**Where the book sends you here from**: the troubleshooting matrix rows "The incline position
doesn't match console - Console is not calibrated - Calibrate the console" and "INCLINE ERR, INCLINE
window displays Err - Turn off the AC switch and turn on power again. Calibrate the monitor", and
the Err test procedure's step 1, "Run calibration again". Those are held with the error cards.

**The maintenance menu on the same page has no calibration entry** - its Functions list ends in a
Motor Test and a Safety item (`spirit-xe-console-engineering-mode-with-an-lcd-test-and-a-safety-item`).
