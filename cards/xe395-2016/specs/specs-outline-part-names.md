**p. 5, the shrouded machine:** Swing Arm Bushing, Console Assembly, Handle Bar Cover (L) and (R),
Console Mast Cover, Pedal Arm Cover (R), Side Case (L), Round Disk, Round Disk Cover, Pedal (R)
and (L), **Bottom Cover**, Slide Wheel Cover (L) and (R), **Inclinable Rail Cover**, **Incline
Cover**, **Rear Bar Cover**.

**p. 6, the frame:** Swing Arm (L) and (R), Console Mast, Lower Handle Bar (L) and (R), Bushing
Housing, Pedal Arm, Cross Bar, Drive Pulley, Flywheel, Connecting Arm (L) and (R), Adjustable
Pedal (L) and (R), Main Frame, Pedal Arm (L) and (R), **Rail Base Assembly**, Rear Rail Assembly,
**Incline Motor**.

The four cover names in bold on the first drawing are what set this book apart from the XE295's:
they dress the powered rail. The incline motor sits under the rear rail on the second drawing.
The Outlines chapter prints **no dimensions, no machine weight and no user weight limit**, and
no part numbers. The XE395ENT book, which shares this frame, prints a bare drawing with no labels
at all (`xe395ent-2021-specs-no-outline-part-names`).
