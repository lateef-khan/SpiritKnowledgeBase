**The WS200 has no incline.** Its owner's manual prints no incline keys on the Window Display page, no "Incline
Range" line in the *Display and Key Distribution Specs* list, no incline step in *How to use the treadmill*, and no
incline item on the *Parts of your Treadmill* drawing. The WS300 book - the same layout, page for page - prints all
four: Incline Up and Incline Down keys, "Incline Range: 0 - 10%", step 9 "press the Incline Up / Down keys to adjust
the incline level", and part M "Incline".

The only places the word appears in the WS200 book are the electrical page's boilerplate "Route the power cord away
from any moving part of the unit including the elevation mechanism and transport wheels" and the heart-rate essay's
"the machine will automatically adjust speed or incline", both shared with every other Xterra book. Proved from the
native text, the OCR supplements and a fresh extraction of the PDF.
