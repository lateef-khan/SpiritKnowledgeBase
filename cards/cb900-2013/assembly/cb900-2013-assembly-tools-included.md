Three tools, printed as a drawing headed **Assembly Tools**:

- **#87. Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screwdriver**
- **#86. 14/15mm Wrench**
- **#85. 6mm Allen Wrench**

The CB900 is the only indoor cycle in this repository whose carton holds a **separate
6mm Allen wrench** as well as the combination tool. Steps 1 and 2 use **85 and 86
together** - the Allen wrench holds the button head bolt while the 14/15mm wrench turns
the nylon nut on the other side.

**No tool is named for the cleat binding screws.** The pedal page says to use an Allen
key on the tension adjusting screws without giving a size; the 6mm supplied here is
almost certainly too large for them.

## The XIC600 carton, on a similar machine, holds only two

The XIC600 supplies a 14/15mm wrench and a combination Allen/Phillips driver and no
separate Allen wrench. The CIC800 and CIC850 supply Allen wrenches and a 13/17mm
combination wrench instead, which does not fit this machine's 14/15mm nuts.
