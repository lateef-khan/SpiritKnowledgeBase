Numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing item numbers. Quantities are on
[the hardware card](cb900-2013-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Unpacking

1. Cut the straps, then **pull the staples in the outer cardboard away from the layer
   beneath**; lift the box over the unit and unpack.
2. Locate the hardware package. The hardware is separated into **three steps**. Remove
   the tools first, then the hardware for each step as you reach it.

The staple instruction is unique to this book - every later Spirit indoor cycle manual
just says to cut the straps and lift the box.

## Step 1 - rear stabilizer and rear cover

1. Screw two **Casters (24)** onto the **Rear Stabilizer (3)**.
2. Attach the Rear Stabilizer (3) by sliding two washers (74) and two bolts (51) through
   the holes and fastening with two washers (74) and two nuts (69), using **wrenches
   (85 & 86)**.
3. Attach the **Rear Cover (37)** to the frame with two screws (57), using the Allen
   wrench/screwdriver (87).

## Step 2 - front stabilizer

1. Screw two **Casters (24)** onto the **Front Stabilizer (3)**.
2. Attach it exactly as the rear: two washers (74) and two bolts (51) through, two
   washers (74) and two nuts (69) on, wrenches (85 & 86). **There is no front cover.**

**Both stabilizers are printed as item (3).** The manual gives the front and the rear
the same number and never distinguishes them; the CIC800 and XIC600 books number theirs
separately and warn that the two are different. Fit them by shape.

## Step 3 - handlebars, saddle and pedals

1. Attach the **Handle Bar (6)** to the stem with four flat washers (73), four split
   washers (76) and four bolts (53). Tighten with the Allen wrench/screwdriver (87).
2. Secure the **Drink Bottle Holder (47)** to its bracket with two screws (57), using
   the screwdriver (87).
3. Secure the **Saddle (26)** onto the bike with the wrench (86).
4. Slide a **Flat Washer (89)** over the threads of each **Pedal (25L & 25R)**, screw
   the pedals into the crank arms and tighten with the wrench (86). The left pedal is
   left-hand threaded - see
   [the pedal card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-cycle-assembly-pedal-reverse-thread-no-torque-figure.md).

## There is no parts list and no labelled diagram

The pre-assembly page names no carton contents, and the book prints no drawing with part
names on it. The only place a part name is tied to a number is inside the three steps
above.
