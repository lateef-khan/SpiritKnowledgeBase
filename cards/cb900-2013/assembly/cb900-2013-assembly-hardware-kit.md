**Steps 1 and 2 call for identical hardware** except that step 1 adds the two M5 x 10mm
screws for the rear cover; step 2 has no cover and no screws. Screw **57** is used in
two places - the rear cover in step 1 and the drink bottle holder in step 3 - so the kit
holds four of them in total.

**Washer 89 goes under the pedals, not into a joint.** It is the only 14mm washer in the
kit and the only pedal washer in any Spirit indoor cycle manual in this repository.

The four **Casters (24)** the steps fit are not listed in any hardware panel; they arrive
loose with the stabilizers.

## The M5 x 15mm handlebar bolt is not the XIC600 bolt

The XIC600 uses a **5/16"** handlebar bolt - #88 at 15mm in the 2018 printing, #36 at
3/4" in the 2021 printing. The CB900's is **M5**, a much smaller thread, with 5/16"
washers under it.
