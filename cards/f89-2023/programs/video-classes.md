Only the F89 prints these tabs: switch between **Programs, Fitness Tests, Classes, Collections, Templates and Favorites**. The Home screen shortcuts Start, Timer, favorite programs **and video classes**, with "See All" blocks per section.

Class tabs seen in screenshots include **Running** and **Full Sweat** (also Sculpt, Meditation & Stretching, Boxing on the tab strip) with titled sessions such as **Bubblegum Pop Run**. All other program behavior (setup pages, HR, Custom, templates, tests) matches the second-generation touchscreen cards (see links).
