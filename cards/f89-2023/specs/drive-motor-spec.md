**Rating.** A variable speed 0-180 volt DC motor on 230V. On a 120Vac electronic power system it is a
0-90 volts DC motor.

**Wiring.** Three wires: red, black and green.

- DC voltage on the **Red (white) wire (M+)** turns the treadmill motor clockwise.
- DC voltage on the **Black wire (M-)** turns it counterclockwise.
- The higher the voltage, the faster the motor turns.
- The **green wire is ground**.

**As printed.** The manual writes the M+ wire as "Red (white)". Section 9.3 of the same manual says plainly
**Red to M+ and Black to M-** when the motor is refitted. Use the section 9.3 wording.
