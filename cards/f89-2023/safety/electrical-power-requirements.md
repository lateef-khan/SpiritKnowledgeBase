- Install the treadmill on a flat level surface with access to a **220-volt, 10-amp grounded outlet**, with
  only the treadmill plugged into that circuit. The manual adds in brackets: **a 120VAC electronic power system is
  110-volt, 15-amp**.
- Do not use an extension cord unless it is **16 AWG or better with only one outlet on the end**. Do not disable
  the grounded plug with adapters, and do not modify the cord outlet.
- **Never use a ground fault circuit interrupt (GFCI) wall outlet with this treadmill.** As with any appliance
  with a large motor, the GFCI will trip often.
- Route the power cord away from any moving part of the treadmill, including the elevation mechanism and the
  transport wheels.
- To reduce the risk of electric shock, disconnect the treadmill from the outlet before cleaning or service work.

**Which figure applies.** A North American machine is the 120VAC system: **110-volt, 15-amp**. The 220-volt,
10-amp figure is the export machine.
