Seven illustrated assembly steps for the F89-2023. Steps 1-4 and 6-7 follow the F80/F85-2023 shape; step 2 connects two lower cables and step 5 adds the console-top bolts plus a rear shroud (hardware item numbers in brackets).

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill from the carton onto a smooth, level floor with the rear at least 3 ft from any wall. Do not remove the Velcro belt until the wrap and Styrofoam are out; turning it on its side may cause it to fold up.
2. Insert the left upright (5) through Frame Base Cover (L) (61) and the right upright (4) through Frame Base Cover (R) (62). Connect 6P Computer Cable Middle (168) to 7P Computer Cable Lower (169), and 8P Computer Cable Middle (153) to 8P Computer Cable Lower (154). Do not pinch the cables.
3. Insert the right and left uprights (4) and (5) into the frame base (2) and tighten eight Flat Head Socket Bolts (86) with eight Flat Washers (106). Leave loose until step 5 is finished.
4. Connect 6P middle (168) to 6P upper (53-41) and 8P middle (153) to 8P upper (53-42); insert Console Assembly Bottom (53) into the uprights and tighten four Button Head Socket Bolts (139) with four Split Washers (113). Do not pinch the cables.
5. Connect the five pairs (52-10 to 53-13, 52-11 to 53-29, 52-15 to 53-14, 52-20 to 53-41, 52-21 to 53-42), place Console Assembly Top (52) onto the bottom (53), and tighten four Button Head Socket Bolts (121) with four Flat Washers (122). Then fit the Console Rear Shroud (149) with two Phillips Head Screws (127).
6. Place the Tablet Holder (177) on the console top (52) with four Sheet Metal Screws (175).
7. Install Frame Base Cover left (61) and right (62) into the frame base (2) with four Phillips Head Screws (127) and two Phillips Head Screws (74), then pull the locking pin (157) out and insert the End Cap (156) into the hole.
