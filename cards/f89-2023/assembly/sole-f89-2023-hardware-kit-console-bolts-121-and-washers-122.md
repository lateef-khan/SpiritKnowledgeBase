Hardware supplied in the box with the Sole F89-2023, grouped by assembly step. Steps 3, 4, 6 and 7 match the F80/F85-2023 kit; step 5 is F89-only (console-top bolts plus rear-shroud screws). Sizes read off the page rendering.

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | #106 | Dia 3/8" x Dia 19 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 8 pcs |
| 3 | #86 | 3/8" x UNC16 x 2" | Flat Head Socket Bolt | 8 pcs |
| 4 | #113 | Dia 10 x 1.5T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 4 | #139 | 3/8" x 1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #121 | 5/16" x 1-1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #122 | Dia 8.5 x Dia 18 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #127 | M5 x 15 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 2 pcs |
| 6 | #175 | 3.5 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #127 | M5 x 15 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #74 | M5 x 25 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 2 pcs |

Tools in the box: #132 M6 L Allen Wrench, #131 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench. There is no hardware step 1 or 2.
