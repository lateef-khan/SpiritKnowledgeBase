1. Use a screwdriver to remove the **4 Phillips head screws** that secure the console to the console
   support.
2. Unplug all the wires, then replace the console.

Check the computer cable orientation when you plug the new console in. A console cable fitted backwards leaves the
buttons dead even though the machine looks powered.
