1. The chest belt is not worn properly. Check the belt has proper contact with the skin and is oriented
   correctly.
2. The distance is too far and exceeds the range of the receiver. **Use the chest belt in front of the console
   within 3 feet.**
3. The chest belt battery is weak or dead. Replace it with a new **lithium battery, type CR2032**.

**A weak battery is also the answer the matrix gives for a chest belt held too close to the treadmill**: replace
it with a new CR2032 lithium battery. That row is split across a page break in the source and its condition text
is incomplete.
