1. Turn the Passcode feature **ON**, then press **">"** to open the **Enter Old Passcode** page.
2. Enter the correct old passcode. A new passcode can then be created.
3. **Re-enter the new passcode** to confirm it, then return to the Settings page.

If the passcode is entered incorrectly the system prompts **"Wrong passcode. Please try again."**

The manual does not print a default or factory passcode.
