**450 Newton.**

Set it by tightening the **M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88)** on the **J-bolt (87)** with a **13 mm wrench** until the **idler wheel (10)** presses the belt tight and the tension gauge reads 450 N.

**The units in the manual are muddled.** Section 9-7 step 8 says "use belt tension gauge to make sure the tension reads 450N", which is correct. Section 9-14 says "turn M8 x 7T Nyloc Nut (88) clockwise until sound wave frequency falls between 450N", which mixes a frequency reading with a force unit. Take 450 N as the figure.

The LCB and LCR bikes use a frequency figure of 180 to 205 Hz instead. Do not carry that to this machine.
