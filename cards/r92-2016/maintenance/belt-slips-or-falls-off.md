**If the belt slips:**

1. Take both left and right front shrouds apart (29, 30).
2. Use a **13 m/m wrench** to turn the **M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88)** clockwise to **450 N**.
3. Because the machine is belt driven, the rider's weight or the way they ride can make the belt slip. Slippage is rare.
4. The belt is malleable and expendable, so it is normal for it to loosen over time.

**If the belt falls off:** follow section 9-7 step 7 to fit it, then turn the belt forward and backward to test. If it falls off, adjust the **drive belt (54)** **one notch at a time opposite to the direction it fell** and test again. If it still falls off, the machine has probably been dropped and a dimension has moved. Replace the **drive pulley (20)**, the **idler wheel assembly (10)** or the **generator/brake (55)**. If the problem stays, the main frame is deformed and the whole machine needs replacing. That is rare, as every machine is tested before shipping.
