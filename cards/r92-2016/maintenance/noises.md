Most noise comes from **loose screws**. Sometimes a part has deformed or shifted and is rubbing or moving roughly.

1. **Noise from the seat carriage (4)** is normally a loose **seat wheel adjustment plate (9L, 9R)**. Some wobble is normal on a long-used machine. Follow section 9-11 to fix it.
2. **Noise from the footrests (116)(117)** is normally wear. Replace them.
3. **Left and right crank (51L, 51R).** A loose crank makes noise and rough movement. Tightening the crank fixes it.
4. **Noise inside the front shrouds.** Remove both front shrouds to find where the noise comes from. In many cases the **drive pulley (20)** rubs the right shroud. Replace the drive belt pulley (20), or add an extra **5/16" x 16 x 1T flat washer** at the right front shroud (30).
5. If there is still noise with both front shrouds off, find the noise and replace that part.

The manual calls parts 116 and 117 both "pedal" in section 9-3 and "footrest" here. They are the same parts. The closing note calls this an elliptical manual; it is a bike manual.
