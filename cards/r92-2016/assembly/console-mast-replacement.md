1. Take the console apart first.
2. Use the **12/14 m/m wrench (112)** to release **two 5/16" x 5/8" hex head screws (68)**, **two 5/16" x 1.5T split washers** and **two 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T flat washers (76)**. The **handlebar assembly (3)** is then released.
3. Reverse the step to refit the handlebar assembly (3).
4. Separate the **console mast cover (31)** from the **front shrouds L and R (29)(30)** at the seam on each side with a nail or something flat, release the latches that lock them together, and pull the console mast cover (31) up.
5. Unscrew **six 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts (68)**, **four 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T flat washers (76)** and **two 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T curved washers (83)** with the 12 m/m wrench, then pull out the **console mast (2)**.
6. To refit the console mast (2), guide the **computer cable (44)** and the **hand pulse sensor assembly with cable (45)** through the console mast (2) and out of the console securing plate, then tighten **six 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts (68)**, **six 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T flat washers (76)** and **two 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T curved washers (83)** with a 12 m/m open wrench.

Step 5 counts four flat washers and step 6 counts six. The manual does not explain the difference.
