1. Take both left and right front shrouds apart (29)(30).
2. Remove the **steel cable (62)** from the **flywheel (55)**. **Treat the aluminium plate gently. It deforms easily and then scratches and makes noise.**
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **two M5 x 12 m/m Phillips head screws (99)** on the **gear motor (43)** and take it apart.
4. Remove the steel cable (62) from the gear motor (43) end and take it off.
5. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **M5 x 12 m/m Phillips head screw (99)** and take the **reed switch sensor (46)** apart.
6. **Setting the cable range.** When you refit the steel cable (62), use the **console (19)** to raise resistance to the maximum level, then tighten the steel cable with its screw. The aluminium plate sits at its lowest position at maximum resistance. Secure with two **8 mm open end wrenches**.
