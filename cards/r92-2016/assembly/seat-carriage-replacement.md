1. Use two Phillips head screwdrivers to release **two M6 x 15 m/m Phillips head screws (98)** to take the **seat stop axle (11)** apart, then pull the **seat front/aft adjustment lever (167)** upward to take the **seat carriage (4)** apart.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **four M6 x 10L flat Phillips head screws (161)**, **four 7 x 15 x 1.5T flat washers (162)** and **four sleeves (163)**. Then use a **13 m/m wrench** to release **four M8 x 7T nyloc nuts (88)** and **four 8 x 18 x 3T knurled lock washers (79)** and take the **seat wheel adjustment plates (9L, 9R)** apart.
3. Use an **M4 Allen wrench** and a **10 m/m open end wrench** to release the **M5 x 45 m/m socket head cap bolt (171)** and the **M5 x 5T nyloc nut (173)**.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **two M5 x 25 m/m flat head socket screws (169)**, then pull out the **lever anchor (168)** to take the seat front/aft adjustment lever (167) apart.
5. Use an **M5 Allen wrench** and an **11 m/m wrench** to release the **M6 x 38 m/m socket head cap bolt (93)**, the **1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washer (72)** and the **M6 nyloc nut (129)**, then take the **seat position latch (12)** apart.
6. To install the lever (167), join it to the lever anchor (168) with two M5 x 25 m/m flat head socket screws (169). Put the **spring (104)** in the seat position latch (12) and secure with the M5 x 45 m/m socket head cap bolt (171), running it top to bottom through the seat position latch (12), the spring (104), the **15 x 6 x 4T nylon washer (170)**, the lever (167) and the **3/16" x 10 x 1T flat washer (172)**, then tighten with the M5 x 5T nyloc nut (173).
7. Put the finished seat carriage (4) on the **aluminium track (14)**. Adjust the seat wheel adjustment plates (9L, 9R) with **11 and 13 m/m wrenches**: at the front left turn the 11 m/m wrench **counterclockwise** first, then tighten the M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88) with the 13 m/m wrench. At the front right turn the 11 m/m wrench **clockwise**, then tighten the nyloc nut (88). At the rear right turn the hex bolt counterclockwise, and at the rear left clockwise, to secure.
8. Test the smoothness. If it is not acceptable, turn all four hex bolts back and tighten again until it is. Fit the four sleeves (163) on the hex bolts, secure with four 7 x 15 x 1.5T flat washers (162) and four M6 x 10L flat Phillips head screws (161), then install the seat stop axle (11) and the **aluminium axle end cap (135)**.
