1. Take both left and right front shrouds (29)(30) off.
2. Use a **13 mm open end wrench** to loosen the **nyloc nut (88)** on the **J bolt (87)** and remove the J bolt (87).
3. Use a **6 mm Allen wrench** and a **14 mm open end wrench** to release the **3/8" x 3/4" button head socket bolt (81)**, the **3/8" x 7T nyloc nut (89)** and the **3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washer (77)**, then take the **idler wheel assembly (10)** apart.
4. Remove the **drive belt (54)**.
5. Remove the steel cable from the **flywheel (55)** and use a **15 mm open end wrench** to release the **3/8"-UNF26 nut (121)** on the flywheel (55) to remove it.
6. To refit, hang the drive belt (54) on the flywheel (55), then install the flywheel on the **mainframe (1)**. Line the flywheel up with the **pulley (20)** and turn the **nut (120)** outward until it touches the mainframe (1), then use **15 mm and 17 mm** open end wrenches to tighten the nut (120) and the 3/8"-UNF26 nut (121). Tighten the **nut (119)** and the 3/8"-UNF26 nut (121) on the other side.
7. Use the 6 mm Allen wrench and 14 mm open end wrench to tighten the 3/8" x 3/4" button head socket bolt (81), the 3/8" x 7T nyloc nut (89) and the 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washer to secure the idler wheel assembly on the mainframe (1), then turn back **1/4 turn** to release it a little. Install the J-bolt (87) with the **M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88)** onto the idler wheel assembly (10) and the mainframe (1).
8. Return the drive belt (54) to the **drive pulley (20)** and the flywheel (55), then turn the drive pulley to check the belt runs smoothly in the centre. If it is off centre, loosen the 3/8"-UNF26 nut (121) on the flywheel (55) and adjust nuts (119) and (120). Use a **13 mm wrench** to tighten the M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88) on the J-bolt until the **idler wheel (10)** presses the belt tight. Use a belt tension gauge to reach **450 N**. Turn the drive pulley (20) to check the belt is centred and does not come off.
