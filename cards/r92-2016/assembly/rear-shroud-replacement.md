1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **seven 3.5 x 16 self tapping screws (103)** and **two 5 x 16 tapping screws (101)** on the **left rear chain cover (35)**. Unplug the **handpulse wire (26)** and the left rear chain cover is released.
2. On the **right rear chain cover (36)**, unscrew the **3.5 x 16 self tapping screws (103)** with the **3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T flat washers (78)** and **two 5 x 16 tapping screws (101)** to release it.
3. To refit the right rear shroud (36), put it back on the **main frame (1)** and tighten it lightly with a 3.5 x 16 m/m sheet metal screw (103) and a 3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T flat washer (78), then use **two 5 x 16 m/m tapping screws (101)** to secure it on the main frame (1) and lock it with the 3.5 x 16 m/m sheet metal screw (103).
4. Join the left rear shroud (35) and the right rear shroud (36) and secure them with **seven 3.5 x 16 m/m sheet metal screws (103)** and **two 5 x 16 m/m tapping screws (101)**.
