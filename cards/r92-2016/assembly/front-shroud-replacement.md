1. Take the crank arm and the console mast cover apart.
2. To release the **left front chain cover (29)**, use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **seven 3.5 x 16 sheet metal screws (103)** and **two 5 x 16 tapping screws (101)**.
3. To release the **right front chain cover (30)**: on the mainframe unscrew **two 3.5 x 16 m/m sheet metal screws (103)** with **two 3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T flat washers (78)**; on the cover unscrew **two 5 x 16 tapping screws**; finally take the **DC adaptor (48)** cable away from the **AC power switch connector (159)** and remove the right cover (30).
4. To refit the right front chain cover, put it on the mainframe, fit the two 3.5 x 16 m/m sheet metal screws (103) with the two flat washers (78) loosely, then tighten the two 5 x 16 tapping screws (101) on the mainframe and tighten everything.
5. Match the left front chain cover (29) with the right (30) and secure both with **seven 3.5 x 16 m/m sheet metal screws (103)** and then **two 5 x 16 tapping screws (101)**.
6. **Watch the on/off switch (159) when you fit the right front shroud (30).** The switch must be held by both front shrouds (29)(30). Its top and bottom go inside both shrouds, and its left and right sides go outside both shrouds.
