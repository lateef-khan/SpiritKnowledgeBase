**Aluminium track**

1. Remove both rear shrouds (35, 36) and the **seat carriage (4)**.
2. Use a **12 mm open end wrench** to remove **six 5/16" x 3/4" hex head bolts (94)**, **six 5/16" x 16 x 1.5T flat washers** and **six 5/16" x 1.5T split washers (82)** to take the **aluminium track (14)** apart.

**Beam cover**

1. Remove both rear shrouds (35, 36) and both front shrouds (29, 30).
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **four M5 x 12 m/m Phillips head screws (99)** to take the step cover apart.

The section heading calls this the "Aluminum Trail and Stabilizer Cover". The body covers the aluminium track and the beam cover.
