1. Use an open wrench to unscrew **six 3/8" x 3/4" hex head bolts (176)** and **six 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washers (77)** to take the **seat back frame (5)** apart.
2. Use the combination **M5 Allen wrench** to release the **M8 x 15 m/m button head socket bolt (179)** and take the **seatback cushion (63)** apart.
3. Reverse the steps to refit.
