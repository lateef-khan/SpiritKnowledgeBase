Hardware step 1: **6 button head socket bolts (32), 10 flat washers (33), 1 curved washer (34), 11 split washers (35), 4 M5 x 12 Phillips screws (41), 5 socket head cap bolts (112)**.

1. First, connect the hand pulse wire (middle) (108) and hand pulse wire (upper) (94). Then join the rear main frame (104) and front main frame (1) together, using the combination M5 Allen wrench & Phillips screwdriver (68) to tighten **six button head socket bolts (32), six split washers (35) together with six flat washers (33)**.
2. Locate the console mast (2) and slide on the console mast cover (80). **Make sure the cover is facing the correct direction, as shown, before sliding onto the mast.** Install the wiring harness into the bottom of the mast and out the top. Be careful when installing the console mast to the main frame so as not to pinch or cut the wiring harness; damage to the console may occur.
3. Install the console mast on the main frame using the 6 mm Allen wrench (92) to tighten **five socket head cap bolts (112), five split washers (35), four flat washers (33) and one curved washer (34)**. Snap the console mast cover (80) down onto the plastic side covers.
4. Connect the cable (93) to the console assembly (47), then install the console onto the console mast (2) with four Phillips head screws (41), using the Phillips screwdriver (68).

The mast takes five bolts: four with flat washers and one with the curved washer.
