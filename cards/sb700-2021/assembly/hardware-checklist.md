Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #82 | 3/8" | Nut | 4 pcs |
| #81 | 3/8" x 3-1/2" | **Carriage Bolt** | 4 pcs |
| #83 | 3/8" x 23mm | Curved Washer | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #91 | M5 x 10mm | Screw | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 4**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #48 | 5/16" x 16mm | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| #149 | 5/16" x 1" | Socket Head Cap Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #143 | 5/16" x 15L | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #144 | 5/16" x 7T | Nylon Nut | 4 pcs |
| #150 | 5/16" x 1.5T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 6**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #101 | 4 x 16mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 3 pcs |

**Step 1 changed for 2021.** #81 is a **Carriage Bolt** here; on the 2011-2020 SB700 the same #81 is a Button Head Socket Bolt.

**Step 4 was completely re-specified for 2021.** The old #49 split washer and #88 screw are gone. The handlebar now mounts on a Sliding Handlebar Mount with four #149 socket head cap bolts, and #143/#144 are for the two drink bottle holders.

**The printed step 2 heading in the checklist reads "HARDWARE STEP 2J99C0137".** That is what the page actually says - a stray artwork or part code left in the heading. It is not an item number, and no part called 2J99C0137 exists anywhere else in the manual.

**The last block is numbered differently in the two halves of the manual.** The checklist heads it HARDWARE STEP 6; the instruction page heads it HARDWARE STEP 5 and prints it as numbered step 5, TABLET HOLDER.

**Item numbers are not shared between models.** The 2023 machines renumbered the whole pack - there `#93` is a button head socket bolt, while on the 2019-2021 machines `#93` is an M5 tapping screw. Always use the checklist printed in your own machine's manual.
