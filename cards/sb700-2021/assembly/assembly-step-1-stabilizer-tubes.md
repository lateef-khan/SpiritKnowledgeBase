Hardware: **#82** 3/8" Nut (4 pcs), **#81** 3/8" x 3-1/2" **Carriage Bolt** (4 pcs), **#83** 3/8" x 23mm Curved Washer (4 pcs).

1. Attach the front and rear floor stabilizer tubes by inserting **two Carriage Bolts (81) through each tube**. Fasten two Curved Washers (83) and two Nuts (82) on each tube, using the wrench (85) provided.

**The front stabilizer is the one with the wheels mounted in the bottom of the tube.**

**#81 is a Carriage Bolt on this machine.** On the SB700 2011 to 2020 the same item number is a Button Head Socket Bolt of the same 3/8" x 3-1/2" size. The SB900 2021 made the identical change in the same year.
