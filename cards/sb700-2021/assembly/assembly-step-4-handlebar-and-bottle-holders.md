Hardware: **#48** 5/16" x 16mm Flat Washer (4 pcs), **#149** 5/16" x 1" Socket Head Cap Bolt (4 pcs), **#143** 5/16" x 15L Button Head Socket Bolt (4 pcs), **#144** 5/16" x 7T Nylon Nut (4 pcs), **#150** 5/16" x 1.5T Split Washer (4 pcs).

1. Install the Handlebar (6), the Fixing Plate (140) and the Sliding Handlebar Mount (8) with four 5/16" x 1" Socket Head Cap Bolts (149), four 5/16" x 16 x 1T Flat Washers (48) and four 5/16" x 1.5T Split Washers (150). Tighten the bolts securely by using the **Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (86)**.
2. Install the Drink Bottle Holder (L) (141) and the Drink Bottle Holder (R) (142) with four 5/16" x 15L Button Head Socket Bolts (143) and four 5/16" x 7T Nylon Nuts (144), by using the Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (86) and the **13/14m/m Wrench (145)**.

**Completely different from the 2020 machine.** The SB700 2011-2020 fits the handlebars with four #88 screws, four #49 split washers and four #48 flat washers, and its drink bottle holder goes on in the tablet-holder step with two tapping screws. None of #149, #143, #144, #150 or the 13/14mm wrench exists on the earlier bikes.

The SB900 2021 does the same job with #88 (M8 x P1.25 x 25L), #47 and #146 for the handlebar, and the same #143/#144 for the bottle holders.
