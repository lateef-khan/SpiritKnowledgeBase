Nine illustrated assembly steps for the F63-2025. The sequence matches the F63-2023 book step for step; only step 2 differs (3-inch button-head upright bolts with split and flat washers, tightened with the M5 wrench, instead of 3/4-inch hex-head bolts with flat washers and the 13 mm wrench). Read the linked F63-2023 procedure card with this substitution:

2. With help holding the uprights, connect Computer Cable Middle (117) to Computer Cable Lower (118), insert the right and left uprights (4) and (5) into the frame base (2), and tighten eight Button Head Socket Bolts (99) with eight Flat Washers (190) and eight Split Washers (80) using the M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (102). Leave loose until step 6 is finished; do not pinch the cables.

Steps 1 and 3-9 are otherwise identical to the F63-2023 book: unpack onto a smooth, leveled floor (rear 3 ft from any wall); console bottom (22) on four bolts (166) with four washers (188); fan plate (24) with two screws (168); console top (21) on four screws (167); handrail support (9) on four bolts (125), then tighten everything; four mast covers (119-122) with six screws (168); tablet holder (177) with four screws (168) plus the beverage holder (109) onto the support (same reversed-numbers sentence as the 2023 book, same drawing reading); frame base covers (38/39) with four screws (126); locking pin (157) out and end cap (115) in.
