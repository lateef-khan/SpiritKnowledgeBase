Hardware supplied in the box with the Sole F63-2025, grouped by assembly step. This is the 3-inch-bolt printing: the eight upright bolts (#99) are 5/16" x UNC18 x 3" button-head socket bolts used with split washers (#80) and flat washers (#190), unlike the F63-2023 book which prints 3/4" hex-head bolts for the same step (see the linked card). Sizes read off the page rendering; the 3-inch length is printed in both the checklist and the step-2 instruction, so it is not an OCR artifact.

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | #80 | Dia 8 x 1.5T | Split Washer | 8 pcs |
| 2 | #99 | 5/16" x UNC18 x 3" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 pcs |
| 2 | #190 | Dia 8 x Dia 25 x 2.0T | Flat Washer | 8 pcs |
| 3 | #166 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #188 | Dia 10 x 1.5T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 4 | #168 | 3.5 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 2 pcs |
| 5 | #167 | M5 x 30 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| 6 | #125 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #168 | 3.5 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 6 pcs |
| 8 | #168 | 3.5 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |
| 9 | #126 | M5 x 15 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |

Tools in the box: #44 Safety Key, #104 Lubricant, #103 M6 L Allen Wrench, #102 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver (no 13 mm wrench: the upright bolts here are socket bolts, tightened with tool 102).
