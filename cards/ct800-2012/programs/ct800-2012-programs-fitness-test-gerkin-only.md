**This console has one fitness test and it is the Gerkin protocol.** It has no Army, Navy, Air
Force, Marines, PEB or Coast Guard test - those arrive on later CT800 generations, see
`ct800-2016-programs-fitness-test-menu-five-tests` and
`spirit-ct800-programs-fitness-test-menu-seven-tests`.

## Fitness Test programming

1. Press the **Fit-Test** button and press **Enter**.
2. The Message Window asks you to enter your **Age**. The age setting is shown in the **Incline**
   window; adjust with the Up and Down keys, then press Enter.
3. You are now asked to enter your **Weight**. The weight setting is shown in the **Distance**
   window; adjust with the Up and Down keys, then press Enter.
4. Press **Start** to begin the test.

**This console does not ask for gender.** The 2016 and 2020 consoles ask for weight, then age, then
gender - a different order and a third value - see
`spirit-ct800-programs-fitness-test-entry-weight-age-gender`.
