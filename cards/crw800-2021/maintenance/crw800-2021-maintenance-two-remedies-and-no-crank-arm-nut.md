**This machine's page lists two remedies, not three. There is no crank arm nut on it.**

1. **Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each workout.**
2. If a squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling develops, the main cause is most likely one of
   **two** reasons:
   - **The hardware was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.** All bolts installed during
     assembly need to be tightened as much as possible. It may be necessary to use a larger wrench
     than the one provided if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently. The manual is emphatic:
     **"90% of calls to the service department for noise issues can be traced to loose hardware."**
   - If squeaks or other noises persist, **check that the unit is properly leveled**. There are
     **2 leveling pads on the bottom of the rear stabilizer**; use a **14 mm wrench** (or an
     adjustable wrench) to adjust the levelers.

The three figures are the same as everywhere else in the range — **90%, 2 pads, 14 mm** — and here
the lead-in count is right, because two items are printed.

## Its own successor put the crank arm nut back

The **CRW800 2024** owner's manual prints the same page under **MACHINE CARE - CONTINUED** with a
third remedy folded into item 2: *"The crank arm nut needs to be retightened."* Nothing about the
machine changed to justify it — a CRW800 has no crank arm in either printing, and the phrase occurs
nowhere else in either book. The 2024 wording also swaps "I cannot stress this point enough" for
"It is important to note that".

So the sentence arrived on this machine **between the 2021 and 2024 printings**, and it is a
carry-over on both. `ct800-2016-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking` holds the three-remedy version
for the CRW800 2024 and for the rest of the Spirit range.

**Do not send a CRW800 2021 owner looking for a crank arm nut.** Their book does not mention one,
and their machine does not have one.

## What else is on those pages

**SANITIZING YOUR SPIRIT FITNESS EQUIPMENT** follows on printed p. 34 — the standard four bullets,
75% isopropyl alcohol on hard plastics and a conditioner on soft ones (`ct900-sanitizing-equipment`).

**BATTERY CHANGING INSTRUCTIONS** is printed p. 35, and the wireless handle has to be re-coded
afterwards or its Up and Down buttons do nothing
(`crw800-2024-maintenance-battery-changing-instructions`).

**The service manual answers noise differently** — a five-part list with part numbers, from loose screw kits to
the fan and flywheel (`spirit-crw800-maintenance-noise-screw-kits-foot-pads-seat-knob-thread-slide-fan-flywheel`) — and adds a seat that will not slide (`spirit-crw800-maintenance-slide-not-smooth-clean-track-and-pulleys-loosen-three-nyloc-nuts`).

**There is no schedule table in this book.** No daily/weekly/monthly grid, no item list, no
intervals of any kind — the whole maintenance chapter is the two pages above. Its 2024 successor
added a seven-row daily-to-biannual table
(`crw800-2024-maintenance-machine-care-schedule`); do not quote that table for a 2021 machine.
