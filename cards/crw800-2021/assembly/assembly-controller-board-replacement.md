1. Lie the machine down.
2. Use a screwdriver to remove **4 screws (M5 x 10L)**.
3. Unplug the control wire, then replace the controller board.
4. Reverse the steps to install it back.

The board is reached from underneath, which is why the machine goes on its side first. The generator controller's
input and output pins (AC in on 1 and 3, DC out positive on 1 and negative on 3) are on the wiring page of the
same book and belong to the specs cards, not here.

**Only the CRW800 (2020) book prints this.** The CW800-YR001 and XRW600 books have no controller board section.
