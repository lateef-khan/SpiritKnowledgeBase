1. Follow the chain-cover procedure to take off the chain cover.
2. Loosen the **nut (M8 x 7T)** that locks with the **idle wheel bolt**, then remove the **button socket cap screw**
   and take off the generator.
3. Reverse the steps to install the generator.

No screw size for the button socket cap screw is printed. Refitting the generator belt and setting its tension
are steps 8 to 11 of the flywheel-pulley procedure: mount the belt on the generator pulley, insert the idle wheel
screw into the generator fixing plate, lock the generator with an M6 x 1.0 x 18L bolt and a flat washer, and
adjust the idle wheel nut until the belt does not skid.

**Only the CRW800 (2020) book has a generator.** The CW800-YR001 and XRW600 books describe adapter-powered
machines with no generator section.
