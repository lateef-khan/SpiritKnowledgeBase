Use a screwdriver to remove **a screw of the battery cover on the back of the console**, then replace the
batteries. That is the whole printed procedure; no battery type or count is given.

**Only the CRW800 (2020) book prints this.** The CW800-YR001 and XRW600 books have no battery section - those
machines take a 12 V adapter. On this generator-powered machine the console keeps batteries, and the
troubleshooting table's first step for a dark or incomplete LCD is "check the battery".

The battery cover the handle-controller card names (36~3) is a different one, on the handle's controller
assembly, held by one 3.5 x 12L sheet-metal screw.
