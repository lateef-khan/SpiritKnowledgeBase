**Two rows, four columns**, and the frame is Lifetime in both:

| Warranty | Frame | Brake | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial *(Non-dues paying facility)* | Lifetime | 5 Years | 5 Years | 2 Years |
| Residential | Lifetime | 10 Years | 10 Years | 2 Years |

with the footnote *\*Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage.*

**"Commercial" here means a non-dues-paying facility, and the manual says so in the row label itself.** A dues-paying gym or health club is not this row. The exclusion list narrows it the same way, at item 8 - `spirit-rower-warranty-use-environment` - so a club machine has no row to sit in.

**Brake and parts move together and halve in a facility**, from ten years to five. The frame does not move, and labor does not move.

**Labor is two years in both rows**, which is the Spirit commercial figure rather than the residential XRW600's one year: `spirit-ct800-warranty-labor-period`. The SERVICE paragraph two pages later still dates the labor term from twelve months on the bill of sale or eighteen from factory shipping, which does not agree with a two-year column. The manual reconciles nothing. Quote both.

**Do not answer a 2024 CRW800 from this card.** The CRW800 reissued in autumn 2024 is the same nameplate on a redesigned warranty page - the frame drops from Lifetime and the brake column disappears - and its figures are `spirit-2024-warranty-coverage-periods-parts-labor`. This card is the October 2021 predecessor.

**Do not answer a water rower from it either.** The CRW800H2O shares the CRW800 name and almost nothing else on this page: it prints no brake column and its warranty section matches this one at only 86.5% at word level (`crw800h2o-warranty-coverage-periods`).
