Hardware step 1: **2 socket head cap bolts 5/16" x 15 (75), 2 carriage bolts 3/8" x 3" (77), 1 M5 x 15 Phillips screw (82), 2 cap nuts 3/8" (96), 2 flat washers 8.5 x 26 x 2.0T (104), 2 curved washers 3/8" x 23 x 1.5T (112), 4 button head socket bolts 3/8" x 3/4" (143), 4 flat washers 3/8" x 25 x 2.0T (144), 4 split washers 10 x 2T (145)**.

1. Put the front stabilizer (14) on the main frame mounting plate **with the transportation wheels pointing forward** and use the 13/14 mm open-end wrench to secure it with two carriage bolts (77), two curved washers (112) and cap nuts (96).
2. Put the rear rail assembly (2) at the rear of the main frame (be aware of the orientation) and secure it with four button head socket bolts (143), four split washers (145) and four flat washers (144) using the Allen wrench (146).
3. **Adjust the heights of the foot pads at the rear to make sure that the machine is level and stable.** Then use the combination M5 Allen wrench & Phillips screwdriver (118) to secure the middle stabilizer cover (147) with the Phillips head screw (82).
4. Attach the left connecting arm (7) together with the pedal arm (5) to the main frame (1) on the left side crank axle and secure with a socket head cap bolt (75) and flat washer (104) using the 12 mm wrench.
5. Attach the right connecting arm (8) together with the pedal arm (5) to the main frame (1) on the right side crank axle and secure with a socket head cap bolt (75) and flat washer (104) using the L Allen wrench (146).

Item 4 names the 12 mm wrench for the left cap bolt and item 5 the Allen wrench for the right; the bolt is the same part (75) on both sides, so either tool fits both. Levelling is done here, at step 1, on the rear rail's foot pads; the book has no separate levelling or moving page.
