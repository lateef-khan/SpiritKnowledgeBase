Hardware step 2: **6 button head socket bolts 5/16" x 15 (80), 4 M5 x 10 Phillips screws (84), 4 flat washers 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T (105), 6 split washers 5/16" x 1.5T (111), 2 curved washers 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T (113)**.

1. Use the cable tie to guide the 1150 mm computer cable (36) through the console mast (11) and then pull it out of the console holding plate; insert the console mast into the main frame and secure it with six button head socket bolts (80), six split washers (111), four flat washers (105) and two curved washers (113) using the combination M5 Allen wrench & Phillips screwdriver (118).
2. Take off the cable tie that ties the computer cable (36) and plug the connectors of the computer cable and the two handpulse w/cable assemblies (40) into the console. Secure the console on the console assembly (35) with four Phillips head screws (84) using the combination M5 Allen wrench & Phillips screwdriver (118). **Pay attention to avoiding scratching the cables.**

Six bolts on the mast: four with flat washers, two with curved.
