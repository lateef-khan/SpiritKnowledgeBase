Hardware step 3: **2 hex head bolts 5/16" x 15 (74), 2 flat washers 8.7 x 20 x 1.5T (102)**.

1. Install the lower handle bar (L) (9) in the left shaft of the console mast (11) and the lower handle bar (R) (10) in the right shaft, and secure them with two hex head bolts (74) and two flat washers (102) using the 12 mm wrench (116).
2. **Release the rod end shafts (18) which are on the left and right connecting arms.** Connect the connecting arm with the lower handle bar and secure with the rod end shafts (18) and the screw, using the combination M5 Allen wrench & Phillips screwdriver (118) and the 12 mm wrench (116).

The rod end shafts ship fitted to the connecting arms; they are unscrewed, passed through the handle bar bracket and screwed back.
