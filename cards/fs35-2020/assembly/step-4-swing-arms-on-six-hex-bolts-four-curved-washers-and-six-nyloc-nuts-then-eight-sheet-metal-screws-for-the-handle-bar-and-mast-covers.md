Hardware step 4: **6 hex head bolts 5/16" x 1-3/4" (76), 8 sheet metal screws 3.5 x 12 (87), 6 nyloc nuts 5/16" x 7T (91), 4 curved washers 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T (113)**.

1. Connect the swing arm (L) (15) to the left lower handle bar and the swing arm (R) (16) to the right lower handle bar, and secure them with six hex head bolts (76), four curved washers (113) and six nyloc nuts (91) using the 12 mm wrench (116) and 13/14 mm wrench (115).
2. Put the front handle bar cover (L) (69) and the rear handle bar cover (L) (69-1) together on the left handle bar and secure with three sheet metal screws (87) using the combination M5 Allen wrench & Phillips screwdriver (118). Repeat for the right side.
3. Connect the console mast cover (L) (42) and the console mast cover (R) (43) together on the console mast and secure with two sheet metal screws (87).

Three bolts a side, curved washers on two of them. The page is headed "Conecting Arm Assembly" as printed. Last assembly step; no moving or plug-in step is printed.
