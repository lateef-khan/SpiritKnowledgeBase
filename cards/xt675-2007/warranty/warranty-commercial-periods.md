**The XT675 is the only one of the five 2007 XT treadmills that gets a commercial warranty table at all.** It is headed **XT675 (Commercial Warranty)** and sits beside the home table:

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| Frame | Lifetime |
| Motor | 5 Years |
| Parts and Deck | 3 Years |
| Labor | 1 Year |

**Against the home table the motor drops from lifetime to 5 years and the deck from lifetime to 3, and the ten-year "all other components" row disappears** - parts are 3 years in a facility (`spirit-xt-2007-warranty-home-periods-xt375-xt475-xt675`). Frame and labour are unchanged. The page defines neither "commercial" nor any hours-per-day limit; the later XT685 books do (`xt685-2023-light-commercial-warranty-periods`), and their light-commercial figures happen to be the same five - frame lifetime, motor 5, deck 3, parts 3, labour 1.

The base term under both tables - parts 5 years from retail sale or 18 months from factory shipping, whichever comes first - is on `spirit-xt-2007-warranty-five-years-or-eighteen-months-from-shipping`. The dealer policy page adds that **residential units in a commercial setting** are not covered at all (`spirit-xt-2007-warranty-what-the-dealer-policy-does-not-cover`).
