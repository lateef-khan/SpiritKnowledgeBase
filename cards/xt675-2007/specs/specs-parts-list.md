These are the callout numbers on the **XT675 Exploded View** on PDF page 34, a drawing printed sideways on a portrait page and stamped **960727PM0830** in small type at its edge. The drawing is a picture with bare numbers on it, so this table is the only readable form of it. It was read from the native text layer of the 2008 dealer service manual; every part number on the pages the 300 dpi OCR sweep supplemented agrees with that layer, and pages 18 and 26 were also read from a render.

**Three columns, no quantity.** The list prints `No.`, `Description` and `Spirit Part #` only - it never says how many of an item the machine uses. A part number is a six-digit Spirit number. **`X` in the part-number column is printed on the page**; the book does not say what it means, and 2 rows carry it (37~27, 138). 9 numbered rows carry no part number at all (1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 98, 114, 115, 118).

**145 rows are printed.** The whole item numbers that appear are 1-14, 17-56, 58-98, 100-119, 121-124, 126, 137-141, 162. Any number outside that run - 15-16, 57, 99, 120, 125, 127-136, 142-161 - is not printed for this machine at all, not a missing page. Sub-items are written with a tilde (`37~1`, `37~2`); paired parts carry a letter (`8A`, `8B`).

**These numbers belong to the 2007 model year only.** The XT185, XT285, XT385, XT485 and XT685 of 2015 have no printed parts list anywhere (`xt-2015-specs-no-parts-list`), and the 2023 XT lists are numbered per manual with no Spirit part-number column - the XT185-2023 Drive Motor is item 027, the XT385/XT485-2023 Drive Motor is 34. Nothing establishes that any number here fits a later machine, or that a later item number fits this one.

**This list shares no numbering with the other four.** The XT675 is the non-folding machine and its drawing is stamped 960727PM0830 on its own; item 12 here is the running deck, as it is on the XT175, but the rollers sit at 10-11 rather than 15-16 (XT175, XT275) or 21-22 (XT375, XT475), and the drive belt is item 36 rather than 14 or 20. Read a number only against this list.

**Rows that repeat or look wrong on the page, kept as printed.** Items 81 and 137 are both `3x8 m/m_Self Tapping Screw 007125`. Items 87 and 92 are both a 5x16 mm tapping screw, but 87 is `010110` and 92 is `010150`. Item 98 `3/8" _Nut`, item 118 `5T_Nyloc Nut` and the two front-handlebar foams at 114 and 115 carry no part number. Item 56 is printed `%25x50_Square End Cap` on the page.

**Tools are items in the list.** Item 111 is the combination M5 Allen wrench and Phillips screwdriver (000089 - a different number from the 000072 the other four lists give the same tool), item 112 an Allen wrench (022973), item 113 a 12 mm wrench (000342) and item 18 the lubricant (022271). They are drawn beside the machine on the exploded view.
