Press and hold **Start, Stop and Enter together for about 5 seconds**, until the display shows "Engineering Mode". Press Enter to access the menu. Use the Level UP/DOWN keys to navigate.

- **A. KEY TEST** - test all the keys to make sure they are functioning.
- **B. UNIT MODE** - English or Metric display readings.
- **C. MANUAL TEST** - allows stepping of the gear motor.
- **D. DISPLAY MODE** - turn off to have the console power down automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity.
- **E. PAUSE MODE** - turned on to allow 5 minutes of pause.
- **F. KEY TONE** - turn on or off the beep sound when a key is pressed.
- **G. CHILD LOCK** - locks the keypad. When the child lock is enabled the keypad will not operate unless you press and hold Start and Enter for 3 seconds.

**This is a different menu from the E25, E35 and E55 of the same year**, which print a lettered A to F menu with a nested Functions submenu holding ODO Reset, Units, Display Mode, Motor Test, Manual, Pause Mode and Key Tone, plus Security and Factory Set. **This menu has no ODO Reset and no Factory Set entry.**
