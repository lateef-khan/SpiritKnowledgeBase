The manual's cover code is **XE895A-YE022**. Section 1 is titled "XE895A-YE022 Outlines". The table of contents heads section 1 simply "Elliptical Outlines" with no model name.

This is the Sole E95S of model year 2016. The Spirit database SKU taken for it is **595616**.

**This machine adjusts stride length, not incline.** It has a console with a **10.1" TFT display**, a main controller (DC power supply, stride driver and tension motor driver), a **tension motor** that changes brake resistance through a steel cable, and a **115 volt AC stride motor**. The general information page calls it the STRIDE MOTOR; the LED and disassembly sections head the same part "Incline Motor".

The parts drawings name: handle bar cover (R), console assembly, console mast covers L and R, side cases L and R, left and right slider covers, side case rear shroud, connecting arm covers (R)(A) and (R)(B), pedals L and R, rear stabilizer cover, slide wheel cover; seat handle bar (L), console mast, swing arm (R), lower handle bars L and R, moving range adjusting assembly, flywheel, main frame, connecting arms L and R, left and right pedal bases, pedal arms L and R, rear rail assembly, adjusting lever, swing assembly, bushing housing (pedal arm), crank arm assembly, incline motor, slider adjustment (L), wheel base, left and right driving assemblies, gear motor, joint assemblies and connecting component.
