1. Remove the seat handle bar and lower handle mast assembly following 11-1.
2. Remove **two 4 x 15L sheet metal screws** from the console mast cover with a Phillips head screwdriver.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **eight 4 x 15L sheet metal screws** and remove the left chain cover.
4. Unscrew side case (R) from the main frame with a Phillips head screwdriver.
5. Remove the **5 x 19L tapping screw** on side case (L) of the main frame with a Phillips head screwdriver.
6. **Remember the position of the AC input module and the colour of the cables.** Remove the two connecting wires and the ground wire, then side case (L) can be removed.
7. Remove the **5 x 19L tapping screw** on the side case rear shroud of the main frame with a Phillips head screwdriver.
8. Reassemble following the above steps. Do not pinch the cables between side cases (R) and (L) and the side case rear shroud, then lock the screws.
