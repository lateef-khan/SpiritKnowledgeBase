1. Remove side cases (L) and (R) and pedal arms (L) and (R) following the disassembly steps.
2. Pull out the **17 x 34L rotate axle A** from the inside. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** from the outside of rotate axle A for the slider adjustment (L) and (R) of the joint assembly, using a **12 m/m wrench**.
3. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** from the crank arm assembly for the bushing housing, pedal arm, using a 12 m/m wrench.
4. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **8.5 x 26 x 2.0T flat washer** from the rear rail assembly for the joint assembly, using a 12 m/m wrench.
5. To remove the **J4FM-1719-09 bushing** from the joint assembly, use a bar smaller than 17 and drive the bushing out.
6. Use a **#8 hex wrench and a #14 wrench** to remove the **gap socket screw 3/8" x 2-1/4"**, **flat washer 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T** and **nyloc nut 3/8" x 11T** connecting the inclinable rail assembly and rotating block, to remove the inclinable rail assembly.
7. To remove the **WFM-2528-21 bushing** from the bushing housing, pedal arm, use a bar smaller than 25 and drive it out.
8. To remove the **WFM-1719-12 bushing** from the rear rail assembly, use a bar smaller than 17 and drive it out.
9. Remove **two M5 x 15L Phillips head screws** from the slide wheel cover when taking the **78 slide wheel, urethane** apart.
10. Remove the **17 C ring** and **17 x 0.5T wave washer** from pedal arms (L) and (R) at the 78 slide wheel, urethane.
11. Reverse the above procedures to refit the pedal arm.
12. When assembling the 78 slide wheel, urethane, follow the order **17 x 0.5T wave washer, 78 slide wheel urethane, 17 x 0.5T wave washer, 17 C ring**.
13. When assembling the joint assembly and adjusting lever, **line up the wheel base with the surface of the 17 x 34L rotate axle A** and lock with the screw.
14. **Lock the bushing housing, pedal arm onto pedal arms (L) and (R) using a torque wrench up to 550 lb.**

Step 6 names an inclinable rail assembly, a part this manual's own parts drawings do not list for this machine.
