1. Remove the left and right pedal bases following the disassembly steps. Separate the screws of the **M12 x P1.75 rod end bearing** from lower handle bars (L) and (R) and pedal arms (L) and (R).
2. Remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** from pedal arms (L) and (R) and the rear rail assembly with a **12 m/m wrench**.
3. Pull the **pedal axle (17 x 95L)** out from inside pedal arms (L) and (R).
4. Remove **six M5 x 6L Phillips head screws** from the frame cover and brush with a Phillips head screwdriver.
5. Remove the **M5 x 6L Phillips head screws** from the aluminium axle end cap on pedal arms (L) and (R).
6. Remove the **three 5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolts** from the aluminium track on pedal arms (L) and (R) using an **M5 L Allen wrench**; the track can then be taken off.
7. Release the nut from the **M12 x P1.75 rod end bearing** with a **19 m/m wrench**.
8. Reassemble following the above steps.
9. **Line up the pedal axle with the surface of the breach of the fixing plate and lock with the screw.**

Step 6 is printed twice in the source, once in Chinese and once in English; the two agree.
