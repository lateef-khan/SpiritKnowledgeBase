1. Remove the joint assembly following the slider adjustment disassembly steps.
2. Use a **no. 12 wrench** to remove the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** from the **B 178 x 41L rotate axle** fixed on the swing assembly on the main frame.
3. Pull out the B 178 x 41L rotate axle.
4. Use a **12 m/m wrench** to release the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **8.5 x 26 x 2.0T flat washer**, and remove the swing assembly and slider adjustment fixed at the adjusting lever on the moving range adjusting assembly.
5. Use a 12 m/m wrench to release the **5/16" x 1/2" hex head bolt** and **8.5 x 26 x 2.0T flat washer** fixed at the adjusting lever on the slider adjustment, then remove the adjusting lever.
6. Use a round bar smaller than 17 to knock the **J4FM-1719-09 bushing** out of the adjusting lever.
7. Use a **13 m/m wrench** to release the **M8 x 6.3T nut** fixed on the wheel base, and release the **M8 x 20L socket head cap bolt**. The swing assembly can then be pulled out.
8. Use a round bar smaller than 17 to knock the J4FM-1719-09 bushing out of the swing assembly.
9. Use an **M5 Allen wrench and no. 13 m/m wrench** to remove the **5/16" x 1-3/4" button head socket bolt** and **5/16" x 7T nyloc nut** fixed on the **40 adjustment transportation wheel** on the slider adjustment, then remove the wheel and wheel base.
10. Reverse the procedures to return the parts.
11. When refitting the swing assembly to the main frame, the **B 178 x 41L rotate axle must be aligned with the flat portion of the notch on the U-seat** before the screw is locked.
12. When locking the 40 adjustment transportation wheel back, the **5/16" x 7T nyloc nut must be in the same position as the crank locked in the slider adjustment axle**.
13. When every part is assembled, **use a torque wrench to lock the M8 x 20L socket head cap bolt to 150 lbs** on the wheel base, lock the M8 x 6.3T nut tight, and cover the slider cover back on the slider adjustment.
