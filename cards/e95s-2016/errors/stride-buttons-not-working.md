The matrix groups three symptoms into one block: the UP/DOWN button of the STRIDE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH cannot be used; the stride button can only be pressed UP, not DOWN; the stride button can only be pressed DOWN, not UP.

Reasons and fixes as printed:

1. The connector of STRIDE CABLE and CONSOLE not connected properly. — Connect the wires again.
2. The connector of STRIDE CABLE and STRIDE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/CABLE not connected properly. — Connect the wires again.
3. The connector of STRIDE CABLE or STRIDE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH CABLE got damage. — Replace the cable.
4. Button of STRIDE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH is broken. — Replace buttons.
5. The connector of STRIDE CABLE or STRIDE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH CABLE got damage. — Replace the cable.
6. The connector of STRIDE CABLE or STRIDE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH CABLE damaged. — Replace the cable.

Reasons 3, 5 and 6 are printed as three near-identical rows in the source. They are reproduced as printed.

If the display also shows E3, work the E3 card instead.
