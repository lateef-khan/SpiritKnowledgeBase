The matrix prints two rows for this symptom.

**LCDs not bright, incomplete or imperfect**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1. LCD light is broken. | 1. Replace with new LCD or console. |
| 2. Power to console too low. | 2. Check AC power is 110-120V (or 220-230V). 3. Check power to console. 4. Replace lower controller. |

**LCD displays not bright, incomplete or imperfect**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1. LCD displays are broken. | 1. Replace with new console. |

Note the voltage: this manual is the only one in the 2016 Sole elliptical family that adds the 220-230V alternative to this row.
