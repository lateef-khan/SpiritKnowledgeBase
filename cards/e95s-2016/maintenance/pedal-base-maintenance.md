The left and right pedal bases and pedal arms are described as **the most important parts of this model**. Pay attention to their maintenance.

1. If there are noises, check whether something is rubbing or has dropped in, then check the **aluminium track**. If the track is damaged, replace it as soon as possible.
2. Check the **38 slide wheel, urethane**. If it is damaged, replace it directly.
3. Clean the aluminium track and the 38 slide wheel, urethane, and add some lubricant.
4. If it is loose, use an **11 m/m wrench** to adjust the 38 slide wheel, urethane on the adjustment wheel fixing plate until it is appropriately tight.
