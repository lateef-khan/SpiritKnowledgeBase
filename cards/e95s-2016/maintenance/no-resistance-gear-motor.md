If there is no resistance, first check whether the console is functioning normally. Next check the **gear motor cable**; if it is worn or damaged, remove the **steel cable**. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **tapping screw 5x19** securing the gear motor cable and replace the cable with a new one.

Section 9-4 adds the mechanical half: when the console adjusts the resistance and nothing happens while the gear motor is working, **check whether the steel cable is engaged with the flywheel or has gone loose**.

If the display also shows E2, work the E2 card instead.
