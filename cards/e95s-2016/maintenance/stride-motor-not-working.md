1. The incline motor, controller and gear motor are all electronic components. **Do not disassemble them if you are unfamiliar with these parts.**
2. When the motor is not functioning, check all the wires for a secure connection, and check whether the incline controller and power adaptor are damaged or broken. Replace damaged or broken parts.
3. **To refit the motor, make sure it has been reset.** Turn the tubing **clockwise to the end**, then turn the same tubing **counterclockwise two and a half circles**, or set the centre distance between the two holes to **245 +/- 1 mm**.
