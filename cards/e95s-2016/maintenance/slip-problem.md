1. **The CAP socket head cap bolt 3/8" x 2-1/4" on top and the nyloc nut 3/8" x 11T must be tightened securely.**
2. **Belt** - this appears quite often, because after a period of time most belts become loose from use. The solution depends on weight and adjustment method; use the belt disassembly procedure.

**This manual lists only two situations, and prints no pounds-force figure here.** The E35, E55, E95 and E98 manuals of the same year list four and suggest 500~600 lbs at the bushing housing and 550~600 lbs at the cross bar.

The disassembly sections do print two torque figures for this machine: **lock the bushing housing, pedal arm onto the pedal arm with a torque wrench up to 550 lb** (section 11-5 step 14) and **lock the M8 x 20L socket head cap bolt on the wheel base to 150 lbs** (section 11-6 step 13).
