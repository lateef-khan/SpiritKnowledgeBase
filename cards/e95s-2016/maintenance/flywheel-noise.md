The manual describes two kinds of flywheel problem: **adjusted resistance does not work**, and **noise**.

1. When the user adjusts the resistance from the console and nothing happens while the gear motor is working, **check whether the steel cable works with the flywheel or is loose**.
2. Check whether the flywheel, the **330 drive pulley** and the idler wheel assembly are rubbing while the flywheel spins and causing the noise. If so, follow the flywheel disassembly steps and reset it. If the flywheel itself is causing the noise, the manual suggests replacing it directly.
