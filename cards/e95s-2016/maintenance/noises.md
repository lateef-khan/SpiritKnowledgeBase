There are many places noise can come from and it is not easy to isolate one. At the front the manual lists the chain cover and the round disk, EPE, round disk, sleeve, cross bar, belt and belt pulley, idler wheel plate, swing arm, swing arm axle, flywheel, steel cable, slide wheel and crank arm during assembly, along with the side inclinable rail assemblies and the pedals.

The main reasons are usually **not enough lubrication, unsmooth rotations, and loose screws**.

Because the product is built on a linked design, resolving a noise issue varies from machine to machine depending on the actual situation.

The list is carried over from the fixed-stride machines, so it names a cross bar, a round disk and inclinable rail assemblies. This machine has pedal bases on an aluminium track and a slider adjustment instead.
