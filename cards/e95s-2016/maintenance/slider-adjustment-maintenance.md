Slider adjustment is described as **the most important part of stride adjustment**.

1. Check whether the **40 adjustment transportation wheel** is damaged. If it is, replace it following the disassembly steps.
2. Check whether the **swing assembly (#16)** is defaced on the surface. Wipe it and apply some lubricant.
3. If there is space between two parts, adjust the **wheel base** following the disassembly steps, and adjust it to the standard scales.
