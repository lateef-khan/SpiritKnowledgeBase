**Four deck screws with an Allen key, under a six-screw top step cover.** The XT285, XT385 and XT485 2015
books take eight tapping screws out of the deck with a Phillips screwdriver and six out of the foot rail;
those counts are not this machine's.

The heading adds "(Please take the cylinder before replacement)".

1. After the running board has been folded, use an **M5 L Allen wrench** and a **12mm wrench** to remove the
   securing screws on the cylinder, and remove the cylinder.
2. Perform step 12.6 (the roller procedure) and remove the front and rear rollers.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to loosen **6 screws**, and remove the **top step cover** in the direction of
   the indicating arrows.
4. Use a "Combination M5 Allen Wrench" to remove the **4 screws** securing the running board. Remove the running
   board and replace the board or the running belt. If the cushions need replacing, remove the **6 cushions** and
   replace them. Reassemble in the reverse order.
5. When reassembled, centre and adjust the running belt tension. The drive belt tension also needs adjusting -
   "refer to step 12.3-5", the motor procedure's 70-75 lbs step.

No torque is printed. The cylinder is the gas spring that holds the folded deck up; the sibling books call
it a gas spring in their heading and a cylinder in their steps.
