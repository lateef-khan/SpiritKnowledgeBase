**This book takes the eight mast-cover screws out with an M5 L Allen wrench. Its three 2015 siblings take the
same eight screws out with a Phillips screwdriver.** That tool is the only thing that separates this card from
theirs; the counts are the same.

1. Use an **M5 L Allen wrench** to loosen the **8 Sheet Metal Screws**, then remove the console mast cover.
2. Use an L Allen wrench to remove the **4 button head socket bolts** from the console support.
3. Disconnect the console wirings and replace with the new console.

The Allen size for the four bolts is not printed. No torque is printed, and the procedure does not say to
unplug the machine first.
