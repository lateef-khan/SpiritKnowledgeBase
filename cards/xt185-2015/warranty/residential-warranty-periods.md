**The XT185 is the only Spirit XT treadmill whose parts period is 5 Years.** Every other XT treadmill in this knowledge base, 2015 and 2023 alike, is warranted 10 Years on parts.

| Warranty | Frame | Motor | Deck | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | Lifetime | Lifetime | Lifetime | **5 Years** | 1 Year |

Printed above the table: **Treadmill Warranty - Effective March 13, 2021**.

Frame, motor and deck are Lifetime and labor is 1 Year, exactly as on the other machines. **Parts is the only figure that differs, and it is half.** `xt-2023-warranty-residential-coverage-periods` holds the 10-Year row shared by the XT285, XT385, XT485 and XT685 of both generations; quoting it for an XT185 of this generation would promise the customer five years the manual does not give.

This manual prints **no light commercial table**. Its responsibilities paragraph nevertheless says the warranty applies to "ordinary household **or Light Commercial** use (see restrictions above)", with no restrictions and no periods printed anywhere to support it - see `xt-2015-warranty-use-environment`.

Two things this table does not tell you on its own. When the clock starts is stated in the paragraph above it (`xt-2023-warranty-start-date`), and the one-year labor figure is dated separately in the SERVICE paragraph on the next page (`xt-2023-warranty-labor-period`).
