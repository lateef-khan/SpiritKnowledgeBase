**The XT185 2015 service manual and the XT185 2015 owner's manual print two different schedules for the same deck. This card is the service manual's. The owner's manual's 180-hour figure is `xt-2015-maintenance-lubrication-interval-180-hours`.**

IMPORTANT: ONLY USE HALF THE BOTTLE OF LUBRICANT PER APPLICATION.

Lubrication schedule:

1. **After the first 25 hours of use (2-3 months)**, apply one half bottle of lubricant.
2. **Every 50 hours of use (5-8 months)**, apply one half bottle of lubricant.

Between scheduled applications the book's own check applies: lift one side of the belt and feel the top of the treadboard; if it is dry to the touch, lubricate — `xt185-2015-maintenance-deck-lubrication-half-bottle-procedure`.

## Three schedules for one machine

| Where | Interval | Quantity |
|---|---|---|
| XT185 2015 service manual (this card) | first 25 h, then every 50 h | half a bottle |
| XT185 2015 owner's manual | every 180 h, hand check every other month | two S-pattern lines, 1/8" x 15" |
| XT285, XT385, XT485 2015 and XT485ENT service manuals | every 90 h, hand check every other month | two S-pattern lines |

None of these books mentions the others, and the service manual says nothing about the console's "Lube" reminder, which on the XT185 2015 is set to 180 hours by the owner's manual. A customer told "every 50 hours" by a technician and "every 180 hours" by the book in the box is reading two Spirit documents, not misreading one. The safe reading is the shorter interval with the hand check: the deck is never wrong to be lubricated when it feels dry.

## The same schedule is printed for a Sole machine

The Sole F60 2016 and 2020 owner's manuals print this 25-hour, then 50-hour, half-bottle schedule word for word (`f60-2016-belt-deck-lubrication`). It is Dyaco text shared across brands; it is carded there for Sole and here for the XT185 2015, and a Sole card must never be answered for a Spirit machine or the reverse.
