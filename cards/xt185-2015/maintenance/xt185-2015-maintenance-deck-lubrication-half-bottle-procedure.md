**This is the service manual's procedure. The owner's manual for the same machine prints a different one — two 1/8" x 15" lines in an S pattern, 18" from the motor cover, then five minutes' walking — at `xt-2015-maintenance-deck-lubrication-procedure`.**

Your treadmill should require little maintenance other than periodically applying lubricant. Lubricating under the treadbelt ensures superior performance and extends its life expectancy.

## How to check the treadbelt for proper lubrication

Lift one side of the treadbelt and feel the top surface of the treadboard.

- If the surface is **slick to the touch**, no further lubrication is required.
- If the surface is **dry to the touch**, apply **one packet of lubricant or half of the bottle** of lubricant.

## How to apply lubricant

1. Lift one side of the treadbelt.
2. **Pour one half of the lubricant bottle under the centre of the treadbelt**, on the top surface of the treadboard.
3. **Walk on the treadmill at a slow speed for 3 to 5 minutes** to distribute the lubricant evenly.

NOTE: **Do not over-lubricate the treadboard.** Any excess lubricant that comes out should be wiped off.

IMPORTANT: **Only use half the bottle of lubricant per application.**

The schedule this goes with — first 25 hours, then every 50 — is `xt185-2015-maintenance-lubrication-schedule-25-then-50-hours`. The book does not say to unplug the machine first, does not say to clean under the belt first, and names no lubricant; the owner's manual does all three (`xt-2015-maintenance-cleaning-between-belt-and-deck`, `xt-2015-maintenance-approved-lubricant`).

## Only the XT185 book prints this version

The XT285, XT385 and XT485 2015 service manuals and the XT485ENT service manual print the owner's-manual procedure instead, with its S-pattern lines and its clean-first step. The XT185 2015 service manual is the odd one out, and it is the one book that also prints the 25/50-hour schedule.

## The same procedure is printed for a Sole machine

The Sole F60 2016 and 2020 owner's manuals print the slick-or-dry check and the half-bottle procedure in the same words (`f60-2016-belt-deck-lubrication`). Same Dyaco text, two brands, two cards.
