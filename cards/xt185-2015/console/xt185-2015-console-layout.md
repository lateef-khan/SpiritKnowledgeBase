The console overview diagram labels these parts, and nothing else:

- **Large LCD with scrolling feedback and scrolling message center**
- **Swivel Fan to keep you cool**
- A bank of **program keys** ("Six programs offer a variety of work-outs")
- **Select Control Speed & Incline** keys
- **Easy-Touch Control Keys**

**This console has no speakers and no audio jack, no muscle activation figure and no heart rate
percentage bar graph.** The diagram labels none of them and the manual describes none of them. The
XT285-2015 adds speakers and an audio jack; the XT385-2015, XT485-2015 and XT685-2015 add a muscle
activation figure and a heart rate bar graph as well.

**The manual describes no handrail speed or incline controls and no handrail disable switch**,
although its Quick Start steps do mention hand rail keys - see `xt-2015-console-quick-start`.

**This is the 2015 panel, not the 2023 one.** The 2023 XT185 console is labelled with a USB
charging port and a dedicated Child Lock button, and it has no Select keys - see
`xt185-2023-console-layout`.
