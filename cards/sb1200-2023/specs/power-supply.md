When power is connected the console powers up automatically.

- The machine runs on **DC 12 V / 3.3 A**, supplied by the power pack.
- The power pack connects to **115 VAC**.
- The **power socket is on the front of the bike**, where the line cord plugs in.

**The manual gives a second, wider figure elsewhere.** The troubleshooting matrix on page 19 says to "Check AC power is 110-120V" when the screen is dim or incomplete. 115 VAC sits inside that band. The manual has no separate safety or grounding section.
