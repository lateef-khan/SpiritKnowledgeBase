**180 lbs.** That is the only figure the manual gives, and it is stated once, in the idler wheel refit step.

To set it, use a **6 mm hex key** on the **hex screw M8 x 80L (65)**. **Clockwise increases** the belt tension, **counterclockwise loosens** it.

**The unit does not match any other Sole bike manual.** Every other Sole indoor cycle states belt tension as a force in Newtons or as a frequency in Hertz:

| Machine | Printed figure |
|---|---|
| SB1200 2023, SB900 2023 | 180 lbs |
| SB700 2019 | 700 to 750 N |
| SB700 2020 | 700 to 750 N, and 1000 N in the belt slippage answer |
| SB900 2020 | 1000 N |
| B94 and R92 | 450 N |
| LCB and LCR | 180 to 205 Hz |

180 lbs is about 800 N, which sits between the SB700 and SB900 2020 figures, but the manual does not say the two are equivalent and gives no gauge type. **Confirm the unit with the technical department before setting a belt to it.** The SB900 2023 manual prints the identical sentence.
