1. Remove the **console rotary shaft cover (122)** and the **console assembly (26)**. Use a **6 mm hex key** to unscrew the **six M8 x P1.25 x 15L hex screws (96)** in the socket below the **handlebar assembly (4)**. The handlebar assembly can then be taken apart. Pull the **screen holder assembly (7)** forward to detach it.
2. To remove the **screen rack (121)**, use a **6 mm hex key** and a **17 mm ratchet wrench**. First unscrew the **M10 x 20L hex screw (68)** inside the umbrella head with the hex key. Remove the **plastic washer Ø10 x Ø24 x 3T (110)** and the **hex nut M10 x 8T (111)**. The screen rack (121) can then be taken apart.
   Next, use the 17 mm ratchet wrench to unscrew the **upper hex nut M10 x 8T (111)**. Remove the **flat washer Ø10 x Ø25 x 1.5T (107)** by taking out the hex nut. Use a **4 mm hex key** to unscrew the **set screw M8 x P1.25 x 6L (115)**. Then use a **5 mm hex key** to unscrew the **set screw M10 x P1.5 x 8 (116)** and take out the **spring (117)** and the **steel ball (118)**. The **screen rotation assembly (120)** can then be lifted upward and removed.
3. Loosen and remove the fixed bracket of the **quick release lever (39)**. Remove **2 pieces of aluminium blocks (40)** and the **brake spring (41)**. Pull up the **handlebar post (5)** along with the **hollow sleeve (42)**. Use a **4 mm hex key** to remove the **hexagon screw M5 x 10L** inside the socket and take out the hollow sleeve (42).
4. Reassemble in the reverse order.

**Two hex nuts M10 x 8T (111) are removed in step 2**, one before the screen rack comes off and one described as the upper nut. The manual does not distinguish them further.
