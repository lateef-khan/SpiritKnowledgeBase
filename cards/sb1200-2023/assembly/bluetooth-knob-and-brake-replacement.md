1. Remove the **flywheel guard (49)** and the **water bottle holder (50)**. Use a **10 mm ratchet wrench** to remove the **cap nut M6 x 6T (83)**.
2. Use a **29 mm open-end wrench** to turn the **hex nut adjuster (21) counterclockwise** so the adjustment assembly lifts upward. Use a **2 mm hex key** to remove the **set screw M4 x P0.7 x 12L (78)**, so the adjustment assembly and the **brake inner gear adjuster (22)** can be turned apart. Then use a **2.5 mm hex key** to remove the **two set screws M5 x 5 (104)** on either side of the **adjuster knob (105)** and the **push rod axle (24)**, turning them counterclockwise.
3. Use a **13 mm ratchet wrench** to loosen and remove the **outer hex screw M8 x 20L (85)**, the **spring washer Ø8 x 1.5T (86)** and the **flat washer Ø5/16" x 18 x 1.5T (87)** that secure the magnetic control brake assembly on both ends. Pull the magnetic control brake assembly **forward** to remove it.
4. Reverse the steps to reinstall. **When reinstalling, adjust the spacing between the magnetic control brake assembly's magnet and the flywheel so they are centred.**

The manual does not give a gap dimension for step 4, only "centred".
