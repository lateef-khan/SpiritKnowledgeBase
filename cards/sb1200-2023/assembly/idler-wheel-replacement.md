1. Remove the **right crank (32)** and the **right chain cover (47)**. Take the **belt (37)** off the **belt pulley (30)**.
2. Use a **5 mm hex key** to remove the **umbrella head hex screw M8 x P1.25 x 15L (66)** and the **anti-slip washer Ø8 x Ø18 x 3T (72)**. Then remove the **umbrella head hex screw M8 x P1.25 x 10L (70)** and the **flat washer 5/16" x 23 x 2T (73)**. Finally remove the **umbrella head hex screw M10 x P1.5 x 15L (67)** to detach the idler wheel assembly.
3. Reverse the steps to reinstall. **Make sure the belt tension is at 180 lbs.** If it is insufficient, use a **6 mm hex key** to turn the **hex screw M8 x 80L (65) clockwise** to increase the tension. Turning it counterclockwise loosens the tension.

The belt tension figure and its unit are covered in a separate card.
