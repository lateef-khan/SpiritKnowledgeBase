1. Pull the wireless transmitter **downwards** to unmount it.
2. Push the **back cover** of the wireless transmitter in the direction of the arrow to reach the batteries and replace them.
3. Reverse the steps to reinstall.

The manual does not name the battery type for this transmitter.
