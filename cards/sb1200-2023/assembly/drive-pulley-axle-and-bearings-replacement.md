1. Remove the **right crank (32)**, **right chain cover (47)**, **left crank (33)**, **left chain cover (48)** and the **belt (37)**.
2. Use a **C-ring clip clamp** to remove the **C-ring Ø20 (75)**, then use a **rubber mallet** to tap out the **turntable spindle assembly (17)**.
3. Use a **6 mm hex key** to remove **five hex screws M10 x 20L (68)** from the socket, so the **belt pulley (30)** can be removed. **Replace the 6004 bearings (53) at this step** if they need replacing.
4. Reassemble in the reverse order.
