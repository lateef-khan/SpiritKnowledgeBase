Hardware: **#80** M5 × 12mm Phillips Head Screw (6 pcs), **#99** 4 × 16mm Sheet Metal Screw (5 pcs).

1. Install the Console (26) on the **Screen Rack (121)** with four Phillips Head Screws (80), by using the **Short Phillips Head Screw Driver (103)**. Connect the Computer Cable to the Console (26), **reserve the length of the Computer Cable for turning the Console** and tighten the wire tie.
2. Install the **Rotary Shaft Cover (122)** on the Console (26) with two Phillips Head Screws (80), by using the Short Phillips Head Screw Driver (103).
3. Install the **Tablet Holder (130)** on the Console (26) with **five** Sheet Metal Screws (99), by using the Short Phillips Head Screw Driver (103).

Six #80 screws: four for the console, two for the rotary shaft cover. **Five** #99 sheet metal screws for the tablet holder.

**The console on this machine swivels**, which is why the cable must be left with slack and why there is a rotary shaft cover. The SB900 2023 console is a twist-lock unit on a console holder, fitted with no screws at all, and its tablet holder takes only **three** #99 screws and goes on the handlebar rather than on the console.

Instruction 1 names the part "Screen Rack (121)" while the step 2 instruction on the previous page calls the part it fits the "Screen Holder (7)". Both numbers appear in the drawings; the manual does not explain the relationship.
