Hardware: **#80** M5 × 12mm Phillips Head Screw (4 pcs).

1. Install the **Drink Bottle Holder (50)** and the **Flywheel Cover (49)** in front of the Main Frame (1) with four Phillips Head Screws (80), by using the **Short Phillips Head Screw Driver (103)**.

These are the same two parts and the same four screws as the SB900 2023, which does this work as part of its **step 3** and also calls for the M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (101) alongside the short screwdriver. The SB1200 instruction names only the screwdriver.

#80 appears in two hardware steps on this machine: six in step 3 and four here. Ten M5 × 12mm screws in total.
