This step needs no hardware from the assembly pack.

1. Loosen the flywheel and remove the **Aluminum Ring Mount** from the flywheel: unfasten the velcro on the knob holder (1), connect the wireless resistance knob, then turn the wireless resistance knob (2) **counterclockwise to the minimum value** and remove the Aluminum Ring Mount (3) from the machine.

The circled numbers 1, 2 and 3 are positions on the drawing, not item numbers from the parts list. The Aluminum Ring Mount has no item number in this manual.

**The Aluminum Ring Mount is a shipping part.** It comes out during assembly and is not refitted.

**The SB900 2023 prints the equivalent step under a different name and with a different part.** Its step 6 is headed BLUETOOTH WIRELESS KNOB and removes a **flywheel spacer**, not an aluminium ring mount.
