Hardware: **#96** M8 × P1.25 × 15L Socket Head Cap Bolt (6 pcs).

1. Pass the Computer Cable under the Handlebar (4) and pull it out to the front. Install the Handlebar (4) on the Handlebar Post (5) with **four** Socket Head Cap Bolts (96), by using the **M6 L Allen Wrench (102)**. **Ensure that cables are not pinched.**
2. Then tie the Computer Cable to the velcro, pass it from the Handlebar (4) to the Screen Holder (7) and pull it out of the top. Install the Screen Holder (7) on the Handlebar (4) with **two** Socket Head Cap Bolts (96), by using the M6 L Allen Wrench (102).

Six #96 bolts: four for the handlebar, two for the screen holder.

**The SB900 2023 uses #96 for a different bolt** - a 5/16" × 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt supplied 4 pcs - and has no screen holder. It fits its handlebar to a Sliding Handlebar Mount with #96, #97 and #98 and drives them with the M5 Allen wrench (101), not the M6 L Allen wrench.
