| Item | Tool |
|---|---|
| #100 | 14/15mm Wrench |
| #102 | M6 L Allen Wrench |
| #103 | Short Phillips Head Screw Driver |

Three tools. The SB900 2023 uses the same three numbers for the same three tools but adds a fourth, **#101 M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench**, which is not in the SB1200 pack.
