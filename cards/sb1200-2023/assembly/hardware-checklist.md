Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #94 | ø10 × ø16.5 × 2T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| #93 | 3/8" × 3-3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #95 | ø10 × ø23 × 1.5T | Curved Washer | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #96 | M8 × P1.25 × 15L | Socket Head Cap Bolt | 6 pcs |

**Hardware step 3**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #80 | M5 × 12mm | Phillips Head Screw | 6 pcs |
| #99 | 4 × 16mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 5 pcs |

**Hardware step 4**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #80 | M5 × 12mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |

#80 appears in two steps: six screws in step 3 (four for the console, two for the rotary shaft cover) and four more in step 4 for the drink bottle holder and flywheel cover. Ten M5 × 12mm screws in total.

The checklist runs 1, 2, 3, 4. The machine has seven numbered assembly steps - 1 stabilizer tubes, 2 screen holder, 3 console, 4 drink bottle holder / flywheel cover, 5 dumbbells holder, 6 left/right pedals, 7 brake tension knob - and steps 5 to 7 use no hardware out of the assembly pack.

**The SB900 2023 uses the same item numbers for a different pack.** Its step 1 is identical, but its `#96` is a 5/16" × 3/4" button head socket bolt supplied 4 pcs, its step 3 supplies four #80 and three #99 rather than six and five, and it has an extra step-2 block of #97 split washers and #98 flat washers that the SB1200 does not carry.
