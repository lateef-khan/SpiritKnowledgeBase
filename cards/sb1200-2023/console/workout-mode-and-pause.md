Once a workout has started the console switches to workout mode.

- Switch between **Stats** and **Charts** views in the Training section, or go to the Media section for content.
- In the **Stats** view, tap a number to choose which parameters are displayed.
- **Turn the knob to adjust the resistance level.** There are no level keys on this console.
- Press **Pause** to pause. From there you can return to training, or end the workout and see the summary.

**After 5 minutes of inactivity in Pause mode the workout ends automatically.**
