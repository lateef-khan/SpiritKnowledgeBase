The first time the console is launched it asks you to enter as a **guest** or create a new profile. A profile lets the machine remember your physical parameters and lets your workout records sync with the **SOLE+** mobile app.

- Tap **+** to create a new profile.
- A maximum of **9 profiles** can be created.
- Each profile can be protected with a **passcode**.

**To link a SOLE+ account:**

1. Scan the QR code on the console screen to download the mobile app.
2. Install the app and complete registration.
3. Press **Link** at the bottom of the console screen.
4. Open the QR code scanning camera in the SOLE+ app and scan the console's QR code again. That links your local profile with the SOLE+ account.

**A console profile cannot be linked to more than one SOLE+ account at a time.** If you skip linking, you can reach it later in Profile settings.
