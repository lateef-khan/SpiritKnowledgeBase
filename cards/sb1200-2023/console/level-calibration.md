This is the **Spin Bike Level Calibration** item on the FUNCTION page of Engineer Mode. It calibrates the resistance system's **G-sensor**, and the manual states that user intervention is required during the process.

1. Press **START**. The calibration process begins.
2. Click **"start"** to calibrate the resistance level. The screen shows **"Cali Step 1"**.
3. Turn the resistance knob to the **maximum** level, then press **NEXT** so the console reads the AD value and records it into **MAX AD**.
4. Turn the resistance knob to the **minimum** level, then press **NEXT** so the console reads the AD value and records it into **MIN AD**.
5. The process is done.

The manual prints both a **START** button in the opening sentence and a **"start"** click in the first step. It does not say whether they are the same control.
