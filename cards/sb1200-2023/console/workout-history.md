Press **History** on the main screen of the Training section to see your latest workout records.

- **Up to 10 records are stored locally on the console.**
- Link your profile to the **SOLE+** mobile app to reach your full workout history.
- Tap a record preview to see the full summary.
- **Each user can only see his or her own workouts.**

Changing Machine Type in Engineer Mode clears every local account and record, and they cannot be recovered. Records already synced to SOLE+ survive that.
