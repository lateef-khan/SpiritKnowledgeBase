**In a double-insulated product, two systems of insulation are provided instead of grounding. No grounding means is provided on a double-insulated product, nor should a means for grounding be added to the product.**

**Servicing a double-insulated product requires extreme care and knowledge of the system, and should be done only by qualified service personnel.** **Replacement parts for a double-insulated product must be identical to the parts they replace.**

A double-insulated product is marked with the words **"DOUBLE INSULATION"** or **"DOUBLE INSULATED"**, and with the symbol - a square within a square.

**The SB1200 2023 is the only bike in this set that is double-insulated.** Every Sole bike grounding instruction in this knowledge base - the LCB/LCR 2023 grounding section and the shared outlet card built from the bike service manuals - is about grounded machines and must not be applied here. Adding a ground to this machine is explicitly forbidden by its own manual.

The SB1200 does run on mains power: the console page says it operates on DC 12V/3.3A (12V/3A) supplied by a power pack connected to 115 (230) VAC, with the power socket where the line cord plugs into the unit on the front of the cycle.
