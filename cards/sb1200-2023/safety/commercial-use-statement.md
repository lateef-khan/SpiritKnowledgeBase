Item 23 of the safety list reads, in full:

> **The appliance is intended for commercial use.**

That is the whole sentence. The manual gives no definition of commercial use, no hours-per-day limit and no facility type.

**This manual is also the only bike owner's manual in the set with no "residential use only" panel on its cover.** Every other Sole bike and indoor cycle manual that has one prints "THIS FITNESS BIKE (or INDOOR CYCLE) IS INTENDED FOR RESIDENTIAL USE ONLY AND IS WARRANTED FOR THE APPLICATION. ANY OTHER APPLICATION VOIDS THIS WARRANTY IN ITS ENTIRETY." The SB1200 2023 manual has no such panel.

**This line is a safety-page statement, not a warranty grant.** What the SB1200 warranty actually covers is a separate question and is answered by the warranty cards; exclusion 8 of the same manual still limits cover to a residential setting, which does not sit easily beside item 23. Both are printed in this one manual and neither reconciles the other.
