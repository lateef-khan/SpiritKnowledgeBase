The manual states this from both sides on the same page.

- **Consumer responsibility 7:** provide **pictures or video** of any issues via email as requested by the service technician, and **assist with troubleshooting/diagnosing prior to a home visit**.
- **Service step 5:** the service department **assists in troubleshooting prior to sending a field technician**, and SOLE may request pictures and video via email of issues to help with diagnosing.

Being asked for a photo before a visit is booked is the documented process, not a delay tactic.
