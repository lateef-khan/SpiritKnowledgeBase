One section of this manual calls the machine a **stepper**. The rest calls it a **climber**. Both are printed in the same document and no winner is picked here.

**Where it says stepper**
- The page 13 heading reads **"OPERATION OF YOUR FITNESS STEPPER"**.
- The POWER UP text on that page reads "the total time the **stepper** has been used since it was new" and "Your **stepper** is now ready for use."

**Where it says climber**
- The table of contents on page 2 names that same section **"Operation of Your New Fitness Climber"**.
- The cover reads "CC81 **Climber** Trainer", every page footer reads "CC81 **CLIMBER**", the warranty is headed "CC81 CLIMBER WARRANTY" and warrants "all its **climber** parts", and page 15 reads "The **Climber** has five different programs".

A manual that reads "stepper" on page 13 is not the wrong manual for this machine.

**How this was read.** The manual draws its body text as outlined glyphs, so `pdftotext` dropped the page 13 heading and the whole of page 2, so neither half of this mismatch was visible before. This card was read from the rendered page image.
