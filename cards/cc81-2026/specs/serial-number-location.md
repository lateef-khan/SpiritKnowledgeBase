The manual asks you to record the serial number of this product in the space provided on page 3, under **RECORD YOUR SERIAL NUMBER**.

The page shows the location with a photograph of the **lower frame and mast area of the climber, with a rectangular serial-number label on the frame**. That photograph is the only location given; no written description of the spot is printed.

The serial number matters later: warranty service is dated from it when there is no bill of sale, and every service request has to quote it.
