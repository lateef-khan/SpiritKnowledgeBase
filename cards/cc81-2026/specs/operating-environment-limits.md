| Limit | Figure |
| --- | --- |
| Operating temperature | 40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit |
| Humidity | 95% non-condensing (no water drops forming on surfaces) |

The same paragraph forbids rain, moisture, outdoor use, and use near a pool or spa or in any other high humidity environment.

Note that meeting these limits is not the same as keeping the warranty: exclusion 10 voids the warranty for a unit that is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment.
