The console masthead reads **SOLE CC81**, with a **SOLE+ COMPATIBLE** badge at the upper right of the display.

**Keys**

| Position | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Left of the display, top to bottom | MANUAL (hand icon), HILL (mountain icon), FAT BURN (flame icon) |
| Right of the display, top to bottom | CARDIO (heart icon), STRENGTH (dumbbell icon), HIIT (bar-chart icon) |
| Below the display | START (triangle), STOP (square), and a round **LEVEL/ENTER knob** marked `- LEVEL +` and `ENTER` |

**Display fields, top to bottom**

| Field | Digits |
| --- | --- |
| TIME | four |
| CALORIES | four |
| VERTICAL, with FEET and METERS sub-labels | five |
| Heart symbol, and TOTAL STEPS PER MIN | three, and five |
| LEVEL, with a Bluetooth symbol | two |
| A heart-with-pulse symbol, and two small unit badges | - |
| A dot-matrix band and a segmented message line | - |

**Read with less confidence.** The two small unit badges beside the LEVEL field appear to read **MIL** and **KM**, but they are set in a very small stylised LCD face and are at the limit of legibility in the page image. Every other label above is legible.

**How this was read.** The manual draws its body text as outlined glyphs, so `pdftotext` dropped the page 13 heading and every key and display label on the console photograph. This card was read from the rendered page image.
