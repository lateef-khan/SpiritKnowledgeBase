- **FLS** - Representative floors.
- **FPM** - Floors Per Minute.

These are the two climb-specific readouts on this console. The printed note is spaced "FLS(Representative floors) , FPM( Floors Per Minute)".

The console's own field labels for the climb figures read VERTICAL (with FEET and METERS) and TOTAL STEPS PER MIN; those are on the control panel card.
