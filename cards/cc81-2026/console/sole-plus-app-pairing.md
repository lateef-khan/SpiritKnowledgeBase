1. Download the App by **scanning the QR code** printed on page 14.
2. Open the App on your device (phone or tablet) and make sure **Bluetooth is enabled** on the device.
3. In the App, click the **Bluetooth icon** to search for your Sole + equipment.
4. Under the Bluetooth scan result list, **select the machine to connect**. When the App and equipment are synced, the **Bluetooth icon on the equipment's console display will light up**. Click **"DISPLAY"** and you may now start using the Sole + App.
5. When your exercise is complete, press **"END & SAVE WORKOUT"** in the App to store the workout data. You will then be prompted to sync your data with each available fitness cloud site.

Pressing **"DISPLAY"** in the App at any time shows the current workout data.

**Note on the source.** Steps 4 and 5 are printed as "select the machine for connect" and "When your exercise is complete pressed in the APP". The wording above is the same instruction read straight.

**The QR codes cannot be reproduced here.** The two codes on page 14 are images, one under a "GET IT ON Google Play" badge and one under a "Download on the App Store" badge; the addresses they encode are not printed on the page and have not been guessed.
