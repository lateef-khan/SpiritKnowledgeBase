The Sole + App can sync workout data with one of many fitness cloud sites, listed in the manual as **Apple Health, Google Fit, Strava, MapMyFitness or Fitbit**, "with more to come".

**Why an icon may be inactive.** You have to download the applicable compatible fitness App - Apple Health, Google Fit, Strava, MapMyFitness, Fitbit and so on - **in order for the icon to be active and available**.

The prompt to sync appears after you press "END & SAVE WORKOUT" in the App.
