This is the quickest way to start a workout.

1. After the console powers up, press the **Start** key. This starts Quick Start mode.
2. Time counts **up from zero**, and the workload/resistance is adjusted manually by **rotating the Level knob**.
3. Press **Stop** once to enter pause mode, and **Start** to resume the workout.
4. Press **Stop twice** to exit the program.
