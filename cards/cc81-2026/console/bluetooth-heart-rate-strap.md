The machine's Bluetooth device (**BLE 5.0**) can be connected to a **Bluetooth wireless heart rate chest strap transmitter (BLE 5.0)**.

- The chest strap transmitter **can receive signals even when the App is already in use**.
- **Wear the chest strap first, then commence connection.**
- While connected, the console shows the heart rate value via Bluetooth.
- The heart-with-Bluetooth icon **lights up when the strap connects successfully**, and the heart rate is then displayed. **The icon goes off if the strap is disconnected.**

The hand pulse sensors are a separate thing, and the manual limits what they are for on its own card.
