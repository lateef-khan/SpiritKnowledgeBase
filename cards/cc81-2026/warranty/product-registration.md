**How to register (page 3)**
- The **self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to SOLE**.
- You can also go to **www.soletreadmills.com** under the **support tab** to register online.
- Record the **serial number** in the space provided on that page.
- The page also gives spaces to record the **name of the dealer, the dealer's telephone number, and the purchase date** for easy contact in the future.

**The deadline (page 20, responsibility 1)**
- The card must be returned to the address listed on it **within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.
- **Outside the 10 day period** the warranty can still be registered if you have **proof of purchase**: a receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation.

Exclusion 6 is the other side of this: products without a warranty registration card on file at SOLE are not covered, and SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase where no warranty record exists.
