- Page 2, ATTENTION: **"THIS FITNESS CLIMBER IS INTENDED FOR RESIDENTIAL USE ONLY AND IS WARRANTED FOR THE APPLICATION. ANY OTHER APPLICATION VOIDS THIS WARRANTY IN ITS ENTIRETY."**
- Page 20, exclusion 8: **product use in any environment other than a residential setting** is not covered.
- Page 20, responsibilities: the warranty applies **only to products in ordinary household use**, and exclusion 2 names a Climber "not in ordinary household use".

The manual states this rule in three places and they agree.
