Exclusion 10 reads: **"Warranty will be void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment. (Examples: Garage, outside, carport, etc)"**

This is separate from the operating temperature and humidity limits on page 5: a spot can be inside the 40 to 120 degree Fahrenheit envelope and still void the warranty because it is not climate-controlled and insulated.

The full exclusion list is on its own card.
