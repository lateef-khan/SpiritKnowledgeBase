SOLE warrants all its climber parts for the periods listed **from the date of retail sale, as determined by sale receipt**, or, in the absence of a sales receipt, **eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date**.

This is why the manual asks you to keep the bill of sale and to record the serial number: without a receipt, the factory shipping date, determined by the serial number, is what dates the machine.
