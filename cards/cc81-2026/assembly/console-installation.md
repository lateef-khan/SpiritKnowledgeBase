Plug the Computer Cable (41) into the Console (40). Place the Console on the mounting plate of the Main Mast and secure with four Phillips Head Screws (100), using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (137).

The screws are the **M5 × P0.8 × 10L** Phillips head screws; the Hardware Step 4 box for this page holds **8 pcs** of them, four for this step and four for the handgrip caps.

The cable is the one routed up through the mast, so route it before the console goes on.
