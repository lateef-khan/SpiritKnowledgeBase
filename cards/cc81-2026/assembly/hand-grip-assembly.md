Secure the left Hand Grip Assembly (16) on the Vertical Tube (11) with two Hex Head Bolts (91), two Flat Washers (119), two Nylon Nuts (111), one Hex Head Bolt (92) and one Flat Washer (118), using Wrench (135) - the **13/14mm** wrench - and Wrench (136), the **12/14mm** wrench.

**On the right-hand side.** The step is written out for the **left** hand grip only, and no "repeat on the right side" line is printed, unlike the side tube step. What the page does show is the assembly drawing, which carries both the left and right hand grips, and the Hardware Step 3 box, which lists exactly **twice** the hardware this step consumes:

| Item | This step uses | Hardware Step 3 box holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 91 Hex Head Bolt | two | 4 pcs |
| 111 Nylon Nut | two | 4 pcs |
| 119 Flat Washer | two | 4 pcs |
| 92 Hex Head Bolt | one | 2 pcs |
| 118 Flat Washer | one | 2 pcs |

The manual still prints no instruction for the right side, so none is written here.
