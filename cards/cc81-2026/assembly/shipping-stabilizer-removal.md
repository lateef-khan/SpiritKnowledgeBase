Use the L Allen Wrench (138) to remove the two Socket Head Cap Bolts that retain the shipping stabilizer (23) on the mounting plate of the Main Frame. Remove the stabilizer and **discard** it.

The shipping stabilizer is packing hardware, not part of the finished machine. The drawing shows it as a T-shaped bar held by two bolts.

Tool 138 is the **8mm L Allen Wrench** on the page 7 tool list; the step itself gives no size.
