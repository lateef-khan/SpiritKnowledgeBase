1. Using a razor knife (Box Cutter), cut the outside, bottom, edge of the box along the dotted line. **Lift the box over the unit** and unpack.
2. Carefully remove all parts from the carton and **inspect for any damage or missing parts**. If damaged parts are found, or parts are missing, **contact your dealer immediately**.
3. Locate the hardware package. **Remove the tools first.** Remove the hardware for each step as needed to avoid confusion.

**The numbers in parentheses.** The numbers in the instructions that are in parenthesis (#) are the **item number from the assembly drawing**, for reference.
