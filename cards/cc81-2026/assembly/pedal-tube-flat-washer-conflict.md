The manual names two different flat washers for the same bolt, and this card picks no winner.

| Where it is printed | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| Page 10, step 1, bottom part | "fasten with one Bolt (98), one **Flat Washer (115)**, and one Nylon Nut (112)" |
| Page 10, HARDWARE STEP 2 box (same page) | #98 Bolt (2 pcs), **#119 Ø3/8" × Ø19 × 1.5T Flat Washer (2 pcs)**, #112 Nylon Nut (2 pcs) |
| Pages 6 and 7, assembly pack checklist | **No part 115 is listed anywhere.** The flat washers in the pack are #119 (Ø3/8" × Ø19 × 1.5T) and #118 (Ø10 × Ø25 × 1.5T) |

Both readings are transcribed as printed. The manual gives no correction, no note, and no part 115 anywhere else, so a customer who cannot find a 115 in the box is not necessarily missing a part.

**How this was read.** The manual draws its body text as outlined glyphs, so `pdftotext` dropped the Hardware Step 2 box and the pages 6 to 7 checklist, which is why this conflict could not be seen at all before. This card was read from the rendered page image.
