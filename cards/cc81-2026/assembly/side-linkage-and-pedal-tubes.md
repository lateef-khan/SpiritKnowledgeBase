**Top part.** Remove the pre-installed axle and screw from the top of the Left Side Linkage (18). Now assemble the Left Side Linkage to the Vertical Arm (11) with the axle and screw removed previously. Make sure the axle is aligned with the indentation of the U-bracket on the left side tube (18) when assembling. **Repeat the same procedures on the right side tube assembly.**

**Bottom part.** Connect the rod end bearing with the U-bracket of the left pedal tube, then fasten with one Bolt (98), one Flat Washer (115), and one Nylon Nut (112), using Wrench (139) - the **17mm** wrench - and the **6mm** L Allen Wrench (140). **Repeat the same procedures on the right side tube assembly.**

**Which flat washer.** The step calls for a Flat Washer **(115)**, but the Hardware Step 2 box on this same page lists only **#119**, and no part 115 appears anywhere in the checklists on pages 6 and 7. Both readings are printed; neither is corrected here. That conflict has its own card.
