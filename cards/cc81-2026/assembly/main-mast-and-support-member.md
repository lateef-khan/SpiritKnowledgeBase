1. Route the computer cable (41) through the bottom of the Main Mast (2) and out of the mast on top, using the wire snake that is attached to the cable. Secure the Main Mast on the mounting plate on the Main Frame with four Socket Head Cap Bolts (96), four **Split** Washers (126) and four Flat Washers (119), using the L Allen Wrench (138). **Use extreme caution during this step to ensure the cable does not get pinched between the mast and main frame.**
2. Secure the Support Member (13) on the mounting plate of the Main Frame and Main Mast (2) with four Socket Head Cap Bolts (96), four **Split** Washers (126), four Flat Washers (119), two Hex Head Bolts (90) and two Curved Washers (125), using the L Allen Wrench (138) and Wrench (135).

Tool 138 is the **8mm L Allen Wrench** and 135 is the **13/14mm** wrench, from the page 7 tool list.

The support member ties the frame and the mast together, so the mast is left on its bolts until it is fitted.
