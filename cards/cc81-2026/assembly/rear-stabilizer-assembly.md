Attach the Rear Stabilizer with handle (12) to the mounting plate on the main frame and secure it with two Hex Head Bolts (93), two **Split** Washers (126) and Curved Washers (125), using Wrench (135) - the **13/14mm** wrench.

**The count for the curved washers is not printed.** The step gives "two Split Washers (126) **and Curved Washers (125)**" with no number in front of the curved washers. The Hardware Step 1 box on the same page lists **4 pcs** of #125 for this whole step group, and step 3 of the group calls for two of them. The manual prints no count for this step and none is supplied here.

**How this was read.** The manual draws its body text as outlined glyphs, so `pdftotext` dropped the section heading, the whole Hardware Step 1 side box, the step numbering and the word "Split" before each "Washers (126)". This card was read from the rendered page image.
