1. Match the Left Console Mast Cover (68) with the Right Console Mast Cover (69) on top of the left and right Side Covers and around the Console Mast, and secure with four Sheet Metal Screws (102), using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (137).
2. Match the Left and Right Handgrip Caps (79, 80) together on the **left** side of the Handgrip Stabilizer Assembly and use two Phillips Head Screws (100) and one Sheet Metal Screw (102), using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (137). Match the Left and Right Handgrip Caps (79, 80) together on the **right** side of the Handgrip Stabilizer Assembly and secure with the same way and same amount of hardware.

**The screw count works out exactly.** The Hardware Step 4 box holds 8 pcs of #100 and 6 pcs of #102. The console takes four #100; the mast covers take four #102; the two sides of handgrip caps take two #100 and one #102 each. That is 8 and 6.
