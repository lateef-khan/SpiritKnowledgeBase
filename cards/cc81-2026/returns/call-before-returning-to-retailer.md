**"Before returning your Sole product to any retailer, or if you require any assistance with assembly or technical support please call us first for assistance at 866-780-7653. Thank you for your Sole purchase."**

The manual prints this notice three times: on the front cover, on page 2 under the ATTENTION block, and again on page 21.

The support numbers on page 3 are the other route in: **866-780-SOLE (7653)** for questions about the product, and **866-MYSOLE1 (1-866-697-6531)** for technical service.

**How this was read.** The manual draws its body text as outlined glyphs, so `pdftotext` dropped pages 1, 2 and 21 entirely, so this notice was missing from all three of its printings. This card was read from the rendered page image.
