**DO NOT SHIP ANY UNIT TO OUR FACTORY WITHOUT A RETURN AUTHORIZATION NUMBER. All units arriving without a return authorization number will be refused.**

The same step sets the terms around it: transportation costs both ways are the owner's, the owner is responsible for adequate packaging on the way back, SOLE is not responsible for damage in transit, and freight damage claims go to the freight carrier.

**Note on the source.** The word is printed "with- out", broken by a hyphen mid-line.
