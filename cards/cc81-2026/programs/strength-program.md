The Strength program is designed to increase muscular strength in your lower body. It steadily increases resistance to a high level and forces you to sustain it, which strengthens and tones the legs and glutes.

**About the profile chart.** The manual prints a level profile for this program as a bitmap grid with **no printed level values**. Read from the page image, its shape is a steady staircase rising to a high point, then dropping back. No level numbers are printed on the chart and none have been supplied here.
