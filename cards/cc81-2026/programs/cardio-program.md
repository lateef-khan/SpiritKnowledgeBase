The Cardio program is designed to increase cardiovascular function and endurance - exercise for your heart and lungs. It builds up the heart muscle and increases blood flow and lung capacity, by incorporating a higher level of exertion with slight fluctuations in work.

**About the profile chart.** The manual prints a level profile for this program as a bitmap grid with **no printed level values**. Read from the page image, its shape is a stepped rise to a broad plateau, then a stepped fall. No level numbers are printed on the chart and none have been supplied here.
