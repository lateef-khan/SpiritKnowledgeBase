The HIIT program takes you through high levels of intensity followed by periods of low intensity. It increases endurance by depleting your oxygen level and then giving recovery periods to replenish it, so the cardiovascular system is programmed to use oxygen more efficiently. The spikes in heart rate between recovery periods also make the body more efficient and aid heart rate recovery from intense activity.
