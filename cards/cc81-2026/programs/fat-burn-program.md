The Fat Burn program is designed to maximise the burning of fat. Most experts agree that a lower exertion level held at a steady workload is the best way to do that, and that the absolute best way is to keep your heart rate at around **60% to 70%** of its maximum potential.

**This program does not use heart rate.** It simulates a lower, steady exertion workout instead.

**About the profile chart.** The manual prints a level profile for this program as a bitmap grid with **no printed level values**. Read from the page image, its shape is a low flat plateau across the middle of the grid. No level numbers are printed on the chart and none have been supplied here.
