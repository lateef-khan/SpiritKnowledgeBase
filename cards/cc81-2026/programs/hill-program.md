The Hill program simulates going up and down a hill. The resistance in the pedals steadily increases and then decreases during the program.

**About the profile chart.** The manual prints a level profile for this program as a bitmap grid with **no printed level values**. Read from the page image, its shape is a symmetrical stepped pyramid that rises to a peak in the middle of the grid and falls away again. No level numbers are printed on the chart and none have been supplied here.
