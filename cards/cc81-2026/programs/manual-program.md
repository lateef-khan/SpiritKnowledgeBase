The Manual program works as the name implies: you control the workload yourself, not the computer.

1. Press the **Manual** key, then press **Enter**.
2. Enter your **Age**. Adjust with the **Level knob**, then press **Enter** to accept and go to the next screen.
3. Enter your **Weight**. Adjust with the Level knob, then press **Enter**.
4. Set the **Time**. Adjust with the Level knob, then press **Enter**.
5. Press **Start** to begin the workout. To go back and change a setting, press **Stop** to go back one level of the program setting.
6. The program starts at **level one**, the easiest level. Staying at level one for a while to warm up is a good idea. Rotate the Level knob at any time to increase or decrease the workload.
7. The level can be adjusted manually with the Level knob throughout the program.
8. Press **Stop** once to pause and **Start** to resume. Press **Stop twice** to exit the program.
9. When the program ends the display shows a **summary** of your workout for a short time, then the console returns to the start-up display.

The resistance follows a level change gradually rather than at once, which has its own card.
