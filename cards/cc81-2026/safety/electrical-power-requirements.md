**WARNING** - To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons, install the climber on a **flat level surface with access to a 110 to 240 volt ac, 5 amp grounded outlet with only the climber plugged into the circuit**.

**DO NOT USE AN EXTENSION CORD UNLESS IT IS A 16 AWG OR BETTER.**
