- **Do not use outdoors.**
- **NEVER expose this fitness climber to rain or moisture.** This product is **NOT** designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.

The temperature and humidity figures that go with this rule are on the operating environment card, and the warranty is separately void outside a climate-controlled, insulated environment.
