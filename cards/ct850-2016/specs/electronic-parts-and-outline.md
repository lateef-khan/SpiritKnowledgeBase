**Outlines (p. 6)** labels only two items on the machine drawing: **Console** and
**Hand Pulse Sensor**.

**Upper controllers (p. 8)** - **Display**, **Cooling FAN**, **Safety key**.

**Lower controller and driver (p. 9)** - **Speed sensor**, **Inverter**, **AC motor**, **Filter**.

The Outlines chapter of this manual carries **no dimensions, no machine weight, no maximum user
weight and no power rating**. Those figures are not printed anywhere in the manual.
