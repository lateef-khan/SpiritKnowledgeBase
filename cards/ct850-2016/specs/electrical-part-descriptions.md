- **Safety key** - fits on the console and activates all functions. With no safety key the
  console cannot be controlled.
- **Console** - the interface that controls all functions of the treadmill; key controls and an
  LED display.
- **Main controller** - carries the AC power supply for the console, the incline driver and the
  AC motor driver. It links the console and outputs the voltages for the
  **incline interface board and inverter**.
- **AC motor** - work voltage **AC 120V~**; increases and decreases speed.
- **Incline motor** - a **120 volt AC** motor with four wires (red, black, white, green) plus one
  3-pin position-sensor cable. AC on **red = UP**, AC on **black = DOWN**, **white = COM
  (neutral)**, **green = ground**.

The 2020 CT850 manual prints the same section with the incline motor as a **110 volt** AC motor
and the main controller feeding a "DC control Board" rather than an incline interface board and
inverter. Do not carry a figure from one manual to the other.
