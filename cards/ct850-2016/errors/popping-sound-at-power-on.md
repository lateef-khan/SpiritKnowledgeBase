| Condition | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| After turning on power, treadmill has a popping sound | Incorrect input power, varistor is blown broken on controller | Check the voltage of power is 200-240V. Replace controller. |

**The voltage in this row does not agree with the rest of this manual.** The cover reads
`ST8100-CT001 Treadmill Service Manual (110V)`, the safety section calls for a 120-volt 20-amp
outlet, and the row two below this one asks for 110-120V at the console. Treat `200-240V` as the
figure for a 230-volt build and check the machine's own rating plate first.

The CT850 2020 manual prints the same row with **120V**: see
`ct850-2020-popping-sound-at-power-on`.
