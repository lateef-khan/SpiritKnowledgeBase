The manual's answer, word for word:

> This indicates motor should be receiving power to operate. Low AC voltage to treadmill. Do not use
> an extension cord. If an extension cord is required. it should be as short as possible and heavy
> duty 12 gauge minimum. Low household voltage. Contact an electrician or your dealer. A minimum of
> 110 volt AC current, 60 hz is required.

Two numbers to hold on to: **12 gauge minimum** for an extension cord, and **110 volt AC, 60 Hz
minimum** at the outlet.

**The CT850 2020 manual prints different figures for the same fault** - 16-gauge and a minimum of
100 volt AC - and adds `/10 kph` to the condition. See `ct850-2020-only-reaches-7-mph`.
