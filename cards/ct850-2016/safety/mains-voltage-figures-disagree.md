**This manual does not state one supply voltage.** It prints at least eight figures. They are
listed here as printed, with the page each is on. The manual never reconciles them, so neither does
this card.

| PDF page (printed) | Where | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover, under `Treadmill Service Manual` | `(110V)` |
| 11 (10) | Electronic parts, motor description | `Work voltage:AC 120V~` and `This is a 120 volt AC motor.` |
| 27 (26) | **7.1 Important Safety Instructions** | `a120-volt, 20-amp grounded outlet` |
| 27 (26) | **7.3 Important Grounding Instructions** | `for use on a nominal 120-volt circuit` |
| 43 (42) | Incline test, step on the Up wire | `about the same as the mains voltage ~ 230VAC` |
| 49 (48) | Diagnosis row, popping sound at power on | `Check the voltage of power is 200-240V` |
| 50 (49) | Diagnosis row, power to console too low | `Check AC power is 110-120V` |
| 56 (55) | Diagnosis row, only reaches 7 mph | `A minimum of 110 volt AC current, 60 hz is required` |
| 56 (55) | Diagnosis row, display does not light | `120 VAC wall outlet` |
| 59 (58) | Section heading | `Incline troubleshooting, AC 120V system with AC motor` |
| 61 (60) | The same incline test, repeated later | `about the same as the mains voltage ~ 120VAC` |

**The safety chapter's own answer is 120 volt.** Sections 7.1 and 7.3 both say so, and that is the
figure to give an installer: `spirit-ct850-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`.

Note that pages 43 and 61 are the **same incline test printed twice**, once saying ~230VAC and once
~120VAC.

**The CT850 2020 manual is consistent at 120 V** in its safety chapter, its troubleshooting rows and
its outlines. Do not read the 2016 manual's 230 V or 200-240 V figures onto it.

The rows on pages 49, 50 and 56 belong to the troubleshooting matrix; the cards holding them are
`ct850-2016-popping-sound-at-power-on`, `ct850-2016-only-reaches-7-mph` and
`ct850-2016-display-does-not-light`.
