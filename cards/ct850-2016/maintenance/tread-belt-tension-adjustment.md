Adjustment must be made **from the rear roller**. The adjustment bolts are at the end of the step
rails, in the end caps. The manual notes: "Adjustment is through small hole in the end cap."

1. Tighten the rear roller bolts **only enough to prevent slippage at the front roller**.
2. Turn **both** tread-belt tension adjustment bolts in increments of **1/4 turn each**.
3. Inspect for proper tension by walking on the belt at a low speed, making sure the belt does not
   slip.
4. Keep tensioning the bolts until the belt stops slipping.

**DO NOT OVERTIGHTEN — over tightening will cause belt damage and premature bearing failure.**

## If it is tight and still slips

The manual says directly: "If you feel the belt is tight enough, but it still slips, the problem
may be a loose Motor drive belt under the front cover." That is the same cause the troubleshooting
matrix gives for a noise under the motor cover — see `ct850-noise-under-motor-cover`.

The same bolts set tracking, and turning them unevenly walks the belt off centre — see
`ct850-2016-treadbelt-tracking-adjustment`.

**The CT850 2020 manual has no General Maintenance chapter.** Its troubleshooting row for a
hesitating belt points at a "General Maintenance section on running belt Tension" that is not in
that document. This procedure is printed only in the 2016 manual.
