Performance depends on the frame running on a **reasonably level surface**. If the frame is not
level, the front and back rollers cannot run parallel and constant belt adjustment will be
necessary. Level the machine before adjusting anything.

The treadmill is designed to keep the tread belt reasonably centred while in use. It is normal for
some belts to drift near one side while running with nobody on them; after a few minutes of use
the belt should tend to centre itself. Adjust only if the belt keeps moving toward one side
**during use**.

## To set tread belt tracking

1. Use the **10 mm Allen wrench** provided to adjust the rear roller.
2. Make tracking adjustments **from the left side only**.
3. Set belt speed to approximately **3 to 5 kph**.
4. **Turn the bolt clockwise to move the belt to the right.** Turn only **1/4 turn**, then wait a
   few minutes for the belt to adjust itself.
5. Continue in 1/4 rotations until the belt stabilises in the centre of the running deck.

A small adjustment makes a dramatic difference.

The belt may need periodic tracking adjustment depending on use and on walking or running
characteristics; different users affect tracking differently. Adjustments become less of a
maintenance concern as the belt is used. Proper belt tracking is an owner responsibility common
to all treadmills.

## This machine's own owner's manual says 8 mm, not 10 mm

**The two CT850 2016 documents disagree.** This *service* manual says a **10 mm** Allen wrench at
**3 to 5 kph**. The CT850 2016 *owner's* manual says an **8 mm** Allen wrench at **3 mph** for the
same adjustment, in the same words the CT800 and CT900ENT owner's manuals use
(`ct900ent-treadbelt-tracking-adjustment`, which now covers this machine too). The speeds are
compatible - 3 mph is about 4.8 kph - but the wrench sizes are not.

The owner's manual undercuts its own figure: its pre-assembly page lists the tools in the box as a
**3/8" Allen wrench, a 10 mm Allen wrench and a Phillips screwdriver**, with **no 8 mm wrench**,
even while the tracking paragraph says an 8 mm one "is provided for this adjustment". That tools
list agrees with this service manual. Check the bolt head before quoting a size.

**The CT850 2020 *service* manual has no General Maintenance chapter** and prints no tracking
procedure. The CT850 2020 *owner's* manual does - see `ct900ent-treadbelt-tracking-adjustment`.
