This treadmill uses a very high-efficiency low-friction bed. Performance is maximised when the
bed is kept as clean as possible.

## Monthly, belt and bed

1. Use a soft, damp cloth or paper towel to wipe the edge of the belt and the area between the
   belt edge and the frame.
2. Reach as far as practical directly under the belt edge.
3. **Use water only — no cleaners and no abrasives.**
4. A mild soap and water solution with a nylon scrub brush will clean the top of the textured
   belt. Allow the belt to dry before using.

Doing this once a month extends belt and bed life.

## Belt dust

Belt dust occurs during normal break-in, or until the belt stabilises. Wiping the excess off with
a damp cloth minimises the buildup. Black particles on the floor are a different thing — see
`ct850-black-particles-under-treadmill`.

## General cleaning

Dirt, dust and pet hair block air inlets and accumulate on the running belt.

- **Monthly**: vacuum underneath the treadmill to prevent buildup.
- **Once a year**: remove the black motor hood and vacuum out the dirt that accumulates.
  **UNPLUG THE POWER CORD BEFORE THIS TASK.**

## What this section does not say

Section 9.1 never mentions lubricant, wax or flipping the deck, and this manual has no
lubrication procedure. Do not read the troubleshooting matrix's "insufficient lubricant" row as
permission to lubricate — see `ct850-2016-tread-belt-lubrication`.

## The owner's manuals supply the missing half

The CT850 2016 and 2020 owner's manuals both print a BELT/DECK section on page 39 that covers the
same monthly wipe-down **and** the two things this service manual omits: the deck is **dual-sided
and rated 4,000 hrs a side** before it must be flipped, and **no lubricant or wax goes on the
surface**. See `spirit-ct800-maintenance-belt-deck-cleaning-4000-hours`.

**The CT850 2020 *service* manual has no General Maintenance chapter**, so it contains no cleaning
procedure of its own; do not assume this one applies to it. The CT850 2020 *owner's* manual does.
