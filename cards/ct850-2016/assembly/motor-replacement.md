The manual prints these as unnumbered paragraphs. They are numbered here in the printed order.

1. Use a **Phillips head screwdriver** to unscrew the left and right motor base caps
   (**4 Phillips head screws**), then remove the motor top cover (**6 tapping screws**).
2. Remove the motor grounding wire (**green yellow**) and the computer cables (**U, V, W**), and use
   a **14mm wrench** on the tension screw and an **M8 L-shape Allen wrench** to release the motor
   adjusting bolts.
3. Use the **M8 L-shape Allen wrench** to release the **4 bolts** securing the motor on the bottom of
   the treadmill.
4. Replace the motor with the new one after removing the motor belt, then resume parts in reverse
   order, including the motor belt. **Make sure that the belt is parallel to the frame, and do not
   tighten the 4 bolts yet.**
5. Use the **14mm wrench** to adjust the belt tension with a tension gauge until the tension reads in
   the **white range between 70~75 Lbs**. Then use the **M8 L-shape Allen wrench** to tighten the
   **4 motor adjusting bolts** to secure.
6. Connect the grounding wire and computer cable back.

The manual labels the three motor leads **U, V and W**.

The photograph beside step 5 shows the gauge being used: an **SPX OTC 6673 Belt Tension Gauge**, set
against the motor belt and read on its **LBS** scale. The text never names the gauge; the model
number is legible on the tool in the picture, and the 2020 manual reuses the same photograph.

The 2020 manual prints the same six steps as its section 11-3 with the same **70~75 Lbs**, the same
14mm and M8 tools and the same 4 bolts, but calls the 14mm tool an **open end wrench** and gives no
screw counts for the caps and the top cover. See `ct850-2020-motor-replacement`.

The roller procedure sends you back here for the belt tension. It cites "step 12.3 (5)", which does
not exist in this manual - the belt tension step is step 5 above, printed under heading 10.3.

**The 4.0T 2026 service manual prints these six steps as its section 9.3**, numbered 1 to 6, with the same 4
Phillips head screws, 6 tapping screws, 4 bolts, M8 Allen wrench and 70~75 Lbs, calling the 14mm tool an
"open end wrench" as the 2020 book does. Same figures, same order, so this card covers that machine too.

The **ST8700A-ST026-01 revision** of this book (`spirit-treadmill-40t-2026-service-manual-st8700a`, cover "4.0T-740885 (ST8700A-ST017-01)") prints this procedure word for word - its chapter 9 measures 99.8% the ST017 chapter at word level, one space apart.
