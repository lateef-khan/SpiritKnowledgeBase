1. Use a **Phillips head screwdriver** to remove the left and right motor base caps
   (**4 Phillips head screws**), then remove the motor top cover (**6 tapping screws**).
2. Disconnect all lower controller wirings.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the controller, fit the new lower controller and
   reconnect all wirings.

The screw counts are printed in the 2016 manual only. The CT850 2020 manual gives the same three
steps as its section 11-1 and prints **no screw counts at all** - only "use a screwdriver to remove
the Motor Base Cap (L) and (R), then remove the Motor Top Cover". Do not carry the 4-and-6 counts to
a 2020 machine; that version is on `ct850-2020-lower-controller-replacement`.

The manual gives no torque figure for any of these screws.
