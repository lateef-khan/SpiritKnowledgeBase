**Zero the incline motor before you take it off the machine or fit it onto the frame.**

1. Use a **Phillips head screwdriver** to release the screws and remove the left and right motor base
   caps and the motor top cover. Turn the treadmill to stand on one side, remove the cables connected
   on the lower controller that run to the incline motor, then remove the incline motor with a
   **17mm open end wrench**.
2. Use the **17mm open end wrench** to install the new incline motor and resume all cable
   connections.

**The zeroing caution is cut off in this manual.** It is printed in full as:

> (※Zeroing the incline motor before take it apart from the machine or assemble it onto the frame. The

and it stops there, mid-sentence, with no closing bracket. The 2016 manual **never gives the zeroing
distance**. This was checked against the 300 dpi page image, not only the extracted text; nothing
further is printed on the page.

The CT850 2020 manual finishes that same sentence: **"The zeroing distance is 235mm."** That figure
is printed for the 2020 machine only - see `ct850-2020-incline-motor-replacement`. Confirm it with
the factory before applying it to a 2016 machine.
