1. Use an **M8 L Allen wrench** to remove the **6 button head socket bolts** from the
   **console support**.
2. Disconnect the console wirings and replace with the new console.

That is the whole procedure the manual prints. The figure shows three bolts on each side of the
console support.

**The 2020 machine is not the same.** The CT850 2020 manual's section 11-2 removes **8 bolts** from
**Locking Plate (R) and (L)**, not 6 bolts from the console support. See
`ct850-2020-console-replacement` before you work on a 2020 machine.
