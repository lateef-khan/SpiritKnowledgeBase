1. Use a **Phillips head screwdriver** to release the **2 screws** for the rear roller end caps and
   remove the end caps.
2. Use an **M8 L-shape Allen wrench** to two screws for the rear rollers and remove the roller.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release screws and remove the left and right motor base caps
   (**4 Phillips head screws**). Then remove the motor top cover (**6 tapping screws**). Use a
   **14mm wrench** to loosen the tension screw and an **M8 L-shape Allen wrench** to loosen the motor
   adjusting bolts and the **4 securing bolts**, and loosen the motor belt.
4. Use the **M8 L-shape Allen wrench** to release the front roller screw and remove the front roller.
   Replace with new front and rear rollers and resume all parts in reverse order.
5. Adjust the belt tension and run the belt in the centre.

Two things to know before you follow this:

- Step 2 is printed with its verb missing - "Use M8 L-shape Allen wrench to two screws for rear
  rollers and remove the roller". It is quoted here as printed.
- Step 5 says "refer to step 12.3 (5) for adjusting the belt tension". **There is no section 12.3 in
  this manual.** The belt tension step is the last step of Motor Replacement, printed under heading
  10.3, on `ct850-2016-motor-replacement`.

The 2020 manual prints five steps here too, but its step 3 gives no screw counts and it cites
"step 11.3 (5)", which is correct for that manual. See `ct850-2020-front-rear-roller-replacement`.
