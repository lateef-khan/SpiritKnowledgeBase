**Step two takes three Socket Head Cap Bolts on each side. Every other manual on
this platform says two.** That is the one instruction that separates this build
from the CT850-2020, the CT800-2020 and the CT800ENT, whose steps are otherwise
word for word the same. Read the step-two note at the bottom before you start.

## Step one - right upright, then left

1. Gather Hardware for Step 1.
2. Thread the Computer Cable (No.53) up through the center of the Right Upright
   Column (No.9).
3. Use four sets of Button Head Socket Bolt (No.150), Split Washer (No.133)
   against a Flat Washer (No.115) to secure the Upright Fixing Plate (No.10) to
   the Right Uprights (No.9) to the Main Frame (No.1) using the Allen Wrench
   (No.160).
4. Use two Phillips Head Screws (No.144) to secure the Motor Base Cap Right
   (No.35) to Main Frame (No.1) using the Screw Driver (No.158).
5. Repeat the previous two steps on the left side.

Four sets a side is eight, and the step one hardware table lists **ten** of each
of 150, 133 and 115. The manual does not reconcile the two.

## Step two - locking plates and console support

1. Gather Hardware for Step 2.
2. On the underside of the Console Support (No.5) is where the Locking Plate
   Assembly (No. 3 & 4) can be attached. Use **3** Socket Head Cap Bolts (No.152)
   and **3** Flat Washers (No.153) on each side to secure.
3. Connect the Computer Cable (No. 53) with the Upper Computer Cable (No.52). **Be
   careful not to pinch the wires** and slide them into the Right Upright (No.9).
4. Gently slot the Console Support (No.5) into the Left/Right Uprights (No.8 & 9)
   and secure with 3 Button Head Socket Bolts (No.151), 3 Split Washers (No.133),
   and 3 Flat Washers (No.125) on both sides using the Allen Wrench (No.160).

Three a side is six, and this manual's step two table lists **six** of item 152
and six of item 153. **On this machine the step and the table agree.** They do not
agree on any other manual of the platform.

## Step three - rear upright bolts, cap and safety key

1. Gather Hardware For Step 3.
2. Further secure the uprights by inserting a Button Head Socket Bolt (No.154)
   through a Split Washer (No.133), and a Curved Washer (No.155) onto the backside
   of both Left/Right Uprights (No. 8 & 9) using the Allen Wrench (No.160).
3. Plug the Round Cap (No.29) into the Right Upright (No.9).
4. Plug the Safety Key (No.72) into the Safety Key base on the Console Support
   (No.5).

## Step four - console

1. Gather Hardware for Step 4.
2. Place the Console Assembly (No.38) onto the Console Support (No.5). Secure with
   4 Socket Head Cap Bolts (No.156), 4 Split Washers (No.120), and 4 Flat Washers
   (No.153) using the Allen Wrench (No.159).
3. Connect the Upper Handpulse Wires (No.50 & 51) and Upper Computer Cable (No.52)
   to Console Assembly (No.38).
4. Fasten the Console Cover (No.41) with 1 Phillips Head Screw (No.144) to the
   Console Support (No.5) using the Screw Driver (No.158).

The step four table lists **two** of item 144; the step uses one. Three cables
reach the console on this machine - two handpulse wires and one computer cable.

## Why this card exists separately

The CT850-2020 owner's manual prints this whole chapter again with **one** change:
its step two says two bolts and two washers a side and its table drops to five of
each. The CT800-2020 and CT800ENT manuals print the same reduced figures. Nothing
else in the four steps differs by a word, so use this card only for the 2016 book
and the linked cards for the others.
