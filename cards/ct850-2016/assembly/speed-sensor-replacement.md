1. First remove the motor top cover.
2. Remove the speed sensor wiring and proceed with the parts replacement.
3. After replacement, **a test to check if the sensor registers the magnet is required**.

That is the whole procedure. It names no tool, no screw and no gap.

The **magnet-to-sensor gap is printed elsewhere**: the LOW SPEED troubleshooting says the gap must be
**less than 3 mm**, and it also says where the sensor sits and how to align it. That half is on
`ct850-2016-low-speed-check-rpm-sensor`. Set the gap from there after fitting the new sensor.

**The CT850 2020 manual has no speed sensor replacement procedure at all.** Its Part Replacement
Guide runs 11-1 to 11-7 and stops at the incline motor, so there is no 2020 card to compare this to.
