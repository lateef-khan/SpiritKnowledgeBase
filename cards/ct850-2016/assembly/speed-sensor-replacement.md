1. First remove the motor top cover.
2. Remove the speed sensor wiring and proceed with the parts replacement.
3. After replacement, **a test to check if the sensor registers the magnet is required**.

That is the whole procedure. It names no tool, no screw and no gap.

**Seven books print these three lines word for word**: the CT850 2016 (section 12.7, headed "including the
wire"), the 4.0T 2026 medical treadmill (9.7), the XT185, XT285, XT385 and XT485 2015 service manuals (12.8)
and the XT485ENT (12.8). The XT285 and XT485ENT books print "(including the wire)" in the heading too; the
XT385 and XT485 books do not. This card covers all seven machines.

**Where the gap is printed differs by machine.** On the CT850 2016 the **magnet-to-sensor gap is in the LOW
SPEED troubleshooting**, which says the gap must be **less than 3 mm** and says where the sensor sits and how
to align it - that half is on `ct850-2016-low-speed-check-rpm-sensor`. On the XT machines the gap, where a
book prints one, is in the E1 error chapter. Set the gap from the right card after fitting the new sensor.

**The CT850 2020, CT800 2020, CT800ENT and CT850ENT manuals have no speed sensor replacement procedure at
all** - their Part Replacement Guide stops at the incline motor. The XT 2023 books print a one-line version
without the magnet test, on its own card.

The **ST8700A-ST026-01 revision** of this book (`spirit-treadmill-40t-2026-service-manual-st8700a`, cover "4.0T-740885 (ST8700A-ST017-01)") prints this procedure word for word - its chapter 9 measures 99.8% the ST017 chapter at word level, one space apart.
