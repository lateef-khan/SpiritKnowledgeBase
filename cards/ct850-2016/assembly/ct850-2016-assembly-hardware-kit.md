**Step two lists six of items 152 and 153. Every other manual on this platform
lists five.** That is the only figure on this card that differs from the CT850-2020,
CT800-2020 and CT800ENT tables; all twelve other rows are identical to theirs.

## Hardware for step 1

| Part | Type | Description | QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 150 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 3/8" x 3" | 10 |
| 133 | Split Washer | Ø10 x 2T | 10 |
| 115 | Flat Washer | Ø3/8" x 35 x 2.0T | 10 |
| 144 | Phillips Head Screws | M5 x 12mm | 4 |

## Hardware for step 2

| Part | Type | Description | QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 152 | Socket Head Cap Bolt | M8 x 12mm | **6** |
| 153 | Flat Washer | Ø8 x Ø16 x 2T | **6** |
| 151 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 3/8" x 3/4" | 6 |
| 133 | Split Washer | Ø10 x 2T | 6 |
| 125 | Flat Washer | Ø3/8" x Ø25 x 2.0T | 6 |

## Hardware for step 3

| Part | Type | Description | QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 154 | Button Head Socket Bolt | 3/8" x UNC16 x1-1/4 | 2 |
| 133 | Split Washer | Ø10 x 2T | 2 |
| 155 | Curved Washer | Ø10 x Ø23 x 1.5T | 2 |
| 29 | Round Cap | *(no description printed)* | 1 |

The Round Cap is a part, not a fastener, and it is listed in the hardware table
rather than in the carton list.

## Hardware for step 4

| Part | Type | Description | QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 156 | Socket Head Cap Bolt | M8 x P1.25 x 20L | 4 |
| 120 | Split Washer | Ø8 x 1.5T | 4 |
| 153 | Flat Washer | Ø8 x Ø16 x 2T | 4 |
| 144 | Phillips Head Screw | M5 x 12mm | 2 |

## Where a step uses fewer than the table lists

These are the manual's own numbers, reproduced as printed:

| Table | Table says | The step uses |
|---|---|---|
| Step 1, items 115, 133, 150 | 10 each | 8 - four sets a side |
| Step 2, items 152 and 153 | 6 each | **6 - three a side. This is the one step that balances.** |
| Step 4, item 144 | 2 | 1 |

The manual does not acknowledge either gap.

**Do not answer a step-two question about a 2020 or CT800ENT machine from this
card.** Those tables read five, their steps say two a side, and the mismatch runs
the other way.
