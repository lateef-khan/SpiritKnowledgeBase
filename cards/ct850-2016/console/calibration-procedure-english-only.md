**This manual prints the calibration procedure twice, with different speed limits.** This is the
copy inside the incline troubleshooting section. The other copy, section 8.2 on p. 47 (printed 46),
offers **Metric or English** and sets **maximum 20.0 kph / minimum 0.8 kph**. Every other step is
word for word the same. Read both before you commit a value.

1. Remove the safety key.
2. Press and hold down the **INCLINE** and **SPEED** buttons with one hand and replace the safety key
   with the other. Keep holding both keys until the window displays **"Factory settings"**, then
   press **Enter**.
3. Set the display to show **English** settings with the UP/DOWN or FAST/SLOW key, then press Enter.
   This copy offers English only; the section 8.2 copy offers Metric or English.
4. Make sure the **wheel size diameter is 2.98**, then press Enter.
5. Adjust the maximum speed to **12.0 mph**, then press Enter. No "(if needed)" here - section 8.2
   writes this step and the next one as optional.
6. Adjust the minimum speed to **0.5 mph**, then press Enter.
7. The message window shows **"INCLINE STEPS"**. Adjust the maximum elevation (if needed) to **15**,
   then press Enter.
8. The message window shows **"CALIBRATION ON"**. Press **START** to begin. The process is
   automatic; **the speed will start up without warning, so do not stand on the belt.** When it
   finishes it returns to IDLE MODE automatically.

12.0 mph is about 19.3 kph, not the 20.0 kph the other copy sets. 0.5 mph and 0.8 kph are the same
speed. So the two copies disagree on the maximum by about 0.7 kph and agree on the minimum.

This copy is step 1 of the incline troubleshooting sequence: run calibration, and if it fails work
through the wiring and voltage checks that follow it.
