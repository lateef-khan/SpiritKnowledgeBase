**The minimum speed here is 0.5 and the maximum is 15.0.** The CT850-2020 owner's manual prints the
same eight steps with a minimum of **0.3**: `ct850-2020-console-calibration-minimum-speed-0-3`. The
CT800-2020 and CT900 routine sets a maximum of **12.0** (`ct900-calibration-procedure`), and the
CT800 2012 and 2016 routine adds a ninth Grade return step and holds Start with **Fast +** instead
(`spirit-ct800-console-calibration-with-grade-return`). Running one machine's numbers on another is
the classic mistake.

1. **Remove the safety key.**
2. **Press and hold down the Start and Speed Up buttons and replace the safety button.** Continue to
   hold Start and Speed Up until the window displays **"Factory settings"**, then press **Enter**.
3. Set the display to show **Metric or Imperial** settings (Meters vs. Miles) with the **Up** or
   **Down** button, then press **Enter**.
4. Make sure the **wheel size diameter is 2.98**, then press **Enter**.
5. Adjust the **minimum speed** (if needed) to **0.5**, then press **Enter**.
6. Adjust the **maximum speed** (if needed) to **15.0**, then press **Enter**.
7. Adjust the **maximum elevation** (if needed) to **15**, then press **Enter**.
8. **Press Start to begin calibration.** The process is automatic; **the speed will start up without
   warning, so do not stand on the belt.**

Step 1 says safety key and step 2 says safety button; they are the same part.

This manual prints no adjustable range for any of these values and does not say what to do when
calibration fails. Ranges for this console are printed in the CT850-2020 service manual:
`ct850-2020-factory-setting-ranges`.

**The CT850-2016 service manual prints two other calibration routines for this same machine**, both
entered with INCLINE and SPEED rather than Start and Speed Up, and both setting a maximum of 12.0
mph or 20.0 kph: `ct850-2016-calibration-procedure-metric-or-english` and
`ct850-2016-calibration-procedure-english-only`. **Three documents therefore give this machine three
different maxima - 15.0 here, 12.0 mph and 20.0 kph there - and two different entry gestures.**
Nothing in any of them resolves it. Read all three before you commit a value.
