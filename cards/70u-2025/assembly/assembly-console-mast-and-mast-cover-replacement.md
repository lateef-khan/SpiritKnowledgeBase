**Three screws on each side hold the mast, and three brackets come off it first.** The 7.0R mast takes six bolts and has no cable hold-down bracket.

1. Refer to 6.1 to remove the console.
2. **Remove the steel cover and the bracket of the manual brake lever.** As shown in figure 6.4.1, take off the **2 screws** to remove the **steel cover of the manual brake lever** first. As shown in figure 6.4.2, take off the **2 screws** to remove the **bracket of the manual brake lever** on the mast.
3. **Remove the bracket of the brake cable hold down.** As shown in figure 6.4.3, take off the **two screws** to remove the bracket of the brake cable hold down.
4. **Remove the console mast.** As shown in figure 6.4.4, take off the **3 screws on both sides of the mast** to remove the mast.
5. Refit by following steps 1 to 4 in reverse order.

**Console mast cover (6.5).** Refer to 6.4 to remove the console mast, then, as shown in figure 6.5.1, take off the console mast cover to remove it; refit in reverse.

No screw sizes are printed. **The owner's manual bolts the same mast with seven bolts, not six**: seven 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts (51), six 5/16" flat washers (72) on the six side bolts and one 5/16" curved washer (99) on the front bolt - so "3 screws on both sides" leaves the front bolt uncounted. That book fits the brake lever (133) with two 6 mm Phillips screws (144), its cover (151) with two 4 mm screws (106-2), and the **two brake cable tie downs (136) with two 5 mm screws (166)**, which is the bracket of step 3.
