**6.2.1 Remove the adjustment knob.** As shown in figure 6.2.1, remove the **screws on both sides**. As shown in figure 6.2.2, **release the knob counterclockwise** to replace it. (Please refer to step 6.4 to remove the side cases.)

**6.2.2** Refit by following 6.2.1 in reverse order.

No screw size or count is printed. The cross-reference is wrong as printed: **6.4 is the console mast procedure; the side cases are 6.8**. The parts list carries the knob as **Adjustment Knob, Seat Vertical (106)** with a **Barrel Nut (106-1)** and **four M4 x 5L Phillips head screws (106-2)** - the owner's manual's hardware panel shows two of those screws (106-2) in its step two. The seat post procedure and the right side case procedure both begin by removing this knob; the troubleshooting chapter sends a console mast that sways left and right to "step 6.2 knob and quick release locking".
