**Belt tension 260 to 280 N, set on the eye-bolt nuts.** The 7.0R recumbent on the same template prints 220 to 240 N; the 4.0U prints 180 +/- 10 Hz on a J-bolt. Neither figure is this machine's.

1. Refer to the shroud procedure to remove the side case (6.8).
2. **Remove the eye bolt and tension spring**: as shown in figures 6.9.1 and 6.9.2, **loosen the nuts on the eye bolt** to remove the eye bolt and the tension spring at the same time.
3. Refit: refer to figures 6.9.3 and 6.9.4 and follow step 2 in reverse order. **Then use the nuts of the eye bolts to adjust the belt tension. The belt tension should be in 260N to 280N.** Then refit the side case (6.8).

**Drive belt (6.9.2).** Remove the side case (6.8), remove the eye bolt and tension spring as above, and the drive belt can be removed. On refit, as shown in figures 6.9.5 and 6.9.6, **the drive belt should be kept in the middle of the groove of the drive pulley and the pulley of the induction brake**, then refit the side case (6.8).

Neither book says how the tension is measured or names a gauge. The troubleshooting chapter sends a slipping or shedding belt back to this step to tighten the nut. The parts list carries the eye bolt as **M6 x P1.0 x 57 mm Eye Bolt, Idler Spring Tension (147), 2 pcs** and the springs as **Tension Spring, Idler Assembly (137), 2 pcs**.
