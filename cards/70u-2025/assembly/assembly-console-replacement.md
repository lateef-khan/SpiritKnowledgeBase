**6.1.1 Remove the electronic console.** As shown in figure 6.1.1, **remove the cover of the handle bar first**. As shown in figure 6.1.2, remove the **four screws from the bottom of the console**. As shown in figure 6.1.3, unplug the connectors of the wires to remove the electronic console.

**6.1.2** Refit by following 6.1.1 in reverse order.

No screw size and no connector count are printed. The owner's manual fits the console with **two M5 x 12 mm bottom screws (58) first** and then the **cover plate (12) with two top screws (58)** - four M5 x 12 in all - and the handle bar cover is its **Cover, Handle Bar (105)**, aligned last in step three. The 7.0R has no handle bar cover to take off.
