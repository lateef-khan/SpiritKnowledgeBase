**6.8.1 Side case (L).** As shown in figures 6.8.1 and 6.8.1.1, take off the **round disk**. As shown in figures 6.8.2 and 6.8.3, **loosen the 10 screws** on the side case (L) to remove it. Refit by following 6.8.1.1 to 6.8.1.2 in reverse order.

**6.8.2 Side case (R).** Refer to 6.2 to remove the **adjustment knob**. As shown in figures 6.8.4 and 6.8.4.1, take off the round disk. As shown in figure 6.8.5, take off the **screw on the main frame** first. As shown in figure 6.8.6, **loosen the 3 screws** on the side case (R). As shown in figure 6.8.7, **unplug 3 wires** in the front and inside the side case (R) to remove it. Refit by following 6.8.2.1 to 6.8.2.3 in reverse order.

No screw sizes are printed and the procedure does not say to take the crank arms off - the round disks come off around them. The parts list carries **Side Case (L) (36)**, **Side Case (R) (37)**, **Round Disk Cover (85), 2**, seven Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet metal screws (63), one Ø3.5 x 20 mm sheet metal screw (64) with a 3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T flat washer (57), and a **Support Bracket, Side Case (13)**. On this machine the controller, the power supply and the reed sensor all sit behind the side case, so every one of those procedures begins here. The 7.0R recumbent splits the same covers into front and rear shrouds with different counts.
