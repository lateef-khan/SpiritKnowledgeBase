Ten letters on a line drawing headed PARTS OF YOUR UPRIGHT BIKE:

| Letter | Part |
|---|---|
| A | Console |
| B | Pulse Grips |
| C | Accessory Pocket |
| D | Manual Mechanical Brake |
| E | Seat |
| F | Fore/Aft Seat Adjustment |
| G | Vertical Seat Adjustment Knob |
| H | Crank Arm |
| I | Rear Stabilizer |
| J | Pedal |

Eight of the ten are the 4.0U's lettered set; this diagram swaps the 4.0U's tablet rack and water bottle holder for an **accessory pocket** and the **manual mechanical brake**. It does not label the console mast, the levelling glides or the power input, which the 7.0R recumbent's diagram does. The service manual's component drawing numbers fourteen parts of the same machine, differently; that is a specs card.
