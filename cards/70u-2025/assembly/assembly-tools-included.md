Four captioned drawings in the TOOLS column of the parts-included panel:

| Item | Tool |
|---|---|
| 93 | Phillips screwdriver (captioned "ψ8 x 140L") |
| 100 | 12/14 mm wrench |
| 117 | 13/15 mm wrench |
| 165 | Short Phillips screwdriver |

**No Allen key**, and nothing in the build takes one: the mast, handle bar and stabilizer bolts are hex head and the rest are Phillips screws. The short screwdriver is for the brake lever cover in step two - "the top screw needs to be tightened with the short screwdriver". Item 93's caption is its shank size, 8 mm by 140 mm, not a name; the service manual's parts list calls it the Phillips Head Screw Driver (93) and carries 100, 117 and 165 under the same numbers.

The 7.0R recumbent carton holds eight tools, including two Allen wrenches this build has no use for; the 4.0U carton holds these three sizes plus a 6 mm L Allen wrench for its handlebar bolts. **A 14mm is in this carton** in the 12/14 mm, the size the machine care page asks for on the levellers; the setting-up page asks for a 1/2" wrench for the same nut.
