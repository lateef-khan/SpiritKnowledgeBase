**Seat post (6.6).** Refer to 6.2 to remove the adjustment knob. As shown in figure 6.6.1, **pull out the seat post** to remove it. Refit by following the steps in reverse order.

**Seat post cover (6.7).** Refer to 6.6 to remove the seat post. As shown in figure 6.7.1, **take off the seat post cover upward** to remove it. Refit in reverse.

That is the whole of both procedures: no fasteners beyond the knob's screws, no rubber hammer, no spacer. The CU800 and 4.0U inner slide comes out around a centre spacer with two sheet-metal screws; not this machine. The parts list carries **Seat Post (6)**, **Seat Post Cover (39)**, a **Sleeve, Seat Post (107)** and an **End Cap, Seat Post (114)**.
