One boxed panel headed PARTS INCLUDED, hardware on the left by step and tools on the right. The captions give item number, size and count and no part-type words.

## Step 1

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 50 | 3/8" x 2-1/4" | 4 |
| 71 | 3/8" x 25 x 2T | 4 |

## Step 2

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 51 | 5/16" x 5/8" | 7 |
| 72 | 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T | 6 |
| 99 | 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T | 1 |
| 106-2 | M4 x 5L | 2 |
| 144 | M6 x 15L | 2 |

Seven mast bolts with six flat washers and one curved washer (99), then the two 4 mm screws for the brake lever cover and the two 6 mm for the lever itself. **The two 5 mm screws (166) that step two fits to the brake cable tie-downs are not pictured in any step.**

## Step 3

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 51 | 5/16" x 5/8" | 2 |
| 72 | 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T | 2 |
| 103 | 5/16" x 1.5T | 2 |

The CU800's step-three table - two bolts, two flat washers, two split washers.

## Step 4

| Item | Size | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| 58 | M5 x 12L | 10 |

Ten of item 58: two bottom console screws, two top screws through the cover plate, four for the stabilizer covers, two for the bottle holder. **No torque figure is printed anywhere.** The service manual's parts list carries the same numbers: 50 (4), 51 (9 - seven plus two), 72 (8), 99 (1), 103 (2), 106-2 (4), 144 (2), 58 (17) and the unpictured 166 (2).
