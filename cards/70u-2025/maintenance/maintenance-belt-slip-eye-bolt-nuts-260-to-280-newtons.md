**This is the 7.0U figure, 260 to 280 N at the nuts of the eye bolts. The 7.0R of the same year, on the same
spring-loaded idler, sets its belt to 220 to 240 N** (`70r-2025-maintenance-belt-slip-eye-bolt-nuts-220-to-240-newtons`),
and the CU900 upright to 180-210 N (`cu900ent-belt-slips-or-falls-off`). Forty newtons separate the two 2025
medical bikes; do not carry one onto the other.

7.1 Slip/Shedding of Drive Belt, as printed: "If you find the belt slipping or falling off, please refer to Figure
7.1.1 and Figure 7.1.2, and **follow step 6.9.1.3 to tighten the nut to adjust the tension**."

Step 6.9.1.3, the install half of the eye-bolt replacement, gives the figure:

1. The idler is spring-loaded on **two eye bolts** (parts-list item 147, M6 × P1.0 × 57 mm) with **two tension
   springs** (item 137). Removing the belt means loosening the nuts on the eye bolts and lifting eye bolt and
   spring out together (6.9.1.2).
2. To tension, refit and **"use the nuts of the eye bolts to adjust the belt tension. The belt tension should be
   in 260N to 280N."**
3. The drive belt sits in the middle of the groove of the drive pulley and of the induction brake's pulley
   (6.9.2.3); the idler wheel assembly is locked counterclockwise on refitting (6.9.3.5).

The side case comes off first (6.8) and no gauge is named; the figure is a force. **No new-belt figure, no
stretch allowance and no interval** is printed.

A rough feeling underfoot is the next section, 7.2 - lock the pedal, then check the crank
(`cu900ent-noise-and-feet-feeling`); a seat or console mast that swings is 7.3
(`70u-2025-maintenance-sway-seat-screws-and-console-mast-knob-and-quick-release`).
