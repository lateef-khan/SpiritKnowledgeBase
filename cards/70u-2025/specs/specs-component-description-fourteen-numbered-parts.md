The **Side Cases (4, 5)** are the drive housing; the replacement chapter takes ten screws out of the
left one and three out of the right, and it is behind the right one that the three wires to the
controller are unplugged. The **Lever, Manual Brake (12)** is the flywheel stop on the mast, adjusted
at its steel cable when the felt pad wears. The **Adjustment Knob, Seat Vertical (9)** locks the seat
post in the mast; the seat's fore-aft knob is a separate part on the parts list (86).

**No part numbers, quantities or dimensions are on this page.** The recumbent 7.0R numbers thirteen
components (`70r-2025-specs-component-description-thirteen-numbered-parts`).
