**Only one quantity disagrees: item 164** - two `Ø3/8" × Ø19 × 1.5T_Flat Washer` in the owner's manual, four `Ø 10 × Ø 25 × 0.8T_Nylon Washer` here. The two books are naming different hardware at the same number.

**The names that help on the phone**: item 23 is the crank `Reed Sensor` (the owner's manual's `Speed Sensor`); item 122 is the `Induction Brake` (the owner's manual's `Flywheel`); item 161 is a **hospital-grade** power cord; items 129-136 are the manual brake by function - bracket, felt pad, lever bracket, lever, torsion spring, cable hold-down bracket; item 147 is the idler spring-tension eye bolt.
