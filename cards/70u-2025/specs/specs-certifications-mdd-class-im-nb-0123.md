**The block, exactly as printed:**

> CAN/CAS-C22.2 No. 60601-1:14
> ANS/AAMI ES60601-1:2005+A2 (R2012) +A1
> IEC 60601-1-2:2014
> EN 60601-1-2:2015
> IEC 60601-1:2015+A1:2012
> EN 60601-1:2006+A1:2013+A12:2014
> EN ISO 20957:2013
> MDD 93/42/EEC Class Im, NB: 0123

(*CAN/CAS* and *ANS/AAMI* are the page's spellings; *IEC 60601-1:2015+A1:2012* is printed with the
amendment year before the base year.)

**Same directive and class as the 7.0R, different editions.** The 7.0R-2025 page cites the 2007 EMC
standards and no notified body (`spirit-med-70r-specs-certifications-mdd-class-im-2007-editions`);
this page cites 2014/2015 and names **NB: 0123**. Both are MDD Class Im - Class I with a measuring
function - and neither cites ISO 14971 or the MDR that the 8.0U and 8.5R print
(`spirit-med-8-bike-specs-certifications-eu-mdr-class-i`).

The same page's Disposal line reads "Reference should be made to local regulations concerning the
disposal of this product at the end of useful life." No certificate number, UDI or FDA listing is
printed; the EMC declaration follows on the next page (`spirit-med-bike-specs-emc-declaration`).
