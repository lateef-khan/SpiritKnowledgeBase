| Figure | Printed |

| **Length** | **43.3" (110 cm)** |
| **Width** | **22.4" (57 cm)** |
| **Height** | **54.3" (138 cm)** |
| **Weight** | **117.5 Lbs. (53.3 Kgs)** |

All four convert correctly. **Height is the dimension to plan around** - the upright is taller than
it is long, unlike the recumbent 7.0R at 140 cm long and 119 cm high
(`70r-2025-specs-dimensions-and-weight`). The seat post telescopes in the mast, so 138 cm is the
machine as photographed, not the seat at its highest.

**The page has no text layer for these rows.** A text search of the manual finds only the
certifications from this page; the figures above were read off the rendered page, and the
supplement's *10 cm* is an OCR loss of a digit, not a printed value.

No shipping weight or carton size is printed. The 8.0U upright on the touchscreen platform prints a
different and improbable set of figures (`80u-2025-specs-dimensions-and-weight`).
