These are the balloon numbers on the owner's manual's exploded view, a flat drawing stamped `20251201`. **No Spirit part number is printed.** 124 rows are printed and the numbers run 1 to 185; 62 whole numbers are absent, gaps rather than a missing page. Item 106 has a sub-row 106-2 (its screws).

**Item 48 is printed as a number with no description and no quantity** - a blank row on the page, not an extraction loss. Nothing in the book says what it is.

**The service manual (MU470-SB018) prints a list with the same numbers and different words** - `Reed Sensor W/Cable` for `Speed Sensor W/Cable`, `Induction Brake` for `Flywheel`, `Power Cord, Hospital Grade` for `Power Cord`, and 70 rows in all - plus 6 rows this list lacks (`70u-2025-specs-parts-service-manual-list`). The quantities agree wherever both print a row except item 164. Quote the book you are reading from.

## What the list names

- Drive and brake: 14 `Drive Belt`, 15 `Drive Pulley`, 122 `Flywheel`, 123 `Brake Controller`, 124 `Switching Power Supply`, 27 `AC Input Module`, 161 `Power Cord`, 23 `Speed Sensor W/Cable`, 22 `Magnet`.
- The manual brake on the handlebar: 134 `Release Lever`, 131 `Brake Pad`, 133 `Lever Fixing Plate`, 135 `ChenChin Torsion-Spring`, 149 `Steel Cable`, 151 `Iron Cover`.
- Cables by length: 153 and 154 brake-coil harnesses (200 mm, 250 mm), 155 and 157 computer cables (1650 mm, 1850 mm), 159 `1400mm_Connecting Wire,Power Cord`, 160 `600mm_Connecting Wire`, 158 and 181 ground wires.
- Tools packed as items: 93 `Phillips Head Screw Driver`, 100 `12/14mm_Wrench`, 117 `13/15mm_Wrench`, 165 `Short Phillips Head Screw Driver`. No separate tool list is printed.
