**260 N to 280 N**, a force, set by turning the nuts on the idler assembly's **eye bolts** after the
eye bolts and tension spring are refitted (6.9.1.3). The slipping-belt remedy (7.1) sends you back to
the same step: "follow step 6.9.1.3 to tighten the nut to adjust the tension." No gauge, frequency or
pound figure is printed.

**The recumbent 7.0R on the same electrical platform is set lower - 220 to 240 N** - which is the
figure the CR900 recumbents also use (`spirit-cr900-specs-drive-belt-tension-220-240-newtons`). Do
not carry the upright's figure onto the recumbent or the reverse; the two books print the sentence
identically apart from the numbers.

When the belt goes back on it "should be kept in the middle of groove of the drive pulley and the
pulley of the induction brake" (6.9.2.3). The idler wheel assembly is refitted by locking it
counterclockwise (6.9.3.5) and the induction brake by pushing it forward to the end
(6.9.4.6).
