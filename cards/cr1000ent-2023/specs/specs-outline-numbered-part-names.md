One drawing of the recumbent, printed landscape on a portrait page, with a 27-row item table.
**The same shape of page as the CU1000ENT's twenty items, with different parts** - this is the
recumbent's seat, rails and beam (`cu1000ent-2023-specs-outline-numbered-part-names`).

| No. | Description | No. | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Screen Cover(Top) | 15 | Front Stabilizer Cover |
| 2 | Screen Cover(Bottom) | 16 | Rear Stabilizer Cover (L) |
| 3 | Keyboard cover | 17 | Rear Stabilizer Cover (R) |
| 4 | Console Chin Cover (Front) | 18 | Cap |
| 5 | Console Chin Cover(Rear) | 19 | Aluminum Axle End Cap |
| 6 | Handgrip Side Cap | 20 | Seat Back Cover |
| 7 | Console Mast Cover-L | 21 | Drink Bottle Holder (R) |
| 8 | Console Mast Cover-R | 22 | Drink Bottle Holder (L) |
| 9 | Front Shroud (L) | 23 | Handpulse W/Cable Assembly-L |
| 10 | Front Shroud (R) | 24 | Handpulse W/Cable Assembly-R |
| 11 | Beam Cover | 25 | Pedal |
| 12 | Rear Shroud (L) | 26 | Release Lever |
| 13 | Rear Shroud (R) | 27 | AC switch cover |
| 14 | Block | | |

The leaders group items that share a location: (1, 2) the screen covers, (4, 5) the chin covers,
(7, 8) the mast covers, (9, 10, 27) the front shrouds with the AC switch cover between them,
(12, 13) the rear shrouds, (16, 17) the rear stabilizer covers, (21, 22) the bottle holders and
(23, 24) the two hand-pulse grips on the seat arms, each supplied with its cable.

These are cover and trim names, not a parts list: **no part numbers and no quantities** are
printed, the book has no exploded view, and no dimension or weight appears on the page. The
**Beam Cover (11)** is the long cover over the seat-rail beam; the **Block (14)** is the seat-rail
stop. The electrical pages of this book are the CU1000ENT's, extended to this machine
(`cu1000ent-2023-specs-electrical-configuration-tft-console-and-hybrid-generator`,
`cu1000ent-2023-specs-circuit-diagram`).
