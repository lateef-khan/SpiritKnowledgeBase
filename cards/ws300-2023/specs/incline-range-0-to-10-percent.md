The WS300 book prints **Incline Range: 0 - 10%** in its *Display and Key Distribution Specs* list, and the Incline Up
and Incline Down keys "When the Treadmill is running, press the button to increase [decrease] the incline". Item M on
the Parts of your Treadmill drawing is the incline. No number of levels is printed - only the percentage range - and
no book prints a specification table.

The WS200, which shares every other figure on this page, prints no incline line and has no incline keys; that absence
has its own card.
