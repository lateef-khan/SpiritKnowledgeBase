**If voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more, the performance of your bike may be affected. Such conditions are not covered under your warranty.**

If you suspect the voltage is low, **contact your local power company or a licensed electrician for proper testing**.

Warranty exclusion 3 says the same thing from the other side: damage from external causes including **inadequate power supply** and **power surges** is not covered.
