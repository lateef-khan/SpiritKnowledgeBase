**The hand pulse sensors are not medical devices.** Various factors, including the user's movement, may affect the accuracy of heart rate readings. The pulse sensors are intended only as **exercise aids in determining heart rate trends in general**.

This manual gives **no troubleshooting for a hand pulse that reads nothing**, and it never says where the sensors are. The repo's shared bike card `sole-bike-hand-pulse-no-reading` carries a cause list, but it is built from the 2016 to 2019 Dyaco bike service manuals and does not list this machine.
