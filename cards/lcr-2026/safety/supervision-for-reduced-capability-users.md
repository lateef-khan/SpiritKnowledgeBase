**This appliance is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge**, unless they have been given supervision or instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible for their safety (page 2).

Two more medical cautions are printed elsewhere in the manual:

- Before the fitness test (page 18): **check with your physician before performing any exercise if you are over the age of 35 or have any pre-existing health conditions.**
- With the heart rate material (page 23): **consult your physician before participating in any exercise program.**
