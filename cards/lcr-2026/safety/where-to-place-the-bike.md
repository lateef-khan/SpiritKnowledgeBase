- Install the bike on a **flat level surface**.
- **Do not operate the bike on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet.** Damage to both the carpet and the bike may result.
- **Do not use outdoors.**
- **Never expose the bike to rain or moisture.** It is not designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.

Levelling matters after setup too: if squeaks or other noises persist, the maintenance page tells you to check that the unit is properly levelled before calling the service department.

Where you put it also decides whether you keep your warranty. Exclusion 10 voids the warranty if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment, and names a garage, outside and a carport as examples.
