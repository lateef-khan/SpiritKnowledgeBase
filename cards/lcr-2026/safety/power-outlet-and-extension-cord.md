- Install the bike on a **flat level surface with access to a 115-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet, with only the bike plugged into that circuit**.
- **Do not use an extension cord unless it is 14AWG or better, with only one outlet on the end.**
- **Do not attempt to disable the grounded plug by using improper adapters, and do not modify the cord set in any way.** The manual warns that a serious shock or fire hazard may result along with computer malfunctions.
- **Disconnect the bike from the electrical outlet before cleaning and/or service work**, to reduce the risk of electric shock.

**Two supply figures are printed in this one manual.** Page 2 asks for a 115-volt, 15-amp outlet. Page 3, Grounding Instructions, says the product "is for use on a nominal 110-volt circuit". The manual never reconciles them, and neither does this card.

The repo's shared bike card `sole-bike-power-outlet-requirements` says 120-volt, 15-amp and an extension cord of 16 AWG or better. That card is built from the 2016 to 2019 Dyaco bike manuals and does not list this machine. The figures above are what this machine's own manual prints.
