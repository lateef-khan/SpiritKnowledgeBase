| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Operating temperature | **40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit** |
| Humidity | **95% non-condensing** (no water drops forming on surfaces) |

**Never expose this bike to rain or moisture.** It is not designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.

**The manual prints this paragraph twice, word for word**, once on page 3 under Important Electrical Instructions and again on page 4 under Important Operation Instructions.

This is an operating specification, not a warranty rule. **Warranty exclusion 10 is stricter** and voids the warranty if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment. A garage that stays between 40 and 120 degrees still fails exclusion 10.
