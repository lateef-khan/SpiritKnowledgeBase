- **NEVER operate this fitness bike without reading and completely understanding the results of any operational change you request from the computer.**
- **Use caution while participating in other activities while pedaling**, such as watching television or reading. **These distractions may result in serious injury.**

The manual puts these next to two console facts worth knowing before you ride: resistance changes happen gradually, and the console keys need only light finger pressure.
