**This product must be grounded.** If the bike should malfunction or break down, grounding provides a path of least resistance for electric current, reducing the risk of electric shock. The bike has a cord with an **equipment-grounding plug**, which must be plugged into an appropriate outlet that is properly installed and grounded in accordance with all local codes and ordinances.

**DANGER - improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric shock.** Check with a qualified electrician or serviceman if you are in doubt as to whether the product is properly grounded.

**Do not modify the plug.** If it will not fit the outlet, have a proper outlet installed by a qualified electrician.

**Temporary adapter.** The product is for use on a **nominal 110-volt circuit** and has a grounding plug. A temporary adapter may be used to connect this plug to a **2-pole receptacle** if a properly grounded outlet is not available. Use the adapter **only until a properly grounded outlet can be installed by a qualified electrician**. The **green colored rigid ear-lug**, or the like, extending from the adapter **must be connected to a permanent ground such as a properly grounded outlet box cover**. Whenever the adapter is used, **it must be held in place by a metal screw**.

**The 110-volt figure here does not match page 2**, which asks for a 115-volt, 15-amp outlet. Both are printed in this manual.
