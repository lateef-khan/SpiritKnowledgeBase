**Access video classes from the "Videos" tab of the bottom navigation bar.**

**Video classes are pre-recorded, manually controlled workouts of fixed duration.** There are hundreds of them.

**Seven activity types are named:** Elliptical, Yoga, Stretching, Boxing, Sculpt, Meditation and Full Sweat.

**Filter classes by instructor, duration, difficulty level and so on.**

**The additional tab menu at the top of the page features Collections** - sequences of video classes that follow a certain theme.

**"Elliptical" is printed as one of the seven types in this recumbent bike's manual, and no cycling type is listed.** Reproduced as printed.
