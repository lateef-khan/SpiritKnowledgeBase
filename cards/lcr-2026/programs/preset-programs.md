**Nine programs**, which matches the "nine different programs" the heart rate page on page 23 refers to.

| Program | What the manual says |
|---|---|
| **Manual** | A timed workout with resistance and incline **controlled manually at any time during the workout**. |
| **Hill** | Follows a **preset resistance and incline changing pattern**. |
| **Fatburn** | Follows a preset resistance and incline changing pattern. |
| **Cardio** | Follows a preset resistance and incline changing pattern. |
| **Strength** | Follows a preset resistance and incline changing pattern. |
| **Interval** | Follows a preset resistance and incline changing pattern. |
| **HIIT** | A customisable interval program - see `lcr-2026-hiit-program`. |
| **Heart Rate** | Follows your heart rate - see `lcr-2026-heart-rate-program`. |
| **Custom** | Fully customisable patterns you draw - see `lcr-2026-custom-program`. |

**Hill, Fatburn, Cardio, Strength and Interval share one sentence in the manual and are not described individually.** There is **no profile chart, no segment table and no difference stated between them** anywhere in this document. Do not tell a customer what shape any of the five follows.

**For those five, "The Total Time and Max Resistance settings define the duration and overall intensity of your workout."**

A **fitness test** is also on the Programs page, and **Templates** is a tab on the same page. Neither is one of the nine.

Every mention of incline above is printed on a machine whose manual describes no incline mechanism. See `lcr-2026-does-the-bike-have-incline`.
