**HIIT is a customisable interval program.** You manually choose:

- **the duration of the sprint interval**
- **the duration of the rest interval**
- **the resistance of the sprint interval**
- **the resistance of the rest interval**
- **the total number of intervals**

**The manual sets no limits** on any of those - no minimum or maximum interval length, no cap on the number of intervals.

**HIIT is not the same program as Interval.** Interval is one of the five preset profile programs and follows a fixed pattern you cannot edit. See `lcr-2026-preset-programs`.
