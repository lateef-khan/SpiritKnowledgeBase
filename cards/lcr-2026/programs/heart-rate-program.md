**This manual says two different things about what this program controls, and never resolves them.**

- **Page 17:** "Heart Rate program uses **incline** to control your heart rate. **The incline gradually increases until you reach your target heart rate, then adjusts automatically to keep you within 5 bpm of your goal.**"
- **Page 23:** "The Heart Rate program automatically controls **resistance at the pedals**."

Both are printed in this manual. **Do not tell a customer which one the machine does**; this bike's manual describes no incline mechanism at all. See `lcr-2026-does-the-bike-have-incline`.

Everything else about the program is stated only once and is not disputed:

- **A heart rate monitor is required for this program.**
- On the program setup page you can **either set the target bpm directly, or select 60% or 80% of your age-predicted maximum heart rate** and let the machine calculate the target.
- **If you choose the percentage option, make sure the machine knows how old you are**, otherwise the calculation is based on the default values.
- **The program finishes when the time is up, or if your heart rate is 20% higher than the target.**

A heart rate source can be a **Bluetooth chest strap**, a **Garmin watch** through the Garmin view, or the **hand pulse sensors**, which the manual warns are not medical devices.
