**Templates are preset programs with a preserved set of parameters.** The tab menu at the top of the Programs page holds them.

Two ways to make one:

- **Save a program as a template upon completion.**
- **Create one from scratch in the Templates tab:** tap the **"New Template"** button, **choose your base program**, then **save it with adjusted properties**.

- **A maximum of 12 template programs can be stored.**
- **To remove unwanted templates, press "Edit" in the Templates tab.**

**A template is not a favourite.** A favourite is a bookmark to a program or class; a template stores a program together with its parameters. Each has its own limit of 12.
