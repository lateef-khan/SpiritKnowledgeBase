Hardware for this step: **#71 hex head bolt (2), #77 flat washer (4), #89 nyloc nut (4), #175 hex head bolt (2)**.

1. **Install the Seat Handle Bar (6) onto the Seat Carriage (4)** with 2 pcs of Hex Head Bolts (71), 2 pcs of Hex Head Bolts (175), 4 pcs of Flat Washers (77) and 4 pcs of Nylon Nuts (89), **using the Wrench (112) and the 14/15mm Wrench (132)**.
2. **Plug the Resistance Connecting Cable (150) into the front socket located on the left rear cover.**

**Two different bolt lengths go into this one joint** - the 3/8" x 1-3/4" (71) and the 3/8" x 2-3/4" (175), two of each. The manual does not say which pair goes in which hole.

The location in item 2 is printed as written: **"the front socket located on the left rear cover"**.
