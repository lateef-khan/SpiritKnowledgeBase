Hardware for this step: **#98 M6 screw (4), #99 M5 screw (4), #105 sheet metal screw (4), #76 flat washer (4), #196 star washer (4), #205 button head socket bolt (4)**.

1. **Install the Seat (61) on the Seat Carriage (4)** with 4 pcs of Phillips Head Screws (98), using the Phillips Head Screwdriver (114).
2. **Install the Computer Cable (44), Switch Cable (Upper) (148) and Computer Cable (186) into the connector in the back of the Center Console (190).** Install the Center Console (190) onto the mounting plate with **4 pcs of Phillips Head Screws (99) and 4 pcs of Star Washers (196)**, using the Phillips Head Screwdriver (114). **Be careful not to pinch the wires between the Center Console and the mounting plate. This could short the wires and damage the electronics.**
3. **Connect the Computer Cable (186) to the Console Assembly (19).** Install the Console Assembly (19) onto the mounting bracket with **4 pcs of Button Head Socket Bolts (205) and 4 pcs of Flat Washers (76)**, using the L Allen Wrench (115). Then **install the Rotate Axle Mast Cover (182) onto the Console Assembly (19)**.
4. **Install the Drink Bottle Holders (L/39, R/38) onto the Seat Handle Bar (6)** with 4 pcs of Sheet Metal Screws (105), using the Phillips Head Screwdriver (114).
5. **Install the Pedals (L/116, R/117) in the Cranks (51L, 51R)** using the Wrench (132). The left pedal is reverse threaded - the full pedal instruction has its own card, `lcr-2026-pedal-thread-direction`.

**The hardware callout on this page is headed "HARDWARE STEP 3"**, but the parts it lists are the page 6 **Hardware Step 4** parts. Assembly step 3 on page 9 uses the real hardware step 3.

**Two different Phillips screws are used and they are easy to swap:** the M6 x 15mm (98) holds the seat and the M5 x 12mm (99) holds the Center Console.
