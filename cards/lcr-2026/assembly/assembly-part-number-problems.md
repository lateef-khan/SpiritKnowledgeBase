Four places where the assembly pages contradict themselves. **Every card in this set reproduces the text as printed; nothing below has been corrected in the other cards.**

1. **Step 1, item 2 asks for "six of Hex Head Bolts (167)".** No hardware list in this manual contains an item 167. Hardware step 1 lists **#176. 3/8" x 3/4" Hex Head Bolt (6 pcs)** - the only six-piece hex head bolt in the pack, and the only unused item in that step. The manual does not say 167 is a typo for 176.
2. **Step 1, item 1 calls item (65) a "Hex Head Bolt".** Both hardware lists call **#65 a 3/8" x 53L Carriage Bolt**. It is the same item number in both places, described two ways.
3. **Step 1, item 3 says to insert an end cap (32) into each opening of the Front Stabilizer Tube**, inside a step titled "Rear Stabilizer & Seat Back". **End cap 32 appears in no hardware list**, and **no assembly step in this manual installs a front stabilizer.**
4. **The hardware callout on page 10 is headed "HARDWARE STEP 3"** while listing the page 6 **hardware step 4** parts (98, 99, 105, 76, 196, 205). The real hardware step 3 is consumed by assembly step 3 on page 9.

Two smaller inconsistencies:

- Washer **#76** is printed as "5/16" x 18 x 1.5T" on page 6 and "5/16" x 18mm x 1.5T" on page 9.
- The page 5 and 6 checklist gives **no quantity for #76 in hardware step 3**; the page 9 callout gives 6 pcs.

**What does add up:** hardware step 3's 8 hex head bolts (68) split 6 + 2 across assembly step 3 items 1 and 3, and its 6 flat washers (76) split 4 + 2. Hardware steps 2 and 4 are consumed exactly.
