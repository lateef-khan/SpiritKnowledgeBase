**Install the Pedals (L/116, R/117) in the Cranks (51L, 51R) using the Wrench (132).**

- **The left pedal (116) has a reverse thread** and screws into the crank in the **opposite rotation from normal threads**.
- **There is an "L" stamped into the end of the threaded post of the left pedal and an "R" in the right.** Use the stamp, not guesswork.
- **Tighten the pedals as much as you possibly can.**
- **It may be necessary to re-tighten the pedals if you feel a thumping during pedaling.**
- **A clicking noise, or thumping sound, during pedaling is usually caused by the pedals being too loose.**

The General Maintenance page on page 25 gives a different pair of causes for the same noise - loose assembly hardware in general, and dirt build-up on the rear rails. Neither list mentions the other, so work through both. See `lcr-2026-noise-when-pedaling`.
