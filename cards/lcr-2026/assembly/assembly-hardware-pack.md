| #205 | 5/16" x 1/2" Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |

The page 5 and page 6 checklist prints **no quantity for #76 in hardware step 3**; the 6 pcs above is the figure repeated in the page 9 callout for that step, and it matches the 4 + 2 the two instructions consume. Everything else above is the printed quantity.

**Two washers are easy to mix up:** #76 and #77 are both flat washers but different sizes, and #82 (split), #83 (curved) and #196 (star) are three different washer types. Check the item number, not the shape.

Several part numbers in the instruction text do not agree with these lists. See `lcr-2026-assembly-part-number-problems` before you start.
