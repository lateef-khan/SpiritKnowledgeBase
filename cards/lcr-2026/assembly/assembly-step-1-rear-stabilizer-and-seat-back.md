Hardware for this step: **#65 carriage bolt (2), #77 flat washer (6), #84 flat washer (2), #176 hex head bolt (6), #191 cap nut (2)**.

1. **Install the Rear Stabilizer (7) onto the Main Frame (1)** with the two Hex Head Bolts (65), two Flat Washers (84) and two Cap Nuts (191), **using the 12/14mm Wrench (112)**.
2. **Install the Seat Back Bracket (5) onto the Seat Carriage (4)** with the six Hex Head Bolts (167) and six Flat Washers (77), **using the 12/14mm Wrench (112)**.
3. **Insert an end cap (32) into each opening of the Front Stabilizer Tube.** You may need to tap them in with a rubber mallet if they are tight.

Three problems in this step, reproduced above exactly as printed:

- **Item 2 asks for bolt (167). No hardware list contains a 167.** The only six-piece hex head bolt in the pack is **#176. 3/8" x 3/4" Hex Head Bolt (6 pcs)**.
- **Item 1 calls (65) a "Hex Head Bolt". Both hardware lists call #65 a Carriage Bolt.**
- **Item 3 fits a Front Stabilizer Tube in a step titled "Rear Stabilizer & Seat Back".** End cap 32 appears in no hardware list, and no step in this manual installs a front stabilizer.
