Hardware for this step: **#68 hex head bolt (8), #76 flat washer (6), #82 split washer (2), #83 curved washer (2), #207 button head socket bolt (4)**.

1. **Install the Console Mast Cover (31) onto the Console Mast (2)**, in the correct orientation. **Feed the Computer Cable (44) and Switch Cable (Upper) (148) through the bottom of the Console Mast (2) and out through the opening at the top.** Then install the Console Mast (2) into the receiving tube of the Main Frame (1) with **6 pcs of Hex Head Bolts (68), 4 pcs of Flat Washers (76) on the sides of the tube and 2 pcs of Curved Washers (83) on the front of the tube**, using the 12/14mm Wrench (112). **Make sure not to pinch the wire cable, as damage to the electronics could occur.**
2. **Feed the Computer Cable (186) from the TV Rack Assembly (155) through the Console Mast (2)** and pull it out through the top opening. Then **install the TV Rack Assembly (155) onto the Console Mast (2) with 4 pcs of Button Head Socket Bolts (207)**, using the L Allen Wrench (115).
3. **Install the Mast Handle Bar Assembly (3) onto the Console Mast (2)** with **2 pcs of Hex Head Bolts (68), 2 pcs of Flat Washers (76) and 2 pcs of Split Washers (82)**, using the 12/14mm Wrench (112).

**The counts add up:** the 8 hex head bolts (68) split 6 in item 1 and 2 in item 3, and the 6 flat washers (76) split 4 and 2. Do not use all eight bolts in item 1.

**Three cables run up this mast** - Computer Cable (44), Switch Cable Upper (148) and Computer Cable (186) from the TV rack. All three land in step 4.

Page 6 spells washer #76 as "5/16" x 18 x 1.5T" and this page spells it "5/16" x 18mm x 1.5T". Same item number.
