Four tools are supplied in the hardware package, and the instructions call them by item number:

| Item | Tool |
|---|---|
| **#114** | Phillips head screwdriver |
| **#115** | L Allen wrench |
| **#112** | 12/14mm wrench |
| **#132** | 14/15mm wrench |

The manual says to **remove the tools from the hardware package first**, before taking out any hardware.

**The supplied wrenches may not be enough.** The maintenance page says that all bolts installed during assembly need to be tightened as much as possible, and that **it may be necessary to use a larger wrench than the one provided** if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently.
