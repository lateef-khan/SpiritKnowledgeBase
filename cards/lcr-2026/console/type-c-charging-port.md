Charge a personal device during a workout from the **on-console Type-C port**.

1. **Make sure the fitness equipment power is on.**
2. **Connect your Type-C charging cable to the Type-C Power Port and to your device.**
3. **Check to make sure your device icon indicates it is charging.**

Notes printed with the procedure:

- **The Type-C charging cable is not included.** Make sure a compatible Type-C charging cable is used.
- **Your device's "charging" icon may or may not indicate that it is charging.** Depending on the amount of current your device requires, the icon may be off while the device is still charging, possibly at a lower charge rate.
- **The port provides up to 5Vdc / 1.5A of power** and is capable of powering Type-C devices.
- **You cannot save workout data through this port. It is for charging purposes only.**

The console drawing on page 12 labels this same port **USB CHARGER**. The manual describes only one charging port.
