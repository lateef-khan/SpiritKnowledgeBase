**On the Home page, choose from a variety of pre-installed Apps, or use the Mirroring function to cast content from your smartphone directly to the console screen.**

**Once the content is on, use the bottom panel for navigation and view settings.**

**This manual does not name a single app** and gives no mirroring steps, no supported-phone list and no app-store instructions. Do not promise a named service from this document.
