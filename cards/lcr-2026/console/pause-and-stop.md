- **Use the side control panels or the physical keys to adjust resistance and incline.**
- **Stop at any time by pressing the physical "STOP" key, or by tapping the red pause button in the bottom right corner of the screen.**
- **After 5 minutes of inactivity in the Pause mode, the workout will end automatically.**

A workout that ends on its own after a break is the documented behaviour, not a fault.

**"Incline" is printed here on a machine whose manual describes no incline mechanism.** See `lcr-2026-does-the-bike-have-incline`.
