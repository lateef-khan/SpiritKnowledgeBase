The equipment can play music wirelessly over Bluetooth.

1. **Turn on your mobile phone or tablet's Bluetooth function.**
2. **Search for the name "BT-Speaker" in your device's Bluetooth menu.**
3. **Tap to connect.**

Your device can then transmit music to the exercise equipment.

**"BT-Speaker" is the exact name the manual prints**, and it is not the name the SOLE+ app looks for. This audio link is a **separate Bluetooth connection** from the app pairing and from the heart rate chest strap.

Volume is set with the **volume control on the console panel**.
