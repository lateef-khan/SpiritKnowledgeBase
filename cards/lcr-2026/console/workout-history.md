**Press "History" on the Home page** to see your latest workout records.

- **Up to 10 records are stored locally on the console.**
- **Your full workout history is available if you link your profile to the SOLE+ mobile app.**
- **Tap a record preview to see the full summary.**

Older records disappearing from the console after ten workouts is the documented behaviour, not a fault. Link a SOLE+ account if the customer needs to keep more.
