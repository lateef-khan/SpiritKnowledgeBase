**Access community challenges by selecting the "Club" tab in the bottom navigation bar.** The events are created by other members of the SOLE community.

**To view all available events, your profile must be linked to your SOLE+ account.**

Two event formats:

| Format | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Shared goals** | Challenges with a **predefined target score**, which must be achieved **within a limited time period** to complete the challenge. |
| **Rankings** | Competitions in which users try to **earn the highest score possible within a limited time period**, to defeat as many competitors as possible. |

**The score can be represented by one of four focus parameters: calories, distance, active time and workout count.**

**Press the "filter" icon in the upper menu** to filter events by **format, duration and focus parameter**.
