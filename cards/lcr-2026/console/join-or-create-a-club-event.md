**Join a private event** with the **"Join via ID"** function: enter the event ID that was shared with you by the creator of the event, and you automatically become a participant.

**Create your own challenge** by pressing the **+ icon in the upper menu**. You choose:

- **event format**
- **focus parameter**
- **dates**
- **event name**
- **an optional description**
- whether the event is **private or public**

**Privacy, printed as a condition of taking part:** "By joining your first event, you agree to share your **username, profile image, and workout data (such as workout dates, distances, active time, and calories)** with other participants."

The manual does not say how to leave an event or delete one you created.
