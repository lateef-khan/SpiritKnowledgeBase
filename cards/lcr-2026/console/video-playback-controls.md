Video classes use the same bottom panel and the same three view layouts as any other media.

- **Navigate through the video by sliding on the progress bar.**
- **Skip 10-second intervals** by pressing the skip icons.
- **The "Pause" button pauses both your workout and the video at the same time.** You cannot pause one and leave the other running.
