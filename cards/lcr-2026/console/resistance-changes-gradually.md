**Changes in resistance do not occur immediately.** Set your desired resistance on the computer console and **release the adjustment key**. **The computer will obey the command gradually.**

A slow ramp after a key press is normal behaviour, not a fault.

**The manual gives no time figure** for how long the ramp takes, and **no resistance level count** for this machine, so there is nothing to quote about how many levels there are or how big a step is.
