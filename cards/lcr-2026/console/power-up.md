1. **Plug the bike into an appropriate wall outlet.**
2. **Turn on the power switch located at the front of the bike.**
3. **When power is connected to the bike the console will automatically power up.** There is no separate console power button.

The manual gives **no power-off instruction** other than that switch, and **no troubleshooting for a console that does not light up**.
