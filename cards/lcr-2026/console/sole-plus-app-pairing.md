The bike ships with a **Bluetooth transceiver** that lets it interact with compatible iOS and Android smartphones and tablets through the **SOLE+ App**.

1. **Download the free SOLE+ App** from the Apple Store or Google Play.
2. **Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your device.**
3. **Tap the icon at the top right corner to search for Sole equipment.**
4. **When the equipment is detected, tap Connect.**
5. **When the App and equipment are synced, the Bluetooth icon on the equipment's console will light up.**

**This is a different connection from two others on the same machine**: the **BT-Speaker** audio link (`lcr-2026-bluetooth-audio-speaker`) and the **heart rate chest strap** (`lcr-2026-bluetooth-chest-strap`). Linking the console profile to a SOLE+ account is a further, separate step done by QR code on the console (`lcr-2026-link-sole-plus-account`).

The manual gives **no troubleshooting** for an app that will not find the machine.
