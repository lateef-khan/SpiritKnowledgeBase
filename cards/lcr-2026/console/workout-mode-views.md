**Once the workout has started, after a short countdown the console appears in its workout mode.**

- **Warm-up and cool down phases may be skipped by pressing "Skip".**
- During the workout, **switch between Stats (Focus), Charts and Track views** to check your progress.
- **Go to the Media section** for media content.
- **Open Garmin** to see your Garmin smart watch real-time data.
- **In the Focus view, tap any of the number blocks to choose which parameter it displays.**

**The manual does not list which parameters are available** in the Focus blocks, and gives no Garmin pairing steps of its own. The repo card `sole-garmin-pair-touchscreen` carries the touchscreen pairing procedure.
