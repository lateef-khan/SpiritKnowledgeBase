The SOLE+ App can sync your workout data with one of the fitness cloud sites Sole supports:

- **Apple Health**
- **Google Health Connect**
- **Strava**
- **MapMyFitness**

The manual adds "with more to come" and names no others.

**There is no other route off the machine.** The Type-C port is for charging only and cannot save workout data, and the console itself keeps only the last 10 workouts.

The repo card `sole-plus-strava-sync` carries the Strava setup detail, including that **every workout must be shared by hand** - there is no automatic sync. That card is written from Sole support material rather than from this manual.
