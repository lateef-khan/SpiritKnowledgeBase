**The exercise equipment's Bluetooth device can also be connected to a Bluetooth wireless heart rate chest strap transmitter.**

**The chest strap can still connect to the equipment even though the App has already been connected to it.** The two connections do not exclude each other.

The manual **names no brand, no protocol and no pairing steps** for the strap itself, and gives no chest strap troubleshooting.

A heart rate source is not optional for one program: the **Heart Rate program requires a heart rate monitor**.
