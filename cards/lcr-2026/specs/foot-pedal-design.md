**Q factor** is the manual's name for **the distance between the pedals**. Stationary exercise bikes are wider than a road bike to make room for the braking mechanism, pulleys, drive components and plastic covers, so the pedals sit further apart.

What the manual claims for this pedal system:

- The **Q factor is the smallest in the industry**.
- The pedal is custom designed and tooled to give a **two degree inward tilt**, compensating for the Q factor not being perfect.
- Together these put the rider in **biomechanical neutral alignment**, so feet, ankles, knees and hips line up properly.

The manual credits the design to research with a leading sports scientist and physical rehabilitation expert. **It gives no number for the Q factor itself**, so there is no figure to quote.
