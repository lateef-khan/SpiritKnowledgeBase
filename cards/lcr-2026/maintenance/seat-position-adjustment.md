**You can adjust the seat position while seated.**

1. **Pull up on the lever located in front of the seat.**
2. **Slide the carriage forward or backwards.**
3. **Release the lever.**

**Correct position:** there is a **slight bend in your knee when the pedal is at its farthest position forward** - the 3 o'clock position, looking at the bike from the right side.

The manual describes **no seat height adjustment and no backrest angle adjustment** on this machine.
