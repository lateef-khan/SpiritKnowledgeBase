**Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each workout.** That is item 1 of the General Maintenance list and the only cleaning instruction for the machine as a whole.

The manual **names no cleaner, no disinfectant and no schedule** beyond "after each workout". The only chemical it names anywhere is **rubbing alcohol**, and that is for the rear rails and wheels, not for the frame or the console.

This is not optional housekeeping: **warranty exclusion 3 excludes damage from corrosion (RUST)**.
