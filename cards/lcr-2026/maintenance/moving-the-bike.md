**The fitness bike is equipped with two transport wheels, which are engaged when the rear of the fitness bike is lifted.**

That single sentence is the whole of the Transport Instructions. The manual gives **no machine weight, no lifting technique, no two-person warning and no folding** - this bike does not fold.
