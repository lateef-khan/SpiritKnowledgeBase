As printed:

- **Warranties outside of North America may vary.**
- **Sole USA warranty coverage is limited to the Continental United States.**
- **Sole USA does not sell or service equipment in Hawaii, Alaska, or outside of the United States.**
- **Contact your local dealer for details regarding warranty availability in your region.**

There is a matching practical limit in the Service section: **if no local service is available**, SOLE will repair or replace parts at no charge for parts within the warranty period, but **all transportation costs both ways are the owner's responsibility**. See `lcr-2026-return-authorization-required`.
