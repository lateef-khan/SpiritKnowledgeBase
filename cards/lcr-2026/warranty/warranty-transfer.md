**"Warranties DO NOT transfer."**

Exclusion 5 excludes **products that have been sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party**. A second owner has no manufacturer's limited warranty on this machine, however much of the original period is left.

Exclusion 4 is the related rule: **products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered** are not covered either.

The warranty is tied to the original purchaser's registration and proof of purchase. See `lcr-2026-warranty-registration`.
