**Fitness bike warranty, effective February 1, 2023** - that is the date printed in this 2026 manual, reproduced as printed.

| Component | Residential (all service in-home) | Light commercial (all service in-facility) |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame Weldments** | **Lifetime** | **Lifetime** |
| **Parts** | **3 Years** | **2 Years** |
| **Labor** | **1 Years** | **1 Year** |
| **Cosmetic Items\*** | **90 Days** | **90 Days** |

\* **Cosmetic items including, but not limited to: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.**

"1 Years" in the residential column is how the manual prints it.

**The period runs from the date of retail sale**, as determined by sale receipt, **or in the absence of a sales receipt, eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date**. See `lcr-2026-warranty-start-date`.

**SOLE's responsibilities** are **providing new or remanufactured parts, at SOLE's option**, and technical support to independent dealers and servicing organizations. **In the absence of a dealer or service organization, these warranties are administered by SOLE directly to the consumer.**

**Whether light commercial use is allowed at all is disputed inside this manual.** See `lcr-2026-who-and-what-use-is-covered`.
