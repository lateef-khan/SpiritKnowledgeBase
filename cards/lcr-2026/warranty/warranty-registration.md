**Record the serial number** of the product in the space provided on page 1 of the manual.

**Two ways to register:**

1. **The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to SOLE.**
2. **Register online at www.soletreadmills.com under the support tab.**

**The warranty section adds a deadline that page 1 does not mention:** the card must be returned to the address listed on it **within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty. **Outside the 10 day period the warranty can still be registered if you have proof of purchase** - receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation.

**Exclusion 6 is why this matters:** products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at SOLE are not covered, and **SOLE reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists** for the product.

**The manual does not say where the serial number label is on the machine.** It only provides a blank space to write it in.

The front pages also ask you to record **the name of the dealer, the dealer's telephone number and the purchase date**.
