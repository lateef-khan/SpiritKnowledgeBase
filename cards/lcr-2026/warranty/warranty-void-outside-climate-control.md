**"Warranty will be void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment. (Examples: Garage, outside, carport, etc)"**

Two things this rule is **not**:

- **It is not the operating specification.** Pages 3 and 4 give an operating range of **40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit and 95% humidity, non-condensing**. A garage that stays inside that range still fails exclusion 10, because the rule is about the space being climate-controlled and insulated, not about the numbers.
- **It sets no temperature figure of its own.** The manual gives none for this rule.

The related exclusion is item 3, which excludes damage from **corrosion (RUST)** and from **water damage**.
