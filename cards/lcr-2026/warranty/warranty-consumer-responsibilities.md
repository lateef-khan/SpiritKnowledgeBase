The consumer or light commercial facility is responsible for these six items, as printed:

1. **The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within 10 days of the original purchase** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty, **or, if outside the 10 day period, the warranty can be registered if you have proof of purchase** (receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation).
2. **"Proper use of the treadmill in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual, including general maintenance (i.e. belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under motor cover, and lubrication. See all components under General Maintenance section)."**
3. **Expenses for making the fitness bike accessible for servicing**, including any item that was not part of the fitness bike at the time it was shipped from the factory.
4. **Damages to the fitness bike finish during shipping, installation or following installation.**
5. **Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual.**
6. **Provide pictures or video of any issues via email as requested by the service technician. Assist with troubleshooting/diagnosing prior to a home visit.**

**Item 2 is treadmill text printed in a bike manual.** It names belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under a motor cover and belt lubrication. **This bike's General Maintenance section on page 25 contains none of those.** What it does contain is wiping the sweat path, cleaning and lubricating the rear rails and polyurethane wheels, and checking that the unit is levelled. Reproduced above as printed; the bike's real maintenance is in `lcr-2026-wipe-down-after-each-workout` and `lcr-2026-noise-when-pedaling`.
