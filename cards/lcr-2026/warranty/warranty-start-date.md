**Parts and the periods in the warranty table** run **from the date of retail sale, as determined by sale receipt**. **In the absence of a sales receipt, from eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date.**

**The Service section states the labor period separately:** "**Twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale or eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number** establishes the labor warranty period should service be required."

Two things follow from that:

- **Keep your bill of sale.** It is what fixes the start date.
- **If service is performed, it is in your best interest to obtain and keep all receipts.**

**The serial number is the fallback.** Where there is no bill of sale, the factory shipping date is determined from the serial number.
