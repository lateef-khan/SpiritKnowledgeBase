**The manual answers this question two different ways and never reconciles them.**

**The unnumbered ATTENTION page before page 1:**
"THIS FITNESS BIKE IS INTENDED FOR **RESIDENTIAL USE ONLY** AND IS WARRANTED FOR THE APPLICATION. **ANY OTHER APPLICATION VOIDS THIS WARRANTY IN ITS ENTIRETY.**"

**Page 26, Normal Responsibilities of the Consumer:**
"This warranty applies only to products in **ordinary household use or in a non-dues paying, light commercial facility with 5 hours fitness bike use or less per day**."

Page 26 is supported by two more passages on the same page: the warranty table carries a full **LIGHT COMMERCIAL WARRANTY** column with its own periods, and **exclusion 8** excludes only "product use in any environment other than a **residential or light commercial (as defined above)** setting".

**Pick no winner from this manual.** One page says any non-residential application voids the warranty entirely; the rest of the warranty is written around a light commercial tier with a 5 hour per day limit.

**A dues-paying facility is outside both readings.** The light commercial tier is explicitly non-dues-paying.
