**You must wear the heart rate transmitter strap for these programs.**

Both programs operate the same and are programmed the same way; the only difference is the target. **HR1 is set to 60% and HR2 is set to 80% of the maximum heart rate.** After you select your heart rate target the program attempts to keep you at or within **3-5 heart beats per minute** of that value. Follow the prompts in the Message Center to maintain your selected heart rate value.

1. Press the **HR 1** key (60% of max heart rate default) or the **HR 2** key (80% of max heart rate default), then press **Enter**.
2. The Message Center asks for your **Age**. Enter it with the Speed + / - keys, then press Enter.
3. Enter your **Weight** with the Speed + / - keys, then press Enter.
4. Adjust the **Time** with the Speed + / - keys, then press Enter.
5. Adjust the **Heart Rate Target** — the heart rate level you will strive to maintain during the program — with the Speed + / - keys, then press Enter. *The heart rate that appears is based on the % you accepted in step 1; changing this number increases or decreases that percentage.*
6. Press **Start** to begin your workout, or **Stop** to go back and modify your settings.
7. To increase or decrease the workload at any time during the program, press the **Speed + / -** key on the console or the **right handlebar**. This changes your target heart rate at any time during the program.
8. During the HR 1 or HR 2 programs you can scroll through the data in the Message Center by pressing **Enter**.
9. When the program ends you may press **Start** to begin the same program again, or **Stop** to exit the program.

**On the XT385, XT485 and XT685 the workload is changed from the Incline keys on the left handlebar instead**, and those manuals print no step 9 — see the linked card. This console has two separate HR keys; the XT485 and XT685 have one HR 1-2 key.

**The 2010 XT685 matches this console on the Speed keys, the right handlebar and the step 9**, but has one HR 1-2 key rather than two, and its step 9 adds a third option — saving the finished workout to a User key. See `xt685-2010-programs-heart-rate-program-speed-keys`.
