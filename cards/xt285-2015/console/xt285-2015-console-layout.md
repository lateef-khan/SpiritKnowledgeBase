The console overview diagram labels these parts:

- **Large LCD with scrolling feedback and scrolling message center**
- **Integrated Speakers for MP3 Player**
- **Audio Jack**
- **Swivel Fan to keep you cool**
- A bank of **program keys** ("Eight programs offer a variety of work-outs")
- **Select Keys Control Speed & Incline**
- **Easy-Touch Control Keys**

The manual also describes, in its text rather than on the diagram, **speed and incline controls on
the side handrails** and a **switch that disables them** - see
`xt-2015-console-handrail-controls-and-disable-switch`.

**The diagram labels speakers and an audio jack, but this manual prints no Speakers paragraph**, so
it never says whether the console has a volume control. The XT385-2015, XT485-2015 and XT685-2015
manuals do say theirs has none - see `xt-2015-console-speakers-and-audio-jack`.

**This console has no muscle activation figure and no heart rate percentage bar graph.** The diagram
labels neither and the manual describes neither.

**This is the 2015 panel, not the 2023 one.** The 2023 XT285 console is labelled with a USB port and
a handrail lock and has no Select keys - see `xt285-2023-console-layout`.
