Hardware: **#71** Hex Head Bolt (2 pcs), **#77** Flat Washer (4 pcs), **#89** Nyloc Nut (4 pcs), **#175** Hex Head Bolt (2 pcs).

1. Install the Seat Handle Bar (6) onto the Seat Carriage (4) with **two vertical Hex Head Bolts (71)**, **two horizontal Hex Head Bolts (175)**, four Flat Washers (77) and four Nyloc Nuts (89), using the **12/14mm Wrench (112)** and **14/15mm Wrench (132)**.
2. Plug the two Hand Pulse Cables (21) and (27) into the rear two ports (26) located on the **left side rear cover, just under the seat** — see the inset illustration.
