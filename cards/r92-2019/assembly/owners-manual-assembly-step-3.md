Hardware: **#68** Hex Head Bolt (8 pcs), **#76** Flat Washer (6 pcs), **#82** Split Washer (2 pcs), **#83** Curved Washer (2 pcs).

1. Install the Console Mast Cover (31) onto the Console Mast (2). Make sure the plastic cover is in the correct orientation.
2. Feed the twist tie attached to the Computer Cable (44) and Hand Pulse Cable (45) through the bottom of the Console Mast Tube (2) and out through the opening at the top.
3. Install the Console Mast Tube (2) into the receiving tube of the Main Frame (1) with six Hex Head Bolts (68), **four Flat Washers (76) on the sides** of the tube and **two Curved Washers (83) on the front**, using the **12/14mm Wrench (112)**. Pull gently upwards on the cable ends to prevent pinching the cables, because damage to the electronics could occur.
4. Remove the white Styrofoam pad, factory installed to prevent bolts from being accidentally dropped into the bike.
5. Remove the plastic tie from the centre of the handlebar (3). Install the Mast Handle Bar Assembly (3) onto the Console Mast (2) with two Hex Head Bolts (68), two Flat Washers (76) and two Split Washers (82), using the **12/14mm Wrench (112)**.
