Hardware supplied, by assembly step. The number in `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #65 | 3/8" x 53L | Carriage Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #77 | 3/8" x 19mm x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #191 | 3/8" x16 x 12.5T | Cap Nut | 2 pcs |
| #84 | 3/8" x 25mm x 2T | Flat Washer | 2 pcs |
| #176 | 3/8" x 3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 6 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #71 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #77 | 3/8" x 19mm x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| #89 | 3/8" x 7T | Nyloc Nut | 4 pcs |
| #175 | 3/8" x 2-3/4" | Hex Head Bolt | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 3**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #68 | 5/16" x 5/8" | Hex Head Bolt | 8 pcs |
| #76 | 5/16" x 18mm x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| #82 | 5/16" x 1.5T | Split Washer | 2 pcs |
| #83 | 5/16" x 19mm x 1.5T | Curved Washer | 2 pcs |

**Hardware step 4**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #98 | M6 x 15mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| #99 | M5 x 12mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| #105 | 4 x 16mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |
| #192 | 3.5 x 12L | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |

**Item numbers are not shared with the B94.** On the B94, #71 is a flat washer and #99 is a curved washer; here #71 is a hex head bolt and #99 is a Phillips head screw. Always use the list for your own model.
