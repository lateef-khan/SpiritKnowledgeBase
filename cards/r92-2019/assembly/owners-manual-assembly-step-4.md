Hardware: **#98** M6 x 15mm Phillips Head Screw (4 pcs), **#99** M5 x 12mm Phillips Head Screw (4 pcs), **#105** 4 x 16mm Sheet Metal Screw (4 pcs), **#192** 3.5 x 12L Sheet Metal Screw (4 pcs).

1. Install the Seat (61) on the Seat Carriage (4) with four Phillips Head Screws (98), using the Phillips Head Screwdriver (114).
2. Plug the Computer Cable (44) and Hand Pulse Cable (45) into the connector in the back of the Console Assembly (19). Install the console onto the mounting plate with four Phillips Head Screws (99). **Be careful not to pinch the cables between the console and the mounting plate — this could short the cables and damage the electronics.**
3. Install the Drink Bottle Holders (39 L, 38 R) onto the left and right sides of the Seat Handle Bar (6) with four Sheet Metal Screws (105).
4. Install the Pedals (116 L, 117 R) in the pedal cranks using the **14/15mm Wrench (132)**. **The Pedal (116 L) has a reverse thread** and screws into the crank opposite to right hand threads. There is an "L" stamped into the end of the threaded post of the left pedal and an "R" in the right. Tighten the pedals as firmly as you possibly can.
5. Install the Tablet Holder (190) to the back of the console with four Sheet Metal Screws (192).

**A clicking noise or thumping sound while pedalling is usually caused by loose pedals.** It may be necessary to re-tighten them. The maintenance section repeats this and adds the crank arm nut and levelling as the other causes.
