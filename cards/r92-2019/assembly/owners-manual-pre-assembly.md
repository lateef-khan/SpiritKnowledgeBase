1. Using a razor knife (box cutter), cut the outside bottom edge of the box along the dotted line. Lift the box over the unit and unpack.
2. Carefully remove all parts from the carton and inspect for any damage or missing parts. If damaged or missing parts are found, contact your dealer immediately.
3. Locate the hardware package. **Remove the tools first.** Remove the hardware for each step as needed to avoid confusion.

The numbers in parenthesis (#) throughout the instructions are the item number from the assembly drawing.
