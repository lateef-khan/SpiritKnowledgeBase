**Causes**

- Not paired correctly.
- Battery is low or dead.

**Solution**

- Try to pair again.
- Replace the battery. The switch uses a coin battery; take off the plastic cover
  to reach it.

**Check the battery is in the right way round.** The lights tell you:

| Lights | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Blue and red flashing at the same time | the battery is **not** installed correctly |
| Blue only | the battery is installed correctly and the switch is ready to pair |
| Blue flashing five times | pairing succeeded |

The seminar points at a chart of other flashing sequences. **That chart is an
image and its contents could not be extracted**, so the three rows above are the
only sequences this knowledge base holds.
