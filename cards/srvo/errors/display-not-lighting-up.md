1. Turn the SRVO off and on using the power switch on the unit.
2. Listen for the **cooling fans**.
   - **Fans not running**: no electricity is reaching the unit. Check the machine
     is plugged in and the power switch is on.
   - **Fans running but the display is dark**: go to step 3.
3. Engage resistance and pull a cable.
   - **Resistance works properly**: the display screen is defective.
   - **Resistance does not work properly**: this points to a problem with the
     control board.

Contact customer service for further assistance in either case at step 3.
