The encoder is on the controller module. The seminar marks it as **number 4** in
its diagram.

There are **two controllers** in the SRVO.

The seminar identifies the encoder only by pointing at a numbered photograph. The
photograph and its number key are images, so this card cannot say what parts 1,
2, 3 and 5 are. Open the seminar PDF at page 49 to see them.
