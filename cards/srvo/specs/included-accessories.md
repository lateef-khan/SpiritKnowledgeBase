The SRVO Strength Trainer ships with:

- SRVO Strength Trainer
- Long Bar
- Single-Hand Grips (2)
- Strength Belt
- Ankle Straps (2)
- Bluetooth Remote

The Bluetooth Remote is the same part the seminar elsewhere calls the wireless
switch or wireless button.

The SRVO Multi-Angle Bench is a separate product with its own accessory pack. It
is not in this box.
