Part number **004.039.0052708**, listed once in the key component list. The machine has two of them.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | 98 × 98 × 167 mm |
| Type | DC motor, servo motor |
| Voltage | 220 V |
| Current | 3.4 A |
| Speed | 4000 rpm |
| Torque | 24 N×m |
| Ratio | 6:1 |
| Efficiency | 80% |

The printed row also carries a bare **5** between the 6:1 ratio and the 80%, with no unit or label. It is reproduced here as a fact of the source; the manual gives nothing that says what it measures.

The row ends: "overseas motor, optical brining, motor body with shielding cover, motor with ground wire, magnetic ring, LCJD". The ground wire is the one that has to be released during disassembly step 2.

**The 220 V rating is the motor, not the wall socket.** The power module in the same list takes 90 to 130 V in and puts 0 to 330 V out.
