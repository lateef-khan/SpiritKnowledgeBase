Parts called out on the machine:

- **Cables** - the two pull cables.
- **Speaker** - plays the audible announcements.
- **Wheels** - for moving the unit.
- **Power Switch & Outlet** - the mains switch and socket.
- **Controls** - the control panel.

The control panel shows:

- **Mode** - the training mode number, in the top left corner.
- **Weight Display** - the resistance figure.
- **Control Button** - the knob and start/stop button.
- **Bluetooth/Wi-Fi** - connection indicators.
- **Rep Counter** - counted repetitions.

The seminar labels these on a photograph. It does not print the exact position
of each label, so this card lists what is labelled, not where each label points.
