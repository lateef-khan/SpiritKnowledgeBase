The SRVO Multi-Angle Bench is a separate product made to work with the SRVO
Strength Trainer.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Back pad angles | 5 |
| Safety rack height settings | 16, quick change |
| Folded height | 9.25" |
| Padded contact points with the SRVO | 6 |
| Total load capacity | 530 lb |

The accessory pack holds a Short Bar, Extension Cable (3 sets), a Tricep Rope and
Carabiner Clips (2).

The bench folds flat for storage and has onboard wheels for moving it while
folded. The 6 contact points hold it in position on the SRVO Strength Trainer,
which the seminar says widens the range of workouts and adds stability.

The bench and its accessories are not in the SRVO Strength Trainer box.
