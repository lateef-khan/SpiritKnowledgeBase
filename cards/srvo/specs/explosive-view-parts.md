The counts worth remembering: **two reels modules, two controllers, two cooling fans, two speakers, four shock pads, one full range power board.**

The drawing the numbers point at is an image and did not survive text extraction, so this card gives the names and counts but cannot say where each number sits on the machine.

There is no servo motor row in this list even though the machine has two. The motor appears in the key component list instead.
