The SRVO Strength Trainer is a compact cable machine. Two commercial-grade
servomotors and a calculation system simulate weight instead of using a stack of
plates. The sales sheet calls it "the ultimate compact home workout solution".

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Resistance per cable | 4 to 132 lb |
| Resistance, both cables | 264 lb total |
| Smallest resistance step | 1 lb |
| Training modes | 3 |
| Connectivity | Bluetooth and Wi-Fi |
| Footprint | 20.6" x 41.5" x 5" |
| User weight capacity | 330 lb |
| Accessory pack | included |

The SRVO is sold under two SKUs: **578712** and **578722**.

The three training modes are Standard, Eccentric and Isokinetic. Each has its
own card.
