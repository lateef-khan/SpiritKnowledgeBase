The service manual's outline drawings number sixteen parts across the top, bottom and left side views.

| No | Part | View |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Display Panel | top |
| 2 | Rope Hatch | top |
| 3 | Left End Cover | top |
| 4 | Right End Cover | top |
| 5 | Frame | top |
| 6 | Foot Board Panel | top |
| 7 | Shock Pad | bottom |
| 8 | Wheels | bottom |
| 9 | Back Cover | bottom |
| 10 | AC Switch and Socket | left side |
| 11 | Lifting Handle Cover | left side |
| 12 | Display Module | left side |
| 13 | Servo Motor Module | bottom, without back cover |
| 14 | Cooling Fan | bottom, without back cover |
| 15 | Controller | bottom, without back cover |
| 16 | Full Range Power Supply | bottom, without back cover |

The manual splits these across two pages: 1-9 on the top and bottom views, 10-16 on the left side view and the bottom view with the back cover off. The View column above is read from which drawing each number is grouped under; the manual does not tag the rows itself.

The **Rope Hatch** is where the two cables leave the machine. Parts 13 to 16 are only reachable once the back cover is off.

A separate card covers the labels the SRVO seminar puts on the control panel readouts.
