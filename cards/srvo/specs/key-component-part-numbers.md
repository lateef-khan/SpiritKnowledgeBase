**Three rows share one part number and two rows share one description. Check with the parts desk before ordering from this table.**

- `004.049.0052894` appears on rows 5, 7 and 14. Row 5 calls it a low voltage PFC output power cable; rows 7 and 14 call it the PC260 power, 485 communication and fan cable.
- Rows 9 and 11 print word-for-word the same speaker description under two different part numbers, `004.028.0052625` and `004.057.0054999`.

Line breaks and stray spaces from the PDF have been closed up; every digit, unit and code above is as printed. Note also that the SRVO knowledge base carries a policy card saying individual parts are not replaced on this machine - a whole unit is sent instead. This list has not been reconciled with that policy.
