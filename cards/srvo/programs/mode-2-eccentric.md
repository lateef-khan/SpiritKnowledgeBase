**This is Eccentric mode, shown as Mode 2. It is not Standard (Mode 1) and not
Isokinetic (Mode 3).**

Resistance is greater during the eccentric phase of the exercise, when the cable
retracts, than during the concentric phase. **The concentric phase is 70% of the
eccentric phase resistance.**

The SRVO app states the same rule as a ratio: the number you set is the eccentric
(pull-back) resistance, and its ratio to the concentric (pull-out) resistance is
**10:7**.

Claimed benefits:

- Greater gains in strength, muscle mass and neural adaptations (Reeves et al., 2009).
- Improved calorie burning and flexibility.

Set the weight with the knob, then press start/stop to engage the resistance.
