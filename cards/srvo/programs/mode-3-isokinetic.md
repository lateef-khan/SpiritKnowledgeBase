**This is Isokinetic mode, shown as Mode 3. It is not Standard (Mode 1) and not
Eccentric (Mode 2).**

The pulling speed stays constant and the resistance adjusts by itself. You choose
between fast and slow instead of setting a weight. On the control panel the knob
selects **slow (0)** or **fast (1)**, and that 0 or 1 shows in the weight display
area. Press start/stop to engage the resistance.

Claimed benefits:

- Complete muscle stimulation through the range of motion.
- Part of many injury rehabilitation programs.
- More effective at improving functional performance than isometric and isotonic
  exercises (Lee et al., 2018).
- Low-velocity (slow) exercise generally increases muscle strength.
- High-velocity (fast) exercise is used to recover muscle endurance after an injury.
