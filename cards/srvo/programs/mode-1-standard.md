**This is Standard mode, shown as Mode 1. It is not Eccentric (Mode 2) and not
Isokinetic (Mode 3).**

Standard mode gives constant resistance through the whole range of motion,
similar to free weight or traditional machine-based training. The pull-out and
the pull-back resistance are the same: set 10 kg and both directions are 10 kg.

The SRVO app adds that Standard mode is **the default on startup**.

Set the weight with the knob, then press start/stop to engage the resistance.
