**Do this only after every step on the stuck cable card has failed.** The seminar
says so plainly: this is only to be done if all other methods have been
unsuccessful.

**Open the machine**

1. Remove the back cover. This exposes the two servomotors.
2. Remove the Allen head bolts holding the motor in place, with a long Allen key.
3. Remove all screws holding the plastic guard connected to the SRVO motor.

**Free the cable**

With the plastic covers off the servomotor you can see the pulley and cable
system.

1. Turn the machine on while holding the cable lightly with your other hand.
2. While the machine is starting up, try to move the pulley in both directions
   until the pulley starts to retract.
   - The hand on the cable applies a small amount of tension so the cable retracts
     smoothly.
3. Once the cable retracts correctly, put the machine back together.

The seminar gives no torque figures and no Allen key size. It also gives no
warranty guidance for opening the unit, and the separate cables-not-retracting
note says the whole SRVO is replaced rather than individual parts. Confirm with
customer service before opening a customer's machine.
