**Step 11. The manual recommends completing steps 4 and 5 - both end covers - first.**

With a Phillips screwdriver, remove the screws in the circle marked in the manual's figure.

**The manual contradicts itself on the screw count in this one sentence.** It reads: "Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the four screws in the circle. (Four on the left end cap and two on the right end cap.)" - a total of four, then a breakdown adding to six. Count the screws on the machine in front of you; the manual does not settle it.

The explosive view lists **Cooling fan, quantity 2**, one behind each end cap.
