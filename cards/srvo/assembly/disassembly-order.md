The service manual numbers eleven disassembly steps and states the prerequisite for some of them. Read down this table before you start.

| Step | What comes off | Must be done first |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Back cover | nothing |
| 2 | Motor | step 1 |
| 3 | Lifting handle cover | nothing stated |
| 4 | Right end cover | steps 1, 2 |
| 5 | Left end cover | steps 1, 2, 3 |
| 6 | Full range PFC power board | nothing stated |
| 7 | Controller | nothing stated |
| 8 | Display module | nothing stated |
| 9 | Frame | steps 1-5; step 6 recommended |
| 10 | Speaker | nothing stated |
| 11 | Cooling fan | steps 4, 5 recommended |

"Nothing stated" is the manual's silence, not permission: steps 6, 7 and 10 all reach parts that sit under the back cover, so step 1 comes first in practice.

The manual prints **no refit or reassembly sequence and no torque figures**.
