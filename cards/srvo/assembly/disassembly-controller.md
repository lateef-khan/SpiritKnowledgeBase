**Step 7. The manual lists no prerequisite, but the controllers sit under the back cover, so step 1 is needed in practice.**

1. With a **4mm Allen wrench**, remove the **four bolts** in the upper circle of **each** of the two electronic control components.
2. Pull out all the wires on the electronic control components.

There are **two** controllers, one per servo motor. The manual writes "the four bolts in the upper circle of the two electronic control components respectively", which is four bolts each, eight in total; it does not print a total, so treat the eight as read from the wording rather than stated outright.
