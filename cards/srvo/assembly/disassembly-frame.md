**Step 9. Steps 1 to 5 must be done first, and the manual recommends completing step 6 (the PFC power board) before starting.**

1. With a **10mm open-end wrench**, remove the **three nuts** on each of the two side bars - **six nuts in total**.
2. Draw the frame **outwards**.

The explosive view lists **Frame, quantity 2**, so there is one of these on each side.
