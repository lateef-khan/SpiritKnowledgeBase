**Step 4. Steps 1 and 2 - back cover, then motor - must be done first.**

1. Remove the connection terminals for the **fan** and the **speaker**.
2. With a Phillips screwdriver, remove the **four screws** marked in the manual's figure.
3. Draw the right end cover **outwards**.

**This is the right end cover. The left end cover is step 5 and has a different prerequisite list and different connectors** - the fan and the AC switch socket, not the fan and the speaker.
