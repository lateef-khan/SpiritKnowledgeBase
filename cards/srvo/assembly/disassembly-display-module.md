**Step 8. The manual lists no prerequisite for this step.**

1. With a Phillips screwdriver, remove **all four screws** of the display and control assembly.
2. Pull out all the wire connectors.

The connectors on the board behind the display are CN5 (RS485 and 12V power), CN1 (debug), and the two speaker ports. Each has its own card.
