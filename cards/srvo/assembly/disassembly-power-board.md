**Step 6. The manual lists no prerequisite for this step, but step 9 (the frame) recommends doing it first.**

1. With a Phillips screwdriver, remove the **three screws** marked in the manual's figure.
2. With a screwdriver, loosen **all** the wiring connectors in the frame.
3. Lift the PFC power board out.

The manual calls this board the **full range PFC power board** here, the **Full Range Power Supply** in the outline drawings, the **full range power board** in the explosive view, and a **Controller module** in the heading of section 8-3. They are the same board.
