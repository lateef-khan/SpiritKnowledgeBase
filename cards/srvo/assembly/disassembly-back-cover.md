**Step 1 of the service manual's disassembly sequence. Nothing has to be removed first.**

1. Unscrew the **four shock pads** in the four-corner frame **counterclockwise**.
2. With a Phillips screwdriver, remove the **14 screws** marked in the manual's figure.
3. Lift the back cover off.

The screw positions are shown only in a photograph in the manual, which did not survive text extraction, so this card gives the count and not the pattern.

Removing the back cover exposes the two servomotors, the two controllers and the full range power board. Steps 2, 4, 5 and 9 all require this step first.
