**Step 2. The back cover (step 1) must be off first.**

1. With a **6mm Allen wrench**, remove the **four screws** holding the motor - **8 in total for the two motors**.
2. Remove the **ground wire** locked on the rack.
3. Remove the wire on the **control assembly**.

That completes the removal of the motor assembly.

The manual gives no torque figures for refitting, and prints no refit sequence at all.
