Four tools cover all eleven disassembly steps in the service manual:

| Tool | Used on |
|---|---|
| Phillips screwdriver | back cover, right and left end covers, PFC power board, display module, speaker, cooling fan |
| 4mm Allen wrench | lifting handle cover, controller |
| 6mm Allen wrench | servo motor |
| 10mm open-end wrench | frame side bars |

The four shock pads at the corners of the frame unscrew by hand, counterclockwise.

This table is assembled from the eleven steps; the manual prints no tool list of its own.
