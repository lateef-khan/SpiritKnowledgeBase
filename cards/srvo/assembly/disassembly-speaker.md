**Step 10. The manual lists no prerequisite, but the speaker connector is released during step 4, the right end cover.**

1. With a Phillips screwdriver, remove the **two screws** in the circle marked in the manual's figure.

That completely disassembles the speaker.

The explosive view lists **Speaker, quantity 2** and **Speaker bracket, quantity 1**. The display main board drives them as a left channel and a right channel on two separate ports.
