**Step 5. Steps 1, 2 and 3 - back cover, motor, lifting handle cover - must be done first.**

1. Remove the connector for the **fan** and the connector for the **AC switch socket**.
2. With a Phillips screwdriver, remove the **four screws** marked in the manual's figure.
3. Draw the left end cover **outwards**.

**This is the left end cover. The right end cover is step 4**, needs only steps 1 and 2, and carries the speaker connector rather than the AC switch socket.
