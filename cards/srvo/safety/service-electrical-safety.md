The manual states plainly that **the current of the power supply and communication cable is dangerous.** To avoid the danger of electric shock:

- Do not connect or disconnect any cables, or perform installation, maintenance or reconfiguration, **during an electrical storm**.
- Connect power cords to a **properly wired and grounded** electrical outlet.
- Whenever possible, use **one hand only** to connect or disconnect signal cables.
- Never turn on any equipment when there is evidence of **fire, water or structural damage**.
- **Disconnect the attached power cords before you open the device covers**, unless the installation and configuration procedures instruct otherwise.
- Connect and disconnect cables in the order the manual gives.

One more rule sits in the manual's "Matters need attention" list: **do not remove any component housing that has a hazard label.**

The order for connecting and disconnecting has its own card.
