**This is tilt safety. It is not the resistance stop point, static safety or drop
safety.**

When one cable is pulled further out than the other, the machine drops resistance
to a minimum and pauses the workout until you reset the resistance.

**The distance and the percentage of weight reduction are adjustable in the app.**
The app screen also carries an on/off toggle for the feature. See the SRVO app
safety settings card.
