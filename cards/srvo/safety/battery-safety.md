The manual's rules, as printed:

1. **Use the same battery when replacing a battery.**
2. Battery abuse or mishandling can cause overheating, liquid leakage or an explosion. To avoid possible injury:
   - Do not short-circuit the battery, or expose it to water or other liquids.
   - Do not heat the battery above **100 degrees C (212 degrees F)**.
   - Do not open, disassemble or service any battery.

The manual gives no battery type, size or part number, and the explosive view and key component list contain no battery at all. The battery a technician actually meets on this product is the **coin cell in the wireless switch**, which has its own cards.

The second bullet is printed as a bare phrase, "Heating the battery to more than 100 degrees C (212 degrees F)", inside a list of things to avoid. It is written above as the prohibition the list clearly intends.
