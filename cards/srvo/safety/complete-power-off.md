**The power control button on the device may not completely cut off the current supplied to the device.** The manual says so directly, and adds that the device may have multiple power cords. To completely power off the device, make sure **all** power cords are disconnected.

Switching the machine off at its AC switch is therefore not enough before opening covers. Pull the plug.

The "multiple power cords" wording is the manual's general safety text. The explosive view lists **Power cord, quantity 1** for this machine, and the key component list holds one mains cord, so on an SRVO there is one cord to pull.
