The manual gives two sequences. **They are not simply the reverse of each other** - the connect list has five steps and the disconnect list has four, and power goes on last but comes off first.

**To connect**

1. Turn everything OFF.
2. First, attach all cables to devices.
3. Attach signal cables to connectors.
4. Attach power cords to outlets.
5. Turn devices ON.

**To disconnect**

1. Turn everything OFF.
2. First, remove power cords from outlets.
3. Remove signal cables from connectors.
4. Remove all cables from devices.

Use these whenever you install, move, or open covers on the machine or on anything attached to it.
