**This is drop safety. It is not the resistance stop point, static safety or tilt
safety.**

Letting go of the cable at any point activates this feature straight away and all
weight is released. After a brief period the cables retract using minimal
resistance.

The seminar gives no adjustable settings for drop safety, and the app safety
screen in the screenshots shows toggles only for static safety and tilt safety.
Cables that do not retract at all are a separate fault; see the cables not
retracting card.
