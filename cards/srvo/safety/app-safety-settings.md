Open the app, go to the **Settings** tab, then **Safety Settings** under the
**SRVO** heading. The screen is titled **Safety**.

**Static Safety** - an on/off toggle, described on screen as: "When the cable is
being held at the same position for a long time, the machine will drop resistance
to minimum and pause the workout until you are ready to start a new set." Three
settings sit under it:

| Setting | What it does | Value in the screenshot |
|---|---|---|
| Time Trigger | how long the cable must be held still | 10 sec |
| Retract After | delay before the cable retracts | 5 sec |
| Resistance Reduction | how much weight is dropped | 100 % |

**Tilt Safety** - an on/off toggle, described on screen as: "When one cable gets
pulled further out than the other, the machine will drop resistance to minimum
and pause the workout until you are ready to start a new set."

Two cautions:

- The values above are one demo machine's settings, **not factory defaults**. The
  seminar separately states that static safety triggers at ten seconds, which
  matches this screenshot's Time Trigger.
- The screenshot is cut off below the Tilt Safety toggle, so any settings under
  that toggle are not recorded. The seminar says the tilt **distance** and the
  percentage of weight reduction are adjustable, so expect rows there.
