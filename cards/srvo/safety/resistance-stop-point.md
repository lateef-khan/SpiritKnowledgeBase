**This is the resistance stop point. It is not static safety, tilt safety or drop
safety.** The SRVO has four separate safety features; each has its own card.

The stop point works as a digital safety spotter. You set the point at which the
weight begins. **Below the stop point the weight is released.**

**To set it** (same process from the SRVO controls or the wireless button):

1. Make sure resistance is **not** active. The weight display must be blinking.
2. Press the start/stop button **twice**, at a cadence of "1 Mississippi, 2
   Mississippi".
3. An audible announcement confirms it worked.

**If it does not take:**

- Check that resistance is not activated. The weight display should be blinking.
- Vary the cadence of the two start/stop presses.

**To reset it:** retract the cables to the start point, then press the start/stop
button twice.
