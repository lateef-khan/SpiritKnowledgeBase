**Name warning.** The store listing is called **SRVO** and its Android package is
`com.dyaco.srvo`. The SRVO seminar deck calls the same app the **SOLE+ App**. Use
the links above rather than searching for a name; a customer told to look for
"SOLE+" may not find the listing.
