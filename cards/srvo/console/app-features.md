Linking the app to the SRVO Strength Trainer adds:

- A library of over 150 animated exercises.
- Control of the SRVO: weight, training mode, start/stop, speaker volume and
  safety settings.
- Trainer-led workout videos.
- Curated programs, programs you create yourself, and freestyle mode.
- Firmware updates for the SRVO and Wi-Fi setup.
- History tracking, and pushing results to third-party apps.

The seminar also states the connection purpose plainly: connecting the SRVO to
the app lets you apply firmware updates, change volume levels, connect to Wi-Fi
and view additional device information.
