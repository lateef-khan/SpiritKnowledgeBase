1. Make sure resistance is **not** active. The number on the weight display must
   be flashing.
2. Press and hold the start/stop button for **three seconds** to move to the next
   mode.
3. Read the mode number in the **top left corner** of the display. It shows 1, 2
   or 3.
4. Set the level for that mode:
   - **Standard (1)** and **Eccentric (2)** - turn the knob to set the weight.
   - **Isokinetic (3)** - turn the knob to pick slow (0) or fast (1). The 0 or 1
     appears in the weight display area.
5. Press start/stop to engage the resistance.

Holding start/stop steps through the modes in turn; there is no direct jump to a
mode number.
