The app finds the SRVO on a screen called **Ble Connect**. It has a **Done**
button on the left and a **refresh** button on the top right.

- If the machine is not found the screen reads "Looks like you don't have any
  devices nearby". Press refresh to search again.
- Once paired, the SRVO appears under an **SRVO** heading on the app's
  **Settings** tab, showing the device name and a status line such as
  "Connected", with an information (i) button beside it.
- **Unpair Device** on the same Settings tab removes the machine from the app.

The screenshots show the search screen and the paired result. They do not show
the steps in between, so this card cannot list what the app asks for during
pairing.
