Settings tab, then **Preferences**, then **Workout Settings**.

**ALL WORKOUTS**

- **Default Resistance** - the weight a workout starts at.

**PRESET & FREE**

- **App Voice Announcements** - on/off.

**VIDEO CLASS**

- **Show Subtitles** - on/off.
- **Language** - subtitle language.
- **Music** - a volume slider for the class music.

The **Music** slider here is the class soundtrack in the app. It is not the SRVO
machine's speaker volume; that lives on the Device Info screen.
