The app has five tabs along the bottom. Left to right:

| Tab | What is on it |
|---|---|
| Workouts | Free Workout, plus named workouts filtered by bookmark, "Created by me" and Muscles. Tabs for ALL and SPORTS SERIES. |
| Exercises | Muscle group tiles (Arms, Shoulders, Back, Chest, Core, Glutes, Legs) and an alphabetical exercise list. |
| Classes | Trainer-led video classes, filtered by Duration and Instructors. |
| Activity | Muscle Map, History, Training Volume and One Rep Max. |
| Settings | Account, the paired SRVO, Preferences and Other. |

The **Settings** tab holds the machine itself. Under **Account** are the profile,
My Favorites, My Workouts, Reset Password and Delete Account. Under **SRVO** are
the paired machine with its (i) button, **Safety Settings** and **Unpair Device**.
Scrolling further gives **Preferences** (Units, Language, Heart Rate Monitor,
Connected Apps, Workout Settings) and **Other** (About Accessories, About
Training Modes, FAQ & Support, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy).
