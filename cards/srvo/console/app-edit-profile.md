1. Open the **Settings** tab.
2. Press the account row at the top of the **Account** section. That opens
   **Profile**, which shows USERNAME, BIRTHDAY with the age worked out from it,
   GENDER, HEIGHT and WEIGHT as read-only rows.
3. Press **Edit Profile** at the bottom.
4. Change USERNAME, BIRTHDAY, GENDER (a Male / Female switch), HEIGHT,FT or
   WEIGHT,LB.
5. Press **Save** at the top right. **Cancel** at the top left throws the changes
   away.

The photo is changed from the **Edit** overlay on the picture at the top of the
Profile screen.

Height and weight units follow the **Units** setting under Preferences; the
screenshot was taken with Units set to Imperial.
