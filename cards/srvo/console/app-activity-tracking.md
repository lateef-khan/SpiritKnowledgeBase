The **Activity** tab holds four blocks, top to bottom:

- **Muscle Map** - work distribution over the last 30 days, drawn on a front and
  a back body diagram.
- **History** - the workout records. Before any workout it reads "Your workout
  records will appear here."
- **Training Volume** - a line chart of **Weekly Load, lb** over dated weeks.
- **One Rep Max** - "Peak weight for benchmark moves", as one tile per benchmark
  move. Bar Squat and Bench Press are the two tiles visible in the screenshot.

The screenshot list of benchmark moves is cut off, so this card cannot say how
many there are in total.
