Settings tab, then **Preferences**, then **Connected Apps**.

One entry is listed on that screen: **Health Connect** - "Share calories and
workout records."

The seminar says results can be pushed to "one of the many 3rd party apps". The
screenshot shows only Health Connect. **Do not promise a customer any other named
service.** The screenshot was taken on one phone, and the list may differ by
platform, so check the customer's own Connected Apps screen before answering.
