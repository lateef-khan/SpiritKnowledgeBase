1. Dismantle **end cap (55) -> foam (50) -> covers at the centre of the handle bar (50)** to take off the **handle
   bar (10)**. *When you take off the handle bar, hook the strap (30) onto the handle bar holder* to prevent the
   strap pulling back into the main cover.
2. The **foam (50)** or **end caps (55)** can be replaced directly without dismantling the centre covers (36).
3. Use a Phillips screwdriver to loosen the **3.5 x 12 mm self-tapping screw (112)** and **four 5 x 16 mm screws**;
   the **handle bar holder (60) (61)** can then be taken off.

**The book gives the foam and the centre covers the same item number, 50**, and calls the centre covers 36 in
step 2. Those numbers are printed as-is.

The CRW800 books describe a different handle, with a controller assembly and a battery on it; see the CRW800 card.
The DW400-YR002 book is otherwise 89 % the CW800-YR001 book, and this is one of the sections that changed.
