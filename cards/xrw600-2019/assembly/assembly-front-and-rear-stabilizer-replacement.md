1. Use the **6 mm wrench** to take out the **3/8" x 3/4" button head socket bolts (120), 3 pcs**, and the
   **3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washers (122), 3 pcs**. This releases the **Front Stabilizer**.
2. Release the **Locking Knob (47)** and use a **5 mm hex key and a 14 mm open-end wrench** to loosen the
   **3/8" x 2" button head socket bolts (102)**, the **3/8" x 19 x 1.5T washer (109)** and the **3/8" x 7T nut
   (110)**; the **Rear Stabilizer (3)** can then be taken off.
3. Reverse the steps to refit the parts.

**Only the XRW600 book has this section.** The two CRW800 service manuals print no stabilizer procedure of their
own - the rear stabilizer comes off with the aluminum track there. The book prints the front bolt as "3/8"x3/4"mm
3_Button Head Socket Bolt (120)x3pcs"; the stray "mm" and "3" are in the source.
