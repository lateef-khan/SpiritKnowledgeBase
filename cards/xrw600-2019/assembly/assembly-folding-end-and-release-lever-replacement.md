**A release lever and a dual torsion spring, not a knob nut.** The CRW800 books hold the fold with a seat
adjustment knob on a 22 mm nut; this book's steps 3 onward are different.

1. Remove the **Aluminum Track (51)** and the **Pedal (L) (R)**.
2. Use the **6 mm wrench** to take apart the **M8 x P1.25 x 15L socket head cap bolts (98), 2 pcs**, and the
   **Folding End Assembly (6)**. *It is not allowed to take out both pieces of bolt (98): fit a new M8 x P1.25 x
   15L bolt (98) to lock the original position, then follow the same steps for the other one.*
3. Use a **4 mm hex key** to take off **two M6 x 10 mm button head socket bolts (101)** and **two 6 x 19 x 1.5T
   washers (90)** (figure 2), then use a **5 mm hex key and a 13 mm open-end wrench** to loosen the
   **5/16" x 2" button head socket bolts (99)** and the **5/16" x 6T nut (88)**; the **release lever set (12)** and
   the **Dual torsion spring (48)** can then be taken off (figure 3).
4. Reverse the steps to refit the parts.

Item 48 is the tension spring in the CRW800 books and the dual torsion spring here. The rear stabilizer this
lever locks has its own removal card.
