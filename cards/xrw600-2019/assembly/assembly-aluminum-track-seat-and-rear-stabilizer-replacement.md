**Different fasteners from the CRW800.** The CRW800 books free the track with six M8 x 12L button head bolts and
flat washers on a 5 mm key; this book uses **six M8 x 15 mm socket head cap bolts with 8 x 15T spring washers on
a 6 mm hex key**, and the two pulley bolts are different sizes.

1. To take off the **Aluminum track (51)**, **Seat (49)**, **Seat attaching board (16)**, **Aluminum track pulley
   (53)**, **PU wheel (54)**, **Rear folding end assembly (9)** and **Rear stabilizer (3)**, use a **6 mm hex key**
   to loosen the **six M8 x 15 mm socket head cap bolts and 8 x 15T spring washers** (figure 1).
2. Use a **4 mm hex key** to loosen **two M6 x 18 mm button head socket bolts** and the **Rear cover (18)** /
   **Seat stop cover (65)**; the seat set (49), seat attaching board (16), aluminum track pulley (53) and PU wheel
   (54) can then be taken off the aluminum track (51) (figure 2).
3. Use a 4 mm hex key to loosen **four M6 x 10 mm button head socket bolts (101)** to take off the rear folding
   end assembly (9) and the rear stabilizer (3).
4. Use the **8 mm wrench and the 14 mm wrench** to take apart the **3/8" x UNC16 x 4-1/4" socket head cap bolt
   (104)**, the **3/8" x 11T nyloc nut (106)** and the **Pulley (54)** (figure 4).
5. Use the **5 mm wrench and the 14 mm wrench** to take apart the **5/16" x 1" button head socket bolt (105)**, the
   **5/16" x 6 nyloc nut (88)** and the Pulley (54) (figure 4).
6. Use a driver to take apart the **M6 x 15L Phillips head screw (124)**, the Seat (49) and the Seat Attaching
   Board (16) (figure 5).
7. Reverse the steps to refit the parts.

**Steps 4 and 5 both take "Pulley (54)" apart** with different bolts and both point at figure 4; the 4-1/4" bolt
is the through-bolt and the 5/16" x 1" bolt is the short one. Item 54 is the PU wheel in step 1 and the pulley
in steps 4 and 5 - the book uses both names for it.
