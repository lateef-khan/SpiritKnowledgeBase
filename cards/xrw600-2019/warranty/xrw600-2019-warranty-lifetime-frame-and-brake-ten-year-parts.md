**One row, four columns.** The table has a single **Residential** row:

| Warranty | Frame | Brake | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residential | Lifetime | Lifetime | 10 Years | 1 Year |

**This manual is the only one of the five Spirit rower manuals that warrants the brake for life.** The XRW600 manual effective November 30, 2021 - the same machine, two years later - prints no Brake column at all, so a brake fault on that book falls under Parts at ten years: `xrw600-2021-warranty-no-brake-column-ten-year-parts`. Read the date off the customer's own manual before quoting Lifetime on a brake.

**There is no commercial row.** The table offers Residential and nothing else, and the manual never prints a figure for a gym, a club or a facility. What it does instead is bar the environment in its exclusion list - home or a non-dues-paying facility used five hours a day or less: `spirit-rower-warranty-use-environment`. So a facility machine is not covered on a shorter term here; on this manual it is outside the warranty.

**Labor is one year, and the SERVICE paragraph dates it differently.** That paragraph names twelve months from the bill of sale or eighteen months from factory shipping as determined by the serial number, and does not say which governs when the two disagree. Quote both.

**Lifetime is not defined.** The manual gives no owner-lifetime or product-lifetime wording and no transfer clause here; what it does say is that a product sold, transferred, bartered or given to a third party is excluded, which ends the cover at the first resale: `spirit-rower-warranty-exclusions-nine-items`.

**Do not carry the CRW800 figures onto this machine.** The CRW800 commercial rower warrants parts for five or ten years depending on the row and labor for two - `crw800-2021-warranty-commercial-and-residential-periods` - and the two water rowers are different again.
