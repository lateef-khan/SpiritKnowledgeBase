1. **Install the Incline Rail Assembly (2) into the U-channel bracket of the Main Frame (1).** From the sides install **two Hex Head Bolts (107)** with **two Flat Washers (157)** and **two Nylon Nuts (134)**. From the top install **four Hex Head Bolts (108)**, **four Split Washers (158)**, **four Flat Washers (157)**, and **four Star Washers (151)**, as shown in figure 1. Tighten with **Wrenches (159 & 162)**.
2. **Connect the 3 wire harness (48)** to the female receiver from the Incline Rail Assembly (2). Connect the **Three pin position Sensor connector (49)** the same way.
3. **Slide the Console Mast Cover (72) onto the Console Mast (12).** Tie the twist tie to the **computer cable (50)** at the top opening of the Main Frame, pull it up through the mast until it exits the top, and install the mast into the receiving bracket. **Pull slightly on the cable at the top while installing** so it does not get pinched and shorted.
4. **Put one Split Washer (155) onto the Long Hex Head Bolt (101)** through the left side of the receiving bracket, and **two Curved Washers (156) onto the two Short Hex Head Bolts (106)** through the front. Using **Wrench (159)**, tighten the (106) bolts first, then the (101) bolt, and lastly the pre-installed fourth bolt, firmly. **This is the main joint of the unit; if not tightened sufficiently it leads to noise and instability issues.** (The book doubles "the" before "(106) bolts".)
5. **Plug the connectors into the back of the console: Computer Cable (50), Two Hand Pulse cables (35), Incline switch cable (40), and Resistance switch cable (39).** Secure the **Console (45)** with **four M5x10mm Phillips Head Screws (117)**. **An empty four-pin port stays empty; this model does not use it.**
