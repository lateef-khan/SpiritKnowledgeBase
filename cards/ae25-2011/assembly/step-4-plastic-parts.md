1. **Connecting Arm Covers (87 & 88)** on the Left and Right Connecting Arms (8 & 9), each side with **one Sheet Metal Screw (126)** and **two Phillips Head Screws (115)** using the Short driver (160), arrows on the stickers deciding the orientation.
2. **Sliding Wheel Covers (82)**, each side with **four Phillips Head Screws (115)**.
3. **Middle Stabilizer Covers (93) and (94)** on the middle stabilizer bar with **Phillips Head Screws (115)**. (No count is printed for these two covers; the kit's 23 Phillips screws otherwise account exactly for the counted uses, so these covers take none.)
4. **Front Stabilizer Cover (79)** with **two Phillips Head Screws (115)**.
5. **Incline Cover brackets (21)**, bent-tab hole facing rearward, on the Incline Rail Assembly with **four Phillips Head Screws (125)**; **Rear Incline Bar Cover (91)** with **two Screws (115)**.
6. **Rear Stabilizer Cover (92)** with **four Phillips Head Screws (115)**.
7. **Console covers (95 & 96)** with **three Phillips Head Screws (115)** and **two Sheet Metal Screws (126)**. **Tighten the Sheet Metal screws first.**
8. **After the elliptical power is plugged in, elevate the incline to Level 8** and install the **Incline Rail Front Cover (89)** up against the Middle Stabilizer tube with **four Phillips Head Screws (115)**, tightened with **Wrench (161)**.
