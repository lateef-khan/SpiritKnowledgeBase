**Untie the wire holding the Sleeve Spacer (25)** in place on the rod-end of the **Connecting Arms (8 & 9)**. Align the rod-end holes with the holes in the brackets of the left and right **Handle Bars (10 & 11)**, ends of the connecting arms inside the brackets. Secure with a **Hex Head Bolt (109)**, **Flat Washer (157)** and **Nylon Nut (137)** using **Wrenches (159 & 162)**. (The book numbers this step "1. 1.".)
