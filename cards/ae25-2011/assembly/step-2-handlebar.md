1. (No numbered step 1 is printed.)
2. **Install two Wave Washers (153)** onto the left and right sides of the Handle Bar axles. **Slide the Left (10) and Right (11) Handle Bars** on; the small stickers mark L and R.
3. **Place two Flat Washers (145) onto the two Hex Head Bolts (100)** and tighten in the threaded holes at the end of the axles.
4. **Install the Front Handle Bar Covers (83 & 84 right side; 85 & 86 left side)** over the Handle Bar axle connections with the **six screws (126)**.
