Your current pulse, **40 to 220 bpm**, is displayed whenever you wear the wireless chest belt
(optional, not included). **If no heart rate signal is detected for 3 seconds the console shows "P".**

Set a target heart rate before training by pressing UP and DOWN, **within the rate zone 90 to 220,
in 1-beat increments**. "If you go above your target heart rate, the LCD Pulse value will flash and
ask you to reduce the intensity of your exercise."

That is the whole heart rate program: a ceiling that flashes. There is no zone below the target, no
beep, and no change of load. The other targets on this console sound a 3-second alarm instead -
see `see_also`.

**Other Xterra rowers set other ranges**: 30 to 240 with a beep on the ERG550W and ERG600W, 90 to
200 on the ERG650W, 100 to 180 with a zone on the ERG800W - see `not_to_be_confused_with`.
