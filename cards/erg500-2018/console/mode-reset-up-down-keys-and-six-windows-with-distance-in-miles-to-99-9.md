**The face:** an LCD with six windows in three rows - TIME and COUNT, DISTANCE (Km / Mile marks) and CALORIES, STROKES/MIN and PULSE - with a bar-graph and rower-figure strip along the bottom, and **five blue keys: DOWN, RECOVERY, MODE, RESET, UP.**

- **MODE:** select between the presets TIME, COUNT, DISTANCE, CALORIES and PULSE.
- **RESET:** press and hold 2 seconds to reset every value to zero.
- **UP / DOWN:** raise or lower the setting value of TIME, COUNT, DISTANCE, CALORIES and PULSE.
- **RECOVERY:** the pulse-recovery test, chest belt only ([recovery card](pulse-recovery-scores-from-excellent-to-poor-with-the-chest-belt-only.md)).

| Window | Accumulates | Target step |
|---|---|---|
| TIME | 00:00 up to 99:59 | 1:00 |
| DISTANCE | **0.0 up to 99.9 M (MILE)** | 0.1 mile |
| COUNT | 0 up to 9999 | 10 |
| CALORIE | 0 up to 9999 kcal, in 0.1 kcal | 1 kcal |
| PULSE | 40 to 220 bpm, chest belt only ([pulse card](pulse-40-to-220-only-from-a-wireless-chest-belt-and-p-without-a-signal.md)) | 1 bpm, 90 to 220 |
| TRAVEL DISTANCE | "Displays your stroke travel distance visually on the LCD" - the strip | - |

**Distance is in miles only** - "M (MILE)" - and the book prints no units setting; the ERG400 is the rower that switches ([units card](../../erg400-2015/console/change-km-to-miles-with-up-and-enter-pressed-together.md)). When a target is reached the value starts counting up and a **3-second alarm** sounds; setting and running those targets is a programs question. The calorie figure "is a rough guide for comparison between different exercise sessions and should not be used as a medical standard" ([calorie card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-calorie-display-is-a-rough-guide.md)). The 4-minute shut-off and the two AAA cells are on [the shut-off card](shuts-off-after-4-minutes-without-a-signal-and-zeroes-every-value.md) and [the battery card](two-aaa-batteries-with-two-different-access-procedures-printed.md).
