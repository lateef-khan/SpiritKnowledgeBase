"**It is functional only when used with the wireless chest belt (not included).**" The console has a Recovery Mode that shows how quickly you recover from exercising: after your session press the **RECOVERY** button, the LCD counts down from **60 seconds to zero**, and then a fitness score appears:

| Score | Meaning as printed |
|---|---|
| F1.0 | Excellent |
| F2.0 | Good |
| F3.0 | Fair |
| F4.0 | below average |
| F5.0 | No Good |
| F6.0 | Poor |

Without a belt signal the key has nothing to measure ([pulse card](pulse-40-to-220-only-from-a-wireless-chest-belt-and-p-without-a-signal.md)); the book prints no P or ERR message for a failed test and does not say what the intermediate values (F2.5, say) mean. The ERG600W grades the same test outstanding to poor with a 00:60 count-down ([ERG600W card](../../erg600w-2021/console/recovery-counts-down-from-00-60-and-grades-from-outstanding-to-poor.md)); the ERG800W adds REC: FAIL ([ERG800W card](../../erg800w-2023/console/recovery-counts-down-60-seconds-shows-rec-fail-and-grades-from-very-good-to-very-poor.md)); the Xterra bikes' wordings are on [the SB240 card](../../shared/console/xterra-console-recovery-grades-from-outstanding-to-poor.md).
