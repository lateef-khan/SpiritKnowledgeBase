"**If no signal has been transmitted into console for 4 minutes, the LCD display will shut off automatically and all function data will reset to Zero.** You may press any button or start training to have the console power on again."

So a four-minute rest at the end of a row costs the totals - read them before you stop. The ERG220 keeps its values across its 4.5-minute shut-off ([ERG220 card](../../erg220-2023/console/auto-on-and-4-5-minute-auto-off-that-keeps-values-and-a-2-second-reset.md)), and the SB150-family bike consoles keep theirs across four minutes ([bike card](../../shared/console/xterra-console-shuts-off-after-four-minutes-and-keeps-all-values.md)); do not quote either for this rower. Removing the batteries also zeroes everything ([battery card](two-aaa-batteries-with-two-different-access-procedures-printed.md)).
