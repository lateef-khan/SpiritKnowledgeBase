**Two AAA batteries** in the battery case inside the console. Fit them the right way round and make sure the battery spring makes proper contact. **Whenever you remove the batteries all the function values reset to zero.** "If the display appears blank or displays only partial segments, remove the batteries, wait 15 seconds, then restore them."

**The book prints two ways in, and they disagree.**

| Page | Procedure as printed |
|---|---|
| Console page, "BATTERY REPLACEMENT" | "Quick release the lock on the bottom of the console, slide cover out carefully. Install two AAA batteries in the battery case inside the console." |
| Assembly step 9, "Battery Replacement" | "1. Loosen the 3 screws on the right side of the console. Gently ease the console panel out of the shroud. 2. On the back panel, replace batteries. Make sure they fit tightly in the correct positions. 3. Place console back into shroud and retighten the 3 screws." |

The card follows the **console page**: it is the page that names the cell type and it describes the console the customer sees, while the assembly step reads like a carried-over procedure for a console mounted in a shroud. Try the quick-release cover on the bottom first; if there is none, the three screws on the right side are the other route. Neither page says whether the cells are in the box. The shut-off that empties the screen before the cells do is on [the shut-off card](shuts-off-after-4-minutes-without-a-signal-and-zeroes-every-value.md); the Xterra bike consoles take two AAA too ([bike card](../../shared/console/xterra-console-two-aaa-um4-batteries-and-a-faint-display.md)).
