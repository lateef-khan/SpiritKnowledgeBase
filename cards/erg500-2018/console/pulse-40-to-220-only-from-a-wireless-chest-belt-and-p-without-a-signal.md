"Your current pulse rate from **40 to 220 bpm** will be displayed on the LCD console **whenever you wear the wireless chest belt (optional item, not included)**." There are no hand-pulse grips on this rower and no other heart-rate source.

- **"P"** in the pulse window means **no heart-rate signal has been detected for 3 seconds** - the belt is off, out of range or not wetted, not a fault.
- A **target heart rate** can be set before training with UP and DOWN, **90 to 220 in steps of 1**. Above the target the pulse value **flashes** to ask you to reduce the intensity.

The book prints neither the belt's frequency nor a wearing page; the other Xterra rowers that name one say 5.3 kHz ([water rower pulse card](../../shared/console/xterra-ergw-console-pulse-30-to-240-only-from-a-wireless-chest-strap-sold-separately.md)). The RECOVERY key needs the same belt ([recovery card](pulse-recovery-scores-from-excellent-to-poor-with-the-chest-belt-only.md)).
