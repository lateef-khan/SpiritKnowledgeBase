**One cause, and it is not electrical.** This is an air rower: resistance comes from the fan's damper, and the only thing the book names for "no tension" is its setting.

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| No Tension | Air Damper needs Adjustment | Adjust tension lever accordingly |

There is no wiring check here and no part to replace, unlike the magnetic ERG220's version of the row, which starts with the computer wires and ends at a tension knob (`erg220-2023-errors-no-tension-computer-wires-then-tension-knob`). On an air rower the harder you pull the more resistance the fan gives, so a light feel at a low damper setting is the design, not a fault.

The squealing row beneath this one - *Belt slipping / Adjust belt* - is a belt-tension adjustment and a maintenance matter, not a fault row.
