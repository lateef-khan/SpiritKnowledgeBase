**Hardware step 3:** 2 Cushioned Rubbers (36), 2 Allen Bolts (34), 1 Allen Bolt (19), 3 Flat Washers (43), 2 Allen Bolts (42), 2 End Caps (40).

1. Insert the **seat (53)** onto the **slide rail (45)**.
2. Insert the **two cushioned rubbers (36)** into the slide rail and **slightly** fasten them with **two Allen bolts (34)** - these are the seat stops.
3. Fasten the **two foot mats (55)** on the **rear support tube (54)**.
4. Attach the rear support tube to the slide rail with **one Allen bolt (19), three flat washers (43) and two Allen bolts (42)**.
5. Insert the **two end caps for side rail (40L / 40R)** into the end of the slide rail.

Three bolts of two kinds hold the rear tube - one 19 and two 42 - under three washers. The book gives no thread sizes.
