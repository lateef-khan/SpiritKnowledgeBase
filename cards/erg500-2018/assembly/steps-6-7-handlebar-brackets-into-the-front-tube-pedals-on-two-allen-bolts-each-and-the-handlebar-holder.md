**Step 6 - pedals**

1. Insert the **two fixed brackets for handlebar (11L / 11R)** into the holes on the front support tube of the main frame (1).
2. Attach the **left pedal (14L)** to the left side of the main frame with **two Allen bolts (13)**.
3. Repeat for the **right pedal (14R)** on the right side.

The four bolts 13 are listed with the parts as "Allen Bolt for Pedal, 4", not in the hardware packs.

**Step 7 - handlebar.** "The handlebar (29) also can be placed into the handlebar holder" - the two brackets fitted a moment ago are that holder. Nothing is bolted in this step.
