**Hardware step 2:** 2 Allen Bolts (34), 2 Spring Washers (37), 2 Flat Washers (38), 1 Axle (41).

1. Pull out the **ball pin (35)** from the **main frame (1)**.
2. Place the **slide rail (45)** onto the **U-shaped connection tube** of the main frame and fasten with **one axle for slide rail (41)** and **two sets of Allen bolts (34), spring washers (37) and flat washers (38)**.
3. Then insert the ball pin (35).

The axle is the hinge the rail folds on; the ball pin locks it. Step 5 pulls the same pin again to unfold the frame for use.
