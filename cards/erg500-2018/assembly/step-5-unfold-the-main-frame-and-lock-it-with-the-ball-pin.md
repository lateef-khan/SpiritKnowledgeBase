1. Turn over the main frame and place the front support tube on the floor.
2. Pull out the **ball pin (35)** from the main frame and unfold the main frame. Then insert the ball pin into the main frame again for fixation.

**Note - before inserting the ball pin:** slightly lift up the main frame, make sure the ball pin holes are lined up, then insert the pin into the holes.

The same pin comes out again to fold the rower for storage (the book's step 8 - a storage instruction, held by the maintenance section).
