**Hardware step 4:** 4 Allen Bolts (19).

1. Turn over the main frame and place the rear support tube on the floor.
2. Take the **four self-tapping screws (10)** off the **end caps for the front stabilizer tube (8L / 8R)** - they arrive fitted to the caps - then attach the end caps to the **front support tube (9)** with the same four screws.
3. Attach the front support tube to the **main frame (1)** with **four Allen bolts (19)**.

The fifth bolt 19 in the pack went into the rear support tube in step 3.
