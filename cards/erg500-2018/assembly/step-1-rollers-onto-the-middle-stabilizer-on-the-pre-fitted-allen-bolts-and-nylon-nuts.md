Attach the **rollers (3)** onto the middle stabilizer and fasten with **two sets of Allen bolt (4) and nylon nut (2)** "which are pre-assembled on the middle stabilizer" - no hardware from the packs.

The rollers are the two wheels on the parts checklist ("3 Roller, 2"); the middle stabilizer is part of the main frame (1/91). The parts-drawing numbers 4 and 2 here are not the hardware-pack numbers.
