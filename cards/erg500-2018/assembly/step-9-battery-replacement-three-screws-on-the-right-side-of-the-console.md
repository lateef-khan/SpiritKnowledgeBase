The assembly chapter's step 9:

1. Loosen the **3 screws on the right side of the console**. Gently ease the console panel out of the shroud.
2. On the back panel, replace the batteries. Make sure they fit tightly in the correct positions.
3. Place the console back into the shroud and retighten the 3 screws.

**The console chapter describes a different console.** Its BATTERY REPLACEMENT paragraph says: "Quick release the lock on the bottom of the console, slide the cover out carefully. Install **two AAA batteries** in the battery case inside the console" - no screws, a slide-out cover. The two pages cannot both describe the same console; the step-9 drawing shows the screwed panel, so this card follows step 9 for the mechanical opening and takes the cell count (2 x AAA) from the console page, the only place the book states it. The console chapter's page is the console section's card.
