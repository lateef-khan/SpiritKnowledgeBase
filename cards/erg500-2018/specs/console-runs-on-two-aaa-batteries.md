**Two AAA cells** - AAA, where the other battery-console Xterra rowers take AA. "Install two AAA batteries in the
battery case inside the console. Ensure that the batteries are correctly fitted. Battery must be correctly
positioned and make sure the battery spring has proper contact with batteries."

**The book prints two procedures for reaching the case, and they disagree.** The console chapter (printed p. 16)
says: "Quick release the lock on the bottom of the console, slide cover out carefully." The assembly chapter's step
9 (printed p. 13) says: "Loosen the 3 screws on the right side of the console. Gently ease the console panel out of
the shroud ... On the back panel, replace batteries ... Place console back into shroud and retighten the 3
screws." This card follows the console chapter, because it is the page that names the cell type and describes
this console; the three-screw step reads as text carried over from another console. A customer who finds no
release lock should look for the three screws.

Two consequences the console page states: "Whenever you remove the batteries, all the functional values will be
reset to zero", and "IF the displays appear blank or display only partial segments, remove the batteries and wait
15 seconds then restore them." The console shuts off after 4 minutes without a signal and resets its data. The
ERG160, ERG180, ERG220 and the three 2021-2023 water rowers take two AA cells
(`xterra-rower-specs-console-runs-on-two-aa-batteries`).
