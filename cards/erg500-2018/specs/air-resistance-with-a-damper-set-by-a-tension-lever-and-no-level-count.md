**Air, with a damper.** The only place the book says what the resistance is comes in the troubleshooting table:
"No Tension - Air Damper needs Adjustment - Adjust tension lever accordingly." So the load is a fan-type air brake
whose damper opening is set by a **tension lever** on the machine, and the next row - "Squealing - Belt slipping -
Adjust Belt" - says the fan is belt-driven.

Nothing else in the book describes it: the assembly steps never mention the lever, the parts checklist has no
damper or fan item, the console (two AAA cells, `erg500-2018-specs-console-runs-on-two-aaa-batteries`) has no
level window and no resistance button, and **no level count is printed**. Where the lever is, how many positions
it has and which way is harder are not stated.

The Xterra magnetic rowers use a tension knob and a magnet bracket instead
(`xterra-erg-specs-magnetic-resistance-from-a-manual-tension-knob-with-no-level-count-printed`); the Xterra AIR350
and AIR650 bikes are fan machines with no damper (`xterra-air-specs-fan-resistance-with-no-brake`). The Spirit
CRW800 air rower brakes its fan with a gear motor from the console (`crw800-2024-specs-resistance-system`), and
the Spirit XRW600 book, like this one, never names its mechanism (`spirit-xrw600-specs-resistance-mechanism-never-named`).
