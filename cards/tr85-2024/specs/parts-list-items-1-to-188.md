**135 rows.** This is the TR75 list (`tr75-2024-specs-parts-list-items-1-to-188`) with five quantities changed - 35 of screw 21-23, 6 concave washers (87), 4 of screw 91, 37 of screw 93, 4 cushion spacers (161) - a *Foot Rail Back Plate* (141, 2) in place of the 4 x 19 mm screw (140), and four added rows: a 4 x 25 mm sheet-metal screw (164), M5 x 30 mm Phillips screws (167, 6), M5 nylon nuts (169, 6) and 5 x 13 mm flat washers (172, 6). The TR65 list is the 2023 shape with a Bluetooth row (`tr65-2023-specs-parts-list-items-001-to-188`). Rows 090, 102, 103 and 104 - a 13 mm wrench, an M5 Allen-head screw wrench, an M6 L-shaped Allen wrench and the lubricant - are the tools and lube packed in the carton; the Parts Included page of the assembly chapter shows them again.
