Select **BODY FAT Measurement** with UP or DOWN and press ENTER. "**Male**" flashes - adjust
with UP/DOWN, press ENTER. Height shows flashing as **5' 8"** - adjust, press ENTER. Weight of
**165 lb** flashes - adjust, press ENTER. Age of **30** flashes - adjust, press ENTER to save
all values.

"Press START / STOP to start measurement, **grasp hand pulse grips and do not pedal (remain
still)**. After 15 seconds the display will show Body Fat %, BMR, BMI & BODY TYPE." Press
START/STOP to return to the main display.

Nine body types by the FAT% calculated: 1 = 5-9%, 2 = 10-14%, 3 = 15-19%, 4 = 20-24%,
5 = 25-29%, 6 = 30-34%, 7 = 35-39%, 8 = 40-44%, 9 = 45-50%. "BMR: Basal Metabolism Ratio.
BMI: Body Mass Index."

**Two printings disagree.** This page is in the 2021 printing (RSX1500_20210712) only; the
2017 printing has no Body Fat page at all, and both printings still list "6 basic workout
programs" without it. The printed page is the fact for the machine this book ships with.
The SB2.5r, FS1.5, FS2.5 and FS3.5 print the same test with metric defaults (175, 75); see
`see_also`.
