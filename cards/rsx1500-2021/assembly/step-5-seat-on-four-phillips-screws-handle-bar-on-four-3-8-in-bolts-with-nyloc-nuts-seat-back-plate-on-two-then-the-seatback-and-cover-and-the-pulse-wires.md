Hardware step 5: **4 M6 x 35L Phillips head screws (106), 4 button head socket bolts 3/8" x 1-3/4" (92), 6 flat washers 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T (93), 6 nyloc nuts 3/8" x 7T (94), 2 button head socket bolts 3/8" x 3/4" (95), 4 Phillips head screws 1/4" x 1" (97), 4 flat washers 1/4" x 13 x 1.0T (89)**; tools: combination driver (130), L Allen wrench (131), 14/15 mm wrench (129).

1. Install the seat (65) on the sliding seat assembly and use the Allen wrench & Phillips screwdriver (130) to secure it with 4 Phillips head screws (106).
2. Install the handle bar (7) on the sliding seat assembly using the Allen wrench (131) to tighten 4 button head socket bolts (92) going through the handle bar (7) and 4 flat washers (93), then use the wrench (129) to tighten 4 nyloc nuts (94).
3. Install the seat back fixing plate (16) on the handle bar (7) using the Allen wrench (131) to tighten 2 button head socket bolts (95) going through the handle bar (7) and 2 flat washers (93), then use the wrench (129) to tighten 2 nyloc nuts (94).
4. Install the seatback (64) on the seat back fixing plate (16) and use the Allen wrench & Phillips screwdriver (130) to tighten 4 Phillips head screws (97) and 4 flat washers (89) to secure the seat back fixing plate (16), then the seatback cover (56).
5. Connect the lower and upper handpulse wires (27, 26).
