Hardware step 3: **1 button head socket bolt 5/16" x 3/4" (112), 1 split washer (98), 1 flat washer (99) (printed "Split Washer" in the box), 8 sheet metal screws 4 x 12L (96)**; tools: combination drivers (130), 2 pcs.

1. *(printed as an empty item 1)*
2. Slide the lower handle bar (L) (5) through the handgrip axle (76) (refer to step 2) and use 2 Allen wrench & Phillips screwdrivers (130) to tighten 1 button head socket bolt (112), 1 split washer (98) and 1 flat washer (99) - one driver in each end, "tighten each other".
3. Install the front handle bar cover (38) and rear handle bar cover (39) on the right and left lower handle bars (6, 5) with 8 sheet metal screws (96) using the Allen wrench & Phillips screwdriver (130).

The page is headed "Upper Swing Arms" but every sentence on it is about the lower handle bars and their covers; the upper handgrips come fitted. Item 1 is blank in both printings.
