Hardware step 1: **9 socket head cap bolts 5/16" x 3/4" (100), 9 split washers (98), 8 flat washers (99), 1 curved washer (101), 4 M5 x 12 Phillips head screws (81)**; tools: L Allen wrench (131), combination driver (130).

1. Use the Allen wrench (131) to tighten both the front and rear stabilizers (12, 13) with 4 socket head cap bolts (100), 4 split washers (98) and 4 flat washers (99).
2. Connect the computer cable (21) and handpulse wire (26) in the console mast (2) with the wires from the main frame **before you slide the console mast onto the main frame. Be careful not to pinch any of the wires during this step.**
3. Insert the console mast (2) onto the main frame (1) and use the Allen wrench (131) to tighten with "4 Socket Head Cap Bolts (100), 4 Flat Washers (99), 5 Split Washers (98) and 1 Curved Washer (101)".
4. Connect the console (19) with the computer cable (21) and handpulse wire (26) to the console mast (2) and use the combination M5 Allen wrench & Phillips screwdriver (130) to secure it with 4 Phillips head screws (81).

**The mast takes five bolts, not four.** Item 3 prints "4 Socket Head Cap Bolts" with "5 Split Washers" and a curved washer; the pack holds nine bolts and nine split washers, of which the stabilizers take four each, leaving five bolts and five split washers for the mast - four on flat washers and one on the curved washer. The pack count and the split-washer count agree; only the bolt figure in item 3 is short by one. The drawing on the facing page labels bolt 100 at four points around the mast, one of them with the curved washer 101. Both printings carry the same figures. The transport wheels are labelled on the rear stabilizer's end in the same drawing.
