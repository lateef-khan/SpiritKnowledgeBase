Hardware step 4: **4 button head socket bolts 5/16" x 3/4" (112), 4 flat washers 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T (99), 4 split washers 5/16" x 1.5T (98)**; tools: combination driver (130), 14/15 mm wrench (129).

1. **Make sure that the powder metallurgy bushings (42) are mounted on the crank arm (15) before assembly.**
2. Joint the pedal bar assembly (10) with the crank axle and use the Allen wrench & Phillips screwdriver (130) to secure it with 4 button head socket bolts (112), 4 flat washers (99) and 4 split washers (98).
3. Use the wrench (129) to tighten the pedal (62) on the lower handle bar.

Two bolts a side hold each pedal bar to the crank. No thread direction and no torque are printed for the pedals - on this machine they are the foot plates that bolt to the lower handle bars, not screw-in bicycle pedals.
