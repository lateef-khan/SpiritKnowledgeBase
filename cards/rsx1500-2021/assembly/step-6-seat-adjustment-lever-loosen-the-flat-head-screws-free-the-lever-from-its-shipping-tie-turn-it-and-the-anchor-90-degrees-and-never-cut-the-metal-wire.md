No new hardware; tool: the combination driver (130), used as an Allen key.

1. Use the Allen wrench (130) to loosen the flat head socket screws (105) from the seat assembly (18).
2. Detach the lever (78) from the plastic tie that secures the handle for shipping. Move the lever (78) out from the seat assembly (18). **DO NOT CUT OR DETACH THE METAL WIRE ON THE END OF THE LEVER.**
3. Turn the lever (78) and lever anchor (44) to 90 degrees as seen in the diagram so the anchor can slide into the seat assembly (18) with the holes aligned.
4. Use the Allen wrench (130) to secure the flat head socket screws (105) on the seat assembly (18).

The metal wire is the release cable for the sliding seat; the lever ships tied inside the seat assembly and is only turned out into place. This is the last assembly step; the book prints no levelling instruction and its only moving note is the "transport wheels engaged when the rear is lifted" line under Features (the linked card).
