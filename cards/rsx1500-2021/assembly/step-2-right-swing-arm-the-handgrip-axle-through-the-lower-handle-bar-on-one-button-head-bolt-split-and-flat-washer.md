Hardware step 2: **1 button head socket bolt 5/16" x 3/4" (112), 1 split washer (98), 1 flat washer 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T (99)** - the hardware box prints the flat washer as a "Split Washer"; the pack and the step text say flat. Tool: combination driver (130).

1. Insert the handgrip axle (76) to slide through the lower handle bar (R) (6) and use the Allen wrench & Phillips screwdriver (130) to secure it with the button head socket bolt (112), split washer (98) and flat washer (99).

The axle passes through the console mast; the left arm goes onto its other end in step 3.
