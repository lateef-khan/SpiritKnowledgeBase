Three heading errors in this manual. **The document is still the right one for the LCR 2016.**

| Where | What it says | What it should say |
|---|---|---|
| Section 9 heading | "9. SR420-YB004 Troubleshooting" | SR620B-YB012-01, the code this manual carries everywhere else |
| Circuit diagram page | "ELLIPICAL CIRCUIT DIAGRAM" | Bike circuit diagram |
| Closing note | none in this manual | |

SR420-SB004 is the R92 2016 manual code. The two manuals share most of their text, and this heading was copied across.

The manual's own cover reads SR620B-YB012, and the inside pages read SR620B-YB012-01.

The section 8 troubleshooting table also carries untranslated Chinese next to the English, and section 9-10 step 4 does the same.
