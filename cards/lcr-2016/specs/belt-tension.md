**180 to 205 Hz, read with an audio (sound wave) tension gauge.** This machine is set by frequency, not by Newtons.

Set it by tightening the **M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88)** on the J-Bolt with a **13 mm wrench** until the gauge reads 180 to 205 Hz.

**Section 9-13 misprints the range as "180~20HZ".** That is a typo with a digit dropped. Section 9-5 step 5 gives the full range, and the LCB 2016 and LCB 2019 manuals both give 180 to 205 Hz for the same belt system. Use 180 to 205 Hz.

The R92 2016 recumbent uses 450 N instead. Do not carry that to this machine.
