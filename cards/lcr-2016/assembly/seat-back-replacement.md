1. Use an open wrench to unscrew **six 3/8" x 3/4" hex head bolts (176)** and **six 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washers (77)** to take the **seat back frame (5)** apart.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew the **seat back cover (128)**, held by **four M5 x 15 m/m Phillips head screws (136)**.
3. Use the combination **M5 Allen wrench** to release the **M8 x 15 m/m button head socket bolt (179)** and take the **seatback cushion (63)** apart.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.

This machine has a seat back cover (128) that the R92 2016 does not.
