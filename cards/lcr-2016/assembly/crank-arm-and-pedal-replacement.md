1. Use a **13/15 m/m wrench** to turn the **left pedal (116) clockwise** and the **right pedal (117) counterclockwise** to remove them.
2. To refit, turn the pedals the opposite way.
3. Take off the **crank arm end cap (28)** and release the **M10 x 1.0 nut (108)** with the wrench.
4. Use the plug that matches the **cranks (51L, 51R)** and turn with a hex wrench to release the cranks.
5. To refit the cranks (51L, 51R), use a hammer to seat the cranks tightly on the **drive pulley axle (8)**, turn the nut tight onto the axle, and plug in the crank arm end cap.

The R92 2016 uses an **M10 x 1.25** nut here instead of M10 x 1.0.
