1. Take both left and right shrouds (29, 30) and the **drive belt (54)** apart.
2. Use C-ring pliers to take off the **20 mm C-ring (86)** on the axle. The **drive pulley axle (8)** is then released.
3. To release the drive pulley, use two wrenches to unscrew **four 1/4" x 3/4" hex head screws (66)** with **eight 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers (72)** and **four 1/4" x 5T nuts (90)**.
4. Follow steps 3 and 2 in order to refit the crank axle.

The R92 2016 uses **1/4" x 5.5T** nuts (90) at the same joint.
