1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **four M6 x 15 m/m Phillips head screws (98)** to take the **seat (61)** apart.
2. Use two **14 m/m wrenches** to unscrew the **seat carriage (4)**, **two 3/8" x 2-3/4" hex head bolts (175)**, **four 3/8" x 1-3/4" hex head bolts (71)**, **six 3/8" x 7T nyloc nuts (89)** and **six 3/8" x 19 m/m x 1.5T flat washers (77)** to take the **seat handle bar (6)** apart.
3. Unplug the **HGP wire grommet (126)** that holds the **handpulse cable assembly (21, 27)**, and use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **four 3 x 20 m/m tapping screws (97)** to take the handpulse cable assembly (21, 27) apart.
4. Reverse the steps to refit.
