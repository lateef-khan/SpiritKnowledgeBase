1. Take the **crank arms (51L, 51R)** and the **console mast cover (31)** apart.
2. To release the **left front chain cover (29)**, use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **seven 3.5 x 12 self tapping screws (103)** and **two 5 x 16 tapping screws (102)**.
3. To release the **right front chain cover (30)**: on the mainframe unscrew **two 3.5 x 20 self tapping screws (107)** with **two 5 x 16 x 1.5T flat washers (78)**; on the cover unscrew **two 5 x 16 tapping screws** and unplug.
4. To refit the right front chain cover, put it on the mainframe, fit **two 3.5 x 20 self tapping screws (103)** with **two 3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T flat washers (78)** loosely, then tighten **two 5 x 16 tapping screws (101)** on the mainframe and tighten everything.
5. Match the left front chain cover (29) with the right (30) and secure both with **seven 3.5 x 12 self tapping screws (103)** and then **two 5 x 16 tapping screws (101)**.
6. **Watch the on/off switch (159) when you fit the right front shroud (30).** The switch must be held by both front shrouds (29)(30). Its top and bottom go inside both shrouds, and its left and right sides go outside both shrouds.

**The screw part numbers do not line up.** Step 3 removes part (107) but step 4 refits part (103). Step 2 uses part (102) for the tapping screws but step 5 uses part (101). The R92 2016 manual, which is otherwise the same procedure, uses (103) and (101) throughout.
