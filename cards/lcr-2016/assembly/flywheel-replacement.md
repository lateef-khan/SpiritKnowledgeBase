1. Take both left and right front shrouds (29)(30) and the **belt (54)** apart.
2. Unplug the electric wires connected to the **generator flywheel (55)**, then use an **11 m/m wrench** to unscrew **four 1/4" x 3/4" hex head screws (66)** with **four 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers (72)** and **four 1/4" split washers (80)**. The generator flywheel (55) is then released.
3. Follow the steps in order to refit the flywheel.
