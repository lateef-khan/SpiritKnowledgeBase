1. Take both left and right front shrouds apart (29)(30).
2. Use a **13 m/m wrench** to release the **M8 x 7T nut (88)** and take off the **J-Bolt (87)**.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **three M6 x 7T Phillips head screws** on the **idle arm (10)**, and the **belt (54)** can be taken off.
4. To release the idle arm (10), unscrew **three M6 x 15 Phillips head screws (98)** with **three 1/4" split washers (80)** and **three 1/4" x 13 x 1T washers (72)**.
5. To refit the belt (54) and the idle arm (10), use a Phillips head screwdriver to secure the **generator flywheel (55)** with three M6 x 15 Phillips head screws, three 1/4" split washers (80) and three 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers (72). Turn the **drive pulley (20)** to get the belt onto the pulley, hook the J-Bolt on the mainframe, and use a **13 m/m wrench** to tighten the M8 x 7T nut (88) on the J-Bolt until the belt (54) has acceptable tension. **Use an audio tension gauge to reach 180 to 205 Hz.** Finally turn the drive pulley (20) and check the drive belt (54) sits in the centre without coming off the pulley.
