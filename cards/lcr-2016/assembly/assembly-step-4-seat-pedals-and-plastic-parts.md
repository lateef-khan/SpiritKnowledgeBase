Hardware: **#98** M6 x 15mm Phillips Head Screw (4 pcs), **#99** M5 x 12mm Phillips Head Screw (8 pcs), **#105** Ø4 x 16mm Sheet Metal Screw (4 pcs).

1. Install the Seat (61) on the Seat Carriage (4) with the four Phillips Head Screws (98), by using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (114).
2. Install the Front Stabilizer Cover (32) and the Rear Stabilizer Cover (37) on the Main Frame (1) with the four Phillips Head Screws (99).
3. Install the Computer Cable (44) and Hand pulse Wire, Coiled (45) into the connector in the back of the Console Assembly (19). Install the console onto the mounting plate with the four Phillips Head Screws (99). **Be careful not to pinch the wires between the console and the mounting plate - this could short the wires and damage the electronics.**
4. Install the Drink Bottle Holders (L /39, R/38) onto **left of** the Seat Handle Bar (6) with the 4 pcs of Sheet Metal Screws (105).
5. Install the Pedals (L/116, R/117) in the Cranks by using the **14/15m/m Wrench (132)**. **The left Pedal (116 L) has a reverse thread** and screws into the crank in the opposite rotation from normal threads. There is an "L" stamped into the end of the threaded post of the left pedal and an "R" in the right. Make sure to tighten the pedals as much as you possibly can.

It may be necessary to re-tighten the pedals if you feel a thumping during pedalling. A clicking noise, or thumping sound during pedalling, is usually caused by the pedals being too loose. **That last sentence is printed without a number**, run on after instruction 5.

**"onto left of the Seat Handle Bar" is what the manual says**, for a pair of holders labelled L/39 and R/38. The LCR 2011 and 2013 manuals say "onto left and right sides of the Seat Handle Bar", which is what the parts imply. The wording is left as printed; the LCR 2019 and 2023 repeat the same "onto left of" phrasing.

**This is the last LCR year that fits the stabilizer covers in this step.** From 2019 they are gone from the assembly instructions and a tablet holder is added instead.
