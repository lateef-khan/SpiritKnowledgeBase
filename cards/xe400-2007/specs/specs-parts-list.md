- Fifteen numbered rows print no part number at all - among them the incline device (17), the incline motor holder (18, spelt *Wolder*), the console holder assembly (20), the on/off switch (40, whose wires 40~1 and ground 40~2 are numbered), the cover holder (156) and six fasteners. Item 168, the controller back plate, is `X`.
- Items 71 and 72, the connecting arm covers, both carry 006099, as on the XE100. Items 147 and 147~1 - the lower resistance and incline handle wires - are bracketed *SAME WIRE* and share 006088; the upper ones differ (146 resistance 006089, 146~1 incline with a red end 006167).
- The electrical parts with numbers: incline motor 006143, incline controller / lower board 006095, its transformer (`Incline Adaptor`) 006096, gear motor `M-100A` 022170, power cord 022812, 1450 mm incline computer cable 006068, 800 mm incline motor power cord 006091, 850 mm incline motor wire 006092, 1000 mm computer cable 000183, 300 mm sensor 000184, handpulse 008041 and the Polar chest-strap receiver 32~13, 004074.
- Items 124 to 126 and 129 are the packed tools (13/14 mm wrench 022596, two screwdrivers 023109 and 023091, 12 mm wrench 000342); item 138 is the lubricant 022271. The unnumbered rows are the Hardware Kit (006104), the Footplate Replacement Kit (006153), its Locking Pin Bracket (006238) and 5x10 screw (000051).

**Order against the serial number.** The same book's parts purchase order treats a line without a serial number as a stock order (`spirit-xt-2007-policy-parts-purchase-order-form`); the owner's manual for this machine prints drawings but no list at all (`spirit-ce-assembly-exploded-view-and-parts-list`), so this is the only place its part numbers are printed. Which elliptical service manuals print a list is on `spirit-elliptical-specs-parts-which-service-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.
