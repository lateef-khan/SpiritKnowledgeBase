The TT9 offers **twelve preset programs: HILL, FAT BURN, CARDIO, STRENGTH, CALORIE, INTERVAL, 5K RUN, 10K RUN, HR, CUSTOM, a Gerkin protocol based Fitness Test, and one Manual program**. Menus and setup run on the touchscreen with numeric keypads.

- **Time**: darkened window, default **20 minutes**.
- **Age**: affects the heart-rate bar graph and HR programs. Default **30 years, range 10 to 110**.
- **Weight**: affects the calorie readout. Default **70 kg, range 10 to 150**.
- **Mix Speed**: default **5.0 km/h**.
- Then **START**.

**A 3-minute warm-up starts the workout** (clock counts down); pressing START bypasses straight into the workout. **5K RUN, 10K RUN, HR, CUSTOM, CALORIE, Military Fitness Test and the manual program have no warm-up mode.**

The five classic presets' segment figures are on `tt9-2018-programs-preset-chart`; CALORIE, Custom, 5K/10K, HR, Manual and the tests each have their own card.
