**Speed numbers are a percentage of the program's top speed**, set during setup: the first HILL speed of 20 means 20% of top speed (2 mph of a 10 mph top, 3.2 kph of 16 kph). Segment 12 showing 100 means full top speed.

**Extraction damage, read before trusting a cell.** The printed header names 22 columns (Warm up, 1-20, Cool down) but every row carries about 26 values, so the warm-up/cool-down spans cannot be mapped to headings; the dividers above are editorial. One Hill and one or two Fatburn segment speeds are illegible in the extraction. **Negative incline marks (-5 to -2) are not real inclines** — the machine has no decline — and are separator artifacts; read the row as printed. The Interval row alternates 100/50 speed with 4/0 incline throughout.
