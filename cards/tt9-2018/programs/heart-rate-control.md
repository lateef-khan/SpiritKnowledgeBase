**How it works.** HRC uses the **incline system** to control heart rate; elevation changes move heart rate more efficiently than speed alone, and elevation shifts gradually to the target.

**One HR program.** At setup the **Weight Control program holds 65% of Maximum Heart Rate** (220 minus the entered age, auto-computed). The twelve-program list names it HR.

1. Press the **HR button**, then ENTER.
2. The touch **Time/Age/Weight/Target HR (65%)** window darkens for the numbers.
3. Press **START**.
4. Mid-program, the **Incline Up/Down buttons change the target heart rate**.

The 60/80 and 70/90 generations differ in both percentages and keys: see `sole-treadmill-programs-hr-60-80-menu` and `sole-treadmill-programs-hrc-70-90-autopilot`.
