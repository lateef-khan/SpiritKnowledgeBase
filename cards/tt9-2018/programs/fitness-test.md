The Fit-Test key shows **ten entries: Air Force, PEB, Army, Marines Corps, Coast Guard, Navy, Gerkin, WFI, CTT Performance, CTT prediction**. Each military page is an image with a timed manual-speed run ("A timed 1.5 mile run. You control the speed manually. Maximum time allowed ..."); the manual-speed timed-distance six are shared with the 2026 console — see `f80-2026-military-fitness-tests`, extended to this machine.

**Contradiction ruled.** The programming paragraph says "8 different tests" where the menu page lists ten names. This card follows the ten, which are enumerated outright.

**Programming.** Enter **gender**, adjust **Age and Weight** with numeric keypads, press **START**.

**Before:** health check (physician if over 35 or pre-existing conditions), warm up and stretch, **no caffeine**, hold grips gently.

**During:** the console needs a **steady heart rate** (grips or chest strap) for the test to begin. **3-minute warm-up at 4.8 kph (3 mph)** applies to **Gerkin and WFI only**. Shown: elapsed time, incline %, distance, speed, and target vs actual heart rate.

**After: cool down one to three minutes**, note the score — the console returns to start-up after a few minutes.

Score charts are on `sole-tt8-tt9-programs-vo2max-charts`.
