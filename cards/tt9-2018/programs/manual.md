The Menu holds **12 program modes**; touch **Manual**, optionally arrow through **Profile** screens, and touch **ENTER** to confirm (HOME/return icons abandon). Then set program time, age and weight, press ENTER, and press **START**.

The running screen is **SIMPLE**: all workout values along the bottom, **speed value at right, incline level at left**, adjustable with UP/DOWN. A middle **bar chart shows speed and incline**, switched with the SPEED/INSLINE buttons (sic). **TRACK** shows a lap loop with laps and values; **DASHBOARD** is a third equivalent interface, selectable at the bottom at will.

Ending from the first-page button or STOP shows the **summary image** with all accumulated values; HOME offers uploading training data to the cloud (OK shows a QR code and account screen, Reject returns HOME).

Unlike the button consoles, there is no quick-keys manual start here; the 2026 touchscreen twin is on `f80-2026-manual-program`.
