Four assembly steps (hardware item numbers in brackets). CJK separators ("、") in cable lists are printing artifacts.

1. Uprights: with Lower Computer Cables (42) and (72) already inside the right upright (34), feed them into the main frame without clamping them, align the foam pad (98), and fix each upright with five 3/8" x 16 x 3" Button Head Socket Bolts (144), each with a Dia 10 x 2.0T Split Washer (117) and a Dia 3/8" x 35 x 2T Flat Washer (116), screwed up from the middle hole of the foam pad. Fit the left upright (33) the same way. Fix motor base caps R/L (27)/(26) with four M5 x 12L Phillips Head Screws (134) and caps R/L (197)/(196) with four 3.5 x 12 mm Sheet Metal Screws (193).
2. Console wiring: connect the ground wire (35), handpulse wire upper/lower (39), computer cables upper (41) and (71), pad/backlit cable (43) and connecting cable (61) to the board on the back of the console (28); fix the console assembly onto the console support (5) with four M5 x 20L Phillips Head Screws (133).
3. Console mounting: fix the console to the interface mounting bracket on the uprights (3, 4) with six M8 x 1.25 x 12L Socket Head Cap Bolts (146); join upper computer cables (41)/(42) and (71)/(72); fix the upright tops with six 3/8" x 16 x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolts (145), each with a Split Washer (117) and a 3/8" x 25 x 2T Flat Washer (125), without pinching the computer cable.
4. Mast covers: fix console mast covers R/L (194)/(195) to each upright with four M5 x 12L Phillips Head Screws (134) per side, then tie the covers and both uprights together with two 3/8" x 16 x 1-3/4" Button Head Socket Bolts (162), each with a Split Washer (117) and a Dia 10 x 23 x 1.5T Curved Washer (163).

IMPORTANT ASSEMBLY INSTRUCTION: slide the console mounting interface brackets (items 3 and 4) all the way toward the back of the console before tightening the bolts.
