First power-on, the last step of assembly, identical in the TT9 2018, F85 2021, ST90 2021/2023 and TT8 2021 books:

Plug in the power cord and switch on the main power switch located at the front, under the motor hood of the treadmill, and make sure that the safety key is put on, as the treadmill is unable to operate without the safety key. When the power is turned on, the screen will show the initial image and then enter the ready mode, which is the beginning of the treadmill operation.

Power-switch location varies by book: front under the motor hood (TT9-2018, F85-2021, TT8-2021), right under the motor hood (ST90-2021), right-front (ST90-2023). The ST90-2023 book also prints the sleep-mode paragraph carded separately.
