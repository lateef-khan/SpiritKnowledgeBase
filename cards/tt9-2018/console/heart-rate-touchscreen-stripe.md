The on-screen Pulse (heart rate) shows current beats per minute. Hold **both left and right stainless steel sensors**; the value shows continuously. The hand pulse sensors also serve heart rate control, and the console detects pulse from a **heart rate stripe which is bipolar including signal transmitting** (the book's wording).
