Press **STOP** once (or the first-page button on the screen) to pause: the belt slows gradually to a full stop while time, distance and calorie stay on screen. The screen resets after **5 minutes of counting down** and returns to the previous status. Press **START** during pause to continue. Pressing **STOP twice** terminates the setting and shows the workout summary; a **third** press returns to the initial status. From the summary image, HOME offers to upload the training data to the cloud (OK shows a QR code and the account screen, Reject goes HOME).
