**This is the CE800 (2020) book, whose figure is printed as "185Khz~210Khz". The 2016 residential
XE books and the CE800 (2016) book set the same J-bolt to 190 Hz plus or minus 10
(`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-belt-slipping-hook-screw-cap-13-mm-190-hz`); the CE900 family uses a
pressure roller at 280-310 N. Three figures, three cards.**

## Belt loose or dropped off the pulley

1. Remove the **Chain Cover (R)** and **loosen the Idler Wheel Assembly**, then remount the belt.
2. Operate at **low speed** and watch the degree of belt swing. Decide whether the problem is the
   pulley swinging too much, or the belt, pulley and flywheel not being aligned.
3. If the belt, pulley and flywheel are not aligned, **adjust the flywheel** to the right position.

The book prints no RPM for this check, unlike the 2016 residential books.

## Belt slipping

"If the problem is belt slipping, then the technician can use a **13mm open-end wrench to adjust
the nut of J Bolt**." If the belt is damaged, replace it.

## The target figure

The idler procedure sets the value: "Adjust the nut which on the J bolt to adjust the tension of
the belt to the right value (**About 185Khz~210Khz by using sonic belt tension meter**)."

**The unit is a misprint.** A sonic belt tension meter reads the belt's plucked frequency in hertz;
a kilohertz belt frequency is impossible. Read it as **about 185 Hz to 210 Hz**. The CE800ENT service
manual prints the identical misprint in the identical step (`ce800ent-drive-belt-tension`), so the
two books share the figure and the error.
