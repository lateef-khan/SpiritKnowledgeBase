Two drawings, printed page numbers one behind the PDF's.

**p. 5 (printed 4), the shrouded machine:** TPR Handle Bar Axle Inner Cover, Console, Handle Bar
Cover, Console Mast Cover (L) & (R), Pedal Arm Cover (R), Round Disk, Round Disk Cover, Connecting
Arm Cover A (L) & (R), Front Stabilizer Cover, Pedal (L), Connecting Arm Cover B (L) & (R), Chain
Cover (R), Beam Cover, Rear Stabilizer Cover (B), Slide Wheel Cover, Rear Stabilizer Cover (A).

**p. 6 (printed 5), the frame with the shrouds off:** Console Mast, Swing Arm (R) (labelled twice),
**Generator/Brake Controller**, Drive Pulley, Connecting Arm (L), Main Frame, Cross Bar, Pedal Arm
(L), **Induction Brake**, Connecting Arm (R), Rail Support Assembly, Pedal Arm (R), Rear Rail
Assembly, Aluminum Track.

**The "Round Disk" is the crank disc behind the side case**, the part the troubleshooting chapter
means when it says the disk is off centre or swinging. The skeleton drawing labels the brake unit
*Induction Brake* and the lower board *Generator/Brake Controller*; the wiring chapter calls the
same two parts the generator flywheel and the driver board.

The Outlines chapter prints **no dimensions, no machine weight and no user weight limit**. The
2016-generation CE800 book labels a different frame with an aluminium step rail
(`ce800-2016-specs-outline-part-names`), and the CE800ENT a third
(`ce800ent-outlines-part-names`).
