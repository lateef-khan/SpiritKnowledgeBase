These are the callout numbers on the exploded view drawing on page 51. The
drawing itself is a picture with numbers on it and no text, so this table is the
only readable form of it.

**Three rows differ from the 2010 MT200 list.** 198 numbers appear on both and
carry the same part; the exceptions are:

| No. | On this 2022 manual | On the 2010 manual |
|---|---|---|
| 49 | Insulation bushing, 1 | not printed |
| 50 | not printed | Ground Isolation Cap, 1 |
| 60 | M8 × P1.25 × 45L Socket Head Cap Bolt, **4** | M8 × P1.25 × 45m/m Socket Head Cap Bolt, **10** |
| 253 | Linear Bushing | Linear Bearing, Step Sensor |
| 308 | M8 x 40L Socket Head Cap Bolt, 6 | not printed |

This book splits the earlier ten 45 mm socket head cap bolts into four 45 mm
(60) and six 40 mm (308), and it renumbers the grounding part at the A.C. input
from 50 to 49 and renames it. Do not order a Ground Isolation Cap 50 against
this list. See `mt200-2010-specs-parts-list`.

**On the printed numbering.** 200 rows are printed. The whole numbers that
appear are 1-21, 24, 26, 27, 29-45, 49, 51-68, 71-74, 76-78, 104, 108, 110, 112,
113, 117, 119, 125, 131, 132, 134, 136-140, 147, 148, 151-153, 178-193, 195-214,
216-218, 221-224, 226-228, 230, 231, 236, 240, 243-262, 264-271, 274-288,
290-300, 302-305 and 308, plus the three console sub-numbers 36-15, 36-16 and
36-17. Any number outside that run is not printed in this manual at all - it is
not a missing page.

**Twenty-one descriptions were repaired, because the conversion of this manual
lost the "fl" and "fi" ligatures and split capitals mid-word.** The page prints
"Ø10 × Ø19 × 1.5t\_ at washer" for what is a flat washer, "Upright xing plate"
for the fixing plate, "transportation wh Eel", "Right h Andgrip",
"socket hea D cap bolt", "he X head bolt", "spl It washer",
"Console mast locking a Ssembly", "poly -V", "Rs -485" and "200m/M". Each of
those was restored above and every one is confirmed word for word by the 2010
manual's own list, which prints the same 198 shared rows undamaged. **No number
or quantity was changed** - the damage is confined to letters. The rest of the
list's lowercase styling is the manual's own and is left alone.

**Item 2 and item 180 are both "Incline bracket", quantity 1.** The manual
prints them that way; check the drawing before ordering either.

**No motor rating, roller size or belt size is printed for any of these parts.**
Item 279 is "Drive motor" with no horsepower or wattage, item 16 is
"Drive motor belt, poly-V" with no length or profile, and items 10, 11, 12 and
13 give the roller, deck and belt no dimensions. The specification page on
page 67 does not carry them either.
