**The compliance list, exactly as printed.**

- Comply to CAN/CAS-C22.2 No. 60601-1:14
- ANS/AAMI ES60601-1:2005+A2 (R2012) +A1
- IEC 60601-1-2:2014 , EN 60601-1-2:2015
- IEC 60601-1:2015+A1:2012
- EN 60601-1:2006+A1:2013+A12:2014
- EN ISO 20957:2013

**Three of those are misprints on the page, and they are left as printed.** It
really does say **"CAN/CAS"** rather than CAN/CSA, and **"ANS/AAMI"** rather
than ANSI/AAMI. And **"IEC 60601-1:2015+A1:2012"** puts the amendment year
before the base year; the 2026 7.0T manual prints the same entry as
"IEC 60601-1:2005+A1:2012", which is the form that makes sense. Nothing on the
page reconciles them, so the entry is quoted here as this manual has it and the
7.0T card holds the other reading.

**Classification.**

> Class I **measuring**, Type B, ordinary equipment, continuous operation. This
> product is classed as ordinary equipment according to IEC/EN/UL60601-1 and is
> not protected against the ingress of water.

**Do not quote this list for the 2010 MT200.** That book prints four entries in
one running sentence, names **TUV** as the listing body, uses the words "CE
conformity to EN 60601-1 EMC", carries no EN ISO 20957 and classes the machine
as **"Class I, Type B"** without the word *measuring*. See
`mt200-2010-specs-certifications-and-classification`.

**Nor for the 2026 7.0T or 8.0T.** The 7.0T list adds ISO 14971; 2019; the 8.0T
adds EU MDR 2017/745 Class I and splits EN ISO 20957 into parts 1 and 6. Neither
appears here.

**No EU MDR entry appears in this list**, and no notified body number is printed
anywhere in the manual.

**Ingress protection.** The Important electrical information page says the same
thing in other words - the treadmill "is not protected against the ingress of
water or particulate matter" and "is not suitable for use in an oxygen rich
environment". No IP code is given.

**Disposal.** "Reference should be made to local regulations concerning the
disposal of this product at the end of useful life." The 2010 manual prints the
same sentence. No take-back scheme, no WEEE marking, no dismantling instruction.

**The Manufacturer block on this page is blank in the converted text.** The 2010
manual names Dyaco International Inc. of Taipei; do not assume the same entry
here without checking the printed page. See `mt200-2010-specs-manufacturer`.
