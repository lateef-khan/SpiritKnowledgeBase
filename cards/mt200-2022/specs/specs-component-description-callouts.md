The drawing is a side view of the treadmill with nine call-outs, printed as words on the picture:

1. **Electronic Console**
2. **Safety Lanyard**
3. **Hand Rails**
4. **Rails Horizontal Adjustment**
5. **Foot Step**
6. **Front Incline**
7. **Rail Vertical Adjustment**
8. **Rail Lift Bar**
9. **Rear Incline**

The two incline call-outs are the two incline motors this machine has - a front incline that raises the
deck and a rear incline that lowers it for a decline - and the wiring diagram shows both, on separate
connectors of the lower control board. The names here are the ones the replacement procedures and the
wiring diagram use.

The chapter carries no dimension, weight or rating; those are on the owner's-manual specification cards.

**This book is also the 7.0T 770885's service manual.** Spirit's February 2026 export of it is titled *7.0T-770885 (MT8000-ST021-01)* and is 99.7% the same text (the parts-list header reads MT8000 where the 2021 export reads MT7000), so the 2026 7.0T (`70t-2026`) is listed here alongside the MT200.
