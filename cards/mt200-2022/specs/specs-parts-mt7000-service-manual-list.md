**This is the list printed in the 7.0T (MT8000-ST021-02) service manual, Ver 1.3 of April 2021, and it is not the list in the 2022 owner's manual.** The book heads the drawing and the list **MT7000**, not MT200 and not 7.0T, and the list it prints matches the **2010** owner's manual list at 194 of its 198 rows. Against the 2022 owner's manual list (`mt200-2022-specs-parts-list`) it differs in five places; against the 2010 list (`mt200-2010-specs-parts-list`) in four. A part ordered by item number needs the book named as well as the number.

**Item 60 is the row to be careful with.** Three books give three answers for the same bolt position: ten 40 mm bolts here, ten 45 mm bolts in the 2010 book, four 45 mm bolts in the 2022 book (which moves six 40 mm bolts to a new item 308). This book is the only one that prints 40 mm at item 60, and it was read off the page, not the extraction. Items 196 and 197 are worded `Powder metal Sleeve` here and `Podwer metallurgy Sleeve` in both owner's manuals - the same part.

**Three columns.** `Dwg #`, `Description` and `Qty.` - no Spirit part number anywhere. The whole numbers printed are 1-21, 24, 26-27, 29-45, 50-68, 71-74, 76-78, 104, 108, 110, 112-113, 117, 119, 125, 131-132, 134, 136-140, 147-148, 151-153, 178-193, 195-214, 216-218, 221-224, 226-228, 230-231, 236, 240, 243-262, 264-271, 274-288, 290-300, 302-304; every other number up to 304 (22-23, 25, 28, 46-49, 69-70, 75, 79-103, 105-107, 109, 111, 114-116, 118, 120-124, 126-130, 133, 135, 141-146, 149-150, 154-177, 194, 215, 219-220, 225, 229, 232-235, 237-239, 241-242, 263, 272-273, 289, 301) is absent from this list, not a missing page. The drawing on p. 43 carries the same numbers and draws the three tools - items 113 (Phillips screwdriver), 137 (5 mm Allen wrench) and 259 (6 mm Allen wrench) - beside the machine.

**The service manual prints no `8-2 Prepare tools` page and no tool list of its own**; these three items in the parts list are the only tools it names.
