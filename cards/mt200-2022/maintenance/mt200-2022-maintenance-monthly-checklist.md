**The service manual refers to the user's manual for regular maintenance and then prints its own monthly checklist.** The 7.0T/MT200 owner's manuals print no schedule table, so this is the only list of intervals for the machine.

3.1 Refer to the User's Manual for regular maintenance.

3.2 A monthly checklist of items to perform:

1. Inspect the running belt and adjust as needed — `mt200-2022-maintenance-belt-alignment-forward-and-reverse`.
2. Inspect the deck for excessive wear. **Use the motor current test as an aid** (see the electronics section).
3. **Vacuum the interior of the motor cover.** Be sure to disconnect power beforehand.
4. Inspect the drive belt.
5. **Lubricate the lead screw of the incline motor as needed.**

The chapter goes on to two further checks that are not on the monthly list: the adjustable hand rails (`mt200-2022-maintenance-adjustable-hand-rail-checks`) and the aluminium uprights (`mt200-2022-maintenance-aluminium-upright-wobble-check`).

## The motor current test

Item 2's aid is the Service Mode **Drive Motor** test in the maintenance (engineering) menu, which shows RPM and motor amps as the belt runs and prints nominal no-load readings to compare against — a worn or dry deck draws more current. The menu and its figures are a console item; the checklist only says to use them.

## Monthly here, once a year in the owner's manual

Item 3 vacuums inside the motor cover **monthly**. The 7.0T and MT200 owner's manuals say to remove the motor hood and vacuum it out **once a year**, with a monthly vacuum *underneath* the machine (`spirit-2026t-maintenance-vacuuming-and-motor-hood`). The two books do not reconcile the interval; the service manual asks for it twelve times as often.

## The deck takes no lubricant; the incline lead screw does

Item 5 is the only lubrication on the list, and it is the incline motor's lead screw, "as needed", with no product named. The owner's manuals say the hard wax deck takes no lubricant or wax (`spirit-2026t-maintenance-deck-takes-no-lubricant`); nothing in the service manual contradicts that.
