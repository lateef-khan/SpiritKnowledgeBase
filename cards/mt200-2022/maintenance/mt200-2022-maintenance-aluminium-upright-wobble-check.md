**If the uprights and console seem unstable or wobble during use, check that the mounting bolts are securely tightened** — the three bolt positions are Figures 3.3.1, 3.3.2 and 3.3.3.

Reaching the lower bolts means taking off the **upright base cap**; the manual points to section 6.4, Motor Base Cap, where two screws release each side cover.

The book gives no torque and no bolt size. The 7.0T owner's manual's noise advice — that most squeaks, thumps and clicks trace to hardware not tightened at assembly, and that a larger wrench than the one supplied may be needed — is the same fix from the other direction (`70t-2026-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`); this check is the service manual's, for a wobble rather than a noise.
