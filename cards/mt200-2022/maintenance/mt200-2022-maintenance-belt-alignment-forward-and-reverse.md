**This is the service manual's version, and it differs from the owner's manual's: it turns the bolt on the side the belt drifts to, and it checks the belt in both directions.** The owner's-manual procedure carded at `70t-2026-maintenance-treadbelt-tracking-adjustment` works from the left side only, with a 10 mm Allen wrench at 2 to 3 mph.

If the belt is running too far to one side, turn the adjusting bolts as shown in Figures 3.1.1 and 3.1.2. For example, **if the belt is too far to the right, turn the right bolt clockwise 1/4 turn at a time until the belt is in the centre.**

**Always check alignment with the belt travelling in both the forward and reverse direction.** Several adjustments may be necessary to ensure proper tracking in both directions.

The service manual names no wrench size and no belt speed for this check; the owner's manual gives both. It is item 1 of the monthly checklist (`mt200-2022-maintenance-monthly-checklist`).

## Why reverse matters on this machine

The 7.0T/MT200 runs the belt backwards for rehabilitation walking, which no other Spirit treadmill does, and a belt centred in one direction can walk off in the other. The fuller procedure printed after roller replacement says this outright and accepts a belt that sits slightly toward one side forward and the other side in reverse — `mt200-2022-maintenance-belt-tension-and-tracking-after-roller-work`.
