The service manual's maintenance chapter lists three things to confirm on the adjustable hand rails; the figures it cites are photographs, so the text is all there is.

1. **Removal of the assist handle** for therapist access to the patient's legs (Figure 3.2.1).
2. The **release/lock handles** for horizontal adjustment of the handle bar (Figure 3.2.2), and the **release/lock knobs** for vertical adjustment (Figure 3.2.3).
3. When the handle bars are adjusted vertically, **always check they are even — both sides set to the same number** (Figures 3.2.4 and 3.2.5).

The chapter gives no interval for these checks; they sit after the monthly checklist (`mt200-2022-maintenance-monthly-checklist`) rather than on it.

## Replacing rather than checking

Knob, hand rail, thrust bearing and sliding-wheel/brake-pad replacement for the same rails are in the part-replacement chapter (6.10) and are carded as replacements, not here.
