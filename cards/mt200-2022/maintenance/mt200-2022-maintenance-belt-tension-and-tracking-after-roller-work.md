**This is the fuller procedure the service manual prints after roller replacement. It sets tension by feel and slip test rather than by turns of the bolt, and it checks tracking in reverse.** The owner's manual's 1/4-to-1/2-turn procedure is `70t-2026-maintenance-treadbelt-tension-adjustment`.

1. Install both rollers and turn the **front roller adjustment bolt** (Figure 6.7.12) so the roller is in the same position as the old one — measure the axle's position on the long bolt before removal.
2. Tighten the **two rear roller bolts** until the running belt is taut (Figure 6.7.13). **The belt should be tight enough that you can just barely slide your hand under the centre of the belt to the middle — about where the Spirit logo is located.**
3. Start the treadmill at about **3 mph (5 kph)** and watch whether the belt drifts left or right. Adjust the rear roller bolts as needed until the belt stays centred (Figure 6.7.14).
4. With tracking set, check the **gap from the edge of the belt to the side of the deck** (or to the aluminium step rail if installed) is **even all the way from front to back**. If it is not, adjust the **front roller bolt** until the gap is even.
5. Check tension: walk on the belt and make sure it does not slip. Then **hold the front handles and put as much force on the belt as you can, trying to stop it with your foot.** If the belt slips, tighten the two rear roller adjustment bolts — **turn both bolts the same exact amount** so the tracking stays even.
6. Finally **run the belt in the reverse direction and check tracking.** Further adjustments may be needed as you test in both directions.

**The belt may track toward one side in forward and toward the other in reverse. This is OK as long as the belt does not start to go too far under the step rail.**

## What is different from the everyday procedure

The monthly alignment check (`mt200-2022-maintenance-belt-alignment-forward-and-reverse`) turns the bolt on the side the belt drifts to, a quarter turn at a time. This procedure adds the front-roller alignment (the gap test), the hand-under-belt tension feel and the foot-stop slip test, because after roller work the front roller's position is no longer known. Neither service-manual procedure names a wrench size; the owner's manual says 10 mm.
