The step sensor is the plunger-and-magnet assembly under each side of the deck that tells the console
someone has stepped on. Both sides are the same.

1. Raise the main frame as in the maintenance-mode procedure the book calls "Process 4.2.1", then cut the
   cable ties shown in figure 6.5.1 and remove the **metal cover** (figure 6.5.5).
2. Remove the **two screws** attaching the top part of the **plunger assembly** to the running deck (figure
   6.5.2). Both sides are the same.
3. Remove the screws on both sides of the **mounting plate**, disconnect the wire and remove the assembly
   (figures 6.5.3 and 6.5.4). Both sides are the same.
4. To adjust the step sensor magnets, remove the screws on the side of the steel cover and release the
   **locknut** to adjust the magnets (figures 6.5.5 and 6.5.6). The book sends you to its step 4.2.2 for the
   magnet calibration itself.
5. Installation is steps 1 to 4 in reverse; then "refer to Process 4.2.2 for strong magnetic calibration".

The magnet calibration is a console (maintenance-mode) procedure and is not repeated here. No screw size or
torque is printed.
