1. Loosen the screw and remove the **rear cover** (figure 6.6.1). Both sides are the same. The picture on the
   left of the figure shows the cover being refitted **using a rag and a rubber hammer**.
2. On the underside of the main frame, **just loosen - do not remove - the 4 screws** to slide the **aluminum
   step rails** off the back (figures 6.6.2 and 6.6.3). Both sides are the same.

The contents page calls this section "Rear Adjustment Base/ Aluminum Foot Rail Replacement" and the body
heading calls it "Rear End Cap/Aluminum Step Rail" - the same two parts under two names. The deck procedure
requires it first.

**This book is also the 7.0T 770885's service manual.** Spirit's February 2026 export of it is titled *7.0T-770885 (MT8000-ST021-01)* and is 99.7% the same text (the parts-list header reads MT8000 where the 2021 export reads MT7000), so the 2026 7.0T (`70t-2026`) is listed here alongside the MT200.
