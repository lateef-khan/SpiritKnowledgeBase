With the top motor cover and the front motor cover off (their own card): as shown in figures 6.3.5 and 6.3.6,
**disconnect the AC switch. If the AC switch needs replacing, remove the two screws holding it to the front
cover and install the new switch. L is black, N is white.**

That is the only wiring rule printed for the switch: the black wire goes to the L (line) terminal and the
white wire to the N (neutral) terminal. No switch rating or part number is printed here.
