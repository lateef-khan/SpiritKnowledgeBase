**Running deck (6.7.1).** Remove at least one side **parallel bar assembly** and loosen or remove the rear
roller. Do the step sensor (Process 6.5) and rear end cap / step rail (Process 6.6) procedures before removing
the deck. Then, as shown in figures 6.7.1 to 6.7.3, remove the **8 screws** on the running deck and slide the
deck out from the treadmill.

**Cushions A and B (6.7.2).** Remove the **three deck support tubes** (figure 6.7.4). Figure 6.7.6 shows the
**middle deck support tube with the belt guide attached**: put this support tube back into the middle
position, with the belt guides facing the correct way, so the belt guide can work. You can now replace the
elastomer cushions. **The two centre cushions are softer than the other four**, so make sure you replace them
with the correct part.

Six cushions in all, in two grades; the book gives no part numbers here - the exploded parts list holds
them. No screw size or torque is printed.
