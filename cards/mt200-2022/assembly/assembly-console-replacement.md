As shown in figures 6.1.1 and 6.1.2, **unscrew the four screws and the electrical connectors to remove the
console.**

That is the whole procedure - one sentence and two photographs. No screw size, no note on which connectors,
and no instruction to unplug the machine first. The 2025 support email on this machine's E27 error describes
the same access as "loosen the screws under the console bracket in order to check the wirings of the console
back".
