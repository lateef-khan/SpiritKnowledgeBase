Four small procedures, printed together as section 6.10. Their figures are numbered 6.11.1 to 6.11.12.

**Knob (6.10.1).** Loosen the screws on both sides and fully unscrew the knob to remove it (figure 6.11.1). To
install, screw the knob in and tighten the two screws.

**Hand rail (6.10.2).** Remove the **four handrail screws** and loosen the **quick release handle** (figures
6.11.3 to 6.11.6). The hand rail can now be lifted off.

**Thrust bearing (6.10.3).** Remove the mounting bolt from the adjusting arm and lift the arm off its mating
stud (figures 6.11.7 and 6.11.8). **Note the orientation of the arm on the stud** so you can put it back in the
same position (figure 6.11.10). Remove the hold-down cylinder from the arm and replace the thrust bearing
(figure 6.11.9).

**Sliding wheel and brake assembly (6.10.4).** With the knob fully loosened or removed, lift out the
**telescopic tube** and replace or adjust the brake pads and wheels (figures 6.11.11 and 6.11.12).

How the rails are adjusted in use - the knobs, the height scale and the quick-release levers - is on the
owner's-manual cards linked here; this card is the service book's disassembly.

**This book is also the 7.0T 770885's service manual.** Spirit's February 2026 export of it is titled *7.0T-770885 (MT8000-ST021-01)* and is 99.7% the same text (the parts-list header reads MT8000 where the 2021 export reads MT7000), so the 2026 7.0T (`70t-2026`) is listed here alongside the MT200.
