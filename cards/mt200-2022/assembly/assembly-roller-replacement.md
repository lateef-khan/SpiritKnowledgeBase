**Rear roller (6.7.3).** Remove the **two rear roller bolts** and slide the roller out (figures 6.7.7 and
6.7.8).

**Front roller (6.7.4).** First loosen the motor drive belt by loosening the motor bolts and sliding the motor
towards the front roller (Process 6.8). Remove the bolt holding down the left side of the roller (figure
6.7.9). Then remove the **access plate** and the **long mounting/adjustment bolt** holding the roller in
place (figures 6.7.10 and 6.7.11). **Hint: before removing the bolt, measure the position of the roller axle
on the bolt**, so that when you reassemble the new roller you can thread the bolt into the axle and land the
roller in approximately the same place as the old one.

**Setting the rollers and tracking (6.7.5).** Install both rollers and turn the front roller adjustment bolt
(figure 6.7.12) so the roller sits where the old one did. Tighten the two rear roller bolts until the running
belt is taut - tight enough that **you can just barely slide your hand under the centre of the belt to the
middle, about where the Spirit logo is**. Start the treadmill at about **3 mph (5 kph)** and watch whether the
belt drifts left or right; adjust the rear roller bolts until it stays centred (figure 6.7.14). With tracking
set, check the gap from the edge of the belt to the side of the deck (or to the aluminum step rail, if
fitted) is even from front to back; if not, adjust the front roller bolt until it is. Then check tension:
walk on the belt, and hold the front handles and push as hard as you can against the belt with your foot to
try to stop it. **If it slips, tighten the two rear roller adjustment bolts by exactly the same amount** so
tracking stays even. Finally run the belt **in reverse** and check tracking again. It may track to one side
forward and to the other in reverse; that is acceptable as long as the belt does not go too far under the
step rail.

No bolt size, torque or tension figure is printed; the hand-under-the-belt test is the only tension check.
Tracking is checked both ways because this treadmill runs in reverse as well as forward.
