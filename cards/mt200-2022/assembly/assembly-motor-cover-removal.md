Three covers, three short procedures, printed as sections 6.2 to 6.4 of the guide.

**Motor top cover (6.2).** Loosen the **front four screws and the side 4 screws** (figures 6.2.1 to 6.2.3),
then pull the motor cover up and tilt it to one side to remove it (figure 6.2.4).

**Front motor cover (6.3).** First remove the top motor cover. Then loosen the **seven screws** and remove the
**ground wire** to take the front cover off (figures 6.3.1 to 6.3.5). The AC switch is mounted in this cover;
its replacement is a separate card.

**Motor base caps (6.4).** Remove the **two screws** to remove the side covers (figures 6.4.1 and 6.4.2).

Eight screws for the top cover is this machine's count; the Dyaco commercial books use six tapping screws
and two four-screw base caps for the same job. No screw size is printed, and the guide does not say to unplug
the machine before opening the covers.

**This book is also the 7.0T 770885's service manual.** Spirit's February 2026 export of it is titled *7.0T-770885 (MT8000-ST021-01)* and is 99.7% the same text (the parts-list header reads MT8000 where the 2021 export reads MT7000), so the 2026 7.0T (`70t-2026`) is listed here alongside the MT200.
