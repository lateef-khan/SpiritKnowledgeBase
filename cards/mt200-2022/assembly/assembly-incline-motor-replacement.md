**No zeroing distance is printed for either motor.** The 4.0T book for the sister machine gives 257 mm and
210 mm; this book gives no figure.

**Front incline motor, if it can still move (6.9.1).** Raise the front to the top setting and place a
**sturdy object under the front of the machine** (figure 6.9.1). Then lower the motor until the wheels on the
lower incline frame are off the ground, taking the load off the motor. Cut any wire tie-wraps holding the
wiring to the frame members and disconnect the wiring from the controller. Then remove the motor mounting
bolts and remove the motor (figures 6.9.2 and 6.9.3).

**Front incline motor, if it cannot move (6.9.2).** Flip the treadmill onto its side to reach the motor
mounts.

**Rear incline motor (6.9.3).** Flip the treadmill on its side, cut the wiring tie-wraps and disconnect the
wires from the controller board. Then remove the motor mounting bolts and remove the motor (figures 6.9.4
and 6.9.5).

The book prints a heading "6.9.4" with nothing under it. No bolt size, wrench size or torque is printed, and
the book does not say to recalibrate the incline afterwards in this procedure.
