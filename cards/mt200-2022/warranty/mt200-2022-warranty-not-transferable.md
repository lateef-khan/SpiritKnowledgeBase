**No.** This manual says it twice, in two different places.

The opening paragraph ends with a flat sentence:

> Warranties are not transferable.

And the exclusion list repeats the rule as a case:

> Products that have been; sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party.

The same paragraph also ties the periods to "the **original purchaser** of the equipment", which is the third place the manual makes the point.

Four routes out of the original buyer's hands are named and all four end the cover: a sale, a transfer, a barter, or a gift. The manual states no way to reinstate it, names no transfer fee, and describes no procedure for a second owner.

Two neighbouring exclusions matter for the same question:

- **Products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered.**
- **Products that are used as store display models.** A demonstration unit sold on from a showroom floor is excluded twice over.

**The flat sentence is what makes this card different from its neighbours.** The 2010 MT200 manual and every XT manual rely on the third-party exclusion alone and never print "Warranties are not transferable" - see `mt200-2010-warranty-transfer` and `xt-2023-warranty-transfer`. The outcome is the same; only this manual states it as a rule rather than leaving it to be inferred from an exclusion.

The full exclusion list, and what the conversion damaged in it, is in `mt200-2022-warranty-exclusions`.
