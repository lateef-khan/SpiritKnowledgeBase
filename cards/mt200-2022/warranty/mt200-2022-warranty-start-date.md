The periods in the warranty table are counted **from the date of sale, as determined by sale receipt**.

**In the absence of a sales receipt**, they are counted from **eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date**.

Three things in the same paragraph go further than any XT manual does:

- **Spirit covers labor as well as parts, and both at its own discretion.** Spirit provides, at no charge, new or remanufactured parts "as determined by Spirit at its sole and absolute discretion", and covers the cost of labor "deemed necessary by Spirit, at its sole and absolute discretion", to remedy faults giving rise to applicable warranty claims. The phrase *sole and absolute discretion* is used twice and appears in no other Spirit warranty in this knowledge base.
- **The periods are conditional on maintenance.** They are "subject to the performance of proper care and maintenance, as set out in this user manual, **by the original purchaser** of the equipment". A clinic that cannot show it maintained the machine has no unconditional periods to rely on.
- **Warranties are not transferable**, stated flatly in the same paragraph: `mt200-2022-warranty-not-transferable`.

**The warrantor is Spirit Fitness**, stated on the back page: "This product has been manufactured by and is sold under the responsibility of Spirit Fitness, and Spirit Fitness is the warrantor in relation to this product." The copyright line reads 2020© Spirit Fitness. That is a different company name from the 2010 MT200 manual, which is warranted throughout by **Spirit Medical Systems Group** - see `mt200-2010-warranty-start-date`.

**A second, different clock is stated in the Service section**, and it is not twelve months as in every other Spirit manual here: `mt200-2022-warranty-service-clock`. The two paragraphs are never reconciled, so read both before answering how long anything runs.
