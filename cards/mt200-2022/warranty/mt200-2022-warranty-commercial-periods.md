One row, labelled **commercial**. This manual prints no residential row and no dues-paying/non-dues-paying split.

| Warranty | Frame | Drive motor | Parts | Labor | Wear items \* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| commercial | Lifetime | 10 years | 3 years | 3 years | 6 months |

The printed page breaks this single row across two stacked blocks - Frame and Drive motor above, Parts, Labor and Wear items below - which is why it can look like two tables. It is one row; the headings and the values line up as shown.

Two footnotes are printed under it:

> \* Wear items are rubber hand grips, pedals, console overlay and drive belt
>
> \*Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage.

**Both footnotes are marked with the same single asterisk** as the Wear items column, which is a defect in the source: the prisons line is a coverage exclusion for the whole warranty, not a note about wear items. Read it as a general exclusion.

**Nothing here matches the 2010 MT200 table.** That manual has no Motor column at all, splits the middle into Mech. Parts and Electronics at 3 years each, and gives Wear Items **1 year** rather than 6 months - six times as long. It also defines wear items with "drive belt**s**" plural. Two generations, two tables: `mt200-2010-warranty-coverage-periods`.

**It is close to, but not, the CT900ENT table.** The CT900ENT prints Frame Lifetime, Drive Motor 10 Years, Parts 3 Years and Labor 3 Years - the same four figures - but has no wear items row and no wear items definition, and it carries an effective date of November 19, 2021 that this manual does not print. A CT900ENT answer would leave a wear part uncovered by six months without saying so. Keep them apart: `ct900ent-warranty-coverage-terms`.

**This manual states no hours-per-day limit.** The 2010 MT200 restricts cover to clinical use up to 5 hours per day; no equivalent sentence is printed anywhere here, and none should be quoted for this machine.
