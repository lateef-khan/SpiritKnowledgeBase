**Read this card knowing the source is damaged.** Under the heading *Normal responsibilities of the facility*, the manual says "The facility is responsible for the items listed below" and then prints a bulleted list. In the converted text **only one bullet survives whole**; the rest lost their opening words to the page break between printed pages 73 and 74. The surviving text is reproduced exactly below and nothing has been filled in.

The one complete item:

> - The warranty registration must be completed online to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.

Everything else that survives, in printed order, verbatim:

```
                                                                    -
  tions provided in this manual.
• Proper installation in accordance with instructions provided with

 replacement of blown fuses, repair of loose connections or defects
 in house or facility wiring.

 time it was shipped from the factory.

 installation or following installation.
```

**What can be said and what cannot.** The fragments show that the list covers, in this order: registration, something ending "...tions provided in this manual", proper installation, the power supply and facility wiring, something about an item not part of the equipment "at the time it was shipped from the factory", and something about damage during or following installation. **What each bullet actually requires is not recoverable from this file** - the verbs and subjects are missing - so do not state any of them as a requirement.

**Do not repair the gaps from another manual.** The CT900, CT900ENT and CTSBS900 print a facility list with matching-looking tails, and the 2010 MT200 prints a five-item consumer list. Any of them would let you guess a plausible sentence, and a guess is not what this manual says. Two of those lists disagree with each other on whether "proper connection to a **grounded** power supply" is required, so the guess would not even be stable.

**Only registration is safe to quote as a requirement**, and it is backed elsewhere in the manual: `mt200-2022-warranty-registration`. For anything else on this page, read the printed manual rather than this file, and treat the gap as an unresolved documentation defect.

The other half of what governs the warranty is the exclusion list, which the same conversion damaged in the same way: `mt200-2022-warranty-exclusions`.
