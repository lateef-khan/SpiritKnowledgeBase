## What this list does and does not settle

- **Two exclusions have no XT counterpart**: store display models, and damages due to improper storage or transport. The 2010 MT200 carries the display-model one too; no XT, CT900 or CTSBS900 list carries either.
- **Improper storage or transport is new to this generation.** The 2010 MT200 does not print it.
- **There is no prisons bullet in this list.** The prisons exclusion is printed as a footnote to the warranty table instead - see `mt200-2022-warranty-commercial-periods`.
- **There is no "warranties outside of the United States may vary" bullet.** The geographic limit appears only in the closing note: `mt200-2022-warranty-geographic-coverage`.
- **An unregistered product IS excluded outright**, recovered by OCR: "Products that do not have a warranty registration on file at Spirit Fitness." This matches the 2010 MT200's exclusion 7. The of the right to request proof of purchase. Whether this manual also excludes an unregistered product outright, as the 2010 MT200 does, **cannot be read from this file**. Do not answer that question from here; see `mt200-2022-warranty-registration`.
- **The merchantability bullet is recovered in full** by OCR. Quote it as a complete sentence.
