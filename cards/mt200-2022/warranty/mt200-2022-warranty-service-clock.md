> Keep your bill of sale. **Twenty four (24) months** from the date on the bill of sale or **eighteen (18) months** from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number establishes the warranty period should service be required.

**Twenty-four months is unique to this manual.** Every other Spirit warranty in this knowledge base - the ten XT manuals, the CT900, the 2026 4.0T, 7.0T and 8.0T, and the 2010 MT200 - prints **twelve (12) months** in that slot. Nothing else in the sentence changes: the eighteen-month factory-shipping fallback and the serial-number rule are word for word the usual ones. Do not answer this from `xt-2023-warranty-labor-period` or from any card written for another machine; the figure there is half.

**The sentence sets "the warranty period", not "the labor warranty period."** The XT and 2026 manuals name labor explicitly in the same place. This manual does not, and the 2010 MT200 does not either. So the sentence reads as fixing the warranty period generally, while the table above it gives labor 3 years and parts 3 years. **The manual never reconciles the two**, and states no rule for which governs when they disagree. Both halves are real; quote the table for component periods and this paragraph for what the service section itself says.

**It also names two dates without ranking them** - twenty-four months from the bill of sale, or eighteen months from factory shipping read off the serial number - and does not say which governs when they disagree.

The manual's own advice follows from it: keep the bill of sale, and if service is performed, obtain and keep all receipts. It adds that this written warranty gives specific legal rights and that other rights vary from state to state.

The rest of that paragraph runs into the numbered service steps: `mt200-2022-warranty-service-steps`. The other clock, the one the table is counted from, is in `mt200-2022-warranty-start-date`.
