Printed **once**, at the foot of the warranty section under the service steps, and itself damaged at the start of the first line:

> ...subject to change without notice. All warranties are made by Spirit Fitness. This warranty applies only in the **48 contiguous United States**.
>
> Note: This warranty **does not apply to Alaska or Hawaii**.

The missing opening is the standard "Product features or specifications as described or illustrated are", which every other Spirit manual prints ahead of "subject to change without notice". The two sentences that carry the geographic rule are intact.

Alaska and Hawaii are named as outside the covered area. The manual states no separate Alaska or Hawaii terms and no shipping surcharge for them.

**This manual has no overseas exclusion.** The XT, CT900 and CTSBS900 lists all carry "Warranties outside of the United States may vary. Please contact your local dealer for details." No bullet in this manual's exclusion list says it, and no other sentence in the manual mentions coverage outside the United States. So the manual limits the warranty to the 48 contiguous states and then says nothing further - it does not point the facility at a local dealer, and it does not say the terms merely vary.

Treat a machine outside the 48 contiguous states as something this manual does not cover and does not explain. Do not answer it from `xt-2023-warranty-geographic-coverage`, whose second sentence is not printed here.

The **Note:** is worded "does not **apply to** Alaska or Hawaii", the same as the 2010 MT200 and the 2015-generation XT manuals; the 2023 XT manuals print "does not **include**" in the first of their two copies. The meaning does not change.
