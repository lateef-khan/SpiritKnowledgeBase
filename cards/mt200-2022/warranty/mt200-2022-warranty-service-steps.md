Keep your bill of sale. Service under this warranty must be obtained by following these steps, **in order**. The manual prints them as bullets rather than numbers, but says "in order":

1. Contact your selling authorized Spirit Fitness dealer. **OR**
2. Contact your local authorized Spirit Fitness service organization.
3. If there is a question as to where to obtain service, contact our service department at **1-870-935-1107**.
4. Spirit Fitness's obligation under this warranty is limited to repairing or replacing, at Spirit's option, the product through one of our authorized service centers. **All repairs must be preauthorized by Spirit Fitness.** If the product is shipped to a service center, freight charges to and from the service center will be the customer's responsibility. For replacement parts shipped while the product is under warranty, the customer will be responsible for shipping and handling charges.
5. The owner is responsible for adequate packaging upon return to Spirit Fitness. Spirit is not responsible for damages in shipping. Make all freight damage claims with the appropriate freight carrier. **Do not ship any unit to our factory without a return authorization number. All units arriving without a return authorization number will be refused.**
6. For any further information, or to contact our service department by email, or phone call, and also please refer to website for additional information:
   - Consumer care service email address: **spiritservice@spiritfitness.com**
   - Consumer care service phone number: **1-870-935-1107**

## What the facility pays even inside the warranty period

Two charges, both in step 4:

| What the customer pays | Where |
|---|---|
| Freight **to and from** a service center, if the product is shipped to one | step 4 |
| **Shipping and handling** on replacement parts shipped while under warranty | step 4 |

Exclusion 2 refuses **service call reimbursement to the consumer** outright.

**There is no trip charge here and no 100-mile clause.** The 2010 MT200 charges a trip charge for in-facility service and an additional one beyond 100 miles from the nearest service center; the XT manuals charge the same two for in-home service. Neither sentence is printed in this manual. Do not quote `mt200-2010-warranty-service-steps` or `xt-2023-warranty-service-steps` for this machine - they would invoice the customer for something this manual does not mention.

**There is also no mailing address.** Where the 2010 MT200 and every XT manual give a P.O. Box for written correspondence, this manual replaces step 6 with an email address and a phone number, and points at the website. **P.O. Box 2037, Jonesboro** appears nowhere in this manual.

**The phone number is formatted differently and the email is the older one.** The service department is written **1-870-935-1107** rather than (870) 935-1107, and consumer care is **spiritservice@spiritfitness.com**, not questions@spiritfitness.com.

The paragraph above the steps fixes a period this manual alone puts at twenty-four months: `mt200-2022-warranty-service-clock`. The closing note repeats the 48-states limit: `mt200-2022-warranty-geographic-coverage`.
