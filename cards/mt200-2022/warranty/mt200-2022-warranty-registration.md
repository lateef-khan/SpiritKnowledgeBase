## Is registration required?

**Yes.** It is the first of the facility's numbered responsibilities, and the only one of them the conversion left intact:

> The warranty registration must be completed **online** to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.

## How to register

> Register your product and get support at: **www.spiritfitness.com/commercialwarrantyregistration.html**

The front matter adds that this "will ensure we have all your details quickly at hand in dealing with any after sales support", and points at **www.spiritfitness.com** for chat and self-service.

**Online is the only route this manual gives.** There is no registration card, no address to post one to, and no serial-number box to fill in. That is the sharpest difference from the 2010 MT200 manual, which requires a self-addressed card back within 10 days and registers at spiritmedicalsystems.com - `mt200-2010-warranty-registration`.

## What the manual does not say

**No deadline is printed anywhere.** The 10-day window that the 2010 MT200 manual, the 2015-generation XT manuals and the 2026 Spirit treadmills all impose is absent here. Do not quote one to this machine.

**An unregistered machine IS excluded.** The exclusion list carries "Products that do not have a warranty registration on file at Spirit Fitness. Spirit Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product." `pdftotext` lost the first sentence at a page break; it was recovered by rendering printed page 74 of the source PDF at 300 dpi and reading it with `tesseract --psm 4`. See `mt200-2022-warranty-exclusions`.

So: tell a facility that registration is required to validate the warranty, and that Spirit Fitness may ask for proof of purchase without a warranty record. Do not tell them the machine is void unregistered, and do not give them a deadline.
