**p. 4** labels the shrouded machine: **Console**, **Handlebar Cover**,
**Track Slide Wheel Cover**, **Drink Bottle Holder**, **Slide Wheel Cover**, **Upright Cover**,
**Right side Shroud**, **Left Pedal**, **Rear Shroud**, **Right Slider Cover**.

**p. 5** labels the frame with the shrouds off: **Left / Right Handle Bar Assembly**,
**Console Mast**, **Connection Welding Group**, **Mast Handle bar Assembly**,
**Right Swing Assembly**, **Handgrip Stabilizer Assembly**, **Drive Pulley**,
**Driving Assembly**, **Left Driving Assembly**, **Right Driving Assembly**, **Crank Arm**,
**Right Linked Assembly**, **Left / Right Pedal Arm Assembly**, **Main Frame**, **Rail Tube**,
**Rear Stabilizer**.

The Outlines chapter carries **no dimensions, no machine weight, no maximum user weight and no
step-rate or resistance range**. Those figures are not printed anywhere in this manual.
