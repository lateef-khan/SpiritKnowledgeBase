**Upper controllers (p. 7)** - **Display** only.

**Lower controller and driver (p. 8)** - **Tension Motor** and **Speed Sensor**.

The climber has no separate driver board: the block diagram on p. 17 wires the tension motor and
RPM sensor straight to the console, and p. 10 says the main controller, including the motor
driver control circuit, is in the console.
