**190 Hz, plus or minus 10 Hz, measured with a sonic belt tension meter.**

Set it by adjusting the **idle wheel assembly**; step 3 of the same procedure slackens it with a
**13 mm open-end wrench on the J-bolt**. Before you tension it, use **17 mm box wrenches** on the
flywheel to centre the drive belt on the drive pulley, then tighten the flywheel locking nuts.
After tensioning, rotate the crank and check the flywheel, drive belt and drive pulley all run
smoothly.

The figure is printed once, in that one step. It is a **frequency**, not a newton figure. The
CU900ENT bike uses **180 to 210 newtons** instead, and the CE800ENT elliptical a different
frequency range, so do not carry a number across machines.
