- **Console** - the interface that controls all functions of the climber; key controls and an
  **LCD display**. The main controller sits inside it and includes the power supply and the
  **motor driver control circuit**.
- **Tension motor** - **work voltage DC 4~5.5 V**; controls resistance increases and decreases.

Two other pages give different figures for the same motor and are about testing, not about the
rating: p. 30 says the console drives it at **+2.5 VDC up / -2.5 VDC down** in one row and
**+4.5 VDC / -4.5 VDC** in the next, and the test procedure on p. 31 calls a normal reading
**+4 to 5.5 V DC up, -4 to -5.5 V DC down**. Only the 4~5.5 V figure here is printed as the
motor's rating.
