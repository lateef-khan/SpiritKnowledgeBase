Read from the 300 dpi render; the page is a flattened image.

The drawing names only four things: the console, marked **XS550**; a mains **power adapter**;
a **POWER SWITCH**; and the **TENSION MOTOR**. Harnesses run from the console down the mast to a
junction, and from there to the power switch, the tension motor and the speed sensor through a
stack of in-line connector shells.

**XS550** is the only console model number printed anywhere in this manual.

No voltages, no wire colours and no pin numbers are on this sheet. For pins, use the tension
motor connector cards; for the power switch and adapter, the manual prints no rating at all.
