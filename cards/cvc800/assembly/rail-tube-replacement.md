**Step 1:** Follow the steps of **9-5 Pedal Arm Assembly replacement** to take off Pedal Arm
Assembly.

**Step 2:** Use a screwdriver to remove **Slider Cover**.

**Step 3:** Use **2 14mm open-end wrench** to remove **all the bolts of Rail Tube**, and then take off
Rail Tube.

**Step 4:** To do the reverse of above steps to install all parts.

**Two 14mm wrenches** - one on each end of the bolt. The manual says "all the bolts" and gives no
count and no size.
