**Step 1:** Use a screwdriver to remove **2 screws from Slide wheel cover**, then take off Slide
wheel cover.

**Step 2:** Use a **12mm open-end wrench** to remove the **outer bolt from the joint of Linked
Assembly and Pedal Arm Assembly**. Then unmount the axle from the joint and disassemble Pedal Arm
Assembly from Linked Assembly.

**Step 3:** Use a **12mm open-end wrench** to remove the **outer bolt of the Joint of Swing Arm and
Pedal Arm**. Then unmount the axle and disassemble Pedal Arm from Swing Arm.

**Step 4:** To do the reverse of the above steps to install Pedal Arm Assembly.
**Note: The axle must be aligned with the notch when assembling.**

**Step 5:** Use a **C-ring pliers** to remove **C-ring from Slide Wheel** then unmount Slide Wheel
from Pedal Arm.

**Step 6:** Use a screwdriver to remove **Pedal from Pedal Arm Assembly**.

**Step 7:** Use a **12mm open-end wrench** to remove the bolt that secure **Swing Arm** with Main
Frame, and then take off Swing Arm.

**Step 8:** Use a **12mm open-end wrench** to remove the bolt that secure **Linked Assembly** with
**Crank**, and then take off Linked Assembly.

**Step 9:** To do the reverse of above steps to install all parts.

**This procedure prints two "reverse" steps, and step 4 is not the end of it.** Step 4 installs the
pedal arm assembly; steps 5 to 8 then go further into the machine (slide wheel, pedal, swing arm,
linked assembly) and step 9 closes the whole job. The order above is the printed order, read from the
page images of pp. 40, 41 and 42 - the two-column layout on these pages reads left column first, then
right.

**The axle-to-notch alignment in step 4's note is the one orientation the manual calls out.**

Belt** and **9-8 Crank and Drive Pulley**.
