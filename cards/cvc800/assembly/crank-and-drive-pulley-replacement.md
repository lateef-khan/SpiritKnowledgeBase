**Step 1:** Follow the steps of **9-5 Pedal Arm Assembly replacement** to take off Linked Assembly.

**Step 2:** Use a **12mm open-end wrench** to remove the bolt that secures **Crank** on Main Frame.

**Step 3:** Use a **M6 L-Allen wrench and a 13mm open-end wrench** to loose the nuts of **Crank**.
Then take off Crank.

**Step 4:** Use a **M4 L-Allen wrench** to loose **4 bolts** that secure the **Drive Pulley Axle** on
**6005 bearings**. Then take off Drive Pulley.

**Step 5:** **The Axle of Drive Pulley requires aligned with bolts of bearing before tightening up.**

**Step 6:** To do the reverse of above steps to install Crank.
**Note: The direction of the woodruff key, the round head direct to the axle.**

**The bearing is a 6005.** That is the only bearing part number printed in this manual's assembly
section.

**Two alignments, both easy to miss.** Step 5: the drive pulley axle lines up with the bearing bolts
*before* anything is tightened. Step 6's note: the **woodruff key's round head points at the axle**.

**Three wrenches for the crank alone:** 12mm open-end (frame bolt), M6 L-Allen plus 13mm open-end
(crank nuts), M4 L-Allen (drive pulley axle bolts).
