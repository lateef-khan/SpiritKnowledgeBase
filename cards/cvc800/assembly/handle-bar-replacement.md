**Step 1:** Use a screwdriver to remove **Handle Bar Cover**.

**Step 2:** Use **2 14mm open-end wrenches** to remove the **bolts and nuts** which secured Handle
Bar, then take off Handle Bar.

**Step 3:** To do the reverse of above steps to install all parts.

**Two 14mm wrenches**, one on the bolt and one on the nut. The manual gives no bolt length and no
count.

This is step 1 of the handgrip stabilizer and console mast procedure (9-3) as well.
