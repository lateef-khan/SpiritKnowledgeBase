Numbers in parentheses are assembly-drawing item numbers; quantities are on
[the hardware card](cvc800-assembly-hardware-by-step.md).

## Step zero - remove two bolts and throw them away

Use the **L Allen Wrench (No.138)** to remove the two 3/8" x 3/4" socket head cap bolts
that hold the protective **Side Back (No.23)** to the mounting plate on the main frame.
**Discard the bolts** and set the Side Back aside.

Those two bolts are shipping hardware. They are not in any hardware table and nothing
later in the build asks for them.

## Step one - rear stabiliser, console mast and mast bracket

1. Attach the **Rear Stabilizer with Handle (No.12)** to the main frame (No.1) with **2
   hex head bolts (No.93), 2 spring washers (No.126) and curved washers (No.125)**,
   using wrench No.135.
2. Pull the tie on the **Console Mast (No.2)** so **computer cable (No.41)** runs up
   through the mast and out of the top. Secure the mast to the main frame with **4 socket
   head cap bolts (No.96), 4 split washers (No.126) and 4 flat washers (No.119)**, using
   L Allen wrench No.138.
3. Secure the **Console Mast Bracket (No.13)** to the main frame with **4 socket head cap
   bolts (No.96), 4 split washers (No.126) and 4 flat washers (No.119)**. Then use **2
   hex head bolts (No.90) and 2 curved washers (No.125)** to secure the **Strap Hold
   Down (No.2)** to the console mast bracket.

**Item No.126 is called a Spring Washer in item 1 and a Split Washer in items 2 and 3.**
Its hardware table calls it SPLIT WASHER. One part, three sentences, two names.

## Step two - driving assemblies and the mast handlebar

1. Take the axle and screw out of the **Right Driving Assembly (No.19)**. Fit its lower
   hole to the axle of **Connecting Arm (R) (#8)** and fasten one **hex head bolt (No.88)
   through one flat washer (No.121)** with wrench No.136.
2. Fit the upper hole of the right driving assembly to the lower U-shaped plate of the
   **Handgrip Stabilizer Assembly (No.11)**, insert the axle into the upper hole through
   that plate and fasten it with **the screw you removed in item 1**, using wrench
   No.136. Repeat on the left side.
3. Install the **Mast Handlebar Assembly (No.14)** onto the console mast with **2 hex
   head bolts (No.90) and 2 curved washers (No.125)**, using wrench No.135.

*Item 2 re-uses hardware you took off the machine. Do not lose the axle screws in item
1; nothing in the hardware bag replaces them.*

## Step three - the seat handlebars

Install the **Seat Handle Bar (No.15L)** onto the upper handgrip stabilizer assembly
(No.11), fastening **1 hex head bolt (No.91) through 1 curved washer (No.125)** with
wrench No.135, and **2 hex head bolts (No.92) through 4 flat washers (No.119) and 2
nylon nuts (No.111)** with wrench No.136. Repeat on the right side.

## Step four - console, covers, end caps and the beverage holder

1. Plug **computer cable (No.41)** into the **Console Assembly (No.40)**, seat the
   console on the top of the mast, stuff the remaining cables into the mast tube and
   fasten **4 tapping screws (No.100)**.
2. Install **Console Mast Cover (No.68L)** and **(No.69R)** to **Chain Cover (No.70L)**
   and **(No.71R)** with **4 sheet metal screws (No.102)**.
3. Install one **Handlebar Cover (No.79L)** and one **(No.80R)** onto the upper left
   handgrip stabilizer assembly with **2 tapping screws (No.100) and 1 sheet metal screw
   (No.102)**. Repeat on the upper right.
4. Plug the **Oval End Caps (No.59)** into the ends of the front stabilizer tube of the
   main frame.
5. Install **Track Slide Wheel Cover A (No.66)** and **B (No.67)** onto the upper left
   Strap Hold Down with **2 tapping screws (No.100)**. Repeat for the lower left, upper
   right and lower right - **four sets in all**.
6. Install the **Beverage Holder (No.78)** onto the Strap Hold Down with **2 tapping
   screws (No.100)**.

## Item number 2 names two different parts

**No.2 is the Console Mast** in step one item 2 and in step two item 3, and **No.2 is the
Strap Hold Down** in step one item 3 and in step four items 5 and 6. Both readings are
printed. Go by the part name, not the number - the console mast is one tube up the
middle and the strap hold down is the rail the track slide wheel covers screw onto.

## What the build never does

No step fits the **four levellers** or the **two transport wheels** the carton panel
lists, and the book prints no way to move the machine. The build ends at step four; there
is no setting-up page and no first-power-on step.
