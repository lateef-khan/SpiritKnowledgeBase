## Every count closes

This is the only carton build in the Spirit climber and stepper range whose tables all
balance against their own steps, tested row by row from the PDF's own text:

| Item | Table | Where the step spends it |
|---|---|---|
| 96, socket head cap bolt | 8 | 4 on the mast, 4 on the mast bracket |
| 119, flat washer | 8 in step 1 | 4 on the mast, 4 on the mast bracket |
| 119, flat washer | 8 in step 3 | 4 a side on the seat handlebars |
| 125, curved washer | 4 in step 1 | 2 on the rear stabiliser, 2 on the strap hold down |
| 126, split washer | 10 | 2 on the rear stabiliser, 4 on the mast, 4 on the mast bracket |
| 100, screw | 18 | 4 console, 2 a side handlebar covers, 2 a set for four cover sets, 2 beverage holder |
| 102, sheet metal screw | 6 | 4 mast covers, 1 a side handlebar covers |

**The 18 in step four closes only if you count the beverage holder.** Item 6 is the last
two of the eighteen; on the CS800 stepper, which ships the same panel and the same
holder, the 2021 printing has no such item and its own count is two over. See
[that card](../../cs800-2021/assembly/cs800-2021-assembly-water-bottle-holder-never-fitted.md).

## The odd descriptions are the manual's own

Three rows are printed as they read here and not tidied:

- **126, ø10 x 2T"** carries a stray inch mark after a metric thickness;
- **92, 3/8" X UNC16 X 2-1/2** gives a thread series where every other row gives a
  diameter and a length;
- **88, 5/16" x 15mm** mixes an imperial diameter with a metric length, and is the only
  fastener in the book that does.

## Two fasteners in the build are in no table

**Step zero's two 3/8" x 3/4" socket head cap bolts** come off the machine and are
thrown away, and **step two item 2 re-uses the axle screw** taken out of the driving
assembly in item 1. Neither is in any hardware table, and no spare is supplied for the
axle screw.

## No torque figure anywhere

None of the four tables gives one, and no step does either.
