**Step 1:** Please follow the steps of **9-2 Handle Bar replacement** to remove Handle Bar then
process next step.

**Step 2:** Use a **12mm open-end wrench** to remove the **Axle of Driving Assembly** then take off
Driving Assembly.

**Step 3:** Use a screwdriver to remove **4 screws of Console Mast Cover** then take off Console Mast
Cover.

**Step 4:** Use a **14mm open-end wrench** to remove the bolts of **Connection Welding Group**.

**Step 5:** Use an **M8 L-Allen wrench** to remove **4 bolts** that secured Connection Welding Group
on Main Frame, then take off Connection Welding Group.

**Step 6:** Use an **M8 L-Allen wrench** to remove **4 bolts** that secure Console Mast on Main Frame
then take off Console Mast.

**Step 7:** To remove the **limit bolts** of Handgrip Stabilizer Assembly and remove its Covers.

**Step 8:** Remove the bolts that secured **Adjustable Wheels**. Use a **C-ring plier** to remove the
**C-ring on Wheel axle**, then unmount Axle and take off Wheel. Pull out Handgrip Stabilizer Assembly
from Console Mast.

**Step 9:** To loose **Adjustable Wheels** then **Slide Wheels** in the front Console Mast will be
removable.

**Step 10:** To do the reverse of the above steps to install all parts.
**Note: All Axles need to be aligned then secured back. The Adjustable wheels need to adjust to
proper tightness to let the Handgrip Stabilizer Assembly not too tight not too loose.**

**Two sets of 4 bolts, both M8 L-Allen.** Step 5's four bolts hold the **Connection Welding Group**;
step 6's four hold the **Console Mast**. Keep them apart - the manual gives no lengths.

**The note in step 10 is the whole adjustment.** Set the adjustable wheels so the handgrip stabilizer
assembly is neither tight nor loose. There is no figure and no torque.
