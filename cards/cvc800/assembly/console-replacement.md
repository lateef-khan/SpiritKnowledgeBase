**Step 1:** Use a screwdriver to remove **4 `M5*10mm`**, then unplug **computer wire** and take off
Console from console mast.

**Step 2:** Install the Console in the opposite of the above steps. **Be careful not to pinch the
computer cable during the installation process.**

The pinch warning in step 2 is the manual's own, and it is the failure that brings the console back:
a cable trapped between the console and the mast.

**The CE800ENT elliptical's console step reads almost the same but is not the same.** It removes
4 bolts of the same **`M5*10mm`** size, but it also unplugs **Handpulse wires**, which this machine's
step does not mention.
