**Step 1:** Use a screwdriver to take off **Console Mast Cover**.

**Step 2:** Use a screwdriver to remove **4 screws of Shroud upper part**.

**Step 3:** Use a screwdriver to remove **6 self-drilling screws** that fix Shrouds on Main Frame.

**Step 4:** Use a screwdriver to remove **4 thread cutting screws in front of Shrouds**.

**Step 5:** Use a **Needle nose plier** to remove the **nut of DC wire assembly** from left side
Shroud, then take off **left side Shroud**.

**Step 6:** Use a screwdriver to remove **2 thread cutting screws from Rear Shroud**. Then take off
Rear Shroud.

**Step 2 *(printed; this is the last step and the manual repeats the number 2)*:** To do the reverse
of above steps to install all parts.
**Note: Do not pinched wires when assembling parts.**

**The step numbering in this procedure is broken in the source.** It runs 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and then
the final "reverse" step is printed **"Step 2"** again on p. 40, where it should read Step 7. The
order above is the printed order, read from the page image; nothing has been resequenced.

**Three different screws.** 4 screws on the shroud upper part, **6 self-drilling** screws into the
main frame, **4 thread cutting** screws at the front, **2 thread cutting** screws in the rear shroud.
No sizes are given for any of them.

**The DC wire assembly is nutted to the left shroud.** That nut has to come off with needle nose
pliers before the left shroud will move - it is the step people tear the wire on.
