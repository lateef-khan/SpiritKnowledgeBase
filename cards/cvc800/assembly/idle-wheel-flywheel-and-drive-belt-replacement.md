**Step 1:** Follow the steps of **9-5 Pedal Arm Assembly replacement** to take off Pedal Arm
Assembly.

**Step 2:** Use a **13mm open-end wrench** to loose the nut which secure **Idle Wheel Assembly** on
Main Frame.

**Step 3:** Use a **13mm open-end wrench** to adjust **J-bolt** till Drive Belt is loose enough to
unmount from Drive Pulley. Then unmount Drive Belt from Drive Pulley.

**Step 4:** Use a **13mm open-end wrench** to remove **Idle Wheel Assembly**.

**Step 5:** **Use the console to set resistance level to max, then turn off power to keep the
resistance level on max.** Now unmount **Steel Cable** from Flywheel.

**Step 6:** Unmount **Drive Belt** from Drive Pulley.

**Step 7:** Use a **15mm open-end wrench** to loose the nuts of **Flywheel** then take off both
Flywheel and Drive belt.

**Step 8:** To do the reverse of above steps to install Flywheel and Drive belt. Use **17mm box
wrenches** to adjust **Flywheel location to let Drive belt center on Drive pulley** then secure
Flywheel locking nuts.

**Step 9:** **Adjust Idle wheel assembly to let Drive belt at right tension. (Use a sonic belt
tension meter to measure, the right value is 190Hz+/-10Hz.)** Rotate Crank to check Flywheel, Drive
belt, and Drive pulley is operating smoothly. Then install all other parts.

**Step 5 has to happen in that order.** Resistance to **maximum on the console first**, then power
**off** - that holds the tension motor at max so the steel cable can come off the flywheel. Cut the
power first and the cable stays loaded.

**Drive belt tension: 190Hz +/- 10Hz, on a sonic belt tension meter.** Do not carry a figure over
from another Spirit machine: the CU900ENT bike specifies **180 to 210 N**, a force, and the CE800ENT
elliptical prints its figure as `185Khz~210Khz`.

**Three wrench sizes, one job:** 13mm (idle wheel and J-bolt), 15mm (flywheel nuts), 17mm box (belt
centring on reassembly).
