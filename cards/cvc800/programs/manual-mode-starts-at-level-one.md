*"The Manual program works as the name implies, manually. This means that you control the program
yourself and not the computer."*

1. Press the **Manual** key, then press **ENTER**.
2. Enter your **age** with the UP/DOWN keys, then ENTER.
3. Enter your **weight** with the UP/DOWN keys, then ENTER. This is used to help generate a more
   accurate caloric reading.
4. Enter the desired length of **Time** with the UP/DOWN keys, then ENTER.
5. Press **START** to begin. To modify any setting, press **STOP** to go back one step and repeat.
6. **The program will start at level 1 to warm up.** Press UP or DOWN to increase or decrease the
   workload.
7. During the Manual program you may adjust the level at any time with the UP/DOWN keys.
8. **STOP once enters PAUSE mode; START resumes. STOP twice exits the program.**
9. When the program ends the display shows a **summary of your workout** for a short time, then the
   console returns to the start-up display.

**Unlike the Spirit steppers, a finished Manual workout cannot be saved.** This machine has no
Custom or User program, and no numbered setup procedure is printed for the five preset profiles
either - see `cvc800-programs-what-each-preset-profile-does`. A loose search of the whole book for
"custom", "user 1" and "segments" turns up no program-building procedure of any kind.

Pressing **START** from the start-up display without choosing a program instead begins **Quick
Start**, where Time counts up from zero - see `cvc800-quick-start`.
