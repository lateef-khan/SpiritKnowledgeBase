*"The climber has five different programs that have been designed for a variety of workouts. These
five programs have factory preset work level profiles for achieving different goals."* Five, and
this book describes exactly five.

- **Hill.** Simulates going up and down a hill. The resistance in the pedals steadily increases and
  then decreases during the program.
- **Fat Burn.** Designed to maximise the burning of fat. *"There are many schools of thought on the
  best way to burn fat but most experts agree that a lower exertion level that stays at a steady
  workload is the best. The absolute best way to burn fat is to keep your heart rate at around 60% to
  70% of its maximum potential. This program does not use heart rate but simulates a lower, steady
  exertion workout."*
- **Cardio.** Increases cardiovascular function and endurance - exercise for heart and lungs. Builds
  heart muscle and increases blood flow and lung capacity, by incorporating a higher level of
  exertion with slight fluctuations in work.
- **Strength.** Increases muscular strength in the lower body. Resistance steadily increases to a
  high level and you are forced to sustain it. Designed to strengthen and tone **legs and glutes**.
- **HIIT.** High levels of intensity followed by periods of low intensity. Increases endurance by
  depleting your oxygen level followed by recovery periods to replenish it. Also forces the body to
  become more efficient through spikes in heart rate between recovery periods, which aids heart rate
  recovery from intense activities.

**These are not the Spirit stepper's descriptions and must not be swapped for them.** The steppers'
Hill is a triangle from 10% of maximum effort to a peak lasting 10% of the workout; this Hill is
described in one sentence with no figures. The steppers' Fat Burn sustains maximum resistance for
2/3 of the workout; this one names a 60% to 70% heart rate instead. **The vertical climber has no
Interval program**, and its HIIT description is the wording the steppers give to *Interval*, not the
one they give to HIIT. See `spirit-stepper-programs-what-each-preset-profile-does`.

**The manual prints no setup procedure for these five programs** - no age, weight, time or max level
sequence of the kind the steppers print, and no HIIT sprint/rest/interval entry. Only the Manual
program gets a numbered procedure; see `cvc800-programs-manual-mode-starts-at-level-one`.
