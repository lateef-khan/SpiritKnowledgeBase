The climber's safety chapter prints:

> To reduce the risk of burns, fire, electric shock, or injury to persons, install the climber on a
> flat level surface with access to a 120 Volt, 15-amp grounded outlet with only the climber
> plugged into the circuit.

- **120 volt, 15 amp, grounded.**
- **Only the climber on that circuit.** Nothing else may share it.
- **A flat, level surface.**

**15 amps, not 20.** The two CT850 treadmill service manuals print this sentence otherwise
word for word, with a **20-amp** outlet: `spirit-ct850-outlet-and-circuit-requirement`. The amp
figure is the only number that differs between the two chapters. Do not carry the treadmill figure
over to this machine.

This is the figure as a requirement the installer must meet. A supply figure printed in the
outlines or specification pages is a separate specification, and this manual's electrical parts
page states only a **DC 4~5.5V** working voltage for the console, not a mains figure.

The grounding instructions on the next page (p. 26, printed 26) name the same circuit a second
time, as `a nominal 120-volt circuit`: `spirit-product-must-be-grounded`.

## The owner's manual gives three more figures, and none of them is 120 volt / 15 amp

The CVC800-2021 owner's manual, a different book from the service manual quoted above, states the
supply three times and agrees with the service manual nowhere.

| Book | Page | Printed as |
|---|---|---|
| Service manual | printed 25 | `120 Volt, 15-amp grounded outlet` |
| Owner's manual | printed 4 | `a 110-volt, **1.5-amp** grounded outlet` |
| Owner's manual | printed 5 | `The electrical outlet used should have a dedicated **5-amp** circuit breaker.` |
| Owner's manual | printed 6 | `This product is for use on a nominal **110-volt/5-amp** dedicated circuit` |

**All three owner's-manual figures were read from 400 dpi renders of the printed pages.** `1.5-amp`
is what the page prints; it is not an extraction artefact and it is not a broken `15`. The space
before the decimal point that the text layer shows is a kerning artefact of the same typeface.

**Four figures, one machine, and the manuals never reconcile them.**

- **Voltage: 120 in the service manual, 110 in both owner's-manual statements.**
- **Amperage: 15 in the service manual, 1.5 in the owner's-manual outlet sentence, 5 in both
  owner's-manual circuit sentences.**

**A 1.5-amp outlet is not a thing you can buy.** North American receptacles are rated 15 or 20 amps.
Read the 1.5 as the machine's draw rather than an outlet rating if you must reason about it, but
**say that is a reading and not what the manual says** - the sentence around it is the standard
Spirit *install on a flat level surface with access to a ... grounded outlet* sentence, which
everywhere else in the range carries a circuit rating.

**What to tell an installer.** The two independent circuit sentences both say **5 amp** and both say
**110 volt nominal**, so a dedicated 110-120 volt circuit with a 5-amp breaker satisfies every
statement except the service manual's. Quote the figures and the page each came from; do not average
them and do not pick one silently.

**The safety list around the 1.5-amp sentence is the CS800-2021 page with the nouns changed**
(`spirit-climber-safety-instructions-list-2021-stepper-and-climber`), and that book prints **5 amp**
in the same position. The CVC800 is the only Spirit machine in the range printing 1.5.
