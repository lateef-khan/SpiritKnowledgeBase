**`FLS` is Total Floors Climbed. `FPM` is Floors Per Minute.**

That is the whole of the note, printed as one line under the QUICK START procedure:
`NOTE: FLS (Total Floors Climbed), FPM (Floors Per Minute)`.

**The service manual never prints this note**, so the two abbreviations are readable only from the
owner's manual. **Neither book defines a floor** - no height, no step count - and neither gives a
range or a maximum for either reading.

**No other abbreviation on this console is expanded anywhere in either book.**
