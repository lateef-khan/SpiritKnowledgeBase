The manual calls out three things:

| Call-out | What it is |
|---|---|
| 5.5" LCD Display | The screen |
| PROGRAM BUTTONS | **Manual, Hill, Fat Burn** down the left of the screen; **Cardio, Strength, HIIT** down the right |
| Control Keys | The row below the screen |

The control keys, as drawn: **down arrow**, **ENTER**, **up arrow** on the upper row, with **START**
at the lower left and **STOP** at the lower right.

The LCD carries a **TIME** window at the top, a row of smaller value windows below it, a heart
symbol beside a pulse field, a **LEVEL** window, and a dot-matrix profile block underneath.

The console is a self-contained head on the console mast; there is no separate handlebar keypad.
