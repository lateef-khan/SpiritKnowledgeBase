The Bluetooth connection pairs with **either** a phone or tablet (**APP**) **or** a wireless
Bluetooth chest strap (**BLECHESTSTRAP**) - not both. **The default is APP.**

To change it:

1. In **IDLE MODE**, press **ENTER for 2 seconds**. You are now in SETTING MODE.
2. The message window shows **`APP`**. Press **LEVEL UP** or **LEVEL DOWN** to change between `APP`
   and `BLECHESTSTRAP`.
3. Press **STOP / ENTER** to return to IDLE MODE.

The connection will now pair with a Bluetooth chest strap.

**The setting does not stick.**

> NOTE: If machine is powered off/on, the console will automatically revert to the default setting of
> connecting to "APP" - to change to connect to "BLECHESTSTRAP" repeat steps above.

So a facility that leaves chest straps on the machines has to reselect BLECHESTSTRAP after every
power cycle. That is by design, not a fault.
