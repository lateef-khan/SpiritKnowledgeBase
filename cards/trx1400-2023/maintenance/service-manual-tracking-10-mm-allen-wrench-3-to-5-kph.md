**The service manual (Dyaco T3-NT053-01) and the owner's manual give different figures for the same bolt.** The owner's manual says a 6 mm Allen wrench at 3 mph (`xterra-treadmill-maintenance-belt-tracking-6-mm-allen-wrench-left-bolt-3-mph`); this page says:

- The performance of the treadmill depends on the frame running on a reasonably level surface. If the frame is not level, the front and back rollers cannot run parallel and constant belt adjustment may be necessary.
- It is normal for some belts to drift near one side while the belt is running with no one on it; after a few minutes of use the belt should tend to centre itself.
- **A 10 mm Allen wrench** is provided to adjust the rear roller. Make tracking adjustments **from the left side only**. Set belt speed at **approximately 3 to 5 kph**.
- **Turn the bolt clockwise to move the belt to the right.** Turn the bolt only a 1/4 turn and wait a few minutes for the belt to adjust itself. Continue in 1/4 rotations until the belt stabilizes in the centre.

The 10 mm figure and the kph speed sit in a chapter that also cites 220 VAC and 50 Hz, so this is a generic Dyaco page rather than one written for the North-American TRX1400. The TRX2500, TRX3500 and TRX4500 service manuals print the owner's-manual figures (6 mm, 3 mph) instead. Every one of these pages closes with: **damage to the running belt resulting from improper tracking / tension adjustments is not covered under the warranty**.
