Two line drawings with boxed callouts and no numbers.

**Page 7, the whole machine**: **Console Assembly** (an inset of the console head with its safety-key lanyard),
**Incline/Hand Pulse Complex** pointing at the left handlebar end, and **Speed/Hand Pulse Complex** pointing at the
right. Incline keys on the left grip, speed keys on the right.

**Page 8, the motor compartment seen from the front with the hood off**: **Motor Controller Board** (the finned
board at the left), **Incline Motor** (the gearbox with its telescopic tube, centre) and **Drive Motor** (the
cylinder at the right).

**Ghost text.** The text layer of these two pages also carries Chinese labels - 電子錶(含安全開關、喇叭), 速度開關與手握心跳
複合組, 揚升開關與手握心跳複合組, 馬達, 揚升馬達, 下控板 - that are not printed on the rendered page. Only the English
callouts above are visible.

No dimension is printed and the book has no specification table.
