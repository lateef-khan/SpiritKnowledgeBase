The board photographed is silkscreened **A0127-V14**, dated **2017-01-03**, with an "EW" logo, a "P0034 Passed"
sticker and a serial sticker 04210361A00021. Nine callouts:

| Callout | Where on the board |
|---|---|
| Connection for 5-pin Main control wires | black 5-pin header, left edge, upper |
| Connection for Safety key | small white socket, left edge, below the header |
| Connection for Bluetooth | white socket, left edge, lower |
| Connection for KEYBOARD | long white socket along the bottom edge (silkscreen KEY) |
| Connection for FAN | small white socket, bottom centre |
| Connection for 2-pin of AMP power wires | red 2-pin socket, bottom centre-right |
| Connection for Wireless heart receiver | white socket, bottom right (silkscreen WP) |
| Connection for Handrail Pulse | white 2-pin sockets at the right edge, by the EARTH pad |
| Heart Module | daughter board at the top right with a round sensor can |

A red 6-pin socket in the middle of the board (silkscreen "Program") is not called out. Section 6.2's "PCB Board
Top" and "PCB Board Bottom" are uncaptioned photographs of the same board. The block diagram draws the Bluetooth box
this socket serves.
