**105 rows.** Two console key boards are named by code - *Key Board-AK0127F* (020-11) and *Key Board-AK0127G* (020-12) - beside an *Interface Board* (020-13), a *Resistance Button W/Cable* (020-10) and a *Bluetooth* module (020-20); the lower controller is *Motor Controller* (042) with the computer cables at 1000 / 1150 / 1000 mm (045-047). Rows 104 and 105 are the packed M5 Allen wrench and the lubricant. The drawing's text layer also carries *SPIRIT 美國* beside the code, a Dyaco job label not printed for the reader.
