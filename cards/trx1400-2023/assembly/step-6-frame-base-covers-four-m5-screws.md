Install the **left and right frame base covers (24, 25)** on the frame base and the uprights and fix them
by tightening **four M5 x 15 mm Phillips head screws (83)** with the combination M5 Allen wrench and
Phillips head screwdriver (104).

"NOTE: Please tighten all screws after all components assembly is complete." This is the last assembly
step; the setting-up page (levelling and first power-on) follows.

The TRX4500 and TRX5500 fit the same kind of covers with **six** M5 x 15 mm screws (their item 126);
this machine's four screws are item 83. The PDF page carries the same instruction twice in its text
layer (a ghost "STEP 6" block); it is printed once.
