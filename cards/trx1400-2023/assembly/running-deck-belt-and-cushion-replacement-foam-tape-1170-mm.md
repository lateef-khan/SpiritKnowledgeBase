"Please take the cylinder before replacement."

1. With the running board folded, use an **M5 L Allen wrench and a 12 mm wrench** to remove the securing
   screws on the **cylinder** and remove it.
2. Perform step **12.6** (roller replacement) and remove the front and rear rollers.
3. Take down the foot rail tablet and remove each **foot rail** in the arrow direction. Clean the foam
   tape from the foot rail and the running deck.
4. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **8 screws securing the running board**. Remove the board and
   replace the board or the running belt. If the cushions need replacing, **remove the 6 cushions** and
   replace them. Reassemble in reverse order.
5. Use a **14 mm open-end wrench** to adjust the drive belt tension with a tension measuring device to
   the **white LBS area between 70-75 lb**; tighten the 4 motor securing screws with the 14 mm T-shaped
   socket wrench.
6. Prepare **double-sided foam tape 1T x 10 mm x 1170 L, 4 pieces** - each foot rail gets two, as the
   arrows show.
7. Paste the foot rails onto the running deck.

**The tape here is 1170 mm; the TR260 book says 1155 mm** for its foot rails. The TRX2500-5500 books do
not use tape at all - their rails are screwed.
