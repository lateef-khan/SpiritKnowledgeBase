**This machine's rear roller screws take an M5 Allen key and its front roller an 11 mm wrench**; the
TR260 and TRX2500-5500 books say M6 and 13 mm for the same job.

1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to loosen the **2 screws on the rear adjustment base**.
2. Use an **M5 L Allen wrench** to loosen the **2 screws on the rear roller**.
3. Remove the motor cover; use a **14 mm T-shaped socket wrench** to loosen the 4 motor screws and a **14
   mm open-end wrench** to loosen the 1 belt tension screw, and loosen the drive belt. Use an **11 mm
   wrench** to loosen the front roller screws; remove the front and back rollers and replace. Reassemble
   in reverse order. When reassembled, the running belt tension needs to be adjusted and centred.
4. With the 14 mm open-end wrench adjust the drive belt tension with a tension measuring device to
   the **white LBS area between 70-75 lb**, then tighten the 4 motor screws with the T-socket wrench.

The deck replacement procedure (12-7) tells you to "perform step #12.6" - this one - to get the rollers
out. The owner's manual's tracking page says a 6 mm Allen wrench is provided for the rear roller
adjustment bolts; the service manual's M5 is for the roller's own locking screws.
