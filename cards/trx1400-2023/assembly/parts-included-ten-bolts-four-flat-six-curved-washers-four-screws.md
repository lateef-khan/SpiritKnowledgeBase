**Tools included:** Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screwdriver (#104, 1 pc) - the only tool.

**Parts included:** 1 Main Frame, 2 Uprights, 2 Frame Base Covers, 1 Console, 1 Deck, 1 Power Cord,
1 Lubricant, 1 Hardware Kit.

**Assembly Pack Check List**

| Item | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| #95 | Ø8 x Ø23 x 1.5T Curved Washer | 6 |
| #79 | Ø8 x Ø18 x 1.5 mm Flat Washer | 4 |
| #94 | 5/16" x UNC18 x 1/2" Button Head Socket Bolt | 10 |
| #83 | M5 x 15L Phillips Head Screw | 4 |
| #105 | Lubricant | 1 |
| #48 | Safety Key | 1 |

Six bolts, the four flat washers and two curved washers go into the uprights (step 3); four bolts and
four curved washers hold the console support (step 5); the four M5 screws fix the frame base covers
(step 6). Step 3's text describes the flat washer as "Ø5/16" x Ø18 x 1.5T" - the same #79 washer.
