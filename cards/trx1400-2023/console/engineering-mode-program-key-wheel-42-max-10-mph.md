After removing the safety key, press **PROGRAM** and put the safety key on again to enter the engineering mode. The settings come in this sequence:

| Step | Setting | Default |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wheel size | 42 |
| 2 | Unit of speed | metric 0, English 1 |
| 3 | Minimum speed | 0.5 MPH |
| 4 | Maximum speed | 10 MPH |
| 5 | Incline level | 10 |

Press Speed Up/Down or Incline Up/Down to change a parameter and **ENTER** to move to the next. After the last parameter, press **START** to start calibrating and return to ready mode.

The Dyaco T3-NT053-01 service manual prints the same entry and order but writes the speeds as "minimum speed to 10" and "maximum speed (if needed) to 160" — tenths of a kilometre per hour, 1.0 and 16.0 km/h, which are the metric equivalents of 0.5 and 10 mph — and adds "do not stand on the belt" because the belt starts without warning. The service manual gives the console's speed work range as 1.0-16.0 km or 0.5-10 miles ([ranges card](display-modes-and-window-ranges-per-the-service-manual.md)).
