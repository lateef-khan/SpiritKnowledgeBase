**This is the TRX1400 row**; the TR260 and TRX books print a six-item cable version (`xterra-treadmill-errors-incline-buttons-not-working-incline-cable-six-checks`).

*Condition:* UP/DOWN button of INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH can't be used. Incline button can only press UP, can't press DOWN. Incline button can only press DOWN, can't press UP.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1. The incline VR or incline power wires not connected properly | 1. Connect the wires again |
| 2. The incline VR or incline power wires are damaged | 2. Replace the cable |
| 3. Maybe keys stuck | 3. Replace buttons |
| 4. Inspect whether the incline motor is stuck | 4. Replace the incline |
| 5. Inspect whether the incline gears are cracked | 6. Replace the incline |

The solutions are numbered 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 in the book; there is no 5. The same motor and gear checks appear in the INCLINE Err troubleshooting table (`trx1400-2023-errors-incline-err-no-vr-change-when-incline-runs`), and the voltage test for the incline circuit is `trx1400-2023-errors-incline-test-procedure-nine-steps`.
