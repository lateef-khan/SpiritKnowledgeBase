The TRX1400 service manual's 10.2 *Service Troubleshooting Checklist* repeats the owner's five-item row with one figure changed:

1. Tether cord not in position.
2. Circuit breaker on front grill tripped. Push circuit breaker in until it locks.
3. Plug is disconnected. Make sure plug is firmly pushed into **220 VAC** wall outlet.
4. Breaker panel circuit breaker may be tripped.
5. Treadmill defect. Contact your dealer.

The owner's manual for the same machine says "AC wall outlet" with no figure (`xterra-treadmill-errors-display-does-not-light-five-checks-ac-outlet`), and the service manual's own circuit diagrams are drawn for a 110 V build (T3-NT053 110V) and a 220 V CEGS build. The 220 VAC line is the book's export wording, not evidence of the US machine's supply; the safety cards hold the supply the machine is built for. The same checklist's speed row is worded for 220 V 50 Hz too (`trx1400-2023-errors-only-reaches-10-kph-16-awg-220-volt-50-hz`).
