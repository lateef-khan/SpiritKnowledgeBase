The TRX1400 service manual's 10.2 *Service Troubleshooting Checklist* prints a four-item row no other Xterra book has:

*Treadmill with noises*

1. If the noise is coming from the rollers, . (the sentence is printed unfinished)
2. If the noise comes when the user is running with the lowest level of incline, it could be due to too much pressure on the incline cylinder (only at the lowest incline level).
3. If there is a knocking noise during the workout, check and make sure all bolts are tightened.
4. A thumping noise while the belt is running happens with a brand-new treadmill, or one that has not been used for a long time: the belt has taken the shape of the rollers and hardened because of low temperature. Running the belt for tens of minutes, the thumping noise will gradually go away.

The book's other two noise rows are shared with the matrix: `xterra-treadmill-errors-noise-under-motor-cover-brushes-bearings-drive-belt` and `xterra-treadmill-errors-noise-at-rear-roller-bearings-or-misaligned`.
