1. Press **PROGRAM** to select **H-1** and press **ENTER** to enter HRC mode.
2. Enter the **time** setting with Speed up/down, press ENTER.
3. Enter the **heart rate** setting with Speed up/down, and press **START**. The treadmill
   begins to run.
4. After starting, the **initial speed is 0.5 mph**; speed and incline can be adjusted
   manually. **The treadmill automatically adjusts the incline according to the user's heart
   rate.**
5. **You must grip both pulse grips for the HR reading during the program.**

The book prints no default or range for the time or the heart-rate target, and no percentage
formula. The service manual says the ENTER button in HRC "can set time, age and the value of
heart rate" - an **age** step the owner's manual does not print - and in one place says the
console "also has H-1 and H-2", though its PROGRAM description and the owner's manual name
only H-1.
