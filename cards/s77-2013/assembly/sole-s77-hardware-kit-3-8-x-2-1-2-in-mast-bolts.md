Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | #123 | 3/8" x 2-1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 6 pcs |
| 3 | #86 | 3/8" x 2T | Split Washer | 6 pcs |
| 3 | #79 | Dia 10 mm x Dia 19 mm x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| 3 | #100 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #124 | Dia 8 mm x Dia 23 mm x 1.5T | Curved Washer | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #91 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #86 | 3/8" x 2T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 6 | #125 | 3.5 mm x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 6 pcs |
| 7 | #100 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 8 | #127 | M5 x 10 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |

Tools in the box: #103 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver, #104 M6 L Allen Wrench.

This kit differs from the AS77 2012 kit: mast bolts 3/8" x 2-1/2" (not 2"), flat washers (not star), plus curved washers, 3.5 x 16 mm cover screws (not M5 x 10 mm plus self-tapping), and step-8 screws numbered #127 (not #92). Only four #100 bolts are supplied but they are cited in both step 3 (mast fronts, with the curved washers) and step 7 (handrail support); this card follows the supplied count of four.
