Eight illustrated assembly steps (hardware item numbers in brackets):

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill from the carton onto a smooth, level floor with the rear at least 3 ft from any wall. Do not remove the Velcro belt until the plastic wrap and Styrofoam are out from under the unit.
2. Slide the console mast tube covers (99 & 112 onto 5, 98 & 114 onto 4) onto the mast tubes with the holes of 99 & 98 facing outward, and connect Lower Computer Cable (32) to Middle Computer Cable (33) on the right side.
3. Bolt the left (5) and right (4) mast tubes with brackets (7 left, 8 right) to the base frame: three Flat Washers (79), three Split Washers (86) and three Button Head Socket Bolts (123) per side, plus two Button Head Socket Bolts (100) with Curved Washers (124) at the front of each side. Partially tighten; do not finish until after step 7. Do not pinch the cables in the tubing.
4. Rest the console at height or have someone hold it; connect Speed Switch Cable (37) to upper Speed Cable (34), Computer Cable (33) to upper Computer Cable (31), and Incline Switch Cable (38) to upper Incline Cable (35).
5. Fix the Console Assembly (36) onto the uprights with two Button Head Socket Bolts (91) and two Split Washers (86) per side; tighten completely with the M6 wrench.
6. Fix the left (111 & 112) and right (113 & 114) handgrip side caps with six Sheet Metal Screws (125).
7. Bolt the handrail support (3) between the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (100), fit the beverage-holder covers (129 & 130) on the Beverage Holder (120), set it on the support, then completely tighten the four mast-to-frame bolts on each side.
8. Slide the mast tube covers (99 & 98) to the bottom of each tube and fix with two Phillips Head Screws (127) per side.

Only four #100 bolts are supplied but they are cited in both step 3 and step 7; this card follows the supplied count of four.
