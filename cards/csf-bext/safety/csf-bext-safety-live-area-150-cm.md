**150 cm (59.06") beyond the training area**, on every side the bench is entered from, and the live
area must include the room for an emergency dismount.

> The live area shall be not less than 150cm (59.06") greater than the training area in the
> directions from which the equipment is accessed. The live area must also include the area for
> emergency dismount.

The plan view on the same page dimensions the training area as **820 x 1422 mm**, so the live area is
at least 3820 x 4422 mm if the bench is approached from all four sides.

This figure belongs to **CSF-BEXT, the functional back extension bench** — not to **CSS-BEXT**, the
selectorized back extension machine, which asks for 600 mm. They are different machines with similar
ids.
