To find the benefit range you want to train in, first work out your **Maximum Heart Rate**:
**220 minus your age**. Your heart rate training zone is **50% to 90%** of that maximum.

**This manual prints 65% and 80%** - 65% of your MHR recommended for burning fat, 80% for
strengthening the cardiovascular system, and 65% to 80% as the zone to stay in for maximum benefit.

**Its worked example does not add up.** Exactly as printed:

- 220 - 40 = **180** (maximum heart rate)
- 180 x .65 = **117** beats per minute (65% of maximum)
- 180 x .80 = **225** beats per minute (80% of maximum)
- "So for a 40 year old the training zone would be **117 to 225** beats per minute."

**180 x 0.80 is 144, not 225. 225 is above the stated maximum heart rate of 180**, which the same
page defines as the ceiling. The manual is internally inconsistent and the printed upper figure
cannot be right. The CE850 2016, CE800 2013 and CES880 2025 manuals, which use the same 80% figure,
all print **144** and a 108-to-144 zone.

**Do not repeat 225 to a customer as a training target.** The manual's own rule - a percentage of
220 minus your age - is what the console applies when you enter your age.

If you enter your age during programming the console performs the calculation automatically. The
page closes by saying that with all Heart Rate programs you may use the heart rate monitor feature
without using the Heart Rate program.
