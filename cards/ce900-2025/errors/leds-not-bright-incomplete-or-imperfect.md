| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| LED light is broken | Replace with new LED or console |
| Power to console too low | Check power to console. Replace lower controller. |

**This manual prints no voltage figure.** The CT850 2020 treadmill prints the same row with
`Check AC power is 120V` added, and the CT850 2016 treadmill and CVC800 climber print
`Check AC power is 110-120V`. Do not quote a voltage from those manuals against this machine:
`ct850-2020-led-dim-or-incomplete` and `spirit-lcd-dim-or-incomplete`.

The CES880 2025 elliptical answers the same symptom with **"make sure pedal more than 25 RPM"**
instead, because that machine powers its console from a generator:
`ces880-2025-errors-lcd-not-bright-incomplete-or-imperfect`.

Dead segments rather than a dim light are the next row down:
`ct850-2020-led-displays-dim-or-incomplete`.
