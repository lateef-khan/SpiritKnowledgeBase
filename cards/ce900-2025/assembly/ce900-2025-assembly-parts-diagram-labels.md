The drawing is headed **PARTS OF YOUR ELLIPTICAL** and its labels are lettered A to M:

| Letter | Part |
|---|---|
| A | Swing Arms |
| B | Console |
| C | Console Fan |
| D | Tablet Rack |
| E | Pulse Sensors |
| F | Water Bottle Holder |
| G | Step Rail |
| H | Flywheel |
| I | Transport Wheel |
| J | Front Stabilizer |
| K | Pedals |
| L | Main Frame |
| M | Rear Stabilizer |

## F is the cup holder

The assembly step for the same part calls it **CUP HOLDER (99)**, and the carton list
on the facing page says "1 Cup Holder". One part, two names in one manual. It bolts to
the handlebar bracket with two self-tapping screws, not to the console mast like the
CE800 and CE850 holders. See
[the bottle holder card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ce-assembly-water-bottle-holder.md).

## The 2021 manual prints no diagram at all

The CE900 2021 manual has no PARTS OF YOUR ELLIPTICAL page and no labelled drawing. It
goes from the safety pages straight to pre-assembly. **This diagram is the only thing
about the CE900 that the 2025 printing adds** - the four assembly steps and all four
hardware panels are word for word the same in both books. See
[the build card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ce900-assembly-procedure.md).

## Two labels name parts the carton list does not

**I, Transport Wheel** and **K, Pedals** are on the drawing and on no parts panel;
neither is fitted by any assembly step. They arrive attached to the main frame.
**Levellers are on neither** the drawing nor the carton list, although the noise item
says the machine has six.
