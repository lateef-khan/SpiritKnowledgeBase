Three labels, each in a different place:

| Label | Where the manual says it is |
|---|---|
| Serial number sticker | on the **front stabilizer** |
| Proposition 65 warning | on the **side of the console** |
| Safety warning sticker | on the **right side of the console** |

**Serial number sticker**

> This sticker will be found on the front stabilizer. Please record the number below the barcode for
> the purpose of registering your Elliptical's warranty.

Record the number **below the barcode**. The label artwork shows a row of `x` characters in place of
a number and the telephone number **(870) 935-1107**; neither is your machine's serial number.

**Proposition 65 warning**

> This sticker will be found on the side of the console. The State of California requires us to
> inform you that this unit was manufactured using chemicals that could cause harm with improper
> use.

**That is all this manual says about Proposition 65.** It names **no chemical** and prints **no
www.P65Warnings.ca.gov address**. The earlier Spirit elliptical manuals print the other Proposition
65 text, which names Toluene and Acrylamide and gives the web address, in the manual rather than on
a sticker (`xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`). The two are not interchangeable in either
direction.

**Safety warning sticker**

> This sticker will be found on the right side of the console. The same warning can be found in this
> manual. Please read and be aware of the precautions before operating your unit.

The manual does not print the sticker's own wording anywhere and does not say which page it means,
so there is no sticker text to quote for a CE900. What the manual prints as its warning is the
eleven-item safety list (`spirit-ce900-safety-instructions-list`).

**The CES880 puts all three somewhere else** - serial number and Proposition 65 on the rear
stabilizer, safety warning on the front of the shroud
(`ces880-2025-safety-product-labels`). Never read a location off one machine for the other.

**No other Spirit elliptical manual has a product labels page.** The CE800, CE850, CE800ENT and
CE900-2021 manuals describe no stickers at all
(`spirit-ce-safety-serial-number-location`).
