Hardware supplied in the box with the Sole ST90-2023, grouped by assembly step. Unlike the ST90-2021 kit there are no armrest-ring bolts and no M8 cap bolts: the console bottom bolts straight to the uprights. The #123 size (3/8" x 1/2") read off the page rendering.

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | #120 | Dia 3/8" x 16 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 8 pcs |
| 1 | #121 | Dia 10 x 1.5T | Split Washer | 8 pcs |
| 1 | #122 | M10 x P1.5 x 15L | Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 pcs |
| 2 | #120 | Dia 3/8" x 16 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| 2 | #121 | Dia 10 x 1.5T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 2 | #123 | 3/8" x 1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #125 | M5 x 15 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| 4 | #126 | 3.5 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |

Tools in the box: #128 Combination M5 Allen Wrench & Phillips Head Screw Driver, #127 M6 L Allen Wrench.
