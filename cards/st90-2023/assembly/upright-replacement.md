1. Use a **No. 5 L-Allen wrench** to remove the **4 M10 button head socket bolts, split washers and flat washers** that lock the upright.
2. Pull the upright out along with the wires.
3. Reverse the steps to install.

Take the upright cover off first; it is held by a plastic latch.
