1. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **3.5 x 16 mm umbrella head self-tapping screws** from the PAD frame, then take the frame off.
2. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **M5 x 15 mm umbrella head screws** from the bracket, disconnect the wires, and remove the console (the manual calls it the electronic meter).
3. Reassemble in reverse order.

A new console needs a calibration afterwards.

**The screw sizes differ from the earlier ST90 manual**, which gives 3.5 x 12 mm for the pad holder and four Allen bolts with spring and flat washers for the console.
