1. Use a **No. 8 L-Allen wrench and a No. 17 open-end wrench** to remove the screws and the nylon nuts of the back roulette wheel.
2. **The belt falls off once the back roulette wheel is out.**
3. Reverse the steps to install.

**This manual gives no belt tension or alignment figures for the refit.** The earlier ST90 manual sets the roller axle to mainframe gap at 15 to 18 mm on both sides and judges the tension from the rear roller gear and belt rack gap.
