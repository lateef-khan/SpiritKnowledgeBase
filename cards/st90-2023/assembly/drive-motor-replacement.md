1. Use a **No. 6 L-Allen wrench** to remove the **4 M8 socket head cap bolts, split washers and flat washers** from the main frame.
2. Unplug all the motor wires **from the inverter**.
3. Reverse the steps to install.

The drive motor lands on the inverter's AC MOTOR terminal on three wires, U red, V white and W black, plus an encoder cable. It has no M+/M- pair.
