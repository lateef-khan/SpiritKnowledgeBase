1. Use a **No. 8 open-end wrench** to remove the **M5 nylon nuts** and the fixed washer on each side.
2. To replace the front roulette wheel, remove **10 aluminium pedals** first. Then use a **No. 8 L-Allen wrench and a No. 17 open-end wrench** to remove the screws and nylon nuts of the front roulette wheel.
3. Repeat steps 1 and 2 to remove all the aluminium pedals.
4. Reverse the steps to install.

**This manual gives no belt alignment or roller gap figures for the refit.** The earlier ST90 manual does: set the gap between the roller axle and the mainframe to 15 to 18 mm on both sides, then use the rear roller gear and belt rack gap to judge the belt tension.
