Four illustrated assembly steps for the slat-belt ST90-2023. Unlike the ST90-2021 book there are no armrest rings and no drink-bottle holder: a split console bottom (60) bolts straight to the uprights (hardware item numbers in brackets).

1. Place the left upright (5) and right upright (4) on the main frame, connect Computer Cable (45-1) of the right upright (4) to Computer Cable (45) of the main frame (1), and secure with eight Button Head Socket Bolts (122) through eight Split Washers (121) and eight Flat Washers (120) using the L wrench (127).
2. Connect Computer Cable (45-1) of the right upright (4) to Computer Cable Upper (60-24) of Console Assembly Bottom (60). Install the console bottom (60) on the uprights (4) and (5) with four Button Head Socket Bolts (123) through four Split Washers (121) and four Flat Washers (120). Then tighten the step-1 bolts and install Upright Covers (L&R) (55 & 54) on the uprights (5 & 4).
3. Connect Computer Cables Upper (60-24, 60-25), Computer Cable (60-26) and Computer Cable Lower (60-27) of the console bottom (60) to Computer Cables (83-17, 83-14, 83-15) and Handpulse Wire (83-16) of Console Assembly Top (83). Place the top (83) on the bottom (60) and secure with four Phillips Head Screws (125) using the Phillips driver (128).
4. Place the PAD holder on the console assembly (83) and secure with four 3.5 x 16 mm Sheet Metal Screws (126) using the Phillips driver (128).
