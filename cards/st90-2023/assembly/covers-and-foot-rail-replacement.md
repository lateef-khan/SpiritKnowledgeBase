1. The **upright cover** is fixed with a plastic latch. Apply force evenly on each side and pull it out.
2. Remove the **8 self-tapping screws** from the front and back cover and take it off.
3. Remove the **8 self-tapping screws** of the foot rail limit piece and take the foot rail off.
4. Reverse the steps to install.
