1. Use **two No. 17 open-end wrenches** to remove the bolts and nuts from the bearing seat.
2. Reverse the steps to install.

**This manual carries no alignment procedure for the bearing seat.** The earlier ST90 manual does, and warns against doing this replacement unnecessarily: with the rollers screwed to the end of the mainframe, mount the seat loose, use a ruler of at least 50 cm to align the limit wheels with the roller so the ruler-to-roller distance is about 0.5 mm at both the front and the rear roller, then tighten all three seat bolts.
