1. Use a screwdriver to remove the **4 M5 Phillips head screws** from the inverter and unplug all the wires.
2. Use a screwdriver to remove the **4 M5 Phillips head screws** from the adaptor and the filter, and unplug their wires.
3. Reverse the steps to install.

**A new inverter needs a calibration afterwards.** The inverter is what the troubleshooting matrix means whenever it says "controller" or "lower controller" on this machine.
