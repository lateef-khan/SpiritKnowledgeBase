1. Remove the rapid button **housing plug**, then use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the self-tapping screws. The housing comes off.
2. Pull the rapid button **circuit board** outward to remove it, and disconnect the wiring.
3. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **two screws** holding the rapid button **inner shell**, and take it off.
4. The left and right upright tube rapid buttons come apart the same way. Follow the reverse steps to reinstall.

Run the console **Key Test** afterwards to confirm the console sees the new button.
