1. Use a **No. 17 open-end wrench** to remove the bolts and nuts from the incline motor.
2. Use a **No. 13 open-end wrench** to remove the **M8 hex head bolts** from the connecting seat, and remove it to get at the incline rack.
3. Reverse the steps to install.

**Calibrate after fitting the new motor.** The incline motor carries four power wires (red UP, black DOWN, white COM, green ground) and a 3-pin position sensor, the VR.
