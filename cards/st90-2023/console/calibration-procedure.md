1. From the settings page, press **Settings** ten times to reach the engineering page.
2. Press **Engineering Mode** to enter Calibration.
3. Hit the block to type a value on the digital keyboard, then press **ENTER** to save and close the keyboard. If the value you type is outside the range, the display shows the maximum or minimum instead.

| Unit system | Max | Min |
|---|---|---|
| English | 12.0 | 0.5 |
| Metric | 20.0 | 1.0 |

4. Press the **Calibration** button to start the procedure.

Calibrate after replacing the console, the inverter or the incline motor, and after an incline error.

**The earlier ST90 manual prints a wider set of limits** for the same machine family, with a max speed range of 12.0 to 13.0 MPH / 20.0 to 22.0 KPH and a separate max incline of 10 to 15%. This manual prints only the speed pair above and no incline range.
