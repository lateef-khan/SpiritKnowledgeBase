**Language** is in the settings page on this console, not in the engineering menu.

Choose the interface language you want from the list.

**The manual does not print the list of available languages for this machine.** The earlier ST90 manual listed 13 languages under the engineering menu's Maintenance page; this manual gives no list at all.
