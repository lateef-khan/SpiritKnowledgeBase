Two different timers, and they are not the same feature.

| Feature | Where | Timer |
|---|---|---|
| Sleep mode | Settings page, Sleep Mode | The unit goes into an energy save state after **15 minutes** of inactivity, when sleep mode is on |
| Retail mode | Same page | The console runs a **demo video** after **3 minutes** of inactivity |
| Display Mode | Engineering menu, Machine Information | Off means the console powers down automatically after **30 minutes** of inactivity when the safety key is removed |

**The manual gives 15 minutes and 30 minutes on two different pages** for what a reader may take to be the same timer. They are separate settings: Sleep Mode is the settings page energy saver, Display Mode is the safety key behaviour in the engineering menu.

Display Mode on (the default) means the console stays powered when the safety key is removed.
