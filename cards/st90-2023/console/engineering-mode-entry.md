From the **settings page**, press ten times on the word **Settings**.

The engineering page holds six functions:

1. Engineering Mode
2. Diagnostics
3. Machine Information
4. Maintenance
5. Lube Setup
6. Key Test

**The earlier ST90 console had Belt Setup in this slot instead of Lube Setup.**
