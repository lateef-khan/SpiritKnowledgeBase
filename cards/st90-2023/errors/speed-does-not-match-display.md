| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Controller is not calibrated, or the parameters of the controller are incorrect | 1. Execute the calibration procedure. 2. Replace the controller. |

**This row appears only in the second copy of the matrix**, section 8.3. The first copy, on pages 17-18, does not carry it.

If the belt tops out around 7 mph while the display reads higher, that is a supply voltage problem instead, not a calibration one.
