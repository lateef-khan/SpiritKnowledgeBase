**This manual prints its troubleshooting matrix twice**, once on pages 17-18 and again as section 8.3 on pages 28-30, and the two copies do not always agree. Where they differ this card gives both.

| Copy | Solve |
|---|---|
| Pages 17-18 | 1. Replace with a new safety key device. |
| Section 8.3 | 1. Replace with a new safety key device. 2. Replace controller. 3. Replace console. |

Both give the same single reason: the safety key device is broken. Section 8.3 carries two further steps if a new key device does not fix it.

**Take the machine out of use until this is fixed.**
