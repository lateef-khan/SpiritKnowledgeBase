**This manual prints its troubleshooting matrix twice**, once on pages 17-18 and again as section 8.3 on pages 28-30, and the two copies do not always agree. Where they differ this card gives both.

**The screen itself dim or incomplete**

| Copy | Reasons | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| Pages 17-18 | 1 TFT light is broken. 2 Power to console too low. | Replace with a new TFT or console; **check AC power is 120V**; check power to console; replace the lower controller. |
| Section 8.3 | 1 **Connector fall off**. 2 TFT LCD light is broken. 3 Power to console too low. | Check the connector again; replace with a new LCD or console; check power to console; replace the lower controller. |

Section 8.3 adds the check worth doing first: a connector that has fallen off. Pages 17-18 add the supply figure, 120V.

**The displays not bright, incomplete or imperfect** - both copies: the TFT LCD displays are broken, replace with a new console.

Check the brightness slider before replacing anything.
