Nine causes, in the manual's order:

| Cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| 1 Power cord isn't plugged into outlet | Plug the power cord into the outlet |
| 2 Power cord isn't plugged into the unit | Plug the power cord into the unit |
| 3 The voltage of outlet is too low | Check the voltage of the outlet |
| 4 Plug or connector of power cord is open | Replace the power cord |
| 5 Connector of power cord is broken | Replace the power cord |
| 6 Connecting cables disconnected | Check if the wire is disconnected, connect it again |
| 7 Breaker tripped | Press the small red button to return it to its original state |
| 8 Breaker is broken | Replace the breaker |
| 9 ON/OFF switch is broken | Replace the AC switch |

**This row appears only in the first copy of the matrix**, on pages 17-18. The second copy, section 8.3, drops it.
