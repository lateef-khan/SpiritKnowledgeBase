The motor is getting power but not enough of it. The cause is low AC voltage at the treadmill.

- **Do not use an extension cord.** If one is unavoidable it must be as short as possible and heavy duty, **14 gauge minimum**.
- The machine needs **a minimum of 110 volt AC**.
- If the supply is low, contact an electrician or your SOLE dealer.
