| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Safety device is broken (short) | Replace the safety key device or the console |

Both copies of the matrix print this row identically.

**This is a safety fault.** The safety key is the machine's emergency stop; a shorted safety device means it cannot stop the belt. When the key is missing and the safety circuit is healthy, the display reads "Please Replace the Safety Key" and the inverter reports E-25H.
