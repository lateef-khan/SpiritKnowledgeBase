**This is the incline window message, not the inverter code E-3H and not E-52H.**

**The manual prints this row twice and the two copies do not agree on what the window shows.**

| Copy | Condition, as printed | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pages 17-18 | INCLINE ERR, INCLINE window displays "INCLINE ERR" | Position sensor value of incline motor is wrong | 1 Turn off the AC switch and turn the power on again. 2 Calibrate the monitor. |
| Section 8.3 | INCLINE ERR, INCLINE window displays "E3" | 1 connector fall off. 2 Position sensor value of incline motor is wrong | 1 Check connector of cable. 2 Calibrate the console. |

Take both: check the incline connector, power cycle at the AC switch, then calibrate. The earlier ST90 manual prints only the `INCLINE ERR` wording.
