**This manual prints its troubleshooting matrix twice**, once on pages 17-18 and again as section 8.3 on pages 28-30, and the two copies do not always agree. Where they differ this card gives both.

Causes, in the manual's order:

1. The connector of the SPEED CABLE and the CONSOLE is not connected properly. Connect the cables again.
2. The connector of the SPEED CABLE and the SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH with cable is not connected properly. Connect the cables again.
3. The connector of the SPEED CABLE or the switch cable is damaged. Replace with a new cable.
4. The button of the SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH is broken. Replace the switch.

Section 8.3 folds the incline switch row into the same block but lists causes for the **speed** cable only; the pages 17-18 copy keeps the incline switch as its own row with its own causes.

Run the console **Key Test** first: it tells you whether the console is seeing the button at all.
