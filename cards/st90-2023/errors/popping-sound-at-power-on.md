**This manual prints its troubleshooting matrix twice**, once on pages 17-18 and again as section 8.3 on pages 28-30, and the two copies do not always agree. Where they differ this card gives both.

| Copy | Reason | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| Pages 17-18 | Incorrect input power, varistor is blown broken on controller | Check the voltage of power is **120V**. Replace controller. |
| Section 8.3 | Same | Check the voltage of power is **220V**. Replace controller. (On 120Vac electronic power system need 110V) |

**Use the figure that matches the machine in front of you.** A North American machine runs on a nominal 120 V supply; a 230 Vac machine runs on 220 V. The two copies are not a measurement disagreement, they are the same row written for two different power systems.
