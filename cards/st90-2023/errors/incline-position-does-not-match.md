| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Console is not calibrated | Calibrate the console |

Both copies of the matrix print this row identically. A calibration is also needed after fitting a new console, inverter or incline motor.

If the incline window shows the words `INCLINE ERR` or `E3` rather than a wrong number, use that card instead.
