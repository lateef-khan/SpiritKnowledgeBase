**The ST90-2023 book answers an off-center or hesitating belt with a dealer defect — no tracking adjustment, unlike every other stencil book.**

> Tread-belt does not stay centered / Treadmill belt hesitates when walked/run on — Treadmill defect. Contact your SOLE dealer.

The ST90-2021 book (and all the folding-belt books) send this complaint to the General Maintenance belt-tracking adjustment instead. A slat belt has no user tracking adjustment, so the newer book calls it a defect outright. Read from the render (folio ST90 TREADMILL 24); the row is short on the page, not OCR damage.
