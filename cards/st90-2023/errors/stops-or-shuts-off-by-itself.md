**This manual prints its troubleshooting matrix twice**, once on pages 17-18 and again as section 8.3 on pages 28-30, and the two copies do not always agree. Where they differ this card gives both.

| Copy | Reasons | Solve |
|---|---|---|
| Pages 17-18 | 1 House breaker tripped. 2 Treadmill breaker tripped. 3 Treadmill controller **fuse is broken**. 4 Treadmill controller shut down and TFT would be ON. | Reset it; reset the treadmill breaker; replace with a new fuse; turn off the AC switch and turn the power on again. |
| Section 8.3 | 1 House breaker tripped. 2 Treadmill breaker tripped. 3 Treadmill controller shut down and LED would be ON. | Reset it; reset the treadmill breaker; **belt / deck lubrication**; turn off the AC switch and turn the power on again. |

Between them: reset both breakers, check the controller fuse, lubricate the belt and deck, then power cycle at the AC switch.
