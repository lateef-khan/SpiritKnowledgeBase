The pages 17-18 matrix covers three conditions with one list: the UP/DOWN switch not working at all, only UP working, and only DOWN working.

1. The connector of the INCLINE CABLE and the CONSOLE is not connected properly. Connect the wires again.
2. The connector of the INCLINE CABLE and the INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH with cable is not connected properly. Connect the wires again.
3. The connector of the INCLINE CABLE or the switch cable is damaged. Replace the cable.
4. The button of the INCLINE ADJUSTMENT SWITCH is broken. Replace the buttons.
5. and 6. Further damaged connector entries. Replace the cable.

**Section 8.3 names this condition but gives no incline causes**, only speed cable ones. Use the pages 17-18 copy.

This is a button and cable fault; if the incline motor itself is not moving, that is an incline error instead.
