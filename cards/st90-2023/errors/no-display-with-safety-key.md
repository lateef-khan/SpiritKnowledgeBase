**This manual prints its troubleshooting matrix twice**, once on pages 17-18 and again as section 8.3 on pages 28-30, and the two copies do not always agree. Where they differ this card gives both.

Eight causes, in the manual's order:

1. The ON/OFF switch has not been switched on. Switch the AC switch on.
2. The safety key is in the wrong position. Fit it correctly.
3. Connector not plugged in properly. Check the wire and connect it again.
4. Cable is broken. Replace it.
5. Fuse on controller is blown. Replace the fuse or the controller.
6. Varistor on controller is blown. Replace the varistor or the controller.
7. Safety device is broken (open). Replace the safety key device.
8. Other components are faulty. Replace the console.

**The two copies name the cable differently.** Pages 17-18 say "console connectors" and "console cable". Section 8.3 says **"12 PIN Computer connector"** and "12 PIN computer cable" - a description carried over from a DC treadmill manual. This machine's console bundle is the 9-pin control lead plus two keyboard leads, not a single 12-pin cable.
