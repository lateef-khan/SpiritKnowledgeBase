**This is a known characteristic of this model, not a failure.** The drive motor is pushing heavier aluminium pedals, and the noise resonates between the plastic parts and the iron parts.

**Fix**: paste foam **1 to 2 mm thick** in the middle, between the parts. The manual says the related noise problem can be solved this way.

This is a separate answer from the roller and motor noise rows in the troubleshooting matrix. It is not carried by the earlier ST90 manual.
