**The user's stride.** Someone who favours one foot moves the belt. If that walking pattern is natural for them, track the belt slightly **off-centre to the side opposite the belt movement**.

**Belt tension.** The matrix row reads "Running belt tension not even across tread belt" and sends you to the treadmill running belt adjustment.

**This manual does not print the belt adjustment procedure.** Section 7 and the matrix both refer to a "General Maintenance section" and a "treadmill running belt adjustment" that the manual does not contain. The earlier ST90 manual for this machine family does carry it, as notes 1 and 2 of its section 10-6: set the gap between the roller axle and the mainframe to 15 to 18 mm on both sides, and use the rear roller gear and belt rack gap to judge whether the belt is too tight.
