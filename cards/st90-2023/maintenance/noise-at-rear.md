| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1 Rear roller bearings are defective | Replace with a new rear roller |
| 2 Rear roller misaligned | Adjust the rear roller position |

Noise from **under the motor cover** at the front is a different row, and a hum or buzz that follows the running cadence is the resonant noise the manual answers with foam.
