1. Press **Stop** once, or the first page button on the screen. The belt slows down gradually until it stops, and the time, distance and calorie values stay on the screen.
2. Press **Resume** to carry on with the same workout.
3. Press **Finish** to complete the workout.

With **GR Mode** on, the incline goes to zero when you pause and again on the summary page.
