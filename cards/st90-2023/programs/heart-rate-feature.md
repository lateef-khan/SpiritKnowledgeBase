Pulse on the screen is the current heart rate in beats per minute.

- **Hand pulse**: you must hold **both** the left and the right stainless steel sensors. Holding one gives no reading.
- **Chest strap**: the console can also read the pulse from a heart rate chest strap transmitter.
- **Heart rate bar graph**: shows your heart rate as a percentage of your estimated maximum, worked out from the age you enter during programming.
- **Message window**: guides you through programming and shows workout data during a program.
