1. Plug in the power cord and switch on the main power switch, which is on the right, under the motor hood.
2. Fit the safety key. The treadmill cannot run without it.
3. The screen shows the start-up image and then goes to ready mode.
4. Press any button to wake the display if it is not already on.
5. Press **Quick Start**. The belt moves at **0.5 mph (1.0 KMPH)**.
6. Adjust with the Speed + or - keys, on the console or on the handrail.
7. To stop, press and release **Stop**.

The screen is a touch screen: every function button can be touched directly, or driven from the quick buttons along the bottom.
