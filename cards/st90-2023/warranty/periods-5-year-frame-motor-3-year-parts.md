**Treadmill warranty, effective February 1, 2023.**

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| **Frame** | **5 years** |
| **Motor** | **5 years** |
| **Deck** | **3 years** |
| **Parts** | **3 year** (sic — the book writes "year") |
| **Labor** | **1 year** |
| **Cosmetic Items** | **90 Days** |

No electronics row is printed. Do not confuse with the 2020 ST90 terms (`sole-st90-warranty-periods-5-year-frame-motor-12-month-parts-2020`), which have an electronics row and 12-month parts and labor. Duties, exclusions and service are the 2023 set — see `sole-treadmill-warranty-responsibilities-8-item-photo-duty-2023`, `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-10-item-rust-climate-2023` and `sole-treadmill-warranty-service-troubleshoot-first-2023`.
