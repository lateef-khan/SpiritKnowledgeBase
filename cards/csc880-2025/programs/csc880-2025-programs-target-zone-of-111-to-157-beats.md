**This machine's worked example uses a 35 year old, not the 40 year old every other Spirit climber
manual uses.** The percentages are the same; the answer is not.

Determine your **Maximum Heart Rate** with the formula **220 minus your age**. Your heart rate
training zone is **50% to 90%** of maximum. **60% of MHR is recommended for burning fat while 85% is
recommended for strengthening the cardio vascular system. This 60% to 85% is the zone to stay in for
maximum benefit.**

**Example**, for someone who is 35 years old:

- 220 - 35 = **185** (maximum heart rate)
- 185 x .6 = **111** beats per minute (60% of maximum)
- 185 x .85 = **157** beats per minute (85% of maximum)
- So for a 35 year old the training zone would be **111 to 157 beats per minute.**

The arithmetic on this page is correct.

The page also prints a **Target Heart Rate Training Chart** - maximum heart rate as black columns by
age from 20 to 65, with a Weight Loss line at 60% and a Cardiovascular line at 85% cutting
diagonally across it - and ends with the claim that the heart rate monitor may be used **"during
manual mode or during any of the nine different programs"**. This console has neither a manual mode
nor nine programs; see
`csc880-2025-programs-heart-rate-control-program-named-but-not-present`.

**No CSC900 printing has any version of this page** - see
`csc900-2024-programs-no-heart-rate-training-page`. The Spirit steppers print a 40-year-old example
giving 108 to 153 - see `spirit-stepper-programs-target-zone-of-108-to-153-beats`.
