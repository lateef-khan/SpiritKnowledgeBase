1. First, remove the **tensioning bolts at the bottom of the belt pressure roller**, then remove the belt from the
   magnetic control roller.
2. Remove the **four bolts** securing the magnetic control wheel at the bottom.
3. Disconnect the **motor wire, light sensor wire and power brake wire**. Remove the magnetic control unit.

No bolt sizes and no refit step are printed. The light sensor sits on this unit - when it goes back, the motor fan
blade must be centred in the sensor's U-shaped groove (the light sensor card). The CSC900 2022 book replaces its
magnetic wheel by pulling a set of pedals first; a different sequence with its own card.
