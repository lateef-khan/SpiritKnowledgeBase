1. Open the back cover of the console.
2. Remove the mounting plate.
3. Remove the circuit board connection cable.
4. Disconnect the button cable.
5. Remove the screws of the chip.
6. Replace the clip.

**The book calls the part a "clip" in its heading and step 6 and a "chip" in step 5.** It is the console's circuit
board - the same sequence the CSC900 2022 book prints as "console board replacement". No screw count or size is
printed.
