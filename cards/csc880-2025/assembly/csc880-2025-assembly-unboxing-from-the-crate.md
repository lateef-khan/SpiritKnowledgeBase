1. **Open the wooden box.**
2. **Remove the left and right handrails.** They are packed one either side and the
   pictures label them Left and Right.
3. **Remove the console and packing connector.** The console and the piece the drawing
   calls the **packing connector** come out together.
4. **Remove the column.**
5. **Lift the main frame off the pallet.**

That is the whole page: five steps, five drawings and no text beyond the captions.

## No other Spirit climber or stepper manual has this page

The other twelve manuals of this range open with a PRE-ASSEMBLY list that begins "cut
the straps, then lift the box over the unit and unpack" - a cardboard carton, not a
crate. **The CSC880 is the only one that ships in a wooden box**, and the only one whose
book shows how to empty it.

Its own PRE-ASSEMBLY page follows on the next spread and is the six-item stair climber
list the CSC900 prints, which repeats the two lifting warnings: **do not attempt to move
the unit by yourself**, and **do not grasp any plastic parts while lifting and moving**
because the covers are non-structural. See
[that card](../../csc900-2024/assembly/csc900-2024-assembly-pre-assembly-six-items.md).

## The page gives no weight and no crate dimensions

It never says how heavy the frame is or how many people the lift in step 5 takes. The
pre-assembly page's "have at least one other person help" is the only figure the book
offers.

## The packing connector is not a machine part

It appears in step 3 of this page and nowhere else in the manual - not in PARTS
INCLUDED, not in the hardware pack, and in no assembly step. Nothing tells you to keep
it.
