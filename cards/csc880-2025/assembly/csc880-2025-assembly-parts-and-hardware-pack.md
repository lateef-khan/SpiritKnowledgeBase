**One panel does the work of three.** Where every other Spirit climber prints PARTS
INCLUDED and TOOLS INCLUDED as separate lists, the CSC880 runs parts, loose fasteners and
the tools together in a single seventeen-line column:

| Qty | Line |
|---|---|
| 1 | Frame Assembly |
| 2 | Hexagon socket countersunk head screws |
| 1 | Front Column Assembly |
| 1 | Front Column Assembly Welding |
| 1 | Column Decorative Rubber |
| 1 | Left Handrail Assembly |
| 1 | Right Handrail Assembly |
| 1 | B Console Assembly |
| 1 | Console Support Decorative Rubber |
| 2 | Flat Washer |
| 4 | Curved Washer |
| 4 | Hexagon Socket Head Cap Screws |
| 4 | Hexagon Socket Countersunk Head Screws |
| 2 | Hexagon Socket Cylindrical Head Bolts |
| 1 | 5mm Allen Wrench |
| 1 | 6mm Allen Wrench |
| 1 | 8mm Allen Wrench |

**Three tools, all Allen wrenches**: 5mm, 6mm and 8mm.

## The hardware pack panel, as printed on the page

| Marking | What it is |
|---|---|
| M10 x 20 - 2 | the front column bolts, step one |
| Φ10 - 2 | their flat washers, step one |
| M8 X 16 - 4 | the console bolts, step four |
| M8 X 25 - 4 | the handrail bolts, step three |
| Arc Washers Φ8 - 4 | their curved washers, step three |

Five lines. Every one of them matches a line of the parts panel and a call-out in a
step.

## Two fasteners the build needs and neither panel holds

- **The M10 x 100 countersunk bolts.** Step two takes two of them out of the frame and
  step three puts them back through the handrails. The parts panel counts them - *2
  Hexagon socket countersunk head screws* - and **the hardware pack panel does not show
  them**, because they arrive in the machine rather than in the bag.
- **The ST4.8 x 10 cross recessed pan head tapping screws.** Step four fixes the console
  back cover with them. They are **in no panel, in no list and given no quantity
  anywhere in the book.**

## There is no screwdriver in the carton

The three Allen wrenches drive every bolt in the build. The **ST4.8 x 10 tapping screws
of step four are cross recessed** and there is nothing to turn them with. The manual
never says to supply your own.

**This is the second Spirit stair climber carton with that defect.** The CSC900 packs a
5mm and a 6mm Allen wrench and no screwdriver for the six driven screws its step four
calls for. See
[that card](../../csc900-2024/assembly/csc900-2024-assembly-tools-included.md).

## No levellers on the panel, and no wrench for one

The machine diagram labels **J. Leveler** and this panel lists none. See
[the diagram card](csc880-2025-assembly-parts-diagram-and-oem-label.md).

## What the panel does not say

No item numbers, no torque figures, no carton count, no shipping weight and no crate
dimensions.
