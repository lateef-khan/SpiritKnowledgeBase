**Take a photo of the wiring before you unplug anything**, and **red to +, black to -** on the power cable. Both
are the book's own instructions.

1. Before replacing internal parts, remove the left and right handrails first.
2. Remove the protective cover from one side.
3. The controller has **two fixed screws**. Remove the screws and disconnect the wiring harness one by one. *Before
   disconnecting the harness, take a photo to record the wiring position.*

The seven cables, numbered on the photograph:

| # | Cable |
|---|---|
| 1 | DC 24 V power cable |
| 2 | Brake cable |
| 3 | Proximity switch cable |
| 4 | Communication cable (communication and upper control power supply) |
| 5 | Infrared emergency stop cable |
| 6 | Light sensor cable |
| 7 | Motor cable |

*When plugging or unplugging the harness, check whether there is a latch at the connector; if there is, press it
while pulling the wire.* *The power cord has positive and negative poles: connect the red wire to + and the black
wire to -.*

No refit step is printed. This is the magnetic CSC880; the CSC900 2022 magnetic book's control board and the
CSC900 V1.0 alternator book's controller are different boards with their own cards.
