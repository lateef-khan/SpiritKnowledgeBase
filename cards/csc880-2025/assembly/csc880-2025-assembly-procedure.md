**This build names no item numbers and prints no hardware table.** Fasteners are labelled
on the drawings beside each step, by thread and length, and the whole machine is metric.

## Step one - the front column

1. Put the **Column Decorative Rubber** over the front column assembly.
2. Connect the connecting wires of the front column assembly to the frame assembly **in
   pairs**.
3. Insert the front column assembly onto the frame assembly and bolt them in place.

Fasteners on the drawing: **hexagon socket cylindrical head bolts M10 x 20 - 2 pcs** and
**Φ10 flat washers - 2 pcs**.

## Step two - the front handrails, and two bolts out

1. Insert the **left and right front handrail assemblies** into the positions shown.
2. **Remove the two bolts from the position shown.**

The drawing labels each of those as a **hexagon socket countersunk head bolt M10 x 100,
1 PC**, one a side, and marks the holes: *the hole on the left side is for the left front
handrail assembly, the hole on the right side is for the right front handrail assembly.*

**Do not throw these two away.** Unlike the CS800 and CVC800 step zero, which discards
its shipping bolts, step three puts these back through the handrails. Nothing in the
carton replaces them.

## Step three - the side handrails

Install the left and right handrail into the position shown and bolt it in place.

Fasteners on the drawing: **hexagon socket flat round head bolts M8 x 25 - 2 pcs** and
**Φ8 arc washers - 2 pcs** *on each side*, and the **M10 x 100 countersunk bolt - 1 pc**
a side, going back into the holes step two emptied.

## Step four - the console

1. Connect the connecting wire of the console to the connecting wire of the main body.
2. Install the console to the position shown and fix it with the bolts.
3. **Fix the back cover of the console.**

Fasteners on the drawing: **hexagon socket countersunk head screws M8 x 16 - 4 PCS** for
the console, and **cross recessed pan head tapping screws ST4.8 x 10** for the back
cover. The ST4.8 screws are given **no quantity** and appear in no parts list, and the
carton holds no screwdriver to drive them. See
[the carton card](csc880-2025-assembly-parts-and-hardware-pack.md).

## An untranslated second version of every step is buried in the file

The PDF's text layer carries a second wording of each step that is **not printed on the
page**. Step two's hidden copy reads *"Insert the left and right front handrail
assemblies into the positions as shown. **Eliminate the two bolts** in the position
shown."* A 300 dpi render of the page shows only "Remove the two bolts".

"Eliminate" would tell a reader to throw away the two bolts step three needs. It is not
on the page, and anyone answering from the converted text will find it. This is the same
book whose parts page still carries its untranslated OEM model label - see
[that card](csc880-2025-assembly-parts-diagram-and-oem-label.md).

## What the build never does

No step fits a leveller, though the machine diagram labels one. No step fits a transport
wheel, and the diagram labels none. There is no first-power-on step: step four is
followed by QUICK START, which shows only the power input location and the safety-stop
sensors.
