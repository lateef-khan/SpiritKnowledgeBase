1. Remove the screws securing the light sensor bracket.
2. Unplug the light sensor cable from the controller (**the red plug**).
3. When reinstalling, make sure the **motor fan blade is positioned exactly in the middle of the light sensor's
   U-shaped groove and does not come into contact with the light sensor**. This prevents misdetection or
   operational friction noise.

No screw size is printed. The troubleshooting table in the same book names this sensor - its wire loose, its
distance to the light grid, or a bad sensor - as the cause when the speed window shows no data after START and the
machine stops after 3 seconds; those diagnoses are errors cards. On the controller it is cable 6 of seven.
