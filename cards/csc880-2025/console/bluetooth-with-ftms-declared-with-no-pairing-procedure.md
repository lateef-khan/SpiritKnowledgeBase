**The certification is stated. The procedure is not.**

The compliance page lists the standards this product is certified to:

- **FCC part 15**
- **UL**
- **Bluetooth (with FTMS)**

**FTMS is the Fitness Machine Service profile**, the Bluetooth protocol third-party apps use to read
and control a fitness machine. **This is the only Spirit stair climber manual that names it**; the
CSC900 books of 2019, 2021 and 2024 declare no Bluetooth standard at all.

**No page in this book tells you how to use it.** There is no pairing procedure, no device list, no
app name, no settings menu and no engineering mode to hide one in
(`spirit-stairclimber-console-has-no-engineering-or-maintenance-mode-in-any-printing`). The word
**app** appears nowhere in the manual.

**The only Bluetooth behaviour the book describes is heart rate**, and it is automatic: the console
**detects the closest Bluetooth heart rate chest strap by itself** and shows the reading in the upper
left window (`csc900-2024-console-heart-rate-bluetooth-and-contact`). Nothing says the same radio
serves an app, and nothing says it does not.

**So the honest answer to an owner is: the radio is certified and the manual documents no way to
reach it.** Do not promise a phone connection from this line, and do not name an app the book never
names. The Spirit CR800 and CU800 bikes and the CS800 stepper are in the same position from the other
direction - a Bluetooth part in the parts list and no procedure:
`spirit-cr800-cu800-console-bluetooth-module-with-no-procedure`.
