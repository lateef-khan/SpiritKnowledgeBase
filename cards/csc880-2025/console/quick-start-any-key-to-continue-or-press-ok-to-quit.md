**The whole procedure is two bullets, and it is not written like any other Spirit quick
start.**

- **Press the Start/Stop button. All data starts counting.** "Display intensity can be adjusted by
  pressing the **+/-** buttons. The data can be switched."
- **Press the Start/Stop button** to clear all the data. The display then reads
  **`Any Key To Continue or Press OK to Quit`**.

**"Display intensity" means the resistance level.** The +/- buttons are the LEVEL controls, and
nothing in the book adjusts screen brightness. It is a translation artefact in a manual that also
prints an untranslated Chinese OEM label on its cover, and it should not be repeated to a caller as
written. **"The data can be switched"** means the button under each window changes that window's
reading (`csc900-2024-console-four-display-windows`).

**This page prints no time count-up, no dot matrix description and no level count**, all of which
every CSC900 quick start has. **Do not fill the gaps from a CSC900 manual.**

**The thirty-second pause is described elsewhere in this same book**, on printed p. 19, in the same
words as the CSC900-2021 and CSC900-2024 (`csc900-2024-console-start-stop-pause-thirty-seconds`).
**That paragraph and this one describe the same button and do not agree about what a second press
does** - p. 19 says a second press within 30 seconds unlocks the stairs at resistance level one,
p. 16 says a second press clears the data. The manual never reconciles them.
