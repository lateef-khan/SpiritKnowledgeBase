| Warranty | Frame\* | Brake | Parts | Labor | Wear Items\*\* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial (Dues Paying Facilities) | 10 Years | 5 Years | 3 Years | 3 Years | 6 Months |
| Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities) | 10 Years | 5 Years | 5 Years | 2 Years | 6 Months |
| Residential | **Lifetime** | 5 Years | 10 Years | 2 Years | 6 Months |

\*Frame is defined as the welded metal base of the unit and does not include removable parts, wear items, or coatings.

\*\*Wear items include: plastic covers, overlay, grips, hardware, pull pins/knobs, decals/stickers, headphone jack, USB port, paint/coatings/finish, straps, and pedals.

> Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage.

**A dues-paying gym has its own row.** This is the only Spirit climber or stepper in this knowledge base that gives one. The CSC900, the CRS800S, the CS800 and the CVC800 print a *Commercial (Non-Dues Paying Facilities)* row and leave a health club with no periods at all (`spirit-climber-warranty-use-environment`).

**The dues-paying row is not simply the worst one.** It gives **3 Years** of labor where the other two give 2 - more labor and fewer parts. Read the row, not a rule of thumb.

**Lifetime appears once, in the Residential frame cell.** Every commercial frame is ten years.

**The brake is 5 Years in all three rows**, the only column that does not move.

**Wear items are 6 months in every row, and the footnote names eleven of them** - including the headphone jack, the USB port and the paint. A part on that list is covered for six months whichever row the machine is bought under.

## The opening paragraph does not match the table

The page is headed **WARRANTY**, and the sentence above the table reads:

> Spirit Fitness Inc. warrants all its **home use** Stair Climber parts for a period of time listed below...

administered "directly to a **consumer**", above a duty list addressed to a **facility** and a table whose first two rows are commercial. **The manual never reconciles it.** The phrase is a leftover; the table is what states the terms, and it covers three environments, not one. The same slip appears on the 2024 CSC900, whose table has no home row at all (`spirit-2024-stair-climber-warranty-coverage-periods`).

## The manual carries an untranslated OEM label, and it is not a model you can be sold

`CLE450S-3A1楼梯机` is printed on the cover and three times more on the parts pages, there prefixed 机种 - Chinese for *model type* - with 楼梯机 meaning *stair machine*. It is the factory's internal designation for the unit, left untranslated in an English book.

**It names no product a customer owns and no warranty terms.** A CSC880 whose paperwork or serial plate shows that string is still a CSC880 and still warranted by the table above. Do not treat it as a second model, and do not go looking for warranty terms under it.
