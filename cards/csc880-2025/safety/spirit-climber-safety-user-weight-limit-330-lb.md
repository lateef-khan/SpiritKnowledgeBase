**330 lb.** The manual prints it in two places, and only one of them gives a metric figure.

| Where | Printed as |
|---|---|
| Safety instructions, item 8 | `User Weight Limit: 330 lb.` |
| Electrical safety, club rules | `Do not exceed the maximum allowable weight limit of 330 lbs. / 150 kg.` |

150 kg is 331 lb, so the two statements are the same allowance. The manual does not say what happens
above it.

**This is the lowest user weight limit on any Spirit climber, and 70 lb below the CSC900 it
replaces.** The CSC900 stairclimber is 400 lbs / 180 kg in every printing from 2019 to 2024
(`csc900-2024-safety-user-weight-limit-400-lb`). Never quote a CSC900 figure for a CSC880.

The boxed note beside the safety list asks the reader to review and adhere to the user weight
restrictions **and power requirements** of the machine, and says failure to do so may result in
serious injury or damage to the machine (`spirit-climber-safety-instructions-list-csc880`).
