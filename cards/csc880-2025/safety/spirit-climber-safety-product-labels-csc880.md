**Three stickers, and two of the three are on the main frame.**

| Sticker | Where the manual says it is |
|---|---|
| Serial number | the **main frame** |
| Proposition 65 | the **main frame** |
| Safety warning | the **Stair Climber's upright** |

> **SERIAL NUMBER STICKER** - This sticker will be found on the main frame. Please record the number
> below the barcode for the purpose of registering your Stair Climber's warranty.

The sticker also carries the Spirit customer-care number, `(870) 935-1107`.

> **PROPOSITION 65 WARNING** - This sticker will be found on the main frame The State of California
> requires us to inform you that this unit was manufactured using chemicals that could cause harm
> with improper use.

The sticker reads `WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm - www.P65Warnings.ca.gov` and names no
chemical. The CSC900 manuals print a longer form that names `Toluene and Acrylamide`.

**The third sticker carries six numbered precautions of its own**, and the manual reproduces it on
this page as an image with no text behind it
(`spirit-climber-safety-warning-sticker-csc880`).

## The cover is not finished

The front cover prints the OEM's own model designation above the Spirit branding:

```
CLE450S-3A1楼梯机
```

`楼梯机` is Chinese for *stair machine*. The same string is printed a second time above the PARTS OF
YOUR STAIR CLIMBER page, and three more times in the title blocks of the exploded-view drawings on
printed pages 35 to 37, beside untranslated CAD fields - `机种` (model), `设计` (designed by, with a
name), `校对` (checked), `审核` (reviewed), `工艺` (process) and `批准` (approved).

- **`CLE450S-3A1` is the factory's part designation, not a Spirit model number.** A customer reading
  it off the cover will not find it in any Spirit list. The Spirit model is **CSC880**, printed
  beside it as `CSC880 STAIR CLIMBER`, and the manual's own file stamp is `CSC880_880665_OM_20251030`.
- **It is a proofing failure, not a different machine.** Everything else in the book is a CSC880.
- **Do not repeat `CLE450S-3A1` to a customer as a model, a part number or a serial number.** If one
  quotes it, tell them what it is.
