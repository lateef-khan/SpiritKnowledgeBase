**120 VAC nominal, and nothing else. Match the outlet to the plug already on the cord.**

> This product will be wired for either 120 VAC nominal power input. It is factory-equipped with a
> specific electric cord and plug to permit connection to the proper electric circuit. Make sure that
> the product is connected to a dedicated power line having an outlet with the same configuration as
> that of the plug.

- **120 VAC nominal.**
- **A dedicated power line.** Nothing else on that circuit.
- **The manual prints no plug type, no NEMA number and no amperage or breaker rating.** There is no
  figure to give for the circuit size; the plug on the machine is the only test the manual offers.

**The sentence is damaged.** `wired for either 120 VAC nominal power input` offers a choice with one
option in it. The CSC900 manuals of 2019, 2021 and 2024 print the complete sentence -
`either 120 VAC nominal power input **OR 230 VAC nominal input**`
(`csc900-2024-safety-outlet-120-or-230-volt`). The CSC880 book kept the word *either* and deleted the
alternative. **Do not read the missing half back in.** As printed, this manual offers a CSC880 owner
no 230 volt option, and nothing else in the book mentions one.

**No adapter may be used.**

> Improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric shock.
> Check with a qualified electrician or serviceman if you are in doubt as to whether the product is
> properly grounded. Do not modify the plug provided with the product. **NO adapter should be used
> with this product.**

Never offer a CSC880 owner the temporary two-pole adapter procedure written for the Spirit treadmills
and steppers (`spirit-temporary-adapter-for-a-two-pole-receptacle`).

**Changing the circuit is a service job.**

> If the product must be reconnected for use on a different type of electric circuit, the
> reconnection should be made by qualified service personnel in order to avoid a hazard.

**This manual has no GROUNDING INSTRUCTIONS page.** Items 3, 4 and 5 of the safety list are the whole
of what it says about supply and earthing - the same as the CSC900 books, and unlike the CS800 and
XS895 steppers, which print a full grounding page.
