The 8.0T manual gives this its own heading, PROPER USAGE, beside an illustration:

> Position user's feet on the belt, bend user's arms slightly and grab the parallel handlebars (as
> shown). While exercising user's feet should be located in the center of the belt.

- **Feet in the centre of the belt** while exercising, not at the front, the back or over a
  siderail.
- **Arms bent slightly**, holding the parallel handlebars.
- The handlebars are the adjustable parallel bars of this machine, not a treadmill's front handrail.

**Only the 8.0T manual prints this.** The 7.0T manual has an Application Specification page but no
PROPER USAGE section, and the 4.0T manual has neither. The 4.0T's nearest equivalent is a different
rule with a different purpose - its handrails are `used for initial stability and not for continuous
use` (`40t-2026-safety-getting-on-and-off-the-treadmill`), while these parallel handlebars are held
throughout.

Safety instruction 23 of the same manual asks that the handlebars be set before use: `Ensure that
adjustment levers and or knobs (parallel handlebars width and height position levers) are properly
secured and do not interfere with range of motion during exercise.`

How to get on and off is `80t-2026-safety-getting-on-and-off-the-treadmill`.
