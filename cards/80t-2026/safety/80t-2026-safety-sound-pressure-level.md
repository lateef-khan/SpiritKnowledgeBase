> 24. Measured at typical head height, the A-weighted sound pressure level when the treadmill is
>     running at 12 km/h (without load) is no greater than 70dB.

> 25. Noise emission under load is higher than without load.

| What | Figure |
|---|---|
| A-weighted sound pressure level | no greater than 70 dB |
| Where measured | typical head height |
| Belt speed at measurement | 12 km/h |
| Load | none |

- **The figure is a no-load one**, and item 25 says noise under load is higher without giving a
  figure for it.
- **A-weighted, at typical head height** - this manual states both, where the 4.0T states neither.

**The 4.0T's 70 dB is a different measurement.** It is taken at **8 km/hr**, with no A-weighting
and no measurement height stated (`40t-2026-safety-sound-pressure-level`). The two numbers happen
to match; the conditions do not, so do not present them as the same test.

**The 7.0T manual prints no sound pressure figure at all.** Do not carry either machine's number
onto it.
