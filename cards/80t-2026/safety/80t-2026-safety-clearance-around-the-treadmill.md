> 14. Ensure there is a minimum space on the sides of the 8.0T of two feet for proper operation,
>     easy access and to prevent possible injuries to others standing or walking nearby. There
>     should be a minimum of at least one foot of free space at the front and six feet, six inches
>     at the rear.

| Side | Minimum free space |
|---|---|
| Each side | two feet |
| Front | one foot |
| Rear | six feet, six inches |

The reason the manual gives for the side figure is other people: proper operation, easy access,
and preventing injuries to anyone standing or walking nearby.

**The rear figure is the one that differs from the 7.0T**, whose otherwise identical instruction
asks for **three and a half feet** (`70t-2026-safety-clearance-around-the-treadmill`). Nearly
twice the run-off; the manuals do not explain the difference, and the 8.0T is the longer machine -
its specification page gives 249.7 cm (98.3 in.) in length with the rear step fitted, against
213.3 cm (84 in.) without it.

**Do not carry this figure to the other machines.** The 4.0T manual prints no clearance distances
at all (`40t-2026-safety-placement-and-floor`), and the XT and CT900 families print different
figures again (`xt-2023-safety-placement-and-clearance`, `ct900ent-clearance-requirements`).
