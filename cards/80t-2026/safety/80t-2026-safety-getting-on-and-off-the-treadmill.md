The Operation Instructions page prints these as WARNING bullets:

> - To avoid injury, use extreme caution when stepping onto or off of a moving belt.
> - While you are preparing to use the treadmill, do not stand on the belt. Place your feet on the
>   siderails before starting the treadmill.
> - Never start the treadmill at a fast running speed and attempt to jump on. In case of an
>   emergency, place both hands on the handlebars to hold yourself up and place your feet onto the
>   siderails.
> - Face toward the treadmill controls (toward the front of the treadmill) when the treadmill is in
>   operation. Keep your body and head facing forward. Do not attempt to turn around or look
>   backwards whole the treadmill is running.
> - Waiting for moving surface of treadmill to come to a complete stop before dismounting from the
>   treadmill.

Safety instruction 18 adds: `Hold the handlebar for support when getting on or off the 8.0T.`

1. **Feet on the siderails, not the belt**, before you start it.
2. **Never start at a fast speed and jump on.**
3. **Face forward the whole time.** Do not turn round or look backwards while the belt runs.
4. **In an emergency**, both hands on the handlebars to hold yourself up, feet onto the siderails.
5. **Wait for a complete stop before getting off.**
6. **Hold the handlebar getting on or off.**

**This manual is not consistent with itself about a moving belt.** The first bullet asks for
extreme caution when stepping onto or off of a moving belt, which permits it; the last says to wait
for a complete stop before dismounting, which does not. Both are printed on the same page and the
manual never reconciles them. **The safe reading is the last bullet** - wait for the stop - and the
emergency method above is what to do when you cannot.

The tether cord is the way to stop the belt when you cannot reach the console
(`spirit-2026t-safety-tether-cord-stops-the-belt`).

**The 4.0T manual forbids mounting or dismounting a moving belt outright** and starts the user on
the step rails instead (`40t-2026-safety-getting-on-and-off-the-treadmill`); the 7.0T manual prints
only the handlebar sentence and none of these bullets.
