The treadmill offers **10 different tests**: **Gerkin, WFI, Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Physical
Efficiency Battery, Coast Guard, CTT Performance, CTT Prediction.**

**Personal data - age, weight, height and gender - must be entered for each Fitness Test.** After all the
required data is selected, press **Start** to begin the test.

**During the Gerkin protocol, the WFI test and CTT Prediction the console must be receiving a steady
heart rate for the test to begin.** You may wear a heart rate sensor (heart rate accessories are not
included). The target heart rate and actual heart rate are shown on the screen.

The 4.0T lists nine tests on its VO2 key, counting the two CTT modes as one entry; the 7.0T offers the
Gerkin protocol only.
