The HIIT (High, Intensity Interval Training) program takes advantage of the latest trend in wellness.
During the program you perform **short bursts of high intensity sprinting followed by short rest
periods**. It is a highly customizable interval training program: you select the number of intervals,
the time of each Sprint and Rest interval, and the work intensity of the speeds.

1. Set **how many intervals** you want and the **intensity of the speeds** for sprint and rest.
2. Click **Next**.
3. Set the **sprint time** and the **rest time**.
4. **Program time is calculated automatically.** Press **Start**.

**The 8.0T manual prints no default values and no permitted ranges** for the interval count, the sprint
and rest times or the speeds. The CT900 manual does print them for its own HIIT program (default 10
intervals, range 3 to 15, 30 to 60 seconds, sprint 6.0 mph, rest 3.0 mph, 3 minute warm-up at 50% of
sprint speed); those figures are for that machine and are not repeated here.
