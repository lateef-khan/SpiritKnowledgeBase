The WFI test ends when one of the following occurs:

- **The user presses the Stop key.** The test ends with no score. The screen shows `**TEST STOPPED**` for
  5 seconds, then exits to idle mode.
- **The heart rate exceeds 85% of max for 15 seconds.**

**If there is no heart rate detected for 30 seconds**, the screen shows `TEST STOPPED` then
`NO HR RECEIVED` for 5 seconds, then exits to idle mode.

These conditions are printed under the WFI heading; the manual does not say whether they also apply to
the Gerkin protocol, which shares the same chart.
