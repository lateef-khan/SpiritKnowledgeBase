A quick set-up, **large font, egg timer type display for patient warm-ups**.

1. Press **Timer**.
2. Set your **target exercise time**.
3. Press **Start** to begin the exercise.

**Workout adjustment:** press **+** or **-** to adjust the session time while it runs.
