**This program controls the patient workload to maintain a steady MET level throughout the exercise
session: perfect for heart patients who are prescribed exercise in METs by their cardiologist.**

METs Target uses the treadmill's **speed and grade (incline & decline)** system to help you achieve your
METs target. The program automatically changes grade gradually to maintain the METs at target.

1. Set your **target METS** and **exercise time**.
2. Press **Start** to begin the exercise.

For what a MET is and the MET value of everyday activities, see the MET table card. The 4.0T has a
comparable METs program driven from a METs key.
