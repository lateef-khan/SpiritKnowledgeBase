Heart Rate Target uses the treadmill's **grade (incline & decline) system** to control your heart rate
from the information a heart rate sensor sends. Increases and decreases in grade affect heart rate more
efficiently than changes in speed alone. **The program automatically changes grade gradually to achieve
the programmed target heart rate.**

Your maximum heart rate is based on a formula that subtracts your age from a constant of 220. Your target
heart rate setting is calculated automatically during setup once you enter your age, and you can adjust
it from there:

**MHR = (220 - age) x 65%**

On the setup screens you can:
- **Switch the target heart rate setting between BPM and % of the user's maximum heart rate.**
- Set the **target heart rate** and the **program time**.
- Click **Start** to begin the program.

This program steers **grade**, not speed. The 4.0T's heart rate programs offer fixed 65% and 80% targets
plus an HR interval program instead.
