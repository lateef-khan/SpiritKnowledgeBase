Distance Target uses the treadmill's **speed system** to help you achieve your distance target. Increases
and decreases in tread belt speed are used to calculate the actual moving distance.

**The program manually changes speed to achieve the programmed target distance.**

The manual prints no numeric range for the distance you may set.
