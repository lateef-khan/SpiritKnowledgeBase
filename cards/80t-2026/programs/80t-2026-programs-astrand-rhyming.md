The Astrand test is used to measure a client's aerobic fitness; specifically it is a **predictive test of
the client's VO2max**.

**The Astrand protocol maintains a constant running speed (5 mph / 8 km/h) with a 2.5% increase in
gradient every two minutes, until exhaustion.**

The manual prints no stage table and no scoring chart for this test. The name is printed as "Astrand
Rhyming".
