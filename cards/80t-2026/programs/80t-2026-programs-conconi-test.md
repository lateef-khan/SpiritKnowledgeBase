The Conconi Test is a relatively simple field test which measures the heart rate at different predefined
intensity levels. It is used to estimate the **Anaerobic Threshold**, the point at which the body
predominantly uses the anaerobic energy system. **It is also known as the ramp test.** A heart rate monitor
(not included) is required.

1. Set up the **speed increment** and the **stage period**.
2. Start the program. **Warm up with self-adjusted speed for 10 minutes** - the warm up may be skipped.
3. The test begins when the warm up is over, **at the final warm up speed**.
4. The speed increases by the speed increment **every stage period**.
5. **The test ends when the user presses STOP.**

**The Conconi Test only starts after the console connects to the user's heart rate monitor (not included).
It cannot proceed without the user's heart rate.**
