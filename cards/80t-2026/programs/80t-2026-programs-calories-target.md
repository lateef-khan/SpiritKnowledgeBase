Calories Target uses the treadmill's **grade (incline & decline) system** to help you achieve your
calories target. Increases and decreases in speed and grade change the efficiency of burning calories.

**You change the speed and grade manually, and the program ends when calorie consumption reaches the
target.**

Setup asks you to **set the target calories**.

Unlike Heart Rate Target and METs Target, this program does not steer the machine for you.
