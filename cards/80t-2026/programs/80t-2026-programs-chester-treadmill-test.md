**These are the 8.0T's figures, which add a cool down phase the 4.0T and CT900 manuals do not print - see
`ct900-chester-treadmill-test`, which the 4.0T shares.**

The CTT (Chester Treadmill Test) has two modes: **CTT Performance** and **CTT Prediction** (of aerobic
capacity). On this console they are two of the ten fitness tests.

## CTT Performance
A **12 minute graded treadmill walk test with a fixed speed of 6.2 km/h**, designed to assess whether the
subject can achieve the minimum recommended standard for aerobic capacity, namely **42 mlsO2/kg/min**. The
test is completed when the 12 minutes counts down to zero.

If the user presses **Stop** once the test ends: the screen shows `TEST ENDED EARLY` once, then
`COOL DOWN 3:00`. **Cool down speed is 4.0 km/h.** The time counts down to zero and the console shows the
**VO2 SCORE**.

**Procedure**
1. Check there are no medical contraindications to performing exhaustive exercise.
2. Subject walks at 6.2 km/h at 0% for 2 minutes.
3. Every 2 minutes increase gradient by 3%.
4. Test is completed after 12 minutes.
5. Test should be stopped if the subject is showing overt signs of distress and exhaustion, or RPE=18+.

## CTT Prediction
A submaximal test designed to predict aerobic capacity. Same walk protocol as CTT Performance but **wearing
a heart rate sensor**. **The test is stopped when the subject reaches 80% heart rate max or RPE=14**, and a
VO2 score is given at the end.

If any of those conditions occur the test ends and shows `TEST INVALID`, then `COOL DOWN 3:00` counting
down to zero at a cool down speed of **4.0 km/h**. When the time ends, or the user presses Stop again, it
exits to idle mode.

**CTT Prediction can only start after the console connects to the user's heart rate monitor. It cannot
proceed without the user's heart rate.**

**Procedure**
1. Check there are no medical contraindications to performing exhaustive exercise.
2. Subject walks at 6.2 km/h at 0% for 2 minutes.
3. Every 2 minutes increase gradient by 3%.
4. Allow the user to change speed manually during cool down.
5. Test is completed after 12 minutes.
6. Test should be stopped if the subject is showing overt signs of distress and exhaustion, or RPE=18+.
