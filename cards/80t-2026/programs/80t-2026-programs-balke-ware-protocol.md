The Balke-Ware protocol is a standardized exercise protocol designed for a patient to perform a stress
test under known speed and grade conditions on a treadmill. It can be used to estimate cardiovascular
condition and endurance by measuring maximum oxygen uptake, known as **VO2 max**. It can be undertaken at
a slow pace, making it suitable for people with heart problems as well as healthy people.

**The Balke-Ware protocol can only start after the console connects to the user's heart rate monitor. It
cannot proceed without the user's heart rate.**

**The test ends in two ways:**
- **The user reaches 85% of maximum heart rate**, and the screen shows the VO2 max.
  **[ Reaching 85% of maximum heart rate = 208-(0.7*Age)*0.85 ]**
- **The user goes through all stages without reaching 85% of maximum heart rate**, and the screen shows
  "Test invalid".

*The manual prints those two end conditions in the column beside the Bruce protocol table, so which of the
two protocols they belong to is ambiguous; they are recorded here because the sentence that follows them
names Balke-Ware. Note also that the formula as printed subtracts 0.7 x Age from 208 x 0.85 unless the
multiplication is bracketed differently; it is reproduced exactly as printed.*
