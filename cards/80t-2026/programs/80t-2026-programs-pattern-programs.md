**Pattern programs** are preset exercise programs designed for a variety of goals. On the 8.0T there are
two of them, **Hill** and **Plateau**. The initial built-in level of difficulty is set to a relatively
easy level; you may select the level before beginning.

Setup asks for three values, in this order:
1. Select the **max grade**.
2. Select the **max speed**.
3. Select the **workout time**.

Then press **Start** to begin the program.

**The 8.0T manual prints no description of what Hill or Plateau does individually and no numeric segment
values** - the profiles are pictures only. The 4.0T manual describes its own Hill and Plateau in words,
and the 7.0T manual prints a numeric segment chart for its Plateau.
