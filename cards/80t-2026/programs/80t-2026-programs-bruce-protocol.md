The Bruce protocol is the most common one used during treadmill exercise stress testing. It is divided
into successive **3-minute stages**, each requiring the patient to walk faster and at a steeper grade. It
is a standardized exercise protocol designed for a patient to perform a stress test under known speed and
grade conditions.

**The Bruce protocol can only start after the console connects to the user's heart rate monitor. It cannot
proceed without the user's heart rate.**

| Stage No. | Duration | Speed (km/h) | Speed (mph) | Grade (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | 3:00 | 2.7 | 1.7 | 0 |
| 1 | 3:00 | 2.7 | 1.7 | 10 |
| 2 | 3:00 | 4.0 | 2.5 | 12 |
| 3 | 3:00 | 5.7 | 3.4 | 14 |
| 4 | 3:00 | 6.8 | 4.2 | 16 |
| 5 | 3:00 | 8.1 | 5.0 | 18 |
| 6 | 3:00 | 8.9 | 5.5 | 20 |
| 7 | 3:00 | 8.9 | 6.0 | 22 |
| Cool-down | 3:00 | 2.7 | 1.7 | 0 |

*Stages 6 and 7 print the same km/h speed (8.9) but different mph speeds (5.5 and 6.0). Preserved as
printed.*

*The page prints a "The test ends in two ways" paragraph in the column beside this table, but that
paragraph names the Balke-Ware protocol in its last sentence, so it is recorded on the

which of the two protocols those end conditions belong to.*
