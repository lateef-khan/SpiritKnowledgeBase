A quick set-up that **elevates the entire deck from the floor** for patient step-up, step-down,
stretching and PNF/PTA exercises.

1. Select the required **deck height** and the **exercise time period**.
2. Press **Start** to begin.
3. After starting the exercise you can **control the speed manually**.

**The program page prints no height figures.** The machine description elsewhere in the manual states 30
levels in 6.35 mm (0.25 inch) increments. The 7.0T manual prints the full height table for its
equivalent deck-lift program.
