A timed **1.5 mile run**. You control the speed manually. There is a maximum time allowed to pass the test.

**The rating chart is printed as a picture in this manual and no times survive in the extracted text**, so
no Coast Guard times are recorded for this machine. For more information the manual gives:
http://bit.ly/SF-CoastGuard

The 4.0T manual and the CT900 manual each print a readable Superior-to-Failure rating chart, and those two
charts differ from each other by a few seconds. Neither can be assumed to be this machine's chart.
