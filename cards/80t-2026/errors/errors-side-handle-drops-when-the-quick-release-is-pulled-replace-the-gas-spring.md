Section 7-3, *Eliminating Side Handle Wobble*, second item:

> If you find that the **side handle drops vertically when pulling the quick-release latch**, please refer to 6-9-1 to 6-9-7 to **replace the gas spring**.

**The handle is held up by a gas spring, and a handle that falls instead of floating has lost it.** The book names no adjustment for this - the remedy is the part, reached through the side-handle disassembly (6-9: the armrest screws and two quick-release handles off, the round iron block and thrust bearing, the small quick-release, the eight armrest-assembly screws, the telescopic oval tube, the thrust weld assembly, then the gas spring behind one screw). That procedure is carded under `section: assembly`.

**A handle that wobbles rather than drops is the first item of the same section** - tighten the handle, make sure the quick-release latch is engaged in its groove, then the screws under and beside the handle - a care procedure under `section: maintenance`. The two symptoms share a section but not a part.
