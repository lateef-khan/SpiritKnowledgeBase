Section 7-1, *Belt Abnormalities and Noise Elimination*, second item:

> If you hear a **regular abnormal noise when starting the treadmill**, loosen the screws at the lower rear of the machine to **remove and replace the guide wheel assembly**.

**A noise that repeats in time with the belt, heard as the belt starts, is the guide wheel assembly, and the book replaces the assembly rather than diagnosing it.** No bearing, roller or belt is named as an alternative, and no test is printed - the remedy is the part. The assembly sits at the lower rear of the machine under two screws (Figures 7.1.1 and 7.1.2).

The first item of the same section is a belt that runs off centre, which the book sends back to the running-belt inspection and the roller replacement chapter - a tracking adjustment, carded under `section: maintenance`. The rest of chapter 7 is wobble: the console upright, the side handles and the small pedal, all answered with screws and foot pads, and the console rotation O-ring; those are care procedures and sit under `section: maintenance` too.

Other Spirit treadmills answer a noise at the back of the machine with the rear roller and its bearings (`spirit-2024-errors-noise-in-the-rear-of-the-treadmill`); this book does not, and a guide wheel assembly is a part the other books do not have.
