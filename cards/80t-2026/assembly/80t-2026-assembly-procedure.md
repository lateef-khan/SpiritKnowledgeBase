The manual numbers the build **1-1, 1-2, 1-3, 2-1, 2-2, 2-3 and 3**. The bolts
at the bottom of the uprights stay hand tight until step 1-3.

## Step 1-1 - right upright, then left

1. Snake the computer cable (254-1, 257-1, 273-1) through the right side upright
   tube. **Make sure the cable does not get pinched in the right side upright
   tube.** Lift the tube off the walking surface and attach it to the base frame.
   Gently pull on the computer cables as you align the upright tube into the
   bracket on the base frame, taking the slack out so the cable does not get
   pinched between the tube and the base frame.
2. Assemble 2 bolts (202) and 2 flat washers (204) through the corresponding
   hole (**lower two**) in the bottom of the right upright tube, screw into the
   base frame and hand tighten all two. **Do not torque the bolts yet until the
   upper console frame is installed.**
3. Install 2 bolts (205) and 2 flat washers (206) through the corresponding hole
   (**both sides**) in the bottom of the right upright tube and hand tighten. Do
   not torque the bolts yet until the upper console frame is installed.
4. Install the left upright tube in the same manner.

## Step 1-2 - console frame and the elbow covers

1. Connect the connectors (254-1, 257-1, 273-1 lower terminal)(254, 257, 273)
   between the top of the right side upright tube and the console frame. Make
   sure the cable does not get pinched.
2. Install the top and bottom riser twist covers (77 & 78) on each side on both
   elbows of the console frame. Install the top cover (77) to the console frame
   (7) using the screw (200), flat washer (201) and Phillips Screwdriver (216).
   Install the bottom cover (78) to the console frame (7) and attach it with the
   top cover with 2 screws (200) using the Phillips Screwdriver (216). **The ribs
   on these elbow covers should be up towards** (see the drawing). Make sure the
   computer cable does not get pinched in between the top and bottom elbow cover
   (right side).
3. Mount the console frame (7) to both upright tubes (6) with 6 bolts (207), 6
   split washers (203) and 6 flat washers (204) using the Allen Wrench (219). Be
   careful not to pinch the console cables.

## Step 1-3 - square the uprights, then torque

1. **Ensure that both upright tubes (6) are parallel, and both upright tubes are
   vertical with the base frame.** Then firmly tighten the bolts at the bottom of
   both upright tubes (202 & 205, shown in Step 1-1).
2. Assemble 2 bolts (202) and 2 flat washers (204) through the corresponding
   hole (**upper two**) in the bottom of the right upright tube, screw them into
   the base frame, and then firmly tighten the bolts. Be careful not to damage
   the console cables when assembled.
3. Install the left upright tube in the same way.
4. Install and attach the two Motor Covers (82 & 83).

## Step 2-1 - the console articulating arm

1. Connect the connectors (251, 254, 257, 273 lower terminal)(252, 256, 259,
   275) between the console mast and the console articulating arm.
2. Mount the console articulating arm on the console mast with 5 socket set
   screws (209) using the 3mm Allen Wrench (220). **Do not firmly tighten the
   socket set screws.** Loosen a quarter circle at each adjustment if the swivel
   arm feels too tight, and adjust the tightness to get a proper swivel function.
3. Attach the end cap (63) to the centre bottom of the console mast.

## Step 2-2 - the console

1. Plug all the connectors (252, 256, 259, 275) from the top of the console
   articulating arm to the console back.
2. Install the console (250) to the console articulating arm with 4 bolts (211)
   using the Phillips Screwdriver (216).
3. Attach the yellow cover (86) to the console articulating arm with 2 screws
   (208) using the Phillips Screwdriver (216).

## Step 2-3 - the power cord holder

1. Plug the power cord into the input connector located in the front of the
   product.
2. Align the Fixing plate (26) with the power cord and attach the power cord
   holder to the input module with 2 screws (210) and 2 split washers (212).
   **The C open of the power cord holder should face down towards the power
   cord.**

## Step 3 - handrails, rear step and bottle holder

1. Install the bolts (213) and flat washers (215) into the holes on the
   underside of the frame. **Only thread them into the holes two or three turns**
   so the slots in the handrail brackets can slide onto the bolts easily.
2. Slide the handrail onto the bolts and hand-tighten them.
3. Thread the bolts (214) into the sides of the handrails.
4. Once all the bolts are installed, tighten all of them securely.
5. Install the rear step by sliding it into the receiving tubes under the rear of
   the deck and secure with the two pins (42).
6. Raising the rear incline may make it easier to line up the holes for the pins.
7. Attach the bottle holder (85) to the console frame by snapping it into locking
   sockets on the bottom of the console frame.

## Damaged and unresolved text

- Step 1-3 item 3 is printed **"Install the left upright tube in the same
  installed."** The words are as shown; the sense is the same-manner instruction
  used in step 1-1.
- Step 1-1 item 3 says the two bolts (205) go through "the corresponding hole
  (both sides)" of one tube. The hardware table gives 4 of item 205 for the pair
  of uprights, two per tube.
