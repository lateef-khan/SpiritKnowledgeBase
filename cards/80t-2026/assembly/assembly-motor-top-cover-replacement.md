1. Refer to figures 6.2.1 to 6.2.4: **use a flathead screwdriver to remove the left and right motor covers**, then remove the **two screws**.
2. As shown in figure 6.2.5, **pull the motor upper cover upward at the seven clip positions** to remove it.

No screw size is printed. The part description names the **Motor Top Cover (02)**, **Motor Cover (L) (03)** and **Motor Cover (R) (04)**. The motor front cover, the power cord and the AC switch are reached after this cover is off (6-3), and the upright inspection sends you to 6-4 - as printed - "for removing the left (right) motor cover", which is this procedure's first step. The MT8000-based 7.0T loosens four front and four side screws and tilts its cover off; the 4.0T removes two four-screw motor base caps and six tapping screws. Seven clips and two screws are this machine's count.
