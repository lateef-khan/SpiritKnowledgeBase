**Calibrate the replacement motor first, then raise the incline frame until the connection hole lines up with the bottom of the motor.** No zeroing distance in millimetres is printed - the 4.0T prints 257 mm and 210 mm for its two motors; this book prints none.

**Front incline motor.**

1. **Raise the front incline and elevate the main frame** as in figures 6.7.1 and 6.7.2. **Loosen the screws on the top and bottom of the incline motor.** (Disconnect the wiring and cut the cable ties.)
2. When replacing the front incline motor, **calibrate it first**, and **raise the incline bracket until the connection hole aligns with the bottom of the incline motor**.
3. Refer to step 1 to reassemble the incline motor.

**Rear incline motor.**

4. **Raise the rear incline and elevate the main frame** as in figures 6.7.3 and 6.7.4. Loosen the screws on the top and bottom of the incline motor. (Disconnect the wiring and cut the cable ties.)
5. When replacing the rear lift motor, **calibrate first** and raise the rear lift frame to the position of the connection hole below the lift motor.

No screw sizes, wrench sizes or torque are printed, and "calibrate" is not defined on this page - the maintenance-mode menu's Incline Motor item has Front Motor D/A, Rear Motor D/A and Calibration entries, which is the console section's card. The MT8000-based 7.0T props the raised front on a sturdy object and prints no calibration step and no zeroing figure either; that is its own card.
