**Two wires on the switch: L is black, N is white.**

1. Remove the motor upper cover first (6-2). As shown in figures 6.3.1 to 6.3.3, **loosen the grounding screw**, then **pull the motor front cover forward to disengage the inner clips**, and disconnect or remove the related connectors. The motor front cover can then be removed.
2. As shown in figure 6.3.4, **to replace the power cord, loosen the two screws**.
3. As shown in figures 6.3.5 and 6.3.6, **to replace the AC switch, loosen the screws (L = black, N = white)**.

No screw sizes or counts for the switch are printed. The MT8000-based 7.0T prints the same L black, N white rule for a switch held by two screws in its front motor cover; the 4.0T and CT850 books replace their AC power switch behind a seven-screw front cover with an overload protective device and name no colours.
