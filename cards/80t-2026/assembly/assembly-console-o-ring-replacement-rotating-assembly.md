**Four wire connectors come off here; the 8.5R, 8.5UE and 8.5S rotating structures have three.**

1. Check whether the **console rotation assembly** operates smoothly.
2. **Loosen the four screws attached to the upright tube** (figure 7.5.1), then **pull out the console rotation assembly** (figure 7.5.2).
3. After **disconnecting the four wire connectors**, you can remove the O-ring (figures 7.5.3 and 7.5.4).

The book files this under troubleshooting as "Resolving Electronic Console O-ring Breakage", but it is a disassembly: no diagnosis, no refit step, no screw size and no O-ring size. The owner's manual's setting-up page describes the 360 degree swivel arm and how stiffly it should turn; that is the linked card.
