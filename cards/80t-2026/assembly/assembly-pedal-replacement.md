As shown in figure 6.8.1, **loosen the 4 screws at the bottom of the pedal** to replace it.

That is the whole procedure. The "pedal" is the small step pedal at the side of the deck - the troubleshooting chapter cures a wobbling small pedal by **adjusting its foot pad to an appropriate height** (figure 7.4.1), not by these screws. The owner's manual's rear step, which lowers the step-up height to 4 inches, is a different part on its own card.
