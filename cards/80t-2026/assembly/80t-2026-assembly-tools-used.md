**This manual prints no tools panel and no carton parts list.** The 4.0T and the
7.0T both print one; between the PRE-ASSEMBLY page and step 1-1 this manual
prints nothing of the kind. The only tools named anywhere are the ones the steps
call for by item number:

| Item | Tool | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| 216 | Phillips Screwdriver | steps 1-2, 2-2, 2-3 |
| 219 | Allen Wrench | step 1-2, mounting the console frame |
| 220 | 3mm Allen Wrench | step 2-1, the console arm set screws |

**The manual never gives a size for the Allen Wrench (219)**, only for the 3mm
one. It also never says these three are packed in the carton - it just uses
them. Do not tell a customer a tool is supplied on the strength of this list.

Two other tools are named elsewhere in the manual and are **not** part of the
build. A **1/2" wrench** is called for to loosen the top nut of a levelling
glide. An **8mm Allen wrench** turns the tread-belt tension and tracking
adjustment bolts, and the Machine Care section is the one place in this manual
that says a tool **is provided**: "A 8mm Allen wrench is provided to adjust the
rear roller."

The 4.0T and 7.0T are supplied with a 5mm Allen wrench, a 6mm Allen wrench and a
Phillips screwdriver - a different set from the one used here.
