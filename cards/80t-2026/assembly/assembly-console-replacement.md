1. Refer to figure 6.1.1: **loosen 2 screws and remove the back lower console cover**.
2. Refer to figures 6.1.2 and 6.1.3: **loosen four screws and plug off the terminals**, then remove the console.

No screw sizes, no terminal count and no refit note are printed. The 4.0T console comes off a four-screw console cover and a four-screw console support on a Phillips screwdriver; the 8.0U and 8.5R bikes lose two M4 screws from the cover sides and four M5 from a console bracket. The O-ring under the console rotation assembly is reached from the upright tube, not from here.
