## To lift

1. Loosen the left and right vertical adjustment **quick-release levers**.
2. Grab the handrail and pull up to adjust the height. Use the **numbered scale
   on the tubes** for repeatable settings.
3. Tighten the quick-release lever when the desired position is set.

## To lower

1. Loosen the left and right vertical adjustment quick-release levers.
2. Hold the handrail bar and lower the handrail.
3. Tighten the quick-release lever when the desired position is set.

**This is the quick-release handrail, not the knob-and-pin one.** The 7.0T uses
vertical adjustment knobs whose locking pins ratchet into indexing holes, is
raised and lowered with a lift bar, and has to be lowered past the position you
want and brought back up so the pin can catch. **None of that applies here** -
this machine has no lift bar and no ratcheting pin.

The horizontal adjustment is the same on both machines and is held on its own
card.
