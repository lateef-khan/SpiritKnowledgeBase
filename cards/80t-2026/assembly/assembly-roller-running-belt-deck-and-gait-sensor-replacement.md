**The middle crossbar has a guide plate, and the crossbar screw heads face forward on refit.** That is the one orientation rule the procedure prints.

1. Before removing the running deck, refer to 6-4 (rear adjustment trim covers and aluminum trim). As shown in figure 6.5.1, **disconnect the gait sensor connector and its lead wire**, then remove the **eight screws on the running deck**.
2. As shown in figures 6.5.3 and 6.5.4, **loosen the rear roller screws** to pull out and replace the rear roller.
3. As shown in figures 6.5.5 and 6.5.6, **pull out the running deck while pulling the gait sensor cable from underneath the deck**.
4. As shown in figures 6.5.7 and 6.5.8, **turn the running deck so its back faces outward**, then remove the screws and connectors to **detach the gait sensor**.
5. As shown in figures 6.5.9 to 6.5.11, **remove the three crossbars** (note: during installation, the middle crossbar has a guide plate, and **the screw heads should face forward**), then loosen the bottom screws to **replace the shock pads**.
6. As shown in figure 6.5.12, **loosen the screws on the left side** to remove the drive belt and running belt for replacement. (For belt installation, refer to 6-6; for running belt adjustment, refer to 6-5-8.)
7. As shown in figure 6.5.13, loosen the screws to **replace the front roller**.
8. First **tighten the two screws on the rear roller and start the treadmill**. Then **adjust the three screws on the front and rear rollers to align the running belt**. The step cites "Fig. 6.7.12 and Fig. 6.7.13" - figures that do not exist; its own figures are captioned 6.5.14 to 6.5.16.

No screw sizes, no belt tension and no torque are printed. Step 4's figures are captioned 6.5.5 and 6.5.6 for the second time. The **gait sensor** is the step sensor of the MT8000-based 7.0T, which has its own removal procedure with a magnet-setting figure; the 8.0T prints no gap for it. The MT8000 book also prints a separate cushion (A and B) procedure; here the shock pads come out with the crossbars.
