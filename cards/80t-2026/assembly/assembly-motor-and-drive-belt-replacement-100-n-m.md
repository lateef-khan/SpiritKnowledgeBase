**The belt-tension figure is printed as "100 N-m" - a torque unit, for a belt.** The MT8000-based 7.0T prints the same 100 N-m for its poly-V belt; the 4.0T and the CT850 books set theirs to 70 to 75 lb on a tension gauge. Which quantity the figure describes, the book does not say; it is printed beside the adjustment screws, and no gauge is named.

1. To remove the belt, first refer to 6-5-2 to loosen the rear roller (figure 6.6.1), then refer to 6-5-6 to loosen and replace the belt.
2. As shown in figures 6.6.2 and 6.6.3, **loosen the four screws under the motor and then loosen the two adjustment screws**.
3. As shown in figures 6.6.4 and 6.6.5, after loosening the screws as in step 2, **the plastic bushing can be replaced**.
4. As shown in figure 6.6.6, when installing the motor, **the adjustment screws are used to adjust the belt tension (100 N-m)**.
5. As shown in figures 6.6.7 and 6.6.8, when installing the motor, **the adjustment screws are used to adjust the motor vertically**.
6. After completing the adjustments of steps 3 to 4, refer to step 2 to **tighten the four screws at the bottom**.

No screw sizes are printed and the motor's wiring is not mentioned - disconnect it before the four screws come out. Step 6 says "6-6-3 to 6-6-4" as printed. The belt itself comes off from the left side in the deck procedure, which sends you back here for installation.
