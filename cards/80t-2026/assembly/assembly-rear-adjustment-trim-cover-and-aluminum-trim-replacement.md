1. As shown in figure 6.4.1, **loosen the screws to remove the rear adjustment trim cover** (same for left and right).
2. As shown in figures 6.4.2 and 6.4.3, **loosen the four screws at the bottom of the main frame to pull out the aluminum trim** (same for left and right).

No screw sizes are printed. The part description names the **Rear Adjustment Base (R) (19)** and **(L) (20)**. The running deck procedure begins here ("before removing the running deck, please refer to section 6-4"). The MT8000-based 7.0T loosens - without removing - four screws under the frame and slides its aluminum step rails off the back after a rear cover; that is its own card.
