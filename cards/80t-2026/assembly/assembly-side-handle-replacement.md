**Mind the direction of the thrust bearing on refit** (figure 6.9.8) - the one orientation rule printed in eight steps.

1. As shown in figures 6.9.1 to 6.9.4, **loosen the 4 screws and 2 quick-release handles** to replace the **armrest**.
2. As shown in figures 6.9.5 to 6.9.7, loosen the screws and **remove the round iron block** to replace the **thrust bearing**. (As shown in figure 6.9.8, pay attention to the direction during installation.)
3. As shown in figure 6.9.9, loosen the screw to replace the **small quick-release**.
4. As shown in figure 6.9.10, **loosen the 8 screws** to remove the **armrest assembly**.
5. As shown in figures 6.9.11 and 6.9.12, **loosen the 3 screws to pull down the telescopic oval tube**.
6. As shown in figures 6.9.13 and 6.9.14, use an **open-end wrench to loosen the nut** and remove the **thrust weld assembly**.
7. As shown in figures 6.9.15 and 6.9.16, loosen the screw to pull out and replace the **gas spring**.
8. As shown in figure 6.9.17, you can pull out the **telescopic tube**.

No sizes are printed. **A side handle that drops vertically when its quick-release latch is pulled has a failed gas spring** - the troubleshooting chapter sends that fault to steps 1 to 7 here. A side handle that merely feels unstable is tightened at the handle and checked at the screws under and beside it (3-2 and 7-3), which is the maintenance section's. The MT8000-based 7.0T's adjustable hand rails have their own knob, rail, thrust bearing and sliding-wheel procedure.
