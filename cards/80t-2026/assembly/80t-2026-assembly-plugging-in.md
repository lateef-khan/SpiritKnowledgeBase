The A.C. mains input connector is **in the front of the machine**. The input
module holds three things:

- an input connector for the line cord,
- a power switch,
- a **15 amp circuit breaker**.

**Turn the power switch to off when the machine is not in use.**

**The breaker rating is 15 amp on this machine and 20 amp on the 7.0T**, whose
Plugging in paragraph is otherwise word for word the same. Check the machine in
front of you before quoting a rating.

Assembly step 2-3 fits a **power cord holder** over this input module, so the
cord is retained at the connector; that is part of the build, not of setting up.

The parts diagram labels the same area from the outside: **P** is Power ON/OFF
and **Q** is the Power socket.
