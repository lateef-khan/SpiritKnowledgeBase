## Items counted in more than one table

**202 and 204 each appear twice** and the two entries are additive, not a
restatement: step 1-1 fills the lower holes at the bottom of each upright and
step 1-3 fills the upper ones. The manual prints no total. Adding its own rows
gives **8 bolts (202)** and **14 flat washers (204)**.

**204 and 206 are both Ø3/8" flat washers and they are not interchangeable** -
204 is Ø19 x 1.5T, 206 is Ø25 x 2T.

## Numbers that mean something else on a sibling machine

- **207** is a Bolt, M10 x 20L, here. On the 7.0T it is the knob that secures a
  handrail lift bar.
- **26** is the Fixing plate of the power cord holder here. On the 4.0T it is
  the Right Lower Console Mast.
- **259** labels a console connector here. On the 7.0T it is the 6mm Allen
  wrench.

The handrail hardware does the same job on the 7.0T under three different
numbers: 154, 155 and 226 there stand where 213, 215 and 214 do here, at the
same three sizes. The 7.0T also secures its rear step with pins (217); this
machine uses pins (42).
