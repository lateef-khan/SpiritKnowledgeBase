| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Compatible battery type | CR1220 |
| Nominal battery voltage | 3V |

"Non-rechargeable batteries are not to be recharged."

**The same page says the machine "Contains a non-removable battery" and that
"Only professionals are allowed to disassemble it."** So the page names a
compatible cell and in the next breath says the cell cannot be removed by the
user. Both statements are printed; read the CR1220 as the cell a service
technician fits, not as a user-replaceable part.

The rest of that page is battery handling and disposal warning text - recycle or
dispose of used cells per local regulations, keep away from children, never bin
or incinerate them, never force-discharge, recharge, disassemble, heat above
"85oC" (printed with a lower-case o where the degree sign belongs) or incinerate.
Those are safety warnings rather than a specification.

Neither the 7.0T nor the 4.0T manual names a battery type.
