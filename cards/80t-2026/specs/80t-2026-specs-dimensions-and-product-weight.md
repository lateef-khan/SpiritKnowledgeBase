| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Length | 213.3 cm (84") |
| Length with rear step | 249.7 cm (98.3") |
| Width | 95.1 cm (37.4") |
| Height | 161.5 cm (63.6") |
| Product weight | 252.5 kgs (556.7 lbs) |

**Two lengths are printed, not one.** 213.3 cm is the machine; 249.7 cm is the
machine with the rear step fitted. Quote the one that matches how the unit is
being installed.

The page gives no running deck or belt size, no folded dimensions and no shipping
weight.

The 7.0T of the same 2026 line is a smaller machine - 94.8" long and 496 lbs - and
the 4.0T manual prints no dimensions at all.
