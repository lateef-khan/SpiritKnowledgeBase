**The 8.0T (MT2000-ST027-01) service manual prints a numbered tool list of seventeen items** - the same list, in the same order, that the 8.5S, 8.0U, 8.5R and 8.5UE service manuals print as eighteen (`85s-2025-specs-parts-required-maintenance-tools-eighteen`), **minus their last row, the Crank Removal Tool**, which a treadmill has no use for. It is headed *List of Required Maintenance Tools* and reads:

| No. | Tool Name |
|---|---|
| 1 | Multi-meter |
| 2 | Cordless Impact Driver Set |
| 3 | Phillips Screwdriver |
| 4 | Diagonal Cutting Pliers |
| 5 | Needle-nose Pliers |
| 6 | Electric Soldering Iron |
| 7 | Solder Wire |
| 8 | Anti-static Tweezers |
| 9 | Heat Shrink Tubing |
| 10 | Utility Knife |
| 11 | Hex Key Set (Allen Wrench Set) |
| 12 | Socket Wrench Set |
| 13 | Cable Ties (Minimum Length: 20 cm) |
| 14 | Electrical Insulation Tape |
| 15 | Lead Wire (for guiding cable through tubing) |
| 16 | Insulated Gloves |
| 17 | Anti-static Wrist Strap |

**Only one size is printed** - the cable ties, minimum 20 cm. No hex-key size, no socket size and no meter range; the sizes the replacement chapter uses are inside its steps. Six of the seventeen are consumables or protective gear (solder wire, heat shrink tubing, cable ties, insulation tape, insulated gloves, the wrist strap), listed without distinction.

**This is not the one-multimeter page** the Dyaco-format treadmill service manuals print (`spirit-treadmill-specs-parts-service-manual-tool-list-is-one-multimeter`) - the 4.0T's ST8700 and ST8700A books and the 7.0T's MT8000 book name a multi-meter and nothing else. The 8.0T book, like the 8.0U, 8.5R and 8.5UE books of the same 2025-2026 template, names the whole bench.

**No parts list goes with it.** The 8.0T service manual prints no exploded view and no parts list; its owner's manual prints a drawing with no list (`spirit-2026t-specs-no-parts-list`).
