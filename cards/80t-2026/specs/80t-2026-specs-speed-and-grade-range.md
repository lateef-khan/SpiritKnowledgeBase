| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Speed, Forward | 0.0 to 22 km/h (0.0 to 13.6 mph) |
| Speed, Reverse | 0.0 to 8 km/h (0.0 to 5 mph) |
| Grade, Front | 0 to 25 % grade |
| Grade, Rear | 0 to 12 % grade |

**The manual contradicts itself on the rear grade.** *Unique Uses for the 8.0T*,
page 10, says "The front and rear incline allows for uphill(up to 25% grade) and
downhill(down to **-10%** grade) walking." The specification table on page 65 says
the rear grade is **0 to 12 %**. The front figure agrees at 25 %; the rear does
not, and nothing in the manual reconciles 10 % with 12 %. Quote both and say the
manual disagrees with itself rather than picking one.

Page 10 restates the speeds and they do agree - forward up to 22 km/h, reverse up
to 8 km/h - and adds that belt speed settings start at 0.1 km/hr, that forward is
the default direction at power-on, and that a reverse speed is shown with a minus
sign.

**Do not carry these to the 7.0T.** That machine runs to 10.0 mph (16 kph)
forward with a 0 to 15 % front grade.
