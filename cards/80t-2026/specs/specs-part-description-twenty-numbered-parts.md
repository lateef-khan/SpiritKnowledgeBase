The drawing is the treadmill from the front left with its side handrails up. The **Riser Twist
Covers (8, 9)** are the two collars at the top and bottom of each handrail upright's twist
adjustment - 9 is called out three times, once per riser and once at the rear; the **Safety Key
Module (16)** with its front and back covers (14, 15) sits on the console front, which is where the
wiring diagram's #251 *Safety Switch Module* plugs in; the **Rear Adjustment Bases (19, 20)** are
the two rear feet, with the **Square End Cap (18)** beside them. The **Console LED Cover (5)** is the
beacon light bar the maintenance mode's Beacon Test cycles through white, blue, green, yellow and red.

**No part numbers, quantities or dimensions here**; the machine's specification page is in the
owner's manual (`80t-2026-specs-dimensions-and-product-weight`). The 7.0T's book names nine
components on a different drawing (`mt200-2022-specs-component-description-callouts`).
