**Certifications, exactly as printed.**

- CAN/CSA-C22.2 No. 60601-1:14/A2:22 (R2022)
- ANSI/AAMI ES60601-1:2005+A1:2012+A2:2021
- IEC 60601-1-2:2014
- EN60601-1-2:2015
- IEC60601-1:2005+A1:2012+A2:2020
- EN60601-1:2006+A2:2021
- EN ISO 20957-1
- EN ISO 20957-6
- EU MDR 2017/745 Class I
- ISO 14971:2012

**This list is not the 7.0T's list.** The 7.0T carries earlier amendment years
throughout, cites EN ISO 20957:2013 as a single part rather than parts 1 and 6,
gives ISO 14971 as 2019 rather than 2012, and carries **no EU MDR entry at all**.
The two must never be quoted for each other; see `70t-2026-specs-certifications`.

The specification page prints no disposal paragraph for this machine - the 7.0T's
page does.
