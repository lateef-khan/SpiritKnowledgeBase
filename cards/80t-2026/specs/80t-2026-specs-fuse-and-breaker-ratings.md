Two separate devices, with separate ratings. Do not order one against the other's
numbers.

**Fuse rating - the circuit breaker.**

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Circuit breaker | AC 250V, 10A |
| Mode of tripping | thermal |
| Operating speed | 0.25 ~ 3.0 sec |
| Breaking capacity | 500 A, 250 Vac |

**Fuse on inverter.**

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Voltage | 125 Vac |
| Current | 1 A |
| Operating speed | 0.4 ~ 1.0 sec |
| Breaking capacity | 50 A, 125 Vac |
| Size | 2.65 x 7.1 mm |

The manual gives no part number for either, and no replacement procedure on this
page. The 7.0T and 4.0T manuals print no fuse or breaker rating at all.
