**Press STOP to pause or stop the exercise.** The pause screen offers two buttons:

- **Resume** - continue the session.
- **Finish** - end the session.

**When the program ends** - either by the set time reaching zero, or by pressing
STOP at any time and then pressing Finish - **a summary is shown on the screen.
The summary provides detailed data for the amount of time the user exercised.**

**This console has no press-twice or hold-three-seconds behaviour.** The 4.0T and
7.0T count Stop presses; this one puts Resume and Finish on the screen instead.

If the user did not log in at the start, they can still log in afterwards to
record the session; see `80t-2026-console-user-login-methods`.
