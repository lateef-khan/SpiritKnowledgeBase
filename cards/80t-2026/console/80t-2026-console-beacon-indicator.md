**The Beacon uses brightly coloured lights on the top of the console to gauge
exercise effort and remind physical therapists of their patients' progress at a
glance.**

**This function may be turned on or off in the setup.** The colour indicators can
be set to show one of three measures: **heart rate, power, or progress**.

| Colour | % HR | % Watts | % Workout Progress |
|---|---|---|---|
| White | 0-59% | 0-20% | 0-24% |
| Blue | 60-69% | 21-40% | 25-49% |
| Green | 70-79% | 41-60% | 50-74% |
| Yellow | 80-89% | 61-80% | 75-99% |
| Red | 90-100% | 81-100% | 100% |

What the colours mean: **White & Blue** - initial condition of warm up, or low
stage. **Green** - ready for use, or medium stage. **Yellow** - the high stage
caution, or close to the end. **Red** - the highest stage warning, or the final
stage.
