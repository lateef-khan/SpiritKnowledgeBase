**Wireless connectivity: Wi-Fi, BLE, ANT+.**

- **Wi-Fi** - uploads data to the cloud.
- **BLE** - the FTMS protocol, for 3rd party apps.
- **BLE & ANT+** - connect with a Heart Rate sensor.

The console supports wireless connection through ANT+ and Bluetooth, so it can
work with whatever third party heart rate sensor unit the user wants to connect.
**Dyaco 8 series products support ANT+ natively, having an integrated ANT+
chipset.** ANT+ is a communication protocol using very low energy that can
broadcast to several types of equipment at the same time.

**FTMS (Fitness Machine Service)** is the wireless communication protocol that
allows third party software to control exercise equipment such as bike trainers,
treadmills and rowing machines. The manual notes that making Bluetooth machines
available to connect has historically been difficult because each sensor required
a specific protocol.

**Data transfer:** real-time streaming of the patient's workout data is available
for download. **Login:** NFC, PIN or QR Code.

**This page and the heart rate transmitter page disagree.** Page 59 says most
transmitters that operate at **5kHz** will work, which is not BLE or ANT+; see
`spirit-2026t-console-chest-strap-5khz`.
