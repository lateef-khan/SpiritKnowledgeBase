After the language and WiFi step (`80t-2026-console-wifi-setup`), the console runs
through four screens:

1. **Organization account sign in.** Enter the organization's account info -
   **email and password** - to sign in. **If you do not have an organization
   account yet, click "No account yet" at the left below.**
2. **Create new organization member's account.** **Scan the QR code** and complete
   the form that follows to create a new account.
3. **Product Registration.** Enter the **Administrator's email and password** to
   sign in, then **enter the product's Serial number for registration**.
4. **Time and Date.** Set the local time/date and **click "Start" to finish
   setup**.

**This is the machine-level setup, not the per-user login.** Individual users sign
in separately with a QR code, an NFC tag or a PIN; see
`80t-2026-console-user-login-methods`.

**Organization accounts exist only on this machine.** The 4.0T and 7.0T manuals
never mention them.
