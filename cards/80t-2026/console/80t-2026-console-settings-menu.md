The Settings screen adjusts the preferences. The manual names six:

- **Beacon feature setting**
- **Language**
- **Time & date**
- **WiFi**
- **Standby or Pause mode**
- **Units of measurement**

**This is the only settings menu this manual prints.** Unlike the 4.0T and the
7.0T, **the 8.0T manual describes no maintenance mode, no service mode and no
factory settings**, and gives no key combination to reach one. Do not quote the
7.0T's Start + Stop + Enter sequence for this machine.
