Choose the preferred log in method, **or enter the system as a guest**. **Make sure
the WiFi connection is available, or you will enter the system as a guest in
offline status.**

| Method | How it works |
|---|---|
| **QR code** | The user scans the QR code with their own phone and logs in |
| **NFC** | The console is equipped with an **RFID reader**. Hold your phone or RFID tag to **the symbol located on the bottom left of the console** |
| **Pin code** | The clinician **generates the pin code in the Dyaco Insight App** and provides it to the user. **The user can log in using the pin code for one day** |

**You can also log in after exercising.** If you did not log in at the beginning,
you can still log in to the system to record your exercise data after finishing
the session.
