**Safe Steps is for forward walking only.**

**The program starts with zero speed.** It **moves the distance of a step length
every time the user steps on the deck.**

That is everything this manual prints about it. **It gives no speed figure, no
setup screen and no way to change the step distance**, and Safe Steps does not
appear in the program list on page 34.
