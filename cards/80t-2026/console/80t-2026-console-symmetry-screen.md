**Symmetry mode may aid in achieving a more balanced step stroke for patients with
bilateral deficiencies** - such as neurological patients and patients with limited
lower body function.

The mode **measures the left and right step lengths through the motion** and
displays the step length measurements on the screen. The screen shows a **graph, a
step length value and a symmetry index**, giving visual feedback to improve gait
balance.

**The Symmetry Index number above the chart shows the difference percentage between
the left and right step length. If the left and right steps produce the same step
length the Symmetry Index will be 0%.**

The manual's sample picture shows the **right step producing more step length than
the left, 31 vs. 28 cm.**
