**The service manual's alignment is two photographs, and they show the wrench in whichever end cap the belt
has drifted away from - not "left side only" as the owner's manual says.** 3-1 Running Belt Inspection, as
printed: "if the running belt is drifting improperly, please follow the instruction in below figures to align the
running belt."

What the figures show, read from the page:

| Figure | Belt | Wrench | Turn |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1.1 | arrow pointing **left** | in the **right** end cap | **clockwise** |
| 3.1.2 | arrow pointing **right** | in the **left** end cap | **clockwise** |

Both arrows curve the same way, so the rule the pictures teach is: **turn the bolt on one side clockwise and the
belt moves away from that side.** Figure 3.1.2 is the owner's manual's instruction - left bolt, clockwise, belt
goes right (`80t-2026-maintenance-treadbelt-tracking-adjustment`); figure 3.1.1 is the mirror the owner's manual
does not print. The service manual prints **no turn increment, no belt speed and no wrench size**; the owner's
manual supplies those - a 1/4 turn at a time, 2 to 3 mph, an 8 mm Allen wrench. Use them together.

## Two more places the book sends you

- **7-1 item 1:** "If the running belt is misaligned, please refer to Sections 3-1 and 6-5-8 to adjust the belt."
- **6-5 step 8**, after roller, belt or deck work: "first tighten the two screws on the rear roller and start the
  treadmill. Then adjust the three screws on the front and rear rollers to align the running belt" (the step
  cites figures 6.7.12 and 6.7.13; the captions on the page read 6.5.14-6.5.16). That is the post-replacement alignment; the Dyaco MT8000 book carded for the 7.0T says the same
  job with tension steps and a gap check (`mt200-2022-maintenance-belt-tension-and-tracking-after-roller-work`).

**No running-belt tension figure and no lubricant anywhere in the book.** The only tension it prints is the motor
drive belt's, set with the motor adjustment screws (100 N·m, an assembly step). Belt tension for the running belt
is the owner's manual's 1/4-to-1/2-turn procedure (`80t-2026-maintenance-treadbelt-tension-adjustment`).

The MT8000 book for the 7.0T checks alignment with the belt running **in both directions**
(`mt200-2022-maintenance-belt-alignment-forward-and-reverse`); this book does not say that.
