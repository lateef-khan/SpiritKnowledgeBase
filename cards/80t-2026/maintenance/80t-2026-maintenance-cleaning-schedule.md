The 8.0T schedules by **period**, and its weekly row is written in facility terms — *Weekly / Daily
(Daily member Use)* — not in the residential intervals the rest of the range uses.

| Period | Task | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| After Each Use | General cleaning | Use water only. Use a soft, damp cloth or paper towel to clean the console, and to wipe the equipment surface down, front hand grip, motor cover, handlebar and levers. |
| Weekly / Daily (Daily member Use) | Functional & safety checking | Ensure the equipment is functioning properly. Check around the equipment and inspect the tread belt for damage and wear. Use a soft, damp cloth or paper towel to wipe clean the full surface and console. |
| Monthly | General cleaning | Use water only. Use a soft, damp cloth or paper towel to clean the console, and to wipe the equipment surface down, the edge of the belt, reach as far as practical directly under the belt, and the area between the belt edge and frame. |
| Monthly | Treadbelt cleaning | No cleaners or abrasives. A mild soap and water solution along with a nylon scrub brush to clean the top of the textured belt (**10:1 water to soap**). Vacuum underneath and around the equipment to prevent dirt, dust and carpet fibres buildup. |
| Monthly | Functional & safety checking | Check treadbelt tension and centered orientation. |
| Yearly | General cleaning, tread belt cleaning, functional & safety checking | Do a round of the above tasks. Remove the motor cover and vacuum out dirt (**unplug the power cord before this task**). |

## Read with the cleaning cautions

The page-62 cleaning instructions above this table add: turn the main power switch OFF and
disconnect the equipment from its outlet first; never use wet cleaning materials near a power
source; and the same 10:1 water-to-soap ratio. The page-64 warning list says what must never be
used — `80t-2026-maintenance-cleaning-cautions`.

## This is not the 4.0T schedule

The 4.0T prints a task-by-interval table with different intervals — a **weekly** belt tension and
tracking check where this machine asks monthly, and a **semi-annual** motor-cover clean where this
machine asks yearly. See `40t-2026-maintenance-schedule`. The 7.0T prints no schedule at all.
