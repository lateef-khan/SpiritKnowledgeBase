Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each workout.

If a squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling develops the main cause is most likely one of two
reasons:

1. **The hardware was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.** All bolts that were installed
   during assembly need to be tightened as much as possible. It may be necessary to use a larger
   wrench than the one provided if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently. **90% of calls to the
   service department for noise issues can be traced to loose hardware.**
2. **The treadbelt tension/tracking need retightening or adjusting.** If squeaks or other noises
   persist, check that the unit is properly levelled. There are **2 leveling pads on the bottom of
   the rear incline unit**. Use a **1/2" wrench** (or an adjustable wrench) to loosen the top nut
   of the levelers and adjust as necessary to remove any wobble in the equipment. Then tighten all
   the top nuts against the bottom of the stabilizer tubes. Make sure the bottom nut remains
   cinched against the leveling foot.

For the adjustments themselves see `80t-2026-maintenance-treadbelt-tension-adjustment` and
`80t-2026-maintenance-treadbelt-tracking-adjustment`.

## The same levelling is also described in the setup chapter

Page 21, "Leveling the 8.0T", gives the same procedure in the machine's setup instructions: adjust
the **leveling glides** on the bottom of the rear incline unit with a 1/2" wrench, tighten the top
nut against the bottom of the **stabilizer tube** (singular there, plural here), and keep the
bottom nut cinched against the leveling foot. Same wrench, same parts, two wordings.

## This is not the CT900 or CTSBS900 wording

On those machines reason 2 is a **crank arm nut** and the levellers are on the **rear stabilizer**
with a **14mm** wrench (`ct900-post-workout-care-and-hardware-check`,
`ctsbs900-post-workout-cleaning`). Different part, different wrench.
