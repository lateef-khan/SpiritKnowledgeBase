**Three checks in the preventive chapter, and the troubleshooting chapter sends each wobble back to one of
them.** No interval, no torque and no bolt size is printed for any of them.

| Check | As printed |
|---|---|
| 3-2 Handlebars | check that the handlebars are **secured** and that the **quick release levers are inserted firmly into the groove** (figures 3.2.1, 3.2.2); then check whether the handlebars on both sides are **uneven** (figure 3.2.3) |
| 3-3 Uprights | check whether the **bolts on the side and top of the uprights** are loose; if so tighten them (figures 3.3.1-3.3.4). Reaching them means taking the left or right motor cover off - "refer to 6-4" |

## The wobbles, and where each goes

- **Console or upright post rocking left and right** in use (7-2): "refer to Section 3-3 and tighten the screws"
  - the upright bolts.
- **Side handle unstable** (7-3 item 1): "refer to 3-2 to tighten the handle and ensure the quick-release latch is
  properly engaged in the groove"; if it persists, check the **screws under and on the side of the side handle**
  (figure 7.3.1), both sides alike. A side handle that **drops** when the latch is pulled is not a screw - its gas
  spring is replaced (7-3 item 2, steps 6-9-1 to 6-9-7, an assembly job).
- **Small pedal unstable** (7-4): "adjust the foot pad to an appropriate height" (figure 7.4.1) - the step pedal
  is levelled on its own foot, not bolted tighter.

The console swivel's O-ring (7.5) is reached the same way as on the 8.5R and 8.5UE - four screws, pull the
rotation assembly, disconnect the connectors
(`85r-2025-maintenance-preventive-check-console-mast-bolts-and-rotating-structure-o-ring`).

**Not the MT8000 checks.** The Dyaco book carded for the 7.0T checks its adjustable hand rails' release handles
and knobs and its aluminium uprights with different figures
(`mt200-2022-maintenance-adjustable-hand-rail-checks`, `mt200-2022-maintenance-aluminium-upright-wobble-check`);
this book's handlebars lock with quick-release levers in a groove. The owner's manual's noise page for this
machine is `80t-2026-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`.
