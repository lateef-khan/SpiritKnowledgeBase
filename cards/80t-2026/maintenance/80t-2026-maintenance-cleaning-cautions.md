## Cleaning instructions (p. 62)

1. Turn the main power switch to **OFF** and disconnect the equipment from its power outlet. Never
   use wet cleaning materials near a power source or you could suffer an electrical shock.
2. **Use water only — no cleaners or abrasives**, with a soft, damp cloth or paper towel. Manually
   wipe all surfaces and clean the most touched points (e.g. emergency brake, front handlebar,
   saddle, console display and buttons, main body cover, adjustment levers) and hard-to-reach
   spots.
3. A mild soap and water solution (**10:1 water to soap**) along with a nylon scrub brush will
   clean the top of the textured pedals. Allow them to dry before using.

Avoid excessive cleaning, which can damage the item being cleaned.

## Warning list (p. 64)

- Disconnect the device from the power line before any cleaning, maintenance or disinfection.
- Never use wet cleaning materials near a power source.
- Do not allow liquids to enter the equipment.
- Do not spray water or cleaning chemicals directly on the equipment.
- Equipment into which liquids have entered must be immediately cleaned and checked by a service
  technician before it can be reused.
- **Do not use acids, alkaline solutions (household cleaners) or caustic disinfectants.**
- **The use of cleaning agents and disinfectants containing alcohol is not permitted.**
- Do not use any equipment that is damaged or has worn or broken parts. Use only replacement parts
  supplied by your local dealer.
- Equipment needs to be inspected at regular intervals. If signs of damage or wear are found,
  remove the equipment from service. Have a service technician inspect and repair it before putting
  it back into service.

## The manual contradicts itself on alcohol

Page 61 instructs you to prepare **20 ml of 70% or greater isopropyl alcohol** and disinfect with it
(`80t-2026-maintenance-disinfection-procedure`). Page 64 says cleaning agents and disinfectants
**containing alcohol are not permitted**. The manual never reconciles the two, and neither page
refers to the other. Do not answer from one half alone; quote both and refer the customer to Spirit
Fitness service before choosing.

The steps 1 and 3 above are the same instructions the page-62 schedule table sits under —
`80t-2026-maintenance-cleaning-schedule`. Note that step 3 says "textured **pedals**" on a
treadmill; the schedule table one page later says "textured **belt**" for the same task.
