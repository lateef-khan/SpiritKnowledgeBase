**This machine takes an 8mm Allen wrench. The 7.0T takes a 10mm one for the same job.**

1. Adjustment must be made from the **rear roller**. The adjustment bolts are at the end of the
   step rails, in the end caps. *Adjustment is through a small hole in the end cap.*
2. Tighten the rear roller bolts only enough to prevent slippage at the front roller.
3. Turn **both** tread-belt tension adjustment bolts with an **8mm Allen wrench** in increments of
   **1/4 to 1/2 turn each**.
4. Inspect for proper tension by walking on the belt at a low speed, making sure the belt does not
   slip.
5. Keep tensioning the bolts until the belt stops slipping.

**Do not overtighten.** Over tightening will cause belt damage and premature bearing failure.

If you feel the belt is tight enough but it still slips, the problem may be a **loose motor drive
belt** under the front motor cover.

The 8.0T's own cleaning table asks for a monthly check of "treadbelt tension and centered
orientation" — `80t-2026-maintenance-cleaning-schedule`.

Damage caused by a bad tension adjustment is not a warranty item —
`spirit-2026t-warranty-belt-adjustment-damage`.

## A second block on the same page prints a different turn increment

Page 63 carries a duplicated "GENERAL MAINTENANCE" block over the manual's own column. That block
says "in increments of 1/4 turn each" and names no wrench. The figures above are the ones in the
8.0T's own text.
