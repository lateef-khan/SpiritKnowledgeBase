- **Watch item 92.** The XE200 list gives the `˜25x15_Foot Pad` the number 022919, which is also item 94's number, the 1/4"x19 flat washer; the XE100 and XE300 lists give the same foot pad 010124. Item 85 is 010113 here (the XE100 list says 010150 for the same 5x16 tapping screw, and both lists give 010113 to item 87). Item 64 is printed `M8x3_Socket Head Cap Screw - 3M` where the other lists read M8x30. Kept as printed; check with Spirit before ordering.
- Items 46~1 and 46~2, the handgrip foams, carry a change note: `38x420L_Handgrip Foam (L) After #1121 - #006163` - from serial 1121 the part becomes 006163 (right, 006164) - while the numbers in the column stay 006022 and 006023.
- Items 57/57~1 and 58/58~1 are connecting arm covers A and B, each a *SET* with its unnumbered twin; the XE100 has one cover each side instead.
- The list ends with eight unnumbered rows: the Hardware Kit (006013), the handle switch sold three ways - overlay only 006011, switch only 006012, on its plastic housing 006070 - the Footplate Replacement Kit (006153), the side decal (006103), the Locking Pin Bracket alone (006238) and its 5x10 screw (000051).

**Order against the serial number.** The same book's parts purchase order treats a line without a serial number as a stock order (`spirit-xt-2007-policy-parts-purchase-order-form`); the owner's manual for this machine prints drawings but no list at all (`spirit-ce-assembly-exploded-view-and-parts-list`), so this is the only place its part numbers are printed.
