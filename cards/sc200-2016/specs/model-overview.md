The manual's cover code is **XS110A-YS003**. Section 1 is titled "XS110A-YS003 Outlines"; the table of contents heads it simply "Outlines" with no model name.

**The manual describes this machine as a climber.** Section 3 reads "CONSOLE: Interface that controls all functions of the Climber", the block diagram page is headed "CLIMBER Configuration" and the wiring diagram is headed "CLIMBER CIRCUIT DIAGRAM".

**But sections 7, 4 and the troubleshooting matrix call it a bike.** The whole of section 7, Product Safety Instructions, is written about "your bike"; the READY MODE and RUN MODE button descriptions say "Pressing START button to start bike" and "press STOP button to stop bike"; the PULSE entry says "if the bike doesn't have a signal for 8 seconds"; and the matrix rows read "Another chest belt in use around bike" and "Chest belt too close to the bike". These are the fixed phrases carried over from the Sole bike manual. **This is the same self-contradiction the SC200 2019 manual carries.** Nothing in the manual describes a bike seat.

The machine has a console with a **7.5" LCD display**, a **tension motor** that changes brake resistance, a speed sensor, a flywheel with a drive belt and idler wheel, connecting arms, linked assemblies, a curved rail tube and a crank arm. There is no incline motor and no incline or stride adjustment; **the manual carries no calibration procedure and no fuse replacement section.**

The parts drawings name: console assembly, handle bar covers L and R, console mast covers L and R, chain covers L and R, pedals L and R, rear shroud, slide wheel cover (R) and two covers both labelled Stabilizer Cover (L); console mast, console holder assembly, left and right upper handgrips, drive pulley, crank arm, idler wheel assembly, curved rail tube, flywheel, connecting arms L and R, main frame, rear stabilizer with handle and linked assemblies L and R.
