**190 Hz, plus or minus 10 Hz**, read on a sound wave tester while the belt vibrates with a finger.

After the idler wheel assembly is installed, **tighten the M8 x 9T nut until the tester reads 190 Hz**.

Note the nut in the source: the removal step in the same section loosens an **M8 x 7T nyloc nut on the hook screws**, while the refit step tightens an **M8 x 9T nut**. Check the nut on the machine.

The manual prints the figure once, inside the idler wheel assembly procedure. The belt slipping entry in the troubleshooting section does not repeat it; it only tells you to adjust the cap on the hook-type screw with a #13 wrench.
