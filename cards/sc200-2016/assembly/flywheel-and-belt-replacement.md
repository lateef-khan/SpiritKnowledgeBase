1. Remove the idler wheel assembly. **Use the console to adjust the resistance to level 20** and remove the steel cable.
2. Take the steel cable apart from the flywheel, then take the drive belt off the drive pulley.
3. Use a **15 mm wrench** to release **two 3/8"-UNF26 x 9T nuts** securing the flywheel onto the main frame. Take apart the drive belt connected to the flywheel.
4. Reassemble the flywheel and belt in the reverse order, using the crank arm to rotate the drive pulley. Use a **#17 wrench** to tighten the **inner flywheel nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 4T** and adjust the belt to the centre of the belt wheel. Tighten the nut and use the crank arm to adjust the belt to the appropriate tension.

The belt tension figure is not repeated here; it is **190 Hz +/- 10 Hz**, printed in the idler wheel assembly procedure.
