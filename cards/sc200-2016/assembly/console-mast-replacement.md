1. First remove the console mast.
2. Unplug the round cap under the handpulse tube and pull out the handpulse cables to take the handpulse sensor assembly apart.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **two 3x20 tapping screws** which secure the hand pulse assembly, pull it out and disconnect the cables.
4. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly. Be sure not to crush or damage wiring during the process.

**Step 1 is circular in the source**: the first step of the console mast procedure is "First remove the Console Mast". No fasteners are named for taking the mast off the frame.
