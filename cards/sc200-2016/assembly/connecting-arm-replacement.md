1. Remove the connecting arm set and swing axis following the swing assembly set and linked assembly disassembly procedures.
2. Remove **2 pcs of M5 x 10 m/m Phillips head screws** which fix the slide wheel cover, divide the slide wheel cover from the connecting arm set, then remove the swing axis from the connecting arm set.
3. Use **C-ring pliers** to release the **17 C-ring** and take apart the slide wheel and pedal.
4. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **head screw M5x10mm** securing the pedal, and remove the pedal.
5. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
