1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **four M5x10mm Phillips head screws** which secure the handle bar covers.
2. Use **12 and 13mm wrenches** to release **six 5/16" x 1-3/4 hex head screws**, **six 5/16" x 7T nyloc nuts** and **six 5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washers** which secure the swing arm.
3. Use a screwdriver to release **3 pcs of 4 x 15L sheet metal screws** fixed on the right and left console mast covers, and remove both covers.
4. Use a screwdriver to remove **7 pcs of 4 x 15L sheet metal screws** fixed on the left and right chain covers.
5. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the round **tapping screw 5 x 16L** from the left and right covers.
6. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **5 tapping screws 5x19mm** that secure the right chain cover to the mainframe.
7. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **5 tapping screws 5x19mm** that secure the left chain cover to the mainframe.
8. Release the nut of the **DC power plug**, then pull out the DC power cables; chain cover (L) can then be released.
9. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
