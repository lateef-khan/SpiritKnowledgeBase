1. Remove the left and right chain covers following their disassembly steps.
2. Use a **10mm wrench** to release the **M6 x 6T nyloc nut** fixed on the **M6 x 45L eye bolt**.
3. Use a **12mm wrench** to remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 3/4"** and **flat washer 5/16" x 23 x 3T** fixed on the swing axis.
4. Remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 3/4"** and **flat washer 5/16" x 23 x 3T** fixed on the swing axis and linked assembly.
5. Divide the connecting U-seat from the swing axis and linked assembly, then remove the swing axis.
6. Use a **12mm wrench** to release the **5/16" x 3/4" hex head bolt** and **8.5 x 26 x 2T flat washer** fixed on the linked assembly and crank arm, then separate them.
7. Reassemble in the reverse order. **When refitting the swing axis, the notch plane must be aligned with the U-slot gap plane before the screws go in.**
