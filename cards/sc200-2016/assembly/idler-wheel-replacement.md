1. Remove the linked assembly.
2. Use a **#13 double open-end wrench** to loosen the **nyloc nut M8 x 7T on the hook screws** to remove the belt. Use the #13 wrench to remove the **screws M8 x 20**, **nyloc nut M8 x 7T** and **flat washer 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T** to remove the idler wheel.
3. Reverse the above steps to refit the idler wheel assembly. After the idler wheel assembly is installed, **make sure the M8 x 9T nut is tight enough that the sound wave tester reads 190 Hz +/- 10 Hz when the belt vibrates with a finger**.

Note the nuts: step 2 loosens an **M8 x 7T** nyloc nut on the hook screws and step 3 tightens an **M8 x 9T** nut. Check the nut on the machine.

Step 1 in the source reads "Refer to step 9-8", which is the crank arm section; the linked assembly is section 9-4.
