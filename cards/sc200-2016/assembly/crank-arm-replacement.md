1. Remove the linked assembly.
2. Use a **12mm open end wrench** to remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 3/4"** and **flat washer 8.5 x 26 x 2.0T** securing the crank axle of the crank arm.
3. Use an **L Allen wrench and a #13 open end combination spanner wrench** to loosen the outer **nut M8 x 6.3T**. Use the #13 wrench and an **M6 hex wrench** to loosen the inner nut M8 x 6.3T, then remove the crank arm.
4. When reassembling, **place the crank arm into the centre of the crank axle**. Next place the **Woodruff key with the rounded part facing inwards**. Reassemble the remaining parts in reverse order. **Tighten the socket head cap bolt in the range of 550 Lbs.**

Note the source: this section refers to the socket head cap bolt without a size, while the slip problem section names it an **M8 x 35mm** socket head cap bolt on the crank arm assembly. Its own step 1 cross reference reads "Refer to step 11.4", but the linked assembly procedure in this manual is section 9-4.
