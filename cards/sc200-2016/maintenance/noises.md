The product is QC inspected in the plant before delivery. Possible sources of noise are the **bearings, the flywheel and the slide wheel on the rear side**.

Check whether the corrugated washer is installed, and check for a damaged bearing or a damaged pedal.

This is a much shorter list than the 2016 Sole elliptical manuals print for the same question.
