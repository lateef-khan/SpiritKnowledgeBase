Shaking is mainly due to loose screws, so **check and tighten loose screws first**.

Check connection points to see if gaps have been created after prolonged use, causing wear and tear, or from improper assembly. For example, shaking of the foot pedal is caused by loose carriage bolts from improper assembly.

**For unit wobble, adjust the thumb head socket screw for the leveling foot beneath the unit.** Apply thick lubricant when replacing a leveling foot.
