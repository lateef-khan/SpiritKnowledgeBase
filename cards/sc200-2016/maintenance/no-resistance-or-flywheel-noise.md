The manual describes two kinds of flywheel problem: **unresponsive resistance adjustment** and **abnormal noise**.

1. If resistance adjustment on the console is unresponsive and the gear motor is functioning normally, **check the cable to see whether the flywheel is in place properly or whether the cable is loose**.
2. If there is abnormal noise during flywheel rotation, check the flywheel, drive pulley and idler wheel assembly for foreign objects or friction first. Disassemble and reassemble the flywheel; if the noise persists, replace the flywheel.

If the display also shows E2, work the E2 card instead.
