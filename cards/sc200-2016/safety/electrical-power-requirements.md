- Install the machine on a flat level surface with access to a **120-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet**, with only this machine plugged into the circuit.
- Do not use an extension cord unless it is **16 AWG or better with only one outlet on the end**. Do not disable the grounded plug with adapters or modify the cord outlet.
- **Never use a GFCI wall outlet with this machine.** As with any appliance with a large motor, the GFCI will trip often. Route the power cord away from any moving part, including the elevation mechanism and transport wheels.
- To reduce the risk of electric shock, disconnect the machine from the outlet before cleaning or service work.
- The product must be grounded, and the grounding instructions state it is **for use on a nominal 120-volt circuit**. Improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric shock.

Two notes on the source. Section 7 is written throughout about "your bike", not a climber. And the voltage is **120 volt**, not the 115 volt the 2016 Sole elliptical manuals print in the same two places; the troubleshooting matrix still says to check AC power is 110-120V.
