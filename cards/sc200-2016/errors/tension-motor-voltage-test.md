1. Put the multi-meter to the **12VDC** setting. Place probes on the motor control wire (**red probe in blue wire, black probe in green wire**) on the drive board.
2. Turn on unit power. The display lights up.
3. Press LEVEL UP. Normal reading: **+5.5~6.0VDC**. Motor operates. Resistance increases.
4. Press LEVEL DOWN. Normal reading: **-5.5~6.0VDC**. Motor operates. Resistance decreases.
5. If there is no voltage, inspect the power socket holder FUSE. If broken, replace it.
6. Inspect whether the drive board POWER LED is lit. If it is not lit the drive board is bad. Replace it.

The manual repeats the probe placement under the photo on the next page in the same words, so the wire colours are stated twice and agree.

**Note the meter setting and the readings.** This manual says **12VDC** on the meter and **+/-5.5~6.0VDC** as the normal reading. The 2016 Sole elliptical manuals say 20VDC and +/-5~6.0VDC. The wire colours here, blue and green, match the E95 and E95S manuals rather than the brown and black of the E25, E35 and E55.

Step 5 sends you to the power socket holder fuse, but **this manual has no fuse replacement section**.
