Main control side, 5 pins:

| Pin | Signal |
|---|---|
| 1 | M- |
| 2 | M+ |
| 3 | VR1 |
| 4 | VR2 |
| 5 | GND |

The drawing numbers the mating side 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 against these, and shows the steel rope leaving the motor.

**This is not the pinout of the 2019 Sole ellipticals.** Those read 1.M+, 2.M-, 3.+5V, 4.VR, 5.GND: pins 1 and 2 are the other way round, and this machine has two VR pins where they have one VR and a +5V. Read the plug on the machine.
