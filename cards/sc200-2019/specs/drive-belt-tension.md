**190 Hz, plus or minus 10 Hz**, read on a sound wave tester while the belt is vibrated with a finger.

After the idler wheel assembly is installed, **tighten the M8 x 9T nut until the tester reads 190 Hz**.

The manual prints this figure once, inside the idler wheel assembly procedure. The belt slipping entry in the troubleshooting section does not repeat it; it only tells you to adjust the cap on the hook-type screw with a #13 wrench.
