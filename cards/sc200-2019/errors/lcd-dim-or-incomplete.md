The matrix prints two rows for this symptom.

**LCDs not bright, incomplete or imperfect**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1. LCD light is broken. | 1. Replace with new LCD or console. |
| 2. Power to console too low. | 2. **Check AC power is 220~230V.** 3. Check power to console. 4. Replace lower controller. |

**LCD displays not bright, incomplete or imperfect**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1. LCD displays are broken. | 1. Replace with new console. |

**A contradiction in this manual, left as printed.** This row says to check for **220~230V**, but the safety instructions on page 30 say the machine is installed on a **120-volt, 15-amp** outlet and the grounding paragraph says it is for use on a nominal **120-volt** circuit. The 2019 Sole elliptical manuals print 110-120V in this same matrix row. Confirm the supply the machine is actually built for before acting on the 220~230V figure.
