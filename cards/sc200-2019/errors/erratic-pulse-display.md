| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1. Another chest belt in use around the climber. | 1. Check for other chest belt use around the climber. |
| 2. Other magnetic field disturbance. | 2. Change the position or direction of the climber. |
| 3. Receiver is broken. | 3. Replace with a new receiver. |

A separate matrix row covers a chest belt held too close to the climber: the cause given is a weak battery, and the fix is a new **CR2032** lithium battery.
