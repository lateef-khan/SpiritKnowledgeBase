Symptom row: **Hand pulse lost its function (no pulse displayed on monitor).**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1. Hands not on the hand pulse sensors, or only one hand on a sensor. | 1. Hold the hand pulse with two hands. |
| 2. The connector of HANDPULSE W/WIRE and console not connected properly. | 2. Connect the cable again. |
| 3. The wires got damaged when connecting the HANDPULSE W/WIRE and console. | 3. Replace with a new cable. |
| 4. Hand pulse board is broken. | 4. Replace console or hand pulse board. |

On this machine the **BLUETOOTH button toggles between Bluetooth APP mode and hand pulse mode** in ready mode; check the mode before condemning the sensors.

The chest belt has its own row and its own card.
