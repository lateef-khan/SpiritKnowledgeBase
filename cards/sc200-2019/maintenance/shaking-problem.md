The major cause is a loose screw. A loose screw at a joint comes either from wear over time or from an assembly fault. A wobbly pedal is mostly caused by an assembly fault of the carriage bolt.

**For unit wobble, adjust the thumb head socket screw for the leveling foot beneath the unit.** Apply thick lubricant when replacing a leveling foot.
