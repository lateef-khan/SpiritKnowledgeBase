1. First check the **socket head cap bolt (M8 x 35mm)** and the **nut M8 x 6.3T** on the crank arm assembly to see whether they are loose.
2. **The belt** - this is where the problem occurs most often, because the belt gets loose after a certain time of operation. Time duration and body weight are factors. Use the belt disassembly procedure for belt adjustment.

The crank arm procedure gives the tightening figure: **tighten the socket head cap bolt in the range of 550 Lbs**. That procedure names no size for the bolt; the M8 x 35mm size is printed only here.
