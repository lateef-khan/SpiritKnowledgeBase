There are generally two types of problem with the flywheel: **unresponsive resistance adjustment** and **abnormal noises**.

1. If resistance adjustment on the console is unresponsive **and the gear motor is functioning normally**, check the cable to see whether the flywheel is in place properly or whether the cable is loose.
2. If there are abnormal noises during flywheel rotation, first check the flywheel, drive pulley and idler wheel assembly for foreign objects or friction. Disassemble and reassemble the flywheel; if the noise persists, **replace with a new flywheel**.

If the console shows E2, work the E2 card first: a dead tension motor and a loose steel cable produce the same complaint.
