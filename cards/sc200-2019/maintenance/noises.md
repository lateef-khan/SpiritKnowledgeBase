The product is QC inspected in the plant before delivery.

Possible locations of noise include the **bearings, the flywheel, and the slide wheel on the rear side**. Check whether the corrugated washer is installed; check for a damaged bearing, or even the pedal.

This machine's noise section is much shorter than the 2019 Sole elliptical noise sections, which list a chain cover, round disk, cross bar and swing arm the climber does not have.
