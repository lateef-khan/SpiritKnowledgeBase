1. Remove the linked assembly.
2. Use a **#13 double open-end wrench** to loosen the **nyloc nut M8 x 7T** on the hook screws to remove the belt. Use the #13 wrench to remove the **screws M8 x 20**, **nyloc nut M8 x 7T** and **flat washer 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T** to remove the idler wheel.
3. Reverse the above to refit the idler wheel assembly. After it is installed, **make sure the M8 x 9T nut is tight enough that the sound wave tester reads 190 Hz +/- 10 Hz when the belt vibrates with a finger.**

Note the source's cross reference: step 1 says "Remove the Linked Assembly. (Refer to step 9-8)", but 9-8 is the crank arm procedure; the linked assembly is 9-4.
