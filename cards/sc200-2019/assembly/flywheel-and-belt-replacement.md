1. Remove the idler wheel assembly. **Use the console to adjust the resistance to level 20 and remove the steel cable.**
2. Take the steel cable apart from the flywheel, then take the drive belt off the drive pulley.
3. Use a **15 mm wrench** to release the **3/8"-UNF26 x 9T x 2 nuts** securing the flywheel onto the main frame. Take the drive belt connected to the flywheel apart.
4. Reassemble the flywheel and belt in reverse order, using the crank arm to rotate the drive pulley. Use a **#17 wrench** to tighten the **inner flywheel nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 4T** and adjust the belt to the centre of the belt wheel. Tighten the nut and use the crank arm to adjust the belt to the appropriate tension.

Set the final tension with the idler wheel M8 x 9T nut to 190 Hz +/- 10 Hz.
