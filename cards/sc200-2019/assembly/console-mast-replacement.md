1. First remove the console mast.
2. Unplug the round cap under the hand pulse tube and pull out the hand pulse cables to take the hand pulse sensor assembly apart.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **two 3x20 tapping screws** which secure the hand pulse assembly, pull it out and disconnect the cables.
4. Reassemble in the reverse order. Be sure not to crush or damage wiring during the process.

The steps are printed in this order in the source: the round cap comes off before the two screws that hold the assembly.
