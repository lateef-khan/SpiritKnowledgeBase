1. Release four **M5x10mm Phillips head screws** securing the handle bar covers.
2. Use **12 and 13mm wrenches** to release six **5/16" x 1-3/4 hex head screws**, six **5/16" x 7T nyloc nuts** and six **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washers** which secure the swing arm.
3. Release **3 pcs of 4 x 15L sheet metal screws** on the right and left console mast covers, and remove both covers.
4. Remove **7 pcs of 4 x 15L sheet metal screws** on the left and right chain covers.
5. Remove the left and right **round tapping screw 5 x 16L**.
6. Remove **5 tapping screws 5x19mm** securing the right chain cover to the mainframe.
7. Remove **5 tapping screws 5x19mm** securing the left chain cover to the mainframe.
8. **Release the nut of the DC power plug, then pull out the DC power cables**, and chain cover (L) can be released.
9. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
