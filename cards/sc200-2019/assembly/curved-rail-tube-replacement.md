1. Following the pedal connecting arm disassembly steps, remove the pedal connecting arm.
2. Remove the **M5 x 10 m/m Phillips head screw** fixed on the stabilizer cover, then remove the stabilizer cover.
3. Use **2 pcs of 14mm wrench** to release the **3/8" x 2-1/4 hex head bolt**, **3/8" x 23 x 2.0T curved washer**, **3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washer** and **3/8" x 7T nyloc nut**, then remove the curved rail tube.
4. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
