The matrix puts the incline adjustment switch in the **same row** as the speed adjustment switch, and every reason it lists names the SPEED CABLE and the SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH. Read the same checks against the incline cable and incline switch, and see the E3 card for the incline-specific checks.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| The connector of the SPEED CABLE and CONSOLE is not connected properly. | Connect the cables again. Check the wire of the SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH. |
| The connector of the SPEED CABLE and SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH W/CABLE is not connected properly. | Replace with a new cable. |
| The button of the SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH is broken. | Replace with a new SPEED ADJUSTMENT SWITCH. |
