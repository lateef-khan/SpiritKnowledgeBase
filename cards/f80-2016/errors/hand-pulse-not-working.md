| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Hands not on the hand pulse sensors, or only one hand on a sensor. | Hold the hand pulse with **both hands**. |
| The connector of the HANDPULSE W/WIRE and the console is not connected properly. | Connect the cable again. |
| The wires were damaged when connecting the HANDPULSE W/WIRE and the console. | Replace with a new cable. |
| Hand pulse board is broken. | Replace the console or the hand pulse board. |

In RUN mode, if there is no pulse signal for 8 seconds the displayed value becomes 0.
