| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Chest belt not worn properly. | Check the chest belt has proper contact with skin and is oriented correctly. |
| Distance is too far and exceeds the range of the receiver. | Use the chest belt in front of the console **within 3 feet**. |
| Chest belt battery is weak or dead. | Replace with a new lithium battery, **type CR2032**. |

A separate row covers a chest belt held too close to the treadmill: the cause given is a weak battery, and the fix is the same CR2032 lithium battery.
