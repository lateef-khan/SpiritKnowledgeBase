**Reason**: the console is not calibrated.

**Solve**: calibrate the console.

**INCLINE ERR with "E3" in the INCLINE window** is listed separately in the same matrix.

**Reasons**: a connector has fallen off; the position sensor value of the incline motor is wrong.

**Solve**: check the connector of the cable, then calibrate the console.
