1. The tether cord (safety key) is not in position.
2. The **circuit breaker on the front grill** is tripped. Push it in until it locks.
3. The plug is disconnected. Make sure it is firmly pushed into a **220 Vac wall outlet. (On 120Vac electronic power system need 110Vac power)**
4. The breaker panel circuit breaker may be tripped.
5. Treadmill defect - contact your dealer.
