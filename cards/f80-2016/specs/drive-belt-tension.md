Every procedure in this manual that disturbs the drive belt ends with the same setting:

> "Use 14mm open end wrench to adjust belt tension using tension measuring device. Adjust to **white LBS area between 70-75LBS**. Use 14mm T-shaped socket wrench to secure and tighten 4 securing screws."

The four securing screws are the motor mounting screws. Do not tighten them until the tension is set.

This is the **drive belt** between the motor and the front roller, measured with a tension measuring device. It is not the running belt: that is tensioned at the rear roller adjustment bolts by feel, with no pound figure given.
