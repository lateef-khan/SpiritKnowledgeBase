**Section 3 summary**: "This is an AC motor, used to control variable elevation through the console within main controller."

**General Information detail**: This is a 230 volt AC motor. (115 volts AC motor on 120Vac electronic power system) Have four wires, red, black, white and green. Has one 3 pins cable of position sensor.

- AC voltage on the **Red wire (UP)** makes the incline motor raise the incline.
- AC voltage on the **Black wire (DOWN)** makes it lower the incline.
- The **White wire (COM)** is neutral.
- The **green (green/yellow) wire** is the grounding wire.

At the controller the incline power output socket is labelled **BLACK-DOWN, WHITE-NEUTRAL, RED-UP**.

Before the motor is installed, the **incline range must be adjusted to 225 mm minimum**.
