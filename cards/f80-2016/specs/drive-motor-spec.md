**Section 3 summary**: "This is a DC motor with variable speed. Control the 0-90 (or 0-180) voltages from the main controller to increase or decrease speed of the running belt."

**General Information detail**: It’s a variable speed on 0-180 volt DC motor. (0-90 volts DC motor on 120Vac electronic power system) Have three wires red, black and green. If there is DC voltage on the Red (white) wire (M+) the treadmill motor will turn clockwise. If there is DC voltage on the Black wire (M-) the treadmill motor will turn counter-clockwise. The higher the voltage the faster the motor turns. The green wire is ground.

The motor replacement procedure gives the terminal connections as **red M+, black M-**. Section 6.4 in this manual is a photograph with no readable labels.

Note the wire list and the terminal assignment in the General Information block do not use the same colour name for the M- lead. Take the colours from the replacement procedure, which says motor wire (+) red and motor wire (-) black.
