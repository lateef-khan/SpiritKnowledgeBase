Two causes are given.

1. **Uneven tension** - the tread-belt tension is not even across the belt. Adjust the belt as described in the tracking and tension procedures.
2. **The user's gait** - the user may be walking while favouring or putting more weight on the left or right foot. If that walking pattern is natural, **track the belt slightly off-centre to the side opposite from the belt movement**.
