The frame has to sit on a reasonably level surface. If the frame is not level the front and back rollers cannot run parallel, and constant belt adjustment will be needed.

Some belts drift to one side while running with nobody on them; after a few minutes of use the belt should tend to centre itself. Only adjust if the belt keeps moving toward one side **during use**.

1. A **10 mm Allen wrench** is provided for the rear roller. Make tracking adjustments **from the left side only**.
2. Set belt speed to approximately **3 to 5 kph**.
3. Turn the bolt **clockwise to move the belt to the right**. Turn only **1/4 turn**, then wait a few minutes for the belt to adjust itself.
4. Continue in 1/4 turns until the belt stabilises in the centre of the running deck.

A small adjustment makes a dramatic difference. Expect to repeat this as the belt is used; it becomes less of a concern over time and is an owner responsibility common to all treadmills.

Damage to the running belt from improper tracking or tension adjustment **is not covered under the warranty**.
