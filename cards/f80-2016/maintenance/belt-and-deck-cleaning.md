The treadmill uses a high-efficiency low-friction bed, and performance is best when the bed is kept clean.

- **Monthly**: wipe the edge of the belt and the area between the belt edge and the frame with a soft damp cloth or paper towel, reaching as far as practical directly under the belt edge. **Use water only - no cleaners or abrasives.** A mild soap and water solution with a nylon scrub brush will clean the top of the textured belt. Let the belt dry before use.
- **Monthly**: vacuum underneath the treadmill. Dirt, dust and pet hair block air inlets and build up on the running belt.
- **Once a year**: remove the black motor hood and vacuum out the dirt inside. **Unplug the power cord before this task.**

**Belt dust** happens during normal break-in, until the belt stabilises. Wiping the excess off with a damp cloth keeps the buildup down.
