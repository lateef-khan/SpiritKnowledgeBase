1. Use M5 L Allen wrench to loosen the **6 sheet metal screws**, then remove the console mast (upright) cover.
2. Use M6 L Allen wrench to remove the **4 button head socket bolts** from the console support.
3. Disconnect the console wiring and fit the new console.

The manual gives no calibration step in this section. Speed and incline readings are set by the calibration procedure in section 8.9.
