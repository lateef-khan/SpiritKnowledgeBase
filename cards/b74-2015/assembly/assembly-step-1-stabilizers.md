Hardware: **#23** 3/8" x 3-1/2" Carriage Bolt (4 pcs), **#24** 3/8" Curved Washer (4 pcs), **#30** 3/8" Cap Nut (4 pcs).

1. Secure the **front and rear stabilizers (4 & 5)** to the main frame with the four 3/8" x 3-1/2" carriage bolts (23), four 3/8" curved washers (24) and four 3/8" cap nuts (30). Using the 14/15mm wrench provided (106), tighten the cap nuts securely.

**Both stabilizers go on in this step**, even though the step is headed REAR STABILIZER. Two bolts per stabilizer.

The B94 uprights of every year fit only the rear stabilizer in step 1; their front stabilizer is part of the frame.
