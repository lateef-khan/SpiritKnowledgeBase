Hardware supplied in the box, grouped by assembly step. The number after `#` is the item number from the assembly drawing.

**Hardware step 1**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #23 | 3/8" × 3-1/2" | Carriage Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #24 | 3/8" | Curved Washer | 4 pcs |
| #30 | 3/8" | Cap Nut | 4 pcs |

**Hardware step 2**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #71 | 3/8" × 1-1/2" | Carriage Bolt | 1 pc |
| #72 | 3/8" | Flat Washer | 1 pc |

**Hardware step 3**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #36 | 4mm × 12mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 3 pcs |
| #32 | 5/16" × 3/4" | Socket Head Cap Bolt | 4 pcs |
| #35 | 5/16" | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| #33 | 5/16" | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |

The checklist notes: **screws 32, 33 and 35 are already on the mainframe.**

**Hardware step 4**

| Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| #104 | 5/16" × 5/8" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 2 pcs |
| #35 | 5/16" | Split Washer | 2 pcs |
| #33 | 5/16" | Flat Washer | 2 pcs |
| #41 | 5mm × 12mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |

The step 4 heading reads "HARDWARE STEP 4 (This screw is already on the mainframe.)" and the same note is repeated inside the step. The manual does not say which of the four items the singular "this screw" refers to.

**The B74 numbering does not carry over to the R72 half of the same manual.** In the R72 checklist `#87` is a 5/16" flat washer while in the B74 tool list it is the L Allen wrench, and `#104` is a split washer in the R72 pack but a button head socket bolt here.
