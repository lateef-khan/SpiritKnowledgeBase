**To raise the seat:** loosen the knob and pull the knob out. Now lift up from under the rear of the seat. Once the seat is at the desired setting, tighten the knob to ensure the seat post won't accidentally slide downward during use.

**To lower the seat:** loosen the knob, then hold under the rear of the seat and pull the knob out to disengage the pin from the seat post.

**This is the B74 only.** The same manual prints a separate SEAT ADJUSTMENT R72 paragraph for the recumbent, which uses a lever in front of the seat and a sliding carriage instead. The B74 has no seat carriage lever.
