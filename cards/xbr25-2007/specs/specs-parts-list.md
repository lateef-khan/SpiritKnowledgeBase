These are the callout numbers on the **XBR25 Exploded View** on PDF page 12 - a drawing printed sideways on a
portrait page and stamped **960907AM1130** in small type at its edge. The drawing is a picture with bare
numbers on it, so the table below is the only readable form of it. Every row was read from the native text
layer of the 2008 dealer manual, and the part numbers on the one page the 300 dpi OCR sweep supplemented
(p. 15) agree with that layer.

**Three columns, no quantity.** The list prints `Parts No.`, `Description` and `Part #` only - it never says
how many of an item the machine uses. A part number is a six-digit Spirit number. **`X` in the part-number
column is printed on the page**; the book does not say what it means. Sub-items are written with a tilde
(`19~1`, `27~3`) and paired parts carry a letter (`51L`, `51R`).

**The page prints a small `˜` where a diameter sign belongs.** Rows such as `˜32 (1.8T)_End Cap` and
`˜20_C Clip` are set with that glyph on the printed page (checked on a 200 dpi render), not with the `Ø`
that the later Dyaco books use. It is kept here as printed.

**139 rows are printed**, 134 numbered and 5 without a number at the foot of the list (Hardware Kit 002129,
Decal (Side) 002181, Decal (Console) 008020, Console Overlay 006198, Button Overlay 006199). The whole item
numbers that appear are 1-20, 22-37, 39-40, 43-44, 46-48, 51-56, 59-61, 63-68, 71-73, 75-80, 82-91, 93,
95-99, 101-112, 114-117, 120-127, 129, 131-134 and 140-145; any number outside that run is not printed for
this machine at all, not a missing page. Four rows carry `X` (19~11, 19~15, 27~1, 27~2) and ten numbered rows
carry no part number (12, 13, 15, 19~16, 78, 127, 142, 143, 144, 145). **Row 143 is printed as a bare number
with neither a description nor a part number.**

**Item 104 is printed with a seven-digit number, `0002051`.** Every other part number on both lists has six
digits, and the XBR55 list prints the same seat release spring as `002051`. The extra zero is on the page;
treat `002051` as the number to order and say so. **Row 18 is set as `˜ 5 4 Transportation Wheel`**, with the
digits spaced on the page; the XBR55 list prints the same wheel, 000179, as `~54`, so read it as a 54 mm wheel.
Row 17's number, 022943, is printed but sits a column to the right of the others.

**Row 25 carries two numbers.** It reads `˜38_Seat Wheel Assy. Wheel ONLY 002086` against part number
`002017`: the assembly is 002017 and the wheel alone is 002086. The XBR55 list confirms the split - its row 9
prints the adjustment plate with `Assy# 002017` and its row 25 prints the wheel alone as 002086.

**Four rows are tools, not bike parts** - 112 (12/14mm Wrench 030036), 114 (Phillips Head Screw Driver
023091), 115 (M5 Allen Wrench 000089) and 132 (14/15mm Wrench 030023). They are the tools packed with the
machine, drawn at the top left of the exploded view. The dealer manual prints no other tool list; see
`spirit-bike-specs-parts-which-service-manuals-print-a-parts-list`.
