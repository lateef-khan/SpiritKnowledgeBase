1. **Install the two 25mm Wave Washers** onto the handle bar axle (part of the console mast).
2. **Slide the left and right handle bars** onto the axle.
3. **Put the two 3/8 x 30mm Flat Washers onto the two 3/8 x 3/4 Hex Head Bolts** into the threaded holes in the ends of the axle with the **13/14mm Wrench**.
4. **Front left and rear left handle bar covers** onto the left handle bar with **four 3.5 x 12mm Self Tapping Screws** using the Phillips Head Screw Driver.
5. Repeat the covers step (the book says "repeat step 6", but no step 6 is printed; it means the covers step) for the front right and rear right handle bar covers on the right handle bar.
