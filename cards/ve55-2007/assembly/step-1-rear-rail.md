1. **Place the main frame on the floor and the Rear Rail Assembly underneath it** so the mounting holes line up and the wheels are on the tracks.
2. **Put the two 3/8 x 19mm Flat Washers on the two 3/8 x 2-1/4 Hex Head Bolts** and hand-tighten them through the top of the rear stabilizer tube (main frame) into the Rear Rail Assembly with the **13/14 m/m Wrench**.
3. **Put the four 3/8 x 23mm Curved Washers on the four 3/8 x 3-3/4 Hex Head Bolts** and hand-tighten them through the front of the rear stabilizer tube (Main Frame) into the Rear Rail Assembly. **Tighten all six bolts as much as you possibly can.**
4. **Front Stabilizer Cover** with **2pcs of M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws** using the Phillips head screw driver.
