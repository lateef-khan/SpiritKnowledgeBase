1. **Slide the console mast cover onto the console mast**, facing the correct way.
2. **Unravel the guiding wire** at the top opening of the Main Frame and feed the **computer cable** into the bottom of the console mast tube and out of the top opening.
3. **Install the console mast into the receiving bracket** on the top of the Main Frame. **One bolt is already installed** and engages the slot at the bottom of the Console Mast; it gets tightened at the end with the three other console mast bolts.
4. **Put one 3/8" Split Washer onto 1pc of 3/8" x 2-1/4" Hex Head Bolt** and **two 3/8 x 23mm Curved Washers onto two 3/8" x 3/4" Hex Head Bolts**. The 3/8" x 2-1/4" bolt goes **through the left side of the receiving bracket and the Console Mast and threads into the right side** of the receiving bracket. (The Computer Cable runs through the tube; do not damage or pinch it or the Console Assembly may be damaged.) Hand-tighten the two 3/8" x 3/4" bolts with curved washers through the front of the receiving bracket.
5. **Using the 13/14m/m Wrench, tighten the three bolts plus the pre-installed fourth firmly, as much as you possibly can.**
6. **Locate the console assembly** and tighten it with the **four M5 x 10 m/m Phillips Head Screws** using the Phillips Head Screw Driver.
7. **Three connectors sit at the top opening of the Console Mast: two hand pulse cables and one computer cable.** Connect them to the mating keyed connectors on the back of the console assembly; do not force them.
8. **Store the excess cable back into the console mast**, install the Console Assembly onto the mounting plate, and secure with the **four M5 x 10 m/m Phillips Head Screws**.
