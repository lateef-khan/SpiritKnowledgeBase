1. **Align the hole in the rod end of the left connecting arm** with the hole in the bracket of the left Handle Bar. Make sure the tie-wrap is removed from the rod end and the sleeve spacer does not fall out. **Take one 5/16" x 1-1/4" Hex Head Bolt** through the bracket and rod end; fit **one 5/16" x 20mm Flat Washer** and a **5/16" Nyloc Nut** and tighten as tight as possible with the **two 12m/m Wrenches**. Repeat for the right pedal/connecting arm.
2. **Connecting arm covers** over the rod-end and handle bar connections with **eight M5 x 15m/m Phillips Head Screws** and **eight 3.5 x 12m/m Self Tapping Screws** using the Phillips head screw driver.
3. **Two wheel covers** over the urethane slide wheels with **four M5 x 12mm Phillips Head Screws**.
4. **Four stabilizer covers** on the rail assembly with **four M5 x 15mm Phillips Head Screws.**

**Ensure all fasteners are tight after the components have been assembled.** The power connector sits on the front, left hand side of the unit.
