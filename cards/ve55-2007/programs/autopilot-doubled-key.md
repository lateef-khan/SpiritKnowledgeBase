**Only the E55 in this book has it, and the button differs from every other Auto-pilot book.** The HR2 program button doubles as the Auto-Pilot button: during any program, pressing it enters heart rate control holding your current heart rate automatically, and the elevation/incline Up/Down buttons change the target heart rate while in Auto-pilot mode.
