The condition is printed as `LCDs not bright, incomplete, or imperfect`.

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| LCD light is broken | Replace with new LCD or console |
| Power to console too low | Check power to console |

**Two fixes, not three.** Every other Spirit matrix that prints this row adds a controller to replace after the power check - the lower controller on the 2016 books, the generator controller on the XBR55 2023 and XBR95 2023 (`spirit-xb-2023-errors-lcd-not-bright-replace-generator-controller`). This book stops at the power check and names no part after the console. No voltage figure is printed either; the 2016 books' `110-120V` (`spirit-lcd-dim-or-incomplete`) is not in this row.

The matrix has three rows - this one, erratic pulse (`spirit-erratic-pulse-display`) and hand pulse (`spirit-hand-pulse-not-working`) - and no row for dead segments.

The XBU55 2023 book prints no Q&A chapter at all, so a display that shows nothing has no service-manual answer for this machine beyond the owner's-manual row that ends at a 115 VAC outlet: `spirit-residential-bike-errors-display-does-not-light-115-vac`.
