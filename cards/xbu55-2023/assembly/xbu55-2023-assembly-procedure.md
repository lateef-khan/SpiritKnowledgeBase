Item numbers in parentheses are the assembly-drawing numbers. Hardware quantities are on
[the hardware card](xbu55-2023-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

## Step one - stabilizer and seat back

1. Attach the **Rear Stabilizer (5)** to the **Main Frame (1)** with 2 **Bolts (50)**, 2
   **Flat Washers (71)** and 2 **Nuts (135)**. Tighten completely with the **Wrench
   (100)**.
2. Insert an **End Cap (41)** into each opening of the front stabilizer tube. A rubber
   mallet may be needed; **no mallet is in the carton**.

**The heading says STABILIZER & SEAT BACK and the step never touches a seat back.** This
machine has none; the heading is carried over from the recumbent books, which use the
same page layout.

## Step two - console mast

1. Slide the **Plastic Cover (38)** onto the console mast the right way up. Unravel the
   **Computer Cable (29)** and snake it through the **Console Mast Cover (38)** and the
   **Console Mast (2)** until the connector comes out of the top opening. **Be sure not
   to pinch the cable.**
2. Install the Console Mast (2) onto the Main Frame (1) with **7 Bolts (51)**, **6 Flat
   Washers (72) on the side bolts** and **1 Curved Washer (99) on the front bolt**.
   Tighten with the Wrench (100).

## Step three - handlebar

1. Run the **2 Hand Pulse Wires (26)** into the hole in the handlebar mounting plate and
   out through the hole in the console plate. **Remove the black plastic cover from the
   handlebars by cutting the plastic tie.**
2. Install the **Handle Bar (3)** onto the Console Mast (2) with 2 **Bolts (51)**, 2 Flat
   Washers (72) and 2 **Split Washers (103)**, careful not to pinch the hand pulse wires.
   Tighten with the Wrench (100). **Snap the black plastic handlebar cover over the
   joint.**

## Step four - console, tablet holder, pedals, seat and bottle holder

1. Insert the **Computer Cable (29)** and **2 Hand Pulse Cables (26)** into the
   connectors behind the **Console (34)**. Install the Console onto the **Console Mast
   (2)** with 4 **Phillips Head Screws (58)**, using the **Phillips Head Screwdriver
   (93)**.
2. **Install the Tablet Holder (160) onto the Console (34) with 3 Phillips Head Screws
   (58).**
3. Install the **Pedals (45)** onto the **Crank Arms (16L, 16R)**. The **left pedal has a
   reverse thread** and is screwed in with the opposite rotation; there is an L stamped
   in the end of the threaded post of the left pedal and an R in the right. Tighten with
   the **Wrench (92)**. **No torque figure is printed** - see
   [the pedal card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-bike-assembly-pedal-tighten-as-much-as-possible.md).
4. Install the **Seat Mount Slide (7)**, **post at the front**, onto the track on top of
   the **Seat Bracket (6)**: slide a **Flat Washer (71)** onto the **Seat Adjustment Knob
   (86)**, put it through the slots of each, and fit a **Flange Nut (8)** on the end.
   Install the **Seat (19)** onto the seat post, tightening the nut on each side beneath
   the seat.
5. Install the **Bottle Holder (116)** onto the Console Mast (2) with 2 Phillips Head
   Screws (58).

**Item 2 is the whole of what is new in this build**, and it is why the carton holds nine
M5 screws rather than six.

## Then set the machine up

This book carries a **SETTING UP YOUR BIKE** page after the build, which the older
printings do not: levelling with an M14 wrench, plugging in and powering on, and moving
the bike on its two transport wheels. See
[the levelling card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xb-2023-assembly-levelling-m14-wrench.md)
and [the transport card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xb-2023-assembly-transport-wheels.md).

## What changed from the older XBU55 build

The four steps run in the same order and use the same item numbers. Apart from the tablet
holder:

- the **pedal is written "(45)"** with no L or R, where the 2019, 2021 and ENT books write
  **"(45L, 46R)"**;
- the hand pulse cables are written **"(26)"**, as in the 2021 and ENT books, where the
  2019 book writes "(26, 28)";
- item 116 is called the **Bottle Holder** rather than the Drink Bottle Holder.

**The carton list dropped the Seat and the Handle Bar that steps three and four fit.**
See [the parts card](xbu55-2023-assembly-parts-included.md).
