tabulating it. Item numbers are the assembly-drawing numbers.

| 135 | 3/8" | Cap Nut | 2 |

## One quantity changed from the older printings, and only one

Compared row by row against the XBU55 2019, XBU55 2021 and XBU55ENT tables: **item 58
goes from six to nine.** Every other item number, size and quantity is unchanged.

The three extra screws are the ones step four uses to fit the **Tablet Holder (160)** to
the console - four hold the console, three the tablet holder, two the bottle holder. See
[the older hardware card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xbu55-assembly-hardware-kit.md).

**Item 135 is named a Cap Nut here** and a plain **NUT** in the 2019, 2021 and ENT
tables. Same part, same size, named differently.

**The "FLASH WASHER" misprint is gone.** Item 71 reads Flat Washer in this book, where
all three older printings misprint it.

## The 2023 recumbents use nine screws too, but under a different number

The XBR55 2023 and XBR95 2023 draw **seven** of item **99**, not nine of item 58 - a
different item number on a different machine, for the same job. See
[that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xbr-2023-assembly-hardware-kit.md).
