## The only XB diagram that points at the seat adjustment knob

**I** is the knob step four fits, with a flat washer and a flange nut, to clamp the seat
mount slide on its track. **No other diagram in the fourteen residential XB manuals names
it**, and no manual in the family prints a procedure for using it - see
[the absence card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xb-assembly-no-seat-or-handlebar-adjustment.md).

## What the diagram does not point at

It does not point at the **levellers**, which the same book's setting-up page tells you to
turn with an M14 wrench - see
[the levelling card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xb-2023-assembly-levelling-m14-wrench.md).
It names one **Pedal**, singular, where the carton packs two, and it does not name the
console mast, the console mast cover, the crank arms, the front stabilizer or the main
frame, all of which the older XBU55 drawing labels.

## The recumbent lists are longer and shifted

The XBR55 2023 and XBR95 2023 diagrams run **A to N** and the XBR25 2023 diagram **A to
M**, and none of the three shares this machine's letters past D. Do not read a letter off
the wrong book. See
[the A-to-N card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xbr-2023-assembly-parts-diagram-letters.md)
and [the XBR25 card](../../xbr25-2023/assembly/xbr25-2023-assembly-parts-diagram-letters.md).

## The older XBU55 drawing is unlettered

The 2019, 2021 and ENT books write thirteen names on the picture instead, with no tablet
holder, no adjustable fan, no console buttons, no seat adjustment knob and no transport
wheel. See
[that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xbu55-assembly-parts-diagram-callouts.md).
