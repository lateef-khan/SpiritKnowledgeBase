## Three lines the build needs are not on the panel

The XBU55 2019, 2021 and XBU55ENT panels list **fourteen**. This one adds a **Tablet
Holder** and drops four: the **Seat**, the **Handle Bar**, the **Front Stabilizer Cover**
and the **Rear Stabilizer Cover**.

**The Seat and the Handle Bar are both fitted by this manual's own steps.** Step three
installs the **Handle Bar (3)** onto the console mast with two bolts, and step four
installs the **Seat (19)** onto the seat post. Both parts are in the box and neither is
on the list. Only the two stabilizer covers were genuinely never fitted by any step in
this family, so dropping those two matches the build.

The same panel keeps the **Console Mast Cover**, which is the part the 2023 recumbent
panels wrongly drop.

See [the fourteen-line card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-xbu55-assembly-parts-included.md).

## What the panel does not say

No carton count, no shipping weight, no box dimensions. It does not name the **Seat Mount
Slide (7)**, the **Seat Adjustment Knob (86)** or the **Flange Nut (8)** that step four
fits, nor the **End Cap (41)** that step one taps into the front stabilizer tube.

The panel calls item 116 a **Water Bottle Holder**; step four calls the same item the
**Bottle Holder**.
