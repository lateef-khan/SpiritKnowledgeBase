**5 watts up to 650 watts.**

**The MS300 is the only machine in its family that stops at 650.** The 7.0S, 7.5S,
8.5S and 8.5S-FIT all print `5 watts up to 1000 watts` - see
`spirit-climber-specs-workload-5-to-1000-watts`. The bottom of the range is 5
watts on all five.

That matters because the MS300 and the 7.0S are otherwise near twins: the same
257.4 lb weight, the same 12 VDC input, the same Sinpro power supply, the same
`Isokinetic with 20 levels of effort` resistance row and the same readouts list.
**The workload ceiling is one of the few figures that separates them**, along
with the length. Check which machine is in front of you before quoting a
wattage.

## No level-to-watts table is printed

The manual gives the end points of the range and never says which of the twenty
levels produces which wattage. The console displays watts left and right during a
workout; the book does not map them to levels.
