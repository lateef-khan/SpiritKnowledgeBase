**5 watts up to 650 watts.**

**Three books in this family stop at 650: the MS300 and the two MED-badged 7.0S and
7.5S books of 2021.** The Spirit-badged 7.0S and 7.5S of 2025, the 8.5S and the
8.5S-FIT all print `5 watts up to 1000 watts` - see
`spirit-climber-specs-workload-5-to-1000-watts`. The bottom of the range is 5
watts on every one of them.

That matters because the MS300 and the 2025 7.0S are otherwise near twins: the same
257.4 lb weight, the same 12 VDC input, the same Sinpro power supply, the same
`Isokinetic with 20 levels of effort` resistance row and the same readouts list.
**The workload ceiling is one of the few figures that separates them**, along
with the length. Check which machine is in front of you before quoting a
wattage - `spirit-rehab-stepper-specs-two-different-machines-are-called-7-0s`.

The **MED 7.0S** prints this same 650-watt row and the MS300's whole specification
page; it is the same machine under another badge, not a third variant.

## No level-to-watts table is printed

The manual gives the end points of the range and never says which of the twenty
levels produces which wattage. The console displays watts left and right during a
workout; the book does not map them to levels.

## The features page in the same book says 750 watts

**"Work range up to 750 watts."** is printed on the features page of this book and of the two
MED-badged 7.0S and 7.5S books, forty-odd pages before the specification page. It is not this
machine's ceiling; see
`spirit-rehab-stepper-specs-the-features-page-says-750-watts-and-the-specification-page-does-not`.
