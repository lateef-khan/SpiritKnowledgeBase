| Figure | Printed |
|---|---|
| Length | **67" (172 cm)** |
| Width | **30" (77 cm)** |
| Height | **48" (122 cm)** |
| Weight | **257.4 lbs. (117 kg)** |

**That weight is the machine, not the shipping weight.** No shipping weight, no
gross weight and no carton size is printed anywhere in the 71-page manual, and
neither is a step height, a stride length or a seat travel.

**The 7.0S of 2025 weighs the same 257.4 lbs. and is a different size** - 69.7"
long against this machine's 67". Do not treat the matching weight as proof you
have the right book; check the length. See
`70s-2025-specs-dimensions-and-weight`.

## The inch and centimetre figures are rounded independently

67 inches is 170.2 cm, not 172; 30 inches is 76.2 cm, not 77. Each pair is about
a centimetre apart, which is rounding in the source rather than a contradiction -
unlike the 8.5S height, where the two units disagree by seven inches. Use the
unit the customer works in and say the manual rounds.

## The user weight limit is a different figure

The specification page also prints **Patient weight capacity 440 Lbs. (200
Kgs.)**. That is a safety limit and lives in the `safety` section, not here.
