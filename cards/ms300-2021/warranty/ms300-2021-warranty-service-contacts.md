Service under this warranty must be obtained by following these steps, **in order**:

1. Contact your selling authorized Spirit Fitness dealer. **OR**
2. Contact your local authorized Spirit service organization.
3. If there is a question as to where to obtain service, contact the service department at **1-870-935-1107**.
4. Spirit Fitness's obligation is limited to repairing or replacing, at Spirit Fitness's option, through one of its authorized service centers. **All repairs must be preauthorized.** Freight to and from a service center is the customer's responsibility, and so is shipping and handling on replacement parts shipped while the product is under warranty.
5. The owner is responsible for adequate packaging on return. Spirit Fitness is not responsible for damages in shipping; freight damage claims go to the carrier. **Do not ship any unit to the factory without a return authorization number** - units arriving without one will be refused.
6. For anything further:
   - Consumer care service email: **spiritservice@spiritfitness.com**
   - Consumer care service phone: **1-870-935-1107**

**This is the only warranty page in the Spirit climber and stepper range that prints an email address, and the only one that prints no mailing address.** Every other manual of the thirteen ends step 6 with a postal address in Jonesboro - *SPIRIT FITNESS, INC., P.O. Box 2037, Jonesboro, AR 72402-2037*. The MS300 replaces it with an email address and a repeated phone number, and refers the reader to the website for anything else.

**There is no trip charge and no mileage surcharge.** Step 4 stops at shipping and handling. The XS895, CRS800S, CS800 and CVC800 manuals go further and charge the customer a trip charge for in-home service plus an additional one beyond 100 miles from the nearest service center (`xt-2023-warranty-owner-costs`); **none of those two sentences is printed in the MS300**, and quoting them would invent a charge the source does not impose.

**The clinic still pays four things** while the machine is inside its warranty period: freight to and from a service center, shipping and handling on replacement parts, packaging for a return, and the expense of making the machine accessible for servicing. Its exclusion list also refuses service call reimbursement to the consumer (`ms300-2021-warranty-exclusions`).

**The warranty applies only in the 48 contiguous United States** and does not apply to Alaska or Hawaii - printed as a note at the foot of this page, not as a numbered exclusion. Unlike its siblings the MS300 carries no "Warranties outside of the United States may vary" item at all.

**The step order is a requirement, not a suggestion.** The dealer or the local service organization comes first; the factory number is for when it is unclear where to go.
