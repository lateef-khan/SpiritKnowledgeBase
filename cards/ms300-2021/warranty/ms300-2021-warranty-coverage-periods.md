| Warranty | Frame | EMS brake | Parts | Labor | Wear items |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| commercial | Lifetime | 5 years | 3 years | 3 years | 6 months |

> \*Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded from warranty coverage.
>
> \* Wear items are rubber hand grips, pedals, console overlay and drive belt

**One commercial row, and the row label is lower-case *commercial* in the source.** There is no residential row: the MS300 is a clinical machine and the manual states no home terms.

**Warranties are not transferable.** The paragraph above the table says so outright:

> The warranty periods set out below are subject to the performance of proper care and maintenance, as set out in this user manual, by the original purchaser of the equipment. Warranties are not transferable.

**No other manual in the Spirit climber and stepper range states this rule as a sentence.** The others reach the same end sideways, by excluding products that have been "sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party" in their exclusion list. The MS300 does both - it prints that exclusion too - so a clinic asking whether cover follows a resold machine gets a plain no here, not an inference.

**Warranty is tied to the original purchaser and to proper care.** The same sentence makes the periods conditional on the maintenance the manual specifies, performed by the original purchaser. No other Spirit climber manual makes the table conditional in its own paragraph.

**The brake is an *EMS brake*** - the electromagnetic resistance unit - and it is the only warranty table in this range to name one. Five years.

**Wear items are named, and there are only four**: rubber hand grips, pedals, console overlay and drive belt. That is a much shorter list than the CSC880's eleven, and it does not include paint, decals, the USB port or the headphone jack (`csc880-2025-warranty-coverage-periods`). Six months.

**Spirit Fitness decides the remedy.** The paragraph gives Spirit Fitness "sole and absolute discretion" over both whether a part is new or remanufactured and how much labor is "deemed necessary". No other manual in this range uses that phrase.

**This manual prints no warranty effective date** - the page is headed simply *Product warranty*, with no *Effective* line, no copyright year and no revision stamp anywhere in the document: `spirit-climber-warranty-effective-dates`.
