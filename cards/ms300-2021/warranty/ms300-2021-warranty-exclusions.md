## What is different about it, and why it must not be answered from a sibling card

**The items are bullets, not numbers.** Every other Spirit climber and stepper manual numbers its exclusions one to nine or one to ten, and support staff quote them by number. There is no "exclusion 8" on an MS300 - do not cite one.

**It is the only list in the range that excludes a store display model**, and the only one that excludes use "in any way other than described within products' operation manual, either intentionally or by error" - a clause broad enough to reach almost any misuse.

**It puts clinical responsibility on the facility or clinician.** The biophysical-agents paragraph is a medical-device clause with no counterpart on any other Spirit machine. It disclaims loss of life and personal injury, including re-injury, and assigns the responsibility to whoever selected and prescribed the treatment.

**It does not fix an indemnity; it says one will be "fairly negotiated".** No figure, no cap and no procedure. Say that plainly rather than reading a limit into it.

**Three items every sibling manual carries are missing here.** There is **no prisons item** in the list - the exclusion appears only as the footnote under the warranty table (`spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`); there is **no environment item** capping use or barring a dues-paying gym; and there is **no "Warranties outside of the United States may vary"** item. The 48-contiguous-states note is printed instead at the end of the Service page (`ms300-2021-warranty-service-contacts`).

**Its service-call item stops one clause short of every other manual's.** It ends at "or units not requiring component replacement" and never adds "or units not in ordinary household or light commercial use". That closing clause is the only place the other manuals restrict *where* the machine may be used, so on an MS300 there is no such restriction stated anywhere outside the prisons footnote.

**Both pages were read from a render.** The native extraction of printed page 67 loses whole lines and interleaves stray digits from a hidden text layer. Every item above was confirmed against a 300 dpi render read with `tesseract --psm 4`.
