**200 kg. The MS300 never prints a `User Weight Limit` line, and states the allowance three times as
a patient weight instead.**

| Where | Printed as |
|---|---|
| Intended patient population, page 10 | `Maximal patient's weight is 200kg.` |
| Contraindications, page 9 | `Do not use for patients weighing greater than 200kgs (bariatric).` |
| Specifications table, page 61 | `440 Lbs. (200 Kgs.)` |

**Only the specification table gives a pound figure**, and it is 440 lbs. The safety chapter is
metric throughout.

**This is the one Spirit stepper whose safety list has no weight limit on it.** The 7.0S, 7.5S, 8.5S
and 8.5S-Fit all close their lists with `User Weight Limit: 440 lbs.`
(`spirit-climber-safety-user-weight-limit-440-lbs`); the CRS800S and CS800 with 450 lb; the CSC900
with 400 lb. If a caller reads the MS300 safety page and finds nothing, that is correct - the figure
is on page 9, page 10 and page 61.

**It is a clinical exclusion, not just a capacity.** The same sentence bars patients with severe
osteoporosis, non-union fractures, debilitating dizziness or poor safety awareness/cognition, and
patients with acute conditions such as pulmonary embolus, thrombus, acute MI, acute fractures, or BP
over 180/110 Hg. A person under 200 kg may still be outside the intended population.

**200 kg is 441 lb**, so the metric and imperial statements are the same allowance rounded
differently. The manual does not say what happens above it.
