**The MS300 is the only Spirit-badged climber manual with a packaging-symbol legend.** It runs over
two pages at the back of the book. The two MED-badged rehabilitation stepper books print the same
legend with two of its rows corrected and a CE row added -
`spirit-med-stepper-safety-packaging-symbols-and-the-ce-mark`.

| Symbol | Legend |
|---|---|
| Two figures lifting | Indicates that the package is heavy and two or more people are required to lift. **Team lift with care** |
| Broken glass | Package contains fragile electronic equipment. Care should be used when handling. The product inside the packaging could be easily damaged if dropped or handled without care and attention. The contents are fragile! |
| Crossed fork | Do not use forklift truck here. |
| Crossed spade | Do not use Spade truck here. |
| Crossed razorback | Do not use Razorback truck here. |
| Hand truck | Hand truck only. |
| Mobius loop | Indicates that an object is capable of being recycled - not that the object has been recycled or will be accepted in all recycling collection systems. |
| Arrows up | Top. This side up. |
| Sine wave | **AC power 220-240 Volt, 10Amps, 50Hz** |
| Open book with i | **read this entire manual carefully before operating your new treadmill** |
| Standing figure | Type B applied parts. This is used for parts applied to the patient that require only ordinary electrical protection, e.g. headphones. |
| Reading figure | Follow Instructions for use |

## Two of these legends are wrong for this machine

**The AC power symbol contradicts the safety page.** Page 5 asks for `100 to 240-volt AC, 50/60 Hz,
15-amp`; this page says `220-240 Volt, 10Amps, 50Hz`. That is a narrower voltage band, two thirds of
the amperage and no 60 Hz at all. The manual never reconciles them. **Install to the safety page
figure** (`spirit-climber-safety-outlet-100-to-240-volt-15-amp`) and treat the symbol legend as
carton marking for a European shipment; but say that both are printed, because a reader who finds
this page will quote it.

**The MS300 is not a treadmill.** `read this entire manual carefully before operating your new
treadmill` is a leftover from the template the book was assembled from, as are the three safety
bullets that call the machine a bike (`ms300-2021-safety-instructions-list`) and the stray `7.0 S` on
the cover. Nothing in the book describes a running belt.

**The MED book this page came from prints both rows correctly** - `100-240 Volt, 15Amps, 50Hz` and
`before operating your new 7.0S`. That book is the same machine under another badge, which is what
makes the two rows here template damage rather than a specification
(`spirit-rehab-stepper-specs-two-different-machines-are-called-7-0s`).

**`Type B applied parts` is a real medical classification, not a defect.** It is the IEC 60601-1
class for parts that touch the patient and need only ordinary electrical protection. It is consistent
with the rest of the MS300's medical framing. The two MED books declare the same class; no other
Spirit climber manual declares one at all.
