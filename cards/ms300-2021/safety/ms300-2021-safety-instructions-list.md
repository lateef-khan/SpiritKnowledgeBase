**Bullets, not numbers, and the same template as the 7.0S and 7.5S lists.** The MS300 safety chapter
matches the 7.0S one at 83.9% on an order-free word count of the printed pages; the difference is
mostly the product name.

> **Attention** - Read all instructions in this manual before using this device.

> **Danger** - To reduce the risk of electric shock disconnect your from the electrical outlet prior
> to cleaning and/or service work.

**The Danger line is printed with the product name missing** - `disconnect your from the electrical
outlet`. The 7.0S book prints `disconnect your 7.0S from`. Read it as the MS300; nothing else in the
manual offers a different reading.

**Three bullets call the machine a bike.** An MS300 is a recumbent stepper with linked arms; it has
no bicycle in it. The word survives from the template the book was made from, and the electrical page
repeats it - `Never expose this bike to rain or moisture`. Read *bike* as the MS300 throughout
(`ms300-2021-safety-packaging-symbols-and-template-defects`).

**There is no user weight limit on this list.** Every other Spirit stepper ends its list with one.
The MS300 states the limit only as a patient weight, elsewhere in the book
(`ms300-2021-safety-patient-weight-limit-200-kg`).

**Item on aftermarket parts names Spirit Fitness**, where the 7.0S, 7.5S, 8.5S and 8.5S-Fit lists
name `Spirit by Dyaco`. Same instruction, different manufacturer name.

**What this list does not contain.** No carpet rule, no proper-shoes rule, no aerosol or oxygen rule,
no extension-cord gauge and no children-under-13 age - the same omissions as the 7.0S and 7.5S lists.
