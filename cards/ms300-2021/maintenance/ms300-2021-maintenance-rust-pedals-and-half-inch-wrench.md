**The wrench on this machine is a ½", not the 14 mm every other Spirit stepper prints.** A ½"
spanner is 12.7 mm and will not do the same job. This is also the only Spirit stepper page that
gives the wipe-down a reason — rust — and the only one that puts an interval on the pedal check.

- **Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each use, to prevent rust.**
- **Check the pedals to make sure they are tight (monthly).**
- If a squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling develops, the main cause is most likely one of two
  reasons:
  - **The hardware was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.** All bolts installed during
    assembly need to be tightened as much as possible. It may be necessary to use a larger wrench
    than the one provided if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently. **90% of calls to the
    service department for noise issues can be traced to loose hardware.**
  - **The crank arm nut and/or the pedals need to be retightened.**
- If squeaks or other noises persist, check that the unit is properly levelled. There are **2
  levelling pads on the bottom of the rear stabiliser**; use a **½" wrench** (or an adjustable
  wrench) to adjust the levellers.

## Two differences from the rest of the range, and one carry-over

**"After each use", not "after each workout".** The MS300 is a clinical machine used by a
succession of patients, and this is the only Spirit maintenance page phrased for that.

**"To prevent rust" is unique to this book.** No other Spirit stepper or climber manual gives a
reason for the wipe-down.

**The crank arm nut is a carry-over.** An MS300 has no crank arm; the phrase occurs nowhere else in
its 71 pages. Send the customer to the **pedals**, which the monthly check already names.

## The carton does not contain a ½" wrench

The parts list supplies a **12/14 mm** wrench (item 209), a **13/14 mm** wrench (item 210) and a
**10 mm** wrench (item 211). None of them is a ½". The manual asks for a tool it does not ship, and
offers "or adjustable wrench" as the alternative. Those two rows are also the only places "14mm"
appears in the book, so do not read a 14 mm levelling figure out of them.

## What this page does not contain

- **No lubricant of any kind.** "Lubricat" returns no hits in the whole manual.
- **No schedule table.** The monthly pedal check is the only interval printed anywhere in the book;
  there is no daily/weekly/quarterly grid.
- **No cleaning procedure for between patients** beyond the damp cloth here and the standard 75%
  sanitizing bullets — `ms300-2021-maintenance-hygienic-requirements-and-cleaning`.
