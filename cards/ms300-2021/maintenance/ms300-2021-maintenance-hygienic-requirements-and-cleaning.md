**The manual states that there is no special hygiene rule for this machine, and it prints no
disinfection procedure at all.** That is the answer, and it is worth giving plainly rather than
reaching for another book's procedure.

Two places in the manual bear on it, and they are 48 pages apart.

## The intended-use chapter (printed p. 10)

Under *Intended conditions of use → Environment including hygienic requirements*:

> **Hygienic requirements: there is no particular restriction on hygienic requirements.**

The same chapter says the machine is intended for **hospital use, clinic use, home use and research
in academic institutions**, and that frequency of use is *dependent on the therapist's plan*. So the
manufacturer contemplates clinical use and still sets no hygiene restriction.

## The care chapter (printed pp. 57-58)

The only cleaning the book asks for is:

- **Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each use, to prevent rust**
  (`ms300-2021-maintenance-rust-pedals-and-half-inch-wrench`).
- The standard Spirit sanitizing bullets — a **75% isopropyl alcohol** solution on unupholstered
  hard plastics, a conditioner afterwards on upholstered or soft-plastic surfaces
  (`ct900-sanitizing-equipment`).

## The word "disinfect" does not occur in this manual

Nor do "infection", "between patients" or "each patient". Searched across all 71 pages.

**Do not answer an MS300 question from the 8.5S or 8.0T procedure.** Those two books print a
seven-step clinical routine — 20 ml of 70% or greater isopropyl alcohol, a 15-minute application, a
3-to-5-minute dwell and a rinse (`80t-2026-maintenance-disinfection-procedure`) — and a nine-item
warning list that **forbids alcohol-containing cleaners** outright
(`80t-2026-maintenance-cleaning-cautions`). Neither is printed for the MS300, and the two of them
contradict each other in their own book. Importing either would put a figure and a prohibition on
this machine that its manufacturer never stated for it.

**What to tell a clinic.** Quote the two lines above as what the manual requires, and say that any
infection-control protocol beyond them is the facility's own decision under its own policy, not
something this manual specifies. If the facility needs a manufacturer-approved disinfectant, that
is a question for Spirit Fitness service, not for this book.

## The two MED-badged books go further: they print no sanitizing bullets at all

The MED 7.0S and 7.5S print the same **`there is no particular restriction on hygienic
requirements`** line and the same damp-cloth sentence, and then stop. A loose search of both books
for `sanitiz`, `isopropyl` and `disinfect` returns **zero hits of each**. So on those two machines
the damp cloth is the whole of the printed cleaning, without even the 75% isopropyl bullets this
book carries. Say so plainly rather than importing the bullets from here.
