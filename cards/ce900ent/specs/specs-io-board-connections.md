Nine connections are called out on the I/O board behind the console:

- **Internet CONNECT** (the RJ45)
- **GROUND** (the tab silkscreened *TO GND*)
- **Console Power CONNECT**
- **HDMI CONTACT**
- **SYSTEM CABLE CONNECT (8 PINS)**
- **Right Hand Pulse Wire**
- **Left Hand Pulse Wire**
- **C-safe CONNECT**
- **TV CONNECT** (the coaxial socket)

The board label in the photograph reads **P/N: DYA-W10A-IMX6-COM-R...**, the same family as the
driver board. The 8-pin system cable is the **8 PIN COMPUTER CABLE** the circuit diagram runs from
the console to the driver board.

The CU900ENT and CR900ENT bikes print the same page with the same nine call-outs
(`cu900ent-io-board-connections`). The CE1000ENT's I/O board is a different panel with pin counts
on every socket and a 20-pin keyboard connector in place of the two hand-pulse wires
(`ce1000ent-2023-specs-io-board-bottom-connections`).
