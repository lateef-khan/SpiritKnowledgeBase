- **Console** - "interface that controls all functions of the Elliptical"; contains the key
  controls and a **TFT Display**.
- **Main controller** - "the circuit board consist of the DC power supply for console"; includes
  the power supply and the driver control circuit.
- **EMS flywheel** - "it can change to increase or decrease resistance level of brake".

**The Display Windows page names a 10.1" TFT Display** - the one screen size in the book. The
chapter-2 photographs caption **Cooling FAN** and **DISPLAY** as the upper controllers, and **HYBRID
GENERATOR**, **SPEED SENSOR** and **Driver board** as the lower ones.

The section prints **no voltages**; the supply figures are on the circuit diagram, where the
machine runs from an external adapter delivering DC 24 V. The CU900ENT and CR900ENT bikes print
the same three paragraphs - their console entry even says "Elliptical", copied from this book
(`cu900ent-electrical-part-descriptions`). The generator-powered CE900-2021 names a *generator
brake* and an LED display instead (`ce900-2021-specs-electrical-configuration-generator-brake`).
