**What is not covered? EXCLUSIONS** - *This warranty does not cover the following:*

1. CONSEQUENTIAL, COLLATERAL, OR INCIDENTAL DAMAGES SUCH AS PROPERTY DAMAGE AND INCIDENTAL EXPENSES RESULTING FROM ANY BREACH OF THIS WRITTEN OR ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY. *Note: Some states do not allow the exclusion or limitation of incidental or consequential damages, so this limitation or exclusion may not apply to you.*
2. Service call reimbursement to the consumer. Service call reimbursement to the dealer that does not involve malfunction or defects in workmanship or material, for units that are beyond the warranty period, for units that are beyond the service call reimbursement period, for Folding Bike not requiring component replacement, or Folding Bike **not in ordinary household or light commercial use**.
3. Damages caused by services performed by persons other than authorized XTERRA Fitness service companies; use of parts other than original XTERRA Fitness parts; or external causes such as **corrosion, discoloration of paint or plastic**, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, or acts of God.
4. Products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered.
5. Products that have been: sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party.
6. XTERRA Fitness reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record exists for the product.
7. THIS WARRANTY IS EXPRESSLY IN LIEU OF ALL OTHER WARRANTIES EXPRESSED OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING THE WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND/OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.
8. Product use in any environment other than a **residential setting**.
9. Warranties outside of the United States may vary. Please contact your local dealer for details.

**This is the Online-Support list (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-with-a-non-dues-facility-allowance`) with one difference: item 8 stops at *residential setting*.** The SB240, SB600 and the eight Online-Support treadmill books go on *or non-dues paying facility with 5 hours use or less per day*; the FB180 does not, so no facility of any kind is admitted - which agrees with its front-page 90-day statement (*shall not apply to exercise units used for commercial or other income producing purposes*, `xterra-fb-warranty-front-page-90-day-statement-not-transferable-and-no-commercial-use`) and its ATTENTION box (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-use-environment-residential-only-any-other-use-voids-it`). Item 2's *household or light commercial use* is the same Dyaco sentence the other Online-Support books print and the only place the FB180 admits any commercial use; this card follows item 8 and the front page, which are the two sentences written for this bike, and treats item 2 as boilerplate carried over.

Like the other Online-Support books it has no display-model item and no registration-on-file item - item 6 only reserves the right to ask for proof of purchase (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-qr-code-and-support-tab-no-deadline`) - and item 9 sits beside the 48-states sentence at the foot of the page (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-48-contiguous-states-not-alaska-or-hawaii`). Item 5 is the transfer rule (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-not-transferable-sold-bartered-or-given-away`). The five consumer duties above the list are `xterra-treadmill-warranty-consumer-responsibilities-five-items-without-registration`; the periods are `xterra-bike-warranty-periods-frame-1-year-parts-90-days-labor-none`. **The page after this one is the back cover**: the FB180 prints no SERVICE steps (`fb180-2025-warranty-service-steps-not-printed-use-the-cover-contacts`).

**The ERG750W water rower prints the same nine items**, with *Water Rowing Machine* in item 2, on its second warranty page (PDF p. 25, printed 24; effective November 12, 2025): item 8 stops at *residential setting* and no facility is admitted. Unlike the FB180 it has no front-page warranty statement and no ATTENTION box, so item 8 and the lead sentence *ordinary household use* carry the rule alone; item 2's *light commercial use* is the same boilerplate and this card treats it the same way. Like the FB180 its warranty stops after this page - no SERVICE steps (`fb180-2025-warranty-service-steps-not-printed-use-the-cover-contacts`); its periods are `erg750w-2025-warranty-periods-frame-5-years-parts-2-years-labor-none`, and its tank page adds two things that are *exempt from the warranty* (`xterra-ergw-warranty-tank-chlorine-bleach-or-filling-past-the-mark-voids-it`). The ERG180 and ERG800W, the other Online-Support rowers, print the non-dues version of item 8 (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-with-a-non-dues-facility-allowance`).
