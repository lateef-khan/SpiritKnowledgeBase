**The FB180 warranty runs two pages and stops.** Page 18 (printed 17) prints the table and the five consumer duties; page 19 (printed 18) the nine exclusions and the 48-states sentence; page 20 is the back cover. The third warranty page that the SB240, SB600 and every Online-Support treadmill book print - *Responsibilities of the Owner / SERVICE*, with the twelve- and eighteen-month sentence, the six numbered steps, the return-authorization rule, the trip charge and the P.O. Box 2037 address (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-service-steps-dealer-service-organization-then-the-service-department`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-owner-pays-freight-parts-shipping-and-trip-charges`) - is not in this book. Checked in the text layer, in a render of pages 18-20, and with loose searches for *service*, *authorization* and *P.O.*: the only *service* on the warranty pages is exclusion 2's *service call reimbursement* and exclusion 3's *service companies*.

**So the book gives the FB180 owner no procedure and no service-department number.** What it does give:

| Contact | Where |
|---|---|
| **1-800-258-8511** and **questions@xterrafitness.com** - *If you require assistance or are experiencing issues with your XTERRA Machine, please contact customer care* | Online Support panel, front cover |
| **(870) 335-5500** - *If you have questions, or if parts are missing or damaged, or you require customer service* | Product Registration, PDF p. 4 (printed 3) |
| **(870) 333-5500** beside the serial-number sticker | Product Labels, PDF p. 5 (printed 4) |
| **800-258-8511**, fax 870-935-7611, 3000 Nestle Road, Jonesboro, AR 72401, www.xterrafitness.com | back cover |

The two (870) numbers differ by one digit on facing pages; **333-5500 is the number** - it is what the label page, the FB150/160/350/360 books and the SB150/SB250/EU/FS150 books print, and 335-5500 appears nowhere else in the Xterra range (`xterra-warranty-support-contacts-which-phone-number-each-bike-elliptical-and-climber-book-prints`). The customer-care line and email are the same ones the other Online-Support books print (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-support-contacts-three-phone-numbers-email-and-addresses`).

**What to tell an FB180 owner.** Call customer care; expect the same preauthorization, freight and return-authorization rules the other Xterra books print, because exclusion 2 and the consumer duties on these pages assume them - but do not quote a step list or a trip charge from this book, because it has none. The clock is the opening sentence's *date of retail sale ... or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date* (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`); no twelve-month sentence is printed. The periods are `xterra-bike-warranty-periods-frame-1-year-parts-90-days-labor-none`, the exclusions `fb180-2025-warranty-exclusions-nine-items-residential-only-with-proof-of-purchase-and-outside-us-items`.
