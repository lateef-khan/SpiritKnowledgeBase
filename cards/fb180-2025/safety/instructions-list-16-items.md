**The 2025 FB180 book is the first folding-bike book in the new XTERRA layout**, and it prints the
older eight-item appliance list and the Fitness Equipment bullets
(`xterra-safety-instructions-list-8-item-appliance-list`) as one numbered list of sixteen, in two
columns, under `! WARNING - To reduce the risk of injury to persons`. Two things are new: item 4, and
the weight limit moving into the numbered list.

> Read all instructions before using this appliance.

| # | Instruction |
|---|---|
| 1 | Do not operate under blanket or pillow. Excessive heating can occur and cause fire, electric shock, or injury to persons. |
| 2 | This exercise equipment is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge. |
| 3 | Use this appliance only for its intended use as described in this manual. Do not use attachments not recommended by the manufacturer. |
| 4 | **Never operate this appliance if it is not working properly, if it has been dropped or damaged, or dropped into water. Return the appliance to a service center for examination and repair.** |
| 5 | Never operate the appliance with the air openings blocked. Keep the air openings free of lint, hair, and the like. |
| 6 | Never drop or insert any object into any opening. |
| 7 | Do not use outdoors. |
| 8 | Do not operate where aerosol (spray) products are being use or where oxygen is being administered. |
| 9 | The appliance is intended for household use. |
| 10 | Do not operate equipment on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet. Damage to both carpet and equipment may result. |
| 11 | Before beginning this or any exercise program, consult a physician. This is especially important for persons over the age of 35 or persons with pre-existing health conditions. |
| 12 | Keep hands away from all moving parts. |
| 13 | The pulse sensors are not medical devices. Various factors, including the user's movement, may affect the accuracy of heart rate readings. The pulse sensors are intended only as exercise aids in determining heart rate trends in general. |
| 14 | Do not attempt to use your equipment for any purpose other than for the purpose it is intended. |
| 15 | Wear proper shoes. High heels, dress shoes, sandals or bare feet are not suitable for use on your equipment. Quality athletic shoes are recommended to avoid leg fatigue. |
| 16 | **User Weight Limit: 250 lbs.** (`xterra-bike-safety-user-weight-limit-250-lbs`) |

A boxed note follows: `Please ensure that you review and adhere to the user weight restrictions and
power requirements of your new machine. Failure to do so may result in serious injury or damage to
your machine.` The *power requirements* are a template phrase - the FB180 has no adapter and no cord;
its console runs on two AAA batteries (`xterra-safety-battery-console-no-power-adapter`).

Item 4 is the *damaged cord or plug* item of the plug-in list with the cord clause cut out. The
Proposition 65 warning is not on this page; the new layout shows it as a sticker on the Product
Labels page (`xterra-bike-safety-product-labels-page`). Nothing on the page, or anywhere in the book,
tells the rider to check the frame lock pin before riding
(`xterra-fb-safety-no-folding-or-lock-before-riding-rule-printed`).

The same twenty-one-item list on the new-layout recumbents adds the cord, plug and outlet items back
(`xterra-sb-safety-instructions-list-21-items`).
