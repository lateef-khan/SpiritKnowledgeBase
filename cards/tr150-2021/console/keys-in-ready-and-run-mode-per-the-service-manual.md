From the Dyaco GT65-NT014 service manual:

**READY mode**
- SAFETY KEY: fit it to power on the computer; pulling it away shuts the computer down and shows E0.
- STOP: hold 3 seconds and the display resets.
- START: a **3-second final countdown** on the display, then the machine runs — at minimum speed in Manual, at the program's preset value in a Program. In calibration it is the confirmation key.
- FAST / SLOW: non-functional unless a setting is being entered. RAPID and PROGRAM keys: non-functional. (The same page then says Program ▲/▼ chooses among 12 programs, confirmed with ENTER, started with START, and that "when into calibration, press the program key to enter to calibration mode".)

**RUN mode**
- SAFETY KEY: pulling it away shuts the computer down and shows E0.
- STOP stops the treadmill; START is non-functional.
- ENTER: takes turns showing calories and distance.
- ▲ (FAST) / ▼ (SLOW): 0.1 kph (0.1 mph) per press; held continuously, speed runs to MAX or MIN quickly.
- RAPID: "9 preset buttons for rapid speed: 0 to 9" — press a speed key, then select one or two digits, or the treadmill adjusts to that value automatically. The owner's manual and the console face show keys 1 through 9.
- PROGRAM: non-functional.

The owner's manual describes the same keys more briefly on [the quick-start card](../../shared/console/xterra-treadmill-console-quick-start-with-speed-keys-1-to-9.md).
