The owner's manual prints no calibration procedure; this is from the Dyaco GT65-NT014 service manual.

1. Remove the Safety Key.
2. Press and hold down **PROGRAM** and at the same time replace the Safety Key; "**Eng**" appears in the speed window.
3. Press **START**, adjust the wheel size diameter to **42**, then press START.
4. Set the minimum speed to **1 km** (0.5 miles for imperial units) and press START.
5. Set the maximum speed to **16 km** (10 miles for imperial units).
6. Press Start to begin calibration. The process is automatic; the belt starts without warning, so **do not stand on the belt**.

The service manual's own speed function says the console's work range is 1.0-16.0 km or 0.5-12 miles ([ranges card](display-modes-and-window-ranges-per-the-service-manual.md)), which disagrees with the 10-mile maximum set here and the "0-10 MPH" the TR200 badge prints for the sister machine; the calibration figure is the one a technician enters. The service manual's contents list an "8.11 Maintenance Menu", but the page under that number is the troubleshooting matrix — no maintenance menu is printed anywhere in the book. The TR260 service manual prints the same procedure with a maximum incline step added ([TR260 card](../../tr260-2023/console/calibration-program-key-eng-wheel-42-max-16.md)).
