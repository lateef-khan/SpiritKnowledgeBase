**This is the 250 lb figure, the lowest in the Xterra treadmill range, and it is printed on the TR150
only.**

> Product Weight Limit: 250 lbs.

It is one bullet in the FITNESS EQUIPMENT SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list, between the 3 1/2 feet clearance
bullet and `Keep hands away from all moving parts`. The manual calls it a *product* weight limit; every
later Xterra book says *User Weight Limit*. The book has no specification table, so this bullet is
the whole answer.

**The TR200, printed the same day with the same page layout, drops the line and prints no limit at
all** (`xterra-treadmill-safety-no-user-weight-limit-printed`). Do not carry 250 lbs across to it.

| Figure | Printed on | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 250 lbs | TR150 | `Product Weight Limit: 250 lbs.`, a bullet in the Fitness Equipment Safety Instructions |
| 265 lbs | TRX1400, WS200, WS300 | item 21 of the SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| 265 lb | TR75H | item 22 of the SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| 300 lbs | TR65, TRX2500 | item 21 (TR65) or the last Fitness Equipment bullet (TRX2500) |
| 300 lbs | TR95H | item 22 of the SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS list |
| 350 lbs | TR75, TR85, TRX3500, TRX4500, TRX5500 | item 21 (TR75, TR85) or the last Fitness Equipment bullet (TRX books) |
| none | TR200, TR300, TR6.6, TRX1000, TR260, TR6.4 | no weight limit anywhere in the book |
