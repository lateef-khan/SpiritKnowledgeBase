The drawing, headed *Treadmill Configuration*, has nine boxes:

**Upper half.** **KEY BOARD** and **HR HANDLEBAR** feed the **DISPLAY BOARD**; the **SAFETY KEY** feeds it from the
right. A double arrow joins the display board to the driver board.

**Lower half.** **POWER** goes through the **POWER SWITCH** and a **CURRENT BRAKER** into the **DRIVER BOARD**, whose
only output is the **MOTOR**.

That is the whole drawing. There is no incline motor, no VR set, no RPM sensor box, no cooling fan, no amplifier or
speakers and no wireless receiver - the GT65 is the simplest configuration in the Xterra service manuals. **The text
layer of the page carries the word "BULETOOTH" that is not printed on the rendered page**; read from the render, the
drawing has no Bluetooth box.

The Spirit DC-drive treadmills draw the same display-board-over-driver-board layout with an incline motor and
amplifier added (`spirit-dc-treadmill-specs-unit-block-diagram`).
