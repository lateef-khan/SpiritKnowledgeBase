**No.** The GT65-NT014 service manual (50 pages, a 2019 Dyaco file) runs Outlines, Electronic Parts,
Electrical Configuration, Operation, Block Diagrams, Wiring and PCB, Safety, and chapter 8 Error
Messages / Troubleshooting - ending at 8.10 Calibration Procedure and 8.11 the Condition / Reason /
Solve matrix on the last two pages. There is no chapter 9, and the words "replacement" and
"disassembl-" occur only inside troubleshooting advice ("Replace motor", "Replace controller"),
never as a procedure.

Proved three ways: the table of contents (PDF p. 2), a loose grep of the whole text layer and its OCR
supplements for replace / assembl / unscrew / bolts, and the last pages themselves. Every other Dyaco
Xterra service manual in the set (TR260, TRX1400, TRX2500, TRX3500/4500, TRX5500) has such a chapter;
the closest platform with one is the **TR260 GT75A** book, whose procedures are on the linked cards,
but they are evidence for the TR260 only.
