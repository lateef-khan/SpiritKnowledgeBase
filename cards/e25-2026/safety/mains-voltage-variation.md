**If voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more, the performance of the elliptical may be affected. Such conditions are not covered under the warranty.**

If you suspect the voltage is low, contact your local power company or a licensed electrician for proper testing.

The warranty exclusions list "inadequate power supply" among the external causes that are not covered.
