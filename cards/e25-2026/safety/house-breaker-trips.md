Some circuit breakers used in homes are not rated for the high inrush currents that can occur when the elliptical is first turned on, or even during use.

If the elliptical trips the house circuit breaker - even though it is the proper current rating - **but the circuit breaker on the elliptical itself does not trip**, the home breaker must be replaced with a **high inrush type**.

**This is not a warranty defect.** The manual states it is a condition the manufacturer has no ability to control. The part is available through most electrical supply stores. Examples given: **Grainger part # 1D237**, or online at www.squared.com part **# QO120HM**.
