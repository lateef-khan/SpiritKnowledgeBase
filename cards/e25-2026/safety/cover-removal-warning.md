**NEVER remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power.**

The safety page states the same rule for routine work: to reduce the risk of electric shock, disconnect the elliptical from the electrical outlet prior to cleaning and/or service work.

The 2011-2015 natives, both WE books and every stencil book except the E20 pair print both sentences. The 2007/2008 books print the cover sentence but no DANGER cleaning paragraph, and the E20 pair words its cleaning rule differently, so those do not join this card.
