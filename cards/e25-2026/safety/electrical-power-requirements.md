- Install the elliptical on a flat level surface with access to a **120-volt, 15-amp grounded outlet**, with only the elliptical plugged into that circuit.
- Do not use an extension cord unless it is **16 AWG or better with only one outlet on the end**. Do not disable the grounded plug with an adapter or modify the cord outlet.
- **Never use a GFCI wall outlet with this elliptical.** As with any appliance with a large motor the GFCI will trip often. Route the power cord away from any moving part, including the elevation mechanism and transport wheels.
- Disconnect the elliptical from the outlet before cleaning or service work.
- The product must be grounded. The grounding section states the product "is for use on a nominal **120-volt** circuit". Improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric shock.

**Two numbers that look like a conflict but are not.** The outlet is specified as 120 volt nominal, while the machine itself is described elsewhere as connected directly to **115 VAC** with a 115 volt AC incline motor, and the troubleshooting matrix asks you to "Check AC power is 110-120V". All three describe ordinary North American mains.
