Install the elliptical on a **flat level surface**.

**Do not operate the elliptical on deeply padded, plush or shag carpet.** Damage to both the carpet and the elliptical may result.

The manual gives no leveling procedure, but the maintenance section tells you to check the unit is properly leveled before calling the service department about a noise. Adjustment feet are fitted to the front, middle and rear stabilizers during assembly.
