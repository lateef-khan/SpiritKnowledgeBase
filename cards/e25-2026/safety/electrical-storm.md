**NEVER use the elliptical during an electrical storm.** Surges may occur in the household power supply that could damage elliptical components. **Unplug the elliptical during an electrical storm as a precaution.**

Damage from power surges, lightning storms, water damage or fire damage is listed among the warranty exclusions.
