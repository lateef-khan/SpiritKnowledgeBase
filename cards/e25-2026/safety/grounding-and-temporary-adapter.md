This product must be grounded. Grounding provides a path of least resistance for electric current if the elliptical malfunctions, reducing the risk of electric shock. The cord has an equipment-grounding plug that must be plugged into an appropriate outlet installed and grounded in accordance with all local codes and ordinances.

**DANGER** - improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric shock. Check with a qualified electrician or serviceman if you are in doubt whether the product is properly grounded. **Do not modify the plug.** If it will not fit the outlet, have a proper outlet installed by a qualified electrician.

**Temporary adapter.** A temporary adapter may be used to connect the plug to a 2-pole receptacle if a properly grounded outlet is not available, and only until a properly grounded outlet can be installed by a qualified electrician. The green colored rigid ear-lug extending from the adapter must be connected to a permanent ground such as a properly grounded outlet box cover. **Whenever the adapter is used, it must be held in place by a metal screw.**

**The two manuals disagree on the circuit voltage and do not agree with each other's outlet figure.** This owner's manual says the product "is for use on a nominal **110-volt** circuit" (page 3, Grounding Instructions) and calls for a **115-volt**, 15-amp grounded outlet (page 2, Important Safety Instructions). The card `e25-2026-electrical-power-requirements`, taken from the service manual, gives a **120-volt** 15-amp outlet and a "nominal 120-volt circuit". No winner is picked here; all four figures describe ordinary North American mains, but the printed numbers differ.
