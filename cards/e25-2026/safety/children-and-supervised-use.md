- **Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine.** There are obvious pinch points and other caution areas that can cause harm.
- The appliance is not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge, unless they have been given supervision or instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible for their safety.

The console has a keypad lock (Security, item F13 in the maintenance menu) that stops unauthorised use; see the maintenance menu card.
