**Keep your bill of sale.** It establishes the labor warranty period.

Service under the warranty must be obtained by following these steps, **in order**:

1. **Contact your selling authorized SOLE dealer**, OR
2. **Submit the service request online** at http://www.soletreadmills.com/serviceparts.html, including the **serial number**, contact information, and a brief description of the problem.
3. If there is a question as to where to obtain service, contact the service department by phone.
4. **If no local service is available, SOLE will repair or replace the parts, at SOLE's option, within the warranty period at no charge for parts.**

**Costs and shipping.** All transportation costs, both to the factory and on return to the owner, are the **owner's responsibility**. The owner is responsible for adequate packaging on return. **SOLE is not responsible for damage that occurs during shipping** - make all freight damage claims with the appropriate freight carrier.

**Before a technician is sent.** The service department assists with troubleshooting before sending a field technician, and SOLE may request pictures and video of the issue by email to help with diagnosis.

If service is performed, it is in your best interest to obtain and keep all receipts. This written warranty gives you specific legal rights, and you may also have other rights that vary from state to state.

Nothing may be shipped back without a return authorization number first - see the return authorization card.
