**Warranties do not transfer.**

The warranty does not cover products that have been **sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party**.

A machine bought second hand therefore carries no manufacturer's warranty, regardless of how much of the original period is left. This sits alongside the registration rule: the warranty is validated against the original purchase.
