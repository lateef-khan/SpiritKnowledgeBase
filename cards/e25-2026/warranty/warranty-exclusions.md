The warranty does not cover:

1. **Consequential, collateral or incidental damages** such as property damage and incidental expenses resulting from any breach of this written or any implied warranty. Some states do not allow this exclusion, so it may not apply to you.
2. **Service call reimbursement to the consumer.** Service call reimbursement to the dealer is also excluded where the call does not involve a malfunction or defect in workmanship or material, where the unit is beyond the warranty period or beyond the service call reimbursement period, where no component replacement is required, or where the elliptical is not in ordinary household use.
3. **Damage caused by service performed by anyone other than an authorized SOLE service company**, use of parts other than original SOLE parts, or external causes such as **corrosion (rust), discoloration of paint or plastic, alterations, modifications, abuse, misuse, accident, improper maintenance, inadequate power supply, natural disasters, or elemental damage** (power surges, lightning storms, water damage, fire damage).
4. **Products with original serial numbers that have been removed or altered.**
7. This warranty is **expressly in lieu of all other warranties expressed or implied**, including the warranties of merchantability and/or fitness for a particular purpose.

Four more exclusions have their own cards because they are asked about on their own: **transfer to a new owner (5)**, **non-residential use (8)**, **coverage outside the continental United States (9)**, and **keeping the machine outside a climate-controlled environment (10)**.
