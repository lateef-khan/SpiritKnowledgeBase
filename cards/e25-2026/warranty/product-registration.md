- **Record the serial number** of the machine in the space provided on the registration page of the manual.
- **The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to SOLE.**
- You can also **register online at www.soletreadmills.com under the support tab**.

The deadline for returning the card, and what to do if you miss it, is the other half of this rule and is printed in the warranty section - see the registration deadline card.
