The warranty applies only to products in ordinary household use, and the consumer is responsible for:

1. Returning the **warranty registration card** within 10 days, or registering later with proof of purchase.
2. **Proper use in accordance with the instructions in this manual**, including general maintenance.
3. **Proper installation** in accordance with the instructions provided with the elliptical and with all local electric codes.
4. **Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage**, replacement of blown fuses, and repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring.
5. **Expenses for making the elliptical accessible for servicing**, including any item that was not part of the elliptical when it shipped from the factory.
6. **Damage to the elliptical finish during shipping, installation or following installation.**
7. **Routine maintenance** of the unit as specified in this manual.
8. **Providing pictures or video of any issue by email** as requested by the service technician, and **assisting with troubleshooting and diagnosis before a home visit**.

**Item 2 in this list is reprinted from a treadmill manual.** It reads "Proper use of the **treadmill** in accordance with the instructions provided in this manual, including general maintenance (i.e. **belt tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under motor cover, and lubrication**)". None of those tasks exists on this elliptical, and the elliptical's own General Maintenance section on page 25 lists wipe-down, hardware tightening and rail cleaning instead. The printed wording is preserved here; treat page 25 as the maintenance this clause is asking for.
