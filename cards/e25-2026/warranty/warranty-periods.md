**Effective February 1, 2026.** SOLE warrants all its elliptical parts for the periods below **from the date of retail sale as determined by the sale receipt**, or, in the absence of a sales receipt, **eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date**.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| Frame Weldments | **Lifetime** |
| Parts | **2 Years** |
| Labor | **1 Year** |
| Cosmetic Items* | **90 Days** |

\* Cosmetic items include, but are not limited to: **grips, console overlays, and labels/decals**.

**Labor period, stated a second time under SERVICE:** twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale, or eighteen (18) months from the date of factory shipping as determined by the serial number, establishes the labor warranty period should service be required.

SOLE's responsibilities are to provide new or remanufactured parts, **at SOLE's option**, and technical support to independent dealers and servicing organizations. In the absence of a dealer or service organization, SOLE administers these warranties directly to the consumer.

The warranty applies **only to products in ordinary household use**, and it is void or excluded in several situations that have their own cards.
