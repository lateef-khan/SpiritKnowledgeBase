**The warranty will be void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment.**

The examples printed are: **garage, outside, carport.**

Note that this is stricter than the machine's own operating limits. The elliptical is specified to operate from 40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit at up to 95% non-condensing humidity, but meeting those numbers in an uninsulated space still voids the warranty.
