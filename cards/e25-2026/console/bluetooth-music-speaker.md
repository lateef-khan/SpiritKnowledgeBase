The exercise equipment can play music wirelessly over Bluetooth.

1. Turn on the Bluetooth function on your mobile phone or tablet.
2. Search for the name **"BT-Speaker"** in your device's Bluetooth menu.
3. Tap to connect.

The device can then transmit music to the exercise equipment.

This is a separate Bluetooth connection from the SOLE+ App pairing, which appears under the equipment's own name.
