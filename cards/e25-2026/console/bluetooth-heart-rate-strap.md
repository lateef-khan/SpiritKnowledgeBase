**You can use a wireless Bluetooth heart rate transmitter on this machine.**

- The equipment's Bluetooth device can be connected to a **Bluetooth wireless heart rate chest strap transmitter**.
- **The chest strap can still connect to the equipment even though the SOLE+ App is already connected** to it.
- To check whether a **Garmin** device can be used for heart rate transmission, refer to the list of supported devices provided at www.soletreadmills.com.

The manual does not list the supported devices itself, and it names no other brand.
