The console ships with a sleep mode you can choose:

- **ON** - the unit goes into an energy-saving state after **15 minutes of inactivity**.
- **OFF** - the console stays lit while the power is on.

To change it, press and hold **Start, Stop and Mode/Enter for 3 seconds** to enter the maintenance menu, then choose the sleep mode item and set **1 for ON, 0 for OFF**.

**The manual contradicts itself over which setting sleeps.** Page 9 says ON is the energy-saving state. The maintenance menu entry for the same item says "Turn off to have the console power down automatically after 15 minutes of inactivity", which reverses the meaning. Set it, then leave the console idle and watch what it does rather than trusting either sentence.

The same item is called **P5** on page 9 and **F5** in the maintenance menu list. It is one item.
