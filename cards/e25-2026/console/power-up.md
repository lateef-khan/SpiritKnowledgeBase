The console powers up automatically when power is connected. This model is connected **directly to 115 VAC**, and the **power switch is where the line cord plugs into the unit, on the left side near the front**.

At first power on the console runs an internal self-test. During the self-test:

- all the lights turn on,
- the **Level Window** shows a software version, for example **VER 1.0**,
- the **Distance Window** shows the odometer - how many virtual miles the elliptical has gone,
- the **Time Window** shows how many total hours the elliptical has been used.

The odometer and time stay up for only a few seconds, then the console goes to the startup display and is ready to use.
