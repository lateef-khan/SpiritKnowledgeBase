The console has a **Type-C port** for charging a personal device during a workout.

1. The fitness equipment power must be **on**.
2. Connect a Type-C charging cable (**not included**) to the Type-C power port and to your device.
3. Check that the device icon indicates it is charging.

Notes printed with the procedure:

- Make sure a compatible Type-C charging cable is used; one is not supplied.
- **The device's charging icon may or may not show.** Depending on how much current the device needs, the icon may stay off while the device is still charging, possibly at a lower charge rate.
- The port **provides up to 5Vdc/1.0 amp**.
- **You cannot save workout data to a Type-C device through this port; it is for charging only.**
