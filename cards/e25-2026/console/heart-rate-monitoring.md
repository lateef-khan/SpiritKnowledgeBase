The elliptical has a built-in heart rate monitoring system. Grasp the **Contact Heart Rate Sensors on the stationary handlebars**, or wear the **chest strap transmitter**. The heart icon starts blinking - this may take a few seconds - and the **Pulse Window** displays heart rate in beats per minute.

**The chest strap is the more accurate and reliable method.** The hand pulse sensors are subject to false readings depending on user physiology and workout habits, including how the sensors are gripped and how sweaty the hands are.
