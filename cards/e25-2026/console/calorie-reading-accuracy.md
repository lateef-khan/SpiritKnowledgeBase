**Calorie readings on every piece of exercise equipment, whether in a gym or at home, are not accurate and tend to vary widely.** They are meant only as a guide to monitor your progress from workout to workout. The only way to measure calorie burn accurately is in a clinical setting connected to a host of machines, because every person is different and burns calories at a different rate.

Entering your **weight** during programming aids a more correct calorie reading. The manual states plainly that an exact calorie count cannot be provided.
