The machine has a **Bluetooth transceiver** that works with compatible iOS and Android phones and tablets through the **SOLE+ App**.

1. Download the free **SOLE+ App** from the Apple Store or Google Play.
2. Make sure **Bluetooth is enabled** on your device.
3. **Tap the icon at the top right corner** of the app to search for Sole equipment.
4. When the equipment is detected, **tap Connect**.
5. When the app and equipment are synced, the **Bluetooth icon on the console lights up**. You may now start using the machine.

The app also syncs workout data with one of the supported fitness cloud sites: **Apple Health, Google Health Connect, Strava, or MapMyFitness**, with more to come.

A Bluetooth heart rate chest strap can be connected at the same time - see the chest strap card.
