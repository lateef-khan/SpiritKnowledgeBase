Tighten the **M8 x 9T nut** on the **M8x170mm J-bolt** until the **acoustic gauge reads 190 Hz (+/- 10 Hz)** when the belt is plucked with a finger. Then tighten the **M8 x 7T nut**.

The figure is given only inside the belt replacement procedure; the manual prints no separate tension table.
