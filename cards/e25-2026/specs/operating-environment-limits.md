| Condition | Specification |
|---|---|
| Operating temperature | **40 to 120 degrees Fahrenheit** |
| Humidity | **95% non-condensing** (no water drops forming on surfaces) |

**NEVER expose this elliptical to rain or moisture.** The product is **not** designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.

Note that meeting these operating limits is not the same as keeping the warranty: the warranty is void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment.
