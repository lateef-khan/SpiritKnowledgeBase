The Hill program simulates going up and down a hill. **The resistance in the pedals steadily increases and then decreases during the program.**

| Segment | Level | Incline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 2 |
| 2 | 2 | 7 |
| 3 | 3 | 7 |
| 4 | 3 | 7 |
| 5 | 4 | 10 |
| 6 | 4 | 10 |
| 7 | 5 | 10 |
| 8 | 5 | 10 |
| 9 | 7 | 10 |
| 10 | 7 | 12 |
| 11 | 5 | 12 |
| 12 | 5 | 10 |
| 13 | 4 | 10 |
| 14 | 4 | 10 |
| 15 | 3 | 10 |
| 16 | 3 | 10 |
| 17 | 2 | 7 |
| 18 | 1 | 7 |

The profile is printed as two rows of eighteen columns (SEGMENT / LEVEL / INCLINE). It is transposed here so each segment reads on one line; no value and no order has been changed.

Setting up any of the five preset programs uses the same steps; see the preset setup card.
