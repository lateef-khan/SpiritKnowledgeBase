The Strength program is designed to increase muscular strength in the lower body. It **steadily increases resistance to a high level and forces you to sustain it**, to strengthen and tone the legs and glutes.

| 2 | 2 | 10 |
| 3 | 2 | 10 |
| 4 | 3 | 10 |
| 5 | 3 | 10 |
