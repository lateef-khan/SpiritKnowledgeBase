These steps set up any of the five preset programs, P2 to P6.

1. Press the **Program key** to select P2-P6 and press **Enter** to get in.
2. The Time window asks for your **Age**. Adjust it with the **Level +/-** keys, then press **Enter**.
3. You are asked for your **Weight**. Adjust it with the **Level +/-** keys, then press **Enter**.
4. Next is **Time**. Adjust it and press **Enter**.
5. The settings are finished. Press **Start** to begin the workout, or press **Stop** to go back one level of the programming screen and change a setting.
6. **During the program** you can raise or lower the resistance or the incline at any time with the **Level +/-** keys or the **Incline +/-** keys.
7. Each press of the **Display** button shows the next set of information.
8. When the program ends the Message window shows a **summary of your workout**, displayed for a short time before the console returns to the start-up display.
