**This card covers both heart rate programs, H1 and H2.** They operate the same way and are programmed the same way; the only difference is the default target: **H1 defaults to 60% and H2 to 80% of the maximum heart rate**.

**A heart rate monitor is required.** The Pulse window displays **[Err]** if there is no pulse input after the Start key is pressed - that message has its own card.

1. Press the **Program key** to select H1 or H2 and press **Enter** to get in.
2. The Time window asks for your **Age**. Enter it with the **Level +/-** keys and press **Enter**.
3. You are asked for your **Weight**. Adjust it with the **Level +/-** keys and press **Enter**.
4. Next is **Time**. Adjust it and press **Enter**.
5. Adjust your **Target Heart Rate** - the heart rate you will strive to reach and maintain during the program. Adjust the level and press **Enter**.
6. The settings are finished. Press **Start** to begin the workout.
7. Press the **Level +/-** keys at any time during the program to change the workload. This changes your Target Heart Rate during the program.
8. Each press of the **Display** button shows the next set of information.
9. When the program ends, press **Start** to run the same program again or **Stop** to exit.

**The heart rate program controls resistance at the pedals automatically** (page 23). You can also use the heart rate monitor feature on its own, in manual mode or in any other program, without running a heart rate program.

**Step 6 contradicts the note printed with it.** The step says you can go back and modify your settings "by pressing the **Enter** key", while the note immediately after it says "At any time during the editing of Data you can press the **Stop** key to go back one level, or screen." Every other program in this manual uses Stop for Previous Screen. Both readings are printed on page 25; no winner is picked here.
