The Manual program works as the name implies: **you control the workload yourself and not the computer.**

1. Select P1 and press the **Enter** key.
2. The Time window asks you to enter your **Age**. Adjust it with the **Level +/-** keys, then press **Enter** to accept the number and go on to the next setting.
3. You are now asked for your **Weight**. Adjust it with the **Level +/-** keys, then press **Enter**.
4. Next is the **Time**. Adjust the length of time by pressing the keys, then press **Enter**.
5. The settings are finished. Press **Start** to begin the workout. You can also go back and change a setting by pressing the **Stop** key to go back one level of the programming screen.
6. Once the program starts the elliptical is set to **level one**. This is the easiest level and it is a good idea to stay at level one for a while to warm up. Press **Level +** to increase the workload at any time; **Level -** decreases it.
7. Each press of the **Display** button shows the next set of information.
8. When the program ends the Message window shows a **summary of your workout**. The summary is displayed for a short time, then the console returns to the start-up display.
