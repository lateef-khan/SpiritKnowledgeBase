Each program can be customized with your personal information. Some of it is needed for the readouts to be correct.

- **Age** is necessary during the Heart Rate control program so that the correct settings are entered. If you enter your age during programming, the console calculates your maximum heart rate automatically.
- **Weight** aids in calculating a more correct Calorie reading. The manual states an exact calorie count cannot be provided.

**The values are remembered.** Age and Weight default settings change when you enter a new number, so the **last Age and Weight entered are saved as the new defaults**. Enter them the first time you use the elliptical and you will not have to enter them again unless one of them changes, or unless someone else enters a different Age and Weight.
