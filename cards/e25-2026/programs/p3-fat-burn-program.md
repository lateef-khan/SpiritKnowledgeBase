The Fat Burn program is designed to maximize the burning of fat. The manual notes that most experts agree a lower exertion level held at a steady workload is best, and that the best way to burn fat is to keep your heart rate at around **60% to 70%** of its maximum potential. **This program does not use heart rate**; it simulates a lower, steady exertion workout.

The profile is printed as two rows of eighteen columns (SEGMENT / LEVEL / INCLINE). It is transposed here so each segment reads on one line; no value and no order has been changed.

If you want a program that actually reads and controls to your heart rate, use a heart rate program instead.
