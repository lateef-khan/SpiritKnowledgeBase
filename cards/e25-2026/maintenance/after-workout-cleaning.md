**Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each workout.**

Disconnect the elliptical from the electrical outlet before cleaning.

Sweat left on the machine matters for the warranty as well as the hardware: corrosion (rust) is listed among the external causes the warranty does not cover.
