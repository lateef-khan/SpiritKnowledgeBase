1. Clean the **rails and the polyurethane wheels** with a **lint free cloth and rubbing alcohol**.
2. Remove stubborn build-up with **your thumbnail or a non-metallic scraper**, such as the back edge of a plastic knife.
3. After cleaning, **apply a small amount of lubricant on the rails** with your fingers or a lint free cloth.
4. **You only need a thin coat of lubrication. Wipe off any excess.**

**The manual does not name the lubricant** - no type, brand or part number is printed anywhere in it - and it gives no interval for how often to do this.
