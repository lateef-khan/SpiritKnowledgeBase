If a squeak, thump, clicking or rough feeling develops, the main cause is most likely one of two things.

**I. Hardware that was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.** All bolts installed during assembly need to be tightened as much as possible. **It may be necessary to use a larger wrench than the one provided** if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently. The manual states that **90% of calls to the service department for noise issues can be traced to loose hardware or the rear rails being dirty.**

**II. Dirt build-up on the rear rails and polyurethane wheels.** Build-up on the rails can cause a thumping sound that seems to come from inside the main body of the machine, because noise travels and is amplified in the tubing of the frame. Cleaning and lubricating the rails has its own card.

**If squeaks or other noises persist, check that the unit is properly leveled before calling the service department.**

The service manual cards for this machine list no noise causes at all, so there is no second list to work through - but neither does this manual give a leveling procedure or a torque figure, so "tightened as much as possible" is the only specification printed.
