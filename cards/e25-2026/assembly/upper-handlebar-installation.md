Hardware for this step: **#124 Hex Head Bolt (6 pcs), #149 Nylon Nut (6 pc), #168 Curved Washer (4 pcs)**.

Insert the **left Top Handle Bar (19)** onto the left Lower Handle Bar and secure with **3 pcs Hex Head Bolts (124), 2 pcs Curved Washers (168) and 3 pcs Nylon Nuts (149)**, using Wrench (179) and Wrench (178) together.

Secure the **right Top Handle Bar (20)** on the right Lower Handle Bar the same way.

Note the asymmetry printed in the step: **three bolts and three nuts per side but only two curved washers per side.**
