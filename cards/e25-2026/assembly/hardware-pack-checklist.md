The "O" in a washer description is the diameter symbol on the printed page; the symbol did not survive the text extraction cleanly, and the printed order of the items within each step has been kept.

The same quantities are repeated in the sidebar of each instruction page and agree with this checklist, with one exception: **step 4-2 supplies more hardware than its instruction uses.** The checklist supplies 6 pcs of #136 and 6 pcs of #139, while the step 4-2 instruction fits 2 pcs of #136 and 1 pc of #139 per side, so 4 and 2 in total. Both figures are printed; no reconciliation is given in the manual.
