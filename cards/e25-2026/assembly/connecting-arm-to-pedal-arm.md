Hardware for this step: **#122 Hex Head Bolt (4 pc), #162 Flat Washer (4 pc)**.

Align the plate of the **Left Connecting Arm (8)** with the foot pad axle of the **Left Pedal Arm (6)** and put it in. Secure the **Fixing Axle (25)** with **2 pcs Flat Washer (162)** and **2 pcs Hex Head Bolt (122)** using Wrench (179).

Secure the **Right Connecting Arm (9)** on the **Right Pedal Arm (7)** the same way.

Replacing either arm later is a different procedure with different hardware; the service manual cards cover that.
