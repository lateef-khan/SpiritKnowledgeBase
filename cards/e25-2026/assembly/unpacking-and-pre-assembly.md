1. Use a **razor knife or box cutter** to cut the outside, the bottom, and the edge of the box along the dotted line. **Lift the box over the unit** and unpack.
2. Carefully remove all parts from the carton and **inspect for any damage or missing parts**. If damaged parts are found, or if parts are missing, **contact your dealer immediately**.
3. Locate the hardware package. **Remove the tools first**, then remove the hardware for each step as needed, to avoid confusion.

**The numbers in parentheses (#) throughout the instructions are the item numbers from the assembly drawing**, not part order numbers.
