Hardware for this step: **#131 Socket Head Cap Bolt (4 pcs), #136 (2 pc), #165 Flat Washer (4 pcs), #169 Split Washer (4 pcs)**.

1. Run the **Computer Cable (50)** through the **Console Mast (12)**, pulling the opposite end of the twist tie up through the mast until the cable exits the top. Install the Console Mast (12) into the receiving bracket on the top of the **Main Frame (1)**. Install **4 pcs Socket Head Cap Bolts (131)** with **4 pcs Split Washers (169)** and **4 pcs Flat Washers (165)** and tighten with the provided **L Allen Wrench (177)**.
   **Pull slightly on the computer cable at the top of the mast while installing.** This ensures the cable does not get pinched and shorted during console mast assembly.
2. Install the **Bottle Holder (92)** on the Console Mast (12) with **2 pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)**, tightened with the provided Phillips Head Screw Driver (176).

A pinched computer cable is a plausible cause of a dead or partial console display; the console replacement and display cards cover what to check afterwards.
