1. Follow procedures 9.2, 9.3 and 9.4 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm and both side cases.
2. Take off the **round disk cover** using a tapering stick.
3. Remove the round disk cover, use a **12 mm wrench** to remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 15mm** and **flat washer 5/16" x 35 x 1.5T** securing the cross bar. Use a **13 mm wrench and an M6 hex wrench** to loosen the **socket head cap bolt M8 x 25mm with nut M8 x 6.3T**, then remove the cross bar.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **6 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws with 1/4" x 19 flat washers** to separate the cross bar from the round disk cover.
5. To reassemble, secure the round disk on the cross bar and return it on the crank axle. Align the cross bar with the square hole, put the **7x7x25L woodruff key** in the hole and tighten the **M8x40 socket head cap screw together with two M8 x 6.3T nuts until it reaches 500 Kg-cm**. Return and tighten the 5/16" x 15 m/m hex head bolt with the 5/16" x 35 x 1.5T flat washer.
6. Follow the above procedures to resume the connecting arm, pedal arm and both side cases with the round disk cover.

**The bolt is named twice and the two names differ.** Step 3 removes an **M8 x 25mm** socket head cap bolt with one M8 x 6.3T nut; step 5 refits an **M8x40** socket head cap screw with two M8 x 6.3T nuts. Measure the bolt you removed before ordering.

**Screw count changed from 2019.** The 2019 E25 manual releases the round disk cover with 8 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws; this manual says 6 pcs.
