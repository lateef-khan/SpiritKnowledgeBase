1. Follow procedures 9.2, 9.3, 9.4 and 9.5 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, both covers and the cross bar.
2. Use a **13 mm wrench** to loosen the **M8 x 9T nut on the M8x170mm J-bolt** and on the **idler wheel**, and another **M8 x 7T nut**; the belt is then released.
3. To reassemble, return the belt onto the **drive pulley and flywheel**, then tighten the M8 x 9T nut until the **acoustic gauge reads 190 Hz (+/- 10 Hz)** when the belt is plucked with a finger, then tighten the M8 x 7T nut.
4. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.

Step 1 of the printed procedure names "both Chain Covers", but procedure 9.4 in this manual is the **side cases**; the machine has no separate chain cover section.
