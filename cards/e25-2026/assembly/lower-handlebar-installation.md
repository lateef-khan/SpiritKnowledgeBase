Hardware for this step: **#122 Hex Head Bolt (2 pc), #123 Hex Head Bolt (2 pc), #149 Nylon Nut (2 pc), #160 Flat Washer (2 pc), #164 Flat Washer (2 pc)**.

1. Install the left and right **Lower Handle Bars (10) (11)** onto the left and right **console mast shafts** respectively, and secure with **Hex Head Bolts (122)** together with **Flat Washers (164)** using Wrench (179).
2. Untie the **rod end bearing** on the **left Connecting Arm (8)**. Insert **Hex Head Bolt (123)** through the rod end bearing bracket on **Swing Arm (10)** and use Wrench (178) and Wrench (179) to tighten it together with **Flat Washer (160)** and **Nylon Nut (149)** in the rod end bearing bracket on the left swing arm.
3. Fix the **right Swing Arm (11)** and the **right Connecting Arm (9)** the same way with the same hardware.

**The manual calls parts 10 and 11 both "Lower Handle Bars" and "Swing Arms" within the same step.** They are the same two parts.
