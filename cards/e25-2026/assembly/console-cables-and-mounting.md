Hardware for this step: **#122 Hex Head Bolt (4 pc), #136 (4 pc), #160 Flat Washer (4 pc)**.

**Left side.** Run the **Handpulse w/Cable (57)** from the **Left Handpulse Assembly (15)**, together with the **Handle Wire, Resistance (60)**, through the **left round hole** of the console mast and pull it out at the top. Secure it to the left side of the console mast with **2 pcs Hex Head Bolt (122)** and **2 pcs Flat Washer (160)** using Wrench (179).

**Right side.** The same way, run the **Handpulse w/Cable (58)** from the **Right Handpulse Assembly (16)**, together with the **Handle Wire, Incline (61)**, through the **right round hole** of the console mast and pull it out at the top, then secure it to the right side.

**Connections.** Plug all of the connectors into the back of the console:

- **Computer Cable (50)**
- the two **Hand pulse Cables (57 and 58)**
- **Resistance switch wire (60)**
- **Incline switch wire (61)**

Secure the **Console (45)** on the console mounting plate with **4 pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)** using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (176).

**The left handlebar carries the resistance switch and the right handlebar carries the incline switch.** That is worth knowing when only one pair of handlebar buttons is dead.
