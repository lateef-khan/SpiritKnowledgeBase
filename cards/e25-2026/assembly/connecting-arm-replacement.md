1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **connecting arm cover (left and right)**.
2. Use **12 mm and 13 mm open end wrenches** to release the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 1-1/4"**, **flat washer 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T** and **nut 5/16" x 7T** which secure the **rod end bearing**.
3. Use a **12 mm open-end wrench** to remove the **hex socket screw 5/16" x 15mm** and **flat washer 5/16" x 23 x 3.0T**. Pull out the **pedal axle fix plates** to remove the **pedal bar assembly**.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **4 Phillips head screws M5x10mm** securing the pedal, and remove the pedal.

Steps 9.3 to 9.9 all start by sending you back here, so this is the first procedure in any of the drivetrain jobs.
