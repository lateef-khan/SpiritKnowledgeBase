1. Remove the connecting arm and pedal arm (steps 9.2 and 9.3).
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **4 pcs of 3.5x12 self-tapping screws** securing the console mast cover.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **9 pcs of 3.5x16 self-tapping screws** and **3 pcs of 5x19 x 3 tapping screws** to take the **right side case** apart.
4. To take the **left side case** apart, release the **3.5x16 self-tapping screws with 1/4" x 19 flat washers** on the main frame and **3 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws**. Then disconnect two red connecting wires and the white grounding wire - **mark the red connecting wires first** - to release the left side case.
5. On refit, connect the red connecting wires and the white grounding wire back onto the **AC Electronic Module**.
6. Return the left side case, with one **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** on it, onto the main frame. Use a 3.5x16 self-tapping screw with a 1/4" x 19 flat washer to hold it - not too tight for now - then tighten with **3 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws**, making sure the side case matches with the round disk. Tighten the 3.5x16 self-tapping screws.
7. Match both side cases with each other and use **9 pcs of 3.5x16 self-tapping screws** and **3 pcs of 5x16 tapping screws** to secure them.
8. Reverse the above procedures to resume the pedal arm and connecting arm.

**One screw size is printed two ways.** Step 3 removes the right side case with "3pcs of 5x19 x 3 tapping screws"; step 7 refits both side cases with "3pcs of 5x16 tapping screws". The 2019 manual printed 5x16 in both places.
