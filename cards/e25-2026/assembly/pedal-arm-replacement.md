1. Refer to step 9.2 to remove the **pedal bar assembly**, then use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew the **pedal arm cover**.
2. Use an **M8 hex wrench and a 14 mm wrench** to remove the **gap socket screw 3/8" x 2-1/4"**, **two flat washers 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T** and **nylon nut 3/8" x 11T** connecting the **inclinable rail assembly** and the rotating block, then remove the inclinable rail assembly.
3. Use a **12 mm hex wrench** to remove the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 15mm** and **flat washer 5/16" x 35 x 1.5T**, to remove the **bushing housing** and **pedal arm**.
4. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
