Hardware supplied for this step: **#136 Phillips Head Screw (6 pcs), #139 Sheet Metal Screw (6 pcs)**.

Match the **Connecting Arm Covers (L) and (R), (110) and (111)**, on the **Connecting Arm (L) (8)** and secure with **2 pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)** and **a Sheet Metal Screw (139)**, using the **Short Phillips Head Screw Driver (175)**. Repeat for the other side.

**Leftover hardware is expected at this step, and the manual does not say so.** Following the instruction uses 4 pcs of #136 and 2 pcs of #139 for both sides, while the checklist supplies 6 pcs of each. Both numbers are printed; no reconciliation is given.

This is the last assembly step. All the bolts left loose earlier should now be tightened as much as possible - the maintenance section traces most noise complaints back to hardware that was never fully tightened.
