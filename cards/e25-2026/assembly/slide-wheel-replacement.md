1. To take the slide wheel apart, use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **two Phillips head screws M5x15mm** and take off the **slide wheel cover** first.
2. Use a **12 mm open wrench** to remove the **5/16" x 15 m/m hex head bolt** with the **5/16" x 35 x 1.5T flat washer** and remove the sliding wheels.
3. **Apply a little lubricant to the axle**, then reverse the above steps to reassemble the slide wheels and the slide wheel cover.
