1. Follow procedures 9.2, 9.3, 9.4, 9.5 and 9.6 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, both left and right side cases, the cross bar and the belt.
2. Use a **13 mm wrench** to loosen the **M8 x 9T nut on the M8x170mm J-bolt** together with the **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** until the J-bolt is released. With the same wrench release the **M8 x 20 carriage bolt** which secures the idler wheel assembly, together with the **5/16" x 20 x 1.5T flat washer** and **M8 x 7T nut**, to take the idler wheel assembly apart.
3. To reassemble, tighten the M8x20 carriage bolt, 5/16" x 20 x 1.5T flat washer and M8 x 7T nut to secure the idler wheel assembly - **tighten these only after the belt has been adjusted** - and return the J-bolt and other parts in the reverse order.
