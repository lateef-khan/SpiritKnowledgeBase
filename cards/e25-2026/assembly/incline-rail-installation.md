Hardware for this step: **#126 (2 pcs), #127 (4 pcs), #136 (6 pc), #141 (4 pcs), #165 Flat Washer (6 pcs), #169 Split Washer (6 pcs), #195 Star Washer (4 pcs)**.

1. Install the **Incline Rail Assembly (2)** into the **U-channel bracket of the Main Frame (1)**. Secure with six bolts and their hardware: from the sides install **4 pcs Hex Head Bolts (127)** with **2 pcs Hex Head Bolts (126)**, plus **6 pcs Split Washers (169), 6 pcs Flat Washers (165) and 4 pcs Star Washers (195)**, and tighten with Wrench (178).
   **NOTE: do not tighten all the screws yet.** Tighten all screws only after all components are assembled.
2. **Connect the two wire harnesses.** Connect the **Incline Motor Power Cord (48)** to the female receiver cable coming from the Incline Rail Assembly (2). Connect the **three pin position sensor connector, Incline Motor Connecting Wire (49)**, to the female receiver coming from the Incline Rail Assembly (2).
3. Install **2 pcs Adjustment Foots (90)** to the rear stabilizer of the Incline Rail Assembly (2).
4. Install the **Cover Holders (23)** with the hole for mounting the plastic cover on the bent tab facing **rearward**, and secure them on the Incline Rail Assembly (2) with **4 pcs Phillips Head Screws (141)**. Install the **Inclinable Rail Cover (115)** on the rail base with **2 pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)**.
5. Secure the **Rear Bar Cover (116)** on the rear stabilizer and the Cover Holders (23) with **4 pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)** using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (176).

The two connectors in step 2 are the ones the incline fault cards send a technician to check: an unplugged or reversed position sensor connector is what the incline test procedure measures.
