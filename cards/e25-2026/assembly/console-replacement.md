1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **Phillips head screws M5x10mm (4 pcs)** securing the console. Unfasten all connected wires and remove the console.
2. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly. Be sure not to crush or damage wiring during the process.
3. To remove both the upper and lower console cover, remove the **sheet metal screws 3.5x12** securing the console. The manual marks this step "should only be done by a professional".
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **Phillips head screws M5x10mm (2 pcs)**, then move out the **drink bottle holder**.
