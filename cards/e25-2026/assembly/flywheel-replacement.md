1. Follow procedures 9.2, 9.3, 9.4, 9.5, 9.6 and 9.7 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, both side covers, the cross bar, the belt and the idler wheel assembly.
2. **Plug in the power and adjust the resistance to level 20**, then release the steel cable.
3. Use a **15 wrench** to loosen the **nylon nut 3/8"-UNF26 x 9T** on the flywheel and main frame. Remove the flywheel and belt.
4. To reassemble the flywheel, return the belt onto the grooves on the flywheel and return the flywheel onto the mainframe.
5. Tighten both **3/8"-UNF26 x 11T nuts**. Return the steel cable onto the flywheel **at 45 degrees**, then return the idler wheel assembly.
6. Turn the drive pulley and check that the belt is secure. Adjust both **3/8"-UNF26 x 4T nuts** to position the belt in the middle of the drive pulley, then tighten all other screws. Adjust the belt to a proper tension.
7. Follow procedures 9.7, 9.6, 9.5, 9.4, 9.3 and 9.2 to return the other parts.

**The same nut is given three thicknesses.** Removal names 3/8"-UNF26 x 9T, refit names 3/8"-UNF26 x 11T, and belt centring names 3/8"-UNF26 x 4T. The 2019 E25 manual prints the same three figures, so this is long-standing in the printed text rather than new.
