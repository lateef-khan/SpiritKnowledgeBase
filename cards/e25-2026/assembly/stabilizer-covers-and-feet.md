Hardware for this step: **6 pc of #136, M5 x 10mm Phillips Head Screw**.

1. Use **2 pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)** to secure the **Front Stabilizer Cover (112)** on the front stabilizer, with the Phillips Head Screw Driver (176).
2. Install the **Slide Wheel Covers (95)** above each side of the slide wheel urethanes and secure with **4 pcs Phillips Head Screws (136)** using the Phillips Head Screw Driver (176).
3. Install **4 pcs Adjustment Foots (90)** to the front stabilizer and middle stabilizer of the **Main Frame (1)**.
4. Plug both **Oval End Caps (76)** onto both ends of the stabilizer tube.

Two further adjustment feet go on the rear stabilizer in the next step.
