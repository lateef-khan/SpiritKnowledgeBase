1. Follow procedures 9.2 and 9.3 to take apart the connecting arm and pedal arm. (The printed step also lists the side cases, cross bar and belt, but 9.2 and 9.3 do not remove those; use 9.4, 9.5 and 9.6 if you need them off.)
2. Use a **12 mm wrench** to release the **5/16" x 15 m/m hex head bolt** with the **5/16" x 35 x 1.5T flat washer**, and the bushing housing is released.
3. To reassemble, return the bushing housing onto the shaft and tighten with the 5/16" x 15 m/m hex head bolt and 5/16" x 35 x 1.5T flat washer.
4. Refit the pedal arm and connecting arm.

**The printed step 4 points at sections that do not exist in this manual.** It reads "Follow procedures 11-2, 11-5 and 11-3, 11-4", which are section numbers from the older E25 manual. The equivalent sections here are 9.2, 9.3, 9.4 and 9.5.
