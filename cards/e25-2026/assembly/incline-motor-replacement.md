1. Follow procedures 9.2 and 9.3 to take apart the connecting arm and pedal arm.
2. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **Phillips head screws M5x15mm** from the incline cover, then remove the parts.
3. Use **two 14 mm wrenches** to remove the **hex head bolt 3/8" x 2-1/2"** together with the **flat washer 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T**, **nylon nut 3/8" x 7T** and **two nylon washers 3/8" x 35 x 5T** which secure the incline tube and incline motor.
4. Use **two 17 mm open end wrenches** to remove the **hex head bolt M10x90mm**, **flat washer 3/8 x 19 x 1.5T** and **nylon nut M10 x 8T** from the outer rail tube and the incline adjustable assembly. Remove the inclined adjustable assembly.
5. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **tapping screw 5x19** from the incline motor grounding wire. **Cut the wire tie**, then take off the incline motor.
6. Use a **12 mm wrench** to remove from the rail assembly the **hex head bolt 5/16" x 1** and **flat washer 5/16" x 35 x 2T**, pull out the locking tube assembly and remove the rail assembly.
7. If the aluminum rail must come off, remove the **Phillips head screws M5x10mm** from it.
8. **Before assembling the motor onto the frame, make sure it has been zeroed.** Then rotate the lifting tube **clockwise to the end, then counterclockwise one and a half turns**, or measure the **centre of the two holes to be 206mm (+/- 1mm)**.
9. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.

**The zeroing distance changed.** The 2019 E25 manual gives 207mm (+/- 1mm) for the same measurement; this manual gives 206mm (+/- 1mm) and adds the one-and-a-half-turn method.
