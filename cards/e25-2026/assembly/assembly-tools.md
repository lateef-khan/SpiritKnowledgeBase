| Item | Tool |
|---|---|
| #175 | Short Phillips Head Screw Driver |
| #176 | Phillips Head Screw Driver |
| #177 | 8mm L Allen Wrench |
| #178 | 13/14 m/m Wrench |
| #179 | 12/14 m/m Wrench |

No other tool is called for in the assembly steps. The maintenance section notes that **it may be necessary to use a larger wrench than the one provided** if you cannot tighten the assembly bolts sufficiently by hand with the supplied wrenches.
