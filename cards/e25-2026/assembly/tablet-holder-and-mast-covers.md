Hardware for this step: **#139, 3.5 x 12mm Sheet Metal Screw (8 pcs)**.

1. Install the **Tablet Holder (89)** to the **back of the console** with **4 pcs Sheet Metal Screws (139)**.
2. Match the right and left **Console Mast Covers (100 and 101)** and install them on the **Console Mast (12)** with **4 pcs Sheet Metal Screws (139)**.
