1. Put the multi-meter to the **20VDC** setting. Place the probes on the motor control wire (**red probe in brown wire, black probe in black wire**) on the drive board.
2. Turn on unit power. The display lights up.
3. Press LEVEL UP. Normal reading: **+5~6.0VDC**. The motor operates and resistance increases.
4. Press LEVEL DOWN. Normal reading: **-5~6.0VDC**. The motor operates and resistance decreases.
5. If there is no voltage, inspect the power socket holder **FUSE**. If it is broken, replace it.

The probe placement is repeated in the same words under the photograph on page 26, so the wire colours are stated twice and agree.

**This procedure got shorter.** The 2019 E25 manual added a sixth step - inspect whether the drive board POWER LED is lit, and replace the drive board if it is not. This manual stops at the fuse.
