**This is the [Err] message in the Pulse window during a heart rate program. It is not error E1, E2 or E3, which are console and motor faults from the service manual.**

A heart rate monitor is asked for by the heart rate program. **The Pulse window displays [Err] if there is no pulse input after the Start key is pressed.**

Supply a pulse signal before or as you press Start: grip the contact heart rate sensors on the stationary handlebars, or wear a chest strap transmitter. The chest strap is the more accurate and reliable method.

If a pulse signal is present but the reading is missing or erratic, the service manual's hand pulse cards cover the wiring and console checks.
