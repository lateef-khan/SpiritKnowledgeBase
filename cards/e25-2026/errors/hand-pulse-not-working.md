Condition as printed: "Hand pulse lost its function. (No pulse displayed on monitor)".

| Reason | Solution |
|---|---|
| 1. Hands not on the hand pulse sensors, or only one hand on the sensor. | Hold the hand pulse with **two hands**. |
| 2. The connector of HANDPULSE W/WIRE and console not connected properly. | Connect the cable again. |
| 3. The wires got damaged where the HANDPULSE W/WIRE meets the console. | Replace with a new cable. |
| 4. Hand pulse board is broken. | Replace console or hand pulse board. |
