The console overview drawing labels these parts:

- **Tablet Rack**
- **Touchscreen**
- **Adjustable Fan**
- **Audio Jack**
- **Cup Holder**
- **Handrail Lock**
- **USB Charging**
- **Safety Key**
- **Hand Pulse Grip**
- **Child Lock**
- **Handlebar Incline Control** (left) and **Handlebar Speed Control** (right)
- **Direct Speed & Incline Buttons**

**This is the base XT685 panel with the LCD window replaced by a Touchscreen.** Every
other label is the same as `xt-2023-console-layout`, which is why that card is not
extended to cover this machine - the display itself is the difference, and it is the
thing people ask about.

**The XT485ENT touchscreen console is laid out differently again**: it labels
Integrated Speakers, a Swivel Fan, Easy-Touch Control Keys and an input-only audio
jack, and labels **no cup holder, no child lock and no safety key**. See
`xt485ent-2023-console-panel-and-physical-buttons`.
