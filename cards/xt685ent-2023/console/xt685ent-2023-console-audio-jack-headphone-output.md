**There is an Audio output Jack on the front of the console, and built-in speakers.**

**The output jack is a headphone jack for private listening.**

**Its sibling is the opposite way round.** The XT485ENT of the same year labels its
socket **"3.5mm Audio-input only (no output)"** - headphones will not work in it; see
`xt485ent-2023-console-audio-jack-input-only`. **The two ENT treadmills contradict
each other on this point.** Check the label on the machine before answering.

**The 2015 XT consoles have an audio *input* jack** and no volume control on the
console; see `xt-2015-console-speakers-and-audio-jack`. This manual states no volume
control either, but the console does pair as a Bluetooth speaker under the name
"Bt-speaker" - see `xt-2023-console-bluetooth-speaker`.
