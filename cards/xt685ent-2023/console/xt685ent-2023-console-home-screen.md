**The main screen of the Training section displays an overview of your activity** and
offers shortcuts to the most frequently used training modes:

- **an immediate Start**
- **a manually controlled timed workout, "Set Timer"**
- **3 to 6 programs you have marked as favorites.** **How many appear depends on your
  display size.**

**If you are in Guest mode you can set your age and weight here**; it helps the
machine calculate your workout summary more accurately.

**The XT485ENT home screen is arranged differently** - it names an icon bar of Log
out, Pair HR device, volume, fan, activity overview, current user and Wifi settings,
and six tabs along the bottom; see `xt485ent-2023-console-homescreen`.
