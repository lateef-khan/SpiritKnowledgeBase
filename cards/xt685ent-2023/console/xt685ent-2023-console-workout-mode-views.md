Once the workout has started, **after a short countdown the console appears in its
workout mode**. **Warm-up and cool down phases may be skipped by pressing "Skip".**

- **During the workout, switch between Stats, Charts and Track views** of the Training
  section, or go to the Media section for content.
- **In the Stats view you can select which parameters to display by tapping on the
  number.**

**Speed and incline must be adjusted with the physical keys.** The manual says so in
as many words: *"Please use physical keys to adjust speed and incline."* There is no
on-screen speed or incline control on this machine.

**Press the physical "STOP" key to pause the workout.** From the pause you can **get
back to running, start a cool down, or end the workout and see the summary**.

**After 5 minutes of inactivity in Pause mode the workout ends automatically.**

**The XT485ENT is the opposite on the point people ask about**: its speed and incline
*are* adjusted by tapping arrows in a right-hand panel, and its views are named Goal,
Profile and Track. See `xt485ent-2023-console-workout-views`.
