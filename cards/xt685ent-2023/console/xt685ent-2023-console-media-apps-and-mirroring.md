**Tap the right tab of the bottom panel to reach the Media section.** From there,
**choose a pre-installed third party App, or use the Mirroring function to cast
content from your smartphone directly to the console screen**. **For detailed
instructions press the ? icon.**

Once content is playing, use the **floating panel** to navigate and to control full
screen mode:

- **Touch the panel's top edge and drag it around the screen** to a place where it
  does not block the content.
- **Press Hide Panels** to enter full screen with no stats on top and no tabs at the
  bottom; **press Show Panels** to bring them back.
- **Use the arrow button on the right side of the panel to hide and show the text
  labels**, which shrinks the panel further.
- **Press "Apps" to go back to the content sources.**

**The manual names no app** and prints no iOS or Android mirroring steps of its own.

**The XT485ENT splits this into two separate sections** - Internet & Apps and Screen
Mirroring - with no floating panel; see `xt485ent-2023-console-internet-and-apps` and
`xt485ent-2023-console-screen-mirroring`.
