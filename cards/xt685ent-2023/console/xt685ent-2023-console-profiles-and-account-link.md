**When launched for the first time the console prompts you to enter as a guest, or
create a new profile.** A profile lets the machine remember your physical parameters
and favourite programs, gives you custom programs with adjustable intensity patterns,
and lets your workout records synchronise with the SPIRIT+ mobile app.

- **Tap + to create a new profile.**
- **A maximum of 9 profiles can be created.**
- **Each profile can be protected with a passcode.**

**Linking a SPIRIT+ account:**

1. **Scan the QR code on the console screen** to download the mobile app first.
2. **Install the app and complete the registration.**
3. **Press "Link" at the bottom of the console screen.**
4. **Open the QR code scanning camera in your SPIRIT+ app and scan the QR code
   again**, which links your local profile with the SPIRIT+ account.

**Your console profile cannot be linked to multiple SPIRIT+ accounts at the same
time.** **If you skip this step you can reach it again later in Profile settings.**

**The XT485ENT does it the other way round** - you scan the QR code the console shows
*because you already have an account*, which uploads your existing personal info to
the machine, and there is no passcode; see `xt485ent-2023-console-profile-tabs`.

**Sole's SB1200 and SR550 print this procedure word for word with SOLE+ in place of
SPIRIT+.** They are a different brand and are kept on their own cards; see
`sb1200-2023-getting-started-and-profiles`.
