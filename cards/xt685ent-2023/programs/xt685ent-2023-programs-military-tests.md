**Air Force, Army, Navy, Coast Guard, PEB and Marine Corps are US military tests that measure the
time required for you to cover a certain distance. The speed is controlled manually.**

That is everything this manual prints about the six of them. **It gives no distance for any
individual test**, so the 1.5 mile Air Force run, the 2 mile Army run and the rest cannot be
answered for this machine from its own manual. The CT900ENT manual prints a distance for each of its
tests, but it is a different console with a different test list - it has no Coast Guard or PEB test -
so its figures must not be carried across. See `ct900ent-fitness-test-air-force` and its neighbours.

Coast Guard and PEB appear on this list and on no CT900ENT card.
