**To reach every category of program, press "All Programs" on the Home screen.** Inside, switch
between categories with the tabs across the top of the screen: **Preset, Targets, Fitness Tests,
Custom and Favorites.**

- Tap any **program card** to open its **Program Setup page**, where you can read more about the
  program, set the properties of your workout, or keep browsing with the arrow buttons in the upper
  top right corner of the screen.
- To adjust a workout parameter you can **drag the ruler**, tap the **+ and -** buttons, or **tap on
  the number to open a numpad** and type the value directly.

What each tab holds, from the pages that describe them:

| Tab | Programs |
|---|---|
| Preset | Manual (Timer), Hill, Fatburn, Cardio, Strength, HIIT, Fusion, 5K, 10K |
| Targets | Heart rate, Time, Distance, Calories |
| Fitness Tests | Gerkin protocol, Air Force, Army, Navy, Coast Guard, PEB, Marine Corps |
| Custom | Up to 12 programs you build yourself |
| Favorites | Any program you have marked with the ribbon icon |

**The manual prints no total program count**, so this table is assembled from the pages that
describe each program.

This is not the eight-program list of the button-console XT385, XT485 and XT685, and it is not the
two-category layout (Programs and Templates) of the XT485ENT.
