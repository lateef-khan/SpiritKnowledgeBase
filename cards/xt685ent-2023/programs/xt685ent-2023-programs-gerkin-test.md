**Gerkin Protocol is a sub maximal VO2 (volume of oxygen) test that increases speed and incline
alternately until you reach 85% of your max heart rate. The time it takes for you to reach 85%
determines the test score (VO2 Max).**

**A heart rate monitor is required for this test.**

**The test starts with a 3-minute warm-up at a lower speed.**

**That warm-up speed is all this manual prints.** It does not print a figure, so do not supply the
3 mph (4.8 km/h) that the CT900ENT, 4.0T, 7.0T, 8.0T and MT200 manuals print for their own Gerkin
warm-up. **This manual also prints no stage chart and no VO2max score chart**, so the stage speeds,
grades and scores cannot be answered for this machine from its own manual.
