**Choose from 7 options under the Fitness Tests tab.** They are the Gerkin protocol and six US
military tests - Air Force, Army, Navy, Coast Guard, PEB and Marine Corps.

Tap a **test card** to open the **test setup page**. **Make sure the physical parameters displayed on
that page are correct: this data may affect the course of the test and its results.** If the data is
not accurate, press **"Edit Physical Data"** to adjust the numbers.

## Before the test

- **Make sure you are in good health;** check with your physician before performing any exercise if
  you are over the age of 35 or have any pre-existing health conditions.
- **Make sure you have warmed up and stretched** before taking the test.
- **Do not take in caffeine** before the test.

**This manual prints no "during the test" or "after the test" guidance,** and no hand-grip
instruction. Do not answer those from the CT900ENT or the 2026 commercial cards: those machines
print a 3-minute warm-up **at 3 mph**, where this manual says only "at a lower speed" - a different
figure. See `ct900ent-fitness-test-before-during-after-guidelines` and
`spirit-2026t-programs-fitness-test-before-during-after`.

The XT485ENT owner's manual documents no fitness tests at all.
