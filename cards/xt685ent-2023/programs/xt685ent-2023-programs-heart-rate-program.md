**The Heart Rate program uses incline, not speed, to control your heart rate.** The incline
gradually increases until you reach your target heart rate, then adjusts automatically to **keep you
within 5 bpm of your goal**.

**A heart rate monitor is required for this program.**

On the program setup page you can either:

- **set the target bpm directly**, or
- **select 55%, 65%, 75% or 85% of your age-predicted maximum heart rate** and let the machine
  calculate the target for you.

If you choose the percentage option, **make sure the machine knows how old you are, otherwise the
calculation is based on default values.**

**The program finishes when the time is up, or if your heart rate is significantly higher than the
target.**

Three Spirit consoles print three different answers here, so check which machine you have:

- **This console** - one Heart Rate program, offering 55 / 65 / 75 / 85% or a typed bpm, holding
  within 5 bpm, driving **incline**.
- **XT485ENT** - two programs, HR1 at 60% and HR2 at 80%; see
  `xt485ent-2023-programs-heart-rate-programs`.
- **Button-console XT385, XT485, XT685** - four programs, HR1 to HR4 at 55 / 65 / 75 / 85%, holding
  within 3-5 bpm; see `xt-2023-programs-heart-rate-program-xt385-xt485-xt685`.
