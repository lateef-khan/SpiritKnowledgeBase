Section 6.1 names eleven sockets on the photograph: **SPEED HANDRAIL WIRE**, **USB BOARD SOCKET**,
**SPEAKER SOCKET**, **KEYBOARD WIRE SOCKET**, **GROUNDING**, **HAND PULSE WIRES SOCKET**, **COMPUTER
CABLE SOCKET**, **AUDIO OUTPUT**, **DC FAN SOCKET**, **SAFETY KEY WIRE SOCKET** and **HR WIRELESS
BOARD**.

Section 6.2 is a photograph with no call-outs, and its second page (PDF p. 13) is blank.

No pin definitions are printed; the only pin table in the book is the 5-pin computer cable. Against the
XT485ENT board this one drops the incline handrail wire, the MP3 and Bluetooth audio boards and the 12 V
transfer board, and adds an audio output.
