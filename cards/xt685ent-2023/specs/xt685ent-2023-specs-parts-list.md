**Do not use the base XT685-2023 parts list for this machine.** The two lists
look almost the same and are not. 178 item numbers appear on both;
**35 of those 178 carry a different description or a different quantity**, and
the console sub-numbers are renumbered wholesale:

fan connecting cables, but each list assigns the black and the white cable to the
opposite number. See `xt685-2023-parts-list` for the base machine's list.

**On the printed numbering.** 193 rows are printed. The whole numbers that
appear are 1-13, 15, 17, 19-21, 23-30, 32-33, 36-38, 41-46, 48-54, 56-60, 62,
64-79, 81, 83-91, 93-94, 96-104, 106-107, 109-110, 112-114, 117, 123-129,
131-132, 151-153, 163, 165-166, 168, 170-174, 178-182 and 300-301. The
sub-numbers are 22-01; 33-01; 36-01, 36-03, 36-04, 36-10 to 36-18, 36-25 to
36-28; 37-01, 37-02, 37-04 to 37-08, 37-11 to 37-20, 37-35, 37-36, 37-38;
39-01 and 39-04; 169-01 to 169-09 and 169-11 to 169-23; and 170-1 to 170-8.
Any number outside that run is not printed in this manual at all - it is not a
missing page.

**The 170 handlebar sub-numbers are written with one digit here** (`170-1`
through `170-8`) where every other sub-number in the same list is written with
two (`37-01`, `169-01`). Both forms are the manual's own and are reproduced as
printed; the base XT685-2023 list writes the same eight parts as `170-01` to
`170-08`.

**Four printing faults, left as printed.**

- **Items 36 and 36-01 carry the same description**, "Console Top Cover(big)".
  Check the exploded view before ordering either.
- **Item 84 is cut off**, as "Ø6 × Ø23 × Ø13 × 5.5T × 3T_Nylon Dished" with no
  "Washer" at the end. The word is not on the page.
- **Item 65 and item 85 are printed across two lines**, with "Steel)" and
  "Washer" carried to the following row. They are rejoined here.
- **Items 113 and 114 are printed across two lines** as
  "150/400m/m_Motor Fan Connecting Ca-" then "ble(Black)" and "ble(White)". They
  are rejoined here.

**This list has no lubricant, no chest strap and no audio cable in it, and no
numbered cup holder** - though the Parts Included page names a lubricant tube
and the console overview names a cup holder. Do not fill those gaps from the
XT485ENT list; that is a different machine's numbering
(`xt485ent-2023-specs-parts-list`).
