**This is the ENT machine's wiring, not the XT685-2023's.** The two share their
hardware kit, their bolt counts and their steps 3 and 4 word for word, but
**every cable connection in steps 1 and 2 is different**. The XT685-2023 lands
its speed and incline cables on Speed Cable (Upper) (34) and Incline Cable
(Upper) (35); this machine lands both of them on the same Handrail Wire (36-18)
and adds a ground wire that the other does not have. Working from the wrong card
will have you hunting for connectors that are not on your machine.

Item numbers in brackets are from the exploded view. This machine does not fold,
so there is no Velcro shipping strap to cut; it is shipped with bolted-on
stabilizer plates instead.

**Step 1 - shipping plates, uprights and gussets.** Remove **4 pcs of
3/8" x 2 1/4" Flat Head Socket Bolts (102)** using the L Allen Wrench (104), then
remove the 2 Fixing Plate Stabilizers (101) on both sides of the unit.

Install the Right and Left Uprights (4, 5) into the Upright Cover (L, R) (52, 53)
and Console Mast Side Cap (L & R) (98, 99). Connect the Computer Cable (Middle)
(33) and the Computer Cable (Lower) (32).

Insert the Right and Left Uprights (4, 5) onto the Mainframe (1) and use the
Combination Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (103) to tighten **four
5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolts (100)** together with **four
Ø8 x 23 x 1.5T Curved Washers (124)**. Then continue with the L Allen Wrench
(104) to tighten the left and right Gusset (7, 8) with **six 3/8" x 2 1/2" Button
Head Socket Bolts (123)** together with **six Ø3/8" x Ø19 x 1.5T Flat Washers
(79)** and **six Ø10 x 2.0T Spring Washers (86)**.

**Step 2 - console.** Connect the Speed Cable (170-7) into the Handrail Wire
(36-18). Connect the Incline Cable (170-8) into the Handrail Wire (36-18).
Connect the Computer Cable (Middle) (33) and the Computer Cable (Middle & Upper)
(33-01).

Insert Console Assembly (36) into the Right and Left Uprights (4) and (5) and
secure with **4 pcs of 3/8" x 1 1/2" Button Head Socket Bolts (91)** with **4 pcs
of Ø10 x 2.0T Split Washers (86)**, using the L Allen Wrench (104).
**NOTE: do NOT tighten all screws yet.**

Then make five more connections:

| From | To |
|---|---|
| 400/350mm Handrail Wire (37-19) | Handrail Wire (36-18) |
| 12P Connecting Wire (37-12) | 12P Connecting Wire (36-16) |
| 6P Connecting Wire (37-13) | 6P Connecting Wire (36-17) |
| 5P Connecting Cable (Upper) (37-28) | Connecting Cable (Middle & Upper) (33-01) |
| Ground Wire (37-20) | Console Ground Wire (180) |

Place Console Assembly (Top) (37) onto the Console Assembly (Bottom) (36) and use
the Phillips Head Screw Driver (126) to tighten **4 pcs of M5 x 45mm Phillips
Head Screws (165)**.

**Step 3 - handrail support.** Install the Handrails Support (3) between the
Uprights (R, L) (4, 5) with **5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolts (100)**,
using the Combination Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (103). The printed sentence
reads "the 4pcs of 5 5/16" x 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolts (100)" - the count is
garbled here exactly as it is in the XT685-2023 manual. The hardware panel
allocates **4 pcs** of item 100 to step 3.

**Step 4 - caps, covers and tablet holder.** Install the Console Mast Side Cap
(R) and (L), (99) and (98), on the Main Frame (1) and secure with **4 pcs of
M5 x 10mm Phillips Head Screws (127)**, using the Phillips Head Screw Driver
(126). Install the Upright Cover (L) and (R), (52) and (53), on the right and
left Uprights (4) and (5) and Console Support (6) with **8 pcs of Ø3.5 x 16L
Sheet Metal Screws (125)**, same screwdriver. Place the Tablet Holder (169) on
Console Assembly (Top) (37) and tighten **3 pcs of M5 x 25mm Phillips Head Screws
(166)**.

**NOTE: tighten all screws only after all components have been assembled.**

## One thing the manual does not line up

The step text uses **(36)** for the Console Assembly and then for the Console
Assembly (Bottom), and **(37)** for the Console Assembly (Top). The parts list
gives item **37** as the Console Assembly. Follow the step text: the top half
that carries the screen is 37, and the half you bolt to the uprights is 36.
