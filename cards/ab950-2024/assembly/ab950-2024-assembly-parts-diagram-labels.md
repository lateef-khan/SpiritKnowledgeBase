## Three names that appear nowhere else in the book

**Swing Arm**, **Shroud** and **Hand Grips**. The assembly steps call the same arm a
**HANDLEBAR (3, 4)** and never say swing arm; the maintenance chapter says shroud once;
the hand grips are unnamed in every step. **Foot Peg** is the one that matters for use -
it is where your feet go when riding the arms only, and the instructions page calls the
same part a "front foot platform".

**E is an adjustment, not a part.** Seat Adjustments points at the pop-pin and slide
rather than at a component, the same way the CIC diagrams call out SEAT HEIGHT ADJ.

## K and L are where the moving and levelling instruction acts

Transport Wheels sit on the front leg and Levelers under both legs - four of them. See
[that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-airbike-jb950-assembly-moving-and-levelling.md).

## The 2018 AB900 has no such page

The older air bike manual prints no labelled drawing, so these names cannot be carried
back to it - and the AB950 machine has armrest handles and pedal bars the AB900 lacks.
See [that card](../../ab900-2018/assembly/ab900-2018-assembly-no-parts-list-or-labelled-diagram.md).
