Six tools, drawn in a TOOLS panel on the PARTS INCLUDED page:

- **#93. Phillips Head Screw driver** (1 pc)
- **#94. 10mm Wrench** (1 pc)
- **#100. 14/15mm Wrench** (1 pc)
- **#126. #4 L Allen Wrench** (1 pc)
- **#157. #8 L Allen Wrench** (1 pc)
- **#158. 13/15mm Wrench** (1 pc)

## Two tools the AB900 carton does not hold, and one it does

- **#157, the #8 L Allen wrench**, is new: it drives the M10 x 40L socket head cap bolts
  into the armrest handles, a part the AB900 has not got.
- **#158, the 13/15mm wrench**, is new: the AB950 fastens the seat to the sliding seat
  mount with it, where the AB900 uses its 14/15mm (100).
- **The AB900's #95 22mm wrench is gone.** It existed only to fasten the handlebars over
  a star washer, and the AB950 has no star washer.

## Nothing you must supply yourself

The AB950 prints **no rubber mallet note**. The AB900 asks for one for the pedals and the
JB950 recommends a large pedal wrench; this book asks for neither, and names no tool you
do not already have in the box.

## The #4 and #8 are wrench numbers, not millimetres

Both L Allen wrenches are given as **#4** and **#8**. The AB900 calls its equivalent an
**M4 L Allen Wrench**, so #4 is 4mm; nothing in the AB950 book states the size of the #8.
