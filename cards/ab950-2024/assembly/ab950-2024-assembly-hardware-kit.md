The AB950 prints its hardware as a picture list on the PARTS INCLUDED page rather than as
a table under each step.

| Step | Part | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 50 | 3/8" x 53L | 2 |
| 1 | 71 | 3/8" x 25 x 2T | 4 |
| 1 | 84 | 3/8" x 2-1/4" | 2 |
| 1 | 135 | 3/8" | 2 |
| 2 | 90 | M6 x 10L | 3 |
| 3 | 75 | M6 | 2 |
| 3 | 81 | 5/16" x 20 x 3T | 2 |
| 3 | 136 | 8 x 20L | 2 |
| 3 | **159** | **M10 x 40L** | 2 |
| 4 | 58 | M5 x 12L | 4 |
| 4 | 90 | M6 x 10L | 2 |

The AB900's tables name the types: 50 is a carriage bolt, 84 a hex head bolt, 135 a cap
nut, 71 and 81 flat washers, 75 a nut, 136 a shoulder bolt, 58 and 90 Phillips head
screws. **The AB950 page prints sizes and quantities only.** The step text calls 159 a
**SOCKET HEAD CAP BOLT** and 75 a **NYLON NUT** where the AB900 table says nyloc.

## What changed from the AB900 kit

**The star washer (148) is gone** and **two M10 x 40L socket head cap bolts (159)** take
its place. The 159s fasten the **left and right armrest handles (51, 52)** to the
handlebars - parts the AB900 does not have. Every other row is identical between the two
kits.

The **OVAL END CAPS (54)**, **PEDAL BARS (48)** and **ROD END BEARINGS (64)** that step
three fits are not in this panel; they arrive attached or loose with the arms.

## Steps that use the same screw twice

Screw **90** is drawn under both STEP2 (3 pcs) and STEP4 (2 pcs) - five in the box, two
different jobs.
