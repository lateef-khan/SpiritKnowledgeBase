> **NOTE: The pedal to the crank arm bolt torque is: 33-37 lb-ft.**

**This figure is printed in the maintenance chapter, not in the assembly step.** The
assembly step says only to fasten the pedals onto the crank arms with wrench (100), with
no figure and no reverse-thread warning; see
[that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-airbike-jb950-assembly-pedals-no-reverse-thread-warning.md).

## The re-torque interval printed with it

> Verify that the pedals are re-torqued **after the first 10 hours of use and every 100
> hours of operation, thereafter**. Use a torque wrench if components are loose.
>
> **IMPORTANT: It is not recommended that members change out the pedals on the bike.**

The JB950 manual prints the same interval and the same warning **without any figure**.
The AB900 prints neither.

## This is not the 300 in-lb figure from the other Spirit bikes

**33-37 lb-ft is roughly 396-444 in-lb.** The Spirit CR900, CR900ENT, CU900 and CU900ENT
manuals print **300 in-lb (34 N-m)** for their pedals - a different machine, a different
crank, and about a third less. Do not substitute one for the other. See
[that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-bike-assembly-pedal-torque-300-in-lb.md).

**No torque wrench is in the AB950 carton.** The six supplied tools are two Allen
wrenches, two open wrenches and a screwdriver; the maintenance chapter lists a torque
wrench among the tools an owner should keep, and this is the one figure in the book that
needs one.
