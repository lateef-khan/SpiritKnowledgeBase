**The AB950 has no written list of major components.** Its PARTS INCLUDED page is a
drawing of the **fasteners and tools only**, grouped under STEP1, STEP2, STEP3, STEP4 and
TOOLS. There is no "1 Main Frame, 1 Front Stabilizer" list of the kind the CIC and XIC600
manuals print.

- The fasteners under each step heading are on
  [the hardware card](ab950-2024-assembly-hardware-kit.md).
- The six tools under TOOLS are on
  [the tools card](ab950-2024-assembly-tools-needed.md).

## What to use to identify a large part

The next page, PARTS OF YOUR BIKE, labels twelve parts of the assembled machine A to L.
That is the closest thing to a component list this book has; see
[that card](ab950-2024-assembly-parts-diagram-labels.md). Between the two pages, and the
item numbers inside the four assembly steps, every part in the carton can be named.

## The AB900 has neither page

The 2018 AB900 manual prints no hardware picture list and no labelled drawing at all -
its pre-assembly promises a parts list and never gives one. See
[that card](../../ab900-2018/assembly/ab900-2018-assembly-no-parts-list-or-labelled-diagram.md).
