1. Place all parts from the box in a **cleared area** and position them on the floor in
   front of you.
2. Remove all packing materials from your area and put them back into the box. **Do not
   dispose of the packing materials until assembly is completed.**
3. **Read each step carefully before beginning.**

That is the whole page.

## The 2018 AB900 page said a great deal more

The AB900 pre-assembly runs to three items plus four NOTE and CAUTION blocks, and the
2024 book keeps none of them:

- **"Position the shipping carton so the Heavy End is located at the bottom."** Gone.
- **The caution that damage during assembly is not covered by the warranty**, and the
  instruction not to drop or lean the bike on its side. Gone. See
  [that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-airbike-jb950-assembly-damage-during-assembly-is-not-covered.md).
- **The note that a rubber mallet is needed for the pedals** and is not in the box. Gone.
- **The instruction to call service with the serial number if parts are missing.** Gone
  from this page; the front of the book carries an Online Support page instead.

**None of that means the AB950 build is different** - steps one, two and four are word
for word the AB900's. It means the warnings were dropped from the printing, so an
installer working only from the 2024 book will not be told which way up the carton goes
or that assembly damage is uninsured.
