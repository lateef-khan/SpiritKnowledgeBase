**Three stickers, and all three are on the back of the machine.**

| Sticker | Where it is | What it carries |
|---|---|---|
| Serial number | **Near the rear stabilizer** | The number below the barcode, and (870) 935-1107 |
| Proposition 65 warning | **Near the rear stabilizer** | That the unit was manufactured using chemicals that could cause harm with improper use |
| Safety warning | **On the rear of the Bike's shroud** | The same warning printed in the manual |

> **SERIAL NUMBER STICKER** - This sticker will be found near the rear stabilizer. Please record the
> number below the barcode for the purpose of registering your Bike's warranty.
>
> **PROPOSITION 65 WARNING** - This sticker will be found near the rear stabilizer. The State of
> California requires us to inform you that this unit was manufactured using chemicals that could
> cause harm with improper use.
>
> **SAFETY WARNING STICKER** - This sticker will be found on the rear of your Bike's shroud. The
> same warning can be found in this manual. Please read and be aware of the precautions before
> operating your unit.

**This is the rear of the machine, not the front.** The commercial CR900, CU900 and CU800ENT put
their serial number and Prop 65 stickers on the **front** stabilizer and their safety warning on the
console or the upright (`spirit-bike-2024-2025-safety-product-labels`). Sending an AB950 owner to
the front stabilizer sends them to the wrong end of the bike.

## What the safety warning sticker actually says

The sticker is drawn on the page as a picture. Recovered from a 300 dpi render, English above
French:

> **SERIOUS INJURY COULD OCCUR IF THESE PRECAUTIONS ARE NOT OBSERVED.**
>
> - Consult your physician before use.
> - Stop immediately if you become dizzy or experience chest pains and consult your physician.
> - Heart Rate monitoring systems may be inaccurate, use them for reference only.
> - Read all warnings on the unit and be properly instructed or read the Owner's Manual on how to
>   use before beginning.
> - Inspect this machine for damage prior to use.
> - Keep body, clothing, and fitness accessories clear of moving parts.
> - Risk of personal injury - Keep children under the age of 13 away from machine.

**That is the same decal the AB900 and JB950 manuals call the EXERCISE WARNING DECAL**, word for
word (`spirit-air-bike-safety-warning-labels-and-stickers`). The page's own claim that `The same
warning can be found in this manual` is not quite right: the manual's numbered SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS
list covers the same ground but does not print these seven sentences.

**The Prop 65 sticker text is the short form.** It names **no chemical** and prints **no web
address** in the descriptive paragraph, though the drawn sticker itself reads `WARNING: Cancer and
Reproductive Harm - www.P65Warnings.ca.gov`. It is not the Toluene and Acrylamide box the AB900,
JB950, CIC and XIC600 manuals print inside the book
(`xt-2015-safety-proposition-65-warning`). The two are not interchangeable in either direction.
