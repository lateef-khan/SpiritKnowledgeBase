The manual lists the main causes in this order:

1. **Screw kits not fixed.** Check them.
2. Check that all four **Adjustment Foot Pads (59)** are fixed to the ground, and adjust each of the nuts.
3. Tie the **Seat Up/Down Adjustment Knob (47)**. If it makes noise, apply some lubrication at the position of the screw.
4. **Noise from the slide.** Clean the **Aluminum Track Pulley (53)**, **Pulley (54)** and **Aluminum Board (52)**. If that does not work, replace the Aluminum Track Pulley (53) and Pulley (54) directly.
5. **Noise from the Fan (22) or Flywheel (23).** Release the **Chain Cover (L) (71)** and **Chain Cover (R) (72)** and check.
