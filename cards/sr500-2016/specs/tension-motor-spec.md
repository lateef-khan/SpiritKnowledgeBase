Working voltage: **DC 4.5~7.5V**. It increases and decreases the resistance level of the brake.

The cable tensioner also **supplies the console with DC12V power**. That is why a dead console can be a tensioner fault: the quick lookup table asks "Has the cable tensioner output DC12V, if not, please replace the new cable tensioner."

One part, three names in this manual: **tension motor** in section 3, **cable tensioner** in the E2 section, **Gear Motor (35)** in the parts replacement steps.
