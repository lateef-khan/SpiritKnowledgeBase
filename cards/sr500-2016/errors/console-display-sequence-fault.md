The printed question is short: **"Q With a sequence of displays"**.

The answer: **check the connections with Board (34) or other parts. If they are not good, replace.**

**What the symptom means is not spelled out.** The manual gives no further description of what "a sequence of displays" looks like, and Board (34) is not named in the section 1 outline. Board (34) is the same part the console holder procedure tells you to separate the computer cables from, and on the 2023 SR550 the equivalent part is called the Optical Coupler Board (34).
