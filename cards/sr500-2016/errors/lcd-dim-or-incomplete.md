Happening as printed: "LCD display does not shine, incomplete or imperfect."

Causes:

1. LCD backlight damage.
2. Console power is too low.

Processing steps, in the manual's order:

1. Replace the new LCD module or the control electronics.
2. Check whether the **AC power input is AC100 ~ 240V** and the **output is DC12V**.
3. Check the power of the console.
4. Check whether the **cable tensioner outputs DC12V**; if not, replace it with a new cable tensioner.
