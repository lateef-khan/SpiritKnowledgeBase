The manual answers this in two places and the two answers complement each other.

**Section 7.6, console (electronic desk) problem.** If the screen does not light up:

1. Check the power adapter is supplying normally, or swap in another adapter to check.
2. Check the adapter is correctly inserted into the **DC jack**.
3. Check all the wires are indeed plugged into the console.
4. Remove the console cover and check whether all the wires are inserted in the correct location, and whether any wire is fractured.

**Section 8-10, "No displays".** Check the connection with the **750mm DC Power Cord (39)**, the **Gear Motor (35)** and the **500mm Computer Cable (Upper) (44)**.

The gear motor appears in this list because on this machine the cable tensioner supplies the console with DC12V.
