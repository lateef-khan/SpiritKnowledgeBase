Checks, in the manual's order:

1. Check that the **receiving board is in the correct position**.
2. Check whether it is disturbed by a nearby source of interference - the manual names **fluorescent lamp rectifiers and cable interference power**.
3. Check the cable connection.
4. Replace the **RF radio receiver module**.

This is the wireless link from the resistance buttons on the handle. When it fails the symptom the customer reports is usually that the resistance will not change, so read the no-adjustable-resistance card as well.
