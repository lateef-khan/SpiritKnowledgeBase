Happening as printed: "Wireless heartbeat has no effect (The heartbeat value is not displayed on the monitor)."

| Cause | Processing step |
|---|---|
| 1. The heart rate strap is not properly worn. | Check the strap is in proper contact with the skin and in the right direction. |
| 2. The distance is too far above the receiver. | **Keep the chest strap within 3 feet of the console.** |
| 3. The battery of the heart rate strap is low or has no voltage. | Replace with a new **lithium battery type CR2032**. |

A separate row covers **reception that is too short**, where the strap must be very close to the console: the cause is a low battery and the fix is the same CR2032.

Section 7.7 lists the same checks in order: strap worn in place, battery sufficient, range not exceeded, wireless heartbeat module in the correct position, and finally replace the receiver module.
