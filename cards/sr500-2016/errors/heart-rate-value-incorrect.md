Happening as printed: "The heartbeat display value is incorrect."

| Cause | Processing step |
|---|---|
| 1. May be receiving another heart rate strap signal. | Check that no other heart rate strap is in use around the machine. |
| 2. There may be other electric field noise interference. | Try changing the machine direction or position. |
| 3. The wireless heartbeat receiver is damaged. | Replace with a new wireless heartbeat receiver. |

Section 7.7 adds a related symptom - heart rate unexpectedly increasing when the keypad is pressed - and asks you to **confirm whether the keypad sound is too loud**, as well as checking the receiving board position and nearby interference before replacing the receiver module.
