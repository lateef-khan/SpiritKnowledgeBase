A profile mode run against a time you set.

1. Press **Start/Stop** to begin, or set the workout time first. Use **UP/DOWN** to adjust the time in **5-minute increments, up to a 99:00 maximum**. Press Start/Stop to start.
2. The centre of the LCD shows the **strength profile**.
3. The Time window counts down from the set time and shows the remaining time.
4. At 0:00 the workout completes and the buzzer sounds one long beep. **If paddling continues the count-down repeats.**

**The manual does not say resistance can be adjusted by hand in this mode.** Fat Burn and Cardio both carry a step saying UP or DOWN adjusts the resistance level; the Strength steps omit it. Treat that as unstated rather than forbidden.
