Press the Start/Stop button from idle to begin, **or simply pull the paddle** - pulling the handle under idle mode enters Manual workout mode directly.

The image at the centre of the LCD scans every 5 seconds between the stroke speed wave pattern and 500M/TIME. Pressing **MODE** cancels the scan and leaves the wave pattern only; pressing MODE again switches to 500M/TIME.

Press **UP** or **DOWN** to adjust the resistance level, shown at the bottom right corner of the LEVEL window.
