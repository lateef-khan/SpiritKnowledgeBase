A profile mode run against a time you set.

1. Press **Start/Stop** to begin, or set the workout time first. Use **UP/DOWN** to adjust the time in **5-minute increments, up to a 99:00 maximum**. Press Start/Stop to start.
2. The centre of the LCD shows the **cardio profile**.
3. Pressing UP or DOWN during the workout adjusts the resistance level.
4. The Time window counts down from the set time and shows the remaining time.
5. At 0:00 the workout completes and the buzzer sounds one long beep. **If paddling continues the count-down repeats.**
