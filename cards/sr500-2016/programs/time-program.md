A target time count-down mode.

1. Use the **UP/DOWN** buttons to set the workout time. The **default is 1:00**, adjusted in **1-minute increments up to a 99:00 maximum**.
2. Press **Start/Stop** to confirm and start.
3. The centre of the LCD scans every 5 seconds between the wave pattern and 500M/TIME; MODE holds one or the other.
4. Pressing UP or DOWN during the workout adjusts the resistance level.
5. The Time window counts down from the set time and shows the remaining time.
6. At 0:00 the workout completes and the buzzer sounds one long beep. **If paddling continues the count-down repeats.**

The printed step reads "The default distance is 1:00"; 1:00 is a time, and this is the Time mode.
