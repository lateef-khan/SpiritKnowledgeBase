A target calorie count-down mode.

1. Use the **UP/DOWN** buttons to set the target calorie. The **default value is 100** and it changes in **increments of 10**.
2. Press **Start/Stop** to confirm and start.
3. The centre of the LCD scans every 5 seconds between the wave pattern and 500M/TIME; MODE holds one or the other.
4. The Calorie window counts down from the target and shows the remaining calories.
5. Pressing UP or DOWN during the workout adjusts the resistance level.
6. At 0 the workout completes and the buzzer sounds one long beep. **If paddling continues the count-down repeats.**
