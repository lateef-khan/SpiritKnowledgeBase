A fixed interval mode of **20 seconds exercise / 10 seconds rest**.

1. Press **Start/Stop** to start the mode.
2. The centre of the LCD shows the count-down of the current exercise period with the wave, or the rest time count-down with the rest mark.
3. Pressing UP or DOWN during the workout adjusts the resistance level.
4. There are **10 exercise/rest cycles** for each workout time.
5. When the workout completes the buzzer sounds one long beep. **If paddling continues the count-down repeats.**

This is the 20/10 mode. The next mode in the list is 10/20, which reverses the two periods.
