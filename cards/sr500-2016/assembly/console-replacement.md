1. Take out the **3.5 x 12L Sheet Metal Screw (112) x4pcs** from the **Console (43)** with a driver, working on the top cover of the console, then remove the **500mm Computer Cable (Upper) (44)** and the wireless cables or receivers.
2. To take out the **Console Assembly (43)**, remove the **3/8" x UNC16 x 3-3/4" Socket Head Cap Bolt (83)** with the **8mm L Allen wrench**. Separate the 500mm Computer Cable (Upper) (44) from the controller.
3. To remove the **Console Holder Assembly**, take out Chain Cover (L) and Chain Cover (R) first. Then separate the **500mm Computer Cable (Upper) (44)** and the **500mm Computer Cable (Lower) (45)**, apart from the **Board (34)**, and take out the 3/8" x UNC16 x 3-3/4" Socket Head Cap Bolt (83). The **Console Holder Assembly (7)** and **Console Assembly (43)** come away together.
4. Reverse the above steps. **Note that the 3/8" x UNC16 x 3-3/4" Socket Head Cap Bolt (83) and the 500mm Computer Cable (Upper) can interfere with each other on the way through; the manual says this needs technique.**
