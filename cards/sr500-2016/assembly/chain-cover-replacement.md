1. Remove the **Ø5 x 16L Tapping Screw (111) x2pcs** from the **Chain Cover (L) (71)** with a Phillips head screwdriver, and take apart the **3.5 x 12L Sheet Metal Screw (112) x9pcs** from the **Chain Cover (R) (72)**. The Chain Cover (L) (71) then comes off completely.
2. Loosen the **3/8" x UNC16 x 3-3/4" Socket Head Cap Bolt (83)** with the **8mm L Allen wrench**, pushing the **3/8" x 11T Nyloc Nut**. **Do not take the screw out.**
3. Remove the nut of the DC power cord with a **15mm wrench**, then take out the **Ø5 x 16L Tapping Screw (111) x2pcs** with the Phillips screwdriver. The Chain Cover (R) can then be removed.
4. Reverse the above steps to install Chain Cover (L) (71) and Chain Cover (R) (72). **The chain cover foam must aim at the hole.**
