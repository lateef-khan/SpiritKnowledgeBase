1. Take out the **3.5 x 12L Sheet Metal Screw (112) x2pcs** and the **Sheet Metal Screw (132) x2pcs** from the **Controller Assembly (36)** with a driver, and pass the handle through the **Ribbon Roll (30)**.

   **Pass the Handle (10) through the Ribbon Roll (30) and fix it on the Hook (9)**, to stop the Ribbon Roll (30) retracting back into the chain cover.
2. To change the **PVC Sleeve (50)**, remove the **Controller Assembly (36)** directly.
3. **To get at the battery only**, remove the **3.5 x 12L Sheet Metal Screw (112) x1pc** from the **Battery Cover (36~3)**.
