1. Use a **5mm wrench** to take out the **M8 x P1.25 x 12L Button Head Socket Bolt (123) x6pcs** and the **Ø5/16" x Ø18 x 1.5T Flat Washer (122) x6pcs**. This releases the **Pedal (L) and (R)**, **Pedal (68)**, **Pedal Plate (69)** and **Wire Tie Mount (67)**.
2. Use a driver to take out the **M4 x 5L Phillips Head Screw (113) x4pcs**, the **Pedal (68)**, **Pedal Plate (69)** and **Wire Tie Mount (67)**.
3. Use a driver to take out the **M5 x 12L Flat Head Socket Screw (107) x4pcs** and the **Wire Tie Mount (67)**.
4. Reverse the above steps to resume the parts.
