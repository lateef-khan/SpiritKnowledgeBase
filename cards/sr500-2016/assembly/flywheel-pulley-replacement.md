1. **Adjust the resistance to the highest on the console and remove the power cord.** Take out Chain Cover (L) (71), Chain Cover (R) (72), Handle (10), Controller Assembly (36), and Console Holder Assembly (7) with Console Assembly (43).
2. Loosen the **M6 x 6T Nyloc Nut (103)** and the **3/8" x UNF26 x 11T Nut (82)** with a **10/15mm wrench**.
3. Take out the **M8 x P1.25 x 20L Socket Head Cap Bolt (84) x4pcs** with a **6mm L Allen wrench**. Move forward the **Flywheel Pulley (25)**, **Latch (26)**, **Spring Latch (27)**, **Attaching Plate (8L)** and **Attaching Plate (8R)**.
4. Hold the Flywheel Pulley (25) by hand and take out the **M6 x P1.0 x 18L Button Head Socket Bolt (89)** with a **4mm L Allen wrench**. Take out the **M5 x 10L Phillips Head Screw (86) x3pcs** with a driver and remove the **Attaching Plate (8R)** with **Bearing (6201 UOU)**.
5. Take out the **M5 x 10L Phillips Head Screw (86) x2pcs** with a driver. Separate the Attaching Plate (8R) with Bearing (6201 UOU).
6. Remove the **Ø20 x 1.2t C Ring (108)** with a clip, and also the **Ø20 x 29 x 0.3T Wave Washer (96)**, **Spring Latch (27)** and **Latch (26)**.
7. On reassembly:
   - watch the position of the **Spring Latch (27)** against the **Attaching Plate (8R)**;
   - fix Attaching Plate (8L) and (8R) **after** putting the **Drive Belt 6PJ-787L (24)** on the Flywheel Pulley (25);
   - tie up the **3/8" x UNF26 x 11T Nut (82)** and adjust the **M6 x 6T Nyloc Nut (103)**. Adjust the drive belt to the **tension value of 70~80BLS** against the Flywheel (23), then tie up the 3/8" x UNF26 x 11T Nut (82).

**Steps 4 and 5 both remove the same plate with a different screw count.** Step 4 says x3pcs of the M5 x 10L Phillips Head Screw (86) for the Attaching Plate (8R), step 5 says x2pcs for the same plate. Count what is in front of you.
