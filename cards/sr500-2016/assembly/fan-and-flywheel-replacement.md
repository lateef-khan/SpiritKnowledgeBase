1. **Adjust the console resistance to the highest and remove the power cord.** Take out Chain Cover (L) (71) and Chain Cover (R) (72).
2. Pull the **Steel Cable (66)** out of the **Flywheel (23)**.
3. Use a **10/15mm wrench** to loosen the **M6 x 6T Nyloc Nut (103)** and the **3/8" x UNF26 x 11T Nut (82)**.
4. Use a **6mm L Allen wrench** to take out the **M8 x P1.25 x 20L Socket Head Cap Bolt (84) x4pcs**. Move forward the **Flywheel Pulley (25)**, **Latch (26)**, **Spring Latch (27)** and **Attaching Plate (8L) (8R)** so the **Drive Belt (24)** can be removed.
5. Release the **Flywheel (23)** and **Fan (22)**.
6. Use a driver to remove the **M5 x P0.8 x 15L Phillips Head Screw (78) x6pcs**, and the Flywheel (23) and Fan (22) can be separated.
7. Reverse step 4 to resume the Flywheel Pulley, Latch and Spring Latch.
