1. Use a driver to take out the **M5 x 10L Phillips Head Screw (85) x3pcs** and the **Connecting Cover (63)**.
2. Use a **5mm wrench** to take out the **M8 x P1.25 x 12L Button Head Socket Bolt (123) x6pcs** and the **Ø5/16" x Ø18 x 1.5T Flat Washer (122) x6pcs**. This releases the **Aluminum Track (51)**, the seat sets **(49) (16) (53) (54)** and the **Rear Stabilizer (3)**.
3. Take the seat sets (49) (16) (53) (54) out at the front.
4. Use a **4mm wrench** to release the **M6 x P1.0 x 18L Button Head Socket Bolt (89) x2pcs** and the **M6 x P1.0 x 10L Button Head Socket Bolt (129) x4pcs**. Remove the **Seat Stop Cover (65)** and **Rear Stabilizer (3)**.
5. Use a **6mm wrench and a 14mm wrench** to take apart the **3/8" x UNC16 x 1-1/4" Button Head Socket Bolt (105)**, the **3/8" x 11T Nyloc Nut (106)** and the **Pulley (54)**.
6. Use a **6mm wrench and a 14mm wrench** to take apart the **3/8" x UNC16 x 4-1/4" Socket Head Cap Bolt (104)**, the **3/8" x 11T Nyloc Nut (106)** and the **Pulley (54)**.
7. Use a driver to take apart the **M6 x 15L Phillips Head Screw (124)**, the **Seat (49)** and the **Seat Attaching Board (16)**.
8. Reverse the above steps to resume the parts.

Steps 5 and 6 are two different pulley mountings with two different bolts - a 1-1/4" button head and a 4-1/4" socket head - not the same job written twice.
