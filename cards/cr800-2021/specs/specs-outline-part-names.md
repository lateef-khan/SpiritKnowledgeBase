One drawing, fifteen labels: **Console**, **Handpulse W/Cable Assembly**, **Mast Handle bar
Assembly**, **Seat Back**, **Slider Cover B**, **Seat Back Cover** (labelled twice), **Console Mast
Cover**, **Seat**, **Aluminum Track**, **Front Shroud (R)**, **Rear Stabilizer Cover (L)**,
**Pedal (R)**, **Rear Stabilizer Cover (R)**, **Beam Cover**, **Rear Shroud (R)**.

The *Beam Cover* hides the beam between the front and rear frame sections, under the aluminium
seat track; the replacement chapter numbers the track item 14 and the seat carriage item 4.

No skeleton drawing, no dimensions and no weights are printed. The recumbent and the upright
share their electrical pages but not their outlines: compare `cu800-2021-specs-outline-part-names`.
