Five steps, and none of them shares a hardware panel with the rest of the range.

**Step 1 - the two doorframes and two link spans.** 16 x M10x25 socket cap screws,
16 x Φ20xΦ10.5x2 flat washers, 16 x M10 spring washers. **Sixteen of each is the largest
single fastener count in the Spirit strength range.**

**Step 2 - the upper beam bracket** onto the two doorframes. 6 x M10x25 socket cap screws,
6 x flat washers, 6 x spring washers.

**Step 3 - both weight stacks at once.** Two roller hangers, **4 guide rods Φ20x1726**,
2 guide rod fixing plates, **32 ten-pound weight stacks**, 2 ten-pound counter weight stack
sets, 2 seventeen-hole shafts, **2 select pins**, 8 rod end covers, 4 rubber bumpers
Φ62xΦ20x26, and 4 each of M8x25 umbrella head hexagon socket screws, Φ24xΦ8.4x2 big
washers and M8 spring washers.

**Step 4 - the shrouds.** **2 black front shields, 20 ST4.2x16 cross recessed pan head
tapping screws and 20 Φ15xΦ5.3x1.2 big washers.**

**Step 5 - the accessories**: short handle set, knee cover set, pull back rope set, two
handle grips and a squat handle set.

## Nothing here matches the selectorized machines

- **Two towers, not one.** Four guide rods, two fixing plates, two selector pins, two
  counterweight sets - everything the single-stack machines have one of.
- **The shroud is tapping screws into plastic**, not the eight-row panel of countersunk
  machine screws and socket bolts that the twenty-eight weight-stack machines share. See
  [that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-strength-assembly-the-shroud-hardware-panel.md).
- **The rubber bumper is Φ62xΦ20x26**, four of them, against the two-sizes-two-each
  Φ64 and Φ38 pairs of the rest of the range.
- **No cable appears in any step table and there is no cable routing diagram** - on a
  two-tower cable machine. See
  [that card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-strength-assembly-no-cable-routing-diagram-on-the-functional-range.md).

Its workout placard part number is **#311006816**.

Its tools note contradicts its own unpacking list - see
[the tools card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-strength-assembly-three-tools-you-must-supply.md).
