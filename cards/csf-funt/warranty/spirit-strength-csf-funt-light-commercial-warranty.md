**Light Commercial.** The CSF-FUNT is the only one of the thirty-nine Spirit strength
manuals whose warranty page is headed

> WARRANTY - LIGHT COMMERCIAL

and whose table row is labelled **Light Commercial Warranty**. The other thirty-eight -
every CSD, CSS, CSI and the remaining five CSF books - head the page
*WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL* and label the row *Commercial Warranty*.

**The periods themselves are unchanged.** The Light Commercial row carries exactly the
four columns the Commercial CSD and CSF-HRAC tables carry, counter-timer included:
`spirit-strength-csd-warranty-periods-with-counter-timer`.

**So the label is the whole of the difference, and the manual never defines it.** No
page says what Light Commercial excludes that Commercial would allow, states an hours
per day limit, or names a facility type. The only environment wording in the book is
exclusion 2, which is worded identically to the other thirty-eight:
`spirit-strength-warranty-use-environment`.

Its effective date, December 29, 2023, is also the oldest of the thirty-six current-template
books: `spirit-strength-warranty-effective-dates`.
