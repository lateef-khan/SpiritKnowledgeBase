The manual's whole exercise instruction for this machine is one picture of the
placard fitted to the machine. The placard is titled **FUNCTIONAL TRAINER**.

It is a **pair** of tall placards, both headed FUNCTIONAL TRAINER, listing **ten**
exercises in two-frame drawings. Labels as printed, left placard then right:

| left placard | right placard |
|---|---|
| CHEST PRESS | TRICEPS |
| ROW | LEG SQUAT |
| SHOULDER PRESS | LEG BENDING |
| BILATERAL LAT PULL | TORSO ROTATION |
| BICEPS | ABDOMINAL |

**Placard part number #311006816**, printed beside the picture.

**No words go with the drawing.** The manual names no muscle, gives no starting
position in prose, no range of motion, no rep or set count and no warm-up. See
`spirit-strength-programs-exercise-instruction-is-the-machine-placard` for what
that means across the range.
