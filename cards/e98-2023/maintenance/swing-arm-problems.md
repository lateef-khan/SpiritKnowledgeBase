**Slight play is normal.** There are **0.05mm to 0.07 mm tolerances** in these parts so a user can assemble the machine easily. Noises may appear from these tolerances after a certain amount of use.

1. Add grease or thick lubricant on the **diameter 25 x 296L console mast shaft** for the handlebar.
2. If noises come from the **6005 bearing**, replace the bearing.
3. If a handlebar button does not function, tear off the sticker and use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the **M5x20mm flat head socket screw** which secures the handlebar button. Check that the handlebar button is connected with the lower cable properly.
4. Check that the upper and lower handle wires are connected properly and free from damage.
