The manual says a problem with this part is **rare**.

1. The most likely cause is improper **steel cable adjustment** causing noise or shifting from improper assembly. Follow the flywheel replacement procedure for the steel cable adjustment.
2. If there is noise with the flywheel, check for foreign material or abnormal rubbing. Replace the flywheel with a new one if the noise cannot be fixed.

**Point 1 does not fit this machine.** This is an EMS brake machine with no gear motor and no steel cable, and section 9.9 - the "flywheel replacement" it points at - describes removing the induction brake, not adjusting a cable. The paragraph is carried over from the gear-motor models. Point 2 still applies.
