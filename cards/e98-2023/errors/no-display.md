1. Make sure the power is plugged in properly and the **AC breaker is not activated**.
2. Make sure all connectors, including the cables for the console and the AC power, are connected properly. Disassemble the console to check the cables inside it if necessary.
3. If all connections are fine, check to determine whether a cable is broken, or the console or the lower controller has failed.

Check the fuse on the motor controller too; that has its own card.
