The matrix prints two rows for this symptom.

**TFT LCDs not bright, incomplete, or imperfect**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| 1. TFT LCD light is broken. | 1. Replace with new TFT LCD or console. |
| 2. Power to console too low. | 2. Check AC power is **110-120V**. 3. Check power to console. 4. Replace lower controller. |

**TFT LCD displays not bright, incomplete, or imperfect**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| TFT LCD displays are broken. | Replace with new console. |
