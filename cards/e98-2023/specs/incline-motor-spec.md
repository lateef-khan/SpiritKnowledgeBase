The manual writes it as **"This is a 115(220V)-volt AC motor"**. That is the full text of the line: the same sentence carries both figures, and the manual never says which market gets which. The safety and grounding sections of this same manual describe a **nominal 120-volt circuit** only.

It has four wires - red, black, white and green - plus one **3-pin position sensor cable**.

- AC voltage on the **red** wire (UP) makes the incline motor increase the incline.
- AC voltage on the **black** wire (DOWN) makes the incline motor decrease the incline.
- The **white** wire (COM) is neutral.
- The **green** wire is ground.

The E25, E35, E95 and E95s 2023 manuals print "115-volt AC motor" with no second figure. Confirm the machine's own rating plate before quoting 220V to anyone.
