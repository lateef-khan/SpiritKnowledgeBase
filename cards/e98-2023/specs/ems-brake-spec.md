**Work voltage: DC 0 ~ 21V.** It controls resistance increases and decreases.

The controller carries the DC power supply for the console, the incline driver and the **EMS Brake driver**, and links the console to output the appropriate voltages for the EMS brake.

**This is not a tension motor.** There is no gear motor and no steel cable on this machine. The E25, E35, E95 and E95s of the same year carry a gear motor at **DC 4.5~7.5V** pulling a steel cable; those figures do not apply here.

**The 2019 E98 manual prints a different figure for the same part: DC 0 ~ 23V.** This 2023 manual prints DC 0~21V. They are different machine years; use the figure for the year in front of you.

The parts replacing guide calls the same part the **induction brake**.
