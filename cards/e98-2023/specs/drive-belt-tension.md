**190 Hz, plus or minus 10 Hz**, measured with a sonic belt tensioner.

The manual gives a feel test as the equal alternative: tighten the **M8 nut on the J-bolt** until the drive belt is **lightly tensioned with a crisp sound**, or use the sonic belt tensioner to measure 190Hz (+/- 10).

The figure is printed once, inside the idler wheel assembly procedure. This manual has no separate Poly-V belt section - the belt comes off with the idler wheel.
