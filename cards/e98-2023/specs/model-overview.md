The cover reads **E98(2023)**, **SE868-SE042**, "ENT Elliptical Trainer". That factory code is the manual's own name for the machine. **ENT** is how the cover marks the entertainment console model.

This is the Sole E98 of model year 2023. The Spirit database SKU is **598022**.

What the manual says the machine is made of:

- **Console** - the interface that controls all functions. It holds key controls and a **TFT LCD touch panel**.
- **Controller** - the circuit board holding the DC power supply for the console, the incline driver and the **EMS Brake driver**.
- **EMS Brake** - increases and decreases the resistance level of the brake. There is **no gear motor and no steel cable** on this machine.
- **Incline motor** - an AC motor the user drives from the console to change elevation.

The E25, E35, E95 and E95s of the same year all use a gear motor pulling a steel cable. Their resistance parts and their resistance troubleshooting do not transfer to this machine.

This manual has no "Gear Motor connector definition function" section; the wiring chapter stops at 6.5 Driver Board function.
