**Press 10 times on the word "Settings"** while you are on the Settings page. There are four pages: FUNCTION, SERVICE, FACTORY SETTING and CALIBRATION.

**FUNCTION**

1. **Unit** - set the default unit.
2. **Odo** - records the ODO time and distance; press RESET to clear.
3. **Sleep Mode** - OFF, the screen never turns off. ON, no operation on the IDLE MODE page for **15 minutes** enters sleep and the screen goes off; any key wakes it. RETAIL, the display shows the RETAIL video full screen.
4. **Beep** - whether a physical button press beeps.
5. **Software Update** - COMMAND (reserved, no function), OSOTA (force a check for OS updates), USB (update from a flash drive through the Console APP).
6. **Machine Type** - switches the machine model. **Switching resets every setting to default and clears all local accounts and records, and they cannot be recovered.**

**SERVICE**

1. **Key Test** - press TEST, then press physical buttons; their values appear in the blank space.
2. **Motor Test** - fine-tuning and lifespan testing of the gear motor.
3. **Incline Test** - fine-tuning and lifespan testing of the incline motor.
4. **Sensor Test** - checks the hand pulse, the 5K wireless heart rate and the RPM sensor. Press TEST; a normal signal shows its value for about 1-2 seconds.

**FACTORY SETTING**

1. **Restore Factory** - every setting except the ODO records returns to default, and all local accounts and records are cleared.
2. **First Launch** - reserved, no function.
3. **Grade Return** - only on models with an incline motor; defines whether to reset the incline.
4. **Territory** - US version, Global version or Mandarin (Simplified Chinese) version. Switching resets all settings and clears all local accounts and records.
5. **BLE NAME** - the device name for local Bluetooth broadcasting. Press SAVE and the app restarts.

**CALIBRATION** holds one button, Incline Calibration. The result is displayed on the screen.
