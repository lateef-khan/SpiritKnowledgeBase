You can charge a personal device during a workout using the machine's wireless charger. **The fitness equipment power must be on.**

1. Make sure the device itself is wireless chargeable.
2. Put the device in **landscape**, inside the charge area.
3. If charging does not start, adjust the position of the device to match the charge circuit.

The manual gives no wattage, no supported-phone list and no troubleshooting beyond step 3.
