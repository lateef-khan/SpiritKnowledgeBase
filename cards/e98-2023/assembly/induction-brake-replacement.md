**The section is headed "Replacing the Flywheel", but every step in it removes the induction brake.** The manual never describes removing the flywheel itself on this machine.

1. Follow 9.3, 9.4, 9.6, 9.7 and 9.8 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, both side covers, cross bar, the belt and the idler wheel assembly.
2. First disconnect the wires connecting the induction brake. Then cut off the cable ties.
3. To remove the induction brake, use an **11mm wrench** to unscrew the **1/4" x 3/4" carbon steel hex screws** that secure it - **there are four screws in total**. Then remove **four 1/4" spring washers** and **four 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers**. This lets you detach the induction brake.
4. To reinstall, place the induction brake back onto the **flywheel mounting plate of the main frame assembly**. Secure it by tightening the four **1/4" x 3/4" carbon steel hex screws**, using four **1/4" spring washers** and four **1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers**. Reconnect the wires.
5. Follow the reverse of the steps above and **adjust the belt to the central position on the belt pulley**. Finally reinstall the other components.

The induction brake is the part section 3 calls the **EMS Brake**, working voltage DC 0~21V.
