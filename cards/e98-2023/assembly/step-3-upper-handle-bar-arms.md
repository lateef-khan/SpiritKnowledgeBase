1. Fit the left Swing Arm onto the left Lower Handle Bar with **Hex Head Bolts (133)**, **4pcs Flat Washers (170)**, **2pcs Curved Washers (173)** and **3pcs Nylon Nuts (159)** using **2 Wrenches (183 & 184)**. **Do the same way to secure the Swing Arm (R) (20)** on the right Lower Handle Bar. (The left arm's item number is cut off in the extraction; the right is (20).)
2. **Connect the Upper Resistance Handle Wire (187) with the Lower Resistance/Incline Handle Wire (186)**, store it in the frame tube, and **snap in the Switch Wire Cap (71)** on the console mast. Then connect the **Upper Incline Handle Wire (188)** with the Lower wire (186) the same way and snap in the second cap (71).
