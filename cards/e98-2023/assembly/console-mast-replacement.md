1. Remove the swing arm (section 9.2).
2. Reverse the owner's manual assembly instructions and use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the console mast cover.
3. Reverse the owner's manual assembly instructions and remove the console mast.
4. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release the two **diameter 3 x 20 tapping screws** which secure the hand pulse assembly, pull it out and disconnect the cables.
5. Unplug the round cap under the hand pulse tube and pull out the hand pulse cables to take the hand pulse sensor assembly apart.
6. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.
