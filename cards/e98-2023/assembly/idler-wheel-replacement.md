1. Follow 9.3, 9.4, 9.6 and 9.7 to take apart the connecting arm, pedal arm, both left and right side cases and the cross bar.
2. Use a **13 mm wrench** to loosen the **M8 x 7T nut on the M8x170mm J-bolt** together with the **5/16" x 23 x 1.5T flat washer** until the J-bolt is released. To remove the idler wheel assembly, use a Phillips screwdriver to unscrew the **M6x15 cross screws** on the fixing bracket of the assembly - **there are three screws in total**. Then remove **three 1/4" spring washers** and **three 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers**. This lets you detach the idler wheel assembly.
3. To reinstall, follow the reverse of the steps above. Use the **M8 nut on the J-bolt** and tighten it until the drive belt is **lightly tensioned with a crisp sound**. Alternatively use a **sonic belt tensioner to measure the value at 190Hz (+/- 10)**. Then reinstall the other components.

**This machine's idler wheel differs from the other 2023 ellipticals.** The E25, E35 and E95 manuals loosen an **M8 x 9T** nut and release an **M8 x 20 carriage bolt**. This manual loosens an **M8 x 7T** nut and releases three **M6x15 cross screws** on a fixing bracket.

This manual has no separate Poly-V belt section. The belt is handled here.
