1. Remove the swing arm assembly and swing arm (section 9.2). If the pedal tube is coming off, simply remove the swing arm with the rod end bearing attached.
2. Use a **12mm open-end wrench** to remove the **hex socket screw 5/16" x 15mm** and the **flat washer 5/16" x 23**. Remove the **pedal axle lock plate** to take off the pedal bar assembly.
3. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove the **Phillips head screw M5x10mm** securing the pedal and remove the pedal.
4. Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly. **Ensure the pedal axle lock plate is aligned with the pedal arm.**

The E95 2023 manual's equivalent step uses a **5/16" x 20** flat washer and pedal carriage bolts, and it carries extra steps for an adjustable pedal. This machine's procedure is the shorter one printed above.
