The hardware is bagged per assembly step. Numbers in parentheses are assembly-drawing item numbers. Close to the E95-2023 kit, but #132 is 3/8" x 2-1/4" here against 3/8" x 2" there, Step 2 adds a #197 star washer, and Step 4 is split into bags 4-1 and 4-2.
