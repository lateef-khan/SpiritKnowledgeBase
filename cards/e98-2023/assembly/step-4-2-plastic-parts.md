1. **Slide Wheel Covers (21)** above each side of the Slide Wheel Urethanes with **4pcs M5 x 10 m/m Phillips Head Screws (143)**.
2. **Cover Holders (24)**, bent-tab hole facing rearward, on the Incline Rail Assembly (2) with **4pcs M6 x 10 m/m Phillips Head Screws (148)**; **Inclinable Rail Cover (115)** with **2pcs M5 x 10 m/m Phillips Head Screws (143)**; **Rear Bar Cover (23)** on the Rear Stabilizer and the Cover Holders (24) with **4pcs M5 x 10 m/m Phillips Head Screws (143)**.
3. **Bottom Cover (113)** on the Incline Rail Assembly (2) with **2pcs M5 x 10 m/m Phillips Head Screws (143)**.
4. **Plug both O40 x O80 Oval End Caps (76)** into both ends of the stabilizer tube.
