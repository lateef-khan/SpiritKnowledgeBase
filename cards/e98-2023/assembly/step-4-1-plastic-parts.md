1. **Match the Connecting Arm Covers B (L) and (R), (121) and (120)**, on the Pedal Bar Assembly (L) (8) with **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)** and **2pcs Sheet Metal Screws (146)**; then match the **Connecting Arm Covers A (R) and (L), (118) and (119)**, on the lower Lower Handle Bar (L) (10) the same way using the Short driver (180). Repeat on the other side.
2. **Front Stabilizer Cover (112)** with **2pcs Phillips Head Screws (143)**.
3. **Handle Bar Covers (106 & 107, 108 & 109)** with **4pcs Sheet Metal Screws (146)**.
4. **Console Mast Covers (100 & 101)** with **4pcs Sheet Metal Screws (146)**.
5. **Tablet Holder (89)** on the back of the console with **4pcs Sheet Metal Screws (146)**.
