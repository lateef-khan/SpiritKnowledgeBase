| Frame & Brake | Parts | Labor |
|---|---|---|
| Lifetime | 5 Years | 1 Year |

**Home Use Limited Warranty**, as printed on the Manufacturer's Limited Warranty page of the SB4.5r book, headed *Effective August 1, 2012 - FITNESS BIKE LIMITED WARRANTY*. The book is a scan stamped `SB4.5r_20130605`; the page was read from a 300 dpi render, not from the OCR alone. The periods run **from the date of retail sale as shown on a sales receipt, or eighteen (18) months from the original factory shipping date when there is no receipt** (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-start-date-sales-receipt-or-18-months-from-factory-shipping`).

**Five years on parts is the second-longest parts term in the Xterra range** - only the FS5.9e, effective the same day, prints more (`fs59e-2014-warranty-periods-frame-and-brake-lifetime-parts-7-years-labor-1-year`). The SB500 and SB4500 that followed print the same *Frame & Brake / Lifetime* column with three years on parts (`xterra-warranty-periods-frame-and-brake-lifetime-parts-3-years-labor-1-year`).

**The exclusion list on this page has eight items and no residential-setting item** (`sb45r-2013-warranty-exclusions-eight-items-no-residential-setting-item`); the residential-only rule survives only in the contents-page ATTENTION box and the *ordinary household use* lead sentence (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-use-environment-residential-only-any-other-use-voids-it`). The registration card duty, the SERVICE steps with the trip charge and the 48-states sentence are the standard ones (`xterra-treadmill-warranty-registration-card-within-10-days-or-no-cover`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-service-steps-dealer-service-organization-then-the-service-department`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-owner-pays-freight-parts-shipping-and-trip-charges`, `xterra-treadmill-warranty-48-contiguous-states-not-alaska-or-hawaii`); the electrical page adds the ten-percent voltage sentence (`xterra-warranty-what-the-electrical-and-console-pages-say-is-not-covered`).
