**Quick Start.** After the console powers up press **Start**. Time counts up from zero and the workload is adjusted with the Up or Down keys - on the console **or on the stationary hand grips**, which carry their own Up and Down keys. The dot-matrix shows a work profile with a **quarter-mile track around it and a lap counter to the left**; pressing **Enter** switches the display from the track to a hill-type profile. To the right of the dot-matrix a number shows the work level.

**Data displayed.** Pulse (from the hand pulse sensors), Time (elapsed unless the timer is set to count down), Distance, Calories (an estimate - "the only way to get an accurate calorie reading is in a laboratory setting").

**Message window.** Instructions during programming, and during a workout: Speed in mph if "M" shows to the left of the track/profile window, km/h if "Km"; press Enter to see speed as RPM; press Enter again for the current work level.

The heart icon, pause and reset pages are shared with the SB500 family ([heart icon](../../shared/console/xterra-console-heart-icon-blinks-when-the-grips-or-strap-are-read.md), [pause](../../shared/console/xterra-console-stop-once-pauses-5-minutes-twice-ends-with-a-summary-held-3-seconds-resets.md)). The SB500's later console cycles Speed, Watts, Laps, Level and Scan instead ([card](../../sb500-2020/console/quick-start-lap-track-and-enter-cycling-speed-watts-laps-level-and-scan.md)). The book is a scan read from the render.
