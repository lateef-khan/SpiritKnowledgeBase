"The SB4.5r has seven different programs that have been designed to provide a variety of
workouts. These seven programs have factory preset profiles for achieving different goals."
Each is printed with a paragraph and a 20-segment RESISTANCE picture (no figures; the scan is
too grey to count cells reliably, so the shapes are described):

| Program | As described | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| P-1 Sunrise | "simulates going up and down a gentle hill early in the morning. The resistance in the pedals will steadily increase and then decrease" | a low, gentle hill |
| P-2 Journey | "designed to maximize the burning of fat ... most experts agree that a lower exertion level that stays at a steady workload is the best. The absolute best way to burn fat is to keep your heart rate at around 60% to 70% of its maximum potential. This program does not use heart rate but simulates a lower, steady exertion workout" | a quick rise to a long steady plateau, then down |
| P-3 Wild Ride | "designed to increase your cardio vascular function; this is exercise for your heart and lungs ... a higher level of exertion with slight fluctuations in work" | a high, jagged plateau |
| P-4 Xtreme | "designed to increase muscular strength in your lower body with extreme resistance. This program will steadily increase in resistance to a high level and then keeps you there ... to strengthen and tone your legs and glutes" | a staircase climbing to a high plateau, dropping at the end |
| P-5 Canyons | "takes you through high levels of intensity followed by periods of low intensity ... depleting your oxygen level followed by periods of recovery" | five tall blocks separated by low columns |
| P-6 Thrill Hill | "combines a few types of workout in one, with variations of cardio and strength portions to give you a thrill" | uneven hills of differing height |
| P-7 X-Country | "simulates a cross country run across uneven terrain" | rolling, moderate, uneven |

Setup (weight, age, time, max level) is on the family's preset setup card. The later SB500
has a different seven; see `see_also`.
