**The manual prints no weight-stack rating.** This total is the sum of the plate quantities its
assembly hardware table lists, and it is not a figure the manufacturer states anywhere in the book -
say so when you quote it. Why that is, and how it was checked, is at
`spirit-strength-specs-no-weight-stack-rating-printed`.

**This is a 2025-revision-only figure.** The 2024 CSD-ACBE printing has a 200 lb stack -
`spirit-strength-specs-stack-of-200-lb`. The 2025 revision changes the quantities at rows 49 and 50,
not just their names, and the total goes up by 15 lb:

| No. | Description | Qty | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 47 | `10lb top plate` | 1 | 10 lb |
| 48 | `10lb counterweight plate` | 1 | 10 lb |
| 49 | `15lb weight stack` | 9 | 135 lb |
| 50 | `10lb weight stack` | 6 | 60 lb |
| | **Total** | **17** | **215 lb** |

Confirmed on 600 dpi renders of both printings' PDF page 18: the 2024 book reads row 49 qty 6 and row
50 qty 9 (6 x 15 lb + 9 x 10 lb = 180 lb, and 200 lb with the two 10 lb top pieces); the 2025 book
reads row 49 qty 9 and row 50 qty 6 (9 x 15 lb + 6 x 10 lb = 195 lb, and 215 lb with the same two
10 lb top pieces). The renders are unambiguous on both counts - this is a genuine change in the
assembly table, not an OCR misread.

**Do not confuse this with the CSD-LELC's 225 lb stack**, which is fifteen uniform 15 lb plates
(`spirit-strength-specs-stack-of-225-lb`). This stack is a 10/15 lb mix like the 2024-printing
200 lb CSD dual stations, and it is 10 lb lighter.

The optional 5 lb increment weight rides on top of the stack on the machines that have one -
`spirit-strength-specs-five-pound-increment-weight`.
