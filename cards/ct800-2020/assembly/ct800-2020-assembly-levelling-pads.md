Ensure that the unit is properly levelled **after assembly and before each use**.
Use the **levelling pads on the bottom of the feet** to adjust the height. The
troubleshooting table adds only that the levellers are adjusted "using a wrench".

**That is everything these manuals print.** They do not say how many pads there are
and they do not give a wrench size. Both blanks are filled in on the CT800 2016 and
the CT850 2018 - two pads on the rear stabiliser, a 14mm wrench - and **that answer
must not be carried across**: this is a different frame, whose parts list holds four
Rubber Foot Pads (45) and two Foot Pads (31).

**All three manuals print these two passages word for word.** Nothing about
levelling appears in any of their assembly chapters; both passages sit at the back,
in general maintenance and in the noise row of troubleshooting.

**Do not answer a CT850ENT question from this card.** That manual prints no
levelling instruction at all.

The instruction sits beside its neighbour in the same list: ensure all bolts are
properly tightened after assembly and before each use.
