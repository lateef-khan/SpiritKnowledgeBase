Ensure that the unit is properly levelled **after assembly and before each use**.
Use the **levelling pads on the bottom of the feet** to adjust the height. The
troubleshooting table adds only that the levellers are adjusted "using a wrench".

**That is everything this manual prints.** It does not say how many pads there are
and it does not give a wrench size. Both blanks are filled in on the previous
generation - two pads on the rear stabiliser, a 14mm wrench - and **that answer
must not be carried across**: this is a different frame, whose parts list holds
four Rubber Foot Pads (45) and two Foot Pads (31).

The instruction sits beside its neighbour in the same list: ensure all bolts are
properly tightened after assembly and before each use.
