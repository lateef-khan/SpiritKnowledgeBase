**These are the 65% and 80% figures.** The five-preset CT800 generation targets **60% and 85%**
instead - see `spirit-ct800-programs-heart-rate-control-60-and-85-percent`.

Heart Rate Control (HRC) uses the treadmill's **incline system**, not speed, to control your heart
rate. Increases and decreases in elevation affect heart rate much more efficiently than changes in
speed alone. The HRC program changes elevation gradually to achieve the programmed target heart
rate.

During setup you choose either the **Weight Control (HR-1)** program or the **Cardiovascular
(HR-2)** program.

- **Weight Control (HR-1)** attempts to maintain your heart rate at **65% of your Maximum Heart
  Rate.**
- **Cardiovascular (HR-2)** attempts to maintain your heart rate at **80% of your Maximum Heart
  Rate.**

Your Maximum Heart Rate is a formula that subtracts your age from a constant of **220**. Your HR
setting is calculated automatically during setup when you enter your age.

**The same manual's heart rate training page recommends 85% for strengthening the cardiovascular
system, while this page sets the Cardiovascular program to 80%.** The manual does not reconcile the
two figures; both are recorded as printed. For the training page, see
`ct900-heart-rate-exertion-target-zones`.

## Programming steps

1. You must receive a strong, steady value in the heart rate window or the program will not start.
   The Dot Matrix Message Center shows **"Check Pulse"** if there is no pulse signal.
2. Use the **Program** button to select the HR program.
3. The Dot Matrix Message Center shows "Press Enter to modify or Start to begin workout". Press
   **Enter** for settings, or **Start** to run the default program.
4. When Enter is pressed the Center reads "Press 1 or 2 to select then press Enter", and shows "1".
5. The Center shows "Adjust Time then press Enter" with the Time window blinking. Use the Up/Down
   and Fast/Slow buttons to adjust, then press Enter.
6. The Center prompts you to enter **Age**. Press Enter once adjusted.
7. The Center prompts you to enter **Body Weight**. Press Enter once adjusted.
8. The Center prompts you to enter **Heart Rate**. Press Enter once adjusted.
9. Press **Start** to begin your workout, **Enter** to modify, or **Stop** to return to the previous
   screen.

**HR 1 and HR 2 have no warm-up** - see `ct850-2020-starting-and-customising-a-program`.
