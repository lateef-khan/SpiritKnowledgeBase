These are the callout numbers on the exploded view drawing on page 50. The
drawing is a picture with bare numbers on it and no text, so this table is the
only readable form of it.

**The list heads itself `CT800 - ST8600 - YT057 - 01 Part List SPIRIT (LED, 120V`**
and is date-stamped **230809** at its foot. The closing bracket after `120V` is
lost in the conversion; nothing else follows it on the page. ST8600 and YT057 are
the same driver-family codes the CT850 service manuals print - see
`spirit-commercial-service-manual-document-codes` - so a search for either code
can land on the wrong machine.

**132 rows are printed.** The whole numbers that appear are 1-29, 31-72, 75, 94,
101-109, 111-140, 142-148, 150-156 and 158-163. Any number outside that run is
not printed in this manual at all - **30, 73, 74, 76-93, 95-100, 110, 141, 149
and 157 are absent, not a missing page.** Fifteen of those gaps - 77-82 and
85-93 - are exactly the numbers the CT800ENT-2022 list fills with its
entertainment-console hardware, and that list adds two more numbers, 164 and 166,
past the end of this one.

**Do not read this list against the CT800ENT-2022 list.** 131 numbers appear on
both, but 22 of them carry a different part, a different quantity or a different
description, and item 71 exists only here. See `ct800ent-2022-specs-parts-list`
for the full comparison.

**Items 50 and 51 are printed identically** - both read
`400m/m_Handpulse Wire (Upper)`, quantity 1 each - so this list cannot tell the
two upper handpulse wires apart. The CT800ENT-2022 list distinguishes them as
`(XHB-4)` and `(XHB-3)`; nothing in this manual says which of the two is which
here.

**No motor rating, roller size, deck size or belt size is printed for any of
these parts.** Item 55 is "Drive Motor" and item 66 "Incline Motor", each with no
horsepower or wattage; items 15, 16, 17 and 18 give the front roller, rear
roller, running deck and running belt no dimensions.

**Item 112 is printed `3/8" × UCN16 × 2"` and item 118 `3/8” × UCN 16x2”`, while
item 154 is printed `3/8" × UNC16 × 1-1/4"`.** UCN and UNC are the same thread
callout spelled two ways in the one list. Both spellings are left exactly as
printed above; nothing here changes a number, a size or a quantity.
