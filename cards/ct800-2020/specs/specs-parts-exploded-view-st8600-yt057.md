**Chapter 10 is one full-page drawing and nothing else.** Its title block reads **ST8600-YT057 Explode View**, drawn under the **dyaco** mark, with numbered callouts and a boxed **Optional** inset at top left showing item **70** on its own. The highest callout legible is **163**. The chapter prints no table of any kind: nothing in the service manual maps a callout to a part name or a part number.

**The list that decodes this drawing is in the 2020 owner's manual, not here.** That book heads its parts list `CT800 - ST8600 - YT057 - 01 Part List` - the same drawing code - and its 132 rows run over the same 1-163 callout range, with item 70 the Power Cord, which is what the Optional inset shows. See `ct800-2020-specs-parts-list`. Nothing in either book says so in words; the match is the drawing code and the numbering.

**Do not decode this drawing from a CT800ENT, CT850 or 2024 list.** The CT850-2020 service manual prints the sister drawing **ST8600-YT058** (`ct850-2020-exploded-view-drawing`), and the four CT800-family lists number the same positions differently - `spirit-ct800-specs-no-parts-list` sets out the clash. ST8600 and YT057 are also the driver-family codes the manual prints on its circuit diagram (`CT800_ST8600-YT57`, p. 52) and its driver board chapter - see `spirit-commercial-service-manual-document-codes` - so a search on either code lands on wiring pages as readily as on this drawing.

**The CT800ENT-2022 and CT850ENT-2022 service manuals print no exploded view at all**; their ten chapters run from Outlines to a Q&A with no Exploded View heading among them.
