**"DO NOT SHIP ANY UNIT TO OUR FACTORY WITHOUT A RETURN AUTHORIZATION NUMBER. All units arriving without a
return authorization number will be refused."**

The surrounding terms that come with a return:

- **All transportation costs, both to the factory and on return to the owner, are the responsibility of the
  owner.**
- **The owner is responsible for adequate packaging on return to Sole.**
- **Sole is not responsible for damage that occurs during shipping.** Make all freight damage claims with the
  appropriate freight carrier.

The manual does not say how to obtain a return authorization number; the service steps route every request
through the selling dealer, the online service request form, or the service department.
