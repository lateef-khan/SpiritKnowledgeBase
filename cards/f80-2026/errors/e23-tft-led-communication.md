**This is E23, not E22 (LED watch to control board) and not E03 (hardware current too large).**

**Meaning:** the TFT electronic watch has a communication error with the LED electronic watch.

**Solution / cause, as printed:**

1. **Check whether the cable between the LED electronic watch and the TFT electronic watch is loose.**
2. **Check whether the LED electronic watch and the TFT electronic watch are damaged.**
3. **After 2 minutes of power failure, power it on again.**

If the TFT screen is the failed part, page 24 says you can unplug the TFT connection cable and run the
machine from the LED display alone.
