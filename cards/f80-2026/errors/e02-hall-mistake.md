**This is E02, not E01 (over current) and not E22 (LED to control board communication).**

**Meaning:** Hall mistake.

**Solution / cause, as printed:**

1. **Motor is not connected properly, or not connected to the controller.**
2. **Replace the motor.**
3. **Replace the controller.**

The manual gives no test to tell a wiring fault from a failed motor; the list is in the order printed.
