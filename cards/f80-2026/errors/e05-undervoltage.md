**This is E05, not E06 (overvoltage) and not E5 in the older service manuals.**

**Meaning:** undervoltage.

**Solution / cause, as printed:**

1. **Check whether the input power voltage is normal.**
2. **Check whether the connector of the power socket is loose.**

The electrical section adds the background: if voltage varies by ten percent or more the performance of the
treadmill may be affected, and such conditions are not covered under the warranty.
