**This is E31, not E3 (incline adjustment error) and not E03 (hardware current too large).**

**Meaning:** overtemperature.

**Solution / cause, as printed:**

1. **Wait until the temperature of the machine is normal before running.**

That is the entire remedy the manual gives. It names no temperature threshold and no waiting time.

Related figures elsewhere in the manual: the maximum operating temperature specification is **40 degrees C**
with humidity **95% non-condensing**, and a dry deck raises belt friction and stresses the drive motor,
drive belt and motor control board.
