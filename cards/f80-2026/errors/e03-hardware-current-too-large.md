**This is E03, not E3 (incline adjustment error) and not E01 (over current protection). All three are in the
same table on the same page of this manual and they are three different faults.**

**Meaning:** the hardware current is too large.

**Solution / cause, as printed:**

1. Exceeding the rated load leads to excessive current; the system self-protection shows the error code,
   and re-power recovery.
2. **Check whether the connection between the power socket plug and the motor is loose.**
3. **Replace the controller.**

**How this differs from E01.** E01 is described as over current protection with the treadmill over loaded
and the controller protection activated, and its list runs to replacing the motor. E03 is described as the
hardware current being too large, and its list stops at the controller.
