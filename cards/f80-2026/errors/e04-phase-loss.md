**This is E04, not E4 in the older service manuals and not E01 (over current).**

**Meaning:** Phase loss.

**Solution / cause, as printed:**

1. **Motor is not connected properly, or not connected to the controller.**
2. **Replace the motor.**
3. **Replace the controller.**

The printed remedy list is identical to E02's; only the meaning differs.
