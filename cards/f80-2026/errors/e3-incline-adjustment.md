**This is E3, not E03 (hardware current too large) and not E31 (overtemperature). All three appear in the
same table on this page.**

**Meaning:** Incline adjustment error.

**Solution / cause, as printed:**

1. **Check whether the incline cable is properly connected.**
2. **Connect the incline cable and power it on.**
3. **Connect the incline wire and re-calibrate it.**

**The manual gives no calibration procedure.** Step 3 asks for a re-calibration that is described nowhere in
this owner's manual.

**A cross-year note.** The 2016, 2019 and 2023 F80 service manuals also print an E3 for an incline fault,
described there as the console board not detecting the incline VR voltage, or the voltage being out of
range. Those are different machines with a fuller diagnostic; this manual's description is the three lines
above.
