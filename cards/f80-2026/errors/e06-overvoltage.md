**This is E06, not E05 (undervoltage) and not E6 in the older service manuals.**

**Meaning:** overvoltage.

**Solution / cause, as printed:**

1. **Check whether the input power voltage is normal.**

That single line is the entire remedy the manual gives for this code. Unlike E05, it does not mention the
power socket connector.
