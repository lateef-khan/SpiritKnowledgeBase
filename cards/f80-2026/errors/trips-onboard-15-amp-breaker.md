**Problem, as printed:** "Treadmill trips on board 15 amp circuit".

**Solution / cause, as printed:** "High belt/deck friction. See General Maintenance".

**The sentence is cut short on the page.** Every other row of this table names the maintenance topic to look
up; this one ends at "See General Maintenance" with no topic. The neighbouring row with the same cause -
tread-belt stops quickly when the tether cord is pulled - points to **lubrication**, and lubrication is the
maintenance topic that explains high belt/deck friction, so that is the section this row is pointing at. The
manual does not say so itself.

The reset is described in the display-does-not-light row: **the circuit breaker is on the front grill, and
you push it in until it locks.**

**This is the treadmill's own breaker, not the house breaker.** If the house breaker trips and this one does
not, that is a different row of the same table.
