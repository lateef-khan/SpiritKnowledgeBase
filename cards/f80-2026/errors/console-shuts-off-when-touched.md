**Problem:** computer shuts off when the console is touched (on a cold day) while walking or running.

**Solution / cause, as printed:** **the treadmill may not be grounded. Static electricity is "crashing" the
computer.** Refer to the Grounding Instructions on page 3.

That page reference is correct - the Grounding Instructions really are on page 3, unlike two other
cross-references in this manual.

The grounding section's requirements are the fix: the product must be grounded, the plug must go into a
properly installed and grounded outlet, and a temporary adapter must have its green ear-lug connected to a
permanent ground and be held in place by a metal screw.
