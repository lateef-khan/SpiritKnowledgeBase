**This is E01, not E03 (hardware current too large) and not E31 (overtemperature).**

**Meaning:** Over Current Protection. The treadmill is over loaded and the controller protection is
activated.

**Solution / cause, as printed:**

1. Exceeding the rated load leads to excessive current; the system self-protection shows the error code,
   and re-power recovery.
2. A certain part of the treadmill is stuck, resulting in the motor not being able to turn; the load is too
   heavy, the current is too large and the system reports an error. **Adjust the treadmill to restart the
   operation, or increase the lubricating oil.**
3. Check whether there is a flowing sound or burning smell when the motor is running, and **replace the
   motor**.
4. **Replace the controller.**
