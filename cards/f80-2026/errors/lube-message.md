**Meaning:** reminder to check lubrication under the walking deck.

**Solution / cause, as printed:**

1. **Check lubrication under the walking belt.** Reference the belt/deck lubrication procedure.
2. **Power the treadmill three times to reset the lube message.**

**Two things to be careful about.**

- The manual points at "page 28" for the lubrication procedure, but page 28 is the Rate of Perceived
  Exertion page. **The Belt/Deck Lubrication procedure is on page 31.**
- **The reset here is "power the treadmill three times".** The 2023 F80 service manual resets the same
  message by pressing STOP, or by a Zeroing button in Lube Setup. That is a different machine and a
  different console; do not carry that method across.

The lubrication section adds that if you check and there is no lubricant present, you should lubricate even
though the "Lube" indicator is not lit.
