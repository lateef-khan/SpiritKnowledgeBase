**Problem:** tread-belt stops quickly/suddenly when the tether cord is pulled.

**Solution / cause, as printed:** **high belt/deck friction.** See the General Maintenance section on
lubrication.

**Why this is a symptom at all.** The safety section says the treadmill should stop **with a one to two step
coast** when the safety key is pulled off the console. A belt that stops dead instead is telling you the
deck has gone dry.

The lubrication section gives the rest of the story: dried-out lubricant raises friction between belt and
deck and puts undue stress on the drive motor, drive belt and electronic motor control board.
