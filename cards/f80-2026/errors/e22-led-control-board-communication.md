**This is E22, not E23 (TFT to LED communication) and not E02 (hall mistake).**

**Meaning:** error in communication between the LED electronic watch and the control board.

**Solution / cause, as printed:**

1. **Check whether the cable between the electronic watch and the LED controller is loose.**
2. **Check whether the controller is damaged.**
3. **Check whether the LED electronic watch is damaged.**
4. **After 2 minutes of power failure, power it on again.**

**E22 and E23 name different links in the same chain.** E22 is the LED board talking to the control board;
E23 is the TFT screen talking to the LED board.
