1. **Scan the QR code on the console screen** to download the mobile app.
2. Once the app is installed and registration is complete, **open the QR code scanning camera in the SOLE+
   app**.
3. **Scan the second QR code** to link your local profile with the SOLE+ account.

**Your console profile cannot be linked to multiple SOLE+ accounts at the same time.**

If you skip this step during first-time setup, you can reach it again later in **Profile settings**.

Two features depend on this link: seeing all available Club events, and accessing your full workout history
beyond the 10 records stored on the console.
