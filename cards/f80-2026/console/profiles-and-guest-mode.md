When the console is launched for the first time it prompts you to **either enter as a guest, or create a new
profile**. Sole recommends setting up your own profile: it lets the machine remember your physical
parameters, favourite programs and templates, gives you a custom program with an adjustable intensity
pattern, and lets your workout records synchronise with the SOLE+ mobile app.

- **Tap + to create a new profile.**
- **A maximum of 9 profiles can be created.**
- **Each profile can be protected with a passcode.**
- **In Guest mode you can set your age and weight on the Home screen**, which helps the machine calculate
  your workout summary more accurately.
