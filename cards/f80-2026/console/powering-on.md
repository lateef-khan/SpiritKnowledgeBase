1. Power the treadmill on by **plugging it into an appropriate wall outlet**.
2. **Turn on the power switch located at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood.**
3. **Ensure that the Safety Key is installed** - the treadmill will not power on without it.

**A wrong cross-reference in the manual.** This paragraph says "see assembly step 7 for reference" for the
safety key, but Assembly Step 7 is about the frame base covers and the shipping locking pin. The Safety Key
is listed as part **#47** in the Assembly Tools on page 7.
