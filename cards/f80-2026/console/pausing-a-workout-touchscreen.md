**Stop anytime by pressing the physical "STOP" key, or by tapping the red pause button in the bottom right
corner of the screen.**

**After 5 minutes of inactivity in the Pause mode, the workout will end automatically.**

**The manual states the pause behaviour a second time, for the LED display, on page 24** - with different
detail about what the incline does, which readings hold and what the display returns to. Read both halves;
neither page mentions the other.
