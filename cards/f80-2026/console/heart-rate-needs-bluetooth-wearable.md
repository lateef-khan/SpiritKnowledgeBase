**"Heart rate programming, caution! Heart Rate programs are intended for wireless Bluetooth wearables only.
You must receive a strong/steady value in the Heart Rate Window."**

The Programs section says the same thing from the other side: **a heart rate monitor is required for the
Heart Rate program**, and one is required for the Gerkin Protocol fitness test as well.

**Note what this rules out.** The safety instructions on page 2 describe hand-grip pulse sensors and say
they are not medical devices, but page 24 says the heart rate **programs** are for wireless Bluetooth
wearables **only**. The manual does not say whether the hand sensors can drive a heart rate program.
