**The exercise equipment can play music wirelessly via Bluetooth.**

1. Turn on your mobile phone or tablet's Bluetooth function.
2. **Search for the name "BT-Speaker" in your device's Bluetooth menu.**
3. **Tap to connect.**

Your device can now transmit music to the exercise equipment.

**"BT-Speaker" is a separate Bluetooth device from the one the SOLE+ App connects to.** The app searches for
Sole equipment; the audio connection is made from your phone's own Bluetooth menu.
