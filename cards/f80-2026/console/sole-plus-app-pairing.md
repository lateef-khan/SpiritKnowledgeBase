The treadmill comes equipped with a **Bluetooth transceiver** that lets it interact with compatible iOS and
Android smartphones or tablets via the **SOLE+ App**.

1. Download the free **SOLE+ App** from the Apple Store or Google Play.
2. **Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your device.**
3. **Tap the icon at the top right corner to search for Sole equipment.**
4. **After the equipment is detected, tap Connect.**
5. **When the App and equipment are synced, the Bluetooth icon on the equipment's console will light up.**

The console-side profile link, which uses two QR codes, is a separate step described on page 14.
