Charge your personal device during your workout using the fitness equipment's **on-console type-C USB port**.
**The fitness equipment power must be on.**

1. **Connect your type-C USB charging cable to the USB Power Port and to your device.**
2. **Check to make sure your device icon indicates it is charging.**

**Notes as printed:**

- **The USB charging cable is not included.** Make sure a compatible USB charging cable is being used.
- Your device's "charging" icon **may or may not indicate it is charging**. Depending on the amount of
  current your device requires, the icon may not come on but the device is still charging, possibly at a
  lower charge rate.
- **The port provides up to 5Vdc / 1.5 amp of power.**
- **You will not be able to save your workout data to a USB via this port; it is used for charging purposes
  only.**
