While watching a video class:

- **Navigate through the video by sliding on the progress bar**, or **skip 10-second intervals** by pressing
  the skip icons.
- **The "Pause" button stops the treadmill, and pauses both your workout and the video at the same time.**

The three media view layouts - full, partial and minimal interface - work the same way here as they do for
other media.
