**The SOLE+ App allows you to sync your workout data with one of many fitness cloud sites Sole supports:**

- **Apple Health**
- **Google Health Connect**
- **Strava**
- **MapMyFitness**

The manual adds "with more to come", so this list is the set supported at the time of printing rather than a
closed list.

The sync runs through the SOLE+ app, not from the console directly, so the app has to be paired with the
machine and linked to your console profile first.
