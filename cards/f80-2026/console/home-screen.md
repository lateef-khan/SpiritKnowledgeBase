The Home screen displays an overview of your activity and offers shortcuts to:

- an **immediate start**
- a **manually controlled timed workout**
- your **favourite programs and video classes**
- **club events** you currently take part in, or might be interested to join

**The blocks displayed on this page are customizable.** Press **"Edit Home"** to configure a layout that
suits your preferences; you can enable or disable optional blocks and change their order.

**If you are in Guest mode you can set your age and weight here**, which helps the machine calculate your
workout summary more accurately.

History and Favorites are both reached from this page.
