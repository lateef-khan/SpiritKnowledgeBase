**The exercise equipment's Bluetooth device can also be connected to Bluetooth wearables.**

- **The Bluetooth wearables can receive signals even when the App is already in use.**
- **Wear the Bluetooth wearable first, then commence connection.**
- **While in connection, the console will show the heart rate value via Bluetooth.**

The manual does not name supported brands or models, and gives no pairing menu path on the console.
