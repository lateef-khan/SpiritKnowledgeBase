The console ships with Sleep Mode offering three choices:

| Setting | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **ON** | The unit goes into an energy-saving state after **15 minutes** of inactivity. |
| **OFF** | The console will stay lit while the power is on. |
| **RETAIL** | The console will run the **demo video** after **3 minutes** of inactivity. |

**How to change it depends on the display type:**

- **LED displays:** hold the **Stop** and **Display** keys for **5 seconds** to switch between ON and OFF.
- **LCD displays:** press and hold the **Stop**, **Enter** and **Display** keys for **5 seconds** to switch
  between ON and OFF.
- **TFT displays:** click **"Sleep Mode" under Settings** to switch among all three.

**Only the TFT route reaches RETAIL.** The LED and LCD key combinations toggle ON and OFF only, as printed.
