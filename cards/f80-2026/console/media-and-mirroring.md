**On the Home page, choose from a variety of pre-installed Apps, or use the Mirroring function to cast the
content from your smartphone directly to the console screen.**

Once the content is on, **use the bottom panel for navigation and view settings**.

The manual does not name which apps are pre-installed and gives no separate instructions for setting up
mirroring beyond this sentence.
