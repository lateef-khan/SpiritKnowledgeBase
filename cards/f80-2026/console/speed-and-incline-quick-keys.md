- **Press the Start key to begin belt movement.**
- **Use the speed knob button to adjust to the desired speed.** You may also use the **speed keys 3, 6, 9,
  12** to adjust the speed.
- **To slow down the tread belt, use the speed knob** to adjust it to the desired speed. You may also press
  the **speed adjust keys, 3 through 12**.
- **Incline may be adjusted anytime after belt movement.** Use the **incline knob** to adjust to the effect
  you want. You may also choose a more rapid increase or decrease by selecting the desired key **0, 5, 10,
  15** on the **left hand side of the console**.

The manual does not print units for the quick keys; it lists them as bare numbers.
