Access video classes by selecting the **"Videos" tab of the bottom navigation bar**.

Video classes are **pre-recorded, manually controlled workouts of fixed duration**. There are hundreds of
videos across **7 activity types**:

- Running
- Yoga
- Stretching
- Boxing
- Sculpt
- Meditation
- Full Sweat

For easier search, **filter classes by instructor, duration, difficulty level, music genre, etc.**

The additional tab menu at the top of the page features **Collections** - sequences of video classes that
follow a certain theme.
