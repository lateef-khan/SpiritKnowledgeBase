**Any class, collection or program can be labeled as a Favorite.**

- **Up to 12 favorite programs will appear on the Home page** for easier access.
- To "like" a program, open the **program setup page** and tap the **"ribbon" icon in the upper left corner**
  of the screen.
- Manage previously labeled programs on the **Favorites page**, reached from the Home page or the user
  Profile page.
- **To remove the label, un-tap the "ribbon" icon.**
