This treadmill is equipped with **not only TFT displays but also LED displays**.

**During operation the LED displays only synchronously show the motion data of the TFT displays.**

- The four windows of the LED display show **INCLINE, CALORIE, PACE and SPEED**.
- **Pressing the DISPLAY/ENTER key switches the display to LAPS, DISTANCE, TIME and PULSE.**

So there are eight readouts across two pages of four.
