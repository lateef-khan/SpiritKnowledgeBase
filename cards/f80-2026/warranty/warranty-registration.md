**The manual describes registration in two places and this card carries both halves.**

**Page 1, Product Registration.**

- **Record the Serial Number** of this fitness product in the space provided on that page.
- **The self-addressed product registration card must be completed in full and returned to Sole.**
- **You can also register online at www.soletreadmills.com under the support tab.**

**Page 34, Normal Responsibilities of the Consumer.**

- **The warranty registration card must be completed and returned to the address listed on the card within
  10 days of the original purchase to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty.**
- **If you are outside the 10 day period, the warranty can still be registered if you have proof of
  purchase** - a receipt, bank statement, credit card statement, or email order confirmation.

Exclusion 6 is the other side of this: products that do not have a warranty registration card on file at
Sole are not covered, and **Sole reserves the right to request proof of purchase if no warranty record
exists**.

The manual does not say where on the machine the serial number is found.
