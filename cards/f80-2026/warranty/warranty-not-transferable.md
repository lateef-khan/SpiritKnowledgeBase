**"Products that have been: sold, transferred, bartered, or given to a third party. (Warranties DO NOT
transfer.)"**

So the warranty belongs to the original retail purchaser. A machine that has changed hands - sold, given
away, traded or handed on - is outside the warranty, whatever time is left on the original period.

Two neighbouring exclusions work the same way in practice: the registration card has to be on file at Sole
under the purchaser's name, and products whose original serial numbers have been removed or altered are
excluded.
