**"Warranties outside of North America may vary. Sole USA warranty coverage is limited to the Continental
United States. Sole USA does not sell or service equipment in Hawaii, Alaska, or outside of the United
States. Please contact your local dealer for details regarding warranty availability in your region."**

Three separate limits are packed into that one exclusion:

- **Sole USA warranty coverage: the Continental United States only.**
- **Sole USA does not sell or service equipment in Hawaii, Alaska, or outside the United States.**
- **Outside North America, warranties may vary** - your local dealer is the authority on what applies.
