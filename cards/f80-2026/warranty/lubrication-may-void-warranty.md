**"Failure to lubricate the deck at regular intervals may void the warranty."**

The maintenance section gives the mechanism: dried-out lubricant raises friction between belt and deck and
places undue stress on the **drive motor, drive belt and electronic motor control board**, which could
result in catastrophic failure of these expensive components.

The warranty section restates it as an exclusion. Exclusion 3 excludes damage from "improper maintenance -
(see general maintenance instructions section), **including lack of consistent lubrication of the deck**".
The Normal Responsibilities of the Consumer list names lubrication as one of the owner's duties.
