The warranty applies only to products in **ordinary household use**, and the consumer is responsible for:

- **Completing and returning the warranty registration card** within 10 days of purchase, or registering
  later with proof of purchase.
- **Proper use of the treadmill in accordance with this manual, including general maintenance** - belt
  tracking, belt tension, vacuuming under the motor cover, and lubrication.
- **Proper installation** in accordance with the instructions and with all local electric codes.
- **Proper connection to a grounded power supply of sufficient voltage**, replacement of blown fuses, and
  repair of loose connections or defects in house wiring.
- **Expenses for making the treadmill accessible for servicing**, including any item that was not part of
  the treadmill when it shipped from the factory.
- **Damage to the treadmill finish during shipping, installation or following installation.**
- **Routine maintenance of this unit as specified in this manual.**
- **Providing pictures or video of any issues by email as requested by the service technician, and assisting
  with troubleshooting/diagnosing prior to a home visit.**
