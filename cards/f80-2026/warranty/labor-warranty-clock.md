**Keep your bill of sale.**

**"Twelve (12) months from the date on the bill of sale, or eighteen (18) months from the date of factory
shipping as determined by the serial number, establishes the labor warranty period should service be
required."**

So the labor clock runs from the bill of sale if you have one, and from the factory shipping date - read off
the serial number - if you do not. **If service is performed, it is in your best interest to obtain and keep
all receipts.**

The parts warranty uses the same two anchors: the date of retail sale as determined by sale receipt, or in
the absence of a receipt eighteen months from the original factory shipping date.

**This written warranty gives you specific legal rights. You may also have other rights that vary from state
to state.**
