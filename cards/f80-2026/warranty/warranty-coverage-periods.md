**Effective February 1, 2026.** Sole warrants all its treadmills' parts for the periods below, **from the
date of retail sale as determined by sale receipt, or in the absence of a receipt eighteen (18) months from
the original factory shipping date**.

| Component | Period |
|---|---|
| Frame / Drive motor | **Lifetime** |
| Parts | **3 years** |
| Labor | **1 year** |
| Cosmetic Items * | **90 Days** |

\* Cosmetic items include, but are not limited to: **grips, console overlays, and labels/decals**.

Sole's responsibilities include **providing new or remanufactured parts, at Sole's option**, and technical
support to independent dealers and servicing organizations. **In the absence of a dealer or service
organization, these warranties will be administered by Sole directly to a consumer.**

**This warranty applies only to products in ordinary household use.**
