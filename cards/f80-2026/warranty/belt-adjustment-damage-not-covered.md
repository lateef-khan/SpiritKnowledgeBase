Printed as its own ATTENTION block in the maintenance section:

**"DAMAGE TO THE RUNNING BELT RESULTING FROM IMPROPER TRACKING / TENSION ADJUSTMENTS IS NOT COVERED UNDER
THE SOLE WARRANTY."**

The tension section adds the mechanism: over-tightening will cause belt damage and premature bearing
failure. The warranty section on page 34 makes the same point more generally, excluding damage from improper
maintenance.
