**"Warranty will be void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled, insulated environment. (Examples:
Garage, outside, carport, etc)"**

The manual names a garage, outside, and a carport as examples of places that void the warranty. It gives no
temperature or humidity range that defines "climate-controlled".

Two related limits sit elsewhere in the manual: exclusion 8 excludes **product use in any environment other
than a residential setting**, and the electrical section gives a **maximum operating temperature of 40
degrees C with humidity of 95% non-condensing** and says the product is not designed for use outdoors, near
a pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.
