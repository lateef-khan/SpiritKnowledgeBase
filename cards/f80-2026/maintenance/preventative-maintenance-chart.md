The chart is a blank log the owner fills in. Its four task columns and their intervals, as printed:

| Task | Interval |
|---|---|
| Vacuum under motor cover & check wiring | Every other month |
| Clean & inspect deck for lubrication | Every other month |
| Lubricate deck | Every 90 hours or sooner if dry |
| Inspect belt tracking, adjust if necessary | Monthly |

There is a fifth column headed **Date** for recording when each task was done.

**Two other intervals live outside this chart**: vacuum underneath the treadmill monthly, and wipe the belt
edge and the area between belt and frame once a month.

**The manual points at the wrong page for this chart.** The General Cleaning paragraph on page 29 says "Use
the chart on Pg. 5 to record your maintenance", but page 5 is the unpacking warning and the chart is here on
page 30.

**The A1 books print the same four columns with the same intervals**, including the same wrong "chart on Pg. 5" pointer in their cleaning paragraphs. Exception: on the TT9-2018 the "Lubricate Deck (Every 90 hours or sooner if dry)" column is stale boilerplate — that machine has a wax-coated deck with no lubrication schedule; see `sole-treadmill-maintenance-wax-deck-no-lubrication-20000-miles`.
