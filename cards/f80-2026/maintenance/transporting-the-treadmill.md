The treadmill is equipped with **transport wheels that are engaged when the treadmill is folded**. After
folding, simply roll the treadmill away.

The folding section adds the conditions: do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and
locked position, secure the power cord to avoid damage, and use both handrails to manoeuvre the unit.
