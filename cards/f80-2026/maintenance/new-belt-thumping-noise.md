**This noise is normal on new higher end belts and there is no need to contact service.**

When a treadmill belt is brand new, and has been in the package for a few months before use, the area of the
belt that wraps around the rollers takes on the shape of the roller. On starting the treadmill, this shape
causes a **thumping sound each time the belt moves past the rollers**. Once the treadmill has been used for
a few workouts the belt wears in and the thumping sound goes away.

The manual does not put a number on "a few workouts".
