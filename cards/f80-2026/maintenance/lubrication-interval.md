The belt and deck come **pre-lubricated**. After that:

- Lubricate **every 90 hours of use, or every 90 days regardless of usage** as the lubricant can dry out, or
  **whenever you notice the deck is dry**.
- **Check for lubricant every other month** by reaching between the belt and deck. **If there is no
  lubricant present, lubricate even though the "Lube" indicator is not lit on the console.**
- Otherwise, lubricate when the console's lubrication reminder lights **after 90 hours of use**.

Keeping the deck lubricated at the recommended intervals ensures the longest life possible for the
treadmill. If the lubricant dries out, friction between the belt and deck rises and places undue stress on
the **drive motor, drive belt and electronic motor control board**, which could result in catastrophic
failure of these expensive components.

**The Preventative Maintenance Chart on page 30 states the same interval more tersely**: "Lubricate Deck
(Every 90 hours or sooner if dry)". It does not carry the 90-day calendar rule, which appears only here.
