**Problem, as printed:** "Tread-belt does not stay centered / Treadmill belt hesitates when walked/run on".
The manual treats both symptoms as one row.

**Solution / cause as printed:** the user may be walking while favouring or putting more weight on either
the left or right foot. **If this walking pattern is natural, track the belt slightly off-centre to the side
opposite from the belt movement.** See the General Maintenance section on Tread-belt Tension. Adjust as
necessary.

**Two things to notice about this row.** It is the only place in the manuals that tells you to deliberately
leave the belt off-centre, which is the opposite of the tracking section's goal of a centred belt. And it
sends you to the **tension** section for a **tracking** symptom; the tracking procedure is the neighbouring
section (page 30 in the F80-2026 book; the general-maintenance pages in the A1 books).
