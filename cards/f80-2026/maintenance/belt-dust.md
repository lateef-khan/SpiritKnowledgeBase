**This is normal.** Belt dust occurs during normal break-in, or until the belt stabilises. Sometimes the
black dust from the belt will appear on the floor behind the treadmill.

The manual does not say how long break-in lasts and does not ask you to contact service about it.

**The A1 books print the same sentence word for word** ("Sometimes the black dust from the belt will appear on the floor behind the treadmill, this is normal"). The older books with the "wipe the excess off" variant are not covered here; see the extended `f60-2016-belt-and-deck-cleaning` card.
