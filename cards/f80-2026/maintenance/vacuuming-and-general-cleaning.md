Dirt, dust and hair can block air inlets and accumulate on the running belt.

- **Vacuum underneath the treadmill on a monthly basis** to prevent excess build-up of dirt that can get
  sucked up into the inner workings under the motor cover.
- **Every other month, remove the motor cover** and carefully vacuum out dirt and hair that may have
  accumulated.
- **UNPLUG THE POWER CORD BEFORE THIS TASK.**

**A wrong page reference in the manual.** This paragraph is headed "GENERAL CLEANING (Use the chart on Pg. 5
to record your maintenance)", but page 5 is the unpacking warning. The Preventative Maintenance Chart is
printed on **page 30**.

**The A1 books print the same two intervals word for word** (vacuum underneath monthly; remove the cover/hood every other month and unplug first). They carry the same wrong "chart on Pg. 5" pointer; the charts are on PDF p.7 in the 2013-batch and TT9 books, PDF p.8 in the 2011-batch books, and PDF p.2 in the F60-2016 book.
