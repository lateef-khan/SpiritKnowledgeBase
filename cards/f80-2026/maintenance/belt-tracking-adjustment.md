The treadmill is designed so that the tread-belt remains reasonably centred while in use. **It is normal for
some belts to drift near one side while in use**, depending on a user's gait and whether they favour one
leg. Adjust only if the belt **continues** to move toward one side during use.

**Setting tread-belt tracking**

1. A **6 mm Allen wrench (103)** is provided for this adjustment.
2. **Make tracking adjustments on the left side bolt.**
3. Set belt speed at **3 mph**.
4. If the belt is too close to the left side, turn the bolt only **a 1/4 turn to the right (clockwise)** and
   wait a few minutes for the belt to adjust itself.
5. Continue to make 1/4 turns until the belt stabilises in the centre of the running deck.
6. **If the belt is too close to the right side, turn the bolt counter-clockwise.**

Be aware that a small adjustment can make a dramatic difference which may not be apparent right away. The
belt may require periodic tracking adjustment depending on use and walking/running characteristics.
**Proper belt tracking is an owner responsibility common with all treadmills.**
