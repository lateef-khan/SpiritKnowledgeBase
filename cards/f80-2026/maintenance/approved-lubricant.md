**Do not lubricate with anything other than Sole Fitness approved lubricant: 100% food grade silicone
lubricant for treadmills.**

**DO NOT USE silicone caulking for bathroom and kitchen projects, or a lubricant that has a spray/foam
base.**

- The treadmill comes with **one tube of "Lube"**. Extra tubes can be ordered directly from Sole Fitness or
  an authorized Sole Fitness dealer.
- A **Lube-n-Walk kit** can be purchased through either of those sellers.
- **Use 2-3 ounces of lubrication each application.**

The lubricant that ships with the machine is listed in the assembly tools as part **#104**.
