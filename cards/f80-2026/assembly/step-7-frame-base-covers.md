Hardware for this step: **Phillips Head Screw (126) 4 pcs**. Tool: **M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench
(102)**.

Attach **Frame Base Cover (L&R) (38 & 39)** to **Right Upright (4)** and **Left Upright (5)**.

**NOTE: make sure to pull Locking Pin Assembly (157) out and insert End Cap (115) into before first time
operating the machine.**

This is the last assembly step in the manual. The screws left loose in Steps 2 to 4 are tightened once all
components are assembled.
