The assembly pack is sorted by the step that uses it. Part numbers are the manual's own.

| Step | Part no. | Description | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | #80 | Ø 8 × 1.5T Split Washer | 8 pcs |
| 2 | #99 | 5/16" × UNC18 × 3" Button Head Socket Bolt | 8 pcs |
| 3 | #80 | Ø 8 × 1.5T Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #100 | Ø 5/16" × Ø 18 × 1.5T Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #125 | 5/16" × 3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 4 | #80 | Ø 8 × 1.5T Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 4 | #100 | Ø 5/16" × Ø 18 × 1.5T Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| 4 | #79 | 5/16" × 65mm Button Head Socket Bolt | 2 pcs |
| 4 | #72 | 5/16" × 1-3/4" Button Head Socket Bolt | 2 pcs |
| 5 | #74 | 3.5 × 12mm Sheet Metal Screw | 6 pcs |
| 6 | #80 | Ø 8 × 1.5T Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 6 | #100 | Ø 5/16" × Ø 18 × 1.5T Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| 6 | #84 | 5/16" × 1-1/4" Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 6 | #126 | M5 × 15mm Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #126 | M5 × 15mm Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |

**There is no Step 1 hardware.** The checklist starts at Step 2 because Step 1 is unpacking.

**A quantity to watch.** The Step 4 checklist lists 6 flat washers (#100), and the Step 4 instructions use
them as 4 in the first sub-step and 2 in the second, which is 6 in total.
