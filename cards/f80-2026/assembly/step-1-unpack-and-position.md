1. Remove the treadmill from the carton and position it aside on a smooth, levelled floor. **The rear
   should be at least 3' from any wall.**
2. **You must remove the plastic wrap and Styrofoam from beneath the unit before removing the Velcro belt.**
3. Tilting the unit sideways after removing the belt may cause the unit to fold and bounce upwards. **This
   can cause serious injury.**

**The clearance figure here disagrees with page 2**, which requires a minimum of 3 1/2 feet between the rear
of the treadmill and any fixed object. Both figures are printed in this manual and neither page mentions the
other.
