Hardware for this step: **Split Washer (80) 4 pcs**, **Flat Washer (100) 6 pcs**, **Button Head Socket Bolt
(79) 2 pcs**, **Button Head Socket Bolt (72) 2 pcs**. Tool: **M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (102)**.

1. Attach **Console Outer Cover TOP Assembly (21)** to **Console Outer Cover Bottom Assembly (22)** using
   the M5 Allen wrench (102), Button Head Socket Bolt (79) 2 pcs, Split Washer (80) 2 pcs and Flat Washer
   (100) 4 pcs.
2. After slightly securing the above screws, secure the following parts at the middle bottom of Console
   Outer Cover Bottom Assembly (22) with the M5 Allen wrench (102), Button Head Socket Bolt (72) 2 pcs,
   Split Washer (80) 2 pcs and Flat Washer (100) 2 pcs.
3. Connect **Computer Cable (21-16)** with **Computer Cable (22-14)**. Connect **hub board cable (21-15)**
   with **Computer Cable (22-16)**. Connect **hub board cable (21-14)** with **Computer Cable (22-15)**.
4. Push the connected cables into Console Outer Cover Bottom Assembly (22).

**NOTE: tighten all screws from Steps 2 to Step 4 after all components assembly is complete.**
