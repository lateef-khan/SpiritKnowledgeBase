The manual lists five items under Assembly Tools:

| Part no. | Item |
|---|---|
| #47 | Safety Key |
| #115 | End Cap |
| #104 | Lubricant |
| #103 | M6 L Allen Wrench |
| #102 | M5 Allen Wrench (Head Screw Wrench) |

The **M5 wrench (102)** is the one named in every assembly step. The **M6 L Allen wrench (103)** is the one
the maintenance section uses for belt tension and belt tracking; the tracking section calls the same tool a
6 mm Allen wrench.

The **End Cap (115)** goes into the hole left by the shipping locking pin, and the **Safety Key (47)** must
be in the console before the treadmill will power on.
