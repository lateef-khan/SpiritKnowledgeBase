Hardware for this step: **Sheet Metal Screw (74), 6 pcs in total**. Tool: **M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw
Wrench (102)**.

Attach **Front Console Mast Cover (L) (119)**, **Rear Console Mast Cover (L) (120)**, **Front Console Mast
Cover (R) (121)**, **Rear Console Mast Cover (R) (122)** and **Console Repair Mast Cover (113)** to
**Console Outer Cover Bottom Assembly (22)**.

The manual names the M5 Allen wrench (102) as the tool for this step even though the fasteners are sheet
metal screws; that is what is printed.
