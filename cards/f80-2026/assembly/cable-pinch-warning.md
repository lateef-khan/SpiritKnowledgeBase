The manual warns twice, in two different steps, that a trapped cable is an assembly fault that stops the
machine working:

- **Step 2:** "Before attaching the hardware in Step 3, make sure the cables you just connected don't get
  pinched in between the steel tubing. If they do, this may cause issues that prevent the treadmill from
  operating properly."
- **Step 6:** "(NOTE: Please make sure not to pinch the cable)" when attaching Console Rotate Axle (12-01)
  to Console Outer Cover TOP Assembly (21).

Steps 3, 4 and 6 all end by pushing connected cables back into the console covers, which is where a cable
gets trapped. If a newly assembled machine has no display or behaves oddly, check these joints before
looking for a component fault.

**The manual does not say how to tell a pinched cable from a failed part.** It gives no continuity test and
no symptom list for this fault.
