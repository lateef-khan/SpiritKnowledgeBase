**The manual describes this pin twice, under two names, and this card carries both halves.**

**Page 5, unpacking warning.** There is a **safety pin** locked on the treadmill base that prevents the
treadmill from unfolding accidentally during shipping. **This safety pin has to be removed before powering
on or using the incline function**, to avoid any damage to the machine. When removing the safety pin, pull
it away from the treadmill base completely.

**Page 11, Assembly Step 7 note.** "Please make sure to pull **Locking Pin Assembly (157)** out and insert
**End Cap (115)** into before first time operating the machine."

Page 5 gives the reason and the timing; page 11 gives the part numbers and the end cap that fills the hole
afterwards. Neither page mentions the other. Do both: pull the pin out completely, then fit the end cap
before the first run.
