Hardware for this step: **Split Washer (80) 4 pcs**, **Flat Washer (100) 4 pcs**, **Button Head Socket Bolt
(125) 4 pcs**. Tool: **M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (102)**.

1. Fit the **inner square hole B** of the iron part on **Console Outer Cover Bottom Assembly (22)** into the
   **L-type barb plate A** on both **Right Upright (4)** and **Left Upright (5)** as arrow 1, then push
   forward as arrow 2.
2. Attach Console Outer Cover Bottom Assembly (22) to Right Upright (4) and Left Upright (5) using the M5
   Allen wrench (102), Button Head Socket Bolt (125) 4 pcs, Split Washer (80) 4 pcs and Flat Washer (100)
   4 pcs.
3. Connect **Computer Cable (Middle) (117)** and **Computer Cable (22-14)**, then push the cable into
   Console Outer Cover Bottom Assembly (22).

**NOTE: do not fully tighten all screws yet.**

The two arrow callouts in the printed step are drawn on the illustration; the extraction preserved only the
numbers 1 and 2.
