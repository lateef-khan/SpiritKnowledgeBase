Hardware for this step: **Split Washer (80) 8 pcs**, **Button Head Socket Bolt (99) 8 pcs**. Tool: **M5
Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (102)**.

1. Connect **Computer Cable (Middle) (117)** and **Computer Cable (Lower) (118)**.
2. Attach **Right Upright (4)** and **Left Upright (5)** to **Frame Base (2)** using the M5 Allen wrench
   (102), Button Head Socket Bolt (99) 8 pcs and Split Washer (80) 8 pcs.

**NOTE: do not fully tighten all screws yet.**

**Before attaching the hardware in Step 3, make sure the cables you just connected do not get pinched in
between the steel tubing.** If they do, this may cause issues that prevent the treadmill from operating
properly.
