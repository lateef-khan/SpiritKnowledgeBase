Hardware for this step: **Split Washer (80) 4 pcs**, **Flat Washer (100) 4 pcs**, **Button Head Socket Bolt
(84) 4 pcs**, **Phillips Head Screw (126) 4 pcs**. Tool: **M5 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (102)**.

1. Connect **Computer Cable (Middle) (12-06)** at the L-type sheet metal side of **Console Rotate Axle
   (12-01)** with **Computer Cable (21-16)** of **Console Outer Cover TOP Assembly (21)**, using the M5
   Allen wrench (102), Button Head Socket Bolt (84) 4 pcs, Split Washer (80) 4 pcs and Flat Washer (100)
   4 pcs.
2. Attach Console Rotate Axle (12-01) to Console Outer Cover TOP Assembly (21). **NOTE: make sure not to
   pinch the cable.**
3. Connect Computer Cable (Middle) (12-06) at the **Console Fixing Plate (12-03)** side of Console Rotate
   Axle (12-01) with **Computer Cable (Upper) (23-04)** of **Console Assembly (23)**.
4. Attach Console Assembly (23) to Console Fixing Plate (12-03) using the M5 Allen wrench (102) and Phillips
   Head Screw (126) 4 pcs.
5. Attach **Rotate Axle Mast Cover Assembly (24)** to Console Assembly (23).
