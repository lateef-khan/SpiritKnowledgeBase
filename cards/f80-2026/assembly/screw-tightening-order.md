The manual spreads one rule across three steps:

- **Step 2** ends with "NOTE: Please DON'T Fully Tighten All Screws Yet."
- **Step 3** ends with the same note.
- **Step 4** ends with "NOTE: Please Tighten All Screws from Steps2 to Steps4 After All Components Assembly
  Complete."

So leave the upright bolts (99), the console outer cover bottom bolts (125) and the console outer cover top
bolts (79 and 72) hand-tight until the console cover assemblies are all in place, then go back and tighten
everything from Steps 2 to 4.

The manual gives **no torque figure** for any fastener.
