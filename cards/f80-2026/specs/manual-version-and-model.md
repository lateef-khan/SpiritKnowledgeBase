The version string printed under the table of contents is **"F80 -2026 Ver.A"**, and every page footer reads
**"F80 TREADMILL"**.

This is the **owner's manual**, not a service manual: it runs 35 pages, from Product Registration on page 1
to the Manufacturer's Limited Warranty ending on page 35. The warranty inside it is **effective February 1,
2026**.

**What it does not contain.** No factory model code, no six-digit SKU, no motor or incline motor
specification, no wiring diagram, no parts list, no calibration or engineering-mode procedure, and no
electrical parts overview. The older F80 service manuals carry those, but they cover different machines.

Contents, with the page each section starts on: Product Registration 1, Important Safety Instructions 2,
Important Electrical Information 3, Important Operation Instructions 4, Assembly Instructions 5, Folding
Instructions 12, Transport Instructions 12, Operation of Your New Treadmill 13, Bluetooth Connectivity 25,
Charging Function 26, General Maintenance 29, Service Checklist - Diagnosis Guide 32, Manufacturer's Limited
Warranty 34.
