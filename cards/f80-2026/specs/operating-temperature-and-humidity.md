- **The maximum operating temperature specification is 40 degrees C.**
- **Humidity is 95% non-condensing** - no water drops forming on surfaces.

**NEVER expose this treadmill to rain or moisture.** This product is **NOT designed for use outdoors, near a
pool or spa, or in any other high humidity environment.**

The manual gives **no minimum operating temperature** and no storage range.

The warranty adds the other half of this: **the warranty is void if the unit is not in a climate-controlled,
insulated environment**, with a garage, outside and a carport given as examples.
