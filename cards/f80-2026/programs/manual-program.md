**"Manual program is a timed workout with speed and incline controlled manually at any time during the
run."**

That is the manual's whole definition. Unlike the preset pattern programs, Manual has no speed or incline
chart and no Max Speed setting; you set the time and drive the machine yourself.

The Home screen also offers a shortcut to **a manually controlled timed workout**, and to an **immediate
start**, without going through the Programs tab.
