**Templates are preset programs with a preserved set of parameters.** They live in the additional tab menu
at the top of the Programs page.

- **Programs can be saved as templates upon completion**, or
- **create one from scratch in the Templates tab**: tap the **"New Template"** button, choose your base
  program, then save it with adjusted properties.
- **A maximum of 12 template programs can be stored.**
- **To remove unwanted templates, press "Edit" in the Templates tab.**

**Templates and Favorites are different things** with the same limit of 12: a Favorite is a bookmark to a
program or class, a Template stores the settings you chose.
