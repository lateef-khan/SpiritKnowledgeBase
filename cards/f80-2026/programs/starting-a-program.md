**Once you have decided on the program, press the "Start This Program" button on the screen, or the physical
"START" key on the machine, to begin your workout.**

- **After a short countdown the console appears in its workout mode.**
- **Warm-up and cool down phases may be skipped by pressing "Skip".**

If the touchscreen is out of action, page 24 describes starting from the LED display instead: press the
START key directly, or press the PROGRAM key to select a program, and the machine uses preset values for
age, weight and other program-specific information.
