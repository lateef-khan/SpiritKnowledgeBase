**"Air Force, Army, Navy, Coast Guard, PEB and Marine Corps are US military tests that measure the time
required for you to cover a certain distance. The speed is controlled manually."**

So all six work the same way: a fixed distance, a manually driven speed, and a time as the result. The
manual does not print the distance for any of them, and does not print scoring tables.

**These differ from the Gerkin Protocol**, which is automatic - it drives speed and incline itself and needs
a heart rate monitor.
