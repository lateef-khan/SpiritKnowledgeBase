**"By joining your first event, you agree to share your username, profile image, and workout data (such as
workout dates, distances, active time, and calories) with other participants."**

That is the whole of the manual's statement on the subject. It names four workout fields explicitly -
**workout dates, distances, active time and calories** - and prefixes them with "such as", so the list is
not stated to be exhaustive.

**The manual gives no way to withdraw this consent** and does not say what happens if you leave an event.
