**Problem:** house circuit breaker trips, but not the treadmill circuit breaker.

**Solution / cause as printed:** check that the treadmill is the only appliance on the circuit. See
"Important Electrical Information" in the front of the manual for more details.

That electrical section adds that AFCI/GFCI breakers may trip from the treadmill's high inrush current,
lists other devices that commonly share a circuit and cause tripping, and says the treadmill should
optimally be the only device on the circuit.

**This owner's manual gives no high-inrush replacement breaker part numbers.** The older F80 service
manuals name specific replacement breakers; this manual does not, so nothing here should be read as
recommending a part.
