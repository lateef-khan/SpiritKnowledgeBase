**If voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more, the performance of the treadmill may be affected.
Such conditions are not covered under the warranty.**

If you suspect the voltage is low, contact your local power company or a licensed electrician for proper
testing.

Two other places in the manual reach the same problem from the other direction: the Service Checklist says
a treadmill that only reaches about 7 mph while the display shows a higher speed has low AC voltage and
that a minimum of 120 volt AC current is required, and the warranty excludes damage from inadequate power
supply.
