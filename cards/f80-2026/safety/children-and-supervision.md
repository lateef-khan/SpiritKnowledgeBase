- **Keep children under the age of 13 away from this machine.** There are obvious pinch points and other
  caution areas that can cause harm.
- This appliance is **not intended for use by persons with reduced physical, sensory or mental
  capabilities, or lack of experience and knowledge**, unless they have been given supervision or
  instruction concerning use of the appliance by a person responsible for their safety.
- **Remove the tether cord after use to prevent unauthorized treadmill operation.** Removing the safety key
  also secures the treadmill from unauthorized use.

The manual gives no upper age limit and no user weight limit.
