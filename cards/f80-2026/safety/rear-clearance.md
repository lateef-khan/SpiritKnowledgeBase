**The manual prints two different figures and this card picks neither.**

- **Page 2, Important Safety Instructions:** "Do not block the rear of the treadmill. Provide a minimum of
  **3 1/2 feet** clearance between the rear of the treadmill and any fixed object."
- **Page 8, Assembly Step 1:** "Remove the treadmill from the carton and position it aside on a smooth,
  leveled floor. The rear should be **at least 3'** from any wall."

3 1/2 feet and 3 feet are different requirements for the same distance, stated six pages apart in the same
manual. Nothing in the manual reconciles them, so a technician should treat 3 1/2 feet as the figure the
safety section demands and know that the assembly page says something else.
