**The pulse sensors are not medical devices.** Various factors, including the user's movement, may affect
the accuracy of heart rate readings. The pulse sensors are intended **only as exercise aids in determining
heart rate trends in general**.

This is the manual's only statement about heart rate accuracy. It does not give a tolerance figure and does
not say what to do about a reading that looks wrong.

**Note the other half of the picture.** Page 24 says heart rate programs are intended for wireless
Bluetooth wearables only, and that you must receive a strong, steady value in the heart rate window.
