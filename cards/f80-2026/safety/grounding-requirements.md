**This product must be grounded.** If the treadmill malfunctions or breaks down, grounding gives electric
current a path of least resistance and reduces the risk of electric shock. The treadmill ships with a cord
carrying an equipment-grounding plug, which must go into an outlet that is properly installed and grounded
to all local codes and ordinances.

**DANGER** - improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric
shock. Check with a qualified electrician or serviceman if you are in doubt whether the product is
properly grounded.

- **Do not modify the plug.** If it will not fit the outlet, have a proper outlet installed by a qualified
  electrician.
- The product is for use on a **nominal 120-volt circuit**.
- A **temporary adapter** may be used to connect the plug to a 2-pole receptacle only until a properly
  grounded outlet can be installed by a qualified electrician. The green rigid ear-lug extending from the
  adapter must be connected to a permanent ground such as a properly grounded outlet box cover, and
  whenever the adapter is used it must be held in place by a metal screw.
