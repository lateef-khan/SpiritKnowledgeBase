| 5 | #125 | 3/8" x 1-3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 6 | #90 | 3.5 mm x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 6 pcs |
| 7 | #99 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Flat Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 8 | #126 | M5 x 10 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
