Eight illustrated assembly steps (hardware item numbers in brackets). This book prints no Velcro-strap unpacking warning.

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill base frame from the carton onto firm, level ground.
2. Slide the handrail bottom covers (118, 120) onto the left and right console mast tubes (5 & 4); connect the main-frame Computer Cable (32) to the Computer Cable (33) at the bottom of the right tube (4).
3. Bolt the upright tubes (5 & 4) to the main frame (1) through brackets (151 & 152), hand-tight only until the console is on: three Split Washers (85), three Flat Washers (102) and three Button Head Socket Bolts (100) per tube, plus two Curved Washers (131) and two Flat Head Socket Bolts (99) at the front of each tube. Partially tighten bolts (100) with the M6 L wrench (105) and bolts (99) with the M5 driver (104). Do not pinch the right-upright harness.
4. Connect lower Computer Cable (33) to upper Computer Cable (31), Remote Speed Cable (34) to upper Speed Cable (112), and Remote Incline Cable (35) to upper Incline Cable (113).
5. Fix the Console Assembly (36) on top of the uprights with four Button Head Socket Bolts (125) and four Split Washers (85); tighten completely with the M6 wrench without pinching cables.
6. Join the two halves of each handrail cover (117 & 118; 119 & 120) with three Sheet Metal Screws (90) per set of plastics.
7. Bolt the handrail support (3) between the uprights with four Flat Head Socket Bolts (99); fix the end tabs (146 & 147), slip the Beverage Holder (48) over the support, and check the safety-key magnet (63) is on the console.
8. Fully tighten the mast-to-frame bolts with the M6 wrench and fix the console mast covers (40R & 41L) to each mast with two Phillips Head Screws (126) per side.
