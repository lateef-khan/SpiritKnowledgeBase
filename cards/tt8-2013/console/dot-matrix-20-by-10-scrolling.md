**Twenty columns of dots, 10 high.** The dots show approximate effort only; the 20 columns divide the total program time (1/20th each). The window shows the **left half** of the speed and incline profiles, and once the program begins the profiles **scroll right to left**. Press **Enter** to change views; scrolling through both starts an automatic alternation of the two displays.
