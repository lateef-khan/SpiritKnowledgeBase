1. Press and hold **Start, Stop and Enter** at the same time, until the display shows 'Maintenance Menu'. Press Enter.
2. Scroll with the **Fast/Slow** keys; **Stop** returns to the previous menu selection. The selections are:
   - **A. Key Test**.
   - **B. Display Test**.
   - **C. Functions** (Enter; Fast/Slow to scroll): **I. Display Mode** (off = power down after 30 minutes inactivity); **II. Pause Mode** (on = 5 minutes of pause, off = pause indefinitely); **III. Maintenance** (odometer readings); **IV. Units** (English or Metric); **V. Key Tone** (beep off or on when a button is pressed).
   - **D. Security** - locks the keypad; with the child lock on, the keypad stays dead unless you press and hold **Start and Enter for 3 seconds**.
   - **E. Exit**.
