The bag closes exactly against the six steps: bolts 4 go 4 + 4 + 4 into steps 1-3; washers 8 + 8 + 8 + 2 into steps 1, 2, 3 and 5; lock nuts 4 + 4 + 4 + 2 into steps 1, 3, 4 and 5; the four acorn nuts into step 2; bolts 11 go 4 + 2 + 4 into steps 4, 5 and 6; the four bolts 13 into step 6. The stand's parts (two stabilizers, two stand tubes, connecting plate, two dumbbell brackets, storage holder, tablet holder, four foot pads) are on the parts card. Dumbbells and cradles are sold separately.
