**Step 3.** Attach the **Dumbbell Brackets (9)** to the **Stand Tubing (3)**, securing with **4 Allen Bolts (4, M8 x 55), 8 Flat Washers (5) and 4 Lock Nuts (6)** - two bolts per bracket, one bracket per upright. This uses the last four of the twelve long bolts.

**Step 4.** Attach the **Storage Tray (10)** to the dumbbell brackets, securing with **4 Allen bolts (11, M8 x 16) and 4 Lock Nuts (6)** - no washers in this step. The parts list calls the tray the "Storage holder".
