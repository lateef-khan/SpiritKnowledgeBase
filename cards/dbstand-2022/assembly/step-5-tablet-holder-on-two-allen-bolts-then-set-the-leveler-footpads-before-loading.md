1. Attach the **Tablet Holder (12)** to the **Storage Tray (10)**, securing with **2 Allen Bolts (11, M8 x 16), 2 Flat Washers (5) and 2 Lock Nuts (6)**.
2. **Adjust the Leveler Footpads (14) if you find the dumbbell stand is unbalanced - before you put the dumbbells on.** There are four, one under each end of the two stabilizers (the parts list: "Foot pad M8, 4").

The two washers here are the last of the twenty-six in the bag.
