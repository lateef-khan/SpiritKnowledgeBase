1. Attach the **Stabilizer Left (1)** to the **Stand Tubing (3)**, securing with **2 Allen Bolts (4, M8 x 55), 4 Flat Washers (5) and 2 Lock Nuts (6)** - a washer under the bolt head and under the nut.
2. Repeat for the **Stabilizer Right (2)**. **Hand tighten the bolts and nuts** - the frame is squared up when the connecting plate and brackets go on.

Each stabilizer is a foot; the two stand tubes (3) are the uprights.
