Attach the **Stand Tube Connecting Plate (8)** to the **Stand Tubing (3)**, securing with **4 Allen Bolts (4, M8 x 55), 8 Flat Washers (5) and 4 Acorn Nuts (7)**.

This is the only step that uses the acorn (cap) nuts; every other nut on the stand is a lock nut. The plate is the 4 mm cross-member low between the uprights.
