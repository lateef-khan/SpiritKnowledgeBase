1. Attach the **12.5 lb dumbbell cradles** to the **Dumbbell Brackets (9)** with **4 Allen Bolts (11, M8 x 16)**.
2. Attach the **25 lb or 55 lb dumbbell cradles** to the brackets with **4 Allen Bolts (13, M8 x 35)**.

**"Only XTERRA Adjustable Dumbbell Cradles can be mounted to this stand (Not included.)"** - the cover repeats that dumbbells and cradles are sold separately. The cradle is the dumbbell tray that ships with each ADB dumbbell; the heavier trays are deeper, hence the longer bolts. The bag holds bolts for both sizes, so four are always left over.
