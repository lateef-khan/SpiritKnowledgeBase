**Xterra cradles only, and none in the box.** The cover says "Dumbbells and cradles are sold separately", and the
last assembly step: "Only XTERRA Adjustable Dumbbell Cradles can be mounted to this stand (Not included.)" The
cradles are the trays the adjustable dumbbells sit in, and the stand takes both sizes on its two brackets -
"the 12.5lb Dumbbell cradles" with four M8 x 16 bolts (part 11), "the 25lb or 55lb Dumbbell cradles" with four
M8 x 35 bolts (part 13). The assembly section holds those steps.

**No load rating is printed.** The book never states how much the stand carries, what it weighs, or its assembled
height and width; the only size figures are in the parts list - two stabilizers "38*38*515", two stand tubes
"38*38*900", a 4 mm connecting plate - millimetres by the bolt sizes beside them, and the safety page's "This
product is very heavy." The heaviest Xterra dumbbell it is built for is the 55 lb model
(`adb55-2022-specs-five-weight-settings-11-to-55-lb-with-eight-plates`), which is the nearest thing to a capacity
the books offer.

Also on the stand: a **tablet holder** (12) bolted to the storage tray (10), and four **leveler footpads** (14) to
be adjusted "if you find the dumbbell stand is unbalanced before you put the dumbbells on." The dumbbell books
themselves are `adb125pr-2022-specs-five-weight-settings-2-5-to-12-5-lb-per-hand-with-sixteen-plates` and
`adb25-2022-specs-five-weight-settings-5-to-25-lb-with-eight-plates`.
