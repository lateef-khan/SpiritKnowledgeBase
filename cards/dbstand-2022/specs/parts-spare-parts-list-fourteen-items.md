The Dumbbell Stand manual prints a two-page **Exploded View** (PDF pp. 12-13, printed 10-11 - the second page is a picture of the loaded stand captioned *Dumbbells and cradles are sold separately*) and a **DBSTAND SPARE PARTS LIST** (PDF p. 14, printed 12) headed *S/N / DESCRIPTION / SPEC / QTY*, fourteen rows.

| S/N | Description | Spec | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stabilizer (Left) | 38*38*515 | 1 |
| 2 | Stabilizer (Right) | 38*38*515 | 1 |
| 3 | Stand tubing | 38*38*900 | 2 |
| 4 | Allen bolt | M8*55 | 12 |
| 5 | Flat washer | 8*19*1 | 26 |
| 6 | Lock nut | M8 | 14 |
| 7 | Acorn nut | M8 | 4 |
| 8 | Stand tube connecting plate | 4T | 1 |
| 9 | Dumbbell bracket |  | 2 |
| 10 | Storage holder |  | 1 |
| 11 | Allen bolt | M8*16 | 10 |
| 12 | Tablet holder |  | 1 |
| 13 | Allen bolt | M8*35 | 4 |
| 14 | Foot pad | M8 | 4 |

**What the list tells a service call.** The frame is two stabilizers (1, 2; 38 x 38 x 515 mm) and two stand tubes (3; 38 x 38 x 900 mm) joined by the connecting plate (8); the two dumbbell brackets (9) carry the storage holder (10) and the tablet holder (12); the four foot pads (14) are the levelers. The fasteners are the pack checklist's counts exactly - 12 Allen bolts M8*55 (4), 26 flat washers (5), 14 lock nuts (6), 4 acorn nuts (7), 10 Allen bolts M8*16 (11) and 4 Allen bolts M8*35 (13). **Two things the stand needs are not rows**: the wrench and Allen key the pack checklist shows are not listed, and the **dumbbell cradles are sold separately** - the assembly page mounts *XTERRA Adjustable Dumbbell Cradles (Not included)* with bolts 11 (12.5 lb cradles) or 13 (25 lb or 55 lb cradles). The dumbbells themselves are `adb125pr-2022-specs-parts-spare-parts-list-for-the-pair-with-sixteen-plates`, `adb25-2022-specs-parts-spare-parts-list-for-one-dumbbell-with-eight-plates-of-1-1-kg` and `adb55-2022-specs-parts-spare-parts-list-for-one-dumbbell-with-eight-plates-of-2-5-kg`.
