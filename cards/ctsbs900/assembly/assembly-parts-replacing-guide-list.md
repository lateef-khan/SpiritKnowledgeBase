The whole service manual (PS040081-20-01, 31 pages) is one chapter, the PARTS REPLACING GUIDE. It holds nine
procedures; the contents page lists eight.

| Page | Procedure | In the contents |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Replacing the Pedal | yes |
| 5 | Replacing the Drive Motor | yes |
| 10 | Replacing the Incline Motor | yes |
| 14 | Replacing the Control box | yes |
| 15 | Replacing the Bearing 608VV on the Track | yes |
| 18 | Replacing the Front Roller | yes |
| **21** | **Replacing the Rear Roller** | **no** |
| 25 | Replacing the Inverter, Filter, and Braking resistor | yes |
| 29 | Replacing the Electromagnetic Brake | yes |

Every procedure but the control box opens the same way: unplug the machine, release the electromagnetic
brake behind the motor with a flat tool such as a ruler, and take off the rear (or front and rear) end caps
and the frame side trim covers. Most then take out four or five slats to open a gap and rotate it over the
part being worked on.

Figures the book prints, each on its own card: slat screws protrude **2 mm** on reassembly; lift motor
sleeve-to-frame screws **296-298 mm** after calibration; belt vertical gap **260-265 mm** at the centre; a
**#13** wrench for the track bearings and a **#2** Allen key to check the motor-shaft hex screws.

Nothing else is in the book - no troubleshooting, no wiring, no exploded view, no torque table. Its text
layer is letter-spaced ("Tu rn of f th e p ow er"), so the pages were read from the OCR supplement.
