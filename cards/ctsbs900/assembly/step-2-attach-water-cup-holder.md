Step 2 of 6. Previous: [step 1](step-1-attach-handlebar-upright-tubes.md). Next: [step 3](step-3-attach-handrail-tubes.md).

1. Position the **Water Cup Holder Assembly (F)** on the Left and Right Handlebar Upright Tubes, and pull the **Heartbeat Wire** of the Water Cup Holder Assembly out through the upper hole of the **Right Handlebar Upright Tube**. After connecting the **Control Wires**, place the wires inside the Upright Tube.
2. Secure it with **Screws (d)** and **Washers (e, f)** without tightening them. Ensure the screws for the Water Cup Holder Assembly (F) can all be tightened simultaneously with the Left and Right Handlebar Upright Tubes before using a tool to fully tighten all screws.

**Note:** During assembly, take care not to pinch any wires.
