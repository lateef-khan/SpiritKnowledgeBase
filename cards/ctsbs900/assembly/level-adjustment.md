Placing the treadmill on an uneven surface or unstable supports can cause it to
wobble during use, and the incline adjustment may also be affected.

The method for level adjustment is as follows:

1. Rotate **A** counterclockwise to loosen it.
2. Adjust **B** to the appropriate height.
3. Rotate **A** clockwise to tighten it.
4. The adjustment method is the same for both sides.

**A** and **B** are called out on the illustration only; the text never names them
in words. From the drawing, **A** is the lock nut and **B** the adjustable foot.

## The other half of this fact is at the back of the manual

This page gives the method but neither the location nor the tool. Page 44, in
MACHINE CARE, gives both: **2 levelling pads on the bottom of the rear stabiliser,
adjusted with a 14mm wrench (or adjustable wrench)**. The two pages never refer to
each other. See [the levelling pads card](../../ct800-2016/assembly/ct800-2016-assembly-levelling-pads.md), which holds that half, and [post-workout cleaning](../maintenance/post-workout-cleaning.md), where the manual prints it.

No 14mm wrench is packed with this machine; see [the tools included card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-ct900-assembly-tools-included.md).
