Placing the treadmill on an uneven surface or unstable supports can cause it to wobble during use, and the incline adjustment may also be affected.

The method for level adjustment is as follows:
1. Rotate **A** counterclockwise to loosen it.
2. Adjust **B** (the leveling foot) to the appropriate height.
3. Rotate **A** clockwise to tighten it.
4. The adjustment method is the same for both sides.
