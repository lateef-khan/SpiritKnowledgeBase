1. Turn off the power and unplug the power cord. Using a flat tool (such as a ruler), lift the lever of the
   **electromagnetic brake** located behind the motor to release the brake.

2. Loosen the fixing screws of the front and rear left and right **end caps**, as well as the left and right
   **upright covers**, then remove these covers.
3. Pull the left and right **Frame Side Trim Covers** toward the rear to remove them.
4. Use a screwdriver to gently pry up one side of the **Pedal Decorative Strip**, then carefully remove it.
5. Remove the mounting screws of the **Plastic Pedals**, then remove both the left and right plastic pedals.
6. Loosen the nuts and washers on both the left and right sides of **1 to 2 pedals**, as shown.
7. Remove the 1 to 2 pedals.
8. Rotate the pedal to align the opening with the bearing that needs to be replaced.
9. Use a **#13 open-end or combination wrench** to remove and replace the bearing. After replacement,
   reinstall the remaining components in the reverse order of disassembly.

Note: **if there is abnormal noise from the bearings, check each one individually and replace any that
produce noise.**

The bearing is named by its size code, 608VV, and no part number is printed for it. The rear roller
procedure in the same book removes the whole belt; this one opens a one-or-two-slat gap and works through it.
