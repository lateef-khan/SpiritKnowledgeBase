**The lift motor is calibrated on the machine after fitting, and the check is a length: 296-298 mm from the
screws at the sleeve to the screws at the frame.**

1. Turn off the power and unplug the power cord. Using a flat tool (such as a ruler), lift the lever of the
   **electromagnetic brake** located behind the motor to release the brake.

2. Loosen the fixing screws of the left and right **rear end caps**, then remove the caps.
3. Pull the left and right **Frame Side Trim Covers** toward the rear to remove them.
4. Prepare **two supports** to elevate the frame, placing them on the left and right sides so that the
   **Lift Seat rollers are 2-3 cm off the ground**.
5. Loosen the nuts and washers on both the left and right sides of **4 to 5 pedals**, as shown.
6. Remove the 4 to 5 pedals.
7. Rotate the empty pedal positions to align above the **Lower Control Water Guard**.
8. Loosen the screws and remove the Lower Control Water Guard.
9. First disconnect the wires, then remove the screws and nuts at the **sleeve**, followed by the screws and
   nuts at the **frame**. The lift motor can then be replaced. After replacing it, perform a calibration:
   **hold the lift motor body by hand, turn on the power, and press the start button. Calibration is complete
   once the lift roller stops moving.**

Note printed under step 9: the distance from the screws at the sleeve to the screws at the frame should be
**296-298 mm**, as shown in the illustration.

After calibration, reinstall the remaining components in the reverse order of disassembly. When
reassembling the pedal board, **the screws should protrude 2 mm** as shown.

Like the old CT800 and XT dealer procedures, this motor is zeroed by running it under power; unlike them, a
length is checked afterwards. Every other current Spirit book sets a length before fitting instead. No
wrench size, nut size or torque is printed.
