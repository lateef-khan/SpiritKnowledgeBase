Step 6 of 6 (final step). Previous: [step 5](step-5-attach-console.md). Next: [Powering On](powering-on.md).

1. Cover the **Left and Right Upright Tube Covers (I, J)** and secure them with **Screws (i)**. Next, insert the power cord into the power cord socket as indicated by the icon, then cover the **Power Cord Switch Cover (P)** and secure it with **Screws (q)** and **Spring Washers (r)**, tightening them.

Once this step is complete, proceed to [Setting Up Your Slat Belt Sled Treadmill](powering-on.md) to power on the unit.
