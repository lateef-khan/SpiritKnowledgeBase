Power the treadmill on by plugging it into an appropriate wall outlet, then turn on the power switch located at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood.

**Ensure that the safety key is installed — the treadmill will not power on without it.** See [safety/safety-stop-key-and-tether-cord.md](../safety/safety-stop-key-and-tether-cord.md).

When the power switch is turned on, the treadmill console will take around **10 seconds** to power on. The console will then enter idle mode, which is the starting point for operation.

The power switch is located on the panel at the bottom of the treadmill. Switch position **"1"** = ON, **"0"** = OFF.

**The page gives the switch two different places.** Its first sentence puts the switch "at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood"; its last sentence puts it "on the panel at the bottom of the treadmill". The manual never reconciles them. Assembly step 6 is the only other mention, and it puts the power cord socket and its switch cover at the bottom of the machine - see [step 6](step-6-install-covers-and-power-cord.md). The motor-hood wording is word for word the CT900 paragraph and is most likely carried over from it.

**The whole paragraph above is printed word for word in the CT900 manual** (p. 15), which calls the same part the safety *button*. That machine has its own card - see [plugging in and powering on the CT900](../../ct900/assembly/plugging-in-and-power-on.md). The two cards hold one fact and should be merged once a pass can also fix the CTSBS900 safety and errors cards that link here.

If the console does not power on at all, see [errors/no-code-no-power-to-console.md](../errors/no-code-no-power-to-console.md).
