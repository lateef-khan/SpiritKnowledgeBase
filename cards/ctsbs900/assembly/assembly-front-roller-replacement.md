**Slat-belt tension is set as a height, not a pull: the vertical gap of the belt, measured at the centre of
the running belt, is 260-265 mm.**

1. Turn off the power and unplug the power cord. Using a flat tool (such as a ruler), lift the lever of the
   **electromagnetic brake** located behind the motor to release the brake.

2. Loosen the fixing screws of the front and rear left and right **end caps**, as well as the left and right
   **upright covers**, then remove these covers.
3. Pull the left and right **Frame Side Trim Covers** toward the rear to remove them.
4. Use a screwdriver to gently pry up one side of the **Pedal Decorative Strip**, then carefully remove it.
5. Remove the mounting screws of the **Plastic Pedals**, then remove both the left and right plastic pedals.
6. Use an Allen wrench to remove the **front roller adjustment screw**.
7. With **two people** working together, one person lifts the running belt while the other removes the front
   roller. **Be careful not to damage the front roller during removal.**

After replacement, reinstall the remaining components in the reverse order of disassembly. When placing the
front roller back, **insert a protective spacer between the shaft and the frame plate** before positioning
it into the frame. Take extra care to avoid impacting the shaft. Once the roller is in place, remove the
spacer.

When adjusting the belt tension, set the **vertical gap of the belt to 260-265 mm**, measured at the centre of
the running belt.

No Allen key size is printed, and the illustration with the 260~265 dimension is the only guide to where the
gap is taken. The rear roller procedure repeats the spacer and the gap word for word.
