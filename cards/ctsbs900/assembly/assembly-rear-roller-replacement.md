**The rear roller comes out only after the front roller has: steps 7 and 8 take the front roller out on the
way.** Both rollers go back with the same spacer caution and the same 260-265 mm belt gap.

1. Turn off the power and unplug the power cord. Using a flat tool (such as a ruler), lift the lever of the
   **electromagnetic brake** located behind the motor to release the brake.

2. Loosen the fixing screws of the front and rear left and right **end caps**, as well as the left and right
   **upright covers**, then remove these covers.
3. Pull the left and right **Frame Side Trim Covers** toward the rear to remove them.
4. Use a screwdriver to gently pry up one side of the **Pedal Decorative Strip**, then carefully remove it.
5. Remove the mounting screws of the **Plastic Pedals**, then remove both the left and right plastic pedals.
6. Remove the **Multi-Ribbed Elastic Belt** from the motor pulley. **Before removing the belt, mark its
   position.**
7. Use an Allen wrench to remove the **front roller adjustment screw**.
8. With two people working together, one person lifts the running belt while the other removes the front
   roller.
9. Use an Allen wrench to remove the **rear roller screw**.
10. With two people working together, one person lifts the running belt while the other holds the
    Multi-Ribbed Elastic Belt and lifts the rear roller to remove it.

Note: do not damage the rear roller. When installing the Multi-Ribbed Elastic Belt, take care not to cut or
damage it.

After replacement, reinstall the remaining components in the reverse order of disassembly, ensuring the belt
is positioned according to the marks made earlier. When placing the front roller, **insert a protective
spacer between the shaft and the frame plate** before positioning it into the frame; take extra care to avoid
impacting the shaft, and remove the spacer once the roller is in place. When adjusting the belt tension, set
the **vertical gap of the belt to 260-265 mm**, measured at the centre of the running belt.
