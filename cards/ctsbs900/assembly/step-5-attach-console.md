Step 5 of 6. Previous: [step 4](step-4-attach-console-bracket.md). Next: [step 6](step-6-install-covers-and-power-cord.md).

1. Connect the wiring of the console rack to the wiring of the **Console (L)**.
2. Place the Console (L) onto the previously assembled console rack, then secure it with **screws (j)** and **washers (k)** to lock the console assembly onto the console rack.

The console rack wiring has six connectors, in upper/lower pairs: Control Cable (Upper/Middle), Handrail Heart Rate Cable (Upper/Lower), and Keypad Connection Cable (Upper/Lower).

**Note:** During assembly, take care not to pinch any wires. **The cable slot shown is reserved for a TV system; this model does not require it, and the console does not have a TV cable.**
