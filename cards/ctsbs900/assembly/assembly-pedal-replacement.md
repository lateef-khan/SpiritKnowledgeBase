1. Turn off the power and unplug the power cord. Using a flat tool (such as a ruler), lift the lever of the
   **electromagnetic brake** located behind the motor to release the brake.

2. Loosen the fixing screws of the left and right **rear end caps**, then remove the caps.
3. Pull the left and right **Frame Side Trim Covers** toward the rear to remove them.
4. Loosen the **nylon nuts and washers** on both the left and right sides of the pedal that needs replacing,
   as shown in the illustration.
5. Remove the pedal and install the new slat. After replacement, reinstall the previously removed nylon nuts.
   When reassembling the pedal as shown in the illustration, **ensure the screw protrudes 2 mm**. Finally,
   reinstall the remaining parts in the reverse order of disassembly.

"Pedal" and "slat" are the same part in this book: the running surface is a belt of slats, and the manual
uses both words in one step. The 2 mm protrusion is repeated as a note in the drive motor and incline motor
procedures, which also have slats off. No nut size or torque is printed.
