1. Turn off the power and unplug the power cord. Using a flat tool (such as a ruler), lift the lever of the
   **electromagnetic brake** located behind the motor to release the brake.

2. Loosen the fixing screws of the front and rear left and right **end caps**, as well as the left and right
   **upright covers**, then remove these covers.
3. Pull the left and right **Frame Side Trim Covers** toward the rear to remove them.
4. Use a screwdriver to gently pry up one side of the **Pedal Decorative Strip**, then carefully remove it.
5. Remove the mounting screws of the **Plastic Pedals**, then remove both the left and right plastic pedals.
6. Loosen the **nuts and spacers** on both the left and right sides of **4 to 5 pedals**, as shown.
7. Remove the 4 to 5 pedals.
8. Rotate the empty pedal positions to align above the **Lower Control Water Guard**.
9. Loosen the screws and remove the Lower Control Water Guard.
10. At the positions shown in the illustration, disconnect the **motor wires (U/V/W)**.
11. Rotate the empty pedal positions to where the motor is visible, then remove the belt on the left side of
    the motor. **Before removing the belt, mark its position.**
12. Loosen the **four motor mounting screws**, lift the motor upward, then pull it backward to remove it.
13. After replacement, reinstall the remaining components in the reverse order of disassembly, ensuring the
    belt is positioned according to the marks made earlier.

Two notes are printed under the last step: when installing a **multi-groove belt**, avoid cutting or damaging
it; and when reassembling the running board, **the screws should protrude 2 mm** as shown.

The motor wires are labelled U, V and W; no belt tension figure, screw size or torque is printed. The book
uses "pedal" for two parts: the **Plastic Pedals** of step 5 are a left and a right part held by mounting
screws, and the **pedals** of steps 6 and 7 are the slats, held by nuts and spacers.
