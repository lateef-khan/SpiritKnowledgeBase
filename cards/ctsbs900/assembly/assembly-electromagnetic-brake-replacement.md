1. Turn off the power and unplug the power cord. Using a flat tool (such as a ruler), lift the lever of the
   **electromagnetic brake** located behind the motor to release the brake.

2. Loosen the fixing screws of the front and rear left and right **end caps**, as well as the left and right
   **upright covers**, then remove these covers.
3. Pull the left and right **Frame Side Trim Covers** toward the rear to remove them.
4. Loosen the nuts and washers on both the left and right sides of **4 to 5 pedals**, as shown.
5. Remove the 4 to 5 pedals.
6. Rotate the pedals so that the opening is positioned above the motor.
7. Remove the mounting screws at the **rear of the motor** to take out the electromagnetic brake. **Before
   replacing, first disconnect the inline connectors of the wires.** After replacement, secure the wires in
   the same manner as they were originally routed.

Before installing a new electromagnetic brake, **check whether the two hex screws on the hex block of the
motor shaft are loose. Use a #2 Allen wrench for inspection.** If the screws are secure, the new brake can be
installed.

The same brake is the one every procedure in this book starts by releasing with a flat tool, so that the
slat belt can be turned by hand. The book does not say what to do if the two hex screws are loose beyond
implying they must be tightened first.
