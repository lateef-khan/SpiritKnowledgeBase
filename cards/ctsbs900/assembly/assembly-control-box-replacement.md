There are **three screws on the underside** of the control box. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove them, and
then lift the upper cover of the control box. Disconnect the inline connectors, and remove the **four screws on
the back** to open the upper and lower covers of the control box. The control box PCB can then be replaced.

**It is recommended to take photos of the wiring beforehand to avoid incorrect reconnection.**

Note: **ensure the keypad connector is securely connected** to prevent poor contact.

That is the whole procedure - one paragraph and a photograph labelled "Control Box PCB". Unlike every other
procedure in this book it does not start by unplugging the machine and releasing the electromagnetic brake,
and it removes no covers or pedals: the control box is the console-side unit, not part of the base.
