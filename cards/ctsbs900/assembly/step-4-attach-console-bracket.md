Step 4 of 6. Previous: [step 3](step-3-attach-handrail-tubes.md). Next: [step 5](step-5-attach-console.md).

1. Place the **Console Bracket (H)** onto the previously assembled Water Cup Holder Assembly, then secure it with **Screws (s)** and **Washers (b, c)** to lock the Console Rack onto the Water Cup Holder Assembly.

**Note:** The upper and lower cables must be passed through the slot to be connected.
