**"When adjusting the belt tension, set the vertical gap of the belt to 260-265 mm. Measure this at the
center of the running belt."**

Both roller-replacement procedures end with that sentence. It is a **sag measurement of the slat belt**,
not a gauge force and not a sonic frequency, and the book gives no other tension figure. The multi-ribbed
elastic belt from the motor pulley to the front roller has no figure at all - the book only says to mark
its position before removal and refit it to the marks.

Two adjacent figures in the same book are not belt tension: the **296-298 mm** between the incline
motor's sleeve screws and frame screws, and the **2 mm** the pedal screws must protrude when a slat is
refitted.

The CT900 gives 120 ~ 130 HZ, the CT800 / CT850 / 40T / XT books 70-75 lbs, for a conventional drive belt;
none of them applies here.
