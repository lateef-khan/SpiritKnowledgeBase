| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Motor | 3.0HP AC |
| Power | 120 volts, 60 Hz |
| Circuit | Dedicated 20-amp circuit |
| Plug/receptacle | NEMA 5-20P standard power plug/receptacle type |

This treadmill must be grounded and plugged directly into a properly installed, grounded outlet — extension cords are discouraged (a required extension cord should be as short as possible and heavy-duty 16-gauge minimum). A minimum of 110 volt AC current is required for normal operation. See the full explanation and grounding diagram at [safety/grounding-instructions.md](../safety/grounding-instructions.md), and voltage-tolerance/circuit-breaker guidance at [safety/electrical-safety.md](../safety/electrical-safety.md).

Low voltage symptoms (e.g. the treadmill only reaching ~7mph despite showing a higher speed on the display) are covered in [maintenance/troubleshooting-common-problems.md](../maintenance/troubleshooting-common-problems.md).
