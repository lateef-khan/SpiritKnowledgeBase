These are the adjustable ranges available in [Factory Mode](../console/factory-mode-min-max-speed.md):

| Setting | Metric range | English range |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum speed | 0.8–1.0 KPH | 0.5–0.6 MPH |
| Maximum speed | 16.0–25.0 KPH | 10.0–15.6 MPH |

The default Quick Start belt speed is 0.5 mph / 0.8 kph — see [assembly/quick-start.md](../assembly/quick-start.md) — which falls at the low end of the minimum-speed range above.
