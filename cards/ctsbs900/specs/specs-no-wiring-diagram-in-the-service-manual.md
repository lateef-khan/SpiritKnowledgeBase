**The CTSBS900 service manual (PS040081-20-01) contains no wiring diagram, no circuit diagram, no pin
definition, no board component map and no electrical-configuration chapter.** Its table of contents is a
single heading, PARTS REPLACING GUIDE, with eight replacement procedures: pedal, drive motor, incline
motor, control box, 608VV track bearing, front roller, rear roller (untitled in the contents), inverter /
filter / braking resistor, and electromagnetic brake. The book runs from page 3 to page 31 and stops.

The word *wiring* appears once, in the control-box procedure: "It is recommended to take photos of the
wiring beforehand to avoid incorrect reconnection." The letter-spaced extraction ("w ir in g") and the OCR
of every page were both searched; nothing else electrical is printed.

What the book does name, without a diagram: a **control box PCB** under three screws and an upper cover,
motor wires **U / V / W** on the drive motor, an **inverter, filter and braking resistor** under the pedals
near the lower control water guard, and an **electromagnetic brake** behind the motor with a release lever
and two hex screws on the motor-shaft hex block (check with a #2 Allen wrench). No voltage, rating or part
number goes with any of them.

For power and outlet figures the owner's manual is the source - see `ctsbs900-electrical-requirements`.
