Per **ASTM F2115-05 Specifications 6.1.2.11**, the minimum clearance required around the Treadmill for access, passage, and emergency dismount is:

- **0.5 m (19.7 in.)** on each side of the Treadmill
- **1 m (39 in.)** behind the machine

This is listed as item 15 of the general safety warnings — see [safety/safety-instructions.md](../safety/safety-instructions.md) for the full warnings list.
