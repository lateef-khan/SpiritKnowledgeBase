When unusual noises are detected from the machine, first check whether the tooth surface of the drive belt is devoid of lubricating oil. If lubrication is required, use the manufacturer-specified **SL-41031** lubricating grease.

1. Remove the adjustment boxes from both front sides, and pull the red handle at the back of the motor upwards to loosen the running belt.
2. Slowly rotate the running belt by hand and inspect the tooth profile on the belt at the red circle to determine if there is any grease remaining. If there is no grease, apply solid grease.

**Where to apply grease:**
- **Apply grease only to the toothed surface** of the belt.
- **Do not add grease to the flat surface** of the belt.
- Also apply oil/lubricant to the slanted surface on the inner side of the belt — this can effectively reduce abnormal noise.

Several inverter error codes (OC, OH, OL, OL1, OLO, HT) list "running belt/deck aging" as a possible cause and "apply lubricant to running belt" as the corrective action — see [errors/oc-inverter-output-overcurrent.md](../errors/oc-inverter-output-overcurrent.md), [errors/oh-inverter-overheat.md](../errors/oh-inverter-overheat.md), [errors/ol-motor-overload.md](../errors/ol-motor-overload.md), [errors/ol1-inverter-overload.md](../errors/ol1-inverter-overload.md), [errors/olo-system-overload.md](../errors/olo-system-overload.md), and [errors/ht-inverter-high-temperature.md](../errors/ht-inverter-high-temperature.md).

For belt tension (as opposed to lubrication), see [maintenance/adjusting-belt-tightness.md](adjusting-belt-tightness.md).

## The opposite rule applies to every other CT9xx

The CT900 and CT900ENT run a hard waxed deck and their manuals say in as many words: **do not apply
any type of lubricant or wax to the surface** (`ct900-belt-deck-cleaning`,
`ct900ent-belt-deck-cleaning`). The CTSBS900 is a slat belt sled - no wax deck, no deck-life figure,
and greasing is a printed maintenance step. Do not carry either instruction across.

## What the manual does not say

The pages give no interval for this - no hours, no miles, no calendar. The trigger is "when unusual
noises are detected". They also give no quantity of grease and no alternative to SL-41031.

The two figures on these pages are labelled only **"L-shaped right-angle ruler"**, **"Do not add
grease to this flat surface"** and **"Apply grease only to this toothed surface"**; the ruler is
named in a caption with no instruction attached, so what it is for is not stated. The heading says
*tooth surface and side of the **running** belt* while the first sentence says the *tooth surface of
the **drive** belt* - the manual uses the two names for the same part on this page.
