Congratulations on your new Slat Belt Sled Treadmill, and welcome to the Spirit Fitness family!

If not purchased directly from Spirit Fitness, the local dealership where you purchased this unit is your administrator for all Spirit Fitness warranty and service needs.

Please record the following at time of purchase:
- Serial Number
- Date of Purchase
- Dealer / Place of Purchase

## Online Support
If you require assistance or are experiencing issues with your Spirit Machine, contact customer care:
- Phone: **1-800-258-8511**
- Email: **questions@spiritfitness.com**

## Warranty Registration
Register online at **spiritfitness.com/warranty** (under the Support tab), or scan the QR code in the manual.

**There is no day count.** The facility's duty is only that "the warranty registration must be completed online to validate the manufacturer's limited warranty", and the exclusion list does not exclude an unregistered product - it reserves the right to request proof of purchase. Do not carry the ten-day card deadline across from the CT900ENT or the 2026 manuals (`spirit-2026t-warranty-registration`), and do not carry the CT800's outright exclusion of an unregistered product across either (`spirit-ct800-warranty-registration`).

The serial number sticker is on the **side frame near the plug-in** on this machine, not on the back of the motor cover as on the CT900.

For the full warranty terms, see [warranty/warranty-coverage-commercial.md](warranty-coverage-commercial.md). For mail-in service contact details, see [warranty/warranty-service-and-contact.md](warranty-service-and-contact.md).
