**A dues-paying facility is the only environment this warranty puts a period against.**
The section is headed WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL and the table holds exactly one row:

| Warranty | Frame | Motor | Parts | Labor | Wear Items |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial (Dues Paying Facilities) | 10 Years | 5 Years | 3 Years | 3 Years | 6 Months |

**There is no Non-Dues Paying row and no Residential row.** That is what separates this
machine from every other Spirit treadmill in this knowledge base. The CT900 prints all
three rows; the CT800, CT850 and XT ranges print a commercial row and a residential one;
this manual prints one row and stops (`ctsbs900-warranty-coverage-commercial`).

**Two sentences in the same section nevertheless speak of home use, and the manual never
reconciles them with the table.** Quote all three to a customer rather than choosing:

- The opening paragraph warrants "all its **home use** Slat Belt Sled Treadmill parts" -
  under a heading that reads WARRANTY - COMMERCIAL.
- Exclusion 2 refuses service call reimbursement for "units not in **ordinary household
  or light commercial use**" - which reads as a limit on the dues-paying placement the
  single row covers (`ctsbs900-warranty-exclusions`).
- The row itself covers a **dues-paying** facility, which is neither ordinary household
  nor obviously light commercial use.

So the manual states no period for a home and no period for a non-dues-paying facility,
while twice implying that home use is what the warranty is for. **It does not say a
machine in a home is uncovered; it says nothing at all.** Absence is the finding -
refer a residential question to the dealer rather than reading the commercial row onto
it.

**Do not carry the CT800 or CT850 answer across.** Those eight exclude a dues-paying
facility outright as item 8 of their exclusion list, which is the exact opposite of the
only row printed here (`spirit-ct800-warranty-use-environment`).

**What the manual never says.** It does not define a dues-paying facility, gives no
examples, and caps no hours of use per day. Do not import the XT five-hours-a-day limit
(`xt-2023-warranty-use-environment`).

**Prisons and correctional facilities are excluded**, by the footnote under the table
and again by exclusion 8 - `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.
