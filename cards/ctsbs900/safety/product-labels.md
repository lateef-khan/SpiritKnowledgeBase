Four labels are applied to the machine. Here is what each one is and where to find it.

## Serial Number Sticker
Found on the side frame near the plug-in. Record the number below the barcode — you will need it to register your Slat Belt Sled Treadmill's warranty. It also shows the manufacturer contact number (870) 935-1107.

## Proposition 65 Warning
Found on the side frame near the plug-in. The State of California requires notice that this unit was manufactured using chemicals that could cause harm with improper use. See www.P65Warnings.ca.gov.

## Console Warning Sticker
Found in the accessory pocket of the console. Read and be aware of the precautions before operating your unit.

## Safety Warning Sticker
Found on the left side of the Slat Belt Sled Treadmill's upright. The same warning text also appears in this manual — see [Safety Instructions](safety-instructions.md). It lists precautions including: consult your physician before use, stop immediately if dizzy or experiencing chest pain, heart rate monitoring is for reference only, read all warnings before use, inspect for damage before use, keep body/clothing/accessories clear of moving parts, and keep children under 13 away from the machine.
