**Every replacement procedure in this manual opens with the same step, word for word:**

> Step 1: Turn off the power and unplug the power cord. Using a flat tool (such as a ruler), lift the
> lever of the electromagnetic brake located behind the motor to release the brake.

- **Two actions on the power** - switch it off, then pull the cord from the outlet. Switching off
  alone is not the step.
- **Then release the brake.** The electromagnetic brake behind the motor holds the slat belt when the
  machine is unpowered, so with the cord out the belt will not turn to bring a pedal, a bearing or a
  roller into reach until its lever is lifted. The tool named is `a flat tool (such as a ruler)`.

**Eight procedures print it**: replacing the pedal, the drive motor, the incline motor, a 608VV track
bearing, the front roller, the rear roller, the inverter/filter/braking resistor, and the
electromagnetic brake itself. **The control-box procedure is the one that does not** - it starts at
the three screws under the control box and prints no power step; it is a console-side box on the
handrail, and the manual gives no reason for the omission.

**No discharge time and no lock-out procedure is printed anywhere in the book**, and the manual has
no safety chapter of its own - this step is its only power-safety instruction. The owner's manual's
disconnect rule for cleaning and service is `spirit-disconnect-from-the-outlet-before-cleaning-or-service`.

**This brake is not the emergency-stop safety key.** The key between the handrails that you hit
downwards or pull out, and push back in to reset, is the stop system described in
`ctsbs900-safety-stop-key-and-tether-cord`; it does not release the belt for service. The lever
this card names is on the electromagnetic brake behind the drive motor, under the pedals.
