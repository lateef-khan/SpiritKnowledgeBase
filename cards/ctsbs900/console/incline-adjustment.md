- Incline may be adjusted anytime **after the belt starts moving**.
- Press and hold the **Incline Up/Down** buttons to achieve the desired level of incline.
- The display will indicate incline numbers as percent of grade (the same as the grade of a road) as adjustments are made.

**Exception:** In Free Run Mode, incline is locked at a fixed 6% by design and cannot be adjusted — see [programs/free-run-mode.md](../programs/free-run-mode.md).
