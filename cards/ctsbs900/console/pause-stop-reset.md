When the treadmill is running, the pause feature may be used by pressing the red **Stop** button once. This will slowly decelerate the treadmill belt to a stop. The incline will go to zero percent. The **Time, Distance, and Calorie** readings will hold while the unit is in pause mode. After 5 minutes the display will reset and return to the start-up screen.

- To resume your exercise when in Pause mode, press the **Start** button. Speed and incline will return to their previous settings.
- Pressing the **Stop** button twice will end the program and a workout summary will be displayed. If the **Stop** button is pressed a third time, the console will return to the idle mode (start-up) screen.
- If the **Stop** button is held down for more than **3 seconds**, the console will reset.
- When you are setting data (such as age and time) for a program, pressing the **Stop** button will allow you to go back one step for each button press.

Note: In Sled Mode specifically, pressing **Stop** once pauses, twice shows the Workout Summary, and three times exits Sled Mode to the idle screen — see [programs/sled-mode-manual.md](../programs/sled-mode-manual.md).
