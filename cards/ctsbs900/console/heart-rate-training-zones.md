"No pain, no gain" is a myth — exercise is much more enjoyable, and effective, when you maintain heart rate in the desired benefit range.

**Maximum Heart Rate (MHR) formula:** 220 minus your age.

Your Heart Rate training zone is 50% to 90% of your MHR:
- **65% of MHR** is recommended for burning fat.
- **85% of MHR** is recommended for strengthening the cardiovascular system.

### Example: 40-year-old user
- 220 − 40 = 180 (maximum heart rate)
- 180 × 0.65 = 117 beats per minute (65% of maximum)
- 180 × 0.85 = 153 beats per minute (85% of maximum)
- Training zone: **117 to 153 beats per minute**

If you enter your age during programming, the console performs this calculation automatically for the Heart Rate programs. With any Heart Rate program you may use the heart rate monitor feature without using the HR program — this works during manual mode or any of the nine fitness tests.

See also [console/heart-rate-perceived-exertion-rpe.md](heart-rate-perceived-exertion-rpe.md) for a non-numeric way to judge intensity, and [programs/heart-rate-control-hrc.md](../programs/heart-rate-control-hrc.md) for how the console's HR1/HR2 programs use 60%/85% of MHR automatically.
