Heart rate is important, but listening to your body also has advantages. Stress level, physical health, emotional health, temperature, humidity, time of day, and what/when you last ate all contribute to the intensity at which you should work out.

The Rate of Perceived Exertion (RPE), also known as the **Borg scale**, was developed by Swedish physiologist G.A.V. Borg. It rates exercise intensity from 6 to 20 depending on how you feel.

You can get an approximate heart rate for each rating by adding a zero: a rating of 12 corresponds to roughly 120 beats per minute.

| Rating | Perception of Effort |
|---|---|
| 6 | Minimal |
| 7 | Very, Very Light |
| 8 | Very, Very Light + |
| 9 | Very Light |
| 10 | Very Light + |
| 11 | Fairly Light |
| 12 | Comfortable |
| 13 | Somewhat Hard |
| 14 | Somewhat Hard + |
| 15 | Hard |
| 16 | Hard + |
| 17 | Very Hard |
| 18 | Very Hard + |
| 19 | Very, Very Hard |
| 20 | Maximal |
