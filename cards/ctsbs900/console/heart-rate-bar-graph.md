Displays a graphical representation of your heart rate as a percentage of your estimated maximum heart rate. When you enter your age during programming, the console calculates your maximum heart rate then lights up the graph to show the percent of estimated maximum heart rate you are currently achieving.

See [console/heart-rate-training-zones.md](heart-rate-training-zones.md) for how maximum heart rate is calculated from age.
