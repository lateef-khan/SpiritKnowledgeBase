The Bluetooth heart rate chest strap is sold separately (not included).

1. Attach the transmitter to the elastic strap using the interlocking key.
2. Adjust the strap as tightly as possible as long as it remains comfortable.
3. Position the transmitter with the logo centered in the middle of your torso facing away from your chest (some people must position the transmitter slightly left of center). Attach the final end of the elastic strap by inserting the round end and, using the locking parts, secure the transmitter and strap around your chest.
4. Position the transmitter directly below the pectoral muscles.
5. Sweat is the best conductor to measure very minute heart-beat electrical signals. Plain water can also be used to pre-wet the electrodes (2 ribbed oval areas on the reverse side of the belt and both sides of the transmitter). It's also recommended to wear the transmitter strap a few minutes before your workout — some users, due to body chemistry, have a harder time achieving a strong steady signal at first; this lessens after "warming up."
6. Your workout must be within range — the distance between transmitter and receiver — to achieve a strong, steady signal. The range may vary but generally stays close enough to the console to maintain good, reliable readings. Wearing the transmitter directly on bare skin assures proper operation. You may wear the transmitter over a shirt if you wet the areas of the shirt the electrodes will rest upon.

**Note:** The transmitter automatically activates when it detects heart activity, and automatically deactivates when it does not receive any activity. Although water resistant, moisture can create false signals, so completely dry the transmitter after use to prolong battery life (estimated transmitter battery life is **2500 hours**). The replacement battery is **Panasonic CR2032**.

If readings are erratic or missing, see [console/heart-rate-chest-strap-troubleshooting.md](heart-rate-chest-strap-troubleshooting.md).
