The one diagnostic instruction in the CTSBS900 service manual, printed at the end of *Replacing the Bearing 608VV on the Track*:

> Note: If there is abnormal noise from the bearings, check each one individually and replace any that produce noise.

So a noise from the track is traced bearing by bearing, not by replacing a set. Reaching them means releasing the electromagnetic brake behind the motor with a flat tool, removing the rear end caps, side trim covers, the pedal decorative strip and the plastic pedals, loosening the nuts on one or two pedals, and rotating the belt so the opening lines up with the bearing to be tested; the bearing comes out with a #13 open-end or combination wrench. Those steps are the replacement procedure and a parts fact; this card is only the diagnosis.

No other noise is diagnosed anywhere in the CTSBS900 service manual (`ctsbs900-errors-service-manual-prints-no-error-codes-or-troubleshooting`). The belt-drive treadmills' noise rows name rollers and drive belts instead (`spirit-2024-errors-noise-in-the-rear-of-the-treadmill`).
