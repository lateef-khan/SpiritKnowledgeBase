**NTCF** means the inverter's temperature *sensor* has failed — it is not the same as **OH** (inverter overheat, an actual overheat condition — see [errors/oh-inverter-overheat.md](oh-inverter-overheat.md)) or **HT** (inverter high temperature — see [errors/ht-inverter-high-temperature.md](ht-inverter-high-temperature.md)).

| Possible Cause | Corrective Action |
|---|---|
| Inverter temperature sensor failure | Replace inverter |
