**The CTSBS900 service manual (PS040081-20-01, 31 pages) is a parts-replacing guide and nothing else.** Its contents are: replacing the pedal, the drive motor, the incline motor, the control box, the 608VV track bearing, the front roller, the rear roller, the inverter, filter and braking resistor, and the electromagnetic brake. There is no error-code table, no per-code page, no Condition / Reason / Solve matrix, no service checklist and no LED debugging page. Every page was read, the flattened pages from their OCR supplements; the words *error*, *troubleshoot* and *code* do not occur.

**The codes for this machine are in its owner's manual**, which prints a seventeen-row inverter table - `LE1`, `NTCF`, `OC`, `OE`, `GF`, `OH`, `OL`, `OL1`, `OLO`, `LF`, `DBUP`, `PRER`, `EER`, `LP`, `ESP`, `HT` (`ctsbs900-le1-inverter-low-voltage` and its siblings, `ctsbs900-no-code-no-power-to-console`). The service manual's one diagnostic sentence is about noise: *If there is abnormal noise from the bearings, check each one individually and replace any that produce noise* (`ctsbs900-errors-abnormal-noise-from-a-track-bearing`).

Two things in the guide bear on a fault call without being fault pages: every procedure starts by lifting the lever of the **electromagnetic brake behind the motor with a flat tool** to release the brake, and the incline motor is recalibrated after replacement by holding its body, powering on and pressing start until the lift roller stops - both parts procedures.
