The Spirit Fitness Slat Belt Sled Treadmill offers a variety of exercise program options:
- **Manual**
- **Four Preset Programs:** Hill, Fat Burn, Cardio, Interval
- **5K/10K Run**
- **Heart Rate Control**
- **High Intensity Interval Training (HIIT)**
- **Nine Fitness Testing Protocols:** Gerkin, WFI, Army (pft), Navy (prt), Air Force (prt), Marines (pft), Law Enforcement (peb), U.S. Coast Guard, and U.K. Chester Fireman (Performance & Prediction protocols)

In Idle mode, press the **Mode** key to select from:
- **Treadmill Mode** — standard motorized treadmill operation.
- **Free Run Mode** — belt moves freely under the user's own power. See [programs/free-run-mode.md](free-run-mode.md).
- **Sled Trainer Mode** — belt generates resistance to simulate pushing a sled. See [programs/sled-mode-manual.md](sled-mode-manual.md).

Note: the source manual does not provide a separate detailed procedure/scoring table for the U.K. Chester Fireman test (unlike the other eight protocols, which each have their own page — see the [fitness test files](fitness-test-overview.md)).
