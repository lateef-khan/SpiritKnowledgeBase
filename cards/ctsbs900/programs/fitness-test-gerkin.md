The **Gerkin** protocol, also known as the fireman's protocol, is a sub-max VO2 (volume of oxygen) test. The test increases speed and elevation alternately until you reach 85% of your Max heart rate. The time it takes to reach 85% determines the test score (VO2max), per the stage chart below.

The **WFI** test uses this same stage chart, but calculates the score differently — see [programs/fitness-test-wfi.md](fitness-test-wfi.md). Before/during/after-test guidance that applies to both Gerkin and WFI is at [programs/fitness-test-gerkin-wfi-protocol-guidance.md](fitness-test-gerkin-wfi-protocol-guidance.md). To interpret a VO2max score, see [programs/fitness-test-score-charts-vo2max.md](fitness-test-score-charts-vo2max.md).
