Heart Rate Control (HRC) uses the Slat Belt Sled Treadmill's incline system to control your heart rate via information from the pulse grips or wireless chest strap (sold separately). Increases and decreases in elevation affect heart rate much more efficiently than changes in speed alone. The HRC program automatically changes elevation gradually to achieve the programmed target heart rate.

## Selecting a Heart Rate Control Program
During setup mode, you may choose either:
- **Weight Control (HR 1)** — attempts to maintain your heart rate at **60%** of your Maximum Heart Rate.
- **Cardiovascular (HR 2)** — attempts to maintain your heart rate at **85%** of your Maximum Heart Rate.

Your Maximum Heart Rate is based on the formula 220 minus your age (see [console/heart-rate-training-zones.md](../console/heart-rate-training-zones.md)) and is automatically calculated during setup mode when you enter your age.

## Heart Rate Control Programming
1. Using the **Program** button, choose the **Heart Rate Control** program, then press **Enter**.
2. The window shows **PRESS ENTER TO MODIFY OR START TO BEGIN WORKOUT**. Press **Enter** for settings, or press **Start** to execute the program.
3. When Enter is pressed, the Message Window shows **PRESS 1 OR 2 TO SELECT THEN PRESS ENTER**. The Dot Matrix window shows "1" — press numeric button 1 or 2, then Enter, to choose HR1 or HR2.
4. Message Window shows **ADJUST TIME THEN PRESS ENTER** with the Time Window blinking. Use Up/Down buttons or numeric buttons to adjust, then press Enter again.
5. Message Window shows **ADJUST AGE THEN PRESS ENTER** with the Incline Window blinking. Use Up/Down/Fast/Slow buttons or numeric buttons to adjust — adjusting age changes the HR value (per the MHR formula). Press Enter again.
6. Message Window shows **ADJUST BODY WEIGHT THEN PRESS ENTER** with the Incline Window blinking. Adjust with Up/Down buttons or numeric buttons, then press Enter again.
7. Message Window shows **ADJUST HEART RATE THEN PRESS ENTER** with the Speed Window blinking. Adjust the target heart rate with Up/Down buttons or numeric buttons, then press Enter again.
8. Message Window shows **PRESS START TO BEGIN WORKOUT OR ENTER TO MODIFY**. Press **Start** to begin the workout, or **Stop** to return to the previous screen.

**Note:** When the Message Window shows **CHECK PULSE**, there is no pulse signal being received and the program cannot be performed. Check and make sure the heart rate strap is functioning normally — see [console/heart-rate-chest-strap-troubleshooting.md](../console/heart-rate-chest-strap-troubleshooting.md).
