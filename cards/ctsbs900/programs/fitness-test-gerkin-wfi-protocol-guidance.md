This guidance applies to both the [Gerkin](fitness-test-gerkin.md) and [WFI](fitness-test-wfi.md) fitness test protocols.

## Before the Gerkin/WFI Test
- Make sure you are in good health; check with your physician before performing any exercise if you are over the age of 35 or have pre-existing health conditions.
- Make sure you have warmed up and stretched before taking the test.
- Do not take in caffeine before the test.
- Hold the hand grips gently, do not tense up.

## During the Test
- The console must be receiving a steady heart rate for the test to begin. You may use the hand pulse sensors or wear a heart rate chest strap transmitter.
- The test starts with a **3 minute warm-up at 3 MPH** before the actual test begins.
- The data shown during the test is:
  a. Time — indicates total elapsed time
  b. Incline — in percent grade
  c. Distance — in Miles or Kilometers depending on preset parameter
  d. Speed — in MPH or KPH depending on preset parameter
  e. Target Heart Rate and Actual Heart Rate — shown in the Message Window

## After the Test
- Cool down for about one to three minutes.
- Take note of your score, because the console will automatically return to the start-up mode after a few minutes.
