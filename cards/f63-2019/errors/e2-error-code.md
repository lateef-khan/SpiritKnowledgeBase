**This is E2, not E0 and not E7.** On this machine the incline fault is E3, not E2.

**Definition**: when the lower board detects over current the Limit current LED lights up and the display shows E2. The lower board is protecting itself and the motor so that neither is burned.

**Solve over current**:

1. Check whether silicone oil has been smeared between the belt and the running board.
2. While the treadmill is used, do not block the running belt.
3. If neither of those is the problem, replace the lower control board, or replace the motor.
