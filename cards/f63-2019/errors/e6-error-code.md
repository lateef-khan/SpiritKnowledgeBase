**This is E6, not E5 (communication) and not E0.**

**Definition**: a lower controller component is at fault, such as a transistor, an IGBT or a control module.

| Part | Troubleshooting |
|---|---|
| Lower controller | Replace Lower controller board. |
| Display board | Only replace the upper control board. |
