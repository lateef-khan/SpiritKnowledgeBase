**The belt speed does not match the console display**: the console is not calibrated. Calibrate the console.

**The treadmill only reaches about 10 kph but shows a higher speed**: this means the motor should be receiving power to operate, and the AC voltage to the treadmill is low. Do not use an extension cord. If one is unavoidable it should be as short as possible and heavy duty 16 AWG minimum. Household voltage may be low; contact an electrician or the dealer. A minimum of 220 volt AC current, 50 hz is required (or 110 volt AC current, 60 hz).

**The motor is not responsive after START is pressed**:

- If the belt moves but stops after a short time and the display shows **LS1/LOW SPEED**, run a calibration.
- If you press start, the belt never moves, and then the display shows LS1/LOW SPEED, contact service.

**Gap in this manual**: the service checklist points at "section 8.2 on Error Message: LS1/LOW SPEED", but this manual has no LS1 section. Section 8.2 is E1. The manual does not define LS1 anywhere.
