**Noise under the motor cover**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Worn brushes or bearings on the motor | Replace with a new motor, or new motor brushes |
| Front roller bearings are defective | Replace with a new front roller |
| The drive belt is misadjusted, too tight or too loose | Adjust the motor position |

**Noise at the rear of the treadmill**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Rear roller bearings are defective | Replace with a new rear roller |
| Rear roller misaligned | Adjust the rear roller position |

**Other noises the service checklist names**

- Noise while running at the **lowest incline level only** can be too much pressure on the incline cylinder.
- A **knocking noise** during a workout: check that all bolts are tightened.
- A **thumping noise** while the belt runs happens with a brand new treadmill, or one that has not been used for a long time. The belt has been shaped by the rollers and hardened by low temperature. Running the belt for tens of minutes makes the thumping go away.
- **Black particles under the treadmill**: the drive belt is breaking in. Vacuum periodically.
- **The belt hesitates when stepped on**: insufficient lubricant, or insufficient tread belt tension.
