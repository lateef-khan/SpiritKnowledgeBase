**Hand pulse gives no reading**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Hands not on the sensors, or only one hand on a sensor | Hold both hand pulse grips |
| The HANDPULSE W/WIRE to console connector is not connected properly | Connect the cable again |
| The wires were damaged when connecting the HANDPULSE W/WIRE to the console | Replace with a new cable |
| The hand pulse board is broken | Replace the console or the hand pulse board |

**The wireless chest belt gives no reading**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| Chest belt not worn properly | Check the belt has proper contact with the skin and is oriented correctly |
| Distance too far, beyond the receiver range | Use the chest belt in front of the console, within 3 feet |
| Chest belt battery weak or dead | Replace with a new **CR2032** lithium battery |

**Erratic pulse display**: another chest belt in use around the treadmill, other magnetic field disturbance, or a broken receiver. Check for other chest belts nearby, change the position or direction of the treadmill, or replace the receiver.

The console reads pulse from the grips or from a receiver, and it shows 0 after 8 seconds with no signal.
