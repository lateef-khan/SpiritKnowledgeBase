**Do not attempt to move the unit unless it is in the folded and locked position.** Secure the power cord to avoid damage, and use both handrails to move the unit.

**To fold**: make certain the treadmill is at **minimum incline**. Lift the running deck until it is secured by the locking telescoping tube assembly in the centre back of the base.

**To unfold**: apply slight forward pressure on the running deck with one hand, at the rear roller area, to relieve pressure on the locking system. Pull down on the unlocking lever and slowly lower the running deck to the floor. **The deck lowers unassisted once it reaches about waist high.**

**Transport**: the treadmill has four transport wheels that engage when it is folded. After folding, simply roll it away.
