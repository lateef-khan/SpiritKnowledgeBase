Whether the console shows the error when the safety key is pulled is a software setting, not a fault. **Check this before you check any hardware for that error.**

1. Remove the safety key.
2. Press **STOP**, **START** and **ENTER** and, at the same time, insert the safety key. The display enters ENGINEERING MODE.
3. Press **FAST / SLOW** or **UP / DOWN** to find **"functions"**, then press **Enter**.
4. Go into **DISPLAY MODE**, then press Enter to choose on or off.

- **off** - the display goes off after the safety key is removed.
- **on** - the display stays on and shows the error after the safety key is removed.

The other half of this fact, the fault meaning of that error, is on the error card.
