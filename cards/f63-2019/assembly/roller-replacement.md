1. With a Phillips head screwdriver, loosen the **2 screws** on the rear adjustment base.
2. With an **M6 L Allen wrench**, loosen the **2 screws** on the rear roller.
3. Remove the motor cover. With a **14mm T-shaped socket wrench** loosen the **4 screws**, and with a **14mm open end wrench** loosen the **1 belt tension screw** and loosen the drive belt. With a **13mm wrench** loosen the front roller screws, remove the front and back rollers and fit the new ones. Reassemble in the reverse order. The running belt tension then needs to be adjusted and centred.
4. With the **14mm open end wrench**, adjust the belt tension using a tension measuring device. **Adjust to the white LBS area, between 70 and 75 LBS.** Use the 14mm T-shaped socket wrench to secure and tighten the 4 securing screws.
