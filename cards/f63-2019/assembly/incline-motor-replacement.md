1. Adjust the treadmill to the folded position, then remove the old incline motor.
2. **The incline range must be adjusted to 225 mm minimum before installation.**
3. Use a combination **M8 Allen wrench and a 14 mm wrench** to fit the new incline motor.
4. Connect the incline motor wiring to the controller:
   - **Red** wire to **UP**
   - **White** wire to **COM**
   - **Black** wire to **DOWN**

Section 11 of the manual repeats the same 225 mm figure for installation.
