**Three wires, one warning.** The parts list names a **Hand pulse wire** (18) from the two hand pulse sensors (25)
on the handlebar, a **Middle sensor wire** (19) and a **Lower sensor wire** (27) that ends at the sensor bracket (21)
beside the magnet assembly (33) on the crank. The owner's manual's handlebar step is one line: "Connect the Middle
Sensor Wire (No. 19) with the Console (No. 08)", then the handlebar tube goes into the rear support tube.

The service document (November 2019, for the FB160 sold as SKU 116419) adds what the picture shows and the manual
does not: **"DO NOT PINCH MIDDLE SENSOR WIRE TO CONSOLE!** TIP: Console front support tube has cutout, and be sure to
slip the wire into notch, secure **Molex connection for speed sensor**, and push terminals down the tubing. Then, use
5mm Allen wrench to tighten handlebars to main frame." Its photograph labels the same two things - "Console wire
slips into notch, secure connection" and "Secure middle speed sensor wire & push down tube".

So the speed signal is a wired Molex pair, not a wireless transmitter, and a pinched or unseated wire in the notch is
the first thing to check when the display shows speed 0; the errors section holds that table. The console itself
runs on two AAA cells that are not in the carton.
