**What the list tells a service call.** The console is item 08, model **X3529**; the seat item 07, model **DD-306**; the pedals item 09, **JG-16B**; the flywheel item 39; the drive belt item 65 (quantity 2); the tension knob item 47; the hand-pulse sensors item 25 and their wire item 18; the magnet assembly item 33 and the sensor bracket item 21. The two tools packed with the bike are the last two rows, items 72 (Allen key) and 73 (hex head wrench).

**The FB360 list is this list with seventeen rows added (74-90) and a few quantities changed** - it is the same frame with a back cushion, rear handlebar and remote-control holder, and its serial numbers read `FB360-665H-PARTnn` (`fb360-2019-specs-parts-list-items-01-to-90`). The FB180 exploded view (2025) numbers its parts 01-74 in the same way but prints no list (`xterra-specs-parts-which-bike-elliptical-and-climber-manuals-print-an-exploded-view-and-parts-list`). The FB160 service document (November 2019) names the SKU **116419** and lists the carton contents but no part numbers.
