**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

Some circuit breakers used in homes are **not rated for the high inrush currents** that occur when a
treadmill is first turned on or even during use.

If the house circuit breaker trips (even at the proper current rating) but the breaker **on the treadmill
itself** does not, replace the home breaker with a **high inrush type**.

**This is not a warranty defect.** It is a condition the manufacturer has no ability to control. The part is
available through most electrical supply stores — the manual names **Grainger part # 1D237**, or online at
www.squared.com **part # QO120HM**.
