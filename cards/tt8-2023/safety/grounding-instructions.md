**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

**This product must be grounded.** Grounding provides a path of least resistance for electric current if the
treadmill malfunctions or breaks down, reducing the risk of electric shock. The product ships with a cord
having an equipment-grounding plug, which must go into an outlet properly installed and grounded to all
local codes and ordinances.

**DANGER — improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric
shock.** Check with a qualified electrician or serviceman if in doubt. Do not modify the plug if it will not
fit the outlet; have a proper outlet installed by a qualified electrician.

The product is for use on a nominal **220-volt circuit (110 VAC on a 120 VAC electronic power system)**.

A temporary adapter may be used to connect the plug to a 2-pole receptacle if a properly grounded outlet is
not available, and only until a properly grounded outlet can be installed. The green rigid ear plug
extending from the adapter must be connected to a permanent ground such as a properly grounded outlet box
cover, and **whenever the adapter is used it must be held in place by a metal screw**.
