**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

**Note: the cylinder has to be unmounted first for these replacements.**

1. Follow the front and rear roller procedure to remove the front and rear roller.
2. Remove the **foot rail locking screws** with a screwdriver and follow the direction shown to remove the
   foot rails.
3. Remove the **8 running deck locking screws** and take the running deck off. The running deck, running
   belt and cushions can now be replaced. Reverse the steps to install them back.
4. **Adjust the running belt tension and centre it.**
