**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

1. Unplug all the rear incline motor wires from the **rear incline control board**.
2. Use **two 14 mm open-end wrenches** to unmount the rear incline motor.
3. **Adjust the spare incline motor to lower — 220 mm.**
4. Install the rear incline motor back with a 14 mm open-end wrench.
5. Plug the rear incline motor wires into its control board.

**220 mm is the rear figure.** The front incline motor on this machine is 225 mm, and the rear motor on the
2016 and 2019 TT8 is 210 mm. Do not carry a figure across.
