Hardware supplied in the box with the Sole TT8-2023, grouped by assembly step. The uprights bolt through left and right gussets (7 & 8), a shape no earlier TT8 book prints. Sizes read off the page rendering.

| Step | Item | Size | Part | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | #86 | Dia 10 x 2.0T | Split Washer | 6 pcs |
| 3 | #124 | Dia 5/16" x Dia 18 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #79 | Dia 3/8" x Dia 19 x 1.5T | Flat Washer | 6 pcs |
| 3 | #100 | 5/16" x 3/4" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 3 | #123 | 3/8" x 2-1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 6 pcs |
| 4 | #86 | Dia 10 x 2.0T | Split Washer | 4 pcs |
| 4 | #91 | 3/8" x 1/2" | Button Head Socket Bolt | 4 pcs |
| 5 | #94 | M5 x 15 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |
| 6 | #125 | 3.5 x 16 mm | Sheet Metal Screw | 4 pcs |
| 7 | #94 | M5 x 15 mm | Phillips Head Screw | 4 pcs |

Tools in the box: #103 Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench, #104 L Allen Wrench, #126 Phillips Head Screw Driver. There is no hardware step 1 or 2.
