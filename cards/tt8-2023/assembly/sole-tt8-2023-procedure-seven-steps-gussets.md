Seven illustrated assembly steps for the TT8-2023. Both uprights are numbered (4) throughout this book (unlike the 5 & 4 of the 2016-2021 books), and they bolt through left and right gussets (7 & 8); the console is a split top/bottom assembly (hardware item numbers in brackets; upright numbering and step-3/4 hardware read off the page rendering).

1. Unpack: remove the treadmill base frame from the carton onto firm, level ground.
2. Install the left and right uprights (4) in the main frame (1) as drawn; connect Computer Cable Lower (33) to Computer Cable Middle (32). Do not pinch the cables.
3. Insert the uprights (4) onto the main frame (1): tighten four Button Head Socket Bolts (100) with four Flat Washers (124) using the Allen Wrench Head Screw Wrench (103), then six Button Head Socket Bolts (123) with six Flat Washers (79) and six Split Washers (86) through the left and right gussets (7 & 8) using the L wrench (104). Do not tighten all screws yet.
4. Connect Computer Cable Middle (32) to Computer Cable Middle (36-27) [as printed], insert Console Assembly Bottom (36) into the uprights (4), and tighten four Button Head Socket Bolts (91) with four Split Washers (86) using the M6 L wrench (104). Do not tighten all screws yet; do not pinch the cables.
5. Connect Computer Cable Upper (35-17) to Computer Cable Middle (36-27), Computer Cable (35-15) to Computer Cable (36-26), and Computer Cable (35-14) to Computer Cable (36-25). Place Console Assembly Top (35) onto the bottom (36) and tighten four Phillips Head Screws (94) with the Phillips driver (126). Then tighten all screws left loose above.
6. Place the Tablet Holder (130) on the console top (35) and tighten four Sheet Metal Screws (125) with the Phillips driver (126).
7. Install Frame Base Cover left (99) and right (98) in the main frame (1) with four Phillips Head Screws (94).
