**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

1. Loosen the **5 motor cover front locking screws** with a screwdriver.
2. Unmount the drive motor **ground wire** and its **2 input wires (black and red)**.
3. Remove the **4 drive motor locking bolts** with a **14 mm wrench** and loosen the drive belt. Reverse the
   moves to mount the new drive motor and its 4 locking bolts, **but do not secure them yet**.
4. Adjust the drive belt tension with a **14 mm open-end wrench** and measure with a tension meter. **The
   tension needs to be about 70~75 LBS.**
5. Plug the drive motor's 2 input wires into the controller — **red to M+, black to M-** — and mount the
   ground wire.
