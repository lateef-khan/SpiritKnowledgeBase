**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

1. Take off the motor cover.
2. Unplug the incline motor wires and unmount the incline motor from the treadmill.
3. **Adjust the spare incline motor to lower — 225 mm.**
4. Install the incline motor back with an **M8 hex wrench** and a **14 mm open-end wrench**.
5. Plug the incline motor wires into the controller.

**225 mm is the front figure on this machine.** The 2016 and 2019 TT8 manuals set their front incline motor
to 235 mm, and the rear motor on this machine is 220 mm. Three different numbers — use the one for the
machine and the position in front of you.
