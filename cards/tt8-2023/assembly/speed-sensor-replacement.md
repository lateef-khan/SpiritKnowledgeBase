**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

Remove the motor cover, unplug the speed sensor wire from the controller, and replace the sensor.

Set the gap before closing the cover: the sensor must sit as close to the front roller pulley as possible
without touching it, aligned with the magnet on the pulley face, and **less than 3 mm away**.
