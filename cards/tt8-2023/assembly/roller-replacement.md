**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

1. Remove both **adjustment base cover screws** with a screwdriver and take the covers off.
2. Remove the **2 rear roller locking bolts** with an **M6 L-Allen wrench** and take the rear roller off.
3. Remove the drive motor cover, then unmount the drive belt from the front roller.
4. Remove the **front roller locking bolt** with a **13 mm wrench** and take the front roller off.
5. **Adjust the running belt to the centre when installing the front and rear roller back** — this is
   necessary, not optional.

Unlike the 2016 and 2019 TT8 manuals, this procedure does not restate the 70~75 LBS drive belt figure; take
it from the drive motor procedure.
