**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

The manual treats the incline and decline motors as one part, "Incline/Decline Motor".

- It is a **230 volt AC motor**. **On a 120 Vac electronic power system it is a 115 volt AC motor.**
- It has **four wires: red, black, white and green**, plus one **3-pin position sensor cable**.
- **AC voltage on the red wire (UP)** increases the incline.
- **AC voltage on the black wire (DOWN)** decreases the incline.
- The **white wire (COM)** is neutral.
- The **green** wire is ground.

The user controls elevation from the console through the motor controller and the decline controller.
