**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

Adjust the drive belt with a **14 mm open-end wrench** and measure with a **tension meter**.

**The tension needs to be about 70~75 LBS.**

This figure is quoted in the drive motor replacement procedure. Unlike the 2016 and 2019 manuals, this one
does not repeat it in the roller and running deck procedures — those simply say to adjust the running belt
and centre it.
