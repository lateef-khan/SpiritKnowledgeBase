**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

**This machine uses a DC drive motor, not an AC inverter.**

- It is a variable speed **0-180 volt DC motor on 230 V**. **On a 120 Vac electronic power system it is a
  0-90 volt DC motor.**
- Three wires: **red, black and green**.
- DC voltage on the **red (white) wire (M+)** turns the motor **clockwise**.
- DC voltage on the **black wire (M-)** turns the motor **counterclockwise**.
- The higher the voltage, the faster the motor turns.
- The **green** wire is ground.

The main controller carries the DC power supply for the console, the incline driver and the **DC motor
driver**, and links the console to the motor.

The console itself is a **15.6 inch TFT touch panel**.
