**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

**This is E4, not E1 (no RPM signal) and not E7 (input power).**

Definition: motor power wire error.

Cause: the power wire of the motor is not inserted into the lower controller.

| Part | Troubleshooting |
|---|---|
| Controller | Insert the power wire of the motor. |
| Drive motor | Replace the motor. |
| Display board | Replace the upper control board. |

The drive motor's two power wires land on the controller as **red to M+** and **black to M-**; a swapped or
loose pair is the first thing to check.
