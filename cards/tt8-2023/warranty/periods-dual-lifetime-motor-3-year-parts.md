**Treadmill warranty, effective February 1, 2023.**

| | **Residential** | **Light commercial (5 hours or less use per day)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame** | **Lifetime** | **Lifetime** |
| **Drive Motor** | **Lifetime** | **Lifetime** |
| **Parts** | **3 Years** | **2 Years** |
| **Labor** | **1 Year** | **1 Year** |
| **Cosmetic Items** * | **90 Days** | **90 Days** |

*Cosmetic items including, but not limited to the following: grips, console overlays, and labels/decals.

**This is a lifetime light-commercial motor**, where the 2016–2021 dual card gives 5 years there — see `sole-tt8-warranty-periods-dual-lifetime-motor-2016-2021`. Same tension as those books: the residential 9-item exclusions sit beside the light-commercial column (see `sole-treadmill-warranty-exclusions-10-item-rust-climate-2023`). Duties, exclusions and service are the 2023 set — see `sole-treadmill-warranty-responsibilities-8-item-photo-duty-2023` and `sole-treadmill-warranty-service-troubleshoot-first-2023`.
