**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

Lube Setup reminds the user to lube the deck. **Default time is 90 hours; the setting range is 90 to 200
hours.** The upper right of the display shows the total hours used.

- At power on, once the total hours reach the set figure the display shows **"LUBE DECK PRESS STOP TO RESET
  LUBE MESSAGE"** for 3 seconds, then closes and goes to IDLE MODE.
- Once at IDLE MODE the message does not appear again until the next interval.
- **Pressing STOP while the message is on screen clears it.**
- **The total hours used and the distance are never cleared by the message** — they keep accumulating.
- To reset the counter deliberately, enter Lube Setup and hit **"Zeroing"**. The display asks "Are you sure
  the lube parameter needs to be reset?"; select Yes to reset the total hours used.
- If the total hours have not reached the set figure and the user resets anyway, the accumulated hours reset
  to zero.
