**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

**Display Mode. Default: ON. Setting: ON/OFF.**

- **ON**: the console does **not** power down when the user removes the SAFETY KEY.
- **OFF**: the console powers down when the SAFETY KEY is removed, and the display turns off to have the
  console power down automatically after **30 minutes of inactivity**. Any key wakes the console.

This is a different setting from **Sleep Mode** on the settings page, which uses a **15 minute** timer.
