**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

**A yellow dot on the gear icon of the IDLE MODE page also appears when a third-party media or mirroring
application has an update waiting.**

1. Go to the Settings page and open the **Media Apps** section.
2. Click the **">"** button to enter the update list.
3. Click the update button for the app you want to update.
4. Click **Back** to return to the Settings page.

**The power supply must not be interrupted during the update**, or the system may be damaged and unable to
function properly again.
