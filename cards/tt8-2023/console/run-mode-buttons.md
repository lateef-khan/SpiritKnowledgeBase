**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

- **Safety Key**: pulling it away shuts the computer down automatically.
- **Stop Key**: stops the treadmill.
- **Start Key**: non-functional.
- **Speed quick keys**: set speed to **2/3/4/5/7/9/12 MI (3/6/9/12/15/18/22 KM)** directly.
- **Incline quick keys**: set incline to **-4/-2/2/4/6/9/12** directly.
- **Fan key**: turns the fan on and off (optional).
- **Display key**: cycles the three training diagrams — **Stats, then Charts, then Track**.
