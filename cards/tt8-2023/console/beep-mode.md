**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

**Beep Mode (Buzzer Switch). Default: ON. Setting: ON/OFF.**

It controls **the sound made when a finger hits the touch panel**, not the physical keys on the console
keyboard. ON gives the buzzer sound on a touch; OFF closes the buzzer sound.

Turning Beep Mode off will not silence the hardware keys.
