**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

On the settings page press the **">" button** to enter the date and time settings.

The device supports switching between **AM/PM and 24-hour clock format**.

Slide up and down to adjust the date and time, then press **"Save"** to keep the changes.
