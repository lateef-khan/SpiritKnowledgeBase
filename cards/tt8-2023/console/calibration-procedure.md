**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

1. From engineering mode, press **Engineering Mode** to enter Calibration.
2. Hit the block to enter the value with the digital keyboard, and press **ENTER** to save and close the
   keyboard. If the entered value exceeds the range, the display shows the max or min value instead.
3. Press the **Calibration** button to start the procedure.

**Accepted range:**

| Units | Max | Min |
|---|---|---|
| English | 12.0 | 0.5 |
| Metric | 20.0 | 1.0 |

**The metric maximum accepted here is 20.0, while the display function table in the same manual gives a work
range up to 22.0 km/h and the metric quick keys go to 22.** Both figures are printed; the calibration page
is where 20.0 appears.
