**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

Turn on WIFI in settings and the system automatically scans for nearby networks. Click the network name,
enter its password, and the system connects with what you entered.

**If the connection succeeds**, pressing the Training button returns you to the Settings page.

**If it fails, the device only shows "Saved" and does not connect.** "Saved" on its own is the failure
state, not a success.

A WIFI status icon on the page shows Connected or Disconnected.
