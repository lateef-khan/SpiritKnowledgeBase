**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

Press the **gear icon above the screen while in IDLE MODE** to open the settings page.

The manual's own sentence says there are **8 sub-menu modes** and then names ten:

1. Unit System
2. Child Lock
3. Sleep Mode
4. Screen Brightness
5. Date & Time
6. Wi-Fi
7. Language
8. Software
9. Media Apps
10. Passcode

**Ten items are listed. The count of 8 in the same sentence is wrong.**

A yellow dot on the gear icon means either the main program or a third-party media app has an update
waiting.
