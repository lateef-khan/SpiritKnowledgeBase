**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

| Reason | Solve |
|---|---|
| House breaker tripped | Reset it |
| Treadmill breaker tripped | Reset the treadmill breaker |
| Treadmill controller shut down and the LED would be ON | Belt/deck lubrication; turn the ON/OFF switch off and turn the power on again |

If the machine stops immediately after START is pressed, the matrix blames a broken controller: turn the AC
switch off and on again, then replace the controller and calibrate it.

**This manual has no General Maintenance section**, so "belt/deck lubrication" here has no accompanying
procedure — use the lube reminder in engineering mode and the owner's manual.
