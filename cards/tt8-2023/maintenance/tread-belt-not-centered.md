**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

| Symptom | Reason | What the manual says to do |
|---|---|---|
| Tread belt does not run in centre | Tread belt tension not even across the belt | "See treadmill belt adjustment" |
| Tread belt hesitates while being stepped on | Insufficient lubricant on the tread belt; tread belt tension insufficient | "See treadmill belt lubrication" |
| Black particles collecting under the treadmill | Drive belt is breaking in | Vacuum under the treadmill periodically |

**Both cross-references point at sections this manual does not contain.** There is no General Maintenance
chapter here — the manual goes from the troubleshooting matrix straight to the parts replacing guide. Use
the owner's manual, or the equivalent section of the 2016 or 2019 TT8 service manual, for the actual
tension, tracking and lubrication procedures.
