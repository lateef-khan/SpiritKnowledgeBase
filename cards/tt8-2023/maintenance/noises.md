**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

**Noise under the motor cover**

1. Worn brushes or bearings on the motor — replace the motor.
2. Front roller bearings defective — replace the front roller.
3. Drive belt misadjusted, too tight or too loose — adjust the motor position.

**Noise at the rear of the treadmill**

1. Rear roller bearings defective — replace the rear roller.
2. Rear roller misaligned — adjust the rear roller position.

**Black particles collecting under the treadmill** is the drive belt breaking in — vacuum under the
treadmill periodically.

This manual carries no general-noises list; the 2016 and 2019 TT8 manuals add entries for incline cylinder
pressure at the lowest incline and for thumping from a cold, newly shaped belt.
