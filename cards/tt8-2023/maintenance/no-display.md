**TT8 2023 ST738-YT066, DC drive motor.**

From the troubleshooting matrix, in the manual's order:

1. The ON/OFF switch has not been switched on — switch the AC switch.
2. The safety key is in the wrong position — insert it correctly.
3. The **12-pin computer connector** is not plugged in properly — check the wire and connect again.
4. The **12-pin computer cable** is broken — replace it.
5. The fuse on the controller is blown — replace the fuse or the controller.
6. The varistor on the controller is blown — replace the varistor or the controller.
7. The safety device is broken (open) — replace the safety key device.
8. Other components are faulty — replace the console.

A machine that displays or operates **without** the safety key has the opposite fault: a shorted safety
device. Replace the safety key device or the console.
