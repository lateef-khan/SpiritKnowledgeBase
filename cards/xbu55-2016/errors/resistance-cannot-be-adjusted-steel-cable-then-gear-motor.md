Section 9-14, `Flywheel, steel cable and gear motor`, `Q: Resistance can't be adjusted`:

1. Take **chain cover (L) (36)** down to see if the **steel cable** has come off. If yes, reassemble it. *As steel cable is fastened on both sides, this situation will happen rarely.*
2. If the steel cable is no problem, check whether **gear motor (21)** is workable. If not, it needs to be measured by surveying instrument to check whether the malfunction is on gear motor (21) or console (34).

The steel cable is the wire between the gear motor and the brake plate at the flywheel - the book's own replacement steps warn that its aluminium plate bends easily. "Measured by surveying instrument" is the multi-meter test on the drive board that the error chapter of the same book prints in full: `spirit-xb-2016-errors-tension-motor-voltage-test-blue-and-green-wires`. A motor that does not move when Level Up or Down is pressed also puts `--` on the display: `spirit-xb-2016-errors-dashes-tension-motor-failure`.

**This Q&A is the XBU55 2016's own**; the recumbent books of the same year have no resistance Q&A and rely on the error chapter alone.
