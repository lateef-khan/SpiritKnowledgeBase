Section 9-14, `Q: The noise on Flywheel`:

1. **Flywheel (20) is no problem itself, but it will make some noise after using a long time.** The main reason rubbing sounds occur is some wear of the bearing housing. **But it will not affect its function.**
2. A few noises come from an improper way to maintain the machine which **distorts the aluminium sheet on Flywheel (20)**.

So the book gives one noise that is wear and needs nothing, and one that is damage. The aluminium sheet is the spring plate the steel cable pulls against the flywheel to make resistance; the flywheel replacement steps in the same book say to handle it with care because *if it doesn't handle with care, it will become distorted and cause noise when rubbing. This is a very important point.*

Pedal, crank and chain-cover noises on the same bike are `spirit-upright-errors-noises-pedals-crank-then-chain-covers-or-drive-pulley`; resistance that will not change is `xbu55-2016-errors-resistance-cannot-be-adjusted-steel-cable-then-gear-motor`.
