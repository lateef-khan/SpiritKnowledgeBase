Section 9-15, `Other`, items 3 and 4 (items 1 and 2 repeat the pedal and crank noise answers of section 9-12):

3. **The sway on seat post** results from insufficient tightening of **Seat Up/Down Adjustment Knob (106)** or **Brake Tension Knob (86)**. Fasten it.
4. **If sway happens on Console Mast (2) or Handle Bar (3)**, it almost always results from an unlocked screw. Fasten it.

Two knobs and a screw, nothing to replace. The seat post on this bike is held by a locking knob through the main frame and a brake tension knob on the fix plate under the seat, both named in the seat replacement steps of the same book.

The CR900 recumbents answer a shaking handlebar or seat with the mast bolts and the seat-track wheels (`spirit-cr900-errors-handlebar-seat-or-whole-bike-shaking`); the parts are different and so is the fix. Noises from the pedals, crank or chain covers on this bike are `spirit-upright-errors-noises-pedals-crank-then-chain-covers-or-drive-pulley`.
