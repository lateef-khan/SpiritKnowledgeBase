Section 9-13, `Belt falling Off`:

> Take down chain cover (R/L) (36, 37) and reassemble belt (14). **Suggest not assemble chain cover (R/L) (36, 37) before belt (14) is adjusted and assembled well and test it if it would come off or not.** In the meanwhile, observe and adjust the position and elastic of belt. Then, make sure it's no problem to assemble chain cover (R/L) (36, 37).

That is the whole answer: no part to replace and no frame check - unlike the recumbent books, which escalate to the drive pulley, the idler, the brake and finally the main frame (`spirit-recumbent-errors-drive-belt-falls-off-one-notch-then-drive-pulley-idler-or-brake`), and the CU800 2020-book, which does the same for an upright (`cu800-2021-errors-belt-falls-off-one-groove-then-drive-pulley-bearing-housing-or-flywheel`). The advice here is procedural: test with the covers off so you can see the belt walk.

**The printed answer is in Chinese first and English second** on this page; the English sentence above is the book's own translation, not a new one. The belt tension it is refitted to is the slip entry directly below it, 450 N on an M6 nyloc nut (`spirit-xbr-maintenance-belt-slip-nyloc-nut-88-450-newtons`).
