**Outlines:** Console Assembly, Handgrip Side Cap, Drink Bottle Holder, Console Mast Cover, Seat,
Pedal(L), **Seat Post Cover**, **Round Disk Cover**, Chain Cover (L), Handgrip End Cap.

**Skeleton:** Handle Bar, **Sliding Seat Mount**, Console Mast, **Seat Slider**, Crank Arm, Drive
Pulley, **Crank Axle**, **Bearing Housing**, Main Frame, Rear Stabilizer.

The *Seat Slider* is the seat post and the *Sliding Seat Mount* the clamp on it; the replacement
chapter numbers them 6 and 7. The *Bearing Housing* carries the idler wheel and is what the belt
procedure loosens first. No resistance part is drawn on either page - the gear motor is
photographed in section 2 instead.

No dimensions or weights are printed.
