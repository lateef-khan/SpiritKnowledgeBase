The CE850 (2020) service manual is an electronics book. Its eight chapters cover the outlines, the
electronic parts, the electrical configuration, product operation, the block diagram, the wiring,
the safety instructions and the error messages - EEPROM ERROR, Err and the stride "- - -" - with
the calibration procedure, the fuse and the troubleshooting matrix. **It prints no chapter 9
Troubleshooting, no chapter 10 Q&A and no chapter 11 disassembly.**

So for this machine the book says nothing about:

- a slipping or fallen-off belt, or any belt tension figure;
- the 38 mm slide wheel, the aluminium track or the 40 mm slider wheel of the stride mechanism;
- what to lubricate, what to clean or what to tighten.

## Where the same machine's care is written

The **CE850 (2016)** service manual, titled XE898-SE011, carries all of it - the hook-screw belt
adjustment to 190 Hz, the pedal-base and slider checks, the slip, play and smooth-motion items
(`spirit-xe-2016-maintenance-belt-slipping-hook-screw-cap-13-mm-190-hz`,
`spirit-ce850-2016-maintenance-pedal-bases-clean-and-lubricate-the-track-and-38-mm-slide-wheel`,
`spirit-ce850-2016-maintenance-slider-adjustment-40-mm-wheel-and-lubricated-swing-assembly`). The
2020 book's own electronics chapters match the 2016 book closely - the same tension-motor voltage
test, the same stride test procedure - but its stride error is "- - -" where the 2016 book prints
E3, so the two books are not one document and the 2016 care chapters are not carried onto the 2020
machine here. The **owner's manual** for the 2020 machine prints the general maintenance list -
loose hardware, the crank arm nut, two levelling pads with a 14 mm wrench
(`ct800-2016-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`) - and no lubrication step.
