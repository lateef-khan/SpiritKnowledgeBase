**The CE850 (2020) service manual is 50 pages and ends with the 8-7 troubleshooting procedure matrix.** Its table of
contents runs 1 (Outlines) to 8.7 and lists no chapter 9, 10 or 11; the only "replacement" it prints is 8-6 Fuse
Replacement. There is no swing arm, side case, pedal base, pedal arm, slider, stride motor or flywheel procedure,
and no wrench size anywhere in the book.

Checked three ways: the table of contents; a loose grep for "replac", "disassembl", "wrench" and "allen" across the
native text and the 32 pages of OCR supplement (only the fuse page and the matrix hit); and the last content pages
themselves.

**What to use instead.** The 2016 CE850 book (XE898-SE011) prints the full eight-section chapter 11, and the 2020
book's own schematic is headed `#XE898D-SE028` - the same XE898 platform. Those cards are linked below; they are
evidence for the 2016 machine, and this manual gives nothing to confirm or deny them for the 2020 one.
