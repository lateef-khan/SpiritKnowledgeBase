One photograph of the finished machine (cover code SE551-SE023-01), twelve labels:

Console Assembly, **Handgrip Thin Film Label**, Swing Arm, **Emergency Brake Assembly**, Main Frame,
Drink Bottle Holder, **Sliding Pedal Arm**, Flywheel, Side Case, Pedal, **Connecting Pedal Arm**,
**Slide Wheel Cover, Black**.

The *Emergency Brake Assembly* is the lever on the right handgrip - the safety brake the error
chapter blames for starting resistance that is too high. The *Handgrip Thin Film Label* is the
membrane key strip on the left grip. There is **no second, frame-only drawing** in this book, so
the cross bar, rails and drive parts are not labelled anywhere in the Outlines chapter; the belts
and gear motor are named by type in the electronic-parts pages instead
(`xg400-2016-specs-parts-gear-motor-and-belts-named-by-type`). **No dimensions, weight or user
weight limit** is printed.
