**The XG400 book prints no parts list, but it names three parts by type inside its troubleshooting pages.**

| Part | What the book calls it | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Gear (tension) motor | **YJ-9902** - "Replace YJ-9902 gear motor to remedy noise issue" when the motor gear has chipped | Motor Error, cause a, p. 41 |
| Left drive belt | **PJ type, 8 notches (ribs), 1000 mm long** | Step 4, p. 52 |
| Right drive belt | **PK type, 6 notches (ribs), 1000 mm long** | Step 4, p. 52 |

**The two belts are not interchangeable.** The XG400 drives its flywheel from both sides, and Step 4 sets
the left PJ belt to 540 and the right PK belt to as high as 1000 (printed "NT", the book's unit for belt
tension) after the flywheel's bearing housing is tightened "until 300 kgf". No bearing is named by
size anywhere in this book, unlike the other 2016 books.

**YJ-9902 is the only motor model number any 2016 Spirit elliptical service manual prints.** The chapter-2
page calls the same part the TENSION MOTOR and gives it no number
(`spirit-xe195-xe295-xg400-2016-specs-parts-electronic-parts-named`); no Spirit part number for it, or for
the belts, is printed anywhere in the book
(`spirit-elliptical-specs-parts-which-service-manuals-print-a-parts-list`). The Err message that leads to
the replacement, and the belt-tension procedure itself, are on the errors and maintenance cards for this
book.
