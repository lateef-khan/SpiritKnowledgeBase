Row 4 of the mechanical `Troubleshooting guide`:

| Check | As printed |
|---|---|
| 1. Check if the wave washer is on the wheel shaft | Remove the wheel cover and release the C-ring. Check if the wavy washer is broken or missing. Resume the parts if the wavy washer is OK; **replace if it is broken or missing**. |
| 2. Check if there is any damage with the roller | Refer to checking procedure 4-1. **Replace the wheel if it is damaged.** |
| 3. Check if crank axle crooks | Check if the crank axle leans or the screw is loose. |

**Two of the three checks end in a part** - the wave washer or the wheel - and the third is a look at the crank axle with no remedy printed beyond a loose screw.

The XE100-XE500 2007 dealer manual answers *slide wheels jumping off the track* with cleaning first and a new aluminium track second; the XE 2016 books' equivalent is the general connecting-arm and slide-wheel alignment note. Both are care answers and live with the maintenance cards.
