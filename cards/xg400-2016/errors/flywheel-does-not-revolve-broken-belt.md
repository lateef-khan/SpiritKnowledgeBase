Row 5 of the mechanical `Troubleshooting guide`, one check:

> 1. Check if the belt breaks. (Replace the belt following step 4 if it is broken.) Refer to 1-3 for checking procedure.

**This machine has two belts, one each side of the flywheel**, and the guide's Step 4 replaces either: loosen both idler wheels, take the PJ belt off the flywheel pulley, loosen the bearing-housing screws, lift the flywheel assembly and fit the new belt - **PJ type with 8 notches, 1000 mm long, on the left; PK type with 6 notches, 1000 mm long, on the right** - then tighten the bearing housing to 300 kgf, return the C-ring, the positioning post and the belt, and set the tension **540 NT on the left belt and as high as 1000 NT on the right** (the book's unit; read as newtons).

The diagnosis is this card. The belt replacement and its tension figures are Step 4 of the guide, a procedure the assembly and maintenance cards carry; the XE 2016 books' belt figure is a different one, 190 Hz by sonic meter, for a single Poly-V belt.
