Row 2 of the mechanical `Troubleshooting guide`:

| Check | As printed |
|---|---|
| 1. Check if gear motor runs normally | If there is any damage, **replace the gear motor** (Step 3: remove the gear motor and unplug the control wire; after replacing, make sure the drive pulley does not touch the gear motor). |
| 2. Check if the magnet is installed properly | Refer to checking procedure 1-3 (turn the flywheel with the right chain cover off) and Step 2 for the proper position of the magnet - **2mm** from the flywheel at maximum resistance. |
| 3. Check if steel cable breaks | Refer to checking procedure 1-3. Step 3: if the steel cable breaks, press the magnet housing, release the screw to separate it from the magnet housing, and **replace the cable with a new one**. |

**Three checks, two of which end in a part** - the gear motor or the steel cable - and one in the 2 mm magnet position.

The same book gives the gear motor two other answers. Its error chapter prints `--` for a motor that does not move when Level is pressed, with a drive-board voltage test (`spirit-elliptical-2016-errors-dashes-tension-motor-does-not-move`), and its `Troubleshooting for Motor Error` page prints `Err` with a `YJ-9902` gear motor and a cable as the parts (`xg400-2016-errors-err-gear-motor-lost-reset-point-or-no-feedback`). Nothing in the book joins the three. The other XE 2016 books answer no resistance with a console check and a new gear-motor cable (`spirit-xe-errors-no-resistance-check-console-then-replace-the-gear-motor-cable`); the XBU55 2016 bike prints a steel-cable-then-gear-motor row of its own (`xbu55-2016-errors-resistance-cannot-be-adjusted-steel-cable-then-gear-motor`).
