Row 3 of the mechanical `Troubleshooting guide`:

| Check | As printed |
|---|---|
| 1. Check if safety brake is released | Release the safety brake if it is not released. |
| 2. Check if steel cable is stuck | **Replace the cable if it is stuck.** Refer to step 1-3 to replace the cable (open the right chain cover; Step 3 separates the cable from the magnet housing). |

**The XG400 is the one 2016 Spirit elliptical with an emergency brake** - the outline drawing labels an `Emergency Brake Assembly` - and this row is the only place the book says what a brake left applied feels like: hard pedalling from level 1. Release it before anything is taken apart.

The second check ends in a part, the steel cable. A cable that is not stuck but slack or broken is the no-resistance row (`xg400-2016-errors-no-resistance-gear-motor-magnet-or-steel-cable`). The XE100-XE500 2007 dealer manual answers *resistance too high* with a brake-cable adjustment instead, a care procedure the maintenance cards carry.
