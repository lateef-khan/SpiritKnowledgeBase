The XG400 (2016) service manual is the odd one out of the 2016 residential XE books. The XE195,
XE295, XE395 and XE795 books each end with a Troubleshooting chapter (belt, swing arm, connecting
arm) and a Q&A chapter (noise, slip, shaking, smooth motion) full of lubrication and cleaning
advice. **The XG400 book has neither.** Its contents run Outlines, Electronic Parts, Electrical
Configurations, Product Operation, Unit Block Diagrams, Basic Connections and Wiring, Product Safety
Instructions, Messages, Troubleshooting for Motor Error, Troubleshooting guide - and stop.

## What the Troubleshooting guide does print

A five-row MECHANICAL SYSTEM table, every remedy a check or a part swap:

| Symptom | Checks |
|---|---|
| Noises | chain cover touching the flywheel; a cracked roller; the magnet system touching the flywheel |
| No resistance | gear motor damaged; magnet not installed properly; steel cable broken |
| Starting resistance too high | safety brake not released; steel cable stuck |
| Sliding wheels do not stay on the rail | wave washer missing from the wheel shaft; roller damaged; crank axle crooked |
| Flywheel does not revolve while pedalling | belt broken |

The one adjustment among the follow-up steps is the magnet gap
(`xg400-2016-maintenance-magnet-to-flywheel-gap-2-mm-and-steel-cable-tightness`). Nothing in the
book names a lubricant, a cleaning agent or a rail to wipe. **The only belt tension figures sit
inside the belt replacement step** (Step 4, p. 52): after the two-belt flywheel goes back in, "adjust
the belt tension at both sides, left belt at 540 NT, right side belt to as high as 1000 NT". That is
a replacement setting, printed as the book prints it, not an answer to a slipping belt - the book
has no slipping-belt item.

## Where the XG400's care instructions are

The **owner's manuals** carry them: the 2019 and 2021 XG400 books clean the rear rails and
polyurethane wheels with rubbing alcohol and add a thin coat of lubricant
(`ce800-2013-maintenance-rear-rail-cleaning-and-lubrication`) and answer a squeak with the loose-hardware
list (`spirit-xe-maintenance-squeak-thump-or-clicking`). Whether the 2016 machine is the same
machine as those later reprints is not settled by this book, so those cards are not extended to it
here.

This is a different absence from the XE150/XE350/XE550 owner's manual, which prints no maintenance
chapter of any kind (`spirit-xe-maintenance-manual-prints-no-maintenance-section`).
