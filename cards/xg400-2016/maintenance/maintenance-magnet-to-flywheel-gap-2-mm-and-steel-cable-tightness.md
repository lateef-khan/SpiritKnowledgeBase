**This is the XG400 (2016) magnet-brake setting. The 2007 XE100-XE500 dealer manual adjusts a
different cable brake by feel with no measurement
(`spirit-xe-2007-maintenance-brake-cable-adjustment-for-resistance-too-high`).**

The book reaches this step from a noise: "Check if the magnet system interacts with the flywheel" -
with the right chain cover off, turn the flywheel and see whether it touches the external magnet
system. If it does, Step 2:

1. **Adjust the resistance to maximum value** so that the magnet housing and the screw on the stopper
   touch each other.
2. **Adjust the screw until the distance between the magnet and the flywheel is 2 mm.**
3. Check with the control key that it runs correctly.
4. If not, **adjust the screw so that the steel cable is as tight as possible, and still keeps the
   2 mm distance.**
5. Resume all parts in order when everything is OK.

Getting at it means the pedal arm assembly and both chain covers off - the sequence in the book's
1-3 check, which is the disassembly the assembly section carries. A broken cable is replaced rather
than adjusted (Step 3 of the same chapter), and the gear motor and the console are diagnosed in the
errors section.

This is the only adjustment the XG400 (2016) service manual prints. It has no belt-tension
adjustment, no lubrication step and no cleaning step -
`xg400-2016-maintenance-service-manual-prints-no-lubrication-or-cleaning-procedure`.
