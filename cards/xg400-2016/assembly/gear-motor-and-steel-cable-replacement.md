**Step 3 of the Troubleshooting Procedures is the book's gear-motor and cable replacement.** It is reached from
"No resistance" check 2-1 ("If there is any damage, replace the gear motor. Refer to step 3") and from the two
steel-cable checks. Remove the chain covers first (check 1-3).

1. **If the steel cable breaks:** press the **magnet housing** and release the screw to separate it from the magnet
   housing (figure B-4), and replace the cable with a new one.
2. **If the cable does not break:** remove the **gear motor** (figure C-1) and **unplug the control wire**.
3. After replacing the parts, resume all parts in reverse order. **Make sure the drive pulley does not touch the gear
   motor** (figure C-2).

The screw in step 1 is the same stopper screw that Troubleshooting Procedures step 2 sets for a **2 mm** gap between
magnet and flywheel with the resistance at maximum; set that gap again after the cable goes back on. The gear
motor is named **YJ-9902** on the Motor Error page ("Replace YJ-9902 gear motor to remedy noise issue").
