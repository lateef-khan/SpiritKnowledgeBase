**This book has no parts-replacement chapter.** Its only disassembly sequence is check 1-3 of the troubleshooting
guide, which every later repair step ("refer to step 1-3 to remove left and right chain covers") sends you back to.
It names no screw sizes and no wrench sizes; the figures carry that.

1. Release the **bolt under the right handlebar assembly** (figure 1-3-1), then the **bolt which secures the crank
   axle** (figure 1-3-2), and remove the **pedal arm assembly** (figure 1-3-3).
2. Remove the **crank axle cover** (figure 1-3-4) and release the **bolt on the crank axle** (figure 1-3-5).
3. Release the **two screws at the rear of the left chain cover** (figure 1-3-6), then the **screws for the right chain
   cover** (figure 1-3-7).
4. **Unplug the DC power cable** and the right chain cover can be taken apart (figure 1-3-8).
5. Turn the flywheel to see whether it touches the external **magnet system** (figure 1-3-9). If it does, go to
   Troubleshooting Procedures step 2 (the 2 mm magnet gap adjustment).

The book gives no refit line; "resume all parts in order" appears only at the end of the later steps.
