**This machine has two belts of different profile, and the book tensions them to different values.** The XE-series
single Poly-V belt is set by frequency (190 Hz); nothing from that card applies here.

1. Refer to step 1 to remove the left and right chain covers and check whether the left and right side belts are
   broken. If either is, replace it.
2. **Loosen both left and right idler wheels** (figure D-1). Loosen and turn the flywheel to take the **PJ type belt**
   off the flywheel pulley.
3. Use the Allen wrench to loosen the **screw on the bearing housing** (figure D-2), then release the **screws on the
   bearing housing** (figure D-3).
4. Lift the flywheel assembly and replace the belt: **PJ type with 8 notches, 1000 mm long, at the left side**, and
   **PK type with 6 notches, 1000 mm long, at the right side**.
5. When the belt is positioned (figure D-4), resume the whole flywheel assembly and bearing housing and **tighten
   until 300 kgf**.
6. Return the **C-ring** to the end of the bearing and return the **positioning post** and the belt to the drive wheel.
7. **Adjust the belt tension at both sides: left belt at 540 NT, right side belt to as high as 1000 NT**, and return
   all parts.

**The units are printed as "kgf" and "NT".** `300 kgf` on a bearing-housing screw reads as a torque figure with its
length unit missing; `NT` is a belt-tension reading in newtons (the same meter unit the CE900 books print as "N").
Both are quoted as printed. The page is native text; nothing more is on the render.
