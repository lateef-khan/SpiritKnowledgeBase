**Two rules, and the anchoring one is the one people miss.**

**Capacity.** The last bullet of the safety instructions:

> **TOTAL WEIGHT LIMIT: 1,000LBS**

One figure, for everything on the rack at once. No metric conversion, no per-tier or per-cradle
breakdown, and no user weight limit — a rack is not something you sit on, and the manual sets none.

**Anchoring.** The facility safety precautions carry a bullet that appears in no other Spirit
strength manual except the ST800FT's:

> Securely anchor each dumbbell rack to the floor using the anchor holes provided in each rack.

It is written as a requirement, not a recommendation, and the holes are already in the rack. The
bullet sits second in the list, ahead of *make sure that each rack is set up and operated on a solid
level surface. Do not install equipment on an uneven surface.* The manual gives **no fastener size,
no torque and no pull-out force** for the ST800DR3 — the figures on the ST800FT's securing page
belong to that machine and must not be quoted here.

The proper usage page adds *do not use rack if it is not located on a solid level surface or if it is
not properly installed*, which is where an unanchored rack lands.

**None of the thirty-six CSS, CSD, CSF or CSI machines is anchored**, so do not read this rule
across to one of them.
