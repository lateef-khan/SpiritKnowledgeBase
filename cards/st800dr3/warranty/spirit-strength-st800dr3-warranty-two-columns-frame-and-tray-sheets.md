| | Frame | ABS Protective Tray Sheets |
|---|---|---|
| **Residential** (vendor defects only) | Lifetime | Lifetime |
| **Commercial** (non-dues paying facilities) | 10 Years | 6 Months |

**Two columns, and neither is *Parts* or *Upholstery*.** The rack has no upholstery and
the manual names no general parts term for it, so a component that is neither the frame
nor an ABS protective tray sheet has no period stated at all. That is what the table
prints; the manual does not fall back on an *all other parts* line the way the CSD, CSS
and CSF books do.

The ABS protective tray sheets - the plastic liners the dumbbells rest on - are the only
non-frame item named, and in a facility they carry the shortest term in the ST800 range
at six months.

The home row is qualified *vendor defects only*: lifetime cover for a manufacturing
fault, not for wear.

**No labour column and no labour row.** The labour term is stated separately, in the
SERVICE paragraph: `spirit-strength-warranty-labor-period`.

**A prisons footnote sits directly under this table**, in addition to exclusion 8 on the
next page: `spirit-2026t-warranty-prisons-excluded`.
