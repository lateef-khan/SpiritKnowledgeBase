Three steps, each done on the left frame and then repeated on the right:

**Step one - connect the Left Frame (No. 1) to the Big Dumbbell Tray (No. 4).**
**Step two - attach a Dumbbell Tray (No. 3) to the Left Frame (No. 1).**
**Step three - attach the other Dumbbell Tray (No. 3) to the Left Frame (No. 1).**

Every one of the three uses the same fasteners, per side:

- 2 x M12 x 25 Hex Head Bolts (No. 15)
- 4 x Flat Washers Φ13 x Φ24 x 1.5 (No. 13)
- 2 x Nylon Lock Nuts M12 (No. 14)

**Hand tighten the bolts and nuts** on steps one and two.

## Every tray has two angles, chosen at assembly

*"You can choose between two angles. Place bolts in holes 0 & 1 for a more tilted tray.
0 & 2 for a less tilted tray."*

The same note is printed against all three steps, so **each of the three trays can be set
independently**. There is no adjustment afterwards - changing a tray's angle means taking
its bolts out.

## The rack is the one machine in the range that must be bolted down

Its user safety list says *"Securely anchor each dumbbell rack to the floor using the
anchor holes provided in each rack"*, without the residential exemption the functional
trainer gives. See
[the anchoring card](../../shared/assembly/spirit-st800-assembly-anchoring-and-shimming-with-flat-washers.md).

**Its parts list is fifteen items** - the shortest in the range - and holds no gloves and
no tool.
