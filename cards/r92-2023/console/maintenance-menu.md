Press and hold **Start, Stop and Enter together for about 5 seconds**. The message window displays "Engineering Mode". Press **Enter** to open the menu below. Use the **Level up / down** keys to navigate.

- **A. Key Test** - test all the keys to make sure they are functioning.
- **B. Display Test** - automatically tests all LCDs.
- **C. Functions** - press Enter to access settings, use Level up / down to scroll.
  - I. ODO Reset - resets the odometer.
  - II. Units - English (Imperial) or Metric display readings.
  - III. Sleep Mode - turn off to have the console power down automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity.
  - IV. Motor Test - continually runs the tensioning gear motor.
  - V. Manual - allows stepping of the gear motor.
  - VI. Pause Mode - turn on to allow 5 minutes of pause, turn off to have the console pause indefinitely.
  - VII. Key Tone - turn on or off the beep sound when a key is pressed.
- **D. Security** - locks the keypad against unauthorised use. With child lock enabled the keypad will not operate unless you press and hold Start and Enter for 3 seconds.
- **E. Factory Set** - the manual does not say what this changes.
- **F. Exit** - leaves the Maintenance Menu.

The manual notes the menu may be called Engineering Mode depending on version.

**Item C.III disagrees with Section 4.** Section 4 says the console sleeps after 15 minutes and that sleep mode has to be turned **on**. This item says 30 minutes and that it has to be turned **off**. Confirm before quoting a figure.
