1. Take both left and right front shrouds apart.
2. Remove the **steel cable (62)** from the **flywheel (55)**. **Treat the aluminium plate gently: it deforms easily and then causes scratching noises.**
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove **two M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screws (99)** on the **gear motor (43)** and take it apart.
4. Remove the steel cable (62) from the gear motor (43) end and take it apart.
5. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to remove the **M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screw (99)** and take the **reed switch sensor (46)** apart.
6. To set the cable range when refitting the steel cable (62), use the **console (19)** to drive the resistance to its maximum level, then tighten the steel cable with the screw. **At maximum resistance the aluminium plate sits at its lowest position.** Use **two 8 mm open end wrenches** to secure it.
