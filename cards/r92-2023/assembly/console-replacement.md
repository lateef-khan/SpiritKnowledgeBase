1. Unscrew **4 pcs of M5 x 12 mm Phillips head screws (99)** with the Phillips head screwdriver (114) and release the console (19).
2. Unplug all the cables behind the console.
3. Reverse the steps to install the new console.
4. To remove the PAD holder, use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **four M5 3.5 x 12 mm Phillips head screws**.

Step 4's screw is printed as "M5 3.5 x 12mm", two sizes run together. The manual does not resolve it.
