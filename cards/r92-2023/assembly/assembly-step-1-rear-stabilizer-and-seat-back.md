Hardware: **#65** 3/8" x 53L Carriage Bolt (2 pcs), **#77** 3/8" x 19mm x 1.5T Flat Washer (6 pcs), **#84** 3/8" x 25mm x 2T Flat Washer (2 pcs), **#176** 3/8" x 3/4" Hex Head Bolt (6 pcs), **#191** 3/8" x16 x 12.5T Cap Nut (2 pcs).

1. Install the Rear Stabilizer (7) onto the Main Frame (1) with the 2 pcs of Carriage Bolts (65), 2 pcs of Flat Washers (84) and 2 pcs of Cap Nuts (191), by using the Wrench (112).
2. Install the Seat Back Bracket (5) onto the Seat Carriage (4) with the 6 pcs of Hex Head Bolts (**176**) and 6 pcs of Flat Washers (77), by using the Wrench (112).
3. Insert an end cap (32) into each opening of the Front Stabilizer Tube. You may need to tap them in with a rubber mallet if they are tight.

**This step is internally consistent, and the LCR 2019 and 2023 versions of it are not.** They call #65 a hex head bolt in the instruction while their checklist calls it a carriage bolt, and they call the six 3/8" x 3/4" bolts (167) instead of 176. The R92 2023 says carriage bolt and 176, matching its own checklist.

**The R92 2019 prints no cap nuts in this instruction** even though its checklist supplies two #191. This 2023 step uses them.
