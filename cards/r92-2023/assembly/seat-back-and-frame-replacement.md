1. Use an open wrench to unscrew **6 pcs of 3/8" x 3/4" hex head bolts (176)** and **6 pcs of 3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washers (77)** to take the **seat back frame (5)** apart.
2. Use a **combination M5 Allen wrench** to release the **M8 x 15 mm button head socket bolt (179)** to take the **seatback cushion (63)** apart.
3. Reverse the steps to refit.

The LCR 2023 manual prints the same procedure with an extra step between these two, releasing 4 pcs of M5 x 15 mm Phillips head screws to take off a seat back cover. This manual has no such step.
