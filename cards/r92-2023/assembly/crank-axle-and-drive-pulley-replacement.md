1. Take apart both left and right shrouds (29, 30) and the drive belt (54).
2. Use **C-ring pliers** to take off the **Ø20 C-ring (86)** axle. The **drive pulley axle (8)** can then be released.
3. To release the drive pulley, use **2 wrenches** to unscrew **4 pcs of 1/4" x 3/4" hex head screws (66)** with **8 pcs of 1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washers (72)** and **4 pcs of 1/4" x 5.5T nuts (90)**.
4. Reverse the steps to install the new crank axle.
