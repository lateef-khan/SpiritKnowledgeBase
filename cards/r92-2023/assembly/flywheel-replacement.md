1. Take both left and right front shrouds (29) (30) off.
2. Use a **13 mm open end wrench** to loosen the **nyloc nut (88)** on the **J bolt (87)** and remove the J bolt.
3. Use a **6 mm Allen wrench** and a **14 mm open end wrench** to release the **3/8" x 3/4" button head socket bolt (81)**, the **3/8" x 7T nyloc nut (89)** and the **3/8" x 19 x 1.5T flat washer (77)**, then take the **idler wheel assembly (10)** apart.
4. Remove the **drive belt (54)**.
5. Remove the steel cable on the **flywheel (55)** and use a **15 mm open end wrench** to release the **3/8"-UNF26 nut (121)** on the flywheel to remove it.
6. To refit, hang the drive belt (54) on the flywheel (55), then install the flywheel on the mainframe (1). Align the flywheel with the **pulley (20)** in line, turn the **nut (120)** outward until it touches the mainframe, then use **15 mm and 17 mm open end wrenches** to tighten nut (120) and the 3/8"-UNF26 nut (121). Tighten **nut (119)** and the 3/8"-UNF26 nut (121) on the other side.
7. Use the 6 mm Allen wrench and 14 mm open end wrench to tighten the button head socket bolt (81), nyloc nut (89) and flat washer to secure the idler wheel assembly on the mainframe, then **turn back a quarter turn to release it a little**. Install the J-bolt (87) with the **M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88)** onto the idler wheel assembly (10) and the mainframe.
8. Return the drive belt (54) to the drive pulley (20) and the flywheel (55), then turn the drive pulley to check the belt runs smoothly and centred. If it is off centre, loosen the 3/8"-UNF26 nut (121) on the flywheel and adjust nuts (119) and (120). Use a **13 mm wrench** to tighten the **M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88)** on the J-bolt until the idler wheel (10) presses the belt tight. Use a belt tension gauge to make sure the tension reads **450 N (Note 1)**. Turn the drive pulley (20) to confirm the belt stays centred.

The belt tension figure and the dangling "(Note 1)" are covered in a separate card.
