1. Take the console apart first.
2. Use a **12/14 mm wrench (112)** to release **2 pcs of 5/16" x 5/8" hex head screws (68)**, **2 pcs of 5/16" x 1.5T split washers** and **2 pcs of 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T flat washers (76)**. The handlebar assembly (3) can be released.
3. Reverse the step to refit the handlebar assembly (3).
4. Separate the console mast cover (31) from the front shrouds L/R (29) (30) at the seam on each side with a suitable tool, release the latches locking them together, and pull the console mast cover (31) up.
5. Unscrew **6 pcs of 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts (68)**, **4 pcs of 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T flat washers (76)** and **2 pcs of 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T curved washers (83)** with the 12 mm wrench, to pull out the console mast (2).
6. To refit the console mast (2), guide the computer cable (44) and the hand pulse sensor assembly with cable (45) through the console mast (2) and out of the console securing plate, then tighten **6 pcs of 5/16" x 5/8" hex head bolts (68)**, **6 pcs of 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T flat washers (76)** and **2 pcs of 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T curved washers (83)** with the 12 mm open wrench.

**The flat washer count does not match.** Step 5 removes 4 flat washers (76); step 6 refits 6 of them. The manual does not resolve it.
