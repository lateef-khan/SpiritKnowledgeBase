Hardware: **#68** 5/16" x 5/8" Hex Head Bolt (8 pcs), **#76** 5/16" x 18 x 1.5T Flat Washer (6 pcs), **#82** 5/16" x 1.5T Split Washer (2 pcs), **#83** 5/16" x 19 x 1.5T Curved Washer (2 pcs).

1. Install the Console Mast Cover (31) onto the Console Mast (2). Make sure the plastic cover is in the correct orientation.
2. Feed the Computer Cable (44) and the Handpulse Wire (45) through the bottom of the Console Mast (2) and out through the opening at the top.
3. Install the Console Mast (2) into the receiving tube of the Main Frame (1) with the 6 pcs of Hex Head Bolts (68), **4 pcs of Flat Washers (76) on the sides** of the tube and **2 pcs of Curved Washers (83) on the front** of the tube, by using the Wrench (112). Make sure not to pinch the wire cable, as damage to the electronics could occur.
4. Install the Mast Handle bar Assembly (3) onto the Console Mast (2) with the 2 pcs of Hex Head Bolts (68), 2 pcs of Flat Washers (76) and 2 pcs of Split Washers (82), by using the Wrench (112).

**There is no Styrofoam pad instruction in this step.** Every other R92 and LCR manual, 2011 to 2023, includes "Remove the white Styrofoam pad (factory installed to prevent bolts from being accidentally dropped into the Bike)" as item 4. The R92 2023 has only four items and does not mention it. This is an absence in the source, not an omission here - if a 2023 machine does have a pad, the manual never tells the owner to take it out.

Eight #68 bolts in the bag: six for the mast, two for the handlebar.
