1. Take the **aluminium axle end cap (135)** apart.
2. Use **2 Phillips head screwdrivers** to release two **M6 x 15 mm Phillips head screws (98)** to take the **seat stop axle (11)** apart, and pull the **seat front/aft adjustment lever (167)** upward to take the **seat carriage (4)** apart.
3. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **4 pcs of M6 x 10L flat Phillips head screws (161)**, **4 pcs of Ø7 x Ø15 x 1.5T flat washers (162)** and **4 pcs of sleeves (163)**. Then use a **13 mm wrench** to release **4 pcs of M8 x 7T nyloc nuts (88)** and **4 pcs of Ø8 x Ø18 x 3T knurled lock washers (79)**, and take the **seat wheel adjustment plate (9L, 9R)** apart.
4. Use an **M4 Allen wrench** and a **10 mm open end wrench** to release the **M5 x 45 mm socket head cap bolt (171)** and the **M5 x 5T nyloc nut (173)**.
5. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to release two **M5 x 25 mm flat head socket screws (169)**, then pull out the **lever anchor (168)** to take the seat front/aft adjustment lever (167) apart.
6. Use an **M5 Allen wrench** and an **11 mm wrench** to release the **M6 x 38 mm socket head cap bolt (93)**, the **1/4" x 13 x 1T flat washer (72)** and the **M6 nyloc nut (129)**, and take the **seat position latch (12)** apart.
7. To install the adjustment lever (167), join it to the lever anchor (168) and secure with two M5 x 25 mm flat head socket screws (169). Place the **spring (104)** in the seat position latch (12) and secure with the **M5 x 45 mm socket head cap bolt (171)**, running it from top to bottom through the seat position latch (12), the spring (104), the **Ø15 x 6 x 4T nylon washer (170)**, the adjustment lever (167) and the **Ø3/16" x 10 x 1T flat washer (172)**, then tighten with the **M5 x 5T nyloc nut (173)**.
8. Install the completed seat carriage (4) onto the **aluminium track (14)** and adjust the seat wheel adjustment plate (9L, 9R) with **11 and 13 mm wrenches**. At the front left, turn the 11 mm wrench counterclockwise first, then tighten the M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88) with the 13 mm wrench. At the front right, turn the 11 mm wrench clockwise and tighten the M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88) with the 13 mm wrench. Turn the hex bolt at the rear right counterclockwise and the rear left clockwise to secure.
9. Test whether the movement is smooth enough. If not, turn the four hex bolts back and re-tighten until it is. Install the four sleeves (163) on the hex bolts, secure with the four Ø7 x Ø15 x 1.5T flat washers (162) and the four M6 x 10L flat Phillips head screws (161), then install the seat stop axle (11) and the aluminium axle end cap (135).
