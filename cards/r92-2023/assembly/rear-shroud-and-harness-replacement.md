1. Use a Phillips head screwdriver to unscrew **7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 16 self tapping screws (103)** and **2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws (101)** on the **left rear chain cover (35)**. Unplug the **hand pulse wire (26)** and the left rear chain cover can be released.
2. On the **right rear chain cover (36)**, unscrew the **Ø3.5 x 16 self tapping screws (103)** with **3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T flat washers (78)** and **2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws (101)**. The right rear chain cover can then be released.
3. To refit rear shroud (R) (36), return it onto the main frame (1) and tighten it slightly with the **Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet metal screw (103)** and the **3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T flat washer (78)**, then use **2 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (101)** to secure it on the main frame and lock it with the Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet metal screw.
4. Combine rear shroud (L) (35) and rear shroud (R) (36) and secure with **7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet metal screws (103)** and **2 pcs of 5 x 16 mm tapping screws (101)**.

Step 2 does not give a count for the self tapping screws (103) on the right cover.
