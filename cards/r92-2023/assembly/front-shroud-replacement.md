1. Take apart the crank arm and the console mast cover.
2. To release the **left front shroud (29)**, use a Phillips head screwdriver to release **7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 16 sheet metal screws (103)** and **2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws (101)**.
3. To release the **right front shroud (30)**: on the mainframe, unscrew **2 pcs of Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet metal screws (103)** with **2 pcs of 3/16" x 15 mm x 1.5T flat washers (78)**; then, on the right front shroud, unscrew **2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws**. Last, take the **DC adaptor (48)** cable away from the **AC power switch connector (159)** to remove front shroud (R) (30).
4. To refit the right front shroud, put it on the mainframe, locate the 2 sheet metal screws (103) with the 2 flat washers (78) on the mainframe loosely, tighten the 2 tapping screws (101) on the mainframe, then tighten every loose screw.
5. Match the left front chain cover (29) with the right front chain cover (30) and secure both with **7 pcs of Ø3.5 x 16 mm sheet metal screws (103)** and **2 pcs of 5 x 16 tapping screws (101)**.
6. Pay special attention when refitting front shroud (30): the **on/off switch (159)** must be secured by both front shrouds. Top and bottom of the switch sit **inside** the left and right front shrouds; left and right of the switch sit **outside** them.

Steps 2 and 3 call the parts front shrouds; step 5 calls the same parts front chain covers.
