Most noise comes from **loose screws**. Sometimes a part has deformed or shifted and is rubbing or moving roughly.

1. **Noise from the seat carriage (4)** is generally a loose **seat wheel adjustment plate (9L, 9R)**. A wobble is normal on a long-used machine. Follow the seat carriage procedure to resolve it.
2. **Noise from the pedals (116) (117)** is generally pedal wear. Replace the pedal.
3. **Left and right crank (51L, 51R).** A loose crank makes noise and rough movement. Tightening the crank fixes it.
4. **Noise inside the front shrouds.** Remove both front shrouds to find where the noise comes from. In many cases the **drive pulley (20)** is rubbing the right shroud. Replace the drive pulley, or add an extra **5/16" x 16 x 1T flat washer** at the right front shroud (30).
5. If there is still noise with both front shrouds off, find the source and replace that part.

**The cross reference in item 1 is wrong.** It points at "Section 9.10 of the Parts Replacement Guide 10". Section 9.10 is the rear shrouds and harness; the seat carriage is section 9.11.
