**450 Newton.**

Set it by tightening the **M8 x 7T nyloc nut (88)** on the **J-bolt (87)** with a **13 mm wrench** until the **idler wheel assembly (10)** presses the belt tight and a belt tension gauge reads 450 N.

**The units in the manual are muddled, exactly as they are in the R92 2016 manual.** Section 9.7 step 8 says "use belt tension gauge to make sure the tension reads 450N", which is correct. Section 10.2 says "turn M8x7T Nyloc Nut (88) clockwise until sound wave frequency falls between 450N", which mixes a frequency reading with a force unit. Take 450 N as the figure.

**There is also a dangling reference.** Step 8 ends "450N. (Note 1)", but no Note 1 follows anywhere in the manual. The B94 2016 manual carries an equivalent remark saying a brand new belt must read 540 N; whether that applies here is not stated. Confirm with the technical department before setting a new belt.

The LCB and LCR bikes are set by frequency instead. Do not carry that to this machine.
