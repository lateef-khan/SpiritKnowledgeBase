                                                      TROUBLESHOOTING
Common Issues and Troubleshooting Methods

THE SCREEN IS BLANK
Troubleshooting:
This is usually caused by depleted batteries.
The batteries are located on the back of the console. Please open the battery cover on
the rear of the console and replace all four AA batteries with new ones. If the batteries
are confirmed to have sufficient power and the screen still does not display anything
after installing new batteries, then the console is defective and should be replaced.
Please replace the console with a new one.




NO SPEED/RPM DISPLAY
Troubleshooting:
First, please make sure that the speed sensor cable is properly connected to the back of
the console. If the cable is connected correctly, the issue may be caused by the speed
sensor being positioned too far away from the magnet, or the speed sensor itself may be
defective.
Please refer to the Parts Replacing Guide and remove the front right plastic cover of the
main frame. Then verify that the gap between the speed sensor and the magnet on the
front roller is less than 2 mm.
If the sensor position is correct, the speed sensor should be replaced.




**If the console buttons are damaged or not functioning, please replace the console
with a new one.
