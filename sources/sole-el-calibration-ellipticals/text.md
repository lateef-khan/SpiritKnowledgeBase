Calibration (Ellipticals)

For Ellipticals with Physical Buttons:

Clear Error Codes (if present):
Power the elliptical off, wait a few seconds, then power it back on to clear any error codes.

Enter Calibration Mode:
With the machine on, press and hold the Start and Incline Up buttons on the main console (the one directly under the screen, not on the arms). (2026 models will have you press the Start and Fan keys simultaneously)
Keep holding the buttons for about 10 seconds.
The screen should display a calibration menu. If it doesn't, repeat the steps.

Start Calibration:
Step off the elliptical before starting—it will move on its own.
The display should prompt you to press Start to begin calibration.
Press Start to begin. The elliptical will automatically move the incline from its lowest to highest settings.

After Calibration:
If successful, the elliptical will reboot to the home screen.
Please note any error codes that appear during the process and observe the machine’s behavior (e.g., if it stops early, doesn't incline, etc.)

For Ellipticals with a Touchscreen:

Clear Error Codes (if present):
Power the elliptical off, wait a few seconds, then power it back on.

Login to a Profile:
Login to either your personal account or use the Guest profile.

Open the Engineering Menu:
Tap the gear icon in the top left corner of the screen to open Settings.
At the top of the Settings screen, you'll see the word “Settings” (it doesn’t look like a button, but it is!).
Rapidly tap the word “Settings” several times until the Engineering Menu appears.

Start Calibration:
Tap “Calibration” in the top right corner.
Step off the elliptical before starting—it will move on its own.
Then press the start button located to the right of Incline Calibration.
The elliptical will automatically run through the incline ranges.

After Calibration:
The machine should reboot back to the login screen once everything has stopped moving. If it doesn’t, press the X in the top-left to return to the settings menu once complete.
Please note any error codes that appear during the process and observe the machine’s behavior (e.g., if it stops early, doesn't incline, etc.)
