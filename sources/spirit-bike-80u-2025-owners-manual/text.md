GROUNDING INSTRUCTIONS

This product must be grounded. In the unlikely event
that the 8.0U’s electrical system should malfunction
or breakdown grounding provides a path of the least
resistance for electric current, reducing the risk of electric
shock. This product is equipped with a cord having an
equipment-grounding plug. The plug must be plugged into
an appropriate outlet that is properly installed and grounded
in accordance with all local codes and ordinances..

DANGER - Improper connection of the equipment-
grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric shock.
Check with a qualified electrician or serviceman if you are
in doubt whether the product is properly grounded. Do
not modify the plug provided with the product if it will not
fit the outlet; have a proper outlet installed by a qualified
electrician.

OPERATING PRINCIPLE

The patient pushes the pedals with their feet. The operator can increase the workload using the Up and Down Key on the console.
When the workload change is requested, an electromagnet is energized at field strengths relative to the workload requested. The
magnetic field generated by the coil is induced into a flywheel creating eddy currents to flow in the flywheel. The eddy currents then
create an opposing magnetic field to the electromagnetic field creating more or less resistance at the pedal, determined by workload
setting by the operator.

INTENDED OPERATOR
 Education: medical professional or medical occupation with physical therapist
   license.
 Knowledge: the operator should read the user’s manual before use.

INTENDED INSTALLER
 Education: high school or above.
 Knowledge/Experience: the installer shall be given a specific training by
   manufacturer. The installer must have experience in this equipment assembly and
   disassembly.
 Background: the installer must be electro-mechanically trained.
 Professional competence: normal vision ability required.

OPERATING THE 8.0U

Unique uses for the 8.0U
•   Strong, solid, and steady upright mast frame with a step-
    through design makes it extremely easy to get on and off.
•   (Optional) Adjustable crank arms help patients with limit-
    ed mobility or injuries to have smaller joint range of motion
    due to smaller circular lower body motions. The pedal can
    be moved along the crank to adjust the arm length to ac-
    commodate a range of motion as small as 15 degrees.
•   Symmetry feature measures balance between left and
    right leg power at the pedals.
•   Bidirectional resistance allows both forward and reverse
    pedaling for instantaneous retro-cycling. It provides a low-
    er start-up, smooth, and comfortable resistance.
•   The exclusive 2% slope on the pedal surface is ergonomic,
    and provides neutral alignment of leg joints for a comfort-
    able motion.
•   The up/down seat height positioning adjustment provides
    smooth and easy operation.

is being done during physical activity. This program
