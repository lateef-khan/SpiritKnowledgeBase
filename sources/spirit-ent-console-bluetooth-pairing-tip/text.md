SPIRIT FITNESS CONNECTING BLUETOOTH

HOW TO CONNECT TO BLUETOOTH

1. Make sure Bluetooth on your device is turned on.

2. Click the Bluetooth symbol on the main screen of your Spirit Fitness machine
and make sure it is turned on (click the toggle switch it it is turned off).

3. After pushing the Bluetooth button on the console (bottom lefthand corner]
make sure you push the ‘box’ symbol on the right hand side of the console to
get to ‘Tull screen’ which removes the speed and incline columns.

4. Find the device name under the list of “Available Devices” if this is your first
time pairing them, or under “Paired Devices" if you've paired this device to
your machine before.

5. Once you see your device listed on the console, click the name of the device
and wait until it says “connect.” The machine and your device will pair auto-
matically. Make sure to follow any prompts on the screen or on your device to
ensure successful pairing.
