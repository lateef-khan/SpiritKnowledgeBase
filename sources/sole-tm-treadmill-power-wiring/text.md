Treadmill Power Wiring

Power Socket has wires permanently attached.
Green wire is ground that goes to frame
Black wire goes to Breaker, top prong
White wire goes to On/Off Switch/AC Electronic Module, top left prong (as if looking at the back of the On/Off Switch/AC Electronic Module)
Breaker does not come with wires
Small black wire from bottom prong on Breaker to On/Off Switch/AC Electronic Module, top right prong (as if looking at the back of On/Off Switch/AC Electronic Module)
On/Off Switch/AC Electronic Module does not come with wire. Top prong should be filled with wires from Power Socket and Breaker
White wire goes from bottom left prong (as if looking at the back of On/Off Switch/AC Electronic Module) to Filter (if machine does not have Filter, then direct to Motor Controller)
Black wire goes from bottom right prong (as if looking at the back of On/Off Switch/AC Electronic Module) to Filter (if machine does not have Filter, then direct to Motor Controller)
