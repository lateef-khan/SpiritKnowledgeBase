Replacing the Front Stabilizer Tube

1. Remove the two screws from the front stabilizer tube.

2. Remove the screws securing the floor mat, then take off the floor mat.

3. Use an Allen wrench and an open-end wrench to remove the moving wheel.

4. When reinstalling the moving wheel, ensure it can rotate smoothly after tightening.

5. Reassemble all components by following the above steps in reverse order.
