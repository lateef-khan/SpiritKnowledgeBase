∆ Nu-Sport Folding Bike # 116419

1. WARRANTY:
   • Residential Use ONLY.
   • 90 Days from date of purchase.
   • Must register WARRANTY.

16 Digit Serial Number Location- Users R- side on main frame front support tube.




2. Assembly Part Checklist:

  • Main Frame, Seat Support Tube, Handlebar Tube w/ Console, Front/ Rear Stabilizer Tubes, Seat
Cushion, and L/R Pedals w/ Straps.
  • Tools Required (Included) - 13 & 15mm Wrench, 2- AAA Batteries, 5mm Allen Key Screw Driver
combo, and Club Knob. Parts have preassembled hardware for assembly!
           ***NOTE***
3. FB160 Assembly Instructions with TROUBLESHOOTING TIPS:

   • Front/Rear Stabilizer- Remove Lock Pin. 2- curved washers & 2- doomed nuts are preinstalled on
tubes. Tighten with 13 mm wrench.

   • Pedals to Crankarms- ***NOTE***
R- Pedal tighten CLOCKWISE & L- Pedal COUNTER CLOCKWISE with 15 mm wrench.
✓TIP: Use rubber mallet to strike wrench, confirm max tightening!




• Handlebar- ***NOTE***
DO NOT PINCH MIDDLE SENSOR WIRE TO CONSOLE!
✓TIP: Console front support tube has cutout, and be sure to slip the wire into notch, secure Molex
connection for speed sensor, and push terminals down the tubing. Then, use 5mm Allen wrench to
tighten handlebars to main frame.
