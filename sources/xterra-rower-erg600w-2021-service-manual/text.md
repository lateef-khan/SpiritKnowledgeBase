Service        1.Replacing Parts
Parts          Tank/Impeller                                   Rev.
Procedures:                                                           1
1. Disassemble tank plate after finishing all steps as
   above guides and take out from the main frame.
2. Take out the tank assembly and remove all screws
   from the middle of upper and lower tank, then you can              2
   replace defective tank or impeller or related rubber
   parts.
3. Prepare new plastic impeller, put rubber seal through
   shaft, make sure it in right direction, flat side towards
   plastic impeller. Prepare upper tank and turn it over,
   insert a black rubber seal between the middle hole of
   tank. Place big rubber sealing ring properly in upper
   tank. As shown in figure 1.
4. Insert impeller shaft to hole of tank as shown in right
   picture, you may need a base to support the tank to
   make steps go on smoothly. Then cover another tank
   and secure with 12 M3*20 stainless screws in the
   middle. As shown in figure 2. Insert a white foam
   spacer into middle sunken area of upper tank.
5. Assemble tank assembly onto main frame. Put tank
   plate on the tank, use S4 Allen wrench to tight 6
   M6*15 screws on upper tank and use S5 Allen wrench
   to tight 4 M8*15 screws , as shown in figure 3.         3
6. Put magnet seat onto the tank plate, match the hole
   with shaft then drive in a pin to fix the position of
   shaft. Attach a large stainless washer and brush some
   oil.
7. Go on above steps to assemble mesh belt wheel and
   handlebar.                                              4
8. Stand the main frame up, secure the lower tank plate
   with 6 M6*15 screws in bottom of tank, and 2 M8*15
   screws in side frame.

Service 2.Q&A
Problem Console Error                                                    Rev.
Q:The console without date                                                      1
A:When there is no display,check as below:
1.Check if batteries have enough power? If not, change new
one to try again.
1. Check if wire are connected properly? If not, try to connect   25MM
 all the wire again.
2. Check if magnets are installed properly in belt wheel? If
not, install a new magnet in hole of belt wheel.
3. Check the distance to confirm if sensor bar is moved
away from magnet? If distance is over 5MM,move sensor bar                       2
to keep standard distance
4. Check if wire have breakage? If yes, change new wire.
5. If all is OK, change a new console.

Q:The console can power on by itself
A:The control key gets stuck, change a new console.

Q:The console without display                                                   3
A:Check if batteries have enough power? If not, change new
one to try. If not, change a new console.

Problem         Strap can’t retract                               Rev.
Q: When you do exercise, the handlebar strap can’t retract               1
smoothly?
A:Check as below
1. Check if bungee cord loses elasticity, adjust bungee cord to
another hook to improve its elasticity.
2. Check if handlebar can’t return to the handlebar seat, you
need to change the mesh belt wheel as above related Manual.

Service        2.Q&A
Problem        Leaking water                                      Rev.
Q:If you find a little water spill out from the middle of upper          1
tank when you stand the rower up, this is a normal
phenomenon but may check if you put too much water.
A: You can go on running this machine.
Q: Some water leaks from bottom of the tank?
A: Maybe the lower tank has a little crack, please replace tank
as above related Manual.
Q: Some water leaks from middle of upper tank and lower
tank?                                                                    2
A: Sealing ring is defective or not properly assembled
change a new sealing ring to assemble tank.
