                             Extended Labor Warranty Agreement



Thank you for choosing to extend the labor coverage on your new SOLE equipment! This
Extended Labor Warranty Agreement outlines the terms and conditions of the additional 1 or 2
year labor coverage provided by Sole Fitness for customers who opt for the extended labor
warranty.​


Coverage Period


The coverage period for the extended labor warranty goes into effect after the manufacturer's
labor warranty period has expired. The specific duration of coverage for your equipment is
outlined in the warranty registration documents provided to you upon purchase.

During the extended labor coverage period, Sole Fitness will provide:

Coverage Details:

      ●​ What’s Covered?
          ○​ Technician labor costs and extended trip fees.
          ○​ Repairs for mechanical or electrical failures—as long as they meet the warranty’s
             conditions.​

      ●​ What’s Not Covered?
          ○​ Cosmetic items including but not limited to the following: grips, console overlays,
             and labels/decals.
          ○​ Damage from misuse or unauthorized repairs.
          ○​ Machines located outside the Continental US.

Additional coverage details, including exclusions and limitations, are provided in the
Manufacturer's Warranty documentation found in the included owner's manual. ​
It can also be found online at https://www.soletreadmills.com/pages/service-and-manuals
