Parts                                                          Tank/Impeller       Rev.
Procedures:                                                                               1
1. Disassemble the tank plate after finishing all above
   guided steps and take it out from the main frame.
2. Take out the tank assembly and remove all screws in the
   middle of upper and lower tank, then you can replace
   the defective tank or impeller or related rubber parts.                                2
3. Prepare a new plastic impeller, put one stainless washer
   through shaft, press Mechanical Sealing Ring into
   shaft, make sure it touch stainless washer closely,as
   shown in figure 1.
4. Prepare upper tank and turn it over, insert a white
   graphite spacer between middle hole of tank, and
   keep it fixed,you need to add some special glue for
   plastic at edge of tank hole.Place a big rubber sealing
   ring properly in upper tank. Insert impeller shaft to the
   hole of tank as shown in right pictures, you may need a
   base to support the tank to make sure the steps go on
   smoothly. As shown in figure 2.
5. Cover another tank and secure with 12 M3*20 stainless
   screws in the middle. As shown in figure 3.
6. Put one Lower Bearing base with bearing through
   shaft, take care the direction; Assemble total tank
   assembly onto the main frame. Put tank plate up on the

   tank, use S4 Allen wrench to tight 6 M6*15 screws on
   upper tank and use S5 Allen wrench to tight 4 M8*15    3
   screws, as shown in figure 3.
7. Go on above steps to assemble mesh belt wheel and
   handlebar.

Service                                                           2.Q&A
Problem                                                           Console Error   Rev.
Q:The console without data                                                               1
A:When there is no display, check as below:
1. Check if batteries have enough power? If not, change new
battery to try.
2. Check if wires are connected properly? If not, try to
connect all the wires again.
3. Check if magnets are right in mesh belt wheel? If not, press
a new magnet on hole of mesh belt wheel.
4. Check if wires have breakage? If yes, change new wire.
5. If all is OK, change a new console.                                                   2
Q:The console can power on by itself
A: The control key gets stuck, change a new console.

Q:The console without display
A: Check if batteries have enough power? If not, change new
battery to try. If still not OK, change a new console.
                                                                                         3
Q:The console can not get pulse data
A:Check if use a 5.0KHZ chest belt, you must wear chest belt
correctly.

Service                                                            2.Q&A
Problem                                                            Leaking water   Rev.
Q: If you find a little water spill out from the middle of upper                          1
tank when you stand the rower up, this is a normal
phenomenon but may check if you put too much water.
A: You can go on running this machine.
Q: Some water leaks from the bottom of the tank?
A: Maybe the lower tank has little crack, please replace tank
as above related Manual.
Q: Some water leaks from the middle of upper tank and lower
tank?                                                                                     2
A: Sealing ring is defective or not properly assembled,
change a new sealing ring to assemble tank.
