How to tension Running Belt

- On side of the belt in the middle, lift the belt straight up off the deck. Measure between where you are lifting the belt and the deck surface. It should measure anywhere from 2-3 inches. If belt is too loose, and the back of the machine with the 2 end caps, the top holle is the rear roller bolts. Take either a 5 or 6 mm allen wrench and tighten those bolts quarter turns at a time. Make sure you are doing the same number of turns on both bolts. And you will have to walk on it periodically to check to see if the slipping is getting better.
