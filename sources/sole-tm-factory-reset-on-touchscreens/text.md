Factory Reset on Touchscreens

On the Log In screen, tap the words “Log In” (or ”Welcome” or the Sole Logo if neither words are present) a bunch of times rapidly
You will get a message that says “Are you sure you want to Factory Reset?” Tap “Yes”
Console will eventually reset to the login screen. When it comes back, you will notice all profiles that were on the machine have been deleted. If you have a profile on the Sole+ app, you can relink it to the machine and all your information will still be there.

Note: This factory reset will only remove the user profiles and clear the media apps’ cache. It will not wipe any logins in the media apps or reset any app data.
