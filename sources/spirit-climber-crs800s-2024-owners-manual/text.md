SETTING UP YOUR SEMI-RECUMBENT STEPPER

SEAT ROTATION
Lift left lever and hold while rotating the seat to desired position.
Release lever.


SEAT RECLINE
Lift right lever and hold while adjusting seat position to the desired position. Release
lever.

SEAT FORE/AFT ADJUSTMENT
Lift lever and hold while adjusting seat position to the desired distance from the
console. Release lever.

ARMREST ADJUSTMENT
Push the button and hold while adjusting armrests position to the desired angle.

HANDHOLD ADJUSTMENT
Open lever. Adjust handhold to desired distance from seat. Close lever securely.

Powering On & Starting Operation
