1. Assembly sequence and precautions

1-1. Front post assembly
    When assembling the front post, you need to first connect the signal cables on the
    right front post, and then insert the excess wire length into the tube to avoid
    compression and damage to the signal cables.




1-2. Assembling console, storage box and desktop rack
    a.   Connect the cables
    b. Place the console set on the front post
         CAUTION: When placing the console stand assembly, you need to insert the
         excessive wire length into the tube to avoid compression and damage to the
         signal cables.
    c.   Fix the console set by fastening the screws

       b. Fix the left and right upper handrail by screws.

5. Remove the motor
  a. Follow step 2-1. Remove the left/right end cap covers.
  b. Remove the screws fixing the back cover and remove the back cover.




  c. Loosen the nut that adjusts the belt tightness (cannot be removed)




  d. Use a #13 wrench to remove the screws fixing the motor and take out the motor.

6. Steps for incline motor replacement
  a. Place the new incline motor on the side of the machine and connect to the power and
     signal cables of the original incline motor.
  b. Press the START button on the upper controller to start the treadmill.
  c. Press the Incline UP button to incline LEVEL 2 position.
  d. Wait until the incline motor stops rotating, press the incline DOWN button to incline
     LEVEL 0 position.
  e. Wait until the incline motor stops rotating, turn the telescopic tube to adjust the
     assembly size, measure the assembly size before installation (should be 268.5mm, as
     in figure below).
  f. Remove the old incline motor and install the new incline motor.

11. Engineering Mode Operation Instructions
    A. Entering Engineering Mode
      - Remove the SAFETY KEY.
      - Press and hold the ENTER + START buttons simultaneously while turning on the
        power.

    B. The system will enter Engineering Mode:




      - The dot matrix display will show "En".
      - The Heart Rate window will show the software Version.
      - The Time window will show the software Date.

    C. Metric/Imperial Unit Switching




      - Press the ENTER key to enter the unit switching interface.
      - The window will display "Km" (Metric) or "Mile" (Imperial).
      - Press the FAST key to toggle between "Km" and "Mile".
      - Press ENTER to save your selection and proceed to the Total Time display.
      - To exit and restart, replace the SAFETY KEY.

D. Total Time Display and Reset




  - The Time Window will display the total accumulated time.
  - The display range is 0–9999. It will not reset to 0 automatically after reaching
    9999 unless manually cleared.
  - To Reset: Long-press the STOP button for three seconds to clear the total usage
    time memory.
  - Press ENTER to proceed to the Total Distance display.
  - Alternatively, replace the SAFETY KEY to restart the system.

E. Total Distance Display and Reset




  - The Distance Window will display the total accumulated distance.
  - The display range is 0–9999. It will not reset to 0 automatically unless manually
    cleared.
  - To Reset: Long-press the STOP button for three seconds to clear the total usage
    distance memory.
  - Press ENTER or replace the SAFETY KEY to restart the machine.

12. Explanation and troubleshooting of error messages

1. The console does not light up.
Cause: The console has no power supply.
Troubleshooting: (a). Check whether the AC power is supplied to the driver board.
                   (b). Check if the upper and lower controller connection cable is connected
                        or damaged.
                   (c). Console or driver board failure. Need replacement.



2. The console is on but displays "- - -".
Cause: The safety key connection of the console is abnormal.
Troubleshooting: (a). Check whether the micro switch in the safety key box is faulty
                   (b). Check whether the wire connecting the safety key to the console is
                        correct.



3. The consoles displays “E1”
Cause I: The console does not receive the speed signal
          Scenario 1 - The console displays (E1) after no operation of the driver motor for
                        about 6 seconds.
          Troubleshooting: (a). Check if the connection cable of the driver motor is
                                connected correctly.
                              (b). Driver board or driver motor failure. Need replacement.

          Scenario 2 - The console displays (E1) about 6 seconds after the driver motor
                        operates.
          Troubleshooting: (a). Check whether the speed sensor cable is connected
                                correctly.
                              (b). Check whether the speed sensor is faulty.
                              (c). Check whether the distance between the speed sensor and
                                   the magnet is within the range of 3~6mm

Cause II: Current limit protection.
          Scenario 3 - After operating for some time, the console displays (E1) and shuts
                        down. After being shut down for some time, the treadmill can be
                        used again.
          Troubleshooting: (a). Add lubricant between the running deck and running belt.
                              (b). Motor failure. Need replacement.

4. The Console displays “E3”
Cause: Poor communication between upper and lower controllers.
Troubleshooting: (a). Check whether the upper and lower controller connection cable is
                      connected or damaged.
                  (b). Console or driver board failure. Need replacement.



5. The Console displays “E6”
Cause: The console did not receive the change in the VR value of the incline motor.

        Scenario 1 - The VR value of the incline motor is out of range, causing the telescopic
                      tube to get stuck.
        Troubleshooting: (a). Remove the incline motor and connect it to the driver board.
                           (b). Turn on the power of the treadmill, and the incline motor
                                will automatically rotate to the position of correct VR value.
                           (c). Rotate the telescopic tube and adjust to the correct
                                assembly size (268.5mm between the upper and lower
                                holes).
                           (d). Install the incline motor back to its original position.

        Scenario 2 - Incline motor does not operate.
        Troubleshooting: (a). Check whether the connection cable of the incline motor is
                              connected properly or damaged.
                           (b). Incline motor failure. Need replacement.
