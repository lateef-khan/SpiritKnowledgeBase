# Cables Not Retracting

If the cables aren't retracting automatically, power off the SRVO, pull out both
cables all the way, and turn the machine back on while maintaining some tension
on the cables. The machine should recognize the cables are pulled out and try to
automatically retract. Some older SRVOs will have a button on the side to also
trigger the machine to retract. If that does not fix, the full SRVO will need to
be replaced since we do not replace individual parts.
