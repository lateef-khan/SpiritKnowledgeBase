              Chapter 1.Safety Instructions
    Please read the safety information carefully before use the machine.

1. Safety warning
    Electrical current from power, telephone, and communication cables is

dangerous . To avoid the risk of electric shock：

       plug or unplug cables , nor install, maintain , or reconfigure this product
        during a thunderstorm .

       Connect the power cord correctly and use a well - grounded power outlet.

       Whenever possible, use only one hand to connect or disconnect device
        cables .

       Never turn on any equipment when there are signs of fire, flooding, or house
        collapse.

       Unless otherwise instructed during installation and configuration, disconnect
        equipment such as power cords, telecommunication systems, networks, and
        modems that are connected before opening the equipment cover .

       installing, moving , or removing the back cover of this product , plug and
        unplug device cables as described in the following table :


   When connected , do the following:         When disconnected , do the following:
   1. Turn off all devices                    1. Turn off all devices
   2. Connect the power cable to the device   2. Unplug the power cord from the
   3. Connect the signal cable to the power outlet
   corresponding port                         3. Unplug the signal cable from each
   4. Connect the power cable to the socket   connector
   5. Turn on the device                      4. Unplug the power cord from the

2. Matters needing attention
     1. When replacing the system battery, please use the same type of battery for
replacement.
     2. Some batteries contain lithium, which may explode if used, handled or
handled improperly . To prevent accidents, do not:

       Throwing or immersing the battery in water

       Heat the battery to over 100°C (212°F)

       Repair or disassemble the battery without authorization

     3. The power control button on the device and the switch on the power supply
may not completely cut off the current supplied to the device ; at the same time, the
device may also have multiple power cords . To completely power down the device,
make sure all power cords are disconnected.
     4. Never remove any component housing that has a hazard label attached .

                 Chapter 3.Disassembly guide
 I. Removed rear cover




(Tools: Phillips screwdriver)

1. Remove screws No. 2 and No. 3.

2. First lift the rear cover upward, then pull it outward to remove it.




                                                   8
II. Removed bottom plate




(Tool: Phillips screwdriver)

1. First remove Screw No. 1, then remove Screw No. 2.

2. After keeping the foot pads properly, the bottom plate can be removed.




                                              9
 III. Remove motor module




                                                                                   3




(Tools: 6mm Hex wrench, 4mm Hex wrench, Phillips screwdriver)

1. Pull out the communication wire, power supply wire, ground wire and temperature sensing wire

of the motor.

(After the wire is pulled out, the red, black and blue terminals of the motor must be separated. If

any two terminals contact, the winding wheel will not move, affecting disassembly and assembly.)

2. Remove 4 screws (3) and gaskets with a 6mm hex wrench to separate the motor module from

the resistance box.

 IV. Remove reel and rope




 (Tool: Phillips screwdriver)
1. Remove the (1) and (3)screws, and the (2) flange, (4) bearing, (5) motor fixing
plate can be separated.




(Tool: Phillips screwdriver, circlip pliers)
1.First use the circlip pliers to remove (1) the spring, and unscrew (9) the limit post
counterclockwise.
2.Twist (2) the special nut out clockwise, remove (3) the screws and (4) rope baffle
rod.
3.Then the whole rope reel of (5) steel rope, (6) rope wheel, (7) rope stopper block, (8)
rope baffle and (10) screw can be removed from the cover.
4.Remove (10) the screw can to remove (5) the rope.
(Note: During installation, (2) the special nut and (4) rope baffle rod need to be
completely screwed to the bottom by counter-clockwise rotation)

2. Remove (4) the screw, you can separate (2) the cover and (3) the connecting plate
3. Pull (5) the drive shaft from (6) the motor.


(Note: Don't lose the iron on the drive shaft)
