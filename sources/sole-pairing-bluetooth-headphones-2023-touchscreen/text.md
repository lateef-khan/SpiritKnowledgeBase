# Pairing Bluetooth Headphones to SOLE 2023 Touchscreen Models

Step transcript of the video file `Pairing Bluetooth Headphones to SOLE 2023 Touchscreen Models.mp4`.

This video demonstrates the process for pairing Bluetooth headphones with 2023 SOLE touchscreen machines, applicable to models including bikes, ellipticals, and treadmills.

## Pairing Instructions

1. Log In: Log into a user profile on the machine.
2. Access Bluetooth Settings: Select the Bluetooth button located in the top left corner of the screen.
3. Enable Bluetooth: Ensure the Bluetooth setting is switched to "on".
4. Enter Pairing Mode: Place your headphones into pairing mode according to the manufacturer's instructions (as this varies by device).
5. Connect: Select your headphones from the list on the screen. A green dot will appear next to the Bluetooth icon once an active connection is established.

## Troubleshooting and Important Considerations

- Prevent Interference: Ensure your headphones are not currently connected to another device, such as a phone, as this can prevent them from appearing in the machine's search list.
- Multi-Audio Limitations: The machine does not support multi-audio connections, meaning it cannot play audio from multiple sources simultaneously.
- Refreshing the Search: If the menu stops searching for nearby devices, toggle the Bluetooth setting off and back on to refresh the list.
- Reconnection: If you connect your headphones to a different device, you may need to disconnect them from that device and manually select the headphones in the machine's Bluetooth menu to reconnect.
- Hardware Diagnostics: If your headphones still do not appear, try using a different pair of headphones to determine if the issue lies with the machine or the specific pair you are attempting to use.
