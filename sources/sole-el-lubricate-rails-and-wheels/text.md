How to Lubricate Elliptical Rails and Wheels

Lubricating the rails and wheels should be performed once a month and is important to reduce noise as well as wear on the aluminum rails and slide wheels.

What you need: 100% Silicone Lubricant (preferably gel form), rag
Clean off rails and slide wheels of dust of old lubricant
Put a small line on each rail
Ride machine slowly to spread rails and slide wheels
