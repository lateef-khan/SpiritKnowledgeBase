correct measurement of your resistance level.

Setting up steps
1. Hold the button until a green light is on, then turn right half of circle and turn left half of circle, keep doing
   this until the green light is turn off automatically.
                                                  Repeat 3 To 5 Times

                                                                                                 Until Light Turn Off

2. Turn the tension knob to the lowest resistance level, then short press the button, the (R) LED will be red.
   Once the red light comes on, turn the knob to the highest resistance. When you are at the highest
   resistance (the tension knob cannot turn any more) short press the button to turn off the red light. This
   finish the setting of levels on tension knob.
