# SRVO phone app screens

Transcribed from 16 screenshots of the phone app taken on iOS. Each heading is
one screenshot file. Only text and controls visible on screen are recorded.
Values shown next to a setting are that demo account's values, not defaults,
unless the screen labels them otherwise.

## SETTINGS PAGE.png

Screen title: **Settings**. Bottom tab bar across the whole app, left to right:
Workouts, Exercises, Classes, Activity, Settings.

Section **Account**:
- account avatar, username and e-mail address
- My Favorites
- My Workouts
- Reset Password
- Delete Account

Section **SRVO**:
- the paired SRVO, shown by device name with a status line beneath it
  ("Connected" in the screenshot) and an information (i) button to its right
- Safety Settings
- Unpair Device

## BLE CONNECT PAGE.png

Screen title: **Ble Connect**, with a Done button on the left and a refresh
button on the right. This is the Bluetooth device search screen. When nothing is
found it reads "Looks like you don't have any devices nearby".

## SAFETY PAGE.png

Screen title: **Safety**. Reached from Safety Settings on the Settings screen.

**Static Safety** - on/off toggle. On-screen description: "When the cable is
being held at the same position for a long time, the machine will drop
resistance to minimum and pause the workout until you are ready to start a new
set." Adjustable rows beneath it:

| Setting | Value in the screenshot |
|---|---|
| Time Trigger | 10 sec |
| Retract After | 5 sec |
| Resistance Reduction | 100 % |

**Tilt Safety** - on/off toggle. On-screen description: "When one cable gets
pulled further out than the other, the machine will drop resistance to minimum
and pause the workout until you are ready to start a new set."

The screenshot is cut off below Tilt Safety, so any settings under that toggle
are not recorded here.

## ABOUT TRAINING MODES (TRAINING MODES INFO PAGE.png)

Screen title: **About Training Modes**.

**Standard** - "In Standard mode, resistance remains constant: the pull-out and
pull-back resistance values are equal. For example, if you set the resistance to
10 kg, both pull-out and pull-back resistance will be set to 10 kg. Standard
mode is indicated as "Mode 1" on the SRVO control panel and is the default on
startup."

**Eccentric** - "The resistance value indicates the eccentric (pull-back)
resistance. Its ratio to the concentric (pull-out) resistance is 10:7. It means
if you set the resistance to 10 kg, eccentric and concentric resistance ..."

The screenshot is cut off in the middle of the Eccentric paragraph. The third
mode is not visible in this screenshot.

## WORKOUTS PAGE.png

Screen title: **Workouts**, with an add (+) button and a search button. Tabs:
ALL and SPORTS SERIES. Filter chips: a bookmark, "Created by me", "Muscles".

First entry: **Free Workout** - "Improvise as you go: your reps and weight will
be recorded", with a play button.

Below it, a list of picture cards for named workouts with their muscle groups,
for example "Back Essentials - Arms, Back, Shoulders, Glutes".

## EXERCISES PAGE.png

Screen title: **Exercises**. Filter chips: a bookmark, Search, Warm Up, and more
off-screen. Muscle group tiles: Arms, Shoulders, Back, Chest, Core, Glutes,
Legs.

Beneath the tiles, an alphabetical exercise list with a thumbnail, an exercise
name and its muscle group, for example "Airplane Stretch - Glutes" and
"Alternating Biceps Curl - Arms".

## CLASSES PAGE.png

Screen title: **Classes**, with a search button. Filter chips: a bookmark,
Duration, Instructors. Each class is a picture card with its length overlaid,
the class name with the instructor's name, and the muscle groups it works, for
example "15min - Strength And Grit With Margaret Valenzuela - Arms, Back, Chest,
Core, Glutes, Legs, Shoulders".
