Burning Smell from Treadmill

The smell is most likely coming from the motor controller of the machine. Rarely will it ever be coming from the drive motor itself. This smell can sometimes be solved by lubricating between the belt and deck so the motor controller isn’t working as hard. Perform the following to verify lubrication habits and any wear to the parts of the machine. Any visibly damaged parts should be replaced.

Ask about lubrication (Question: How often do you lubricate?)
Whether the machine has been lubricated properly or not, check Running Belt and Running Deck are worn or damaged (Slide hand between running belt and deck palm up first. See if texture of running belt is gone in the middle. Palm down, see if running deck is damaged.)
If Running Belt or Running Deck are worn or damaged, replace Running Belt or Running Deck (If Running Deck is not damaged, do not replace), and replace motor controller. Continue to Step 2 to check for any damaged parts.
If the machine has not been lubricated properly, but the Running Belt and Running Deck are in good condition, just have the customer lubricate properly. If lubricating does not resolve, the motor controller will need to be replaced. Continue to Step 2 to check for any damaged parts.
If the Running Belt and Running Deck are in good condition and the customer has been lubricating properly, move on to Step 2 to check for damaged parts. The motor controller will need to be replaced if nothing else is found.
Remove Motor Hood Cover.
Have the customer look at wires plugged into the Motor Controller to see if any wires and Motor Controller have been damaged.
If the customer does find damage, have them send you a picture of damage to determine what all needs to be replaced. If any wires are damaged that plug into the motor controller, replace the motor controller along with the parts that are damaged.
If the customer does not find damage, just replace parts from Step 1.
