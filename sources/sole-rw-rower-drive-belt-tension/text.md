Rower Drive Belt Tension




Circled in Blue, loosen nuts on both sides of flywheel
Circled in Red, Tighten nut to pull Flywheel and tighten the drive belt
Circled in Blue, tighten nuts on both sides of flywheel

[Picture note added at ingest; not in the original document. The original is one
photo of the flywheel with two nuts circled, and the steps above cannot be
followed without it. Blue circles the hex nut on the flywheel bracket bolt, at
the point where the bracket arm crosses the flywheel hub; there is one of these
on each side of the flywheel. Red circles the smaller nut on the threaded
adjuster rod that sits just above and outboard of the blue one; turning it draws
the flywheel away from the drive pulley and so tightens the drive belt. The
photo is saved beside this file as drive-belt-tension.png.]
