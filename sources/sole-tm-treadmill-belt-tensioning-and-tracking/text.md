Treadmill Belt Tensioning and Tracking

Tensioning
At the very back of the machine where the end caps are, the top hole in each end cap is the rear roller bolts used for tensioning.
Use Allen Wrench to both left and right bolts to tension the running belt.
NOTE: Make sure to turn both bolts the same number of turns, otherwise the belt walk off to one side
NOTE: Instructions for tension the belt are in the Owner’s Manual

Tracking
Very back of the machine, user’s left side end cap is the only one you will use
Turn rear roller bolt quarter turns at a time in the direction you want the belt to go (left, counter-clockwise; right, clockwise)
Run the machine at low speed (max of 2) and watch the belt as it walks over. Once it looks center, press Stop button
Undo the number of turns you did on the left rear roller bolt to straighten the roller back out
NOTE: You may have to straighten out the rear roller before doing the adjustment
NOTE: Instructions for belt tracking are in the Owner’s Manual
