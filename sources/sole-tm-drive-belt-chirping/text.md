Drive Belt Chirping

If you hear a chirping noise from coming under the motor hood cover, most likely it is coming from the drive belt as it moves. Make sure the drive belt is tensioned properly and not damaged. If everything looks good, try the following:

Take a regular bar of soap (do not wet the soap)
Run the machine at its lowest speed
Hold bar of soap to the ribbed side of the drive belt
Hold it there for a few minutes

This will “lubricate” the drive belt and will help the chirping noise.
