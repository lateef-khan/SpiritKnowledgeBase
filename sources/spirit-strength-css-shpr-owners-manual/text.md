to commencing an exercise program. If at any time during
exercise you feel faint, dizzy or experience pain,

* STOP EXERCISING and consult your physician.

* Keep body parts (hands, feet, hair, etc.), clothing and jewelry
away from moving parts to avoid injury.

¢ Follow instructions provided in this manual for correct foot

Operating Warnings
