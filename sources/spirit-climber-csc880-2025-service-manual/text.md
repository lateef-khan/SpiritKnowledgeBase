1.Maintenance Cautions

1.Turn off the power when replacing any parts such as electronic meter, brake system, generator, etc. Avoid
personal or product injury that may be caused by energized operation.

2.When replacing parts, please use Anyfit's original parts and make sure the product type and specification
match. If you need to replace parts with different specifications for testing in special condition, please
contact our technical staff for confirmation before operation.

3.When replacing parts, especially electrical parts, clean the parts and their surroundings before assembly
to avoid damage caused by screws and other debris.

1） Console : Console is a kind of human-machine interactive operation component, which can display
   fitness data: time, speed, distance, energy consumed, fitness program, etc.. The users can control the
   machine operation, stop, speed increase or decrease through the button operation, and realize the
   input of instructions through the electronic meter.

2） Handrails: handrails as part of the machine main frame. As a safety auxiliary parts, at the same time
   the handrail has a design hand grip heartbeat, emergency stop switch and other operating parts.

3） Step : Important structural parts of the stair machine, stair component parts.

4） Main Frame ：The most important part of the stair machine, internal running structure, motor, drive
   chain, lower control panel, etc. are working inside the machine.
