OPERATING PRINCIPLE

The patient pushes the handles with their hands. The operator can adjust the workload by selecting the increase/decrease indicators
on the console to select the resistance. When the workload change is requested, and electromagnet is energized at field strengths
relative to the workload requested. The magnetic field generated by the coil is induced into a flywheel creating eddy currents to flow in
the flywheel. The eddy currents then create an opposing magnetic field to the electromagnetic field creating more or less resistance at
the handles, determined by workload setting by the operator.

INTENDED OPERATOR
 Education: medical professional or medical occupation with physical therapist
   license.
 Knowledge: the operator should read the user’s manual before use.

INTENDED INSTALLER
 Education: high school or above.
 Knowledge/Experience: the installer shall be given a specific training by
   manufacturer. The installer must have experience in this equipment assembly and
   disassembly.
 Background: the installer must be electro-mechanically trained.
 Professional competence: normal vision ability required.

OPERATING THE 8.5UE

Unique uses for the 8.5UE
•   Strong, solid, and steady frame with a step-through design
    makes it extremely easy to get on and off.
•   Adjustable crank arms help patients with limited mobility
    or injuries to have smaller joint range of motion due to
    smaller circular upper body motions. The handle can
    be moved along the crank to adjust the arm length to
    accommodate a range of motion as small as 15 degrees.
•   Symmetry feature measures balance between left and right
    upper extremity at the handles.
•   Bidirectional resistance allows both forward and reverse
    pedaling for instantaneous retro-cycling. It provides a
    lower start-up, smooth, and comfortable resistance.
•   The revolving shaped rotating handle to allow wrist
    patients to use the upper body without discomfort and
    wrist pain. The difference in grip angle apply the different
    shoulder and arm muscles activities.
•   The removable seat provides accessibility. Roll the
    removable seat away to allow for direct wheelchair access
•   The swivel seat can turn 180 degrees in both directions for
    assistance in moving patients onto the seat.
•   Adjustable seat back allows user to find a comfortable hip
    angle.
•   The wheelchair anchor design incorporates a strap that
    secures the wheelchair at the wheel or frame member.

FEATURES - WORKLOAD TYPE

Constant Power
The program will automatically control the resistance level
at the handles, depending on user speed, to maintain a
steady power workload.

Isokinetic
It provides accommodating resistance at a fixed speed level.
The user controls the resistance at the handles by pushing
harder or lighter. The desired pedaling speed is entered and
the computer increases the resistance automatically if the
user tries to overcome the set speed.

Constant Resistance
It provides a fixed resistance level independent of pedaling
speed. The user controls the power level by pushing harder
or lighter.
