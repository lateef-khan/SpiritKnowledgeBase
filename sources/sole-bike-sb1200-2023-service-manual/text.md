4.Product Operation




Power Up
When power is connected to the fitness bike the console will automatically power up. These models
operate on DC 12V/3.3A which is supplied by the power pack that is connected to 115 VAC. There is a
power socket located where the line cord plugs into the unit on the front of the bike.

Operation
Use the touchscreen console to select from preset programs and enjoy media content of your choice
while on the go.


                                                        -5-
GETTING STARTED
Launched for the first time, the console will prompt you to either enter as a guest or create a new profile. It is recommended to
set up your own profile: it allows your machine to remember your physical parameters, and your workout records can be
synchronized with the SOLE+ mobile app. Tap + to create a new profile. A maximum of 9 profiles can be created. Each profile can
be protected with a passcode.




To link a SOLE+ account, scan the QR code on the console screen to download the mobile app first. Once the app is installed and
the registration is complete, press “Link” on the bottom of the console screen, then open the QR code scanning camera in your
SOLE+ app and scan the QR code once again to link your local profile with the SOLE+ account. Please keep in mind that your
console profile cannot be linked to multiple SOLE+ accounts at the same time. If you skip this step, you can access it again later
in Profile settings.


HOME SCREEN
The main screen of the Training section displays an overview of your activity, and offers 5 training modes: Start, Timer (Manual),
Distance, Calories and Heart Rate.
If in the Guest mode, you can set your age and weight here; it will help the machine calculate your workout summary more
accurately.




                                                                -6-
WORKOUT PROGRAMS
Tap on any of the program cards to open a Program Setup page, where you can set properties of your workout, or switch
between programs by pressing on the arrow buttons in the upper top right corner of the screen. To adjust workout parameters,
you can drag the ruler, tap on the + and – buttons, or tap on the number to open a numpad and input the value directly.




Timer (Manual) program is a timed workout with resistance level controlled manually at any time during the workout.
Distance, Calories programs set a target value for your workout. The program ends once the target has been reached.




In the Heart Rate program, use resistance to control your heart rate. Follow the resistance level recommendations to keep your
heart rate within 5 bpm of your target. A heart rate monitor is required for this program.
On the program setup page, you can either set the target bpm directly or select 60% or 80% of your age-predicted maximum
heart rate, allowing the machine to calculate your target automatically. Choosing the second option, make sure you have let the
machine know how old you are, otherwise the calculations will be based on the default values. The program is finished when the
time is up, or if your heart rate is 20% higher than the target.




                                                                   -7-
HISTORY
To see your latest workout records, press “History” on the main screen of the Training section. Up to 10 records can be stored
locally on the console, but you can access your full workout history if you link your profile to the SOLE+ mobile app. Tap on the
record preview to see the full summary. Each user can only see his or her workouts.




MEDIA
Tap on the right tab of the bottom panel to access the Media section. Choose from a variety of pre-installed third-party Apps or
use the Mirroring function to cast the content from your smartphone directly to the console screen. For detailed instructions,
press the ? icon.




Once the content is on, use the floating panel for navigation and operating full screen mode. Touch the panel’s top edge and
drag the panel around the screen to find the perfect place for it, where it will not prevent you from interacting with the content.
Press Hide Panels to enter the full screen mode without stats on the top and tabs on the bottom and Show Panels to bring them
back. Use the arrow button on the right side of the panel to hide & show the text labels: it allows to further minimize the panel’s
size. Press “Apps” to go back to the content sources selection.




                                                                  -8-
WORKOUT MODE
Once the workout has been started, the console will appear in its workout mode. During the workout, switch between Stats and
Charts views of the Training section or go to Media section to enjoy your favorite media content. In the Stats view, you can
select parameters you would like to display by tapping on the number. Please turn the knob to adjust the resistance level. Press
“Pause” to pause the workout. Here, you can get back to training, or end your workout and see the summary. After 5 minutes of
inactivity in the Pause mode, the workout will end automatically.

7.1 Error Message: EEPROM ERR

Definition:
All screens are off, and outputs are stop when EEPROM damaged or malfunction. Display message will show “EEPROM ERR”.


Troubleshooting:
The EEPROM is abnormal, please replace the Display Board directly.


7.2 Error Message: LWR not found
Definition:
Driver board controller is not found.


Troubleshooting:
1. Check connector of 7 PIN computer cable.
2. Replace lower controller.
3. Replace upper controller.


7.3 Error Message: LWR not match
Definition:
Driver board controller is not match with console.


Troubleshooting:
1. Check driver board controller number is CS51006-02.
2. Replace upper controller.

7.5 Engineer Mode
Press 10 times on “Settings” letter to enter engineering mode when you’re at the Settings page.




There are 4 pages in the Engineer Mode, FUNCTION, SERVICE, FACTORY SETTING, and CALIBRATION.

FUNCTION




1)Beep: Set whether there is a beep sound when pressing the physical button.
2)Software Update:
-COMMAND: Reserved, no function.
-OSOTA: Forcibly check if the OS has updates available.
-USB: Use a USB flash drive to update through the Console APP.
3)Machine Type:
-Switch the machine models.
-When switching machine models, all settings will be reset to default values, and all local accounts and records will be cleared
and cannot be recovered.
4)Spin Bike Level Calibration:
After pressing the START button, the resistance system's G-sensor calibration process will begin. User intervention is required
during the calibration process.
-Click “start” to calibrate resistance level and a message “Cali Step 1” will show on the screen.
-Turn the resistance knob to the Maximum level then press “NEXT” button to let the console read the AD value and record the
value into the MAX AD.
-Turn the resistance knob to the minimum level then press “NEXT” button to let the console read the AD value and record the
value into the MIN AD. Then the process is done.

8.Parts Replacing Guide
8.1 Replacing the Batteries of Wireless Transmitter
STEP 1: Pull down the wireless transmitter downwards to unmount it. (As shown in Figure 1)
STEP 2: Push the back cover of the wireless transmitter in the direction of the arrow to replace the batteries. Reverse the steps to
reinstall. (As shown in Figure 2)




8.2 Replacing the Chain Cover
STEP 1: Use an 8mm hex key to remove the center axle screw CP-M8×P1.0(34). Use a crank puller to extract the left crank (33).
Then, use a Phillips screwdriver to remove three umbrella head self-tapping screws - 3.5×12L (79) and three umbrella head
screws - M5×12L(80), to detach the left chain cover (48). (As shown in Figure 1)
STEP 2: Use an 8mm hex key to remove the center axle screw CP-M8×P1.0(34). Use a crank puller to extract the right crank (32).
Then, use a Phillips screwdriver to remove three umbrella head screws - M5×12L (80), to detach the right chain cover (47). (As
shown in Figure 2)
STEP 3: Reverse the steps to reassemble.




                                                               - 20 -
8.3 Replacing the Bluetooth Wireless Knob and Brake Assembly
STEP 1: Remove the flywheel guard (49) and water bottle holder (50). Then, use a 10mm ratchet wrench to remove the cap nut -
M6×6T (83). (Figure 1)
STEP 2: Start by using a 29mm open-end wrench to turn the hex nut adjuster (21) counterclockwise, allowing you to lift the
adjustment assembly upward. Then, use a 2mm hex key to remove the set screw - M4xP0.7x12L (78), allowing you to
disassemble the adjustment assembly and brake inner gear adjuster (22) by turning them apart. Next, use a 2.5mm hex key to
remove the two set screws - M5×5(104) on either side of the adjuster knob (105) and push rod axle (24), turning them
counterclockwise. (Figure2)
STEP 3: Use a 13mm ratchet wrench to loosen and remove the outer hex screw - M8×20L (85), the spring washer - ø8×1.5T (86),
and the flat washer - ø5/16"×18×1.5T (87) that secure the magnetic control brake assembly (on both ends). Then, you can pull
the magnetic control brake assembly forward to remove it. (As shown in Figure 3)
STEP 4: Reverse the steps to reinstall. When reinstalling, pay special attention to adjusting the spacing between the magnetic
control brake assembly's magnet and the flywheel to ensure they are centered.




                                                              - 21 -
8.4 Replacing the Idler Wheel Assembly
STEP 1: Start by removing the right crank (32) and the right chain cover (47). Then, take off the belt (37) from the belt pulley (30).
(Figure 1)
STEP 2: Use a 5mm hex key to remove the umbrella head hex screw - M8×P1.25×15L (66) and the anti-slip washer - ø8×ø18×3T
(72). Then, remove the umbrella head hex screw - M8×P1.25×10L (70) and the flat washer - 5/16"x23x2T (73). Finally, remove
the umbrella head hex screw - M10×P1.5×15L (67) to detach the idler wheel assembly. (As shown in Figure 2)
STEP 3: Reverse the steps to reinstall. When reinstalling, pay special attention to ensuring that the belt tension is at 180 lbs. If it
is insufficient, use a 6mm hex key to tighten the hex screw - M8×80L (65) clockwise to increase the belt tension. Conversely,
turning it counterclockwise will loosen the tension. (As shown in Figure 3)

8.5 Replacing the Flywheel, Axle, and Bearings
STEP 1: Start by removing the right crank (32), right chain cover (47), left crank (33), left chain cover (48), belt (37), and idler
wheel assembly. Rotate the Bluetooth wireless resistance knob to the lightest setting, then remove the nylon nut - M6×6T (83)
that secures the fixed magnetic control assembly. Pull down the push rod axle (24) and detach it from the movable assembly A
weldment (16). Push the magnetic control assembly upward to remove it. (As shown in Figure 1)
STEP 2: Use a 6mm hex key to remove the hex screw - M8×P1.25×30L (74) from the Flywheel Fixed Plate and use a 13mm open-
end wrench to remove the nylon nut - M8×7T (89). Then, use a 4mm hex key to remove three hex screws - M6×P1.0×10L (71)
from the Flywheel Fixed Plate. (As shown in Figure 2)
STEP 3: Remove the Flywheel Fixed Plate assembly (15). (As shown in Figure 3)
STEP 4: Take out the flywheel assembly. (As shown in Figure 4)
STEP 5: Use a C-ring clip clamp to remove the C-ring - ø17(77), allowing you to extract the flywheel axle (28). If you need to
replace the 6003 bearings (62), perform the replacement at this step. (As shown in Figure 5)
STEP 6: Reverse the steps to reassemble.

8.6 Replacing the Drive pulley, Axle, and Bearings
STEP 1: Start by removing the right crank (32), right chain cover (47), left crank (33), left chain cover (48), and belt (37). (Figure 1)
STEP 2: Use a C-ring clip clamp to remove the C-ring - ø20 (75), and then use a rubber mallet to tap out the turntable spindle
assembly (17). (Figure 2)
STEP 3: Use a 6mm hex key to remove five hex screws - M10×20L(68) from the socket, allowing you to remove the belt pulley
(30). If you need to replace the 6004 bearings (53), perform the replacement at this step. (Figure 3)
STEP 4: Reassemble in the reverse order as disassembly.

8.7 Replacing the Handlebar, Sliding Handlebar Mount (Front), and Handlebar Post
STEP 1: Remove the console Rotary Shaft Cover (122) and the console assembly (26). Use a 6mm hex key to unscrew the six
M8×P1.25×15L hex screws (96) located in the socket below the handlebar assembly (4). Once the screws are removed, the
handlebar assembly (4) can be taken apart. Pull the Screen Holder Assembly (7) forward to detach it. (As shown in Figure 1)
STEP 2: To remove the Screen Rack (121), use a 6mm hex key and a 17mm ratchet wrench. First, use the 6mm hex key to unscrew
the M10×20L hex screw (68) inside the umbrella head. Then, remove the plastic washer - round 10×round 24×3T (110) and the
hex nut - M10×8T (111). Once these components are removed, the Screen Rack (121) can be taken apart.
Next, use the 17mm ratchet wrench to unscrew the upper hex nut - M10×8T (111). Remove the flat washer - round 10×round
25×1.5T (107) by taking out the hex nut. Then, use a 4mm hex key to unscrew the set screw - M8×P1.25×6L (115). Afterward, use
a 5mm hex key to unscrew the set screw - M10×P1.5×8 (116) and take out the spring (117) and steel ball (118). This will allow
you to lift the Screen Rotation Assembly (120) upward and remove it. (As shown in Figure 2)
STEP 3: Loosen and remove the fixed bracket of the quick release Lever (39). Remove 2 pieces of aluminum blocks (40) and the
brake spring (41). Then, pull up the Handlebar Post (5) along with the hollow sleeve (42). Use a 4mm hex key to remove the
hexagon screw inside the socket - M5×10L and take out the hollow sleeve (42). (As shown in Figure 3)
STEP 4: Reassemble the components in the reverse order.

8.8 Replacing the Seat Post, Sliding Seat Mount, and Seat
STEP 1: Use a 14mm open-end wrench to loosen the nut underneath the seat cushion (36) to remove the seat cushion (36).
(Figure 1)
STEP 2: Loosen and remove the fixed bracket of the quick-release Lever (38). This will allow you to detach the dumbbell rack
assembly (27) and the Sliding Seat Mount (6). (As shown in Figure 2)
STEP 3: Loosen and remove the fixed bracket of the quick-release Lever (39). Then, remove 2 pieces of aluminum blocks (40) and
the brake spring (41). Pull up the seat post's Seat sliding tube assembly (8) along with the hollow sleeve (42). Use a 4mm hex key
to remove the hexagon screw inside the socket - M5×10L and take out the hollow sleeve (42). (As shown in Figure 3)
STEP 4: Reassemble the components following the reverse steps.
