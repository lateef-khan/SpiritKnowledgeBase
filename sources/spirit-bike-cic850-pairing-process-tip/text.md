New SB700/900 Console | CIC X50
Changing from KM to Miles =
-Press Mode to highlight DIST :

-Press and hold Page until you see a blinking TIME
_-Press Mode so you see a blinking heart

-Press Page 6 times, you should see a bling KG or
LB |

-Press Mode to change from KG to LB or vice versa
-Let console reset, then it should show correctly

Pairing
-Press Mode to highlight DIST
-Press and hold Page until you see a blinking TIME
-Press and hold Mode and Page until you see PAIR
with 3 blink lines.
-Start pedaling until you see ERR or 0
| -If you see ERR, the pairing failed. The battery
may need to be changed in the transmitter, or
the speed sensor is not aligned properly
-If you see 0, pairing was successful
