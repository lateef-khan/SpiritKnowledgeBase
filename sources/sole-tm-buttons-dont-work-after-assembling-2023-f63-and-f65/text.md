Buttons Don’t Work After Assembling
(2023 F63 and F65)

Sometimes it will appear that the machine will turn on, but the machine won’t respond to the start button after the machine is assembled. Try pressing the 12 button and see if it turns on the fan. If the fans turned on, a cable between the bottom and top consoles was plugged in backwards. To fix, unmount the top console and find the backwards cable as seen in the pictures below and plug the cable in the right way. The buttons should now function properly.

If the fan does not turn on when pressing 12, double check the cabling in the images below. If everything looks good and still doesn’t work, replace the top and bottom console assemblies.
