Netflix

Back in August 2025, Netflix removed support of devices running Android 6 which includes our first generation 2020 touchscreen machines. There is no fix as Netflix made the decision and changed things on their end. Replacing the console will not fix and newer touchscreen consoles are not compatible to use on the older machines. There is no workaround as Netflix does not allow itself to be screenshared from another device due to copyright protection.

YouTube

In early 2025, Google dropped support for YouTube on Android 6. As a workaround, we added Google Chrome which then later also dropped support for the machine. The only workaround available is to screenshare with the Sole Mirroring App or with Apple AirPlay.

Google Chrome

Chrome was originally exposed as a workaround when YouTube dropped support of Android 6. In late 2025, Google dropped support for the Google Play Services running on our first generation 2020 touchscreens. Google Chrome does not work as a result and there is no workaround.
