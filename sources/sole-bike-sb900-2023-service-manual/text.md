4.Product Operation




Function
POWER        Watt is the approximation of Applied force during work out.
(WATT)       This is measured by RPM.
TIME         Time is the length of time (min. / sec.)
             The time will count up or count down when user is pedaling. When pedaling stops, timer will stop to
             count up or count down after 3 seconds.
SPEED &      Cadence is measurement of how fast the cranks are rotating in RPM’s. The approximate speed of the bike
CADENCE      can also be displayed (MPH/KPH) in this section.
             In addition to MPH/KPH, CADENCE will also display a bar graph allow riders to keep track of approximate
             cadence.
             Average Cadence or Speed will be automatically shown after rider stop pedaling for 3 seconds.
DISTANCE     Distance is measurement of the approximate distance achieved on the bike. This distance is calculated
             based on user riding a bike with tires having same diameter as flywheel.
CALORIES     Kcal is the approximation of calories burned during work out. Calories are calculated by measuring rider’s
             instantaneous heart rate, age, and weight.
HEART RATE   This is the approximation of heart rate detect from chest belt during work out. Average Heart Rate will
             automatically shown after 3 seconds if console cannot detect current Heart Rate.

Operation
QUICK START
In power saving mode, press page key to bring the console to ‘QUICK Start’ state.




MODE SELECT
Press ‘MODE’ key to select TIME, DIST, KCAL display.




RESETTING ALL MEASURED VALUE
To clear all measured values, press and hold Page key under TIME Mode for 3 seconds. All measured value AVG SPEED, AVG
PULSE, TIME, DIST, KCAL will reset.


TIMER SETTING
TIMER displays user’s workout duration. If the user does not set the COUNT DOWN TIME, TIMER will count up from 00:00 to
99:59. If user presets the COUNT DOWN TIME, the TIME will count down to zero with flashing display, then counting up the
Timer. Under DIST or KCAL, user can go into TIMER setting by holding ‘PAGE’ key for 3 seconds.

5. Troubleshooting
5.1 Common Problems

 Situation 1: No Display on Console
 Countermeasure:
1.   Press any key to bring the console to ‘Quick Start’ mode.
2.   Ensure the battery icon is not shown on the Console and transmitter have batteries installed properly. Red LED light will
     flash on the transmitter when battery is low.


 Situation 2: RPM or HR does not change.
 Countermeasure:
1.   Press ‘MODE key’ repeatedly to toggle between SPEED (SPD), DISTANCE (DIST), TIME (TIME) and CLOCK (CLK) values.
2.   Holding ‘PAGE key’ to clear past measured value or go into setting and exit.


 Situation 3: Cadence number jumps high or low.
 Countermeasure:
1.   Separate bikes may be paired to same console and are cross-talking, simply run transmitter pair stage again on the bike.
2.   Relocate the bike to a different part of the room, away from any RF interference areas.


 Situation 4: Heart Rate signal gets interrupted or drops out.
 Countermeasure:
1.   Ensure that there is a minimum distance of 36 inches between bikes.
2.   Make sure your chest strap is secure and electrodes are contacting your chest at all times.


 Situation 5: No Heart Rate signal displayed
 Countermeasure:
Ensure your chest strap is worn correctly, and there is moist under electrodes of the chest strap.

5.2 Smart Tension Knob Outlines / Calibration Procedure / Batteries Replace Guide

Smart Tension Knob Outlines




How to pair the smart tension knob
1. Press any button to wake up the console, then press the MODE button to switch the display to DIST or KCAL. Next,
    press and hold the PAGE key to enter the setting mode.


2. Smart Tension knob must be awake before pairing to ensure it does not enter sleep mode during the process.
    Smart Tension knob sleep mechanism: It enters sleep mode 15 seconds after losing Bluetooth connection to
    conserve power.


3. In configuration mode, press and hold the MODE and PAGE keys simultaneously to enter pairing mode.


4. When Smart Tension knob is successfully paired, the LEVEL symbol at the bottom left of the console display will light
    up, indicating a successful pairing.




How to calibrate the smart tension knob
1. Confirm that the console is connected to Smart Tension. When the calibration is successful, the LEVEL symbol on the
    console will flash rapidly for 2 seconds, indicating the Smart Tension connection.


2. The console enters calibration mode after long-pressing the PHONE key (middle button) while the TIME display is
    active. The screen will then display the left-level indicator and "LE" in the center.


3. In calibration mode, press and hold the MODE and PAGE keys to start the calibration. The number (LEVEL) 1 will begin
    flashing below "LE" on the screen.


4. Turn Smart Tension to the lightest resistance level, then press the MODE button to confirm. This indicates that the
    LEVEL 1 setting is complete, and the number (LEVEL) 100 will begin flashing at the bottom of the screen.


5. Turn the Smart Tension to the heaviest resistance level, then briefly press the MODE button to confirm. This
    completes the heaviest resistance setting, and the screen will exit calibration mode and return to normal operation.


6. Calibration complete.

NOTE: A blue light will show when the tension knob is touching, and when it is working properly. The blue light will go off when it
is not touch.


How to replace the batteries
Remove both sides' screws in order to change the batteries. To open turn counterclockwise, to close turn clockwise.




Other Instructions and Operations

1. In power-saving mode (standby mode) with no active connection, the user must press any button to wake up the
    console before use. The LEVEL symbol on the console screen will start flashing, indicating it is ready to connect to the
    Smart Tension knob. To wake up the Smart Tension knob, gently touch it instead of turning it quickly. The Smart
    Tension knob will flash a blue light to indicate it is awake. If there is no flashing blue light, touch a larger surface area
    of the Smart Tension knob. Once the blue light flashes and the LEVEL symbol on the console stops flashing, the
    connection is successful, and the device is ready for use.


2. The Smart Tension knob is activated by touch and will flash a blue light rapidly for 2 seconds upon waking up.
    If the Smart Tension knob is not connected to any Bluetooth device, touched, or rotated, it will enter sleep mode after
    20 seconds.




3. Due to its mechanical structure, slight discrepancies in resistance level readings may occur. If turning to the first
    resistance level does not display the correct level but instead shows a different value, press the side button on the
    Smart Tension knob to reset it. The resistance level will then display correctly as the first level, and the software will
    automatically complete the correction.

5.3 Console and Speed Sensor Transmitter Pairing Guide
STEP 1: press and hold both ‘MODE’ and ‘PAGE’ key for 3 seconds to go into setting mode.
STEP 2: Wake up speed sensor by stepping on the bike, the speed transmitter will send signals to the console for pair stage.




STEP 3: If the number ‘0’ is displayed on LCD, it means that pair stage is successfully completed. If the monitor displays ‘Err’, it
means the pair stage has failed. The user can press ‘PAGE’ key and repeat the pair stage again.
STEP 4: Console will exit pairing stage automatically after 10 seconds with previously paired speed transmitter.

5.4 The Wheel Diameter Ratio Adjustment Guide
The wheel diameter ratio is related to the calculation of RPM (speed). Therefore, if this parameter is set incorrectly, it will affect
the RPM (speed) calculation. Users will feel that the RPM (speed) is not accurate when pedaling the SB900.
The wheel diameter ratio on SB900 is 1:3.25.
The factory presets this parameter, the default setting should be “325”.
If the parameter is not correct, users can follow the steps below to adjust:
STEP 1: Press the MODE key to select KCAL mode.
STEP 2: Long press the PAGE key to enter the setting, press the MODE key to select the screen with this icon.
STEP 3: Press the PAGE key to enter wheel diameter ratio setting.
STEP 4: Press the MODE key to input the wheel diameter setting to be “325”.
STEP 5: Press the PAGE key for 3 seconds to return to the main screen when the setting is finished.

6. Parts Replacing Guide
6.1 Replacing the Batteries of Wireless Transmitter and Bluetooth Wireless Knob
STEP 1: Pull down the wireless transmitter downwards to unmount it. (As shown in Figure 1)
STEP 2: Push the back cover of the wireless transmitter in the direction of the arrow to replace the batteries. Reverse the steps to
reinstall. (As shown in Figure 2)
STEP 3: Use a 2.5mm hex key to remove three M3×6L hex screws from the socket under the Bluetooth wireless knob. Pull up the
top cover to access the battery for replacement. Reverse the steps to reassemble. (As shown in Figure 3)




6.2 Replacing the Chain Cover
STEP 1: Use an 8mm hex key to remove the center axle screw CP-M8×P1.0(34). Use a crank puller to extract the left crank (33).
Then, use a Phillips screwdriver to remove three umbrella head self-tapping screws - 3.5×12L (79) and three umbrella head
screws - M5×12L(80), to detach the left chain cover (48). (As shown in Figure 1)
STEP 2: Use an 8mm hex key to remove the center axle screw CP-M8×P1.0(34). Use a crank puller to extract the right crank (32).
Then, use a Phillips screwdriver to remove three umbrella head screws - M5×12L (80), to detach the right chain cover (47). (As
shown in Figure 2)
STEP 3: Reverse the steps to reassemble.

6.3 Replacing the Bluetooth Wireless Knob and Brake Assembly
STEP 1: Remove the flywheel guard (49) and water bottle holder (50). Then, use a 10mm ratchet wrench to remove the cap nut -
M6×6T (83). (Figure 1)
STEP 2: Start by using a 29mm open-end wrench to turn the hex nut adjuster (21) counterclockwise, allowing you to lift the
adjustment assembly upward. Then, use a 2mm hex key to remove the set screw - M4xP0.7x12L (78), allowing you to
disassemble the adjustment assembly and brake inner gear adjuster (22) by turning them apart. Next, use a 2.5mm hex key to
remove the two set screws - M5×5(104) on either side of the adjuster knob (105) and push rod axle (24), turning them
counterclockwise. (Figure2)
STEP 3: Use a 13mm ratchet wrench to loosen and remove the outer hex screw - M8×20L (85), the spring washer - ø8×1.5T (86),
and the flat washer - ø5/16"×18×1.5T (87) that secure the magnetic control brake assembly (on both ends). Then, you can pull
the magnetic control brake assembly forward to remove it. (As shown in Figure 3)
STEP 4: Reverse the steps to reinstall. When reinstalling, pay special attention to adjusting the spacing between the magnetic
control brake assembly's magnet and the flywheel to ensure they are centered.

6.4 Replacing the Idler Wheel Assembly
STEP 1: Start by removing the right crank (32) and the right chain cover (47). Then, take off the belt (37) from the belt pulley (30).
(Figure 1)
STEP 2: Use a 5mm hex key to remove the umbrella head hex screw - M8×P1.25×15L (66) and the anti-slip washer - ø8×ø18×3T
(72). Then, remove the umbrella head hex screw - M8×P1.25×10L (70) and the flat washer - 5/16"x23x2T (73). Finally, remove
the umbrella head hex screw - M10×P1.5×15L (67) to detach the idler wheel assembly. (As shown in Figure 2)
STEP 3: Reverse the steps to reinstall. When reinstalling, pay special attention to ensuring that the belt tension is at 180 lbs. If it
is insufficient, use a 6mm hex key to tighten the hex screw - M8×80L (65) clockwise to increase the belt tension. Conversely,
turning it counterclockwise will loosen the tension. (As shown in Figure 3)

6.5 Replacing the Flywheel, Axle, and Bearings
STEP 1: Start by removing the right crank (32), right chain cover (47), left crank (33), left chain cover (48), belt (37), and idler
wheel assembly. Rotate the Bluetooth wireless resistance knob to the lightest setting, then remove the nylon nut - M6×6T (83)
that secures the fixed magnetic control assembly. Pull down the push rod axle (24) and detach it from the movable assembly A
weldment (16). Push the magnetic control assembly upward to remove it. (As shown in Figure 1)
STEP 2: Use a 6mm hex key to remove the hex screw - M8×P1.25×30L (74) from the Flywheel Fixed Plate and use a 13mm open-
end wrench to remove the nylon nut - M8×7T (89). Then, use a 4mm hex key to remove three hex screws - M6×P1.0×10L (71)
from the Flywheel Fixed Plate. (As shown in Figure 2)
STEP 3: Remove the Flywheel Fixed Plate assembly (15). (As shown in Figure 3)
STEP 4: Take out the flywheel assembly. (As shown in Figure 4)
STEP 5: Use a C-ring clip clamp to remove the C-ring - ø17(77), allowing you to extract the flywheel axle (28). If you need to
replace the 6003 bearings (62), perform the replacement at this step. (As shown in Figure 5)
STEP 6: Reverse the steps to reassemble.

STEP 1: Start by removing the right crank (32), right chain cover (47), left crank (33), left chain cover (48), and belt (37). (Figure 1)

6.7 Replacing the Handlebar, Sliding Handlebar Mount (Front), and Handlebar Post
STEP 1: Use a 5mm hex key to remove the following components from the handle assembly (Figure 1):
4 pieces of hexagon screws - 5/16"×UNC18×3/4" (96)
4 pieces of spring washers - round 8×1.5T (5/16"×1.5T) (97)
4 pieces of flat washers - round 5/16"×16×1T (98)
STEP 2: Loosen and remove the fixed bracket of the quick release Lever (38). Then, use a 5mm hex key to remove the hexagon
screw inside the handle - 5/16"×UNC18×1" (92) to detach the Sliding Handlebar Mount (7). (As shown in Figure 2)
STEP 3: Loosen and remove the fixed bracket of the quick release Lever (39). Remove 2 pieces of aluminum blocks (40) and the
brake spring (41). Then, pull up the handle's telescopic tube assembly (5) along with the hollow sleeve (42). Use a 4mm hex key
to remove the hexagon screw inside the socket - M5×10L and take out the hollow sleeve (42). (As shown in Figure 3)
STEP 4: Reassemble the components in the reverse order.
