SCREEN REMAINS BLANK
Condition:
The console does not power on and shows no display.


Troubleshooting :
1.   Verify that the console cable and computer cable are securely connected, as shown in
     Figures.




2.   If all connections are correct, use a multimeter to measure the power output and identify
     any issues.

NO SPEED READING APPEARS ON THE
SCREEN
Condition:
The screen displays normally, but the speed remains at 0 or is not shown while pedaling.


Troubleshooting :
1. If the console is on bu t the speed is not displayed, remove the main chain cover (L&R) (A48,
   A49) and inspect the computer cables (A33/A36) and speed sensor (A6) to ensure they are
   properly installed. (Re fer to Figures)




2. If all components are installed correctly, the issue is likely with the sensor (A6) or the magnet
   (A45). Use a test magnet to check the functionality of the sensor (A6). Replace either the
   sensor (A6) or the magnet (A45), depending on which component is fa ulty.

NO HEART RATE READING APPEARS ON
THE SCREEN
Condition:
The screen displays normally, but the heart rate remains at 0 or is not shown while holding
the hand rate sensor.


Troubleshooting :
1.   First, check whether all cab le connectors between the console and the hand heart rate
     sensor are properly connected (see images).




2.   If all connectors are properly connected, use a multimeter to test the continuity of the hand
     grip wir ing.

ABNORMAL BEHAVIOR OF THE BIKE
WHEN PEDALING
Condition 1:
The user experiences slipping during pedaling.


Troubleshooting :
1.   Remove the left and right front shrouds (A48 & A49).
2.   Use an open -end wrench to adjust the idler wheel assembly adjustment screw (A13) until
     the belt no longer slip s.




Condition 2:
During pedaling, resistance suddenly drops to zero, similar to a bicycle chain coming off.

Troubleshooting :
This is usually caused by the drive belt slipping off. Please reinstall the drive belt following
the installation steps.
After installation and adjustment, rotate the drive belt (A12) forward and backward to test
its stability.
If the belt slips off, adjust the drive belt (A12) one notch in the opposite direction of the
slipping and test again.
If the belt continues to fall off, it is likely that the machine has been subjected to improper
handling, such as dropping or impact, causing dimensional deviations.
In this case, it is recommended to replace the Small Iron Tube (A84) and the Idler Wheel
Assembly (A16/A17).
If the issue still persists, it indicates possible deformation of the Main Frame (A), and the
entire machine may need to be replaced. However, this situation is rare since all machines
undergo testing before shipping.
