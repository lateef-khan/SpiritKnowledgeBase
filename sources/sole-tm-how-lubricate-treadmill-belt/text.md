How to Lubricate Treadmill Belt

How often you should lubricate: every 3 months or 90 hours of use
What you need: 2 ounces of 100% silicone based lubricant (preferably in gel form), 5mm or 6mm Allen Wrench
Go to the back of the machine with the Allen Wrench. Top hole on the end caps are the rear roller bolts used for tensioning.
Turn counter-clockwise 10 full turns on both bolts
Lift belt
Lay down lube in a “S” pattern towards center of deck where you will be
Tighten rear roller bolts clockwise 10 full turns to tension belt
Run machine at low speed (max of 2), belt will spread out the lubricant for you
