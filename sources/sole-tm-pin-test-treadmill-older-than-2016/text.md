Pin Test (Treadmill older than 2016)
(You need a Phillips screwdriver and 2 twist ties with paper cut off on each end)

-Use screwdriver to remove console mast cover on the user’s right and side.
-Disconnect the bigger of the 2 connectors.
-With the portion of that connector coming out of the upright, hold it so the thumb latch is sitting on top. The bottom left corner is #1, count to the right to #6. The top right corner is #7, count to the right to #12.
-You will use the twist ties to connect pins together. Below the table is the pins you pin together to make speed or incline happen.
