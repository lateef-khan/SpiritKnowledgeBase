       Please turn off the power when replacing any parts such as electronic watch, brake system, generator, etc.
        Avoid possible personal or product injury caused by power operation.
       When replacing parts, please use the original parts from manufacturer and make sure the product model and
        specification match.
       Replace parts, especially electrical parts, clean the parts and parts around before assembly to avoid damage
        caused by entering screws and other sundries.

     7. Determination of defective accessories:


    Communication line continuity measurement




     Use the multi-meter's two test leads to contact the upper and lower terminals of the control line, measure
     the same color line, and set the buzzer position. The multi-meter has a beeping sound and the resistance
     value is close to 0 ohms, then the line is normally conducting. If there is no resistance value, the line is
     disconnected. Generally it is pinch off or the terminal is not in good contact. You can replace the control
     wire for troubleshooting.

                           Red test lead test position     Control upper Control line (inside the fuselage cover)
