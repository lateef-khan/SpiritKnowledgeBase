# Level adjustment (leveling feet)

Placing the treadmill on an uneven surface or unstable supports can cause it to wobble during use, and the incline adjustment may also be affected.

The method for level adjustment is as follows:
1. Rotate **A** counterclockwise to loosen it.
2. Adjust **B** (the leveling foot) to the appropriate height.
3. Rotate **A** clockwise to tighten it.
4. The adjustment method is the same for both sides.

# Powering on the treadmill

Power the treadmill on by plugging it into an appropriate wall outlet, then turn on the power switch located at the front of the treadmill below the motor hood.

**Ensure that the safety key is installed — the treadmill will not power on without it.** See [safety/safety-stop-key-and-tether-cord.md](../safety/safety-stop-key-and-tether-cord.md).

When the power switch is turned on, the treadmill console will take around **10 seconds** to power on. The console will then enter idle mode, which is the starting point for operation.

The power switch is located on the panel at the bottom of the treadmill. Switch position **"1"** = ON, **"0"** = OFF.

If the console does not power on at all, see [errors/no-code-no-power-to-console.md](../errors/no-code-no-power-to-console.md).

# Quick start — first workout in three steps

1. Press any key to wake the display up if it is not already on.
2. Press the **Start** key to begin belt movement at 0.5 mph / 0.8 kph, then adjust to the desired speed using the **Speed +/− keys**, or by typing the desired speed on the numeric keypad. Once the desired setting is selected, press **Enter**.
3. To stop the slat belt, press and release the **Stop** key.

# Assembly step 1 — attach the handlebar upright tubes

This is step 1 of 6 in the main assembly sequence (followed by [step 2](step-2-attach-water-cup-holder.md), [step 3](step-3-attach-handrail-tubes.md), [step 4](step-4-attach-console-bracket.md), [step 5](step-5-attach-console.md), and [step 6](step-6-install-covers-and-power-cord.md)).

**Note:** To avoid any potential hazards, assembly of the machine must be carried out by two or more individuals; do not attempt to assemble it alone.

1. Use the magic tape attached inside the **Right Handlebar Upright Tube (C)** to secure the wires of the platform tightly, then thread them through the upright tube.
2. Use **Screws (a), Spring washers (b), and Washers (c)** to secure the left and right **Handlebar Upright Tubes (B, C)** to the **Frame assembly (A)**, but do not tighten them at this stage.

**Note:** During assembly, take care not to pinch any wires.

# Bluetooth connectivity and auto-disconnect behavior

After powering on, the device automatically activates Bluetooth functionality, remaining ready to connect and allowing simultaneous pairing with both the app and the Bluetooth chest strap (sold separately).

Automatic disconnect rules:
1. When the user returns to idle mode, if no signal connection is detected within **3 minutes** of standby, both the app and heart rate monitor will automatically disconnect.
2. When the user presses pause, if no signal connection is detected within **3 minutes** of standby, both the app and heart rate monitor will automatically disconnect.
3. When the user pauses a workout but does not return to idle mode, if no signal connection is detected within **3 minutes** of standby, both the app and heart rate monitor will automatically disconnect.

For chest strap pairing/wearing instructions, see [console/heart-rate-chest-strap-pairing.md](heart-rate-chest-strap-pairing.md).

# Console screen overview — layout and controls

The console layout includes:
- **LED Data Display Windows** — numeric columns down each side (0–20 incline scale on the left, 1–15 speed scale on the right) with **Direct Access Speed Buttons** on the right side and an incline scale on the left.
- **Dot Matrix Message Center** — the central pixel display showing the 1/4-mile track, workout data, and program setup prompts.
- **LEVEL / SPEED indicator** and the small runner icons showing current activity.
- **Scan button** — cycles the LED data display windows.
- **MODE SELECT** — toggles between Treadmill, Free (Neutral), and Sled Trainer modes.
- **SLED RESISTANCE LEVELS** — direct-access buttons 1–15 for Sled Mode resistance.
- **PROGRAMS** button with **Up/Down/Enter** controls.
- Lower message window with **DISTANCE, CALORIES, PULSE, TIME ELAPSED, PACE, WATTS, METS, TIME REMAINING** indicators.
- **Tablet-friendly reading rack** below the display.
- **Start, Stop, Speed, and Incline controls** on the console arms.
- **Safety Stop Key** — the emergency stop button between the handrails. Full procedure: [safety/safety-stop-key-and-tether-cord.md](../safety/safety-stop-key-and-tether-cord.md).
- **Contact Heart Rate Grips** — stainless steel hand pulse sensors on both handrails.

For the full list of programs and modes reachable from this console, see [programs/programmable-features-overview.md](../programs/programmable-features-overview.md).

# Dot Matrix Message Center (workout profile display)

Ten rows of dots indicate each level of a workout in Manual mode. The dots only show an approximate level (speed/incline) of effort — they do not necessarily indicate a specific value, only an approximate percent to compare levels of intensity.

In Manual Operation, the Speed/Incline Dot Matrix Message Center will build a profile "picture" as values are changed during a workout. There are twenty-four columns, indicating time. The 24 columns are divided into 1/24th of the total time of the program. When the time is counting up from zero (as in Quick Start), each column represents 1 minute.

# Factory Mode — setting minimum and maximum speed

**Entering Factory Mode:** When the console performs a power-on reset, press the **Start** and **Speed Up** keys simultaneously to enter Factory Mode.

1. After entering Factory Mode, the MW first displays "FACTORY SETTING," then displays "UNITS – ENGLISH" or "UNITS – METRIC." Use the Up/Down keys to switch between English and Metric units, and press Enter to confirm.
2. The MW displays "ADJUST MIN SPEED," and the SPEED window shows the value. Use the Up/Down keys to adjust the setting.
   - Metric range: 0.8–1.0 KPH
   - English range: 0.5–0.6 MPH
   Press Enter to confirm.
3. The MW displays "ADJUST MAX SPEED," and the SPEED window shows the value. Use the Up/Down keys to adjust the setting.
   - Metric range: 16.0–25.0 KPH
   - English range: 10.0–15.6 MPH
   Press Enter to confirm.
4. The MW displays "FINISHED," followed by the scrolling message "CONSOLE RESET."

A quick-reference version of these ranges is at [specs/speed-and-incline-ranges.md](../specs/speed-and-incline-ranges.md). Note the default Quick Start speed (0.5 mph / 0.8 kph — see [assembly/quick-start.md](../assembly/quick-start.md)) falls at the low end of the min-speed range set here.

# Heart rate chest strap — attaching and wearing (not included)

The Bluetooth heart rate chest strap is sold separately (not included).

1. Attach the transmitter to the elastic strap using the interlocking key.
2. Adjust the strap as tightly as possible as long as it remains comfortable.
3. Position the transmitter with the logo centered in the middle of your torso facing away from your chest (some people must position the transmitter slightly left of center). 4. Position the transmitter directly below the pectoral muscles.
5. Sweat is the best conductor to measure very minute heart-beat electrical signals. Plain water can also be used to pre-wet the electrodes (2 ribbed oval areas on the reverse side of the belt and both sides of the transmitter). It's also recommended to wear the transmitter strap a few minutes before your workout — some users, due to body chemistry, have a harder time achieving a strong steady signal at first; this lessens after "warming up."
6. Your workout must be within range — the distance between transmitter and receiver — to achieve a strong, steady signal. The range may vary but generally stays close enough to the console to maintain good, reliable readings. Wearing the transmitter directly on bare skin assures proper operation. You may wear the transmitter over a shirt if you wet the areas of the shirt the electrodes will rest upon.

**Note:** The transmitter automatically activates when it detects heart activity, and automatically deactivates when it does not receive any activity. Although water resistant, moisture can create false signals, so completely dry the transmitter after use to prolong battery life (estimated transmitter battery life is **2500 hours**). # Heart rate chest strap — erratic or wild readings (troubleshooting)

# Heart rate training zones and maximum heart rate formula

"No pain, no gain" is a myth — exercise is much more enjoyable, and effective, when you maintain heart rate in the desired benefit range.

**Maximum Heart Rate (MHR) formula:** 220 minus your age.

Your Heart Rate training zone is 50% to 90% of your MHR:
- **65% of MHR** is recommended for burning fat.
- **85% of MHR** is recommended for strengthening the cardiovascular system.

### Example: 40-year-old user
- 220 − 40 = 180 (maximum heart rate)
- 180 × 0.65 = 117 beats per minute (65% of maximum)
- 180 × 0.85 = 153 beats per minute (85% of maximum)
- Training zone: **117 to 153 beats per minute**

If you enter your age during programming, the console performs this calculation automatically for the Heart Rate programs. With any Heart Rate program you may use the heart rate monitor feature without using the HR program — this works during manual mode or any of the nine fitness tests.

See also [console/heart-rate-perceived-exertion-rpe.md](heart-rate-perceived-exertion-rpe.md) for a non-numeric way to judge intensity, and [programs/heart-rate-control-hrc.md](../programs/heart-rate-control-hrc.md) for how the console's HR1/HR2 programs use 60%/85% of MHR automatically.

# Pause, Stop, and Reset behavior

# Adjusting the tightness of the running belt

If it is necessary to adjust the tension of the running belt, follow these steps:

1. After assembling and stabilizing the treadmill, check whether the running belt operates normally. First ensure the power supply meets the requirements. Plug in the power cord, start the treadmill by pressing the quick start button (START), then press the increase speed button to raise the speed to **4.0 miles per hour (MPH)**, which is equivalent to **6.4 km per hour (KPH)**.
2. To tighten the belt, rotate both sides clockwise by 1/4 turn. To loosen the belt, rotate both sides counterclockwise by 1/4 turn.

**DO NOT OVER TIGHTEN** — over tightening will cause belt damage and premature bearing failure. If you tighten the belt a lot and it still slips, the problem could actually be the **drive belt** — located under the motor cover — that connects the motor to the front roller. If that belt is loose, it feels similar to the walking belt being loose. Tightening the motor/drive belt should be done by a trained service person.

If the belt hesitates or does not stay centered, see [maintenance/troubleshooting-common-problems.md](troubleshooting-common-problems.md) — off-center tracking is often caused by the user's natural walking/running pattern rather than a tension problem.

# Applying grease to the drive belt tooth surface

When unusual noises are detected from the machine, first check whether the tooth surface of the drive belt is devoid of lubricating oil. If lubrication is required, use the manufacturer-specified **SL-41031** lubricating grease.

1. Remove the adjustment boxes from both front sides, and pull the red handle at the back of the motor upwards to loosen the running belt.
2. Slowly rotate the running belt by hand and inspect the tooth profile on the belt at the red circle (near the rear roller) to determine if there is any grease remaining. If there is no grease, apply solid grease. An L-shaped right-angle ruler can help you check/align the belt position at the front roller.

**Where to apply grease:**
- **Apply grease only to the toothed surface** of the belt.
- **Do not add grease to the flat surface** of the belt.
- Also apply oil/lubricant to the slanted surface on the inner side of the belt — this can effectively reduce abnormal noise.

Several inverter error codes (OC, OH, OL, OL1, OLO, HT) list "running belt/deck aging" as a possible cause and "apply lubricant to running belt" as the corrective action — see [errors/oc-inverter-output-overcurrent.md](../errors/oc-inverter-output-overcurrent.md), [errors/oh-inverter-overheat.md](../errors/oh-inverter-overheat.md), [errors/ol-motor-overload.md](../errors/ol-motor-overload.md), [errors/ol1-inverter-overload.md](../errors/ol1-inverter-overload.md), [errors/olo-system-overload.md](../errors/olo-system-overload.md), and [errors/ht-inverter-high-temperature.md](../errors/ht-inverter-high-temperature.md).

# Post-workout cleaning and noise prevention

Wipe down all areas in the sweat path with a damp cloth after each workout. If a squeak, thump, clicking, or rough feeling develops, the main cause is most likely one of two reasons:

1. **The hardware was not sufficiently tightened during assembly.** All bolts installed during assembly need to be tightened as much as possible. It may be necessary to use a larger wrench than the one provided if you cannot tighten the bolts sufficiently. **90% of calls to the service department for noise issues can be traced to loose hardware.**
2. **The crank arm nut needs to be retightened.** If squeaks or other noises persist, check that the unit is properly leveled. There are 2 leveling pads on the bottom of the rear stabilizer; use a 14mm wrench (or adjustable wrench) to adjust the levelers. See also [assembly/level-adjustment.md](../assembly/level-adjustment.md).

## Post-Workout Machine Care
1. Store your machine according to the instructions when not in use.
2. Use a slightly damp cloth to clean areas where sweat or oil made contact with the machine.
3. Use a microfiber cloth to clean the screen and remove unwanted oils and other things that may damage the screen.
4. Avoid leaving paper or other small debris in the cup holders.

## Warning
The safety level of the equipment can be maintained only if it is examined regularly for damage and wear.
1. Replace defective components immediately and/or keep the equipment out of use until repair.
2. The components most susceptible to wear: Belt, PU wheel, Bearing, Idler, Shaft, Pedal.

For sanitizing/disinfecting (as opposed to routine sweat wipe-down), see [maintenance/sanitizing-equipment.md](sanitizing-equipment.md). If noise persists after tightening hardware and leveling, it may be a lubrication issue — see [maintenance/lubricating-drive-belt-teeth.md](lubricating-drive-belt-teeth.md).

# Gerkin/WFI test guidance — before, during, and after

This guidance applies to both the [Gerkin](fitness-test-gerkin.md) and [WFI](fitness-test-wfi.md) fitness test protocols.

## Before the Gerkin/WFI Test
- Make sure you are in good health; check with your physician before performing any exercise if you are over the age of 35 or have pre-existing health conditions.
- Make sure you have warmed up and stretched before taking the test.
- Do not take in caffeine before the test.
- Hold the hand grips gently, do not tense up.

## During the Test
- The console must be receiving a steady heart rate for the test to begin. You may use the hand pulse sensors or wear a heart rate chest strap transmitter.
- The test starts with a **3 minute warm-up at 3 MPH** before the actual test begins.
- The data shown during the test is:
  a. Time — indicates total elapsed time
  b. Incline — in percent grade
  c. Distance — in Miles or Kilometers depending on preset parameter
  d. Speed — in MPH or KPH depending on preset parameter
  e. Target Heart Rate and Actual Heart Rate — shown in the Message Window

## After the Test
- Cool down for about one to three minutes.
- Take note of your score, because the console will automatically return to the start-up mode after a few minutes.

# Heart Rate Control (HRC) program

Heart Rate Control (HRC) uses the Slat Belt Sled Treadmill's incline system to control your heart rate via information from the pulse grips or wireless chest strap (sold separately). Increases and decreases in elevation affect heart rate much more efficiently than changes in speed alone. The HRC program automatically changes elevation gradually to achieve the programmed target heart rate.

## Selecting a Heart Rate Control Program
During setup mode, you may choose either:
- **Weight Control (HR 1)** — attempts to maintain your heart rate at **60%** of your Maximum Heart Rate.
- **Cardiovascular (HR 2)** — attempts to maintain your heart rate at **85%** of your Maximum Heart Rate.

Your Maximum Heart Rate is based on the formula 220 minus your age (see [console/heart-rate-training-zones.md](../console/heart-rate-training-zones.md)) and is automatically calculated during setup mode when you enter your age.

## Heart Rate Control Programming
1. Using the **Program** button, choose the **Heart Rate Control** program, then press **Enter**.
2. The window shows **PRESS ENTER TO MODIFY OR START TO BEGIN WORKOUT**. Press **Enter** for settings, or press **Start** to execute the program.
3. When Enter is pressed, the Message Window shows **PRESS 1 OR 2 TO SELECT THEN PRESS ENTER**. The Dot Matrix window shows "1" — press numeric button 1 or 2, then Enter, to choose HR1 or HR2.
4. Message Window shows **ADJUST TIME THEN PRESS ENTER** with the Time Window blinking. Use Up/Down buttons or numeric buttons to adjust, then press Enter again.
5. Message Window shows **ADJUST AGE THEN PRESS ENTER** with the Incline Window blinking. Use Up/Down/Fast/Slow buttons or numeric buttons to adjust — adjusting age changes the HR value (per the MHR formula). Press Enter again.
6. Message Window shows **ADJUST BODY WEIGHT THEN PRESS ENTER** with the Incline Window blinking. Adjust with Up/Down buttons or numeric buttons, then press Enter again.
7. Message Window shows **ADJUST HEART RATE THEN PRESS ENTER** with the Speed Window blinking. Adjust the target heart rate with Up/Down buttons or numeric buttons, then press Enter again.
8. Message Window shows **PRESS START TO BEGIN WORKOUT OR ENTER TO MODIFY**. Press **Start** to begin the workout, or **Stop** to return to the previous screen.

**Note:** When the Message Window shows **CHECK PULSE**, there is no pulse signal being received and the program cannot be performed. Check and make sure the heart rate strap is functioning normally — see [console/heart-rate-chest-strap-troubleshooting.md](../console/heart-rate-chest-strap-troubleshooting.md).

# HIIT (High Intensity Interval Training) program

The HIIT program takes advantage of the latest trend in fitness. During the program you perform short bursts of high-intensity sprinting followed by short rest periods. HIIT is a fully customizable interval training program — you can enter the number of intervals, the time of each interval's Sprint and Rest periods, and the work intensity of the levels.

1. Using the **Program** button, select the **HIIT** program, then press **Enter**. The Dot Matrix Message Center will ask you to enter your **Age**. Enter your age using the Up/Down keys, then press Enter to proceed to the next screen.
2. You are asked to enter your **Weight**. Adjust using the Up/Down keys, then press Enter to continue.
3. You are asked for the number of intervals. **Default is 10, range is 3 to 15.** One interval equals 1 Sprint and 1 Rest segment.
4. You enter the Interval time. The Dot Matrix Message Center shows: Sprint Time:30 and Rest Time:30. The Sprint time blinks — use Up/Down to adjust from **30 to 60 seconds**, then press Enter. The Rest period time then blinks — adjust and press Enter. The Dot Matrix Message Center will display the total program time.
5. The Dot Matrix Message Center now displays SPRINT SPD 6.0 MPH. Use Up/Down to adjust the sprint speed you desire, then press Enter.
6. The Dot Matrix Message Center now displays REST SPEED 3.0 MPH. Use Up/Down to adjust the rest speed you desire, then press Enter.
7. Press **Start** to begin the HIIT program. The program starts with a 3-minute warm-up period with speed set to 50% of the sprint speed selected previously. You can manually adjust the speed during warm-up if you wish.

Custom Interval is a sub-mode of [Sled Mode](sled-mode-manual.md) that alternates timed sled-pushing intervals with timed rest periods.

5. The user continues to alternate back and forth between sled time and rest time until they press **STOP**.

# Electrical safety precautions

**WARNING!** Route the power cord away from any moving part of the unit, including the elevation mechanism and transport wheels.

**NEVER** remove any cover without first disconnecting AC power. If voltage varies by ten percent (10%) or more, the performance of your unit may be affected. **Such conditions are not covered under your warranty.** If you suspect the voltage is low, contact your local power company or a licensed electrician for proper testing.

**NEVER** expose this unit to rain or moisture. This product is **NOT** designed for use outdoors, near a pool or spa, or in any other high-humidity environment. The temperature specification is 40 degrees C, and humidity is 95%, non-condensing (no water drops forming on surfaces). See [specs/environmental-operating-conditions.md](../specs/environmental-operating-conditions.md) for the quick-reference numbers.

**Circuit breakers:** Avoid AFCI/GFCI circuit breakers if possible. These breakers may trip occasionally during exercise because of the high inrush currents of the unit's drive electronics and motor — this affects all treadmill brands. New laws in your area may require these breakers. If you have these breakers or outlets and are experiencing nuisance tripping, check whether other devices are plugged into the same circuit. Examples of devices that may also cause tripping: fluorescent lights with electronic ballasts, coffee makers, space heaters, hair dryers.

Additional precautions:
- **NEVER** operate this unit without reading and completely understanding the results of any operational change you request from the computer.
- Understand that changes in speed and incline do not occur immediately. Set your desired work level on the computer console and release the adjustment key — the computer will obey the command gradually.
- **NEVER** use your unit during an electrical storm. Surges in household power supply could damage unit components. Unplug the unit as a precaution.
- Use caution while participating in other activities while walking on your unit (watching TV, reading, etc.) — these distractions may cause you to lose balance, which may result in serious injury.
- Do not use excessive pressure on console control keys. They are precision-set to function properly with little finger pressure.

# Grounding instructions and power outlet requirements

**This product must be grounded.** In the unlikely event that the treadmill's electrical system malfunctions or breaks down, grounding provides a path of least resistance for electric current, reducing the risk of electric shock. This product is equipped with a cord having an equipment-grounding plug. The plug must be plugged into an outlet that is properly installed and grounded in accordance with all local codes and ordinances.

**DANGER** — Improper connection of the equipment-grounding conductor can result in a risk of electric shock. Check with a qualified electrician or serviceman if you are in doubt whether the product is properly grounded. Do not modify the plug provided with the product if it will not fit the outlet; have a proper outlet installed by a qualified electrician.

Wiring should comply with local electrical regulations. High-voltage lines, low-voltage lines, and underground cables must be routed separately and must not be connected to or intertwined with other lines.

## Power and motor specification
- **Motor:** 3.0HP AC
- **Power:** 120 volts, 60 Hz, dedicated 20-amp circuit (NEMA 5-20P standard power plug/receptacle type)

A quick-reference version of these numbers is at [specs/electrical-requirements.md](../specs/electrical-requirements.md).
