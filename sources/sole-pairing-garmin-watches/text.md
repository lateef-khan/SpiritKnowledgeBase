# Pairing your GARMIN wearable device with SOLE equipment

Certain Garmin watch models can be connected to supported machines to operate like a wireless heart rate monitor. The process for connecting the Garmin watch to the machine is dependent on if the machine has a touchscreen or not. Connecting a Garmin watch can be used in place of needing a separate wireless heart rate monitor for displaying heart rate and using the built-in heart rate programs if present.

## Touchscreens

CAUTION: Please ensure your GARMIN wearable device has been DISCONNECTED from your smartphone before pairing with SOLE equipment. Turn Bluetooth off on the phone if needed so the watch doesn't automatically re-connect to the phone before it can be paired to the machine.

Step 1: Press GARMIN button at the top of the screen and press Add Device when it is your FIRST time pairing GARMIN wearable device with SOLE equipment.

Step 2: Switch your GARMIN wearable device into Pairing Mode and make sure it has been DISCONNECTED from your smartphone.

Notice: Different models of GARMIN wearable devices may have different operation flow and interfaces to switch into pairing mode. Please check out your GARMIN user's manual or contact GARMIN Support for more detailed instructions. Some GARMIN watches will display a QR code when it is in pairing mode and there is no need to go through the GARMIN Connect app.

Step 3: You may find your GARMIN wearable device model name appear on the console screen once SOLE equipment detected it. Press to select.

Notice: If the GARMIN wearable device DOES NOT show on the list, please try Step 2 again to re-connect.

Step 4: PIN code will be generated on your GARMIN wearable device, please enter it on SOLE equipment. And now you have completed the pairing process, enjoy.

SOLE equipment should automatically pair with your GARMIN wearable device after you complete the pairing process. Please make sure your GARMIN device is disconnected from mobile devices for SOLE equipment to do so.

If you have multiple GARMIN wearable devices connected to SOLE equipment, you may check out the connected list by pressing the GARMIN button at the top of the screen.

## Non-Touchscreens

For connecting a Garmin watch to a non-touchscreen, one directional communication is supported through the wireless HRM protocol. The process to enable HRM broadcasting on each watch may vary depending on the model, but the general process is as follows:

- On the Garmin watch, make sure that Broadcast During Activity (sometimes called Broadcast Heart Rate) is enabled in the settings.
  - Might be under Sensors & Accessories > Wrist Heart Rate or under the Bluetooth settings
- The machine should automatically connect to the watch when a workout is started.
- Check the owner's manual of the watch for the specific process on how to enable it if the watch supports HRM.
