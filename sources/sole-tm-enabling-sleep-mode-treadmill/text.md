Enabling Sleep Mode
(Treadmills)

For the 2023 F63 and F65:

Turn on the power switch of the machine if it isn’t already powered on.
Insert the safety key.
Press and hold the Stop and Enter buttons. While holding those buttons, press the Display button. The machine should pop up Sleep Mode.
Press speed up to change from OFF to ON.
Press enter to confirm the selection.

The machine will go into sleep mode after 15-30 minutes of inactivity while the safety key is in. The screen will also turn off when the safety key is removed. The machine can be woken up by pressing the start button if the safety key is still in.

For All Non-Touchscreen Treadmills:

Turn on the power switch of the machine if it isn’t already powered on.
Press and hold the Stop, Enter, and Start buttons.
Insert the safety key while holding those buttons, and keep holding the buttons until the screen says EM or Engineering Menu. This should only take 10 seconds.
You may let go of all the buttons and press Enter. This menu can be navigated by pressing the speed up and down buttons to change selections, pressing enter to choose a selection, and pressing stop to navigate back a menu.
Press speed up multiple times until it says Functions, then press Enter.
Press speed up multiple times until it says Display Mode or Sleep Mode, then press enter.
If the selection was Display Mode, press speed down to set it to OFF. If the selection was Sleep Mode, press speed up to set it to ON. Then press enter to lock in the selection.
You may then press the stop button until it says Functions, then press speed up until it says Exit, then press enter to exit the Engineering Menu.

The machine will go into sleep mode after 15-30 minutes of inactivity while the safety key is in. The screen will also turn off when the safety key is removed. The machine can be woken up by pressing the start button if the safety key is still in.

For All Touchscreen Treadmills:

Turn on the power switch of the machine if it isn’t already powered on.
Login to a personal account or choose the Guest profile.
Open the settings by pressing the gear icon in the top-left corner.
Under the settings, change the selection under Sleep Mode to On.

The machine will go into sleep mode after 15-30 minutes of inactivity while the safety key is in. The machine can be woken up by pressing the start button.
