On the SRVO control panel, you can perform resistance adjustments, switch
modes, and start/stop operations. If the number on the weight display is
flashing, SRVO is in an adjustable state.
▪ Adjusting Resistance
      ▪ Adjust the resistance weight by turning the knob. Turn to the right
        to increase and turn to the left to decrease.
      ▪ Press the start/stop button to engage resistance.
      ▪ To ensure your safety, you cannot switch between modes or weights
        while resistance is active. When the resistance is activated, the
        number on the screen is always on and will not flash.

▪ Changing Resistance Mode
    ▪ Press and hold the Start/Stop button for three seconds to alternate
      between modes.
    ▪ Mode will show as 1, 2, or 3 in the top left corner of the display.
    ▪ For Standard mode (1) and Eccentric mode (2), you can now adjust

Direction to access stuck cable on SRVO Strength Trainer
1. Remove the back cover, exposing the servomotors.
2. Remove the Allen Head bolts holding the motor in place with a long Allen key.
3. Remove all screws holding the plastic guard connected to the SRVO motor.
Once the plastic covers on the servomotor is removed, you will be able to see the pulley and cable system. To unstick the
cable you can do the following.
1. Turn the machine on while holding the cable lightly with the other hand.
2. While the machine is starting up, try to move the pulley in both directions until the pulley starts to retract.
     a. Your other hand should be holding the cable and applying a small amount of tension so that the cable retracts
         smoothly.
3. Once the cable retracts correctly, then put the machine back together.
