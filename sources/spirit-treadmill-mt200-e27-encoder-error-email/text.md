Please refer to the below troubleshooting for E27 error code.

For the E27 error, it is the warning message of the encoder on the driving motor.
| will give you some suggestions of this error message before your further feedback.
If the error message was from the first using, it can be the wiring issues due to the connectors might came loose to cause
bad signal.
But your unit has been used, you still can check the wiring again.
1. Please loosen the screws under the console bracket in order to check the wirings of the console back.
You need to make sure all the connectors are fitted firmly and without pinched or damaged situation.

2. Please remove the motor top cover to find the wirings of the relevant parts which are marked below.
Please make sure all the connectors are fitted firmly and without pinched or damaged situation as well.

If the situation is abnormal on your treadmill, please try to replace the #275 encoder.

2. Then it might be the fault inverter due to the unstable voltage was supplied from the inverter to the driving

The way to sort whether brake function is correct or not.
L]

There are some ways to check the brake function, better to do all to make sure.
1. Please find attached video, it’s the simple test as you can have a quick start on the machine.
When you start the speed, the LED of Encoder will turn to red and appear a short “clicking”
sound, it means that brake is released and allow the running belt to operate. Then stop the
machine, the brake is returned and also appear a short clicking sound. This situation states
the brake function acts no problem.
2. Please find section 4.2.1 maintenance mode(engineering mode) in service manual.
There is Motor Brake test under Service Mode.
