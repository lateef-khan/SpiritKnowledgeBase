OPERATING PRINCIPLE

The operator will start the 8.0T and use the speed and incline keys to control motors.
When the speed key is pressed, a signal will be sent to the AC motor inverter to
request a change. The inverter will then send the appropriate amount of power the
AC drive motor to reach the requested speed. The motor drives the front roller (which
in turn moves the walking belt) through pulleys and fan belt. The incline motor is
operated when the operator presses the incline keys. A command is generated that is
transmitted to the incline control board where a relay is energized sending AC power
to the motor until it reaches the desired position.


INTENDED OPERATOR
 Education: medical professional or medical occupation with physical therapist
   license.
 Knowledge: the operator should read the user’s manual before use.

INTENDED INSTALLER
 Education: high school or above.
 Knowledge/Experience: the installer shall be given a specific training by
   manufacturer. The installer must have experience in this equipment assembly and
   disassembly.
 Background: the installer must be electro-mechanically trained.
 Professional competence: normal vision ability required.

OPERATING THE 8.0T

The 8.0T is an easy product to set up and use, from the adjustments to the intuitive interface. This section explains how to set up,
adjust and operate the 8.0T.

UNIQUE USES FOR THE 8.0T
 The walking belt allows for both forward(speed up to 22 km/h) and reverse(speed up to 8 km/h) walking.
   Forward is the default direction setting when the 8.0T is powered on.
   The reverse speed will show a minus(-) sign indicating the speed is set to reverse.
   Belt speed settings start at 0.1 km/hr.
   True Zero Speed, when the belt is not moving the motor is locked for patient safety. We using a lock on our motor instead of
    trying to use current control means absolutely no vibration at zero speed.
   Belt Speed Acceleration/Deceleration Adjustments, offers belt speed adjustment range from 1 second to 1 minute for every 1
    km/h(1 mph) of belt speed. The default setting is 3 seconds, which means the belt will take 3 seconds to go from zero to 1 km/
    h(mph). The range can be set from 1 second to 60 seconds.
   Symmetry program measures distance between left and right step lengths. Graphical bio-feedback display motivates patients to
    maintain even step symmetry between left and right legs.
   The walking belt provides basic gait assessment and training with a graphic sign.
   The front and rear incline allows for uphill(up to 25% grade) and downhill(down to -10% grade) walking.
   The parallel handrails are fully adjustable height/width to accommodate for pediatric to large adult sizes.
   Deck Lift program offers 30 levels of step height in 6.35 mm(0.25 inch) increments, increasing precision for step-up training, also
    assisting therapists in many ways.
   Safe Step offers user move the distance of step length every time user steps on the deck.
