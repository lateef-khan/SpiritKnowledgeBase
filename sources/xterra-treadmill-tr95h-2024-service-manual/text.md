                 1. Assembly sequence and precautions

1-1 front post assembly
When assembling the front post, you need to first connect the signal cables on the right front
post, and then insert the excess wire length into the tube to avoid compression and damage
to the signal cables.




1-2. Assembling console, storage box and desktop rack
1. Connect the cables
2. Put the storage box onto the console stand first.
3. Place the console stand and storage box assembly on the desktop rack at the same time
PS: When placing the console stand assembly, you need to insert the excessive wire length
into the tube to avoid compression and damage to the signal cables.
4. Fix the console stand assembly by fastening the screws
1-3. Storage box installation
Turn the desktop assembly to the back and lock the storage box




1-4. Assembling the desktop assembly and front posts
1. Connect the signal cable properly.
2. Place the desktop assembly on the front post and lock it with screws
PS: Before placing the desktop assembly and locking it, the excess wire length needs to be
inserted into the tube to avoid compression and damage to the signal cables.
1-5. Left/right upper handrail assembly
1. Connect the left and right fast keys and hand pulse signal cables.
2. Fix the left and right upper handrail by screws.
PS: Before locking the left and right upper handrails, the excessive wire length needs to be
inserted into the tube to avoid compression and damage to the cables.

                  6. Steps for incline motor replacement
1. Place the new incline motor on the side of the machine and connect to the power and
signal cables of the original incline motor.
2. Press the START button on the upper controller to start the treadmill.
3. Press the Incline UP button to incline LEVEL 2 position.
4. Wait until the incline motor stops rotating, press the incline DOWN button to incline LEVEL
0 position.
5. Wait until the incline motor stops rotating, turn the telescopic tube to adjust the assembly
size, measure the assembly size before installation (should be 268.5mm, as in figure below).
6. Remove the old incline motor and install the new incline motor.

      11.Explanation and troubleshooting of error messages
1. The console does not light up.
Reason: The console has no power supply.
Troubleshooting: (a). Check whether the AC power is supplied to the driver board.
                  (b). Check if the upper and lower controller connection cable is connected
                       or damaged.
                  (c). Console or driver board failure.
2. The console is on but displays "- - -".
Reason: The safety key connection of the console is abnormal.
Troubleshooting: (a). Check whether the micro switch in the safety key box is faulty
                  (b). Check whether the wire connecting the safety key to the console is
                       correct.
3.E1.
(A). The console does not receive the speed signal.
     (1). The console displays (E1) after no operation of the driver motor for about 6 seconds.
          Troubleshooting: (a). Check if the connection cable of the driver motor is connected
                                   correctly.
                              (b). Driver board or driver motor failure.
     (2). The console displays (E1) about 6 seconds after the driver motor operates.
          Troubleshooting: (a). Check whether the speed sensor cable is connected correctly.
                              (b). Check whether the speed sensor is faulty.
                              (c). Check whether the distance between the speed sensor and the
                                   magnet is within the range of 3~6mm
(B). Current limit protection.
     Scenario: After operating for some time, the console displays (E1) and shuts down. After
     being shut down for some time, the treadmill can be used again.
     Troubleshooting: (a). Add lubricant between the running deck and running belt.
                         (b). Motor failure.
4.E3.
Reason: Poor communication between upper and lower controllers.
Troubleshooting: (a). Check whether the upper and lower controller connection cable is
                        connected or damaged.
                   (b). Console or driver board failure.
5.E6
Reason: The console did not receive the change in the VR value of the incline motor.
Scenario 1: The VR value of the incline motor is out of range, causing the telescopic tube to
             get stuck.
            Troubleshooting: (a). Remove the incline motor and connect it to the driver
                                   board.
                              (b). Turn on the power of the treadmill, and the incline motor
                                   will automatically rotate to the position of correct VR value.
                              (c). Rotate the telescopic tube and adjust to the correct
                                   assembly size (268.5mm between the upper and lower
                                   holes).
                              (e). Install the incline motor back to its original position.
Scenario 2: Incline motor does not operate.
             Troubleshooting: (a). Check whether the connection cable of the incline motor is
                                   connected properly or damaged.
                              (b). Incline motor failure.
