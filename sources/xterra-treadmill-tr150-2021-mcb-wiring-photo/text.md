# TR150 / T500 motor control board (MCB): what plugs where (annotated photo)

Source file: `Treadmills/TR150 T500 MCB wiring.jpg` in the Xterra Service Manuals folder.
A 4032x3024 phone photograph dated 21 November 2019, held in a hand, of a green motor
control board on a finned aluminium base plate. Someone has laid **hand-written labels**
over the photo. The label text is copied here exactly; the board's own silkscreen markings
were read from the photo on 2026-09-11 and are noted as far as they are legible.

Title label (top right): **TR150/T500 MCB**. "T500" names a second machine that shares
this board; nothing else in the picture identifies it.

| Label on the photo (verbatim) | Where it points | Board silkscreen, as far as legible |
|---|---|---|
| 5 pin lower cable | a black 5-pin header at the top left of the board | — |
| If you have speed sensor, then it plugs here. | a small white 2-pin connector at the left edge, below the 5-pin header | **SPD** |
| M+ Red lead from drive motor | a spade terminal at the left edge, mid-height | **M+**, with **RED** printed beside it |
| M- Black lead from drive motor | a spade terminal at the left edge, lower | **M-**, with **BLACK** printed beside it |
| White lead from On/off switch | a spade terminal at the right edge, upper | **AC N** (partly hidden) |
| Black lead from on/ off switch | a spade terminal at the right edge, lower | **AC L** |

Other markings visible on the board: an oval **110V** label next to the AC terminals; a
large radial capacitor marked **1000µF 200V** carrying a sticker "Shanghai Electronics Way
Co.,Ltd"; a transformer marked **EE22-1.2mH**; a green RoHS sticker; a row of four
opto-isolators below the 5-pin header; two red LEDs at the bottom edge near the AC
terminals. The board is screwed to a slotted aluminium plate that acts as the heat sink.

What the photo establishes: on the TR150 / T500 MCB the console's 5-pin lower cable, the
optional speed sensor (SPD), the drive motor leads (M+ red, M- black) and the two on/off
switch leads (white to AC N, black to AC L) each have a marked position. It does not
establish the 5-pin cable's pin-out, the switch's other connections, or any voltage or
resistance test value.
